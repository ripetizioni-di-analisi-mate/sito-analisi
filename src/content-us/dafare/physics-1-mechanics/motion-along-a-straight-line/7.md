A ball is thrown vertically upward from the ground with initial speed $25\,\mathrm{m/s}$, with air resistance negligible. Find the maximum height reached and the total time of flight. Take $g = 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

:::soluzione
$h = \dfrac{v_0^2}{2g} = 31.9\,\mathrm{m}$, $\ t_f = \dfrac{2\,v_0}{g} = 5.10\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
