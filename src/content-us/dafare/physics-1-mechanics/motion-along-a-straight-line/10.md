titolo: Maximum velocity with a decreasing acceleration
teoria: [integration-of-motion, instantaneous-acceleration, slope-of-graphs]

A body starts from rest with acceleration $a(t) = 4.0 - 1.0\,t$, in SI units. Find the instant at which its velocity is maximum and the value of that maximum velocity.

:::soluzione
$v(t) = 4.0\,t - 0.50\,t^2$, maximum at $t = 4.0\,\mathrm{s}$, where $v = 8.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
