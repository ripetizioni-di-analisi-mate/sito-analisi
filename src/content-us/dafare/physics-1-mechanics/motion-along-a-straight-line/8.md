A stone is thrown vertically downward with initial speed $8.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ from a height of $35\,\mathrm{m}$, with air resistance negligible. Find its impact speed and the time it takes to reach the ground. Take $g = 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

:::soluzione
$v = \sqrt{v_0^2 + 2\,g\,h} = 27.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$, $\ t = \dfrac{v - v_0}{g} = 1.98\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
