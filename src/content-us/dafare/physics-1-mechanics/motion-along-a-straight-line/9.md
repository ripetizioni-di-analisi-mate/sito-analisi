The velocity-time graph of a body is a straight segment from $v = 0$ at $t = 0$ to $v = 16\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at $t = 5.0\,\mathrm{s}$, followed by a horizontal segment at $16\,\mathrm{m/s}$ up to $t = 14\,\mathrm{s}$. Find the displacement and the average velocity over the whole interval.

:::soluzione
$\Delta x = 40\,\mathrm{m} + 144\,\mathrm{m} = 184\,\mathrm{m}$, $\ v_{\mathrm{av}} = 13.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
