A train starts from rest and accelerates with $0.50\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ for $40\,\mathrm{s}$, then travels at constant speed for $120\,\mathrm{s}$, and finally brakes uniformly to rest in $25\,\mathrm{s}$. Find the total distance covered and the average speed of the trip.

:::soluzione
$d = 400\,\mathrm{m} + 2400\,\mathrm{m} + 250\,\mathrm{m} = 3050\,\mathrm{m}$, average speed $= \dfrac{3050\,\mathrm{m}}{185\,\mathrm{s}} = 16.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
