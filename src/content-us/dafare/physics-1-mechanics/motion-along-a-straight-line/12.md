A cyclist travelling at the constant speed of $8.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ passes a car that is at rest at a traffic light. At that same instant the car starts with constant acceleration $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ in the same sense. Find the instant at which the car catches up with the cyclist and the distance covered up to that instant.

:::soluzione
$t = \dfrac{2\,v}{a} = 8.0\,\mathrm{s}$, $\ x = 64\,\mathrm{m}$ (the car then travels at $16\,\mathrm{m/s}$).
:::
