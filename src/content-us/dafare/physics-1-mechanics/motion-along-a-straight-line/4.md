A car traveling at $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$ brakes with constant acceleration of magnitude $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ until it stops. Find the stopping distance and the duration of the braking.

:::soluzione
$d = \dfrac{v_0^2}{2\,|a|} = 40\,\mathrm{m}$, $\ t = \dfrac{v_0}{|a|} = 4.0\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
