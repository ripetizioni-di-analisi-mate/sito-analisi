A body is released from rest from a height of $80\,\mathrm{m}$ above the ground, with air resistance negligible. Find the time it takes to reach the ground and its impact speed. Take $g = 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

:::soluzione
$t = \sqrt{\dfrac{2h}{g}} = 4.04\,\mathrm{s}$, $\ v = \sqrt{2\,g\,h} = 39.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
