A body moves along the $x$ axis with the law of motion $x(t) = 5.0 - 3.0\,t + 1.0\,t^2$, with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. Find the instantaneous velocity at $t = 4.0\,\mathrm{s}$ and the instant at which the body is momentarily at rest.

:::soluzione
$v(t) = -3.0 + 2.0\,t$, hence $v(4.0\,\mathrm{s}) = 5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the body is at rest at $t = 1.5\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
