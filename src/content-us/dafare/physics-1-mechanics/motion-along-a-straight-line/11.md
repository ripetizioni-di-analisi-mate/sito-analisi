titolo: Position from a velocity growing with the square of the time
teoria: [integration-of-motion, integral-recall, area-under-velocity-graph]

A body moves along the $x$ axis with velocity $v(t) = 2.0 + 6.0\,t^2$, in SI units, and at $t = 0$ it is at $x_0 = 1.0\,\mathrm{m}$. Find its position at $t = 3.0\,\mathrm{s}$ and its displacement over that interval.

:::soluzione
$x(t) = 1.0 + 2.0\,t + 2.0\,t^3$, hence $x(3.0\,\mathrm{s}) = 61\,\mathrm{m}$ and $\Delta x = 60\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
