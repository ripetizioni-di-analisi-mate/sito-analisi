A runner covers $600\,\mathrm{m}$ due north along a straight track in $150\,\mathrm{s}$, then turns around and covers $200\,\mathrm{m}$ due south in $50\,\mathrm{s}$. Taking the positive sense toward the north, find the average velocity and the average speed over the whole run.

:::soluzione
$v_{\mathrm{av}} = \dfrac{400\,\mathrm{m}}{200\,\mathrm{s}} = 2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ toward the north; average speed $= \dfrac{800\,\mathrm{m}}{200\,\mathrm{s}} = 4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
