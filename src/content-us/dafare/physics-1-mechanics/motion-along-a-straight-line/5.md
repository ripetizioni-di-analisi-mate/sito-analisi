A body with constant acceleration increases its velocity from $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $16\,\mathrm{m/s}$ while covering $60\,\mathrm{m}$. Find the acceleration and the time employed.

:::soluzione
$a = \dfrac{v^2 - v_0^2}{2\,\Delta x} = 2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, $\ t = \dfrac{v - v_0}{a} = 6.0\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
