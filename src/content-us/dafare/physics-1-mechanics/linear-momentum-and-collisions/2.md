A ball of mass $0.400\,\mathrm{kg}$ strikes a horizontal floor moving downwards at $7.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and leaves it moving upwards at $5.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The contact lasts $20.0\,\mathrm{ms}$. Determine the magnitude of the average normal force exerted by the floor during the contact, taking the weight of the ball into account.

:::soluzione
$N_{\mathrm{av}} = \dfrac{m\,(v_1 + v_2)}{\Delta t} + mg = 244\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
