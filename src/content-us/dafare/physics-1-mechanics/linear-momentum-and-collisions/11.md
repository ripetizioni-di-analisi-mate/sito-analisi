A body of mass $3.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ moves at $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ along the positive $x$ axis and a body of mass $5.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ moves at $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ along the negative $x$ axis. Determine the total momentum of the system and the velocity of its center of mass.

:::soluzione
$P_x = +2.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$ and $v_{\mathrm{cm},x} = \dfrac{P_x}{M} = +0.25\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
