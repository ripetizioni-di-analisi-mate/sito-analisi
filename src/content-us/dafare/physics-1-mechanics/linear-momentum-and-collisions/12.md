---
titolo: Bullet embedded in a block that slides with friction
teoria: [conservation-of-momentum, perfectly-inelastic-1d]
parole: ["bullet and block", "kinetic friction", "two-stage problem", "stopping distance"]
---

A bullet of mass $10.0\,\mathrm{g}$ moving horizontally at $420\,\mathrm{m/s}$ embeds itself in a block of mass $2.000\,\mathrm{kg}$ resting on a horizontal surface with coefficient of kinetic friction $0.300$. Determine the distance traveled by the block before it stops.

:::soluzione
$d = \dfrac{V^2}{2\mu_k g} = 0.742\,\mathrm{m}$, with $V = \dfrac{mv}{m + M} = 2.09\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
