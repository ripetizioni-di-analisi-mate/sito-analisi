---
titolo: Head-on elastic collision between two moving bodies
teoria: [elastic-1d, relative-velocity-reversal]
parole: ["head-on elastic collision", "relative velocity", "signs of the velocities"]

Two bodies move toward each other along a horizontal frictionless line. The first has mass $1.20\,\mathrm{kg}$ and velocity $+4.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$, the second has mass $0.800\,\mathrm{kg}$ and velocity $-2.50\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The collision is elastic. Determine the velocity of each body after the collision.

:::soluzione
$v_1' = -1.20\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_2' = +5.30\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
