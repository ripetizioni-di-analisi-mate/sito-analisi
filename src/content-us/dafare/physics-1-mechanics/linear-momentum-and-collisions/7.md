A body of mass $1.50\,\mathrm{kg}$ at rest on a horizontal frictionless plane explodes into two fragments that move along the same line. The first has mass $0.600\,\mathrm{kg}$ and speed $5.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Determine the speed of the second fragment and the energy released by the explosion.

:::soluzione
$v_2 = 3.33\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the opposite sense, and $E = 12.5\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
