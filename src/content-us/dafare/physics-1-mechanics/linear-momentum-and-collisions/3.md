A body of mass $0.900\,\mathrm{kg}$ slides at $2.40\,\mathrm{m/s}$ on a horizontal frictionless track and sticks to a body of mass $1.600\,\mathrm{kg}$ at rest. Determine the velocity of the pair after the collision and the kinetic energy lost.

:::soluzione
$V = \dfrac{m_1v_1}{m_1 + m_2} = 0.864\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $|\Delta K| = 1.66\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
