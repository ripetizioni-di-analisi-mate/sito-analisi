titolo: Center of mass of three particles

Three particles lie in the $xy$ plane: $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ at $(0,\ 0)\,\mathrm{m}$, $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ at $(3.0,\ 0)\,\mathrm{m}$ and $3.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ at $(0,\ 4.0)\,\mathrm{m}$. Determine the coordinates of the center of mass.

:::soluzione
$x_{\mathrm{cm}} = 1.0\,\mathrm{m}$ and $y_{\mathrm{cm}} = 2.0\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
