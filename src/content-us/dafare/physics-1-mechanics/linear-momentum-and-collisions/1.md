titolo: Impulse and average force on a struck ball

A ball of mass $0.060\,\mathrm{kg}$ reaches a racket horizontally at $25.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and leaves it along the same line, in the opposite sense, at $32.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The contact lasts $5.00\,\mathrm{ms}$. Determine the magnitude of the impulse delivered to the ball and the magnitude of the average force exerted by the racket.

:::soluzione
$J = m\,(v_1 + v_2) = 3.42\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$ and $F_{\mathrm{av}} = \dfrac{J}{\Delta t} = 684\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
