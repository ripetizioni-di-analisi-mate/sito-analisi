titolo: Elastic collision between equal masses in a plane
teoria: [equal-mass-elastic-2d]

A puck slides at $5.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$ on a horizontal frictionless surface and collides elastically with an identical puck at rest. After the collision the first puck moves at $25.0^\circ$ from its original direction. Determine the speed of each puck after the collision.

:::soluzione
$v_1' = v\cos 25.0^\circ = 4.53\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_2' = v\sin 25.0^\circ = 2.11\,\mathrm{m/s}$, the two directions forming an angle of $90^\circ$.
:::
