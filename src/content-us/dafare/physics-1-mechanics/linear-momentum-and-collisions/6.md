---
titolo: Recoil of a person on ice
teoria: [conservation-of-momentum, recoil]
parole: ["recoil", "isolated system", "conservation of momentum"]
---

A person of mass $55\,\mathrm{kg}$ stands at rest on ice of negligible friction and throws a stone of mass $3.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ horizontally at $10.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ with respect to the ice. Determine the speed of the person after the throw.

:::soluzione
$V = \dfrac{m}{M}\,v = 0.55\,\mathrm{m/s}$, in the sense opposite to the stone.
:::
