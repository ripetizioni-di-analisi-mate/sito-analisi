A sphere of mass $0.250\,\mathrm{kg}$ moving at $3.60\,\mathrm{m/s}$ collides head-on and elastically with a sphere of mass $0.750\,\mathrm{kg}$ at rest on a horizontal frictionless track. Determine the velocity of each sphere after the collision.

:::soluzione
$v_1' = -1.80\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_2' = +1.80\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
