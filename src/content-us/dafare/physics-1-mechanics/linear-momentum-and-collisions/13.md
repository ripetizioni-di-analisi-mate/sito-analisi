A rocket moves far from every other body and expels gas with speed $2.50\,\mathrm{km/s}$ relative to itself. It burns fuel until its mass is reduced to one third of the initial value. Determine the increase in its speed.

:::soluzione
$\Delta v = u\ln\dfrac{m_i}{m_f} = 2.50\,\mathrm{km/s} \cdot \ln 3 = 2.75\,\mathrm{km/s}$.
:::
