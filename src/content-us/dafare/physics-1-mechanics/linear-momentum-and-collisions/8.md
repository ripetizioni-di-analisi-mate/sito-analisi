A car of mass $1\,400\,\mathrm{kg}$ travelling east at $18.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ collides with a car of mass $1\,000\,\mathrm{kg}$ travelling north at $14.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and the two remain locked together. Determine the magnitude and the direction of the velocity of the wreck immediately after the collision.

:::soluzione
$V = 12.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$, at $29.1^\circ$ north of east.
:::
