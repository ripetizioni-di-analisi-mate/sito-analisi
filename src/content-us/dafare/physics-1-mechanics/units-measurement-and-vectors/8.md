A force of magnitude $240\,\mathrm{N}$ lies in the $xy$ plane and makes the angle $58^\circ$ with the positive $x$ axis, measured counterclockwise. Compute its two components.

:::soluzione
$F_x = 127\,\mathrm{N}$ and $F_y = 204\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
