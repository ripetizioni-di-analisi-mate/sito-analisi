Three bodies have masses $1.2\,\mathrm{kg}$, $0.456\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $12.07\,\mathrm{kg}$. Compute the total mass with the correct number of significant figures, then write $0.0000780\,\mathrm{m}$ in scientific notation and state how many significant figures it carries.

:::soluzione
$m = 13.7\,\mathrm{kg}$; $0.0000780\,\mathrm{m} = 7.80 \times 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{m}$, with three significant figures.
:::
