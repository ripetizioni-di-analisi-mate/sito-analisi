A rectangular floor is measured and the two sides come out as $6.25\,\mathrm{m}$ and $3.4\,\mathrm{m}$. Compute the area with the correct number of significant figures.

:::soluzione
$A = 21\,\mathrm{m^2}$, with two significant figures.
:::
