teoria: [unit-conversion, si-units]

A sprinter covers $100\,\mathrm{m}$ in $9.85\,\mathrm{s}$. Compute the average speed in meters per second and in miles per hour, with $1\,\mathrm{mi} = 1609\,\mathrm{m}$.

:::soluzione
$v = 10.2\,\mathrm{m/s} = 22.7\,\mathrm{mi/h}$.
:::
