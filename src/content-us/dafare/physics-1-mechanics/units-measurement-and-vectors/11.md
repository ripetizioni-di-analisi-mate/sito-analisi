A point moves from the position $\vec{r}_1 = (3.0,\ -2.0,\ 5.0)\,\mathrm{m}$ to the position $\vec{r}_2 = (-1.0,\ 4.0,\ 2.0)\,\mathrm{m}$ in the time interval $4.0\,\mathrm{s}$. Compute the displacement, its magnitude and the magnitude of the average velocity.

:::soluzione
$\Delta\vec{r} = (-4.0,\ 6.0,\ -3.0)\,\mathrm{m}$, $|\Delta\vec{r}| = 7.81\,\mathrm{m}$ and $v_{\mathrm{av}} = 1.95\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
