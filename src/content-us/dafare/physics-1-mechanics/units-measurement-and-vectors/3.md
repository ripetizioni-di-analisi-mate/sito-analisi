A machine does $3.6\,\mathrm{MJ}$ of work in $25\,\mathrm{min}$. Compute the average power in watts and in kilowatts, and express that power using base SI units alone.

:::soluzione
$P = 2.4 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{W} = 2.4\,\mathrm{kW} = 2.4 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s^3}$.
:::
