Aluminium has density $2.70\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. Express the density in $\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and compute the mass of a rectangular sheet measuring $1.20\,\mathrm{m}$ by $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$ with thickness $2.0\,\mathrm{mm}$.

:::soluzione
$\rho = 2.70 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and $m = 5.2\,\mathrm{kg}$.
:::
