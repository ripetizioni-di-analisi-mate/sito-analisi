A particle has velocity $\vec{v} = (-5.0\,\hat{\imath} + 8.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Compute the magnitude of the velocity and the angle it makes with the positive $x$ axis, measured counterclockwise.

:::soluzione
$v = 9.43\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $\theta = 122.0^\circ$.
:::
