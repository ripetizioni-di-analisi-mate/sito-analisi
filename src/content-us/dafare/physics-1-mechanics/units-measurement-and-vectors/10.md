Three forces act on the same point of a body, all in the $xy$ plane: $55\,\mathrm{N}$ at $0^\circ$, $40\,\mathrm{N}$ at $120^\circ$ and $30\,\mathrm{N}$ at $250^\circ$, each angle measured counterclockwise from the positive $x$ axis. Compute the magnitude and the direction of the resultant.

:::soluzione
$R = 25.6\,\mathrm{N}$ at $\theta = 14.6^\circ$ from the positive $x$ axis.
:::
