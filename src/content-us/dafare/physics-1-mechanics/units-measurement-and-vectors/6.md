titolo: Exponents from dimensional analysis
teoria: [dimensions, dimensional-homogeneity]

The speed $v$ of a transverse wave on a stretched string is assumed to have the form $v = C\,F^{\,a}\mu^{\,b}$, where $F$ is the tension of the string, $\mu$ its mass per unit length and $C$ a dimensionless constant. Determine the exponents $a$ and $b$.

:::soluzione
$a = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and $b = -\dfrac{1}{2}$, hence $v = C\sqrt{\dfrac{F}{\mu}}$.
:::
