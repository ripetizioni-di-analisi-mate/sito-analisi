---
titolo: Rejecting a relation with dimensions
teoria: [dimensions, dimensional-homogeneity, dimensional-check]
parole: [dimensional homogeneity, dimensions of acceleration, checking a formula]
---

Among the three relations $x = \dfrac{1}{2}a t^2$, $v = \sqrt{2\,a\,x}$ and $a = \dfrac{v}{t^2}$, where $x$ is a length, $v$ a velocity, $a$ an acceleration and $t$ a time, determine which one is not dimensionally homogeneous and write the dimensions of its right-hand side.

:::soluzione
The third relation, since $\left[\dfrac{v}{t^2}\right] = [L\,T^{-3}]$ while $[a] = [L\,T^{-2}]$.
:::
