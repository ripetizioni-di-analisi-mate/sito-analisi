titolo: Torque of a force about the origin

A force $\vec{F} = (-20\,\hat{\imath} + 40\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{N}$ is applied at the point located by $\vec{r} = (0.35\,\hat{\imath} + 0.25\,\hat{k})\,\mathrm{m}$ with respect to the origin. Compute the torque of the force about the origin and its magnitude.

:::soluzione
$\vec{\tau} = (-10.0\,\hat{\imath} - 5.0\,\hat{\jmath} + 14.0\,\hat{k})\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$ and $\tau = 17.9\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$.
:::
