A ball rolls off a horizontal table $0.90\,\mathrm{m}$ high with speed $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and leaves the edge horizontally. Air resistance is negligible. Determine the time of flight and the horizontal distance from the edge of the table to the landing point.

:::soluzione
$t = \sqrt{\dfrac{2h}{g}} = 0.43\,\mathrm{s}$, $x = 1.07\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
