titolo: Centripetal acceleration of a car on a bend

A car travels along a bend of radius $80\,\mathrm{m}$ at the constant speed $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Determine its centripetal acceleration, its angular velocity and the time it would take to cover a whole circle of that radius.

:::soluzione
$a_{\mathrm{rad}} = 2.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, $\omega = 0.188\,\mathrm{rad/s}$, $T = 33.5\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
