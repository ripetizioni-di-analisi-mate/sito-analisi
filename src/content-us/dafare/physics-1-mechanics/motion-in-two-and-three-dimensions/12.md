A river $90\,\mathrm{m}$ wide flows at $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. A boat whose speed with respect to the water is $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ heads perpendicular to the banks. Determine the time of the crossing, the distance by which the boat is carried downstream and the speed of the boat with respect to the ground.

:::soluzione
$t = 36\,\mathrm{s}$, $\Delta = 54\,\mathrm{m}$, $v = 2.9\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
