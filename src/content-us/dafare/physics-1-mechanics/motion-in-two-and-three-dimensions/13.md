An airplane whose speed with respect to the air is $180\,\mathrm{km/h}$ must travel due east. A wind of $50\,\mathrm{km/h}$ blows from the south toward the north. Determine the direction in which the airplane must be headed and its speed with respect to the ground.

:::soluzione
Heading $16.1^\circ$ south of east; $v = 173\,\mathrm{km/h}$.
:::
