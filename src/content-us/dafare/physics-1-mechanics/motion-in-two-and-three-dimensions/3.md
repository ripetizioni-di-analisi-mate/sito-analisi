A particle starts at the origin at $t = 0$ with velocity $\vec{v}_0 = (2.0\,\hat{\imath} + 5.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and moves with the constant acceleration $\vec{a} = (3.0\,\hat{\imath} - 2.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Determine its velocity and its position at $t = 2.0\,\mathrm{s}$.

:::soluzione
$\vec{v} = (8.0\,\hat{\imath} + 1.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s}$, $\vec{r} = (10\,\hat{\imath} + 6.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
