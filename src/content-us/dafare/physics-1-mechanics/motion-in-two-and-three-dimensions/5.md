An airplane flies horizontally at $45\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at an altitude of $180\,\mathrm{m}$ and releases a package, which leaves the airplane with the velocity of the airplane. Air resistance is negligible. Determine the time of fall and the horizontal distance covered by the package.

:::soluzione
$t = 6.1\,\mathrm{s}$, $x = 273\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
