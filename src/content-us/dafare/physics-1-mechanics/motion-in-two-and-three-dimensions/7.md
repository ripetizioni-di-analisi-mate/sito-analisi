A ball is launched from the ground with speed $16\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and lands $22\,\mathrm{m}$ away on the same level. Air resistance is negligible. Determine the two launch angles that are compatible with these data.

:::soluzione
$\theta_1 = 28.7^\circ$ and $\theta_2 = 61.3^\circ$.
:::
