A car travels on a circular track of radius $40\,\mathrm{m}$ and at a certain instant its speed is $18\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and it is increasing at the rate $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Determine the two components of the acceleration, the magnitude of the total acceleration and the angle that it makes with the radius.

:::soluzione
$a_{\mathrm{rad}} = 8.10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, $a_{\mathrm{tan}} = 3.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, $a = 8.64\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ at $20.3^\circ$ from the radius.
:::
