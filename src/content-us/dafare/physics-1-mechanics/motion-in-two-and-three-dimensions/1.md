A particle moves in the $xy$ plane with position vector
$$
\vec{r}(t) = (2t^2 - 3)\,\hat{\imath} + (t^3 - 4t)\,\hat{\jmath} ,
$$
with $t$ in seconds and the components in meters. Determine the velocity and the acceleration at $t = 2.0\,\mathrm{s}$ and the speed at that instant.

:::soluzione
$\vec{v} = (8.0\,\hat{\imath} + 8.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s}$, $\vec{a} = (4.0\,\hat{\imath} + 12\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, $v = 11.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
