A stone is thrown from the edge of a cliff $40\,\mathrm{m}$ high with speed $18\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at $20^\circ$ above the horizontal. Air resistance is negligible. Determine the time of flight, the horizontal distance from the base of the cliff to the landing point and the impact speed.

:::soluzione
$t = 3.55\,\mathrm{s}$, $x = 60.1\,\mathrm{m}$, $v = 33.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
