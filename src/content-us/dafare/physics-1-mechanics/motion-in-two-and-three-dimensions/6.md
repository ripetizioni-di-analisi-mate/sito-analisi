A projectile is launched from the ground with speed $24\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at $35^\circ$ above the horizontal, over horizontal ground. Air resistance is negligible. Determine the time of flight, the range and the maximum height.

:::soluzione
$T = 2.81\,\mathrm{s}$, $R = 55.2\,\mathrm{m}$, $H = 9.66\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
