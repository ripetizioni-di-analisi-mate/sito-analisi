A wheel of radius $0.22\,\mathrm{m}$ turns at $900$ revolutions per minute. Determine the angular velocity, the speed of a point on the rim and its centripetal acceleration.

:::soluzione
$\omega = 94.2\,\mathrm{rad/s}$, $v = 20.7\,\mathrm{m/s}$, $a_{\mathrm{rad}} = 1.95 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
:::
