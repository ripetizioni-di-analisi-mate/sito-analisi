A particle moves in the $xy$ plane with position vector
$$
\vec{r}(t) = t^2\,\hat{\imath} + (6t - t^2)\,\hat{\jmath} ,
$$
with $t$ in seconds and the components in meters. Determine the average velocity between $t = 1.0\,\mathrm{s}$ and $t = 4.0\,\mathrm{s}$ and its magnitude.

:::soluzione
$\vec{v}_{\mathrm{av}} = (5.0\,\hat{\imath} + 1.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s}$, $|\vec{v}_{\mathrm{av}}| = 5.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
