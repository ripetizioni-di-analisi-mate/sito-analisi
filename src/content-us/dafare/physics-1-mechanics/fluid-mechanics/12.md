---
titolo: Efflux speed from an opening in a tank
teoria: [torricellis-theorem, bernoullis-equation]
parole: [Torricelli theorem, efflux speed, open tank, small opening]
---

A large open tank contains water. A small opening is made in its vertical wall at the depth of $3.6\,\mathrm{m}$ below the free surface, and the opening is in contact with the atmosphere. Compute the speed at which the water leaves the opening.

:::soluzione
$v = \sqrt{2gh} = 8.40\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
