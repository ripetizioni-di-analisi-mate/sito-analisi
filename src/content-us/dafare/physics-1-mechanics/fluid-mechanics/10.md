titolo: Largest load carried by a floating block
teoria: [floating-condition, archimedes-principle]

---

A cubical block of edge $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$ and density $4.0 \times 10^{2}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ floats in fresh water. Compute the largest mass that can be placed on it before its upper face reaches the level of the water.

:::soluzione
$m_c = (\rho_f - \rho_b)\,L^3 = 75\,\mathrm{kg}$.
:::
