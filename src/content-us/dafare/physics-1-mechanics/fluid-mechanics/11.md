Water flows steadily at $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in a pipe of inner diameter $6.0\,\mathrm{cm}$, which narrows to the diameter $2.0\,\mathrm{cm}$. Compute the volume flow rate and the speed in the narrow section.

:::soluzione
$Q = A_1 v_1 = 4.2 \times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ and $v_2 = \left(\dfrac{d_1}{d_2}\right)^{2} v_1 = 13.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
