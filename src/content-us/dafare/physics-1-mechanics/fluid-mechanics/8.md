A body weighs $4.90\,\mathrm{N}$ in air and $3.43\,\mathrm{N}$ when completely submerged in water, $\rho_f = 1.00 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. The buoyancy of the air is negligible. Compute the density of the body.

:::soluzione
$\rho_b = \rho_f\,\dfrac{W_1}{W_1 - W_2} = 3.33 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.
:::
