---
titolo: Gauge pressure read on a mercury manometer
teoria: [manometer, pressure-depth, gauge-pressure]
parole: [open-tube manometer, mercury, gauge pressure, level difference]
---

An open-tube mercury manometer is connected to a vessel containing a gas. The mercury in the open arm stands $18.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ higher than in the arm connected to the vessel. Taking $\rho_{\mathrm{Hg}} = 1.36 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$, compute the gauge pressure of the gas.

:::soluzione
$p_{\mathrm{gauge}} = \rho_{\mathrm{Hg}}\,g\,h = 2.47 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
:::
