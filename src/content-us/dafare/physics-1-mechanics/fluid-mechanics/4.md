A vertical dam wall of width $12\,\mathrm{m}$ holds back fresh water of depth $5.0\,\mathrm{m}$, with the free surface level with the top of the wall and the atmosphere acting on the other face. Compute the resultant force of the water on the wall and the depth at which it is applied.

:::soluzione
$F = \dfrac{1}{2}\rho g w H^2 = 1.47 \times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{N}$, applied at the depth $\dfrac{2}{3}H = 3.3\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
