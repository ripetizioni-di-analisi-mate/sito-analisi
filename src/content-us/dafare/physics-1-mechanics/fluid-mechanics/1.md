titolo: Absolute pressure at a given depth
teoria: [pressure-depth, gauge-pressure]
parole: [hydrostatic pressure, absolute pressure, depth, fresh water]
---

A diving bell is lowered to the depth of $18\,\mathrm{m}$ in a fresh-water lake, where $\rho = 1.00 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and the pressure at the surface is $p_{\mathrm{atm}} = 1.013 \times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Compute the absolute pressure of the water at that depth.

:::soluzione
$p = p_{\mathrm{atm}} + \rho g h = 2.78 \times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
:::
