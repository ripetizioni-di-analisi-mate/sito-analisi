A Venturi meter in a horizontal pipe carrying water has a wide section of area $30\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and a throat of area $7.5\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. The pressure in the wide section exceeds the pressure in the throat by $8.0 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Compute the speed in the wide section and the volume flow rate.

:::soluzione
$v_1 = A_2\sqrt{\dfrac{2\,(p_1 - p_2)}{\rho\,(A_1^2 - A_2^2)}} = 1.03\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $Q = A_1 v_1 = 3.1 \times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$.
:::
