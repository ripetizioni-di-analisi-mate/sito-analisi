---
titolo: Apparent weight of a submerged block
teoria: [archimedes-principle, density]
parole: [apparent weight, buoyant force, spring scale, granite block]
---

A granite block of mass $15\,\mathrm{kg}$ and density $2.75 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ hangs from a spring scale and is completely submerged in water. Compute the reading of the scale.

:::soluzione
$T = m g\left(1 - \dfrac{\rho_f}{\rho_b}\right) = 93.6\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
