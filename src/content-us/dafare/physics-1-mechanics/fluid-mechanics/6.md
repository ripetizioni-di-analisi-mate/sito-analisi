---
titolo: Buoyant force on a fully submerged body
teoria: [archimedes-principle]
parole: [buoyant force, Archimedes principle, displaced volume, fresh water]
---

A body of volume $0.25\,\mathrm{m^3}$ is held completely submerged in fresh water, $\rho_f = 1.00 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. Compute the magnitude of the buoyant force acting on it.

:::soluzione
$F_B = \rho_f\,g\,V = 2.45 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
