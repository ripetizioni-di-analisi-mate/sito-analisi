A hydraulic press has two pistons at the same height, of diameters $2.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $25\,\mathrm{cm}$. A force of $150\,\mathrm{N}$ is applied to the small piston. Compute the force exerted by the fluid on the large piston.

:::soluzione
$F_2 = \left(\dfrac{d_2}{d_1}\right)^{2} F_1 = 1.5 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
