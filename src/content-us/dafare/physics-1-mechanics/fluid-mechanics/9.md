---
titolo: Submerged fraction of a floating body
teoria: [floating-condition, archimedes-principle]
parole: [floating body, submerged fraction, density ratio, emerged volume]
---

A block of uniform density $6.8 \times 10^{2}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ floats in fresh water. Compute the fraction of its volume that stands above the free surface.

:::soluzione
$\dfrac{V_{\mathrm{em}}}{V} = 1 - \dfrac{\rho_b}{\rho_f} = 0.32$, that is $32\%$.
:::
