A circular porthole of diameter $30\,\mathrm{cm}$ is set in the hull of a submarine at the depth of $6.0\,\mathrm{m}$ in sea water, $\rho = 1.025 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. The air inside the submarine is at atmospheric pressure. Compute the magnitude of the net force exerted by the water on the porthole.

:::soluzione
$F = \rho g h\,\dfrac{\pi d^2}{4} = 4.26 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
