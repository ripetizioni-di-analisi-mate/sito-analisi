---
titolo: How the force changes with mass and distance
teoria: [universal-gravitation]
parole: [inverse-square law, proportionality, gravitational force]
---

Two particles separated by the distance $d$ attract each other with the force of magnitude $F_0$. One of the two masses is then tripled and the distance is doubled. Express the new force in terms of $F_0$.

:::soluzione
$F = \dfrac{3}{4}\,F_0$.
:::
