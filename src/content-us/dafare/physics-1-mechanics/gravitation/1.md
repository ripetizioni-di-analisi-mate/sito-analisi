Two uniform spheres of masses $m_1 = 250\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $m_2 = 180\,\mathrm{kg}$ have their centers $d = 1.50\,\mathrm{m}$ apart. Compute the magnitude of the gravitational force between them.

:::soluzione
$F = G\,\dfrac{m_1m_2}{d^2} = 1.33 \times 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
