---
titolo: Field at depth in a uniform planet
teoria: [inside-a-uniform-sphere, shell-theorem]
parole: [field inside a sphere, depth, uniform density]
---

Model the Earth as a uniform sphere of radius $R_E = 6.37 \times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{m}$ with surface gravity $g_0 = 9.82\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Compute the gravitational field at the depth $p = 2.00 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{km}$ below the surface.

:::soluzione
$g = g_0\,\dfrac{R_E - p}{R_E} = 6.74\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
:::
