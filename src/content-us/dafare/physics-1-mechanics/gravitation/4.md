Two bodies of masses $M$ and $9M$ have their centers $d = 1.20\,\mathrm{m}$ apart. Find the distance from the center of the smaller body at which the resultant gravitational field vanishes.

:::soluzione
$x = \dfrac{d}{4} = 0.30\,\mathrm{m}$ from the center of the body of mass $M$.
:::
