---
titolo: Mass of a planet from its surface gravity
teoria: [surface-gravity, shell-theorem]
parole: [surface gravity, planet mass, shell theorem]
---

A planet has radius $R = 2.44 \times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{m}$ and surface gravity $g_0 = 3.70\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Compute its mass, with $G = 6.674 \times 10^{-11}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m^2/kg^2}$.

:::soluzione
$M = \dfrac{g_0R^2}{G} = 3.30 \times 10^{23}\,\mathrm{kg}$.
:::
