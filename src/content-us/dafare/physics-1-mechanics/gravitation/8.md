A satellite travels a circular orbit at the altitude $h = 2.00 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{km}$ above the surface of the Earth. Compute its speed and its period, with $GM_E = 3.984 \times 10^{14}\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$ and $R_E = 6.37 \times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{m}$.

:::soluzione
$v = \sqrt{\dfrac{GM_E}{r}} = 6.90 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $T = \dfrac{2\pi r}{v} = 7.62 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{s} = 2.12\,\mathrm{h}$, with $r = 8.37 \times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
