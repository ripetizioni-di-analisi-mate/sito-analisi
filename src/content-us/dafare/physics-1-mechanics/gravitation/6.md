---
titolo: Altitude at which the field is reduced
teoria: [g-with-altitude, surface-gravity]
parole: [variation of g, altitude, inverse-square law]
---

Compute the altitude above the surface of the Earth at which the gravitational field is one third of its surface value. Take $R_E = 6.37 \times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{m}$.

:::soluzione
$h = R_E\left(\sqrt{3} - 1\right) = 4.66 \times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
