titolo: Orbit radius from the period
teoria: [circular-orbit, keplers-third-law]

A satellite of the Earth has the period $T = 100\,\mathrm{min}$. Compute the radius of its circular orbit and the corresponding altitude, with $GM_E = 3.984 \times 10^{14}\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$ and $R_E = 6.37 \times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{m}$.

:::soluzione
$r = \left(\dfrac{GM_E\,T^2}{4\pi^2}\right)^{1/3} = 7.14 \times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{m}$, hence $h = 7.66 \times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
