---
titolo: Point of zero force between two masses
teoria: [superposition, gravitational-field]
parole: [superposition, zero net force, gravitational field, collinear masses]
---

Two spheres of masses $M_1 = 40\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $M_2 = 90\,\mathrm{kg}$ have their centers $L = 1.00\,\mathrm{m}$ apart. Find the position, measured from the center of $M_1$, of the point of the segment joining them at which a third body feels no resultant gravitational force.

:::soluzione
$x = \dfrac{L}{1 + \sqrt{\dfrac{M_2}{M_1}}} = 0.40\,\mathrm{m}$ from the center of $M_1$.
:::
