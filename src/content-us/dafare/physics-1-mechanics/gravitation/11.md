A body is fired vertically upward from the surface of the Earth with the speed $v_0 = 8.00\,\mathrm{km/s}$. Compute the maximum distance it reaches from the center of the Earth, with $GM_E = 3.984 \times 10^{14}\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$ and $R_E = 6.37 \times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{m}$, and neglecting air resistance.

:::soluzione
$r_{\max} = \dfrac{GM_E}{\dfrac{GM_E}{R_E} - \dfrac{1}{2}v_0^2} = 1.30 \times 10^{7}\,\mathrm{m}$, that is an altitude of $6.67 \times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
