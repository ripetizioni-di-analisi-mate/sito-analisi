A satellite of mass $m = 900\,\mathrm{kg}$ is moved from the circular orbit of radius $r_1 = 7.50 \times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{m}$ to the circular orbit of radius $r_2 = 1.00 \times 10^{7}\,\mathrm{m}$ around the Earth. Compute the energy that must be supplied, with $GM_E = 3.984 \times 10^{14}\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$.

:::soluzione
$W = \dfrac{GM_E m}{2}\left(\dfrac{1}{r_1} - \dfrac{1}{r_2}\right) = 5.98 \times 10^{9}\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
