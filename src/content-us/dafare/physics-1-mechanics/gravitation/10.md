Venus has mass $M = 4.87 \times 10^{24}\,\mathrm{kg}$ and radius $R = 6.05 \times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{m}$. Compute the escape speed from its surface, with $G = 6.674 \times 10^{-11}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m^2/kg^2}$ and neglecting the atmosphere.

:::soluzione
$v_f = \sqrt{\dfrac{2GM}{R}} = 1.04 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
