---
titolo: Mass of a planet from the orbit of its moon
teoria: [keplers-third-law, circular-orbit]
parole: [Kepler's third law, planet mass, moon, orbital period]
---

A moon travels a circular orbit of radius $r = 9.38 \times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{m}$ around a planet, with period $T = 7.65\,\mathrm{h}$. Compute the mass of the planet, with $G = 6.674 \times 10^{-11}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m^2/kg^2}$.

:::soluzione
$M = \dfrac{4\pi^2 r^3}{G\,T^2} = 6.44 \times 10^{23}\,\mathrm{kg}$.
:::
