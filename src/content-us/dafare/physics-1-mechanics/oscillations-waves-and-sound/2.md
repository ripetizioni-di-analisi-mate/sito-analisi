---
titolo: Frequency of a mass-spring oscillator
teoria: [spring-mass-frequency]
parole: [spring constant, frequency, mass-spring oscillator]
---

A block of mass $m = 0.35\,\mathrm{kg}$ oscillates on a frictionless horizontal surface, attached to a spring of constant $k = 140\,\mathrm{N/m}$. Determine the frequency of the oscillation.

:::soluzione
$f = \dfrac{1}{2\pi}\sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m}} = 3.2\,\mathrm{Hz}$.
:::
