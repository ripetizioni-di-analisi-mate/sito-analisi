A particle moves along the $x$ axis with $x(t) = 0.080\cos\!\left(12\,t + \dfrac{\pi}{4}\right)\,\mathrm{m}$, where $t$ is in seconds. Determine the period of the motion and the maximum modulus of the acceleration.

:::soluzione
$T = \dfrac{2\pi}{\omega} = 0.52\,\mathrm{s}$ and $a_{\max} = \omega^2 A = 11.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
:::
