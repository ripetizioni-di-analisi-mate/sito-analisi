A body of mass $m = 0.50\,\mathrm{kg}$ oscillates on a spring of constant $k = 320\,\mathrm{N/m}$ with amplitude $A = 5.0\,\mathrm{cm}$. Determine the mechanical energy of the oscillation and the speed of the body at $x = 2.0\,\mathrm{cm}$.

:::soluzione
$E = \dfrac{1}{2}kA^2 = 0.40\,\mathrm{J}$ and $|v| = \omega\sqrt{A^2 - x^2} = 1.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
