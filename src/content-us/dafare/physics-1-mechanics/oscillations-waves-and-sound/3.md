---
titolo: Period of a vertical spring from its static elongation
teoria: [hookes-law, vertical-spring]
parole: [vertical spring, static elongation, spring constant, period]
---

A body of mass $m = 0.45\,\mathrm{kg}$ hangs at rest from a vertical spring and stretches it by $3.2\,\mathrm{cm}$. Determine the spring constant and the period of the vertical oscillations about the equilibrium position.

:::soluzione
$k = \dfrac{mg}{x_{\mathrm{eq}}} = 1.4 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{N/m}$ and $T = 2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{x_{\mathrm{eq}}}{g}} = 0.36\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
