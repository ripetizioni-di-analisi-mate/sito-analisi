---
titolo: Frequency of a harmonic of a string fixed at both ends
teoria: [normal-modes, standing-waves]
parole: [standing wave, harmonics, fundamental frequency, string fixed at both ends]
---

A string of length $L = 1.50\,\mathrm{m}$ is fixed at both ends, and transverse waves travel along it with speed $v = 180\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Determine the frequency of the fourth harmonic.

:::soluzione
$f_4 = 4\,\dfrac{v}{2L} = 240\,\mathrm{Hz}$.
:::
