titolo: Period of a rod pivoted at one end

A uniform rod of length $L = 0.90\,\mathrm{m}$ swings with small amplitude about a horizontal axis through one of its ends, with negligible friction. Determine its period.

:::soluzione
$T = 2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{2L}{3g}} = 1.55\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
