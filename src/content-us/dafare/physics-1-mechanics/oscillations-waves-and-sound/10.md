---
titolo: Speed of a wave on a stretched string
teoria: [wave-speed-string]
parole: [linear mass density, tension, wave speed on a string]
---

A string of linear mass density $\mu = 8.0\,\mathrm{g/m}$ is kept under a tension of $125\,\mathrm{N}$. Determine the speed of a transverse wave on it.

:::soluzione
$v = \sqrt{\dfrac{F_T}{\mu}} = 125\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
