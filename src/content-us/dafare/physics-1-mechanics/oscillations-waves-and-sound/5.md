---
titolo: Length of a pendulum of given period
teoria: [simple-pendulum]
parole: [simple pendulum, period, pendulum length]
---

A clock is regulated by a simple pendulum of period $T = 2.00\,\mathrm{s}$, swinging with small amplitude where $g = 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Determine the length of the pendulum.

:::soluzione
$L = \dfrac{g\,T^2}{4\pi^2} = 0.994\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
