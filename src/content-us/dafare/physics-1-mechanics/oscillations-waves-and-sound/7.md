titolo: Decay time of a damped oscillation
teoria: [damped-oscillations, damping-regimes]

A body of mass $m = 0.40\,\mathrm{kg}$ oscillates on a spring of constant $k = 160\,\mathrm{N/m}$ and is subject to the resistive force $-b\dot{x}$ with $b = 0.50\,\mathrm{kg/s}$. Determine the time in which the amplitude falls to $\dfrac{1}{e}$ of its initial value, and the damped angular frequency.

:::soluzione
$\tau = \dfrac{2m}{b} = 1.6\,\mathrm{s}$ and $\omega' = \sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m} - \dfrac{b^2}{4m^2}} = 19.99\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
:::
