A transverse wave on a string is described by $y(x,t) = 0.015\sin(8.0\,x - 24\,t)$, with $x$ and $y$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. Determine the wavelength, the frequency and the speed of the wave.

:::soluzione
$\lambda = \dfrac{2\pi}{k} = 0.79\,\mathrm{m}$, $f = \dfrac{\omega}{2\pi} = 3.8\,\mathrm{Hz}$ and $v = \dfrac{\omega}{k} = 3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
