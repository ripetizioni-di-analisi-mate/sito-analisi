---
titolo: Frequency received from a receding source
teoria: [doppler-effect]
parole: [Doppler effect, receding source, received frequency]
---

An ambulance whose siren emits the frequency $f = 850\,\mathrm{Hz}$ moves away from a stationary observer at $25\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and the speed of sound in the air is $343\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Determine the frequency received by the observer.

:::soluzione
$f' = f\,\dfrac{v}{v + v_s} = 792\,\mathrm{Hz}$.
:::
