---
titolo: Intensity level in decibels
teoria: [sound-intensity]
parole: [sound intensity, decibel, intensity level]

At a point of a room the intensity of a sound is $I = 4.0 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$. Determine the intensity level, taking $I_0 = 10^{-12}\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$ as the reference intensity.

:::soluzione
$\beta = 10\,\log_{10}\dfrac{I}{I_0} = 76\,\mathrm{dB}$.
:::
