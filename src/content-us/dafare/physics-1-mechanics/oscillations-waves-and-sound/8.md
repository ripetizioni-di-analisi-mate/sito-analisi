---
titolo: Amplitude of a driven oscillator at resonance
teoria: [driven-amplitude, resonance]
parole: [driven oscillator, resonance, steady amplitude, damping constant]
---

A body of mass $m = 1.2\,\mathrm{kg}$ on a spring of constant $k = 300\,\mathrm{N/m}$ is subject to the resistive force $-b\dot{x}$ with $b = 3.0\,\mathrm{kg/s}$ and is driven by the force $F = F_0\cos\omega_d t$ with $F_0 = 12\,\mathrm{N}$. Determine the steady amplitude when the driving angular frequency equals the natural one.

:::soluzione
$A = \dfrac{F_0}{b\,\omega_0}$ with $\omega_0 = \sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m}} = 15.8\,\mathrm{rad/s}$, hence $A = 0.25\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
