---
titolo: Tension between two blocks pulled on a table
teoria: [connected-blocks, inextensible-string-constraint, system-as-a-single-body]
parole: [connected blocks, tension, frictionless table, constant force]
---

Two blocks of masses $3.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $5.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ lie on a frictionless horizontal table, joined by an ideal string. A horizontal force of $20\,\mathrm{N}$ is applied to the block of $3.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ along the line of the string. Find the tension of the string.

:::soluzione
$T = \dfrac{m_2}{m_1 + m_2}\,F = 12.5\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
