titolo: Two blocks hanging in series and lifted
teoria: [tension, ideal-string, system-as-a-single-body, newtons-third-law]
parole: [hanging blocks, tension, upward acceleration, string in series]
---

Two blocks hang one below the other from the ceiling of a lift: the upper string joins the ceiling to a block of $4.0\,\mathrm{kg}$, and the lower string joins that block to one of $6.0\,\mathrm{kg}$. The whole system is raised with an upward acceleration of $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the tension of the upper string.

:::soluzione
$T = (m_1 + m_2)(g + a) = 113\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
