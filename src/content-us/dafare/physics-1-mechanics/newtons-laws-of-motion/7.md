An elevator cabin with its load has a total mass of $950\,\mathrm{kg}$ and hangs from a single cable. The cabin accelerates upward with $1.1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the tension of the cable.

:::soluzione
$T = m\,(g + a) = 1.04 \cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
