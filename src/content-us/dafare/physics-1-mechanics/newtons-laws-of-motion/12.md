titolo: Block on a table pulled by a hanging body
teoria: [ideal-pulley, inextensible-string-constraint, system-as-a-single-body, tension]

---

A block of mass $8.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ lies on a frictionless horizontal table. An ideal string attached to it runs to an ideal pulley at the edge of the table and then hangs vertically, carrying a body of mass $3.0\,\mathrm{kg}$. The system is released from rest. Find the acceleration.

:::soluzione
$a = \dfrac{m_2}{m_1 + m_2}\,g = 2.68\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
:::
