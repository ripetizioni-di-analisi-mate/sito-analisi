Two bodies of masses $7.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $5.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ hang from the two ends of an ideal string that passes over a fixed ideal pulley, and the system is released from rest. Find the tension of the string.

:::soluzione
$T = \dfrac{2\,m_1m_2}{m_1 + m_2}\,g = 57.2\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
