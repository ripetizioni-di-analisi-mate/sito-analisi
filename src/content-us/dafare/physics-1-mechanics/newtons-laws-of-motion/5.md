A sign of mass $20\,\mathrm{kg}$ hangs at rest from two cables that leave the same point and make equal angles of $40^\circ$ with the horizontal, one on each side. Find the tension of each cable.

:::soluzione
$T = \dfrac{m g}{2\sin\theta} = 153\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
