---
titolo: Scale reading in a descending elevator
teoria: [apparent-weight, weight, normal-force, newtons-third-law]
parole: [apparent weight, scale reading, elevator, normal force]
---

A person of mass $58\,\mathrm{kg}$ stands on a scale inside an elevator that accelerates downward with $1.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the reading of the scale, expressed as a force.

:::soluzione
$N = m\,(g - a) = 465\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
