A hockey puck of mass $0.17\,\mathrm{kg}$ slides on frictionless ice at $14\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and is brought to rest in a distance of $3.0\,\mathrm{m}$ by a constant horizontal force opposite to its velocity. Find the magnitude of that force.

:::soluzione
$F = \dfrac{m\,v_0^2}{2\,d} = 5.6\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
