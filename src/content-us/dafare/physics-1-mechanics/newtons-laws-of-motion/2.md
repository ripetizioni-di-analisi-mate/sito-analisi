A hockey puck of mass $0.17\,\mathrm{kg}$ slides at $14\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and is brought to rest in $0.80\,\mathrm{s}$ by a constant horizontal force. Find the magnitude of that force.

:::soluzione
$F = m\,\dfrac{v_0}{\Delta t} = 2.98\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
