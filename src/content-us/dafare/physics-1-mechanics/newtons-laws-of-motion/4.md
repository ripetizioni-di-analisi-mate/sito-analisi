---
titolo: Acceleration produced by two perpendicular forces
teoria: [force, second-law-in-components, newtons-second-law]
parole: [resultant of two forces, components, direction of the acceleration]
---

Two horizontal forces act on a body of mass $5.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ lying on a frictionless surface: one of $12\,\mathrm{N}$ along the positive $x$ axis and one of $16\,\mathrm{N}$ along the positive $y$ axis. Find the magnitude of the acceleration and the angle it makes with the $x$ axis.

:::soluzione
$a = \dfrac{\sqrt{F_1^2 + F_2^2}}{m} = 4.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, at $53.1^\circ$ from the $x$ axis.
:::
