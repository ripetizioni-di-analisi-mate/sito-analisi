A block of mass $9.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ lies on a frictionless horizontal floor and is pulled by a rope with a force of $50\,\mathrm{N}$ directed at $35^\circ$ above the horizontal. Find the acceleration of the block and the normal force exerted by the floor.

:::soluzione
$a = \dfrac{F\cos\theta}{m} = 4.55\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, $N = m g - F\sin\theta = 59.6\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
