A block of mass $7.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ is held at rest on a frictionless incline of $35^\circ$ by a string parallel to the plane. Find the tension of the string and the normal force exerted by the plane.

:::soluzione
$T = m g\sin\theta = 39.4\,\mathrm{N}$, $N = m g\cos\theta = 56.3\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
