teoria: [newtons-second-law, acceleration-as-a-derivative]

A body of mass $12\,\mathrm{kg}$ starts from rest on a frictionless horizontal plane and reaches the speed of $9.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in $4.0\,\mathrm{s}$. The net force on the body is constant. Find its magnitude.

:::soluzione
$F_{\text{net}} = m\,\dfrac{\Delta v}{\Delta t} = 27\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
