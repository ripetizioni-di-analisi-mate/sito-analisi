A car of mass $1100\,\mathrm{kg}$ travels at a constant $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$ along a straight road whose perpendicular distance from a point $O$ at the roadside is $35\,\mathrm{m}$. Compute the magnitude of the angular momentum of the car about $O$ and state whether it changes with time.

:::soluzione
$\ell = m\,v\,b = 7.7 \times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$, constant, since the resultant force on the car is zero.
:::
