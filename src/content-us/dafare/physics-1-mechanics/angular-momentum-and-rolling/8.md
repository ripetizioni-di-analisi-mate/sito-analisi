A truck of mass $8.0 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg}$ travels at a constant $25\,\mathrm{m/s}$ along a straight road whose perpendicular distance from a point $O$ at the roadside is $40\,\mathrm{m}$. Compute the magnitude of the angular momentum of the truck about $O$ and state whether it changes with time.

:::soluzione
$\ell = m\,v\,b = 8.0 \times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$, constant, since the resultant force on the truck is zero.
:::
