A thin spherical shell, whose moment of inertia about a diameter is $I_{\mathrm{cm}} = \dfrac{2}{3}MR^2$, rolls without slipping down a plane inclined at $\theta = 18^\circ$. Compute the acceleration of its centre.

:::soluzione
$a_{\mathrm{cm}} = \dfrac{3}{5}\,g\sin\theta = 1.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
:::
