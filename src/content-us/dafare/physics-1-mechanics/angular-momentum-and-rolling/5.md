A homogeneous solid cylinder is released on a plane inclined at $\theta = 30^\circ$ and rolls without slipping. Compute the smallest coefficient of static friction between the cylinder and the plane for which this motion is possible.

:::soluzione
$\mu_s \ge \dfrac{1}{3}\tan\theta = 0.19$.
:::
