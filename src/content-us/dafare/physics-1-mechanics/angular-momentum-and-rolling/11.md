---
titolo: Two coaxial disks brought to a common angular speed
teoria: [rotational-collisions, conservation-of-angular-momentum]
parole: [conservation of angular momentum, coupled disks, kinetic energy lost, inelastic collision]
---

A disk of moment of inertia $0.25\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ turns at $60\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ about a vertical frictionless axis. A second disk, of moment of inertia $0.40\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ and at rest, is lowered onto it along the same axis, and friction brings the two to a common angular speed. Compute that angular speed and the kinetic energy lost.

:::soluzione
$\omega = 23\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ and $|\Delta K| = 2.8 \times 10^{2}\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
