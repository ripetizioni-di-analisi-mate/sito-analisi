A particle of mass $m = 0.60\,\mathrm{kg}$ passes through the point $\vec{r} = (3.0\,\hat{\imath} + 1.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m}$ with velocity $\vec{v} = (-2.0\,\hat{\imath} + 5.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Compute its angular momentum about the origin.

:::soluzione
$\vec{\ell} = m\,(x\,v_y - y\,v_x)\,\hat{k} = 10.2\,\hat{k}\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$.
:::
