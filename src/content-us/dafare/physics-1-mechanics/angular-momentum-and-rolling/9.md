A hoop of mass $M = 2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ and radius $R = 0.40\,\mathrm{m}$ rolls without slipping on a horizontal floor at $v_{\mathrm{cm}} = 3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Compute its angular momentum about the contact line.

:::soluzione
$L = \left(I_{\mathrm{cm}} + MR^2\right)\omega = 2\,M\,R\,v_{\mathrm{cm}} = 4.8\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$.
:::
