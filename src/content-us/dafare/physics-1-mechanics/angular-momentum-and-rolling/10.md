titolo: Clay falling on a turntable
teoria: [conservation-of-angular-momentum, rotational-collisions, angular-momentum-of-a-rigid-body]

---

A turntable of moment of inertia $12\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ turns at $2.5\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ about a vertical frictionless axis. A lump of clay of mass $1.5\,\mathrm{kg}$ falls vertically and sticks to the turntable at a distance of $1.4\,\mathrm{m}$ from the axis. Compute the new angular speed.

:::soluzione
$\omega = \dfrac{I_0\,\omega_0}{I_0 + m r^2} = 2.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
:::
