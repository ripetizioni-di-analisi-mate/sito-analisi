titolo: Speed of a rim point level with the axle

A wheel of radius $R = 0.25\,\mathrm{m}$ rolls without slipping with centre speed $v_{\mathrm{cm}} = 4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Compute the speed of the point of the rim that is level with the axle and ahead of it, and the angle its velocity makes with the horizontal.

:::soluzione
$v_P = \sqrt{2}\,v_{\mathrm{cm}} = 5.7\,\mathrm{m/s}$, directed at $45^\circ$ below the horizontal.
:::
