titolo: Speed of a sphere at the bottom of a slope

A homogeneous solid sphere is released from rest and rolls without slipping down a slope, descending a height $h = 2.0\,\mathrm{m}$. Compute the speed of its centre at the bottom.

:::soluzione
$v_{\mathrm{cm}} = \sqrt{\dfrac{10\,g\,h}{7}} = 5.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
