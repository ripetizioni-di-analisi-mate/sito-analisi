A truck tyre of radius $R = 0.52\,\mathrm{m}$ rolls without slipping on a road while the truck travels in a straight line at $22\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Compute the angular speed of the tyre and the speed of the point of the tread that is at the top of the tyre.

:::soluzione
$\omega = \dfrac{v_{\mathrm{cm}}}{R} = 42\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ and $v_{\mathrm{top}} = 2v_{\mathrm{cm}} = 44\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
