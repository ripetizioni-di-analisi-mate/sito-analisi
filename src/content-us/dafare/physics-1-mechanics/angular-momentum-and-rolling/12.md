titolo: Putty striking the end of a hinged rod

---

A uniform rod of mass $M = 1.8\,\mathrm{kg}$ and length $L = 0.90\,\mathrm{m}$ turns freely in a horizontal plane about a vertical axis through one of its ends and is at rest. A ball of putty of mass $m = 0.25\,\mathrm{kg}$ moving at $7.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ perpendicular to the rod strikes the free end and sticks to it. Compute the angular speed just after the impact.

:::soluzione
$\omega = \dfrac{m\,v\,L}{\dfrac{1}{3}M L^2 + m L^2} = 2.3\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
:::
