A gyroscope wheel of moment of inertia $I = 4.0 \times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ spins at $120\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ about its own axle. The axle is horizontal and supported at one point, the total mass is $0.30\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the centre of mass is $0.050\,\mathrm{m}$ from the support. Compute the precession angular speed.

:::soluzione
$\Omega = \dfrac{M g d}{I \omega} = 0.31\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
:::
