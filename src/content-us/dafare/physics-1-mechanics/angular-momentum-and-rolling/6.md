A homogeneous solid sphere of radius $R = 0.12\,\mathrm{m}$ is launched along a horizontal floor with speed $v_0 = 6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and no rotation. The coefficient of kinetic friction is $\mu_k = 0.10$. Compute the speed of the centre at the instant at which the sphere begins to roll without slipping, and the time needed to reach that instant.

:::soluzione
$v^{*} = \dfrac{5}{7}v_0 = 4.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at $t^{*} = \dfrac{2\,v_0}{7\,\mu_k\,g} = 1.7\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
