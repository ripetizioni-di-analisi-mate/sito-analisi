A ball is thrown vertically upwards with the speed $11\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Air resistance is negligible. Compute the height it reaches above the launch point.

:::soluzione
$h = \dfrac{v_0^2}{2g} = 6.17\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
