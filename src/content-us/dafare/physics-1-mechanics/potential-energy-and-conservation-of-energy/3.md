---
titolo: Work needed to stretch a spring further
teoria: [elastic-potential-energy, hookes-law, potential-energy]
parole: [elastic potential energy, spring constant, work on a spring]
---

A spring of constant $480\,\mathrm{N/m}$ is already stretched by $0.040\,\mathrm{m}$. Compute the work an external agent must do in order to stretch it to $0.090\,\mathrm{m}$, the body attached to the spring being at rest at the start and at the end.

:::soluzione
$W = \dfrac{1}{2}k\left(x_2^2 - x_1^2\right) = 1.56\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
