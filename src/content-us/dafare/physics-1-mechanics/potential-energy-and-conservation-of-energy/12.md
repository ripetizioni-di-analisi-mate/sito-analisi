titolo: Minimum height for a vertical loop
teoria: [conservation-of-mechanical-energy, gravitational-potential-energy, mechanical-energy]

A small body is released from rest at the height $h$ on a frictionless track and then runs along a vertical circular loop of radius $0.40\,\mathrm{m}$. Compute the smallest value of $h$ for which the body stays in contact with the track at the top of the loop.

:::soluzione
$h_{\min} = \dfrac{5}{2}R = 1.0\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
