A body of mass $1.8\,\mathrm{kg}$ is released from rest at the height $4.5\,\mathrm{m}$ above the ground and slides down a frictionless track. Compute its speed when it reaches the ground.

:::soluzione
$v = \sqrt{2gh} = 9.40\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
