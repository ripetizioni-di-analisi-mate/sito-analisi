A block of mass $0.60\,\mathrm{kg}$ is attached to the lower end of a vertical spring of constant $45\,\mathrm{N/m}$ and is released from rest with the spring at its natural length. Compute the maximum extension of the spring.

:::soluzione
$y_{\max} = \dfrac{2mg}{k} = 0.262\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
