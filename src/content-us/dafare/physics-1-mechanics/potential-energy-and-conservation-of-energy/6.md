A block of mass $4.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ slides on a horizontal floor with the initial speed $3.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The coefficient of kinetic friction is $0.35$. Compute the distance travelled before the block stops.

:::soluzione
$d = \dfrac{v_0^2}{2\mu_k g} = 1.78\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
