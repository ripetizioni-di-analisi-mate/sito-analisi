---
titolo: Equilibrium position of a rational potential energy
teoria: [equilibrium, force-from-potential-energy, double-well]

A body moves along the positive $x$ axis with potential energy $U(x) = \dfrac{3}{x^2} - \dfrac{6}{x}$, with $U$ in joules and $x$ in meters. Determine the equilibrium position, establish whether it is stable or unstable, and compute the value of $U$ there.

:::soluzione
$x_0 = 1.0\,\mathrm{m}$, stable, since $\dfrac{d^2U}{dx^2}(x_0) = 6\,\mathrm{N/m} > 0$; the value is $U(x_0) = -3.0\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
