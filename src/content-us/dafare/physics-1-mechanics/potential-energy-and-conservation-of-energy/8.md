A crate of mass $2.5\,\mathrm{kg}$ slides down a ramp from the height $1.8\,\mathrm{m}$ and arrives at the bottom with the speed $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Compute the mechanical energy transformed into thermal energy during the descent.

:::soluzione
$|W_{\text{nc}}| = mgh - \dfrac{1}{2}mv^2 = 24.1\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
