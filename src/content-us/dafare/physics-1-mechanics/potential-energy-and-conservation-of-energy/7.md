A block is released from rest on a plane inclined at $20^\circ$ and slides $3.0\,\mathrm{m}$ along the plane. The coefficient of kinetic friction is $0.15$. Compute the speed of the block at the end of the run.

:::soluzione
$v = \sqrt{2gd\,(\sin\alpha - \mu_k\cos\alpha)} = 3.44\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
