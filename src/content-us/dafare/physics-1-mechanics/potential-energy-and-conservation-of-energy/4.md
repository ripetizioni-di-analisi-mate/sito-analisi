A spring of constant $300\,\mathrm{N/m}$ is compressed by $0.080\,\mathrm{m}$ against a block of mass $0.25\,\mathrm{kg}$ lying on a frictionless horizontal surface. Compute the speed of the block when it leaves the spring.

:::soluzione
$v = x\sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m}} = 2.77\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
