A pendulum of length $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$, made of a bob and a massless inextensible string, is released from rest with the string making the angle $40^\circ$ with the vertical. Air resistance is negligible. Compute the speed of the bob at the lowest point of the swing.

:::soluzione
$v = \sqrt{2gL\,(1 - \cos\theta_0)} = 3.03\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
