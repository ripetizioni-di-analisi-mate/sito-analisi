---
titolo: Force obtained from a potential energy function
teoria: [force-from-potential-energy, potential-energy]

---

A body moves along the $x$ axis under a conservative force whose potential energy is $U(x) = 5x^2 - 2x^3$, with $U$ in joules and $x$ in meters. Compute the force as a function of $x$ and its value at $x = 1.5\,\mathrm{m}$.

:::soluzione
$F_x = -\dfrac{dU}{dx} = 6x^2 - 10x$, hence $F_x(1.5\,\mathrm{m}) = -1.5\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
