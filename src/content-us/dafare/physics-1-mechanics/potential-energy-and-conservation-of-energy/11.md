A body attached to a spring of constant $150\,\mathrm{N/m}$ slides on a frictionless horizontal surface with mechanical energy $3.0\,\mathrm{J}$. Compute the turning points of the motion, measured from the natural length of the spring.

:::soluzione
$x_t = \pm\sqrt{\dfrac{2E}{k}} = \pm 0.20\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
