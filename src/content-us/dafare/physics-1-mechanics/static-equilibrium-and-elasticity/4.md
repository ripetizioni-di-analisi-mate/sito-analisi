titolo: Center of gravity of a drilled disk

A homogeneous disk of radius $20\,\mathrm{cm}$ has a circular hole of radius $5.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ whose center lies at $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the center of the disk. Determine the distance of the center of gravity of the body from the center of the disk.

:::soluzione
$\left|x_G\right| = \dfrac{r^2 d}{R^2 - r^2} = 0.667\,\mathrm{cm}$, on the side opposite to the hole.
:::
