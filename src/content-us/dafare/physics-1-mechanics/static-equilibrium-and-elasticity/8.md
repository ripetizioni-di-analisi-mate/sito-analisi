titolo: Friction required by a ladder with a person on it

A uniform ladder of mass $14\,\mathrm{kg}$ and length $4.0\,\mathrm{m}$ leans against a smooth vertical wall at $70^\circ$ with the floor. A person of mass $75\,\mathrm{kg}$ stands on it at $3.0\,\mathrm{m}$ from the foot. Determine the smallest coefficient of static friction between the foot of the ladder and the floor for which the ladder stays in place.

:::soluzione
$\mu_s \ge 0.259$.
:::
