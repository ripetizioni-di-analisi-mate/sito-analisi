titolo: Force that tips a crate

A uniform crate of mass $60\,\mathrm{kg}$, width $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$ and height $1.50\,\mathrm{m}$ stands on a horizontal floor. A horizontal force is applied at its upper edge, and friction is large enough to prevent sliding. Determine the value of that force at which the crate begins to tip.

:::soluzione
$F = \dfrac{m g\,w}{2h} = 157\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
