---
titolo: Volume change of oil under pressure
teoria: [bulk-modulus, hookes-law-solids]

---

A volume of $0.50\,\mathrm{m^3}$ of oil, of bulk modulus $1.6 \times 10^{9}\,\mathrm{Pa}$, is subjected to a pressure increase of $8.0 \times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Determine the change in its volume.

:::soluzione
$\Delta V = -\,\dfrac{V_0\,\Delta p}{B} = -2.5 \times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^3}$, that is a contraction of $2.5\,\mathrm{L}$.
:::
