A homogeneous plate of constant thickness has the shape of an L formed by a horizontal rectangle $50\,\mathrm{cm} \times 10\,\mathrm{cm}$ and a vertical rectangle $10\,\mathrm{cm} \times 40\,\mathrm{cm}$ standing on its left end. Taking the origin at the lower left corner, determine the coordinates of the center of gravity.

:::soluzione
$x_G = y_G = 16.1\,\mathrm{cm}$.
:::
