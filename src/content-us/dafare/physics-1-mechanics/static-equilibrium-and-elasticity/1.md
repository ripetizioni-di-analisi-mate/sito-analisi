A uniform plank of mass $16\,\mathrm{kg}$ and length $3.0\,\mathrm{m}$ rests on two smooth supports placed at its ends. A child of mass $30\,\mathrm{kg}$ stands on the plank at $1.0\,\mathrm{m}$ from the left support. Determine the force exerted by each support.

:::soluzione
$N_1 = 275\,\mathrm{N}$ at the left support and $N_2 = 177\,\mathrm{N}$ at the right one.
:::
