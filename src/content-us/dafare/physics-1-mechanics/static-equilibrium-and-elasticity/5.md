A uniform beam of mass $30\,\mathrm{kg}$ and length $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ is pinned to a wall at one end and held horizontal by a cable fastened to the other end, making the angle $35^\circ$ with the beam. Determine the tension of the cable and the magnitude of the force exerted by the pin.

:::soluzione
$T = \dfrac{Mg}{2\sin 35^\circ} = 257\,\mathrm{N}$, and the pin reaction has the same magnitude, $257\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
