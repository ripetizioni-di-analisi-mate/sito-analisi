A uniform bar of mass $10\,\mathrm{kg}$ and length $3.0\,\mathrm{m}$ rests on two supports, each placed at $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$ from one end. A body of mass $m$ hangs from the right end of the bar. Determine the largest value of $m$ for which the bar stays in equilibrium.

:::soluzione
$m = M\,\dfrac{\dfrac{L}{2} - c}{c} = 20\,\mathrm{kg}$.
:::
