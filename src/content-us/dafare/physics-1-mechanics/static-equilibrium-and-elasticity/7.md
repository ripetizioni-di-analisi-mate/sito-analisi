---
titolo: Smallest angle for a ladder
teoria: [ladder, equilibrium-conditions, choice-of-pivot]
parole: [ladder problem, static friction, smooth wall, minimum angle]
---

A uniform ladder leans against a smooth vertical wall, on a floor with coefficient of static friction $0.30$. Determine the smallest angle with the floor at which the ladder stays in place.

:::soluzione
$\tan\theta \ge \dfrac{1}{2\mu_s}$, hence $\theta_{\min} = 59.0^\circ$.
:::
