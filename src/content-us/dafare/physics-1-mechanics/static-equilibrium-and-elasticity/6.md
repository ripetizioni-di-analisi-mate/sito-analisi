A uniform beam of mass $20\,\mathrm{kg}$ and length $3.0\,\mathrm{m}$ is pinned to a wall at its left end and held horizontal by a cable fastened to its right end, making the angle $50^\circ$ with the beam. A load of mass $60\,\mathrm{kg}$ hangs from the point of the beam at $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ from the pin. Determine the tension of the cable.

:::soluzione
$T = \dfrac{\left(M\,\dfrac{L}{2} + m\,d\right)g}{L\sin 50^\circ} = 640\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
