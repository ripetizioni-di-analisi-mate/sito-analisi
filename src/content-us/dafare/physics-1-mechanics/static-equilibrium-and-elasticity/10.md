A uniform block of width $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$ and height $1.20\,\mathrm{m}$ rests on a plank whose surface has coefficient of static friction $0.60$. The plank is slowly tilted. Determine whether the block slides or tips, and the angle at which equilibrium ends.

:::soluzione
It tips, at $\theta = \arctan\dfrac{w}{h} = 22.6^\circ$, before the sliding angle $\arctan\mu_s = 31.0^\circ$.
:::
