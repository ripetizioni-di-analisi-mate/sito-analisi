A steel wire of diameter $2.5\,\mathrm{mm}$ and length $4.0\,\mathrm{m}$ hangs vertically and supports a body of mass $70\,\mathrm{kg}$. Young's modulus of steel is $2.0 \times 10^{11}\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Determine the elongation of the wire, neglecting its own weight.

:::soluzione
$\Delta L = \dfrac{F L_0}{A Y} = 2.80\,\mathrm{mm}$.
:::
