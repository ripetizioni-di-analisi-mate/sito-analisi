A rubber pad has square faces of side $0.10\,\mathrm{m}$ and thickness $0.030\,\mathrm{m}$. Its lower face is fixed and a force of $150\,\mathrm{N}$ parallel to the faces is applied to the upper one. The shear modulus of the rubber is $3.0 \times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Determine the horizontal displacement of the upper face.

:::soluzione
$x = \dfrac{F_\parallel h}{A S} = 0.15\,\mathrm{mm}$.
:::
