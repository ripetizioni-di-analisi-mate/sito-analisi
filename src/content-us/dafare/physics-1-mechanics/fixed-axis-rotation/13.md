A wheel with moment of inertia $0.45\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ turns on a frictionless axle, initially at rest. A constant torque of $9.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$ is applied for $4.0\,\mathrm{s}$. Find the work done by the torque and the power delivered at the final instant.

:::soluzione
$W = \tau\,\theta = 1.44 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{J}$ and $P = \tau\,\omega = 720\,\mathrm{W}$.
:::
