A wheel of radius $0.33\,\mathrm{m}$ turns at the constant rate of $850\,\mathrm{rev/min}$. Find the speed of a point of the rim and its centripetal acceleration.

:::soluzione
$v = 29.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $a_r = 2.61 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
:::
