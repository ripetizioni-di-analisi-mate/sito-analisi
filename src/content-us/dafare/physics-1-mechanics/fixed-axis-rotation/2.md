A wheel turning about a fixed axis has angular position $\theta(t) = 4.0\,t^2 - 0.60\,t^3$, with $\theta$ in radians and $t$ in seconds. Find the angular velocity and the angular acceleration at $t = 2.0\,\mathrm{s}$, and the instant at which the angular velocity is largest.

:::soluzione
$\omega(2.0) = 8.8\,\mathrm{rad/s}$, $\alpha(2.0) = 0.80\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$; the angular velocity is largest at $t = 2.2\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
