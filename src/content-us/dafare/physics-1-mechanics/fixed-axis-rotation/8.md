A uniform rod of mass $3.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ and length $1.6\,\mathrm{m}$ carries a small body of mass $0.80\,\mathrm{kg}$ at each end. Find the moment of inertia about the axis perpendicular to the rod through its center.

:::soluzione
$I = \dfrac{1}{12}ML^2 + 2\,m\left(\dfrac{L}{2}\right)^2 = 1.66\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$.
:::
