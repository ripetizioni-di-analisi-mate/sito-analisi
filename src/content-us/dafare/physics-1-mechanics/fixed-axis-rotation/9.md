A uniform solid sphere of mass $5.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ and radius $0.12\,\mathrm{m}$ rotates about an axis tangent to its surface. Find its moment of inertia about that axis.

:::soluzione
$I = \dfrac{2}{5}MR^2 + MR^2 = \dfrac{7}{5}MR^2 = 0.101\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$.
:::
