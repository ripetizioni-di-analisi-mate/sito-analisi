titolo: Torque of a force applied to a wrench
teoria: [torque]
parole: [torque, "lever arm", wrench, "line of action"]

A force of magnitude $120\,\mathrm{N}$ is applied at the end of a wrench $0.45\,\mathrm{m}$ long, making an angle of $35^\circ$ with the handle. Find the torque about the axis of the bolt.

:::soluzione
$\tau = r F \sin\varphi = 31.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$.
:::
