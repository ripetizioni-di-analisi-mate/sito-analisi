A uniform rod of length $1.2\,\mathrm{m}$ turns about a horizontal frictionless axis through one end. The rod is held horizontal and released from rest. Find the angular velocity when it passes through the vertical position.

:::soluzione
$\omega = \sqrt{\dfrac{3g}{L}} = 4.95\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
:::
