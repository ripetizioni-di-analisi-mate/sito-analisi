A disk of radius $0.25\,\mathrm{m}$ turns about a fixed axis through its center. At a given instant its angular velocity is $4.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ and its angular acceleration is $6.0\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$. Find the magnitude of the acceleration of a point of the rim at that instant.

:::soluzione
$a = \sqrt{a_r^2 + a_t^2} = 4.27\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, with $a_r = 4.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and $a_t = 1.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
:::
