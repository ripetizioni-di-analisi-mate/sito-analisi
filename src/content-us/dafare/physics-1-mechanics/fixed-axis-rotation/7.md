titolo: Net torque on a stepped pulley

A stepped pulley turns about a fixed axis. A cord wound on the rim, of radius $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$, is pulled tangentially with a force of $40\,\mathrm{N}$ that tends to turn the pulley counterclockwise, while a cord wound on the hub, of radius $0.12\,\mathrm{m}$, is pulled tangentially with a force of $70\,\mathrm{N}$ that tends to turn it clockwise. Find the net torque about the axis.

:::soluzione
$\tau_{\mathrm{net}} = +3.6\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$, counterclockwise.
:::
