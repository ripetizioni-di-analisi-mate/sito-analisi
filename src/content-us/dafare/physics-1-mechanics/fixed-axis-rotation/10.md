A uniform disk of radius $0.40\,\mathrm{m}$ and mass $8.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ has a circular hole of radius $0.20\,\mathrm{m}$ drilled through it, centered at $0.20\,\mathrm{m}$ from the center of the disk. Find the moment of inertia of the drilled disk about the axis through the center of the original disk, perpendicular to its face.

:::soluzione
$I = 0.52\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$.
:::
