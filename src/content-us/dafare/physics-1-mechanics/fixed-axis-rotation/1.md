A flywheel starts from rest and reaches $900\,\mathrm{rev/min}$ in $15\,\mathrm{s}$ with constant angular acceleration. Find the angular acceleration and the number of revolutions made during the start-up.

:::soluzione
$\alpha = 6.28\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$ and $\theta = 707\,\mathrm{rad} = 112.5\,\mathrm{rev}$.
:::
