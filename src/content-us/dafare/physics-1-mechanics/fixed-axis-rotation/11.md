A body of mass $5.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ hangs from a cord wound around a pulley of mass $4.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ and radius $0.10\,\mathrm{m}$, treated as a uniform disk on a frictionless axle. The cord is massless and inextensible and does not slip. Find the acceleration of the body and the tension of the cord.

:::soluzione
$a = \dfrac{mg}{m + \dfrac{M}{2}} = 7.01\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and $T = m(g - a) = 14.0\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
