A belt that does not slip connects a pulley of radius $6.0\,\mathrm{cm}$, turning at $900\,\mathrm{rev/min}$, to a second pulley of radius $15\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find the speed of the belt and the rate of the second pulley.

:::soluzione
$v = 5.65\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $\omega_2 = 37.7\,\mathrm{rad/s} = 360\,\mathrm{rev/min}$.
:::
