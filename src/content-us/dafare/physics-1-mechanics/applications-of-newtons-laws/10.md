A curve of radius $150\,\mathrm{m}$ is to be banked so that a vehicle travelling at $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$ needs no sideways friction. Compute the bank angle.

:::soluzione
$\theta = \arctan\dfrac{v^2}{g\,r} = 31.4^\circ$.
:::
