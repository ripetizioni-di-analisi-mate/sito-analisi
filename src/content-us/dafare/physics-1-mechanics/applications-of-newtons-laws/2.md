---
titolo: Stopping distance of a sliding puck
teoria: [kinetic-friction, stopping-distance]
parole: [kinetic friction, stopping distance, uniformly accelerated motion]
---

A puck is launched along a horizontal surface with speed $8.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the coefficient of kinetic friction between puck and surface is $\mu_k = 0.12$. Compute the distance the puck covers before stopping.

:::soluzione
$d = \dfrac{v_0^2}{2\,\mu_k\,g} = 27.2\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
