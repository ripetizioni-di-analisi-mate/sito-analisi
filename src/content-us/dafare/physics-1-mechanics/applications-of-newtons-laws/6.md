titolo: Two connected blocks with friction on the table

A block of mass $3.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ lies on a horizontal table with coefficient of kinetic friction $\mu_k = 0.25$ and is connected by an ideal string, over an ideal pulley at the edge of the table, to a hanging block of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$. The system is released from rest and the blocks move. Compute the acceleration of the system.

:::soluzione
$a = \dfrac{m_2 - \mu_k m_1}{m_1 + m_2}\,g = 2.45\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
:::
