A block is released from rest on a plane inclined at $40^\circ$ to the horizontal, with coefficient of kinetic friction $\mu_k = 0.30$. Compute the magnitude of its acceleration.

:::soluzione
$a = g\,(\sin 40^\circ - \mu_k\cos 40^\circ) = 4.05\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
:::
