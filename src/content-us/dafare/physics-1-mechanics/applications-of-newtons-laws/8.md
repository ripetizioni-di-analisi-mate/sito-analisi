A body of mass $6.0\,\mathrm{g}$ falls from rest in a fluid that exerts the drag force $F_D = b\,v$ with $b = 0.020\,\mathrm{kg/s}$. Compute its speed after $0.40\,\mathrm{s}$.

:::soluzione
$v = \dfrac{m g}{b}\left(1 - e^{-\frac{b t}{m}}\right) = 2.17\,\mathrm{m/s}$, with $v_t = 2.94\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $\tau = 0.30\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
