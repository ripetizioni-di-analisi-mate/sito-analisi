---
titolo: Force needed to start a crate moving
teoria: [static-friction, friction-force]
parole: [static friction, maximum friction force, horizontal push]
---

A crate of mass $45\,\mathrm{kg}$ rests on a horizontal floor, with coefficient of static friction $\mu_s = 0.55$. Compute the smallest horizontal force that sets the crate in motion.

:::soluzione
$F = \mu_s\,m g = 243\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
