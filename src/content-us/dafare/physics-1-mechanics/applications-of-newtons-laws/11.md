---
titolo: Minimum speed at the top of a loop
teoria: [vertical-loop, centripetal-force]
parole: [vertical loop, minimum speed, normal force, centripetal force]
---

A car travels the inside of a vertical circular loop of radius $5.5\,\mathrm{m}$. Compute the smallest speed at the top of the loop that keeps the car in contact with the track.

:::soluzione
$v_{\min} = \sqrt{g\,r} = 7.35\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
