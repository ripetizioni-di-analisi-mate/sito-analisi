A car travels a flat horizontal curve of radius $45\,\mathrm{m}$, with coefficient of static friction $\mu_s = 0.70$ between tyres and road. Compute the largest speed at which the curve can be taken without sliding.

:::soluzione
$v_{\max} = \sqrt{\mu_s\,g\,r} = 17.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
