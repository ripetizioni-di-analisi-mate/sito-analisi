A coin lies on a horizontal turntable at a distance of $0.12\,\mathrm{m}$ from the axis, and the turntable rotates at $45$ revolutions per minute. Compute the smallest coefficient of static friction that keeps the coin from sliding.

:::soluzione
$\mu_s = \dfrac{\omega^2 r}{g} = 0.272$, with $\omega = 4.71\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
:::
