titolo: Pushing a block up an incline at constant speed
teoria: [incline-components, kinetic-friction]

A block of mass $20\,\mathrm{kg}$ is pushed up a plane inclined at $15^\circ$ by a force parallel to the plane, with coefficient of kinetic friction $\mu_k = 0.25$, and moves at constant speed. Compute the magnitude of the applied force.

:::soluzione
$F = m g\,(\sin 15^\circ + \mu_k\cos 15^\circ) = 98.2\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
