---
titolo: Terminal speed of a baseball in air
teoria: [drag-force, terminal-speed]
parole: [quadratic drag, terminal speed, air resistance]
---

A baseball of mass $0.145\,\mathrm{kg}$ falls in air, which exerts the drag force $F_D = D\,v^2$ with $D = 1.3 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{kg/m}$. Compute its terminal speed.

:::soluzione
$v_t = \sqrt{\dfrac{m g}{D}} = 33.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
