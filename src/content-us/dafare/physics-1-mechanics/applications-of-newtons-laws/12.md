---
titolo: Speed at which a car leaves the crest of a hill
teoria: [car-over-hill, centripetal-force]
parole: [crest of a hill, normal force, radius of curvature, centripetal force]
---

A car passes the crest of a hill whose profile has radius of curvature $25\,\mathrm{m}$. Compute the speed at which the road stops pushing on the car.

:::soluzione
$v = \sqrt{g\,r} = 15.7\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
