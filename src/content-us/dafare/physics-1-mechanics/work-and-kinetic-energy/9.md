---
titolo: Work of a variable force along a straight line
teoria: [work-variable-force]
parole: [variable force, work as an integral, area under the graph]
---

A body moves along the $x$ axis from $x_1 = 0$ to $x_2 = 3.0\,\mathrm{m}$ under a force whose component along the axis is $F_x(x) = b\,x^2$, with $b = 5.0\,\mathrm{N/m^2}$. Compute the work done by that force.

:::soluzione
$W = \dfrac{b\,x_2^3}{3} = 45\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
