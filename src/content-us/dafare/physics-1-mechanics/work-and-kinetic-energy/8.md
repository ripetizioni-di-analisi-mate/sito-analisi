titolo: Distance traveled up a frictionless incline
teoria: [work-energy-theorem, work-of-weight]

A block of mass $3.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ is launched up a frictionless inclined plane that makes the angle $25^\circ$ with the horizontal, with the initial speed $7.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ directed along the plane. Compute the distance traveled along the plane before the block stops.

:::soluzione
$L = \dfrac{v_0^2}{2\,g\sin\theta} = 5.91\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
