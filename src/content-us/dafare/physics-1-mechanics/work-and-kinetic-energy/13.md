---
titolo: Power of an elevator motor at constant speed
teoria: [power, power-force-velocity]
parole: [instantaneous power, constant speed, watt, cable tension]
---

The motor of an elevator raises a cabin of total mass $1500\,\mathrm{kg}$ at the constant speed of $0.80\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Compute the power delivered by the cable.

:::soluzione
$P = m g v = 1.18 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{W}$.
:::
