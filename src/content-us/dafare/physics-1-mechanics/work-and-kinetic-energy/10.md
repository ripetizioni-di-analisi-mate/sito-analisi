titolo: Work done in stretching a spring
teoria: [hookes-law, work-of-a-spring]
parole: [spring constant, Hooke's law, elastic work, stretch]
---

A spring of constant $600\,\mathrm{N/m}$ is stretched slowly from its natural length to an elongation of $0.25\,\mathrm{m}$. Compute the work done by the person who stretches it.

:::soluzione
$W = \dfrac{1}{2}k\,x^2 = 18.8\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
