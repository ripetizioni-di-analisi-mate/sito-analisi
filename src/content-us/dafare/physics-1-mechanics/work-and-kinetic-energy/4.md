A body moves in the $xy$ plane from the origin to the point $(3.0,\ 2.0)\,\mathrm{m}$ under the force $\vec{F} = (c\,x)\,\hat{\imath} + F_0\,\hat{\jmath}$, with $c = 2.0\,\mathrm{N/m}$ and $F_0 = 3.0\,\mathrm{N}$. Compute the work done by that force.

:::soluzione
$W = \dfrac{c\,x_B^2}{2} + F_0\,y_B = 15\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
