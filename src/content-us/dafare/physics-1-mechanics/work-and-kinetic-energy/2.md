A trolley is pulled along a straight horizontal track by a constant force of magnitude $120\,\mathrm{N}$ directed at $25^\circ$ above the direction of the motion, and it moves $15\,\mathrm{m}$. Compute the work done by that force.

:::soluzione
$W = F\,d\cos\theta = 1.63 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
