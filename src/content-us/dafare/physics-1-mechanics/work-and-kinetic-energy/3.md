A crate of mass $40\,\mathrm{kg}$ is dragged $7.0\,\mathrm{m}$ along a horizontal floor by a constant horizontal force of magnitude $90\,\mathrm{N}$. The coefficient of kinetic friction between the crate and the floor is $0.18$. Compute the total work done on the crate.

:::soluzione
$W_{\text{tot}} = (F - \mu_k\,m g)\,d = 136\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
