---
titolo: Block launched by a spring onto a rough floor
teoria: [work-of-a-spring, work-energy-theorem, distance-against-friction]

---

A block of mass $0.80\,\mathrm{kg}$ is pressed against a spring of constant $700\,\mathrm{N/m}$, compressing it by $0.090\,\mathrm{m}$, and is then released on a horizontal surface. The stretch under the spring is frictionless, while beyond the natural length of the spring the coefficient of kinetic friction is $0.22$. Compute the distance the block travels on the rough stretch before stopping.

:::soluzione
$d = \dfrac{k\,x^2}{2\,\mu_k\,m g} = 1.64\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
