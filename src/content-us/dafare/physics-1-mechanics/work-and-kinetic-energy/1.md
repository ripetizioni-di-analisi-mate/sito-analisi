titolo: Work of a horizontal constant force
teoria: [work-constant-force]
parole: [work of a constant force, joule, horizontal displacement]
---

A box is pushed along a horizontal floor by a constant horizontal force of magnitude $75\,\mathrm{N}$, in the direction of the motion, and it moves $6.0\,\mathrm{m}$. Compute the work done by that force.

:::soluzione
$W = F\,d = 450\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
