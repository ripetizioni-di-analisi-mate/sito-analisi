titolo: Average power in climbing a staircase
teoria: [power, work-of-weight]
parole: [average power, watt, work of the weight, climb]
---

A person of mass $70\,\mathrm{kg}$ climbs a staircase of height $5.0\,\mathrm{m}$ in $4.5\,\mathrm{s}$, arriving with negligible speed. Compute the average power developed against the weight.

:::soluzione
$P_{\text{av}} = \dfrac{m g h}{\Delta t} = 763\,\mathrm{W}$.
:::
