A sled of mass $20\,\mathrm{kg}$, initially at rest on a horizontal surface, is pulled by a constant horizontal force of magnitude $70\,\mathrm{N}$ for $10\,\mathrm{m}$. The coefficient of kinetic friction is $0.20$. Compute the speed of the sled at the end of the stretch.

:::soluzione
$v = \sqrt{\dfrac{2\,(F - \mu_k\,m g)\,d}{m}} = 5.55\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
