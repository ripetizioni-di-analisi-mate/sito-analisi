titolo: Work needed to reach a given speed
teoria: [kinetic-energy, work-energy-theorem]
parole: [kinetic energy, work-energy theorem, starting from rest]
---

A car of mass $900\,\mathrm{kg}$, initially at rest, reaches the speed of $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$ on a horizontal road. Compute the total work done on the car.

:::soluzione
$W_{\text{tot}} = \dfrac{1}{2}m v^2 = 1.80 \times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
