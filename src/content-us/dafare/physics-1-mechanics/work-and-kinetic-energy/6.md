titolo: Braking force from the stopping distance
teoria: [work-energy-theorem, kinetic-energy, work-energy-strategy]

---

A car of mass $1100\,\mathrm{kg}$ traveling at $22\,\mathrm{m/s}$ on a horizontal road stops in $45\,\mathrm{m}$. Compute the magnitude of the braking force, assumed constant.

:::soluzione
$F = \dfrac{m\,v_0^2}{2\,d} = 5.92 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
