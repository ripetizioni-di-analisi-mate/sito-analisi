Two pulleys of different radii are joined by a belt that does not slip. Which quantity is the same for the points of the two rims?

- The centripetal acceleration
- The period of rotation
- The angular velocity
- The speed

## Explanation

Each rim moves together with the belt, which does not slip, so the points of both rims have the speed of the belt. From $v = \omega r$ the angular velocities are in the inverse ratio of the radii, and the period and the centripetal acceleration $\dfrac{v^2}{r}$ differ as well.
