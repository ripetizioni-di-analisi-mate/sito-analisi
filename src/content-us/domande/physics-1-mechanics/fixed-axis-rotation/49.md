What is the moment of inertia of a uniform solid sphere of mass $3.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ and radius $0.10\,\mathrm{m}$ about an axis through its center?

- $0.012\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.030\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.020\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.015\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$

## Explanation

For a uniform solid sphere about a diameter,
$$
I = \dfrac{2}{5}\,M R^2 = \dfrac{2}{5} \cdot 3.0\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot (0.10\,\mathrm{m})^2 = 0.012\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2} .
$$
The coefficient $\dfrac{2}{3}$ belongs to the thin spherical shell, whose mass lies entirely at the distance $R$ from the center.
