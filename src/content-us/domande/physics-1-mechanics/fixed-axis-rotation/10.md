With counterclockwise taken as positive, the angular position of a wheel goes from $\theta_1 = 4.0\,\mathrm{rad}$ to $\theta_2 = 1.5\,\mathrm{rad}$. Which statement is correct?

- $\Delta\theta = +2.5\,\mathrm{rad}$, a net counterclockwise rotation
- $\Delta\theta = -2.5\,\mathrm{rad}$, a net clockwise rotation
- $\Delta\theta = +5.5\,\mathrm{rad}$, a net clockwise rotation
- $\Delta\theta = -2.5\,\mathrm{rad}$, a net counterclockwise rotation

## Explanation

The angular displacement is the final position minus the initial one, $\Delta\theta = 1.5\,\mathrm{rad} - 4.0\,\mathrm{rad} = -2.5\,\mathrm{rad}$. Counterclockwise rotations are positive, therefore a negative displacement corresponds to a net clockwise rotation.
