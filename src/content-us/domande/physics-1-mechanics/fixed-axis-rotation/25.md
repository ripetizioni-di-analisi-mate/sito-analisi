For rotation with constant angular acceleration, which equation connects the angular velocity and the angular position without the time?

- $\omega^2 = \omega_0^2 + \alpha\,(\theta - \theta_0)$
- $\omega^2 = \omega_0^2 + 2\,\alpha\,(\theta - \theta_0)$
- $\theta = \theta_0 + \omega_0\,t + \dfrac{1}{2}\,\alpha\,t^2$
- $\omega = \omega_0 + \alpha\,(\theta - \theta_0)$

## Explanation

Eliminating $t$ between $\omega = \omega_0 + \alpha t$ and $\theta = \theta_0 + \omega_0 t + \dfrac{1}{2}\alpha t^2$ gives $\omega^2 = \omega_0^2 + 2\,\alpha\,(\theta - \theta_0)$. The factor $2$ comes from the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$ of the equation for $\theta(t)$, and the result is the one used in braking problems, where the time is not given.
