A uniform rod of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ and length $1.0\,\mathrm{m}$ is pivoted at one end and makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with the vertical. What is the magnitude of the torque of its weight about the pivot?

- $8.50\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $4.91\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $17.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $9.81\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$

## Explanation

The weight acts at the center of mass, at $\dfrac{L}{2} = 0.50\,\mathrm{m}$ from the pivot, and its line of action is vertical. The lever arm is the horizontal distance $\dfrac{L}{2}\sin 60^\circ = 0.433\,\mathrm{m}$, therefore
$$
\tau = M g\,\dfrac{L}{2}\sin 60^\circ = 2.0 \cdot 9.81 \cdot 0.433\,\mathrm{N\cdot m} = 8.50\,\mathrm{N\cdot m} .
$$
