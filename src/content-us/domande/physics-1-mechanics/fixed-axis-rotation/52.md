In the integral $I = \displaystyle\int r^2\,dm$ that gives the moment of inertia of a body about an axis, what is $r$?

- The distance of the center of mass from the axis
- The largest distance of the body from the axis
- The distance of the element $dm$ from the axis
- The distance of the element $dm$ from the center of mass of the body

## Explanation

The moment of inertia weights each element of mass with the square of its own distance from the axis, the perpendicular distance from the line about which the body turns. This is why the same body has different moments of inertia about different axes, while its center of mass stays the same.
