A circular saw blade of diameter $0.20\,\mathrm{m}$ turns at $3000\,\mathrm{rev/min}$. What is the speed of its teeth?

- $1880\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $62.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $31.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The angular velocity is $\omega = 3000 \cdot \dfrac{2\pi}{60}\,\mathrm{rad/s} = 314\,\mathrm{rad/s}$, and the teeth are at the distance $r = 0.10\,\mathrm{m}$ from the axis, half the diameter. Therefore
$$
v = \omega\,r = 314\,\mathrm{rad/s} \cdot 0.10\,\mathrm{m} = 31.4\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
