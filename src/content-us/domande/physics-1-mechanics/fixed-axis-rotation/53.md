A uniform solid disk of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ and radius $0.20\,\mathrm{m}$ rotates about an axis perpendicular to its face through a point of its rim. What is its moment of inertia about that axis?

- $0.040\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.080\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.12\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.36\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$

## Explanation

The axis through the center is at distance $d = R$ from the new one, and the **parallel-axis theorem** applies, so
$$
I = \dfrac{1}{2}\,M R^2 + M R^2 = \dfrac{3}{2}\,M R^2 = \dfrac{3}{2} \cdot 2.0 \cdot 0.040\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2} = 0.12\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2} .
$$
The distance $d$ is the radius, measured from the center of mass, and not the diameter.
