Two small bodies of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ each are fixed to a rod of negligible mass, at $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$ from an axis on opposite sides of it. What is the moment of inertia about that axis?

- $1.2\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $1.4\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.18\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.36\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$

## Explanation

Each body contributes its mass times the square of its distance from the axis, so
$$
I = \sum_i m_i\,r_i^2 = 2 \cdot 2.0\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot (0.30\,\mathrm{m})^2 = 0.36\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2} .
$$
The distance to be squared is $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$ for both bodies, and the distance $0.60\,\mathrm{m}$ between them plays no role.
