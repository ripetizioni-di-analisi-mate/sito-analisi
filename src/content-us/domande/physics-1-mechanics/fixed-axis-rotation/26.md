For a wheel rotating about a fixed axis, what does the area under the graph of $\omega(t)$ between two instants represent?

- The angular acceleration between the two instants
- The average angular velocity between the two instants
- The angular displacement between the two instants
- The change in angular velocity between the two instants

## Explanation

Since $\omega = \dfrac{d\theta}{dt}$, the integral of $\omega(t)$ between two instants equals $\theta_2 - \theta_1$, the angular displacement. The change in angular velocity is instead the area under the graph of $\alpha(t)$, and the angular acceleration is the slope of the graph of $\omega(t)$.
