A force of $50\,\mathrm{N}$ is applied at a point at $0.40\,\mathrm{m}$ from an axis, and the angle between the position vector $\vec{r}$ of the point and the force is $30^\circ$. What is the torque of the force about the axis?

- $17.3\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $10.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $600\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $20.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$

## Explanation

The torque is $\tau = r F \sin\varphi$, where $\varphi$ is the angle between $\vec{r}$ and $\vec{F}$, so
$$
\tau = 0.40\,\mathrm{m} \cdot 50\,\mathrm{N} \cdot \sin 30^\circ = 10.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m} .
$$
The cosine would give the product of $r$ and the component of the force along $\vec{r}$, which produces no rotation.
