A wheel slows down uniformly from $20\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ to $8.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ in $3.0\,\mathrm{s}$. What is its angular acceleration?

- $-6.7\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $+4.0\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $+9.3\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $-4.0\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$

## Explanation

The angular acceleration is constant, therefore
$$
\alpha = \dfrac{\omega - \omega_0}{t} = \dfrac{8.0\,\mathrm{rad/s} - 20\,\mathrm{rad/s}}{3.0\,\mathrm{s}} = -4.0\,\mathrm{rad/s^2} .
$$
The sign is opposite to that of $\omega$, consistently with the wheel slowing down.
