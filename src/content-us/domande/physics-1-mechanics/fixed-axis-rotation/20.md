A wheel starts from rest with constant angular acceleration $\alpha = 4.0\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$. What is its angular velocity after $3.5\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $25\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $7.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $14\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $49\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

The angular acceleration is constant and $\omega_0 = 0$, therefore $\omega = \omega_0 + \alpha\,t = 4.0\,\mathrm{rad/s^2} \cdot 3.5\,\mathrm{s} = 14\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. The value $25$ comes from the angular displacement $\dfrac{1}{2}\alpha t^2 = 24.5\,\mathrm{rad}$ over the same time.
