A point of a wheel is at $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$ from the axis, and the wheel turns at the constant rate $\omega = 6.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. What is the centripetal acceleration of the point?

- $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $9.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $72\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The radial component of the acceleration is
$$
a_r = \omega^2\,r = (6.0\,\mathrm{rad/s})^2 \cdot 0.50\,\mathrm{m} = 18\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
The product $\omega r = 3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is the speed of the point.
