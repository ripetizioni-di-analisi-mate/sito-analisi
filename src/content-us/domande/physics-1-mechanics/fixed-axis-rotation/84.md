A motor delivers a power of $2.0\,\mathrm{kW}$ while turning at $100\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. What torque does it exert?

- $20\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $0.020\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $0.050\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $2.0 \cdot 10^{5}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$

## Explanation

The power delivered by a torque is $P = \tau\,\omega$, therefore
$$
\tau = \dfrac{P}{\omega} = \dfrac{2000\,\mathrm{W}}{100\,\mathrm{rad/s}} = 20\,\mathrm{N\cdot m} .
$$
The power must be expressed in watts, so $2.0\,\mathrm{kW}$ becomes $2000\,\mathrm{W}$.
