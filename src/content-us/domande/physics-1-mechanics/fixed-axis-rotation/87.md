A thin ring and a uniform solid disk of the same mass and radius turn about their central axes with the same angular velocity. What is the ratio $\dfrac{K_{\mathrm{ring}}}{K_{\mathrm{disk}}}$ of their kinetic energies?

- $4$
- $1$
- $2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The kinetic energy is $K = \dfrac{1}{2}\,I\,\omega^2$, and with the same $\omega$ it is proportional to the moment of inertia. The ring has $I = M R^2$ and the disk $I = \dfrac{1}{2}M R^2$, therefore the ratio is $2$.
