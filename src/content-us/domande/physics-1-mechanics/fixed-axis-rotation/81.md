A disk with moment of inertia $0.20\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ turns at $15\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ about a fixed axis. What is its kinetic energy?

- $3.00\,\mathrm{J}$
- $45.0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $22.5\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1.50\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The kinetic energy of a body rotating about a fixed axis is
$$
K = \dfrac{1}{2}\,I\,\omega^2 = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 0.20\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2} \cdot (15\,\mathrm{rad/s})^2 = 22.5\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
