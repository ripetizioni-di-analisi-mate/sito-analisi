A body of mass $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ hangs from a cord wound around a pulley and descends with acceleration $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the tension of the cord?

- $15.8\,\mathrm{N}$
- $6.00\,\mathrm{N}$
- $9.81\,\mathrm{N}$
- $3.81\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Newton's second law for the body, with the downward direction positive, gives $m g - T = m a$, therefore
$$
T = m\,(g - a) = 1.0\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot (9.81 - 6.0)\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 3.81\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The tension is smaller than the weight, consistently with the downward acceleration.
