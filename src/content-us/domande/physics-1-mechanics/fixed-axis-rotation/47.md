A uniform rod rotates about an axis perpendicular to it. What is the ratio $\dfrac{I_{\mathrm{end}}}{I_{\mathrm{center}}}$ of its moments of inertia about the axis through one end and about the axis through its center?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $4$
- $2$
- $1$

## Explanation

The two values are $I_{\mathrm{end}} = \dfrac{1}{3}ML^2$ and $I_{\mathrm{center}} = \dfrac{1}{12}ML^2$, so their ratio is $\dfrac{12}{3} = 4$. The mass and the length are the same, and the moment of inertia changes because it depends on the axis.
