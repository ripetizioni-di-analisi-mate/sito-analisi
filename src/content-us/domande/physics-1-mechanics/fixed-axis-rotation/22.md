A fan turning at $30\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ is braked with a constant angular acceleration of magnitude $5.0\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$. Through what angle does it turn before stopping?

- $90\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $6.0\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $180\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $45\,\mathrm{rad}$

## Explanation

The time is neither given nor requested, so the equation that links angular velocity and angular displacement applies. With $\omega = 0$ and $\alpha = -5.0\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$,
$$
0 = \omega_0^2 + 2\,\alpha\,\Delta\theta
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
\Delta\theta = \dfrac{\omega_0^2}{2\,|\alpha|} = \dfrac{900}{10}\,\mathrm{rad} = 90\,\mathrm{rad} .
$$
The number $6.0$ is the stopping time in seconds.
