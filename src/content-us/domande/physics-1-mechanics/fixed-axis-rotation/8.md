A wheel makes $3.0$ complete revolutions. What arc does a point at $0.20\,\mathrm{m}$ from the axis travel?

- $0.600\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3.77\,\mathrm{m}$
- $7.54\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.88\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Three revolutions correspond to $\theta = 3.0 \cdot 2\pi\,\mathrm{rad} = 6\pi\,\mathrm{rad}$, therefore
$$
s = r\,\theta = 0.20\,\mathrm{m} \cdot 6\pi = 3.77\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The arc equals three times the circumference $2\pi r$ of the circle described by the point.
