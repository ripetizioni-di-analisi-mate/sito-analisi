With counterclockwise taken as positive, a wheel has $\omega = -6.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ and $\alpha = +2.0\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$ at a given instant. How is it moving at that instant?

- It turns counterclockwise and is slowing down
- It turns clockwise and is speeding up
- It turns clockwise and is slowing down
- It turns counterclockwise and is speeding up

## Explanation

The sign of $\omega$ gives the sense of rotation, so $\omega < 0$ means a clockwise rotation. The angular velocity and the angular acceleration have opposite signs, therefore the magnitude of $\omega$ decreases and the wheel is slowing down.
