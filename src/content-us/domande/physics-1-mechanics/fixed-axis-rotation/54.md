Among all the axes parallel to a given direction, about which one does a body have the smallest moment of inertia?

- An axis tangent to the surface of the body
- The axis through the point of the body farthest from the center of mass
- The axis through the center of mass
- Every parallel axis gives the same moment of inertia

## Explanation

By the **parallel-axis theorem** $I = I_{\mathrm{cm}} + M d^2$, where $d$ is the distance from the axis through the center of mass. The term $M d^2$ is positive for $d \neq 0$, therefore the smallest value is reached for $d = 0$.
