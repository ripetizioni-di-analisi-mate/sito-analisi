A wheel has angular position $\theta(t) = 5.0\,t^3$, with $\theta$ in radians and $t$ in seconds. What is its angular acceleration at $t = 1.0\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $10\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $30\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $15\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $5.0\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$

## Explanation

Differentiating twice,
$$
\omega(t) = \dfrac{d\theta}{dt} = 15\,t^2 ,
\qquad
\alpha(t) = \dfrac{d\omega}{dt} = 30\,t .
$$
At $t = 1.0\,\mathrm{s}$ the angular acceleration is $30\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$, while $15$ is the value of $\omega$ at the same instant in radians per second.
