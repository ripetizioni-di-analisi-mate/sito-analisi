A disk turns at $8.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ about a fixed axis. What is the speed of a point at $0.25\,\mathrm{m}$ from the axis?

- $32\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.50\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The speed of a point of the body is $v = \omega\,r = 8.0\,\mathrm{rad/s} \cdot 0.25\,\mathrm{m} = 2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The radian is dimensionless, so the product of $\mathrm{rad/s}$ and $\mathrm{m}$ is in $\mathrm{m/s}$.
