The angular position of a wheel is $2.0\,\mathrm{rad}$ at $t = 1.0\,\mathrm{s}$ and $14\,\mathrm{rad}$ at $t = 4.0\,\mathrm{s}$. What is its average angular velocity in this interval?

- $3.5\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $2.4\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $3.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

The average angular velocity is the angular displacement divided by the time interval,
$$
\omega_{\mathrm{av}} = \dfrac{\Delta\theta}{\Delta t} = \dfrac{14\,\mathrm{rad} - 2.0\,\mathrm{rad}}{4.0\,\mathrm{s} - 1.0\,\mathrm{s}} = 4.0\,\mathrm{rad/s} .
$$
Both the angle and the time enter as differences, since the interval starts at $t = 1.0\,\mathrm{s}$.
