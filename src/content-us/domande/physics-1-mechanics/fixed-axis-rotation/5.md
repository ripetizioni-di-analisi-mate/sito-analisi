With counterclockwise taken as positive, a wheel turns through $2.5$ revolutions clockwise. What is its angular displacement?

- $-7.85\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $-900\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $-15.7\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $15.7\,\mathrm{rad}$

## Explanation

Each revolution corresponds to $2\pi\,\mathrm{rad}$, and a clockwise turn has a negative angular displacement with the stated convention, so
$$
\Delta\theta = -2.5 \cdot 2\pi\,\mathrm{rad} = -5\pi\,\mathrm{rad} = -15.7\,\mathrm{rad} .
$$
The angle is not reduced to a single turn, since it counts the whole rotation. The number $900$ is the same rotation measured in degrees.
