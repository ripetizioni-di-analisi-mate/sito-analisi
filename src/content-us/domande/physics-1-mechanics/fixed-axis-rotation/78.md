A wheel with moment of inertia $0.80\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ turning at $20\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ is brought to rest in $4.0\,\mathrm{s}$ by a constant frictional torque. What is the magnitude of that torque?

- $2.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $6.3\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $16\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$

## Explanation

The torque is constant, hence the angular acceleration is constant and its magnitude is $|\alpha| = \dfrac{20\,\mathrm{rad/s}}{4.0\,\mathrm{s}} = 5.0\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$. By **Newton's second law for rotation**, $|\tau| = I\,|\alpha| = 0.80 \cdot 5.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m} = 4.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$.
