A constant torque of $12\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$ acts on a wheel in the direction of its rotation, at an instant when the wheel turns at $25\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. What power does the torque deliver at that instant?

- $150\,\mathrm{W}$
- $2.1\,\mathrm{W}$
- $300\,\mathrm{W}$
- $0.48\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

The power delivered by a torque is $P = \tau\,\omega = 12\,\mathrm{N\cdot m} \cdot 25\,\mathrm{rad/s} = 300\,\mathrm{W}$. The radian is dimensionless, hence the product of $\mathrm{N\cdot m}$ and $\mathrm{rad/s}$ is in watts.
