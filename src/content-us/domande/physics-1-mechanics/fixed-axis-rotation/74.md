A body hangs from a cord wound around a pulley with mass that turns on a frictionless axle. The cord does not slip, and the body descends with increasing speed. How does the tension $T$ of the cord compare with the weight $m g$ of the body?

- $T = m g$
- $T = 0$
- $T > m g$
- $T < m g$

## Explanation

The body accelerates downwards, hence the net force on it points down and $m g - T = m a$ with $a > 0$, which gives $T < m g$. The tension is positive, because the cord must exert on the rim the torque $T R = I\,\alpha$ that spins up the pulley.
