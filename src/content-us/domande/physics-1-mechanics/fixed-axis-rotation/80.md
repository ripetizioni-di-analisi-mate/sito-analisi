A uniform rod of mass $1.5\,\mathrm{kg}$ and length $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$ is pivoted at one end on a frictionless horizontal axis. It is held horizontal and released. What is its angular acceleration at the instant of release?

- $18.4\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $12.3\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $6.13\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $36.8\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$

## Explanation

The torque of the weight about the pivot is $M g\,\dfrac{L}{2}$, and the moment of inertia about the end is $\dfrac{1}{3}ML^2$. By **Newton's second law for rotation**,
$$
\alpha = \dfrac{M g\,\dfrac{L}{2}}{\dfrac{1}{3}M L^2} = \dfrac{3 g}{2 L} = \dfrac{3 \cdot 9.81}{2 \cdot 0.80}\,\mathrm{rad/s^2} = 18.4\,\mathrm{rad/s^2} ,
$$
a value independent of the mass.
