With counterclockwise taken as positive, the graph of $\theta(t)$ for a wheel is a straight line with negative slope. Which statement describes the motion?

- The wheel turns clockwise and is slowing down
- The wheel turns clockwise with constant angular velocity
- The wheel turns counterclockwise with constant angular velocity
- The wheel has a constant negative angular acceleration

## Explanation

The angular velocity is the slope of the graph of $\theta(t)$. A straight line has constant slope, hence $\omega$ is constant and $\alpha = 0$. The slope is negative, therefore $\omega < 0$ and the wheel turns clockwise.
