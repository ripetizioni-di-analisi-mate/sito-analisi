A uniform rod of mass $0.60\,\mathrm{kg}$ and length $1.2\,\mathrm{m}$ rotates about an axis perpendicular to it at $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$ from its center. What is its moment of inertia about that axis?

- $0.126\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.054\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.288\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.072\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$

## Explanation

The **parallel-axis theorem** applies with $I_{\mathrm{cm}} = \dfrac{1}{12}ML^2 = 0.072\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ and $d = 0.30\,\mathrm{m}$, so
$$
I = 0.072\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2} + 0.60\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot (0.30\,\mathrm{m})^2 = 0.126\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2} .
$$
The value $0.288\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ belongs to the axis through one end, at $0.60\,\mathrm{m}$ from the center.
