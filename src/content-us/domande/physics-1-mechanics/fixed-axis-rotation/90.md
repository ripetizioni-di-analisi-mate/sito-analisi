A uniform rod pivoted at one end on a frictionless horizontal axis swings down from the horizontal position. Why does the force exerted by the axis not appear in the energy balance of the rod?

- It is always perpendicular to the weight of the rod
- It is applied at the axis, which does not move, so it does no work
- It is balanced by the weight of the rod at every instant
- It is zero throughout the motion

## Explanation

A force does work only if its point of application moves. The force of the axis acts at points of the axis, which stay at rest, therefore its work is zero and only the weight, which is conservative, does work. The force of the axis is not zero, since it supports the rod and supplies part of the centripetal force on it.
