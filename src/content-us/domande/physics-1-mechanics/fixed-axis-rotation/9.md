How many degrees correspond to an angle of $1.0\,\mathrm{rad}$?

- $0.0175^\circ$
- $57.3^\circ$
- $180^\circ$
- $28.6^\circ$

## Explanation

From $2\pi\,\mathrm{rad} = 360^\circ$ it follows that $1\,\mathrm{rad} = \dfrac{180^\circ}{\pi} = 57.3^\circ$. The factor $\dfrac{\pi}{180} = 0.0175$ converts in the opposite direction, from degrees to radians.
