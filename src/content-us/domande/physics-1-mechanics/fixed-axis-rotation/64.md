What is the lever arm of a force about an axis?

- The perpendicular distance from the axis to the line of action of the force
- The length of the vector that represents the force
- The distance from the point of application of the force to the center of mass
- The distance from the axis to the point of application of the force

## Explanation

The lever arm is $\ell = r\sin\varphi$, the perpendicular distance from the axis to the line of action of the force, so that $\tau = F\,\ell$. It equals the distance $r$ of the point of application only when the force is perpendicular to $\vec{r}$.
