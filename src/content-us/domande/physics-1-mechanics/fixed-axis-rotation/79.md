A body hangs from a cord wound around a pulley of radius $R$. Under which condition are the acceleration $a$ of the body and the angular acceleration $\alpha$ of the pulley related by $a = \alpha R$?

- The axle of the pulley is frictionless
- The mass of the pulley is negligible
- The cord is inextensible and does not slip on the rim
- The body moves with constant velocity

## Explanation

The relation states that the body moves as the point of the rim where the cord leaves the pulley, whose tangential acceleration is $\alpha R$. This holds when the cord keeps its length and does not slide along the rim, whatever the mass of the pulley and the friction of the axle.
