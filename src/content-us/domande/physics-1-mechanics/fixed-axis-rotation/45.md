What is the moment of inertia of a uniform solid disk of mass $4.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ and radius $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$ about the axis through its center, perpendicular to its face?

- $0.60\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.14\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.18\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.36\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$

## Explanation

For a uniform solid disk about this axis,
$$
I = \dfrac{1}{2}\,M R^2 = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 4.0\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot (0.30\,\mathrm{m})^2 = 0.18\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2} .
$$
The value $M R^2 = 0.36\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ belongs to a thin ring of the same mass and radius.
