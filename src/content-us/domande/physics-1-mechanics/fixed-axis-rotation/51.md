What is the moment of inertia of a uniform rectangular plate of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ and sides $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$ and $0.40\,\mathrm{m}$ about the axis through its center, perpendicular to the plate?

- $0.50\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.042\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.082\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.17\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$

## Explanation

For a rectangular plate about the perpendicular axis through its center,
$$
I = \dfrac{1}{12}\,M\,(a^2 + b^2) = \dfrac{1}{12} \cdot 2.0\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot (0.09 + 0.16)\,\mathrm{m^2} = 0.042\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2} .
$$
The sum of the squares of the sides is the square of the diagonal, which differs from the square $(a + b)^2$ of the sum.
