What is the torque about an axis of a force whose line of action meets the axis?

- Zero, whatever the magnitude of the force
- A torque whose sign depends on the direction of the force
- The largest torque that a force of that magnitude can produce
- The product of the force and the distance of its point of application from the axis

## Explanation

The torque is $\tau = F\,\ell$, where the lever arm $\ell$ is the distance from the axis to the line of action of the force. A line of action that meets the axis has $\ell = 0$, therefore the torque is zero, as for a push applied at the hinges of a door.
