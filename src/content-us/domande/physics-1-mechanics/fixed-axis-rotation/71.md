A net torque of $6.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$ acts on a wheel with moment of inertia $1.5\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ about its fixed axis. What is the angular acceleration of the wheel?

- $0.25\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $9.0\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$

## Explanation

By **Newton's second law for rotation** $\tau_{\mathrm{net}} = I\,\alpha$, therefore
$$
\alpha = \dfrac{\tau_{\mathrm{net}}}{I} = \dfrac{6.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}}{1.5\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}} = 4.0\,\mathrm{rad/s^2} .
$$
