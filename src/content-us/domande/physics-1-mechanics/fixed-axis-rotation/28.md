A wheel starts from rest with constant angular acceleration $\alpha = 1.5\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$. How many revolutions does it make in the first $8.0\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $7.6\,\mathrm{rev}$
- $48\,\mathrm{rev}$
- $3.8\,\mathrm{rev}$
- $15\,\mathrm{rev}$

## Explanation

The angular displacement is
$$
\Delta\theta = \dfrac{1}{2}\,\alpha\,t^2 = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 1.5 \cdot 8.0^2\,\mathrm{rad} = 48\,\mathrm{rad} ,
$$
and one revolution is $2\pi\,\mathrm{rad}$, so the wheel makes $\dfrac{48}{2\pi} = 7.6$ revolutions. The number $48$ is the angle in radians.
