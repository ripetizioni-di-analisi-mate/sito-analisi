A hole is drilled in a uniform disk. How is the moment of inertia of the drilled disk about the original axis of the disk obtained?

- By subtracting from that of the full disk the removed mass times the square of the radius of the disk
- By subtracting from that of the full disk the moment of inertia of the removed material about the same axis
- By subtracting from that of the full disk the moment of inertia of the removed material about the axis of the hole
- By multiplying that of the full disk by the ratio of the remaining mass to the original mass

## Explanation

The moment of inertia is a sum over the particles of the body, so the full disk is the drilled disk plus the removed material, both about the same axis. The moment of inertia of the removed material must refer to the axis of the disk, and it is obtained from its value about the axis of the hole with the parallel-axis theorem.
