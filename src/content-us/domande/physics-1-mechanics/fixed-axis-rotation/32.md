Two points $A$ and $B$ of a rotating disk are at distances $r$ and $2r$ from the axis. How do their angular velocities and speeds compare?

- Same angular velocity, and $B$ has twice the speed of $A$
- Same angular velocity, and $B$ has four times the speed of $A$
- Same speed, and $B$ has half the angular velocity of $A$
- $B$ has twice the angular velocity and twice the speed of $A$

## Explanation

All the points of a rigid body turn with the same $\omega$. The speed is $v = \omega r$, proportional to the distance from the axis, therefore the point at $2r$ moves twice as fast as the point at $r$.
