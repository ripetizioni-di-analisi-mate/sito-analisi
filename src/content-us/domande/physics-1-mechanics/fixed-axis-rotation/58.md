A thin ring of mass $0.80\,\mathrm{kg}$ and radius $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$ rotates about an axis perpendicular to its plane through a point of the ring. What is its moment of inertia about that axis?

- $1.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.30\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.20\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.40\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$

## Explanation

About its central axis the ring has $I_{\mathrm{cm}} = M R^2$, and the new axis is at distance $d = R$. By the **parallel-axis theorem**,
$$
I = M R^2 + M R^2 = 2\,M R^2 = 2 \cdot 0.80 \cdot 0.25\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2} = 0.40\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2} .
$$
