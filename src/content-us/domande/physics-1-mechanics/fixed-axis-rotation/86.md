A uniform rod of length $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ turns about a horizontal frictionless axis through one end. It is held horizontal and released from rest. What is the speed of its free end when the rod passes through the vertical position?

- $4.43\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $7.67\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.84\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6.26\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The center of mass descends by $\dfrac{L}{2}$ and the mechanical energy is conserved, so $M g\,\dfrac{L}{2} = \dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{1}{3}M L^2\,\omega^2$, which gives $\omega = \sqrt{\dfrac{3g}{L}} = 3.84\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. The free end is at the distance $L$ from the axis, therefore
$$
v = \omega L = \sqrt{3 g L} = \sqrt{3 \cdot 9.81 \cdot 2.0}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 7.67\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The free end moves faster than a body falling freely through the height $L$, which would reach $\sqrt{2gL} = 6.26\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
