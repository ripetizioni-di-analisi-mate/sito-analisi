At a given instant a point of a rotating wheel has radial acceleration $12\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and tangential acceleration $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the magnitude of its acceleration?

- $13\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $17\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $7.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $11\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The radial and tangential components are perpendicular, therefore
$$
a = \sqrt{a_r^2 + a_t^2} = \sqrt{144 + 25}\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 13\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
The magnitudes of perpendicular components do not add as numbers, so $17\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ overestimates the result.
