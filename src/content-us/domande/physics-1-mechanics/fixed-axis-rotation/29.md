During a braking with constant angular acceleration, the angular velocity of a wheel halves in $2.0\,\mathrm{s}$. How much longer does the wheel take to stop?

- $4.0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $6.0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

With constant $\alpha$ the angular velocity decreases by equal amounts in equal times. In $2.0\,\mathrm{s}$ it decreases by $\dfrac{\omega_0}{2}$, and the remaining $\dfrac{\omega_0}{2}$ is lost in another $2.0\,\mathrm{s}$, so the total braking time is $4.0\,\mathrm{s}$.
