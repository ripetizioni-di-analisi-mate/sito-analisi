A wheel is braked with constant angular acceleration, and its angular velocity halves in $2.0\,\mathrm{s}$. How much longer does the wheel take to stop?

- $4.0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $6.0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

With constant $\alpha$ the angular velocity decreases by equal amounts in equal times. In $2.0\,\mathrm{s}$ it decreases by $\dfrac{\omega_0}{2}$, and the remaining $\dfrac{\omega_0}{2}$ is lost in the same time. The wheel therefore stops $2.0\,\mathrm{s}$ later, after a total braking time of $4.0\,\mathrm{s}$.
