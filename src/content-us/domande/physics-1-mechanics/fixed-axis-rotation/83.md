A constant torque of $5.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$ turns a wheel through $4.0$ revolutions in the direction of the torque. What work does the torque do?

- $62.8\,\mathrm{J}$
- $20.0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $7200\,\mathrm{J}$
- $126\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The work of a constant torque is $W = \tau\,\Delta\theta$ with $\Delta\theta$ in radians. Four revolutions are $8\pi\,\mathrm{rad}$, therefore
$$
W = 5.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m} \cdot 8\pi\,\mathrm{rad} = 126\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
The values $20.0\,\mathrm{J}$ and $7200\,\mathrm{J}$ come from measuring the angle in revolutions and in degrees.
