A uniform rod of mass $1.2\,\mathrm{kg}$ and length $1.0\,\mathrm{m}$ carries a small body of mass $0.50\,\mathrm{kg}$ at one end. What is the moment of inertia about the axis perpendicular to the rod through its other end?

- $0.90\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $1.7\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.40\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.60\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$

## Explanation

The moments of inertia of the parts about the same axis add up. The rod about one end gives $\dfrac{1}{3}ML^2 = 0.40\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ and the small body at distance $L$ gives $m L^2 = 0.50\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$, therefore
$$
I = 0.40\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2} + 0.50\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2} = 0.90\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2} .
$$
