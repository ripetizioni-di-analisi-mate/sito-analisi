A force of given magnitude is applied to a door in the plane perpendicular to the axis of the hinges. Which application gives the largest torque about that axis?

- Near the hinges, perpendicular to the door
- At the handle, perpendicular to the door
- Halfway between hinges and handle, at $45^\circ$ to the door
- At the handle, directed toward the hinges

## Explanation

The torque is $\tau = r F \sin\varphi$. It is largest when the distance $r$ from the axis is largest, at the handle, and when $\sin\varphi = 1$, that is, when the force is perpendicular to the door. A force directed toward the hinges has its line of action through the axis and gives no torque.
