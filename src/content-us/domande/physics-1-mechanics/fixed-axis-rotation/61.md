A force of $25\,\mathrm{N}$ is applied perpendicular to a door at $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$ from the axis of the hinges. What is its torque about that axis?

- $20\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $31\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $16\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$

## Explanation

The force is perpendicular to the door, hence the lever arm is the whole distance $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$ and
$$
\tau = F\,\ell = 25\,\mathrm{N} \cdot 0.80\,\mathrm{m} = 20\,\mathrm{N\cdot m} .
$$
