A point of a wheel at $0.40\,\mathrm{m}$ from the axis travels an arc of $1.0\,\mathrm{m}$. Through what angle has the wheel turned?

- $5.0\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $1.3\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $0.40\,\mathrm{rad}$

## Explanation

The angle in radians is the ratio of the arc to the radius of the circle described by the point, so
$$
\theta = \dfrac{s}{r} = \dfrac{1.0\,\mathrm{m}}{0.40\,\mathrm{m}} = 2.5\,\mathrm{rad} .
$$
The radius is the distance from the axis, $0.40\,\mathrm{m}$. Using twice this distance would give half the angle.
