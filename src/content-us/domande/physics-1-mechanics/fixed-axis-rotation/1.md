A rigid body rotates about a fixed axis. Which statement describes the path of a point of the body that does not lie on the axis?

- A straight line perpendicular to the axis
- A circle lying in a plane that contains the axis
- A circle whose radius changes during the motion
- A circle centered on the axis, in a plane perpendicular to the axis

## Explanation

The body is rigid, hence every point keeps its distance from every point of the axis, which is at rest. A point off the axis therefore moves on a circle centered on the axis, and the plane of this circle is perpendicular to the axis. The radius of the circle is the distance of the point from the axis, which stays constant.
