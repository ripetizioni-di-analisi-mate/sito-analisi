A small body fixed to a rotating platform is moved from $0.20\,\mathrm{m}$ to $0.40\,\mathrm{m}$ from the axis. By what factor is its contribution to the moment of inertia multiplied?

- $\sqrt{2}$
- $8$
- $4$
- $2$

## Explanation

The contribution of a small body is $m r^2$, proportional to the square of its distance from the axis. Doubling the distance therefore multiplies the contribution by $2^2 = 4$.
