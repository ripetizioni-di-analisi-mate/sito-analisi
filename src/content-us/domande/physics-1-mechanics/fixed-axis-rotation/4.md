A turntable turns at $45\,\mathrm{rev/min}$. What is its angular velocity in radians per second?

- $2.36\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $283\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $4.71\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $0.750\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

One revolution is $2\pi\,\mathrm{rad}$ and one minute is $60\,\mathrm{s}$, therefore
$$
\omega = 45 \cdot \dfrac{2\pi\,\mathrm{rad}}{60\,\mathrm{s}} = 4.71\,\mathrm{rad/s} .
$$
Both conversions are required: omitting the factor $2\pi$ gives the rate in revolutions per second, and omitting the division by $60$ leaves the time in minutes.
