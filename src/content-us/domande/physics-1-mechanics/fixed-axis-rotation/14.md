With counterclockwise taken as positive, a wheel turns clockwise and is speeding up. Which signs do $\omega$ and $\alpha$ have?

- $\omega > 0$ and $\alpha < 0$
- $\omega > 0$ and $\alpha > 0$
- $\omega < 0$ and $\alpha < 0$
- $\omega < 0$ and $\alpha > 0$

## Explanation

A clockwise rotation has $\omega < 0$ with the stated convention. The wheel speeds up when $\omega$ and $\alpha$ have the same sign, therefore $\alpha < 0$ as well. A positive $\alpha$ together with a negative $\omega$ would describe a wheel turning clockwise ever more slowly.
