A belt that does not slip joins a pulley of radius $5.0\,\mathrm{cm}$, turning at $30\,\mathrm{rad/s}$, to a pulley of radius $12\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the angular velocity of the larger pulley?

- $5.21\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $72.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $12.5\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $30.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

The belt does not slip, hence the rims of the two pulleys move with the speed of the belt and $\omega_1 r_1 = \omega_2 r_2$. Therefore
$$
\omega_2 = \omega_1\,\dfrac{r_1}{r_2} = 30\,\mathrm{rad/s} \cdot \dfrac{5.0}{12} = 12.5\,\mathrm{rad/s} .
$$
The larger pulley turns more slowly, as the inverse ratio of the radii requires.
