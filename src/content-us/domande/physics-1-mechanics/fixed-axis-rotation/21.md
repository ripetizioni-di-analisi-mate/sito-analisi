A wheel starts from rest with constant angular acceleration $\alpha = 2.0\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$. Through what angle does it turn in the first $6.0\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $36\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $5.7\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $72\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $12\,\mathrm{rad}$

## Explanation

With $\omega_0 = 0$ the angular displacement is
$$
\Delta\theta = \dfrac{1}{2}\,\alpha\,t^2 = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 2.0\,\mathrm{rad/s^2} \cdot (6.0\,\mathrm{s})^2 = 36\,\mathrm{rad} .
$$
Dropping the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$ doubles the result, and $5.7$ is the same angle expressed in revolutions.
