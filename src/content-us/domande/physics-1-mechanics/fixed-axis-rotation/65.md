A force of $60\,\mathrm{N}$ is applied at a point $0.25\,\mathrm{m}$ from an axis, and its line of action passes at a perpendicular distance of $0.15\,\mathrm{m}$ from the axis. What is its torque about the axis?

- $6.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $9.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $24\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$

## Explanation

The lever arm is the perpendicular distance from the axis to the line of action, $\ell = 0.15\,\mathrm{m}$, therefore $\tau = F\,\ell = 60\,\mathrm{N} \cdot 0.15\,\mathrm{m} = 9.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$. The distance $0.25\,\mathrm{m}$ of the point of application would be the lever arm only for a force perpendicular to $\vec{r}$.
