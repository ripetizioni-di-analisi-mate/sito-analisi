A wheel starting from rest with constant angular acceleration turns through the angle $\theta_1$ in the time $t$. Through what angle does it turn, starting from rest, in the time $2t$?

- $4\,\theta_1$
- $2\,\theta_1$
- $\sqrt{2}\,\theta_1$
- $8\,\theta_1$

## Explanation

Starting from rest, the angular displacement is $\theta = \dfrac{1}{2}\,\alpha\,t^2$, proportional to the square of the time. Doubling the time therefore multiplies the angle by $2^2 = 4$.
