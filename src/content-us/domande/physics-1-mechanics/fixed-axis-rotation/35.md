A wheel of radius $0.40\,\mathrm{m}$ has angular acceleration $\alpha = 5.0\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$. What is the tangential acceleration of a point of the rim?

- $0.80\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $13\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The tangential component of the acceleration of a point at distance $r$ from the axis is $a_t = \alpha\,r = 5.0\,\mathrm{rad/s^2} \cdot 0.40\,\mathrm{m} = 2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The distance is the radius $0.40\,\mathrm{m}$, while the diameter would double the result.
