A wheel of radius $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$ turns through $60^\circ$. What arc does a point of the rim travel?

- $0.262\,\mathrm{m}$
- $30.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.05\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.524\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The relation $s = r\theta$ holds with $\theta$ in radians, and $60^\circ = \dfrac{\pi}{3}\,\mathrm{rad}$. Therefore
$$
s = 0.50\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \dfrac{\pi}{3} = 0.524\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
Substituting the number $60$ in place of the angle in radians gives $30\,\mathrm{m}$, a length larger than the whole circumference $3.14\,\mathrm{m}$.
