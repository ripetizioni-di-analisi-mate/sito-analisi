A uniform rod of mass $M$ and length $L$ is pivoted at one end and held horizontal. What is the magnitude of the torque of its weight about the pivot?

- $0$
- $M g L$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,M g L$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}\,M g L$

## Explanation

The weight of the rod gives the same torque as a single force $M g$ applied at the center of mass, which is at the horizontal distance $\dfrac{L}{2}$ from the pivot. The weight is vertical, hence the lever arm is $\dfrac{L}{2}$ and $\tau = M g\,\dfrac{L}{2}$.
