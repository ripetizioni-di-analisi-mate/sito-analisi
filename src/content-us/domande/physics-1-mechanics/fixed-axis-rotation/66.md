With counterclockwise taken as positive, two forces act on a wheel: one has torque of magnitude $8.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$ tending to turn it counterclockwise, the other has torque of magnitude $12\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$ tending to turn it clockwise. What is the net torque about the axis?

- $-4.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $+20\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $+4.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$

## Explanation

The net torque is the algebraic sum of the torques with their signs, so $\tau_{\mathrm{net}} = +8.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m} - 12\,\mathrm{N\cdot m} = -4.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$. The negative sign means that the net torque tends to turn the wheel clockwise.
