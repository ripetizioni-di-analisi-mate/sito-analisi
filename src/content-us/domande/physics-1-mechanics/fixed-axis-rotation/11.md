A wheel has angular position $\theta(t) = 3.0\,t^2 + 2.0\,t$, with $\theta$ in radians and $t$ in seconds. What is its angular velocity at $t = 2.0\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $12\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $8.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $14\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $16\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

The angular velocity is the derivative of the angular position, $\omega(t) = \dfrac{d\theta}{dt} = 6.0\,t + 2.0$. At $t = 2.0\,\mathrm{s}$ it is $\omega = 12 + 2.0 = 14\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. The value $16$ is the angular position $\theta(2.0)$ in radians, and $8.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ is the average angular velocity over the first two seconds.
