A disk turns with constant angular velocity. Two points $A$ and $B$ are at distances $r$ and $2r$ from the axis. What is the ratio $\dfrac{a_B}{a_A}$ of their centripetal accelerations?

- $2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $4$
- $1$

## Explanation

The two points have the same $\omega$, and the centripetal acceleration is $a_r = \omega^2 r$, proportional to $r$. The ratio is therefore $\dfrac{\omega^2 \cdot 2r}{\omega^2 r} = 2$. The same result follows from $\dfrac{v^2}{r}$, since the speed of $B$ is twice that of $A$ and its radius is also twice as large.
