A wheel has angular position $\theta(t) = 8.0\,t - 1.0\,t^2$, with $\theta$ in radians and $t$ in seconds. At what instant does the wheel stop momentarily?

- $2.0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2.8\,\mathrm{s}$
- $8.0\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The wheel stops when its angular velocity vanishes. From $\omega(t) = \dfrac{d\theta}{dt} = 8.0 - 2.0\,t$, the condition $\omega = 0$ gives $t = 4.0\,\mathrm{s}$. At $t = 8.0\,\mathrm{s}$ the angular position returns to zero, which is a different condition.
