A block on a frictionless horizontal surface is attached to two springs, of constants $100\,\mathrm{N/m}$ and $300\,\mathrm{N/m}$, fixed to walls on opposite sides. Both springs are at their natural length when the block is at $x = 0$. What is the total elastic potential energy when the block is at $x = 0.10\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $0.50\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1.0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

One spring is stretched and the other is compressed by $0.10\,\mathrm{m}$, and both store positive energy. The total is $\dfrac{1}{2}(100 + 300)\,\mathrm{N/m} \cdot (0.10\,\mathrm{m})^2 = 2.0\,\mathrm{J}$.
