A skateboarder moves at $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at the bottom of a frictionless ramp. What is the speed of the skateboarder at the height $1.0\,\mathrm{m}$ above the bottom?

- $5.12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.57\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4.05\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $7.46\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Conservation of mechanical energy gives $v^2 = v_0^2 - 2gh = 36\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2} - 19.6\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2} = 16.4\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}$, hence $v = 4.05\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The kinetic energy decreases because the skateboarder rises, so the term $2gh$ is subtracted.
