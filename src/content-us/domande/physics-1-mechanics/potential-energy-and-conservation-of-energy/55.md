A block of mass $3.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ slides $4.0\,\mathrm{m}$ on a horizontal floor with $\mu_k = 0.20$. How much mechanical energy is transformed into thermal energy?

- $2.40\,\mathrm{J}$
- $11.8\,\mathrm{J}$
- $118\,\mathrm{J}$
- $23.5\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The friction force is $f_k = \mu_k mg = 0.20 \cdot 3.0\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 5.89\,\mathrm{N}$. The energy transformed is $f_k s = 5.89\,\mathrm{N} \cdot 4.0\,\mathrm{m} = 23.5\,\mathrm{J}$.
