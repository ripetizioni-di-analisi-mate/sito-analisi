One spring is stretched by $0.10\,\mathrm{m}$ and an identical spring is compressed by $0.10\,\mathrm{m}$. How do their elastic potential energies compare, with the reference at the natural length?

- They are equal and positive
- They are opposite, the compressed spring having negative energy
- They are opposite, the stretched spring having negative energy
- Only the stretched spring stores energy

## Explanation

The energy is $U = \dfrac{1}{2}kx^2$, in which the deformation appears squared. A deformation $x = -0.10\,\mathrm{m}$ and a deformation $x = +0.10\,\mathrm{m}$ therefore give the same positive value.
