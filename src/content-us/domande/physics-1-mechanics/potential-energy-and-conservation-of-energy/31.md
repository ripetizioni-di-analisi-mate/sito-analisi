The force needed to hold a spring deformed by $x$ is proportional to $x$ and reaches $6.0\,\mathrm{N}$ at $x = 0.30\,\mathrm{m}$. What work is needed to deform the spring from $x = 0$ to $x = 0.30\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $1.8\,\mathrm{J}$
- $20\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0.90\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0.27\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The work is the area of the triangle under the graph of the force, $\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 6.0\,\mathrm{N} \cdot 0.30\,\mathrm{m} = 0.90\,\mathrm{J}$. The product $6.0\,\mathrm{N} \cdot 0.30\,\mathrm{m}$ would be correct only for a force that is constant during the deformation.
