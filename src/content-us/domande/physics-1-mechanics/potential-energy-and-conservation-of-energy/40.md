A roller coaster car passes the top of a hill, at the height $10\,\mathrm{m}$, with the speed $8.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Friction is negligible. What is its speed at a point at the height $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $14.9\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $12.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $20.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Conservation of mechanical energy gives $v^2 = v_1^2 + 2g\,(y_1 - y_2) = 64\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2} + 2 \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot 8.0\,\mathrm{m} = 221\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}$, hence $v = 14.9\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The kinetic energies add, while the speeds $8.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $12.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ do not.
