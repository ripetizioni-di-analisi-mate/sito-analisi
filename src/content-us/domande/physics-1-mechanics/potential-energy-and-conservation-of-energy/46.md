A block slides on a frictionless horizontal surface with the speed $v$ and runs into a spring, which it compresses by at most $x_{\max}$. What is the speed of the block when the compression is $\dfrac{x_{\max}}{2}$?

- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,v$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,v$
- $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\,v$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}\,v$

## Explanation

The spring energy at half compression is $\dfrac{1}{4}$ of its maximum $\dfrac{1}{2}mv^2$, hence the kinetic energy left is $\dfrac{3}{4}$ of $\dfrac{1}{2}mv^2$. The speed is therefore $\sqrt{\dfrac{3}{4}}\,v = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,v$.
