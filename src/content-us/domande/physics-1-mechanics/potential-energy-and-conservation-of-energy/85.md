A block of mass $0.30\,\mathrm{kg}$ hangs at rest from a vertical spring of constant $60\,\mathrm{N/m}$. By how much is the spring stretched?

- $9.8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.50\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $4.9\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The equilibrium is the minimum of $U(y) = \dfrac{1}{2}ky^2 - mgy$, where $ky = mg$, hence $y_0 = \dfrac{mg}{k} = \dfrac{0.30\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}}{60\,\mathrm{N/m}} = 0.049\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $9.8\,\mathrm{cm}$ is the lowest point reached by a block released from the natural length.
