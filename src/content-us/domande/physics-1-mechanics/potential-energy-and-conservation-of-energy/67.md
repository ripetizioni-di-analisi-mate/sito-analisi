In a region where the potential energy $U(x)$ of a body increases with $x$, what is the sign of the force component $F_x$?

- Positive
- Zero
- It has the sign of $U(x)$
- Negative

## Explanation

The force is $F_x = -\dfrac{dU}{dx}$, and the derivative is positive where $U$ increases, hence $F_x < 0$. The force always points towards lower potential energy.
