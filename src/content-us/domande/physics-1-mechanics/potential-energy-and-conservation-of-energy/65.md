Kinetic friction slows a block sliding on a floor. Which quantity is conserved during the process?

- The mechanical energy of the block
- The total energy, mechanical plus thermal
- The kinetic energy of the block
- The thermal energy of the block and the floor

## Explanation

The mechanical energy lost to friction, $f_k s$, appears as thermal energy of the block and of the floor. The sum of the two forms is conserved, while the mechanical energy decreases and the thermal energy increases.
