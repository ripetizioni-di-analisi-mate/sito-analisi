A small body travels once around a closed circuit on a frictionless track, acted on only by its weight and by the normal force of the track. How does its final speed compare with its initial speed?

- It is smaller
- It is larger
- It is equal
- It is zero

## Explanation

The weight is conservative, so its work around a closed path is zero, and the normal force is perpendicular to the velocity, so it does no work. The total work is zero, hence $\Delta K = 0$ and the speed is the same.
