Which property defines a conservative force acting on a body?

- The work it does along every path is zero
- It is perpendicular to the displacement at every instant
- Its magnitude stays constant during the motion
- The work it does between two positions is the same for every path joining them

## Explanation

A force is conservative when its work from a position $1$ to a position $2$ does not depend on the path followed. Its work vanishes only around closed paths, and kinetic friction has constant magnitude and is still nonconservative.
