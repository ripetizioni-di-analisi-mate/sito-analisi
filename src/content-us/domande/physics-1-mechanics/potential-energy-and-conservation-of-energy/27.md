A spring stores $3.0\,\mathrm{J}$ when it is compressed by $x$. What energy does it store when it is compressed by $3x$?

- $27\,\mathrm{J}$
- $9.0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $81\,\mathrm{J}$
- $5.2\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The energy $U = \dfrac{1}{2}kx^2$ grows with the square of the deformation, so tripling $x$ multiplies $U$ by $9$. The new energy is $9 \cdot 3.0\,\mathrm{J} = 27\,\mathrm{J}$.
