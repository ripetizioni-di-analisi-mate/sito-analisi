Two blocks of masses $m$ and $2m$, made of the same material, slide on the same rough horizontal floor with the same initial speed. How does the stopping distance of the heavier block compare with that of the lighter one?

- It is twice as large
- It is half as large
- It is equal
- It is four times as large

## Explanation

The stopping distance is $d = \dfrac{v_0^2}{2\mu_k g}$, since the mass appears both in the kinetic energy and in the friction force and cancels. Blocks with the same $\mu_k$ and the same initial speed stop after the same distance.
