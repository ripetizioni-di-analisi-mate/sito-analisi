A body is released from rest at the height $5.0\,\mathrm{m}$ above the lowest point of a frictionless track. What is its speed at the lowest point?

- $9.90\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $7.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.16\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $98.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Conservation of mechanical energy gives $mgh = \dfrac{1}{2}mv^2$, hence $v = \sqrt{2gh} = \sqrt{2 \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot 5.0\,\mathrm{m}} = 9.90\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The value $98.1$ is $v^2$ in $\mathrm{m^2/s^2}$, before the square root.
