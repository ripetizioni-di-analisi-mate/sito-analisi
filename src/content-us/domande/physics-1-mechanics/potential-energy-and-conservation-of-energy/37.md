A body released from rest at the height $h$ on a frictionless track reaches the lowest point with the speed $v$. What speed does it reach at the same point when it is released from rest at the height $4h$?

- $2v$
- $4v$
- $\sqrt{2}\,v$
- $16v$

## Explanation

Conservation of mechanical energy gives $v = \sqrt{2gh}$, so the speed is proportional to the square root of the drop. Multiplying the drop by $4$ multiplies the speed by $\sqrt{4} = 2$.
