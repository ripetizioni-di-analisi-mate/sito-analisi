Two identical boxes descend the same drop $h$, one falling vertically and the other sliding along a frictionless ramp of length $2h$. How does the work of the weight on the box on the ramp compare with the work on the falling box?

- It is twice as large
- It is equal
- It is half as large
- It depends on the angle of the ramp

## Explanation

The work of the weight is $mgh$ for every path with the drop $h$, since the horizontal part of the displacement is perpendicular to the weight. The longer ramp is compensated by the smaller component of the weight along it.
