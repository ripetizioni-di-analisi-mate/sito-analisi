Two springs $A$ and $B$ have constants $k_A = 2k_B$. Each of them is stretched and held by a force of the same magnitude $F$. What is the ratio $\dfrac{U_A}{U_B}$ of their elastic potential energies?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $2$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $4$

## Explanation

Each deformation is $x = \dfrac{F}{k}$, hence $U = \dfrac{1}{2}kx^2 = \dfrac{F^2}{2k}$. The energy is inversely proportional to $k$, and the stiffer spring stores half as much energy.
