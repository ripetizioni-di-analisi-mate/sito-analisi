A body reaches a turning point at which $\dfrac{dU}{dx} \neq 0$. What happens to the body there?

- Its speed is zero and it reverses the sense of its motion
- Its speed is zero and it stays at rest
- Its speed is the largest of the motion
- Its acceleration is zero

## Explanation

At a turning point $U = E$, hence $K = 0$ and the speed vanishes. The force $-\dfrac{dU}{dx}$ is not zero there, so the body does not stay at rest and starts moving back.
