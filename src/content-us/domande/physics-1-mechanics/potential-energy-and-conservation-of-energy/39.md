A ball is thrown with the speed $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at $40^\circ$ above the horizontal. Air resistance is negligible. What is its speed when it is $3.0\,\mathrm{m}$ above the launch point?

- $10.7\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $14.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.798\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $9.23\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Conservation of mechanical energy gives $v^2 = v_0^2 - 2gh = 144\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2} - 2 \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot 3.0\,\mathrm{m} = 85.1\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}$, hence $v = 9.23\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The launch angle does not appear, because the kinetic energy depends on the whole speed.
