A block is launched up a rough incline, stops, and slides back down to its starting point. How does its speed on return compare with its launch speed?

- It is equal
- It is larger
- It depends on the mass of the block
- It is smaller

## Explanation

Friction does negative work on both the way up and the way down, while the potential energy at the start and at the end is the same. The kinetic energy on return is therefore smaller than at launch.
