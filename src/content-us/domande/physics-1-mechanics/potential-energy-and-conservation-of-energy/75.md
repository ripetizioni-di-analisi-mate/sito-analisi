A body moves along the $x$ axis with potential energy $U(x) = 3x^2$, with $U$ in joules and $x$ in meters, and with the mechanical energy $12\,\mathrm{J}$. Where are its turning points?

- $x = \pm 4.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $x = \pm 2.8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $x = \pm 2.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $x = \pm 1.4\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The turning points satisfy $U(x) = E$, that is $3x^2 = 12$, hence $x^2 = 4.0\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and $x = \pm 2.0\,\mathrm{m}$. Forgetting the square root gives $\pm 4.0\,\mathrm{m}$.
