The mass of a falling body is doubled and the drop is halved. How does the work of the weight change?

- It stays the same
- It doubles
- It is halved
- It quadruples

## Explanation

The work of the weight is $mgh$. Doubling $m$ and halving $h$ leaves the product $mh$ unchanged, hence the work is the same.
