A body moves along the $x$ axis with potential energy $U(x) = 6 - 3x$, with $U$ in joules and $x$ in meters. What is the force component $F_x$ at $x = 2.0\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $3.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-3.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $6.0\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The force is $F_x = -\dfrac{dU}{dx} = -(-3) = 3.0\,\mathrm{N}$ at every position. At $x = 2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ the value of $U$ is zero, while the force depends on the slope of $U$ and is not zero.
