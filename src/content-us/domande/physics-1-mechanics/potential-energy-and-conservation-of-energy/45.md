A ball falls from rest from the height $h$ above the ground. Air resistance is negligible and the ground is the reference level. At what height are its kinetic energy and its gravitational potential energy equal?

- $\dfrac{h}{4}$
- $\dfrac{h}{\sqrt{2}}$
- $\dfrac{h}{2}$
- $\dfrac{h}{3}$

## Explanation

The mechanical energy is $mgh$ at every point. Where $K = U$, each of them is half of it, so $mgy = \dfrac{1}{2}mgh$ and $y = \dfrac{h}{2}$.
