A spring of constant $800\,\mathrm{N/m}$ is compressed by $0.050\,\mathrm{m}$. What energy is stored in the spring?

- $2.0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $40\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1.0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1.0 \cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The elastic potential energy is
$$
U = \frac{1}{2}kx^2 = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 800\,\mathrm{N/m} \cdot (0.050\,\mathrm{m})^2 = 1.0\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
The product $kx = 40\,\mathrm{N}$ is the force of the spring, not its energy.
