A block of mass $0.20\,\mathrm{kg}$ hangs at rest from a vertical spring of constant $40\,\mathrm{N/m}$. It is pulled $0.050\,\mathrm{m}$ further down. By how much does the total potential energy, elastic plus gravitational, increase?

- $0.148\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0.0981\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0.0500\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-0.0500\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Measured from the equilibrium position, the total potential energy is $\dfrac{1}{2}ku^2$ plus a constant, hence the increase is $\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 40\,\mathrm{N/m} \cdot (0.050\,\mathrm{m})^2 = 0.0500\,\mathrm{J}$. The elastic energy alone grows by $0.148\,\mathrm{J}$, and the gravitational energy decreases by $0.0981\,\mathrm{J}$.
