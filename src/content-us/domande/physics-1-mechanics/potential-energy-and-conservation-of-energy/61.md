A motor raises a crate of mass $50\,\mathrm{kg}$ through the height $4.0\,\mathrm{m}$, starting from rest and ending with the speed $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Friction is negligible. What work does the motor do?

- $2.86\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $1.96\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $1.06\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $3.76\,\mathrm{kJ}$

## Explanation

The energy equation gives $W_{\text{nc}} = \Delta K + \Delta U = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 50\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot (6.0\,\mathrm{m/s})^2 + 50\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot 4.0\,\mathrm{m}$. This is $900\,\mathrm{J} + 1962\,\mathrm{J} = 2.86\,\mathrm{kJ}$.
