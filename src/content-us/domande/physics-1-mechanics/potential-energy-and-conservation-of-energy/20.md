A hiker of mass $60\,\mathrm{kg}$ climbs a staircase that rises three floors, each $3.0\,\mathrm{m}$ high. What is the change of the hiker's gravitational potential energy?

- $1.77\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $0.540\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $5.30\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $-5.30\,\mathrm{kJ}$

## Explanation

The total rise is $3 \cdot 3.0\,\mathrm{m} = 9.0\,\mathrm{m}$, therefore $\Delta U = mgh = 60\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot 9.0\,\mathrm{m} = 5.30 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{J}$. The change is positive because the hiker ends higher.
