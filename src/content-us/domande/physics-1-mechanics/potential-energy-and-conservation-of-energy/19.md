A box of mass $4.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ is carried $5.0\,\mathrm{m}$ horizontally at constant height. What is the work of its weight?

- $196\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-196\,\mathrm{J}$
- $20\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The weight is vertical and the displacement is horizontal, so $\vec{w} \cdot d\vec{s} = 0$ at every step. Equivalently, the height does not change, hence $\Delta U = 0$ and $W = -\Delta U = 0$.
