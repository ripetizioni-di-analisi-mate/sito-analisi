A body at the height $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$ above the ground has the gravitational potential energy $49\,\mathrm{J}$, with the ground as the reference level. What is its mass?

- $19.6\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.50\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

From $U = mgh$,
$$
m = \frac{U}{gh} = \frac{49\,\mathrm{J}}{9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot 2.5\,\mathrm{m}} = 2.0\,\mathrm{kg} .
$$
Dividing by $h$ alone gives a force in newtons, which is not a mass.
