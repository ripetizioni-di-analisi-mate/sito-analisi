A block slides on a rough horizontal floor and stops after the distance $d$. If its initial speed were doubled on the same floor, what would its stopping distance be?

- $2d$
- $\sqrt{2}\,d$
- $4d$
- $8d$

## Explanation

The energy equation gives $\mu_k mg\,d = \dfrac{1}{2}mv_0^2$, so the stopping distance is proportional to $v_0^2$. Doubling the speed multiplies the distance by $4$.
