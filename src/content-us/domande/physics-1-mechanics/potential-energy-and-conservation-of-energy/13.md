A box of mass $3.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ is raised by $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the change of its gravitational potential energy?

- $6.0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $29.4\,\mathrm{J}$
- $58.9\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-58.9\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The change is $\Delta U = mgh = 3.0\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot 2.0\,\mathrm{m} = 58.9\,\mathrm{J}$. It is positive because the box ends higher, and no factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$ appears in $mgh$.
