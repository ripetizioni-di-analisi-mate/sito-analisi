The work needed to compress a spring from its natural length to the deformation $x$ is $W_0$. What work is needed to compress it further, from $x$ to $2x$?

- $W_0$
- $3W_0$
- $2W_0$
- $4W_0$

## Explanation

The stored energy goes from $\dfrac{1}{2}kx^2 = W_0$ to $\dfrac{1}{2}k\,(2x)^2 = 4W_0$. The second stage therefore costs $4W_0 - W_0 = 3W_0$.
