A body moves along the $x$ axis with potential energy $U(x) = 4x^2$, with $U$ in joules and $x$ in meters. What is the force component $F_x$ at $x = 0.50\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $-4.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-1.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-2.0\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The force is $F_x = -\dfrac{dU}{dx} = -8x$, hence $F_x(0.50\,\mathrm{m}) = -4.0\,\mathrm{N}$. The value $1.0\,\mathrm{J}$ is $U$ at that position, which is different from the force.
