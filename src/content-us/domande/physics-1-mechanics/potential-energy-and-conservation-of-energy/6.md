A force does the work $12\,\mathrm{J}$ on a body that moves from $A$ to $B$ along a path $\gamma_1$, and the work $8.0\,\mathrm{J}$ along a different path $\gamma_2$ from $A$ to $B$. What is its work around the closed path that goes from $A$ to $B$ along $\gamma_1$ and returns to $A$ along $\gamma_2$?

- $20\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-4.0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Traveling $\gamma_2$ in the opposite sense reverses the sign of its work, hence $W_{\text{closed}} = 12\,\mathrm{J} - 8.0\,\mathrm{J} = 4.0\,\mathrm{J}$. The work around the closed path is not zero, therefore the force is nonconservative.
