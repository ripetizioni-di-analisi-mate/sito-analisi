A body of mass $m$ falls from rest, and air resistance is negligible. Which graph describes its kinetic energy $K$ as a function of the distance $d$ fallen?

- A straight line through the origin with slope $mg$
- A parabola through the origin
- A straight line through the origin with slope $g$
- A horizontal line

## Explanation

Conservation of mechanical energy gives $K = mgd$, which is linear in $d$ with slope $mg$. The kinetic energy is a parabola as a function of time, since $d$ itself grows with the square of the time.
