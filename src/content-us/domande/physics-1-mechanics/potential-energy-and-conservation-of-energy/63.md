A box is dragged from $A$ to $B$ across a rough floor along a curved path of length $8.0\,\mathrm{m}$, while the straight segment $AB$ is $5.0\,\mathrm{m}$ long. The friction force has constant magnitude $3.0\,\mathrm{N}$. What is the work of friction along the curved path?

- $-24\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-15\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $+24\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The work of friction is $-f_k s$, where $s$ is the length of the path actually traveled, hence $W = -3.0\,\mathrm{N} \cdot 8.0\,\mathrm{m} = -24\,\mathrm{J}$. The straight segment would give $-15\,\mathrm{J}$, which shows that the work depends on the path.
