A body moves along the $x$ axis with potential energy $U(x) = (x^2 - 1)^2$, with $U$ in joules and $x$ in meters. It is released from rest at $x = 1.3\,\mathrm{m}$. Does it reach $x = -1\,\mathrm{m}$?

- It does, since $E = 0.48\,\mathrm{J}$ is larger than $U(1) = 0$
- It does, since the force at $x = 1.3\,\mathrm{m}$ points towards negative $x$
- It does, since $U(x)$ is symmetric about $x = 0$
- It does not, since $E = 0.48\,\mathrm{J}$ is smaller than $U(0) = 1\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Released from rest, the body has $E = U(1.3\,\mathrm{m}) = (1.69 - 1)^2\,\mathrm{J} = 0.48\,\mathrm{J}$. To reach the left well it would have to cross $x = 0$, where $U = 1\,\mathrm{J} > E$, hence it oscillates inside the right well.
