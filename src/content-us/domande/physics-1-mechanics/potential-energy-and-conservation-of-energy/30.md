A spring of constant $300\,\mathrm{N/m}$ is stretched from $0.10\,\mathrm{m}$ to $0.20\,\mathrm{m}$. What work does the spring force do on the body attached to it?

- $+4.5\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-1.5\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-6.0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-4.5\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The work of the spring force is
$$
W = \frac{1}{2}k\,(x_1^2 - x_2^2) = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 300\,\mathrm{N/m} \cdot (0.010 - 0.040)\,\mathrm{m^2} = -4.5\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
It is negative because the spring pulls against the stretching, and it involves the difference of the squares, which is different from $(x_2 - x_1)^2$.
