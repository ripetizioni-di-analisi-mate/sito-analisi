A body of mass $m$ near the Earth's surface has the potential energy $U(y) = mgy$, with $y$ measured upwards. What does the relation $F_y = -\dfrac{dU}{dy}$ give?

- $F_y = mg$
- $F_y = -mgy$
- $F_y = -mg$
- $F_y = -g$

## Explanation

The derivative of $mgy$ with respect to $y$ is $mg$, therefore $F_y = -mg$. This is the weight, directed downwards because the $y$ axis points upwards.
