Two balls of masses $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $4.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ are released from rest at the same height on two frictionless tracks of different shapes, whose lowest points are at the same height. How do their speeds at the lowest points compare?

- The heavier ball is twice as fast
- The heavier ball is four times as fast
- They are equal
- They depend on the shapes of the tracks

## Explanation

From $mgh = \dfrac{1}{2}mv^2$ the mass cancels and $v = \sqrt{2gh}$. The normal force does no work, so the shape of the track does not enter either.
