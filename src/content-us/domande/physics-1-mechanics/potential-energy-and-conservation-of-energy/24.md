A spring of constant $400\,\mathrm{N/m}$ is stretched by $5.0\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the magnitude of the force exerted by the spring?

- $2000\,\mathrm{N}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.50\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Converting the deformation to meters, $x = 0.050\,\mathrm{m}$, and by Hooke's law $|F| = kx = 400\,\mathrm{N/m} \cdot 0.050\,\mathrm{m} = 20\,\mathrm{N}$. The value $0.50$ is the stored energy in joules, $\dfrac{1}{2}kx^2$.
