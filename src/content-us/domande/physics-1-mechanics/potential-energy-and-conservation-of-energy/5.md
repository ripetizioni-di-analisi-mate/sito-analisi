A box is dragged $3.0\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right along a floor and then $3.0\,\mathrm{m}$ back to its starting position. The kinetic friction force has constant magnitude $5.0\,\mathrm{N}$. What is the total work done by friction?

- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-15\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-30\,\mathrm{J}$
- $+30\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Friction is opposite to the displacement on both legs, so each leg contributes $-f_k s = -5.0\,\mathrm{N} \cdot 3.0\,\mathrm{m} = -15\,\mathrm{J}$ and the total is $-30\,\mathrm{J}$. The work is not zero although the box returns to its starting position, which shows that friction is nonconservative.
