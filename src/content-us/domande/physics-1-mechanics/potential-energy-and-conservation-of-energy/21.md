A graph shows the gravitational potential energy $U$ of a body of mass $m$ as a function of its height $y$, measured upwards. What is the slope of the line?

- $mg$
- $-mg$
- $g$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}mg$

## Explanation

The potential energy is $U(y) = mgy$, a straight line through the origin whose slope is $\dfrac{dU}{dy} = mg$. The slope is positive because $U$ grows with the height.
