A body of mass $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ rests at the bottom of a pit, $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ below the reference level of the gravitational potential energy. What is its mechanical energy?

- $19.6\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-9.81\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-19.6\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The body is at rest, so $K = 0$, and its height is $y = -2.0\,\mathrm{m}$, hence $E = mgy = 1.0\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot (-2.0\,\mathrm{m}) = -19.6\,\mathrm{J}$. The mechanical energy is negative because the potential energy below the reference level is negative.
