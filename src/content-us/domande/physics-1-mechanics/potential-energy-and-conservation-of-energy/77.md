A body of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ moves along the $x$ axis with the mechanical energy $10\,\mathrm{J}$. What is its speed at a position where $U = 5.5\,\mathrm{J}$?

- $3.16\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.50\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.94\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.12\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The kinetic energy is $K = E - U = 4.5\,\mathrm{J}$, hence
$$
v = \sqrt{\frac{2K}{m}} = \sqrt{\frac{2 \cdot 4.5\,\mathrm{J}}{2.0\,\mathrm{kg}}} = 2.12\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
