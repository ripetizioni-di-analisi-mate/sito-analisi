A conservative force acts on a body that travels once around a closed path and returns to its starting position. What is the work done by that force?

- Zero
- Positive and proportional to the length of the path
- Negative and proportional to the length of the path
- It depends on the starting position

## Explanation

The work of a conservative force is the same for every path between two positions, and this is equivalent to zero work around every closed path. A work proportional to the length of the path is the behavior of kinetic friction, which is nonconservative.
