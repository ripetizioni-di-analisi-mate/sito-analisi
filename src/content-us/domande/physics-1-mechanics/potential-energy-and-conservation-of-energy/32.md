A body attached to a spring of constant $200\,\mathrm{N/m}$ moves from $x = -0.10\,\mathrm{m}$ to $x = +0.10\,\mathrm{m}$, with $x$ measured from the natural length. What work does the spring force do?

- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-4.0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $+4.0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-1.0\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The work is $U_1 - U_2 = \dfrac{1}{2}k\,(0.10\,\mathrm{m})^2 - \dfrac{1}{2}k\,(0.10\,\mathrm{m})^2 = 0$. The spring does positive work while it returns to its natural length and an equal negative work while it is stretched.
