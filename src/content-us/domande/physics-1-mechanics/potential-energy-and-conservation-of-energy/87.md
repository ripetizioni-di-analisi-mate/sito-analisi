A block of mass $m$ hangs from a vertical spring of constant $k$, and $y$ is measured downwards from the natural length. Which function is the total potential energy, with the reference at $y = 0$?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}ky^2 + mgy$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}ky^2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}ky^2 - mgy$
- $-\dfrac{1}{2}ky^2 - mgy$

## Explanation

The potential energies of conservative forces add. The elastic part is $\dfrac{1}{2}ky^2$, and the gravitational part is $-mgy$ because the body goes down as $y$ increases.
