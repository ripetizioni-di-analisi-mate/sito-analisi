A body of given mass moves between two given heights. On what does the change $\Delta U$ of its gravitational potential energy depend?

- On the reference level but not on the path
- On the path but not on the reference level
- On both the reference level and the path
- Neither on the reference level nor on the path

## Explanation

The change is $\Delta U = mg\,(y_2 - y_1)$. The height of the reference level cancels in the subtraction, and the weight is conservative, so the path does not enter either.
