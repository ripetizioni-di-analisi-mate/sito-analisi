A crate of mass $20\,\mathrm{kg}$ is pulled at constant speed up a rough incline and rises $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$. Friction does the work $-40\,\mathrm{J}$ on the crate. What work does the pulling force do?

- $334\,\mathrm{J}$
- $294\,\mathrm{J}$
- $254\,\mathrm{J}$
- $70\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The speed is constant, so $\Delta K = 0$ and the energy equation gives $W_{\text{pull}} + W_{\text{f}} = \Delta U = 20\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot 1.5\,\mathrm{m} = 294\,\mathrm{J}$. Hence $W_{\text{pull}} = 294\,\mathrm{J} + 40\,\mathrm{J} = 334\,\mathrm{J}$.
