A body moves along the $x$ axis with potential energy $U(x) = 2x^3 - 6x$, with $U$ in joules and $x$ in meters. Where is its position of stable equilibrium?

- $x = 1.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $x = -1.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $x = 0$
- $x = 1.7\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The derivative $\dfrac{dU}{dx} = 6x^2 - 6$ vanishes at $x = \pm 1.0\,\mathrm{m}$, and $\dfrac{d^2U}{dx^2} = 12x$ is positive only at $x = 1.0\,\mathrm{m}$. That position is a minimum of $U$, hence it is the stable one, while $x = -1.0\,\mathrm{m}$ is unstable.
