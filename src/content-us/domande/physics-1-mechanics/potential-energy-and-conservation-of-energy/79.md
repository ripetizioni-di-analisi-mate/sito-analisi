A body moves along the $x$ axis with potential energy $U(x) = x^4 - 4x^2$, with $U$ in joules and $x$ in meters. How are its positions of equilibrium classified?

- $x = \pm\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{m}$ unstable, $x = 0$ stable
- $x = \pm 2\,\mathrm{m}$ stable, $x = 0$ unstable
- $x = \pm\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{m}$ stable, $x = 0$ unstable
- $x = \pm\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{m}$ and $x = 0$ all stable

## Explanation

The derivative $\dfrac{dU}{dx} = 4x\,(x^2 - 2)$ vanishes at $x = 0$ and $x = \pm\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{m}$, and $\dfrac{d^2U}{dx^2} = 12x^2 - 8$ is $-8$ at $x = 0$ and $16$ at $x = \pm\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{m}$. The outer positions are therefore stable and the central one is unstable, while $x = \pm 2\,\mathrm{m}$ are only zeros of $U$.
