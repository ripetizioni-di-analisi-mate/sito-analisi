A body of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ is at the height $3.0\,\mathrm{m}$ and has the mechanical energy $100\,\mathrm{J}$, with the ground as the reference level. What is its kinetic energy?

- $58.9\,\mathrm{J}$
- $41.1\,\mathrm{J}$
- $94.0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $159\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The potential energy is $U = mgy = 2.0\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot 3.0\,\mathrm{m} = 58.9\,\mathrm{J}$. Since $E = K + U$, the kinetic energy is $K = 100\,\mathrm{J} - 58.9\,\mathrm{J} = 41.1\,\mathrm{J}$.
