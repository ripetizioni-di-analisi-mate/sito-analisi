The same constant $C$ is added to the potential energy $U$ of a conservative force at every position. What effect does this have on the work of the force and on the force itself?

- Every work increases by $C$
- Neither the work nor the force changes
- The force increases by $C$ per meter
- Every work changes sign

## Explanation

Every work is a difference $U_1 - U_2$, in which $C$ cancels, and the force $F_x = -\dfrac{dU}{dx}$ is unchanged because the derivative of a constant is zero. Adding $C$ only moves the reference position at which $U = 0$.
