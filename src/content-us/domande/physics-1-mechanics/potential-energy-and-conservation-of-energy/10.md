Why can no potential energy be assigned to kinetic friction?

- Its work is zero along every path
- Its work between two positions depends on the path
- Its magnitude depends on the speed of the body
- It has no component along the displacement

## Explanation

A function of position alone gives a single value of $U_1 - U_2$ for two given positions, while the work of friction is $-f_k s$ and grows with the length $s$ of the path. Kinetic friction has the magnitude $\mu_k N$, independent of the speed, and it is opposite to the displacement, so its work is negative.
