The weight does the positive work $W$ on a falling body. How does the gravitational potential energy of the body change?

- It increases by $W$
- It decreases by $W$
- It stays the same
- It decreases by $2W$

## Explanation

The work of a conservative force is $W = -\Delta U$, therefore a positive work corresponds to $\Delta U = -W$. The body loses potential energy as it falls.
