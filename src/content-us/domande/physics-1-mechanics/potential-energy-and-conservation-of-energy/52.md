A block of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ starts from rest at the top of a ramp of height $3.0\,\mathrm{m}$, and friction does the work $-20\,\mathrm{J}$ on it during the descent. What is its speed at the bottom?

- $7.67\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8.82\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6.23\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4.41\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The energy equation gives $\dfrac{1}{2}mv^2 = mgh + W_{\text{nc}} = 58.9\,\mathrm{J} - 20\,\mathrm{J} = 38.9\,\mathrm{J}$. With $m = 2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ this is $v^2 = 38.9\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}$, hence $v = 6.23\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
