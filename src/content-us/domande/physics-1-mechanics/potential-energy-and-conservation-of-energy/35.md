Under which condition is the mechanical energy of a body guaranteed to be conserved?

- The net force on the body is zero
- Every force acting on the body has constant magnitude
- The body moves along a straight line
- Every force that does work on the body is conservative

## Explanation

When the only forces doing work are conservative, $\Delta K = -\Delta U$ and $K + U$ is constant. A box lifted at constant speed has zero net force and increasing mechanical energy, and a block slowed by kinetic friction moves in a straight line under forces of constant magnitude while losing mechanical energy.
