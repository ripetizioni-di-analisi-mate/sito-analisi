A body is at a position of equilibrium where its potential energy $U(x)$ has a minimum. What happens after a small displacement?

- A force arises directed away from the equilibrium position
- The force stays zero and the body remains at the new position
- A force arises perpendicular to the $x$ axis
- A force arises directed back towards the equilibrium position

## Explanation

On each side of a minimum $U$ increases away from it, and $F_x = -\dfrac{dU}{dx}$ points towards lower $U$, that is back towards the minimum. For this reason a minimum of $U$ is a position of stable equilibrium.
