On an energy diagram, at which position of the allowed region is the speed of the body largest?

- Where $U$ is largest
- At the turning points
- Where $U$ is smallest
- Where the force has the largest magnitude

## Explanation

The speed is $v = \sqrt{\dfrac{2\,(E - U)}{m}}$ with $E$ constant, so it is largest where $U$ is smallest. At a minimum of $U$ the force is zero, while at the turning points the speed is zero.
