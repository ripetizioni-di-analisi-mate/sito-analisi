Let $y_0$ be the equilibrium position of a block hanging from a vertical spring of constant $k$, and let $u = y - y_0$ be the displacement from it. Which form does the total potential energy take in terms of $u$?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}ku^2 - mgu$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}ku^2 + \text{constant}$
- $ku^2 + \text{constant}$
- $mgu + \text{constant}$

## Explanation

Substituting $y = y_0 + u$ with $y_0 = \dfrac{mg}{k}$ in $\dfrac{1}{2}ky^2 - mgy$, the terms linear in $u$ cancel and $U = \dfrac{1}{2}ku^2 - \dfrac{m^2g^2}{2k}$. The total potential energy has the quadratic form of a spring alone, apart from an additive constant.
