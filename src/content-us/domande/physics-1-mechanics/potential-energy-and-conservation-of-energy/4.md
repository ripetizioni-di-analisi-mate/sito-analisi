Which of the following forces is conservative?

- The resistance of the air on a falling ball
- Kinetic friction on a sled
- The force of an ideal spring on the body attached to it
- The thrust of a motor that always pushes along the velocity

## Explanation

The work of the spring force is $\dfrac{1}{2}kx_1^2 - \dfrac{1}{2}kx_2^2$, which depends only on the initial and the final deformation. Air resistance and friction always oppose the motion, and the thrust always acts along it, hence each of them does a nonzero work around a closed path.
