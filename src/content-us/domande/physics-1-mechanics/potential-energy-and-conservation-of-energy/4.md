A small body travels once around a closed circuit on a rough track and returns to its starting position with a smaller speed. What does this show about kinetic friction?

- Its work around a closed path is zero, so it is conservative
- It is perpendicular to the velocity at every instant
- Its work around a closed path is not zero, so it is nonconservative
- Its work depends only on the initial and the final position

## Explanation

The weight does zero work around the closed circuit and the normal force does no work, so the loss of kinetic energy is the work of friction. A force with nonzero work around a closed path is nonconservative, by the theorem on closed paths.
