A conservative force acts along the $x$ axis. Which quantity is equal to the difference $U(x_2) - U(x_1)$?

- The signed area under the graph of $F_x$ from $x_1$ to $x_2$
- The slope of the graph of $F_x$ between $x_1$ and $x_2$
- Minus the signed area under the graph of $F_x$ from $x_1$ to $x_2$
- The difference $F_x(x_2) - F_x(x_1)$

## Explanation

The definition of potential energy gives $U(x_2) - U(x_1) = -W = -\displaystyle\int_{x_1}^{x_2} F_x\,dx$. The integral is the signed area under the graph of $F_x$, taken with the minus sign.
