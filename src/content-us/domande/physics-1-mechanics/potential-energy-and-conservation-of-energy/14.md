A book of mass $1.5\,\mathrm{kg}$ is lowered by $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$. What work does its weight do on it?

- $-11.8\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{J}$
- $11.8\,\mathrm{J}$
- $5.89\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The weight points downwards and the book moves downwards, therefore the work is positive: $W = mgh = 1.5\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot 0.80\,\mathrm{m} = 11.8\,\mathrm{J}$. Correspondingly the potential energy decreases by $11.8\,\mathrm{J}$.
