A crate of mass $10\,\mathrm{kg}$ is lifted at constant speed through the height $1.2\,\mathrm{m}$. What work does the lifting force do?

- $118\,\mathrm{J}$
- $12\,\mathrm{J}$
- $58.9\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-118\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The kinetic energy does not change, therefore the energy equation gives $W_{\text{nc}} = \Delta U = mgh = 10\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot 1.2\,\mathrm{m} = 118\,\mathrm{J}$. The weight does the opposite work $-118\,\mathrm{J}$.
