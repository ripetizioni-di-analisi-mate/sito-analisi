A body attached to a spring has the potential energy $U(x) = 50x^2$, with $U$ in joules and $x$ in meters. What is the spring constant?

- $50\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $25\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $200\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $100\,\mathrm{N/m}$

## Explanation

The force is $F_x = -\dfrac{dU}{dx} = -100x$, which is Hooke's law with $k = 100\,\mathrm{N/m}$. Equivalently, $\dfrac{1}{2}k = 50\,\mathrm{N/m}$.
