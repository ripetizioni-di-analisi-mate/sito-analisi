During a displacement the only nonconservative force acting on a body is friction, which does the work $-20\,\mathrm{J}$. What is the change of the mechanical energy of the body?

- $+20\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-10\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The energy equation states $E_2 - E_1 = W_{\text{nc}}$, hence $\Delta E = -20\,\mathrm{J}$. Friction lowers the mechanical energy by exactly the magnitude of its work.
