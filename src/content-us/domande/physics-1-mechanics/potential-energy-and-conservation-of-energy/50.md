A small body slides on a frictionless track and passes two points that are at the same height. How do its speeds at those two points compare?

- The speed is larger at the later point
- They are equal
- The speed is smaller at the later point
- They depend on the slope of the track at the two points

## Explanation

The quantity $\dfrac{1}{2}mv^2 + mgy$ has the same value at every point of a frictionless track. At equal heights the potential energies are equal, hence the kinetic energies and the speeds are equal too.
