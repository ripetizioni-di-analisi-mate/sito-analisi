A body of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ goes from a point $A$ at the height $3.0\,\mathrm{m}$ to a point $B$ at the height $0$. One path is the straight segment $AB$, of length $5.0\,\mathrm{m}$; the other is a vertical drop of $3.0\,\mathrm{m}$ followed by a horizontal run of $4.0\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the work of the weight along the second path?

- $98.1\,\mathrm{J}$
- $58.9\,\mathrm{J}$
- $137\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-58.9\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The work of the weight is $mg\,(y_A - y_B) = 2.0\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot 3.0\,\mathrm{m} = 58.9\,\mathrm{J}$, since the horizontal run contributes nothing. The same value holds along the straight segment, because only the drop enters the result.
