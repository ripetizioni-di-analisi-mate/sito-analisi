A body moves in a plane with potential energy $U(x, y) = 3x + 4y$, with $U$ in joules and $x$, $y$ in meters. What is the magnitude of the force on the body?

- $7.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $25\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $5.0\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The components are $F_x = -\dfrac{\partial U}{\partial x} = -3\,\mathrm{N}$ and $F_y = -\dfrac{\partial U}{\partial y} = -4\,\mathrm{N}$. The magnitude is $\sqrt{3^2 + 4^2}\,\mathrm{N} = 5.0\,\mathrm{N}$.
