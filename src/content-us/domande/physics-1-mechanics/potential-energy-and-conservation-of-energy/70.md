A body moves along the $x$ axis with potential energy $U(x) = x^3 - 3x$, with $U$ in joules and $x$ in meters. What is the force component $F_x$ at $x = 2.0\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $9.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-9.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-2.0\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The force is $F_x = -\dfrac{dU}{dx} = 3 - 3x^2$, hence $F_x(2.0\,\mathrm{m}) = 3 - 12 = -9.0\,\mathrm{N}$. The value $2.0$ is $U(2.0\,\mathrm{m})$ in joules.
