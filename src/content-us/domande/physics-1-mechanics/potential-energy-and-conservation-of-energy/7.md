A conservative force does the work $15\,\mathrm{J}$ on a body that moves from position $1$ to position $2$. What is the change $\Delta U = U_2 - U_1$ of the potential energy of that force?

- $-15\,\mathrm{J}$
- $15\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-7.5\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

By the definition of potential energy $W = U_1 - U_2 = -\Delta U$, hence $\Delta U = -15\,\mathrm{J}$. The potential energy decreases whenever the force does positive work.
