A block attached to a horizontal spring oscillates on a rough floor. How do its successive turning points change?

- They stay at the same positions
- They move farther from the natural length of the spring
- They get closer to the natural length of the spring
- They alternate between closer and farther positions

## Explanation

At a turning point the mechanical energy is $\dfrac{1}{2}kx^2$. Friction lowers the mechanical energy during every swing, hence $|x|$ at the turning points decreases.
