A spring of constant $200\,\mathrm{N/m}$ lies along the $x$ axis, with the origin at its natural length. The spring is compressed so that the body attached to it is at $x = -0.10\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the component $F_x$ of the spring force on the body?

- $-20\,\mathrm{N}$
- $+20\,\mathrm{N}$
- $+1.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-1.0\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Hooke's law gives $F_x = -kx = -200\,\mathrm{N/m} \cdot (-0.10\,\mathrm{m}) = +20\,\mathrm{N}$. The force is positive because it pushes the body back towards the natural length.
