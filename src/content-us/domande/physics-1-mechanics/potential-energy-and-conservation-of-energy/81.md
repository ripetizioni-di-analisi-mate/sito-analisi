A ball rests on a horizontal table, where its gravitational potential energy has the same value at every point. What kind of equilibrium is this?

- Stable
- Unstable
- Neutral
- No equilibrium, since the table pushes the ball upwards

## Explanation

The potential energy is constant along the table, hence $\dfrac{dU}{dx} = 0$ on the whole interval and the force along the table vanishes everywhere. The ball stays wherever it is placed, which defines a neutral equilibrium.
