A body moves along the $x$ axis with mechanical energy $E$ and potential energy $U(x)$. At which positions can the body be found?

- Where $U(x) \ge E$
- Where $U(x) \le E$
- Where $U(x) \le 0$
- Only where $U(x) = E$

## Explanation

The kinetic energy is $K = E - U(x)$ and cannot be negative, hence the motion takes place where $U(x) \le E$. The positions with $U(x) = E$ are the turning points, at the border of that region.
