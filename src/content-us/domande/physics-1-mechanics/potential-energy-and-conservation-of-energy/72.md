A body moves along the positive $x$ axis with potential energy $U(x) = -\dfrac{8}{x}$, with $U$ in joules and $x$ in meters. What is the force component $F_x$ at $x = 2.0\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $-2.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-4.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The derivative is $\dfrac{dU}{dx} = \dfrac{8}{x^2}$, hence $F_x = -\dfrac{8}{x^2}$ and $F_x(2.0\,\mathrm{m}) = -2.0\,\mathrm{N}$. The force points toward the origin, where $U$ is lower.
