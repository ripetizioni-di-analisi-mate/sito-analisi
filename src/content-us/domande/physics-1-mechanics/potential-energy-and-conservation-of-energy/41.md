A pendulum bob hangs from a string of length $1.0\,\mathrm{m}$. The bob is pulled aside until the string makes the angle $60^\circ$ with the vertical. How high is the bob above its lowest position?

- $0.87\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.13\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The bob is at the vertical distance $L\cos\theta$ below the point of suspension, hence its height above the lowest position is $L\,(1 - \cos\theta) = 1.0\,\mathrm{m} \cdot (1 - 0.50) = 0.50\,\mathrm{m}$. Using $\sin 60^\circ$ in place of $\cos 60^\circ$ gives a wrong height.
