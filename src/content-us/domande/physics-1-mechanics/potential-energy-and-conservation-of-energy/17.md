The reference level is a table top. A ball of mass $0.50\,\mathrm{kg}$ lies on the floor, $0.75\,\mathrm{m}$ below the table top. What is the gravitational potential energy of the ball?

- $3.68\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-0.375\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-3.68\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The ball is below the reference level, so its height is $y = -0.75\,\mathrm{m}$ and $U = mgy = 0.50\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot (-0.75\,\mathrm{m}) = -3.68\,\mathrm{J}$. The value of $U$ is zero only at the chosen reference level.
