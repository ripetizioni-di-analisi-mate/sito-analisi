A block attached to a vertical spring is released from rest with the spring at its natural length. How does the largest extension it reaches compare with the extension at the equilibrium position?

- It is equal
- It is twice as large
- It is half as large
- It is $\sqrt{2}$ times as large

## Explanation

With $y$ measured downwards from the natural length, $E = 0$ and the turning points solve $\dfrac{1}{2}ky^2 - mgy = 0$, that is $y = 0$ and $y = \dfrac{2mg}{k}$. The equilibrium position is $\dfrac{mg}{k}$, which is half of the largest extension.
