A block slides along a frictionless curved track. Why does the normal force of the track not change the mechanical energy of the block?

- It is conservative, with potential energy $ny$
- It balances the weight at every point
- It is smaller than the weight at every point
- It is perpendicular to the velocity, so it does no work

## Explanation

The normal force is perpendicular to the track, hence to the velocity, and its work vanishes at every instant. It needs no potential energy, and on a curved track it does not balance the weight.
