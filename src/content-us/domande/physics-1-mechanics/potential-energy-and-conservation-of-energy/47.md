A spring of constant $200\,\mathrm{N/m}$, compressed by $0.10\,\mathrm{m}$, launches a block of mass $0.10\,\mathrm{kg}$ along a frictionless horizontal surface, and the block then runs up a frictionless ramp. What height does the block reach?

- $2.04\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.02\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $20.4\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

All the elastic energy becomes gravitational energy, $\dfrac{1}{2}kx^2 = mgh$, hence
$$
h = \frac{kx^2}{2mg} = \frac{200\,\mathrm{N/m} \cdot (0.10\,\mathrm{m})^2}{2 \cdot 0.10\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}} = 1.02\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
