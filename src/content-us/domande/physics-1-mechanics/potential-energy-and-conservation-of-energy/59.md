A block launched with the speed $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ along a rough horizontal floor stops after $9.0\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the coefficient of kinetic friction?

- $0.20$
- $0.41$
- $2.0$
- $0.068$

## Explanation

The energy equation gives $\mu_k mg\,d = \dfrac{1}{2}mv_0^2$, hence
$$
\mu_k = \frac{v_0^2}{2gd} = \frac{(6.0\,\mathrm{m/s})^2}{2 \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot 9.0\,\mathrm{m}} = 0.20 .
$$
