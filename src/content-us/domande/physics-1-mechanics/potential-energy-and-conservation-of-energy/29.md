A spring stores $2.5\,\mathrm{J}$ when it is stretched by $0.10\,\mathrm{m}$. What is its spring constant?

- $250\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $25\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $50\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $500\,\mathrm{N/m}$

## Explanation

From $U = \dfrac{1}{2}kx^2$,
$$
k = \frac{2U}{x^2} = \frac{2 \cdot 2.5\,\mathrm{J}}{(0.10\,\mathrm{m})^2} = 500\,\mathrm{N/m} .
$$
Dropping the factor $2$ or the square of $x$ gives a wrong constant.
