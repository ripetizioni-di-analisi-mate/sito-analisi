A shelf is $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ above the floor and a table top is $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$ above the floor. A book of mass $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ lies on the shelf. Taking the table top as the reference level, what is the gravitational potential energy of the book?

- $19.6\,\mathrm{J}$
- $11.8\,\mathrm{J}$
- $27.5\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-11.8\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The height of the book above the reference level is $2.0\,\mathrm{m} - 0.80\,\mathrm{m} = 1.2\,\mathrm{m}$, hence $U = mgy = 1.0\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot 1.2\,\mathrm{m} = 11.8\,\mathrm{J}$. The value $19.6\,\mathrm{J}$ belongs to the floor as reference level.
