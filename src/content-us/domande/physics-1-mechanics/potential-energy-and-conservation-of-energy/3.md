Which of the following forces is nonconservative?

- The weight of a body near the surface of the Earth
- Kinetic friction on a sliding block
- The force of an ideal spring on the body attached to it
- The gravitational attraction between two bodies

## Explanation

The work of kinetic friction is $-f_k s$, where $s$ is the length of the path, therefore it depends on the path and the force is nonconservative. The weight, the force of an ideal spring and the gravitational attraction do a work that depends only on the initial and the final position.
