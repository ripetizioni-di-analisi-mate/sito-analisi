A mass is measured as $2300\,\mathrm{kg}$ with three significant figures. Which writing states this without ambiguity?

- $2.3 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $2300\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $2.30 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $2.300 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

In the writing $2300\,\mathrm{kg}$ the final zeros may be measured digits or may only place the decimal point, so the number of significant figures cannot be read. Scientific notation removes the ambiguity, since only the significant digits appear: $2.30 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg}$ has three, $2.3 \times 10^{3}$ has two and $2.300 \times 10^{3}$ has four.
