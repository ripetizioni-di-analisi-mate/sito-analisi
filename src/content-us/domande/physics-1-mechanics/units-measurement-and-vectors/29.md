Power is defined as work divided by time, $P = \dfrac{W}{t}$. What are the dimensions of power?

- $[M\,L^2\,T^{-2}]$
- $[M\,L\,T^{-3}]$
- $[M\,L^2\,T^{-1}]$
- $[M\,L^2\,T^{-3}]$

## Explanation

Work is a force times a distance, so $[W] = [M\,L\,T^{-2}][L] = [M\,L^2\,T^{-2}]$. Dividing by a time gives
$$
[P] = \frac{[M\,L^2\,T^{-2}]}{[T]} = [M\,L^2\,T^{-3}] .
$$
The dimensions $[M\,L^2\,T^{-2}]$ are those of work itself, before the division by time.
