A box slides $3.0\,\mathrm{m}$ along a horizontal floor, and the floor exerts on it a normal force of $50\,\mathrm{N}$. What work does the normal force do?

- $150\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-150\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $50\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The normal force is vertical and the displacement is horizontal, so the angle between them is $90^\circ$. Since $W = F\,d\cos\varphi$ and $\cos 90^\circ = 0$, the normal force does no work. The value $150\,\mathrm{J}$ multiplies the magnitudes as if the two vectors were parallel.
