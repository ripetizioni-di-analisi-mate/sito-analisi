In a calculation with several steps, why is rounding applied only to the final result?

- Intermediate values carry no unit, so they have no significant figures.
- Rounding the intermediate values accumulates and can shift the last digit of the result.
- The rules for significant figures apply only to sums and differences.
- Exact numbers in the formula must be rounded first.

## Explanation

Every rounding introduces a small error, and in a long calculation the errors of several intermediate roundings add up and can change the last significant digit. Rounding once, at the end, keeps the result as close as possible to the value fixed by the data. Exact numbers carry no uncertainty and are never rounded.
