For which value of $k$ are the vectors $\vec{A} = (k,\ 2)$ and $\vec{B} = (3,\ -6)$ perpendicular?

- $k = -4$
- $k = 0.25$
- $k = 4$
- $k = -0.25$

## Explanation

The vectors are perpendicular when $\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B} = 0$, that is, $3k + 2 \cdot (-6) = 0$. Hence $3k = 12$ and $k = 4$. Losing the sign of $-6$ gives $k = -4$, and dividing $3$ by $12$ instead of $12$ by $3$ gives $0.25$.
