A body of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ moves at $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ due north. What is its momentum $m\vec{v}$?

- $6.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$ south
- $6.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$ north
- $1.5\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$ north
- $6.0\,\mathrm{N}$ north

## Explanation

The product of the positive scalar $m$ and $\vec{v}$ has the direction and sense of the velocity and magnitude $2.0\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 3.0\,\mathrm{m/s} = 6.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$. The scalar changes the unit as well, and $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$ differs from the newton, which is $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s^2}$.
