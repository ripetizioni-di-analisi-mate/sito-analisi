Given $\vec{A} = (5,\ -2,\ 7)$, what is the scalar product $\hat{k} \cdot \vec{A}$?

- $5$
- $-2$
- $7$
- $10$

## Explanation

The unit vector $\hat{k}$ has components $(0,\ 0,\ 1)$, so by the **scalar product in components** $\hat{k} \cdot \vec{A} = 0 \cdot 5 + 0 \cdot (-2) + 1 \cdot 7 = 7$. The scalar product with a unit vector of an axis picks the component of $\vec{A}$ along that axis. The value $10$ is the sum of all three components.
