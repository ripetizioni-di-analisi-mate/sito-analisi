Two vectors have magnitudes $A = 4.0$ and $B = 6.0$, and their scalar product is $\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B} = -12$. What is the angle between them?

- $120^\circ$
- $60^\circ$
- $30^\circ$
- $150^\circ$

## Explanation

From the definition of the **scalar product**, $\cos\varphi = \dfrac{\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B}}{A\,B} = \dfrac{-12}{24} = -0.50$, hence $\varphi = 120^\circ$. The negative product implies an obtuse angle, so $60^\circ$ and $30^\circ$ are excluded at once; $150^\circ$ has $\sin\varphi = 0.50$, not $\cos\varphi = -0.50$.
