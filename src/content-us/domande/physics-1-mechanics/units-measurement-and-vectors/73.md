Two vectors of magnitudes $4.0$ and $5.0$ form the angle $120^\circ$. What is their scalar product?

- $10$
- $17$
- $-17$
- $-10$

## Explanation

By definition $\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B} = A\,B\cos\varphi = 4.0 \cdot 5.0 \cdot \cos 120^\circ = 20 \cdot (-0.50) = -10$. The product is negative because the angle exceeds $90^\circ$. The values $\pm 17$ use $\sin 120^\circ \approx 0.87$ in place of the cosine.
