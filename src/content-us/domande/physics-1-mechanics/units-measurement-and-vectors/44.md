How are the vectors $\vec{A} + \vec{B}$ and $\vec{B} + \vec{A}$ related?

- They are equal only when $\vec{A}$ and $\vec{B}$ are perpendicular.
- They are always opposite.
- They are always equal.
- They are equal only when $A = B$.

## Explanation

Drawing $\vec{A}$ and $\vec{B}$ from the same point, both $\vec{A} + \vec{B}$ and $\vec{B} + \vec{A}$ are the diagonal of the parallelogram with sides $\vec{A}$ and $\vec{B}$. Therefore the **sum of two vectors** does not depend on the order, whatever the magnitudes and the angle.
