How many square meters are there in $1\,\mathrm{km^2}$?

- $10^{3}\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $10^{6}\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $10^{9}\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

The prefix and the symbol form a single unit, therefore the exponent applies to the prefix as well:
$$
1\,\mathrm{km^2} = (10^{3}\,\mathrm{m})^2 = 10^{6}\,\mathrm{m^2} .
$$
The value $10^{3}\,\mathrm{m^2}$ applies the prefix without the exponent, and $10^{9}$ belongs to $\mathrm{km^3}$.
