What is the magnitude of the vector $\vec{A} = (2.0,\ -3.0,\ 6.0)\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $7.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $11\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $49\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

By the Pythagorean theorem applied in space,
$$
A = \sqrt{A_x^2 + A_y^2 + A_z^2} = \sqrt{4.0 + 9.0 + 36}\,\mathrm{m} = 7.0\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $49$ is the sum of the squares before the square root, and $11$ adds the absolute values of the components.
