A circular plate has radius $r = 1.50\,\mathrm{m}$. With the correct significant figures, what is its area?

- $7.07\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $9.42\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $28.3\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $2.25\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

The area is $\pi r^2 = \pi \cdot 1.50^2\,\mathrm{m^2} \approx 7.0686\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The number $\pi$ and the exponent are exact, so the result keeps the three significant figures of $r$, and the area is $7.07\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Using the diameter in place of the radius gives $28.3\,\mathrm{m^2}$, and $9.42$ is the number of the circumference $2\pi r$.
