The same length is written as $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$ and as $250\,\mathrm{cm}$. Which statement is correct?

- The two values describe different lengths, because the numbers differ.
- The value in centimeters is more precise, because its number is larger.
- The length is the same; the number changes because the unit changes.
- The conversion is wrong, because $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$ equals $25\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

The value of a **physical quantity** is the product of a number and a unit, and the number counts how many times the unit is contained in the quantity. Since $1\,\mathrm{m} = 100\,\mathrm{cm}$, the length $2.5 \cdot (1\,\mathrm{m})$ equals $250 \cdot (1\,\mathrm{cm})$, and changing the unit adds no information on the precision of the measurement.
