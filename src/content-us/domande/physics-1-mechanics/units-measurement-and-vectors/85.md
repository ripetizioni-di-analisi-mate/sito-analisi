For two nonzero vectors, the vector product $\vec{A} \times \vec{B}$ is zero exactly when

- the two vectors are perpendicular.
- the two vectors have the same magnitude.
- the two vectors lie in the $xy$ plane.
- the two vectors are parallel.

## Explanation

The magnitude of the **vector product** is $A\,B\sin\varphi$, and for nonzero vectors it vanishes exactly when $\sin\varphi = 0$, that is, for $\varphi = 0^\circ$ or $\varphi = 180^\circ$. Perpendicular vectors give the largest magnitude, $A\,B$, and two vectors of the $xy$ plane have a product along $\hat{k}$, which is zero only if they are parallel.
