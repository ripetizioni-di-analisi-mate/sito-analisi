A particle moves from the position $\vec{r}_1 = (2.0,\ 1.0)\,\mathrm{m}$ to the position $\vec{r}_2 = (5.0,\ -3.0)\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the magnitude of its displacement?

- $7.3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3.6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $25\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The displacement is the difference $\Delta\vec{r} = \vec{r}_2 - \vec{r}_1 = (3.0,\ -4.0)\,\mathrm{m}$, whose magnitude is $\sqrt{3.0^2 + 4.0^2}\,\mathrm{m} = 5.0\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $7.3\,\mathrm{m}$ is $|\vec{r}_1 + \vec{r}_2|$, and $3.6\,\mathrm{m}$ is the difference of the two magnitudes $r_2 - r_1$.
