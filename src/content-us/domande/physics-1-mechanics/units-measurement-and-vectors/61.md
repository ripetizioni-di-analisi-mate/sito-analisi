A vector equation $\vec{A} = \vec{B}$ between two vectors of the $xy$ plane is equivalent to

- two numerical equations, one for each axis.
- one equation between the magnitudes.
- one equation between the angles with the $x$ axis.
- three numerical equations, one for each unit vector.

## Explanation

Two vectors are equal exactly when their components are equal, so in the plane $\vec{A} = \vec{B}$ means $A_x = B_x$ and $A_y = B_y$. Equal magnitudes alone allow different directions, and equal angles alone allow different magnitudes. This is how a balance of forces in the plane gives two equations.
