The stiffness of a spring is measured in $\mathrm{N/m}$. Which expression gives this unit in SI base units?

- $\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s^2}$
- $\mathrm{kg/s^2}$
- $\mathrm{kg/(m\cdot s^2)}$
- $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$

## Explanation

Since $1\,\mathrm{N} = 1\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s^2}$, dividing by one meter gives
$$
1\,\mathrm{N/m} = 1\,\frac{\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s^2}}{\mathrm{m}} = 1\,\mathrm{kg/s^2} .
$$
Multiplying by the meter instead of dividing gives $\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s^2}$, which is the joule.
