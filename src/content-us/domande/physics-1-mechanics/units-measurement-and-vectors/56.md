A force of $60\,\mathrm{N}$ makes the angle $130^\circ$ with the positive $x$ axis, measured counterclockwise. What is its $x$ component?

- $-38.6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $38.6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-46.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $46.0\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The component is $F_x = F\cos\theta = 60\,\mathrm{N} \cdot \cos 130^\circ \approx -38.6\,\mathrm{N}$. The force lies in the second quadrant, so its projection on the $x$ axis opposes the axis and the component is negative. The value $46.0\,\mathrm{N}$ is $F\sin 130^\circ$, the $y$ component.
