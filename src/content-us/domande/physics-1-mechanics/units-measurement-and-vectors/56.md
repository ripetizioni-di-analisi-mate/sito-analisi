A force of $50\,\mathrm{N}$ makes the angle $140^\circ$ with the positive $x$ axis, measured counterclockwise. What is its $x$ component?

- $-38.3\,\mathrm{N}$
- $38.3\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-32.1\,\mathrm{N}$
- $32.1\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The component is $F_x = F\cos\theta = 50\,\mathrm{N} \cdot \cos 140^\circ \approx -38.3\,\mathrm{N}$. The force lies in the second quadrant, so its projection on the $x$ axis opposes the axis and the component is negative. The value $32.1\,\mathrm{N}$ is $F\sin 140^\circ$, the $y$ component.
