For $\vec{A} = (-2.0,\ -2.0)$ a student computes $\arctan\left(\dfrac{A_y}{A_x}\right) = 45^\circ$. What angle does $\vec{A}$ make with the positive $x$ axis, measured counterclockwise?

- $45^\circ$
- $135^\circ$
- $315^\circ$
- $225^\circ$

## Explanation

The function $\arctan$ returns angles between $-90^\circ$ and $90^\circ$, so it gives the angle of $\vec{A}$ only when $A_x > 0$. Here both components are negative, the vector lies in the third quadrant, and its angle is $45^\circ + 180^\circ = 225^\circ$. The angle $45^\circ$ belongs to the opposite vector $(2.0,\ 2.0)$.
