The vectors $\vec{A}$ and $\vec{B}$ are drawn from the same point. Which vector is the difference $\vec{A} - \vec{B}$?

- the vector from the tip of $\vec{A}$ to the tip of $\vec{B}$
- the diagonal of the parallelogram from the common point
- the vector from the tip of $\vec{B}$ to the tip of $\vec{A}$
- the vector from the common point to the tip of $\vec{B}$

## Explanation

Since $\vec{B} + (\vec{A} - \vec{B}) = \vec{A}$, the difference is the vector that, drawn from the tip of $\vec{B}$, reaches the tip of $\vec{A}$. The vector from the tip of $\vec{A}$ to the tip of $\vec{B}$ is $\vec{B} - \vec{A}$, and the diagonal from the common point is the sum $\vec{A} + \vec{B}$.
