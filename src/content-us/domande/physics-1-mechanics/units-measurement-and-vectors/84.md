A parallelogram has sides $\vec{A} = (4.0,\ 0,\ 0)\,\mathrm{m}$ and $\vec{B} = (1.0,\ 3.0,\ 0)\,\mathrm{m}$. What is its area?

- $12\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $6.0\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

The area equals the magnitude of the vector product, and for vectors of the $xy$ plane $|\vec{A} \times \vec{B}| = |A_xB_y - A_yB_x| = |4.0 \cdot 3.0 - 0 \cdot 1.0|\,\mathrm{m^2} = 12\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The value $6.0\,\mathrm{m^2}$ is the area of the triangle with the same two sides, and $4.0$ is the scalar product $\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B}$.
