A snail crawls at $1.2\,\mathrm{mm/s}$. What is its speed in meters per hour?

- $4.3 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{m/h}$
- $7.2 \times 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m/h}$
- $3.3 \times 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m/h}$
- $4.3\,\mathrm{m/h}$

## Explanation

Since $1\,\mathrm{mm} = 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m}$ and $1\,\mathrm{s} = \dfrac{1}{3600}\,\mathrm{h}$,
$$
1.2\,\frac{\mathrm{mm}}{\mathrm{s}} = 1.2 \cdot \frac{10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m}}{\frac{1}{3600}\,\mathrm{h}} = 1.2 \times 10^{-3} \cdot 3600\,\mathrm{m/h} \approx 4.3\,\mathrm{m/h} .
$$
Leaving the millimeters unconverted gives $4.3 \times 10^{3}$, and using $60\,\mathrm{s}$ for the hour gives $7.2 \times 10^{-2}$.
