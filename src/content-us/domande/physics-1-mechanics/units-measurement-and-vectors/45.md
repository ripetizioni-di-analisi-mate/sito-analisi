A runner moves $30\,\mathrm{m}$ north and then $50\,\mathrm{m}$ south along the same straight road. What is the runner's displacement?

- $80\,\mathrm{m}$ south
- $20\,\mathrm{m}$ north
- $20\,\mathrm{m}$ south
- $80\,\mathrm{m}$ north

## Explanation

The two displacements have opposite senses, so the magnitude of the sum is the difference of the magnitudes, $50\,\mathrm{m} - 30\,\mathrm{m} = 20\,\mathrm{m}$, and the sense is that of the larger one, south. The value $80\,\mathrm{m}$ is the distance covered, which is a scalar and differs from the magnitude of the displacement.
