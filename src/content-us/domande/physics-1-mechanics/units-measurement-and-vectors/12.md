A room has a floor area of $120\,\mathrm{ft^2}$. What is this area in square meters, with $1\,\mathrm{ft} = 0.3048\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $36.6\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $11.1\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $394\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $1290\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

When a unit is raised to a power, the conversion factor is raised to the same power, so $1\,\mathrm{ft^2} = (0.3048\,\mathrm{m})^2 \approx 0.0929\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and
$$
120\,\mathrm{ft^2} \approx 120 \cdot 0.0929\,\mathrm{m^2} \approx 11.1\,\mathrm{m^2} .
$$
The factor used only once gives $36.6\,\mathrm{m^2}$, and dividing instead of multiplying gives the two larger values.
