What is the free-fall acceleration $g = 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ expressed in $\mathrm{km/h^2}$?

- $35.3\,\mathrm{km/h^2}$
- $1.27 \times 10^{8}\,\mathrm{km/h^2}$
- $7.57 \times 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{km/h^2}$
- $1.27 \times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{km/h^2}$

## Explanation

The second appears squared, so its conversion factor is squared too: $\dfrac{1}{\mathrm{s^2}} = \dfrac{3600^2}{\mathrm{h^2}}$. Hence
$$
9.81\,\frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s^2}} = 9.81 \cdot \frac{10^{-3}\,\mathrm{km} \cdot 3600^2}{\mathrm{h^2}} \approx 1.27 \times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{km/h^2} .
$$
Using the factor $3.6$ of speeds gives $35.3$, and leaving the meters unconverted gives $1.27 \times 10^{8}$.
