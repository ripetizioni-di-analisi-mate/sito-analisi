Which list contains only vector quantities?

- velocity, mass, force
- displacement, time, acceleration
- energy, force, velocity
- velocity, force, acceleration

## Explanation

A **vector** is determined by a magnitude, a direction and a sense, as velocity, force, acceleration and displacement are. Mass, time and energy are **scalars**, determined by a number and a unit alone, so every other list contains one scalar.
