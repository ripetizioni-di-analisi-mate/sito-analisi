Given $\vec{A} \times \vec{B} = (2,\ -5,\ 3)$, what is $\vec{B} \times \vec{A}$?

- $(2,\ -5,\ 3)$
- $(-2,\ 5,\ -3)$
- $(3,\ -5,\ 2)$
- $(0,\ 0,\ 0)$

## Explanation

Reversing the order of the factors reverses the sense of the **vector product**, $\vec{B} \times \vec{A} = -\,\vec{A} \times \vec{B}$, so every component changes sign. The vector product is not commutative, and it is zero only for parallel vectors.
