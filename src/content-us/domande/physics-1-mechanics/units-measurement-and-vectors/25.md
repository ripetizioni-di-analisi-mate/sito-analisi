Which of the following measured values carries the greatest number of significant figures?

- $0.00700\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3.040 \times 10^{2}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $52.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6.0 \times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

In scientific notation every digit written is significant, so $3.040 \times 10^{2}\,\mathrm{m}$ has four significant figures. The value $0.00700\,\mathrm{m}$ has three, since the zeros on the left only place the decimal point; $52.0\,\mathrm{m}$ has three and $6.0 \times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{m}$ has two.
