A vector makes the angle $200^\circ$ with the positive $x$ axis, measured counterclockwise. What are the signs of its components?

- $A_x < 0$ and $A_y < 0$
- $A_x < 0$ and $A_y > 0$
- $A_x > 0$ and $A_y < 0$
- $A_x > 0$ and $A_y > 0$

## Explanation

An angle between $180^\circ$ and $270^\circ$ lies in the third quadrant, where $\cos\theta < 0$ and $\sin\theta < 0$. Since $A_x = A\cos\theta$ and $A_y = A\sin\theta$, both components are negative: $\cos 200^\circ \approx -0.94$ and $\sin 200^\circ \approx -0.34$.
