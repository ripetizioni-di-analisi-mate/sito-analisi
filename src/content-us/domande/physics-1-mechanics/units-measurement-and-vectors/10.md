A car moves at $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is its speed in miles per hour, with $1\,\mathrm{mi} = 1609\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $13.4\,\mathrm{mi/h}$
- $67.1\,\mathrm{mi/h}$
- $108\,\mathrm{mi/h}$
- $1.12\,\mathrm{mi/h}$

## Explanation

Since $1\,\mathrm{m} = \dfrac{1}{1609}\,\mathrm{mi}$ and $1\,\mathrm{s} = \dfrac{1}{3600}\,\mathrm{h}$,
$$
30\,\frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}} = 30 \cdot \frac{3600}{1609}\,\mathrm{mi/h} \approx 67.1\,\mathrm{mi/h} .
$$
The value $108$ is the speed in $\mathrm{km/h}$, and $13.4$ comes from inverting the conversion factor.
