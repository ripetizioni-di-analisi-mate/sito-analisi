Given $\vec{A} = (3.0,\ -1.0)$ and $\vec{B} = (-5.0,\ 4.0)$, what is the magnitude of $\vec{A} + \vec{B}$?

- $9.6$
- $1.0$
- $3.6$
- $13$

## Explanation

By the **operations in components** the sum is $\vec{A} + \vec{B} = (3.0 - 5.0,\ -1.0 + 4.0) = (-2.0,\ 3.0)$, hence its magnitude is $\sqrt{4.0 + 9.0} = \sqrt{13} \approx 3.6$. The value $9.6$ adds the two magnitudes $\sqrt{10}$ and $\sqrt{41}$, which is correct only for vectors with the same direction and sense.
