What is the scalar product $\vec{A} \cdot \vec{A}$ of a vector with itself?

- $0$
- $2A$
- a vector of magnitude $A^2$
- $A^2$, the square of its magnitude

## Explanation

The angle between a vector and itself is $0^\circ$ and $\cos 0^\circ = 1$, hence $\vec{A} \cdot \vec{A} = A \cdot A \cdot 1 = A^2$. In components the same result reads $A_x^2 + A_y^2 + A_z^2$. The scalar product is a number, never a vector.
