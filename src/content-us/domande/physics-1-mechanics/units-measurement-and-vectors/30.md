A body of mass $m$ moves at speed $v$ on a circle of radius $r$. What are the dimensions of $\dfrac{mv^2}{r}$?

- $[M\,L^2\,T^{-2}]$
- $[M\,T^{-2}]$
- $[M\,L^3\,T^{-2}]$
- $[M\,L\,T^{-2}]$

## Explanation

Since $[v^2] = [L^2\,T^{-2}]$ and $[r] = [L]$,
$$
\left[\frac{mv^2}{r}\right] = \frac{[M][L^2\,T^{-2}]}{[L]} = [M\,L\,T^{-2}] ,
$$
which are the dimensions of a force. Forgetting the division by $r$ gives the dimensions of an energy, $[M\,L^2\,T^{-2}]$.
