Given $\vec{A} = (1,\ 2,\ 0)$ and $\vec{B} = (3,\ -1,\ 0)$, what is $\vec{A} \times \vec{B}$?

- $7\,\hat{k}$
- $5\,\hat{k}$
- $\hat{k}$
- $-7\,\hat{k}$

## Explanation

For two vectors of the $xy$ plane only the $z$ component survives, $\vec{A} \times \vec{B} = (A_xB_y - A_yB_x)\,\hat{k} = (1 \cdot (-1) - 2 \cdot 3)\,\hat{k} = -7\,\hat{k}$. The negative sign means that the turn from $\vec{A}$ to $\vec{B}$ is clockwise. The vector $7\,\hat{k}$ is $\vec{B} \times \vec{A}$, and the number $1$ is the scalar product $\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B}$.
