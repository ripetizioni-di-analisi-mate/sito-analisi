Since 2019 the kilogram has been defined by fixing the exact value of which constant of nature?

- the Planck constant
- the speed of light in vacuum
- a transition frequency of the cesium-133 atom
- the free-fall acceleration $g$

## Explanation

In the present SI each base unit is defined by fixing the exact value of a constant of nature. The kilogram is fixed by the Planck constant, while the speed of light in vacuum defines the meter and the cesium-133 transition frequency defines the second. The free-fall acceleration varies from place to place, therefore it cannot define a unit.
