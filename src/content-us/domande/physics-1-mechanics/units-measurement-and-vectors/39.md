Newton's law of gravitation is $F = G\,\dfrac{m_1 m_2}{r^2}$. What are the dimensions of the constant $G$?

- $[M\,L^3\,T^{-2}]$
- $[M^{-1}\,L\,T^{-2}]$
- $[M^{-2}\,L^3\,T^{-2}]$
- $[M^{-1}\,L^3\,T^{-2}]$

## Explanation

Solving for $G$ gives $G = \dfrac{F\,r^2}{m_1 m_2}$, hence
$$
[G] = \frac{[M\,L\,T^{-2}][L^2]}{[M^2]} = [M^{-1}\,L^3\,T^{-2}] .
$$
Forgetting the factor $r^2$ gives $[M^{-1}\,L\,T^{-2}]$, and forgetting the mass inside the force gives $[M^{-2}\,L^3\,T^{-2}]$.
