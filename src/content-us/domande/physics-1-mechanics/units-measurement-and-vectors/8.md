A tablet contains $350\,\mathrm{mg}$ of an active ingredient. What is this mass in kilograms?

- $3.5 \times 10^{-1}\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $3.5 \times 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $3.5 \times 10^{2}\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $3.5 \times 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

For mass the prefixes apply to the gram, so $1\,\mathrm{mg} = 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{g} = 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{kg}$ and
$$
350\,\mathrm{mg} = 350 \times 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{kg} = 3.5 \times 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{kg} .
$$
Applying milli to the kilogram gives $0.35\,\mathrm{kg}$, and multiplying instead of dividing when passing from grams to kilograms gives $350\,\mathrm{kg}$.
