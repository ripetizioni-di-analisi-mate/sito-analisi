Two vectors of magnitudes $3.0$ and $4.0$ form the angle $30^\circ$. What is the magnitude of their vector product?

- $10$
- $12$
- $6.0$
- $7.0$

## Explanation

By definition $|\vec{A} \times \vec{B}| = A\,B\sin\varphi = 3.0 \cdot 4.0 \cdot \sin 30^\circ = 12 \cdot 0.50 = 6.0$. The value $10$ uses $\cos 30^\circ$, which belongs to the scalar product, and $12$ is the magnitude for perpendicular vectors.
