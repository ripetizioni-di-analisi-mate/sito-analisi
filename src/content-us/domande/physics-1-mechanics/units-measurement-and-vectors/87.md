A force of $25\,\mathrm{N}$ is applied at a distance $r = 0.40\,\mathrm{m}$ from a pivot, and the angle between $\vec{r}$ and $\vec{F}$ is $30^\circ$. What is the magnitude of the torque?

- $8.7\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $5.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $63\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$

## Explanation

The **torque of a force** is $\vec{\tau} = \vec{r} \times \vec{F}$, with magnitude $\tau = r\,F\sin\varphi = 0.40\,\mathrm{m} \cdot 25\,\mathrm{N} \cdot \sin 30^\circ = 5.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$. The value $8.7\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$ uses $\cos 30^\circ$, and $10\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$ treats the force as perpendicular to $\vec{r}$.
