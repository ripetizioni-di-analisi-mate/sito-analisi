A particle has velocity $\vec{v} = (-6.0\,\hat{\imath} + 2.5\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is its speed?

- $8.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $42\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The speed is the magnitude of the velocity, $v = \sqrt{v_x^2 + v_y^2} = \sqrt{36 + 6.25}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 6.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The square of a negative component is positive, so the sign of $v_x$ does not affect the magnitude. The value $42$ is the sum of the squares before the square root.
