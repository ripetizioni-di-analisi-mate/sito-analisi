A particle has velocity $\vec{v} = (-7.0\,\hat{\imath} + 2.4\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is its speed?

- $9.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $55\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $7.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The speed is the magnitude of the velocity, $v = \sqrt{v_x^2 + v_y^2} = \sqrt{49 + 5.76}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 7.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The square of a negative component is positive, so the sign of $v_x$ does not affect the magnitude. The value $55$ is the sum of the squares before the square root, and $9.4$ adds the absolute values of the components.
