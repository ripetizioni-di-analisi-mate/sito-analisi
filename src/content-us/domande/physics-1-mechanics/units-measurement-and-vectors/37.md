Let $g$ be an acceleration and $h$ a height. What are the dimensions of $\sqrt{2gh}$?

- $[L\,T^{-1}]$
- $[L^2\,T^{-2}]$
- $[L\,T^{-2}]$
- $[T]$

## Explanation

The factor $2$ is dimensionless, and $[g\,h] = [L\,T^{-2}][L] = [L^2\,T^{-2}]$. The square root halves every exponent, therefore $\left[\sqrt{2gh}\right] = [L\,T^{-1}]$, the dimensions of a velocity. The dimensions $[L^2\,T^{-2}]$ are those of $2gh$ before the square root.
