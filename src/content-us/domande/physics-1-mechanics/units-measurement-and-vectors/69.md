What is the scalar product of $\vec{A} = (2,\ -3,\ 1)$ and $\vec{B} = (4,\ 1,\ -2)$?

- $3$
- $13$
- $7$
- $-3$

## Explanation

By the **scalar product in components**,
$$
\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B} = 2 \cdot 4 + (-3) \cdot 1 + 1 \cdot (-2) = 8 - 3 - 2 = 3 .
$$
The value $13$ ignores the minus signs, and $7$ drops the sign of the last product.
