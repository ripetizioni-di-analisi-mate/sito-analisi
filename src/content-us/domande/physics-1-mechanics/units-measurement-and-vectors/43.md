Two forces of magnitudes $7\,\mathrm{N}$ and $3\,\mathrm{N}$ act on the same body, with any directions. Which value can the magnitude of their resultant NOT take?

- $4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $7\,\mathrm{N}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N}$
- $2\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The resultant is largest when the forces have the same sense, $7\,\mathrm{N} + 3\,\mathrm{N} = 10\,\mathrm{N}$, and smallest when they have opposite senses, $7\,\mathrm{N} - 3\,\mathrm{N} = 4\,\mathrm{N}$. As the angle between them varies, the magnitude takes every value between $4\,\mathrm{N}$ and $10\,\mathrm{N}$, therefore $2\,\mathrm{N}$ cannot be reached.
