Two forces of $10\,\mathrm{N}$ each act on the same point, and the angle between them is $120^\circ$. What is the magnitude of their resultant?

- $20\,\mathrm{N}$
- $17\,\mathrm{N}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Take the first force along the $x$ axis, $\vec{F}_1 = (10,\ 0)\,\mathrm{N}$, so that $\vec{F}_2 = (10\cos 120^\circ,\ 10\sin 120^\circ)\,\mathrm{N} \approx (-5.0,\ 8.66)\,\mathrm{N}$. The resultant is $(5.0,\ 8.66)\,\mathrm{N}$, with magnitude $\sqrt{5.0^2 + 8.66^2}\,\mathrm{N} = 10\,\mathrm{N}$. The value $17\,\mathrm{N}$ is the resultant for an angle of $60^\circ$.
