The pascal is defined as $1\,\mathrm{Pa} = 1\,\mathrm{N/m^2}$. Which expression gives it in SI base units?

- $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s^2}$
- $\mathrm{kg/(m^2\cdot s^2)}$
- $\mathrm{kg/(m\cdot s^2)}$
- $\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s^2}$

## Explanation

Since $1\,\mathrm{N} = 1\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s^2}$, dividing by $\mathrm{m^2}$ gives
$$
1\,\mathrm{Pa} = \frac{1\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s^2}}{1\,\mathrm{m^2}} = 1\,\mathrm{kg/(m\cdot s^2)} .
$$
The meter in the numerator cancels one of the two meters in the denominator; keeping $\mathrm{m^2}$ in the denominator forgets that cancellation.
