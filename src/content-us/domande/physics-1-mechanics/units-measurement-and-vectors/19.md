How many significant figures does the measured value $0.03050\,\mathrm{m}$ carry?

- $6$
- $4$
- $3$
- $5$

## Explanation

The **significant figures** are counted from the leftmost nonzero digit, so the digits $3$, $0$, $5$, $0$ count and the two zeros on the left do not, since they only place the decimal point. The final zero is a measured digit, therefore it counts as well. In scientific notation the value is $3.050 \times 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m}$.
