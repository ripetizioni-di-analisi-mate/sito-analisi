A car moving at $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ east turns and moves at $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ north. What is the magnitude of the change in velocity $\Delta\vec{v}$?

- $14\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The change in velocity is the vector difference $\Delta\vec{v} = \vec{v}_2 - \vec{v}_1$. The two velocities are perpendicular, so
$$
|\Delta\vec{v}| = \sqrt{10^2 + 10^2}\,\mathrm{m/s} \approx 14\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The magnitude of the velocity is unchanged, yet its direction changes, therefore $\Delta\vec{v}$ is not zero.
