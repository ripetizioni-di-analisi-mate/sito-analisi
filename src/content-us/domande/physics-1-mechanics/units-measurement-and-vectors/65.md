Given $\vec{A} = (1,\ -2)$ and $\vec{B} = (4,\ 1)$, what is $3\vec{A} - \vec{B}$?

- $(7,\ -5)$
- $(-1,\ -7)$
- $(-1,\ -5)$
- $(-11,\ -5)$

## Explanation

By the **operations in components**, $3\vec{A} = (3,\ -6)$, and subtracting $\vec{B}$ axis by axis gives $(3 - 4,\ -6 - 1) = (-1,\ -7)$. The vector $(7,\ -5)$ is $3\vec{A} + \vec{B}$, and $(-11,\ -5)$ is $\vec{A} - 3\vec{B}$.
