A displacement $\vec{A}$ has magnitude $3.0\,\mathrm{m}$ and points east. What is the vector $-2\vec{A}$?

- $6.0\,\mathrm{m}$ west
- $6.0\,\mathrm{m}$ east
- $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ west
- $3.0\,\mathrm{m}$ west

## Explanation

The vector $k\vec{A}$ has magnitude $|k|\,A$, the direction of $\vec{A}$ and the opposite sense when $k < 0$. With $k = -2$ the magnitude is $2 \cdot 3.0\,\mathrm{m} = 6.0\,\mathrm{m}$ and the sense is reversed, hence $6.0\,\mathrm{m}$ west. The vector $-\vec{A}$ alone has magnitude $3.0\,\mathrm{m}$.
