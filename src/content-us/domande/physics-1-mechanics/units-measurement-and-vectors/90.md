In the $xy$ plane, $\vec{A}$ makes the angle $30^\circ$ and $\vec{B}$ the angle $100^\circ$ with the positive $x$ axis, both measured counterclockwise. In which direction does $\vec{A} \times \vec{B}$ point?

- along the negative $z$ axis
- along the positive $z$ axis
- along $\vec{A}$
- along $\vec{A} + \vec{B}$

## Explanation

The turn from $\vec{A}$ to $\vec{B}$ through the angle between them is counterclockwise, by $100^\circ - 30^\circ = 70^\circ$. By the right-hand rule the fingers turn counterclockwise in the $xy$ plane and the thumb points along the positive $z$ axis; equivalently, $A_xB_y - A_yB_x = A\,B\sin 70^\circ > 0$. The product is perpendicular to the plane, so it cannot lie along $\vec{A}$ or $\vec{A} + \vec{B}$.
