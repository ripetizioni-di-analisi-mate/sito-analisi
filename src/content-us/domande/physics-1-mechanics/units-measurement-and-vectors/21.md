A cart covers $48.6\,\mathrm{m}$ in $3.1\,\mathrm{s}$. With the correct number of significant figures, what is its average speed?

- $16\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $15.7\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $15.68\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $15.677\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The calculator gives $\dfrac{48.6}{3.1} \approx 15.677$. By the rule for **significant figures in a calculation**, a quotient keeps as many significant figures as the factor with the fewest, and $3.1\,\mathrm{s}$ has two, therefore the speed is written $16\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Three significant figures would follow the distance, which is the more precise datum.
