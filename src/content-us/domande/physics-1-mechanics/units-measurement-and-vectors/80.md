What is the vector product $\hat{k} \times \hat{\jmath}$?

- $-\hat{\imath}$
- $\hat{\imath}$
- $0$
- $\hat{k}$

## Explanation

The unit vectors satisfy $\hat{\jmath} \times \hat{k} = \hat{\imath}$, and reversing the order reverses the sense of a **vector product**. Therefore $\hat{k} \times \hat{\jmath} = -\,\hat{\jmath} \times \hat{k} = -\hat{\imath}$. The product vanishes only for parallel vectors, and it is perpendicular to both factors, so it cannot be $\hat{k}$.
