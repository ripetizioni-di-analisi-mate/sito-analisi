A force $\vec{F}$ acts on a body moving with velocity $\vec{v}$. In which unit is the scalar product $\vec{F} \cdot \vec{v}$ measured?

- $\mathrm{J}$
- $\mathrm{N\cdot s}$
- $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

The unit of a scalar product is the product of the units of the two factors, so $\vec{F} \cdot \vec{v}$ is measured in $\mathrm{N \cdot m/s} = \mathrm{J/s}$, which is the watt. The joule is the unit of $\vec{F} \cdot \vec{d}$, where the second factor is a displacement.
