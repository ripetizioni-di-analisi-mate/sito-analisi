Which statement about the unit vectors $\hat{\imath}$, $\hat{\jmath}$, $\hat{k}$ of a Cartesian frame is correct?

- They are dimensionless and have magnitude $1$.
- They carry the unit of the vector they decompose.
- Their magnitude equals the magnitude of the vector they decompose.
- They are measured in meters.

## Explanation

The **unit vectors** are dimensionless vectors of magnitude $1$ pointing along the axes. In $\vec{A} = A_x\,\hat{\imath} + A_y\,\hat{\jmath} + A_z\,\hat{k}$ the unit of $\vec{A}$ is carried by the components, so the same unit vectors serve for displacements, velocities and forces.
