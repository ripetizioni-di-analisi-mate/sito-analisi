What is the vector product of $\vec{A} = 2\,\hat{\imath}$ and $\vec{B} = 3\,\hat{\jmath}$, in this order?

- $-6\,\hat{k}$
- $5\,\hat{k}$
- $6\,\hat{k}$
- $0$

## Explanation

Since $\hat{\imath} \times \hat{\jmath} = \hat{k}$, the product is $\vec{A} \times \vec{B} = 2 \cdot 3\,(\hat{\imath} \times \hat{\jmath}) = 6\,\hat{k}$. The vector $-6\,\hat{k}$ is $\vec{B} \times \vec{A}$. The value $0$ is the scalar product of the two perpendicular vectors, while their vector product has the largest possible magnitude $A\,B$.
