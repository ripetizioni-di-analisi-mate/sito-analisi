A force $\vec{F} = (3.0,\ 4.0)\,\mathrm{N}$ acts during the displacement $\vec{d} = (5.0,\ -2.0)\,\mathrm{m}$. What work does it do?

- $23\,\mathrm{J}$
- $7.0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-7.0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $27\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The work is the scalar product, computed in components:
$$
W = \vec{F} \cdot \vec{d} = (3.0 \cdot 5.0 + 4.0 \cdot (-2.0))\,\mathrm{J} = (15 - 8.0)\,\mathrm{J} = 7.0\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
The value $23\,\mathrm{J}$ ignores the sign of $d_y$, and $27\,\mathrm{J}$ is the product $F\,d$ of the magnitudes, which equals the work only when the two vectors are parallel.
