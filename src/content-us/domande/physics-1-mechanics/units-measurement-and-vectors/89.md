The vector $\vec{A}$ points along the positive $x$ axis and $\vec{B}$ along the positive $z$ axis. In which direction does $\vec{A} \times \vec{B}$ point?

- along the positive $y$ axis
- along the positive $z$ axis
- along the negative $y$ axis
- along the negative $z$ axis

## Explanation

The product is proportional to $\hat{\imath} \times \hat{k}$. Since $\hat{k} \times \hat{\imath} = \hat{\jmath}$ and reversing the order reverses the sense, $\hat{\imath} \times \hat{k} = -\hat{\jmath}$. The vector product is perpendicular to both factors, so it cannot lie along the $z$ axis, which is the direction of $\vec{B}$.
