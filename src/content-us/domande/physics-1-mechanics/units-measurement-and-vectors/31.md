Let $v$ be a velocity, $g$ an acceleration, $L$ a length and $t$ a time. Which combination is dimensionless?

- $\dfrac{v}{gL}$
- $\dfrac{g\,t^2}{v}$
- $\dfrac{v^2}{gL}$
- $\dfrac{v\,t^2}{L}$

## Explanation

The **dimensions** of each combination follow from those of its factors. For $\dfrac{v^2}{gL}$,
$$
\left[\frac{v^2}{gL}\right] = \frac{[L^2\,T^{-2}]}{[L\,T^{-2}][L]} = [1] .
$$
The other three combinations are not dimensionless: $\left[\dfrac{v}{gL}\right] = [L^{-1}\,T]$, while $\left[\dfrac{g\,t^2}{v}\right]$ and $\left[\dfrac{v\,t^2}{L}\right]$ both reduce to $[T]$.
