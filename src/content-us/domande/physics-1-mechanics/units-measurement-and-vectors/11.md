The density of air at room conditions is $1.2\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. What is this density in $\mathrm{g/cm^3}$?

- $1.2 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $1.2 \times 10^{-1}\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $1.2 \times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $1.2 \times 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$

## Explanation

Since $1\,\mathrm{kg} = 10^{3}\,\mathrm{g}$ and $1\,\mathrm{m^3} = (10^{2}\,\mathrm{cm})^3 = 10^{6}\,\mathrm{cm^3}$,
$$
1.2\,\frac{\mathrm{kg}}{\mathrm{m^3}} = 1.2 \cdot \frac{10^{3}\,\mathrm{g}}{10^{6}\,\mathrm{cm^3}} = 1.2 \times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{g/cm^3} .
$$
Squaring the factor of the meter instead of cubing it gives $1.2 \times 10^{-1}$, and leaving the kilogram unconverted gives $1.2 \times 10^{-6}$.
