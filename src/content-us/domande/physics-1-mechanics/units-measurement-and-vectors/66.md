A force of $80\,\mathrm{N}$ in the $xy$ plane makes the angle $25^\circ$ with the positive $y$ axis and points into the first quadrant. What is its $x$ component?

- $33.8\,\mathrm{N}$
- $72.5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $189\,\mathrm{N}$
- $88.3\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The angle with the positive $x$ axis is $90^\circ - 25^\circ = 65^\circ$, hence $F_x = 80\,\mathrm{N} \cdot \cos 65^\circ = 80\,\mathrm{N} \cdot \sin 25^\circ \approx 33.8\,\mathrm{N}$. The value $72.5\,\mathrm{N}$ is $80\,\mathrm{N} \cdot \cos 25^\circ$, the $y$ component, obtained by using the given angle as if it were measured from the $x$ axis.
