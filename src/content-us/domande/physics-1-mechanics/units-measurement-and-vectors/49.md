A ball hits a wall at $8.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and bounces straight back at $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the magnitude of the change in its velocity?

- $14\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $7.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Taking the initial sense as positive, $v_1 = 8.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_2 = -6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$, hence $\Delta v = v_2 - v_1 = -14\,\mathrm{m/s}$, with magnitude $14\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The value $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ ignores the reversal of the sense, and $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ treats the two velocities as perpendicular.
