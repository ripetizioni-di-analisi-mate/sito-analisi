A train travels at $90\,\mathrm{km/h}$. What is its speed in meters per second?

- $324\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $25\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1500\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Each unit is replaced by its value in the new units, $1\,\mathrm{km} = 1000\,\mathrm{m}$ and $1\,\mathrm{h} = 3600\,\mathrm{s}$, hence
$$
90\,\frac{\mathrm{km}}{\mathrm{h}} = 90 \cdot \frac{1000\,\mathrm{m}}{3600\,\mathrm{s}} = 25\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
Multiplying by $3.6$ instead of dividing gives $324\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and using $60\,\mathrm{s}$ for the hour gives $1500\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
