A vector of the $xy$ plane has magnitude $12\,\mathrm{m}$ and $x$ component $A_x = -12\,\mathrm{m}$. What is its $y$ component?

- $A_y = 12\,\mathrm{m}$
- $A_y = -12\,\mathrm{m}$
- $A_y = 17\,\mathrm{m}$
- $A_y = 0$

## Explanation

From $A^2 = A_x^2 + A_y^2$ follows $A_y^2 = (12\,\mathrm{m})^2 - (-12\,\mathrm{m})^2 = 0$, therefore $A_y = 0$. The whole magnitude is along the $x$ axis, so the vector points in the negative $x$ direction and makes the angle $180^\circ$ with the positive $x$ axis.
