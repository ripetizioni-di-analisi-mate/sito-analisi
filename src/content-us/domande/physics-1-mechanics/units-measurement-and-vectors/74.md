A constant force of $40\,\mathrm{N}$ acts on a body that moves $5.0\,\mathrm{m}$, and the angle between the force and the displacement is $150^\circ$. What work does the force do?

- $173\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-100\,\mathrm{J}$
- $100\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-173\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The **work of a constant force** is $W = F\,d\cos\varphi = 40\,\mathrm{N} \cdot 5.0\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \cos 150^\circ \approx -173\,\mathrm{J}$. The work is negative because the force has a component opposite to the displacement. The values $\pm 100\,\mathrm{J}$ use $\sin 150^\circ = 0.50$ in place of the cosine.
