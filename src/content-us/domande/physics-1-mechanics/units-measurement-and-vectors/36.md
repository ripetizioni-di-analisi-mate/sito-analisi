At high speed the drag force on a body is written $F = b\,v^2$, where $v$ is the speed. What is the SI unit of $b$?

- $\mathrm{kg/s}$
- $\mathrm{kg\cdot m}$
- $\mathrm{kg/(m\cdot s)}$
- $\mathrm{kg/m}$

## Explanation

Solving for $b$ gives $b = \dfrac{F}{v^2}$, hence
$$
[b] = \frac{[M\,L\,T^{-2}]}{[L^2\,T^{-2}]} = [M\,L^{-1}] ,
$$
measured in $\mathrm{kg/m}$. The unit $\mathrm{kg/s}$ belongs to the coefficient of a drag proportional to $v$, not to $v^2$.
