A stopwatch reads $105.3\,\mathrm{s}$ at the end of a race and $2.47\,\mathrm{s}$ at the start. With the correct number of figures, what is the duration of the race?

- $102.8\,\mathrm{s}$
- $102.83\,\mathrm{s}$
- $103\,\mathrm{s}$
- $102.830\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The difference is $105.3 - 2.47 = 102.83$ on the calculator. In a sum or a difference the result keeps as many decimal places as the term with the fewest, and $105.3$ has one decimal place, therefore the duration is $102.8\,\mathrm{s}$. The value $103\,\mathrm{s}$ applies the rule for products, which counts significant figures instead of decimal places.
