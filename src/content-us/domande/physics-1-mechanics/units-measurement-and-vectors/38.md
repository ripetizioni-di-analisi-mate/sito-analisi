In a correct physical relation, two terms can be added only when

- they have the same numerical value.
- they are both vector quantities.
- they have the same dimensions.
- they carry the same number of significant figures.

## Explanation

By the **principle of dimensional homogeneity** only terms with the same dimensions are added or subtracted, as in $x = x_0 + v_0 t$, where both terms are lengths. A length and a time have no common unit, so their sum has no physical meaning whatever the numbers are.
