Which of the following is one of the seven base quantities of the International System (SI)?

- force
- energy
- velocity
- electric current

## Explanation

The SI takes seven base quantities as independent: length, mass, time, electric current, thermodynamic temperature, amount of substance and luminous intensity. Force, energy and velocity are **derived quantities**, defined from the base ones by a relation such as $F = ma$, $W = Fd$ and $v = \dfrac{\Delta x}{\Delta t}$.
