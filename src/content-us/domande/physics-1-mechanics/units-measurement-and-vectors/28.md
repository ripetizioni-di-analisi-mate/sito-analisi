A length of $1.2 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{m}$ is added to a length of $38\,\mathrm{m}$. With the correct number of figures, what is the total length?

- $1.2 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1238\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.24 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.238 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The value $1.2 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{m}$ is known only to the hundreds of meters, since its last significant digit is the $2$ of $1200$. In a sum the result keeps the precision of the least precise term, therefore $1238\,\mathrm{m}$ is rounded to the hundreds and the total is $1.2 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{m}$. The other writings claim digits that the datum $1.2 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{m}$ does not carry.
