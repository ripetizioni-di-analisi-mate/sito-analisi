Two displacements of $5.0\,\mathrm{m}$ and $12\,\mathrm{m}$ are perpendicular to each other. What is the magnitude of their sum?

- $17\,\mathrm{m}$
- $13\,\mathrm{m}$
- $7.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4.1\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The two displacements and their sum form a right triangle, so by the Pythagorean theorem
$$
|\vec{A} + \vec{B}| = \sqrt{5.0^2 + 12^2}\,\mathrm{m} = 13\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $17\,\mathrm{m}$ adds the magnitudes, which is correct only for vectors with the same sense, and $7.0\,\mathrm{m}$ holds only for opposite senses.
