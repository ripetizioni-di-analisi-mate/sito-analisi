A displacement of magnitude $20\,\mathrm{m}$ makes the angle $30^\circ$ with the positive $x$ axis. What is its $x$ component?

- $10.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $23.1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $40.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $17.3\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

With the angle measured from the positive $x$ axis, $A_x = A\cos\theta = 20\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \cos 30^\circ \approx 17.3\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $10.0\,\mathrm{m}$ uses the sine and is the $y$ component. A component cannot exceed the magnitude, so dividing by $\cos\theta$ or $\sin\theta$ gives impossible values.
