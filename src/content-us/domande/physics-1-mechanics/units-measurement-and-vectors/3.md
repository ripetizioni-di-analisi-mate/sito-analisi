Which expression gives the joule in SI base units?

- $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s^2}$
- $\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s^2}$
- $\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s^3}$

## Explanation

By definition $1\,\mathrm{J} = 1\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$, and $1\,\mathrm{N} = 1\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s^2}$, therefore $1\,\mathrm{J} = 1\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s^2}$. The unit $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s^2}$ is the newton itself, and $\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s^3}$ is the watt, that is, a joule per second.
