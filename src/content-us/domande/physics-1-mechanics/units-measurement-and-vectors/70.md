Which pair of vectors is perpendicular?

- $(2,\ 3)$ and $(3,\ 2)$
- $(2,\ 3)$ and $(3,\ -2)$
- $(2,\ 3)$ and $(-2,\ -3)$
- $(2,\ 3)$ and $(4,\ 6)$

## Explanation

Two nonzero vectors are perpendicular exactly when their **scalar product** vanishes. For $(2,\ 3)$ and $(3,\ -2)$ the product is $2 \cdot 3 + 3 \cdot (-2) = 0$. With $(3,\ 2)$, $(-2,\ -3)$ and $(4,\ 6)$ the products are $12$, $-13$ and $26$, and the last two of these vectors are in fact parallel to $(2,\ 3)$.
