The position of a damped body is $x = A\,e^{-bt}$, where $A$ is a length and $t$ a time. What are the dimensions of $b$?

- $[T^{-1}]$
- $[T]$
- $[1]$
- $[L\,T^{-1}]$

## Explanation

By the **principle of dimensional homogeneity** the argument of the exponential is dimensionless, so $[b\,t] = [1]$ and $[b] = [T^{-1}]$. The dimensions of $x$ are already supplied by $A$, therefore $b$ carries no length.
