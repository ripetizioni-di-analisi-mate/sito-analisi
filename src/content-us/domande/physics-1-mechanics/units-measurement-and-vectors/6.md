A power plant delivers $4.5\,\mathrm{GW}$. What is this power in megawatts?

- $4.5 \times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{MW}$
- $4.5 \times 10^{15}\,\mathrm{MW}$
- $4.5 \times 10^{9}\,\mathrm{MW}$
- $4.5 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{MW}$

## Explanation

The prefix giga is $10^{9}$ and mega is $10^{6}$, hence $1\,\mathrm{GW} = \dfrac{10^{9}}{10^{6}}\,\mathrm{MW} = 10^{3}\,\mathrm{MW}$ and
$$
4.5\,\mathrm{GW} = 4.5 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{MW} .
$$
Multiplying the two factors gives $10^{15}$, and stopping at $4.5 \times 10^{9}$ converts to watts and leaves the result in the wrong unit.
