Which writing of $0.000507\,\mathrm{kg}$ in scientific notation keeps exactly its significant figures?

- $5.07 \times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $5.070 \times 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $5.07 \times 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $5.1 \times 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The value has three significant figures, $5$, $0$ and $7$, and the decimal point moves four places to the right to give a number between $1$ and $10$, hence $5.07 \times 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{kg}$. The writing $5.070 \times 10^{-4}$ claims a fourth measured digit, and $5.1 \times 10^{-4}$ drops one; $5.07 \times 10^{-3}$ is ten times the given mass.
