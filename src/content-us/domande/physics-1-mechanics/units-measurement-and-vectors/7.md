A beaker holds $250\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ of water. What is this volume in cubic meters?

- $2.5 \times 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $2.5 \times 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $2.5 \times 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m^3}$

## Explanation

The prefix and the symbol form a single unit, so the exponent applies to the prefix: $1\,\mathrm{cm^3} = (10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m})^3 = 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^3}$. Therefore
$$
250\,\mathrm{cm^3} = 250 \times 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^3} = 2.5 \times 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^3} .
$$
The factor $10^{-2}$ not raised to the third power gives $2.5\,\mathrm{m^3}$, and raised only to the second power gives $2.5 \times 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m^3}$.
