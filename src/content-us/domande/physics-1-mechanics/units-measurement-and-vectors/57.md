A vector has components $A_x = 5.0$ and $A_y = -12$. What angle does it make with the positive $x$ axis, measured counterclockwise between $0^\circ$ and $360^\circ$?

- $67.4^\circ$
- $292.6^\circ$
- $247.4^\circ$
- $112.6^\circ$

## Explanation

Since $A_x > 0$, the arctangent gives the angle directly: $\arctan\left(-\dfrac{12}{5.0}\right) \approx -67.4^\circ$. In the range from $0^\circ$ to $360^\circ$ this angle is $360^\circ - 67.4^\circ = 292.6^\circ$, in the fourth quadrant, as the signs $A_x > 0$ and $A_y < 0$ require.
