On a dry road a flat curve can be taken at up to $22\,\mathrm{m/s}$. When the road is wet, the coefficient of static friction is halved. What is the largest safe speed on the wet road?

- $11.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $15.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $31.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The largest speed $\sqrt{\mu_s g r}$ is proportional to $\sqrt{\mu_s}$. Halving $\mu_s$ therefore divides it by $\sqrt{2}$:
$$
v = \dfrac{22\,\mathrm{m/s}}{\sqrt{2}} = 15.6\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
