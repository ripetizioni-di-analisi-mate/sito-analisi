A small sphere of mass $5.0\,\mathrm{g}$ falls in a liquid that exerts the drag force $F_D = b\,v$ with $b = 0.010\,\mathrm{kg/s}$. What is its terminal speed?

- $0.50\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.20\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4.9\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

With linear drag the terminal speed is
$$
v_t = \dfrac{m g}{b} = \dfrac{0.0050\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}}{0.010\,\mathrm{kg/s}} = 4.9\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The mass must be converted to kilograms before the substitution.
