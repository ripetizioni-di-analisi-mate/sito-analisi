A book lies at rest on a horizontal table and no horizontal force acts on it. The coefficient of static friction between book and table is $\mu_s$ and the normal force is $N$. What is the magnitude of the static friction force on the book?

- $0$
- $\mu_s N$
- $\mu_k N$
- $N$

## Explanation

The book is at rest, hence the sum of the horizontal forces is zero. No horizontal force other than friction acts on it, therefore the static friction force is zero. The product $\mu_s N$ is only the largest value static friction can reach, at the threshold of sliding.
