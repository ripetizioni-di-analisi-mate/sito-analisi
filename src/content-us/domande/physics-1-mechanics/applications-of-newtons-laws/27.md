A block rests on a plane whose inclination $\theta$ is slowly increased from $0^\circ$ towards $90^\circ$, with no applied force. How does the normal force change?

- It increases from $0$ to $m g$
- It decreases from $m g$ to $0$
- It stays equal to $m g$
- It decreases from $m g$ to $\dfrac{m g}{2}$

## Explanation

The normal force on an incline is $N = m g\cos\theta$. The cosine decreases from $1$ at $0^\circ$ to $0$ at $90^\circ$, therefore $N$ decreases from $m g$ to zero.
