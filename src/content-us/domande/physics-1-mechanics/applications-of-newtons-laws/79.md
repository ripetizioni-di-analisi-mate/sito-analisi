A car of mass $1200\,\mathrm{kg}$ passes the crest of a hill whose profile has radius of curvature $50\,\mathrm{m}$, at $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the normal force of the road on the car?

- $17.2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $6.37\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $11.8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $5.40\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

At the crest the center lies below the car, hence $m g - N = m\dfrac{v^2}{r}$. Therefore
$$
N = m\left(g - \dfrac{v^2}{r}\right) = 1200\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot (9.81 - 4.50)\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 6.37\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
The normal force is smaller than the weight, $11.8\,\mathrm{kN}$.
