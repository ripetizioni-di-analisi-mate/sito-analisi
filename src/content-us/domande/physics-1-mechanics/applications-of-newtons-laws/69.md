A car takes a flat horizontal curve at constant speed without sliding. In which direction does the friction force from the road on the car point?

- Away from the center of the curve
- Along the velocity
- Towards the center of the curve
- Opposite to the velocity

## Explanation

The car moves at constant speed on a circle, hence its acceleration points towards the center. On a flat road the weight and the normal force are vertical, therefore static friction is the only force that can supply the net force towards the center.
