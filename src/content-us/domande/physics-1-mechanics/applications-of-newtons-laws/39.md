A block is launched up an incline and slides up the slope. In which direction does the kinetic friction force on it point while it moves up?

- Up the slope
- Down the slope
- Perpendicular to the plane
- Horizontally

## Explanation

Kinetic friction is opposite to the velocity of the body relative to the surface. The block moves up the slope, therefore friction points down the slope and adds to the component of the weight along the plane.
