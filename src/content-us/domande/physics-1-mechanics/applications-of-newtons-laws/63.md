A conical pendulum has a string of length $1.0\,\mathrm{m}$ that makes the constant angle $60^\circ$ with the vertical. What is the period of the motion?

- $2.0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.9\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2.8\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The period of a conical pendulum is $\mathcal{T} = 2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{L\cos\theta}{g}}$, therefore
$$
\mathcal{T} = 2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{1.0\,\mathrm{m} \cdot 0.500}{9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}}} = 1.4\,\mathrm{s} .
$$
Omitting $\cos\theta$ gives $2.0\,\mathrm{s}$, and putting it in the denominator gives $2.8\,\mathrm{s}$.
