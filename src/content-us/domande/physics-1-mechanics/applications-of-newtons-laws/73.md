On which quantities does the bank angle of a curve designed to be taken with no sideways friction depend?

- The speed and the radius only
- The speed, the radius and the mass
- The mass and the radius only
- The speed, the radius and $\mu_s$

## Explanation

The bank angle satisfies $\tan\theta = \dfrac{v^2}{g r}$. The mass cancels when the two equations are divided, and friction does not appear because the design assumes none, therefore $\theta$ depends on $v$ and $r$ alone.
