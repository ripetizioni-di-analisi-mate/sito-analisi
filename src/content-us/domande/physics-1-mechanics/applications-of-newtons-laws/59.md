A stone of mass $0.50\,\mathrm{kg}$ is whirled on a horizontal frictionless table at the end of a string of length $0.90\,\mathrm{m}$, and the string breaks when its tension exceeds $50\,\mathrm{N}$. What is the largest speed the stone can have?

- $90\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $13\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6.7\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $9.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The tension supplies the net force toward the center, $T = m\dfrac{v^2}{r}$. Therefore
$$
v_{\max} = \sqrt{\dfrac{T r}{m}} = \sqrt{\dfrac{50\,\mathrm{N} \cdot 0.90\,\mathrm{m}}{0.50\,\mathrm{kg}}} = 9.5\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
Using the diameter in place of the radius gives $13\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
