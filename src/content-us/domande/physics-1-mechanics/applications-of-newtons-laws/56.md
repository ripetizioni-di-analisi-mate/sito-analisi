A particle moves on a circle with constant speed. In which direction does the net force on it point?

- Along the velocity
- Away from the center of the circle
- There is no net force
- Toward the center of the circle

## Explanation

In uniform circular motion the acceleration is radial and directed toward the center, with magnitude $\dfrac{v^2}{r}$. Since $\vec{F}_{\text{net}} = m\vec{a}$, the net force points toward the center as well.
