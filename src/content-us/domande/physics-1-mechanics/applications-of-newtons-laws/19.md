A puck is launched at $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ along a horizontal surface with $\mu_k = 0.10$. How long does it take to stop?

- $2.0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $40\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4.1\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The deceleration is $\mu_k g = 0.981\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and is constant, therefore the speed falls to zero after
$$
t = \dfrac{v_0}{\mu_k g} = \dfrac{4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}}{0.981\,\mathrm{m/s^2}} = 4.1\,\mathrm{s} .
$$
