A loaded truck and the same truck empty take the same flat curve, with the same tyres. How do the largest speeds at which they can take the curve without sliding compare?

- The loaded truck can go faster
- The empty truck can go faster
- They are equal
- It depends on the radius of the curve

## Explanation

The largest speed on a flat curve is $\sqrt{\mu_s g r}$, which does not contain the mass. A larger mass requires a larger friction force, but the largest friction force $\mu_s m g$ grows in the same proportion, therefore the two speeds are equal.
