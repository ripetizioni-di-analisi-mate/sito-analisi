Why can the string of a conical pendulum never be exactly horizontal?

- The tension would be zero
- The tension would have no vertical component to balance the weight
- The speed of the ball would be zero
- The net force would point away from the center

## Explanation

The ball has no vertical acceleration, hence $T\cos\theta = m g$. A horizontal string has $\cos\theta = 0$, so no finite tension could balance the weight. The angle is therefore always smaller than $90^\circ$, and $T = \dfrac{m g}{\cos\theta}$ grows without bound as $\theta$ approaches $90^\circ$.
