A crate is pushed across a horizontal floor at constant velocity by a horizontal force of $80\,\mathrm{N}$. What is the magnitude of the kinetic friction force on the crate?

- Smaller than $80\,\mathrm{N}$
- Larger than $80\,\mathrm{N}$
- Zero
- Equal to $80\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The velocity is constant, hence the acceleration is zero and the net force vanishes. The only horizontal forces are the push and kinetic friction, therefore the friction force has magnitude $80\,\mathrm{N}$ and points opposite to the push.
