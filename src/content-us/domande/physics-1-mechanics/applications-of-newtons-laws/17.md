A car brakes with the wheels locked on a level road. If its initial speed is doubled, by what factor does the braking distance change?

- $2$
- $\sqrt{2}$
- $1$
- $4$

## Explanation

The braking distance is $d = \dfrac{v_0^2}{2\mu_k g}$, which is proportional to the square of the initial speed. Doubling $v_0$ therefore multiplies the distance by $2^2 = 4$.
