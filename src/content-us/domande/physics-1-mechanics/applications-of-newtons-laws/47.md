A body of mass $0.010\,\mathrm{kg}$ falls in a fluid with drag $F_D = b\,v$ and $b = 0.050\,\mathrm{kg/s}$. What is the time constant of the motion?

- $5.0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.00050\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.20\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The time constant of a fall with linear drag is
$$
\tau = \dfrac{m}{b} = \dfrac{0.010\,\mathrm{kg}}{0.050\,\mathrm{kg/s}} = 0.20\,\mathrm{s} .
$$
The quotient $\dfrac{m g}{b} = 1.96\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is the terminal speed, which is a different quantity.
