A body is released from rest in a fluid that exerts a drag force increasing with the speed. What is the magnitude of its acceleration just after release?

- $0$
- $\dfrac{g}{2}$
- $g$
- $\dfrac{b}{m}\,g$

## Explanation

At the instant of release the speed is zero, hence the drag force is zero and the only force is the weight. Newton's second law therefore gives an acceleration of magnitude $g$, which then decreases as the speed grows.
