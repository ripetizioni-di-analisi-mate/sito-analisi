A body falls with quadratic drag $F_D = D\,v^2$. At what speed is the drag force equal to half the weight?

- $0.71\,v_t$
- $0.50\,v_t$
- $0.25\,v_t$
- $1.41\,v_t$

## Explanation

The terminal speed satisfies $D v_t^2 = m g$. The condition $D v^2 = \dfrac{m g}{2}$ therefore gives $v^2 = \dfrac{v_t^2}{2}$, that is
$$
v = \dfrac{v_t}{\sqrt{2}} = 0.71\,v_t .
$$
The value $0.50\,v_t$ is the answer for linear drag.
