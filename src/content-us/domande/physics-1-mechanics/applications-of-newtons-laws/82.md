A ball of mass $0.20\,\mathrm{kg}$ on a string of length $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$ passes the top of a vertical circle at $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the tension of the string at that point?

- $1.6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $5.6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $3.6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

At the top both the tension and the weight point downwards, towards the center, hence $T + m g = m\dfrac{v^2}{r}$. Therefore
$$
T = m\left(\dfrac{v^2}{r} - g\right) = 0.20\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot (18.0 - 9.81)\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 1.6\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
