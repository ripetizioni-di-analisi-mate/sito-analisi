In the model of fluid resistance $F_D = D\,v^2$, in which unit is the constant $D$ measured?

- $\mathrm{kg/s}$
- $\mathrm{kg/m}$
- $\mathrm{N \cdot s/m}$
- $\mathrm{kg \cdot m}$

## Explanation

The product $D\,v^2$ is a force, hence $D$ has the units of $\mathrm{N} \cdot \mathrm{s^2/m^2} = \mathrm{kg \cdot m/s^2} \cdot \mathrm{s^2/m^2} = \mathrm{kg/m}$. The unit $\mathrm{kg/s}$, equal to $\mathrm{N \cdot s/m}$, belongs to the constant $b$ of the linear model.
