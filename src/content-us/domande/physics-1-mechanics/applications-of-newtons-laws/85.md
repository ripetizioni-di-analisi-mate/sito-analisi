A frame of reference translates with acceleration $\vec{A}$ relative to an inertial frame. What is the inertial force on a body of mass $m$ in that frame?

- $-m\vec{A}$
- $m\vec{A}$
- $-m\vec{g}$
- $\vec{0}$

## Explanation

In a frame translating with acceleration $\vec{A}$ the equation of motion is $\vec{F}_{\text{net}} + \vec{F}_{\text{in}} = m\vec{a}'$ with $\vec{F}_{\text{in}} = -m\vec{A}$. The inertial force is opposite to the acceleration of the frame.
