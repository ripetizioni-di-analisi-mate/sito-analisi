A body falls from rest with quadratic drag $F_D = D\,v^2$. What is the magnitude of its acceleration when its speed equals half the terminal speed?

- $7.4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $4.9\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $9.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Since $D v_t^2 = m g$, at $v = \dfrac{v_t}{2}$ the drag is $\dfrac{m g}{4}$. Newton's second law gives
$$
a = g - \dfrac{g}{4} = \dfrac{3}{4}\,g = 7.4\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
The value $4.9\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ is the answer for linear drag, where the drag at half the terminal speed is half the weight.
