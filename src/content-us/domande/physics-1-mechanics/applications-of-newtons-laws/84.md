A small body hangs from the ceiling of a train that speeds up along a straight horizontal track. Which way does the string tilt from the vertical?

- Towards the front of the train
- Towards the rear of the train
- It stays vertical
- Sideways, perpendicular to the track

## Explanation

In the ground frame the body accelerates forward with the train, so the horizontal component of the tension must point forward. The string therefore hangs from its attachment point backwards, tilted towards the rear. In the frame of the train the same result follows from the inertial force $-m\vec{A}$, which points backwards.
