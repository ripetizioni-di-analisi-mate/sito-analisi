A box of mass $15\,\mathrm{kg}$ rests on the flat bed of a truck that accelerates forward at $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, and the box does not slide. In the frame of the truck, what is the static friction force on the box?

- $30\,\mathrm{N}$ backward
- $147\,\mathrm{N}$ forward
- $30\,\mathrm{N}$ forward
- $7.5\,\mathrm{N}$ forward

## Explanation

In the frame of the truck the box is at rest, and the inertial force $-m\vec{A}$ points backward with magnitude $m A = 15\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 30\,\mathrm{N}$. Static friction balances it, therefore it has magnitude $30\,\mathrm{N}$ and points forward. In the ground frame the same force is the net force $m A$ that accelerates the box with the truck.
