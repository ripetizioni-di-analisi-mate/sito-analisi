A body falls from rest with linear drag, and the time constant of the motion is $0.40\,\mathrm{s}$. After how long does its speed reach $95\%$ of the terminal speed?

- $0.40\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.80\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The condition $1 - e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} = 0.95$ gives $t = \tau\ln 20 = 3.0\,\tau$. Therefore
$$
t = 3.0 \cdot 0.40\,\mathrm{s} = 1.2\,\mathrm{s} .
$$
After one time constant the speed is only $63\%$ of the terminal speed.
