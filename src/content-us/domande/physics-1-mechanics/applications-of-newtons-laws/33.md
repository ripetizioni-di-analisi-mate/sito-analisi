A block is launched up a plane inclined at $30^\circ$ with $\mu_k = 0.20$. While it moves up the slope, what is the magnitude of its deceleration?

- $6.6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3.2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $4.9\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

While the block moves up, kinetic friction points down the slope, in the same direction as the component of the weight along the plane. Therefore
$$
a = g\,(\sin\theta + \mu_k\cos\theta) = 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot (0.500 + 0.20 \cdot 0.866) = 6.6\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
