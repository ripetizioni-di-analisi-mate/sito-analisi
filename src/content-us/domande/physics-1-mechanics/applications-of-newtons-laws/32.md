A block slides down a plane inclined at $30^\circ$. Its acceleration is half the acceleration it would have on the same plane without friction. What is the coefficient of kinetic friction?

- $0.58$
- $0.29$
- $0.25$
- $0.43$

## Explanation

Without friction the acceleration is $g\sin\theta$, and with friction it is $g\,(\sin\theta - \mu_k\cos\theta)$. Setting the second equal to half the first gives $\mu_k\cos\theta = \dfrac{1}{2}\sin\theta$, therefore
$$
\mu_k = \dfrac{1}{2}\tan 30^\circ = 0.29 .
$$
