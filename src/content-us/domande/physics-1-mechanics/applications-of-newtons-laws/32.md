A block is released on a plane inclined at $25^\circ$ with $\mu_k = 0.20$ and slides down. What is the magnitude of its acceleration?

- $4.1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2.4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $5.9\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $8.1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Kinetic friction acts up the slope, therefore
$$
a = g\,(\sin\theta - \mu_k\cos\theta) = 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot (0.423 - 0.20 \cdot 0.906) = 2.4\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
Adding the friction term instead of subtracting it gives $5.9\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
