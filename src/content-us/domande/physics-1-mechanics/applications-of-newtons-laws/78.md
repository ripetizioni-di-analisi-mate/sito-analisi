A cart passes the top of a vertical loop of radius $r$ at exactly the speed $\sqrt{g r}$. What is the normal force exerted by the track at that point?

- $m g$
- $0$
- $2 m g$
- $\dfrac{m g}{2}$

## Explanation

At the top $N + m g = m\dfrac{v^2}{r}$. With $v^2 = g r$ the right side equals $m g$, therefore $N = 0$ and the weight alone supplies the net force toward the center.
