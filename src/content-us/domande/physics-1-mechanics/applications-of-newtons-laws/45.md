A body falls in a fluid with drag $F_D = b\,v$. If the constant $b$ is doubled and the mass is unchanged, how does the terminal speed change?

- It is doubled
- It is halved
- It is divided by $\sqrt{2}$
- It does not change

## Explanation

With linear drag $v_t = \dfrac{m g}{b}$, which is inversely proportional to $b$. Doubling $b$ therefore halves the terminal speed. The factor $\sqrt{2}$ belongs to the quadratic model.
