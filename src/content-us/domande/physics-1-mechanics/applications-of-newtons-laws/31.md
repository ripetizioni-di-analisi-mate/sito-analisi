A block is placed at rest on a plane inclined at $35^\circ$, with $\mu_s = 0.65$. What happens to it?

- It stays at rest, because $\sin 35^\circ < 0.65$
- It slides, because $\cos 35^\circ > 0.65$
- It slides, because $\tan 35^\circ > 0.65$
- It stays at rest, because $\tan 35^\circ < 1$

## Explanation

A body on an incline stays at rest if and only if $\tan\theta \le \mu_s$. Here $\tan 35^\circ = 0.70$, which exceeds $0.65$, therefore the block slides.
