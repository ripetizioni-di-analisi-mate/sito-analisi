A skydiver of mass $80\,\mathrm{kg}$ falls in air with drag $F_D = D\,v^2$ and reaches the terminal speed $60\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the constant $D$?

- $13\,\mathrm{kg/m}$
- $0.022\,\mathrm{kg/m}$
- $0.44\,\mathrm{kg/m}$
- $0.22\,\mathrm{kg/m}$

## Explanation

At terminal speed the drag balances the weight, $D v_t^2 = m g$. Therefore
$$
D = \dfrac{m g}{v_t^2} = \dfrac{80\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}}{(60\,\mathrm{m/s})^2} = 0.22\,\mathrm{kg/m} .
$$
Dividing by $v_t$ in place of $v_t^2$ gives a quantity with the units of the linear constant $b$.
