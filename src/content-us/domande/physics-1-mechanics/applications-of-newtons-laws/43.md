A skydiver of mass $60\,\mathrm{kg}$ falls in air with drag $F_D = D\,v^2$ and $D = 0.20\,\mathrm{kg/m}$. What is the terminal speed?

- $17\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $77\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $38\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $54\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

With quadratic drag the terminal speed is
$$
v_t = \sqrt{\dfrac{m g}{D}} = \sqrt{\dfrac{60\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}}{0.20\,\mathrm{kg/m}}} = 54\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
Omitting $g$ under the root gives $17\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
