A block moves to the right at $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ on a conveyor belt that moves to the right at $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and the block slides on the belt. In which direction does the kinetic friction force on the block point?

- To the left
- Vertically upwards
- To the right
- It is zero

## Explanation

Kinetic friction is opposite to the velocity of the body *relative to the surface*. Relative to the belt the block moves to the left at $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$, therefore the friction force on the block points to the right. It drags the block forward until it reaches the speed of the belt.
