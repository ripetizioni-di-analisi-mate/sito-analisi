A box on a horizontal floor is pushed with a force of fixed magnitude, first horizontally and then directed $20^\circ$ below the horizontal. How does the largest static friction force in the second case compare with the first?

- It is smaller
- It is larger
- It is the same
- It is zero

## Explanation

In the second case the push has a downward component, so the floor must push up harder and $N$ exceeds $m g$. The largest static friction force $\mu_s N$ grows with $N$, therefore it is larger than for the horizontal push.
