In the formula $a = g\,(\sin\theta - \mu_k\cos\theta)$ for a block sliding down an incline, what does the acceleration become for $\theta = 90^\circ$?

- $g\,(1 - \mu_k)$
- $\mu_k g$
- $0$
- $g$

## Explanation

For $\theta = 90^\circ$ the surface is vertical, $\cos\theta = 0$ and the normal force $m g\cos\theta$ vanishes. Friction vanishes with it, therefore the formula gives $a = g$, the acceleration of free fall.
