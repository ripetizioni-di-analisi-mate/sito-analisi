A brick slides on a horizontal floor at the same speed, first on its largest face and then on its narrowest face. How do the kinetic friction forces in the two cases compare?

- The force is larger on the largest face
- They are equal
- The force is larger on the narrowest face
- The force is zero on the narrowest face

## Explanation

The kinetic friction force is $f_k = \mu_k N$, and $\mu_k$ is nearly independent of the area of contact. The normal force equals the weight of the brick in both cases, therefore the two friction forces are equal.
