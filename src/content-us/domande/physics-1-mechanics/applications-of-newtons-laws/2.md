A crate of mass $25\,\mathrm{kg}$ rests on a horizontal floor with $\mu_s = 0.40$. A horizontal push of $60\,\mathrm{N}$ is applied and the crate does not move. What is the magnitude of the static friction force?

- $60\,\mathrm{N}$
- $98\,\mathrm{N}$
- $38\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The largest static friction force is $\mu_s m g = 0.40 \cdot 25\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 98.1\,\mathrm{N}$, which exceeds the push, so the crate stays at rest. The equilibrium equation along the horizontal then gives $f_s = 60\,\mathrm{N}$. The value $98\,\mathrm{N}$ is only the upper limit of $f_s$.
