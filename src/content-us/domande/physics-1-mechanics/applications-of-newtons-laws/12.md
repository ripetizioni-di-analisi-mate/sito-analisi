For a body at rest on a surface, under which condition does the static friction force have magnitude $\mu_s N$?

- Only at the threshold of sliding
- Whenever the body is at rest
- Whenever the body slides
- Whenever $N$ equals the weight

## Explanation

The law of static friction states $f_s \le \mu_s N$, and the equality holds at the instant sliding is about to begin. At any other moment of rest the value of $f_s$ follows from the equilibrium equations and can be smaller than $\mu_s N$, down to zero.
