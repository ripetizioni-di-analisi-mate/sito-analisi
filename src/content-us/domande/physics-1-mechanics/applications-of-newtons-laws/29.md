A block starts to slide on a plank tilted at $30^\circ$. A second block of the same material and twice the mass is placed on the same plank. At what inclination does the second block start to slide?

- $15^\circ$
- $60^\circ$
- $30^\circ$
- $41^\circ$

## Explanation

The condition for rest is $\tan\theta \le \mu_s$, which contains neither the mass nor the area of contact. Both blocks have the same $\mu_s$, therefore the second one also starts to slide at $30^\circ$.
