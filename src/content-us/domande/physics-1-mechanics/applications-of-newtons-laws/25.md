A block of mass $3.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ rests on a plane inclined at $40^\circ$, and no force other than its weight and the contact force acts on it. What is the magnitude of the normal force?

- $18.9\,\mathrm{N}$
- $29.4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $22.5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $38.4\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The block does not leave the surface, hence its acceleration perpendicular to the plane is zero and
$$
N = m g\cos\theta = 3.0\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot \cos 40^\circ = 22.5\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
