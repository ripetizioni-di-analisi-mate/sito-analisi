A block of mass $10\,\mathrm{kg}$ rests on a plane inclined at $20^\circ$ with $\mu_s = 0.50$. What is the smallest force parallel to the plane, directed up the slope, that starts the block moving up?

- $34\,\mathrm{N}$
- $80\,\mathrm{N}$
- $13\,\mathrm{N}$
- $109\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

At the threshold of sliding up the slope, static friction reaches $\mu_s m g\cos\theta$ and points down the slope, like the component of the weight. Therefore
$$
F = m g\,(\sin\theta + \mu_s\cos\theta) = 98.1\,\mathrm{N} \cdot (0.342 + 0.50 \cdot 0.940) = 80\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
