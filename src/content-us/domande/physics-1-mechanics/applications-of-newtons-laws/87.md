A person of mass $60\,\mathrm{kg}$ stands on a scale in an elevator that accelerates upwards at $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What does the scale read?

- $469\,\mathrm{N}$
- $709\,\mathrm{N}$
- $589\,\mathrm{N}$
- $120\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

In the frame of the elevator the person is at rest, and the inertial force $m A$ points downwards, opposite to the acceleration of the elevator. The normal force balances the weight and the inertial force, therefore
$$
N = m\,(g + A) = 60\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 11.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 709\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
