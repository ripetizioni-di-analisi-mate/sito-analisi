A small body hangs from the ceiling of a car that accelerates horizontally, and the string settles at $12^\circ$ from the vertical. What is the acceleration of the car?

- $2.1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $46\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $9.6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0.21\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

In the frame of the car the tension, the weight and the inertial force $m A$ add to zero, which gives $\tan\theta = \dfrac{A}{g}$. Therefore
$$
A = g\tan 12^\circ = 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot 0.213 = 2.1\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
