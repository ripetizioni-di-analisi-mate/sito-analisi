A body falls from rest with linear drag. Long after the release, what does the graph of the distance fallen $y(t)$ look like?

- A parabola
- A straight line of slope $g$
- A straight line of slope $v_t$
- A horizontal straight line

## Explanation

The distance fallen is $y(t) = v_t\left[\,t - \tau\left(1 - e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}}\right)\right]$. For large $t$ the exponential vanishes and $y \approx v_t\,(t - \tau)$, therefore the graph becomes a straight line of slope $v_t$, as uniform motion at the terminal speed requires.
