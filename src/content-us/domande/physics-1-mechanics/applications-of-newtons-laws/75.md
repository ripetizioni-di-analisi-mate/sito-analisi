A car travels the inside of a vertical loop and passes the top at $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the largest radius of the loop for which the car stays in contact with the track at the top?

- $0.61\,\mathrm{m}$
- $7.3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3.7\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Contact at the top requires $v_{\text{top}}^2 \ge g r$, therefore
$$
r \le \dfrac{v_{\text{top}}^2}{g} = \dfrac{(6.0\,\mathrm{m/s})^2}{9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}} = 3.7\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
