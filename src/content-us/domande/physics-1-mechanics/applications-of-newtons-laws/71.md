A car takes a banked curve at its design speed, with no sideways friction. How does the normal force on the car compare with its weight?

- It is equal
- It is larger
- It is smaller
- It is zero

## Explanation

The car has no vertical acceleration, hence $N\cos\theta = m g$ and $N = \dfrac{m g}{\cos\theta}$. Since $\cos\theta < 1$ on a banked road, the normal force is larger than the weight.
