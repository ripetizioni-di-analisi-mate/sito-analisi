A puck tied to a string travels a horizontal circle at constant speed on a frictionless table. Which forces appear in its free-body diagram?

- Weight, normal force, tension
- Weight, normal force, tension, centripetal force
- Weight, normal force, tension, centrifugal force
- Tension and centripetal force

## Explanation

The real forces on the puck are its weight, the normal force of the table and the tension of the string. The quantity $m\dfrac{v^2}{r}$ is not an additional force: it is the value that the sum of the real forces must have, and here the tension supplies it.
