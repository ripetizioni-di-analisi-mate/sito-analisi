A block rests on an incline with no applied force. In which direction does the static friction force on it point?

- Up the slope
- Down the slope
- Perpendicular to the plane
- It is zero

## Explanation

The component of the weight along the plane, $m g\sin\theta$, pulls the block down the slope. The block is at rest, therefore static friction balances it and points up the slope.
