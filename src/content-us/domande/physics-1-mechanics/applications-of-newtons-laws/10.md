A horizontal force of $30\,\mathrm{N}$ is needed to start a crate of mass $10\,\mathrm{kg}$ moving on a horizontal floor, and a horizontal force of $22\,\mathrm{N}$ keeps it moving at constant speed. What is the coefficient of kinetic friction?

- $0.22$
- $0.31$
- $0.082$
- $2.2$

## Explanation

At constant speed the net force is zero, hence the kinetic friction force equals the push, $f_k = 22\,\mathrm{N}$. The normal force is $m g = 98.1\,\mathrm{N}$, therefore
$$
\mu_k = \dfrac{22\,\mathrm{N}}{98.1\,\mathrm{N}} = 0.22 .
$$
The force of $30\,\mathrm{N}$ measures the static coefficient, $\mu_s = 0.31$.
