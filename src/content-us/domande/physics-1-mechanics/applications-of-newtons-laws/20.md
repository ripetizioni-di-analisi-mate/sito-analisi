After an accident, the skid marks of a car on a level road are $32\,\mathrm{m}$ long and $\mu_k = 0.75$. Assuming the car skidded to rest, what was its speed at the start of the skid?

- $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6.9\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $471\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $22\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The deceleration is $\mu_k g$, and the car comes to rest after the distance $d$, hence $v_0^2 = 2\mu_k g d$. Therefore
$$
v_0 = \sqrt{2 \cdot 0.75 \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot 32\,\mathrm{m}} = 22\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
