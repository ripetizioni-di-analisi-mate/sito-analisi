A rider of mass $50\,\mathrm{kg}$ passes the bottom of a vertical loop of radius $5.0\,\mathrm{m}$ at $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the magnitude of the force exerted by the seat on the rider?

- $1930\,\mathrm{N}$
- $950\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1440\,\mathrm{N}$
- $491\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

At the bottom the center lies above the rider, so the seat pushes upwards and the weight points downwards. Along the radial axis $N - m g = m\dfrac{v^2}{r}$, therefore
$$
N = m\left(g + \dfrac{v^2}{r}\right) = 50\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot (9.81 + 28.8)\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 1930\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
