Which statement about the coefficient of static friction is correct?

- It is always smaller than $1$
- It is measured in newtons
- It is proportional to the area of contact
- It can exceed $1$ for rubber on a rough surface

## Explanation

The coefficient is a dimensionless number that depends on the two materials and on the state of their surfaces. Rubber on rough surfaces gives values above $1$, therefore $\mu_s \le 1$ is not a law.
