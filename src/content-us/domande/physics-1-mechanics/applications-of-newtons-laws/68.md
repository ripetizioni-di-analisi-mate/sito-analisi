A car of mass $1000\,\mathrm{kg}$ takes a flat curve of radius $50\,\mathrm{m}$ at $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$, with $\mu_s = 0.80$. What is the magnitude of the friction force exerted by the road on the car?

- $7850\,\mathrm{N}$
- $9810\,\mathrm{N}$
- $300\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4500\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Static friction is the only horizontal force, therefore it equals the net force toward the center:
$$
f_s = m\,\dfrac{v^2}{r} = 1000\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot \dfrac{(15\,\mathrm{m/s})^2}{50\,\mathrm{m}} = 4500\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The value $\mu_s m g = 7850\,\mathrm{N}$ is only the largest friction the road can supply.
