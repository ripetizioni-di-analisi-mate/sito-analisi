The coefficient of static friction between a block and a plank is $0.80$. What is the angle of repose?

- $38.7^\circ$
- $53.1^\circ$
- $51.3^\circ$
- $45.8^\circ$

## Explanation

The angle of repose satisfies $\tan\theta_s = \mu_s$, therefore
$$
\theta_s = \arctan 0.80 = 38.7^\circ .
$$
The value $53.1^\circ$ comes from $\arcsin 0.80$, which confuses the tangent with the sine.
