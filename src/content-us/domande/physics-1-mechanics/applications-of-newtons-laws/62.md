A ball on a string travels a horizontal circle as a conical pendulum. Which force supplies the net force towards the center of the circle?

- The vertical component of the tension
- The horizontal component of the tension
- The weight of the ball
- The centrifugal force

## Explanation

The forces on the ball are its weight, which is vertical, and the tension along the string. The vertical component of the tension balances the weight, therefore the horizontal component alone provides the net force $m\dfrac{v^2}{r}$ towards the center.
