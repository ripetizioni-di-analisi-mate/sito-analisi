A block sliding freely on a horizontal floor slows from $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in $2.0\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the coefficient of kinetic friction?

- $0.41$
- $0.20$
- $2.0$
- $0.10$

## Explanation

The deceleration is $\dfrac{6.0\,\mathrm{m/s} - 2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}}{2.0\,\mathrm{s}} = 2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. For a body sliding with no other horizontal force the deceleration equals $\mu_k g$, therefore
$$
\mu_k = \dfrac{2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}}{9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}} = 0.20 .
$$
