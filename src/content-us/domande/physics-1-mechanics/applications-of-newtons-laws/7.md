A block of mass $10\,\mathrm{kg}$ on a horizontal floor is pulled by a rope with a force of $50\,\mathrm{N}$ directed at $30^\circ$ above the horizontal. What is the magnitude of the normal force exerted by the floor?

- $98\,\mathrm{N}$
- $123\,\mathrm{N}$
- $73\,\mathrm{N}$
- $55\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The block has no vertical acceleration, hence $N + F\sin 30^\circ - m g = 0$. Therefore
$$
N = m g - F\sin 30^\circ = 98.1\,\mathrm{N} - 25\,\mathrm{N} = 73\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The upward component of the pull lifts part of the weight, which is why $N$ is smaller than $m g$.
