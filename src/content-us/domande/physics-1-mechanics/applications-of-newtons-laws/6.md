A box of mass $12\,\mathrm{kg}$ is on a horizontal floor with $\mu_s = 0.50$ and $\mu_k = 0.40$. A horizontal push of $70\,\mathrm{N}$ is applied. What is the magnitude of the acceleration of the box?

- $1.9\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0.93\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $5.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The largest static friction force is $\mu_s m g = 58.9\,\mathrm{N} < 70\,\mathrm{N}$, therefore the box slides and the friction force is kinetic, $\mu_k m g = 47.1\,\mathrm{N}$. Newton's second law gives
$$
a = \dfrac{70\,\mathrm{N} - 47.1\,\mathrm{N}}{12\,\mathrm{kg}} = 1.9\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
Once the box slides, the coefficient $\mu_s$ no longer enters the equation.
