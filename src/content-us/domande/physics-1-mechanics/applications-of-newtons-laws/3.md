According to the laws of friction, which relation holds for every pair of surfaces in contact?

- $\mu_k \ge \mu_s$
- $\mu_s \le 1$
- $\mu_k \le \mu_s$
- $\mu_s + \mu_k = 1$

## Explanation

For the same pair of surfaces the coefficient of kinetic friction does not exceed the coefficient of static friction, therefore $\mu_k \le \mu_s$. The bound $\mu_s \le 1$ is not a law, since rubber on rough surfaces has $\mu_s > 1$.
