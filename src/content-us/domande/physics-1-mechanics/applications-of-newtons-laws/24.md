A block of mass $4.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ lies on a plane inclined at $25^\circ$ to the horizontal. What is the magnitude of the component of its weight parallel to the plane?

- $35.6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $39.2\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1.69\,\mathrm{N}$
- $16.6\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The component of the weight along the plane is
$$
m g \sin\theta = 4.0\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot \sin 25^\circ = 16.6\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The cosine gives the component perpendicular to the plane, $35.6\,\mathrm{N}$.
