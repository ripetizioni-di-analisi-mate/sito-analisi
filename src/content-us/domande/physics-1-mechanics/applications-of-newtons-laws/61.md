Two bodies of equal mass lie on a rotating turntable at distances $r$ and $2r$ from the axis, and rotate with it. How does the net force on the farther body compare with that on the nearer one?

- It is twice as large
- It is half as large
- It is four times as large
- It is the same

## Explanation

Both bodies have the same angular speed $\omega$, and the net force is $m\omega^2 r$, which is proportional to $r$ at fixed $\omega$. Doubling the distance therefore doubles the force. The formula $m\dfrac{v^2}{r}$ leads to the same result, since $v = \omega r$ also doubles.
