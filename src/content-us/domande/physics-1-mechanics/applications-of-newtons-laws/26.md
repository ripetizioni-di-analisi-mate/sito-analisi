A body lies on a plane inclined at the angle $\theta$ to the horizontal. What is the angle between its weight and the perpendicular to the plane?

- $90^\circ - \theta$
- $90^\circ + \theta$
- $\theta$
- $2\theta$

## Explanation

The perpendicular to the plane is obtained from the vertical by the same rotation that takes the horizontal into the plane, therefore it makes the angle $\theta$ with the vertical. This is why the component of the weight across the plane is $m g\cos\theta$ and the component along it is $m g\sin\theta$.
