A block of mass $5.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ is held at rest on a frictionless plane inclined at $30^\circ$ by a force parallel to the plane. What is the magnitude of that force?

- $42.5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $24.5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $49.1\,\mathrm{N}$
- $28.3\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The force must balance the component of the weight along the plane, therefore
$$
F = m g\sin 30^\circ = 5.0\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot 0.500 = 24.5\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The value $28.3\,\mathrm{N} = m g\tan 30^\circ$ is the horizontal force that holds the block, which is a different direction.
