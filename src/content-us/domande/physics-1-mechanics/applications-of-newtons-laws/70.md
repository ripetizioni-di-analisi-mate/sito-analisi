A curve of radius $150\,\mathrm{m}$ is banked at $20^\circ$. At what speed can a vehicle take it with no sideways friction?

- $37\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $23\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $7.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $536\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

A banked curve without friction requires $\tan\theta = \dfrac{v^2}{g r}$, therefore
$$
v = \sqrt{g\,r\tan\theta} = \sqrt{9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot 150\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \tan 20^\circ} = 23\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
