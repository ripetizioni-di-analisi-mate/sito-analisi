A truck brakes on a straight level road and a box rests on its flat bed without sliding. In the frame of the truck, in which direction does the inertial force on the box point?

- Backward, opposite to the motion
- Vertically downwards
- It is zero
- Forward, in the direction of motion

## Explanation

While braking, the acceleration $\vec{A}$ of the truck points backward. The inertial force $-m\vec{A}$ therefore points forward, and static friction from the bed, directed backward, balances it.
