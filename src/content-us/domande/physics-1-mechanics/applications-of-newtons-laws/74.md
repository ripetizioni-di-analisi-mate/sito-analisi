A car of mass $1000\,\mathrm{kg}$ takes a curve banked at $30^\circ$ at the design speed, with no sideways friction. What is the magnitude of the normal force on the car?

- $8.50\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $9.81\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $19.6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $11.3\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The vertical equation gives $N\cos\theta = m g$, therefore
$$
N = \dfrac{m g}{\cos 30^\circ} = \dfrac{9.81\,\mathrm{kN}}{0.866} = 11.3\,\mathrm{kN} .
$$
The value $m g\cos\theta = 8.50\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the normal force on a block at rest on an incline, where the acceleration perpendicular to the surface is zero.
