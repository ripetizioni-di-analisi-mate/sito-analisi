A sled of mass $20\,\mathrm{kg}$ is pulled at constant velocity along level ground with $\mu_k = 0.60$, by a rope at $37^\circ$ above the horizontal. What is the tension in the rope?

- $102\,\mathrm{N}$
- $118\,\mathrm{N}$
- $147\,\mathrm{N}$
- $269\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The vertical equation gives $N = m g - T\sin 37^\circ$, and the horizontal equation at constant velocity gives $T\cos 37^\circ = \mu_k N$. Substituting $N$,
$$
T = \dfrac{\mu_k m g}{\cos 37^\circ + \mu_k \sin 37^\circ} = \dfrac{117.7\,\mathrm{N}}{0.799 + 0.361} = 102\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The value $118\,\mathrm{N}$ ignores that the rope lifts part of the weight and reduces the normal force.
