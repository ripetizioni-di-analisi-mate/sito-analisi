A car traveling at $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$ brakes with the wheels locked on a level road with $\mu_k = 0.70$. What is its speed after it has skidded $15\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $13.9\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $17.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $19.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $9.7\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The deceleration is $\mu_k g = 6.87\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and is constant, hence $v^2 = v_0^2 - 2\mu_k g\,x$. Therefore
$$
v = \sqrt{(20\,\mathrm{m/s})^2 - 2 \cdot 6.87\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot 15\,\mathrm{m}} = 13.9\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The speed does not decrease in proportion to the distance, since it is $v^2$ that decreases linearly with $x$.
