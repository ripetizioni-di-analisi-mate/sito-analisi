A car traveling at $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$ brakes with the wheels locked on a road with $\mu_k = 0.70$. What is its braking distance?

- $29\,\mathrm{m}$
- $58\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $286\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Kinetic friction gives the constant deceleration $\mu_k g = 6.87\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, therefore
$$
d = \dfrac{v_0^2}{2\mu_k g} = \dfrac{(20\,\mathrm{m/s})^2}{2 \cdot 0.70 \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}} = 29\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
Dropping the factor $2$ gives twice the correct distance.
