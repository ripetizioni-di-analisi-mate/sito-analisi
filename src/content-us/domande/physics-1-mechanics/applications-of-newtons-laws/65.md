A car must take a flat horizontal curve at $25\,\mathrm{m/s}$, with $\mu_s = 0.60$ between the tyres and the road. What is the smallest radius of the curve that allows it without sliding?

- $53\,\mathrm{m}$
- $38\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4.2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $106\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

On a flat curve the condition is $v^2 \le \mu_s g r$, therefore the radius must satisfy
$$
r \ge \dfrac{v^2}{\mu_s g} = \dfrac{(25\,\mathrm{m/s})^2}{0.60 \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}} = 106\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
