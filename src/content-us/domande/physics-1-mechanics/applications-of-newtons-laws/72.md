A car takes a banked curve at a speed higher than the design speed, without sliding. In which direction does the static friction force on the car point?

- Up the slope of the bank
- It is zero
- Down the slope of the bank
- Along the velocity

## Explanation

At the design speed the normal force and the weight alone give the net force $m\dfrac{v^2}{r}$. At a higher speed a larger force toward the center is required, and without friction the car would slide up and outwards. Static friction opposes that tendency, therefore it points down the slope, and its horizontal component points toward the center.
