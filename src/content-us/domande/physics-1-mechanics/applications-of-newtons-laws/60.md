A body moves on a circle of fixed radius at constant speed. If its speed is doubled, by what factor does the net force on it change?

- $2$
- $\sqrt{2}$
- $4$
- $1$

## Explanation

The net force is $m\dfrac{v^2}{r}$, which is proportional to the square of the speed at fixed radius. Doubling $v$ therefore multiplies the force by $4$.
