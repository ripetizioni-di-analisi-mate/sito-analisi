A car drives at constant speed through the lowest point of a dip in the road, where the profile is curved. How does the normal force of the road compare with the weight of the car?

- It is larger
- It is smaller
- It is equal
- It is zero

## Explanation

At the lowest point of the dip the center of curvature lies above the car, so the net force points upwards. Along the radial axis $N - m g = m\dfrac{v^2}{r} > 0$, therefore the normal force exceeds the weight.
