Which statement about an inertial force is correct?

- Its reaction acts on the accelerating frame
- It is exerted by the Earth
- It has no third-law partner
- It also appears in inertial frames

## Explanation

An inertial force is not exerted by any body, therefore it has no agent and no reaction partner. It appears only in the equations written in the accelerating frame.
