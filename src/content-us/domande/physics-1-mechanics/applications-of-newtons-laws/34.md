A block slides down a plane inclined at $35^\circ$ at constant speed. What is the coefficient of kinetic friction?

- $0.57$
- $0.82$
- $1.4$
- $0.70$

## Explanation

At constant speed the net force is zero, hence $\mu_k m g\cos\theta = m g\sin\theta$. Therefore
$$
\mu_k = \tan 35^\circ = 0.70 .
$$
