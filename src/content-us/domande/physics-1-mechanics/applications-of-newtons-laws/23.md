A block of mass $5.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ rests on a horizontal table with $\mu_s = 0.40$ and is tied by an ideal string, over an ideal pulley at the edge, to a hanging block. What is the smallest hanging mass that sets the system in motion?

- $12\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $3.0\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The system starts to move when the weight of the hanging block reaches the largest static friction force on the block on the table, $m_2 g = \mu_s m_1 g$. Therefore
$$
m_2 = \mu_s m_1 = 0.40 \cdot 5.0\,\mathrm{kg} = 2.0\,\mathrm{kg} .
$$
