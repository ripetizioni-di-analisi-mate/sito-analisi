A passenger of mass $70\,\mathrm{kg}$ sits in a car that takes a curve of radius $45\,\mathrm{m}$ at $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$. In the frame that rotates with the car, what is the centrifugal force on the passenger?

- $350\,\mathrm{N}$ towards the center
- $23\,\mathrm{N}$ away from the center
- $350\,\mathrm{N}$ away from the center
- $1050\,\mathrm{N}$ away from the center

## Explanation

In the rotating frame the centrifugal force points away from the center and has magnitude
$$
m\,\dfrac{v^2}{r} = 70\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot \dfrac{(15\,\mathrm{m/s})^2}{45\,\mathrm{m}} = 350\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The seat and the friction forces directed inwards balance it, and the passenger is in equilibrium in that frame.
