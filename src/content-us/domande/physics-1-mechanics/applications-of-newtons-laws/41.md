A skydiver falls vertically at terminal speed. How does the magnitude of the drag force compare with the weight of the skydiver?

- The drag force is larger
- The drag force is smaller
- They are equal
- The drag force is zero

## Explanation

At terminal speed the speed no longer changes, hence the acceleration is zero. The only forces are the weight and the drag, therefore the drag force balances the weight exactly.
