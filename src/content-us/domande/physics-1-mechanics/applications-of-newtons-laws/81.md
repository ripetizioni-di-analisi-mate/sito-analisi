A ball on a string travels a vertical circle. Why must its speed at the top be at least $\sqrt{g r}$?

- Because the tension is largest at the top
- Because the weight points away from the center at the top
- Because the string can push but cannot pull
- Because the string can pull but cannot push

## Explanation

At the top $T + m g = m\dfrac{v^2}{r}$. An ideal string can only pull, hence $T \ge 0$, and that requires $\dfrac{v^2}{r} \ge g$. Below that speed the string goes slack and the ball leaves the circle.
