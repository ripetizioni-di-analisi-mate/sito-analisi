Two bodies of the same shape and size fall in air with drag $F_D = D\,v^2$ and the same constant $D$. The mass of the second is four times the mass of the first. What is the ratio of the terminal speed of the second to that of the first?

- $4$
- $16$
- $2$
- $1$

## Explanation

The terminal speed $v_t = \sqrt{\dfrac{m g}{D}}$ is proportional to the square root of the mass. Multiplying the mass by $4$ therefore multiplies $v_t$ by $\sqrt{4} = 2$.
