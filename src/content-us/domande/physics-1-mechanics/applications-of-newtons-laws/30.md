A block of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ rests on a plane inclined at $20^\circ$ with $\mu_s = 0.60$. What is the magnitude of the static friction force on it?

- $11\,\mathrm{N}$
- $18\,\mathrm{N}$
- $6.7\,\mathrm{N}$
- $2.2\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Since $\tan 20^\circ = 0.36 < 0.60$, the block stays at rest, and the equilibrium equation along the plane gives
$$
f_s = m g\sin\theta = 2.0\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot \sin 20^\circ = 6.7\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The value $\mu_s m g\cos\theta = 11\,\mathrm{N}$ is the largest friction the contact can supply.
