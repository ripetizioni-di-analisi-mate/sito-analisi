A body is thrown vertically downwards in a fluid with a speed larger than its terminal speed. What happens to its speed afterwards?

- It increases
- It stays constant
- It decreases to zero
- It decreases towards the terminal speed

## Explanation

For $v > v_t$ the drag force exceeds the weight, hence the net force points upwards and the acceleration is opposite to the velocity. The speed therefore decreases, and it approaches $v_t$, where drag and weight balance.
