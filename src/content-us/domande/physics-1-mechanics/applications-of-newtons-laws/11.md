A box of mass $10\,\mathrm{kg}$ slides on a horizontal floor with $\mu_k = 0.30$. A brick of mass $5.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ is placed on top of the box and moves with it. What is the kinetic friction force exerted by the floor on the box?

- $29\,\mathrm{N}$
- $15\,\mathrm{N}$
- $59\,\mathrm{N}$
- $44\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The floor supports both bodies, therefore the normal force is $(10\,\mathrm{kg} + 5.0\,\mathrm{kg})\,g = 147\,\mathrm{N}$. The kinetic friction force is
$$
f_k = \mu_k N = 0.30 \cdot 147\,\mathrm{N} = 44\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The value $29\,\mathrm{N}$ ignores the brick, which increases the force pressing the surfaces together.
