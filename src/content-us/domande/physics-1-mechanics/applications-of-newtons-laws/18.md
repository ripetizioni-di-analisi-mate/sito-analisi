A loaded truck and a small car, with tyres of the same material, brake with locked wheels on the same level road from the same speed. How do their braking distances compare?

- They are equal
- The truck needs a longer distance
- The car needs a longer distance
- They are in the ratio of the masses

## Explanation

The friction force $\mu_k m g$ is proportional to the mass, hence the deceleration $\mu_k g$ is the same for both vehicles. Since the initial speed is also the same, the braking distances $\dfrac{v_0^2}{2\mu_k g}$ are equal.
