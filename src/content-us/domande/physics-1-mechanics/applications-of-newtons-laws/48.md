A body falls from rest with linear drag and its terminal speed is $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is its speed after one time constant?

- $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5.7\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The speed is $v(t) = v_t\left(1 - e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}}\right)$, therefore at $t = \tau$
$$
v = 6.0\,\mathrm{m/s} \cdot \left(1 - e^{-1}\right) = 6.0\,\mathrm{m/s} \cdot 0.632 = 3.8\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The value $2.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is $v_t e^{-1}$, the speed still missing at that instant.
