A puck of mass $0.20\,\mathrm{kg}$ moves at $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ on a horizontal circle of radius $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$ on a frictionless table, held by a string tied to the center. What is the tension of the string?

- $1.2\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1.8\,\mathrm{N}$
- $5.6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $3.6\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The tension is the only horizontal force, therefore it supplies the net force toward the center:
$$
T = m\,\dfrac{v^2}{r} = 0.20\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot \dfrac{(3.0\,\mathrm{m/s})^2}{0.50\,\mathrm{m}} = 3.6\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
Using the diameter in place of the radius gives $1.8\,\mathrm{N}$.
