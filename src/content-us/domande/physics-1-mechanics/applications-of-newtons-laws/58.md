A body of mass $0.50\,\mathrm{kg}$ moves on a circle of radius $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$ with constant angular speed $4.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. What is the magnitude of the net force on it?

- $2.4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.60\,\mathrm{N}$
- $27\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4.8\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The net force in uniform circular motion is
$$
F_{\text{net}} = m\,\omega^2 r = 0.50\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot (4.0\,\mathrm{rad/s})^2 \cdot 0.30\,\mathrm{m} = 2.4\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
Dividing by $r$ in place of multiplying confuses $m\omega^2 r$ with $m\dfrac{v^2}{r}$.
