A horizontal force of $245\,\mathrm{N}$ is just sufficient to start a crate moving on a horizontal floor with $\mu_s = 0.50$. What is the mass of the crate?

- $25\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $50\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $490\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

At the threshold of sliding the push equals the largest static friction force, $F = \mu_s m g$. Therefore
$$
m = \dfrac{F}{\mu_s\, g} = \dfrac{245\,\mathrm{N}}{0.50 \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}} = 50\,\mathrm{kg} .
$$
