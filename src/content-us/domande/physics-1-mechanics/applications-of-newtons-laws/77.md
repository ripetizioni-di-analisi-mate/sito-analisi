A cart travels a vertical circular loop on the inside of the track. At which point is the normal force of the track largest?

- At the top
- At the two sides
- It is the same at every point
- At the bottom

## Explanation

At the bottom the weight points away from the center, so the normal force must exceed the weight to supply $m\dfrac{v^2}{r}$. At the top the weight points toward the center and helps, so the normal force is smaller. For a cart that coasts on a frictionless track the speed is also largest at the bottom, therefore the normal force is largest there.
