A straight light bar is pinned at both ends, and forces act on it only at those two pins. What is the direction of the force that each pin exerts on the bar?

- Along the axis of the bar
- Vertical
- Perpendicular to the bar
- Any direction, fixed by the loads on the rest of the structure

## Explanation

The bar is acted on by exactly two forces, so by the theorem on the **body acted on by two forces** they are equal, opposite and directed along the line joining the two points of application. That line is the axis of the bar, hence the bar is in tension or in compression.
