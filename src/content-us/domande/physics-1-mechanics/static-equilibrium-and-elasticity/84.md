Mercury, with bulk modulus $2.8 \times 10^{10}\,\mathrm{Pa}$, is subjected to a pressure increase of $1.4 \times 10^{8}\,\mathrm{Pa}$. By what percentage does its density change?

- It increases by $0.50\,\%$
- It decreases by $0.50\,\%$
- It increases by $5.0\,\%$
- It increases by $0.050\,\%$

## Explanation

The relative volume change is $\dfrac{\Delta V}{V_0} = -\dfrac{1.4 \times 10^{8}}{2.8 \times 10^{10}} = -5.0 \times 10^{-3}$. The mass does not change, so the density $\rho = \dfrac{m}{V}$ increases by the same small relative amount, $5.0 \times 10^{-3}$, that is $0.50\,\%$.
