A homogeneous plate of constant thickness is formed by a square of side $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ and a square of side $20\,\mathrm{cm}$ placed side by side, with their lower sides on the $x$ axis. The small square occupies $0 \le x \le 10\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the large one $10\,\mathrm{cm} \le x \le 30\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the $x$ coordinate of the center of gravity?

- $8.00\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $17.0\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $15.0\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $12.5\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The plate is homogeneous, hence the areas $100\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and $400\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ replace the masses, with centers at $x = 5\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $x = 20\,\mathrm{cm}$. Therefore $x_G = \dfrac{100 \cdot 5 + 400 \cdot 20}{500}\,\mathrm{cm} = 17.0\,\mathrm{cm}$. The midpoint of the plate, $15.0\,\mathrm{cm}$, ignores that the large square carries four fifths of the mass.
