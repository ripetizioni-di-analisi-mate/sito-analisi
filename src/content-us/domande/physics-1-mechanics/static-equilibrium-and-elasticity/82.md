Under a uniform pressure increase, a metal cube undergoes the relative volume change $\dfrac{\Delta V}{V_0} = -6.0 \times 10^{-4}$. What is the relative change $\dfrac{\Delta a}{a}$ of the length of its side?

- $-8.4 \times 10^{-2}$
- $-1.8 \times 10^{-3}$
- $-2.0 \times 10^{-4}$
- $-6.0 \times 10^{-4}$

## Explanation

A uniform pressure keeps the shape, and for $V = a^3$ small changes satisfy $\dfrac{\Delta V}{V_0} \simeq 3\,\dfrac{\Delta a}{a}$. Hence $\dfrac{\Delta a}{a} = \dfrac{-6.0 \times 10^{-4}}{3} = -2.0 \times 10^{-4}$, the same in every direction.
