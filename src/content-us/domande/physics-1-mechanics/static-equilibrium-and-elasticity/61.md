What is the SI unit of stress?

- $\mathrm{N/m^2}$
- $\mathrm{N}$
- $\mathrm{N \cdot m}$
- $\mathrm{N/m}$

## Explanation

Stress is the force per unit area of the section, $\sigma = \dfrac{F_\perp}{A}$, hence it is measured in $\mathrm{N/m^2}$, the pascal. The unit $\mathrm{N/m}$ belongs to the stiffness of a spring or of a bar.
