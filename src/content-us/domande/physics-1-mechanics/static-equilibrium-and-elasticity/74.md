A steel wire under load behaves as a spring of stiffness $1.0 \times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{N/m}$ and is stretched by $2.0\,\mathrm{mm}$. What elastic potential energy does it store?

- $100\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0.40\,\mathrm{J}$
- $200\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0.20\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

A bar loaded within its proportional limit is a spring of stiffness $k$, so it stores $U = \dfrac{1}{2} k (\Delta L)^2 = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 1.0 \times 10^{5} \cdot (2.0 \times 10^{-3})^2\,\mathrm{J} = 0.20\,\mathrm{J}$. The product $k\,\Delta L = 200\,\mathrm{N}$ is the tension, which is a force and not an energy.
