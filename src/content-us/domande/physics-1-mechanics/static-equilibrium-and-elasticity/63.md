A bar of cross section $4.0\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ is pulled by a force of $2000\,\mathrm{N}$ perpendicular to the section. What is the tensile stress?

- $5.0 \times 10^{8}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $5.0 \times 10^{2}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $5.0 \times 10^{11}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $5.0 \times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

One square millimeter is $10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^2}$, so $A = 4.0 \times 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The stress is $\sigma = \dfrac{F_\perp}{A} = \dfrac{2000}{4.0 \times 10^{-6}}\,\mathrm{Pa} = 5.0 \times 10^{8}\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Converting the area as if $1\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ were $10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^2}$ gives a value a thousand times too small.
