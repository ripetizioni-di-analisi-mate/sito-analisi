A uniform horizontal beam of mass $M$ is pinned to a wall at its left end and held by a vertical rope fastened to its right end. What is the horizontal component of the force exerted by the pin?

- $Mg$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{Mg}{2}$
- $2Mg$

## Explanation

The weight and the tension of the rope are both vertical, so the pin reaction is the only force that could have a horizontal component. The equation $\sum F_x = 0$ then gives $H = 0$. The rope and the pin share the weight equally, since they are at the same distance from the center of the beam.
