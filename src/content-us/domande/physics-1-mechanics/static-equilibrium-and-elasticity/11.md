A body is acted on by three forces, two of which are $\vec{F}_1 = (3.0,\ 4.0)\,\mathrm{N}$ and $\vec{F}_2 = (-5.0,\ 2.0)\,\mathrm{N}$. What must the third force be for the net force to vanish?

- $(-2.0,\ 6.0)\,\mathrm{N}$
- $(2.0,\ 6.0)\,\mathrm{N}$
- $(2.0,\ -6.0)\,\mathrm{N}$
- $(-8.0,\ -2.0)\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The first condition requires $\vec{F}_3 = -(\vec{F}_1 + \vec{F}_2)$. The sum of the two known forces is $(-2.0,\ 6.0)\,\mathrm{N}$, hence $\vec{F}_3 = (2.0,\ -6.0)\,\mathrm{N}$. The equilibrium of the body requires in addition that the torques balance, which depends on the points of application.
