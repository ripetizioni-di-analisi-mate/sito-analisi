A force of $60\,\mathrm{N}$ is applied to a body at a point $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$ from $O$, and its line of action passes through $O$. What is the torque of the force about $O$?

- $30\,\mathrm{N \cdot m}$
- $120\,\mathrm{N \cdot m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N \cdot m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{N \cdot m}$

## Explanation

The torque has magnitude $F d$, where $d$ is the distance from $O$ to the line of action. The line of action passes through $O$, hence $d = 0$ and the torque vanishes, whatever the distance of the point of application from $O$. The product $0.50\,\mathrm{m} \cdot 60\,\mathrm{N}$ would be the torque of a force perpendicular to $\vec{r}$.
