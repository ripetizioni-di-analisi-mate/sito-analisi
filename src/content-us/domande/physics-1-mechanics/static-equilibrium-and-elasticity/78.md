Which elastic moduli does a liquid have?

- Young's modulus and the shear modulus
- All three moduli
- Only the shear modulus
- Only the bulk modulus

## Explanation

A liquid does not resist a change of shape, so it has no shear modulus. It does resist a change of volume, hence it has a bulk modulus, for water $B = 2.2 \times 10^{9}\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
