A uniform block of width $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$ and height $0.60\,\mathrm{m}$ stands on an incline whose angle is slowly increased, and friction is large enough to prevent sliding. At what angle does the block start to tip?

- $14.0^\circ$
- $30.0^\circ$
- $63.4^\circ$
- $26.6^\circ$

## Explanation

The block tips when the vertical line through $G$ reaches the lower edge of the base. The segment from $G$ to that edge has the component $\dfrac{w}{2}$ along the incline and the component $\dfrac{h}{2}$ along the normal to the incline. At the limit this segment is vertical, and the vertical makes the angle $\theta$ with the normal, hence $\tan\theta = \dfrac{w}{h} = 0.50$ and $\theta = 26.6^\circ$.
