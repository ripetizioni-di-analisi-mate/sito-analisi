A uniform ladder of mass $15\,\mathrm{kg}$ leans against a smooth vertical wall, and a person of mass $75\,\mathrm{kg}$ stands on it. What is the normal force exerted by the floor on the ladder?

- $736\,\mathrm{N}$
- $883\,\mathrm{N}$
- $441\,\mathrm{N}$
- $147\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The smooth wall pushes horizontally, so the only vertical forces are the two weights and the normal force of the floor. The equation $\sum F_y = 0$ gives $N_2 = (15 + 75)\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 883\,\mathrm{N}$, whatever the angle of the ladder and the position of the person.
