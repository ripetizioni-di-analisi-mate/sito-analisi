A uniform beam of mass $40\,\mathrm{kg}$ and length $5.0\,\mathrm{m}$ rests on two supports at its ends. A person of mass $50\,\mathrm{kg}$ stands $1.0\,\mathrm{m}$ from the right end. What force does the left support exert?

- $589\,\mathrm{N}$
- $294\,\mathrm{N}$
- $98.1\,\mathrm{N}$
- $441\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Torques about the right support exclude its force: $N_1 \cdot 5.0\,\mathrm{m} = 40 \cdot 9.81 \cdot 2.5\,\mathrm{N \cdot m} + 50 \cdot 9.81 \cdot 1.0\,\mathrm{N \cdot m}$, hence $N_1 = 30 \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{N} = 294\,\mathrm{N}$. The right support carries the rest of the total weight $883\,\mathrm{N}$, that is $589\,\mathrm{N}$, since the person stands nearer to it.
