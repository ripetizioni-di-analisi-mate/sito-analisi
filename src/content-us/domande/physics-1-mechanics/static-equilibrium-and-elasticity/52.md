The figure shows the forces on a ladder that leans against a smooth wall: the wall force $\vec{N}_1$, the weight $M\vec{g}$ applied at the center of gravity $G$, and the normal force $\vec{N}_2$ and the friction force $\vec{f}$ at the foot. About which point does the torque equation contain only one unknown force?

- The point of the floor directly below the top of the ladder
- The top of the ladder
- The foot of the ladder
- The center of gravity $G$

## Explanation

Both $\vec{N}_2$ and $\vec{f}$ are applied at the foot, so their torques about the foot vanish, and the torque equation contains only $N_1$ and the known weight. About the top, $\vec{N}_1$ drops out but $\vec{N}_2$ and $\vec{f}$ both remain, and about the base of the wall $N_1$ and $N_2$ both remain.
