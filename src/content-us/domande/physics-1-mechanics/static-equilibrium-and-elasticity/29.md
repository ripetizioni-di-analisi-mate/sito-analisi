A horizontal beam is pinned to a wall at its left end and held by an inclined cable fastened to its right end. About which point does the torque equation give the tension of the cable directly, with no other unknown?

- The right end of the beam
- The center of the beam
- The point where the cable is fastened to the wall
- The pin

## Explanation

The two unknown components $H$ and $V$ of the pin reaction are applied at the pin, so their torques about the pin vanish, and the torque equation contains only the tension and the known weights. About the right end or about the point on the wall the tension drops out instead, and a component of the pin reaction is found.
