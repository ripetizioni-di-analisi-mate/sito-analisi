A uniform beam rests on two supports at its ends. A load of weight $W$ is then placed on the beam exactly above the left support. How does the force of the right support change?

- It does not change
- It increases by $W$
- It increases by $\dfrac{W}{2}$
- It decreases by $\dfrac{W}{2}$

## Explanation

The load acts on the line of action of the left support force, so its torque about the left support is zero. The torque equation about that support, which determines $N_2$, is therefore unchanged, and the vertical equation assigns the whole additional weight $W$ to the left support.
