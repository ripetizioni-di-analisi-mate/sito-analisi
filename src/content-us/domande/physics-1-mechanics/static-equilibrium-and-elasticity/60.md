A crate on a horizontal floor is pushed by a horizontal force at its upper edge, and the force has reached the value at which tipping starts. Where does the normal force exerted by the floor act?

- Directly below the center of gravity
- Nowhere, since the normal force has become zero
- At the edge of the base about which the crate tips
- At the opposite edge of the base

## Explanation

As the push grows, the line of action of the normal force moves toward the front edge, because its torque about $G$ must balance the torque of the push and of the friction. At the tipping limit that line has reached the front edge, the edge about which the crate tips, and the magnitude of the normal force is still $M g$, as the vertical equation requires.
