Under what condition does the center of gravity of a body coincide with its center of mass?

- When the body has an axis of symmetry
- When the body is at rest
- When $\vec{g}$ has the same value at every point of the body
- When the body is homogeneous

## Explanation

The torque of the weights equals the torque of $M\vec{g}$ applied at the center of mass because $\vec{g}$ can be taken out of the sum $\sum_i \vec{r}_i \times m_i\vec{g}$. That step requires the gravitational field to be uniform over the body, and in that case $\vec{r}_G = \dfrac{\sum m_i \vec{r}_i}{\sum m_i}$. Homogeneity and symmetry help to locate the point, but they are not the condition.
