A uniform block of width $0.40\,\mathrm{m}$ and height $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$ rests on a plank with coefficient of static friction $0.60$, and the plank is slowly tilted. What happens first, and at what angle?

- It slides, at $38.7^\circ$
- It slides, at $31.0^\circ$
- It tips, at $31.0^\circ$
- It tips, at $38.7^\circ$

## Explanation

Sliding starts when $\tan\theta = \mu_s = 0.60$, that is at $31.0^\circ$, and tipping when $\tan\theta = \dfrac{w}{h} = 0.80$, that is at $38.7^\circ$. The sliding angle is reached first, so the block slides before it can tip.
