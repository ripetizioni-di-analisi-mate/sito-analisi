A bolt of diameter $10\,\mathrm{mm}$ joins two plates that are pulled apart, and its cross section carries a shear force of $3.0\,\mathrm{kN}$. What is the shear stress in the bolt?

- $9.5 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $3.0 \times 10^{7}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $3.8 \times 10^{7}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $9.5 \times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The shear stress is the tangential force divided by the area of the section, $A = \pi\,(5.0 \times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m})^2 = 7.85 \times 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Hence $\sigma_s = \dfrac{3.0 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{N}}{7.85 \times 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{m^2}} = 3.8 \times 10^{7}\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
