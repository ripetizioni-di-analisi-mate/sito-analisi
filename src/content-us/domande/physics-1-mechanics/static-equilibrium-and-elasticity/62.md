A wire of initial length $2.000\,\mathrm{m}$ is stretched by $1.5\,\mathrm{mm}$. What is its strain?

- $1.3 \times 10^{3}$
- $7.5 \times 10^{-4}$
- $7.5 \times 10^{-1}$
- $3.0 \times 10^{-3}$

## Explanation

The strain is the relative change in length, $\varepsilon = \dfrac{\Delta L}{L_0}$, with both lengths in the same unit. Since $\Delta L = 1.5 \times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m}$, $\varepsilon = \dfrac{1.5 \times 10^{-3}}{2.000} = 7.5 \times 10^{-4}$, a dimensionless number.
