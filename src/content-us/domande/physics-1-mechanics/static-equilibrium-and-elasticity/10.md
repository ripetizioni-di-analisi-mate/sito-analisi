Two forces of magnitude $30\,\mathrm{N}$, equal and opposite, act on a rigid body along parallel lines of action $0.20\,\mathrm{m}$ apart. What is the magnitude of their net torque about any point?

- $12\,\mathrm{N \cdot m}$
- $6.0\,\mathrm{N \cdot m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N \cdot m}$
- $3.0\,\mathrm{N \cdot m}$

## Explanation

The net force is zero, hence the net torque has the same value about every point. About a point on the line of action of one force, only the other force contributes, with moment arm $0.20\,\mathrm{m}$, so $\tau = 30\,\mathrm{N} \cdot 0.20\,\mathrm{m} = 6.0\,\mathrm{N \cdot m}$. A body at rest acted on by these two forces alone starts to rotate, although the net force vanishes.
