The figure shows a light bar pivoted at $O$ and three forces applied to it, with the distances from $O$ marked along the bar. Taking counterclockwise torques as positive, what is the net torque of the three forces about $O$?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="0,-0.06 4,-0.06 4,0.06 0,0.06;0,-0.06 -0.22,-0.45 0.22,-0.45" punti="0,0,pieno,O" vettori="1,-0.06,1,-1.25,30 N;4,0.06,4,1.25,20 N;4,0,5.5,0,40 N" segmenti="0,0.15,0,0.75;1,0.15,1,0.75;0,0.6,1,0.6;0,-0.55,0,-1.75;4,-0.15,4,-1.75;0,-1.6,4,-1.6" etichette="0.5,0.8,1.0 m;2.5,-1.42,4.0 m" xmin=-0.8 xmax=6.0 ymin=-2.0 ymax=1.6 titolo="A light bar pivoted at $O$ and the three forces applied to it"}

- $110\,\mathrm{N \cdot m}$
- $50\,\mathrm{N \cdot m}$
- $210\,\mathrm{N \cdot m}$
- $-50\,\mathrm{N \cdot m}$

## Explanation

The force of $40\,\mathrm{N}$ acts along the bar, so its line of action passes through $O$ and its torque is zero. The force of $30\,\mathrm{N}$ points downward at $1.0\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right of $O$ and turns the bar clockwise, while the force of $20\,\mathrm{N}$ points upward at $4.0\,\mathrm{m}$ and turns it counterclockwise. Therefore $\sum \tau = -30 \cdot 1.0\,\mathrm{N \cdot m} + 20 \cdot 4.0\,\mathrm{N \cdot m} = 50\,\mathrm{N \cdot m}$.
