A uniform horizontal beam is pinned to a wall at one end and held by a cable fastened to the other end. The upper end of the cable is moved to a lower point of the wall, so that the angle between cable and beam decreases while the beam stays horizontal. How does the tension change?

- It decreases
- It becomes zero
- It stays the same
- It increases

## Explanation

Torques about the pin give $T = \dfrac{M g}{2 \sin\theta}$, since the moment arm of the tension is $L \sin\theta$. As $\theta$ decreases the moment arm shrinks, therefore the tension grows, without bound as $\theta \to 0$.
