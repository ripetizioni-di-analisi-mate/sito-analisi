In a ladder problem, equilibrium requires the friction force $f = 50\,\mathrm{N}$ at the foot, where the normal force is $N_2 = 200\,\mathrm{N}$ and $\mu_s = 0.30$. Does the ladder stay in place?

- Yes, since $50\,\mathrm{N}$ does not exceed $\mu_s N_2 = 60\,\mathrm{N}$
- No, since $\dfrac{f}{N_2} = 0.25$ is smaller than $\mu_s$
- Yes, and the friction force is $60\,\mathrm{N}$
- No, since the friction force must equal $\mu_s N_2$

## Explanation

Static friction takes the value required by equilibrium, provided that $f \le \mu_s N_2$. Here $\mu_s N_2 = 0.30 \cdot 200\,\mathrm{N} = 60\,\mathrm{N}$ and the required $50\,\mathrm{N}$ is smaller, so the floor supplies exactly $50\,\mathrm{N}$ and the ladder stays in place.
