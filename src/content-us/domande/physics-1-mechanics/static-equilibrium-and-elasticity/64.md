A wire of diameter $1.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries a tension of $157\,\mathrm{N}$. What is the tensile stress in the wire?

- $2.0 \times 10^{8}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $2.0 \times 10^{2}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $5.0 \times 10^{7}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $1.6 \times 10^{8}\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The cross section is a circle of radius $0.50 \times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m}$, so $A = \pi r^2 = 7.85 \times 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The stress is $\sigma = \dfrac{157\,\mathrm{N}}{7.85 \times 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m^2}} = 2.0 \times 10^{8}\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Using the diameter as the radius makes the area four times too large and the stress four times too small.
