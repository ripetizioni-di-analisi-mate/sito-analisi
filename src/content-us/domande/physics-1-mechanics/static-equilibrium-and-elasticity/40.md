A uniform horizontal beam of mass $M$ is pinned to a wall at one end and held by an inclined cable fastened to the other end. What is the vertical component of the force exerted by the pin?

- $\dfrac{Mg}{2}$, for every angle of the cable
- $\dfrac{Mg}{2}$ only when the cable makes $45^\circ$ with the beam
- $Mg$, for every angle of the cable
- $0$, for every angle of the cable

## Explanation

Torques about the free end, where the cable is fastened, exclude the tension and the horizontal component of the pin reaction. The vertical component $V$ has moment arm $L$ and the weight has moment arm $\dfrac{L}{2}$, hence $V L = M g\,\dfrac{L}{2}$ and $V = \dfrac{M g}{2}$, whatever the angle. The vertical component of the tension supports the other half of the weight.
