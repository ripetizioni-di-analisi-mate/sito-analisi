A seesaw consists of a uniform board pivoted at its center. A child of mass $25\,\mathrm{kg}$ sits $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left of the pivot. How far to the right of the pivot must a child of mass $40\,\mathrm{kg}$ sit for the board to balance horizontally?

- $3.20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.00\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.625\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.25\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The pivot is at the center of gravity of the board, so the weight of the board and the pivot force both have zero torque about it. Balance of the torques of the two children gives $25\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 2.0\,\mathrm{m} = 40\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot x$, hence $x = 1.25\,\mathrm{m}$, and the heavier child sits closer to the pivot.
