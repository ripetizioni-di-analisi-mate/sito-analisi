A uniform horizontal beam of mass $30\,\mathrm{kg}$ and length $L$ is pinned to a wall at its left end. A cable joins its right end to a point of the wall at the height $L$ above the pin. What is the tension of the cable?

- $147\,\mathrm{N}$
- $416\,\mathrm{N}$
- $208\,\mathrm{N}$
- $104\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The cable joins two points separated by $L$ both horizontally and vertically, hence it makes the angle $45^\circ$ with the beam. Torques about the pin give $T L \sin 45^\circ = M g\,\dfrac{L}{2}$, so $T = \dfrac{30 \cdot 9.81}{2 \cdot 0.707}\,\mathrm{N} = 208\,\mathrm{N}$.
