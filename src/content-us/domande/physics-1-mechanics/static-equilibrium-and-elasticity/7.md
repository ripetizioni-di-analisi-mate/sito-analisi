A uniform beam rests on three smooth supports and carries a load. The vertical forces exerted by the three supports cannot be determined from the equilibrium conditions alone. What is the reason?

- The independent equations are fewer than the unknown forces
- A beam on three supports cannot be in equilibrium
- The weight of the beam does not act at its center
- The supports exert no torque on the beam

## Explanation

All the forces are vertical, hence $\sum F_x = 0$ is satisfied identically and only $\sum F_y = 0$ and $\sum \tau = 0$ remain. Two equations cannot determine three unknown forces, therefore infinitely many sets of support forces satisfy the equilibrium conditions. With two supports the same two equations give both forces.
