Two small bodies of masses $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $6.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ are fixed at the ends of a light rod of length $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$. How far from the $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ body is the center of gravity of the system?

- $0.40\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.60\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.20\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

With the origin at the $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ body, $x_G = \dfrac{2.0 \cdot 0 + 6.0 \cdot 0.80}{2.0 + 6.0}\,\mathrm{m} = 0.60\,\mathrm{m}$. The center of gravity lies closer to the heavier body, and the midpoint, $0.40\,\mathrm{m}$, would be correct only for equal masses.
