A solid sphere is subjected to a uniform increase of the pressure acting on its whole surface. How does it deform?

- Both its shape and its volume change
- Neither its shape nor its volume changes
- Its shape changes and its volume stays the same
- Its volume decreases and its shape stays spherical

## Explanation

A uniform pressure compresses the body equally in every direction, so every linear dimension changes by the same relative amount and the shape is preserved. The volume decreases by $\dfrac{\Delta V}{V_0} = -\dfrac{\Delta p}{B}$. A change of shape at constant volume is the effect of a shear stress.
