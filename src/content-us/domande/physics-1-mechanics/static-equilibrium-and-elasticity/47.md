A uniform ladder leans against a smooth vertical wall and makes the angle $70^\circ$ with a horizontal floor. What is the smallest coefficient of static friction between the ladder and the floor for which the ladder stays in place?

- $0.0910$
- $0.182$
- $1.37$
- $0.364$

## Explanation

Torques about the foot give the wall force $N_1 = \dfrac{M g}{2 \tan\theta}$, the horizontal equation gives $f = N_1$ and the vertical one gives $N_2 = M g$. Equilibrium requires $f \le \mu_s N_2$, hence $\mu_s \ge \dfrac{1}{2 \tan 70^\circ} = 0.182$.
