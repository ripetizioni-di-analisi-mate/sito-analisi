What is the name of the largest stress after which a specimen still returns to its original length once the load is removed?

- The allowable stress
- The proportional limit
- The tensile strength
- The elastic limit

## Explanation

Beyond the **elastic limit** a permanent deformation remains after the load is removed, while below it the specimen recovers its length. The proportional limit marks instead the end of the proportionality between stress and strain, and the tensile strength is the stress at which the specimen breaks.
