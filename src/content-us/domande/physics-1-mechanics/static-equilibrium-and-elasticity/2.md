The net force on a body is zero, and the net torque of the forces about a point $O$ is $12\,\mathrm{N \cdot m}$. What is the net torque of the same forces about a point $P$ located $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ from $O$?

- $12\,\mathrm{N \cdot m}$
- $6.0\,\mathrm{N \cdot m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N \cdot m}$
- $24\,\mathrm{N \cdot m}$

## Explanation

Since $\sum \vec{F}_i = \vec{0}$, the **independence of the reference point** applies: moving from $O$ to $P$ changes the total torque by $-\vec{d} \times \sum \vec{F}_i$, which vanishes. The torque about $P$ is therefore $12\,\mathrm{N \cdot m}$, whatever the distance between the two points.
