A bar of initial length $3.0\,\mathrm{m}$ is under a strain of $0.20\,\%$. What is its elongation?

- $6.0\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $60\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.60\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $600\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

A strain of $0.20\,\%$ is $\varepsilon = 2.0 \times 10^{-3}$, and the elongation is $\Delta L = \varepsilon L_0 = 2.0 \times 10^{-3} \cdot 3.0\,\mathrm{m} = 6.0 \times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m}$, that is $6.0\,\mathrm{mm}$. Taking $0.20$ as the strain, without dividing by $100$, gives $600\,\mathrm{mm}$.
