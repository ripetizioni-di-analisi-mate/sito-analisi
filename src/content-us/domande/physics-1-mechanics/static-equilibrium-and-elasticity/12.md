A crate rests on the floor of an elevator that accelerates upward. Is the crate in static equilibrium with respect to the ground?

- Yes, since it is at rest with respect to the floor of the elevator
- No, since its center of mass accelerates and the net force on it is not zero
- No, since the net torque about its center of mass is not zero
- Yes, since the net torque on it is zero

## Explanation

Static equilibrium is defined with respect to an inertial frame, and the ground is one. With respect to the ground the crate has the upward acceleration of the elevator, hence $\sum \vec{F} = M\vec{a}_{\mathrm{cm}} \neq \vec{0}$ and the normal force exceeds the weight. Rest with respect to the elevator, an accelerated frame, does not satisfy the definition.
