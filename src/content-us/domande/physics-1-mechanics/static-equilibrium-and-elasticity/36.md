A beam rests on a smooth support whose contact surface is horizontal. What is the direction of the force exerted by the support?

- Horizontal
- Vertical, upward or downward according to the loads
- Any direction, fixed by the equilibrium equations
- Vertical and upward

## Explanation

A smooth support exerts a force normal to the contact surface, so the force is vertical here. Its sense is fixed, since a support pushes and does not pull, hence the force points upward. A downward force would require the support to hold the beam down, and the beam would instead lift off.
