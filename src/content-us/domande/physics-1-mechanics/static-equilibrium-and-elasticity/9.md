A wheel turns with constant angular velocity about a fixed frictionless axle through its center of mass, so its center of mass stays at rest. Which statement about the wheel is correct?

- The net torque on it is not zero, since it rotates
- The net force and the net torque on it are zero, and it is not in static equilibrium
- It is in static equilibrium, since $\vec{a}_{\mathrm{cm}} = \vec{0}$ and $\vec{\alpha} = \vec{0}$
- The net force on it is not zero, since its points move on circles

## Explanation

The center of mass does not accelerate and the angular velocity is constant, hence $\sum \vec{F}_i = M\vec{a}_{\mathrm{cm}} = \vec{0}$ and $\sum \vec{\tau}_i = I\vec{\alpha} = \vec{0}$. **Static equilibrium** requires in addition that every point of the body be at rest, and the points of the rotating wheel move. The two conditions keep at rest only a body that is already at rest.
