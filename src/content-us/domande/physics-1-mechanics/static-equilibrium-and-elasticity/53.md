A uniform crate of mass $50\,\mathrm{kg}$ and width $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$ stands on a horizontal floor, with friction large enough to prevent sliding. A horizontal force is applied at the height $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$ above the floor. What value of the force makes the crate start to tip?

- $392\,\mathrm{N}$
- $307\,\mathrm{N}$
- $153\,\mathrm{N}$
- $76.6\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

At the limit the normal force and the friction force act at the front edge of the base, so their torques about that edge vanish. The push has moment arm $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$ and the weight has moment arm $\dfrac{w}{2} = 0.25\,\mathrm{m}$, hence $F = \dfrac{50 \cdot 9.81 \cdot 0.25}{0.80}\,\mathrm{N} = 153\,\mathrm{N}$.
