A uniform rod of mass $5.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ and length $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ is pivoted at its lower end and makes the angle $60^\circ$ with the horizontal. What is the magnitude of the torque of its weight about the pivot?

- $49.1\,\mathrm{N \cdot m}$
- $42.5\,\mathrm{N \cdot m}$
- $24.5\,\mathrm{N \cdot m}$
- $12.3\,\mathrm{N \cdot m}$

## Explanation

The weight $Mg = 49.05\,\mathrm{N}$ acts at the midpoint, $1.0\,\mathrm{m}$ from the pivot along the rod. Its line of action is vertical, so its distance from the pivot is the horizontal distance $1.0\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \cos 60^\circ = 0.50\,\mathrm{m}$, and $\tau = 49.05\,\mathrm{N} \cdot 0.50\,\mathrm{m} = 24.5\,\mathrm{N \cdot m}$. The sine would give the height of the midpoint, which is not the moment arm of a vertical force.
