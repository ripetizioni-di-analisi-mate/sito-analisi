A beam rests on two supports at its ends, and a load slides slowly along it from the left support to the right one. How does the sum $N_1 + N_2$ of the two support forces change?

- It decreases
- It increases
- It stays constant
- It first decreases and then increases

## Explanation

The vertical equation gives $N_1 + N_2 = (M + m)g$, which does not depend on the position of the load. The torque equation decides how that sum is shared: $N_1$ decreases and $N_2$ increases linearly as the load moves to the right.
