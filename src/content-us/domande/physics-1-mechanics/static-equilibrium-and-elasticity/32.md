A uniform plank of mass $12\,\mathrm{kg}$ and length $3.0\,\mathrm{m}$ rests on two supports, one at its left end and one $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ from that end. What force does the second support exert?

- $88.3\,\mathrm{N}$
- $58.9\,\mathrm{N}$
- $118\,\mathrm{N}$
- $29.4\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The weight acts at the midpoint of the plank, $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from the left end. Torques about the left support give $N_2 \cdot 2.0\,\mathrm{m} = 12 \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{N} \cdot 1.5\,\mathrm{m}$, hence $N_2 = 88.3\,\mathrm{N}$, and the left support carries the rest of the weight, $29.4\,\mathrm{N}$.
