Wires $A$ and $B$ are made of the same material and carry the same tension. Wire $B$ is twice as long as $A$ and has twice its diameter. What is the ratio $\dfrac{\Delta L_B}{\Delta L_A}$ of their elongations?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $2$

## Explanation

The elongation $\Delta L = \dfrac{F L_0}{A Y}$ is proportional to $\dfrac{L_0}{A}$ when $F$ and $Y$ are the same. Wire $B$ has twice the length and four times the area, hence $\dfrac{\Delta L_B}{\Delta L_A} = \dfrac{2}{4} = \dfrac{1}{2}$.
