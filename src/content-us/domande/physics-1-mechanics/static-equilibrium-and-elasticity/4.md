A force of magnitude $50\,\mathrm{N}$ is applied at a point $0.40\,\mathrm{m}$ from $O$, and the angle between the position vector $\vec{r}$ of that point and the force is $30^\circ$. What is the magnitude of the torque of the force about $O$?

- $40\,\mathrm{N \cdot m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N \cdot m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N \cdot m}$
- $17\,\mathrm{N \cdot m}$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the torque is $\tau = rF\sin\varphi$, with $\varphi$ the angle between $\vec{r}$ and $\vec{F}$. Hence $\tau = 0.40\,\mathrm{m} \cdot 50\,\mathrm{N} \cdot \sin 30^\circ = 10\,\mathrm{N \cdot m}$. The cosine of the angle would give the component of $\vec{F}$ along $\vec{r}$, which produces no rotation.
