An aluminum rod of length $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ and cross section $4.0 \times 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{m^2}$ has $Y = 7.0 \times 10^{10}\,\mathrm{Pa}$. What is its stiffness, regarded as a spring?

- $1.4 \times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $5.6 \times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $2.8 \times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $7.0 \times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{N/m}$

## Explanation

By the theorem on the **stiffness of a bar**, $k = \dfrac{Y A}{L_0} = \dfrac{7.0 \times 10^{10} \cdot 4.0 \times 10^{-5}}{2.0}\,\mathrm{N/m} = 1.4 \times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{N/m}$. A longer rod is less stiff, so the length divides the product $Y A$.
