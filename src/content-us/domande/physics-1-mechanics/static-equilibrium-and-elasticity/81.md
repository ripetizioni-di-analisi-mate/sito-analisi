A steel body, with bulk modulus $B = 1.6 \times 10^{11}\,\mathrm{Pa}$, is subjected to a uniform pressure increase of $1.6 \times 10^{8}\,\mathrm{Pa}$. What is its relative change in volume $\dfrac{\Delta V}{V_0}$?

- $1.0 \times 10^{-3}$
- $-1.0 \times 10^{3}$
- $-1.0 \times 10^{-3}$
- $-3.3 \times 10^{-4}$

## Explanation

The definition of the bulk modulus gives $\dfrac{\Delta V}{V_0} = -\dfrac{\Delta p}{B} = -\dfrac{1.6 \times 10^{8}}{1.6 \times 10^{11}} = -1.0 \times 10^{-3}$. The minus sign expresses that an increase in pressure reduces the volume.
