For a metal, how does the shear modulus $S$ compare with Young's modulus $Y$?

- $S$ is between a third and a half of $Y$
- $S$ equals $Y$
- $S$ is about one hundredth of $Y$
- $S$ is about twice $Y$

## Explanation

For metals the shear modulus is smaller than Young's modulus, between a third and a half of it. For steel, $Y = 2.0 \times 10^{11}\,\mathrm{Pa}$ and $S = 7.5 \times 10^{10}\,\mathrm{Pa}$, and the ratio $\dfrac{S}{Y}$ is $0.375$.
