A light horizontal strut is pinned to a wall at one end. Its outer end carries a load of weight $300\,\mathrm{N}$ and is held by a cable that rises to the wall at $60^\circ$ above the horizontal. The strut is loaded only at its two ends. What is the compression force in the strut?

- $173\,\mathrm{N}$
- $520\,\mathrm{N}$
- $346\,\mathrm{N}$
- $300\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The strut is light and loaded only at its two ends, therefore the force it exerts at each end is directed along its own axis, horizontally. At the outer end the vertical component of the tension balances the load, $T \sin 60^\circ = 300\,\mathrm{N}$, so $T = 346\,\mathrm{N}$, and the horizontal component balances the push of the strut, $C = T \cos 60^\circ = 173\,\mathrm{N}$.
