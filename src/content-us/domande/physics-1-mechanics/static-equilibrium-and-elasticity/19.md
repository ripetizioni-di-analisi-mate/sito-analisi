A uniform rod of mass $M$ and length $L$ is pivoted at one end and held horizontal by a support at the other end. What is the magnitude of the torque of its weight about the pivot?

- $\dfrac{MgL}{4}$
- $\dfrac{MgL}{2}$
- $MgL$
- $0$

## Explanation

By the theorem on the **torque of the weight**, the weights of all the particles of the rod act as the single force $Mg$ applied at the center of gravity, which lies at the midpoint, $\dfrac{L}{2}$ from the pivot. The weight is perpendicular to the rod, hence the torque is $Mg\,\dfrac{L}{2}$.
