A steel tie rod must carry a force of $2.0 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{N}$. The tensile strength of the steel is $4.0 \times 10^{8}\,\mathrm{Pa}$ and the design uses the safety factor $4$. What is the smallest cross section of the rod?

- $0.13\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $200\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $0.50\,\mathrm{cm^2}$

## Explanation

The allowable stress is $\sigma_{\mathrm{all}} = \dfrac{\sigma_u}{n} = \dfrac{4.0 \times 10^{8}}{4}\,\mathrm{Pa} = 1.0 \times 10^{8}\,\mathrm{Pa}$. The cross section must satisfy $A \ge \dfrac{F}{\sigma_{\mathrm{all}}} = \dfrac{2.0 \times 10^{4}}{1.0 \times 10^{8}}\,\mathrm{m^2} = 2.0 \times 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}$, that is $2.0\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. Dividing by the tensile strength alone gives a rod that reaches its breaking stress under the design load itself.
