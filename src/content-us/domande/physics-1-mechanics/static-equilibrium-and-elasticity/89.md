A cable is designed for a given load. If the safety factor is raised from $2$ to $4$, how does the smallest admissible cross section change?

- It does not change
- It becomes four times larger
- It doubles
- It halves

## Explanation

The allowable stress is $\dfrac{\sigma_u}{n}$, so doubling $n$ halves it. The smallest cross section $A = \dfrac{F}{\sigma_{\mathrm{all}}} = \dfrac{n F}{\sigma_u}$ is proportional to $n$, therefore it doubles.
