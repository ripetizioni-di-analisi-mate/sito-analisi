Which of the following constraints exerts on a beam a torque in addition to a force?

- A pin
- A smooth support
- An ideal cable
- A built-in end

## Explanation

A **built-in end** prevents the end of the beam both from moving and from turning, hence its reaction consists of two force components and a torque. A pin lets the body turn about it and exerts no torque about its own point, while a support and a cable each exert a single force.
