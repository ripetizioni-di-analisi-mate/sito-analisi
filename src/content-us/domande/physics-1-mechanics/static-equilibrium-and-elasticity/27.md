In the solution of an equilibrium problem, the horizontal component of the force exerted by a pin is found to be $H = -45\,\mathrm{N}$, where $H$ was drawn pointing to the right. What is the horizontal component of the pin reaction?

- $45\,\mathrm{N}$, pointing to the left
- $45\,\mathrm{N}$, pointing to the right
- Zero, since a negative result is discarded
- Undetermined, since the assumed arrangement cannot be in equilibrium

## Explanation

A pin can exert a force in any direction, so a negative component only means that the sense assumed in the diagram is the opposite of the real one. The component has magnitude $45\,\mathrm{N}$ and points to the left. A negative value signals an impossible arrangement only for a constraint whose sense is fixed, such as a cable or a support.
