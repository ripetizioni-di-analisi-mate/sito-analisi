A uniform ladder of mass $10\,\mathrm{kg}$ leans against a smooth vertical wall at $45^\circ$ with the floor. What friction force does the floor exert on the foot of the ladder?

- $24.5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $98.1\,\mathrm{N}$
- $69.4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $49.1\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Torques about the foot give $N_1 = \dfrac{M g}{2 \tan 45^\circ} = \dfrac{98.1\,\mathrm{N}}{2} = 49.1\,\mathrm{N}$, and the horizontal equation gives $f = N_1$. The friction force is directed toward the wall, against the tendency of the foot to slide away from it.
