A wire is replaced by one of the same material and length but twice the diameter, carrying the same tension. How does the elongation change?

- It does not change
- It becomes one quarter
- It doubles
- It becomes one half

## Explanation

The elongation is $\Delta L = \dfrac{F L_0}{A Y}$, and the area of a circular section is proportional to the square of the diameter. Doubling the diameter multiplies $A$ by $4$, therefore the elongation becomes one quarter of the original value.
