A uniform crate of width $w$ and height $h$ stands on a floor with coefficient of static friction $\mu_s$. A horizontal force applied at its upper edge is slowly increased. What is the necessary and sufficient condition for the crate to tip before it slides?

- $\mu_s < \dfrac{2h}{w}$
- $\mu_s > \dfrac{2h}{w}$
- $\mu_s > \dfrac{w}{2h}$
- $\mu_s < \dfrac{w}{2h}$

## Explanation

Tipping starts when $F h = M g\,\dfrac{w}{2}$, that is at $F = \dfrac{M g w}{2h}$, and sliding starts when $F = \mu_s M g$. The crate tips first when the tipping force is the smaller one, $\dfrac{M g w}{2h} < \mu_s M g$, which gives $\mu_s > \dfrac{w}{2h}$.
