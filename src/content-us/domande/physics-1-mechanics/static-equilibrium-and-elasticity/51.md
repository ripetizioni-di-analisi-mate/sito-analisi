A person climbs a ladder that leans against a smooth wall. As the person goes higher, how does the friction force that the floor must exert on the foot of the ladder change?

- It stays the same
- It increases
- It becomes zero when the person reaches the top
- It decreases

## Explanation

Torques about the foot show that the wall force grows with the horizontal distance of the person from the foot, and the horizontal equation gives $f = N_1$. The normal force stays $(M + m)g$, therefore the required friction increases as the person climbs, and the ladder slips at the height where it reaches $\mu_s N_2$.
