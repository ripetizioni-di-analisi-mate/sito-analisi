A light horizontal beam is pinned to a wall at its left end and held by a cable fastened to its right end, making the angle $30^\circ$ with the beam. A load of mass $50\,\mathrm{kg}$ hangs from the right end. What is the tension of the cable?

- $850\,\mathrm{N}$
- $566\,\mathrm{N}$
- $491\,\mathrm{N}$
- $981\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Torques about the pin contain the tension, with moment arm $L \sin 30^\circ$, and the weight of the load, with moment arm $L$. Hence $T = \dfrac{m g}{\sin 30^\circ} = \dfrac{50 \cdot 9.81}{0.500}\,\mathrm{N} = 981\,\mathrm{N}$. The tension exceeds the weight of the load, because only its vertical component $T \sin 30^\circ$ supports the load.
