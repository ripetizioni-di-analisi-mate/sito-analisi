A uniform rod of mass $3.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ and length $1.0\,\mathrm{m}$ carries a small body of mass $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ fixed at its right end. How far from the left end is the center of gravity of the system?

- $0.750\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.375\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.500\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.625\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

By the **center of gravity of a composite body**, the rod counts as $3.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ at its midpoint and the small body as $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ at $1.0\,\mathrm{m}$. Hence $x_G = \dfrac{3.0 \cdot 0.50 + 1.0 \cdot 1.0}{3.0 + 1.0}\,\mathrm{m} = 0.625\,\mathrm{m}$. The plain average of the two positions ignores the different masses.
