A bone of length $0.40\,\mathrm{m}$ and cross section $6.0\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, with $Y = 1.6 \times 10^{10}\,\mathrm{Pa}$, is compressed by a force of $1200\,\mathrm{N}$ along its length. By how much does it shorten?

- $0.31\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.050\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $5.0\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.00050\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

One square centimeter is $10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}$, so $A = 6.0 \times 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Young's modulus applies to compression as well as to tension, hence $\Delta L = \dfrac{F L_0}{A Y} = \dfrac{1200 \cdot 0.40}{6.0 \times 10^{-4} \cdot 1.6 \times 10^{10}}\,\mathrm{m} = 5.0 \times 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{m}$, that is $0.050\,\mathrm{mm}$.
