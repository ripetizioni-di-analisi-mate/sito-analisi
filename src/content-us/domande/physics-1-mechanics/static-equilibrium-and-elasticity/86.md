A steel wire of diameter $2.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ has a proportional limit of $2.5 \times 10^{8}\,\mathrm{Pa}$. What is the largest tension it can carry while its elongation is still proportional to the force?

- $1000\,\mathrm{N}$
- $785\,\mathrm{N}$
- $250\,\mathrm{N}$
- $3140\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The cross section is $A = \pi\,(1.0 \times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m})^2 = 3.14 \times 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^2}$, and proportionality holds as long as $\dfrac{F}{A}$ does not exceed the proportional limit. Hence $F_{\max} = 2.5 \times 10^{8}\,\mathrm{Pa} \cdot 3.14 \times 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^2} = 785\,\mathrm{N}$.
