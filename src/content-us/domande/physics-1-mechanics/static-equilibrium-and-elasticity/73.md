A wire of length $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ and cross section $0.50\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ stretches by $2.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ under a tension of $100\,\mathrm{N}$. What is Young's modulus of its material?

- $1.0 \times 10^{11}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $2.0 \times 10^{8}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $2.0 \times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $2.0 \times 10^{11}\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

Solving the definition for $Y$ gives $Y = \dfrac{F L_0}{A\,\Delta L} = \dfrac{100 \cdot 2.0}{0.50 \times 10^{-6} \cdot 2.0 \times 10^{-3}}\,\mathrm{Pa} = 2.0 \times 10^{11}\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Both the area and the elongation are converted to SI units before dividing.
