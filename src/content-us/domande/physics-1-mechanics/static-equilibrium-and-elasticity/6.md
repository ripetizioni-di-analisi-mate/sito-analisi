How many independent scalar equations do the equilibrium conditions provide for a rigid body acted on by forces that all lie in the $xy$ plane?

- $6$
- $2$
- $4$
- $3$

## Explanation

The condition $\sum \vec{F}_i = \vec{0}$ gives $\sum F_x = 0$ and $\sum F_y = 0$, since no force has a $z$ component. The torques of forces in the $xy$ plane are all directed along $z$, hence the second condition gives the single equation $\sum \tau = 0$. The total is three equations; six is the count for forces in space.
