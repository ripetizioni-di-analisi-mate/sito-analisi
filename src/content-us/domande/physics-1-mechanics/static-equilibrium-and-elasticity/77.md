The upper face of a block of thickness $5.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ slides by $1.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ with respect to the lower face under a shear stress. What is the shear strain?

- $2.0 \times 10^{-1}$
- $2.0 \times 10^{-3}$
- $5.0 \times 10^{1}$
- $2.0 \times 10^{-2}$

## Explanation

The shear strain is $\varepsilon_s = \dfrac{x}{h}$, the displacement of one face divided by the distance between the faces. With both lengths in meters, $\varepsilon_s = \dfrac{1.0 \times 10^{-3}}{5.0 \times 10^{-2}} = 2.0 \times 10^{-2}$.
