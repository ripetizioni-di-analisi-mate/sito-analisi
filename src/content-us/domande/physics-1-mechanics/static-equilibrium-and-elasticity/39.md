A uniform horizontal beam of mass $16\,\mathrm{kg}$ is pinned to a wall at its left end and held by a cable fastened to its right end, making the angle $40^\circ$ with the beam. What is the horizontal component of the force exerted by the pin?

- $187\,\mathrm{N}$
- $122\,\mathrm{N}$
- $78.5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $93.5\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Torques about the pin give $T = \dfrac{M g}{2 \sin 40^\circ} = 122\,\mathrm{N}$. The horizontal equation balances the horizontal component of the tension, so $H = T \cos 40^\circ = 93.5\,\mathrm{N}$, directed away from the wall. The sine would give the vertical component of the tension, $78.5\,\mathrm{N}$.
