A uniform ladder of mass $20\,\mathrm{kg}$ leans against a smooth vertical wall and makes the angle $60^\circ$ with the floor. What force does the wall exert on the ladder?

- $56.6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $113\,\mathrm{N}$
- $98.1\,\mathrm{N}$
- $170\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The wall is smooth, so its force $N_1$ is horizontal, and its moment arm about the foot is the height of the top, $L \sin 60^\circ$. The moment arm of the weight is the horizontal distance $\dfrac{L}{2} \cos 60^\circ$, hence $N_1 L \sin 60^\circ = M g\,\dfrac{L}{2} \cos 60^\circ$ and $N_1 = \dfrac{196.2\,\mathrm{N}}{2 \tan 60^\circ} = 56.6\,\mathrm{N}$.
