A uniform meter stick of mass $200\,\mathrm{g}$ carries a small body of mass $100\,\mathrm{g}$ fixed at the $90\,\mathrm{cm}$ mark. At what mark must a single support be placed for the stick to balance horizontally?

- $63.3\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $76.7\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $36.7\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $70.0\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The support force is the only force besides the weights, so the torques about the support vanish only when the total weight, applied at $G$, acts along a line through the support. The stick counts as $200\,\mathrm{g}$ at the $50\,\mathrm{cm}$ mark, hence $x_G = \dfrac{200 \cdot 50 + 100 \cdot 90}{300}\,\mathrm{cm} = 63.3\,\mathrm{cm}$. The midpoint between the two marks would require equal masses.
