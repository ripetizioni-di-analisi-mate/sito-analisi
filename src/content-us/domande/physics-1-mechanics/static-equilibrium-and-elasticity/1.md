A rigid body is at rest in an inertial frame. Which conditions on the external forces acting on it are necessary and sufficient for the body to stay at rest?

- The net torque about the center of mass is zero, whatever the net force
- Each force is balanced by an equal and opposite force applied at the same point
- The net force is zero, whatever the torques
- The net force is zero and the net torque about one point is zero

## Explanation

By the **conditions for the equilibrium of a rigid body**, the body stays at rest if and only if $\sum \vec{F}_i = \vec{0}$ and $\sum \vec{r}_i \times \vec{F}_i = \vec{0}$. The first condition keeps the center of mass at rest and the second keeps the body from starting to rotate. Forces balanced in pairs at the same points satisfy both conditions, but equilibrium does not require that arrangement.
