A seesaw consists of a uniform board of mass $10\,\mathrm{kg}$ pivoted at its center, and two children of masses $25\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $40\,\mathrm{kg}$ sit on it in balance. What is the magnitude of the force exerted by the pivot on the board?

- $147\,\mathrm{N}$
- $368\,\mathrm{N}$
- $736\,\mathrm{N}$
- $638\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

All the forces are vertical, and $\sum F_y = 0$ gives the pivot force $N = (10 + 25 + 40)\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 736\,\mathrm{N}$. The positions of the children decide whether the board balances, while the vertical sum contains all three weights, the board included.
