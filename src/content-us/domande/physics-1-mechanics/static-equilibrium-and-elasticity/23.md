A light plank rests on two supports placed at its ends, and a load of weight $W$ sits at one quarter of the length from the left support. What is the ratio $\dfrac{N_1}{N_2}$ between the force of the left support and the force of the right support?

- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $4$
- $3$

## Explanation

Torques about the left support give $N_2 L = W\,\dfrac{L}{4}$, hence $N_2 = \dfrac{W}{4}$, and the vertical equation gives $N_1 = W - N_2 = \dfrac{3W}{4}$. The ratio $\dfrac{N_1}{N_2}$ is therefore $3$, and the support nearer to the load carries more.
