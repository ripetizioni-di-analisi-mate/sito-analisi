A beam is held by two pins, one at each end. How many unknown reaction components does the problem contain, and how many equilibrium equations does a plane body provide?

- Three unknowns and three equations
- Four unknowns and three equations
- Two unknowns and three equations
- Four unknowns and four equations

## Explanation

Each pin exerts a force of unknown magnitude and direction, written through two components $H$ and $V$, so two pins give four unknowns. A plane body provides the three equations $\sum F_x = 0$, $\sum F_y = 0$ and $\sum \tau = 0$, hence the four components cannot all be found from equilibrium alone. A pin and a cable, or a pin and a smooth support, give three unknowns.
