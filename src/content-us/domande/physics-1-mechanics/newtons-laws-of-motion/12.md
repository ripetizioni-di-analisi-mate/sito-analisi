Two forces of magnitudes $5.0\,\mathrm{N}$ and $12\,\mathrm{N}$ act on the same body, with directions not specified. Which value cannot be the magnitude of the net force?

- $7.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N}$
- $13\,\mathrm{N}$
- $17\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the sum of two vectors lies between the difference and the sum of their magnitudes. The net force therefore satisfies $7.0\,\mathrm{N} \le F_{\text{net}} \le 17\,\mathrm{N}$, with the ends reached for opposite and for parallel forces, and $13\,\mathrm{N}$ for perpendicular forces. A net force of $20\,\mathrm{N}$ is outside this range.
