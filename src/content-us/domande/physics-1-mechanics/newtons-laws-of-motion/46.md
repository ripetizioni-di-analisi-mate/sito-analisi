Why is the normal force not given by a formula of its own?

- It always equals the weight, so no separate formula is needed.
- It depends only on the area of contact, which is rarely known.
- Its magnitude takes whatever value prevents the body from penetrating the surface.
- It depends on the speed of the body along the surface.

## Explanation

The **normal force** is a constraint force, so its magnitude is the one required to keep the body from moving into the surface. It is found from the equations of motion together with the other unknowns, and it equals $mg$ only in particular cases.
