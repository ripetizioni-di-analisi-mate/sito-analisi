A body moves with constant velocity. A single additional constant force is then applied to it, directed opposite to the velocity, and nothing else changes. What is the subsequent motion?

- The body slows down and then remains at rest.
- The body slows down, stops for an instant and then moves in the sense of the force.
- The body keeps its velocity, because it was already in equilibrium.
- The body slows down to a smaller constant speed.

## Explanation

The net force becomes the added constant force, so by the **second law** the acceleration is constant and opposite to the initial velocity. The velocity therefore decreases, reaches zero and then grows in the sense of the force, since the acceleration does not vanish when the body stops.
