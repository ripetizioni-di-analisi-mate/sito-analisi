Two blocks of masses $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $4.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ are in contact on a frictionless floor, and a horizontal force $F$ pushes the pair. To which block must $F$ be applied to make the contact force between them as small as possible?

- To the $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ block
- To either block, since the contact force is the same
- To the $4.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ block
- To neither, since the contact force always equals $F$

## Explanation

The acceleration is $\dfrac{F}{5.0\,\mathrm{kg}}$ in both cases, and the contact force is the only force on the block that is not pushed, so it equals that block's mass times $a$. Pushing the $4.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ block gives a contact force of $\dfrac{F}{5}$, while pushing the other one gives $\dfrac{4F}{5}$.
