A rocket far from every other body expels gas backward from its engine. Which force accelerates the rocket forward?

- The forward push of the expelled gas on the rocket
- The push of the gas against the surrounding empty space
- No force: the rocket moves forward by inertia
- A force exerted by the engine on the rocket, with no other body involved

## Explanation

The rocket pushes the gas backward, and by the **third law** the gas pushes the rocket forward with the same magnitude. This external force accelerates the rocket, with no need for any surrounding medium. A body cannot accelerate itself through forces it exerts on itself.
