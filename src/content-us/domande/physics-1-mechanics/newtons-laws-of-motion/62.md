An ideal string has negligible mass and constant length. Which of these two properties makes its two ends move with speeds of equal magnitude along the string?

- The negligible mass
- Both properties together
- Neither: it follows from the equal tension
- The constant length

## Explanation

If the length does not change, whatever the string gains at one end it loses at the other, so the two ends move with equal speeds along the string. The negligible mass is the property that makes the tension uniform, a different consequence.
