Two forces of magnitudes $3.0\,\mathrm{N}$ and $4.0\,\mathrm{N}$, perpendicular to each other, act on the same body. What is the magnitude of the net force?

- $7.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $5.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $25\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Forces add as vectors, so the magnitude of the **net force** is not the sum of the magnitudes. The two forces are perpendicular, hence by the Pythagorean theorem
$$
F_{\text{net}} = \sqrt{(3.0\,\mathrm{N})^2 + (4.0\,\mathrm{N})^2} = 5.0\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
