An Atwood machine carries two bodies of equal masses. The bodies are given a push and then released while moving. What happens next?

- They stop at once.
- The descending body speeds up.
- They slow down until they stop.
- They keep moving at constant speed.

## Explanation

With $m_1 = m_2$ the **Atwood machine** formula gives $a = 0$, and the tension equals each weight. Each body is in equilibrium, so by the **first law** it keeps the velocity it had when released.
