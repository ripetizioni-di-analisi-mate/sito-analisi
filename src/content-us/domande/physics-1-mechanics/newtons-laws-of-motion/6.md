A lamp of weight $25\,\mathrm{N}$ hangs at rest from a single vertical cable of negligible mass. What is the tension of the cable?

- $2.5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $245\,\mathrm{N}$
- $25\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12.5\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The lamp is at rest, so by **Newton's first law** the net force on it is zero. The only forces are the weight, downward, and the pull of the cable, upward, hence $T = w = 25\,\mathrm{N}$. The weight is already a force, so it is not multiplied or divided by $g$.
