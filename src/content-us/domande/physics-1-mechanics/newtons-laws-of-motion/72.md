In an Atwood machine with an ideal string, the heavier body descends with acceleration $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the motion of the lighter body?

- It rises with acceleration $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- It descends with acceleration $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- It rises with an acceleration smaller than $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, because it is lighter.
- It remains at rest.

## Explanation

By the **constraint of an inextensible string**, the two bodies have accelerations of equal magnitude along the string, and one advances by as much as the other retreats. The lighter body therefore rises with $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
