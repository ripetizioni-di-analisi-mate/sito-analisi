An Atwood machine carries bodies of masses $5.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $3.0\,\mathrm{kg}$, and the system is moving. Without computing it exactly, which statement about the tension $T$ of the string is correct?

- $T = 49.1\,\mathrm{N}$, the weight of the heavier body
- $29.4\,\mathrm{N} < T < 49.1\,\mathrm{N}$
- $T < 29.4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $T > 49.1\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The lighter body accelerates upward, so the tension exceeds its weight $3.0 \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{N} = 29.4\,\mathrm{N}$. The heavier body accelerates downward, so the tension is smaller than its weight $49.1\,\mathrm{N}$. The exact value $\dfrac{2m_1m_2}{m_1+m_2}g = 36.8\,\mathrm{N}$ lies in between.
