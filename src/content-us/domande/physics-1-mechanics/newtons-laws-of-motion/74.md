Two blocks on a frictionless table are joined by an ideal string, and a horizontal force $F$ pulls the first block, of mass $m_1$, along the string. Which value does the tension approach when the mass $m_2$ of the second block is much larger than $m_1$?

- $F$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{F}{2}$
- $2F$

## Explanation

The tension is $T = \dfrac{m_2}{m_1 + m_2}F$. When $m_2 \gg m_1$ the fraction tends to $1$, so $T$ approaches $F$, and almost the whole force goes into accelerating the second block.
