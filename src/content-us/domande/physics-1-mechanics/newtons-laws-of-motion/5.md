A frame $S'$ moves with constant velocity $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$ with respect to an inertial frame $S$. A body has acceleration $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ in $S$. What is its acceleration measured in $S'$?

- $22\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The velocities measured in the two frames differ by the constant $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and the derivative of a constant is zero. Therefore the two frames measure the same acceleration, $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, and $S'$ is inertial as well.
