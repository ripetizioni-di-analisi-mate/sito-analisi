How is the constraint between the accelerations of two bodies joined by an inextensible string obtained?

- By applying the third law to the two ends of the string
- By setting the tension equal at all points of the string
- By applying the first law to the pulley
- By differentiating twice in time the condition that the length of the string is constant

## Explanation

The lengths $s_1$ and $s_2$ of string on the two sides satisfy $s_1 + s_2 + c = L$ with $c$ and $L$ constant. Differentiating twice gives $\ddot{s}_1 = -\ddot{s}_2$, which is the **constraint of an inextensible string**.
