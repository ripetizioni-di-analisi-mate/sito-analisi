Which of the following frames of reference is not inertial?

- A car taking a curve at constant speed
- A train moving along a straight track at constant speed
- An airplane in level flight with constant velocity
- A boat drifting with constant velocity on a still lake

## Explanation

A frame is **inertial** when it moves with constant velocity with respect to an inertial frame, and the surface of the Earth is taken as inertial. The car on the curve changes the direction of its velocity, so it accelerates and its frame is not inertial, although its speed is constant. The other three frames move with constant velocity with respect to the ground.
