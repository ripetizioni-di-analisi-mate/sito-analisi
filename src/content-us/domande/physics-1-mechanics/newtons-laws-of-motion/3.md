Two forces, each of magnitude $6.0\,\mathrm{N}$, act on the same body, and the angle between them is $120^\circ$. What is the magnitude of the net force?

- $12\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $10.4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $6.0\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Take the first force along the $x$ axis, so the second has the components $6.0\cos 120^\circ\,\mathrm{N} = -3.0\,\mathrm{N}$ and $6.0\sin 120^\circ\,\mathrm{N} = 5.2\,\mathrm{N}$. The **net force** therefore has the components $(3.0, 5.2)\,\mathrm{N}$, and its magnitude is $\sqrt{3.0^2 + 5.2^2}\,\mathrm{N} = 6.0\,\mathrm{N}$. The value $10.4\,\mathrm{N}$ belongs to an angle of $60^\circ$ between the forces.
