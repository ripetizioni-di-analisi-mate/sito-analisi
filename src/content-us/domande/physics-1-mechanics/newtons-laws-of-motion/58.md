A block of mass $3.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ hangs at rest from a string that passes over an ideal pulley and then runs horizontally to a wall, where it is tied. What is the tension in the horizontal part of the string?

- $14.7\,\mathrm{N}$
- $58.9\,\mathrm{N}$
- $29.4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $20.8\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The block is at rest, so the vertical part of the string has $T = mg = 29.4\,\mathrm{N}$. An **ideal pulley** changes the direction of the string without changing its tension, hence the horizontal part has the same tension.
