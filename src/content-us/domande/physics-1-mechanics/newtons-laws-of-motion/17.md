The net force on a body is doubled, and at the same time a load is attached that triples the mass. How does the new acceleration compare with the original one?

- It is $\dfrac{3}{2}$ of the original.
- It is $6$ times the original.
- It is $\dfrac{1}{6}$ of the original.
- It is $\dfrac{2}{3}$ of the original.

## Explanation

By the **second law** $a = \dfrac{F_{\text{net}}}{m}$, so the acceleration is proportional to the force and inversely proportional to the mass. Doubling the force and tripling the mass multiply the acceleration by $\dfrac{2}{3}$.
