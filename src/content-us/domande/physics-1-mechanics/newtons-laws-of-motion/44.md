A box of mass $10\,\mathrm{kg}$ rests on a horizontal floor and is pushed vertically downward with a force of $20\,\mathrm{N}$. What is the normal force exerted by the floor?

- $118\,\mathrm{N}$
- $98.1\,\mathrm{N}$
- $78.1\,\mathrm{N}$
- $20.0\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The box is at rest, so the vertical forces balance: $N - mg - F = 0$. Hence
$$
N = mg + F = 98.1\,\mathrm{N} + 20\,\mathrm{N} = 118\,\mathrm{N} ,
$$
larger than the weight, because the floor supports the push as well.
