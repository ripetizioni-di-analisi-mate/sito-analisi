Which combination of units is equal to one newton?

- $1\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s^2}$
- $1\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s^2}$
- $1\,\mathrm{g\cdot cm/s^2}$

## Explanation

The **second law** $F = ma$ fixes the unit of force, since $1\,\mathrm{N}$ is the net force that gives a mass of $1\,\mathrm{kg}$ an acceleration of $1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, hence $1\,\mathrm{N} = 1\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s^2}$. The combination $\mathrm{g\cdot cm/s^2}$ equals $10^{-5}\,\mathrm{N}$.
