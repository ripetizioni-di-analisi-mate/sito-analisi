The velocity of a body of mass $5.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ moving along a line is $v(t) = 4.0 + 2.0\,t$, with $v$ in $\mathrm{m/s}$ and $t$ in seconds. What is the magnitude of the net force on the body?

- $20\,\mathrm{N}$
- $30\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.40\,\mathrm{N}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The acceleration is the derivative of the velocity, $a = \dfrac{dv}{dt} = 2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, constant in time. By the **second law** $F = ma = 5.0\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 10\,\mathrm{N}$. The initial velocity $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ does not enter the force.
