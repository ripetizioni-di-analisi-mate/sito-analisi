A block released on a frictionless incline slides down with acceleration $4.90\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the angle of the incline?

- $60.0^\circ$
- $26.6^\circ$
- $30.0^\circ$
- $45.0^\circ$

## Explanation

From $a = g\sin\theta$ one gets $\sin\theta = \dfrac{4.90}{9.81} = 0.500$, hence $\theta = 30.0^\circ$. Solving $\cos\theta = 0.500$ instead gives $60.0^\circ$.
