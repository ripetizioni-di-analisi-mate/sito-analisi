A body of mass $0.50\,\mathrm{kg}$ moves along the $x$ axis with $x(t) = 2.0\,t^3$, where $x$ is in meters and $t$ in seconds. What is the net force on it at $t = 3.0\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $27\,\mathrm{N}$
- $9.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $18\,\mathrm{N}$
- $72\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The acceleration is the second derivative of the position: $v = 6.0\,t^2$ and $a = 12\,t$, which at $t = 3.0\,\mathrm{s}$ gives $a = 36\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. By the **second law** $F = ma = 0.50\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 36\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 18\,\mathrm{N}$. The force grows with time, since the acceleration does.
