Why is the net force not drawn beside the other forces in a free-body diagram?

- It is always zero.
- It acts on a different body.
- It is the sum of the forces already drawn, not an additional force exerted by another body.
- It is drawn only when the body is in equilibrium.

## Explanation

A **free-body diagram** lists the forces exerted on the body by other bodies. The net force has no agent of its own, since it is the vector sum of those forces, so drawing it as well would count each force twice.
