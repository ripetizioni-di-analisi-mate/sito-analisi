A block slides down a frictionless plane inclined at $40^\circ$. The normal force exerted by the plane is what fraction of the weight of the block?

- $0.643$
- $1.00$
- $1.31$
- $0.766$

## Explanation

The equation perpendicular to the plane gives $N = mg\cos\theta$, so $\dfrac{N}{mg} = \cos 40^\circ = 0.766$. The plane supports only the component of the weight perpendicular to its surface, hence the fraction is less than $1$.
