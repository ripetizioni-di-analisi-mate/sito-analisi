A string with tension $50\,\mathrm{N}$ passes over an ideal pulley; one part of the string is horizontal and the other is vertical. What is the magnitude of the force the string exerts on the pulley?

- $100\,\mathrm{N}$
- $50\,\mathrm{N}$
- $35\,\mathrm{N}$
- $71\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Each part pulls the pulley along itself with the same tension $T = 50\,\mathrm{N}$, since the **pulley is ideal**. The two pulls are perpendicular, so their resultant has magnitude $\sqrt{2}\,T = 71\,\mathrm{N}$. The value $2T$ holds only when the two parts are parallel.
