A sled on frictionless snow is pulled by a rope with a force of fixed magnitude $F$ at the angle $\theta$ above the horizontal, and the sled stays on the snow. What happens when $\theta$ is increased?

- The acceleration increases and the normal force decreases.
- The acceleration decreases and the normal force increases.
- The acceleration and the normal force do not change.
- The acceleration decreases and the normal force decreases.

## Explanation

The horizontal equation gives $a = \dfrac{F\cos\theta}{m}$, and the vertical one gives $N = mg - F\sin\theta$. As $\theta$ grows, $\cos\theta$ decreases and $\sin\theta$ increases, so both $a$ and $N$ decrease.
