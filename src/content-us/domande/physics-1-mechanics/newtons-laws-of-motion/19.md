A ball moves east while the net force on it points west. In which direction does the ball accelerate?

- East
- West
- Its acceleration is zero until it stops.
- Vertically downward

## Explanation

By the **second law** the acceleration has the direction and the sense of the net force, whatever the direction of the velocity. The acceleration therefore points west, and the ball is slowing down.
