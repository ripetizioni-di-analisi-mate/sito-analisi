A block of mass $8.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ on a frictionless horizontal floor is pushed by a force directed below the horizontal, and the block slides along the floor. Which of the following can be the normal force exerted by the floor?

- $50\,\mathrm{N}$
- $39\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $95\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The push has a downward component $F\sin\theta > 0$, and the vertical equation gives $N = mg + F\sin\theta$. The normal force therefore exceeds the weight $mg = 78.5\,\mathrm{N}$, and among the values listed only $95\,\mathrm{N}$ satisfies this condition.
