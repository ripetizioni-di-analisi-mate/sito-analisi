A hockey puck slides on frictionless horizontal ice with constant velocity. Which statement about the forces acting on the puck is correct?

- The net force on the puck is zero.
- A small forward force is needed to keep the puck moving.
- The net force on the puck points along its velocity.
- No force at all acts on the puck.

## Explanation

The velocity of the puck is constant, so the puck is in **equilibrium** and the vector sum of the forces on it is zero. Forces do act on it, namely the weight and the normal force of the ice, but they have equal magnitudes and opposite senses. By **Newton's first law** uniform motion requires no net force, therefore no forward push is needed.
