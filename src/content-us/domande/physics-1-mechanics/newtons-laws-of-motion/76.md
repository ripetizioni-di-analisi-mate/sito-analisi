An Atwood machine carries bodies of masses $3.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$. What is the magnitude of their acceleration?

- $6.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $4.9\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0.50\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

By the **Atwood machine** formula
$$
a = \frac{m_1 - m_2}{m_1 + m_2}\,g = \frac{2.0}{4.0} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 4.9\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
The unbalanced weight $(m_1 - m_2)g$ accelerates the total mass $m_1 + m_2$, not $m_1$ alone.
