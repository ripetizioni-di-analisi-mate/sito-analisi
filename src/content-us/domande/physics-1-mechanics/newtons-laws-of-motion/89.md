A block on a frictionless incline of angle $\theta$ is given a push up the plane and then released while still moving upward. What is its acceleration while it moves up?

- $g\sin\theta$, directed up the incline
- Zero, until the block stops
- $g$, directed vertically downward
- $g\sin\theta$, directed down the incline

## Explanation

After the release only the weight and the normal force act, exactly as for a block sliding down. Hence the acceleration is $g\sin\theta$ down the incline whatever the sense of the velocity, and the block slows down on the way up.
