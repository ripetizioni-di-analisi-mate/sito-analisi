Two blocks of masses $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $5.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ are released together from rest on the same frictionless incline. How do they move?

- The heavier block slides faster.
- The lighter block slides faster.
- They slide side by side with the same acceleration.
- The heavier block slides more slowly, because the plane pushes it harder.

## Explanation

On a **frictionless incline** the acceleration is $a = g\sin\theta$, independent of the mass. The heavier block has a larger component of the weight along the plane, and also a larger mass to accelerate, so the two blocks stay side by side.
