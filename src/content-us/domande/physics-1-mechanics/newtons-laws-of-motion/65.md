A block on a frictionless incline is held at rest by a string parallel to the incline. How many forces appear in its free-body diagram?

- $2$
- $4$
- $5$
- $3$

## Explanation

Three bodies act on the block: the Earth with the weight, the incline with the normal force and the string with the tension. The plane is frictionless, so there is no force along the surface, and the components of the weight are not additional forces.
