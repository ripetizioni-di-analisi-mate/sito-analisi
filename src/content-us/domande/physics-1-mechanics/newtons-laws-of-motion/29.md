On a velocity-time graph, the velocity of a cart of mass $3.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ moving along a straight track is a straight line from $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at $t = 0$ to $0$ at $t = 3.0\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the net force on the cart?

- $6.0\,\mathrm{N}$, along the initial velocity
- $6.0\,\mathrm{N}$, opposite to the initial velocity
- $2.0\,\mathrm{N}$, opposite to the initial velocity
- $18\,\mathrm{N}$, opposite to the initial velocity

## Explanation

The slope of the graph is the acceleration, $a = \dfrac{0 - 6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}}{3.0\,\mathrm{s}} = -2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, negative with respect to the initial velocity. By the **second law** $F = ma = -6.0\,\mathrm{N}$, so the net force has magnitude $6.0\,\mathrm{N}$ and points opposite to the initial velocity.
