The same net force acts in turn on two bodies $A$ and $B$, and the acceleration of $A$ is three times the acceleration of $B$. How are their masses related?

- $m_B = 3\,m_A$
- $m_A = 3\,m_B$
- $m_B = 9\,m_A$
- $m_A = m_B$

## Explanation

For the same force, accelerations and masses are inversely proportional, $\dfrac{a_A}{a_B} = \dfrac{m_B}{m_A}$. Since $\dfrac{a_A}{a_B} = 3$, the mass of $B$ is three times the mass of $A$, so the body with the larger mass receives the smaller acceleration.
