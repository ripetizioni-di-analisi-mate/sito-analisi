A body of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ is acted on by its weight and by an upward vertical pull of $15\,\mathrm{N}$. With the $y$ axis pointing upward, what is $a_y$?

- $-2.3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2.3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $17\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The weight is $mg = 19.6\,\mathrm{N}$, directed downward, so the **second law along $y$** gives
$$
a_y = \frac{15\,\mathrm{N} - 19.6\,\mathrm{N}}{2.0\,\mathrm{kg}} = -2.3\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
The negative sign means that the acceleration points downward, since the pull is smaller than the weight.
