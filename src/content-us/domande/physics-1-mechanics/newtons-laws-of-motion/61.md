Which properties define an ideal pulley?

- Negligible radius and a fixed axle
- Negligible mass and negligible friction at the axle, with the pulley free to turn
- A rope that does not slip on it, whatever the mass of the pulley
- Being fixed so that it cannot turn

## Explanation

A pulley is **ideal** when its mass and the friction at its axle are negligible and it turns freely. Under these conditions it changes the direction of the string without changing the tension, so a single symbol $T$ serves for both sides.
