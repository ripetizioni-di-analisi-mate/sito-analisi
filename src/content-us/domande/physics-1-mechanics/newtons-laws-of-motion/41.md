A body has a weight of $49\,\mathrm{N}$ on the surface of the Earth. What is its mass?

- $49\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $481\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $5.0\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.20\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The **weight** is $w = mg$, therefore
$$
m = \frac{w}{g} = \frac{49\,\mathrm{N}}{9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}} = 5.0\,\mathrm{kg} .
$$
A weight in newtons and a mass in kilograms are different quantities.
