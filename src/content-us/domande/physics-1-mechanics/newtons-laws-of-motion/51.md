A person of mass $80\,\mathrm{kg}$ stands on a scale in an elevator, and the scale reads $700\,\mathrm{N}$. What is the acceleration of the elevator?

- $1.1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, downward
- $1.1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, upward
- $8.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, downward
- $8.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, upward

## Explanation

With the $y$ axis upward the **second law** gives $N - mg = ma$, hence
$$
a = \frac{N}{m} - g = 8.75\,\mathrm{m/s^2} - 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = -1.06\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
The negative sign means that the acceleration points downward, consistent with a reading below the weight $785\,\mathrm{N}$.
