A block rests on a frictionless ramp. What is the direction of the normal force exerted by the ramp?

- Perpendicular to the ramp, away from it
- Vertically upward
- Parallel to the ramp, uphill
- Perpendicular to the ramp, into it

## Explanation

The **normal force** is perpendicular to the surface of contact and directed away from it, since a surface can only push. On a ramp this direction is tilted with respect to the vertical by the angle of the ramp.
