An elevator descends at a constant speed of $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. A person of mass $50\,\mathrm{kg}$ stands on a scale on its floor. What does the scale read?

- $341\,\mathrm{N}$
- $641\,\mathrm{N}$
- $491\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The speed is constant, so the acceleration is zero and the person is in equilibrium. The normal force therefore equals the weight, $N = mg = 50\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 491\,\mathrm{N}$. A velocity does not enter the formula $N = m(g+a)$.
