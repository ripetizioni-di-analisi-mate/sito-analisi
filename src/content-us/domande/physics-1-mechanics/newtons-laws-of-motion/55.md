A uniform rope of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ hangs vertically from the ceiling and carries a block of mass $5.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ at its lower end, everything at rest. What is the tension at the top of the rope?

- $49.1\,\mathrm{N}$
- $58.9\,\mathrm{N}$
- $19.6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $68.7\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The rope has appreciable mass, so its tension is not uniform: at each point it supports everything below. At the top it supports the block and the whole rope, hence
$$
T = (5.0 + 2.0)\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 68.7\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The value $58.9\,\mathrm{N}$ is the tension at the midpoint of the rope.
