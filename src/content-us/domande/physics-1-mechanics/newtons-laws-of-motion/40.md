A body is carried from the Earth to the Moon. Which statement is correct?

- Its mass and its weight both decrease.
- Its weight is unchanged and its mass decreases.
- Its mass is unchanged and its weight decreases.
- Its mass and its weight are both unchanged.

## Explanation

Mass measures the resistance to a change of velocity and is the same at every place. The **weight** $w = mg$ depends on $g$, which on the Moon is about $1.6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, hence the weight is smaller there.
