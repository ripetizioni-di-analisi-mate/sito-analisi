A person standing on the ground jumps straight up. Which force gives the person the upward acceleration while the feet are still on the ground?

- The downward push of the person on the ground
- The weight of the person
- The upward push of the ground on the person
- The internal forces of the muscles, with no external force needed

## Explanation

The person pushes the ground downward, and by the **third law** the ground pushes the person upward with the same magnitude. Only forces exerted on the person enter the person's equation, and when this upward push exceeds the weight the person accelerates upward. Internal forces appear in pairs and cancel.
