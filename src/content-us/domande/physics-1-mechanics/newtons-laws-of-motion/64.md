A block is pulled along a frictionless floor by a rope. Which arrow belongs in the free-body diagram of the block?

- The pull of the block on the rope
- The pull of the rope on the block
- The net force on the block
- The acceleration of the block

## Explanation

A **free-body diagram** contains only the forces exerted on the chosen body by other bodies. The pull of the block on the rope belongs to the diagram of the rope, the net force is the sum of the forces already drawn, and the acceleration is not a force.
