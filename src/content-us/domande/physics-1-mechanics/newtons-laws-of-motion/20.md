The net force on a body of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ has the components $F_x = 6.0\,\mathrm{N}$ and $F_y = -8.0\,\mathrm{N}$. What is the magnitude of the acceleration?

- $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $7.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $1.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the net force is $\sqrt{6.0^2 + 8.0^2}\,\mathrm{N} = 10\,\mathrm{N}$, and by the **second law**
$$
a = \frac{F_{\text{net}}}{m} = \frac{10\,\mathrm{N}}{2.0\,\mathrm{kg}} = 5.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
The sign of a component does not affect the magnitude.
