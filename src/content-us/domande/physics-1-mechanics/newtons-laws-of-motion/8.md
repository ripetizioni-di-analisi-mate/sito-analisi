Two forces act on a body: $\vec{F}_1 = (3.0, -2.0)\,\mathrm{N}$ and $\vec{F}_2 = (-1.0, 5.0)\,\mathrm{N}$. What is the net force?

- $(4.0,\ 3.0)\,\mathrm{N}$
- $(2.0,\ 3.0)\,\mathrm{N}$
- $(2.0,\ 7.0)\,\mathrm{N}$
- $(-2.0,\ -3.0)\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The **net force** is the vector sum, obtained by adding the components one axis at a time:
$$
F_x = 3.0 - 1.0 = 2.0\,\mathrm{N},
\qquad
F_y = -2.0 + 5.0 = 3.0\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
Each component keeps its own sign in the sum.
