A body is in equilibrium under three forces. Two of them are $5.0\,\mathrm{N}$ along the positive $x$ axis and $5.0\,\mathrm{N}$ along the positive $y$ axis. What is the third force?

- $7.1\,\mathrm{N}$ at $45^\circ$ from the positive $x$ axis
- $10\,\mathrm{N}$ at $225^\circ$ from the positive $x$ axis
- $7.1\,\mathrm{N}$ at $225^\circ$ from the positive $x$ axis
- $10\,\mathrm{N}$ at $45^\circ$ from the positive $x$ axis

## Explanation

In equilibrium the three forces add to zero, so the third force is the opposite of the sum of the other two. That sum is $(5.0, 5.0)\,\mathrm{N}$, of magnitude $5.0\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{N} = 7.1\,\mathrm{N}$ at $45^\circ$. Hence the third force is $(-5.0, -5.0)\,\mathrm{N}$, with the same magnitude and direction $225^\circ$.
