A body of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ starts from rest under a constant net force and covers $9.0\,\mathrm{m}$ in $3.0\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the magnitude of the net force?

- $2.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $6.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The force is constant, so the motion from rest is uniformly accelerated and $d = \dfrac{1}{2}at^2$. Hence
$$
a = \frac{2d}{t^2} = \frac{2 \cdot 9.0\,\mathrm{m}}{(3.0\,\mathrm{s})^2} = 2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2},
\qquad
F = m a = 4.0\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
Losing the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$ gives half of this value.
