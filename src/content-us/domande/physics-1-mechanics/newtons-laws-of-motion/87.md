For a block on a frictionless incline, what happens to the acceleration $a$ and to the normal force $N$ as the angle of the incline approaches $90^\circ$?

- $a$ approaches $0$ and $N$ approaches $mg$.
- $a$ approaches $g$ and $N$ approaches $0$.
- $a$ approaches $g$ and $N$ approaches $mg$.
- $a$ and $N$ both approach $0$.

## Explanation

From $a = g\sin\theta$ and $N = mg\cos\theta$, the limit $\theta \to 90^\circ$ gives $a \to g$ and $N \to 0$. The block falls freely along a vertical wall, which does not press on it.
