A box of mass $4.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ rests on a horizontal floor and is pulled by a vertical upward force. What is the smallest pull that lifts the box off the floor?

- $19.6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $78.5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.408\,\mathrm{N}$
- $39.2\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

While the box is on the floor, $N + F - mg = 0$, so $N = mg - F$. The box leaves the floor when the **normal force** vanishes, that is when $F = mg = 4.0\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 39.2\,\mathrm{N}$.
