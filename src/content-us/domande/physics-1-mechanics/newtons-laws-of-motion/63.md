A ball has left the hand of the thrower and is moving upward; the resistance of the air is neglected. Which forces act on the ball?

- Only its weight
- Its weight and an upward force from the throw
- Only an upward force from the throw
- No force, until the ball reaches the top

## Explanation

Every force has an agent, another body that exerts it. Once the ball has left the hand, the hand exerts nothing on it, and the only body still acting is the Earth, through the weight. The upward motion continues because of the velocity the ball already has, which a **free-body diagram** does not show.
