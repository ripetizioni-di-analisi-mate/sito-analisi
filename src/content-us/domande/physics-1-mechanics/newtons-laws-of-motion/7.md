A body is in equilibrium. Which quantity is necessarily zero?

- The net force acting on the body
- The velocity of the body
- Each of the forces acting on the body
- The sum of the magnitudes of the forces acting on the body

## Explanation

A body is in **equilibrium** when its velocity is constant, and then the vector sum of the forces on it vanishes. The velocity itself can have any constant value, and the individual forces can be large, provided that their vector sum is zero.
