A frictionless incline is $5.0\,\mathrm{m}$ long and its top is $3.0\,\mathrm{m}$ above its base. What is the acceleration of a block sliding down it?

- $7.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $5.9\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $9.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $7.4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The sine of the angle is the height divided by the length of the incline, $\sin\theta = \dfrac{3.0}{5.0} = 0.60$. For a **block on a frictionless incline**
$$
a = g\sin\theta = 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot 0.60 = 5.9\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
The horizontal base is $4.0\,\mathrm{m}$, so the ratio $\dfrac{4.0}{5.0}$ is the cosine and $\dfrac{3.0}{4.0}$ is the tangent.
