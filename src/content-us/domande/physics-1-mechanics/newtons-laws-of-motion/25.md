The same net force gives a standard body of mass $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ the acceleration $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and a body of unknown mass the acceleration $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the unknown mass?

- $0.40\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $10\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $3.0\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Under the same force, masses are inversely proportional to the accelerations, $\dfrac{m}{m_0} = \dfrac{a_0}{a}$. Hence $m = 1.0\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot \dfrac{5.0}{2.0} = 2.5\,\mathrm{kg}$; the unknown body has the smaller acceleration, so it has the larger mass.
