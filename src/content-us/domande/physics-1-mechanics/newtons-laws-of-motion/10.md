A spaceship far from every other body moves at $300\,\mathrm{m/s}$ when its engines are switched off. What happens to its velocity afterward?

- It decreases gradually until the ship stops.
- It drops at once to zero.
- It stays $300\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the same direction.
- It stays $300\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in magnitude while its direction slowly changes.

## Explanation

With the engines off and no other body nearby, the net force on the ship is zero. By **Newton's first law** the ship then moves with constant velocity, so both the speed and the direction of motion stay the same. A force is needed only to change the velocity.
