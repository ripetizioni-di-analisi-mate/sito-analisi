A book rests on a table. Which force forms an action-reaction pair with the weight of the book?

- The upward normal force of the table on the book
- The downward push of the book on the table
- The upward push of the floor on the table
- The upward gravitational pull of the book on the Earth

## Explanation

The weight is the force of the Earth on the book, so by the **third law** its partner is the force of the book on the Earth, of the same kind and opposite sense. The normal force acts on the same body as the weight and has a different agent, hence it is not the partner, even though its magnitude here equals $mg$.
