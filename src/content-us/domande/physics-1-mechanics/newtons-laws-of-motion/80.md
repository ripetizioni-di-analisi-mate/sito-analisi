A block on a frictionless horizontal table is pulled by a string that passes over an ideal pulley at the edge and carries a hanging body of fixed mass. The mass of the block on the table is made larger and larger. Which value does the tension approach?

- Zero
- Half the weight of the hanging body
- The weight of the hanging body
- The weight of the block on the table

## Explanation

For the block $T = m_1 a$ and for the hanging body $m_2 g - T = m_2 a$, which give $T = \dfrac{m_1 m_2}{m_1 + m_2}g$. When $m_1 \gg m_2$ the acceleration tends to zero and the tension approaches $m_2 g$, the weight of the hanging body.
