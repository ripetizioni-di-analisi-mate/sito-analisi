A body weighs $98.1\,\mathrm{N}$ on the Earth. What is its weight on the Moon, where $g = 1.6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$?

- $98\,\mathrm{N}$
- $16\,\mathrm{N}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N}$
- $600\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The mass is $m = \dfrac{98.1\,\mathrm{N}}{9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}} = 10\,\mathrm{kg}$, and it is the same on the Moon. The **weight** there is $w = mg_{\text{Moon}} = 10\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 1.6\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 16\,\mathrm{N}$.
