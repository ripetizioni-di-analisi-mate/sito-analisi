An elevator is moving upward and slowing down. How does the reading of a scale on its floor, with a person standing on it, compare with the weight of the person?

- The reading is larger than the weight, because the elevator moves upward.
- The reading equals the weight.
- The reading is zero.
- The reading is smaller than the weight.

## Explanation

The elevator moves upward and slows down, so its acceleration points downward. By the law of **apparent weight** $N = m(g+a)$ with $a < 0$, therefore $N < mg$. The reading depends on the sense of the acceleration and not on the sense of the velocity.
