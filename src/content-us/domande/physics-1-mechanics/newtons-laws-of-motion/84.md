A block of mass $3.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ slides down a frictionless plane inclined at $20^\circ$. What is the magnitude of its acceleration?

- $3.36\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $9.22\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $1.12\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $10.1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

For a **block on a frictionless incline** $a = g\sin\theta$, independent of the mass:
$$
a = 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot \sin 20^\circ = 9.81 \cdot 0.342\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 3.36\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
The cosine gives the component perpendicular to the plane, and $g\cos 20^\circ = 9.22\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
