In which situation does a scale on the floor of an elevator, with a person standing on it, read zero?

- When the elevator moves downward at constant speed
- When the elevator is momentarily at rest at the top of its motion
- When the elevator has the downward acceleration $g$
- When the elevator has the upward acceleration $g$

## Explanation

By the law of **apparent weight** the reading is $N = m(g+a)$, with $a$ counted positive upward. The reading vanishes for $a = -g$, which is free fall, the state of apparent weightlessness. An instant at rest does not make the reading zero, since the reading depends on the acceleration.
