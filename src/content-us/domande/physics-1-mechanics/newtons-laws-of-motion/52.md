An elevator has an upward acceleration equal to $\dfrac{g}{2}$. How many times the weight of a passenger is the reading of the scale on which the passenger stands?

- $0.5$
- $1.5$
- $2.0$
- $1.0$

## Explanation

By the law of **apparent weight** $N = m(g+a) = m\left(g + \dfrac{g}{2}\right) = 1.5\,mg$. The reading is therefore one and a half times the weight.
