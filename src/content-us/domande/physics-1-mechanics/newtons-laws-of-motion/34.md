The Earth pulls an apple of mass $0.20\,\mathrm{kg}$ with a force of $1.96\,\mathrm{N}$. What force does the apple exert on the Earth?

- $1.96\,\mathrm{N}$, directed upward
- $1.96\,\mathrm{N}$, directed downward
- $0.20\,\mathrm{N}$, directed upward
- Zero, because the Earth is much more massive

## Explanation

By the **third law** the apple pulls the Earth with a force of the same magnitude and opposite sense, hence $1.96\,\mathrm{N}$ directed upward, toward the apple. The large mass of the Earth makes its acceleration negligible, but the force is not small.
