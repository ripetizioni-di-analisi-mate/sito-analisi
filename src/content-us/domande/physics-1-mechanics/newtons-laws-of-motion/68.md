A block of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ lies on a frictionless horizontal floor and is pulled by a rope at $30^\circ$ above the horizontal. For what magnitude of the pull does the block leave the floor?

- $22.7\,\mathrm{N}$
- $39.2\,\mathrm{N}$
- $19.6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $9.81\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The vertical equation gives $N = mg - F\sin\theta$, and the block leaves the floor when $N = 0$. Hence
$$
F = \frac{mg}{\sin\theta} = \frac{19.6\,\mathrm{N}}{0.500} = 39.2\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
