A net force of $15\,\mathrm{N}$ acts on a body of mass $4.0\,\mathrm{kg}$. What is the magnitude of its acceleration?

- $60\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0.27\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $11\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

By **Newton's second law** the acceleration is the net force divided by the mass:
$$
a = \frac{F_{\text{net}}}{m} = \frac{15\,\mathrm{N}}{4.0\,\mathrm{kg}} = 3.75\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \approx 3.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
