A table pushes a book upward with the normal force. Which force is the reaction to this normal force?

- The downward push of the book on the table
- The weight of the book
- The upward pull of the book on the Earth
- The downward push of the table on the floor

## Explanation

The normal force is exerted by the table on the book, so by the **third law** its partner is the force exerted by the book on the table, a contact force directed downward. The weight acts on the book itself and is exerted by the Earth, therefore it is not the partner.
