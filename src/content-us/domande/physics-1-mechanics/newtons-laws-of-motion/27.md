A body moves along the $x$ axis, so $a_y = 0$. What does the equation of the second law along the $y$ axis give?

- The $y$ component of each force is zero.
- The sum of the $y$ components of the forces is zero.
- The normal force always equals the weight.
- The net force equals the weight.

## Explanation

By the **second law in components**, $\sum F_y = m a_y$, and $a_y = 0$ gives $\sum F_y = 0$. Individual forces can have nonzero $y$ components, provided that they cancel. The normal force equals the weight only when no other force has a vertical component.
