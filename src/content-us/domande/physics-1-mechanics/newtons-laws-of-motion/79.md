In an Atwood machine the acceleration is $\dfrac{g}{3}$. What is the ratio $\dfrac{m_1}{m_2}$ of the heavier mass to the lighter one?

- $3.0$
- $1.5$
- $4.0$
- $2.0$

## Explanation

From $\dfrac{m_1 - m_2}{m_1 + m_2} = \dfrac{1}{3}$ one gets $3m_1 - 3m_2 = m_1 + m_2$, hence $m_1 = 2m_2$. With a ratio of $3.0$ the acceleration would be $\dfrac{g}{2}$.
