A person holds a load of mass $20\,\mathrm{kg}$ at rest with a rope that passes over a fixed ideal pulley attached to the ceiling, both parts of the rope being vertical. What force does the rope exert on the pulley?

- $392\,\mathrm{N}$
- $196\,\mathrm{N}$
- $98.1\,\mathrm{N}$
- $785\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The load is at rest, so the tension is $T = mg = 196\,\mathrm{N}$, and the **ideal pulley** leaves it unchanged on the other side. The two parts of the rope both pull the pulley downward with $T$, so the total force on it is $2T = 392\,\mathrm{N}$.
