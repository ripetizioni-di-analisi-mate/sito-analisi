A particle moves counterclockwise along a circle centered at the origin of the $xy$ plane. In which direction does its velocity point when the particle crosses the positive $y$ axis?

- Along $+y$
- Along $+x$
- Along $-x$
- Along $-y$

## Explanation

The velocity is tangent to the circle, and at the point $(0, R)$ the tangent is parallel to the $x$ axis. Moving counterclockwise, the particle goes from the first quadrant to the second one, where $x < 0$, therefore the velocity points along $-x$.
