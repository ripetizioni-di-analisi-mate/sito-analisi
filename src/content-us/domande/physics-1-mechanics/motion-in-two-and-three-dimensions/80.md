A point on a circle of radius $0.20\,\mathrm{m}$ has, at a certain instant, angular velocity $4.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ and angular acceleration $20\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$. What is the magnitude of its acceleration at that instant?

- $5.1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $7.2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0.80\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3.2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The radial component is $\omega^2 R = 16 \cdot 0.20\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 3.2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and the tangential one is $R\alpha = 0.20 \cdot 20\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 4.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. They are perpendicular, hence $a = \sqrt{3.2^2 + 4.0^2}\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 5.1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
