A car takes a bend at the constant speed of $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Which statement is correct?

- Its velocity is constant, because its speed is constant.
- Its velocity changes, because its direction changes.
- Its velocity changes only if the bend is not an arc of a circle.
- Its speed changes, because its direction changes.

## Explanation

The velocity is tangent to the path, and the tangent turns along a bend, therefore the velocity vector changes even though its magnitude stays $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The speed is the magnitude alone, and it is constant by hypothesis.
