A small stone tied to a string moves on a circle of radius $0.40\,\mathrm{m}$ and completes one revolution every $0.25\,\mathrm{s}$ at constant speed. What is its speed?

- $10.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $20.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.60\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5.03\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

In one period the stone covers the circumference $2\pi R$ at constant speed. Hence $v = \dfrac{2\pi R}{T} = \dfrac{2\pi \cdot 0.40}{0.25}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 10.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
