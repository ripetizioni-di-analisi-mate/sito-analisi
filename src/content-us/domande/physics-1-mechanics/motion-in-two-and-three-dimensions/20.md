A ball is thrown vertically upwards. Air resistance is negligible. What is its acceleration at the highest point?

- Zero
- $9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ upwards
- $9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ downwards
- $4.91\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ downwards

## Explanation

At the highest point the velocity is zero, and yet it is still changing, since it passes from upwards to downwards. The only force is the weight, therefore the acceleration is $g = 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ downwards at that instant as at every other one.
