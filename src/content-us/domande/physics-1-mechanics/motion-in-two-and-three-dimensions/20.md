A ball is thrown vertically upwards. Air resistance is negligible. What is its acceleration at the highest point?

- Zero
- $9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ upwards
- $9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ downwards
- $4.91\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ downwards

## Explanation

At the highest point the velocity is zero, but the velocity is still changing: it passes from upwards to downwards. The only force is the weight, therefore the acceleration is $g = 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ downwards at that instant as at every other one.
