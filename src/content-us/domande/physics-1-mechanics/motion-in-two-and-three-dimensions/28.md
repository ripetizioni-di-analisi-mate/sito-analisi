From the same height and at the same instant, a coin is dropped from rest and a second coin is thrown horizontally at $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Air resistance is negligible. Which coin reaches the horizontal floor first?

- They reach the floor at the same instant.
- The coin that is dropped
- The answer depends on the height.
- The coin that is thrown

## Explanation

The two coins have the same acceleration $g$ downwards and the same initial vertical velocity, zero. The vertical motion is independent of the horizontal one, therefore the two vertical motions coincide and the coins land together, at different horizontal positions.
