Car $A$ travels east at $25\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and car $B$ travels west at $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$ on the same straight road. What is the velocity of $B$ with respect to $A$?

- $45\,\mathrm{m/s}$ east
- $45\,\mathrm{m/s}$ west
- $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ east
- $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ west

## Explanation

The velocity of $B$ with respect to $A$ is $\vec{v}_{BG} - \vec{v}_{AG}$. With east positive it equals $(-20 - 25)\,\mathrm{m/s} = -45\,\mathrm{m/s}$, that is, $45\,\mathrm{m/s}$ west.
