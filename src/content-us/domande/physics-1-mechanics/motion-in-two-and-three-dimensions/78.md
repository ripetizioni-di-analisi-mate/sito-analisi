A car starts from rest on a circular track and speeds up with constant tangential acceleration. What is the direction of its acceleration at the instant of the start?

- There is no direction, since the acceleration is zero.
- At $45^\circ$ to the radius
- Toward the center of the track
- Tangent to the track

## Explanation

The radial component is $\dfrac{v^2}{R}$, which vanishes when $v = 0$. At the start only the tangential component is left, and it is not zero, therefore the acceleration is tangent to the track.
