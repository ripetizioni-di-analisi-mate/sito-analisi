The velocity of a particle is $\vec{v} = (-3.0\,\hat{\imath} + 4.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is its speed?

- $7.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The speed is the magnitude of the velocity, $v = \sqrt{v_x^2 + v_y^2} = \sqrt{9.0 + 16}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The sign of a component disappears in the square, and a speed is never negative.
