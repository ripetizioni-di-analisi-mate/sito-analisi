The position of a particle is $\vec{r}(t) = (t^3)\,\hat{\imath} + (3t)\,\hat{\jmath}$, with $t$ in seconds and the components in meters. What is its speed at $t = 2.0\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $15.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $12.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6.7\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Differentiating each component gives $\vec{v}(t) = (3t^2)\,\hat{\imath} + 3\,\hat{\jmath}$, hence $\vec{v}(2.0\,\mathrm{s}) = (12\,\hat{\imath} + 3.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The speed is the magnitude of this vector, $\sqrt{144 + 9.0}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 12.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
