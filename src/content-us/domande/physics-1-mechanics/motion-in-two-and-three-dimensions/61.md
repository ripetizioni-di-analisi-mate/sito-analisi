A ball launched from level ground stays in the air for $3.0\,\mathrm{s}$ before landing on the same level. Air resistance is negligible. How high does it rise?

- $11.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $22.1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $44.1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $14.7\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The vertical motion is symmetric, so the ball reaches the top at $t_H = 1.5\,\mathrm{s}$ and falls from rest for the remaining $1.5\,\mathrm{s}$. The height is therefore $\dfrac{1}{2} g t_H^2 = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 9.81 \cdot 1.5^2\,\mathrm{m} = 11.0\,\mathrm{m}$, whatever the horizontal component.
