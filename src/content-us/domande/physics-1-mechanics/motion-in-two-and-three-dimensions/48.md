A ball is launched at $16\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at $50^\circ$ above the horizontal. Air resistance is negligible. How high above the launch level does it rise?

- $7.66\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5.39\,\mathrm{m}$
- $13.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $15.3\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The maximum height depends on the vertical component of the initial velocity alone, $v_0\sin\theta_0 = 12.26\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Hence $H = \dfrac{v_0^2\sin^2\theta_0}{2g} = \dfrac{(12.26)^2}{2 \cdot 9.81}\,\mathrm{m} = 7.66\,\mathrm{m}$.
