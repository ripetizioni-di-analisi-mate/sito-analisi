The position of a particle is $\vec{r}(t) = (5.0 - 2.0\,t)\,\hat{\imath} + 3.0\,\hat{\jmath}$, with $t$ in seconds and the components in meters. Which description of the motion is correct?

- Rest at the point $(5.0, 3.0)\,\mathrm{m}$
- Motion with constant acceleration $-2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ along $x$
- Uniform motion along a straight line, in the $-x$ sense
- Uniform motion along a straight line, in the $+x$ sense

## Explanation

Differentiating gives $\vec{v} = -2.0\,\hat{\imath}\ \mathrm{m/s}$, a constant vector, hence the acceleration is zero. The velocity has constant direction and magnitude $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and its component along $x$ is negative, therefore the particle moves uniformly along the line $y = 3.0\,\mathrm{m}$ in the $-x$ sense.
