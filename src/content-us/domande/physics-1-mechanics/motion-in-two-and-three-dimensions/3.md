A particle moves along half of a circle of radius $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$, from one end of a diameter to the other end. What are the magnitude of its displacement and the length of its path?

- $6.28\,\mathrm{m}$ and $6.28\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{m}$ and $6.28\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{m}$ and $12.6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ and $6.28\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The two endpoints are the ends of a diameter, therefore the displacement has magnitude $2R = 4.0\,\mathrm{m}$. The path is half the circle, whose length is $\pi R = 6.28\,\mathrm{m}$.
