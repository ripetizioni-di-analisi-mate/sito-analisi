A dart is thrown horizontally at $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ toward the center of a board $3.0\,\mathrm{m}$ away. Air resistance is negligible. How far below the center does it hit the board?

- $0.31\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.61\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The horizontal motion is uniform, so the dart reaches the board after $t = \dfrac{3.0\,\mathrm{m}}{12\,\mathrm{m/s}} = 0.25\,\mathrm{s}$. In that time the vertical motion, which starts from rest, gives a drop $\dfrac{1}{2} g t^2 = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 9.81 \cdot 0.0625\,\mathrm{m} = 0.31\,\mathrm{m}$.
