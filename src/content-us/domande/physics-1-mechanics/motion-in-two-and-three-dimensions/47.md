A ball leaves a horizontal ledge $7.2\,\mathrm{m}$ high with a horizontal velocity of $8.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Air resistance is negligible. What angle does its velocity make with the horizontal just before it reaches the ground below?

- $36.6^\circ$
- $56.1^\circ$
- $33.9^\circ$
- $46.4^\circ$

## Explanation

The vertical component on landing is $v_y = \sqrt{2gh} = \sqrt{2 \cdot 9.81 \cdot 7.2}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 11.9\,\mathrm{m/s}$ downwards, and the horizontal one is still $8.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The angle below the horizontal satisfies $\tan\beta = \dfrac{11.9}{8.0}$, hence $\beta = 56.1^\circ$.
