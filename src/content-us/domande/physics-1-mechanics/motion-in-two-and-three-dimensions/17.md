The position of a particle is $\vec{r}(t) = (t^3)\,\hat{\imath} + (2t^2)\,\hat{\jmath}$, with $t$ in seconds and the components in meters. What is its acceleration at $t = 1.0\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $(3.0\,\hat{\imath} + 4.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $(1.0\,\hat{\imath} + 2.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $(3.0\,\hat{\imath} + 2.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $(6.0\,\hat{\imath} + 4.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Differentiating twice component by component gives $\vec{v}(t) = (3t^2)\,\hat{\imath} + (4t)\,\hat{\jmath}$ and then $\vec{a}(t) = (6t)\,\hat{\imath} + 4\,\hat{\jmath}$. At $t = 1.0\,\mathrm{s}$ the acceleration is $(6.0\,\hat{\imath} + 4.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
