A cyclist rides at the constant speed of $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$ along a circular track of radius $45\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the magnitude of her acceleration?

- $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0.33\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0$

## Explanation

The speed is constant, so the acceleration is the centripetal one alone. Its magnitude is $\dfrac{v^2}{R} = \dfrac{225}{45}\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 5.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, directed towards the center of the track.
