At a certain instant a particle in circular motion has radial acceleration $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and tangential acceleration $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the magnitude of its acceleration?

- $7.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $25\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $1.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The radial and the tangential components are perpendicular, hence the magnitude is $\sqrt{a_{\mathrm{rad}}^2 + a_{\mathrm{tan}}^2} = \sqrt{9.0 + 16}\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 5.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
