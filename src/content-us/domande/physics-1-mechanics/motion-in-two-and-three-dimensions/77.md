A car on a circular track of radius $60\,\mathrm{m}$ has speed $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and is slowing down at the rate of $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the magnitude of its acceleration?

- $3.9\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0.90\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The radial component is $\dfrac{v^2}{R} = \dfrac{144}{60}\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 2.4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and the tangential one has magnitude $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. They are perpendicular, whatever the sign of the tangential component, hence $a = \sqrt{2.4^2 + 1.5^2}\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 2.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
