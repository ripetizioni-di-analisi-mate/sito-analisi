Frame $B$ moves with constant velocity with respect to frame $A$. In frame $A$ a particle has acceleration $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ east. What is its acceleration in frame $B$?

- It depends on the speed of frame $B$.
- $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ west
- $0$
- $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ east

## Explanation

Differentiating $\vec{v}_{PA} = \vec{v}_{PB} + \vec{v}_{BA}$ gives $\vec{a}_{PA} = \vec{a}_{PB} + \dfrac{d\vec{v}_{BA}}{dt}$. The relative velocity of the frames is constant, so the last term vanishes and the two accelerations are equal.
