A ball is launched at $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at $40^\circ$ above the horizontal. Air resistance is negligible. What is the vertical component of its velocity $1.0\,\mathrm{s}$ after the launch, taking upwards as positive?

- $29.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $14.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $13.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $9.47\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The vertical motion has initial velocity $v_0\sin\theta_0 = 30\,\mathrm{m/s} \cdot \sin 40^\circ = 19.28\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and acceleration $-g$. Hence $v_y = 19.28\,\mathrm{m/s} - 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot 1.0\,\mathrm{s} = 9.47\,\mathrm{m/s}$, still positive, so the ball is rising.
