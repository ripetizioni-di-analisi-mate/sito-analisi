The position of a particle is $\vec{r}(t) = (4t)\,\hat{\imath} + (t^2)\,\hat{\jmath}$, with $t$ in seconds and the components in meters. What is its average velocity between $t = 0$ and $t = 2.0\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $(8.0\,\hat{\imath} + 4.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $(4.0\,\hat{\imath} + 2.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $(4.0\,\hat{\imath})\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $(4.0\,\hat{\imath} + 4.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The displacement is $\vec{r}(2.0\,\mathrm{s}) - \vec{r}(0) = (8.0\,\hat{\imath} + 4.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m}$, and the elapsed time is $2.0\,\mathrm{s}$. The average velocity is their ratio, $(4.0\,\hat{\imath} + 2.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s}$, which differs from the instantaneous velocities at both endpoints.
