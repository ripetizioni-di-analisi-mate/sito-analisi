A turntable rotates uniformly at $3.0$ revolutions per second. What is its angular velocity?

- $0.48\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $18.8\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $9.4\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $3.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

One revolution corresponds to $2\pi$ radians, therefore $\omega = 2\pi f = 2\pi \cdot 3.0\,\mathrm{rad/s} = 18.8\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
