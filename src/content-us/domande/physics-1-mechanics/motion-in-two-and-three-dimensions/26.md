A particle has initial velocity $\vec{v}_0 = (3.0\,\hat{\imath} + 2.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and constant acceleration $\vec{a} = (1.0\,\hat{\imath} - 2.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is its velocity at $t = 2.0\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $(4.0\,\hat{\imath})\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $(1.0\,\hat{\imath} + 6.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $(5.0\,\hat{\imath} - 2.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $(5.0\,\hat{\imath} + 6.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

With constant acceleration $\vec{v} = \vec{v}_0 + \vec{a}\,t$, read on each axis: $v_x = (3.0 + 1.0 \cdot 2.0)\,\mathrm{m/s} = 5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_y = (2.0 - 2.0 \cdot 2.0)\,\mathrm{m/s} = -2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
