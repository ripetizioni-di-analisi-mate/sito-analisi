A train moves east at $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$ with respect to the ground, and a passenger walks towards the rear of the train at $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ with respect to the train. What is the velocity of the passenger with respect to the ground?

- $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ west
- $21.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ east
- $18.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ east
- $18.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ west

## Explanation

The addition of velocities gives $\vec{v}_{PG} = \vec{v}_{PT} + \vec{v}_{TG}$, where the passenger's velocity with respect to the train points west. With east positive, $v_{PG} = (-1.5 + 20)\,\mathrm{m/s} = 18.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$, directed east.
