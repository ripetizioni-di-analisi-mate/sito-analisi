The origin of frame $B$ is at $(4.0\,\hat{\imath})\,\mathrm{m}$ with respect to frame $A$, and the axes of the two frames are parallel. A particle is at $(1.0\,\hat{\imath} + 2.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m}$ with respect to $B$. What is its position with respect to $A$?

- $(-3.0\,\hat{\imath} + 2.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m}$
- $(1.0\,\hat{\imath} + 2.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m}$
- $(5.0\,\hat{\imath} + 2.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m}$
- $(3.0\,\hat{\imath} - 2.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The position vectors satisfy $\vec{r}_{PA} = \vec{r}_{PB} + \vec{r}_{BA}$. Adding $(1.0\,\hat{\imath} + 2.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m}$ and $(4.0\,\hat{\imath})\,\mathrm{m}$ gives $(5.0\,\hat{\imath} + 2.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m}$.
