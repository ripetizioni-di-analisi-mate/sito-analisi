The angular velocity of a wheel grows uniformly from $0$ to $12\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ in $3.0\,\mathrm{s}$. What is its angular acceleration?

- $0.25\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $36\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$

## Explanation

The angular velocity changes at a constant rate, so $\alpha = \dfrac{\Delta\omega}{\Delta t} = \dfrac{12}{3.0}\,\mathrm{rad/s^2} = 4.0\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$.
