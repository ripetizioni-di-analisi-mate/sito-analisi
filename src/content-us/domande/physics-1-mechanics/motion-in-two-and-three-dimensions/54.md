A ball thrown straight up with speed $v_0$ rises to the height $H$. Air resistance is negligible. To what height does it rise when it is launched with the same speed at $30^\circ$ above the horizontal?

- $0.75\,H$
- $0.50\,H$
- $0.25\,H$
- $0.87\,H$

## Explanation

The maximum height is $\dfrac{v_0^2\sin^2\theta_0}{2g}$, which equals $H$ for $\theta_0 = 90^\circ$. At $30^\circ$ it is $H\sin^2 30^\circ = 0.25\,H$, because only the vertical component $v_0\sin 30^\circ = 0.5\,v_0$ counts and the height goes as its square.
