A passenger in a train moving at constant velocity on a straight track drops a ball from rest with respect to the train. Air resistance is negligible. What is the path of the ball seen from the ground?

- An arc of a circle
- A parabola
- A straight line inclined toward the rear of the train
- A vertical straight line

## Explanation

In the frame of the train the ball starts from rest and falls along a vertical line. With respect to the ground its initial velocity is the horizontal velocity of the train, so it is a projectile launched horizontally and its path is a parabola.
