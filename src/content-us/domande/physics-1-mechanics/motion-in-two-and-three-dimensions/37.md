A projectile is launched at $40^\circ$ above the horizontal. Air resistance is negligible. What angle does its velocity make with its acceleration at the highest point of the path?

- $90^\circ$
- $0^\circ$
- $180^\circ$
- $40^\circ$

## Explanation

At the highest point $v_y = 0$, so the velocity is horizontal. The acceleration is $g$ vertically downwards at every point, therefore at that point the two vectors are perpendicular.
