Two balls are launched from level ground with the same speed, one at $30^\circ$ and one at $60^\circ$ above the horizontal, and land on the same level. Air resistance is negligible. Which statement is correct?

- They reach the same maximum height.
- The ball launched at $60^\circ$ has the greater range.
- They have the same range, and the ball launched at $60^\circ$ stays longer in the air.
- They have the same range and the same time of flight.

## Explanation

The range contains the angle through $\sin 2\theta_0$, and $\sin 60^\circ = \sin 120^\circ$, so the two complementary angles give the same range. The time of flight $\dfrac{2\,v_0\sin\theta_0}{g}$ and the height $\dfrac{v_0^2\sin^2\theta_0}{2g}$ contain $\sin\theta_0$, which is larger at $60^\circ$.
