A particle moves with constant acceleration, and its initial velocity is neither zero nor parallel to the acceleration. What is its path?

- A straight line along the acceleration
- A straight line along the initial velocity
- A circle
- A parabola

## Explanation

Choosing the $y$ axis along the acceleration, the motion along $x$ is uniform with $v_{0x} \ne 0$ and the motion along $y$ has constant acceleration. Eliminating the time between $x$ and $y$ gives $y$ as a polynomial of degree two in $x$, which is a parabola, as for a projectile.
