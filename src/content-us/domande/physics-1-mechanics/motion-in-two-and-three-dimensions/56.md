A ball is thrown from the top of a cliff $15\,\mathrm{m}$ high, with an upward vertical component of the velocity of $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and lands at the base of the cliff. Air resistance is negligible. What is its time of flight?

- $3.04\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.02\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.75\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2.04\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

With the origin at the launch point the landing is at $y = -15\,\mathrm{m}$, hence $-15 = 10\,t - 4.905\,t^2$ in SI units. The positive root of $4.905\,t^2 - 10\,t - 15 = 0$ is $t = \dfrac{10 + \sqrt{100 + 294.3}}{9.81}\,\mathrm{s} = 3.04\,\mathrm{s}$; the formula for level ground would give only the $2.04\,\mathrm{s}$ needed to return to the launch level.
