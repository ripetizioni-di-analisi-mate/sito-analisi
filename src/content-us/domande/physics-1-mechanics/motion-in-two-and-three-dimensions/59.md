A ball is launched from level ground at $18\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at $35^\circ$ above the horizontal. Air resistance is negligible. What is its speed just before it lands on the same level?

- $18.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $14.7\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $28.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The horizontal component keeps its value $v_0\cos\theta_0$. Substituting $T = \dfrac{2\,v_0\sin\theta_0}{g}$ into $v_y = v_0\sin\theta_0 - g t$ gives $v_y = -v_0\sin\theta_0$, so the vertical component has the initial magnitude and the opposite sense, and the speed is again $18.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
