The position of a particle is $\vec{r}(t) = 3.0\,\hat{\imath} + (2.0\,t^2)\,\hat{\jmath} + 5.0\,\hat{k}$, with $t$ in seconds and the components in meters. What is its path?

- A straight line through the origin
- A parabola in the $xy$ plane
- A parabola in the $yz$ plane
- A straight line parallel to the $y$ axis

## Explanation

The coordinates $x = 3.0\,\mathrm{m}$ and $z = 5.0\,\mathrm{m}$ do not depend on time, and only $y$ changes. Two components stay constant, therefore the particle moves along the straight line through $(3.0, 0, 5.0)\,\mathrm{m}$ parallel to the $y$ axis; the quadratic dependence on $t$ concerns the time schedule along that line, not the shape of the path.
