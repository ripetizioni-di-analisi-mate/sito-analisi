The velocity of a particle changes from $(2.0\,\hat{\imath} + 3.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $(6.0\,\hat{\imath} - 1.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in $4.0\,\mathrm{s}$. What is its average acceleration?

- $(4.0\,\hat{\imath} - 4.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $(1.0\,\hat{\imath} - 1.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $(-1.0\,\hat{\imath} + 1.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $(2.0\,\hat{\imath} + 0.50\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The change of velocity is the final velocity minus the initial one, $\Delta\vec{v} = (4.0\,\hat{\imath} - 4.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Dividing by $4.0\,\mathrm{s}$ gives $\vec{a}_{\mathrm{av}} = (1.0\,\hat{\imath} - 1.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
