The position of a particle is $\vec{r}(t) = (2t)\,\hat{\imath} + (t^2)\,\hat{\jmath}$, with $t$ in seconds and the components in meters. What is its displacement between $t = 1.0\,\mathrm{s}$ and $t = 3.0\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $(2.0\,\hat{\imath} + 4.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m}$
- $(8.0\,\hat{\imath} + 10\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m}$
- $(4.0\,\hat{\imath} + 8.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m}$
- $(6.0\,\hat{\imath} + 9.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The positions at the two instants are $\vec{r}(3.0\,\mathrm{s}) = (6.0\,\hat{\imath} + 9.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m}$ and $\vec{r}(1.0\,\mathrm{s}) = (2.0\,\hat{\imath} + 1.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m}$. The displacement is their difference, $(4.0\,\hat{\imath} + 8.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m}$.
