A wheel of radius $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$ has angular acceleration $6.0\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$. What is the tangential acceleration of a point on its rim?

- $0.050\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $1.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $10.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Differentiating $v = R\omega$ with $R$ constant gives $a_{\mathrm{tan}} = R\alpha = 0.30 \cdot 6.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 1.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
