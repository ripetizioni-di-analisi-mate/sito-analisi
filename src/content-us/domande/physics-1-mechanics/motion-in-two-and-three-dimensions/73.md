A record turns uniformly at $45$ revolutions per minute. What is the speed of a point at $0.15\,\mathrm{m}$ from its center?

- $0.11\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $42\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.71\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The frequency is $\dfrac{45}{60}\,\mathrm{s^{-1}} = 0.75\,\mathrm{Hz}$, so $\omega = 2\pi f = 4.71\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. The speed is $\omega r = 4.71 \cdot 0.15\,\mathrm{m/s} = 0.71\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
