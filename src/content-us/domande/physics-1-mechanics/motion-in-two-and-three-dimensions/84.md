The velocity of car $B$ with respect to car $A$ is $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ north. What is the velocity of car $A$ with respect to car $B$?

- $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ south
- $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ north
- $0$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ south

## Explanation

Reversing the order of the labels reverses the vector, since $\vec{v}_{AB} = -\,\vec{v}_{BA}$. The velocity of $A$ with respect to $B$ is therefore $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ south.
