A particle goes once round a circle of radius $3.0\,\mathrm{m}$ and stops at its starting point. What are the magnitude of its displacement and the length of its path?

- $18.8\,\mathrm{m}$ and $18.8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6.0\,\mathrm{m}$ and $18.8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0$ and $9.42\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0$ and $18.8\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The displacement depends on the two endpoints alone, and here they coincide, therefore $\Delta\vec{r} = \vec{0}$. The path is the whole circle, whose length is $2\pi R = 2\pi \cdot 3.0\,\mathrm{m} = 18.8\,\mathrm{m}$.
