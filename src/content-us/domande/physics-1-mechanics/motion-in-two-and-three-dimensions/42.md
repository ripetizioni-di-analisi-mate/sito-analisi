A ball is launched from ground level at $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at $45^\circ$ above the horizontal. Air resistance is negligible. How high above the ground is it when it has advanced $10\,\mathrm{m}$ horizontally?

- $6.92\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5.64\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.28\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10.0\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The equation of the path gives $y = x\tan\theta_0 - \dfrac{g}{2\,v_0^2\cos^2\theta_0}\,x^2$, with no need to find the time. Substituting, $y = 10\,\mathrm{m} - \dfrac{9.81 \cdot 100}{2 \cdot 225 \cdot 0.5}\,\mathrm{m} = (10 - 4.36)\,\mathrm{m} = 5.64\,\mathrm{m}$.
