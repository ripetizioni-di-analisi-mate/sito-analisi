At an instant at which the velocity of a particle is not zero, what is the direction of the instantaneous velocity?

- Along the position vector
- Tangent to the path, in the sense of the motion
- Along the chord from the starting point to the current position
- Perpendicular to the path

## Explanation

The average velocity is parallel to the chord joining two positions, and as the time interval shrinks that chord tends to the tangent line. The instantaneous velocity is therefore tangent to the path and points in the sense in which the particle advances.
