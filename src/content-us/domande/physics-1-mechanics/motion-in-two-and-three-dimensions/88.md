An airplane whose speed with respect to the air is $120\,\mathrm{km/h}$ heads due north, while a wind blows toward the east at $90\,\mathrm{km/h}$. What is the speed of the airplane with respect to the ground?

- $79\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $30\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $210\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $150\,\mathrm{km/h}$

## Explanation

The velocity with respect to the ground is the velocity with respect to the air plus the velocity of the air with respect to the ground. The two vectors are perpendicular, hence the ground speed is $\sqrt{120^2 + 90^2}\,\mathrm{km/h} = 150\,\mathrm{km/h}$.
