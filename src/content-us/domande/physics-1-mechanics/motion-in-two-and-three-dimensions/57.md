A ball is launched at $24.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at $30^\circ$ above the horizontal. Air resistance is negligible. How long after the launch does it reach the highest point?

- $2.16\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.25\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2.50\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.62\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The highest point is where $v_y = v_0\sin\theta_0 - g t$ vanishes, hence $t_H = \dfrac{v_0\sin\theta_0}{g} = \dfrac{24.5 \cdot 0.5}{9.81}\,\mathrm{s} = 1.25\,\mathrm{s}$.
