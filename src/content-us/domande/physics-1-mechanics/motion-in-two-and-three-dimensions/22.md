The acceleration of a particle makes an angle of $120^\circ$ with its velocity. Which statement is correct?

- The speed is decreasing and the path is bending.
- The speed is decreasing and the path is straight.
- The speed is increasing and the path is bending.
- The speed is constant and the path is bending.

## Explanation

The component of the acceleration along the velocity is $a\cos 120^\circ < 0$, therefore the speed is decreasing. The component perpendicular to the velocity is $a\sin 120^\circ \ne 0$, therefore the direction of the motion is changing and the path bends.
