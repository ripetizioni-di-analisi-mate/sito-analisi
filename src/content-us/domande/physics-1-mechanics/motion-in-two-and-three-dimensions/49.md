A ball is launched from level ground at $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at $60^\circ$ above the horizontal and lands on the same level. Air resistance is negligible. What is its time of flight?

- $2.65\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.53\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.32\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3.06\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Launch and landing are at the same level, therefore $T = \dfrac{2\,v_0\sin\theta_0}{g}$ applies. Substituting, $T = \dfrac{2 \cdot 15 \cdot \sin 60^\circ}{9.81}\,\mathrm{s} = 2.65\,\mathrm{s}$, twice the time needed to reach the highest point.
