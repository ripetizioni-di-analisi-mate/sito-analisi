Which statement describes the acceleration of a projectile, with air resistance neglected?

- It is directed upwards while the projectile rises.
- It is zero at the highest point.
- It is $g$ downwards at every point of the path.
- It is tangent to the path at every point.

## Explanation

After the launch the weight is the only force, hence the acceleration is constant and equal to $g$ downwards, on the way up, at the top and on the way down. It is tangent to the path only at the instants at which the velocity is vertical, which do not occur for an oblique launch.
