Rain falls vertically at $8.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ with respect to the ground, and a car moves horizontally at $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the speed of the rain with respect to the car?

- $14\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The velocity of the rain with respect to the car is $\vec{v}_{RC} = \vec{v}_{RG} - \vec{v}_{CG}$, the sum of a vertical vector of $8.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and a horizontal vector of $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ pointing backwards. The two are perpendicular, therefore its magnitude is $\sqrt{64 + 36}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 10\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
