A ball is launched at $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at $30^\circ$ above the horizontal. Air resistance is negligible. What is the horizontal component of its velocity $1.5\,\mathrm{s}$ after the launch?

- $8.66\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $20.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $17.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The only acceleration is $g$ downwards, hence $a_x = 0$ and the horizontal component never changes. It equals $v_0\cos\theta_0 = 20\,\mathrm{m/s} \cdot \cos 30^\circ = 17.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at every instant of the flight.
