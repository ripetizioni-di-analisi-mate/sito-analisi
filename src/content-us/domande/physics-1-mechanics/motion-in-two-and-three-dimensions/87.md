A boat whose speed with respect to the water is $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ must cross a river flowing at $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and land at the point directly opposite the start. At what angle from the perpendicular to the banks must it head upstream?

- $59.0^\circ$
- $53.1^\circ$
- $36.9^\circ$
- $31.0^\circ$

## Explanation

The component of the boat's velocity along the banks must cancel the current, hence $5.0\,\sin\theta = 3.0$, with $\theta$ measured from the perpendicular. This gives $\sin\theta = 0.60$ and $\theta = 36.9^\circ$.
