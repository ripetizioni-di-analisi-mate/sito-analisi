The acceleration of a particle has magnitude $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with its velocity. What is the magnitude of the component of the acceleration perpendicular to the velocity?

- $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $1.7\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The component along the velocity is $a\cos 60^\circ = 2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, and the component perpendicular to it is $a\sin 60^\circ = 4.0 \cdot 0.866\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 3.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The angle is measured from the velocity, therefore the perpendicular component carries the sine.
