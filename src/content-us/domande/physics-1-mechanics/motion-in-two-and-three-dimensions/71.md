A particle moves on a circle of radius $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ at the constant speed of $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the magnitude of its average velocity over half a revolution?

- $0$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Half a revolution takes $\dfrac{\pi R}{v} = 1.57\,\mathrm{s}$, and it brings the particle to the opposite end of a diameter, so the displacement has magnitude $2R = 4.0\,\mathrm{m}$. The average velocity has magnitude $\dfrac{4.0\,\mathrm{m}}{1.57\,\mathrm{s}} = 2.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$, smaller than the speed.
