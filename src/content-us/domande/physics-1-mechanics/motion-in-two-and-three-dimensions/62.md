A ball launched from level ground at $45^\circ$ above the horizontal lands $40\,\mathrm{m}$ away on the same level. Air resistance is negligible. What is its maximum height?

- $20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $40\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

At $45^\circ$ the range is $R = \dfrac{v_0^2}{g}$ and the maximum height is $H = \dfrac{v_0^2\sin^2 45^\circ}{2g} = \dfrac{v_0^2}{4g}$. Hence $H = \dfrac{R}{4} = 10\,\mathrm{m}$.
