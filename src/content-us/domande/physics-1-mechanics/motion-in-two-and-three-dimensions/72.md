A rider of a rotating amusement ride moves on a horizontal circle of radius $10\,\mathrm{m}$ and has a centripetal acceleration of $9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the rider's speed?

- $9.90\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $14.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.981\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $98.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

From $a_{\mathrm{rad}} = \dfrac{v^2}{R}$ the speed is $v = \sqrt{a_{\mathrm{rad}} R} = \sqrt{9.81 \cdot 10}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 9.90\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
