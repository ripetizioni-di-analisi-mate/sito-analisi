At a certain instant a particle has velocity $\vec{v} = (3.0\,\hat{\imath} + 4.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and acceleration $\vec{a} = (-2.0\,\hat{\imath} + 1.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the rate of change of its speed $\dfrac{dv}{dt}$ at that instant?

- $-0.40\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2.2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0.40\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The rate of change of the speed is the component of $\vec{a}$ along the velocity, that is, $\dfrac{\vec{a}\cdot\vec{v}}{v}$. The scalar product is $(-6.0 + 4.0)\,\mathrm{m^2/s^3} = -2.0\,\mathrm{m^2/s^3}$ and $v = 5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$, hence $\dfrac{dv}{dt} = -0.40\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and the particle is slowing down.
