A ball is launched at $25\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at $53.0^\circ$ above the horizontal. Air resistance is negligible. What is its speed at the highest point of its path?

- $25.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $20.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0$
- $15.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

At the highest point the vertical component of the velocity vanishes, while the horizontal one keeps its initial value. The speed there is therefore $v_0\cos\theta_0 = 25\,\mathrm{m/s} \cdot \cos 53.0^\circ = 15.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
