Two projectiles are launched from the same point with the same vertical component of the initial velocity and different horizontal components. Air resistance is negligible. Which quantity is the same for both?

- Their speed at the highest point
- Their launch angle
- Their range
- Their maximum height

## Explanation

The vertical motion depends on $v_{0y}$ and $g$ alone, and the maximum height is $\dfrac{v_{0y}^2}{2g}$, hence it is equal for the two projectiles. The range and the speed at the top contain $v_{0x}$, and the launch angle depends on the ratio of the two components.
