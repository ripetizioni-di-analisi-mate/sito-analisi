A ball is launched from level ground at $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at $45^\circ$ above the horizontal and lands on the same level. Air resistance is negligible. What is its range?

- $10.4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $14.7\,\mathrm{m}$
- $7.34\,\mathrm{m}$
- $29.4\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The range on level ground is $R = \dfrac{v_0^2\sin 2\theta_0}{g}$, and $\sin 90^\circ = 1$. Hence $R = \dfrac{144}{9.81}\,\mathrm{m} = 14.7\,\mathrm{m}$.
