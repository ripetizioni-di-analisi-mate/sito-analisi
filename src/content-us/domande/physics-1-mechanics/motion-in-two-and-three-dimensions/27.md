A particle starts at the origin with velocity $\vec{v}_0 = (3.0\,\hat{\imath} + 2.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and moves with constant acceleration $\vec{a} = (1.0\,\hat{\imath} - 2.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is its position at $t = 2.0\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $(8.0\,\hat{\imath})\,\mathrm{m}$
- $(10\,\hat{\imath} - 4.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m}$
- $(7.0\,\hat{\imath} + 2.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m}$
- $(6.0\,\hat{\imath} + 4.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

With constant acceleration $\vec{r} = \vec{v}_0\,t + \dfrac{1}{2}\,\vec{a}\,t^2$. On the axes, $x = (6.0 + 2.0)\,\mathrm{m} = 8.0\,\mathrm{m}$ and $y = (4.0 - 4.0)\,\mathrm{m} = 0$, hence the particle is back on the $x$ axis.
