A puck slides on horizontal ice with initial velocity $(4.0\,\hat{\imath})\,\mathrm{m/s}$ from the origin, and a steady wind gives it the constant acceleration $(2.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is its $x$ coordinate when $y = 4.0\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $5.7\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8.0\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The $y$ motion starts from rest, hence $y = \dfrac{1}{2} a_y t^2 = (1.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2})\,t^2$, and $y = 4.0\,\mathrm{m}$ at $t = 2.0\,\mathrm{s}$. The $x$ motion is uniform, therefore at that instant $x = 4.0\,\mathrm{m/s} \cdot 2.0\,\mathrm{s} = 8.0\,\mathrm{m}$.
