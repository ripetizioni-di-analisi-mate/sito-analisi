A ball rolls off the edge of a horizontal table with a given speed and lands on the floor. Air resistance is negligible. If the table were four times as high, with the same speed at the edge, how would the horizontal distance to the landing point change?

- It would be sixteen times as large.
- It would not change.
- It would double.
- It would be four times as large.

## Explanation

The fall from rest takes $t = \sqrt{\dfrac{2h}{g}}$, and the horizontal distance is $v_0 t = v_0\sqrt{\dfrac{2h}{g}}$. The distance grows with the square root of the height, therefore four times the height gives twice the distance.
