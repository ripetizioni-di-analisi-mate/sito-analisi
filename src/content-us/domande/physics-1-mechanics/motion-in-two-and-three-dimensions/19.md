The component of the acceleration of a particle perpendicular to its velocity is zero at every instant, and the velocity is never zero. What follows?

- The acceleration is zero.
- The path is a straight line.
- The path is a circle.
- The speed is constant.

## Explanation

The perpendicular component $v\,\dfrac{d\hat{u}_T}{dt}$ is the one that changes the direction of the motion. With $v \ne 0$ its vanishing means $\dfrac{d\hat{u}_T}{dt} = \vec{0}$, hence the direction of the velocity is fixed and the path is straight, while the speed may still change.
