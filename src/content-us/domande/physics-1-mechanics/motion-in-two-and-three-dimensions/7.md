The position of a particle is $\vec{r} = (1.0\,\hat{\imath} + 2.0\,\hat{\jmath} + 2.0\,\hat{k})\,\mathrm{m}$. What is its distance from the origin?

- $3.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $9.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5.0\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The distance from the origin is the magnitude of the position vector, computed with all three components: $\sqrt{1.0^2 + 2.0^2 + 2.0^2}\,\mathrm{m} = \sqrt{9.0}\,\mathrm{m} = 3.0\,\mathrm{m}$.
