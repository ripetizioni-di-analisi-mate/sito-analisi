A point moves on a circle of radius $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$ with constant angular velocity $4.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. What is the magnitude of its acceleration?

- $8.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $32\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $1.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

In uniform circular motion the acceleration is centripetal, with magnitude $\omega^2 R = 16 \cdot 0.50\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 8.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The value $\omega R = 2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is the speed, not the acceleration.
