A particle is in uniform circular motion. What is the direction of its acceleration?

- Tangent to the circle, in the sense of the motion
- There is no direction, since the acceleration is zero.
- Away from the center of the circle
- Toward the center of the circle

## Explanation

Differentiating twice the position $R\cos(\omega t)\,\hat{\imath} + R\sin(\omega t)\,\hat{\jmath}$ gives $\vec{a} = -\omega^2\,\vec{r}$, a negative multiple of the position vector measured from the center. The acceleration therefore points from the particle toward the center, with magnitude $\dfrac{v^2}{R}$.
