The component of the acceleration of a particle along its velocity is zero at every instant. What follows?

- The path is a straight line.
- The speed is constant.
- The velocity is constant.
- The acceleration is zero.

## Explanation

The component of the acceleration along the velocity is $\dfrac{dv}{dt}$, hence its vanishing at every instant means that the speed does not change. The direction of the velocity can still change, as in uniform circular motion, so neither the velocity nor the acceleration need be constant.
