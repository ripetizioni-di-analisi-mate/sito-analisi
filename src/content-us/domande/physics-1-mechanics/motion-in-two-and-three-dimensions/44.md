A projectile is launched with horizontal component of the velocity $16\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and vertical component $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ upwards, from the origin. Air resistance is negligible. What are its coordinates $(x, y)$ at $t = 2.0\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $(32\,\mathrm{m},\ 43.6\,\mathrm{m})$
- $(32\,\mathrm{m},\ -15.2\,\mathrm{m})$
- $(32\,\mathrm{m},\ 4.4\,\mathrm{m})$
- $(32\,\mathrm{m},\ 24\,\mathrm{m})$

## Explanation

The horizontal motion is uniform, so $x = 16\,\mathrm{m/s} \cdot 2.0\,\mathrm{s} = 32\,\mathrm{m}$. The vertical one gives $y = v_{0y}t - \dfrac{1}{2}g t^2 = (24 - 19.6)\,\mathrm{m} = 4.4\,\mathrm{m}$.
