Which statement about the displacement of a particle between two instants and the length of the path traveled between them holds for every motion?

- The displacement vanishes only if the particle has stayed at rest.
- The magnitude of the displacement is greater than or equal to the length of the path.
- The magnitude of the displacement is less than or equal to the length of the path.
- The magnitude of the displacement equals the length of the path.

## Explanation

The magnitude of the displacement is the length of the straight segment joining the two positions, and no path between two points is shorter than that segment. The two quantities are equal only for motion along a straight line in one sense, and the displacement vanishes for every return to the starting point, even after a long path.
