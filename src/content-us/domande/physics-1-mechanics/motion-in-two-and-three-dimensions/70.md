A particle moves counterclockwise at constant speed on a circle centered at the origin of the $xy$ plane. What are the directions of its velocity and of its acceleration when it crosses the positive $x$ axis?

- Velocity along $-y$, acceleration along $-x$
- Velocity along $+y$, acceleration along $-x$
- Velocity along $-x$, acceleration along $+y$
- Velocity along $+y$, acceleration along $+x$

## Explanation

At the point $(R, 0)$ the tangent is vertical, and a counterclockwise motion goes from there towards positive $y$, so the velocity is along $+y$. The acceleration points towards the center, which lies along $-x$ from that point.
