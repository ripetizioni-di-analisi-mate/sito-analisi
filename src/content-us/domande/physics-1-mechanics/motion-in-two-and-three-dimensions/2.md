A particle moves from the position $\vec{r}_1 = (2.0\,\hat{\imath} + 1.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m}$ to the position $\vec{r}_2 = (5.0\,\hat{\imath} + 5.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the magnitude of its displacement?

- $5.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $7.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $25\,\mathrm{m}$
- $9.3\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The displacement is the difference of the two position vectors, $\Delta\vec{r} = \vec{r}_2 - \vec{r}_1 = (3.0\,\hat{\imath} + 4.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m}$. Its components are perpendicular, hence its magnitude is $\sqrt{3.0^2 + 4.0^2}\,\mathrm{m} = 5.0\,\mathrm{m}$.
