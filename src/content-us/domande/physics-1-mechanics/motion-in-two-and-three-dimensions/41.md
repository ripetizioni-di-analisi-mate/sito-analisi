A projectile is launched from the origin at $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at $45^\circ$ above the horizontal, with $g = 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Its path has the equation $y = x - k\,x^2$. What is the value of $k$?

- $0.0694\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $0.196\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $0.0491\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $0.0981\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$

## Explanation

The coefficient of $x^2$ in the equation of the path is $\dfrac{g}{2\,v_0^2\cos^2\theta_0}$. With $\cos^2 45^\circ = 0.5$ it equals $\dfrac{9.81}{2 \cdot 100 \cdot 0.5}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}} = 0.0981\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$.
