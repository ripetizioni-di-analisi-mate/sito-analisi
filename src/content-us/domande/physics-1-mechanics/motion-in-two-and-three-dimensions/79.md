A car moves counterclockwise on a circular track centered at the origin of the $xy$ plane and is slowing down. What are the signs of the components of its acceleration when it crosses the positive $x$ axis?

- $a_x > 0$ and $a_y < 0$
- $a_x < 0$ and $a_y > 0$
- $a_x < 0$ and $a_y < 0$
- $a_x = 0$ and $a_y < 0$

## Explanation

At the point $(R, 0)$ the radial component points towards the center, along $-x$, so $a_x < 0$. The velocity is along $+y$ and the car is slowing down, therefore the tangential component is opposite to the velocity and $a_y < 0$.
