A ball launched from level ground at $14\,\mathrm{m/s}$ must land $15\,\mathrm{m}$ away on the same level. Air resistance is negligible. What is the smaller of the two launch angles that achieve this?

- $41.3^\circ$
- $65.7^\circ$
- $48.7^\circ$
- $24.3^\circ$

## Explanation

The range formula gives $\sin 2\theta_0 = \dfrac{R g}{v_0^2} = \dfrac{15 \cdot 9.81}{196} = 0.751$, hence $2\theta_0 = 48.7^\circ$ or $131.3^\circ$. The two launch angles are $24.3^\circ$ and $65.7^\circ$, and the smaller is $24.3^\circ$.
