A disk rotates uniformly about its axis. Point $P$ is at $5.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the axis and point $Q$ at $10\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the ratio $\dfrac{a_Q}{a_P}$ of their centripetal accelerations?

- $2$
- $4$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $1$

## Explanation

All the points of the disk share the same angular velocity $\omega$, and the centripetal acceleration is $\omega^2 r$. At fixed $\omega$ it is proportional to the distance from the axis, therefore the ratio is $\dfrac{10}{5.0} = 2$.
