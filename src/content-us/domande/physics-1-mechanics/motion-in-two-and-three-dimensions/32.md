A particle starts from rest at the origin and moves with constant acceleration $\vec{a} = (3.0\,\hat{\imath} + 4.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. How far is it from the origin at $t = 2.0\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $14\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Starting from rest, $\vec{r} = \dfrac{1}{2}\,\vec{a}\,t^2$, so the particle moves along the straight line of $\vec{a}$. The magnitude of the acceleration is $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, hence the distance is $\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 5.0 \cdot 2.0^2\,\mathrm{m} = 10\,\mathrm{m}$.
