A car takes a circular bend at constant speed. If the radius of the bend were halved and the speed kept the same, by what factor would the centripetal acceleration change?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $4$
- $2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The centripetal acceleration is $\dfrac{v^2}{R}$, inversely proportional to the radius at fixed speed. Halving the radius therefore doubles it.
