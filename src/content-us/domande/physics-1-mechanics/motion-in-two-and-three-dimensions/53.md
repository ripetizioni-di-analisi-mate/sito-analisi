Two balls are launched from level ground with the same speed, one at $30^\circ$ and one at $60^\circ$ above the horizontal. Air resistance is negligible. What is the ratio of the time of flight of the ball launched at $60^\circ$ to that of the ball launched at $30^\circ$?

- $1.00$
- $2.00$
- $1.73$
- $3.00$

## Explanation

The time of flight on level ground is proportional to $\sin\theta_0$, the speed being the same. The ratio is therefore $\dfrac{\sin 60^\circ}{\sin 30^\circ} = \dfrac{0.866}{0.500} = 1.73$; the value $3.00$ is the ratio of the maximum heights, which goes as $\sin^2\theta_0$.
