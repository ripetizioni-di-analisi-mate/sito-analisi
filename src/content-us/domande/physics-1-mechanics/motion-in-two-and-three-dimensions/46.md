At a certain instant the velocity of a projectile is $\vec{v} = (12\,\hat{\imath} - 5.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s}$, with the $y$ axis vertical upwards. Which description is correct?

- Speed $13\,\mathrm{m/s}$, projectile falling
- Speed $7.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$, projectile falling
- Speed $13\,\mathrm{m/s}$, projectile rising
- Speed $17\,\mathrm{m/s}$, projectile falling

## Explanation

The speed is $\sqrt{12^2 + 5.0^2}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 13\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The vertical component is negative with the $y$ axis upwards, therefore the projectile is moving downwards, past the highest point of its path.
