A projectile is launched over level ground. Air resistance is negligible. If the launch speed is doubled and the launch angle is unchanged, by what factor does the range change?

- $2$
- $8$
- $4$
- $\sqrt{2}$

## Explanation

The range on level ground is $R = \dfrac{v_0^2\sin 2\theta_0}{g}$, proportional to the square of the launch speed at fixed angle. Doubling $v_0$ therefore multiplies the range by $2^2 = 4$.
