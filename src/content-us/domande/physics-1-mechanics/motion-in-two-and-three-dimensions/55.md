A stone is thrown at an angle above the horizontal from the roof of a building and lands on the street below. Air resistance is negligible. What does the expression $\dfrac{2\,v_0\sin\theta_0}{g}$ give in this case?

- The time to reach the highest point
- A time longer than the time of flight
- The time of flight
- The time to return to the level of the roof, shorter than the time of flight

## Explanation

The expression comes from imposing $y = 0$, that is, the return to the launch level. The stone keeps falling below the roof after that instant, therefore its actual time of flight is longer.
