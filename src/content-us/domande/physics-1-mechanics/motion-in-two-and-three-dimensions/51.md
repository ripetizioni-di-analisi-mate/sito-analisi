What is the greatest range that a projectile launched at $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$ can reach on level ground, with air resistance neglected?

- $28.8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $40.8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $20.4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $81.5\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The factor $\sin 2\theta_0$ in $R = \dfrac{v_0^2\sin 2\theta_0}{g}$ has maximum $1$, reached at $\theta_0 = 45^\circ$. The greatest range is therefore $\dfrac{v_0^2}{g} = \dfrac{400}{9.81}\,\mathrm{m} = 40.8\,\mathrm{m}$.
