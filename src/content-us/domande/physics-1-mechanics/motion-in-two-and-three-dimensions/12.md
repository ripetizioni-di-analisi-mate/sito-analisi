Which vector is parallel to the average velocity of a particle over a time interval, for every motion?

- The average acceleration over that interval
- The displacement over that interval
- The velocity at the beginning of the interval
- The velocity at the end of the interval

## Explanation

The average velocity is $\dfrac{\Delta\vec{r}}{\Delta t}$ with $\Delta t > 0$, hence it is the displacement multiplied by a positive number and has its direction and sense. The instantaneous velocities at the endpoints are tangent to the path, which in general bends between the two positions.
