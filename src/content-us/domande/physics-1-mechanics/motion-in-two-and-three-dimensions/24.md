For which motion is the acceleration perpendicular to the velocity at every instant?

- Free fall from rest
- Uniform circular motion
- A car speeding up along a straight road
- The motion of a projectile launched at $30^\circ$

## Explanation

The component of the acceleration along the velocity is zero exactly when the speed is constant, and uniform circular motion is the only listed motion with constant speed and nonzero acceleration. A projectile has its acceleration perpendicular to the velocity only at the highest point, while free fall from rest and a car on a straight road have the acceleration along the velocity.
