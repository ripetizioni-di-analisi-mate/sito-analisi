A wheel of diameter $0.70\,\mathrm{m}$ rolls without slipping along a straight distance of $1.0\,\mathrm{km}$. About how many revolutions does it make?

- $227$
- $455$
- $909$
- $1430$

## Explanation

In one revolution the center advances by the circumference $\pi D = \pi \cdot 0.70\,\mathrm{m} = 2.20\,\mathrm{m}$. Therefore
$$
N = \frac{1000\,\mathrm{m}}{2.20\,\mathrm{m}} \approx 455 .
$$
Writing the circumference as $2\pi D$ treats the diameter as a radius and halves the result.
