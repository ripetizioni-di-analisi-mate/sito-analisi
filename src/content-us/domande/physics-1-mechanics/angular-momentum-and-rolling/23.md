A homogeneous solid cylinder rolls without slipping on a horizontal floor at $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and then rolls up a ramp, still without slipping. How high does its center rise before the cylinder stops?

- $0.815\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.63\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.408\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.22\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Static friction does no work, so the mechanical energy is conserved, and at the highest point both $v_{\mathrm{cm}}$ and $\omega$ are zero. Hence $\dfrac{3}{4}Mv_{\mathrm{cm}}^2 = Mgh$, which gives
$$
h = \frac{3\,v_{\mathrm{cm}}^2}{4\,g} = \frac{3 \cdot 16}{4 \cdot 9.81}\,\mathrm{m} = 1.22\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $0.815\,\mathrm{m} = \dfrac{v^2}{2g}$ ignores the rotational energy, which is also converted into potential energy.
