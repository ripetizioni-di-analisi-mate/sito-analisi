The formula $\Omega = \dfrac{Mgd}{I\,\omega}$ for the precession of a gyroscope rests on an approximation. Which one?

- The spin is so fast that the angular momentum of the precession is negligible
- The spin is so slow that the angular momentum of the spin is negligible
- The weight of the wheel is negligible
- The force of the support on the axle is negligible

## Explanation

The derivation takes the whole angular momentum along the axle, $\vec{L} = I\,\omega\,\hat{u}$. The precession adds an angular momentum about the vertical axis, which is negligible only when the spin is fast. The weight cannot be negligible, since its torque drives the precession, and the support carries the weight of the wheel.
