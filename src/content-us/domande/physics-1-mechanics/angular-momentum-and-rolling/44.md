A homogeneous solid cylinder is released from rest on a plane inclined at $30^\circ$ and rolls without slipping. How long does it take to travel $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ along the plane?

- $0.90\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.3\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.1\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.78\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The acceleration of the center is $a_{\mathrm{cm}} = \dfrac{2}{3}\,g\sin 30^\circ = 3.27\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, constant, so the motion starting from rest gives $d = \dfrac{1}{2}a_{\mathrm{cm}}t^2$ and
$$
t = \sqrt{\frac{2d}{a_{\mathrm{cm}}}} = \sqrt{\frac{2 \cdot 2.0}{3.27}}\,\mathrm{s} = 1.1\,\mathrm{s} .
$$
The value $0.90\,\mathrm{s}$ uses $g\sin\theta$, the acceleration of a body sliding without friction.
