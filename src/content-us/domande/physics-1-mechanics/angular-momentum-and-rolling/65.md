A disk turns counterclockwise as seen from above, about a vertical axis through its center. What is the direction of its angular momentum about the center?

- Vertical and downward
- Horizontal and tangent to the rim
- Horizontal and from the center to the rim
- Vertical and upward

## Explanation

Each particle contributes $\vec{r}_i \times m_i\vec{v}_i$, and for a counterclockwise rotation seen from above the right-hand rule gives a vector pointing up. All the contributions point the same way along the axis, so $\vec{L}$ is vertical and upward.
