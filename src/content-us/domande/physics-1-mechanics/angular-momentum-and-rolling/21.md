A homogeneous solid cylinder rolls without slipping from rest down a height $h$. It is replaced by a homogeneous solid cylinder of twice the mass and half the radius. How does the speed of the center at the bottom change?

- It doubles
- It increases by a factor $\sqrt{2}$
- It is halved
- It does not change

## Explanation

Conservation of mechanical energy gives
$$
v_{\mathrm{cm}} = \sqrt{\frac{2gh}{1 + \beta}} ,
$$
which contains neither the mass nor the radius. Both cylinders have $\beta = \dfrac{1}{2}$, hence they reach the same speed.
