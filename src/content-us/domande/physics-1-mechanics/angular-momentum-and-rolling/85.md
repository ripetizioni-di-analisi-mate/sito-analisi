A spinning wheel has a horizontal axle supported at one end, as in a gyroscope. What is the direction of the torque of the weight about the support?

- Vertical and downward
- Horizontal and along the axle
- Horizontal and perpendicular to the axle
- Vertical and upward

## Explanation

The torque is $d\,\hat{u} \times (-Mg\,\hat{\jmath})$, the vector product of a horizontal vector along the axle and a vertical vector. It is therefore perpendicular to both, that is horizontal and perpendicular to the axle.
