A particle moves with constant velocity along a straight line that passes through the point $O$. What is its angular momentum about $O$?

- Constant and different from zero
- Increasing while the particle moves away from $O$
- Zero at every instant
- Decreasing while the particle approaches $O$

## Explanation

The position vector from $O$ lies along the line of motion, hence it is parallel to $\vec{p}$ and $\vec{r} \times \vec{p} = \vec{0}$. Equivalently, the lever arm $r_\perp$ is zero.
