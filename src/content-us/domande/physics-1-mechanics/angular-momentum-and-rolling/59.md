A homogeneous disk of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ and radius $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$ turns at $10\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ about its fixed symmetry axis. What is its angular momentum along that axis?

- $1.8\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $3.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $0.90\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $0.45\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$

## Explanation

The disk has $I = \dfrac{1}{2}MR^2 = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 2.0 \cdot 0.090\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2} = 0.090\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$, hence
$$
L_z = I\,\omega = 0.090 \cdot 10\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s} = 0.90\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s} .
$$
The value $1.8\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$ uses the moment of inertia $MR^2$ of a hoop, and $3.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$ leaves the radius unsquared.
