A homogeneous solid cylinder of mass $4.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ rolls without slipping with center speed $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is its kinetic energy?

- $18\,\mathrm{J}$
- $27\,\mathrm{J}$
- $9.0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $36\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

With $I_{\mathrm{cm}} = \dfrac{1}{2}MR^2$ the **kinetic energy of a rolling body** is
$$
K = \frac{1}{2}\left(M + \frac{I_{\mathrm{cm}}}{R^2}\right)v_{\mathrm{cm}}^2 = \frac{3}{4}M v_{\mathrm{cm}}^2 = \frac{3}{4} \cdot 4.0 \cdot 9.0\,\mathrm{J} = 27\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
The translational part alone is $18\,\mathrm{J}$ and the rotational part is $9.0\,\mathrm{J}$.
