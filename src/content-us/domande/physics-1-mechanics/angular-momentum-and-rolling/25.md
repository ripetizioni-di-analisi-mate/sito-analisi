A wheel with $I_{\mathrm{cm}} = 0.60\,MR^2$ rolls without slipping. What percentage of its kinetic energy is rotational?

- $60\%$
- $63\%$
- $30\%$
- $38\%$

## Explanation

With $\beta = 0.60$, the rotational part is $\dfrac{1}{2}\beta Mv_{\mathrm{cm}}^2$ and the total is $\dfrac{1}{2}(1 + \beta)Mv_{\mathrm{cm}}^2$, hence the fraction is
$$
\frac{\beta}{1 + \beta} = \frac{0.60}{1.60} = 0.375 ,
$$
that is $38\%$. The value $63\%$ is the translational fraction, and $60\%$ is the ratio of the rotational to the translational part.
