A homogeneous solid sphere rolls without slipping. What is the ratio $\dfrac{K_{\mathrm{rot}}}{K_{\mathrm{transl}}}$ of its rotational to its translational kinetic energy?

- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2}{7}$
- $\dfrac{5}{7}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

By the rolling condition $\omega = \dfrac{v_{\mathrm{cm}}}{R}$, therefore
$$
\frac{K_{\mathrm{rot}}}{K_{\mathrm{transl}}} = \frac{\frac{1}{2}I_{\mathrm{cm}}\,\omega^2}{\frac{1}{2}M v_{\mathrm{cm}}^2} = \frac{I_{\mathrm{cm}}}{MR^2} = \frac{2}{5}
$$
for a solid sphere. The value $\dfrac{2}{7}$ is the ratio of the rotational part to the total kinetic energy.
