A student sits on a stool that turns without friction about a vertical axis and holds a bicycle wheel spinning with angular momentum $3.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$ directed upward, while the student and the stool are at rest. The student turns the wheel upside down. What angular momentum do the student and the stool acquire?

- $3.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$, directed upward
- $6.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$, directed downward
- $6.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$, directed upward
- $3.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$, directed downward

## Explanation

The weight is vertical and the frictionless bearing of the stool exerts no torque about the vertical axis, so the vertical component of the total angular momentum is conserved and stays $+3.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$. After the flip the wheel contributes $-3.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$, hence the student and the stool carry $+6.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$, directed upward.
