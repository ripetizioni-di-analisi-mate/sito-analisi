A homogeneous solid sphere is released from rest on a frictionless inclined plane. How does it move?

- It slides down without rotating, with acceleration $g\sin\theta$
- It rolls down with acceleration $\dfrac{5}{7}g\sin\theta$
- It rolls down with acceleration $g\sin\theta$
- It slides down without rotating, with acceleration $\dfrac{5}{7}g\sin\theta$

## Explanation

Without friction the only forces are the weight and the normal force, and both have zero torque about the center, so the angular speed stays zero. The translation is then that of a block on a frictionless incline, with acceleration $g\sin\theta$.
