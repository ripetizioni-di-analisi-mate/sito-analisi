A homogeneous solid sphere of mass $5.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ and radius $0.20\,\mathrm{m}$ spins at $3.0$ revolutions per second about a fixed diameter. What is its angular momentum along that axis?

- $0.24\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $3.8\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $1.9\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$

## Explanation

The moment of inertia is $I = \dfrac{2}{5}MR^2 = 0.080\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ and the angular speed is $\omega = 2\pi \cdot 3.0\,\mathrm{rad/s} = 18.8\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. Hence
$$
L_z = I\,\omega = 0.080 \cdot 18.8\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s} = 1.5\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s} .
$$
Using $3.0$ revolutions per second as if it were an angular speed in $\mathrm{rad/s}$ gives $0.24\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$.
