A wheel of radius $0.40\,\mathrm{m}$ rolls without slipping with angular speed $15\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. What is the speed of the highest point of its rim?

- $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $24\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The highest point is at distance $2R$ from the contact line, which is the instantaneous axis of rotation, therefore it moves at
$$
v = \omega \cdot 2R = 15\,\mathrm{rad/s} \cdot 0.80\,\mathrm{m} = 12\,\mathrm{m/s} ,
$$
twice the speed $\omega R = 6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ of the center.
