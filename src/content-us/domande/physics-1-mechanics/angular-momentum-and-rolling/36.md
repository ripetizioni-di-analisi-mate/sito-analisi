A homogeneous solid sphere is placed on an inclined plane with coefficient of static friction $0.20$. What is the largest angle of the plane at which it can roll without slipping?

- $11.3^\circ$
- $35.0^\circ$
- $15.6^\circ$
- $44.4^\circ$

## Explanation

For a solid sphere $\dfrac{\beta}{1 + \beta} = \dfrac{2}{7}$, and the condition $\mu_s \ge \dfrac{2}{7}\tan\theta$ gives
$$
\tan\theta \le \frac{7}{2}\,\mu_s = 0.70 ,
$$
hence $\theta \le 35.0^\circ$. Solving $\sin\theta = 0.70$ in place of $\tan\theta = 0.70$ gives $44.4^\circ$, and $11.3^\circ$ is the angle at which a block begins to slide.
