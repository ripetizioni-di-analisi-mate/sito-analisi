A body rolls without slipping down an inclined plane. Which of the forces acting on it have a nonzero torque about its center?

- Only the weight
- The weight and the normal force
- The normal force and the static friction
- Only the static friction

## Explanation

The weight is applied at the center, so its lever arm is zero. The normal force acts at the contact point along the line that joins that point to the center, so its lever arm is zero as well. Only the friction, tangent to the surface at the distance $R$ from the center, has a torque, of magnitude $fR$.
