About which points can the equation $\dfrac{d\vec{L}}{dt} = \vec{\tau}^{\,\mathrm{ext}}$ be applied to a system of particles?

- Only the center of mass
- Any point, even one that accelerates
- Any point fixed in an inertial frame, and the center of mass
- Only points fixed in an inertial frame

## Explanation

The theorem is proved for a point fixed in an inertial frame. It also holds about the center of mass, even when the center of mass accelerates, because the inertial forces $-m_i\vec{a}_{\mathrm{cm}}$ have zero total torque about that point. About an arbitrary accelerating point those inertial forces have, in general, a nonzero torque.
