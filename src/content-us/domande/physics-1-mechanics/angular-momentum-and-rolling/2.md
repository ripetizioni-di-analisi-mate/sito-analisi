A wheel of radius $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$ rolls without slipping, and its center moves at $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the angular speed of the wheel?

- $10\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $1.8\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $40\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

The **rolling condition** $v_{\mathrm{cm}} = \omega R$ gives
$$
\omega = \frac{v_{\mathrm{cm}}}{R} = \frac{6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}}{0.30\,\mathrm{m}} = 20\,\mathrm{rad/s} .
$$
Dividing by the diameter $0.60\,\mathrm{m}$ in place of the radius gives half of this value.
