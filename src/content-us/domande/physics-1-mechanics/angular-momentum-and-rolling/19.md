A hoop rolls without slipping and a block slides without rotating, with the same mass and the same speed of the center of mass. What is the ratio $\dfrac{K_{\mathrm{hoop}}}{K_{\mathrm{block}}}$ of their kinetic energies?

- $2$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

For the hoop $I_{\mathrm{cm}} = MR^2$, hence
$$
K_{\mathrm{hoop}} = \frac{1}{2}\left(M + M\right)v^2 = M v^2 ,
$$
while the block has $\dfrac{1}{2}Mv^2$. The hoop stores in its rotation as much energy as in its translation.
