A wheel rolls without slipping toward the right. What is the direction of the velocity of the point of the rim that is level with the axle and behind it?

- Forward, at $45^\circ$ above the horizontal
- Forward, at $45^\circ$ below the horizontal
- Backward, at $45^\circ$ above the horizontal
- Horizontal and forward

## Explanation

The rotation gives this point a velocity of magnitude $\omega R = v_{\mathrm{cm}}$ tangent to the rim. The wheel turns clockwise, hence the point behind the axle moves upward. Adding the forward velocity $v_{\mathrm{cm}}$ of the center gives two equal perpendicular components, forward and upward, so the velocity points forward at $45^\circ$ above the horizontal. The same direction is perpendicular to the segment that joins the point to the contact point, as the instantaneous axis requires.
