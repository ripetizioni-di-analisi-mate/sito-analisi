A solid sphere, a solid cylinder, a hoop and a thin spherical shell, all homogeneous, are released together from rest at the same height on the same slope and roll without slipping. Which one reaches the bottom first?

- The hoop
- The solid sphere
- The solid cylinder
- All four at the same instant

## Explanation

The acceleration of the center is $\dfrac{g\sin\theta}{1 + \beta}$ with $\beta = \dfrac{I_{\mathrm{cm}}}{MR^2}$, which is larger for a smaller $\beta$. The solid sphere has the smallest value, $\beta = \dfrac{2}{5}$, therefore it arrives first, and the hoop, with $\beta = 1$, arrives last.
