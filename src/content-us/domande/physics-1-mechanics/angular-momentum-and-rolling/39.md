A hoop and a homogeneous solid sphere roll without slipping down the same inclined plane. What is the ratio $\dfrac{a_{\mathrm{hoop}}}{a_{\mathrm{sphere}}}$ of the accelerations of their centers?

- $1.0$
- $1.4$
- $0.50$
- $0.70$

## Explanation

The acceleration is $\dfrac{g\sin\theta}{1 + \beta}$, so the ratio is
$$
\frac{a_{\mathrm{hoop}}}{a_{\mathrm{sphere}}} = \frac{1 + \beta_{\mathrm{sphere}}}{1 + \beta_{\mathrm{hoop}}} = \frac{1.4}{2} = 0.70 .
$$
The value $1.4$ is the inverse ratio, and $0.50$ is the ratio of the acceleration of the hoop to $g\sin\theta$.
