Two small spheres, each of mass $0.50\,\mathrm{kg}$, are fixed at the ends of a light rod of length $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$ that turns at $5.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ about a perpendicular axis through its midpoint. What is the angular momentum of the system along the axis?

- $3.2\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $0.80\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $0.40\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$

## Explanation

Each sphere is at $r = 0.40\,\mathrm{m}$ from the axis, hence the moment of inertia of the system is $I = 2\,m\,r^2 = 0.16\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ and
$$
L_z = I\,\omega = 0.16 \cdot 5.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s} = 0.80\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s} .
$$
The value $3.2\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$ uses the whole length of the rod as the distance from the axis, and $0.40\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$ counts one sphere only.
