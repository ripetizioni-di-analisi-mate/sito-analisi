A car accelerates at $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ on a straight road, and its wheels, of radius $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$, roll without slipping. What is the angular acceleration of a wheel?

- $0.90\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $5.0\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $10\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $20\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$

## Explanation

Differentiating the rolling condition with respect to time gives $a_{\mathrm{cm}} = \alpha R$, hence
$$
\alpha = \frac{a_{\mathrm{cm}}}{R} = \frac{3.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}}{0.30\,\mathrm{m}} = 10\,\mathrm{rad/s^2} .
$$
