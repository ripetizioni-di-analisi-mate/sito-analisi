A hoop of mass $0.80\,\mathrm{kg}$ is released from rest and rolls without slipping down a slope, descending a height of $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$. What is its rotational kinetic energy at the bottom?

- $12\,\mathrm{J}$
- $3.9\,\mathrm{J}$
- $5.9\,\mathrm{J}$
- $3.4\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Static friction does no work, so the whole loss of potential energy becomes kinetic energy, $K = Mgh = 0.80 \cdot 9.81 \cdot 1.5\,\mathrm{J} = 11.8\,\mathrm{J}$. For a hoop $\beta = 1$, hence the rotational fraction is $\dfrac{\beta}{1 + \beta} = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and
$$
K_{\mathrm{rot}} = \frac{1}{2}\,Mgh = 5.9\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
The value $3.9\,\mathrm{J}$ uses the fraction $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of a solid cylinder, and $3.4\,\mathrm{J}$ the fraction $\dfrac{2}{7}$ of a solid sphere.
