A hoop is released from rest and rolls without slipping down a slope, descending a height of $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the speed of its center at the bottom?

- $5.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.7\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

For a hoop $\beta = 1$, therefore conservation of mechanical energy gives $Mgh = Mv_{\mathrm{cm}}^2$ and
$$
v_{\mathrm{cm}} = \sqrt{gh} = \sqrt{9.81 \cdot 1.5}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 3.8\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The value $\sqrt{2gh} = 5.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ ignores the rotational energy, and $4.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is the speed of a solid cylinder.
