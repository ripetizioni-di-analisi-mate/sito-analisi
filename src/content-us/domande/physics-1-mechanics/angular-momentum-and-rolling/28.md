A homogeneous solid cylinder of radius $5.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ is released from rest and rolls without slipping down a height of $0.60\,\mathrm{m}$. What is its angular speed at the bottom?

- $69\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $28\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $0.56\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $56\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

Conservation of mechanical energy with $\beta = \dfrac{1}{2}$ gives
$$
v_{\mathrm{cm}} = \sqrt{\frac{4gh}{3}} = \sqrt{\frac{4 \cdot 9.81 \cdot 0.60}{3}}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 2.80\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The rolling condition then gives $\omega = \dfrac{v_{\mathrm{cm}}}{R} = \dfrac{2.80\,\mathrm{m/s}}{0.050\,\mathrm{m}} = 56\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. The value $69\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ uses the speed $\sqrt{2gh}$ of a body without rotation.
