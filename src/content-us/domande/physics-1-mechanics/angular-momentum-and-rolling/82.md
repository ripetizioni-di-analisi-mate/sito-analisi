Two disks turning at different angular speeds about the same frictionless axis are coupled by friction until they turn together. Which quantities of the pair are conserved?

- The kinetic energy, but not the angular momentum along the axis
- Both the angular momentum along the axis and the kinetic energy
- Neither the angular momentum along the axis nor the kinetic energy
- The angular momentum along the axis, but not the kinetic energy

## Explanation

The friction between the faces is internal, and the external forces have no torque about the axis, so the total angular momentum along the axis is conserved. While the friction acts the two faces slide on each other, therefore it dissipates energy, as in a perfectly inelastic collision.
