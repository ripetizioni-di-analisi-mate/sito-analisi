The horizontal axle of a gyroscope extends east from the support on which it rests, and the spin angular momentum of the wheel points east, away from the support. Seen from above, how does the axle turn?

- Counterclockwise, from east towards north
- Clockwise, from east towards south
- It does not turn, and it falls downward
- It does not turn, and it rises upward

## Explanation

With $x$ east, $y$ north and $z$ up, the torque of the weight about the support is $d\,\hat{\imath} \times (-Mg\,\hat{k}) = Mgd\,\hat{\jmath}$, directed north. The increment $d\vec{L} = \vec{\tau}\,dt$ adds a northward component to $\vec{L}$, and the axle, which follows $\vec{L}$, turns from east towards north, counterclockwise as seen from above.
