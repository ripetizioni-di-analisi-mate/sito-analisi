A homogeneous solid sphere of mass $3.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ and radius $0.10\,\mathrm{m}$ rolls without slipping at $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the magnitude of its angular momentum about the contact line?

- $0.24\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $0.60\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $0.84\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$

## Explanation

The angular momentum of a rolling body about the contact line is
$$
L = (1 + \beta)\,M\,v_{\mathrm{cm}}\,R = 1.4 \cdot 3.0 \cdot 2.0 \cdot 0.10\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s} = 0.84\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s} .
$$
It is the sum of the part $I_{\mathrm{cm}}\,\omega = 0.24\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$ due to the rotation about the center and of the part $M\,v_{\mathrm{cm}}\,R = 0.60\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$ due to the motion of the center.
