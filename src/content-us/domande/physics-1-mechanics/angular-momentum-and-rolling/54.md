A particle moves in the $xy$ plane, turning clockwise as seen from the origin, with the $x$ axis to the right and the $y$ axis up. What is the sign of the $z$ component of its angular momentum about the origin?

- Positive
- Negative
- Zero
- Positive or negative, depending on the speed

## Explanation

The component $\ell_z = m\,(x\,v_y - y\,v_x)$ is positive for a counterclockwise motion as seen from the origin and negative for a clockwise one. A particle at $(R,\ 0)$ moving towards $-y$, for instance, has $\ell_z = m\,R\,v_y < 0$.
