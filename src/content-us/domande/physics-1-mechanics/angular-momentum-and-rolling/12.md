A wheel rolls without slipping along the $x$ axis in the sense of increasing $x$, with the $y$ axis pointing up and the $z$ axis pointing out of the page. What is the direction of its angular velocity $\vec{\omega}$?

- $+\hat{k}$, out of the page
- $+\hat{\imath}$, along the motion
- $-\hat{k}$, into the page
- $-\hat{\jmath}$, toward the ground

## Explanation

A wheel that advances towards $+x$ turns clockwise as seen with $x$ to the right and $y$ up. The right-hand rule, with the fingers curled clockwise in the page, gives a thumb pointing into the page, hence $\vec{\omega} = -\omega\hat{k}$. With this sign the rotation gives the contact point the velocity $(-\omega\hat{k}) \times (-R\hat{\jmath}) = -\omega R\,\hat{\imath}$, which cancels $\vec{v}_{\mathrm{cm}}$.
