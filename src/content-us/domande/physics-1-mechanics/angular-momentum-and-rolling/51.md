A stone of mass $0.25\,\mathrm{kg}$ moves in uniform circular motion at $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ on a circle of radius $1.2\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the magnitude of its angular momentum about the center of the circle?

- $0.83\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $4.8\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $2.4\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$

## Explanation

With the origin at the center, $\vec{r}$ is perpendicular to $\vec{v}$, hence
$$
\ell = m\,v\,r = 0.25 \cdot 4.0 \cdot 1.2\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s} = 1.2\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s} .
$$
The value $2.4\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$ uses the diameter in place of the radius.
