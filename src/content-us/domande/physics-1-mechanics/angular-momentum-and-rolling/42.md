A homogeneous solid cylinder is released on an inclined plane whose coefficient of static friction is smaller than $\dfrac{1}{3}\tan\theta$, while the coefficient of kinetic friction is positive. Which statement describes its motion?

- It rolls without slipping, and its mechanical energy is conserved
- It slides without rotating, and its mechanical energy is conserved
- It rolls without slipping, and its mechanical energy decreases
- It slides while rotating, and its mechanical energy decreases

## Explanation

The condition for rolling without slipping, $\mu_s \ge \dfrac{1}{3}\tan\theta$ for a solid cylinder, is not satisfied, therefore the contact point moves and kinetic friction acts. Kinetic friction has a torque about the center, so the cylinder turns, though more slowly than the rolling condition requires. The point on which friction acts moves, hence friction does negative work and the mechanical energy decreases.
