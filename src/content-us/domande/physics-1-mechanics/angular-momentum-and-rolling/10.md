A vehicle has a front wheel of radius $0.75\,\mathrm{m}$ and a rear wheel of radius $0.25\,\mathrm{m}$, and both roll without slipping. How do their angular speeds compare?

- Both wheels turn at the same angular speed
- The front wheel turns three times as fast as the rear wheel
- The rear wheel turns three times as fast as the front wheel
- The rear wheel turns nine times as fast as the front wheel

## Explanation

Both axles belong to the same vehicle, hence both centers move at the same speed $v$. The rolling condition gives $\omega = \dfrac{v}{R}$, so the angular speed is inversely proportional to the radius and
$$
\frac{\omega_{\mathrm{rear}}}{\omega_{\mathrm{front}}} = \frac{0.75\,\mathrm{m}}{0.25\,\mathrm{m}} = 3 .
$$
