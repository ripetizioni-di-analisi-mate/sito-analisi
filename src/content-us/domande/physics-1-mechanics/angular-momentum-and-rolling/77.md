A homogeneous sphere spinning freely in space with period $T$ contracts to half its radius, keeping its mass and remaining homogeneous. What is its new period?

- $\dfrac{T}{4}$
- $\dfrac{T}{2}$
- $4T$
- $\dfrac{T}{8}$

## Explanation

No external torque acts, so $I\,\omega$ is conserved. The moment of inertia $\dfrac{2}{5}MR^2$ becomes one quarter of its initial value, therefore $\omega$ becomes four times larger and the period $\dfrac{2\pi}{\omega}$ becomes $\dfrac{T}{4}$.
