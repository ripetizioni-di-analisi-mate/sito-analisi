A homogeneous solid cylinder of mass $6.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ rolls without slipping down a plane inclined at $30^\circ$. What is the magnitude of the static friction at the contact?

- $9.81\,\mathrm{N}$
- $29.4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $14.7\,\mathrm{N}$
- $17.0\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

With $\beta = \dfrac{1}{2}$ the static friction is
$$
f = \frac{\beta}{1 + \beta}\,Mg\sin\theta = \frac{1}{3} \cdot 6.0 \cdot 9.81 \cdot 0.50\,\mathrm{N} = 9.81\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The value $29.4\,\mathrm{N}$ is the whole component of the weight along the slope, and $17.0\,\mathrm{N}$ uses the cosine in place of the sine.
