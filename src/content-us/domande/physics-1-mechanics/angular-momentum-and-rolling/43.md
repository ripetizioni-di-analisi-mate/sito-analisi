Which homogeneous body, rolling without slipping down an inclined plane, has an acceleration exactly half of $g\sin\theta$?

- A solid cylinder
- A hoop
- A solid sphere
- A thin spherical shell

## Explanation

The acceleration is $\dfrac{g\sin\theta}{1 + \beta}$, which equals $\dfrac{1}{2}g\sin\theta$ for $\beta = 1$, that is for $I_{\mathrm{cm}} = MR^2$. Among the four bodies only the hoop has this moment of inertia, while the solid cylinder, for instance, has $a_{\mathrm{cm}} = \dfrac{2}{3}g\sin\theta$.
