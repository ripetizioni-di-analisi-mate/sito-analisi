A homogeneous solid sphere of radius $0.10\,\mathrm{m}$ rolls without slipping down a plane inclined at $30^\circ$. What is its angular acceleration?

- $49.1\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $17.5\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $35.0\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $60.7\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$

## Explanation

The center accelerates at $a_{\mathrm{cm}} = \dfrac{5}{7}\,g\sin 30^\circ = 3.50\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, and the rolling condition $a_{\mathrm{cm}} = \alpha R$ gives
$$
\alpha = \frac{a_{\mathrm{cm}}}{R} = \frac{3.50\,\mathrm{m/s^2}}{0.10\,\mathrm{m}} = 35.0\,\mathrm{rad/s^2} .
$$
The value $49.1\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$ uses $g\sin\theta$, the acceleration of a body sliding without friction.
