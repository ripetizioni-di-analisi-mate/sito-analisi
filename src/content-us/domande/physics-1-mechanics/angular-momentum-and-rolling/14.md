A wheel of radius $R$ rolls without slipping. Which points of its rim move at the same speed as its center?

- The two points at height $\dfrac{R}{2}$ above the ground
- The two points level with the axle
- The highest point only
- No point of the rim

## Explanation

Each point moves at $\omega$ times its distance from the contact line, and the center is at distance $R$ from it. A rim point at height $\dfrac{R}{2}$ has $\cos\varphi = \dfrac{1}{2}$, hence its distance from the contact point is $R\sqrt{2 - 2\cos\varphi} = R$ and its speed is $\omega R = v_{\mathrm{cm}}$. The points level with the axle are at distance $\sqrt{2}\,R$ and move at $\sqrt{2}\,v_{\mathrm{cm}}$.
