A skater spins at $0.80$ revolutions per second with moment of inertia $5.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ and pulls her arms in until her moment of inertia is $2.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$. Friction is negligible. What is her new rate of rotation?

- $0.32$ revolutions per second
- $2.0$ revolutions per second
- $5.0$ revolutions per second
- $1.3$ revolutions per second

## Explanation

The weight and the reaction of the ice are vertical, so their torque about the vertical axis is zero and $I_1\,\omega_1 = I_2\,\omega_2$. The rate of rotation is proportional to $\omega$, hence
$$
f_2 = \frac{I_1}{I_2}\,f_1 = \frac{5.0}{2.0} \cdot 0.80 = 2.0
$$
revolutions per second. Equating the kinetic energies instead of the angular momenta gives $1.3$ revolutions per second.
