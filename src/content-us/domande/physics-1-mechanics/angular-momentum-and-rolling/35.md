A hoop is released on a plane inclined at $20^\circ$. What is the smallest coefficient of static friction for which it rolls without slipping?

- $0.364$
- $0.121$
- $0.470$
- $0.182$

## Explanation

The **condition for rolling without slipping** is $\mu_s \ge \dfrac{\beta}{1 + \beta}\tan\theta$, and for a hoop $\beta = 1$, hence
$$
\mu_s \ge \frac{1}{2}\tan 20^\circ = 0.182 .
$$
The value $\tan 20^\circ = 0.364$ is the coefficient that would keep a block at rest on the same plane, and $0.121$ belongs to a solid cylinder.
