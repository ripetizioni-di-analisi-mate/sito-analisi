A homogeneous solid cylinder rolls without slipping. What fraction of its total kinetic energy is rotational?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

With $\beta = \dfrac{I_{\mathrm{cm}}}{MR^2} = \dfrac{1}{2}$, the rotational part is $\dfrac{1}{2}\beta M v_{\mathrm{cm}}^2$ and the total is $\dfrac{1}{2}(1 + \beta)M v_{\mathrm{cm}}^2$, so the fraction is
$$
\frac{\beta}{1 + \beta} = \frac{1}{3} .
$$
The value $\dfrac{1}{2}$ is the ratio of the rotational to the translational part.
