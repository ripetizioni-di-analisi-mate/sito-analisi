The angular momentum of a particle about a fixed point $O$ grows uniformly from $2.0$ to $5.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$ in $1.5\,\mathrm{s}$, without changing direction. What is the magnitude of the torque about $O$ of the resultant force on the particle?

- $4.5\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $3.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $3.3\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$

## Explanation

By $\dfrac{d\vec{\ell}}{dt} = \vec{\tau}$, a uniform change gives
$$
\tau = \frac{\Delta\ell}{\Delta t} = \frac{3.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}}{1.5\,\mathrm{s}} = 2.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m} .
$$
The value $3.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$ is the change itself, without the division by the time, and $3.3\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$ divides the final value instead of the change.
