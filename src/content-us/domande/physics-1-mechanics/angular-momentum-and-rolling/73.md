A disk of moment of inertia $0.40\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$, turning about a fixed axis, is coupled by friction to a coaxial disk of moment of inertia $0.60\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$, initially at rest, until they turn together. What fraction of the initial kinetic energy is lost?

- $0.60$
- $0.40$
- $0.84$
- $0.16$

## Explanation

Conservation of angular momentum gives $\omega = \dfrac{I_1}{I_1 + I_2}\,\omega_0$, therefore the final kinetic energy is
$$
\frac{1}{2}(I_1 + I_2)\,\omega^2 = \frac{I_1}{I_1 + I_2} \cdot \frac{1}{2}I_1\,\omega_0^2 .
$$
The fraction retained is $\dfrac{0.40}{1.00} = 0.40$, hence the fraction lost is $0.60$.
