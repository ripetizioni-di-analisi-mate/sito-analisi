A wheel of radius $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$ moves on a road with center velocity $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ forward while turning at $10\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ in the sense of rolling. What is the velocity of the point of the wheel that touches the road?

- $8.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ forward
- $0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ backward
- $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ forward

## Explanation

The velocity of the contact point is $\vec{v}_{\mathrm{cm}} + \vec{\omega} \times \vec{r}\,'$, where the rotation contributes $\omega R = 3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ directed backward. The result is $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s} - 3.0\,\mathrm{m/s} = 2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ forward. Since $\omega R \ne v_{\mathrm{cm}}$, the wheel slides on the road and does not roll without slipping.
