A body of mass $M$, radius $R$ and moment of inertia $I_{\mathrm{cm}}$ about its symmetry axis rolls without slipping with angular speed $\omega$. Which expression gives its kinetic energy?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}I_{\mathrm{cm}}\,\omega^2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}MR^2\omega^2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\left(I_{\mathrm{cm}} + MR^2\right)\omega^2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\left(I_{\mathrm{cm}} + 2MR^2\right)\omega^2$

## Explanation

The kinetic energy is $\dfrac{1}{2}Mv_{\mathrm{cm}}^2 + \dfrac{1}{2}I_{\mathrm{cm}}\,\omega^2$, and the rolling condition gives $v_{\mathrm{cm}} = \omega R$, therefore $K = \dfrac{1}{2}\left(I_{\mathrm{cm}} + MR^2\right)\omega^2$. The sum in the parentheses is the moment of inertia about the contact line, given by the parallel-axis theorem, in agreement with the fact that the body turns about that line at each instant.
