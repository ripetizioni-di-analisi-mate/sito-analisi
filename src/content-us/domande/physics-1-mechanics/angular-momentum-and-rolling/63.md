A body turns about a fixed axis, and the total external torque about that axis is zero. Can its angular speed change?

- Yes, if the body changes its moment of inertia about the axis
- No, its angular speed is constant in every case
- Yes, if its angular momentum about the axis changes
- Yes, but only if its mass changes

## Explanation

With zero torque about the axis $L_z = I\,\omega$ is constant, therefore $\omega$ changes exactly when $I$ changes, as for a skater who pulls in her arms. The angular momentum itself cannot change without an external torque, and the mass does not need to change, since $I$ depends on how the mass is distributed.
