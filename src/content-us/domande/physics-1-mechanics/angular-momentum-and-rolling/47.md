A particle of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ passes through the point $\vec{r} = (1.5\,\hat{\imath} + 2.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m}$ with velocity $\vec{v} = (3.0\,\hat{\imath} - 1.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is its angular momentum about the origin?

- $+15\,\hat{k}\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $-15\,\hat{k}\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $+9.0\,\hat{k}\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $-7.5\,\hat{k}\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$

## Explanation

For a particle in the $xy$ plane
$$
\ell_z = m\,(x\,v_y - y\,v_x) = 2.0 \cdot \left[1.5 \cdot (-1.0) - 2.0 \cdot 3.0\right]\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s} = -15\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s} .
$$
The sign is negative because the particle turns clockwise as seen from the origin. Omitting the mass gives $-7.5\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$.
