A homogeneous solid sphere rolls without slipping down a plane inclined at $25^\circ$. What is the acceleration of its center?

- $2.96\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $4.15\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2.07\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $6.35\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

With $\beta = \dfrac{2}{5}$ the **acceleration of a body rolling down an incline** is
$$
a_{\mathrm{cm}} = \frac{g\sin\theta}{1 + \beta} = \frac{5}{7} \cdot 9.81 \cdot \sin 25^\circ\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 2.96\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
The value $4.15\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = g\sin\theta$ belongs to a body sliding without friction, and $6.35\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ uses the cosine in place of the sine.
