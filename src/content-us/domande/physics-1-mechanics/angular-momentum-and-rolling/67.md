A rigid body turns about a fixed axis with angular momentum $12\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$ along the axis and kinetic energy $30\,\mathrm{J}$. What is its moment of inertia about the axis?

- $2.4\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $4.8\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.40\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$

## Explanation

Since $L = I\,\omega$, the kinetic energy can be written as $K = \dfrac{L^2}{2I}$, hence
$$
I = \frac{L^2}{2K} = \frac{144}{60}\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2} = 2.4\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2} .
$$
Omitting the factor $2$ gives $4.8\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$.
