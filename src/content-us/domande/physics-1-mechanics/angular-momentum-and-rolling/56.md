A particle of mass $0.20\,\mathrm{kg}$ is released from rest at a horizontal distance of $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from a point $O$ and falls freely. What is the magnitude of its angular momentum about $O$ after $2.0\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $5.9\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $2.9\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $3.9\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$

## Explanation

The particle falls along a vertical line at distance $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $O$, which is the lever arm at every instant. After $2.0\,\mathrm{s}$ its momentum is $p = m\,g\,t = 0.20 \cdot 9.81 \cdot 2.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s} = 3.9\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$, hence
$$
\ell = p\,r_\perp = 3.9\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s} \cdot 1.5\,\mathrm{m} = 5.9\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s} .
$$
The same value follows from the constant torque of the weight, $m\,g \cdot 1.5\,\mathrm{m} = 2.9\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$, acting for $2.0\,\mathrm{s}$.
