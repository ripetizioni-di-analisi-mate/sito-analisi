During the precession of a gyroscope with horizontal axle, the magnitude of its spin angular momentum does not change. Why?

- The torque of the weight is zero
- The torque of the weight is perpendicular to the angular momentum
- The torque of the weight is parallel to the angular momentum
- The support exerts a torque that cancels that of the weight

## Explanation

In a time $dt$ the angular momentum changes by $d\vec{L} = \vec{\tau}\,dt$. Since $\vec{\tau}$ is perpendicular to $\vec{L}$, the increment is perpendicular to $\vec{L}$ and rotates it without changing its length. The torque is not zero, otherwise the axle would not turn, and the force of the support has zero torque about the support itself.
