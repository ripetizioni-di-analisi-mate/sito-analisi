Friction slowly reduces the spin of a top whose axis precesses. What happens to the precession angular speed?

- It decreases
- It stays the same
- It becomes zero
- It increases

## Explanation

The precession angular speed is $\Omega = \dfrac{Mgd}{I\,\omega}$, inversely proportional to the spin $\omega$. As $\omega$ decreases the precession becomes faster, until the approximation of a fast spin no longer holds and the top falls over.
