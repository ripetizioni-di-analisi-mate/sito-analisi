What is the SI unit of angular momentum?

- $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s^2}$

## Explanation

The magnitude $\ell = p\,r_\perp$ is the product of a momentum, in $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$, and a length, in $\mathrm{m}$, which gives $\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$. The newton meter is the unit of torque, which is the rate of change of angular momentum.
