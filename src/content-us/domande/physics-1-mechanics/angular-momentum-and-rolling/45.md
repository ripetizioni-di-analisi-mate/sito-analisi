A particle of momentum $\vec{p}$ is located by the position vector $\vec{r}$ with respect to an origin $O$. Which expression defines its angular momentum about $O$?

- $\vec{p} \times \vec{r}$
- $(\vec{r} \cdot \vec{p})\,\hat{k}$
- $\vec{r} \times \vec{p}$
- $\vec{r} \times \dfrac{d\vec{p}}{dt}$

## Explanation

The angular momentum is $\vec{\ell} = \vec{r} \times \vec{p}$, with the factors in this order. The product $\vec{p} \times \vec{r}$ has the opposite direction, because the vector product is anticommutative, and $\vec{r} \times \dfrac{d\vec{p}}{dt}$ is the torque of the resultant force.
