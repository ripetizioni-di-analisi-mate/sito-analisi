Seen from the ground, what is the path of a single point of the rim of a wheel that rolls without slipping on a straight road?

- A cycloid, with a cusp at each contact with the road
- A circle centered on the axle
- A straight line parallel to the road
- A smooth wave that never reaches the road

## Explanation

The point turns about the axle while the axle advances, and the composition of the two motions is a cycloid. At each contact with the road the point has zero velocity, hence the path has a cusp there. A circle centered on the axle is the path seen by an observer who moves with the axle.
