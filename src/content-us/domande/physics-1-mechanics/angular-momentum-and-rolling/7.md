At a given instant, a body rolling without slipping moves as if it were rotating about which axis, and with which angular speed?

- About its own axle, with angular speed $\omega$
- About the contact line, with angular speed $2\omega$
- About the contact line, with angular speed $\omega$
- About the line through its highest point, with angular speed $\omega$

## Explanation

The contact point has zero velocity, therefore the motion at that instant is a pure rotation about the contact line. The angular speed is the same $\omega$ as that of the rotation about the center, since the center, at distance $R$ from the contact line, moves at $\omega R = v_{\mathrm{cm}}$. The axle itself moves, so it cannot be the axis of a pure rotation.
