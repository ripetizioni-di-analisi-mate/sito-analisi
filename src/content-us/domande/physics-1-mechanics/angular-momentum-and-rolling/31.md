A homogeneous solid cylinder is released from rest on a plane inclined at $40^\circ$ and rolls without slipping. What is the speed of its center after $1.5\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $9.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4.7\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

With $\beta = \dfrac{1}{2}$ the acceleration of the center is constant and equal to
$$
a_{\mathrm{cm}} = \frac{g\sin\theta}{1 + \beta} = \frac{2}{3} \cdot 9.81 \cdot \sin 40^\circ\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 4.20\,\mathrm{m/s^2} ,
$$
hence, starting from rest, $v_{\mathrm{cm}} = a_{\mathrm{cm}}\,t = 4.20 \cdot 1.5\,\mathrm{m/s} = 6.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The value $9.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ uses $g\sin\theta$, the acceleration of a body sliding without friction, and $7.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ uses the cosine in place of the sine.
