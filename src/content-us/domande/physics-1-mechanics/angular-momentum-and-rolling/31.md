A homogeneous solid cylinder rolls without slipping down a plane inclined at $40^\circ$. What is the acceleration of its center?

- $6.31\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $5.01\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $4.20\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3.15\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

With $\beta = \dfrac{1}{2}$ the acceleration is
$$
a_{\mathrm{cm}} = \frac{g\sin\theta}{1 + \beta} = \frac{2}{3} \cdot 9.81 \cdot \sin 40^\circ\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 4.20\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
The value $3.15\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ uses $\beta = 1$, which belongs to a hoop.
