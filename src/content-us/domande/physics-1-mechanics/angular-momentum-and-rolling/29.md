A hoop of mass $0.50\,\mathrm{kg}$ rolls without slipping on a horizontal floor with kinetic energy $9.0\,\mathrm{J}$. What is the speed of its center?

- $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4.9\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

For a hoop $\beta = 1$, hence $K = \dfrac{1}{2}(1 + \beta)Mv_{\mathrm{cm}}^2 = Mv_{\mathrm{cm}}^2$ and
$$
v_{\mathrm{cm}} = \sqrt{\frac{K}{M}} = \sqrt{\frac{9.0}{0.50}}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 4.2\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The value $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ treats the whole energy as translational, and $4.9\,\mathrm{m/s}$ uses the moment of inertia of a solid cylinder.
