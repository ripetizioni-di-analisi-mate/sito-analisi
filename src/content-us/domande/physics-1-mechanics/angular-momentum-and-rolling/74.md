Two identical disks, each of moment of inertia $0.50\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$, turn about the same vertical axis in opposite senses, one at $10\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ and the other at $6.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. They are pressed together until they turn as one body. What is the final angular velocity?

- $2.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$, in the sense of the faster disk
- $8.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$, in the sense of the faster disk
- $2.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$, in the sense of the slower disk
- $4.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$, in the sense of the faster disk

## Explanation

Taking as positive the sense of the faster disk, the total angular momentum is $0.50 \cdot 10 - 0.50 \cdot 6.0 = 2.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$, and it is conserved. Dividing by the total moment of inertia $1.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ gives $\omega = +2.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. Adding the two angular momenta as if they had the same sign gives $8.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
