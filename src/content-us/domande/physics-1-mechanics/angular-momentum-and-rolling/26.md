A homogeneous solid sphere rolls without slipping and a block slides without friction, both released from rest at the same height. What is the ratio $\dfrac{v_{\mathrm{sphere}}}{v_{\mathrm{block}}}$ of their speeds at the bottom?

- $0.71$
- $0.85$
- $1.0$
- $0.63$

## Explanation

The sphere reaches $\sqrt{\dfrac{2gh}{1 + \beta}}$ with $\beta = \dfrac{2}{5}$, and the block reaches $\sqrt{2gh}$. The ratio is
$$
\sqrt{\frac{1}{1 + \beta}} = \sqrt{\frac{5}{7}} = 0.85 ,
$$
independent of the height. The value $0.71 = \dfrac{5}{7}$ omits the square root.
