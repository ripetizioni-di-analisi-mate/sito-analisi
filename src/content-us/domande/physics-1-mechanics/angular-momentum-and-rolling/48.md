A particle of mass $0.50\,\mathrm{kg}$ moves at $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ along a straight line whose distance from the point $O$ is $3.0\,\mathrm{m}$. At the instant considered the particle is $5.0\,\mathrm{m}$ from $O$. What is the magnitude of its angular momentum about $O$?

- $10\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $6.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $8.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$

## Explanation

The magnitude is $\ell = p\,r_\perp$, where the lever arm $r_\perp$ is the distance from $O$ to the line of motion, here $3.0\,\mathrm{m}$. Hence
$$
\ell = 0.50 \cdot 4.0 \cdot 3.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s} = 6.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s} .
$$
The distance $5.0\,\mathrm{m}$ would give the value only if $\vec{r}$ were perpendicular to $\vec{v}$.
