A wheel of radius $R$ rolls without slipping with center speed $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the speed of a point of the rim at height $1.5R$ above the ground?

- $6.9\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5.7\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The height of the rim point at the angle $\varphi$ from the contact point is $R(1 - \cos\varphi)$, which equals $1.5R$ for $\cos\varphi = -\dfrac{1}{2}$, that is $\varphi = 120^\circ$. The velocity of the points of a rolling body then gives
$$
v_P = 2\,v_{\mathrm{cm}}\sin\frac{\varphi}{2} = 2 \cdot 4.0\,\mathrm{m/s} \cdot \sin 60^\circ = 6.9\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
