A homogeneous solid cylinder rolls without slipping at constant speed on a horizontal floor, and no force acts on it other than the weight and the force of the floor. What is the friction at the contact?

- Zero
- Static friction directed backward
- Kinetic friction directed backward
- Static friction directed forward

## Explanation

The center does not accelerate, so the horizontal force on the cylinder is zero, and the angular speed is constant, so the torque about the center is zero. A friction force would produce both an acceleration and an angular acceleration, therefore it vanishes, and a rigid body keeps rolling at constant speed on a level floor without any friction.
