A wheel of radius $0.25\,\mathrm{m}$ rolls without slipping along a straight line and makes exactly $10$ complete turns. How far does its center travel?

- $2.50\,\mathrm{m}$
- $31.4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $7.85\,\mathrm{m}$
- $15.7\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

In one turn the whole circumference comes into contact with the ground, hence the center advances by $2\pi R$. The rotation angle is $\theta = 10 \cdot 2\pi\,\mathrm{rad}$, and $s = R\,\theta$ gives
$$
s = 0.25\,\mathrm{m} \cdot 20\pi = 15.7\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
