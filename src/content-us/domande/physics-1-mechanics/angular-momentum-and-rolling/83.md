A gyroscope wheel of moment of inertia $2.0 \times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ spins at $200\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. Its axle is horizontal and supported at one point, the mass is $0.50\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the center of mass is $4.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the support. How long does the axle take to complete one turn of precession?

- $2.0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.13\,\mathrm{s}$
- $13\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3.1\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The **precession angular speed** is
$$
\Omega = \frac{M g d}{I\,\omega} = \frac{0.50 \cdot 9.81 \cdot 0.040}{2.0 \times 10^{-3} \cdot 200}\,\mathrm{rad/s} = 0.49\,\mathrm{rad/s} ,
$$
hence one turn takes $\dfrac{2\pi}{\Omega} = 13\,\mathrm{s}$. The value $2.0\,\mathrm{s}$ is $\dfrac{1}{\Omega}$, without the factor $2\pi$, and $0.13\,\mathrm{s}$ follows from leaving the distance in centimeters.
