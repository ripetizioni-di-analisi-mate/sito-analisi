A ball of putty strikes and sticks to a rod hinged at one end on a fixed frictionless pivot. Which quantity of the system formed by the ball and the rod has the same value just before and just after the impact?

- The angular momentum about the pivot
- The linear momentum
- The kinetic energy
- Both the linear momentum and the angular momentum about the pivot

## Explanation

During the impact the pivot exerts an external force of unknown magnitude, so the linear momentum is not conserved. That force is applied at the pivot, hence its torque about the pivot is zero and the angular momentum about the pivot is conserved. The collision is perfectly inelastic, so kinetic energy is lost.
