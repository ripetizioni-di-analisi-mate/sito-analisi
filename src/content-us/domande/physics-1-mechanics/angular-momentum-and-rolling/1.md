A rigid body rolls without slipping on a fixed horizontal floor. What is the velocity of the point of the body that touches the floor?

- Equal to $v_{\mathrm{cm}}$ and directed backward
- Equal to $v_{\mathrm{cm}}$ and directed forward
- Zero at every instant
- Equal to $2v_{\mathrm{cm}}$ and directed forward

## Explanation

Rolling without slipping is defined by the condition that the contact point has zero velocity. In the sum $\vec{v}_P = \vec{v}_{\mathrm{cm}} + \vec{\omega} \times \vec{r}\,'$ the rotation gives the contact point the velocity $\omega R = v_{\mathrm{cm}}$ directed backward, which cancels the forward velocity of the center.
