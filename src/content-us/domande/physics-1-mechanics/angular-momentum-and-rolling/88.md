A gyroscope of mass $1.2\,\mathrm{kg}$, with moment of inertia $0.020\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ about its axle and center of mass $0.10\,\mathrm{m}$ from the support, is to precess at $1.5\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ with its axle horizontal. At what angular speed must its wheel spin?

- $4.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $88\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $39\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $26\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

Solving $\Omega = \dfrac{Mgd}{I\,\omega}$ for the spin gives
$$
\omega = \frac{M g d}{I\,\Omega} = \frac{1.2 \cdot 9.81 \cdot 0.10}{0.020 \cdot 1.5}\,\mathrm{rad/s} = 39\,\mathrm{rad/s} .
$$
Leaving out $g$ gives $4.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$, and multiplying by $\Omega$ instead of dividing gives $88\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
