A wheel rolls without slipping with center speed $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the speed of the point of the vertical spoke that lies midway between the axle and the top of the rim?

- $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $9.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The point lies at height $1.5R$ directly above the contact point, hence at distance $1.5R$ from the instantaneous axis. Its speed is
$$
\omega \cdot 1.5R = 1.5\,v_{\mathrm{cm}} = 9.0\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The value $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is only the speed of the rotation about the center, without the translation.
