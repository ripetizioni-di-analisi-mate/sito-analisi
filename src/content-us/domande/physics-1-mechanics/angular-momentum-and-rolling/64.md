A constant torque of $5.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$ acts for $4.0\,\mathrm{s}$ on a flywheel of moment of inertia $2.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$, initially at rest on a fixed axis. What is its angular momentum at the end?

- $10\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $40\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $1.3\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$

## Explanation

The **angular momentum theorem** with a constant torque gives
$$
\Delta L_z = \tau\,\Delta t = 5.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m} \cdot 4.0\,\mathrm{s} = 20\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s} ,
$$
independent of the moment of inertia, which only fixes the final angular speed $\omega = \dfrac{L_z}{I} = 10\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
