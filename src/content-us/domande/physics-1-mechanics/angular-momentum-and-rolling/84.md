The spin angular speed of a gyroscope is doubled, while its support and its mass distribution do not change. What happens to its precession angular speed?

- It doubles
- It does not change
- It becomes four times smaller
- It is halved

## Explanation

The torque $Mgd$ of the weight does not change, while the angular momentum $I\,\omega$ doubles. Since $\Omega = \dfrac{Mgd}{I\,\omega}$, the precession angular speed is halved, because the same torque turns a larger angular momentum more slowly.
