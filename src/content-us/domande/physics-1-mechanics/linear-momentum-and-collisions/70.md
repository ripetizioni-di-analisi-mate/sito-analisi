Where is the center of mass of a uniform thin ring?

- At any point of the ring, since all its points are equivalent
- At the center of the ring, where there is no material
- Nowhere, since there is no mass at the center
- At the midpoint of a radius

## Explanation

Each element of the ring has a symmetric element on the opposite side of the center, and with the origin at the center their contributions to $\displaystyle\int \vec{r}\,dm$ cancel. Hence the center of mass is the center of the ring, and the center of mass of a body need not lie inside its material.
