A cart of mass $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ moving at $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ hits an identical cart at rest on a straight horizontal frictionless track. Which pair of final velocities $(v_1',\ v_2')$ is impossible?

- $(0,\ 2.0)\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $(1.0,\ 1.0)\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $(0.50,\ 1.5)\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $(0,\ 3.0)\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The total momentum before the collision is $2.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$, while the pair $(0,\ 3.0)\,\mathrm{m/s}$ has $3.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$, therefore it violates conservation of momentum. The other pairs conserve momentum and do not increase the kinetic energy, and they describe an elastic, a perfectly inelastic and an inelastic collision.
