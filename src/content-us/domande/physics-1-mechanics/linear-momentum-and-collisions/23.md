Two carts of masses $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $3.0\,\mathrm{kg}$, initially at rest on a horizontal frictionless track, are pushed apart by a spring. What is the ratio $\dfrac{K_{\text{light}}}{K_{\text{heavy}}}$ of their kinetic energies after the release?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $1$
- $9$
- $3$

## Explanation

Conservation of momentum gives two momenta of equal magnitude $p$. Since $K = \dfrac{p^2}{2m}$, the kinetic energies are inversely proportional to the masses, so $\dfrac{K_{\text{light}}}{K_{\text{heavy}}} = \dfrac{3.0}{1.0} = 3$.
