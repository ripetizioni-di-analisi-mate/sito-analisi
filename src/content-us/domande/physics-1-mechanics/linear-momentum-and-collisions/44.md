Two bodies of putty move in the same sense along a line with different speeds and collide perfectly inelastically. Which change of the initial data leaves the kinetic energy lost unchanged?

- Doubling both velocities.
- Doubling the mass of one body.
- Adding the same velocity to both bodies.
- Reversing the velocity of one body.

## Explanation

The loss is $\dfrac{1}{2}\,\dfrac{m_1 m_2}{m_1 + m_2}\,(v_1 - v_2)^2$, which depends only on the masses and on the relative velocity. Adding the same velocity to both bodies leaves $v_1 - v_2$ unchanged, whereas doubling both velocities multiplies the loss by $4$ and reversing one velocity changes $|v_1 - v_2|$ into $|v_1 + v_2|$.
