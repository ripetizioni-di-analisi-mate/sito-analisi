A body of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ moving at $+3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ collides head-on with a body of mass $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ moving at $-3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ along the same line, and the two stick together. What is their common velocity?

- $+3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $+1.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-1.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Conservation of momentum gives $V = \dfrac{2.0 \cdot 3.0 + 1.0 \cdot (-3.0)}{3.0}\,\mathrm{m/s} = +1.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The two bodies have equal speeds and different masses, hence their momenta do not cancel and the pair moves in the sense of the heavier body.
