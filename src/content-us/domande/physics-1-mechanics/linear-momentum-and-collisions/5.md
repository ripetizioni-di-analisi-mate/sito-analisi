Newton's second law in momentum form reads $\vec{F}_{\text{net}} = \dfrac{d\vec{p}}{dt}$. Under which condition does it reduce to $\vec{F}_{\text{net}} = m\vec{a}$?

- The velocity of the body is constant.
- The net force is constant.
- The mass of the body is constant.
- The motion is along a straight line.

## Explanation

Differentiating $\vec{p} = m\vec{v}$ gives $\dfrac{d\vec{p}}{dt} = m\,\dfrac{d\vec{v}}{dt} = m\vec{a}$ only when $m$ does not depend on time. A constant velocity gives $\vec{F}_{\text{net}} = \vec{0}$, which is a special case of the law and not the condition for the reduction.
