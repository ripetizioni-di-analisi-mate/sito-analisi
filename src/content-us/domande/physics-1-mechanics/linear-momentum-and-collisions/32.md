A ball is dropped from a height of $1.25\,\mathrm{m}$ onto a floor, and the coefficient of restitution between ball and floor is $0.80$. Air resistance is negligible. To what height does the ball rebound?

- $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.51\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

From $e = \sqrt{\dfrac{h_2}{h_1}}$ it follows that $h_2 = e^2 h_1 = 0.64 \cdot 1.25\,\mathrm{m} = 0.80\,\mathrm{m}$. Multiplying the height by $e$ instead of $e^2$ treats the coefficient as a ratio of energies, while it is a ratio of speeds.
