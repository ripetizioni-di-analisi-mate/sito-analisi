A ball of mass $0.50\,\mathrm{kg}$ and a ball of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ have the same kinetic energy. What is the ratio $\dfrac{p_{\text{heavy}}}{p_{\text{light}}}$ of the magnitudes of their momenta?

- $2.0$
- $4.0$
- $0.50$
- $1.0$

## Explanation

From $p = \sqrt{2mK}$ with the same $K$, the momentum is proportional to $\sqrt{m}$. Therefore $\dfrac{p_{\text{heavy}}}{p_{\text{light}}} = \sqrt{\dfrac{2.0}{0.50}} = \sqrt{4.0} = 2.0$.
