A car of mass $1\,500\,\mathrm{kg}$ moving at $+10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ collides head-on with a truck of mass $3\,000\,\mathrm{kg}$ moving at $-6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and the two remain locked together. What is their common velocity just after the collision?

- $+0.67\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $+7.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-0.67\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $+2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Conservation of momentum gives $V = \dfrac{1\,500 \cdot 10 + 3\,000 \cdot (-6.0)}{4\,500}\,\mathrm{m/s} = \dfrac{-3\,000}{4\,500}\,\mathrm{m/s} \approx -0.67\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The truck has the larger momentum in magnitude, hence the wreck moves in its sense.
