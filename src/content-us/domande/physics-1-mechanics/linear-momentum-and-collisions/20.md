A shell is fired over level ground and explodes in flight into several fragments. Air resistance is negligible. From the launch until the first fragment lands, which component of the total momentum of the shell and its fragments is constant?

- Only the vertical component.
- Both components.
- Only the horizontal component.
- Neither component.

## Explanation

The only external force is the weight, which is vertical. Therefore the horizontal component of the external resultant vanishes and $P_x$ is constant, whereas the weight changes $P_y$ at the rate $Mg$.
