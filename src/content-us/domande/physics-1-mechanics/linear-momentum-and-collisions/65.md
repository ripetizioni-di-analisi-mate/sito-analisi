Two pucks of equal mass collide on a horizontal frictionless surface, the second one initially at rest. After the collision both move, and the angle between their velocities is $75^\circ$. What happened to the total kinetic energy?

- It stayed the same.
- It decreased.
- It increased.
- Nothing can be said without the speeds.

## Explanation

With equal masses, momentum conservation gives $\vec{v}_1 = \vec{v}_1\,' + \vec{v}_2\,'$, hence $v_1^2 = v_1'^2 + v_2'^2 + 2v_1'v_2'\cos 75^\circ$. The last term is positive, therefore $v_1'^2 + v_2'^2 < v_1^2$ and the kinetic energy decreased; it would be unchanged only for an angle of $90^\circ$.
