A ball of mass $0.20\,\mathrm{kg}$ hits a wall perpendicularly at $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and rebounds along the same line at $8.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the magnitude of the impulse exerted by the wall on the ball?

- $0.40\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$
- $3.6\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$

## Explanation

With the $x$ axis towards the wall, $v_{1x} = +10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_{2x} = -8.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$, hence $J_x = m\,(v_{2x} - v_{1x}) = 0.20\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot (-18\,\mathrm{m/s}) = -3.6\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$. The magnitude is $3.6\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$; the difference of the two speeds ignores the reversal of the sense of motion.
