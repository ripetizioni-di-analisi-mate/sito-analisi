A ball is dropped from a height of $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ onto a hard floor and rebounds to a height of $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$. Air resistance is negligible. What is the coefficient of restitution between ball and floor?

- $0.25$
- $0.75$
- $0.50$
- $2.0$

## Explanation

The speeds just before and just after the contact are $\sqrt{2gh_1}$ and $\sqrt{2gh_2}$, hence $e = \sqrt{\dfrac{h_2}{h_1}} = \sqrt{\dfrac{0.50}{2.0}} = 0.50$. The ratio of the heights, $0.25$, is the ratio of the kinetic energies, which equals $e^2$.
