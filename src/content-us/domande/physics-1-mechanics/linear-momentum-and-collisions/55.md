In a ballistic pendulum a bullet embeds itself in a hanging block, which then swings up. Which conservation laws hold for the impact and for the swing?

- Mechanical energy for both.
- Momentum for both.
- Mechanical energy for the impact, momentum for the swing.
- Momentum for the impact, mechanical energy for the swing.

## Explanation

The impact is perfectly inelastic, so kinetic energy is not conserved, while the external forces are vertical and the contact is short, so the horizontal momentum is conserved. During the swing the tension and the weight change the momentum, whereas only the weight does work, hence mechanical energy is conserved.
