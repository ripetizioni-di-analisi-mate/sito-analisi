Under which condition is the total linear momentum of a system of particles constant?

- No internal forces act between the particles.
- The total kinetic energy of the system is constant.
- All the forces acting on the particles are conservative.
- The resultant of the external forces on the system is zero.

## Explanation

Summing Newton's second law over the particles gives $\dfrac{d\vec{P}}{dt} = \vec{F}^{\text{ext}}$, because the internal forces cancel in pairs. Hence $\vec{P}$ is constant whenever the external resultant vanishes, whatever the internal forces and the kinetic energy do.
