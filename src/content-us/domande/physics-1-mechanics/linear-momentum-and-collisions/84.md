A firecracker in free fall explodes into many fragments. Air resistance is negligible. What is the acceleration of the center of mass of the fragments just after the explosion?

- Equal to $\vec{g}$
- Zero
- Directed downwards, with magnitude larger than $g$
- Dependent on the directions of the fragments

## Explanation

The forces of the explosion are internal, and the only external force on the fragments is their total weight $M\vec{g}$. Hence $\vec{a}_{\text{cm}} = \vec{g}$ before, during and after the explosion, until a fragment reaches the ground.
