A puck moving at $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ collides elastically with an identical puck at rest, and the first puck is deflected by $60^\circ$ from its original direction. What is the speed of the second puck after the collision?

- $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

With equal masses the relations $\vec{v}_1 = \vec{v}_1\,' + \vec{v}_2\,'$ and $\vec{v}_1\,' \perp \vec{v}_2\,'$ make $\vec{v}_1$ the hypotenuse of a right triangle. The first puck leaves at $v_1\cos 60^\circ = 3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the second at $v_1\sin 60^\circ \approx 5.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
