A body at rest on a horizontal frictionless plane explodes into three fragments. A fragment of $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ moves at $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ along the positive $x$ axis, a fragment of $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ moves at $8.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ along the positive $y$ axis, and the third fragment has mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$. What is the speed of the third fragment?

- $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $7.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The total momentum stays zero, so the third fragment carries $\vec{p}_3 = -(6.0,\ 8.0)\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$, of magnitude $10\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$. Its speed is $\dfrac{10}{2.0}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$, in the direction opposite to the sum of the other two momenta.
