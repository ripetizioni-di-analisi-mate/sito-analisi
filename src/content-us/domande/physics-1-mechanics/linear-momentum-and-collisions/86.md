A rocket expels $30\,\mathrm{kg/s}$ of gas at $2.0\,\mathrm{km/s}$ relative to itself. What is the magnitude of its thrust?

- $6.0 \cdot 10^{1}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $6.0 \cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $3.0 \cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1.2 \cdot 10^{5}\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The thrust is $F_{\text{thrust}} = u\left|\dfrac{dm}{dt}\right| = 2.0 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{m/s} \cdot 30\,\mathrm{kg/s} = 6.0 \cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{N}$. The exhaust speed enters in $\mathrm{m/s}$, and the value $2.0$ in $\mathrm{km/s}$ gives a result $1\,000$ times too small.
