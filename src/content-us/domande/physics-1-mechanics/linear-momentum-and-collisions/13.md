A ball moving along the positive $x$ axis is caught and stopped by a glove. Let $J_{\text{ball}}$ be the $x$ component of the impulse exerted by the glove on the ball and $J_{\text{glove}}$ the $x$ component of the impulse exerted by the ball on the glove. Which statement is correct?

- $J_{\text{ball}} < 0$ and $J_{\text{glove}} = J_{\text{ball}}$
- $J_{\text{ball}} > 0$ and $J_{\text{glove}} = -J_{\text{ball}}$
- $J_{\text{ball}} < 0$ and $J_{\text{glove}} = -J_{\text{ball}}$
- $J_{\text{ball}} = 0$ and $J_{\text{glove}} = 0$

## Explanation

The momentum of the ball goes from a positive value to zero, hence $J_{\text{ball}} = \Delta p_x < 0$. By Newton's third law the two forces are opposite at every instant and act for the same time, therefore the two impulses are opposite.
