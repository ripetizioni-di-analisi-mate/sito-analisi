A neutron collides head-on and elastically with a helium nucleus at rest whose mass is $4$ times that of the neutron. What fraction of the kinetic energy of the neutron is transferred to the nucleus?

- $0.36$
- $0.64$
- $0.16$
- $0.40$

## Explanation

The fraction transferred to a target at rest is $\dfrac{4m_1m_2}{(m_1 + m_2)^2} = \dfrac{4 \cdot 1 \cdot 4}{5^2} = 0.64$. The speed of the nucleus after the collision is $\dfrac{2}{5}$ of the initial speed of the neutron, and the ratio of the kinetic energies also contains the ratio $4$ of the masses.
