A person of mass $60\,\mathrm{kg}$ walks $4.0\,\mathrm{m}$ along a boat of mass $140\,\mathrm{kg}$ that floats at rest on still water. The resistance of the water is negligible. How far does the person move relative to the water?

- $4.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.8\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The center of mass of person and boat stays at rest, hence $m\,\Delta x_p + M\,\Delta x_b = 0$, while $\Delta x_p - \Delta x_b = 4.0\,\mathrm{m}$. Solving, $\Delta x_b = -\dfrac{60 \cdot 4.0}{200}\,\mathrm{m} = -1.2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $\Delta x_p = 4.0\,\mathrm{m} - 1.2\,\mathrm{m} = 2.8\,\mathrm{m}$.
