In a graph of force against time, a force pulse is a triangle: the force grows linearly from $0$ to $400\,\mathrm{N}$ in $2.0\,\mathrm{ms}$ and then decreases linearly to $0$ in the following $3.0\,\mathrm{ms}$. What is the magnitude of the impulse?

- $2.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$
- $1.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$
- $0.40\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$
- $0.60\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$

## Explanation

The impulse is the area under the graph, which is a triangle with base $5.0\,\mathrm{ms}$ and height $400\,\mathrm{N}$. Hence $J = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 5.0 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{s} \cdot 400\,\mathrm{N} = 1.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$, and both the rising and the falling parts of the pulse contribute.
