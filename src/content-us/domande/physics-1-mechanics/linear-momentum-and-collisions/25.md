A body of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ moves at $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ along the positive $x$ axis and a body of mass $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ moves at $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ along the positive $y$ axis. What is the magnitude of the total momentum of the two bodies?

- $10\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $7.2\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $5.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$

## Explanation

The total momentum is the vector sum $\vec{P} = (6.0,\ 4.0)\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$. Its magnitude is $\sqrt{6.0^2 + 4.0^2}\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s} \approx 7.2\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$, which is smaller than the sum of the two magnitudes because the momenta are perpendicular.
