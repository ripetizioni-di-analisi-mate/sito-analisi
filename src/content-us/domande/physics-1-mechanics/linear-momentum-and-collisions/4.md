Two bodies of masses $m$ and $4m$ have momenta of the same magnitude. What is the ratio $\dfrac{K_{\text{light}}}{K_{\text{heavy}}}$ of their kinetic energies?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $1$
- $4$
- $2$

## Explanation

The kinetic energy in terms of the momentum is $K = \dfrac{p^2}{2m}$. With the same $p$ the kinetic energy is inversely proportional to the mass, hence $\dfrac{K_{\text{light}}}{K_{\text{heavy}}} = \dfrac{4m}{m} = 4$.
