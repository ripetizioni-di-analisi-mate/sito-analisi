Which two-dimensional collision between two bodies is completely determined by the initial velocities and conservation of momentum alone?

- A perfectly inelastic collision.
- An elastic collision.
- Every collision, since momentum has two components.
- No collision, since the kinetic energy is always needed.

## Explanation

In a perfectly inelastic collision the two bodies share one final velocity, whose two components are fixed by the two scalar equations of momentum conservation. In every other case there are four unknown components, and two equations are not sufficient.
