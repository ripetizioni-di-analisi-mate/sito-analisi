A body moving along the $x$ axis strikes a body at rest on a horizontal frictionless plane. After the collision the $y$ component of the momentum of the first body is $+0.30\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$. What is the $y$ component of the momentum of the second body?

- $-0.30\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $+0.30\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $-0.60\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$

## Explanation

Before the collision both momenta lie along the $x$ axis, so the total $P_y$ is zero. Conservation of momentum keeps $P_y = 0$ after the collision, hence the second body has $p_{2y}' = -0.30\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$.
