A body moving at speed $v$ collides head-on and elastically with an identical body at rest. What happens after the collision?

- Both bodies move at $\dfrac{v}{2}$.
- The first body rebounds at $v$ and the second stays at rest.
- The first body stops and the second moves at $v$.
- Both bodies move at $v$ in the original sense.

## Explanation

For $m_1 = m_2$ and $v_2 = 0$ the elastic formulas give $v_1' = \dfrac{m_1 - m_2}{m_1 + m_2}\,v = 0$ and $v_2' = \dfrac{2m_1}{m_1 + m_2}\,v = v$. Both bodies at $\dfrac{v}{2}$ is the result of a perfectly inelastic collision, which loses half of the kinetic energy.
