In a two-dimensional collision of two bodies that do not stick together, the four components of the final velocities are unknown. How many data besides conservation of momentum are needed to determine them?

- One
- None
- Four
- Two

## Explanation

Conservation of momentum in a plane gives two scalar equations, one per axis, for four unknowns. Two further data are therefore required, such as the two final directions, or one direction together with the condition that the collision is elastic.
