Which unit is equivalent to the unit of impulse, $\mathrm{N\cdot s}$?

- $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s^2}$
- $\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $\mathrm{J/s}$

## Explanation

Since $1\,\mathrm{N} = 1\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s^2}$, multiplying by one second gives $1\,\mathrm{N\cdot s} = 1\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$, which is the unit of momentum. This agrees with the impulse-momentum theorem, which equates an impulse to a change of momentum.
