A body of mass $4.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ moving east at $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ collides with a body of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ moving north at $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and the two stick together. What is the speed of the pair after the collision?

- $2.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.7\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The total momentum is $(8.0,\ 6.0)\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$, of magnitude $10\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$. Dividing by the total mass $6.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ gives $V \approx 1.7\,\mathrm{m/s}$, which is smaller than $\dfrac{8.0 + 6.0}{6.0}\,\mathrm{m/s}$ because the two momenta are perpendicular.
