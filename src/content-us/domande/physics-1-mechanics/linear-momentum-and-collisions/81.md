A diver leaves the board and rotates in the air, bending and stretching the body. Air resistance is negligible. What is the path of the center of mass of the diver?

- A straight line
- A path that depends on how the diver moves the limbs
- A parabola
- A circle about the hands of the diver

## Explanation

The only external force is the weight $M\vec{g}$, hence $\vec{a}_{\text{cm}} = \vec{g}$ and the center of mass moves as a projectile. The motion of the limbs is due to internal forces, which change the positions of the parts relative to the center of mass but not the path of the center of mass.
