A person of mass $60\,\mathrm{kg}$ moving horizontally at $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ jumps onto a cart of mass $20\,\mathrm{kg}$ at rest on a horizontal frictionless floor, and stays on it. What is the speed of the cart with the person on it?

- $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The collision is perfectly inelastic, so conservation of horizontal momentum gives $V = \dfrac{60\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}}{80\,\mathrm{kg}} = 3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Kinetic energy is not conserved in this collision; imposing its conservation would give $3.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
