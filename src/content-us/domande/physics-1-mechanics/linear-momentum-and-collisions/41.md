A body collides with an identical body at rest, and the two stick together. What fraction of the initial kinetic energy is lost?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The common velocity is $V = \dfrac{v}{2}$, so the final kinetic energy is $\dfrac{1}{2}(2m)\,\dfrac{v^2}{4} = \dfrac{1}{4}mv^2$, which is half of the initial value $\dfrac{1}{2}mv^2$. The fraction lost is therefore $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
