A ball falls towards the Earth, and no other body is involved. For which choice of system is the total momentum conserved?

- The ball alone.
- The ball and the Earth together.
- The Earth alone.
- No choice of system.

## Explanation

For the ball alone the gravitational force of the Earth is external, so the momentum of the ball changes, and the same holds for the Earth alone. For the system formed by the ball and the Earth the two gravitational forces are internal and opposite, therefore the total momentum is constant.
