The mass of the Earth is $81$ times the mass of the Moon, and the distance between their centers is $3.84 \cdot 10^{5}\,\mathrm{km}$. How far from the center of the Earth is the center of mass of the Earth-Moon system?

- $3.8 \cdot 10^{5}\,\mathrm{km}$
- $1.9 \cdot 10^{5}\,\mathrm{km}$
- $4.7 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{km}$
- $4.7 \cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{km}$

## Explanation

With the origin at the center of the Earth, $x_{\text{cm}} = \dfrac{M_{\text{Moon}}\,d}{M_{\text{Earth}} + M_{\text{Moon}}} = \dfrac{d}{82} = \dfrac{3.84 \cdot 10^{5}}{82}\,\mathrm{km} \approx 4.7 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{km}$. This distance is smaller than the radius of the Earth, about $6.4 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{km}$, hence the center of mass lies inside the Earth.
