A body of mass $3.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ moving at $+4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ collides head-on and elastically with a body of mass $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ at rest. What is the velocity of the first body after the collision?

- $+2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $+4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The elastic formula gives $v_1' = \dfrac{m_1 - m_2}{m_1 + m_2}\,v_1 = \dfrac{2.0}{4.0} \cdot 4.0\,\mathrm{m/s} = +2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The projectile is heavier than the target, therefore it continues in the original sense; it rebounds only when $m_1 < m_2$.
