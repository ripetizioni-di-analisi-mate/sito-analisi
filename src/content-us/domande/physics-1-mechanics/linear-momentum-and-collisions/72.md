A uniform disk of radius $R$ has its center at the origin. A circular hole of radius $\dfrac{R}{2}$ is cut out, with its center at $x = \dfrac{R}{2}$. What is the $x$ coordinate of the center of mass of the remaining piece?

- $-\dfrac{R}{6}$
- $+\dfrac{R}{6}$
- $-\dfrac{R}{8}$
- $-\dfrac{R}{2}$

## Explanation

The hole is treated as a part of negative mass: its area is $\dfrac{1}{4}$ of the area of the disk, so its mass is $\dfrac{M}{4}$ and the remaining mass is $\dfrac{3M}{4}$. Then $x_{\text{cm}} = \dfrac{M \cdot 0 - \dfrac{M}{4} \cdot \dfrac{R}{2}}{\dfrac{3M}{4}} = -\dfrac{R}{6}$, on the side opposite to the hole.
