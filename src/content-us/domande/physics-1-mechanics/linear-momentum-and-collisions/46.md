A very heavy body moving at speed $v$ collides head-on and elastically with a light body at rest, with $m_1 \gg m_2$. What is approximately the speed of the light body after the collision?

- $2v$
- $v$
- $\dfrac{v}{2}$
- $4v$

## Explanation

With $v_2 = 0$ the elastic formula gives $v_2' = \dfrac{2m_1}{m_1 + m_2}\,v$, and the fraction tends to $2$ for $m_1 \gg m_2$. In the frame of the heavy body, which keeps its velocity, the light body approaches at $v$ and rebounds at $v$, so its speed relative to the ground is $2v$.
