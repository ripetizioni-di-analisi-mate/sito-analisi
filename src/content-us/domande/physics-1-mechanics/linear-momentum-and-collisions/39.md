In an inelastic collision the total kinetic energy of the two bodies decreases. What becomes of the missing kinetic energy?

- It becomes momentum of the bodies.
- It becomes gravitational potential energy of the bodies.
- It becomes kinetic energy of the center of mass.
- It becomes internal energy and deformation of the bodies.

## Explanation

Energy is conserved, while kinetic energy is not: the forces during the contact deform the bodies and warm them. Momentum is a different quantity, measured in different units and conserved by itself, so it cannot absorb energy, and the kinetic energy of the center of mass is fixed by the total momentum.
