Two carts collide on a horizontal frictionless track. How does the velocity of their center of mass after the collision compare with its value before?

- It is smaller if the collision is inelastic.
- It is the same, whatever the type of collision.
- It becomes zero if the collision is perfectly inelastic.
- It is larger if the collision is elastic.

## Explanation

The system of the two carts is isolated along the track, so its total momentum is constant. Since $\vec{P} = M\vec{v}_{\text{cm}}$ with constant $M$, the velocity of the center of mass is the same before and after any collision.
