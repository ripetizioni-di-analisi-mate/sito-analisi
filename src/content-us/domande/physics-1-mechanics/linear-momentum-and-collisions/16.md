A puck of mass $0.40\,\mathrm{kg}$ moving at $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ along the positive $x$ axis receives an impulse, after which it moves at $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ along the positive $y$ axis. What is the magnitude of the impulse?

- $0.40\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$
- $2.8\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$
- $1.6\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$

## Explanation

The impulse equals the change of momentum, $\vec{J} = m\,(\vec{v}_2 - \vec{v}_1) = 0.40\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot (-3.0,\ 4.0)\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Its magnitude is $0.40 \cdot \sqrt{3.0^2 + 4.0^2}\,\mathrm{N\cdot s} = 2.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$, since momenta are vectors and their difference is not obtained from the speeds alone.
