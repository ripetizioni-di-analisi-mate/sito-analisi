A body of mass $m_1$ collides head-on and elastically with a body of mass $m_2$ at rest. Under which condition does the first body reverse its sense of motion?

- $m_1 > m_2$
- $m_1 = m_2$
- $m_1 < m_2$
- For any masses

## Explanation

The first body leaves with $v_1' = \dfrac{m_1 - m_2}{m_1 + m_2}\,v_1$. This component has the sign opposite to $v_1$ exactly when $m_1 - m_2 < 0$, and for $m_1 = m_2$ the first body stops.
