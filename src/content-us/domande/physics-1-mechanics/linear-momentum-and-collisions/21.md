A cannon of mass $800\,\mathrm{kg}$ rests on horizontal frictionless rails and fires a shell of mass $10\,\mathrm{kg}$. The velocity of the shell relative to the ground has magnitude $400\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and makes $30.0^\circ$ with the horizontal. What is the recoil speed of the cannon along the rails?

- $4.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The rails exert a vertical force during the firing, so only the horizontal momentum is conserved. From $0 = mv\cos 30.0^\circ - MV$ it follows that $V = \dfrac{10\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 400\,\mathrm{m/s} \cdot \cos 30.0^\circ}{800\,\mathrm{kg}} \approx 4.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
