A rocket of mass $5\,000\,\mathrm{kg}$ stands on the launch pad, and its engines develop a thrust of $7.5 \cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{N}$. What is its acceleration at liftoff?

- $15\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $25\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $5.2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2.6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Newton's second law with the weight included gives $a = \dfrac{F_{\text{thrust}} - mg}{m} = \dfrac{7.5 \cdot 10^{4} - 5\,000 \cdot 9.81}{5\,000}\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \approx 5.2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Omitting the weight gives $15\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
