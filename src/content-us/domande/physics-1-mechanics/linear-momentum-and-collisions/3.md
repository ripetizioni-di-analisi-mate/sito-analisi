A car of mass $1\,200\,\mathrm{kg}$ travels at $72\,\mathrm{km/h}$. What is the magnitude of its momentum?

- $8.6 \cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $2.4 \cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $3.1 \cdot 10^{5}\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $1.2 \cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$

## Explanation

The speed in SI units is $\dfrac{72}{3.6}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 20\,\mathrm{m/s}$, therefore $p = mv = 1\,200\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 20\,\mathrm{m/s} = 2.4 \cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$. Using the speed in $\mathrm{km/h}$ without conversion gives a value $3.6$ times too large.
