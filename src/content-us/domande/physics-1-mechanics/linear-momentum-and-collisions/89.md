A rocket in free space burns fuel at a constant rate with constant exhaust speed. How does its acceleration change during the burn?

- It decreases.
- It stays constant.
- It increases.
- It is zero, since no external force acts.

## Explanation

The thrust $u\left|\dfrac{dm}{dt}\right|$ is constant, while the mass $m$ of the rocket decreases as the fuel is consumed. Since $m\,\dfrac{dv}{dt} = u\left|\dfrac{dm}{dt}\right|$, the acceleration grows during the burn.
