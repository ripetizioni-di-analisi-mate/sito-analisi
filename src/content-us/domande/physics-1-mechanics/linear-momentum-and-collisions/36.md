A collision along a line has coefficient of restitution $e = 0$. How is it classified?

- Elastic.
- A collision in which both bodies stop.
- Inelastic, with the bodies separating after the contact.
- Perfectly inelastic.

## Explanation

With $e = 0$ the relative velocity after the collision vanishes, so the two bodies move together, which is the definition of a perfectly inelastic collision. They stop only if the total momentum is zero.
