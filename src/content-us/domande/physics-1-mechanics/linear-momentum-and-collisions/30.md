Which statement defines a perfectly inelastic collision between two bodies?

- Both bodies come to rest after the collision.
- The total momentum after the collision is zero.
- The total kinetic energy is conserved while the momentum is not.
- The two bodies move with a common velocity after the collision.

## Explanation

In a perfectly inelastic collision the bodies stick together and move with one common velocity, which conservation of momentum fixes at $\dfrac{m_1\vec{v}_1 + m_2\vec{v}_2}{m_1 + m_2}$. This velocity vanishes only when the initial total momentum is zero, so in general the bodies keep moving and part of the kinetic energy remains.
