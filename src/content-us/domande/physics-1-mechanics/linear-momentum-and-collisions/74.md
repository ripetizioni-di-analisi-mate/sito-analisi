A system of particles has total mass $10\,\mathrm{kg}$. One particle of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ is moved by $5.0\,\mathrm{m}$ along the positive $x$ axis, while the others stay where they are. By how much does the center of mass move?

- $5.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.0\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Only one term of $\sum_i m_i x_i$ changes, by $2.0\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 5.0\,\mathrm{m} = 10\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m}$. Dividing by the total mass gives $\Delta x_{\text{cm}} = \dfrac{10\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m}}{10\,\mathrm{kg}} = 1.0\,\mathrm{m}$ along the positive $x$ axis.
