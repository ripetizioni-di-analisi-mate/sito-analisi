Two given bodies approach each other along a line with given velocities. Which type of collision dissipates the largest amount of kinetic energy?

- An elastic collision.
- An inelastic collision with $e = 0.50$.
- A perfectly inelastic collision.
- All types dissipate the same amount.

## Explanation

The kinetic energy $\dfrac{1}{2}Mv_{\text{cm}}^2$ of the motion of the center of mass cannot change, because momentum is conserved, so only the kinetic energy relative to the center of mass can be dissipated. A perfectly inelastic collision removes all of it, since both bodies end with the velocity of the center of mass, whereas an elastic collision dissipates none.
