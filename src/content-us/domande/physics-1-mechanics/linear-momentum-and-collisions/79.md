Two blocks of total mass $5.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ on a horizontal frictionless surface are pushed apart by a compressed spring, which exerts a force of $30\,\mathrm{N}$ on each block. The only other horizontal force is an external force of $10\,\mathrm{N}$ toward east. What is the acceleration of the center of mass?

- $8.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ east
- $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ east
- $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ east
- $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ east

## Explanation

By Newton's second law for a system $\vec{F}^{\text{ext}} = M\vec{a}_{\text{cm}}$, and the spring forces are internal. Therefore $a_{\text{cm}} = \dfrac{10\,\mathrm{N}}{5.0\,\mathrm{kg}} = 2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ toward east, however the spring moves the two blocks relative to each other.
