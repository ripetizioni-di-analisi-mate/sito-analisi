Two bodies collide on a horizontal frictionless surface. Which quantity is necessarily the same immediately before and immediately after the collision?

- The total kinetic energy of the two bodies.
- The momentum of each body.
- The kinetic energy of each body.
- The total momentum of the two bodies.

## Explanation

The forces between the bodies are internal and the external forces balance, therefore the total momentum is conserved in every collision. The total kinetic energy is conserved only in elastic collisions, and each body exchanges momentum with the other, so the individual momenta change.
