In a crash the momentum of a passenger changes by the same amount with or without an airbag. The airbag increases the stopping time from $10\,\mathrm{ms}$ to $50\,\mathrm{ms}$. How does the magnitude of the average force on the passenger change?

- It becomes five times as large.
- It becomes one fifth as large.
- It does not change.
- It becomes one twenty-fifth as large.

## Explanation

The average force is $F_{\text{av}} = \dfrac{|\Delta p|}{\Delta t}$, and $|\Delta p|$ is the same in both cases. Multiplying $\Delta t$ by $5$ therefore divides the average force by $5$.
