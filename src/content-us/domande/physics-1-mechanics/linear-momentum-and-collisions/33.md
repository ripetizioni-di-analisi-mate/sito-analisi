Two bodies move along a line. Before the collision their velocity components are $v_1 = +5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_2 = 0$; after it they are $v_1' = +1.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_2' = +3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the coefficient of restitution?

- $0.40$
- $0.60$
- $0.80$
- $0.20$

## Explanation

The relative speed is $|v_2 - v_1| = 5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ before the collision and $|v_2' - v_1'| = 2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ after it. Therefore $e = \dfrac{2.0}{5.0} = 0.40$, since the coefficient compares relative velocities and not the speed of one body.
