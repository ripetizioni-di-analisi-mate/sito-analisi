A particle of mass $m$ has kinetic energy $K$. What is the magnitude of its linear momentum?

- $\sqrt{\dfrac{2K}{m}}$
- $2mK$
- $\sqrt{mK}$
- $\sqrt{2mK}$

## Explanation

Since $K = \dfrac{1}{2}mv^2$ and $p = mv$, eliminating $v$ gives $K = \dfrac{p^2}{2m}$, hence $p = \sqrt{2mK}$. The expression $\sqrt{\dfrac{2K}{m}}$ is the speed of the particle.
