A body at rest on a horizontal frictionless plane explodes into two fragments that slide on the plane. Which statement about the system of the two fragments is correct?

- The total momentum remains zero and the total kinetic energy increases.
- The total momentum and the total kinetic energy both remain zero.
- The total momentum and the total kinetic energy both increase.
- The total momentum increases and the total kinetic energy remains zero.

## Explanation

The forces of the explosion are internal, so the total momentum keeps its initial value, which is zero. The same internal forces do positive work on the fragments, converting chemical energy into kinetic energy, therefore the total kinetic energy increases from zero.
