A body of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ moving east at $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ collides with a body of mass $3.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ moving north at $1.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and the two stick together. In which direction does the pair move after the collision?

- $27^\circ$ north of east
- $63^\circ$ north of east
- $18^\circ$ north of east
- $45^\circ$ north of east

## Explanation

With the $x$ axis toward east and the $y$ axis toward north, the total momentum is $(6.0,\ 3.0)\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$. The common velocity has the direction of $\vec{P}$, hence $\tan\theta = \dfrac{3.0}{6.0}$ and $\theta \approx 27^\circ$ north of east; the ratio of the speeds, $\dfrac{1.0}{3.0}$, ignores the masses.
