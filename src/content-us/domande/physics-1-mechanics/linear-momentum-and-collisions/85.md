A rocket far from every other body expels gas at speed $u$ relative to itself. What fraction of its initial mass must be fuel for the rocket to gain a speed equal to $u$?

- $0.50$
- $0.37$
- $0.86$
- $0.63$

## Explanation

From $\Delta v = u\ln\dfrac{m_i}{m_f} = u$ it follows that $\dfrac{m_i}{m_f} = e \approx 2.718$, so the final mass is $\dfrac{m_i}{e} \approx 0.37\,m_i$. The fuel is the remaining $1 - 0.37 = 0.63$ of the initial mass.
