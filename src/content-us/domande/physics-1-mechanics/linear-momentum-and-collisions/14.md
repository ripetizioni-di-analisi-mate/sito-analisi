A person of mass $60\,\mathrm{kg}$ lands vertically at $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$, bends the knees and comes to rest in $0.20\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the magnitude of the average force exerted by the floor during the landing?

- $1.2 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $6.1 \cdot 10^{2}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1.8 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $2.4 \cdot 10^{2}\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

With the $y$ axis upwards, the impulse-momentum theorem gives $(N_{\text{av}} - mg)\,\Delta t = 0 - m\,(-v)$, therefore $N_{\text{av}} = \dfrac{mv}{\Delta t} + mg = 1\,200\,\mathrm{N} + 589\,\mathrm{N} \approx 1.8 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{N}$. The weight is not negligible here, because the contact lasts much longer than an impact between hard bodies.
