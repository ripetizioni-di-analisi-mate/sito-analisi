A neutron of mass $m$ collides head-on and elastically with a nucleus of mass $M$ at rest. For which value of $M$ does the neutron lose the largest fraction of its kinetic energy?

- $M = 2m$
- $M = m$
- $M \gg m$
- $M \ll m$

## Explanation

The fraction transferred is $\dfrac{4mM}{(m + M)^2}$, which equals $1$ for $M = m$ and is smaller than $1$ for every other ratio. For $M = 2m$ it is $\dfrac{8}{9}$, and it tends to $0$ when $M$ is much larger or much smaller than $m$.
