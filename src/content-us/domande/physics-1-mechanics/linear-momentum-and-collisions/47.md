A body of mass $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ moving at $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ collides head-on and elastically with a body of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ at rest. What is the speed of the second body after the collision?

- $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The elastic formula with the target at rest gives $v_2' = \dfrac{2m_1}{m_1 + m_2}\,v_1 = \dfrac{2 \cdot 1.0}{3.0} \cdot 6.0\,\mathrm{m/s} = 4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The value $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is the common velocity of a perfectly inelastic collision with the same data.
