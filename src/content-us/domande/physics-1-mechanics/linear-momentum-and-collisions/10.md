A force pulse has the shape of a triangle in the graph of force against time, with peak value $F_{\max}$. What is the average force over the whole duration of the pulse?

- $F_{\max}$
- $\dfrac{F_{\max}}{2}$
- $\dfrac{F_{\max}}{4}$
- $\dfrac{F_{\max}}{3}$

## Explanation

The average force is the impulse divided by the duration $\Delta t$. The area of the triangle is $\dfrac{1}{2}F_{\max}\,\Delta t$, therefore $F_{\text{av}} = \dfrac{F_{\max}}{2}$, wherever the peak is located within the interval.
