A bullet of mass $10\,\mathrm{g}$ embeds itself in a block of mass $0.99\,\mathrm{kg}$ hanging at rest from long cords, and the block with the bullet rises by $5.0\,\mathrm{cm}$. Air resistance is negligible. What was the speed of the bullet?

- $9.9\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.99\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $99\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $50\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The swing conserves mechanical energy, so the speed just after the impact is $V = \sqrt{2gh} = \sqrt{2 \cdot 9.81 \cdot 0.050}\,\mathrm{m/s} \approx 0.990\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The impact conserves momentum, hence $v = \dfrac{m + M}{m}\,V = 100 \cdot 0.990\,\mathrm{m/s} \approx 99\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Energy conservation applied to the impact gives $9.9\,\mathrm{m/s}$, which ignores the energy dissipated when the bullet embeds.
