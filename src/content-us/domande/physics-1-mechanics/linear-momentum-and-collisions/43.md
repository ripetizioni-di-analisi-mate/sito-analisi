Two bodies of masses $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $3.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ approach each other along a line with relative speed $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and stick together. How much kinetic energy is lost?

- $30\,\mathrm{J}$
- $63\,\mathrm{J}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{J}$
- $15\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The loss in a perfectly inelastic collision is $|\Delta K| = \dfrac{1}{2}\,\dfrac{m_1 m_2}{m_1 + m_2}\,(v_1 - v_2)^2$. The reduced mass is $\dfrac{2.0 \cdot 3.0}{5.0}\,\mathrm{kg} = 1.2\,\mathrm{kg}$, hence $|\Delta K| = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 1.2 \cdot 5.0^2\,\mathrm{J} = 15\,\mathrm{J}$, whatever the individual velocities are.
