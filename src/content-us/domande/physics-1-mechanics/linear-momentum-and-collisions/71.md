A plate is made of two square pieces placed side by side: one of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ with its center at $x = 0.50\,\mathrm{m}$ and one of mass $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ with its center at $x = 1.5\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the $x$ coordinate of the center of mass of the plate?

- $1.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.83\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Each piece is treated as a particle placed at its own center of mass, hence $x_{\text{cm}} = \dfrac{2.0 \cdot 0.50 + 1.0 \cdot 1.5}{3.0}\,\mathrm{m} \approx 0.83\,\mathrm{m}$. The result is closer to the heavier piece.
