A rocket of mass $2.0 \cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{kg}$ stands on the launch pad, and its exhaust speed is $2.5\,\mathrm{km/s}$. Above which rate of fuel consumption does it lift off?

- $8.0\,\mathrm{kg/s}$
- $78\,\mathrm{kg/s}$
- $7.8 \cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{kg/s}$
- $39\,\mathrm{kg/s}$

## Explanation

The rocket lifts off when the thrust exceeds the weight, $u\left|\dfrac{dm}{dt}\right| > mg$. Hence $\left|\dfrac{dm}{dt}\right| > \dfrac{2.0 \cdot 10^{4} \cdot 9.81}{2.5 \cdot 10^{3}}\,\mathrm{kg/s} \approx 78\,\mathrm{kg/s}$.
