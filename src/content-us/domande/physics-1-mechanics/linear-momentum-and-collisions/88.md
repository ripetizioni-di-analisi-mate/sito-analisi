According to the rocket equation $v_f - v_i = u\ln\dfrac{m_i}{m_f}$, on which quantities does the gain in speed of a rocket in free space depend?

- The rate at which the fuel is burned and the exhaust speed
- The duration of the burn and the exhaust speed
- The rate at which the fuel is burned and the duration of the burn
- The exhaust speed and the ratio of the initial to the final mass

## Explanation

The formula contains only $u$ and the ratio $\dfrac{m_i}{m_f}$. Burning the same fuel faster or more slowly changes the thrust and the duration of the burn, and it leaves the final speed unchanged.
