Two carts of masses $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $3.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ are held at rest on a horizontal frictionless track with a compressed spring between them. After the release, the $3.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ cart moves at $0.50\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the speed of the $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ cart?

- $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.17\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.50\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The system is isolated along the track and starts at rest, hence $0 = m_1 v_1 + m_2 v_2$. Then $|v_1| = \dfrac{m_2}{m_1}\,|v_2| = 3.0 \cdot 0.50\,\mathrm{m/s} = 1.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$, in the sense opposite to the heavier cart.
