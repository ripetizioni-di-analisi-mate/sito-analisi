A body of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ slides east at $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ on a horizontal frictionless plane and splits into two pieces of $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ each. One piece moves east at $8.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the velocity of the other piece?

- $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ west
- $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ east
- $8.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ west
- $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ east

## Explanation

With the $x$ axis towards east, conservation of momentum gives $2.0 \cdot 3.0 = 1.0 \cdot 8.0 + 1.0 \cdot v_x$ in SI units, hence $v_x = -2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The negative sign means that the piece moves west; the initial momentum $6.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$ enters the balance, since the body is not at rest before the splitting.
