A wrench is thrown so that it slides and spins on a horizontal frictionless table. What is the path of its center of mass?

- A straight line traveled at constant speed
- A circle around one end of the wrench
- A spiral, because of the rotation
- A straight line traveled with decreasing speed

## Explanation

The weight and the normal force balance, so the resultant external force vanishes and $\vec{a}_{\text{cm}} = \vec{0}$. The center of mass therefore moves with constant velocity, while the other points of the wrench also rotate about it.
