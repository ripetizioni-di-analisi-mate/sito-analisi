A puck collides elastically with an identical puck at rest, and after the collision the first puck moves at $40^\circ$ from its original direction. In which direction does the second puck move?

- At $50^\circ$ from the original direction, on the opposite side
- At $40^\circ$ from the original direction, on the opposite side
- At $50^\circ$ from the original direction, on the same side
- At $90^\circ$ from the original direction

## Explanation

For equal masses in an elastic collision with the target at rest, the two final velocities are perpendicular. The second puck therefore moves at $90^\circ - 40^\circ = 50^\circ$ from the original direction, and on the opposite side, since the components of the two final momenta perpendicular to the original direction must cancel.
