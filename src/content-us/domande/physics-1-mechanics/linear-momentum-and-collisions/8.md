A constant force of magnitude $15\,\mathrm{N}$ acts on a body for $0.40\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the magnitude of its impulse?

- $38\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$
- $6.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$
- $3.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$
- $0.027\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$

## Explanation

For a constant force the impulse is $\vec{J} = \vec{F}\,\Delta t$, so $J = 15\,\mathrm{N} \cdot 0.40\,\mathrm{s} = 6.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$. The factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$ appears only when the force grows linearly from zero, which is not the case for a constant force.
