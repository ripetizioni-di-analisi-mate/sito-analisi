A body of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ moves at $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ along the positive $x$ axis and a body of mass $3.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ is at rest. What is the velocity of their center of mass?

- $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ along $+x$
- $3.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ along $+x$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ along $+x$
- $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ along $+x$

## Explanation

The total momentum is $\vec{P} = M\vec{v}_{\text{cm}}$, so $v_{\text{cm},x} = \dfrac{2.0 \cdot 5.0}{5.0}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The plain average of the two velocities, $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$, ignores the masses.
