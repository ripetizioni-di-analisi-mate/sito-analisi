A body of mass $0.50\,\mathrm{kg}$ moving at $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ along the positive $x$ axis strikes a body of mass $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ at rest on a horizontal frictionless plane. After the collision the first body moves at $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ along the positive $y$ axis. What is the speed of the second body?

- $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Along $x$, $0.50 \cdot 4.0 = 1.0\,v_{2x}'$ in SI units, so $v_{2x}' = 2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$; along $y$, $0 = 0.50 \cdot 2.0 + 1.0\,v_{2y}'$, so $v_{2y}' = -1.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The speed is $\sqrt{2.0^2 + 1.0^2}\,\mathrm{m/s} \approx 2.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
