The momentum of a particle moving along the $x$ axis is $p_x(t) = 2.0\,t^3$, with $p_x$ in $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$ and $t$ in seconds. What is the net force on the particle at $t = 2.0\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $16\,\mathrm{N}$
- $8.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$
- $24\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

By Newton's second law in momentum form $F_x = \dfrac{dp_x}{dt} = 6.0\,t^2$, hence $F_x = 6.0 \cdot 2.0^2\,\mathrm{N} = 24\,\mathrm{N}$ at $t = 2.0\,\mathrm{s}$. The value $16\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$ is the momentum at that instant, and $\dfrac{16}{2.0}\,\mathrm{N} = 8.0\,\mathrm{N}$ is the average force over the first two seconds.
