Which of the following bodies floats in mercury, of density $1.36 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$?

- An iron block, of density $7.87 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- A platinum block, of density $2.14 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- An osmium block, of density $2.26 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- A gold block, of density $1.93 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

By the **condition for floating** a homogeneous body floats when its density is smaller than that of the fluid. Iron is the only one of the four with $\rho_b < 1.36 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$, and it floats with the fraction $\dfrac{7.87}{13.6} = 0.58$ of its volume submerged. The comparison of the two densities is the only criterion, therefore a metal can float in a denser liquid.
