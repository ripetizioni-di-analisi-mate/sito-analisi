A river with a rectangular cross-section $20\,\mathrm{m}$ wide and $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ deep flows at $0.50\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Downstream the channel is $8.0\,\mathrm{m}$ wide and $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$ deep. Treating the water as incompressible and the speed as uniform over each section, what is the speed downstream?

- $0.250\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.400\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.25\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The areas of the two sections are $A_1 = 20 \cdot 2.0\,\mathrm{m^2} = 40\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and $A_2 = 8.0 \cdot 2.5\,\mathrm{m^2} = 20\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The **continuity equation** gives
$$
v_2 = \frac{A_1}{A_2}\,v_1 = \frac{40}{20} \cdot 0.50\,\mathrm{m/s} = 1.00\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The value $0.250\,\mathrm{m/s}$ inverts the ratio of the areas, while $1.25\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $0.400\,\mathrm{m/s}$ account for the change of width only or of depth only.
