At what depth below the free surface of a pool of mercury, of density $1.36 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$, does the gauge pressure equal $1.00 \times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{Pa}$?

- $0.750\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.375\,\mathrm{m}$
- $7.35\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10.2\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Setting $\rho g h = 1.00 \times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{Pa}$ gives
$$
h = \frac{1.00 \times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{Pa}}{1.36 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}} = 0.750\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $7.35\,\mathrm{m}$ omits $g$, and $10.2\,\mathrm{m}$ is the depth in water, whose density is smaller by the factor $13.6$.
