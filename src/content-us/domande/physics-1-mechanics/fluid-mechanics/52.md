A boat floating in fresh water takes on $500\,\mathrm{kg}$ of cargo and stays afloat. By how much does the volume of water it displaces increase?

- $500\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $0.0510\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $0.500\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $4.91\,\mathrm{m^3}$

## Explanation

The boat floats before and after loading, so the buoyant force equals the total weight each time, and its increase equals the weight of the cargo, $\rho_f\,g\,\Delta V = m g$. Therefore
$$
\Delta V = \frac{m}{\rho_f} = \frac{500\,\mathrm{kg}}{1.00 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}} = 0.500\,\mathrm{m^3} .
$$
The factor $g$ cancels, while the values $4.91$ and $0.0510$ keep it on one side only, and $500\,\mathrm{m^3}$ omits the density.
