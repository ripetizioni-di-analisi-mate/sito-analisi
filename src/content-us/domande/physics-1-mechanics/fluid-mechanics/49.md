A homogeneous body of density $6.0 \times 10^{2}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ floats in oil of density $8.0 \times 10^{2}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. What fraction of its volume is submerged?

- $0.75$
- $1.33$
- $0.25$
- $0.60$

## Explanation

By the **condition for floating** the submerged fraction is
$$
\frac{V_{\mathrm{sub}}}{V} = \frac{\rho_b}{\rho_f} = \frac{6.0 \times 10^{2}}{8.0 \times 10^{2}} = 0.75 .
$$
The value $0.25$ is the fraction above the surface, $1.33$ inverts the ratio and exceeds $1$, which is impossible for a fraction, and $0.60$ uses the density of water instead of that of the oil.
