A liquid at rest presses on a flat wall of its container that is inclined at $30^\circ$ to the vertical. What is the direction of the force that the liquid exerts on the wall?

- Perpendicular to the wall, from the liquid toward the wall.
- Horizontal, from the liquid toward the wall.
- Parallel to the wall, directed downward.
- Vertical, directed downward.

## Explanation

The force produced by a pressure on a surface is directed along the normal of the surface, toward the surface, since a fluid at rest exerts no tangential force. On an inclined wall the force is therefore inclined as well, at $30^\circ$ to the horizontal, and it is neither vertical nor horizontal.
