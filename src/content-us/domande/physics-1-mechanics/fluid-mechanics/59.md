Which two properties define an ideal fluid?

- It is incompressible and has no viscosity.
- It is incompressible and its flow is steady.
- Its flow is steady and irrotational.
- It has no viscosity and its flow is irrotational.

## Explanation

An **ideal fluid** is incompressible and has no viscosity, so no tangential force acts between adjacent layers or between the fluid and the walls. Steady and irrotational are properties of the *flow*, not of the fluid, and they are the other two hypotheses of the model used for Bernoulli's equation.
