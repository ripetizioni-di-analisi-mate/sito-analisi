A barometer is filled with a liquid of density $8.0 \times 10^{2}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ instead of mercury, at a place where $p_{\mathrm{atm}} = 1.013 \times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{Pa}$. How tall is the column of liquid?

- $10.3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $12.9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6.45\,\mathrm{m}$
- $127\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The pressure at the top of the column is zero, therefore $p_{\mathrm{atm}} = \rho g h$ and
$$
h = \frac{p_{\mathrm{atm}}}{\rho g} = \frac{1.013 \times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{Pa}}{8.0 \times 10^{2}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}} = 12.9\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $10.3\,\mathrm{m}$ is the height of a water column, and $127\,\mathrm{m}$ omits $g$.
