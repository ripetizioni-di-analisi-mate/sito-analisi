A blood pressure of $120\,\mathrm{mmHg}$, with $\rho_{\mathrm{Hg}} = 1.36 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$, expressed in kilopascals is

- $0.902\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $16.0\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $160\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $1.60\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

A pressure of $120\,\mathrm{mmHg}$ is the pressure at the bottom of a mercury column of height $0.120\,\mathrm{m}$, therefore
$$
p = \rho_{\mathrm{Hg}}\,g\,h = 1.36 \times 10^{4} \cdot 9.81 \cdot 0.120\,\mathrm{Pa} = 1.60 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{Pa} = 16.0\,\mathrm{kPa} .
$$
The value $0.902\,\mathrm{kPa}$ divides $120$ by $133$, the number of pascals in one millimeter of mercury, instead of multiplying.
