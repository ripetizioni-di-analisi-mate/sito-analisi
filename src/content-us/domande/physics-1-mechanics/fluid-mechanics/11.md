At what depth below the surface of a fresh-water lake is the absolute pressure twice the atmospheric pressure $p_{\mathrm{atm}} = 1.013 \times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{Pa}$?

- $5.16\,\mathrm{m}$
- $103\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10.3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $20.7\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The absolute pressure is $p = p_{\mathrm{atm}} + \rho g h$, and setting it equal to $2p_{\mathrm{atm}}$ gives $\rho g h = p_{\mathrm{atm}}$, therefore
$$
h = \frac{p_{\mathrm{atm}}}{\rho g} = \frac{1.013 \times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{Pa}}{1.00 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}} = 10.3\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $20.7\,\mathrm{m}$ sets the gauge pressure, and not the absolute pressure, equal to $2p_{\mathrm{atm}}$.
