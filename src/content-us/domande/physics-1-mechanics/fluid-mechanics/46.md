A beaker of water rests on a scale that reads $12.0\,\mathrm{N}$. A stone of volume $5.0 \times 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^3}$, hanging from a string held by hand, is lowered until it is completely submerged without touching the beaker. What does the scale read now?

- $16.9\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12.5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $7.1\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The water pushes the stone upward with the buoyant force $F_B = \rho_f g V = 1.00 \times 10^{3} \cdot 9.81 \cdot 5.0 \times 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{N} = 4.9\,\mathrm{N}$. By Newton's third law the stone pushes the water downward with a force of the same magnitude, and this force reaches the scale through the water and the beaker, therefore the reading becomes
$$
12.0\,\mathrm{N} + 4.9\,\mathrm{N} = 16.9\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The reading does not stay the same, because the stone interacts with the water although it does not touch the beaker.
