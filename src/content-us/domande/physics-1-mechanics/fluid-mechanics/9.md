A room measures $5.0\,\mathrm{m} \times 4.0\,\mathrm{m} \times 3.0\,\mathrm{m}$ and is filled with air of density $1.20\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. What is the mass of the air in the room?

- $50\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $72\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $706\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $24\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The volume of the room is $V = 5.0 \cdot 4.0 \cdot 3.0\,\mathrm{m^3} = 60\,\mathrm{m^3}$, hence the mass is
$$
m = \rho V = 1.20\,\mathrm{kg/m^3} \cdot 60\,\mathrm{m^3} = 72\,\mathrm{kg} .
$$
The value $50\,\mathrm{kg}$ divides the volume by the density, and $706$ is the weight in newtons, $mg$, written as a mass.
