A body of density $4.0 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ weighs $20\,\mathrm{N}$ in air. It hangs from a spring scale and is completely submerged in water. What does the scale read?

- $25\,\mathrm{N}$
- $15\,\mathrm{N}$
- $5.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $80\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The scale reads the **apparent weight**
$$
W_{\mathrm{app}} = \left(1 - \frac{\rho_f}{\rho_b}\right) W = \left(1 - \frac{1.0 \times 10^{3}}{4.0 \times 10^{3}}\right) \cdot 20\,\mathrm{N} = 15\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The value $5.0\,\mathrm{N}$ is the buoyant force, $25\,\mathrm{N}$ adds the buoyant force to the weight instead of subtracting it, and $80\,\mathrm{N}$ multiplies the weight by the inverted ratio $\dfrac{\rho_b}{\rho_f}$.
