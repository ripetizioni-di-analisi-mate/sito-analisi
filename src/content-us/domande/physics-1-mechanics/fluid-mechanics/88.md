Two spheres of the same material, of radii $R$ and $2R$, fall at their terminal speeds through the same viscous liquid in slow flow. How does the terminal speed of the larger sphere compare with that of the smaller one?

- It is half as large.
- It is $8$ times as large.
- It is $2$ times as large.
- It is $4$ times as large.

## Explanation

The terminal speed is $v_t = \dfrac{2R^2 g\,(\rho_b - \rho_f)}{9\eta}$, proportional to $R^2$, therefore doubling the radius multiplies it by $4$. The weight minus the buoyant force grows as $R^3$, while the drag at a given speed grows as $R$, and their balance leaves the factor $R^2$.
