A homogeneous body whose density equals that of a liquid is released at rest while completely submerged, halfway between the surface and the bottom. What happens to it?

- It rises until its top is level with the free surface.
- It stays at rest at that depth.
- It sinks slowly to the bottom.
- It rises until half of its volume is above the surface.

## Explanation

While the body is completely submerged the weight $\rho_b g V$ and the buoyant force $\rho_f g V$ are equal, since $\rho_b = \rho_f$. The resultant force is zero and the body starts at rest, therefore it remains in equilibrium at that depth, and the same holds at any other depth.
