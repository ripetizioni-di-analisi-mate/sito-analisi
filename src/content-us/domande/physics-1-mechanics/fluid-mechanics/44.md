When a body is completely submerged in water, a spring scale reads three quarters of the value it reads in air. The buoyancy of the air is negligible. What is the density of the body?

- $2.5 \times 10^{2}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $7.5 \times 10^{2}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $4.0 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $1.3 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

The apparent weight is $\left(1 - \dfrac{\rho_f}{\rho_b}\right) W = \dfrac{3}{4}W$, hence $\dfrac{\rho_f}{\rho_b} = \dfrac{1}{4}$ and
$$
\rho_b = 4\rho_f = 4.0 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3} .
$$
The buoyant force is one quarter of the weight. The value $1.3 \times 10^{3}$ divides $\rho_f$ by $\dfrac{3}{4}$, the fraction read by the scale, instead of the fraction lost, and the two smallest values give a body less dense than water, which would float.
