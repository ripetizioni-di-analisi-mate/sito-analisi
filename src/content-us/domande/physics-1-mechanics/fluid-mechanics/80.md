In a large open tank, water leaves a small opening at depth $h$ with speed $v$. With what speed does it leave a second small opening at depth $4h$?

- $2v$
- $4v$
- $\sqrt{2}\,v$
- $16v$

## Explanation

By **Torricelli's theorem** the speed is $\sqrt{2gh}$, which is proportional to $\sqrt{h}$. Multiplying the depth by $4$ multiplies the speed by $\sqrt{4} = 2$, therefore the speed is $2v$. The value $4v$ treats the speed as proportional to the depth, which holds for the gauge pressure at the opening.
