In SI units, each term of Bernoulli's equation $p + \rho g y + \dfrac{1}{2}\rho v^2 = \text{constant}$ is measured in

- $\mathrm{J}$
- $\mathrm{J/m^3}$
- $\mathrm{N}$
- $\mathrm{J/kg}$

## Explanation

The first term is a pressure, and $1\,\mathrm{Pa} = 1\,\mathrm{N/m^2} = 1\,\dfrac{\mathrm{N \cdot m}}{\mathrm{m^3}} = 1\,\mathrm{J/m^3}$. Every term of a sum has the same units, therefore each term is an energy per unit volume. Dividing the equation by $\rho$ would give energies per unit mass, in $\mathrm{J/kg}$, which is a different form of the equation.
