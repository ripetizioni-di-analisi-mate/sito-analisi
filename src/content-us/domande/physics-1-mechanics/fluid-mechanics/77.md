A large open tank of water has a small opening in its side at the depth $1.8\,\mathrm{m}$ below the free surface. With what speed does the water leave the opening?

- $35.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4.20\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.90\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5.94\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

By **Torricelli's theorem**
$$
v = \sqrt{2gh} = \sqrt{2 \cdot 9.81 \cdot 1.8}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 5.94\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The value $35.3$ is $2gh$ without the square root, $4.20\,\mathrm{m/s}$ loses the factor $2$, and $1.90\,\mathrm{m/s}$ omits $g$.
