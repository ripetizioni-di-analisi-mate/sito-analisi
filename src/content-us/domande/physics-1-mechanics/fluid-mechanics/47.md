An aluminum cube and a lead cube of the same volume are both held completely submerged in water. How do the buoyant forces on them compare?

- They are in the ratio of the densities of the two metals.
- They are equal.
- The force on the lead cube is larger, since lead is denser.
- The force on the aluminum cube is larger, since aluminum is less dense.

## Explanation

The buoyant force on a completely submerged body is $\rho_f\,g\,V$, which depends on the density of the fluid and on the volume of the body, and not on the material the body is made of. The two cubes have the same volume and are in the same fluid, therefore the forces are equal. The material affects the weight, hence the apparent weight differs.
