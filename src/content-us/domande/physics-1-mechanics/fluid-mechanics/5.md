A pressure of $45\,\mathrm{psi}$, with $1\,\mathrm{psi} = 6.895 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{Pa}$, expressed in bar is

- $0.310\,\mathrm{bar}$
- $6.53\,\mathrm{bar}$
- $3.10\,\mathrm{bar}$
- $31.0\,\mathrm{bar}$

## Explanation

In pascals the pressure is $45 \cdot 6.895 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{Pa} = 3.10 \times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Since $1\,\mathrm{bar} = 10^{5}\,\mathrm{Pa}$, this is $3.10\,\mathrm{bar}$. The value $6.53$ comes from dividing $45$ by $6.895$ instead of multiplying, and the other two values from taking the bar as $10^{4}$ or $10^{6}\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
