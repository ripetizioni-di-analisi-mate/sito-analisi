Two points of the same lake lie at the depths $3.0\,\mathrm{m}$ and $7.5\,\mathrm{m}$. By how much does the pressure at the deeper point exceed the pressure at the shallower one?

- $1.0 \times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $4.4 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $7.4 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $4.5 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

Two points of the same body of fluid satisfy $p_2 - p_1 = \rho g (h_2 - h_1)$, therefore
$$
p_2 - p_1 = 1.00 \times 10^{3} \cdot 9.81 \cdot (7.5 - 3.0)\,\mathrm{Pa} = 4.4 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{Pa} .
$$
The value $1.0 \times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{Pa}$ adds the two depths instead of subtracting them, and $7.4 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{Pa}$ is the gauge pressure at the deeper point alone.
