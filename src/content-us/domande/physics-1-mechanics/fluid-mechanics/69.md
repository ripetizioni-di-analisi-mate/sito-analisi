An ideal fluid of density $\rho$ flows steadily along a flow tube, with $y$ the height of a section, $p$ its pressure and $v$ its speed. Which quantity has the same value at every section of the tube?

- $p + \rho g y + \dfrac{1}{2}\rho v^2$
- $p - \rho g y + \dfrac{1}{2}\rho v^2$
- $p + \rho g y - \dfrac{1}{2}\rho v^2$
- $p + \rho g y + \rho v^2$

## Explanation

**Bernoulli's equation** states that $p + \rho g y + \dfrac{1}{2}\rho v^2$ is the same at every section. The term $\rho g y$ is the potential energy per unit volume and $\dfrac{1}{2}\rho v^2$ the kinetic energy per unit volume, both with the positive sign, since the equation is the conservation of mechanical energy for a unit volume of fluid. The kinetic term carries the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$ of the kinetic energy.
