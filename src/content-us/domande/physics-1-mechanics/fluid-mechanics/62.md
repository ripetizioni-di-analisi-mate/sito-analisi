Water flows at $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ through a pipe of cross-sectional area $12\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. What is the volume flow rate?

- $300\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $3.0\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $0.0030\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $0.030\,\mathrm{L/s}$

## Explanation

Since $1\,\mathrm{cm^2} = 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}$, the area is $1.2 \times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^2}$, and the **volume flow rate** is
$$
Q = A v = 1.2 \times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^2} \cdot 2.5\,\mathrm{m/s} = 3.0 \times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^3/s} = 3.0\,\mathrm{L/s} .
$$
The value $0.0030\,\mathrm{L/s}$ writes the flow rate in cubic meters per second with the unit of liters, and the other two values convert the area with $10^{-2}$ or $10^{-6}$.
