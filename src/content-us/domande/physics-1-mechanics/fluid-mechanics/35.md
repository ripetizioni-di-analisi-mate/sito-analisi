A force of $120\,\mathrm{N}$ acts perpendicularly on a piston of area $3.0\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ that closes a cylinder of oil. What pressure does the piston add to the oil?

- $40\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $4.0 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $4.0 \times 10^{7}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $4.0 \times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

Since $1\,\mathrm{cm^2} = 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}$, the area is $3.0 \times 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}$, hence
$$
p = \frac{F}{A} = \frac{120\,\mathrm{N}}{3.0 \times 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}} = 4.0 \times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{Pa} .
$$
The value $40\,\mathrm{Pa}$ keeps the area in square centimeters, and the other two values convert it with $10^{-2}$ or $10^{-6}$.
