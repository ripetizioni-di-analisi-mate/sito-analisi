A window of area $0.50\,\mathrm{m^2}$ in the wall of an aquarium has water on one side and the atmosphere on the other. The gauge pressure of the water averaged over the window is $1.2 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{Pa}$. What is the net force exerted on the window by the water and the air?

- $5.7 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $6.0 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $3.0 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $2.4 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The atmosphere acts on both faces of the window, therefore the two atmospheric contributions cancel and the net force is computed from the **gauge pressure**, which gives
$$
F = p_{\mathrm{gauge}}\,A = 1.2 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{Pa} \cdot 0.50\,\mathrm{m^2} = 6.0 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The value $5.7 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{N}$ uses the absolute pressure on the water side and ignores the air on the other side, while $2.4 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{N}$ divides the pressure by the area.
