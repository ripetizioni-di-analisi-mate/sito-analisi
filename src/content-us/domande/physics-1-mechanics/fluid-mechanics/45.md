A balloon of volume $20\,\mathrm{L}$ is surrounded by air of density $1.20\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. What is the buoyant force exerted on it by the air?

- $235\,\mathrm{N}$
- $196\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.235\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.0240\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Archimedes' principle holds for every fluid, gases included. Since $1\,\mathrm{L} = 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^3}$, the volume is $0.020\,\mathrm{m^3}$, and
$$
F_B = \rho_{\mathrm{air}}\,g\,V = 1.20 \cdot 9.81 \cdot 0.020\,\mathrm{N} = 0.235\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The value $235\,\mathrm{N}$ keeps the volume in liters, $196\,\mathrm{N}$ uses the density of water, and $0.0240\,\mathrm{N}$ omits $g$.
