A hydraulic press has two pistons at the same height, of diameters $2.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $16\,\mathrm{cm}$. A force of $40\,\mathrm{N}$ is applied to the small piston. What force does the fluid exert on the large piston?

- $2.56 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $5.00\,\mathrm{N}$
- $3.20 \times 10^{2}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.625\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The pressure is the same on the two pistons, hence $F_2 = \dfrac{A_2}{A_1}F_1$. The areas are proportional to the squares of the diameters, therefore
$$
F_2 = \left(\frac{16}{2.0}\right)^{2} \cdot 40\,\mathrm{N} = 64 \cdot 40\,\mathrm{N} = 2.56 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The value $3.20 \times 10^{2}\,\mathrm{N}$ uses the ratio of the diameters instead of the ratio of the areas, and the two smallest values divide by the ratio instead of multiplying.
