In a hydraulic press the diameter of the small piston and the input force are kept fixed. By what factor must the diameter of the large piston be multiplied to double the output force?

- $0.707$
- $2.00$
- $1.41$
- $4.00$

## Explanation

The output force is $F_2 = \dfrac{A_2}{A_1}F_1$, and the area $A_2$ is proportional to the square of the diameter $d_2$. Doubling $F_2$ requires doubling $A_2$, therefore $d_2$ is multiplied by $\sqrt{2} = 1.41$. Doubling the diameter would multiply the force by $4$.
