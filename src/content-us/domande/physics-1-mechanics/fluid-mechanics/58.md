A block of volume $2.0 \times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^3}$ and density $6.0 \times 10^{2}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ floats in fresh water. What additional downward force keeps it completely submerged at rest?

- $0.800\,\mathrm{N}$
- $19.6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $11.8\,\mathrm{N}$
- $7.85\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

When the block is completely submerged the buoyant force is $\rho_f g V = 19.6\,\mathrm{N}$ and the weight is $\rho_b g V = 11.8\,\mathrm{N}$. Equilibrium requires the extra force to make up the difference, therefore
$$
F = (\rho_f - \rho_b)\,g\,V = 4.0 \times 10^{2} \cdot 9.81 \cdot 2.0 \times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{N} = 7.85\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The values $11.8\,\mathrm{N}$ and $19.6\,\mathrm{N}$ are the weight and the buoyant force taken separately, and $0.800\,\mathrm{N}$ omits $g$.
