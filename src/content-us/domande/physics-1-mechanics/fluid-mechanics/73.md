Setting $v_1 = v_2 = 0$ in Bernoulli's equation $p_1 + \rho g y_1 + \dfrac{1}{2}\rho v_1^2 = p_2 + \rho g y_2 + \dfrac{1}{2}\rho v_2^2$ gives

- Poiseuille's law.
- the continuity equation.
- Torricelli's theorem.
- the law of pressure with depth in a fluid at rest.

## Explanation

With zero speeds the equation becomes $p_1 + \rho g y_1 = p_2 + \rho g y_2$, that is $p_2 - p_1 = \rho g\,(y_1 - y_2)$. This is the **law of pressure with depth**, since $y_1 - y_2$ is the depth of point $2$ below point $1$. Torricelli's theorem needs a nonzero speed at the opening, the continuity equation contains no pressure, and Poiseuille's law describes a viscous fluid.
