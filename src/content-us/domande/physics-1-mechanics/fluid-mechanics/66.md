Blood flows through the aorta, of radius $1.0\,\mathrm{cm}$, with a volume flow rate of $5.0\,\mathrm{L/min}$. Treating the speed as uniform over the section, what is its value?

- $0.27\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.066\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The flow rate is $Q = \dfrac{5.0 \times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^3}}{60\,\mathrm{s}} = 8.3 \times 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ and the area is $A = \pi r^2 = 3.14 \times 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}$, therefore
$$
v = \frac{Q}{A} = \frac{8.3 \times 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}}{3.14 \times 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}} = 0.27\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The value $16\,\mathrm{m/s}$ leaves the minutes unconverted, $0.066\,\mathrm{m/s}$ computes the area as $\pi d^2$ with the diameter $d = 2.0\,\mathrm{cm}$, and $1.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$ takes $1.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ as the diameter.
