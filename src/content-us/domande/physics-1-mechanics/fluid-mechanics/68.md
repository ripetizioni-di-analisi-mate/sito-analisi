A stream of water falling vertically from a tap becomes narrower as it falls. Which statement explains it?

- The surrounding air compresses the stream more and more as it falls.
- The pressure inside the stream increases as it falls.
- The speed of the water increases as it falls, while the product $Av$ stays constant.
- The density of the water increases as it falls.

## Explanation

The water accelerates under its weight, so its speed $v$ grows with the distance fallen. The water is incompressible and the flow is steady, therefore the **continuity equation** keeps the flow rate $Q = Av$ the same at every section of the stream, and the area $A$ decreases as $v$ increases. The density of water does not change appreciably.
