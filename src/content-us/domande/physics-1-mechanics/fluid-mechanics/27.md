Which quantity does an open-tube manometer give directly through the difference between its two liquid levels?

- The gauge pressure of the vessel it is connected to.
- The absolute pressure of the vessel it is connected to.
- The density of the gas in the vessel.
- The atmospheric pressure.

## Explanation

The pressures at the lower of the two levels, computed along the two arms, are equal, therefore $p = p_{\mathrm{atm}} + \rho g h$ and $p - p_{\mathrm{atm}} = \rho g h$. The difference of the levels $h$ thus measures the **gauge pressure** of the vessel. Obtaining the absolute pressure requires the atmospheric pressure in addition, which a barometer measures.
