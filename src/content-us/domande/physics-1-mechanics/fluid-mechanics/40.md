A stone of volume $400\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ is held completely submerged in sea water of density $1.03 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. What is the buoyant force acting on it?

- $4.04\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4.04 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $2.02\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.412\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Since $1\,\mathrm{cm^3} = 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^3}$, the volume is $4.00 \times 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^3}$, and by **Archimedes' principle**
$$
F_B = \rho_f\,g\,V = 1.03 \times 10^{3} \cdot 9.81 \cdot 4.00 \times 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{N} = 4.04\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The value $4.04 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{N}$ converts the volume with $10^{-3}$, and $0.412\,\mathrm{N}$ omits $g$.
