A body held completely submerged in sea water, of density $1.03 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$, is acted on by a buoyant force of $4.04\,\mathrm{N}$. What is the volume of the body?

- $400\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $3.92 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $4.00 \times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $0.400\,\mathrm{cm^3}$

## Explanation

By **Archimedes' principle** $F_B = \rho_f\,g\,V$, therefore
$$
V = \frac{F_B}{\rho_f\,g} = \frac{4.04\,\mathrm{N}}{1.03 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}} = 4.00 \times 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^3} = 400\,\mathrm{cm^3} ,
$$
since $1\,\mathrm{m^3} = 10^{6}\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. The value $3.92 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ omits $g$, while the other two values convert cubic meters into cubic centimeters with $10^{3}$ or $10^{9}$.
