What is the gauge pressure at the depth of $8.0\,\mathrm{m}$ in sea water of density $1.03 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$?

- $1.82 \times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $8.08 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $4.04 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $8.24 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

By the law of **pressure as a function of depth**, the gauge pressure is
$$
p_{\mathrm{gauge}} = \rho g h = 1.03 \times 10^{3} \cdot 9.81 \cdot 8.0\,\mathrm{Pa} = 8.08 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{Pa} .
$$
The value $1.82 \times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{Pa}$ is the absolute pressure, which includes the atmosphere, and $8.24 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{Pa}$ omits the factor $g$.
