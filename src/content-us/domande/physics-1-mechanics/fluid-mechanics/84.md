Two vertical tubes are connected to the wide section and to the throat of a Venturi meter carrying water, and the water in them stands at levels that differ by $6.0\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the pressure difference between the two sections?

- $5.9 \times 10^{2}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $5.9 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $60\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $2.9 \times 10^{2}\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

In each vertical tube the water is at rest, so its level is fixed by the law of pressure with depth, and the difference of the levels gives
$$
p_1 - p_2 = \rho g h = 1.00 \times 10^{3} \cdot 9.81 \cdot 0.060\,\mathrm{Pa} = 5.9 \times 10^{2}\,\mathrm{Pa} .
$$
The value $5.9 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{Pa}$ keeps the height in centimeters, and $60\,\mathrm{Pa}$ omits $g$.
