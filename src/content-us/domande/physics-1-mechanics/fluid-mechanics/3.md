A person of mass $60\,\mathrm{kg}$ stands on one heel, whose area of contact with the floor is $1.0\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. What pressure does the heel exert on the floor?

- $5.9 \times 10^{8}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $5.9 \times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $5.9 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $6.0 \times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The force on the floor is the weight $F = mg = 60 \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{N} = 589\,\mathrm{N}$, and the area is $A = 1.0 \times 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}$, since $1\,\mathrm{cm^2} = 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Therefore
$$
p = \frac{F}{A} = \frac{589\,\mathrm{N}}{1.0 \times 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}} = 5.9 \times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{Pa} .
$$
Dividing the mass instead of the weight gives $6.0 \times 10^{5}$, and converting the area with $10^{-2}$ or $10^{-6}$ gives the other two values.
