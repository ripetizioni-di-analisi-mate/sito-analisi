A vertical square gate of side $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ holds fresh water whose free surface is level with the top of the gate, and the atmosphere acts on its other face. What is the resultant force of the water on the gate?

- $7.8 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $2.6 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $2.0 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $3.9 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The gauge pressure grows linearly from zero at the top to $\rho g H$ at the bottom, therefore integrating over the gate gives
$$
F = \frac{1}{2}\rho g H^2 w = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 1.00 \times 10^{3} \cdot 9.81 \cdot 2.0^2 \cdot 2.0\,\mathrm{N} = 3.9 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The value $7.8 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{N}$ uses the pressure at the bottom over the whole gate, which loses the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$, and $2.0 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{N}$ omits the width.
