An open-tube manometer filled with water is connected to a gas pipe, and the water in the open arm stands $25\,\mathrm{cm}$ above its level in the arm connected to the pipe. What is the gauge pressure of the gas?

- $2.45 \times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $1.04 \times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $2.45 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $2.50 \times 10^{2}\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The two levels are joined through the water, therefore the gauge pressure of the gas is
$$
p_{\mathrm{gauge}} = \rho g h = 1.00 \times 10^{3} \cdot 9.81 \cdot 0.25\,\mathrm{Pa} = 2.45 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{Pa} .
$$
The value $2.45 \times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{Pa}$ keeps the height in centimeters, and $1.04 \times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{Pa}$ is the absolute pressure of the gas.
