A vertical rectangular gate of height $4.50\,\mathrm{m}$ holds water whose free surface is level with its top edge, and the atmosphere acts on its other face. At what depth below the free surface is the resultant force of the water applied?

- $2.25\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.50\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4.50\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3.00\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Taking torques about the free surface, the resultant of the pressure forces is applied at the depth
$$
y_{\mathrm{cp}} = \frac{\displaystyle\int_0^H \rho g y^2 w\,dy}{\dfrac{1}{2}\rho g H^2 w} = \frac{2}{3}H = 3.00\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The point lies below the middle of the gate, since the deeper strips carry a larger pressure. The value $1.50\,\mathrm{m}$ is one third of the height measured from the top, which is the distance of the point from the bottom edge.
