A mercury barometer at a mountain site shows a column of $700\,\mathrm{mm}$, with $\rho_{\mathrm{Hg}} = 1.36 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. What is the atmospheric pressure there?

- $9.34 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $9.52 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $9.34 \times 10^{7}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $9.34 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The pressure at the top of the column is zero, therefore
$$
p_{\mathrm{atm}} = \rho_{\mathrm{Hg}}\,g\,h = 1.36 \times 10^{4} \cdot 9.81 \cdot 0.700\,\mathrm{Pa} = 9.34 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{Pa} .
$$
The value is smaller than at sea level, as expected at altitude. The value $9.34 \times 10^{7}\,\mathrm{Pa}$ keeps the height in millimeters, $9.34 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{Pa}$ converts $700\,\mathrm{mm}$ into $0.070\,\mathrm{m}$, and $9.52 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{Pa}$ omits $g$.
