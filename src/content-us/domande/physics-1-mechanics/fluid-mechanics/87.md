A sphere of radius $1.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ moves slowly at $2.0\,\mathrm{cm/s}$ through oil of viscosity $8.0 \times 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{Pa \cdot s}$. What is the magnitude of the viscous drag on the sphere?

- $9.6 \times 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $6.0 \times 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $3.0 \times 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $3.0 \times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

By **Stokes' law**, with $R = 1.0 \times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m}$ and $v = 2.0 \times 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m/s}$,
$$
F_d = 6\pi\eta R v = 6\pi \cdot 8.0 \times 10^{-2} \cdot 1.0 \times 10^{-3} \cdot 2.0 \times 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{N} = 3.0 \times 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The value $3.0 \times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{N}$ keeps the speed in centimeters per second, $6.0 \times 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{N}$ uses the diameter in place of the radius, and $9.6 \times 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{N}$ omits the factor $\pi$.
