At what depth below the free surface of a large open tank must a small opening be made for the water to leave it at $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$?

- $0.306\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3.67\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.83\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.612\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Solving **Torricelli's theorem** $v = \sqrt{2gh}$ for $h$ gives
$$
h = \frac{v^2}{2g} = \frac{36}{2 \cdot 9.81}\,\mathrm{m} = 1.83\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $3.67\,\mathrm{m}$ loses the factor $2$ in the denominator, while $0.306\,\mathrm{m}$ and $0.612\,\mathrm{m}$ do not square the speed.
