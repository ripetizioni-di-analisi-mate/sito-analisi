A large open tank stands on the ground. A small opening in its side is $1.25\,\mathrm{m}$ above the ground and $0.45\,\mathrm{m}$ below the free surface of the water. Air resistance is negligible. At what horizontal distance from the wall does the jet reach the ground?

- $0.750\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.50\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.06\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.75\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The water leaves horizontally with $v = \sqrt{2 g h}$, with $h = 0.45\,\mathrm{m}$, and then falls the height $y_0 = 1.25\,\mathrm{m}$ in the time $t = \sqrt{\dfrac{2y_0}{g}}$. The range is therefore
$$
x = v\,t = \sqrt{2gh}\,\sqrt{\frac{2y_0}{g}} = 2\sqrt{h\,y_0} = 2\sqrt{0.45 \cdot 1.25}\,\mathrm{m} = 1.50\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $1.06\,\mathrm{m}$ takes the time of fall as $\sqrt{\dfrac{y_0}{g}}$, and $1.75\,\mathrm{m}$ uses the height of the free surface above the ground as the height of fall.
