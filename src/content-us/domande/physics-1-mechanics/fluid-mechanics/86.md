Under Poiseuille's law, a pipe of the same radius as another but twice its length, with the same pressure difference between its ends, carries

- the same flow rate.
- half the flow rate.
- one quarter of the flow rate.
- twice the flow rate.

## Explanation

**Poiseuille's law** $Q = \dfrac{\pi R^4}{8\eta L}\,(p_1 - p_2)$ makes the flow rate inversely proportional to the length $L$ at fixed radius and pressure difference. Doubling $L$ therefore halves $Q$, since the viscous forces act along a longer pipe and the same pressure difference drives a slower flow.
