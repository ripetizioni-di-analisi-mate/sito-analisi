The water in a large open tank is replaced by oil, of smaller density, filled to the same level above a small opening in the side. How does the speed of efflux from the opening change?

- It increases as the square root of the ratio of the densities.
- It decreases in the ratio of the densities.
- It increases in the ratio of the densities.
- It does not change.

## Explanation

**Torricelli's theorem** gives $v = \sqrt{2gh}$, where the density does not appear. In Bernoulli's equation every term contains $\rho$, therefore the density cancels and the liquid leaves with the speed of a body falling freely from the height $h$.
