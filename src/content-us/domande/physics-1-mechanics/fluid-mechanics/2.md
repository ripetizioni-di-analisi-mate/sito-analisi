A metal cylinder has mass $0.540\,\mathrm{kg}$ and volume $200\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. What is its density?

- $2.70 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $2.70\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $2.70 \times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $27.0\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

Since $1\,\mathrm{cm^3} = 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^3}$, the volume is $V = 2.00 \times 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^3}$, and the **density** is
$$
\rho = \frac{m}{V} = \frac{0.540\,\mathrm{kg}}{2.00 \times 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^3}} = 2.70 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3} .
$$
The value $2.70\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ follows from the conversion $1\,\mathrm{cm^3} = 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^3}$, which is the liter, and $2.70 \times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ from $1\,\mathrm{cm^3} = 10^{-9}\,\mathrm{m^3}$, which is the cubic millimeter.
