In a graph of the absolute pressure in a liquid at rest against the depth, the data lie on a straight line with intercept $101.3\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and slope $8.0\,\mathrm{kPa/m}$. What is the density of the liquid?

- $1.0 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $8.0 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $8.2 \times 10^{2}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $7.8 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

The law $p = p_{\mathrm{atm}} + \rho g h$ is a straight line in $h$ with slope $\rho g$, therefore
$$
\rho = \frac{8.0 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{Pa/m}}{9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}} = 8.2 \times 10^{2}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3} .
$$
The value $8.0 \times 10^{3}$ takes the slope itself as the density, $7.8 \times 10^{4}$ multiplies it by $g$ instead of dividing, and $1.0 \times 10^{4}$ uses the intercept, which is the atmospheric pressure and carries no information on the liquid.
