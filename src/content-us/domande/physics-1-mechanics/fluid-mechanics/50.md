A cylinder floats upright in fresh water with $30\%$ of its height above the free surface. What is the density of the cylinder?

- $3.3 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $7.0 \times 10^{2}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $3.0 \times 10^{2}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $1.4 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

For an upright cylinder the submerged fraction of the volume equals the submerged fraction of the height, which is $0.70$. The condition for floating gives $\dfrac{\rho_b}{\rho_f} = 0.70$, therefore $\rho_b = 0.70 \cdot 1.00 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3} = 7.0 \times 10^{2}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. The value $3.0 \times 10^{2}$ uses the emerged fraction, and the two largest values divide by the fractions, which gives a body denser than water, which would sink.
