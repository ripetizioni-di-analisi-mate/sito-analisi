Water flows steadily through a pipe whose diameter is halved at a constriction. By what factor does the speed change in the narrow part?

- It is multiplied by $4$.
- It is divided by $4$.
- It is divided by $2$.
- It is multiplied by $2$.

## Explanation

The **continuity equation** gives $A_1 v_1 = A_2 v_2$, and the area of a circular section is proportional to the square of the diameter. Halving the diameter divides the area by $4$, therefore the speed is multiplied by $4$. The speed grows where the section shrinks, so the two values that divide the speed have the wrong direction.
