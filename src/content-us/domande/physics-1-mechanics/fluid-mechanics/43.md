Where is the buoyant force on a partly submerged floating body applied?

- At the centroid of the whole body.
- At the center of the section cut by the free surface.
- At the center of mass of the body.
- At the centroid of the submerged part of the body.

## Explanation

The buoyant force is the resultant of the pressure forces, and it equals the resultant that would act on the fluid filling the submerged volume. That fluid is in equilibrium, hence the resultant passes through its centroid, which is the centroid of the submerged part. The weight of the body, instead, acts at its center of mass, which in general is a different point.
