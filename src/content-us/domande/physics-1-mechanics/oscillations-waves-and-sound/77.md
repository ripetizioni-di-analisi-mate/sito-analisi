The tension of a string fixed at both ends is increased by $21\%$, with its length and its linear density unchanged. By how much does its fundamental frequency increase?

- By $4.9\%$
- By $10\%$
- By $42\%$
- By $21\%$

## Explanation

The fundamental $f_1 = \dfrac{1}{2L}\sqrt{\dfrac{F_T}{\mu}}$ is proportional to $\sqrt{F_T}$, hence multiplying the tension by $1.21$ multiplies the frequency by $\sqrt{1.21} = 1.10$, an increase of $10\%$. The value $21\%$ treats the frequency as proportional to the tension.
