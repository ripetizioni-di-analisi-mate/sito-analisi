A particle in simple harmonic motion takes $0.30\,\mathrm{s}$ to go from $x = +A$ to $x = -A$. What is the period?

- $0.60\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.30\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.15\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

From one turning point to the opposite one the particle covers half a cycle, hence $\dfrac{T}{2} = 0.30\,\mathrm{s}$ and $T = 0.60\,\mathrm{s}$. The period is the time to return to $x = +A$, which requires the way back as well.
