A particle in simple harmonic motion with $\omega = 10\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ is at $x_0 = 3.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ at $t = 0$, with velocity $v_0 = 0.40\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the amplitude?

- $3.0\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $5.0\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $7.0\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The term $\dfrac{v_0}{\omega} = \dfrac{0.40}{10}\,\mathrm{m} = 4.0\,\mathrm{cm}$, hence
$$
A = \sqrt{x_0^2 + \frac{v_0^2}{\omega^2}} = \sqrt{3.0^2 + 4.0^2}\,\mathrm{cm} = 5.0\,\mathrm{cm} .
$$
The value $7.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ adds the two terms without squaring them, while $3.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $4.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ each keep only one of them.
