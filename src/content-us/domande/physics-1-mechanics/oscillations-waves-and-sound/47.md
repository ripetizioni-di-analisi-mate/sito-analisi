A uniform rod of length $1.20\,\mathrm{m}$ swings with small amplitude about a horizontal axis through a point $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$ from its center. What is the length of the simple pendulum with the same period?

- $0.40\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.70\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

With $I_{\mathrm{cm}} = \dfrac{1}{12}mL^2 = 0.12\,m$ and $d = 0.30\,\mathrm{m}$, the parallel-axis theorem gives $I = m(0.12 + 0.090)\,\mathrm{m^2} = 0.21\,m\,\mathrm{m^2}$, hence
$$
L_{\mathrm{eq}} = \frac{I}{md} = \frac{0.21}{0.30}\,\mathrm{m} = 0.70\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$ is $\dfrac{2L}{3}$, valid for an axis through the end, and $0.40\,\mathrm{m}$ uses $I_{\mathrm{cm}}$ in place of $I$.
