Two loudspeakers driven in phase emit sound of wavelength $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the smallest nonzero path difference that gives destructive interference?

- $1.6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.40\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Destructive interference requires $\Delta r = \left(n + \dfrac{1}{2}\right)\lambda$, and the smallest positive value, with $n = 0$, is $\dfrac{\lambda}{2} = 0.40\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$ is one wavelength and gives constructive interference.
