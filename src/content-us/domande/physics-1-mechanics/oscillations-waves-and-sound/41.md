The bob of a simple pendulum has mass $0.50\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the string makes an angle of $10^\circ$ with the vertical. What is the magnitude of the tangential component of the weight?

- $1.7\,\mathrm{N}$
- $49\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.85\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4.8\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The tangential component of the weight is
$$
mg\sin\theta = 0.50 \cdot 9.81 \cdot \sin 10^\circ\,\mathrm{N} = 0.85\,\mathrm{N} ,
$$
directed toward the lowest point. The value $4.8\,\mathrm{N}$ is the radial component $mg\cos\theta$, balanced by the tension; $1.7\,\mathrm{N}$ omits the mass, and $49\,\mathrm{N}$ uses the angle in degrees as if it were in radians.
