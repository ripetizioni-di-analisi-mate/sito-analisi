A body of mass $0.20\,\mathrm{kg}$ hangs at rest from a vertical spring and stretches it by $2.5\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the spring constant?

- $8.0\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $0.78\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $1.6 \cdot 10^{2}\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $78\,\mathrm{N/m}$

## Explanation

At equilibrium the spring force balances the weight, $kx = mg$, hence
$$
k = \frac{mg}{x} = \frac{0.20 \cdot 9.81}{0.025}\,\mathrm{N/m} = 78\,\mathrm{N/m} .
$$
The value $8.0\,\mathrm{N/m}$ uses the mass in place of the weight, $0.78\,\mathrm{N/m}$ keeps the elongation in centimeters, and $1.6 \cdot 10^{2}\,\mathrm{N/m}$ equates $mgx$ to $\dfrac{1}{2}kx^2$, an energy balance that describes the lowest point reached by a body released at the natural length, not the equilibrium position.
