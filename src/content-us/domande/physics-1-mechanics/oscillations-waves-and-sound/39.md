Why does the formula $T = 2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{L}{g}}$ for the simple pendulum require small angular displacements?

- Because air resistance grows with the amplitude
- Because the mass of the string becomes relevant at large angles
- Because the weight has no tangential component at small angles
- Because it replaces $\sin\theta$ by $\theta$ in the equation of motion

## Explanation

The tangential equation is $\ddot{\theta} = -\dfrac{g}{L}\sin\theta$, which is not the equation of simple harmonic motion. Only for small $\theta$ is $\sin\theta \approx \theta$, and the equation becomes $\ddot{\theta} = -\dfrac{g}{L}\theta$. Air resistance and the mass of the string are excluded by the hypotheses of the model.
