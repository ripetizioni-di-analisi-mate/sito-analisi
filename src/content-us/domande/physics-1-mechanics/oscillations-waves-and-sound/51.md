A car suspension is designed to bring the body back to equilibrium in the shortest time without oscillating. Which damping regime does it use?

- Critically damped
- Overdamped
- Undamped
- Underdamped

## Explanation

In the underdamped regime the body overshoots and oscillates, while in the overdamped regime it returns without oscillating but more slowly. The critically damped regime, $\gamma = \omega_0$, gives the fastest return without overshoot.
