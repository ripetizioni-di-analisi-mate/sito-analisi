A pulse $y = f(x - vt)$ travels on a string with $v = 5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and at $t = 0$ its peak is at $x = 0$. Where is the peak at $t = 0.40\,\mathrm{s}$?

- At $x = -2.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- At $x = 2.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- At $x = 0.080\,\mathrm{m}$
- At $x = 12.5\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The peak corresponds to a fixed value of the argument, $x - vt = 0$, hence $x = vt = 5.0 \cdot 0.40\,\mathrm{m} = 2.0\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $-2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ reverses the direction of travel, which for $f(x - vt)$ is toward increasing $x$.
