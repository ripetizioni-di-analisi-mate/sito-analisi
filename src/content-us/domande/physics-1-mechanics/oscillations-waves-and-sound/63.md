A string $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ long has a mass of $5.0\,\mathrm{g}$ and is kept under a tension of $40\,\mathrm{N}$. What is the speed of a transverse wave on it?

- $126\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.60 \cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $63.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The linear mass density is $\mu = \dfrac{m}{L} = \dfrac{0.0050}{2.0}\,\mathrm{kg/m} = 2.5 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{kg/m}$, hence
$$
v = \sqrt{\frac{F_T}{\mu}} = \sqrt{\frac{40}{2.5 \cdot 10^{-3}}}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 126\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The value $63.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ multiplies the mass by the length, $4.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$ keeps the mass in grams, and $1.60 \cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{m/s}$ omits the square root.
