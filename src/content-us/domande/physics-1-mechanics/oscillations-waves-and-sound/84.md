A listener moves from $3.0\,\mathrm{m}$ to $12\,\mathrm{m}$ from a point source that radiates uniformly in a medium absorbing no energy. By how much does the intensity level decrease?

- $12\,\mathrm{dB}$
- $24\,\mathrm{dB}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{dB}$
- $6.0\,\mathrm{dB}$

## Explanation

The distance is multiplied by $4$, hence the intensity, proportional to $\dfrac{1}{r^2}$, is divided by $16$, and the level falls by $10\log_{10}16\,\mathrm{dB} = 12\,\mathrm{dB}$. The value $6.0\,\mathrm{dB}$ takes the intensity proportional to $\dfrac{1}{r}$, and $1.2\,\mathrm{dB}$ omits the factor $10$.
