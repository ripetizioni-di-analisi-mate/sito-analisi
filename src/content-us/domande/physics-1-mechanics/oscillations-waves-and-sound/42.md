A uniform rod $0.60\,\mathrm{m}$ long swings with small amplitude about a horizontal axis through one end. What is the length of the simple pendulum with the same period?

- $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.40\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.60\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.20\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

About one end $I = \dfrac{1}{3}mL^2$ and $d = \dfrac{L}{2}$, hence the equivalent length is
$$
L_{\mathrm{eq}} = \frac{I}{md} = \frac{\frac{1}{3}mL^2}{m\frac{L}{2}} = \frac{2L}{3} = 0.40\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$ is the distance of the center of mass from the axis, and $0.60\,\mathrm{m}$ treats the rod as a point mass at its far end.
