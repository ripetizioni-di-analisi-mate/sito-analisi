An underdamped oscillator has $\omega_0 = 5.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ and $\gamma = 3.0\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$. What is the angular frequency of its oscillations?

- $4.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $5.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $5.8\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

The damped angular frequency is
$$
\omega' = \sqrt{\omega_0^2 - \gamma^2} = \sqrt{25 - 9.0}\,\mathrm{rad/s} = 4.0\,\mathrm{rad/s} .
$$
The value $5.8\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ adds the squares, $2.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ subtracts $\gamma$ from $\omega_0$ without squaring, and $5.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ ignores the damping.
