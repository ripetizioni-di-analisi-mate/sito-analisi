A particle in simple harmonic motion has amplitude $A = 0.10\,\mathrm{m}$ and angular frequency $\omega = 8.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. What is its speed when it is at $x = 0.060\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $0.48\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.32\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.64\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.80\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The speed at the position $x$ is
$$
|v| = \omega\sqrt{A^2 - x^2} = 8.0\sqrt{0.010 - 0.0036}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 8.0 \cdot 0.080\,\mathrm{m/s} = 0.64\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The value $0.80\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is the maximum speed, reached only at $x = 0$; the value $0.32\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is $\omega(A - x)$, and the value $0.48\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is $\omega x$.
