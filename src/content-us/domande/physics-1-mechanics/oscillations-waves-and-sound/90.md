What intensity corresponds to an intensity level of $0\,\mathrm{dB}$?

- $10^{12}\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $10^{-12}\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $0\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $1\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$

## Explanation

The condition $10\log_{10}\dfrac{I}{I_0} = 0$ requires $\dfrac{I}{I_0} = 1$, hence $I = I_0 = 10^{-12}\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$, the reference intensity near the threshold of hearing. A zero level does not mean a zero intensity, since $I \to 0$ gives $\beta \to -\infty$.
