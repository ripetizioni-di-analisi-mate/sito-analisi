A thin ring of radius $R$, whose moment of inertia about its center is $MR^2$, hangs on a horizontal nail through a point of the ring and swings in its own plane. What is the length of the simple pendulum with the same period?

- $\dfrac{3R}{2}$
- $R$
- $2R$
- $\dfrac{R}{2}$

## Explanation

The nail is at distance $d = R$ from the center, hence by the parallel-axis theorem $I = MR^2 + MR^2 = 2MR^2$, and
$$
L_{\mathrm{eq}} = \frac{I}{Md} = \frac{2MR^2}{MR} = 2R .
$$
The value $R$ ignores the moment of inertia about the center, and $\dfrac{3R}{2}$ is the equivalent length of a uniform disk, whose $I_{\mathrm{cm}}$ is $\dfrac{1}{2}MR^2$.
