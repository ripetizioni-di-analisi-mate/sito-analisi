A particle moves along the $x$ axis under one of the resultant forces listed below, where $k$ and $c$ are positive constants. Which force produces simple harmonic motion about $x = 0$?

- $F_x = k\,x$
- $F_x = -k\,x$
- $F_x = -k\,x^3$
- $F_x = -c$

## Explanation

Simple harmonic motion requires a restoring force proportional to the displacement, $F_x = -kx$, which gives $\ddot{x} = -\omega^2 x$. The force $kx$ points away from the origin, therefore the origin is unstable. The force $-kx^3$ is restoring but not linear, and the constant force $-c$ does not depend on the position at all.
