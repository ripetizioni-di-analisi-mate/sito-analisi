The intensity of a sound at a point is multiplied by $100$. By how much does the intensity level increase?

- $2.0\,\mathrm{dB}$
- $10\,\mathrm{dB}$
- $100\,\mathrm{dB}$
- $20\,\mathrm{dB}$

## Explanation

The increase in level is $10\log_{10}\dfrac{I_2}{I_1} = 10\log_{10}100\,\mathrm{dB} = 20\,\mathrm{dB}$. Each factor $10$ in intensity adds $10\,\mathrm{dB}$, and $100 = 10 \cdot 10$. The value $2.0\,\mathrm{dB}$ omits the factor $10$ in front of the logarithm.
