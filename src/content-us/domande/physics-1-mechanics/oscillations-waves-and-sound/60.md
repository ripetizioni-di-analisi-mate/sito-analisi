A wave on a string is described by $y(x,t) = 0.030\sin(2.5\,x + 10\,t)$, with $x$ and $y$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. What are the speed and the direction of propagation?

- $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ toward increasing $x$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{m/s}$ toward decreasing $x$
- $25\,\mathrm{m/s}$ toward decreasing $x$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ toward decreasing $x$

## Explanation

The wave speed is $v = \dfrac{\omega}{k} = \dfrac{10}{2.5}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The phase is $kx + \omega t$, hence a fixed value of it requires $x$ to decrease as $t$ grows, and the wave travels toward decreasing $x$. The value $0.25\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is $\dfrac{k}{\omega}$, and $25\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is $k\omega$.
