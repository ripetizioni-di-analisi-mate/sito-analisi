What is the wavelength in air of a sound of frequency $440\,\mathrm{Hz}$, if sound travels at $343\,\mathrm{m/s}$?

- $1.51 \cdot 10^{5}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.124\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.28\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.780\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

From $v = \lambda f$,
$$
\lambda = \frac{v}{f} = \frac{343}{440}\,\mathrm{m} = 0.780\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $1.28\,\mathrm{m}$ inverts the ratio, $0.124\,\mathrm{m}$ uses $\omega$ in place of $f$, and $1.51 \cdot 10^{5}\,\mathrm{m}$ multiplies speed and frequency.
