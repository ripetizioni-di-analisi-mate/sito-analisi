A body of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ oscillates on a spring with mechanical energy $4.0\,\mathrm{J}$. What is its maximum speed?

- $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

At the equilibrium position the energy is all kinetic, $E = \dfrac{1}{2}mv_{\max}^2$, hence
$$
v_{\max} = \sqrt{\frac{2E}{m}} = \sqrt{\frac{2 \cdot 4.0}{2.0}}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 2.0\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The value $1.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ loses the factor $2$, $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ omits the square root, and $2.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ doubles the factor $2$.
