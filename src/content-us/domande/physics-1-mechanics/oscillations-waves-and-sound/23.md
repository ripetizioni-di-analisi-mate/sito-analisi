The same body and the same spring are used first on a frictionless horizontal surface and then with the body hanging from the spring. How does the period of the vertical oscillations compare with that of the horizontal ones?

- The vertical period is smaller, since the weight adds to the spring force
- The vertical period depends on the amplitude, the horizontal one does not
- The vertical period is larger, since the spring is already stretched
- The two periods are equal

## Explanation

Measured from the new equilibrium position, the resultant force on the hanging body is $-k(u + x_{\mathrm{eq}}) + mg = -ku$, since $kx_{\mathrm{eq}} = mg$. The weight only shifts the equilibrium position, therefore the motion is simple harmonic with the same $\omega = \sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m}}$ and the same period.
