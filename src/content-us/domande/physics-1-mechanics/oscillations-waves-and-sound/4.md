A particle moves along the $x$ axis with $x(t) = 0.050\cos(20\,t)\,\mathrm{m}$, where $t$ is in seconds. What is its maximum speed?

- $1.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.0025\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.16\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The amplitude is $A = 0.050\,\mathrm{m}$ and the angular frequency is $\omega = 20\,\mathrm{rad/s}$, hence
$$
v_{\max} = \omega A = 20 \cdot 0.050\,\mathrm{m/s} = 1.0\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The value $20$ is $\omega^2 A$, the maximum acceleration in $\mathrm{m/s^2}$; the value $0.0025\,\mathrm{m/s}$ divides $A$ by $\omega$, and the value $0.16\,\mathrm{m/s}$ multiplies $A$ by the frequency $f$ instead of $\omega$.
