The amplitude of a mass-spring oscillator is doubled, while the mass and the spring are unchanged. How does the period change?

- It doubles
- It is multiplied by $\sqrt{2}$
- It is halved
- It does not change

## Explanation

The period is $T = 2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k}}$, which contains neither the amplitude nor the initial conditions. This independence of the period from the amplitude is the isochronism of the harmonic oscillator.
