A block of mass $0.40\,\mathrm{kg}$ oscillates on a spring of constant $250\,\mathrm{N/m}$ with amplitude $4.0\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the maximum magnitude of its acceleration?

- $10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2.5 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $25\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The largest force is exerted at $x = \pm A$, with magnitude $kA = 250 \cdot 0.040\,\mathrm{N} = 10\,\mathrm{N}$, hence by Newton's second law
$$
a_{\max} = \frac{kA}{m} = \frac{10}{0.40}\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 25\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
The value $10$ is the force in newtons, $4.0$ multiplies the force by the mass, and $2.5 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ keeps the amplitude in centimeters.
