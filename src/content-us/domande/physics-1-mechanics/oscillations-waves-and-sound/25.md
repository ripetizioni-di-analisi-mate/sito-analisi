A block of mass $0.40\,\mathrm{kg}$ oscillates on a spring of constant $360\,\mathrm{N/m}$ with amplitude $5.0\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the maximum magnitude of its acceleration?

- $18\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $4.5 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $45\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $7.2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The largest force is exerted at $x = \pm A$, with magnitude $kA = 360 \cdot 0.050\,\mathrm{N} = 18\,\mathrm{N}$, hence by Newton's second law
$$
a_{\max} = \frac{kA}{m} = \frac{18}{0.40}\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 45\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
The value $18$ is the force in newtons, $7.2$ multiplies the force by the mass, and $4.5 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ keeps the amplitude in centimeters.
