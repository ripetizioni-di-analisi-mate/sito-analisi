A standing wave on a string has wavelength $0.60\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the distance between two adjacent nodes?

- $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.60\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.15\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The nodes are the points where $\sin kx = 0$, that is $kx = n\pi$, hence they are spaced by $\dfrac{\pi}{k} = \dfrac{\lambda}{2} = 0.30\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $0.15\,\mathrm{m}$ is the distance between a node and the adjacent antinode.
