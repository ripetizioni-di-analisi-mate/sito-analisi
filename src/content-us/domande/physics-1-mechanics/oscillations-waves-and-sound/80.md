At a point the intensity of a sound is $1.0 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$. What is the intensity level, with $I_0 = 10^{-12}\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$?

- $160\,\mathrm{dB}$
- $-80\,\mathrm{dB}$
- $80\,\mathrm{dB}$
- $8.0\,\mathrm{dB}$

## Explanation

The intensity level is
$$
\beta = 10\log_{10}\frac{I}{I_0} = 10\log_{10}\frac{10^{-4}}{10^{-12}}\,\mathrm{dB} = 10 \cdot 8\,\mathrm{dB} = 80\,\mathrm{dB} .
$$
The value $8.0\,\mathrm{dB}$ omits the factor $10$, $-80\,\mathrm{dB}$ inverts the ratio, and $160\,\mathrm{dB}$ uses the factor $20$ in place of $10$.
