Transverse waves of frequency $60\,\mathrm{Hz}$ travel on a string at $24\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is their wavelength?

- $1.4 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.064\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.40\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

From $v = \lambda f$,
$$
\lambda = \frac{v}{f} = \frac{24}{60}\,\mathrm{m} = 0.40\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$ inverts the ratio, $0.064\,\mathrm{m}$ uses $\omega$ in place of $f$, and $1.4 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{m}$ multiplies speed and frequency.
