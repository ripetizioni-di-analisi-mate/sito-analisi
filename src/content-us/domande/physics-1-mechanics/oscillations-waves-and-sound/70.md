Two loudspeakers driven in phase emit sound of frequency $343\,\mathrm{Hz}$ in air, where sound travels at $343\,\mathrm{m/s}$. A listener is $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$ farther from one loudspeaker than from the other. What kind of interference occurs there?

- Neither, since the phase difference is a quarter of a cycle
- Constructive interference
- It cannot be decided without the power of the loudspeakers
- Destructive interference

## Explanation

The wavelength is $\lambda = \dfrac{v}{f} = 1.0\,\mathrm{m}$, hence $\Delta r = 2.5\,\mathrm{m} = \left(2 + \dfrac{1}{2}\right)\lambda$. A path difference of a whole number of wavelengths plus one half brings the waves in antiphase, therefore the interference is destructive.
