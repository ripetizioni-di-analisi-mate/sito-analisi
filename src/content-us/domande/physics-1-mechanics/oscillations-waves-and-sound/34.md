A simple pendulum of length $2.45\,\mathrm{m}$ swings with small amplitude. What is its period?

- $3.14\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.57\,\mathrm{s}$
- $12.6\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.500\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

For small amplitudes
$$
T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{L}{g}} = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{2.45}{9.81}}\,\mathrm{s} = 2\pi \cdot 0.500\,\mathrm{s} = 3.14\,\mathrm{s} .
$$
The value $0.500\,\mathrm{s}$ omits $2\pi$, $1.57\,\mathrm{s}$ uses $\pi$ in place of $2\pi$, and $12.6\,\mathrm{s}$ inverts the ratio under the root.
