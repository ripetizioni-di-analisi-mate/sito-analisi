A harmonic wave has wave number $k = 5.0\,\mathrm{rad/m}$. What is its wavelength?

- $0.796\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.26\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.200\,\mathrm{m}$
- $31.4\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The wave number is $k = \dfrac{2\pi}{\lambda}$, hence
$$
\lambda = \frac{2\pi}{k} = \frac{2\pi}{5.0}\,\mathrm{m} = 1.26\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $0.200\,\mathrm{m}$ omits $2\pi$, $0.796\,\mathrm{m}$ inverts the ratio, and $31.4\,\mathrm{m}$ multiplies $k$ by $2\pi$.
