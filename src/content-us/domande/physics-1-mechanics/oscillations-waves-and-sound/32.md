A body oscillates in simple harmonic motion with amplitude $A$. At which positions is its potential energy one quarter of the mechanical energy?

- $x = \pm\dfrac{A}{\sqrt{2}}$
- $x = \pm\dfrac{A}{4}$
- $x = \pm\dfrac{A}{2}$
- $x = \pm\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}A$

## Explanation

The condition $\dfrac{1}{2}kx^2 = \dfrac{1}{4} \cdot \dfrac{1}{2}kA^2$ gives $x^2 = \dfrac{A^2}{4}$, hence $x = \pm\dfrac{A}{2}$. The positions $\pm\dfrac{A}{4}$ treat the energy as linear in $x$, $\pm\dfrac{A}{\sqrt{2}}$ is where the two forms of energy are equal, and $\pm\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}A$ is where the kinetic energy is one quarter.
