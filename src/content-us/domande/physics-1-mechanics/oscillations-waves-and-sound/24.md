A block attached to a horizontal spring of constant $400\,\mathrm{N/m}$ is at $x = -3.0\,\mathrm{cm}$, with the spring compressed. What is the force $F_x$ the spring exerts on the block?

- $+12\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{N}$
- $+6.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $+1.2 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

By **Hooke's law**,
$$
F_x = -kx = -400 \cdot (-0.030)\,\mathrm{N} = +12\,\mathrm{N} ,
$$
directed towards the equilibrium position, since the compressed spring pushes the block back. The value $-12\,\mathrm{N}$ loses the sign of $x$, $+1.2 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{N}$ keeps $x$ in centimeters, and $+6.0\,\mathrm{N}$ adds a factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$ that belongs to the potential energy.
