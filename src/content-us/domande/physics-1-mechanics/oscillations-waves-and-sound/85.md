A jet flies at $150\,\mathrm{m/s}$ directly toward a stationary observer, and its engine emits a tone of $600\,\mathrm{Hz}$. With the speed of sound $343\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what frequency does the observer receive?

- $0.600\,\mathrm{kHz}$
- $0.862\,\mathrm{kHz}$
- $0.417\,\mathrm{kHz}$
- $1.07\,\mathrm{kHz}$

## Explanation

The source approaches, hence $v_s = +150\,\mathrm{m/s}$, $v_o = 0$, and
$$
f' = f\,\frac{v}{v - v_s} = 600\,\mathrm{Hz} \cdot \frac{343}{193} = 1.07\,\mathrm{kHz} .
$$
The value $0.862\,\mathrm{kHz}$ applies the formula of a moving observer, $0.417\,\mathrm{kHz}$ treats the source as receding, and $0.600\,\mathrm{kHz}$ ignores the motion.
