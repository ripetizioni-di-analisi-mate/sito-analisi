A mass-spring oscillator is given. Which quantities fix the amplitude and the phase constant of its motion?

- The period of the motion
- The mass and the spring constant
- The initial position and the initial velocity
- The initial position alone

## Explanation

The equation $\ddot{x} = -\omega^2 x$ is of the second order, hence its solution carries two constants, $A$ and $\varphi$, and they are fixed by two initial conditions, $x_0$ and $v_0$. The mass and the spring constant fix $\omega$ and the period, which are the same for every motion of the given oscillator.
