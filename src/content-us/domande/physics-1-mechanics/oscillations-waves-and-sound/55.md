A damped oscillator of spring constant $k$ is driven by $F_0\cos\omega_d t$. To what value does the steady amplitude tend as $\omega_d \to 0$?

- It grows without bound
- $0$
- $\dfrac{F_0}{b\,\omega_0}$
- $\dfrac{F_0}{k}$

## Explanation

Setting $\omega_d = 0$ in $A(\omega_d) = \dfrac{F_0}{\sqrt{(k - m\omega_d^2)^2 + b^2\omega_d^2}}$ leaves $\dfrac{F_0}{k}$, the static elongation produced by the constant force $F_0$. The value $\dfrac{F_0}{b\,\omega_0}$ is the amplitude at resonance.
