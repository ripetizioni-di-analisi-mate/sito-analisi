The amplitude of a mass-spring oscillator is doubled. By what factor does its mechanical energy change?

- $2$
- $4$
- $1$
- $\sqrt{2}$

## Explanation

The mechanical energy is $E = \dfrac{1}{2}kA^2$, hence it is proportional to the square of the amplitude, and doubling $A$ multiplies $E$ by $2^2 = 4$.
