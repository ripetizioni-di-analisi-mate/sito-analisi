A block on a spring of constant $500\,\mathrm{N/m}$ oscillates with amplitude $6.0\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the mechanical energy of the oscillation?

- $0.90\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1.8\,\mathrm{J}$
- $15\,\mathrm{J}$
- $9.0 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

At $x = \pm A$ the speed vanishes, hence the energy is all potential:
$$
E = \frac{1}{2}kA^2 = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 500 \cdot (0.060)^2\,\mathrm{J} = 0.90\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
The value $1.8\,\mathrm{J}$ omits the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$, $15\,\mathrm{J}$ omits the square of the amplitude, and $9.0 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{J}$ keeps the amplitude in centimeters.
