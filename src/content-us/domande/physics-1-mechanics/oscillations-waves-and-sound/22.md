A body oscillates vertically on a spring with period $0.50\,\mathrm{s}$. By how much is the spring stretched when the body hangs at rest?

- $3.1\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $6.2\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $78\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The period of the vertical oscillations is $T = 2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{x_{\mathrm{eq}}}{g}}$, hence
$$
x_{\mathrm{eq}} = \frac{g\,T^2}{4\pi^2} = \frac{9.81 \cdot 0.25}{39.5}\,\mathrm{m} = 0.062\,\mathrm{m} = 6.2\,\mathrm{cm} .
$$
The value $78\,\mathrm{cm}$ uses $T$ in place of $T^2$ and $2\pi$ in place of $4\pi^2$, while $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $3.1\,\mathrm{cm}$ come from a wrong factor $2$ in the denominator.
