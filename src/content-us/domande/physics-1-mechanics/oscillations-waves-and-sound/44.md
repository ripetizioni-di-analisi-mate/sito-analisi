A rigid body of mass $1.5\,\mathrm{kg}$ swings about a horizontal axis at $0.20\,\mathrm{m}$ from its center of mass, and its moment of inertia about that axis is $0.30\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$. What is the period of its small oscillations?

- $2.01\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.897\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.319\,\mathrm{s}$
- $6.28\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The period of the physical pendulum is
$$
T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{I}{mgd}} = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{0.30}{1.5 \cdot 9.81 \cdot 0.20}}\,\mathrm{s} = 2.01\,\mathrm{s} .
$$
The value $0.319\,\mathrm{s}$ omits $2\pi$, $0.897\,\mathrm{s}$ omits the distance $d$, and $6.28\,\mathrm{s}$ omits $g$.
