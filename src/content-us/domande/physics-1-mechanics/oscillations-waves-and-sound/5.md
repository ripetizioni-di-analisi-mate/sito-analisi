A particle in simple harmonic motion has amplitude $4.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ and frequency $5.0\,\mathrm{Hz}$. What is the maximum magnitude of its acceleration?

- $1.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $39\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3.9 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $1.3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The angular frequency is $\omega = 2\pi f = 31.4\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ and the amplitude is $A = 0.040\,\mathrm{m}$, hence
$$
a_{\max} = \omega^2 A = (31.4\,\mathrm{rad/s})^2 \cdot 0.040\,\mathrm{m} = 39\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
The value $1.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ uses $f^2$ in place of $\omega^2$, the value $1.3$ is $\omega A$, and $3.9 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ keeps the amplitude in centimeters.
