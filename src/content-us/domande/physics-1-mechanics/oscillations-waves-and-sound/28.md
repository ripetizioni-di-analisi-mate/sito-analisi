A body in simple harmonic motion with amplitude $A$ is at $x = \dfrac{A}{3}$. What fraction of its mechanical energy is kinetic?

- $\dfrac{8}{9}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$

## Explanation

The potential energy is $U = \dfrac{1}{2}kx^2 = \dfrac{1}{2}k\dfrac{A^2}{9} = \dfrac{E}{9}$, therefore the kinetic energy is $K = E - U = \dfrac{8}{9}E$. The value $\dfrac{1}{9}$ is the potential fraction, and $\dfrac{2}{3}$ treats the energy as linear in $x$.
