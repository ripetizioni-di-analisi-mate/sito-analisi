An observer moves with speed $u$ directly away from a stationary source of frequency $f$, and sound travels at $v$. Which expression gives the frequency received?

- $f\,\dfrac{v + u}{v}$
- $f\,\dfrac{v}{v - u}$
- $f\,\dfrac{v - u}{v}$
- $f\,\dfrac{v}{v + u}$

## Explanation

In $f' = f\,\dfrac{v + v_o}{v - v_s}$ the observer speed is positive towards the source, hence an observer moving away has $v_o = -u$, while the source at rest has $v_s = 0$. The result is $f' = f\,\dfrac{v - u}{v}$, lower than $f$. The expression $f\,\dfrac{v}{v + u}$ describes a source receding from a stationary observer, which gives a different value.
