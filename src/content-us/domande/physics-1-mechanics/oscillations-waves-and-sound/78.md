A string $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$ long with linear mass density $2.0\,\mathrm{g/m}$ is fixed at both ends. What tension gives it a fundamental frequency of $200\,\mathrm{Hz}$?

- $8.0 \cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.40\,\mathrm{N}$
- $80\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The fundamental requires $v = 2Lf_1 = 200\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and $v = \sqrt{\dfrac{F_T}{\mu}}$ gives
$$
F_T = \mu\,(2Lf_1)^2 = 2.0 \cdot 10^{-3} \cdot (200)^2\,\mathrm{N} = 80\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The value $20\,\mathrm{N}$ takes $\lambda_1 = L$, $0.40\,\mathrm{N}$ omits the square, and $8.0 \cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{N}$ keeps $\mu$ in grams per meter.
