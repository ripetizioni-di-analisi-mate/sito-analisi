The wave equation $\dfrac{\partial^2 y}{\partial x^2} = \dfrac{1}{v^2}\dfrac{\partial^2 y}{\partial t^2}$ is linear. Which property of waves follows from this?

- Every solution is a sinusoidal function
- A pulse changes its shape while it travels
- The wave speed depends on the amplitude
- The sum of two solutions is also a solution

## Explanation

A linear equation is satisfied by any sum of its solutions, which is the **superposition principle**. Every function $f(x - vt)$ is a solution, therefore a pulse of any shape travels undistorted, and the speed $v$ does not depend on the amplitude.
