A source radiates $1.0\,\mathrm{W}$ of acoustic power uniformly in all directions, in a medium that absorbs no energy. What is the intensity at $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ from the source?

- $0.020\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $0.040\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $0.0050\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$

## Explanation

The power is spread over a sphere of area $4\pi r^2$, hence
$$
I = \frac{P}{4\pi r^2} = \frac{1.0}{4\pi \cdot 4.0}\,\mathrm{W/m^2} = 0.020\,\mathrm{W/m^2} .
$$
The value $0.040\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$ uses $r$ in place of $r^2$, $0.25\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$ omits $4\pi$, and $0.0050\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$ uses $4.0\,\mathrm{m}$ as the radius of the sphere.
