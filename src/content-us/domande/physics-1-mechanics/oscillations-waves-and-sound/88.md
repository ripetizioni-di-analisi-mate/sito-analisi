A source moving at speed $u$ towards a stationary observer is compared with an observer moving at speed $u$ towards a stationary source of the same frequency, with $u < v$. In which case is the received frequency higher?

- The two frequencies are equal
- With the observer moving
- With the source moving
- It depends on the frequency of the source

## Explanation

The moving source gives $f\,\dfrac{v}{v - u}$ and the moving observer gives $f\,\dfrac{v + u}{v}$. Their ratio is $\dfrac{v^2}{(v - u)(v + u)} = \dfrac{v^2}{v^2 - u^2} > 1$, therefore the frequency is higher with the source moving. The formula is not symmetric, since the medium fixes a preferred frame.
