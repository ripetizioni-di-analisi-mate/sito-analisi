A body of mass $0.10\,\mathrm{kg}$ oscillates with frequency $2.0\,\mathrm{Hz}$ and amplitude $0.15\,\mathrm{m}$. What is its mechanical energy?

- $0.0045\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0.36\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0.18\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The angular frequency is $\omega = 2\pi f = 12.6\,\mathrm{rad/s}$, hence
$$
E = \frac{1}{2}m\omega^2A^2 = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 0.10 \cdot (12.6)^2 \cdot (0.15)^2\,\mathrm{J} = 0.18\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
The value $0.0045\,\mathrm{J}$ uses $f$ in place of $\omega$, $0.36\,\mathrm{J}$ omits the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$, and $1.2\,\mathrm{J}$ omits the square of the amplitude.
