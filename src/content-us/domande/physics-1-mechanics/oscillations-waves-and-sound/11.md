At which positions is the magnitude of the acceleration of a particle in simple harmonic motion largest?

- At $x = \pm A$
- It has the same value at every position
- At $x = \pm\dfrac{A}{2}$
- At $x = 0$

## Explanation

The acceleration is $a = -\omega^2 x$, hence its magnitude $\omega^2|x|$ grows with the distance from equilibrium and is largest at the turning points $x = \pm A$. At $x = 0$ the acceleration vanishes, while the speed is largest.
