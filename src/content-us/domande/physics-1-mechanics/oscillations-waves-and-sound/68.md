A harmonic wave on a string is $y(x,t) = A\sin(kx - \omega t)$. What is the transverse velocity $\dfrac{\partial y}{\partial t}$ of the particle at $x = 0$ at $t = 0$?

- $+\omega A$
- $-\omega A$
- $0$
- $-kA$

## Explanation

Differentiating with respect to $t$ at fixed $x$ gives $\dfrac{\partial y}{\partial t} = -\omega A\cos(kx - \omega t)$, which at $x = 0$ and $t = 0$ equals $-\omega A$. The minus sign comes from the derivative of $-\omega t$; the particle passes through $y = 0$ with its largest speed, moving downward.
