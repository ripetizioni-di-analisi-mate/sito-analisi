A string fixed at both ends vibrates in its third harmonic. How many nodes does the standing wave have, including the two ends?

- $6$
- $3$
- $4$
- $5$

## Explanation

In the $n$-th mode the string is $\dfrac{n}{2}$ wavelengths long, hence it contains $n$ segments of length $\dfrac{\lambda}{2}$ separated by nodes. Three segments have $3 + 1 = 4$ nodes, the two ends included, and $3$ antinodes.
