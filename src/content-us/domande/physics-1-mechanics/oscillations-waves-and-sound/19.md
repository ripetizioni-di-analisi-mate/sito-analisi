The mass of a body attached to a given spring is multiplied by $4$. How does the period of its oscillations change?

- It is multiplied by $4$
- It doubles
- It is halved
- It does not change

## Explanation

The period is $T = 2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k}}$, hence it is proportional to $\sqrt{m}$, and multiplying the mass by $4$ multiplies the period by $\sqrt{4} = 2$. A larger mass has a larger inertia, therefore the oscillation is slower.
