In a longitudinal mechanical wave, how do the particles of the medium move?

- They oscillate perpendicular to the direction of propagation
- They oscillate along the direction of propagation
- They travel with the wave at the wave speed
- They move along circles in the plane perpendicular to the propagation

## Explanation

In a longitudinal wave the particles oscillate about their equilibrium positions along the direction of propagation, as in a column of air. The particles do not travel with the wave: the wave carries energy and momentum, not matter.
