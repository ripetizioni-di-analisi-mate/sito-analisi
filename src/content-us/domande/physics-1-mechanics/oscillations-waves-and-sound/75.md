A string $1.20\,\mathrm{m}$ long is fixed at both ends. What is the wavelength of its fourth harmonic?

- $4.8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.60\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.4\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The normal modes have $\lambda_n = \dfrac{2L}{n}$, hence $\lambda_4 = \dfrac{2 \cdot 1.20}{4}\,\mathrm{m} = 0.60\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$ omits the factor $2$, and $2.4\,\mathrm{m}$ is the wavelength of the fundamental.
