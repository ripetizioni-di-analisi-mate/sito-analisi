A simple pendulum swings with angular amplitude $30^\circ$. How does its exact period compare with $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{L}{g}}$?

- It is equal
- It is smaller by about $1.7\%$
- It is larger by about $1.7\%$
- It is larger by about $17\%$

## Explanation

The exact period exceeds the harmonic one by the factor $1 + \dfrac{\theta_0^2}{16} + \dots$ with $\theta_0$ in radians. For $\theta_0 = 30^\circ = 0.524\,\mathrm{rad}$ the correction is $\dfrac{(0.524)^2}{16} = 0.017$, that is about $1.7\%$.
