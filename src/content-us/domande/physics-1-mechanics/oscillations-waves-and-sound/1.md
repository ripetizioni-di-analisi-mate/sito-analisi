An oscillator completes $15$ cycles in $6.0\,\mathrm{s}$. What is its angular frequency?

- $15.7\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $0.400\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $1.05\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $2.50\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

The frequency is the number of cycles per unit time, $f = \dfrac{15}{6.0\,\mathrm{s}} = 2.50\,\mathrm{Hz}$, hence
$$
\omega = 2\pi f = 2\pi \cdot 2.50\,\mathrm{rad/s} = 15.7\,\mathrm{rad/s} .
$$
The value $2.50\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ is the frequency without the factor $2\pi$, the value $0.400$ is the period in seconds, and the value $1.05\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ takes the whole $6.0\,\mathrm{s}$ as one period.
