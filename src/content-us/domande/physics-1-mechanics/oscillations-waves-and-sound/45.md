In the equation of motion of a physical pendulum the force exerted by the axis on the body does not appear. Why?

- It is always perpendicular to the velocity of the center of mass
- It vanishes for small angles
- It is balanced by the weight at every instant
- Its moment arm about the axis is zero

## Explanation

The equation used is the second law for fixed-axis rotation, $I\ddot{\theta} = \tau$, which contains only torques about the axis. The force of the axis acts at the axis itself, hence its moment arm and its torque vanish. The force is not zero, and it does not balance the weight while the body swings.
