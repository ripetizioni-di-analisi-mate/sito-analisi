On the same axes are drawn the parabola $U(x) = \dfrac{1}{2}kx^2$ and the horizontal line at height $E$, the mechanical energy of the oscillator. What represents the kinetic energy at the position $x$?

- The area under the parabola between $0$ and $x$
- The vertical gap between the line and the parabola at $x$
- The height of the line
- The height of the parabola at $x$

## Explanation

The mechanical energy is conserved, hence $K = E - U(x)$ at each position, which is the vertical distance from the parabola up to the line. The height of the parabola is the potential energy, and the height of the line is the total energy.
