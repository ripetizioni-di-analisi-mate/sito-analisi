A simple pendulum of length $0.750\,\mathrm{m}$ has a measured period of $1.74\,\mathrm{s}$ for small oscillations. What value of $g$ does the measurement give?

- $9.78\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $17.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2.44\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0.248\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Solving $T = 2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{L}{g}}$ for $g$ gives
$$
g = \frac{4\pi^2 L}{T^2} = \frac{39.5 \cdot 0.750}{(1.74)^2}\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 9.78\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
The value $17.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ uses $T$ in place of $T^2$, $0.248\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ omits $4\pi^2$, and $2.44\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ squares $\pi$ but not the factor $2$.
