The position of a particle satisfies $\ddot{x} = -25\,x$, in SI units. What is the period of its motion?

- $0.796\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.26\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.251\,\mathrm{s}$
- $31.4\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Comparison with $\ddot{x} = -\omega^2 x$ gives $\omega^2 = 25\,\mathrm{s^{-2}}$, hence $\omega = 5.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ and
$$
T = \frac{2\pi}{\omega} = \frac{2\pi}{5.0}\,\mathrm{s} = 1.26\,\mathrm{s} .
$$
The value $0.251\,\mathrm{s}$ divides $2\pi$ by $\omega^2$, the value $0.796$ is the frequency in hertz, and $31.4\,\mathrm{s}$ is $2\pi\omega$.
