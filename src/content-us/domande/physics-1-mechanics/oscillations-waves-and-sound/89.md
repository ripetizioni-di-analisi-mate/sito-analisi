Two tuning forks of frequencies $512\,\mathrm{Hz}$ and $518\,\mathrm{Hz}$ sound together with equal amplitudes. What does a listener hear?

- A tone of $515\,\mathrm{Hz}$ whose loudness peaks $3$ times per second
- A tone of $1030\,\mathrm{Hz}$ whose loudness peaks $6$ times per second
- A tone of $6\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- A tone of $515\,\mathrm{Hz}$ whose loudness peaks $6$ times per second

## Explanation

The sum of the two sounds is a rapid oscillation at the average frequency $\dfrac{512 + 518}{2}\,\mathrm{Hz} = 515\,\mathrm{Hz}$, modulated by a slow factor. The loudness peaks twice in each period of the modulation, hence $f_{\mathrm{beat}} = |f_1 - f_2| = 6\,\mathrm{Hz}$. The value $3$ per second is the frequency of the modulating cosine, $\dfrac{|f_1 - f_2|}{2}$.
