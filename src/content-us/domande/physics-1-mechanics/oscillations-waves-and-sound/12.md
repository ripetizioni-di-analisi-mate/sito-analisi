A particle in simple harmonic motion with $\omega = 5.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ is at $x = -0.040\,\mathrm{m}$. What is its acceleration $a_x$?

- $-1.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $+1.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $+0.20\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-0.20\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The acceleration is
$$
a_x = -\omega^2 x = -25 \cdot (-0.040)\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = +1.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2} ,
$$
directed towards the origin, as for any restoring force. The value $-1.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ loses the sign of $x$, and the values $\pm 0.20\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ use $\omega$ in place of $\omega^2$.
