Both the mass of a mass-spring oscillator and the spring constant are doubled. How does the period change?

- It does not change
- It doubles
- It is multiplied by $\sqrt{2}$
- It is halved

## Explanation

The period $T = 2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k}}$ depends only on the ratio $\dfrac{m}{k}$, and doubling both quantities leaves the ratio unchanged. Hence the period does not change.
