A string $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$ long is fixed at both ends, and transverse waves travel on it at $240\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is its fundamental frequency?

- $75\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $300\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $192\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $150\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

The fundamental has $\lambda_1 = 2L$, hence
$$
f_1 = \frac{v}{2L} = \frac{240}{1.6}\,\mathrm{Hz} = 150\,\mathrm{Hz} .
$$
The value $300\,\mathrm{Hz}$ takes $\lambda_1 = L$, $75\,\mathrm{Hz}$ takes $\lambda_1 = 4L$, and $192$ is the product $vL$.
