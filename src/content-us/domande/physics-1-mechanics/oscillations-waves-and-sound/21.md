On an orbiting station a chair attached to a spring of constant $600\,\mathrm{N/m}$ oscillates with period $2.0\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the mass of the chair?

- $61\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $30\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $2.4 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $1.9 \cdot 10^{2}\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Solving $T = 2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k}}$ for the mass gives
$$
m = \frac{k\,T^2}{4\pi^2} = \frac{600 \cdot 4.0}{39.5}\,\mathrm{kg} = 61\,\mathrm{kg} .
$$
The period depends on the inertia and not on the weight, therefore the method works where a balance does not. The value $30\,\mathrm{kg}$ uses $T$ in place of $T^2$, the value $1.9 \cdot 10^{2}\,\mathrm{kg}$ is $\dfrac{kT}{2\pi}$, and $2.4 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg}$ omits $4\pi^2$.
