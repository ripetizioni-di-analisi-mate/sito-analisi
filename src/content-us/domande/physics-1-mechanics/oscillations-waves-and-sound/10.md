A particle in simple harmonic motion about $x = 0$ is at $x = \dfrac{A}{2}$ and moves toward $x = A$. What are the signs of its velocity $v_x$ and of its acceleration $a_x$?

- $v_x > 0$ and $a_x > 0$
- $v_x < 0$ and $a_x < 0$
- $v_x > 0$ and $a_x < 0$
- $v_x < 0$ and $a_x > 0$

## Explanation

The particle moves toward larger $x$, therefore $v_x > 0$. The acceleration is $a_x = -\omega^2 x$, and $x = \dfrac{A}{2} > 0$, therefore $a_x < 0$. The velocity and the acceleration have opposite signs, hence the particle is slowing down on its way to the turning point.
