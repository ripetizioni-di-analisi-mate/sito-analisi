A student doubles the frequency with which she shakes the end of a long string, while the tension is unchanged. How does the wavelength change?

- It doubles
- It is divided by $\sqrt{2}$
- It is halved
- It does not change

## Explanation

The wave speed $v = \sqrt{\dfrac{F_T}{\mu}}$ depends only on the string, hence it does not change. Since $\lambda = \dfrac{v}{f}$, doubling the frequency halves the wavelength.
