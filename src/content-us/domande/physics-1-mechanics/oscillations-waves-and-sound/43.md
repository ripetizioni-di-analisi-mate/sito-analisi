A uniform disk of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ and radius $0.20\,\mathrm{m}$ has $I_{\mathrm{cm}} = \dfrac{1}{2}MR^2$. What is its moment of inertia about an axis perpendicular to the disk through a point of its rim?

- $0.36\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$
- $0.12\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$
- $0.080\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$
- $0.040\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$

## Explanation

The axis through the rim is parallel to the axis through the center at distance $d = R$, hence by the **parallel-axis theorem**
$$
I = \frac{1}{2}MR^2 + MR^2 = \frac{3}{2} \cdot 2.0 \cdot (0.20)^2\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2} = 0.12\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2} .
$$
The value $0.040\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$ is $I_{\mathrm{cm}}$, $0.080\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$ is $Md^2$ alone, and $0.36\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$ uses the diameter as the distance $d$.
