The third harmonic of a string fixed at both ends has frequency $540\,\mathrm{Hz}$. What is the fundamental frequency?

- $180\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $360\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $270\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $1620\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

The harmonics are integer multiples of the fundamental, $f_n = nf_1$, hence $f_1 = \dfrac{540}{3}\,\mathrm{Hz} = 180\,\mathrm{Hz}$. The value $1620\,\mathrm{Hz}$ multiplies by $3$ instead of dividing, and $360\,\mathrm{Hz}$ is the second harmonic.
