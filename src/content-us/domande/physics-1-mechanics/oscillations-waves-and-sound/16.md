A particle moves along the $x$ axis with $x(t) = 0.12\cos(3\pi t)\,\mathrm{m}$, where $t$ is in seconds. What is the frequency of the motion?

- $3.0\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $9.4\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $0.67\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

The angular frequency is $\omega = 3\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}$, hence
$$
f = \frac{\omega}{2\pi} = \frac{3\pi}{2\pi}\,\mathrm{Hz} = 1.5\,\mathrm{Hz} .
$$
The value $9.4$ is $\omega$ itself, the value $0.67$ is the period in seconds, and $3.0\,\mathrm{Hz}$ divides $\omega$ by $\pi$ instead of $2\pi$.
