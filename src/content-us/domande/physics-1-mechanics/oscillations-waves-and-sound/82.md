The intensity level at a point rises from $60\,\mathrm{dB}$ to $90\,\mathrm{dB}$. By what factor has the intensity been multiplied?

- $3.0$
- $1.5$
- $1000$
- $30$

## Explanation

The difference of levels is $30\,\mathrm{dB} = 10\log_{10}\dfrac{I_2}{I_1}$, hence $\log_{10}\dfrac{I_2}{I_1} = 3$ and $\dfrac{I_2}{I_1} = 10^3 = 1000$. The value $1.5$ is the ratio of the levels, which has no physical meaning on a logarithmic scale.
