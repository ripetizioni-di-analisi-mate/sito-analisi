A harmonic wave of amplitude $2.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ and frequency $50\,\mathrm{Hz}$ travels on a string at $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the maximum transverse speed of a particle of the string?

- $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.40\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Each particle performs simple harmonic motion of amplitude $A$ and angular frequency $\omega = 2\pi f$, hence
$$
v_{y,\max} = \omega A = 2\pi \cdot 50 \cdot 0.020\,\mathrm{m/s} = 6.3\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The value $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is the wave speed, a different quantity; $1.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ uses $f$ in place of $\omega$, and $0.40$ is the wavelength in meters.
