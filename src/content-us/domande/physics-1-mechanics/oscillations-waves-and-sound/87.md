A car with its siren on passes a person standing at the roadside. How does the pitch heard by the person compare with the emitted one?

- Higher both while the car approaches and while it recedes
- Higher while the car approaches, lower while it recedes
- Lower while the car approaches, higher while it recedes
- Equal to the emitted pitch throughout

## Explanation

In $f' = f\,\dfrac{v}{v - v_s}$ the source speed is positive while the car approaches, which gives $f' > f$, and negative while it recedes, which gives $f' < f$. The pitch therefore drops as the car passes.
