A body of mass $0.25\,\mathrm{kg}$ performs underdamped oscillations under a resistive force $-b\dot{x}$ with $b = 0.10\,\mathrm{kg/s}$. By what factor is the amplitude multiplied after $10\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $0.37$
- $0.018$
- $0.86$
- $0.14$

## Explanation

The amplitude decays as $e^{-\gamma t}$ with $\gamma = \dfrac{b}{2m} = 0.20\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$, hence after $10\,\mathrm{s}$ it is multiplied by $e^{-2.0} = 0.14$. The value $0.018$ uses $\gamma = \dfrac{b}{m}$, $0.86$ is the fraction lost, and $0.37 = e^{-1}$ is the factor after one decay time $\tau = 5.0\,\mathrm{s}$.
