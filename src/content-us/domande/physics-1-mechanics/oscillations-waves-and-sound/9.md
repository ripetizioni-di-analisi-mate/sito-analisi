A particle in simple harmonic motion is at $x = A$ at $t = 0$ and passes through $x = 0$ for the first time at $t = 0.25\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the period?

- $0.25\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.50\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.6\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

From $x = A$ to the equilibrium position the particle covers one quarter of a cycle, hence $\dfrac{T}{4} = 0.25\,\mathrm{s}$ and $T = 1.0\,\mathrm{s}$. The value $0.50\,\mathrm{s}$ treats the interval as half a cycle, and the value $1.6\,\mathrm{s}$ multiplies $0.25\,\mathrm{s}$ by $2\pi$.
