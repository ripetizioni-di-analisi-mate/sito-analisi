A damped oscillator with natural angular frequency $\omega_0$ is driven by the force $F_0\cos\omega_d t$. With what angular frequency does it oscillate once the transient has decayed?

- $\omega_d$
- $\omega_0 + \omega_d$
- $\omega_0$
- $\sqrt{\omega_0^2 - \gamma^2}$

## Explanation

The free part of the motion, with angular frequency $\sqrt{\omega_0^2 - \gamma^2}$, decays as $e^{-\gamma t}$. What remains is $x = A(\omega_d)\cos(\omega_d t - \delta)$, which follows the driving force at its angular frequency $\omega_d$.
