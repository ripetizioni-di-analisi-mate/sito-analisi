A body of mass $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ hangs from a spring of constant $100\,\mathrm{N/m}$ and is subject to a resistive force $-b\dot{x}$. For which value of $b$ is the motion critically damped?

- $200\,\mathrm{kg/s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{kg/s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kg/s}$
- $40\,\mathrm{kg/s}$

## Explanation

Critical damping requires $\gamma = \omega_0$, that is $\dfrac{b}{2m} = \sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m}} = 10\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$, hence $b = 2m\omega_0 = 20\,\mathrm{kg/s}$. The value $10\,\mathrm{kg/s}$ forgets the factor $2$ in $\gamma = \dfrac{b}{2m}$, and $200\,\mathrm{kg/s}$ uses $\omega_0^2$ in place of $\omega_0$.
