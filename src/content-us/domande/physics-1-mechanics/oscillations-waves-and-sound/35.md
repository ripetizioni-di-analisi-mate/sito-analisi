The bob of a simple pendulum is replaced by one of twice the mass, with the length unchanged. How does the period of the small oscillations change?

- It is multiplied by $\sqrt{2}$
- It doubles
- It is divided by $\sqrt{2}$
- It does not change

## Explanation

The mass appears on both sides of $mL\ddot{\theta} = -mg\sin\theta$ and cancels, therefore the period $T = 2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{L}{g}}$ depends only on the length and on $g$.
