The length of a simple pendulum is multiplied by $9$. How does the period of its small oscillations change?

- It is multiplied by $\sqrt{3}$
- It is divided by $3$
- It is multiplied by $9$
- It is multiplied by $3$

## Explanation

The period $T = 2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{L}{g}}$ is proportional to $\sqrt{L}$, hence multiplying the length by $9$ multiplies the period by $\sqrt{9} = 3$.
