A standing wave on a string is $y(x,t) = 0.040\sin(\pi x)\cos(50\,t)$, with $x$ and $y$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. What is the amplitude of oscillation of the particle at $x = 0.25\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $0.028\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.057\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.020\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.040\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The amplitude at $x$ is $0.040\,|\sin\pi x|\,\mathrm{m}$, hence at $x = 0.25\,\mathrm{m}$ it is
$$
0.040\sin\frac{\pi}{4}\,\mathrm{m} = 0.040 \cdot 0.707\,\mathrm{m} = 0.028\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $0.040\,\mathrm{m}$ is the amplitude at the antinodes, $0.020\,\mathrm{m}$ is the amplitude of each of the two traveling waves, and $0.057\,\mathrm{m}$ doubles the correct value a second time.
