A block of mass $0.20\,\mathrm{kg}$ attached to a spring of constant $80\,\mathrm{N/m}$ passes through the equilibrium position with speed $1.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the amplitude of its oscillation?

- $4.2\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $8.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.36\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $6.0\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The conservation of energy gives $\dfrac{1}{2}mv_{\max}^2 = \dfrac{1}{2}kA^2$, hence
$$
A = v_{\max}\sqrt{\frac{m}{k}} = 1.2\sqrt{\frac{0.20}{80}}\,\mathrm{m} = 0.060\,\mathrm{m} = 6.0\,\mathrm{cm} .
$$
The values $4.2\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $8.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ keep the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$ on one side of the balance only, and $0.36\,\mathrm{cm}$ omits the square root.
