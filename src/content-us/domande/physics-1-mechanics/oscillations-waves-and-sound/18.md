A block of mass $0.50\,\mathrm{kg}$ on a frictionless horizontal surface is attached to a spring of constant $200\,\mathrm{N/m}$. What is the period of its oscillations?

- $0.016\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.050\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.31\,\mathrm{s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The period of the mass-spring oscillator is
$$
T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{m}{k}} = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{0.50}{200}}\,\mathrm{s} = 2\pi \cdot 0.050\,\mathrm{s} = 0.31\,\mathrm{s} .
$$
The value $0.050\,\mathrm{s}$ omits the factor $2\pi$, the value $0.016\,\mathrm{s}$ omits the square root, and $20$ is $\omega$ in $\mathrm{rad/s}$.
