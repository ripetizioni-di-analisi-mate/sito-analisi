Which of the following functions, with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds, satisfies the wave equation with wave speed $v = 2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$?

- $y = A\sin(2.0\,x - 2.0\,t)$
- $y = A\sin(6.0\,x - 3.0\,t)$
- $y = A\sin(3.0\,x - 2.0\,t)$
- $y = A\sin(3.0\,x - 6.0\,t)$

## Explanation

A function $A\sin(kx - \omega t)$ satisfies the wave equation with speed $v = \dfrac{\omega}{k}$. Only $\dfrac{6.0}{3.0} = 2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ gives the required speed; the other functions travel at $0.50$, $0.67$ and $1.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
