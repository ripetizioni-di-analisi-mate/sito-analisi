A constant force $\vec{F} = (4.0\,\hat{\imath} + 3.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{N}$ acts on a body during the displacement $\vec{d} = (2.0\,\hat{\imath} - 1.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m}$. How much work does the force do?

- $11\,\mathrm{J}$
- $5.0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-5.0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $8.0\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The work is the scalar product of the force and the displacement, computed component by component:
$$
W = F_x\,d_x + F_y\,d_y = 4.0 \cdot 2.0\,\mathrm{J} + 3.0 \cdot (-1.0)\,\mathrm{J} = 5.0\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
The value $11\,\mathrm{J}$ comes from ignoring the minus sign of $d_y$, and $8.0\,\mathrm{J}$ from keeping the $x$ components alone.
