A sled is pulled $5.0\,\mathrm{m}$ along horizontal snow by a rope that exerts $60\,\mathrm{N}$ at $30^\circ$ above the horizontal, while kinetic friction exerts $40\,\mathrm{N}$ on it. What is the total work done on the sled?

- $100\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-50\,\mathrm{J}$
- $460\,\mathrm{J}$
- $60\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The rope does $W_T = 60\,\mathrm{N} \cdot 5.0\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \cos 30^\circ = 260\,\mathrm{J}$ and friction does $W_f = -40\,\mathrm{N} \cdot 5.0\,\mathrm{m} = -200\,\mathrm{J}$. The weight and the normal force do no work, hence
$$
W_{\text{tot}} = 260\,\mathrm{J} - 200\,\mathrm{J} = 60\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
Omitting the cosine gives $100\,\mathrm{J}$, and using the sine gives $-50\,\mathrm{J}$.
