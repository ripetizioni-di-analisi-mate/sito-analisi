A ball is thrown vertically upwards, reaches the height $h$ above the hand and falls back into the same hand. Air resistance is negligible. How much work does the weight do on the ball during the whole flight?

- $-2\,m g h$
- $2\,m g h$
- $-m g h$
- $0$

## Explanation

The work of the weight is $W = -m g\,(y_2 - y_1)$, and it depends only on the initial and the final height. The ball returns to its starting point, so $y_2 = y_1$ and $W = 0$. During the rise the weight does $-m g h$, and during the fall it does $+m g h$.
