An engine delivers $15\,\mathrm{kW}$. What is its power in horsepower, with $1\,\mathrm{hp} = 746\,\mathrm{W}$?

- $11.2\,\mathrm{hp}$
- $15.0\,\mathrm{hp}$
- $20.1\,\mathrm{hp}$
- $2.01\,\mathrm{hp}$

## Explanation

The power in watts is $1.5 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{W}$, so
$$
P = \frac{1.5 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{W}}{746\,\mathrm{W/hp}} = 20.1\,\mathrm{hp} .
$$
Multiplying by $0.746$ instead of dividing gives $11.2\,\mathrm{hp}$.
