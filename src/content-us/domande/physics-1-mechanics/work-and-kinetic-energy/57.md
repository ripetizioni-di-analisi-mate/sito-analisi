Crane A raises a load to a given height in $10\,\mathrm{s}$, and crane B raises an identical load to the same height in $20\,\mathrm{s}$, both at constant speed. How do the works and the average powers of the two cranes compare?

- The work of A is twice the work of B; equal powers.
- Equal works; the power of A is twice the power of B.
- Equal works and equal powers.
- Equal works; the power of B is twice the power of A.

## Explanation

At constant speed each crane exerts a force equal to the weight of the load over the same height, hence both do the work $m g h$. The average power is $P_{\text{av}} = \dfrac{\Delta W}{\Delta t}$, and A does the same work in half the time, so its power is twice the power of B.
