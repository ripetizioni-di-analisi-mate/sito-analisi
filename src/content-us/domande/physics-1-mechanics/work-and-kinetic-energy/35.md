A spring of constant $400\,\mathrm{N/m}$ is stretched from its natural length, and the spring force does $-2.0\,\mathrm{J}$ of work. By how much is the spring stretched?

- $7.1\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $1.0\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $14\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $10\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

From the natural length the spring force does $W = -\dfrac{1}{2}k\,x^2$, hence
$$
x = \sqrt{\frac{2\,|W|}{k}} = \sqrt{\frac{2 \cdot 2.0\,\mathrm{J}}{400\,\mathrm{N/m}}} = 0.10\,\mathrm{m} = 10\,\mathrm{cm} .
$$
Forgetting the square root gives $0.010\,\mathrm{m}$, and losing the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$ gives $7.1\,\mathrm{cm}$.
