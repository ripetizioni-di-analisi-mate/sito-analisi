Several constant forces act on a body during the displacement $\vec{d}$. Which statement about the total work is correct?

- It equals the sum of the magnitudes of the forces times the magnitude of $\vec{d}$.
- It equals the scalar product of the resultant force with $\vec{d}$.
- It equals the work of the largest force.
- It equals the magnitude of the resultant times the magnitude of $\vec{d}$, whatever the direction of the resultant.

## Explanation

The scalar product is distributive with respect to the sum of vectors, hence
$$
W_{\text{tot}} = \vec{F}_1 \cdot \vec{d} + \dots + \vec{F}_n \cdot \vec{d} = \vec{R} \cdot \vec{d} .
$$
The direction of the resultant matters, because the scalar product contains the cosine of the angle between $\vec{R}$ and $\vec{d}$.
