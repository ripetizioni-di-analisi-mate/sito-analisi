A puck slides at $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ on a horizontal surface, and kinetic friction exerts a force of $0.30\,\mathrm{N}$ on it. What is the instantaneous power delivered by friction at that moment?

- $-1.5\,\mathrm{W}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{W}$
- $0\,\mathrm{W}$
- $-0.060\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

Friction is opposite to the velocity, so $\varphi = 180^\circ$ and
$$
P = F\,v\cos 180^\circ = -0.30\,\mathrm{N} \cdot 5.0\,\mathrm{m/s} = -1.5\,\mathrm{W} .
$$
The power is negative because friction takes kinetic energy away from the puck.
