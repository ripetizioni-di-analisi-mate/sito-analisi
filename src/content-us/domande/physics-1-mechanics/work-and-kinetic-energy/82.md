A hand lifts a book of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ through $1.0\,\mathrm{m}$, pushing it upwards with a constant force of $25\,\mathrm{N}$. What is the total work done on the book?

- $25\,\mathrm{J}$
- $44.6\,\mathrm{J}$
- $5.4\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-19.6\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The hand does $W_h = 25\,\mathrm{N} \cdot 1.0\,\mathrm{m} = 25\,\mathrm{J}$, and the weight does $W_P = -2.0\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot 1.0\,\mathrm{m} = -19.6\,\mathrm{J}$. Therefore
$$
W_{\text{tot}} = 25\,\mathrm{J} - 19.6\,\mathrm{J} = 5.4\,\mathrm{J} ,
$$
and by the **work-energy theorem** the kinetic energy of the book increases by that amount.
