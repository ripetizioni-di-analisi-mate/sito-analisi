A stone of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ falls vertically, and at a given instant its speed is $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the instantaneous power delivered by the weight at that instant?

- $-58.9\,\mathrm{W}$
- $58.9\,\mathrm{W}$
- $6.0\,\mathrm{W}$
- $29.4\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

The weight points downwards, in the same direction as the velocity, so $\varphi = 0$ and
$$
P = m g\,v = 2.0\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot 3.0\,\mathrm{m/s} = 58.9\,\mathrm{W} .
$$
The power is positive because the weight increases the speed of the stone.
