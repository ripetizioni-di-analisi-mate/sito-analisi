A rope pulls a sled with a constant force of $50\,\mathrm{N}$ directed at $30^\circ$ above the horizontal, while the sled moves $8.0\,\mathrm{m}$ along a horizontal path. How much work does the rope do?

- $346\,\mathrm{J}$
- $200\,\mathrm{J}$
- $400\,\mathrm{J}$
- $173\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Only the component of the force along the displacement does work, and that component is $F\cos 30^\circ$. Therefore
$$
W = F\,d\cos 30^\circ = 50\,\mathrm{N} \cdot 8.0\,\mathrm{m} \cdot 0.866 = 346\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
The value $200\,\mathrm{J}$ comes from using $\sin 30^\circ$, which is the factor of the vertical component.
