The resultant of the forces acting on a body has magnitude $18\,\mathrm{N}$ and makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with the displacement, which has magnitude $4.0\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the total work done on the body?

- $72\,\mathrm{J}$
- $36\,\mathrm{J}$
- $62\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-36\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The total work equals the work of the resultant, therefore
$$
W_{\text{tot}} = R\,d\cos 60^\circ = 18\,\mathrm{N} \cdot 4.0\,\mathrm{m} \cdot 0.50 = 36\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
The value $62\,\mathrm{J}$ comes from using $\sin 60^\circ$.
