Under which condition does Hooke's law $F_x = -k\,x$ describe the force of a spring?

- The spring is compressed, and never stretched.
- The free end of the spring moves at constant speed.
- The deformation can be of any size, however large.
- The deformation is small enough for the spring to return to its natural length.

## Explanation

**Hooke's law** is an experimental law that holds within the elastic range of the spring, that is, as long as the deformation is small enough for the spring to return to its natural length when released. It holds both for stretches and for compressions, and it does not depend on how the free end moves.
