A crate is pushed at constant velocity for $4.0\,\mathrm{m}$ along a rough horizontal floor by a horizontal force of $60\,\mathrm{N}$. How much work does kinetic friction do on the crate?

- $240\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-240\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-120\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The velocity is constant, so the resultant force is zero and the friction force has the same magnitude as the push, $f = 60\,\mathrm{N}$. Friction is opposite to the displacement, therefore
$$
W_f = -f\,d = -60\,\mathrm{N} \cdot 4.0\,\mathrm{m} = -240\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
The total work is zero, while the work of friction alone is not.
