A block of mass $0.40\,\mathrm{kg}$ leaves a spring with kinetic energy $3.0\,\mathrm{J}$ and slides on a horizontal surface with coefficient of kinetic friction $0.25$. What distance does it travel before stopping?

- $1.53\,\mathrm{m}$
- $30.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6.12\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3.06\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Friction has magnitude $\mu_k m g$ and does the work $-\mu_k m g\,d$. By the **work-energy theorem** $-\mu_k m g\,d = -K_1$, hence
$$
d = \frac{K_1}{\mu_k\,m g} = \frac{3.0\,\mathrm{J}}{0.25 \cdot 0.40\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}} = 3.06\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
Dividing by $\mu_k m$ without the factor $g$ gives $30.0\,\mathrm{m}$.
