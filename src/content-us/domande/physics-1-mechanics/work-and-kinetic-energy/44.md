A bead of mass $0.20\,\mathrm{kg}$ slides along a wire from a point $A$ to a point $B$ at the same height, following an arc that first rises by $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$ and then comes back down. How much work does the weight do between $A$ and $B$?

- $0.98\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-0.98\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1.96\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

By the theorem on the **work done by the weight**, $W = -m g\,(y_B - y_A)$ along any path. The points $A$ and $B$ are at the same height, therefore $W = 0$. The rise and the descent along the arc contribute $-0.98\,\mathrm{J}$ and $+0.98\,\mathrm{J}$, which cancel.
