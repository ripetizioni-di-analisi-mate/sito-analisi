The mass of a body is halved and its speed is doubled. By what factor does its kinetic energy change?

- It is multiplied by $2$.
- It does not change.
- It is multiplied by $4$.
- It is divided by $2$.

## Explanation

The kinetic energy is $K = \dfrac{1}{2}m v^2$. Halving the mass divides it by $2$, while doubling the speed multiplies it by $4$, therefore the kinetic energy is multiplied by $\dfrac{4}{2} = 2$.
