Which condition is required for the work-energy theorem $W_{\text{tot}} = \Delta K$ to hold for a particle?

- The motion is described in an inertial frame, and $W_{\text{tot}}$ includes the work of every force acting on the particle.
- The forces acting on the particle are constant.
- The particle moves along a straight line.
- No friction acts on the particle.

## Explanation

The theorem follows from **Newton's second law**, which holds in an inertial frame, and it applies to the total work, that is, to the work of the resultant of all the forces. Its proof holds for variable forces, for any path and in the presence of friction.
