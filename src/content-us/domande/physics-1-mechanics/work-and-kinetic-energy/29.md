A spring of constant $300\,\mathrm{N/m}$ lies along the $x$ axis, with its free end at the origin at natural length. The free end is pushed to $x = -0.10\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the component $F_x$ of the force the spring exerts?

- $-30\,\mathrm{N}$
- $+1.5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-1.5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $+30\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

By **Hooke's law** $F_x = -k\,x$, therefore
$$
F_x = -300\,\mathrm{N/m} \cdot (-0.10\,\mathrm{m}) = +30\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The spring is compressed, so it pushes the free end back toward the origin, in the positive direction of the axis.
