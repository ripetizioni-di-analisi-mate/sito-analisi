Which combination of SI base units is equal to the joule?

- $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s^2}$
- $\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s^2}$
- $\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$

## Explanation

Work is a force times a displacement, hence $1\,\mathrm{J} = 1\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$. The newton is $1\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s^2}$ by Newton's second law, therefore $1\,\mathrm{J} = 1\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s^2}$. The combination $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s^2}$ is the newton itself, and $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$ is the unit of momentum.
