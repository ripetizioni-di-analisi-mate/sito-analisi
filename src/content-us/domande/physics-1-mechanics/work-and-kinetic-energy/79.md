A crate is pushed at constant speed along a rough horizontal floor. Which statement about the works done on the crate is correct?

- No force does work on the crate.
- The push does zero work, because the speed is constant.
- The total work is zero, while the push and friction do works of equal magnitude and opposite sign.
- The total work equals the work of the push.

## Explanation

The speed is constant, so $\Delta K = 0$ and by the **work-energy theorem** the total work is zero. The push points in the direction of the motion and does positive work, while friction does negative work of the same magnitude. The theorem constrains only the total work.
