A block of mass $0.20\,\mathrm{kg}$ is to be launched at $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ by a spring of constant $180\,\mathrm{N/m}$ on a horizontal frictionless surface. By how much must the spring be compressed?

- $0.010\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.14\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.071\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The spring force is the only force doing work, and from the compression $x$ to the natural length it does $\dfrac{1}{2}k\,x^2$. By the **work-energy theorem** $\dfrac{1}{2}k\,x^2 = \dfrac{1}{2}m v^2$, so
$$
x = v\sqrt{\frac{m}{k}} = 3.0\,\mathrm{m/s} \cdot \sqrt{\frac{0.20\,\mathrm{kg}}{180\,\mathrm{N/m}}} = 0.10\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
Forgetting the square root gives $0.010\,\mathrm{m}$.
