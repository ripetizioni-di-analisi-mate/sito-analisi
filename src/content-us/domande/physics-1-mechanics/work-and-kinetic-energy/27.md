A body moves along the $x$ axis from $x = 0$ to $x = 1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ under a force whose component is $F_x = b\,x^3$, with $b = 4.0\,\mathrm{N/m^3}$. How much work does the force do?

- $5.06\,\mathrm{J}$
- $20.3\,\mathrm{J}$
- $13.5\,\mathrm{J}$
- $6.75\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

By the definition of the **work of a variable force**
$$
W = \int_{0}^{1.5} b\,x^3\,dx = \frac{b\,x^4}{4}\bigg|_{0}^{1.5} = \frac{4.0 \cdot (1.5)^4}{4}\,\mathrm{J} = 5.06\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
The value $20.3\,\mathrm{J}$ is the final force times the displacement, which treats the force as constant.
