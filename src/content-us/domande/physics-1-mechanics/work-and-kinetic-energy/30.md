A body of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ hangs at rest from a vertical spring, which is stretched by $4.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ with respect to its natural length. What is the spring constant?

- $4.91\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $50.0\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $981\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $491\,\mathrm{N/m}$

## Explanation

The body is at rest, so the spring force balances the weight, $k\,x = m g$. With $x = 0.040\,\mathrm{m}$,
$$
k = \frac{m g}{x} = \frac{2.0\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}}{0.040\,\mathrm{m}} = 491\,\mathrm{N/m} .
$$
Dividing the mass instead of the weight gives $50.0\,\mathrm{N/m}$.
