A spring of constant $200\,\mathrm{N/m}$ is stretched from its natural length by $0.10\,\mathrm{m}$. How much work does the spring force do on the body attached to it?

- $+1.0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-2.0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-1.0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

By the theorem on the **work done by a spring force**, with $x_1 = 0$ and $x_2 = 0.10\,\mathrm{m}$,
$$
W = \frac{1}{2}k\,x_1^2 - \frac{1}{2}k\,x_2^2 = -\frac{1}{2} \cdot 200\,\mathrm{N/m} \cdot (0.10\,\mathrm{m})^2 = -1.0\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
The work is negative because the spring force opposes the stretch.
