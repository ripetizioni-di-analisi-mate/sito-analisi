A spring of constant $400\,\mathrm{N/m}$ is stretched by $5.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ from its natural length. What is the magnitude of the force the spring exerts?

- $20\,\mathrm{N}$
- $2.0 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.50\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

By **Hooke's law** the magnitude of the spring force is $k\,|x|$. With $x = 0.050\,\mathrm{m}$,
$$
k\,|x| = 400\,\mathrm{N/m} \cdot 0.050\,\mathrm{m} = 20\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
Using $x = 5.0$ without converting the centimeters gives $2.0 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{N}$.
