A car travels at the constant speed of $25\,\mathrm{m/s}$ against a total resistive force of $800\,\mathrm{N}$. What power does the propulsive force deliver?

- $10\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $40\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $0.032\,\mathrm{kW}$

## Explanation

The speed is constant, so the propulsive force balances the resistive force and has magnitude $800\,\mathrm{N}$, in the direction of the velocity. By the theorem on the **power of a force**
$$
P = F\,v = 800\,\mathrm{N} \cdot 25\,\mathrm{m/s} = 2.0 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{W} = 20\,\mathrm{kW} .
$$
