The engine of a car delivers the constant power of $40\,\mathrm{kW}$. What is the propulsive force when the car moves at $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$?

- $8.0 \times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $2.0 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1.0 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The propulsive force is parallel to the velocity, so $P = F\,v$ and
$$
F = \frac{P}{v} = \frac{4.0 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{W}}{20\,\mathrm{m/s}} = 2.0 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
Leaving the power in kilowatts gives $2.0\,\mathrm{N}$.
