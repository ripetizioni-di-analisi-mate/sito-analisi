A constant force of magnitude $20\,\mathrm{N}$ makes an angle of $120^\circ$ with the displacement of a body, which moves $3.0\,\mathrm{m}$. How much work does the force do?

- $30\,\mathrm{J}$
- $52\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-30\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-60\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

By the definition of the **work of a constant force**
$$
W = F\,d\cos 120^\circ = 20\,\mathrm{N} \cdot 3.0\,\mathrm{m} \cdot (-0.50) = -30\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
The angle is larger than $90^\circ$, so the force has a component opposite to the motion and the work is negative.
