Body A has mass $m$ and speed $2v$, and body B has mass $2m$ and speed $v$. How do their kinetic energies compare?

- $K_A = K_B$
- $K_A = 2\,K_B$
- $K_B = 2\,K_A$
- $K_A = 4\,K_B$

## Explanation

The kinetic energies are
$$
K_A = \frac{1}{2}m\,(2v)^2 = 2\,m v^2 , \qquad K_B = \frac{1}{2}(2m)\,v^2 = m v^2 ,
$$
therefore $K_A = 2\,K_B$. The speed enters squared, while the mass enters linearly.
