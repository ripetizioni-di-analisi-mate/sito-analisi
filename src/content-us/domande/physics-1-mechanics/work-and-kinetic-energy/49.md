A block of mass $3.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ slides $5.0\,\mathrm{m}$ down an inclined plane that makes the angle $30^\circ$ with the horizontal. How much work does the weight do on the block?

- $73.6\,\mathrm{J}$
- $127\,\mathrm{J}$
- $147\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-73.6\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The block descends by $h = L\sin 30^\circ = 2.5\,\mathrm{m}$, so by the theorem on the **work done by the weight**
$$
W = m g h = 3.0\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot 2.5\,\mathrm{m} = 73.6\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
Using $\cos 30^\circ$ gives $127\,\mathrm{J}$, and using the whole length $L$ as a height gives $147\,\mathrm{J}$.
