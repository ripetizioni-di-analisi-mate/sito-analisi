Which of the following statements about the work done by a force is correct?

- Work is a vector parallel to the force.
- Work is a scalar, and it can be positive, negative or zero.
- Work is always positive, because $F$ and $d$ are magnitudes.
- Work is a vector parallel to the displacement.

## Explanation

Work is defined as the scalar product $W = \vec{F} \cdot \vec{d}$, hence it is a scalar. Its sign is the sign of $\cos\varphi$, so it is positive for $\varphi < 90^\circ$, zero for $\varphi = 90^\circ$ and negative for $\varphi > 90^\circ$.
