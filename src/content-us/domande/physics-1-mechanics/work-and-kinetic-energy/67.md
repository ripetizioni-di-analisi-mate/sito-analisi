A body of mass $4.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ has kinetic energy $50\,\mathrm{J}$. What is its speed?

- $25.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.54\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $12.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

From $K = \dfrac{1}{2}m v^2$,
$$
v = \sqrt{\frac{2K}{m}} = \sqrt{\frac{2 \cdot 50\,\mathrm{J}}{4.0\,\mathrm{kg}}} = 5.00\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
Forgetting the square root gives $25.0$, and losing the factor $2$ gives $3.54\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
