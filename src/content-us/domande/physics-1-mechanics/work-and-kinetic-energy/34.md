The stretch of a spring from its natural length is doubled. By what factor does the work needed to stretch it slowly from its natural length increase?

- $2$
- $4$
- $\sqrt{2}$
- $8$

## Explanation

The work needed to stretch a spring slowly by $x$ from its natural length is $\dfrac{1}{2}k\,x^2$, which is proportional to the square of the stretch. Doubling $x$ therefore multiplies the work by $2^2 = 4$.
