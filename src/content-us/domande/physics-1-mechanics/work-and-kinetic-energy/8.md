A traveler holds a bag of weight $150\,\mathrm{N}$ at rest, at a fixed height, for $60\,\mathrm{s}$. How much work does the traveler do on the bag?

- $9.0 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{J}$
- $150\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Work is the product of a force and a displacement, $W = F\,d\cos\varphi$. The bag does not move, so $d = 0$ and the work is zero. The time during which the bag is held does not enter the definition of work.
