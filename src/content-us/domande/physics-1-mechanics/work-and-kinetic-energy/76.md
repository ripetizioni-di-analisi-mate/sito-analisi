A body of mass $5.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ starts from rest, and the total work done on it is $90\,\mathrm{J}$. What is its final speed?

- $36\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The body starts from rest, so by the **work-energy theorem** $W_{\text{tot}} = \dfrac{1}{2}m v^2$ and
$$
v = \sqrt{\frac{2\,W_{\text{tot}}}{m}} = \sqrt{\frac{2 \cdot 90\,\mathrm{J}}{5.0\,\mathrm{kg}}} = 6.0\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
Forgetting the square root gives $36$, and losing the factor $2$ gives $4.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
