A puck slides $3.0\,\mathrm{m}$ along a horizontal surface, and the kinetic friction force on it has the constant magnitude $12\,\mathrm{N}$. How much work does friction do on the puck?

- $36\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-4.0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-36\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Kinetic friction points opposite to the velocity, hence the angle between the friction force and the displacement is $180^\circ$. Therefore
$$
W_f = -f\,d = -12\,\mathrm{N} \cdot 3.0\,\mathrm{m} = -36\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
The work is negative because the force opposes the motion.
