A body of mass $3.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ slows down from $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the total work done on it?

- $-24\,\mathrm{J}$
- $+48\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-96\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-48\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

By the **work-energy theorem**
$$
W_{\text{tot}} = \frac{1}{2}m\,(v_2^2 - v_1^2) = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 3.0\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot (4.0 - 36)\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2} = -48\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
The work is negative because the kinetic energy decreases. Squaring the change of speed gives $-24\,\mathrm{J}$.
