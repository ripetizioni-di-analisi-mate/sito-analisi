A block launched on a rough horizontal surface stops after the distance $d$. The initial speed is doubled and the coefficient of kinetic friction is halved. After what distance does the block stop?

- $4\,d$
- $8\,d$
- $2\,d$
- $16\,d$

## Explanation

By the **work-energy theorem** the stopping distance is $d = \dfrac{v^2}{2\,\mu_k\,g}$. Doubling $v$ multiplies the numerator by $4$, and halving $\mu_k$ multiplies the distance by $2$, therefore the new distance is $8\,d$.
