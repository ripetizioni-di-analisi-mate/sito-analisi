The free end of a spring of constant $200\,\mathrm{N/m}$ moves from $x_1 = -0.10\,\mathrm{m}$ (compressed) to $x_2 = +0.10\,\mathrm{m}$ (stretched), both measured from the natural length. How much work does the spring force do?

- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-4.0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-1.0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-2.0\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The work of a spring force is $W = \dfrac{1}{2}k\,x_1^2 - \dfrac{1}{2}k\,x_2^2$, and it depends on the squares of the deformations. Here $x_1^2 = x_2^2$, therefore $W = 0$. The spring does $+1.0\,\mathrm{J}$ while returning to its natural length and $-1.0\,\mathrm{J}$ while being stretched.
