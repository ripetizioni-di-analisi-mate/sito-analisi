Two carts of mass $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ each move toward each other, each at $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the total kinetic energy of the two carts?

- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $8.0\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Each cart has $K = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 1.0\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot (2.0\,\mathrm{m/s})^2 = 2.0\,\mathrm{J}$. The kinetic energy is a scalar that is never negative, so the kinetic energies of the parts are added and the total is $4.0\,\mathrm{J}$. The opposite directions of the velocities have no effect, since only the speeds appear.
