The component of a force along the $x$ axis is $F_x = 4.0\,\mathrm{N}$ for $0 < x < 2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ and $F_x = -4.0\,\mathrm{N}$ for $2.0\,\mathrm{m} < x < 3.0\,\mathrm{m}$. How much work does the force do on a body that moves from $x = 0$ to $x = 3.0\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $12\,\mathrm{J}$
- $8.0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-4.0\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The work is the area under the graph of $F_x$ taken with its sign. The first stretch gives $4.0\,\mathrm{N} \cdot 2.0\,\mathrm{m} = 8.0\,\mathrm{J}$, and the second gives $-4.0\,\mathrm{N} \cdot 1.0\,\mathrm{m} = -4.0\,\mathrm{J}$, because there the graph lies below the axis. Therefore $W = 4.0\,\mathrm{J}$.
