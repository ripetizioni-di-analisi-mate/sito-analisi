A constant force parallel to the displacement does $900\,\mathrm{J}$ of work while a body moves $150\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the magnitude of the force?

- $6.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1200\,\mathrm{N}$
- $300\,\mathrm{N}$
- $600\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The force is parallel to the displacement, hence $W = F\,d$. With $d = 1.50\,\mathrm{m}$,
$$
F = \frac{W}{d} = \frac{900\,\mathrm{J}}{1.50\,\mathrm{m}} = 600\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
Dividing by $150$ without converting the centimeters to meters gives $6.0\,\mathrm{N}$.
