A puck is launched at $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ on a horizontal surface, and the coefficient of kinetic friction is $0.10$. What distance does the puck travel before stopping?

- $16.3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4.08\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.04\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8.15\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Friction has magnitude $\mu_k m g$ and does the work $-\mu_k m g\,d$, while the final kinetic energy is zero. By the **work-energy theorem** $-\mu_k m g\,d = -\dfrac{1}{2}m v^2$, so
$$
d = \frac{v^2}{2\,\mu_k\,g} = \frac{(4.0\,\mathrm{m/s})^2}{2 \cdot 0.10 \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}} = 8.15\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
Losing the factor $2$ in the denominator gives $16.3\,\mathrm{m}$.
