A constant force has a component of $12\,\mathrm{N}$ along the displacement of a body and a component of $16\,\mathrm{N}$ perpendicular to it. The body moves $5.0\,\mathrm{m}$. How much work does the force do?

- $100\,\mathrm{J}$
- $80\,\mathrm{J}$
- $60\,\mathrm{J}$
- $140\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Only the component along the displacement contributes to the work, while the perpendicular component contributes nothing. Therefore
$$
W = F_{\parallel}\,d = 12\,\mathrm{N} \cdot 5.0\,\mathrm{m} = 60\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
The value $100\,\mathrm{J}$ is the magnitude of the force, $20\,\mathrm{N}$, times the displacement.
