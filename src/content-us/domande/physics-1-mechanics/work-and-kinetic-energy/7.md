A box slides $5.0\,\mathrm{m}$ along a horizontal floor, and the floor pushes on it with a normal force of magnitude $200\,\mathrm{N}$. How much work does the normal force do on the box?

- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1.0 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-1.0 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $40\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The normal force is vertical and the displacement is horizontal, so the angle between them is $90^\circ$. Since $\cos 90^\circ = 0$, the work of the normal force is zero, whatever its magnitude.
