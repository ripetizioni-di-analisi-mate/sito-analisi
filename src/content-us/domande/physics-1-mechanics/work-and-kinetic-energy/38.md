What is the spring constant unit $\mathrm{N/m}$ in SI base units?

- $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s^2}$
- $\mathrm{kg/s}$
- $\mathrm{kg/s^2}$
- $\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s^2}$

## Explanation

The newton is $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s^2}$, therefore
$$
1\,\mathrm{N/m} = 1\,\frac{\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s^2}}{\mathrm{m}} = 1\,\mathrm{kg/s^2} .
$$
The combination $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s^2}$ is the newton itself, and $\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s^2}$ is the joule.
