A box slides to the right on a rough floor and slows down, while a person pushes it to the right with a horizontal force. What is the sign of the total work done on the box?

- Positive
- Zero
- Negative
- It depends on the mass of the box.

## Explanation

The speed of the box decreases, so its kinetic energy decreases and $\Delta K < 0$. By the **work-energy theorem** $W_{\text{tot}} = \Delta K$, therefore the total work is negative. The push does positive work, and friction does a negative work of larger magnitude.
