A constant force $\vec{F} = (2.0\,\hat{\imath} - 5.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{N}$ acts on a body that moves from $A = (1.0,\ 4.0)\,\mathrm{m}$ to $B = (4.0,\ 2.0)\,\mathrm{m}$. How much work does the force do?

- $-4.0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $6.0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $16\,\mathrm{J}$
- $10\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The displacement is $\vec{d} = B - A = (3.0\,\hat{\imath} - 2.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m}$. The force is constant, hence
$$
W = F_x\,d_x + F_y\,d_y = 2.0 \cdot 3.0\,\mathrm{J} + (-5.0) \cdot (-2.0)\,\mathrm{J} = 16\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
The value $-4.0\,\mathrm{J}$ comes from losing one of the two minus signs in the $y$ term.
