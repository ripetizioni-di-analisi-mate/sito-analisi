A car of mass $1000\,\mathrm{kg}$ travels at $72\,\mathrm{km/h}$. What is its kinetic energy?

- $2.0 \times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $2.6 \times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $4.0 \times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1.0 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The speed in SI units is $v = \dfrac{72}{3.6}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 20\,\mathrm{m/s}$, hence
$$
K = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 1000\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot (20\,\mathrm{m/s})^2 = 2.0 \times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
Using $72$ directly, without converting the kilometers per hour, gives $2.6 \times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{J}$.
