A constant force acts on a body that undergoes a nonzero displacement. For which angle between the force and the displacement is the work done by the force equal to zero?

- $0^\circ$
- $180^\circ$
- $90^\circ$
- $45^\circ$

## Explanation

The work of a constant force is $W = F\,d\cos\varphi$, and with $F$ and $d$ different from zero it vanishes only when $\cos\varphi = 0$, that is, for $\varphi = 90^\circ$. At $0^\circ$ the work is maximum and positive, at $180^\circ$ it is $-F\,d$.
