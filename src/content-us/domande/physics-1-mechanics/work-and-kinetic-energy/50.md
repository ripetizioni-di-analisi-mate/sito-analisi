A ball of mass $0.50\,\mathrm{kg}$ is thrown from a height of $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ and lands on level ground $12\,\mathrm{m}$ away, after a parabolic flight. Air resistance is negligible. How much work does the weight do during the flight?

- $58.9\,\mathrm{J}$
- $7.36\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-7.36\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0.75\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The work of the weight depends only on the difference in height, and the horizontal displacement does not enter it. The ball descends by $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$, hence
$$
W = m g h = 0.50\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot 1.5\,\mathrm{m} = 7.36\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
The value $58.9\,\mathrm{J}$ comes from using the horizontal distance as if it were a height.
