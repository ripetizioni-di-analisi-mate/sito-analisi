Which of the following statements about the kinetic energy of a particle is correct?

- It is negative when the particle moves in the negative direction of the $x$ axis.
- It is a vector with the direction of the velocity.
- It is never negative, and it is zero only when the particle is at rest.
- It changes when the direction of the velocity changes at constant speed.

## Explanation

The kinetic energy $K = \dfrac{1}{2}m v^2$ contains the square of the speed, which is never negative and vanishes only for $v = 0$. Since only the speed appears, $K$ is a scalar and does not depend on the direction of the velocity.
