A body of mass $4.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ moves at $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is its kinetic energy?

- $18\,\mathrm{J}$
- $36\,\mathrm{J}$
- $6.0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $12\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

By the definition of **kinetic energy**
$$
K = \frac{1}{2}m v^2 = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 4.0\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot (3.0\,\mathrm{m/s})^2 = 18\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
Losing the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$ gives $36\,\mathrm{J}$, and forgetting to square the speed gives $6.0\,\mathrm{J}$.
