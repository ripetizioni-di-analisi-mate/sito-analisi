A hand lifts a box of mass $5.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ through $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ at constant velocity. What is the total work done on the box?

- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $98\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-98\,\mathrm{J}$
- $196\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The velocity is constant, so the resultant force on the box is zero. The total work is the work of the resultant, $W_{\text{tot}} = \vec{R} \cdot \vec{d}$, therefore it is zero. The hand does $+98\,\mathrm{J}$ and the weight does $-98\,\mathrm{J}$, and the two works cancel.
