A box hanging from a rope is lowered at constant speed. What is the sign of the work done on the box by the tension of the rope?

- Positive
- Zero
- Negative
- Positive while the box moves, zero when it stops

## Explanation

The tension points upwards, while the displacement of the box points downwards, so the angle between them is $180^\circ$ and $\cos\varphi = -1$. The work of the tension is therefore $W = -T\,d$, which is negative. The constant speed fixes the magnitude of the tension, $T = m g$, and it has no effect on the sign.
