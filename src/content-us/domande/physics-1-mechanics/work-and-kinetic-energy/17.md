A crate is dragged $6.0\,\mathrm{m}$ along a horizontal floor by a horizontal force of $40\,\mathrm{N}$, while kinetic friction exerts a force of $15\,\mathrm{N}$ on it. What is the total work done on the crate?

- $330\,\mathrm{J}$
- $240\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-90\,\mathrm{J}$
- $150\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The applied force does $W_F = 40\,\mathrm{N} \cdot 6.0\,\mathrm{m} = 240\,\mathrm{J}$ and friction does $W_f = -15\,\mathrm{N} \cdot 6.0\,\mathrm{m} = -90\,\mathrm{J}$. The weight and the normal force are perpendicular to the displacement and do no work, therefore
$$
W_{\text{tot}} = 240\,\mathrm{J} - 90\,\mathrm{J} = 150\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
