A force along the $x$ axis grows linearly from $0$ at $x = 0$ to $12\,\mathrm{N}$ at $x = 3.0\,\mathrm{m}$. How much work does it do on a body that moves from $x = 0$ to $x = 3.0\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $36\,\mathrm{J}$
- $18\,\mathrm{J}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-18\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The work of a variable force is the area under the graph of $F_x(x)$. Here the graph is a triangle of base $3.0\,\mathrm{m}$ and height $12\,\mathrm{N}$, so
$$
W = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 3.0\,\mathrm{m} \cdot 12\,\mathrm{N} = 18\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
Multiplying the final force by the whole displacement gives $36\,\mathrm{J}$, the area of the rectangle.
