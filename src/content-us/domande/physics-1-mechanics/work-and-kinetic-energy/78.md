Two blocks of different mass are launched with the same speed on the same horizontal surface, with the same coefficient of kinetic friction. Which statement about their stopping distances is correct?

- The heavier block travels farther.
- The lighter block travels farther.
- The stopping distance is proportional to the mass.
- The two blocks stop after the same distance.

## Explanation

By the **work-energy theorem** $-\mu_k m g\,d = -\dfrac{1}{2}m v^2$, and the mass appears on both sides. It cancels, hence $d = \dfrac{v^2}{2\,\mu_k\,g}$, which is the same for the two blocks.
