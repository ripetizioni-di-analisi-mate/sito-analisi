A body of mass $3.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ moves at $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and then the total work done on it is $24\,\mathrm{J}$. What is its final speed?

- $4.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4.47\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $20.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The initial kinetic energy is $K_1 = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 3.0\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot (2.0\,\mathrm{m/s})^2 = 6.0\,\mathrm{J}$, so by the **work-energy theorem** $K_2 = 6.0\,\mathrm{J} + 24\,\mathrm{J} = 30\,\mathrm{J}$. Therefore
$$
v_2 = \sqrt{\frac{2K_2}{m}} = \sqrt{\frac{2 \cdot 30\,\mathrm{J}}{3.0\,\mathrm{kg}}} = 4.47\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
Ignoring the initial kinetic energy gives $4.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
