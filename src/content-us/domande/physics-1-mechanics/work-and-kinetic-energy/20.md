A block slides down a rough inclined plane. Which of the forces acting on the block does zero work?

- The weight
- The kinetic friction force
- The normal force
- None of them

## Explanation

The block moves along the surface of the plane, and the normal force is perpendicular to that surface, so the angle between the normal force and the displacement is $90^\circ$ and its work is zero. The weight has a component along the plane and does positive work, while friction points up the plane and does negative work.
