A constant force of $30\,\mathrm{N}$ acts on a cart in the direction of its motion while the cart moves $250\,\mathrm{cm}$ along a straight track. How much work does the force do?

- $7.5 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $38\,\mathrm{J}$
- $12\,\mathrm{J}$
- $75\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The displacement in meters is $d = 2.50\,\mathrm{m}$, and the force is parallel to it, so $\cos\varphi = 1$. By the definition of the **work of a constant force**
$$
W = F\,d = 30\,\mathrm{N} \cdot 2.50\,\mathrm{m} = 75\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
Multiplying by $250$ without converting the centimeters gives a value one hundred times too large.
