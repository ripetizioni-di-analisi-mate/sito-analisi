A rope pulls a crate along a horizontal floor with a force of fixed magnitude directed at $40^\circ$ above the horizontal. The angle is reduced to $20^\circ$, while the magnitude of the force and the displacement stay the same. How does the work done by the rope change?

- It decreases.
- It stays the same.
- It increases.
- It changes sign.

## Explanation

The work of the rope is $W = F\,d\cos\varphi$, and the cosine is a decreasing function between $0^\circ$ and $90^\circ$. Since $\cos 20^\circ = 0.940$ is larger than $\cos 40^\circ = 0.766$, the work increases. Both angles are smaller than $90^\circ$, so the sign stays positive.
