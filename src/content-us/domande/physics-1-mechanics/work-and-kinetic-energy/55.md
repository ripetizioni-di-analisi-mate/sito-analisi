A force of magnitude $50\,\mathrm{N}$ acts on a body moving at $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and the angle between the force and the velocity is $60^\circ$. What is the instantaneous power delivered by the force?

- $173\,\mathrm{W}$
- $200\,\mathrm{W}$
- $12.5\,\mathrm{W}$
- $100\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

By the theorem on the **power of a force**
$$
P = F\,v\cos\varphi = 50\,\mathrm{N} \cdot 4.0\,\mathrm{m/s} \cdot 0.50 = 100\,\mathrm{W} .
$$
Using $\sin 60^\circ$ gives $173\,\mathrm{W}$, and omitting the angle gives $200\,\mathrm{W}$.
