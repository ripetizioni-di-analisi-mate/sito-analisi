A spring compressed by $x$ launches a block of mass $m$ on a horizontal frictionless surface at the speed $v$. A block of mass $2m$ is launched with the same spring and the same compression. What is its speed?

- $\dfrac{v}{2}$
- $v$
- $\dfrac{v}{\sqrt{2}}$
- $\sqrt{2}\,v$

## Explanation

The spring does the same work $\dfrac{1}{2}k\,x^2$ on both blocks, so by the **work-energy theorem** both receive the same kinetic energy, $\dfrac{1}{2}m v^2 = \dfrac{1}{2}(2m)\,v'^2$. Therefore $v'^2 = \dfrac{v^2}{2}$ and $v' = \dfrac{v}{\sqrt{2}}$.
