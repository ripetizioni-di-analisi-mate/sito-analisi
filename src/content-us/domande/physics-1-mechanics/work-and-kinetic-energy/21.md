Two constant forces $\vec{F}_1 = 3.0\,\hat{\imath}\,\mathrm{N}$ and $\vec{F}_2 = (-1.0\,\hat{\imath} + 2.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{N}$ act on a body during the displacement $\vec{d} = 4.0\,\hat{\imath}\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the total work done on the body?

- $8.0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $12\,\mathrm{J}$
- $16\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-4.0\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The resultant is $\vec{R} = (2.0\,\hat{\imath} + 2.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{N}$, and the total work is its scalar product with the displacement:
$$
W_{\text{tot}} = R_x\,d_x + R_y\,d_y = 2.0 \cdot 4.0\,\mathrm{J} + 2.0 \cdot 0\,\mathrm{J} = 8.0\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
The same value follows from the single works, $12\,\mathrm{J}$ and $-4.0\,\mathrm{J}$.
