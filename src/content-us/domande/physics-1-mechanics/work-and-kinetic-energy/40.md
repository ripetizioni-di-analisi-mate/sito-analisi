The component of a force along the $x$ axis is $10\,\mathrm{N}$ from $x = 0$ to $x = 2.0\,\mathrm{m}$, and it then decreases linearly to zero at $x = 4.0\,\mathrm{m}$. How much work does the force do between $x = 0$ and $x = 4.0\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $30\,\mathrm{J}$
- $40\,\mathrm{J}$
- $20\,\mathrm{J}$
- $10\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The work is the area under the graph of $F_x$. The rectangle from $0$ to $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ gives $10\,\mathrm{N} \cdot 2.0\,\mathrm{m} = 20\,\mathrm{J}$, and the triangle from $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ to $4.0\,\mathrm{m}$ gives $\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 2.0\,\mathrm{m} \cdot 10\,\mathrm{N} = 10\,\mathrm{J}$. Therefore $W = 30\,\mathrm{J}$.
