Spring A has constant $k$ and spring B has constant $2k$. Each is stretched slowly from its natural length until it exerts the same force $F$. How do the works $W_A$ and $W_B$ done on the two springs compare?

- $W_A = W_B$
- $W_B = 2\,W_A$
- $W_A = 4\,W_B$
- $W_A = 2\,W_B$

## Explanation

A spring that exerts the force $F$ is stretched by $x = \dfrac{F}{k}$, so the work done to stretch it is
$$
W = \frac{1}{2}k\,x^2 = \frac{F^2}{2k} .
$$
At equal force the work is inversely proportional to the constant, therefore the softer spring A requires twice the work, $W_A = 2\,W_B$.
