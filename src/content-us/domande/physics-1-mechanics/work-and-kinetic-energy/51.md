A machine does $1200\,\mathrm{J}$ of work in $4.0\,\mathrm{s}$. What is its average power?

- $4.8 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{W}$
- $300\,\mathrm{W}$
- $150\,\mathrm{W}$
- $600\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

By the definition of **average power**
$$
P_{\text{av}} = \frac{\Delta W}{\Delta t} = \frac{1200\,\mathrm{J}}{4.0\,\mathrm{s}} = 300\,\mathrm{W} .
$$
Multiplying the work by the time gives $4.8 \times 10^{3}$, which is not a power.
