A motor delivers $600\,\mathrm{W}$ to raise a box of mass $30\,\mathrm{kg}$ vertically at constant speed. What is that speed?

- $20.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.491\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.02\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.04\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

At constant speed the force of the motor equals the weight, $F = m g = 294\,\mathrm{N}$, and it is parallel to the velocity. From $P = F\,v$,
$$
v = \frac{P}{m g} = \frac{600\,\mathrm{W}}{30\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}} = 2.04\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
Dividing by the mass instead of the weight gives $20.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
