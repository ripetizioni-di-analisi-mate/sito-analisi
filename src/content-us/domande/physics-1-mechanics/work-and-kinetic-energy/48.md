In the expression $W = \displaystyle\int_{A}^{B} F\cos\varphi\,ds$ for the work along a curved path, what is the angle $\varphi$?

- The angle between the force and the tangent to the path at that point
- The angle between the force and the position vector of that point
- The angle between the force and the $x$ axis
- The angle between the tangent to the path and the $x$ axis

## Explanation

Each element of the path is a short displacement $d\vec{r}$ directed along the tangent, and its work is $\vec{F} \cdot d\vec{r} = F\cos\varphi\,ds$. Therefore $\varphi$ is the angle between the force and the tangent to the path at the point considered.
