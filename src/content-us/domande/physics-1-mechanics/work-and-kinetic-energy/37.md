On the graph of the spring force $F_x = -k\,x$ against $x$, what represents the work done by the spring force while the free end moves from $0$ to $x > 0$?

- The area of the triangle between the graph and the $x$ axis, taken with the negative sign
- The slope of the graph
- The value of $F_x$ at $x$
- The area of the rectangle of base $x$ and height $k\,x$

## Explanation

The work of a variable force along the $x$ axis is the integral of $F_x$, that is, the area between the graph and the axis with its sign. For $x > 0$ the graph of $-k\,x$ lies below the axis, so the area is the triangle of base $x$ and height $k\,x$, counted negatively, which gives $-\dfrac{1}{2}k\,x^2$. The slope of the graph is $-k$.
