A block is released from rest and slides $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ down a frictionless inclined plane that makes the angle $30^\circ$ with the horizontal. What is its speed at the end of that stretch?

- $6.26\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5.83\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.13\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4.43\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The normal force does no work, and the weight does $m g L\sin 30^\circ$. By the **work-energy theorem** $\dfrac{1}{2}m v^2 = m g L\sin 30^\circ$, so
$$
v = \sqrt{2\,g L\sin 30^\circ} = \sqrt{2 \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot 2.0\,\mathrm{m} \cdot 0.50} = 4.43\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
Using $\cos 30^\circ$ gives $5.83\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
