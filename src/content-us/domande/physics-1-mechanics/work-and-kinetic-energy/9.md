A stone slides on a frictionless horizontal table along a circle of radius $r$ at constant speed, held by a string whose other end is fixed at the center of the circle. The tension of the string has the constant magnitude $T$. How much work does the tension do during half a revolution?

- $T\,\pi r$
- $0$
- $-T\,\pi r$
- $2\,T r$

## Explanation

The tension points towards the center of the circle, while the velocity is tangent to it, therefore the force is perpendicular to every element of the displacement. The work of each element is zero, hence the work over half a revolution is zero as well. This agrees with the speed being constant.
