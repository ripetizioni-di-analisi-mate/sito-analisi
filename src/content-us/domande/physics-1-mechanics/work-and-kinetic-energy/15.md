A constant force directed at $60^\circ$ to the displacement of a body does the work $W$. How much work does a force of the same magnitude do over the same displacement when it is directed along the displacement?

- $\dfrac{W}{2}$
- $2\,W$
- $1.15\,W$
- $W$

## Explanation

The force directed at $60^\circ$ does $W = F\,d\cos 60^\circ = \dfrac{1}{2}F\,d$, so $F\,d = 2\,W$. The force directed along the displacement does $F\,d$, which is $2\,W$. The factor $1.15$ is $\dfrac{1}{\sin 60^\circ}$, obtained by using the sine instead of the cosine.
