A book of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ is lifted from the floor to a shelf $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ higher. How much work does the weight do on the book?

- $+29.4\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-29.4\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-3.0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-14.7\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The book climbs by $h = 1.5\,\mathrm{m}$, so by the theorem on the **work done by the weight**
$$
W = -m g h = -2.0\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot 1.5\,\mathrm{m} = -29.4\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
The work is negative because the weight points downwards while the book rises.
