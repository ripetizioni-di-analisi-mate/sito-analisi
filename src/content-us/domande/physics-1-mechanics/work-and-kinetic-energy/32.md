The free end of a spring of constant $200\,\mathrm{N/m}$ moves from a stretch of $0.10\,\mathrm{m}$ to a stretch of $0.20\,\mathrm{m}$, both measured from the natural length. How much work does the spring force do?

- $-3.0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-1.0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-4.0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $+3.0\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The work depends on the initial and the final deformation:
$$
W = \frac{1}{2}k\,(x_1^2 - x_2^2) = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 200\,\mathrm{N/m} \cdot (0.010 - 0.040)\,\mathrm{m^2} = -3.0\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
The value $-1.0\,\mathrm{J}$ comes from squaring the difference $x_2 - x_1$ instead of taking the difference of the squares.
