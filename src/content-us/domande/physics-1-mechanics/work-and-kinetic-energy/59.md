A motor of power $1.5\,\mathrm{kW}$ works at constant power until it has done $9.0 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{J}$ of work. How long does it work?

- $60\,\mathrm{s}$
- $6.0 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.017\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.4 \times 10^{8}\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

At constant power the work is $W = P\,\Delta t$, hence
$$
\Delta t = \frac{W}{P} = \frac{9.0 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{J}}{1.5 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{W}} = 60\,\mathrm{s} .
$$
Leaving the power in kilowatts gives $6.0 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{s}$.
