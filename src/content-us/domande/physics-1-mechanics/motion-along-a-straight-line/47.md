Which graph describes a uniform motion with nonzero velocity?

- A velocity-time graph that is a straight line through the origin with positive slope.
- An acceleration-time graph that is a horizontal line at a positive value.
- A velocity-time graph that is a horizontal line not lying on the time axis.
- A position-time graph that is an arc of a parabola.

## Explanation

In a uniform motion the velocity is constant, hence its graph is a horizontal line, and it lies off the time axis when the velocity is not zero. The other graphs describe motions with nonzero acceleration.
