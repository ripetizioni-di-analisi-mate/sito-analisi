A dog runs $30\,\mathrm{m}$ in a straight line away from its owner and then runs back along the same line to the owner, who stands still. The whole run lasts $15\,\mathrm{s}$. What are the average velocity and the average speed of the dog?

- $v_{\mathrm{av}} = 4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$, average speed $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $v_{\mathrm{av}} = 0$, average speed $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $v_{\mathrm{av}} = 0$, average speed $0$
- $v_{\mathrm{av}} = 2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$, average speed $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The dog returns to its starting point, hence the displacement is zero and so is the average velocity. The distance traveled is $60\,\mathrm{m}$, therefore the average speed is $\dfrac{60\,\mathrm{m}}{15\,\mathrm{s}} = 4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
