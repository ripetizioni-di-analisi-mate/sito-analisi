On the velocity-time graph of a body, how is the average acceleration between the instants $t_1$ and $t_2$ obtained?

- As the area under the velocity-time graph between $t_1$ and $t_2$.
- As the slope of the secant line through the points of the position-time graph at $t_1$ and $t_2$.
- As the slope of the secant line through the points of the velocity-time graph at $t_1$ and $t_2$.
- As the area under the position-time graph between $t_1$ and $t_2$.

## Explanation

The average acceleration is $\dfrac{v(t_2) - v(t_1)}{t_2 - t_1}$, which has the same form as the average velocity with $v$ in place of $x$. It is therefore the slope of the secant line of the velocity-time graph, while the area under that graph is the displacement.
