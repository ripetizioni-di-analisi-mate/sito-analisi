A body moves along the $x$ axis with constant velocity $-3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and is at $x = 15\,\mathrm{m}$ at the instant $t = 2.0\,\mathrm{s}$. What is its position at $t = 6.0\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $27\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-3.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $33\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

By the law of uniform motion $x(t) = x_0 + v\,(t - t_0)$ with $t_0 = 2.0\,\mathrm{s}$,
$$
x(6.0) = 15\,\mathrm{m} + (-3.0\,\mathrm{m/s})(4.0\,\mathrm{s}) = 3.0\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
Using $t$ instead of $t - t_0$ would give $-3.0\,\mathrm{m}$.
