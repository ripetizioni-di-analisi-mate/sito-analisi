A car travels along a straight road for $1.0\,\mathrm{h}$ at $30\,\mathrm{km/h}$ and then for another $1.0\,\mathrm{h}$ at $90\,\mathrm{km/h}$, in the same sense. What is its average speed?

- $60\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $45\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $120\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $30\,\mathrm{km/h}$

## Explanation

The distance traveled is $30\,\mathrm{km} + 90\,\mathrm{km} = 120\,\mathrm{km}$ and the time is $2.0\,\mathrm{h}$, hence the average speed is $\dfrac{120\,\mathrm{km}}{2.0\,\mathrm{h}} = 60\,\mathrm{km/h}$. When the two speeds are held for equal times, the average speed is their arithmetic mean.
