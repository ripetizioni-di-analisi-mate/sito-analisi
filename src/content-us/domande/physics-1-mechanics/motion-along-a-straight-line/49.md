A car starts from rest with constant acceleration $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What distance does it cover in the first $8.0\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $160\,\mathrm{m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $80\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

With $v_0 = 0$ the law of motion reduces to $x = \dfrac{1}{2}\,a\,t^2$, hence
$$
x = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 2.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot (8.0\,\mathrm{s})^2 = 80\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
Dropping the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$ gives $160\,\mathrm{m}$.
