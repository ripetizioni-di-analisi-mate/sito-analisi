A body moves along the $x$ axis with initial velocity $+10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and constant acceleration $-2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. At which instant is its speed equal to $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for the second time?

- $3.0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $7.0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $5.0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The velocity is $v(t) = 10 - 2.0\,t$, and the speed equals $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ when $v = +4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ or $v = -4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The first condition gives $t = 3.0\,\mathrm{s}$ and the second gives $t = 7.0\,\mathrm{s}$, after the reversal at $t = 5.0\,\mathrm{s}$, therefore the second time is $t = 7.0\,\mathrm{s}$.
