A body moves along the $x$ axis with the law of motion $x(t) = 1.0 + 6.0\,t - 2.0\,t^2$, with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. What is its velocity at $t = 2.0\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $14\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The velocity is the derivative of the law of motion, $v(t) = 6.0 - 4.0\,t$, hence $v(2.0) = 6.0 - 8.0 = -2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The value $5.0$ is the position $x(2.0)$ in meters, which is a different quantity.
