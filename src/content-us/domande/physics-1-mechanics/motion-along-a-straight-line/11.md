A runner moves along the $x$ axis from $x = 20\,\mathrm{m}$ to $x = -40\,\mathrm{m}$ in $12\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the average velocity of the runner?

- $-5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-1.7\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-3.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The displacement is $\Delta x = -40\,\mathrm{m} - 20\,\mathrm{m} = -60\,\mathrm{m}$, hence
$$
v_{\mathrm{av}} = \dfrac{\Delta x}{\Delta t} = \dfrac{-60\,\mathrm{m}}{12\,\mathrm{s}} = -5.0\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The sign is negative because the runner ends up on the negative side of the starting point.
