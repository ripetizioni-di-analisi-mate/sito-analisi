Over a certain time interval the magnitude of the displacement of a body moving along a line equals the distance traveled. Which conclusion follows necessarily?

- The velocity of the body was constant during the interval.
- The body did not reverse its sense of motion during the interval.
- The body started from the origin of the axis.
- The acceleration of the body was positive during the interval.

## Explanation

The distance traveled and the magnitude of the displacement coincide exactly when the body moves always in the same sense. A body that speeds up or slows down without reversing satisfies the condition as well, therefore constant velocity and positive acceleration are not necessary.
