A body moves along the $x$ axis from $x = 2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ to $x = 9.0\,\mathrm{m}$, then reverses and stops at $x = 5.0\,\mathrm{m}$. What distance does it travel?

- $11\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $7.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The distance traveled is the sum of the lengths of the single stretches. The first stretch is $9.0\,\mathrm{m} - 2.0\,\mathrm{m} = 7.0\,\mathrm{m}$ and the second is $9.0\,\mathrm{m} - 5.0\,\mathrm{m} = 4.0\,\mathrm{m}$, hence the distance traveled is $11\,\mathrm{m}$, while the displacement is only $3.0\,\mathrm{m}$.
