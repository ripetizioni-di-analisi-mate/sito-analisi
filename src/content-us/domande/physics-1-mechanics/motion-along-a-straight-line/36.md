A body moves along the $x$ axis with the law of motion $x(t) = 3.0\,t^2 - 0.50\,t^3$, with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. What is its acceleration at $t = 1.0\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $4.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The velocity is $v(t) = 6.0\,t - 1.5\,t^2$ and the acceleration is its derivative, $a(t) = 6.0 - 3.0\,t$. Therefore $a(1.0) = 3.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, while $4.5$ is the value of the velocity at the same instant.
