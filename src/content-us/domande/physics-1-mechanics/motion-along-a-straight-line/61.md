A car at rest starts with constant acceleration $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ at the instant when a truck traveling at the constant speed of $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$ passes it in the same sense. What is the distance between them when the car reaches the speed of the truck?

- $75\,\mathrm{m}$
- $38\,\mathrm{m}$
- $19\,\mathrm{m}$
- $113\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The car reaches $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at $t = \dfrac{15\,\mathrm{m/s}}{3.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}} = 5.0\,\mathrm{s}$. At that instant the truck has covered $75\,\mathrm{m}$ and the car $\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 3.0 \cdot 5.0^2\,\mathrm{m} = 37.5\,\mathrm{m}$, therefore the distance between them is $37.5\,\mathrm{m} \approx 38\,\mathrm{m}$.
