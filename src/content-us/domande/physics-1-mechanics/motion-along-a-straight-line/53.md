For a body with constant acceleration, which equation is used directly when the time is neither given nor asked?

- $v^2 = v_0^2 + 2\,a\,(x - x_0)$
- $v = v_0 + a\,t$
- $x = x_0 + v_0\,t + \dfrac{1}{2}\,a\,t^2$
- $x = x_0 + v\,t$

## Explanation

The relation $v^2 = v_0^2 + 2\,a\,(x - x_0)$ is obtained by eliminating the time between the two equations of uniformly accelerated motion, hence it contains no $t$. The other equations contain the time, and $x = x_0 + v\,t$ holds only for uniform motion.
