The speed of a body moving in a straight line increases uniformly by $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ every $2.0\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the magnitude of its acceleration?

- $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0.67\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The acceleration is the change in velocity divided by the time in which it occurs, therefore
$$
a = \dfrac{3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}}{2.0\,\mathrm{s}} = 1.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
