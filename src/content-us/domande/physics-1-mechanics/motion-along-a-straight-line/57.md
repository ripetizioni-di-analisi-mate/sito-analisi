A body starts from rest with constant acceleration and covers $6.0\,\mathrm{m}$ in the first $2.0\,\mathrm{s}$. What distance does it cover in the following $2.0\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $6.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m}$
- $24\,\mathrm{m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Starting from rest, the distance grows with the square of the time, therefore in $4.0\,\mathrm{s}$ the body covers $4 \cdot 6.0\,\mathrm{m} = 24\,\mathrm{m}$. The distance covered between $2.0\,\mathrm{s}$ and $4.0\,\mathrm{s}$ is $24\,\mathrm{m} - 6.0\,\mathrm{m} = 18\,\mathrm{m}$.
