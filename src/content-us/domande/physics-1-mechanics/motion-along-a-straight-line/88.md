The acceleration $a(t)$ of a body is known at every instant. Which further data determine the law of motion $x(t)$?

- Only the position at one instant $t_0$.
- Only the velocity at one instant $t_0$.
- The velocity and the position at one instant $t_0$.
- Only the acceleration at one instant $t_0$.

## Explanation

The law of motion is obtained from $a(t)$ with two integrations, and each integration introduces one constant. Two data are therefore needed, namely the velocity and the position at the same instant $t_0$.
