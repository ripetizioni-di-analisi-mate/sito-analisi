A body moves along the $x$ axis with the law of motion $x(t) = 0.50\,t^3 - 6.0\,t$, with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. What is its velocity at $t = 4.0\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $18\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $24\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The derivative of the law of motion is $v(t) = 1.5\,t^2 - 6.0$, hence $v(4.0) = 24 - 6.0 = 18\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The value $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is the average velocity between $t = 0$ and $t = 4.0\,\mathrm{s}$, since $x(4.0) = 8.0\,\mathrm{m}$.
