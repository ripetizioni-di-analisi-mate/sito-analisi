A body moves along the $x$ axis with velocity $+5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for $4.0\,\mathrm{s}$ and then with velocity $-3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for the following $6.0\,\mathrm{s}$. What distance does it travel in the whole interval?

- $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $80\,\mathrm{m}$
- $38\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The areas under the velocity-time graph are $+20\,\mathrm{m}$ and $-18\,\mathrm{m}$. The distance traveled adds their magnitudes, hence it is $38\,\mathrm{m}$, while the displacement is $20\,\mathrm{m} - 18\,\mathrm{m} = 2.0\,\mathrm{m}$.
