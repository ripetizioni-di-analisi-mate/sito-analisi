Which quantity equals the signed area between the acceleration-time graph of a body and the time axis over an interval?

- The displacement over the interval.
- The change in acceleration over the interval.
- The average velocity over the interval.
- The change in velocity over the interval.

## Explanation

The velocity is an antiderivative of the acceleration, therefore, by the fundamental theorem of calculus, $\displaystyle\int_{t_1}^{t_2} a(t)\,dt = v(t_2) - v(t_1)$. The displacement is instead the area under the velocity-time graph.
