At a certain instant the velocity and the acceleration of a body moving along a line have opposite signs. Which statement is correct?

- The speed of the body is increasing at that instant.
- The velocity of the body is constant around that instant.
- The body is at rest at that instant.
- The speed of the body is decreasing at that instant.

## Explanation

When $v$ and $a$ have opposite signs the acceleration acts against the motion, hence the speed $|v|$ decreases. The body cannot be at rest, since a velocity with a sign is different from zero.
