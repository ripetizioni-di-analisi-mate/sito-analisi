Two balls are thrown vertically upward, the second with three times the initial speed of the first. Air resistance is negligible. What is the ratio of the maximum height of the second ball to that of the first?

- $9$
- $3$
- $\sqrt{3}$
- $6$

## Explanation

The maximum height is $h = \dfrac{v_0^2}{2g}$, hence it is proportional to the square of the initial speed. Tripling $v_0$ multiplies the height by $3^2 = 9$.
