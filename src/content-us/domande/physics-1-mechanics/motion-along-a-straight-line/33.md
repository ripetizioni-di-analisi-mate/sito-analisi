A ball hits a wall with velocity $+8.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and bounces back with velocity $-6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The contact lasts $0.020\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the average acceleration of the ball during the contact?

- $-100\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $700\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-700\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $100\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The change in velocity is $\Delta v = -6.0\,\mathrm{m/s} - 8.0\,\mathrm{m/s} = -14\,\mathrm{m/s}$, since the velocity reverses its sign. Therefore
$$
a_{\mathrm{av}} = \dfrac{-14\,\mathrm{m/s}}{0.020\,\mathrm{s}} = -700\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
Subtracting the speeds instead of the velocities would give the much smaller value $-100\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
