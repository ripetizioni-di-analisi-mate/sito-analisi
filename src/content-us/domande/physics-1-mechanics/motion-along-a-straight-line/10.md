The origin of the $x$ axis is moved $8.0\,\mathrm{m}$ in the positive sense, and the positive sense is left unchanged. Which quantity, computed between two given instants, keeps the same value?

- The initial position of the body.
- The final position of the body.
- The displacement of the body.
- The ratio of the final position to the initial position.

## Explanation

Moving the origin by $8.0\,\mathrm{m}$ subtracts $8.0\,\mathrm{m}$ from every coordinate, hence both positions change. The displacement is the difference of the two positions, therefore the shift cancels and the displacement keeps its value.
