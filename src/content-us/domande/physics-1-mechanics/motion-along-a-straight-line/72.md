A stone is thrown vertically downward with speed $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and air resistance is negligible. What is its speed after $1.5\,\mathrm{s}$? Take $g = 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $18.7\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10.7\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $14.7\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $15.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The initial velocity and the acceleration are both directed downward, hence the speed grows as $|v| = |v_0| + g\,t$:
$$
|v| = 4.0\,\mathrm{m/s} + 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot 1.5\,\mathrm{s} = 18.7\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
