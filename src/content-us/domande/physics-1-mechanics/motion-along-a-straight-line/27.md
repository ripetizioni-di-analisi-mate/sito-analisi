The law of motion $x(t)$ of a body is differentiable, and the average velocity over the interval from $t = 0$ to $t = 6.0\,\mathrm{s}$ is $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Which statement follows necessarily?

- The instantaneous velocity is $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at every instant of the interval.
- At some instant of the interval the instantaneous velocity is $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- The instantaneous velocity is $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at $t = 3.0\,\mathrm{s}$.
- The body does not reverse during the interval.

## Explanation

By the mean value theorem applied to $x(t)$, the average velocity over an interval equals the instantaneous velocity at some intermediate instant. The other statements hold only for particular motions, for instance with constant velocity or with constant acceleration.
