A body moves along the $x$ axis with the law of motion $x(t) = 4.0 - 3.0\,t + 2.5\,t^2$, with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. What is its acceleration?

- $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-3.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $1.25\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The law has the form $x = x_0 + v_0\,t + \dfrac{1}{2}\,a\,t^2$, hence $\dfrac{1}{2}\,a = 2.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and $a = 5.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The same value is the second derivative of $x(t)$.
