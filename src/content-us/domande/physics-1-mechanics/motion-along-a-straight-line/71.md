A ball is thrown vertically downward with speed $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The $y$ axis points upward. Which values of the initial velocity and of the acceleration are used in the equations of motion?

- $v_0 = -5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $a = -9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $v_0 = +5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $a = -9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $v_0 = -5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $a = +9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $v_0 = +5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $a = +9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

With the $y$ axis upward, every vector quantity directed downward has a negative component. The ball is thrown downward and the acceleration of free fall points downward, hence both values are negative.
