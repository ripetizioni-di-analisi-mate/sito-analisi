Which statement correctly describes the position-time graph of a body moving along a straight line?

- It is the path followed by the body, drawn in the plane.
- It has the same shape as the path, with the time used as a scale factor.
- It records the instant at which each position is occupied, and it differs from the path of the body.
- It gives the distance traveled by the body at each instant.

## Explanation

The position-time graph is the graph of the law of motion $x(t)$ in the plane of the axes $t$ and $x$. The path is only a segment of the line, while the graph tells at which instant each point of that segment is occupied, and it gives the signed position, which differs in general from the distance traveled.
