A body starts from rest with constant acceleration and covers $50\,\mathrm{m}$ in $5.0\,\mathrm{s}$. What is its acceleration?

- $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

From $x = \dfrac{1}{2}\,a\,t^2$ with $v_0 = 0$ it follows that
$$
a = \dfrac{2x}{t^2} = \dfrac{2 \cdot 50\,\mathrm{m}}{(5.0\,\mathrm{s})^2} = 4.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
