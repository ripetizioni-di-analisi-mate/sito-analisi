The velocity-time graph of a body is a straight segment from $v = 2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at $t = 0$ to $v = 10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at $t = 4.0\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the displacement over this interval?

- $40\,\mathrm{m}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m}$
- $24\,\mathrm{m}$
- $48\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The displacement is the area under the velocity-time graph, here a trapezoid:
$$
\Delta x = \dfrac{2.0\,\mathrm{m/s} + 10\,\mathrm{m/s}}{2} \cdot 4.0\,\mathrm{s} = 24\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
