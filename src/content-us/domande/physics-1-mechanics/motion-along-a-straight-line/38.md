The velocity of a body moving along the $x$ axis is $v(t) = 2.0\,t^2 - 4.0$, with $v$ in meters per second and $t$ in seconds. What is its acceleration at $t = 3.0\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $12\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $14\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The acceleration is the derivative of the velocity, $a(t) = 4.0\,t$, hence $a(3.0) = 12\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The value $14$ is the velocity $v(3.0)$, and $6.0$ follows from losing the factor $2$ of the derivative of $t^2$.
