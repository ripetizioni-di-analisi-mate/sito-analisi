The position-time graph of a body is a straight line through the points $(0,\ 2.0\,\mathrm{m})$ and $(4.0\,\mathrm{s},\ 14\,\mathrm{m})$. What is the velocity of the body?

- $3.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.33\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

A straight position-time graph describes a uniform motion, whose velocity is the slope of the line:
$$
v = \dfrac{14\,\mathrm{m} - 2.0\,\mathrm{m}}{4.0\,\mathrm{s}} = 3.0\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The value $3.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ ignores the initial position $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$.
