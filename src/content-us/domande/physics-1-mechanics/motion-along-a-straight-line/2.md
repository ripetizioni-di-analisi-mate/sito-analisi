The $x$ axis points east and has its origin at a traffic light. A car is $0.25\,\mathrm{km}$ west of the light. What is the position of the car?

- $250\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-250\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-25\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-2500\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The distance in meters is $0.25\,\mathrm{km} = 250\,\mathrm{m}$. The car is on the side opposite to the positive sense, hence its coordinate is negative, and $x = -250\,\mathrm{m}$.
