A body with constant acceleration covers $100\,\mathrm{m}$ in $5.0\,\mathrm{s}$ without reversing, and at the end of this interval its velocity is $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What was its initial velocity?

- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $40\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $50\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

With constant acceleration the average velocity is the arithmetic mean of the initial and final velocities, and here it equals $\dfrac{100\,\mathrm{m}}{5.0\,\mathrm{s}} = 20\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Therefore $\dfrac{v_0 + 30\,\mathrm{m/s}}{2} = 20\,\mathrm{m/s}$, which gives $v_0 = 10\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
