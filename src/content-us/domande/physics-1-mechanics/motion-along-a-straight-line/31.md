At the same instant the tangents to the position-time graphs of two bodies have slopes $+3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $-5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Which body has the larger speed at that instant?

- The first body, since its velocity is the larger number.
- The two bodies have the same speed.
- The speeds cannot be compared without the positions of the bodies.
- The second body, whose speed is $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

The slope of the tangent is the instantaneous velocity, and the speed is its magnitude. The speeds are therefore $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and the second body is the faster one, although it moves in the negative sense.
