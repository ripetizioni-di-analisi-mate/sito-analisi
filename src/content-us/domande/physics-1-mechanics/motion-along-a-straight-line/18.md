A body moves along the $x$ axis with the law of motion $x(t) = 2.0\,t^2$, with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. What is its average velocity between $t = 1.0\,\mathrm{s}$ and $t = 3.0\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The positions are $x(1.0) = 2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ and $x(3.0) = 18\,\mathrm{m}$, hence
$$
v_{\mathrm{av}} = \dfrac{18\,\mathrm{m} - 2.0\,\mathrm{m}}{3.0\,\mathrm{s} - 1.0\,\mathrm{s}} = 8.0\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The values $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ are the instantaneous velocities at the two ends of the interval.
