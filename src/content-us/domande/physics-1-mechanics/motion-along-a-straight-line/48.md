Two runners move in the same sense along a straight track. Runner $A$ runs at $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and runner $B$, who is $45\,\mathrm{m}$ ahead, runs at $4.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Both speeds are constant. After how long does $A$ catch up with $B$?

- $4.3\,\mathrm{s}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{s}$
- $30\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The runners move in the same sense, therefore the gap closes at the difference of the speeds, $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s} - 4.5\,\mathrm{m/s} = 1.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The time is
$$
t = \dfrac{45\,\mathrm{m}}{1.5\,\mathrm{m/s}} = 30\,\mathrm{s} .
$$
