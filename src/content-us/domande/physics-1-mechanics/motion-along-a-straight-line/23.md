A body moves along the $x$ axis with the law of motion $x(t) = 2.0\,t^3 - 24\,t$, with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds, for $t \ge 0$. At which instant $t > 0$ is the body momentarily at rest?

- $3.5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The body is at rest when the velocity vanishes. The velocity is $v(t) = 6.0\,t^2 - 24$, which vanishes for $t^2 = 4.0\,\mathrm{s^2}$, hence at $t = 2.0\,\mathrm{s}$. The instant $3.5\,\mathrm{s}$ is the one at which the position vanishes, which is a different condition.
