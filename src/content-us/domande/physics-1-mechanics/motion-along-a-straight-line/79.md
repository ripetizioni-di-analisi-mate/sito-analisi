A body has initial velocity $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Its acceleration is $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ from $t = 0$ to $t = 4.0\,\mathrm{s}$ and zero afterward. What is its velocity at $t = 6.0\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $14\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The change in velocity is the area under the acceleration-time graph, that is $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot 4.0\,\mathrm{s} = 12\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and after $t = 4.0\,\mathrm{s}$ the velocity no longer changes. Therefore $v(6.0) = 2.0\,\mathrm{m/s} + 12\,\mathrm{m/s} = 14\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
