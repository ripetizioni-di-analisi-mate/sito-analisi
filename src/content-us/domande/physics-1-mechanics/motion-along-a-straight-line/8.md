A body moves along the $x$ axis with the law of motion $x(t) = 4.0\,t - 1.0\,t^2$, with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. What distance does it travel between $t = 1.0\,\mathrm{s}$ and $t = 3.0\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.0\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The velocity $v(t) = 4.0 - 2.0\,t$ vanishes at $t = 2.0\,\mathrm{s}$, hence the body reverses at $x(2.0) = 4.0\,\mathrm{m}$. Since $x(1.0) = x(3.0) = 3.0\,\mathrm{m}$, the body covers $1.0\,\mathrm{m}$ forward and $1.0\,\mathrm{m}$ back, therefore the distance traveled is $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$, while the displacement is zero.
