Which relation holds, over the same time interval, between the magnitude of the average velocity and the average speed of a body moving along a line?

- The two quantities are always equal.
- The average speed is less than or equal to the magnitude of the average velocity.
- The magnitude of the average velocity is less than or equal to the average speed, with equality when the body does not reverse.
- The average speed is twice the magnitude of the average velocity whenever the body reverses.

## Explanation

The average velocity uses the magnitude of the displacement, while the average speed uses the distance traveled, and both are divided by the same elapsed time. The magnitude of the displacement never exceeds the distance traveled, and the two coincide when the body moves always in the same sense.
