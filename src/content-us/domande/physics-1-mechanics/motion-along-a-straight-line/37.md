On a certain time interval the position-time graph of a body is concave down. Which statement is correct on that interval?

- The acceleration of the body is negative.
- The velocity of the body is negative.
- The body is moving toward the origin.
- The body is slowing down.

## Explanation

The acceleration is the second derivative of $x(t)$, and a graph that is concave down has negative second derivative. The sign of the velocity is not fixed by the concavity, hence the body can move in either sense and can speed up or slow down.
