On the position-time graph of a body, the point at $t = 2.0\,\mathrm{s}$ has $x = 4.0\,\mathrm{m}$ and the point at $t = 6.0\,\mathrm{s}$ has $x = 16\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the average velocity between these two instants?

- $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The average velocity is the slope of the secant line through the two points:
$$
v_{\mathrm{av}} = \dfrac{16\,\mathrm{m} - 4.0\,\mathrm{m}}{6.0\,\mathrm{s} - 2.0\,\mathrm{s}} = \dfrac{12\,\mathrm{m}}{4.0\,\mathrm{s}} = 3.0\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
Dividing a single position by a single instant does not give the average velocity, since both the positions and the instants enter as differences.
