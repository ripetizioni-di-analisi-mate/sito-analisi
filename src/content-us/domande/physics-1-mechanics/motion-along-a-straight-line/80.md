At a certain instant the tangent to the velocity-time graph of a body is horizontal. What can be concluded about that instant?

- The body is at rest.
- The body is at the origin of the axis.
- The acceleration of the body is zero.
- The body reverses its sense of motion.

## Explanation

The slope of the tangent to the velocity-time graph is the acceleration, therefore a horizontal tangent means $a = 0$. The velocity can have any value at that instant, and the speed attains there a maximum or a minimum.
