At a certain point the tangent to the position-time graph of a body is horizontal. What can be concluded about the body at that instant?

- The body is at rest at that instant.
- The acceleration of the body is zero at that instant.
- The body is at the origin at that instant.
- The body moves with constant nonzero velocity around that instant.

## Explanation

The slope of the tangent to the position-time graph is the instantaneous velocity, therefore a horizontal tangent means $v = 0$. The acceleration can be different from zero at that instant, as at the highest point of a vertical throw.
