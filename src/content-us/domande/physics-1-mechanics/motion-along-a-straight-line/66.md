A stone is dropped from rest into a well and hits the water $1.8\,\mathrm{s}$ later. Air resistance and the time taken by the sound are negligible. How deep is the water surface below the starting point? Take $g = 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $31.8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $17.7\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8.83\,\mathrm{m}$
- $15.9\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

A body dropped from rest falls a distance $h = \dfrac{1}{2}\,g\,t^2$, therefore
$$
h = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot (1.8\,\mathrm{s})^2 = 15.9\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
