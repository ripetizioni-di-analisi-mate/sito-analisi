The velocity-time graph of a body is a straight segment from $v = 12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at $t = 0$ to $v = 0$ at $t = 3.0\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the acceleration of the body?

- $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-4.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-18\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-0.25\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The acceleration is the slope of the velocity-time graph, therefore
$$
a = \dfrac{0 - 12\,\mathrm{m/s}}{3.0\,\mathrm{s}} = -4.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
The number $18$ is the area under the graph, which gives the displacement in meters.
