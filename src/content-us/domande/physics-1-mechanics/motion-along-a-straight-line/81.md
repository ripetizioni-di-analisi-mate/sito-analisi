The velocity-time graph of a body is a straight line with positive slope. Which shape does its position-time graph have?

- A straight line with positive slope.
- An arc of a parabola that is concave down.
- A horizontal straight line.
- An arc of a parabola that is concave up.

## Explanation

A straight velocity-time graph with positive slope means a constant positive acceleration. The position is then a polynomial of degree two in $t$ with positive coefficient of $t^2$, whose graph is a parabola concave up.
