A ball is thrown vertically upward with speed $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and air resistance is negligible. What maximum height does it reach above the launch point? Take $g = 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $22.9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $11.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.53\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5.73\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

At the highest point the velocity vanishes, therefore the relation between velocity and position gives
$$
h = \dfrac{v_0^2}{2g} = \dfrac{(15\,\mathrm{m/s})^2}{2 \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}} = 11.5\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
