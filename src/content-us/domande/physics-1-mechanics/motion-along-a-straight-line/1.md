A passenger sits in a train that moves along a straight track at constant velocity. Which statement about the position of the passenger is correct?

- It is constant in the frame of the train and changes in the frame of the ground.
- It is constant both in the frame of the train and in the frame of the ground.
- It changes both in the frame of the train and in the frame of the ground.
- It changes in the frame of the train and is constant in the frame of the ground.

## Explanation

Motion is described with respect to a frame of reference. The passenger keeps the same coordinate with respect to the train, while with respect to the ground the passenger is carried along by the train, therefore the position changes in that frame.
