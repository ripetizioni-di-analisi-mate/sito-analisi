A body passes the origin at $t = 0$ with velocity $v_0 = 4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and has acceleration $a(t) = -2.0\,t$, with $a$ in $\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and $t$ in seconds. At which instant does it reverse its sense of motion?

- $1.4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Integrating the acceleration from $0$ gives $v(t) = 4.0 - 1.0\,t^2$. The body reverses when the velocity vanishes, hence at $t = 2.0\,\mathrm{s}$. Losing the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$ of the integral of $t$ gives $v = 4.0 - 2.0\,t^2$ and the wrong instant $1.4\,\mathrm{s}$.
