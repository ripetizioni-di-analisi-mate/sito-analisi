A body starts from rest with acceleration $a(t) = 2.0\,t$, with $a$ in $\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and $t$ in seconds. What is its velocity at $t = 3.0\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $9.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Integrating the acceleration from $0$ with $v_0 = 0$ gives
$$
v(t) = \int_0^t 2.0\,\tau\,d\tau = 1.0\,t^2 ,
$$
hence $v(3.0) = 9.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The value $6.0$ is the acceleration at that instant.
