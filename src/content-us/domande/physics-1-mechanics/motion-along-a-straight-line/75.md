A body is dropped from rest and air resistance is negligible. What is its speed after it has fallen $5.0\,\mathrm{m}$? Take $g = 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $98.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $9.90\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $7.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $49.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The time is not given, therefore the relation between velocity and position is used with $v_0 = 0$:
$$
|v| = \sqrt{2\,g\,h} = \sqrt{2 \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot 5.0\,\mathrm{m}} = 9.90\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
