At a certain instant a body moving along a line has velocity $v = 0$ and acceleration $a \neq 0$. Which statement is correct?

- The body stays at rest, since its velocity is zero.
- The acceleration must vanish as well, since the body is at rest.
- The body is momentarily at rest, and its velocity becomes different from zero immediately afterwards.
- The body is at the origin of the axis.

## Explanation

A zero velocity only tells that the body is at rest at that instant. Since $a = \dfrac{dv}{dt} \neq 0$, the velocity is changing, hence it is different from zero just after that instant, as happens at the highest point of a vertical throw.
