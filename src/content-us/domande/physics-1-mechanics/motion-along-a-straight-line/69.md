A ball is thrown vertically upward with speed $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and air resistance is negligible. After how long does it return to the launch point? Take $g = 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $2.04\,\mathrm{s}$
- $8.16\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4.08\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.02\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Setting $y = 0$ in $y = v_0\,t - \dfrac{1}{2}\,g\,t^2$ gives the positive root
$$
t = \dfrac{2v_0}{g} = \dfrac{2 \cdot 20\,\mathrm{m/s}}{9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}} = 4.08\,\mathrm{s} ,
$$
which is twice the rising time $2.04\,\mathrm{s}$.
