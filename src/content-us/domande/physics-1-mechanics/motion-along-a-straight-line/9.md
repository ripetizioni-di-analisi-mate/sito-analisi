At a certain instant a body on the $x$ axis is at the position $x = -4.0\,\mathrm{m}$. What does the minus sign tell?

- The body is moving in the negative sense of the axis.
- The body is slowing down.
- The body is on the negative side of the origin, at a distance of $4.0\,\mathrm{m}$ from it.
- The body is $4.0\,\mathrm{m}$ behind the point from which it started.

## Explanation

The position is the signed distance from the origin, therefore its sign only tells on which side of the origin the body is. The sense of motion is given by the sign of the velocity, and the starting point is not known from the position at one instant.
