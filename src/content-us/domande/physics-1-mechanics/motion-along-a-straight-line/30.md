A body moves along the $x$ axis with the law of motion $x(t) = 10 + 8.0\,t - 2.0\,t^2$, with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds, for $t \ge 0$. What is the largest coordinate the body reaches?

- $26\,\mathrm{m}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8.0\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The velocity $v(t) = 8.0 - 4.0\,t$ is positive up to $t = 2.0\,\mathrm{s}$ and negative afterwards, hence the body reverses at that instant. The largest coordinate is therefore $x(2.0) = 10 + 16 - 8.0 = 18\,\mathrm{m}$.
