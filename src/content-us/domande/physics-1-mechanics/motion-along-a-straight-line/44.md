A truck travels at the constant speed of $72\,\mathrm{km/h}$ along a straight road. How long does it take to cover $1.5\,\mathrm{km}$?

- $75\,\mathrm{s}$
- $21\,\mathrm{s}$
- $48\,\mathrm{s}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

In SI units the speed is $\dfrac{72}{3.6}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 20\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the distance is $1500\,\mathrm{m}$, hence
$$
t = \dfrac{1500\,\mathrm{m}}{20\,\mathrm{m/s}} = 75\,\mathrm{s} .
$$
