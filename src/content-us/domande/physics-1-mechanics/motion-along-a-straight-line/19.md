A cyclist covers $9.0\,\mathrm{km}$ along a straight road in $30\,\mathrm{min}$, without reversing. What is the average speed in meters per second?

- $0.30\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $300\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

In SI units the distance is $9000\,\mathrm{m}$ and the time is $30 \cdot 60\,\mathrm{s} = 1800\,\mathrm{s}$, therefore the average speed is $\dfrac{9000\,\mathrm{m}}{1800\,\mathrm{s}} = 5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The value $18$ is the same speed expressed in $\mathrm{km/h}$.
