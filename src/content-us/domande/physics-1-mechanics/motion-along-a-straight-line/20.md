A car travels along a straight road for $10\,\mathrm{min}$ at $72\,\mathrm{km/h}$ and then, in the same sense, for $30\,\mathrm{min}$ at $36\,\mathrm{km/h}$. What is its average speed?

- $45\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $54\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $30\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $36\,\mathrm{km/h}$

## Explanation

The two stretches are $72\,\mathrm{km/h} \cdot \dfrac{1}{6}\,\mathrm{h} = 12\,\mathrm{km}$ and $36\,\mathrm{km/h} \cdot \dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{h} = 18\,\mathrm{km}$. The total time is $\dfrac{2}{3}\,\mathrm{h}$, therefore the average speed is $\dfrac{30\,\mathrm{km}}{\dfrac{2}{3}\,\mathrm{h}} = 45\,\mathrm{km/h}$.
