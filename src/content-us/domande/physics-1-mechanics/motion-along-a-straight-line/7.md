A body moves along the $x$ axis with the law of motion $x(t) = 3.0 + 2.0\,t - 1.0\,t^2$, with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. What is its position at $t = 3.0\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-3.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6.0\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The position is obtained by substituting $t = 3.0\,\mathrm{s}$ into the law of motion:
$$
x(3.0) = 3.0 + 2.0 \cdot 3.0 - 1.0 \cdot 3.0^2 = 3.0 + 6.0 - 9.0 = 0 .
$$
The body is therefore at the origin at that instant.
