The position-time graph of a body is a straight segment from $(0,\ 0)$ to $(4.0\,\mathrm{s},\ 12\,\mathrm{m})$, followed by a horizontal segment up to $t = 10\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the velocity of the body at $t = 6.0\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

At $t = 6.0\,\mathrm{s}$ the graph is horizontal, hence the slope of the tangent is zero and the body is at rest. The value $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is the average velocity from $t = 0$ to $t = 6.0\,\mathrm{s}$, which differs from the instantaneous one.
