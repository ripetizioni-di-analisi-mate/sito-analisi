A body passes the origin at $t = 0$ with velocity $v_0 = +12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and has constant acceleration $a = -4.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is its position at $t = 5.0\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m}$
- $110\,\mathrm{m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The law of motion holds for the whole interval, reversal included, since the acceleration is constant:
$$
x(5.0) = 12 \cdot 5.0\,\mathrm{m} - \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 4.0 \cdot 5.0^2\,\mathrm{m} = 60\,\mathrm{m} - 50\,\mathrm{m} = 10\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $18\,\mathrm{m}$ is the position at the reversal, reached at $t = 3.0\,\mathrm{s}$, and $20\,\mathrm{m}$ is the distance traveled.
