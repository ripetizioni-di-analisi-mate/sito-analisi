A car moves with constant acceleration and its velocity increases from $8.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in $6.0\,\mathrm{s}$. What distance does it cover in this time?

- $120\,\mathrm{m}$
- $48\,\mathrm{m}$
- $84\,\mathrm{m}$
- $36\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

With constant acceleration the average velocity is $\dfrac{8.0\,\mathrm{m/s} + 20\,\mathrm{m/s}}{2} = 14\,\mathrm{m/s}$, therefore the distance is $14\,\mathrm{m/s} \cdot 6.0\,\mathrm{s} = 84\,\mathrm{m}$.
