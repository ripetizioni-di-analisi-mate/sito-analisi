At $t = 0$ body $A$ is at $x = 0$ and moves with constant velocity $+4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$, while body $B$ is at $x = 60\,\mathrm{m}$ and moves with constant velocity $-2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. At which instant do they meet?

- $30\,\mathrm{s}$
- $15\,\mathrm{s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The laws of motion are $x_A = 4.0\,t$ and $x_B = 60 - 2.0\,t$. The bodies meet when $x_A = x_B$, that is, when $6.0\,t = 60$, hence at $t = 10\,\mathrm{s}$. The two bodies approach each other, therefore the speeds add and the gap closes at $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
