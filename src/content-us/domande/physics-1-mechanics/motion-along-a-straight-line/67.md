A ball is thrown vertically upward and air resistance is negligible. Which statement describes its acceleration?

- It is directed downward during the rise, zero at the highest point, and directed downward during the descent.
- It is directed downward with magnitude $g$ throughout the flight, highest point included.
- It is directed upward during the rise, zero at the highest point, and directed downward during the descent.
- It is directed downward during the rise and upward during the descent.

## Explanation

The ball is free during the whole flight, hence its acceleration is $-g$ at every instant, also at the highest point. At that point the velocity vanishes, while the acceleration keeps its value and makes the ball start to fall.
