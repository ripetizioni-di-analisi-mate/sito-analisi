Two balls of different mass are dropped from rest from the same height at the same instant, and air resistance is negligible. Which statement is correct?

- The heavier ball reaches the ground first.
- The lighter ball reaches the ground first.
- The heavier ball reaches the ground first only if the height is large.
- The two balls reach the ground at the same instant.

## Explanation

By the law of free fall the acceleration $g$ is the same for every body, independently of its mass. Both balls start from rest at the same height with the same acceleration, therefore they have the same law of motion and land together.
