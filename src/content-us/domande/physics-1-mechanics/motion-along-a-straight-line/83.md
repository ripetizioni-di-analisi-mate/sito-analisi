The velocity-time graph of a body is a horizontal line at $v = -3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ from $t = 0$ to $t = 5.0\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the displacement over this interval?

- $-15\,\mathrm{m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-0.60\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0$

## Explanation

The displacement is the signed area between the graph and the time axis. The rectangle lies below the axis, therefore $\Delta x = -3.0\,\mathrm{m/s} \cdot 5.0\,\mathrm{s} = -15\,\mathrm{m}$.
