At a certain instant a body moving along the $x$ axis has velocity $v = -8.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and acceleration $a = -2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Which statement is correct?

- The body is moving in the negative sense and speeding up.
- The body is moving in the negative sense and slowing down.
- The body is moving in the positive sense and slowing down.
- The body is moving in the positive sense and speeding up.

## Explanation

The sign of the velocity gives the sense of motion, which is negative. The velocity and the acceleration have the same sign, therefore the speed $|v|$ increases and the body is speeding up.
