A body moves with initial velocity $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and constant acceleration $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ in the same sense. What is its velocity after $4.0\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $9.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

By the equation of the velocity of uniformly accelerated motion,
$$
v = v_0 + a\,t = 6.0\,\mathrm{m/s} + 1.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot 4.0\,\mathrm{s} = 12\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
