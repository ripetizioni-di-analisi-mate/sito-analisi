A bus covers $6.0\,\mathrm{km}$ in $10\,\mathrm{min}$, stops for $5.0\,\mathrm{min}$, and then covers $3.0\,\mathrm{km}$ in $5.0\,\mathrm{min}$, always in the same sense. What is its average speed over the whole trip?

- $27\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $36\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $18\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $54\,\mathrm{km/h}$

## Explanation

The average speed is the distance traveled divided by the total elapsed time, and the stop is part of that time. The distance is $9.0\,\mathrm{km}$ and the time is $20\,\mathrm{min} = \dfrac{1}{3}\,\mathrm{h}$, therefore the average speed is $27\,\mathrm{km/h}$.
