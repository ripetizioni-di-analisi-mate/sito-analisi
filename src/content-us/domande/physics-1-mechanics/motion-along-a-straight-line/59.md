A car starts from rest, accelerates with $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ for $5.0\,\mathrm{s}$, and then moves at constant speed for $10\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the total distance covered?

- $100\,\mathrm{m}$
- $150\,\mathrm{m}$
- $75\,\mathrm{m}$
- $125\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The first stage gives $\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot (5.0\,\mathrm{s})^2 = 25\,\mathrm{m}$ and ends with the speed $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The second stage is covered at that speed, hence it gives $100\,\mathrm{m}$, and the total distance is $125\,\mathrm{m}$.
