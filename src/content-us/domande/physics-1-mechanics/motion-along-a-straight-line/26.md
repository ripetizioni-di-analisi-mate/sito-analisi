At a certain instant a body moving along the $x$ axis has velocity $v = -12\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Which statement is correct?

- Its speed is $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and it is moving in the negative sense.
- Its speed is $-12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and it is moving in the negative sense.
- Its speed is $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and it is slowing down.
- Its speed is $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and it is on the negative side of the origin.

## Explanation

The instantaneous speed is the magnitude $|v|$, hence it is $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and the sign of the velocity gives the sense of motion. Whether the body slows down depends on the acceleration, and on which side of the origin it is depends on the position, and neither is given.
