A car covers the first half of a straight route at $40\,\mathrm{km/h}$ and the second half at $120\,\mathrm{km/h}$. What is its average speed over the whole route?

- $80\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $60\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $40\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $30\,\mathrm{km/h}$

## Explanation

Each speed must be weighted with the time spent at it. With $d$ the length of each half,
$$
v_{\mathrm{av}} = \dfrac{2d}{\dfrac{d}{40\,\mathrm{km/h}} + \dfrac{d}{120\,\mathrm{km/h}}} = \dfrac{2 \cdot 40 \cdot 120}{40 + 120}\,\mathrm{km/h} = 60\,\mathrm{km/h} .
$$
The arithmetic mean $80\,\mathrm{km/h}$ would hold for equal times, and here the car spends three times as long on the slower half.
