A car speeds up uniformly along a straight road from $18\,\mathrm{km/h}$ to $72\,\mathrm{km/h}$ in $6.0\,\mathrm{s}$. What is its average acceleration?

- $9.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3.3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

In SI units the two speeds are $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$, hence $\Delta v = 15\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and
$$
a_{\mathrm{av}} = \dfrac{\Delta v}{\Delta t} = \dfrac{15\,\mathrm{m/s}}{6.0\,\mathrm{s}} = 2.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
The value $9.0$ comes from dividing the change in $\mathrm{km/h}$ by the time without converting the units.
