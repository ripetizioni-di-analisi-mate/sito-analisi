A body starts from rest at the origin with acceleration $a(t) = 6.0\,t$, with $a$ in $\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and $t$ in seconds. What is its position at $t = 2.0\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $12\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $24\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

A first integration gives $v(t) = 3.0\,t^2$ and a second one gives $x(t) = 1.0\,t^3$, since $v_0 = 0$ and $x_0 = 0$. Therefore $x(2.0) = 8.0\,\mathrm{m}$, while $24\,\mathrm{m}$ follows from treating the final acceleration $12\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ as if it were constant.
