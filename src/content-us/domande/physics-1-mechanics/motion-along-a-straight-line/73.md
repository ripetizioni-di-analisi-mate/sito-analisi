A ball is thrown vertically upward from the ground with speed $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and air resistance is negligible. What is its velocity when it passes again through the launch point?

- Zero
- $24\,\mathrm{m/s}$ directed downward
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ directed upward
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ directed downward

## Explanation

The relation $v^2 = v_0^2 + 2\,a\,(y - y_0)$ gives $v^2 = v_0^2$ when the ball is back at the launch height. The speed is therefore $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and the ball is falling, so the velocity is directed downward.
