On the position-time graph of a body, how is the average velocity between the instants $t_1$ and $t_2$ obtained?

- As the slope of the tangent to the graph at $t_1$.
- As the area under the graph between $t_1$ and $t_2$, divided by $t_2 - t_1$.
- As the slope of the tangent to the graph at $t_2$.
- As the slope of the secant line through the points of the graph at $t_1$ and $t_2$.

## Explanation

The average velocity is $\dfrac{x(t_2) - x(t_1)}{t_2 - t_1}$, which is the slope of the straight line through the two points of the graph. The slope of a tangent gives an instantaneous velocity, which in general differs from the average one.
