The position-time graph of a body is a straight line with negative slope that crosses the time axis at $t = 4.0\,\mathrm{s}$. Which statement is correct?

- The velocity is zero at $t = 4.0\,\mathrm{s}$.
- The body slows down until $t = 4.0\,\mathrm{s}$ and speeds up afterward.
- The velocity is constant and negative.
- The acceleration is constant and negative.

## Explanation

A straight position-time graph has the same slope at every point, hence the velocity is constant, and here it is negative. Crossing the time axis means that the body passes through the origin, while the velocity keeps its value and the acceleration is zero.
