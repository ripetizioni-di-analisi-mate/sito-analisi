The velocity of a body decreases linearly from $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at $t = 0$ to $-4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at $t = 7.0\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the displacement over this interval?

- $21\,\mathrm{m}$
- $29\,\mathrm{m}$
- $49\,\mathrm{m}$
- $70\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The slope of the graph is $-2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, hence the velocity vanishes at $t = 5.0\,\mathrm{s}$ and the signed areas are $\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 5.0 \cdot 10\,\mathrm{m} = 25\,\mathrm{m}$ and $-\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 2.0 \cdot 4.0\,\mathrm{m} = -4.0\,\mathrm{m}$. The displacement is their sum, $21\,\mathrm{m}$, while $29\,\mathrm{m}$ is the distance traveled.
