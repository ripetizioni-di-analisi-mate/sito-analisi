The velocity of a body is $v(t) = 3.0\,t^2$, with $v$ in meters per second and $t$ in seconds, and at $t = 0$ the body is at $x_0 = 2.0\,\mathrm{m}$. What is its position at $t = 2.0\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $8.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $14\,\mathrm{m}$
- $26\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Integrating the velocity gives
$$
x(t) = x_0 + \int_0^t 3.0\,\tau^2\,d\tau = 2.0 + 1.0\,t^3 ,
$$
hence $x(2.0) = 2.0\,\mathrm{m} + 8.0\,\mathrm{m} = 10\,\mathrm{m}$. Forgetting the initial position gives $8.0\,\mathrm{m}$.
