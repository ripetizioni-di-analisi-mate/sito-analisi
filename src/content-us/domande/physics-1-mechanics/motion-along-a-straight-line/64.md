A train moving in the positive sense slows down uniformly from $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $8.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ while covering $168\,\mathrm{m}$. What is its acceleration?

- $-2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $1.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-1.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-0.071\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The time is not given, therefore the relation $v^2 = v_0^2 + 2\,a\,\Delta x$ is used:
$$
a = \dfrac{v^2 - v_0^2}{2\,\Delta x} = \dfrac{64 - 400}{2 \cdot 168}\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = -1.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
The sign is negative because the train moves in the positive sense and slows down.
