A body moves along the $x$ axis from the position $x_1 = 7.0\,\mathrm{m}$ to the position $x_2 = -3.0\,\mathrm{m}$. What is its displacement?

- $10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-4.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-10\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The displacement is the final position minus the initial one, therefore
$$
\Delta x = x_2 - x_1 = -3.0\,\mathrm{m} - 7.0\,\mathrm{m} = -10\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The sign is negative because the body ends up on the negative side of its starting point.
