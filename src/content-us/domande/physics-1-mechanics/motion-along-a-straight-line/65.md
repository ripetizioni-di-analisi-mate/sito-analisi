A stone is dropped from rest and air resistance is negligible. What is its speed after $2.5\,\mathrm{s}$ of fall? Take $g = 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $12.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $30.7\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.92\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $24.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Free fall is uniformly accelerated with acceleration of magnitude $g$, and $v_0 = 0$, hence the speed is
$$
|v| = g\,t = 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot 2.5\,\mathrm{s} = 24.5\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
