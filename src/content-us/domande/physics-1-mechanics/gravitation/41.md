Two planets with spherical symmetry have the same surface gravity, and planet B has twice the radius of planet A. What is the ratio $\dfrac{M_B}{M_A}$ of their masses?

- $2$
- $8$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $4$

## Explanation

The surface gravity is $g_0 = \dfrac{GM}{R^2}$, hence $M = \dfrac{g_0R^2}{G}$. With the same $g_0$ the mass is proportional to $R^2$, therefore doubling the radius requires four times the mass.
