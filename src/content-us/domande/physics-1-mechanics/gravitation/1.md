Two particles attract each other with a force of magnitude $F_0$. Both masses are halved and the distance between them is also halved. What is the new magnitude of the force?

- $\dfrac{F_0}{2}$
- $4F_0$
- $\dfrac{F_0}{16}$
- $F_0$

## Explanation

By the **law of universal gravitation** the force is proportional to $m_1m_2$ and to $\dfrac{1}{r^2}$. Halving both masses multiplies the product $m_1m_2$ by $\dfrac{1}{4}$, while halving the distance multiplies $\dfrac{1}{r^2}$ by $4$, hence the two factors cancel and the force is again $F_0$.
