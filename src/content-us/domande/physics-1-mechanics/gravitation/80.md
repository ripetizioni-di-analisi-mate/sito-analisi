According to Kepler's first law, where is the Sun located with respect to the elliptical orbit of a planet?

- At one focus of the ellipse
- At the center of the ellipse
- On the ellipse itself
- At one end of the major axis

## Explanation

**Kepler's first law** states that every planet travels an ellipse with the Sun at one focus; the other focus is empty. The Sun is at the center only in the particular case of a circle, where the two foci coincide.
