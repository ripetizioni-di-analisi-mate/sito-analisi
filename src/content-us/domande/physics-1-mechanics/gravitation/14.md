Which of the following is a unit of the gravitational field?

- $\mathrm{N\cdot kg}$
- $\mathrm{J/kg}$
- $\mathrm{N/kg}$
- $\mathrm{N\cdot m^2/kg^2}$

## Explanation

The gravitational field is the force per unit mass, $\vec{g} = \dfrac{\vec{F}}{m}$, therefore it is measured in $\mathrm{N/kg}$, which equals $\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The unit $\mathrm{J/kg}$ belongs to the gravitational potential, and $\mathrm{N\cdot m^2/kg^2}$ is the unit of $G$.
