Which statement about the constant $G$ and the acceleration $g$ at the surface of a planet is correct?

- $G$ is the same for every pair of bodies, while $g$ depends on the mass and the radius of the planet
- $G$ and $g$ are both universal constants
- $G$ depends on the masses of the two bodies, while $g$ is the same on every planet
- $G$ depends on the planet, while $g$ is the same on every planet

## Explanation

The constant $G$ appears in the law of universal gravitation and has the same value for every pair of masses. The surface gravity is $g_0 = \dfrac{GM}{R^2}$, therefore it changes from planet to planet with $M$ and $R$; on the Moon, for instance, it is about one sixth of the terrestrial value.
