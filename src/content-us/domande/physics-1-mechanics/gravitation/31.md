The surface gravity of the Moon is $1.62\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the weight on the Moon of an astronaut of mass $70.0\,\mathrm{kg}$?

- $687\,\mathrm{N}$
- $43.2\,\mathrm{N}$
- $113\,\mathrm{N}$
- $70.0\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The weight of a body resting on the surface of a planet is $P = mg_0$, so
$$
P = 70.0 \cdot 1.62\,\mathrm{N} = 113\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The mass of the astronaut is the same as on the Earth, while the weight is about one sixth of the terrestrial one.
