A uniform spherical shell has mass $M$ and radius $R$. What is the gravitational field at a point inside the shell, at the distance $r < R$ from its center?

- $\dfrac{GM}{R^2}$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{GM}{r^2}$
- $\dfrac{GM}{R^3}\,r$

## Explanation

By the **shell theorem** the field inside a uniform spherical shell is zero at every point, because the contributions of all the mass elements of the shell cancel. The expression $\dfrac{GM}{R^3}\,r$ describes the inside of a uniform solid sphere, not of a shell.
