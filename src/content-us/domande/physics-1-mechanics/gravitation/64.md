A probe of mass $1000\,\mathrm{kg}$ moves in the field of a planet with the mechanical energy $E = 2.0 \times 10^{9}\,\mathrm{J}$, the potential energy being zero at infinite distance. What speed does it have very far from the planet?

- $2.0 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.4 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6.3 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4.0 \times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Far from the planet the potential energy is zero, so the whole mechanical energy is kinetic, $E = \dfrac{1}{2}mv_\infty^2$. Hence
$$
v_\infty = \sqrt{\frac{2E}{m}} = \sqrt{\frac{2 \cdot 2.0 \times 10^{9}}{1000}}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 2.0 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
