Take $g_0 = 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ at the surface of the Earth. What is the gravitational field at the altitude $h = \dfrac{R_E}{2}$?

- $4.36\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $6.54\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $39.2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2.45\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The distance from the center is $r = \dfrac{3}{2}R_E$, hence
$$
g = g_0\left(\frac{R_E}{r}\right)^2 = 9.81 \cdot \frac{4}{9}\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 4.36\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
