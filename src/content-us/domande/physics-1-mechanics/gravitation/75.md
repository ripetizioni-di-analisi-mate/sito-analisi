A satellite on a circular orbit has the potential energy $-6.0 \times 10^{9}\,\mathrm{J}$, with the potential energy zero at infinite distance. What is its kinetic energy?

- $6.0 \times 10^{9}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-3.0 \times 10^{9}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $3.0 \times 10^{9}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1.2 \times 10^{10}\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

On a circular orbit $K = \dfrac{GMm}{2r}$ and $U = -\dfrac{GMm}{r}$, hence $K = -\dfrac{U}{2} = 3.0 \times 10^{9}\,\mathrm{J}$. The kinetic energy is positive, as every kinetic energy is.
