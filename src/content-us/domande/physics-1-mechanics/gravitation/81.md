A planet travels an elliptical orbit around the Sun. Where is its speed greatest?

- At aphelion, the point farthest from the Sun
- At perihelion, the point closest to the Sun
- At the ends of the minor axis
- It is the same at every point of the orbit

## Explanation

By **Kepler's second law** the segment from the Sun to the planet sweeps equal areas in equal times. Near the Sun the segment is short, so the planet must cover a longer arc in the same time, hence its speed is greatest at perihelion.
