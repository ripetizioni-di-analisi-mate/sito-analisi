For a planet that is a uniform sphere of radius $R$, where does the magnitude of the gravitational field reach its maximum?

- At the surface, $r = R$
- At the center, $r = 0$
- At $r = \dfrac{R}{2}$
- Nowhere, because it is the same at every point inside the planet

## Explanation

Inside the planet the field grows in proportion to $r$, and outside it decreases as $\dfrac{1}{r^2}$. The two expressions both equal $g_0 = \dfrac{GM}{R^2}$ at $r = R$, therefore the maximum is at the surface.
