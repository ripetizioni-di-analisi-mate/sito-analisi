The surface gravity of a planet with spherical symmetry is $g_0$. What is the gravitational field at the altitude $h = 2R$ above its surface, where $R$ is the radius of the planet?

- $\dfrac{g_0}{9}$
- $\dfrac{g_0}{4}$
- $\dfrac{g_0}{3}$
- $\dfrac{g_0}{2}$

## Explanation

The distance from the center is $r = R + h = 3R$. By the variation of $g$ with altitude,
$$
g = \frac{g_0}{\left(1 + \dfrac{h}{R}\right)^2} = \frac{g_0}{3^2} = \frac{g_0}{9} .
$$
