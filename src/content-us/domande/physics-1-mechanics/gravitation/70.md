A satellite travels a circular orbit around the Earth with its engines off, far above the atmosphere. Which forces act on it?

- The gravitational attraction and an outward force that balances it
- Only the gravitational attraction of the Earth, directed toward its center
- The gravitational attraction and a forward force along the velocity
- No force, because the speed is constant

## Explanation

The only interaction is the gravitational attraction of the Earth, directed toward its center. It is not balanced, since it supplies the centripetal acceleration $\dfrac{v^2}{r}$ that keeps the satellite on the circle. A constant speed on a circle still requires a resultant force, because the direction of the velocity changes.
