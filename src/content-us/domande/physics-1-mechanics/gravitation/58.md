A probe is launched from the surface of a planet with the speed $2v_f$, where $v_f$ is the escape speed. Neglecting every force except the attraction of the planet, what speed does the probe retain very far from the planet?

- $v_f$
- $\sqrt{5}\,v_f$
- $3v_f$
- $\sqrt{3}\,v_f$

## Explanation

Conservation of energy with $\dfrac{GMm}{R} = \dfrac{1}{2}mv_f^2$ gives
$$
\frac{1}{2}mv_\infty^2 = \frac{1}{2}m(2v_f)^2 - \frac{1}{2}mv_f^2 = \frac{3}{2}mv_f^2 ,
$$
hence $v_\infty = \sqrt{3}\,v_f$. The squares of the speeds are subtracted, not the speeds.
