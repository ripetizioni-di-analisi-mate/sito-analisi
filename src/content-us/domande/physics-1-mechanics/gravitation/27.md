Which hypothesis allows the field outside a planet to be computed as if the whole mass were a particle at its center?

- The density of the planet depends only on the distance from its center
- The planet does not rotate
- The mass of the planet is much larger than the mass of the test body
- The point lies at an altitude small compared with the radius of the planet

## Explanation

The shell theorem requires spherical symmetry, that is, a density that depends only on the distance from the center. Under that hypothesis the field at every point outside the body equals the field of a particle of the same total mass at the center, at any altitude.
