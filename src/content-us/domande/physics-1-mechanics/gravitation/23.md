A particle has mass $500\,\mathrm{kg}$. With $G = 6.674 \times 10^{-11}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m^2/kg^2}$, what is the magnitude of the gravitational field it produces at the distance $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $1.7 \times 10^{-8}\,\mathrm{N/kg}$
- $3.3 \times 10^{-8}\,\mathrm{N/kg}$
- $5.3 \times 10^{-13}\,\mathrm{N/kg}$
- $8.3 \times 10^{-9}\,\mathrm{N/kg}$

## Explanation

The field of a particle is
$$
g = \frac{GM}{r^2} = \frac{6.674 \times 10^{-11} \cdot 500}{(2.0)^2}\,\mathrm{N/kg} = 8.3 \times 10^{-9}\,\mathrm{N/kg} ,
$$
directed toward the particle.
