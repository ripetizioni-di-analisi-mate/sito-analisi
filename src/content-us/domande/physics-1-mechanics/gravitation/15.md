A particle of mass $M$ produces the field of magnitude $g$ at the distance $r$. To what distance must a point be moved for the field to have magnitude $2g$?

- $\dfrac{r}{2}$
- $\dfrac{r}{\sqrt{2}}$
- $2r$
- $\sqrt{2}\,r$

## Explanation

The field of a particle is $\dfrac{GM}{r^2}$, so doubling it requires $r^2$ to be halved. The new distance is therefore $\sqrt{\dfrac{r^2}{2}} = \dfrac{r}{\sqrt{2}}$.
