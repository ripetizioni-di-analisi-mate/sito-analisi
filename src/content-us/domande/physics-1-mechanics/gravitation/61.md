The Moon has retained no atmosphere. Which explanation agrees with the theory of escape speed?

- Its escape speed is small, and the fastest molecules of a gas at its surface temperature exceed it
- Its surface has no gravitational field
- The constant $G$ is smaller near the Moon
- The attraction of the Earth exceeds that of the Moon at its surface

## Explanation

The escape speed from the Moon is $2.37\,\mathrm{km/s}$, about one fifth of the terrestrial value. At the temperature of its surface the fastest molecules of a gas exceed that speed and leave, so over time the Moon has lost any atmosphere.
