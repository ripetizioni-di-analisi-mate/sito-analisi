Four particles of equal mass $m$ occupy the vertices of a square of side $a$. What is the gravitational potential energy of the system?

- $-\left(4 + \sqrt{2}\right)\dfrac{Gm^2}{a}$
- $-\dfrac{4Gm^2}{a}$
- $-\left(8 + 2\sqrt{2}\right)\dfrac{Gm^2}{a}$
- $-\left(4 + 2\sqrt{2}\right)\dfrac{Gm^2}{a}$

## Explanation

The energy is the sum over the six pairs. Four pairs are sides of length $a$ and two are diagonals of length $\sqrt{2}\,a$, hence
$$
U = -Gm^2\left(\frac{4}{a} + \frac{2}{\sqrt{2}\,a}\right) = -\left(4 + \sqrt{2}\right)\frac{Gm^2}{a} .
$$
Each pair is counted once.
