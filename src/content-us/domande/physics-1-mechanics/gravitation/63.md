At the distance $r$ from the center of a planet, what is the ratio of the escape speed from that distance to the speed of a circular orbit of radius $r$?

- $\sqrt{2}$
- $2$
- $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$
- $1$

## Explanation

From the distance $r$ the escape speed is $\sqrt{\dfrac{2GM}{r}}$, obtained by setting the mechanical energy to zero, while the speed of a circular orbit is $\sqrt{\dfrac{GM}{r}}$. Their ratio is $\sqrt{2}$.
