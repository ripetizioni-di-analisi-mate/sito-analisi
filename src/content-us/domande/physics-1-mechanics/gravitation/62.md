A meteoroid starts at rest very far from the Earth and falls towards it. With $GM_E = 3.984 \times 10^{14}\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$ and $R_E = 6.37 \times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{m}$, and neglecting air resistance and every other body, what is its speed when it reaches the surface?

- $7.91\,\mathrm{km/s}$
- $11.2\,\mathrm{km/s}$
- $15.8\,\mathrm{km/s}$
- $5.59\,\mathrm{km/s}$

## Explanation

The mechanical energy is zero at the start, since both the kinetic and the potential energy vanish. At the surface $\dfrac{1}{2}mv^2 - \dfrac{GM_Em}{R_E} = 0$, hence
$$
v = \sqrt{\frac{2GM_E}{R_E}} = \sqrt{\frac{2 \cdot 3.984 \times 10^{14}}{6.37 \times 10^{6}}}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 11.2\,\mathrm{km/s} ,
$$
which is the escape speed.
