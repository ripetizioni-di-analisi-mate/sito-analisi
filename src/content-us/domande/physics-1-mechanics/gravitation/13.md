Two identical small spheres whose centers are $0.10\,\mathrm{m}$ apart attract each other with a force of magnitude $1.0 \times 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{N}$. With $G = 6.674 \times 10^{-11}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m^2/kg^2}$, what is the mass of each sphere?

- $15\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $3.9\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

With equal masses the law gives $F = G\,\dfrac{m^2}{r^2}$, hence
$$
m = r\sqrt{\frac{F}{G}} = 0.10 \cdot \sqrt{\frac{1.0 \times 10^{-7}}{6.674 \times 10^{-11}}}\,\mathrm{kg} = 3.9\,\mathrm{kg} .
$$
The square root is needed because the product of the two masses is $m^2$.
