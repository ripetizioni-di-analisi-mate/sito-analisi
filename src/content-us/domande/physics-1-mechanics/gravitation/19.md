Two particles of masses $M$ and $16M$ are held at the distance $d$. At what distance from $M$, on the segment joining them, is the resultant gravitational field zero?

- $\dfrac{d}{17}$
- $\dfrac{d}{5}$
- $\dfrac{d}{4}$
- $\dfrac{4d}{5}$

## Explanation

On the segment the two fields have opposite senses, so the resultant vanishes where $\dfrac{GM}{x^2} = \dfrac{16GM}{(d-x)^2}$, that is $(d-x)^2 = 16x^2$. Since $0 < x < d$, the positive root gives $d - x = 4x$, hence $x = \dfrac{d}{5}$, on the side of the smaller mass.
