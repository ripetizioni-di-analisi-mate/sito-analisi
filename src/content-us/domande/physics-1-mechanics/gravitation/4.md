An apple of mass $m = 0.20\,\mathrm{kg}$ falls near the surface of the Earth, whose mass is $M_E = 5.97 \times 10^{24}\,\mathrm{kg}$. With $g = 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the magnitude of the acceleration of the Earth produced by the attraction of the apple?

- $1.6 \times 10^{-24}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3.4 \times 10^{-26}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3.3 \times 10^{-25}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The apple attracts the Earth with a force equal in magnitude to its weight, $F = mg = 0.20 \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{N} = 1.96\,\mathrm{N}$. By Newton's second law the acceleration of the Earth is
$$
a = \frac{F}{M_E} = \frac{1.96\,\mathrm{N}}{5.97 \times 10^{24}\,\mathrm{kg}} = 3.3 \times 10^{-25}\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
