A particle feels two gravitational forces: $6.0 \times 10^{-10}\,\mathrm{N}$ along $+x$ and $8.0 \times 10^{-10}\,\mathrm{N}$ along $+y$. What angle does the resultant force make with the positive $x$ axis, measured counterclockwise?

- $53^\circ$
- $37^\circ$
- $127^\circ$
- $233^\circ$

## Explanation

The resultant has components $F_x = 6.0 \times 10^{-10}\,\mathrm{N}$ and $F_y = 8.0 \times 10^{-10}\,\mathrm{N}$, both positive, so it lies in the first quadrant. Its angle with the $x$ axis is
$$
\theta = \arctan\frac{F_y}{F_x} = \arctan\frac{8.0}{6.0} = 53^\circ .
$$
