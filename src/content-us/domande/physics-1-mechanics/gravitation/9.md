The Sun, of mass $1.99 \times 10^{30}\,\mathrm{kg}$, and the Earth, of mass $5.97 \times 10^{24}\,\mathrm{kg}$, are $1.496 \times 10^{8}\,\mathrm{km}$ apart. With $G = 6.674 \times 10^{-11}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m^2/kg^2}$, what is the magnitude of the force between them?

- $3.54 \times 10^{28}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $3.54 \times 10^{22}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $5.30 \times 10^{33}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $5.93 \times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The distance must be converted to meters, $d = 1.496 \times 10^{11}\,\mathrm{m}$. Then
$$
F = G\,\frac{M_SM_E}{d^2} = 6.674 \times 10^{-11} \cdot \frac{1.99 \times 10^{30} \cdot 5.97 \times 10^{24}}{(1.496 \times 10^{11})^2}\,\mathrm{N} = 3.54 \times 10^{22}\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
