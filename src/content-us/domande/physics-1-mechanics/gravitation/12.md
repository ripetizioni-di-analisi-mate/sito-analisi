An astronaut of mass $70\,\mathrm{kg}$ stands on the surface of the Earth, with $M_E = 5.97 \times 10^{24}\,\mathrm{kg}$, $R_E = 6.37 \times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{m}$ and $G = 6.674 \times 10^{-11}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m^2/kg^2}$. What is the magnitude of the gravitational force of the Earth on the astronaut?

- $172\,\mathrm{N}$
- $687\,\mathrm{N}$
- $9.82\,\mathrm{N}$
- $6.87 \times 10^{8}\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

By the shell theorem the Earth acts as a particle at its center, at the distance $R_E$ from the astronaut. Hence
$$
F = G\,\frac{M_Em}{R_E^2} = 6.674 \times 10^{-11} \cdot \frac{5.97 \times 10^{24} \cdot 70}{(6.37 \times 10^{6})^2}\,\mathrm{N} = 687\,\mathrm{N} ,
$$
which is the weight $mg_0$ with $g_0 = 9.82\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
