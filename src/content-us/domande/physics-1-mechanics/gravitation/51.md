A body is released from rest at the distance $2R_E$ from the center of the Earth and falls freely. With $GM_E = 3.984 \times 10^{14}\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$ and $R_E = 6.37 \times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{m}$, and neglecting air resistance, what is its speed when it reaches the surface?

- $11.2\,\mathrm{km/s}$
- $7.91\,\mathrm{km/s}$
- $5.59\,\mathrm{km/s}$
- $15.8\,\mathrm{km/s}$

## Explanation

Mechanical energy is conserved, so
$$
\frac{1}{2}v^2 = \frac{GM_E}{R_E} - \frac{GM_E}{2R_E} = \frac{GM_E}{2R_E}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
v = \sqrt{\frac{GM_E}{R_E}} = \sqrt{\frac{3.984 \times 10^{14}}{6.37 \times 10^{6}}}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 7.91\,\mathrm{km/s} .
$$
