A body is launched from the surface of a planet with exactly the escape speed, and no force other than the attraction of the planet acts on it. What is its speed when it is very far from the planet?

- It tends to the escape speed
- It tends to zero
- It tends to half the escape speed
- It tends to $\sqrt{2}$ times the escape speed

## Explanation

At the escape speed the mechanical energy is $E = \dfrac{1}{2}mv_f^2 - \dfrac{GMm}{R} = 0$. Far from the planet the potential energy tends to zero, so the kinetic energy tends to $E = 0$ as well, and the body reaches arbitrarily large distances with a speed that tends to zero.
