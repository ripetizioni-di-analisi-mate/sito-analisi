Two particles of equal mass $M$ are held at the distance $d$. What is the magnitude of the resultant gravitational field at the midpoint of the segment joining them?

- $\dfrac{8GM}{d^2}$
- $\dfrac{4GM}{d^2}$
- $\dfrac{2GM}{d^2}$
- $0$

## Explanation

At the midpoint each particle produces a field of magnitude $\dfrac{GM}{\left(\dfrac{d}{2}\right)^2} = \dfrac{4GM}{d^2}$ directed towards itself. The two fields have equal magnitudes and opposite senses, therefore by superposition their sum is zero.
