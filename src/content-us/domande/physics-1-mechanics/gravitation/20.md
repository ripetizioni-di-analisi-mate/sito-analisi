A particle feels two gravitational forces: $6.0 \times 10^{-10}\,\mathrm{N}$ along $+x$ and $8.0 \times 10^{-10}\,\mathrm{N}$ along $+y$. What is the magnitude of the resultant force?

- $1.4 \times 10^{-9}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $2.0 \times 10^{-10}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1.0 \times 10^{-9}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1.0 \times 10^{-18}\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

By the **principle of superposition** the resultant is the vector sum of the two forces. They are perpendicular, therefore
$$
F = \sqrt{(6.0 \times 10^{-10})^2 + (8.0 \times 10^{-10})^2}\,\mathrm{N} = 1.0 \times 10^{-9}\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
