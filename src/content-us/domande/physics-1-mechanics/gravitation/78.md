A satellite of mass $1000\,\mathrm{kg}$ travels a circular orbit of radius $7.0 \times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{m}$ around the Earth, with $GM_E = 3.984 \times 10^{14}\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$. What is its mechanical energy, with the potential energy zero at infinite distance?

- $-5.7 \times 10^{10}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $+2.8 \times 10^{10}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-2.8 \times 10^{13}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-2.8 \times 10^{10}\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The mechanical energy of a circular orbit is
$$
E = -\frac{GM_Em}{2r} = -\frac{3.984 \times 10^{14} \cdot 1000}{2 \cdot 7.0 \times 10^{6}}\,\mathrm{J} = -2.8 \times 10^{10}\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
The value $-5.7 \times 10^{10}\,\mathrm{J}$ is the potential energy alone.
