Under which condition is the expression $U = mgh$ a good approximation of the gravitational potential energy near a planet?

- The altitude $h$ is small compared with the radius of the planet
- The mass $m$ is small compared with the mass of the planet
- The body moves along a vertical line
- The speed of the body is small

## Explanation

The expansion of $-\dfrac{GMm}{R+h}$ for $\dfrac{h}{R}$ small gives a constant plus $mg_0h$. The approximation therefore rests on $h \ll R$, and it does not depend on the mass, on the path or on the speed of the body.
