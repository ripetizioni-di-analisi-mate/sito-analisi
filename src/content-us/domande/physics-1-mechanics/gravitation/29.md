A planet has twice the mass of the Earth and twice its radius. Taking $9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ for the surface gravity of the Earth, what is the surface gravity of the planet?

- $9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2.45\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $19.6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $4.91\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Since $g_0 = \dfrac{GM}{R^2}$, doubling $M$ multiplies $g_0$ by $2$ and doubling $R$ divides it by $4$. Hence
$$
g_0 = 9.81 \cdot \frac{2}{4}\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 4.91\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
