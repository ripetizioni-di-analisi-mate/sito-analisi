Planet B has the same radius as planet A and four times its mass. If the escape speed from A is $v_A$, what is the escape speed from B?

- $4v_A$
- $\sqrt{2}\,v_A$
- $16v_A$
- $2v_A$

## Explanation

The escape speed is $\sqrt{\dfrac{2GM}{R}}$, proportional to $\sqrt{M}$ at fixed radius. Four times the mass gives $\sqrt{4} = 2$ times the escape speed.
