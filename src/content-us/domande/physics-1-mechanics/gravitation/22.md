Two masses attract each other with a certain force. A third mass is then placed near them. What happens to the force that the first mass exerts on the second?

- It does not change
- It increases
- It decreases
- It becomes zero if the third mass lies between the other two

## Explanation

By the principle of superposition the presence of a third body changes neither the magnitude nor the direction of the force between the first two. The resultant force on the second mass does change, because the force of the third mass is added to it.
