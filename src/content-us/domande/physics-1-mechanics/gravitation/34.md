With $R_E = 6.37 \times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{m}$, what percentage of the surface value is the gravitational field of the Earth at the altitude $h = 640\,\mathrm{km}$?

- $90.9\%$
- $82.6\%$
- $68.2\%$
- $17.4\%$

## Explanation

The ratio of the field at altitude $h$ to the surface value is
$$
\frac{g}{g_0} = \left(\frac{R_E}{R_E + h}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{6.37}{7.01}\right)^2 = 0.826 ,
$$
that is $82.6\%$. The field decreases slowly, since the altitude is only a tenth of the radius.
