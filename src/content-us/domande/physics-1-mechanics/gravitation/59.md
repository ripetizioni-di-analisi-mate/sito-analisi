A body of mass $m$ moves in the gravitational field of a fixed mass $M$ with a negative mechanical energy $E$. Which statement is correct?

- Its distance from $M$ never exceeds $-\dfrac{GMm}{E}$
- It reaches infinite distance with zero speed
- It necessarily travels a circular orbit
- Its distance from $M$ never exceeds $-\dfrac{E}{GMm}$

## Explanation

With $E < 0$ the motion is bound. The body stops moving outward where its kinetic energy is zero, that is where $-\dfrac{GMm}{r} = E$, which gives $r_{\max} = -\dfrac{GMm}{E}$, a positive distance because $E$ is negative. Circular orbits are only one case of bound motion.
