The radius of the circular orbit of a satellite is multiplied by $4$. By what factor is its period multiplied?

- $4$
- $16$
- $2$
- $8$

## Explanation

The period is $T = 2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{r^3}{GM}}$, proportional to $r^{\frac{3}{2}}$. Multiplying $r$ by $4$ multiplies $T$ by $4^{\frac{3}{2}} = 8$.
