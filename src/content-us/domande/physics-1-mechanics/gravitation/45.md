A payload of mass $1000\,\mathrm{kg}$ is raised from the surface of the Earth to the altitude $h = R_E$. With $GM_E = 3.984 \times 10^{14}\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$ and $R_E = 6.37 \times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the change in its gravitational potential energy?

- $+6.25 \times 10^{10}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-3.13 \times 10^{10}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $+3.13 \times 10^{10}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $+1.56 \times 10^{10}\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The payload goes from $r = R_E$ to $r = 2R_E$, hence
$$
\Delta U = -\frac{GM_Em}{2R_E} + \frac{GM_Em}{R_E} = \frac{GM_Em}{2R_E} = \frac{3.984 \times 10^{14} \cdot 1000}{2 \cdot 6.37 \times 10^{6}}\,\mathrm{J} = 3.13 \times 10^{10}\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
The formula $mgh$ would give twice this value, because $g$ is not constant over such an altitude.
