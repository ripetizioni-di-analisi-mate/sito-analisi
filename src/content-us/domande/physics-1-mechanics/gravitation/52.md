A probe moves away from a planet, from the distance $r_A$ to the larger distance $r_B$. What is the sign of the work done on it by the gravitational force of the planet?

- Positive
- Zero, because the force is conservative
- Negative
- It depends on the path followed

## Explanation

The work of the gravitational force is $W = \dfrac{GMm}{r_B} - \dfrac{GMm}{r_A}$, which is negative when $r_B > r_A$. The force points towards the planet while the probe moves away from it. Being conservative, the force does work that depends only on the endpoints, not work equal to zero.
