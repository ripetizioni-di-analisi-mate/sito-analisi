A satellite of mass $m$ is moved from the circular orbit of radius $r$ to the circular orbit of radius $2r$ around a fixed mass $M$. How much work must the engines do?

- $\dfrac{GMm}{2r}$
- $-\dfrac{GMm}{4r}$
- $\dfrac{3GMm}{4r}$
- $\dfrac{GMm}{4r}$

## Explanation

The work of the engines equals the change in mechanical energy, with $E = -\dfrac{GMm}{2r}$ on a circular orbit:
$$
W = -\frac{GMm}{4r} - \left(-\frac{GMm}{2r}\right) = \frac{GMm}{4r} .
$$
The change in potential energy alone would be $\dfrac{GMm}{2r}$, but the kinetic energy decreases by $\dfrac{GMm}{4r}$.
