Two satellites, of masses $m$ and $3m$, travel the same circular orbit around the Earth. What is the ratio of the speed of the heavier satellite to that of the lighter one?

- $\sqrt{3}$
- $1$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

On a circular orbit $\dfrac{GMm}{r^2} = m\,\dfrac{v^2}{r}$, and the mass of the satellite cancels, giving $v = \sqrt{\dfrac{GM}{r}}$. The speed depends on the radius alone, so the two satellites have the same speed.
