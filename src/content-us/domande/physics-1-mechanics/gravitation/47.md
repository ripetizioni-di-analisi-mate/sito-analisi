With $GM_E = 3.984 \times 10^{14}\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$ and $R_E = 6.37 \times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{m}$, and with zero potential at infinite distance, what is the gravitational potential at the surface of the Earth?

- $+6.25 \times 10^{7}\,\mathrm{J/kg}$
- $-6.25 \times 10^{7}\,\mathrm{J/kg}$
- $-9.82\,\mathrm{J/kg}$
- $-6.25 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{J/kg}$

## Explanation

The gravitational potential of a body with spherical symmetry at the distance $r$ from its center is $V = -\dfrac{GM}{r}$, hence
$$
V = -\frac{3.984 \times 10^{14}}{6.37 \times 10^{6}}\,\mathrm{J/kg} = -6.25 \times 10^{7}\,\mathrm{J/kg} .
$$
