Planet B has the same uniform density as planet A and twice its radius. If the surface gravity of A is $g_0$, what is the surface gravity of B?

- $2g_0$
- $\dfrac{g_0}{4}$
- $8g_0$
- $\dfrac{g_0}{2}$

## Explanation

With uniform density $\rho$ the mass is $M = \rho\,\dfrac{4}{3}\pi R^3$, hence
$$
g_0 = \frac{GM}{R^2} = \frac{4}{3}\pi G\rho R .
$$
At fixed density the surface gravity is proportional to $R$, therefore doubling the radius doubles it: the mass grows by $8$ and $R^2$ by $4$.
