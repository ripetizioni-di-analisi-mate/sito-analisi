A body of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ and a body of mass $6.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ are released from rest at the same point of a gravitational field, and no other force acts on them. What is the ratio of the acceleration of the heavier body to that of the lighter one?

- $3$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $9$
- $1$

## Explanation

The force on each body is $\vec{F} = m\vec{g}$, and Newton's second law gives $\vec{a} = \dfrac{\vec{F}}{m} = \vec{g}$. The acceleration equals the field at that point, independently of the mass, hence the ratio is $1$.
