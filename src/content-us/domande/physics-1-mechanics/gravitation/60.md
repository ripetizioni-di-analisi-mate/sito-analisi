A body is launched vertically from the surface of a planet of mass $M$ and radius $R$ with the speed $v_0 = \sqrt{\dfrac{GM}{R}}$. Neglecting air resistance, what is the maximum distance it reaches from the center of the planet?

- $1.5R$
- $R$
- $2R$
- It escapes to infinite distance

## Explanation

Conservation of energy between the surface and the highest point gives
$$
\frac{1}{2}\,\frac{GMm}{R} - \frac{GMm}{R} = -\frac{GMm}{r_{\max}}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
r_{\max} = 2R .
$$
The constant-field formula $h = \dfrac{v_0^2}{2g_0} = \dfrac{R}{2}$ underestimates the altitude, because the field decreases as the body rises.
