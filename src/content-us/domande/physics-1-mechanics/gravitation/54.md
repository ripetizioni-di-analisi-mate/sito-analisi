A probe of mass $1000\,\mathrm{kg}$ and a stone of mass $1\,\mathrm{kg}$ are launched from the surface of the same planet. How does the escape speed of the probe compare with that of the stone?

- It is $\sqrt{1000}$ times smaller
- It is $1000$ times smaller
- It is the same
- It is $\sqrt{1000}$ times larger

## Explanation

The escape speed is $v_f = \sqrt{\dfrac{2GM}{R}}$, where $M$ and $R$ belong to the planet. The mass of the launched body cancels in the energy balance, therefore the escape speed is the same for both.
