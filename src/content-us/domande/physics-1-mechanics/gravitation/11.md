A particle of mass $m_1$ is at the origin and a particle of mass $m_2$ is at $x = d$, with $d > 0$. What is the $x$ component of the gravitational force exerted by $m_2$ on $m_1$?

- $-G\,\dfrac{m_1m_2}{d^2}$
- $+G\,\dfrac{m_1m_2}{d}$
- $+G\,\dfrac{m_1m_2}{d^2}$
- $-G\,\dfrac{m_1m_2}{d}$

## Explanation

The force on $m_1$ points from $m_1$ towards $m_2$, that is, towards positive $x$. Its magnitude is $G\,\dfrac{m_1m_2}{d^2}$, therefore its $x$ component is positive and equal to that magnitude.
