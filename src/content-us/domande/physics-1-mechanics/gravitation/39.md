The Earth rotates once in $8.62 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{s}$ and has the equatorial radius $6.37 \times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{m}$. What centripetal acceleration does a body at rest on the ground at the equator need?

- $5.4 \times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $4.6 \times 10^{2}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2.2 \times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3.4 \times 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The angular speed is $\omega = \dfrac{2\pi}{T} = 7.29 \times 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{rad/s}$, hence
$$
a = \omega^2R_E = (7.29 \times 10^{-5})^2 \cdot 6.37 \times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 3.4 \times 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
This acceleration is supplied by the balance of weight and normal force, therefore a scale at the equator reads slightly less than the weight.
