The gravitational potential at a point is $-4.0 \times 10^{7}\,\mathrm{J/kg}$. What is the gravitational potential energy of a body of mass $50\,\mathrm{kg}$ placed at that point?

- $-8.0 \times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $+2.0 \times 10^{9}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-2.0 \times 10^{9}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-4.0 \times 10^{7}\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The potential is the potential energy per unit mass, so $U = mV$:
$$
U = 50 \cdot (-4.0 \times 10^{7})\,\mathrm{J} = -2.0 \times 10^{9}\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
