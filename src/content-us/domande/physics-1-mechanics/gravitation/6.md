Two uniform spheres, each of mass $10\,\mathrm{kg}$ and radius $0.10\,\mathrm{m}$, touch each other. With $G = 6.674 \times 10^{-11}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m^2/kg^2}$, what is the magnitude of the gravitational force between them?

- $6.7 \times 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $3.3 \times 10^{-8}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $6.7 \times 10^{-9}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1.7 \times 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

By the shell theorem each sphere acts as a particle at its center, and the centers of two touching spheres are $r = 0.20\,\mathrm{m}$ apart, twice the radius. Hence
$$
F = G\,\frac{m_1m_2}{r^2} = 6.674 \times 10^{-11} \cdot \frac{10 \cdot 10}{(0.20)^2}\,\mathrm{N} = 1.7 \times 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
