The center of the Moon is about $60$ Earth radii from the center of the Earth. By the inverse-square law, what fraction of the surface gravity $g_0$ of the Earth is the acceleration of the Moon towards the Earth?

- $\dfrac{1}{60}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3600}$
- $\dfrac{1}{120}$
- $\dfrac{1}{216\,000}$

## Explanation

The field of the Earth decreases as $\dfrac{1}{r^2}$, with $r$ measured from the center. At $r = 60R_E$ it is therefore
$$
\frac{g}{g_0} = \left(\frac{R_E}{60R_E}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{3600} ,
$$
which agrees with the centripetal acceleration of the Moon computed from its period.
