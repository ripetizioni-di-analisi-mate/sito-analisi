Two particles of mass $M$ each are held at the distance $2d$, and $P$ is the midpoint of the segment joining them. With zero potential at infinite distance, what is the gravitational potential at $P$?

- $-\dfrac{2GM}{d}$
- $0$
- $-\dfrac{GM}{d}$
- $-\dfrac{2GM}{d^2}$

## Explanation

The potential is a scalar, so the potentials of the two particles add as numbers. Each particle is at the distance $d$ from $P$ and contributes $-\dfrac{GM}{d}$, hence $V = -\dfrac{2GM}{d}$. The field at $P$ is zero, because the two fields are vectors with opposite senses, while the potential is not.
