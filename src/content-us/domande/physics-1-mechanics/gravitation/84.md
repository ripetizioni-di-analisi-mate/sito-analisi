A planet of mass $m$ has the angular momentum of magnitude $L$ about the Sun. At what rate does the segment from the Sun to the planet sweep area?

- $\dfrac{L}{2m}$
- $\dfrac{L}{m}$
- $\dfrac{2L}{m}$
- $\dfrac{mL}{2}$

## Explanation

In the time $dt$ the segment sweeps the triangle of sides $\vec{r}$ and $\vec{v}\,dt$, of area $dA = \dfrac{1}{2}|\vec{r} \times \vec{v}|\,dt$. Since $L = m|\vec{r} \times \vec{v}|$, the rate is $\dfrac{dA}{dt} = \dfrac{L}{2m}$. The factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$ comes from the area of a triangle being half that of the parallelogram.
