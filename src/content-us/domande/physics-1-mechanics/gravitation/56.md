A planet has surface gravity $g_0 = 24.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and radius $R = 6.99 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{km}$. Neglecting its rotation and atmosphere, what is the escape speed from its surface?

- $58.9\,\mathrm{km/s}$
- $41.6\,\mathrm{km/s}$
- $83.3\,\mathrm{km/s}$
- $1.86\,\mathrm{km/s}$

## Explanation

With the radius in meters, $R = 6.99 \times 10^{7}\,\mathrm{m}$, the escape speed is
$$
v_f = \sqrt{2g_0R} = \sqrt{2 \cdot 24.8 \cdot 6.99 \times 10^{7}}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 5.89 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 58.9\,\mathrm{km/s} .
$$
