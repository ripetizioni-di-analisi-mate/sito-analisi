A planet with spherical symmetry has mass $M = 4.87 \times 10^{24}\,\mathrm{kg}$ and radius $R = 6.05 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{km}$. With $G = 6.674 \times 10^{-11}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m^2/kg^2}$, what is the gravitational field at its surface?

- $2.22\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $8.88 \times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $5.37 \times 10^{7}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $8.88\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The radius in meters is $R = 6.05 \times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{m}$. The surface gravity is
$$
g_0 = \frac{GM}{R^2} = \frac{6.674 \times 10^{-11} \cdot 4.87 \times 10^{24}}{(6.05 \times 10^{6})^2}\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 8.88\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
