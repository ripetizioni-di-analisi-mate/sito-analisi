How does the gravitational potential energy $U(r) = -\dfrac{GMm}{r}$ of two masses change as their distance $r$ grows from a finite value to very large values?

- It decreases and tends to zero
- It increases and tends to zero
- It increases without bound
- It decreases without bound

## Explanation

The function $-\dfrac{GMm}{r}$ is negative and its absolute value $\dfrac{GMm}{r}$ decreases as $r$ grows. The potential energy therefore increases toward zero, which is its value at infinite distance.
