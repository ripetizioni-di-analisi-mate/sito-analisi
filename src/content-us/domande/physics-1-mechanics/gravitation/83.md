Kepler's second law follows from a conservation law. Which one, and why does it hold?

- Mechanical energy is conserved, because gravitation is conservative
- Linear momentum of the planet is conserved, because the Sun is fixed
- Angular momentum is conserved, because the force decreases as $\dfrac{1}{r^2}$
- Angular momentum about the Sun is conserved, because the force on the planet is directed along $\vec{r}$

## Explanation

The rate at which area is swept is $\dfrac{dA}{dt} = \dfrac{|\vec{L}|}{2m}$. Since the gravitational force is parallel to $\vec{r}$, the torque $\vec{r} \times \vec{F}$ is zero and $\vec{L}$ is constant. Any force directed along $\vec{r}$ would give the same result, so the inverse-square form is not what the law requires.
