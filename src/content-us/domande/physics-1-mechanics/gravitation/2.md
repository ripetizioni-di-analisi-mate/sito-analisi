Two particles attract each other with a force of magnitude $F_0$. By what factor must their distance be multiplied, with the masses unchanged, so that the force becomes $\dfrac{F_0}{4}$?

- $2$
- $4$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $16$

## Explanation

The force is proportional to $\dfrac{1}{r^2}$. A force four times smaller requires $r^2$ four times larger, therefore the distance must be multiplied by $\sqrt{4} = 2$.
