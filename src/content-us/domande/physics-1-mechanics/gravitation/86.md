A minor planet orbits the Sun with a semi-major axis of $9.0\,\mathrm{AU}$. What is its period?

- $81\,\mathrm{yr}$
- $27\,\mathrm{yr}$
- $4.3\,\mathrm{yr}$
- $729\,\mathrm{yr}$

## Explanation

For bodies orbiting the Sun, with $T$ in years and $a$ in astronomical units, Kepler's third law reads $T^2 = a^3$. Hence
$$
T = a^{\frac{3}{2}} = 9.0^{\frac{3}{2}}\,\mathrm{yr} = 27\,\mathrm{yr} .
$$
