A satellite on a circular orbit has the kinetic energy $4.0 \times 10^{10}\,\mathrm{J}$, with the potential energy zero at infinite distance. What is its mechanical energy?

- $-8.0 \times 10^{10}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $+4.0 \times 10^{10}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-2.0 \times 10^{10}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-4.0 \times 10^{10}\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

On a circular orbit $K = \dfrac{GMm}{2r}$ and $U = -\dfrac{GMm}{r} = -2K$, therefore
$$
E = K + U = K - 2K = -K = -4.0 \times 10^{10}\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
