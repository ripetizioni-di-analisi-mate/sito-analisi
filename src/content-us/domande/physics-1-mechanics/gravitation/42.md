With the potential energy taken to be zero at infinite distance, what is the sign of the gravitational potential energy of two masses at a finite distance?

- Always positive
- Always negative
- Zero
- Positive or negative, depending on the two masses

## Explanation

The potential energy of two masses is $U(r) = -\dfrac{GMm}{r}$. The product $GMm$ and the distance $r$ are positive, therefore $U$ is negative at every finite distance and tends to zero as $r$ grows.
