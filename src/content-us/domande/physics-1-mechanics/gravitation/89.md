With $GM_S = 1.328 \times 10^{20}\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$ for the Sun, what is the value of $\dfrac{T^2}{a^3}$ for the planets of the solar system, in SI units?

- $7.43 \times 10^{-20}\,\mathrm{s^2/m^3}$
- $4.73 \times 10^{-20}\,\mathrm{s^2/m^3}$
- $2.97 \times 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{s^2/m^3}$
- $3.36 \times 10^{18}\,\mathrm{s^2/m^3}$

## Explanation

By Kepler's third law
$$
\frac{T^2}{a^3} = \frac{4\pi^2}{GM_S} = \frac{39.48}{1.328 \times 10^{20}}\,\mathrm{s^2/m^3} = 2.97 \times 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{s^2/m^3} .
$$
