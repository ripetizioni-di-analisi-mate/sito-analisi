A body of mass $m$ is at rest at the distance $r$ from a fixed mass $M$. How much work must an external agent do to take it to infinite distance, with the body at rest at the end?

- $-\dfrac{GMm}{r}$
- $+\dfrac{GMm}{r^2}$
- $+\dfrac{GMm}{r}$
- $0$

## Explanation

The kinetic energy is zero at both ends, so the work of the external agent equals the change in potential energy:
$$
W = U(\infty) - U(r) = 0 - \left(-\frac{GMm}{r}\right) = +\frac{GMm}{r} .
$$
The work is positive, because the agent must pull against the attraction.
