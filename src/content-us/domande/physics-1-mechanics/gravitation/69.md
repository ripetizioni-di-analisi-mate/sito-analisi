A satellite travels a circular orbit around the Earth with the speed $5.00\,\mathrm{km/s}$. With $GM_E = 3.984 \times 10^{14}\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$, what is the radius of its orbit?

- $1.59 \times 10^{7}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3.19 \times 10^{7}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $7.97 \times 10^{10}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.59 \times 10^{13}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

From $v^2 = \dfrac{GM_E}{r}$, with $v = 5.00 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{m/s}$,
$$
r = \frac{GM_E}{v^2} = \frac{3.984 \times 10^{14}}{(5.00 \times 10^{3})^2}\,\mathrm{m} = 1.59 \times 10^{7}\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
