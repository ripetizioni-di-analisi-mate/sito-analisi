A uniform solid sphere has surface gravity $g_0$ and radius $R$. What is the gravitational field inside it, at the distance $\dfrac{R}{2}$ from its center?

- $4g_0$
- $\dfrac{g_0}{4}$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{g_0}{2}$

## Explanation

Inside a uniform sphere the field is $g(r) = \dfrac{GM}{R^3}\,r = g_0\,\dfrac{r}{R}$, proportional to the distance from the center. At $r = \dfrac{R}{2}$ it is therefore $\dfrac{g_0}{2}$. The outer shells contribute nothing, which is why the formula $\dfrac{GM}{r^2}$ does not apply here.
