An apple of mass $0.20\,\mathrm{kg}$ hangs from a tree. Which statement describes the gravitational force that the apple exerts on the Earth?

- It is zero, because the Earth does not move
- It has the same magnitude as the force of the Earth on the apple and the opposite sense
- It is smaller than the force of the Earth on the apple by the ratio of the two masses
- It is larger than the force of the Earth on the apple, because the Earth has the larger mass

## Explanation

The two gravitational forces form an action-reaction pair, therefore they have equal magnitudes and opposite senses. The ratio of the masses affects the two accelerations, not the two forces.
