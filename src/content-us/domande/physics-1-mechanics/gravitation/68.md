A satellite travels a circular orbit of radius $r = 1.00 \times 10^{7}\,\mathrm{m}$ around the Earth, with $GM_E = 3.984 \times 10^{14}\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$. What is its speed?

- $8.93\,\mathrm{km/s}$
- $4.46\,\mathrm{km/s}$
- $6.31\,\mathrm{km/s}$
- $3.98 \times 10^{4}\,\mathrm{km/s}$

## Explanation

The speed of a circular orbit is
$$
v = \sqrt{\frac{GM_E}{r}} = \sqrt{\frac{3.984 \times 10^{14}}{1.00 \times 10^{7}}}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 6.31 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 6.31\,\mathrm{km/s} .
$$
