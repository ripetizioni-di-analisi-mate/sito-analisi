The radius of the circular orbit of a satellite is multiplied by $4$. By what factor is its orbital speed multiplied?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $2$
- $\dfrac{1}{16}$

## Explanation

The orbital speed is $v = \sqrt{\dfrac{GM}{r}}$, proportional to $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{r}}$. Multiplying $r$ by $4$ multiplies $v$ by $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{4}} = \dfrac{1}{2}$.
