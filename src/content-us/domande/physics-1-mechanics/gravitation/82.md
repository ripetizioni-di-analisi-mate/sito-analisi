A comet has its perihelion at $1.0\,\mathrm{AU}$ and its aphelion at $3.0\,\mathrm{AU}$ from the Sun. Its speed at perihelion is $30\,\mathrm{km/s}$. What is its speed at aphelion?

- $90\,\mathrm{km/s}$
- $3.3\,\mathrm{km/s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{km/s}$
- $17\,\mathrm{km/s}$

## Explanation

At perihelion and aphelion the velocity is perpendicular to $\vec{r}$, and conservation of angular momentum gives $r_pv_p = r_av_a$. Hence
$$
v_a = v_p\,\frac{r_p}{r_a} = 30 \cdot \frac{1.0}{3.0}\,\mathrm{km/s} = 10\,\mathrm{km/s} .
$$
