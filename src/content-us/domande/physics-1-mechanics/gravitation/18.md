Two particles of masses $M$ and $3M$ are held at the distance $d$. What is the resultant gravitational field at the midpoint of the segment joining them?

- $\dfrac{16GM}{d^2}$, directed towards $3M$
- $\dfrac{8GM}{d^2}$, directed towards $3M$
- $\dfrac{8GM}{d^2}$, directed towards $M$
- $\dfrac{2GM}{d^2}$, directed towards $3M$

## Explanation

The midpoint is at the distance $\dfrac{d}{2}$ from both particles, so the two fields have magnitudes $\dfrac{4GM}{d^2}$ and $\dfrac{12GM}{d^2}$. They point in opposite senses, each towards its own source, hence the resultant has magnitude $\dfrac{12GM}{d^2} - \dfrac{4GM}{d^2} = \dfrac{8GM}{d^2}$ and points towards the larger mass $3M$.
