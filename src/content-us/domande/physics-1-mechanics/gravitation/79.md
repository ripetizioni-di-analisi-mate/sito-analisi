A moon travels a circular orbit of radius $4.00 \times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{m}$ around a planet with $GM = 4.28 \times 10^{13}\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$. What is its period?

- $1.22 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $9.40 \times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $7.68 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3.84\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The period of a circular orbit is
$$
T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{r^3}{GM}} = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{(4.00 \times 10^{6})^3}{4.28 \times 10^{13}}}\,\mathrm{s} = 2\pi \cdot 1.22 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{s} = 7.68 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{s} .
$$
