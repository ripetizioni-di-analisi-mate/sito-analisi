A satellite is moved from a circular orbit to a circular orbit of larger radius around the same planet. How do its kinetic energy $K$, potential energy $U$ and mechanical energy $E$ change?

- $K$, $U$ and $E$ all increase
- $K$ decreases, while $U$ and $E$ increase
- $K$ increases, while $U$ and $E$ decrease
- $K$ and $U$ decrease, while $E$ does not change

## Explanation

On a circular orbit $K = \dfrac{GMm}{2r}$, $U = -\dfrac{GMm}{r}$ and $E = -\dfrac{GMm}{2r}$. A larger $r$ makes $K$ smaller, while $U$ and $E$, both negative, move toward zero and therefore increase. The increase of $U$ is twice the decrease of $K$.
