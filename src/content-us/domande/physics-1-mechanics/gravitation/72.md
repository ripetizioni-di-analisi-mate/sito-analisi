An astronaut of mass $70.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ is in a station in circular orbit, at an altitude where the gravitational field is $8.69\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the magnitude of the gravitational force of the Earth on the astronaut?

- $608\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $687\,\mathrm{N}$
- $70.0\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The gravitational force at a point is $F = mg$, with $g$ the field at that point:
$$
F = 70.0 \cdot 8.69\,\mathrm{N} = 608\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The scale in the station reads zero, but the attraction of the Earth is far from zero and supplies the centripetal acceleration of the orbit.
