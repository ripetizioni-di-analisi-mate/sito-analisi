For two given masses $m_1$ and $m_2$, the magnitude $F$ of their gravitational force is plotted against $\dfrac{1}{r^2}$, where $r$ is their distance. What does the graph look like?

- A straight line through the origin with slope $G$
- A straight line through the origin with slope $Gm_1m_2$
- A hyperbola that decreases as $\dfrac{1}{r^2}$ grows
- A parabola with its vertex at the origin

## Explanation

The law of universal gravitation gives $F = Gm_1m_2 \cdot \dfrac{1}{r^2}$. With $\dfrac{1}{r^2}$ on the horizontal axis, $F$ is a direct proportion, hence the graph is a straight line through the origin whose slope is the constant factor $Gm_1m_2$.
