A satellite is at the altitude $h$ above the surface of a planet of radius $R$ with spherical symmetry. In the formula $F = \dfrac{GMm}{r^2}$, what is $r$?

- $h$
- $R$
- $R + h$
- $R - h$

## Explanation

By the shell theorem the planet acts as a particle placed at its center. The distance in the law of universal gravitation is therefore measured from center to center, and a point at altitude $h$ is at $r = R + h$ from the center.
