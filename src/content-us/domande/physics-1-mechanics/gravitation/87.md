A moon of planet X travels a circular orbit with the same radius as the orbit of our Moon around the Earth, but with half the period. What is the mass of planet X?

- $2M_E$
- $\dfrac{M_E}{4}$
- $4M_E$
- $\dfrac{M_E}{2}$

## Explanation

From Kepler's third law $M = \dfrac{4\pi^2r^3}{GT^2}$. With the same $r$, the mass is proportional to $\dfrac{1}{T^2}$, therefore half the period means four times the mass of the Earth.
