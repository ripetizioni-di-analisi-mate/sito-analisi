Four particles of equal mass $M$ occupy the vertices of a square of side $a$. What is the magnitude of the resultant gravitational field at the center of the square?

- $\dfrac{8GM}{a^2}$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{2GM}{a^2}$
- $\dfrac{4GM}{a^2}$

## Explanation

Each particle is at the distance $\dfrac{a}{\sqrt{2}}$ from the center, so the four fields have the same magnitude $\dfrac{2GM}{a^2}$. The fields of opposite vertices have opposite senses, therefore by superposition they cancel in pairs and the resultant field is zero.
