Two masses at the distance $r$ have the gravitational potential energy $U_0$, with $U = 0$ at infinite distance. What is their potential energy at the distance $2r$?

- $\dfrac{U_0}{4}$
- $2U_0$
- $-\dfrac{U_0}{2}$
- $\dfrac{U_0}{2}$

## Explanation

The potential energy $U = -\dfrac{GMm}{r}$ is inversely proportional to $r$, not to $r^2$. Doubling the distance halves it, so the new value is $\dfrac{U_0}{2}$, which is negative and closer to zero than $U_0$.
