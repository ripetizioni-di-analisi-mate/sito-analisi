Two satellites of equal mass orbit the same planet. One travels a circle of radius $R$; the other travels an ellipse with closest distance $\dfrac{R}{2}$ and farthest distance $\dfrac{3R}{2}$ from the center of the planet. Which one has the greater mechanical energy?

- The satellite on the ellipse
- They have the same mechanical energy
- The satellite on the circle
- It depends on the position of the satellite along the ellipse

## Explanation

The semi-major axis of the ellipse is $a = \dfrac{1}{2}\left(\dfrac{R}{2} + \dfrac{3R}{2}\right) = R$. The mechanical energy of an orbit is $E = -\dfrac{GMm}{2a}$, which depends only on the semi-major axis, therefore the two energies are equal. The mechanical energy is conserved, so it does not change along the ellipse.
