Three groups with identifiers in $A=\{11;12;13\}$ must each receive one of the time slots in $B=\{1;2;3\}$. Several groups may receive the same slot. Which relation is a function from $A$ to $B$?

- $\{(11;1),(12;2),(13;2)\}$
- $\{(11;1),(11;2),(12;2),(13;3)\}$
- $\{(11;1),(12;2)\}$
- $\{(11;1),(12;2),(13;4)\}$

:::soluzione
$\{(11;1),(12;2),(13;2)\}$
:::
