The domain is $A=\left\{11, 12, 13\right\}$ and the codomain is $B=\{1;2;3\}$. Which relation, written as a set of ordered pairs, is a function from $A$ to $B$?

- $\{(11;1),(12;2),(13;2)\}$
- $\{(11;1),(11;2),(12;2),(13;3)\}$
- $\{(11;1),(12;2)\}$
- $\{(11;1),(12;2),(13;4)\}$

:::soluzione
$\{(11;1),(12;2),(13;2)\}$
:::
