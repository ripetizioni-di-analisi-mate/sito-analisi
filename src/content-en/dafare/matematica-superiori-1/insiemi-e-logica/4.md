The compartments on a board are numbered with $U=\{11;12;13;14;15;16;17;18\}$. Those in $A=\{11;12;13;17;18\}$ are already occupied. What is the complement of $A$ in $U$?

:::soluzione
$\left\{14, 15, 16\right\}$
:::
