Let $D=\{1;2;3;4;5;6\}$ and let $C$ be the set of elements of $D$ divisible by $3$. Which relation is true?

- $D\subseteq C$
- $C=D$
- $C\subsetneq D$
- $C\cap D=\varnothing$

:::soluzione
$C\subsetneq D$
:::
