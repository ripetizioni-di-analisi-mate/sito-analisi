A transversal forms a pair of alternate interior angles of $71^\circ$ with two lines. Which conclusion is justified by the converse angle test?

- The lines must be perpendicular
- The two lines must coincide
- The transversal is parallel to both lines
- The two lines are parallel

:::soluzione
The two lines are parallel
:::
