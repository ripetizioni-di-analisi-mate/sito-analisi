The diagonals of parallelogram $ABCD$ meet at $O$. If $AO=7\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $BD=20\,\mathrm{cm}$, find $AC$ and $BO$.

:::soluzione
$AC=14\,\mathrm{cm}$, $BO=10\,\mathrm{cm}$
:::
