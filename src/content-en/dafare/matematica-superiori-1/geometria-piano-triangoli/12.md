An isosceles triangle has perimeter $45\,\mathrm{cm}$. The base is $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ longer than each equal side. Find the lengths and compare the sum of the equal sides with the base.

:::soluzione
$14\,\mathrm{cm}$, $14\,\mathrm{cm}$, $17\,\mathrm{cm}$
:::
