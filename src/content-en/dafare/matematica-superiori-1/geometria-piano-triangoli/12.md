An isosceles triangle has perimeter $45\,\mathrm{cm}$. Its base is $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ longer than an equal side. Find the three side lengths.

:::soluzione
$14\,\mathrm{cm}$, $14\,\mathrm{cm}$, $17\,\mathrm{cm}$
:::
