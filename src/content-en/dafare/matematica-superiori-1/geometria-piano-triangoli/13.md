In triangles $ABC$ and $PQR$, $AB=PQ$, $AC=PR$, and $\widehat A=\widehat P$. Identify the congruence criterion and match the endpoints of side $BC$ to find its counterpart.

:::soluzione
The SAS criterion. The corresponding side is $QR$.
:::
