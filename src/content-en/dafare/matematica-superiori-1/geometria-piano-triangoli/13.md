In triangles $ABC$ and $PQR$, $AB=PQ$, $AC=PR$, and $\widehat A=\widehat P$. State the criterion that gives congruence and the side that corresponds to $BC$.

:::soluzione
The SAS criterion. The corresponding side is $QR$.
:::
