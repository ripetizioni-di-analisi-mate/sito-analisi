For $x\ne11$, reduce $\dfrac{1+\dfrac{x-11}{11}}{\dfrac{x-11}{11}}$ to a single fraction. Write the numerator as one fraction before dividing.

:::soluzione
$\dfrac{x}{x - 11}$
:::
