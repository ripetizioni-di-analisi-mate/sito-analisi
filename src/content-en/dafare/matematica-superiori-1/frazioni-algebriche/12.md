The fraction $\dfrac{x-11}{x-11}$ simplifies to $1$. Which statement correctly preserves its domain?

- It equals $1$ for every real number, including $x=11$
- It equals $0$ when $x=11$
- It is defined only for $x>11$
- It equals $1$ for every $x\ne 11$

:::soluzione
It equals $1$ for every $x\ne 11$
:::
