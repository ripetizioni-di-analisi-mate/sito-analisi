For $x\ne-11,11$, write the difference $\dfrac2{x-11}-\dfrac{2x}{x^2-121}$ as a single quotient. Once the denominators match, subtract the entire second numerator.

:::soluzione
$\dfrac{22}{x^{2} - 121}$
:::
