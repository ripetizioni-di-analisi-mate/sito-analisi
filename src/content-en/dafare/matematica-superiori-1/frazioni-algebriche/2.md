Which values make the denominator in $\dfrac{x+2}{(x-11)^2(x+11)}$ zero? Consider each of the two linear factors separately.

- $x=-2\;\text{or}\;x=11$
- $x=-11\;\text{or}\;x=11$
- $x=11$
- $x=-11$

:::soluzione
$x=-11\;\text{or}\;x=11$
:::
