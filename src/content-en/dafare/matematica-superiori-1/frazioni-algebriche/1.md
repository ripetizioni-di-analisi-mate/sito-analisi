Find the conditions under which $\dfrac{(x+2)^2}{(x-11)^2}$ is defined. Check whether the multiplicity of a factor changes the inputs that make the denominator zero.

- $x\ne 11$
- $x\ne -1$
- $x>11$
- $x\ne -11$

:::soluzione
$x\ne 11$
:::
