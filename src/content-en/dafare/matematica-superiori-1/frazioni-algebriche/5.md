For $x\ne-1,11$, add $\dfrac{2}{(x-11)(x+1)}+\dfrac{5x+3}{(x-11)(x+1)}$ and reduce the fraction. The denominators already match, so try factoring the sum of the numerators.

:::soluzione
$\dfrac{5}{x - 11}$
:::
