Light with vacuum wavelength $\lambda = 480\,\mathrm{nm}$ propagates in glass with $n = 1{,}50$. Compute the speed, the frequency and the wavelength of the light in the glass.

:::soluzione
$v = 2{,}00 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$, $\nu = 6{,}25 \cdot 10^{14}\,\mathrm{Hz}$, $\lambda_n = 320\,\mathrm{nm}$.
:::
