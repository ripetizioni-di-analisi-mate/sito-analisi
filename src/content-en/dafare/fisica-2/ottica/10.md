---
titolo: Second diffraction minimum
teoria: [minimi-di-diffrazione]
parole: [diffraction, single slit, minimum]
---

A slit $0.050\,\mathrm{mm}$ wide is illuminated perpendicularly with light of wavelength $600\,\mathrm{nm}$. Compute the angle at which the second diffraction minimum is observed and its distance from the center on a screen at $1.0\,\mathrm{m}$.

:::soluzione
$\sin\theta_2 = \dfrac{2\lambda}{a} = 0.024$, $\theta_2 \approx 1.4^\circ$; $y_2 \approx 2.4\,\mathrm{cm}$.
:::
