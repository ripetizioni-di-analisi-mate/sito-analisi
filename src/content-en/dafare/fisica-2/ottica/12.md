Vertically polarized light, of intensity $50\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$, passes through a polarizer with its axis at $60^\circ$ from the vertical and then through a second polarizer with a horizontal axis. Compute the final intensity.

:::soluzione
$I = 50 \cdot \cos^2 60^\circ \cdot \cos^2 30^\circ\,\mathrm{W/m^2} \approx 9.4\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.
:::
