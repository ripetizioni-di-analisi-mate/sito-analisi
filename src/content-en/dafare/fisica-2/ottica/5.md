An object is placed at a distance of $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ from a concave mirror with focal length $f = 15\,\mathrm{cm}$. Determine the position of the image and the magnification, and state whether the image is real or virtual.

:::soluzione
$q = -30\,\mathrm{cm}$, $m = 3$: virtual image, behind the mirror, upright and magnified three times.
:::
