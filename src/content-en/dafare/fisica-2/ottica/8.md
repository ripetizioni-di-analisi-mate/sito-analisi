An object $4.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ tall is placed at $20\,\mathrm{cm}$ from a diverging lens with $f = -20\,\mathrm{cm}$. Determine the position and the height of the image.

:::soluzione
$q = -10\,\mathrm{cm}$, $m = \dfrac{1}{2}$, $y' = 2.0\,\mathrm{cm}$: virtual and upright image, on the same side as the object.
:::
