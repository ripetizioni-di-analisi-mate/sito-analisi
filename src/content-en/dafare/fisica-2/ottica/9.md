In a Young double-slit experiment with light of wavelength $550\,\mathrm{nm}$ and a screen at $1{,}8\,\mathrm{m}$, the bright fringes are $3{,}3\,\mathrm{mm}$ apart from each other. Compute the distance between the slits.

:::soluzione
$d = \dfrac{\lambda L}{\Delta y} = 3{,}0 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m} = 0{,}30\,\mathrm{mm}$.
:::
