Unpolarized light is incident from air on a glass plate with $n = 1.50$. Compute the angle of incidence for which the reflected light is totally polarized and the corresponding angle of refraction.

:::soluzione
$\theta_B = \arctan 1.50 \approx 56.3^\circ$, $\theta_2 = 90^\circ - \theta_B \approx 33.7^\circ$.
:::
