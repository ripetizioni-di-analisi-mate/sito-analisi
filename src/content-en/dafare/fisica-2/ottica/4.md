titolo: Pesce in uno stagno
teoria: [profondita-apparente]
parole: [profondità apparente, rifrazione, acqua]
---

When viewed almost vertically from above through the surface of a pond ($n = 1{,}33$), a fish appears to be at a depth of $0{,}90\,\mathrm{m}$. Compute the actual depth of the fish.

:::soluzione
$h = \dfrac{n_1}{n_2}\, h' = 1{,}33 \cdot 0{,}90\,\mathrm{m} \approx 1{,}2\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
