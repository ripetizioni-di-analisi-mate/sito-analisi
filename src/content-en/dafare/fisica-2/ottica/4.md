---
titolo: Fish in a pond
teoria: [profondita-apparente]
parole: [apparent depth, refraction, water]
---

Looking almost vertically from above at the surface of a pond ($n = 1.33$), a fish appears to be at a depth of $0.90\,\mathrm{m}$. Compute the actual depth of the fish.

:::soluzione
$h = \dfrac{n_1}{n_2}\, h' = 1.33 \cdot 0.90\,\mathrm{m} \approx 1.2\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
