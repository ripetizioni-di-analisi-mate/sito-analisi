An object and a screen are $90\,\mathrm{cm}$ apart. A converging lens with $f = 20\,\mathrm{cm}$ is moved between the two. At which positions of the lens is a sharp image of the object formed on the screen? Compute the magnification in each position.

:::soluzione
$p = 30\,\mathrm{cm}$, with $q = 60\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $m = -2$, or $p = 60\,\mathrm{cm}$, with $q = 30\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $m = -\dfrac{1}{2}$.
:::
