A ray of light passes from air to glass with index of refraction $n = 1{,}55$, with angle of incidence $35^\circ$. Compute the angle of refraction.

:::soluzione
$\theta_2 = \arcsin\dfrac{\sin 35^\circ}{1{,}55} \approx 21{,}7^\circ$.
:::
