A convex mirror has a radius of curvature of magnitude $40\,\mathrm{cm}$. An object is $30\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the mirror. Determine the position of the image and the magnification.

:::soluzione
$f = -20\,\mathrm{cm}$, $q = -12\,\mathrm{cm}$, $m = 0.40$: virtual, upright and reduced image.
:::
