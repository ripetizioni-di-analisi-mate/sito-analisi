A grating with $600$ lines per millimeter, illuminated perpendicularly, gives the first-order maximum at $20{,}0^\circ$ from the direction of incidence. Compute the wavelength of the light and the maximum observable order.

:::soluzione
$\lambda = d \sin 20{,}0^\circ \approx 570\,\mathrm{nm}$; since $\dfrac{d}{\lambda} \approx 2{,}92$, the maximum order is $2$.
:::
