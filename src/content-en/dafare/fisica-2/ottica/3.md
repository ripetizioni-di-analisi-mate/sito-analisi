Diamond has index of refraction $n = 2{,}42$. Compute the critical angle for light that, from inside a diamond, strikes the interface with air.

:::soluzione
$\theta_c = \arcsin\dfrac{1}{2{,}42} \approx 24{,}4^\circ$.
:::
