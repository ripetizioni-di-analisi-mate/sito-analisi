---
titolo: Energy and energy density in a parallel-plate capacitor
teoria: [energia-condensatore, densita-energia, condensatore-piano]
parole: [energy, energy density, parallel-plate capacitor, uniform field]
---

A parallel-plate capacitor in vacuum has plates of area $A = 0.010\,\mathrm{m^2}$ at a distance $d = 0.50\,\mathrm{mm}$ and is charged to $\Delta V = 300\,\mathrm{V}$. Compute the stored energy and the energy density of the field between the plates.

:::soluzione
$U = \dfrac{1}{2} C\,\Delta V^2 \approx 8.0\,\mu\mathrm{J}$, $u = \dfrac{1}{2} \varepsilon_0 \left( \dfrac{\Delta V}{d} \right)^2 \approx 1.6\,\mathrm{J/m^3}$.
:::
