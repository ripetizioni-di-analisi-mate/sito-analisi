---
titolo: Equivalent capacitor with double voltage
teoria: [condensatori-in-serie, condensatori-in-parallelo]
parole: [series, parallel, maximum voltage, design]
---

Four identical capacitors are available, each of $10\,\mu\mathrm{F}$ with maximum voltage $100\,\mathrm{V}$. State how to connect them to obtain an equivalent capacitor of $10\,\mu\mathrm{F}$ that withstands $200\,\mathrm{V}$.

:::soluzione
Two branches in parallel, each made of two capacitors in series: each branch has $5.0\,\mu\mathrm{F}$ and withstands $200\,\mathrm{V}$, and the parallel combination of the two branches has $C_{\text{eq}} = 10\,\mu\mathrm{F}$.
:::
