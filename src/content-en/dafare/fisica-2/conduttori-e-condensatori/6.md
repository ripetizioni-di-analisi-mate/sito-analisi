The capacitors $C_1 = 6.0\,\mu\mathrm{F}$ and $C_2 = 3.0\,\mu\mathrm{F}$ are connected in series, and the series is connected in parallel with $C_3 = 4.0\,\mu\mathrm{F}$. The potential difference $\Delta V = 30\,\mathrm{V}$ is applied to the network, initially uncharged. Compute the equivalent capacitance, the charge of each capacitor and the potential differences across $C_1$ and $C_2$.

:::soluzione
$C_{\text{eq}} = 6.0\,\mu\mathrm{F}$; $Q_3 = 120\,\mu\mathrm{C}$, $Q_1 = Q_2 = 60\,\mu\mathrm{C}$; $\Delta V_1 = 10\,\mathrm{V}$, $\Delta V_2 = 20\,\mathrm{V}$.
:::
