---
titolo: Induced charges in a conducting shell
teoria: [cavita-nel-conduttore, carica-sulla-superficie, campo-interno-nullo]
parole: [conducting shell, cavity, induced charges, electrostatic shield]
---

A neutral conducting spherical shell has outer radius $10\,\mathrm{cm}$. In the cavity, at an arbitrary position, there is a point charge $q = 5.0\,\mathrm{nC}$. Compute the charge on the inner surface and on the outer surface of the shell, and the magnitude of the field at $20\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the center of the shell.

:::soluzione
$-5.0\,\mathrm{nC}$ on the inner surface, $+5.0\,\mathrm{nC}$ on the outer surface; $E = \dfrac{kq}{r^2} \approx 1.1 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{V/m}$.
:::
