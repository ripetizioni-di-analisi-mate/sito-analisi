---
titolo: Capacitance of a spherical capacitor
teoria: [condensatore-sferico]
parole: [spherical capacitor, capacitance, concentric plates]
---

A spherical capacitor consists of a conducting sphere of radius $R_1 = 10\,\mathrm{cm}$ and a concentric shell of inner radius $R_2 = 12\,\mathrm{cm}$, with vacuum in between. Compute the capacitance.

:::soluzione
$C = 4\pi\varepsilon_0\,\dfrac{R_1 R_2}{R_2 - R_1} \approx 67\,\mathrm{pF}$.
:::
