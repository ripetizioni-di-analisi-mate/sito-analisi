titolo: Field near the surface of a conductor

At a point of the surface of a conductor in equilibrium the surface charge density is $\sigma = 5.0\,\mu\mathrm{C/m^2}$. Compute the magnitude of the electric field at points just outside and state its direction.

:::soluzione
$E = \dfrac{\sigma}{\varepsilon_0} \approx 5.6 \cdot 10^{5}\,\mathrm{V/m}$, perpendicular to the surface and pointing out of the conductor.
:::
