titolo: Dielectric inserted in an isolated capacitor

A $20\,\mathrm{pF}$ capacitor in vacuum is charged to $500\,\mathrm{V}$ and disconnected from the source; then the space between the plates is filled with a dielectric of constant $\varepsilon_r = 5.0$. Compute the final potential difference and the energy stored before and after the insertion.

:::soluzione
$\Delta V = 100\,\mathrm{V}$; $U_0 = 2.5\,\mu\mathrm{J}$, $U = \dfrac{U_0}{\varepsilon_r} = 0.50\,\mu\mathrm{J}$.
:::
