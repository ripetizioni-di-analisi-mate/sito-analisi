A parallel-plate capacitor has plates of area $A = 0{,}020\,\mathrm{m^2}$ at a distance $d = 3{,}0\,\mathrm{mm}$. A glass slab of area $A$, thickness $s = 2{,}0\,\mathrm{mm}$ and relative permittivity $\varepsilon_r = 5{,}0$ is inserted between the plates, parallel to them. Compute the capacitance.

:::soluzione
$C = \dfrac{\varepsilon_0 A}{d - s + \dfrac{s}{\varepsilon_r}} \approx 1{,}26 \cdot 10^{-10}\,\mathrm{F} = 126\,\mathrm{pF}$.
:::
