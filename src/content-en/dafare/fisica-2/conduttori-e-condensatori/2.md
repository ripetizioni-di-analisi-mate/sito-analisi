A conducting sphere of radius $R = 15\,\mathrm{cm}$ is in air, whose dielectric strength is $3{,}0 \cdot 10^{6}\,\mathrm{V/m}$. Compute the maximum charge the sphere can carry without the surrounding air becoming conducting, and the corresponding potential relative to infinity.

:::soluzione
$Q_{\max} = \dfrac{R^2 E_{\max}}{k} \approx 7{,}5\,\mu\mathrm{C}$, $V_{\max} = E_{\max} R = 4{,}5 \cdot 10^{5}\,\mathrm{V}$.
:::
