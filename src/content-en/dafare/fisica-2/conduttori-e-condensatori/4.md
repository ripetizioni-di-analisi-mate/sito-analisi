---
titolo: Plate area of a parallel-plate capacitor
teoria: [condensatore-piano]
parole: [parallel-plate capacitor, area, capacitance]
---

Compute the plate area of a parallel-plate capacitor in vacuum that has capacitance $C = 100\,\mathrm{pF}$ with the plates at a distance $d = 0.40\,\mathrm{mm}$.

:::soluzione
$A = \dfrac{C\,d}{\varepsilon_0} \approx 4.5 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^2} = 45\,\mathrm{cm^2}$.
:::
