---
titolo: Charged capacitors connected with opposite polarity
teoria: [condensatori-in-parallelo, energia-condensatore]
parole: [connection, opposite polarity, conservation of charge, dissipated energy]
---

A capacitor $C_1 = 2.0\,\mu\mathrm{F}$ is charged to $60\,\mathrm{V}$ and a capacitor $C_2 = 3.0\,\mu\mathrm{F}$ is charged to $20\,\mathrm{V}$. Both are disconnected from the sources and connected by joining the positive plate of each with the negative plate of the other. Compute the final potential difference and the energy dissipated in the connection.

:::soluzione
$\Delta V = \dfrac{C_1 \cdot 60\,\mathrm{V} - C_2 \cdot 20\,\mathrm{V}}{C_1 + C_2} = 12\,\mathrm{V}$; dissipated energy $(4.2 - 0.36)\,\mathrm{mJ} \approx 3.8\,\mathrm{mJ}$.
:::
