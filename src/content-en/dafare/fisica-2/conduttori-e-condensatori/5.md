An isolated parallel-plate capacitor in vacuum has plates of area $A = 0.10\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and carries the charge $Q = 0.50\,\mu\mathrm{C}$. Compute the magnitude of the force with which the plates attract each other.

:::soluzione
$F = \dfrac{Q^2}{2\varepsilon_0 A} \approx 0.14\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
