---
titolo: Capacitance of a coaxial cable with a dielectric
teoria: [condensatore-cilindrico, costante-dielettrica-relativa]
parole: [coaxial cable, cylindrical capacitor, polyethylene, capacitance per unit length]
---

A coaxial cable has an inner conductor of radius $a = 1.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ and an outer conductor of inner radius $b = 3.5\,\mathrm{mm}$; the space between the conductors is filled with polyethylene, with $\varepsilon_r = 2.3$. Compute the capacitance per unit length and the capacitance of a $25\,\mathrm{m}$ length of cable.

:::soluzione
$\dfrac{C}{\ell} = \dfrac{2\pi\varepsilon_0 \varepsilon_r}{\ln \dfrac{b}{a}} \approx 1.0 \cdot 10^{2}\,\mathrm{pF/m}$, $C \approx 2.6\,\mathrm{nF}$.
:::
