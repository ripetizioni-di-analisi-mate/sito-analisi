---
titolo: Self-induced emf with a sinusoidal current
teoria: [fem-autoindotta]
parole: [self-induced emf, sinusoidal current, inductor]
---

An inductor of inductance $20\,\mathrm{mH}$ carries the current $i(t) = I_0 \sin(\omega t)$, with $I_0 = 2.0\,\mathrm{A}$ and frequency $50\,\mathrm{Hz}$. Compute the maximum value of the self-induced emf.

:::soluzione
$|\mathcal{E}_L|_{\max} = L I_0\,\omega \approx 13\,\mathrm{V}$.
:::
