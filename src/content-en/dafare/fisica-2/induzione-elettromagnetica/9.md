An ideal source of $9.0\,\mathrm{V}$, a resistor of $3.0\,\mathrm{\Omega}$ and an ideal inductor of $0.15\,\mathrm{H}$ are connected in series, and the switch is closed at time $t = 0$. Compute the time constant and the current at time $t = 0.10\,\mathrm{s}$.

:::soluzione
$\tau = \dfrac{L}{R} = 0.050\,\mathrm{s}$, $i = \dfrac{\mathcal{E}}{R}\left( 1 - e^{-2} \right) \approx 2.6\,\mathrm{A}$.
:::
