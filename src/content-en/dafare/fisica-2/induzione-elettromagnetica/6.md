titolo: Coil rotating in a uniform field
teoria: [spira-rotante]

---

A coil of $200$ turns of area $50\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ rotates at $3000$ revolutions per minute about an axis perpendicular to a uniform magnetic field of $0.10\,\mathrm{T}$. Compute the maximum value and the frequency of the induced emf.

:::soluzione
$f = 50\,\mathrm{Hz}$, $\mathcal{E}_{\max} = N B A\,\omega \approx 31\,\mathrm{V}$.
:::
