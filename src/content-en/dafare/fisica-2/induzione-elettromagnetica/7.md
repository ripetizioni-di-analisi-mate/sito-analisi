An air-core solenoid $30\,\mathrm{cm}$ long has $1200$ turns of radius $1.5\,\mathrm{cm}$. Neglecting edge effects, compute its inductance.

:::soluzione
$L = \dfrac{\mu_0 N^2 \pi r^2}{\ell} \approx 4.3 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{H}$.
:::
