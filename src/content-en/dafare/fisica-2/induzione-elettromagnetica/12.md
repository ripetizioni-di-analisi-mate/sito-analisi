A long solenoid with $1500$ turns per meter contains at its center a coaxial coil of $300$ turns of radius $1.2\,\mathrm{cm}$. Compute the mutual inductance and the magnitude of the emf induced in the coil when the current in the solenoid changes by $80\,\mathrm{A}$ per second.

:::soluzione
$M = \mu_0 n_1 N_2 \pi r_2^2 \approx 2.6 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{H}$, $|\mathcal{E}_2| \approx 2.0 \cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{V}$.
:::
