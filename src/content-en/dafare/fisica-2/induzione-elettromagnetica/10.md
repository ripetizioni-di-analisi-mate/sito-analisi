---
titolo: Halving of the current in an RL circuit
teoria: [circuito-rl-apertura]
parole: [RL circuit, opening, half-life]
---

An inductor of $0.25\,\mathrm{H}$, carrying a current, is closed at time $t = 0$ on a resistor of $50\,\mathrm{\Omega}$, after the source has been cut out. Compute after how much time the current has halved.

:::soluzione
$t = \dfrac{L}{R} \ln 2 \approx 3.5\,\mathrm{ms}$.
:::
