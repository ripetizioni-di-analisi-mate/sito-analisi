An inductor of $0{,}25\,\mathrm{H}$, carrying a current, is connected at time $t = 0$ in a closed circuit with a resistor of $50\,\mathrm{\Omega}$, after the source has been disconnected. Compute the time it takes for the current to halve.

:::soluzione
$t = \dfrac{L}{R} \ln 2 \approx 3{,}5\,\mathrm{ms}$.
:::
