---
titolo: Force needed to pull a rod
teoria: [sbarretta-su-binari, bilancio-energetico-sbarretta]
parole: [rod, braking force, power, rails]
---

A rod $80\,\mathrm{cm}$ long is pulled without friction at constant velocity $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ on two rails closed by a resistor of $0.40\,\mathrm{\Omega}$, in a uniform field of $0.25\,\mathrm{T}$ perpendicular to the plane of the rails. Neglecting the other resistances and self-induction, compute the force to be applied to the rod and the power it develops.

:::soluzione
$F = \dfrac{B^2 \ell^2 v}{R} = 0.60\,\mathrm{N}$, $P = F v = 3.6\,\mathrm{W}$.
:::
