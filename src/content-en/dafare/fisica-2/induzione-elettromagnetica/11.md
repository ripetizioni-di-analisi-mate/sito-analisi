In the air gap of an electromagnet, a region of air of volume $3{,}0\,\mathrm{cm^3}$, the magnetic field is uniform and equals $0{,}80\,\mathrm{T}$. Compute the magnetic energy density and the energy contained in the air gap.

:::soluzione
$u_B = \dfrac{B^2}{2 \mu_0} \approx 2{,}5 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{J/m^3}$, $U = u_B \cdot 3{,}0 \cdot 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^3} \approx 0{,}76\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
