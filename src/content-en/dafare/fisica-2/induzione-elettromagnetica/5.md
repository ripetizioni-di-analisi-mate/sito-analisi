---
titolo: Terminal velocity on vertical rails
teoria: [bilancio-energetico-sbarretta, sbarretta-su-binari]
parole: [terminal velocity, rod, vertical rails, fall]
---

A rod of mass $50\,\mathrm{g}$ and length $40\,\mathrm{cm}$ falls without friction along two vertical rails connected at the top by a resistor of $0.20\,\mathrm{\Omega}$, in a uniform horizontal field of $0.50\,\mathrm{T}$ perpendicular to the plane of the rails. Neglecting the other resistances and self-induction, compute the terminal velocity.

:::soluzione
$v_{\text{lim}} = \dfrac{m g R}{B^2 \ell^2} \approx 2.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
