titolo: Rod at constant velocity
teoria: [fem-mozionale, corrente-indotta]
parole: [rod, rails, motional emf, current]

A conducting rod $30\,\mathrm{cm}$ long slides at constant velocity $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ on two rails closed by a resistor of $1.5\,\mathrm{\Omega}$, in a uniform magnetic field of $0.60\,\mathrm{T}$ perpendicular to the plane of the rails. Neglecting the other resistances and self-induction, compute the induced emf and the current.

:::soluzione
$\mathcal{E} = B \ell v = 0.45\,\mathrm{V}$, $I = \dfrac{\mathcal{E}}{R} = 0.30\,\mathrm{A}$.
:::
