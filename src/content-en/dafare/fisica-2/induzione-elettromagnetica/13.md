In a long solenoid of radius $a = 3.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ the magnetic field is uniform and increases by $0.40\,\mathrm{T}$ per second. Compute the magnitude of the induced electric field at $1.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ and at $6.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the axis.

:::soluzione
$E(1.0\,\mathrm{cm}) = \dfrac{r}{2}\,\dfrac{dB}{dt} = 2.0 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{V/m}$, $E(6.0\,\mathrm{cm}) = \dfrac{a^2}{2 r}\,\dfrac{dB}{dt} = 3.0 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{V/m}$.
:::
