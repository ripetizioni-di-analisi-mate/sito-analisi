---
titolo: Carica indotta nell'estrazione di una spira
teoria: [carica-indotta, flusso-concatenato]
parole: [legge di Felici, carica indotta, spira, estrazione]
---

A circular loop of radius $4{,}0\,\mathrm{cm}$ and resistance $0{,}050\,\mathrm{\Omega}$ is perpendicular to a uniform magnetic field of $0{,}25\,\mathrm{T}$ and is pulled completely out of the field region. Neglecting self-induction, compute the charge that passes through the loop.

:::soluzione
$|q| = \dfrac{B \pi r^2}{R} \approx 2{,}5 \cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{C}$.
:::
