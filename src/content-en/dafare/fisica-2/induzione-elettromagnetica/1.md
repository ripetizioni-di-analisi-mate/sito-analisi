A coil of $150$ turns, each of area $12\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, has its axis parallel to a uniform magnetic field that changes from $0.20\,\mathrm{T}$ to $0.80\,\mathrm{T}$ in $30\,\mathrm{ms}$. Compute the magnitude of the average emf induced in the coil.

:::soluzione
$|\mathcal{E}_m| = \dfrac{N A\,\Delta B}{\Delta t} = 3.6\,\mathrm{V}$.
:::
