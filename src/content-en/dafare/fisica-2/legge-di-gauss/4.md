---
titolo: Flux through one face of a cube
teoria: [legge-di-gauss, flusso-carica-puntiforme]
parole: [cube, charge at the center, symmetry, flux]
---

A point charge of $3.0\,\mathrm{nC}$ is at the center of a cube of edge $20\,\mathrm{cm}$. Compute the outward electric flux through one face of the cube.

:::soluzione
$\Phi_E = \dfrac{q}{6\,\varepsilon_0} \approx 56\,\mathrm{N \cdot m^2/C}$, independent of the edge.
:::
