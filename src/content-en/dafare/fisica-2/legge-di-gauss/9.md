---
titolo: Electron near a charged plane
teoria: [campo-piano-infinito]
parole: [infinite plane, surface density, acceleration, electron]
---

A very large plane is charged uniformly with density $5.0\,\mathrm{nC/m^2}$. Compute the magnitude of the electric field near the plane and the acceleration of an electron in the field, neglecting its weight.

:::soluzione
$E = \dfrac{\sigma}{2\varepsilon_0} \approx 2.8 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{N/C}$, $a = \dfrac{eE}{m_e} \approx 5.0 \cdot 10^{13}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, toward the plane.
:::
