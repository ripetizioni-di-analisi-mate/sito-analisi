---
titolo: Charge density from a radial field
teoria: [forma-locale-gauss]
parole: [divergence, local form, uniform density]
---

In a region of space the electric field is $\vec{E} = a\,(x\,\hat{\imath} + y\,\hat{\jmath} + z\,\hat{k})$, with $a = 1.5 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{N/(C \cdot m)}$. Compute the charge density in the region.

:::soluzione
$\rho = \varepsilon_0\, \nabla \cdot \vec{E} = 3\,\varepsilon_0\, a \approx 4.0 \cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{C/m^3}$.
:::
