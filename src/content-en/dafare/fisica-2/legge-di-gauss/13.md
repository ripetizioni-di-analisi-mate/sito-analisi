titolo: Sphere with density proportional to the radius

---

An insulating sphere of radius $R = 5.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ is charged with density $\rho(r) = \rho_0\,\dfrac{r}{R}$, with $\rho_0 = 4.0\,\mathrm{\mu C/m^3}$. Compute the total charge, the field inside as a function of $r$ and its magnitude at the surface.

:::soluzione
$Q = \pi \rho_0 R^3 \approx 1.6 \cdot 10^{-9}\,\mathrm{C}$, $E(r) = \dfrac{\rho_0 r^2}{4\varepsilon_0 R}$, $E(R) = \dfrac{\rho_0 R}{4\varepsilon_0} \approx 5.6 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
:::
