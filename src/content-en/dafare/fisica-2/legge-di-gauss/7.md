---
titolo: Densità lineare di un filo
teoria: [campo-filo-infinito]
parole: [filo infinito, densità lineare, campo radiale]
---

At $20\,\mathrm{cm}$ from a very long straight wire, charged uniformly, the electric field has magnitude $900\,\mathrm{N/C}$ and points away from the wire. Compute the linear charge density of the wire.

:::soluzione
$\lambda = 2\pi\varepsilon_0\, r E \approx 1{,}0 \cdot 10^{-8}\,\mathrm{C/m}$.
:::
