---
titolo: Field of a spherical shell
teoria: [campo-guscio-sferico]
parole: [spherical shell, zero interior field, surface charge]
---

A thin spherical shell of radius $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ carries the charge $5.0\,\mathrm{nC}$ distributed uniformly. Compute the magnitude of the electric field at $5.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ and at $20\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the center.

:::soluzione
$E(5.0\,\mathrm{cm}) = 0$, $E(20\,\mathrm{cm}) = \dfrac{kQ}{r^2} \approx 1.1 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
:::
