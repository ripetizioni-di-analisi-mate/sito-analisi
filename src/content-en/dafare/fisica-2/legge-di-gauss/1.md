---
titolo: Flux through a tilted disk
teoria: [flusso-campo-elettrico]
parole: [flux, uniform field, disk, angle]
---

A disk of radius $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ is in a uniform electric field of magnitude $6.0 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{N/C}$, and the normal to the disk makes an angle of $30^\circ$ with the field. Compute the flux of the field through the disk.

:::soluzione
$\Phi_E = E\,\pi r^2 \cos 30^\circ \approx 1.6 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{N \cdot m^2/C}$.
:::
