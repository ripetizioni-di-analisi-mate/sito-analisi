titolo: Field inside and outside a charged sphere
teoria: [campo-sfera-piena, superficie-gaussiana]

An insulating sphere of radius $4.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ has the charge $12\,\mathrm{nC}$ distributed uniformly throughout its volume. Compute the magnitude of the electric field at $1.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ and at $6.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the center.

:::soluzione
$E(1.0\,\mathrm{cm}) = \dfrac{kQr}{R^3} \approx 1.7 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{N/C}$, $E(6.0\,\mathrm{cm}) = \dfrac{kQ}{r^2} \approx 3.0 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
:::
