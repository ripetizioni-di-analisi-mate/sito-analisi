---
titolo: Charge enclosed by a surface
teoria: [legge-di-gauss, flusso-superficie-chiusa]
parole: [Gauss's law, enclosed charge, negative flux]
---

The outward electric flux through a closed surface is $-4.5 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{N \cdot m^2/C}$. Compute the total charge contained in the surface.

:::soluzione
$q_{\text{int}} = \varepsilon_0\,\Phi_E \approx -4.0 \cdot 10^{-8}\,\mathrm{C}$.
:::
