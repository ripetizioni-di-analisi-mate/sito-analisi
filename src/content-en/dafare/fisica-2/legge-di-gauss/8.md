---
titolo: Field of a solid cylinder
teoria: [campo-cilindro-carico, superficie-gaussiana]
parole: [charged cylinder, cylindrical symmetry, volume density]
---

A very long insulating cylinder of radius $3.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ is charged uniformly with density $2.0\,\mathrm{\mu C/m^3}$. Compute the magnitude of the electric field at $1.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ and at $9.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the axis.

:::soluzione
$E(1.5\,\mathrm{cm}) = \dfrac{\rho r}{2\varepsilon_0} \approx 1.7 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{N/C}$, $E(9.0\,\mathrm{cm}) = \dfrac{\rho R^2}{2\varepsilon_0 r} \approx 1.1 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
:::
