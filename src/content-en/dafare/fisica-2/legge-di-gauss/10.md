---
titolo: Density of two parallel planes
teoria: [campo-due-piani]
parole: [two parallel planes, opposite charges, uniform field]
---

Two very large parallel planes have uniform surface densities $+\sigma$ and $-\sigma$. Between the planes, far from the edges, the electric field has magnitude $2.0 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{N/C}$. Compute $\sigma$ and the field outside the planes.

:::soluzione
$\sigma = \varepsilon_0 E \approx 1.8 \cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{C/m^2}$; outside, the field is zero.
:::
