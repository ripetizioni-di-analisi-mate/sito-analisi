---
titolo: Radius and period of a proton
teoria: [moto-circolare-campo-magnetico]

---

A proton, of mass $m_p = 1.67 \cdot 10^{-27}\,\mathrm{kg}$, moves with speed $v = 2.0 \cdot 10^6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ perpendicular to a uniform magnetic field $B = 0.50\,\mathrm{T}$. Compute the radius of the trajectory and the period of the motion.

:::soluzione
$r = \dfrac{m_p v}{e B} \approx 4.2 \cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m}$, $T = \dfrac{2\pi m_p}{e B} \approx 1.3 \cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
