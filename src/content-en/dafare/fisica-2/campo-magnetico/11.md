A horizontal rod of length $\ell = 0.20\,\mathrm{m}$ and mass $m = 10\,\mathrm{g}$ hangs from two insulating vertical threads in a uniform horizontal magnetic field $B = 0.40\,\mathrm{T}$, perpendicular to the rod. Compute the current that makes the tension in the threads vanish.

:::soluzione
$I = \dfrac{m g}{\ell B} \approx 1.2\,\mathrm{A}$.
:::
