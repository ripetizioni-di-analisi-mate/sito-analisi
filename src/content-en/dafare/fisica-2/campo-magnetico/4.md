Compute the cyclotron frequency of an electron in a uniform magnetic field $B = 0.10\,\mathrm{T}$, and determine whether it depends on the speed of the electron.

:::soluzione
$f = \dfrac{e B}{2\pi m_e} \approx 2.8 \cdot 10^9\,\mathrm{Hz}$, independent of the speed.
:::
