An electron, initially at rest, is accelerated by the potential difference $\Delta V = 500\,\mathrm{V}$ and enters a uniform magnetic field $B = 1{,}0 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{T}$ perpendicular to its velocity. Compute the radius of the trajectory.

:::soluzione
$r = \dfrac{1}{B}\sqrt{\dfrac{2 m_e\,\Delta V}{e}} \approx 7{,}5 \cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
