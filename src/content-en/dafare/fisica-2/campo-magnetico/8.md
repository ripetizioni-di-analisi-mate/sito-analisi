---
titolo: Mass of an ion in the spectrometer
teoria: [spettrometro-di-massa]
parole: [mass spectrometer, ion, atomic mass unit]
---

In a mass spectrometer ions of charge $e$, starting practically at rest, are accelerated by the potential difference $\Delta V = 1.0\,\mathrm{kV}$ and enter a uniform magnetic field $B = 0.20\,\mathrm{T}$ perpendicular to the velocity, where they describe a semicircle of radius $r = 12.0\,\mathrm{cm}$. Compute the mass of the ions in kilograms and in atomic mass units ($1\,\mathrm{u} = 1.66 \cdot 10^{-27}\,\mathrm{kg}$).

:::soluzione
$m = \dfrac{e B^2 r^2}{2\,\Delta V} \approx 4.6 \cdot 10^{-26}\,\mathrm{kg}$, about $28\,\mathrm{u}$.
:::
