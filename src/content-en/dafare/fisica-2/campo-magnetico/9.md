---
titolo: Electron density with the Hall effect
teoria: [effetto-hall]
parole: [Hall effect, carrier density, aluminum]
---

An aluminum strip of thickness $s = 0.10\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries the current $I = 8.0\,\mathrm{A}$ in a magnetic field $B = 1.2\,\mathrm{T}$ perpendicular to its wide faces, and the Hall voltage $\Delta V_H = 3.3\,\mu\mathrm{V}$ is measured between its edges. Compute the density of the carriers, of charge $-e$.

:::soluzione
$n = \dfrac{I B}{e\, s\,\Delta V_H} \approx 1.8 \cdot 10^{29}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}}$.
:::
