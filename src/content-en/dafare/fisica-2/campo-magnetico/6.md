---
titolo: Raggio e passo di un'elica
teoria: [moto-elicoidale]
parole: [elica, passo, raggio, protone]
---

A proton, of mass $m_p = 1{,}67 \cdot 10^{-27}\,\mathrm{kg}$, enters a uniform magnetic field $B = 0{,}050\,\mathrm{T}$ with velocity $v = 3{,}0 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ forming an angle of $60^\circ$ with the field. Compute the radius and the pitch of the helix.

:::soluzione
$r = \dfrac{m_p v \sin 60^\circ}{e B} \approx 5{,}4 \cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m}$, $p = \dfrac{2\pi m_p v \cos 60^\circ}{e B} \approx 0{,}20\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
