teoria: [energia-momento-magnetico]

A coil with magnetic moment $\mu = 0.25\,\mathrm{A\cdot m^2}$ is in stable equilibrium in a uniform magnetic field $B = 0.80\,\mathrm{T}$. Compute the work needed to rotate it slowly until the magnetic moment forms an angle of $120^\circ$ with the field.

:::soluzione
$W = \mu B\,(1 - \cos 120^\circ) = 0.30\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
