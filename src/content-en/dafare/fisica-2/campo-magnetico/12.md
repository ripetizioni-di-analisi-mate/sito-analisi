---
titolo: Torque on a circular coil
teoria: [momento-magnetico, momento-su-una-spira]
parole: [coil, torque, magnetic moment]
---

A circular coil of $N = 20$ turns of radius $r = 3.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ carries the current $I = 1.5\,\mathrm{A}$ in a uniform magnetic field $B = 0.60\,\mathrm{T}$ parallel to the plane of the coil. Compute the torque on the coil.

:::soluzione
$\tau = N I \pi r^2 B \approx 5.1 \cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$.
:::
