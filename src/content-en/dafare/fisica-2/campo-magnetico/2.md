---
titolo: Force on a proton in oblique motion
teoria: [forza-di-lorentz]
parole: [Lorentz force, proton, angle]
---

A proton moves with speed $v = 2.0 \cdot 10^6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in a uniform magnetic field $B = 0.30\,\mathrm{T}$; the velocity forms an angle of $45^\circ$ with the field. Compute the magnitude of the magnetic force on the proton.

:::soluzione
$F = e v B \sin 45^\circ \approx 6.8 \cdot 10^{-14}\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
