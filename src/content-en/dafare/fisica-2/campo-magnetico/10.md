---
titolo: Force on a quarter-circle wire
teoria: [forza-su-un-filo-curvo]
parole: [curved wire, uniform field, force on a wire]
---

A wire in the shape of a quarter circle of radius $R = 0.20\,\mathrm{m}$, carrying the current $I = 3.0\,\mathrm{A}$, lies in a plane perpendicular to a uniform magnetic field $B = 0.50\,\mathrm{T}$. Compute the magnitude of the force on the wire.

:::soluzione
$F = I \sqrt{2}\,R\, B \approx 0.42\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
