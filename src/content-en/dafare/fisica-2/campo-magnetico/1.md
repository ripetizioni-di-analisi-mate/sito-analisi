An electron moves with speed $v = 5.0 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ perpendicular to a uniform magnetic field $B = 0.40\,\mathrm{T}$. Compute the magnitude of the magnetic force on the electron.

:::soluzione
$F = e v B = 3.2 \cdot 10^{-14}\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
