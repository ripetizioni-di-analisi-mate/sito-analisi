---
titolo: Proton moving parallel to a wire
teoria: [campo-filo-rettilineo, regola-mano-destra]
parole: [proton, Lorentz force, straight wire]
---

A proton moves with velocity $3.0 \cdot 10^{5}\,\mathrm{m/s}$ parallel to an infinite straight wire, $2.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the wire, in the same sense as the current $25\,\mathrm{A}$ in the wire. Compute the magnitude of the magnetic force on the proton and state whether it points toward the wire or away from it.

:::soluzione
$F = e v\,\dfrac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi d} = 1.2 \cdot 10^{-17}\,\mathrm{N}$, pointing toward the wire.
:::
