---
titolo: Flux through an inclined loop
teoria: [flusso-campo-magnetico]
parole: [magnetic flux, square loop, angle]
---

A square loop of side $20\,\mathrm{cm}$ is in a uniform magnetic field of $0.50\,\mathrm{T}$, which forms an angle of $30^\circ$ with the *plane* of the loop. Compute the flux of the field through the loop.

:::soluzione
$\Phi_B = B\,\ell^2 \sin 30^\circ = 1.0 \cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{Wb}$, where $\ell$ is the side of the loop.
:::
