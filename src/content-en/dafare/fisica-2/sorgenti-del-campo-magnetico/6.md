---
titolo: Circuit in the shape of a quarter circle
teoria: [campo-arco]
parole: [arc, quarter circle, radial segments]
---

A circuit consists of a circular arc of radius $3.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ and angle $90^\circ$ and of the two radii joining its endpoints to the center $C$. The circuit carries the current $9.0\,\mathrm{A}$. Compute the magnitude of the magnetic field at $C$.

:::soluzione
$B = \dfrac{\mu_0 I}{8R} \approx 4.7 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{T}$.
:::
