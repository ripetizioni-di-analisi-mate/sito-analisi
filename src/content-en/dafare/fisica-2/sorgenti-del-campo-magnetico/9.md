We want to build a solenoid $50\,\mathrm{cm}$ long that produces inside a magnetic field of $0.020\,\mathrm{T}$ with a current of $5.0\,\mathrm{A}$. Compute the number of turns per unit length and the total number of turns needed.

:::soluzione
$n = \dfrac{B}{\mu_0 I} \approx 3.2 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$, $N = n\ell \approx 1.6 \cdot 10^{3}$ turns.
:::
