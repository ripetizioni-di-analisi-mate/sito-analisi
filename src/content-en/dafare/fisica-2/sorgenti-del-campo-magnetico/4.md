---
titolo: Due fili con correnti opposte
teoria: [campo-filo-rettilineo, regola-mano-destra]
parole: [fili paralleli, correnti opposte, sovrapposizione]
---

Two infinite parallel straight wires are $6{,}0\,\mathrm{cm}$ apart and carry the current $12\,\mathrm{A}$ in opposite directions. Compute the magnitude of the magnetic field at the midpoint of the segment perpendicular to the wires that joins them.

:::soluzione
$B = 2 \cdot \dfrac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi \frac{d}{2}} = 1{,}6 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{T}$, perpendicular to the plane of the wires.
:::
