---
titolo: Field at the center of a flat coil
teoria: [campo-spira-asse]
parole: [coil, circular loop, center]
---

A flat coil consists of $200$ circular turns of radius $8.0\,\mathrm{cm}$, carrying the current $0.50\,\mathrm{A}$. Compute the magnitude of the magnetic field at the center of the coil.

:::soluzione
$B = \dfrac{N \mu_0 I}{2R} \approx 7.9 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{T}$.
:::
