A long straight wire of radius $R = 3{,}0\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries the current $45\,\mathrm{A}$, distributed uniformly over the cross section. Compute the maximum magnetic field and the distances from the axis at which the field is half the maximum value.

:::soluzione
$B_{\max} = \dfrac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi R} = 3{,}0 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{T}$ at the surface; the field is half at $r = \dfrac{R}{2} = 1{,}5\,\mathrm{mm}$ and at $r = 2R = 6{,}0\,\mathrm{mm}$.
:::
