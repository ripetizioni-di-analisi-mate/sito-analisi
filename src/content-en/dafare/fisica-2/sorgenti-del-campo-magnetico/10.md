---
titolo: Campo massimo e minimo in un toroide
teoria: [campo-toroide]
parole: [toroide, raggio interno, raggio esterno]
---

A toroid of $1000$ turns, carrying the current $1{,}2\,\mathrm{A}$, has inner radius $20\,\mathrm{cm}$ and outer radius $25\,\mathrm{cm}$. Compute the maximum value and the minimum value of the magnetic field inside the ring.

:::soluzione
$B_{\max} = \dfrac{\mu_0 N I}{2\pi a} = 1{,}2 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{T}$ at the inner edge, $B_{\min} = \dfrac{\mu_0 N I}{2\pi b} \approx 9{,}6 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{T}$ at the outer edge.
:::
