titolo: Forza tra due sbarre conduttrici
teoria: [forza-tra-fili-paralleli]
parole: [forza tra fili, attrazione, sbarre]
---

Two straight parallel conducting bars, $2{,}0\,\mathrm{m}$ long, are $8{,}0\,\mathrm{mm}$ apart and carry the currents $40\,\mathrm{A}$ and $25\,\mathrm{A}$ in the same direction. Treating the bars as infinite wires, compute the force between them and state whether it is attractive or repulsive.

:::soluzione
$F = \dfrac{\mu_0 I_1 I_2\,\ell}{2\pi d} = 5{,}0 \cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{N}$, attractive.
:::
