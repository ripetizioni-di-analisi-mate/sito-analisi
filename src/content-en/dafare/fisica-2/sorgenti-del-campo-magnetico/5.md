---
titolo: Force between two conducting bars
teoria: [forza-tra-fili-paralleli]
parole: [force between wires, attraction, bars]
---

Two straight parallel conducting bars, $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ long, are $8.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ apart and carry the currents $40\,\mathrm{A}$ and $25\,\mathrm{A}$ in the same sense. Treating the bars as infinite wires, compute the force between them and state whether it is attractive or repulsive.

:::soluzione
$F = \dfrac{\mu_0 I_1 I_2\,\ell}{2\pi d} = 5.0 \cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{N}$, attractive.
:::
