---
titolo: Flusso attraverso la superficie laterale di un cono
teoria: [legge-gauss-magnetismo, flusso-campo-magnetico]
parole: [legge di Gauss, superficie chiusa, cono, flusso]
---

A right circular cone has a base of radius $4{,}0\,\mathrm{cm}$ and is placed in a uniform magnetic field of magnitude $0{,}30\,\mathrm{T}$, parallel to the axis of the cone and directed from the vertex toward the base. Compute the flux of the field through the lateral surface of the cone, oriented with the outward normal.

:::soluzione
$\Phi_{\text{lat}} = -B\pi r^2 \approx -1{,}5 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{Wb}$, where $r$ is the base radius.
:::
