---
titolo: Field on the axis of a loop
teoria: [campo-spira-asse]
parole: [circular loop, axis, distance]
---

A circular loop has radius $R = 3.0\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find at what distance from the center, along the axis, the magnetic field is one eighth of the field at the center.

:::soluzione
$z = \sqrt{3}\,R \approx 5.2\,\mathrm{cm}$.
:::
