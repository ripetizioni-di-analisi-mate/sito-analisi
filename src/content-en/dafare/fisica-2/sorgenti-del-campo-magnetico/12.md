titolo: Field in a coaxial cable
teoria: [campo-cavo-coassiale, legge-di-ampere]

---

A coaxial cable has an inner conductor of radius $0.50\,\mathrm{mm}$ and an outer conductor with radii $3.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $3.5\,\mathrm{mm}$; the two conductors carry the current $10\,\mathrm{A}$ in opposite senses. Compute the magnitude of the magnetic field at $1.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ and at $4.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ from the axis.

:::soluzione
$B(1.0\,\mathrm{mm}) = \dfrac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r} = 2.0 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{T}$; $B(4.0\,\mathrm{mm}) = 0$.
:::
