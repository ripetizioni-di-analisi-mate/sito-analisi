---
titolo: Field of a wire and distance
teoria: [campo-filo-rettilineo]
parole: [straight wire, magnetic field, distance]
---

A very long straight wire carries the current $I = 8.0\,\mathrm{A}$. Compute the magnitude of the magnetic field $4.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the wire and the distance at which the field equals $1.0 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{T}$.

:::soluzione
$B = \dfrac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi d} = 4.0 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{T}$; the field equals $1.0 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{T}$ at $d' = \dfrac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi B} = 0.16\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
