A parallel-plate capacitor in vacuum with circular plates of radius $R = 6{,}0\,\mathrm{cm}$ is charged with current $i = 3{,}0\,\mathrm{A}$. Neglecting edge effects, compute the magnitude of the magnetic field between the plates at distance $r = 3{,}0\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the axis.

:::soluzione
$B = \dfrac{\mu_0\, i\, r}{2\pi R^2} = 5{,}0 \cdot 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{T}$.
:::
