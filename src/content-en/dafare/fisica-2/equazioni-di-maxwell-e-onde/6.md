A microwave oven operates at the frequency $f = 2{,}45\,\mathrm{GHz}$. Compute the wavelength in vacuum and state the band of the electromagnetic spectrum to which it belongs.

:::soluzione
$\lambda = \dfrac{c}{f} \approx 12{,}2\,\mathrm{cm}$, in the microwave band.
:::
