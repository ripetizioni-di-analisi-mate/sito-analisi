The uniform electric field between the plates of a parallel-plate capacitor in vacuum, whose plates have area $A = 0{,}040\,\mathrm{m^2}$, grows at the rate $\dfrac{dE}{dt} = 5{,}0 \cdot 10^{11}\,\mathrm{V/(m\cdot s)}$. Neglecting edge effects, compute the displacement current between the plates.

:::soluzione
$i_s = \varepsilon_0 A \dfrac{dE}{dt} \approx 0{,}18\,\mathrm{A}$.
:::
