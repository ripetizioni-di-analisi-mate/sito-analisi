---
titolo: Displacement current in a parallel-plate capacitor
teoria: [corrente-di-spostamento]
parole: [displacement current, electric flux, parallel-plate capacitor]
---

The uniform electric field between the plates of a parallel-plate capacitor in vacuum, of area $A = 0.040\,\mathrm{m^2}$, grows at the rate $\dfrac{dE}{dt} = 5.0 \cdot 10^{11}\,\mathrm{V/(m\cdot s)}$. Neglecting edge effects, compute the displacement current between the plates.

:::soluzione
$i_s = \varepsilon_0 A \dfrac{dE}{dt} \approx 0.18\,\mathrm{A}$.
:::
