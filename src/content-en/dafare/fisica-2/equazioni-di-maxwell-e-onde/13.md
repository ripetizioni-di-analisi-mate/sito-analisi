In vacuum the electric field of a plane wave is
$$
\vec{E}(x,t) = 30 \sin\left( 1.0 \cdot 10^{7}\, x - \omega t \right)\hat{\jmath}\ \mathrm{V/m} ,
$$
with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. Compute the wavelength and the angular frequency, and write the magnetic field.

:::soluzione
$\lambda = \dfrac{2\pi}{k} \approx 6.3 \cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m}$, $\omega = ck = 3.0 \cdot 10^{15}\,\mathrm{rad/s}$, $\vec{B} = 1.0 \cdot 10^{-7} \sin\left( 1.0 \cdot 10^{7}\, x - \omega t \right)\hat{k}\ \mathrm{T}$.
:::
