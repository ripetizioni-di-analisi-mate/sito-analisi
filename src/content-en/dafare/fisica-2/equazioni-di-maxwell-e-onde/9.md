---
titolo: Campo di un trasmettitore lontano
teoria: [sorgente-isotropa, intensita]
parole: [sorgente isotropa, intensità, ampiezza del campo]
---

A transmitter, which can be treated as an isotropic source, emits the average power $P = 50\,\mathrm{kW}$. Neglecting absorption, compute the intensity and the amplitude of the electric field at distance $r = 10\,\mathrm{km}$.

:::soluzione
$I = \dfrac{P}{4\pi r^2} \approx 4{,}0 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$ and $E_0 = \sqrt{\dfrac{2I}{c\, \varepsilon_0}} \approx 0{,}17\,\mathrm{V/m}$.
:::
