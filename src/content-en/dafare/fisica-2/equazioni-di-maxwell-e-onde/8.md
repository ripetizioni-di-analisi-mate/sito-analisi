---
titolo: Intensity from the field amplitude
teoria: [intensita, vettore-di-poynting]
parole: [intensity, amplitude, Poynting vector]
---

A harmonic plane wave in vacuum has an electric field of amplitude $E_0 = 60\,\mathrm{V/m}$. Compute the intensity of the wave.

:::soluzione
$I = \dfrac{1}{2}\, c\, \varepsilon_0 E_0^2 \approx 4.8\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.
:::
