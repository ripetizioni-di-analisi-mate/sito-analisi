A capacitor of capacitance $C = 4.0\,\mathrm{nF}$ is charged with a current $i = 1.5\,\mathrm{mA}$. Compute the displacement current between the plates and the rate at which the potential difference grows.

:::soluzione
$i_s = i = 1.5\,\mathrm{mA}$ and $\dfrac{dV}{dt} = \dfrac{i}{C} \approx 3.8 \cdot 10^{5}\,\mathrm{V/s}$.
:::
