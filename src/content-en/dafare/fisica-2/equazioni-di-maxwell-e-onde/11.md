Outside the Earth's atmosphere solar radiation has intensity $I = 1{,}36\,\mathrm{kW/m^2}$. Compute the average electromagnetic energy density.

:::soluzione
$\langle u \rangle = \dfrac{I}{c} \approx 4{,}5 \cdot 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{J/m^3}$.
:::
