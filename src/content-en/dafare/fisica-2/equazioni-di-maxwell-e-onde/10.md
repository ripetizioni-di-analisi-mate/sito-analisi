titolo: Force of a laser on an absorbing target

A laser beam of power $P = 2.0\,\mathrm{W}$ is incident perpendicularly on a target that absorbs it completely. Compute the force exerted by the beam on the target.

:::soluzione
$F = \dfrac{P}{c} \approx 6.7 \cdot 10^{-9}\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
