---
titolo: Magnetic field outside a region with a varying electric field
teoria: [legge-ampere-maxwell, campo-magnetico-nel-condensatore]
parole: [Ampère-Maxwell law, varying electric field, circulation]
---

In a cylindrical region of radius $R = 5.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ there is a uniform electric field, parallel to the axis, that grows at the rate $\dfrac{dE}{dt} = 2.0 \cdot 10^{12}\,\mathrm{V/(m\cdot s)}$; outside the region the electric field is zero and there are no currents. Compute the magnitude of the magnetic field at distance $r = 8.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the axis.

:::soluzione
$B = \dfrac{\mu_0 \varepsilon_0 R^2}{2r}\,\dfrac{dE}{dt} \approx 3.5 \cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T}$.
:::
