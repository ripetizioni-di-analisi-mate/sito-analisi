titolo: Eco radar dalla Luna
teoria: [velocita-della-luce]
parole: [velocità della luce, radar, tempo di propagazione]
---

A radar pulse is sent toward the Moon, which is at a distance $d = 3{,}84 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m}$, and is reflected by it. Compute the time taken for the echo to return to the starting point.

:::soluzione
$t = \dfrac{2d}{c} \approx 2{,}56\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
