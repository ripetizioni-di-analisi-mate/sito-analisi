---
titolo: Radar echo from the Moon
teoria: [velocita-della-luce]
parole: [speed of light, radar, propagation time]
---

A radar pulse is sent toward the Moon, which is at distance $d = 3.84 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m}$, and is reflected by it. Compute after how much time the echo returns to the starting point.

:::soluzione
$t = \dfrac{2d}{c} \approx 2.56\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
