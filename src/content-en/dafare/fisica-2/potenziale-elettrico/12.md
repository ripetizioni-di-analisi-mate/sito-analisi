---
titolo: Potential difference near a charged wire
teoria: [potenziale-filo-infinito]
parole: [infinite wire, linear density, logarithm, potential difference]
---

A very long straight wire carries the uniform charge $\lambda = 2.0\,\mathrm{nC/m}$. Compute the difference between the potential at $2.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the potential at $8.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the wire.

:::soluzione
$V(2.0\,\mathrm{cm}) - V(8.0\,\mathrm{cm}) = 2k\lambda \ln 4 \approx 50\,\mathrm{V}$.
:::
