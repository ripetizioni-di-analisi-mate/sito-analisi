A proton with kinetic energy $1.2\,\mathrm{MeV}$, very far from an iron nucleus of charge $26e$, moves head-on toward the nucleus, considered at rest. Compute the distance of closest approach.

:::soluzione
$r_{\min} = \dfrac{k \cdot 26 e^2}{K_0} \approx 3.1 \cdot 10^{-14}\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
