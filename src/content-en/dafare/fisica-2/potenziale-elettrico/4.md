A proton, of mass $m_p = 1.67 \cdot 10^{-27}\,\mathrm{kg}$, has kinetic energy $2.0\,\mathrm{keV}$. Express the energy in joules and compute the speed of the proton.

:::soluzione
$K = 3.2 \cdot 10^{-16}\,\mathrm{J}$, $\ v = \sqrt{\dfrac{2K}{m_p}} \approx 6.2 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
