---
titolo: Potenziale di un dipolo
teoria: [potenziale-dipolo]
parole: [dipolo, momento di dipolo, potenziale a grande distanza]
---

A dipole has moment $p = 4{,}0 \cdot 10^{-12}\,\mathrm{C \cdot m}$. Compute the potential at a distance $5{,}0\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the center of the dipole, much greater than the separation between the charges, in a direction that makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with the dipole moment.

:::soluzione
$V \approx \dfrac{k p \cos\theta}{r^2} \approx 7{,}2\,\mathrm{V}$.
:::
