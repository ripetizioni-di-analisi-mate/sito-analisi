---
titolo: Potential of a dipole
teoria: [potenziale-dipolo]
parole: [dipole, dipole moment, potential at large distance]
---

A dipole has moment $p = 4.0 \cdot 10^{-12}\,\mathrm{C \cdot m}$. Compute the potential at a point $5.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the center of the dipole, much greater than the distance between the charges, in a direction that makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with the dipole moment.

:::soluzione
$V \approx \dfrac{k p \cos\theta}{r^2} \approx 7.2\,\mathrm{V}$.
:::
