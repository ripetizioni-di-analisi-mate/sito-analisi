---
titolo: Energy of three charges at the vertices of a triangle
teoria: [energia-sistema-cariche]
parole: [electrostatic energy, equilateral triangle, system of charges]
---

The charges $+q$, $+q$ and $-2q$, with $q = 1.0\,\mu\mathrm{C}$, occupy the vertices of an equilateral triangle of side $15\,\mathrm{cm}$. Compute the electrostatic energy of the system.

:::soluzione
$U = -\dfrac{3kq^2}{a} \approx -0.18\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
