titolo: Potential of a point charge
teoria: [potenziale-carica-puntiforme]

---

Compute the electric potential at $15\,\mathrm{cm}$ from a point charge $Q = -3.0\,\mathrm{nC}$, with zero potential at infinite distance.

:::soluzione
$V = \dfrac{kQ}{r} \approx -1.8 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{V}$.
:::
