An electron starts from rest and crosses a potential difference of $250\,\mathrm{V}$. Compute its final speed, neglecting the weight.

:::soluzione
$v = \sqrt{\dfrac{2 e\, |\Delta V|}{m_e}} \approx 9{,}4 \cdot 10^6\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
