In a region with a uniform field the equipotential surfaces are parallel planes; two consecutive planes are $5.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ apart and their potentials differ by $20\,\mathrm{V}$. Compute the magnitude of the field and the work done by the field on an electron that goes from the surface at $10\,\mathrm{V}$ to the surface at $50\,\mathrm{V}$.

:::soluzione
$E = \dfrac{|\Delta V|}{\Delta s} = 4.0 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{V/m}$; $\ W = e \cdot 40\,\mathrm{V} = 6.4 \cdot 10^{-18}\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
