---
titolo: Work to separate two charges
teoria: [energia-potenziale-due-cariche, energia-sistema-cariche]
parole: [potential energy, charges of opposite sign, external work]
---

Two point charges $q_1 = +5.0\,\mu\mathrm{C}$ and $q_2 = -2.0\,\mu\mathrm{C}$ are at rest at a distance of $30\,\mathrm{cm}$. Compute the external work needed to bring them slowly to infinite distance from each other.

:::soluzione
$W_{\text{ext}} = -\dfrac{k q_1 q_2}{r} \approx 0.30\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
