---
titolo: Potential at the center of a charged sphere
teoria: [potenziale-sfera]
parole: [uniformly charged sphere, interior potential, center]
---

An insulating sphere of radius $5.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ carries the charge $2.0\,\mathrm{nC}$ distributed uniformly throughout its volume. Compute the potential at the center of the sphere.

:::soluzione
$V(0) = \dfrac{3}{2}\,\dfrac{kQ}{R} \approx 5.4 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{V}$.
:::
