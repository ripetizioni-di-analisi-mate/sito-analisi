---
titolo: Field from a potential in two variables
teoria: [campo-gradiente-potenziale]
parole: [gradient, partial derivatives, field from the potential]

In a charge-free region the potential is $V(x, y) = a\,(3x^2 y - y^3)$, with $a = 20\,\mathrm{V/m^3}$. Compute the electric field at the point $(1.0,\ 1.0)\,\mathrm{m}$.

:::soluzione
$\vec{E} = (-6axy,\ -3a\,(x^2 - y^2))$, so $\vec{E} = -120\,\hat{\imath}\,\mathrm{V/m}$.
:::
