In a uniform electric field of magnitude $E = 1.2 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{V/m}$ a charge $q = 5.0\,\mu\mathrm{C}$ moves $8.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ in the direction opposite to the field. Compute the work done by the electric force.

:::soluzione
$W = -q E d = -4.8 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
