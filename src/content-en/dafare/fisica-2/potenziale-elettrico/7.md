titolo: Potenziale sull'asse di un anello

A thin ring of radius $5{,}0\,\mathrm{cm}$ carries the charge $10\,\mathrm{nC}$ distributed uniformly. Compute the potential at the point of the axis $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the center.

:::soluzione
$V = \dfrac{kQ}{\sqrt{R^2 + x^2}} \approx 6{,}9 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{V}$.
:::
