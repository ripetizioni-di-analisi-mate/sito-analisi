titolo: Campo sull'asse di un anello
teoria: [campo-anello]
parole: [anello carico, campo sull'asse]
---

A thin ring of radius $R = 4{,}0\,\mathrm{cm}$ has charge $Q = 8{,}0\,\mathrm{nC}$ uniformly distributed. Compute the electric field on the axis at distance $z = 3{,}0\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the center.

:::soluzione
$E_z = \dfrac{kQz}{\left( z^2 + R^2 \right)^{\frac{3}{2}}} \approx 1{,}7 \cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{N/C}$, directed along the axis and pointing away from the ring.
:::
