---
titolo: Punto di campo nullo tra due cariche
teoria: [sovrapposizione-campi, campo-carica-puntiforme]
parole: [campo nullo, sovrapposizione, cariche allineate]
---

The charges $q_1 = 1{,}0\,\mathrm{nC}$ and $q_2 = 9{,}0\,\mathrm{nC}$ are at rest on the $x$-axis, at $x = 0$ and at $x = 40\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find the point of the $x$-axis at which the electric field is zero.

:::soluzione
$x = 10\,\mathrm{cm}$, between the charges, at one quarter of the distance starting from $q_1$.
:::
