---
titolo: Field at the vertex of an equilateral triangle
teoria: [sovrapposizione-campi, campo-carica-puntiforme]
parole: [equilateral triangle, superposition, charges in the plane]
---

Two charges $q = 3.0\,\mathrm{nC}$ occupy two vertices of an equilateral triangle of side $a = 10\,\mathrm{cm}$. Compute the magnitude of the electric field at the third vertex and indicate its direction.

:::soluzione
$E = \sqrt{3}\,k\,\dfrac{q}{a^2} \approx 4.7 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{N/C}$, along the bisector of the angle at the third vertex, outward from the triangle.
:::
