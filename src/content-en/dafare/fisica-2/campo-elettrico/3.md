An electron is in a uniform electric field of magnitude $E = 3.0 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{N/C}$. Compute the magnitude of the force it experiences and of its acceleration, and indicate the orientation of the force with respect to the field.

:::soluzione
$F = eE = 4.8 \cdot 10^{-16}\,\mathrm{N}$, $a = \dfrac{eE}{m_e} \approx 5.3 \cdot 10^{14}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, with orientation opposite to the field.
:::
