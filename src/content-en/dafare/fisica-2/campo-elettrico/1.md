Compute the magnitude of the electric field produced by a point charge $Q = 6.0\,\mathrm{nC}$ at distance $r = 20\,\mathrm{cm}$, and determine its orientation.

:::soluzione
$E = k\,\dfrac{Q}{r^2} \approx 1.3 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{N/C}$, pointing away from the charge.
:::
