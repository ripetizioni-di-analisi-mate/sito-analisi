---
titolo: Campo di una carica puntiforme
teoria: [campo-carica-puntiforme]
parole: [campo elettrico, carica puntiforme]
---

Compute the magnitude of the electric field produced by a point charge $Q = 6{,}0\,\mathrm{nC}$ at distance $r = 20\,\mathrm{cm}$, and determine its direction.

:::soluzione
$E = k\,\dfrac{Q}{r^2} \approx 1{,}3 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{N/C}$, pointing away from the charge.
:::
