Two charges $q = 2.0\,\mathrm{nC}$ are at rest at $(0,\, 0)$ and at $(6.0\,\mathrm{cm},\, 0)$. Compute the electric field at the point $P = (3.0\,\mathrm{cm},\, 4.0\,\mathrm{cm})$.

:::soluzione
$\vec{E} = 2\,k\,\dfrac{q}{r^2} \cdot \dfrac{4}{5}\,\hat{\jmath} \approx 1.2 \cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{N/C}\;\hat{\jmath}$, with $r = 5.0\,\mathrm{cm}$.
:::
