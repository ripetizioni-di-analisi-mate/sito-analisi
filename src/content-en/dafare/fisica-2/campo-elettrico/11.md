---
titolo: Field on the axis of a disk
teoria: [campo-disco]
parole: [charged disk, surface density]
---

A thin disk of radius $R = 3.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ has uniform surface density $\sigma = 2.0 \cdot 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{C/m^2}$. Compute the electric field on the axis at distance $z = 4.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the center.

:::soluzione
$E_z = \dfrac{\sigma}{2\varepsilon_0} \left( 1 - \dfrac{z}{\sqrt{z^2 + R^2}} \right) \approx 2.3 \cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
:::
