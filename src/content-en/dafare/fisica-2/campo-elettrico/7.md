A dipole is formed by the charges $\pm 1{,}5\,\mathrm{nC}$ at distance $d = 1{,}0\,\mathrm{mm}$. Compute the dipole moment and the electric field on the axis of the dipole at $r = 5{,}0\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the center.

:::soluzione
$p = 1{,}5 \cdot 10^{-12}\,\mathrm{C\cdot m}$, $E \approx \dfrac{2kp}{r^3} \approx 2{,}2 \cdot 10^{2}\,\mathrm{N/C}$, parallel to $\vec{p}$ and pointing in the same direction.
:::
