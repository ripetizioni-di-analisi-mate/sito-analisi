---
titolo: Deflection of a proton
teoria: [moto-perpendicolare-al-campo, moto-campo-uniforme]

A proton, of mass $m_p = 1.67 \cdot 10^{-27}\,\mathrm{kg}$, enters with velocity $v_0 = 2.0 \cdot 10^{5}\,\mathrm{m/s}$ perpendicular to the field a region of length $\ell = 4.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ where there is a uniform field $E = 1.0 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{N/C}$. Compute the lateral displacement of the proton at the exit from the region.

:::soluzione
$y_u = \dfrac{eE\ell^2}{2 m_p v_0^2} \approx 1.9 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
