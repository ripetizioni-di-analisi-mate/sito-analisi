A proton of mass $m_p = 1{,}67 \cdot 10^{-27}\,\mathrm{kg}$, moving with velocity $v_0 = 2{,}0 \cdot 10^{5}\,\mathrm{m/s}$ perpendicular to the field, enters a region of length $\ell = 4{,}0\,\mathrm{cm}$ with a uniform field $E = 1{,}0 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{N/C}$. Compute the lateral displacement of the proton at the exit from the region.

:::soluzione
$y_u = \dfrac{eE\ell^2}{2 m_p v_0^2} \approx 1{,}9 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
