---
titolo: Two planes with opposite charges
teoria: [piani-paralleli, campo-piano-infinito]
parole: [parallel planes, uniform field, surface density]
---

Two parallel infinite planes have uniform charge densities $+3.0\,\mathrm{nC/m^2}$ and $-3.0\,\mathrm{nC/m^2}$. Compute the electric field between the planes and outside.

:::soluzione
Between the planes $E = \dfrac{\sigma}{\varepsilon_0} \approx 3.4 \cdot 10^{2}\,\mathrm{N/C}$, directed from the positive plane to the negative one; outside $E = 0$.
:::
