---
titolo: Distance at which the field has a given value
teoria: [campo-carica-puntiforme]
parole: [electric field, distance, point charge]
---

At what distance from a point charge $Q = 2.0\,\mathrm{nC}$ does the electric field have magnitude $500\,\mathrm{N/C}$?

:::soluzione
$r = \sqrt{\dfrac{kQ}{E}} \approx 0.19\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
