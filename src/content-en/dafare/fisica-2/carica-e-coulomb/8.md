---
titolo: Two negative charges at a right angle
teoria: [principio-di-sovrapposizione, metodo-delle-componenti]

In vacuum the charge $q_0 = +1.0\,\mu\mathrm{C}$ is at the origin, and two equal charges $q = -2.0\,\mu\mathrm{C}$ are at the points $(0.20\,\mathrm{m};\,0)$ and $(0;\,0.20\,\mathrm{m})$. Compute the magnitude and direction of the total force on $q_0$.

:::soluzione
$F = \sqrt{2}\,k\,\dfrac{|q|\,q_0}{0.20^2} \approx 0.64\,\mathrm{N}$, directed along the bisector of the first quadrant, at $45^\circ$ from the $x$-axis.
:::
