---
titolo: Distance between two charges
teoria: [legge-di-coulomb, carica-puntiforme]
parole: [Coulomb's law, distance, equal charges]
---

Two equal point charges of $1.0\,\mu\mathrm{C}$ repel each other in vacuum with a force of magnitude $1.0\,\mathrm{N}$. Compute their distance.

:::soluzione
$r = q\sqrt{\dfrac{k}{F}} \approx 9.5 \cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m}$, that is about $9.5\,\mathrm{cm}$.
:::
