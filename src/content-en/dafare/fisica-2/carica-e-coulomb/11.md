---
titolo: Charged pendulums
teoria: [pendolini-carichi, legge-di-coulomb]
parole: [electric pendulums, angle, tension, charge]
---

Two identical small spheres of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{g}$, with the same charge, hang from the same point on two insulating strings $30\,\mathrm{cm}$ long. At equilibrium each string forms an angle of $15^\circ$ with the vertical. Compute the absolute value of the charge of each sphere.

:::soluzione
$|q| = 2\ell\sin\theta\,\sqrt{\dfrac{mg\tan\theta}{k}} \approx 1.2 \cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{C} = 0.12\,\mu\mathrm{C}$.
:::
