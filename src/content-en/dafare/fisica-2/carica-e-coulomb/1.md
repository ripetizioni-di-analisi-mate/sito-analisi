A plastic rod rubbed with a wool cloth acquires the charge $q = -4.0\,\mathrm{nC}$. Compute the number of electrons that the rod received from the cloth and the final charge of the cloth, assuming that both were initially neutral.

:::soluzione
$n = \dfrac{|q|}{e} = 2.5 \cdot 10^{10}$ electrons; the cloth has charge $+4.0\,\mathrm{nC}$.
:::
