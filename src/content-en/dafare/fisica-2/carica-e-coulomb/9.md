---
titolo: Equilibrium point between charges of the same sign
teoria: [equilibrio-tra-due-cariche]
parole: [equilibrium, equilibrium position, fixed charges]
---

Two point charges $q_1 = +1.0\,\mathrm{nC}$ and $q_2 = +9.0\,\mathrm{nC}$ are fixed in vacuum at $x = 0$ and at $x = 0.40\,\mathrm{m}$. Determine the point at which a third charge, subject only to the forces of $q_1$ and $q_2$, is in equilibrium.

:::soluzione
$x_0 = \dfrac{\sqrt{q_1}}{\sqrt{q_1} + \sqrt{q_2}}\,d = 0.10\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
