---
titolo: Carica negativa tra due cariche uguali
teoria: [principio-di-sovrapposizione, cariche-allineate]
parole: [cariche allineate, sovrapposizione, forza risultante]
---

On the $x$-axis, in vacuum, there are the point charges $q_1 = +5{,}0\,\mu\mathrm{C}$ at $x = 0$ and $q_2 = +5{,}0\,\mu\mathrm{C}$ at $x = 0{,}40\,\mathrm{m}$. Compute the magnitude and direction of the total force on the charge $q_0 = -1{,}0\,\mu\mathrm{C}$ placed at $x = 0{,}10\,\mathrm{m}$.

:::soluzione
$F_x = -k\,|q_0|\,q_1 \left( \dfrac{1}{0{,}10^2} - \dfrac{1}{0{,}30^2} \right) \approx -4{,}0\,\mathrm{N}$, so the force has magnitude $4{,}0\,\mathrm{N}$ and is directed toward $q_1$.
:::
