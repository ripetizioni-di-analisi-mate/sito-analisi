titolo: Negative charge between two equal charges
teoria: [principio-di-sovrapposizione, cariche-allineate]

On the $x$-axis, in vacuum, there are the point charges $q_1 = +5.0\,\mu\mathrm{C}$ at $x = 0$ and $q_2 = +5.0\,\mu\mathrm{C}$ at $x = 0.40\,\mathrm{m}$. Compute the magnitude and orientation of the total force on the charge $q_0 = -1.0\,\mu\mathrm{C}$ placed at $x = 0.10\,\mathrm{m}$.

:::soluzione
$F_x = -k\,|q_0|\,q_1 \left( \dfrac{1}{0.10^2} - \dfrac{1}{0.30^2} \right) \approx -4.0\,\mathrm{N}$, so the force has magnitude $4.0\,\mathrm{N}$ and is directed toward $q_1$.
:::
