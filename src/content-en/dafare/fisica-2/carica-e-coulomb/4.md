Two protons in an atomic nucleus are $2{,}0 \cdot 10^{-15}\,\mathrm{m}$ apart. Compute the magnitude of the electric force between the two protons and the ratio between the electric force and the gravitational force, with $G = 6{,}67 \cdot 10^{-11}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m^2/kg^2}$ and proton mass $m_p = 1{,}67 \cdot 10^{-27}\,\mathrm{kg}$.

:::soluzione
$F_e = k\,\dfrac{e^2}{r^2} \approx 58\,\mathrm{N}$, repulsive; $\dfrac{F_e}{F_g} = \dfrac{k e^2}{G m_p^2} \approx 1{,}2 \cdot 10^{36}$.
:::
