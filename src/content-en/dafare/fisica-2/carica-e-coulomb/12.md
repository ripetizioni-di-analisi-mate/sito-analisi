---
titolo: Sphere suspended above a charge
teoria: [equilibrio-di-una-carica, legge-di-coulomb]
parole: [levitation, weight, vertical equilibrium]
---

A small sphere of mass $0.50\,\mathrm{g}$ and charge $+20\,\mathrm{nC}$ can slide without friction in a vertical insulating tube, at the bottom of which the point charge $+50\,\mathrm{nC}$ is fixed. Compute the distance between the two charges when the sphere is in equilibrium.

:::soluzione
$h = \sqrt{\dfrac{kQq}{mg}} \approx 4.3 \cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m}$, that is about $4.3\,\mathrm{cm}$.
:::
