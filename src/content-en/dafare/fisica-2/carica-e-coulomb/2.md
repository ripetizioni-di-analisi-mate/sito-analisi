titolo: Force between two point charges
teoria: [legge-di-coulomb]
parole: [Coulomb's law, attractive force, magnitude]
---

Two point charges $q_1 = +5.0\,\mathrm{nC}$ and $q_2 = -8.0\,\mathrm{nC}$ are in vacuum at distance $4.0\,\mathrm{cm}$. Compute the magnitude of the force between the two charges and determine whether it is attractive or repulsive.

:::soluzione
$F = k\,\dfrac{|q_1|\,|q_2|}{r^2} \approx 2.2 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{N}$, attractive.
:::
