---
titolo: Punto di equilibrio con cariche di segno opposto
teoria: [equilibrio-cariche-opposte]
parole: [equilibrio, cariche opposte, fuori dal segmento]
---

Two point charges $q_1 = -2{,}0\,\mu\mathrm{C}$ and $q_2 = +8{,}0\,\mu\mathrm{C}$ are fixed in vacuum at $x = 0$ and at $x = 0{,}30\,\mathrm{m}$. Determine the point of the $x$-axis at which a third charge is in equilibrium.

:::soluzione
$x = -0{,}30\,\mathrm{m}$, that is $0{,}30\,\mathrm{m}$ from $q_1$ on the side opposite to $q_2$.
:::
