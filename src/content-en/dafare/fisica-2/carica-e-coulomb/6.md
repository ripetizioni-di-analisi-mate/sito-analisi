---
titolo: Force with modified charges and distance
teoria: [inverso-del-quadrato, legge-di-coulomb]
parole: [proportionality, inverse square, ratio of forces]
---

Two point charges attract each other with a force of magnitude $F$. One of the two charges is halved and the distance is doubled. Express the magnitude of the new force in terms of $F$.

:::soluzione
$F' = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot \dfrac{1}{4}\,F = \dfrac{F}{8}$.
:::
