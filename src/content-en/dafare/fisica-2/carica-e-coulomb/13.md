Three equal point charges $q > 0$ are at the vertices of an equilateral triangle of side $a$, in vacuum. Determine the charge $Q$ to be placed at the center of the triangle so that all four charges are in equilibrium.

:::soluzione
$Q = -\dfrac{q}{\sqrt{3}} \approx -0.58\,q$.
:::
