Two resistors, $R_1 = 10\,\Omega$ and $R_2 = 20\,\Omega$, are connected to an ideal source with $\mathcal{E} = 30\,\mathrm{V}$, first in series and then in parallel. In both cases compute the power dissipated in each resistor.

:::soluzione
In series $P_1 = 10\,\mathrm{W}$ and $P_2 = 20\,\mathrm{W}$; in parallel $P_1 = 90\,\mathrm{W}$ and $P_2 = 45\,\mathrm{W}$.
:::
