An aluminum cable with a cross section of $2.5\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ carries a current of $10\,\mathrm{A}$. Aluminum has $1.8 \cdot 10^{29}$ conduction electrons per cubic meter. Compute the current density and the drift velocity of the electrons.

:::soluzione
$J = 4.0 \cdot 10^{6}\,\mathrm{A/m^2}$, $v_d = \dfrac{J}{n e} \approx 1.4 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
