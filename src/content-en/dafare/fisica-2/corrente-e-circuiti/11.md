---
titolo: Measuring a resistance with the Wheatstone bridge
teoria: [leggi-di-kirchhoff, legge-di-ohm]
parole: [Wheatstone bridge, galvanometer, balance]
---

In a Wheatstone bridge the resistor $R_1 = 120\,\Omega$ connects the node $A$ to the node $C$, the resistor $R_2 = 360\,\Omega$ connects $C$ to the node $B$, the resistor $R_3 = 50\,\Omega$ connects $A$ to the node $D$ and the unknown resistor $R_x$ connects $D$ to $B$. The source is connected between $A$ and $B$, and a galvanometer connected between $C$ and $D$ carries no current. Compute $R_x$.

:::soluzione
$R_x = \dfrac{R_2 R_3}{R_1} = 150\,\Omega$.
:::
