A $470\,\mu\mathrm{F}$ capacitor, charged to a voltage of $20\,\mathrm{V}$, discharges through a $1.0\,\mathrm{k\Omega}$ resistor. Compute the initial current, the voltage across the capacitor after $1.0\,\mathrm{s}$ and the total energy dissipated in the resistor.

:::soluzione
$i(0) = 20\,\mathrm{mA}$, $\Delta V(1.0\,\mathrm{s}) = 20\, e^{-\frac{1.0}{0.47}}\,\mathrm{V} \approx 2.4\,\mathrm{V}$, $W_{\text{diss}} = \dfrac{1}{2} C\, \Delta V_0^2 = 94\,\mathrm{mJ}$.
:::
