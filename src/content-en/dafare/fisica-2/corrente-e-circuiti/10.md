An ideal source with $\mathcal{E}_1 = 9.0\,\mathrm{V}$ in series with $R_1 = 1.0\,\Omega$ and an ideal source with $\mathcal{E}_2 = 3.0\,\mathrm{V}$ in series with $R_2 = 2.0\,\Omega$ are connected between the nodes $A$ and $B$ with the positive terminals toward $A$; $R_3 = 4.0\,\Omega$ is connected between the same nodes. Compute the currents in the three branches, with their directions.

:::soluzione
In the branch of $\mathcal{E}_1$, $3.0\,\mathrm{A}$ flows toward $A$; in $R_3$, $1.5\,\mathrm{A}$ flows from $A$ to $B$; in the branch of $\mathcal{E}_2$, $1.5\,\mathrm{A}$ flows from $A$ to $B$, therefore $\mathcal{E}_2$ absorbs power.
:::
