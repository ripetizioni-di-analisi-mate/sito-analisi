---
titolo: Charge transported by a constant current
teoria: [corrente-elettrica]
parole: [electric current, charge, electrons]
---

A wire carries a constant current of $0.80\,\mathrm{A}$ for $5.0$ minutes. Compute the charge that crosses a section of the wire and the corresponding number of electrons.

:::soluzione
$Q = 2.4 \cdot 10^{2}\,\mathrm{C}$, $N = \dfrac{Q}{e} = 1.5 \cdot 10^{21}$ electrons.
:::
