titolo: Length of a wire of known resistance
teoria: [resistenza-di-un-filo]
parole: [resistivity, resistance, copper wire]

A copper wire, with resistivity $1.7 \cdot 10^{-8}\,\Omega \cdot \mathrm{m}$ and diameter $0.50\,\mathrm{mm}$, has resistance $1.7\,\Omega$. Compute the length of the wire.

:::soluzione
$\ell = \dfrac{R A}{\rho} = \dfrac{\pi d^2 R}{4 \rho} \approx 20\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
