A source has electromotive force $6{,}0\,\mathrm{V}$ and, with its terminals connected by a wire of negligible resistance, delivers $3{,}0\,\mathrm{A}$. Compute the internal resistance, the load resistance that receives the maximum power and the value of that power.

:::soluzione
$r = 2{,}0\,\Omega$, $R = r = 2{,}0\,\Omega$, $P_{\max} = \dfrac{\mathcal{E}^2}{4r} = 4{,}5\,\mathrm{W}$.
:::
