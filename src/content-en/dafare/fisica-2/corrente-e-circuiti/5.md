An ideal source with $\mathcal{E} = 24\,\mathrm{V}$ supplies a $4{,}0\,\Omega$ resistor in series with the parallel combination of two resistors of $6{,}0\,\Omega$ and $12\,\Omega$. Compute the equivalent resistance, the current delivered and the current in the $12\,\Omega$ resistor.

:::soluzione
$R_{\text{eq}} = 8{,}0\,\Omega$, $I = 3{,}0\,\mathrm{A}$, current in the $12\,\Omega$ resistor equal to $1{,}0\,\mathrm{A}$.
:::
