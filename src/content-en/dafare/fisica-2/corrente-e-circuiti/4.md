A copper winding, with temperature coefficient $3.9 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$, has resistance $50.0\,\Omega$ at $20\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. While a motor is running its resistance becomes $57.8\,\Omega$. Compute the temperature of the winding.

:::soluzione
$T = 20\,^\circ\mathrm{C} + \dfrac{1}{\alpha} \left( \dfrac{R}{R_0} - 1 \right) = 60\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$.
:::
