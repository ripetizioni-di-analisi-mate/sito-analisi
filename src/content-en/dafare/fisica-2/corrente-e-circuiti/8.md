A battery with electromotive force $12\,\mathrm{V}$ and internal resistance $0.40\,\Omega$ supplies a $5.6\,\Omega$ resistor. Compute the current, the terminal voltage and the power dissipated inside the battery.

:::soluzione
$I = 2.0\,\mathrm{A}$, $\Delta V = 11.2\,\mathrm{V}$, $r I^2 = 1.6\,\mathrm{W}$.
:::
