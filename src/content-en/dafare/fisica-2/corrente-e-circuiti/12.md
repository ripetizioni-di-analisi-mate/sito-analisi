An uncharged $10\,\mu\mathrm{F}$ capacitor is charged through a $47\,\mathrm{k\Omega}$ resistor by an ideal source with $\mathcal{E} = 10\,\mathrm{V}$. Compute the time constant and the time after which the voltage across the capacitor is $6.0\,\mathrm{V}$.

:::soluzione
$\tau = 0.47\,\mathrm{s}$, $t = -\tau \ln\left( 1 - \dfrac{6.0}{10} \right) \approx 0.43\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
