A $2.2\,\mathrm{kW}$ electric heater operates at $230\,\mathrm{V}$ for $3.0$ hours. Compute the current drawn, the resistance of the heater and the energy consumed, in kilowatt-hours and in joules.

:::soluzione
$I \approx 9.6\,\mathrm{A}$, $R \approx 24\,\Omega$, $W = 6.6\,\mathrm{kWh} \approx 2.4 \cdot 10^{7}\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
