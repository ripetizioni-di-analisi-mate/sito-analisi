---
titolo: Frequency of an LC circuit
teoria: [circuito-lc]
parole: [LC circuit, natural frequency, inductance, capacitance]
---

An ideal oscillating circuit consists of an inductor of inductance $50\,\mathrm{mH}$ and a capacitor of capacitance $20\,\mathrm{nF}$. Compute the frequency of the oscillations.

:::soluzione
$f_0 = \dfrac{1}{2\pi\sqrt{LC}} \approx 5.0\,\mathrm{kHz}$.
:::
