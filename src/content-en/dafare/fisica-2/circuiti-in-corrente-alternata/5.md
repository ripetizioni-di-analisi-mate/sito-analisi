A capacitor of capacitance $47\,\mu\mathrm{F}$ is connected to the grid, with rms voltage $230\,\mathrm{V}$ and frequency $50\,\mathrm{Hz}$. Compute the rms current.

:::soluzione
$I_{\text{rms}} = 2\pi f C\, V_{\text{rms}} \approx 3.4\,\mathrm{A}$.
:::
