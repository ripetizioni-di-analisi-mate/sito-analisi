A series $RLC$ circuit has, at the frequency of the source, $R = 50\,\Omega$, $X_L = 150\,\Omega$ and $X_C = 30\,\Omega$, and is supplied with rms voltage $260\,\mathrm{V}$. Compute the impedance, the rms current and the phase shift.

:::soluzione
$Z = 130\,\Omega$, $I_{\text{eff}} = 2{,}0\,\mathrm{A}$, $\varphi = \arctan\dfrac{12}{5} \approx 67^\circ$.
:::
