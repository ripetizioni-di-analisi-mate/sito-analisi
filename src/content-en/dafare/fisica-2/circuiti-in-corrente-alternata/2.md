A capacitor of capacitance $5{,}0\,\mu\mathrm{F}$, charged to $20\,\mathrm{V}$, is connected to an inductor of inductance $0{,}20\,\mathrm{H}$. Neglecting the resistance of the circuit, compute the maximum value of the current.

:::soluzione
$I_0 = \Delta V \sqrt{\dfrac{C}{L}} = 0{,}10\,\mathrm{A}$.
:::
