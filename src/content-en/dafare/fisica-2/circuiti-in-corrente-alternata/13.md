---
titolo: Damped angular frequency and energy decay
teoria: [circuito-rlc-libero, energia-rlc]
parole: [damping, damped angular frequency, energy, time constant]
---

A free $RLC$ circuit has $L = 50\,\mathrm{mH}$, $C = 2.0\,\mu\mathrm{F}$ and $R = 100\,\Omega$. Compute the angular frequency of the damped oscillations and the time after which the energy of the circuit has decreased by a factor $e$, in the approximation $U(t) \approx U_0\,e^{-\frac{R}{L} t}$.

:::soluzione
$\omega_s = \sqrt{\dfrac{1}{LC} - \dfrac{R^2}{4L^2}} = 3.0 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{rad/s}$, $\dfrac{L}{R} = 0.50\,\mathrm{ms}$.
:::
