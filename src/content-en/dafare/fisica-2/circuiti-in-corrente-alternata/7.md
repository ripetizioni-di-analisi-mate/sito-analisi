An $80\,\Omega$ resistor and an ideal $0.30\,\mathrm{H}$ inductor are connected in series to the grid, at $230\,\mathrm{V}$ rms and $50\,\mathrm{Hz}$. Compute the impedance, the rms current and the phase shift between voltage and current.

:::soluzione
$Z \approx 124\,\Omega$, $I_{\text{rms}} \approx 1.9\,\mathrm{A}$, $\varphi \approx 50^\circ$ (current lagging).
:::
