An ideal inductor of inductance $0.15\,\mathrm{H}$ is connected to a source with rms voltage $120\,\mathrm{V}$ and frequency $60\,\mathrm{Hz}$. Compute the inductive reactance and the rms current.

:::soluzione
$X_L = 2\pi f L \approx 57\,\Omega$, $I_{\text{rms}} \approx 2.1\,\mathrm{A}$.
:::
