titolo: Rms value and frequency of a current
teoria: [grandezza-alternata, efficace-sinusoidale]

---

In a circuit the current is $i(t) = 2.5 \sin(100\pi\, t)\,\mathrm{A}$, with $t$ in seconds. Compute the rms value and the frequency of the current.

:::soluzione
$I_{\text{rms}} = \dfrac{2.5}{\sqrt{2}}\,\mathrm{A} \approx 1.8\,\mathrm{A}$, $f = 50\,\mathrm{Hz}$.
:::
