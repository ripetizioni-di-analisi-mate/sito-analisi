---
titolo: Frequenza e corrente di risonanza
teoria: [risonanza-rlc]
parole: [risonanza, frequenza di risonanza, corrente massima]
---

A series $RLC$ circuit has $R = 20\,\Omega$, $L = 0{,}40\,\mathrm{H}$ and $C = 25\,\mu\mathrm{F}$, and is supplied by a source of rms voltage $10\,\mathrm{V}$ and adjustable frequency. Compute the resonant frequency and the rms current at resonance.

:::soluzione
$f_0 = \dfrac{1}{2\pi\sqrt{LC}} \approx 50\,\mathrm{Hz}$, $I_{\text{eff}} = \dfrac{V_{\text{eff}}}{R} = 0{,}50\,\mathrm{A}$.
:::
