The primary of an ideal transformer has $500$ turns and is connected to an rms voltage of $230\,\mathrm{V}$; the secondary has $2500$ turns and supplies a resistive load that draws the rms current $0.20\,\mathrm{A}$. Compute the rms voltage at the secondary and the rms current in the primary.

:::soluzione
$V_2 = 1150\,\mathrm{V}$, $I_1 = 1.0\,\mathrm{A}$.
:::
