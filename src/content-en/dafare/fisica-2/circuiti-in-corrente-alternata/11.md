A series $RLC$ circuit has $R = 10\,\Omega$, $L = 1.0\,\mathrm{mH}$ and $C = 10\,\mathrm{nF}$. Compute the resonant angular frequency, the quality factor and the width of the bandwidth in angular frequency.

:::soluzione
$\omega_0 \approx 3.2 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{rad/s}$, $Q \approx 32$, $\Delta\omega = \dfrac{R}{L} = 1.0 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
:::
