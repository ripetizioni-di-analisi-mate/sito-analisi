---
titolo: Capacitance for a given frequency
teoria: [circuito-lc]
parole: [LC circuit, capacitance, natural frequency]
---

Compute the capacitance to be connected to an inductor of inductance $10\,\mathrm{mH}$ so that the $LC$ circuit oscillates at the frequency of $1.0\,\mathrm{kHz}$.

:::soluzione
$C = \dfrac{1}{4\pi^2 f_0^2 L} \approx 2.5\,\mu\mathrm{F}$.
:::
