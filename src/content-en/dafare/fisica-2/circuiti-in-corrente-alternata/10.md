A load connected to the grid at $230\,\mathrm{V}$ rms draws the rms current $5.0\,\mathrm{A}$ with power factor $0.80$. Compute the average power absorbed.

:::soluzione
$P = V_{\text{rms}} I_{\text{rms}} \cos\varphi = 920\,\mathrm{W}$.
:::
