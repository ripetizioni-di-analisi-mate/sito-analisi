Compute the cube roots of
$$
w = -27i .
$$

:::soluzione
$$
z_0 = \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2} - \frac{3}{2}\, i, \qquad
z_1 = 3i, \qquad
z_2 = -\frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2} - \frac{3}{2}\, i
$$
:::
