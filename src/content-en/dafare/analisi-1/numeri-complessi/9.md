Solve in $\mathbb{C}$ the equation
$$
z^4 - 2z^2 - 8 = 0 .
$$

:::soluzione
Setting $t = z^2$ one obtains $t = 4$ or $t = -2$, so the solutions are $z = \pm 2$ and $z = \pm i\sqrt{2}$.
:::
