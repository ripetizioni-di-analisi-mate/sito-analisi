titolo: Real factorization of a polynomial with a known complex root
teoria: [radici-coniugate, fattorizzazione-reale, teorema-fondamentale-algebra]
parole: [real factorization, conjugate roots, polynomial]
---

Given that $z = i$ is a root of the polynomial
$$
p(z) = z^3 - 2z^2 + z - 2 ,
$$
determine all the roots and the factorization of $p$ into real factors.

:::soluzione
The roots are $i$, $-i$ and $2$; the real factorization is $p(z) = (z - 2)(z^2 + 1)$.
:::
