Solve in $\mathbb{C}$ the equation
$$
|z|^2 + z - \bar{z} = 9 + 6i .
$$

:::soluzione
Setting $z = x + iy$ the equation becomes $x^2 + y^2 + 2iy = 9 + 6i$, from which $y = 3$ and $x = 0$: the only solution is $z = 3i$.
:::
