Solve in $\mathbb{C}$ the equation
$$
z^2 - 4z + 29 = 0 .
$$

:::soluzione
$\Delta = 16 - 116 = -100$, so $z = \dfrac{4 \pm 10i}{2} = 2 \pm 5i$.
:::
