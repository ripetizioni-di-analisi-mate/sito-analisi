---
titolo: Locus defined by the modulus
teoria: [piano-di-gauss, modulo]
parole: [locus, circle, distance]
---

Describe the locus of the points $z \in \mathbb{C}$ such that
$$
|z + 1 - 2i| = 3 .
$$

:::soluzione
The equation is equivalent to $|z - (-1 + 2i)| = 3$: the locus is the circle with center $(-1, 2)$ and radius $3$.
:::
