---
titolo: Power with De Moivre's formula
teoria: [de-moivre, forma-trigonometrica]
parole: [power, De Moivre, argument]
---

Compute
$$
(1 - i)^{10} .
$$

:::soluzione
$1 - i = \sqrt{2}\, e^{-i \frac{\pi}{4}}$, so $(1 - i)^{10} = 2^5 \, e^{-i \frac{5\pi}{2}} = 32\, e^{-i \frac{\pi}{2}} = -32i$.
:::
