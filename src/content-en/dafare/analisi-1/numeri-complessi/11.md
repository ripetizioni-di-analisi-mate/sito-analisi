titolo: Equation with the conjugate
teoria: [equazioni-in-c, coniugato]

---

Solve in $\mathbb{C}$ the equation
$$
2z + 3\bar{z} = 10 - i .
$$

:::soluzione
Setting $z = x + iy$ the equation becomes $5x - iy = 10 - i$, so $x = 2$, $y = 1$ and $z = 2 + i$.
:::
