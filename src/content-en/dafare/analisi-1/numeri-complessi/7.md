---
titolo: Radici quarte di un numero reale positivo
teoria: [radici-n-esime, radici-unita]
parole: [radici quarte, radici n-esime, poligono regolare]
---

Solve in $\mathbb{C}$ the equation
$$
z^4 = 16
$$
and state which polygon the solutions form in the complex plane.

:::soluzione
The solutions are $2$, $2i$, $-2$, $-2i$: the vertices of a square inscribed in the circle centered at the origin with radius $2$, with its vertices on the axes.
:::
