---
titolo: Differentiability of a function with absolute value
teoria: [punti-di-non-derivabilita, derivate-laterali]
parole: [absolute value, corner point]
---

Find the points at which the function
$$
f(x) = \bigl| x^2 - 4 \bigr|
$$
is not differentiable and classify them.

:::soluzione
$f$ is differentiable on $\mathbb{R} \setminus \{-2, 2\}$. At $x = -2$ the one-sided derivatives equal $-4$ and $4$, at $x = 2$ they equal $-4$ and $4$: both points are corner points.
:::
