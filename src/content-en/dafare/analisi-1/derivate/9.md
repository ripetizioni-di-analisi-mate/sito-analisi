titolo: Verifying Lagrange's theorem
teoria: [teorema-di-lagrange]
parole: [Lagrange's theorem, Lagrange point, mean value]
---

Verify that $f(x) = x^2$ satisfies the hypotheses of Lagrange's theorem on the interval $[1, 3]$ and find the points $c$ given by the theorem.

:::soluzione
The hypotheses are satisfied since $f$ is a polynomial; the only point is
$$
c = 2 .
$$
:::
