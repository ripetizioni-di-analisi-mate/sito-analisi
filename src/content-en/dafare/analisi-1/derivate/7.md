Determine $a \in \mathbb{R}$ so that the function
$$
f(x) =
\begin{cases}
\sin x & \text{se } x \leq 0 \\[4pt]
x^2 + a x & \text{se } x > 0
\end{cases}
$$
is differentiable at $x = 0$.

:::soluzione
$$
a = 1
$$
:::
