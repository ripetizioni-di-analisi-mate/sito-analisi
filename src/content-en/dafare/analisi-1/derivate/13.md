titolo: Limit with Taylor's formula
teoria: [formula-di-taylor, sviluppi-di-mclaurin, algebra-degli-o-piccolo]

---

Compute
$$
\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{x - \ln(1 + x) - \dfrac{x^2}{2}}{x^3} .
$$

:::soluzione
$$
\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{x - \ln(1 + x) - \dfrac{x^2}{2}}{x^3} = -\frac{1}{3}
$$
:::
