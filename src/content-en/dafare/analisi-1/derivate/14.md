Write the Maclaurin polynomial of order $2$ of
$$
f(x) = \sqrt{1 + 2x}
$$
and use the Lagrange remainder to bound the error made when approximating $f\!\left( \dfrac{1}{10} \right)$ with the polynomial.

:::soluzione
$$
T_2(x) = 1 + x - \frac{x^2}{2} , \qquad
\left| f\!\left( \frac{1}{10} \right) - T_2\!\left( \frac{1}{10} \right) \right| \leq \frac{1}{2000}
$$
:::
