Find the intervals of monotonicity and the local extrema of
$$
f(x) = x^4 - 4x^3 .
$$

:::soluzione
$f$ is strictly decreasing on $(-\infty, 3]$ and strictly increasing on $[3, +\infty)$; the point $x = 3$ is an absolute minimum point with $f(3) = -27$. The stationary point $x = 0$ is not an extremum.
:::
