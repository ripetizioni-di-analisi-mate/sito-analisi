---
titolo: Points with a horizontal tangent
teoria: [retta-tangente, derivate-elementari, regole-di-derivazione]
parole: [horizontal tangent, stationary points]
---

Find the points of the graph of
$$
f(x) = x\,e^{-x}
$$
at which the tangent line is horizontal and write the corresponding tangents.

:::soluzione
The only point is $\left( 1, \dfrac{1}{e} \right)$; the tangent is the line $y = \dfrac{1}{e}$.
:::
