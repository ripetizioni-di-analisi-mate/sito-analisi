---
titolo: One-sided limits at a point
teoria: [limite-destro-sinistro, limite-infinito]
parole: [one-sided limits, infinity]
---

Compute the right-hand and left-hand limits as $x \to 1$ of
$$
f(x) = \frac{x + 2}{x^2 - 1}
$$
and determine whether the limit as $x \to 1$ exists.

:::soluzione
$$
\lim_{x \to 1^{+}} \frac{x + 2}{x^2 - 1} = +\infty, \qquad
\lim_{x \to 1^{-}} \frac{x + 2}{x^2 - 1} = -\infty
$$
The limit as $x \to 1$ does not exist, since the one-sided limits are different.
:::
