---
titolo: Difference of roots at infinity
teoria: [forme-indeterminate, limite-infinito]
parole: [infinity minus infinity, rationalization]
---

Compute
$$
\lim_{x \to +\infty} \left( \sqrt{x^2 + 2x} - \sqrt{x^2 - x} \right) .
$$

:::soluzione
$$
\lim_{x \to +\infty} \left( \sqrt{x^2 + 2x} - \sqrt{x^2 - x} \right) = \frac{3}{2}
$$
:::
