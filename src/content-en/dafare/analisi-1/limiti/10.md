---
titolo: Asymptotes of a rational function
teoria: [definizione-asintoti]
parole: [vertical asymptote, oblique asymptote]
---

Find the asymptotes of
$$
f(x) = \frac{2x^2 + 1}{x + 1} .
$$

:::soluzione
Vertical asymptote $x = -1$; oblique asymptote $y = 2x - 2$, both as $x \to +\infty$ and as $x \to -\infty$. No horizontal asymptote.
:::
