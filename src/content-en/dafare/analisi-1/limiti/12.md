---
titolo: Classifying two discontinuities
teoria: [discontinuita]
parole: [discontinuity, jump, removable]
---

Classify the point $x = 0$ for $f(x) = \dfrac{|x|}{x}$ and the point $x = 1$ for $g(x) = \dfrac{x^2 - 1}{x - 1}$.

:::soluzione
For $f$ the point $0$ is a jump discontinuity: the one-sided limits equal $1$ and $-1$. For $g$ the point $1$ is a removable discontinuity: the limit equals $2$ and $g(1)$ is not defined.
:::
