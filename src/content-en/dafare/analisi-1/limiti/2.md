---
titolo: Sign of a function near a point
teoria: [permanenza-del-segno]
parole: [sign preservation]
---

Let $f(x) = \dfrac{x^2 + 1}{x - 3}$. Determine whether there exists a neighborhood of $x_0 = 5$ in which $f$ is positive, and explain which theorem guarantees it.

:::soluzione
Yes. We have $\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 5} f(x) = \frac{26}{2} = 13 > 0$, therefore by the sign-preservation theorem there exists a neighborhood of $5$ in which $f(x) > 0$.
:::
