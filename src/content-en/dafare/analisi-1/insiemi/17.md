---
titolo: Divisibility by 4 of a difference of powers
teoria: [induzione]
parole: [induction, divisibility]
---

Prove by induction that $5^n - 1$ is divisible by $4$ for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$.

:::soluzione
Base case: for $n = 0$ one obtains $0 = 4 \cdot 0$. Step: $5^{n+1} - 1 = 5\,(5^n - 1) + 4$; if $5^n - 1 = 4m$, then $5^{n+1} - 1 = 4\,(5m + 1)$.
:::
