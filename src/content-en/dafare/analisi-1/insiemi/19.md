---
titolo: Coefficient of a term in a binomial expansion
teoria: [coefficiente-binomiale, binomio-di-newton]
parole: [binomial theorem, coefficient, expansion]
---

Determine the coefficient of $x^3$ in the expansion of $(2x - 1)^5$.

:::soluzione
The term with $x^3$ corresponds to $k = 2$ and equals $\dbinom{5}{2} (2x)^3 (-1)^2 = 80x^3$: the coefficient is $80$.
:::
