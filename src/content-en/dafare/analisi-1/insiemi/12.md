---
titolo: Sum of the first n cubes
teoria: [induzione, somma-dei-primi-n]
parole: [induction, sum, cubes]
---

Prove by induction that, for every $n \geq 1$,
$$
\sum_{k=1}^{n} k^3 = \frac{n^2 (n+1)^2}{4} .
$$

:::soluzione
Base case: for $n = 1$ both sides equal $1$. Step: $\dfrac{n^2(n+1)^2}{4} + (n+1)^3 = \dfrac{(n+1)^2 \bigl( n^2 + 4(n+1) \bigr)}{4} = \dfrac{(n+1)^2 (n+2)^2}{4}$.
:::
