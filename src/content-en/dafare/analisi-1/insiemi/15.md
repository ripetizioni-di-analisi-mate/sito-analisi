---
titolo: Inequality between a factorial and a power of 2
teoria: [induzione, fattoriale]
parole: [induction, inequality, factorial]
---

Prove by induction that $n! \geq 2^{\,n-1}$ for every $n \geq 1$.

:::soluzione
Base case: for $n = 1$ we have $1 \geq 1$. Step: $(n+1)! = (n+1) \cdot n! \geq 2 \cdot 2^{\,n-1} = 2^n$, since $n + 1 \geq 2$.
:::
