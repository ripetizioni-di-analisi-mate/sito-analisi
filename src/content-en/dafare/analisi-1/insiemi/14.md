---
titolo: Inequality between a power and a successor
teoria: [induzione, bernoulli]
parole: [induction, inequality, powers]
---

Prove by induction that $2^n \geq n + 1$ for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$.

:::soluzione
Base case: for $n = 0$ we have $1 \geq 1$. Step: $2^{n+1} = 2 \cdot 2^n \geq 2(n + 1) = (n + 2) + n \geq n + 2$. The same inequality also follows from Bernoulli's inequality with $x = 1$.
:::
