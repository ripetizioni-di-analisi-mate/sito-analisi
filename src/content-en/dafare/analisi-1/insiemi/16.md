---
titolo: Inequality between powers with different bases
teoria: [induzione]
parole: [induction, inequality, powers]
---

Prove by induction that $3^n > 2^n + n$ for every $n \geq 2$.

:::soluzione
Base case: for $n = 2$ we have $9 > 6$. Step: $3^{n+1} = 3 \cdot 3^n > 3 \cdot 2^n + 3n \geq 2^{n+1} + n + 1$, since $3 \cdot 2^n \geq 2^{n+1}$ and $3n \geq n + 1$ for $n \geq 1$.
:::
