Given $A = \{x \in \mathbb{R} : |x| < 2\}$ and $B = \{x \in \mathbb{R} : x^2 \geq 1\}$, determine $A \cap B$ and $A \setminus B$.

:::soluzione
$$
A \cap B = (-2, -1] \cup [1, 2), \qquad A \setminus B = (-1, 1)
$$
:::
