---
titolo: Inverse of a linear fractional function
teoria: [iniettiva-suriettiva, funzione-inversa]
parole: [inverse function, injectivity, linear fractional function]
---

Verify that the function
$$
f(x) = \frac{x + 3}{x - 2} ,
$$
defined on $\mathbb{R} \setminus \{2\}$, is injective; determine its range and its inverse.

:::soluzione
The range is $\mathbb{R} \setminus \{1\}$ and the inverse is
$$
f^{-1}(y) = \frac{2y + 3}{y - 1} .
$$
:::
