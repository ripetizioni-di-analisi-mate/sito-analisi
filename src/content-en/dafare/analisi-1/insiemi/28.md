Sketch the graph of
$$
f(x) = 3 - (x + 1)^2
$$
starting from that of $y = x^2$, indicating the transformations used, the vertex and the range.

:::soluzione
From $x^2$: horizontal translation to the left by $1$, reflection across the $x$-axis, vertical translation upward by $3$. The vertex is $(-1, 3)$ and the range is $(-\infty, 3]$.
:::
