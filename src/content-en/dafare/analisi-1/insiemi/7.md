---
titolo: Bounds of a set with an alternating-sign term
teoria: [massimo-minimo, sup-inf]
parole: [alternating signs, supremum, infimum]
---

Determine the supremum, infimum, maximum and minimum of
$$
A = \left\{ (-1)^n \, \frac{n}{n+1} : n \in \mathbb{N},\ n \geq 1 \right\} .
$$

:::soluzione
$\sup A = 1$ and $\inf A = -1$; neither the maximum nor the minimum exists, because $1 \notin A$ and $-1 \notin A$.
:::
