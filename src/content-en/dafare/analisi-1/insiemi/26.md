Verify that the function
$$
f(x) = e^{2x} + 1
$$
is strictly increasing on $\mathbb{R}$; determine its range and its inverse.

:::soluzione
The range is $(1, +\infty)$ and the inverse is
$$
f^{-1}(y) = \frac{\ln(y - 1)}{2} .
$$
:::
