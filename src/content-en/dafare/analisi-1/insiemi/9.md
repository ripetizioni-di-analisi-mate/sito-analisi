---
titolo: Verifying an infimum with the characterization
teoria: [sup-inf, caratterizzazione-sup]
parole: [characterization, epsilon, infimum]
---

Prove with the characterization that
$$
\inf \left\{ \frac{3n + 1}{n} : n \in \mathbb{N},\ n \geq 1 \right\} = 3
$$
and determine whether the set has a minimum.

:::soluzione
From $\dfrac{3n + 1}{n} = 3 + \dfrac{1}{n} > 3$ it follows that $3$ is a lower bound; given $\varepsilon > 0$, the inequality $3 + \dfrac{1}{n} < 3 + \varepsilon$ is satisfied for $n > \dfrac{1}{\varepsilon}$. Hence $\inf A = 3$; the minimum does not exist, because $3 \notin A$.
:::
