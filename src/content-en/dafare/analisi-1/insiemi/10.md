---
titolo: Irrationality of the square root of 5
teoria: [insiemi-numerici, radice-di-due]
parole: [irrational, proof by contradiction]
---

Prove that $\sqrt{5}$ is irrational.

:::soluzione
Suppose, by contradiction, that $\sqrt{5} = \dfrac{m}{n}$ with the fraction in lowest terms; then $m^2 = 5n^2$, so $5$ divides $m^2$ and, since $5$ is prime, $5$ divides $m$. Setting $m = 5k$ one obtains $n^2 = 5k^2$, so $5$ also divides $n$, contradicting the assumption that the fraction is in lowest terms.
:::
