---
titolo: Verifying a supremum with the characterization
teoria: [sup-inf, caratterizzazione-sup]
parole: [characterization, epsilon, supremum]
---

Prove with the characterization that
$$
\sup \left\{ \frac{n}{n+2} : n \in \mathbb{N},\ n \geq 1 \right\} = 1
$$
and determine whether the set has a maximum.

:::soluzione
From $\dfrac{n}{n+2} = 1 - \dfrac{2}{n+2} < 1$ it follows that $1$ is an upper bound; given $\varepsilon > 0$, the inequality $1 - \dfrac{2}{n+2} > 1 - \varepsilon$ is satisfied for $n > \dfrac{2}{\varepsilon} - 2$. Hence $\sup A = 1$; the maximum does not exist, because $1 \notin A$.
:::
