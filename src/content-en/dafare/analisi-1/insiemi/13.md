---
titolo: Sum of the powers of 2
teoria: [induzione]
parole: [induction, geometric sum, powers]
---

Prove by induction that, for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$,
$$
\sum_{k=0}^{n} 2^k = 2^{n+1} - 1 .
$$

:::soluzione
Base case: for $n = 0$ both sides equal $1$. Step: $\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{n+1} 2^k = \bigl( 2^{n+1} - 1 \bigr) + 2^{n+1} = 2^{n+2} - 1$.
:::
