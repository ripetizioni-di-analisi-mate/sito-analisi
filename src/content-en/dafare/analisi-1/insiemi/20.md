Determine the natural number $n \geq 2$ such that
$$
\binom{n}{2} = 36 .
$$

:::soluzione
The equation $\dfrac{n(n-1)}{2} = 36$ is equivalent to $n(n-1) = 72$, which is satisfied by $n = 9$.
:::
