titolo: Domain with a root and a logarithm in the denominator

---

Determine the natural domain of
$$
f(x) = \frac{\sqrt{2 - x}}{\ln(x + 3)} .
$$

:::soluzione
$$
D = (-3, -2) \cup (-2, 2]
$$
:::
