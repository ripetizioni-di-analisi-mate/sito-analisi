---
titolo: Parity of three functions
teoria: [pari-dispari]
parole: [even function, odd function, symmetry]
---

Determine whether the following functions are even, odd, or neither even nor odd:
$$
f(x) = x\,|x|, \qquad
g(x) = \cos x + x^2, \qquad
h(x) = x^3 + 1 .
$$

:::soluzione
$f$ is odd, $g$ is even, $h$ is neither even nor odd, because $h(-1) = 0$ while $h(1) = 2$ and $-h(1) = -2$.
:::
