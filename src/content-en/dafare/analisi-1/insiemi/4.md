Given $A = \{1, 2\}$ and $B = \{0, 1\}$, write $A \times B$ and $B \times A$ by listing their elements, and determine whether the pair $(0, 1)$ belongs to each of the two sets.

:::soluzione
$A \times B = \{(1, 0), (1, 1), (2, 0), (2, 1)\}$ and $B \times A = \{(0, 1), (0, 2), (1, 1), (1, 2)\}$. The pair $(0, 1)$ does not belong to $A \times B$, because $0 \notin A$, while it belongs to $B \times A$.
:::
