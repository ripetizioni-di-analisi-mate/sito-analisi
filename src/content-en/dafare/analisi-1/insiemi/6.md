---
titolo: Bounds of a set defined by an increasing sequence
teoria: [massimo-minimo, sup-inf]
parole: [supremum, infimum, maximum, minimum]
---

Determine the supremum, infimum, maximum and minimum of
$$
A = \left\{ 3 - \frac{1}{n} : n \in \mathbb{N},\ n \geq 1 \right\} .
$$

:::soluzione
$\inf A = \min A = 2$, obtained with $n = 1$; $\sup A = 3$, which is not a maximum because $3 \notin A$.
:::
