---
titolo: Divisibility by 6 of a polynomial
teoria: [induzione]
parole: [induction, divisibility, polynomial]
---

Prove by induction that $n^3 + 5n$ is divisible by $6$ for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$.

:::soluzione
Base case: for $n = 0$ one obtains $0 = 6 \cdot 0$. Step: $(n+1)^3 + 5(n+1) = (n^3 + 5n) + 3n(n+1) + 6$; the product $n(n+1)$ is even, so $3n(n+1)$ is divisible by $6$, and a sum of multiples of $6$ is a multiple of $6$.
:::
