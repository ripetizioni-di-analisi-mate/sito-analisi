---

Study the function
$$
f(x) = x^2 \ln x
$$
and sketch its qualitative graph.

:::soluzione
Domain $(0, +\infty)$; $f(x) \to 0^-$ as $x \to 0^+$, therefore no vertical asymptote, and $f(x) \to +\infty$ as $x \to +\infty$ without an oblique asymptote. Relative and absolute minimum at $x = e^{-1/2}$ with $f\!\left(e^{-1/2}\right) = -\dfrac{1}{2e}$. Inflection point at $x = e^{-3/2}$ with $f\!\left(e^{-3/2}\right) = -\dfrac{3}{2e^3}$, zero of $f''(x) = 2\ln x + 3$.
:::
