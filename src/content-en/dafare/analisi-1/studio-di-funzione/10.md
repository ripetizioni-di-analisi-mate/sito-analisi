Study the function
$$
f(x) = \ln x - x
$$
and sketch its qualitative graph.

:::soluzione
Domain $(0, +\infty)$; vertical asymptote $x = 0$, with $f(x) \to -\infty$, and $f(x) \to -\infty$ as $x \to +\infty$ without an oblique asymptote, because $\dfrac{f(x)}{x} \to -1$ but $f(x) + x = \ln x \to +\infty$. Only stationary point $x = 1$, relative and absolute maximum with $f(1) = -1$. $f''(x) = -\dfrac{1}{x^2} < 0$, therefore concave and without inflection points.
:::
