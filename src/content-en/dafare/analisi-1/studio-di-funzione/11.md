---
titolo: Massimo e minimo assoluti su un intervallo chiuso
teoria: [massimi-minimi-assoluti, test-di-monotonia]
parole: [massimo assoluto, minimo assoluto, Weierstrass, intervallo chiuso]
---

Determine the absolute maximum and minimum of the function
$$
f(x) = x^3 - 6x^2 + 9x + 1
$$
on the interval $[0, 5]$.

:::soluzione
The interior stationary points are $x = 1$ and $x = 3$; the comparison of the values
$$
f(0) = 1, \qquad f(1) = 5, \qquad f(3) = 1, \qquad f(5) = 21
$$
gives an absolute maximum $21$, attained at $x = 5$, and an absolute minimum $1$, attained both at $x = 0$ and at $x = 3$.
:::
