Study the function
$$
f(x) = \arctan x - \frac{x}{2}
$$
and sketch its qualitative graph.

:::soluzione
Domain $\mathbb{R}$, odd function; oblique asymptotes $y = -\dfrac{x}{2} + \dfrac{\pi}{2}$ as $x \to +\infty$ and $y = -\dfrac{x}{2} - \dfrac{\pi}{2}$ as $x \to -\infty$. Relative minimum at $x = -1$ with $f(-1) = \dfrac{1}{2} - \dfrac{\pi}{4}$, relative maximum at $x = 1$ with $f(1) = \dfrac{\pi}{4} - \dfrac{1}{2}$. Inflection point at the origin, because $f''(x) = -\dfrac{2x}{(1 + x^2)^2}$ changes sign at $0$.
:::
