Study the function
$$
f(x) = \frac{x^2 - 3}{x - 2}
$$
and sketch its qualitative graph.

:::soluzione
Domain $(-\infty, 2) \cup (2, +\infty)$; vertical asymptote $x = 2$, oblique asymptote $y = x + 2$ on both sides. Relative maximum at $x = 1$ with $f(1) = 2$, relative minimum at $x = 3$ with $f(3) = 6$. $f''(x) = \dfrac{2}{(x - 2)^3}$, therefore concave for $x < 2$, convex for $x > 2$, no inflection points.
:::
