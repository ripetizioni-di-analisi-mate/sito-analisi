---
titolo: Convexity and inflection points of a polynomial
teoria: [convessita, convessita-derivata-seconda, punti-di-flesso]
parole: [convexity, concavity, inflection points, second derivative]
---

Determine on which intervals the function
$$
f(x) = x^4 - 4x^3 + 10
$$
is convex and on which it is concave, and find its inflection points.

:::soluzione
$f''(x) = 12x^2 - 24x = 12x(x - 2)$, therefore $f$ is convex on $(-\infty, 0]$ and on $[2, +\infty)$, concave on $[0, 2]$. The inflection points are $x = 0$ with $f(0) = 10$ and $x = 2$ with $f(2) = -6$.
:::
