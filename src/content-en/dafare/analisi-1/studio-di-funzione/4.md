titolo: Study of a function with an exponential and an oblique asymptote

---

Study the function
$$
f(x) = x\,e^{1/x}
$$
and sketch its qualitative graph.

:::soluzione
Domain $(-\infty, 0) \cup (0, +\infty)$; $f(x) \to +\infty$ as $x \to 0^+$ and $f(x) \to 0^-$ as $x \to 0^-$, therefore $x = 0$ is a vertical asymptote only from the right; oblique asymptote $y = x + 1$ on both sides. Relative minimum at $x = 1$ with $f(1) = e$, no other stationary point. $f''(x) = \dfrac{e^{1/x}}{x^3}$, therefore concave for $x < 0$, convex for $x > 0$, no inflection points.
:::
