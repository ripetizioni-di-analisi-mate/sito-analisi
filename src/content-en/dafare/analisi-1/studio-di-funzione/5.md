titolo: Study of an even function with a logarithm
teoria: [schema-dello-studio, criterio-derivata-seconda, flessi-derivata-seconda]

---

Study the function
$$
f(x) = \ln\!\left(x^2 + 1\right)
$$
and sketch its qualitative graph.

:::soluzione
Domain $\mathbb{R}$, even function, $f(x) \geq 0$ with zero only at $0$; $f(x) \to +\infty$ as $x \to \pm\infty$, without asymptotes. Only stationary point $x = 0$, relative and absolute minimum with $f(0) = 0$. Inflection points at $x = \pm 1$ with $f(\pm 1) = \ln 2$, because $f''(x) = \dfrac{2(1 - x^2)}{(x^2 + 1)^2}$.
:::
