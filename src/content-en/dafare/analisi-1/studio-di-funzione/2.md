Study the function
$$
f(x) = \frac{x}{x^2 + 1}
$$
and sketch its qualitative graph.

:::soluzione
Domain $\mathbb{R}$, odd function; horizontal asymptote $y = 0$ on both sides. Relative minimum at $x = -1$ with $f(-1) = -\dfrac{1}{2}$, relative maximum at $x = 1$ with $f(1) = \dfrac{1}{2}$, both absolute. Inflection points at $x = 0$ and at $x = \pm\sqrt{3}$, with $f(\pm\sqrt{3}) = \pm\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4}$.
:::
