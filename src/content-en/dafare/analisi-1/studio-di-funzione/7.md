titolo: Study of a function with a root
teoria: [schema-dello-studio, massimi-minimi-relativi, criterio-derivata-prima]

---

Study the function
$$
f(x) = x \sqrt{x + 3}
$$
and sketch its qualitative graph.

:::soluzione
Domain $[-3, +\infty)$, zeros at $-3$ and $0$; no asymptote, vertical tangent at $x = -3$. Only stationary point $x = -2$, relative and absolute minimum with $f(-2) = -2$; decreasing on $[-3, -2]$, increasing on $[-2, +\infty)$. $f''(x) = \dfrac{3(x + 4)}{4(x + 3)^{\frac{3}{2}}} > 0$ on the domain, therefore convex and without inflection points.
:::
