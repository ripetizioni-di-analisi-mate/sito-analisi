---
titolo: Study of a function with an absolute value
teoria: [schema-dello-studio, criterio-derivata-prima, punti-di-flesso]
parole: [absolute value, corner point, exponential]
---

Study the function
$$
f(x) = |x - 1|\,e^{x}
$$
and sketch its qualitative graph.

:::soluzione
Domain $\mathbb{R}$, $f(x) \geq 0$ with zero only at $1$; horizontal asymptote $y = 0$ as $x \to -\infty$. Relative maximum at $x = 0$ with $f(0) = 1$; corner point at $x = 1$, relative and absolute minimum with $f(1) = 0$, with one-sided derivatives $-e$ and $e$. Inflection point at $x = -1$ with $f(-1) = \dfrac{2}{e}$; the change of concavity at $x = 1$ is not an inflection point, because at the corner point the tangent line does not exist.
:::
