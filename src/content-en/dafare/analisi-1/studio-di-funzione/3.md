---

Study the function
$$
f(x) = x^2 e^{x}
$$
and sketch its qualitative graph.

:::soluzione
Domain $\mathbb{R}$; horizontal asymptote $y = 0$ as $x \to -\infty$, no asymptote as $x \to +\infty$. Relative maximum at $x = -2$ with $f(-2) = \dfrac{4}{e^2}$, relative minimum at $x = 0$ with $f(0) = 0$, which is also the absolute minimum. Inflection points at $x = -2 - \sqrt{2}$ and $x = -2 + \sqrt{2}$, zeros of $f''(x) = (x^2 + 4x + 2)\,e^{x}$.
:::
