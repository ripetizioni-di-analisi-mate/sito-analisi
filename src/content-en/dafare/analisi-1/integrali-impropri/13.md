---
titolo: Integral with a parameter and critical point at zero
teoria: [confronto-asintotico, integrali-campione, ordine-di-infinitesimo]
parole: [parameter, standard limit, finite critical point]
---

Determine for which $\alpha \in \mathbb{R}$ the following integral converges:
$$
\int_0^{1} \frac{e^{x} - 1}{x^{\alpha}}\,dx .
$$

:::soluzione
The integral converges if and only if $\alpha < 2$; since $e^{x} - 1 \sim x$ as $x \to 0$, the integrand is asymptotic to $\dfrac{1}{x^{\alpha - 1}}$, and the benchmark at $0$ converges for $\alpha - 1 < 1$.
:::
