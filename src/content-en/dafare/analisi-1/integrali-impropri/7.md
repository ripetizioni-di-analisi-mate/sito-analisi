titolo: Behavior of a rational integral at infinity
teoria: [confronto-asintotico, integrali-campione]
parole: [limit comparison, rational function, behavior]
---

Determine the behavior of
$$
\int_1^{+\infty} \frac{x^{2} + 1}{x^{4} + x + 1}\,dx .
$$

:::soluzione
The integral converges, because the integrand is positive and asymptotic to $\dfrac{1}{x^{2}}$ as $x \to +\infty$, and the benchmark with $\alpha = 2$ converges.
:::
