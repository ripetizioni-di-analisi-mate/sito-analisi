---
titolo: Behavior of an integral with a square root at infinity
teoria: [confronto-asintotico, integrali-campione]
parole: [square root, limit comparison, behavior]
---

Determine the behavior of
$$
\int_1^{+\infty} \frac{dx}{x + \sqrt{x}} .
$$

:::soluzione
The integral diverges, because the integrand is positive and asymptotic to $\dfrac{1}{x}$ as $x \to +\infty$, and the benchmark with $\alpha = 1$ diverges.
:::
