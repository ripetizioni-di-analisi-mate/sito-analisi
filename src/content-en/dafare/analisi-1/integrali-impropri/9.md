---
titolo: Behavior of an integral with two critical points
teoria: [piu-punti-critici, confronto-asintotico, integrali-campione]
parole: [two critical points, splitting the integral, behavior]
---

Determine the behavior of
$$
\int_0^{+\infty} \frac{dx}{x^{\frac{1}{3}}\,(1 + x)} .
$$

:::soluzione
The integral converges. Split at $1$: near $0$ the integrand is asymptotic to $\dfrac{1}{x^{\frac{1}{3}}}$, with order $\dfrac{1}{3} < 1$; at infinity it is asymptotic to $\dfrac{1}{x^{\frac{4}{3}}}$, with order $\dfrac{4}{3} > 1$, therefore both pieces converge.
:::
