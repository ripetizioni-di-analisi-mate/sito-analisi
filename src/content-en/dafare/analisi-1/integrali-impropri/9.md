---
titolo: Carattere di un integrale con due punti critici
teoria: [piu-punti-critici, confronto-asintotico, integrali-campione]
parole: [due punti critici, spezzare l'integrale, carattere]
---

Determine whether the following integral converges or diverges:
$$
\int_0^{+\infty} \frac{dx}{x^{\frac{1}{3}}\,(1 + x)} .
$$

:::soluzione
The integral converges. Split at $1$: near $0$ the integrand is asymptotically equivalent to $\dfrac{1}{x^{\frac{1}{3}}}$, with blow-up order $\dfrac{1}{3} < 1$; at infinity it is asymptotically equivalent to $\dfrac{1}{x^{\frac{4}{3}}}$, with decay order $\dfrac{4}{3} > 1$, therefore both pieces converge.
:::
