titolo: Behavior via the standard limit of the exponential
teoria: [confronto-asintotico, integrali-campione, ordine-di-infinitesimo]

---

Determine the behavior of
$$
\int_0^{1} \frac{e^{2x} - 1}{x^{2}}\,dx .
$$

:::soluzione
The integral diverges, because $e^{2x} - 1 \sim 2x$ as $x \to 0$ and the integrand is asymptotic to $\dfrac{2}{x}$, with a divergent benchmark.
:::
