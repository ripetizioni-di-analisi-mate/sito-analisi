---
titolo: Integral with the logarithm in the denominator
teoria: [integrale-di-funzione-illimitata, campione-logaritmico]
parole: [logarithm, critical point, antiderivative]
---

Compute
$$
\int_0^{1/2} \frac{dx}{x \ln^{2} x} .
$$

:::soluzione
The integral converges and equals $\dfrac{1}{\ln 2}$; an antiderivative is $-\dfrac{1}{\ln x}$, which tends to $0$ as $x \to 0^{+}$ and at $\dfrac{1}{2}$ equals $\dfrac{1}{\ln 2}$.
:::
