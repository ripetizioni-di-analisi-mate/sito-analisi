---
titolo: Integral with a parameter over a half-line
teoria: [confronto-asintotico, integrali-campione]
parole: [parameter, behavior as alpha varies, benchmark]
---

Determine for which $\alpha \in \mathbb{R}$ the following integral converges:
$$
\int_1^{+\infty} \frac{dx}{x^{\alpha}\,(1 + x^{2})} .
$$

:::soluzione
The integral converges if and only if $\alpha > -1$; the integrand is asymptotic to $\dfrac{1}{x^{\alpha + 2}}$ as $x \to +\infty$ and the benchmark converges for $\alpha + 2 > 1$.
:::
