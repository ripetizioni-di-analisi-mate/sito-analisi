Determine whether the following integral converges or diverges:
$$
\int_1^{+\infty} \frac{\cos x}{x^{3}}\,dx .
$$

:::soluzione
The integral converges, because it converges absolutely: $\left| \dfrac{\cos x}{x^{3}} \right| \leq \dfrac{1}{x^{3}}$ and the comparison integral with $\alpha = 3$ converges.
:::
