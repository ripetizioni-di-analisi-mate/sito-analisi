---
titolo: Carattere con il limite notevole del coseno
teoria: [confronto-asintotico, integrali-campione, ordine-di-infinitesimo]
parole: [limite notevole, coseno, ordine di infinito]
---

Determine whether the following integral converges or diverges:
$$
\int_0^{1} \frac{1 - \cos x}{x^{\frac{7}{2}}}\,dx .
$$

:::soluzione
The integral diverges, because $1 - \cos x \sim \dfrac{x^{2}}{2}$ as $x \to 0$ and the integrand is asymptotically equivalent to $\dfrac{1}{2\,x^{\frac{3}{2}}}$, with blow-up order $\dfrac{3}{2} \geq 1$ at the singular point $0$.
:::
