---
titolo: Behavior via the standard limit of the cosine
teoria: [confronto-asintotico, integrali-campione, ordine-di-infinitesimo]
parole: [standard limit, cosine, order of infinity]
---

Determine the behavior of
$$
\int_0^{1} \frac{1 - \cos x}{x^{\frac{7}{2}}}\,dx .
$$

:::soluzione
The integral diverges, because $1 - \cos x \sim \dfrac{x^{2}}{2}$ as $x \to 0$ and the integrand is asymptotic to $\dfrac{1}{2\,x^{\frac{3}{2}}}$, with order of infinity $\dfrac{3}{2} \geq 1$ at the critical point $0$.
:::
