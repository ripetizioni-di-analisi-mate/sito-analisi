---
titolo: Integral of an exponential over a half-line
teoria: [integrale-su-intervallo-illimitato]
parole: [exponential, antiderivative, convergent]
---

Compute
$$
\int_0^{+\infty} e^{-3x}\,dx .
$$

:::soluzione
The integral converges and equals $\dfrac{1}{3}$, since $\displaystyle\int_0^{t} e^{-3x}\,dx = \frac{1}{3} - \frac{e^{-3t}}{3} \to \frac{1}{3}$.
:::
