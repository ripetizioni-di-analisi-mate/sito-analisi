---
titolo: Integral of a power over a half-line
teoria: [integrale-su-intervallo-illimitato, integrali-campione]
parole: [unbounded interval, power, antiderivative]
---

Compute
$$
\int_2^{+\infty} \frac{dx}{x^{4}} .
$$

:::soluzione
The integral converges and equals $\dfrac{1}{24}$, since $\displaystyle\int_2^{t} \frac{dx}{x^{4}} = \frac{1}{24} - \frac{1}{3t^{3}} \to \frac{1}{24}$.
:::
