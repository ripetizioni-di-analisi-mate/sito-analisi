---
titolo: Integral over a half-line by substitution
teoria: [integrale-su-intervallo-illimitato]
parole: [substitution, exponential, antiderivative]
---

Compute
$$
\int_0^{+\infty} x\,e^{-x^{2}}\,dx .
$$

:::soluzione
The integral converges and equals $\dfrac{1}{2}$; an antiderivative of the integrand is $-\dfrac{e^{-x^{2}}}{2}$ and $e^{-t^{2}} \to 0$ as $t \to +\infty$.
:::
