Find the general solution of
$$
y'' + 4y = 8 \sin(2x) .
$$

:::soluzione
$y = C_1 \cos(2x) + C_2 \sin(2x) - 2x \cos(2x)$ with $C_1, C_2 \in \mathbb{R}$.
:::
