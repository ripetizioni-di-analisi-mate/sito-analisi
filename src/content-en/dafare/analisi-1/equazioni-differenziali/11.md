---
titolo: Homogeneous equation with distinct real roots
teoria: [secondo-ordine-omogenea, equazione-caratteristica]
parole: [characteristic equation, real roots, second order]
---

Find the general solution of
$$
y'' - 5y' + 6y = 0 .
$$

:::soluzione
$y = C_1 e^{2x} + C_2 e^{3x}$ with $C_1, C_2 \in \mathbb{R}$.
:::
