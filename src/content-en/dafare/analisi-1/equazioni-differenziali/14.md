---
titolo: Second-order Cauchy problem with a double root
teoria: [cauchy-secondo-ordine, equazione-caratteristica]
parole: [Cauchy problem, second order, double root]
---

Solve the Cauchy problem
$$
\begin{cases}
y'' - 2y' + y = 0 \\[4pt]
y(0) = 1 \\[4pt]
y'(0) = 3
\end{cases}
$$

:::soluzione
$y = \left( 1 + 2x \right) e^{x}$, defined on all of $\mathbb{R}$.
:::
