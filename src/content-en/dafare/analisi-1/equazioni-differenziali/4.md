---
titolo: Cauchy problem with an interval of existence on a half-line
teoria: [variabili-separabili, problema-di-cauchy]
parole: [Cauchy problem, interval of existence, square root]
---

Solve the Cauchy problem and state the interval of existence of the solution:
$$
\begin{cases}
y' = \dfrac{1}{2y} \\[8pt]
y(0) = 1
\end{cases}
$$

:::soluzione
$y = \sqrt{x + 1}$, defined on the half-line $(-1, +\infty)$.
:::
