titolo: Cauchy problem for a first-order linear equation
teoria: [lineari-primo-ordine, fattore-integrante, problema-di-cauchy]
parole: [Cauchy problem, linear equation, initial condition]
---

Solve the Cauchy problem
$$
\begin{cases}
y' + 2y = 4 \\[4pt]
y(0) = 3
\end{cases}
$$

:::soluzione
$y = 2 + e^{-2x}$, defined on all of $\mathbb{R}$.
:::
