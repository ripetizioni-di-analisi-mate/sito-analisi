---
titolo: Homogeneous equation with complex roots
teoria: [secondo-ordine-omogenea, equazione-caratteristica]
parole: [characteristic equation, complex roots, second order]
---

Find the general solution of
$$
y'' + 2y' + 2y = 0 .
$$

:::soluzione
$y = e^{-x} \left( C_1 \cos x + C_2 \sin x \right)$ with $C_1, C_2 \in \mathbb{R}$.
:::
