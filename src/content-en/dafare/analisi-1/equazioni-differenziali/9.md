---
titolo: Bernoulli equation with constant coefficients
teoria: [bernoulli, fattore-integrante]
parole: [Bernoulli equation, substitution, linear equation]
---

Find the general solution of
$$
y' - 2y = -y^2 .
$$

:::soluzione
$y = \dfrac{2}{1 + C e^{-2x}}$ with $C \in \mathbb{R}$, together with the solution $y \equiv 0$; for $C = 0$ one obtains the constant solution $y \equiv 2$.
:::
