titolo: Cauchy problem with a bounded interval of existence

---

Solve the Cauchy problem and state the interval of existence of the solution:
$$
\begin{cases}
y' = 2x\, y^2 \\[4pt]
y(0) = 1
\end{cases}
$$

:::soluzione
$y = \dfrac{1}{1 - x^2}$, defined on the interval $(-1, 1)$.
:::
