Find the general solution of
$$
y'' - 4y = 8x^2 .
$$

:::soluzione
$y = C_1 e^{2x} + C_2 e^{-2x} - 2x^2 - 1$ with $C_1, C_2 \in \mathbb{R}$.
:::
