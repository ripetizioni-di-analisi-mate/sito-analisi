titolo: General solution by separation of variables
teoria: [variabili-separabili, metodo-di-separazione]
parole: [separable variables, general solution]
---

Find the general solution of
$$
y' = 2x\, y .
$$

:::soluzione
$y = C e^{x^2}$ with $C \in \mathbb{R}$; the value $C = 0$ gives the constant solution $y \equiv 0$.
:::
