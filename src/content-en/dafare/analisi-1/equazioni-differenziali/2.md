---
titolo: Separable equation with the cosine
teoria: [variabili-separabili, metodo-di-separazione]
parole: [separable variables, constant solutions]
---

Find the general solution of
$$
y' = y \cos x .
$$

:::soluzione
$y = C e^{\sin x}$ with $C \in \mathbb{R}$; the value $C = 0$ gives the constant solution $y \equiv 0$.
:::
