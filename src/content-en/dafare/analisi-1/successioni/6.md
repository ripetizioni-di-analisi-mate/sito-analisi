---
titolo: Sine of n in a ratio
teoria: [limitata-per-infinitesima]
parole: [bounded times infinitesimal, sine]
---

Compute
$$
\lim_{n \to +\infty} \frac{n \sin n}{n^2 + 1} .
$$

:::soluzione
The sequence $\sin n$ is bounded and $\dfrac{n}{n^2 + 1} \to 0$, hence
$$
\lim_{n \to +\infty} \frac{n \sin n}{n^2 + 1} = 0 .
$$
:::
