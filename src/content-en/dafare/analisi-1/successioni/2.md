---
titolo: Ratio of polynomials of the same degree
teoria: [algebra-dei-limiti, forme-indeterminate]
parole: [leading term, indeterminate form]
---

Compute
$$
\lim_{n \to +\infty} \frac{n^2 - 3n + 1}{4n^2 + n} .
$$

:::soluzione
$$
\lim_{n \to +\infty} \frac{n^2 - 3n + 1}{4n^2 + n} = \frac{1}{4}
$$
:::
