---
titolo: Recurrence with a square root
teoria: [successioni-monotone, successione-monotona]
parole: [recurrence, monotonicity, induction]
---

Prove that the sequence defined by
$$
a_1 = 2, \qquad a_{n+1} = \sqrt{3a_n}
$$
converges and compute its limit.

:::soluzione
By induction $2 \leq a_n < 3$ and the sequence is increasing, hence it converges. Passing to the limit gives $L^2 = 3L$ and, since $L \geq 2$,
$$
\lim_{n \to +\infty} a_n = 3 .
$$
:::
