---
titolo: Difference between a square root and n
teoria: [forme-indeterminate, algebra-dei-limiti]
parole: [rationalisation, infinity minus infinity]
---

Compute
$$
\lim_{n \to +\infty} \left( \sqrt{n^2 + 5n} - n \right) .
$$

:::soluzione
$$
\lim_{n \to +\infty} \left( \sqrt{n^2 + 5n} - n \right) = \frac{5}{2}
$$
:::
