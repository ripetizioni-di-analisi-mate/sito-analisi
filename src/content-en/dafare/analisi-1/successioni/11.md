---
titolo: Factorial, exponential and n to the n
teoria: [criterio-del-rapporto, numero-e]
parole: [ratio test, factorial]
---

Compute
$$
\lim_{n \to +\infty} \frac{2^n\, n!}{n^n} .
$$

:::soluzione
The ratio $\dfrac{a_{n+1}}{a_n} = \dfrac{2}{\left( 1 + \frac{1}{n} \right)^{n}}$ tends to $\dfrac{2}{e} < 1$, hence
$$
\lim_{n \to +\infty} \frac{2^n\, n!}{n^n} = 0 .
$$
:::
