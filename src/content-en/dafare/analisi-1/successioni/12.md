---
titolo: Behaviour of a sequence with alternating signs
teoria: [limite-delle-estratte, successione-estratta]
parole: [subsequences, limit does not exist]
---

Determine the behaviour of the sequence
$$
a_n = (-1)^n\, \frac{2n + 1}{n + 2} .
$$

:::soluzione
The subsequence of even index tends to $2$ and the one of odd index tends to $-2$, hence the limit does not exist and the sequence oscillates.
:::
