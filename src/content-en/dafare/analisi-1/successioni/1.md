---
titolo: Checking a limit and choosing the threshold
teoria: [limite-finito]
parole: [definition of limit, epsilon, threshold N]
---

Verify from the definition that
$$
\lim_{n \to +\infty} \frac{4n - 1}{2n + 1} = 2 ,
$$
and find the smallest $N$ such that $|a_n - 2| < \dfrac{1}{100}$ for every $n > N$.

:::soluzione
We have $|a_n - 2| = \dfrac{3}{2n + 1}$, which is less than $\varepsilon$ for $n > \dfrac{3 - \varepsilon}{2\varepsilon}$. With $\varepsilon = \dfrac{1}{100}$ the condition is $n > 149.5$, hence
$$
N = 149 .
$$
:::
