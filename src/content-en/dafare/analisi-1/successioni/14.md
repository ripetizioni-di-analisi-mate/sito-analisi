---
titolo: Exponential with an infinitesimal exponent
teoria: [limiti-notevoli-successioni, teorema-ponte]
parole: [standard limit, bridge theorem, zero times infinity]
---

Compute
$$
\lim_{n \to +\infty} n \left( e^{\frac{3}{n}} - 1 \right) .
$$

:::soluzione
$$
\lim_{n \to +\infty} n \left( e^{\frac{3}{n}} - 1 \right) = 3
$$
:::
