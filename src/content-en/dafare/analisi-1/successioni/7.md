---
titolo: n-th root of a sum of powers
teoria: [teorema-dei-carabinieri, radice-n-esima]
parole: [squeeze theorem, n-th root]
---

Compute
$$
\lim_{n \to +\infty} \sqrt[n]{4^n + 5^n} .
$$

:::soluzione
From $5^n \leq 4^n + 5^n \leq 2 \cdot 5^n$ it follows that $5 \leq a_n \leq 5\sqrt[n]{2}$, hence
$$
\lim_{n \to +\infty} \sqrt[n]{4^n + 5^n} = 5 .
$$
:::
