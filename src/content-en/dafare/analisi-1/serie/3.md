---
titolo: Somma di una serie telescopica
teoria: [serie-telescopica]
parole: [serie telescopica, fratti semplici, somma]
---

Compute the sum of the series
$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{(2n-1)(2n+1)} .
$$

:::soluzione
Since $\dfrac{1}{(2n-1)(2n+1)} = \dfrac{1}{2} \left( \dfrac{1}{2n-1} - \dfrac{1}{2n+1} \right)$, the series is telescoping, the partial sums equal $\dfrac{1}{2} \left( 1 - \dfrac{1}{2N+1} \right)$ and the sum is $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
:::
