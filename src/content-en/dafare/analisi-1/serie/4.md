---
titolo: Telescoping series with three factors
teoria: [serie-telescopica]
parole: [telescoping series, partial fractions, sum]
---

Compute the sum of the series
$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n(n+1)(n+2)} .
$$

:::soluzione
Since $\dfrac{1}{n(n+1)(n+2)} = \dfrac{1}{2} \left( \dfrac{1}{n(n+1)} - \dfrac{1}{(n+1)(n+2)} \right)$, the series is telescoping with $b_n = \dfrac{1}{2\, n(n+1)}$ and its sum equals $b_1 = \dfrac{1}{4}$.
:::
