---
titolo: Behavior by limit comparison
teoria: [confronto-asintotico, serie-armonica-generalizzata]
parole: [limit comparison, logarithm, standard limits]
---

Study the behavior of the series
$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \ln\!\left( 1 + \frac{1}{n^2} \right) .
$$

:::soluzione
Since $\dfrac{1}{n^2} \to 0$, by the standard limit of the logarithm $\ln\!\left( 1 + \dfrac{1}{n^2} \right) \sim \dfrac{1}{n^2}$, and $\displaystyle\sum \frac{1}{n^2}$ converges; therefore the series converges.
:::
