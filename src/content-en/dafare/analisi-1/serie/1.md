---
titolo: Somma di una serie geometrica
teoria: [serie-geometrica]
parole: [serie geometrica, somma, ragione]
---

Compute the sum of the series
$$
\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \left( \frac{2}{5} \right)^{\! n} .
$$

:::soluzione
The series is geometric with ratio $q = \dfrac{2}{5}$, with $|q| < 1$, therefore it converges and its sum equals
$$
\frac{1}{1 - \dfrac{2}{5}} = \frac{5}{3} .
$$
:::
