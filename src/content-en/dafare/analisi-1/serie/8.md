---
titolo: Ratio test with a factorial
teoria: [criterio-del-rapporto]
parole: [ratio test, factorial]
---

Study the behavior of the series
$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{n^3}{n!} .
$$

:::soluzione
The ratio equals $\dfrac{a_{n+1}}{a_n} = \dfrac{(n+1)^3}{(n+1)\, n^3} = \dfrac{(n+1)^2}{n^3} \to 0 < 1$, therefore the series converges.
:::
