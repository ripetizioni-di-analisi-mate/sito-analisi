---
titolo: Serie geometrica con parametro
teoria: [serie-geometrica]
parole: [serie con parametro, serie geometrica, discussione]
---

Determine for which $x \in \mathbb{R}$ the series
$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \left( \frac{x+1}{3} \right)^{\! n}
$$
converges and compute its sum.

:::soluzione
The series is geometric with ratio $q = \dfrac{x+1}{3}$ and converges if and only if $|x+1| < 3$, that is, for $-4 < x < 2$; in that case its sum equals
$$
\frac{\dfrac{x+1}{3}}{1 - \dfrac{x+1}{3}} = \frac{x+1}{2-x} .
$$
:::
