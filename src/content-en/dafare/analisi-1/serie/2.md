---
titolo: Geometric series with a shifted index
teoria: [serie-geometrica, operazioni-sulle-serie]
parole: [geometric series, sum, first term]
---

Compute the sum of the series
$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{3^{n-1}}{4^n} .
$$

:::soluzione
Since $\dfrac{3^{n-1}}{4^n} = \dfrac{1}{3} \left( \dfrac{3}{4} \right)^{\! n}$, the series is geometric with ratio $\dfrac{3}{4}$ and its sum equals
$$
\frac{1}{3} \cdot \frac{\dfrac{3}{4}}{1 - \dfrac{3}{4}} = 1 .
$$
:::
