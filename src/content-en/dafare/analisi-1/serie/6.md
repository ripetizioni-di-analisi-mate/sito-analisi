---
titolo: Serie con differenza di radici
teoria: [confronto-asintotico, serie-armonica-generalizzata]
parole: [confronto asintotico, razionalizzazione, radici]
---

Determine whether the series converges or diverges
$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{\sqrt{n+1} - \sqrt{n}}{n} .
$$

:::soluzione
By rationalizing, $\sqrt{n+1} - \sqrt{n} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{n+1} + \sqrt{n}} \sim \dfrac{1}{2\sqrt{n}}$, therefore the general term is asymptotically equivalent to $\dfrac{1}{2\, n^{\frac{3}{2}}}$; since $\dfrac{3}{2} > 1$, the series converges.
:::
