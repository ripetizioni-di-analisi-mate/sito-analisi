---
titolo: Series with a difference of square roots
teoria: [confronto-asintotico, serie-armonica-generalizzata]
parole: [limit comparison, rationalization, square roots]
---

Study the behavior of the series
$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{\sqrt{n+1} - \sqrt{n}}{n} .
$$

:::soluzione
By rationalizing, $\sqrt{n+1} - \sqrt{n} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{n+1} + \sqrt{n}} \sim \dfrac{1}{2\sqrt{n}}$, therefore the general term is asymptotic to $\dfrac{1}{2\, n^{3/2}}$; since $\dfrac{3}{2} > 1$, the series converges.
:::
