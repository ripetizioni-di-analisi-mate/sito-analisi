Study the absolute convergence and the convergence of the series
$$
\sum_{n=2}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^n}{n \ln n} .
$$

:::soluzione
The series of absolute values diverges by the integral test, because the integral of $\dfrac{1}{x \ln x}$ over $[2, +\infty)$ is infinite; the sequence $\dfrac{1}{n \ln n}$ is positive, decreasing and tends to zero, therefore by the alternating series test the series converges conditionally.
:::
