Study the absolute convergence and the convergence of the series
$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^n}{2n+1} .
$$

:::soluzione
The series of absolute values diverges, because $\dfrac{1}{2n+1} \sim \dfrac{1}{2n}$ and the harmonic series diverges; the sequence $\dfrac{1}{2n+1}$ is positive, decreasing and infinitesimal, therefore by the Leibniz test the series converges conditionally.
:::
