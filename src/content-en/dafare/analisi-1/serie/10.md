---
titolo: Root test
teoria: [criterio-della-radice]
parole: [root test, n-th powers]
---

Study the behavior of the series
$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \left( \frac{2n+1}{3n+2} \right)^{\! n} .
$$

:::soluzione
The $n$-th root of the general term is $\dfrac{2n+1}{3n+2} \to \dfrac{2}{3} < 1$, therefore the series converges.
:::
