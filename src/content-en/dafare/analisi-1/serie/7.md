Determine whether the series converges or diverges
$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \left( e^{\frac{1}{n}} - 1 \right) .
$$

:::soluzione
Since $\dfrac{1}{n} \to 0$, by the standard exponential limit $e^{\frac{1}{n}} - 1 \sim \dfrac{1}{n}$, and the harmonic series diverges; therefore the series diverges to $+\infty$.
:::
