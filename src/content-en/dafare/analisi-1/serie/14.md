Determine the set of convergence of the power series
$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{x^n}{n\, 2^n} .
$$

:::soluzione
The ratio of the absolute values of the coefficients tends to $\dfrac{1}{2}$, therefore $R = 2$; at $x = 2$ the harmonic series remains, which diverges, and at $x = -2$ what remains is $\displaystyle\sum \frac{(-1)^n}{n}$, which converges by the Leibniz test. The set of convergence is $[-2, 2)$.
:::
