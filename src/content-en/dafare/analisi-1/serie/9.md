---
titolo: Ratio test with two factorials and powers
teoria: [criterio-del-rapporto]
parole: [ratio test, factorial, powers]
---

Study the behavior of the series
$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{(2n)!}{(n!)^2\, 5^n} .
$$

:::soluzione
The ratio equals $\dfrac{a_{n+1}}{a_n} = \dfrac{(2n+1)(2n+2)}{(n+1)^2 \cdot 5} \to \dfrac{4}{5} < 1$, therefore the series converges.
:::
