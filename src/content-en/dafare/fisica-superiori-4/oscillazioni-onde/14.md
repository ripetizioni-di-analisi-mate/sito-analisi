The wave $y(x,t)=A\cos(\pi x+4\pi t)$, with $A>0$, uses $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. Find at $t=0.25\,\mathrm{s}$ the position of the crest initially at $x=4\,\mathrm{m}$.

:::soluzione
$3\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
