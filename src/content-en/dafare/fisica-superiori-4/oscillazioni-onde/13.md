A simple pendulum has a small-angle period of $2.0\,\mathrm{s}$ where the gravitational acceleration is $g_1$. At the same length, find the period if $g_2=\dfrac{g_1}{4}$.

:::soluzione
$4.0\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
