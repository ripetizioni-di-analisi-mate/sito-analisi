A charge $q=-2.0\,\mathrm{nC}$ is placed where $(E_x,E_y)=(300,400)\,\mathrm{N/C}$. What is the vertical force component $F_y$, taking upward as positive?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 punti="0,0,pieno,P" vettori="0,0,1.5,2,E" segmenti="0,2,1.5,2;1.5,0,1.5,2" xmin=-2.5 xmax=3.3 ymin=-2.4 ymax=3.2 titolo="The field components at P are 300 and 400 N/C"}

:::soluzione
$-0.80\,\mathrm{\mu N}$.
:::
