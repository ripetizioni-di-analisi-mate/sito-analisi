An initially neutral object reaches charge $+0.48\,\mathrm{\mu C}$ by electron transfer. With $e=1.60\cdot10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}$, what is $\Delta N=N_f-N_i$, the signed change in the number of electrons?

:::soluzione
$-3\cdot10^{12}$.
:::
