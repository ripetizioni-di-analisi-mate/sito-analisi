---
titolo: "Acceleration of an electron"
teoria: [moto-campo]
parole: ["acceleration", "electron"]
---

An electron is in a uniform field of $144\,\mathrm{N/C}$ pointing right. With $e=1.60\cdot10^{-19}\,\mathrm C$ and $m_e=9.11\cdot10^{-31}\,\mathrm{kg}$, what approximates its acceleration component, with right positive and other forces neglected?

:::soluzione
$-2.53\cdot10^{13}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
:::
