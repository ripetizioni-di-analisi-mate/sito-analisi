---
titolo: "Coulomb force in vacuum"
teoria: [coulomb]
parole: ["Coulomb", "distance"]
---

Two point charges in vacuum have magnitudes $2.4\,\mathrm{nC}$ and $3.0\,\mathrm{nC}$. They are $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ apart. With $k_e=8.99\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$, which value approximates the force magnitude?

:::soluzione
$4.50\,\mathrm{\mu N}$.
:::
