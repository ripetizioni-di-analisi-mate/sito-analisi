---
titolo: "Separation inferred from force"
teoria: [coulomb]
parole: ["Coulomb", "distance"]
---

Two charges of magnitude $2\,\mathrm{nC}$ and $8\,\mathrm{nC}$ exert a force of magnitude $3.6\,\mathrm{\mu N}$ on each other in vacuum. How far apart are they with $k_e=9.0\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$?

:::soluzione
$0.20\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
