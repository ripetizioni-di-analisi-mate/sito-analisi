titolo: "Balance with heat and work"
teoria: ["primo-principio"]

A gas in a closed system absorbs $1080\,\mathrm{J}$ and does $250\,\mathrm{J}$ of work on the surroundings. What is $\Delta U$ if macroscopic changes in kinetic and potential energy are negligible?

:::soluzione
$\Delta U=830\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
