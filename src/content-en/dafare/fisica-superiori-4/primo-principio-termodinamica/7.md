---
titolo: "Work in isothermal expansion"
teoria: ["isoterma"]
parole: ["reversible isothermal process"]
---

An ideal gas containing $0.78\,\mathrm{mol}$ expands reversibly at $320\,\mathrm{K}$ from $10$ to $25\,\mathrm{L}$. Find the work done by the gas and round to the nearest ten joules. Use $R=8.314\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$ and $\ln(2.5)\simeq0.916$.

:::soluzione
$1900\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
