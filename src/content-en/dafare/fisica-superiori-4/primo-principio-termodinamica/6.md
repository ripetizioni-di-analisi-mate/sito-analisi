teoria: ["isocora-isobara"]

A monatomic ideal gas containing $0.90\,\mathrm{mol}$ is heated quasistatically by $60\,\mathrm{K}$ at constant pressure. Find the heat absorbed using $R=8.314\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$ and round to the nearest ten joules.

:::soluzione
$1120\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
