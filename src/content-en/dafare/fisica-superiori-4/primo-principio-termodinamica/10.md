---
titolo: "Balance over two stages"
teoria: ["primo-principio"]
parole: ["net heat"]
---

During a first stage, a gas absorbs $410\,\mathrm{J}$ and does $100\,\mathrm{J}$ of work. During a second stage it releases $90\,\mathrm{J}$ and the piston does $80\,\mathrm{J}$ of work on the gas. Calculate the total change in internal energy.

:::soluzione
$\Delta U=300\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
