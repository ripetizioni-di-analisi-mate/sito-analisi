The temperature of $0.96\,\mathrm{mol}$ of a monatomic ideal gas increases by $45\,\mathrm{K}$. Using $R=8.314\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$, what is $\Delta U$ rounded to the nearest joule?

:::soluzione
$539\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
