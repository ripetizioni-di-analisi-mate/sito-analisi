On the $p$-$V$ diagram, the gas quasistatically follows the segment from $A=(1\,\mathrm{L},300\,\mathrm{kPa})$ to $B=(4\,\mathrm{L},100\,\mathrm{kPa})$. How much work does it do?

::grafico{linee="1,300 4,100" punti="1,300,pieno,A;4,100,pieno,B" vettori="2,233.3333,3,166.6667" xmin=0 xmax=5 ymin=0 ymax=400 nomi="V,p (kPa)" titolo="During expansion the pressure falls from A to B. Volume is in liters."}

:::soluzione
$W=600\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
