A rectangular cycle runs counterclockwise between $100$ and $250\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and between $2$ and $6\,\mathrm{L}$. What is the net heat exchanged by the gas?

::grafico{linee="2,100 6,100 6,250 2,250 2,100" vettori="3,100,5,100;6,145,6,205;5,250,3,250;2,205,2,145" xmin=0 xmax=7 ymin=0 ymax=350 nomi="V,p (kPa)" titolo="The horizontal sides are traversed in opposite directions. Volume is in liters."}

:::soluzione
$Q_{\mathrm{tot}}=-600\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
