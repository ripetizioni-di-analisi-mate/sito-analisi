An infinite insulating plane has uniform surface density $1.8\cdot10^{-8}\,\mathrm{C/m^2}$. Calculate the field magnitude on one side. Use $\varepsilon_0=8.85\cdot10^{-12}\,\mathrm{C^2/(N\,m^2)}$. Round to three significant figures.

:::soluzione
$1020\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
:::
