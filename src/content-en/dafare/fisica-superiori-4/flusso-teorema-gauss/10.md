parole: ["Flux after a charge moves inside"]
---

A closed surface encloses net charge $+4\,\mathrm{nC}$. A charge of $-9\,\mathrm{nC}$ moves from outside to inside. Calculate the new net outward flux. Use $\varepsilon_0=8.85\cdot10^{-12}\,\mathrm{C^2/(N\,m^2)}$. Round to three significant figures.

:::soluzione
$-565\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$.
:::
