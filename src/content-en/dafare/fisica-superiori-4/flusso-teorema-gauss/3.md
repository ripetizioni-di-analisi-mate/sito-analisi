parole: ["Flux with an exterior charge"]
---

A closed surface contains $+3.6\,\mathrm{nC}$ and $-1.0\,\mathrm{nC}$. Another charge of $+5\,\mathrm{nC}$ is outside. Calculate the total flux. Use $\varepsilon_0=8.85\cdot10^{-12}\,\mathrm{C^2/(N\,m^2)}$. Round to three significant figures.

:::soluzione
$294\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$.
:::
