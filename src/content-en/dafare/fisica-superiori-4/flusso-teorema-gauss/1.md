A uniform field of magnitude $300\,\mathrm{N/C}$ makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with the normal to a flat surface of area $0.040\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Calculate the flux.

:::soluzione
$\Phi_E=6\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$.
:::
