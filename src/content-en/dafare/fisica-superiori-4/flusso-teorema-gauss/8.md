parole: ["Field between opposite plates"]
---

Two oppositely charged plates have surface densities of magnitude $9.6\cdot10^{-9}\,\mathrm{C/m^2}$. Neglecting edges, calculate the field magnitude between them. Use $\varepsilon_0=8.85\cdot10^{-12}\,\mathrm{C^2/(N\,m^2)}$. Round to three significant figures.

:::soluzione
$1080\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
:::
