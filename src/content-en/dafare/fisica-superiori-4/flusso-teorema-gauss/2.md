---

A closed surface has total outward flux $288\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$. Calculate the net enclosed charge in nanocoulombs. Use $\varepsilon_0=8.85\cdot10^{-12}\,\mathrm{C^2/(N\,m^2)}$. Round to three significant figures.

:::soluzione
$2.55\,\mathrm{nC}$.
:::
