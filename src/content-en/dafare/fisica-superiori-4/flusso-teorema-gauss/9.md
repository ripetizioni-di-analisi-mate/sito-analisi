---

A field of magnitude $160\,\mathrm{N/C}$ makes a $30^\circ$ angle with a flat surface of area $0.20\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The component along the chosen normal is negative. Calculate the flux.

:::soluzione
$\Phi_E=-16\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$.
:::
