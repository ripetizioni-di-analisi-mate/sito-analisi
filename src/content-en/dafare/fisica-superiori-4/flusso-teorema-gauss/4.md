A spherically symmetric distribution of radius $0.050\,\mathrm{m}$ has total charge $2.4\,\mathrm{nC}$. Calculate the field magnitude $0.20\,\mathrm{m}$ from the center. Use $\varepsilon_0=8.85\cdot10^{-12}\,\mathrm{C^2/(N\,m^2)}$. Round to three significant figures.

:::soluzione
$540\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
:::
