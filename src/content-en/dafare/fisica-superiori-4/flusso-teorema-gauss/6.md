---

An infinite wire has uniform linear density $1.44\,\mathrm{nC/m}$. Calculate the field magnitude at a distance of $0.080\,\mathrm{m}$. Use $\varepsilon_0=8.85\cdot10^{-12}\,\mathrm{C^2/(N\,m^2)}$. Round to three significant figures.

:::soluzione
$324\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
:::
