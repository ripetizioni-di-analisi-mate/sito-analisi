---

Outside an isolated spherically symmetric sphere, $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$ from the center, the field points inward with magnitude $200\,\mathrm{N/C}$. Use $k_e=9\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$. Calculate the total charge in nanocoulombs.

:::soluzione
$Q=-2\,\mathrm{nC}$.
:::
