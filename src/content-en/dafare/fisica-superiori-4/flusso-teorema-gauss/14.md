parole: ["Induced charge in a cavity"]
---

A closed cavity in a conductor at equilibrium contains a point charge of $+6\,\mathrm{nC}$. Calculate the total charge induced on the inner wall.

:::soluzione
$Q_{\mathrm{parete}}=-6\,\mathrm{nC}$.
:::
