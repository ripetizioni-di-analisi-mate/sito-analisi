An insulating sphere of radius $0.10\,\mathrm{m}$ has uniformly distributed total charge $1.2\,\mathrm{nC}$. Calculate the field magnitude $0.040\,\mathrm{m}$ from the center. Use $\varepsilon_0=8.85\cdot10^{-12}\,\mathrm{C^2/(N\,m^2)}$. Round to three significant figures.

:::soluzione
$432\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
:::
