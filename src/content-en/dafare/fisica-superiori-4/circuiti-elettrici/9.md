A $16\,\mathrm V$ divider has two $6\,\mathrm{k\Omega}$ resistors in series. A $3\,\mathrm{k\Omega}$ load is connected across the output resistor. Find the load voltage.

:::soluzione
$4\,\mathrm{V}$.
:::
