---
titolo: "Current with opposing sources"
teoria: [kirchhoff]
parole: ["Kirchhoff","balance"]
---

A loop has opposing ideal sources of $14\,\mathrm V$ and $6\,\mathrm V$ and a total resistance of $4\,\Omega$. Take the direction driven by the $6\,\mathrm V$ source as positive. Find the signed current.

:::soluzione
$-2\,\mathrm{A}$.
:::
