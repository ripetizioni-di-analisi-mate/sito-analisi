An initially uncharged capacitor is connected to an ideal $12\,\mathrm V$ source through a resistor. The time constant is $\tau=1\,\mathrm s$. Find $V_C$ at $(\ln 2)\,\mathrm s$.

:::soluzione
$6\,\mathrm{V}$.
:::
