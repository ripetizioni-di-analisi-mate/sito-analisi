parole: ["Sound speed in a second medium"]
---

A source emits sound in two media separated by a stationary interface. In the first, $u_1=340\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $\lambda_1=0.40\,\mathrm m$. In the second, $\lambda_2=1.60\,\mathrm m$. What is $u_2$?

:::soluzione
$1360\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
