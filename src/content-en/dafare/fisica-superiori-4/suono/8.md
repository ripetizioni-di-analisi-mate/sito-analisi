---

A $744\,\mathrm{Hz}$ source approaches a stationary observer at $18\,\mathrm{m/s}$ along the line between them. Sound travels at $340\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in still air. What frequency is received?

:::soluzione
$786\,\mathrm{Hz}$.
:::
