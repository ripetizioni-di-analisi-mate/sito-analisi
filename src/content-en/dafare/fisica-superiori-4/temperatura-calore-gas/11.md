---
titolo: "Equilibrium with a calorimeter"
teoria: [equilibrio-calorimetrico]
parole: ["calorimeter", "heat capacity"]
---

An insulated calorimeter receives $0.25\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water at $60\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. The vessel is initially at $20\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ and has heat capacity $1045\,\mathrm{J/K}$. With $c_{\mathrm{acqua}}=4180\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$, at what temperature do they reach equilibrium?

:::soluzione
$40^\circ\mathrm{C}$.
:::
