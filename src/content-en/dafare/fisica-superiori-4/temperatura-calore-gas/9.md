---
titolo: "Temperature after heating"
teoria: [calorimetria]
parole: ["heat capacity", "final temperature"]
---

A mass of $0.50\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water receives $20.9\,\mathrm{kJ}$. Its initial temperature is $18\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ and its specific heat capacity is $4180\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$. With no phase change, what is its final temperature?

:::soluzione
$28^\circ\mathrm{C}$.
:::
