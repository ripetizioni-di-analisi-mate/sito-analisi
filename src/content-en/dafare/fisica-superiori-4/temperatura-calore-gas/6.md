An ideal gas in equilibrium contains $n=0.72\,\mathrm{mol}$ in volume $V=0.018\,\mathrm{m^3}$ at $T=310\,\mathrm K$. What is its absolute pressure with $R=8.314\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$?

:::soluzione
$1.03\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
:::
