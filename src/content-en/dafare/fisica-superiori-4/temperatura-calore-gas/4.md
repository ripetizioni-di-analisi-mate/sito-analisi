---
titolo: "Energy required to melt ice"
teoria: [calore-latente]
parole: ["melting", "latent heat"]
---

A mass of ice $m=0.144\,\mathrm{kg}$ is already at $0\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. How much energy melts it completely at atmospheric pressure, given $\ell_f=3.34\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{J/kg}$?

:::soluzione
$4.81\cdot10^4\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
