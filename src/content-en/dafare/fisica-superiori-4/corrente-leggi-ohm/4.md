A uniform wire has $\rho=1.7\cdot10^{-8}\,\Omega\,\mathrm{m}$, length $14.4\,\mathrm{m}$, and cross-sectional area $1.5\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Find its resistance.

:::soluzione
$0.163\,\Omega$.
:::
