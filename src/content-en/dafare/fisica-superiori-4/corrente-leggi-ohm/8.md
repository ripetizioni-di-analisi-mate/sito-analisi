A $1200\,\mathrm{W}$ heater transfers a fraction $0.80$ of its electric energy to water. Find the time to heat $1.08\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water by $25\,\mathrm{K}$ with $c=4180\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$.

:::soluzione
$118\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
