An ohmic characteristic passes through $(V,I)=(2\,\mathrm{V},0.20\,\mathrm{A})$ and $(6\,\mathrm{V},0.60\,\mathrm{A})$. Use the slope of $I$ versus $V$ to find resistance.

:::soluzione
$10\,\Omega$.
:::
