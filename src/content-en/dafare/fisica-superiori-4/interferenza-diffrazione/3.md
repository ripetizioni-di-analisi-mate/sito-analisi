Two slits separated by $d=0.36\,\mathrm{mm}$ are illuminated in phase with $\lambda=600\,\mathrm{nm}$. The screen is $2.0\,\mathrm m$ away. Estimate the fringe spacing. Round to three significant figures.

:::soluzione
$\Delta y=3.33\,\mathrm{mm}$.
:::
