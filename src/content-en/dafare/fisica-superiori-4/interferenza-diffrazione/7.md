A grating of spacing $2.0\,\mathrm{\mu m}$ receives light of wavelength $600\,\mathrm{nm}$ at normal incidence. Find the angle of its first positive maximum. Round to three significant figures.

:::soluzione
$\theta_1=17.5^\circ$.
:::
