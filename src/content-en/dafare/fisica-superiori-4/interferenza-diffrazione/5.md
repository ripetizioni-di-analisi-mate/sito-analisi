A slit $0.24\,\mathrm{mm}$ wide receives $600\,\mathrm{nm}$ light. The screen is $2.0\,\mathrm m$ away. Find the central maximum width at small angles.

:::soluzione
$w=10\,\mathrm{mm}$.
:::
