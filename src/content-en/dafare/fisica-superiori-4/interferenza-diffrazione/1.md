- Frequency from wavelength in vacuum

In vacuum $\lambda_0=660\,\mathrm{nm}$. Use $c=3.00\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to calculate the frequency. Round to three significant figures.

:::soluzione
$f=4.55\cdot10^{14}\,\mathrm{Hz}$.
:::
