For an ideal circular aperture of diameter $61\,\mathrm{mm}$ at $\lambda=500\,\mathrm{nm}$, estimate the Rayleigh angular separation in radians.

:::soluzione
$\theta_{\min}=1\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{rad}$.
:::
