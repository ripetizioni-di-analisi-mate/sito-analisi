In Young's experiment $\lambda=550\,\mathrm{nm}$, $L=2.16\,\mathrm m$, and $d=0.25\,\mathrm{mm}$. Estimate the coordinate of maximum $m=+2$ in the small-angle model. Round to three significant figures.

:::soluzione
$y_2=9.50\,\mathrm{mm}$.
:::
