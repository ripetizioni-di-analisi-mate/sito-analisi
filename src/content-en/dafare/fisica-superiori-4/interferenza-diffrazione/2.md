Two coherent waves start in phase and have $\lambda=600\,\mathrm{nm}$. Their paths through the same medium differ by $480\,\mathrm{nm}$. Calculate the phase difference before reducing it modulo $2\pi$. Round to three significant figures.

:::soluzione
$\Delta\varphi=5.03\,\mathrm{rad}$.
:::
