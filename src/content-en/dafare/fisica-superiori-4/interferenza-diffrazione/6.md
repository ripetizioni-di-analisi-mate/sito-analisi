For $\lambda=500\,\mathrm{nm}$ and $L=1.5\,\mathrm m$, a single slit has a central maximum $14.4\,\mathrm{mm}$ wide. Estimate the slit width. Round to three significant figures.

:::soluzione
$a=104\,\mathrm{\mu m}$.
:::
