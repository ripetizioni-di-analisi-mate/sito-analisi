parole: ["vacuum permittivity"]

A vacuum parallel-plate capacitor has plate area $180\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and separation $1\,\mathrm{mm}$. What is its capacitance in the ideal model with $\varepsilon_0=8.85\,\mathrm{pF/m}$?

:::soluzione
$C=159.3\,\mathrm{pF}$.
:::
