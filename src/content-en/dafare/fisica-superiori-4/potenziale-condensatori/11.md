A uniform field of $400\,\mathrm{V/m}$ points to the right. The displacement from $A$ to $B$ is $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ right and $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ up. What is $V_B-V_A$?

::grafico{vettori="1,2,6,2,E;5,5,10,17,d" punti="5,5,pieno,A;10,17,pieno,B" segmenti="5,5,10,5;10,5,10,17" etichette="7.5,3.7,5 cm;12,11,12 cm" xmin=0 xmax=16 ymin=0 ymax=20 uguale=1 assi=0 titolo="The dashed segments separate the displacement components."}

:::soluzione
$\Delta V=-20\,\mathrm{V}$.
:::
