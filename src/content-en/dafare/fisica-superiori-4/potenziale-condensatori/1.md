A point source of $+3.6\,\mathrm{nC}$ is in vacuum. What is the potential $20\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the source, with $k_e\simeq9\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$ and zero at infinity?

:::soluzione
$V=162\,\mathrm{V}$.
:::
