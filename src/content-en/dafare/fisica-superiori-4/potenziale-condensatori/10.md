---
titolo: "Slow separation of charges"
teoria: ["energia-potenziale"]
parole: ["external work"]
---

A pair of opposite charges has $U_i=-360\,\mathrm{nJ}$ at a separation of $10\,\mathrm{cm}$. How much external work is needed to slowly increase the separation to $30\,\mathrm{cm}$?

:::soluzione
$W_{\mathrm{est}}=240\,\mathrm{nJ}$.
:::
