Two charges $+4.8\,\mathrm{nC}$ and $-2\,\mathrm{nC}$ are $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ apart. What is their potential energy in nanojoules? Use $k_e\simeq9\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$ and zero at infinite separation.

:::soluzione
$U=-864\,\mathrm{nJ}$.
:::
