parole: ["Entropy during heating"]
---

A body with constant heat capacity $192\,\mathrm{J/K}$ goes from $300$ to $450\,\mathrm{K}$ without a phase change. Use $\ln(1.5)\simeq0.405465$. Calculate its entropy change. Round to one decimal place.

:::soluzione
$77.8\,\mathrm{J/K}$.
:::
