titolo: "Entropy in direct heat transfer"
teoria: [entropia-totale]
parole: ["Entropy in direct heat transfer"]
---

A reservoir at $600\,\mathrm{K}$ transfers $1440\,\mathrm{J}$ directly to a reservoir at $300\,\mathrm{K}$. Calculate the total entropy change.

:::soluzione
$\Delta S_{\mathrm{tot}}=2.4\,\mathrm{J/K}$.
:::
