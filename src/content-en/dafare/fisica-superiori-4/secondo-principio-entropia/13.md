A body with constant heat capacity $250\,\mathrm{J/K}$ cools from $400$ to $200\,\mathrm{K}$ without a phase change. Use $\ln2\simeq0.693$. Calculate its entropy change. Round to the nearest integer in joules per kelvin.

:::soluzione
$-173\,\mathrm{J/K}$.
:::
