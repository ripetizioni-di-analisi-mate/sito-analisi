---

A cyclic engine absorbs $Q_h=2880\,\mathrm{J}$ from the hot reservoir and rejects $Q_c=1500\,\mathrm{J}$ to the cold one. Calculate its efficiency. Round the result to three decimal places.

:::soluzione
$0.479$.
:::
