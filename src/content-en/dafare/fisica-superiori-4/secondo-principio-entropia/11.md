parole: ["Checking a reported cycle"]
---

A cyclic engine absorbs $600\,\mathrm{J}$ at $600\,\mathrm{K}$ and is reported to reject only $240\,\mathrm{J}$ at $300\,\mathrm{K}$. The two reservoirs are its only thermal contacts. Find the total entropy change of the two reservoirs per cycle.

:::soluzione
$-0.20\,\mathrm{J/K}$.
:::
