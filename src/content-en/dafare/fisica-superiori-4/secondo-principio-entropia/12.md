parole: ["Entropy in free expansion"]
---

Half a mole of ideal gas expands into a vacuum inside an insulated vessel until it occupies twice its initial volume. Use $R=8.31\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$ and $\ln2\simeq0.693$. Calculate the gas entropy change. Round to two decimal places.

:::soluzione
$2.88\,\mathrm{J/K}$.
:::
