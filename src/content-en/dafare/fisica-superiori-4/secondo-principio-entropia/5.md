parole: ["Coefficient of a refrigerator"]
---

A refrigerator removes $864\,\mathrm{J}$ from the cold reservoir and receives $240\,\mathrm{J}$ of work per cycle. Calculate its refrigeration coefficient of performance.

:::soluzione
$\mathrm{COP}_f=3.6$.
:::
