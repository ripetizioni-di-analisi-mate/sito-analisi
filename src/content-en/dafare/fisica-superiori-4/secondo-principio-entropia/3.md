A heat engine absorbs $2400\,\mathrm{J}$ and delivers $620\,\mathrm{J}$ of work per cycle. Calculate the magnitude of the heat rejected to the cold reservoir.

:::soluzione
$Q_c=1780\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
