titolo: "Minimum heating input power"
teoria: [cop-carnot]
parole: ["Minimum heating input power"]
---

A heat pump heats a room at $300\,\mathrm{K}$ using outdoor air at $275\,\mathrm{K}$. It must deliver $12\,\mathrm{kW}$ of heating power to the room. Calculate the minimum input power.

:::soluzione
$P_{\min}=1\,\mathrm{kW}$.
:::
