titolo: "Minimum work for a refrigerator"
teoria: [cop-carnot]
parole: ["Minimum work for a refrigerator"]
---

A refrigerator operates between a compartment at $260\,\mathrm{K}$ and surroundings at $300\,\mathrm{K}$. Calculate the minimum work required to remove $520\,\mathrm{J}$ from the compartment.

:::soluzione
$W_{\min}=80\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
