A concave mirror has $f=0.18\,\mathrm m$. A real object is at $p=0.648\,\mathrm m$. Find $q$ in the paraxial model, with $q>0$ for a real image. Round to three significant figures.

:::soluzione
$q=0.249\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
