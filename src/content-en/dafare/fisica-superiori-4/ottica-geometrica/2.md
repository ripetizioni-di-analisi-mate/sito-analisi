Light travels from air ($n_1=1.00$) into glass ($n_2=1.50$) at $\theta_1=42^\circ$ from the normal. Find the refraction angle from the normal. Round to three significant figures.

:::soluzione
$\theta_2=26.5^\circ$.
:::
