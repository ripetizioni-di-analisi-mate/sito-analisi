A ray goes from a medium with $n_1=\sqrt{2}$ toward air with $n_2=1$. The incidence angle is $45^\circ$ from the normal. Find the angle of the transmitted ray from the normal in the geometrical model.

:::soluzione
$90^\circ$.
:::
