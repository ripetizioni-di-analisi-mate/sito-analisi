An object $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ high is $8\,\mathrm{cm}$ from a lens. Its image is upright and $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ high. Find the signed value of $q$.

:::soluzione
$q=-24\,\mathrm{cm}$.
:::
