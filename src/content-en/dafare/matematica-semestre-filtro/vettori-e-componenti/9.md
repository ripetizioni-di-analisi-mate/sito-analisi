A force of magnitude $10\,\mathrm{N}$ makes an angle of $150^\circ$ measured counterclockwise from the positive $x$ axis. Calculate its two Cartesian components.

:::soluzione
$\left(-5\sqrt3,\,5\right)\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
