titolo: Magnitudes with mixed units
teoria: [somma, modulo]

Two successive displacements are $400\,\mathrm{cm}$ in the positive $x$ direction and $3\,\mathrm{m}$ in the positive $y$ direction. Calculate the magnitude of the resultant displacement in meters.

:::soluzione
$5\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
