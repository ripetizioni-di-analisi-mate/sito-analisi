The response of an instrument is $A(c)=(2\,\mathrm{L/mol})c+0.10$ for $0\le c\le 0.20\,\mathrm{mol/L}$. Determine which concentration in the allowed range produces the response $A=0.60$.

:::soluzione
No concentration in the allowed range.
:::
