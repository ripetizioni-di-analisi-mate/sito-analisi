An empty container has mass $16\,\mathrm{g}$. With $30\,\mathrm{mL}$ of a homogeneous liquid in the container, the total mass is $52\,\mathrm{g}$. Calculate the density of the liquid.

:::soluzione
$1.2\,\mathrm{g/mL}$.
:::
