A flat surface of area $0.025\,\mathrm{m^2}$ is subject to a uniform pressure of $12\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Use $p=\dfrac{F}{A}$ to find the magnitude $F$ of the normal force.

:::soluzione
$300\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
