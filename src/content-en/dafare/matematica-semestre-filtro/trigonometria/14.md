---
titolo: "Components along an inclined plane"
teoria: [piano-inclinato]
parole: ["Trigonometry", "filter semester"]
---

An object of mass $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ is on a plane inclined at $30^\circ$ to the horizontal. Take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and the uphill direction as positive. Calculate the component of weight along the plane.

:::soluzione
$-20\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
