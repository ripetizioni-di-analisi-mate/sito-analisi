---
titolo: "Projections from an angle measured from the vertical"
teoria: [proiezioni]
parole: ["Trigonometry", "filter semester"]
---

A segment of length $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ makes an acute angle of $30^\circ$ with the vertical. Calculate the length of its horizontal projection.

:::soluzione
$5\,\mathrm{cm}$.
:::
