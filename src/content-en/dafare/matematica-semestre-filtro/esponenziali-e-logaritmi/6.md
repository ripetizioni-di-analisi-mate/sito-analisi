The ratio of sound intensity to the reference intensity is $\dfrac{I}{I_0}=10^5$. Calculate $L=10\log_{10}\left(\dfrac{I}{I_0}\right)\,\mathrm{dB}$.

:::soluzione
$50\,\mathrm{dB}$.
:::
