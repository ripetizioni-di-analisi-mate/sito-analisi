Two solutions of the same solute at $2\,\mathrm{g/L}$ and $8\,\mathrm{g/L}$ are mixed. The volumes are additive and the solute is conserved. The mixture has a volume of $300\,\mathrm{mL}$ and a concentration of $6\,\mathrm{g/L}$. Calculate the volume of the solution at $8\,\mathrm{g/L}$.

:::soluzione
$200\,\mathrm{mL}$.
:::
