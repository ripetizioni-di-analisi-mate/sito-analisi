A regular hexagon has side length $4\,\mathrm{cm}$. Split an equilateral triangle with a vertex at the centre in half. In the resulting right triangle, use the leg of length $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ and hypotenuse of length $4\,\mathrm{cm}$ to find the apothem. Find the hexagon’s area.

:::soluzione
$24 \sqrt{3}$ cm²
:::
