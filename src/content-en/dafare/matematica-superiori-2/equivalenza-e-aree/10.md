In triangle $ABC$, base $AB$ is $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ long. The altitude from $C$ meets the base line at $H$ beyond $A$, with $AH=12\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $AC=13\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find the area by first finding the altitude.

:::soluzione
$15$ cm²
:::
