In meters, the components of two displacements are $A_x=5$, $A_y=-2$, $B_x=-2$, and $B_y=6$. Calculate the components and magnitude of their sum.

:::soluzione
$R_x=3\,\mathrm{m}$, $R_y=4\,\mathrm{m}$, $R=5\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
