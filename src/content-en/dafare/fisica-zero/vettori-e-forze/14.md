A spring exerts a force of $4\,\mathrm{N}$ when its extension is $2\,\mathrm{cm}$. A second spring exerts the same force at an extension of $5\,\mathrm{cm}$. Calculate both spring constants, assuming that both springs obey Hooke's law.

:::soluzione
$k_1=200\,\mathrm{N/m}$; $k_2=80\,\mathrm{N/m}$.
:::
