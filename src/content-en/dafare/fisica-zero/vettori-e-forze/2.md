A displacement has magnitude $9\,\mathrm{m}$ and points west. The positive direction of the axis is east. Write the component of the displacement and the component of the opposite vector.

:::soluzione
$A_x=-9\,\mathrm{m}$. The component of the opposite vector is $+9\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
