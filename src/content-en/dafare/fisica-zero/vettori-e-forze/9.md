---
titolo: "Reading a spring scale"
teoria: ["dinamometro"]
parole: [vectors, components, forces, weight, spring]
---

There are ten equal intervals between $2\,\mathrm{N}$ and $4\,\mathrm{N}$ on a spring scale. The pointer is seven intervals past $2\,\mathrm{N}$. What is the reading?

:::soluzione
The reading is $3.4\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
