In newtons, the components of two forces acting at the same point are $(4,-6)$ and $(-4,6)$. Calculate the components and magnitude of the resultant.

:::soluzione
$R_x=0\,\mathrm{N}$, $R_y=0\,\mathrm{N}$, $R=0\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
