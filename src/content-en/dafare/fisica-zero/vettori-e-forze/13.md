A spring with a natural length of $16\,\mathrm{cm}$ has a spring constant of $200\,\mathrm{N/m}$. When stretched, it exerts a spring force of magnitude $8\,\mathrm{N}$. Find the final length, assuming Hooke's law holds.

:::soluzione
$\Delta\ell=4\,\mathrm{cm}$. The final length is $20\,\mathrm{cm}$.
:::
