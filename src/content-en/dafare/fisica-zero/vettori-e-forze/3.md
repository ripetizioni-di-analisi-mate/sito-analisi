titolo: "Forces acting in the same direction"
teoria: ["somma-collineare"]
parole: [vectors, components, forces, weight, spring]

Two forces of $7\,\mathrm{N}$ and $5\,\mathrm{N}$ act to the right on the same particle. Determine the magnitude and direction of the resultant.

:::soluzione
The resultant has magnitude $12\,\mathrm{N}$ and points to the right.
:::
