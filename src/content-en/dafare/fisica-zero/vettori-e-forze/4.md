---
titolo: "Three forces along an axis"
teoria: ["somma-collineare"]
parole: [vectors, components, forces, weight, spring]
---

Three forces act on the same particle, $3\,\mathrm{N}$ to the right, $12\,\mathrm{N}$ to the left, and $4\,\mathrm{N}$ to the right. Calculate the resultant, taking right as positive.

:::soluzione
$R_x=-5\,\mathrm{N}$. The magnitude is $5\,\mathrm{N}$ and the resultant points left.
:::
