A spring with $k=120\,\mathrm{N/m}$ is stretched from its natural length of $14\,\mathrm{cm}$ to $19\,\mathrm{cm}$. Calculate the extension and the magnitude of the spring force, assuming Hooke's law holds.

:::soluzione
$\Delta\ell=0.05\,\mathrm{m}=5\,\mathrm{cm}$; $F_e=6\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
