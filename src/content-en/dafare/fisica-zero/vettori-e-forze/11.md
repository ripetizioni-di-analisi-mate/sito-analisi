---
titolo: "Finding mass from a known weight"
teoria: ["massa-e-peso"]
parole: [vectors, components, forces, weight, spring]
---

At a location where $g=10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$, an object has a weight of $3.5\,\mathrm{N}$. Calculate its mass in kilograms and grams.

:::soluzione
$m=0.35\,\mathrm{kg}=350\,\mathrm{g}$.
:::
