Two forces of $7\,\mathrm{N}$ and $24\,\mathrm{N}$ act on the same particle, the first to the right and the second upward. Determine the magnitude of the resultant.

:::soluzione
The resultant has magnitude $25\,\mathrm{N}$ and points up and to the right.
:::
