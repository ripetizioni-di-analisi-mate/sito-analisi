---
titolo: "Net work on a crate"
teoria: ["lavoro-totale"]
parole: ["work", "energy", "energy balance"]
---

A crate moves horizontally through $5\,\mathrm{m}$, pulled by a constant force of $20\,\mathrm{N}$ in the direction of motion. Friction has magnitude $6\,\mathrm{N}$. The weight and the normal force are vertical. Calculate the net work.

:::soluzione
$W_{\mathrm{tot}}=70\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
