An ideal spring with spring constant $400\,\mathrm{N/m}$ is compressed by $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ within its elastic limit. Calculate its elastic potential energy relative to its undeformed configuration.

:::soluzione
$U_e=0.50\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
