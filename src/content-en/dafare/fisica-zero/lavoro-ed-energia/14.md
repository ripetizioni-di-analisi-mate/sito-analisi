A machine delivers $900\,\mathrm{J}$ of useful energy with efficiency $60\%$. Calculate the energy input and the energy transferred in forms that do not contribute to the useful output, assuming no change in stored energy.

:::soluzione
$E_{\mathrm{ass}}=1500\,\mathrm{J}$. The energy transferred in forms that do not contribute to the useful output is $600\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
