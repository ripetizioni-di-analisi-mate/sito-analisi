A motor delivers a constant useful mechanical power of $120\,\mathrm{W}$ to raise a mass of $12\,\mathrm{kg}$ through $4\,\mathrm{m}$. The initial and final speeds are equal and friction is negligible. Calculate the time required using $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

:::soluzione
$\Delta t=4\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
