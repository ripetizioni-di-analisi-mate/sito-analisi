titolo: "Speed at the end of a fall"
teoria: ["conservazione"]

A small ball falls from rest through $1.8\,\mathrm{m}$. Calculate its final speed using $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and assuming negligible air resistance.

:::soluzione
$v_f=6\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
