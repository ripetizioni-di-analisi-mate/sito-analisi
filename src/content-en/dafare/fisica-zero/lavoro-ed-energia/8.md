A cart of mass $10\,\mathrm{kg}$, modeled as a particle, travels at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ on a horizontal surface. A constant resistive force of $20\,\mathrm{N}$ is the only force doing work. Calculate the stopping distance.

:::soluzione
$s=4\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
