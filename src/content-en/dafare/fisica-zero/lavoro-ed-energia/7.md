teoria: ["teorema-energia-cinetica"]

A body of mass $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ starts from rest. The forces acting on it do net work of $49\,\mathrm{J}$. Calculate its final speed.

:::soluzione
$v_f=7\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
