---
titolo: "Adding charges of opposite signs"
teoria: [carica-elettrica, conservazione-carica]
parole: [electricity, circuits, electric charge]
---

Two bodies have charges $-4\,\mathrm{\mu C}$ and $+9\,\mathrm{\mu C}$. Calculate the total charge of the system.

:::soluzione
$q_{\mathrm{tot}}=+5\,\mathrm{\mu C}$.
:::
