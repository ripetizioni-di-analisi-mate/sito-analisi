titolo: "Energy dissipated by a resistor"
teoria: [legge-ohm, potenza-elettrica, energia-elettrica]
parole: [electricity, circuits, electric charge]

An ohmic resistor of $12\,\Omega$ maintains constant resistance while connected across $12\,\mathrm{V}$ for $2$ hours. Calculate the power dissipated and the energy absorbed in kilowatt-hours.

:::soluzione
$P_e=12\,\mathrm{W}$; $E=0.024\,\mathrm{kWh}$.
:::
