An ohmic resistor of $300\,\Omega$ at constant temperature has a voltage of $6\,\mathrm{V}$ across its terminals. Calculate the current in amperes and milliamperes.

:::soluzione
$I=0.020\,\mathrm{A}=20\,\mathrm{mA}$.
:::
