---
titolo: "Charge after a transfer"
teoria: [conservazione-carica]
parole: [electricity, circuits, electric charge]
---

Two initially neutral bodies exchange charge only with each other. In the end, the first has charge $-7\,\mathrm{\mu C}$. What is the final charge of the second?

:::soluzione
$q_2=+7\,\mathrm{\mu C}$.
:::
