titolo: "Voltages in a series connection"
teoria: [resistori-serie, legge-ohm]

Two ohmic resistors $R_1=5\,\Omega$ and $R_2=10\,\Omega$ are connected in series across an ideal $12\,\mathrm{V}$ source. Assuming constant resistances and ideal wires, determine the current and the two voltages.

:::soluzione
$I=0.80\,\mathrm{A}$, $V_1=4\,\mathrm{V}$, $V_2=8\,\mathrm{V}$.
:::
