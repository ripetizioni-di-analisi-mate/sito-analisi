Two ohmic resistors $R_1=4\,\Omega$ and $R_2=12\,\Omega$ are connected in parallel across an ideal $12\,\mathrm{V}$ source. Assuming constant resistances and ideal wires, find the equivalent resistance, the branch currents, and the total current.

:::soluzione
$R_{\mathrm{eq}}=3\,\Omega$; $I_1=3\,\mathrm{A}$, $I_2=1\,\mathrm{A}$, $I=4\,\mathrm{A}$.
:::
