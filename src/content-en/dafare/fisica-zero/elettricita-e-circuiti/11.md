A resistor of $4\,\Omega$ is in series with the parallel combination of $12\,\Omega$ and $6\,\Omega$. An ideal source maintains $12\,\mathrm{V}$ across the combination. All the resistors are ohmic with constant resistances, and the wires are ideal. Calculate the total resistance, the total current, and the currents in the two branches.

:::soluzione
$R_{\mathrm{eq}}=8\,\Omega$ and $I=1.5\,\mathrm{A}$. The $12\,\Omega$ branch carries $0.50\,\mathrm{A}$, and the $6\,\Omega$ branch carries $1\,\mathrm{A}$.
:::
