---
titolo: "Average current in a wire"
teoria: [corrente-elettrica]
parole: [electricity, circuits, electric charge]
---

A charge of $15\,\mathrm{C}$ passes through a cross section of a wire in the conventional direction in $3\,\mathrm{s}$. Calculate the average current.

:::soluzione
$I=5\,\mathrm{A}$.
:::
