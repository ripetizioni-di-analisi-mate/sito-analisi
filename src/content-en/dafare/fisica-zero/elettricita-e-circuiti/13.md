teoria: [energia-elettrica]

A device draws a constant power of $18\,\mathrm{W}$ for $20$ minutes. Calculate the energy in joules and kilowatt-hours.

:::soluzione
$E=21\,600\,\mathrm{J}=0.0060\,\mathrm{kWh}$.
:::
