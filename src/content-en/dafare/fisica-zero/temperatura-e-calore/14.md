titolo: From ice to lukewarm water
teoria: [processi-in-piu-fasi, calore-latente, legge-calorimetria]

Calculate the heat needed to turn $0.080\,\mathrm{kg}$ of ice at $0\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ into water at $25\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ at standard atmospheric pressure without heat losses. Use $L_f=334\,000\,\mathrm{J/kg}$ and $c_a=4180\,\mathrm{J/(kg\cdot K)}$.

:::soluzione
$Q_{\mathrm{tot}}=35\,080\,\mathrm{J}\approx35.1\,\mathrm{kJ}$.
:::
