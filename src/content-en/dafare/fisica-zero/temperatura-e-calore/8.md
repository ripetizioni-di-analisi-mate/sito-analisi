titolo: Unknown specific heat capacity
teoria: [legge-calorimetria, calore-specifico]
parole: [temperature, heat, thermal equilibrium]

A homogeneous sample of mass $0.70\,\mathrm{kg}$ absorbs $12\,600\,\mathrm{J}$ and its temperature rises by $20\,\mathrm{K}$. Determine its specific heat capacity, assuming it is constant and there is no change of state.

:::soluzione
$c=900\,\mathrm{J/(kg\cdot K)}$.
:::
