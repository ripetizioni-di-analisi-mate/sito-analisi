---
titolo: Final temperature after heating
teoria: [legge-calorimetria]
parole: [temperature, heat, thermal equilibrium]
---

A sample of $0.25\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water at $15\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ absorbs $20\,900\,\mathrm{J}$. Use $c_a=4180\,\mathrm{J/(kg\cdot K)}$ to find the final temperature, assuming no change of state.

:::soluzione
The final temperature is $35\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$.
:::
