An iron block of mass $0.80\,\mathrm{kg}$ cools from $90\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ to $30\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. With $c=450\,\mathrm{J/(kg\cdot K)}$ constant, calculate the heat transferred to or from the block in the absence of a change of state.

:::soluzione
$Q=-21\,600\,\mathrm{J}$. The block releases $21\,600\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
