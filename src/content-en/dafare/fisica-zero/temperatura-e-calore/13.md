titolo: Mass of water vaporized
teoria: [calore-latente]

Water already at $100\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ absorbs $67\,800\,\mathrm{J}$ solely for vaporization at standard atmospheric pressure. Given $L_v=2\,260\,000\,\mathrm{J/kg}$, determine the mass converted to vapor.

:::soluzione
$m=0.030\,\mathrm{kg}=30\,\mathrm{g}$.
:::
