---
titolo: Temperature rise of an object
teoria: [capacita-termica]
parole: [temperature, heat, thermal equilibrium]
---

An object with a constant heat capacity $C=900\,\mathrm{J/K}$ absorbs $4500\,\mathrm{J}$. Determine the temperature rise, assuming no change of state.

:::soluzione
$\Delta T=5\,\mathrm{K}$.
:::
