---
titolo: Temperature change during cooling
teoria: [variazione-temperatura]
parole: [temperature, heat, thermal equilibrium]
---

An object's temperature changes from $45\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ to $12\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. Calculate the temperature change in kelvins.

:::soluzione
$\Delta T=-33\,\mathrm{K}$.
:::
