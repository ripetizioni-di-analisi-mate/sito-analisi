---
titolo: Two equal masses of water
teoria: [bilancio-termico]
parole: [temperature, heat, thermal equilibrium]
---

Mix $0.20\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water at $60\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ with $0.20\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water at $20\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. The container is insulated and has negligible heat capacity. The specific heat capacity of the water is the same for both samples and remains constant. Determine the final temperature.

:::soluzione
The final temperature is $40\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$.
:::
