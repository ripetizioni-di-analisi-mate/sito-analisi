---
titolo: Mixing unequal masses
teoria: [bilancio-termico]
parole: [temperature, heat, thermal equilibrium]
---

Mix $0.30\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water at $80\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ with $0.70\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water at $20\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. The container is insulated and has negligible heat capacity. Assume the specific heat capacities are equal and constant, and calculate the final temperature.

:::soluzione
The final temperature is $38\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$.
:::
