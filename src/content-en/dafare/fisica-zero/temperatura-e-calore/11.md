---
titolo: Equilibrium between aluminum and iron
teoria: [bilancio-termico, capacita-termica]
parole: [temperature, heat, thermal equilibrium]
---

An aluminum block of mass $0.30\,\mathrm{kg}$ at $90\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ is brought into contact with $0.60\,\mathrm{kg}$ of iron at $30\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. The system is isolated and the supports have negligible heat capacity. Assuming $c_{\mathrm{Al}}=900\,\mathrm{J/(kg\cdot K)}$ and $c_{\mathrm{Fe}}=450\,\mathrm{J/(kg\cdot K)}$ are constant, determine the final temperature.

:::soluzione
The final temperature is $60\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$.
:::
