A rectangle has a base of $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ and a height of $8\,\mathrm{cm}$. A second rectangle has the same area and a base of $6\,\mathrm{cm}$. Calculate the height of the second rectangle.

:::soluzione
$h=4\,\mathrm{cm}$.
:::
