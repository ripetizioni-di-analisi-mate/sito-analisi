A container with an empty mass of $4\,\mathrm{g}$ holds $3\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ of a liquid with density $1.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. Calculate the total mass and the value at which the line for total mass intersects the mass axis.

:::soluzione
$m=8.5\,\mathrm{g}$. The intercept on the mass axis is $4\,\mathrm{g}$.
:::
