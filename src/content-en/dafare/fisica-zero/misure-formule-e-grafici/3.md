A scale has a smallest division of $0.4\,\mathrm{mL}$. Take half the smallest division as the only uncertainty. For a reading of $7.2\,\mathrm{mL}$, report the measurement with its uncertainty.

:::soluzione
$V=(7.2\pm0.2)\,\mathrm{mL}$.
:::
