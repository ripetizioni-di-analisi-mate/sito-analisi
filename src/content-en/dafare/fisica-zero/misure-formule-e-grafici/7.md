Compare $(40\pm1)\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $(100\pm2)\,\mathrm{cm}$, assuming their uncertainties were estimated using comparable rules. Which has the smaller relative uncertainty?

:::soluzione
The second measurement. The relative uncertainties are $2.5\%$ and $2\%$, respectively.
:::
