A rectangle has an area of $48\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and a height of $6\,\mathrm{cm}$. Derive a formula for the base and calculate its value.

:::soluzione
$b=\dfrac{A}{h}=8\,\mathrm{cm}$.
:::
