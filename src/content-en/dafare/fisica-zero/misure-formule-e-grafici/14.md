A table lists the volume–mass pairs $(2\,\mathrm{cm^3},8\,\mathrm{g})$ and $(4\,\mathrm{cm^3},16\,\mathrm{g})$. Assume that direct proportionality holds between the data points. Calculate the mass at $V=3\,\mathrm{cm^3}$.

:::soluzione
$m=12\,\mathrm{g}$.
:::
