A homogeneous material has a mass of $15\,\mathrm{g}$ for a volume of $5\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. Under the same conditions, what is the mass of a sample with a volume of $12\,\mathrm{cm^3}$?

:::soluzione
$m=36\,\mathrm{g}$.
:::
