A body of mass $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ rests on a horizontal surface and is also subject to a downward vertical push of $7\,\mathrm{N}$. Calculate the normal force using $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

:::soluzione
$N=27\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
