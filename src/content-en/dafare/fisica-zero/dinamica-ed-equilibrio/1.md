A body of mass $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ is subject to a force of $15\,\mathrm{N}$ to the right and a force of $6\,\mathrm{N}$ to the left. The vertical forces balance. Calculate the net force and the acceleration, taking rightward as positive.

:::soluzione
$R_x=9\,\mathrm{N}$; $a_x=3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
:::
