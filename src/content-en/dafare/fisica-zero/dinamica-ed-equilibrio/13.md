A cart of mass $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ starts from rest on a horizontal frictionless track. A constant horizontal force of $6\,\mathrm{N}$ acts on it for $4\,\mathrm{s}$. Calculate its final velocity and the distance traveled. The vertical forces balance.

:::soluzione
$v=12\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $s=24\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
