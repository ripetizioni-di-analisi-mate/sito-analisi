A body slides across a horizontal surface at $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Friction is the only horizontal force, with $\mu_d=0.40$. Use $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the acceleration component along the initial direction of motion, the stopping time, and the stopping distance.

:::soluzione
$a=-4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$; $t=2\,\mathrm{s}$; $s=8\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
