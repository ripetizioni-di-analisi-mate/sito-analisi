A crate of mass $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ is initially at rest on a horizontal surface with $\mu_s=0.50$ and $\mu_d=0.25$. A constant horizontal force of $24\,\mathrm{N}$ pulls it to the right. Use $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Determine whether it slides and find its acceleration once it starts moving.

:::soluzione
The crate begins to slide. Then $F_d=10\,\mathrm{N}$ and $a=3.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ to the right.
:::
