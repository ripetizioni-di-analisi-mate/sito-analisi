A crate of mass $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ is initially at rest on a horizontal surface with $\mu_s=0.50$. A horizontal force of $8\,\mathrm{N}$ pulls it to the right. Use $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the maximum and actual static friction.

:::soluzione
$F_{s,\max}=20\,\mathrm{N}$. The crate remains at rest and the actual frictional force is $8\,\mathrm{N}$ to the left.
:::
