A mass of $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ hangs from an ideal vertical string and accelerates upward at $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Calculate the tension using $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and neglecting air resistance.

:::soluzione
$T=36\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
