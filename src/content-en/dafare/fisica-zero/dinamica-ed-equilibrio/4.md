A crate moves to the right at constant velocity. The only horizontal resistive force has magnitude $9\,\mathrm{N}$. Find the horizontal pulling force and the net horizontal force.

:::soluzione
The pull is $9\,\mathrm{N}$ to the right. The net horizontal force is zero.
:::
