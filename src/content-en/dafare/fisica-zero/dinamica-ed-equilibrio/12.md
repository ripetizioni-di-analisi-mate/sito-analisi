A body of mass $8\,\mathrm{kg}$ slides at constant velocity on a horizontal surface with $\mu_d=0.15$. Calculate the horizontal pull required using $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The only other forces are weight, the normal force, and friction.

:::soluzione
$F=12\,\mathrm{N}$ in the direction of motion.
:::
