Two carts of masses $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $6\,\mathrm{kg}$ push against each other on a horizontal frictionless track. A force of $12\,\mathrm{N}$ acts on the first cart to the left. Find the force on the second cart and the magnitudes of the accelerations. The vertical forces balance.

:::soluzione
The force on the second cart is $12\,\mathrm{N}$ to the right. The first cart accelerates at $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ to the left and the second at $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ to the right.
:::
