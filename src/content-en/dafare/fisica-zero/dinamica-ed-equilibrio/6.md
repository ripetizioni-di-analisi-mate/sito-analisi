A mass of $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ rests on a horizontal surface. Only its weight and the normal force from the surface act on it. Calculate the normal force using $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

:::soluzione
$N=29.43\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
