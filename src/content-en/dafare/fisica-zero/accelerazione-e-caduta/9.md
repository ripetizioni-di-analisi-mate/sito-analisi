A vehicle traveling at $54\,\mathrm{km/h}$ comes to rest in $5\,\mathrm{s}$ with constant acceleration. Calculate the acceleration along the initial direction of motion and the braking distance.

:::soluzione
$a=-3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$; $s=37.5\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
