The velocity along an axis changes from $-12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in $2\,\mathrm{s}$. Calculate the average acceleration and compare the initial and final speeds.

:::soluzione
$a_m=4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The speed decreases from $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
