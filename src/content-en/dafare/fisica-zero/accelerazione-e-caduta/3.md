A particle has an initial velocity of $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and a constant acceleration of $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Calculate its velocity after $5\,\mathrm{s}$.

:::soluzione
$v=13\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
