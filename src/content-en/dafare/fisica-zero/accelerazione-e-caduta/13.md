An object is launched upward at $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and returns to its initial height without encountering any obstacles. Neglect air resistance and use $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the flight time, total displacement, and distance traveled.

:::soluzione
$t_r=2\,\mathrm{s}$; $\Delta y=0$; $s=10\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
