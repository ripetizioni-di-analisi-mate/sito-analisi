A cart travels at $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and brakes with a constant acceleration of $-2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Calculate the time and distance it takes to stop. The axis is positive in the initial direction of motion.

:::soluzione
$t_{\mathrm{arresto}}=6\,\mathrm{s}$; $s_{\mathrm{frenata}}=36\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
