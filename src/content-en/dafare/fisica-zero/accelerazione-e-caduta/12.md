An object is launched upward at $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Neglect air resistance and use $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Calculate the ascent time and the maximum height above the launch point.

:::soluzione
$t_s=3\,\mathrm{s}$; $h_{\max}=45\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
