An object is released from rest and falls freely for $1.5\,\mathrm{s}$ without reaching the ground. Neglect air resistance and use $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Calculate the distance fallen and the final speed.

:::soluzione
$h=11.25\,\mathrm{m}$, $v=15\,\mathrm{m/s}$ downward.
:::
