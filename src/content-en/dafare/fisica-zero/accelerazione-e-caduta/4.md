A cart's velocity changes from $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ with a constant acceleration of $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Calculate the elapsed time.

:::soluzione
$t=4\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
