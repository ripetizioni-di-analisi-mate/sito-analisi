A vehicle travels at $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for $1\,\mathrm{s}$ after a signal, then brakes uniformly and comes to rest in $2\,\mathrm{s}$. Find the total distance traveled between the signal and the stop.

:::soluzione
$s_{\mathrm{tot}}=20\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
