A particle starts at $x_0=5\,\mathrm{m}$ with initial velocity $v_0=2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and constant acceleration $a=2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Calculate its position and displacement after $3\,\mathrm{s}$.

:::soluzione
$x=20\,\mathrm{m}$; $\Delta x=15\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
