An object is released from rest $20\,\mathrm{m}$ above the ground. Neglect air resistance and use $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the time of fall and the speed just before the object reaches the ground.

:::soluzione
$t=2\,\mathrm{s}$; $v=20\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
