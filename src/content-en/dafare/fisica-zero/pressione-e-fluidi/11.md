A body displaces $0.0015\,\mathrm{m^3}$ of water with a density of $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. Calculate the buoyant force using $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

:::soluzione
$F_A=15\,\mathrm{N}$, upward.
:::
