An open container holds still water to a depth of $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$. Its horizontal bottom has an area of $0.050\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Calculate the force on the bottom in excess of the force due to atmospheric pressure, using $\rho=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

:::soluzione
$F=150\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
