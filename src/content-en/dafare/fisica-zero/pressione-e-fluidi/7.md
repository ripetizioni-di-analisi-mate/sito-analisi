An open tank contains still water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. Atmospheric pressure is $100000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Calculate the absolute pressure at a depth of $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$, using $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

:::soluzione
$p=108000\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
:::
