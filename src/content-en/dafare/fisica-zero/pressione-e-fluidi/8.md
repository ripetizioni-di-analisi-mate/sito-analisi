A sample of oil at rest has a density of $800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. At a certain depth the pressure exceeds the surface pressure by $12000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Calculate the depth using $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

:::soluzione
$h=1.5\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
