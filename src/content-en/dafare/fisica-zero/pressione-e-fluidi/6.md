In still water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$, calculate the pressure relative to the surface at a depth of $3\,\mathrm{m}$. Use $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

:::soluzione
$p_{\mathrm{rel}}=30000\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
:::
