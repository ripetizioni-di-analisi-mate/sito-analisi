---
titolo: "Percentage of a block submerged"
teoria: ["galleggiamento"]
parole: ["pressure", "liquids in equilibrium", "forces"]
---

A homogeneous block of density $750\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ floats in water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. Calculate the percentage of its volume submerged, neglecting the buoyant force of the air.

:::soluzione
$75\%$ of the volume is submerged.
:::
