---
titolo: "Force on a flat face"
teoria: ["pressione"]
parole: ["pressure", "liquids in equilibrium", "forces"]
---

A uniform pressure of $6000\,\mathrm{Pa}$ acts on a flat face of area $0.015\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Calculate the force exerted on that face.

:::soluzione
$F=90\,\mathrm{N}$, perpendicular to the face.
:::
