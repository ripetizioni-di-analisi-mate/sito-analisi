A homogeneous liquid has a mass of $0.48\,\mathrm{kg}$ and a volume of $0.60\,\mathrm{L}$. Calculate its density in $\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.

:::soluzione
$\rho=800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.
:::
