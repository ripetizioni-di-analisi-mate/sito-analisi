A body of mass $5\,\mathrm{kg}$ and volume $0.002\,\mathrm{m^3}$ hangs at rest from a spring scale, fully submerged in water and clear of the bottom. Calculate the scale reading using $\rho_f=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

:::soluzione
$T=30\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
