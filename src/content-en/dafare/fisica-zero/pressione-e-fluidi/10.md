The pistons of an ideal hydraulic press are at the same height and have areas of $5\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and $50\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. Neglect the piston weights and friction, and assume the external pressure is the same on both pistons. What force on the smaller piston balances $700\,\mathrm{N}$ on the larger one?

:::soluzione
$F_1=70\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
