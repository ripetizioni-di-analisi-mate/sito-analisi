titolo: "Immersed volume of a float"
teoria: ["galleggiamento"]
parole: ["pressure", "liquids in equilibrium", "forces"]
---

An object with a mass of $1.2\,\mathrm{kg}$ floats at rest in water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. Calculate its immersed volume in cubic meters and liters, neglecting the buoyant force of the air.

:::soluzione
$V_{\mathrm{imm}}=0.0012\,\mathrm{m^3}=1.2\,\mathrm{L}$.
:::
