An object starts moving when a stopwatch reads $1\,\mathrm{min}$ and $20\,\mathrm{s}$ and stops when it reads $2\,\mathrm{min}$ and $5\,\mathrm{s}$. Calculate the elapsed time in seconds.

:::soluzione
The elapsed time is $45\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
