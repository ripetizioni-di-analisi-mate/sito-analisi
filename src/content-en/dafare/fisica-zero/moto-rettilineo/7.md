At $t=0$ a cart starts at $x_0=-5\,\mathrm{m}$ and maintains velocity $v=3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for $4\,\mathrm{s}$. Calculate its final position.

:::soluzione
$x=7\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
