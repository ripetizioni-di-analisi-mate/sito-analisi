An object moves from $x=15\,\mathrm{m}$ to $x=-9\,\mathrm{m}$ in $6\,\mathrm{s}$. Calculate the average velocity along the axis.

:::soluzione
$v_m=-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
