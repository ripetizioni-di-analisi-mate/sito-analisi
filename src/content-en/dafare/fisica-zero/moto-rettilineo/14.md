---
titolo: "Catching up after a delayed start"
teoria: ["partenza-ritardata", "incontro"]
parole: [straight-line motion, position, average velocity, position equation, time]
---

Object $A$ starts at the origin at $t=0$ with a velocity of $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Object $B$ starts at the same origin at $t=6\,\mathrm{s}$ with a velocity of $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the same direction. Assume uniform motion for each object from the time it starts. Calculate the time at which $B$ catches up with $A$ and the position they share.

:::soluzione
After $B$ starts, the objects meet at $t=10\,\mathrm{s}$ at position $x=20\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
