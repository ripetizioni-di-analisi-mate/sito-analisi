An object starts at $x_0=24\,\mathrm{m}$ at time $t=0$ with a constant velocity of $-6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. It continues moving until it reaches the origin. At what time does it reach the origin?

:::soluzione
$t=4\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
