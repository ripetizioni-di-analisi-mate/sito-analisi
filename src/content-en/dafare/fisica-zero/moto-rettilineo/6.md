Convert $72\,\mathrm{km/h}$ to meters per second and $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to kilometers per hour.

:::soluzione
$72\,\mathrm{km/h}=20\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $12\,\mathrm{m/s}=43.2\,\mathrm{km/h}$.
:::
