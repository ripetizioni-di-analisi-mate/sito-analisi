A person travels $40\,\mathrm{m}$ in $20\,\mathrm{s}$ and then remains at rest for $5\,\mathrm{s}$. Calculate the average speed over the entire interval.

:::soluzione
$u_m=1.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
