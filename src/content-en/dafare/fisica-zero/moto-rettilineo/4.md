An object moves from $x=4\,\mathrm{m}$ at $t=2\,\mathrm{s}$ to $x=28\,\mathrm{m}$ at $t=10\,\mathrm{s}$. Calculate the average velocity.

:::soluzione
$v_m=3\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
