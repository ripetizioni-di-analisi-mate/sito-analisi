A person starts at $x=1\,\mathrm{m}$, reaches $x=9\,\mathrm{m}$, and returns to $x=3\,\mathrm{m}$. Calculate the displacement and the distance traveled.

:::soluzione
$\Delta x=2\,\mathrm{m}$; $s=14\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
