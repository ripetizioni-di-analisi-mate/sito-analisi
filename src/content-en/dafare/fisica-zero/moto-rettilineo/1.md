An object moves from $x_i=6\,\mathrm{m}$ to $x_f=-2\,\mathrm{m}$. Calculate its displacement along the axis and the magnitude of that displacement.

:::soluzione
The displacement is $\Delta x=-8\,\mathrm{m}$ and its magnitude is $8\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
