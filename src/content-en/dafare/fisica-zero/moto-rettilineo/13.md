At $t=0$, object $A$ starts at $x=0$ with velocity $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and object $B$ starts at $x=15\,\mathrm{m}$ with velocity $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Both move uniformly until they meet. Calculate when and where they meet.

:::soluzione
$t=5\,\mathrm{s}$; $x=5\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
