titolo: "A straight line on a position-time graph"
teoria: ["grafico-orario"]
parole: [straight-line motion, position, average velocity, position equation, time]
---

The graph of uniform motion passes through $(0\,\mathrm{s},4\,\mathrm{m})$ and $(3\,\mathrm{s},13\,\mathrm{m})$. Find the velocity and the position equation.

:::soluzione
$v=3\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $x(t)=4\,\mathrm{m}+(3\,\mathrm{m/s})t$.
:::
