An object starts moving at $t_0=5\,\mathrm{s}$ from $x_0=2\,\mathrm{m}$ with a constant velocity of $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Calculate its position at $t=9\,\mathrm{s}$.

:::soluzione
$x=18\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
