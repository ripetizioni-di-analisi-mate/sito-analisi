A person travels $90\,\mathrm{m}$ along a straight line in $60\,\mathrm{s}$ and returns to the starting point in another $90\,\mathrm{s}$. Calculate the average speed and average velocity for the entire trip.

:::soluzione
$u_m=1.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $v_m=0\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
