An ideal gas mixture has total pressure $750\,\mathrm{mmHg}$ and water-vapour pressure $50\,\mathrm{mmHg}$. Oxygen represents $20.0\%$ of dry-gas moles. Calculate its partial pressure.

:::soluzione
$140\,\mathrm{mmHg}$.

Dry-gas pressure is $750-50=700\,\mathrm{mmHg}$. Because oxygen fraction refers to dry gases, $p_{\mathrm{O_2}}=0.200\cdot700=140\,\mathrm{mmHg}$. Applying the fraction to total pressure would also allocate an oxygen share to water vapour. Dalton contributions must use fractions and totals defined for the same mixture.
:::
