An ideal dilute aqueous $\mathrm{NaCl}$ solution has molality $0.0500\,\mathrm{mol\,kg^{-1}}$ and van’t Hoff factor $i=2$. Use $K_f=1.86\,\mathrm{K\,kg\,mol^{-1}}$ and pure-water freezing temperature $0\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. Calculate solution freezing temperature.

:::soluzione
$T_f=-0.186\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$.

The specified factor accounts for two particles per formula unit. The depression is $\Delta T_f=iK_fb=2\cdot1.86\cdot0.0500=0.186\,\mathrm K$. Final temperature is $0-0.186=-0.186\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. The depression is a positive magnitude that must be subtracted from the pure-solvent temperature. Omitting dissociation would halve the predicted change.
:::
