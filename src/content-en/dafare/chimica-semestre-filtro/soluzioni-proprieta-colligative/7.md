Mix $30.0\,\mathrm{mL}$ of a $0.400\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$ solution and $70.0\,\mathrm{mL}$ of a $0.100\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$ solution of the same solute. Assume additive volumes and no reactions. Calculate final molarity.

:::soluzione
$0.190\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$.

Total amount is $0.400\cdot0.0300+0.100\cdot0.0700=0.0190\,\mathrm{mol}$. Total volume is $0.1000\,\mathrm L$, so concentration is $0.190\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$. An arithmetic mean would ignore the unequal volumes. The answer lies closer to the more dilute solution because that solution contributes the larger volume. It also lies between the two initial concentrations.
:::
