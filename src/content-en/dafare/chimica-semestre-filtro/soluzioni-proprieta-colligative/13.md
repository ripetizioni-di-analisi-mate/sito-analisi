An ideal dilute glucose solution is $0.200\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$ at $310\,\mathrm K$. Calculate its osmotic pressure against pure water across a glucose-retaining membrane. Use $i=1$ and $R=0.082057\,\mathrm{L\,atm\,mol^{-1}\,K^{-1}}$.

:::soluzione
$\pi\simeq5.09\,\mathrm{atm}$.

The van’t Hoff relation gives $\pi=icRT=1\cdot0.200\cdot0.082057\cdot310=5.087534\,\mathrm{atm}$. Absolute temperature is provided and glucose is undissociated, giving $5.09\,\mathrm{atm}$ after rounding. The pressure describes the excess required to stop osmosis; it is not a prescribed mechanical pressure for every biological compartment. The comparison across the membrane must always be specified.
:::
