A solution contains $4.50\,\mathrm g$ of glucose in a final volume of $250\,\mathrm{mL}$. Use $M=180\,\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$ to calculate molarity.

:::soluzione
$0.100\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$.

Glucose amount is $n=\dfrac{4.50}{180}=0.0250\,\mathrm{mol}$. Volume is $0.250\,\mathrm L$, giving $c=\dfrac{0.0250}{0.250}=0.100\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$. The amount in moles and the concentration are different quantities. Failing to convert millilitres into litres would introduce a factor of one thousand.
:::
