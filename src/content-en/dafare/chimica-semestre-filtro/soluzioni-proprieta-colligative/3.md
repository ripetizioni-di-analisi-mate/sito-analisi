A $15.0\,\mathrm{mL}$ ethanol aliquot is brought to a final solution volume of $250\,\mathrm{mL}$. Calculate its volume percentage.

:::soluzione
$6.00\%$ by volume.

The final solution volume is specified, so the volume percentage is $100\dfrac{15.0}{250}=6.00\%$. No assumption of additive water and ethanol volumes is needed. The operation is completed at the final volume mark; subtracting the aliquot volume does not necessarily give the exact volume of water added.
:::
