---

Write the complement of 5′-ACGTA-3′ first aligned and then in the 5′→3′ direction. Count phosphodiester links in each linear strand and the whole duplex without nicks.

:::soluzione
The aligned complement is 3′-TGCAT-5′; in the requested direction it is 5′-TACGT-3′. Complement the bases, then reverse their order to respect antiparallelism. Each five-nucleotide strand has $5-1=4$ links; the duplex has eight. These covalent links must be distinguished from hydrogen bonds between paired bases.
:::
