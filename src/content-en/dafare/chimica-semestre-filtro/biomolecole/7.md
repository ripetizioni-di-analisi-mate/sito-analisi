---

In the conventional D-glucopyranose Haworth projection, CH₂OH and C1 OH are above the ring. Identify the anomer and explain its interconversion with the other in water. Compare with a methyl glucoside whose anomeric centre is an acetal, without hydrolysis.

:::soluzione
The form is β because both groups are on the same side. The hemiacetal can open to the planar aldehyde and reclose as α or β; the rotation change during equilibration is mutarotation. The methyl glucoside has an acetal-locked centre and requires hydrolysis to recover that opening mechanism.
:::
