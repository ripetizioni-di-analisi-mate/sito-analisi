Identify the amino acids with $R_1=\mathrm{CH_2COO^-}$ and $R_2=\mathrm{CH_2CONH_2}$. At pH 7, assume the shown forms predominate. Calculate the side-chain charge change when replacing the first with the second while retaining all other groups.

:::soluzione
The first is aspartate (Asp, D), with carboxylate charge $-1$; the second is asparagine (Asn, N), with a neutral amide. Thus $\Delta q=0-(-1)=+1$. A negative charge is lost while amide polarity remains. The difference concerns side chains and does not require recalculating protein-terminal charges.
:::
