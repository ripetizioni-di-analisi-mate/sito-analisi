---
titolo: "Complex-lipid backbones"
teoria: ["lipidi-complessi"]
parole: ["Complex-lipid backbones"]
---

Identify: A, sphingosine with an amide-linked fatty acid and no other head; B, the same structure with phosphocholine; C, glycerol with two ester-linked acyl residues and phosphocholine. For each, state the family and fatty-acid bond type.

:::soluzione
A is ceramide, a sphingolipid with an amide bond. B is sphingomyelin, both a sphingolipid and phospholipid, with an amide-linked fatty acid. C is phosphatidylcholine, a glycerophospholipid with two esters. The same head in B and C does not make their backbones identical: sphingosine or glycerol and linkage through N or O must be identified.
:::
