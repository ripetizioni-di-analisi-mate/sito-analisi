In two separate experiments, an unmethylated cytosine is deaminated and $0.040\,\mathrm{mol}$ GSH is completely oxidised to GSSG, the only sulfur-containing product. Identify the base product and calculate GSSG formation. State which group changes in each experiment.

:::soluzione
Cytosine becomes uracil because its exocyclic amine is replaced by a carbonyl. In glutathione, two thiols are oxidised to one disulfide, so $n(\mathrm{GSSG})=\dfrac{0.040}{2}=0.020\,\mathrm{mol}$. The modifications are distinct: the first changes a base group, while the second joins two glutathione units through sulfur.
:::
