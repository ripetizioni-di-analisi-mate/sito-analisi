A basic amino acid has successive $pK_a$ values $2.20$, $9.00$, $10.50$ and successive charges $+2,+1,0,-1$. In dilute solution, approximate $pI$ using the two equilibria adjacent to the neutral species. State the mean-charge sign at pH 8.

:::soluzione
The neutral species is bracketed by $9.00$ and $10.50$, so $pI\simeq\dfrac{9.00+10.50}{2}=9.75$. At pH 8 the mean charge is positive because pH is below $pI$. The value $2.20$ describes the $+2$ to $+1$ transition and does not bracket the neutral form; its contribution is neglected in the approximation.
:::
