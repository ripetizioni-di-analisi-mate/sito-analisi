Compare cellulose, amylose and glycogen in bond configuration and position, branching and monomer type. Can an enzyme specific for α$(1\to4)$ be assumed active on cellulose? Explain using structure.

:::soluzione
Cellulose: β$(1\to4)$ and linear; amylose: mainly linear with α$(1\to4)$; glycogen: α$(1\to4)$ with α$(1\to6)$ branches. All are glucose homopolymers. Cellulose β configuration changes the geometry recognised by the enzyme, so the stated α specificity does not justify inferring cellulose activity.
:::
