$0.060\,\mathrm{mol}$ of a pure triacylglycerol with three identical tails is completely saponified. Calculate minimum NaOH consumption and amounts of glycerol and carboxylate produced. Distinguish the number of glycerol backbones from hydrolysed bonds.

:::soluzione
Each molecule has one backbone and three esters. Thus $n(\mathrm{NaOH})=3(0.060)=0.180\,\mathrm{mol}$, $n(\mathrm{glycerol})=0.060\,\mathrm{mol}$ and $n(\mathrm{RCOO^-})=0.180\,\mathrm{mol}$. The three tails reappear in three carboxylates; any experimental excess of base does not change stoichiometric consumption.
:::
