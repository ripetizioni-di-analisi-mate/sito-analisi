The cysteine Fischer projection has COOH at the top, CH₂SH at the bottom, NH₂ on the left and H on the right. Assign D/L and R/S and explain the difference from alanine with the same arrangement. Also explain why glycine receives no Cα R/S assignment.

:::soluzione
It is L-cysteine with Cα R. NH₂ on the left assigns L; in CIP priorities, side-chain sulfur makes CH₂SH outrank carboxyl. Two priorities are swapped relative to alanine, so absolute configuration becomes R rather than S. Glycine has two identical Cα hydrogens and lacks that stereogenic centre.
:::
