parole: ["Glucose oxidation selectivity"]
---

Compare three D-glucose oxidations: only the C1 aldehyde; only the C6 primary alcohol; both ends. Identify the products and number of carboxyl groups. Retain all other groups and the carbon skeleton.

:::soluzione
C1 alone gives gluconic acid with one carboxyl; C6 alone gives glucuronic acid with one carboxyl; both ends give glucaric acid, an aldaric acid with two carboxyls. The count of one in the first two cases does not distinguish them: position matters. Carbonyl reduction would instead give sorbitol and belongs to the opposite transformation.
:::
