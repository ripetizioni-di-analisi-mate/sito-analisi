Calculate molar mass and peptide-bond count of linear tripeptide Ala-Ala-Ala with free termini. Use $M(\mathrm{Ala})=89.09\,\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$ and $M(\mathrm{H_2O})=18.02\,\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$. Explain why subtracting three waters would describe a different product.

:::soluzione
Three linear residues have two links, so $M=3(89.09)-2(18.02)=231.23\,\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$. A third water subtraction would correspond to another bond, as in cyclic closure between termini. The requested product instead retains free N and C termini and excludes that closure.
:::
