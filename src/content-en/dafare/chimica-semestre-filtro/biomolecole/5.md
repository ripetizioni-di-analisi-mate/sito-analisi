One protein chain contains eight cysteines, all paired into internal disulfides. Only these bonds are completely reduced. Calculate bridges removed and thiols formed. Distinguish the certain effect on sequence from possible folding changes.

:::soluzione
There are $\dfrac{8}{2}=4$ bridges, and reduction generates eight thiols. The peptide backbone remains intact, so sequence and chain count are unchanged. Folding can change because four covalent constraints disappear, but the data require neither loss of every secondary structure nor a specific final conformation.
:::
