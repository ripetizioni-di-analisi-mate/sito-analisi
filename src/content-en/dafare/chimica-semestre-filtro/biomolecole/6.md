Open-chain D-glucose and D-galactose have identical connectivity and differ only at C4. Classify their stereochemical relationship. Which centres would instead need inversion to obtain the enantiomer of open-chain D-glucose, with centres C2–C5?

:::soluzione
They are C4 epimers and therefore diastereomers. The enantiomer of D-glucose requires inversion of all centres C2, C3, C4 and C5, giving L-glucose. Inverting C4 alone retains C5 and thus the D series. Open-chain C1 is planar and does not enter the stereocentre count.
:::
