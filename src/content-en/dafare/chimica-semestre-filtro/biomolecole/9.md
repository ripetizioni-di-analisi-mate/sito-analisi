In lactose, glucose C1 is free; in sucrose, glucose C1 and fructose C2 are joined. Compare reducing character before hydrolysis and products after complete hydrolysis. Explain the initial difference without using total OH count.

:::soluzione
Lactose is reducing because its terminal glucose retains an opening-capable hemiacetal; it gives glucose and galactose. Sucrose is nonreducing because both anomeric centres are locked; it gives glucose and fructose. Nonanomeric OH groups do not replace the free hemiacetal’s role. Sucrose products are reducing in the common alkaline test.
:::
