parole: ["Two oxides with different geometries"]
---

CO₂ and SO₂ each have two oxygen atoms bonded to their central atom. In VSEPR, CO₂ carbon has no lone pair, whereas SO₂ sulfur has one. Count domains, assign molecular shapes and discuss polarity, given that the bonds to O are polar.

:::soluzione
CO₂ has **two domains, linear shape and zero total dipole**: its equal and opposite polar contributions cancel. SO₂ has three domains, including one lone pair, giving trigonal planar domain arrangement and a bent molecular shape. The S-O contributions are not opposite, so the total dipole is nonzero. Bonded-atom count alone does not determine shape: lone pairs must also be counted. Each multiple bond is one domain.
:::
