A $2.00\,\mathrm L$ vessel contains $2.80\,\mathrm g$ of ideal nitrogen at $300\,\mathrm K$. Use $M=28.0\,\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$ and $R=8.314\,\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}K^{-1}}$. Calculate absolute pressure in kilopascals. Predict the change after adding the same mass again while maintaining volume and temperature.

:::soluzione
$n=\dfrac{2.80}{28.0}=0.100\,\mathrm{mol}$ and $V=2.00\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^3}$. Thus $p=\dfrac{nRT}{V}=\dfrac{0.100\cdot8.314\cdot300}{2.00\cdot10^{-3}}\simeq1.25\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}=125\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Adding the same mass doubles n and therefore p: **approximately $249\,\mathrm{kPa}$**, retaining the unrounded value before doubling. Litres and cubic metres are not interchangeable when R uses SI units.
:::
