parole: ["Heat release and internal-energy increase"]
---

A closed system releases $120\,\mathrm J$ of heat while compression transfers $200\,\mathrm J$ of work to it. Neglect macroscopic kinetic and potential energies. Calculate $\Delta U$ with received work positive. Does heat release imply decreasing U? Can $\Delta H=q$ be assumed from these data alone?

:::soluzione
$q=-120\,\mathrm J$, $w=+200\,\mathrm J$ and **$\Delta U=q+w=+80\,\mathrm J$**. Internal energy rises because received work exceeds released heat: q alone does not close the balance. The data do not imply $\Delta H=q$, since a constant-pressure transformation under the required conditions is not specified. In general, $\Delta H=\Delta U+\Delta(pV)$ and the second term is not given.
:::
