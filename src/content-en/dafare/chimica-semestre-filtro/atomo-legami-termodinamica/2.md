In the isolated-atom model, neutral N has $Z=7$ and configuration $1s^2\,2s^2\,2p^3$. Write the ground-state configuration of $\mathrm{N^+}$ after loss of one 2p electron and compare the species’ unpaired counts. Explain why the two remaining 2p electrons do not immediately pair.

:::soluzione
$\mathrm{N^+}$ has six electrons and configuration **$1s^2\,2s^2\,2p^2$**, with two unpaired electrons compared with neutral N’s three. The two p electrons occupy different degenerate orbitals with parallel spins according to Hund. Pairing them in one orbital with opposite spins would satisfy Pauli but would not give the requested ground-state arrangement. Removal concerns an outer electron and leaves the seven nuclear protons unchanged.
:::
