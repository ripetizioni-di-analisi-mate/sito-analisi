Describe the average orientation of water molecules in the first hydration region around Na⁺ and Cl⁻. Compare the interaction with hydrogen bonding between two water molecules. Does water’s electrical neutrality prevent orientation?

:::soluzione
The partially negative oxygen end points towards **Na⁺**, while partially positive hydrogen ends point towards **Cl⁻**. These are **ion–dipole interactions**. Between two water molecules, an O-bound H can interact with an acceptor site on the other O, forming a hydrogen bond. Neutrality means zero total charge rather than uniform charge distribution: bent geometry and polar O-H bonds leave a dipole. Average orientation does not imply motionless molecules.
:::
