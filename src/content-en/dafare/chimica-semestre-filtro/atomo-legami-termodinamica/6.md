For methanal, $\mathrm{H_2C=O}$, count $\sigma$ and $\pi$ bonds throughout the molecule. Assign carbon hybridisation and local geometry. Given that C, H and O contribute respectively 4, 1 and 6 valence electrons, determine the number of oxygen lone pairs.

:::soluzione
The two C-H bonds contribute two $\sigma$ bonds; C=O contains one $\sigma$ and one $\pi$. The total is **three $\sigma$ and one $\pi$**. Carbon has three domains and trigonal planar geometry, described as $sp^2$. There are $4+2+6=12$ valence electrons: eight in four shared pairs and four in **two oxygen lone pairs**. The double bond contains two shared pairs but only one direction in VSEPR counting.
:::
