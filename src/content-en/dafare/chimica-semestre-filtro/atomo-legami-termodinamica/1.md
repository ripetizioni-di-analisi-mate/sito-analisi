A student assigns 17 protons, 37 neutrons and 16 electrons to ${}^{37}_{17}\mathrm{Cl^-}$. Correct each count and compare the ion with ${}^{35}_{17}\mathrm{Cl^-}$. Which quantities differ between the isotopes?

:::soluzione
${}^{37}_{17}\mathrm{Cl^-}$ contains **17 protons, 20 neutrons and 18 electrons**. Neutrons follow from $A-Z=37-17=20$, while negative charge indicates one more electron than the neutral atom: $N_e=17+1=18$. The value 37 counts all nucleons rather than neutrons alone. ${}^{35}_{17}\mathrm{Cl^-}$ still has 17 protons and 18 electrons but only $35-17=18$ neutrons. Mass number and neutron number change; element and charge do not.
:::
