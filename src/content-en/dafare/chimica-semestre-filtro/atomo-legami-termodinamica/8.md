Build and name the neutral compounds formed by Ca²⁺ with OH⁻ and by Al³⁺ with SO₄²⁻, using the smallest ratios. Assign sulfur’s oxidation state in sulfate with O at $-2$. Explain why external subscripts do not change the ions’ internal formulas.

:::soluzione
The compounds are **$\mathrm{Ca(OH)_2}$, calcium hydroxide**, and **$\mathrm{Al_2(SO_4)_3}$, aluminium sulfate**. Charge balances are $(+2)+2(-1)=0$ and $2(+3)+3(-2)=0$. For S, $x+4(-2)=-2$ gives **$x=+6$**. Each OH retains one oxygen and one hydrogen, and each sulfate retains four oxygens. An external subscript multiplies group count without converting OH to another ion or changing sulfate composition.
:::
