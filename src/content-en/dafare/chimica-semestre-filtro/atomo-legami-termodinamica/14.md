A transformation has $\Delta H=-30.0\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$ and $\Delta S=-100\,\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}K^{-1}}$, assumed constant. Calculate $\Delta G$ at $200\,\mathrm K$ and $400\,\mathrm K$ and the sign-change temperature. Discuss the favoured direction for a closed system at constant T and p with only expansion work. Does a catalyst change the threshold?

:::soluzione
Converting $\Delta S=-0.100\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}K^{-1}}$ gives $\Delta G(200)=-30.0-200(-0.100)=-10.0\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$ and $\Delta G(400)=+10.0\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$. The threshold is **$T_*=\dfrac{-30000}{-100}=300\,\mathrm K$**. The written direction is favoured below the threshold and unfavoured above it under the stated assumptions. A catalyst changes pathway and rate rather than Gibbs difference between identical states, so it does not move the model threshold.
:::
