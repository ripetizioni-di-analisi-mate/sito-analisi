A $\mathrm{Ba(OH)_2}$ solution has concentration $2.00\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$ at $25\,^\circ\mathrm C$. Find hydroxide concentration, pOH and pH in sequence, assuming complete dissociation, ideal dilute behaviour and $pK_w=14.00$.

:::soluzione
**1. Set-up.** First count hydroxides released by each formula unit; apply the logarithm to their concentration rather than to solute concentration.

**2. Working and check.** Each formula unit releases two hydroxides, so $[\mathrm{OH^-}]=4.00\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$. This gives $\mathrm{pOH}=2.39794$ and $\mathrm{pH}=14.00-2.39794=11.60206$. Using solute concentration directly would miss the stoichiometric factor. The pOH must not be confused with the requested pH.
:::
