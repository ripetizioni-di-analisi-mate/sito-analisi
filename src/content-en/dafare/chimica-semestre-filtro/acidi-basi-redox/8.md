A titration has a steep pH rise containing the equivalence point at pH $8.8$. Compare the ranges $1.0$–$2.0$, $3.1$–$4.4$, $6.0$–$7.0$ and $8.2$–$10.0$ to identify which contains equivalence. Check both bounds and explain why this does not presume neutral pH at equivalence.

:::soluzione
**1. Set-up.** Compare the assigned pH with both ends of each interval: it must be above the lower bound and below the upper bound.

**2. Working and check.** The given equivalence pH, $8.8$, lies in $8.2$–$10.0$. The other ranges end at lower pH. Indicator selection concerns its colour-change range relative to the steep titration region. This question explicitly requires inclusion of equivalence; in an actual titration, check the complete curve rather than assuming that equivalence is always neutral.
:::
