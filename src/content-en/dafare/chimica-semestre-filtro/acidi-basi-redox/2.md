In $\mathrm{BF_3+NH_3\rightarrow F_3B\!\leftarrow\!NH_3}$, assign both species their Lewis roles and justify your choice by tracing the electron pair forming the bond.

:::soluzione
**1. Set-up.** Follow the electron pair as the bond forms: the base is its donor, regardless of whether a species contains hydrogen.

**2. Working and check.** Lewis defines the base as the electron-pair donor. The nitrogen lone pair in ammonia forms the new bond to boron, so $\mathrm{NH_3}$ is the base. $\mathrm{BF_3}$ accepts the pair and is the acid. No proton transfer is needed: the criterion here is the source of the pair forming the bond.
:::
