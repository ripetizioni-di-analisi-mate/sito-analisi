An acetate buffer contains $0.0200\,\mathrm{mol}$ acetate and $0.0100\,\mathrm{mol}$ acetic acid in the same solution. Justify using the mole ratio and calculate pH with $pK_a=4.76$, assuming that the approximations of concentration-based Henderson–Hasselbalch apply.

:::soluzione
**1. Set-up.** Identify the conjugate base and acid. Both amounts occupy the same final volume, which cancels in their ratio.

**2. Working and check.** The concentration ratio equals the mole ratio because volume is common. Conjugate base is present in twice the acid amount, so $\mathrm{pH}=pK_a+\log_{10}2=5.06103\simeq5.06$. Reversing the ratio would incorrectly give a lower pH. The excess of conjugate base is consistent with pH above the pair's logarithmic acid constant.
:::
