Calculate the pH of $\mathrm{HNO_3}$ at $2.00\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$ and $25\,^\circ\mathrm C$. Assume complete dissociation, ideal dilute behaviour and negligible water contribution. Show how acid concentration gives hydronium concentration and then the logarithm.

:::soluzione
**1. Set-up.** Write acid dissociation and use the one-to-one ratio between initial acid and hydronium in the stated model.

**2. Working and check.** Each mole of nitric acid forms one mole of hydronium, so $[\mathrm{H_3O^+}]\simeq2.00\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$. Thus pH is $-\log_{10}(2.00\cdot10^{-3})=2.699\simeq2.70$. Ignoring the factor two would alter the logarithm; subtracting this result from fourteen would confuse pH with pOH. The result is appropriately acidic.
:::
