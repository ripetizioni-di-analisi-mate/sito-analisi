Derive the conjugate base and conjugate acid of $\mathrm{H_2PO_4^-}$ by losing and gaining one proton. Explain the charge change in each step.

:::soluzione
**1. Set-up.** Identify the single proton that can be lost or gained. Do not simultaneously change the number of oxygen or phosphorus atoms.

**2. Working and check.** The conjugate base is obtained by removing one proton, lowering the charge by one unit and giving $\mathrm{HPO_4^{2-}}$. The conjugate acid is obtained by adding one proton, giving $\mathrm{H_3PO_4}$. Each conjugation step involves just one proton. Removing two protons would skip a step; adding a proton must also change the charge correctly.
:::
