---

Name $\mathrm{CH_2{=}CHCH(CH_3)CH_3}$, assigning the lower locant to the double bond.

:::soluzione
3-Methylbut-1-ene.

The continuous chain containing the double bond has four carbons. Numbering from the unsaturated end gives the double bond locant 1 and places methyl at C3. Reverse numbering gives the double bond locant 3. Pent-1-ene would require five consecutive carbons, absent from the supplied structure.
:::
