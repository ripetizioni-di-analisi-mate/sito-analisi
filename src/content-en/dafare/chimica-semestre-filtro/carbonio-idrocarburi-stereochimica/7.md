One double-bond carbon bears Br and Cl; the other bears ethyl and methyl. Br and ethyl are on the same side. Which descriptor applies?

:::soluzione
$Z$.

Br outranks Cl by atomic number. The ethyl/methyl tie is resolved by comparing C,H,H with H,H,H, so ethyl wins. The two higher-priority groups are on the same side, giving Z. Hydrogen is not required: each carbon needs two different substituents.
:::
