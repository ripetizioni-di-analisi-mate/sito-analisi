A neutral hydrocarbon with ordinary valences has formula $\mathrm{C_4H_8}$. What conclusion about connectivity follows from the formula alone?

:::soluzione
It may contain a ring or a double bond.

Two hydrogens are missing relative to the four-carbon acyclic alkane, corresponding to one degree of unsaturation. Either a ring or a double bond satisfies that count. The formula does not locate the unsaturation or select one structure; a triple bond would require two degrees of unsaturation.
:::
