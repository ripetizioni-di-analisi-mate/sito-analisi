Which constitutional product predominates in ionic HBr addition to propene without peroxides?

:::soluzione
2-Bromopropane.

Protonation at the terminal carbon produces the secondary carbocation, more stable than the alternative primary one in this model. Bromide attacks that centre to form 2-bromopropane. HBr adds one H and one Br; a dibromide requires a different reagent. Allylic bromide would result from substitution rather than the specified addition.
:::
