Write the condensed formula of 2-methylbutane-1,3-diol. Classify its two alcohol groups separately and explain why neither the total carbon count nor the OH count determines the degree of each alcohol.

:::soluzione
The structure is $\mathrm{HOCH_2CH(CH_3)CH(OH)CH_3}$. The OH at position 1 is primary: its CH2 carbon is bonded to one carbon. The OH at position 3 is secondary: its CH carbon is bonded to two carbons and one H.

The molecule contains five carbons overall and two OH groups. The suffix “diol” counts hydroxyl groups, whereas “primary” and “secondary” describe the neighbors of each hydroxyl-bearing carbon. The branched carbon at position 2 is bonded to three carbons but bears no OH: it does not make either alcohol group tertiary.
:::
