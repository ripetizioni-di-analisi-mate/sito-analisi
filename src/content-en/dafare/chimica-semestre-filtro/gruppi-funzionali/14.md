Heat $1.16\,\mathrm g$ of 3-oxopentanoic acid, $\mathrm{CH_3CH_2COCH_2COOH}$, assuming quantitative decarboxylation followed by tautomerization. Use teaching molar masses of 116, 72 and 44 $\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$ for the acid, final ketone and CO2. Identify the ketone, calculate product masses and locate a possible label initially placed on carboxyl carbon.

:::soluzione
The organic product is butan-2-one, $\mathrm{CH_3CH_2COCH_3}$, accompanied by CO2. Initial amount is $\dfrac{1.16}{116}=0.0100\,\mathrm{mol}$, and each product forms in the same amount. Masses are therefore $0.720\,\mathrm g$ of ketone and $0.440\,\mathrm g$ of CO2, totaling 1.160 g.

Carboxyl carbon leaves entirely in CO2: a label at that site would follow the gas, not the ketone. The remaining chain retains four carbons. An enol precedes the ketone; tautomerization redistributes H and bonds without losing further carbons. The rule depends on the carbonyl's beta position relative to the carboxyl group.
:::
