Completely hydrolyze $0.030\,\mathrm{mol}$ of $\mathrm{CH_3CH(OC_2H_5)_2}$ in excess acidic water, with no other reactions. Identify the original carbonyl compound, write the balance and calculate the moles of alcohol released. Explain why no ketone is obtained.

:::soluzione
The acetal center bears H and CH3, so the regenerated carbonyl compound is ethanal:

$$
\mathrm{CH_3CH(OC_2H_5)_2}+\mathrm{H_2O}\rightleftharpoons\mathrm{CH_3CHO}+2\,\mathrm{C_2H_5OH}.
$$

The products are $0.030\,\mathrm{mol}$ of ethanal and $0.060\,\mathrm{mol}$ of ethanol. The two ethoxy groups explain the factor of two; they do not give two carbonyl molecules. The acetal-center H stays on the aldehyde carbon, whereas a ketone would require two carbon substituents. Complete conversion is an exercise assumption: actual hydrolysis is an equilibrium favored by abundant water, with acid catalysis.
:::
