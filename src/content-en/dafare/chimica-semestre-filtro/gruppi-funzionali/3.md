Separately oxidize $\mathrm{C_6H_5CH_2OH}$ and $\mathrm{C_6H_5CH(OH)CH_3}$ with anhydrous PCC under customary conditions while preserving the skeleton. Write the carbonyl products, distinguish aldehyde from ketone, and identify which product could undergo further oxidation to an acid with a suitable aqueous oxidant.

:::soluzione
Benzyl alcohol gives $\mathrm{C_6H_5CHO}$, benzaldehyde. 1-Phenylethanol gives $\mathrm{C_6H_5COCH_3}$, acetophenone. The OH-bearing carbon becomes carbonyl carbon in both cases: the first retains an H, while the second is bonded to phenyl and methyl.

Proximity to the ring therefore does not determine product class by itself. Benzaldehyde can be oxidized to benzoic acid under suitable aqueous conditions. Acetophenone does not simply give that same acid while retaining every C–C bond: further skeletal transformation would be required. The stated anhydrous PCC conditions stop at the two indicated carbonyl compounds.
:::
