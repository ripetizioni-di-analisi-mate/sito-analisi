At 25 °C a buffer maintains $pH=8.50$. For a single B/BH+ pair, measurements give $[\mathrm B]=0.0020\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$ and $[\mathrm{BH^+}]=0.0180\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$. Approximate activities by concentrations. Infer the pKa of BH+ and the pH at which the two forms would have equal concentrations, with no other reactions.

:::soluzione
The ratio is $r=\dfrac{0.0020}{0.0180}=\dfrac19$. From $pH=pK_a+\log r$, $pK_a=8.50-\log\!\left(\dfrac19\right)\approx9.45$.

Equal concentrations require $r=1$, hence $pH=pK_a\approx9.45$. Initially, BH+ accounts for $\dfrac{0.0180}{0.0200}=0.900$ of the total; in the equimolar state it would account for half. The pKa is a property of the equilibrium under the stated conditions: it does not equal the initial pH simply because both species are present.
:::
