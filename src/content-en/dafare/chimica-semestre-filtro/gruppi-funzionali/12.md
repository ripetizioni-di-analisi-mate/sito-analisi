Treat $0.030\,\mathrm{mol}$ of dimethyl oxalate, $\mathrm{CH_3OOC{-}COOCH_3}$, with $0.080\,\mathrm{mol}$ of OH− in water. Assume complete hydrolysis of both ester groups, no side reactions and no final acidification. Calculate moles of oxalate, methanol and remaining hydroxide.

:::soluzione
Each molecule contains two esters and consumes two OH−:

$$
\mathrm{CH_3OOC{-}COOCH_3}+2\,\mathrm{OH^-}\longrightarrow\mathrm{{^-}OOC{-}COO^-}+2\,\mathrm{CH_3OH}.
$$

Hydroxide consumption is $0.060\,\mathrm{mol}$. Products are $0.030\,\mathrm{mol}$ of oxalate dianion and $0.060\,\mathrm{mol}$ of methanol; $0.020\,\mathrm{mol}$ of OH− remains. Two carboxylate groups belong to one oxalate molecule: they must not be counted as two moles of that dianion. Total negative charge is conserved, and without acidification neutral oxalic acid is not written as the final form.
:::
