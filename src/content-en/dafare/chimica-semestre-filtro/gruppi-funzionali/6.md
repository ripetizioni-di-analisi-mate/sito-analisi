titolo: "When reduction creates chirality"

Propanal, pentan-3-one and pentan-2-one are reduced separately with NaBH4 and then protonated. Assume achiral substrates, reagents and environment, with no product selection. Write the three alcohols and determine when a stereogenic center is created, explaining the expected stereoisomer composition.

:::soluzione
The products are propan-1-ol, pentan-3-ol and pentan-2-ol. In the first, the reduced carbon becomes CH2OH and bears two identical H atoms. In the second, the OH-bearing carbon has two identical ethyl groups. Neither is stereogenic.

In pentan-2-ol that carbon bears OH, H, methyl and propyl: four different groups. The initial carbonyl faces are enantiotopic and, under the achiral assumptions, produce equal amounts of both enantiomers. A racemate is therefore expected only in this third case. Converting a planar center into a tetrahedral one is insufficient by itself to create chirality: all four substituents must be checked.
:::
