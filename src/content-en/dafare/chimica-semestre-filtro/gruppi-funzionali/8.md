For pentan-2-one, $\mathrm{CH_3COCH_2CH_2CH_3}$, identify both alpha carbons. Write the two enol connectivities obtainable by transferring an H from either one to oxygen, without assigning percentages or E/Z configurations. Explain why these are not two resonance contributors of the ketone.

:::soluzione
The alpha carbons are C1 and C3, adjacent to carbonyl C2. The structures are $\mathrm{CH_2{=}C(OH)CH_2CH_2CH_3}$ and $\mathrm{CH_3C(OH){=}CHCH_2CH_3}$. Both retain formula $\mathrm{C_5H_{10}O}$ and a neutral charge.

The new double bond joins the alpha carbon that lost H to the former carbonyl carbon; O gains H. Nuclear positions and connectivity therefore change, unlike resonance. Being able to draw two enols does not imply equal amounts: stability and conditions determine equilibrium. No data are supplied for assigning quantitative ratios.
:::
