Helium is added to an equilibrium ideal-gas mixture at constant volume and temperature, with no helium reactions. Predict the immediate changes in reacting partial pressures and Q; justify them using the ideal-gas equation.

:::soluzione
Reacting partial pressures and Q remain unchanged.

Each reacting gas obeys $p_i=\dfrac{n_iRT}{V}$. Addition changes neither its mole number nor T or V, so $p_i$ stays unchanged. Mole fraction decreases, but total pressure increases so their product is preserved. At constant temperature, K is unchanged in the specified ideal model.
