At constant volume, over $30\,\mathrm{s}$, $0.060\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$ of peroxide disappears according to $2\mathrm{H_2O_2}\longrightarrow2\mathrm{H_2O}+\mathrm{O_2}$. Calculate average oxygen formation rate and explain its relationship to peroxide disappearance.

:::soluzione
$1.0\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}\,s^{-1}}$.

Peroxide disappearance is $\dfrac{0.060}{30}=0.0020\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}\,s^{-1}}$. One oxygen mole forms for every two consumed moles, so the requested value is half. Its sign is positive because product formation is measured. This is an average and does not establish a constant rate throughout the interval.

:::
