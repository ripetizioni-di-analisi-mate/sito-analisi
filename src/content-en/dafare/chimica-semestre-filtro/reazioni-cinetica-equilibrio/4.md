At constant temperature and $[B]$, doubling $[A]$ changes initial rate from $v_0$ to $4v_0$. For $v=k[A]^\alpha[B]^\beta$, derive the order in A from the rate ratio and explain what this observation does not establish about molecularity.

:::soluzione
$\alpha=2$.

Taking the rate ratio cancels $k$ and the B factor, leaving $4=2^\alpha$. The exponent is therefore two. Four is the observed increase factor, not the order; first order would double rate, while half order would give a square-root-of-two factor. The result does not assign a molecularity to the overall reaction: molecularity is defined only for an individual elementary step.

:::
