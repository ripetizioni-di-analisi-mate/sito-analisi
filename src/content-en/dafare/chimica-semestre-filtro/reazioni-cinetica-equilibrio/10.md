For a reaction at fixed temperature and pressure, $Q=0.50$ and $K=0.10$. Determine the sign of $\Delta_rG$ and the favoured direction, justifying them by comparing quotient and constant.

:::soluzione
$\Delta_rG>0$; reverse direction.

The ratio $\dfrac{Q}{K}=5$ exceeds one, so its logarithm is positive. From $\Delta_rG=RT\ln\dfrac{Q}{K}$, forward progress increases Gibbs energy while reverse progress lowers it. Comparing Q only with one is insufficient; K is the compositional reference. Zero would require Q to equal K.

:::
