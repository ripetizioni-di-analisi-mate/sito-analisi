For MX at fixed temperature, $K_{\mathrm{ps}}=1.0\cdot10^{-10}$. In an ideal solution already containing X at $0.010\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$, estimate solubility with excess solid present and no other reactions, then check the approximation quantitatively.

:::soluzione
$1.0\cdot10^{-8}\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$.

The ideal balance gives $K_{\mathrm{ps}}=s(c_0+s)$ using normalised concentrations. Neglecting s relative to $c_0$ gives $s\simeq\dfrac{10^{-10}}{10^{-2}}=10^{-8}\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$. The ratio $\dfrac{s}{c_0}=10^{-6}$ confirms the approximation. The square root of the constant applies in pure water; multiplying by common-ion concentration would reverse its effect on the requested value.

:::
