A closed mixture is at equilibrium at fixed temperature. Adding a catalyst increases isotope exchange between reactants and products without changing final composition. Explain how isotope exchange can increase at constant composition and describe the effect on both reaction directions.

:::soluzione
Processes in both directions increase while preserving the equilibrium condition.

Isotope exchange detects microscopic transformations even when macroscopic amounts do not change. A catalyst speeds approach to and dynamic maintenance of the same equilibrium. Accelerating only the forward direction would break equality of rates. At constant temperature, unchanged K also preserves the associated standard Gibbs-energy change.
