A plot of $\ln k$ against $\dfrac1T$ has slope $-6000\,\mathrm{K}$ over an Arrhenius range. With $R=8.314\,\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}\,K^{-1}}$, calculate activation energy in kilojoules per mole, showing its relationship to the slope.

:::soluzione
Approximately $49.9\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$.

The linear form is $\ln k=\ln A-\dfrac{E_a}{R}\dfrac1T$. Hence $E_a=-R\cdot\text{slope}=49884\,\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}}$, approximately 49.9 kilojoules per mole. The negative slope does not make the energy negative; dividing by R would invert the relation. The intercept instead determines the pre-exponential factor.

:::
