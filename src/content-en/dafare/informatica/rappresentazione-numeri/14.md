Round $(101.11)_2$ to the nearest value representable in normalized binary floating point with four significant digits, including the leading $1$ bit, and unrestricted integer exponents. If two values are equally close, choose the one whose significand ends in $0$. Give the result in normalized form.

:::soluzione
$$
(1.100)_2\cdot 2^2 .
$$
:::
