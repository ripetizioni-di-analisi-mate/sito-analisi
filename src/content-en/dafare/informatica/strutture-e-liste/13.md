---
titolo: "Value of the second FIFO removal"
teoria: ["coda-collegata"]
parole: ["FIFO", "insertion order"]
---

Insert $4$, $8$ and $12$ in that order into an empty queue. Remove an element, insert $9$, and then remove again. Each operation succeeds. Which value is returned by the second removal?

:::soluzione
$8$.
:::
