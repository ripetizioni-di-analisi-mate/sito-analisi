titolo: "Lines within the allowed range"
teoria: [validazione-righe]
parole: ["validating remaining characters"]
---

A procedure accepts a line if it contains exactly one decimal integer between $-12$ and $12$, allowing leading and trailing spaces. Consider, in order, `-12`, `12x`, `10` and `  +2`. How many lines are accepted?

:::soluzione
$3$
:::
