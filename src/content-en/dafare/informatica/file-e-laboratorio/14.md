The `(time, level)` records are `(3, 4)`, `(7, 4)`, `(9, 8)`, `(15, 8)`, `(17, 4)`. Each level holds from the time of its record until the time of the next record. The final record ends the observation. Find the total time at level $8$.

:::soluzione
$8$
:::
