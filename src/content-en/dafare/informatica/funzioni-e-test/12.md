teoria: [partizioni-di-test]

The domain of `band_fee(x)` consists of the integers from $0$ to $20$. All three bands of the specification, $0\leq x<5$, $5\leq x\leq 12$ and $12<x\leq 20$, must be exercised. Each test calls the function with one input. What is the minimum number of tests?

:::soluzione
$3$.
:::
