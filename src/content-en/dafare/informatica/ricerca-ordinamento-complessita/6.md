On $32$ sorted elements, `lower_bound` starts from $[0,32)$ and uses `mid = lo + (hi - lo) / 2`. If `a[mid] < key` it sets `lo = mid + 1`, otherwise it sets `hi = mid`. Find the maximum number of `a[mid] < key` comparisons before `lo == hi`.

:::soluzione
$$
6 .
$$
:::
