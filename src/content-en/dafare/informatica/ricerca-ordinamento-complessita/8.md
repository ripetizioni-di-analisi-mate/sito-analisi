Insertion sort on $8$ already sorted elements processes each position `i` from `1` to `7`. Its guard is `j > 0 && a[j - 1] > key` with `j = i` at the start of the pass. How many `a[j - 1] > key` comparisons are evaluated in total?

:::soluzione
$$
7 .
$$
:::
