---
titolo: "Maximum representable element count"
teoria: ["dimensione-allocazione"]
parole: ["SIZE_MAX", "allocation size"]
---

On an implementation where `SIZE_MAX` is $65535$ and `sizeof(int)` is $4$, find the greatest $n$ for which the mathematical product $n\cdot 4$ is representable as `size_t`. Consider the limit on the byte count alone.

:::soluzione
$$
16383 .
$$
:::
