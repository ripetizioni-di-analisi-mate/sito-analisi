---
titolo: "Sum after the first writes"
teoria: ["inizializzazione-allocata"]
parole: ["calloc", "unsigned char"]
---

With `<stdlib.h>` included, the fragment is executed inside a function. Assume `calloc` returns a non-null pointer. Find the sum of all six elements before the block is released.

```c
unsigned char *p = calloc(6, sizeof *p);
p[3] = 9;
p[5] = 4;
```

:::soluzione
$$
13 .
$$
:::
