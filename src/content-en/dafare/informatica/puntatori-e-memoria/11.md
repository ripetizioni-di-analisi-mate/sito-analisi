With `<stdlib.h>` included, the fragment is executed inside a function. Assume the byte count is representable and `malloc` succeeds. Give the smallest index of an element that has yet to be initialized by a write.

```c
int *p = malloc(5 * sizeof *p);
p[2] = 23;
p[0] = 7;
p[1] = p[2] - p[0];
```

:::soluzione
$$
3 .
$$
:::
