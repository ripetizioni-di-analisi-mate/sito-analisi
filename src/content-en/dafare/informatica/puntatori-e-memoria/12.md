The pointer `p` owns a live block obtained with `malloc`. The block contains $10$, $20$ and $30$ as three integers in that order. The fragment is executed with `<stdlib.h>` included. Assume the new byte count is representable and `realloc` succeeds. Give the value of `next[2]`.

```c
int *next = realloc(p, 5 * sizeof *p);
```

:::soluzione
$$
30 .
$$
:::
