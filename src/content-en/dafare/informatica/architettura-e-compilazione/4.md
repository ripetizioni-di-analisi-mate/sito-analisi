---
titolo: "Memory after a write"
teoria: ["stato-macchina", "istruzioni-modello"]
parole: ["accumulator", "write"]
---

In an instructional machine, the accumulator holds an integer. `LOAD a` sets it to the contents of cell `a`, `ADD a` adds the contents of `a` to the accumulator, and `STORE a` writes the accumulator to `a`. `HALT` stops execution. Initially, cell $7$ contains $4$ and cell $8$ contains $-3$. Compute the final contents of cell $9$ after this sequence.

```text
LOAD 7
ADD 8
STORE 9
HALT
```

:::soluzione
$1$.
:::
