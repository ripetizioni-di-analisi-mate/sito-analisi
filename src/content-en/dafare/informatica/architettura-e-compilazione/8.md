---
titolo: "Mean access time with a miss penalty"
teoria: ["costo-medio"]
parole: ["mean access time", "miss penalty"]
---

Each access checks the cache for $2\,\mathrm{ns}$. The hit fraction is $\dfrac{3}{4}$. Only a miss takes an extra $40\,\mathrm{ns}$ to complete. Compute the mean access time in nanoseconds.

:::soluzione
$12\,\mathrm{ns}$.
:::
