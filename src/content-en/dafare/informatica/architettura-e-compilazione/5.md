---
titolo: "Next address after a branch"
teoria: ["istruzioni-modello"]
parole: ["conditional branch", "program counter"]
---

The program counter is $1$ and the accumulator contains $0$. Address $1$ holds `JZ 4`. By definition, `JZ a` sets the counter to `a` when the accumulator is zero. With a nonzero accumulator, it advances the counter by one cell. Which address does the counter point to immediately after `JZ 4`?

:::soluzione
$4$.
:::
