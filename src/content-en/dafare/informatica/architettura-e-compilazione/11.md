---
titolo: "Filename produced by cc -c"
teoria: ["file-oggetto"]
parole: ["separate translation", "object file"]
---

Assume a GCC-compatible `cc` driver. `scale.c` is a valid C17 source file, and the following command succeeds. What output filename does the command specify?

```shell
cc -std=c17 -c scale.c -o scale.o
```

:::soluzione
`scale.o`.
:::
