---
titolo: "Hits with two cache lines"
teoria: ["cache-diretta"]
parole: ["direct-mapped cache", "hit"]
---

A direct-mapped cache has two initially empty lines. A memory block contains $4$ bytes. Block $b$ uses the line given by the remainder of $b$ divided by $2$. Every read miss loads the requested block and replaces the block already in that line if the line is occupied. With no other accesses, how many hits does the address sequence $0$, $1$, $8$, $0$, $1$ produce?

:::soluzione
$2$.
:::
