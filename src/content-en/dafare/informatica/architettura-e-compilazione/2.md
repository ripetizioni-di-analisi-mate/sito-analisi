---
titolo: "Registers in a byte range"
teoria: ["indirizzi-e-capacita"]
parole: ["half-open range", "registers"]
---

A device occupies every byte address from `0x340` inclusive to `0x37C` exclusive. Each register occupies $4$ consecutive bytes, and each byte belongs to exactly one register. How many registers does the range contain?

:::soluzione
$15$.
:::
