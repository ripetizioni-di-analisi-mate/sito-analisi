---
titolo: "Stage of an unresolved symbol"
teoria: ["risoluzione-simboli", "diagnosi-fasi"]
parole: ["linking", "external symbol"]
---

`main.c` declares `int scale(int);`, and its `main` returns the result of calling `scale`. `cc -c main.c -o main.o` succeeds and retains the external reference to `scale`. The attempt to create an executable passes `main.o` as the only project object, and no library supplies `scale`. At which stage is the unresolved reference detected?

:::soluzione
Linking.
:::
