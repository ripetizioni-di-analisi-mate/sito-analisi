titolo: "Temperature change from a sensor scale"
teoria: [taratura-termometro, intervalli]
parole: ["Temperature change from a sensor scale"]
---

On the linear part of a sensor calibration, a rise of $15\,\mathrm K$ increases the reading by $6$ divisions. By how much does the temperature rise when the reading goes from $18$ to $36$ divisions?

:::soluzione
$\Delta T=45\,\mathrm K$.
:::
