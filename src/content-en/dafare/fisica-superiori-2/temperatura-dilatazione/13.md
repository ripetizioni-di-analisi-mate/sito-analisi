A rod of length $7\,\mathrm m$ expands by $0.005775\,\mathrm m$ when warmed by $55\,\mathrm K$. What is its linear expansion coefficient?

:::soluzione
$\alpha\simeq 1.5\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$.
:::
