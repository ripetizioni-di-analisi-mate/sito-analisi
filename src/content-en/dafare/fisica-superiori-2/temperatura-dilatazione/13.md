Two rods joined end to end are free to expand and both warm by $50\,\mathrm K$. The first is $2\,\mathrm m$ long with $\alpha_1=10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$. The second is $4\,\mathrm m$ long. The total extension is $4.0\,\mathrm{mm}$. What is the linear expansion coefficient of the second rod?

:::soluzione
$\alpha\simeq1.5\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$.
:::
