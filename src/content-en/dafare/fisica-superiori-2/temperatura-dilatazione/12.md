A rod of length $6\,\mathrm m$ has linear expansion coefficient $\alpha=1.2\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$ and is warmed by $70\,\mathrm K$. Find the increase in length in millimetres.

:::soluzione
$\Delta L\simeq 5.04\,\mathrm{mm}$.
:::
