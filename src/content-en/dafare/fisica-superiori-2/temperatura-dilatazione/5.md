A free rod $6\,\mathrm m$ long extends by $0.0042\,\mathrm m$ when its temperature rises uniformly by $50\,\mathrm K$. Estimate its linear expansion coefficient.

:::soluzione
$\alpha\simeq1.4\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$.
:::
