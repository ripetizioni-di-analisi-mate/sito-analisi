A rod of length $6\,\mathrm m$ expands by $0.0042\,\mathrm m$ when warmed by $50\,\mathrm K$. What is its linear expansion coefficient?

:::soluzione
$\alpha\simeq 1.4\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$.
:::
