An isotropic solid has volume $180\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ and linear expansion coefficient $1.5\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$. It is warmed by $60\,\mathrm K$. Find the increase in volume in the small-deformation approximation.

:::soluzione
$\Delta V\simeq 0.486\,\mathrm{cm^3}$.
:::
