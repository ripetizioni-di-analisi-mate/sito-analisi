A rod of length $5\,\mathrm m$ has linear expansion coefficient $\alpha=1.2\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$ and is warmed by $60\,\mathrm K$. Find the increase in length in millimetres.

:::soluzione
$\Delta L\simeq 3.6\,\mathrm{mm}$.
:::
