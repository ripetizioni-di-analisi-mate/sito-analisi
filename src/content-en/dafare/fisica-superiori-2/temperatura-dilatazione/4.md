A free rod $5\,\mathrm m$ long has a linear expansion coefficient of $\alpha=1.2\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$ and warms uniformly by $60\,\mathrm K$. Estimate its extension in millimetres.

:::soluzione
$\Delta L\simeq3.6\,\mathrm{mm}$.
:::
