A liquid with a volume expansion coefficient of $7.9\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$ fills a freely expanding container to its $180\,\mathrm{mL}$ mark. The container has a volume expansion coefficient of $1.2\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$. Both warm by $30\,\mathrm K$ at constant pressure. Estimate the apparent expansion.

:::soluzione
$\Delta V_{\mathrm{app}}\simeq3.618\,\mathrm{mL}$.
:::
