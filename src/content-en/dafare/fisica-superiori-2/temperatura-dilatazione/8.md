A liquid of volume $180\,\mathrm{mL}$ has volume expansion coefficient $0.0007\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$. The container has volume expansion coefficient $3\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$. What is the apparent expansion for an increase of $30\,\mathrm K$?

:::soluzione
$\Delta V_{\mathrm{app}}\simeq 3.618\,\mathrm{mL}$.
:::
