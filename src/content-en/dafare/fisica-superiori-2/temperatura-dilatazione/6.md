An isotropic plate has area $5\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and linear expansion coefficient $2\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$. Find the increase in area when heated by $50\,\mathrm K$, using the small-deformation approximation.

:::soluzione
$\Delta A\simeq 0.01\,\mathrm{m^2}$.
:::
