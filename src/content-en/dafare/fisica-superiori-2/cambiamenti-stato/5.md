parole: ["Heating ice into liquid water"]
---

Take $0.5\,\mathrm{kg}$ of ice from $-10\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}$ to liquid water at $18\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}$. Use $c_g=2100$, $c_a=4200\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$ and $L_f=334000\,\mathrm{J/kg}$ at normal atmospheric pressure. How much heat is needed?

:::soluzione
$Q\simeq 215300\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
