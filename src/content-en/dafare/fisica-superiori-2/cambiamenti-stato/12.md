A mass of water of $0.6\,\mathrm{kg}$ freezes completely at $0\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}$ at normal atmospheric pressure. With $L_f=334000\,\mathrm{J/kg}$, what is the heat for the sample, taken as positive if absorbed?

:::soluzione
$Q\simeq -200400\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
