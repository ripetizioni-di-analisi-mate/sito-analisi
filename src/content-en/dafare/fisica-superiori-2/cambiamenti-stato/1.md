A mass of ice of $0.5\,\mathrm{kg}$, already at $0\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}$, melts completely at normal atmospheric pressure. Use $L_f=334000\,\mathrm{J/kg}$. How much heat does it absorb?

:::soluzione
$Q\simeq 167000\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
