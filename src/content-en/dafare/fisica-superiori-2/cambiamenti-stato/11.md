A $0.030\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass of water already at $100\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ must vaporise at normal atmospheric pressure. Only $50\%$ of the energy supplied by the source reaches the sample. With $L_v=2260000\,\mathrm{J/kg}$, what energy $Q$ must the source supply?

:::soluzione
$Q\simeq 135600\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
