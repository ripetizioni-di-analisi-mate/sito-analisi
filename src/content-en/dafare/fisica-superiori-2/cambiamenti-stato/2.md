Ice at $0\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}$ receives $16700\,\mathrm J$ as it melts. Use $L_f=334000\,\mathrm{J/kg}$ and assume enough ice is available. What mass melts?

:::soluzione
$m\simeq 0.05\,\mathrm{kg}$.
:::
