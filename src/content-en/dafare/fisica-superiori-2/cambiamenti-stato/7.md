parole: ["Partial melting of ice in water"]
---

Mix $0.5\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water at $10\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}$ with $0.5\,\mathrm{kg}$ of ice at zero degrees Celsius in an insulated vessel of negligible heat capacity. Use $c=4200\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$ and $L_f=334000\,\mathrm{J/kg}$. How much ice melts?

:::soluzione
$m_{\mathrm{fusa}}\simeq 0.0629\,\mathrm{kg}$.
:::
