A condenser receives $5\,\mathrm{g/s}$ of saturated water vapour for $10\,\mathrm s$. At normal atmospheric pressure all the vapour condenses at $100\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. The water leaves at that temperature. With $L_v=2260000\,\mathrm{J/kg}$, what is the magnitude $|Q|$ of the energy released?

:::soluzione
$|Q|\simeq 113000\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
