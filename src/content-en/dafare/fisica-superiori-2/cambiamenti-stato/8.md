A mass of steam of $0.05\,\mathrm{kg}$ condenses at $100\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}$ at normal atmospheric pressure. Use $L_v=2.26\cdot10^{6}\,\mathrm{J/kg}$. What is the magnitude of the energy released?

:::soluzione
$|Q|\simeq 113000\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
