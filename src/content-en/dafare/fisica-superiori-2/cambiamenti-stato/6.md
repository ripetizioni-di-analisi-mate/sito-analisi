Ice of mass $0.5\,\mathrm{kg}$, already at $0\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}$, absorbs constant power $400\,\mathrm W$ without heat loss. Use $L_f=334000\,\mathrm{J/kg}$. How long does complete melting take?

:::soluzione
$t\simeq 417.5\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
