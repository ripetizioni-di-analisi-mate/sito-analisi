---

A closed vessel with a movable piston keeps the sample at normal atmospheric pressure. It contains water and vapour in equilibrium at $100\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. Their total mass is $0.08\,\mathrm{kg}$, and $0.03\,\mathrm{kg}$ is already vapour. With $L_v=2260000\,\mathrm{J/kg}$, how much heat $Q$ is needed for all the water to become vapour?

:::soluzione
$Q\simeq 113000\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
