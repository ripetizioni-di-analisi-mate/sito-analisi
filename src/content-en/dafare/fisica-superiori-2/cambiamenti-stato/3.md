A mass of water of $0.05\,\mathrm{kg}$, already at $100\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}$, vaporises completely at normal atmospheric pressure. Use $L_v=2.26\cdot10^{6}\,\mathrm{J/kg}$. How much heat is needed?

:::soluzione
$Q\simeq 113000\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
