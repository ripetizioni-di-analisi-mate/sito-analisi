A $0.100\,\mathrm{kg}$ sample of ice initially at $-10\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ receives $22140\,\mathrm J$. At normal atmospheric pressure, use $c_g=2100\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$ and $L_f=334000\,\mathrm{J/kg}$. What mass $m$ of ice melts?

::grafico{linee="0,-10 2.1,0 22.14,0" punti="2.1,0,pieno,A;22.14,0,pieno" segmenti="2.1,-4,2.1,0;22.14,0,22.14,2" etichette="2.6,-4,2.10 kJ;16,2,22.14 kJ" xmin=0 xmax=24.5 ymin=-12 ymax=6 nomi="Q (kJ),T (°C)" titolo="From point A onward, the incoming energy begins to turn ice into water."}

:::soluzione
$m\simeq 0.06\,\mathrm{kg}$.
:::
