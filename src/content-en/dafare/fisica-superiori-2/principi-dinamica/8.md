A block initially at rest is pushed with $6\,\mathrm{N}$ to the right. Maximum static friction is $20\,\mathrm{N}$ and the block stays at rest. What is the actual friction magnitude?

:::soluzione
$F_s\simeq 6\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
