An object of mass $5\,\mathrm{kg}$ follows a circle of radius $6\,\mathrm{m}$ at constant speed $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the magnitude of the radial net force?

:::soluzione
$F_c\simeq 40.8\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
