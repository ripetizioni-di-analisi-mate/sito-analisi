parole: ["Acceleration from a scale reading"]
---

A $5\,\mathrm{kg}$ sample rests on a scale in a lift. The scale exerts an upward normal force of $65\,\mathrm N$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the acceleration component when upwards is positive?

:::soluzione
$3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
:::
