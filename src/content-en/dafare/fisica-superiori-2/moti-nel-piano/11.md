A ball is launched horizontally at $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$ from $180\,\mathrm{m}$ above the ground. Use $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and neglect air resistance. What is the horizontal range?

:::soluzione
$R\simeq 42\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
