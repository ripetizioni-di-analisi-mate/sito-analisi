An object moves around a circle of radius $6\,\mathrm{m}$ at constant speed $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the magnitude of the centripetal acceleration?

:::soluzione
$a_c=\dfrac{49}{6}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}\simeq 8.17\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
:::
