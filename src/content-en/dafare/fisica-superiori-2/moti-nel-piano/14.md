parole: ["Period from half a turn"]
---

A point on the rim of a wheel in uniform circular motion starts at a mark. It first reaches the diametrically opposite point after $\dfrac{1}{12}\,\mathrm s$. How long does one complete turn take?

:::soluzione
$T=\dfrac{1}{6}\,\mathrm s$.
:::
