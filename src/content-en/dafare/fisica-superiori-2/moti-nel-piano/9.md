A probe moves at constant velocity. Over $2\,\mathrm s$ it travels from $(29;-14)$ to $(-7;34)$, with coordinates in metres. What is the magnitude of its velocity?

:::soluzione
$v=30\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
