---
titolo: "Average speed in two media"
teoria: [tempo-in-piu-mezzi]
parole: ["Average speed in two media"]
---

A pulse travels at normal incidence through $0.60\,\mathrm m$ of index $1.40$ and then $0.40\,\mathrm m$ of index $2.15$. Neglect dispersion and use $c=3\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is its average speed over the full metre?

:::soluzione
$v_{\mathrm{m}}=\dfrac{3000000000}{17}\,\mathrm{m/s}\simeq1.76471\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
