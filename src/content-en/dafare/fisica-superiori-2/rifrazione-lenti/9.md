A transparent material has refractive index $1.7$. Use $c=3\cdot10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the speed of light in the material?

:::soluzione
$v\simeq 1.76471\cdot10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
