Monochromatic light has wavelength $440\,\mathrm{nm}$ in a medium where its speed is $2.4\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. It crosses a stationary boundary into glass where its speed is $2.0\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is its new wavelength?

:::soluzione
$\lambda_2=\dfrac{1100}{3}\,\mathrm{nm}\simeq366.667\,\mathrm{nm}$.
:::
