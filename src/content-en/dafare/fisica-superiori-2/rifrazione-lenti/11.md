A protractor laid along a glass surface reads $50^\circ$ between the surface and an incident ray in air. The indices of air and glass are $1.00$ and $1.50$. What is the angle of refraction from the normal?

:::soluzione
$\theta_2\simeq25.374^\circ$.
:::
