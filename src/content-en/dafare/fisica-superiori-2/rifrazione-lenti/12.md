For the same monochromatic light the speed is $1.50\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in medium A and $2.85\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in medium B. What is the critical angle for light travelling from A to B, measured from the normal?

:::soluzione
$\theta_c\simeq31.8^\circ$.
:::
