A real point source is $27\,\mathrm{cm}$ from a diverging lens in air. The focal length has magnitude $9\,\mathrm{cm}$. Set the lens centre at coordinate zero and orient the axis in the direction of light. What is the image coordinate?

:::soluzione
$q\simeq -6.75\,\mathrm{cm}$.
:::
