A thin converging lens has focal length $f=9\,\mathrm{cm}$. A real object is at $p=25.2\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find the signed image distance in the paraxial approximation.

:::soluzione
$q\simeq 14\,\mathrm{cm}$.
:::
