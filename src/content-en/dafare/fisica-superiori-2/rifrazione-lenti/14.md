---
titolo: "Reduced image from a diverging lens"
teoria: [diottrie]
parole: ["Reduced image from a diverging lens"]
---

A thin lens has power $-10\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$. Its image of a real object is upright and one quarter as tall as the object. What is the signed image distance in centimetres?

:::soluzione
$-7.5\,\mathrm{cm}$.
:::
