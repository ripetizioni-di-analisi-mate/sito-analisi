parole: ["Heaters with a stated efficiency"]
---

A $500\,\mathrm W$ heater transfers $80\%$ of its input energy to the water. How long will it take to warm $1\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water by $30\,\mathrm K$? Use $c=4200\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$ and assume that the water stays in the same phase.

:::soluzione
$t\simeq 315\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
