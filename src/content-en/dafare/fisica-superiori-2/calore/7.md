A plane wall has area $6\,\mathrm{m^2}$, thickness $0.05\,\mathrm m$ and thermal conductivity $0.04\,\mathrm{W/(m\,K)}$. Its faces differ in temperature by $18\,\mathrm K$. What is the power transferred by conduction in steady state?

:::soluzione
$P\simeq 86.4\,\mathrm{W}$.
:::
