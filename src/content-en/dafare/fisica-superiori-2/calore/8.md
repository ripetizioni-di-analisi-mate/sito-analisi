---

A mass of water of $5\,\mathrm{kg}$ and a calorimeter of heat capacity $600\,\mathrm{J/K}$ are initially in equilibrium. How much heat is needed to warm both by $14\,\mathrm K$? Use $c=4200\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$ and neglect heat losses.

:::soluzione
$Q\simeq 302400\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
