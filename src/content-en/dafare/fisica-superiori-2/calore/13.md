A solid with heat capacity $210\,\mathrm{J/K}$ initially at $85\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ is immersed in water at $25\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. The container is insulated and has negligible heat capacity. What is the minimum mass of water giving an equilibrium temperature no higher than $45\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$? Use $c=4200\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$ and neglect phase changes.

:::soluzione
$m_{\min}=0.1\,\mathrm{kg}$.
:::
