Mix $5\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water at $20\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}$ and $6\,\mathrm{kg}$ at $60\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}$. The system is insulated and the container has negligible heat capacity. What is the final temperature?

:::soluzione
$T_e\simeq 41.8182\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}$.
:::
