Mix $5\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water at $20\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ and $6\,\mathrm{kg}$ at $60\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. The system is insulated and the container has negligible heat capacity. What is the final temperature?

:::soluzione
$T_e\simeq 41.8182\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$.
:::
