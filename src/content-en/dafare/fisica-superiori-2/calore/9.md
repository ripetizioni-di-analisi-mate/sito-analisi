A mass of water of $0.6\,\mathrm{kg}$ warms by $20\,\mathrm K$ without changing phase. Use $c=4200\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$. How much heat does it absorb?

:::soluzione
$Q\simeq 50400\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
