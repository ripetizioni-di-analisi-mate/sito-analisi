parole: ["Energy lost while heating"]
---

A heater supplies $9000\,\mathrm J$ to a sample and its container. Their combined heat capacity is $600\,\mathrm{J/K}$ and their common temperature increases by $12\,\mathrm K$. Neither undergoes a phase change. How much energy is lost to the surroundings?

:::soluzione
$Q_{\mathrm{disp}}=1800\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
