A sample of mass $0.5\,\mathrm{kg}$ and specific heat capacity $500\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$ absorbs $3000\,\mathrm J$ without changing phase. What is its temperature increase?

:::soluzione
$\Delta T\simeq 12\,\mathrm{K}$.
:::
