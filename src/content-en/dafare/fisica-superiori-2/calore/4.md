A sample of mass $0.5\,\mathrm{kg}$ absorbs $1600\,\mathrm J$ and its temperature rises by $14\,\mathrm K$ without changing phase. Determine its specific heat capacity.

:::soluzione
$c\simeq 228.571\,\mathrm{J/(kg K)}$.
:::
