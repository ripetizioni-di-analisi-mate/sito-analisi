A mass of water of $0.5\,\mathrm{kg}$ warms by $18\,\mathrm K$ without changing phase. Use $c=4200\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$. How much heat does it absorb?

:::soluzione
$Q\simeq 37800\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
