A wall has two side-by-side sections with thermal resistances of $0.50$ and $1.50\,\mathrm{K/W}$. In steady state each section has its inner face at $30\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ and its outer face at $6\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. Neglecting lateral heat transfer, what is the total rate of heat transfer through the wall?

:::soluzione
$P=64\,\mathrm{W}$.
:::
