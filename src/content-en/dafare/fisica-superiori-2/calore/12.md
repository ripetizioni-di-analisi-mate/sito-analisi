A sample of mass $0.6\,\mathrm{kg}$ absorbs $1800\,\mathrm J$ and its temperature rises by $15\,\mathrm K$ without changing phase. Determine its specific heat capacity.

:::soluzione
$c\simeq 200\,\mathrm{J/(kg K)}$.
:::
