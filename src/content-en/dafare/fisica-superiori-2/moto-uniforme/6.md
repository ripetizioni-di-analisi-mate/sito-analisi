---

One cyclist is $70\,\mathrm m$ ahead of another. They travel in the same direction at constant velocities of $7$ and $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$, respectively. How long does the second take to catch the first?

:::soluzione
$t\simeq 14\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
