titolo: "Catching up along a line"
teoria: ["incontri"]
parole: ["Catching up along a line"]
---

Two cyclists travel in the same direction at $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The slower cyclist is $70\,\mathrm{m}$ ahead of the faster one. How long until they meet?

:::soluzione
$t\simeq 14\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
