A conveyor belt carries a parcel at constant velocity from $t=0$ to $t=10\,\mathrm s$. The parcel is at $22\,\mathrm m$ when $t=6\,\mathrm s$ and $34\,\mathrm m$ when $t=10\,\mathrm s$. What is its initial coordinate $x_0$?

:::soluzione
$x_0=4\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
