titolo: "Required velocity on the second leg"
teoria: ["media-tratti"]
parole: ["Required velocity on the second leg"]
---

A runner covers two $24\,\mathrm m$ legs in the same direction with no stops. The velocity on the first is $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What constant velocity $v_2$ on the second gives an average of $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ over the whole route?

:::soluzione
$v_2=6\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
