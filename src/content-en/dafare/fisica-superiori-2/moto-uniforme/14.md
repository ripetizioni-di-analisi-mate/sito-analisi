teoria: ["incontri"]
parole: ["Catching up along a line"]
---

Two cyclists travel in the same direction at $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $13\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The slower cyclist is $80\,\mathrm{m}$ ahead of the faster one. How long until they meet?

:::soluzione
$t\simeq 16\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
