A sledge moves at $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for $8\,\mathrm{s}$ and then at $-6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for $6\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the total displacement?

:::soluzione
$\Delta x\simeq 20\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
