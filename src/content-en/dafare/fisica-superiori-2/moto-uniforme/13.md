Two objects $220\,\mathrm{m}$ apart set off towards each other at speeds $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$. How long until they meet?

:::soluzione
$t\simeq 14.6667\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
