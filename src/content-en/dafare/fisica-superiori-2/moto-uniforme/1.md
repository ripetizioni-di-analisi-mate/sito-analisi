A trolley has initial position $x_0=12\,\mathrm m$ and constant velocity $v=6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What position does it occupy after $t=8\,\mathrm s$?

:::soluzione
$x\simeq 60\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
