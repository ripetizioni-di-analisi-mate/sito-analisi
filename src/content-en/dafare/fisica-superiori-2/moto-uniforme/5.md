---

Two objects $200\,\mathrm{m}$ apart set off towards each other at speeds $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. How long until they meet?

:::soluzione
$t\simeq 15.4\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
