parole: ["Velocities under constant acceleration"]
---

An object has initial velocity $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and constant acceleration $6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the signed velocity after $7\,\mathrm{s}$.

:::soluzione
$v\simeq 50\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
