A vehicle travels at $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$. It maintains that velocity during a reaction time of $0.9\,\mathrm{s}$, then brakes with a constant deceleration of magnitude $5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the total distance before it stops?

:::soluzione
$s_{\mathrm{tot}}\simeq 117\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
