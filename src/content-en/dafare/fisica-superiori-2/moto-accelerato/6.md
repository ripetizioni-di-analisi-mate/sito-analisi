A vehicle travels at $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and brakes with a constant acceleration of magnitude $5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Before braking, it maintains its initial velocity through a reaction time of $0.9\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the total distance before it stops?

:::soluzione
$s_{\mathrm{tot}}\simeq 117\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
