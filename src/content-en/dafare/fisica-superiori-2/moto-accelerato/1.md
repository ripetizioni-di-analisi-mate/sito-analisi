A cart's velocity changes from $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $22\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in $6\,\mathrm{s}$. Find its average acceleration.

:::soluzione
$a=\dfrac{8}{3}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}\simeq 2.67\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
:::
