An object is thrown upward at $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and neglect air resistance. How far above the launch point does it rise?

:::soluzione
$\Delta h\simeq 45\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
