A vehicle travels at $28\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and brakes with a constant acceleration of magnitude $6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the distance travelled from braking to rest.

:::soluzione
$s_f\simeq 65.3333\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
