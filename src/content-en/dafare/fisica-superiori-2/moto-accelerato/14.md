parole: ["Maximum reaction time"]
---

In a vehicle stopping model, its speed remains $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$ during the reaction. It then brakes with a constant deceleration magnitude of $5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the maximum reaction time if the vehicle must stop within $55\,\mathrm m$ from the moment the signal is seen?

:::soluzione
$0.75\,\mathrm s$.
:::
