A trolley moves from $18$ to $22\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in $2\,\mathrm{s}$ with constant acceleration. It maintains $22\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for $4\,\mathrm{s}$, then slows to $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ over another $4\,\mathrm{s}$ with constant acceleration. What is the total displacement?

::grafico{f="18+2*x-2*max(0,x-2)-2.5*max(0,x-6)" xmin=0 xmax=10 ymin=0 ymax=25 punti="0,18,pieno;2,22,pieno;6,22,pieno;10,12,pieno" nomi="t (s),v (m/s)" titolo="The three stages last 2, 4 and 4 s."}

:::soluzione
$196\,\mathrm m$.
:::
