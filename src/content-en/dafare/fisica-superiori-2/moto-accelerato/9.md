---
titolo: "Average over two stages"
teoria: [stessi-estremi]
parole: ["Average over two stages"]
---

For the first $2\,\mathrm{s}$, a trolley maintains a constant velocity of $-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Over the next $5\,\mathrm{s}$ its velocity changes from $-4$ to $14\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the signed average acceleration over the two stages together?

:::soluzione
$\dfrac{18}{7}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
:::
