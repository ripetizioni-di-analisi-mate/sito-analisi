A vehicle travels at $28\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the positive direction and brakes with constant acceleration $a=-6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. How long does it take to brake to rest?

:::soluzione
$t_a\simeq 4.66667\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
