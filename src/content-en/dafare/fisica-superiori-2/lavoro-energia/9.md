A cart moves along a track. The component of the force along the motion is $35\,\mathrm N$ over $4\,\mathrm m$ and $-7\,\mathrm N$ over the next $2.5\,\mathrm m$. How much work does this force do over both sections?

:::soluzione
$W=122.5\,\mathrm J$.
:::
