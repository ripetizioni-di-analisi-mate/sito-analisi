A body of mass $7\,\mathrm{kg}$ is at signed height $6\,\mathrm{m}$ relative to the zero level. What is its gravitational potential energy with $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$?

:::soluzione
$U_g\simeq 420\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
