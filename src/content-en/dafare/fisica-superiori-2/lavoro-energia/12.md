A $7\,\mathrm{kg}$ box has gravitational potential energy $280\,\mathrm J$ at A. Point B is $2\,\mathrm m$ higher. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and the same reference, what is $U_g$ at B?

:::soluzione
$U_g=420\,\mathrm J$.
:::
