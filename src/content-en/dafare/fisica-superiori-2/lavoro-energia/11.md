An object of mass $7\,\mathrm{kg}$ changes speed from $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the net work done by the forces?

:::soluzione
$W_{\mathrm{tot}}\simeq 616\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
