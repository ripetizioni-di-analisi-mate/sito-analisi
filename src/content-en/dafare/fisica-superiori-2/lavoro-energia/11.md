---
titolo: "Net work of motor and gravity"
teoria: ["cinetica"]
parole: ["Net work of motor and gravity"]
---

A motor draws $1000\,\mathrm J$ at $80\%$ efficiency. It transfers all its useful output as work to a $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass that rises by $4.60\,\mathrm m$. Gravity is the only other force doing work. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the net work on the mass?

:::soluzione
$W_{\mathrm{tot}}=616\,\mathrm J$.
:::
