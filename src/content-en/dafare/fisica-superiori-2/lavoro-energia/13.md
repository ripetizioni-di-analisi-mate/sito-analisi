parole: ["Speed after descent and braking"]

A $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ cart starts at rest and descends a smooth track with a vertical drop of $50\,\mathrm m$. It then travels $10\,\mathrm m$ along a horizontal section against a constant resistance of $40\,\mathrm N$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is its speed at the end of the horizontal section?

:::soluzione
$v=10\sqrt6\,\mathrm{m/s}\simeq24.4949\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
