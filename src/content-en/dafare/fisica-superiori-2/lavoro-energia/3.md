An object of mass $6\,\mathrm{kg}$ changes speed from $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $13\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the net work done by the forces?

:::soluzione
$W_{\mathrm{tot}}\simeq 399\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
