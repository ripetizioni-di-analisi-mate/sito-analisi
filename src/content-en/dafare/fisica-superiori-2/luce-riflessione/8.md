A convex mirror has focal length $f=-9\,\mathrm{cm}$ and a real object is at $p=27\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find the signed image distance using the convention in the theory.

:::soluzione
$q\simeq -6.75\,\mathrm{cm}$.
:::
