A pinhole camera has its screen $15\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the pinhole. An object $200\,\mathrm{cm}$ tall is $300\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the pinhole. What is the magnitude of the image height?

:::soluzione
$h\simeq 10\,\mathrm{cm}$.
:::
