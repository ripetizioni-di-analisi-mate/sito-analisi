A real object is $p=27\,\mathrm{cm}$ from a concave mirror of focal length $f=9\,\mathrm{cm}$. Using the paraxial approximation, find the signed image distance.

:::soluzione
$q\simeq 13.5\,\mathrm{cm}$.
:::
