A pinhole camera has its screen $14\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the pinhole. An object $180\,\mathrm{cm}$ tall is $280\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the pinhole. What is the magnitude of the image height?

:::soluzione
$h\simeq 9\,\mathrm{cm}$.
:::
