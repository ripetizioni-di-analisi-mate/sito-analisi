Points $A$ and $B$ in front of a plane mirror are $0.48\,\mathrm m$ and $0.96\,\mathrm m$ from the plane respectively. Their perpendicular projections onto the mirror are $1.92\,\mathrm m$ apart. What is the geometric distance $s$ from $A$ to the image of $B$?

:::soluzione
$2.4\,\mathrm m$.
:::
