titolo: Downward throw from a roof
teoria: [lancio-verticale, relazione-velocita-spazio]

From the roof of a building $20\,\mathrm{m}$ high a stone is thrown vertically downward with velocity $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Neglecting air resistance, compute the velocity with which the stone hits the ground and the time it takes.

:::soluzione
The stone hits the ground with a velocity of magnitude $\sqrt{v_0^2 + 2gh} = 20.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ after $1.57\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
