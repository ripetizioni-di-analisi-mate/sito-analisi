titolo: Reversal of motion from an equation of motion

A point moves along the $x$-axis with equation of motion $x(t) = t^3 - 3t^2 - 9t + 4$, with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds, for $t \geq 0$. Determine the instant at which the point reverses its motion, and the position and the acceleration at that instant.

:::soluzione
The point reverses its motion at the instant $t = 3\,\mathrm{s}$, at $x = -23\,\mathrm{m}$, with acceleration $12\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
:::
