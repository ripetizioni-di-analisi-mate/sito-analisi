---
titolo: Pursuit with delayed start
teoria: [problemi-di-incontro, moto-rettilineo-uniforme]
parole: [pursuit, delayed start, uniform motion]
---

A cyclist passes a point $O$ at the constant speed of $8.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. A second cyclist starts from $O$ $5.0\,\mathrm{s}$ later, in the same direction, at the constant speed of $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Determine the instant, counted from the passage of the first cyclist, and the distance from $O$ at which the second catches up with the first.

:::soluzione
The second cyclist catches up with the first at the instant $t = 25\,\mathrm{s}$, $200\,\mathrm{m}$ from $O$.
:::
