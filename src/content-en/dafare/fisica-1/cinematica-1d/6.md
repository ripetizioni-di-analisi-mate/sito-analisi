A car travels at $72\,\mathrm{km/h}$. The driver sees an obstacle and reacts after $1.0\,\mathrm{s}$, during which the velocity remains constant; then the driver brakes with a constant deceleration of magnitude $8.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Compute the stopping distance.

:::soluzione
The stopping distance is $20\,\mathrm{m} + 25\,\mathrm{m} = 45\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
