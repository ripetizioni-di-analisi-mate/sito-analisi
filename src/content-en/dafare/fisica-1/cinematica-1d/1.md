A car covers a straight stretch of $30\,\mathrm{km}$ on the way out at the constant speed of $60\,\mathrm{km/h}$ and returns to the starting point at the constant speed of $40\,\mathrm{km/h}$. Compute the average speed over the whole trip and the average velocity.

:::soluzione
The average speed is $48\,\mathrm{km/h}$; the average velocity is zero, because the overall displacement is zero.
:::
