A moped starts from rest and reaches the speed of $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in $12\,\mathrm{s}$ with constant acceleration. Compute the acceleration and the distance traveled.

:::soluzione
$a = 2.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$; the distance traveled is $180\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
