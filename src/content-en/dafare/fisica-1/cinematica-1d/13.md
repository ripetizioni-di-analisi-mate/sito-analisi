A body moves in a straight line with initial velocity $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and is slowed down with acceleration $a = -k\,v$, with $k = 0.50\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$. Compute the time needed for the velocity to drop to $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the distance traveled in that time.

:::soluzione
The time is $\dfrac{\ln 4}{k} = 2.77\,\mathrm{s}$ and the distance traveled is $\dfrac{v_0 - v}{k} = 30\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
