A cart starts from rest and its velocity increases uniformly up to $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in $5{,}0\,\mathrm{s}$; it remains constant for $10\,\mathrm{s}$ and then decreases uniformly to zero in $5{,}0\,\mathrm{s}$. Compute the distance traveled and the average velocity.

:::soluzione
The distance traveled is the area of the trapezoid, $150\,\mathrm{m}$, and the average velocity is $7{,}5\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
