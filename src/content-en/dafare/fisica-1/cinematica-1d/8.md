---
titolo: Maximum height and time of flight
teoria: [altezza-massima, lancio-verticale]
parole: [vertical launch, maximum height, time of flight]
---

A ball is thrown vertically upward from the ground with velocity $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Neglecting air resistance, compute the maximum height reached and the time after which the ball returns to the ground.

:::soluzione
$h_{\max} = \dfrac{v_0^2}{2g} = 7.34\,\mathrm{m}$; the ball returns to the ground after $\dfrac{2v_0}{g} = 2.45\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
