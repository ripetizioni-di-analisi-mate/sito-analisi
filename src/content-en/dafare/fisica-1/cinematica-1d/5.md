A train travels at $108\,\mathrm{km/h}$ and brakes with constant deceleration, stopping in $450\,\mathrm{m}$. Compute the magnitude of the deceleration and the duration of the braking.

:::soluzione
The deceleration has magnitude $1{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and the braking lasts $30\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
