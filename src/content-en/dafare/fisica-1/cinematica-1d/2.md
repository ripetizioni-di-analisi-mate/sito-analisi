Two trains travel on straight parallel tracks, toward each other. At the initial instant train $A$ is at station $P$ and travels at $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$, train $B$ is $3.0\,\mathrm{km}$ from $P$ and travels at $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Determine after how much time and at what distance from $P$ the two trains pass each other.

:::soluzione
They pass each other after $60\,\mathrm{s}$, at $1.8\,\mathrm{km}$ from $P$.
:::
