An object is dropped from rest from the top of a tower $80\,\mathrm{m}$ high. Neglecting air resistance, compute the fall time and the speed with which the object hits the ground.

:::soluzione
The fall time is $\sqrt{\dfrac{2h}{g}} = 4.04\,\mathrm{s}$ and the arrival velocity has magnitude $\sqrt{2gh} = 39.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
