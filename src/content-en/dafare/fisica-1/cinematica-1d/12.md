A point moves along the $x$-axis with acceleration $a(t) = 6t$, with $a$ in $\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and $t$ in seconds. At the instant $t = 0$ it is at $x_0 = 1\,\mathrm{m}$ with velocity $v_0 = -3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Determine the position function and compute the position and the velocity at the instant $t = 2\,\mathrm{s}$.

:::soluzione
$v(t) = 3t^2 - 3$ and $x(t) = t^3 - 3t + 1$; at the instant $2\,\mathrm{s}$ the point is at $x = 3\,\mathrm{m}$ with velocity $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
