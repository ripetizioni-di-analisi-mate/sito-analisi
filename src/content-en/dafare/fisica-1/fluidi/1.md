Compute the gauge pressure and the absolute pressure at a depth of $12.0\,\mathrm{m}$ in a freshwater lake, with atmospheric pressure $p_0 = 1.013 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$.

:::soluzione
Gauge pressure $\rho g h = 1.18 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$, absolute pressure $2.19 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
:::
