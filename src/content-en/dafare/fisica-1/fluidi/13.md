A horizontal pipe $2{,}0\,\mathrm{m}$ long and of radius $1{,}0\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries water ($\eta = 1{,}0 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{Pa \cdot s}$) with a pressure difference of $4{,}0 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{Pa}$ between its ends. Compute the volumetric flow rate with Poiseuille's law and verify with the Reynolds number that the flow is laminar.

:::soluzione
$Q = \dfrac{\pi R^4\, \Delta p}{8 \eta L} = 7{,}9 \cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$; the average speed is $0{,}25\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $\mathrm{Re} = 500$, so the flow is laminar.
:::
