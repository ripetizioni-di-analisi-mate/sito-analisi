---
titolo: Pressure in a horizontal narrowing
teoria: [equazione-di-continuita, teorema-di-bernoulli]
parole: [Bernoulli's theorem, horizontal duct, pressure]
---

In a horizontal duct water flows with speed $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and pressure $1.80 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Further along, the cross section of the duct is reduced to half. Treating water as an ideal fluid, compute the speed and the pressure in the narrow section.

:::soluzione
$v_2 = 4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $p_2 = p_1 - \dfrac{1}{2}\rho\,(v_2^2 - v_1^2) = 1.74 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
:::
