titolo: Pressione in un restringimento orizzontale
teoria: [equazione-di-continuita, teorema-di-bernoulli]
parole: [teorema di Bernoulli, condotto orizzontale, pressione]
---

In a horizontal duct water flows with speed $2{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and pressure $1{,}80 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Further along, the cross-sectional area of the duct is reduced to half. Treating water as an ideal fluid, compute the speed and the pressure in the narrow section.

:::soluzione
$v_2 = 4{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $p_2 = p_1 - \dfrac{1}{2}\rho\,(v_2^2 - v_1^2) = 1{,}74 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
:::
