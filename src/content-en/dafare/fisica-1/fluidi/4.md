titolo: Pressione atmosferica in montagna

At a mountain location a mercury barometer ($\rho = 13{,}6 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$) reads $640\,\mathrm{mm}$. Compute the atmospheric pressure in pascals.

:::soluzione
$p_0 = \rho g h = 8{,}54 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
:::
