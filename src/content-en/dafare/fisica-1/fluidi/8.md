A body hanging from a spring scale weighs $3{,}00\,\mathrm{N}$ in air, $2{,}00\,\mathrm{N}$ completely immersed in water and $2{,}20\,\mathrm{N}$ completely immersed in an unknown liquid. Compute the density of the liquid.

:::soluzione
$\rho_L = \dfrac{3{,}00 - 2{,}20}{3{,}00 - 2{,}00} \cdot 1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3} = 800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.
:::
