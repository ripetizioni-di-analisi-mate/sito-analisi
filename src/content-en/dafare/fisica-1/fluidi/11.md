---
titolo: Velocità di efflusso da un foro
teoria: [teorema-di-torricelli]
parole: [teorema di Torricelli, efflusso, foro]
---

A very wide open tank has a small hole $5{,}0\,\mathrm{m}$ below the free surface of the water. Derive the formula for the exit speed and compute its value.

:::soluzione
$v = \sqrt{2 g h} = 9{,}9\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
