titolo: Flow rate of a Venturi tube
teoria: [tubo-di-venturi, equazione-di-continuita, teorema-di-bernoulli]

---

A horizontal Venturi tube through which water flows has cross section $12\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ in the wide section and $4.0\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ in the constriction; the pressure difference between the two sections is $2.4 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Compute the velocity in the wide section and the flow rate.

:::soluzione
$v_1 = \sqrt{\dfrac{2\, \Delta p}{\rho\left( \dfrac{A_1^2}{A_2^2} - 1 \right)}} = 0.77\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $Q = A_1 v_1 = 9.3 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$.
:::
