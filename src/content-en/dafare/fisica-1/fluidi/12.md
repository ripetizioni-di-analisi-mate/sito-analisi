A horizontal Venturi tube through which water flows has cross-sectional area $12\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ in the wide section and $4{,}0\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ in the constriction; the pressure difference between the two sections is $2{,}4 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Compute the speed in the wide section and the volumetric flow rate.

:::soluzione
$v_1 = \sqrt{\dfrac{2\, \Delta p}{\rho\left( \dfrac{A_1^2}{A_2^2} - 1 \right)}} = 0{,}77\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $Q = A_1 v_1 = 9{,}3 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$.
:::
