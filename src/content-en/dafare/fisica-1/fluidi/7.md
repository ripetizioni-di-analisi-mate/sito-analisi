---
titolo: Volume and density of a stone
teoria: [peso-apparente, principio-di-archimede]

---

A stone hanging from a spring scale weighs $12.0\,\mathrm{N}$ in air and $7.5\,\mathrm{N}$ when completely immersed in water. Compute the volume and the density of the stone, neglecting the buoyancy of the air.

:::soluzione
$V = \dfrac{P - P_{\mathrm{app}}}{\rho_a g} = 4.6 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^3}$ and $\rho = \dfrac{P}{P - P_{\mathrm{app}}}\, \rho_a = 2.7 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.
:::
