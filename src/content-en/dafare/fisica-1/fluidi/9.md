titolo: Speed at the outlet of a nozzle
teoria: [portata, equazione-di-continuita]
parole: [flow rate, continuity equation, nozzle]
---

A garden hose of diameter $2.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ carries water with flow rate $0.30\,\mathrm{L/s}$ and ends with a nozzle of diameter $0.50\,\mathrm{cm}$. Compute the speed of the water in the hose and at the outlet of the nozzle.

:::soluzione
In the hose $v_1 = 0.95\,\mathrm{m/s}$, at the outlet of the nozzle $v_2 = 15\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
