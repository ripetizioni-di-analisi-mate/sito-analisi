A tub contains water $40\,\mathrm{cm}$ deep. In the bottom there is a circular plug of diameter $4{,}0\,\mathrm{cm}$, below which there is air at atmospheric pressure. Compute the resultant force that the water and the air exert on the plug.

:::soluzione
$F = \rho g h\, \pi r^2 = 4{,}9\,\mathrm{N}$, directed downward.
:::
