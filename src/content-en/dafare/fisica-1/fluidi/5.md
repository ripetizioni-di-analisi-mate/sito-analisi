---
titolo: Mass that can be lifted with a press
teoria: [torchio-idraulico, principio-di-pascal]
parole: [hydraulic press, pistons, force]
---

In a hydraulic press the pistons, at the same height, have radii $1.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $18\,\mathrm{cm}$. A force of $150\,\mathrm{N}$ is applied to the small piston. Compute the maximum mass that the large piston can support, neglecting the masses of the pistons.

:::soluzione
$m = \dfrac{F_1}{g} \left( \dfrac{r_2}{r_1} \right)^2 = 2.2 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{kg}$.
:::
