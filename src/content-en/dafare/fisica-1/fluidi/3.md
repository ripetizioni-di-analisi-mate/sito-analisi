---
titolo: Density of a liquid with the U-tube
teoria: [vasi-comunicanti, legge-di-stevino]
parole: [U-tube, density, immiscible liquids]
---

Into one arm of a U-tube containing water a liquid that does not mix with water is poured. When the column of the liquid is $15.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ high, in the other arm the water stands $12.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ above the level of the interface. Compute the density of the liquid.

:::soluzione
$\rho = \dfrac{12.0}{15.0} \cdot 1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3} = 800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.
:::
