titolo: Velocity and acceleration from a position vector

The position of a point is $\vec{r}(t) = 1.5\,t^2\,\hat{\imath} + \left( 2.0\,t^3 - 6.0\,t \right)\hat{\jmath}$, with lengths in meters and time in seconds. Compute the velocity and the acceleration at the instant $t = 2.0\,\mathrm{s}$ and their magnitudes.

:::soluzione
$\vec{v} = (6.0\,\hat{\imath} + 18\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s}$ with $v \approx 19\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $\vec{a} = (3.0\,\hat{\imath} + 24\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ with $a \approx 24\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
:::
