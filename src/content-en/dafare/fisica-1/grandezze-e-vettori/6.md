titolo: Dimensions of a drag coefficient
teoria: [dimensioni, omogeneita-dimensionale]
parole: [dimensional analysis, dimensions, air resistance]
---

The air resistance on a fast body is written $F = \beta v^2$, where $F$ is a force and $v$ a velocity. Determine the dimensions and the SI unit of the coefficient $\beta$.

:::soluzione
$[\beta] = [M\,L^{-1}]$, so $\beta$ is measured in $\mathrm{kg/m}$.
:::
