Treating the Earth as a sphere of radius $R = 6{,}37 \cdot 10^{6}\,\mathrm{m}$, compute its volume and state its order of magnitude.

:::soluzione
$V = \dfrac{4}{3}\pi R^3 \approx 1{,}08 \cdot 10^{21}\,\mathrm{m^3}$, with order of magnitude $10^{21}\,\mathrm{m^3}$.
:::
