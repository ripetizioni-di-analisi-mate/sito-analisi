The force $\vec{F} = (10\,\hat{\imath} - 5.0\,\hat{\jmath} + 20\,\hat{k})\,\mathrm{N}$ is applied at the point with position $\vec{r} = (0.30\,\hat{\imath} + 0.40\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m}$ relative to the pole $O$. Compute the torque of the force about $O$ and its magnitude.

:::soluzione
$\vec{\tau} = (8.0\,\hat{\imath} - 6.0\,\hat{\jmath} - 5.5\,\hat{k})\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$, with magnitude $\tau \approx 11\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$.
:::
