Seawater has a density of $1.03\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. Express the density in $\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and compute the mass of $2.5\,\mathrm{L}$ of seawater.

:::soluzione
$\rho = 1.03 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$; the mass is $m \approx 2.6\,\mathrm{kg}$.
:::
