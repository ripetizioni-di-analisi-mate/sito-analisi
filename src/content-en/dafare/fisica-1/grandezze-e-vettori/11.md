titolo: Lavoro con il prodotto scalare

A body undergoes the displacement $\vec{s} = (3{,}0\,\hat{\imath} + 4{,}0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m}$ under the action of the constant force $\vec{F} = (5{,}0\,\hat{\imath} - 2{,}0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{N}$. Compute the work done by the force and the angle between the force and the displacement.

:::soluzione
$L = 7{,}0\,\mathrm{J}$; the angle is $\theta \approx 74{,}9^\circ$.
:::
