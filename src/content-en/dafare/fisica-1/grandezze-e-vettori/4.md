A rectangular sheet measures $21{,}0\,\mathrm{cm}$ by $29{,}7\,\mathrm{cm}$. Compute its area in $\mathrm{m^2}$, in scientific notation and with the correct number of significant figures.

:::soluzione
$A = 6{,}24 \cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m^2}$.
:::
