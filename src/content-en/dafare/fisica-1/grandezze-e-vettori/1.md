A regional train travels at $72\,\mathrm{km/h}$ and a runner at $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Express the speed of the train in $\mathrm{m/s}$ and that of the runner in $\mathrm{km/h}$.

:::soluzione
The train travels at $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$, the runner at $54\,\mathrm{km/h}$.
:::
