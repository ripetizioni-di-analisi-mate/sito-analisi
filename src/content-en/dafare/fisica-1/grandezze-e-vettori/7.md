titolo: Exponents from dimensional analysis
teoria: [dimensioni, omogeneita-dimensionale]
parole: [dimensional analysis, exponents, fall, velocity]
---

The speed $v$ with which an object dropped from rest reaches the ground depends on the gravitational acceleration $g$ and on the height $h$. Assuming $v = k\,g^{a} h^{b}$ with $k$ dimensionless, determine $a$ and $b$.

:::soluzione
$a = b = \dfrac{1}{2}$, so $v = k\sqrt{g h}$.
:::
