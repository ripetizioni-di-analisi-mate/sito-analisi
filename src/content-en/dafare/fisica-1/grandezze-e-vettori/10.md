Three forces in the $xy$-plane act on a point particle: $40\,\mathrm{N}$ at $0^\circ$, $30\,\mathrm{N}$ at $90^\circ$ and $20\,\mathrm{N}$ at $225^\circ$, with the angles measured counterclockwise from the positive $x$-semiaxis. Determine the magnitude and direction of the resultant.

:::soluzione
$\vec{R} = (25.9\,\hat{\imath} + 15.9\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{N}$, with magnitude $R \approx 30.3\,\mathrm{N}$ and angle $\theta \approx 31.5^\circ$.
:::
