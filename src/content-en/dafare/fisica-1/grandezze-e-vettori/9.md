A velocity has components $v_x = -6{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_y = 8{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Determine its magnitude and the angle with the positive $x$-axis, measured counterclockwise.

:::soluzione
$v = 10\,\mathrm{m/s}$, $\theta \approx 126{,}9^\circ$.
:::
