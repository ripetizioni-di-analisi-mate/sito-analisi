A force of magnitude $25\,\mathrm{N}$ forms an angle of $210^\circ$ with the positive $x$-axis, measured counterclockwise. Compute its Cartesian components.

:::soluzione
$\vec{F} = (-21{,}7\,\hat{\imath} - 12{,}5\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
