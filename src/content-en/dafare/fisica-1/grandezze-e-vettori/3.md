Express in SI units, in scientific notation, the volume $V = 350\,\mathrm{cm^3}$, the area $A = 2.4\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ and the concentration $72\,\mathrm{g/L}$.

:::soluzione
$V = 3.50 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^3}$, $A = 2.4 \cdot 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^2}$, $72\,\mathrm{g/L} = 72\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.
:::
