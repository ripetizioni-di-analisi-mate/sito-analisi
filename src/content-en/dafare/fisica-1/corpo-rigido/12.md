A uniform beam of mass $80\,\mathrm{kg}$ and length $6.0\,\mathrm{m}$ rests horizontally on two supports placed at the ends $A$ and $B$. A person of mass $70\,\mathrm{kg}$ stands on the beam $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Compute the reactions of the two supports.

:::soluzione
$R_A = 907\,\mathrm{N}$ and $R_B = 564\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
