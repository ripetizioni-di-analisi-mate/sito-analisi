A motor applies a constant torque of $12\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$ to a wheel, initially at rest, with moment of inertia $0.80\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ about the axis; friction is negligible. Compute, after $4.0\,\mathrm{s}$, the angular velocity, the kinetic energy of the wheel and the power delivered by the motor at that instant.

:::soluzione
$\omega = 60\,\mathrm{rad/s}$, $K = 1.44 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{J}$, $P = \tau_z\,\omega = 720\,\mathrm{W}$.
:::
