---
titolo: Minimum friction for the rolling of a sphere
teoria: [rotolamento-piano-inclinato]
parole: [pure rolling, sphere, static friction, inclined plane]
---

A uniform solid sphere descends along a plane inclined at $40^\circ$. Compute the minimum coefficient of static friction between sphere and plane for which the motion is pure rolling.

:::soluzione
$\mu_s \geq \dfrac{2}{7}\tan 40^\circ = 0.24$.
:::
