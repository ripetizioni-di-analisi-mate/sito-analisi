---
titolo: Attrito minimo per il rotolamento di una sfera
teoria: [rotolamento-piano-inclinato]
parole: [rotolamento puro, sfera, attrito statico, piano inclinato]
---

A uniform solid sphere descends along a plane inclined at $40^\circ$. Compute the minimum coefficient of static friction between sphere and plane for which the sphere rolls without slipping.

:::soluzione
$\mu_s \geq \dfrac{2}{7}\tan 40^\circ = 0{,}24$.
:::
