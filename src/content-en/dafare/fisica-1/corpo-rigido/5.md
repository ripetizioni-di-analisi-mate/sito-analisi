titolo: Atwood machine with a massive pulley

Two blocks of masses $5.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $3.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ hang from the ends of an inextensible string of negligible mass that passes, without slipping, over a pulley in the shape of a uniform disk of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ and radius $0.10\,\mathrm{m}$, free to rotate without friction. Compute the acceleration of the blocks and the tensions in the two segments of string.

:::soluzione
$a = 2.18\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$; the tension is $38.2\,\mathrm{N}$ on the side of the $5.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ block and $36.0\,\mathrm{N}$ on the side of the $3.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ block.
:::
