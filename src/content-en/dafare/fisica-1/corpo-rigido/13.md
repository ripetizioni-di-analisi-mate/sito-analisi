---
titolo: Minimum angle of a leaning ladder
teoria: [condizioni-di-equilibrio, scelta-del-polo, momento-della-forza-peso]
parole: [leaning ladder, static friction, equilibrium]
---

A uniform ladder leans against a smooth vertical wall; the coefficient of static friction between the ladder and the floor is $0.35$. Compute the minimum angle that the ladder can form with the floor while remaining in equilibrium.

:::soluzione
$\tan\theta_{\min} = \dfrac{1}{2\mu_s} = 1.43$, therefore $\theta_{\min} = 55.0^\circ$.
:::
