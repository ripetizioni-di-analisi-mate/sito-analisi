teoria: [urti-con-asse-fisso, conservazione-momento-angolare, momenti-di-inerzia-notevoli]

A uniform rod of mass $1.5\,\mathrm{kg}$ and length $1.2\,\mathrm{m}$ hangs at rest from a frictionless hinge located at its upper end. A bullet of mass $10\,\mathrm{g}$, traveling horizontally at $300\,\mathrm{m/s}$ perpendicular to the axis of the hinge, strikes the lower end of the rod and stays embedded in it. Compute the angular velocity of the rod immediately after the collision.

:::soluzione
$I_O = 0.734\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ and $\omega = \dfrac{m v_0 L}{I_O} = 4.90\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
:::
