A uniform thin ring of mass $0{,}50\,\mathrm{kg}$ and radius $0{,}30\,\mathrm{m}$ rotates about an axis perpendicular to the plane of the ring through a point on the ring. Compute the moment of inertia about this axis.

:::soluzione
$I = 2 M R^2 = 0{,}090\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$.
:::
