A uniform solid cylinder of mass $8.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ and radius $0.20\,\mathrm{m}$ rotates without friction about its own horizontal axis. An inextensible string of negligible mass, wound around the cylinder without slipping, supports a block of mass $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$. Compute the acceleration of the block and the tension of the string.

:::soluzione
$a = \dfrac{m g}{m + \dfrac{M}{2}} = 1.96\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and $T = 7.85\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
