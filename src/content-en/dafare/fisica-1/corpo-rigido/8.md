A uniform solid cylinder starts from rest and rolls without slipping down an inclined plane, descending through a height difference of $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$. Compute the velocity of the center of the cylinder at the end of the descent.

:::soluzione
$v_C = \sqrt{\dfrac{4 g h}{3}} = 3.23\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
