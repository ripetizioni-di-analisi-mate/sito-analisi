A uniform rod $0{,}90\,\mathrm{m}$ long, hinged without friction at one end, is released from rest in a horizontal position. Compute the angular velocity of the rod and the speed of the free end when the rod passes through the vertical.

:::soluzione
$\omega = \sqrt{\dfrac{3g}{L}} = 5{,}72\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ and $v = \sqrt{3gL} = 5{,}15\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
