A wheel with moment of inertia $2{,}5\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ about its own axis turns at $90\ \text{giri al minuto}$. A brake applies a constant torque of $3{,}0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$ that opposes the rotation; the friction of the axle is negligible. Compute the stopping time and the number of revolutions made during braking.

:::soluzione
$|\alpha| = 1{,}2\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$, stopping time $7{,}85\,\mathrm{s}$, angle swept out $37{,}0\,\mathrm{rad}$, that is $5{,}89$ revolutions.
:::
