titolo: Masses at the vertices of a square

Four point masses of $0.50\,\mathrm{kg}$ each are fixed at the vertices of a square frame of side $0.40\,\mathrm{m}$ and negligible mass. Compute the moment of inertia of the system about the axis perpendicular to the plane of the square through the center and about the parallel axis through a vertex.

:::soluzione
$I_C = 0.16\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ about the center and $I = 0.32\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ about the vertex.
:::
