A platform in the shape of a uniform disk, of mass $150\,\mathrm{kg}$ and radius $1{,}5\,\mathrm{m}$, rotates without friction about its own vertical axis at $0{,}80\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. A person of mass $75\,\mathrm{kg}$, with zero initial angular momentum about the axis, jumps onto the rim of the platform and stays there at rest relative to it. Treating the person as a point mass, compute the final angular velocity.

:::soluzione
$\omega = \dfrac{168{,}75}{337{,}5} \cdot 0{,}80\,\mathrm{rad/s} = 0{,}40\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
:::
