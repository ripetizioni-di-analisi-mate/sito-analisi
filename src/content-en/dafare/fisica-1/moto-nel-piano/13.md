---
titolo: Swimmer crossing a river
teoria: [composizione-delle-velocita]
parole: [velocity addition, river, current, drift]
---

A swimmer crosses a river $30\,\mathrm{m}$ wide, swimming perpendicular to the banks with velocity $1.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ relative to the water; the current has velocity $0.50\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Compute the velocity of the swimmer relative to the bank, the crossing time and the downstream displacement.

:::soluzione
$v = 1.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$, time $25\,\mathrm{s}$, downstream displacement $12.5\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
