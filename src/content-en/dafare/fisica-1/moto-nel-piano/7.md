A bicycle travels at $18\,\mathrm{km/h}$ and its wheels have radius $0{,}35\,\mathrm{m}$. Assuming that the wheels roll without slipping, so that the speed of the center of each wheel is $\omega R$, compute the angular velocity of the wheels and the number of revolutions per minute.

:::soluzione
$\omega = \dfrac{5{,}0}{0{,}35}\,\mathrm{rad/s} = 14{,}3\,\mathrm{rad/s}$, that is, $136$ revolutions per minute.
:::
