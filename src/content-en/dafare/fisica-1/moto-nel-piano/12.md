A point moves with equation of motion $\vec{r}(t) = 4.0\,t\;\hat{\imath} + 3.0\,t^2\;\hat{\jmath}$, with $\vec{r}$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. At time $t = 2.0\,\mathrm{s}$ compute the magnitude of the velocity, the tangential and normal components of the acceleration and the radius of curvature of the trajectory.

:::soluzione
$\vec{v} = (4,\ 12)\,\mathrm{m/s}$ with $v = 12.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $a_t = 5.69\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, $a_n = 1.90\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, $\rho = 84.3\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
