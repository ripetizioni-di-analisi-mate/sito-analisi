---
titolo: Radius of curvature at the highest point
teoria: [raggio-di-curvatura, altezza-massima]
parole: [radius of curvature, centripetal acceleration, projectile]
---

A projectile is launched with velocity $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$ inclined at $60^\circ$ to the horizontal. Neglecting air resistance, compute the radius of curvature of the trajectory at the highest point.

:::soluzione
$\rho = \dfrac{(v_0\cos\theta)^2}{g} = \dfrac{7.5^2}{9.81}\,\mathrm{m} = 5.73\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
