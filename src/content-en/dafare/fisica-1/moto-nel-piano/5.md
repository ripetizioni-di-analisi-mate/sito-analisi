---
titolo: Heading of a plane in a crosswind
teoria: [composizione-delle-velocita, sistemi-in-moto-relativo]
parole: [relative velocity, wind, heading, velocity addition]
---

A plane flies at $200\,\mathrm{km/h}$ relative to the air and must reach a city located $300\,\mathrm{km}$ north of the starting point. A uniform wind of $40\,\mathrm{km/h}$ blows toward the east. Compute in which direction the plane must head, its velocity relative to the ground and the duration of the trip.

:::soluzione
The plane heads $11.5^\circ$ west of north; the velocity relative to the ground is $\sqrt{200^2 - 40^2}\,\mathrm{km/h} = 196\,\mathrm{km/h}$ and the trip lasts $1.53\,\mathrm{h}$, about $92$ minutes.
:::
