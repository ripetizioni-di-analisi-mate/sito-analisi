A plane flies at $200\,\mathrm{km/h}$ relative to the air and must reach a city located $300\,\mathrm{km}$ north of the starting point. A uniform wind of $40\,\mathrm{km/h}$ blows toward the east. Determine the heading the plane must take, its velocity relative to the ground and the duration of the trip.

:::soluzione
The plane heads $11{,}5^\circ$ west of north; the velocity relative to the ground is $\sqrt{200^2 - 40^2}\,\mathrm{km/h} = 196\,\mathrm{km/h}$ and the trip lasts $1{,}53\,\mathrm{h}$, about $92$ minutes.
:::
