A small ball leaves the edge of a table $1.25\,\mathrm{m}$ high horizontally and hits the floor $1.50\,\mathrm{m}$ from the vertical through the edge. Neglecting air resistance, compute the velocity with which the ball left the table.

:::soluzione
The fall time is $\sqrt{\dfrac{2 \cdot 1.25}{9.81}}\,\mathrm{s} = 0.505\,\mathrm{s}$, therefore $v_0 = 2.97\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
