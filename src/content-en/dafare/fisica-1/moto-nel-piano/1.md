A small ball leaves horizontally from the edge of a table $1{,}25\,\mathrm{m}$ high and hits the floor $1{,}50\,\mathrm{m}$ from the vertical through the edge. Neglecting air resistance, compute the velocity with which the ball left the table.

:::soluzione
The time of fall is $\sqrt{\dfrac{2 \cdot 1{,}25}{9{,}81}}\,\mathrm{s} = 0{,}505\,\mathrm{s}$, so $v_0 = 2{,}97\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
