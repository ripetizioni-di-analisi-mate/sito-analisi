The International Space Station travels in an orbit that can be considered circular, of radius $6.78 \cdot 10^6\,\mathrm{m}$ measured from the center of the Earth, with a period of $92.7$ minutes. Compute its speed and its centripetal acceleration.

:::soluzione
$v = \dfrac{2\pi r}{T} = 7.66 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $a_n = \dfrac{v^2}{r} = 8.65\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
:::
