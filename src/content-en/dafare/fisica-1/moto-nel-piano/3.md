An object launched from the ground must land $50\,\mathrm{m}$ from the launch point, on level ground. Neglecting air resistance, compute the minimum launch speed required and the corresponding launch angle.

:::soluzione
With $\theta = 45^\circ$ and $v_0 = \sqrt{g R} = 22{,}1\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
