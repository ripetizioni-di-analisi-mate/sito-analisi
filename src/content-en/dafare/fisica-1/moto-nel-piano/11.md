A disk starts from rest and rotates with constant angular acceleration $4{,}0\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$. For a point $0{,}20\,\mathrm{m}$ from the axis, compute tangential acceleration, centripetal acceleration and magnitude of the acceleration at time $t = 2{,}0\,\mathrm{s}$.

:::soluzione
$a_t = 0{,}80\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, $a_n = (8{,}0\,\mathrm{rad/s})^2 \cdot 0{,}20\,\mathrm{m} = 12{,}8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, $|\vec{a}| = 12{,}8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
:::
