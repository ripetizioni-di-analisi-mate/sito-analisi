The shaft of an engine goes from $600$ to $1500$ revolutions per minute in $6.0\,\mathrm{s}$, with constant angular acceleration. Compute the angular acceleration and the number of revolutions completed in the $6.0\,\mathrm{s}$.

:::soluzione
$\alpha = 5\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s^2} = 15.7\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$; the revolutions completed are $105$.
:::
