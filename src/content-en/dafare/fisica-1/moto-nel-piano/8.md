A car travels at a constant speed of $72\,\mathrm{km/h}$ around a circular curve of radius $60\,\mathrm{m}$. Compute its acceleration.

:::soluzione
The acceleration is centripetal and equals $\dfrac{(20\,\mathrm{m/s})^2}{60\,\mathrm{m}} = 6{,}67\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
:::
