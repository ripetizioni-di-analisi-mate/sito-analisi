titolo: Oblique launch from a balcony
teoria: [lancio-da-quota, velocita-e-quota]
parole: [launch from a height, quadratic equation, impact velocity]
---

From a balcony $10\,\mathrm{m}$ high a stone is thrown with velocity $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ inclined at $45^\circ$ upward. Neglecting air resistance, compute after how much time and at what horizontal distance from the base of the balcony the stone hits the ground, and the magnitude of its velocity at impact.

:::soluzione
$t = 2.32\,\mathrm{s}$, distance $16.4\,\mathrm{m}$, velocity at impact $\sqrt{100 + 2 \cdot 9.81 \cdot 10}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 17.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
