From a balcony $10\,\mathrm{m}$ high a stone is thrown with velocity $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ directed $45^\circ$ above the horizontal. Neglecting air resistance, compute the time until the stone hits the ground, the horizontal distance from the base of the balcony to the impact point, and the magnitude of its velocity at impact.

:::soluzione
$t = 2{,}32\,\mathrm{s}$, distance $16{,}4\,\mathrm{m}$, impact speed $\sqrt{100 + 2 \cdot 9{,}81 \cdot 10}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 17{,}2\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
