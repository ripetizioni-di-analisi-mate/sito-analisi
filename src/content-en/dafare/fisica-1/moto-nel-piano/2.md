A projectile is launched from the ground with velocity $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$ inclined at $60^\circ$ to the horizontal, on level ground. Neglecting air resistance, compute the time of flight, the maximum height and the range.

:::soluzione
$T = 5.30\,\mathrm{s}$, $H = 34.4\,\mathrm{m}$, $R = 79.5\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
