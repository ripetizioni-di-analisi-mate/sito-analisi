---
titolo: Design speed of a banked curve
teoria: [curva-sopraelevata, forza-centripeta]
parole: [banked curve, centripetal force, design speed]
---

A curve of radius $120\,\mathrm{m}$ has its roadway inclined at $15^\circ$ toward the center. Compute the speed at which a car travels around it without needing lateral friction.

:::soluzione
$v = \sqrt{g r \tan 15^\circ} = 17.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$, about $64\,\mathrm{km/h}$.
:::
