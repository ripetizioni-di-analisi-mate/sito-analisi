A block of mass $4{,}0\,\mathrm{kg}$ on a smooth horizontal table is connected, by an ideal string that passes over an ideal pulley at the edge of the table, to a hanging body of mass $1{,}0\,\mathrm{kg}$. Compute the tension of the string during the motion.

:::soluzione
$a = \dfrac{m_2}{m_1 + m_2}\,g = 1{,}96\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and $T = m_1 a = 7{,}85\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
