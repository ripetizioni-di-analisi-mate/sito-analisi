---
titolo: Misura del coefficiente di attrito statico
teoria: [angolo-limite, leggi-dell-attrito]
parole: [angolo limite, attrito statico, coefficiente di attrito]
---

A coin rests on a book, which is slowly tilted. The coin starts to slide when the cover forms an angle of $27^\circ$ with the horizontal. Compute the coefficient of static friction between the coin and the cover.

:::soluzione
$\mu_s = \tan 27^\circ = 0{,}51$.
:::
