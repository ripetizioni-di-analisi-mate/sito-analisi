---
titolo: Accelerazione di un ascensore dalla bilancia
teoria: [peso-apparente]
parole: [ascensore, peso apparente, bilancia]
---

A person of mass $60\,\mathrm{kg}$ stands on a scale in an elevator. At a certain instant, the scale reads $540\,\mathrm{N}$. Compute the acceleration of the elevator and state its direction.

:::soluzione
$a = \dfrac{N}{m} - g = -0{,}81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, that is, $0{,}81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ directed downward.
:::
