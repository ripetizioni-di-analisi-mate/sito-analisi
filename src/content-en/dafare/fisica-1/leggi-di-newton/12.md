---
titolo: Acceleration of an elevator from the scale
teoria: [peso-apparente]
parole: [elevator, apparent weight, scale]
---

A person of mass $60\,\mathrm{kg}$ stands on a scale in an elevator, which at a certain instant reads $540\,\mathrm{N}$. Compute the acceleration of the elevator, indicating its sense.

:::soluzione
$a = \dfrac{N}{m} - g = -0.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, that is, $0.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ directed downward.
:::
