A pendulum hangs from the ceiling of a railway car that accelerates on a straight horizontal track with constant acceleration $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Compute the angle that the string forms with the vertical when the pendulum is at rest relative to the car.

:::soluzione
$\tan\theta = \dfrac{A}{g} = 0.306$, therefore $\theta = 17^\circ$.
:::
