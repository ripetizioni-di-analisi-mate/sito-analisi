---
titolo: Crate dragged with friction
teoria: [leggi-dell-attrito, secondo-principio]
parole: [kinetic friction, acceleration, crate]
---

A crate of mass $50\,\mathrm{kg}$ is dragged across a horizontal floor by a horizontal force of $300\,\mathrm{N}$. The coefficient of kinetic friction is $\mu_d = 0.40$. Compute the acceleration of the crate.

:::soluzione
$a = \dfrac{F - \mu_d m g}{m} = 2.08\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
:::
