---
titolo: Discesa lungo un piano liscio
teoria: [piano-inclinato]
parole: [piano inclinato, piano liscio, tempo di discesa]
---

A block starts from rest and slides down a frictionless plane inclined at $20^\circ$. Compute the time it takes to travel $4{,}0\,\mathrm{m}$ along the plane.

:::soluzione
$a = g\sin 20^\circ = 3{,}36\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, so $t = \sqrt{\dfrac{2d}{a}} = 1{,}54\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
