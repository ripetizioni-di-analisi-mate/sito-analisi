---
titolo: Sliding down a smooth plane
teoria: [piano-inclinato]
parole: [inclined plane, smooth plane, descent time]
---

A block starts from rest and slides down a smooth plane inclined at $20^\circ$. Compute the time it takes to travel $4.0\,\mathrm{m}$ along the plane.

:::soluzione
$a = g\sin 20^\circ = 3.36\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, therefore $t = \sqrt{\dfrac{2d}{a}} = 1.54\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
