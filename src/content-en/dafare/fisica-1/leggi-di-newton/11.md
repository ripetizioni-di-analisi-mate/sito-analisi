---
titolo: Bucket rotating vertically
teoria: [giro-della-morte, forza-centripeta]
parole: [vertical circular motion, minimum speed, tension]
---

A bucket full of water is swung in a vertical plane along a circle of radius $0.90\,\mathrm{m}$. Compute the minimum speed the bucket must have at the highest point for the water to stay in the bucket.

:::soluzione
$v_{\min} = \sqrt{g r} = 2.97\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
