titolo: Corpo appeso a due fili simmetrici
teoria: [equilibrio-del-punto, tensione]
parole: [equilibrio, tensione, fili]
---

A body of mass $12\,\mathrm{kg}$ hangs at rest from two symmetric ideal strings, each forming an angle of $45^\circ$ with the horizontal. Compute the tension of each string.

:::soluzione
$T = \dfrac{mg}{2 \sin 45^\circ} = 83{,}2\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
