A body of mass $8{,}0\,\mathrm{kg}$, initially at rest, moves on a smooth horizontal surface and reaches a velocity of $6{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in $3{,}0\,\mathrm{s}$. Assuming the net force is constant, compute its magnitude.

:::soluzione
$R = m\,\dfrac{\Delta v}{\Delta t} = 16\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
