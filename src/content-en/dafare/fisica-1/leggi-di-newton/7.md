A body of mass $5.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ hangs from two equal springs, connected in parallel, each with spring constant $400\,\mathrm{N/m}$. Compute the extension of the springs at equilibrium.

:::soluzione
$x = \dfrac{mg}{2k} = 6.13\,\mathrm{cm}$.
:::
