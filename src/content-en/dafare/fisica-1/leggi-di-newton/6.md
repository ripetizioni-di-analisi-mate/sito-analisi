---
titolo: Pushing uphill at constant velocity
teoria: [piano-inclinato-con-attrito, equilibrio-del-punto]
parole: [inclined plane, kinetic friction, constant velocity]
---

A body of mass $10\,\mathrm{kg}$ must be pushed up a plane inclined at $30^\circ$ at constant velocity, with coefficient of kinetic friction $\mu_d = 0.20$. Compute the magnitude of the required force, parallel to the plane.

:::soluzione
$F = mg\,(\sin 30^\circ + \mu_d \cos 30^\circ) = 66.0\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
