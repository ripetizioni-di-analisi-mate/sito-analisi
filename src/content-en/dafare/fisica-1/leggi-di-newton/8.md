---
titolo: Tension in an Atwood machine
teoria: [macchina-di-atwood]
parole: [Atwood machine, tension, pulley]
---

An Atwood machine carries at the ends of the string two bodies of masses $2.5\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $1.5\,\mathrm{kg}$. String and pulley are ideal. Compute the tension of the string during the motion.

:::soluzione
$T = \dfrac{2\,m_1 m_2}{m_1 + m_2}\,g = 18.4\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
