Two bodies hang from opposite ends of the string of an Atwood machine. Their masses are $2{,}5\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $1{,}5\,\mathrm{kg}$. The string and pulley are ideal. Compute the tension of the string during motion.

:::soluzione
$T = \dfrac{2\,m_1 m_2}{m_1 + m_2}\,g = 18{,}4\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
