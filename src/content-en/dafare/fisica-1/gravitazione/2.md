titolo: Gravitational acceleration on the Moon

The Moon has mass $M_L = 7.35 \cdot 10^{22}\,\mathrm{kg}$ and radius $R_L = 1.74 \cdot 10^6\,\mathrm{m}$. Compute the gravitational acceleration at its surface ($G = 6.67 \cdot 10^{-11}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m^2/kg^2}$).

:::soluzione
$g_L = \dfrac{G M_L}{R_L^2} = 1.62\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, about one sixth of the Earth's value.
:::
