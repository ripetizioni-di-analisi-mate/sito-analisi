Two ships, each of mass $5.0 \cdot 10^7\,\mathrm{kg}$, have their centers of mass at a distance $d = 1.0 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{m}$. Treating them as point masses, compute the magnitude of the gravitational force with which they attract each other ($G = 6.67 \cdot 10^{-11}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m^2/kg^2}$).

:::soluzione
$F = G\,\dfrac{m^2}{d^2} = 17\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
