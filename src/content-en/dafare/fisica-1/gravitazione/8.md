Mars has mass $6.42 \cdot 10^{23}\,\mathrm{kg}$ and rotates about its axis in $88\,642\,\mathrm{s}$. Compute the radius of the equatorial circular orbit of a satellite that always stays above the same point of the Martian surface ($G = 6.67 \cdot 10^{-11}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m^2/kg^2}$).

:::soluzione
$r = \sqrt[3]{\dfrac{G M T^2}{4\pi^2}} = 2.04 \cdot 10^7\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
