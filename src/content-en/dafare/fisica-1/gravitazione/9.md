---
titolo: Escape velocity from Mars
teoria: [velocita-di-fuga]
parole: [escape velocity, Mars]
---

Compute the escape velocity from the surface of Mars, which has mass $6.42 \cdot 10^{23}\,\mathrm{kg}$ and radius $3.39 \cdot 10^6\,\mathrm{m}$, neglecting the atmosphere and the rotation ($G = 6.67 \cdot 10^{-11}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m^2/kg^2}$).

:::soluzione
$v_f = \sqrt{\dfrac{2GM}{R}} = 5.03 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
