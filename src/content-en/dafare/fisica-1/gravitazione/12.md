---
titolo: Speed of a comet at aphelion
teoria: [seconda-legge-di-keplero]
parole: [Kepler's second law, angular momentum, comet]
---

A comet passes perihelion at $1.0\,\mathrm{AU}$ from the Sun with speed $40\,\mathrm{km/s}$ and reaches aphelion at $9.0\,\mathrm{AU}$. Compute its speed at aphelion.

:::soluzione
$v_a = \dfrac{r_p}{r_a}\,v_p = 4.4\,\mathrm{km/s}$.
:::
