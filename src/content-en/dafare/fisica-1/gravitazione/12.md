A comet passes through perihelion at $1{,}0\,\mathrm{UA}$ from the Sun with speed $40\,\mathrm{km/s}$ and reaches aphelion at $9{,}0\,\mathrm{UA}$. Compute its speed at aphelion.

:::soluzione
$v_a = \dfrac{r_p}{r_a}\,v_p = 4{,}4\,\mathrm{km/s}$.
:::
