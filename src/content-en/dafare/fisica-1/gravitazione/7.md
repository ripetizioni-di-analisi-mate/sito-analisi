The Moon travels around the Earth on a nearly circular orbit of radius $3.84 \cdot 10^8\,\mathrm{m}$ in $27.3$ days. Find the mass of the Earth ($G = 6.67 \cdot 10^{-11}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m^2/kg^2}$).

:::soluzione
$M_T = \dfrac{4\pi^2 r^3}{G T^2} = 6.02 \cdot 10^{24}\,\mathrm{kg}$.
:::
