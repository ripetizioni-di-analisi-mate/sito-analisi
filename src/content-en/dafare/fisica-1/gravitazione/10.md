A body is launched vertically from the surface of the Moon with speed $v_0 = 2.0\,\mathrm{km/s}$. Compute the maximum altitude reached. Data: $M_L = 7.35 \cdot 10^{22}\,\mathrm{kg}$, $R_L = 1.74 \cdot 10^6\,\mathrm{m}$, $G = 6.67 \cdot 10^{-11}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m^2/kg^2}$; the Moon has no atmosphere.

:::soluzione
$\dfrac{1}{r_{\max}} = \dfrac{1}{R_L} - \dfrac{v_0^2}{2 G M_L}$ gives $r_{\max} = 6.00 \cdot 10^6\,\mathrm{m}$, so the maximum altitude is $h = 4.26 \cdot 10^6\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
