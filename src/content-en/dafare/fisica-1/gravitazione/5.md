---
titolo: Depth with g reduced by ten percent
teoria: [variazione-di-g-con-la-profondita, campo-di-una-sfera-omogenea]
parole: [g at depth, homogeneous sphere]
---

In the model of a spherical Earth of uniform density, with $R_T = 6.37 \cdot 10^6\,\mathrm{m}$, at what depth is the gravitational acceleration $90\%$ of its value at the surface?

:::soluzione
$d = 0.10\,R_T = 6.37 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
