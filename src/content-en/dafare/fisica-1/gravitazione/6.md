A satellite travels on a circular orbit at an altitude $h = 1000\,\mathrm{km}$ above the Earth's surface. Compute the speed and the period, with $G M_T = 3.98 \cdot 10^{14}\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$ and $R_T = 6.37 \cdot 10^6\,\mathrm{m}$.

:::soluzione
$v = \sqrt{\dfrac{G M_T}{R_T + h}} = 7.35 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{m/s}$, $T = 6.30 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{s} \approx 105\,\mathrm{min}$.
:::
