A satellite of mass $800\,\mathrm{kg}$ travels on a circular orbit of radius $r = 8.0 \cdot 10^6\,\mathrm{m}$ around the Earth ($G M_T = 3.98 \cdot 10^{14}\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$). Compute its mechanical energy and the minimum work needed to bring it to infinite distance.

:::soluzione
$E = -\dfrac{G M_T m}{2r} = -1.99 \cdot 10^{10}\,\mathrm{J}$; the minimum work is $1.99 \cdot 10^{10}\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
