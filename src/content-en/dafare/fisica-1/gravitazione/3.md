titolo: Altitude with a given value of g
teoria: [variazione-di-g-con-la-quota]
parole: [g at altitude, altitude]
---

At what altitude above the Earth's surface is the gravitational acceleration equal to $9.00\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$? Use $g_0 = 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and $R_T = 6.37 \cdot 10^6\,\mathrm{m}$.

:::soluzione
$h = R_T \left( \sqrt{\dfrac{g_0}{g}} - 1 \right) = 2.80 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
