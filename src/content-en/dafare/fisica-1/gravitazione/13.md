titolo: Semi-major axis of the orbit of an asteroid

parole: [Kepler's third law, asteroid, semi-major axis]
---

An asteroid travels its orbit around the Sun in $8.0$ years. Compute the semi-major axis of the orbit in astronomical units.

:::soluzione
$a = \sqrt[3]{T^2} = \sqrt[3]{64}\,\mathrm{AU} = 4.0\,\mathrm{AU}$, with $T$ in years.
:::
