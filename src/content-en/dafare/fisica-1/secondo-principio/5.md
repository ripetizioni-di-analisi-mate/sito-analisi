A Carnot heat pump heats a house at $22\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ taking heat from the outside at $0\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$, and it must deliver a thermal power of $5.0\,\mathrm{kW}$ to the house. Compute the coefficient of performance and the minimum mechanical power required.

:::soluzione
$\varepsilon_{\text{hp}} = \dfrac{295}{22} \approx 13.4$ and $P_{\min} \approx 3.7 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{W}$.
:::
