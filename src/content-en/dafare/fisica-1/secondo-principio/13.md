In an insulated container of negligible heat capacity $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water at $90\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ and $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water at $10\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ are mixed ($c = 4186\,\mathrm{J/(kg\cdot K)}$). Compute the final temperature and the change in entropy of the universe.

:::soluzione
$T_e = 323\,\mathrm{K} = 50\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ and $\Delta S_{\text{u}} = 4186 \left( \ln\dfrac{323}{363} + \ln\dfrac{323}{283} \right)\mathrm{J/K} \approx 65\,\mathrm{J/K}$.
:::
