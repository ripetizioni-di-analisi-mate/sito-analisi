$3.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water is heated from $10\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ to $90\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ ($c = 4186\,\mathrm{J/(kg\cdot K)}$). Compute the change in entropy of the water.

:::soluzione
$\Delta S = 3.0 \cdot 4186 \cdot \ln\dfrac{363}{283}\,\mathrm{J/K} \approx 3.13 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{J/K}$.
:::
