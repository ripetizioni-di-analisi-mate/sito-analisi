titolo: Entropy of melting
teoria: [entropia-di-corpi-e-serbatoi]
parole: [entropy, melting, latent heat]
---

Compute the change in entropy of $0.20\,\mathrm{kg}$ of ice that melts at $0\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ ($\lambda_f = 3.33 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{J/kg}$).

:::soluzione
$\Delta S = \dfrac{0.20 \cdot 3.33 \cdot 10^5}{273}\,\mathrm{J/K} \approx 244\,\mathrm{J/K}$.
:::
