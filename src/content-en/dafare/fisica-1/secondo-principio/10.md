Two moles of ideal gas expand isothermally and reversibly at $350\,\mathrm{K}$ from $10\,\mathrm{L}$ to $25\,\mathrm{L}$. Compute the change in entropy of the gas and the heat absorbed.

:::soluzione
$\Delta S = 2 \cdot 8.314 \cdot \ln 2.5\,\mathrm{J/K} \approx 15.2\,\mathrm{J/K}$ and $Q = T\,\Delta S \approx 5.33\,\mathrm{kJ}$.
:::
