A heat of $5.0\,\mathrm{kJ}$ passes by conduction from a reservoir at $600\,\mathrm{K}$ to a reservoir at $300\,\mathrm{K}$. Compute the change in entropy of the universe.

:::soluzione
$\Delta S_{\text{u}} = -8.33\,\mathrm{J/K} + 16.7\,\mathrm{J/K} \approx 8.3\,\mathrm{J/K}$.
:::
