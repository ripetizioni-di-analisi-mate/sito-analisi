A Carnot engine operates between $450\,\mathrm{K}$ and $300\,\mathrm{K}$ and absorbs $900\,\mathrm{J}$ of heat per cycle. Compute the efficiency and the work produced in one cycle.

:::soluzione
$\eta_C = \dfrac{1}{3} \approx 0{,}333$ and $L = 300\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
