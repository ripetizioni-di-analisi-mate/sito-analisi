---
titolo: Efficiency of a rectangular cycle
teoria: [rendimento, macchina-termica]
parole: [cycle, isochoric, isobaric, efficiency, ideal gas]
---

A monatomic ideal gas goes clockwise through a rectangular cycle in the $(V, p)$ plane formed by two isochoric processes at $3.0\,\mathrm{L}$ and $6.0\,\mathrm{L}$ and by two isobaric processes at $1.0 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$ and $2.0 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Compute the work per cycle and the efficiency.

:::soluzione
$L = 300\,\mathrm{J}$, $Q_{\text{in}} = 450\,\mathrm{J} + 1500\,\mathrm{J} = 1950\,\mathrm{J}$, therefore $\eta = \dfrac{2}{13} \approx 0.154$.
:::
