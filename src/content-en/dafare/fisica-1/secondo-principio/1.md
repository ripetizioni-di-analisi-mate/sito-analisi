A heat engine has efficiency $0{,}35$ and produces $700\,\mathrm{J}$ of work in each cycle. Compute the heat absorbed and the heat released in one cycle.

:::soluzione
$Q_{\text{ass}} = 2{,}0\,\mathrm{kJ}$ and $Q_{\text{ced}} = 1{,}3\,\mathrm{kJ}$.
:::
