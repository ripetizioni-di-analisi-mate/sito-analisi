---
titolo: Heats of a heat engine
teoria: [rendimento, macchina-termica]
parole: [efficiency, heat absorbed, heat released]
---

A heat engine has efficiency $0.35$ and produces $700\,\mathrm{J}$ of work in each cycle. Compute the heat absorbed and the heat released in one cycle.

:::soluzione
$Q_{\text{in}} = 2.0\,\mathrm{kJ}$ and $Q_{\text{out}} = 1.3\,\mathrm{kJ}$.
:::
