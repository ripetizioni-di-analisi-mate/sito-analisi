In a rigid, insulated container $0.50\,\mathrm{mol}$ of ideal gas expands freely into vacuum until it occupies twice the initial volume. Compute the change in entropy of the universe.

:::soluzione
$\Delta S_{\text{u}} = 0.50 \cdot 8.314 \cdot \ln 2\,\mathrm{J/K} \approx 2.88\,\mathrm{J/K}$.
:::
