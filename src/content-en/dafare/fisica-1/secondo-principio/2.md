A refrigerator with coefficient of performance $\varepsilon = 5{,}0$ extracts $2{,}5 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{J}$ of heat from its interior. Compute the work input and the heat released to the surroundings.

:::soluzione
$|L| = 5{,}0 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{J}$ and $|Q_1| = 3{,}0 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
