A refrigerator with coefficient of performance $\varepsilon = 5.0$ extracts $2.5 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{J}$ from its interior. Compute the work spent and the heat released to the surroundings.

:::soluzione
$|L| = 5.0 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{J}$ and $|Q_1| = 3.0 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
