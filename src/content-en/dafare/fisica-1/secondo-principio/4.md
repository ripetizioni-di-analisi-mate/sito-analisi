titolo: Temperature of the hot reservoir
teoria: [rendimento-di-carnot, teorema-di-carnot]
parole: [Carnot cycle, temperature, efficiency]
---

A reversible engine releases heat to a reservoir at $290\,\mathrm{K}$. Compute the temperature of the hot reservoir needed to obtain an efficiency of $0.42$.

:::soluzione
$T_1 = \dfrac{290\,\mathrm{K}}{0.58} = 500\,\mathrm{K}$.
:::
