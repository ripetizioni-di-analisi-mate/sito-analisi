A manufacturer claims that a freezer has a coefficient of performance $9{,}0$ while keeping its interior at $255\,\mathrm{K}$ in a room at $295\,\mathrm{K}$. Determine whether the claim is compatible with the second law.

:::soluzione
The claim is incompatible with the second law, since the maximum coefficient of performance is $\varepsilon_C = \dfrac{255}{40} \approx 6{,}4 < 9{,}0$.
:::
