A manufacturer claims a freezer with coefficient of performance $9.0$ that keeps its interior at $255\,\mathrm{K}$ in a room at $295\,\mathrm{K}$. Determine whether the claim is compatible with the second law.

:::soluzione
The claim is not compatible, because the maximum coefficient of performance is $\varepsilon_C = \dfrac{255}{40} \approx 6.4 < 9.0$.
:::
