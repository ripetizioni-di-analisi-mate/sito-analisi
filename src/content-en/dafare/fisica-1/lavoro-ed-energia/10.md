A package of mass $2{,}0\,\mathrm{kg}$ starts from rest at the top of a slide $3{,}0\,\mathrm{m}$ high and reaches the bottom with speed $6{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Compute the mechanical energy dissipated by friction.

:::soluzione
$E_{\text{diss}} = mgh - \dfrac{1}{2} m v^2 \approx 23\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
