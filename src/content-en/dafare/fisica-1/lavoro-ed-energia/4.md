---
titolo: Work of a position-dependent force
teoria: [lavoro-forza-variabile]
parole: [variable force, integral, work]
---

A body moves along the $x$-axis from $x = 0$ to $x = 4.0\,\mathrm{m}$ under the action of a force with component $F_x(x) = 2.0 + 1.5\,x$, where $x$ is in meters and $F_x$ in newtons. Compute the work done by the force.

:::soluzione
$L = \displaystyle\int_0^{4} (2.0 + 1.5\,x)\,dx\,\mathrm{J} = 20\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
