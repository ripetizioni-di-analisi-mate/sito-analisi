A simple pendulum has an inextensible string of negligible mass and length $0{,}80\,\mathrm{m}$. Compute the minimum speed the ball must have at the lowest point for the string to reach the horizontal position.

:::soluzione
$v = \sqrt{2 g \ell} \approx 4{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
