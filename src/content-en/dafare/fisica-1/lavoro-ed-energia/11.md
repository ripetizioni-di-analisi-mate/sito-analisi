A block starts from rest at the top of a plane inclined at $30^\circ$ and slides along it for $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$. The coefficient of kinetic friction is $0.30$. Compute the speed of the block at the base of the plane.

:::soluzione
$v = \sqrt{2 g d\,(\sin\alpha - \mu_d \cos\alpha)} \approx 3.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
