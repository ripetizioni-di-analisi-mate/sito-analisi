A child pulls a small cart for $5.0\,\mathrm{m}$ along a horizontal sidewalk with a rope that forms an angle of $40^\circ$ with the horizontal. The tension in the rope is constant and equal to $60\,\mathrm{N}$. Compute the work done by the tension.

:::soluzione
$L = 60 \cdot 5.0 \cdot \cos 40^\circ\,\mathrm{J} \approx 2.3 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
