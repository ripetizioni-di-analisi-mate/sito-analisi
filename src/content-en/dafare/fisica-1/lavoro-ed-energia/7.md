A ball is thrown vertically upward with speed $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ from a point located $1{,}5\,\mathrm{m}$ above the ground. Neglecting air resistance, compute the maximum height reached by the ball, measured from the ground.

:::soluzione
$h_{\max} = 1{,}5\,\mathrm{m} + \dfrac{v_0^2}{2g} \approx 8{,}8\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
