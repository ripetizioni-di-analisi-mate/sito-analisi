---
titolo: Power of a cyclist going uphill
teoria: [potenza-e-velocita, potenza]
parole: [power, constant velocity, slope]
---

A cyclist, with a total mass including the bicycle of $80\,\mathrm{kg}$, climbs at constant speed $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ along a road inclined at $6.0^\circ$. Neglecting friction and air resistance, compute the power the cyclist must develop.

:::soluzione
$P = m g \sin\alpha\, v \approx 3.3 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{W}$.
:::
