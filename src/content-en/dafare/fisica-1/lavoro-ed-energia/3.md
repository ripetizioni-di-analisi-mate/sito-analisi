A spring with spring constant $k = 250\,\mathrm{N/m}$ is stretched from $4.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ to $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ beyond its rest length. Compute the work done by the elastic force during the stretching.

:::soluzione
$L = \dfrac{1}{2} k\,(x_A^2 - x_B^2) = -1.6\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
