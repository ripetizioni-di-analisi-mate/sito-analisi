A body of mass $800\,\mathrm{g}$ goes from a speed of $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to a speed of $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Compute the total work done by the forces acting on it.

:::soluzione
$L_{\text{tot}} = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 0.800 \cdot (6.0^2 - 2.0^2)\,\mathrm{J} = 12.8\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
