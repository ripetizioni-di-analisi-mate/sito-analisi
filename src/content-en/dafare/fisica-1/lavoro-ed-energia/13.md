A particle moves along the $x$-axis subject only to a conservative force with potential energy $U(x) = 3x^2 - x^3$, where $x$ is in meters and $U$ in joules. Determine the equilibrium positions and their stability, and determine for which values of the mechanical energy a particle located near $x = 0$ stays confined to a bounded interval.

:::soluzione
$x = 0$ stable equilibrium, $x = 2\,\mathrm{m}$ unstable equilibrium; the motion near $x = 0$ is confined for $0 \leq E < 4\,\mathrm{J}$, that is, for energies below that of the maximum $U(2) = 4\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
