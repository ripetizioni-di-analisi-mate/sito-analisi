A block of mass $0.25\,\mathrm{kg}$ rests against a spring of constant $k = 400\,\mathrm{N/m}$ compressed by $5.0\,\mathrm{cm}$, on a smooth horizontal plane. Once the spring is released, the block moves up a smooth ramp. Compute the speed of the block after it leaves the spring and the maximum height it reaches on the ramp.

:::soluzione
$v = x\sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m}} = 2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$, $h = \dfrac{k x^2}{2 m g} \approx 0.20\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
