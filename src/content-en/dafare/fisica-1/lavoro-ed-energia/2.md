titolo: Work of the weight on a smooth inclined plane

---

A block of mass $5.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ starts from rest and slides for $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ down a smooth plane inclined at $30^\circ$. Compute the work done by the weight, the work done by the normal force and the final speed of the block.

:::soluzione
$L_P = mg\,d\sin\alpha \approx 49\,\mathrm{J}$, $L_N = 0$, $v = \sqrt{2 g\, d \sin\alpha} \approx 4.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
