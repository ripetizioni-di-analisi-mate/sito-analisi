A car travels at $90\,\mathrm{km/h}$ on a horizontal road and brakes with locked wheels. The coefficient of kinetic friction between tires and asphalt is $0{,}70$. Compute the braking distance.

:::soluzione
$d = \dfrac{v_0^2}{2 \mu_d\, g} \approx 46\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
