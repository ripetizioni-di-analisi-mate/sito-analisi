A baseball of mass $0.15\,\mathrm{kg}$ reaches the bat horizontally with speed $35\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and is hit back along the same direction, in the opposite sense, at $45\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The contact lasts $1.5\,\mathrm{ms}$. Compute the magnitude of the impulse received by the ball and the average force exerted by the bat, neglecting the weight during the contact.

:::soluzione
$J = 0.15 \cdot 80\,\mathrm{N\cdot s} = 12\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$ and $F_m = \dfrac{12\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}}{1.5 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{s}} = 8.0 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
