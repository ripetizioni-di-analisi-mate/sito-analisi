A ball is dropped from rest from a height of $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ above a rigid floor and, after the bounce, rises back to $1.28\,\mathrm{m}$. Neglecting air, compute the coefficient of restitution of the collision and the fraction of kinetic energy dissipated in the bounce.

:::soluzione
$e = \sqrt{\dfrac{1.28}{2.0}} = 0.80$; the fraction dissipated is $1 - e^2 = 0.36$, that is $36\,\%$.
:::
