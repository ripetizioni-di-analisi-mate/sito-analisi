---
titolo: Persona che cammina su una zattera
teoria: [conservazione-quantita-di-moto, moto-del-centro-di-massa, centro-di-massa]
parole: [zattera, centro di massa fermo, sistema isolato]
---

A person of mass $60\,\mathrm{kg}$ stands still on a raft of mass $240\,\mathrm{kg}$, at rest in calm water. The person walks in a straight line for $5{,}0\,\mathrm{m}$ relative to the raft and stops. Neglecting the horizontal resistance of the water, compute the displacement of the raft and that of the person relative to the water.

:::soluzione
The raft moves by $\dfrac{60 \cdot 5{,}0}{300}\,\mathrm{m} = 1{,}0\,\mathrm{m}$ in the direction opposite to the person, who moves by $4{,}0\,\mathrm{m}$ relative to the water.
:::
