A railcar of mass $3.0 \cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{kg}$, moving at $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ on a horizontal track, collides with a railcar at rest of mass $2.0 \cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the two stay coupled. Neglecting friction, compute the velocity after coupling and the fraction of kinetic energy dissipated.

:::soluzione
$V = 1.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$; the fraction dissipated is $\dfrac{m_2}{m_1 + m_2} = 0.40$, that is $40\,\%$ of the initial kinetic energy.
:::
