---
titolo: Impulse of a time-varying force
teoria: [impulso, teorema-dell-impulso]
parole: [impulse, variable force, integral]
---

A body of mass $4.0\,\mathrm{kg}$, initially at rest on a smooth horizontal plane, is pushed by a horizontal force of fixed direction and magnitude $F(t) = 6.0\,t$, with $F$ in newtons and $t$ in seconds, for $0 \leq t \leq 2.0\,\mathrm{s}$. Compute the impulse of the force and the velocity of the body at time $t = 2.0\,\mathrm{s}$.

:::soluzione
$J = \displaystyle\int_0^{2.0} 6.0\,t\,dt\ \mathrm{N\cdot s} = 12\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$ and $v = \dfrac{J}{m} = 3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
