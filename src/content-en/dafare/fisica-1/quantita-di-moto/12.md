Two bodies of masses $4.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ move along the same line with velocities $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $-3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Compute the velocity of the center of mass, the kinetic energy in the center-of-mass frame and the maximum energy that a collision between the two bodies can dissipate.

:::soluzione
$v_{cm} = 1.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$, $K = 12.5\,\mathrm{J}$ and $K^{*} = 12.5\,\mathrm{J} - 2.5\,\mathrm{J} = 10\,\mathrm{J}$; the maximum energy that can be dissipated is $10\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
