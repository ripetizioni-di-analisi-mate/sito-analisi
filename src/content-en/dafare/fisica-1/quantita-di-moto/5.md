teoria: [conservazione-quantita-di-moto, conservazione-per-componenti]

A rifle of mass $3.5\,\mathrm{kg}$, free to move, fires horizontally a bullet of mass $7.0\,\mathrm{g}$ that leaves with velocity $500\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Compute the recoil velocity of the rifle.

:::soluzione
$V = -\dfrac{0.0070 \cdot 500}{3.5}\,\mathrm{m/s} = -1.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$, that is $1.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the sense opposite to the bullet.
:::
