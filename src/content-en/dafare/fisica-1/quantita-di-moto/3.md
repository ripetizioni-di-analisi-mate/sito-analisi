Three particles of masses $m_1 = 2{,}0\,\mathrm{kg}$, $m_2 = 3{,}0\,\mathrm{kg}$, $m_3 = 5{,}0\,\mathrm{kg}$ are located at $(1{,}0;\ 0)$, $(0;\ 2{,}0)$ and $(4{,}0;\ 4{,}0)$ respectively, with coordinates in meters. Determine the center of mass of the system.

:::soluzione
$x_{cm} = \dfrac{2 + 0 + 20}{10}\,\mathrm{m} = 2{,}2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $y_{cm} = \dfrac{0 + 6 + 20}{10}\,\mathrm{m} = 2{,}6\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
