---
titolo: Height reached by a ballistic pendulum
teoria: [pendolo-balistico, urto-completamente-anelastico]
parole: [ballistic pendulum, bullet, height]
---

A bullet of mass $5.0\,\mathrm{g}$, with horizontal velocity $400\,\mathrm{m/s}$, embeds itself in a block of mass $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ hanging at rest from inextensible strings of negligible mass. Neglecting air, compute the velocity of the block immediately after the collision and the maximum height to which it rises.

:::soluzione
$V = \dfrac{0.0050 \cdot 400}{1.005}\,\mathrm{m/s} \approx 1.99\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $h = \dfrac{V^2}{2g} \approx 0.20\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
