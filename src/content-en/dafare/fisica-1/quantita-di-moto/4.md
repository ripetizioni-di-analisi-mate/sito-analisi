---
titolo: Center of mass of a rod with a sphere
teoria: [centro-di-massa-corpi-composti, centro-di-massa-corpi-continui]

---

A uniform rod of length $1.2\,\mathrm{m}$ and mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ carries, fixed at one end, a small sphere of mass $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ and negligible size. Determine the distance of the center of mass of the system from the free end of the rod.

:::soluzione
$d = \dfrac{2.0 \cdot 0.60 + 1.0 \cdot 1.2}{3.0}\,\mathrm{m} = 0.80\,\mathrm{m}$ from the free end.
:::
