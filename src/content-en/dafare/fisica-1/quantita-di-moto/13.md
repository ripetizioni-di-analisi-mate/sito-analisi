A rocket in space, in the absence of external forces, expels gas with velocity $3.0\,\mathrm{km/s}$ relative to the rocket. Compute the ratio between initial mass and final mass needed to go from rest to $6.0\,\mathrm{km/s}$ and the fraction of the initial mass made up of fuel.

:::soluzione
$\dfrac{M_i}{M_f} = e^{2} \approx 7.4$; the fuel is the fraction $1 - e^{-2} \approx 0.86$ of the initial mass.
:::
