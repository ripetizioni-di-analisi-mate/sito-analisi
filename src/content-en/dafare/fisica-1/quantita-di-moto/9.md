---
titolo: Velocities after an elastic collision
teoria: [urto-elastico, casi-particolari-urto-elastico]
parole: [elastic collision, final velocities]
---

A cart of mass $3.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ with velocity $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ collides elastically, along a straight frictionless horizontal track, with a cart at rest of mass $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$. Compute the velocities of the two carts after the collision.

:::soluzione
$v_1' = \dfrac{3.0 - 1.0}{4.0} \cdot 4.0\,\mathrm{m/s} = 2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_2' = \dfrac{2 \cdot 3.0}{4.0} \cdot 4.0\,\mathrm{m/s} = 6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$, both in the initial direction.
:::
