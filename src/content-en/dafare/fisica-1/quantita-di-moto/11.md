On a smooth horizontal plane a puck of mass $0.20\,\mathrm{kg}$ with velocity $3.0\,\hat{\imath}\,\mathrm{m/s}$ collides with a puck of mass $0.30\,\mathrm{kg}$ with velocity $2.0\,\hat{\jmath}\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and the two stick together. Compute the magnitude and direction of the common velocity after the collision.

:::soluzione
$\vec{V} = (1.2\,\hat{\imath} + 1.2\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m/s}$, of magnitude $1.2\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{m/s} \approx 1.7\,\mathrm{m/s}$, at $45^\circ$ from the $x$ axis.
:::
