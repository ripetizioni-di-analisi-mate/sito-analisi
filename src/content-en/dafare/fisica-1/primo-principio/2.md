---
titolo: Elongation of a steel bridge
teoria: [dilatazione-lineare]
parole: [linear expansion, expansion joint, steel]
---

A bridge has a steel structure ($\alpha = 1.2 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$) $200\,\mathrm{m}$ long at a temperature of $-10\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. Compute the elongation of the structure when the temperature rises to $40\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$.

:::soluzione
$\Delta L = 0.12\,\mathrm{m} = 12\,\mathrm{cm}$.
:::
