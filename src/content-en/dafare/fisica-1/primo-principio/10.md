$3.00\,\mathrm{mol}$ of diatomic ideal gas are heated at constant pressure and their temperature increases by $50.0\,\mathrm{K}$. Compute the heat absorbed, the work done by the gas and the change in internal energy.

:::soluzione
$Q \approx 4.36 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{J}$, $L \approx 1.25 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{J}$, $\Delta U \approx 3.12 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
