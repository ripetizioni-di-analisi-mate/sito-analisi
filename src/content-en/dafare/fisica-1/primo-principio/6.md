titolo: Ice to cool a drink

We want to cool $0.30\,\mathrm{kg}$ of a drink, which can be treated as water, from $25\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ to $5.0\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ by adding ice at $0\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. Neglecting exchanges with the outside, compute the mass of ice needed ($c_a = 4186\,\mathrm{J/(kg\cdot K)}$, $\lambda_f = 3.33 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{J/kg}$).

:::soluzione
$m_g = \dfrac{m\,c_a\,(25 - 5.0)}{\lambda_f + c_a \cdot 5.0} \approx 0.071\,\mathrm{kg}$, that is, about $71\,\mathrm{g}$.
:::
