---
titolo: Espansione adiabatica di un gas monoatomico
teoria: [trasformazione-adiabatica]
parole: [adiabatica, espansione, temperatura finale]
---

A monatomic ideal gas at a temperature of $400\,\mathrm{K}$ expands adiabatically and quasi-statically until its volume doubles. Compute the final temperature and the ratio of the final pressure to the initial one.

:::soluzione
$T_f = 400 \cdot 2^{-\frac{2}{3}}\,\mathrm{K} \approx 252\,\mathrm{K}$ and $\dfrac{p_f}{p_i} = 2^{-\frac{5}{3}} \approx 0{,}315$.
:::
