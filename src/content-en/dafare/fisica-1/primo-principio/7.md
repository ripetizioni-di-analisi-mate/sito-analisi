Compute the heat needed to bring to the boil and completely evaporate $0.500\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water initially at $20\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$, at atmospheric pressure ($c_a = 4186\,\mathrm{J/(kg\cdot K)}$, $\lambda_v = 2.26 \cdot 10^6\,\mathrm{J/kg}$).

:::soluzione
$Q \approx 1.30 \cdot 10^6\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
