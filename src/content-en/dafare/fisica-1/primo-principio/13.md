---
titolo: Work of a triangular cycle
teoria: [lavoro-di-un-ciclo, ciclo-termodinamico]
parole: [cycle, work as area, Clapeyron plane]
---

An ideal gas goes through the triangular cycle $A \to C \to B \to A$ with $A$ ($1.00\,\mathrm{L}$; $1.00 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$), $C$ ($1.00\,\mathrm{L}$; $3.00 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$) and $B$ ($3.00\,\mathrm{L}$; $1.00 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$), where each side is traversed quasi-statically along a segment of the $(V, p)$ plane. Compute the total work and the total heat exchanged in the cycle.

:::soluzione
The cycle is traversed clockwise, therefore $L = Q_{\text{tot}} = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 2.00 \cdot 10^{-3} \cdot 2.00 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{J} = 200\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
