titolo: Equal temperature in degrees Celsius and Fahrenheit
teoria: [scale-termometriche]

---

Determine the temperature at which a thermometer in degrees Celsius and one in degrees Fahrenheit read the same number, and express it in kelvins.

:::soluzione
$t_C = t_F = -40$, that is, $-40\,^\circ\mathrm{C} = -40\,^\circ\mathrm{F}$, which correspond to $T = 233.15\,\mathrm{K}$.
:::
