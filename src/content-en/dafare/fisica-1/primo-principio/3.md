A tank contains $50{,}0\,\mathrm{L}$ of gasoline ($\beta = 9{,}5 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$) at a temperature of $10\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. Neglecting the expansion of the tank, compute the increase in volume of the gasoline when the temperature rises to $30\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$.

:::soluzione
$\Delta V \approx 0{,}95\,\mathrm{L}$.
:::
