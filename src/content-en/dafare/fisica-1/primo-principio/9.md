A room measures $5.0\,\mathrm{m} \times 4.0\,\mathrm{m} \times 3.0\,\mathrm{m}$ and contains air at $1.013 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$ and $20\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. Treating air as an ideal gas with molar mass $29\,\mathrm{g/mol}$, compute the number of moles and the mass of the air.

:::soluzione
$n \approx 2.49 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{mol}$ and $m \approx 72\,\mathrm{kg}$.
:::
