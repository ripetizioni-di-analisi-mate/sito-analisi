A piece of iron of mass $0.25\,\mathrm{kg}$ at a temperature of $200\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ is immersed in $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water at $20\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$, in an insulated container of negligible heat capacity. Compute the equilibrium temperature ($c_{\mathrm{Fe}} = 450\,\mathrm{J/(kg\cdot K)}$, $c_a = 4186\,\mathrm{J/(kg\cdot K)}$).

:::soluzione
$T_e \approx 24.7\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$.
:::
