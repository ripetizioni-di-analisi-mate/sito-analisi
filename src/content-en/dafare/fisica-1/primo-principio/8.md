The glass of a window has area $1{,}5\,\mathrm{m^2}$, thickness $4{,}0\,\mathrm{mm}$ and thermal conductivity $k = 0{,}80\,\mathrm{W/(m\cdot K)}$. Its two faces are at temperatures that differ by $2{,}0\,\mathrm{K}$. Compute the power lost by conduction.

:::soluzione
$P = 600\,\mathrm{W}$.
:::
