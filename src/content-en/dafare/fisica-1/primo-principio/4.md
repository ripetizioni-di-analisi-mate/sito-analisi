An electric kettle with a power of $2000\,\mathrm{W}$ heats $1.5\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water from $15\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ to $100\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. Assuming that all the electrical energy is absorbed by the water, compute the heat required and the time taken ($c_a = 4186\,\mathrm{J/(kg\cdot K)}$).

:::soluzione
$Q \approx 5.34 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{J}$ and $\Delta t \approx 267\,\mathrm{s}$, that is, about $4.4$ minutes.
:::
