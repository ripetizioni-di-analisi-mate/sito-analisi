titolo: Isothermal compression
teoria: [trasformazione-isoterma]
parole: [isothermal, compression, work]

$1.00\,\mathrm{mol}$ of ideal gas at a temperature of $27.0\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ is compressed quasi-statically and isothermally from a volume of $20.0\,\mathrm{L}$ to a volume of $10.0\,\mathrm{L}$. Compute the work done by the gas and the heat exchanged.

:::soluzione
$L = Q = nRT \ln\dfrac{1}{2} \approx -1.73 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{J}$; the gas releases about $1.73\,\mathrm{kJ}$ of heat.
:::
