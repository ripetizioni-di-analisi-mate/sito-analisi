---
titolo: Ring hanging from a nail
teoria: [pendolo-fisico]
parole: [physical pendulum, ring, parallel-axis theorem, period]
---

A thin uniform ring of mass $m$ and radius $R = 0.25\,\mathrm{m}$ hangs from a horizontal nail that passes through a point on its rim and is perpendicular to the plane of the ring. Given that the moment of inertia of the ring about the perpendicular axis through its center is $mR^2$, determine the period of small oscillations in the plane of the ring.

:::soluzione
$I = 2mR^2$ and $d = R$, so $T = 2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{2R}{g}} = 1.42\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
