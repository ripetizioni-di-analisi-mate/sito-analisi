titolo: Asta sospesa a un estremo
teoria: [pendolo-fisico]

A uniform rigid rod of mass $m$ and length $L = 1{,}50\,\mathrm{m}$ is suspended by one end from a frictionless horizontal pivot. Given that its moment of inertia about the perpendicular axis through its center is $\dfrac{1}{12}mL^2$, determine the period of small oscillations.

:::soluzione
$T = 2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{2L}{3g}} = 2{,}01\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
