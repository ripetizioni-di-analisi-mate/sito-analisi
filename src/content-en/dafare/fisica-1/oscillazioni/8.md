titolo: Block against a spring
teoria: [energia-oscillatore, massa-molla]

A block of mass $1.2\,\mathrm{kg}$ slides on a smooth horizontal plane with speed $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and hits an ideal spring of constant $300\,\mathrm{N/m}$ fixed to a wall. Determine the maximum compression of the spring and the duration of the contact.

:::soluzione
Maximum compression $A = v_0\sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k}} = 0.0949\,\mathrm{m}$; duration of the contact $\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k}} = 0.199\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
