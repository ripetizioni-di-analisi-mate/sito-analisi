A body of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ is attached to an ideal spring of constant $128\,\mathrm{N/m}$. Determine the coefficient $b$ of the viscous force for which the damping is critical.

:::soluzione
$b_c = 2\sqrt{km} = 32\,\mathrm{kg/s}$.
:::
