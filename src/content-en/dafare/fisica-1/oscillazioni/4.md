---
titolo: Period of a vertical spring
teoria: [molla-verticale]
parole: [vertical spring, static extension, period]
---

A body hanging from an ideal vertical spring stretches it, at equilibrium, by $2.5\,\mathrm{cm}$. Determine the period of the small vertical oscillations of the body about the equilibrium position.

:::soluzione
$T = 2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{\Delta\ell}{g}} = 0.317\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
