---
titolo: Costante elastica dal periodo
teoria: [massa-molla]
parole: [massa-molla, costante elastica, periodo]
---

A body of mass $0{,}80\,\mathrm{kg}$, attached to an ideal spring and on a smooth horizontal plane, oscillates with period $0{,}40\,\mathrm{s}$. Determine the spring constant.

:::soluzione
$k = \dfrac{4\pi^2 m}{T^2} = 197\,\mathrm{N/m}$.
:::
