---
titolo: Measuring g with a pendulum
teoria: [periodo-del-pendolo]
parole: [simple pendulum, measurement of g, period]
---

A simple pendulum $0.800\,\mathrm{m}$ long performs small oscillations with a measured period of $1.80\,\mathrm{s}$. Determine the value of the gravitational acceleration at the place of the measurement.

:::soluzione
$g = \dfrac{4\pi^2 \ell}{T_0^2} = 9.75\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
:::
