A particle in harmonic motion with angular frequency $\omega = 4.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ is at the instant $t = 0$ at the position $x_0 = 0.060\,\mathrm{m}$ with velocity $v_0 = -0.32\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Write the equation of motion in the form $A\cos(\omega t + \varphi)$.

:::soluzione
$A = 0.10\,\mathrm{m}$ and $\varphi = 0.927\,\mathrm{rad}$ (with $\cos\varphi = 0.60$ and $\sin\varphi = 0.80$), so $x(t) = 0.10\cos(4.0\,t + 0.927)\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
