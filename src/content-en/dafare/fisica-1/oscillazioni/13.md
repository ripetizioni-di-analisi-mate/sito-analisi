---
titolo: Resonant angular frequency and amplitude
teoria: [risonanza, regime-stazionario, oscillatore-forzato]
parole: [resonance, resonant angular frequency, maximum amplitude]
---

A body of mass $0.25\,\mathrm{kg}$, attached to an ideal spring of constant $100\,\mathrm{N/m}$ and subject to a viscous force with $b = 0.50\,\mathrm{kg/s}$, is driven by a force $F_0\cos(\Omega t)$ with $F_0 = 1.0\,\mathrm{N}$. Determine the resonant angular frequency and the maximum amplitude of the steady state.

:::soluzione
$\Omega_r = \sqrt{\omega_0^2 - 2\gamma^2} = \sqrt{398}\,\mathrm{rad/s} = 19.9\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ and $A_{\max} = \dfrac{F_0}{2m\gamma\sqrt{\omega_0^2 - \gamma^2}} = 0.100\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
