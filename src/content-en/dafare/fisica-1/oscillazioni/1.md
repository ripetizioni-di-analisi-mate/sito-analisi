A particle moves along the $x$-axis with equation of motion $x(t) = 0.080\cos(10\pi t)$, with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. Determine the period, frequency, maximum velocity and maximum acceleration.

:::soluzione
$T = 0.20\,\mathrm{s}$, $f = 5.0\,\mathrm{Hz}$, $v_{\max} = 0.80\pi\,\mathrm{m/s} = 2.51\,\mathrm{m/s}$, $a_{\max} = 8.0\pi^2\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 79.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
:::
