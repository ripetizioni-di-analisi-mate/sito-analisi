A body of mass $0.10\,\mathrm{kg}$ oscillates on a smooth horizontal plane attached to an ideal spring of constant $250\,\mathrm{N/m}$, with amplitude $4.0\,\mathrm{cm}$. Determine the mechanical energy and the magnitude of the velocity at the position $x = 2.0\,\mathrm{cm}$.

:::soluzione
$E = \dfrac{1}{2}kA^2 = 0.20\,\mathrm{J}$ and $|v| = \omega\sqrt{A^2 - x^2} = 50 \cdot 0.0346\,\mathrm{m/s} = 1.73\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
