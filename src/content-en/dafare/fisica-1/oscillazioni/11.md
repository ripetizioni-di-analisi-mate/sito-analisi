In a weakly damped oscillator the amplitude is halved in $4{,}0\,\mathrm{s}$. Determine the damping constant $\gamma$ and the fraction of the initial energy that remains after $4{,}0\,\mathrm{s}$.

:::soluzione
$\gamma = \dfrac{\ln 2}{4{,}0\,\mathrm{s}} = 0{,}173\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$; one quarter of the initial energy remains.
:::
