---
titolo: Prize in a fair game
teoria: [gioco-equo, valore-medio]
parole: [fair game, net gain, mean, cards]
---

Draw a card from a deck of $40$ cards containing $4$ aces. If the card is an ace, you win $v$ euros, otherwise you lose $3$ euros. Find $v$ so that the game is fair.

:::soluzione
$\dfrac{1}{10}\,v - \dfrac{9}{10} \cdot 3 = 0$, so $v = 27$ euros.
:::
