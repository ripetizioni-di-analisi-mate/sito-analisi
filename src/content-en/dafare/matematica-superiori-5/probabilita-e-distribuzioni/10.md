---
titolo: Binomial distribution for children in a family
teoria: [distribuzione-binomiale]
parole: [binomial distribution, repeated trials, at least three]
---

In a family with $4$ children, each child is a boy with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$, independently of the others. Calculate the probability that at least $3$ children are boys.

:::soluzione
$\dfrac{\dbinom{4}{3} + \dbinom{4}{4}}{2^4} = \dfrac{5}{16}$.
:::
