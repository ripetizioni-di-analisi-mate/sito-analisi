The waiting time at a service counter, in minutes, has an exponential distribution with a mean of $5$ minutes. Calculate the probability of waiting more than $10$ minutes.

:::soluzione
$\lambda = \dfrac{1}{5}$ and $P(X > 10) = e^{-2} \approx 0.135$.
:::
