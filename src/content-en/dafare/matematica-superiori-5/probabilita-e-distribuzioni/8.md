A random variable $X$ takes the values $-1$, $0$, $2$, and $5$ with probabilities $0.2$, $0.3$, $0.4$, and $0.1$. Calculate the mean, variance, and standard deviation of $X$.

:::soluzione
$M(X) = 1.1$, $\operatorname{var}(X) = 4.3 - 1.21 = 3.09$, $\sigma \approx 1.76$.
:::
