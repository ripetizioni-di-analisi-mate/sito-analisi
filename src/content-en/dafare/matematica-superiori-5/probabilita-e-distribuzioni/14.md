The heights of the students at a school are normally distributed with $\mu = 175\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $\sigma = 6\,\mathrm{cm}$. Calculate the probability that a randomly chosen student is taller than $184\,\mathrm{cm}$, given that $\Phi(1.5) = 0.9332$.

:::soluzione
$P(X > 184) = 1 - \Phi(1.5) = 0.0668$.
:::
