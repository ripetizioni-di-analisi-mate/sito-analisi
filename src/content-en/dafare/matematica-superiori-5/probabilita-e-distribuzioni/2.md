titolo: Drawing balls together from an urn
teoria: [probabilita-classica, combinazioni]
parole: [combinations, urn, simultaneous draw]

An urn contains $10$ balls numbered from $1$ to $10$. Draw $3$ balls together. Calculate the probability that all three numbers drawn are even.

:::soluzione
$\dfrac{\dbinom{5}{3}}{\dbinom{10}{3}} = \dfrac{10}{120} = \dfrac{1}{12}$.
:::
