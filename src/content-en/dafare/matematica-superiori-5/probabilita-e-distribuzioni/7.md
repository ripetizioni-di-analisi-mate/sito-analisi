A disease affects $2\%$ of the population. A test gives a positive result in $95\%$ of people with the disease and $10\%$ of those without it. Calculate the probability that a person who tests positive has the disease.

:::soluzione
$P(+) = 0.117$ and $P(M \mid +) = \dfrac{0.019}{0.117} = \dfrac{19}{117} \approx 0.162$.
:::
