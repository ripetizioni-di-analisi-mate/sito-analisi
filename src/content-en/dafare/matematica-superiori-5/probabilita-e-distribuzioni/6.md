Draw two cards, one after the other, from a deck of $40$ cards containing $4$ aces, without replacing the first card. Calculate the probability that the second card is an ace given that the first is an ace, and the probability that both cards are aces.

:::soluzione
$P(A_2 \mid A_1) = \dfrac{3}{39} = \dfrac{1}{13}$ and $P(A_1 \cap A_2) = \dfrac{4}{40} \cdot \dfrac{3}{39} = \dfrac{1}{130}$.
:::
