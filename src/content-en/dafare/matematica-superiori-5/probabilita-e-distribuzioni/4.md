Draw a card from a deck of $40$ cards with $4$ suits of $10$ cards each. Each suit has $3$ face cards: a jack, a knight, and a king. Calculate the probability that the card drawn is a face card or a card of the suit of cups.

:::soluzione
$\dfrac{12}{40} + \dfrac{10}{40} - \dfrac{3}{40} = \dfrac{19}{40}$.
:::
