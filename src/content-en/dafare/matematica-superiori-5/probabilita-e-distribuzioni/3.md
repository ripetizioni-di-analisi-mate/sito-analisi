In a group of $5$ people, each person was born in one of the $12$ months of the year, and the months are equally likely. Calculate the probability that at least two people were born in the same month.

:::soluzione
$1 - \dfrac{12 \cdot 11 \cdot 10 \cdot 9 \cdot 8}{12^5} = 1 - \dfrac{55}{144} = \dfrac{89}{144} \approx 0.618$.
:::
