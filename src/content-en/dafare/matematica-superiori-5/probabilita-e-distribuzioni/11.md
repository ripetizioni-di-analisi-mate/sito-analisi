A book has an average of $0.5$ printing errors per page, and the number of errors on a page has a Poisson distribution. Calculate the probability that a page contains no errors and the probability that it contains at least $2$ errors.

:::soluzione
$P(X = 0) = e^{-0.5} \approx 0.607$ and $P(X \geq 2) = 1 - 1.5\,e^{-0.5} \approx 0.090$.
:::
