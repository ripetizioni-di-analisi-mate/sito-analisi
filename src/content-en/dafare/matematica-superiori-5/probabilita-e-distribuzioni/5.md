Three light bulbs fail independently of one another with probabilities $0.1$, $0.2$, and $0.3$. Calculate the probability that at least one bulb fails.

:::soluzione
$1 - 0.9 \cdot 0.8 \cdot 0.7 = 1 - 0.504 = 0.496$.
:::
