---
titolo: Skew lines
teoria: [posizione-due-rette]
parole: [skew lines, relative positions of two lines]
---

Determine the relative positions of the lines
$$
r : \ x = t, \ \ y = 1, \ \ z = 2t
\qquad \text{and} \qquad
s : \ x = 1, \ \ y = s, \ \ z = 3 + s .
$$

:::soluzione
The lines are skew because the direction vectors $(1, 0, 2)$ and $(0, 1, 1)$ are not parallel and the system of coordinate equations has no solutions.
:::
