titolo: Distance between two points and their midpoint
teoria: [distanza-due-punti, punto-medio]

Given the points $A(2, -1, 4)$ and $B(-2, 1, 0)$, calculate the length of the segment $AB$ and the coordinates of its midpoint.

:::soluzione
$\overline{AB} = 6$, and the midpoint is $M(0, 0, 2)$.
:::
