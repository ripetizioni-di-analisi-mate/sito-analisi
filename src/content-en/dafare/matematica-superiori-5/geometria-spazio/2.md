Calculate the angle between the vectors $\vec{u} = (1, 1, 0)$ and $\vec{v} = (-1, 0, 1)$.

:::soluzione
$\cos \theta = -\dfrac{1}{2}$, so $\theta = \dfrac{2}{3}\pi$.
:::
