---
titolo: Circle of intersection of a plane and a sphere
teoria: [equazione-sfera, posizione-piano-sfera]
parole: [sphere, secant plane, center and radius of the circle]
---

Verify that the plane $z = 6$ intersects the sphere $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 - 2x - 4z - 20 = 0$ in a circle, and find the center and radius of the circle.

:::soluzione
The sphere has center $(1, 0, 2)$ and radius $5$. The plane is at distance $4 < 5$ from the center, and the circle has center $(1, 0, 6)$ and radius $3$.
:::
