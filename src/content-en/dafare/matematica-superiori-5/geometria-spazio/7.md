Write the parametric equations of the line
$$
\begin{cases}
x + y + z - 3 = 0 \\
x - y - 1 = 0
\end{cases}
$$

:::soluzione
For example, $x = 2 + t$, $y = 1 + t$, $z = -2t$, with direction vector $(1, 1, -2)$ and point $(2, 1, 0)$.
:::
