---
titolo: Intersection of a line and a plane
teoria: [posizione-retta-piano]
parole: [line-plane intersection, parametric equations]
---

Find the point where the line $r : x = 2 - t$, $y = 1 + 2t$, $z = 3t$ intersects the plane $x + y + z - 7 = 0$.

:::soluzione
This gives $t = 1$, so the point is $(1, 3, 3)$.
:::
