titolo: Distance from a point to a plane and reflection of the point
teoria: [distanza-punto-piano, retta-perpendicolare-piano, punto-medio]

---

Given the point $P(3, 1, 0)$ and plane $\pi : 2x - 2y + z + 5 = 0$, calculate the distance from $P$ to $\pi$, the orthogonal projection $H$ of $P$ onto $\pi$, and the reflection $P'$ of $P$ across $\pi$.

:::soluzione
$d(P, \pi) = 3$, $H(1, 3, -1)$ and $P'(-1, 5, -2)$.
:::
