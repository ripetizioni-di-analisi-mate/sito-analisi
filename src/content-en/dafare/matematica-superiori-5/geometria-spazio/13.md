Write the equation of the sphere with center $C(1, -2, 2)$ tangent to the plane $x + 2y - 2z - 2 = 0$ and find the point of tangency.

:::soluzione
$x^2 + y^2 + z^2 - 2x + 4y - 4z = 0$, with radius $3$. The point of tangency is $T(2, 0, 0)$.
:::
