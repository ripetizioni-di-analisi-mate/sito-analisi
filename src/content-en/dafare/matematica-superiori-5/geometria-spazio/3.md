Calculate the area of the triangle with vertices $A(1, 0, 0)$, $B(0, 2, 0)$ and $C(0, 0, 2)$.

:::soluzione
$\overrightarrow{AB} \times \overrightarrow{AC} = (4, 2, 2)$, so the area is $\sqrt{6}$.
:::
