Determine the relative positions of the lines
$$
r : \ x = 1 + t, \ \ y = t, \ \ z = 2 - t
\qquad \text{and} \qquad
s : \ x = 1 + s, \ \ y = s, \ \ z = -1 + 2s .
$$

:::soluzione
The lines intersect at the point $(2, 1, 1)$ and are perpendicular because $(1, 1, -1) \cdot (1, 1, 2) = 0$.
:::
