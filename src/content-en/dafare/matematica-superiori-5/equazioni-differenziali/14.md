titolo: An object attached to a spring
teoria: [moto-armonico]
parole: [simple harmonic motion, spring, period, maximum velocity]
---

An object of mass $0.5\,\mathrm{kg}$ is attached to a spring with spring constant $50\,\mathrm{N/m}$ and moves without friction. At time $t = 0$ it is at rest at position $x = 0.1\,\mathrm{m}$, measured from equilibrium. Find its position as a function of time, its period, and its maximum velocity.

:::soluzione
$x(t) = 0.1 \cos 10t$, with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. The period is $\dfrac{\pi}{5} \approx 0.63\,\mathrm{s}$ and the maximum velocity is $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
