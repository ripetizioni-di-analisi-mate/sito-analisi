titolo: Growth of a bacterial colony
teoria: [crescita-popolazione]
parole: [Malthusian model, exponential growth, population]

A bacterial colony grows according to the Malthusian model $P'(t) = k\,P(t)$, with $t$ in hours. Initially there are $500$ bacteria, and their number doubles every $2$ hours. Find $k$, the function $P(t)$, and the time when there are $8000$ bacteria.

:::soluzione
$k = \dfrac{\ln 2}{2}$, $P(t) = 500 \cdot 2^{\frac{t}{2}}$. There are $8000$ bacteria after $8$ hours.
:::
