---
titolo: Homogeneous second-order equation with a parameter
teoria: [equazione-caratteristica, soluzioni-omogenea]
parole: [parameter, negative discriminant, characteristic equation]
---

Find the values of the real parameter $k$ for which the solutions of the equation $y'' + 2y' + k\,y = 0$ contain sine and cosine functions, and write the general solution for $k = 10$.

:::soluzione
The discriminant $4 - 4k$ is negative for $k > 1$. For $k = 10$ the general solution is $y = e^{-x} \left( c_1 \cos 3x + c_2 \sin 3x \right)$.
:::
