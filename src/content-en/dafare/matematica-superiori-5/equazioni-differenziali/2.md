Find the general solution of the equation $y' = 2x + \cos x$ and the solution that satisfies the condition $y(0) = 3$.

:::soluzione
The general solution is $y = x^2 + \sin x + c$. The solution of the initial value problem is $y = x^2 + \sin x + 3$.
:::
