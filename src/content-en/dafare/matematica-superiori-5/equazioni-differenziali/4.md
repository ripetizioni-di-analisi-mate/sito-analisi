titolo: A separable equation and its interval of definition
teoria: [metodo-separazione, problema-di-cauchy]
parole: [separable equations, interval of definition, initial value problem]
---

Solve the initial value problem $y' = y^2 \cos x$, $y(0) = 1$, and state the interval on which the solution is defined.

:::soluzione
$y = \dfrac{1}{1 - \sin x}$, defined on the interval $\left( -\dfrac{3\pi}{2}, \dfrac{\pi}{2} \right)$.
:::
