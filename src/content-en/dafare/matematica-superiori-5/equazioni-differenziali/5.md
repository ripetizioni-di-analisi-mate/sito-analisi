---
titolo: A separable equation with a radical
teoria: [metodo-separazione, problema-di-cauchy]
parole: [separable equations, square root, initial value problem]
---

Solve the initial value problem $y\,y' = x$, $y(0) = 2$.

:::soluzione
Separating the variables gives $y^2 = x^2 + 4$. Since $y(0) > 0$, the solution is $y = \sqrt{x^2 + 4}$, defined on $\mathbb{R}$.
:::
