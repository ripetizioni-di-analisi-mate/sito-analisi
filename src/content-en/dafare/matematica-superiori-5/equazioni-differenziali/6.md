Solve the initial value problem $y' + 3y = 6$, $y(0) = 0$, and calculate the limit of the solution as $x \to +\infty$.

:::soluzione
$y = 2 - 2\,e^{-3x}$. The limit as $x \to +\infty$ is $2$.
:::
