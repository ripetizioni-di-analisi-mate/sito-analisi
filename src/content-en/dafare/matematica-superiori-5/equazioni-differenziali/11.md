Find the general solution of the equation $y'' - 3y' + 2y = e^{x}$.

:::soluzione
The number $1$ satisfies the characteristic equation, so $\bar{y} = A\,x\,e^{x}$ with $A = -1$. The general solution is $y = c_1\,e^{x} + c_2\,e^{2x} - x\,e^{x}$.
:::
