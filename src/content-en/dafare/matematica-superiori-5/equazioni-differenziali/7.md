Solve the initial value problem $y' - \dfrac{2y}{x} = x^3$, $y(1) = 1$, on the interval $x > 0$.

:::soluzione
The general solution is $y = \dfrac{x^4}{2} + c\,x^2$. The solution of the initial value problem is $y = \dfrac{x^4 + x^2}{2}$.
:::
