Find the intervals of concavity and the inflection points of $f(x) = \ln\left( x^2 + 4 \right)$.

:::soluzione
$f''(x) = \dfrac{2\,\left( 4 - x^2 \right)}{\left( x^2 + 4 \right)^2}$. The graph is concave up on $[-2, 2]$ and concave down outside that interval, with inflection points at $(\pm 2, 3\ln 2)$.
:::
