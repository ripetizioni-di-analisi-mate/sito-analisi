An open-top box with a square base must have volume $32\,\mathrm{dm^3}$. Find the dimensions that minimize its surface area and calculate the minimum surface area.

:::soluzione
The base has side length $4\,\mathrm{dm}$ and the height is $2\,\mathrm{dm}$. The minimum surface area is $48\,\mathrm{dm^2}$.
:::
