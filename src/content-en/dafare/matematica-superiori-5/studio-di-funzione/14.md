A function $f$ that is differentiable on $\mathbb{R}$ has derivative $f'(x) = (x - 1)^2\,(x + 2)$. Find the intervals on which $f$ increases or decreases, the points where it attains local maxima or minima, and the horizontal coordinates of its inflection points.

:::soluzione
$f$ decreases for $x \leq -2$ and increases for $x \geq -2$. There is a minimum at $x = -2$ and an inflection point with a horizontal tangent at $x = 1$. Since $f''(x) = 3\,(x - 1)(x + 1)$, the inflection points have horizontal coordinates $-1$ and $1$.
:::
