Find the intervals on which $f(x) = x^3 - 12x + 1$ increases or decreases and the points where it has local maxima or minima.

:::soluzione
The function increases on $(-\infty, -2]$ and $[2, +\infty)$ and decreases on $[-2, 2]$. It has a local maximum at $(-2, 17)$ and a local minimum at $(2, -15)$.
:::
