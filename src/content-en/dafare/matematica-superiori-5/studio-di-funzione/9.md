Among the rectangles inscribed in a circle of radius $5\,\mathrm{cm}$, find the one with the greatest area.

:::soluzione
It is the square with side length $5\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{cm}$ and area $50\,\mathrm{cm^2}$.
:::
