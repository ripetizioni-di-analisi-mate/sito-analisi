A particle moves along a straight line with position function $s(t) = t^3 - 9t^2 + 24t$, where $s$ is in meters and $t$ in seconds, for $0 \leq t \leq 5$. Find the maximum and minimum velocities over this time interval.

:::soluzione
$v(t) = 3t^2 - 18t + 24$. The maximum velocity is $24\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at $t = 0$ and the minimum velocity is $-3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at $t = 3\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
