---
titolo: Minimum of an exponential function
teoria: [criterio-derivata-prima, unico-punto-stazionario]
parole: [exponential function, absolute minimum]
---

Find the intervals on which $f(x) = x\,e^{2x}$ increases or decreases, and find its minimum.

:::soluzione
$f'(x) = (1 + 2x)\,e^{2x}$. The function decreases for $x \leq -\dfrac{1}{2}$ and increases for $x \geq -\dfrac{1}{2}$, so its absolute minimum is $f\left( -\dfrac{1}{2} \right) = -\dfrac{1}{2e}$.
:::
