Find the points where $f(x) = x^4 - 8x^2 + 3$ attains local or absolute maxima and minima.

:::soluzione
There is a local maximum at $(0, 3)$ and there are absolute minima at $(-2, -13)$ and $(2, -13)$. The function has no absolute maximum.
:::
