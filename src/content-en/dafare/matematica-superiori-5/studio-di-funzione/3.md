---
titolo: Local extrema of a rational function
teoria: [criterio-derivata-prima]
parole: [rational function, local maximum, local minimum]
---

Find the points where $f(x) = \dfrac{x^2}{x + 2}$ has local maxima and minima.

:::soluzione
$f'(x) = \dfrac{x\,(x + 4)}{(x + 2)^2}$. There is a local maximum at $(-4, -8)$ and a local minimum at $(0, 0)$.
:::
