For each value of the real parameter $k$, find the number of real solutions of the equation $x^4 - 2x^2 = k$.

:::soluzione
There are no solutions for $k < -1$, two for $k = -1$, four for $-1 < k < 0$, three for $k = 0$, and two for $k > 0$.
:::
