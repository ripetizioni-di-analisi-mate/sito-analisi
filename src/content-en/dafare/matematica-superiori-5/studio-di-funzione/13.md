Verify that the equation $x^3 + 3x - 5 = 0$ has a unique real solution between $1$ and $2$, and calculate the first two approximations using the tangent method, starting at $x_0 = 2$.

:::soluzione
$x_1 = \dfrac{7}{5} = 1.4$ and $x_2 = \dfrac{437}{370} \approx 1.181$. The solution is approximately $1.154$.
:::
