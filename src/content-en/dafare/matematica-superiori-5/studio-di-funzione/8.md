---
titolo: Absolute maximum and minimum on a closed interval
teoria: [estremi-intervallo-chiuso]
parole: [absolute maximum, absolute minimum, closed interval, trigonometric function]
---

Find the absolute maximum and minimum of $f(x) = x - 2\sin x$ on the interval $[0, \pi]$.

:::soluzione
The absolute minimum is $\dfrac{\pi}{3} - \sqrt{3} \approx -0.68$ at $x = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$, and the absolute maximum is $\pi$ at $x = \pi$.
:::
