---
titolo: A limit of the form infinity minus infinity
teoria: [forma-infinito-meno-infinito, teorema-de-l-hopital]
parole: [infinity minus infinity form, l'Hôpital's rule, logarithm]
---

Evaluate $\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 0} \left( \frac{1}{x} - \frac{1}{\ln (1 + x)} \right)$.

:::soluzione
The limit equals $-\dfrac{1}{2}$.
:::
