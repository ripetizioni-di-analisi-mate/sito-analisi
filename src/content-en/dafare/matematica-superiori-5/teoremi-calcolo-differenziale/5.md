---
titolo: The point given by the mean value theorem for a hyperbola
teoria: [teorema-lagrange]
parole: [Lagrange's mean value theorem, rectangular hyperbola, reciprocal function]
---

Verify that the function $f(x) = \dfrac{1}{x}$ satisfies the hypotheses of the mean value theorem on the interval $[1, 3]$, and find the point $c$ given by the theorem.

:::soluzione
The line through the endpoints has slope $-\dfrac{1}{3}$, and $-\dfrac{1}{c^2} = -\dfrac{1}{3}$ gives $c = \sqrt{3}$.
:::
