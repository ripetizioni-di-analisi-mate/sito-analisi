---
titolo: When the hypotheses of Rolle's theorem fail
teoria: [teorema-rolle]
parole: [Rolle's theorem, differentiability, cusp, cube root]
---

Determine whether Rolle's theorem applies to the function $f(x) = \sqrt[3]{x^2}$ on the interval $[-1, 1]$ and whether there is a point in $(-1, 1)$ where the derivative is zero.

:::soluzione
The theorem does not apply because $f$ is not differentiable at $0$, where its graph has a cusp. The derivative $f'(x) = \dfrac{2}{3\sqrt[3]{x}}$ is never zero on $(-1, 1)$.
:::
