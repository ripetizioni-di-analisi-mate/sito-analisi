---
titolo: A function with zero derivative on an interval
teoria: [derivata-nulla]
parole: [function with zero derivative, constant function, arctangent, identity]
---

Verify that the function $f(x) = \arctan \dfrac{1 + x}{1 - x} - \arctan x$ is constant on the interval $(-\infty, 1)$, and find the value of the constant.

:::soluzione
We have $f'(x) = 0$ for $x \neq 1$, so $f$ is constant on $(-\infty, 1)$ and its value is $f(0) = \dfrac{\pi}{4}$.
:::
