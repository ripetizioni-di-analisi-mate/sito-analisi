---
titolo: The mean value theorem for a piecewise function
teoria: [teorema-lagrange]
parole: [Lagrange's mean value theorem, piecewise function, continuity, differentiability, parameters]
---

Consider the function
$$
f(x) =
\begin{cases}
x^2 + a & \text{if } 0 \leq x \leq 1 \\
b x - 1 & \text{if } 1 < x \leq 3
\end{cases}
$$
Find $a$ and $b$ so that $f$ satisfies the hypotheses of the mean value theorem on the interval $[0, 3]$, and find the point $c$ given by the theorem.

:::soluzione
$a = 0$ and $b = 2$. The slope of the line through the endpoints is $\dfrac{5}{3}$, and the point is $c = \dfrac{5}{6}$.
:::
