---
titolo: A limit of the form one to the power of infinity
teoria: [forme-esponenziali, teorema-de-l-hopital]
parole: [one to the power of infinity form, exponential function with base e, cosine]
---

Evaluate $\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 0} \left( \cos (2x) \right)^{\frac{1}{x^2}}$.

:::soluzione
The limit equals $e^{-2} = \dfrac{1}{e^2}$.
:::
