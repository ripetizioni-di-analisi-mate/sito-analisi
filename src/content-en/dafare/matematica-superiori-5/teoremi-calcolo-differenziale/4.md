---
titolo: The point given by the mean value theorem for a polynomial
teoria: [teorema-lagrange]
parole: [Lagrange's mean value theorem, mean value, polynomial]
---

Verify that the function $f(x) = x^3 - x$ satisfies the hypotheses of the mean value theorem on the interval $[0, 2]$, and find the point $c$ given by the theorem.

:::soluzione
The line through the endpoints has slope $3$, and $3c^2 - 1 = 3$ gives $c = \dfrac{2\sqrt{3}}{3} \approx 1.15$.
:::
