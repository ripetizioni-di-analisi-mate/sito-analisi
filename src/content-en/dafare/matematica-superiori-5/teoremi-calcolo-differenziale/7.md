---
titolo: Intervals of increase and decrease for a function involving a logarithm
teoria: [criterio-monotonia]
parole: [monotonicity test, logarithm, increasing function, decreasing function]
---

Find the intervals on which the function $f(x) = \dfrac{\ln x}{x}$ increases or decreases.

:::soluzione
$f'(x) = \dfrac{1 - \ln x}{x^2}$. The function is increasing on $(0, e]$ and decreasing on $[e, +\infty)$.
:::
