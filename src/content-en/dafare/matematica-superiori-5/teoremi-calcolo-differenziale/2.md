---
titolo: Rolle's theorem for a trigonometric function
teoria: [teorema-rolle]
parole: [Rolle's theorem, trigonometric function, sine]
---

Verify that the function $f(x) = \sin (2x)$ satisfies the hypotheses of Rolle's theorem on the interval $[0, \pi]$, and find the points $c$ given by the theorem.

:::soluzione
We have $f(0) = f(\pi) = 0$. The points are $c = \dfrac{\pi}{4}$ and $c = \dfrac{3}{4}\pi$.
:::
