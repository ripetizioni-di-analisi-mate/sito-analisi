---
titolo: The point in Cauchy's mean value theorem
teoria: [teorema-cauchy]
parole: [Cauchy's mean value theorem, two functions, powers]
---

Verify that the functions $f(x) = x^2$ and $g(x) = x^3$ satisfy the hypotheses of Cauchy's mean value theorem on the interval $[0, 1]$, and find the point $c$ given by the theorem.

:::soluzione
We have $g'(x) = 3x^2 \neq 0$ on $(0, 1)$. The equation $\dfrac{2c}{3c^2} = 1$ gives $c = \dfrac{2}{3}$.
:::
