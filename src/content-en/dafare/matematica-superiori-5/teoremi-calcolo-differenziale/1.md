---
titolo: Rolle's theorem for a polynomial
teoria: [teorema-rolle]
parole: [Rolle's theorem, checking the hypotheses, polynomial]
---

Verify that the function $f(x) = x^3 - 4x$ satisfies the hypotheses of Rolle's theorem on the interval $[-2, 2]$, and find the points $c$ whose existence the theorem guarantees.

:::soluzione
The hypotheses hold because $f$ is a polynomial and $f(-2) = f(2) = 0$. The points are $c = -\dfrac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}$ and $c = \dfrac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}$.
:::
