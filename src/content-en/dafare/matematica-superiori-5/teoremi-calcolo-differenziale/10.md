---
titolo: An inequality involving the logarithm
teoria: [criterio-monotonia]
parole: [inequality, logarithm, monotonicity test, minimum]
---

Prove that $\ln x \leq x - 1$ for every $x > 0$.

:::soluzione
The function $g(x) = x - 1 - \ln x$ has derivative $1 - \dfrac{1}{x}$, so it decreases on $(0, 1]$ and increases on $[1, +\infty)$. Its minimum value is $g(1) = 0$, so $g(x) \geq 0$ for every $x > 0$.
:::
