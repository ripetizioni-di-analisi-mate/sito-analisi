Classify the discontinuity of $f(x) = \dfrac{x^2 + x}{|x|}$. If it is of the first kind, find the size of the jump.

:::soluzione
At $x = 0$ there is a discontinuity of the first kind because the left-hand limit is $-1$ and the right-hand limit is $1$. The jump has size $2$.
:::
