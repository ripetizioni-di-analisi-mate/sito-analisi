---
titolo: Discontinuity of the arctangent of a quotient
teoria: [discontinuita-prima-specie, punto-discontinuita]
parole: [discontinuity of the first kind, jump, arctangent]
---

Classify the discontinuity of $f(x) = \arctan \dfrac{1}{x}$ and find the size of the jump.

:::soluzione
At $x = 0$ there is a discontinuity of the first kind with left-hand limit $-\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, right-hand limit $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ and a jump of size $\pi$.
:::
