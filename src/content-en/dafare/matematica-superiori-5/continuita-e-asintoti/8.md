---
titolo: Asymptotes with two zeros of the denominator
teoria: [asintoto-verticale, asintoto-orizzontale, asintoti-funzioni-razionali]
parole: [vertical asymptotes, horizontal asymptote, rational function]
---

Find the asymptotes of $f(x) = \dfrac{x}{x^2 - x - 2}$.

:::soluzione
The lines $x = -1$ and $x = 2$ are vertical asymptotes, and the line $y = 0$ is a horizontal asymptote.
:::
