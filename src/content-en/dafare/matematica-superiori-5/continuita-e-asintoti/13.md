Prove that the equation $x^5 + x - 1 = 0$ has exactly one real solution and that it lies between $0$ and $1$. Perform two bisection steps starting from $[0, 1]$.

:::soluzione
The function is continuous and strictly increasing, with $f(0) = -1$ and $f(1) = 1$. After two steps the solution lies between $\dfrac{3}{4}$ and $1$ because $f\left( \dfrac{1}{2} \right) < 0$ and $f\left( \dfrac{3}{4} \right) < 0$.
:::
