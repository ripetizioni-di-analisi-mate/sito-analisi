---
titolo: Continuity using a standard limit
teoria: [continuita-punto, continuita-funzioni-elementari]
parole: [continuity at a point, standard limit, exponential function]
---

Determine whether the function
$$
f(x) =
\begin{cases}
\dfrac{e^{2x} - 1}{x} & \text{if } x \neq 0 \\[8pt]
2 & \text{if } x = 0
\end{cases}
$$
is continuous at $x_0 = 0$.

:::soluzione
The function is continuous at $0$ because $\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 0} \dfrac{e^{2x} - 1}{x} = 2 = f(0)$.
:::
