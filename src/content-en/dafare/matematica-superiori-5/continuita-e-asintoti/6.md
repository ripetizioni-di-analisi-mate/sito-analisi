---
titolo: Finding extrema with the extreme value theorem
teoria: [teorema-weierstrass]
parole: [extreme value theorem, maximum, minimum, closed interval]
---

Determine whether the extreme value theorem applies to $f(x) = \dfrac{1}{x - 2}$ on $[0, 1]$, and find the maximum and minimum of $f$ on this interval.

:::soluzione
The theorem applies because $f$ is continuous on $[0, 1]$. The maximum is $f(0) = -\dfrac{1}{2}$ and the minimum is $f(1) = -1$.
:::
