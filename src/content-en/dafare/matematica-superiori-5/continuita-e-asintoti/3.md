Find and classify the points of discontinuity of $f(x) = \dfrac{x^2 - 9}{x^2 - 3x}$.

:::soluzione
At $x = 3$ there is a discontinuity of the third kind because the limit equals $2$. At $x = 0$ there is a discontinuity of the second kind.
:::
