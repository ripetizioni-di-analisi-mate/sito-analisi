---
titolo: Continuity of a piecewise function at a point
teoria: [continuita-punto, continuita-destra-sinistra]
parole: [continuity at a point, piecewise function, right-hand limit, left-hand limit]
---

Determine whether the function
$$
f(x) =
\begin{cases}
x^2 - 1 & \text{if } x \leq 2 \\
2x - 1 & \text{if } x > 2
\end{cases}
$$
is continuous at $x_0 = 2$.

:::soluzione
The function is continuous at $2$ because $f(2) = 3$ and the left- and right-hand limits both equal $3$.
:::
