---
titolo: Oblique asymptotes of a radical function
teoria: [asintoto-obliquo, formule-asintoto-obliquo]
parole: [oblique asymptote to the right, oblique asymptote to the left, radical function]
---

Find the asymptotes of $f(x) = \sqrt{x^2 - 2x + 5}$.

:::soluzione
The line $y = x - 1$ is an oblique asymptote to the right, and the line $y = -x + 1$ is an oblique asymptote to the left. There are no vertical asymptotes.
:::
