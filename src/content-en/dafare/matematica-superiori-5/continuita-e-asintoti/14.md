---
titolo: Approximating a square root by bisection
teoria: [metodo-bisezione, teorema-esistenza-zeri]
parole: [bisection method, square root, number of steps, interval length]
---

Approximate $\sqrt{7}$ by performing three bisection steps on $f(x) = x^2 - 7$ over the interval $[2, 3]$. Then determine how many steps are needed for the interval to have length less than $0.001$.

:::soluzione
After three steps $\sqrt{7}$ lies between $2.625$ and $2.75$. To obtain a length less than $0.001$ takes $10$ steps.
:::
