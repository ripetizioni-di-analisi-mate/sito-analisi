---
titolo: Parameters for differentiability of a piecewise function
teoria: [derivabilita-continuita, criterio-derivabilita]
parole: [piecewise function, parameters, continuity and differentiability]
---

Find the values of the real parameters $a$ and $b$ for which the function
$$
f(x) =
\begin{cases}
a x^2 + b x & \text{if } x \leq 1 \\
\ln x & \text{if } x > 1
\end{cases}
$$
is differentiable on $\mathbb{R}$.

:::soluzione
$a = 1$ and $b = -1$.
:::
