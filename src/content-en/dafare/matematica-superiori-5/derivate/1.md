---
titolo: Derivative at a point from the definition
teoria: [rapporto-incrementale, derivata]
parole: [difference quotient, definition of the derivative]
---

Use the definition to calculate the derivative of $f(x) = 2x^2 + x$ at $x_0 = -1$.

:::soluzione
The difference quotient is $-3 + 2h$, so $f'(-1) = -3$.
:::
