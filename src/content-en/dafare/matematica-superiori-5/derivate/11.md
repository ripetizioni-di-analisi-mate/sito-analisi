---
titolo: Classifying points where a function is not differentiable
teoria: [punti-non-derivabilita, criterio-derivabilita]
parole: [corner, inflection point with a vertical tangent, absolute value, cube root]
---

Find and classify the points where $f(x) = |x - 1| + \sqrt[3]{x}$ is not differentiable.

:::soluzione
At $x = 1$ there is a corner, with $f'_-(1) = -\dfrac{2}{3}$ and $f'_+(1) = \dfrac{4}{3}$. At $x = 0$ there is an inflection point with a vertical tangent.
:::
