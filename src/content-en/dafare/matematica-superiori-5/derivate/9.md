---
titolo: Derivative of an inverse function at a point
teoria: [derivata-funzione-inversa]
parole: [inverse function, derivative of the inverse function]
---

The function $f(x) = x^5 + x^3 + 1$ is strictly increasing on $\mathbb{R}$. Calculate the derivative of its inverse at $y_0 = 3$.

:::soluzione
Since $f(1) = 3$ and $f'(1) = 8$, we have $\left( f^{-1} \right)'(3) = \dfrac{1}{8}$.
:::
