titolo: Second derivative and verification of an equality
teoria: [derivate-successive, regole-somma-prodotto, derivata-funzione-composta]
parole: [second derivative, successive derivatives, exponential function]
---

Calculate the first and second derivatives of $f(x) = x\,e^{-x}$ and verify that $f''(x) + 2f'(x) + f(x) = 0$ for every $x$.

:::soluzione
$f'(x) = (1 - x)\,e^{-x}$ and $f''(x) = (x - 2)\,e^{-x}$. The sum $(x - 2) + 2\,(1 - x) + x$ equals $0$, so the equality holds.
:::
