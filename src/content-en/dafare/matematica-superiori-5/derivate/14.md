A stone thrown upward has height $h(t) = 20t - 5t^2$, where $h$ is in meters and $t$ is in seconds. Find its velocity at time $t = 1\,\mathrm{s}$, the time when the stone reaches its greatest height, that height, and its acceleration.

:::soluzione
$v(1) = 10\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The stone reaches its greatest height at $t = 2\,\mathrm{s}$, and that height is $20\,\mathrm{m}$. The acceleration is constant and equals $-10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
:::
