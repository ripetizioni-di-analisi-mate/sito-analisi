titolo: Derivative of a rational function from the definition

Use the definition to calculate the derivative of $f(x) = \dfrac{1}{x^2}$ at $x_0 = 1$.

:::soluzione
The difference quotient is $-\dfrac{2 + h}{(1 + h)^2}$, so $f'(1) = -2$.
:::
