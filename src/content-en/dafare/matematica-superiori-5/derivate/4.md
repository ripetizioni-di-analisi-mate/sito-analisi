Find the points on the graph of $f(x) = x^3 - 3x$ where the tangent line is parallel to the line $y = 9x$, and write the equations of these tangents.

:::soluzione
The points are $(2, 2)$ and $(-2, -2)$. The tangents are $y = 9x - 16$ and $y = 9x + 16$.
:::
