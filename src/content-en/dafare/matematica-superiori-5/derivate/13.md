---
titolo: Estimation using the differential
teoria: [differenziale]
parole: [differential, approximation, volume of a sphere]
---

The radius of a sphere increases from $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ to $5.1\,\mathrm{cm}$. Use the differential to estimate the increase in volume $V(r) = \dfrac{4}{3}\,\pi r^3$.

:::soluzione
$dV = 4\pi r^2\,dr = 10\pi \approx 31.4\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ (the exact value is approximately $32.0\,\mathrm{cm^3}$).
:::
