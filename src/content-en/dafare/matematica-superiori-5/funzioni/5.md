Find the domain and intercepts and determine the sign of the function $f(x) = \dfrac{x^2 - 1}{x - 2}$.

:::soluzione
$D = \mathbb{R} \setminus \{2\}$. The graph meets the $x$-axis at $(-1, 0)$ and $(1, 0)$ and the $y$-axis at $\left( 0, \dfrac{1}{2} \right)$. We have $f(x) > 0$ for $-1 < x < 1$ and for $x > 2$, and $f(x) < 0$ for $x < -1$ and for $1 < x < 2$.
:::
