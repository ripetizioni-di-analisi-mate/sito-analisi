titolo: Area of a rectangle with a given perimeter
teoria: [dominio, immagine]

---

A rectangle has perimeter $20\,\mathrm{cm}$. Express its area $A$ as a function of the base $x$, give the domain in the context of the problem, and find the range.

:::soluzione
$A(x) = x\,(10 - x)$, with $0 < x < 10$. The range is $(0, 25]$, and the area $25\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ is attained for $x = 5$, when the rectangle is a square.
:::
