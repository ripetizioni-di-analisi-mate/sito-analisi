---
titolo: A function that is neither injective nor surjective
teoria: [iniettiva-suriettiva, immagine]
parole: [injective function, surjective function, range, parabola]
---

Determine whether the function $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, $f(x) = x^2 + 2x$, is injective and whether it is surjective, and find its range.

:::soluzione
It is not injective because $f(-2) = f(0) = 0$. It is not surjective because its range is $[-1, +\infty)$.
:::
