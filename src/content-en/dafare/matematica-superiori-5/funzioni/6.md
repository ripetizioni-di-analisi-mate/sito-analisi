---
titolo: Even and odd functions
teoria: [funzioni-pari-dispari]
parole: [even function, odd function, reflection]
---

Determine whether the functions $f(x) = x^4 - 3x^2 + 1$, $g(x) = \dfrac{x^3}{x^2 - 1}$, and $h(x) = e^{x} + x$ are even, odd, or neither.

:::soluzione
$f$ is even, and $g$ is odd. The function $h$ is neither even nor odd because $h(1) = e + 1$ and $h(-1) = \dfrac{1}{e} - 1$.
:::
