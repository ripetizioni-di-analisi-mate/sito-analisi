Find the inverse of $f(x) = \dfrac{3x - 1}{x + 2}$, defined for $x \neq -2$, and the domain of the inverse.

:::soluzione
$f^{-1}(x) = \dfrac{2x + 1}{3 - x}$, with domain $\mathbb{R} \setminus \{3\}$.
:::
