Find the periods of the functions $f(x) = 2 \sin \dfrac{\pi x}{3} - 1$ and $g(x) = \tan (4x)$.

:::soluzione
$f$ has period $6$, and $g$ has period $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$.
:::
