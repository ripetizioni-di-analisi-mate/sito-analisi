---
titolo: Compositions of a root function and a polynomial
teoria: [funzione-composta, dominio]
parole: [composite function, domain of the composite, root]
---

Given $f(x) = \sqrt{x}$ and $g(x) = x^2 - 9$, write down $f \circ g$ and $g \circ f$ and find their domains.

:::soluzione
$(f \circ g)(x) = \sqrt{x^2 - 9}$ has domain $(-\infty, -3] \cup [3, +\infty)$, and $(g \circ f)(x) = \left( \sqrt{x} \right)^2 - 9 = x - 9$ has domain $[0, +\infty)$.
:::
