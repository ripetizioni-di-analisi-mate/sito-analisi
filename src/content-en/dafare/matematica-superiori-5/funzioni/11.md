Find the domain and range of $f(x) = 1 + \ln (x - 2)$ and its inverse function.

:::soluzione
$D = (2, +\infty)$, with range $\mathbb{R}$. The inverse is $f^{-1}(x) = 2 + e^{x - 1}$, defined on $\mathbb{R}$ with range $(2, +\infty)$.
:::
