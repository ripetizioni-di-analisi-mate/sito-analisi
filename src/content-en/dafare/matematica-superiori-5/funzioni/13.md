---
titolo: Counting solutions from an absolute value graph
teoria: [valore-assoluto, segno-intersezioni]
parole: [absolute value, equation with a parameter, number of solutions, graph]
---

Sketch the graph of $y = \left| x^2 - 4x \right|$ and use it to determine, as the real parameter $k$ varies, the number of solutions of the equation $\left| x^2 - 4x \right| = k$.

:::soluzione
There are no solutions for $k < 0$, two for $k = 0$, four for $0 < k < 4$, three for $k = 4$, and two for $k > 4$.
:::
