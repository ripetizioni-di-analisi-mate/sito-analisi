titolo: Area of a region bounded by an exponential curve
teoria: [area-tra-curve, formula-fondamentale]
parole: [area between two curves, exponential function]
---

Find the area of the region bounded by the graph of $y = e^{x}$ and the lines $y = 1$ and $x = 2$.

:::soluzione
$\displaystyle\int_0^2 \left( e^{x} - 1 \right) dx = e^2 - 3$.
:::
