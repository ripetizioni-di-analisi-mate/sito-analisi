---
titolo: Volume of the solid generated by an exponential curve
teoria: [volume-rotazione-x]
parole: [volume, solid of revolution, exponential function]
---

Find the volume of the solid generated by a full rotation about the $x$-axis of the curvilinear trapezoid under $f(x) = e^{x}$ on the interval $[0, 1]$.

:::soluzione
$V = \dfrac{\pi \left( e^2 - 1 \right)}{2}$.
:::
