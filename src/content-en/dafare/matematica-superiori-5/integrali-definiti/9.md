Find the average value of $f(x) = \sin x$ on the interval $[0, \pi]$ and the points $c$ in $[0, \pi]$ where $f(c)$ equals its average value.

:::soluzione
The average value is $\dfrac{2}{\pi}$. The points are $c = \arcsin \dfrac{2}{\pi} \approx 0.69$ and $c = \pi - \arcsin \dfrac{2}{\pi} \approx 2.45$.
:::
