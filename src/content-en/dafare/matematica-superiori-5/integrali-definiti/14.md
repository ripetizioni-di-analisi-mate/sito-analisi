---
titolo: Distance traveled by a body with variable acceleration
teoria: [spazio-velocita]
parole: [acceleration, velocity, distance traveled]
---

A body starts from rest and moves along a straight line with acceleration $a(t) = 6 - 2t$, in $\mathrm{m/s^2}$, for $0 \leq t \leq 6$, with $t$ in seconds. Find the velocity $v(t)$ and the distance traveled in the first $6$ seconds.

:::soluzione
$v(t) = 6t - t^2$, in $\mathrm{m/s}$, and the distance traveled is $36\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
