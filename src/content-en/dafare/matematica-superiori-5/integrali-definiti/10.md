titolo: Intervals of increase and decrease of an accumulation function
teoria: [funzione-integrale, teorema-fondamentale]

Let $F(x) = \displaystyle\int_2^x \frac{(t - 1)(t - 3)}{t^2 + 1}\,dt$. Find the intervals on which $F$ increases or decreases and its local maximum and minimum points.

:::soluzione
$F$ is increasing on $(-\infty, 1]$ and on $[3, +\infty)$ and decreasing on $[1, 3]$. At $x = 1$ it has a local maximum and at $x = 3$ a local minimum.
:::
