---
titolo: Work required to stretch a spring
teoria: [lavoro-forza]
parole: [work, spring force, spring]
---

A spring has spring constant $k = 400\,\mathrm{N/m}$. Find the work required to increase its extension from $0.1\,\mathrm{m}$ to $0.3\,\mathrm{m}$, given that the applied force is $F(x) = kx$.

:::soluzione
$W = \displaystyle\int_{0.1}^{0.3} 400\,x\,dx = 16\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
