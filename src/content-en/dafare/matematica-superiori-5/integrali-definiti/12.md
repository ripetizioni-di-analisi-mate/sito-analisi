titolo: Volume of the solid generated by an arc of a sine curve
teoria: [volume-rotazione-x]

Find the volume of the solid generated by a full rotation about the $x$-axis of the region between the graph of $y = \sin x$ and the $x$-axis for $0 \leq x \leq \pi$.

:::soluzione
$V = \pi \displaystyle\int_0^{\pi} \sin^2 x\,dx = \dfrac{\pi^2}{2}$.
:::
