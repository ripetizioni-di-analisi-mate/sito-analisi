Find the area of the region bounded by the parabola $y = -x^2 + 2x + 3$ and the $x$-axis.

:::soluzione
The parabola meets the $x$-axis at $x = -1$ and $x = 3$, and the area is $\dfrac{32}{3}$.
:::
