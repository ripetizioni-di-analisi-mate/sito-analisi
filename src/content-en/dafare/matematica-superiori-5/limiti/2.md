titolo: Verifying a finite limit at a point

Use the definition to verify that $\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 1} (5 - 2x) = 3$, and find $\delta$ when $\varepsilon = 0.02$.

:::soluzione
The condition $|5 - 2x - 3| < \varepsilon$ is equivalent to $|x - 1| < \dfrac{\varepsilon}{2}$, so $\delta = \dfrac{\varepsilon}{2}$. With $\varepsilon = 0.02$ we have $\delta = 0.01$.
:::
