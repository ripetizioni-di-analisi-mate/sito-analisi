---
titolo: A parameter in a rational function at infinity
teoria: [limiti-razionali-infinito]
parole: [limit with a parameter, rational function, limit at infinity]
---

Find the value of the real parameter $k$ for which $\displaystyle\lim_{x \to +\infty} \frac{k x^3 - x + 2}{5 - 2x^3} = 3$.

:::soluzione
$k = -6$.
:::
