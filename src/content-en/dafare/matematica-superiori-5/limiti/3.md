---
titolo: Verifying a finite limit at infinity
teoria: [limite-finito-all-infinito, quattro-definizioni]
parole: [verifying a limit, limit at infinity, horizontal asymptote]
---

Use the definition to verify that $\displaystyle\lim_{x \to +\infty} \frac{x + 3}{2x} = \frac{1}{2}$, and find how large $x$ must be for the function to differ from $\dfrac{1}{2}$ by less than $0.01$.

:::soluzione
For $x > 0$ we have $\left| f(x) - \dfrac{1}{2} \right| = \dfrac{3}{2x} < \varepsilon$ when $x > \dfrac{3}{2\varepsilon}$. With $\varepsilon = 0.01$ we obtain $x > 150$.
:::
