A geometric sequence has first term $a_1 = 6$ and common ratio $q = -\dfrac{1}{3}$. Calculate the limit of $a_n$ and the limit of the sum $S_n$ of the first $n$ terms as $n \to +\infty$.

:::soluzione
$\displaystyle\lim_{n \to +\infty} a_n = 0$ and $\displaystyle\lim_{n \to +\infty} S_n = \dfrac{9}{2}$.
:::
