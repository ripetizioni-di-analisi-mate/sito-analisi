---
titolo: A limit involving a difference of square roots at infinity
teoria: [forme-indeterminate, limiti-razionali-infinito]
parole: [rationalization, infinity minus infinity form, radicals]

Calculate $\displaystyle\lim_{x \to +\infty} \left( \sqrt{x^2 + 3x} - \sqrt{x^2 - x} \right)$.

:::soluzione
$2$.
:::
