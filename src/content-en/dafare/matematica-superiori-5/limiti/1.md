Find the domain $D$ of the function $f(x) = \sqrt{x^2 (x - 3)}$ and determine which points of $D$ are isolated and which are accumulation points.

:::soluzione
$D = \{0\} \cup [3, +\infty)$. The point $0$ is isolated, and every point of $[3, +\infty)$ is an accumulation point.
:::
