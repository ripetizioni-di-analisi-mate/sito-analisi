Calculate the right-hand and left-hand limits of $f(x) = \dfrac{2x - 1}{x^2 - x}$ as $x \to 0$.

:::soluzione
$\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 0^+} f(x) = +\infty$ and $\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 0^-} f(x) = -\infty$.
:::
