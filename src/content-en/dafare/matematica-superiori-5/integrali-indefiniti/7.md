titolo: Finding position from velocity
teoria: [moto-da-accelerazione, primitiva-per-un-punto]

A particle moves along a straight line with velocity $v(t) = 3t^2 + 2t$, where $v$ is in $\mathrm{m/s}$ and $t$ is in seconds. At time $t = 1\,\mathrm{s}$, its position is $s = 4\,\mathrm{m}$. Find the position function $s(t)$ and the position at time $t = 3\,\mathrm{s}$.

:::soluzione
$s(t) = t^3 + t^2 + 2$, so $s(3) = 38\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
