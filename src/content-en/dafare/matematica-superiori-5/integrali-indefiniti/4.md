---
titolo: Numerator equal to the derivative of the denominator
teoria: [integrali-funzioni-composte, numeratore-derivata-denominatore]
parole: [composite function, logarithm, derivative of the denominator]
---

Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \frac{6x + 2}{3x^2 + 2x + 5}\,dx$.

:::soluzione
$\ln \left( 3x^2 + 2x + 5 \right) + c$. The absolute value is unnecessary because the denominator is always positive.
:::
