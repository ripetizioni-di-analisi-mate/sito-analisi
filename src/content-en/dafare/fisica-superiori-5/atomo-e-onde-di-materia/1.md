Calculate the wavelength of the hydrogen Balmer line corresponding to $n_i = 6$. Use $R_H = 1.097 \cdot 10^{7}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$.

:::soluzione
$\lambda = \dfrac{1}{R_H \left( \frac{1}{4} - \frac{1}{36} \right)} \approx 410\,\mathrm{nm}$.
:::
