titolo: Energy uncertainty of an excited state
teoria: [indeterminazione-energia-tempo]

An excited atomic state has mean lifetime $\Delta t = 2.0 \cdot 10^{-9}\,\mathrm{s}$. Calculate the minimum energy uncertainty of the state in joules and in electronvolts. Use $h = 6.63 \cdot 10^{-34}\,\mathrm{J\cdot s}$ and $1\,\mathrm{eV} = 1.60 \cdot 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{J}$.

:::soluzione
$\Delta E \geq \dfrac{h}{4\pi \Delta t} \approx 2.6 \cdot 10^{-26}\,\mathrm{J} \approx 1.6 \cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{eV}$.
:::
