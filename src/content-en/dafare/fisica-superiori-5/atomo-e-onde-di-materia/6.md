An $\alpha$ particle (charge $+2e$) of kinetic energy $7.7\,\mathrm{MeV}$ moves directly toward a gold nucleus ($Z = 79$), which is treated as stationary. Calculate its distance of closest approach. Use $\dfrac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0} = 8.99 \cdot 10^{9}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m^2/C^2}$, $e = 1.60 \cdot 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}$, and $1\,\mathrm{MeV} = 1.60 \cdot 10^{-13}\,\mathrm{J}$.

:::soluzione
$d = \dfrac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0} \dfrac{2 Z e^2}{K} \approx 3.0 \cdot 10^{-14}\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
