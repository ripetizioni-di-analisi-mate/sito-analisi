Electrons accelerated through $100\,\mathrm{V}$ are normally incident on a crystal whose rows of atoms are spaced $d = 0.215\,\mathrm{nm}$ apart. Calculate the angle to the normal at which the first diffraction maximum is observed. Use $m_e = 9.11 \cdot 10^{-31}\,\mathrm{kg}$, $e = 1.60 \cdot 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}$, and $h = 6.63 \cdot 10^{-34}\,\mathrm{J\cdot s}$.

:::soluzione
$\lambda \approx 0.123\,\mathrm{nm}$, $\sin\theta = \dfrac{\lambda}{d} \approx 0.571$, $\theta \approx 35^\circ$.
:::
