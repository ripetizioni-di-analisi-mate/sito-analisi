An electron is confined to a one-dimensional box of width $L = 1.0\,\mathrm{nm}$. Calculate the energy of level $n = 3$ in electronvolts. Use $m_e = 9.11 \cdot 10^{-31}\,\mathrm{kg}$, $h = 6.63 \cdot 10^{-34}\,\mathrm{J\cdot s}$, and $1\,\mathrm{eV} = 1.60 \cdot 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{J}$.

:::soluzione
$E_3 = \dfrac{9 h^2}{8 m_e L^2} \approx 3.4\,\mathrm{eV}$.
:::
