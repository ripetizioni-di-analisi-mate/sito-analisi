In a Franck–Hertz-type experiment using the vapor of a particular element, the current drops sharply each time the accelerating voltage increases by $6.7\,\mathrm{V}$. Calculate the wavelength of the radiation emitted by the vapor. Use $h = 6.63 \cdot 10^{-34}\,\mathrm{J\cdot s}$, $c = 3.00 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and $1\,\mathrm{eV} = 1.60 \cdot 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{J}$.

:::soluzione
$\lambda = \dfrac{h c}{6.7\,\mathrm{eV}} \approx 186\,\mathrm{nm}$.
:::
