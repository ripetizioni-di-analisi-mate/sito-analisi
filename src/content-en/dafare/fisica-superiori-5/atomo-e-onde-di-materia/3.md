Given the Bohr radius $r_1 = 5.3 \cdot 10^{-11}\,\mathrm{m}$, calculate the radius of the hydrogen orbit with $n = 4$ and the ratio of the electron’s speed in this orbit to its speed in orbit $n = 1$.

:::soluzione
$r_4 = 16\, r_1 \approx 8.5 \cdot 10^{-10}\,\mathrm{m}$, $\dfrac{v_4}{v_1} = \dfrac{1}{4}$.
:::
