titolo: Limit of the Paschen series
teoria: [formula-di-rydberg, serie-spettrali]

---

Find the limiting wavelength of the hydrogen Paschen series ($n_f = 3$) and identify its spectral region. Use $R_H = 1.097 \cdot 10^{7}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$.

:::soluzione
$\lambda_\infty = \dfrac{9}{R_H} \approx 820\,\mathrm{nm}$, in the infrared.
:::
