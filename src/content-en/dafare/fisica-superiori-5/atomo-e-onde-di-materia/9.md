Find the potential difference through which an electron initially at rest must be accelerated to have de Broglie wavelength $0.050\,\mathrm{nm}$. Use $m_e = 9.11 \cdot 10^{-31}\,\mathrm{kg}$, $e = 1.60 \cdot 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}$, and $h = 6.63 \cdot 10^{-34}\,\mathrm{J\cdot s}$.

:::soluzione
$V = \dfrac{h^2}{2 m_e e \lambda^2} \approx 603\,\mathrm{V}$.
:::
