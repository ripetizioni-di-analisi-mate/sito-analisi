---
titolo: Wavelength of a slow proton
teoria: [ipotesi-di-de-broglie]
parole: ["de Broglie", "proton", "wavelength"]
---

Calculate the de Broglie wavelength of a proton moving at speed $v = 2.0 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Use $m_p = 1.67 \cdot 10^{-27}\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $h = 6.63 \cdot 10^{-34}\,\mathrm{J\cdot s}$.

:::soluzione
$\lambda = \dfrac{h}{m_p v} \approx 2.0 \cdot 10^{-10}\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
