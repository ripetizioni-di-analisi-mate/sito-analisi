titolo: Velocity uncertainty of an electron
teoria: [indeterminazione-posizione]
parole: ["uncertainty principle", "velocity uncertainty", "electron"]
---

An electron is confined to a thin wire. Its position along a direction perpendicular to the wire is known with uncertainty $\Delta x = 1.0\,\mathrm{nm}$. Calculate the minimum uncertainty in its velocity component in that direction. Use $m_e = 9.11 \cdot 10^{-31}\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $h = 6.63 \cdot 10^{-34}\,\mathrm{J\cdot s}$.

:::soluzione
$\Delta v_x \geq \dfrac{h}{4\pi m_e \Delta x} \approx 5.8 \cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
