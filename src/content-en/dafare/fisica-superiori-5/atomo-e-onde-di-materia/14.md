---
titolo: Wavelength in the n = 3 orbit
teoria: [onde-stazionarie-bohr, raggi-orbite-bohr]
parole: ["standing wave", "Bohr orbits", "de Broglie wavelength"]
---

Use the standing-wave condition to calculate the de Broglie wavelength of the electron in the $n = 3$ orbit of hydrogen. The Bohr radius is $r_1 = 5.3 \cdot 10^{-11}\,\mathrm{m}$.

:::soluzione
$\lambda_3 = \dfrac{2\pi r_3}{3} = 6\pi r_1 \approx 1.0 \cdot 10^{-9}\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
