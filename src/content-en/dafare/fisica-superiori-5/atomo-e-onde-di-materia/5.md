A hydrogen atom goes from level $n = 5$ to level $n = 3$. Calculate the energy of the emitted photon in electronvolts and its wavelength. Use $h = 6.63 \cdot 10^{-34}\,\mathrm{J\cdot s}$, $c = 3.00 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and $1\,\mathrm{eV} = 1.60 \cdot 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{J}$.

:::soluzione
$E \approx 0.967\,\mathrm{eV}$, $\lambda \approx 1.29 \cdot 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m}$ (infrared, Paschen series).
:::
