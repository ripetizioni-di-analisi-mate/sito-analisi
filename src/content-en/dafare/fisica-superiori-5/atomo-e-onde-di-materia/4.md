Calculate the ionization energy in electronvolts for a hydrogen atom in level $n = 3$ and the minimum frequency of a photon capable of ionizing it. Use $h = 6.63 \cdot 10^{-34}\,\mathrm{J\cdot s}$ and $1\,\mathrm{eV} = 1.60 \cdot 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{J}$.

:::soluzione
$E_{\text{ion}} = \dfrac{13.6\,\mathrm{eV}}{9} \approx 1.51\,\mathrm{eV}$, $f_{\min} \approx 3.65 \cdot 10^{14}\,\mathrm{Hz}$.
:::
