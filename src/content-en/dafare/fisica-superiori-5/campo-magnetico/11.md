titolo: Magnetic moment of a circular coil
teoria: [momento-magnetico, momento-su-una-spira]

A coil of $40$ circular turns of radius $1.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ carries a current $I = 0.60\,\mathrm{A}$. The normal to the coil makes an angle of $45^\circ$ with a uniform magnetic field $B = 0.25\,\mathrm{T}$. Calculate the magnetic moment of the coil and the torque acting on it.

:::soluzione
$\mu = N I \pi r^2 \approx 1.7 \cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{A\cdot m^2}$, $M = \mu B \sin 45^\circ \approx 3.0 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$.
:::
