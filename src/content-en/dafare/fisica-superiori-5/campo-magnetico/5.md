A proton travels at $v = 6.0 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ perpendicular to a uniform magnetic field $B = 0.50\,\mathrm{T}$. Calculate the magnitude of the Lorentz force on the proton. Use $e = 1.60 \cdot 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}$.

:::soluzione
$F = e v B = 4.8 \cdot 10^{-14}\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
