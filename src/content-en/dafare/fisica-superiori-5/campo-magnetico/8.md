titolo: An accelerated proton entering a magnetic field
teoria: [forza-di-lorentz, lavoro-forza-lorentz]

A proton initially at rest is accelerated through a potential difference of $2.0\,\mathrm{kV}$ and enters a uniform magnetic field $B = 0.10\,\mathrm{T}$ with its velocity perpendicular to the field. Calculate the proton's speed and the Lorentz force it experiences in the field. Explain what its speed will be when it leaves the field if only the magnetic force acts on it. Use $e = 1.60 \cdot 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}$ and $m_p = 1.67 \cdot 10^{-27}\,\mathrm{kg}$.

:::soluzione
$v = \sqrt{\dfrac{2 e \Delta V}{m_p}} \approx 6.2 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{m/s}$, $F = e v B \approx 9.9 \cdot 10^{-15}\,\mathrm{N}$. On leaving the field, the speed is still $6.2 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ because the Lorentz force does no work.
:::
