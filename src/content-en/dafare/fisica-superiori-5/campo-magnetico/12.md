---
titolo: Current in a moving-coil ammeter
teoria: [amperometro-bobina-mobile]
parole: [ammeter, moving coil, radial field, spiral spring]
---

A moving-coil ammeter has a coil of $100$ turns, each of area $1.5\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, in a radial field of $0.30\,\mathrm{T}$ and a spiral spring with torsion constant $k = 2.0 \cdot 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m/rad}$. Calculate the current that deflects the pointer by $30^\circ$.

:::soluzione
$I = \dfrac{k \varphi}{N S B}$ with $\varphi = \dfrac{\pi}{6}\,\mathrm{rad}$, so $I \approx 2.3 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{A}$.
:::
