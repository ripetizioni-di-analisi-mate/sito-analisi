---
titolo: Force on a wire at an angle to the field
teoria: [forza-su-un-filo]
parole: [force on a wire, angle between the wire and the field]
---

A straight wire of length $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ carrying a current $I = 6.0\,\mathrm{A}$ makes an angle of $45^\circ$ with a uniform magnetic field $B = 0.50\,\mathrm{T}$. Calculate the magnitude of the force on the wire.

:::soluzione
$F = I \ell B \sin 45^\circ \approx 0.25\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
