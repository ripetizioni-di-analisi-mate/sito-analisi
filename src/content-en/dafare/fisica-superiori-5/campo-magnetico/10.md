titolo: Maximum torque on a square loop
teoria: [momento-su-una-spira]
parole: [torque, square loop, maximum torque]
---

A square loop of side length $8.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ carrying a current $I = 2.5\,\mathrm{A}$ is in a uniform magnetic field $B = 0.30\,\mathrm{T}$ parallel to its plane. Calculate the torque on the loop.

:::soluzione
$M = I S B = 4.8 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$.
:::
