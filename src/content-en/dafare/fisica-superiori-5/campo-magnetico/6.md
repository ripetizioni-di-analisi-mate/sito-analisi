An electron moves with velocity $v = 2.5 \cdot 10^6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in a uniform magnetic field $B = 4.0\,\mathrm{mT}$. Its velocity makes an angle of $30^\circ$ with the field. Calculate the magnitude of the force on the electron and the magnitude of its acceleration. Use $e = 1.60 \cdot 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}$ and $m_e = 9.11 \cdot 10^{-31}\,\mathrm{kg}$.

:::soluzione
$F = e v B \sin 30^\circ = 8.0 \cdot 10^{-16}\,\mathrm{N}$, $a = \dfrac{F}{m_e} \approx 8.8 \cdot 10^{14}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
:::
