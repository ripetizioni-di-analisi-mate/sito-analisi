---
titolo: Maximum and average torque in a motor
teoria: [motore-elettrico, momento-magnetico]
parole: [electric motor, commutator, average torque]
---

The coil of a DC motor has $250$ turns of area $20\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and carries a current $I = 2.0\,\mathrm{A}$ in a uniform magnetic field $B = 0.30\,\mathrm{T}$. The commutator reverses the current every half turn. Calculate the maximum torque and the average torque over one revolution, assuming that the coil rotates at constant angular speed.

:::soluzione
$M_{\max} = N I S B = 0.30\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$, $M_{\text{avg}} = \dfrac{2}{\pi}\,M_{\max} \approx 0.19\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$.
:::
