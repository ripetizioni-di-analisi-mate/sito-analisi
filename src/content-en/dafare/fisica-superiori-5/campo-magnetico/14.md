---
titolo: Finding the angle of a coil from the torque
teoria: [momento-su-una-spira, momento-magnetico, equilibrio-spira]
parole: [coil, torque, angle, equilibrium]
---

A coil of $10$ turns, each of area $100\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, carries a current $I = 3.0\,\mathrm{A}$ in a uniform magnetic field $B = 0.40\,\mathrm{T}$ and experiences a torque of $6.0 \cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$. Determine the possible angles between the normal to the coil and the field, and identify the position in which the coil is in stable equilibrium.

:::soluzione
$\sin\theta = \dfrac{M}{N I S B} = 0.50$, so $\theta = 30^\circ$ or $\theta = 150^\circ$. Stable equilibrium occurs at $\theta = 0^\circ$.
:::
