An electron moves horizontally northward with velocity $v = 2.0 \cdot 10^6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in a uniform vertical magnetic field of magnitude $B = 5.0 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{T}$ directed upward. Calculate the magnitude of the Lorentz force on the electron and determine the direction in which it acts. Use $e = 1.60 \cdot 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}$.

:::soluzione
$F = e v B = 1.6 \cdot 10^{-17}\,\mathrm{N}$, directed horizontally westward.
:::
