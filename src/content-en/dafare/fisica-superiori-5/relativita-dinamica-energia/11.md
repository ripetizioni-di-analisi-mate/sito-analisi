A $\Lambda$ particle at rest, with rest energy $1115.7\,\mathrm{MeV}$, decays into a proton of rest energy $938.3\,\mathrm{MeV}$ and a negative pion of rest energy $139.6\,\mathrm{MeV}$. Calculate the kinetic energies of the proton and the pion.

:::soluzione
$K_p \approx 5.4\,\mathrm{MeV}$ and $K_\pi \approx 32.4\,\mathrm{MeV}$.
:::
