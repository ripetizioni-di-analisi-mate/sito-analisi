Calculate the kinetic energy of a proton moving at $v = 0.50\,c$ using the relativistic formula and the classical formula $\dfrac{1}{2} m_p v^2$. Use $m_p c^2 = 938\,\mathrm{MeV}$.

:::soluzione
$K = (\gamma - 1)\, m_p c^2 \approx 145\,\mathrm{MeV}$. The classical formula gives $\dfrac{1}{2} m_p v^2 \approx 117\,\mathrm{MeV}$.
:::
