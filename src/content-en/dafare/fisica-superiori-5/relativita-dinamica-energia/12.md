A carbon-12 atom has $6$ protons, $6$ neutrons, and mass $12.000000\,\mathrm{u}$. Calculate the binding energy of its nucleus and its binding energy per nucleon. Use the mass of a hydrogen atom $m_{\text{H}} = 1.007825\,\mathrm{u}$, the neutron mass $m_n = 1.008665\,\mathrm{u}$, and $1\,\mathrm{u} \cdot c^2 = 931.5\,\mathrm{MeV}$.
