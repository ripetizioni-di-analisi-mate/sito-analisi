In the Sun, four hydrogen atoms are transformed into one helium-4 atom through a chain of reactions. Calculate the energy released over the entire process, including annihilation of the positrons produced. Use the atomic masses $m_{\text{H}} = 1.007825\,\mathrm{u}$ and $m(^{4}\mathrm{He}) = 4.002603\,\mathrm{u}$, with $1\,\mathrm{u} \cdot c^2 = 931.5\,\mathrm{MeV}$.

:::soluzione
$Q = \left[ 4\, m_{\text{H}} - m(^{4}\mathrm{He}) \right] c^2 \approx 26.7\,\mathrm{MeV}$.
:::
