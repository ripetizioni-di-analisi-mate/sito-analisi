titolo: Momentum of a fast-moving proton

A proton moves at speed $v = 0.80\,c$. Calculate its relativistic momentum and the ratio of that momentum to the classical momentum $m_p v$. Use $m_p = 1.67 \cdot 10^{-27}\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $c = 3.00 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

:::soluzione
$p = \gamma\, m_p v \approx 6.68 \cdot 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$, with $\dfrac{p}{m_p v} = \gamma \approx 1.67$.
:::
