A proton and an antiproton, both at rest, annihilate to produce two photons. Calculate the energy of each photon in $\mathrm{MeV}$ and its momentum in $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$. Use $m_p c^2 = 938\,\mathrm{MeV}$, $1\,\mathrm{MeV} = 1.60 \cdot 10^{-13}\,\mathrm{J}$, and $c = 3.00 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

:::soluzione
$E_\gamma = m_p c^2 = 938\,\mathrm{MeV}$ and $p = \dfrac{E_\gamma}{c} \approx 5.00 \cdot 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$.
:::
