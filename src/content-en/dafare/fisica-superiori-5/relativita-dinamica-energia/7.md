A proton has momentum $p = 500\,\mathrm{MeV}/c$. Calculate its total and kinetic energies. Use $m_p c^2 = 938\,\mathrm{MeV}$.

:::soluzione
$E = \sqrt{p^2 c^2 + m_p^2 c^4} \approx 1063\,\mathrm{MeV}$ and $K = E - m_p c^2 \approx 125\,\mathrm{MeV}$.
:::
