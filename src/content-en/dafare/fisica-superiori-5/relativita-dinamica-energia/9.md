Determine the speed at which the classical formula $\dfrac{1}{2} m v^2$ gives $90\%$ of the relativistic kinetic energy, meaning the classical formula is in error by $10\%$. Use $c = 3.00 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

:::soluzione
$\beta \approx 0.363$, or $v \approx 1.09 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
