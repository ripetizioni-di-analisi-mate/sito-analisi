An electron starts at rest and is accelerated through a potential difference of $1.00\,\mathrm{MV}$. Calculate its final speed. Use $m_e c^2 = 0.511\,\mathrm{MeV}$ and $c = 3.00 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

:::soluzione
$\gamma = 1 + \dfrac{K}{m_e c^2} \approx 2.96$, so $v = c\sqrt{1 - \dfrac{1}{\gamma^2}} \approx 0.941\,c \approx 2.82 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
