A gamma-ray photon has energy $E = 1.00\,\mathrm{MeV}$. Calculate its momentum in $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$. Use $1\,\mathrm{MeV} = 1.60 \cdot 10^{-13}\,\mathrm{J}$ and $c = 3.00 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

:::soluzione
$p = \dfrac{E}{c} \approx 5.33 \cdot 10^{-22}\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$.
:::
