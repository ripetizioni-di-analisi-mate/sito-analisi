---
titolo: Speed at which kinetic energy is three times rest energy
teoria: [energia-cinetica-relativistica, energia-totale]
parole: ["kinetic energy", "rest energy", "Lorentz factor", "speed"]
---

Find the speed of a particle whose kinetic energy is three times its rest energy. Use $c = 3.00 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

:::soluzione
$\gamma = 4$, so $v = \dfrac{\sqrt{15}}{4}\, c \approx 0.968\,c \approx 2.90 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
