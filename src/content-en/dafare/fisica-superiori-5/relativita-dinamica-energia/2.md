An electron moves at speed $v = 0.980\,c$. Calculate its Lorentz factor and total energy in $\mathrm{MeV}$. Use $m_e c^2 = 0.511\,\mathrm{MeV}$.

:::soluzione
$\gamma \approx 5.03$ and $E = \gamma\, m_e c^2 \approx 2.57\,\mathrm{MeV}$.
:::
