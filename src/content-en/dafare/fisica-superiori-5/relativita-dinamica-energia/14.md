titolo: A clock on a mountain and gravitational time dilation

Two identical atomic clocks are placed at sea level and at an altitude of $1000\,\mathrm{m}$, respectively. Calculate how far the higher clock runs ahead of the other in one year because of gravitational time dilation. Use $g = 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, $c = 3.00 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and take a year to be $3.15 \cdot 10^{7}\,\mathrm{s}$.

:::soluzione
$\Delta t = \dfrac{g h}{c^2}\, t \approx 3.4 \cdot 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{s}$, or approximately $3.4\,\mu\mathrm{s}$.
:::
