---
titolo: Polarized light passing through a filter
teoria: [legge-di-malus, polarizzazione]
parole: [Malus's law, polarized light, polarizer]

A beam of linearly polarized light with intensity $I_0 = 60\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$ strikes a polarizing filter whose transmission axis makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with the polarization direction. Calculate the transmitted light intensity.

:::soluzione
$I = I_0 \cos^2 60^\circ = 15\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.
:::
