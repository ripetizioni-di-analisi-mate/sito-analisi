---
titolo: Intensity from the field amplitude
teoria: [intensita-campo-efficace, intensita-onda]
parole: [intensity, electric field amplitude]
---

A sinusoidal electromagnetic wave in a vacuum has an electric field of amplitude $E_0 = 200\,\mathrm{V/m}$. Calculate the wave intensity ($c = 3.00 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$, $\varepsilon_0 = 8.85 \cdot 10^{-12}\,\mathrm{C^2/(N\cdot m^2)}$).

:::soluzione
$I = \dfrac{1}{2}\, c\,\varepsilon_0 E_0^2 \approx 53\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.
:::
