An electromagnetic wave traveling in a vacuum has electric field $E(x,t) = E_0 \sin\left( 1.57 \cdot 10^{7}\,x - \omega t \right)$, with $x$ in meters. Calculate the wavelength and frequency, and identify the band of the electromagnetic spectrum to which the wave belongs ($c = 3.00 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$).

:::soluzione
$\lambda = \dfrac{2\pi}{k} \approx 4.0 \cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m}$ and $f = \dfrac{c}{\lambda} \approx 7.5 \cdot 10^{14}\,\mathrm{Hz}$. The wave lies in the violet part of the visible spectrum.
:::
