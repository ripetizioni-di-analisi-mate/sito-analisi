---
titolo: Force exerted by an absorbed laser beam
teoria: [pressione-di-radiazione, quantita-di-moto-onda]
parole: [radiation pressure, momentum of radiation, laser]

A laser beam of power $P = 20\,\mathrm{W}$ strikes a surface at normal incidence and is completely absorbed. Calculate the force exerted by the beam on the surface ($c = 3.00 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$).

:::soluzione
$F = \dfrac{P}{c} \approx 6.7 \cdot 10^{-8}\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
