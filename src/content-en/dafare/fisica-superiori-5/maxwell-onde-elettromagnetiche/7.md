titolo: Electric field from a wave's magnetic field
teoria: [relazione-e-cb, densita-energia-onda]

The magnetic field of a sinusoidal electromagnetic wave in a vacuum has amplitude $B_0 = 4.0 \cdot 10^{-8}\,\mathrm{T}$. Calculate the amplitude and root-mean-square value of its electric field ($c = 3.00 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$).

:::soluzione
$E_0 = c B_0 = 12\,\mathrm{V/m}$ and $E_{\text{rms}} = \dfrac{E_0}{\sqrt{2}} \approx 8.5\,\mathrm{V/m}$.
:::
