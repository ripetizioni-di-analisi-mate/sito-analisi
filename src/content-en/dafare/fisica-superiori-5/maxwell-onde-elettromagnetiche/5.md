---
titolo: Magnetic field inside and outside the radius of capacitor plates
teoria: [campo-magnetico-condensatore, legge-ampere-maxwell]
parole: [Ampère–Maxwell law, magnetic field, circular plates]
---

A parallel-plate capacitor in a vacuum has circular plates of radius $R = 3.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ and charges at a constant current $i = 2.0\,\mathrm{A}$. Neglecting edge effects, calculate the magnetic field magnitude between the planes of the plates at distances $r_1 = 1.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $r_2 = 6.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the axis ($\mu_0 = 4\pi \cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\cdot m/A}$).

:::soluzione
$B(r_1) = \dfrac{\mu_0\, i\, r_1}{2\pi R^2} \approx 6.7 \cdot 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{T}$ and $B(r_2) = \dfrac{\mu_0\, i}{2\pi r_2} \approx 6.7 \cdot 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{T}$.
:::
