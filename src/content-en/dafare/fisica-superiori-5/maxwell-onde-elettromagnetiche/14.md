The average energy density of a sinusoidal electromagnetic wave in a vacuum is $\bar{u} = 4.0 \cdot 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{J/m^3}$. Calculate the wave intensity and the rms electric field ($c = 3.00 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$, $\varepsilon_0 = 8.85 \cdot 10^{-12}\,\mathrm{C^2/(N\cdot m^2)}$).

:::soluzione
$I = c\,\bar{u} = 1.2 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$ and $E_{\text{rms}} = \sqrt{\dfrac{\bar{u}}{\varepsilon_0}} \approx 6.7 \cdot 10^{2}\,\mathrm{V/m}$.
:::
