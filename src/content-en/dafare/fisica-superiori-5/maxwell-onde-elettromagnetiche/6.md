A medium-wave radio station transmits at frequency $f = 1.0\,\mathrm{MHz}$. Calculate the wavelength and period of the emitted waves ($c = 3.00 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$).

:::soluzione
$\lambda = \dfrac{c}{f} = 300\,\mathrm{m}$ and $T = \dfrac{1}{f} = 1.0 \cdot 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
