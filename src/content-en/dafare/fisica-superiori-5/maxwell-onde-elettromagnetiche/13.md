titolo: Angle between a polarizer and an analyzer

A beam of unpolarized light passes through two polarizing filters. The final intensity is $\dfrac{3}{8}$ of the initial intensity. Determine the acute angle between the transmission axes of the two filters.

:::soluzione
$\cos^2\theta = \dfrac{3}{4}$, so $\theta = 30^\circ$.
:::
