titolo: Induced electric field in a circular region

A uniform magnetic field perpendicular to a circular region of radius $R = 8.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ increases at a constant rate $\dfrac{dB}{dt} = 0.25\,\mathrm{T/s}$. Calculate the magnitude of the circulation of the induced electric field around the boundary of the region and the field magnitude at the boundary.

:::soluzione
$|\Gamma(\vec{E})| = \pi R^2 \dfrac{dB}{dt} \approx 5.0 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{V}$ and $E = \dfrac{R}{2} \dfrac{dB}{dt} = 1.0 \cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{V/m}$.
:::
