---
titolo: Electric field from a lamp
teoria: [sorgente-puntiforme, intensita-campo-efficace]
parole: [point source, intensity, rms field]
---

A lamp can be treated as a point source that emits $40\,\mathrm{W}$ of electromagnetic radiation uniformly in all directions. Neglecting absorption by the air, calculate the intensity and the rms electric field at a distance $r = 2.5\,\mathrm{m}$ ($c = 3.00 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$, $\varepsilon_0 = 8.85 \cdot 10^{-12}\,\mathrm{C^2/(N\cdot m^2)}$).

:::soluzione
$I = \dfrac{P}{4\pi r^2} \approx 0.51\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$ and $E_{\text{rms}} = \sqrt{\dfrac{I}{c\,\varepsilon_0}} \approx 14\,\mathrm{V/m}$.
:::
