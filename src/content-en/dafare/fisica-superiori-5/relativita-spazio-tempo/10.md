---
titolo: Two simultaneous flashes in a rocket frame
teoria: [lorentz-intervalli, relativita-simultaneita, trasformazioni-lorentz]
parole: [simultaneity, Lorentz transformations, time order]
---

Two flashes occur at the same instant in the rest frame $S$ of a runway at two points separated by $600\,\mathrm{m}$ along the $x$-axis. A rocket moves along the runway at velocity $v = 0.80\,c$ in the positive direction of the $x$-axis. Calculate the time interval between the flashes in the rocket frame and determine which flash occurs first. Use $c = 3.00 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

:::soluzione
$\Delta t' = -\gamma\, \dfrac{v\, \Delta x}{c^2} \approx -2.7\,\mu\mathrm{s}$, so in the rocket frame the flash with the larger horizontal coordinate occurs first.
:::
