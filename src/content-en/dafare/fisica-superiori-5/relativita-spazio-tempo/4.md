---
titolo: Length of a moving rod
teoria: [contrazione-lunghezze, lunghezza-propria]
parole: [length contraction, proper length, measuring rod]
---

A rod of length $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ in its rest frame moves along its length at speed $v = 0.60\,c$ relative to a laboratory. Calculate the length of the rod as measured in the laboratory.

:::soluzione
$L = L_0 \sqrt{1 - \beta^2} = 2.0\,\mathrm{m} \cdot 0.80 = 1.6\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
