A sound signal aboard a spacecraft lasts $30\,\mathrm{s}$ as measured by the crew. The spacecraft moves at velocity $v = 0.60\,c$ relative to Earth. Calculate the duration of the signal as measured by observers on Earth.

:::soluzione
$\Delta t = \gamma\, \Delta t_0 = 1.25 \cdot 30\,\mathrm{s} = 37.5\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
