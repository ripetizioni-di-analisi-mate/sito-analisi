titolo: Simultaneity on a train

A train car moves at velocity $v = 0.60\,c$ relative to the platform. A lamp at the center of the car emits a flash toward both ends. At the instant of emission, each end is $30\,\mathrm{m}$ from the lamp in the platform frame. In that frame, calculate the time interval between the flash reaching the rear and reaching the front. Use $c = 3.00 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

:::soluzione
$\Delta t = \dfrac{30\,\mathrm{m}}{c - v} - \dfrac{30\,\mathrm{m}}{c + v} \approx 1.9 \cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{s}$. The flash reaches the rear first.
:::
