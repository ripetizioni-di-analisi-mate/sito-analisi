In frame $S$, two events are separated by $\Delta x = 1500\,\mathrm{m}$ and $\Delta t = 4.0\,\mu\mathrm{s}$. Calculate $(\Delta s)^2$, classify the interval, and establish whether the first event can cause the second. Finally, determine the velocity of the frame moving along the $x$-axis in which the two events are simultaneous. Use $c = 3.00 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

:::soluzione
$(\Delta s)^2 = -8.1 \cdot 10^{5}\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The interval is spacelike, so the first event cannot cause the second. The frame has velocity $v = \dfrac{c^2\, \Delta t}{\Delta x} = 0.80\,c$.
:::
