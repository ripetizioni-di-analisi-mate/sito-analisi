In an accelerator, two protons move toward each other, each at speed $0.70\,c$ relative to the laboratory. Calculate the speed of one proton relative to the other.

:::soluzione
$|u'| = \dfrac{1.40\,c}{1 + 0.49} \approx 0.94\,c$.
:::
