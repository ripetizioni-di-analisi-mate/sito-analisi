---
titolo: Proper time from an interval
teoria: [intervallo-invariante, tipi-di-intervallo, tempo-proprio]
parole: [invariant interval, proper time, timelike]

In frame $S$, two events are separated by $\Delta x = 900\,\mathrm{m}$ and $c\,\Delta t = 1500\,\mathrm{m}$. Calculate the interval $\Delta s$ and the proper time measured by a clock traveling at constant velocity from the first event to the second. Use $c = 3.00 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

:::soluzione
$\Delta s = 1200\,\mathrm{m}$ and $\Delta t_0 = \dfrac{\Delta s}{c} = 4.0\,\mu\mathrm{s}$.
:::
