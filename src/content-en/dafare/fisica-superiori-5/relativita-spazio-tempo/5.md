titolo: Speed from a contracted length

A spacecraft of length $75\,\mathrm{m}$ in its rest frame passes a space station, which measures its length as $45\,\mathrm{m}$. Determine the speed of the spacecraft relative to the station. Use $c = 3.00 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

:::soluzione
$\beta = \sqrt{1 - \left( \dfrac{45}{75} \right)^2} = 0.80$, so $v = 2.4 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
