A muon with a mean lifetime at rest of $\tau = 2.2\,\mu\mathrm{s}$ moves at speed $v = 0.99\,c$ relative to Earth. Calculate its mean lifetime as measured on Earth and the average distance it travels in Earth’s frame before decaying. Use $c = 3.00 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

:::soluzione
$\gamma \approx 7.09$, $\gamma\, \tau \approx 16\,\mu\mathrm{s}$, and $d = v\, \gamma\, \tau \approx 4.6\,\mathrm{km}$.
:::
