---
titolo: Coordinates of an event in a moving frame
teoria: [trasformazioni-lorentz]
parole: [Lorentz transformations, coordinates, event]
---

Frame $S'$ moves relative to $S$ at velocity $v = 0.60\,c$ along the $x$-axis. Their origins coincide at time $t = t' = 0$. In $S$, an event has coordinates $x = 1200\,\mathrm{m}$ and $t = 5.0\,\mu\mathrm{s}$. Calculate its coordinates $x'$ and $t'$ in $S'$. Use $c = 3.00 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

:::soluzione
$x' = 375\,\mathrm{m}$ and $t' = 3.25\,\mu\mathrm{s}$.
:::
