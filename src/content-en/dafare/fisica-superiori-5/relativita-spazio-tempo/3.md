---
titolo: Speed required to double a time interval
teoria: [dilatazione-tempi, fattore-di-lorentz]
parole: [Lorentz factor, time dilation, speed]
---

Determine how fast a clock must move relative to a laboratory for each second on the clock to last $2.0\,\mathrm{s}$ in the laboratory. Use $c = 3.00 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

:::soluzione
$\gamma = 2$, so $v = c\sqrt{1 - \dfrac{1}{4}} = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,c \approx 2.60 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
