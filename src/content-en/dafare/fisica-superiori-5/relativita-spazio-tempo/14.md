An astronaut leaves Earth for a star $8.0$ light-years away in Earth’s frame, travels at $0.80\,c$, and immediately returns at the same speed. Neglecting the acceleration phases, calculate the duration of the trip for the astronaut’s twin brother who stays on Earth and for the astronaut.

:::soluzione
On Earth, $\Delta t = 20$ years. For the astronaut, $\Delta t_0 = \dfrac{\Delta t}{\gamma} = 12$ years.
:::
