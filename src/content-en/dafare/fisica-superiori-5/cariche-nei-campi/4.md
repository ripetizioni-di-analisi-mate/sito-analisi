---
titolo: Deflection of an electron between two plates
teoria: [deflessione-campo-elettrico]
parole: [deflection, parabolic path, uniform electric field, electron]
---

An electron travels at speed $v_0 = 3.0 \cdot 10^7\,\mathrm{m/s}$ perpendicular to a uniform electric field $E = 4.0 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{V/m}$ as it enters the space between two plates of length $L = 6.0\,\mathrm{cm}$. Calculate the electron’s displacement at the end of the plates and its angle of deflection. ($e = 1.60 \cdot 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}$, $m_e = 9.11 \cdot 10^{-31}\,\mathrm{kg}$)

:::soluzione
$y_L = \dfrac{e E L^2}{2 m_e v_0^2} \approx 1.4\,\mathrm{mm}$, $\tan\theta = \dfrac{e E L}{m_e v_0^2} \approx 0.047$, so $\theta \approx 2.7^\circ$.
:::
