---
titolo: Force direction and direction of rotation of an electron
teoria: [verso-di-rotazione, moto-circolare-campo-magnetico]
parole: [direction of rotation, right-hand rule, electron, Lorentz force]
---

An electron moves to the right in the plane of the page in a uniform magnetic field pointing out of the page. Determine the direction of the Lorentz force on the electron at this instant and the direction in which the electron travels around its circular path.

:::soluzione
The force points upward in the plane of the page. The electron rotates counterclockwise.
:::
