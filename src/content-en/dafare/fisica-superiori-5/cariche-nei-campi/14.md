A semiconductor strip of thickness $s = 0.20\,\mathrm{mm}$ has $n = 1.0 \cdot 10^{22}$ carriers of charge $e$ per cubic meter. It carries a current $I = 10\,\mathrm{mA}$ and is placed in a magnetic field $B = 0.30\,\mathrm{T}$ perpendicular to the strip. Calculate the Hall voltage. ($e = 1.60 \cdot 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}$)

:::soluzione
$\Delta V_H = \dfrac{I B}{n e s} \approx 9.4 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{V}$.
:::
