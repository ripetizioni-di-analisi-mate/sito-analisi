A cyclotron with magnetic field $B = 1.5\,\mathrm{T}$ and radius $R = 40\,\mathrm{cm}$ accelerates alpha particles of charge $2e$ and mass $4 m_p$. Calculate the frequency of the alternating voltage and the maximum kinetic energy of the particles in megaelectronvolts. ($e = 1.60 \cdot 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}$, $m_p = 1.67 \cdot 10^{-27}\,\mathrm{kg}$)

:::soluzione
$f = \dfrac{2 e B}{2\pi \cdot 4 m_p} \approx 1.1 \cdot 10^7\,\mathrm{Hz}$, $K_{\max} = \dfrac{(2e)^2 B^2 R^2}{2 \cdot 4 m_p} \approx 17\,\mathrm{MeV}$.
:::
