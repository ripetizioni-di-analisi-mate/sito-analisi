Two flat parallel plates separated by $d = 5.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ have a potential difference $\Delta V = 120\,\mathrm{V}$. Calculate the electric field and the acceleration of an electron between the plates, neglecting its weight. ($e = 1.60 \cdot 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}$, $m_e = 9.11 \cdot 10^{-31}\,\mathrm{kg}$)

:::soluzione
$E = 2.4 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{V/m}$, $a = \dfrac{e\,\Delta V}{m_e d} \approx 4.2 \cdot 10^{15}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
:::
