Calculate the cyclotron frequency of an electron in a uniform magnetic field $B = 0.10\,\mathrm{T}$. Explain whether the frequency changes if the electron’s speed doubles while remaining small compared with $c$. ($e = 1.60 \cdot 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}$, $m_e = 9.11 \cdot 10^{-31}\,\mathrm{kg}$)

:::soluzione
$f = \dfrac{e B}{2\pi m_e} \approx 2.8 \cdot 10^9\,\mathrm{Hz}$. The frequency does not change because it is independent of speed.
:::
