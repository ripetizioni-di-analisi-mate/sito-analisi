Ions $^{39}\mathrm{K}^+$ and $^{41}\mathrm{K}^+$, each with charge $e$ and with masses $39\,\mathrm{u}$ and $41\,\mathrm{u}$, respectively, start from rest. They are accelerated through $\Delta V = 1.5\,\mathrm{kV}$ and enter a uniform magnetic field $B = 0.40\,\mathrm{T}$ perpendicular to their velocity. Calculate the radii of their semicircular paths and the distance between their impact points on the plate. ($e = 1.60 \cdot 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}$, $1\,\mathrm{u} = 1.66 \cdot 10^{-27}\,\mathrm{kg}$)

:::soluzione
$r_{39} \approx 8.71\,\mathrm{cm}$, $r_{41} \approx 8.93\,\mathrm{cm}$, and a separation of $2\,(r_{41} - r_{39}) \approx 4.4\,\mathrm{mm}$.
:::
