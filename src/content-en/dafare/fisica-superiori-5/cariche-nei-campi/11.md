An electron beam passes without deflection through a region with crossed fields $E = 2.0 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{V/m}$ and $B = 8.0 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{T}$. When the electric field is switched off, the electrons move in a circle of radius $r = 17.8\,\mathrm{cm}$. Determine the ratio $\dfrac{e}{m_e}$.

:::soluzione
$\dfrac{e}{m_e} = \dfrac{E}{B^2 r} \approx 1.76 \cdot 10^{11}\,\mathrm{C/kg}$.
:::
