---
titolo: Radius and pitch of a helix
teoria: [moto-elicoidale]
parole: [helical motion, helix, pitch, radius, electron]
---

An electron enters a uniform magnetic field $B = 1.0 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{T}$ at a speed $v = 2.0 \cdot 10^6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in a direction that makes an angle of $30^\circ$ with the field. Calculate the radius and the pitch of the helix. ($e = 1.60 \cdot 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}$, $m_e = 9.11 \cdot 10^{-31}\,\mathrm{kg}$)

:::soluzione
$r = \dfrac{m_e v \sin 30^\circ}{e B} \approx 5.7\,\mathrm{mm}$, $p = \dfrac{2\pi m_e v \cos 30^\circ}{e B} \approx 6.2\,\mathrm{cm}$.
:::
