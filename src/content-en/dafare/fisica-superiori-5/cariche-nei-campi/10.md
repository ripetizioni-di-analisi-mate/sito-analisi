---
titolo: Magnetic field in a velocity selector
teoria: [selettore-di-velocita]
parole: [velocity selector, crossed fields, magnetic field]
---

In a velocity selector, two plates separated by $d = 5.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ with a potential difference $\Delta V = 30\,\mathrm{V}$ produce the electric field. Determine the magnetic field that allows particles traveling at speed $v = 3.0 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to pass through without deflection.

:::soluzione
$B = \dfrac{\Delta V}{d\, v} = 2.0 \cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{T}$.
:::
