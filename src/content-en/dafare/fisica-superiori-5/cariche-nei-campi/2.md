A proton travels at speed $v = 1.0 \cdot 10^6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Calculate its kinetic energy in joules and in electronvolts, and the potential difference needed to accelerate it from rest to this speed. ($e = 1.60 \cdot 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}$, $m_p = 1.67 \cdot 10^{-27}\,\mathrm{kg}$)

:::soluzione
$K \approx 8.4 \cdot 10^{-16}\,\mathrm{J} \approx 5.2 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{eV}$, so $\Delta V \approx 5.2\,\mathrm{kV}$.
:::
