The flux linkage of a circuit of resistance $3.0\,\mathrm{\Omega}$ increases uniformly from $0$ to $12\,\mathrm{mWb}$ between $t = 0$ and $t = 0.40\,\mathrm{s}$, remains constant until $t = 0.60\,\mathrm{s}$, and then decreases uniformly to $-2.0\,\mathrm{mWb}$ at $t = 0.80\,\mathrm{s}$. Calculate the emf and the magnitude of the induced current in each of the three intervals.

:::soluzione
$\mathcal{E}_1 = -30\,\mathrm{mV}$, $i_1 = 10\,\mathrm{mA}$; $\mathcal{E}_2 = 0$, $i_2 = 0$; $\mathcal{E}_3 = 70\,\mathrm{mV}$, $i_3 \approx 23\,\mathrm{mA}$ in the direction opposite to the current in the first interval.
:::
