---
titolo: Force and power for a rod moving at constant speed
teoria: [fem-cinetica, forza-frenante]
parole: ["rod", "external force", "power", "energy balance", "rails"]
---

A rod of length $50\,\mathrm{cm}$ is pulled at a constant speed of $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ along two rails connected by a $1.8\,\mathrm{\Omega}$ resistor. The magnetic field is uniform, has magnitude $0.30\,\mathrm{T}$, and is perpendicular to the plane of the rails. Friction and the resistances of the rails and rod are negligible. Calculate the current, the force needed to maintain constant velocity, and the power dissipated in the resistor.

:::soluzione
$I = 0.50\,\mathrm{A}$, $F = 0.075\,\mathrm{N}$, $P = 0.45\,\mathrm{W}$.
:::
