---
titolo: Emf induced by an exponentially decreasing field
teoria: [fem-istantanea, flusso-concatenato]
parole: ["instantaneous emf", "exponential", "derivative", "loop"]
---

A loop of area $0.010\,\mathrm{m^2}$ is perpendicular to a uniform magnetic field that decreases according to $B(t) = 0.80\,e^{-\frac{t}{2}}$, with $B$ in teslas and $t$ in seconds. Write the induced emf as a function of time and calculate it for $t = 2.0\,\mathrm{s}$.

:::soluzione
$\mathcal{E}(t) = 4.0 \cdot 10^{-3}\,e^{-\frac{t}{2}}$ (in volts), $\mathcal{E}(2.0\,\mathrm{s}) \approx 1.5 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{V}$.
:::
