---
titolo: Charge induced when a loop is removed from a field
teoria: [carica-indotta, flusso-concatenato]
parole: ["induced charge", "square loop", "removal from a magnetic field", "resistance"]
---

A square loop of side $15\,\mathrm{cm}$ and resistance $0.10\,\mathrm{\Omega}$ is perpendicular to a uniform magnetic field of $0.20\,\mathrm{T}$ and is pulled completely out of the field. Calculate the charge that passes through the loop.

:::soluzione
$q = \dfrac{B \ell^2}{R} = 4.5 \cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{C}$.
:::
