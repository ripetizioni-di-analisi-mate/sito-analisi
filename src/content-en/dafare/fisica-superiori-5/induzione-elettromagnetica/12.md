A square loop of side $20\,\mathrm{cm}$ and resistance $0.10\,\mathrm{\Omega}$ enters a region at a constant speed of $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The magnetic field there is uniform, has magnitude $0.30\,\mathrm{T}$, and is perpendicular to the loop. Calculate the induced current during entry and the magnitude of the magnetic force on the loop.

:::soluzione
$i = \dfrac{B \ell v}{R} = 0.90\,\mathrm{A}$, $F = i \ell B \approx 5.4 \cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
