---
titolo: Instantaneous emf when the field increases as t squared
teoria: [fem-istantanea, flusso-concatenato]
parole: ["instantaneous emf", "derivative of magnetic flux", "coil", "changing field"]
---

A coil of $120$ turns, each of area $15\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, is perpendicular to a uniform magnetic field that varies according to $B(t) = 0.050\,t^2$, with $B$ in teslas and $t$ in seconds. Write the induced emf as a function of time and calculate it for $t = 4.0\,\mathrm{s}$.

:::soluzione
$\mathcal{E}(t) = -0.018\,t$ (in volts), $\mathcal{E}(4.0\,\mathrm{s}) = -0.072\,\mathrm{V}$.
:::
