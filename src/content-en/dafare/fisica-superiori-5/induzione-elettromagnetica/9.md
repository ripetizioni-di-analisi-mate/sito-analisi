---
titolo: Distance traveled by a rod from the induced charge
teoria: [carica-indotta, fem-cinetica]
parole: ["induced charge", "rod", "rails", "distance traveled", "change in area"]
---

A rod of length $20\,\mathrm{cm}$ slides along two rails connected by a $0.50\,\mathrm{\Omega}$ resistor in a uniform magnetic field of $0.40\,\mathrm{T}$ perpendicular to the plane of the rails. The rod moves away from the resistor throughout. The resistances of the rails and rod are negligible. A charge of $0.80\,\mathrm{C}$ passes through the resistor. Determine the distance traveled by the rod.

:::soluzione
$x = \dfrac{q R}{B \ell} = 5.0\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
