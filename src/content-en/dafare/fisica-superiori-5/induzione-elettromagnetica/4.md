titolo: Current direction in a ring as a magnet moves away

The north pole of a bar magnet lies on the axis of a conducting ring. The magnet is moved away from the ring along the axis. Explain which way the induced current flows in the ring, as viewed from the magnet, and whether the ring attracts or repels the magnet.

:::soluzione
The current flows clockwise as viewed from the magnet. The side of the ring facing the magnet becomes a south pole, so the ring attracts the magnet.
:::
