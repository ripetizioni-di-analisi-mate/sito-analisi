An airplane flies horizontally at $250\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in a region where the vertical component of Earth’s magnetic field is $4.0 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{T}$. The distance between the wingtips is $30\,\mathrm{m}$. Calculate the emf between the wingtips, treating the wings as a rod perpendicular to the velocity.

:::soluzione
$\mathcal{E} = B \ell v = 0.30\,\mathrm{V}$.
:::
