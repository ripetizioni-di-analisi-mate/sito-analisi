---
titolo: Charge induced by flipping a coil
teoria: [carica-indotta, flusso-concatenato, angolo-normale]
parole: ["induced charge", "coil", "180-degree rotation", "field measurement"]
---

A coil of $50$ circular turns of radius $2.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ and total resistance $5.0\,\mathrm{\Omega}$ is perpendicular to a uniform magnetic field of $0.25\,\mathrm{T}$. The coil is rotated through $180^\circ$ about one of its diameters. Calculate the charge that passes through the coil.

:::soluzione
$q = \dfrac{2 N B\,\pi r^2}{R} \approx 6.3 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{C}$.
:::
