In an RL circuit with $L = 0.25\,\mathrm{H}$ and $R = 5.0\,\mathrm{\Omega}$, the switch closes at time $t = 0$. Determine how long it takes for the current to reach two-thirds of its steady-state value.

:::soluzione
$t = \tau \ln 3 = 0.050 \cdot \ln 3\,\mathrm{s} = 0.055\,\mathrm{s}$
:::
