An RL circuit has $\mathcal{E} = 20\,\mathrm{V}$, $R = 10\,\mathrm{\Omega}$, and $L = 0.15\,\mathrm{H}$. Calculate the time constant and the steady-state energy stored in the inductor. In steady state a changeover switch disconnects the source and the resistor $R$, then connects the inductor across a $30\,\mathrm{\Omega}$ resistor. Calculate the maximum voltage across this resistor.

:::soluzione
$\tau = 15\,\mathrm{ms}$, $U = 0.30\,\mathrm{J}$, $V_{\max} = 60\,\mathrm{V}$
:::
