An ideal $0.25\,\mathrm{H}$ inductor initially carries a current $1.2\,\mathrm{A}$. At $t = 0$ it is connected across a $8.0\,\mathrm{\Omega}$ resistor after the source is disconnected. Calculate the current at $t = 50\,\mathrm{ms}$.

:::soluzione
$i = 1.2 \cdot e^{-\frac{0.050}{0.03125}}\,\mathrm{A} = 1.2 \cdot e^{-1.6}\,\mathrm{A} = 0.24\,\mathrm{A}$
:::
