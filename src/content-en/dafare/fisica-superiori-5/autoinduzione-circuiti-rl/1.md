---
titolo: "Coil inductance from magnetic flux"
teoria: [induttanza]
parole: ["inductance", "self-flux linkage", "coil", "henry"]
---

A coil with $250$ turns carries a current $I = 0.80\,\mathrm{A}$, and the magnetic flux through each turn is $2.4 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{Wb}$. Calculate the inductance of the coil.

:::soluzione
$L = \dfrac{N\,\Phi_1}{I} = 7.5 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{H} = 7.5\,\mathrm{mH}$
:::
