titolo: "Resistance and inductance from transient data"
teoria: [chiusura-rl, costante-di-tempo]

An ideal coil connected in series with a resistor and an ideal source of emf $3.0\,\mathrm{V}$ reaches a steady-state current $0.60\,\mathrm{A}$. The measured time constant is $8.0\,\mathrm{ms}$. Calculate the resistance of the resistor and the inductance of the coil.

:::soluzione
$R = \dfrac{\mathcal{E}}{I} = 5.0\,\mathrm{\Omega}$, $L = R\,\tau = 4.0 \cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{H}$
:::
