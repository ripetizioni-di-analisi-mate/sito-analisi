Between the pole pieces of an electromagnet, a uniform magnetic field of $0.20\,\mathrm{T}$ occupies a volume of $50\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ in air ($\mu_0 = 4\pi \cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\cdot m/A}$). Calculate the magnetic energy density and the energy contained in that volume.

:::soluzione
$u_B = \dfrac{B^2}{2\mu_0} = 1.6 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{J/m^3}$, $U = u_B V = 0.80\,\mathrm{J}$
:::
