After the source is disconnected, a coil forms a closed circuit of total resistance $25\,\mathrm{\Omega}$. The current falls to one-quarter of its initial value in $12\,\mathrm{ms}$. Determine the time constant and the inductance of the coil.

:::soluzione
$\tau = \dfrac{12\,\mathrm{ms}}{\ln 4} = 8.7\,\mathrm{ms}$, $L = R\,\tau = 0.22\,\mathrm{H}$
:::
