At the center of a long air-core solenoid with $1200$ turns per meter is a coaxial coil with $80$ turns of radius $1.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ ($\mu_0 = 4\pi \cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\cdot m/A}$). Calculate the mutual inductance and the magnitude of the emf induced in the coil when the solenoid current changes by $150\,\mathrm{A}$ per second.

:::soluzione
$M = \mu_0 n_1 N_2 \pi r_2^2 = 3.8 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{H}$, $|\mathcal{E}_2| = 5.7 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{V}$
:::
