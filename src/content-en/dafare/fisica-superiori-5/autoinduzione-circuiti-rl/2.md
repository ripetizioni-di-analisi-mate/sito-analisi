titolo: "Self-induced emf as current decreases"
teoria: [fem-autoindotta, verso-fem-autoindotta]

---

In a coil of inductance $L = 0.12\,\mathrm{H}$, the current decreases uniformly from $2.0\,\mathrm{A}$ to $0$ in $40\,\mathrm{ms}$. Calculate the self-induced emf and explain whether it acts in the same direction as the current or in the opposite direction.

:::soluzione
$\mathcal{E}_L = 6.0\,\mathrm{V}$, in the same direction as the current.
:::
