An ideal source of emf $4.5\,\mathrm{V}$, a $1.5\,\mathrm{\Omega}$ resistor, and an ideal $30\,\mathrm{mH}$ inductor are connected in series. The switch closes at time $t = 0$. Calculate the time constant, the steady-state current, and the current at $t = 30\,\mathrm{ms}$.

:::soluzione
$\tau = \dfrac{L}{R} = 20\,\mathrm{ms}$, $I = \dfrac{\mathcal{E}}{R} = 3.0\,\mathrm{A}$, $i(30\,\mathrm{ms}) = 3.0 \left( 1 - e^{-1.5} \right)\,\mathrm{A} = 2.3\,\mathrm{A}$
:::
