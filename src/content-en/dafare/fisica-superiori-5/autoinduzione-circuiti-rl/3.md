---
titolo: "Inductance of a small solenoid"
teoria: [induttanza-solenoide]
parole: ["inductance of a solenoid", "turns", "length", "radius"]
---

An air-core solenoid of length $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ consists of $300$ turns of radius $8.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ and can be treated as long compared with its radius ($\mu_0 = 4\pi \cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\cdot m/A}$). Calculate its inductance.

:::soluzione
$L = \dfrac{\mu_0 N^2 \pi r^2}{\ell} = 1.9 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{H}$
:::
