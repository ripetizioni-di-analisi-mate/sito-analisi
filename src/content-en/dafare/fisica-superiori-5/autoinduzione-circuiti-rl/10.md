---
titolo: "Current for a specified stored energy"
teoria: [energia-induttore]
parole: ["energy stored in an inductor", "current", "inductance"]
---

An inductor of inductance $0.80\,\mathrm{H}$ must store an energy $0.90\,\mathrm{J}$. Calculate the required current.

:::soluzione
$I = \sqrt{\dfrac{2U}{L}} = 1.5\,\mathrm{A}$
:::
