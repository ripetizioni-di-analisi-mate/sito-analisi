An ideal $0.30\,\mathrm{H}$ inductor carries a current $2.0\,\mathrm{A}$. Calculate the stored energy. At time $t = 0$ the inductor is connected across a $15\,\mathrm{\Omega}$ resistor. Determine how long it takes for the stored energy to fall by half.

:::soluzione
$U = 0.60\,\mathrm{J}$, $t = \dfrac{\tau \ln 2}{2} = 6.9\,\mathrm{ms}$, with $\tau = \dfrac{L}{R} = 20\,\mathrm{ms}$
:::
