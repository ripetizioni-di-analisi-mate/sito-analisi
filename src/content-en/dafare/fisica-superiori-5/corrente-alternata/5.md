An ideal $0.20\,\mathrm{H}$ inductor is connected to a source of rms voltage $230\,\mathrm{V}$ and frequency $50\,\mathrm{Hz}$. Calculate the inductive reactance and the rms current.

:::soluzione
$X_L = 2\pi f L \approx 62.8\,\Omega$, $I_{\text{eff}} = \dfrac{V_{\text{eff}}}{X_L} \approx 3.66\,\mathrm{A}$.
:::
