teoria: [alternatore, grandezze-alternate]

A coil of $60$ turns, each of area $5.0 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^2}$, rotates in a uniform magnetic field perpendicular to its axis of rotation and produces a sinusoidal emf with frequency $40\,\mathrm{Hz}$ and peak value $12\,\mathrm{V}$. Determine the magnitude of the magnetic field.

:::soluzione
$B = \dfrac{\mathcal{E}_0}{N S \cdot 2\pi f} \approx 0.16\,\mathrm{T}$.
:::
