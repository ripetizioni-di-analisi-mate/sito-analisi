A coil of $80$ turns, each of area $0.030\,\mathrm{m^2}$, rotates at $25$ revolutions per second in a uniform magnetic field of $0.10\,\mathrm{T}$ about an axis perpendicular to the field. Calculate the peak induced emf.

:::soluzione
$\mathcal{E}_0 = N B S \cdot 2\pi f \approx 38\,\mathrm{V}$.
:::
