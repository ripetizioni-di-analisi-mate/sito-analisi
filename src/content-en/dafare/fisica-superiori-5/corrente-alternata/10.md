A series $RLC$ circuit has $L = 0.15\,\mathrm{H}$ and $C = 2.2\,\mu\mathrm{F}$. Calculate the resonant frequency.

:::soluzione
$f_0 = \dfrac{1}{2\pi\sqrt{LC}} \approx 277\,\mathrm{Hz}$.
:::
