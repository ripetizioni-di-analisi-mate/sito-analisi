A line of resistance $2.0\,\Omega$ transmits $1.0\,\mathrm{MW}$ of power at rms voltage $50\,\mathrm{kV}$ and power factor $1$. Calculate the line current and the power dissipated in the wires.

:::soluzione
$I = \dfrac{P}{V} = 20\,\mathrm{A}$, $P_{\text{loss}} = R_\ell I^2 = 800\,\mathrm{W}$.
:::
