---
titolo: Capacitance for resonance at the grid frequency
teoria: [risonanza]
parole: [resonance, capacitance, grid frequency]
---

A series $RLC$ circuit contains a $0.80\,\mathrm{H}$ inductor and is supplied by the grid at $50\,\mathrm{Hz}$. Determine the capacitance required for resonance.

:::soluzione
$C = \dfrac{1}{4\pi^2 f^2 L} \approx 1.3 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{F} = 13\,\mu\mathrm{F}$.
:::
