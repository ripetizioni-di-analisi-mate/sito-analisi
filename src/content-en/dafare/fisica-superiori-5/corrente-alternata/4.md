titolo: Peak current in a light bulb

A $100\,\mathrm{W}$ incandescent light bulb operates at a grid voltage of $230\,\mathrm{V}$. Treat it as a resistor and calculate its peak current and its resistance.

:::soluzione
$I_0 = \sqrt{2}\,\dfrac{P}{V_{\text{eff}}} \approx 0.61\,\mathrm{A}$, $R = \dfrac{V_{\text{eff}}^2}{P} = 529\,\Omega$.
:::
