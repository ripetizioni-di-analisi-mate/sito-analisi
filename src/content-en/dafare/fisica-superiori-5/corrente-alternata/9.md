A series $RLC$ circuit with $R = 100\,\Omega$, $L = 0.50\,\mathrm{H}$, and $C = 40\,\mu\mathrm{F}$ is connected to the power grid, with $V_{\text{eff}} = 230\,\mathrm{V}$ and $f = 50\,\mathrm{Hz}$. Calculate the impedance, rms current, and phase difference, and explain whether the circuit is inductive or capacitive.

:::soluzione
$Z \approx 127\,\Omega$, $I_{\text{eff}} \approx 1.82\,\mathrm{A}$, $\varphi \approx 38^\circ$. The circuit is inductive because $X_L \approx 157\,\Omega$ exceeds $X_C \approx 79.6\,\Omega$.
:::
