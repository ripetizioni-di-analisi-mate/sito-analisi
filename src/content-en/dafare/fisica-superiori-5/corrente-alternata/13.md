An ideal transformer has a primary of $500$ turns and a secondary of $25$ turns. The primary is connected to the grid at $230\,\mathrm{V}$, and the secondary supplies a device drawing an rms current of $4.0\,\mathrm{A}$. Calculate the secondary rms voltage and the primary rms current.

:::soluzione
$V_2 = V_1\,\dfrac{N_2}{N_1} = 11.5\,\mathrm{V}$, $I_1 = I_2\,\dfrac{N_2}{N_1} = 0.20\,\mathrm{A}$.
:::
