A $0.10\,\mu\mathrm{F}$ capacitor charged to $20\,\mathrm{V}$ is connected to an ideal $10\,\mathrm{mH}$ inductor in a circuit of negligible resistance. Calculate the oscillation frequency and the peak current in the circuit.

:::soluzione
$f_0 = \dfrac{1}{2\pi\sqrt{LC}} \approx 5.0\,\mathrm{kHz}$, $I_0 = \dfrac{C V_0}{\sqrt{LC}} \approx 63\,\mathrm{mA}$.
:::
