A $33\,\mu\mathrm{F}$ capacitor is connected to the grid, with $V_{\text{eff}} = 230\,\mathrm{V}$ and $f = 50\,\mathrm{Hz}$. Calculate the capacitive reactance and the rms current. Explain whether the current leads or lags the voltage.

:::soluzione
$X_C = \dfrac{1}{2\pi f C} \approx 96.5\,\Omega$, $I_{\text{eff}} \approx 2.38\,\mathrm{A}$. The current leads the voltage by $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.
:::
