A $60\,\Omega$ resistor, an inductor of reactance $80\,\Omega$, and a source of rms voltage $50\,\mathrm{V}$ are connected in series. Calculate the impedance, rms current, and phase difference between voltage and current.

:::soluzione
$Z = 100\,\Omega$, $I_{\text{eff}} = 0.50\,\mathrm{A}$, $\varphi = \arctan \dfrac{4}{3} \approx 53^\circ$, with the current lagging the voltage.
:::
