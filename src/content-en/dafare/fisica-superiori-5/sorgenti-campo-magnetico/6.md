A solenoid of length $50\,\mathrm{cm}$ has $1000$ turns carrying current $4.0\,\mathrm{A}$. Calculate the magnetic field inside it. ($\mu_0 = 4\pi \cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\cdot m/A}$)

:::soluzione
$B = \mu_0\,\dfrac{N}{\ell}\,I \approx 1.0 \cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{T}$
:::
