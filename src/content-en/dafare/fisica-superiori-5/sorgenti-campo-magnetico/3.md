---
titolo: Zero-field point between two wires
teoria: [legge-biot-savart, sovrapposizione-fili]
parole: ["superposition principle", "zero field", "parallel wires"]
---

Two infinitely long, straight, parallel wires are $8.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ apart and carry currents $I_1 = 3.0\,\mathrm{A}$ and $I_2 = 9.0\,\mathrm{A}$ in the same direction. Find the point on the line joining the wires where the magnetic field is zero.

:::soluzione
Between the wires, at a distance $2.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the wire carrying $3.0\,\mathrm{A}$ (and $6.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the other wire).
:::
