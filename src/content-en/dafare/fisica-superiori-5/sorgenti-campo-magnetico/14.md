A long solenoid has $800$ turns per meter carrying current $0.50\,\mathrm{A}$. Calculate the magnetic field inside when it is empty and when it contains an iron core of relative magnetic permeability $\mu_r = 2000$. Explain which class of materials iron belongs to. ($\mu_0 = 4\pi \cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\cdot m/A}$)

:::soluzione
$B_0 = \mu_0\, n I \approx 5.0 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{T}$; $\quad B = \mu_r B_0 \approx 1.0\,\mathrm{T}$. Iron is a ferromagnetic material ($\mu_r \gg 1$).
:::
