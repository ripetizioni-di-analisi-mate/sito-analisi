---
titolo: Current in a wire from the measured field
teoria: [legge-biot-savart]
parole: ["Biot–Savart law", "current", "straight wire"]
---

At a distance $6.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ from a very long straight wire, the measured magnetic field is $5.0 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{T}$. Find the current in the wire. ($\mu_0 = 4\pi \cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\cdot m/A}$)

:::soluzione
$I = \dfrac{2\pi r B}{\mu_0} = 15\,\mathrm{A}$
:::
