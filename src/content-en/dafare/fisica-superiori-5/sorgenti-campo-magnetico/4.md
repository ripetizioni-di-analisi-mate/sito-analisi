titolo: Field between two wires carrying opposite currents
teoria: [legge-biot-savart, sovrapposizione-fili, simboli-entrante-uscente]

Two infinitely long, straight, parallel wires a distance $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ apart carry currents of $5.0\,\mathrm{A}$ in opposite directions. Calculate the magnetic field at the midpoint of the segment joining the wires and explain why the two contributions add. ($\mu_0 = 4\pi \cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\cdot m/A}$)

:::soluzione
$B = 2 \cdot \dfrac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r} \approx 3.3 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{T}$, because between wires carrying opposite currents, the two fields point in the same direction.
:::
