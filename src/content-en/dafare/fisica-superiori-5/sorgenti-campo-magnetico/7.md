Two infinitely long, straight, parallel wires a distance $3.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ apart carry currents $15\,\mathrm{A}$ and $10\,\mathrm{A}$ in the same direction. Calculate the force per unit length between the wires and state whether it is attractive or repulsive. ($\mu_0 = 4\pi \cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\cdot m/A}$)

:::soluzione
$\dfrac{F}{\ell} = \dfrac{\mu_0 I_1 I_2}{2\pi d} = 1.0 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{N/m}$, attractive.
:::
