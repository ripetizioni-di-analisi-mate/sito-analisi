---
titolo: Field at the center of a loop
teoria: [campo-spira]
parole: ["circular loop", "field at the center"]
---

A circular loop of radius $4.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ carries current $3.0\,\mathrm{A}$. Calculate the magnetic field at its center. ($\mu_0 = 4\pi \cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\cdot m/A}$)

:::soluzione
$B = \dfrac{\mu_0 I}{2R} \approx 4.7 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{T}$
:::
