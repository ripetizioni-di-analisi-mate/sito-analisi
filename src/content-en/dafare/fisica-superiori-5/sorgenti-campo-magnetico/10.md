---
titolo: Flux through a closed surface
teoria: [teorema-gauss-magnetismo, flusso-campo-magnetico]
parole: ["Gauss’s law for magnetism", "closed surface", "flux"]
---

A closed cylindrical surface is placed in a nonuniform magnetic field. With the normal pointing outward, the flux through one base is $-3.2 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{Wb}$ and the flux through the other is $1.1 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{Wb}$. Find the flux through the curved surface.

:::soluzione
$\Phi_{\text{lat}} = 3.2 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{Wb} - 1.1 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{Wb} = 2.1 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{Wb}$ (outward), because the total flux is zero.
:::
