An infinitely long straight wire of radius $5.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries current $25\,\mathrm{A}$ distributed uniformly over its cross section. Calculate the magnetic field at distances $2.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $1.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the axis of the wire. ($\mu_0 = 4\pi \cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\cdot m/A}$)

:::soluzione
$B(2.0\,\mathrm{mm}) = \dfrac{\mu_0 I r}{2\pi R^2} = 4.0 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{T}$; $\quad B(1.0\,\mathrm{cm}) = \dfrac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r} = 5.0 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{T}$
:::
