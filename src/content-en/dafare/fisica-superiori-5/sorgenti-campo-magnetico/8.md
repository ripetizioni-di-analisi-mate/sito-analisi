Two infinitely long, straight, parallel wires a distance $20\,\mathrm{cm}$ apart carry currents of the same magnitude $I$ and repel each other with a force of $4.5 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{N}$ per meter of length. Find $I$ and the relative directions of the currents. ($\mu_0 = 4\pi \cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\cdot m/A}$)

:::soluzione
$I = \sqrt{\dfrac{2\pi d}{\mu_0} \cdot \dfrac{F}{\ell}} \approx 6.7\,\mathrm{A}$, with currents in opposite directions.
:::
