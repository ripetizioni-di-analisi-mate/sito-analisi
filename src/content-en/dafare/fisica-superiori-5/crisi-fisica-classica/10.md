The calcium cathode of a photocell ($W_0 = 2.90\,\mathrm{eV}$) is illuminated by ultraviolet light of wavelength $\lambda = 300\,\mathrm{nm}$. Calculate the stopping potential. Use $h = 6.63 \cdot 10^{-34}\,\mathrm{J\cdot s}$, $c = 3.00 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and $e = 1.60 \cdot 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}$.

:::soluzione
$V_0 = \dfrac{1}{e}\left( \dfrac{hc}{\lambda} - W_0 \right) \approx 1.24\,\mathrm{V}$.
:::
