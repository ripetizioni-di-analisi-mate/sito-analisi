---
titolo: Momentum of an ultraviolet photon
teoria: [quantita-di-moto-fotone]
parole: [photon momentum, ultraviolet]
---

Calculate the momentum of an ultraviolet photon of wavelength $\lambda = 250\,\mathrm{nm}$. Use $h = 6.63 \cdot 10^{-34}\,\mathrm{J\cdot s}$.

:::soluzione
$p = \dfrac{h}{\lambda} \approx 2.65 \cdot 10^{-27}\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$.
:::
