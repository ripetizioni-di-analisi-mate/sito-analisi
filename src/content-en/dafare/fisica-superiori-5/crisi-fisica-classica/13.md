X-rays of wavelength $\lambda = 20.0\,\mathrm{pm}$ are scattered by free electrons initially at rest. Calculate the wavelength shift and the wavelength of the radiation scattered at $\theta = 60^\circ$. Use $h = 6.63 \cdot 10^{-34}\,\mathrm{J\cdot s}$, $m_e = 9.11 \cdot 10^{-31}\,\mathrm{kg}$, and $c = 3.00 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

:::soluzione
$\Delta\lambda = \dfrac{h}{m_e c}(1 - \cos 60^\circ) \approx 1.21\,\mathrm{pm}$ and $\lambda' \approx 21.2\,\mathrm{pm}$.
:::
