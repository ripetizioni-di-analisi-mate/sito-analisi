The cosmic background radiation that fills the universe has a blackbody spectrum at temperature $T = 2.7\,\mathrm{K}$. Calculate the peak wavelength and identify the corresponding band of the electromagnetic spectrum. Wien’s constant is $2.90 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m\cdot K}$.

:::soluzione
$\lambda_{\max} \approx 1.1 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m} = 1.1\,\mathrm{mm}$, in the microwave band.
:::
