titolo: Planck’s constant from two measurements

On a metal cathode the stopping potential is $V_1 = 0.30\,\mathrm{V}$ for light of frequency $f_1 = 5.5 \cdot 10^{14}\,\mathrm{Hz}$ and $V_2 = 1.13\,\mathrm{V}$ for light of frequency $f_2 = 7.5 \cdot 10^{14}\,\mathrm{Hz}$. Use the data to find Planck’s constant and the cathode work function in electronvolts. Use $e = 1.60 \cdot 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}$.

:::soluzione
$h = e\,\dfrac{V_2 - V_1}{f_2 - f_1} \approx 6.64 \cdot 10^{-34}\,\mathrm{J\cdot s}$ and $W_0 \approx 1.98\,\mathrm{eV}$.
:::
