titolo: Energy of a blue-light photon
teoria: [fotone, energia-fotone-ev]

Calculate the energy, in joules and electronvolts, of a blue-light photon with $\lambda = 450\,\mathrm{nm}$. Use $h = 6.63 \cdot 10^{-34}\,\mathrm{J\cdot s}$, $c = 3.00 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and $1\,\mathrm{eV} = 1.60 \cdot 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{J}$.

:::soluzione
$E = \dfrac{hc}{\lambda} \approx 4.42 \cdot 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{J} \approx 2.76\,\mathrm{eV}$.
:::
