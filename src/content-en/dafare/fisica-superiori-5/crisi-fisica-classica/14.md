A gamma-ray photon of wavelength $\lambda = 5.00\,\mathrm{pm}$ strikes a free electron initially at rest and is scattered backward ($\theta = 180^\circ$). Calculate the wavelength of the scattered photon and the electron’s kinetic energy in kiloelectronvolts. Use $h = 6.63 \cdot 10^{-34}\,\mathrm{J\cdot s}$, $m_e = 9.11 \cdot 10^{-31}\,\mathrm{kg}$, $c = 3.00 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and $1\,\mathrm{eV} = 1.60 \cdot 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{J}$.

:::soluzione
$\lambda' = \lambda + \dfrac{2h}{m_e c} \approx 9.85\,\mathrm{pm}$ and $K = \dfrac{hc}{\lambda} - \dfrac{hc}{\lambda'} \approx 122\,\mathrm{keV}$.
:::
