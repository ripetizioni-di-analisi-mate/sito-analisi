---
titolo: Finding a star’s temperature with Wien’s law
teoria: [legge-di-wien, temperatura-del-sole]
parole: [Wien’s law, stellar temperature, peak wavelength]
---

The spectrum of a star, treated as a blackbody, peaks at $\lambda_{\max} = 480\,\mathrm{nm}$. Determine the star’s surface temperature. Wien’s constant is $2.90 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m\cdot K}$.

:::soluzione
$T = \dfrac{2.90 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m\cdot K}}{\lambda_{\max}} \approx 6.04 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{K}$.
:::
