An atomic oscillator vibrates at frequency $f = 6.0 \cdot 10^{13}\,\mathrm{Hz}$. Using Planck’s hypothesis, calculate the oscillator’s energy quantum and the energy of its state with $n = 3$. Use $h = 6.63 \cdot 10^{-34}\,\mathrm{J\cdot s}$.

:::soluzione
$hf \approx 4.0 \cdot 10^{-20}\,\mathrm{J}$; $E_3 = 3hf \approx 1.2 \cdot 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
