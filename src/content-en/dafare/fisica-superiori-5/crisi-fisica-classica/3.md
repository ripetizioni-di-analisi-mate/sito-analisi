titolo: Doubling absolute temperature
teoria: [legge-stefan-boltzmann, legge-di-wien]

---

The absolute temperature of a blackbody increases from $300\,\mathrm{K}$ to $600\,\mathrm{K}$. Determine the factor by which the emitted power increases and the factor by which the peak emission wavelength decreases.

:::soluzione
The power increases by a factor of $2^4 = 16$, and the peak wavelength is halved.
:::
