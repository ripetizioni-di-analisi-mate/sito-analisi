Copper has a work function of $W_0 = 4.70\,\mathrm{eV}$. Determine its threshold frequency and threshold wavelength. Use $h = 6.63 \cdot 10^{-34}\,\mathrm{J\cdot s}$, $c = 3.00 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and $1\,\mathrm{eV} = 1.60 \cdot 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{J}$.

:::soluzione
$f_0 = \dfrac{W_0}{h} \approx 1.13 \cdot 10^{15}\,\mathrm{Hz}$ and $\lambda_0 = \dfrac{hc}{W_0} \approx 264\,\mathrm{nm}$.
:::
