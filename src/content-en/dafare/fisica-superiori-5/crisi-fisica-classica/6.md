---
titolo: Number of photons in one joule of red light
teoria: [fotone]
parole: [number of photons, photon energy, red light]
---

Determine how many red-light photons with $\lambda = 620\,\mathrm{nm}$ have a total energy of $1.0\,\mathrm{J}$. Use $h = 6.63 \cdot 10^{-34}\,\mathrm{J\cdot s}$ and $c = 3.00 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

:::soluzione
$N = \dfrac{1.0\,\mathrm{J}\cdot\lambda}{hc} \approx 3.1 \cdot 10^{18}$.
:::
