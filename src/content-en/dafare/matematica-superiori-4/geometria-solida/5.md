A regular tetrahedron has edge length $6$. Find its height, total surface area, and volume. The foot of its altitude is the centroid of its base.

:::soluzione
The height is $2\sqrt{6}$, the total surface area is $36\sqrt{3}$, and the volume is $18\sqrt{2}$.
:::
