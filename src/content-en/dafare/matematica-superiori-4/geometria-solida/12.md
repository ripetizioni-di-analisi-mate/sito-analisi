A sphere has volume $288\pi$.

a) Find the radius and surface area of the sphere.

b) Find the distance from the center to a plane that cuts the sphere in a disk of area $20\pi$.

:::soluzione
a) The radius is $6$ and the surface area is $144\pi$. b) The plane is at a distance of $4$ from the center.
:::
