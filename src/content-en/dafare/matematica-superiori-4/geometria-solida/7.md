titolo: Angle between the space diagonal of a rectangular prism and its base
teoria: [angolo-retta-piano, diagonale-parallelepipedo]

A rectangular prism has base dimensions $3$ and $4$ and height $12$. Find the angle its space diagonal makes with the plane of the base.

:::soluzione
$\theta = \arctan\dfrac{12}{5} \approx 67.4°$.
:::
