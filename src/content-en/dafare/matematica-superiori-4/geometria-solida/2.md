The dimensions of a rectangular prism are proportional to $1$, $2$, and $2$, and its space diagonal has length $12$. Find its dimensions, total surface area, and volume.

:::soluzione
The dimensions are $4$, $8$, and $8$. The total surface area is $256$ and the volume is $256$.
:::
