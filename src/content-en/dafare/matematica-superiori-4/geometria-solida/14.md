A sphere of radius $5$ has an inscribed cylinder of base radius $x$.

a) Express the height of the cylinder in terms of $x$, where $0 < x < 5$.

b) Find the volume of the cylinder for $x = 3$.

c) Find the value of $x$ for which the cylinder is equilateral and calculate its volume.

:::soluzione
a) $h = 2\sqrt{25 - x^2}$. b) $V = 72\pi$. c) $x = \dfrac{5\sqrt{2}}{2}$ and $V = \dfrac{125\sqrt{2}}{2}\,\pi$.
:::
