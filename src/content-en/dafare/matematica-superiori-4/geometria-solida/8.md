titolo: Distance from a point to a line in a plane
teoria: [tre-perpendicolari, distanze]
parole: [three perpendiculars theorem, distance from a point to a line]
---

Point $P$ is at a distance of $12$ from plane $\alpha$, and its projection $H$ onto $\alpha$ is at a distance of $5$ from line $r$ in $\alpha$. Find the distance from $P$ to line $r$.

:::soluzione
The distance is $13$.
:::
