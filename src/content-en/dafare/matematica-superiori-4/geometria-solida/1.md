A cube has a total surface area of $150\,\mathrm{cm}^2$. Find its edge length, space diagonal, and volume.

:::soluzione
The edge length is $5\,\mathrm{cm}$, the diagonal is $5\sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{cm}$, and the volume is $125\,\mathrm{cm}^3$.
:::
