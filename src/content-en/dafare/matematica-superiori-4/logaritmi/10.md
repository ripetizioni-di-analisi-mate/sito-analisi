teoria: [esponenziali-con-logaritmi, logaritmo-potenza]

---

Solve the equation $2^{x + 1} = 3^x$ and give an approximate value of the solution.

:::soluzione
$x = \dfrac{\ln 2}{\ln \frac{3}{2}} \approx 1.710$.
:::
