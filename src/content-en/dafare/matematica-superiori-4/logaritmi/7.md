---
titolo: An equation with a coefficient multiplying a logarithm
teoria: [equazioni-stessa-base, logaritmo-potenza]
parole: [logarithmic equation, logarithm of a power]
---

Solve the equation $2 \log_5 x = \log_5 (3x + 4)$.

:::soluzione
$x = 4$ (the value $x = -1$ does not satisfy the domain conditions).
:::
