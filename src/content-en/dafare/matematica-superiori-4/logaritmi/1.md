Use the definition to evaluate $\log_{\sqrt{2}} 8$ and $\log_{\frac{1}{5}} 125$.

:::soluzione
$\log_{\sqrt{2}} 8 = 6$ and $\log_{\frac{1}{5}} 125 = -3$.
:::
