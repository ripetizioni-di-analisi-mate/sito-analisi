---
titolo: Comparing two earthquakes
teoria: [scala-richter, logaritmo-quoziente]
parole: [Richter scale, magnitude, amplitude, energy]
---

The magnitude of an earthquake is $M = \log_{10} \dfrac{A}{A_0}$, where $A$ is the amplitude recorded by the seismograph and $A_0$ is a reference amplitude. The energy released $E$, in joules, satisfies $\log_{10} E = 4.8 + 1.5\, M$. Two earthquakes have magnitudes $6.2$ and $4.2$.

a) How many times as large is the amplitude recorded for the first earthquake as that recorded for the second?

b) How many times as much energy does the first earthquake release as the second?

:::soluzione
a) $100$ times; b) $10^3 = 1000$ times.
:::
