---
titolo: Inequalities with base less than 1
teoria: [disequazioni-logaritmiche, disequazioni-esponenziali]
parole: [logarithmic inequality, exponential inequality, direction of an inequality]
---

Solve the inequalities:

a) $\log_{\frac{1}{2}} (2x - 3) > -1$;

b) $\left( \dfrac{1}{2} \right)^x < 5$.

:::soluzione
a) $\dfrac{3}{2} < x < \dfrac{5}{2}$; b) $x > -\log_2 5$.
:::
