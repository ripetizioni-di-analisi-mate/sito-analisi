Given $\log_3 2 = a$, express the following in terms of $a$:

a) $\log_3 72$;

b) $\log_{12} 3$.

:::soluzione
a) $\log_3 72 = 3a + 2$; b) $\log_{12} 3 = \dfrac{1}{2a + 1}$.
:::
