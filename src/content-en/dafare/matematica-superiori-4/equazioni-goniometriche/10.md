titolo: Linear equation using the tangent half-angle formulas

---

Solve the equation $\cos x - \sin x = 1$ using the tangent half-angle substitution.

:::soluzione
$x = 2k\pi$ or $x = -\dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2k\pi$, with $k \in \mathbb{Z}$. The values $x = \pi + 2k\pi$ are not solutions.
:::
