---
titolo: A system of trigonometric equations
teoria: [sistemi-goniometrici, equazione-seno]
parole: [system, supplementary angles, sine]
---

Solve the system
$$
\begin{cases}
\sin x + \sin y = 1 \\[4pt]
x + y = \pi
\end{cases}
$$
with $x$ and $y$ in the interval $[0, \pi]$.

:::soluzione
The pairs $\left(\dfrac{\pi}{6}, \dfrac{5\pi}{6}\right)$ and $\left(\dfrac{5\pi}{6}, \dfrac{\pi}{6}\right)$.
:::
