---
titolo: Cosine equation with a linear argument
teoria: [equazione-coseno, argomento-lineare]
parole: [basic equation, cosine, linear argument]
---

Solve the equation $\sqrt{2}\cos\left(x - \dfrac{\pi}{4}\right) = 1$.

:::soluzione
$x = 2k\pi$ or $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2k\pi$, with $k \in \mathbb{Z}$.
:::
