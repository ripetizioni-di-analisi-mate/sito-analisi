Solve the equation $\sin (2x) = \cos x$ and give the solutions that lie in the interval $[0, 2\pi)$.

:::soluzione
$x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + k\pi$, $x = \dfrac{\pi}{6} + 2k\pi$, $x = \dfrac{5\pi}{6} + 2k\pi$. In $[0, 2\pi)$, the solutions are $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$, $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, $\dfrac{5\pi}{6}$, $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$.
:::
