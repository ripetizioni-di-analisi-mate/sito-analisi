---
titolo: A homogeneous quadratic equation
teoria: [equazioni-omogenee, equazione-tangente]
parole: [homogeneous equation, tangent]
---

Solve the equation $3\sin^2 x - 4\sin x\cos x + \cos^2 x = 0$.

:::soluzione
$x = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + k\pi$ or $x = \arctan\dfrac{1}{3} + k\pi$, with $k \in \mathbb{Z}$.
:::
