titolo: Tangent equation involving a half-angle

Write down the domain restrictions, then solve the equation $\tan\dfrac{x}{2} + 1 = 0$.

:::soluzione
Domain restrictions: $x \neq \pi + 2k\pi$. Solutions: $x = -\dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2k\pi$, with $k \in \mathbb{Z}$.
:::
