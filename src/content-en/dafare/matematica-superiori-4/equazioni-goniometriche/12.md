titolo: Number of solutions depending on a parameter
teoria: [discussione-parametro, equazione-coseno]

---

Given the equation $\cos x = 2m - 1$ in the unknown $x$, with real parameter $m$:

a) find the values of $m$ for which the equation has solutions;

b) determine how many solutions it has in the interval $[0, 2\pi)$ as $m$ varies.

:::soluzione
a) $0 \leq m \leq 1$. b) Two solutions for $0 < m < 1$, one solution for $m = 0$ ($x = \pi$) and for $m = 1$ ($x = 0$), and none for $m < 0$ or $m > 1$.
:::
