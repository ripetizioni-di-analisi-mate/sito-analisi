A point moves along a line with position given by $s(t) = 3\cos\dfrac{\pi t}{4}$, where $s$ is measured in centimeters and $t$ in seconds.

a) Find the times in the interval $[0, 8]$ when the point is at $s = \dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{cm}$.

b) Find the times in the same interval when the point passes through the position $s = 0$.

:::soluzione
a) $t = \dfrac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{s}$ and $t = \dfrac{20}{3}\,\mathrm{s}$. b) $t = 2\,\mathrm{s}$ and $t = 6\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
