Find the solutions of the equation $\sin\left(2x + \dfrac{\pi}{3}\right) = \sin x$ that lie in the interval $[0, 2\pi)$.

:::soluzione
$x = \dfrac{2\pi}{9}$, $x = \dfrac{8\pi}{9}$, $x = \dfrac{14\pi}{9}$, $x = \dfrac{5\pi}{3}$.
:::
