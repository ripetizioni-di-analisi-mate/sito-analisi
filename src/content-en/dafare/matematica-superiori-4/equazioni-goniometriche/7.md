---
titolo: Quadratic equation using the Pythagorean identity
teoria: [sostituzione, equazione-coseno]
parole: [Pythagorean identity, substitution, inadmissible values]
---

Solve the equation $2\sin^2 x + 3\cos x = 0$.

:::soluzione
$x = \pm\dfrac{2\pi}{3} + 2k\pi$, with $k \in \mathbb{Z}$ (the value $\cos x = 2$ is inadmissible).
:::
