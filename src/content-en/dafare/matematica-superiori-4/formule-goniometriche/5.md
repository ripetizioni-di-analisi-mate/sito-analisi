titolo: Sine and cosine of twice an angle
teoria: [duplicazione]
parole: [double-angle formulas, double angle, quadrant]
---

Given $\cos\alpha = -\dfrac{1}{3}$ with $\pi < \alpha < \dfrac{3\pi}{2}$, calculate $\sin 2\alpha$, $\cos 2\alpha$, and $\tan 2\alpha$.

:::soluzione
$\sin 2\alpha = \dfrac{4\sqrt{2}}{9}$, $\cos 2\alpha = -\dfrac{7}{9}$, $\tan 2\alpha = -\dfrac{4\sqrt{2}}{7}$.
:::
