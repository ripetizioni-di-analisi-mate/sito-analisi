titolo: Verifying an identity using the double-angle formulas

Verify the identity
$$
\frac{1 - \cos 2x}{\sin 2x} = \tan x
$$
and state the values of $x$ for which it holds.

:::soluzione
The left-hand side is $\dfrac{2\sin^2 x}{2\sin x\cos x} = \tan x$. The identity holds for $x \neq k\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.
:::
