---
titolo: Cosine of three times an angle
teoria: [duplicazione, coseno-somma]
parole: [triple angle, double-angle formulas, addition formulas, proof]
---

a) Prove that $\cos 3\alpha = 4\cos^3\alpha - 3\cos\alpha$ for every real $\alpha$ by writing $3\alpha = 2\alpha + \alpha$.

b) Find $\cos 3\alpha$ given that $\cos\alpha = \dfrac{1}{3}$.

:::soluzione
a) $\cos 3\alpha = \cos 2\alpha\cos\alpha - \sin 2\alpha\sin\alpha = \left( 2\cos^2\alpha - 1 \right)\cos\alpha - 2\left( 1 - \cos^2\alpha \right)\cos\alpha = 4\cos^3\alpha - 3\cos\alpha$. b) $\cos 3\alpha = -\dfrac{23}{27}$.
:::
