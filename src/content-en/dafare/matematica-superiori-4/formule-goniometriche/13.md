Write $f(x) = \sqrt{3}\sin x - \cos x + 1$ in the form $r\sin(x + \varphi) + 1$ and find the maximum and minimum of $f$ and the points where they occur.

:::soluzione
$f(x) = 2\sin\left( x - \dfrac{\pi}{6} \right) + 1$. The maximum $3$ occurs at $x = \dfrac{2\pi}{3} + 2k\pi$ and the minimum $-1$ at $x = \dfrac{5\pi}{3} + 2k\pi$.
:::
