titolo: Rewriting products as sums using Werner's formulas
teoria: [werner, valori-non-notevoli]

---

a) Rewrite the expression $2\cos 3x\cos x$ as a sum.

b) Find the exact value of $\sin\dfrac{\pi}{8}\cos\dfrac{3\pi}{8}$.

:::soluzione
a) $2\cos 3x\cos x = \cos 4x + \cos 2x$. b) $\sin\dfrac{\pi}{8}\cos\dfrac{3\pi}{8} = \dfrac{2 - \sqrt{2}}{4}$.
:::
