Given $\tan\dfrac{x}{2} = -2$, find $\sin x$, $\cos x$, and $\tan x$.

:::soluzione
$\sin x = -\dfrac{4}{5}$, $\cos x = -\dfrac{3}{5}$, $\tan x = \dfrac{4}{3}$.
:::
