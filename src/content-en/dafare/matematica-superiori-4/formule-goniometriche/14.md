A rectangle is inscribed in a circle of radius $2$. Let $x$ be the angle a diagonal makes with the base, with $0 < x < \dfrac{\pi}{2}$.

a) Express the area $S(x)$ and the perimeter $p(x)$ as functions of $x$, using the double-angle formula for the area and the auxiliary angle for the perimeter.

b) Find the maximum area and maximum perimeter, and the values of $x$ at which they occur.

:::soluzione
a) $S(x) = 8\sin 2x$ and $p(x) = 8\sin x + 8\cos x = 8\sqrt{2}\sin\left( x + \dfrac{\pi}{4} \right)$. b) The maximum area is $8$ and the maximum perimeter is $8\sqrt{2}$. Both occur at $x = \dfrac{\pi}{4}$, that is, when the rectangle is a square.
:::
