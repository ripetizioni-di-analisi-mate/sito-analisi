titolo: Sine of a difference and cosine of a sum using quadrants
teoria: [seno-somma-differenza, coseno-somma]
parole: [addition formulas, quadrant, Pythagorean identity]

Given that $\sin\alpha = \dfrac{12}{13}$ with $\dfrac{\pi}{2} < \alpha < \pi$ and $\cos\beta = \dfrac{3}{5}$ with $\dfrac{3\pi}{2} < \beta < 2\pi$, calculate $\sin(\alpha - \beta)$ and $\cos(\alpha + \beta)$.

:::soluzione
$\sin(\alpha - \beta) = \dfrac{16}{65}$ and $\cos(\alpha + \beta) = \dfrac{33}{65}$.
:::
