Calculate $\sin\dfrac{\pi}{12}$ using the half-angle formula and show that the result equals $\dfrac{\sqrt{6} - \sqrt{2}}{4}$.

:::soluzione
$\sin\dfrac{\pi}{12} = \dfrac{\sqrt{2 - \sqrt{3}}}{2}$. Both numbers are positive and have the same square $\dfrac{2 - \sqrt{3}}{4}$, so they are equal.
:::
