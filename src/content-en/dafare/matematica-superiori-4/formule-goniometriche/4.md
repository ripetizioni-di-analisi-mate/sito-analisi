titolo: Tangent of a sum and of a difference

Given $\tan\alpha = 3$ and $\tan\beta = \dfrac{1}{2}$, calculate $\tan(\alpha + \beta)$ and $\tan(\alpha - \beta)$. Deduce the measure of the acute angle between the lines $y = 3x$ and $y = \dfrac{x}{2}$.

:::soluzione
$\tan(\alpha + \beta) = -7$ and $\tan(\alpha - \beta) = 1$. The acute angle between the two lines measures $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$.
:::
