titolo: A difference and a sum of cosines using sum-to-product formulas

---

a) Rewrite the expression $\cos 5x - \cos x$ as a product.

b) Find the exact value of $\cos 75^\circ + \cos 15^\circ$.

:::soluzione
a) $\cos 5x - \cos x = -2\sin 3x\sin 2x$. b) $\cos 75^\circ + \cos 15^\circ = \dfrac{\sqrt{6}}{2}$.
:::
