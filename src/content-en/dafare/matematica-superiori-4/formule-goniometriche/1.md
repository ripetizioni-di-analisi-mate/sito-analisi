Find the exact value of $\cos\dfrac{13\pi}{12}$ by writing the angle as the sum of $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$ and $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$.

:::soluzione
$\cos\dfrac{13\pi}{12} = -\dfrac{\sqrt{6} + \sqrt{2}}{4}$.
:::
