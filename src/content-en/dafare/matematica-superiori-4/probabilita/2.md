titolo: "A multiple of three as the sum of two dice"
teoria: [esperimento-aleatorio, probabilita-classica, somma-incompatibili]
parole: ["rolling two dice", "sum", "multiple"]

Two fair dice are rolled. Find the probability that the sum of the two numbers is a multiple of $3$.

:::soluzione
$\dfrac{12}{36} = \dfrac{1}{3}$.
:::
