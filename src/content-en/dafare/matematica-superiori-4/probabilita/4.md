---
titolo: "Tossing three coins"
teoria: [probabilita-classica, evento-contrario]
parole: ["coin tosses", "heads and tails"]
---

A fair coin is tossed three times. Find:

a) the probability of obtaining at least two tails;

b) the probability of obtaining exactly one head.

:::soluzione
a) $\dfrac{1}{2}$; b) $\dfrac{3}{8}$.
:::
