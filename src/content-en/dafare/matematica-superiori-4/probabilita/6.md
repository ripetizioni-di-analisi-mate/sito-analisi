teoria: [estrazioni-senza-reimmissione]

An urn contains $5$ red balls, $4$ blue balls, and $3$ green balls. Draw $3$ balls at the same time. Find the probability that they are of three different colors.

:::soluzione
$\dfrac{60}{220} = \dfrac{3}{11}$.
:::
