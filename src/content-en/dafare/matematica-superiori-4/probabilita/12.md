titolo: "The production line of a defective part"

Three production lines $L_1$, $L_2$, and $L_3$ produce $25\%$, $35\%$, and $40\%$ of a factory’s parts, respectively. The percentages of defective parts are $4\%$ for $L_1$, $2\%$ for $L_2$, and $1\%$ for $L_3$. A part is selected at random and found to be defective. Find the probability that it came from line $L_1$.

:::soluzione
$P(D) = 0.021$ and $P(L_1 \mid D) = \dfrac{10}{21}$.
:::
