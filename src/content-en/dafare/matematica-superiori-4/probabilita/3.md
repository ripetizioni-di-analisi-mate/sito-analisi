---
titolo: "Cards that are neither face cards nor swords"
teoria: [evento-contrario, teorema-somma, operazioni-eventi]
parole: ["Neapolitan cards", "complement", "face cards"]
---

Draw a card from a deck of $40$ Neapolitan cards. Find the probability that it is neither a face card nor a sword.

:::soluzione
$\dfrac{21}{40}$.
:::
