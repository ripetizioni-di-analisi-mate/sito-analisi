Three shooters hit a target independently of one another with probabilities $0.6$, $0.5$, and $0.4$. Each takes one shot. Find:

a) the probability that the target is hit exactly once;

b) the probability that the target is hit at least once.

:::soluzione
a) $0.38$; b) $0.88$.
:::
