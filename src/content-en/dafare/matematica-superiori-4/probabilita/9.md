---
titolo: "A high sum given that one die shows a six"
teoria: [probabilita-condizionata, condizionata-equiprobabili]
parole: ["conditional probability", "two dice"]
---

Two fair dice are rolled and at least one of them shows $6$. Find the probability that their sum is at least $10$.

:::soluzione
$\dfrac{5}{11}$.
:::
