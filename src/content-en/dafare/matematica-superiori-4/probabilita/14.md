---
titolo: "An urn with an unknown number of balls"
teoria: [teorema-prodotto, estrazioni-senza-reimmissione]
parole: ["parameter problem", "urn", "quadratic equation"]
---

An urn contains $n$ white balls and $2$ black balls. Two balls are drawn without replacement.

a) Find $n$ so that the probability of drawing one white ball and one black ball in either order is $\dfrac{8}{15}$.

b) Using that value, find the probability of drawing two black balls.

:::soluzione
a) $n = 4$ (the solution $n = \dfrac{1}{2}$ of the equation $2n^2 - 9n + 4 = 0$ is not an integer); b) $\dfrac{1}{15}$.
:::
