---
titolo: "Even numbers in five rolls"
teoria: [schema-bernoulli, bernoulli-almeno-uno]
parole: ["Bernoulli model", "repeated trials", "die"]
---

A fair die is rolled $5$ times. Find:

a) the probability of rolling an even number exactly $2$ times;

b) the probability of rolling an even number at least once.

:::soluzione
a) $\dfrac{5}{16}$; b) $\dfrac{31}{32}$.
:::
