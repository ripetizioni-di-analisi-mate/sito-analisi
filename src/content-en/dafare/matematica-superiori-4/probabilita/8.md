For two events $A$ and $B$, we know that $P(A) = \dfrac{1}{3}$, $P(B) = \dfrac{1}{4}$, and $P(A \cup B) = \dfrac{1}{2}$. Find $P(A \cap B)$ and $P(A \mid B)$ and determine whether $A$ and $B$ are independent.

:::soluzione
$P(A \cap B) = \dfrac{1}{12}$ and $P(A \mid B) = \dfrac{1}{3}$. The events are independent since $P(A \mid B) = P(A)$.
:::
