titolo: "The same color with and without replacement"
teoria: [estrazioni-con-reimmissione, estrazioni-senza-reimmissione, teorema-prodotto]

An urn contains $7$ white balls and $3$ black balls. Two balls are drawn. Find the probability that they are the same color:

a) with replacement;

b) without replacement.

:::soluzione
a) $\dfrac{29}{50}$; b) $\dfrac{8}{15}$.
:::
