Find the domain of the function $f(x) = \ln(1 - 2\cos x) + \sqrt{\sin x}$.

:::soluzione
$D$: $\dfrac{\pi}{3} + 2k\pi < x \leq \pi + 2k\pi$, with $k \in \mathbb{Z}$.
:::
