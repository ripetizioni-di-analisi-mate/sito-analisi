---
titolo: Basic cosine inequality in an interval
teoria: [disequazioni-coseno, soluzioni-intervallo]
parole: [cosine inequality, specified interval]
---

Solve the inequality $2\cos x - 1 > 0$ in the interval $[0, 2\pi]$.

:::soluzione
$0 \leq x < \dfrac{\pi}{3}$ or $\dfrac{5\pi}{3} < x \leq 2\pi$.
:::
