A rectangle is inscribed in a circle of radius $1$, and its diagonal makes an angle $x$ with its base, where $0 < x < \dfrac{\pi}{2}$.

a) Express the sides, area, and perimeter of the rectangle in terms of $x$.

b) Find the values of $x$ for which the area is greater than or equal to $1$.

c) Find the values of $x$ for which the perimeter is greater than or equal to $2\sqrt{6}$.

:::soluzione
a) The sides are $2\cos x$ and $2\sin x$, the area is $2\sin 2x$, and the perimeter is $4(\sin x + \cos x)$. b) $\dfrac{\pi}{12} \leq x \leq \dfrac{5\pi}{12}$. c) $\dfrac{\pi}{12} \leq x \leq \dfrac{5\pi}{12}$, the same interval.
:::
