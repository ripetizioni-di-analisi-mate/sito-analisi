---
titolo: Cosine inequality with a half-angle argument
teoria: [argomento-trasformato, disequazioni-coseno]
parole: [transformed argument, period 4 pi]
---

Solve the inequality $\cos\dfrac{x}{2} \geq \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$.

:::soluzione
$-\dfrac{\pi}{2} + 4k\pi \leq x \leq \dfrac{\pi}{2} + 4k\pi$, with $k \in \mathbb{Z}$.
:::
