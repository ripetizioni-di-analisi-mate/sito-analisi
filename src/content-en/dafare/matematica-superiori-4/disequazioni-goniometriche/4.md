---
titolo: Sine inequality with a transformed argument
teoria: [argomento-trasformato, disequazioni-seno]
parole: [transformed argument, sine inequality]
---

Solve the inequality $\sin\left( 2x + \dfrac{\pi}{6} \right) > \dfrac{1}{2}$.

:::soluzione
$k\pi < x < \dfrac{\pi}{3} + k\pi$, with $k \in \mathbb{Z}$.
:::
