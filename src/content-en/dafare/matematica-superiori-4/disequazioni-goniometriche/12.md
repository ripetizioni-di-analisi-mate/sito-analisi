---
titolo: A system of trigonometric inequalities
teoria: [sistemi-disequazioni, disequazioni-seno, disequazioni-coseno]
parole: [system of inequalities, intersection]
---

Solve the system
$$
\begin{cases}
\sin x > 0 \\[4pt]
2\cos x + \sqrt{3} \geq 0
\end{cases}
$$

:::soluzione
$2k\pi < x \leq \dfrac{5\pi}{6} + 2k\pi$, with $k \in \mathbb{Z}$.
:::
