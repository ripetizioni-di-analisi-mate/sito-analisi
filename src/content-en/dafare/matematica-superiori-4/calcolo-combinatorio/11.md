---
titolo: "Proving a binomial coefficient identity"
teoria: [coefficiente-binomiale, legge-stifel]
parole: ["identity", "binomial coefficients", "proof"]
---

Prove that for every natural number $n \geq 2$,
$$
\binom{n}{2} + \binom{n + 1}{2} = n^2 .
$$

:::soluzione
The left-hand side equals $\dfrac{n \, (n - 1)}{2} + \dfrac{(n + 1) \, n}{2} = \dfrac{n \, (n - 1 + n + 1)}{2} = n^2$.
:::
