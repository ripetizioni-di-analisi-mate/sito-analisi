teoria: [termine-generale, binomio-newton]

---

In the expansion of $\left( 3x + \dfrac{2}{x} \right)^6$, with $x \neq 0$:

a) find the coefficient of $x^4$;

b) find the term independent of $x$.

:::soluzione
a) $2916$; b) $4320$.
:::
