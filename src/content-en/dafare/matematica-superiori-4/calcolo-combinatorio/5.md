titolo: "Anagrams of a word with distinct letters"
teoria: [permutazioni-semplici, numero-permutazioni]

Consider the anagrams of the word $NUMERO$, including arrangements that are not words.

a) Find how many there are.

b) Find how many begin with a vowel.

:::soluzione
a) $6! = 720$; b) $3 \cdot 5! = 360$.
:::
