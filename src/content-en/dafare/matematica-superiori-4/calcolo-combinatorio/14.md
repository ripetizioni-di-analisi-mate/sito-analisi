A class consists of $5$ boys and $6$ girls. A committee of $4$ students is to be formed.

a) Find the number of possible committees.

b) Find how many committees have $2$ boys and $2$ girls.

c) Find how many committees include at least one boy.

:::soluzione
a) $\dbinom{11}{4} = 330$; b) $\dbinom{5}{2} \dbinom{6}{2} = 150$; c) $330 - \dbinom{6}{4} = 315$.
:::
