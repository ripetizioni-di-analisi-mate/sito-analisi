A bakery sells $5$ types of pastries and has a large supply of each. Find the number of ways to fill a tray with $3$ pastries if their order on the tray does not matter and a type may be chosen more than once.

:::soluzione
$\dbinom{5 + 3 - 1}{3} = \dbinom{7}{3} = 35$ ways.
:::
