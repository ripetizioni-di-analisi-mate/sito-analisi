---
titolo: "Counting outfits with the fundamental counting principle"
teoria: [principio-fondamentale, diagramma-albero]
parole: ["fundamental counting principle", "outfits"]
---

Marco has $4$ T-shirts, $3$ pairs of pants, and $2$ pairs of shoes. Find the number of different outfits he can wear by choosing one T-shirt, one pair of pants, and one pair of shoes.

:::soluzione
$4 \cdot 3 \cdot 2 = 24$ ways.
:::
