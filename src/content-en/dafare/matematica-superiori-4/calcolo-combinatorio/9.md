---
titolo: "A polygon with a given number of diagonals"
teoria: [combinazioni-semplici, numero-combinazioni]

---

Identify the convex polygon that has $20$ diagonals.

:::soluzione
An octagon: $\dfrac{n \, (n - 3)}{2} = 20$ gives $n = 8$.
:::
