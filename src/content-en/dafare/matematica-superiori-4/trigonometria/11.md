---
titolo: Height of a bell tower from two observation points
teoria: [altezza-torre, angolo-elevazione, secondo-teorema-rettangoli]
parole: [angle of elevation, height, bell tower]
---

From point $A$, the top of a bell tower is seen at an angle of elevation of $30^\circ$. Moving $50\,\mathrm{m}$ toward the base of the tower along the same horizontal line increases the angle of elevation to $45^\circ$. Find the height of the bell tower, neglecting the observer's height.

:::soluzione
$h = 25\left( \sqrt{3} + 1 \right)\,\mathrm{m} \approx 68.30\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
