In triangle $ABC$, we have $b = 4$, $c = 6$, and $\alpha = 120^\circ$. Find side $a$ and the radius of the circumcircle.

:::soluzione
$a = 2\sqrt{19}$, $R = \dfrac{2\sqrt{57}}{3}$.
:::
