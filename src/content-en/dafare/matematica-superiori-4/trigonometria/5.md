In a circle of radius $8$, chord $AB$ corresponds to a central angle of $60^\circ$. Find the length of $AB$ and the inscribed angles subtended by $AB$.

:::soluzione
$AB = 8$. The inscribed angles are $30^\circ$ and $150^\circ$.
:::
