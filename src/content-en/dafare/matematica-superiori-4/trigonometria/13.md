titolo: Inclined plane and resultant of two forces

a) An object of mass $12\,\mathrm{kg}$ rests on a frictionless plane inclined at $30^\circ$. Find the components of its weight parallel and perpendicular to the plane, with $g = 9.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

b) Two forces of $5\,\mathrm{N}$ and $8\,\mathrm{N}$ acting at the same point make an angle of $60^\circ$. Find the magnitude of their resultant.

:::soluzione
a) $P_\parallel = 58.8\,\mathrm{N}$, $P_\perp = 58.8\sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{N} \approx 101.8\,\mathrm{N}$. b) $R = \sqrt{129}\,\mathrm{N} \approx 11.36\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
