Solve triangle $ABC$ with $a = 12$, $\alpha = 60^\circ$, and $\beta = 45^\circ$, and calculate the radius of its circumcircle. Use $\sin 75^\circ = \dfrac{\sqrt{6} + \sqrt{2}}{4}$.

:::soluzione
$\gamma = 75^\circ$, $b = 4\sqrt{6}$, $c = 6\sqrt{2} + 2\sqrt{6}$, $R = 4\sqrt{3}$.
:::
