To find the distances from a point $P$ to two points $A$ and $B$, with $AB = 80\,\mathrm{m}$, the angles $\widehat{PAB} = 60^\circ$ and $\widehat{PBA} = 75^\circ$ are measured. Calculate $AP$ and $BP$.

:::soluzione
$AP = 40\left( \sqrt{3} + 1 \right)\,\mathrm{m} \approx 109.28\,\mathrm{m}$, $BP = 40\sqrt{6}\,\mathrm{m} \approx 97.98\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
