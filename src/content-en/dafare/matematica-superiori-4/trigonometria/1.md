Solve triangle $ABC$, which has a right angle at $A$, given that its hypotenuse has length $a = 16$ and $\beta = 60^\circ$.

:::soluzione
$\gamma = 30^\circ$, $b = 8\sqrt{3}$, $c = 8$.
:::
