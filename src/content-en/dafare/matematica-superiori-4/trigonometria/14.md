Rectangle $ABCD$ is inscribed in a circle of radius $r$. Let $x = \widehat{CAB}$ be the angle between diagonal $AC$ and side $AB$.

a) Write down the restrictions on $x$ and express the side lengths in terms of $x$.

b) Find $x$ such that the area of the rectangle equals $\sqrt{3}\,r^2$.

:::soluzione
a) $0 < x < \dfrac{\pi}{2}$, $AB = 2r\cos x$, $BC = 2r\sin x$. b) $x = \dfrac{\pi}{6}$ or $x = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$.
:::
