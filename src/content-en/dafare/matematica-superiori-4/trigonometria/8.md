---
titolo: Classifying a triangle from its side lengths
teoria: [coseno-di-un-angolo, disuguaglianza-triangolare]
parole: [obtuse triangle, three sides, cosine of an angle]
---

Verify that a triangle exists with sides $6$, $8$, and $11$. Determine whether it is acute, right, or obtuse and calculate its largest angle to the nearest hundredth of a degree.

:::soluzione
The triangle exists and is obtuse. Its largest angle is $\arccos\left( -\dfrac{7}{32} \right) \approx 102.64^\circ$.
:::
