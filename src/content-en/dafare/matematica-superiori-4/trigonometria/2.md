titolo: Solving a right triangle given its legs
teoria: [secondo-teorema-rettangoli, risoluzione-rettangoli]

Triangle $ABC$ has a right angle at $A$ and legs $b = 8$ and $c = 15$. Find the hypotenuse and the acute angles, rounding the angles to the nearest hundredth of a degree.

:::soluzione
$a = 17$, $\beta = \arctan\dfrac{8}{15} \approx 28.07^\circ$, $\gamma \approx 61.93^\circ$.
:::
