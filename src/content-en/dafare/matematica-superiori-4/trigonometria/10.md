---
titolo: Ambiguous case with two solutions
teoria: [caso-ambiguo, teorema-seni, teorema-coseno]
parole: [ambiguous case, two triangles]
---

Solve triangle $ABC$ with $a = 4$, $b = 6$, and $\alpha = 30^\circ$. Round the angles to the nearest hundredth of a degree.

:::soluzione
Two triangles are possible, one with $\beta \approx 48.59^\circ$, $\gamma \approx 101.41^\circ$, and $c = 3\sqrt{3} + \sqrt{7}$, the other with $\beta \approx 131.41^\circ$, $\gamma \approx 18.59^\circ$, and $c = 3\sqrt{3} - \sqrt{7}$.
:::
