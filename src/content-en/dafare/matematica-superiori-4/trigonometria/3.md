Find the area of triangle $ABC$ with $a = 10$, $b = 6$, and $\gamma = 135^\circ$. Then find the altitude to side $a$.

:::soluzione
$S = 15\sqrt{2}$. The altitude to $a$ is $3\sqrt{2}$.
:::
