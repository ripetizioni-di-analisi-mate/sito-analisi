A regular octagon is inscribed in a circle of radius $4$. Find:

a) the area of the octagon.

b) the side length, given that $\sin\dfrac{\pi}{8} = \dfrac{\sqrt{2 - \sqrt{2}}}{2}$.

:::soluzione
a) $32\sqrt{2}$. b) $4\sqrt{2 - \sqrt{2}} \approx 3.06$.
:::
