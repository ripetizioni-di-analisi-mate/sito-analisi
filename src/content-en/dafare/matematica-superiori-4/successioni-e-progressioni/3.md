---
titolo: Proving a sum formula by induction
teoria: [principio-induzione, somma-primi-naturali, somma-quadrati]
parole: [principle of induction, sum formula]
---

Use induction to prove that, for every $n \geq 1$,
$$
1 \cdot 2 + 2 \cdot 3 + 3 \cdot 4 + \dots + n(n + 1) = \frac{n(n + 1)(n + 2)}{3} .
$$

:::soluzione
Base case: $2 = \dfrac{1 \cdot 2 \cdot 3}{3}$. Inductive step: $\dfrac{n(n + 1)(n + 2)}{3} + (n + 1)(n + 2) = \dfrac{(n + 1)(n + 2)(n + 3)}{3}$.
:::
