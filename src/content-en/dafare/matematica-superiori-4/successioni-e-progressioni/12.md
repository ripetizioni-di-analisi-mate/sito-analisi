A principal of $5000$ euros is invested at an annual compound interest rate of $2\%$.

1. Calculate the accumulated amount after $10$ years, rounded to the nearest cent.
2. Find the first year in which the accumulated amount exceeds $6000$ euros.

:::soluzione
1. $5000 \cdot 1.02^{10} \approx 6094.97$ euros.
2. After $10$ years, because $n > \dfrac{\ln 1.2}{\ln 1.02} \approx 9.2$.
:::
