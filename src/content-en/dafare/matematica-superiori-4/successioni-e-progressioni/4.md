---
titolo: Proving divisibility by induction
teoria: [principio-induzione]
parole: [principle of induction, divisibility]
---

Use induction to prove that, for every $n \geq 1$, the number $5^n - 1$ is divisible by $4$.

:::soluzione
Base case: $5 - 1 = 4$. Inductive step: if $5^n - 1 = 4m$, then $5^{n+1} - 1 = 5\left( 5^n - 1 \right) + 4 = 4\,(5m + 1)$.
:::
