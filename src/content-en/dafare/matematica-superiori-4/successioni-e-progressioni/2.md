---
titolo: A recursively defined sequence
teoria: [successione-ricorsiva, principio-induzione]
parole: [recursive sequence, induction, squares]
---

A sequence is defined recursively by
$$
\begin{cases}
a_1 = 1 \\[4pt]
a_{n+1} = a_n + 2n + 1
\end{cases}
$$
1. Calculate the first five terms.
2. Use induction to prove that $a_n = n^2$ for every $n \geq 1$.

:::soluzione
1. $1, 4, 9, 16, 25$.
2. Inductive step: $a_{n+1} = n^2 + 2n + 1 = (n + 1)^2$.
:::
