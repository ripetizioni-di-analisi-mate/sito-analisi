---
titolo: Consecutive terms involving a parameter
teoria: [media-aritmetica, media-geometrica]
parole: [parameter, arithmetic mean, geometric mean]
---

1. Find $k$ so that $k + 1$, $3k$, and $4k + 3$, in that order, are consecutive terms of an arithmetic progression.
2. Find $k$ so that $k$, $k + 4$, and $k + 16$, in that order, are consecutive terms of a geometric progression.

:::soluzione
1. $k = 4$, with terms $5, 12, 19$.
2. $k = 2$, with terms $2, 6, 18$.
:::
