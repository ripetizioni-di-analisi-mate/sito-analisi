In a geometric progression, $a_2 = 12$ and $a_5 = -96$. Find the common ratio and the first term, write the first six terms, and calculate their sum.

:::soluzione
$q = -2$, $a_1 = -6$. The terms are $-6, 12, -24, 48, -96, 192$ and their sum is $S_6 = 126$.
:::
