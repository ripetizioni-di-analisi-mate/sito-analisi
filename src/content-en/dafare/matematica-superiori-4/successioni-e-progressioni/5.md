In an arithmetic progression, $a_4 = 10$ and $a_9 = 25$. Find the common difference, the first term, the thirtieth term, and the sum of the first thirty terms.

:::soluzione
$d = 3$, $a_1 = 1$, $a_{30} = 88$, $S_{30} = 1335$.
:::
