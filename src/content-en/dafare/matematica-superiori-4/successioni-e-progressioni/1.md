titolo: Monotonicity and boundedness of a sequence
teoria: [termine-generale, successione-monotona, successione-limitata]
parole: [sequence, monotonicity, bounded sequence]
---

Given the sequence $a_n = \dfrac{2n - 1}{n + 1}$, with $n \geq 1$, answer the following questions.

1. Calculate the first four terms.
2. Prove that the sequence is strictly increasing.
3. Prove that $\dfrac{1}{2} \leq a_n < 2$ for every $n \geq 1$.

:::soluzione
1. $\dfrac{1}{2}$, $1$, $\dfrac{5}{4}$, $\dfrac{7}{5}$.
2. $a_{n+1} - a_n = \dfrac{3}{(n + 1)(n + 2)} > 0$.
3. The minimum is $a_1 = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and $2 - a_n = \dfrac{3}{n + 1} > 0$.
:::
