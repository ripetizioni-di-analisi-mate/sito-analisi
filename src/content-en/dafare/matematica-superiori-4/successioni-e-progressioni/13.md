---
titolo: Sums of infinite geometric progressions
teoria: [somma-geometrica-infinita]
parole: [infinite sum, repeating decimals, common ratio less than 1]
---

1. Write the repeating decimal $0.\overline{27}$ as the sum of a geometric progression and find the fraction it represents.
2. Calculate the sum $6 + 2 + \dfrac{2}{3} + \dfrac{2}{9} + \dots$

:::soluzione
1. $0.\overline{27} = \dfrac{27}{100} + \dfrac{27}{10000} + \dots = \dfrac{3}{11}$.
2. $9$.
:::
