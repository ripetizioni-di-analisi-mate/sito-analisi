An equilateral triangle $T_1$ has side length $6\ \mathrm{cm}$. Joining the midpoints of its sides gives the equilateral triangle $T_2$. Joining the midpoints of the sides of $T_2$ gives $T_3$, and so on.

1. Prove that the perimeters of the triangles form a geometric progression and find its common ratio.
2. Calculate the sum of the perimeters of the first five triangles.
3. Calculate the sum of the areas of all the triangles.

:::soluzione
1. $q = \dfrac{1}{2}$, because each side is half as long as the preceding one.
2. $\dfrac{279}{8}\ \mathrm{cm}$.
3. $12\sqrt{3}\ \mathrm{cm^2}$.
:::
