---
titolo: An exponential inequality with base less than 1
teoria: [disequazioni-esponenziali]
parole: [exponential inequalities, base less than one, direction of an inequality]
---

Solve the inequality $\left( \dfrac{2}{3} \right)^{x^2 - 3x} \geq \dfrac{9}{4}$.

:::soluzione
$1 \leq x \leq 2$.
:::
