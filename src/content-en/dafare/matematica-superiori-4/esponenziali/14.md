The concentration of a drug in the blood is halved every $4$ hours. Immediately after administration it is $80$ mg/l.

a) Write a formula $c(t)$ for the concentration after $t$ hours.

b) Calculate the concentration after $10$ hours, giving both an exact value and an approximation.

c) Find the time in hours when the concentration falls to $5$ mg/l.

:::soluzione
a) $c(t) = 80 \cdot \left( \dfrac{1}{2} \right)^{\frac{t}{4}}$. b) $c(10) = 10\sqrt{2} \approx 14.1$ mg/l. c) After $16$ hours.
:::
