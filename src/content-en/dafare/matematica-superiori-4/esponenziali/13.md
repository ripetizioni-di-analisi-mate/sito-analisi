A population of $2000$ individuals grows by $10\%$ each year.

a) Write a formula $N(n)$ for the population after $n$ years and calculate $N(3)$.

b) After how many whole years does the population first exceed $3000$ individuals?

:::soluzione
a) $N(n) = 2000 \cdot 1.1^n$ and $N(3) = 2662$. b) After $5$ years.
:::
