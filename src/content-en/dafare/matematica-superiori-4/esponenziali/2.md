Write the following numbers in increasing order:
$$
2^{\sqrt{3}} ,
\qquad
2^{1.7} ,
\qquad
\left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{-2} ,
\qquad
4^{0.8} .
$$

:::soluzione
$4^{0.8} < 2^{1.7} < 2^{\sqrt{3}} < \left( \dfrac{1}{2} \right)^{-2}$.
:::
