Sketch the graph of $f(x) = 3^{x + 1} - 1$ starting from the graph of $y = 3^x$. Give its domain, range, asymptote and intercepts.

:::soluzione
Domain $\mathbb{R}$, range $(-1, +\infty)$, asymptote $y = -1$. The graph intersects the axes at $(0, 2)$ and $(-1, 0)$.
:::
