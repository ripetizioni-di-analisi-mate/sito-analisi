Find the modulus and an argument of $z = -\sqrt{3} + i$, and write $z$ in trigonometric and exponential form.

:::soluzione
$|z| = 2$, $\arg z = \dfrac{5\pi}{6}$, so $z = 2\left( \cos\dfrac{5\pi}{6} + i\sin\dfrac{5\pi}{6} \right) = 2\,e^{i\frac{5\pi}{6}}$.
:::
