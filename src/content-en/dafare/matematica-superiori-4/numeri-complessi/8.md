titolo: A circle in the complex plane
teoria: [modulo, piano-di-gauss]
parole: [locus, circle, modulus, distance]
---

a) Find and describe the set of complex numbers $z$ such that $|z - 1 + 2i| = 3$.

b) Determine whether the number $4 - 2i$ belongs to the set.

:::soluzione
a) It is the circle with center $1 - 2i$, that is, the point $(1, -2)$, and radius $3$. Its equation is $(x - 1)^2 + (y + 2)^2 = 9$. b) Yes, because $|4 - 2i - 1 + 2i| = |3| = 3$.
:::
