---
titolo: Fourth roots and a regular polygon
teoria: [radici-n-esime, poligono-regolare]
parole: [fourth roots, square, area, regular polygon]
---

a) Over $\mathbb{C}$, solve the equation $z^4 + 4 = 0$.

b) Find the area of the polygon whose vertices are the solutions.

:::soluzione
a) $1 + i$, $-1 + i$, $-1 - i$, $1 - i$. b) The solutions are the vertices of a square with side length $2$, so its area is $4$.
:::
