titolo: A specified complex root and a parameter
teoria: [equazioni-secondo-grado, uguaglianza, radici-coniugate]

Consider the equation $z^2 + k z + 5 = 0$, where $k$ is a real parameter.

a) Find $k$ so that $1 + 2i$ is a solution.

b) For this value, find the other solution.

:::soluzione
a) $k = -2$. b) The other solution is $1 - 2i$.
:::
