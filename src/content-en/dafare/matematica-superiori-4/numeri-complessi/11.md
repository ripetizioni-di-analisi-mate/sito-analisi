Find the cube roots of $-27$ in algebraic form.

:::soluzione
$-3$, $\dfrac{3}{2} + \dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}\,i$, and $\dfrac{3}{2} - \dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}\,i$.
:::
