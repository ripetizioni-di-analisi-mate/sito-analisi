Write the number $z = 6\left( \cos\dfrac{3\pi}{4} + i\sin\dfrac{3\pi}{4} \right)$ and its conjugate $\bar z$ in algebraic form.

:::soluzione
$z = -3\sqrt{2} + 3\sqrt{2}\,i$ and $\bar z = -3\sqrt{2} - 3\sqrt{2}\,i$.
:::
