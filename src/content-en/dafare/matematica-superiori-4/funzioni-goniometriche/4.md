Given that $\sin\alpha = \dfrac{2}{3}$ and $\dfrac{\pi}{2} < \alpha < \pi$, find $\cos\alpha$, $\tan\alpha$, and $\cot\alpha$.

:::soluzione
$\cos\alpha = -\dfrac{\sqrt{5}}{3}$, $\tan\alpha = -\dfrac{2\sqrt{5}}{5}$, and $\cot\alpha = -\dfrac{\sqrt{5}}{2}$.
:::
