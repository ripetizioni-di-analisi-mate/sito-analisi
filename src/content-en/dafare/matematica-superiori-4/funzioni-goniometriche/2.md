In a circle of radius $12\ \text{cm}$, a central angle measures $75^\circ$. Find the length of the corresponding arc and the area of its sector.

:::soluzione
The angle measures $\dfrac{5\pi}{12}$, the arc has length $5\pi\ \text{cm}$, and the sector has area $30\pi\ \text{cm}^2$.
:::
