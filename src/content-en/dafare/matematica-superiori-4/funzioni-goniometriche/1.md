Express the angle $300^\circ$ in radians and the angle $\dfrac{11\pi}{12}$ in degrees.

:::soluzione
$300^\circ = \dfrac{5\pi}{3}$ and $\dfrac{11\pi}{12} = 165^\circ$.
:::
