Given the function $y = 4\sin\dfrac{x}{3} - 1$, find the period, the range, and the horizontal coordinates of its maximum points.

:::soluzione
The period is $6\pi$, the range is $[-5, 3]$, and maxima occur at $x = \dfrac{3\pi}{2} + 6k\pi$, with $k \in \mathbb{Z}$.
:::
