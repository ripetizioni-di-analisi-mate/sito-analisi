---
titolo: Trigonometric functions of angles greater than a full turn
teoria: [periodicita-seno-coseno, angoli-opposti, grafico-tangente]
parole: [periodicity, negative angles, reduction]
---

Find $\cos\dfrac{25\pi}{6}$, $\sin\dfrac{31\pi}{3}$, and $\tan\left( -\dfrac{13\pi}{4} \right)$.

:::soluzione
$\cos\dfrac{25\pi}{6} = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$, $\sin\dfrac{31\pi}{3} = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$, and $\tan\left( -\dfrac{13\pi}{4} \right) = -1$.
:::
