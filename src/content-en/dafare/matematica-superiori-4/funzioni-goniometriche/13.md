titolo: Domain and range of an arccosine function
teoria: [arcocoseno]
parole: [arccosine, domain, range]
---

Given the function $f(x) = \arccos\left( 1 - \dfrac{x}{2} \right)$:

a) Find the domain and range.

b) Calculate $f(1)$ and $f(4)$.

:::soluzione
a) The domain is $[0, 4]$ and the range is $[0, \pi]$. b) $f(1) = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$ and $f(4) = \pi$.
:::
