---
titolo: A sinusoid with a parameter
teoria: [sinusoide, periodo-sinusoide]
parole: [parameter, range, amplitude, period]
---

Find the values of the real parameter $k$ for which the function $y = (k - 1)\sin(2x) + 3$ has range $[1, 5]$. For these values, give the period of the function.

:::soluzione
$k = 3$ or $k = -1$, since we must have $|k - 1| = 2$. In either case the period is $\pi$.
:::
