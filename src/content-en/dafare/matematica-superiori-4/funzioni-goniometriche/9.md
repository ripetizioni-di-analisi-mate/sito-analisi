Simplify the expression $\dfrac{\cos(\pi - x)\tan(\pi + x)}{\sin(-x)}$, defined for $x \neq k\,\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ with $k \in \mathbb{Z}$.

:::soluzione
The expression equals $1$ for every $x \neq k\,\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.
:::
