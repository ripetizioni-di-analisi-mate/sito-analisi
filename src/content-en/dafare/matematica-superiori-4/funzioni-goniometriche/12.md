---
titolo: A sum of inverse trigonometric functions
teoria: [arcoseno, arcocoseno, arcotangente]
parole: [arcsine, arccosine, arctangent, principal values]
---

Evaluate the expression $\arcsin\left( -\dfrac{1}{2} \right) + \arccos\dfrac{1}{2} + \arctan(-1)$.

:::soluzione
The expression equals $-\dfrac{\pi}{6} + \dfrac{\pi}{3} - \dfrac{\pi}{4} = -\dfrac{\pi}{12}$.
:::
