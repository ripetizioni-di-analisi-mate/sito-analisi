Given that $\tan\alpha = \dfrac{3}{4}$ and $\pi < \alpha < \dfrac{3\pi}{2}$, find $\sin\alpha$ and $\cos\alpha$.

:::soluzione
$\cos\alpha = -\dfrac{4}{5}$ and $\sin\alpha = -\dfrac{3}{5}$.
:::
