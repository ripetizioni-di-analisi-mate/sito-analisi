---
titolo: Verifying an identity
teoria: [relazione-fondamentale]
parole: [trigonometric identity, difference of squares]
---

Prove that for every real number $x$ the identity $\sin^4 x - \cos^4 x = \sin^2 x - \cos^2 x$ holds.

:::soluzione
The left-hand side factors as $\left( \sin^2 x - \cos^2 x \right)\left( \sin^2 x + \cos^2 x \right)$, and the second factor equals $1$ by the first Pythagorean identity.
:::
