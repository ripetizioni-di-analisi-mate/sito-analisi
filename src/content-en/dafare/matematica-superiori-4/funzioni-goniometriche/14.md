A mass attached to a spring moves according to $x(t) = 8\cos\left( \dfrac{\pi}{3}\,t \right)$, with $x$ in centimeters and $t$ in seconds.

a) Find the amplitude, period, and frequency of the motion.

b) Find the position of the mass at $t = 1\ \text{s}$ and at $t = 4.5\ \text{s}$.

c) Find the first time $t > 0$ at which the mass is at $x = -8\ \text{cm}$.

:::soluzione
a) $A = 8\ \text{cm}$, $T = 6\ \text{s}$, $f = \dfrac{1}{6}\ \text{Hz}$. b) $x(1) = 4\ \text{cm}$ and $x(4.5) = 0$. c) $t = 3\ \text{s}$.
:::
