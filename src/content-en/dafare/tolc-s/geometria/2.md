What is the area of a disk with radius $3\,\mathrm{cm}$?

- $6\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $9\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $3\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $18\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $27\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$

:::soluzione

Answer B: $9\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$.

:::
