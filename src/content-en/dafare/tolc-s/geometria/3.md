A right cone has base radius $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ and height $9\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its volume?

- $36\pi\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $6\pi\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $12\pi\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $18\pi\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $4\pi\,\mathrm{cm^3}$

:::soluzione

Answer C: $12\pi\,\mathrm{cm^3}$.

:::
