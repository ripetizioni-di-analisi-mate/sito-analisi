A body starts from rest with constant acceleration $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What distance does it travel in the first $3\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m}$

:::soluzione
B) $9\,\mathrm{m}$
:::
