How many meters per second correspond to a speed of $72\,\mathrm{km/h}$?

- $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $72\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $25.92\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $200\,\mathrm{m/s}$

:::soluzione
A) $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$
:::
