A fixed amount of ideal gas expands at constant temperature from $2\,\mathrm{L}$ to $4\,\mathrm{L}$. How does pressure change?

- It halves
- It doubles
- It stays constant
- It quadruples
- It becomes one quarter

:::soluzione
A) It halves
:::
