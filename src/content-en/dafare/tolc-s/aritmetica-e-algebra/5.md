What is the complete solution set of $\dfrac{x-1}{x+2}>0$?

- $(-2,1)$
- $(1,+\infty)$
- $[1,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{-2\}$
- $(-\infty,-2)\cup(1,+\infty)$


:::soluzione

Answer E. $(-\infty,-2)\cup(1,+\infty)$

:::
