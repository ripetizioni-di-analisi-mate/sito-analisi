For $a,b>0$, which identity always holds?

- $\ln(a+b)=\ln a+\ln b$
- $\ln(ab)=\ln a\ln b$
- $\ln(ab)=\ln a+\ln b$
- $\ln\dfrac ab=\ln b-\ln a$
- $\ln(a^2)=\ln a+2$

:::soluzione

Correct answer: C. $\ln(ab)=\ln a+\ln b$

:::
