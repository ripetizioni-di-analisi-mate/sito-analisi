In $\mathrm{Zn+Cu^{2+}\to Zn^{2+}+Cu}$, which initial species is the oxidizing agent?

- $\mathrm{Zn}$
- $\mathrm{Cu}$
- $\mathrm{Cu^{2+}}$
- $\mathrm{Zn^{2+}}$
- The solvent alone, regardless of the reaction

:::soluzione

Correct answer: C. $\mathrm{Cu^{2+}}$

:::
