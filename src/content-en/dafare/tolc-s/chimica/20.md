Why can an aqueous electrolyte solution conduct current?

- All water molecules become metals
- Nuclear protons move freely through the solution
- The solute contains no electrons
- Only neutral particles carry current
- It contains mobile positively and negatively charged ions

:::soluzione

Correct answer: E. It contains mobile positively and negatively charged ions

:::
