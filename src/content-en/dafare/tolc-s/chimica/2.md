Which pair of neutral atoms represents two isotopes of the same element? Each number pair states protons and neutrons.

- $(6,6)$ and $(8,6)$
- $(6,6)$ and $(6,8)$
- $(6,8)$ and $(7,7)$
- $(6,6)$ and $(7,6)$
- $(8,8)$ and $(7,9)$

:::soluzione

Correct answer: B. $(6,6)$ and $(6,8)$

:::
