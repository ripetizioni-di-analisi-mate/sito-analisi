How many moles of water molecules are present in $36\,\mathrm g$? Take $M(\mathrm{H_2O})=18\,\mathrm{g/mol}$.

- $18\,\mathrm{mol}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{mol}$
- $2\,\mathrm{mol}$
- $648\,\mathrm{mol}$
- $36\,\mathrm{mol}$

:::soluzione

Correct answer: C. $2\,\mathrm{mol}$

:::
