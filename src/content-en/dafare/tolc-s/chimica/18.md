Which compound has the functional group of an alcohol?

- $\mathrm{CH_3CHO}$
- $\mathrm{CH_3COCH_3}$
- $\mathrm{CH_3CH_2OH}$
- $\mathrm{CH_3COOH}$
- $\mathrm{CH_3OCH_3}$

:::soluzione

Correct answer: C. $\mathrm{CH_3CH_2OH}$

:::
