Which formula represents methane, the alkane with one carbon atom?

- $\mathrm{CO_2}$
- $\mathrm{C_2H_6}$
- $\mathrm{CH_4}$
- $\mathrm{CH_3OH}$
- $\mathrm{C_2H_4}$

:::soluzione

Correct answer: C. $\mathrm{CH_4}$

:::
