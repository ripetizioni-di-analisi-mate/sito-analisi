A sufficiently dilute solution has $[\mathrm{H_3O^+}]=10^{-3}\,\mathrm{mol/L}$. What is its approximate pH?

- $3$
- $-3$
- $11$
- $7$
- $10^{-3}$

:::soluzione

Correct answer: A. $3$

:::
