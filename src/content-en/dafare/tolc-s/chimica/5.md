A solution contains $0.50\,\mathrm{mol}$ of solute and has a final volume of $2.0\,\mathrm L$. What is its molar concentration?

- $1.0\,\mathrm{mol/L}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{mol/L}$
- $0.50\,\mathrm{mol/L}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{mol/L}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{mol/L}$

:::soluzione

Correct answer: E. $0.25\,\mathrm{mol/L}$

:::
