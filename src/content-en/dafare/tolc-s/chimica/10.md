A $100\,\mathrm{mL}$ portion of a $1.0\,\mathrm{mol/L}$ solution is taken to a final volume of $500\,\mathrm{mL}$ without losing solute. What is the final concentration?

- $5.0\,\mathrm{mol/L}$
- $1.0\,\mathrm{mol/L}$
- $0.50\,\mathrm{mol/L}$
- $0.020\,\mathrm{mol/L}$
- $0.20\,\mathrm{mol/L}$

:::soluzione

Correct answer: E. $0.20\,\mathrm{mol/L}$

:::
