Anna arrives before Bruno. Carla arrives after Bruno. Their arrival times are distinct. What is their arrival order?

- Anna, Bruno, Carla
- Bruno, Anna, Carla
- Anna, Carla, Bruno
- Carla, Bruno, Anna
- Bruno, Carla, Anna

:::soluzione

Correct answer: A. Anna, Bruno, Carla.

:::
