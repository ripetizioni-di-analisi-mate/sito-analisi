What is the negation of “Marco studies and Lucia works”? The word “or” is inclusive.

- Marco does not study and Lucia does not work
- Marco does not study or Lucia does not work
- Marco studies or Lucia works
- Marco studies and Lucia does not work
- Marco does not study and Lucia works

:::soluzione

Correct answer: B. Marco does not study or Lucia does not work.

:::
