A micrograph shows DNA enclosed by a double membrane with pores. Which conclusion is justified?

- The cell must be animal
- The cell has eukaryotic organization
- The cell must be photosynthetic
- The cell lacks ribosomes
- The cell is a typical bacterium

:::soluzione

**Correct answer: B.** The cell has eukaryotic organization.

:::
