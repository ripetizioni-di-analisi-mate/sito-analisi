In cultured cells, labeled amino acids are tracked as they are incorporated into new polypeptide chains. Which structure directly catalyzes this assembly?

- Ribosome
- Lysosome
- Cell wall
- Central vacuole
- Centriole

:::soluzione

**Correct answer: A.** Ribosome.

:::
