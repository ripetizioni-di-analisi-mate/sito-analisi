A cell has one set of chromosomes, each still consisting of two chromatids. It comes from a diploid cell that underwent a regular meiotic division. Which division has just ended?

- S phase
- Mitosis of a diploid cell
- Meiosis I
- Meiosis II
- Fertilization

:::soluzione

**Correct answer: C.** Meiosis I.

:::
