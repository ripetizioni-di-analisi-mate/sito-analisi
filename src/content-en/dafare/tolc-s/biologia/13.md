A root cell has a cellulose wall, a nucleus and mitochondria, but no chloroplasts. Which interpretation is correct?

- It must be prokaryotic
- It cannot produce ATP
- It can be a normal nonphotosynthetic plant cell
- It must be an animal cell
- The wall replaces the nucleus

:::soluzione

**Correct answer: C.** It can be a normal nonphotosynthetic plant cell.

:::
