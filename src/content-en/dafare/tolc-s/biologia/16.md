An organism builds its organic compounds using carbon dioxide as its carbon source and light as its energy source. Which role does it normally have in a food web?

- Autotrophic producer
- Obligate primary consumer
- Secondary predator
- Obligate animal parasite
- Consumer using organic matter as its only carbon source

:::soluzione

**Correct answer: A.** Autotrophic producer.

:::
