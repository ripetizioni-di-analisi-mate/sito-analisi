A sample contains elongated cells with organized contractile proteins that develop force and shorten when activated. Which tissue is mainly described?

- Muscle
- Lining epithelium
- Cartilaginous connective tissue
- Nervous
- Bone connective tissue

:::soluzione

**Correct answer: A.** Muscle.

:::
