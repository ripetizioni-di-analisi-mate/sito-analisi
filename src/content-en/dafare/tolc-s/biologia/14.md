In an oxygenic photosynthesis experiment, oxygen atoms in water molecules are labeled. In which gaseous product is this label directly found?

- In atmospheric nitrogen fixed
- In methane produced by the chloroplast
- In the hydrogen of every sugar
- In the molecular oxygen released
- In carbon dioxide absorbed before the experiment

:::soluzione

**Correct answer: D.** In the molecular oxygen released.

:::
