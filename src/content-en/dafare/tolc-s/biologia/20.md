In a phospholipid bilayer with water on both sides, which arrangement is energetically favorable?

- Nonpolar tails are fully exposed to water on both sides
- All heads are only on the outer side
- All tails are only on the cytoplasmic side
- Phospholipids are converted into nucleotides
- Polar heads face water and nonpolar tails face inward

:::soluzione

**Correct answer: E.** Polar heads face water and nonpolar tails face inward.

:::
