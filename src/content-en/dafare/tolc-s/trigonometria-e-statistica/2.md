How many radians correspond to $150^\circ$?

- $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$
- $\dfrac{5\pi}{6}$
- $\dfrac{5\pi}{3}$
- $\dfrac\pi6$
- $\dfrac{6\pi}{5}$

:::soluzione

Correct answer: B. $\dfrac{5\pi}{6}$

:::
