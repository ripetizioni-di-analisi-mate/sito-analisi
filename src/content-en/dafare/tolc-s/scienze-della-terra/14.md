In a simplified model, one event type has an annual probability of $2\%$ and would cause a loss of $300\,000$ euros. Otherwise the loss is zero. What is the expected annual loss?

- $150\,000$ euros
- $300\,000$ euros
- $60\,000$ euros
- $6\,000$ euros
- $600$ euros

:::soluzione

$6\,000$ euros.

:::
