Three sedimentary layers that have not been overturned are A at the bottom, B in the middle, and C at the top. There are no intrusions. Which depositional order, oldest to youngest, follows from the data?

- C, B, A
- A, B, C
- B, A, C
- A, C, B
- C, A, B

:::soluzione

A, B, C.

:::
