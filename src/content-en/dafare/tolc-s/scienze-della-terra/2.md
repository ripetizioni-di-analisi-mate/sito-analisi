In June, daylight is long in the Northern Hemisphere and short in the Southern Hemisphere. Which explanation accounts for opposite seasons at the same time?

- Earth-Sun distance variation alone explains opposite seasons
- Axial tilt and revolution change illumination differently in the two hemispheres
- Daily rotation alone determines the annual seasonal cycle
- In June the axis entirely loses its tilt
- Tilt changes year length in opposite ways in the two hemispheres

:::soluzione

Axial tilt and revolution change illumination differently in the two hemispheres.

:::
