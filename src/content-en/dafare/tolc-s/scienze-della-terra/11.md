In a vertical cross-section, the surface is $z=0$ and the hypocentre is $H=(18,-12)\,\mathrm{km}$. What are the epicentre’s coordinates in the same section?

- $(18,0)\,\mathrm{km}$
- $(0,-12)\,\mathrm{km}$
- $(18,12)\,\mathrm{km}$
- $(0,0)\,\mathrm{km}$
- $(-18,0)\,\mathrm{km}$

:::soluzione

$(18,0)\,\mathrm{km}$.

:::
