Let $A\subseteq B$ and $P(A)=\dfrac7{10}$. Which value can $P(B)$ take?

- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac45$
- $\dfrac23$
- $-\dfrac1{10}$
- $\dfrac{11}{10}$

:::soluzione

Correct answer: B. $\dfrac45$

:::
