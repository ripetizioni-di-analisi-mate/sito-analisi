An urn contains three red balls and two blue balls. Two balls are drawn successively, each uniformly from those present, without replacement. What is the probability that both are red?

- $\dfrac9{25}$
- $\dfrac25$
- $\dfrac3{10}$
- $\dfrac35$
- $\dfrac12$


:::soluzione

Answer C. $\dfrac3{10}$

:::
