Two fair independent dice are rolled, distinguishing the first from the second. Which description uses outcomes that are all equally likely?

- The possible values of the sum
- The possible values of the product
- The possible values of the minimum
- The possible numbers of even results
- The ordered pairs of the two die results

:::soluzione

Correct answer: E. The ordered pairs of the two die results

:::
