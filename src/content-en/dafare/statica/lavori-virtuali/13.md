The beam shown has length $5\,\mathrm{m}$. At $A$ a parallel-link support with horizontal links restrains horizontal translation and rotation. At $B$ a roller moves along a horizontal surface. A uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts over the entire beam. Use virtual work to find the reaction moment $M_A$ at the parallel-link support and reaction $R_B$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,doppio-pendolo,180;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,B,5 m,0.9" titolo="Beam with a parallel-link support at $A$ and a roller at $B$, with $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

:::soluzione
$M_A = -50\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, or $50\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ clockwise, and $R_B = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward.
:::
