The beam shown has a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$ with $AB = 5\,\mathrm{m}$, and an overhang $BC = 2\,\mathrm{m}$. A uniform load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts on $AB$, and a downward force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $C$. Use virtual work to find the roller reaction $R_B$ and the vertical pin reaction $V_A$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;C,7,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" forze="C,0,-1.5,F" quote="A,B,5 m,0.9;B,C,2 m,0.9" titolo="Overhanging beam with $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on $AB$ and $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$"}

:::soluzione
$R_B = 29\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_A = 11\,\mathrm{kN}$, both upward ($H_A = 0$).
:::
