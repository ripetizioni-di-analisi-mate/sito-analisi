The portal frame shown has points $A = (0, 0)$, $D = (0, 3)$, $E = (5, 3)$, and $B = (5, 0)$, a pin at $A$, and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. A horizontal force $F = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right at $D$, and crossbeam $DE$ carries a uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Use virtual work to find the axial force in column $EB$.
