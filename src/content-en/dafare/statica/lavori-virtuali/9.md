The beam shown has length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, a pin at $A$, and a roller at $B$ on an inclined surface. Its reaction points in the direction of unit vector $\left( -\dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5} \right)$. A downward force $F = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at midspan. Use virtual work to find reaction $R_B$ and the horizontal component $H_A$ of the reaction at $A$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,306.87" forze="C,0,-1,F" quote="A,C,2 m,0.8;C,B,2 m,0.8" titolo="Beam with an inclined roller at $B$ and $F = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ at midspan"}

:::soluzione
$R_B = 12.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, with components $7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left and $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, and $H_A = 7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right.
:::
