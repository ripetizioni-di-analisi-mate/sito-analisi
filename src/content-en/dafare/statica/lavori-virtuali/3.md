The simply supported beam shown has span $AB = 6\,\mathrm{m}$ and carries a triangular load that increases from $0$ at $A$ to $q_0 = 12\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $B$. Use virtual work to find the bending moment at midpoint $S$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,3,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0,1.2,q_0" quote="A,S,3 m,0.9;S,B,3 m,0.9" titolo="Simply supported beam with a triangular load varying from $0$ to $q_0 = 12\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

:::soluzione
$M_S = 27\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the bottom fibers in tension.
:::
