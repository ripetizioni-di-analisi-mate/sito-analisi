The three-hinged portal frame shown has pin supports at $A = (0, 0)$ and $B = (6, 0)$, columns of height $3\,\mathrm{m}$, and an internal hinge $C = (3, 3)$ at the midpoint of the crossbeam. A horizontal force $P = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right at $D = (0, 3)$. Use virtual work to find reactions $H_B$ and $V_B$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,0,3;C,3,3;E,6,3;B,6,0" aste="A-D;D-C;C-E;E-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" forze="D,1.4,0,P" quote="D,C,3 m,-0.6;C,E,3 m,-0.6;E,B,3 m,0.7" titolo="Three-hinged portal frame under horizontal force $P = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $D$"}

:::soluzione
$H_B = -4\,\mathrm{kN}$, or $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left, and $V_B = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward.
:::
