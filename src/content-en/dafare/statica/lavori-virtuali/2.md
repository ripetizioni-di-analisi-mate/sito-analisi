The cantilever shown has length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and is fixed at $A$. A downward force $F = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and a counterclockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ acts at the free end $B$. Use virtual work to find the fixed-end moment $M_A$ and the vertical reaction $V_A$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" forze="C,0,-1,F" coppie="B,1,ℳ" quote="A,C,2 m,0.8;C,B,1 m,0.8" titolo="Cantilever with $F = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ and counterclockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at $B$"}

:::soluzione
$M_A = 20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise and $V_A = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward.
:::
