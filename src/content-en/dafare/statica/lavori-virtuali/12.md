The beam shown consists of segment $AB$, pinned at $A$, and segment $BD$, joined to $AB$ at internal hinge $B$ and supported by a roller at $C$. The horizontal coordinates are $A = 0$, $B = 4\,\mathrm{m}$, $C = 6\,\mathrm{m}$, and $D = 9\,\mathrm{m}$. A downward force $F_1 = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Find the vertical force $F_D$ that must act at $D$ for equilibrium.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0;D,9,0" aste="A-B;B-D" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" forze="2 0,0,-1.5,F_1;D,0,-1.5,F_D" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9;B,C,2 m,0.9;C,D,3 m,0.9" titolo="Unstable structure under $F_1 = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ and unknown force $F_D$"}

:::soluzione
$F_D = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward.
:::
