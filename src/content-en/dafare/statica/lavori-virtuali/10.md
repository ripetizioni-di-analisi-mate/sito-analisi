The Gerber beam shown has a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$, an internal hinge $C$, and a roller at $D$. Their respective horizontal coordinates are $0$, $4\,\mathrm{m}$, $6\,\mathrm{m}$, and $10\,\mathrm{m}$. A uniform load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts on segment $CD$. Use virtual work to find the bending moment at section $B$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0;D,10,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" carichi="C,D,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9;B,C,2 m,0.9;C,D,4 m,0.9" titolo="Gerber beam with $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on $CD$"}

:::soluzione
$M_B = -12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the top fibers in tension.
:::
