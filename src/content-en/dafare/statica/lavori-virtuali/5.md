The Gerber beam shown consists of segment $AB$, fixed at $A$, and segment $BC$, joined to $AB$ at internal hinge $B$ and supported by a roller at $C$. The dimensions are $AB = 3\,\mathrm{m}$ and $BC = 4\,\mathrm{m}$. A uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts on $BC$. Use virtual work to find reaction $R_C$ and the fixed-end moment $M_A$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,7,0" aste="A-B;B-C" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;C,carrello" carichi="B,C,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,B,3 m,0.9;B,C,4 m,0.9" titolo="Gerber beam with $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on segment $BC$"}

:::soluzione
$R_C = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward and $M_A = 24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise.
:::
