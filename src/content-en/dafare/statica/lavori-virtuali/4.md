---
titolo: "Shear force between two point loads"
teoria: [taglio-plv, sconnessioni]
parole: ["principle of virtual work", "shear force", "parallel-link support", "simply supported beam"]
---

A simply supported beam of span $10\,\mathrm{m}$ has a pin at $A$ and a roller at $B$. Two downward forces act on the beam: $F_1 = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ at a distance $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ and $F_2 = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ at a distance $7\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Use virtual work to find the midspan shear force.

:::soluzione
$T = -3\,\mathrm{kN}$.
:::
