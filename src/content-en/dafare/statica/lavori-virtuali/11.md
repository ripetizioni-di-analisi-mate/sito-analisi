The simply supported beam shown has span $AB = 6\,\mathrm{m}$. A clockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ acts a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and a downward force $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Use virtual work to find the shear force at midpoint $S$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,3,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" coppie="2 0,-1,ℳ" forze="4 0,0,-1.5,F" quote="A,2 0,2 m,0.9;2 0,S,1 m,0.9;S,4 0,1 m,0.9;4 0,B,2 m,0.9" titolo="Simply supported beam under clockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and force $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

:::soluzione
$T_S = 1\,\mathrm{kN}$.
:::
