The beam shown has length $10\,\mathrm{m}$. Its pin $A$ lies a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from end $D$, and its roller $B$ lies a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from end $E$. A downward force of $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at each end, $D$ and $E$, and a uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts on $AB$. Use virtual work to find the bending moment at the midpoint of the beam.

::struttura{nodi="D,0,0;A,2,0;B,8,0;E,10,0" aste="D-E" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" forze="D,0,-1.6,F;E,0,-1.6,F" quote="D,A,2 m,0.9;A,B,6 m,0.9;B,E,2 m,0.9" titolo="Beam with two overhangs, with $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ at both ends and $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on $AB$"}

:::soluzione
$M = -11\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the top fibers in tension.
:::
