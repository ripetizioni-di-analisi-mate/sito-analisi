The Gerber beam shown has a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$, an internal hinge $C$, and a roller at $D$. Their respective horizontal coordinates are $0$, $4\,\mathrm{m}$, $5\,\mathrm{m}$, and $9\,\mathrm{m}$. A downward force $F = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $x = 7\,\mathrm{m}$. Use virtual work to find reactions $R_B$ and $V_A$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,5,0;D,9,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" forze="7 0,0,-1.6,F" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9;B,C,1 m,0.9;C,7 0,2 m,0.9;7 0,D,2 m,0.9" titolo="Gerber beam with $F = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$ on segment $CD$"}

:::soluzione
$R_B = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward and $V_A = -2\,\mathrm{kN}$, or $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward.
:::
