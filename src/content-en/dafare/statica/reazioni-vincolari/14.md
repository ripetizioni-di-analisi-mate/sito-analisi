Beam $AB$ of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$ rests on two rollers on horizontal surfaces, one at $A$ and the other at $B$. A vertical force $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and a horizontal force $H$ acts a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Determine which values of $H$ permit equilibrium and find the corresponding reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello;B,carrello" forze="2 0,0,-1.5,F;4 0,1.3,0,H" quote="A,2 0,2 m;2 0,4 0,2 m;4 0,B,2 m" titolo="Beam supported on two rollers"}

:::soluzione
There are no reactions in the horizontal force equilibrium equation because horizontal translation is unrestrained. The equation reduces to $H = 0$, so equilibrium is possible only for $H = 0$. In this case the reactions are $V_A = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_B = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$, both upward.
:::
