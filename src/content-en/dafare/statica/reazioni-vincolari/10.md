The sloping roof member $AB$ extends from $A = (0, 0)$ to $B = (8\,\mathrm{m}, 6\,\mathrm{m})$, with a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. It carries self-weight $g = 1.5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ per unit member length, snow load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ per unit horizontal projection, and wind load $p = 1\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ per unit member length. The wind acts perpendicular to the member, downward and to the right. Find the support reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,8,6" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,p,306.87" quote="A,8 0,8 m,1;8 0,B,6 m,-0.6" titolo="Sloping roof member with the wind load $p$ shown"}

:::soluzione
The resultants of the self-weight, snow, and wind loads are $15\,\mathrm{kN}$, $16\,\mathrm{kN}$, and $10\,\mathrm{kN}$, respectively, all acting at the midpoint. The wind components are $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. The support reactions are $H_A = -6\,\mathrm{kN}$, or $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left, and $V_A = 17.25\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_B = 21.75\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward.
:::
