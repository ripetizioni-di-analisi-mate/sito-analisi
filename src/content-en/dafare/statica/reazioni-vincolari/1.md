Beam $AB$ of length $5\,\mathrm{m}$ has a pin support at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. A uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts over the full span, and a vertical force $F = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Find the support reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" forze="2 0,0,-1.3,F" quote="A,2 0,2 m;2 0,B,3 m" titolo="Simply supported beam under uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and force $F = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

:::soluzione
$H_A = 0$, $V_A = 19\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, and $V_B = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward.
:::
