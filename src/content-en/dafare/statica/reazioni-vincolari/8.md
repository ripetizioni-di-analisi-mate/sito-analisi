Beam $AB$ of length $8\,\mathrm{m}$ has a pin at $A$ and a roller at $B$ on a surface inclined at $30^\circ$ to the horizontal. The roller reaction points upward and to the left. A uniform load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts over the entire beam. Find the support reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,300" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,B,8 m" titolo="Beam with a roller on a surface inclined at $30^\circ$"}

:::soluzione
$R_B = 8\sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{kN} \approx 13.86\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts upward and to the left at an angle $30^\circ$ to the vertical. The other reactions are $H_A = 4\sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{kN} \approx 6.93\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and $V_A = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward.
:::
