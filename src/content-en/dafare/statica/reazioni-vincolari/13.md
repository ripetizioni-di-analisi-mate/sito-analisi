Beam $AB$ of length $5\,\mathrm{m}$ has a parallel-link support with vertical links at $A$, which restrains vertical translation and rotation, and a roller against a vertical wall at $B$. A force acting downward and to the right a distance $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ has horizontal component $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and vertical component $8\,\mathrm{kN}$. Find the support reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,doppio-pendolo;B,carrello,0" forze="3 0,0.75,-1,F" quote="A,3 0,3 m;3 0,B,2 m" titolo="Beam with a parallel-link support at $A$ and a roller against a vertical wall at $B$"}

:::soluzione
$H_B = -6\,\mathrm{kN}$, or $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left, with $V_A = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward and $M_A = 24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise.
:::
