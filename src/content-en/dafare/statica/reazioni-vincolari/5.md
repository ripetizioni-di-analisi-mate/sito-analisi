Beam $ACB$ consists of a horizontal segment $AC$ of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and an inclined segment $CB$ welded to it at $C$. Relative to $C$, point $B$ lies $4\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right and $3\,\mathrm{m}$ higher. The beam has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. A vertical force $F_1 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $C$, segment $CB$ carries a vertical load $g = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ per unit member length, and a horizontal force $F_2 = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the left at $B$. Find the support reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,0;B,8,3" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="C,B,0.4,0.4,g" forze="C,0,-1.4,F_1;B,-1.2,0,F_2" quote="A,C,4 m;C,8 0,4 m;8 0,B,3 m,0.7" titolo="Bent beam with a horizontal segment and an inclined segment"}

:::soluzione
$H_A = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right, $V_A = 10.75\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, and $V_B = 11.25\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward.
:::
