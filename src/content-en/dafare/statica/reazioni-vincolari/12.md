Portal frame $ACDB$ has columns $AC$ and $BD$ of height $5\,\mathrm{m}$ and a crossbeam $CD$ of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, welded at the joints. It has a roller on a horizontal surface at $A$ and a pin at $B$. The crossbeam carries a uniform load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, and a horizontal force $F = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right at joint $C$. Find the support reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,0,5;D,4,5;B,4,0" aste="A-C;C-D;D-B" vincoli="A,carrello;B,cerniera" carichi="C,D,0.5,0.5,q" forze="C,1.3,0,F" quote="A,C,5 m,1.1;C,D,4 m,-1.1" titolo="Portal frame with a roller at $A$ and a pin at $B$"}

:::soluzione
$V_A = -4\,\mathrm{kN}$, or $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward, and $H_B = -8\,\mathrm{kN}$, or $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left, with $V_B = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward.
:::
