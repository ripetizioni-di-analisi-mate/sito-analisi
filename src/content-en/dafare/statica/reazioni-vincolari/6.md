Beam $AC$ has a pin at $A$, a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$ a distance $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and an overhang $BC$ of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$. Segment $AB$ carries a triangular load that increases linearly from $0$ at $A$ to $q_0 = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $B$. A vertical force $F = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $C$. Find the support reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,8,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0,0.8,q_0" forze="C,0,-1.6,F" quote="A,B,6 m;B,C,2 m" titolo="Overhanging beam with a triangular load on the span"}

:::soluzione
$H_A = 0$, $V_A = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, and $V_B = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward.
:::
