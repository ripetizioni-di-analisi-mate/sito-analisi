Beam $AC$ has a pin at $A$, a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$ a distance $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and an overhang $BC$ of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$. A uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts on segment $AB$, and a downward vertical force $F$ acts at $C$. Express $V_A$ in terms of $F$, find the value of $F$ that makes the reaction at $A$ zero, and calculate $V_B$ for that case.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,8,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.6,q" forze="C,0,-1.8,F" quote="A,B,6 m;B,C,2 m" titolo="For the overhanging beam, what value of $F$ makes the reaction at $A$ zero?"}

:::soluzione
$V_A = 12 - \dfrac{F}{3}$ (in $\mathrm{kN}$, with $F$ in $\mathrm{kN}$). The reaction is $V_A = 0$ for $F = 36\,\mathrm{kN}$. In this case $V_B = 60\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts upward. For $F > 36\,\mathrm{kN}$ the reaction at $A$ acts downward.
:::
