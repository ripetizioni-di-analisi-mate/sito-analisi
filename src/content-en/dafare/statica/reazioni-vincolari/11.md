The L-shaped beam $ACB$ consists of a column $AC$ of height $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and a crossbeam $CB$ of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, welded at $C$. It has a pin at base $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. The crossbeam carries a uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, and a horizontal force $F = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right at the midpoint of the column. Find the support reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,0,4;B,6,4" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="C,B,0.5,0.5,q" forze="0 2,1.4,0,F" quote="A,0 2,2 m,1;0 2,C,2 m,1;C,B,6 m,-1.1" titolo="L-shaped beam with a horizontal force at the midpoint of its vertical member"}

:::soluzione
$H_A = -9\,\mathrm{kN}$, or $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left, with $V_A = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_B = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward.
:::
