---
titolo: "Reactions of a cantilever under a trapezoidal load"
teoria: [equazioni-cardinali, risultante-carico-distribuito]
parole: ["cantilever", "trapezoidal load", "reactive moment", "fixed support"]
---

Cantilever $AB$ of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ is fixed at $A$. It carries a trapezoidal load whose intensity increases linearly from $3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $A$ to $9\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at the free end $B$. Find the resultant, its distance from $A$, and the fixed-support reactions.

:::soluzione
The resultant is $24\,\mathrm{kN}$, a distance $\dfrac{7}{3}\,\mathrm{m} \approx 2.33\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. The reactions are $H_A = 0$, $V_A = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, and $M_A = 56\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise.
:::
