Beam $AB$ of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$ is pinned at $A$ and supported at $B$ by a two-force link extending downward and to the left from $B$. The link has direction cosines $\left(\dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5}\right)$ relative to axes $x$ and $y$. A vertical force $F = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and a horizontal force $H = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the left at $B$. Find the support reactions and determine whether the link is in tension or compression.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,pendolo,233.13" forze="2 0,0,-1.5,F;B,-1.2,0,H" quote="A,2 0,2 m;2 0,B,4 m" titolo="Beam with a pin support and an inclined link"}

:::soluzione
The link reaction $R_B = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ points upward and to the right along the link, which is therefore in compression. The other reactions are $H_A = -1\,\mathrm{kN}$, or $1\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left, and $V_A = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward.
:::
