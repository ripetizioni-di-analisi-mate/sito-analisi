Beam $AC$ has a pin at $A$, a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$ a distance $5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and an overhang $BC$ of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$. A uniform load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts on segment $AB$, while a vertical force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ and a clockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ act at end $C$. Find the support reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;C,7,0,-" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.6,q" forze="C,0,-1.5,F" coppie="C,-1,ℳ" etichette="7.3,-0.45,C" quote="A,B,5 m;B,C,2 m" titolo="Overhanging beam with a distributed load on the span and a force and couple at the free end"}

:::soluzione
$H_A = 0$, $V_A = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, and $V_B = 31\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward.
:::
