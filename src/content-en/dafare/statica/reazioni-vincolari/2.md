Cantilever $AB$ of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ is fixed at $A$ and carries a uniform load $q = 5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. A leftward horizontal force $F = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and a clockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ act at its free end $B$. Find the fixed-support reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" carichi="A,B,0.3,0.3,q" forze="B,-0.8,0,F" coppie="B,-1,ℳ" quote="A,B,3 m" titolo="Cantilever under a uniform load, axial force $F$, and clockwise couple $\mathcal{M}$"}

:::soluzione
$H_A = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right, $V_A = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, and $M_A = 32.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise.
:::
