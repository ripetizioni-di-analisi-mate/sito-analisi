The Z-section shown has overall height $10\,\mathrm{cm}$. The web and flanges have thickness $2\,\mathrm{cm}$, and each flange has overall width $10\,\mathrm{cm}$, including the web thickness. Find the centroidal moments of area, the principal moments of inertia, and the orientation of the principal axes.

::struttura{poligoni="-9,-5 1,-5 1,3 9,3 9,5 -1,5 -1,-3 -9,-3" frecce="-11,0,11,0,x;0,-6.5,0,7,y" quote="-1 5,9 5,10 cm,-1;-9 -5,1 -5,10 cm,1;9 -5,9 5,10 cm,3.5;9 3,9 5,2 cm,0.8;-1 0,1 0,2 cm,1.5" riferimento=0 titolo="Z-section of height $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ with flange length $10\,\mathrm{cm}$"}

:::soluzione
$I_{x_G} = \dfrac{2068}{3}\,\mathrm{cm^4} = 689.33\,\mathrm{cm^4}$, $I_{y_G} = \dfrac{2932}{3}\,\mathrm{cm^4} = 977.33\,\mathrm{cm^4}$, and $I_{x_G y_G} = 640\,\mathrm{cm^4}$. The principal moment $I_1 = 1489.33\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ is taken about the axis rotated clockwise by $51.34^\circ$ from $x_G$. The principal moment $I_2 = 177.33\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ is taken about the axis rotated counterclockwise by $38.66^\circ$.
:::
