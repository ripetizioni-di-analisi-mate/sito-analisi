The horizontal and vertical legs of the L-section shown measure $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $14\,\mathrm{cm}$, respectively, and both have thickness $2\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find the centroid, $I_{x_G}$, $I_{y_G}$, $I_{x_G y_G}$, the principal moments of inertia, and the orientation of the principal axes.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 12,0 12,2 2,2 2,14 0,14" frecce="0,0,14,0,x;0,0,0,16,y" quote="0 0,12 0,12 cm,1;0 0,0 14,14 cm,-1;12 0,12 2,2 cm,0.8;0 14,2 14,2 cm,-0.8" riferimento=0 titolo="L-section with leg lengths $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $14\,\mathrm{cm}$ and thickness $2\,\mathrm{cm}$"}

:::soluzione
$G = (3.5;\ 4.5)\,\mathrm{cm}$. The moments are $I_{x_G} = 884\,\mathrm{cm^4}$, $I_{y_G} = 596\,\mathrm{cm^4}$, and $I_{x_G y_G} = -420\,\mathrm{cm^4}$. The principal moment $I_1 = 1184\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ acts about the axis rotated counterclockwise by $35.54^\circ$ from $x_G$, and $I_2 = 296\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ about the perpendicular axis.
:::
