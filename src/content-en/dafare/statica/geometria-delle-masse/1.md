Find the centroid of the inverted T-section shown. The bottom flange measures $16 \times 2\,\mathrm{cm}$, and the web measures $2 \times 16\,\mathrm{cm}$.

::struttura{poligoni="-8,0 8,0 8,2 1,2 1,18 -1,18 -1,2 -8,2" frecce="-9.5,0,9.5,0,x;0,-0.8,0,19.5,y" quote="-8 0,8 0,16 cm,1.1;8 0,8 18,18 cm,1.3;-1 18,1 18,2 cm,-2.4;-8 0,-8 2,2 cm,-0.9" riferimento=0 titolo="Inverted T-section with flange dimensions $16 \times 2\,\mathrm{cm}$ and web dimensions $2 \times 16\,\mathrm{cm}$"}

:::soluzione
The centroid lies on the vertical axis of symmetry, $y_G = 5.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ above the bottom edge.
:::
