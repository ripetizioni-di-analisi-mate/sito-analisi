The channel section shown has overall height $16\,\mathrm{cm}$. The web and flanges are $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ thick, and each flange has overall width $6\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find the centroid and the centroidal second moments of area.

:::soluzione
The centroid is $G = (2, 8)\,\mathrm{cm}$ relative to the axes shown. The moments are $I_{x_G} = 1472\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ and $I_{y_G} = 128\,\mathrm{cm^4}$.
:::
