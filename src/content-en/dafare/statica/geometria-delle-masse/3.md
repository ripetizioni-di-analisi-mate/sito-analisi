The rectangular section shown measures $30 \times 20\,\mathrm{cm}$ and has a circular hole of radius $5\,\mathrm{cm}$. The center of the hole lies $20\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the left edge and $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the bottom edge. Find the centroid of the section.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 30,0 30,20 0,20" cerchi="20,10,5,foro" punti="20,10" frecce="0,0,33,0,x;0,0,0,23,y" quote="0 0,30 0,30 cm,1.5;30 0,30 20,20 cm,1.5;0 10,20 10,20 cm,-7;20 10,25 10,r = 5 cm,1.5" riferimento=0 titolo="Rectangle of dimensions $30 \times 20\,\mathrm{cm}$ with a hole of radius $5\,\mathrm{cm}$"}

:::soluzione
$x_G = \dfrac{20\,(18 - \pi)}{24 - \pi}\,\mathrm{cm} = 14.25\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $y_G = 10\,\mathrm{cm}$, with the axes along the left and bottom edges.
:::
