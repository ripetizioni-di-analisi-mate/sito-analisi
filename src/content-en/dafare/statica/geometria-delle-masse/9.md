---
titolo: "Second moment of area and radius of gyration of a tube"
teoria: [momenti-notevoli, momenti-d-inerzia, raggi-d-inerzia]
parole: ["annulus", "Pipe", "polar moment", "radius of gyration"]
---

A tube has outside diameter $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ and inside diameter $8\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find its cross-sectional area, second moment of area about a diameter, polar moment of area about its center, and radius of gyration.

:::soluzione
$A = 20\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2} = 62.83\,\mathrm{cm^2}$; $I_{x_G} = 260\pi\,\mathrm{cm^4} = 816.81\,\mathrm{cm^4}$; $I_p = 520\pi\,\mathrm{cm^4} = 1633.63\,\mathrm{cm^4}$; $\rho = \sqrt{13}\,\mathrm{cm} = 3.61\,\mathrm{cm}$.
:::
