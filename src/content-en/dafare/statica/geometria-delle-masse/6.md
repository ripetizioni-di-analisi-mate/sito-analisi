The box section shown has outside dimensions $20 \times 30\,\mathrm{cm}$ (width by height) and wall thickness $2\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find $I_{x_G}$ and $I_{y_G}$.

::struttura{poligoni="-10,-15 10,-15 10,15 -10,15" fori="-8,-13 8,-13 8,13 -8,13" frecce="-11,0,12.5,0,x_G;0,-15.6,0,17.5,y_G" quote="-10 -15,10 -15,20 cm,1.3;-10 -15,-10 15,30 cm,-1.6;-10 15,-8 15,2 cm,-0.9" riferimento=0 titolo="Box section of outside dimensions $20 \times 30\,\mathrm{cm}$ and wall thickness $2\,\mathrm{cm}$"}

:::soluzione
$I_{x_G} = \dfrac{64\,696}{3}\,\mathrm{cm^4} = 21\,565.33\,\mathrm{cm^4}$, $I_{y_G} = \dfrac{33\,376}{3}\,\mathrm{cm^4} = 11\,125.33\,\mathrm{cm^4}$.
:::
