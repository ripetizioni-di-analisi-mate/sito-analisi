The section shown is a rectangle of width $20\,\mathrm{cm}$ and height $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ with a semicircle of radius $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ removed. The diameter of the semicircle lies along the bottom edge, and its center is at the midpoint of that edge. Find the centroid.

:::soluzione
The centroid lies on the axis of symmetry, $y_G = \dfrac{220}{3\,(16 - \pi)}\,\mathrm{cm} = 5.70\,\mathrm{cm}$ above the bottom edge.
:::
