---
titolo: "Second moments of area of a rectangle about rotated axes"
teoria: [rotazione-degli-assi, momenti-notevoli, cerchio-di-mohr]
parole: ["rotation of axes", "rectangle", "product of inertia", "Mohr's circle"]
---

A rectangle of width $8\,\mathrm{cm}$ along $x$ and height $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ along $y$ has its centroid at the origin. Find $I_\xi$, $I_\eta$, and $I_{\xi\eta}$ about centroidal axes rotated counterclockwise by $60^\circ$ from axes $x$ and $y$.

:::soluzione
$I_\xi = 672\,\mathrm{cm^4}$, $I_\eta = 992\,\mathrm{cm^4}$, $I_{\xi\eta} = 160\sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{cm^4} = 277.13\,\mathrm{cm^4}$.
:::
