The right triangle shown has horizontal leg $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ and vertical leg $6\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find the centroid, the centroidal moments $I_{x_G}$, $I_{y_G}$, and $I_{x_G y_G}$, and the moments $I_x$, $I_y$, and $I_{xy}$ about axes along the legs.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 12,0 0,6" frecce="0,0,13.5,0,x;0,0,0,7.5,y" quote="0 0,12 0,12 cm,0.9;0 0,0 6,6 cm,-0.9" riferimento=0 titolo="Right triangle with legs of lengths $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ along the axes"}

:::soluzione
$G = (4, 2)\,\mathrm{cm}$; $I_{x_G} = 72\,\mathrm{cm^4}$, $I_{y_G} = 288\,\mathrm{cm^4}$, $I_{x_G y_G} = -72\,\mathrm{cm^4}$; $I_x = 216\,\mathrm{cm^4}$, $I_y = 864\,\mathrm{cm^4}$, $I_{xy} = 216\,\mathrm{cm^4}$.
:::
