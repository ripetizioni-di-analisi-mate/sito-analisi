The section shown is bounded by the $x$ axis and the parabola $y = 4 - \dfrac{x^2}{4}$, where $-4 \leq x \leq 4$ and lengths are in $\mathrm{cm}$. Use integration to find its area, its centroid, its second moment of area $I_x$ about the base, and its centroidal moments $I_{x_G}$ and $I_{y_G}$.

$A = \dfrac{64}{3}\,\mathrm{cm^2} = 21.33\,\mathrm{cm^2}$; $G = (0;\ 1.6)\,\mathrm{cm}$; $I_x = \dfrac{8192}{105}\,\mathrm{cm^4} = 78.02\,\mathrm{cm^4}$; $I_{x_G} = \dfrac{4096}{175}\,\mathrm{cm^4} = 23.41\,\mathrm{cm^4}$; $I_{y_G} = \dfrac{1024}{15}\,\mathrm{cm^4} = 68.27\,\mathrm{cm^4}$.
