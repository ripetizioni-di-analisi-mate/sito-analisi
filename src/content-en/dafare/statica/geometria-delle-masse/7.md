Find the centroid and centroidal second moments of area of the asymmetric I-section shown. The bottom flange measures $16 \times 2\,\mathrm{cm}$, the web $2 \times 20\,\mathrm{cm}$, and the top flange $8 \times 2\,\mathrm{cm}$.

::struttura{poligoni="-8,0 8,0 8,2 1,2 1,22 4,22 4,24 -4,24 -4,22 -1,22 -1,2 -8,2" frecce="-9.5,0,9.5,0,x;0,-0.8,0,25.5,y" quote="-8 0,8 0,16 cm,1.2;-4 24,4 24,8 cm,-3.2;8 0,8 24,24 cm,1.6;1 2,1 22,20 cm,3;-8 0,-8 2,2 cm,-1;-4 22,-4 24,2 cm,-1" riferimento=0 titolo="Asymmetric I-section of height $24\,\mathrm{cm}$"}

:::soluzione
The centroid is $y_G = 10\,\mathrm{cm}$ above the bottom edge. The moments are $I_{x_G} = \dfrac{20\,416}{3}\,\mathrm{cm^4} = 6805.33\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ and $I_{y_G} = \dfrac{2344}{3}\,\mathrm{cm^4} = 781.33\,\mathrm{cm^4}$.
:::
