Find the centroid and centroidal second moments of area $I_{x_G}$ and $I_{y_G}$ for the T-section shown. Its web measures $2 \times 10\,\mathrm{cm}$, and its flange measures $10 \times 2\,\mathrm{cm}$.

::struttura{poligoni="-1,0 1,0 1,10 5,10 5,12 -5,12 -5,10 -1,10" frecce="-6.5,0,6.5,0,x;0,-0.8,0,13.4,y" quote="-5 12,5 12,10 cm,-2.7;-1 0,1 0,2 cm,1;5 0,5 12,12 cm,1.2;-5 10,-5 12,2 cm,-0.8" riferimento=0 titolo="T-section with web dimensions $2 \times 10\,\mathrm{cm}$ and flange dimensions $10 \times 2\,\mathrm{cm}$"}

:::soluzione
The centroid is $y_G = 8\,\mathrm{cm}$ above the bottom edge. The moments are $I_{x_G} = \dfrac{1600}{3}\,\mathrm{cm^4} = 533.33\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ and $I_{y_G} = \dfrac{520}{3}\,\mathrm{cm^4} = 173.33\,\mathrm{cm^4}$.
:::
