The triangular truss shown has the joints $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (6, 0)$ and $C = (3, 4)$ in meters. In $A$ there is a hinge and in $B$ a roller support that moves along a vertical plane. Classify the truss and tell if it is in equilibrium with a vertical $P$ force applied at $C$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,3,4" aste="A-B;A-C;B-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,0" forze="C,0,-1.3,P" quote="A,B,6 m,1.1;6 0,6 4,4 m,1.2" titolo="Triangular truss with a roller at $B$ on a vertical surface"}

:::soluzione
It equals $2n = a + v = 6$, but the lines of action of the three reactions all pass through $A$: the truss is unstable and statically indeterminate, with $l = i = 1$. There is no equilibrium, because in the rotation around $A$ the moment of $P$ equals $-3P \neq 0$ and that of the reactions is zero.
:::
