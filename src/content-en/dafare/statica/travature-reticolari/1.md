The truss shown has the joints $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (6, 0)$ and $C = (3, 4)$ in meters, a pin at $A$ and a roller support on a horizontal plane in $B$. In $C$ the horizontal force $P = 48\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. Calculate support reactions and axial forces in $AB$, $AC$ and $BC$ members.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,3,4" aste="A-B;A-C;B-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,1.5,0,P" quote="A,B,6 m,1.1;6 0,6 4,4 m,0.9" titolo="Triangular truss with horizontal force $P = 48\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$"}

:::soluzione
$H_A = 48\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left, $V_A = 32\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward, $V_B = 32\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward; $N_{AC} = 40\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension), $N_{BC} = -40\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression), $N_{AB} = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension).
:::
