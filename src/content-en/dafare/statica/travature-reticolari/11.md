The Warren truss shown consists of triangular panels with base $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and height $2\,\mathrm{m}$. It has a pin at $A$, a roller at $D$, and an overhang formed by joints $I$ and $L$. A downward force $P = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at the overhang tip $L$. Find the support reactions and the axial forces in members $IL$, $DL$, $DI$, and $HI$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;D,9,0;L,12,0;E,1.5,2;G,4.5,2;H,7.5,2;I,10.5,2" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-L;E-G;G-H;H-I;A-E;E-B;B-G;G-C;C-H;H-D;D-I;I-L" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;D,carrello" forze="L,0,-1.3,P" quote="A,B,3 m,1.2;B,C,3 m,1.2;C,D,3 m,1.2;D,L,3 m,1.2;12 0,12 2,2 m,0.9" titolo="Warren truss with force $P = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ at the overhang tip"}

:::soluzione
The reactions are $V_A = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward, $V_D = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, and $H_A = 0$. The forces $N_{IL} = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $N_{HI} = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$ are tensile. The forces $N_{DL} = -9\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $N_{DI} = -15\,\mathrm{kN}$ are compressive.
:::
