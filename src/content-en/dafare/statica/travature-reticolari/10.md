The triangular truss shown has the joints $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (8, 0)$ and $C = (4, 3)$ in meters, a pin at $A$ and $B$ a roller support on inclined plane, with reaction $R_B$ directed as the $\left(-\dfrac{3}{5}, \dfrac{4}{5}\right)$ unit vector. In $C$, a force $F = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts directed along the line $CB$, towards $B$. Calculate reactions and axial forces in members.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,8,0;C,4,3" aste="A-B;A-C;B-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,306.87" forze="C,1.2,-0.9,F" quote="A,B,8 m,1.3;0 0,0 3,3 m,-0.9" titolo="Triangular truss with force $F$ acting along $CB$ and an inclined roller support at $B$"}

:::soluzione
$N_{AC} = 0$, $N_{BC} = -20\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression), $N_{AB} = 7\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension); $R_B = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$, $H_A = 7\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left, $V_A = 0$.
:::
