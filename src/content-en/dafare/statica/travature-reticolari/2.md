The truss shown has the joints $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (4, 0)$ and $C = (4, 3)$ in meters and the members $AB$, $AC$, $BC$; in $A$ there is a hinge and in $B$ a horizontal roller support. In $C$ the vertical force $P = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. Locate the unloaded members and calculate the axial force in the member $BC$ and the reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,4,3" aste="A-B;A-C;B-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1.3,P" quote="A,B,4 m,1.1;4 0,4 3,3 m,0.9" titolo="Triangular truss with force $P$ on the vertical through the roller"}

:::soluzione
At joint $B$ the roller support and the member $BC$ are vertical, so the horizontal equation gives $N_{AB} = 0$; at joint $C$ the force and member $BC$ are vertical, so the horizontal equation gives $N_{AC} = 0$. Also $N_{BC} = -24\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression), $V_B = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, $H_A = V_A = 0$.
:::
