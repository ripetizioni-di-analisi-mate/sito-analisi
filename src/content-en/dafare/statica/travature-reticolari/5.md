The Pratt truss shown has four $3\,\mathrm{m}$ panels and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ height, pin at $A$ and roller support at $E$. At joints $B$, $C$, $D$ of the bottom chord are hung three forces of $30\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. Calculate with the Ritter method the axial forces in the $FG$, $FC$ and $BC$ members.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;D,9,0;E,12,0;F,3,4;G,6,4;H,9,4" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E;F-G;G-H;A-F;E-H;B-F;C-G;D-H;F-C;H-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;E,carrello" forze="3 -1.6,0,-1.6,;6 -1.6,0,-1.6,;9 -1.6,0,-1.6," etichette="3.3,-1.3,P;6.3,-1.3,P;9.3,-1.3,P" quote="A,B,3 m,2.3;B,C,3 m,2.3;C,D,3 m,2.3;D,E,3 m,2.3;12 0,12 4,4 m,0.9" titolo="Pratt truss with three forces $P = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$ suspended from the bottom chord"}

:::soluzione
$V_A = V_E = 45\,\mathrm{kN}$; $N_{FG} = -45\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression), $N_{FC} = 18.75\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension), $N_{BC} = 33.75\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension).
:::
