The Warren truss shown has basic triangles $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and height $2\,\mathrm{m}$, pin at $A$ and roller support at $D$. At joints $E$, $G$, $H$, three forces of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ act downward. Calculate axial forces in all members.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;D,9,0;E,1.5,2;G,4.5,2;H,7.5,2" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;E-G;G-H;A-E;E-B;B-G;G-C;C-H;H-D" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;D,carrello" forze="E,0,-1.3,P;G,0,-1.3,P;H,0,-1.3,P" quote="A,B,3 m,1.2;B,C,3 m,1.2;C,D,3 m,1.2;9 0,9 2,2 m,0.9" titolo="Warren truss under three forces $P = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

:::soluzione
$V_A = V_D = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$; $N_{AB} = N_{CD} = 13.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{BC} = 22.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{EB} = N_{CH} = 7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension); $N_{AE} = N_{HD} = -22.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{EG} = N_{GH} = -18\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{BG} = N_{GC} = -7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression).
:::
