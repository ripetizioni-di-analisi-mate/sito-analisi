The Howe truss shown has four $3\,\mathrm{m}$ panels and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ height, pin at $A$, roller support at $E$ and three $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ forces down at joints $F$, $G$, $H$ of the top chord. Calculate axial forces in the $AF$, $AB$, $BF$, $FG$ and $BG$ members.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;D,9,0;E,12,0;F,3,4;G,6,4;H,9,4" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E;F-G;G-H;A-F;E-H;B-F;C-G;D-H;B-G;D-G" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;E,carrello" forze="F,0,-1.5,P;G,0,-1.5,P;H,0,-1.5,P" quote="A,B,3 m,1.3;B,C,3 m,1.3;C,D,3 m,1.3;D,E,3 m,1.3;12 0,12 4,4 m,0.9" titolo="Howe truss under three forces $P = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

:::soluzione
$N_{AF} = -37.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{FG} = -22.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{BG} = -12.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression); $N_{AB} = 22.5\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{BF} = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension).
:::
