The truss shown has two panels measuring $3\,\mathrm{m}$ by $4\,\mathrm{m}$, the $AD$, $BE$, $CG$ verticals and the diagonals $DB$ and $BG$, which form a V with the vertex in $B$; it is supported by a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $C$. At joints $D$, $E$, $G$ the forces $10\,\mathrm{kN}$, $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ act downward. Calculate axial forces in all members.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;D,0,4;E,3,4;G,6,4" aste="A-B;B-C;D-E;E-G;A-D;B-E;C-G;D-B;B-G" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" forze="D,0,-1.4,P_1;E,0,-1.4,P_2;G,0,-1.4,P_1" quote="A,B,3 m,1.1;B,C,3 m,1.1;6 0,6 4,4 m,0.9" titolo="V-shaped truss with $P_1 = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $D$ and $G$ and $P_2 = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $E$"}

:::soluzione
$V_A = V_C = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$; $N_{AB} = N_{BC} = 0$; $N_{AD} = N_{BE} = N_{CG} = -20\,\mathrm{kN}$, $N_{DE} = N_{EG} = -7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ (compression); $N_{DB} = N_{BG} = 12.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ (tension).
:::
