The truss shown has four $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ height panels, the $AF$ and $EL$ end verticals and the $AG$, $BH$, $HD$, $IE$ diagonals; it is supported by a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $E$. At joint $C$ hangs the downward force $P = 40\,\mathrm{kN}$. Locate the unloaded members and calculate the stresses in the vertical $CH$ and in the chords $BC$ and $CD$.
