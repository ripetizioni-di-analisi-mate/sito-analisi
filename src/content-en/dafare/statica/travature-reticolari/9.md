The roof truss shown has span $8\,\mathrm{m}$, height $3\,\mathrm{m}$, a horizontal tie $ABCDE$ with joints spaced at $2\,\mathrm{m}$, rafters of slope $\dfrac{3}{4}$, king post $CG$, intermediate verticals $BF$ and $DH$, and diagonal struts $FC$ and $HC$. It has a pin at $A$ and a roller at $E$. Downward forces of $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ act at joints $F$, $G$, and $H$. Use a vertical cut between $B$ and $C$ to find the axial forces in rafter segment $FG$, diagonal strut $FC$, and tie segment $BC$.
