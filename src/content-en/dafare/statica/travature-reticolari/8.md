The cantilever truss shown is attached to the wall with a hinge $A = (0, 0)$ and a roller support at $B = (0, 3)$ that moves along the vertical wall. The other joints are $C = (4, 3)$, $D = (4, 0)$, $E = (8, 0)$ and the $AB$, $BC$, $CE$, $AD$, $DE$, $CD$, $AC$ members. In $D$ a force $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts and in $E$ a force $24\,\mathrm{kN}$, both downward. Calculating axial forces in members and reactions.
