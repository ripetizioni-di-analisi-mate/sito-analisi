The beam shown has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, span $4\,\mathrm{m}$, and carries the uniform load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and the uniform distributed couple $m = 2\,\mathrm{kN\,m/m}$ clockwise. Get $T(x)$ and $M(x)$, the reactions, the section where the shear vanishes and the section where the moment is maximum.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.45,0.45,q" coppie="1 0,-1;2 0,-1,m;3 0,-1" quote="A,B,4 m,1" titolo="Simply supported beam with $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and distributed clockwise couple $m = 2\,\mathrm{kN\,m/m}$"}

:::soluzione
$T(x) = 4 - 3x$, $M(x) = 6x - \dfrac{3}{2}\,x^2$; $V_A = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_B = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward; $T = 0$ in $x = \dfrac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$; $M_{\max} = 6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in $x = 2\,\mathrm{m}$, where $T = m = -2\,\mathrm{kN}$, because the clockwise distributed couple has negative $m$.
:::
