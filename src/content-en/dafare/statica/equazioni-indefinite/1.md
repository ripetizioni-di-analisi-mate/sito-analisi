The beam shown has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, span $8\,\mathrm{m}$, and carries the uniform load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Get with the differential equilibrium equations the expressions of $T(x)$ and $M(x)$, with the origin in $A$, the support reactions and the maximum moment.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.8,0.8,q" quote="A,B,8 m,1" titolo="Simply supported beam under uniform load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

:::soluzione
$T(x) = 12 - 3x$ ($\mathrm{kN}$), $M(x) = 12x - \dfrac{3}{2}\,x^2$ ($\mathrm{kN\,m}$); $V_A = V_B = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, $H_A = 0$; $M_{\max} = 24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in $x = 4\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
