The beam shown has a roller support with vertical reaction at $A$ and a pin at $B$, span $4\,\mathrm{m}$, and carries the distributed axial load $p = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ directed to the right. Find $N(x)$, with the origin in $A$, and the horizontal reaction at $B$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello;B,cerniera" frecce="0.1,0.4,0.5,0.4;0.9,0.4,1.3,0.4;1.7,0.4,2.1,0.4;2.5,0.4,2.9,0.4;3.3,0.4,3.7,0.4" etichette="0.3,0.7,p" quote="A,B,4 m,1" titolo="Beam with distributed axial load $p = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ to the right"}

:::soluzione
$N(x) = -3x$ ($\mathrm{kN}$), increasing compression from $0$ in $A$ to $-12\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $B$; $H_B = -12\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left.
:::
