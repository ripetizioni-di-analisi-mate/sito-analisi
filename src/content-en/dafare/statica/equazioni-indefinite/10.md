The Gerber beam shown is fixed at $A$, it has an internal pin at $C$ $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ and a roller support at $B$ $5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$; carries the uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over the entire length. Find $T(x)$ and $M(x)$ with the differential equilibrium equations, reactions and the positive maximum moment.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" cerniere="C" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,C,2 m,1;C,B,3 m,1" titolo="Gerber beam under uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

:::soluzione
$T(x) = 7 - 2x$, $M(x) = -10 + 7x - x^2$; $V_A = 7\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, $M_A = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise, $V_B = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward; $M_{\max} = 2.25\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in $x = 3.5\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
