The beam shown has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, span $8\,\mathrm{m}$; carries the uniform $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ load on the section $AC$, along $4\,\mathrm{m}$, and the force $F = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ acting downward at $D$, $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Find the reactions, position, and value of the maximum moment and moment at $C$ and $D$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,0;D,6,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,C,0.7,0.7,q" forze="D,0,-1.6,F" quote="A,C,4 m,1;C,D,2 m,1;D,B,2 m,1" titolo="Simply supported beam with uniform load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on $AC$ and force $F = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $D$"}

:::soluzione
$V_A = 10.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_B = 7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward; $M_{\max} = 18.375\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in $x = 3.5\,\mathrm{m}$; $M(4) = 18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, $M(6) = 15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
:::
