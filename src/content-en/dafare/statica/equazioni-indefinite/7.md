The cantilever shown is fixed at $A$ and has length $3\,\mathrm{m}$. Its trapezoidal load varies from $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at the fixed end to $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at free end $B$. Determine the fixed-end reactions and the bending moment at a section a distance $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from the fixed end.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.2,q" quote="A,B,3 m,0.6" etichette="-0.15,0.78,6 kN/m;3.15,0.38,2 kN/m" titolo="Cantilever under a trapezoidal load varying from $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ to $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

:::soluzione
$V_A = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, $M_A = 15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise, $H_A = 0$; $M(1) = -\dfrac{52}{9} \approx -5.78\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
:::
