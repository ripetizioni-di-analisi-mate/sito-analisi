The beam shown has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, span $6\,\mathrm{m}$, and carries the uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and downward force $F = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $C$, $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Find the reactions, the left and right shear values of $C$, the position and the maximum moment value.
