The cantilever shown is fixed at $A$ and has length $3\,\mathrm{m}$. Its free end $B$ carries a downward force $F = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ and a counterclockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Determine $T(x)$ and $M(x)$, the fixed-end reactions, and the section where the bending moment is zero.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" forze="B,0,-0.9,F" coppie="B,1,ℳ" quote="A,B,3 m,0.6" titolo="Cantilever with $F = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ and counterclockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at $B$"}

:::soluzione
$T(x) = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$, $M(x) = 9 - 4\,(3 - x) = 4x - 3$ ($\mathrm{kN\,m}$); $V_A = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, $M_A = 3\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise; $M = 0$ in $x = 0.75\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
