The beam shown has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, span $5\,\mathrm{m}$, and carries the uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and couple $\mathcal{M} = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise, applied in the $B$ support. Find $T(x)$ and $M(x)$, reactions and the maximum positive moment.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" coppie="B,-1,ℳ" quote="A,B,5 m,1" titolo="Simply supported beam with uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and clockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at $B$"}

:::soluzione
$T(x) = 8 - 4x$, $M(x) = 8x - 2x^2$; $V_A = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_B = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward; $M(5^-) = -10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$; $M_{\max} = 8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in $x = 2\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
