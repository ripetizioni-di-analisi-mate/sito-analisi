The cantilever shown is fixed at $A$, has length $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$, and carries a uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Taking the fixed end as the origin, use the differential equilibrium equations to determine $T(x)$ and $M(x)$ and find the fixed-end reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2.5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" carichi="A,B,0.3,0.3,q" quote="A,B,2.5 m,0.5" titolo="Cantilever under uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

:::soluzione
$T(x) = 4\,(2.5 - x)$ ($\mathrm{kN}$), $M(x) = -2\,(2.5 - x)^2$ ($\mathrm{kN\,m}$); $V_A = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, $M_A = 12.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise, $H_A = 0$.
:::
