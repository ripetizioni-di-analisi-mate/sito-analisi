The beam shown has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, span $6\,\mathrm{m}$, and carries a triangular load that equals $q_0 = 12\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ in $A$ and vanishes in $B$. Find $T(x)$ and $M(x)$ with the differential equilibrium equations, reactions, the section where the moment is maximum and its value.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.9,0,q_0" quote="A,B,6 m,1" titolo="Simply supported beam under a triangular load of intensity $q_0 = 12\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $A$"}

:::soluzione
$T(x) = 24 - 12x + x^2$, $M(x) = 24x - 6x^2 + \dfrac{x^3}{3}$; $V_A = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_B = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward; $M_{\max} = 16\sqrt{3} \approx 27.71\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in $x = 6 - 2\sqrt{3} \approx 2.54\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
