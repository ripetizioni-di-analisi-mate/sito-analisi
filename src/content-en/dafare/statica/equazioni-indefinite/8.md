A simply supported beam of span $4\,\mathrm{m}$, with $A$ hinge and $B$ roller support, carries the load $q(x) = 3\sin\dfrac{\pi x}{4}$ ($\mathrm{kN/m}$, with $x$ in meters measured by $A$). Find $T(x)$, $M(x)$, reactions and maximum moment.

:::soluzione
$T(x) = \dfrac{12}{\pi}\cos\dfrac{\pi x}{4}$, $M(x) = \dfrac{48}{\pi^2}\sin\dfrac{\pi x}{4}$; $V_A = V_B = \dfrac{12}{\pi} \approx 3.82\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward; $M_{\max} = \dfrac{48}{\pi^2} \approx 4.86\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at midspan.
:::
