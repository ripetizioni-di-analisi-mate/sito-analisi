A simply supported beam of span $4\,\mathrm{m}$ has a pin at $A$ ($x = 0$) and a roller at $B$. Its bending moment is
$$
M(x) = \frac{x\,(16 - x^2)}{2} \quad (\mathrm{kN\,m}) .
$$
Determine the applied load $q(x)$, the support reactions, and the maximum bending moment.

:::soluzione
The load $q(x) = 3x$ (in $\mathrm{kN/m}$) is triangular, increasing from $0$ at $A$ to $12\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $B$. Reactions $V_A = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_B = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$ act upward. The maximum moment is $M_{\max} = \dfrac{64\sqrt{3}}{9} \approx 12.32\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at $x = \dfrac{4}{\sqrt{3}} \approx 2.31\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
