The beam shown has a pin at $A$, a roller support at $B$ $8\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ and an overhang of $2\,\mathrm{m}$ to the free end $C$; carries the uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over the entire length. Find reactions, the moment on $B$ support, the maximum moment in span and the zero point of the moment.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,8,0;C,10,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,C,0.9,0.9,q" quote="A,B,8 m,1;B,C,2 m,1" titolo="Overhanging beam under uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

:::soluzione
$V_A = 7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_B = 12.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward; $M(8) = -4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$; $M_{\max} = \dfrac{225}{16} \approx 14.06\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in $x = 3.75\,\mathrm{m}$; null point in $x = 7.5\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
