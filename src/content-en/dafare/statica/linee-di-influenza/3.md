An $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$ long cantilever is fixed at $A$ and free at the other extreme. Find the $V_A$ and $M_A$ reaction moment influence lines and calculate the reactions produced by an $P = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward force applied to the free end.

:::soluzione
$\eta_{V_A}(\xi) = 1$, $\eta_{M_A}(\xi) = \xi$ (in $\mathrm{m}$, $M_A$ positive counterclockwise); $V_A = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward and $M_A = 30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise.
:::
