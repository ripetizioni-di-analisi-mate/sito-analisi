The simply supported beam shown, of span $8\,\mathrm{m}$, carries downward forces $P_1 = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$, $P_2 = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$, and $P_3 = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ at distances $1\,\mathrm{m}$, $4\,\mathrm{m}$, and $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from pin $A$. Calculate with the influence lines the moment and shear at section $S$ $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,2,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="1 0,0,-1.4,P_1;4 0,0,-2,P_2;6 0,0,-1,P_3" quote="A,1 0,1 m,0.9;1 0,S,1 m,0.9;S,4 0,2 m,0.9;4 0,6 0,2 m,0.9;6 0,B,2 m,0.9" titolo="Simply supported beam under three forces"}

:::soluzione
$M_S = 16 \cdot \dfrac{3}{4} + 24 \cdot 1 + 8 \cdot \dfrac{1}{2} = 40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ (lower fibers in tension); $T_S = 16 \cdot \left(-\dfrac{1}{8}\right) + 24 \cdot \dfrac{1}{2} + 8 \cdot \dfrac{1}{4} = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$.
:::
