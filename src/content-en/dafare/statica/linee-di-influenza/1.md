A simply supported beam of span $5\,\mathrm{m}$ has a pin at $A$ and the roller support at $B$. Dig the influence lines of the reactions $V_A$ and $V_B$ and calculate their ordinate by $\xi = 2\,\mathrm{m}$, with $\xi$ measured by $A$.

:::soluzione
$\eta_{V_A}(\xi) = \dfrac{5 - \xi}{5}$, $\eta_{V_B}(\xi) = \dfrac{\xi}{5}$; in $\xi = 2\,\mathrm{m}$ the ordinate are $\dfrac{3}{5}$ and $\dfrac{2}{5}$.
:::
