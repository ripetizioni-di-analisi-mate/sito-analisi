A simply supported beam of span $6\,\mathrm{m}$ has a pin at $A$ and the roller support at $B$. Get the influence line for shear at section $S$ $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, with the ordinates immediately to the left and immediately to the right of $S$.

:::soluzione
$\eta_T(\xi) = -\dfrac{\xi}{6}$ for $0 \le \xi < 1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ and $\eta_T(\xi) = \dfrac{6 - \xi}{6}$ for $1.5 < \xi \le 6\,\mathrm{m}$; in $S$ jumps from $-\dfrac{1}{4}$ to $\dfrac{3}{4}$.
:::
