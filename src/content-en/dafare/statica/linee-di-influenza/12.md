The beam shown has a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$ a distance $8\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and overhang $BC$ of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$. A moving force $P = 40\,\mathrm{kN}$ and a uniform load $q = 10\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ of variable length can act. Calculate the maximum value and the minimum value of the moment at section $S$ at midspan.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;S,4,0;B,8,0;C,10,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,S,4 m,0.9;S,B,4 m,0.9;B,C,2 m,0.9" titolo="Overhanging beam showing midspan section $S$"}

:::soluzione
$M_{S,\max} = 40 \cdot 2 + 10 \cdot 8 = 160\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with $P$ in $S$ and $q$ on $AB$; $M_{S,\min} = 40 \cdot (-1) + 10 \cdot (-1) = -50\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with $P$ in $C$ and $q$ on $BC$.
:::
