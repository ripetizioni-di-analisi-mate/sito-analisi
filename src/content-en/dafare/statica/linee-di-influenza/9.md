The Gerber beam shown consists of beam $AC$, with a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$ a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and overhang $BC$ of length $1\,\mathrm{m}$, together with beam $CD$, of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$, connected at $C$ and supported by a roller at $D$. Find the $V_B$ and bending moment influence lines in $B$, and calculate the minimum value of $M_B$ produced by an $P = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ moving force and a uniform $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ load of variable length.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,5,0;D,8,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9;B,C,1 m,0.9;C,D,3 m,0.9" titolo="Gerber beam"}

:::soluzione
$\eta_{V_B}(\xi) = \dfrac{\xi}{4}$ on $AC$ (ordinate $\dfrac{5}{4}$ in $C$) and $\eta_{V_B}(\xi) = \dfrac{5}{4} \cdot \dfrac{8 - \xi}{3}$ on $CD$; $\eta_{M_B} = 0$ on $AB$, $-(\xi - 4)$ on $BC$, $-\dfrac{8 - \xi}{3}$ on $CD$ (minimum $-1\,\mathrm{m}$ in $C$). $M_{B,\min} = -32\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with $P$ in $C$ and $q$ from $B$ to $D$.
:::
