The beam shown has a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$ a distance $5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and overhang $BC$ of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$. Get the moment and shear influence lines at section $D$ of the overhang, $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;D,6,0;C,7,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,B,5 m,0.9;B,D,1 m,0.9;D,C,1 m,0.9" titolo="Overhanging beam showing section $D$"}

:::soluzione
$\eta_M = 0$ and $\eta_T = 0$ for $0 \le \xi < 6\,\mathrm{m}$; for $6 < \xi \le 7\,\mathrm{m}$, $\eta_M(\xi) = -(\xi - 6)$ (minimum $-1\,\mathrm{m}$ in $C$) and $\eta_T(\xi) = 1$.
:::
