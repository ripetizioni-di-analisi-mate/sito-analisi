The Gerber beam shown consists of cantilever $AC$, fixed at $A$ and of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$, and beam $CB$ of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, connected at $C$ and supported by a roller at $B$. Derive the influence lines for support moment $M_A$ and reaction $V_B$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,6,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" quote="A,C,2 m,0.9;C,B,4 m,0.9" titolo="Gerber beam with a supporting cantilever"}

:::soluzione
$\eta_{M_A}(\xi) = \xi$ for $0 \le \xi \le 2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $\eta_{M_A}(\xi) = \dfrac{6 - \xi}{2}$ for $2 \le \xi \le 6\,\mathrm{m}$ (maximum $2\,\mathrm{m}$ in $C$); $\eta_{V_B} = 0$ on $AC$ and $\eta_{V_B}(\xi) = \dfrac{\xi - 2}{4}$ on $CB$.
:::
