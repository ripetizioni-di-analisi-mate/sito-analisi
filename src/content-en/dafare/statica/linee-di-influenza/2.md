The beam shown has a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$ a distance $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and overhang $BC$ of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$. Find the $V_A$ and $V_B$ influence lines and their ordinate with the unit load at $C$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,8,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,B,6 m,0.9;B,C,2 m,0.9" titolo="Overhanging beam"}

:::soluzione
$\eta_{V_A}(\xi) = \dfrac{6 - \xi}{6}$ and $\eta_{V_B}(\xi) = \dfrac{\xi}{6}$ for $0 \le \xi \le 8\,\mathrm{m}$; in $C$ the ordinate are $\eta_{V_A} = -\dfrac{1}{3}$ and $\eta_{V_B} = \dfrac{4}{3}$.
:::
