On a simply supported beam of span $12\,\mathrm{m}$, with the pin at $A$ and the roller support at $B$, a uniform $q = 10\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ load along $6\,\mathrm{m}$ passes. Find the position of the load that makes the maximum moment at section $S$ $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ and the maximum value.

:::soluzione
The load occupies the $2\,\mathrm{m}$ to $8\,\mathrm{m}$ section from $A$ (extreme $\dfrac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$ ordinate); area $12\,\mathrm{m^2}$, $M_{S,\max} = 120\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
:::
