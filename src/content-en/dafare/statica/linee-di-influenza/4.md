titolo: "Moment at a section of a simply supported beam"
teoria: [linee-trave-appoggiata, costruzione-grafica]
parole: ["influence line", "bending moment", "simply supported beam", "vertex"]
---

A simply supported beam of span $10\,\mathrm{m}$ has a pin at $A$ and the roller support at $B$. Find the influence line for the bending moment at section $S$ $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ and its ordinate maximum.

:::soluzione
$\eta_M(\xi) = \dfrac{3}{5}\,\xi$ for $0 \le \xi \le 4\,\mathrm{m}$ and $\eta_M(\xi) = \dfrac{2}{5}\,(10 - \xi)$ for $4 \le \xi \le 10\,\mathrm{m}$; ordinate maximum $2.4\,\mathrm{m}$ in $\xi = 4\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
