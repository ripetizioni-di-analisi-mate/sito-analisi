The beam shown has a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$ a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and an overhang $BC$ of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$. A uniform load $q = 10\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts along the entire beam. Find the reactions using the signed areas under their influence lines.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,C,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9;B,C,2 m,0.9" titolo="Overhanging beam under uniform load $q = 10\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

:::soluzione
The areas are $\dfrac{9}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$ for $\eta_{V_B} = \dfrac{\xi}{4}$ and $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$ for $\eta_{V_A} = \dfrac{4 - \xi}{4}$. The reactions $V_B = 45\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_A = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ both act upward.
:::
