The beam shown, of length $9\,\mathrm{m}$, has $A$ to $1\,\mathrm{m}$ roller support from end $E$ and $B$ to $2\,\mathrm{m}$ hinge from end $F$. Trace with Müller–Breslau theorem the influence lines of $V_A$ and $V_B$ and indicate their ordinate in $E$ and $F$.

::struttura{nodi="E,0,0;A,1,0;B,7,0;F,9,0" aste="E-F" vincoli="A,carrello;B,cerniera" quote="E,A,1 m,0.9;A,B,6 m,0.9;B,F,2 m,0.9" titolo="Beam with two overhangs"}

:::soluzione
With $\xi$ measured by $E$: $\eta_{V_A}(\xi) = \dfrac{7 - \xi}{6}$, with $\dfrac{7}{6}$ in $E$ and $-\dfrac{1}{3}$ in $F$; $\eta_{V_B}(\xi) = \dfrac{\xi - 1}{6}$, with $-\dfrac{1}{6}$ in $E$ and $\dfrac{4}{3}$ in $F$.
:::
