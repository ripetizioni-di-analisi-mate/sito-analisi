---
titolo: "Train of two moving point loads"
teoria: [convoglio-di-carichi, linee-trave-appoggiata]
parole: ["load train", "moving load", "maximum moment", "simply supported beam"]
---

On a simply supported beam of span $10\,\mathrm{m}$, with the pin at $A$ and the roller support at $B$, a load train of two forces passes down, $P_1 = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$ on the left and $P_2 = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ on the right, at a fixed distance of $2\,\mathrm{m}$. Calculate the maximum value of the moment at midspan and the position of the load train.

:::soluzione
$M_{\max} = 30 \cdot 2.5 + 20 \cdot 1.5 = 105\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with $P_1$ at midspan and $P_2$ $7\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ (with $P_2$ at midspan you get $95\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$).
:::
