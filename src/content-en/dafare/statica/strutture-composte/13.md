The Gerber beam shown has the segment $AC$ bound by a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, with the $BC$ overhang, and the segment $CD$ connected in $C$ by an internal hinge and resting on a roller support at $D$. In the hinge $C$, the vertical force $P = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward, applied to the extreme of the $BC$ overhang; the section $CD$ carries the uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Calculate the support reactions and the force transmitted by the hinge.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0;D,9,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" forze="C,0,-1.4,P" carichi="C,D,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,B,4 m,0.8;B,C,2 m,0.8;C,D,3 m,0.8" titolo="Gerber beam with force $P = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$ and load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on $CD$"}

:::soluzione
$V_D = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_B = 36\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward; $V_A = -12\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ down; $H_A = 0$. The hinge transmits a vertical force of $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ (upward on the segment $CD$).
:::
