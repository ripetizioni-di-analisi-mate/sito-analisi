The three-hinged arch shown consists of straight members $AC$ and $CB$ with span $10\,\mathrm{m}$. Crown $C$ is at midspan, $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$ above the springings. Springing $A$ has a pin, springing $B$ has a roller on a horizontal surface, and tie $AB$ connects them. The arch carries load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ uniformly over its horizontal projection and a downward force $P = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ applied to member $AC$ at the crown. Find the support reactions and the axial force in the tie.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,5,2.5;B,10,0" aste="A-C;C-B;A-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="0 3,10 3,0.6,0.6,q" forze="C,0,-1.4,P" quote="A,5 0,5 m,0.8;5 0,B,5 m,0.8;5 0,C,2.5 m,-0.6" titolo="Tied arch under load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and force $P = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ at the crown"}

:::soluzione
$H_A = 0$, with $V_A = V_B = 25\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. The tie carries tension $N = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$.
:::
