The three-hinged arch shown is formed by the straight $AC$ and $CB$ members. Compared to the springing $A$, the crown $C$ is located $4\,\mathrm{m}$ on the right and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ higher, the springing $B$ is located $10\,\mathrm{m}$ on the right and $2\,\mathrm{m}$ higher. The member $AC$ carries the load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, uniform on the horizontal projection. Calculate support reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,4;B,10,2" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" carichi="0 4.6,4 4.6,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,4 0,4 m,0.8;4 0,10 0,6 m,0.8;10 0,B,2 m,0.8;A,0 4,4 m,-0.8" tratteggi="0,0 10,0" titolo="Arch with springings at different elevations and load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ distributed over the projection of $AC$"}

:::soluzione
$V_A = 21\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_B = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward; $H_A = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right, $H_B = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left. The reaction at $B$ is directed along $BC$.
:::
