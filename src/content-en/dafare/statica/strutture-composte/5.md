The three-hinged frame shown has columns of height $4\,\mathrm{m}$, a beam of length $8\,\mathrm{m}$, and an internal hinge $C$ at midspan. At joint $D$ a horizontal force $F = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. Calculate the support reactions and the force that body $ABC$ exerts on body $CDE$ at $C$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,4,4;D,8,4;E,8,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,cerniera" forze="9.6 4,1.6,0,F" quote="A,B,4 m,-0.8;B,C,4 m,-0.8;C,D,4 m,-0.8" titolo="Three-hinged portal frame with force $F = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$ at joint $D$"}

:::soluzione
$H_A = -8\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $H_E = -8\,\mathrm{kN}$ (both $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left); $V_A = -8\,\mathrm{kN}$ (down), $V_E = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ (up). On the body $CDE$ acts in $C$ the force $X_C = -8\,\mathrm{kN}$, $Y_C = -8\,\mathrm{kN}$, directed along the line $AC$ because the body $ABC$ is unloaded.
:::
