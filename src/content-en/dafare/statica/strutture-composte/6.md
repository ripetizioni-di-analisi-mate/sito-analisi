---
titolo: "Thrust of a parabolic arch"
teoria: [arco-a-tre-cerniere, spinta-arco]
parole: ["three-hinged arch", "parabolic arch", "thrust", "arch rise", "span"]
---

A parabolic three-hinged arch has the springings $A$ and $B$ at the same elevation, the span $L = 20\,\mathrm{m}$, the $f = 5\,\mathrm{m}$ rise and the crown $C$ at midspan. Carry the load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, uniform on the horizontal projection. Calculate support reactions and thrust.

:::soluzione
$V_A = V_B = 60\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward; $H = \dfrac{qL^2}{8f} = 60\,\mathrm{kN}$ thrust, i.e. $H_A = 60\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and $H_B = 60\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left. In crown the traded force is horizontal and equals $60\,\mathrm{kN}$.
:::
