The beam shown is formed by the section $AC$, fixed at $A$, and the section $CB$, resting on a roller support at $B$; the two segments are connected in $C$ by an internal parallel-link connection with pin-ended links parallel to the axis. The vertical forces $P_1 = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $P_2 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$, directed downward, act at the midpoints of the two segments. Calculate the support reactions and the couple transmitted by the parallel-link support.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,6,0" aste="A-C;C-B" interni="C,doppio-pendolo,0" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" forze="1.5 0,0,-1.3,P_1;4.5 0,0,-1.3,P_2" quote="A,C,3 m,0.8;C,B,3 m,0.8" titolo="Beam with an internal parallel-link connection at $C$ and forces $P_1 = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $P_2 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

:::soluzione
$V_B = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward; $H_A = 0$, $V_A = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, $M_A = -6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, i.e. $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ clockwise. Parallel-link support does not transmit forces and transmits a couple of $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise on the $CB$ and counterclockwise on the $AC$.
:::
