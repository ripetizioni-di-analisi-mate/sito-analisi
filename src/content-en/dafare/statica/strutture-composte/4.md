The three-hinged frame shown has columns of height $5\,\mathrm{m}$, a beam of length $8\,\mathrm{m}$, and an internal hinge $C$ at midspan. The beam carries a uniform load $q = 12\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Calculate the support reactions and the force at hinge $C$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,5;C,4,5;D,8,5;E,8,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,cerniera" carichi="B,D,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,B,5 m,-0.8;B,C,4 m,-1.1;C,D,4 m,-1.1" titolo="Three-hinged portal frame with load $q = 12\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on the crossbeam"}

:::soluzione
$V_A = V_E = 48\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward; $H_A = 19.2\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right, $H_E = 19.2\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left. In $C$ the two parts exchange only a horizontal force of $19.2\,\mathrm{kN}$ (the crossbeam is in compression).
:::
