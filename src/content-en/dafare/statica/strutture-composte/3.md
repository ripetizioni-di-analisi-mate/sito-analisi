The Gerber beam shown has the section $AB$ fixed at $A$, the section $BD$ connected to $AB$ by the hinge $B$ and resting on a $C$ roller support, and the section $DE$ connected to $BD$ by the hinge $D$ and resting on a $E$ roller support. The whole beam carries the uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Calculate the support reactions and forces in the hinges.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,0;C,6,0;D,8,0;E,11,0" aste="A-B;B-D;D-E" cerniere="B;D" vincoli="A,incastro,180;C,carrello;E,carrello" carichi="A,E,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,B,2 m,0.8;B,C,4 m,0.8;C,D,2 m,0.8;D,E,3 m,0.8" titolo="Three-segment Gerber beam under uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

:::soluzione
$V_E = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$, $V_C = 27\,\mathrm{kN}$, $V_A = 11\,\mathrm{kN}$, all up; $M_A = 14\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise, $H_A = 0$. Hinges transmit vertical forces of $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $B$ and $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $D$.
:::
