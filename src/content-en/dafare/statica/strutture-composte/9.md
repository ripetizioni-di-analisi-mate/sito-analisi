The frame shown is formed by the body $ABC$, hinged to the ground in $A$, and the column $CD$, hinged at $C$ and $D$. The columns are $5\,\mathrm{m}$ high and the crossbeam $BC$ is $5\,\mathrm{m}$ long. At joint $B$, the horizontal force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right; on the crossbeam, at $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $B$, the vertical downward force $P = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts. Calculate the support reactions and the axial force in the column $CD$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,5;C,5,5;D,5,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,cerniera" forze="B,1.4,0,F;2 5,0,-1.4,P" quote="A,B,5 m,0.8;B,2 5,2 m,-0.8;2 5,C,3 m,-0.8" titolo="Unsymmetrical frame with forces $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $P = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

:::soluzione
$H_A = -10\,\mathrm{kN}$ (to the left), $V_A = 2\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward; $H_D = 0$, $V_D = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. The column $CD$ is in compression: $N_{CD} = -18\,\mathrm{kN}$.
:::
