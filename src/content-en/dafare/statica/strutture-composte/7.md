The three-hinged arch shown is formed by the straight $AC$ and $CB$ members, with the springings at the same elevation, the span $10\,\mathrm{m}$ and the crown $C$ at midspan, $4\,\mathrm{m}$ above the springings. At the midpoint of the member $CB$, the vertical force $P = 32\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. Calculate support reactions and force in the hinge $C$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,5,4;B,10,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" forze="7.5 2,0,-1.6,P" quote="A,5 0,5 m,0.8;5 0,B,5 m,0.8;5 0,C,4 m,-0.6" titolo="Three-hinged arch with force $P = 32\,\mathrm{kN}$ at the midpoint of $CB$"}

:::soluzione
$V_A = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_B = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward; $H_A = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right, $H_B = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left. On the $CB$ half the $AC$ half exerts the $X_C = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$, $Y_C = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$, force directed along $AC$.
:::
