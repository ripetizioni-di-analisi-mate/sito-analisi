The Gerber beam shown has the segment $AC$ bound by a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, with the $BC$ overhang, and the segment $CD$ connected in $C$ by an internal hinge and resting on a roller support at $D$. The forces $P_1 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $P_2 = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$, vertical and directed downward, act at the points indicated. Calculate support reactions and force in the hinge $C$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;C,7,0;D,11,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" forze="2 0,0,-1.6,P_1;9 0,0,-1.6,P_2" quote="A,2 0,2 m,0.8;2 0,B,3 m,0.8;B,C,2 m,0.8;C,9 0,2 m,0.8;9 0,D,2 m,0.8" titolo="Gerber beam under forces $P_1 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $P_2 = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$"}

:::soluzione
$V_D = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$, $V_B = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$, $V_A = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$, all up; $H_A = 0$. The hinge transmits a vertical force of $8\,\mathrm{kN}$.
:::
