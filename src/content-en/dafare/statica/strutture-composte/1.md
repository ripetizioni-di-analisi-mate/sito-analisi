The Gerber beam shown is formed by the cantilever $AC$, fixed at $A$, and the segment $CB$, connected to the cantilever by the hinge $C$ and resting on a roller support at $B$. The whole beam carries the uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Calculate the support reactions and the force transmitted by the hinge $C$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,8,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,C,3 m,0.8;C,B,5 m,0.8" titolo="Gerber beam under uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

:::soluzione
$V_B = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward; $H_A = 0$, $V_A = 22\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, $M_A = 48\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise. The hinge transmits a vertical force of $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ (upward on the segment $CB$, downward on the cantilever).
:::
