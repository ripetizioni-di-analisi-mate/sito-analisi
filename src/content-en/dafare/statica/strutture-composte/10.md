The beam $AB$ shown, of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, is pinned to the wall at $A$ and is supported by the $ED$ strut, pinned to the wall at $E$, $3\,\mathrm{m}$ below $A$, and to the beam $D$, $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. The beam carries the uniform load $q = 5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Calculate the axial force in the strut and the reactions in $A$ and $E$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,3;D,4,3;B,6,3;E,0,0" aste="A-B;E-D" cerniere="D" vincoli="A,cerniera,180;E,cerniera,180" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,D,4 m,-0.8;D,B,2 m,-0.8;E,A,3 m,-0.8" titolo="Beam pinned to the wall and supported by strut $ED$, carrying load $q = 5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

:::soluzione
In compression point: $N_{ED} = -37.5\,\mathrm{kN}$. In $A$: $H_A = -30\,\mathrm{kN}$ (to the left), $V_A = 7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. In $E$: $H_E = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right, $V_E = 22.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward.
:::
