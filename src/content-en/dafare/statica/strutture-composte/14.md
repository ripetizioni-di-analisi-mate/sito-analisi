The frame shown has three bodies. Column $AB$ has height $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and is fixed to the ground at $A$. Beam $BC$ has length $6\,\mathrm{m}$ and is pinned at $B$ to column $AB$ and at $C$ to column $CD$. Column $CD$ has height $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and is pinned to the ground at $D$. Column $AB$ carries a uniform horizontal load $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ to the right and the beam carries a uniform load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Calculate the support reactions and the forces at hinges $B$ and $C$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,6,4;D,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" cerniere="B;C" vincoli="A,incastro;D,cerniera" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.6,2 kN/m,0;B,C,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,B,4 m,0.8;B,C,6 m,-1" titolo="Three-body frame with internal hinges $B$ and $C$, horizontal load $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on $AB$, and load $q = 6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on the crossbeam"}

:::soluzione
$H_D = 0$, $V_D = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward (the column $CD$ is a pin-ended in compression link); $H_A = -8\,\mathrm{kN}$ (to the left), $V_A = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, $M_A = 16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise. The $B$ and $C$ hinges only transmit vertical forces of $18\,\mathrm{kN}$.
:::
