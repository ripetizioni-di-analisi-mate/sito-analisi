The portal frame shown consists of bodies $1 = AB$, $2 = BC$, and $3 = CD$. Points $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (1, 4)$, $C = (5, 4)$, and $D = (6, 0)$ have coordinates in meters. Points $A$ and $D$ are pin supports, while $B$ and $C$ are internal hinges. Find $C_2$ and $C_{13}$, and express the rotations $\varphi_2$ and $\varphi_3$ in terms of $\varphi_1$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,1,4;C,5,4;D,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" cerniere="B;C" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,cerniera" quote="A,D,6 m,0.8;B,C,4 m,-0.7" titolo="Four-bar linkage with inclined side members"}

:::soluzione
$C_2 = (3, 12)\,\mathrm{m}$. Center $C_{13}$ lies at infinity in the horizontal direction. The rotations are $\varphi_2 = -\dfrac{1}{2}\varphi_1$ and $\varphi_3 = \varphi_1$.
:::
