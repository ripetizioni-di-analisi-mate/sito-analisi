The beam shown consists of bodies $1 = AB$, $2 = BC$, and $3 = CD$, joined by internal hinges $B$ and $C$. It has a pin support at $A$ and rollers on horizontal surfaces at $E$ and $D$. Express the rotations in terms of $\varphi_1$, locate center $C_{13}$, and find the vertical displacements of $B$ and $C$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;E,5,0;C,6,0;D,9,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" cerniere="B;C" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,carrello;D,carrello" quote="A,B,3 m;B,E,2 m;E,C,1 m;C,D,3 m" titolo="Three-body beam"}

:::soluzione
$\varphi_2 = -\dfrac{3}{2}\varphi_1$, $\varphi_3 = \dfrac{1}{2}\varphi_1$; $C_{13} = (-9, 0)\,\mathrm{m}$; $v_B = 3\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$, $v_C = -\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$.
:::
