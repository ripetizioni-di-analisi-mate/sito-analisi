---
titolo: "Finding a beam's rotation and center of rotation from two point displacements"
teoria: [spostamenti-infinitesimi, proiezioni-uguali, centro-di-rotazione]
parole: ["Vertical Shifts", "beam rotation", "center of rotation out of the body"]
---

A horizontal beam $AB$ has points $A = (0, 0)$ and $B = (6, 0)$, with coordinates in meters. It undergoes an infinitesimal rigid-body displacement with $u_A = 2\,\mathrm{mm}$, $v_A = 3\,\mathrm{mm}$, and $v_B = -9\,\mathrm{mm}$. Find the rotation of the beam, the horizontal displacement of $B$, and the center of rotation.

:::soluzione
$\varphi = -0.002\,\mathrm{rad}$ (clockwise) and $u_B = 2\,\mathrm{mm}$. The center is $C = (1.5;\ -1)\,\mathrm{m}$, a distance $1\,\mathrm{m}$ below the beam.
:::
