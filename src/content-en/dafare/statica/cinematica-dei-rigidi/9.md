The horizontal beam shown consists of body $1 = AB$, of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and pinned to the ground at $A$, and body $2 = BC$, of length $5\,\mathrm{m}$ and supported at $C$ by a roller on a horizontal surface. The bodies meet at $B$ through an internal parallel-link connection with horizontal links. Find the ratio of their rotations, the vertical displacements on both sides of $B$, and the displacement jump.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,8,0" aste="A-B;B-C" interni="B,doppio-pendolo,0" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" quote="A,B,3 m;B,C,5 m" titolo="Two-body beam joined by an internal parallel-link connection with horizontal links"}

:::soluzione
$\varphi_2 = \varphi_1$ and $C_2 = C$. The displacements are $v_B^{(1)} = 3\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$ and $v_B^{(2)} = -5\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$, with a jump of $v_B^{(2)} - v_B^{(1)} = -8\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$.
:::
