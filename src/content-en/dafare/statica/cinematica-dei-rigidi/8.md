The frame shown consists of column $AB$ (body $1$), pinned to the ground at $A$, and body $2 = BCD$, joined to the column at internal hinge $B$ and restrained at $D$ by a parallel-link support with vertical links. Find the centers of rotation and the displacement of body $2$ for $\varphi_1 = -0.003\,\mathrm{rad}$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,5,4;D,5,1" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,doppio-pendolo" quote="A,B,4 m,-0.8;B,C,5 m,-0.7;C,D,3 m,-0.8" titolo="Frame with a vertical parallel-link support at $D$"}

:::soluzione
$C_1 = A$ and $C_{12} = B$. Center $C_2$ lies at infinity in the vertical direction. Body $2$ translates horizontally by $u = -4\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1 = 12\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right, with $v = 0$.
:::
