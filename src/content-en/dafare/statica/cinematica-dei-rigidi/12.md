The beam shown consists of body $1 = DB$, pinned to the ground at $A$, and body $2 = BE$, joined to the first at internal hinge $B$ and supported at $C$ by a roller on a horizontal surface. Express $v(x)$ on each body and its values at $D$, $B$, and $E$ in terms of $\varphi_1$.

::struttura{nodi="D,0,0;A,2,0;B,5,0;C,8,0;E,10,0" aste="D-B;B-E" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" quote="D,A,2 m;A,B,3 m;B,C,3 m;C,E,2 m" titolo="Two-body beam with two overhangs"}

:::soluzione
$\varphi_2 = -\varphi_1$. The displacement is $v = \varphi_1\,(x - 2)$ on $DB$ and $v = -\varphi_1\,(x - 8)$ on $BE$, with $x$ measured in meters from $D$. The values are $v_D = -2\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$, $v_B = 3\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$, and $v_E = -2\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$.
:::
