Member $AB$ has length $5\,\mathrm{m}$. A roller at $A = (3, 0)$ moves along a horizontal floor, and another at $B = (0, 4)$ moves along a vertical wall. Point $B$ moves down by $6\,\mathrm{mm}$. Find the center of rotation, the rotation of the member, and the displacement of $A$.

::struttura{nodi="A,3,0;B,0,4" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello;B,carrello,180" quote="0 0,A,3 m,0.6;0 0,B,4 m,-0.6" titolo="Member with one roller on the floor and another against the wall"}

:::soluzione
$C = (3, 4)\,\mathrm{m}$. The rotation is $\varphi = 0.002\,\mathrm{rad}$ (counterclockwise), and the displacement is $u_A = 8\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right, with $v_A = 0$.
:::
