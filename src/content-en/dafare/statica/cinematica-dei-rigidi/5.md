The beam shown consists of body $1 = AB$, pinned to the ground at $A$, and body $2 = BD$, joined to the first at internal hinge $B$ and supported at $C$ by a roller on a horizontal surface. Find the centers of rotation, express $\varphi_2$ in terms of $\varphi_1$, and find the vertical displacements of $B$ and $D$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,9,0;D,11,0" aste="A-B;B-D" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" quote="A,B,6 m;B,C,3 m;C,D,2 m" titolo="Two-body beam with an overhang"}

:::soluzione
$C_1 = A$, $C_{12} = B$, $C_2 = C$; $\varphi_2 = -2\varphi_1$; $v_B = 6\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$, $v_D = -4\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$.
:::
