A planar rigid body undergoes an infinitesimal displacement. The displacement components of point $O = (0, 0)$ are $u_O = 4\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $v_O = -2\,\mathrm{mm}$, and the body rotates counterclockwise through $\varphi = 0.002\,\mathrm{rad}$. Find the displacement of $P = (3, 2)\,\mathrm{m}$ and locate the center of rotation.

:::soluzione
$u_P = 0$ and $v_P = 4\,\mathrm{mm}$ (upward). The center of rotation is $C = (1, 2)\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
