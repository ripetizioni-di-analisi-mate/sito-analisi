The beam shown consists of body $1 = AB$, pinned to the ground at $A$, and body $2 = BC$, joined to the first at internal hinge $B$ and supported at $C$ by a roller on a horizontal surface. An inclined force $F$ acts at point $D$, the midpoint of $BC$, in the direction of unit vector $\left( \dfrac{3}{5},\ -\dfrac{4}{5} \right)$. For $\varphi_1 = 0.005\,\mathrm{rad}$, find the displacement of $D$ and its component in the direction of $F$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;D,6,0;C,8,0" aste="A-B;B-C" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" forze="D,0.9,-1.2,F" quote="A,B,4 m;B,D,2 m;D,C,2 m" titolo="Two-body beam under an inclined force at $D$"}

:::soluzione
$\varphi_2 = -\varphi_1$ and $\vec{s}_D = (0;\ 10)\,\mathrm{mm}$, so $D$ rises by $10\,\mathrm{mm}$. The displacement component in the direction of $F$ is $-8\,\mathrm{mm}$, equivalent to $8\,\mathrm{mm}$ opposite the force.
:::
