Beam $AB$ has length $4\,\mathrm{m}$. A roller at $A$ moves along a horizontal surface, and a two-force link at $B$ slopes downward to the left at $45^\circ$. Find the center of rotation and the displacements of $A$ and $B$ for $\varphi = 0.001\,\mathrm{rad}$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello;B,pendolo,225" quote="A,B,4 m,-0.6" titolo="Beam with a roller at $A$ and a two-force link inclined at $45^\circ$ at point $B$"}

:::soluzione
The center is $C = (0, -4)\,\mathrm{m}$, a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ below $A$. The first displacement is $\vec{s}_A = (-4, 0)\,\mathrm{mm}$, or $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the left. The second is $\vec{s}_B = (-4, 4)\,\mathrm{mm}$, or $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the left and $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ upward.
:::
