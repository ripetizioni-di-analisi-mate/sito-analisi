The frame shown consists of body $1 = ABD$, pinned to the ground at $A = (0, 0)$, and body $2 = DEF$, joined to the first at internal hinge $D = (4, 4)$ and restrained at $F = (6, 2)$ by a roller on a vertical surface. Find $C_2$, express $\varphi_2$ in terms of $\varphi_1$, and find the horizontal displacement of the crossbeam and the displacement of $F$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;D,4,4;E,6,4;F,6,2" aste="A-B;B-D;D-E;E-F" cerniere="D" vincoli="A,cerniera;F,carrello,0" quote="A,B,4 m,-0.8;B,D,4 m,-0.7;D,E,2 m,-0.7;E,F,2 m,-0.8" titolo="Frame with a roller against a vertical surface at $F$"}

:::soluzione
$C_2 = (2, 2)\,\mathrm{m}$ and $\varphi_2 = 2\varphi_1$ (the rotations have the same direction). The crossbeam has $u = -4\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$. The remaining displacement components are $u_F = 0$ and $v_F = 8\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$.
:::
