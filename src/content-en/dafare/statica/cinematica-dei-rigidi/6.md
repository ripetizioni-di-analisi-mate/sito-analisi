The frame shown consists of body $1 = ABC$, pinned to the ground at $A$, and body $2 = CD$, joined to the first at internal hinge $C$. A two-force link at $D$ slopes downward to the right at $45^\circ$. Find center $C_2$, the ratio $\dfrac{\varphi_2}{\varphi_1}$, and the displacement of $D$ in terms of $\varphi_1$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,4,4;D,8,4" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,pendolo,315" quote="A,B,4 m,-0.8;B,C,4 m,-0.7;C,D,4 m,-0.7" titolo="Frame with an inclined link at $D$"}

:::soluzione
$C_2 = (6, 6)\,\mathrm{m}$ and $\dfrac{\varphi_2}{\varphi_1} = -2$. The displacement $\vec{s}_D = (-4, -4)\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \varphi_1$ is perpendicular to the link.
:::
