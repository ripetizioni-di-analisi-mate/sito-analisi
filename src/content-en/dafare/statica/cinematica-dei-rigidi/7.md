The arch shown has two members $AC$ and $CB$ joined by internal hinge $C = (4, 4)$. There is a pin support at $A = (0, 0)$ and a roller at $B = (10, 0)$ on a surface inclined at $45^\circ$, parallel to direction $(1, 1)$. Locate center $C_2$, find the ratio $\dfrac{\varphi_2}{\varphi_1}$, and determine the displacement of $B$ for $\varphi_1 = 0.001\,\mathrm{rad}$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,4;B,10,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,315" quote="A,4 0,4 m,0.8;4 0,B,6 m,0.8" titolo="Two-member arch with a roller on an inclined surface at $B$"}

:::soluzione
$C_2 = (5, 5)\,\mathrm{m}$ and $\dfrac{\varphi_2}{\varphi_1} = -4$. The displacement $\vec{s}_B = (-20, -20)\,\mathrm{mm}$ is directed down and to the left along the roller surface.
:::
