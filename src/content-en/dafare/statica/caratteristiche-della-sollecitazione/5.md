The beam $AD$ shown, of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$, $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. It brings the downward force $F = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ to $C$, $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and the uniform load $q = 3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on the $BD$ overhang. Plot the diagrams of $T$ and $M$ and find the zero moment section between $C$ and $B$.
