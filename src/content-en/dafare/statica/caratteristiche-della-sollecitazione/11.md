The portal frame shown has columns $AB$ and $DC$ of height $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and a crossbeam $BC$ of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$. A pin at $A$ and a roller at $D$ support the frame. Uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts on the crossbeam, and a horizontal force $P = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right at $B$. Using the inner fibers as reference fibers, draw the $N$, $T$, and $M$ diagrams.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;C,4,3;D,4,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,carrello" carichi="B,C,0.4,0.4,q" forze="B,1,0,P" tratteggi="0.12,0 0.12,2.88 3.88,2.88 3.88,0" quote="A,B,3 m,-0.8;B,C,4 m,-1.1" titolo="Portal frame under a uniform load and a horizontal force; dashed lines indicate the reference fibers"}

:::soluzione
The reactions are $H_A = -4\,\mathrm{kN}$, $V_A = 1\,\mathrm{kN}$, and $V_D = 7\,\mathrm{kN}$. In column $AB$, the internal resultants are $N = -1\,\mathrm{kN}$, $T = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$, and $M = 4s$, with $M_B = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ putting the inner fibers in tension. On the crossbeam, $N = 0$, $T(x) = 1 - 2x$, and $M(x) = 12 + x - x^2$. The moment reaches a maximum of $12.25\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at $x = 0.5\,\mathrm{m}$ and vanishes at $C$. In column $CD$, $N = -7\,\mathrm{kN}$, $T = 0$, and $M = 0$.
:::
