The Gerber beam shown consists of cantilever $AC$, fixed at $A$ and of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$, and beam $CD$, connected through internal hinge $C$, supported by roller $B$, and extending to overhang $BD$. The dimensions are $CB = 4\,\mathrm{m}$ and $BD = 2\,\mathrm{m}$. A uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts over the entire beam. Calculate the support reactions, draw the diagram for $M$, and determine the maximum positive bending moment.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,6,0;D,8,0" aste="A-D" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" cerniere="C" carichi="A,D,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,4 m;B,D,2 m" titolo="Gerber beam under a uniform load over its entire length"}

:::soluzione
$V_B = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$, $V_A = 7\,\mathrm{kN}$, $M_A = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise. $M(x) = -x^2 + 7x - 10$ for $0 < x < 6$ (zero in $C$), $M(x) = -(8 - x)^2$ on the overhang; $M(0) = -10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, $M_B = -4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, maximum positive $2.25\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in $x = 3.5\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
