The beam $AB$ shown, $8\,\mathrm{m}$ long and supported at both ends, carries the uniform $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ load on the section $AC$, with $AC = 4\,\mathrm{m}$, and the downward force $F = 4\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $D$, $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Plot diagrams of $T$ and $M$ and calculate the maximum moment.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,0;D,6,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,C,0.6,0.6,q" forze="D,0,-1.6,F" quote="A,C,4 m;C,D,2 m;D,B,2 m" titolo="Simply supported beam with a uniform load on $AC$ and force $F$ at $D$"}

:::soluzione
$V_A = 7\,\mathrm{kN}$, $V_B = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$. Shear: $T(x) = 7 - 2x$ on $AC$, $-1\,\mathrm{kN}$ on $CD$, $-5\,\mathrm{kN}$ on $DB$. Moment: $M(x) = 7x - x^2$ on $AC$, $M_C = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, $M_D = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$; maximum $M_{\max} = \dfrac{49}{4} = 12.25\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in $x = 3.5\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
