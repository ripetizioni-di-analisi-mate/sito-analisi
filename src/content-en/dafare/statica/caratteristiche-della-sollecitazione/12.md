The L-shaped cantilever shown consists of column $AB$ of height $4\,\mathrm{m}$ fixed at its base $A$ and beam $BC$ of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$, rigidly connected to the column. At the free end $C$ a horizontal force $P = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right and a vertical force $F = 2\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. Taking the fibers on the inside of the L as the reference fibers, calculate the support reactions and derive expressions for $N$, $T$, and $M$ in both members.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,2,4" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,incastro" forze="C,0,-0.8,F;2.8 4,0.8,0," etichette="2.7,4.3,P" tratteggi="0.1,0 0.1,3.9 2,3.9" quote="A,B,4 m,-0.7;B,C,2 m,0.6" titolo="L-shaped cantilever with forces $P$ and $F$ at its free end"}

:::soluzione
$H_A = -3\,\mathrm{kN}$, $V_A = 2\,\mathrm{kN}$, $M_A = 16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise. Column, with $s$ from $A$: $N = -2\,\mathrm{kN}$, $T = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$, $M(s) = -16 + 3s$ (outer fibers in tension, $M_B = -4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$). Crossbeam: $N = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ tension, $T = 2\,\mathrm{kN}$, $M(x) = -2\,(2 - x)$.
:::
