The beam $AB$ shown, of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$ and supported at both ends, carries the uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over all span and the clockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at the end $B$. Plot diagrams of $T$ and $M$ and calculate the maximum moment.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" coppie="B,-1,ℳ" quote="A,B,6 m" titolo="Simply supported beam with a uniform load and a clockwise couple at $B$"}

:::soluzione
$V_A = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$, $V_B = 7\,\mathrm{kN}$. $T(x) = 5 - 2x$, $M(x) = 5x - x^2$; maximum $M_{\max} = 6.25\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in $x = 2.5\,\mathrm{m}$; the moment vanishes in $x = 5\,\mathrm{m}$ and equals $-6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ immediately to the left of $B$.
:::
