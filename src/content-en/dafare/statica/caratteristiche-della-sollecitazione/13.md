The three-hinged frame shown is pinned at its bases $A$ and $B$. Columns $AD$ and $BE$ have height $4\,\mathrm{m}$, and beam $DE$ has length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and an internal hinge $C$ at midspan. At $D$ a horizontal force $P = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. Taking the inner fibers as the reference fibers, calculate the support reactions and draw the diagram for $M$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,0,4;C,2,4;E,4,4;B,4,0" aste="A-D;D-E;E-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" cerniere="C" forze="D,1.2,0,P" tratteggi="0.1,0 0.1,3.9 3.9,3.9 3.9,0" quote="A,D,4 m,-0.7;D,E,4 m,-0.7" titolo="Three-hinged portal frame with horizontal force $P$ at $D$"}

:::soluzione
$H_A = H_B = -3\,\mathrm{kN}$ (both left), $V_A = -6\,\mathrm{kN}$ (down), $V_B = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. Moment: $M = 3s$ in column $AD$, with $M_D = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ (inward fibers in tension); in crossbeam $M(x) = 12 - 6x$, null in $C$ and equal to $-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in $E$ (outer fibers in tension); in column $EB$ from $-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in $E$ to zero in $B$. Axial forces: $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $AD$, $-3\,\mathrm{kN}$ in crossbeam, $-6\,\mathrm{kN}$ in $EB$.
:::
