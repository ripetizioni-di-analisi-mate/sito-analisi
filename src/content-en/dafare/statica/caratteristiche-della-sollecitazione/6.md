The beam $CD$ shown, of length $8\,\mathrm{m}$, rests on a hinge $A$ and $B$ roller support, with $CA = BD = 2\,\mathrm{m}$. The $F = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ forces down act at the ends $C$ and $D$ and the uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts on the segment $AB$. Plot diagrams of $T$ and $M$.

::struttura{nodi="C,0,0;A,2,0;B,6,0;D,8,0" aste="C-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" forze="C,0,-1.5,F;D,0,-1.5,F" quote="C,A,2 m;A,B,4 m;B,D,2 m" titolo="Beam with two overhangs, forces at both ends, and a uniform load between the supports"}

:::soluzione
$V_A = V_B = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$. Shear: $-5\,\mathrm{kN}$ on $CA$, $T(x) = 8 - 2x$ on $AB$, $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ on $BD$. Moment: $M_A = M_B = -10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, $M(x) = -10 + 4\,(x - 2) - (x - 2)^2$ on $AB$, with the value $-6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at midspan; the moment is negative along the entire beam (in tension upper fibers).
:::
