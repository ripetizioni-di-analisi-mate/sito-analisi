The Gerber beam shown is formed by the beam $AC$, with a pin at $A$, a roller support at $B$ and the $BC$ overhang, and the beam $CD$, connected with the internal hinge $C$ and resting on the roller support at $D$. Distances are $AB = 4\,\mathrm{m}$, $BC = 2\,\mathrm{m}$, $CD = 4\,\mathrm{m}$; the downward force $F = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts in $E$, midway through $CD$. Calculate reactions and plot diagrams of $T$ and $M$.
