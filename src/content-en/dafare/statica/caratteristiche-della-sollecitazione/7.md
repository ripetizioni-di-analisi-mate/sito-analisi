The beam $AB$ shown, of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$ and carries at the end $A$ the $\mathcal{M} = 8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise couple. Calculate reactions and plot diagrams of $T$ and $M$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" coppie="A,1,ℳ" quote="A,B,4 m" titolo="Simply supported beam with couple $\mathcal{M}$ at $A$"}

:::soluzione
$V_A = 2\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, $V_B = -2\,\mathrm{kN}$, i.e. $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. $T = 2\,\mathrm{kN}$ constant; $M(x) = 2x - 8$, from $-8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ immediately to the right of $A$ to zero in $B$, with the upper fibers in tension.
:::
