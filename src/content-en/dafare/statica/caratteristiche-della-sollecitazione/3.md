The cantilever $AB$ shown, of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and fixed at $A$, carries the uniform load $q = 2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over the entire length and in the free end $B$ the force $F = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ of components $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. Calculate the reactions and expressions of $N$, $T$ and $M$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" carichi="A,B,0.4,0.4,q" forze="B,0.6,-0.8,F" quote="A,B,3 m" titolo="Cantilever with a uniform load and an inclined force at the tip"}

:::soluzione
$H_A = -3\,\mathrm{kN}$ (i.e. $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left), $V_A = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, $M_A = 21\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise. $N = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$ tension, $T(x) = 10 - 2x$, $M(x) = -(3 - x)^2 - 4\,(3 - x)$, with $M(0) = -21\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and the upper fibers in tension.
:::
