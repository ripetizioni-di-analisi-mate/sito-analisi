The cantilever $AB$ shown, of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$, is fixed at the right end $B$ and carries a triangular load that equals zero in the free end $A$ and $q_0 = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ in $B$. Calculate $T(x)$ and $M(x)$, with $x$ measured by $A$, and fixed support reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="B,incastro,0" carichi="A,B,0,0.6,q_0" quote="A,B,3 m" titolo="Cantilever fixed at $B$ under a triangular load"}

:::soluzione
$T(x) = -\dfrac{2x^2}{3}$ and $M(x) = -\dfrac{2x^3}{9}$, with $T(3) = -6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $M(3) = -6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ (upper fibers in tension). Reactions: $H_B = 0$, $V_B = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, $M_B = -6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, i.e. $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ clockwise.
:::
