The straight $AB$ member shown has a pin at $A$ and a roller support at $B$ on a vertical surface, which gives a horizontal reaction; $B$ is $3\,\mathrm{m}$ on the right and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ above $A$. At the midpoint $D$ of the member acts the vertical force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. Calculate the reactions and values of $N$, $T$ and $M$ in segments $AD$ and $DB$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,1.5,2;B,3,4" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,0" forze="D,0,-1.2,F" quote="A,3 0,3 m,0.7;3 0,B,4 m,0.9" titolo="Inclined member with force $F$ at its midpoint"}

:::soluzione
$V_A = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$, $H_A = 3.75\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right, $H_B = -3.75\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left. With $s$ along the member from $A$ ($\cos\alpha = 0.6$, $\sin\alpha = 0.8$): on $AD$ $N = -10.25\,\mathrm{kN}$, $T = 3\,\mathrm{kN}$, $M = 3s$; on $DB$ $N = -2.25\,\mathrm{kN}$, $T = -3\,\mathrm{kN}$, $M = 7.5 - 3\,(s - 2.5)$. Maximum $M_D = 7.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ moment.
:::
