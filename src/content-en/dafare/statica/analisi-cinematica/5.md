The Gerber beam shown is fixed at $A$, has internal hinges at $C$ and $D$, and rests on rollers on horizontal surfaces at $B$ and $E$. Determine whether it is statically determinate. In what order can you establish that its segments are immobile?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,5,0;D,7,0;E,10,0" aste="A-C;C-B;B-D;D-E" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello;E,carrello" cerniere="C;D" quote="A,C,3 m;C,B,2 m;B,D,2 m;D,E,3 m"}

:::soluzione
The beam is statically determinate, with $n = 3$, $s = 9$, and $l = i = 0$. The fixed support holds segment $AC$ stationary. Segment $CD$ cannot move because the candidate center positions $C$ and the vertical through $B$ are distinct. The same reasoning applies to segment $DE$ with candidates $D$ and the vertical through $E$.
:::
