The arch shown consists of members $AC$ and $CB$ joined at internal hinge $C$. It has a pin support at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. A tie connects the bases $A$ and $B$ of the two members. Determine whether the structure is statically determinate.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,5,4;B,10,0" aste="A-C;C-B" catene="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" cerniere="C" quote="A,B,10 m,0.9;B,10 4,4 m,0.6" tratteggi="5,4 10,4"}

:::soluzione
The structure is statically determinate, with $n = 2$ and $s = 2 + 2 + 1 + 1 = 6$. The relative center would have to coincide with $C$ and lie on the tie axis, line $AB$, which does not pass through $C$. The two members therefore have no relative displacement. They act as a single body with a properly arranged pin at $A$ and roller at $B$. $l = i = 0$.
:::
