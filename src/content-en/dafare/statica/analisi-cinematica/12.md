The closed frame shown is a $6\,\mathrm{m}$ by $4\,\mathrm{m}$ rectangle with rigid corners and an internal hinge $E$ at the midpoint of its upper crossbeam. It has a pin support at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. Find its degree of static indeterminacy.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,6,4;E,3,4;D,0,4" aste="A-B;B-C;C-E;E-D;D-A" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" cerniere="E" quote="A,B,6 m,0.9;B,C,4 m"}

:::soluzione
Cutting the loop at hinge $E$ leaves an open body with $s = 3$ properly arranged external restraints, so $l = 0$. The hinge that closes the loop imposes $2$ scalar constraints that prevent no additional motion. Hence $i = 2$.
:::
