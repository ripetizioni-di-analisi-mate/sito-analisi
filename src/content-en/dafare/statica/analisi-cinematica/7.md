The portal frame shown has rigid joints, a fixed support at base $A$, and a roller on a horizontal surface at base $B$. Find its degree of kinematic instability and its degree of static indeterminacy.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,0,4;E,5,4;B,5,0" aste="A-D;D-E;E-B" vincoli="A,incastro;B,carrello" quote="D,E,5 m,-0.7;B,E,4 m"}

:::soluzione
$n = 1$ and $s = 3 + 1 = 4$. The fixed support prevents all rigid-body motion, so $l = 0$ and $i = 1$. The frame is statically indeterminate to degree one.
:::
