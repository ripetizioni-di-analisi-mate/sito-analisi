The four-bar linkage shown consists of moving members $AC$, $CD$, and $DB$, pinned to the ground at $A = (0, 0)$ and $B = (6, 0)\,\mathrm{m}$ and joined by internal hinges $C = (0, 4)\,\mathrm{m}$ and $D = (3, 4)\,\mathrm{m}$. Find $l$ and $i$, locate center $C_2$ of member $CD$, and express rotations $\varphi_2$ and $\varphi_3$ in terms of $\varphi_1$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,0,4;D,3,4;B,6,0" aste="A-C;C-D;D-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" cerniere="C;D" quote="A,B,6 m,0.9;A,C,4 m,-0.7;C,D,3 m,-0.7"}

:::soluzione
$l = 1$ and $i = 0$. Center $C_2 = (0, 8)\,\mathrm{m}$ is the intersection of lines $AC$ and $BD$. The rotations are $\varphi_2 = -\varphi_1$ and $\varphi_3 = \varphi_1$.
:::
