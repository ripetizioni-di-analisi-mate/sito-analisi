The frame shown comprises body $ADC$ (the left column and crossbeam), body $CEB$ (the right crossbeam and column), and column $FC$. A single hinge connects all three at joint $C$. There are pin supports at $A$, $B$, and $F$. Find the degrees of kinematic instability and static indeterminacy.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,0,4;C,4,4;E,8,4;B,8,0;F,4,0" aste="A-D;D-C;C-E;E-B;F-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera;F,cerniera" cerniere="C" quote="A,F,4 m,0.9;F,B,4 m,0.9;B,E,4 m"}

:::soluzione
$n = 3$ and $s = 2 + 2 + 2 + 2\,(3 - 1) = 10$. The two outer bodies form a three-hinged arch with noncollinear hinges and therefore remain stationary. The column has two distinct candidate centers, $F$ and $C$, so it also remains stationary. Thus $l = 0$ and $i = 1$.
:::
