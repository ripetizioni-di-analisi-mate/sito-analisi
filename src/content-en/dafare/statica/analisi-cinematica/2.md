Beam $AB$ has length $6\,\mathrm{m}$ and a pin support at $A$. Its roller at $B$ moves along a surface inclined at $30^\circ$ to the horizontal. Determine whether the beam is statically determinate.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,240" quote="A,B,6 m,0.9"}

:::soluzione
The beam is statically determinate, with $l = i = 0$. The roller reaction makes an angle $30^\circ$ with the vertical, and its line of action does not pass through $A$. Taking $A$ as the reference point gives $6\cos 30^\circ = 3\sqrt{3} \neq 0$ for the determinant of the kinematic matrix.
:::
