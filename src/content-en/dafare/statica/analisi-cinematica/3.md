The frame shown consists of a crossbeam $AB$ of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and a column $BC$ of height $3\,\mathrm{m}$, joined rigidly at $B$. It has a pin support at $A$ and a two-force link at $C$ directed along the line $CA$. Classify the frame.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,4,3" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,pendolo,216.87" tratteggi="0,0 4,3" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9;B,C,3 m"}

:::soluzione
The frame is unstable and statically indeterminate, with $l = i = 1$. The link axis passes through pin $A$, so the frame can rotate about $A$, with point $C$ moving perpendicular to the link axis.
:::
