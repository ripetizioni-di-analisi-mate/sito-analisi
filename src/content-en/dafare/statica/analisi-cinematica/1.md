Beam $AB$ has length $5\,\mathrm{m}$, a fixed support at $A$, and a pin support at $B$. Find its degree of kinematic instability and its degree of static indeterminacy.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,cerniera" quote="A,B,5 m"}

:::soluzione
$n = 1$, $s = 3 + 2 = 5$, and $3n - s = -2$. The fixed support alone prevents all rigid-body motion. Thus $l = 0$, and the beam is statically indeterminate with $i = 2$.
:::
