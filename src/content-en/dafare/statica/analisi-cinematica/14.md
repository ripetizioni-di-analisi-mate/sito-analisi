The beam shown consists of segments $AC$ and $CB$, each of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, joined at internal hinge $C$. It has a parallel-link support with horizontal links at $A$ and a pin support at $B$. Classify the beam and describe its possible motions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,0;B,8,0" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,doppio-pendolo,180;B,cerniera" cerniere="C" quote="A,C,4 m;C,B,4 m"}

:::soluzione
The beam is unstable and statically indeterminate, with $l = i = 1$. The centers $C_1 = X_\infty$ (at infinity in the horizontal direction), $C_{12} = C$, and $C_2 = B$ are collinear on the beam axis. Segment $AC$ translates vertically by $v$, and segment $CB$ rotates about $B$ through $\varphi_2 = -\dfrac{v}{4\,\mathrm{m}}$.
:::
