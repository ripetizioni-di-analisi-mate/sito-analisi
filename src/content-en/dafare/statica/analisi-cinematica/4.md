Beam $AB$ of length $5\,\mathrm{m}$ has a parallel-link support with vertical links at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. Classify the beam and describe its possible motions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,doppio-pendolo;B,carrello" quote="A,B,5 m"}

:::soluzione
The beam is unstable and statically indeterminate, with $l = i = 1$. The roller reaction is parallel to the links, so the beam can translate horizontally, with its center at infinity in the vertical direction.
:::
