The portal frame shown has pin supports at $A = (0, 0)$ and $B = (6, 1)\,\mathrm{m}$ and an internal hinge $C$ on its crossbeam at $(2, 4)\,\mathrm{m}$. Determine whether the frame is statically determinate.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,0,4;C,2,4;E,6,4;B,6,1" aste="A-D;D-C;C-E;E-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" cerniere="C" quote="D,C,2 m,-0.7;C,E,4 m,-0.7;A,D,4 m,-0.7;B,E,3 m"}

:::soluzione
The frame is statically determinate, with $l = i = 0$. The three hinges are not collinear because $(x_C - x_A)(y_B - y_A) - (y_C - y_A)(x_B - x_A) = 2 \cdot 1 - 4 \cdot 6 = -22\,\mathrm{m^2} \neq 0$.
:::
