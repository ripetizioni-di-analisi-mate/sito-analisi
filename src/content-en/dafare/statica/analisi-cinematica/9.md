Inclined beam $AB$ has a pin support at $A = (0, 0)$ and a roller at $B = (4, 3)\,\mathrm{m}$. The roller can run on a surface of any inclination, shown horizontal in the figure. Find the inclination for which the beam is unstable.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,3" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,4 0,4 m,0.9;4 0,B,3 m,0.6"}

:::soluzione
The beam is unstable, with $l = i = 1$, when the normal to the roller surface passes through $A$. The surface must therefore be perpendicular to $AB$ and slope downward to the right at an angle $\alpha$ to the horizontal, with $\tan\alpha = \dfrac{4}{3}$ and $\alpha \approx 53.13^\circ$. At every other inclination the beam is statically determinate.
:::
