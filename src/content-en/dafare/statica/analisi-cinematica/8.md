The arch shown consists of straight members $AC$ and $CB$ with pin supports at $A = (0, 0)$ and $B = (10, 2)\,\mathrm{m}$ and an internal hinge at $C = (5\,\mathrm{m},\ h)$. Find the value of $h$ for which the arch is unstable.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,5,3.5;B,10,2" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" cerniere="C" tratteggi="5,0 5,3.5;0,0 10,0" quote="A,5 0,5 m,0.9;5 0,10 0,5 m,0.9;5 0,C,h,0.5;10 0,B,2 m,0.6"}

:::soluzione
The arch is unstable for $h = 1\,\mathrm{m}$, with $l = i = 1$, when $C$ lies on segment $AB$. The determinant of the system for the rotations is $10 - 10h$, with $h$ in meters. The arch is statically determinate for every other value of $h$.
:::
