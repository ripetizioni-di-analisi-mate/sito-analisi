Beam $AB$ has length $6\,\mathrm{m}$ and carries a trapezoidal load of intensity $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $A$ and $10\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $B$. Find the resultant and the distance from $A$ to its line of action.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" carichi="A,B,0.4,1,q" quote="A,B,6 m,0.8" titolo="Trapezoidal load varying from $4$ to $10\,\mathrm{kN/m}$"}

:::soluzione
$Q = 42\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward, a distance $\dfrac{24}{7}\,\mathrm{m} = 3.43\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$.
:::
