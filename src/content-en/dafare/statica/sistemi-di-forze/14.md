The plate shown has points $A = (0, 0)$, $B = (4, 0)$, and $C = (0, 3)$, with coordinates in meters. A force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right at $C$. Find the vertical force at $B$ and the force of unknown direction at $A$ that, together with $F$, maintain equilibrium.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 4,0 4,3 0,3" nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,0,3" forze="C,1.3,0,F" quote="A,B,4 m,0.8;A,C,3 m,-0.8" titolo="Plate under force $F$ at $C$"}

:::soluzione
At $B$, apply an upward force of $7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$. At $A$, apply a force of magnitude $12.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ with components $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left and $7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward, along the line joining $A$ and $(4, 3)$.
:::
