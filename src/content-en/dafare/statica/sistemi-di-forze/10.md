---
titolo: "Moving a horizontal force to the base of a column"
teoria: [scorrimento-e-trasporto, momento-di-una-forza]

---

A horizontal force $H = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right at the top of the vertical member $AB$, whose height is $4\,\mathrm{m}$. Move the force to the base $A$ and determine the couple needed for equivalence.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4" aste="A-B" forze="B,1.3,0,H" quote="A,B,4 m,-0.8" titolo="Column with a horizontal force $H$ at its top"}

:::soluzione
A rightward force of $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $A$ and a clockwise couple $\mathcal{M}_t = -32\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
:::
