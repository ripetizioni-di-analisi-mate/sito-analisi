Beam $AB$ has length $5\,\mathrm{m}$ and carries a uniform load $q = 4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over its entire length and a downward force $F = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $B$. Find the resultant and the distance from $A$ to its line of action.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" forze="B,0,-1.3,F" quote="A,B,5 m,0.8" titolo="Uniform load $q$ and point force $F$ at end $B$"}

:::soluzione
$Q = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward, a distance $\dfrac{10}{3}\,\mathrm{m} = 3.33\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$.
:::
