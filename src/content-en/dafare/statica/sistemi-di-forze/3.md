An upward force of $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $A = (0, 0)$, and a downward force of $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $B = (3, 0)$. Coordinates are in meters. Find the combined moment of these forces about the origin and about $P = (5, 2)$.

:::soluzione
The moment is $-24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in both cases. The two forces form a clockwise couple with moment arm $3\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
