A beam of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$ carries a triangular load that decreases linearly from $12\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at its left end $A$ to zero at its right end $B$. Find the resultant, the distance from $A$ to its line of action, and the moment of the load about $B$.

:::soluzione
$Q = 36\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward, a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. The moment is $M_B = 144\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise.
:::
