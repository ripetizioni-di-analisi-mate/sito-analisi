A downward load of $30\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts on beam $AB$ of length $5\,\mathrm{m}$ at a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Find two vertical forces at $A$ and $B$ that together are equivalent to the given load.

:::soluzione
$18\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward at $A$ and $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward at $B$.
:::
