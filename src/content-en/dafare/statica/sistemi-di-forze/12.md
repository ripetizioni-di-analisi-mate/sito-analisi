The plate shown carries a downward force $F_1 = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $(1, 0)$, a rightward force $F_2 = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $(0, 2)$, and a clockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Coordinates are in meters. Replace this system with a single force by finding the resultant and the equation of its line of action.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 3,0 3,2 0,2" nodi="O,0,0;P,1,0,-;Q,0,2,-" forze="1 -1.2,0,-1.2,F_1;Q,1,0,F_2" coppie="2 1,-1,ℳ" quote="O,1 0,1 m,1.5;O,Q,2 m,-0.8" titolo="Plate under forces $F_1$ and $F_2$ and clockwise couple $\mathcal{M}$"}

:::soluzione
The resultant is $\vec{R} = (5, -10)\,\mathrm{kN}$, with magnitude $11.18\,\mathrm{kN}$. Its line of action is $2x + y = 5.2$, with $x$ and $y$ in meters.
:::
