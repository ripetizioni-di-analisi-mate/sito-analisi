Beam $AB$ has length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and carries a downward force $P_1 = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $A$, an upward force $P_2 = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$ at a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and a downward force $P_3 = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $B$. Find the resultant and the center of the parallel forces.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" forze="A,0,-1.4,P_1;2 0,0,0.6,P_2;B,0,-1.1,P_3" quote="A,2 0,2 m,1;2 0,B,2 m,1" titolo="Three vertical forces, one acting upward"}

:::soluzione
$R = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward, with the center a distance $\dfrac{5}{3}\,\mathrm{m} = 1.67\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$.
:::
