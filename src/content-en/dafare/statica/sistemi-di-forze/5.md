Beam $AB$ has length $5\,\mathrm{m}$. It carries a downward force $F_1 = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ at a distance $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, a downward force $F_2 = 9\,\mathrm{kN}$ at a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and a counterclockwise couple $\mathcal{M} = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Replace this system with a single equivalent force.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" forze="1 0,0,-1,F_1;4 0,0,-1.4,F_2" coppie="2.5 0,1,ℳ" quote="A,1 0,1 m,0.8;1 0,4 0,3 m,0.8;4 0,B,1 m,0.8" titolo="Beam under two vertical loads and a couple"}

:::soluzione
A downward force of $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ whose line of action lies a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$.
:::
