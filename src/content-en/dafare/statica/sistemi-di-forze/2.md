A force with components $F_x = -8\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $F_y = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $P = (4, 1)$, with coordinates in meters. Find its moment about the origin $O$ and the perpendicular distance from $O$ to its line of action.

:::soluzione
$M_O = 32\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise, with moment arm $b = 3.2\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
