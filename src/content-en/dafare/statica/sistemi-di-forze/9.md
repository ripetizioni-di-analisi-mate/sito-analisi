A beam of length $L = 6\,\mathrm{m}$ carries a load $q(x) = q_0\Big( 1 - \dfrac{x}{L} \Big)^2$, where $q_0 = 12\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and $x$ is measured from the left end $A$. Find the resultant and the distance from $A$ to its line of action.

:::soluzione
$Q = \dfrac{q_0 L}{3} = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward, a distance $\dfrac{L}{4} = 1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$.
:::
