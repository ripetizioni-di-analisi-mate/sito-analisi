A horizontal beam lies along the $x$ axis, with the origin $O$ at its left end. A downward load of $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $P = (2, 0)$, with coordinates in meters. Calculate its moment about $O$ and about $Q = (5, 0)$, and indicate the direction of rotation in each case.

:::soluzione
$M_O = -30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ clockwise and $M_Q = 45\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise.
:::
