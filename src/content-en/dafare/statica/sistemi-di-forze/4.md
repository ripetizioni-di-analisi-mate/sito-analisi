A force system has resultant $\vec{R} = (3, -4)\,\mathrm{kN}$ and resultant moment $M_O = 10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ about the origin. Find its resultant moment about $P = (2, 1)$, whose coordinates are in meters.

:::soluzione
$M_P = 21\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise.
:::
