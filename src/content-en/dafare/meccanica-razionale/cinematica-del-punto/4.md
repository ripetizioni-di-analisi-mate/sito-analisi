---
titolo: Radius of curvature of an ellipse at the vertices
teoria: [terna-intrinseca, formula-raggio-curvatura, componenti-intrinseche]
parole: [ellipse, radius of curvature, vertices, osculating circle]
---

A material point $P$ travels along the ellipse with semiaxes $a > b > 0$ with the equations
$$
x(t) = a\cos(\omega t) ,
\qquad
y(t) = b\sin(\omega t) ,
$$
with $\omega > 0$ constant. Compute the radius of curvature at the vertex $A = O + a\,\hat{\imath}$ and at the vertex $B = O + b\,\hat{\jmath}$, and verify that the result does not depend on $\omega$.

:::soluzione
$\rho_A = \dfrac{b^2}{a}$ and $\rho_B = \dfrac{a^2}{b}$; neither value contains $\omega$, because the radius of curvature depends only on the trajectory and not on the time law with which it is traveled. The trajectory is more curved at $A$, since $\rho_A < \rho_B$.
:::
