A platform rotates in the plane $Oxy$ about the fixed center $O$ with constant angular velocity $\vec{\omega} = \omega\,\hat{k}$, with $\omega > 0$. A slider $P$ runs on a radial guide attached to the platform, as in the figure, starting from $O$ with zero relative velocity and constant relative acceleration of magnitude $a_0$, so that $|P - O| = \dfrac{1}{2}\,a_0 t^2$. Denoting by $\hat{e}_1$ the unit vector of the guide and by $\hat{e}_2$ the orthogonal unit vector rotated by $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ counterclockwise, compute the absolute velocity, the three accelerations of the composition theorem, the absolute acceleration and the instant at which the component of the absolute acceleration along $\hat{e}_1$ vanishes.

::struttura{cerchi="0,0,2" nodi="O,0,0;P,1.22,0.44" nomi=0 punti="0,0,O,centro;1.22,0.44,P" linee="0,0 1.88,0.68" frecce="1.22,0.44,1.78,0.64,v_r;1.22,0.44,1.0,1.05,a_c" coppie="-1.0 -0.9,1,ω" titolo="Slider running with constant relative acceleration on a radial guide of a rotating platform"}

:::soluzione
$\vec{v}_a = a_0 t\,\hat{e}_1 + \dfrac{1}{2}\,\omega\,a_0 t^2\,\hat{e}_2$. Accelerations: $\vec{a}_r = a_0\,\hat{e}_1$, $\vec{a}_\tau = -\dfrac{1}{2}\,\omega^2 a_0 t^2\,\hat{e}_1$, $\vec{a}_c = 2\,\omega\,a_0 t\,\hat{e}_2$, hence
$$
\vec{a}_a = a_0\left( 1 - \frac{\omega^2t^2}{2} \right)\hat{e}_1 + 2\,\omega\,a_0 t\,\hat{e}_2 .
$$
The component along $\hat{e}_1$ vanishes at the instant $t^* = \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{\omega}$, when the absolute acceleration is entirely transverse and equals $2\sqrt{2}\,a_0\,\hat{e}_2$.
:::
