---
titolo: Motion with constant areal velocity and exponential recession
teoria: [velocita-areolare, accelerazione-trasversa, velocita-accelerazione-polari]
parole: [constant areal velocity, central acceleration, polar coordinates, plane motion]
---

A material point $P$ moves in the plane $Oxy$ moving away from the pole with the law
$$
r(t) = r_0\,e^{\omega t} ,
$$
with $r_0 > 0$ and $\omega > 0$ constants, and its areal velocity with respect to $O$ is constant and equal to $c > 0$; at the initial instant $\vartheta = 0$. Determine $\dot{\vartheta}(t)$, the function $\vartheta(t)$, the components of the acceleration and the limit of $\vartheta$ as $t \to +\infty$.

:::soluzione
$\dot{\vartheta} = \dfrac{2c}{r^2} = \dfrac{2c}{r_0^2}\,e^{-2\omega t}$ and $\vartheta(t) = \dfrac{c}{\omega\,r_0^2}\left( 1 - e^{-2\omega t} \right)$. Acceleration: transverse component zero, radial component $\omega^2 r - \dfrac{4c^2}{r^3}$, with $r = r_0e^{\omega t}$. Limit: $\vartheta \to \dfrac{c}{\omega\,r_0^2}$, hence the angle described stays bounded.
:::
