titolo: Uniformly accelerated circular motion and direction of the acceleration

A material point $P$ travels along the circle with center $O$ and radius $R$ starting from rest, with the central angle given by
$$
\vartheta(t) = \frac{1}{2}\,\alpha t^2 ,
\qquad \alpha > 0 .
$$
Compute the intrinsic components of the acceleration, the instant at which the acceleration forms an angle of $30^\circ$ with the radius and the angle described up to that instant.

:::soluzione
$a_t = R\,\alpha$ and $a_n = R\,\alpha^2 t^2$. The angle $\beta$ between the acceleration and the radius satisfies $\tan\beta = \dfrac{1}{\alpha t^2}$, hence $\beta = 30^\circ$ at the instant $t^* = \dfrac{\sqrt[4]{3}}{\sqrt{\alpha}}$, at which the point has described the angle $\vartheta(t^*) = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\ \mathrm{rad}$.
:::
