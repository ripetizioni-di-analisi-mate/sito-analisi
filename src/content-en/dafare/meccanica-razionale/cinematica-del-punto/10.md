A material point $P$ describes the arch of a cycloid in the figure with
$$
x(t) = R\,(\omega t - \sin\omega t) ,
\qquad
y(t) = R\,(1 - \cos\omega t) ,
\qquad 0 \le \omega t \le 2\pi ,
$$
with $R > 0$ and $\omega > 0$ constants. Compute the magnitude of the velocity, the arc traveled, the intrinsic components of the acceleration and the radius of curvature at the instant $\omega t = \pi$, and the length of the whole arch.

::grafico{curve="t-sin(t),1-cos(t),0,6.29" linee="-0.3,0 6.6,0" punti="0,0,pieno;3.14,2,pieno,P" uguale=1 xmin=-0.6 xmax=7.0 ymin=-0.5 ymax=2.6 legenda=0 nomi="x,y" titolo="Arch of a cycloid generated by a circle of radius $R$ that rolls without slipping on the $x$ axis"}

:::soluzione
At the instant $\omega t = \pi$, that is at the highest point: $|\vec{v}_P| = 2R\omega$, $s = 4R$, $a_t = 0$, $a_n = R\omega^2$ and $\rho = 4R$. The length of the whole arch equals $8R$.
:::
