---
titolo: Composition of two harmonic motions in phase
teoria: [moto-armonico, moto-di-un-punto, velocita-e-accelerazione]
parole: [composition of harmonic motions, rectilinear trajectory, amplitude, central acceleration]
---

A material point $P$ moves in the plane $Oxy$ combining two orthogonal harmonic motions of equal angular frequency and in phase with each other:
$$
x(t) = A\cos(\omega t) ,
\qquad
y(t) = B\cos(\omega t) ,
$$
with $A > 0$ and $B > 0$. Determine the trajectory, the magnitude of the velocity as a function of time and its maximum value, and recognize what kind of motion it is.

:::soluzione
Trajectory: the segment of the line $y = \dfrac{B}{A}\,x$ between the points $(-A,\,-B)$ and $(A,\,B)$. The motion is harmonic along that line, with amplitude $\sqrt{A^2 + B^2}$ and angular frequency $\omega$. Magnitude of the velocity: $|\vec{v}_P| = \omega\sqrt{A^2 + B^2}\,|\sin(\omega t)|$, with maximum value $\omega\sqrt{A^2 + B^2}$ at the passages through the origin.
:::
