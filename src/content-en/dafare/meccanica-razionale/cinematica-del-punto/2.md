A material point $P$ travels along the circular helix in the figure with
$$
P - O = R\cos(\omega t)\,\hat{\imath} + R\sin(\omega t)\,\hat{\jmath} + h\,\omega t\,\hat{k} ,
$$
with $R > 0$, $h > 0$ and $\omega > 0$ constants. Compute the magnitude of the velocity, the time law $s(t)$ with the origin of the arcs at the initial position, the radius of curvature and the angle $\gamma$ that the velocity forms with the $z$ axis.

::grafico{curve="cos(t),0.25*t+0.45*sin(t),0,12.57;cos(t),0.45*sin(t),0,6.29" linee="-1,0 -1,3.3;1,0 1,3.3" segmenti="0,0,0,3.6" riferimento="-2.1,0.2,0,0.8,x,z" assi=0 uguale=1 xmin=-2.4 xmax=1.8 ymin=-0.7 ymax=3.8 legenda=0 titolo="Circular helix of radius $R$ and pitch $2\pi h$, drawn in projection"}

:::soluzione
$|\vec{v}_P| = \omega\sqrt{R^2 + h^2}$; $s(t) = \omega t\sqrt{R^2 + h^2}$; $\rho = \dfrac{R^2 + h^2}{R}$; $\cos\gamma = \dfrac{h}{\sqrt{R^2 + h^2}}$, constant throughout the motion.
:::
