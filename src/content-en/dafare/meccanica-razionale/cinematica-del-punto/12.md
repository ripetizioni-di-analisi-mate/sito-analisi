A cart translates along the $x$ axis with constant velocity $V > 0$. At the point $\Omega$ of the cart a rod of length $\ell$ is hinged, which rotates with respect to the cart with constant angular velocity $\omega > 0$, so that the angle it forms with the $x$ axis equals $\vartheta = \omega t$; the system is in the figure. Compute the absolute velocity of the free end $A$ of the rod and determine for which value of $V$ there is an instant at which that velocity is zero, specifying in which configuration this happens.

::struttura{nodi="Ω,1.6,0;A,2.63,1.23" aste="Ω-A" vincoli="Ω,carrello,270" linee="-0.3,0 4.6,0;2.15,0.0 2.15,0.06 2.14,0.12 2.12,0.18 2.1,0.23 2.07,0.29 2.04,0.33 2.0,0.38 1.95,0.42" punti="2.63,1.23" frecce="1.6,-0.55,2.6,-0.55,V;0,0,0,1.5,y" coppie="0.9 1.0,1,ω" etichette="2.3,0.12,ϑ;2.0,0.75,ℓ" titolo="Cart in uniform translation with a hinged rod that rotates with respect to the cart"}

:::soluzione
$\vec{v}_A = \big( V - \ell\omega\sin(\omega t) \big)\,\hat{\imath} + \ell\omega\cos(\omega t)\,\hat{\jmath}$. The absolute velocity vanishes only if $V = \ell\,\omega$, and in that case at the instants with $\omega t = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2k\pi$, that is when the rod is orthogonal to the direction of the motion of the cart and the end $A$ is at the highest position.
:::
