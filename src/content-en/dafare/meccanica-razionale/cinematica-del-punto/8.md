---
titolo: Simple harmonic motion with given initial data
teoria: [moto-armonico, velocita-e-accelerazione]
parole: [simple harmonic motion, amplitude, initial phase, period, passage through the origin]
---

A material point $P$ moves on the $x$ axis with the equation $\ddot{x} = -\omega^2 x$ and at the initial instant it is at $x(0) = x_0 > 0$ with velocity $\dot{x}(0) = -v_0$, where $v_0 > 0$. Determine the law of motion, the amplitude, the period and the first instant at which the point passes through the origin.

:::soluzione
$x(t) = x_0\cos(\omega t) - \dfrac{v_0}{\omega}\sin(\omega t) = A\cos(\omega t + \varphi)$, with $A = \sqrt{x_0^2 + \dfrac{v_0^2}{\omega^2}}$ and $\varphi = \arctan\dfrac{v_0}{\omega\,x_0} \in \left( 0, \dfrac{\pi}{2} \right)$. Period $T = \dfrac{2\pi}{\omega}$. First passage through the origin at the instant $t_1 = \dfrac{1}{\omega}\left( \dfrac{\pi}{2} - \varphi \right)$.
:::
