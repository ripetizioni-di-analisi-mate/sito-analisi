A moving frame has origin $\Omega$ coinciding with the fixed origin $O$ and rotates with constant angular velocity $\vec{\omega} = \omega\,\hat{e}_1$, with $\omega > 0$, where $\hat{e}_1$, $\hat{e}_2$, $\hat{e}_3$ are the moving unit vectors. A point $P$ moves along the $\eta$ axis with $P - \Omega = u t\,\hat{e}_2$ and $u > 0$; the diagram is in the figure. Compute the relative velocity, the transport velocity, the absolute velocity, the three accelerations of the composition theorem and the absolute acceleration, expressed in the moving basis.

::struttura{nodi="Ω,0,0;P,1.3,0" punti="0,0;1.3,0" frecce="0,0,-1.5,-0.7,ξ;0,0,2.5,0,η;0,0,0,1.7,ζ;0,0,-0.64,-0.3,ω;1.3,0,1.8,0,v_r;1.3,0,1.3,0.8,v_τ" riferimento=0 titolo="Moving frame rotating about the $\xi$ axis, drawn in projection, with the point $P$ running along the $\eta$ axis"}

:::soluzione
$\vec{v}_r = u\,\hat{e}_2$; $\vec{v}_\tau = \omega\,u t\,\hat{e}_3$; $\vec{v}_a = u\,\hat{e}_2 + \omega\,u t\,\hat{e}_3$. Accelerations: $\vec{a}_r = \vec{0}$, $\vec{a}_\tau = -\,\omega^2 u t\,\hat{e}_2$, $\vec{a}_c = 2\,\omega\,u\,\hat{e}_3$, hence $\vec{a}_a = -\,\omega^2 u t\,\hat{e}_2 + 2\,\omega\,u\,\hat{e}_3$.
:::
