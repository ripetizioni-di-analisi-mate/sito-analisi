A material point $P$ starts from the origin with velocity of magnitude $v_0$ inclined at the angle $\alpha \in \left( 0, \dfrac{\pi}{2} \right)$ to the $x$ axis and moves in the vertical plane $Oxy$ with constant acceleration $-g\,\hat{\jmath}$, so that
$$
x(t) = v_0\cos\alpha\;t ,
\qquad
y(t) = v_0\sin\alpha\;t - \frac{1}{2}\,g t^2 .
$$
Compute the tangential acceleration at the initial instant, the instant at which the point reaches the maximum height and the radius of curvature of the trajectory at that point.

:::soluzione
$a_t(0) = -g\sin\alpha$, hence the point slows down during the rising phase. Maximum height at the instant $t^* = \dfrac{v_0\sin\alpha}{g}$, where $|\vec{v}_P| = v_0\cos\alpha$, the acceleration is entirely normal and $\rho = \dfrac{v_0^2\cos^2\alpha}{g}$.
:::
