A material point $P$ moves in the plane $Oxy$ with the polar coordinates
$$
r(t) = c\,t ,
\qquad
\vartheta(t) = \frac{1}{2}\,\beta t^2 ,
$$
with $c > 0$ and $\beta > 0$ constants; the trajectory is the spiral in the figure. Compute the radial and transverse components of the velocity and of the acceleration and the areal velocity with respect to $O$.

::grafico{curve="0.25*sqrt(2*t)*cos(t),0.25*sqrt(2*t)*sin(t),0.01,20" punti="0,0,pieno" uguale=1 xmin=-2.0 xmax=2.1 ymin=-1.9 ymax=1.9 legenda=0 nomi="x,y" titolo="Trajectory of the point: the distance from the pole grows while the angular velocity increases in time"}

:::soluzione
Velocity: $\dot{r} = c$ and $r\dot{\vartheta} = c\,\beta t^2$. Acceleration: radial component $-c\,\beta^2 t^3$, transverse component $3\,c\,\beta t$. Areal velocity: $\dfrac{dA}{dt} = \dfrac{1}{2}\,c^2\beta\,t^3$, increasing in time.
:::
