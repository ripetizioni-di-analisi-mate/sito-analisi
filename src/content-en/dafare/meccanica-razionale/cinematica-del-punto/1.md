---
titolo: Plane motion on an ellipse
teoria: [moto-di-un-punto, velocita-e-accelerazione, moto-armonico]
parole: [ellipse, trajectory, velocity, central acceleration, magnitude of the velocity]
---

A material point $P$ moves in the plane $Oxy$ with the equations
$$
x(t) = \ell\cos(\omega t) ,
\qquad
y(t) = 2\,\ell\sin(\omega t) ,
$$
with $\ell > 0$ and $\omega > 0$ constants. Determine the trajectory, prove that the acceleration is directed toward $O$ and compute the maximum and minimum values of the magnitude of the velocity, indicating at which points of the trajectory they are reached.

:::soluzione
Trajectory: the ellipse of equation $\dfrac{x^2}{\ell^2} + \dfrac{y^2}{4\ell^2} = 1$, with semiaxes $\ell$ and $2\ell$. Acceleration: $\vec{a}_P = -\omega^2\,(P - O)$, hence directed toward $O$. Magnitude of the velocity: maximum $2\ell\omega$ at the points $(\pm\ell,\,0)$, minimum $\ell\omega$ at the points $(0,\,\pm 2\ell)$.
:::
