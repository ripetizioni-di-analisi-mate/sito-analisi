---
titolo: Helical guide traveled with increasing angular velocity
teoria: [moto-elicoidale, componenti-intrinseche, formula-raggio-curvatura, ascissa-curvilinea]
parole: [circular helix, accelerated time law, tangential acceleration, normal acceleration, radius of curvature]
---

A material point $P$ is constrained to the helical guide of radius $R$ and pitch $2\pi h$, of equations
$$
P - O = R\cos\varphi\,\hat{\imath} + R\sin\varphi\,\hat{\jmath} + h\,\varphi\,\hat{k} ,
$$
and travels along it starting from rest with $\varphi(t) = \dfrac{1}{2}\,\alpha t^2$, where $\alpha > 0$ is constant. Compute the magnitude of the velocity, the time law $s(t)$, the intrinsic components of the acceleration and the radius of curvature.

:::soluzione
$|\vec{v}_P| = \alpha t\sqrt{R^2 + h^2}$; $s(t) = \dfrac{1}{2}\,\alpha t^2\sqrt{R^2 + h^2}$; $a_t = \alpha\sqrt{R^2 + h^2}$, constant; $a_n = R\,\alpha^2t^2$; $\rho = \dfrac{R^2 + h^2}{R}$, the same value as for the helix traveled with constant angular velocity, because the radius of curvature depends only on the curve.
:::
