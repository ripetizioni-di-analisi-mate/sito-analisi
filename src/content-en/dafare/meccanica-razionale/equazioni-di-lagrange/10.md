The homogeneous disk of center $O$, mass $m$ and radius $R$ is hinged at the center to a smooth fixed horizontal axis and moves in the vertical plane; a material point of mass $m$ is welded at the point $P$ of its rim, as in the diagram in the figure. Write the equation of motion and compute the angular velocity with which the disk passes through the configuration $\vartheta = 0$, knowing that it starts from rest with $\vartheta = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$; the angle $\vartheta$ is the one between the radius $OP$ and the downward vertical.

::struttura{cerchi="0,0,1.6" vincoli="O,cerniera,90" nodi="O,0,0,-" tratteggi="0,0 0,-1.6;0,0 1.226,-1.028" punti="0,0,O,centro;1.226,-1.028,P" etichette="0.3,-0.95,ϑ;1.3,-1.42,m" titolo="Disk with a fixed axis and material point welded at the point $P$ of the rim"}

:::soluzione
$\dfrac{3}{2}\,m\,R^2\,\ddot{\vartheta} = -m\,g\,R\sin\vartheta$, that is $\ddot{\vartheta} = -\dfrac{2\,g}{3\,R}\sin\vartheta$; in the configuration $\vartheta = 0$ one has $|\dot{\vartheta}| = 2\,\sqrt{\dfrac{g}{3\,R}}$, with clockwise rotation.
:::
