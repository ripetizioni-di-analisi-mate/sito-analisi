The ring $P$ of mass $m$ slides without friction on a straight rod which rotates in a vertical plane about the fixed point $O$, with the law $\varphi = \omega\,t$ and $\omega$ constant, as in the diagram in the figure. Write the equation of motion, taking the distance $r$ of $P$ from $O$ as Lagrangian coordinate, and establish whether the Jacobi function is a first integral.

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,3.03,1.75" aste="O-A" cerchi="0,0,0.1" tratteggi="0,0 3.6,0" punti="1.732,1.0,P" forze="1.732 1,0,-0.7,mg" etichette="1.0,0.2,φ = ωt;2.1,0.82,m" quote="O,1.732 1,r,0.62" titolo="Rod rotating in the vertical plane with the ring $P$ sliding on it"}

:::soluzione
$m\,\ddot{r} = m\,\omega^2\,r - m\,g\sin(\omega\,t)$. The Lagrangian depends explicitly on time through the potential of the weight, hence the Jacobi function is not constant: one has $\dfrac{d\mathcal{H}}{dt} = m\,g\,\omega\,r\cos(\omega\,t)$.
:::
