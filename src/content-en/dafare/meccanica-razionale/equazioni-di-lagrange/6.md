The uniform rod $AB$, of mass $m$ and length $2\ell$, has the endpoint $A$ on a smooth horizontal guide and the endpoint $B$ on a smooth vertical guide; the two guides are fixed and intersect at $O$, and the system moves in the vertical plane. A spring of elastic constant $k$ and zero rest length connects $B$ to $O$, as in the diagram in the figure. Write the equation of motion, taking the angle $\vartheta$ between the rod and the vertical guide as Lagrangian coordinate.

::struttura{nodi="A,2.057,0;B,0,2.451" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello,270;B,carrello,180" linee="-0.7,0 3.1,0;0,-0.7 0,3.3;0,0 0.28,0.3 -0.28,0.72 0.28,1.14 -0.28,1.56 0,1.9 0,2.451" punti="1.028,1.226,G,baricentro" etichette="0.3,1.95,ϑ;-0.75,1.1,k" quote="A,B,2ℓ,-0.65" titolo="Rod with the endpoints on two orthogonal guides and spring between $B$ and $O$"}

:::soluzione
$\dfrac{4}{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\ddot{\vartheta} = \big( m\,g\,\ell + 4\,k\,\ell^2\cos\vartheta \big)\sin\vartheta$, that is $\ddot{\vartheta} = \dfrac{3\,\big( m\,g + 4\,k\,\ell\cos\vartheta \big)}{4\,m\,\ell}\sin\vartheta$.
:::
