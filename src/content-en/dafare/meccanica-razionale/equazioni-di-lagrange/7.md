The material point $A$ of mass $m$ slides on a smooth fixed horizontal guide; a rigid rod of negligible mass and length $\ell$ is hinged to it, which carries at its other endpoint the material point $P$ of mass $m$, as in the diagram in the figure. The system moves in the vertical plane. Write the equations of motion and the first integral due to the cyclic coordinate, taking the abscissa $x$ of $A$ and the angle $\vartheta$ between the rod and the downward vertical as Lagrangian coordinates.

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0,-;A,2.4,0;P,3.662,-1.802" catene="A-P" vincoli="A,carrello,90" linee="-0.6,0 6,0" tratteggi="2.4,0 2.4,-2.3" etichette="2.68,-1.3,ϑ;1.75,0.45,m;4.05,-2.2,m" quote="0 0,A,x,0.75" titolo="Slider on a horizontal guide with the pendulum hinged at $A$"}

:::soluzione
$$
\begin{cases}
2\,m\,\ddot{x} + m\,\ell\,\ddot{\vartheta}\cos\vartheta - m\,\ell\,\dot{\vartheta}^2\sin\vartheta = 0 \\[4pt]
\ell\,\ddot{\vartheta} + \ddot{x}\cos\vartheta + g\sin\vartheta = 0
\end{cases}
$$
The first integral is $p_x = 2\,m\,\dot{x} + m\,\ell\,\dot{\vartheta}\cos\vartheta$, constant, that is the horizontal component of the linear momentum.
:::
