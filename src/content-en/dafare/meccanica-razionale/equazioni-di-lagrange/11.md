titolo: Ring with a spring on a rod free to rotate

The uniform rod $OA$, of mass $2m$ and length $\ell$, is hinged at the endpoint $O$ by a smooth fixed hinge and rotates freely in a smooth horizontal plane; a ring of mass $m$, which can be treated as the material point $P$, slides on the rod without friction and is connected to $O$ by a spring of elastic constant $k$ and zero rest length, as in the diagram in the figure, seen from above. Write the equations of motion and the two first integrals, taking the angle $\varphi$ of rotation of the rod and the distance $r$ of $P$ from $O$ as Lagrangian coordinates.

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,2.771,1.6" aste="O-A" cerchi="0,0,0.1" linee="0,0 0.0377,0.218 0.453,0.0653 0.5283,0.5014 0.9437,0.3486 1.019,0.7847 1.4343,0.632 1.472,0.85" tratteggi="0,0 3.5,0" punti="1.472,0.85,P" etichette="0.95,0.18,φ;2.3,1.78,rod 2m and ℓ;1.05,1.05,k" quote="O,1.472 0.85,r,0.62" titolo="Ring connected by a spring, on a rod free to rotate in a horizontal plane"}

:::soluzione
$$
\begin{cases}
m\,\ddot{r} - m\,r\,\dot{\varphi}^2 = -k\,r \\[4pt]
\dfrac{d}{dt}\left[ \left( \dfrac{2}{3}\,m\,\ell^2 + m\,r^2 \right) \dot{\varphi} \right] = 0
\end{cases}
$$
The first integrals are $p_\varphi = \left( \dfrac{2}{3}\,m\,\ell^2 + m\,r^2 \right) \dot{\varphi}$ and the mechanical energy $E = \dfrac{1}{2}\left( \dfrac{2}{3}\,m\,\ell^2 + m\,r^2 \right) \dot{\varphi}^2 + \dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,\dot{r}^2 + \dfrac{1}{2}\,k\,r^2$.
:::
