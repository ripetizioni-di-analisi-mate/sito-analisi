The homogeneous disk of center $C$, mass $2m$ and radius $R$ rolls without slipping on a fixed horizontal guide, in the vertical plane; a material point of mass $m$ is welded at the point $P$ of its rim, as in the diagram in the figure. Write the equation of motion, taking the angle $\vartheta$ by which the disk is rotated, positive counterclockwise, starting from the configuration in which $P$ touches the guide, as Lagrangian coordinate.

::struttura{cerchi="3,1.3,1.3" linee="-0.5,0 7,0" tratteggi="3,1.3 3,0;3,1.3 3.996,0.465" punti="3,1.3,C,baricentro;3.996,0.465,P;3,0,H,centro" etichette="3.22,0.66,ϑ;1.45,1.8,2m;4.3,0.25,m" titolo="Disk of mass $2m$ rolling with the material point $P$ welded on the rim"}

:::soluzione
$m\,R^2\,\big( 5 - 2\cos\vartheta \big)\,\ddot{\vartheta} + m\,R^2\sin\vartheta\,\dot{\vartheta}^2 + m\,g\,R\sin\vartheta = 0$.
:::
