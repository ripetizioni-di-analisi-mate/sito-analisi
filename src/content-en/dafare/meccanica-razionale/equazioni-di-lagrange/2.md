The homogeneous disk of center $C$, mass $2m$ and radius $R$ rolls without slipping on a fixed horizontal guide, in the vertical plane; a spring of elastic constant $k$ and zero rest length connects $C$ to the point $O$ of the line described by $C$, as in the diagram in the figure. Write the equation of motion and the period of the oscillations, taking the abscissa $x$ of $C$ measured from $O$ as Lagrangian coordinate.

::struttura{nodi="O,0,1;C,3.5,1,-" cerchi="3.5,1,1" linee="-0.5,0 6.5,0;0,1 0.4,1.25 0.95,0.75 1.5,1.25 2.05,0.75 2.45,1 2.5,1" punti="3.5,1,C,baricentro;3.5,0,H,centro" etichette="1.2,1.55,k;4.6,1.5,2m" quote="0 0,3.5 0,x,0.75" titolo="Disk of mass $2m$ rolling, pulled back by the spring of constant $k$"}

:::soluzione
$3\,m\,\ddot{x} = -k\,x$, that is $\ddot{x} = -\dfrac{k}{3\,m}\,x$; the motion is harmonic with period $\mathcal{T} = 2\pi\,\sqrt{\dfrac{3\,m}{k}}$.
:::
