The uniform rod $OA$, of mass $m$ and length $\ell$, is hinged at $O$ by a smooth fixed hinge and moves in the vertical plane; a material point of mass $2m$ is welded at the endpoint $A$ and a constant couple $\mathcal{M}$, counterclockwise, is applied to the rod, as in the diagram in the figure. Write the equation of motion, taking the angle $\vartheta$ between the rod and the downward vertical as Lagrangian coordinate.

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,1.2,-2.078" aste="O-A" vincoli="O,cerniera,90" tratteggi="0,0 0,-2.4" punti="0.6,-1.039,G,baricentro" coppie="0.95 -1.65,1,ℳ" etichette="0.3,-1.1,ϑ;1.45,-2.25,2m" quote="O,A,ℓ,-0.65" titolo="Hinged rod with the material point welded at $A$ and the couple $\mathcal{M}$"}

:::soluzione
$\dfrac{7}{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\ddot{\vartheta} = \mathcal{M} - \dfrac{5}{2}\,m\,g\,\ell\sin\vartheta$, that is $\ddot{\vartheta} = \dfrac{3\,\big( 2\,\mathcal{M} - 5\,m\,g\,\ell\sin\vartheta \big)}{14\,m\,\ell^2}$.
:::
