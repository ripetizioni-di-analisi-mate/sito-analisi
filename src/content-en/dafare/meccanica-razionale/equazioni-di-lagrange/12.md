The rigid bracket $AOB$ consists of two uniform rods welded orthogonally at $O$: the rod $OA$ has mass $m$ and length $\ell$, the rod $OB$ has mass $2m$ and the same length $\ell$. The bracket is hinged at $O$ by a smooth fixed hinge and moves in the vertical plane, as in the diagram in the figure. Write the equation of motion, taking the angle $\vartheta$ between the rod $OA$ and the downward vertical as Lagrangian coordinate, with $OB$ obtained from $OA$ by a counterclockwise rotation of $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,0.98,-1.7;B,1.7,0.98" aste="O-A;O-B" vincoli="O,cerniera,90" tratteggi="0,0 0,-2.1" punti="0.49,-0.85,G_1,baricentro;0.85,0.49,G_2,baricentro" etichette="0.26,-1.05,ϑ;1.32,-1.9,m;2.05,0.82,2m" quote="O,A,ℓ,-0.5;O,B,ℓ,-0.5" titolo="Bracket formed by two orthogonal rods of mass $m$ and $2m$"}

:::soluzione
$m\,\ell^2\,\ddot{\vartheta} = -\dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,g\,\ell\,\big( \sin\vartheta + 2\cos\vartheta \big)$, that is $\ddot{\vartheta} = -\dfrac{g}{2\,\ell}\,\big( \sin\vartheta + 2\cos\vartheta \big)$.
:::
