The double pendulum in the figure consists of two rigid rods of negligible mass and length $\ell$: the first one is hinged at the fixed point $O$ and carries at the endpoint $A$ a material point of mass $m$, the second one is hinged at $A$ and carries at the endpoint $B$ a material point of mass $m$. The hinges are smooth and the system moves in the vertical plane. Write the equations of motion, taking the angles $\vartheta_1$ and $\vartheta_2$ that the two rods form with the downward vertical as Lagrangian coordinates.

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,0.803,-1.722;B,2.359,-2.812" catene="O-A;A-B" vincoli="O,cerniera,90" cerniere="A" tratteggi="0,0 0,-2.0;0.803,-1.722 0.803,-3.2" etichette="0.17,-1.02,ϑ_1;1.02,-2.45,ϑ_2;0.28,-2.05,m;2.62,-2.9,m" titolo="Double pendulum with two material points of equal mass"}

:::soluzione
$$
\begin{cases}
2\,\ell\,\ddot{\vartheta}_1 + \ell\cos(\vartheta_1 - \vartheta_2)\,\ddot{\vartheta}_2 + \ell\sin(\vartheta_1 - \vartheta_2)\,\dot{\vartheta}_2^2 + 2\,g\sin\vartheta_1 = 0 \\[4pt]
\ell\,\ddot{\vartheta}_2 + \ell\cos(\vartheta_1 - \vartheta_2)\,\ddot{\vartheta}_1 - \ell\sin(\vartheta_1 - \vartheta_2)\,\dot{\vartheta}_1^2 + g\sin\vartheta_2 = 0
\end{cases}
$$
:::
