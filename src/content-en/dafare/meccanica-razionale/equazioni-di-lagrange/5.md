---
titolo: Sphere rolling on an inclined plane
teoria: [equazioni-di-lagrange, lagrangiana, vincoli-ideali]

A homogeneous solid sphere of mass $m$ and radius $R$ rolls without slipping on a fixed plane inclined at an angle $\alpha$. The moment of inertia of the sphere with respect to an axis through the center is $\dfrac{2}{5}\,m\,R^2$. Compute the acceleration of the center, taking the curvilinear abscissa $s$ of the center along the plane, measured downwards, as Lagrangian coordinate.

:::soluzione
$\ddot{s} = \dfrac{5}{7}\,g\sin\alpha$, directed downwards along the plane.
:::
