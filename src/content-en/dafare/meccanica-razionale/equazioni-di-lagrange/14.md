The material point $P$ of mass $m$ is constrained to the inner surface of a smooth fixed sphere, of center $O$ and radius $R$, as in the diagram in the figure. The angle $\vartheta$ between the radius $OP$ and the downward vertical and the angle $\varphi$ that determines the meridian plane on which $P$ lies are taken as Lagrangian coordinates. Write the equations of motion, the two first integrals and the angular velocity $\dot{\varphi}$ for which $P$ describes the horizontal circle of colatitude $\vartheta_0$.

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 curve="2*cos(t),2*sin(t),0,6.29;1.532*cos(t),-1.286+0.46*sin(t),0,6.29" segmenti="0,2.5,0,-2.5;0,0,1.532,-1.286" punti="0,0,pieno,O;1.532,-1.286,pieno,P" etichette="0.22,-1.15,ϑ;1.02,-0.52,R" xmin=-2.7 xmax=2.7 ymin=-2.9 ymax=2.9 titolo="Point constrained to the inner surface of a smooth sphere of radius $R$"}

:::soluzione
$$
\begin{cases}
m\,R^2\,\ddot{\vartheta} = m\,R^2\sin\vartheta\cos\vartheta\,\dot{\varphi}^2 - m\,g\,R\sin\vartheta \\[4pt]
\dfrac{d}{dt}\big( m\,R^2\sin^2\vartheta\,\dot{\varphi} \big) = 0
\end{cases}
$$
The first integrals are $p_\varphi = m\,R^2\sin^2\vartheta\,\dot{\varphi}$ and the mechanical energy $E = \dfrac{1}{2}\,m\,R^2\,\big( \dot{\vartheta}^2 + \sin^2\vartheta\,\dot{\varphi}^2 \big) - m\,g\,R\cos\vartheta$. The horizontal circle of colatitude $\vartheta_0$ is described with $\dot{\varphi} = \sqrt{\dfrac{g}{R\cos\vartheta_0}}$, which exists for $0 < \vartheta_0 < \dfrac{\pi}{2}$.
:::
