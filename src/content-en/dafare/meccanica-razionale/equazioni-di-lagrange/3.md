The material point $P$ of mass $m$ slides on a smooth fixed horizontal straight guide, is connected to the fixed point $O$ of the guide by a spring of elastic constant $k$ and zero rest length and is subject to the viscous resistance $-h\,\vec{v}_P$, with $h > 0$. Write the Rayleigh dissipation function and the equation of motion, taking the abscissa $x$ of $P$ measured from $O$ as Lagrangian coordinate.

:::soluzione
$\mathcal{D} = \dfrac{1}{2}\,h\,\dot{x}^2$ and $m\,\ddot{x} + h\,\dot{x} + k\,x = 0$.
:::
