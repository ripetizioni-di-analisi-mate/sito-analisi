The pulley is a homogeneous disk of mass $3m$ and radius $R$, hinged at the center to a smooth fixed horizontal axis. An inextensible string of negligible mass is wound on the pulley, does not slip on it and holds at its other endpoint the material point $P$ of mass $m$. Compute the acceleration of $P$ and its velocity after it has descended by the length $d$, starting from rest.

:::soluzione
$\ddot{y} = \dfrac{2}{5}\,g$ downwards; $v = 2\,\sqrt{\dfrac{g\,d}{5}}$.
:::
