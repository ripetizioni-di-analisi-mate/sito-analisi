The homogeneous plate in the figure is the square with vertices $(0,0)$ and $(2\ell, 2\ell)$ with the disk of radius $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$ centered at $\left( \dfrac{\ell}{2},\ \dfrac{\ell}{2} \right)$ removed. Determine the center of mass of the plate.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 3,0 3,3 0,3" cerchi="0.75,0.75,0.75,foro" punti="0.75,0.75,C" quote="0 0,3 0,2ℓ,0.5" frecce="-0.6,0,3.7,0,x;0,-0.6,0,3.7,y" tratteggi="0,0 3,3" riferimento=0 titolo="Square plate of side $2\ell$ with a circular hole of radius $\dfrac{\ell}{2}$"}

:::soluzione
$x_G = y_G = \dfrac{(32 - \pi)\,\ell}{2\,(16 - \pi)} \approx 1.122\,\ell$, on the diagonal issuing from the origin and beyond the center of the square.
:::
