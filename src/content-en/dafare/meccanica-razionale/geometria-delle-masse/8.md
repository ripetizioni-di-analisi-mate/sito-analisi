titolo: Moment of inertia of a rod about a non-central axis
teoria: [momento-di-inerzia, huygens-steiner, momenti-notevoli]
parole: [rod, moment of inertia, Huygens-Steiner, radius of gyration]
---

A homogeneous rigid rod has mass $m$ and length $\ell$. Compute the moment of inertia about the axis orthogonal to the rod through the point $Q$ which is at distance $\dfrac{\ell}{3}$ from one end, and the corresponding radius of gyration.

:::soluzione
$I_Q = \dfrac{m\ell^2}{9}$ and $\rho_Q = \dfrac{\ell}{3}$.
:::
