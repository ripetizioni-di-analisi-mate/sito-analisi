The homogeneous circular sector in the figure has center $O$, radius $R$ and half-angle $\alpha = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$, with the bisector on the $x$ axis. Determine the center of mass of the sector and the center of mass of the homogeneous circular arc that bounds its curved edge.

::struttura{poligoni="1.500,-2.598 1.826,-2.380 2.121,-2.121 2.380,-1.826 2.598,-1.500 2.772,-1.148 2.898,-0.776 2.974,-0.392 3.000,0.000 2.974,0.392 2.898,0.776 2.772,1.148 2.598,1.500 2.380,1.826 2.121,2.121 1.826,2.380 1.500,2.598 0.000,0.000" punti="0,0,O" tratteggi="0,0 1.5,2.598;0,0 1.5,-2.598" etichette="1.25,0.75,α;1.25,-0.95,α" frecce="-0.7,0,4,0,x;0,-3.3,0,3.3,y" riferimento=0 titolo="Circular sector of radius $R$ and half-angle $\alpha = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$"}

:::soluzione
Sector: $x_G = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{\pi}\,R \approx 0.551\,R$. Arc: $x_G = \dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{2\pi}\,R \approx 0.827\,R$. In both cases $y_G = 0$.
:::
