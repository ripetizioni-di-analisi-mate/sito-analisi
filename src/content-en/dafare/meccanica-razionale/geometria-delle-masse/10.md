The system in the figure consists of a homogeneous rigid rod $OA$ of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ and of a homogeneous rigid disk of mass $m$ and radius $\dfrac{\ell}{4}$, welded to the rod with its center at the end $A$. Compute the moment of inertia of the system about the axis through $O$ orthogonal to the plane of the disk.

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,4,0" aste="O-A" cerchi="4,0,1" quote="O,A,ℓ,1.4" etichette="1.8,0.3,m;4.6,1.05,m" riferimento=0 titolo="Rod of length $\ell$ with a disk of radius $\dfrac{\ell}{4}$ welded at the end $A$"}

:::soluzione
$I_O = \dfrac{131}{96}\,m\ell^2 \approx 1.365\,m\ell^2$.
:::
