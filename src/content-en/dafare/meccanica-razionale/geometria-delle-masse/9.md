A homogeneous rigid ring, that is a material circumference, has mass $m$ and radius $R$. Compute the moment of inertia about the axis orthogonal to the plane of the ring through the center, about a diameter and about the axis orthogonal to the plane through a point of the ring.

:::soluzione
$I = mR^2$ about the orthogonal axis through the center; $I = \dfrac{mR^2}{2}$ about a diameter; $I = 2\,mR^2$ about the orthogonal axis through a point of the ring.
:::
