The homogeneous solid in the figure is obtained by joining a right circular cone of radius $R$ and height $2R$, with the base in the plane $z = 0$ and the vertex at $z = -2R$, and the solid half-sphere of radius $R$ with its flat face on the same base and contained in the half-space $z \ge 0$. Determine the height of the center of mass of the solid.

:::soluzione
$z_G = -\dfrac{R}{16}$, because the two pieces have the same volume $\dfrac{2\pi R^3}{3}$; the center of mass lies on the axis of revolution, a little below the common base.
:::
