The homogeneous plate in the figure has mass $m$ and occupies the right triangle with vertices $O = (0,0)$, $A = (2\ell, 0)$ and $B = (0, \ell)$ of the plane $Oxy$. Write the inertia tensor relative to the pole $O$.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 3,0 0,1.5" punti="1,0.5,G,baricentro" quote="0 0,3 0,2ℓ,0.5;0 1.5,0 0,ℓ,0.5" frecce="-0.7,0,3.8,0,x;0,-0.7,0,2.4,y" riferimento=0 titolo="Homogeneous triangular plate with the legs of lengths $2\ell$ and $\ell$ on the axes"}

:::soluzione
$$
\mathbb{I}_O = \frac{m\ell^2}{6}
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & -1 & 0 \\
-1 & 4 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 5
\end{pmatrix}
$$
:::
