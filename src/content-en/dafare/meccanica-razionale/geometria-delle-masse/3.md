---
titolo: Center of mass of a rod with variable density
teoria: [sistema-materiale, centro-di-massa]
parole: [non-homogeneous rod, linear density, center of mass, integral]
---

A straight rod $AB$ of length $\ell$ has linear density
$$
\lambda(s) = \lambda_0 \left( 1 + \frac{s^2}{\ell^2} \right) ,
$$
where $s$ is the abscissa measured from $A$ and $\lambda_0$ is a positive constant. Compute the mass of the rod and the distance of its center of mass from the end $A$.

:::soluzione
$M = \dfrac{4}{3}\,\lambda_0 \ell$ and $s_G = \dfrac{9}{16}\,\ell$, hence the center of mass is shifted towards $B$ with respect to the midpoint.
:::
