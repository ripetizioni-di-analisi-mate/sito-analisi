The homogeneous rigid rod in the figure has mass $m$ and length $\ell$, has one end at the origin $O$ and makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with the $x$ axis, in the plane $Oxy$. Compute the moment of inertia about the line through $O$ that makes an angle of $30^\circ$ with the $x$ axis, and the corresponding radius of gyration.

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,1.5,2.598" aste="O-A" frecce="-0.7,0,3.4,0,x;0,-0.7,0,3.2,y;0,0,2.6,1.5,u" etichette="0.95,0.3,30°;0.75,0.95,30°" quote="O,A,ℓ,-0.6" riferimento=0 titolo="Rod inclined at $60^\circ$ and line with unit vector $\vec{u}$ inclined at $30^\circ$"}

:::soluzione
$I_u = \dfrac{m\ell^2}{12}$ and $\rho_u = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{6}\,\ell \approx 0.289\,\ell$.
:::
