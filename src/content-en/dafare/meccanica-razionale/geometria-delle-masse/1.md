The system in the figure consists of two homogeneous rigid rods welded at $A$: the rod $OA$, horizontal, has length $\ell$ and mass $m$; the rod $AB$, vertical, has length $2\ell$ and mass $2m$. Determine the center of mass of the system in the frame $(O; x, y)$.

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,1,0;B,1,2" aste="O-A;A-B" quote="O,A,ℓ,0.5;A,B,2ℓ,0.9" etichette="0.4,0.25,m;0.65,1.1,2m" frecce="-0.5,0,1.9,0,x;0,-0.5,0,2.6,y" riferimento=0 titolo="Two homogeneous rods welded at a right angle"}

:::soluzione
$G = \left( \dfrac{5}{6}\,\ell,\ \dfrac{2}{3}\,\ell \right)$.
:::
