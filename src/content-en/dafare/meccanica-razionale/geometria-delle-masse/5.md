The homogeneous plate in the figure consists of the rectangle of base $2R$ and height $R$ that occupies the region $-R \le x \le R$, $-R \le y \le 0$, and of the half-disk with center $O$ and radius $R$ contained in the half-plane $y \ge 0$. Determine the center of mass of the plate.

:::soluzione
$x_G = 0$ and $y_G = -\dfrac{2R}{3\pi + 12} \approx -0.093\,R$, that is the center of mass lies a little below the diameter.
