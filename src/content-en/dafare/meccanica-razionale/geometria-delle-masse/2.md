Three equal homogeneous rigid rods, each of length $\ell$ and mass $m$, are welded so as to form the equilateral triangle $ABC$ in the figure, with $A$ at the origin and the side $AB$ on the $x$ axis. Determine the center of mass of the system.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,0;C,1,1.732" aste="A-B;B-C;C-A" punti="1,0.577,G,baricentro" quote="A,B,ℓ,0.5" riferimento=0 titolo="Frame formed by three equal rods in an equilateral triangle"}

:::soluzione
$G = \left( \dfrac{\ell}{2},\ \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{6}\,\ell \right)$, that is the centroid of the triangle, because the three rods have equal masses.
:::
