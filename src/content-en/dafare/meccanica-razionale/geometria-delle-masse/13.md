In the frame $(O; x, y, z)$ there are a material point of mass $2m$ at $(\ell, \ell, 0)$ and a point of mass $3m$ at $(\ell, 0, 0)$, as the diagram in the figure shows. Write the inertia tensor relative to the pole $O$, determine the principal moments of inertia and the directions of the principal axes.

::struttura{punti="2,2,P_1;2,0,P_2;0,0," tratteggi="0,0 2,2;0,0 2,0" etichette="2.2,2.3,2m;2.2,0.35,3m;-0.3,-0.35,O" frecce="-0.7,0,3.2,0,x;0,-0.7,0,3.2,y" quote="0 0,2 0,ℓ,0.6" riferimento=0 titolo="Two material points of masses $2m$ and $3m$ in the plane $Oxy$"}

:::soluzione
$$
\mathbb{I}_O = m\ell^2
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & -2 & 0 \\
-2 & 5 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 7
\end{pmatrix}
$$
Principal moments $I_1 = m\ell^2$, $I_2 = 6\,m\ell^2$ and $I_3 = 7\,m\ell^2$, with principal axes directed as $(2, 1, 0)$, as $(1, -2, 0)$ and as the $z$ axis.
:::
