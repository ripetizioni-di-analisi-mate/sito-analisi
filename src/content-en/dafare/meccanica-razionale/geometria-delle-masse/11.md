In the frame $(O; x, y, z)$ there are a material point of mass $2m$ at $(\ell, \ell, 0)$, a point of mass $m$ at $(\ell, 0, 0)$ and a point of mass $m$ at $(0, \ell, 0)$, as the diagram in the figure shows. Write the inertia tensor relative to the pole $O$.

::struttura{punti="2,2,P_1;2,0,P_2;0,2,P_3;0,0," tratteggi="0,0 2,2" etichette="2.2,2.3,2m;2.2,0.3,m;0.25,2.35,m;-0.3,-0.35,O" frecce="-0.7,0,3.2,0,x;0,-0.7,0,3.2,y" quote="0 0,2 0,ℓ,0.6" riferimento=0 titolo="Three material points in the plane $Oxy$"}

:::soluzione
$$
\mathbb{I}_O = m\ell^2
\begin{pmatrix}
3 & -2 & 0 \\
-2 & 3 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 6
\end{pmatrix}
$$
:::
