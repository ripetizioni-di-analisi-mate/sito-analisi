A particle of mass $m$ slides along the horizontal, smooth and fixed rectilinear guide in the figure, connected to the wall by an ideal spring of constant $k$ and by a damper that exerts the force $-h\,\dot{x}\,\hat{\imath}$, with $h = \sqrt{3 m k}$. Determine the type of damping, the pseudo angular frequency, the pseudo-period and the ratio between two successive maxima of the elongation.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;P,3.6,0" vincoli="A,incastro,180" linee="0,0 0.3,0.35 0.9,-0.35 1.5,0.35 2.1,-0.35 2.7,0.35 3.3,-0.35 3.6,0;3.6,0 6.2,0;3.6,0 3.6,-0.8;0,-0.8 0.6,-0.8;1.2,-0.8 3.6,-0.8;0.6,-1.15 1.8,-1.15 1.8,-0.45 0.6,-0.45 0.6,-1.15;1.2,-1.05 1.2,-0.55" punti="3.6,0" etichette="1.8,0.75,k;1.2,-1.5,h" riferimento=0 titolo="Particle on a smooth guide with a spring of constant $k$ and a damper of constant $h = \sqrt{3 m k}$"}

:::soluzione
Underdamping, because $\gamma = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m}}$ is smaller than $\omega_0 = \sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m}}$; pseudo angular frequency $\omega_s = \dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m}}$; pseudo-period $T_s = 4\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k}}$; ratio between two successive maxima $e^{-2\sqrt{3}\,\pi} \approx 1.9 \cdot 10^{-5}$.
:::
