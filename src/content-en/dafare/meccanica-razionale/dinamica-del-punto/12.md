titolo: Detachment from a cylindrical surface with an initial velocity

A particle of mass $m$ is at the vertex $S$ of the smooth and fixed cylindrical surface of radius $R$ in the figure, with horizontal axis, and is launched with velocity $v_0$ in a plane orthogonal to the axis. The constraint is unilateral, because the surface can only push the particle outwards. Denoting by $\vartheta$ the angle measured from the upward vertical, determine the angle of detachment and the values of $v_0$ for which the detachment occurs at once, at the vertex.

::struttura{cerchi="0,0,2" nodi="C,0,0;S,0,2" linee="-2.6,-2 2.6,-2" punti="0,2;0,0" frecce="0.2,2.3,1.6,2.3,v_0" tratteggi="0,0 0,2.55" riferimento=0 titolo="Particle launched from the vertex of a smooth cylindrical surface of radius $R$"}

:::soluzione
$\cos\vartheta_d = \dfrac{2}{3} + \dfrac{v_0^2}{3 g R}$; the detachment occurs at the vertex if $v_0^2 \geq g R$.
:::
