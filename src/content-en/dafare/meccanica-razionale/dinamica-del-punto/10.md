---
titolo: Equilibrium and small oscillations with a two-term potential
teoria: [moto-unidimensionale, forze-conservative]
parole: [potential energy, equilibrium configuration, minimum of the potential, small oscillations]
---

A particle of mass $m$ slides along the half-line $r > 0$ of a smooth and fixed rectilinear guide, and is subject to a force of potential energy
$$
V(r) = -\frac{A}{r} + \frac{B}{r^2} ,
\qquad
A > 0 ,
\qquad
B > 0 .
$$
Determine the equilibrium configuration and its nature, the minimum value of the potential energy and the angular frequency of the small oscillations about the equilibrium.

:::soluzione
Stable equilibrium at $r_e = \dfrac{2B}{A}$; minimum value $V(r_e) = -\dfrac{A^2}{4B}$; angular frequency $\omega = \dfrac{A^2}{2B\sqrt{2 m B}}$.
:::
