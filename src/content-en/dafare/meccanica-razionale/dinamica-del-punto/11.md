---
titolo: Simple pendulum launched from the lowest point
teoria: [pendolo-semplice, conservazione-energia, equazioni-intrinseche]
parole: [simple pendulum, tension of the string, maximum angle, conservation of energy]
---

A particle of mass $m$ hangs at $O$ from an inextensible massless string of length $\ell$, and moves in the vertical plane. The particle starts from the lowest position with horizontal velocity of modulus $v_0$, with $v_0^2 < 2 g \ell$, and the string remains taut. Determine the maximum angle $\vartheta_{\max}$ that the string makes with the downward vertical and the tension of the string in the lowest position.

:::soluzione
$\cos\vartheta_{\max} = 1 - \dfrac{v_0^2}{2 g \ell}$; tension in the lowest position $\Phi = m\left( g + \dfrac{v_0^2}{\ell} \right)$.
:::
