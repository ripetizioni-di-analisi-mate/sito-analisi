---
titolo: Force that produces a cardioid orbit
teoria: [formula-di-binet, moto-centrale, velocita-areolare]
parole: [central motion, Binet's formula, cardioid, areal constant, central force]
---

A particle of mass $m$ moves with central motion of center $O$ with areal constant $c$, and travels along the orbit of polar equation
$$
r = a\,( 1 + \cos\vartheta ) ,
\qquad
a > 0 ,
$$
represented in the figure. Determine the force that produces that orbit.

::grafico{curve="(1+cos(t))*cos(t),(1+cos(t))*sin(t),0,6.283" legenda=0 uguale=1 punti="0,0,pieno,O" xmin=-1.1 xmax=2.4 ymin=-1.6 ymax=1.6 titolo="Cardioid orbit $r = a\,( 1 + \cos\vartheta )$ with the center of force at $O$"}

:::soluzione
$\vec{F} = -\dfrac{3\,m\,a\,c^2}{r^4}\,\hat{u}_r$, that is, an attractive force inversely proportional to the fourth power of the distance.
:::
