---
titolo: Mass hanging from a vertical spring
teoria: [oscillatore-armonico, forze-conservative, conservazione-energia]
parole: [vertical spring, static elongation, oscillations, tension of the spring]
---

An ideal spring of constant $k$ and rest length $\ell_0$ hangs from the ceiling at $A$; at the other end a particle of mass $m$ is attached, which moves along the vertical. The particle is brought to the distance $a$ below the equilibrium position and released from rest. Determine the static elongation of the spring, the period of the oscillations, the maximum velocity of the particle and the maximum tension of the spring.

:::soluzione
Static elongation $\delta = \dfrac{m g}{k}$; period $T = 2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k}}$; maximum velocity $a\sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m}}$; maximum tension $m g + k\,a$.
:::
