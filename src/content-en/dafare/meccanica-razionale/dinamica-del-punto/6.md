A particle of mass $m$ slides along the horizontal, smooth and fixed rectilinear guide in the figure. It is connected to the wall by two ideal springs placed one after the other, of constants $k$ and $2k$ and of negligible mass, joined at the point $B$. Determine the equivalent elastic constant of the connection and the period of the small oscillations of the particle.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3.1,0;P,6.2,0" vincoli="A,incastro,180" linee="0,0 0.25,0.35 0.75,-0.35 1.25,0.35 1.75,-0.35 2.25,0.35 2.75,-0.35 3.1,0;3.1,0 3.35,0.35 3.85,-0.35 4.35,0.35 4.85,-0.35 5.35,0.35 5.85,-0.35 6.2,0;6.2,0 8.4,0" punti="6.2,0;3.1,0" etichette="1.5,0.75,k;4.7,0.75,2k" riferimento=0 titolo="Two springs in series between the wall and the particle $P$"}

:::soluzione
Equivalent constant $k_{\text{eq}} = \dfrac{2k}{3}$; period $T = 2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{3 m}{2k}} = \pi\sqrt{6}\,\sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k}}$.
:::
