---
titolo: Discussion of the motion with a cubic potential
teoria: [moto-unidimensionale, forze-conservative, oscillatore-armonico]
parole: [potential energy, stable equilibrium, unstable equilibrium, turning points, small oscillations]
---

A particle of mass $m$ slides along a horizontal, smooth and fixed rectilinear guide, and is subject to a force of potential energy
$$
V(x) = c\left( \frac{x^3}{3} - \ell^2\,x \right) ,
\qquad
c > 0 ,
\qquad
\ell > 0 .
$$
Determine the equilibrium configurations and their nature, the angular frequency of the small oscillations about the stable one and the turning points of the motion of energy $E = 0$ that takes place about it.

:::soluzione
Equilibria at $x = \ell$, stable, and at $x = -\ell$, unstable; angular frequency of the small oscillations $\sqrt{\dfrac{2 c\,\ell}{m}}$; turning points $x = 0$ and $x = \sqrt{3}\,\ell$.
:::
