---
titolo: Rectilinear motion with a force decreasing in time
teoria: [equazione-fondamentale, problema-del-moto]
parole: [time dependent force, integration of the equation of motion, terminal velocity, law of motion]
---

A particle of mass $m$, at rest at $O$ at the instant $t = 0$, slides along a horizontal, smooth and fixed rectilinear guide, and is subject to the force $F_0\,e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}}\,\hat{\imath}$, with $F_0 > 0$ and $\tau > 0$, besides the weight. Determine the velocity as a function of the time, its limiting value as $t \to +\infty$ and the law of motion.

:::soluzione
$\dot{x}(t) = \dfrac{F_0\,\tau}{m}\left( 1 - e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} \right)$, with limit $\dfrac{F_0\,\tau}{m}$; law of motion $x(t) = \dfrac{F_0\,\tau}{m}\left( t - \tau + \tau\,e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} \right)$.
:::
