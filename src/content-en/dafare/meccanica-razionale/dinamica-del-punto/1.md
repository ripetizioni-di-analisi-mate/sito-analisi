titolo: Rectilinear motion with a constant force opposing the velocity

A particle of mass $m$ slides along the horizontal, smooth and fixed rectilinear guide in the figure. At the instant $t = 0$ it is at $O$ with velocity $v_0\,\hat{\imath}$, with $v_0 > 0$, and from that instant it is subject to the constant force $-F\,\hat{\imath}$, with $F > 0$, besides the weight. Determine the distance travelled before stopping, the time taken to stop, the instant at which the particle returns to $O$ and the velocity with which it returns there.

::struttura{linee="-0.5,0 7,0" nodi="O,0,0" punti="3,0,P" forze="3 0,-1.3,0,F" frecce="3,0.55,4.3,0.55,v_0" riferimento=0 titolo="Particle on a smooth horizontal guide with the constant force $F$ opposite to the initial velocity"}

:::soluzione
Stopping distance $d = \dfrac{m\,v_0^2}{2F}$; stopping time $t_1 = \dfrac{m\,v_0}{F}$; return to $O$ at the instant $t = \dfrac{2 m\,v_0}{F}$, with velocity of modulus $v_0$ directed in the negative direction of the $x$ axis.
:::
