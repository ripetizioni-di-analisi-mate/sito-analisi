---
titolo: Fall of a body under gravity with viscous resistance
teoria: [resistenza-viscosa, equazione-fondamentale]
parole: [fall, viscous resistance, terminal velocity, characteristic time]
---

A particle of mass $m = 0.50\,\mathrm{kg}$ is released from rest in a fluid that exerts the viscous resistance $-h\,\vec{v}_P$, with $h = 0.25\,\mathrm{kg/s}$; $g = 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ is assumed. Determine the terminal velocity, the expression of the velocity as a function of the time and the instant at which the velocity reaches half of the limiting value.

:::soluzione
$v_\ell = \dfrac{m g}{h} = 19.62\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $v(t) = \dfrac{m g}{h}\left( 1 - e^{-\frac{h}{m}t} \right)$; the instant sought $t_m = \dfrac{m\ln 2}{h} = 1.39\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
