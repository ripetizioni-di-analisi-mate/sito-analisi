---
titolo: Resonance of a forced oscillator
teoria: [oscillatore-forzato, oscillatore-smorzato]
parole: [forced oscillator, resonance, steady-state amplitude, resonant angular frequency]
---

To the system formed by a particle of mass $m$, by a spring of constant $k$ and by a damper of constant $h = \dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{m k}$, on a smooth horizontal guide, the force $F_0\cos(\Omega t)\,\hat{\imath}$ is applied. Determine the resonant angular frequency and the maximum amplitude of the steady-state motion, expressing them as functions of $\omega_0 = \sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m}}$ and of $\dfrac{F_0}{k}$.

:::soluzione
$\Omega_r = \dfrac{\sqrt{14}}{4}\,\omega_0 \approx 0.935\,\omega_0$; maximum amplitude $A(\Omega_r) = \dfrac{8\sqrt{15}}{15}\,\dfrac{F_0}{k} \approx 2.07\,\dfrac{F_0}{k}$.
:::
