A particle of mass $m$ slides along the horizontal, smooth and fixed rectilinear guide in the figure, connected to the wall by an ideal spring of constant $k$. The abscissa $x$ is measured from the position in which the spring has its rest length. At the instant $t = 0$ the particle is at $x = 0$ with velocity $v_0\,\hat{\imath}$. Determine the period of the oscillations, the amplitude and the maximum modulus of the elastic force.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;P,3.6,0" vincoli="A,incastro,180" linee="0,0 0.3,0.35 0.9,-0.35 1.5,0.35 2.1,-0.35 2.7,0.35 3.3,-0.35 3.6,0;3.6,0 6.2,0" punti="3.6,0" frecce="3.6,0.6,5,0.6,v_0" etichette="1.8,0.75,k" riferimento=0 titolo="Particle on a smooth guide pulled back by a spring of constant $k$, launched from the rest position"}

:::soluzione
$T = 2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k}}$; amplitude $A = v_0\sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k}}$; maximum elastic force $k\,A = v_0\sqrt{m k}$.
:::
