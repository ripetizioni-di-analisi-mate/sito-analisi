A smooth circular guide of radius $R$ is placed in a vertical plane and rotates with constant angular velocity $\omega$ about its own vertical diameter, as in the figure; a bead of mass $m$ is threaded on it. Setting $\omega^2 = \dfrac{3 g}{2 R}$ and denoting by $\vartheta$ the angle between the radius that locates the bead and the downward vertical, determine all the configurations of equilibrium relative to the guide and say which ones are stable.

::struttura{cerchi="0,0,2,foro" nodi="C,0,0" punti="0,0;1.491,-1.333" tratteggi="0,2.9 0,-2.9;0,0 1.491,-1.333" coppie="0 2.5,1,ω" etichette="0.42,-1.1,ϑ" riferimento=0 titolo="Circular guide rotating about its own vertical diameter"}

:::soluzione
Relative equilibria at $\vartheta = 0$ and $\vartheta = \pi$, both unstable, and at $\vartheta = \pm\arccos\dfrac{2}{3} \approx \pm 48.19^\circ$, both stable.
:::
