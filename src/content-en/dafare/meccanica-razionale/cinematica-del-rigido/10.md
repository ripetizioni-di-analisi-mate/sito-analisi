---
titolo: Fixed centrode and moving centrode of a disk in a circular guide
teoria: [base-e-rulletta, rotolamento-rulletta, rotolamento-senza-strisciamento, centro-di-istantanea-rotazione]
parole: [fixed centrode, moving centrode, space centrode, body centrode, circular guide, disk, rolling]
---

A homogeneous disk of radius $r$ and center $G$ rolls without slipping inside a fixed circular guide of radius $R > r$ and center $O$. Determine the fixed centrode and the moving centrode of the motion of the disk, and verify that the moving centrode rolls without slipping on the fixed centrode by comparing the lengths of the arcs traveled.

:::soluzione
The fixed centrode is the circle with center $O$ and radius $R$, that is, the guide itself; the moving centrode is the circle with center $G$ and radius $r$, that is, the rim of the disk. In a variation $\Delta\varphi$ of the angle that locates $G$, the arc of the fixed centrode equals $R\,\Delta\varphi$ and the arc of the moving centrode equals $r\,\left| \Delta\vartheta - \Delta\varphi \right| = R\,\Delta\varphi$, where $\vartheta$ is the rotation angle of the disk.
:::
