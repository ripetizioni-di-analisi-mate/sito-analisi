The two homogeneous disks in the figure, of different radii $R_1$ and $R_2$, roll without slipping on the same fixed horizontal guide and are connected by a rigid rod hinged at their centers $G_1$ and $G_2$. The center $G_1$ has velocity $\vec{v}_{G_1} = v\,\hat{\imath}$, with $v > 0$. Determine the state of motion of the rod, the velocity of $G_2$ and the angular velocities of the two disks.

::struttura{cerchi="1.2,1.2,1.2;4.2,0.7,0.7" linee="-0.3,0 5.6,0" aste="G1-G2" nodi="G1,1.2,1.2,-;G2,4.2,0.7,-" punti="1.2,1.2,G_1,baricentro;4.2,0.7,G_2,baricentro;1.2,0,C_1,centro;4.2,0,C_2,centro" titolo="Two disks of different radii connected by a rod hinged at their centers"}

:::soluzione
The rod has a translational state of motion, because the two centers stay at constant heights and their distance does not change, hence $\vec{\omega}_{\text{rod}} = \vec{0}$ and $\vec{v}_{G_2} = v\,\hat{\imath}$. The angular velocities of the disks are $\vec{\omega}_1 = -\dfrac{v}{R_1}\;\hat{k}$ and $\vec{\omega}_2 = -\dfrac{v}{R_2}\;\hat{k}$, both in the clockwise sense and different from each other.
:::
