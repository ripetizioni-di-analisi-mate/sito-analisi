titolo: Mozzi axis of a helical state of motion

A rigid body moves in space. At the instant considered its point $A$, coinciding with the origin, has velocity $\vec{v}_A = v\,\hat{\imath}$ and the angular velocity of the body is $\vec{\omega} = \omega\,\big( \hat{\imath} + 2\,\hat{\jmath} + 2\,\hat{k} \big)$, with $v > 0$ and $\omega > 0$. Classify the state of motion, determine a point of the Mozzi axis, the minimum velocity and the pitch.

:::soluzione
$I = \omega v \neq 0$, hence the state of motion is helical; $|\vec{\omega}| = 3\omega$; a point of the axis is $P^* = \dfrac{2v}{9\omega}\,\hat{\jmath} - \dfrac{2v}{9\omega}\,\hat{k}$, and the axis is the line through $P^*$ with direction $(1,\,2,\,2)$; $\vec{v}_{\min} = \dfrac{v}{9}\,\big( \hat{\imath} + 2\,\hat{\jmath} + 2\,\hat{k} \big)$, of magnitude $\dfrac{v}{3}$; pitch $p = \dfrac{v}{9\omega}$.
:::
