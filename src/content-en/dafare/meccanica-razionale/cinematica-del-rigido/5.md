titolo: Disk rolling without slipping on a straight guide

A homogeneous disk of radius $R$ and center $G$ rolls without slipping on a fixed horizontal guide, in the vertical plane. The center advances to the right with constant velocity $\vec{v}_G = v\,\hat{\imath}$, with $v > 0$. Compute the angular velocity of the disk, the velocity of the point $T$ diametrically opposite to the contact and the acceleration of the point $C$ in contact with the guide.

:::soluzione
$\vec{\omega} = -\dfrac{v}{R}\;\hat{k}$, that is, $\dfrac{v}{R}$ in the clockwise sense; $\vec{v}_T = 2v\;\hat{\imath}$; $\vec{a}_C = \dfrac{v^2}{R}\;\hat{\jmath}$, directed from $C$ toward the center $G$.
:::
