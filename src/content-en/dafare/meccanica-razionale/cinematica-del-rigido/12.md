---
titolo: Angular velocity from the velocities of three points
teoria: [formula-fondamentale, unicita-omega, invariante-cinematico, teorema-di-mozzi, equiproiettivita]
parole: [angular velocity, three points, rotational state of motion, axis of instantaneous rotation, kinematic invariant]
---

For a rigid body moving in space the velocities of three points are known, measured in $\mathrm{m/s}$:
$$
\vec{v}_A = (-2,\, 0,\, -2) ,
\qquad
\vec{v}_B = (-2,\, -2,\, -3) ,
\qquad
\vec{v}_C = (-1,\, -2,\, -2) ,
$$
with $A = (0,\,0,\,0)$, $B = (1,\,0,\,0)$ and $C = (0,\,0,\,1)$ in meters. Determine the angular velocity of the body, the kinematic invariant, the type of state of motion and the Mozzi axis.

:::soluzione
$\vec{\omega} = (2,\, 1,\, -2)\;\mathrm{rad/s}$, with $|\vec{\omega}| = 3\;\mathrm{rad/s}$; $I = 0$, hence the state of motion is rotational; the axis of instantaneous rotation is the line through $(0,\,1,\,0)\,\mathrm{m}$ with direction $(2,\,1,\,-2)$.
:::
