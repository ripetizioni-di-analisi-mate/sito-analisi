The rod in the figure has the endpoint $A$ on a smooth horizontal guide and slides inside a fixed sleeve hinged at $D$, placed at height $h$ above the guide. At the instant considered the rod forms the angle $\vartheta = 60^\circ$ with the horizontal and $\dot{\vartheta} = \omega_0 > 0$. Compute the angular velocity of the rod, the velocity of $A$ and the position of the instantaneous center of rotation.

::struttura{nodi="A,-1.73,0;B,0.77,4.33" aste="A-B" linee="-3,0 1.6,0;0.03,3.45 0.37,3.25 -0.03,2.55 -0.37,2.75 0.03,3.45" punti="-1.73,0;0.77,4.33;0,3,D;0,0,O" vincoli="A,carrello" tratteggi="0,0 0,3" quote="0 0,0 3,h,0.5" etichette="-1.05,0.3,ϑ" riferimento=0 titolo="Rod in the fixed sleeve $D$, with the endpoint $A$ on the horizontal guide"}

:::soluzione
$\vec{\omega} = \omega_0\,\hat{k}$, that is, $\omega_0$ in the counterclockwise sense; $\vec{v}_A = \dfrac{4}{3}\,h\omega_0\;\hat{\imath}$, to the right; instantaneous center of rotation at $C - O = -\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\,h\;\hat{\imath} + \dfrac{4}{3}\,h\;\hat{\jmath}$.
:::
