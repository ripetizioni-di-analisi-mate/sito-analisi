titolo: Angular velocity of a regular precession

A rigid body with a fixed point moves in a regular precession with constant nutation angle $\vartheta = 45^\circ$ and with $\dot{\psi} = \dot{\varphi} = \Omega > 0$. Compute the components of the angular velocity in the attached basis at the instant at which $\varphi = 0$, the magnitude of $\vec{\omega}$ and the half-angles of the fixed cone and of the moving cone.

:::soluzione
$\omega_\xi = 0$, $\omega_\eta = \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\,\Omega$, $\omega_\zeta = \left( 1 + \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \right)\Omega$; $|\vec{\omega}| = \sqrt{2 + \sqrt{2}}\;\Omega \approx 1.85\,\Omega$; the two half-angles are equal, $\alpha = \beta = 22.5^\circ$, and their sum is $\vartheta$.
:::
