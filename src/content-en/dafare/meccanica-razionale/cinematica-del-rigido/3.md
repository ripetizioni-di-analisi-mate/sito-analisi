The rod $AB$ in the figure, of length $\ell$, has the endpoint $A$ on a smooth horizontal guide and the endpoint $B$ on a smooth vertical guide, the guides meeting at $O$. At the instant considered the rod forms the angle $\vartheta = 30^\circ$ with the horizontal and $\dot{\vartheta} = \omega_0 > 0$. Compute the angular velocity of the rod, the position of the instantaneous center of rotation and the velocity of the midpoint $M$.

::struttura{nodi="A,2.6,0;B,0,1.5" aste="A-B" linee="-0.4,0 3.4,0;0,-0.4 0,2.4" vincoli="A,carrello;B,carrello,180" punti="2.6,0;0,1.5;1.3,0.75,M;0,0,O" etichette="2.1,0.2,ϑ" riferimento=0 titolo="Rod with its endpoints on the two guides, inclined at $\vartheta$ to the horizontal"}

:::soluzione
$\vec{\omega} = -\,\omega_0\,\hat{k}$, that is, $\omega_0$ in the clockwise sense; $C - O = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,\ell\;\hat{\imath} + \dfrac{\ell}{2}\;\hat{\jmath}$; $\vec{v}_M = -\dfrac{\ell\omega_0}{4}\;\hat{\imath} + \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4}\,\ell\omega_0\;\hat{\jmath}$, of magnitude $\dfrac{\ell\omega_0}{2}$.
:::
