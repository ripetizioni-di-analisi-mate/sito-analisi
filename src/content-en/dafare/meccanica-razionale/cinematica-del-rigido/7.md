---
titolo: Disk rolling inside a circular guide
teoria: [rotolamento-senza-strisciamento, centro-di-istantanea-rotazione, formula-fondamentale]
parole: [circular guide, rolling without slipping, hypocycloid, angular velocity, disk]
---

A homogeneous disk of radius $r$ and center $G$ rolls without slipping while staying in contact with the inner wall of a fixed circular guide of radius $R > r$ and center $O$. The position is located by the angle $\varphi$ that the radius $OG$ forms with the $x$ axis, and $\dot{\varphi} > 0$. Compute the velocity of the center $G$, the angular velocity of the disk and the angle through which the disk turns about itself while $G$ makes a complete turn about $O$.

:::soluzione
$|\vec{v}_G| = (R - r)\,\dot{\varphi}$, tangent to the circle described by $G$; $\vec{\omega} = -\dfrac{(R-r)\,\dot{\varphi}}{r}\;\hat{k}$, that is, in the clockwise sense; in one turn the disk turns by $\dfrac{R-r}{r}\,2\pi$.
:::
