titolo: Disk rolling on a moving belt

A homogeneous disk of radius $R$ and center $G$ rests on a horizontal belt that translates with velocity $\vec{u} = u\,\hat{\imath}$, and rolls on it without slipping. The center of the disk has velocity $\vec{v}_G = v\,\hat{\imath}$. Compute the angular velocity of the disk, the velocity of the point $T$ diametrically opposite to the contact and state for which value of $u$ the state of motion of the disk is translational.

:::soluzione
$\vec{\omega} = \dfrac{u - v}{R}\;\hat{k}$; $\vec{v}_T = (2v - u)\;\hat{\imath}$; the state of motion is translational when $u = v$, that is, when the disk is dragged by the belt without turning.
:::
