The system in the figure is formed by the rods $OA$ and $AB$, both of length $\ell$, hinged to each other at $A$; the rod $OA$ is hinged to the ground at $O$ and the endpoint $B$ runs on a slider along the horizontal guide passing through $O$. At the instant considered the rod $OA$ forms the angle $\vartheta = 60^\circ$ with the horizontal and $\dot{\vartheta} = \omega_0 > 0$. Compute the angular velocity of the rod $AB$, the velocity of $B$ and the position of the instantaneous center of rotation of $AB$.

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,1.1,1.9;B,2.2,0" aste="O-A;A-B" linee="-0.5,0 3,0" vincoli="O,cerniera;B,carrello" punti="0,0;1.1,1.9;2.2,0" etichette="0.55,0.25,ϑ" riferimento=0 titolo="Two equal rods hinged at $A$, with $B$ on a slider on the guide through $O$"}

:::soluzione
$\vec{\omega}_{AB} = -\,\omega_0\,\hat{k}$, that is, $\omega_0$ in the clockwise sense; $\vec{v}_B = -\sqrt{3}\,\ell\omega_0\;\hat{\imath}$, to the left; instantaneous center of rotation at $C - O = \ell\,\hat{\imath} + \sqrt{3}\,\ell\,\hat{\jmath}$.
:::
