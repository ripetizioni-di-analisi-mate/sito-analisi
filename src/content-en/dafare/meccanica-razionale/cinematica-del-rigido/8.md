The slider-crank system in the figure has the crank $OA$ of length $r$, hinged to the ground at $O$ and turning with constant angular velocity $\Omega\,\hat{k}$, and the connecting rod $AB$ of length $3r$, with $B$ on a slider on the horizontal guide passing through $O$. Compute the velocity of $B$ and the angular velocity of the connecting rod at the instant at which the crank is aligned with the guide and directed in the sense opposite to $B$, that is, $\vartheta = 180^\circ$.

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,-1.4,0;B,2.8,0" aste="O-A;A-B" linee="-2.2,0 3.6,0" vincoli="O,cerniera;B,carrello" punti="0,0;-1.4,0;2.8,0" quote="A,O,r,0.7;A,B,3r,-0.7" coppie="-0.7 0.7,1,Ω" titolo="Slider-crank at the dead center: $OA = r$ aligned with the guide, $AB = 3r$"}

:::soluzione
$\vec{v}_B = \vec{0}$; $\vec{\omega}_{AB} = \dfrac{\Omega}{3}\;\hat{k}$, that is, $\dfrac{\Omega}{3}$ in the counterclockwise sense.
:::
