---
titolo: Velocity and acceleration of a point of a rigid rod
teoria: [formula-fondamentale, campo-accelerazioni, accelerazioni-nel-piano]
parole: [fundamental formula, angular velocity, acceleration, rigid rod, plane motion]
---

A rigid rod $AB$ of length $\ell$ moves in the plane $Oxy$ and forms the angle of $45^\circ$ with the $x$ axis. At the instant considered the endpoint $A$ has velocity $\vec{v}_A = v\,\hat{\jmath}$ and zero acceleration, whereas the rod has constant angular velocity $\vec{\omega} = \omega\,\hat{k}$, with $v > 0$ and $\omega > 0$. Compute the velocity and the acceleration of the endpoint $B$.

:::soluzione
$\vec{v}_B = -\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\,\omega\ell\;\hat{\imath} + \left( v + \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\,\omega\ell \right)\hat{\jmath}$; $\vec{a}_B = -\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\,\omega^2\ell\,\big( \hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath} \big)$, directed from $B$ toward $A$ and of magnitude $\omega^2\ell$.
:::
