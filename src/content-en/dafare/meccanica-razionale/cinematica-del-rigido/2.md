---
titolo: Velocity field compatible with the rigidity constraint
teoria: [equiproiettivita, rigidita-differenziale, formula-fondamentale, centro-di-istantanea-rotazione]
parole: [equiprojectivity, rigidity constraint, angular velocity, instantaneous center of rotation, plane motion]
---

For a rigid plate that moves in the plane $Oxy$ the velocities of two points are known: $A = (0,\,0)\,\mathrm{m}$ has velocity $\vec{v}_A = (2,\,1)\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $B = (4,\,0)\,\mathrm{m}$ has velocity $\vec{v}_B = (2,\,-3)\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Verify that the data are compatible with a rigid motion, compute the angular velocity of the plate, the velocity of the point $C = (0,\,2)\,\mathrm{m}$ and the instantaneous center of rotation.

:::soluzione
Equiprojectivity is satisfied, because $(\vec{v}_B - \vec{v}_A) \cdot (B - A) = 0$; $\vec{\omega} = -1\,\hat{k}\;\mathrm{rad/s}$, that is, $1\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ in the clockwise sense; $\vec{v}_C = (4,\,1)\,\mathrm{m/s}$; instantaneous center of rotation at $(1,\,-2)\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
