The uniform rod $AB$, of mass $m$ and length $\ell$, has the end $A$ hinged to a collar that slides without friction on a fixed vertical guide; the rod rotates in the vertical plane about $A$. An ideal spring of stiffness $k$, arranged along the guide, connects the collar to the fixed point $O$ of the guide and is unloaded when the collar is at $O$. Determine the equilibrium configuration, study its stability and compute the natural angular frequencies of the small oscillations of the system in the figure.

:::soluzione
With $y$ the abscissa of $A$ along the guide, measured from $O$ upward, and $\vartheta$ the angle between the rod and the downward vertical, the equilibrium is at $\left( -\dfrac{m g}{k},\ 0 \right)$, stable. The two coordinates are already uncoupled, hence $\omega_1^2 = \dfrac{k}{m}$ for the translation of the collar and $\omega_2^2 = \dfrac{3g}{2\ell}$ for the rotation of the rod.
:::
