The uniform rod $AB$, of mass $m$ and length $\ell$, is supported in a vertical plane by two vertical ideal springs attached to its ends and to the ground: the one at $A$ has stiffness $k$, the one at $B$ has stiffness $2k$. The rod can translate vertically and rotate by small angles. Determine the equilibrium configuration and the natural angular frequencies of the small oscillations of the system in the figure.

::struttura{nodi="A,0.6,2;B,4.4,2" aste="A-B" linee="0.60,2.00 0.82,1.65 0.38,1.52 0.82,1.39 0.38,1.26 0.82,1.14 0.38,1.01 0.82,0.88 0.38,0.75 0.60,0.40;4.40,2.00 4.62,1.65 4.18,1.52 4.62,1.39 4.18,1.26 4.62,1.14 4.18,1.01 4.62,0.88 4.18,0.75 4.40,0.40;0,0.4 5,0.4" punti="2.5,2,G,baricentro" frecce="2.5,2.5,2.5,3.1,y" etichette="1,1.2,k;4.85,1.2,2k;2.9,1.75,ϑ" titolo="The rod resting on the two springs of stiffness $k$ and $2k$"}

:::soluzione
With $y$ the height of the center of mass measured from the configuration at which the springs are unloaded and $\vartheta$ the counterclockwise rotation, we obtain $y_0 = -\dfrac{3 m g}{8 k}$ and $\vartheta_0 = \dfrac{m g}{4 k \ell}$, stable equilibrium. Natural angular frequencies $\omega_1^2 = \dfrac{2\big(3 - \sqrt{3}\big)k}{m}$ and $\omega_2^2 = \dfrac{2\big(3 + \sqrt{3}\big)k}{m}$; the modes mix translation and rotation, because the springs are different.
:::
