Two uniform rods form a double pendulum in a vertical plane: the rod $OA$, of mass $2m$ and length $\ell$, is hinged at the fixed point $O$; the rod $AB$, of mass $m$ and length $\ell$, is hinged at the end $A$ of the first one. The hinges are smooth. Determine all the equilibrium configurations of the system in the figure and study their stability.

::struttura{nodi="O,0,4.4;A,1.37,2.5;B,3.58,1.71" aste="O-A;A-B" vincoli="O,cerniera,90" cerniere="A" tratteggi="0,4.4 0,2.6;1.37,2.5 1.37,1.1" punti="0.685,3.45,G_1,baricentro;2.47,2.11,G_2,baricentro" etichette="0.3,3.5,ϑ_1;1.65,1.7,ϑ_2;0.95,3.75,2m;2.6,2.45,m" titolo="The double pendulum formed by the rod $OA$ of mass $2m$ and by the rod $AB$ of mass $m$"}

:::soluzione
With $\vartheta_1$ and $\vartheta_2$ the angles of the two rods with respect to the downward vertical we obtain $V = -2 m g \ell \cos\vartheta_1 - \dfrac{m g \ell}{2}\cos\vartheta_2$, hence the equilibrium configurations are the four ones with $\vartheta_1, \vartheta_2 \in \{0, \pi\}$. The Hessian is diagonal with entries $2 m g \ell \cos\vartheta_1$ and $\dfrac{m g \ell}{2}\cos\vartheta_2$: the configuration $(0,0)$ is stable, the other three are unstable.
:::
