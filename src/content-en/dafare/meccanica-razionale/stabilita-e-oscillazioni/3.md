A uniform disk of mass $m$ and radius $R$ rolls without slipping on a plane inclined by the angle $\alpha$ to the horizontal. An ideal spring of stiffness $k$, parallel to the inclined plane, connects the center $C$ of the disk to a fixed point of the plane placed higher up; the spring is unloaded when $C$ is at the initial position. Determine the equilibrium configuration and the angular frequency of the small oscillations of the system in the figure.

::struttura{cerchi="2.087,1.826,0.7,foro" linee="0,0 5.4,2.7;0,0 5.4,0;5.4,0 5.4,2.7;2.09,1.83 2.24,2.15 2.69,1.88 2.74,2.40 3.19,2.13 3.24,2.65 3.69,2.38 3.74,2.90 4.09,2.83" punti="4.087,2.826,D;2.087,1.826,C,baricentro;2.4,1.2,H,centro" etichette="0.75,0.12,α;2.86,2.77,k" titolo="The disk rolling on the inclined plane, connected by the spring to the fixed point $D$"}

:::soluzione
With $s$ the abscissa of $C$ along the plane, positive downward and measured from the position at which the spring is unloaded, we obtain $s_0 = \dfrac{m g \sin\alpha}{k}$, stable equilibrium; $\omega = \sqrt{\dfrac{2k}{3m}}$, independent of the inclination.
:::
