Two point particles of mass $m$ slide without friction on a fixed horizontal guide. The first one is connected to the left wall by a spring of stiffness $k$, the second one to the right wall by an equal spring, and the two particles are connected to each other by a spring of stiffness $3k$. Determine the natural angular frequencies and the normal modes of the small oscillations of the system in the figure.

::struttura{poligoni="2,0 3,0 3,1 2,1;5,0 6,0 6,1 5,1" linee="0,0 8,0;0,0 0,1.6;8,0 8,1.6;0,0.5 0.4,0.75 0.9,0.25 1.4,0.75 1.9,0.5 2,0.5;3,0.5 3.4,0.8 3.9,0.2 4.4,0.8 4.9,0.2 5,0.5;6,0.5 6.4,0.75 6.9,0.25 7.4,0.75 7.9,0.5 8,0.5" etichette="1,1.15,k;4,1.2,3k;7,1.15,k;2.5,0.5,m;5.5,0.5,m" frecce="2.5,1.6,3.3,1.6,x_1;5.5,1.6,6.3,1.6,x_2" titolo="The two point particles with the springs $k$, $3k$ and $k$"}

:::soluzione
$\omega_1^2 = \dfrac{k}{m}$ with mode $u^{(1)} = (1, 1)$, in which the central spring stays unloaded; $\omega_2^2 = \dfrac{7k}{m}$ with mode $u^{(2)} = (1, -1)$.
:::
