The uniform rod $AB$, of mass $m$ and length $\ell$, is hinged at the fixed end $A$ and moves in a vertical plane. An ideal spring of stiffness $k$ and zero natural length connects the end $B$ to the fixed point $D$, placed at the same height as $A$ and at distance $\ell$ from it, as the diagram in the figure shows. Determine the equilibrium configurations, study their stability and compute the angular frequency of the small oscillations about the stable configuration.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,3;B,1.72,0.54;D,3,3" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera,90" linee="1.72,0.54 1.78,1.20 2.33,1.16 1.98,1.59 2.53,1.56 2.19,1.98 2.74,1.95 2.39,2.38 2.94,2.34 3.00,3.00" tratteggi="0,3 0,0.8;0,3 3,3" punti="0.86,1.77,G,baricentro" forze="0.86 1.77,0,-0.8,mg" etichette="0.4,2,ϑ;2.75,1.45,k" titolo="The rod hinged at $A$ with the spring between $B$ and the fixed point $D$"}

:::soluzione
With $\vartheta$ the angle between the rod and the downward vertical, the equilibrium equation is $\tan\vartheta = \dfrac{2 k \ell}{m g}$, hence $\vartheta_1 = \arctan\dfrac{2k\ell}{mg}$ is a stable equilibrium configuration and $\vartheta_2 = \vartheta_1 + \pi$ is an unstable one. Angular frequency of the small oscillations about $\vartheta_1$:
$$
\omega^2 = \frac{3\sqrt{m^2g^2 + 4k^2\ell^2}}{2\,m\,\ell} .
$$
:::
