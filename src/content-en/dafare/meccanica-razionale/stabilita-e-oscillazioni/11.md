A uniform disk of mass $m$ and radius $r$ rolls without slipping inside a fixed circular guide of radius $R > r$, placed in a vertical plane. Determine the stable equilibrium configuration and the period of the small oscillations of the system in the figure.

::struttura{cerchi="0,0,2.6,foro;0.9,-1.559,0.8,foro" punti="0,0,O;0.9,-1.559,C,baricentro;1.3,-2.252,H,centro" tratteggi="0,0 0,-2.6;0,0 1.3,-2.252" etichette="0.3,-1.5,ϑ" titolo="The disk of radius $r$ rolling inside the circular guide of radius $R$"}

:::soluzione
With $\vartheta$ the angle between the line $OC$ and the downward vertical, the configuration $\vartheta = 0$ is the only stable equilibrium configuration; $\omega = \sqrt{\dfrac{2g}{3\,(R - r)}}$ and $\mathcal{T} = 2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{3\,(R - r)}{2g}}$, equal to the period of a simple pendulum of length $\dfrac{3\,(R-r)}{2}$.
:::
