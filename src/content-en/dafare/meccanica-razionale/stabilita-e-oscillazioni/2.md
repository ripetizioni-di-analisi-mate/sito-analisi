A point particle $P$ of mass $m$ is constrained to a smooth fixed circular guide, of center $O$ and radius $R$, placed in a vertical plane. Besides the weight, the constant horizontal force $\vec{F} = F\,\hat{\imath}$, with $F > 0$, acts on it. Determine the equilibrium configurations and study their stability.

:::soluzione
With $\vartheta$ the angle between $OP$ and the downward vertical, the equilibrium equation is $\tan\vartheta = \dfrac{F}{m g}$, hence $\vartheta_1 = \arctan\dfrac{F}{m g}$ is a stable equilibrium configuration and $\vartheta_2 = \vartheta_1 + \pi$ is an unstable one. In the stable configuration the particle lies on the side toward which $\vec{F}$ pushes.
:::
