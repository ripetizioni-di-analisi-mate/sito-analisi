A uniform disk of mass $m$ and radius $R$ is hinged at a point $A$ of its rim and oscillates in a vertical plane about the horizontal axis through $A$. Determine the angular frequency and the period of the small oscillations about the stable equilibrium configuration, and the length of the simple pendulum that oscillates with the same period.

:::soluzione
$I_A = \dfrac{3}{2}\,m R^2$; $\omega = \sqrt{\dfrac{2g}{3R}}$; period $\mathcal{T} = 2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{3R}{2g}}$; reduced length $L = \dfrac{3R}{2}$.
:::
