A point particle of mass $m = 4\,\mathrm{kg}$ slides without friction on a horizontal guide, connected to a spring of stiffness $k = 36\,\mathrm{N/m}$ and to a viscous damper of constant $h = 12\,\mathrm{N\,s/m}$. Determine the critical damping, the pseudo angular frequency, the pseudo-period and the ratio between two successive maximum amplitudes.

:::soluzione
$h_c = 2\sqrt{k m} = 24\,\mathrm{N\,s/m}$, hence the motion is damped oscillatory; $\omega_0 = 3\,\mathrm{rad/s}$, $\beta = 1.5\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$, $\omega = \sqrt{6.75} \simeq 2.60\,\mathrm{rad/s}$, pseudo-period $\mathcal{T} \simeq 2.42\,\mathrm{s}$, logarithmic decrement $\delta = \beta\,\mathcal{T} \simeq 3.63$ and ratio between two successive amplitudes $e^{\delta} \simeq 37.6$.
:::
