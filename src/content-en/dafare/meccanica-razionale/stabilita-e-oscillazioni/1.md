A collar $P$ of mass $m$ slides without friction on a fixed straight guide, inclined by the angle $\alpha$ to the horizontal. An ideal spring of stiffness $k$ and natural length $\ell_0$ connects $P$ to the fixed point $O$ of the guide, placed higher up, as the diagram in the figure shows. Determine the equilibrium configuration, study its stability and compute the angular frequency of the small oscillations.

::struttura{nodi="O,3.4,1.7,-;P,1,0.5,-" linee="0,0 5,2.5;1.00,0.50 1.43,0.96 1.82,0.66 1.81,1.15 2.20,0.86 2.20,1.34 2.59,1.05 2.58,1.54 2.97,1.24 3.40,1.70" punti="3.4,1.7,O;1,0.5,P,pieno" forze="1 0.5,0,-0.8,mg" etichette="0.75,0.1,α;2.6,1.75,k" titolo="The collar $P$ on the inclined guide, pulled back by the spring toward the fixed point $O$"}

:::soluzione
With $s$ the distance of $P$ from $O$ along the guide, measured downward, we obtain $s_0 = \ell_0 + \dfrac{m g \sin\alpha}{k}$, stable equilibrium; $\omega = \sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m}}$, independent of the inclination.
:::
