The uniform rod $AB$, of mass $m$ and length $\ell$, is hinged at the fixed end $A$ and moves in a vertical plane. A point particle $P$ of mass $m$ slides without friction on the smooth horizontal guide through $A$, and is connected to the end $B$ by an ideal spring of stiffness $k$ and zero natural length. Determine the equilibrium configurations of the system in the figure and study their stability as $k$ varies.

::struttura{nodi="A,2.6,1.4;B,3.69,-1.61" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera,90" linee="1,1.4 6,1.4;3.69,-1.61 3.70,-0.86 4.26,-0.79 3.88,-0.38 4.44,-0.31 4.05,0.10 4.61,0.17 4.23,0.58 4.79,0.65 4.80,1.40" tratteggi="2.6,1.4 2.6,-0.9" punti="4.8,1.4,P,pieno" quote="A,4.8 1.4,x,-0.55" etichette="3,0.3,ϑ;4.75,-0.6,k" titolo="The rod hinged at $A$ and the spring connecting $B$ to the particle $P$ on the horizontal guide"}

:::soluzione
With $\vartheta$ the angle between the rod and the downward vertical and $x$ the abscissa of $P$ from $A$, for every $k$ the configurations $(\vartheta, x) = (0,0)$ and $(\pi, 0)$ are equilibrium configurations; if $2k\ell > m g$ the two oblique configurations with $\cos\vartheta = \dfrac{m g}{2 k \ell}$ and $x = \ell\sin\vartheta$ are equilibrium configurations as well. The configuration $(0,0)$ is stable for $2k\ell < m g$ and unstable for $2k\ell > m g$; the configuration $(\pi, 0)$ is always unstable; the two oblique ones, when they exist, are stable.
:::
