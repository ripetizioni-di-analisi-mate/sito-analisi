A uniform disk of mass $M$ and radius $R$ rotates without friction about its fixed center $O$, in a vertical plane. A point particle of mass $m$ is welded on the rim of the disk. Determine the equilibrium configurations of the system in the figure, study their stability and compute the period of the small oscillations about the stable configuration.

::struttura{cerchi="0,0,2,foro" punti="0,0,O,baricentro;1.41,-1.41,P,pieno" tratteggi="0,0 0,-2;0,0 1.41,-1.41" etichette="0.3,-1.15,ϑ;1.7,-1.65,m" titolo="The disk hinged at $O$ with the point particle welded at the point $P$ of the rim"}

:::soluzione
With $\vartheta$ the angle between $OP$ and the downward vertical, the configuration $\vartheta = 0$ is a stable equilibrium configuration, whereas $\vartheta = \pi$ is an unstable one; $\omega = \sqrt{\dfrac{2 m g}{R\,(M + 2m)}}$ and $\mathcal{T} = 2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{R\,(M + 2m)}{2 m g}}$.
:::
