Two equal uniform disks, of mass $m$ and radius $R$, roll without slipping on a fixed horizontal guide. The center of the first one is connected to the left wall by a spring of stiffness $k$, the center of the second one to the right wall by an equal spring, and the two centers are connected to each other by a third spring of stiffness $k$. Determine the natural angular frequencies and the normal modes of the small oscillations of the system in the figure.

:::soluzione
With $x_1$ and $x_2$ the abscissas of the two centers measured from the equilibrium positions, we obtain $\omega_1^2 = \dfrac{2k}{3m}$ with mode $u^{(1)} = (1,1)$, in which the central spring stays unloaded, and $\omega_2^2 = \dfrac{2k}{m}$ with mode $u^{(2)} = (1,-1)$.
:::
