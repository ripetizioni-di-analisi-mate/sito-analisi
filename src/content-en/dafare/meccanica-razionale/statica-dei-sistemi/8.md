Two homogeneous rods $AB$ and $BC$, each of mass $m$ and length $\ell$, are hinged to each other at $B$; the end $A$ is hinged to the ground and the end $C$ is constrained by a roller support that slides on a smooth horizontal guide passing through $A$. An ideal spring of constant $k$ and zero natural length connects the roller support $C$ to the fixed point $A$, as in the diagram in the figure. Denoting by $\vartheta$ the angle that each rod makes with the horizontal, determine the equilibrium configurations with $\vartheta \in \left(0, \dfrac{\pi}{2}\right]$ and the internal reaction at $B$.

:::soluzione
The equilibrium configurations are $\vartheta = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$ and, if $m g \le 4\,k\,\ell$, the one with $\sin\vartheta = \dfrac{m g}{4\,k\,\ell}$. In the second the internal reaction is horizontal: $X_B = 2\,k\,\ell\cos\vartheta$ and $Y_B = 0$; the reaction of the roller support equals $\Phi_C = m g$.
:::
