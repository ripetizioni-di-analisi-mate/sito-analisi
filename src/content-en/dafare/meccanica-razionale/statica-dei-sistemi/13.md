Two homogeneous rods $AB$ and $BC$, each of mass $m$ and length $\ell$, are hinged to each other at $B$ and hinged to the ground at $A$ and at $C$, in a vertical plane; the points $A$ and $C$ lie at the same height and each rod makes the angle $\vartheta = 60^\circ$ with the horizontal, as in the diagram in the figure. All the hinges are smooth. Determine the reactions of the two hinges to the ground and the internal reaction at $B$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,1.5,2.6;C,3,0" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,cerniera" cerniere="B" forze="0.75 1.3,0,-0.9,mg;2.25 1.3,0,-0.9,mg" etichette="0.5,0.33,ϑ" titolo="Arch formed by two rods hinged to each other at $B$ and to the ground at $A$ and at $C$"}

:::soluzione
$\vec{\Phi}_A = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{6}\,m g\,\hat{\imath} + m g\,\hat{\jmath}$ and $\vec{\Phi}_C = -\,\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{6}\,m g\,\hat{\imath} + m g\,\hat{\jmath}$. The internal reaction is horizontal: $X_B = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{6}\,m g$ and $Y_B = 0$.
:::
