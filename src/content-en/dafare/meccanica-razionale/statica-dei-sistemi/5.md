A homogeneous rod $AB$ of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ is hinged at $A$ to a fixed point, in a vertical plane, with a smooth hinge. An ideal spring of constant $k$ and zero natural length connects the end $B$ to the fixed point $D$, placed on the horizontal through $A$ at distance $\ell$ from $A$, as in the diagram in the figure. Denoting by $\vartheta$ the angle that the rod makes with the horizontal, measured below it, determine the equilibrium configuration and the reaction of the hinge.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2.25,-2.68;D,3.5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera,90" tratteggi="0,0 4.1,0" linee="2.25,-2.68 2.48,-2.20 2.75,-1.98 2.60,-1.56 3.02,-1.41 2.86,-0.99 3.29,-0.84 3.27,-0.48 3.50,0.00" forze="1.13 -1.34,0,-1,mg" quote="A,D,ℓ,-0.9" etichette="0.72,-0.5,ϑ;3.5,-1.5,k" titolo="Rod hinged at $A$ with the spring between $B$ and the fixed point $D$"}

:::soluzione
$\tan\vartheta = \dfrac{m g}{2\,k\,\ell}$. The reaction of the hinge has components $\Phi_{Ax} = -\,k\,\ell\,(1 - \cos\vartheta)$, directed toward the left, and $\Phi_{Ay} = m g - k\,\ell\sin\vartheta$.
:::
