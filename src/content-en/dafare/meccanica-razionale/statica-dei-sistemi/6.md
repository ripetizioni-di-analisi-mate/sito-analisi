A homogeneous rod $AB$ of mass $m = 6\,\mathrm{kg}$ and length $\ell$ is hinged at $A$ to a vertical wall, in a vertical plane, and makes the angle $\alpha = 45^\circ$ with the horizontal. The end $B$ is connected to the wall by an ideal horizontal string, as in the diagram in the figure. Determine the tension of the string and the reaction of the hinge.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3.54,3.54;C,0,3.54" aste="A-B" catene="B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera,180" linee="0,-0.7 0,4.3" tratteggi="0,0 1.7,0" forze="1.77 1.77,0,-1.1,mg" etichette="0.82,0.26,α" titolo="Rod hinged to the wall at $A$ and held back by the horizontal string $BC$"}

:::soluzione
$T = \dfrac{m g}{2\tan\alpha} = 29.43\,\mathrm{N}$; $\Phi_{Ax} = 29.43\,\mathrm{N}$ toward the right and $\Phi_{Ay} = 58.86\,\mathrm{N}$ upward.
:::
