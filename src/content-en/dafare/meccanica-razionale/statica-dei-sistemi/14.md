A homogeneous disk of mass $m$ and radius $R$ rolls without slipping on a fixed horizontal guide, in a vertical plane; the coefficient of static friction at the contact equals $f$. A particle of mass $m_1$ is welded to the rim of the disk. At the highest point of the disk the horizontal force $\vec{F} = F\,\hat{\imath}$ is applied, with $F > 0$, as in the diagram in the figure. Denoting by $\varphi$ the angle, measured clockwise, by which the disk has rotated starting from the configuration in which the welded particle touches the guide, determine the equilibrium configurations, the reaction of the contact and the conditions for the equilibrium to be possible.

::struttura{linee="-0.6,0 8.2,0" cerchi="4,1.5,1.5" tratteggi="4,1.5 3.14,0.27;4,1.5 4,0" punti="4,1.5,O,baricentro;4,0,C,centro;3.14,0.27,P;4,3,H" forze="4 3,1.2,0,F;4 1.5,0,-0.85,mg;3.14 0.27,0,-0.8,m_1g" etichette="3.85,0.72,φ" titolo="Disk rolling without slipping with the point $P$ welded to the rim and the force $\vec{F}$ applied at the highest point $H$"}

:::soluzione
$\sin\varphi = \dfrac{2 F}{m_1 g}$, with the two solutions $\varphi_1$ and $\pi - \varphi_1$. The contact exerts $N = (m + m_1)\,g$ upward and a friction component of magnitude $F$ directed opposite to $\vec{F}$. The equilibrium requires $2 F \le m_1 g$ and $F \le f\,(m + m_1)\,g$.
:::
