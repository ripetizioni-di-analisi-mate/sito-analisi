A homogeneous rod $AB$ of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ rests in a vertical plane with the end $A$ on a rough horizontal floor, with coefficient of static friction $f = 0.3$, and with the end $B$ on a smooth vertical wall; the rod makes the angle $\vartheta$ with the horizontal, as in the diagram in the figure. Determine the reactions of the two supports and the minimum angle for which the equilibrium is possible.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2.3,4.4" aste="A-B" linee="-0.9,0 3.4,0;2.3,0 2.3,5" forze="1.15 2.2,0,-1.2,mg" etichette="0.75,0.42,ϑ" titolo="Rod resting on the rough floor at $A$ and on the smooth wall at $B$"}

:::soluzione
$N_A = m g$ upward; $N_B = T_A = \dfrac{m g}{2\tan\vartheta}$. The equilibrium requires $\tan\vartheta \ge \dfrac{1}{2 f}$, hence $\vartheta_{\min} = 59.04^\circ$.
:::
