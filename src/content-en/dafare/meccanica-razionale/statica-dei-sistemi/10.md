A particle $P$ of mass $m$ rests on the concave side of a smooth circular guide of radius $R$ and center $O$, placed in a vertical plane and limited to the arc $|\vartheta| \le \dfrac{\pi}{4}$, where $\vartheta$ is the angle measured from the downward vertical through $O$ counterclockwise. The constraint is unilateral and can only push $P$ toward the center. The horizontal force $\vec{F}_1 = F_1\,\hat{\imath}$ and the vertical force $\vec{F}_2 = F_2\,\hat{\jmath}$ are applied to the particle, as in the diagram in the figure. Determine the equilibrium configuration, the reaction of the guide and the conditions on $F_1$ and $F_2$ for the equilibrium to be possible.

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 curve="2*sin(t),-2*cos(t),-0.785,0.785" segmenti="0,0,0,-2;0,0,1,-1.73;0,0,1.41,-1.41" vettori="1,-1.73,1,-2.83,mg;1,-1.73,2.2,-1.73,F₁;1,-1.73,1,-0.63,F₂" punti="1,-1.73,pieno,P;0,0,pieno,O" etichette="0.28,-1.3,ϑ;0.85,-0.3,45°" xmin=-2.4 xmax=2.9 ymin=-3.2 ymax=0.8 titolo="Particle resting on the concave side of a circular guide limited to the arc $|\vartheta| \le \dfrac{\pi}{4}$"}

:::soluzione
$\tan\vartheta = \dfrac{F_1}{m g - F_2}$ and the reaction, directed toward the center, equals $\Phi = \sqrt{F_1^2 + (m g - F_2)^2}$. The equilibrium is possible if $F_2 < m g$ and $F_1 \le m g - F_2$.
:::
