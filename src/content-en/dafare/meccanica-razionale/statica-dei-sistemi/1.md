A particle $P$ of mass $m = 3\,\mathrm{kg}$ is constrained to a smooth fixed straight guide, placed in a vertical plane and inclined at $\alpha = 45^\circ$ to the horizontal. An ideal spring of constant $k = 150\,\mathrm{N/m}$ and zero natural length connects $P$ to the upper end $O$ of the guide, as in the diagram in the figure. Determine the distance $s$ of $P$ from $O$ at equilibrium and the reaction of the guide, in symbolic form and then with the assigned values.

::struttura{linee="0,3.2 3.2,0;0.00,3.20 0.28,2.92 0.30,2.61 0.75,2.73 0.64,2.28 1.09,2.40 0.97,1.95 1.28,1.92 1.56,1.64" tratteggi="2.0,0 3.2,0" punti="0,3.2,O;1.56,1.64,P" forze="1.56 1.64,0,-1,mg" quote="0 3.2,1.56 1.64,s,-1.1" etichette="0.95,2.6,k;2.75,0.17,α" titolo="Particle on a smooth straight guide inclined at $\alpha$, connected to $O$ by the spring"}

:::soluzione
$s = \dfrac{m g \sin\alpha}{k} = 0.14\,\mathrm{m}$; the reaction is orthogonal to the guide and equals $\Phi = m g \cos\alpha = 20.81\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
