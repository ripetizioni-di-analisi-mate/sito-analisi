A ring $P$ of mass $m$, which can be treated as a particle, is threaded on a smooth fixed circular guide of radius $R$ and center $O$, placed in a vertical plane. A constant horizontal force $\vec{F} = F\,\hat{\imath}$ is applied to the ring, with $F > 0$, as in the diagram in the figure. Denoting by $\vartheta$ the angle that $OP$ makes with the downward vertical, measured counterclockwise, determine the equilibrium configurations and the reaction of the guide in each of them.

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 curve="2*cos(t),2*sin(t),0,6.29" segmenti="0,0,1.29,-1.53;0,0,0,-2" vettori="1.29,-1.53,1.29,-2.63,mg;1.29,-1.53,2.49,-1.53,F" punti="1.29,-1.53,pieno,P;0,0,pieno,O" etichette="0.35,-1.15,ϑ" xmin=-2.6 xmax=3.3 ymin=-3 ymax=2.4 titolo="Ring on a smooth circular guide subject to the weight and to a constant horizontal force"}

:::soluzione
$\tan\vartheta = \dfrac{F}{m g}$, hence $\vartheta_1 = \arctan\dfrac{F}{m g}$ and $\vartheta_2 = \vartheta_1 + \pi$. In both the reaction has magnitude $\sqrt{m^2 g^2 + F^2}$, directed toward the center at $\vartheta_1$ and outward at $\vartheta_2$.
:::
