A homogeneous disk of mass $m$ and radius $R$ can rotate without friction about its own center $O$, fixed to a horizontal axis, in a vertical plane. An ideal string wound around the rim of the disk hangs vertically from the right side and supports a particle of mass $m_1$. A couple of moment $\mathcal{M}$ is applied to the disk, counterclockwise, as in the diagram in the figure. Determine the value of $\mathcal{M}$ that keeps the system in equilibrium and the reaction of the axis.

::struttura{cerchi="0,0,2" nodi="O,0,0;Q,2,-3.2,-" catene="2 0-Q" vincoli="O,cerniera" coppie="-1 0.8,1,ℳ" punti="2,0;2,-3.2" forze="O,0,-1.4,mg;Q,0,-1.1,m_1g" titolo="Disk with a fixed axis at $O$, string wound around the rim and couple $\mathcal{M}$ applied"}

:::soluzione
$\mathcal{M} = m_1 g R$, counterclockwise; the reaction of the axis equals $\vec{\Phi}_O = (m + m_1)\,g\,\hat{\jmath}$, directed upward.
:::
