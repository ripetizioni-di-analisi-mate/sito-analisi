Two particles $P_1$ and $P_2$, of masses $m_1 = 6\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $m_2$, are connected by an ideal string that passes over an ideal pulley placed at the vertex of a plane inclined at $\alpha = 30^\circ$ to the horizontal. The particle $P_1$ lies on the inclined plane, rough with coefficient of static friction $f = 0.25$, while $P_2$ hangs vertically, as in the diagram in the figure. Determine the range of values of $m_2$ for which the system is in equilibrium.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 6,3.46 6,0 0,0" nodi="K,6,3.46,-;P,3.4,1.96,-;Q,6.22,1.5,-" catene="P-K;K-Q" cerchi="6,3.46,0.22" punti="3.4,1.96,P_1;6.22,1.5,P_2" forze="3.4 1.96,0,-1,m_1g;6.22 1.5,0,-1,m_2g" etichette="0.95,0.2,α" titolo="Two particles connected by a string that passes over the pulley at the vertex of the inclined plane"}

:::soluzione
$m_1\,(\sin\alpha - f\cos\alpha) \le m_2 \le m_1\,(\sin\alpha + f\cos\alpha)$, that is, $1.70\,\mathrm{kg} \le m_2 \le 4.30\,\mathrm{kg}$.
:::
