A homogeneous rod $AB$ of mass $m$ and length $4a$ is placed horizontally in a vertical plane and rests on two smooth unilateral supports, one at $A$ and one at the point $C$ with $AC = 3a$. At the end $B$ a particle of mass $m_1$ is hung with a vertical string, as in the diagram in the figure. Determine the reactions of the two supports and the value of $m_1$ beyond which the rod lifts at $A$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4.5,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello;C,carrello" forze="3 0,0,-1.1,mg;B,0,-1.3,m_1g" quote="A,C,3a,1.3;C,B,a,1.3" titolo="Rod resting at $A$ and at $C$, with the weight $m_1 g$ hung at $B$"}

:::soluzione
$\Phi_A = \dfrac{g\,(m - m_1)}{3}$ and $\Phi_C = \dfrac{2\,g\,(m + 2\,m_1)}{3}$, both upward. The rod lifts at $A$ for $m_1 > m$.
:::
