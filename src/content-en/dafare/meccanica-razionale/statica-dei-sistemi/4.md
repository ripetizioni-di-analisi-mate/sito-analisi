A homogeneous rod $OA$ of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ is hinged at $O$ to a fixed point, in a vertical plane, with a smooth hinge. At the end $A$ a particle of mass $m_1$ is welded and at $A$ the horizontal force $\vec{F} = F\,\hat{\imath}$ also acts, with $F > 0$, as in the diagram in the figure. Denoting by $\vartheta$ the angle that $OA$ makes with the downward vertical, measured counterclockwise, determine the equilibrium configurations and the reaction of the hinge.

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,2.12,-2.12" aste="O-A" vincoli="O,cerniera,90" tratteggi="0,0 0,-3.2" punti="2.12,-2.12" forze="A,1.1,0,F;A,0,-1,m_1g;1.06 -1.06,0,-1,mg" etichette="0.5,-1.2,ϑ" titolo="Rod hinged at $O$ with the particle of mass $m_1$ welded at $A$ and the force $\vec{F}$"}

:::soluzione
$\tan\vartheta = \dfrac{2 F}{(m + 2\,m_1)\,g}$, with the two solutions $\vartheta_1$ and $\vartheta_1 + \pi$. The reaction of the hinge equals $\vec{\Phi}_O = -\,F\,\hat{\imath} + (m + m_1)\,g\,\hat{\jmath}$ in both configurations.
:::
