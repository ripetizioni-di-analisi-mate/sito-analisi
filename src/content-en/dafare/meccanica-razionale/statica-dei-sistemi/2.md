A particle $P$ of mass $m = 5\,\mathrm{kg}$ is suspended in a vertical plane from two ideal strings $PA$ and $PB$, fixed to two points $A$ and $B$ placed at the same height, as in the diagram in the figure. The string $PA$ makes the angle $\alpha = 30^\circ$ with the horizontal and the string $PB$ the angle $\beta = 45^\circ$. Determine the tensions of the two strings and say which of the two is more stressed.

::struttura{nodi="A,-3.2,3;B,5,3;P,2,0" catene="A-P;B-P" linee="-3.9,3 5.7,3" tratteggi="0.4,0 3.6,0" forze="P,0,-1.1,mg" etichette="1.1,0.22,α;2.55,0.3,β" titolo="Particle suspended from two strings that make the angles $\alpha$ and $\beta$ with the horizontal"}

:::soluzione
$T_A = \dfrac{m g \cos\beta}{\sin(\alpha + \beta)} = 35.91\,\mathrm{N}$; $T_B = \dfrac{m g \cos\alpha}{\sin(\alpha + \beta)} = 43.98\,\mathrm{N}$. The string $PB$, more steeply inclined to the horizontal, is the more stressed one.
:::
