In the plane $Oxy$ four vectors parallel to $\hat{\jmath}$ are given, with coefficients $F$, $3F$, $2F$ and $2F$, applied respectively at the points $P_1 = O$, $P_2$, $P_3$ and $P_4$ of the rectangle in the figure, with $P_2 - O = 2a\,\hat{\imath}$, $P_3 - O = 2a\,\hat{\imath} + 3a\,\hat{\jmath}$ and $P_4 - O = 3a\,\hat{\jmath}$. Determine the resultant and the center of the system.

::struttura{tratteggi="0,0 2,0 2,3 0,3 0,0" punti="0,0;2,0;2,3;0,3;1.25,1.5,C,centro" forze="0 0.5,0,0.5,;2 1.5,0,1.5,;2 4,0,1,;0 4,0,1," etichette="0.2,0.52,F;2.2,1.52,3F;2.2,4.02,2F;0.2,4.02,2F;-0.5,-0.3,P_1;2.2,-0.3,P_2;2.25,2.7,P_3;-0.55,2.7,P_4" titolo="The four parallel vectors applied at the vertices of the rectangle"}

:::soluzione
$\vec{R} = 8F\,\hat{\jmath}$; the center is the point $C$ with $C - O = \dfrac{5a}{4}\,\hat{\imath} + \dfrac{3a}{2}\,\hat{\jmath}$.
:::
