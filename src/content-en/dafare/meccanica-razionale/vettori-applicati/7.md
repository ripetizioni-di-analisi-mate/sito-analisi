The applied vectors $(O, F\,\hat{\imath})$ and $(P, 2F\,\hat{\jmath})$ with $P - O = 2a\,\hat{k}$ are given, as the diagram in the figure shows. Compute the resultant, the resultant moment about $O$ and the scalar invariant, and determine the central axis and the minimum moment.

::struttura{frecce="0,0,-1.1,-0.792,x;0,0,2.2,0,y;0,0,0,2.2,z" punti="0,0;0,1.6,P" forze="-0.5 -0.36,-0.5,-0.36,;1.4 1.6,1.4,0," etichette="0.2,-0.3,O;-0.8,-0.22,F;1.6,1.66,2F" riferimento=0 titolo="The two applied vectors, with skew lines of action"}

:::soluzione
$\vec{R} = F\,(\hat{\imath} + 2\hat{\jmath})$; $\vec{M}_O = -\,4\,a F\,\hat{\imath}$; $I = -\,4\,a F^2$; the central axis is the line through the point $Q$ with $Q - O = \dfrac{8a}{5}\,\hat{k}$ parallel to $\hat{\imath} + 2\hat{\jmath}$, and the minimum moment equals $\vec{M}_Q = -\dfrac{4\,a F}{5}\,(\hat{\imath} + 2\hat{\jmath})$, of magnitude $\dfrac{4\sqrt{5}}{5}\,a F$.
:::
