The rigid rod $AB$ in the figure, of length $\ell$ and placed along the $x$ axis with $A$ at the origin, carries the distributed load of intensity $q(s) = q_0\,\dfrac{s^2}{\ell^2}$ for $0 \leq s \leq \ell$, directed along $-\hat{\jmath}$. Determine the resultant and the abscissa of its line of action.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 0,0 0.25,0.003 0.5,0.01 0.75,0.023 1,0.042 1.25,0.065 1.5,0.094 1.75,0.128 2,0.167 2.25,0.211 2.5,0.26 2.75,0.315 3,0.375 3.25,0.44 3.5,0.51 3.75,0.586 4,0.667 4.25,0.753 4.5,0.844 4.75,0.94 5,1.042 5.25,1.148 5.5,1.26 5.75,1.378 6,1.5 6,0" nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" etichette="5.1,1.62,q_0" riferimento=0 titolo="The parabolic load, zero at $A$ and of intensity $q_0$ at $B$"}

:::soluzione
$R = \dfrac{q_0\,\ell}{3}$ directed along $-\hat{\jmath}$, with line of action through the point of abscissa $s_C = \dfrac{3\ell}{4}$.
:::
