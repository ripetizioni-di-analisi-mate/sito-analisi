The rigid rod $AB$ in the figure, of length $2\ell$ and placed along the $x$ axis with $A$ at the origin, carries a uniform load of intensity $q_0$ on the segment $0 \leq s \leq \ell$ and a triangular load growing linearly from $0$ at $s = \ell$ to $2q_0$ at $s = 2\ell$, both directed along $-\hat{\jmath}$. Determine the resultant of the overall load and the abscissa of the point through which its line of action passes.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" carichi="A,3 0,0.6,0.6,q_0;3 0,B,0,1.2,2q_0" quote="A,3 0,ℓ,1.6;3 0,B,ℓ,1.6" riferimento=0 titolo="The uniform load on the first half and the triangular load on the second"}

:::soluzione
$R = 2\,q_0\,\ell$ directed along $-\hat{\jmath}$, with line of action through the point of abscissa $s_C = \dfrac{13\ell}{12}$.
:::
