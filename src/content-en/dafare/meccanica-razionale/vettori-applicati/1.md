In the plane $Oxy$ the applied vector $(P, \vec{v})$ is given, with $P - O = a\,(\hat{\imath} + 3\hat{\jmath})$ and $\vec{v} = v\,(2\hat{\imath} - \hat{\jmath})$, with $a > 0$ and $v > 0$. Compute the moment about $O$, the arm of the line of action with respect to $O$ and the moment about the pole $O'$ with $O' - O = a\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath})$.

:::soluzione
$\vec{M}_O = -\,7\,a v\,\hat{k}$, therefore the rotation tends to be clockwise; $b = \dfrac{7\sqrt{5}}{5}\,a$; $\vec{M}_{O'} = -\,4\,a v\,\hat{k}$.
:::
