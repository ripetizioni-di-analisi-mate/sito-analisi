On the rigid rod $AB$ in the figure, of length $4\ell$ and placed along the $x$ axis with $A$ at the origin, there act the vector $-\,2F\,\hat{\jmath}$ at the point of abscissa $\ell$, the vector $F\,\hat{\jmath}$ at the point of abscissa $3\ell$ and a couple of moment $\mathcal{M} = -\,2F\ell\,\hat{k}$. Reduce the system to a single applied vector and determine the abscissa of the point at which its line of action meets the rod.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" forze="1 0,0,-1.6,2F;3 1,0,1," etichette="3.22,1.02,F" coppie="3.6 0,-1,ℳ" quote="A,1 0,ℓ,1.3;1 0,3 0,2ℓ,1.3;3 0,B,ℓ,1.3" titolo="The rod with the two vertical vectors and the clockwise couple"}

:::soluzione
$\vec{R} = -F\,\hat{\jmath}$; $M_A = -F\ell$; the system is equivalent to the vector $-F\,\hat{\jmath}$ alone applied at the point of abscissa $\ell$.
:::
