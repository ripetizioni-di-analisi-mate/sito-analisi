The applied vectors $(O, F\,\hat{\imath})$, $(A, \lambda F\,\hat{\imath})$ with $A - O = a\,\hat{\jmath}$ and $(B, F\,\hat{\jmath})$ with $B - O = a\,\hat{k}$ are given, where $\lambda$ is a real parameter, as the diagram in the figure shows. Determine for which value of $\lambda$ the system is equivalent to a single applied vector and write the central axis in that case.

::struttura{frecce="0,0,-1.1,-0.792,x;0,0,2.2,0,y;0,0,0,2.2,z" punti="0,0;1.4,0,A;0,1.4,B" forze="-0.55 -0.396,-0.55,-0.396,;0.85 -0.396,-0.55,-0.396,;1.1 1.4,1.1,0," etichette="0.2,-0.3,O;-0.85,-0.24,F;0.58,-0.26,λF;1.3,1.46,F" riferimento=0 titolo="The three applied vectors, with the coefficient $\lambda$ of the second one as parameter"}

:::soluzione
$\vec{R} = F\,\big[ (1 + \lambda)\,\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath} \big]$, $\vec{M}_O = -\,a F\,(\hat{\imath} + \lambda\,\hat{k})$ and $I = -\,a F^2\,(1 + \lambda)$, therefore $\lambda = -\,1$. For that value $\vec{R} = F\,\hat{\jmath}$ and the central axis is the line through $Q$ with $Q - O = a\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{k})$ parallel to $\hat{\jmath}$.
:::
