On the rigid rectangular plate in the figure, with sides $3a$ and $2a$, with $O$ at one vertex and the axes along the sides, there act $\vec{v}_1 = 2F\,\hat{\imath}$ at $O$, $\vec{v}_2 = -F\,\hat{\jmath}$ at the point $B$ of coordinates $(3a, 0)$ and $\vec{v}_3 = F\,(-\hat{\imath} + 2\hat{\jmath})$ at the point $C$ of coordinates $(3a, 2a)$. Determine the resultant, the resultant moment about $O$, the line of action of the resultant and the distance of this line from $O$.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 3,0 3,2 0,2" nodi="O,0,0;B,3,0;C,3,2;E,0,2" forze="O,1.1,0,v_1;B,0,-1,v_2;C,-0.55,1.1,v_3" quote="O,B,3a,-3.2;B,C,2a,1" titolo="The plate and the three applied vectors"}

:::soluzione
$\vec{R} = F\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath})$; $M_O = 5\,a F$; line of action $x - y = 5a$; distance from $O$ equal to $\dfrac{5\sqrt{2}}{2}\,a$.
:::
