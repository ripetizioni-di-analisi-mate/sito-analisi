---
titolo: Axial moment about a coordinate axis and about the diagonal of a cube
teoria: [momento-polare, momento-assiale, momento-assiale-indipendente]
parole: [axial moment, scalar triple product, diagonal of the cube, coordinate axis]
---

The applied vector $(P, \vec{v})$ has $P - O = a\,(\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath} + \hat{k})$ and $\vec{v} = F\,(2\hat{\imath} - \hat{\jmath} - \hat{k})$, with $a > 0$ and $F > 0$. Compute the moment about $O$, the axial moment about the $z$ axis and the axial moment about the line $r$ through $O$ with unit vector $\hat{u} = \dfrac{\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath} + \hat{k}}{\sqrt{3}}$, explaining the value obtained.

:::soluzione
$\vec{M}_O = 3\,a F\,(\hat{\jmath} - \hat{k})$; $M_z = -\,3\,a F$; $M_r = 0$, because the point $P$ belongs to $r$ and therefore the line of action and the axis are coplanar.
:::
