The rigid bracket in the figure is formed by the vertical post $AB$ of length $3\ell$ and by the horizontal arm $BC$ of length $2\ell$, with $A$ at the origin. At $C$ the vector $\vec{v} = F\,(\hat{\imath} - 2\hat{\jmath})$ acts. Reduce the system at $A$, determining the vector applied at $A$ and the transfer couple, and then write the line of action of the single equivalent vector and its distance from $A$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;C,2,3" aste="A-B;B-C" forze="C,0.7,-1.4,v" quote="A,B,3ℓ,0.9;B,C,2ℓ,0.9" titolo="The bracket with the vector applied at $C$"}

:::soluzione
$\vec{R} = F\,(\hat{\imath} - 2\hat{\jmath})$ applied at $A$, with transfer couple $\mathcal{\vec{M}}_t = -\,7F\ell\,\hat{k}$; line of action $2x + y = 7\ell$, at distance $\dfrac{7\sqrt{5}}{5}\,\ell$ from $A$.
:::
