---
titolo: Composition of two couples in space
teoria: [coppia, momento-di-una-coppia, riduzione, cambiamento-di-polo]
parole: [couple, composition of couples, moment of a couple, arm, space]
---

On a rigid body two couples act. The first is formed by the applied vectors $(A, F\,\hat{k})$ and $(B, -F\,\hat{k})$ with $B - A = 2a\,\hat{\imath}$; the second is formed by $(C, 2F\,\hat{\imath})$ and $(D, -\,2F\,\hat{\imath})$ with $D - C = a\,\hat{\jmath}$. Determine the moment of each couple, the moment of the overall system and its magnitude.

:::soluzione
$\vec{M}_1 = 2\,a F\,\hat{\jmath}$; $\vec{M}_2 = 2\,a F\,\hat{k}$; the system is equivalent to a single couple of moment $2\,a F\,(\hat{\jmath} + \hat{k})$, of magnitude $2\sqrt{2}\,a F$, the same about every pole.
:::
