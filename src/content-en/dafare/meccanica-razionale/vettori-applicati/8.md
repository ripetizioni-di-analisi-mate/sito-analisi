The applied vectors $(O, F\,\hat{\imath})$, $(A, F\,\hat{\jmath})$ with $A - O = a\,\hat{\imath}$ and $(B, F\,\hat{\imath})$ with $B - O = a\,\hat{k}$ are given, as the diagram in the figure shows. Determine the resultant, the resultant moment about $O$, the scalar invariant, the central axis and the minimum moment.

::struttura{frecce="0,0,-1.1,-0.792,x;0,0,2.2,0,y;0,0,0,2.2,z" punti="0,0;-0.7,-0.504,A;0,1.4,B" forze="-0.55 -0.396,-0.55,-0.396,;0.4 -0.504,1.1,0,;-0.55 1.004,-0.55,-0.396," etichette="0.2,-0.3,O;-0.85,-0.24,F;0.55,-0.92,F;-0.9,1.12,F" riferimento=0 titolo="The three vectors applied at $O$, $A$ and $B$"}

:::soluzione
$\vec{R} = F\,(2\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath})$; $\vec{M}_O = a F\,(\hat{\jmath} + \hat{k})$; $I = a F^2$; the central axis is the line through $Q$ with $Q - O = \dfrac{a}{5}\,(\hat{\imath} - 2\hat{\jmath} + 2\hat{k})$ parallel to $2\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath}$, and the minimum moment equals $\vec{M}_Q = \dfrac{a F}{5}\,(2\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath})$, of magnitude $\dfrac{\sqrt{5}}{5}\,a F$.
:::
