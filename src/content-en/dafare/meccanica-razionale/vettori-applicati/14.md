In the plane $Oxy$ the applied vectors $(O, 2F\,\hat{\imath})$ and $(D, -\,3F\,\hat{\jmath})$ act on a rigid body, with $D - O = 2a\,\hat{\imath}$, as the diagram in the figure shows. Determine the vector applied at the point $B$ with $B - O = 4a\,\hat{\imath}$ and the couple of moment $\mathcal{M}\,\hat{k}$ that make the overall system balanced.

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;D,2,0;B,4,0" aste="O-B" forze="O,1.1,0,2F;D,0,-1.6,3F" quote="O,D,2a,1.2;D,B,2a,1.2" titolo="The two known vectors; the unknown vector is sought at $B$"}

:::soluzione
$\vec{v}_B = -\,2F\,\hat{\imath} + 3F\,\hat{\jmath}$ and $\mathcal{M} = -\,6\,a F$, that is a clockwise couple of magnitude $6\,a F$.
:::
