In the plane $Oxy$ three vectors parallel to $\hat{\jmath}$ are given, with coefficients $2F$, $-F$ and $\mu F$ where $\mu$ is a real parameter, applied at the points $P_1 = O$, $P_2$ and $P_3$ with $P_2 - O = 2a\,\hat{\imath}$ and $P_3 - O = 5a\,\hat{\imath}$, as the diagram in the figure shows. Determine the value of $\mu$ for which the center of the system is the point of abscissa $7a$, and the value of $\mu$ for which the center does not exist, specifying to which system the load reduces in that case.

::struttura{nodi="P_1,0,0;P_2,2,0;P_3,5,0" aste="P_1-P_3" forze="0 1.3,0,1.3,;2 -0.7,0,-0.7,;5 1.6,0,1.6," etichette="0.22,1.34,2F;2.22,-0.72,F;5.22,1.62,μF" quote="P_1,P_2,2a,1.6;P_2,P_3,3a,1.6" titolo="The three parallel vectors, with the coefficient $\mu$ of the third one as parameter"}

:::soluzione
$x_C = \dfrac{(5\mu - 2)\,a}{\mu + 1}$, therefore $\mu = -\dfrac{9}{2}$. The center does not exist for $\mu = -\,1$, and in that case the system is equivalent to a couple of moment $-\,7\,a F\,\hat{k}$, that is clockwise of magnitude $7\,a F$.
:::
