---
titolo: Possible displacement and virtual displacement on a rotating guide
teoria: [spostamento-possibile, spostamento-virtuale, vincolo-fisso, spostamenti-virtuali-e-vincoli]
parole: [moving constraint, rotating guide, possible displacement, virtual displacement, difference]
---

A material point $P$ is threaded on a smooth straight guide that rotates in the plane $Oxy$ about the fixed point $O$ with assigned constant angular velocity $\omega = 4\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. At the instant considered the guide forms the angle of $60^\circ$ with the $x$ axis and the point is at distance $s = 1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $O$. Write the virtual displacement and the possible displacement of $P$ and compute the magnitude and the direction of their difference, for the same advance along the guide.

:::soluzione
$\delta P = \delta s\,\Big( \dfrac{1}{2}\,\hat{\imath} + \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,\hat{\jmath} \Big)$; $dP = ds\,\Big( \dfrac{1}{2}\,\hat{\imath} + \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,\hat{\jmath} \Big) + 6\,dt\,\Big( -\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,\hat{\imath} + \dfrac{1}{2}\,\hat{\jmath} \Big)$, with the lengths in meters. The difference equals $s\,\omega\,dt = 6\,dt$ in magnitude, and it is orthogonal to the guide.
:::
