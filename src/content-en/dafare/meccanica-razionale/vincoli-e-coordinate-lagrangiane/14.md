---
titolo: Admissible configurations with a unilateral support
teoria: [vincolo-bilaterale, vincolo, spostamento-virtuale, vincolo-ideale]
parole: [unilateral constraint, simple support, admissible configurations, boundary configuration, detachment]
---

A homogeneous rod $OA$ of length $\ell$ is hinged to the fixed point $O$ and rotates in a vertical plane; at depth $d = \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\,\ell$ below $O$ there is a smooth horizontal floor on which the end $A$ can rest. Denoting by $\vartheta$ the angle that the rod forms with the horizontal $x$ axis, write the constraint in the Lagrangian coordinate, determine the interval of the admissible values of $\vartheta$ and indicate the boundary configurations together with the reaction exerted by the floor.

:::soluzione
The constraint is $\sin\vartheta \geq -\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$, hence $-\dfrac{\pi}{4} \leq \vartheta \leq \dfrac{5\pi}{4}$. The boundary configurations are $\vartheta = -\dfrac{\pi}{4}$ and $\vartheta = \dfrac{5\pi}{4}$, in which the floor exerts the vertical reaction $\vec{\Phi} = \Phi_n\,\hat{\jmath}$ with $\Phi_n \geq 0$; in the interior configurations the reaction is zero.
:::
