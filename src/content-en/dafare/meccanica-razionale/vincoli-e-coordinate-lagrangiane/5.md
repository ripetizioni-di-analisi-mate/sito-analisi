titolo: Point constrained to a cylindrical surface

A material point $P$ is constrained to the smooth and fixed cylindrical surface of equation $x^2 + y^2 = R^2$, with axis coinciding with the $z$ axis. Determine the number of degrees of freedom, choose the Lagrangian coordinates, compute the magnitude of the velocity of $P$ and establish the direction of the constraint reaction.

:::soluzione
$n = 3 - 1 = 2$, with the angle $\varphi$ about the axis and the height $z$ as Lagrangian coordinates; one has $P - O = R\cos\varphi\,\hat{\imath} + R\sin\varphi\,\hat{\jmath} + z\,\hat{k}$ and $|\vec{v}_P| = \sqrt{R^2\dot{\varphi}^2 + \dot{z}^2}$; the reaction is radial, parallel to $\cos\varphi\,\hat{\imath} + \sin\varphi\,\hat{\jmath}$, hence orthogonal to the axis of the cylinder.
:::
