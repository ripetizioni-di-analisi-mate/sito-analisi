---
titolo: Inclined force on a resting body and friction cone
teoria: [coulomb-morin, attrito-statico]
parole: [friction cone, friction angle, inclined force, rough horizontal plane, Coulomb-Morin]
---

A body of mass $m$ rests on a rough horizontal plane, with static friction coefficient $f_s = 0.30$. On it there acts a force of magnitude $F$ directed downward and inclined by the angle $\gamma$ to the horizontal, which tends to push the body along the plane. Determine for which values of $\gamma$ the body stays in equilibrium whatever the magnitude $F$ is, and interpret the result with the friction cone.

:::soluzione
Equilibrium holds for every $F$ if and only if $\tan\gamma \geq \dfrac{1}{f_s}$, that is, $\gamma \geq \arctan \dfrac{10}{3} = 73.30^\circ$. The applied force forms the angle $90^\circ - \gamma$ with the normal to the plane, and the condition says that this angle does not exceed the friction angle $\rho = \arctan 0.30 = 16.70^\circ$: the force falls inside the friction cone.
:::
