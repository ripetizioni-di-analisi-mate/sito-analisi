---
titolo: Point constrained to an ellipse
teoria: [punto-su-una-curva, coordinate-lagrangiane, reazione-di-un-vincolo-liscio]
parole: [point on a curve, ellipse, Lagrangian coordinate, velocity, normal reaction]
---

A material point $P$ is constrained to the smooth and fixed guide of equation $\dfrac{x^2}{a^2} + \dfrac{y^2}{b^2} = 1$, with $a > b > 0$, lying in the plane $Oxy$. Determine the number of degrees of freedom, write the position and the velocity of $P$ as functions of a Lagrangian coordinate and the direction of the constraint reaction.

:::soluzione
$n = 1$; with the Lagrangian coordinate $\vartheta$ one has $P - O = a\cos\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + b\sin\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath}$ and $\vec{v}_P = \dot{\vartheta}\,( -a\sin\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + b\cos\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath} )$; the reaction is parallel to $\dfrac{\cos\vartheta}{a}\,\hat{\imath} + \dfrac{\sin\vartheta}{b}\,\hat{\jmath}$, that is, normal to the ellipse.
:::
