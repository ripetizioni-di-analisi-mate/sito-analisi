titolo: Stroke of the connecting-rod end in a slider-crank mechanism

In a slider-crank mechanism the crank $OA$ has length $r$ and rotates about the fixed hinge $O$, the connecting rod $AB$ has length $\ell = 3r$ and the end $B$ slides on a horizontal guide through $O$. Denoting by $\vartheta$ the angle of the crank with the guide, write the abscissa of $B$ as a function of $\vartheta$, compute it for $\vartheta = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$ and determine the stroke of the connecting-rod end.

:::soluzione
$x_B = r\cos\vartheta + \sqrt{9r^2 - r^2\sin^2\vartheta} = r\,\big( \cos\vartheta + \sqrt{8 + \cos^2\vartheta} \big)$; for $\vartheta = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$ one has $x_B = 2\sqrt{2}\,r$; the stroke equals $c = x_B(0) - x_B(\pi) = 4r - 2r = 2r$.
:::
