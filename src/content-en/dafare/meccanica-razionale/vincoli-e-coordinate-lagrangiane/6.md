---
titolo: Velocity of the points of a rolling disk
teoria: [puro-rotolamento, coordinate-lagrangiane]
parole: [pure rolling, velocity of the points, disk, instantaneous center of rotation]
---

A homogeneous disk of radius $R$ rolls without slipping on the fixed horizontal guide $y = 0$; let $\vartheta$ be the rotation angle, positive counterclockwise. Compute the velocity of the point of the disk diametrically opposite the contact point and those of the two points of the disk that are at the height of the center, with the respective magnitudes.

:::soluzione
Highest point: $\vec{v} = -2R\,\dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{\imath}$, of magnitude $2R\,|\dot{\vartheta}|$. Point to the right of the center: $\vec{v} = -R\,\dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{\imath} + R\,\dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{\jmath}$; point to the left of the center: $\vec{v} = -R\,\dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{\imath} - R\,\dot{\vartheta}\,\hat{\jmath}$. Both have magnitude $\sqrt{2}\,R\,|\dot{\vartheta}|$.
:::
