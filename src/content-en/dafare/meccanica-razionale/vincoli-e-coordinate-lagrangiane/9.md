The system in the figure is formed by the rods $AB$ and $BC$, both of length $\ell$, hinged to each other at $B$, with a fixed hinge at $A$ and a roller support at $C$ on the horizontal guide through $A$. Denoting by $\vartheta$ the angle that the rod $AB$ forms with the guide, write the virtual displacements of $B$, of $C$ and of the midpoint $G_2$ of the rod $BC$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,2.6;C,4,0" aste="A-B;B-C" cerniere="B" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello,270" linee="-0.6,0 5.2,0" punti="3,1.3,G_2,baricentro" etichette="0.75,0.33,ϑ" titolo="Two equal rods hinged at $B$, with a hinge at $A$ and a roller support at $C$"}

:::soluzione
$\delta B = \ell\,\delta\vartheta\,( -\sin\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + \cos\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath} )$; $\delta C = -2\ell\sin\vartheta\,\delta\vartheta\,\hat{\imath}$; $\delta G_2 = -\dfrac{3\ell}{2}\sin\vartheta\,\delta\vartheta\,\hat{\imath} + \dfrac{\ell}{2}\cos\vartheta\,\delta\vartheta\,\hat{\jmath}$.
:::
