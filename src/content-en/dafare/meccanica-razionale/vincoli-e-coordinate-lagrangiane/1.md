The plane system in the figure is formed by the three rods $AB$, $BC$ and $CD$, hinged to one another at $B$ and at $C$. The rod $AB$ is hinged to the fixed point $A$ and the end $D$ is constrained by a roller support to the horizontal guide through $A$. The constraints are smooth and bilateral. Determine the number of degrees of freedom of the system and indicate a possible choice of the Lagrangian coordinates.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,1.5,2;C,3.5,2.2;D,5,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" cerniere="B;C" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,carrello,270" linee="-0.6,0 6,0" titolo="Chain of three rods with a hinge at $A$ and a roller support at $D$"}

:::soluzione
$n = 3 \cdot 3 - (2 + 2 + 2 + 1) = 2$ degrees of freedom; as Lagrangian coordinates one takes two of the three angles that the rods form with the horizontal, for example those of $AB$ and of $BC$.
:::
