---
titolo: Equilibrium of a body on a rough inclined plane
teoria: [coulomb-morin, attrito-statico, blocco-su-piano-inclinato]
parole: [static friction, inclined plane, friction coefficient, friction angle, Coulomb-Morin]
---

A body of mass $m$ rests on a plane inclined by $35^\circ$ to the horizontal, with static friction coefficient $f_s = 0.60$ between body and plane; the only active forces are the weight and the reaction of the plane. Establish whether the body stays in equilibrium, compute the minimum static friction coefficient that guarantees it and compare the inclination of the plane with the friction angle of the contact.

:::soluzione
The equilibrium condition is $\tan\alpha \leq f_s$. Since $\tan 35^\circ = 0.70 > 0.60$, the condition is not satisfied and the body slides along the plane. The minimum coefficient equals $f_{s,\min} = \tan 35^\circ = 0.70$. The friction angle equals $\rho = \arctan 0.60 = 30.96^\circ$, smaller than the $35^\circ$ of the plane.
:::
