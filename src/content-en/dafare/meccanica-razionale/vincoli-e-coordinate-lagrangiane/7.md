A homogeneous disk of radius $r$ rolls without slipping on the outside of a fixed circular guide of center $O$ and radius $R$, as in the figure. Let $\varphi$ be the angle that the vector $C - O$ forms with the downward vertical, where $C$ is the center of the disk, and let $\vartheta$ be the rotation angle of the disk, positive counterclockwise. Determine the number of degrees of freedom and the relation between $\vartheta$ and $\varphi$, and write it for $R = 2r$.

:::soluzione
$n = 3 - 2 = 1$; the pure rolling condition gives $\dot{\vartheta} = \dfrac{R + r}{r}\,\dot{\varphi}$, which integrates to $\vartheta = \dfrac{R + r}{r}\,\varphi + \text{constant}$, hence the constraint is holonomic. For $R = 2r$ one obtains $\dot{\vartheta} = 3\,\dot{\varphi}$.
:::
