The system in the figure is formed by a homogeneous disk of radius $R$, hinged at its fixed center $O$, and by a body hanging from an inextensible and flexible string wound on the rim of the disk; the string does not slide on the disk and the free portion is vertical. Let $\vartheta$ be the rotation angle of the disk, positive counterclockwise, and $s$ the descent of the body measured positive downward. Determine the number of degrees of freedom, write the constraint that relates $s$ to $\vartheta$ and say what type of constraint it is.

::struttura{cerchi="0,0,1.2" nodi="O,0,0,-;P,1.2,0,-;Q,1.2,-2.2,-" catene="P-Q" vincoli="O,cerniera,90" poligoni="0.7,-2.2 1.7,-2.2 1.7,-2.9 0.7,-2.9" punti="0,0,O,pieno" coppie="-0.55 0.4,1,ϑ" tratteggi="0,0 1.2,0" etichette="0.55,0.2,R" frecce="2.3,-2.2,2.3,-2.9,s" titolo="Pulley of radius $R$ with an inextensible string and a hanging body"}

:::soluzione
$n = 1$; the constraint is $s = -R\,\vartheta$, that is, the body descends when the disk rotates clockwise. It is a holonomic, bilateral and fixed constraint, as long as the string remains taut.
:::
