The plane system in the figure is formed by a homogeneous disk of radius $R$, which rolls without slipping on the fixed horizontal guide, and by a rod $CB$ hinged to the disk at its center $C$, with the end $B$ constrained by a roller support to the same guide. The constraints are smooth and bilateral. Determine the number of degrees of freedom of the system.

::struttura{cerchi="2,1,1" nodi="C,2,1,-;B,5,0" aste="C-B" vincoli="B,carrello,270" linee="-0.5,0 6.2,0" punti="2,1,C,baricentro;2,0,H,centro" titolo="Rolling disk connected by a rod to a roller support on the same guide"}

:::soluzione
$n = 3 \cdot 2 - (2 + 2 + 1) = 1$ degree of freedom; as Lagrangian coordinate one takes the rotation angle of the disk or the abscissa of its center.
:::
