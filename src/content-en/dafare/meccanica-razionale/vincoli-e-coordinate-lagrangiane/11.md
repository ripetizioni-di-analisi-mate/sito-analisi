A rigid rod $AB$ is constrained to a fixed horizontal guide by a smooth sleeve at $A$, which prevents the vertical translation of $A$ and the rotation of the rod and leaves the translation along the guide free. Write the reactions exerted by the sleeve, the virtual displacement of $A$ and the virtual work of the reactions, and establish whether the constraint is ideal.

:::soluzione
The sleeve exerts the normal reaction $\vec{\Phi}_A = \Phi_A\,\hat{\jmath}$ and the reaction moment $\Gamma_A$. The system has one degree of freedom, the abscissa $x_A$, hence $\delta A = \delta x_A\,\hat{\imath}$ and $\delta\vartheta = 0$. The virtual work equals $\delta L^{(v)} = \Phi_A\,\hat{\jmath} \cdot \delta x_A\,\hat{\imath} + \Gamma_A\,\delta\vartheta = 0$, therefore the constraint is ideal.
:::
