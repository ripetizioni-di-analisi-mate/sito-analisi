A homogeneous disk of mass $m$ and radius $R$ rolls without slipping on a fixed horizontal guide and carries a material point of mass $m$ welded on its rim, as in the figure. The angle $\varphi$ measures the rotation of the disk starting from the configuration in which the point occupies the contact. Compute the kinetic energy of the system.

::struttura{cerchi="3,1.5,1.5" linee="0,0 7,0" punti="3,1.5,G,baricentro;3,0,C,centro;1.7,0.75,P,pieno" tratteggi="3,1.5 3,0;3,1.5 1.7,0.75" etichette="2.5,0.92,φ;3.3,0.72,R" titolo="Disk rolling with a mass welded on the rim"}

:::soluzione
$T = m\,R^2\,\dot{\varphi}^2\,\Big( \dfrac{7}{4} - \cos\varphi \Big)$, minimum when the point touches the guide and maximum when it lies at the highest point.
:::
