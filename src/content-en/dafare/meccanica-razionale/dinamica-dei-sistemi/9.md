A homogeneous circular platform of mass $2m$ and radius $R$ rotates without friction about a fixed vertical axis through its center, in a horizontal plane, with angular velocity $\omega_0$, as in the figure, seen from above. Two material points of mass $m$ each are rigidly connected to the platform, diametrically opposite on the rim. The two points are brought to a distance $\dfrac{R}{2}$ from the axis and stay there at rest relative to the platform. Determine the final angular velocity and the change of kinetic energy.

::struttura{cerchi="0,0,2" punti="0,0,O,centro;2,0,P_1,pieno;-2,0,P_2,pieno;1,0,,pieno;-1,0,,pieno" frecce="1.8,0,1.2,0;-1.8,0,-1.2,0" coppie="-1.2 1.2,1,ω_0" etichette="-1.3,-1.2,2m" titolo="Seen from above: the two points are brought to a distance $\dfrac{R}{2}$ from the axis"}

:::soluzione
$\omega_f = 2\,\omega_0$; the kinetic energy increases by $\dfrac{3}{2}\,m\,R^2\,\omega_0^2$, because of the work of the internal forces that move the two points.
:::
