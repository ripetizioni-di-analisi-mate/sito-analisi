A material point of mass $m$ moves on a smooth horizontal plane, tied to an inextensible string of negligible mass that passes through a hole $O$ of the plane, as in the figure. At the beginning the point travels along a circle of radius $3R$ with velocity of magnitude $v_0$. The string is pulled slowly from below until the radius becomes $R$. Determine the final velocity of the point and the work done on the string.

::struttura{punti="0,0,O,centro;4.5,0,P,pieno" linee="0,0 4.5,0" frecce="4.5,0,4.5,2.2,v_0" quote="0 0,4.5 0,3R,0.7" etichette="4.85,-0.35,m" titolo="Seen from above: the point turns about the hole held by the string"}

:::soluzione
$v_f = 3\,v_0$; the work done on the string equals $4\,m\,v_0^2$ and it is positive.
:::
