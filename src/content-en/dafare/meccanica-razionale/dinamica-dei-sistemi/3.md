---
titolo: Angular momentum of two points about the origin
teoria: [momento-angolare, koenig-momento-angolare]
parole: [angular momentum, pole, cross product, two material points]
---

Two material points of equal mass $m$ occupy the positions $P_1 = (2\ell, 0)$ and $P_2 = (0, \ell)$ of the fixed frame $(O; x, y)$ and have velocities $\vec{v}_1 = v\,\hat{\jmath}$ and $\vec{v}_2 = v\,\hat{\imath}$. Compute the angular momentum of the system about the origin $O$.

:::soluzione
$\vec{K}_O = m\,v\,\ell\,\hat{k}$, hence it is directed along $\hat{k}$ and corresponds to a counterclockwise rotation about $O$.
:::
