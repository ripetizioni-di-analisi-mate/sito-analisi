A cart of mass $5m$ slides without friction on a horizontal guide and carries on its upper surface, which is smooth as well, a material point of mass $m$, as in the figure. The system starts from rest and the point moves a distance $d$ to the right relative to the cart. Determine the displacement of the cart and that of the point relative to the guide.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0.3 4,0.3 4,1.3 0,1.3" cerchi="0.9,0.3,0.3;3.1,0.3,0.3" linee="-1,0 5.6,0" punti="1,1.3,P,pieno" frecce="1,1.75,2.8,1.75" etichette="2.2,0.78,5m;0.55,1.8,m;1.9,2.05,d" titolo="The point moves by $d$ relative to the cart"}

:::soluzione
The cart moves by $\dfrac{d}{6}$ to the left; the point moves by $\dfrac{5\,d}{6}$ to the right.
:::
