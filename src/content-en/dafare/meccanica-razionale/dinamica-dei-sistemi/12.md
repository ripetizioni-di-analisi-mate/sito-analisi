Two bodies of masses $m$ and $3m$, which can be treated as material points, are joined by an inextensible string of negligible mass passing over a homogeneous pulley of mass $4m$ and radius $R$, hinged at its center to a fixed point, as in the figure. The string does not slip on the pulley and the system starts from rest. Determine the velocity of the two bodies when the body of mass $3m$ has descended by a distance $h$.

::struttura{nodi="S,0,3.2,-" vincoli="S,incastro,90" cerchi="0,2.6,0.6" punti="0,2.6,,centro" linee="-0.6,2.6 -0.6,1.3;0.6,2.6 0.6,0.7" poligoni="-1,0.7 -0.2,0.7 -0.2,1.3 -1,1.3;0.2,0.1 1,0.1 1,0.7 0.2,0.7" tratteggi="0,2.6 0.6,2.6" etichette="-0.6,1,m;0.6,0.4,3m;-1.3,2.8,4m;0.3,2.8,R" titolo="Pulley of non-negligible mass with two bodies hanging from it"}

:::soluzione
$v = \sqrt{\dfrac{2\,g\,h}{3}}$, the same for the two bodies.
:::
