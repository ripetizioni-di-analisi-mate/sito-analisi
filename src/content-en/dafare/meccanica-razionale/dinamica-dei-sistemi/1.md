Three material points of masses $m$, $2m$ and $m$ occupy the positions $P_1 = (0, 0)$, $P_2 = (\ell, 0)$ and $P_3 = (0, 2\ell)$ of the fixed frame $(O; x, y)$, as in the figure. At the instant considered the velocities are $\vec{v}_1 = \vec{0}$, $\vec{v}_2 = v\,\hat{\jmath}$ and $\vec{v}_3 = -2v\,\hat{\imath}$. Determine the center of mass, the linear momentum of the system and the velocity of the center of mass.

::struttura{punti="0,0,P_1,pieno;2,0,P_2,pieno;0,4,P_3,pieno;1,1,G,baricentro" frecce="2,0,2,1.6;0,4,-1.6,4" etichette="2.5,0.9,v;-1.1,4.3,2v;-0.4,-0.35,m;2.5,-0.35,2m;0.45,4.4,m" quote="0 0,2 0,ℓ,0.6;0 0,0 4,2ℓ,0.6" titolo="The three points with their velocities"}

:::soluzione
$G - O = \dfrac{\ell}{2}\,\hat{\imath} + \dfrac{\ell}{2}\,\hat{\jmath}$; $\vec{Q} = -2m\,v\,\hat{\imath} + 2m\,v\,\hat{\jmath}$; $\vec{v}_G = -\dfrac{v}{2}\,\hat{\imath} + \dfrac{v}{2}\,\hat{\jmath}$, of magnitude $\dfrac{v\,\sqrt{2}}{2}$.
:::
