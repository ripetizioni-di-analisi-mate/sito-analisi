---
titolo: Angular momentum of a rotating rod about the two poles
teoria: [momento-angolare, koenig-momento-angolare, energia-cinetica-atto-rotatorio]
parole: [rotating rod, angular momentum, König's theorem, moment of inertia, Huygens-Steiner]

A homogeneous rod $AB$ of mass $2m$ and length $2\ell$ rotates in a plane about the fixed hinge $A$ with angular velocity $\vec{\omega} = \omega\,\hat{k}$, with $\omega > 0$. Compute the angular momentum about $A$ and about the center of mass $G$, and verify that the two values satisfy König's theorem.

:::soluzione
$\vec{K}_A = \dfrac{8}{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\omega\,\hat{k}$; $\vec{K}_G = \dfrac{2}{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\omega\,\hat{k}$; the transport term $(G - A) \wedge 2m\,\vec{v}_G$ equals $2\,m\,\ell^2\,\omega\,\hat{k}$, and the sum of the two contributions gives $\vec{K}_A$.
:::
