A homogeneous rod $AB$ of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ is hinged at the endpoint $A$ to a fixed point of a vertical plane, with a smooth hinge, and is released at rest in the horizontal position. Determine the angular velocity of the rod and the velocity of the endpoint $B$ at the passage through the vertical position.

:::soluzione
$\dot{\vartheta} = \sqrt{\dfrac{3\,g}{\ell}}$; the velocity of $B$ equals $\sqrt{3\,g\,\ell}$ and it is horizontal.
:::
