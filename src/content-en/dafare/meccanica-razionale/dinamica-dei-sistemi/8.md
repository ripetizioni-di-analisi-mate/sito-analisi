A cart of mass $2m$ has a smooth upper profile, which starts at the height $h$ and ends horizontal at the base, and slides without friction on a horizontal guide, as in the figure. A material point of mass $m$ is released at rest at the highest point of the profile and slides down to the base. Determine the velocities of the point and of the cart at the instant in which the point leaves the cart.

::struttura{poligoni="1.3,2 1.4,1.42 1.7,0.91 2.15,0.53 2.71,0.33 3,0.3 4.2,0.3 4.2,0 1.3,0" linee="-0.4,0 5.6,0" punti="1.3,2,P,pieno" quote="1.3 0.3,1.3 2,h,-0.7" etichette="3.1,1,2m;0.95,2.25,m" titolo="The point slides on the smooth profile of the cart"}

:::soluzione
The point has velocity $2\,\sqrt{\dfrac{g\,h}{3}}$ in the direction of the descent; the cart has velocity $\sqrt{\dfrac{g\,h}{3}}$ in the opposite direction.
:::
