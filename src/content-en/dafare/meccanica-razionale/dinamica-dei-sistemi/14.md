A homogeneous rod $AB$ of mass $2m$ and length $\ell$ is hinged at the endpoint $A$ to a fixed point of a vertical plane, with a smooth hinge, and stands still in the vertical position, as in the figure. A material point of mass $m$, moving horizontally with velocity $v_0$, strikes the endpoint $B$ and sticks to it. Determine the angular velocity of the system right after the impact and the kinetic energy lost in the impact.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,3;B,0,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera,90" punti="-1.8,0,P,pieno;0,1.5,G,baricentro" frecce="-1.8,0,-0.35,0" quote="A,B,ℓ,-0.6" etichette="-2.1,0.35,m;-0.75,2.2,2m;-1.05,0.3,v_0" titolo="The point strikes the endpoint $B$ and sticks to it"}

:::soluzione
$\omega = \dfrac{3\,v_0}{5\,\ell}$, counterclockwise if $v_0$ is directed to the right; the kinetic energy lost equals $\dfrac{1}{5}\,m\,v_0^2$, that is two fifths of the initial one.
:::
