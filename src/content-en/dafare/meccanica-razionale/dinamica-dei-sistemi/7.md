---
titolo: Two bodies pushed by a compressed spring
teoria: [conservazione-della-quantita-di-moto, prima-equazione-cardinale, conservazione-dell-energia-meccanica]
parole: [compressed spring, conservation of linear momentum, elastic energy, smooth plane]
---

Two bodies, which can be treated as material points of masses $m$ and $4m$, rest on a smooth horizontal plane and are held in contact with a compressed ideal spring of negligible mass, which stores the elastic energy $E_0$. The system starts from rest and the bodies separate when the spring reaches its rest length. Determine the velocities of the two bodies after the separation.

:::soluzione
The body of mass $m$ has a velocity of magnitude $2\,\sqrt{\dfrac{2\,E_0}{5\,m}}$; the body of mass $4m$ has a velocity of magnitude $\dfrac{1}{2}\,\sqrt{\dfrac{2\,E_0}{5\,m}}$, directed the opposite way.
:::
