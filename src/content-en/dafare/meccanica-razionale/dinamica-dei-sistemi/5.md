A homogeneous rod $AB$ of mass $2m$ and length $2\ell$ moves in a vertical plane with the endpoint $A$ on a smooth horizontal guide and the endpoint $B$ on a smooth vertical guide, as in the figure. Calling $\vartheta$ the angle that the rod forms with the horizontal guide, compute the kinetic energy of the rod.

::struttura{nodi="A,3.46,0;B,0,2" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello,270;B,carrello,180" linee="-0.6,0 4.6,0;0,-0.6 0,3" punti="1.73,1,G,baricentro" etichette="2.85,0.16,ϑ" titolo="Rod with its endpoints constrained to two orthogonal guides"}

:::soluzione
$T = \dfrac{4}{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\dot{\vartheta}^2$, independent of $\vartheta$.
:::
