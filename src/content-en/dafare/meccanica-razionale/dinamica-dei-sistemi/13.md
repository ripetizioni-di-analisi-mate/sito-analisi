A homogeneous rod $AB$ of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ is hinged at the endpoint $A$ to a fixed point of a vertical plane, with a smooth hinge, and is released at rest in the horizontal position. Determine the reaction of the hinge at the instant in which the rod passes through the vertical position.

:::soluzione
$\vec{\Phi}_A = \dfrac{5}{2}\,m\,g\,\hat{\jmath}$, that is a force of intensity $\dfrac{5}{2}\,m\,g$ directed vertically upward.
:::
