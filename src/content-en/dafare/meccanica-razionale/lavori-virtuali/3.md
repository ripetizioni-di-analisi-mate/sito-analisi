---
titolo: Non-conservative position-dependent force and work along two paths
teoria: [lavoro-di-una-forza, lavoro-di-una-forza-conservativa, forza-conservativa]

A particle in the plane is acted upon by the position-dependent force $\vec{F} = c\,(y\,\hat{\imath} - x\,\hat{\jmath})$, with $c > 0$ constant. Compute the work of the force along the quarter circle of center $O$ and radius $\ell$ that goes from $(\ell, 0)$ to $(0, \ell)$, and along the polygonal path that goes from $(\ell, 0)$ to $O$ and from $O$ to $(0, \ell)$. Determine whether the force is conservative.

:::soluzione
Along the arc $L = -\dfrac{\pi\,c\,\ell^2}{2}$; along the polygonal path $L = 0$. The force is not conservative, since $\dfrac{\partial F_x}{\partial y} = c$ and $\dfrac{\partial F_y}{\partial x} = -c$ are different.
:::
