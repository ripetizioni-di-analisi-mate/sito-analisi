A homogeneous rod $AB$, of mass $m$ and length $2\ell$, has the end $A$ constrained to a smooth vertical guide and the end $B$ to a smooth horizontal guide, meeting at the origin $O$; an ideal spring of constant $k$ and zero natural length connects $B$ to $O$, as in the figure. The plane is vertical. Determine the equilibrium configurations with $\vartheta \in \left[ 0, \dfrac{\pi}{2} \right]$, where $\vartheta$ is the angle between the rod and the horizontal guide, and the condition on the data for more than one of them to exist.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,2.4;B,3.2,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello,180;B,carrello" linee="-0.3,0 4.2,0;0,-0.3 0,3.2;0.00,0.00 0.35,0.00 0.56,0.30 0.97,-0.30 1.39,0.30 1.81,-0.30 2.23,0.30 2.64,-0.30 2.85,0.00 3.20,0.00" punti="1.6,1.2,G,baricentro" forze="1.6 0.15,0,-0.6,mg" etichette="1.6,-0.62,k;2.72,0.22,ϑ" titolo="Rod with its ends on two orthogonal guides and spring between $B$ and the origin"}

:::soluzione
$\vartheta = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$, with the rod vertical, for every value of the data; moreover $\vartheta^* = \arcsin\dfrac{m g}{4\,k\,\ell}$, distinct from the previous one if and only if $m g < 4\,k\,\ell$.
:::
