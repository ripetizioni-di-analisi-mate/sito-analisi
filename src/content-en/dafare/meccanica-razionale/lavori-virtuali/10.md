A homogeneous disk of mass $m$ and radius $R$ rolls without slipping on a plane inclined by the angle $\alpha$ to the horizontal. An inextensible string is attached to the center $C$ of the disk, is parallel to the inclined plane, passes over an ideal pulley placed uphill and holds a hanging body of mass $M$, as in the figure. The plane of the motion is vertical. Determine the value of $M$ for which the system is in equilibrium.

:::soluzione
$M = m\sin\alpha$. The tension of the string is $m g\sin\alpha$.
:::
