A homogeneous rod $AB$, of mass $m$ and length $2\ell$, is hinged at the origin by the end $A$ and moves in the vertical plane. An ideal spring of constant $k$ and zero natural length connects the midpoint $G$ to the fixed point $C = (2\ell, 0)$, as in the figure. Determine the equilibrium configurations as a function of the angle $\vartheta$ between the rod and the downward vertical.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,1.9,-2.1;C,2.83,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera,90" punti="0.95,-1.05,G,baricentro" linee="2.83,0.00 2.61,-0.12 2.59,-0.36 2.15,-0.15 2.11,-0.63 1.67,-0.42 1.63,-0.90 1.19,-0.69 1.17,-0.93 0.95,-1.05" tratteggi="0,0 0,-2.6;0,0 2.83,0" forze="0.95 -2.05,0,-0.6,mg" etichette="0.35,-1,ϑ;2.1,-0.95,k" titolo="Rod hinged at $A$ with the spring between the midpoint $G$ and the fixed point $C$"}

:::soluzione
$U(\vartheta) = m g \ell\cos\vartheta + 2\,k\,\ell^2\sin\vartheta$, up to an additive constant; the equilibrium configurations are given by $\tan\vartheta = \dfrac{2\,k\,\ell}{m g}$, and they are two in $[0, 2\pi)$.
:::
