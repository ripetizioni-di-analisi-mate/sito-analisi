Two homogeneous rods $OA$ and $AB$, each of mass $m$ and length $\ell$, are hinged to each other at $A$ and to the ground at $O$ by smooth hinges, and move in the vertical plane. A constant couple of moment $\mathcal{M} > 0$ in the counterclockwise direction acts on the second rod, as in the figure. Write the potential as a function of the angles $\vartheta$ and $\varphi$ that the two rods form with the downward vertical and determine the equilibrium configurations, with the condition on the data for them to exist.

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,1.1,-2.2;B,2.9,-3.1" aste="O-A;A-B" vincoli="O,cerniera,90" cerniere="A" punti="0.55,-1.1,G_1,baricentro;2,-2.65,G_2,baricentro" tratteggi="0,0 0,-2.6;1.1,-2.2 1.1,-4" coppie="2.6 -2,1,ℳ" etichette="0.3,-1.1,ϑ;1.5,-3.3,φ" titolo="Double pendulum with a constant couple applied to the second rod"}

:::soluzione
$U(\vartheta, \varphi) = \dfrac{3}{2}\,m g \ell\cos\vartheta + \dfrac{1}{2}\,m g \ell\cos\varphi + \mathcal{M}\,\varphi$. At equilibrium $\sin\vartheta = 0$, that is, $\vartheta = 0$ or $\vartheta = \pi$, and $\sin\varphi = \dfrac{2\,\mathcal{M}}{m g \ell}$, which has a solution if and only if $2\,\mathcal{M} \leq m g \ell$.
:::
