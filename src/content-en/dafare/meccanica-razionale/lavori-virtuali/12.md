Two rings $P_1$ and $P_2$, which can be treated as particles of mass $m$ each, slide without friction along a fixed vertical guide. An ideal spring of constant $k$ connects $P_1$ to the fixed point $O$ of the guide, and an ideal spring of constant $2k$ connects $P_1$ to $P_2$; both have zero natural length, as in the figure. Determine the equilibrium configuration, identified by the heights $y_1$ and $y_2$ measured from $O$ upwards.

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;P_1,0,-1.6;P_2,0,-3.6" linee="-0.45,0 0.45,0;0.00,0.00 0.00,-0.25 0.25,-0.36 -0.25,-0.58 0.25,-0.80 -0.25,-1.02 0.25,-1.24 0.00,-1.35 0.00,-1.60;0.00,-1.60 0.00,-1.90 0.25,-2.02 -0.25,-2.26 0.25,-2.50 -0.25,-2.74 0.25,-2.98 -0.25,-3.22 0.00,-3.34 0.00,-3.60" tratteggi="0,0.3 0,-4.3" punti="0,-1.6;0,-3.6" etichette="0.75,-0.8,k;0.85,-2.8,2k;-0.75,-1.6,y_1;-0.75,-3.6,y_2" titolo="Two rings of mass $m$ on a vertical guide, with springs of constant $k$ and $2k$"}

:::soluzione
$y_1 = -\dfrac{2\,m g}{k}$ and $y_2 = -\dfrac{5\,m g}{2\,k}$, both below the point $O$.
:::
