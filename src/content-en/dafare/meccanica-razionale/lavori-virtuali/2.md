titolo: Work of the elastic force of a spring attached to a fixed point
teoria: [potenziale-della-molla, lavoro-di-una-forza-conservativa, potenziale-forza-centrale]
parole: [work, elastic force, spring, potential, fixed point]
---

A particle $P$ is connected to the origin $O$ by an ideal spring of elastic constant $k$ and zero natural length. The particle moves from the position $P_1 = (2\ell, 0)$ to the position $P_2 = (\ell, \ell)$ along an arbitrary path. Compute the work of the elastic force and say whether the result depends on the path.

:::soluzione
$L = k\,\ell^2$, positive. The result does not depend on the path, since the elastic force is conservative.
:::
