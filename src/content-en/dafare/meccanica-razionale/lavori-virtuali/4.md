A homogeneous rod $OA$, of mass $m$ and length $2\ell$, is hinged at the origin by a smooth hinge and moves in the vertical plane. An ideal spring of constant $k$ and zero natural length connects the end $A$ to the fixed point $C = (2\ell, 0)$, as in the figure. Write the potential as a function of the angle $\vartheta$ between the rod and the downward vertical, measured counterclockwise, and determine the equilibrium configurations.

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,1.9,-2.1;C,2.8,0" aste="O-A" vincoli="O,cerniera,90" punti="0.95,-1.05,G,baricentro" linee="2.80,0.00 2.68,-0.28 2.82,-0.47 2.36,-0.53 2.63,-0.91 2.17,-0.97 2.44,-1.35 1.98,-1.41 2.25,-1.79 2.02,-1.82 1.90,-2.10" tratteggi="0,0 0,-2.6;0,0 2.8,0" forze="0.95 -2.05,0,-0.6,mg" etichette="0.35,-1,ϑ;2.75,-1.1,k" titolo="Rod hinged at $O$ with the spring between $A$ and the fixed point $C = (2\ell, 0)$"}

:::soluzione
$U(\vartheta) = m g \ell\cos\vartheta + 4\,k\,\ell^2\sin\vartheta$, up to an additive constant. The equilibrium configurations are given by $\tan\vartheta = \dfrac{4\,k\,\ell}{m g}$, and they are two in $[0, 2\pi)$.
:::
