---
titolo: Work of a constant force along two paths
teoria: [lavoro-di-una-forza, lavoro-di-una-forza-conservativa, forza-conservativa]
parole: [work, constant force, different paths, potential]
---

A particle is acted upon by the constant force $\vec{F} = F\,(2\,\hat{\imath} - \hat{\jmath})$, with $F > 0$. The particle moves from the position $A = (0, 0)$ to the position $B = (\ell, 2\ell)$. Compute the work of the force along the segment $AB$ and along the polygonal path that passes through the point $(\ell, 0)$, and write a potential of the force.

:::soluzione
$L = 0$ along both paths. A potential is $U = F\,(2x - y)$.
:::
