A homogeneous rod $AB$, of mass $m$ and length $2\ell$, has the end $A$ constrained by a smooth sleeve to slide on a fixed horizontal guide and can rotate about $A$ in the vertical plane, below the guide. The constant force $\vec{F} = F\,\hat{\imath}$, with $F > 0$, is applied at the end $B$, as in the figure. Taking the abscissa $s$ of $A$ and the angle $\vartheta$ between the rod and the guide, measured below the guide, as generalized coordinates, compute $Q_s$ and $Q_\vartheta$ and determine whether equilibrium configurations exist.

::struttura{nodi="A,1.8,0;B,4.4,-1.5" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello,90" linee="-0.4,0 5,0" punti="0,0,O;3.1,-0.75,G,baricentro" forze="B,0.75,0,F;3.1 -2.3,0,-0.5,mg" quote="0 0,A,s,0.75" etichette="2.6,-0.35,ϑ" titolo="Rod with the end $A$ on a horizontal guide and constant force applied at $B$"}

:::soluzione
$Q_s = F$ and $Q_\vartheta = \ell\,(m g\cos\vartheta - 2\,F\sin\vartheta)$. There are no equilibrium configurations, since $Q_s = F \neq 0$ in every configuration.
:::
