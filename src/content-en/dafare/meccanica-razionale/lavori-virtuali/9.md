A homogeneous rod $AB$ of length $\ell$ has the end $A$ constrained by a smooth sleeve to slide on a fixed horizontal guide and can rotate about $A$ in the horizontal plane, as in the figure. A force of constant magnitude $F$, always perpendicular to the rod, is applied at the midpoint $G$. The weight is orthogonal to the plane of the motion and does no work. Taking the abscissa $s$ of $A$ and the angle $\vartheta$ between the rod and the guide as generalized coordinates, compute $Q_s$ and $Q_\vartheta$ and determine whether the force system admits a potential.

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,1.2,0;B,3.33,1.49" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello" linee="-0.2,0 4.6,0" punti="2.27,0.75,G,baricentro" forze="2.27 0.75,-0.57,0.82,F" etichette="1.95,0.22,ϑ" quote="O,A,s,0.8" titolo="Rod on a horizontal guide with a force always perpendicular applied at the midpoint"}

:::soluzione
$Q_s = -F\sin\vartheta$ and $Q_\vartheta = \dfrac{F\,\ell}{2}$. The force system admits no potential, since $\dfrac{\partial Q_s}{\partial \vartheta} = -F\cos\vartheta$ and $\dfrac{\partial Q_\vartheta}{\partial s} = 0$ are different.
:::
