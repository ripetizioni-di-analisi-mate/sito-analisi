A homogeneous rod $AB$, of mass $m$ and length $3\ell$, is placed horizontally in the vertical plane, with a smooth hinge at $A$ and a smooth roller support on a horizontal plane at $B$. A constant couple of moment $\mathcal{M}$ in the counterclockwise direction acts on the rod, as in the figure. Compute the reaction of the roller support and say for which value of $\mathcal{M}$ it vanishes.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4.2,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" punti="2.1,0,G,baricentro" forze="2.1 -1.4,0,-0.6,mg" coppie="3.2 0.55,1,ℳ" etichette="3.2,1.1, " quote="A,B,3 ℓ,1.9" titolo="Supported rod with a constant couple applied"}

:::soluzione
$\Phi_B = \dfrac{m g}{2} - \dfrac{\mathcal{M}}{3\,\ell}$, directed upwards if $\mathcal{M} < \dfrac{3\,m g \ell}{2}$; it vanishes for $\mathcal{M} = \dfrac{3\,m g \ell}{2}$.
:::
