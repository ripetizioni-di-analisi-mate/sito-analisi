A homogeneous disk of mass $m$ and radius $R$ rolls without slipping on a fixed horizontal guide, in a vertical plane. The center $C$ is connected by an ideal spring of constant $k$ and zero natural length to the fixed point $O$, placed at the same height as $C$; at the point $E$ of the rim diametrically opposite the contact the constant horizontal force $\vec{F} = F\,\hat{\imath}$ with $F > 0$ is applied, as in the figure. Determine the equilibrium configuration, identified by the abscissa $x$ of the center measured from $O$.

::struttura{cerchi="2.8,0.9,0.9" linee="-0.6,0 5,0;-0.1,0 -0.1,1.8;0.00,0.90 0.30,0.90 0.48,1.16 0.85,0.64 1.22,1.16 1.58,0.64 1.95,1.16 2.32,0.64 2.50,0.90 2.80,0.90" punti="2.8,0.9,C,baricentro;2.8,0,H,centro;2.8,1.8,E" forze="2.8 1.8,0.9,0,F" nodi="O,0,0.9;C,2.8,0.9,-" quote="O,C,x,1.45" etichette="1.4,1.45,k" titolo="Disk rolling on a horizontal guide, with a spring at the center and a force at the highest point"}

:::soluzione
$x = \dfrac{2\,F}{k}$. The factor $2$ comes from the fact that the point $E$ moves twice as much as the center, since it is at distance $2R$ from the instantaneous center of rotation.
:::
