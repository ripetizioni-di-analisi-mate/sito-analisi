A homogeneous rod $AB$, of mass $m$ and length $3\ell$, rests on two unilateral supports that can only push it upwards, placed at the end $A$ and at the point $D$ at distance $2\ell$ from $A$. At the end $B$ the vertical force $F$ directed downwards acts, as in the figure. The plane is vertical. Determine the condition on $F$ for the rod to remain resting at $A$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4.5,0;D,3,0" aste="A-B" linee="-0.6,-0.18 0.6,-0.18;2.4,-0.18 3.6,-0.18" punti="2.25,0,G,baricentro" forze="B,0,-0.95,F;2.25 -1.4,0,-0.6,mg" frecce="0,0,0,0.75,δy_A" quote="A,3 0,2 ℓ,1.75;3 0,B,ℓ,1.75" titolo="Rod on two unilateral supports with a load at the overhanging end"}

:::soluzione
$F \leq \dfrac{m g}{2}$. The reaction of the support at $A$ is $\Phi_A = \dfrac{m g}{4} - \dfrac{F}{2}$ and it vanishes for $F = \dfrac{m g}{2}$.
:::
