A homogeneous rod $AB$ of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ rests with its end $A$ on a smooth horizontal plane and moves in a vertical plane, as in the diagram in the figure. At the initial instant the rod forms the angle $\vartheta_0 = 60^\circ$ with the plane and is at rest. Compute the angular velocity at the instant when the rod reaches the plane and the reaction of the plane at that instant.

::struttura{linee="0,0 6,0" nodi="A,1.2,0;B,3,3.12" aste="A-B" punti="2.1,1.56,G,baricentro" tratteggi="1.2,0 3.9,0" etichette="2,0.33,ϑ_0" titolo="Rod resting with one end on a smooth horizontal plane"}

:::soluzione
$\omega = \sqrt{\dfrac{3\sqrt{3}\,g}{2\,\ell}} \simeq 1.61\,\sqrt{\dfrac{g}{\ell}}$; reaction of the plane $N = \dfrac{m\,g}{4}$, directed vertically upwards.
:::
