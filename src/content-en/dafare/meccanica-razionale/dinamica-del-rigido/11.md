A homogeneous rod $AB$ of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ has the end $A$ resting on a smooth horizontal floor and the end $B$ resting on a smooth vertical wall, as in the diagram in the figure. The rod starts from rest forming the angle $\vartheta_0 = 60^\circ$ with the wall. Compute the angle at which the end $B$ separates from the wall, the angular velocity and the reaction of the floor at that instant.

::struttura{linee="0,4.2 0,0 5,0" nodi="A,2.6,0;B,0,3" aste="A-B" punti="1.3,1.5,G,baricentro" forze="1.3 1.5,0,-1,mg" etichette="0.33,2.25,ϑ" quote="B,A,ℓ,-0.75" titolo="Rod with one end on the floor and the other on the wall, both smooth"}

:::soluzione
$\cos\vartheta^* = \dfrac{2}{3}\cos\vartheta_0 = \dfrac{1}{3}$, that is $\vartheta^* = 70.5^\circ$; $\dot{\vartheta}^* = \sqrt{\dfrac{g}{2\,\ell}}$; reaction of the floor $N_A = \dfrac{m\,g}{4}$, directed vertically upwards.
:::
