A homogeneous disk of mass $m$ and radius $R$ rolls without slipping on a horizontal guide. An inextensible cord of negligible mass is wound on its rim; it comes off horizontally from the highest point, passes over a small pulley of negligible mass and carries a body of mass $m$, as in the diagram in the figure. The system starts from rest. Compute the acceleration of the center of the disk, the acceleration of the hanging body and the tension of the cord.

:::soluzione
Acceleration of the center of the disk $\dfrac{4\,g}{11}$; acceleration of the hanging body $\dfrac{8\,g}{11}$; tension $S = \dfrac{3}{11}\,m\,g$.
