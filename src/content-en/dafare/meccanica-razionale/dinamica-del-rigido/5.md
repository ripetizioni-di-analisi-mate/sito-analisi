A homogeneous rod $AB$ of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ is hinged at the end $A$ to a fixed smooth horizontal axis, perpendicular to the vertical plane of the motion, as in the diagram in the figure. The rod is released from rest in the horizontal position. Compute the initial angular acceleration, the angular velocity when the rod passes through the vertical position and the reaction of the hinge at that instant.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3.2,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera" punti="1.6,0,G,baricentro" forze="1.6 0,0,-1,mg" quote="A,B,ℓ,0.9" coppie="0.7 0,-1,ϑ" titolo="Rod hinged at $A$, initially horizontal and at rest"}

:::soluzione
$\ddot{\vartheta}_0 = \dfrac{3\,g}{2\,\ell}$; $\omega = \sqrt{\dfrac{3\,g}{\ell}}$; reaction in the vertical position $\Phi_A = \dfrac{5}{2}\,m\,g$, directed vertically upwards, with zero horizontal component.
:::
