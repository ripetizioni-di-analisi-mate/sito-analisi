A homogeneous disk of mass $m = 4\,\mathrm{kg}$ and radius $R = 15\,\mathrm{cm}$ is keyed on a spindle of negligible mass pivoted at $O$ and rotates about its own axis of figure with constant spin velocity $\dot{\varphi} = 200\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. The axis of figure is horizontal and the center of the disk lies at distance $d = 20\,\mathrm{cm}$ from $O$, as in the diagram in the figure. Compute the precession velocity of the axis about the vertical through $O$, the period of the precession and the magnitude of the angular momentum with respect to $O$. Assume $g = 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;B,3.4,0,-" aste="O-B" cerchi="3.4,0,1.1" vincoli="O,cerniera" frecce="0,-1.2,0,3,asse di precessione;0,0,4.9,0,ζ" punti="3.4,0,G,baricentro" forze="3.4 0,0,-1.1,mg" coppie="0 2.5,1,Ω;4.6 0,1,φ̇" quote="O,3.4 0,d,1.5" riferimento=0 titolo="Gyroscope with a horizontal axis of figure pivoted at $O$"}

:::soluzione
$\Omega = \dfrac{2\,g\,d}{R^2\,\dot{\varphi}} = 0.87\,\mathrm{rad/s}$; period $\mathcal{T} = \dfrac{2\pi}{\Omega} = 7.21\,\mathrm{s}$; $|\vec{K}_O| \simeq C\,\dot{\varphi} = 9.0\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2/s}$.
:::
