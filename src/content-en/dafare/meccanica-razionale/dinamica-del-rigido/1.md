A homogeneous disk of mass $m$ and radius $R$ rolls without slipping on a plane inclined by $\alpha = 30^\circ$ to the horizontal, as the diagram in the figure shows, and starts from rest. Compute the acceleration of the center, the friction force and the smallest coefficient of static friction that makes pure rolling possible. Repeat the computation of the acceleration for a homogeneous solid sphere of the same mass and the same radius. Assume $g = 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

::struttura{linee="0,0 6,3.46 6,0 0,0" cerchi="2.57,2.48,0.86" punti="2.57,2.48,G,baricentro;3,1.73,C,centro" etichette="1.05,0.26,α;1.75,3.3,m, R" titolo="Homogeneous disk on the plane inclined by $\alpha = 30^\circ$"}

:::soluzione
Disk: $a_G = \dfrac{2}{3}\,g\sin\alpha = \dfrac{g}{3} = 3.27\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, directed down along the plane; $F_a = \dfrac{1}{3}\,m\,g\sin\alpha = \dfrac{m\,g}{6}$, directed up along the plane; $f_s \ge \dfrac{\tan\alpha}{3} = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{9} \simeq 0.19$. Solid sphere: $a_G = \dfrac{5}{7}\,g\sin\alpha = \dfrac{5\,g}{14} = 3.50\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
:::
