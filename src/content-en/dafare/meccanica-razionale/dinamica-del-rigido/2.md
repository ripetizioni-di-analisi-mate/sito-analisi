A homogeneous pulley, modeled as a disk of mass $2m$ and radius $R$, rotates about a fixed smooth horizontal axis through its center $O$. An inextensible cord of negligible mass is wound on it, and a body of mass $m$ hangs from its end, as in the diagram in the figure. The system starts from rest. Compute the acceleration of the hanging body, the tension of the cord and the reaction of the axis.
