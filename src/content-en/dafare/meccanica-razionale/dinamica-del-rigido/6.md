A rigid system is formed by a homogeneous rod $AB$ of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ and by a homogeneous disk of mass $2m$ and radius $R = \dfrac{\ell}{4}$, welded to the rod with its center at $B$ and lying in the same vertical plane, as in the diagram in the figure. The system oscillates about a fixed smooth horizontal axis through $A$ and perpendicular to the plane. Compute the moment of inertia with respect to the axis, the reduced length and the period of the small oscillations.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" cerchi="4,0,1" vincoli="A,cerniera" punti="3.33,0,G,baricentro" quote="A,B,ℓ,1.6;4 0,5 0,R,-1.3" etichette="5.35,1.15,2m;1.5,0.75,m" titolo="Rod with a disk welded at the end, oscillating about the axis through $A$"}

:::soluzione
$I_A = \dfrac{115}{48}\,m\,\ell^2$; $\ell_r = \dfrac{23}{24}\,\ell$; $\mathcal{T} = 2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{23\,\ell}{24\,g}}$.
:::
