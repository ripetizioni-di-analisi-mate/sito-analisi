A homogeneous rod of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ is welded by its midpoint $G$ to a vertical shaft, with which it forms the constant angle $\alpha = 30^\circ$; the point $G$ lies on the axis of rotation, halfway between the two bearings, placed at distance $h$ from each other, as in the diagram in the figure. The shaft has negligible mass and rotates with constant angular velocity $\omega$. Compute the transverse reactions of the bearings due to the rotation and say whether the system is statically and dynamically balanced.

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;B,0,4;D,1,3.73,-;E,-1,0.27,-" aste="O-B;D-E" vincoli="O,cerniera;B,cerniera,90" punti="0,2,G,baricentro" tratteggi="0,2 0,3.7" etichette="0.27,3,α" quote="O,B,h,-0.95" titolo="Rod welded by its midpoint to a vertical shaft, inclined by $\alpha = 30^\circ$"}

:::soluzione
The two transverse reactions are equal and opposite, lie in the plane of the rod and rotate with the shaft; their magnitude is $\dfrac{m\,\ell^2\,\omega^2\,\sin 2\alpha}{24\,h} = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}\,m\,\ell^2\,\omega^2}{48\,h}$. The system is statically balanced, because $G$ lies on the axis, and it is not dynamically balanced, because $I_{\xi\zeta} = \dfrac{m\,\ell^2}{24}\sin 2\alpha \neq 0$.
:::
