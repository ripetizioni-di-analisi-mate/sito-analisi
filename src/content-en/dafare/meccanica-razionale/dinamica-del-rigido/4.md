A homogeneous disk of mass $m$ and radius $R$ carries a circular groove of radius $r = \dfrac{R}{3}$, of negligible mass, cut around the center. An inextensible string, vertical and fastened above, is wound on this groove, as in the diagram in the figure. The disk is released from rest and descends unwinding the string, keeping its axis horizontal. Compute the acceleration of the center and the tension of the string.

:::soluzione
$a = \dfrac{2\,g}{11}$, directed downwards; $S = \dfrac{9}{11}\,m\,g$.
:::
