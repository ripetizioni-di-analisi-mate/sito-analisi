Two bodies of masses $3m$ and $m$ hang from the two ends of an inextensible cord of negligible mass that passes without slipping over a homogeneous pulley of mass $4m$ and radius $R$, with a fixed smooth horizontal axis, as in the diagram in the figure. Compute the acceleration of the bodies and the tensions in the two branches of the cord.
