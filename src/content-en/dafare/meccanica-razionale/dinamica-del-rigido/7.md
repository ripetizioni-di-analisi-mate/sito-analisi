A homogeneous disk of mass $m$ and radius $R$ is hinged to a fixed smooth horizontal axis through a point $O$ of its rim and perpendicular to the plane of the disk, as in the diagram in the figure. Compute the moment of inertia with respect to the axis, the reduced length and the period of the small oscillations. Then compute the angular velocity and the reaction of the hinge when the disk, released from rest with $OG$ horizontal, passes through the position in which $G$ lies below $O$.

::struttura{cerchi="0,-1.5,1.5" nodi="O,0,0" vincoli="O,cerniera,90" punti="0,-1.5,G,baricentro" tratteggi="0,0 0,-3.3" quote="O,0 -1.5,R,0.8" titolo="Disk hinged at a point $O$ of its rim"}

:::soluzione
$I_O = \dfrac{3}{2}\,m\,R^2$; $\ell_r = \dfrac{3}{2}\,R$; $\mathcal{T} = 2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{3\,R}{2\,g}}$. With $G$ below $O$: $\omega = \sqrt{\dfrac{4\,g}{3\,R}}$ and $\Phi_O = \dfrac{7}{3}\,m\,g$, directed vertically upwards.
:::
