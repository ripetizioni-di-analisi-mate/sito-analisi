A homogeneous rectangular lamina of mass $m$ and sides $2\ell$ and $\ell$ moves by inertia about its own center of mass $G$, held fixed, as in the diagram in the figure. Write the principal moments of inertia and the Euler equations of the motion, identify the permanent rotations and establish which ones are stable, computing the growth coefficient of the perturbation in the unstable case.

::struttura{poligoni="-2,-1 2,-1 2,1 -2,1" nodi="G,0,0,-" frecce="0,0,3,0,ξ;0,0,0,2,η" punti="0,0,G,baricentro" etichette="0.45,-0.3,ζ out of page" quote="-2 -1,2 -1,2ℓ,0.8;2 -1,2 1,ℓ,0.8" riferimento=0 titolo="Homogeneous rectangular lamina with a fixed center of mass"}

:::soluzione
$A = \dfrac{m\,\ell^2}{12}$, $B = \dfrac{m\,\ell^2}{3}$, $C = \dfrac{5\,m\,\ell^2}{12}$; Euler equations $\dot{p} = -q\,r$, $\dot{q} = r\,p$, $\dot{r} = -\dfrac{3}{5}\,p\,q$. The permanent rotations are those about the three principal axes; the ones about $\xi$ and about $\zeta$ are stable, the one about the intermediate axis $\eta$ is unstable, with growth coefficient $\lambda = q_0\sqrt{\dfrac{3}{5}} \simeq 0.775\,q_0$.
:::
