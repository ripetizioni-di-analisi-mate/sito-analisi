---
titolo: Balancing a shaft on two correction planes
teoria: [reazioni-asse, bilanciamento, asse-fisso]
parole: [imbalance, static balancing, dynamic balancing, balancing masses, dynamic reactions, bearings]
---

A point mass $m$ is keyed at distance $e$ from the axis, at the height $\zeta = c$, on a vertical shaft that rotates with constant angular velocity $\omega$ and is supported by the bearings $A$ at the height $\zeta = 0$ and $B$ at the height $\zeta = h$, as in the diagram in the figure. Compute the dynamic reactions of the two bearings and determine the products $m_1 e_1$ and $m_2 e_2$ of the two balancing masses to be placed, diametrically opposite to the first mass, in the planes $\zeta = 0$ and $\zeta = h$. Give the numerical values for $m = 2\,\mathrm{kg}$, $e = 5\,\mathrm{cm}$, $c = 20\,\mathrm{cm}$, $h = 60\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $\omega = 100\,\mathrm{rad/s}$, with the balancing masses placed at $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the axis.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;P,1.3,1.33,-" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera,90" cerchi="1.3,1.33,0.3" punti="0,1.33,C" quote="A,0 1.33,c,-1.1;A,B,h,1.75;0 1.33,1.3 1.33,e,-0.75" etichette="2.1,1.85,m" titolo="Shaft with an unbalancing mass at the height $c$"}

:::soluzione
$|\Phi_A| = \dfrac{m\,e\,\omega^2\,(h-c)}{h} = 667\,\mathrm{N}$ and $|\Phi_B| = \dfrac{m\,e\,\omega^2\,c}{h} = 333\,\mathrm{N}$, both directed towards the axis and rotating with the shaft. Balancing masses: $m_1 e_1 = \dfrac{m\,e\,(h-c)}{h} = 0.0667\,\mathrm{kg\,m}$ in the plane $\zeta = 0$ and $m_2 e_2 = \dfrac{m\,e\,c}{h} = 0.0333\,\mathrm{kg\,m}$ in the plane $\zeta = h$, that is $m_1 = 0.67\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $m_2 = 0.33\,\mathrm{kg}$ at $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the axis.
:::
