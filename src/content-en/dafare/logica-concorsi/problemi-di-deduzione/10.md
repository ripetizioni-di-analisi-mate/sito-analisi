Everyone in a village is either a truth-teller who always tells the truth or a liar who always lies. Mario says, “Nora is a liar.” Nora says, “Omar is a liar.” Omar says, “Mario and Nora are both liars.” Who is a truth-teller?

- A) Only Mario
- B) Only Omar
- C) Mario and Omar
- D) Only Nora
- E) Nora and Omar

:::soluzione
Answer D. Only Nora.
:::
