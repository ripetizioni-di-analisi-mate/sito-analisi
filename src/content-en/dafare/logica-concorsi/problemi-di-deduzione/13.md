Elio, Fiona, Gabriele, and Irene finish in the top four places in a cooking competition, with no ties. Elio finishes ahead of Fiona. Irene finishes ahead of Gabriele. Fiona does not finish last. Which of the following statements cannot be true?

- A) Gabriele finishes third
- B) Irene finishes first
- C) Fiona finishes second
- D) Elio finishes second
- E) Irene finishes third

:::soluzione
Answer A. Gabriele finishes third.
:::
