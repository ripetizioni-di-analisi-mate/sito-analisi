Everyone on an island is either a truth-teller who always tells the truth or a liar who always lies. Gaia says, “At least one of us, Lia or me, is a liar.” Lia says nothing. Which of the following statements is true?

- A) Gaia is a truth-teller and Lia is a liar
- B) Gaia and Lia are both truth-tellers
- C) Gaia and Lia are both liars
- D) Gaia is a liar and Lia is a truth-teller
- E) there is not enough information to determine whether Lia is a truth-teller or a liar

:::soluzione
Answer A. Gaia is a truth-teller and Lia is a liar.
:::
