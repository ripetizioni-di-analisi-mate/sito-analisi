Four books with yellow, red, green, and blue covers stand side by side on a shelf in positions numbered $1$ through $4$ from the left. The red book is to the left of the green book. The yellow book is next to the blue book. The yellow book is not at either end. The red book is not next to the yellow book. Which of the following statements must be true?

- A) the green book is in position $4$
- B) the blue book is in position $2$
- C) the blue book is next to the green book
- D) the red book is in position $2$
- E) the yellow book is in position $3$

:::soluzione
Answer E. The yellow book is in position $3$.
:::
