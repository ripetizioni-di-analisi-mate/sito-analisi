Pia, Quinto, Rosa, and Silvio have medical checkups on four different days, Monday through Thursday of the same week. Rosa’s appointment is the day after Pia’s. Quinto’s appointment is on neither Monday nor Thursday. Silvio’s appointment is after Quinto’s. Whose appointment is on Tuesday?

- A) Pia
- B) Quinto
- C) Silvio
- D) The information is insufficient to determine this
- E) Rosa

:::soluzione
Answer E. Rosa (Pia on Monday, Rosa on Tuesday, Quinto on Wednesday, Silvio on Thursday).
:::
