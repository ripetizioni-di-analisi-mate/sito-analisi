titolo: "Who took the last slice?"
teoria: [affermazioni-contraddittorie, chi-e-stato]

Exactly one of four people, Paola, Rita, Sara, and Tina, ate the last slice of cake in the meeting room. Paola says, “It was Rita.” Rita says, “It wasn’t me.” Sara says, “It wasn’t me.” Tina says, “It was Paola.” Exactly one of the four statements is true. Who ate the slice?

- A) Paola
- B) Rita
- C) Sara
- D) Tina
- E) The information is insufficient to determine this

:::soluzione
Answer C. Sara.
:::
