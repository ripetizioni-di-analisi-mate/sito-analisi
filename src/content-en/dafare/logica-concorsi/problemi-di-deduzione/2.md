Agata, Bruna, and Celeste park in three spaces numbered $1$, $2$, and $3$, one car per space. Their cars are red, green, and yellow, in some order. The red car is in space $2$. Agata uses space $3$. Bruna does not have the red car, Celeste does not have the yellow car, and Agata does not have the green car. Who has the green car, and where does she park it?

- A) Bruna, in space $1$
- B) Agata, in space $3$
- C) Bruna, in space $2$
- D) Celeste, in space $1$
- E) Celeste, in space $2$

:::soluzione
Answer A. Bruna, in space $1$.
:::
