Dario, Emma, and Federico each participate in a different sport: swimming, tennis, or soccer. Emma does not play tennis. Federico neither swims nor plays soccer. Dario does not play soccer. Which of the following statements is true?

- A) Emma swims
- B) Dario plays tennis
- C) Emma plays soccer
- D) Dario plays soccer and Emma swims
- E) there is not enough information to determine which sport Emma plays

:::soluzione
Answer C. Emma plays soccer (Federico plays tennis and Dario swims).
:::
