Ivo, Lara, Mirko, and Noemi sit around a square table, one on each side, facing the center. Lara is across from Ivo. Mirko is on Ivo’s left. Which of the following statements is true?

- A) Mirko is on Lara’s left
- B) Noemi is across from Ivo
- C) Noemi is on Ivo’s right
- D) Noemi is on Ivo’s left
- E) Lara is on Mirko’s right

:::soluzione
Answer C. Noemi is on Ivo’s right.
:::
