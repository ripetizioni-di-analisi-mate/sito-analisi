Ada, Bice, and Ciro take turns being on call by phone Monday through Friday, one person per day. Ada and Bice cover two days each and Ciro covers one. No one is on call two days in a row. Ciro is on call on Wednesday and Ada on Monday. Bice is not on call on Friday. Which days is Bice on call?

- A) Tuesday and Friday
- B) Tuesday and Thursday
- C) Tuesday and Wednesday
- D) Monday and Thursday
- E) Thursday and Friday

:::soluzione
Answer B. Tuesday and Thursday.
:::
