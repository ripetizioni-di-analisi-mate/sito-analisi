Gino, Ida, Luigi, Nina, and Olga are standing in line at a university registrar’s office. Luigi is first. Gino is immediately behind Nina. Olga is immediately behind Ida. Ida is neither first nor last, and Gino is not last. Who is fourth in line?

- A) Gino
- B) Olga
- C) Nina
- D) Ida
- E) The information is insufficient to determine this

:::soluzione
Answer D. Ida (the order is Luigi, Nina, Gino, Ida, Olga).
:::
