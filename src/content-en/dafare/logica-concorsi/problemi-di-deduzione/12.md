The keys to the records room are in exactly one of three drawers numbered $1$, $2$, and $3$. The label on drawer $1$ reads “The keys are not here.” The label on drawer $2$ reads “The keys are in drawer $1$.” The label on drawer $3$ reads “The keys are here.” Exactly one of the three labels is false. Which drawer contains the keys?

- A) in drawer $1$
- B) in drawer $2$
- C) in none of them, because the conditions contradict one another
- D) The information is insufficient to determine this
- E) in drawer $3$

:::soluzione
Answer E. In drawer $3$.
:::
