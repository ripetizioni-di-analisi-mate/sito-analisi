Luca, Mara, and Nico start work at 6\:00, 7\:00, and 8\:00, one person at each time. Mara does not start at 6\:00. Nico starts after Luca. Which additional piece of information would let you determine everyone’s start time?

- A) Luca starts at 6\:00
- B) Mara starts after Luca
- C) Nico does not start at 6\:00
- D) Nico starts after Mara
- E) Mara and Nico start one hour apart

:::soluzione
Answer D. Nico starts after Mara (Luca at 6\:00, Mara at 7\:00, and Nico at 8\:00).
:::
