Bea, Carlo, Duilio, Erica, Furio, and Gloria sit around a round table in six equally spaced seats, facing the center. Carlo is across from Bea. Erica is on Carlo’s right. Gloria is on Bea’s left. Furio is next to Carlo. Who sits across from Duilio?

- A) Gloria
- B) Erica
- C) Furio
- D) Carlo
- E) The information is insufficient to determine this

:::soluzione
Answer B. Erica.
:::
