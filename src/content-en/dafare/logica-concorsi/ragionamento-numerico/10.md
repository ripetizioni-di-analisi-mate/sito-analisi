A selection exam ranks $25$ candidates, with no ties. Simone is ninth from the top. What is his position from the bottom?

- A) $16$
- B) $18$
- C) $15$
- D) $9$
- E) $17$

:::soluzione
Answer E: seventeenth.
:::
