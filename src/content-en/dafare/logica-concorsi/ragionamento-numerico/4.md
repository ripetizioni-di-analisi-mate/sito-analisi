At 6:00 a clock is set to the correct time. It gains $3$ minutes per hour at a constant rate. What time does it show at 14:00 that day?

- A) 14:08
- B) 14:16
- C) 14:03
- D) 14:24
- E) 13:36

:::soluzione
Answer D: 14:24.
:::
