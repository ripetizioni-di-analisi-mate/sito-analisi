A flight leaves New York at 18:20 New York time and lasts $8$ hours and $10$ minutes. Suppose Rome is $6$ hours ahead of New York. What is the local arrival time in Rome?

- A) 8:30
- B) 20:30
- C) 2:30
- D) 9:30
- E) 7:30

:::soluzione
Answer A: 8:30 the next day.
:::
