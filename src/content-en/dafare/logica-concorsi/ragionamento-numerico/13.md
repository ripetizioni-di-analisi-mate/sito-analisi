Marco says, “Anna's paternal grandfather is my father's father, but Anna's father is not my father.” Which of the following statements must be true?

- A) Anna is Marco's sister.
- B) Anna is Marco's cousin.
- C) Anna is Marco's niece.
- D) Anna is Marco's aunt.
- E) Anna is Marco's daughter.

:::soluzione
Answer B: Anna is Marco's cousin because her father is a brother of Marco's father.
:::
