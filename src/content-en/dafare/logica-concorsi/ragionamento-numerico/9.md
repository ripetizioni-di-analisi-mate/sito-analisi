The Centrale pharmacy in a small town is on night duty every $4$ days, and the library opens in the evening every $6$ days. Both were open on a Saturday evening. On what day of the week will both next be open in the evening?

- A) Saturday
- B) Tuesday
- C) Monday
- D) Thursday
- E) Friday

:::soluzione
Answer D: Thursday, after $12$ days.
:::
