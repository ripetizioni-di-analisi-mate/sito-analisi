A meeting begins at 6:15 in the morning. What is the smaller of the two angles formed by the hands of the clock in the room at that moment?

- A) $90°$
- B) $105°$
- C) $97.5°$
- D) $82.5°$
- E) $127.5°$

:::soluzione
Answer C: $97.5°$
:::
