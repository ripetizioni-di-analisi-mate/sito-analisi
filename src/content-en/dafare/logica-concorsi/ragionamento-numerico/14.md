A box contains $10$ pens in each of $5$ different colors. What is the smallest number of pens you must draw without looking to guarantee three of the same color?

- A) $6$
- B) $10$
- C) $15$
- D) $11$
- E) $3$

:::soluzione
Answer D: $11$.
:::
