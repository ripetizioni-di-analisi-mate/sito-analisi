A nurse starts her shift at 23:35 and finishes at 7:10 the next day. How long is the shift?

- A) $8$ h $25$ min
- B) $6$ h $35$ min
- C) $8$ h $35$ min
- D) $7$ h $45$ min
- E) $7$ h $35$ min

:::soluzione
Answer E: $7$ h $35$ min.
:::
