Five colleagues arrive at the office one at a time. Marta arrives before Nadia, Olga after Nadia, Piero before Marta, and Rita after Piero but before Marta. Who arrives last?

- A) Olga
- B) Nadia
- C) Marta
- D) Rita
- E) Piero

:::soluzione
Answer A: Olga.
:::
