What is the negation of the statement "Either Teresa or Sandro, but not both, drives the minibus"?

- A) Neither Teresa nor Sandro drives the minibus
- B) Teresa and Sandro both drive the minibus
- C) Teresa drives the minibus if and only if Sandro does
- D) Teresa or Sandro drives the minibus
- E) Teresa does not drive the minibus or Sandro does not drive it

:::soluzione
Answer C: Teresa drives the minibus if and only if Sandro does.
:::
