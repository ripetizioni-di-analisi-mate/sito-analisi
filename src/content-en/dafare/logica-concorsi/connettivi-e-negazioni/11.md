Which of the following propositions is a contradiction?

- A) $(A \lor B) \land (\lnot A \land \lnot B)$
- B) $A \land \lnot B$
- C) $(A \Rightarrow B) \land A$
- D) $(A \land B) \lor \lnot A$
- E) $A \Leftrightarrow \lnot B$

:::soluzione
Answer A: $(A \lor B) \land (\lnot A \land \lnot B)$, because $\lnot A \land \lnot B$ is the negation of $A \lor B$.
:::
