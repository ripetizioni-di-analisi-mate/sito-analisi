Which of the following statements is equivalent to "If a customer has the membership card, they get the discount"?

- A) If a customer gets the discount, they have the membership card
- B) If a customer does not get the discount, they do not have the membership card
- C) If a customer does not have the membership card, they do not get the discount
- D) A customer has the membership card and gets the discount
- E) A customer has the membership card or gets the discount

:::soluzione
Answer B: If a customer does not get the discount, they do not have the membership card (contrapositive).
:::
