---
titolo: Deducing a conclusion with "unless"
teoria: [a-meno-che, contronominale]
parole: [unless, deduction, contrapositive]
---

An office has the rule "Service is suspended unless the connection is active." Today service is not suspended. Which of the following statements must be true?

- A) The connection is active
- B) The connection is not active
- C) It is impossible to determine whether the connection is active
- D) If the connection is active, service is suspended
- E) Service is not suspended and the connection is not active

:::soluzione
Answer A: The connection is active.
:::
