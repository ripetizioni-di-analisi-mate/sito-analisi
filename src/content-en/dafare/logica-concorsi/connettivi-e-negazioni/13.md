The statements "It is not true that Bruna booked the room but did not send the invitations" and "Bruna booked the room" are true. Which of the following must be true?

- A) Bruna did not send the invitations
- B) Bruna sent the invitations
- C) It is impossible to determine whether Bruna sent the invitations
- D) Bruna did not book the room or did not send the invitations
- E) If Bruna sent the invitations, she did not book the room

:::soluzione
Answer B: Bruna sent the invitations.
:::
