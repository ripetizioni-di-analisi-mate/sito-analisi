What is the negation of the statement "Neither Matteo nor Ester will take the professional development course"?

- A) Matteo or Ester will take the professional development course
- B) Both Matteo and Ester will take the professional development course
- C) Matteo will not take the professional development course or Ester will not take it
- D) Only one of Matteo and Ester will take the professional development course
- E) Matteo will take the professional development course and Ester will not

:::soluzione
Answer A: Matteo or Ester will take the professional development course (at least one of the two).
:::
