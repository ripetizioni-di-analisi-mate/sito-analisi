Which of the following statements is equivalent to "It is not true that Silvia and Tullio are both on duty"?

- A) Neither Silvia nor Tullio is on duty
- B) Silvia is on duty and Tullio is not
- C) Exactly one of Silvia and Tullio is on duty
- D) Silvia or Tullio is on duty
- E) If Silvia is on duty, Tullio is not on duty

:::soluzione
Answer E: If Silvia is on duty, Tullio is not on duty.
:::
