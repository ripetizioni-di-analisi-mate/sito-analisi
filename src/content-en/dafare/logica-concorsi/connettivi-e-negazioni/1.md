What is the negation of the statement "The director is on site and the application is ready"?

- A) The director is not on site and the application is not ready
- B) The director is not on site and the application is ready
- C) The director is not on site or the application is not ready
- D) The director is on site or the application is ready
- E) If the director is on site, the application is ready

:::soluzione
Answer C: The director is not on site or the application is not ready.
:::
