Suppose "Pietro competes in the race only if he has a medical certificate" is true and Pietro competes in the race. Which of the following must be true?

- A) Pietro does not have a medical certificate
- B) Pietro competes in the race and does not have a medical certificate
- C) It is impossible to determine whether Pietro has a medical certificate
- D) Pietro has a medical certificate
- E) If Pietro has a medical certificate, he does not compete in the race

:::soluzione
Answer D: Pietro has a medical certificate.
:::
