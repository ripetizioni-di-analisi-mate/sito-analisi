What is the negation of the statement "If the train is late, Ugo takes a taxi"?

- A) If the train is not late, Ugo does not take a taxi
- B) If the train is late, Ugo does not take a taxi
- C) The train is not late and Ugo takes a taxi
- D) If Ugo takes a taxi, the train is late
- E) The train is late and Ugo does not take a taxi

:::soluzione
Answer E: The train is late and Ugo does not take a taxi.
:::
