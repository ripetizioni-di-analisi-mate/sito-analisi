Which of the following statements is equivalent to "If Marina is hired, she signs the contract and attends the training course"?

- A) If Marina signs the contract and attends the training course, she is hired
- B) If Marina does not sign the contract and does not attend the training course, she is not hired
- C) If Marina is not hired, she does not sign the contract and does not attend the training course
- D) If Marina does not sign the contract or does not attend the training course, she is not hired
- E) Marina is hired, signs the contract, and attends the training course

:::soluzione
Answer D: If Marina does not sign the contract or does not attend the training course, she is not hired.
:::
