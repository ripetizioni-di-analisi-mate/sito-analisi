For a business trip, the rule is "Valeria takes either the flight or the train, but not both." Valeria does not take the train. Which of the following statements must be true?

- A) Valeria takes the flight if and only if she takes the train
- B) Valeria takes the flight
- C) Valeria does not take the flight
- D) It is impossible to determine whether Valeria takes the flight
- E) If Valeria takes the flight, she takes the train

:::soluzione
Answer B: Valeria takes the flight.
:::
