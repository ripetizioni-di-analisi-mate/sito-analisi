At a gym with $90$ members, $40$ take Pilates classes, $35$ take Zumba, and $30$ take indoor cycling. Of the members, $12$ take Pilates and Zumba, $10$ take Pilates and indoor cycling, $8$ take Zumba and indoor cycling, and $4$ take all three. The pairwise counts include those who take all three classes. How many members take only Zumba?

- A) $15$
- B) $23$
- C) $11$
- D) $35$
- E) $19$

:::soluzione
Answer E: $19$ members.
:::
