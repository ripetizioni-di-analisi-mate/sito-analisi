Of the $120$ subscribers at a newsstand, $60$ read a daily newspaper, $50$ a weekly publication, and $40$ a monthly publication. There are $20$ who read a daily and a weekly, $15$ who read a daily and a monthly, $10$ who read a weekly and a monthly, and $5$ who read all three. The pairwise counts include those who read all three publications. How many subscribers read none of the three?

- A) $15$
- B) $110$
- C) $10$
- D) $5$
- E) $0$

:::soluzione
Answer C: $10$ subscribers.
:::
