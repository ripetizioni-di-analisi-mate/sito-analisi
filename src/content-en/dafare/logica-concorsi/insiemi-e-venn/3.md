Let $A$ be the set of prime numbers less than $12$, and let $B$ be the set of odd numbers less than $12$. Which of the following is the set $A \setminus B$?

- A) $\{2\}$
- B) $\{1, 9\}$
- C) $\{3, 5, 7, 11\}$
- D) $\{1, 2, 9\}$
- E) $\emptyset$

:::soluzione
Answer A: $A \setminus B = \{2\}$.
:::
