At a company, $A$ is the set of employees who own a car and $M$ is the set of employees who own a motorcycle. Which set consists of employees who own neither a car nor a motorcycle?

- A) $\overline{A} \cup \overline{M}$
- B) $A \setminus M$
- C) $A \cup M$
- D) $\overline{A} \cap \overline{M}$
- E) $M \setminus A$

:::soluzione
Answer D: $\overline{A} \cap \overline{M}$, that is, $\overline{A \cup M}$.
:::
