Of the $90$ employees of a municipality, $52$ took a safety course, $47$ took a privacy course, and $12$ took neither. How many employees took both courses?

- A) $9$
- B) $31$
- C) $26$
- D) $78$
- E) $21$

:::soluzione
Answer E: $21$ employees.
:::
