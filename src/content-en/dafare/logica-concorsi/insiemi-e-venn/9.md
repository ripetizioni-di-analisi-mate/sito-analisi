Which description corresponds to the Venn diagram for “vehicles,” “cars,” and “motorcycles”?

- A) three nested circles
- B) two separate circles inside a third circle
- C) two overlapping circles inside a third circle
- D) three separate circles
- E) one circle inside another and a third circle separate from both

:::soluzione
Answer B: two separate circles, representing cars and motorcycles, inside the circle for vehicles.
:::
