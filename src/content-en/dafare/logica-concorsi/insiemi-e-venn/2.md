Let $A = \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6\}$. Which of the following is a subset of $A$?

- A) $\{0, 1, 2\}$
- B) $\{2, 4, 7\}$
- C) $\{6, 7\}$
- D) $\{1, 3, 5\}$
- E) $\{5, 6, 8\}$

:::soluzione
Answer D: $\{1, 3, 5\}$.
:::
