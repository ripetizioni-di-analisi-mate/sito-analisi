In a group of $48$ young people, $30$ play volleyball, $20$ play basketball, and $9$ play both sports. How many play neither sport?

- A) $41$
- B) $18$
- C) $7$
- D) $9$
- E) $28$

:::soluzione
Answer C: $7$ young people.
:::
