Each of the $100$ customers at a bank uses at least one of three services: an online account, a credit card, or an insurance policy. The online account is used by $70$ customers, the credit card by $50$, and the insurance policy by $30$. There are $10$ customers who use all three services. How many use exactly two?

- A) $50$
- B) $60$
- C) $30$
- D) $40$
- E) $20$

:::soluzione
Answer C: $30$ customers.
:::
