A school has $120$ students, of whom $54$ are boys. The bus is used by $70$ students, and $30$ girls do not use the bus. How many boys use the bus?

- A) $34$
- B) $36$
- C) $20$
- D) $24$
- E) $40$

:::soluzione
Answer A: $34$ boys.
:::
