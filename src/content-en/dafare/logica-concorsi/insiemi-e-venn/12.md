Each of the $40$ people on a trip visited at least one of the city’s three museums. The first museum was visited by $20$ people, the second by $18$, and the third by $15$. There were $7$ who visited the first and second, $6$ who visited the first and third, and $5$ who visited the second and third. The pairwise counts include those who visited all three museums. How many people visited all three?

- A) $5$
- B) $13$
- C) $0$
- D) $18$
- E) $3$

:::soluzione
Answer A: $5$ participants.
:::
