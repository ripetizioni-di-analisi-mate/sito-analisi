In a city, $65\%$ of the residents use public transportation and $55\%$ ride a bicycle. What is the smallest possible percentage who both use public transportation and ride a bicycle?

- A) $55\%$
- B) $20\%$
- C) $35.75\%$
- D) $10\%$
- E) $45\%$

:::soluzione
Answer B: $20\%$.
:::
