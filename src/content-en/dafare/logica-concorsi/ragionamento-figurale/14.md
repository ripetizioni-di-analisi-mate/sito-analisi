Identical unit cubes are stacked on a table with two rows of four positions each. The figure shows the top view, with each number giving the number of cubes in that position. How many cubes must be added so that every position holds a stack as tall as the tallest one?

::figura{tipo="matrice" colonne=4 celle="lettera:3 | lettera:1 | lettera:2 | lettera:4 | lettera:2 | lettera:0 | lettera:1 | lettera:2" titolo="Top view of the eight positions"}

- A) $9$
- B) $13$
- C) $15$
- D) $17$
- E) $32$

:::soluzione
Answer D: $17$.
:::
