Which figure completes the matrix?

::figura{tipo="matrice" celle="stella | esagono grigio | rombo pieno | esagono pieno | rombo | stella grigio | rombo grigio | stella pieno | ?"}

::figura{tipo="opzioni" celle="esagono grigio | esagono | rombo | stella | esagono pieno"}

- A) figure A
- B) figure B
- C) figure C
- D) figure D
- E) figure E

:::soluzione
Answer B: the unfilled hexagon. Each row contains one star, one hexagon, and one diamond, and one unfilled, one gray, and one solid shape.
:::
