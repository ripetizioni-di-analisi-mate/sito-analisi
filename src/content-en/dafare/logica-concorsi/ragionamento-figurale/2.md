The first four figures of a sequence are shown, and the sequence continues according to the same rule. Which figure is in eighth place?

::figura{tipo="serie" celle="rombo no | rombo grigio ne | rombo pieno se | rombo so | ?" titolo="The first four figures, with a question mark for the eighth"}

::figura{tipo="opzioni" celle="rombo grigio so | rombo pieno so | rombo grigio no | rombo so | rombo grigio se"}

- A) figure A
- B) figure B
- C) figure C
- D) figure D
- E) figure E

:::soluzione
Answer A: the gray diamond in the bottom left corner. The fill has period $3$, the position has period $4$, and there are $7 = 2 \cdot 3 + 1 = 1 \cdot 4 + 3$ steps from the first figure to the eighth.
:::
