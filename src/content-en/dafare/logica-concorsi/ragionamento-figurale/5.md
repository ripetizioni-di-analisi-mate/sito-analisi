Which figure completes the matrix?

::figura{tipo="matrice" celle="freccia pieno x1 | freccia pieno r45 x1 | freccia pieno r90 x1 | freccia pieno r45 x2 | freccia pieno r90 x2 | freccia pieno r135 x2 | freccia pieno r90 x3 | freccia pieno r135 x3 | ?"}

::figura{tipo="opzioni" celle="freccia pieno r135 x3 | freccia pieno r180 x2 | freccia pieno r90 x3 | freccia pieno r180 x3 | freccia pieno r-135 x3"}

- A) figure A
- B) figure B
- C) figure C
- D) figure D
- E) figure E

:::soluzione
Answer D: three arrows pointing down. The number of arrows equals the row number, and the arrows rotate $45°$ clockwise from one cell to the next both across the rows and down the columns.
:::
