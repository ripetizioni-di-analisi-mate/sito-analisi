Which figure continues the sequence?

::figura{tipo="serie" celle="semicerchio pieno | semicerchio r-90 | semicerchio pieno r180 | semicerchio r90 | ?"}

::figura{tipo="opzioni" celle="semicerchio | semicerchio pieno r-90 | semicerchio pieno | semicerchio pieno r90 | semicerchio grigio"}

- A) figure A
- B) figure B
- C) figure C
- D) figure D
- E) figure E

:::soluzione
Answer C: the solid semicircle with its arc at the top. At each step the figure rotates $90°$ counterclockwise and the fill alternates between solid and unfilled.
:::
