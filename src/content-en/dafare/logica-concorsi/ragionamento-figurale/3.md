Which figure continues the sequence?

::figura{tipo="serie" celle="croce x1 | croce grigio x3 | croce pieno x5 | croce x7 | ?"}

::figura{tipo="opzioni" celle="croce grigio x7 | croce pieno x9 | croce x9 | croce grigio x8 | croce grigio x9"}

- A) figure A
- B) figure B
- C) figure C
- D) figure D
- E) figure E

:::soluzione
Answer E: nine gray crosses. The number of crosses increases by $2$ at each step and the fill cycles through unfilled, gray, and solid.
:::
