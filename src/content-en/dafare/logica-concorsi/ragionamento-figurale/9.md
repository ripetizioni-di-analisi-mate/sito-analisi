Four of the following figures are rotations of the same figure. Which figure is the odd one out?

::figura{tipo="opzioni" celle="squadra; punto n | squadra specchio; punto n | squadra r90; punto e | squadra r180; punto s | squadra r-90; punto o"}

- A) figure A
- B) figure B
- C) figure C
- D) figure D
- E) figure E

:::soluzione
Answer B: figure B is the mirror image of A, while figures C, D, and E are A rotated $90°$, $180°$, and $270°$ clockwise, respectively.
:::
