How many triangles are in the figure formed by two side-by-side squares with both their diagonals drawn?

::figura{tipo="linee" segmenti="0,3,10,3;0,8,10,8;0,3,0,8;5,3,5,8;10,3,10,8;0,3,5,8;5,3,0,8;5,3,10,8;10,3,5,8" titolo="Two side-by-side squares, each with both diagonals drawn"}

- A) $8$
- B) $12$
- C) $16$
- D) $17$
- E) $18$

:::soluzione
Answer E: $18$.
:::
