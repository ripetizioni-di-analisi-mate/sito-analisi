Which word makes the following sentence coherent? “Given that the application was ___, the clerk returned it to the applicant and requested the missing documents.”

- A) complete
- B) correct
- C) urgent
- D) approved
- E) incomplete

:::soluzione
Answer E: incomplete.
:::
