---
titolo: A statement that cannot be inferred from a local news passage
teoria: [vero-falso-non-deducibile, conoscenze-personali]
parole: [reading comprehension, passage, cannot be inferred]
---

Read the following passage.

“In spring 2022, the town of Pratolungo planted $120$ trees along Station Avenue, choosing species that tolerate drought well. The town paid half the cost, and an association of merchants on the avenue paid the other half. A year after planting, none of the trees had withered.”

Based solely on the passage, which of the following statements cannot be inferred?

- A) The species planted tolerate drought well.
- B) An association of merchants paid half the cost.
- C) A year after planting, some of the trees had withered.
- D) The summer after the trees were planted, no rain fell in Pratolungo for two months.
- E) The town paid less than half the cost.

:::soluzione
Answer D: the summer after the trees were planted, no rain fell in Pratolungo for two months.
:::
