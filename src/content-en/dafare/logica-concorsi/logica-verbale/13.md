---
titolo: A statement that strengthens an argument
teoria: [rafforzare-indebolire, affermazioni-irrilevanti]
parole: [strengthen, argument, cause, speed limit]
---

Consider the following argument.

“Since the speed limit in the North neighborhood was reduced to $30$ km/h, crashes there have fallen by half. In the South neighborhood, where the limit remained at $50$ km/h, the number of crashes has stayed the same. Therefore, lowering the limit reduced crashes in the North neighborhood.”

Which of the following statements, if true, strengthens the argument?

- A) During the same period, many roads in the North neighborhood were closed to traffic.
- B) During the same period, the number of cars on the road and the condition of the roads in the North neighborhood remained unchanged.
- C) There are more schools in the South neighborhood than in the North neighborhood.
- D) The speed limit in the North neighborhood is $30$ km/h.
- E) During the same period, many residents of the North neighborhood began riding bicycles instead of driving.

:::soluzione
Answer B: during the same period, the number of cars on the road and the condition of the roads in the North neighborhood remained unchanged.
:::
