Consider the following argument.

“On Monday mornings the registry office counters are too crowded. In fact, twice as many people wait on Mondays, on average, as on other days, and the wait often exceeds an hour. The registry office should therefore also open on Monday afternoons.”

What is the main conclusion of the argument?

- A) On Monday mornings the registry office counters are too crowded.
- B) On Mondays twice as many people wait, on average, as on other days.
- C) The registry office should also open on Monday afternoons.
- D) On Monday mornings the wait often exceeds an hour.
- E) The registry office is currently closed on Monday afternoons.

:::soluzione
Answer C: the registry office should also open on Monday afternoons.
:::
