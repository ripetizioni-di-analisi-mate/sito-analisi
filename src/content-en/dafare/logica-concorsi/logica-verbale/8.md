Which expression makes the following sentence coherent? “Although the thermometer read $2$ degrees, Paolo went out ___.”

- A) in a coat
- B) wearing gloves
- C) in short sleeves
- D) wearing a scarf
- E) carrying an umbrella

:::soluzione
Answer C: in short sleeves.
:::
