Consider the following argument.

“Carla submitted her refund request within $30$ days of purchase, so she will receive the refund.”

Which of the following statements, when added to the premises, makes the conclusion necessarily true?

- A) Carla submitted her request within $30$ days of purchase.
- B) Anyone who fails to submit a request within $30$ days of purchase does not receive a refund.
- C) Many customers receive a refund.
- D) Anyone who submits a request within $30$ days of purchase receives a refund.
- E) Only those who submit a request within $30$ days of purchase receive a refund.

:::soluzione
Answer D: anyone who submits a request within $30$ days of purchase receives a refund.
:::
