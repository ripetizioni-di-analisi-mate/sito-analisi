titolo: An unstated assumption in an argument about schedules
teoria: [assunzione-implicita, prova-della-negazione, assunzione-troppo-forte]

---

Consider the following argument.

“The 7:40 train arrives in Bologna at 9:10, and the meeting at the Bologna office starts at 9:30. Therefore, if Elena takes the 7:40 train, she will arrive in time for the meeting.”

Which of the following is an unstated assumption of the argument?

- A) It takes Elena no more than $20$ minutes to get from Bologna station to the meeting venue.
- B) The 7:40 train arrives in Bologna at 9:10.
- C) The meeting lasts two hours.
- D) All of Elena’s colleagues take the 7:40 train.
- E) The 7:40 train is the fastest of the day.

:::soluzione
Answer A: it takes Elena no more than $20$ minutes to get from Bologna station to the meeting venue.
:::
