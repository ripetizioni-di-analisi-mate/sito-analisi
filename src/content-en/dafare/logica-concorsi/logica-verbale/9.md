Read the following passage.

“The Riva public swimming pool is open Monday through Saturday. A swim cap is required to enter the water, while goggles are optional. The small pool is reserved for children’s lessons on Mondays and Thursdays from 9:00 to 11:00. At all other times, it is open to everyone. Members over $65$ pay half the full price for a monthly pass.”

Based solely on the passage, which of the following statements is true?

- A) The pool is open every day of the week.
- B) Anyone without a swim cap cannot enter the water.
- C) The children’s lessons are free.
- D) The small pool is reserved for children every day from 9:00 to 11:00.
- E) Most pool users are over $65$.

:::soluzione
Answer B: anyone without a swim cap cannot enter the water.
:::
