The statements “If Sergio is on duty, Alessia does not work” and “If Alessia does not work, Pietro opens the store” are true. Which of the following must be true?

- A) If Pietro opens the store, Sergio is on duty.
- B) If Pietro opens the store, Alessia does not work.
- C) If Sergio is not on duty, Alessia works.
- D) If Pietro does not open the store, Sergio is not on duty.
- E) If Alessia does not work, Sergio is on duty.

:::soluzione
Answer D: if Pietro does not open the store, Sergio is not on duty.
:::
