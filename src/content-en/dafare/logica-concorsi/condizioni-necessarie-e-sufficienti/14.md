The statements “If Luca comes to dinner, Mara comes too” and “If Mara comes to dinner, Elena comes too” are true. Which of the following situations *cannot* occur?

- A) Luca and Elena both come to dinner.
- B) Elena comes to dinner and Luca does not.
- C) Luca comes to dinner and Elena does not.
- D) Neither Luca nor Elena comes to dinner.
- E) Mara comes to dinner and Luca does not.

:::soluzione
Answer C: Luca comes to dinner and Elena does not.
:::
