The following rule holds: “If the river rises above the warning level, the bridge is closed.” Today the bridge is closed. Which conclusion follows from the premises?

- A) Today the river rose above the warning level.
- B) Today the river did not rise above the warning level.
- C) Whenever the bridge is closed, the river has risen above the warning level.
- D) If the river does not rise above the warning level, the bridge is not closed.
- E) Whether the river rose above the warning level today cannot be determined.

:::soluzione
Answer E: whether the river rose above the warning level today cannot be determined.
:::
