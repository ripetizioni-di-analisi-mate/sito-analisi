Which of the following statements has the same meaning as “If someone is a club member, they are entitled to the discount”?

- A) Anyone who is not entitled to the discount is not a club member.
- B) Anyone who is entitled to the discount is a club member.
- C) Only club members are entitled to the discount.
- D) Anyone who is not a club member is not entitled to the discount.
- E) All and only club members are entitled to the discount.

:::soluzione
Answer A: anyone who is not entitled to the discount is not a club member.
:::
