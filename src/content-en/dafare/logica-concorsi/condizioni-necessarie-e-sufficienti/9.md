Which of the following arguments is valid?

- A) If the battery is charged, the phone turns on. The phone turns on. Therefore, the battery is charged.
- B) If the battery is charged, the phone turns on. The battery is not charged. Therefore, the phone does not turn on.
- C) If the battery is charged, the phone turns on. The phone does not turn on. Therefore, the battery is not charged.
- D) The phone turns on only if the battery is charged. The battery is charged. Therefore, the phone turns on.
- E) The phone turns on only if the battery is charged. The phone does not turn on. Therefore, the battery is not charged.

:::soluzione
Answer C: if the battery is charged, the phone turns on. The phone does not turn on. Therefore, the battery is not charged.
:::
