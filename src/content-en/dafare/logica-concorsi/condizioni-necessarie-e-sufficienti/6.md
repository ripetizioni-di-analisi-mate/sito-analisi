A company has this rule: “All and only the employees who work in the laboratory receive a lab badge.” Which of the following must be true?

- A) An employee has a lab badge if and only if they work in the laboratory.
- B) Having a badge is sufficient but not necessary for working in the laboratory.
- C) Working in the laboratory is sufficient but not necessary for having a badge.
- D) Some employee works in the laboratory without having a badge.
- E) Anyone who does not work in the laboratory has a badge.

:::soluzione
Answer A: an employee has a lab badge if and only if they work in the laboratory.
:::
