If the statement “If a student earns at least $90$ points, they receive a scholarship” is true, which of the following must be true?

- A) If a student receives a scholarship, they earned at least $90$ points.
- B) If a student does not receive a scholarship, they did not earn at least $90$ points.
- C) Some student earned at least $90$ points and does not receive a scholarship.
- D) A student receives a scholarship if and only if they earn at least $90$ points.
- E) If a student does not earn at least $90$ points, they do not receive a scholarship.

:::soluzione
Answer B: if a student does not receive a scholarship, they did not earn at least $90$ points.
:::
