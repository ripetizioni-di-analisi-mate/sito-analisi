If the statement “If Paola is on duty, the store opens at 8\:00” is true, which of the following situations *cannot* occur?

- A) The store opens at 8\:00 and Paola is not on duty.
- B) The store does not open at 8\:00 and Paola is on duty.
- C) The store opens at 8\:00 and Paola is on duty.
- D) The store does not open at 8\:00 and Paola is not on duty.
- E) Paola is not on duty.

:::soluzione
Answer B: the store does not open at 8\:00 and Paola is on duty.
:::
