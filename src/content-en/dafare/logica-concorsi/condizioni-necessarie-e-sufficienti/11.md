For a quadrilateral, what kind of condition is “it has four right angles” for “it is a square”?

- A) necessary but not sufficient
- B) sufficient but not necessary
- C) necessary and sufficient
- D) neither necessary nor sufficient
- E) sufficient, because a quadrilateral with four right angles has equal sides

:::soluzione
Answer A: necessary but not sufficient.
:::
