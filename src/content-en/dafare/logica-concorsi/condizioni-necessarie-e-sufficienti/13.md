A rule states, “Only people who work in the legal department or the technical department may access the restricted archive.” Ugo works in neither department, Rita accesses the archive, and Sonia works in the legal department. Which of the following must be true?

- A) Sonia accesses the restricted archive.
- B) Ugo does not access the restricted archive.
- C) Rita works in the legal department.
- D) Rita works in the technical department.
- E) Ugo accesses the restricted archive.

:::soluzione
Answer B: Ugo does not access the restricted archive.
:::
