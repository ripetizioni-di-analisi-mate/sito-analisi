For a positive integer $n$, what kind of condition is “the units digit of $n$ is $5$” for “$n$ is a multiple of $5$”?

- A) necessary but not sufficient
- B) necessary and sufficient
- C) sufficient but not necessary
- D) neither necessary nor sufficient
- E) necessary, because every multiple of $5$ has $5$ as its units digit

:::soluzione
Answer C: sufficient but not necessary.
:::
