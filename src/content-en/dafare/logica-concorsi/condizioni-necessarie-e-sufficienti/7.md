The statements “If Vittoria wins the race, Marco pays for dinner” and “If Marco pays for dinner, Chiara books a table at the restaurant” are true. Chiara did not book a table. Which conclusion follows from the premises?

- A) Marco pays for dinner.
- B) Vittoria won the race and Marco does not pay for dinner.
- C) Whether Vittoria won the race cannot be determined.
- D) Vittoria did not win the race.
- E) Vittoria won the race.

:::soluzione
Answer D: Vittoria did not win the race.
:::
