At an office, “All employees in accounting use the spreadsheet” is true. Marta works in the office and uses the spreadsheet. Which of the following must be true?

- A) Marta works in accounting
- B) Marta does not work in accounting
- C) Everyone who uses the spreadsheet works in accounting
- D) Some accounting employees do not use the spreadsheet
- E) None of the above statements

:::soluzione
Answer E: None of the above statements.
:::
