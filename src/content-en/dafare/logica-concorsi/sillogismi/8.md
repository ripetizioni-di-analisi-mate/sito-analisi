At an art gallery, “No painting in Room A is for sale” and “All the watercolors in the gallery are paintings in Room A” are true. Which conclusion necessarily follows?

- A) Some paintings in Room A are not watercolors
- B) All paintings in Room A are watercolors
- C) Some of the gallery's watercolors are for sale
- D) All works that are not for sale are watercolors
- E) No watercolor from the gallery is for sale

:::soluzione
Answer E. None of the gallery’s watercolors is for sale.
:::
