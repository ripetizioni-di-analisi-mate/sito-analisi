In a competitive exam, the following statements are true. “All applicants eligible for motorcycle duty have a firearms license,” “No one with a firearms license has a criminal record,” and “Some applicants have a criminal record.” Which conclusion necessarily follows?

- A) No applicant is eligible for motorcycle duty
- B) Some applicants are not eligible for motorcycle duty
- C) Some applicants with criminal records are eligible for motorcycle duty
- D) No applicant has a firearms license
- E) Everyone without a criminal record has a firearms license

:::soluzione
Answer B. Some applicants are not eligible for motorcycle duty.
:::
