A club has $30$ members. “All club members play chess” and “Some club members are retirees” are true. Which of the following statements *cannot* be true?

- A) Some retirees do not play chess
- B) Everyone who plays chess is a club member
- C) Some club members are not retirees
- D) No retiree plays chess
- E) All retirees are club members

:::soluzione
Answer D. No retiree plays chess.
:::
