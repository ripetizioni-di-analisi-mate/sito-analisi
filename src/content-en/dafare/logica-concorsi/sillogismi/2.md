What is the negation of “No applicant submitted their paper late”?

- A) All applicants submitted their papers late
- B) Some applicants did not submit their papers late
- C) At least one applicant submitted their paper late
- D) All applicants submitted their papers on time
- E) Exactly one applicant submitted their paper late

:::soluzione
Answer C. At least one applicant submitted their paper late.
:::
