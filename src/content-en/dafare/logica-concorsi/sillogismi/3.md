What is the negation of “Some municipal offices are closed on Mondays”?

- A) Some municipal offices are not closed on Mondays
- B) All municipal offices are closed on Mondays
- C) At most one municipal office is closed on Mondays
- D) No municipal office is closed on Mondays
- E) Most municipal offices are open on Mondays

:::soluzione
Answer D: No municipal office is closed on Mondays.
:::
