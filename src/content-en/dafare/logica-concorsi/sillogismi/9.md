At a sports club, “Some members take the swimming class” and “Everyone who takes the swimming class has a medical certificate” are true. Which conclusion necessarily follows?

- A) All members have a medical certificate
- B) Some members have a medical certificate
- C) Some members do not have a medical certificate
- D) Everyone who has a medical certificate takes the swimming class
- E) Everyone who takes the swimming class is a member

:::soluzione
Answer B. Some members have a medical certificate.
:::
