A library shelf has $12$ books. “All the books from that shelf are on loan” is true. Which of the following must be true?

- A) Some books from the shelf are on loan
- B) Some books from the shelf are not on loan
- C) No book on loan is from the shelf
- D) All books on loan are from the shelf
- E) Exactly one book from the shelf is on loan

:::soluzione
Answer A. Some books from the shelf are on loan.
:::
