At a supermarket, “All cashiers took the course on operating the cash register” and “Everyone who took the course on operating the cash register is listed in the training records” are true. Which conclusion necessarily follows?

- A) All cashiers are listed in the training records
- B) Everyone listed in the training records is a cashier
- C) Some of those who took the course are not cashiers
- D) Everyone who took the course is a cashier
- E) Anyone who is not a cashier is absent from the training records

:::soluzione
Answer A. All cashiers are listed in the training records.
:::
