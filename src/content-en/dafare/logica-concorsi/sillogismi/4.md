Which of the following statements is equivalent to “All employees with minor children are entitled to parental leave”?

- A) All employees entitled to parental leave have minor children
- B) No employee without minor children is entitled to parental leave
- C) No employee with minor children lacks entitlement to parental leave
- D) Some employees with minor children are not entitled to parental leave
- E) Some employees who are entitled to parental leave do not have minor children

:::soluzione
Answer C. No employee with minor children lacks entitlement to parental leave.
:::
