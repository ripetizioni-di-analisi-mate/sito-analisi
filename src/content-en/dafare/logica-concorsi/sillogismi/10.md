At an agency, “No employee working remotely uses the company parking lot” and “Some second-floor employees work remotely” are true. Which conclusion necessarily follows?

- A) No second-floor employees use company parking
- B) Some employees on the second floor use company parking
- C) Some employees on the second floor do not use company parking
- D) Everyone who does not use the company parking lot works remotely
- E) Some of those who use corporate parking work on the second floor

:::soluzione
Answer C: Some employees on the second floor do not use company parking.
:::
