What is the negation of “Every meeting room in the building is booked for Monday”?

- A) No meeting room in the building is booked for Monday
- B) At least one meeting room in the building is not booked for Monday
- C) Some meeting rooms in the building are booked for Monday
- D) Exactly one meeting room in the building is not booked for Monday
- E) Most meeting rooms in the building are not booked for Monday

:::soluzione
Answer B. At least one meeting room in the building is not booked for Monday.
:::
