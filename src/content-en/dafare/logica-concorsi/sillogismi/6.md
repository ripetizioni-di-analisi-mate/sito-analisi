Consider the $200$ applicants taking a competitive exam. The statements “Some applicants live in Lombardy” and “Some applicants do not live in Lombardy” are

- A) Contradictory, because they can neither both be true nor both be false
- B) Contrary, because they cannot both be true but can both be false
- C) Equivalent
- D) Such that the second is a consequence of the first
- E) Subcontrary, because they can both be true but cannot both be false

:::soluzione
Answer E. Subcontrary.
:::
