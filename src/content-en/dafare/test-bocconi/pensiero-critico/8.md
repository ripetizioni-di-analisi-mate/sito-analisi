parole: ["Questions with several propositions"]
---

In a library, every soundproof room offers both individual reading and headphone listening. At least one room is soundproof. All rooms offering headphone listening also open in the evening. Evaluate I ‘At least one room opens in the evening’, II ‘Every room that opens in the evening is soundproof’, and III ‘At least one room opening in the evening offers individual reading’. Which propositions are necessarily true?

- I and II only.
- II and III only.
- All three.
- I and III only.

:::soluzione

I and III only.

:::
