In one year, two school labs, A and B, carry out 20 and 80 trials respectively. A completes half its trials successfully. B completes a quarter of its own successfully. What is the success rate across the two labs combined?

- $50\%$
- $30\%$
- $37.5\%$
- $25\%$

:::soluzione

$30\%$

:::
