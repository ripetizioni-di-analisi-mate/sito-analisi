Group R completes 12 of its 30 worksheets. Group S completes 15 of its 50. Which group completed the larger proportion of its own worksheets?

- R, at $40\%$ compared with $30\%$.
- S, because it completed 15 worksheets compared with 12.
- The proportions are equal.
- The comparison cannot be determined.

:::soluzione

R, at $40\%$ compared with $30\%$.

:::
