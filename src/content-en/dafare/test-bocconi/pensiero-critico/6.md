---
titolo: "True, false or cannot be determined (1)"
teoria: ["vero-falso-indeterminato"]
parole: ["True, false or cannot be determined"]
---

A shipment is due from 4 to 6 April, inclusive. The carrier will deliver on exactly one of these dates. Evaluate I ‘Delivery will take place by 6 April’ and II ‘Delivery will take place on 5 April’.

- Neither I nor II can be determined.
- I true, II cannot be determined.
- I true, II false.
- I and II true.

:::soluzione

I true, II cannot be determined.

:::
