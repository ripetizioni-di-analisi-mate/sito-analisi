This year's policy assigns a review to shipping requests for destinations outside the province. The requirement applies only to requests filed from August onwards. Request X was filed in July and concerns a delivery outside the province. What does this rule establish for X?

- The rule prohibits a review of X.
- The rule requires X to be reviewed starting in August.
- The rule does not determine whether X will be reviewed.
- The rule requires X to be reviewed.
