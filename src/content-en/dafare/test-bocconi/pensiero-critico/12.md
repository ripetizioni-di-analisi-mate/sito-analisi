Vehicles $L,M,N$ park in three distinct bays, numbered 1 through 3. $L$ can use only bay 1 or 3. $N$ uses the bay numbered immediately after the one occupied by $M$. Which bay does $N$ occupy if $L$ uses bay 1?

- Bay 2 or bay 3.
- Bay 3.
- Bay 2.
- Bay 1.

:::soluzione

Bay 3.

:::
