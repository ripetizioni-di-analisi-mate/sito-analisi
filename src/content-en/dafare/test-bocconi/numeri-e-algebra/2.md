A subscription price rises by $15\,\%$ and then falls by $20\,\%$ from its new price. What is the overall percentage change, including its sign?

- $-12\,\%$
- $-8\,\%$
- $+8\,\%$
- $-5\,\%$

:::soluzione

$-8\,\%$

:::
