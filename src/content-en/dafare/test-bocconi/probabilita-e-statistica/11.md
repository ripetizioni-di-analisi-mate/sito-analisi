Of one hundred participants, forty attend workshop $A$, seventy attend workshop $B$, and twenty-eight attend both. A person is picked uniformly from those who do not attend $B$. What is the probability that the person attends $A$?

- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{25}$
- $\dfrac{3}{10}$
- $\dfrac{7}{10}$

:::soluzione

$\dfrac{2}{5}$

:::
