A journey has two legs. Four routes are available for the first leg, one of them scenic. Five are available for the second leg, two of them scenic. All pairings are possible. How many journeys have at least one scenic leg?

- $13$
- $9$
- $20$
- $11$

:::soluzione

$11$

:::
