One fifth of shipments are urgent. A check is applied to $\dfrac34$ of urgent shipments and $\dfrac18$ of the others. A shipment is chosen uniformly from those checked. What is the probability that it was urgent?

- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{20}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$

:::soluzione

$\dfrac{3}{5}$

:::
