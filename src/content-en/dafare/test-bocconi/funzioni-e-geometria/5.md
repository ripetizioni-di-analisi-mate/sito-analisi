A triangle has area $30\,\mathrm{cm}^2$ and base $8\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the height relative to this base?

- $\dfrac{15}{2}\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $15\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $\dfrac{15}{4}\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$

:::soluzione

$\dfrac{15}{2}\,\mathrm{cm}$

:::
