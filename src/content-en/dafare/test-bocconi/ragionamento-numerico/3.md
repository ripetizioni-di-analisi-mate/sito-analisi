A sensor measures the number of litres in a tank at three times.

| Hours from start | Volume (L) |
| --- | ---: |
| 0 | 27 |
| 3 | 51 |
| 5 | 63 |

By how many litres per hour does the average increase over the first three hours exceed that over the last two?

- $12\,\mathrm{L/h}$
- $8\,\mathrm{L/h}$
- $6\,\mathrm{L/h}$
- $2\,\mathrm{L/h}$

:::soluzione

$2\,\mathrm{L/h}$

:::
