One test rig measures $20$ turbines averaging $75\,\mathrm{W}$ and another measures $30$ turbines averaging $65\,\mathrm{W}$. What is the mean power consumption across all $50$ turbines?

- $69\,\mathrm{W}$
- $71\,\mathrm{W}$
- $68\,\mathrm{W}$
- $70\,\mathrm{W}$

:::soluzione

$69\,\mathrm{W}$

:::
