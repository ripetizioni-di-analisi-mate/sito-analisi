A repair initially costs $160\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Its $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ labour cost rises by $20\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and all other costs stay the same. By what percentage does the total repair cost increase?

- $25\%$
- $12.5\%$
- $11.1\%$
- $20\%$

:::soluzione

$12.5\%$

:::
