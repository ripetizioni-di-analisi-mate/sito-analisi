In spring, $54$ of a warehouse's $180$ shipments are urgent. In summer, $72$ of its $160$ shipments are urgent. By how many percentage points does the urgent share rise?

- 15 percentage points
- 50 percentage points
- 30 percentage points
- 18 percentage points

:::soluzione

15 percentage points

:::
