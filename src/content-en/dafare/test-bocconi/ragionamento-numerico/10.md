| Day | Food | Crafts | Total |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Saturday | 37 | ? | 64 |
| Sunday | 31 | 39 | 70 |

What is the total number of craft stall appearances, counting a stall present on both days once per day?

- $66$
- $64$
- $68$
- $39$

:::soluzione
