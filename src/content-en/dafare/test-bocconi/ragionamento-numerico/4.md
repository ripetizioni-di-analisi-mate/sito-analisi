A centre records $240$ requests in one day, including $90$ in the morning. Ten requests arrive before nine and all are resolved. Of the requests that arrive in the morning, $54$ are resolved. What percentage of requests arriving *from nine onwards in the morning* are resolved?

- $55\%$
- $45\%$
- $60\%$
- $67.5\%$

:::soluzione

$55\%$

:::
