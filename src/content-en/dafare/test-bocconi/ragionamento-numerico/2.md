A sports centre records summer enrolments. Each person chooses exactly one activity.

| Age group | Swimming | Gym |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Under 18 | 15 | 21 |
| Adults | 25 | 39 |

How many participants are adults or enrolled in swimming, including those who meet both conditions?

- $79$
- $104$
- $54$
- $64$

:::soluzione

$79$

:::
