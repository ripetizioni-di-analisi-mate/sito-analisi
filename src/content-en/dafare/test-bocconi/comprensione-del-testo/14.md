> To attend the trial day, applicants must choose one of two sessions. Marta has received confirmation of her registration. The message specifies that equipment for her chosen session will be handed out at 2 p.m., while equipment for the morning session is handed out at 9 a.m.

What can be inferred about the session Marta chose?

- Marta chose the session other than the morning one.
- Marta still has to choose between the two sessions.
- Marta has already collected the equipment at 2 p.m.
- Marta can collect equipment at either session.

:::soluzione

Marta chose the session other than the morning one.

:::
