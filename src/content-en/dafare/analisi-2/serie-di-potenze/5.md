Determine the set of convergence of the series
$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{(x - 1)^n}{n \, 2^n} .
$$

:::soluzione
$R = 2$ and the open interval is $(-1, 3)$. At $x = 3$ the harmonic series remains, which diverges; at $x = -1$ the series $\sum \dfrac{(-1)^n}{n}$ remains, which converges by the Leibniz test. Hence $E = [-1,\, 3)$.
:::
