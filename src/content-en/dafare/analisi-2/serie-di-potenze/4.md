---
titolo: Radius when the ratio has no limit
teoria: [calcolo-raggio-radice, insieme-di-convergenza]
parole: [root test, ratio without a limit, radius of convergence]
---

Determine the radius and the set of convergence of the series
$$
\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{x^n}{5^{\,n + (-1)^n}} .
$$

:::soluzione
The ratio between the coefficients oscillates between $5$ and $\dfrac{1}{125}$ and has no limit; the $n$-th root equals $5^{-1 - (-1)^n/n} \to \dfrac{1}{5}$, so $R = 5$. At $x = \pm 5$ the absolute value of the general term oscillates between $\dfrac{1}{5}$ and $5$ and does not tend to $0$, therefore $E = (-5,\, 5)$.
:::
