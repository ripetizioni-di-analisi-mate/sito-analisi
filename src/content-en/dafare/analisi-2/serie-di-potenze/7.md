Determine the radius and the set of convergence of the series
$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{x^{2n}}{n \, 25^n} .
$$

:::soluzione
With $t = \dfrac{x^2}{25} \geq 0$ the series becomes $\sum \dfrac{t^n}{n}$, which converges if and only if $t < 1$, that is, $|x| < 5$; at $x = \pm 5$ the harmonic series remains, which diverges. Hence $R = 5$ and $E = (-5,\, 5)$.
:::
