Determine the radius and the set of convergence of the series
$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{x^n}{n^2 \, 2^n} .
$$

:::soluzione
The ratio between the absolute values of the coefficients tends to $\dfrac{1}{2}$, so $R = 2$. At $x = \pm 2$ the series converges absolutely by comparison with $\sum \dfrac{1}{n^2}$, therefore $E = [-2,\, 2]$.
:::
