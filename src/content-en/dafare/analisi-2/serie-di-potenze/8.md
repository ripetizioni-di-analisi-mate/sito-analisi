Determine the radius and the set of convergence of the series
$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{n \, x^{2n+1}}{4^n} .
$$

:::soluzione
After factoring out $x$, with $t = \dfrac{x^2}{4}$ the series becomes $x \sum n \, t^n$, which converges if and only if $|t| < 1$, that is, $|x| < 2$; at $x = \pm 2$ the general term does not tend to $0$. Hence $R = 2$ and $E = (-2,\, 2)$.
:::
