---
titolo: Radius of convergence with exponential coefficients
teoria: [calcolo-raggio-rapporto, insieme-di-convergenza, comportamento-agli-estremi]
parole: [radius of convergence, ratio test, necessary condition]
---

Determine the radius and the set of convergence of the series
$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} n \, 3^n \, x^n .
$$

:::soluzione
The ratio between the coefficients tends to $3$, so $R = \dfrac{1}{3}$. At $x = \pm\dfrac{1}{3}$ the general term has absolute value $n$, which does not tend to $0$, therefore $E = \left( -\dfrac{1}{3},\, \dfrac{1}{3} \right)$.
:::
