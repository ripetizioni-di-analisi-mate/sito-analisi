Determine the radius and the set of convergence of the series
$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \left( \frac{n+2}{2n+1} \right)^{\! n} x^n .
$$

:::soluzione
The $n$-th root of the coefficient tends to $\dfrac{1}{2}$, so $R = 2$. At $x = \pm 2$ the absolute value of the general term is $\left( 1 + \dfrac{3}{2n+1} \right)^{\! n} \to e^{\frac{3}{2}} \neq 0$, therefore $E = (-2,\, 2)$.
:::
