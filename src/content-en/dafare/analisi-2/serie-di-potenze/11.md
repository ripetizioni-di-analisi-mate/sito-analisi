titolo: Expansion of an integral function
teoria: [integrazione-termine-a-termine, sviluppi-per-derivazione-integrazione, sviluppi-notevoli]
parole: [integral function, term-by-term integration, sine integral]

Determine the Maclaurin expansion of the integral function
$$
F(x) = \int_0^x \frac{\sin t}{t} \, dt ,
$$
where the integrand is extended with the value $1$ at $t = 0$.

:::soluzione
Dividing the expansion of the sine by $t$ and integrating term by term:
$$
F(x) = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^n \, x^{2n+1}}{(2n+1) \, (2n+1)!} , \qquad x \in \mathbb{R} .
$$
:::
