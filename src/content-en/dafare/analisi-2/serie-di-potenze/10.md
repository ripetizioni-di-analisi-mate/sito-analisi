Determine the Maclaurin expansion of
$$
f(x) = \frac{1}{5 + x}
$$
and its region of validity.

:::soluzione
Factoring out the $5$ and using the geometric series with ratio $-\dfrac{x}{5}$:
$$
\frac{1}{5 + x} = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^n \, x^n}{5^{n+1}} , \qquad |x| < 5 .
$$
:::
