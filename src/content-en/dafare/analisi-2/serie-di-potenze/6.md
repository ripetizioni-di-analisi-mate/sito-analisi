Determine the set of convergence of the series
$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{(x + 2)^n}{n^2 \, 3^n} .
$$

:::soluzione
$R = 3$ and the open interval, centered at $-2$, is $(-5, 1)$. At both endpoints the absolute value of the general term is $\dfrac{1}{n^2}$, so the series converges absolutely and $E = [-5,\, 1]$.
:::
