Determine the Maclaurin expansion of
$$
f(x) = x \, e^{-2x}
$$
and its region of validity.

:::soluzione
From the exponential series with $x$ replaced by $-2x$, multiplied by $x$:
$$
x \, e^{-2x} = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{(-2)^n \, x^{n+1}}{n!} , \qquad x \in \mathbb{R} .
$$
:::
