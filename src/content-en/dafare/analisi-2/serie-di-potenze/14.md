Compute, using Maclaurin expansions, the limit
$$
\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{e^{x^2} - 1 - x^2}{x^4} .
$$

:::soluzione
Since $e^{x^2} = 1 + x^2 + \dfrac{x^4}{2} + o(x^4)$, the numerator equals $\dfrac{x^4}{2} + o(x^4)$ and the limit is $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
:::
