Compute the sum of the series
$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{n}{5^n} .
$$

:::soluzione
From the equality $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} n \, x^n = \frac{x}{(1-x)^2}$ evaluated at $x = \dfrac{1}{5}$:
$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{n}{5^n} = \frac{5}{16} .
$$
:::
