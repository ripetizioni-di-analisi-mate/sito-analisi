titolo: Somma di una serie a segni alterni

---

Compute the sum of the series
$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^{n+1}}{n \, 2^n} .
$$

:::soluzione
It is the expansion of $\ln(1 + x)$ evaluated at $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$, interior to the region of validity:
$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^{n+1}}{n \, 2^n} = \ln \frac{3}{2} .
$$
:::
