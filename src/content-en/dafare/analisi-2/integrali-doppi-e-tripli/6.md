---
titolo: Double integral in polar coordinates over the disk
teoria: [coordinate-polari]
parole: [polar coordinates, disk, Jacobian]
---

Compute the double integral
$$
\iint_D \frac{1}{1 + x^2 + y^2}\, dx\, dy ,
\qquad D = \{ (x, y) : x^2 + y^2 \leq 1 \} .
$$

:::soluzione
In polar coordinates the integral equals
$$
2\pi \int_0^1 \frac{\rho}{1 + \rho^2}\, d\rho = \pi \ln 2 .
$$
:::
