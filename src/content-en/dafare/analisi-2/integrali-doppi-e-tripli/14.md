---
titolo: Triple integral in spherical coordinates
teoria: [coordinate-sferiche]
parole: [spherical coordinates, ball, distance from the origin]
---

Compute the triple integral
$$
\iiint_E \left( x^2 + y^2 + z^2 \right) dx\, dy\, dz ,
\qquad E = \{ (x, y, z) : x^2 + y^2 + z^2 \leq 4 \} .
$$

:::soluzione
In spherical coordinates the function is $r^2$ and the volume element is $r^2 \sin\varphi\, dr\, d\varphi\, d\theta$, so
$$
2\pi \cdot 2 \cdot \int_0^2 r^4 \, dr = \frac{128\pi}{5} .
$$
:::
