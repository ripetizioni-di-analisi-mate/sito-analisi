---
titolo: Baricentro di un quarto di cerchio
teoria: [massa-e-baricentro, coordinate-polari]
parole: [baricentro, lamina omogenea, quarto di cerchio]
---

Find the center of mass of the homogeneous lamina
$$
D = \{ (x, y) : x^2 + y^2 \leq 1, \ x \geq 0, \ y \geq 0 \} .
$$

:::soluzione
The area is $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$ and the integral of $x$ over $D$ equals $\dfrac{1}{3}$; by the symmetry with respect to the bisector $y = x$ the two coordinates coincide, so the center of mass is
$$
\left( \frac{4}{3\pi},\ \frac{4}{3\pi} \right) .
$$
:::
