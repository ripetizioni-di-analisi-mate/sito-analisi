---
titolo: Iterated integral with a change of order
teoria: [scambio-ordine, riduzione-domini-normali]
parole: [changing the order of integration, non-elementary antiderivative]
---

Compute the iterated integral
$$
\int_0^1 \left( \int_x^1 \cos( y^2 )\, dy \right) dx .
$$

:::soluzione
Changing the order of integration, the integral becomes
$$
\int_0^1 y \cos( y^2 )\, dy = \frac{\sin 1}{2} .
$$
:::
