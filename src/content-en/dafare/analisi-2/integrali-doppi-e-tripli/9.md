---
titolo: Double integral over an ellipse
teoria: [cambiamento-di-variabili, coordinate-polari]
parole: [ellipse, elliptical coordinates, Jacobian]
---

Compute the double integral
$$
\iint_D \left( \frac{x^2}{9} + \frac{y^2}{4} \right) dx\, dy ,
\qquad D = \left\{ (x, y) : \frac{x^2}{9} + \frac{y^2}{4} \leq 1 \right\} .
$$

:::soluzione
With the transformation $x = 3u$, $y = 2v$, whose Jacobian is $6$, the integral becomes $6$ times the integral of $u^2 + v^2$ over the unit disk, so
$$
6 \cdot \frac{\pi}{2} = 3\pi .
$$
:::
