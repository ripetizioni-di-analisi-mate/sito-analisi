---
titolo: Triple integral over a tetrahedron
teoria: [integrazione-per-fili]
parole: [triple integral, tetrahedron, integration by vertical lines]
---

Compute the triple integral
$$
\iiint_T z \, dx\, dy\, dz ,
$$
where $T$ is the tetrahedron bounded by the coordinate planes and by the plane $x + y + z = 2$.

:::soluzione
Integrating by vertical lines, with $z$ from $0$ to $2 - x - y$ above the triangle $0 \leq y \leq 2 - x$, $0 \leq x \leq 2$, we obtain
$$
\iiint_T z \, dx\, dy\, dz = \frac{2}{3} .
$$
:::
