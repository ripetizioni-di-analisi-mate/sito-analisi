---
titolo: Double integral over a parabolic domain
teoria: [dominio-normale, riduzione-domini-normali]
parole: [normal domain, parabola, reduction formulas]
---

Compute the double integral
$$
\iint_D (x + y)\, dx\, dy ,
\qquad D = \{ (x, y) : 0 \leq x \leq 1, \ 0 \leq y \leq x^2 \} .
$$

:::soluzione
The inner integral in $y$ equals $x^3 + \dfrac{x^4}{2}$, so
$$
\int_0^1 \left( x^3 + \frac{x^4}{2} \right) dx = \frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{10} = \frac{7}{20} .
$$
:::
