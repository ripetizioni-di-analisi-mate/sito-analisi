---
titolo: Triple integral by slices over a paraboloid
teoria: [integrazione-per-strati]
parole: [triple integral, integration by slices, paraboloid, cross-sections]
---

Compute the triple integral
$$
\iiint_E z \, dx\, dy\, dz ,
\qquad E = \{ (x, y, z) : x^2 + y^2 \leq z \leq 4 \} .
$$

:::soluzione
The cross-section at height $z \in [0, 4]$ is a disk of area $\pi z$, so
$$
\int_0^4 z \cdot \pi z \, dz = \frac{64\pi}{3} .
$$
:::
