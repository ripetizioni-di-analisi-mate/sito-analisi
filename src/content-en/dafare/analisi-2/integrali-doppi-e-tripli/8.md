titolo: Area with a change of variables

Compute the area of the region in the first quadrant bounded by the hyperbolas $xy = 1$ and $xy = 3$ and by the lines $y = x$ and $y = 2x$.

:::soluzione
With the variables $u = xy$ and $v = \dfrac{y}{x}$ we have $dx\, dy = \dfrac{du\, dv}{2v}$, so
$$
\operatorname{Area} = \int_1^3 \!\! \int_1^2 \frac{dv\, du}{2v} = \ln 2 .
$$
:::
