---
titolo: Double integral over a rectangle with separated variables
teoria: [teorema-di-fubini, variabili-separate]
parole: [double integral, rectangle, separated variables]
---

Compute the double integral
$$
\iint_R y \sin x \, dx\, dy ,
\qquad R = [0, \pi] \times [0, 1] .
$$

:::soluzione
The function has separated variables on a rectangle, so the integral is the product
$$
\int_0^{\pi} \sin x \, dx \cdot \int_0^1 y \, dy = 2 \cdot \frac{1}{2} = 1 .
$$
:::
