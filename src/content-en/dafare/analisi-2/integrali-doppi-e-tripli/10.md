---
titolo: Double integral with a symmetry
teoria: [simmetrie, area-e-volume]
parole: [symmetry, odd function, symmetric domain, disk]
---

Compute the double integral
$$
\iint_D \left( x^3 + 3 \right) dx\, dy ,
\qquad D = \{ (x, y) : x^2 + y^2 \leq 1 \} .
$$

:::soluzione
The disk is symmetric with respect to the $y$-axis and $x^3$ is odd in $x$, so its integral is zero; what remains is the constant $3$ integrated over the area $\pi$, therefore the result is $3\pi$.
:::
