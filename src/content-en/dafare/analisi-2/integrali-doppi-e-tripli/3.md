---
titolo: Double integral over a triangle
teoria: [dominio-normale, riduzione-domini-normali]
parole: [normal domain, triangle, reduction formulas]
---

Compute the double integral
$$
\iint_T x \, dx\, dy ,
$$
where $T$ is the triangle with vertices $(0, 0)$, $(1, 0)$ and $(1, 1)$.

:::soluzione
The triangle is the normal domain $0 \leq x \leq 1$, $0 \leq y \leq x$, so
$$
\int_0^1 x \cdot x \, dx = \frac{1}{3} .
$$
:::
