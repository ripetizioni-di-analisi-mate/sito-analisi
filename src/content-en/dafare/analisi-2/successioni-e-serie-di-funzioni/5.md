---
titolo: Uniform convergence only away from the origin
teoria: [criterio-del-sup, convergenza-uniforme]
parole: [uniform convergence, half-line, sup criterion]
---

Study the pointwise and uniform convergence of the sequence of functions
$$
f_n(x) = \frac{n x}{1 + n^2 x^2}
$$
on $\mathbb{R}$ and on the half-lines $[\delta, +\infty)$ with $\delta > 0$.

:::soluzione
The pointwise limit is $f = 0$ on $\mathbb{R}$. The maximum of the absolute value is at $x = \pm\dfrac{1}{n}$ and equals $\dfrac{1}{2}$ for every $n$, so the convergence is not uniform on $\mathbb{R}$; on $[\delta, +\infty)$, for $n > \dfrac{1}{\delta}$, the function $f_n$ is decreasing, so $M_n = f_n(\delta) \to 0$ and the convergence is uniform.
:::
