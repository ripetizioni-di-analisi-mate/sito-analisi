---
titolo: Sup criterion with an interior maximum
teoria: [criterio-del-sup, convergenza-uniforme]
parole: [sup criterion, maximum, uniform convergence]
---

Study the pointwise and uniform convergence on $[0, +\infty)$ of the sequence of functions
$$
f_n(x) = x^2 e^{-n x} .
$$

:::soluzione
The pointwise limit is $f = 0$. The maximum of $f_n$ is at $x = \dfrac{2}{n}$ and equals
$$
M_n = \frac{4}{n^2 e^2} \xrightarrow[n \to +\infty]{} 0 ,
$$
so the convergence is uniform on $[0, +\infty)$.
:::
