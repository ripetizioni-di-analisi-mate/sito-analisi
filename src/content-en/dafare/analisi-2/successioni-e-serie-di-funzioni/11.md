---
titolo: Total convergence of a trigonometric series
teoria: [convergenza-totale, totale-implica-uniforme]
parole: [total convergence, Weierstrass test, trigonometric series]
---

Study the total convergence on $\mathbb{R}$ of the series of functions
$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{\cos(n x)}{n^2 + 1} .
$$

:::soluzione
Since $\left| \dfrac{\cos(nx)}{n^2 + 1} \right| \leq \dfrac{1}{n^2 + 1}$ and the series $\sum \dfrac{1}{n^2+1}$ converges, the series converges totally on $\mathbb{R}$, hence uniformly, and the sum is a continuous function.
:::
