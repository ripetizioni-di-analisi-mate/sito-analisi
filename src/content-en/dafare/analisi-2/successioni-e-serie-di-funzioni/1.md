Determine the pointwise limit on $[0, +\infty)$ of the sequence of functions
$$
f_n(x) = \frac{1}{1 + x^n} .
$$

:::soluzione
Distinguishing cases according to the base $x$:
$$
f(x) =
\begin{cases}
1 & \text{if } 0 \leq x < 1 \\[4pt]
\dfrac{1}{2} & \text{if } x = 1 \\[8pt]
0 & \text{if } x > 1
\end{cases}
$$
:::
