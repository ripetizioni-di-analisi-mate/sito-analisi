---
titolo: Uniform convergence on a bounded interval
teoria: [convergenza-uniforme, criterio-del-sup]
parole: [uniform convergence, sup criterion, bounded interval]
---

Study the pointwise and uniform convergence on $[0, 1]$ of the sequence of functions
$$
f_n(x) = x \, e^{-x/n} .
$$

:::soluzione
The pointwise limit is $f(x) = x$. Since $|f_n(x) - x| = x \left( 1 - e^{-x/n} \right) \leq 1 - e^{-1/n} \to 0$, the convergence is uniform on $[0, 1]$.
:::
