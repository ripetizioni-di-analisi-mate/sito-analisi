---
titolo: Invalid interchange of limit and integral
teoria: [scambio-limite-integrale, convergenza-puntuale]
parole: [interchange of limit and integral, counterexample, non-uniform convergence]
---

Compute
$$
\lim_{n \to +\infty} \int_0^1 n^2 x \,(1 - x)^n \, dx
$$
and deduce that the convergence of the sequence $f_n(x) = n^2 x (1 - x)^n$ is not uniform on $[0, 1]$.

:::soluzione
$\displaystyle\int_0^1 n^2 x (1 - x)^n dx = \frac{n^2}{(n+1)(n+2)} \to 1$. The pointwise limit is $f = 0$, so the integral of the limit equals $0 \neq 1$; since the theorem on passing to the limit under the integral sign would give equality, the convergence is not uniform.
:::
