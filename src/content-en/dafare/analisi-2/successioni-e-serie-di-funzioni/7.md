---
titolo: Convergenza uniforme senza convergenza delle derivate
teoria: [criterio-del-sup, derivazione-per-successioni]
parole: [derivazione per successioni, derivate, convergenza uniforme]
---

Verify that the sequence of functions
$$
f_n(x) = \frac{\sin(n x)}{\sqrt{n}}
$$
converges uniformly on $\mathbb{R}$ and that the sequence of the derivatives evaluated at $0$ diverges.

:::soluzione
$\sup_{\mathbb{R}} |f_n| = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{n}} \to 0$, so $f_n \to 0$ uniformly; $f_n'(0) = \sqrt{n} \to +\infty$, so the derivatives do not converge and the differentiation theorem for sequences does not apply, because uniform convergence of the derivatives does not hold.
:::
