---
titolo: Total convergence on a half-line
teoria: [convergenza-totale, criterio-del-sup]
parole: [total convergence, sup via the derivative, half-line]
---

Study the total convergence on $[0, +\infty)$ of the series of functions
$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} x^2 e^{-n x} .
$$

:::soluzione
The maximum of $x^2 e^{-nx}$ on $[0, +\infty)$ is at $x = \dfrac{2}{n}$ and equals $M_n = \dfrac{4}{n^2 e^2}$; since $\sum \dfrac{4}{e^2 n^2}$ converges, the series converges totally on $[0, +\infty)$.
:::
