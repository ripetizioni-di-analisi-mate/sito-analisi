---
titolo: Serie uniformemente ma non totalmente convergente
teoria: [convergenza-uniforme-serie, convergenza-totale]
parole: [convergenza uniforme, convergenza totale, criterio di Leibniz, resto]
---

Prove that the series of functions
$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^n}{2n + x^2}
$$
converges uniformly on $\mathbb{R}$ but not normally.

:::soluzione
For every $x$ the series converges by the alternating series test and the remainder satisfies $|f(x) - s_N(x)| \leq \dfrac{1}{2(N+1) + x^2} \leq \dfrac{1}{2N + 2} \to 0$, so the convergence is uniform on $\mathbb{R}$. However, $\sup_{\mathbb{R}} \left| \dfrac{(-1)^n}{2n + x^2} \right| = \dfrac{1}{2n}$ and $\sum \dfrac{1}{2n}$ diverges, so the convergence is not normal.
:::
