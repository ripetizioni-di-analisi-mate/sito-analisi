---
titolo: Term-by-term differentiation
teoria: [derivazione-termine-a-termine, convergenza-totale]
parole: [term-by-term differentiation, series of derivatives, class C1]
---

Prove that the function
$$
f(x) = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{\sin(n x)}{n^4}
$$
is of class $C^1$ on $\mathbb{R}$ and compute its derivative.

:::soluzione
The series converges totally on $\mathbb{R}$ with $M_n = \dfrac{1}{n^4}$ and the series of derivatives converges totally with $M_n = \dfrac{1}{n^3}$, so by the term-by-term differentiation theorem $f \in C^1(\mathbb{R})$ and
$$
f'(x) = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{\cos(n x)}{n^3} .
$$
:::
