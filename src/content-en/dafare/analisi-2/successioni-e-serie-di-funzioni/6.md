---
titolo: Discontinuous limit and non-uniform convergence
teoria: [convergenza-puntuale, continuita-del-limite-uniforme]
parole: [discontinuous limit, continuity of the uniform limit, n-th root]
---

Determine the pointwise limit on $[0, 1]$ of the sequence of functions
$$
f_n(x) = x^{1/n}
$$
and establish whether the convergence is uniform.

:::soluzione
The pointwise limit equals $0$ at $x = 0$ and $1$ for $0 < x \leq 1$. Since the $f_n$ are continuous and the limit is discontinuous, the convergence is not uniform on $[0, 1]$.
:::
