---
titolo: Limit of integrals with uniform convergence
teoria: [scambio-limite-integrale, convergenza-uniforme]
parole: [passing to the limit under the integral sign, uniform convergence]
---

Compute
$$
\lim_{n \to +\infty} \int_1^2 \frac{e^{x/n}}{x} \, dx .
$$

:::soluzione
On $[1, 2]$ we have $\left| \dfrac{e^{x/n}}{x} - \dfrac{1}{x} \right| \leq e^{2/n} - 1 \to 0$, so the convergence is uniform and
$$
\lim_{n \to +\infty} \int_1^2 \frac{e^{x/n}}{x}\,dx = \int_1^2 \frac{dx}{x} = \ln 2 .
$$
:::
