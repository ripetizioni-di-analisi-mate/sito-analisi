---
titolo: Continuity at the origin
teoria: [continuita-due-variabili, confronto-due-variabili, maggiorazioni-utili]
parole: [continuity in two variables, piecewise-defined functions, upper bounds]
---

Study the continuity on $\mathbb{R}^2$ of the function
$$
f(x, y) =
\begin{cases}
\dfrac{y^3}{x^2 + y^2} & (x, y) \neq (0, 0) \\[8pt]
0 & (x, y) = (0, 0)
\end{cases}
$$

:::soluzione
Away from the origin $f$ is continuous because it is a quotient of polynomials with nonzero denominator. At the origin, since $\dfrac{y^2}{x^2 + y^2} \leq 1$, we have $|f(x, y)| \leq |y| \to 0 = f(0, 0)$, so $f$ is continuous on all of $\mathbb{R}^2$.
:::
