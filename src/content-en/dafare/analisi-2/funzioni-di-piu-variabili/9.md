---
titolo: Limit with upper bounds
teoria: [confronto-due-variabili, maggiorazioni-utili]
parole: [limits in two variables, upper bounds, comparison theorem]
---

Compute the limit
$$
\lim_{(x, y) \to (0, 0)} \frac{x^4 + y^4}{x^2 + y^2} .
$$

:::soluzione
Since $x^4 + y^4 \leq (x^2 + y^2)^2$, the expression lies between $0$ and $x^2 + y^2 \to 0$, so the limit equals $0$.
:::
