---
titolo: Dominio con radice quadrata
teoria: [dominio-naturale, limitati-e-compatti]
parole: [dominio, radice quadrata, disco chiuso, compatto]
---

Determine and describe the domain of
$$
f(x, y) = \sqrt{9 - x^2 - y^2} .
$$

:::soluzione
The condition $9 - x^2 - y^2 \geq 0$ is equivalent to $x^2 + y^2 \leq 9$: the domain is the closed disk centered at the origin with radius $3$, which is a compact set.
:::
