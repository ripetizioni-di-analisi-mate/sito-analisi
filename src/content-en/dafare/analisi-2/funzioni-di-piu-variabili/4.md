Determine the level curves of
$$
f(x, y) = x^2 + y^2 + 2y .
$$

:::soluzione
The equation $x^2 + y^2 + 2y = k$ is equivalent to $x^2 + (y + 1)^2 = k + 1$. For $k > -1$ the level curve is the circle with center $(0, -1)$ and radius $\sqrt{k + 1}$; for $k = -1$ it is the single point $(0, -1)$; for $k < -1$ it is empty.
:::
