---
titolo: Limit with a standard limit
teoria: [coordinate-polari]

---

Compute the limit
$$
\lim_{(x, y) \to (0, 0)} \frac{e^{\,x^2 + y^2} - 1}{x^2 + y^2} .
$$

:::soluzione
With $t = x^2 + y^2 = \rho^2 \to 0^+$ the expression becomes $\dfrac{e^t - 1}{t} \to 1$; it does not contain $\theta$, so the limit equals $1$.
:::
