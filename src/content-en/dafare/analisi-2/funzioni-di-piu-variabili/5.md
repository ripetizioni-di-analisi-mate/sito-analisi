---
titolo: Level curves of an exponential function
teoria: [curve-di-livello]
parole: [level curves, exponential, hyperbolas]
---

Determine the level curves of
$$
f(x, y) = e^{\,xy} .
$$

:::soluzione
For $k \leq 0$ the level set is empty. For $k > 0$ the equation is equivalent to $xy = \ln k$: if $k = 1$ the level curve is the union of the two axes, if $k \neq 1$ it is the rectangular hyperbola $xy = \ln k$, with branches in the first and third quadrants for $k > 1$ and in the second and fourth for $0 < k < 1$.
:::
