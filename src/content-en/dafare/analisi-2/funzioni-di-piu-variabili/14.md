---
titolo: Existence of maximum and minimum on a compact set
teoria: [weierstrass-piu-variabili, limitati-e-compatti, continuita-elementari-due-variabili]
parole: [Weierstrass theorem, compact set, absolute maximum and minimum]
---

Determine whether the function
$$
f(x, y) = x^2 - xy + y^2
$$
attains an absolute maximum and minimum on the set $K = \{ (x, y) \in \mathbb{R}^2 : x^2 + y^2 \leq 1 \}$.

:::soluzione
Yes: $f$ is a polynomial, so it is continuous on $\mathbb{R}^2$, and $K$ is closed and bounded, so it is compact. Both hypotheses of the Weierstrass theorem are satisfied, therefore $f$ attains an absolute maximum and minimum on $K$.
:::
