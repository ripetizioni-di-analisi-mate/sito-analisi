---
titolo: Limite con le coordinate polari
teoria: [coordinate-polari, confronto-due-variabili]
parole: [limiti in due variabili, coordinate polari, maggiorazione uniforme]
---

Compute the limit
$$
\lim_{(x, y) \to (0, 0)} \frac{x y^2}{x^2 + y^2} .
$$

:::soluzione
In polar coordinates the expression becomes $\rho \cos\theta \sin^2\theta$, bounded in absolute value by $g(\rho) = \rho$, which does not depend on $\theta$ and tends to $0$ as $\rho \to 0^+$, so the limit equals $0$.
:::
