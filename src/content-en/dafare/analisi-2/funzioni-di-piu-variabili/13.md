---
titolo: Prolungamento per continuità
teoria: [prolungamento-continuita, coordinate-polari]
parole: [prolungamento per continuità, limite notevole del seno, origine]
---

Determine whether the function
$$
f(x, y) = \frac{\sin\!\left( 3(x^2 + y^2) \right)}{x^2 + y^2},
\qquad (x, y) \neq (0, 0),
$$
can be extended continuously to the origin.

:::soluzione
With $t = 3(x^2 + y^2) \to 0^+$ the expression becomes $3 \cdot \dfrac{\sin t}{t} \to 3$; it does not depend on $\theta$, so the limit equals $3$ and the function extends continuously by setting $\tilde{f}(0, 0) = 3$.
:::
