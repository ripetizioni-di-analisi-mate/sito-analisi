titolo: Dominio con logaritmo
teoria: [dominio-naturale, aperti-e-chiusi]

---

Determine and describe the domain of
$$
f(x, y) = \ln(x + y - 1) .
$$

:::soluzione
The condition $x + y - 1 > 0$ is equivalent to $y > 1 - x$: the domain is the open half-plane above the line $y = 1 - x$, excluding the line. It is an open, connected, unbounded set.
:::
