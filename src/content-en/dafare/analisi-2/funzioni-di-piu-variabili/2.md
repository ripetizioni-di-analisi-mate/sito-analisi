---
titolo: Domain with a logarithm
teoria: [dominio-naturale, aperti-e-chiusi]
parole: [domain, logarithm, half-plane, open set]
---

Determine and describe the domain of
$$
f(x, y) = \ln(x + y - 1) .
$$

:::soluzione
The condition $x + y - 1 > 0$ is equivalent to $y > 1 - x$: the domain is the open half-plane above the line $y = 1 - x$, line excluded. It is an open, connected, unbounded set.
:::
