titolo: Limite lungo gli assi
teoria: [teorema-delle-restrizioni, limite-due-variabili]

---

Determine whether the following limit exists
$$
\lim_{(x, y) \to (0, 0)} \frac{3x^2 - y^2}{x^2 + y^2} .
$$

:::soluzione
The limit does not exist: the restriction to the $x$-axis is constantly $3$, the restriction to the $y$-axis is constantly $-1$, so two restrictions produce different limits.
:::
