---
titolo: Dominio con più condizioni a sistema
teoria: [dominio-naturale, aperti-e-chiusi]
parole: [dominio, sistema di condizioni, parabola, circonferenza]
---

Determine and describe the domain of
$$
f(x, y) = \frac{\sqrt{y - x^2}}{x^2 + y^2 - 4} .
$$

:::soluzione
The system of conditions is
$$
\begin{cases}
y \geq x^2 \\[4pt]
x^2 + y^2 \neq 4
\end{cases}
$$
so the domain is the region above the parabola $y = x^2$, including the parabola, with the points of the circle centered at the origin with radius $2$ that lie in this region removed.
:::
