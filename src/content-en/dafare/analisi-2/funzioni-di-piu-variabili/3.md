---
titolo: Domain with several conditions in a system
teoria: [dominio-naturale, aperti-e-chiusi]
parole: [domain, system of conditions, parabola, circle]
---

Determine and describe the domain of
$$
f(x, y) = \frac{\sqrt{y - x^2}}{x^2 + y^2 - 4} .
$$

:::soluzione
The conditions, as a system, are
$$
\begin{cases}
y \geq x^2 \\[4pt]
x^2 + y^2 \neq 4
\end{cases}
$$
so the domain is the region above the parabola $y = x^2$, parabola included, without the points of the circle with center the origin and radius $2$ that belong to it.
:::
