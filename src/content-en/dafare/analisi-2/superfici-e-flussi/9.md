---
titolo: Solenoidal field with a parameter
teoria: [divergenza, proprieta-divergenza-rotore]
parole: [solenoidal field, zero divergence, parameter]
---

Find $a \in \mathbb{R}$ such that the field
$$
\mathbf{F}(x, y, z) = \bigl( 2xy,\; a y^2,\; 6yz \bigr)
$$
is solenoidal.

:::soluzione
Since $\operatorname{div} \mathbf{F} = 2y + 2ay + 6y = (8 + 2a)\,y$, the divergence is identically zero if and only if
$$
a = -4 .
$$
:::
