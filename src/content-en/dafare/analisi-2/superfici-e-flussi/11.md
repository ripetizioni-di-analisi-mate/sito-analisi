---
titolo: Flux out of the sphere with the divergence theorem
teoria: [teorema-della-divergenza]
parole: [divergence theorem, outward flux, sphere]
---

Compute the outward flux of the field
$$
\mathbf{F}(x, y, z) = \bigl( x^3,\; y^3,\; z^3 \bigr)
$$
through the sphere $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 1$.

:::soluzione
We have $\operatorname{div} \mathbf{F} = 3 (x^2 + y^2 + z^2)$, so in spherical coordinates
$$
\Phi = 3 \int_0^{2\pi} \!\! \int_0^{\pi} \!\! \int_0^1 r^2 \cdot r^2 \sin\varphi \; dr \, d\varphi \, d\theta = \frac{12\pi}{5} .
$$
:::
