---
titolo: Surface integral over the cylinder
teoria: [integrale-di-superficie]
parole: [surface integral, cylinder, parametrization]
---

Compute
$$
\iint_\Sigma y^2 \; d\sigma,
$$
where $\Sigma$ is the lateral surface of the cylinder $x^2 + y^2 = 1$ with $0 \leq z \leq 2$.

:::soluzione
With the parametrization $(\cos\theta, \sin\theta, z)$ the area element is $d\sigma = d\theta \, dz$ and
$$
\iint_\Sigma y^2 \, d\sigma = \int_0^2 \!\! \int_0^{2\pi} \sin^2\theta \; d\theta \, dz = 2\pi .
$$
:::
