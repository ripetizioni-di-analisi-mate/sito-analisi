Find the normal vector and the tangent plane to the surface
$$
\mathbf{r}(u, v) = \bigl( u^2,\; v,\; uv \bigr)
$$
at the point corresponding to $(u_0, v_0) = (1, 1)$.

:::soluzione
From $\mathbf{r}_u(1,1) = (2, 0, 1)$ and $\mathbf{r}_v(1,1) = (0, 1, 1)$ we obtain $\mathbf{N} = (-1,\; -2,\; 2)$; the tangent plane at $P_0 = (1, 1, 1)$ has equation
$$
x + 2y - 2z = 1 .
$$
:::
