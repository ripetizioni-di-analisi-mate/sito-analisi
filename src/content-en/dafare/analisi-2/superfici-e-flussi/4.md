titolo: Area of a spherical cap
teoria: [area-di-una-superficie]

Compute the area of the portion of the sphere $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 9$ with $z \geq \dfrac{3}{2}$.

:::soluzione
In spherical coordinates the condition becomes $\cos\varphi \geq \dfrac{1}{2}$, that is, $\varphi \in \left[ 0, \dfrac{\pi}{3} \right]$, so
$$
\operatorname{Area} = 2\pi \cdot 9 \int_0^{\pi/3} \sin\varphi \; d\varphi = 9\pi .
$$
:::
