Compute with Stokes' theorem the circulation of the field
$$
\mathbf{F}(x, y, z) = \bigl( 2y,\; 3x,\; z^2 \bigr)
$$
along the circle $x^2 + y^2 = 9$ in the plane $z = 0$, traversed counterclockwise as seen from above.

:::soluzione
We have $\operatorname{rot} \mathbf{F} = (0,\; 0,\; 1)$; choosing as the surface the disk $x^2 + y^2 \leq 9$ with normal $\mathbf{k}$,
$$
\oint_\gamma \mathbf{F} \cdot d\mathbf{r} = \operatorname{Area} = 9\pi .
$$
:::
