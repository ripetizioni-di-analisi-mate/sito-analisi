Compute the area of the portion of the paraboloid
$$
z = x^2 + y^2
$$
with $z \leq 4$.

:::soluzione
In polar coordinates
$$
\operatorname{Area} = 2\pi \int_0^2 \sqrt{1 + 4\rho^2}\; \rho \, d\rho = \frac{\pi}{6} \bigl( 17\sqrt{17} - 1 \bigr) .
$$
:::
