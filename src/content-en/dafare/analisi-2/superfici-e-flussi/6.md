---
titolo: Integrale di superficie sulla sfera
teoria: [integrale-di-superficie]
parole: [integrale di superficie, sfera, simmetria]
---

Compute
$$
\iint_\Sigma z^2 \; d\sigma,
$$
where $\Sigma$ is the sphere $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 1$.

:::soluzione
By symmetry the integrals of $x^2$, $y^2$ and $z^2$ over the sphere coincide, so the integral is one third of the integral of $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 1$, that is, one third of the area:
$$
\iint_\Sigma z^2 \, d\sigma = \frac{4\pi}{3} .
$$
:::
