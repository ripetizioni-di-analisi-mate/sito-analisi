Write the equation of the tangent plane to the graph of
$$
f(x, y) = x\, e^{y}
$$
at the point with coordinates $(x_0, y_0) = (2, 0)$.

:::soluzione
Since $f(2, 0) = 2$, $f_x(2, 0) = 1$ and $f_y(2, 0) = 2$, the tangent plane has equation
$$
z = x + 2y .
$$
:::
