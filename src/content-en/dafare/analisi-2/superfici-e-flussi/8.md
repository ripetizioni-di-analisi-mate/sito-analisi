Compute the divergence and the curl of the field
$$
\mathbf{F}(x, y, z) = \bigl( x^2 z,\; xyz,\; -z^2 y \bigr)
$$
at the point $(1, 1, 1)$.

:::soluzione
We have $\operatorname{div} \mathbf{F} = 2xz + xz - 2zy = 3xz - 2yz$ and $\operatorname{curl} \mathbf{F} = \bigl( -z^2 - xy,\; x^2,\; yz \bigr)$, so at the point $(1,1,1)$
$$
\operatorname{div} \mathbf{F} = 1, \qquad \operatorname{curl} \mathbf{F} = (-2,\; 1,\; 1) .
$$
:::
