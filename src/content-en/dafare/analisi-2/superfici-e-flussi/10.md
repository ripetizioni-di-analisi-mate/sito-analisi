---
titolo: Flux through a disk
teoria: [flusso, superficie-orientata]
parole: [flux, disk, direct computation]
---

Compute the flux of the field
$$
\mathbf{F}(x, y, z) = \bigl( 0,\; 0,\; x^2 + y^2 \bigr)
$$
through the disk $x^2 + y^2 \leq 4$ in the plane $z = 3$, oriented upward.

:::soluzione
On the disk $\mathbf{F} \cdot \mathbf{n} = x^2 + y^2$, so in polar coordinates
$$
\Phi = \int_0^{2\pi} \!\! \int_0^2 \rho^3 \, d\rho \, d\theta = 8\pi .
$$
:::
