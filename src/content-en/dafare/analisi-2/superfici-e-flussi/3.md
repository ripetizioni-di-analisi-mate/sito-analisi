titolo: Area of a portion of a plane

Compute the area of the portion of the plane
$$
z = 4 - 2x - 2y
$$
contained in the first octant ($x \geq 0$, $y \geq 0$, $z \geq 0$).

:::soluzione
The projection onto the $xy$-plane is the triangle $x \geq 0$, $y \geq 0$, $x + y \leq 2$, of area $2$; the integrand is $\sqrt{1 + 4 + 4} = 3$, so
$$
\operatorname{Area} = 3 \cdot 2 = 6 .
$$
:::
