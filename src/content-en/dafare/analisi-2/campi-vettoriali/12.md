---
titolo: Conservative field in space
teoria: [campo-irrotazionale, irrotazionale-su-semplicemente-connesso, potenziale-per-integrazione-successiva]
parole: [curl, field in space, potential]
---

Determine whether the field
$$
F(x, y, z) = \bigl( 2xz, \; 3y^2 z^2, \; x^2 + 2y^3 z \bigr)
$$
is conservative on $\mathbb{R}^3$ and, if so, determine its potentials.

:::soluzione
The curl is zero and $\mathbb{R}^3$ is simply connected, hence the field is conservative. The potentials are
$$
U(x, y, z) = x^2 z + y^3 z^2 + k, \qquad k \in \mathbb{R} .
$$
:::
