Compute with Green's formula the area of the quadrilateral with vertices $(0, 0)$, $(4, 1)$, $(3, 4)$, $(1, 3)$, listed counterclockwise.

:::soluzione
On the segment from the vertex $(x_i, y_i)$ to the vertex $(x_{i+1}, y_{i+1})$ the integral of $\dfrac{1}{2} (x \, dy - y \, dx)$ equals $\dfrac{1}{2} (x_i \, y_{i+1} - x_{i+1} \, y_i)$; adding over the four sides the area is
$$
\frac{1}{2} \, (0 + 13 + 5 + 0) = 9 .
$$
:::
