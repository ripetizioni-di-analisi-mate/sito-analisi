Compute the work of the field
$$
F(x, y) = \bigl( y^2, \; 2xy \bigr)
$$
along the curve $r(t) = \left( t^3, \; \sin \dfrac{\pi t}{2} \right)$, $t \in [0, 1]$.

:::soluzione
The field is conservative on $\mathbb{R}^2$ with potential $U(x, y) = x y^2$. The curve goes from $(0, 0)$ to $(1, 1)$, hence the work equals
$$
U(1, 1) - U(0, 0) = 1 .
$$
:::
