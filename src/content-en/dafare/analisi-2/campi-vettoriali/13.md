titolo: Lavoro nello spazio con il potenziale
teoria: [lavoro-campo-conservativo, campo-irrotazionale]

Compute the work of the field
$$
F(x, y, z) = \bigl( yz, \; xz, \; xy \bigr)
$$
along the curve $r(t) = \bigl( 1 + t, \; 1 + t^2, \; 1 + t^3 \bigr)$, $t \in [0, 1]$.

:::soluzione
The field is conservative on $\mathbb{R}^3$ with potential $U(x, y, z) = xyz$. The curve goes from $(1, 1, 1)$ to $(2, 2, 2)$, hence the work equals
$$
U(2, 2, 2) - U(1, 1, 1) = 8 - 1 = 7 .
$$
:::
