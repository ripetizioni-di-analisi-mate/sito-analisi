Compute the circulation of the field
$$
F(x, y) = \bigl( x - y, \; x + y \bigr)
$$
along the circle $x^2 + y^2 = 9$ traversed counterclockwise.

:::soluzione
By Green's theorem the integrand of the double integral is $1 - (-1) = 2$, hence the circulation is twice the area of the disk of radius $3$:
$$
2 \cdot 9\pi = 18\pi .
$$
:::
