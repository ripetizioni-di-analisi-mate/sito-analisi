---
titolo: Rotation field on the half-plane
teoria: [semplicemente-connesso, irrotazionale-su-semplicemente-connesso, lavoro-campo-conservativo]
parole: [rotation field, half-plane, potential, arctangent, work]
---

Compute the work of the field
$$
F(x, y) = \left( \frac{-y}{x^2 + y^2}, \; \frac{x}{x^2 + y^2} \right)
$$
along any piecewise regular curve contained in the half-plane $x > 0$, from $(1, 0)$ to $(1, 1)$.

:::soluzione
On the half-plane $x > 0$, which is simply connected, the irrotational field is conservative with potential $U(x, y) = \arctan \dfrac{y}{x}$. The work equals
$$
U(1, 1) - U(1, 0) = \frac{\pi}{4} .
$$
:::
