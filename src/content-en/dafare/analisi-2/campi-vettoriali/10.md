Compute with Green's formula the area of the region enclosed by the ellipse
$$
r(t) = \bigl( 5 \cos t, \; 2 \sin t \bigr), \quad t \in [0, 2\pi] .
$$

:::soluzione
With the formula $\operatorname{area} = \dfrac{1}{2} \displaystyle\oint (x \, dy - y \, dx)$ the integrand equals $10$ at every point, hence the area is
$$
\frac{10}{2} \cdot 2\pi = 10\pi .
$$
:::
