---
titolo: Potential with an exponential
teoria: [campo-irrotazionale, irrotazionale-su-semplicemente-connesso, potenziale-per-integrazione-successiva]
parole: [potential, conservative field, exponential]
---

Determine whether the field
$$
F(x, y) = \bigl( 2x e^y, \; x^2 e^y - 2y \bigr)
$$
is conservative on $\mathbb{R}^2$ and, if so, determine its potentials.

:::soluzione
The cross derivatives both equal $2x e^y$ and the domain is simply connected, hence the field is conservative. The potentials are
$$
U(x, y) = x^2 e^y - y^2 + k, \qquad k \in \mathbb{R} .
$$
:::
