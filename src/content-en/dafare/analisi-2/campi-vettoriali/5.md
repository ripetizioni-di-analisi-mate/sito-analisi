---
titolo: Work by direct computation
teoria: [lavoro-e-circuitazione, campo-irrotazionale]
parole: [work, direct computation, segment, line integral]
---

Compute the work of the field
$$
F(x, y) = \bigl( y, \; -x \bigr)
$$
along the segment from $(1, 0)$ to $(0, 1)$.

:::soluzione
The field is not conservative and the computation is direct: with the parametrization $r(t) = (1 - t, \, t)$, $t \in [0, 1]$, the integrand equals $-1$ and the work is
$$
\int_0^1 (-1) \, dt = -1 .
$$
:::
