---
titolo: Checking the necessary condition
teoria: [campo-irrotazionale, conservativo-implica-irrotazionale]
parole: [irrotational field, necessary condition, cross derivatives]
---

Determine whether the field
$$
F(x, y) = \bigl( x^2 y, \; x y^2 \bigr)
$$
is conservative on $\mathbb{R}^2$.

:::soluzione
The field is not conservative: the derivative of $F_1$ with respect to $y$ equals $x^2$, the derivative of $F_2$ with respect to $x$ equals $y^2$, and the two functions do not coincide on $\mathbb{R}^2$, hence the necessary condition of irrotationality is not satisfied.
:::
