Compute the circulation of the field
$$
F(x, y) = \bigl( x^2, \; xy \bigr)
$$
along the boundary of the triangle with vertices $(0, 0)$, $(2, 0)$, $(0, 2)$, traversed counterclockwise.

:::soluzione
By Green's theorem the circulation is the double integral of $y$ over the triangle, described by $0 \leq x \leq 2$ and $0 \leq y \leq 2 - x$:
$$
\int_0^2 \frac{(2 - x)^2}{2} \, dx = \frac{4}{3} .
$$
:::
