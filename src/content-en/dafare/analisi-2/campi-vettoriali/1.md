Determine whether the field
$$
F(x, y) = \bigl( 3x^2 + 2y, \; 2x - 5y^4 \bigr)
$$
is conservative on $\mathbb{R}^2$ and, if so, determine its potentials.

:::soluzione
The cross derivatives both equal $2$ and $\mathbb{R}^2$ is simply connected, hence the field is conservative. The potentials are
$$
U(x, y) = x^3 + 2xy - y^5 + k, \qquad k \in \mathbb{R} .
$$
:::
