Compute the circulation of the field
$$
F(x, y) = \bigl( xy, \; x \bigr)
$$
along the circle $x^2 + y^2 = 1$ traversed counterclockwise.

:::soluzione
By Green's theorem the circulation is the double integral of $1 - x$ over the unit disk. The integral of $x$ over the disk is zero by symmetry and the integral of $1$ is the area, hence the circulation equals
$$
\pi .
$$
:::
