---
titolo: Radial field on the plane minus the origin
teoria: [semplicemente-connesso, campo-irrotazionale, caratterizzazione-conservativi]
parole: [domain that is not simply connected, radial field, potential, logarithm]
---

Determine whether the field
$$
F(x, y) = \left( \frac{x}{x^2 + y^2}, \; \frac{y}{x^2 + y^2} \right)
$$
is conservative on $A = \mathbb{R}^2 \setminus \{(0, 0)\}$ and, if so, determine a potential.

:::soluzione
The field is conservative on the whole of $A$, even though $A$ is not simply connected: the function
$$
U(x, y) = \frac{1}{2} \ln (x^2 + y^2)
$$
is defined on $A$ and its gradient is $F$ at every point.
:::
