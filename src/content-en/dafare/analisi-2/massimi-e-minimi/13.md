---
titolo: Scatola senza coperchio di superficie minima
teoria: [impostazione-problemi-geometrici]
parole: [ottimizzazione, volume assegnato, superficie minima, scatola]
---

A box with a square base and no lid must have volume $4\ \mathrm{dm}^3$. Find the dimensions that minimize the surface area (base and four walls).

:::soluzione
With a base of side length $x$ and height $h = \dfrac{4}{x^2}$, the surface area is $S(x) = x^2 + \dfrac{16}{x}$, which is minimized at $x = 2$: the box has a $2 \times 2\ \mathrm{dm}$ base, height $1\ \mathrm{dm}$ and minimum surface area $12\ \mathrm{dm}^2$.
:::
