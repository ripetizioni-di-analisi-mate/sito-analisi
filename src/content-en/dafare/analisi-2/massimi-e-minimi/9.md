Find the maximum and minimum of the function
$$
f(x, y) = 2x + y
\qquad \text{con il vincolo } x^2 + y^2 = 20 .
$$

:::soluzione
The Lagrange system gives the candidates $(4, 2)$ and $(-4, -2)$: the constrained maximum is $10$ at $(4, 2)$, the constrained minimum is $-10$ at $(-4, -2)$.
:::
