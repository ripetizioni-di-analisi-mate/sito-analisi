Find the absolute maximum and minimum of the function
$$
f(x, y) = x + y - xy
$$
on the triangle with vertices $(0, 0)$, $(2, 0)$, $(0, 2)$, boundary included.

:::soluzione
The absolute maximum is $2$, attained at the vertices $(2, 0)$ and $(0, 2)$; the absolute minimum is $0$, attained at the origin. The critical point $(1, 1)$ lies on the hypotenuse and gives the value $1$.
:::
