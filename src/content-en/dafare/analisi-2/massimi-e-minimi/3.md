Find and classify the critical points of the function
$$
f(x, y) = (x - y)^2 + y^3 - 3y .
$$

:::soluzione
The critical points are $(1, 1)$ and $(-1, -1)$. At $(1, 1)$ we have $\det H = 12 > 0$ and $f_{xx} = 2 > 0$, hence it is a local minimum point with $f(1, 1) = -2$; at $(-1, -1)$ we have $\det H = -12 < 0$, hence it is a saddle point.
:::
