---
titolo: Rettangolo di perimetro minimo
teoria: [impostazione-problemi-geometrici]
parole: [ottimizzazione, perimetro minimo, area assegnata, rettangolo]
---

Among the rectangles with area $36$, find the one with minimum perimeter.

:::soluzione
With the constraint $xy = 36$ the perimeter is $P(x) = 2x + \dfrac{72}{x}$ for $x > 0$, with minimum at $x = 6$: the rectangle sought is the square with side length $6$, with perimeter $24$.
:::
