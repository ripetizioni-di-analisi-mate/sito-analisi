titolo: Rectangle of minimum perimeter
teoria: [impostazione-problemi-geometrici]
parole: [optimization, minimum perimeter, given area, rectangle]
---

Among the rectangles with area $36$, find the one with minimum perimeter.

:::soluzione
With the constraint $xy = 36$ the perimeter is $P(x) = 2x + \dfrac{72}{x}$ for $x > 0$, with minimum at $x = 6$: the rectangle sought is the square of side $6$, with perimeter $24$.
:::
