---
titolo: Critical points of a second-degree polynomial
teoria: [punto-critico, criterio-della-hessiana]

---

Find and classify the critical points of the function
$$
f(x, y) = x^2 + xy + y^2 - 3x .
$$

:::soluzione
The only critical point is $(2, -1)$; since $\det H = 3 > 0$ and $f_{xx} = 2 > 0$, it is a local minimum point, with
$$
f(2, -1) = -3 .
$$
:::
