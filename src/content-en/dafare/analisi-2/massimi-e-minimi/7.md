Find the absolute maximum and minimum of the function
$$
f(x, y) = x^2 + y^2 - 2y
\qquad \text{on the disk } x^2 + y^2 \leq 4 .
$$

:::soluzione
The absolute minimum is $-1$, attained at the interior critical point $(0, 1)$; the absolute maximum is $8$, attained at $(0, -2)$ on the boundary, where $f = 4 - 2y$.
:::
