titolo: Degenerate Hessian and completing the square

Find and classify the critical points of the function
$$
f(x, y) = x^2 + y^4 - 2x .
$$

:::soluzione
The only critical point is $(1, 0)$, with a degenerate Hessian. Writing $f(x, y) = (x - 1)^2 + y^4 - 1 \geq -1$ we conclude that $(1, 0)$ is an absolute minimum point, with
$$
f(1, 0) = -1 .
$$
:::
