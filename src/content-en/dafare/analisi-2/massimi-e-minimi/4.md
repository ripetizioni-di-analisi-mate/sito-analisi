teoria: [punto-critico, caso-degenere]

Find all the critical points of the function
$$
f(x, y) = x^2 y^2
$$
and determine which of them are extremum points.

:::soluzione
The critical points are exactly the points of the two axes, where the Hessian is degenerate. Since $f(x, y) \geq 0$ and $f = 0$ exactly on the axes, every point of the axes is a non-strict absolute minimum point.
:::
