---
titolo: Minimum distance of a curve from the origin
teoria: [moltiplicatori-di-lagrange, sistema-di-lagrange]

---

Find the points of the curve $xy = 4$ at minimum distance from the origin and compute that distance.

:::soluzione
Minimizing $x^2 + y^2$ subject to the constraint $xy = 4$ we find the points $(2, 2)$ and $(-2, -2)$, with minimum distance
$$
d = \sqrt{8} = 2\sqrt{2} .
$$
A maximum does not exist, since the curve is unbounded.
:::
