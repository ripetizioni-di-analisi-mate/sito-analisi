Find the absolute maximum and minimum of the function
$$
f(x, y) = x^2 - y^2
\qquad \text{on } K = [-1, 2] \times [0, 1] .
$$

:::soluzione
The absolute maximum is $4$, attained at $(2, 0)$; the absolute minimum is $-1$, attained at $(0, 1)$. All the candidates lie on the boundary.
:::
