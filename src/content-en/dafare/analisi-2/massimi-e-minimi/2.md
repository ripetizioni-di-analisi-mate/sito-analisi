---
titolo: Critical points of a fourth-degree polynomial
teoria: [punto-critico, criterio-della-hessiana]

---

Find and classify the critical points of the function
$$
f(x, y) = x^4 + y^4 - 4xy .
$$

:::soluzione
There are three critical points: $(0, 0)$ is a saddle point ($\det H = -16 < 0$), while $(1, 1)$ and $(-1, -1)$ are local minimum points with
$$
f(1, 1) = f(-1, -1) = -2 .
$$
:::
