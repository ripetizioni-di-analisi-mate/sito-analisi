Find the maximum and minimum of the function
$$
f(x, y, z) = x + y + z
\qquad \text{on the sphere } x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 12 .
$$

:::soluzione
The candidates are $(2, 2, 2)$ and $(-2, -2, -2)$: the constrained maximum is $6$, the constrained minimum is $-6$.
:::
