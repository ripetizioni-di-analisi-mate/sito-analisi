titolo: Hessian matrix at a point

Compute the Hessian matrix of
$$
f(x, y) = x^4 + y^4 - 4xy
$$
at the point $(1, 1)$.

:::soluzione
$$
H_f(1, 1) =
\begin{pmatrix}
12 & -4 \\[4pt]
-4 & 12
\end{pmatrix}.
$$
:::
