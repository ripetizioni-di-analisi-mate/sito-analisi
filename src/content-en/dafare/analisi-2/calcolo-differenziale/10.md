---
titolo: Derivative along a curve
teoria: [regola-della-catena]
parole: [chain rule, derivative along a curve, composite function]
---

Given $F(t) = f\bigl(x(t), y(t)\bigr)$ with
$$
f(x, y) = x^2 + y^2, \qquad x(t) = e^{t}, \qquad y(t) = t^2,
$$
compute $F'(0)$.

:::soluzione
$F'(t) = 2x\,e^{t} + 2y \cdot 2t = 2e^{2t} + 4t^3$, so
$$
F'(0) = 2.
$$
:::
