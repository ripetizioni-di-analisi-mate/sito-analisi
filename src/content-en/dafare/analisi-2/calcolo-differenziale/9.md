---
titolo: Horizontal tangent plane
teoria: [piano-tangente, gradiente]
parole: [horizontal tangent plane, zero gradient, critical point]
---

Determine the point of the graph of
$$
f(x, y) = x^2 + y^2 - 4x + 6y
$$
at which the tangent plane is horizontal, and write the equation of the plane.

:::soluzione
The gradient $(2x - 4,\; 2y + 6)$ vanishes only at $(2, -3)$, where $f(2,-3) = -13$: the point of the graph is $(2, -3, -13)$ and the tangent plane is
$$
z = -13.
$$
:::
