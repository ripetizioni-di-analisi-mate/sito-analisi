---
titolo: Gradient of a polynomial at a point
teoria: [derivata-parziale, gradiente]
parole: [gradient, partial derivatives, polynomial]
---

Compute the gradient of
$$
f(x, y) = x^2 y^3 + 3x - y
$$
at the point $(1, 1)$.

:::soluzione
$f_x = 2xy^3 + 3$ and $f_y = 3x^2 y^2 - 1$, so
$$
\nabla f(1, 1) = (5,\, 2).
$$
:::
