---
titolo: Partial derivatives of a composite function
teoria: [derivata-parziale]
parole: [partial derivatives, composite function, sine]
---

Compute the partial derivatives of
$$
f(x, y) = \sin\!\left( x^2 y \right).
$$

:::soluzione
$$
f_x = 2xy \cos\!\left( x^2 y \right), \qquad f_y = x^2 \cos\!\left( x^2 y \right).
$$
:::
