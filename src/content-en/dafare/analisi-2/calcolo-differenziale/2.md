---
titolo: Gradient with an exponential
teoria: [derivata-parziale, gradiente]
parole: [gradient, partial derivatives, exponential]
---

Compute the gradient of
$$
f(x, y) = x\, e^{y} + y^2
$$
at the point $(2, 0)$.

:::soluzione
$f_x = e^{y}$ and $f_y = x\,e^{y} + 2y$, so
$$
\nabla f(2, 0) = (1,\, 2).
$$
:::
