---
titolo: Chain rule with a given gradient
teoria: [regola-della-catena, gradiente]
parole: [chain rule, intermediate variables, given gradient]
---

Let $F(s, t) = f\bigl(st,\; s - t\bigr)$, with $f = f(u, v)$ differentiable and
$$
\nabla f(2, 1) = (3, -2).
$$
Compute $\dfrac{\partial F}{\partial s}$ and $\dfrac{\partial F}{\partial t}$ at the point $(s, t) = (2, 1)$.

:::soluzione
At the point $(s, t) = (2, 1)$ the intermediate variables equal $(u, v) = (2, 1)$, so
$$
\frac{\partial F}{\partial s} = t\,f_u + f_v = 1, \qquad
\frac{\partial F}{\partial t} = s\,f_u - f_v = 8.
$$
:::
