---
titolo: Mixed second derivative
teoria: [derivate-parziali-seconde, teorema-di-schwarz]
parole: [mixed derivative, second derivatives, Schwarz theorem]
---

Compute the mixed second derivative $f_{xy}$ of
$$
f(x, y) = x^3 e^{2y}
$$
and verify that it coincides with $f_{yx}$.

:::soluzione
$$
f_{xy} = f_{yx} = 6x^2 e^{2y},
$$
in agreement with the Schwarz theorem, since $f$ is of class $C^2$ on $\mathbb{R}^2$.
:::
