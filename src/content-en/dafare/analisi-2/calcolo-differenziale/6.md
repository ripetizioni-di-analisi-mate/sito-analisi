---
titolo: Directional derivative with the gradient formula
teoria: [derivata-direzionale, formula-del-gradiente]
parole: [directional derivative, gradient formula, unit vector]
---

Compute the directional derivative of
$$
f(x, y) = x^2 + xy
$$
at the point $(2, 1)$ along the unit vector $\mathbf{v} = \left( \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2},\, \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \right)$.

:::soluzione
$\nabla f(2, 1) = (5,\, 2)$, so
$$
D_{\mathbf{v}} f(2, 1) = \frac{7\sqrt{2}}{2}.
$$
:::
