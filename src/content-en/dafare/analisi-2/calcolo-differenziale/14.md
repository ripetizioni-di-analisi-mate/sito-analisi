Write the second-order Taylor polynomial of
$$
f(x, y) = e^{x} \ln(1 + y)
$$
centered at $(0, 0)$.

:::soluzione
$$
T_2(x, y) = y + xy - \frac{y^2}{2}.
$$
:::
