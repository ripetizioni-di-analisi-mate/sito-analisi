---
titolo: Differentiability with the absolute value
teoria: [differenziabilita]
parole: [differentiability, absolute value, definition, comparison]
---

Determine whether the function
$$
f(x, y) = x\,|y|
$$
is differentiable at $(0, 0)$.

:::soluzione
On the axes the function is zero, so $f_x(0,0) = f_y(0,0) = 0$; the ratio in the definition is $\dfrac{h\,|k|}{\sqrt{h^2 + k^2}}$ and its absolute value is bounded above by $\dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{h^2 + k^2} \to 0$, therefore $f$ is differentiable at $(0,0)$ with zero differential.
:::
