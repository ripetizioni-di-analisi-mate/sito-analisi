Determine the direction of steepest ascent of
$$
f(x, y) = x^2 y
$$
at the point $(1, 2)$ and the maximum value of the directional derivative at that point.

:::soluzione
$\nabla f(1, 2) = (4,\, 1)$, so the direction of steepest ascent is the unit vector
$$
\mathbf{v} = \left( \frac{4}{\sqrt{17}},\; \frac{1}{\sqrt{17}} \right)
$$
and the maximum value of the directional derivative is $\sqrt{17}$.
:::
