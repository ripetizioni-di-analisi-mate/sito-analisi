---
titolo: Work along an arc of a parabola
teoria: [integrale-seconda-specie]
parole: [work, vector field, second kind, parabola]
---

Compute the work of the field $F(x, y) = (y^2,\; x)$ along the curve
$$
\gamma(t) = (t,\; t^2), \qquad t \in [0, 1].
$$

:::soluzione
One has $F(\gamma(t)) \cdot \gamma'(t) = t^4 \cdot 1 + t \cdot 2t = t^4 + 2t^2$, hence
$$
\int_\gamma F \cdot d\gamma = \int_0^1 \left( t^4 + 2t^2 \right) dt = \frac{1}{5} + \frac{2}{3} = \frac{13}{15}.
$$
:::
