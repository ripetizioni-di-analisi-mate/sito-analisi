---
titolo: Length of a parametric curve
teoria: [formula-della-lunghezza]
parole: [length of a curve, plane curve]
---

Compute the length of the curve
$$
\gamma(t) = (\cos t + t\sin t,\; \sin t - t\cos t), \qquad t \in [0, \pi].
$$

:::soluzione
One has $\gamma'(t) = (t\cos t,\; t\sin t)$, hence $\lVert \gamma'(t) \rVert = t$ for $t \geq 0$ and
$$
L(\gamma) = \int_0^\pi t \, dt = \frac{\pi^2}{2}.
$$
:::
