---
titolo: Integral of the first kind on a helix
teoria: [integrale-prima-specie]
parole: [line integral, first kind, helix]
---

Compute the line integral
$$
\int_\gamma z^2 \, ds, \qquad \gamma(t) = (\cos t,\; \sin t,\; t), \quad t \in [0, \pi].
$$

:::soluzione
One has $\lVert \gamma'(t) \rVert = \sqrt{2}$ and $z = t$ along the curve, hence
$$
\int_\gamma z^2 \, ds = \sqrt{2} \int_0^\pi t^2 \, dt = \frac{\sqrt{2}\, \pi^3}{3}.
$$
:::
