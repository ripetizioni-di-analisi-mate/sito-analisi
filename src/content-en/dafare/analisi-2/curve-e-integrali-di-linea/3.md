---
titolo: Tangent line to a parametrized ellipse
teoria: [vettore-tangente]
parole: [tangent line, ellipse, parametric curve]
---

Write the Cartesian equation of the tangent line to the curve
$$
\gamma(t) = (2\cos t,\; \sin t), \qquad t \in [0, 2\pi],
$$
at the point $\gamma\!\left(\dfrac{\pi}{4}\right)$.

:::soluzione
The point of tangency is $\left(\sqrt{2},\; \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right)$ and the tangent vector is $\gamma'\!\left(\dfrac{\pi}{4}\right) = \left(-\sqrt{2},\; \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right)$, with slope $-\dfrac{1}{2}$. The tangent line has equation
$$
y = -\frac{x}{2} + \sqrt{2}.
$$
:::
