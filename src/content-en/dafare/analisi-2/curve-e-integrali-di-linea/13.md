---
titolo: Lavoro lungo una curva chiusa
teoria: [integrale-seconda-specie, orientazione-e-segno]
parole: [lavoro, curva chiusa, circonferenza, orientazione]
---

Compute the work of the field $F(x, y) = (-y,\; x)$ along the circle with center at the origin and radius $2$, traversed counterclockwise.

:::soluzione
With $\gamma(t) = (2\cos t,\; 2\sin t)$, $t \in [0, 2\pi]$, one has $F(\gamma(t)) \cdot \gamma'(t) = 4\sin^2 t + 4\cos^2 t = 4$, hence
$$
\int_\gamma F \cdot d\gamma = \int_0^{2\pi} 4 \, dt = 8\pi.
$$
On the same circle traversed clockwise the work equals $-8\pi$.
:::
