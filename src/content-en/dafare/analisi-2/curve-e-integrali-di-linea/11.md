---
titolo: Center of mass of a homogeneous arc
teoria: [massa-e-baricentro]
parole: [center of mass, homogeneous wire, quarter circle]
---

Determine the center of mass of the homogeneous wire lying along the quarter circle
$$
\gamma(t) = (\cos t,\; \sin t), \qquad t \in \left[ 0, \frac{\pi}{2} \right].
$$

:::soluzione
The length is $L = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$ and
$$
\bar{x} = \frac{2}{\pi} \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \cos t \, dt = \frac{2}{\pi},
$$
with $\bar{y} = \bar{x}$ by symmetry with respect to the bisector. The center of mass is $G = \left( \dfrac{2}{\pi},\; \dfrac{2}{\pi} \right)$.
:::
