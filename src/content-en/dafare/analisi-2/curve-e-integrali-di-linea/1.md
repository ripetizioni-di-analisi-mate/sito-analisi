---
titolo: Tangent vector to a plane curve
teoria: [curva-regolare, vettore-tangente]
parole: [tangent vector, regular curve]
---

Verify that the curve
$$
\gamma(t) = (t^2 + 1,\; 2t^3), \qquad t \in [1, 2],
$$
is regular and compute the tangent vector at the point $\gamma(1)$.

:::soluzione
One has $\gamma'(t) = (2t,\; 6t^2)$, which does not vanish for $t \in [1, 2]$ because $2t \geq 2$, hence the curve is regular. The tangent vector at $\gamma(1) = (2, 2)$ is
$$
\gamma'(1) = (2,\; 6).
$$
:::
