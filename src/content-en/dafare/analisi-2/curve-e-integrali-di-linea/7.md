teoria: [ascissa-curvilinea]

Determine the arc length function of the curve
$$
\gamma(t) = (3t,\; 4t), \qquad t \in [0, 1],
$$
and write the arc length parametrization.

:::soluzione
One has $\lVert \gamma'(t) \rVert = \sqrt{9 + 16} = 5$, hence $s(t) = 5t$ and $t(s) = \dfrac{s}{5}$. The arc length parametrization is
$$
\tilde{\gamma}(s) = \left( \frac{3s}{5},\; \frac{4s}{5} \right), \qquad s \in [0, 5].
$$
:::
