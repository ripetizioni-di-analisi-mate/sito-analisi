---
titolo: Integrale di prima specie su un segmento
teoria: [integrale-prima-specie]
parole: [integrale di linea, prima specie, segmento]
---

Compute the line integral
$$
\int_\gamma xy \, ds,
$$
where $\gamma$ is the segment from the origin to the point $(1, 1)$.

:::soluzione
With the parametrization $\gamma(t) = (t, t)$, $t \in [0, 1]$, one has $\lVert \gamma'(t) \rVert = \sqrt{2}$ and
$$
\int_\gamma xy \, ds = \int_0^1 t^2 \sqrt{2} \, dt = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{3}.
$$
:::
