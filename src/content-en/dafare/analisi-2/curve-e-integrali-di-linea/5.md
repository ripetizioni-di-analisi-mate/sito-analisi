---
titolo: Length of a helix
teoria: [formula-della-lunghezza]

Compute the length of the helix
$$
\gamma(t) = \left( 2\cos t,\; 2\sin t,\; \sqrt{5}\, t \right), \qquad t \in [0, 4\pi].
$$

:::soluzione
One has $\lVert \gamma'(t) \rVert = \sqrt{4\sin^2 t + 4\cos^2 t + 5} = 3$ for every $t$, hence
$$
L(\gamma) = 3 \cdot 4\pi = 12\pi.
$$
:::
