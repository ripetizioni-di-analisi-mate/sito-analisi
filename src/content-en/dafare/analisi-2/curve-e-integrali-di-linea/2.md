---
titolo: Regularity of a curve
teoria: [curva-regolare, curva-regolare-a-tratti]
parole: [regular curve, piecewise regular, trace]
---

Determine whether the curve
$$
\gamma(t) = (t^3,\; t^6), \qquad t \in [-1, 1],
$$
is regular or piecewise regular, and describe its trace.

:::soluzione
One has $\gamma'(t) = (3t^2,\; 6t^5)$, which vanishes for $t = 0$, an interior point of the interval, hence the curve is not regular; it is piecewise regular with the partition $-1 < 0 < 1$. The trace is the arc of the parabola $y = x^2$ with $x \in [-1, 1]$, since $t^6 = (t^3)^2$: the trace has no singular points even though the parametrization is not regular.
:::
