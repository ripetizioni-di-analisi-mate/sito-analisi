A wire lies along the segment
$$
\gamma(t) = (3t,\; 4t), \qquad t \in [0, 1],
$$
with linear density $\delta(x, y) = x$. Compute its mass.

:::soluzione
One has $\lVert \gamma'(t) \rVert = 5$ and $\delta(\gamma(t)) = 3t$, hence
$$
m = \int_\gamma \delta \, ds = \int_0^1 3t \cdot 5 \, dt = \frac{15}{2}.
$$
:::
