Find the Fourier series of $f(x) = \sin^3 x$ without computing any integral.

:::soluzione
From the triple-angle formulas, $\sin^3 x = \dfrac{3}{4} \sin x - \dfrac{1}{4} \sin(3x)$: the function is a trigonometric polynomial, therefore its Fourier series coincides with this expression, with $b_1 = \dfrac{3}{4}$, $b_3 = -\dfrac{1}{4}$ and all the other coefficients equal to zero.
:::
