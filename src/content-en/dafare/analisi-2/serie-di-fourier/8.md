---
titolo: Convergence of the series of the exponential
teoria: [convergenza-puntuale, regolare-a-tratti, funzione-periodica]
parole: [exponential, jump point, hyperbolic cosine]
---

Let $f$ be the $2\pi$-periodic extension of $f(x) = e^x$, $x \in [-\pi, \pi)$. Find, without computing the coefficients, the sum of the Fourier series of $f$ at $x = 0$ and at $x = \pi$.

:::soluzione
At $x = 0$ the function is continuous, therefore the sum equals $e^0 = 1$. At $x = \pi$ the extension jumps: the left limit is $e^{\pi}$ and the right limit is $e^{-\pi}$, therefore the sum equals
$$
\frac{e^{\pi} + e^{-\pi}}{2} = \cosh \pi .
$$
:::
