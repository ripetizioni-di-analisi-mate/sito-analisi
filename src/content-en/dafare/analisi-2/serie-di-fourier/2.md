Find the Fourier series of the $2\pi$-periodic extension of
$$
f(x) =
\begin{cases}
0 & \text{if } -\pi \leq x < 0 \\
2 & \text{if } 0 \leq x < \pi
\end{cases}
$$

:::soluzione
$a_0 = 2$, $a_n = 0$ for $n \geq 1$ and $b_n = \dfrac{2 \bigl( 1 - (-1)^n \bigr)}{n\pi}$:
$$
f(x) \sim 1 + \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{4}{(2k+1)\pi} \sin\bigl( (2k+1)x \bigr) .
$$
:::
