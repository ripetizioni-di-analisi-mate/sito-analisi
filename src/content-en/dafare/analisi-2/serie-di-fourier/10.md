Given that the $2\pi$-periodic extension of $f(x) = x^3 - \pi^2 x$, $x \in [-\pi, \pi]$, has Fourier series
$$
x^3 - \pi^2 x \sim \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{12\,(-1)^n}{n^3} \sin(nx),
$$
compute with Parseval's identity the sum of the series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^6}$.

:::soluzione
The left-hand side equals $\dfrac{1}{\pi} \displaystyle\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} (x^3 - \pi^2 x)^2\, dx = \dfrac{16\pi^6}{105}$, and the right-hand side equals $144 \displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \dfrac{1}{n^6}$, therefore
$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^6} = \frac{16\pi^6}{105 \cdot 144} = \frac{\pi^6}{945} .
$$
:::
