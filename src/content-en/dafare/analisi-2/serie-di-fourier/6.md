titolo: Fourier series of (x − 1)²

Find the Fourier series of the $2\pi$-periodic extension of $f(x) = (x - 1)^2$, $x \in [-\pi, \pi)$.

:::soluzione
Expanding the square, $f(x) = x^2 - 2x + 1$; by linearity $a_0 = \dfrac{2\pi^2}{3} + 2$, $a_n = \dfrac{4\,(-1)^n}{n^2}$ and $b_n = \dfrac{4\,(-1)^n}{n}$:
$$
(x-1)^2 \sim \frac{\pi^2}{3} + 1 + \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \left( \frac{4\,(-1)^n}{n^2} \cos(nx) + \frac{4\,(-1)^n}{n} \sin(nx) \right) .
$$
:::
