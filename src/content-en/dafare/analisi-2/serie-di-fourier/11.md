Find the Fourier series of the $4$-periodic extension of $f(x) = x$, $x \in [-2, 2)$.

:::soluzione
With $L = 2$ the function is odd, therefore $a_n = 0$ and $b_n = \dfrac{4\,(-1)^{n+1}}{n\pi}$:
$$
x \sim \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{4\,(-1)^{n+1}}{n\pi} \sin\!\left( \frac{n\pi x}{2} \right) .
$$
:::
