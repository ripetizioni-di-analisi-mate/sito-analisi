Find the Fourier series of the $2\pi$-periodic extension of $f(x) = \pi - |x|$, $x \in [-\pi, \pi]$.

:::soluzione
The function is even, therefore $b_n = 0$; moreover $a_0 = \pi$ and $a_n = \dfrac{2 \bigl( 1 - (-1)^n \bigr)}{\pi n^2}$:
$$
\pi - |x| \sim \frac{\pi}{2} + \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{4}{\pi (2k+1)^2} \cos\bigl( (2k+1)x \bigr) .
$$
:::
