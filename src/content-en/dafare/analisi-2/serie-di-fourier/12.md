---
titolo: Absolute value with period 2
teoria: [periodo-generico, serie-di-coseni-e-di-seni, convergenza-uniforme]

---

Find the Fourier series of the $2$-periodic extension of $f(x) = |x|$, $x \in [-1, 1]$, and determine whether the convergence is uniform on $\mathbb{R}$.

:::soluzione
With $L = 1$ the function is even, therefore $b_n = 0$; moreover $a_0 = 1$ and $a_n = \dfrac{2 \bigl( (-1)^n - 1 \bigr)}{n^2 \pi^2}$:
$$
|x| \sim \frac{1}{2} - \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{4}{(2k+1)^2 \pi^2} \cos\bigl( (2k+1)\pi x \bigr) .
$$
The periodic extension is continuous, because $f(-1) = f(1) = 1$, and it is piecewise smooth, therefore the series converges uniformly to $f$ on $\mathbb{R}$.
:::
