---
titolo: Somma della serie nei punti di salto
teoria: [convergenza-puntuale, regolare-a-tratti]
parole: [punto di salto, valore medio, convergenza puntuale]
---

Let $f$ be the $2\pi$-periodic extension of
$$
f(x) =
\begin{cases}
x^2 & \text{se } -\pi \leq x < 0 \\
1 & \text{se } 0 \leq x < \pi
\end{cases}
$$
Find, without computing the coefficients, the sum of the Fourier series of $f$ at $x = 0$ and at $x = \pi$.

:::soluzione
The function is piecewise smooth, therefore at the jump points the series converges to the mean of the one-sided limits. At $x = 0$: $f(0^-) = 0$ and $f(0^+) = 1$, therefore the sum equals $\dfrac{1}{2}$. At $x = \pi$: $f(\pi^-) = 1$ and $f(\pi^+) = f\bigl((-\pi)^+\bigr) = \pi^2$, therefore the sum equals $\dfrac{1 + \pi^2}{2}$.
:::
