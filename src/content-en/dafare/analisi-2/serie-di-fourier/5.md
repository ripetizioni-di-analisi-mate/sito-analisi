Find the Fourier series of the $2\pi$-periodic extension of $f(x) = \dfrac{x}{2}$, $x \in [-\pi, \pi)$.

:::soluzione
The function is odd, therefore $a_n = 0$ and $b_n = \dfrac{(-1)^{n+1}}{n}$:
$$
\frac{x}{2} \sim \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^{n+1}}{n} \sin(nx) .
$$
:::
