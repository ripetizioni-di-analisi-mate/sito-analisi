Find the Fourier series of the $2\pi$-periodic extension of
$$
f(x) =
\begin{cases}
-1 & \text{if } -\pi \leq x < 0 \\
1 & \text{if } 0 \leq x < \pi
\end{cases}
$$
and, by evaluating it at $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$, compute the sum of the numerical series $\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^k}{2k+1}$.

:::soluzione
The series is $\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{4}{(2k+1)\pi} \sin\bigl( (2k+1)x \bigr)$; at $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$, where $f$ is continuous with value $1$, we obtain $1 = \dfrac{4}{\pi} \displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^k}{2k+1}$, therefore the required sum equals $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$.
:::
