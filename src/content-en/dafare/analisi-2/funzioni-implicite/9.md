Write the equation of the tangent plane to the surface
$$
xz + yz^2 + xy = 3
$$
at the point $(1, 1, 1)$.

:::soluzione
With $F(x,y,z) = xz + yz^2 + xy - 3$ we have $\nabla F(1,1,1) = (2, 2, 3)$, therefore the tangent plane is
$$
2x + 2y + 3z = 7 .
$$
:::
