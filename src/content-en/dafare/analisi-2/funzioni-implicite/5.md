---
titolo: Points with a horizontal tangent
teoria: [retta-tangente-implicita, studio-locale-implicita]
parole: [horizontal tangent, stationary points, level curve]
---

Find the points of the curve
$$
x^2 + xy + y^2 = 3
$$
at which the tangent line is horizontal.

:::soluzione
The points with a horizontal tangent are those of the curve with $F_x = 2x + y = 0$ and $F_y \neq 0$: substituting $y = -2x$ into the equation gives $3x^2 = 3$, hence the points are
$$
(1, -2) \qquad \text{and} \qquad (-1, 2),
$$
and at both of them $F_y = x + 2y \neq 0$.
:::
