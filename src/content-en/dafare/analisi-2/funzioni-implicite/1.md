---
titolo: Derivative of the implicit function at a point
teoria: [teorema-di-dini]
parole: [Dini's theorem, first derivative, implicit function]
---

Verify that the equation
$$
x^3 + y^3 + xy = 3
$$
implicitly defines a function $y = \varphi(x)$ near the point $(1, 1)$ and compute $\varphi'(1)$.

:::soluzione
We have $F(1,1) = 0$ and $F_y(1,1) = 3y^2 + x \,\big|_{(1,1)} = 4 \neq 0$, therefore Dini's theorem applies and
$$
\varphi'(1) = -\,\frac{F_x(1,1)}{F_y(1,1)} = -\,\frac{4}{4} = -1 .
$$
:::
