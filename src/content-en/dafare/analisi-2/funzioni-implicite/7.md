---
titolo: Nature of a stationary point of the implicit function
teoria: [studio-locale-implicita, derivata-seconda-implicita]
parole: [stationary point, local maximum, local minimum, implicit function]
---

Verify that the equation
$$
y^3 + 3y - x^2 + 2x - 5 = 0
$$
implicitly defines a function $y = \varphi(x)$ near the point $(1, 1)$, and determine whether $x_0 = 1$ is a local maximum or minimum point for $\varphi$.

:::soluzione
We have $F(1,1) = 0$, $F_y(1,1) = 6 \neq 0$ and $F_x(1,1) = 0$, hence $\varphi'(1) = 0$. Differentiating the identity twice we obtain $\varphi''(1) = \dfrac{1}{3} > 0$, therefore $x_0 = 1$ is a local minimum point.
:::
