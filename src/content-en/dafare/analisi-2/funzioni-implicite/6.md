---
titolo: Second derivative of the implicit function

Verify that the equation
$$
y^3 + y - 2x^2 = 0
$$
implicitly defines a function $y = \varphi(x)$ near the point $(1, 1)$ and compute $\varphi'(1)$ and $\varphi''(1)$.

:::soluzione
We have $F(1,1) = 0$ and $F_y(1,1) = 4 \neq 0$. Differentiating the identity twice we obtain
$$
\varphi'(1) = 1, \qquad \varphi''(1) = -\,\frac{1}{2} .
$$
:::
