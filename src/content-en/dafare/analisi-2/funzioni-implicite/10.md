---
titolo: Points of the curve without an implicit function y(x)
teoria: [scambio-delle-variabili]
parole: [applicability of Dini's theorem, vertical tangent, exchanging the variables]
---

Find the points of the curve
$$
x^3 + y^2 - 3x = 0
$$
at which Dini's theorem does not apply with respect to the variable $y$, and determine what happens at those points with respect to the variable $x$.

:::soluzione
The points of the curve with $F_y = 2y = 0$ are
$$
(0, 0), \qquad (\sqrt{3}, 0), \qquad (-\sqrt{3}, 0) .
$$
At these points $F_x = 3x^2 - 3$ equals $-3$, $6$ and $6$ respectively, always nonzero, therefore near each point the curve is the graph of a function $x = \psi(y)$ and the tangent line is vertical.
:::
