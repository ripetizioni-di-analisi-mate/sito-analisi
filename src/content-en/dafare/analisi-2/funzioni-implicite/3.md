---
titolo: Tangent line to an implicit curve
teoria: [retta-tangente-implicita]
parole: [tangent line, level curve]
---

Write the equation of the tangent line to the curve
$$
x^2 y + y^3 = 2
$$
at the point $(1, 1)$.

:::soluzione
With $F(x,y) = x^2 y + y^3 - 2$ we have $\nabla F(1,1) = (2, 4)$, therefore the tangent is $2(x-1) + 4(y-1) = 0$, that is
$$
y = -\,\frac{1}{2}\,x + \frac{3}{2} .
$$
:::
