---
titolo: System of equations and implicit curve
teoria: [dini-per-sistemi]
parole: [systems, Jacobian matrix, curve of intersection of surfaces]
---

Verify that the system of equations
$$
\begin{cases}
x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 6 \\[4pt]
x + 2y - z = 1
\end{cases}
$$
implicitly defines two functions $y = y(x)$ and $z = z(x)$ near the point $(1, 1, 2)$ and compute $y'(1)$ and $z'(1)$.

:::soluzione
The point solves the system and the Jacobian determinant with respect to $(y, z)$ at the point is
$$
\det \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 4 \\ 2 & -1 \end{pmatrix} = -10 \neq 0 .
$$
Differentiating the two equations with respect to $x$ we obtain
$$
y'(1) = -\,\frac{3}{5}, \qquad z'(1) = -\,\frac{1}{5} .
$$
:::
