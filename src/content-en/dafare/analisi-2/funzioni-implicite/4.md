Write the equation of the tangent line to the curve
$$
\ln(x + y) + xy = 0
$$
at the point $(0, 1)$.

:::soluzione
With $F(x,y) = \ln(x+y) + xy$ we have $F_x(0,1) = 2$ and $F_y(0,1) = 1$, therefore $\varphi'(0) = -2$ and the tangent is
$$
y = 1 - 2x .
$$
:::
