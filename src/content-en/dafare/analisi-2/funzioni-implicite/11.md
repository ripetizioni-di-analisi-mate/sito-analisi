---
titolo: Tangent to a curve with an exponential
teoria: [teorema-di-dini, retta-tangente-implicita]
parole: [tangent line, exponential, derivative of the implicit function]
---

Verify that the equation
$$
e^{x+y} - x^2 - y^2 = 1
$$
implicitly defines a function $y = \varphi(x)$ near the point $(0, 0)$, compute $\varphi'(0)$ and write the tangent line to the curve at the origin.

:::soluzione
We have $F(0,0) = 0$ and $F_y(0,0) = e^{x+y} - 2y \,\big|_{(0,0)} = 1 \neq 0$; since $F_x(0,0) = 1$, we obtain $\varphi'(0) = -1$ and the tangent is
$$
y = -x .
$$
:::
