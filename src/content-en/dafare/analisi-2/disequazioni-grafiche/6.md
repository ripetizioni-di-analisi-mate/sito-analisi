Solve graphically the inequality
$$
y^2 + x - 4 \leq 0 .
$$

:::soluzione
Isolating $x$ we obtain $x \leq 4 - y^2$. The boundary is the parabola with horizontal axis and vertex $V(4, 0)$, which opens to the left and meets the $y$-axis at the points $(0, -2)$ and $(0, 2)$. The solutions are the points **to the left of the parabola**, parabola **included**.

:::
