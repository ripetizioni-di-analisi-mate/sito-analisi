Solve graphically the inequality
$$
\ln(4 - x^2 - y^2) \geq 0 .
$$

:::soluzione
The logarithm is greater than or equal to zero when its argument is greater than or equal to $1$, so the inequality is equivalent to $4 - x^2 - y^2 \geq 1$, that is, to $x^2 + y^2 \leq 3$. The solutions are the **interior points** of the circle with center the origin and radius $\sqrt{3}$, circle **included**.

:::
