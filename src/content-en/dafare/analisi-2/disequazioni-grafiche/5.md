Solve graphically the inequality
$$
x > y^2 - 2y .
$$

:::soluzione
Completing the square the inequality becomes $x > (y - 1)^2 - 1$. The boundary is the parabola with horizontal axis and vertex $V(-1, 1)$, which opens to the right and meets the $y$-axis at the points $(0, 0)$ and $(0, 2)$. The variable $x$ is isolated with the symbol $>$, so the solutions are the points **to the right of the parabola**, parabola **excluded**.
