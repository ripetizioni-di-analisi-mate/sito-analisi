Solve graphically the inequality
$$
x^2 - \frac{y^2}{9} \leq 1 .
$$

:::soluzione
The boundary is the hyperbola centered at the origin with branches to the right and to the left, vertices $(-1, 0)$ and $(1, 0)$ and asymptotes $y = 3x$ and $y = -3x$. The left-hand side is less than or equal to $1$, so the solutions form the **region between the two branches**, which contains the origin, branches **included**.
