Solve graphically the inequality
$$
x^2 + y^2 + 2x - 8 \geq 0 .
$$

:::soluzione
Completing the square in $x$ the inequality becomes $(x + 1)^2 + y^2 \geq 9$. The boundary is the circle with center $C(-1, 0)$ and radius $3$. The solutions are the **exterior points**, circle **included**.
