---
titolo: Half-plane with a line in implicit form
teoria: [semipiano, variabile-isolata, frontiera-e-simboli]

---

Solve graphically the inequality
$$
x - 3y \leq 3 .
$$

:::soluzione
Isolating $y$ we obtain $y \geq \dfrac{1}{3}x - 1$. The boundary is the line $y = \dfrac{1}{3}x - 1$, which meets the axes at the points $(3, 0)$ and $(0, -1)$. The solutions are the points of the half-plane **above the line**, line **included**.

:::
