Solve graphically the inequality
$$
4x^2 + 9y^2 - 16x + 18y - 11 \leq 0 .
$$

:::soluzione
Completing the squares the inequality becomes $\dfrac{(x - 2)^2}{9} + \dfrac{(y + 1)^2}{4} \leq 1$. The boundary is the ellipse with center $C(2, -1)$, horizontal semi-axis $a = 3$ and vertical semi-axis $b = 2$. The solutions are the **interior points**, ellipse **included**.
