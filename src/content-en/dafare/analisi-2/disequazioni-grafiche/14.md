Determine and draw the domain of
$$
f(x, y) = \sqrt{y - \ln x} .
$$

:::soluzione
The argument of the logarithm must be greater than zero and the radicand must be greater than or equal to zero, so the domain is described by the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x > 0 \\[4pt]
y \geq \ln x
\end{cases}
$$
The domain is the region **above the graph** of $y = \ln x$, graph **included**, and it lies to the right of the $y$-axis, axis excluded.

:::
