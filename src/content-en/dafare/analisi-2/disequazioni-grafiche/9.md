Solve graphically the inequality
$$
\frac{x^2}{16} + \frac{y^2}{4} > 1 .
$$

:::soluzione
The boundary is the ellipse centered at the origin with horizontal semi-axis $a = 4$ and vertical semi-axis $b = 2$, whose vertices are $(\pm 4, 0)$ and $(0, \pm 2)$. The left-hand side is greater than $1$, so the solutions are the **exterior points**, ellipse **excluded**.

:::
