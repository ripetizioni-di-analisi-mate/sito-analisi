Write as a normal domain with respect to the $x$-axis and with respect to the $y$-axis the region $D$ lying between the parabola $y = x^2$ and the line $y = 2x$.

:::soluzione
The two curves meet at the points $(0, 0)$ and $(2, 4)$. Every vertical segment goes from the parabola $y = x^2$, at the bottom, to the line $y = 2x$, at the top. Every horizontal segment goes from the line $x = \dfrac{y}{2}$, on the left, to the curve $x = \sqrt{y}$, on the right.
$$
\begin{gathered}
D = \{ (x, y) : 0 \leq x \leq 2,\ x^2 \leq y \leq 2x \} \\[6pt]
D = \left\{ (x, y) : 0 \leq y \leq 4,\ \frac{y}{2} \leq x \leq \sqrt{y} \right\}
\end{gathered}
$$
