Solve graphically the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x^2 + y^2 < 4 \\[4pt]
y \geq x^2 - 2
\end{cases}
$$

:::soluzione
The circle $x^2 + y^2 = 4$ and the parabola $y = x^2 - 2$ meet at the points $(0, -2)$, $(\sqrt{3}, 1)$ and $(-\sqrt{3}, 1)$. The solutions are the **interior points** of the circle that lie **above the parabola**. The arc of the parabola is included, the arc of the circle is excluded and the three intersection points are **excluded**, because they belong to the circle.
