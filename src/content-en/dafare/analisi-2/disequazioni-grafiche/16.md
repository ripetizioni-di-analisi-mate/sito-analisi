Solve graphically the inequality
$$
(x + y)(xy - 1) \geq 0 .
$$

:::soluzione
The line $x + y = 0$ and the hyperbola $xy = 1$ have no points in common and divide the plane into four regions. The two factors are both positive in the region beyond the branch in the first quadrant, and they are both negative in the region lying between the line and the branch in the third quadrant. The product is positive where the two factors have the same sign, so the solutions are the **union of these two regions**, with the line and the two branches **included**.

:::
