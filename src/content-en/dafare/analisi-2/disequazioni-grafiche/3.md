Solve graphically the inequality
$$
y < -x^2 + 6x - 5 .
$$

:::soluzione
The boundary is the parabola $y = -x^2 + 6x - 5$, which has vertical axis, vertex $V(3, 4)$ and opens downward, and meets the $x$-axis at the points with $x$-coordinates $1$ and $5$. The variable $y$ is isolated with the symbol $<$, so the solutions are the points **below the parabola**, parabola **excluded**.

:::
