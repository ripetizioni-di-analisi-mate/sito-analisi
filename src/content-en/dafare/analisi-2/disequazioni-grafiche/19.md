Decompose into interior points, boundary pieces and vertices the set $D$ described by the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x^2 + y^2 \leq 4 \\[4pt]
x \geq 0
\end{cases}
$$

:::soluzione
The set $D$ is the half-disk with center the origin and radius $2$ that lies to the right of the $y$-axis.

- Interior points: $0 < x < \sqrt{4 - y^2}$.
- First boundary piece: the semicircle $x = \sqrt{4 - y^2}$ with $-2 \leq y \leq 2$, which is parametrized by $x = 2\cos t$, $y = 2\sin t$, $-\dfrac{\pi}{2} \leq t \leq \dfrac{\pi}{2}$.
- Second boundary piece: the segment $x = 0$ with $-2 \leq y \leq 2$.
- Vertices: $A(0, -2)$ and $B(0, 2)$.
