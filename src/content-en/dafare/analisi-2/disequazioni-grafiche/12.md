Solve graphically the inequality
$$
x(y - 2) > 1 .
$$

:::soluzione
The boundary is the rectangular hyperbola $x(y - 2) = 1$, whose asymptotes are the lines $x = 0$ and $y = 2$. One branch lies in the region where $x > 0$ and $y > 2$, the other in the region where $x < 0$ and $y < 2$. At the point $(0, 2)$, which belongs to the region between the two branches, the product $x(y - 2)$ equals $0$ and the inequality $0 > 1$ is false. The solutions are therefore the two **regions beyond the branches**, branches **excluded**.
