Solve graphically the inequality
$$
1 \leq x^2 + y^2 - 4y + 4 < 9 .
$$

:::soluzione
Since $y^2 - 4y + 4 = (y - 2)^2$, the inequality is written $1 \leq x^2 + (y - 2)^2 < 9$. The solutions form the **annulus** with center $C(0, 2)$ lying between the circle of radius $1$ and the one of radius $3$. The circle of radius $1$ is **included**, the circle of radius $3$ is **excluded**.
