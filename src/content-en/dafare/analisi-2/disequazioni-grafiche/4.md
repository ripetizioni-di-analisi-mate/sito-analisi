Solve graphically the inequality
$$
x^2 - 2x + 2y - 3 \geq 0 .
$$

:::soluzione
Isolating $y$ we obtain $y \geq -\dfrac{1}{2}x^2 + x + \dfrac{3}{2}$, that is, $y \geq -\dfrac{1}{2}(x - 1)^2 + 2$. The boundary is the parabola with vertical axis, vertex $V(1, 2)$ and opening downward, which meets the $x$-axis at the points with $x$-coordinates $-1$ and $3$. The solutions are the points **above the parabola**, parabola **included**.
