Recognize the curve with equation
$$
4x^2 + y^2 - 8x = 0
$$
and determine whether it is a bounded set.

:::soluzione
The coefficients of $x^2$ and $y^2$ have the same sign and are different from each other. Completing the square in $x$ the equation becomes $(x - 1)^2 + \dfrac{y^2}{4} = 1$, so the curve is the **ellipse** with center $C(1, 0)$, horizontal semi-axis $a = 1$ and vertical semi-axis $b = 2$. The ellipse is contained in the disk with center $C$ and radius $2$, so it is a **bounded** set.
