Solve graphically the inequality
$$
-2 < y - x \leq 1 .
$$

:::soluzione
The double inequality is equivalent to $x - 2 < y \leq x + 1$. The solutions form the **strip** lying between the parallel lines $y = x - 2$ and $y = x + 1$. The line $y = x - 2$ is **excluded**, the line $y = x + 1$ is **included**.

::grafico{regione="y>x-2; y<=x+1" xmin=-4 xmax=4 ymin=-4 ymax=4 uguale=1 titolo="$-2 < y - x \leq 1$"}
:::
