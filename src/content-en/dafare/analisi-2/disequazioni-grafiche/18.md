Write as a normal domain with respect to the $y$-axis the region $D$ described by the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x \geq y^2 - 1 \\[4pt]
x \leq 1 - y^2
\end{cases}
$$

:::soluzione
The parabolas $x = y^2 - 1$ and $x = 1 - y^2$ meet at the points $(0, -1)$ and $(0, 1)$, so the projection of $D$ onto the $y$-axis is the interval $[-1, 1]$. Every horizontal segment goes from the parabola $x = y^2 - 1$, on the left, to the parabola $x = 1 - y^2$, on the right.
$$
D = \{ (x, y) : -1 \leq y \leq 1,\ y^2 - 1 \leq x \leq 1 - y^2 \}
$$
