titolo: "Thresholds for advances under commercial discount"
teoria: [sconto-commerciale]
parole: ["minimum proceeds", "maximum term"]
---

A receivable of $5000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is discounted at an annual commercial discount rate of $10\%$. What is the longest term in years that leaves proceeds of at least $4000\,\mathrm{EUR}$?

:::soluzione
$2$ years.
:::
