---
titolo: "Rollover versus a single deposit"
teoria: [scindibilita]
parole: ["reinvestment", "simple-interest deposit", "multiplicativity"]
---

A deposit of $3000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ earns $10\%$ simple annual interest. After one year its full balance is reinvested for one more year at the same rate. By how much does the final balance exceed that of a two-year simple-interest deposit with the same initial principal?

:::soluzione
$30\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
:::
