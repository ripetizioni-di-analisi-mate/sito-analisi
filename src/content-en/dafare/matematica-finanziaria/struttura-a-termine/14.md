---
titolo: "Coupon bond holdings for a single cash flow"
teoria:
- replicazione
parole: ["short selling", "linear system"]

Bond $A$ pays $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in year one. Bond $B$ pays $20$ and $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in years one and two. Negative holdings are allowed. How much of $A$ is required to replicate a single receipt of $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in year two?

:::soluzione
$-0{,}2$.
:::
