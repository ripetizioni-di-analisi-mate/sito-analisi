Two bonds pay the same amount on the same date. The first can be bought for $94\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and the second sold short for $96\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Without transaction costs, how much does this strategy bring in initially?

:::soluzione
$2\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
:::
