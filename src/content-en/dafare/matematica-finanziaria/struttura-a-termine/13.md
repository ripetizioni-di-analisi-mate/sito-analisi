Two instruments have identical redemption payments. The first can be bought for $90\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and the second sold short for $92\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Each order costs $0.50\,\mathrm{EUR}$. How much cash is left today after these trades?

:::soluzione
$1\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
:::
