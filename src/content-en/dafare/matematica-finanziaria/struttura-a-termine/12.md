An investment costs $125\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today and pays $50$ and $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in years $1$ and $2$. Calculate its net present value using discount factors $0.98$ and $0.9$.

:::soluzione
$14\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
:::
