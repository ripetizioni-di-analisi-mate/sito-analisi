A one-year zero-coupon bond pays $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and costs $95\,\mathrm{EUR}$. A bond paying $10$ and $110\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in years one and two costs $108\,\mathrm{EUR}$. How much does it cost to replicate cash flows of $(60,110)$ euros on those dates?

:::soluzione
$155.5\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
:::
