Halfway through an annual coupon period, a single payment of $1210\,\mathrm{EUR}$ remains, including a coupon of $110\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The effective annual yield is $21\%$. Calculate the clean price in euros using linear accrual and the same fraction of a period for discounting.

:::soluzione
$1045\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
:::
