A bond will repay its face value of $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and pay a coupon of $71\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year. At an effective annual yield of $5\%$, by how much does the price exceed face value?

:::soluzione
$20\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
:::
