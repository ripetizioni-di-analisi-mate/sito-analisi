A bond pays a coupon of $20\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year and another of $30\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years. It repays $90\,\mathrm{EUR}$ with the last coupon. Calculate its price at an effective annual yield of $20\%$.

:::soluzione
$100\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
:::
