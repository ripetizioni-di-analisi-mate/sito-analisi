A bond bought for $800\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is sold for $810\,\mathrm{EUR}$ one year later, immediately after the holder receives a coupon of $30\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Calculate the realized return.

:::soluzione
$5\%$.
:::
