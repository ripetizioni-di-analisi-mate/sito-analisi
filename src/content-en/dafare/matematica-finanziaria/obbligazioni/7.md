---
titolo: "Amount of a semiannual coupon"
teoria: ["cedole-infrannuali"]
parole: ["semiannual coupon", "nominal rate"]
---

A bond with face value $1600\,\mathrm{EUR}$ pays two equal coupons per year at a nominal annual coupon rate of $6\%$. Calculate the amount of one coupon.

:::soluzione
$48\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
:::
