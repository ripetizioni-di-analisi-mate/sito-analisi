A bond costs $990\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and pays a coupon of $49\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and principal of $1040\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year. Calculate its yield to maturity.

:::soluzione
$10\%$.
:::
