A portfolio holds four zero-coupon bonds that each repay $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year, and two coupon bonds that each pay an annual coupon of $15\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and mature in two years. What is the total cash flow in one year?

:::soluzione
$430\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
:::
