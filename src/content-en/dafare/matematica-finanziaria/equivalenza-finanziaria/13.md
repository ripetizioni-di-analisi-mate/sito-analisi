Schedule A costs $400\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today. Schedule B requires $220\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year and $242\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years. Find the present cost of B minus the present cost of A at an annual compound interest rate of $10\%$.

:::soluzione
$0\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
:::
