A schedule is worth $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today. In how many years would a payment of $864\,\mathrm{EUR}$ be equivalent to it at an annual compound interest rate of $20\%$?

:::soluzione
$3\,\mathrm{years}$.
:::
