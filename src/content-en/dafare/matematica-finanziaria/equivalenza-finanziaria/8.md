Debts of $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year and $300\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in three years are replaced by a single payment of $400\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Find the equivalent average due date at an annual commercial discount rate of $10\%$, using today as the valuation date.

:::soluzione
$2.5\,\mathrm{years}$.
:::
