Receipts of $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today and $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at time $1$ are replaced by $300\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The annual compound interest rate is $25\%$. Find the discount factor for the equivalent average due date to four decimal places.

:::soluzione
$0.8667$.
:::
