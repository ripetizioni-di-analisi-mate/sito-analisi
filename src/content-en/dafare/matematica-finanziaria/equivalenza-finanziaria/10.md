---
titolo: "Accumulated values with one additional deposit"
teoria: [tassi-variabili]
parole: ["deposit", "variable rates"]
---

An account opens with $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$. A deposit of $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is made at the end of year one. Find the balance after two years when the effective annual rates are $10\%$ and $20\%$, respectively.

:::soluzione
$1440\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
:::
