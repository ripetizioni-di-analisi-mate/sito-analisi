A business pays $250\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today and receives $396\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years. Find the net value at time $1$ using an annual compound interest rate of $10\%$.

:::soluzione
$85\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
:::
