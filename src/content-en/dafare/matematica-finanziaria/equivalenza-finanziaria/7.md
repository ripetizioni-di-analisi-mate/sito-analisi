---
titolo: "Maturities with a negative interest rate"
teoria: [scadenza-comune]
parole: ["equivalent payment date", "negative rate"]
---

A schedule is worth $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today. Find the equivalent payment date for $405\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at an annual compound interest rate of $-10\%$.

:::soluzione
$2\,\mathrm{years}$.
:::
