Under a multiplicative accumulation law the discount factors from today to times $1$ and $3$ are $0.9$ and $0.72$. Find the value at time $1$ of $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ due at time $3$.

:::soluzione
$400\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
:::
