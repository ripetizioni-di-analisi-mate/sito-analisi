---
titolo: "Replacing payments with two equal installments"
teoria: [equivalenza]
parole: ["equal payments", "value"]
---

A debt of $221\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at time $1$ is replaced by two equal installments at times $0$ and $2$. Find each installment at an annual compound interest rate of $10\%$.

:::soluzione
$110\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
:::
