titolo: "Values at an intermediate date"
teoria: [valore-finanziario]

Find the value at time $1$ of $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at time $0$ and $330\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at time $2$ using an annual compound interest rate of $10\%$. Time is measured in years.

:::soluzione
$520\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
:::
