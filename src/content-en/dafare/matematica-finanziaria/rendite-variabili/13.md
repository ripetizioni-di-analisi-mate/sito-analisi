---
titolo: "Perpetual cash flows at a growing rate"
teoria: [flussi-crescenti]
parole: ["exponential cash-flow rate", "convergence"]
---

A continuous perpetuity pays $c(t)=240e^{0.03t}$ euros per year. Find its present value at a constant annual force of interest of $\delta=0.07\,\mathrm{year}^{-1}$.

:::soluzione
$6000\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
:::
