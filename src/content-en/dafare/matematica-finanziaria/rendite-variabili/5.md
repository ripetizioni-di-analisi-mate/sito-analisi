titolo: "Cash flows with a zero force of interest"
teoria: [flussi-continui]
parole: ["constant cash-flow rate", "zero force of interest"]

A continuous cash flow is paid at a constant rate of $450\,\mathrm{EUR/year}$ from time $1$ to time $4$. Find its present value when the force of interest is zero.

:::soluzione
$1350\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
:::
