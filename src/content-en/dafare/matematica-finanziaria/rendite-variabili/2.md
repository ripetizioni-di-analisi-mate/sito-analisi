titolo: "Payments increasing by a fixed amount"
teoria: [progressione-aritmetica]
parole: ["arithmetic progression", "zero interest rate"]

An annuity makes four year-end payments. The first is $80$ euros and each subsequent payment is $20$ euros larger. Find the present value at a zero annual interest rate.

:::soluzione
$440\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
:::
