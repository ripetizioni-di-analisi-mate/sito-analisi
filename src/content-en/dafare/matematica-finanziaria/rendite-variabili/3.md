---
titolo: "Payments growing at the interest rate"
teoria: [progressione-geometrica]
parole: ["geometric progression", "equal rates"]
---

An annuity makes five year-end payments. The first is $132$ euros and each subsequent payment is $10\%$ larger than the previous one. Find the present value at an effective annual interest rate of $10\%$.

:::soluzione
$600\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
:::
