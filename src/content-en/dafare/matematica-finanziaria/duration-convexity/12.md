Position A is worth $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and has modified duration $2$. Position B is worth $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and has modified duration $3$. A's yield falls by $50$ basis points and B's rises by $50$. Estimate the first-order portfolio change in euros.

:::soluzione
$-10\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
:::
