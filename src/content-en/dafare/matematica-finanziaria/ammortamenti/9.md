A loan of $3100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ bears annual interest at $10\%$. It is repaid with two equal regular installments and an additional principal payment of $1210\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at the end of year two. Find the regular installment.

:::soluzione
$1210\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
:::
