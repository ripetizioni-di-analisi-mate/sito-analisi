teoria: [americano]

A bullet loan of $7500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ bears annual interest at $4\%$. Interest is paid at the end of each year and all principal is repaid at the end of year three. Find the amount paid to the lender at the third payment date.

:::soluzione
$7800\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
:::
