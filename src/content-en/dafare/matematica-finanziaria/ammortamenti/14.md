titolo: "Market value of the remaining payments"
teoria: [estinzione-anticipata]
parole: ["market value", "valuation"]

One contractual payment of $1452\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is due in one year. The contractual interest rate is $10\%$ and the market rate is $21\%$. Find the loan's current market value.

:::soluzione
$1200\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
:::
