Immediately after an interest rate change, the outstanding principal is $1800\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and one installment remains, due a year later. The new effective annual interest rate is $5\%$. Find the final installment.

:::soluzione
$1890\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
:::
