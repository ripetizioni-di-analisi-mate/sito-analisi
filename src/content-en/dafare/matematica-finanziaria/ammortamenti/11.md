---
titolo: "Interest under equal-principal amortization"
teoria: [italiano]
parole: ["total interest", "equal principal portions"]
---

An equal-principal loan of $6000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ is repaid in four annual installments at an interest rate of $4\%$. Find the total interest paid.

:::soluzione
$600\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
:::
