titolo: "Balances with two payments remaining"
teoria: [debito-residuo]
parole: ["outstanding balance", "prospective method"]

Immediately after an installment, the remaining payments are $880\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year and $726\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years. The contractual interest rate is $10\%$ per year. Find the current outstanding balance.

:::soluzione
$1400\,\mathrm{EUR}$.
:::
