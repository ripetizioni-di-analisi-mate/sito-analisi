A project costs $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today and pays $330\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year and $363\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years. Calculate its NPV at an annual rate of $10\%$.

:::soluzione
$100\,\mathrm{EUR}$
:::
