titolo: "Project choice above the crossover rate"

Switching from project $A$ to $B$ requires an extra $400\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today and brings in an extra $460\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year. At an annual rate of $20\%$, which project has the higher NPV?

:::soluzione
Project $A$.
:::
