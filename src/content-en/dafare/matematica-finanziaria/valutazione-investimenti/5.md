Project $A$ costs $300\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and pays $385\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year. Project $B$ costs $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and pays $726\,\mathrm{EUR}$ on the same date. Calculate $\operatorname{NPV}_B-\operatorname{NPV}_A$ at an annual rate of $10\%$.

:::soluzione
$10\,\mathrm{EUR}$
:::
