titolo: "Two IRRs with a closing cost"
teoria: [tir-multipli]

A project costs $100\,\mathrm{EUR}$ today, receives $250\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year and pays out $156\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years. Find all its annual IRRs.

:::soluzione
$20\%$ and $30\%$.
:::
