---
titolo: "Crossover when initial costs are equal"
teoria: [tasso-soglia]
parole: ["threshold", "alternative inflows"]
---

Two projects have the same initial cost. Project $A$ pays $400\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year and project $B$ pays $460\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years. Find the annual crossover rate.

:::soluzione
$15\%$
:::
