---
titolo: "Exact payback on a payment date"
teoria: [recupero-attualizzato]
parole: ["discrete payback", "equality"]
---

A project costs $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and pays $110\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year and $121\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years. Find the discounted payback period at an annual rate of $10\%$.

:::soluzione
$2$ years.
:::
