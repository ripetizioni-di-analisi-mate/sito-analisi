titolo: "Horizon with zero sensitivity"
teoria: ["rischio-reinvestimento"]

The initial force of interest is zero. A portfolio receives $200\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at time $1$ and $600\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at time $5$. Find the horizon at which the derivative of the horizon value with respect to the force of interest is zero.

:::soluzione
$4\,\mathrm{years}$
:::
