---
titolo: "Derivative with respect to the force of interest"
teoria: ["duration-matching"]
parole: ["duration", "sensitivity"]
---

Assets and liabilities are each worth $500\,\mathrm{EUR}$. Their durations are $5$ and $3$ years, respectively. Calculate the derivative of the surplus with respect to the flat force of interest.

:::soluzione
$-1000\,\mathrm{EUR}\cdot\mathrm{year}$
:::
