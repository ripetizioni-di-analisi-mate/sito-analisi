At an effective annual rate of $10\%$, an asset pays $550\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in one year and a liability requires $363\,\mathrm{EUR}$ in two years. Calculate the initial surplus.

:::soluzione
$200\,\mathrm{EUR}$
:::
