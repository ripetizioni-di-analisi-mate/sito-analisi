titolo: "Buying an asset when rebalancing"
teoria: ["portafoglio-due-titoli", "riequilibrio"]
parole: ["remaining maturities", "target value"]
---

At time $2$ the zero-coupon bonds in a portfolio have original maturity dates $3$ and $7$. The shorter bond holding is already worth $300\,\mathrm{EUR}$. The remaining liabilities are worth $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and have a remaining duration of $3$ years. How much more should be invested in the shorter bond to reach its target value?

:::soluzione
$200\,\mathrm{EUR}$
:::
