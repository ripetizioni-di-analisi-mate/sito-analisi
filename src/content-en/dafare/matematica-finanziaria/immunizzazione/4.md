---
titolo: "Derivative with respect to the effective rate"
teoria: ["duration-matching"]
parole: ["modified duration", "effective rate"]

At an effective annual rate of $25\%$, assets are worth $800\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and have a Macaulay duration of $3$ years. Liabilities are worth $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and have a Macaulay duration of $4$ years. Calculate the derivative of the surplus in euros with respect to the effective rate.

:::soluzione
$1280\,\mathrm{EUR}$
:::
