Assets and liabilities are each worth $800\,\mathrm{EUR}$ at the initial force of interest. Their durations are equal, and their convexities are $20$ and $17\,\mathrm{years}^2$, respectively. Find the coefficient of $h^2$ in the expansion of the surplus for a shift $h$ in the force of interest.

:::soluzione
$1200\,\mathrm{EUR}\cdot\mathrm{year}^2$
:::
