The effective annual rate is $10\%$. Two zero-coupon bonds maturing in $1$ and $5$ years are used to match a liability with a present value of $1000\,\mathrm{EUR}$ and a duration of $3$ years. Calculate the face value of the holding in the shorter bond.

:::soluzione
$550\,\mathrm{EUR}$
:::
