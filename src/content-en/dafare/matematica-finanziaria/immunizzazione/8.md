titolo: "Convexity from variance"
teoria: ["convexity-matching"]
parole: ["variance", "second moment"]

A portfolio with positive cash flows has a duration of $4$ years. The present-value-weighted variance of the maturities is $7\,\mathrm{years}^2$. Calculate convexity with respect to the force of interest.

:::soluzione
$23\,\mathrm{years}^2$
:::
