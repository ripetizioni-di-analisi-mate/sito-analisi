---
titolo: "Complex differentiability of a real-valued function at zero"
teoria: [derivata-complessa, cauchy-riemann]
parole: ["differentiability", "paths"]
---

Set $f(0)=0$ and, for $z=x+iy\ne0$, let $f(z)=\dfrac{x^3}{x^2+y^2}$. Determine whether $f$ is continuous and complex differentiable at zero.

:::soluzione
$f$ is continuous at zero and is not complex differentiable there.
:::
