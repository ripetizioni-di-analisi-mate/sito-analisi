titolo: "Differential of a complex power function"
teoria: [conformi, jacobiano-complesso]

Find the angle of rotation and the scale factor of the differential of $f(z)=z^3$ at $z_0=1+i$. Determine the area scale factor.

:::soluzione
Length scale factor $6$, rotation $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, area scale factor $36$.
:::
