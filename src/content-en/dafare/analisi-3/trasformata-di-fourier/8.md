---

Find the transform of $f(x)=e^{-x^2}\sin x$ and determine its parity.

:::soluzione
$\widehat f(\xi)=\dfrac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2i}\left(e^{-\dfrac{(\xi-1)^2}{4}}-e^{-\dfrac{(\xi+1)^2}{4}}\right)$, purely imaginary and odd.
:::
