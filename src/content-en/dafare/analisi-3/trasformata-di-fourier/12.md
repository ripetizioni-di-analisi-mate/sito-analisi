parole: ["partial overlap of two intervals"]
---

For $f=\mathbf1_{[-1,2]}$ and $g=\mathbf1_{[0,2]}$, find the support of $f*g$ and compute $(f*g)(\dfrac52)$.

:::soluzione
The support is $[-1,4]$, and $(f*g)(\dfrac52)=\dfrac32$.
:::
