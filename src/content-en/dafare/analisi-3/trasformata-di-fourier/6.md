Find the transform of $f(x)=e^{-4(x+1)^2}$. Identify the phase factor separately.

:::soluzione
$\widehat f(\xi)=\dfrac{\sqrt{\pi}}2e^{i\xi}e^{-\dfrac{\xi^2}{16}}$, with phase factor $e^{i\xi}$.
:::
