Find the transform of $\mathbf1_{[1,4]}$ and its value at zero.

:::soluzione
$\widehat f(\xi)=e^{-\dfrac52 i\xi}\dfrac{2\sin(\dfrac32\xi)}{\xi}$ for $\xi\ne0$, with $\widehat f(0)=3$.
:::
