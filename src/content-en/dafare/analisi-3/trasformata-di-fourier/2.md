titolo: "A two-sided exponential modulated by a cosine"

---

Find the transform of $f(x)=e^{-|x|}\cos(2x)$ and check that it is real and even.

:::soluzione
$\widehat f(\xi)=\dfrac1{1+(\xi-2)^2}+\dfrac1{1+(\xi+2)^2}$, real and even.
:::
