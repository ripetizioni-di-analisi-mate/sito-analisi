Find the causal inverse Laplace transform of $\dfrac1{(s+3)^2(s+1)}$.

:::soluzione
$f(t)=\dfrac{e^{-t}-e^{-3t}}4-\dfrac t2 e^{-3t}$ for $t\ge0$.
:::
