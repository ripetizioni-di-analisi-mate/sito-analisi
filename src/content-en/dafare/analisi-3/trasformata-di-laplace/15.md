---
titolo: "A periodic signal with a delayed start"
teoria: [segnali-periodici]
parole: ["periodicity", "delay"]
---

A signal of period $4$ equals $1$ on $(1,3)$ in its first period and is zero for the rest of that period. Find its Laplace transform and half-plane of absolute convergence.

:::soluzione
$F(s)=\dfrac{e^{-s}-e^{-3s}}{s(1-e^{-4s})}$ for $\operatorname{Re}s>0$.
:::
