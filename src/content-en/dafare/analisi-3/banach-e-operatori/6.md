---
titolo: "Norm of a combination of three signed evaluations"
teoria: ["duale"]
parole: ["norm of a combination of three signed evaluations"]
---

On $C([0,1])$ with the supremum norm, find the norm of $\ell(f)=f(0)-3f(\dfrac{1}{2})+2f(1)$. Give a function attaining this norm.

:::soluzione
$\|\ell\|=6$, attained by the continuous piecewise linear function interpolating $(0,1)$, $(\dfrac{1}{2},-1)$, and $(1,1)$.
:::
