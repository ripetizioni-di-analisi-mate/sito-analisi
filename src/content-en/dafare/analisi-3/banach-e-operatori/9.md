In a complete metric space with metric $d(x,y)=|x-y|$, a contraction has constant $q=\dfrac{1}{5}$ and two consecutive iterates satisfy $|x_n-x_{n-1}|=1$. What a posteriori upper bound does Banach's theorem give for $|x_*-x_n|$?

:::soluzione
$|x_*-x_n|\le\dfrac{1}{4}$
:::
