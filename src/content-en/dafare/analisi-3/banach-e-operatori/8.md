For $T(x)=\dfrac{1}{2+x}$ on $[0,1]$, verify that the interval is invariant, find a contraction constant, and determine the fixed point.

:::soluzione
$T([0,1])=[\dfrac{1}{3},\dfrac{1}{2}]$, a contraction constant is $\dfrac{1}{4}$, and the fixed point is $\sqrt2-1$.
:::
