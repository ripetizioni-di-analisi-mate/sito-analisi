titolo: "Inverting the identity minus a nilpotent operator"
teoria: ["neumann"]
parole: ["inverting the identity minus a nilpotent operator"]
---

In $\mathbb R^3$ with the Euclidean norm, for $A(x,y,z)=(y,z,0)$ determine $(I-A)^{-1}$ and check whether the hypothesis $\|A\|<1$ holds.

:::soluzione
$(I-A)^{-1}(u,v,w)=(u+v+w,v+w,w)$. The strict inequality fails since $\|A\|=1$.
:::
