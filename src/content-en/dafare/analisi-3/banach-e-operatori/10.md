A real contraction iteration gives $q=\dfrac{2}{5}$, $x_{n-1}=\dfrac{79}{50}$, and $x_n=\dfrac{41}{25}$. Use the a posteriori estimate to find an interval centered at $x_n$ that contains the fixed point.

:::soluzione
$|x_*-x_n|\le\dfrac{1}{25}$, so $x_*\in[\dfrac{8}{5},\dfrac{42}{25}]$.
:::
