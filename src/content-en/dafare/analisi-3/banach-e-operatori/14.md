---
titolo: "Compactness of a diagonal projection"
teoria: ["compatti"]
parole: ["compactness of a diagonal projection"]
---

On $\ell^2$, let $D$ be the operator that retains the even-indexed coordinates and sets the odd-indexed coordinates to zero. Compute $\|D\|$ and determine whether the operator is compact.

:::soluzione
$\|D\|=1$. The operator is not compact.
:::
