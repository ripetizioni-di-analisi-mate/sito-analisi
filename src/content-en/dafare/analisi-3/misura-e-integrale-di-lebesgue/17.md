---
titolo: "Iterated sums of a matrix with signed entries"
teoria: ["fubini"]
parole: ["iterated sums of a matrix with signed entries"]
---

For $i,j\ge1$, set $a(i,j)=\mathbf1_{\{j=i\}}-\mathbf1_{\{j=i+2\}}$. Compute the iterated sums, first summing in $j$ and then in $i$, and then in the reverse order. Determine whether the double series converges absolutely.

:::soluzione
Summing in $j$ first gives $0$. Summing in $i$ first gives $2$. The series of absolute values diverges.
:::
