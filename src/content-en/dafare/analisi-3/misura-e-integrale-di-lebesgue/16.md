---
titolo: "Interchanging a limit and a geometric sum"
teoria: ["convergenza-monotona"]
parole: ["interchanging a limit and a geometric sum"]
---

Compute $\lim_{n\to\infty}\int_0^{\frac12}\sum_{k=0}^n x^k\,dx$ and identify the theorem that justifies the interchange.

:::soluzione
$\log 2$. Apply monotone convergence to the increasing sequence of nonnegative partial sums.
:::
