For $t>-\dfrac12$, set $G(t)=\int_0^2\dfrac1{1+tx}\,dx$. Find $G'(0)$ and give a function that bounds the absolute derivative of the integrand for $|t|\le\dfrac14$.

:::soluzione
$G'(0)=-2$. An integrable majorant on $(0,2)$ is $4x$.
:::
