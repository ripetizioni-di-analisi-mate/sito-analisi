---
titolo: "Positive and negative parts on intervals"
teoria: ["semplici", "integrale-lebesgue"]
parole: ["positive and negative parts on overlapping intervals"]
---

For $f=4\mathbf1_{[0,2]}-3\mathbf1_{[1,4]}$, compute the integrals of $f^+$ and $f^-$ separately and then the integral of $f$.

:::soluzione
$\int f^+\,dm=5$, $\int f^-\,dm=6$ and $\int f\,dm=-1$.
:::
