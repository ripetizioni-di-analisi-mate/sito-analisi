Let $(e_n)$ be the standard basis of $\ell^2$. For $u_n=2e_1+e_n-e_{n+1}$ with $n\ge2$, find the weak limit and determine whether the convergence also holds in norm.

:::soluzione
The weak limit is $2e_1$. The norm distance from it is $\sqrt2$ for every $n\ge2$.
:::
