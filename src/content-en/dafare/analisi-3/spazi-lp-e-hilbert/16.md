In $L^2(-1,1)$, project $x^4+x^3$ onto the span of $1$ and $x$. Also compute the squared distance.

:::soluzione
$P_Mf=\dfrac15+\dfrac35x$. The squared distance is $\dfrac{296}{1575}$.
:::
