titolo: "The Riesz representative in the complex case"
teoria: ["riesz-debole"]
parole: ["complex Riesz representative and functional norm"]
---

In the complex space $L^2(-1,1)$, find the representing vector of $\ell(f)=\int_{-1}^1f(x)(2-ix)\,dx$ and the norm of $\ell$ restricted to the odd functions.

:::soluzione
The representing vector in the entire space is $2+ix$. On the subspace of odd functions the representing vector is $ix$, and the restricted functional has norm $\sqrt{\dfrac23}$.
:::
