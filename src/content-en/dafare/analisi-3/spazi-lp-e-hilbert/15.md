Determine whether $x^{-\dfrac35}$ belongs to $L^1(1,\infty)$, $L^2(1,\infty)$, and $L^\infty(1,\infty)$. Compute the finite norms.

:::soluzione
$f\notin L^1$, $\|f\|_2=\sqrt5$, $\|f\|_\infty=1$.
:::
