---
titolo: "Heat flow with one endpoint at a nonzero temperature"
teoria: [calore, separazione]
parole: ["heat equation", "boundary data"]
---

Solve $u_t=u_{xx}$ on $(0,\pi)$ with $u(t,0)=2$, $u(t,\pi)=0$, and initial data $u(0,x)=2(1-\dfrac{x}{\pi})+\sin(3x)$.

:::soluzione
$u(t,x)=2\left(1-\dfrac{x}{\pi}\right)+e^{-9t}\sin(3x)$.
:::
