---
titolo: "Poisson’s equation with a linear source on an interval"
teoria: [debole, lax-milgram]
parole: ["Poisson", "linear source"]
---

Solve $-u''=6x$ on $(0,1)$ with $u(0)=u(1)=0$ and specify where the solution attains its maximum.

:::soluzione
$u(x)=x-x^3$. The maximum is $\dfrac{2}{3\sqrt3}$, attained at $x=\dfrac1{\sqrt3}$.
:::
