In the rectangle $(0,\pi)\times(0,1)$, solve $\Delta u=0$ with value $\sin(4x)$ on the side $y=1$ and zero on the remaining sides.

:::soluzione
$u(x,y)=\dfrac{\sinh(4y)}{\sinh(4)}\sin(4x)$
:::
