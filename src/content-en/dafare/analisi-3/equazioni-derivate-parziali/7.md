In the rectangle $(0,\pi)\times(0,1)$, solve $\Delta u=0$ with value $\sin(3x)$ on the side $y=1$ and zero on the remaining sides.

:::soluzione
$u(x,y)=\dfrac{\sinh(3y)}{\sinh(3)}\sin(3x)$
:::
