---

Determine the relative position of the lines
$$
r : x = 1 + 2t, \ y = -1 + t, \ z = t,
\qquad
s :
\begin{cases}
x - z = 1 \\[4pt]
y + z = -1
\end{cases}
$$
and, if they are intersecting, find their common point.

:::soluzione
The lines are intersecting at the point $(1, -1, 0)$.
:::
