Compute the angle between the planes $\pi_1 : 2x + y + 2z = 1$ and $\pi_2 : y + z = 4$.

:::soluzione
$$
\cos\theta = \frac{3}{3\sqrt{2}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}},
\qquad
\theta = \frac{\pi}{4} .
$$
:::
