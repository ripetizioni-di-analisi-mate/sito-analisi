Find the orthogonal projection $H$ of the point $P = (3, -1, 2)$ onto the plane $\pi : 2x - y + z = 3$ and the reflection $P'$ of $P$ across $\pi$.

:::soluzione
$H = (1, 0, 1)$ and $P' = (-1, 1, 0)$.
:::
