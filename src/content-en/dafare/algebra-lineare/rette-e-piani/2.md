---
titolo: Piano per un punto parallelo a due rette
teoria: [equazione-cartesiana-piano, parallelismo-perpendicolarita]
parole: [piano, rette parallele al piano, vettore normale]
---

Write the equation of the plane passing through $P = (1, -1, 2)$ and parallel to the lines
$$
r : x = 1 + t, \ y = 2t, \ z = 3 - t,
\qquad
s : x = 2 + u, \ y = 1, \ z = u .
$$

:::soluzione
The normal vector is parallel to $(1, 2, -1) \times (1, 0, 1) = (2, -2, -2)$, and the plane is
$$
x - y - z = 0 .
$$
:::
