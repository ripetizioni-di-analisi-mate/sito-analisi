Compute the distance from the point $P = (4, 1, 2)$ to the line $r : x = 2t$, $y = 1 + 2t$, $z = 1 + t$ and find the orthogonal projection of $P$ onto $r$.

:::soluzione
The distance is $2\sqrt{2}$ and the orthogonal projection of $P$ onto $r$ is $H = (2, 3, 2)$.
:::
