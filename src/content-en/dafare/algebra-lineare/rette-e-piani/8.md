Compute the angle between the line
$$
r :
\begin{cases}
x - y + 1 = 0 \\[4pt]
z = 2
\end{cases}
$$
and the plane $\pi : y - z = 3$.

:::soluzione
$$
\sin\theta = \frac{1}{2},
\qquad
\theta = \frac{\pi}{6} .
$$
:::
