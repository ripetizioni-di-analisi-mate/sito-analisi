Write the parametric equations of the line
$$
r :
\begin{cases}
x + 2y - z = 3 \\[4pt]
x - y + z = 0
\end{cases}
$$

:::soluzione
The direction vector is $(1, -2, -3)$ and a point is $(1, 1, 0)$, hence
$$
x = 1 + t, \qquad y = 1 - 2t, \qquad z = -3t .
$$
:::
