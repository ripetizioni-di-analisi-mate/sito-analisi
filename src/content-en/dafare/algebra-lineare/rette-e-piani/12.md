titolo: Plane of a pencil parallel to a line
teoria: [fascio-di-piani, piani-per-una-retta, parallelismo-perpendicolarita]

---

Find the plane that contains the line
$$
r :
\begin{cases}
x - y + z = 1 \\[4pt]
2x + y - z = 2
\end{cases}
$$
and is parallel to the line $s : x = t$, $y = 1 + 2t$, $z = 3 - t$.

:::soluzione
In the pencil $\lambda\,(x - y + z - 1) + \mu\,(2x + y - z - 2) = 0$ the parallelism condition gives $2\lambda = 5\mu$, and the plane is
$$
3x - y + z - 3 = 0 .
$$
:::
