Write the parametric and Cartesian equations of the line passing through $A = (2, 1, -1)$ and $B = (4, 0, 1)$.

:::soluzione
Parametric equations $x = 2 + 2t$, $y = 1 - t$, $z = -1 + 2t$; Cartesian equations
$$
\begin{cases}
x + 2y - 4 = 0 \\[4pt]
2y + z - 1 = 0
\end{cases}
$$
:::
