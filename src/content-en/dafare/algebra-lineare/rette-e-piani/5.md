---
titolo: Line and plane with a parameter
teoria: [posizione-retta-piano]
parole: [relative position, line and plane, parameter]
---

As $k \in \mathbb{R}$ varies, determine the relative position of the line $r : x = 1 + t$, $y = 1 - t$, $z = 2 + t$ and the plane $\pi_k : x + 2y + kz = 4$; for $k = 0$ find the common point.

:::soluzione
For $k \neq 1$ the line and the plane are intersecting at a point, which for $k = 0$ is $(0, 2, 1)$; for $k = 1$ the line is parallel to the plane and has no points in common with it.
:::
