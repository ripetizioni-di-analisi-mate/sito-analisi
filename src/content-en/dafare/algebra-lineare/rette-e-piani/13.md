Given the skew lines $r : x = 1 + t$, $y = 2 + t$, $z = 1$ and $s : x = 1 + u$, $y = -1$, $z = -2 + u$, find the distance between the lines and the equations of the line of minimum distance.

:::soluzione
The points of minimum distance are $H = (0, 1, 1)$ and $K = (2, -1, -1)$, the distance is $2\sqrt{3}$ and the line of minimum distance is
$$
x = t, \qquad y = 1 - t, \qquad z = 1 - t .
$$
:::
