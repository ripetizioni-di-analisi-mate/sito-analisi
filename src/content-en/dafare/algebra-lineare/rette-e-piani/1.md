Write the Cartesian equation of the plane passing through the points $A = (1, 2, 0)$, $B = (3, 0, 1)$, $C = (0, 1, 1)$.

:::soluzione
A normal vector is $\overrightarrow{AB} \times \overrightarrow{AC} = (-1, -3, -4)$, and the plane is
$$
x + 3y + 4z - 7 = 0 .
$$
:::
