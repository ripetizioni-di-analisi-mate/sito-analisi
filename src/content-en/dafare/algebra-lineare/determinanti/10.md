---
titolo: Matrix of order 4 with a parameter
teoria: [determinanti-con-parametro, teorema-di-laplace, invertibilita-e-determinante]
parole: [matrix with a parameter, invertibility, determinant of order 4]
---

Determine for which values of $k \in \mathbb{R}$ the matrix
$$
A_k = \begin{pmatrix} k & 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 1 & k & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & k & 2 \\ 0 & 0 & 2 & k \end{pmatrix}
$$
is invertible.

:::soluzione
$\det A_k = (k^2 - 1)(k^2 - 4)$, so $A_k$ is invertible for $k \in \mathbb{R} \setminus \{-2, -1, 1, 2\}$.
:::
