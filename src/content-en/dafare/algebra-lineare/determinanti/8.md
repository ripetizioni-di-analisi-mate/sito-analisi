---
titolo: Inverse of a matrix of order 3
teoria: [formula-dell-inversa, matrice-dei-complementi-algebrici]
parole: [inverse matrix, cofactors, order 3]
---

Compute with cofactors the inverse of
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 3 \end{pmatrix} .
$$

:::soluzione
$$
\det A = 2,
\qquad
A^{-1} = \frac{1}{2} \begin{pmatrix} 5 & -3 & 1 \\ -3 & 3 & -1 \\ 1 & -1 & 1 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
:::
