---
titolo: Determinant of an orthogonal matrix
teoria: [teorema-di-binet, proprieta-del-determinante]
parole: [orthogonal matrix, Binet's theorem, transpose]
---

Let $A \in M_n(\mathbb{R})$ be such that $A^T A = I_n$. Determine the possible values of $\det A$.

:::soluzione
By Binet's theorem and by $\det A^T = \det A$ we have $(\det A)^2 = 1$, so $\det A = 1$ or $\det A = -1$.
:::
