Let $A, B \in M_4(\mathbb{R})$ with $\det A = -2$ and $\det B = 3$. Compute
$$
\det \left( 2 A^{-1} B^T \right) .
$$

:::soluzione
$$
\det \left( 2 A^{-1} B^T \right) = 2^4 \cdot \left( -\frac{1}{2} \right) \cdot 3 = -24 .
$$
:::
