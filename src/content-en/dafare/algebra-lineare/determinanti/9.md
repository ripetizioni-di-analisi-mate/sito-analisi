---
titolo: Tridiagonal matrix with a parameter
teoria: [determinanti-con-parametro, invertibilita-e-determinante]
parole: [matrix with a parameter, invertibility, determinant]
---

Determine for which values of $k \in \mathbb{R}$ the matrix
$$
A_k = \begin{pmatrix} k & 1 & 0 \\ 1 & k & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & k \end{pmatrix}
$$
is invertible.

:::soluzione
$\det A_k = k(k^2 - 2)$, so $A_k$ is invertible for $k \in \mathbb{R} \setminus \{-\sqrt{2}, 0, \sqrt{2}\}$.
:::
