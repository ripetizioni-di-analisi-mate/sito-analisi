Compute the determinant of
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 & 2 & 1 \\ 2 & -1 & 3 & 4 \\ -1 & 2 & 1 & 0 \\ 3 & -3 & 5 & 4 \end{pmatrix}
$$
by reducing the matrix to triangular form.

:::soluzione
$$
\det A = 3 .
$$
:::
