---
titolo: Determinant of order 5 with Laplace expansion
teoria: [teorema-di-laplace, complemento-algebrico]
parole: [Laplace expansion, determinant of order 5]
---

Compute the determinant of
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 & 2 & 0 \\ 3 & 2 & 1 & 0 & 4 \\ 0 & 0 & 3 & 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 0 & 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 2 & 0 & 5 & 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix} .
$$

:::soluzione
$$
\det A = -6 .
$$
:::
