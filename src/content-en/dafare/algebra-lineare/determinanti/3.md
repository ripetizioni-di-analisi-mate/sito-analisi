---
titolo: Determinant of order 4 with Laplace expansion
teoria: [teorema-di-laplace, complemento-algebrico]
parole: [Laplace expansion, cofactor, determinant of order 4]
---

Compute the determinant of
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 & 0 & 3 \\ 0 & 3 & 0 & 1 \\ 1 & -1 & 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 4 \end{pmatrix} .
$$

:::soluzione
$$
\det A = 48 .
$$
:::
