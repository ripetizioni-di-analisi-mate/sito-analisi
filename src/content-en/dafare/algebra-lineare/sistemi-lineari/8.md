---
titolo: System of three equations with Cramer's rule
teoria: [regola-di-cramer]
parole: [Cramer's rule, determinant, 3 × 3 system]
---

Solve with Cramer's rule the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x + y + z = 2 \\[4pt]
2x - y + z = 5 \\[4pt]
x + 2y - z = -3
\end{cases}
$$

:::soluzione
Since $\det A = 7$, the system has the unique solution
$$
(x, y, z) = (1, -1, 2) .
$$
:::
