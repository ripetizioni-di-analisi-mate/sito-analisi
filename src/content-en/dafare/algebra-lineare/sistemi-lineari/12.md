Discuss, as $k \in \mathbb{R}$ varies, the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x + y + z = 1 \\[4pt]
x + 2y + kz = 2 \\[4pt]
x + ky + 2z = k
\end{cases}
$$

:::soluzione
Since $\det A = -k(k - 2)$, for $k \neq 0$ and $k \neq 2$ the unique solution is $(0, 1, 0)$; for $k = 2$ the solutions are $(0, 1 - t, t)$ and for $k = 0$ they are $(-2t, 1 + t, t)$, with $t \in \mathbb{R}$.
:::
