titolo: A single unknown with Cramer's rule
teoria: [regola-di-cramer]
parole: [Cramer's rule, one unknown, determinant]
---

Find the value of the unknown $x$ in the solution of the system
$$
\begin{cases}
2x + y + z = 1 \\[4pt]
x + 3y - z = 2 \\[4pt]
x - y + 2z = 0
\end{cases}
$$

:::soluzione
Since $\det A = 3$ and $\det A_1 = -1$,
$$
x = -\frac{1}{3} .
$$
:::
