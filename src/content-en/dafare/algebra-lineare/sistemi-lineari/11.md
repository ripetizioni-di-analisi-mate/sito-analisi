Find the solutions of the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x + 2y - z = 3 \\[4pt]
2x + 4y + z = 0
\end{cases}
$$
and write them as a particular solution plus solutions of the associated homogeneous system.

:::soluzione
$$
S = \{ (1, 0, -2) + t\,(-2, 1, 0) : t \in \mathbb{R} \} .
$$
:::
