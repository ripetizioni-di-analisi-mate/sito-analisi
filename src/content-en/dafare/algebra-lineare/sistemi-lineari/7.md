---
titolo: Consistency and number of solutions
teoria: [rouche-capelli, incognite-libere]
parole: [Rouché-Capelli, consistency, number of solutions]
---

Determine with the Rouché-Capelli theorem whether the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x_1 + x_2 + x_3 + x_4 = 2 \\[4pt]
x_1 - x_2 + x_3 - x_4 = 0 \\[4pt]
2x_1 + 2x_3 = 2
\end{cases}
$$
is consistent, how many solutions it has and what they are.

:::soluzione
Since $\operatorname{rk} A = \operatorname{rk} (A \mid b) = 2$, the system is consistent with $\infty^2$ solutions:
$$
(x_1, x_2, x_3, x_4) = (1 - s, \ 1 - t, \ s, \ t), \qquad s, t \in \mathbb{R} .
$$
:::
