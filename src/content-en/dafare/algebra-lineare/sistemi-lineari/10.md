Find a basis and the dimension of the solution space of the homogeneous system
$$
\begin{cases}
x_1 + x_2 + x_3 + x_4 = 0 \\[4pt]
x_1 - x_2 + 2x_3 = 0
\end{cases}
$$

:::soluzione
The solution space has dimension $2$; a basis is
$$
S_0 = \langle (-3, 1, 2, 0), \ (-1, -1, 0, 2) \rangle .
$$
:::
