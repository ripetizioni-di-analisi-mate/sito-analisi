---
titolo: Underdetermined system with Gaussian elimination
teoria: [sistema-a-scala, metodo-di-gauss]
parole: [Gaussian elimination, free unknown, infinitely many solutions]
---

Solve with Gaussian elimination the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x - y + z = 2 \\[4pt]
2x + y - z = 1 \\[4pt]
x + 2y - 2z = -1
\end{cases}
$$

:::soluzione
The system has $\infty^1$ solutions:
$$
(x, y, z) = (1, \ t - 1, \ t), \qquad t \in \mathbb{R} .
$$
:::
