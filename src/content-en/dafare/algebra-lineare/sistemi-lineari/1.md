titolo: Determined system with Gaussian elimination
teoria: [operazioni-elementari, sistema-a-scala, metodo-di-gauss]

---

Solve with Gaussian elimination the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x + y + z = 3 \\[4pt]
x + 2y - z = 0 \\[4pt]
2x - y + 3z = 10
\end{cases}
$$

:::soluzione
The system is determined, with the unique solution
$$
(x, y, z) = (3, -1, 1) .
$$
:::
