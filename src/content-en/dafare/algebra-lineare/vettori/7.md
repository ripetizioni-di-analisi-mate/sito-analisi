---
titolo: Orthogonal projection onto a vector
teoria: [proiezione-ortogonale]
parole: [orthogonal projection, decomposition]
---

Decompose $\vec{v} = (0, 4, 5)$ as the sum of a vector parallel to $\vec{u} = (1, 2, 2)$ and a vector orthogonal to $\vec{u}$.

:::soluzione
$\vec{v}_{\parallel} = 2\,\vec{u} = (2, 4, 4)$ and $\vec{v}_{\perp} = (-2, 0, 1)$.
:::
