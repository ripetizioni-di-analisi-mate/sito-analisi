Find the unit vectors orthogonal to both $\vec{u} = (2, -2, -1)$ and $\vec{v} = (1, -2, 0)$.

:::soluzione
$\vec{u} \times \vec{v} = (-2, -1, -2)$ has magnitude $3$, therefore the unit vectors are $\pm \left( \dfrac{2}{3}, \dfrac{1}{3}, \dfrac{2}{3} \right)$.
:::
