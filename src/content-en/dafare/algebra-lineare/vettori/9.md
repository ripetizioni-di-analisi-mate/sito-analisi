Compute the area of the triangle with vertices $A = (1, -1, 2)$, $B = (0, 1, 0)$, $C = (3, 3, 2)$.

:::soluzione
$\overrightarrow{AB} \times \overrightarrow{AC} = (8, -4, -8)$ has magnitude $12$, therefore the area is $6$.
:::
