Compute the volume of the tetrahedron with vertices $A = (1, 1, 1)$, $B = (2, 1, 3)$, $C = (1, 3, 2)$, $D = (3, 2, 1)$.

:::soluzione
The scalar triple product of $\overrightarrow{AB}$, $\overrightarrow{AC}$, $\overrightarrow{AD}$ equals $-9$, therefore the volume is $\dfrac{9}{6} = \dfrac{3}{2}$.
:::
