Write the vector $\vec{w} = (8, 1)$ as a linear combination of the vectors $\vec{u} = (2, 1)$ and $\vec{v} = (1, -1)$.

:::soluzione
$\vec{w} = 3\,\vec{u} + 2\,\vec{v}$.
:::
