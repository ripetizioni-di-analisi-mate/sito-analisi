Given the points $A = (3, -1, 2)$ and $B = (-1, 1, 6)$, find the distance $d(A, B)$, the midpoint of the segment $AB$ and the unit vector of $\overrightarrow{AB}$.

:::soluzione
$\overrightarrow{AB} = (-4, 2, 4)$, therefore $d(A, B) = 6$; the midpoint is $M = (1, 0, 4)$ and the unit vector is $\left( -\dfrac{2}{3}, \dfrac{1}{3}, \dfrac{2}{3} \right)$.
:::
