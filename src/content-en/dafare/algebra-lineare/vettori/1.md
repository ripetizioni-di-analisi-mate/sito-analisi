Given $\vec{u} = (2, -2, 3)$ and $\vec{v} = (-3, -1, 3)$, compute the vector $3\vec{u} - 2\vec{v}$ and its magnitude.

:::soluzione
$3\vec{u} - 2\vec{v} = (12, -4, 3)$, with magnitude $\sqrt{144 + 16 + 9} = 13$.
:::
