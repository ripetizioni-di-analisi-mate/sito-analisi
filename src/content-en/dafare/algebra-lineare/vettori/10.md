Compute the distance of the origin $O = (0, 0, 0)$ from the line through $A = (2, 1, 0)$ and $B = (0, -1, -1)$.

:::soluzione
With $\overrightarrow{AB} = (-2, -2, -1)$ and $\overrightarrow{AO} = (-2, -1, 0)$ one has $\overrightarrow{AB} \times \overrightarrow{AO} = (-1, 2, -2)$, therefore the distance is $\dfrac{3}{3} = 1$.
:::
