Find the values of $k \in \mathbb{R}$ for which the vectors $\vec{u} = (k, 1, k)$ and $\vec{v} = (k, -8, 2)$ are orthogonal.

:::soluzione
$\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v} = k^2 + 2k - 8 = (k + 4)(k - 2)$, therefore the vectors are orthogonal for $k = 2$ and for $k = -4$.
:::
