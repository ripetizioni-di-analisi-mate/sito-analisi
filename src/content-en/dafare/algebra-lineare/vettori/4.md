Compute the angle between the vectors $\vec{u} = (0, 1, -1)$ and $\vec{v} = (1, -2, 1)$.

:::soluzione
$\cos\theta = \dfrac{-3}{\sqrt{2}\,\sqrt{6}} = -\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$, therefore $\theta = \dfrac{5\pi}{6}$.
:::
