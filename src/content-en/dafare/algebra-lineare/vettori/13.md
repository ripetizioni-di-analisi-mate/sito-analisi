titolo: Coplanarity with a parameter
teoria: [criterio-di-complanarita, prodotto-misto]

---

Find the values of $k \in \mathbb{R}$ for which the vectors $\vec{u} = (1, 1, k)$, $\vec{v} = (1, k, 1)$, $\vec{w} = (k, 1, 1)$ are coplanar.

:::soluzione
The scalar triple product equals $-(k - 1)^2 (k + 2)$, therefore the vectors are coplanar for $k = 1$ and for $k = -2$.
:::
