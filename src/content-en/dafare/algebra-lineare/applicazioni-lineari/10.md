Let $f : \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}^3$, $f(x, y) = (x,\ y,\ x + y)$. Find $M^{\mathcal{B}}_{\mathcal{C}}(f)$ with
$$
\mathcal{B} = \big( (1, 1), (1, -1) \big),
\qquad
\mathcal{C} = \big( (1, 0, 0), (1, 1, 0), (1, 1, 1) \big) .
$$

:::soluzione
$$
M^{\mathcal{B}}_{\mathcal{C}}(f) =
\begin{pmatrix}
0 & 2 \\
-1 & -1 \\
2 & 0
\end{pmatrix} .
$$
:::
