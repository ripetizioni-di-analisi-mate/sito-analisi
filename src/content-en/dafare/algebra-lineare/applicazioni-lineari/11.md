Let $f(x, y) = (x + y,\ x - y)$ and $g(x, y) = (2x,\ x + y)$. Write $g \circ f$, determine whether it is an isomorphism and, if so, find its inverse.

:::soluzione
$(g \circ f)(x, y) = (2x + 2y,\ 2x)$, with determinant $-4 \neq 0$; the inverse is
$$
(g \circ f)^{-1}(x, y) = \left( \frac{y}{2},\ \frac{x - y}{2} \right) .
$$
:::
