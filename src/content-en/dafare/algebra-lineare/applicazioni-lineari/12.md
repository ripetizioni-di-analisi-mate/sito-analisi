Let $f_k$ be the endomorphism of $\mathbb{R}^3$ with matrix
$$
A_k =
\begin{pmatrix}
k & 1 & 1 \\
1 & k & 1 \\
0 & 1 & 1
\end{pmatrix} .
$$
Find the kernel and the image as $k \in \mathbb{R}$ varies.

:::soluzione
$\det A_k = k\,(k - 1)$. For $k \neq 0, 1$ the map is an isomorphism. For $k = 0$ we have $\ker f_0 = \langle (1, 1, -1) \rangle$ and $\operatorname{Im} f_0$ is the plane $x - z = 0$. For $k = 1$ we have $\ker f_1 = \langle (0, 1, -1) \rangle$ and $\operatorname{Im} f_1$ is the plane $x - y = 0$.
:::
