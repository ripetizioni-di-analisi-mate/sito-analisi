---
titolo: Existence with values on dependent vectors
teoria: [valori-su-vettori-dipendenti, esistenza-e-unicita, iniettivita-e-nucleo]
parole: [existence, uniqueness, dependent vectors, injective]
---

Determine whether there exists a linear map $f : \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}^3$ such that
$$
f(1, 0) = (1, 1, 0), \qquad
f(1, 1) = (1, 0, 1), \qquad
f(2, 1) = (2, 1, 1) ;
$$
if so, determine whether it is unique and whether it is injective.

:::soluzione
It exists, since $(2, 1) = (1, 0) + (1, 1)$ and $(2, 1, 1) = (1, 1, 0) + (1, 0, 1)$, and it is unique, since $(1, 0)$ and $(1, 1)$ form a basis of $\mathbb{R}^2$; we have $f(x, y) = (x,\ x - y,\ y)$, which is injective because the matrix has rank $2$.
:::
