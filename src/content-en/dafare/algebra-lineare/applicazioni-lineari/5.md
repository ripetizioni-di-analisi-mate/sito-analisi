titolo: Kernel and image of an endomorphism of space
teoria: [calcolo-di-nucleo-e-immagine, teorema-della-dimensione]

---

Find a basis of the kernel and the Cartesian equation of the image of
$$
f(x, y, z) = (x - y,\ y - z,\ x - z) .
$$

:::soluzione
$\ker f = \langle (1, 1, 1) \rangle$ and $\operatorname{Im} f$ is the plane $x + y - z = 0$.
:::
