Write the matrix associated with the linear map $f : \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}^3$
$$
f(x, y) = (2x - y,\ x + y,\ 3y)
$$
with respect to the standard bases and compute $f(1, -2)$.

:::soluzione
$$
A =
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & -1 \\
1 & 1 \\
0 & 3
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
f(1, -2) = (4, -1, -6) .
$$
:::
