Let $f : \mathbb{R}^4 \to \mathbb{R}^2$, $f(x, y, z, w) = (x + y - z,\ y + z + w)$. Determine whether $f$ is surjective and find a basis of the kernel.

:::soluzione
The rank is $2$, so $f$ is surjective, and
$$
\ker f = \langle (2, -1, 1, 0), (1, -1, 0, 1) \rangle .
$$
:::
