Determine whether the matrices
$$
A =
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 2 \\
3 & 4
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
B =
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & 0 \\
1 & 3
\end{pmatrix}
$$
are similar.

:::soluzione
They are not similar, since they have the same trace $5$ and different determinants, $\det A = -2$ and $\det B = 6$.
:::
