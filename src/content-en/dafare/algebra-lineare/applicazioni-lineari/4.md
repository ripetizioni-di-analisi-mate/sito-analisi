---
titolo: Inverse map of an endomorphism
teoria: [isomorfismo, matrice-dell-inversa, isomorfismi-e-determinante]
parole: [inverse map, inverse matrix, isomorphism]
---

Prove that $f(x, y, z) = (x + y,\ y + 2z,\ z)$ is an isomorphism of $\mathbb{R}^3$ and find $f^{-1}$.

:::soluzione
The determinant of the matrix is $1$; the inverse is
$$
f^{-1}(x, y, z) = (x - y + 2z,\ y - 2z,\ z) .
$$
:::
