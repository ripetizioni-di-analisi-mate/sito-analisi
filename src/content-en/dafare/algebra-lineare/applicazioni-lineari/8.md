---
titolo: An endomorphism of polynomials
teoria: [matrice-associata, isomorfismi-e-determinante, isomorfismo-delle-coordinate]
parole: [polynomials, derivative, associated matrix, isomorphism]
---

Let $T : \mathbb{R}_2[x] \to \mathbb{R}_2[x]$, $T(p) = p + p'$. Write the matrix of $T$ with respect to the basis $(1, x, x^2)$, determine whether $T$ is an isomorphism and find the polynomial $p$ such that $T(p) = x^2$.

:::soluzione
$$
M(T) =
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 1 & 0 \\
0 & 1 & 2 \\
0 & 0 & 1
\end{pmatrix},
$$
with determinant $1$, so $T$ is an isomorphism; the required polynomial is $p(x) = x^2 - 2x + 2$.
:::
