Find the kernel and the image of the endomorphism of $\mathbb{R}^3$
$$
f(x, y, z) = (x - 2y,\ y + 3z,\ x + z) .
$$

:::soluzione
The determinant of the matrix is $-5 \neq 0$, so $f$ is an isomorphism, with $\ker f = \{(0, 0, 0)\}$ and $\operatorname{Im} f = \mathbb{R}^3$.
:::
