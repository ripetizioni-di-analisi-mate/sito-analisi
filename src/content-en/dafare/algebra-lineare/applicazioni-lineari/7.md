Find the linear map $f : \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}^3$ such that
$$
f(1, 2) = (1, 0, 1), \qquad f(0, 1) = (2, 1, -1) .
$$

:::soluzione
$$
f(x, y) = (-3x + 2y,\ -2x + y,\ 3x - y) .
$$
:::
