titolo: Center and asymptotes of a hyperbola

Verify that the conic
$$
x^2 - 2xy - 3y^2 + 2x + 6y - 7 = 0
$$
is a hyperbola and find its center and asymptotes.

:::soluzione
We have $I_3 = 16$ and $I_2 = -4$, so it is a hyperbola. The center is $(0, 1)$ and the asymptotes are the lines $x + y - 1 = 0$ and $x - 3y + 3 = 0$.
:::
