Find the center, the canonical form and the semi-axes of the conic
$$
6x^2 + 4xy + 9y^2 - 8x + 14y + 1 = 0 .
$$

:::soluzione
The center is $(1, -1)$ and the canonical form is
$$
\frac{X^2}{2} + Y^2 = 1 ,
$$
an ellipse with semi-axes $\sqrt{2}$ and $1$.
:::
