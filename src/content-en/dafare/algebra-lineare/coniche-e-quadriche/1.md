Classify the conic
$$
2x^2 - 4xy - y^2 + 4x - 2y - 1 = 0 .
$$

:::soluzione
We have $I_3 = 16 \neq 0$ and $I_2 = -6 < 0$, so the conic is a hyperbola; it is not rectangular, since $I_1 = 1 \neq 0$.
:::
