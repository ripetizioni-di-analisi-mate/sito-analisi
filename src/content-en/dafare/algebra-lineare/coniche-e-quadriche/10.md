---
titolo: Degenerate conic consisting of parallel lines
teoria: [conica-degenere, coniche-degeneri]
parole: [degenerate conic, parallel lines, factorization]
---

Verify that the conic
$$
x^2 - 4xy + 4y^2 + 2x - 4y - 3 = 0
$$
is degenerate and write it as the union of two lines.

:::soluzione
We have $I_3 = 0$, $I_2 = 0$ and $\operatorname{rk} A = 2$; the conic is the union of the parallel and distinct lines $x - 2y + 3 = 0$ and $x - 2y - 1 = 0$.
:::
