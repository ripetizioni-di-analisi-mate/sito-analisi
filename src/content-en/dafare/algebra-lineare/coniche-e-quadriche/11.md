---
titolo: Riconoscimento di un iperboloide
teoria: [quadrica, quadriche-non-degeneri, riconoscimento-quadriche]
parole: [quadrica, iperboloide a una falda, centro, forma canonica]
---

Identify the quadric
$$
x^2 + y^2 - 2z^2 - 2x + 4y + 8z - 5 = 0
$$
and find its center and canonical form.

:::soluzione
It is a hyperboloid of one sheet, of revolution, with center $(1, -2, 2)$ and canonical form
$$
\frac{X^2}{2} + \frac{Y^2}{2} - Z^2 = 1 .
$$
:::
