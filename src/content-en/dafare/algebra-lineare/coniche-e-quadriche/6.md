Find the axis, the vertex and the canonical form of the parabola
$$
4x^2 - 4xy + y^2 - 14x - 18y + 31 = 0 .
$$

:::soluzione
The equation can be written $(2x - y - 1)^2 = 10(x + 2y - 3)$. The axis is $2x - y - 1 = 0$, the vertex is $(1, 1)$ and the canonical form is $Y^2 = 2\sqrt{5}\, X$.
:::
