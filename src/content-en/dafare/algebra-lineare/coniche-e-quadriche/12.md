Recognize the quadric
$$
x^2 + 2xy + 2y^2 - 2z = 0 .
$$

:::soluzione
We have $\det A_0 = 0$ and $\det A = -1 < 0$, so it is an elliptic paraboloid (the nonzero eigenvalues of $A_0$ are $\dfrac{3 \pm \sqrt{5}}{2}$, both positive).
:::
