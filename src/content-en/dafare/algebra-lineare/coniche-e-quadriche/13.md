Recognize, as $k \in \mathbb{R}$ varies, the quadric
$$
x^2 + y^2 + kz^2 + 2z = 0 .
$$

:::soluzione
We have $\det A = -1$ for every $k$. For $k > 0$ it is a real ellipsoid (a sphere for $k = 1$), for $k = 0$ an elliptic paraboloid, for $k < 0$ a hyperboloid of two sheets.
:::
