Find the axis, the vertex and the canonical form of the parabola
$$
x^2 + 2xy + y^2 - 12x + 4y + 20 = 0 .
$$

:::soluzione
The equation can be written $(x + y - 2)^2 = 8(x - y - 2)$. The axis is $x + y - 2 = 0$, the vertex is $(2, 0)$ and the canonical form is $Y^2 = 4\sqrt{2}\, X$.
:::
