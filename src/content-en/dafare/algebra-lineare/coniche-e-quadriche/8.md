titolo: Conic with a parameter in the coefficient of y²

Classify, as $k \in \mathbb{R}$ varies, the conic
$$
x^2 + 4xy + ky^2 + 2x = 0 .
$$

:::soluzione
We have $I_3 = -k$, $I_2 = k - 4$, $I_1 = k + 1$. For $k = 0$ the conic is degenerate, consisting of the intersecting lines $x = 0$ and $x + 4y + 2 = 0$; for $k = 4$ it is a parabola; for $k > 4$ it is a real ellipse; for $k < 4$ with $k \neq 0$ it is a hyperbola, rectangular for $k = -1$.
:::
