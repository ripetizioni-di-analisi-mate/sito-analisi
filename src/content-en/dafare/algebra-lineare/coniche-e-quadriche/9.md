Classify, as $k \in \mathbb{R}$ varies, the conic
$$
x^2 + 2xy + ky^2 + 2x + 2y = 0 .
$$

:::soluzione
We have $I_3 = 1 - k$, $I_2 = k - 1$, $I_1 = k + 1$. For $k = 1$ the conic consists of the parallel lines $x + y = 0$ and $x + y + 2 = 0$; for $k > 1$ it is a real ellipse; for $k < 1$ it is a hyperbola, rectangular for $k = -1$.
:::
