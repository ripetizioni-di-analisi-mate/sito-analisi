Find the center and the canonical form of the conic
$$
7x^2 + 6xy - y^2 - 14x - 6y - 1 = 0 .
$$

:::soluzione
The center is $(1, 0)$; the eigenvalues of $A_0$ are $8$ and $-2$ and $\dfrac{I_3}{I_2} = -8$, so the conic is the hyperbola
$$
X^2 - \frac{Y^2}{4} = 1 .
$$
:::
