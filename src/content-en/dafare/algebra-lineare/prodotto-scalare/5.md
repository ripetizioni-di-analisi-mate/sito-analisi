---
titolo: Gram-Schmidt on two vectors in space
teoria: [gram-schmidt, gram-schmidt-nei-calcoli]
parole: [Gram-Schmidt, orthonormal basis]
---

Apply the Gram-Schmidt process to the vectors $v_1 = (1, 0, 1)$ and $v_2 = (1, 2, 3)$ and find an orthonormal basis of $\langle v_1, v_2 \rangle$.

:::soluzione
$$
e_1 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\,(1, 0, 1),
\qquad
e_2 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{6}}\,(-1, 2, 1) .
$$
:::
