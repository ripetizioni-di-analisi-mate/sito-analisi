---
titolo: Orthogonal diagonalization of a matrix of order 2
teoria: [teorema-spettrale, metodo-diagonalizzazione-ortogonale, matrici-simmetriche]
parole: [orthogonal diagonalization, symmetric matrix, eigenvalues]
---

Find an orthogonal matrix $P$ and a diagonal matrix $D$ such that $P^T A P = D$, where
$$
A =
\begin{pmatrix}
7 & 6 \\
6 & -2
\end{pmatrix} .
$$

:::soluzione
The eigenvalues are $10$ and $-5$, with eigenvectors $(2, 1)$ and $(1, -2)$; for example
$$
P = \frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & 1 \\
1 & -2
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
D =
\begin{pmatrix}
10 & 0 \\
0 & -5
\end{pmatrix} .
$$
:::
