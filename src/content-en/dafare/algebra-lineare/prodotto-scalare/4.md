---
titolo: Coordinates with respect to an orthonormal basis
teoria: [base-ortonormale, coordinate-base-ortonormale]
parole: [orthonormal basis, coordinates, scalar product]
---

Verify that the vectors
$$
e_1 = \frac{1}{3}\,(1, 2, 2),
\qquad
e_2 = \frac{1}{3}\,(2, 1, -2),
\qquad
e_3 = \frac{1}{3}\,(2, -2, 1)
$$
form an orthonormal basis of $\mathbb{R}^3$ and compute the coordinates of $v = (3, 3, 3)$ with respect to this basis.

:::soluzione
The three vectors are pairwise orthogonal unit vectors, therefore they form an orthonormal basis; the coordinates of $v$ are
$$
\left( \langle v, e_1 \rangle, \langle v, e_2 \rangle, \langle v, e_3 \rangle \right) = (5, 1, 1) .
$$
:::
