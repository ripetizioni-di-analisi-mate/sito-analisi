Let $W = \{ (x, y, z) \in \mathbb{R}^3 : x + y - z = 0 \}$. Compute the orthogonal projection of $v = (2, 3, 2)$ onto $W$ and the distance of $v$ from $W$.

:::soluzione
$$
P_W(v) = (1, 2, 3),
\qquad
d(v, W) = \sqrt{3} .
$$
:::
