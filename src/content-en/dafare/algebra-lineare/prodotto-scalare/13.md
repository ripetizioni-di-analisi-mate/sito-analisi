titolo: Orthogonal diagonalization with a double eigenvalue

Find an orthogonal matrix $P$ and a diagonal matrix $D$ such that $P^T A P = D$, where
$$
A =
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & -2 & -4 \\
-2 & 4 & -2 \\
-4 & -2 & 1
\end{pmatrix} .
$$

:::soluzione
The eigenvalues are $5$, double, whose eigenspace is the plane $2x + y + 2z = 0$, and $-4$, with eigenspace $\langle (2, 1, 2) \rangle$; for example
$$
P =
\begin{pmatrix}
\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}} & \dfrac{1}{3\sqrt{2}} & \dfrac{2}{3} \\[10pt]
0 & -\dfrac{4}{3\sqrt{2}} & \dfrac{1}{3} \\[10pt]
-\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}} & \dfrac{1}{3\sqrt{2}} & \dfrac{2}{3}
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
D =
\begin{pmatrix}
5 & 0 & 0 \\
0 & 5 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & -4
\end{pmatrix} .
$$
:::
