---
titolo: Gram-Schmidt on three vectors in dimension four
teoria: [gram-schmidt, gram-schmidt-nei-calcoli]
parole: [Gram-Schmidt, orthonormal basis]
---

Apply the Gram-Schmidt process to the vectors $v_1 = (1, 1, 1, 1)$, $v_2 = (1, 1, 0, 0)$, $v_3 = (1, 0, 0, 0)$ of $\mathbb{R}^4$, in the given order, and find an orthonormal basis of the subspace they span.

:::soluzione
$$
e_1 = \frac{1}{2}\,(1, 1, 1, 1),
\qquad
e_2 = \frac{1}{2}\,(1, 1, -1, -1),
\qquad
e_3 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\,(1, -1, 0, 0) .
$$
:::
