---
titolo: Completion of an orthogonal matrix
teoria: [matrice-ortogonale, proprieta-matrici-ortogonali]
parole: [orthogonal matrix, determinant, completion]
---

Determine $a, b, c \in \mathbb{R}$ so that the matrix
$$
A = \frac{1}{3}
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 2 & a \\
2 & 1 & b \\
2 & -2 & c
\end{pmatrix}
$$
is orthogonal with $\det A = 1$.

:::soluzione
$$
(a, b, c) = (-2, 2, -1) .
$$
:::
