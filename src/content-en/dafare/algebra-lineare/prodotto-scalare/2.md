titolo: Parameter for the orthogonality of two vectors

Determine the values of $k \in \mathbb{R}$ for which the vectors $u = (k, 1, k)$ and $v = (k, -5, 4)$ are orthogonal.

:::soluzione
The condition $\langle u, v \rangle = k^2 + 4k - 5 = 0$ is satisfied for
$$
k = 1 \qquad \text{or} \qquad k = -5 .
$$
:::
