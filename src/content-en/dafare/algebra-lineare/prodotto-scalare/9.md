Let
$$
W = \left\{ x \in \mathbb{R}^4 :
\begin{cases}
x_1 + x_2 - x_4 = 0 \\[4pt]
x_2 + x_3 = 0 \\[4pt]
x_1 - x_3 - x_4 = 0
\end{cases}
\right\} .
$$
Determine $\dim W$ and a basis of $W^\perp$.

:::soluzione
The coefficient matrix has rank $2$, therefore $\dim W = 2$, $\dim W^\perp = 2$ and
$$
W^\perp = \langle (1, 1, 0, -1), (0, 1, 1, 0) \rangle .
$$
:::
