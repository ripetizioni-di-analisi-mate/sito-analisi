titolo: Base ortonormale di un piano assegnato in forma cartesiana

Let $W = \{ (x, y, z) \in \mathbb{R}^3 : x - 2y + z = 0 \}$. Find an orthonormal basis of $W$ whose first vector is parallel to and pointing in the same direction as $(1, 1, 1)$.

:::soluzione
$$
e_1 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\,(1, 1, 1),
\qquad
e_2 = \pm \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\,(1, 0, -1) .
$$
:::
