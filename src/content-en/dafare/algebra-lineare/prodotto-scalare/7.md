titolo: Orthonormal basis of a plane given in Cartesian form

Let $W = \{ (x, y, z) \in \mathbb{R}^3 : x - 2y + z = 0 \}$. Find an orthonormal basis of $W$ whose first vector is parallel to $(1, 1, 1)$, with the same orientation.

:::soluzione
$$
e_1 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\,(1, 1, 1),
\qquad
e_2 = \pm \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\,(1, 0, -1) .
$$
:::
