Verify that $v = (1, 1, 1)$ is an eigenvector of the matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 & -1 \\ 0 & 3 & -1 \\ 1 & 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix} ,
$$
determine all the eigenvalues of $A$ and determine whether $A$ is diagonalizable.

:::soluzione
$Av = 2v$; the characteristic polynomial is $p_A(\lambda) = (2 - \lambda)^2 (1 - \lambda)$ and, since $m_g(2) = 1 < 2 = m_a(2)$, the matrix $A$ is not diagonalizable.
:::
