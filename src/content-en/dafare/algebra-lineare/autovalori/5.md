---
titolo: Diagonalization of a matrix of order 2
teoria: [procedura-di-diagonalizzazione, base-di-autovettori, autovalori-distinti]
parole: [diagonalization, diagonalizing matrix, 2x2 matrix]
---

Find an invertible matrix $P$ and a diagonal matrix $D$ such that $P^{-1} A P = D$, where
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 4 & -2 \\ 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix} .
$$

:::soluzione
For example
$$
P = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}, \qquad D = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 3 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
:::
