titolo: Eigenspaces of a matrix of order 3 with a double eigenvalue

Determine the eigenvalues, their algebraic and geometric multiplicities and a basis of each eigenspace of the matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 3 & 0 \\ -4 & 4 & 3 \end{pmatrix} .
$$

:::soluzione
$p_A(\lambda) = (1 - \lambda)(3 - \lambda)^2$, with $m_a(3) = m_g(3) = 2$ and $m_a(1) = m_g(1) = 1$;
$$
V_3 = \langle (1, 1, 0), (0, 0, 1) \rangle, \qquad V_1 = \langle (1, 0, 2) \rangle .
$$
:::
