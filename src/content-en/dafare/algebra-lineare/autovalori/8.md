Determine the real eigenvalues and the corresponding eigenspaces of the matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & -1 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}
$$
and determine whether $A$ is diagonalizable over $\mathbb{R}$.

:::soluzione
$p_A(\lambda) = (1 - \lambda)(\lambda^2 - 2\lambda + 2)$; the only real eigenvalue is $1$, with $V_1 = \langle (1, 0, 0) \rangle$, and the matrix is not diagonalizable over $\mathbb{R}$, because the factor $\lambda^2 - 2\lambda + 2$ has no real roots.
:::
