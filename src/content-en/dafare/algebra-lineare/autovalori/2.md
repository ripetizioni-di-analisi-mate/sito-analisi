---
titolo: Parameter for which a vector is an eigenvector
teoria: [autovalori-di-una-matrice, traccia-e-determinante, autospazio]
parole: [eigenvector, parameter, eigenvalues, trace]
---

Determine $k \in \mathbb{R}$ so that $v = (1, 1)$ is an eigenvector of the matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & k \\ 2 & 3 \end{pmatrix} ,
$$
and for that value compute the eigenvalues and the eigenspaces of $A$.

:::soluzione
$k = 4$; the eigenvalues are $5$ and $-1$, with
$$
V_5 = \langle (1, 1) \rangle, \qquad V_{-1} = \langle (2, -1) \rangle .
$$
:::
