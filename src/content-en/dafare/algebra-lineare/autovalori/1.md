Determine the eigenvalues and eigenspaces of the matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 3 & 1 \\ 2 & 2 \end{pmatrix} .
$$

:::soluzione
The characteristic polynomial is $p_A(\lambda) = (\lambda - 1)(\lambda - 4)$; the eigenspaces are
$$
V_1 = \langle (1, -2) \rangle, \qquad V_4 = \langle (1, 1) \rangle .
$$
:::
