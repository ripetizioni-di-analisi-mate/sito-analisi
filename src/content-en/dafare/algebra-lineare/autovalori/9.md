Determine for which $k \in \mathbb{R}$ the matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & k & 0 \\ 1 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 2 \end{pmatrix}
$$
is diagonalizable.

:::soluzione
$p_A(\lambda) = (2 - \lambda)\big[(1 - \lambda)^2 - k\big]$; the matrix is diagonalizable if and only if $k > 0$.
:::
