Diagonalize the matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 2 \\ 0 & 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 3 \end{pmatrix} ,
$$
that is, find an invertible $P$ and a diagonal $D$ such that $P^{-1} A P = D$.

:::soluzione
The eigenvalues are $1$, $2$, $3$; for example
$$
P = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 1 \\ 0 & -1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}, \qquad D = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 3 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
:::
