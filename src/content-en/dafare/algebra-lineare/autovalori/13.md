titolo: Endomorphism given on the vectors of a basis

Let $f : \mathbb{R}^3 \to \mathbb{R}^3$ be the endomorphism such that
$$
f(1, 1, 0) = (2, 2, 0), \qquad f(0, 1, 1) = (0, 2, 2), \qquad f(1, 0, 1) = (0, 0, 0) .
$$
Determine the eigenvalues and eigenspaces of $f$, determine whether $f$ is diagonalizable and write the matrix associated with $f$ with respect to the standard basis.

:::soluzione
The eigenvalues are $2$, with $V_2 = \langle (1, 1, 0), (0, 1, 1) \rangle$, and $0$, with $V_0 = \langle (1, 0, 1) \rangle$; the endomorphism is diagonalizable and
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & -1 \\ 0 & 2 & 0 \\ -1 & 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
:::
