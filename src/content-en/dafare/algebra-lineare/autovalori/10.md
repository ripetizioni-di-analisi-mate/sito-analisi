---
titolo: Diagonalizability with a parameter on the diagonal
teoria: [matrici-con-parametro, criterio-di-diagonalizzabilita, relazione-tra-molteplicita, autovalori-di-matrici-triangolari]
parole: [parameter, diagonalizability, geometric multiplicity, triangular matrix]
---

Determine for which $k \in \mathbb{R}$ the matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & k & 0 \\ 0 & 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & k \end{pmatrix}
$$
is diagonalizable.

:::soluzione
The matrix is diagonalizable if and only if $k = 0$.
:::
