titolo: Translation endomorphism on polynomials
teoria: [autovalori-di-un-endomorfismo, molteplicita, criterio-di-diagonalizzabilita]

Let $f : \mathbb{R}_2[x] \to \mathbb{R}_2[x]$ be the endomorphism defined by $f(p(x)) = p(x + 1)$. Determine the eigenvalues and eigenspaces of $f$ and determine whether $f$ is diagonalizable.

:::soluzione
The only eigenvalue is $1$, with $m_a(1) = 3$ and $V_1 = \langle 1 \rangle$, consisting of the constant polynomials; since $m_g(1) = 1 < 3$, the endomorphism $f$ is not diagonalizable.
:::
