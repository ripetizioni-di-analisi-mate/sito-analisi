---
titolo: n-th power of a matrix of order 2
teoria: [potenze-di-matrici-diagonalizzabili, procedura-di-diagonalizzazione]
parole: [power of a matrix, A^n, diagonalization]
---

Compute $A^n$ for every integer $n \geq 1$, where
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} -4 & 3 \\ -6 & 5 \end{pmatrix} .
$$

:::soluzione
$$
A^n = \begin{pmatrix} 2(-1)^n - 2^n & 2^n - (-1)^n \\ 2(-1)^n - 2^{n+1} & 2^{n+1} - (-1)^n \end{pmatrix} .
$$
:::
