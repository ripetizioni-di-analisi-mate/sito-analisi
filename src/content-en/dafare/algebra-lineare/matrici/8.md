Use the Gauss-Jordan method to compute the inverse of the matrix
$$
A =
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 0 & 2 \\
2 & 1 & 3 \\
1 & 1 & 2
\end{pmatrix} .
$$

:::soluzione
$$
A^{-1} =
\begin{pmatrix}
-1 & 2 & -2 \\
-1 & 0 & 1 \\
1 & -1 & 1
\end{pmatrix}
$$
:::
