Given the matrices
$$
A =
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & -1 & 2 \\
0 & 3 & 1
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
B =
\begin{pmatrix}
5 & 3 \\
1 & 6 \\
7 & 5
\end{pmatrix},
$$
find the matrix $X$ such that $3X + 2A^T = B$.

:::soluzione
$$
X = \frac{1}{3}\left( B - 2A^T \right) =
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 1 \\
1 & 0 \\
1 & 1
\end{pmatrix}
$$
:::
