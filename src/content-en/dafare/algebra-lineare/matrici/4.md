---
titolo: Checking commutativity
teoria: [matrici-che-commutano, prodotto-righe-per-colonne]
parole: [commuting matrices, matrix product]
---

Determine whether the matrices
$$
A =
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 2 \\
0 & 3
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
B =
\begin{pmatrix}
3 & -2 \\
0 & 1
\end{pmatrix}
$$
commute.

:::soluzione
The matrices commute, since
$$
AB = BA =
\begin{pmatrix}
3 & 0 \\
0 & 3
\end{pmatrix}
= 3 I_2 .
$$
:::
