---
titolo: Invertibility depending on a parameter
teoria: [teorema-di-gauss-jordan, matrice-a-scala]
parole: [invertibility, parameter, row echelon reduction, pivot]
---

Find the values of the real parameter $k$ for which the matrix
$$
A_k =
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 1 & k \\
1 & k & 1 \\
k & 1 & 1
\end{pmatrix}
$$
is invertible.

:::soluzione
Row echelon reduction gives $(1 - k)(2 + k)$ as the last diagonal entry, therefore $A_k$ is invertible for $k \neq 1$ and $k \neq -2$.
:::
