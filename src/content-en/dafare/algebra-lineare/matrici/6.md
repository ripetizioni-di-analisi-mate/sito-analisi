Find the reduced row echelon form of the matrix
$$
A =
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 2 & 0 & 1 \\
2 & 4 & 1 & 4 \\
1 & 2 & 1 & 3
\end{pmatrix} .
$$

:::soluzione
$$
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 2 & 0 & 1 \\
0 & 0 & 1 & 2 \\
0 & 0 & 0 & 0
\end{pmatrix}
$$
:::
