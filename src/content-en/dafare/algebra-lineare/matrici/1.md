Given the matrices
$$
A =
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 0 & -1 \\
2 & 3 & 1
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
B =
\begin{pmatrix}
0 & 1 & 1 \\
1 & -1 & 2
\end{pmatrix},
$$
compute $2A - 3B$.

:::soluzione
$$
2A - 3B =
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & -3 & -5 \\
1 & 9 & -4
\end{pmatrix}
$$
:::
