---
titolo: Inverse of a 2 × 2 matrix
teoria: [teorema-di-gauss-jordan, metodo-di-gauss-jordan]
parole: [inverse matrix, Gauss-Jordan]
---

Compute with the Gauss-Jordan method the inverse of the matrix
$$
A =
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & 5 \\
1 & 3
\end{pmatrix} .
$$

:::soluzione
$$
A^{-1} =
\begin{pmatrix}
3 & -5 \\
-1 & 2
\end{pmatrix}
$$
:::
