---
titolo: Matrices that commute with a rotation
teoria: [matrici-che-commutano]
parole: [commuting matrices, AX = XA]
---

Find all matrices $X \in M_2(\mathbb{R})$ that commute with
$$
A =
\begin{pmatrix}
0 & 1 \\
-1 & 0
\end{pmatrix} .
$$

:::soluzione
$$
X =
\begin{pmatrix}
a & b \\
-b & a
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad a, b \in \mathbb{R}
$$
:::
