---
titolo: Matrix equation with an invertible matrix
teoria: [equazioni-matriciali, matrice-invertibile]
parole: [matrix equation, inverse matrix, AX = B]
---

Solve the matrix equation $AX = B$, where
$$
A =
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & 1 \\
5 & 3
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
B =
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 0 \\
2 & 1
\end{pmatrix} .
$$

:::soluzione
$$
X = A^{-1} B =
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & -1 \\
-1 & 2
\end{pmatrix}
$$
:::
