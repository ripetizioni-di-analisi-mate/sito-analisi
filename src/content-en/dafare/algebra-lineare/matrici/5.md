Reduce the matrix
$$
A =
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & 4 & -2 \\
1 & 3 & 0 \\
3 & 5 & -4
\end{pmatrix}
$$
to row echelon form and indicate how many pivots are obtained and in which columns.

:::soluzione
A row echelon form is
$$
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & 4 & -2 \\
0 & 1 & 1 \\
0 & 0 & 0
\end{pmatrix},
$$
with two pivots, in columns $1$ and $2$.
:::
