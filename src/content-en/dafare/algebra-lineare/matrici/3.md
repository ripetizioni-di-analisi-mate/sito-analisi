teoria: [prodotto-righe-per-colonne]

Given the matrices
$$
A =
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 2 \\
0 & -1 \\
3 & 1
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
B =
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & 0 & 1 \\
1 & -1 & 0
\end{pmatrix},
$$
compute, if defined, the products $AB$ and $BA$.

:::soluzione
Both products are defined:
$$
AB =
\begin{pmatrix}
4 & -2 & 1 \\
-1 & 1 & 0 \\
7 & -1 & 3
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
BA =
\begin{pmatrix}
5 & 5 \\
1 & 3
\end{pmatrix} .
$$
:::
