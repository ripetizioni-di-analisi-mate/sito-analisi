Given the matrix
$$
A =
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & 1 \\
1 & 2
\end{pmatrix},
$$
verify that $A^2 - 4A + 3I_2 = O$ and use it to find $A^{-1}$ without reducing to row echelon form.

:::soluzione
From $A(4I_2 - A) = 3I_2$ it follows that
$$
A^{-1} = \frac{1}{3}\,(4I_2 - A) = \frac{1}{3}
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & -1 \\
-1 & 2
\end{pmatrix} .
$$
:::
