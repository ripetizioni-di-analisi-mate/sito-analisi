---
titolo: Complement of a plane with a parameter
teoria: [somma-diretta, criterio-dei-supplementari, formula-di-grassmann]
parole: [direct sum, complementary subspaces, parameter, Grassmann's formula]
---

In $\mathbb{R}^3$ consider the plane $U : x + y - z = 0$ and the line $W_k = \langle (1, k, 1) \rangle$, with $k \in \mathbb{R}$. Determine for which values of $k$ we have
$$
\mathbb{R}^3 = U \oplus W_k .
$$

:::soluzione
$\mathbb{R}^3 = U \oplus W_k$ if and only if $k \neq 0$; for $k = 0$ the line $W_0$ is contained in $U$.
:::
