In $\mathbb{R}^4$ consider
$$
U : x_1 + x_2 + x_3 + x_4 = 0 ,
\qquad
W = \langle (1, -1, 0, 0), (1, 1, 1, 1), (0, 0, 1, -1) \rangle .
$$
Find a basis of $U \cap W$ and the dimension of $U + W$.

:::soluzione
$U \cap W = \langle (1, -1, 0, 0), (0, 0, 1, -1) \rangle$ has dimension $2$, and by Grassmann's formula $\dim (U + W) = 3 + 3 - 2 = 4$, that is
$$
U + W = \mathbb{R}^4 .
$$
:::
