---
titolo: Independence as a parameter varies
teoria: [indipendenza-lineare, indipendenza-e-rango]
parole: [linear independence, parameter, determinant]
---

Determine for which values of $k \in \mathbb{R}$ the vectors
$$
v_1 = (1, k, 0) , \qquad v_2 = (0, 1, k) , \qquad v_3 = (k, 0, 1)
$$
are linearly independent.

:::soluzione
The determinant of the matrix that has the three vectors as columns equals $1 + k^3 = (k + 1)(k^2 - k + 1)$, so the vectors are independent if and only if
$$
k \neq -1 .
$$
:::
