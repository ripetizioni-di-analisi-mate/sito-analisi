---
titolo: Four matrices of order two
teoria: [base, conseguenze-della-dimensione, calcoli-in-coordinate, caratterizzazione-dipendenza]
parole: [basis, matrices, linear independence, coordinates, dimension]
---

Determine whether the matrices
$$
A_1 = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} , \quad
A_2 = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix} , \quad
A_3 = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} , \quad
A_4 = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}
$$
form a basis of $M_2(\mathbb{R})$, and find the dimension of the subspace they span.

:::soluzione
They do not form a basis: the relation $A_1 - A_2 - A_3 + A_4 = O$ holds, and the matrices span a subspace of dimension $3$, with basis $(A_1, A_2, A_3)$.
:::
