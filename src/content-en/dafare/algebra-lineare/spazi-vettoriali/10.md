Complete the vectors $v_1 = (0, 0, 1, 1)$ and $v_2 = (1, 2, 0, 1)$ to a basis of $\mathbb{R}^4$ by adding vectors of the standard basis.

:::soluzione
Reducing the rows $v_2$, $v_1$ to echelon form, the pivots are in columns $1$ and $3$; we add $e_2$ and $e_4$, and $(v_1, v_2, e_2, e_4)$ is a basis of $\mathbb{R}^4$.
:::
