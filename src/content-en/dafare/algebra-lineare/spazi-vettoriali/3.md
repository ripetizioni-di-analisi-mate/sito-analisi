Verify that
$$
W = \{ p \in \mathbb{R}_3[x] : p(-x) = p(x) \ \text{for every } x \}
$$
is a subspace of $\mathbb{R}_3[x]$ and find a basis and the dimension of it.

:::soluzione
$W$ is the subspace of the polynomials $a + cx^2$, with basis $(1, x^2)$ and
$$
\dim W = 2 .
$$
:::
