---
titolo: Subset not closed under addition
teoria: [sottospazio, criterio-sottospazio]
parole: [subspace, subspace criterion, counterexample, closure under addition]
---

Determine whether
$$
W = \{ (x, y) \in \mathbb{R}^2 : x^2 = y^2 \}
$$
is a subspace of $\mathbb{R}^2$.

:::soluzione
$W$ is not a subspace: $(1, 1)$ and $(1, -1)$ lie in $W$, while their sum $(2, 0)$ does not belong to it, since $4 \neq 0$.
:::
