---
titolo: Subspace defined by two equations
teoria: [sottospazio, criterio-sottospazio, sottospazi-ed-equazioni]
parole: [subspace, subspace criterion, homogeneous system, line through the origin]
---

Determine whether
$$
W = \{ (x, y, z) \in \mathbb{R}^3 : x + y = z, \ x = 3y \}
$$
is a subspace of $\mathbb{R}^3$ and, if so, find a basis of it.

:::soluzione
$W$ is a subspace, because it is the solution set of a homogeneous linear system; it is the line
$$
W = \langle (3, 1, 4) \rangle .
$$
:::
