---
titolo: Dependence relation among three vectors
teoria: [indipendenza-lineare, caratterizzazione-dipendenza]
parole: [linear dependence, dependence relation, linear combination]
---

Determine whether the vectors $v_1 = (1, 2, 3)$, $v_2 = (2, 1, 0)$, $v_3 = (1, -1, -3)$ are linearly independent; if not, write one of them as a linear combination of the others.

:::soluzione
The vectors are linearly dependent, with relation $v_1 - v_2 + v_3 = 0$, that is
$$
v_3 = v_2 - v_1 .
$$
:::
