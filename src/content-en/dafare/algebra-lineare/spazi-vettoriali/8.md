Verify that $\mathcal{B} = (1, \ 1 + x, \ 1 + x + x^2)$ is a basis of $\mathbb{R}_2[x]$ and find the coordinates of $p(x) = 3 - 2x + 5x^2$ with respect to $\mathcal{B}$.

:::soluzione
$\mathcal{B}$ is a basis, because the coordinate matrix is triangular with determinant $1$; the coordinates of $p$ are
$$
(5, -7, 5) .
$$
:::
