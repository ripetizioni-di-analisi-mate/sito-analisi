Let $W = \langle (1, 1, 2), (2, 0, 1), (0, 2, 3), (3, 1, 3) \rangle \subseteq \mathbb{R}^3$. Extract a basis of $W$ from the given generators, and find $\dim W$ and a Cartesian equation of $W$.

:::soluzione
$\dim W = 2$, with basis $\big( (1, 1, 2), (2, 0, 1) \big)$; a Cartesian equation is
$$
x + 3y - 2z = 0 .
$$
:::
