A contoured nozzle at a pipe outlet has exit diameter $d = 45\,\mathrm{mm}$, velocity coefficient $C_v = 0.97$, and $C_c = 1$. Total head immediately upstream is $H = 55.0\,\mathrm{m}$ above the outlet axis. Find the jet speed, discharge, and power.

:::soluzione
$v = C_v\sqrt{2gH} = 31.9\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $A = 1.59 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^2}$; $Q = 0.0507\,\mathrm{m^3/s} = 50.7\,\mathrm{L/s}$; $P = \gamma Q \dfrac{v^2}{2g} = 25.7\,\mathrm{kW}$.
:::
