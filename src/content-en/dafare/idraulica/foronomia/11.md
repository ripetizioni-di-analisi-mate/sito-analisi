A rectangular sharp-crested weir has horizontal crest width $b = 1.50\,\mathrm{m}$ and a ventilated nappe. The measured upstream head is $h = 0.32\,\mathrm{m}$ and the discharge coefficient is $\mu = 0.42$. Calculate the discharge.

:::soluzione
$\sqrt{2gh} = 2.51\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $Q = \mu\,b\,h\,\sqrt{2gh} = 0.505\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$.
:::
