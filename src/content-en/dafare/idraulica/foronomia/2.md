titolo: "Bottom orifice in a pressurized tank"
teoria: [portata-di-una-luce, luce, coefficienti-di-efflusso]

A closed tank contains water beneath air at gauge pressure $p_0 = 35\,\mathrm{kPa}$. A circular bottom orifice of diameter $D = 60\,\mathrm{mm}$ with $\mu = 0.60$ discharges to the atmosphere. The free surface is maintained $1.80\,\mathrm{m}$ above the opening. Calculate the discharge.

:::soluzione
The head driving flow through the orifice is $h = \dfrac{p_0}{\gamma} + 1.80 = 3.57 + 1.80 = 5.37\,\mathrm{m}$, giving $Q = 0.0174\,\mathrm{m^3/s} = 17.4\,\mathrm{L/s}$.
:::
