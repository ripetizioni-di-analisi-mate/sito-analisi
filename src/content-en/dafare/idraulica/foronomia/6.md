In a rectangular channel of width $b = 3.00\,\mathrm{m}$ a vertical sluice gate is raised $a = 0.45\,\mathrm{m}$ above the bed. The upstream depth is $2.60\,\mathrm{m}$ and downstream discharge is free, as shown. The gate coefficients are $\mu = 0.58$ and $C_c = 0.61$. Calculate the discharge and the Froude number at the vena contracta.

::schema{solidi="0,-0.35 6,-0.35 6,0 0,0;2.9,0.45 3.1,0.45 3.1,3.2 2.9,3.2" acqua="0,0 2.9,0 2.9,2.6 0,2.6;2.9,0 3.1,0.45 3.5,0.3 6,0.275 6,0 3.1,0" pelo="0,2.9,2.6,;4,5.6,0.275," tratteggi="3.5,0 3.5,0.8" frecce="3.9,0.14,4.7,0.14,v_c" quote="0 0,0 2.6,2.60 m,-0.5;3 0,3 0.45,0.45 m,-0.45" titolo="Flat gate raised by $a = 0.45\,\mathrm{m}$ in a channel of width $b = 3.00\,\mathrm{m}$, with upstream water depth $2.60\,\mathrm{m}$"}

:::soluzione
$Q = \mu\,b\,a\,\sqrt{2 g h} = 5.59\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$, $a_c = 0.275\,\mathrm{m}$ and $v_c = 6.79\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Since $Fr_c = 4.14$, the flow downstream of the sluice gate is supercritical.
:::
