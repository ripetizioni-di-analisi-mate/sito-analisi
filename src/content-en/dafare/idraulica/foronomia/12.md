A sharp-crested Thomson V-notch weir with included angle $\theta = 90^\circ$ and coefficient $\mu = 0.60$ is to measure discharge $Q = 32\,\mathrm{L/s}$. Find the head above the notch vertex and the relative discharge error caused by a head reading error $1\,\mathrm{mm}$.

:::soluzione
$Q = 1.42\,h^{\frac{5}{2}}$, giving $h = \left(\dfrac{0.032}{1.417}\right)^{\frac{2}{5}} = 0.220\,\mathrm{m}$. The relative error is $\dfrac{5}{2}\dfrac{dh}{h} = 1.1\%$.
:::
