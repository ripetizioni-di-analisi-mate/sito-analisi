A tank with constant rectangular plan dimensions $4.00 \times 2.50\,\mathrm{m}$ drains without inflow through a circular bottom orifice of diameter $D = 120\,\mathrm{mm}$ with $\mu = 0.60$. The initial level is $2.80\,\mathrm{m}$ above the orifice center. Calculate the initial discharge and the time to empty completely.

:::soluzione
$\mu A \sqrt{2g} = 0.0301\,\mathrm{m^{5/2}/s}$; $Q_0 = 0.0503\,\mathrm{m^3/s} = 50.3\,\mathrm{L/s}$; $t = 1113\,\mathrm{s} = 18.6\,\mathrm{min}$.
:::
