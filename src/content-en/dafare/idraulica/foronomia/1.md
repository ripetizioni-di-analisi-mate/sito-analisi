A constant-level tank discharges to the atmosphere through a circular orifice of diameter $D = 120\,\mathrm{mm}$ in a thin vertical wall. Its center is $2.50\,\mathrm{m}$ below the free surface and its discharge coefficient is $\mu = 0.61$. Calculate the discharge.

:::soluzione
$A = 0.0113\,\mathrm{m^2}$; $\sqrt{2gh} = 7.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $Q = 0.0483\,\mathrm{m^3/s} = 48.3\,\mathrm{L/s}$.
:::
