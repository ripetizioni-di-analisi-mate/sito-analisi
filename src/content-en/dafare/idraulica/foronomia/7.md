Two tanks communicate through a square orifice of side $\ell = 150\,\mathrm{mm}$ in their partition, submerged on both sides, as shown. Their surfaces are held at a constant level difference $1.20\,\mathrm{m}$ and the discharge coefficient is $\mu = 0.60$. Calculate the discharge.

:::soluzione
$A = 0.0225\,\mathrm{m^2}$; $\sqrt{2 g\,\Delta h} = 4.85\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $Q = 0.0655\,\mathrm{m^3/s} = 65.5\,\mathrm{L/s}$.
