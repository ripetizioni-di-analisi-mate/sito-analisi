Two tanks of constant plan areas $\Omega_1 = 20.0\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and $\Omega_2 = 12.0\,\mathrm{m^2}$ communicate through a circular orifice of diameter $D = 150\,\mathrm{mm}$ with $\mu = 0.60$. The orifice remains submerged on both sides throughout the transfer, as shown. The initial level difference is $2.50\,\mathrm{m}$. Find the time to equalize the levels and the volume transferred.

:::soluzione
$k = \mu A \sqrt{2g} = 0.0470\,\mathrm{m^{5/2}/s}$, $c = k\left(\dfrac{1}{\Omega_1} + \dfrac{1}{\Omega_2}\right) = 6.26 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^{1/2}/s}$ and $t = 505\,\mathrm{s} = 8.4\,\mathrm{min}$. The volume transferred is $V = 18.75\,\mathrm{m^3}$.
:::
