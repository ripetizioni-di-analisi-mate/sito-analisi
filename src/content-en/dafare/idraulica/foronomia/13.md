A rectangular channel has a sharp-edged broad-crested weir of width $b = 2.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and crest length $1.60\,\mathrm{m}$. The upstream surface is $H = 0.45\,\mathrm{m}$ above the crest, and approach velocity is negligible, as shown. Given discharge coefficient $\mu = 0.385$, find the discharge and critical depth on the crest.

::schema{solidi="0,-0.35 7,-0.35 7,0 0,0;3,0 4.6,0 4.6,1.2 3,1.2" acqua="0,0 3,0 3,1.65 0,1.65;3,1.2 4.6,1.2 4.6,1.42 3,1.65;4.6,1.42 5,1.1 5.35,0.3 5.45,0 4.95,0 4.9,0.45 4.75,0.9 4.6,1.2" pelo="0,3,1.65,;3,4.6,1.42," tratteggi="2.2,1.65 3,1.65;2.2,1.2 3,1.2" quote="3 1.2,3 1.65,0.45 m,-0.5;3 0,4.6 0,1.60 m,0.6" titolo="Broad-crested sill of width $b = 2.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and crest length $1.60\,\mathrm{m}$, with upstream head $H = 0.45\,\mathrm{m}$"}

:::soluzione
$Q = \mu\,b\,H\,\sqrt{2 g H} = 1.03\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. Alternatively, using the critical depth $h_c = \dfrac{2}{3}H = 0.30\,\mathrm{m}$ gives the same discharge, $Q = b\,h_c\sqrt{g\,h_c} = 1.03\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$.
:::
