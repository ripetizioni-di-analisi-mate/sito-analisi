A constant-level tank discharges to the atmosphere through an orifice in a thin vertical wall, with head $h = 2.40\,\mathrm{m}$ at the center. A point on the jet axis lies $x = 2.80\,\mathrm{m}$ horizontally and $y = 0.90\,\mathrm{m}$ below the center of the vena contracta, as shown. Find the velocity coefficient and the head loss.

::schema{solidi="-3.3,-0.3 0.3,-0.3 0.3,0 -3.3,0;-3.3,0 -3,0 -3,5.8 -3.3,5.8;0,0 0.3,0 0.3,2.88 0,2.88;0,3.12 0.3,3.12 0.3,5.8 0,5.8" acqua="-3,0 0,0 0,5.4 -3,5.4" pelo="-3,0,5.4,free surface" linee="0,3 0.7,2.944 1.4,2.775 2.1,2.494 2.8,2.1 3.2,1.824" punti="2.8,2.1,P" tratteggi="0,2.1 2.8,2.1;2.8,2.1 2.8,3" quote="0 3,0 5.4,2.40 m,-0.6;0 3,2.8 3,2.80 m,-0.4;2.8 3,2.8 2.1,0.90 m,-0.4" titolo="Jet trajectory. Point $P$ lies $2.80\,\mathrm{m}$ horizontally and $0.90\,\mathrm{m}$ vertically from the vena contracta"}

:::soluzione
$v_c = 6.54\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $C_v = 0.953$. The head loss is $\Delta H = \left(1 - C_v^2\right)h = 0.222\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
