A test on a constant-level tank uses a circular orifice of diameter $D = 40\,\mathrm{mm}$ in a thin vertical wall, with head $h = 2.20\,\mathrm{m}$ at its center. The measured discharge is $Q = 4.90\,\mathrm{L/s}$ and the vena contracta diameter is $D_c = 31.5\,\mathrm{mm}$. Find the contraction, velocity, and discharge coefficients.

:::soluzione
$C_c = \left(\dfrac{31.5}{40}\right)^2 = 0.620$; $\mu = 0.593$; $C_v = 0.957$.
:::
