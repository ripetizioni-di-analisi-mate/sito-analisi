A rectangular opening of width $b = 2.00\,\mathrm{m}$ in a vertical reservoir wall extends from depth $1.00\,\mathrm{m}$ to depth $2.60\,\mathrm{m}$, as shown. The discharge coefficient is $\mu = 0.61$. Find the discharge and its difference from the small-orifice approximation.

::schema{solidi="-3.3,-0.3 0.3,-0.3 0.3,0 -3.3,0;-3.3,0 -3,0 -3,3.4 -3.3,3.4;0,0 0.3,0 0.3,0.4 0,0.4;0,2 0.3,2 0.3,3.4 0,3.4" acqua="-3,0 0,0 0,3 -3,3;0,0.4 0.9,0.5 1.8,0.54 1.8,1.92 0.9,1.95 0,2" pelo="-3,0,3,free surface" quote="0 2,0 3,1.00 m,-0.5;0 0.4,0 3,2.60 m,-1.4" titolo="Rectangular orifice of width $b = 2.00\,\mathrm{m}$ with its edges at depths $1.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and $2.60\,\mathrm{m}$ below the free surface"}

:::soluzione
$Q = \dfrac{2}{3}\,\mu\,b\,\sqrt{2g}\left(h_2^{\frac{3}{2}} - h_1^{\frac{3}{2}}\right) = 11.5\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. The small-orifice approximation gives $Q_p = 11.6\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$, an overestimate of $0.86\%$.
:::
