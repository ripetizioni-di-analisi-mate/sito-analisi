A vertical cylindrical tank of diameter $4.00\,\mathrm{m}$ drains without inflow through a circular side orifice of diameter $D = 100\,\mathrm{mm}$ with $\mu = 0.61$. The orifice center is $0.60\,\mathrm{m}$ above the tank floor. Initially the surface is $3.60\,\mathrm{m}$ above the floor. How long does it take to fall to $1.60\,\mathrm{m}$ above the floor?

:::soluzione
The initial and final heads driving flow through the orifice are $h_0 = 3.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and $h_1 = 1.00\,\mathrm{m}$. The remaining results are $\Omega = 12.6\,\mathrm{m^2}$, $\mu A \sqrt{2g} = 0.0212\,\mathrm{m^{5/2}/s}$ and $t = 867\,\mathrm{s} = 14.4\,\mathrm{min}$.
:::
