A rectangular channel of width $b = 5.00\,\mathrm{m}$ with $k_s = 55\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$ and bed slope $i = 0.0007$ carries $Q = 10.0\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. A barrier at the downstream end imposes a depth of $h = 2.20\,\mathrm{m}$ at the last section. Find the critical depth, normal depth and critical slope, classify the profile and give the direction in which it should be traced.

:::soluzione
$h_c = 0.742\,\mathrm{m}$, $h_0 = 1.454\,\mathrm{m}$ and $i_c = 0.00507$. The slope is mild and the **M1** profile (zone 1) increases in depth downstream. The flow is subcritical ($Fr = 0.196$ at the terminal section), so trace the profile from downstream to upstream.
:::
