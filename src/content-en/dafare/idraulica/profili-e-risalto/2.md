A rectangular channel of width $b = 3.50\,\mathrm{m}$ with $k_s = 45\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$ and bed slope $i = 0.0009$ carries $Q = 5.00\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ and ends in a bed drop. Find the critical depth, normal depth and critical slope. Identify the control section and classify the profile immediately upstream of it.

:::soluzione
$h_c = 0.593\,\mathrm{m}$, $h_0 = 1.290\,\mathrm{m}$ and $i_c = 0.00851$. The slope is mild. The outlet is the control section, where $h = h_c = 0.593\,\mathrm{m}$. The **M2** profile (zone 2) decreases in depth downstream and is traced from downstream to upstream.
:::
