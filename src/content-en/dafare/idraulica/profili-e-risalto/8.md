In a horizontal rectangular channel of width $b = 3.00\,\mathrm{m}$, the measured depths upstream and downstream of a hydraulic jump are $h_1 = 0.40\,\mathrm{m}$ and $h_2 = 1.52\,\mathrm{m}$, respectively. Find the upstream Froude number and the channel discharge.

:::soluzione
$Fr_1 = 3.02$ and $Q = 7.18\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$, or $7180\,\mathrm{L/s}$.
:::
