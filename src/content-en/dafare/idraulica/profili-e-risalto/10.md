A horizontal rectangular channel of width $b = 5.00\,\mathrm{m}$ carries $Q = 8.00\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ at a supercritical depth of $h_1 = 0.30\,\mathrm{m}$. Find the Froude number, conjugate depth, head loss and jump length, and classify the jump.

:::soluzione
$Fr_1 = 3.11$, $h_2 = 1.18\,\mathrm{m}$, $\Delta E = 0.478\,\mathrm{m}$ and $L \simeq 5.26\,\mathrm{m}$. This is an **oscillating** jump ($2.5 < Fr_1 < 4.5$).
:::
