titolo: "Submerged jump downstream of a sluice gate"

A rectangular channel of width $b = 3.00\,\mathrm{m}$ with $k_s = 50\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$ and bed slope $i = 0.0009$ carries $Q = 7.50\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. A sluice gate releases water at depth $h_1 = 0.35\,\mathrm{m}$, and the downstream reach is long enough to establish uniform flow. Find $h_c$, $h_0$ and the depth conjugate to $h_1$, and determine where the jump occurs.

:::soluzione
$h_c = 0.860\,\mathrm{m}$, $h_0 = 1.880\,\mathrm{m}$ and $i_c = 0.00755$, so the slope is mild. The conjugate depth of $h_1$ is $1.74\,\mathrm{m}$, less than $h_0 = 1.880\,\mathrm{m}$. The high tailwater therefore forces the jump back against the gate, which is **submerged**.
:::
