A rectangular channel of width $b = 4.00\,\mathrm{m}$ with $k_s = 50\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$ carries $Q = 10.0\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. The upstream bed slope is $i_1 = 0.030$ and the downstream slope is $i_2 = 0.0015$, as shown. Find $h_c$, $i_c$, the two normal depths and the depth conjugate to the upstream flow. Determine on which side of the slope change the jump forms and which profile follows it.

::schema{solidi="0,2.00 5,1.40 12,1.20 12,0.80 5,1.00 0,1.60" frecce="0.6,2.25,1.7,2.22,Q" tratteggi="5,1.40 5,2.70" etichette="2.2,1.00,i_1 = 0.030;9.0,0.45,i_2 = 0.0015;5.4,2.95,slope change" riferimento=0 titolo="Transition from a steep reach to a mildly sloping reach"}

:::soluzione
$h_c = 0.860\,\mathrm{m}$, $i_c = 0.00665$, $h_{01} = 0.521\,\mathrm{m}$ (steep slope) and $h_{02} = 1.450\,\mathrm{m}$ (mild slope). The conjugate depth of $h_{01}$ is $1.33\,\mathrm{m}$, smaller than $h_{02}$. The jump is forced upstream onto the steep reach. It is followed by an **S1** profile that reaches $h_{02}$ at the slope break.
:::
