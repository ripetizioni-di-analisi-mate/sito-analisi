A horizontal rectangular channel of width $b = 4.00\,\mathrm{m}$ carries $Q = 13.0\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ at a supercritical depth of $h_1 = 0.50\,\mathrm{m}$. Find the conjugate depth, head loss, dissipated power and jump length using Smetana's formula for the length.

:::soluzione
$Fr_1 = 2.93$; $h_2 = 1.84\,\mathrm{m}$; $\Delta E = 0.654\,\mathrm{m}$; $P = 83.4\,\mathrm{kW}$; $L \simeq 8.04\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
