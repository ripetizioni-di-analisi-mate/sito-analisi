A rectangular channel of width $b = 4.50\,\mathrm{m}$ with $k_s = 50\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$ carries $Q = 9.00\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. The upstream bed slope is $i_1 = 0.0008$ and the downstream slope is $i_2 = 0.025$, as shown. Find the critical depth, critical slope and two normal depths, identify the control section, and classify the profile in each reach.

::schema{solidi="0,1.30 6,1.15 12,0.25 12,-0.15 6,0.75 0,0.90" frecce="1.0,1.95,2.8,1.92,Q" tratteggi="6,1.15 6,2.40" etichette="2.8,0.30,i_1 = 0.0008;9.4,-0.20,i_2 = 0.025;6.0,2.65,slope change" riferimento=0 titolo="Change of bed slope from $i_1 = 0.0008$ to $i_2 = 0.025$"}

:::soluzione
$h_c = 0.742\,\mathrm{m}$, $i_c = 0.00634$, $h_{01} = 1.512\,\mathrm{m}$ (mild slope) and $h_{02} = 0.473\,\mathrm{m}$ (steep slope). The slope break is the control section, where $h = h_c$. Trace the upstream **M2** from downstream to upstream and the downstream **S2** from upstream to downstream.
:::
