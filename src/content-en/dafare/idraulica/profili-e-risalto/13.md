A horizontal trapezoidal channel has bottom width $b = 2.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and side slopes $m = 1.5$. The discharge is $Q = 7.00\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ and the supercritical depth is $h_1 = 0.45\,\mathrm{m}$, as shown. Find the upstream flow area, Froude number and momentum function in force units, and the conjugate depth.

::schema{solidi="-6,-0.8 6,-0.8 6,0 -6,0;-6,0 -1.0,0 -4.3,2.2 -6,2.2;6,0 1.0,0 4.3,2.2 6,2.2" acqua="-1.0,0 1.0,0 1.675,0.45 -1.675,0.45" pelo="-1.675,1.675,0.45," quote="-1.0 -0.8,1.0 -0.8,2.00 m,0.9" etichette="0,1.05,h_1 = 0.45 m;-5.2,1.45,m = 1.5;5.2,1.45,m = 1.5" riferimento=0 titolo="Trapezoidal section with bottom width $2.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and side slope $m = 1.5$"}

:::soluzione
$A_1 = 1.204\,\mathrm{m^2}$, $Fr_1 = 3.10$, $S(h_1) = 43.1\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $h_2 = 1.44\,\mathrm{m}$ (critical depth $h_c = 0.862\,\mathrm{m}$).
:::
