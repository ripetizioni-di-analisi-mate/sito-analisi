A horizontal rectangular channel of width $b = 4.00\,\mathrm{m}$ carries $Q = 12.0\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. The measured depth downstream of a hydraulic jump is $h_2 = 1.80\,\mathrm{m}$. Find the downstream Froude number, the upstream supercritical depth and the corresponding upstream Froude number.

:::soluzione
$Fr_2 = 0.397$; $h_1 = 0.453\,\mathrm{m}$; $Fr_1 = 3.15$.
:::
