A rectangular channel of width $b = 5.00\,\mathrm{m}$ with $k_s = 55\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$ and bed slope $i = 0.0006$ carries $Q = 9.00\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. Calculate the distance between the sections with depths $1.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$ using two depth increments of $0.10\,\mathrm{m}$, and classify the profile.

:::soluzione
$h_c = 0.691\,\mathrm{m}$ and $h_0 = 1.425\,\mathrm{m}$, giving an **M2** profile. The two steps give $\Delta x_1 = 44.2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $\Delta x_2 = 21.1\,\mathrm{m}$, for a total distance of $65.3\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
