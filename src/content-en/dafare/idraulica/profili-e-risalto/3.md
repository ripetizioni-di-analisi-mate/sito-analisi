A rectangular channel of width $b = 2.00\,\mathrm{m}$ with $k_s = 65\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$ and bed slope $i = 0.0006$ carries $Q = 2.40\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. A sluice gate releases water at depth $h_1 = 0.18\,\mathrm{m}$, as shown, and the downstream reach is long enough for uniform flow to develop. Classify the profile immediately downstream of the gate. Give the direction in which it should be traced and determine whether the jump forms at the gate or farther downstream.

::schema{solidi="0,0.90 12,0.30 12,-0.20 0,0.40;3.85,0.70 4.15,0.70 4.15,2.90 3.85,2.90" acqua="0,0.90 3.85,0.71 3.85,2.45 0,2.60;4.15,0.69 12,0.30 12,0.52 4.15,0.91" frecce="0.8,1.70,2.4,1.68,Q" etichette="4.0,3.15,gate;7.0,1.25,h_1 = 0.18 m;9.5,-0.50,i = 0.0006" riferimento=0 titolo="Sluice gate in a rectangular channel of width $2.00\,\mathrm{m}$"}

:::soluzione
$h_c = 0.528\,\mathrm{m}$, $h_0 = 1.145\,\mathrm{m}$ and $i_c = 0.00506$. The slope is mild and the **M3** profile (zone 3) increases in depth downstream. The flow is supercritical ($Fr_1 = 5.02$), so trace the profile from upstream to downstream. The conjugate depth of $h_1$ is $1.19\,\mathrm{m}$, greater than $h_0 = 1.145\,\mathrm{m}$, so the jump is **swept downstream**.
:::
