A horizontal rectangular channel of width $b = 6.00\,\mathrm{m}$ carries $Q = 21.0\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ at a supercritical depth of $h_1 = 0.60\,\mathrm{m}$. Find the upstream velocity and Froude number, the conjugate depth and the momentum function in force units.

:::soluzione
$v_1 = 5.83\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $Fr_1 = 2.40$; $h_2 = 1.76\,\mathrm{m}$; $S(h_1) = S(h_2) = 133\,\mathrm{kN}$.
:::
