A rectangular channel of width $b = 5.00\,\mathrm{m}$ carries $Q = 12.0\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. Find the discharge per unit width, critical depth, critical velocity and minimum specific energy.

:::soluzione
$q = 2.40\,\mathrm{m^2/s}$; $h_c = 0.837\,\mathrm{m}$; $v_c = 2.87\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $E_{\min} = 1.26\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
