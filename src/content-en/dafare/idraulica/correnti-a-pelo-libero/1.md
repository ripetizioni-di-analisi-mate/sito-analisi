A rectangular channel of width $b = 3.00\,\mathrm{m}$ carries water at a depth of $h = 1.10\,\mathrm{m}$. Find the flow area, wetted perimeter, hydraulic radius, top width and hydraulic depth.

:::soluzione
$A = 3.30\,\mathrm{m^2}$; $C = 5.20\,\mathrm{m}$; $R = 0.635\,\mathrm{m}$; $B = 3.00\,\mathrm{m}$; $h_m = 1.10\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
