A trapezoidal channel has bottom width $b = 3.00\,\mathrm{m}$, side slope $m = 1$, bed slope $i = 0.0006$ and $k_s = 50\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$. For a discharge of $Q = 6.00\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$, find the normal depth, mean velocity and Froude number.

:::soluzione
$h_0 = 1.29\,\mathrm{m}$, $A = 5.54\,\mathrm{m^2}$, $v = 1.08\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $Fr = 0.347$. The flow is subcritical.
:::
