titolo: "Critical depth in a trapezoidal channel"
teoria: [energia-specifica, stato-critico, numero-di-froude, elementi-geometrici]

A trapezoidal channel of bottom width $b = 3.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and side slope $m = 1$ carries $Q = 10.0\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. Use the condition $Q^2 B = g A^3$ to find the critical depth iteratively, then calculate the critical velocity and minimum specific energy.

:::soluzione
$h_c = 0.934\,\mathrm{m}$; $A_c = 3.67\,\mathrm{m^2}$; $v_c = 2.72\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $E_{\min} = 1.31\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
