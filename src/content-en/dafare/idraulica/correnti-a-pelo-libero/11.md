A rectangular channel of width $b = 4.00\,\mathrm{m}$ carries $Q = 12.0\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ at a depth of $h = 0.600\,\mathrm{m}$. Find the Froude number, classify the flow, determine the alternate depth, and find the upstream and downstream propagation velocities of a small free-surface disturbance.

:::soluzione
$v = 5.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The Froude number $Fr = 2.06$ indicates supercritical flow. $E = 1.874\,\mathrm{m}$. The alternate depth $h'' = 1.72\,\mathrm{m}$ corresponds to subcritical flow. Wave celerity $c = 2.43\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is below the flow speed, so both disturbances travel downstream, at speeds $c + v = 7.43\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v - c = 2.57\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
