A rectangular channel of width $b = 2.00\,\mathrm{m}$, bed slope $i = 0.0015$ and roughness coefficient $k_s = 65\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$ carries uniform flow at a depth of $h_0 = 0.900\,\mathrm{m}$. Find the mean velocity, discharge and Froude number, and classify the flow.

:::soluzione
$R = 0.474\,\mathrm{m}$, $v = 1.53\,\mathrm{m/s}$, $Q = 2.75\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ and $Fr = 0.515$. The flow is subcritical.
:::
