A rectangular channel of width $b = 3.00\,\mathrm{m}$ carries $Q = 6.00\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ at a depth of $h = 1.40\,\mathrm{m}$. Find the Froude number, classify the flow and determine the alternate depth.

:::soluzione
$v = 1.43\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The Froude number $Fr = 0.385$ indicates subcritical flow. $E = 1.504\,\mathrm{m}$. The alternate depth $h' = 0.437\,\mathrm{m}$ corresponds to supercritical flow.
:::
