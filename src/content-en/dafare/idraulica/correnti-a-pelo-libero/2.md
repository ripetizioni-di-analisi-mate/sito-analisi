A trapezoidal channel has bottom width $b = 2.50\,\mathrm{m}$ and side slope $m = 2$, so each bank extends $2\,\mathrm{m}$ horizontally for every meter of vertical rise. The water depth is $h = 1.20\,\mathrm{m}$, as shown. Find the flow area, wetted perimeter, hydraulic radius, top width and hydraulic depth.

::schema{nodi="S,-2.4,1.2;T,4.9,1.2" nomi=0 acqua="0,0 2.5,0 4.9,1.2 -2.4,1.2" solidi="-5,1.7 -3.4,1.7 0,0 2.5,0 5.9,1.7 7.5,1.7 7.5,-0.6 -5,-0.6" pelo="-2.4,4.9,1.2,p_a = 0" quote="2 0,2 1.2,1.20 m,0;0 -0.6,2.5 -0.6,2.50 m,0.45;S,T,B,-0.45" riferimento=0 titolo="Trapezoidal channel with $b = 2.50\,\mathrm{m}$, side slope $m = 2$ and $h = 1.20\,\mathrm{m}$"}

:::soluzione
$A = 5.88\,\mathrm{m^2}$; $C = 7.87\,\mathrm{m}$; $R = 0.747\,\mathrm{m}$; $B = 7.30\,\mathrm{m}$; $h_m = 0.805\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
