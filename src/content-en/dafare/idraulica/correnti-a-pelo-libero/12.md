A rectangular channel of width $b = 4.00\,\mathrm{m}$ with roughness coefficient $k_s = 75\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$ and bed slope $i = 0.006$ carries $Q = 10.0\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. Find the critical depth and critical slope. Classify the bed slope and check your conclusion by calculating the normal depth and its Froude number.

:::soluzione
$h_c = 0.860\,\mathrm{m}$ and $i_c = 0.00295$. Since $i > i_c$, the bed has a steep slope. Normal depth is $h_0 = 0.678\,\mathrm{m} < h_c$, with $v = 3.69\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $Fr = 1.43$, so uniform flow is supercritical.
:::
