A rectangular channel of width $b = 2.50\,\mathrm{m}$, bed slope $i = 0.0008$ and $k_s = 70\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$ must carry $Q = 3.00\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ in uniform flow. Find the normal depth, mean velocity and Froude number.

:::soluzione
$h_0 = 0.924\,\mathrm{m}$, $v = 1.30\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $Fr = 0.431$. The flow is subcritical.
:::
