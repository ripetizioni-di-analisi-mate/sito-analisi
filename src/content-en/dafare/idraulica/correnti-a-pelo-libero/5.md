An earthen trapezoidal channel has bottom width $b = 4.00\,\mathrm{m}$, side slope $m = 1.5$, bed slope $i = 0.0005$ and $k_s = 40\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$. The normal depth is $h_0 = 1.50\,\mathrm{m}$. Find the flow area, hydraulic radius, discharge, mean velocity and Froude number.

:::soluzione
$A = 9.38\,\mathrm{m^2}$, $R = 0.996\,\mathrm{m}$, $Q = 8.37\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$, $v = 0.892\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $Fr = 0.271$. The flow is subcritical.
:::
