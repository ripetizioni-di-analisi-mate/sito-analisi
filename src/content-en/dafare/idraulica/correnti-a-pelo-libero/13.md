Design a lined rectangular channel to carry $Q = 3.00\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ in uniform flow on bed slope $i = 0.0012$ with $k_s = 60\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$, using the most efficient hydraulic section. Find the water depth, bottom width, mean velocity and Froude number.

:::soluzione
$h = 1.05\,\mathrm{m}$; $b = 2h = 2.10\,\mathrm{m}$; $A = 2.21\,\mathrm{m^2}$; $R = \dfrac{h}{2} = 0.526\,\mathrm{m}$; $v = 1.35\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $Fr = 0.422$.
:::
