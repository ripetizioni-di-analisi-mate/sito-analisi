A quarter-cylindrical fillet of radius $1.80\,\mathrm{m}$ and width $2.00\,\mathrm{m}$ connects a vertical wall to the bottom. Water depth is $3.80\,\mathrm{m}$ as shown. Find the hydrostatic force components, the resultant magnitude, and its angle to the horizontal.

:::soluzione
$S_x = 102\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts horizontally toward the wall and $S_z = 121\,\mathrm{kN}$ vertically downward. The resultant $S = 158\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $49.7^\circ$ below the horizontal and passes through the cylinder axis.
