A rectangular pontoon is $10.00\,\mathrm{m}$ long, $4.00\,\mathrm{m}$ wide, and $1.50\,\mathrm{m}$ high. Its total weight is $300\,\mathrm{kN}$ and its center of gravity is $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$ above the keel, as shown. Find the draft, the metacentric radius about the longitudinal axis, and the metacentric height. Determine whether this equilibrium is stable.

::schema{acqua="-4.4,0 4.4,0 4.4,2 -4.4,2" solidi="-4.8,-0.7 4.8,-0.7 4.8,0 -4.8,0" poligoni="-2,1.3 2,1.3 2,2.8 -2,2.8" punti="0,2.1,G,baricentro" pelo="4.0,4.4,2,p_g = 0" quote="-2 2.8,2 2.8,B = 4.00 m,-0.9;2 1.3,2 2.8,H = 1.50 m,1;-2 1.3,-2 2.1,0.80 m,-0.9" riferimento=0 titolo="Cross section of the pontoon of width $4.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and height $1.50\,\mathrm{m}$, with its center of gravity $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$ above the keel"}

:::soluzione
The draft is $d = 0.765\,\mathrm{m}$ and the metacentric radius is $r = 1.74\,\mathrm{m}$. The metacenter is $2.13\,\mathrm{m}$ above the keel, and the metacentric height is $\overline{GM} = 1.33\,\mathrm{m}$, so the equilibrium is stable.
:::
