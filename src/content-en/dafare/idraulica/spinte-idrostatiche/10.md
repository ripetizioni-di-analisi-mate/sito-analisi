The radial gate shown has radius $2.50\,\mathrm{m}$ and width $3.00\,\mathrm{m}$. Its pin $O$ is at the upstream free surface and the upstream depth is $2.50\,\mathrm{m}$. The gate touches the bottom directly below $O$, and the downstream side is dry. Find the two force components, their resultant magnitude, and its inclination.

:::soluzione
$S_x = 92.0\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts horizontally downstream and $S_z = 144\,\mathrm{kN}$ vertically upward. The resultant $S = 171\,\mathrm{kN}$ is inclined at $57.5^\circ$ above the horizontal and passes through the pivot, so its moment about the pivot is zero.
