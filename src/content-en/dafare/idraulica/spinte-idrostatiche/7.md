A vertical rectangular gate of width $2.50\,\mathrm{m}$ and height $3.00\,\mathrm{m}$ is hinged along its bottom edge. A horizontal force $F$ at its top edge, which lies at the free surface, holds it closed, as shown. Find $F$.

::schema{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3" aste="A-B" cerniere="A" acqua="0,0 4.5,0 4.5,3 0,3" solidi="-0.5,-0.7 5,-0.7 5,0 -0.5,0" pelo="0,4.5,3,p_g = 0" reazioni="0 3,1.4,0,F" quote="0 0,0 3,3.00 m,-1.6" riferimento=0 titolo="Gate of height $3.00\,\mathrm{m}$ hinged at its lower edge and held by a force $F$ at its upper edge"}

:::soluzione
The force $F = 36.8\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts horizontally toward the water.
:::
