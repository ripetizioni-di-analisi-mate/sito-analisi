A vertical rectangular gate of width $1.80\,\mathrm{m}$ and height $2.50\,\mathrm{m}$ extends from the free surface to the bottom. Calculate the hydrostatic force and the depth of the center of pressure.

:::soluzione
The force $S = 55.2\,\mathrm{kN}$ is horizontal. The center of pressure is at a depth of $1.67\,\mathrm{m}$, or $0.833\,\mathrm{m}$ above the lower edge.
:::
