A stone weighs $540\,\mathrm{N}$ in air and has apparent weight $330\,\mathrm{N}$ when fully submerged in water. Find its volume and density.

:::soluzione
$V = 21.4\,\mathrm{L}$. The density is $2570\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.
:::
