A rectangular gate of width $2.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and downslope length $1.80\,\mathrm{m}$ is mounted on a wall inclined at $45^\circ$ to the horizontal. Its top edge is at depth $1.20\,\mathrm{m}$ as shown. Find the force and the distance of the center of pressure from the bottom edge, measured along the gate.

::schema{nodi="A,0,0.5272;B,1.2728,1.8" aste="A-B" acqua="-2.6,0 -0.5272,0 2.4728,3 -2.6,3" solidi="-3,-0.8 4,-0.8 4,3.4 2.8728,3.4 -0.5272,0 -3,0" pelo="-2.6,2.4728,3,p_g = 0" quote="A,B,1.80 m,0.9;1.2728 1.8,1.2728 3,1.20 m,-1.6" etichette="-0.42,0.08,α" riferimento=0 titolo="Gate of length $1.80\,\mathrm{m}$ in a wall inclined at $45^\circ$"}

:::soluzione
The force $S = 64.9\,\mathrm{kN}$ is normal to the gate. The center of pressure is $0.796\,\mathrm{m}$ from the lower edge measured along the gate, at a depth of $1.91\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
