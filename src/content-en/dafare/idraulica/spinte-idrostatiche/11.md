A concrete retaining wall of specific weight $23\,\mathrm{kN/m^3}$ has a vertical upstream face, height $5.00\,\mathrm{m}$, and base width $2.80\,\mathrm{m}$. Water depth is $4.20\,\mathrm{m}$ and the base friction coefficient is $0.55$, as shown. There is no uplift. Find the factors of safety against overturning and sliding and locate the resultant on the foundation plane.

::schema{solidi="-3.6,-0.9 5.6,-0.9 5.6,0 -3.6,0;0,0 2.8,0 2.8,5 0,5" acqua="-3.4,0 0,0 0,4.2 -3.4,4.2" pelo="-3.4,-0.6,4.2,p_g = 0" quote="0 0,2.8 0,B = 2.80 m,1.5;2.8 0,2.8 5,5.00 m,1;-3.4 0,-3.4 4.2,4.20 m,-0.7" riferimento=0 titolo="Retaining wall of height $5.00\,\mathrm{m}$ with base width $2.80\,\mathrm{m}$ and water depth $4.20\,\mathrm{m}$"}

:::soluzione
The factors of safety are $F_r = 3.72$ against overturning and $F_s = 2.05$ against sliding. The resultant intersects the base $1.78\,\mathrm{m}$ from the upstream face. The eccentricity $0.376\,\mathrm{m}$ is less than $\dfrac{B}{6} = 0.467\,\mathrm{m}$, so the resultant lies within the middle third.
:::
