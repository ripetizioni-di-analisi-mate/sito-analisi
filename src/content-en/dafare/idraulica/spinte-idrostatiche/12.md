A triangular gravity dam has its upstream heel at $x = 0$, its crest at horizontal coordinate $x = 1.20\,\mathrm{m}$ and elevation $6.00\,\mathrm{m}$, and its downstream toe at $x = 4.80\,\mathrm{m}$, as shown. The concrete has specific weight $24\,\mathrm{kN/m^3}$, the water depth is $5.00\,\mathrm{m}$, and the friction coefficient is $0.60$. Neglecting uplift, find both factors of safety and locate the resultant on the foundation plane.

::schema{nodi="T,0,0;K,1.2,6,-;P,4.8,0" aste="T-K;K-P;T-P" acqua="-3.5,0 0,0 1,5 -3.5,5" solidi="-4,-1 7,-1 7,0 -4,0" pelo="-3.5,-0.3,5,p_g = 0" quote="T,P,B = 4.80 m,1.7;-3.5 0,-3.5 5,5.00 m,-0.7" riferimento=0 titolo="Gravity dam of height $6.00\,\mathrm{m}$ with a sloping upstream face and base width $4.80\,\mathrm{m}$"}

:::soluzione
The factors of safety are $F_r = 5.27$ against overturning and $F_s = 1.81$ against sliding, with $S_x = 123\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, $S_z = 24.5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and $W = 346\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. The resultant intersects the base $2.44\,\mathrm{m}$ from the heel, with eccentricity $0.042\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
