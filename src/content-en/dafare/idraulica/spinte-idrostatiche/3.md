The vertical rectangular gate shown is $2.00\,\mathrm{m}$ wide and $2.50\,\mathrm{m}$ high. It rests on the bottom and separates basins with water depths $3.50\,\mathrm{m}$ upstream and $1.20\,\mathrm{m}$ downstream. Find the net force and the height of its line of action above the bottom.

:::soluzione
The net force $S = 96.2\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downstream. Its line of action is $1.11\,\mathrm{m}$ above the bottom.
