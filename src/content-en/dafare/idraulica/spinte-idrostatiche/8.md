A vertical rectangular gate of width $1.60\,\mathrm{m}$ closes an opening between depths $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$ and $2.80\,\mathrm{m}$. It is hinged along its top edge. Find the moment that must be applied about the hinge to keep it closed.

:::soluzione
The moment is $M = 67.0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with hydrostatic force $S = 56.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ and moment arm $1.19\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
