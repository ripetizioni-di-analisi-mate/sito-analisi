A vertical rectangular gate of width $2.00\,\mathrm{m}$ closes an opening between depths $1.50\,\mathrm{m}$ and $4.00\,\mathrm{m}$ below the free surface. Find the hydrostatic force and the depth of the center of pressure.

:::soluzione
$S = 135\,\mathrm{kN}$. The center of pressure lies at a depth of $2.94\,\mathrm{m}$, or $1.06\,\mathrm{m}$ above the lower edge.
:::
