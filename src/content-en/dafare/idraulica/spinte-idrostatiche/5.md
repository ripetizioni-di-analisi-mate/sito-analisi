A circular gate of diameter $1.00\,\mathrm{m}$ is mounted in a vertical wall with its center at depth $3.00\,\mathrm{m}$. Find the force and the distance between the center of pressure and the center of the gate.

:::soluzione
$S = 23.1\,\mathrm{kN}$. The center of pressure is $20.8\,\mathrm{mm}$ below the center on the vertical diameter.
:::
