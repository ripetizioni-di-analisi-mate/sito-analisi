A circular gate of diameter $0.90\,\mathrm{m}$ is mounted in a wall inclined at $50^\circ$ to the horizontal. Its center is at depth $2.40\,\mathrm{m}$, as shown. Find the force and the distance between the center of pressure and the center, measured in the gate plane.

:::soluzione
The force $S = 15.0\,\mathrm{kN}$ is normal to the gate. The center of pressure lies $16.2\,\mathrm{mm}$ below the center, measured along the gate.
