A pump draws discharge $Q = 60\,\mathrm{L/s}$ from a constant-level reservoir through a pipe of diameter $D = 200\,\mathrm{mm}$. Its inlet is $5.00\,\mathrm{m}$ above the free surface and suction-line head loss is $\Delta H = 0.90\,\mathrm{m}$. Given $p_{\mathrm{atm}} = 101.3\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and water at $20\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ with $p_v = 2.34\,\mathrm{kPa}$, find the absolute and gauge inlet pressures and the margin above vapor pressure.

:::soluzione
The absolute pressure head is $\dfrac{p^{\mathrm{abs}}}{\gamma} = 4.24\,\mathrm{m}$, corresponding to $p^{\mathrm{abs}} = 41.6\,\mathrm{kPa}$. The gauge pressure is $p = -59.7\,\mathrm{kPa}$. The absolute pressure head exceeds the vapor-pressure head by $4.00\,\mathrm{m}$, so cavitation does not occur.
:::
