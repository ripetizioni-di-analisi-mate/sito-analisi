An open constant-level tank discharges through a short converging nozzle of diameter $D = 80\,\mathrm{mm}$ whose axis is $3.20\,\mathrm{m}$ below the free surface. Neglecting head loss, find the exit speed and discharge.

:::soluzione
$v = 7.92\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $Q = 39.8\,\mathrm{L/s}$.
:::
