A pipe of diameter $D = 250\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries discharge $Q = 80\,\mathrm{L/s}$. The centerline at the section of interest is $6.50\,\mathrm{m}$ above the datum and the gauge pressure is $p = 120\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Find the mean speed, velocity head, piezometric head, and total head.

:::soluzione
$v = 1.63\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $\dfrac{v^2}{2g} = 0.135\,\mathrm{m}$; $h = 18.73\,\mathrm{m}$; $H = 18.87\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
