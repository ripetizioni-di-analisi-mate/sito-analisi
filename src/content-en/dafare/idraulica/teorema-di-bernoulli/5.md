A converging nozzle on a horizontal pipe of diameter $D_1 = 200\,\mathrm{mm}$ has exit diameter $D_2 = 80\,\mathrm{mm}$ and discharges to the atmosphere. The upstream gauge pressure is $p_1 = 150\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Neglecting head loss, find the discharge and jet speed.

:::soluzione
$v_1 = 2.81\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $v_2 = 17.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $Q = 88.2\,\mathrm{L/s}$.
:::
