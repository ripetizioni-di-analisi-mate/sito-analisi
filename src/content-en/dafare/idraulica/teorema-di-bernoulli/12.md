A Pitot tube in a rectangular channel of width $b = 3.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and water depth $h = 1.20\,\mathrm{m}$ shows water $0.18\,\mathrm{m}$ above the free surface. Assuming uniform velocity across the section, find the speed, discharge, and Froude number, and classify the flow as subcritical or supercritical.

:::soluzione
$v = 1.88\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $Q = 6.77\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. Since $Fr = 0.548 < 1$, the flow is subcritical.
:::
