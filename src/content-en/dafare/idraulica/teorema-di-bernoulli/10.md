A Venturi meter in a horizontal pipe of diameter $D_1 = 200\,\mathrm{mm}$ has throat diameter $D_2 = 100\,\mathrm{mm}$. A differential mercury manometer reads a level difference $\Delta = 150\,\mathrm{mm}$, and mercury has specific gravity $13.6$. Neglecting losses, find the piezometric head difference, discharge, and pressure difference between the sections.

:::soluzione
$\Delta h = 1.89\,\mathrm{m}$; $Q = 49.4\,\mathrm{L/s}$; $p_1 - p_2 = 18.5\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
:::
