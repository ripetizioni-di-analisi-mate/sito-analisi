A Pitot-static tube in a pipe of diameter $D = 250\,\mathrm{mm}$ connects to a differential mercury manometer showing a level difference $\Delta = 28\,\mathrm{mm}$. Mercury has specific gravity $13.6$. Assuming uniform velocity across the section, find the speed, discharge, and Reynolds number.

:::soluzione
$\Delta h = 0.353\,\mathrm{m}$, $v = 2.63\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $Q = 129\,\mathrm{L/s}$. The Reynolds number is $Re = 6.6 \cdot 10^5$, so the flow is turbulent.
:::
