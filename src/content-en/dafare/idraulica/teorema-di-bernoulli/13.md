Two constant-level reservoirs are connected by the pipe of diameter $D = 300\,\mathrm{mm}$ shown. The difference in free-surface elevations is $\Delta z = 9.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and total head loss is $\Delta H = 45\,\dfrac{v^2}{2g}$. Find the speed and discharge. Taking the downstream reservoir surface as datum, find the total and piezometric heads where the accumulated loss from the upstream reservoir is $15\,\dfrac{v^2}{2g}$.

$\dfrac{v^2}{2g} = 0.200\,\mathrm{m}$, $v = 1.98\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $Q = 140\,\mathrm{L/s}$. The total head $H = 6.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and the piezometric head $h = 5.80\,\mathrm{m}$ are both measured above the downstream free surface.
