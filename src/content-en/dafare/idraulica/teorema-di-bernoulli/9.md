The Venturi meter shown is installed in a horizontal pipe of diameter $D_1 = 300\,\mathrm{mm}$ and has throat diameter $D_2 = 150\,\mathrm{mm}$. Piezometers upstream and at the throat show a level difference $\Delta h = 1.10\,\mathrm{m}$. Neglecting losses, find the discharge and both section speeds.
