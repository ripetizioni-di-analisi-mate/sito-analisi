A closed tank contains water beneath air at gauge pressure $p_0 = 80\,\mathrm{kPa}$. The water level stays constant and a nozzle of diameter $D = 50\,\mathrm{mm}$ discharges from depth $1.50\,\mathrm{m}$ below the surface. Neglecting losses, find the total head at the free surface, the exit speed, and the discharge.

:::soluzione
$H = 9.65\,\mathrm{m}$; $v = 13.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $Q = 27.0\,\mathrm{L/s}$.
:::
