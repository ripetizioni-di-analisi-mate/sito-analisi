The pipe shown rises from section $1$ of diameter $D_1 = 300\,\mathrm{mm}$ and elevation $z_1 = 0$ to section $2$ of diameter $D_2 = 200\,\mathrm{mm}$ and elevation $z_2 = 8.00\,\mathrm{m}$. The discharge is $Q = 100\,\mathrm{L/s}$ and gauge pressure at $1$ is $p_1 = 400\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Neglecting head loss, find the pressure at $2$.

::schema{nodi="A,1,0.8,-;B,4,0.8,-;C,8,8,-;D,11,8,-" condotte="A-B,0.8;B-C,0.55;C-D,0.55" linee="0,0 12,0" frecce="1.2,0.8,2.2,0.8,Q" punti="3.2,0.8,1,pieno;9.6,8,2,pieno" etichette="2,1.7,D_1 = 300 mm;8.4,8.9,D_2 = 200 mm" quote="10.6 0,10.6 8,8.00 m,0.9" riferimento=0 titolo="Diameter reduction in a rising pipe with $p_1 = 400\,\mathrm{kPa}$"}

:::soluzione
$v_1 = 1.41\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $v_2 = 3.18\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $p_2 = 317\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
:::
