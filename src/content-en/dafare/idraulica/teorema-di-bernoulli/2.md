The pipe shown carries discharge $Q = 90\,\mathrm{L/s}$. At section $1$ the diameter is $D_1 = 300\,\mathrm{mm}$, centerline elevation $z_1 = 1.50\,\mathrm{m}$, and gauge pressure $p_1 = 350\,\mathrm{kPa}$. At section $2$ the corresponding values are $D_2 = 200\,\mathrm{mm}$, $z_2 = 4.00\,\mathrm{m}$, and $p_2 = 300\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Find the two total heads, the head loss, and the flow direction.

:::soluzione
$H_1 = 37.26\,\mathrm{m}$, $H_2 = 35.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and $\Delta H = 2.26\,\mathrm{m}$. The water flows from section $1$ to section $2$.
