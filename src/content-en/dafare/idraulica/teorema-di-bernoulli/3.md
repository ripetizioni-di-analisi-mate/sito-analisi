---
titolo: "Pressure downstream of a contraction"
teoria: [teorema-di-bernoulli, continuita, bernoulli-per-correnti]
parole: ["contraction", "Bernoulli’s equation", "pressure", "continuity", "horizontal pipe"]
---

A horizontal pipe contracts from diameter $D_1 = 250\,\mathrm{mm}$ to $D_2 = 150\,\mathrm{mm}$ and carries discharge $Q = 70\,\mathrm{L/s}$. The upstream gauge pressure is $p_1 = 200\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Neglecting head loss, find both section speeds and the pressure in the narrower section.

:::soluzione
$v_1 = 1.43\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $v_2 = 3.96\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $p_2 = 193\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
:::
