A short horizontal nozzle in a vertical wall of a constant-level tank is $3.20\,\mathrm{m}$ below the water surface and $1.80\,\mathrm{m}$ above ground level. Neglecting head loss and air resistance, find the exit speed and horizontal distance from the wall where the jet reaches the ground.

:::soluzione
$v = 7.92\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $x = 4.80\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
