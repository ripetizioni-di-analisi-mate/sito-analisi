The pipe shown has centerline elevation $z_A = 0$ and connects to an open-tube manometer containing mercury of specific gravity $13.6$. The water–mercury interface is at elevation $-0.20\,\mathrm{m}$ and the mercury level in the open limb at $+0.65\,\mathrm{m}$. Find the gauge pressure at point $A$ and the corresponding pressure head.

::schema{nodi="P,0,0,-;Q,2.2,0,-" condotte="P-Q,0.4" acqua="1.74,0 1.86,0 1.86,-0.2 1.74,-0.2" poligoni="1.74,-0.2 1.74,-0.5 2.52,-0.5 2.52,0.65 2.4,0.65 2.4,-0.38 1.86,-0.38 1.86,-0.2" pelo="2.4,2.52,0.65" tratteggi="2.2,0 3.9,0" punti="1.8,0,A" quote="2.75 0,2.75 -0.2,0.20 m,-0.35;2.6 0,2.6 0.65,0.65 m,1.05" etichette="2.1,-0.73,mercury" riferimento=0 titolo="Simple mercury manometer connected to the pipe at point $A$"}

:::soluzione
$p_A = 111\,\mathrm{kPa}$, corresponding to a pressure head of $11.4\,\mathrm{m}$ of water.
:::
