An open tank contains water to depth $6.20\,\mathrm{m}$. Find gauge and absolute pressure at the bottom and gauge pressure at depth $2.00\,\mathrm{m}$ below the free surface. Take $p_{\mathrm{atm}} = 101.3\,\mathrm{kPa}$.

:::soluzione
At the bottom the gauge pressure is $p = 60.8\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and the absolute pressure is $162.1\,\mathrm{kPa}$. At a depth of $2.00\,\mathrm{m}$ the gauge pressure is $p = 19.6\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
:::
