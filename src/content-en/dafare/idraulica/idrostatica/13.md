A rectangular tank truck of length $7.00\,\mathrm{m}$ contains water to a mean depth of $1.40\,\mathrm{m}$ and accelerates horizontally at $a = 1.80\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the inclination of the free surface, the difference in water level between the two ends, and the bottom pressure at the back wall.

:::soluzione
$\tan\theta = 0.183$, giving $\theta = 10.4^\circ$. The water-surface elevations at the two ends differ by $1.28\,\mathrm{m}$. At the bottom against the rear wall, $p = 20.0\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
:::
