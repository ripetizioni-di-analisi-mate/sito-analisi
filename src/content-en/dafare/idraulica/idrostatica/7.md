A flat plate of area $A = 0.40\,\mathrm{m^2}$ moves at constant speed $V = 1.20\,\mathrm{m/s}$ over an oil film of thickness $s = 2.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ and dynamic viscosity $\mu = 0.25\,\mathrm{Pa\,s}$. Find the shear stress, the force needed to maintain motion, and the power dissipated.

:::soluzione
$\tau = 150\,\mathrm{Pa}$; $F = 60.0\,\mathrm{N}$; $P = 72.0\,\mathrm{W}$.
:::
