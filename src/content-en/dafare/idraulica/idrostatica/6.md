A rigid vessel holds $1.00\,\mathrm{m^3}$ of water with bulk modulus $\varepsilon = 2.1 \cdot 10^9\,\mathrm{Pa}$. The internal pressure rises by $5\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Find the decrease in water volume and the speed of a small pressure disturbance in water.

:::soluzione
The volume decreases by $\Delta V = 2.38\,\mathrm{L}$, or $0.238\%$ of its initial value. The pressure-wave speed is $c = 1449\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
