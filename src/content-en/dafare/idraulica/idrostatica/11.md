Two separate pipes of still water connect to the limbs of the mercury differential manometer shown. The centerline of pipe $A$ is $3.00\,\mathrm{m}$ above that of pipe $B$. The interface on the $B$ side is $\Delta = 0.28\,\mathrm{m}$ higher than the one on the $A$ side. Find the differences in piezometric head and pressure between the two points.

:::soluzione
$h_A - h_B = 3.53\,\mathrm{m}$; $p_A - p_B = 5.18\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
:::
