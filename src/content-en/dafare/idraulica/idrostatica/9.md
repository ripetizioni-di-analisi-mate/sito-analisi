A piezometer connects to a pipe with centerline elevation $12.40\,\mathrm{m}$. The water rises to elevation $27.90\,\mathrm{m}$. Find the gauge pressure at the pipe centerline and the piezometric head.

:::soluzione
$p = 152\,\mathrm{kPa}$. The piezometric head $h = 27.90\,\mathrm{m}$ also gives the elevation of the hydrostatic head plane.
:::
