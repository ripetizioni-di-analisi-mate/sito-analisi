A container holds $0.80\,\mathrm{m^3}$ of glycerin with specific gravity $\delta = 1.26$ and dynamic viscosity $\mu = 0.95\,\mathrm{Pa\,s}$. Find its density, specific weight, and kinematic viscosity, and the mass and weight of the glycerin in the container.

:::soluzione
$\rho = 1260\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$, $\gamma = 12.4\,\mathrm{kN/m^3}$ and $\nu = 7.54 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2/s}$. The mass is $1008\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the weight is $9.89\,\mathrm{kN}$.
:::
