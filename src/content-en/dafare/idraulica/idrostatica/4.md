A closed pipe contains water at rest. Points $A$ and $B$ are at elevations $48.50\,\mathrm{m}$ and $61.00\,\mathrm{m}$ above a horizontal datum, respectively. At $A$ the gauge pressure is $220\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Find the piezometric head, the elevation of the hydrostatic head plane, and the pressure at $B$.

:::soluzione
$h = 70.9\,\mathrm{m}$ is also the elevation of the hydrostatic head plane. At $B$ the pressure is $97.4\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
:::
