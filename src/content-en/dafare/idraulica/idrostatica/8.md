A glass tube of inside diameter $D = 0.80\,\mathrm{mm}$ is dipped vertically into water at $20\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$, with surface tension $\sigma = 0.0728\,\mathrm{N/m}$ and zero contact angle. Calculate the capillary rise in the tube.

:::soluzione
$h = 37.1\,\mathrm{mm}$.
:::
