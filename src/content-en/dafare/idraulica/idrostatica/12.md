An open tank contains oil of specific gravity $\delta = 0.86$ and depth $1.50\,\mathrm{m}$ over water of depth $2.20\,\mathrm{m}$, as shown. Find the interface and bottom pressures and the elevation of the water’s hydrostatic head plane above the bottom.

::schema{acqua="0,0 3.6,0 3.6,2.2 0,2.2" poligoni="0,2.2 3.6,2.2 3.6,3.7 0,3.7" solidi="-0.3,-0.3 3.9,-0.3 3.9,0 -0.3,0;-0.3,0 0,0 0,4.1 -0.3,4.1;3.6,0 3.9,0 3.9,4.1 3.6,4.1" pelo="0,3.6,3.7" punti="1.8,0,F" etichette="1.8,2.9,oil δ = 0.86;1.8,1.05,water" quote="0 3.7,0 2.2,1.50 m,-0.8;0 2.2,0 0,2.20 m,-0.8" riferimento=0 titolo="Open tank with a layer of oil above a layer of water"}

:::soluzione
At the interface, $p = 12.7\,\mathrm{kPa}$. At the bottom, $p = 34.2\,\mathrm{kPa}$. The hydrostatic head plane for the water lies $3.49\,\mathrm{m}$ above the bottom and $0.21\,\mathrm{m}$ below the oil surface.
:::
