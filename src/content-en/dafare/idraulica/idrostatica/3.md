A closed tank contains water to depth $3.50\,\mathrm{m}$ under air at gauge pressure $p_0 = -28\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Find the bottom pressure and the depth below the free surface where gauge pressure is zero.

:::soluzione
At the bottom, $p = 6.34\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Gauge pressure is zero at a depth of $2.85\,\mathrm{m}$ below the free surface, within the liquid.
:::
