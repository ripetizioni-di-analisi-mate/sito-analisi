A closed tank contains water to depth $2.80\,\mathrm{m}$ under air at gauge pressure $p_0 = 40\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Find the distance between the hydrostatic head plane and the free surface, and the pressure at the bottom.

:::soluzione
The hydrostatic head plane lies $4.08\,\mathrm{m}$ above the free surface. At the bottom, $p = 67.5\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
:::
