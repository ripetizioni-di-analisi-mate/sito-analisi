A cylindrical vessel of radius $R = 0.30\,\mathrm{m}$ contains water to depth $0.45\,\mathrm{m}$ at rest. It rotates about its vertical axis at $n = 100\,\mathrm{rpm}$, and the water rotates with it as a rigid body. Find the difference in level between the rim and vertex of the free surface, their elevations above the bottom, and the bottom pressure at the rim.

:::soluzione
The free-surface elevation difference is $0.503\,\mathrm{m}$. The vertex is $0.198\,\mathrm{m}$ and the rim is $0.702\,\mathrm{m}$ above the bottom. At the bottom beneath the rim, $p = 6.88\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
:::
