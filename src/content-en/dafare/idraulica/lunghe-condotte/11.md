A pump draws an input power of $P = 22\,\mathrm{kW}$ at overall efficiency $\eta = 0.73$ and raises water through a static lift of $30\,\mathrm{m}$ in a pipe of length $1000\,\mathrm{m}$ with $D = 300\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $\lambda = 0.0235$. Write the two equations governing discharge, solve by successive approximation starting from $Q_0 = 0.050\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$, and find the operating discharge and head.

:::soluzione
$r = 799\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$, $Q = 0.0510\,\mathrm{m^3/s} = 51.0\,\mathrm{L/s}$ and $H_p = 32.1\,\mathrm{m}$, including $30\,\mathrm{m}$ of static lift and $2.08\,\mathrm{m}$ of loss. The result is $v = 0.722\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
