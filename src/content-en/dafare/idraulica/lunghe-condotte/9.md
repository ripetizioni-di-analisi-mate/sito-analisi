A pipe of length $1500\,\mathrm{m}$ with $D = 300\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $\lambda = 0.024$ connects reservoirs with free surfaces at $58\,\mathrm{m}$ and $30\,\mathrm{m}$. At a distance $600\,\mathrm{m}$ from the entrance the pipe crosses a summit at elevation $56\,\mathrm{m}$, as shown. Check the summit pressure with unrestricted flow. For a minimum pressure of $-6\,\mathrm{m}$ of water, find the maximum discharge and the loss coefficient of the downstream gate valve that provides it.

:::soluzione
With the pipe unthrottled, $J_0 = 0.018667$, $Q_0 = 151\,\mathrm{L/s}$ and $\dfrac{p}{\gamma} = -9.20\,\mathrm{m}$, beyond the permissible limit. The allowable gradient is $J = 0.013333$, giving $v = 1.81\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $Q = 0.128\,\mathrm{m^3/s} = 128\,\mathrm{L/s}$, a pipe friction loss of $20.0\,\mathrm{m}$, a head of $8.00\,\mathrm{m}$ to dissipate and $\xi = 48$.
:::
