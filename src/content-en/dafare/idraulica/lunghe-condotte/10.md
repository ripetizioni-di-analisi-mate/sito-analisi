A pump delivers $Q = 55\,\mathrm{L/s}$ against a static lift of $36\,\mathrm{m}$ through a discharge pipe of length $1300\,\mathrm{m}$ with $D = 250\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $\lambda = 0.023$, as shown. The overall efficiency is $\eta = 0.74$. Find the head loss, pump head, hydraulic output power and input power.

$v = 1.12\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $J = 5.89 \cdot 10^{-3}$; $\Delta H = 7.65\,\mathrm{m}$; $H_p = 43.7\,\mathrm{m}$; $P_u = 23.6\,\mathrm{kW}$; $P = 31.8\,\mathrm{kW}$.
