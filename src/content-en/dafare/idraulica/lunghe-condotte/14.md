A station must deliver $Q = 120\,\mathrm{L/s}$ through a static lift of $25\,\mathrm{m}$ in a pipe of length $2500\,\mathrm{m}$ with $\lambda = 0.020$. Its overall efficiency is $\eta = 0.76$. It runs $5000$ hours per year. Electricity costs $0.22\,\text{€/kWh}$, and the pipe costs $900\,\text{€}$ per meter of length per meter of diameter and is depreciated over $20$ years. For $D = 300\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $D = 400\,\mathrm{mm}$, calculate the head loss, pump head, input power and total annual cost, and identify the more economical diameter.

:::soluzione
For $D = 300\,\mathrm{mm}$ the results are $v = 1.70\,\mathrm{m/s}$, $\Delta H = 24.5\,\mathrm{m}$, $H_p = 49.5\,\mathrm{m}$ and $P = 76.7\,\mathrm{kW}$, with energy cost $84\,300\,\text{€/year}$, annualized pipe cost $33\,800\,\text{€/year}$ and total $118\,000\,\text{€/year}$. For $D = 400\,\mathrm{mm}$ the results are $v = 0.955\,\mathrm{m/s}$, $\Delta H = 5.81\,\mathrm{m}$, $H_p = 30.8\,\mathrm{m}$ and $P = 47.7\,\mathrm{kW}$, with energy cost $52\,500\,\text{€/year}$, annualized pipe cost $45\,000\,\text{€/year}$ and total $97\,500\,\text{€/year}$. The more economical diameter is $400\,\mathrm{mm}$.
:::
