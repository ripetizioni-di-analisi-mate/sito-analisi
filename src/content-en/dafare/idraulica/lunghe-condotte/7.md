A pipe of length $2000\,\mathrm{m}$ with $D = 300\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $\lambda = 0.0235$ connects reservoirs with free surfaces at elevations $72\,\mathrm{m}$ and $44\,\mathrm{m}$. At a distance $800\,\mathrm{m}$ from the entrance, the pipe centerline is at elevation $66\,\mathrm{m}$, as shown. Find the discharge and the piezometric head at this section. Express the gauge pressure in meters of water and in kilopascals.

:::soluzione
$J = 0.0140$, $v = 1.87\,\mathrm{m/s}$, $Q = 0.132\,\mathrm{m^3/s} = 132\,\mathrm{L/s}$ and $h(800) = 60.8\,\mathrm{m}$. At the section considered, the pressure head is $\dfrac{p}{\gamma} = -5.20\,\mathrm{m}$, or a gauge pressure of $p = -51.0\,\mathrm{kPa}$, below atmospheric pressure.
:::
