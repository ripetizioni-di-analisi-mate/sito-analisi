A pump draws $Q = 70\,\mathrm{L/s}$ from a tank through the suction pipe shown, of length $10\,\mathrm{m}$, with $D = 250\,\mathrm{mm}$, $\lambda = 0.0235$ and combined minor-loss coefficient $\xi = 1.8$. At the operating discharge the manufacturer specifies $\mathrm{NPSH}_r = 3.0\,\mathrm{m}$. Given $p_{\mathrm{atm}} = 101.3\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and vapor pressure $p_v = 2.34\,\mathrm{kPa}$, calculate the suction-line head loss and the maximum pump elevation above the tank surface.

:::soluzione
$v = 1.43\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $\dfrac{v^2}{2g} = 0.104\,\mathrm{m}$; $\Delta H_a = 0.284\,\mathrm{m}$; $\dfrac{p_{\mathrm{atm}} - p_v}{\gamma} = 10.09\,\mathrm{m}$; $z_a \leq 6.80\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
