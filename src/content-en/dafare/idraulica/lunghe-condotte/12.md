A pump has characteristic curve $H_p = 62 - 1100\,Q^2$, with $Q$ in $\mathrm{m^3/s}$ and $H_p$ in meters. It raises water through a static lift of $38\,\mathrm{m}$ in a pipe of length $1400\,\mathrm{m}$ with $D = 250\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $\lambda = 0.0245$. Find the hydraulic resistance, the discharge and head at the operating point, and the input power for $\eta = 0.75$.

:::soluzione
$r = 2902\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$; $Q = 0.0774\,\mathrm{m^3/s} = 77.4\,\mathrm{L/s}$; $H = 55.4\,\mathrm{m}$; $v = 1.58\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $P = 56.1\,\mathrm{kW}$.
:::
