A transmission main of length $1800\,\mathrm{m}$ with $D = 300\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $\lambda = 0.0225$ carries $Q = 120\,\mathrm{L/s}$. Find the mean velocity, energy gradient, hydraulic resistance and total head loss, which gives the minimum difference in reservoir surface levels needed to produce that discharge.

:::soluzione
$v = 1.70\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $J = 0.01102$; $r = 1377\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$; $\Delta H = 19.8\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
