Two reservoirs with water-surface elevation difference $22\,\mathrm{m}$ are connected by a cast-iron pipe of length $1600\,\mathrm{m}$ with $D = 300\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $\varepsilon = 0.3\,\mathrm{mm}$. Find the discharge without iteration using the fact that $Re\sqrt{\lambda}$ is known from the geometry alone. Check the result by recalculating the Reynolds number.

:::soluzione
$J = 0.013750$; $Re\sqrt{\lambda} = 8.53 \cdot 10^{4}$; $\lambda = 0.0201$; $v = 2.01\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $Q = 0.142\,\mathrm{m^3/s} = 142\,\mathrm{L/s}$; $Re = 6.0 \cdot 10^{5}$.
:::
