A pipe of length $1100\,\mathrm{m}$ must carry $Q = 70\,\mathrm{L/s}$ with an available elevation difference of $18\,\mathrm{m}$. The pipe is steel with $\varepsilon = 0.1\,\mathrm{mm}$. Find the theoretical diameter by successive approximation starting from $\lambda_0 = 0.0200$, select a standard diameter, and calculate the actual head loss and residual head.

:::soluzione
$J = 0.016364$. The theoretical diameter is $D = 0.213\,\mathrm{m}$ with $\lambda = 0.0176$, and the selected standard diameter is $D = 250\,\mathrm{mm}$. $v = 1.43\,\mathrm{m/s}$, $\lambda = 0.0174$, and $\Delta H_{\mathrm{eff}} = 7.93\,\mathrm{m}$. The residual head is $10.1\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
