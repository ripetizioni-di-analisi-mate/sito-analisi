A pipe of length $1700\,\mathrm{m}$ with $D = 250\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $\lambda = 0.025$ connects reservoirs with free surfaces at $64\,\mathrm{m}$ and $30\,\mathrm{m}$. It must cross a ridge $500\,\mathrm{m}$ from the entrance. Find the discharge and the maximum centerline elevation at the ridge that allows a gauge pressure of at least $-7\,\mathrm{m}$ of water.

:::soluzione
$J = 0.0200$, $v = 1.98\,\mathrm{m/s}$, $Q = 0.0972\,\mathrm{m^3/s} = 97.2\,\mathrm{L/s}$ and $h(500) = 54.0\,\mathrm{m}$. The maximum centerline elevation is $z_{\max} = 61.0\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
