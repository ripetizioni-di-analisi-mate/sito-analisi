Two constant-level reservoirs whose free surfaces differ in elevation by $15\,\mathrm{m}$ are connected by the pipe shown, of length $1000\,\mathrm{m}$ with $D = 250\,\mathrm{mm}$ and Darcy friction factor $\lambda = 0.026$. Find the energy gradient, mean velocity, discharge and Reynolds number.

:::soluzione
$J = 0.0150$, $v = 1.68\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $Q = 0.0826\,\mathrm{m^3/s} = 82.6\,\mathrm{L/s}$ toward the downstream reservoir. The Reynolds number is $Re = 4.2 \cdot 10^{5}$, so the flow is turbulent.
:::
