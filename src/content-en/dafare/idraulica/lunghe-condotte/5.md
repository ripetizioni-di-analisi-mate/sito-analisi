A water main must carry $Q = 130\,\mathrm{L/s}$ along a route of length $2200\,\mathrm{m}$ with an available elevation difference of $28\,\mathrm{m}$. The steel pipe has $k_s = 80\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$. Find the theoretical diameter, select a standard size from $100$, $150$, $200$, $250$, $300$, $400$ and $500\,\mathrm{mm}$, and calculate the actual head loss, residual head and velocity.

:::soluzione
$J = 0.012727$. The calculated diameter is $D = 0.316\,\mathrm{m}$. Select standard diameter $D = 400\,\mathrm{mm}$ because at $300\,\mathrm{mm}$ the loss would be $36.8\,\mathrm{m}$, above the available elevation difference. The results are $J_{\mathrm{eff}} = 3.60 \cdot 10^{-3}$ and $\Delta H_{\mathrm{eff}} = 7.93\,\mathrm{m}$, leaving a residual head of $20.1\,\mathrm{m}$ and giving $v = 1.03\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
