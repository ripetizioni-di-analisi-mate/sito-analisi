A steel pipe of length $1400\,\mathrm{m}$ with $D = 250\,\mathrm{mm}$ and absolute roughness $\varepsilon = 0.15\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries $Q = 90\,\mathrm{L/s}$. Find the Reynolds number and use the Colebrook–White equation to find the Darcy friction factor, starting at $\lambda_0 = 0.0200$ and stopping when successive estimates differ by less than $10^{-4}$. Calculate the total head loss.

:::soluzione
$v = 1.83\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $Re = 4.58 \cdot 10^{5}$; $\lambda = 0.0183$; $J = 0.01255$; $\Delta H = 17.6\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
