A horizontal pipe elbow of diameter $D = 200\,\mathrm{mm}$ turns the flow through $\theta = 45^\circ$. It carries discharge $Q = 60\,\mathrm{L/s}$ with gauge pressure $p = 250\,\mathrm{kPa}$ at both sections. Find the force components, the resultant magnitude, and its angle to the inlet axis.

:::soluzione
$v = 1.91\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The components are $F_x = 2.33\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $F_y = 5.63\,\mathrm{kN}$, with the latter acting opposite to the flow deflection. The resultant $F = 6.10\,\mathrm{kN}$ makes an angle of $67.5^\circ$ with the inlet axis.
:::
