A horizontal $180^\circ$ bend of diameter $D = 300\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries discharge $Q = 140\,\mathrm{L/s}$. The upstream gauge pressure is $p_1 = 180\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and the local head loss is $\Delta H = \xi\,\dfrac{v^2}{2g}$ with $\xi = 0.40$. Find the downstream pressure and the fluid force on the bend shown.

::schema{nodi="A,0,0,-;B,3,0,-;C,3,1.9,-;D,0,1.9,-" condotte="A-B-C-D,0.55" frecce="0.1,0,0.9,0,Q" tratteggi="0.4,-0.55 0.4,2.45" etichette="0.25,0.6,1;0.25,1.4,2;1.6,0.95,D = 300 mm" riferimento=0 titolo="Plan view of an $180^\circ$ bend"}

:::soluzione
$v = 1.98\,\mathrm{m/s}$, $\Delta H = 0.0800\,\mathrm{m}$ and $p_2 = 179.2\,\mathrm{kPa}$. The force $F = 25.9\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts in the direction of the inlet velocity.
:::
