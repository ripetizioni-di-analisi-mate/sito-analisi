A water jet of diameter $d = 50\,\mathrm{mm}$ and speed $v = 16\,\mathrm{m/s}$ strikes a smooth stationary flat plate inclined at $\alpha = 45^\circ$ to the jet axis. Neglecting friction, find the force on the plate and the discharges of the two outgoing sheets shown.

::schema{acqua="0,1 3.80,1 4.20,1.4 0,1.4;4.20,1.40 5.12,2.32 4.95,2.49 4.03,1.57;3.80,1.00 3.31,0.51 3.23,0.58 3.73,1.07" solidi="3.29,0.07 5.56,2.33 5.34,2.54 3.08,0.28" frecce="0.6,1.2,1.8,1.2,v = 16 m/s" etichette="1.2,1.65,d = 50 mm;4.9,0.85,α = 45°" riferimento=0 titolo="Jet striking a plate inclined at $45^\circ$"}

:::soluzione
$Q = 31.4\,\mathrm{L/s}$. The force $F = 355\,\mathrm{N}$ is normal to the plate. The two discharges are $Q_1 = 26.8\,\mathrm{L/s}$ and $Q_2 = 4.60\,\mathrm{L/s}$.
:::
