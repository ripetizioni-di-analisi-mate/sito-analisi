A horizontal tee receives discharge $Q_1 = 120\,\mathrm{L/s}$ at section $1$ of diameter $D_1 = 250\,\mathrm{mm}$. The straight branch has diameter $D_2 = 200\,\mathrm{mm}$ and discharge $Q_2 = 70\,\mathrm{L/s}$. The side branch turns through $90^\circ$ and has diameter $D_3 = 150\,\mathrm{mm}$ and discharge $Q_3 = 50\,\mathrm{L/s}$. Gauge pressure at section $1$ is $p_1 = 220\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Neglecting losses, find the components and magnitude of the force on the tee shown.

::schema{nodi="A,0,0,-;B,3,0,-;C,6,0,-;D,3,2.6,-" condotte="A-C,0.6;B-D,0.45" frecce="0.3,0,1.3,0,Q_1;4.4,0,5.4,0,Q_2;3,1.5,3,2.4,Q_3" tratteggi="1.2,-0.55 1.2,0.55;4.8,-0.55 4.8,0.55;2.6,1.9 3.4,1.9" etichette="1.05,0.85,1;5.2,0.85,2;3.5,1.55,3;0.3,-0.85,D_1 = 250 mm;4.6,-0.85,D_2 = 200 mm;4.3,2.3,D_3 = 150 mm" riferimento=0 titolo="Plan view of a pipe tee"}

:::soluzione
$p_2 = 220.5\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and $p_3 = 219.0\,\mathrm{kPa}$. The component $F_x = 4.01\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts in the direction of the incoming flow, and $F_y = 4.01\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts opposite to flow in the side branch. The resultant is $F = 5.67\,\mathrm{kN}$.
:::
