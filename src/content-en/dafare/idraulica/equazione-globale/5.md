A nozzle on a horizontal pipe of diameter $D_1 = 200\,\mathrm{mm}$ discharges to the atmosphere through an outlet of diameter $d_2 = 70\,\mathrm{mm}$. The upstream gauge pressure is $p_1 = 180\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Neglecting losses, find the discharge, jet speed, and fluid force on the nozzle shown.

::schema{acqua="0,0.75 4,0.75 4.6,1.05 7.6,1.05 7.6,1.55 4.6,1.55 4,1.85 0,1.85" linee="0,0.75 4,0.75 4.6,1.05 6.4,1.05;0,1.85 4,1.85 4.6,1.55 6.4,1.55" frecce="0.5,1.3,1.6,1.3,Q" tratteggi="2.4,0.5 2.4,2.1;6.4,0.8 6.4,1.8" etichette="2.4,2.4,1;6.4,2.1,2;0.3,2.1,D_1 = 200 mm;5.4,0.5,d_2 = 70 mm;0.5,2.7,p_1 = 180 kPa" riferimento=0 titolo="Nozzle on a pipe of diameter $200\,\mathrm{mm}$"}

:::soluzione
$Q = 73.6\,\mathrm{L/s}$, $v_1 = 2.34\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_2 = 19.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The force $F = 4.42\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downstream.
:::
