A water jet of diameter $d = 45\,\mathrm{mm}$ and speed $v = 22\,\mathrm{m/s}$ enters a fixed vane and leaves at angle $\theta = 135^\circ$ to its inlet direction. Neglecting friction, find the force components on the vane, the resultant magnitude, and its angle to the incoming jet.

:::soluzione
$Q = 35.0\,\mathrm{L/s}$. The components are $F_x = 1314\,\mathrm{N}$ and $F_y = 544\,\mathrm{N}$. The resultant $F = 1422\,\mathrm{N}$ makes an angle of $22.5^\circ$ with the incoming jet.
:::
