A horizontal conical reducer changes the pipe diameter from $D_1 = 350\,\mathrm{mm}$ to $D_2 = 250\,\mathrm{mm}$ and carries discharge $Q = 200\,\mathrm{L/s}$. The upstream gauge pressure is $p_1 = 280\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Neglecting losses, find the downstream pressure and the fluid force on the reducer.

:::soluzione
$v_1 = 2.08\,\mathrm{m/s}$, $v_2 = 4.07\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $p_2 = 273.9\,\mathrm{kPa}$. The force $F = 13.1\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downstream.
:::
