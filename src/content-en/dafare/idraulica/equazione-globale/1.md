A horizontal $90^\circ$ pipe elbow of diameter $D = 250\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries discharge $Q = 80\,\mathrm{L/s}$. Head loss is negligible, so gauge pressure is $p = 300\,\mathrm{kPa}$ at both sections. Find the components and magnitude of the fluid force on the elbow shown.

::schema{nodi="A,0,0,-;B,3.2,0,-;C,3.2,3.2,-" condotte="A-B-C,0.55" tratteggi="0.9,-0.6 0.9,0.6;2.65,2.2 3.75,2.2" frecce="0.1,0,0.9,0,Q" etichette="0.72,0.9,1;3.95,2.2,2;0.2,-0.95,D = 250 mm" riferimento=0 titolo="Plan view of an $90^\circ$ elbow"}

:::soluzione
$v = 1.63\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The component $F_x = 14.86\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts in the direction of the incoming flow and $F_y = 14.86\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts opposite to the outgoing flow. The resultant $F = 21.0\,\mathrm{kN}$ makes an angle of $45^\circ$ with the inlet axis.
:::
