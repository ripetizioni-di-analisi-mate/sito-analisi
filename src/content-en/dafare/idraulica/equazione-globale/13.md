A horizontal pipe expands abruptly from diameter $D_1 = 200\,\mathrm{mm}$ to diameter $D_2 = 300\,\mathrm{mm}$. The discharge is $Q = 80\,\mathrm{L/s}$ and the gauge pressure immediately upstream is $p_1 = 150\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Taking the pressure over the annular step to be $p_1$, find the downstream pressure, the axial force on the annulus, and the head loss.

:::soluzione
$v_1 = 2.55\,\mathrm{m/s}$, $v_2 = 1.13\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $p_2 = 151.6\,\mathrm{kPa}$. The force on the annular shoulder is $5.89\,\mathrm{kN}$ and acts upstream. The head loss is $\Delta H = 0.102\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
