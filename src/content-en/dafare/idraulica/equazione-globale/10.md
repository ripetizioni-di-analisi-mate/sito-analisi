A vertical gate spans a horizontal rectangular channel of width $b = 4.00\,\mathrm{m}$. Water depths are $h_1 = 3.00\,\mathrm{m}$ upstream and $h_2 = 0.60\,\mathrm{m}$ downstream. Neglecting losses and bed resistance, find the discharge, force on the gate, and Froude number at each section shown.

::schema{acqua="0,0 3,0 3,3 0,3;3,0 3.3,0 3.3,0.6 3,0.6;3.3,0 8,0 8,0.6 3.3,0.6" solidi="0,-0.45 8,-0.45 8,0 0,0;3,0.6 3.3,0.6 3.3,4 3,4" pelo="0,3,3,p_a;3.3,8,0.6" quote="0 0,0 3,h_1 = 3.00 m,-1.0" frecce="0.6,1.4,1.8,1.4,Q" etichette="2,3.4,1;5.2,1.6,2;7.3,1.1,h_2 = 0.60 m;5,2.8,b = 4.00 m" riferimento=0 titolo="Gate in a channel of width $4.00\,\mathrm{m}$"}

:::soluzione
$Q = 16.8\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. The force $S = 75.3\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downstream. The upstream flow has $Fr_1 = 0.26$ and is subcritical. The downstream flow has $Fr_2 = 2.89$ and is supercritical.
:::
