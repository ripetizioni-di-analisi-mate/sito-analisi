A horizontal diffuser increases the pipe diameter from $D_1 = 200\,\mathrm{mm}$ to $D_2 = 350\,\mathrm{mm}$ and carries discharge $Q = 100\,\mathrm{L/s}$. The inlet gauge pressure is $p_1 = 160\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and the local loss is $\Delta H = \xi\,\dfrac{(v_1 - v_2)^2}{2g}$ with $\xi = 0.30$. Find the outlet pressure and the fluid force on the diffuser shown.

::schema{acqua="0,0.95 2.6,0.95 3.8,0.5 7,0.5 7,2.1 3.8,2.1 2.6,1.65 0,1.65" linee="0,0.95 2.6,0.95 3.8,0.5 7,0.5;0,1.65 2.6,1.65 3.8,2.1 7,2.1" frecce="0.5,1.3,1.6,1.3,Q" tratteggi="1.6,0.7 1.6,1.9;5.6,0.25 5.6,2.35" etichette="1.6,2.2,1;5.6,2.65,2;0.3,1.9,D_1 = 200 mm;5.2,0.0,D_2 = 350 mm" riferimento=0 titolo="Diffuser expanding from $200$ to $350\,\mathrm{mm}$"}

:::soluzione
$v_1 = 3.18\,\mathrm{m/s}$, $v_2 = 1.04\,\mathrm{m/s}$, $\Delta H = 0.0703\,\mathrm{m}$ and $p_2 = 163.8\,\mathrm{kPa}$. The force $F = 10.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts upstream.
:::
