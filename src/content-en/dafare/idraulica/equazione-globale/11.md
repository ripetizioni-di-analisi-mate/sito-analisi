A horizontal rectangular channel of width $b = 2.50\,\mathrm{m}$ carries discharge $Q = 4.50\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. A flat gate maintains upstream depth $h_1 = 1.80\,\mathrm{m}$. Neglecting losses, find the downstream depth and the fluid force on the gate.

:::soluzione
$E = 1.851\,\mathrm{m}$. The downstream depth is $h_2 = 0.329\,\mathrm{m}$ and the velocity is $v_2 = 5.46\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The force $S = 18.3\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downstream.
:::
