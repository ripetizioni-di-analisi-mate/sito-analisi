A $90^\circ$ elbow of diameter $D = 250\,\mathrm{mm}$ turns horizontal flow vertically upward. The discharge is $Q = 100\,\mathrm{L/s}$, the upstream gauge pressure $p_1 = 200\,\mathrm{kPa}$, and the outlet is $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$ above the inlet. The elbow contains volume $W = 0.035\,\mathrm{m^3}$ of water. Neglecting losses, find the outlet pressure and the fluid force on the elbow shown.

::schema{nodi="A,0,0,-;B,3,0,-;C,3,3,-" condotte="A-B-C,0.55" tratteggi="0.9,-0.6 0.9,0.6;2.4,2.1 3.6,2.1" quote="0.9 0,0.9 2.1,0.80 m,-1.1" frecce="0.1,0,0.8,0,Q" etichette="0.7,0.85,1;3.75,2.1,2;0.2,-0.95,D = 250 mm" riferimento=0 titolo="Elbow in a vertical plane containing $W = 0.035\,\mathrm{m^3}$ of water"}

:::soluzione
$p_2 = 192.2\,\mathrm{kPa}$. The horizontal component $F_x = 10.02\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts in the direction of the incoming flow, and $F_z = 9.98\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. The resultant is $F = 14.1\,\mathrm{kN}$.
:::
