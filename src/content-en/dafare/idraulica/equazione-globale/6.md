A water jet of diameter $d = 40\,\mathrm{mm}$ leaves a nozzle at speed $v = 18\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and strikes a fixed flat plate normal to the jet axis. Calculate the discharge and the force the jet exerts on the plate.

:::soluzione
$Q = 22.6\,\mathrm{L/s}$. The force $F = 407\,\mathrm{N}$ acts in the direction of the jet velocity.
:::
