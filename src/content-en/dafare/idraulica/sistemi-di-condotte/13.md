The closed loop shown has branches $1\!-\!2$ ($500\,\mathrm{m}$, $250\,\mathrm{mm}$, $\lambda = 0.022$), $2\!-\!3$ ($400\,\mathrm{m}$, $200\,\mathrm{mm}$, $\lambda = 0.023$), $4\!-\!3$ ($300\,\mathrm{m}$, $200\,\mathrm{mm}$, $\lambda = 0.023$) and $1\!-\!4$ ($400\,\mathrm{m}$, $250\,\mathrm{mm}$, $\lambda = 0.022$). At node $1$ the inflow is $120\,\mathrm{L/s}$. Withdrawals at nodes $2$, $3$ and $4$ are $40$, $50$ and $30\,\mathrm{L/s}$, respectively. Starting from $Q_{12} = 70\,\mathrm{L/s}$, $Q_{23} = 30\,\mathrm{L/s}$, $Q_{14} = 50\,\mathrm{L/s}$ and $Q_{43} = 20\,\mathrm{L/s}$ and traversing the loop clockwise, find the corrections at the first two iterations and the final discharges for stopping criterion $|\Delta Q| < 0.5\,\mathrm{L/s}$.
