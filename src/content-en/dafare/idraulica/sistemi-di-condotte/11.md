In the network shown, a reservoir with free surface at $110.00\,\mathrm{m}$ feeds a pipe of length $700\,\mathrm{m}$, diameter $300\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $\lambda = 0.021$, which carries $140\,\mathrm{L/s}$ to node $A$. From node $A$, branch $A\!-\!B$ ($500\,\mathrm{m}$, $200\,\mathrm{mm}$, $\lambda = 0.023$) delivers $50\,\mathrm{L/s}$ to node $B$ at elevation $80.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and branch $A\!-\!C$ ($900\,\mathrm{m}$, $250\,\mathrm{mm}$, $\lambda = 0.022$) delivers $90\,\mathrm{L/s}$ to node $C$ at elevation $75.00\,\mathrm{m}$. Calculate the piezometric heads at the three nodes and the pressures at $B$ and $C$.

::schema{nodi="R,3,12;A,13,12;B,24,17;C,24,6,-" condotte="R-A,0.5,;A-B,0.34,;A-C,0.42," acqua="0,9 3,9 3,15 0,15" solidi="0,8 3,8 3,9 0,9" pelo="0,3,15,110.00 m" frecce="24.8,17,26.6,17,;24.8,6,26.6,6," etichette="16,23,R-A: 700 m - 300 mm;16,21.6,A-B: 500 m - 200 mm;16,20.2,A-C: 900 m - 250 mm;28.6,17,50 L/s;28.6,6,90 L/s;22,18.8,z_B = 80.00 m;22,4.2,z_C = 75.00 m;24.74,5.26,C" titolo="Branched network with two terminal nodes"}

:::soluzione
The junction heads are $h_A = 100.20\,\mathrm{m}$, $h_B = 92.78\,\mathrm{m}$, and $h_C = 86.63\,\mathrm{m}$. The terminal pressure heads are $\dfrac{p_B}{\gamma} = 12.78\,\mathrm{m}$ (a pressure of $125\,\mathrm{kPa}$) and $\dfrac{p_C}{\gamma} = 11.63\,\mathrm{m}$ (a pressure of $114\,\mathrm{kPa}$).
:::
