A pipe of length $2500\,\mathrm{m}$, diameter $300\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $\lambda = 0.021$ connects two reservoirs with head difference $\Delta z = 30.00\,\mathrm{m}$. To increase capacity, an identical pipe is added in parallel along the first $1500\,\mathrm{m}$. Calculate the discharge before and after the addition, and the percentage increase.

:::soluzione
The original discharge is $Q_0 = 130\,\mathrm{L/s}$. Adding the parallel pipe raises it to $Q = 175\,\mathrm{L/s}$, an increase of $35\%$.
:::
