Three reservoirs with free surfaces at $z_A = 65.00\,\mathrm{m}$, $z_B = 50.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and $z_C = 40.00\,\mathrm{m}$ connect to node $N$ through pipes $A\!-\!N$ ($400\,\mathrm{m}$, $350\,\mathrm{mm}$, $\lambda = 0.020$), $B\!-\!N$ ($1000\,\mathrm{m}$, $250\,\mathrm{mm}$, $\lambda = 0.022$) and $C\!-\!N$ ($1500\,\mathrm{m}$, $250\,\mathrm{mm}$, $\lambda = 0.022$). Carry out the trial with $h_N = z_B$, establish the flow direction in branch $B$, and find the nodal head and three discharges.

:::soluzione
At $h_N = z_B$, the trial gives $Q_A = 345\,\mathrm{L/s} > Q_C = 59.8\,\mathrm{L/s}$, so the junction supplies reservoir $B$. The final junction head is $h_N = 61.51\,\mathrm{m}$ and the discharges are $Q_A = 166\,\mathrm{L/s}$, $Q_B = 78.6\,\mathrm{L/s}$, and $Q_C = 87.8\,\mathrm{L/s}$.
:::
