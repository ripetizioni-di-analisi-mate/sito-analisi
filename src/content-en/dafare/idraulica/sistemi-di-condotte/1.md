Two reservoirs whose water surfaces differ in elevation by $\Delta z = 25.00\,\mathrm{m}$ are connected by two pipes in series. The first has length $1200\,\mathrm{m}$, diameter $300\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $\lambda_1 = 0.022$. The second has length $900\,\mathrm{m}$, diameter $250\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $\lambda_2 = 0.023$. Neglect minor losses and velocity heads. Find the two pipe resistances, the common discharge and the head loss in each pipe.

:::soluzione
The pipe resistances are $r_1 = 898\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$ and $r_2 = 1751\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$. The common discharge is $Q = 97.1\,\mathrm{L/s}$, and the respective head losses are $\Delta H_1 = 8.47\,\mathrm{m}$ and $\Delta H_2 = 16.53\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
