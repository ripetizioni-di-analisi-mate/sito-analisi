Two reservoirs with head difference $\Delta z = 22.00\,\mathrm{m}$ are connected by a common pipe of length $600\,\mathrm{m}$, diameter $300\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $\lambda = 0.021$, followed by two parallel branches. The first has length $1000\,\mathrm{m}$, diameter $250\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $\lambda_1 = 0.022$. The second has length $800\,\mathrm{m}$, diameter $200\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $\lambda_2 = 0.023$. Calculate the total discharge, the losses in the common pipe and parallel section, and the two branch discharges.

:::soluzione
The total discharge is $Q = 139\,\mathrm{L/s}$. The common pipe loses $\Delta H_{\text{common}} = 8.32\,\mathrm{m}$ and the parallel branches lose $\Delta H_{\text{parallel}} = 13.68\,\mathrm{m}$. The branch discharges are $Q_1 = 85.7\,\mathrm{L/s}$ and $Q_2 = 53.7\,\mathrm{L/s}$.
:::
