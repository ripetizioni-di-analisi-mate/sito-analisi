A pump conveys $90\,\mathrm{L/s}$ between two reservoirs with head difference $\Delta z = 45.00\,\mathrm{m}$. The pipeline has two sections in series. The first has length $800\,\mathrm{m}$, diameter $250\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $\lambda_1 = 0.021$. The second has length $1200\,\mathrm{m}$, diameter $300\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $\lambda_2 = 0.022$. The overall pumping efficiency is $\eta = 0.74$. Find the head loss in each section, the pump head and the input power.

:::soluzione
The two head losses are $\Delta H_1 = 11.51\,\mathrm{m}$ and $\Delta H_2 = 7.27\,\mathrm{m}$. The required pump head is $H_p = 63.78\,\mathrm{m}$ and the input power is $P = 76.1\,\mathrm{kW}$.
:::
