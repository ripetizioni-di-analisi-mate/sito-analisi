A pipe of length $1200\,\mathrm{m}$ receives $Q_m = 72\,\mathrm{L/s}$ upstream, all of which is withdrawn uniformly along the pipe. No throughflow leaves the downstream end. The available head is $8.00\,\mathrm{m}$. Assuming $\lambda = 0.022$, calculate the equivalent discharge and minimum diameter, select a standard diameter from $200$, $250$ and $300\,\mathrm{mm}$, and find the resulting head loss.

:::soluzione
The equivalent discharge is $Q_e = 41.6\,\mathrm{L/s}$. The diameter must satisfy $D \geq 216\,\mathrm{mm}$. Selecting $D = 250\,\mathrm{mm}$ gives a head loss of $\Delta H = 3.86\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
