A pipe of length $2000\,\mathrm{m}$, diameter $300\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $\lambda = 0.022$ receives $Q_m = 140\,\mathrm{L/s}$ upstream and supplies a uniform withdrawal along its length, leaving $Q_v = 50\,\mathrm{L/s}$ downstream. Find the withdrawal per unit length, the equivalent discharge and the pipe head loss.

:::soluzione
The withdrawal per unit length is $p = 0.0450\,\mathrm{L/(s\,m)}$, the equivalent discharge is $Q_e = 98.5\,\mathrm{L/s}$, and the head loss is $\Delta H = 14.51\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
