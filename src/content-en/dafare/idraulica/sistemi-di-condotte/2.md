titolo: "Diameter of an equivalent pipe"

---

A pipeline consists of two sections in series. The first has length $1000\,\mathrm{m}$, diameter $300\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $\lambda_1 = 0.021$. The second has length $800\,\mathrm{m}$, diameter $200\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $\lambda_2 = 0.024$. A single pipe of length $1800\,\mathrm{m}$ with $\lambda_e = 0.022$ is to replace them, with the same head loss at the same discharge. Find the equivalent resistance and the diameter of the equivalent pipe.

:::soluzione
The equivalent resistance is $r_{\mathrm{eq}} = 5672\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$, giving an equivalent diameter of $D_e = 225\,\mathrm{mm}$.
:::
