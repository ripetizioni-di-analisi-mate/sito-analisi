teoria: [resistenza-idraulica, rete-ramificata, dimensionamento-rami]

In a branched network node $A$ has piezometric head $h_A = 121.00\,\mathrm{m}$. A branch from $A$ of length $900\,\mathrm{m}$ with $\lambda = 0.022$ must deliver $90\,\mathrm{L/s}$ to terminal node $B$ at elevation $95.00\,\mathrm{m}$ while maintaining a pressure of at least $20.00\,\mathrm{m}$ of water. Find the available head loss and minimum diameter. Select the standard diameter from $200$, $250$ and $300\,\mathrm{mm}$ and calculate the resulting pressure at $B$.

:::soluzione
The available head loss is $6.00\,\mathrm{m}$, which requires $D \geq 294\,\mathrm{mm}$. Selecting $D = 300\,\mathrm{mm}$ gives a head loss of $\Delta H = 5.45\,\mathrm{m}$ and a terminal pressure head of $\dfrac{p_B}{\gamma} = 20.55\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
