titolo: "Equivalent pipe for three parallel branches"
teoria: [condotte-in-parallelo, condotta-equivalente, raddoppio]

---

Three identical pipes of length $1500\,\mathrm{m}$, diameter $200\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $\lambda = 0.023$ connect the same nodes in parallel. Find the resistance of one pipe, their combined equivalent resistance, and the diameter of a single equivalent pipe of the same length and friction factor.

:::soluzione
Each pipe has resistance $r = 8908\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$. Together they have equivalent resistance $r_{\mathrm{eq}} = \dfrac{r}{9} = 990\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$ and equivalent diameter $D_e = 9^{\frac{1}{5}}\cdot 200 = 310\,\mathrm{mm}$.
:::
