Two parallel pipes connect nodes $M$ and $V$. The first has length $1000\,\mathrm{m}$, diameter $250\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $\lambda_1 = 0.022$. The second has length $700\,\mathrm{m}$, diameter $200\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $\lambda_2 = 0.023$. At node $M$ the inflow is $Q = 120\,\mathrm{L/s}$. Find the equivalent resistance, the head loss between the nodes and the two branch discharges.

:::soluzione
The equivalent resistance is $r_{\mathrm{eq}} = 668\,\mathrm{s^2/m^5}$ and the head loss is $\Delta H = 9.62\,\mathrm{m}$. The branch discharges are $Q_1 = 71.9\,\mathrm{L/s}$ and $Q_2 = 48.1\,\mathrm{L/s}$.
:::
