Three reservoirs with free surfaces at $z_A = 90.00\,\mathrm{m}$, $z_B = 70.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and $z_C = 45.00\,\mathrm{m}$ connect to node $N$ through pipes $A\!-\!N$ ($1000\,\mathrm{m}$, $300\,\mathrm{mm}$, $\lambda = 0.022$), $B\!-\!N$ ($600\,\mathrm{m}$, $200\,\mathrm{mm}$, $\lambda = 0.023$) and $N\!-\!C$ ($1300\,\mathrm{m}$, $350\,\mathrm{mm}$, $\lambda = 0.021$), as shown. Determine the flow direction in branch $B$ and find the nodal head and three discharges.
