A pipe must carry $Q = 200\,\mathrm{L/s}$ at a mean speed no greater than $1.80\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The available standard diameters are $100$, $150$, $200$, $250$, $300$, $400$, and $500\,\mathrm{mm}$. Find the theoretical minimum diameter, select the standard size, and find the actual mean speed.

:::soluzione
The minimum diameter is $0.376\,\mathrm{m}$. Selecting standard diameter $D = 400\,\mathrm{mm}$ gives an actual mean velocity of $v = 1.59\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
