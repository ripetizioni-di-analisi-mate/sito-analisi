The air duct shown changes from diameter $D_1 = 400\,\mathrm{mm}$ to $D_2 = 300\,\mathrm{mm}$. At the first section the density is $\rho_1 = 1.25\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and mean speed $v_1 = 10.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$, while at the second the density is $\rho_2 = 1.80\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. Find the mass flow rate, downstream mean speed, and both volumetric flow rates.

::schema{nodi="A,0,0,-;B,3,0,-;C,3.8,0,-;D,7.2,0,-" condotte="A-B,1.2,D_1 = 400 mm;C-D,0.9,D_2 = 300 mm" linee="3,0.6 3.8,0.45;3,-0.6 3.8,-0.45" frecce="1.2,0,2.4,0,v_1;4.9,0,6.1,0,v_2" etichette="1.0,-0.95,ρ_1 = 1.25 kg/m³;5.6,-0.9,ρ_2 = 1.80 kg/m³" riferimento=0 titolo="Air duct with $v_1 = 10.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at the first cross section"}

:::soluzione
$Q_m = 1.57\,\mathrm{kg/s}$; $v_2 = 12.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $Q_1 = 1.26\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ and $Q_2 = 0.873\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$.
:::
