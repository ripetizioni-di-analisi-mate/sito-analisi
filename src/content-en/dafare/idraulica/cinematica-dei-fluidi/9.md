In the rectangular channel shown, width is $b = 2.50\,\mathrm{m}$, depth is $h = 0.90\,\mathrm{m}$, and the vertical velocity profile is
$$
u = u_{\max}\left(\frac{2y}{h} - \frac{y^2}{h^2}\right),
$$
where $y$ is distance above the bed and $u_{\max} = 1.50\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is surface speed. Find the mean speed, discharge, and correction factors $\alpha$ and $\beta$.

:::soluzione
$v = 1.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $Q = 2.25\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$; $\alpha = \dfrac{54}{35} = 1.54$; $\beta = \dfrac{6}{5} = 1.20$.
