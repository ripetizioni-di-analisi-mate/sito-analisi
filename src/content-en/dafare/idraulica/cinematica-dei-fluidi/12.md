The tank shown has length $L = 15.0\,\mathrm{m}$ and a trapezoidal cross section with bottom width $b = 3.00\,\mathrm{m}$ and side slope $1:1$. Initially its level is $2.00\,\mathrm{m}$ above the floor. Given net outflow $Q = 60\,\mathrm{L/s}$, find the volume withdrawn and the time for the level to fall to $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$.

::schema{acqua="0,0 3,0 5,2 -2,2" solidi="0,0 -2.5,2.5 -3.6,2.5 -3.6,-1.1 6.6,-1.1 6.6,2.5 5.5,2.5 3,0" pelo="-2,5,2" tratteggi="-0.5,0.5 3.5,0.5" quote="0 0,3 0,b = 3.00 m,0.4" riferimento=0 titolo="Trapezoidal tank with side slope $1:1$, initial level $2.00\,\mathrm{m}$, and final level $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$"}

:::soluzione
The volume withdrawn is $V = 124\,\mathrm{m^3}$. The time is $t = 2060\,\mathrm{s}$, just over $34$ minutes.
:::
