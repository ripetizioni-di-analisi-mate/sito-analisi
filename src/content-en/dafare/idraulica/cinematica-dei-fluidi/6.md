A pipe of diameter $D = 100\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries oil of kinematic viscosity $\nu = 2.5 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{m^2/s}$. Find the mean speed and discharge at Reynolds number $2000$, above which laminar flow becomes unstable.

:::soluzione
$v = 0.500\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $Q = 3.93 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^3/s} = 3.93\,\mathrm{L/s}$.
:::
