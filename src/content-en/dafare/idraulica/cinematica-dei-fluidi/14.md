An incompressible fluid in plane flow has velocity field
$$
\vec{v} = (a\,y,\;a\,x),
$$
with $a = 0.500\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$, coordinates in meters, and velocities in $\mathrm{m/s}$. Verify the differential continuity equation, find the streamline equation, and calculate speed and velocity components at point $P = (3.00;\,1.00)$.

:::soluzione
$\operatorname{div}\vec{v} = 0$, so the field is consistent with incompressible flow. The streamlines $x^2 - y^2 = \mathrm{constant}$ are rectangular hyperbolas whose asymptotes bisect the coordinate axes. The streamline through $P$ is $x^2 - y^2 = 8.00$. At $P$ the components are $v_x = 0.500\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_y = 1.50\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the speed is $1.58\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
