A pipe of diameter $D = 150\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries water at discharge $Q = 25\,\mathrm{L/s}$ and temperature $20\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ with $\nu = 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^2/s}$. Calculate the mean speed and Reynolds number and identify the flow regime.

:::soluzione
$v = 1.41\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The Reynolds number $Re = 2.12 \cdot 10^5$ is much greater than $4000$, so the flow is turbulent.
:::
