A cylindrical tank of diameter $D = 6.00\,\mathrm{m}$ receives inflow $Q_e = 25\,\mathrm{L/s}$ and releases outflow $Q_u = 10\,\mathrm{L/s}$, as shown. Find the speed at which its surface rises and the time for the level to rise by $1.80\,\mathrm{m}$.

The free-surface area is $A = 28.3\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The water level rises at $5.31 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m/s}$, or $1.91\,\mathrm{m/h}$. The time is $t = 3390\,\mathrm{s}$, about $56$ minutes and thirty seconds.
