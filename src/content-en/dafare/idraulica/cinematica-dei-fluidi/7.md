Pipe $1$ supplies $Q_1 = 140\,\mathrm{L/s}$ to the junction shown. Pipes $2$ and $3$ carry water away, with discharge $Q_2 = 55\,\mathrm{L/s}$ in the former and diameter $D_3 = 250\,\mathrm{mm}$ in the latter. Find the discharge and mean speed in pipe $3$.

:::soluzione
$Q_3 = 85\,\mathrm{L/s} = 0.0850\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$; $v_3 = 1.73\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
