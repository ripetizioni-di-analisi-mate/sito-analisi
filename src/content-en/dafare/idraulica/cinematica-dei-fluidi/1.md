A water main of diameter $D = 250\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries discharge $Q = 60\,\mathrm{L/s}$ at temperature $20\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ with $\nu = 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^2/s}$. Find the cross-sectional area, mean speed, mass flow rate, and Reynolds number, and identify the flow regime.

:::soluzione
$A = 0.0491\,\mathrm{m^2}$, $v = 1.22\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $Q_m = 60.0\,\mathrm{kg/s}$. The Reynolds number is $Re = 3.06 \cdot 10^5$, so the flow is turbulent.
:::
