The series pipe sections shown have diameters $D_1 = 250\,\mathrm{mm}$, $D_2 = 200\,\mathrm{mm}$, and $D_3 = 300\,\mathrm{mm}$ and carry discharge $Q = 80\,\mathrm{L/s}$. Find the mean speed in each and identify the section with the highest speed.

::schema{nodi="A,0,0,-;B,2.6,0,-;C,3.2,0,-;D,5.4,0,-;E,6.0,0,-;F,8.6,0,-" condotte="A-B,1.0,D_1 = 250 mm;C-D,0.8,D_2 = 200 mm;E-F,1.2,D_3 = 300 mm" linee="2.6,0.5 3.2,0.4;2.6,-0.5 3.2,-0.4;5.4,0.4 6.0,0.6;5.4,-0.4 6.0,-0.6" frecce="0.6,0,1.6,0,Q = 80 L/s" riferimento=0 titolo="Three pipe sections carrying the same flow rate in series"}

:::soluzione
$v_1 = 1.63\,\mathrm{m/s}$, $v_2 = 2.55\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_3 = 1.13\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The velocity is greatest in the second section, which has the smallest diameter.
:::
