A new cast-iron pipe of diameter $D = 300\,\mathrm{mm}$ and length $L = 1200\,\mathrm{m}$, with roughness coefficient $k_s = 90\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$, carries $Q = 90\,\mathrm{L/s}$ of water. Find the energy gradient, head loss and corresponding Darcy friction factor and Chézy coefficient.

:::soluzione
$v = 1.27\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $R = 0.0750\,\mathrm{m}$; $J = 0.00633$; $\Delta H = 7.59\,\mathrm{m}$; $\lambda = 0.0230$; $\chi = 58.4\,\mathrm{m^{1/2}/s}$.
:::
