A straight pipe of diameter $D = 200\,\mathrm{mm}$ and length $L = 800\,\mathrm{m}$ carries a discharge of $Q = 40\,\mathrm{L/s}$. The measured head loss is $\Delta H = 9.60\,\mathrm{m}$, as shown. Find the Strickler coefficient, Chézy coefficient and Darcy friction factor.

::schema{nodi="A,0,1,-;B,12,1,-" condotte="A-B,0.45" linee="1.5,1.22 1.5,5.2;10.5,1.22 10.5,3.4" piezometrica="1.5,5.2 10.5,3.4" tratteggi="1.5,5.2 11.3,5.2" etichette="0.2,5.4,piezometers;5.4,0.3,D = 200 mm · Q = 40 L/s" quote="1.5 0,10.5 0,L = 800 m,1.4;11.3 3.4,11.3 5.2,ΔH = 9.60 m,0.9" riferimento=0 titolo="Head loss measured over $800\,\mathrm{m}$"}

:::soluzione
$v = 1.27\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $R = 0.0500\,\mathrm{m}$; $J = 0.0120$; $k_s = 85.6\,\mathrm{m^{1/3}/s}$; $\chi = 52.0\,\mathrm{m^{1/2}/s}$; $\lambda = 0.0290$.
