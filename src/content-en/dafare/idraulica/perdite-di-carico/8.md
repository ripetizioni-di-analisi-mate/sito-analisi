The horizontal pipe shown contracts abruptly from diameter $D_1 = 250\,\mathrm{mm}$ to diameter $D_2 = 150\,\mathrm{mm}$ and carries $Q = 45\,\mathrm{L/s}$ of water. The contraction coefficient is $C_c = 0.63$. Find the velocity at the vena contracta, the coefficient $\xi$ based on downstream velocity and the head loss.

:::soluzione
$v_1 = 0.917\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_2 = 2.55\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $v_c = 4.04\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $\xi = 0.345$; $\Delta H = 0.114\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
