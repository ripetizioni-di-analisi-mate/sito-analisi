A cast-iron pipe of diameter $D = 400\,\mathrm{mm}$ and length $L = 600\,\mathrm{m}$, with $\varepsilon = 0.5\,\mathrm{mm}$, carries $Q = 250\,\mathrm{L/s}$ of water. Find the energy gradient, head loss, wall shear stress and total tangential force exerted by the water on the pipe wall.

:::soluzione
$v = 1.99\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $Re = 7.96 \cdot 10^5$; $\lambda = 0.0211$; $J = 0.0106$; $\Delta H = 6.37\,\mathrm{m}$; $\tau_0 = 10.4\,\mathrm{Pa}$; $F = 7.86\,\mathrm{kN}$.
:::
