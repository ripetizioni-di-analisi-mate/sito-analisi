A steel pipe with $\varepsilon = 0.1\,\mathrm{mm}$ must carry $Q = 60\,\mathrm{L/s}$ of water with an energy gradient no greater than $J_{\max} = 0.004$. Find the theoretical diameter, select a standard diameter from $100$, $150$, $200$, $250$, $300$, $400$ and $500\,\mathrm{mm}$, and check the resulting energy gradient.

:::soluzione
The calculated diameter is $D = 265\,\mathrm{mm}$. At $D = 250\,\mathrm{mm}$ the energy gradient is $J = 0.00536 > J_{\max}$, so select $D = 300\,\mathrm{mm}$. For this diameter the results are $v = 0.849\,\mathrm{m/s}$, $\lambda = 0.0175$, and $J = 0.00214$.
:::
