The horizontal pipe shown expands abruptly from diameter $D_1 = 200\,\mathrm{mm}$ to diameter $D_2 = 300\,\mathrm{mm}$ and carries $Q = 75\,\mathrm{L/s}$ of water. Find the head loss, the coefficient $\xi$ based on upstream velocity and the pressure change between the two sections.

::schema{nodi="A,0,2,-;B,5,2,-;C,12,2,-" condotte="A-B,0.8;B-C,1.2" frecce="1.6,2,3.0,2,v_1;8.0,2,9.4,2,v_2" etichette="5.0,3.1,1;9.0,3.1,2;2.2,1.05,D_1 = 200 mm;9.6,0.85,D_2 = 300 mm" riferimento=0 titolo="Sudden expansion from $200$ to $300\,\mathrm{mm}$"}

:::soluzione
The velocities are $v_1 = 2.39\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_2 = 1.06\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The head loss is $\Delta H = 0.0897\,\mathrm{m}$ and the loss coefficient is $\xi = 0.309$. Since $p_2 - p_1 = 1.41\,\mathrm{kPa}$, the pressure increases across the expansion.
:::
