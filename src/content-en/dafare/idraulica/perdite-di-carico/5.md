The pipe shown has diameter $D = 125\,\mathrm{mm}$ and carries $Q = 22\,\mathrm{L/s}$ of water. It includes a sharp-edged entrance ($\xi = 0.5$), two $90^\circ$ bends ($\xi = 0.3$ each), an open butterfly valve ($\xi = 0.3$) and an exit into the downstream reservoir ($\xi = 1$). Find the combined minor loss.

::schema{nodi="A,1,4.6,-;B,1,0.8,-;C,11,0.8,-;D,11,2.6,-" condotte="A-B-C-D,0.35" acqua="-1.4,4.1 1.6,4.1 1.6,6.2 -1.4,6.2;9.4,2.2 12.4,2.2 12.4,3.6 9.4,3.6" pelo="-1.4,1.6,6.2,;9.4,12.4,3.6," linee="-1.4,6.6 -1.4,4.1 1.6,4.1 1.6,6.6;9.4,4.0 9.4,2.2 12.4,2.2 12.4,4.0" etichette="3.0,4.3,inlet;2.1,1.25,bend;8.0,1.25,bend;5.0,1.25,valve;8.2,2.9,outlet;5.4,0.0,D = 125 mm · Q = 22 L/s" riferimento=0 titolo="Pipe with an entrance, two bends, a butterfly valve and an exit"}

:::soluzione
$v = 1.79\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $\dfrac{v^2}{2g} = 0.164\,\mathrm{m}$; $\sum \xi_i = 2.40$; $\Delta H_{\mathrm{loc}} = 0.393\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
