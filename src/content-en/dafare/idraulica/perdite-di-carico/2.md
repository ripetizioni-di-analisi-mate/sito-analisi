A horizontal cast-iron pipe of diameter $D = 200\,\mathrm{mm}$ and length $L = 900\,\mathrm{m}$, with $\varepsilon = 0.5\,\mathrm{mm}$, carries $Q = 45\,\mathrm{L/s}$ of water. The upstream gauge pressure is $p_1 = 500\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Find the head loss and the downstream gauge pressure.

:::soluzione
$v = 1.43\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $\lambda = 0.0254$; $J = 0.0133$; $\Delta H = 12.0\,\mathrm{m}$; $p_2 = 383\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
:::
