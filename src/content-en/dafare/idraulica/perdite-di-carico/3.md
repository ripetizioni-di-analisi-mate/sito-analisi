A pipe of diameter $D = 80\,\mathrm{mm}$ and length $L = 250\,\mathrm{m}$ carries $Q = 3.8\,\mathrm{L/s}$ of oil with $\rho = 900\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and $\nu = 6 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{m^2/s}$. Determine the flow regime and calculate the Darcy friction factor, energy gradient, head loss, centerline velocity and wall shear stress.

:::soluzione
The mean velocity is $v = 0.756\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the Reynolds number is $Re = 1008$, so the flow is laminar. The remaining results are $\lambda = 0.0635$, $J = 0.0231$, $\Delta H = 5.78\,\mathrm{m}$, $v_{\max} = 1.51\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and $\tau_0 = 4.08\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
:::
