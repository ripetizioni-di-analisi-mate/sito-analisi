The pipe shown has diameter $D = 150\,\mathrm{mm}$, length $L = 120\,\mathrm{m}$ and Darcy friction factor $\lambda = 0.023$. It includes a sharp-edged entrance ($\xi = 0.5$), two bends ($\xi = 0.3$ each), a check valve ($\xi = 2.0$) and an exit ($\xi = 1$). Find the equivalent length of each fitting, the total equivalent length and the percentage increase it represents over the physical pipe length.

:::soluzione
The equivalent lengths are $3.26\,\mathrm{m}$ for the entrance, $1.96\,\mathrm{m}$ for each bend, $13.0\,\mathrm{m}$ for the check valve, and $6.52\,\mathrm{m}$ for the outlet. In total, $\sum \xi_i = 4.10$ and $L_{\mathrm{eq}} = 26.7\,\mathrm{m}$, increasing the effective length by $22.3\,\%$.
:::
