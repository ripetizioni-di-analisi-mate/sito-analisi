A straight steel pipe of diameter $D = 250\,\mathrm{mm}$ and length $L = 1500\,\mathrm{m}$, with absolute roughness $\varepsilon = 0.15\,\mathrm{mm}$, carries $Q = 70\,\mathrm{L/s}$ of water at $20\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. Find the mean velocity, Reynolds number, Darcy friction factor, energy gradient and head loss.

:::soluzione
$v = 1.43\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $Re = 3.57 \cdot 10^5$; $\lambda = 0.0186$; $J = 0.00769$; $\Delta H = 11.5\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
