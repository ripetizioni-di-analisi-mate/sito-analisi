The steel pipe shown has $\varepsilon = 0.1\,\mathrm{mm}$, diameter $D = 150\,\mathrm{mm}$ and length $L = 80\,\mathrm{m}$ and carries $Q = 28\,\mathrm{L/s}$ of water. It includes a sharp-edged entrance ($\xi = 0.5$), two bends ($\xi = 0.25$ each), an open gate valve ($\xi = 0.2$) and an exit ($\xi = 1$). Find the friction loss, minor loss and fraction of total head loss due to minor losses.

::schema{nodi="A,1,4.6,-;B,1,0.8,-;C,11,0.8,-;D,11,2.6,-" condotte="A-B-C-D,0.35" acqua="-1.4,4.1 1.6,4.1 1.6,6.2 -1.4,6.2;9.4,2.2 12.4,2.2 12.4,3.6 9.4,3.6" pelo="-1.4,1.6,6.2,;9.4,12.4,3.6," linee="-1.4,6.6 -1.4,4.1 1.6,4.1 1.6,6.6;9.4,4.0 9.4,2.2 12.4,2.2 12.4,4.0" etichette="3.0,4.3,inlet;2.1,1.25,bend;9.3,1.25,bend;6.0,1.25,gate valve;8.2,2.9,outlet;5.4,0.0,D = 150 mm · L = 80 m" riferimento=0 titolo="Short pipe with an entrance, a bend, a gate valve and an exit"}

:::soluzione
The mean velocity is $v = 1.58\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the friction factor is $\lambda = 0.0194$, giving a friction loss of $\Delta H_{\mathrm{cont}} = 1.32\,\mathrm{m}$. For the fittings, $\sum \xi_i = 2.20$ and $\Delta H_{\mathrm{loc}} = 0.282\,\mathrm{m}$. The total head loss is $1.60\,\mathrm{m}$, of which $17.6\,\%$ is due to minor losses.
:::
