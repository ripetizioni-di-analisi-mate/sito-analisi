A pipe of diameter $D = 120\,\mathrm{mm}$ and length $L = 400\,\mathrm{m}$ carries oil with $\rho = 880\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and $\nu = 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2/s}$. The measured head loss between its ends is $\Delta H = 7.00\,\mathrm{m}$. Find the discharge assuming laminar flow, then check the assumption.

:::soluzione
The results are $J = 0.0175$, $Q = 8.74\,\mathrm{L/s}$, and $v = 0.773\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The Reynolds number $Re = 927 < 2000$ confirms the assumption of laminar flow.
:::
