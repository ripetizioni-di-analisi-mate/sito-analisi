Compute the force of interest equivalent to the annual rate of $3\%$, and the accumulated value of $1000$ euros after $4$ years under continuous compounding with this force of interest.

:::soluzione
$\delta = \ln 1.03 \approx 2.956\%$; $M = 1000\,e^{4\delta} = 1000 \cdot 1.03^{4} \approx 1125.51$ euros.
:::
