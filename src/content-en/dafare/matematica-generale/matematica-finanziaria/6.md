titolo: Effective rate from a nominal rate
teoria: [tasso-nominale, tassi-equivalenti]

A deposit account offers the nominal annual rate of $6\%$ compounded quarterly. Compute the effective annual rate.

:::soluzione
$i = 1{,}015^{4} - 1 \approx 6{,}136\%$.
:::
