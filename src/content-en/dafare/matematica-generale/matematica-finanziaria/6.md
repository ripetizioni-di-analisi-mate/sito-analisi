---
titolo: Effective rate from a nominal rate
teoria: [tasso-nominale, tassi-equivalenti]
parole: [APR, EAR, nominal rate, quarterly compounding]
---

A deposit account offers the nominal annual rate of $6\%$ convertible quarterly. Compute the effective annual rate.

:::soluzione
$i = 1.015^{4} - 1 \approx 6.136\%$.
:::
