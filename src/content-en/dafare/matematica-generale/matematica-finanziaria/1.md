titolo: Accumulated value under simple interest
teoria: [interesse-semplice, capitale-montante]

Compute the accumulated value of a principal $C = 2400$ euros invested for $10$ months at the annual rate of $2.5\%$ under simple interest.

:::soluzione
$M = 2400 \left( 1 + 0.025 \cdot \dfrac{10}{12} \right) = 2450$ euros.
:::
