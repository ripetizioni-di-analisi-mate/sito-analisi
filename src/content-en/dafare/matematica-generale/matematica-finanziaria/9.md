Three debts of $1000$, $3000$ and $2000$ euros are due in $3$, $7$ and $12$ months. With commercial discount, determine the due date at which the sum of the three debts can be paid in a single amount.

:::soluzione
$t^* = \dfrac{1000 \cdot 3 + 3000 \cdot 7 + 2000 \cdot 12}{6000} = 8$ months.
:::
