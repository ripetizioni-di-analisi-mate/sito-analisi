---
titolo: Duration of an investment
teoria: [tempo-e-tasso, interesse-composto]
parole: [unknown time, logarithm, duration]
---

Determine after how long a principal of $4000$ euros, invested at $2\%$ per year under compound interest, reaches the accumulated value of $5000$ euros.

:::soluzione
$t = \dfrac{\ln 1.25}{\ln 1.02} \approx 11.27$ years, that is, about $11$ years and $3$ months.
:::
