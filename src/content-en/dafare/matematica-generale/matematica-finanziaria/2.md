titolo: Accumulated value under compound interest
teoria: [interesse-composto, capitale-montante]
parole: [accumulated value, compound interest, interest]

Compute the accumulated value and the interest of a principal $C = 3000$ euros invested for $6$ years at the annual rate of $4.5\%$ under compound interest.

:::soluzione
$M = 3000 \cdot 1.045^{6} \approx 3906.78$ euros, $I \approx 906.78$ euros.
:::
