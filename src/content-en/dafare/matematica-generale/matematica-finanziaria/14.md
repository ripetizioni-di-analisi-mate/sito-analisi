An investment requires $1000$ euros today and returns $600$ euros in one year and $720$ euros in two years. Compute the NPV at the rate of $10\%$ and the IRR.

:::soluzione
$\operatorname{NPV}(0.1) \approx 140.50$ euros; the IRR is $i^* = 20\%$ (from $v = (1 + i^*)^{-1} = \dfrac{5}{6}$).
:::
