A loan of $24\,000$ euros is repaid by Italian amortization in $6$ annual installments at the rate of $5\%$. Compute the principal portion, the interest portion and the installment of the fourth year, and the outstanding balance after the payment of the fourth installment.

:::soluzione
$Q_4 = 4000$ euros, $I_4 = 0.05 \cdot 12\,000 = 600$ euros, $R_4 = 4600$ euros, $S_4 = 8000$ euros.
:::
