A bill of exchange of $2000$ euros matures in $4$ months. Compute the present value and the discount with commercial discount at the annual rate $d = 4.5\%$.

:::soluzione
$V = 2000 \left( 1 - 0.045 \cdot \dfrac{4}{12} \right) = 1970$ euros, discount $D = 30$ euros.
:::
