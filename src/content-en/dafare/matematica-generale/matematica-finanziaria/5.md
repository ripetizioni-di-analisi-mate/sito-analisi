Compute the monthly rate equivalent to the annual rate of $7\%$ under compound interest, and compare it with the proportional monthly rate $\dfrac{7\%}{12}$.

:::soluzione
$i_{12} = 1.07^{\frac{1}{12}} - 1 \approx 0.5654\%$, less than $\dfrac{7\%}{12} \approx 0.5833\%$.
:::
