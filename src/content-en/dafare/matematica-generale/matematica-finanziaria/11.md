A mortgage of $60\,000$ euros is repaid by French amortization in $15$ annual installments in arrears at the rate of $4\%$. Compute the installment and the interest portion of the first installment.

:::soluzione
$R = \dfrac{60\,000}{a_{\overline{15}|0.04}} \approx 5396.47$ euros; $I_1 = 2400$ euros.
:::
