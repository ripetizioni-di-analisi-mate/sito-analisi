titolo: Present value of a future amount
teoria: [valore-attuale, interesse-composto]

---

Compute the present value of $10\,000$ euros available in $7$ years, at the annual rate of $3\%$ under compound interest.

:::soluzione
$V = 10\,000 \cdot 1.03^{-7} \approx 8130.92$ euros.
:::
