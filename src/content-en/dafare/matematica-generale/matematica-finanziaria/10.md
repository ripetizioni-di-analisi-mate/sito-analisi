Compute the present value of a nondeferred ordinary annuity with $15$ annual payments of $1200$ euros at the rate of $3{,}5\%$.

:::soluzione
$V = 1200\,a_{\overline{15}|0{,}035} \approx 1200 \cdot 11{,}517411 \approx 13\,820{,}89$ euros.
:::
