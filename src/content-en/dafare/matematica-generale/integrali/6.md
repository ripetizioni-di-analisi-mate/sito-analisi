---
titolo: Rational function with two real roots in the denominator
teoria: [razionali-secondo-grado]
parole: [partial fractions, rational functions, decomposition]
---

Compute $\displaystyle\int \dfrac{x + 7}{x^2 + 2x - 3}\,dx$.

:::soluzione
$2 \ln \lvert x - 1 \rvert - \ln \lvert x + 3 \rvert + c$.
:::
