---
titolo: Integral function and its relative extrema
teoria: [funzione-integrale, teorema-fondamentale]
parole: [integral function, fundamental theorem, maximum, minimum]
---

Let $F(x) = \displaystyle\int_0^x \left( 4 - t^2 \right) dt$. Find $F'(x)$ and the relative maximum and minimum points of $F$, and compute $F(2)$.

:::soluzione
$F'(x) = 4 - x^2$; $x = 2$ is a relative maximum point, $x = -2$ a relative minimum point; $F(2) = \dfrac{16}{3}$.
:::
