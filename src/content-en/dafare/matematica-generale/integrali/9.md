Let $F(x) = \displaystyle\int_0^x \left( 4 - t^2 \right) dt$. Find $F'(x)$ and locate the local maxima and minima of $F$. Compute $F(2)$.

:::soluzione
$F'(x) = 4 - x^2$. There is a local maximum at $x = 2$ and a local minimum at $x = -2$. $F(2) = \dfrac{16}{3}$.
:::
