---
titolo: Improper integral with an exponential
teoria: [integrale-improprio, impropri-esponenziale, integrazione-per-parti]
parole: [improper integral, exponential, by parts, convergence]
---

Compute $\displaystyle\int_0^{+\infty} x\,e^{-2x}\,dx$.

:::soluzione
The integral converges and equals $\dfrac{1}{4}$.
:::
