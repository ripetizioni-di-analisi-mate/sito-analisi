The inverse demand of a good is $p_d(q) = 52 - q^2$ and the inverse supply is $p_s(q) = 12 + 6q$, with $q \geq 0$. Find the equilibrium and the consumer and producer surpluses.

:::soluzione
$q^* = 4$, $p^* = 36$ euros; $S_C = \dfrac{128}{3}$ euros and $S_P = 48$ euros.
:::
