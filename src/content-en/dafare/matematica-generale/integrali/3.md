---
titolo: Integral by substitution with a root
teoria: [integrazione-per-sostituzione]
parole: [substitution, root, change of variable]
---

Compute $\displaystyle\int x \sqrt{2x + 1}\,dx$ with the substitution $t = 2x + 1$.

:::soluzione
$\dfrac{(2x + 1)^{\frac{5}{2}}}{10} - \dfrac{(2x + 1)^{\frac{3}{2}}}{6} + c$.
:::
