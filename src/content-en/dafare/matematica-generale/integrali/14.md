An investment generates a continuous cash flow at a constant rate of $5000$ euros per year. At a force of interest $\delta = 0{,}05$, calculate its present value when it lasts $4$ years and when it continues indefinitely.

:::soluzione
For $4$ years, $V = 100\,000 \left( 1 - e^{-0{,}2} \right) \approx 18\,126{,}92$ euros. For a perpetual flow, $V = 100\,000$ euros.
:::
