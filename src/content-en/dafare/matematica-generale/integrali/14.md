An investment produces a continuous cash flow of $5000$ euros per year. With instantaneous intensity of interest $\delta = 0.05$, compute the present value of the flow if it lasts $4$ years and if it has no end.

:::soluzione
For $4$ years $V = 100\,000 \left( 1 - e^{-0.2} \right) \approx 18\,126.92$ euros; with no end $V = 100\,000$ euros.
:::
