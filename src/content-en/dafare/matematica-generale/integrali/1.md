---
titolo: Indefinite integral of a sum of powers
teoria: [integrale-indefinito, integrali-immediati]
parole: [indefinite integral, powers, root, logarithm]
---

Compute $\displaystyle\int \left( 4x^3 - 6\sqrt{x} + \dfrac{2}{x} \right) dx$ for $x > 0$.

:::soluzione
$x^4 - 4\,x^{\frac{3}{2}} + 2 \ln x + c$.
:::
