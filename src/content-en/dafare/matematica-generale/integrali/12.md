A firm has marginal cost $C'(q) = 3q^2 - 24q + 60$ and fixed costs of $200$ euros. Find the total cost $C(q)$ and the additional cost of increasing output from $4$ to $10$ units.

:::soluzione
$C(q) = q^3 - 12q^2 + 60q + 200$; the additional cost is $288$ euros.
:::
