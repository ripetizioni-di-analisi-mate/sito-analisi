Let $f(x, y) = xy^2$ and the curve $x(t) = t^2$, $y(t) = 3 - t$. Compute $g'(1)$, where $g(t) = f(x(t), y(t))$.

:::soluzione
$g'(t) = 2t\,(3 - t)^2 - 2t^2 (3 - t)$, so $g'(1) = 8 - 4 = 4$.
:::
