---
titolo: Approximate change in production
teoria: [differenziale, produttivita-marginali, cobb-douglas]
parole: [differential, linear approximation, marginal products, Cobb-Douglas]
---

A firm has a production function $Q(K, L) = 10\,K^{\frac{1}{2}} L^{\frac{1}{2}}$ and uses $K = 100$ units of capital and $L = 400$ units of labor. Use the differential to estimate output when capital rises to $102$ and labor falls to $396$.

:::soluzione
$Q(100, 400) = 2000$, $Q_K = 10$, $Q_L = \dfrac{5}{2}$, so $dQ = 20 - 10 = 10$ and $Q(102, 396) \approx 2010$ (the value rounded to two decimal places is $2009{,}78$).
:::
