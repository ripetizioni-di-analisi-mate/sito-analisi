titolo: Approximate change in production
teoria: [differenziale, produttivita-marginali, cobb-douglas]

A firm has production function $Q(K, L) = 10\,K^{\frac{1}{2}} L^{\frac{1}{2}}$ and employs $K = 100$ and $L = 400$. Using the differential, estimate the production when capital rises to $102$ and labor falls to $396$.

:::soluzione
$Q(100, 400) = 2000$, $Q_K = 10$, $Q_L = \dfrac{5}{2}$, so $dQ = 20 - 10 = 10$ and $Q(102, 396) \approx 2010$ (exact value $2009.78$).
:::
