Find the natural domain of $f(x, y) = \ln(x^2 + y^2 - 9)$ and state whether it is an open, closed, bounded set.

:::soluzione
$D = \{ (x, y) : x^2 + y^2 > 9 \}$, the points exterior to the circle with center at the origin and radius $3$; it is open and not bounded.
:::
