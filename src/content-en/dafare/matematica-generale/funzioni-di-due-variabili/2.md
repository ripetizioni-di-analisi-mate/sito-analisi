Find the natural domain of $f(x, y) = \ln(x^2 + y^2 - 9)$ and determine whether it is open, whether it is closed, and whether it is bounded.

:::soluzione
$D = \{ (x, y) : x^2 + y^2 > 9 \}$, the points outside the circle of radius $3$ centered at the origin. The set is open and unbounded.
:::
