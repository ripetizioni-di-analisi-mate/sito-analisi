Compute the second derivatives of $f(x, y) = x^2 e^{y}$, verify that the mixed derivatives coincide and write the Hessian matrix at the point $(1, 0)$.

:::soluzione
$f_{xx} = 2e^{y}$, $f_{xy} = f_{yx} = 2x\,e^{y}$, $f_{yy} = x^2 e^{y}$; $H(1, 0) = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 2 \\ 2 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$.
:::
