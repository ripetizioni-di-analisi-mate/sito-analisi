---
titolo: Marginal rate of substitution of a utility function
teoria: [saggio-marginale-sostituzione, isoquanti-indifferenza]
parole: [utility, indifference curve, marginal rate of substitution]
---

A consumer has utility function $U(x, y) = x y^2$, with $x > 0$ and $y > 0$. Write the indifference curve through the bundle $(4, 2)$ and compute the marginal rate of substitution $\dfrac{U_x}{U_y}$ at that point.

:::soluzione
The indifference curve is $x y^2 = 16$, that is, $y = \dfrac{4}{\sqrt{x}}$; the marginal rate of substitution at $(4, 2)$ equals $\dfrac{1}{4}$.
:::
