Find the natural domain of $f(x, y) = \dfrac{\sqrt{4 - x^2 - y^2}}{x - y}$ and state whether it is an open, closed, bounded set.

:::soluzione
$D = \{ (x, y) : x^2 + y^2 \leq 4, \ y \neq x \}$, the circular region of radius $2$ with the diameter on the line $y = x$ removed; it is bounded and neither open nor closed.
:::
