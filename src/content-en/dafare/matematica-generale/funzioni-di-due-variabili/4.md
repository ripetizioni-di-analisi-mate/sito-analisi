---
titolo: Level curves of a polynomial function
teoria: [curve-di-livello]
parole: [level curves, parabolas, level through a point]
---

Find the level curves of $f(x, y) = x^2 - y$ and the level curve that passes through the point $(2, 1)$.

:::soluzione
The level curves are the parabolas $y = x^2 - k$, with $k \in \mathbb{R}$; the one through $(2, 1)$ has level $k = 3$ and is $y = x^2 - 3$.
:::
