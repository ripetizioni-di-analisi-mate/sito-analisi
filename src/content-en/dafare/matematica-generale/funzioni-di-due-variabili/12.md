Given the production function $Q(K, L) = 5\,K^{\frac{2}{5}} L^{\frac{3}{5}}$, compute output and the marginal products for $K = 32$ and $L = 32$.

:::soluzione
$Q(32, 32) = 160$, $Q_K(32, 32) = 2$, $Q_L(32, 32) = 3$.
:::
