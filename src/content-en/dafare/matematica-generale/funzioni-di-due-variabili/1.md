Find the natural domain of $f(x, y) = \sqrt{x - y^2}$ and state whether it is an open, closed, bounded set.

:::soluzione
$D = \{ (x, y) : x \geq y^2 \}$, the region to the right of the parabola $x = y^2$, parabola included; it is closed and not bounded.
:::
