Find the natural domain of $f(x, y) = \sqrt{x - y^2}$ and determine whether it is open, whether it is closed, and whether it is bounded.

:::soluzione
$D = \{ (x, y) : x \geq y^2 \}$, the region to the right of the parabola $x = y^2$, including the parabola. The set is closed and unbounded.
:::
