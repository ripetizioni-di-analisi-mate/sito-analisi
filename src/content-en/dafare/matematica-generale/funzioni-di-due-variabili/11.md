---
titolo: A limit that does not exist
teoria: [limiti-continuita]
parole: [limit in two variables, lines through the origin, limit does not exist]
---

Prove that the limit $\displaystyle\lim_{(x, y) \to (0, 0)} \frac{x^2 - y^2}{x^2 + y^2}$ does not exist.

:::soluzione
On the line $y = mx$, with $x \neq 0$, the function equals $\dfrac{1 - m^2}{1 + m^2}$, which depends on $m$ (it equals $1$ on the $x$-axis and $0$ on the line $y = x$); hence the limit does not exist.
:::
