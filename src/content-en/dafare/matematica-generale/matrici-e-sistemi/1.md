Given the matrices
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -2 & 0 \\ 3 & 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix},
\qquad
B = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 0 & -1 \\ 1 & 3 \end{pmatrix},
$$
compute the products $AB$ and $BA$.

:::soluzione
$AB = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 3 \\ 8 & 8 \end{pmatrix}$, $\quad BA = \begin{pmatrix} 5 & -3 & 2 \\ -3 & -1 & -2 \\ 10 & 1 & 6 \end{pmatrix}$.
:::
