---
titolo: Determinant of order 4
teoria: [sviluppo-di-laplace, complemento-algebrico, proprieta-del-determinante]
parole: [determinant, Laplace expansion, order four]
---

Compute the determinant
$$
\begin{vmatrix} 2 & 1 & 0 & 3 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 3 & 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 2 & 0 & 1 \end{vmatrix} .
$$

:::soluzione
The determinant equals $4$.
:::
