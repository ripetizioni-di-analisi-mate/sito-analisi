---
titolo: Inverse of a matrix of order 3
teoria: [esistenza-inversa, complemento-algebrico]
parole: [inverse matrix, cofactors, order three]
---

Compute the inverse of the matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 2 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix} .
$$

:::soluzione
$\det A = -1$ and $A^{-1} = \begin{pmatrix} -1 & -2 & 2 \\ -1 & 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & -1 \end{pmatrix}$.
:::
