titolo: System of order 3 with Cramer
teoria: [regola-di-cramer, regola-di-sarrus]
parole: [Cramer's rule, linear system, three unknowns]
---

Solve with Cramer's rule the system
$$
\begin{cases}
2x + y - z = 3 \\[4pt]
x - y + 2z = 1 \\[4pt]
3x + 2y + z = 8
\end{cases}
$$

:::soluzione
$\det A = -10$ and the solution is $(x, y, z) = (1, 2, 1)$.
:::
