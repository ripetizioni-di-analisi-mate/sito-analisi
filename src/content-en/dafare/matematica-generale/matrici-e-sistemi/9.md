Use Cramer’s rule to solve the system
$$
\begin{cases}
2x + y - z = 3 \\[4pt]
x - y + 2z = 1 \\[4pt]
3x + 2y + z = 8
\end{cases}
$$

:::soluzione
$\det A = -10$ and the solution is $(x, y, z) = (1, 2, 1)$.
:::
