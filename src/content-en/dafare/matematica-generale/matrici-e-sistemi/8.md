---
titolo: Rank as a parameter varies
teoria: [rango, proprieta-del-determinante]
parole: [rank, parameter, minors]
---

Find the rank of the matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 2 \\ 2 & k & 4 \\ 3 & 3 & k + 4 \end{pmatrix}
$$
as the real parameter $k$ varies.

:::soluzione
$\det A = (k - 2)^{2}$; therefore $\operatorname{rk} A = 3$ for $k \neq 2$ and $\operatorname{rk} A = 1$ for $k = 2$, because in that case the three rows are proportional.
:::
