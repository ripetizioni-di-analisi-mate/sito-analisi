---
titolo: Inverse of a matrix of order 2
teoria: [esistenza-inversa, matrice-inversa]
parole: [inverse matrix, determinant, order two]
---

Compute the inverse of the matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 4 & 3 \\ 3 & 2 \end{pmatrix} .
$$

:::soluzione
$A^{-1} = \begin{pmatrix} -2 & 3 \\ 3 & -4 \end{pmatrix}$.
:::
