Discuss with the Rouché-Capelli theorem and solve with Gauss's method the system
$$
\begin{cases}
x - y + 2z = 1 \\[4pt]
2x + y + z = 5 \\[4pt]
3x + 3z = 6
\end{cases}
$$

:::soluzione
$\operatorname{rk} A = \operatorname{rk}(A \mid b) = 2$, so there are $\infty^{1}$ solutions, given by $(x, y, z) = (2 - t,\ 1 + t,\ t)$ with $t \in \mathbb{R}$.
:::
