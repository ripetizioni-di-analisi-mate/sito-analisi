---
titolo: Invertibility as a parameter varies
teoria: [esistenza-inversa, regola-di-sarrus]
parole: [invertible matrix, parameter, determinant]
---

Find for which values of the real parameter $k$ the matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} k & 2 & 1 \\ 2 & k & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}
$$
is invertible.

:::soluzione
$\det A = k\,(k - 2)$, so $A$ is invertible for $k \neq 0$ and $k \neq 2$.
:::
