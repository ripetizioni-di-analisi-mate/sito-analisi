---
titolo: Total expenditure by matrix multiplication
teoria: [prodotto-righe-per-colonne, matrice]
parole: [row-by-column multiplication, expenditure, prices, quantities]
---

Two customers buy three goods. The first buys $2$ units of the first, $1$ of the second, and $3$ of the third. The second buys $0$, $4$, and $1$ units. The unit prices are $5$ euros, $3$ euros, and $2$ euros. Write the quantity matrix $Q$ with one row per customer and the price column vector $p$. Use $Qp$ to find each customer’s total expenditure.

:::soluzione
$Qp = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 & 3 \\ 0 & 4 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} 5 \\ 3 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 19 \\ 14 \end{pmatrix}$, that is, $19$ euros for the first customer and $14$ euros for the second.
:::
