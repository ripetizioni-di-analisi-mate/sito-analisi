---
titolo: Total expenditure with the matrix product
teoria: [prodotto-righe-per-colonne, matrice]
parole: [row-by-column product, expenditure, prices, quantities]
---

Two customers buy three goods. The first buys $2$ units of the first good, $1$ of the second and $3$ of the third; the second buys $0$, $4$ and $1$ units. The unit prices are $5$ euros, $3$ euros and $2$ euros. Write the matrix $Q$ of the quantities (one row per customer) and the column vector $p$ of the prices, and compute with the product $Qp$ the expenditure of each customer.

:::soluzione
$Qp = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 & 3 \\ 0 & 4 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} 5 \\ 3 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 19 \\ 14 \end{pmatrix}$, that is, $19$ euros for the first customer and $14$ euros for the second.
:::
