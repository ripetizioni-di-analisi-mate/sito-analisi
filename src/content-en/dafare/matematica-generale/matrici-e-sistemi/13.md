An economy with two sectors has technology matrix and final demand
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 0.3 & 0.2 \\ 0.1 & 0.4 \end{pmatrix},
\qquad
d = \begin{pmatrix} 20 \\ 40 \end{pmatrix} .
$$
Find the production levels of the two sectors that meet the final demand.

:::soluzione
$\det(I_2 - A) = 0.4$ and $x = (I_2 - A)^{-1} d = \begin{pmatrix} 50 \\ 75 \end{pmatrix}$.
:::
