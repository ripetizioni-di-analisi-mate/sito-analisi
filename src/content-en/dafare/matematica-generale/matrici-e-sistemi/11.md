---
titolo: Classifying a system of order 3 with a parameter
teoria: [discussione-con-parametro, regola-di-cramer, rouche-capelli]
parole: [system with a parameter, classification, Cramer’s rule, Rouché–Capelli theorem]
---

Classify the system as the real parameter $k$ varies
$$
\begin{cases}
x + y - z = 2 \\[4pt]
x + k y + z = 1 \\[4pt]
k x + y + z = 1
\end{cases}
$$
and solve it when it is consistent.

:::soluzione
$\det A = (k - 1)(k + 3)$. For $k \neq 1$ and $k \neq -3$ the unique solution is $\left( \dfrac{3}{k + 3},\ \dfrac{3}{k + 3},\ -\dfrac{2k}{k + 3} \right)$. For $k = 1$ there are infinitely many solutions, given by $\left( \dfrac{3}{2} - t,\ t,\ -\dfrac{1}{2} \right)$ with $t \in \mathbb{R}$. For $k = -3$ the system is inconsistent.
:::
