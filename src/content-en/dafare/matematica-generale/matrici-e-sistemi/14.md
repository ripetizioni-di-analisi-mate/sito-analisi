---
titolo: Production plan with three departments
teoria: [regola-di-cramer, sistema-lineare]
parole: [production plan, linear system, Cramer, departments]
---

A firm produces three items. One unit of the first requires $2$ hours in department A, $1$ in department B and $1$ in department C; one unit of the second requires $1$, $3$ and $1$ hours; one unit of the third requires $1$, $2$ and $3$ hours. Departments A, B and C have $80$, $130$ and $80$ hours available. Find the quantities of the three items that use up all the available hours.

:::soluzione
The system has determinant $11$ and solution $(20, 30, 10)$, that is, $20$ units of the first item, $30$ of the second and $10$ of the third.
:::
