A firm spends $2600$ euros to produce $80$ pieces and $3400$ euros to produce $120$. Assuming the cost is linear, find the fixed cost and the variable unit cost.

:::soluzione
$C(q) = 1000 + 20q$: fixed cost $1000$ euros, variable unit cost $20$ euros.
:::
