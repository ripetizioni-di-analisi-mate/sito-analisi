---
titolo: Composite functions and their domains
teoria: [funzione-composta, dominio-naturale]
parole: [composite function, domain of the composition, root]
---

Let $f(x) = x^2 + 1$ and $g(x) = \sqrt{x - 2}$. Write $g \circ f$ and $f \circ g$ and find their domains.

:::soluzione
$(g \circ f)(x) = \sqrt{x^2 - 1}$ has domain $(-\infty, -1] \cup [1, +\infty)$. The composition $(f \circ g)(x) = x - 1$ has domain $[2, +\infty)$.
:::
