A firm has cost $C(q) = 500 + 20q$ and inverse demand $p = 100 - q$. Find the quantity that maximizes profit, the maximum profit and the break-even points.

:::soluzione
$q = 40$ with maximum profit $1100$ euros; break-even points $q = 40 \pm 10\sqrt{11}$, about $6.83$ and $73.17$.
:::
