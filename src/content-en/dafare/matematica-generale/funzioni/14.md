---
titolo: Doubling time of an investment
teoria: [esponenziale, logaritmo]
parole: [compound interest, doubling time, logarithm]
---

An initial investment of $3000$ euros earns $4\%$ per year with compound interest. Compute its value after $5$ years and the time needed for it to double.

:::soluzione
After $5$ years it is worth $3000 \cdot 1{,}04^{5} \approx 3649{,}96$ euros. It doubles after $\dfrac{\ln 2}{\ln 1{,}04} \approx 17{,}7$ years.
:::
