Find the asymptotes and the relative extrema of $f(x) = \dfrac{x^2 + 9}{x}$.

:::soluzione
Vertical asymptote $x = 0$, oblique asymptote $y = x$; relative maximum $(-3, -6)$ and relative minimum $(3, 6)$.
:::
