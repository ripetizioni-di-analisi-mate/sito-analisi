Find the intervals of monotonicity and the extrema of $f(x) = x\,e^{-x}$.

:::soluzione
$f$ increases on $(-\infty, 1]$ and decreases on $[1, +\infty)$; the absolute maximum is $f(1) = \dfrac{1}{e}$, and there is no minimum.
:::
