---
titolo: Enclosure along a wall
teoria: [metodo-ottimizzazione, unico-punto-stazionario]
parole: [optimization, minimum perimeter, enclosure, constraint]
---

A rectangular enclosure of area $50\,\mathrm{m^2}$ has one side against a wall and the other three sides made of fencing. Find the dimensions that minimize the length of the fencing.

:::soluzione
The two sides perpendicular to the wall measure $5\,\mathrm{m}$, the parallel side $10\,\mathrm{m}$; the minimum fencing is $20\,\mathrm{m}$ long.
:::
