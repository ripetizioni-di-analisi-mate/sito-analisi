Find the intervals of convexity and concavity and the inflection point of $f(x) = x^3 - 6x^2 + 2$.

:::soluzione
$f$ is concave on $(-\infty, 2]$ and convex on $[2, +\infty)$; the inflection point is $(2, -14)$.
:::
