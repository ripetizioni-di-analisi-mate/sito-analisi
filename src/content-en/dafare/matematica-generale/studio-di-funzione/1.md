Find the relative maximum and minimum points of $f(x) = x^3 - 27x + 4$ and the corresponding values.

:::soluzione
Relative maximum at $x = -3$, with $f(-3) = 58$; relative minimum at $x = 3$, with $f(3) = -50$.
:::
