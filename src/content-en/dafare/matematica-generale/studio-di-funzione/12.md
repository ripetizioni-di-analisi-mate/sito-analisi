A firm in perfect competition sells at the price $p = 29$ euros and has cost $C(q) = \dfrac{q^3}{3} - 4q^2 + 20q + 30$. Find the quantity that maximizes profit and the maximum profit.

:::soluzione
$q = 9$, with maximum profit $\pi(9) = 132$ euros.
:::
