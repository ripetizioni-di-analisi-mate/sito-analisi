---
titolo: Maximum profit with a capacity constraint
teoria: [estremi-intervallo-chiuso, profitto-massimo]
parole: [maximum profit, production capacity, closed interval]
---

The profit of a firm is $\pi(q) = -q^3 + 9q^2 - 15q - 10$, and the production capacity allows $0 \leq q \leq 4$. Find the maximum profit and the minimum profit on the admissible interval.

:::soluzione
The maximum profit is $10$ euros, with $q = 4$; the minimum profit is $-17$ euros, with $q = 1$.
:::
