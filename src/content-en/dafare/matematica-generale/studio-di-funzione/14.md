The demand for a good is $q(p) = 108 - p^2$, with $0 \leq p \leq \sqrt{108}$. Find the price that maximizes revenue, the maximum revenue and the elasticity of demand at that price.

:::soluzione
$p = 6$ euros, with $q = 72$ and maximum revenue $432$ euros; the elasticity equals $-1$.
:::
