The production cost is $C(q) = 2q^2 + 8q + 200$, with $q > 0$. Find the quantity that minimizes average cost and the minimum average cost.

:::soluzione
$q = 10$, with minimum average cost $48$ euros, equal to $C'(10)$.
:::
