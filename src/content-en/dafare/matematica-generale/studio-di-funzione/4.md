Find the inflection points of $f(x) = e^{-x^2}$.

:::soluzione
The inflections are the points $\left( \pm\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}, \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{e}} \right)$; $f$ is concave on $\left[ -\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}, \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \right]$ and convex outside.
:::
