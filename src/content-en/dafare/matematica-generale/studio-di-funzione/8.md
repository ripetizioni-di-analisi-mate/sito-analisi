---
titolo: Absolute extrema of a polynomial on a closed interval
teoria: [estremi-intervallo-chiuso, massimi-minimi-assoluti]
parole: [absolute maximum, absolute minimum, closed interval, Weierstrass]
---

Find the absolute maximum and minimum of $f(x) = x^3 - 3x^2$ on the interval $[-1, 4]$.

:::soluzione
The absolute maximum is $16$, at $x = 4$; the absolute minimum is $-4$, attained at $x = -1$ and at $x = 2$.
:::
