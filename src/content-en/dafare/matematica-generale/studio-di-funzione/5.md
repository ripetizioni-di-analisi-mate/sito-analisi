titolo: Stationary points with the second derivative test
teoria: [criterio-derivata-seconda, criterio-derivata-prima]

---

Classify the stationary points of $f(x) = x^4 - 4x^3$, using the second derivative test where possible.

:::soluzione
At $x = 3$ one has $f''(3) = 36 > 0$, so it is a relative (and absolute) minimum, with $f(3) = -27$; at $x = 0$ one has $f''(0) = 0$, and the sign of $f'$ shows that it is a horizontal inflection.
:::
