Study the function $f(x) = \dfrac{\ln x}{x^2}$: asymptotes, extrema and inflections.

:::soluzione
Asymptotes $x = 0$ and $y = 0$; absolute maximum at $x = \sqrt{e}$, with value $\dfrac{1}{2e}$; inflection at $x = e^{\frac{5}{6}}$.
:::
