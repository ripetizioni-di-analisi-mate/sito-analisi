Among the cylinders of volume $54\pi\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ find the one of minimum total surface area.

:::soluzione
Radius $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ and height $6\,\mathrm{cm}$; the minimum surface area is $54\pi\,\mathrm{cm^2}$.
:::
