titolo: Right-hand and left-hand limits
teoria: [limite-destro-sinistro, limiti-infiniti]

---

Compute the right-hand and left-hand limits of $f(x) = \dfrac{2x}{3 - x}$ as $x \to 3$.

:::soluzione
The right-hand limit is $-\infty$ and the left-hand limit is $+\infty$.
:::
