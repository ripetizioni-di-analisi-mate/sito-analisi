Prove that the equation $x^3 - 3x + 1 = 0$ has a solution in the interval $(0, 1)$.

:::soluzione
The function $x^3 - 3x + 1$ is continuous, equals $1$ at $0$ and $-1$ at $1$. By Bolzano's theorem, it vanishes in $(0, 1)$ at a point close to $0{,}347$.
:::
