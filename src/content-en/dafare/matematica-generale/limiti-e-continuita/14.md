---
titolo: Existence of a solution
teoria: [teorema-degli-zeri]
parole: [intermediate zero theorem, existence of solutions, cubic equation]
---

Prove that the equation $x^3 - 3x + 1 = 0$ has a solution in the interval $(0, 1)$.

:::soluzione
The function $x^3 - 3x + 1$ is continuous, equals $1$ at $0$ and $-1$ at $1$; by the intermediate zero theorem it vanishes in $(0, 1)$, at a point close to $0.347$.
:::
