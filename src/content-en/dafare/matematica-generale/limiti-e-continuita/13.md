---
titolo: Continuity with a parameter
teoria: [continuita, limite-destro-sinistro]
parole: [continuity, parameter, piecewise-defined function]
---

Find $a$ so that the function is continuous on $\mathbb{R}$ when it equals $a x^2$ for $x \leq 1$ and $2x + 3$ for $x > 1$.

:::soluzione
$a = 5$.
:::
