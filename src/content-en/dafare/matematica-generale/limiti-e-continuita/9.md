---
titolo: Comparing exponential and power functions
teoria: [gerarchia-infiniti]
parole: [hierarchy of growth rates, exponential, power]
---

Compute $\displaystyle\lim_{x \to +\infty} \frac{2^{x} + x^{10}}{3 \cdot 2^{x}}$.

:::soluzione
The limit is $\dfrac{1}{3}$, because $\dfrac{x^{10}}{2^{x}} \to 0$.
:::
