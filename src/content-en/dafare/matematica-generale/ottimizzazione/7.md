titolo: Absolute extrema on a square
teoria: [metodo-insiemi-compatti, punto-stazionario]

---

Find the absolute maximum and minimum of $f(x, y) = x^2 + y^2 - 2x - 2y$ on the square $[0, 3] \times [0, 3]$.

:::soluzione
The absolute minimum is $-2$, at the point $(1, 1)$; the absolute maximum is $6$, at the vertex $(3, 3)$.
:::
