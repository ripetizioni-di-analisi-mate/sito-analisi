Find the absolute maximum and minimum of $f(x, y) = x^2 - y^2$ on the disk $x^2 + y^2 \leq 4$.

:::soluzione
The absolute maximum is $4$, at the points $(\pm 2, 0)$; the absolute minimum is $-4$, at the points $(0, \pm 2)$.
:::
