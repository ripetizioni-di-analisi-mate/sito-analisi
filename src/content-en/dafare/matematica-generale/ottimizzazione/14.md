A firm has the production function $Q = \sqrt{LK}$ and pays $9$ euros per unit of labor and $4$ euros per unit of capital. Find the combination of factors that produces $Q = 6$ at minimum cost and the minimum cost.

:::soluzione
The minimum-cost combination is $L = 4$, $K = 9$, with a minimum cost of $72$ euros.
:::
