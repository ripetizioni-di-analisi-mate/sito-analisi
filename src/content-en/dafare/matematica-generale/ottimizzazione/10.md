Find the minimum of $f(x, y) = x^2 + y^2$ on the line $2x + y = 5$ and state whether $f$ has a maximum on the line.

:::soluzione
The minimum is $5$, at the point $(2, 1)$; on the line $f$ has no maximum.
:::
