Find the maximum and minimum of $f(x, y) = x + y$ on the ellipse $x^2 + 4y^2 = 20$.

:::soluzione
The maximum is $5$, at the point $(4, 1)$; the minimum is $-5$, at the point $(-4, -1)$.
:::
