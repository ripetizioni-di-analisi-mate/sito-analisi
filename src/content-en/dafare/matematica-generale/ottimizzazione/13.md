A consumer has utility $U(x, y) = x y^2$ and an income of $60$ euros, with prices $p_1 = 2$ and $p_2 = 4$ euros. Find the optimal bundle, with $x, y > 0$, and the maximum utility.

:::soluzione
The optimal bundle is $(10, 10)$, with maximum utility $1000$.
:::
