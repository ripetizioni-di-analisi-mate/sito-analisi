titolo: Stationary point of a second-degree polynomial

Find and classify the stationary points of $f(x, y) = x^2 + y^2 - 4x + 6y$.

:::soluzione
The only stationary point is $(2, -3)$, and it is a minimum point, with $f(2, -3) = -13$.
:::
