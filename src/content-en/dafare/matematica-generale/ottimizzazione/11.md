A monopolist sells two goods with inverse demands $p_1 = 40 - q_1$ and $p_2 = 60 - 2q_2$ and has cost $C(q_1, q_2) = q_1^2 + q_2^2 + 100$. Find the quantities, the prices and the maximum profit.

:::soluzione
Profit is maximum for $q_1 = 10$ and $q_2 = 10$, at prices $p_1 = 30$ and $p_2 = 40$ euros, and equals $400$ euros.
:::
