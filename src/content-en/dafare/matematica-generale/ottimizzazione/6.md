---
titolo: A case with zero Hessian determinant
teoria: [casi-indecisi, test-hessiana]
parole: [inconclusive case, zero determinant, sign analysis, minimum]
---

Verify that the origin is the only stationary point of $f(x, y) = (x - y)^2 + y^4$, then show that the Hessian test is inconclusive there and classify the point.

:::soluzione
We have $\det H(0, 0) = 0$; since $f(x, y) \geq 0 = f(0, 0)$ with equality only at the origin, the origin is an absolute minimum point.
:::
