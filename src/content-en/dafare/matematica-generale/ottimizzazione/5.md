---
titolo: Stationary point of an exponential function
teoria: [punto-stazionario, test-hessiana]
parole: [exponential, stationary point, relative minimum]
---

Find and classify the stationary points of $f(x, y) = e^{x}\,(x + y^2)$.

:::soluzione
The only stationary point is $(-1, 0)$, and it is a relative minimum point, with $f(-1, 0) = -\dfrac{1}{e}$.
:::
