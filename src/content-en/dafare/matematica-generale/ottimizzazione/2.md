titolo: Classification with the Hessian of a polynomial with a mixed term
teoria: [punto-stazionario, test-hessiana]
parole: [stationary point, mixed term, Hessian matrix, relative minimum]
---

Find and classify the stationary points of $f(x, y) = 2x^2 - 2xy + y^2 - 2x$.

:::soluzione
The only stationary point is $(1, 1)$, with $\det H = 4 > 0$ and $f_{xx} = 4 > 0$; it is a minimum point, with $f(1, 1) = -1$.
:::
