---
titolo: Stationary points of a third-degree polynomial in two variables
teoria: [punto-stazionario, test-hessiana, punto-di-sella]
parole: [stationary points, third-degree polynomial, saddle, relative minimum]
---

Find and classify the stationary points of $f(x, y) = x^3 + 3xy - y^3$.

:::soluzione
The origin is a saddle point; the point $(1, -1)$ is a relative minimum point, with $f(1, -1) = -1$.
:::
