---
titolo: Maximum profit in perfect competition with two goods
teoria: [profitto-due-beni, test-hessiana]
parole: [perfect competition, two goods, maximum profit, marginal cost]
---

A firm in perfect competition sells two goods at prices of $32$ and $22$ euros, with cost $C(q_1, q_2) = q_1^2 + q_1 q_2 + q_2^2$. Find the quantities that maximize profit and the maximum profit.

:::soluzione
Profit is maximum for $q_1 = 14$ and $q_2 = 4$, and equals $268$ euros.
:::
