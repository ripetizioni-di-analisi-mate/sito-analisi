---
titolo: Repeating decimal as a fraction
teoria: [serie-geometrica]
parole: [repeating decimal, fraction, geometric series]
---

Use a geometric series to write the repeating decimal $0{,}\overline{45} = 0{,}454545\ldots$ as a fraction.

:::soluzione
$0{,}\overline{45} = \dfrac{45}{99} = \dfrac{5}{11}$.
:::
