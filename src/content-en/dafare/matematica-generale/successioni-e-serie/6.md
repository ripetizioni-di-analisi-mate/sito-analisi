---
titolo: Monotonicity and limit of a sequence
teoria: [successione-monotona, successioni-monotone, regole-limiti-successioni]
parole: [increasing sequence, limit, quotient]
---

Show that the sequence $a_n = \dfrac{3n - 1}{n + 2}$ is increasing and compute its limit.

:::soluzione
$a_{n+1} - a_n = \dfrac{7}{(n + 2)(n + 3)} > 0$, so it is increasing; the limit is $3$.
:::
