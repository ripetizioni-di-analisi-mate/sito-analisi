titolo: Telescoping series
teoria: [serie, condizione-necessaria]
parole: [telescoping series, partial sums]

Compute the sum of the series $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{(k + 1)(k + 2)}$, writing the general term as $\dfrac{1}{k + 1} - \dfrac{1}{k + 2}$.

:::soluzione
$S_n = \dfrac{1}{2} - \dfrac{1}{n + 2}$, so the sum is $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
:::
