A deposit of $300$ euros is made at the end of each year for $10$ years into an account earning $2\%$ compound interest. Find the balance immediately after the last deposit. Then find the present value at a rate of $2{,}5\%$ of a perpetuity paying $800$ euros per year, with the first payment one year from now.

:::soluzione
The balance is $300 \cdot \dfrac{1{,}02^{10} - 1}{0{,}02} \approx 3284{,}92$ euros. The present value of the perpetuity is $\dfrac{800}{0{,}025} = 32\,000$ euros.
:::
