At the end of every year, $300$ euros are deposited for $10$ years into an account at $2\%$ compound interest. Compute the balance immediately after the last deposit. Then compute the present value, at the rate of $2.5\%$, of a perpetuity of $800$ euros a year with the first payment one year from now.

:::soluzione
The balance is $300 \cdot \dfrac{1.02^{10} - 1}{0.02} \approx 3284.92$ euros; the present value of the perpetuity is $\dfrac{800}{0.025} = 32\,000$ euros.
:::
