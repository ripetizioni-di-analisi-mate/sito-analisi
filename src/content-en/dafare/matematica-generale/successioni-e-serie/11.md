---
titolo: Geometric series starting from a different index
teoria: [serie-geometrica]
parole: [geometric series, first term, sum]
---

Compute the sum of the series $\displaystyle\sum_{k=2}^{\infty} \left( \frac{1}{3} \right)^{k}$.

:::soluzione
The first term is $\dfrac{1}{9}$, so the sum is $\dfrac{1}{9} \cdot \dfrac{3}{2} = \dfrac{1}{6}$.
:::
