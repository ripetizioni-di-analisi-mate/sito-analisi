---
titolo: Limit with a difference of roots
teoria: [regole-limiti-successioni, limite-successione]
parole: [limit, rationalization, roots]
---

Compute $\displaystyle\lim_{n \to +\infty} \left( \sqrt{n + 5} - \sqrt{n} \right)$.

:::soluzione
Rationalizing gives $\dfrac{5}{\sqrt{n + 5} + \sqrt{n}} \to 0$.
:::
