The production cost is $C(q) = 2000 + 15q + 0.05\,q^2$. Compute the marginal cost for $q = 50$ and the actual cost of the fifty-first unit.

:::soluzione
$C'(50) = 20$ euros; $C(51) - C(50) = 20.05$ euros.
:::
