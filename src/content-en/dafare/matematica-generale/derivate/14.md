Find the intervals of increase and decrease of $f(x) = x\,e^{-x}$.

:::soluzione
$f'(x) = (1 - x)\,e^{-x}$, so $f$ is increasing on $(-\infty, 1]$ and decreasing on $[1, +\infty)$.
:::
