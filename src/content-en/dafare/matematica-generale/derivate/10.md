The demand for a good is $q(p) = 400 - 8p$. Compute the elasticity at the price $p = 30$ and state whether a price increase raises revenue.

:::soluzione
$\varepsilon(30) = -1.5$, so demand is elastic, and a price increase lowers revenue.
:::
