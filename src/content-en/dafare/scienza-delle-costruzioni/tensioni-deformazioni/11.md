An isotropic, linearly elastic solid in plane strain has $E=200\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\nu=0.25$, $\varepsilon_x=450\cdot10^{-6}$, $\varepsilon_y=-100\cdot10^{-6}$, and $\gamma_{xy}=0$. Find the three normal stresses.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 2.8,0 2.8,1.8 0,1.8" linee="2.8,0 3.6,0.65 3.6,2.45 0.8,2.45 0,1.8;2.8,1.8 3.6,2.45;2.8,1.8 2.8,0" etichette="1.38,0.83,ε_z = 0" titolo="Element with out-of-plane displacement restrained"}

:::soluzione
$\sigma_x=100\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_y=12\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\sigma_z=28\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
:::
