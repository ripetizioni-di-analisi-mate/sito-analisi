In plane stress, $\sigma_x=-30\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_y=10\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\tau_{xy}=15\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Find the normal and shear stresses on the face with $\mathbf n=(\dfrac{4}{5},-\dfrac{3}{5})$ and $\mathbf t=(\dfrac{3}{5},\dfrac{4}{5})$.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 1.8,0 3.15,1.8 0,1.8" frecce="2.475,0.9,3.195,0.36,n;2.475,0.9,2.88,1.44" etichette="3.03,1.53,t" titolo="Inclined face with its normal in the fourth quadrant"}

:::soluzione
$\sigma_n=-30\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\tau_{nt}=-15\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
:::
