An isotropic, linearly elastic solid is in plane strain with $\sigma_x=120\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\sigma_y=80\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Given $E=210\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\nu=0.30$, find $\sigma_z$ and $\varepsilon_x$.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 2.8,0 2.8,1.8 0,1.8" linee="2.8,0 3.6,0.65 3.6,2.45 0.8,2.45 0,1.8;2.8,1.8 3.6,2.45;2.8,1.8 2.8,0" etichette="1.38,0.83,ε_z = 0" titolo="Element with out-of-plane normal strain restrained"}

:::soluzione
$\sigma_z=60\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\varepsilon_x=\dfrac{13}{35\,000}\simeq371.43\cdot10^{-6}$.
:::
