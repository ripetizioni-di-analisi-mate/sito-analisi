A point is in pure shear with $\tau_{xy}=-36\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Find the largest principal stress in the $xy$ plane and the angle of its normal in $[-90^\circ,90^\circ)$.

::struttura{poligoni="-1,-1 1,-1 1,1 -1,1" frecce="1.15,0.55,1.15,-0.55,τ_xy;-1.15,-0.55,-1.15,0.55;0.55,1.15,-0.55,1.15;-0.55,-1.15,0.55,-1.15" titolo="Directions of negative shear stresses"}

:::soluzione
$\sigma_1=36\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\theta_1=-45^\circ$.
:::
