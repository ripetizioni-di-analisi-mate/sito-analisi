A rectangular strain-gauge rosette measures $\varepsilon_0=420\cdot10^{-6}$, $\varepsilon_{45}=300\cdot10^{-6}$, and $\varepsilon_{90}=-180\cdot10^{-6}$. The isotropic, linearly elastic material has $G=80\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Calculate $\gamma_{xy}$ and $\tau_{xy}$.

::struttura{poligoni="-0.2,-0.2 3.2,-0.2 3.2,2.8 -0.2,2.8" frecce="0,0,2.5,0,0°;0,0,1.8,1.8,45°;0,0,0,2.3,90°" titolo="Orientations of the three rosette grids"}

:::soluzione
$\gamma_{xy}=360\cdot10^{-6}$ and $\tau_{xy}=28.8\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
:::
