The strain-gauge rosette shown reads $\varepsilon_0=150\cdot10^{-6}$, $\varepsilon_{45}=250\cdot10^{-6}$, and $\varepsilon_{90}=-50\cdot10^{-6}$. Find the larger in-plane principal strain and its angle to the $x$ axis within $[-90^\circ,90^\circ)$.

::struttura{poligoni="-0.2,-0.2 3.2,-0.2 3.2,2.8 -0.2,2.8" frecce="0,0,2.5,0,0°;0,0,1.8,1.8,45°;0,0,0,2.3,90°" titolo="Rosette with grids at 0°, 45°, and 90°"}

:::soluzione
$\varepsilon_1=(50+100\sqrt{5})\cdot10^{-6}\simeq273.61\cdot10^{-6}$ and $\theta_1\simeq31.72^\circ$.
:::
