In the sheet shown, the measured strains under plane stress are $\varepsilon_x=500\cdot10^{-6}$, $\varepsilon_y=-125\cdot10^{-6}$, and $\gamma_{xy}=-250\cdot10^{-6}$. The isotropic, linearly elastic material has $E=200\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\nu=0.25$. Find $\sigma_x$, $\sigma_y$, and $\tau_{xy}$.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 3,0 3,2 0,2" frecce="0,0,3.8,0,x;0,0,0,2.8,y" punti="1.5,1,P,pieno" riferimento=0 titolo="Measurement point in the midplane of the sheet"}

:::soluzione
$\sigma_x=100\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_y=0$, and $\tau_{xy}=-20\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
:::
