In a simply connected planar region, the prescribed strains are $\varepsilon_x=20y^2\cdot10^{-6}$, $\varepsilon_y=10x^2\cdot10^{-6}$, and $\gamma_{xy}=kxy\cdot10^{-6}$, where $x,y$ are the numerical coordinates in meters. Find $k$ such that the compatibility condition holds.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 3,0 3,2 0,2" frecce="0,0,3.8,0,x;0,0,0,2.8,y" punti="1.5,1,P,pieno" riferimento=0 titolo="Planar region with Cartesian coordinates"}

:::soluzione
$k=60$.
:::
