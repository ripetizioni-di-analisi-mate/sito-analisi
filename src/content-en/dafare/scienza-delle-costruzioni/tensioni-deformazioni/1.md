In plane stress, $\sigma_x=70\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_y=-10\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\tau_{xy}=24\,\mathrm{MPa}$. On the face with normal $\mathbf n=(\dfrac{3}{5},\dfrac{4}{5})$, calculate $\sigma_n$ and the shear component $\tau_{nt}$ directed along $\mathbf t=(-\dfrac{4}{5},\dfrac{3}{5})$.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 2.4,0 0,1.8" frecce="1.2,0.9,1.74,1.62,n;1.2,0.9,0.48,1.44,t" titolo="Normal and tangent to the inclined face"}

:::soluzione
$\sigma_n=41.84\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\tau_{nt}=-45.12\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
:::
