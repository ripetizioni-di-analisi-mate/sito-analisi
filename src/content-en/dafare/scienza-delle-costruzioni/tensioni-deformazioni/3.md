In the plane field shown, the tensor components in $\mathrm{MPa}$ are $\sigma_x=12+3x+y$, $\sigma_y=9-x+5y$, and $\tau_{xy}=2x-4y$. Here $x$ and $y$ are the numerical coordinates in meters. For static equilibrium, find the components of the body force per unit volume $\mathbf b$.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 3,0 3,2 0,2" frecce="0,0,3.8,0,x;0,0,0,2.8,y" punti="1.5,1,P,pieno" riferimento=0 titolo="Interior point in a planar field"}

:::soluzione
$b_x=1\,\mathrm{MN/m^3}$ and $b_y=-7\,\mathrm{MN/m^3}$.
:::
