A thin isotropic, linearly elastic sheet is in plane stress. Let $E=200\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\nu=0.25$, $\sigma_x=120\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_y=60\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\tau_{xy}=30\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Find $\varepsilon_x$, $\varepsilon_y$, $\varepsilon_z$, and $\gamma_{xy}$.

::struttura{poligoni="-1,-1 1,-1 1,1 -1,1" reazioni="1 0,0.55,0,σ_x;-1 0,-0.55,0;0 1,0,0.55,σ_y;0 -1,0,-0.55" frecce="1.13,-0.65,1.13,0.58,τ_xy;-1.13,0.65,-1.13,-0.58;-0.55,1.13,0.58,1.13;0.55,-1.13,-0.58,-1.13" titolo="Sheet with traction-free faces perpendicular to z"}

:::soluzione
$\varepsilon_x=525\cdot10^{-6}$, $\varepsilon_y=150\cdot10^{-6}$, $\varepsilon_z=-225\cdot10^{-6}$, and $\gamma_{xy}=375\cdot10^{-6}$.
:::
