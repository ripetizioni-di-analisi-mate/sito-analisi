In the plane shown, the displacement field is $u_x=(200x+70y)\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm m$ and $u_y=(-30x-100y)\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm m$. The symbols $x,y$ denote the numerical values of the coordinates in meters. Find $\varepsilon_x$, $\varepsilon_y$, and $\gamma_{xy}$ using infinitesimal strain theory.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 3,0 3,2 0,2" frecce="0,0,3.8,0,x;0,0,0,2.8,y" punti="1.5,1,P,pieno" riferimento=0 titolo="Coordinates of the point where the field is examined"}

:::soluzione
$\varepsilon_x=200\cdot10^{-6}$, $\varepsilon_y=-100\cdot10^{-6}$, and $\gamma_{xy}=40\cdot10^{-6}$.
:::
