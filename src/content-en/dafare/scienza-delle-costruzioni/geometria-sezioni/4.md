A rectangular plate $80\,\mathrm{mm}$ wide and $60\,\mathrm{mm}$ high has a square hole of side $20\,\mathrm{mm}$ centered at $(z,y)=(30,20)\,\mathrm{mm}$. The origin is at the lower left corner of the outer rectangle. Find the height $y_G$ of the centroid of the remaining material.

::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="0,0 80,0 80,60 0,60" fori="20,10 40,10 40,30 20,30" punti="30,20,C,centro" frecce="0,0,92,0,z;0,0,0,73,y" quote="0 60,80 60,80 mm,-8;80 0,80 60,60 mm,8;20 10,40 10,20 mm,4" titolo="Rectangular section with an eccentric square hole"}

:::soluzione
$y_G=\dfrac{340}{11}\,\mathrm{mm}\simeq30.91\,\mathrm{mm}$.
:::
