The rectangle shown has width $40\,\mathrm{mm}$ along $z$ and height $60\,\mathrm{mm}$ along $y$. The origin is at the lower left corner. Find the first moment of area $S_z$ about the bottom edge.

::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="0,0 40,0 40,60 0,60" frecce="0,0,53,0,z;0,0,0,72,y" quote="0 60,40 60,40 mm,-8;40 0,40 60,60 mm,9" titolo="Rectangle with the origin at its lower left corner"}

:::soluzione
$S_z=72\,000\,\mathrm{mm^3}$.
:::
