A rectangular section of width $20\,\mathrm{mm}$ and height $100\,\mathrm{mm}$ is centered at the origin. Calculate the first moment about $z$ of only the portion at heights satisfying $y\geq30\,\mathrm{mm}$.

::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="-10,30 10,30 10,50 -10,50" linee="-10,-50 10,-50 10,50 -10,50 -10,-50" frecce="0,0,22,0,z;0,0,0,64,y" quote="-10 50,10 50,20 mm,-22;-10 0,-10 30,30 mm,-8;10 -50,10 50,100 mm,22" titolo="Upper portion of the rectangle bounded by the 30 mm level"}

:::soluzione
$S_z=16\,000\,\mathrm{mm^3}$.
:::
