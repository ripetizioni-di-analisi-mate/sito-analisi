An angle section has a second moment of $50\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$ about its horizontal centroidal axis. A second section is obtained by doubling every linear dimension. Find the second moment of the new section about its horizontal centroidal axis.

::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="0,0 18,0 18,4 4,4 4,22 0,22;35,0 71,0 71,8 43,8 43,44 35,44" frecce="0,0,25,0,z;0,0,0,30,y;35,0,80,0,z;35,0,35,53,y" etichette="10,14,Section I;54,25,Section II" titolo="Two similar angles with a linear scale factor of two"}

:::soluzione
$I_z=800\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$.
:::
