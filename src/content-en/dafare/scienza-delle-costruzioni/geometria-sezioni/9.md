A section is obtained by taking a square of side $60\,\mathrm{mm}$ centered at the origin, with sides parallel to the axes, and removing a square hole of side $20\,\mathrm{mm}$ centered at $(z,y)=(10,10)\,\mathrm{mm}$. The sides of the hole are parallel to those of the outer square. Calculate the product of inertia $I_{yz}$ about the centroidal axes parallel to $y,z$.

::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="-30,-30 30,-30 30,30 -30,30" fori="0,0 20,0 20,20 0,20" punti="10,10,C,centro" frecce="0,0,42,0,z;0,0,0,43,y" quote="-30 -30,30 -30,60 mm,8;0 20,20 20,20 mm,-5" titolo="Square hole in the quadrant with both coordinates positive"}

:::soluzione
$I_{yz}=-45\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$.
:::
