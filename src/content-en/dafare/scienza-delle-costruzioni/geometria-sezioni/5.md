A rectangle of width $30\,\mathrm{mm}$ and height $40\,\mathrm{mm}$ rests with its lower edge on the $z$ axis. Calculate its second moment of area about that axis.

::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="0,0 30,0 30,40 0,40" frecce="0,0,43,0,z;0,0,0,52,y" quote="0 40,30 40,30 mm,-7;30 0,30 40,40 mm,8" titolo="The lower edge of the rectangle coincides with the z axis"}

:::soluzione
$I_z=640\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$.
:::
