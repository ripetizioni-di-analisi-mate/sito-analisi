The outer boundary of a hollow section has width $100\,\mathrm{mm}$ and height $80\,\mathrm{mm}$. Its centered opening has width $60\,\mathrm{mm}$ and height $40\,\mathrm{mm}$. Find the second moment about the horizontal centroidal axis $z$.

::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="-50,-40 50,-40 50,40 -50,40" fori="-30,-20 30,-20 30,20 -30,20" frecce="0,0,64,0,z;0,0,0,53,y" quote="-50 -40,50 -40,100 mm,9;50 -40,50 40,80 mm,27;-30 -20,30 -20,60 mm,-7;-30 -20,-30 20,40 mm,-8" titolo="Concentric rectangles with parallel sides"}

:::soluzione
$I_z=\dfrac{11\,840\,000}{3}\,\mathrm{mm^4}\simeq3.947\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$.
:::
