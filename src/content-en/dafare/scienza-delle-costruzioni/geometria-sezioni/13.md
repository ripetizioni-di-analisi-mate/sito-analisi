A solid rectangular section is $40\,\mathrm{mm}$ wide and $90\,\mathrm{mm}$ high. Calculate its smallest principal radius of gyration.

::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="-20,-45 20,-45 20,45 -20,45" frecce="0,0,35,0,z;0,0,0,58,y" quote="-20 -45,20 -45,40 mm,8;20 -45,20 45,90 mm,27" titolo="Rectangular section with both centroidal axes"}

:::soluzione
$\rho_{\min}=\dfrac{20\sqrt 3}{3}\,\mathrm{mm}\simeq11.55\,\mathrm{mm}$.
:::
