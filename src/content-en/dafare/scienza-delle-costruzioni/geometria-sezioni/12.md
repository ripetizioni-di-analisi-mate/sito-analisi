The web of a T-section is $20\,\mathrm{mm}$ wide and $80\,\mathrm{mm}$ high. The top flange, centered over the web, is $100\,\mathrm{mm}$ wide and $20\,\mathrm{mm}$ thick. Find the elastic section modulus at the bottom fiber about the horizontal centroidal axis.

::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="-10,0 10,0 10,80 50,80 50,100 -50,100 -50,80 -10,80" frecce="0,0,65,0,z;0,0,0,114,y" quote="-50 100,50 100,100 mm,-23;-10 0,10 0,20 mm,9;10 0,10 80,80 mm,10;50 80,50 100,20 mm,9" titolo="T-section with the flange above the web"}

:::soluzione
$W_{\mathrm{lower}}=\dfrac{2\,828\,000}{61}\,\mathrm{mm^3}\simeq46\,361\,\mathrm{mm^3}$.
:::
