The horizontal beam is $10\,\mathrm{m}$ long. It has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$, a distance $7\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Segment $BC$ is the overhang. Measure coordinate $x$ to the right from $A$. On segment $BC$ between $x=7\,\mathrm{m}$ and $x=10\,\mathrm{m}$, a uniform downward load of intensity $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts. Find the signed value of $T(8)$ at section $x=8\,\mathrm{m}$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,7,0;C,10,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,B,7 m;B,C,3 m" carichi="B,C,1.0,1.0,6 kN/m" titolo="Beam supports, loads, and dimensions"}

:::soluzione
$T=12\,\mathrm{kN}$.
:::
