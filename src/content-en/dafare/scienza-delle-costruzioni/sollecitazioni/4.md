The horizontal beam is $10\,\mathrm{m}$ long. It has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$, a distance $10\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Measure coordinate $x$ to the right from $A$. On segment $CD$ between $x=2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $x=6\,\mathrm{m}$, a uniform downward load of intensity $5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts. Find the signed value of $T(4)$ at section $x=4\,\mathrm{m}$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;D,6,0;B,10,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,C,2 m;C,D,4 m;D,B,4 m" carichi="C,D,1.0,1.0,5 kN/m" titolo="Beam supports, loads, and dimensions"}

:::soluzione
$T=2\,\mathrm{kN}$.
:::
