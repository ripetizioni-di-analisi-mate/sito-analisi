The horizontal beam is $6\,\mathrm{m}$ long. It has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$, a distance $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Measure coordinate $x$ to the right from $A$. On segment $AB$ between $x=0\,\mathrm{m}$ and $x=6\,\mathrm{m}$, a downward load varies linearly in intensity from $0\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ to $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Find the signed value of $T(2)$ at section $x=2\,\mathrm{m}$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,B,6 m" carichi="A,B,0.0,0.6,0→6 kN/m" titolo="Beam supports, loads, and dimensions"}

:::soluzione
$T=4\,\mathrm{kN}$.
:::
