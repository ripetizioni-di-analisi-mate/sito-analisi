The horizontal beam is $6\,\mathrm{m}$ long. It has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$, a distance $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Measure coordinate $x$ to the right from $A$. At $C$, a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, couple $\mathcal M_0$ acts clockwise with magnitude $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Find the signed value of $T(4)$ at section $x=4\,\mathrm{m}$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,4 m" coppie="C,-1,M_0" titolo="Beam supports, loads, and dimensions"}

:::soluzione
$T=-3\,\mathrm{kN}$.
:::
