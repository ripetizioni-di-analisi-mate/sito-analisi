The horizontal beam $AB$ is fixed at $A$ and has length $6\,\mathrm{m}$. Measure coordinate $x$ to the right from $A$. At $C$, a distance $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, axial force $F_1$ acts to the left with magnitude $7\,\mathrm{kN}$. At $B$, a distance $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, axial force $F_2$ acts to the right with magnitude $12\,\mathrm{kN}$. Find the signed value of $N(5)$ at section $x=5\,\mathrm{m}$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="A,C,3 m;C,B,3 m" forze="C,-0.87,0,F_1;B,1.2,0,F_2" titolo="Beam supports, loads, and dimensions"}

:::soluzione
$N=12\,\mathrm{kN}$.
:::
