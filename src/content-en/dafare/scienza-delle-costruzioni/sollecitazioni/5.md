The horizontal beam is $8\,\mathrm{m}$ long. It has a pin at $A$ and a bilateral roller on a horizontal surface at $B$, a distance $8\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Measure coordinate $x$ to the right from $A$. At $C$, a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, couple $\mathcal M_0$ acts counterclockwise with magnitude $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Find the signed value of $M(5)$ at section $x=5\,\mathrm{m}$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,C,4 m;C,B,4 m" coppie="C,1,M_0" titolo="Beam supports, loads, and dimensions"}

:::soluzione
$M=-9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
:::
