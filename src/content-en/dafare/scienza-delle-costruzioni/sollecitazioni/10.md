The horizontal beam $AB$ is fixed at $A$ and has length $4\,\mathrm{m}$. Measure coordinate $x$ to the right from $A$. On segment $AB$ between $x=0\,\mathrm{m}$ and $x=4\,\mathrm{m}$, a uniform downward load of intensity $5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts. Find the signed value of $M(0^+)$ at the section immediately to the right of the fixed support.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="A,B,4 m" carichi="A,B,0.4,0.4,5 kN/m" titolo="Beam supports, loads, and dimensions"}

:::soluzione
$M=-40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
:::
