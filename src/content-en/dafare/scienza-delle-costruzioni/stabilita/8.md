The column shown is pinned at both ends in the plane of bending. Its length is $3\,\mathrm m$. The member has $E=200\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and a radius of gyration $30\,\mathrm{mm}$. Calculate the average compressive stress at the critical load.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="B,-0.66,0,P" quote="A,B,3 m,0.75"  titolo="Compression member and its flexural end restraints"}

:::soluzione
$$
\sigma_{\mathrm{cr}}=20 \pi^{2}\,\mathrm{MPa}.
$$
:::
