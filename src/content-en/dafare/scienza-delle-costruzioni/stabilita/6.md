The member shown has length $4\,\mathrm m$. The flexural rigidities about $y$ and $z$ are $240$ and $600\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$, respectively. The values are $K_y=\dfrac12$ for bending about $y$ and $K_z=1$ for bending about $z$. Calculate the smaller of the two critical loads.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="B,-0.88,0,P" quote="A,B,4 m,0.75"  titolo="Flexural model in the plane with $K_z=1$"}

:::soluzione
$$
P_{\mathrm{cr}}=\frac{75 \pi^{2}}{2}\,\mathrm{kN}.
$$
:::
