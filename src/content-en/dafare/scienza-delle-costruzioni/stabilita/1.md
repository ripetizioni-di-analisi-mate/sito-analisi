The column shown is pinned at both ends in the plane of bending. Its length is $4\,\mathrm m$ and its flexural rigidity is $EI=320\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. Find the first elastic buckling load.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="B,-0.88,0,P" quote="A,B,4 m,0.75"  titolo="Compression member and its flexural end restraints"}

:::soluzione
$$
P_{\mathrm{cr}}=20 \pi^{2}\,\mathrm{kN}.
$$
:::
