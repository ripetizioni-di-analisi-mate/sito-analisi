The column shown is pinned at both ends in the plane of bending. Its length is $5\,\mathrm m$. The required critical load in this model is $60\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$. Calculate the flexural rigidity $EI$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="B,-1.1,0,P" quote="A,B,5 m,0.75"  titolo="Compression member and its flexural end restraints"}

:::soluzione
$$
EI=1500\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}.
$$
:::
