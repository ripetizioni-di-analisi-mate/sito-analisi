The coordinates of the truss joints shown are $A=(0,0)$, $B=(6,0)$, and $C=(3,4)$, in meters. The members are $AB$, $AC$, and $BC$. Joint $A$ is pinned and joint $B$ is supported by a roller on a horizontal surface. Two forces act at $C$, one of $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and one of $40\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. Determine the axial force in member $AC$, taking tension as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,3,4" aste="A-B;A-C;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" reticolare=1 forze="C,0.9,0,18 kN;C,0,-0.9,40 kN" quote="A,3 0,3 m;3 0,B,3 m;6.45 4,6.45 0,4 m,-0.55" titolo="Specified geometry, supports, and loads"}

:::soluzione
$$
N_{AC} = -10\,\mathrm{kN}.
$$
:::
