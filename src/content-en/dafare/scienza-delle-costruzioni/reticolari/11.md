The coordinates of the truss joints shown are $A=(0,0)$, $B=(4,0)$, $C=(8,0)$, $D=(0,3)$, $E=(4,3)$, and $F=(8,3)$, in meters. The members are $AB$, $BC$, $AD$, $BE$, $CF$, $DE$, $EF$, $BD$, and $CE$. Joint $A$ is pinned and joint $C$ is supported by a roller on a horizontal surface. Two forces act at $F$, one of $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left and one of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. Determine the axial force in member $DE$, taking tension as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,8,0;D,0,3;E,4,3;F,8,3" aste="A-B;B-C;A-D;B-E;C-F;D-E;E-F;B-D;C-E" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" reticolare=1 forze="F,-1.2,0,16 kN;F,0,-1.2,12 kN" quote="A,B,4 m;B,C,4 m;8.56 3,8.56 0,3 m,-0.55" tratteggi="2,-0.6 2,3.6" etichette="2.2,3.6,s" titolo="Specified geometry, supports, and loads"}

:::soluzione
$$
N_{DE} = -8\,\mathrm{kN}.
$$
:::
