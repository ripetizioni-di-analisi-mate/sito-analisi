The coordinates of the truss joints shown are $A=(0,0)$, $B=(4,0)$, $C=(0,3)$, and $D=(4,3)$, in meters. The members are $AB$, $AC$, $BD$, $CD$, and $BC$. Joint $A$ is pinned and joint $B$ is supported by a roller on a horizontal surface. A rightward force of $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $D$. Determine the axial force in member $BC$, taking tension as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,0,3;D,4,3" aste="A-B;A-C;B-D;C-D;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" reticolare=1 forze="D,0.65,0,15 kN" quote="A,B,4 m;4.45 3,4.45 0,3 m,-0.55" titolo="Specified geometry, supports, and loads"}

:::soluzione
$$
N_{BC} = - \frac{75}{4}\,\mathrm{kN}.
$$
:::
