The coordinates of the truss joints shown are $A=(0,0)$, $B=(4,0)$, $C=(8,0)$, $D=(0,3)$, $E=(4,3)$, and $F=(8,3)$, in meters. A downward force of $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $D$. A downward force of $30\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $E$. Determine the axial force in member $BD$, taking tension as positive.
