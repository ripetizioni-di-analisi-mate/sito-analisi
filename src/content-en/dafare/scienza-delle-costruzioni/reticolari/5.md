The coordinates of the truss joints shown are $A=(0,0)$, $B=(3,0)$, $C=(6,0)$, and $D=(3,4)$, in meters. The members are $AB$, $BC$, $AD$, $BD$, and $CD$. Joint $A$ is pinned and joint $C$ is supported by a roller on a horizontal surface. A downward force of $48\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $D$. Determine the axial force in member $BD$, taking tension as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;D,3,4" aste="A-B;B-C;A-D;B-D;C-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" reticolare=1 forze="D,0,-0.9,48 kN" quote="A,B,3 m;B,C,3 m;6.45 4,6.45 0,4 m,-0.55" titolo="Specified geometry, supports, and loads"}

:::soluzione
$$
N_{BD} = 0\,\mathrm{kN}.
$$
:::
