A simply supported beam of span $4\,\mathrm m$ carries a central force $P=24\,\mathrm{kN}$ and a uniform load $q=6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, both downward. Given $EI=12\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$, find the midspan deflection.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,2,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,2 m" carichi="A,B,0.48,0.48,6 kN/m" forze="C,0,-0.98,24 kN"  titolo="Beam and loading diagram"}

:::soluzione
$v(C)=\dfrac{13}{3}\,\mathrm{mm}$ downward.
:::
