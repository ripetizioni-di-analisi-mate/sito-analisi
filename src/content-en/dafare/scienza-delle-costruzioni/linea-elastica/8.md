A simply supported beam of span $6\,\mathrm m$ has $EI_1=10\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ on the two segments of length $2\,\mathrm m$ adjacent to the supports and $EI_2=20\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ on the middle segment. A downward force $P=12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at midspan. Find the midspan deflection.

:::soluzione
$v(C)=3.5\,\mathrm{mm}$ downward.
:::
