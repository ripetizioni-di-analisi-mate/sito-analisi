A counterclockwise couple of $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ acts at midspan of a beam of span $4\,\mathrm m$ with bilateral supports. The flexural rigidity is $EI=8\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. Find the jump $v^{\prime\prime}(2^+)-v^{\prime\prime}(2^-)$, with $x$ in meters and $v$ positive downward.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,2,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,2 m" coppie="C,1,24 kN m"  titolo="Beam and loading diagram"}

:::soluzione
$v^{\prime\prime}(2^+)-v^{\prime\prime}(2^-)=3\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$.
:::
