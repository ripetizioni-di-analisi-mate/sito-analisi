A counterclockwise couple $\mathcal M=12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ acts at the tip of a cantilever of span $3\,\mathrm m$. Assume $EI=6\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. Find $v(B)$, taking deflection as positive downward.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;Z,3.850,0,-" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="A,B,3 m" coppie="B,1,12 kN m"  titolo="Beam and loading diagram"}

:::soluzione
$v(B)=-9\,\mathrm{mm}$, or $9\,\mathrm{mm}$ upward.
:::
