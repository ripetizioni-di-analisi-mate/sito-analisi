A downward force of $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ and a counterclockwise couple of $36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ act at the tip of a cantilever of span $3\,\mathrm m$ and flexural rigidity $EI=9\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. Find the vertical tip displacement.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;Z,3.850,0,-" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="A,B,3 m" forze="B,0,-1.15,18 kN" coppie="B,1,36 kN m"  titolo="Beam and loading diagram"}

:::soluzione
$v(B)=0$.
:::
