The cantilever shown has span $L=3\,\mathrm m$ and constant flexural rigidity $EI=9\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. A force $P=9\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward at the free end. Find the vertical displacement of $B$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;Z,3.850,0,-" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="A,B,3 m" forze="B,0,-0.85,9 kN"  titolo="Beam and loading diagram"}

:::soluzione
$v_B=9\,\mathrm{mm}$ downward.
:::
