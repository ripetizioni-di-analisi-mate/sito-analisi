The simply supported beam has span $6\,\mathrm m$ and flexural rigidity $EI=18\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. A uniform downward load of intensity $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts over the full span. Calculate the midspan deflection.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,B,6 m" carichi="A,B,0.65,0.65,6 kN/m"  titolo="Beam and loading diagram"}

:::soluzione
$v(\dfrac L2)=5.625\,\mathrm{mm}$ downward.
:::
