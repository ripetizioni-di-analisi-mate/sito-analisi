The cantilever has span $4\,\mathrm m$. Over the first $2\,\mathrm m$ the rigidity is $EI_1=12\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. Over the last $2\,\mathrm m$ it is $EI_2=6\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. A downward force $P=12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at the free end. Find $v(B)$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,2,0;Z,4.850,0,-" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,2 m" forze="B,0,-0.85,12 kN" etichette="0.75,0.25,EI_1;2.75,0.25,EI_2" titolo="Beam and loading diagram"}

:::soluzione
$v(B)=24\,\mathrm{mm}$ downward.
:::
