The simply supported beam of span $4\,\mathrm m$ has $EI=10\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ and carries a uniform downward load $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Support $A$ remains stationary, and support $B$ settles by $6\,\mathrm{mm}$. Find the midpoint displacement relative to the original beam.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,B,4 m" carichi="A,B,0.46,0.46,4 kN/m" frecce="4.3,0.1,4.3,-0.7,6 mm"  titolo="Beam and loading diagram"}

:::soluzione
$v(\dfrac L2)=\dfrac{13}{3}\,\mathrm{mm}$ downward.
:::
