A cantilever of span $4\,\mathrm m$ and constant flexural rigidity $EI=8\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ carries a uniform downward load $q=4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Find the free-end slope $v^\prime$, taking deflection as positive downward.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="A,B,4 m" carichi="A,B,0.56,0.56,4 kN/m"  titolo="Beam and loading diagram"}

:::soluzione
$v^\prime(B)=\dfrac{2}{375}\simeq5.33\cdot10^{-3}$.
:::
