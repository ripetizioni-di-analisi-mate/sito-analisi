A cantilever of span $3\,\mathrm m$ and flexural rigidity $EI=10\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ carries a downward triangular load that varies from zero at the fixed end to $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at the tip. Find the free-end deflection.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="A,B,3 m" carichi="A,B,0,0.8,6 kN/m"  titolo="Beam and loading diagram"}

:::soluzione
$v(B)=4.455\,\mathrm{mm}$ downward.
:::
