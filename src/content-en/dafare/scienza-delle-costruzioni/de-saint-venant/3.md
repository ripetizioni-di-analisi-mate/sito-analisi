A rectangular beam of width $60\,\mathrm{mm}$ and depth $100\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries only a bending moment $M_z=2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, positive according to the chapter’s sign convention. Find the normal stress at the top fiber.

::struttura{poligoni="-30,-50 30,-50 30,50 -30,50" frecce="-37.2,0,37.2,0,z;0,-57.2,0,57.2,y" quote="-30 -50,30 -50,60 mm,7.2;30 -50,30 50,100 mm,14.4" riferimento=0 titolo="Rectangular section in bending"}

:::soluzione
$\sigma_{\mathrm{upper}}=-20\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
:::
