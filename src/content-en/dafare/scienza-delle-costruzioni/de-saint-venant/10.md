A circular tube has outer radius $30\,\mathrm{mm}$ and inner radius $20\,\mathrm{mm}$. A constant torque $M_t=260\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ acts over a length $400\,\mathrm{mm}$. Its shear modulus is $G=80\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Find the angle of twist between the two ends of this segment.

::struttura{cerchi="0,0,30;0,0,20,foro" frecce="-39,0,39,0,z;0,-31.5,0,39,y" quote="-30 -30,30 -30,Ø 60 mm,6" riferimento=0 titolo="Tube with an inner radius of 20 mm"}

:::soluzione
$\varphi=\dfrac{0.004}{\pi}\,\mathrm{rad}$.
:::
