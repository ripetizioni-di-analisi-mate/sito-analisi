A solid circular shaft of radius $20\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries a tensile axial force $N=24\pi\,\mathrm{kN}$ and a torque of magnitude $120\pi\,\mathrm{N\,m}$. Find the von Mises equivalent stress at the outer surface away from the ends.

::struttura{cerchi="0,0,20" frecce="-26,0,26,0,z;0,-21,0,26,y" quote="-20 -20,20 -20,Ø 40 mm,4" riferimento=0 titolo="Solid circular cross section"}

:::soluzione
$\sigma_{\mathrm{eq}}=30\sqrt{7}\,\mathrm{MPa}\simeq79.4\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
:::
