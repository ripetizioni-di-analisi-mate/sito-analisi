An L-section consists of two thin walls of midline lengths $60\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $40\,\mathrm{mm}$, both of thickness $3\,\mathrm{mm}$. It transmits a torque of magnitude $9\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ with free warping. Using the thin-strip model, find the maximum shear stress magnitude away from the junction.

::struttura{poligoni="-1.5,-1.5 60,-1.5 60,1.5 1.5,1.5 1.5,40 -1.5,40" tratteggi="0,40 0,0 60,0" frecce="-6,0,69,0,z;0,-9,0,49,y" quote="0 0,60 0,60 mm,8;0 0,0 40,40 mm,11.2" riferimento=0 titolo="Midline lengths of the open section"}

:::soluzione
$|\tau|_{\max}\simeq30\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
:::
