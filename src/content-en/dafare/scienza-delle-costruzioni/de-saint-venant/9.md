A solid shaft of diameter $40\,\mathrm{mm}$ transmits a torque of magnitude $160\,\mathrm{N\,m}$. Find the maximum shear stress magnitude.

::struttura{cerchi="0,0,20" frecce="-26,0,26,0,z;0,-21,0,26,y" quote="-20 -20,20 -20,Ø 40 mm,4" riferimento=0 titolo="Solid section of diameter 40 mm"}

:::soluzione
$|\tau|_{\max}=\dfrac{40}{\pi}\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
:::
