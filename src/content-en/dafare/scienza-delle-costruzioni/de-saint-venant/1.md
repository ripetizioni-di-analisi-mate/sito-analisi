A rectangular prism of width $40\,\mathrm{mm}$ and height $60\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries a concentric compressive load of magnitude $36\,\mathrm{kN}$. Find the normal stress away from the loaded ends, taking tension as positive.

::struttura{poligoni="-20,-30 20,-30 20,30 -20,30" frecce="-24.8,0,24.8,0,z;0,-34.8,0,34.8,y" quote="-20 -30,20 -30,40 mm,4.8;20 -30,20 30,60 mm,9.6" riferimento=0 titolo="Rectangular cross section of the prism"}

:::soluzione
$\sigma_x=-15\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
:::
