In a single-cell closed section, the wall midline is a rectangle with sides $100\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $60\,\mathrm{mm}$. The horizontal and vertical walls have thicknesses $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $2\,\mathrm{mm}$, respectively. The applied torque is $1.44\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, the shear modulus is $80\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and the member length is $800\,\mathrm{mm}$. Find the angle of twist.

::struttura{poligoni="-51,-32 51,-32 51,32 -51,32" fori="-49,-28 49,-28 49,28 -49,28" tratteggi="-50,-30 50,-30 50,30 -50,30 -50,-30" frecce="-62,0,62,0,z;0,-37.2,0,37.2,y" quote="-50 -30,50 -30,100 mm,9;50 -30,50 30,60 mm,28" riferimento=0 titolo="Dimensions of the cell midline"}

:::soluzione
$\varphi=0.011\,\mathrm{rad}$.
:::
