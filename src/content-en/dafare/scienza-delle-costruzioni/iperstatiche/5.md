A beam of span $4\,\mathrm m$ and constant flexural rigidity is fixed at $A$ and supported vertically at $B$. A downward force of $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at midspan. Calculate the vertical reaction at $B$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,2,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1,P" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,2 m" titolo="Point load at midspan"}

:::soluzione
$R_B=5\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward.
:::
