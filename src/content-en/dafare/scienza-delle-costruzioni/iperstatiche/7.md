A prismatic beam of span $6\,\mathrm m$ is fixed at both ends and carries a uniform downward load of intensity $10\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Calculate the internal bending moment immediately next to the left fixed end. A positive bending moment puts the bottom fibers in tension.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,incastro,0" carichi="A,B,0.7,0.7,q" quote="A,B,6 m" titolo="Fixed-fixed beam under a uniform load"}

:::soluzione
$M(0^+)=-30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
:::
