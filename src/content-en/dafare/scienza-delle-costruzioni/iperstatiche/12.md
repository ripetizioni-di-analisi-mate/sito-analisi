A bar of cross-sectional area $500\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ is restrained axially at both ends. Its Young’s modulus is $200\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\alpha=12\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$. Find the axial force when the temperature increases uniformly by $25\,\mathrm K$ and the bar remains straight.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,incastro,0" etichette="2.5,0.65,uniform ΔT" titolo="Member restrained axially at both ends"}

:::soluzione
$N=-30\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression.
:::
