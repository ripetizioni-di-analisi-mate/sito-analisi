A continuous prismatic beam has two equal spans of length $4\,\mathrm m$ and simple supports at $A$, $B$, and $C$. A uniform downward load of intensity $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts on both spans. Calculate the vertical reaction at $B$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,8,0" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;C,carrello" carichi="A,C,0.7,0.7,q" quote="A,B,4 m;B,C,4 m" titolo="End supports and intermediate support"}

:::soluzione
$R_B=20\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward.
:::
