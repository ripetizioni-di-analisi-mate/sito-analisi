A beam of length $6\,\mathrm m$ is fixed at $A$ and supported vertically at $B$. The flexural rigidity is constant. A uniform load of intensity $8\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts downward. Find the vertical reaction at $B$, taking upward as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.7,0.7,q" quote="A,B,6 m" titolo="Propped cantilever under a uniform load"}

:::soluzione
$R_B=18\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward.
:::
