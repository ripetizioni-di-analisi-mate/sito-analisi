A continuous beam on three supports has two spans of length $4\,\mathrm m$, with $EI=12\,800\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. The end supports remain stationary, and the middle bilateral vertical support settles by $5\,\mathrm{mm}$. With no applied loads, calculate the reaction at the middle support.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,8,0" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;C,carrello" quote="A,B,4 m;B,C,4 m" frecce="4.45,0.12,4.45,-0.32,s_B" titolo="Only the middle support settles, by 5 mm"}

:::soluzione
$R_B=-6\,\mathrm{kN}$, directed downward.
:::
