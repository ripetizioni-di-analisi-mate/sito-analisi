A beam of length $6\,\mathrm m$ and constant flexural rigidity has a fixed end at $A$ and a bilateral vertical restraint at $B$. A counterclockwise couple of $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ acts at $B$. Calculate the reaction at $B$, taking upward as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" coppie="B,1" etichette="5.45,0.52,C" quote="A,B,6 m" titolo="Counterclockwise couple and a bilateral vertical restraint"}

:::soluzione
$R_B=-6\,\mathrm{kN}$, directed downward.
:::
