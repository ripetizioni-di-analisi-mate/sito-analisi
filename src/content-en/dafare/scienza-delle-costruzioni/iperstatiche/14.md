A cantilever propped at its tip has span $5\,\mathrm m$ and $EI=10\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. A temperature gradient uniform along the beam would produce a constant curvature $v_T^{\prime\prime}=2\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$ in the absence of the prop, with $v$ positive downward. Find the vertical reaction of the stationary prop.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" quote="A,B,5 m" etichette="2.5,0.49,ΔT_sup;2.5,-0.37,ΔT_inf" titolo="Beam subjected to a temperature gradient through its depth"}

:::soluzione
$R_B=6\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward.
:::
