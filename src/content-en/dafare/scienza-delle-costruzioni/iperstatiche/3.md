A bar fixed at $A$ consists of two segments in series. The first has length $2\,\mathrm m$ and $EA_1=12\,000\,\mathrm{kN}$. The second has length $1\,\mathrm m$ and $EA_2=15\,000\,\mathrm{kN}$. A tensile force of $30\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at tip $C$. Calculate the displacement of $C$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,0;C,3,0" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,incastro,180" forze="C,0.7,0,P" quote="A,B,2 m;B,C,1 m" titolo="Segments AB and BC in series"}

:::soluzione
$u_C=7\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right.
:::
