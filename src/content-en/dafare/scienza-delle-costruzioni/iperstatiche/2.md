A cantilever of length $4\,\mathrm m$ with $EI=8\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ carries a uniform downward load $9\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Calculate the tip rotation, taking counterclockwise as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" carichi="A,B,0.55,0.55,q" quote="A,B,4 m" titolo="Uniform downward load"}

:::soluzione
$\varphi_B=-0.012\,\mathrm{rad}$.
:::
