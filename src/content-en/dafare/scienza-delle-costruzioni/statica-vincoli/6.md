On the horizontal beam shown, the distance from $A$ to $B$ is $4\,\mathrm{m}$. The beam is fixed at $A$. Between coordinates $0\,\mathrm{m}$ and $4\,\mathrm{m}$, a downward load has uniform intensity $5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. A clockwise couple of magnitude $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ acts at $B$. Find the external reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="0 0,4 0,4 m" carichi="0 0,4 0,0.75,0.75,q" coppie="B,-1," etichette="4.36,0.32,M_a" titolo="Fixed support and loads on the cantilever"}

:::soluzione

$$
H_{A}=0\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad V_{A}=20\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad M_{A}=46\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

:::
