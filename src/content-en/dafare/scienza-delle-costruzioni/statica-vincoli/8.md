On the horizontal beam shown, the distance from $A$ to $B$ is $4\,\mathrm{m}$. There is a pin support at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. The overhang beyond $B$ ends at $D$ and has length $3\,\mathrm{m}$. The supports can resist force in either direction. A downward force of $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at coordinate $1\,\mathrm{m}$ measured from $A$. Between coordinates $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and $7\,\mathrm{m}$, a downward load has uniform intensity $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Find the external reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;D,7,0" aste="A-B;B-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="0 0,1 0,1 m;1 0,4 0,3 m;4 0,7 0,3 m" forze="1 0,0,-1,P_1" carichi="4 0,7 0,0.75,0.75,q" titolo="Span, overhang, and applied loads"}

:::soluzione

$$
H_{A}=0\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad V_{A}=\frac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad V_{B}=\frac{37}{2}\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

:::
