On the horizontal beam shown, the distance from $A$ to $B$ is $8\,\mathrm{m}$. There is a pin support at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. A force with components $7\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward acts at coordinate $3\,\mathrm{m}$ measured from $A$. Find the external reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="0 0,3 0,3 m;3 0,8 0,5 m" forze="3 0,0.4375,-1,P_1" titolo="Supports and concentrated loads"}

:::soluzione

$$
H_{A}=-7\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad V_{A}=10\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad V_{B}=6\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

:::
