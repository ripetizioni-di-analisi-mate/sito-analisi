On the horizontal beam shown, the distance from $A$ to $B$ is $5\,\mathrm{m}$. The beam is fixed at $A$. A force with components $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left and $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward acts at coordinate $4\,\mathrm{m}$ measured from $A$. Find the external reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="0 0,4 0,4 m;4 0,5 0,1 m" forze="4 0,-0.6,-1,P_1" titolo="Fixed support and loads on the cantilever"}

:::soluzione

$$
H_{A}=6\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad V_{A}=10\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad M_{A}=40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

:::
