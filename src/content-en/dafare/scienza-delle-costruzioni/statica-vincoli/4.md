On the horizontal beam shown, the distance from $A$ to $B$ is $10\,\mathrm{m}$. There is a pin support at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. Between coordinates $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $8\,\mathrm{m}$, the intensity of a downward vertical load varies linearly from $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ to $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Find the external reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,10,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="0 0,2 0,2 m;2 0,8 0,6 m;8 0,10 0,2 m" carichi="2 0,8 0,0.25,0.75,q" titolo="Distributed load and beam supports"}

:::soluzione

$$
H_{A}=0\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad V_{A}=\frac{54}{5}\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad V_{B}=\frac{66}{5}\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

:::
