Frame $ACD$ is fixed at $A=(0,0)$. The coordinates of $C$ and $D$, in meters, are $(0,3)$ and $(5,3)$. A force with components $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward acts at point $(5,3)$. Find the external reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,0,3;D,5,3" aste="A-C;C-D" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="A,C,3 m;C,D,5 m,0.8" forze="5 3,0.5,-1,P_1" titolo="Frame geometry and loading"}

:::soluzione

$$
H_{A}=-4\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad V_{A}=8\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad M_{A}=52\,\mathrm{kN\,m}
$$

:::
