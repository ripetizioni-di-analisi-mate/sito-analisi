On the horizontal beam shown, the distance from $A$ to $B$ is $9\,\mathrm{m}$. There is a pin support at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. Between coordinates $0\,\mathrm{m}$ and $6\,\mathrm{m}$, the intensity of a downward vertical load varies linearly from $12\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ to $0\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Find the external reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,9,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="0 0,6 0,6 m;6 0,9 0,3 m" carichi="0 0,6 0,0.75,0,q" titolo="Distributed load and beam supports"}

:::soluzione

$$
H_{A}=0\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad V_{A}=28\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad V_{B}=8\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

:::
