On the horizontal beam shown, the distance from $A$ to $B$ is $6\,\mathrm{m}$. There is a pin support at $A$. The unit normal to the roller surface at $B$ is $\left(\frac{3}{5},\frac{4}{5}\right)$. A force with components $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left and $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward acts at coordinate $3\,\mathrm{m}$ measured from $A$. Find the external reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,233.1301" quote="0 0,3 0,3 m;3 0,6 0,3 m" forze="3 0,-0.125,-1,P_1" titolo="Inclined roller and load position"}

:::soluzione

$$
H_{A}=-4\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad V_{A}=8\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad R_{B}=10\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

:::
