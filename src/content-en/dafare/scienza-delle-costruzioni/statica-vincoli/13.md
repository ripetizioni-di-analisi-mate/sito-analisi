On the horizontal beam shown, the distance from $A$ to $B$ is $9\,\mathrm{m}$. The beam is fixed at $A$, with an internal hinge at $C$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. Segment $AC$ has length $5\,\mathrm{m}$. A downward force of $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at coordinate $8\,\mathrm{m}$ measured from $A$. Find the external reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,9,0;C,5,0" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" quote="0 0,5 0,5 m;5 0,8 0,3 m;8 0,9 0,1 m" cerniere="C" forze="8 0,0,-1,P_1" titolo="Internal hinge and span dimensions"}

:::soluzione

$$
H_{A}=0\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad V_{A}=4\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad M_{A}=20\,\mathrm{kN\,m},\qquad V_{B}=12\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

:::
