On the horizontal beam shown, the distance from $A$ to $B$ is $8\,\mathrm{m}$. There is a pin support at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. A downward force of $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at coordinate $2\,\mathrm{m}$ measured from $A$. Between coordinates $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and $8\,\mathrm{m}$, a downward load has uniform intensity $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Find the external reactions.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="0 0,2 0,2 m;2 0,4 0,2 m;4 0,8 0,4 m" forze="2 0,0,-1,P_1" carichi="4 0,8 0,0.75,0.75,q" titolo="Distributed load and beam supports"}

:::soluzione

$$
H_{A}=0\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad V_{A}=\frac{19}{2}\,\mathrm{kN},\qquad V_{B}=\frac{17}{2}\,\mathrm{kN}
$$

:::
