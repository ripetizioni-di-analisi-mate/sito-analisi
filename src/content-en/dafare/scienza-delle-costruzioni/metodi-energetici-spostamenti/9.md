A prismatic beam of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ is fixed at $A$ and has a vertical prop at $B$ that does not settle. The only applied force is a downward load of $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ at midspan. Neglecting the shear strain energy, calculate the vertical reaction at $B$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" quote="A,B,4 m" forze="2 0,0,-0.8,P" titolo="Propped beam with a point load at midspan"}

:::soluzione
$V_B=5\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward.
:::
