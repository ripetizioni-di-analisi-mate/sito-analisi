A continuous beam has two spans $AB$ and $BC$, each of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$. The beam is fixed at $A$ and $C$. The bilateral support at $B$ prevents vertical displacement, and the beam remains continuous through it. The flexural rigidities are $EI_{AB}=6\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ and $EI_{BC}=3\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. A counterclockwise couple of $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ acts at $B$. Calculate the rotation at $B$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello;C,incastro,0" quote="A,B,3 m;B,C,3 m" coppie="B,1,C_B" titolo="Continuous beam with a couple at the intermediate support"}

:::soluzione
$\varphi_B=0.002\,\mathrm{rad}$ counterclockwise.
:::
