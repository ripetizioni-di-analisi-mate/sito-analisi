A prismatic bar is restrained axially at $A$. Sections $B$ and $C$ are distances $1$ and $3\,\mathrm{m}$, respectively, from $A$. A rightward axial force of $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$ produces a rightward displacement of $0.5\,\mathrm{mm}$ at $B$. Find the displacement at $C$ when the only load is a rightward force of $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $B$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,1,0;C,3,0" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="A,B,1 m;B,C,2 m" forze="C,0.55,0,10 kN" titolo="First load case with the load at C"}

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,1,0;C,3,0" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="A,B,1 m;B,C,2 m" forze="B,0.5,0,20 kN" titolo="Second load case with the load at B"}

:::soluzione
$u_C=1.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right.
:::
