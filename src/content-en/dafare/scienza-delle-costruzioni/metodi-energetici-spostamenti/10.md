A beam of span $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and constant $EI$ has a fixed support at $A$ and a vertical support at $B$ with no settlement. A uniform load of intensity $8\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts downward. Neglecting the shear strain energy, calculate the vertical reaction at $B$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" quote="A,B,4 m" carichi="A,B,0.54,0.54,q" titolo="Propped beam under a uniform load"}

:::soluzione
$V_B=12\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward.
:::
