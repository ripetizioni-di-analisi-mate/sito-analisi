The cantilever has length $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $EI=3\,200\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. A downward vertical force of $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ and a clockwise couple of $4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ act at the tip. Calculate the magnitude of the tip rotation using the Euler–Bernoulli beam model.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="A,B,2 m" forze="B,0,-0.58,P" coppie="B,-1,C" titolo="Vertical force and clockwise couple at the tip"}

:::soluzione
$|\varphi_B|=0.0075\,\mathrm{rad}$.
:::
