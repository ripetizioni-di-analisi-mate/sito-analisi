A counterclockwise couple of $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ acts at the free end of a cantilever of span $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and flexural rigidity $EI=1\,600\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. Find the free-end rotation, taking counterclockwise as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="A,B,2 m" coppie="B,1,C" titolo="Counterclockwise couple at the free end"}

:::soluzione
$\varphi_B=0.010\,\mathrm{rad}$ counterclockwise.
:::
