The principal stresses in descending order are $\sigma_1=140\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_2=20\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\sigma_3=20\,\mathrm{MPa}$. The uniaxial yield strength is $f_y=300\,\mathrm{MPa}$. All stresses are scaled by one load multiplier. Calculate the factor of safety using the von Mises criterion.

::struttura{poligoni="-1,-1 1,-1 1,1 -1,1" frecce="-1.03,0,-1.8,0,;1.03,0,1.8,0,σ_1;0,-1.03,0,-1.8,;0,1.03,0,1.8,σ_3" etichette="-1.9,2,σ_2 = 20 MPa" titolo="State of stress in principal coordinates"}

:::soluzione

$$
n=\frac{5}{2}
$$

:::
