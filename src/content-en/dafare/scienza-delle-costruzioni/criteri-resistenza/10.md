The principal stresses in descending order are $\sigma_1=-10\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_2=-45\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\sigma_3=-84\,\mathrm{MPa}$. The tensile strength is $f_t=36\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and the magnitude of the compressive strength is $f_c=120\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Calculate the Rankine utilization ratio.

::struttura{poligoni="-1,-1 1,-1 1,1 -1,1" frecce="-1.8,0,-1.03,0,;1.8,0,1.03,0,σ_1;0,-1.8,0,-1.03,;0,1.8,0,1.03,σ_3" etichette="-1.9,2,σ_2 = -45 MPa" titolo="State of stress in principal coordinates"}

:::soluzione

$$
\eta_R=\frac{7}{10}
$$

:::
