The principal stresses in descending order are $\sigma_1=75\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_2=0\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\sigma_3=-75\,\mathrm{MPa}$. The same stress $50\,\mathrm{MPa}$ is added to each principal component. Calculate the new von Mises equivalent stress.

::struttura{poligoni="-1,-1 1,-1 1,1 -1,1" frecce="-1.03,0,-1.8,0,;1.03,0,1.8,0,σ_1;0,-1.8,0,-1.03,;0,1.8,0,1.03,σ_3" etichette="-1.9,2,σ_2 = 0 MPa" titolo="State of stress in principal coordinates"}

:::soluzione

$$
\sigma_{\mathrm{eq,V}}^{\,*}=75 \sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{MPa}
$$

:::
