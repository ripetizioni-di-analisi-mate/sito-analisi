The element is in plane stress with $\sigma_x=80\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_y=-10\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\tau_{xy}=-60\,\mathrm{MPa}$. First find the principal stresses, then calculate the von Mises equivalent stress.

::struttura{poligoni="-1,-1 1,-1 1,1 -1,1" frecce="-1.03,0,-1.8,0,;1.03,0,1.8,0,σ_x;0,-1.8,0,-1.03,;0,1.8,0,1.03,σ_y;1.18,0.55,1.18,-0.55,τ_xy;-1.18,-0.55,-1.18,0.55,;0.55,1.18,-0.55,1.18,;-0.55,-1.18,0.55,-1.18," titolo="Stress components in the xy plane"}

:::soluzione

$$
\sigma_{\mathrm{eq,V}}=10 \sqrt{181}\,\mathrm{MPa}
$$

:::
