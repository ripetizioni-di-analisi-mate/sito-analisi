The principal stresses in descending order are $\sigma_1=18\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_2=3\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\sigma_3=-32\,\mathrm{MPa}$. The tensile strength is $f_t=30\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and the magnitude of the compressive strength is $f_c=160\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Calculate the Rankine utilization ratio.

::struttura{poligoni="-1,-1 1,-1 1,1 -1,1" frecce="-1.03,0,-1.8,0,;1.03,0,1.8,0,σ_1;0,-1.8,0,-1.03,;0,1.8,0,1.03,σ_3" etichette="-1.9,2,σ_2 = 3 MPa" titolo="State of stress in principal coordinates"}

:::soluzione

$$
\eta_R=\frac{3}{5}
$$

:::
