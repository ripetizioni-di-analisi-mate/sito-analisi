The principal stresses in descending order are $\sigma_1=24\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_2=12\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\sigma_3=4\,\mathrm{MPa}$. The tensile strength is $f_t=40\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and the magnitude of the compressive strength is $f_c=120\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Calculate the Coulomb–Mohr utilization ratio.

::struttura{poligoni="-1,-1 1,-1 1,1 -1,1" frecce="-1.03,0,-1.8,0,;1.03,0,1.8,0,σ_1;0,-1.03,0,-1.8,;0,1.03,0,1.8,σ_3" etichette="-1.9,2,σ_2 = 12 MPa" titolo="State of stress in principal coordinates"}

:::soluzione

$$
\eta_{CM}=\frac{3}{5}
$$

:::
