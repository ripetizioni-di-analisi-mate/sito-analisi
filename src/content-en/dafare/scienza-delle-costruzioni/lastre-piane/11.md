A square plate simply supported on all four edges has side $a=2\,\mathrm m$ and flexural rigidity $D=4\,\mathrm{MN\,m}$. The load is $q=\pi^4\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi x}{a}\right)\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi y}{a}\right)\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$. Calculate the downward deflection at the center.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 4,0 4,4 0,4" tratteggi="-0.2,0 -0.2,4;4.2,0 4.2,4;0,-0.2 4,-0.2;0,4.2 4,4.2" frecce="0,0,2,0,x;0,0,0,2,y" quote="0 0,4 0,a;4 0,4 4,a" punti="2,2,C" riferimento=0 titolo="Square plate simply supported on all four sides"}

:::soluzione
$w_C=1\,\mathrm{mm}$.
:::
