A plate strip in cylindrical bending is clamped at $x=0$ and free at $x=L=3\,\mathrm m$. The uniform load acts downward with intensity $q=5\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$. Find the moment per unit width $M_x(0)$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" carichi="A,B,0.34,0.34,q" riferimento=0 frecce="-0.45,-0.85,0.05,-0.85,x;-0.45,-0.85,-0.45,-1.25,z" quote="A,B,3 m" titolo="Strip clamped at A and free at B"}

:::soluzione
$M_x(0)=-22.5\,\mathrm{kN}$.
:::
