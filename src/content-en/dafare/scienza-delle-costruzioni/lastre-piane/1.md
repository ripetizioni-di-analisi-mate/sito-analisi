A plate of thickness $h=5\,\mathrm{mm}$ has a stress $\sigma_x=60\,\mathrm{MPa}$ that is uniform through its thickness. Find $N_x$ per unit length, taking tension as positive.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 6,0 6,3.4 0,3.4" frecce="0,0,2,0,x;0,0,0,1.7,y" forze="0 1.7,-0.85,0,σ_x;6 1.7,0.85,0,σ_x" riferimento=0 titolo="Plate under uniform tension in the x direction"}

:::soluzione
$N_x=300\,\mathrm{kN/m}$.
:::
