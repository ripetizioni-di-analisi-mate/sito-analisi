A plate in cylindrical bending is simply supported along $x=0$ and $x=L$, with $L=6\,\mathrm m$. The uniform load acts downward with intensity $q=4\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$. Find the bending moment per unit width $M_x$ at midspan.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.65,0.65,q" riferimento=0 frecce="-0.45,-0.85,0.05,-0.85,x;-0.45,-0.85,-0.45,-1.25,z" quote="A,B,6 m" titolo="Strip under cylindrical bending and a uniform load"}

:::soluzione
$M_x=18\,\mathrm{kN}$.
:::
