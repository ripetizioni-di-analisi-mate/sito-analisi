At a point in a plate, measurements give $w_{,xx}=-0.003\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$ and $w_{,yy}=-0.001\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$. Given $D=8\,\mathrm{MN\,m}$ and $\nu=\dfrac14$, determine the moment per unit length $M_x$.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 6,0 6,3.4 0,3.4" frecce="0,0,2,0,x;0,0,0,1.7,y" punti="3,1.7,P" etichette="4,1.35,w_xx;2.6,2.25,w_yy" riferimento=0 titolo="Normal derivatives at P"}

:::soluzione
$M_x=26\,\mathrm{kN}$.
:::
