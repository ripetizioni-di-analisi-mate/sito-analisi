A square plate simply supported on all four edges is subjected separately to two modal loads of equal amplitude, with indices $(2,1)$ and $(1,1)$. Calculate the ratio $\dfrac{W_{21}}{W_{11}}$ of the positive deflection amplitudes.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 4,0 4,4 0,4" tratteggi="-0.2,0 -0.2,4;4.2,0 4.2,4;0,-0.2 4,-0.2;0,4.2 4,4.2" frecce="0,0,2,0,x;0,0,0,2,y" quote="0 0,4 0,a;4 0,4 4,a" punti="2,2,C" riferimento=0 titolo="Square plate simply supported on all four sides"}

:::soluzione
$\dfrac{W_{21}}{W_{11}}=\dfrac4{25}$.
:::
