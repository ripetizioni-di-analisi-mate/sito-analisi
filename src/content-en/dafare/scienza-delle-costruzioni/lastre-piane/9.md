In a region of the plate, $M_x(x)=(30-5x)\,\mathrm{kN}$, with $x$ in meters. The twisting moment $M_{xy}$ is zero. Calculate the transverse shear resultant $Q_x$ per unit length.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 6,0 6,3.4 0,3.4" frecce="0,0,2,0,x;0,0,0,1.7,y" tratteggi="1.6,0 1.6,3.4;4.3,0 4.3,3.4" etichette="3,2.45,M_x(x)" riferimento=0 titolo="Sections normal to x in the moment field"}

:::soluzione
$Q_x=-5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$.
:::
