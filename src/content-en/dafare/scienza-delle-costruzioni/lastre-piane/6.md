At a point in a plate with flexural rigidity $D=8\,\mathrm{MN\,m}$ and Poisson ratio $\nu=\dfrac14$, the mixed derivative is $w_{,xy}=0.002\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$. Find $M_{xy}$ using the convention $M_{xy}=\int z\tau_{xy}\,\mathrm dz$.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 6,0 6,3.4 0,3.4" frecce="0,0,2,0,x;0,0,0,1.7,y" punti="3,1.7,P" etichette="4,2.2,w_xy" riferimento=0 titolo="Mixed derivative at P"}

:::soluzione
$M_{xy}=-12\,\mathrm{kN}$.
:::
