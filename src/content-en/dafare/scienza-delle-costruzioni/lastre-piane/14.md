A rectangular plate has sides $a=\pi\,\mathrm m$ in the $x$ direction and $b=2\pi\,\mathrm m$ in the $y$ direction. Its deflection is a single Navier mode $(1,1)$ with amplitude $W_{11}=1\,\mathrm{mm}$. Given $D=4\,\mathrm{MN\,m}$ and $\nu=\dfrac14$, calculate $M_x$ at the center.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 3,0 3,6 0,6" tratteggi="-0.2,0 -0.2,6;3.2,0 3.2,6;0,-0.2 3,-0.2;0,6.2 3,6.2" frecce="0,0,1.5,0,x;0,0,0,2,y" quote="0 0,3 0,a;3 0,3 6,b" punti="1.5,3,C" riferimento=0 titolo="Rectangular plate with side b parallel to y"}

:::soluzione
$M_x=4.25\,\mathrm{kN}$.
:::
