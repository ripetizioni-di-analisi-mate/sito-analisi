---
titolo: Equation with substitution and a solution to be discarded
teoria: [sostituzione-esponenziale]
parole: [exponential equation, substitution, discarded solution]
---

Solve the equation
$$
4^{x} + 2^{x} - 6 = 0 .
$$

:::soluzione
$x = 1$: with $t = 2^{x}$ we obtain $t^{2} + t - 6 = 0$, that is, $t = 2$ or $t = -3$; the solution $t = -3$ must be discarded because $t > 0$.
:::
