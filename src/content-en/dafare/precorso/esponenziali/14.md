---
titolo: Equation with a parameter and two solutions
teoria: [sostituzione-esponenziale, funzione-esponenziale]
parole: [exponential equation with a parameter, substitution, number of solutions]
---

Determine the values of the real parameter $k$ for which the equation
$$
4^{x} - 2^{x} + k = 0
$$
has two distinct solutions.

:::soluzione
$0 < k < \dfrac{1}{4}$: with $t = 2^{x}$ the equation $t^{2} - t + k = 0$ must have two distinct positive solutions, hence discriminant $1 - 4k > 0$ and product of the solutions $k > 0$.
:::
