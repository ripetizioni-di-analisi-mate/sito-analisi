Solve the inequality
$$
4^{x} - 2^{x+2} + 3 \leq 0 .
$$

:::soluzione
$0 \leq x \leq \log_2 3$: with $t = 2^{x}$ we obtain $t^{2} - 4t + 3 \leq 0$, that is, $1 \leq t \leq 3$.
:::
