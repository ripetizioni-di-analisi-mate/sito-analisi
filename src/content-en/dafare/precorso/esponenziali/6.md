---
titolo: Equation with a coefficient in front of the power
teoria: [esponenziale-uguale-k]
parole: [exponential equation, logarithm, coefficient]
---

Solve the equation
$$
7 \cdot 3^{x} = 4 .
$$

:::soluzione
$x = \log_3 \dfrac{4}{7}$: dividing both sides by $7$ gives $3^{x} = \dfrac{4}{7}$.
:::
