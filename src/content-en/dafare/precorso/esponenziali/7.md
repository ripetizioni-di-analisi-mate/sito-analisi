---
titolo: Equation with substitution and two solutions
teoria: [sostituzione-esponenziale]
parole: [exponential equation, substitution, second degree]
---

Solve the equation
$$
9^{x} - 10 \cdot 3^{x} + 9 = 0 .
$$

:::soluzione
$x = 0$ and $x = 2$: with $t = 3^{x}$ we obtain $t^{2} - 10t + 9 = 0$, that is, $t = 1$ or $t = 9$.
:::
