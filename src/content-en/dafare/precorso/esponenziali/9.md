---
titolo: Equation with a power with a negative exponent
teoria: [sostituzione-esponenziale, regole-delle-potenze]
parole: [exponential equation, substitution, negative exponent]
---

Solve the equation
$$
2^{x} + 2^{2-x} = 5 .
$$

:::soluzione
$x = 0$ and $x = 2$: since $2^{2-x} = \dfrac{4}{2^{x}}$, with $t = 2^{x}$ we obtain $t^{2} - 5t + 4 = 0$, that is, $t = 1$ or $t = 4$.
:::
