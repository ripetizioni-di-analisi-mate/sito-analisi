titolo: Equation with bases to be reduced to the same base
teoria: [stessa-base, regole-delle-potenze]

---

Solve the equation
$$
\left(\frac{1}{4}\right)^{x} = 8^{\,x-1} .
$$

:::soluzione
$x = \dfrac{3}{5}$: both sides can be written as powers of $2$ and the equation is equivalent to $-2x = 3x - 3$.
:::
