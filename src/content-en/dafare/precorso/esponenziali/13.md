Solve the inequality
$$
\frac{2^{x} - 8}{4^{x} - 2} \geq 0 .
$$

:::soluzione
$x < \dfrac{1}{2}$ or $x \geq 3$: the numerator is nonnegative for $x \geq 3$ and the denominator is positive for $x > \dfrac{1}{2}$.
:::
