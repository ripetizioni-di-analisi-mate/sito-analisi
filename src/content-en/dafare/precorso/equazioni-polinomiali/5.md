---

Solve the equation
$$
x^2 - 7x + 12 = 0
$$
using the sum and product of the roots, and factor the trinomial.

:::soluzione
The solutions are $x = 3$ and $x = 4$, so $x^2 - 7x + 12 = (x - 3)(x - 4)$.
:::
