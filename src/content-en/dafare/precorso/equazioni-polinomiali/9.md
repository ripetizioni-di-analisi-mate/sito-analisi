teoria: [biquadratiche]

Solve the equations
$$
x^4 - 10x^2 + 9 = 0 \qquad \text{and} \qquad x^4 - 3x^2 - 4 = 0 .
$$

:::soluzione
The first has solutions $x = \pm 1$ and $x = \pm 3$; the second $x = \pm 2$, since the value $t = -1$ produces no real solutions.
:::
