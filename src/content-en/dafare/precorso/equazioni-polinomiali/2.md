---
titolo: Complete second-degree equations
teoria: [equazioni-secondo-grado, discriminante]
parole: [second-degree equation, quadratic formula]
---

Solve the equations
$$
x^2 - 7x + 10 = 0 \qquad \text{and} \qquad 2x^2 + 5x - 3 = 0 .
$$

:::soluzione
The first has solutions $x = 2$ and $x = 5$; the second $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and $x = -3$.
:::
