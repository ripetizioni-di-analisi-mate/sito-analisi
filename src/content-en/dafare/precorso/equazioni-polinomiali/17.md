---
titolo: Quadratic equation with a parameter
teoria: [discriminante, equazioni-secondo-grado, segno-della-parabola]
parole: [parametric equation, discriminant, parameter, two distinct solutions]
---

Determine for which values of the real parameter $k$ the equation
$$
x^2 + 2\,(k - 1)\,x + k + 5 = 0
$$
has two distinct real solutions.

:::soluzione
$\dfrac{\Delta}{4} = k^2 - 3k - 4 > 0$, so $k < -1$ or $k > 4$.
:::
