Solve the inequality
$$
\frac{x + 1}{x - 1} \geq \frac{x - 1}{x + 1} .
$$

:::soluzione
The inequality is equivalent to $\dfrac{4x}{(x - 1)(x + 1)} \geq 0$, with solutions $-1 < x \leq 0$ or $x > 1$.
:::
