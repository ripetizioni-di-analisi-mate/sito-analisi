---
titolo: Inequalities with zero or negative discriminant
teoria: [segno-della-parabola, discriminante]
parole: [second-degree inequality, discriminant]
---

Solve the inequalities
$$
x^2 - 6x + 9 > 0 \qquad \text{and} \qquad x^2 + 2x + 2 < 0 .
$$

:::soluzione
The solutions of the first are all $x \neq 3$; the second has no solutions.
:::
