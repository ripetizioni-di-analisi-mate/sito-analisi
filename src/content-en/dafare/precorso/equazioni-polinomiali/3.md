Solve the equations
$$
5x^2 - 20 = 0 \qquad \text{and} \qquad 3x^2 + 7x = 0 .
$$

:::soluzione
The first has solutions $x = \pm 2$; the second $x = 0$ and $x = -\dfrac{7}{3}$.
:::
