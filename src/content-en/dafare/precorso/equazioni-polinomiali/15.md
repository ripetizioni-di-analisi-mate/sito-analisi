Solve the equation
$$
\frac{x}{x + 1} - \frac{1}{x - 1} = \frac{2}{x^2 - 1} .
$$

:::soluzione
With the domain restrictions $x \neq \pm 1$, the numerator $x^2 - 2x - 3$ vanishes at $x = 3$ and at $x = -1$; the value $-1$ is discarded, so the only solution is $x = 3$.
:::
