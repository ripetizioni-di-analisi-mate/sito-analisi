Solve the inequality and the system
$$
\frac{x + 2}{x - 3} \leq 0
\qquad \text{and} \qquad
\begin{cases}
x^2 - 4 \leq 0 \\[2pt]
2x + 1 > 0
\end{cases}
$$

:::soluzione
The rational inequality has solutions $-2 \leq x < 3$; the system $-\dfrac{1}{2} < x \leq 2$.
:::
