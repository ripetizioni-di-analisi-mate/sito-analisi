---
titolo: Second-degree inequalities with two roots
teoria: [segno-della-parabola, discriminante]
parole: [second-degree inequality, parabola]
---

Solve the inequalities
$$
x^2 - 4x + 3 > 0 \qquad \text{and} \qquad -x^2 + x + 6 \geq 0 .
$$

:::soluzione
The first has solutions $x < 1$ or $x > 3$; the second $-2 \leq x \leq 3$.
:::
