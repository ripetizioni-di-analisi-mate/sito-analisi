---
titolo: Zero discriminant and negative discriminant
teoria: [equazioni-secondo-grado, discriminante]
parole: [discriminant, double solution]
---

Solve the equations
$$
9x^2 - 6x + 1 = 0 \qquad \text{and} \qquad x^2 - 3x + 5 = 0 .
$$

:::soluzione
The first has the double solution $x = \dfrac{1}{3}$; the second has $\Delta = -11 < 0$ and no real solution.
:::
