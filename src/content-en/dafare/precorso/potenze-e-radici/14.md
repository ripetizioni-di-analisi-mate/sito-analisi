---
titolo: Comparison between a sum of roots and a root
teoria: [confronto-tra-radici, proprieta-delle-radici]
parole: [comparison, sum of roots, squaring]
---

Determine, without using approximate values, which of $\sqrt{3} + \sqrt{5}$ and $\sqrt{15}$ is greater.

:::soluzione
$\sqrt{3} + \sqrt{5} > \sqrt{15}$: the squares are $8 + 2\sqrt{15}$ and $15$, and $2\sqrt{15} > 7$ because $60 > 49$.
:::
