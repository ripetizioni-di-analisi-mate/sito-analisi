---
titolo: Domain conditions with two roots
teoria: [radici]
parole: [domain conditions, roots, system]
---

Determine for which real $x$ the following expression is defined:
$$
\sqrt{x - 4} + \frac{1}{\sqrt{10 - x}} .
$$

:::soluzione
$$
\begin{cases}
x - 4 \geq 0 \\
10 - x > 0
\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad 4 \leq x < 10 .
$$
:::
