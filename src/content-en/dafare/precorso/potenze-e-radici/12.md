Rationalize
$$
\frac{1}{\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{3} - \sqrt{5}} .
$$

:::soluzione
$\dfrac{2\sqrt{3} + 3\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{30}}{12}$.
:::
