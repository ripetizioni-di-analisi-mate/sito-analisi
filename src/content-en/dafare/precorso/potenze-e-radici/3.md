---
titolo: Simplifying an algebraic expression with powers
teoria: [proprieta-delle-potenze, potenze-a-esponente-intero]
parole: [powers, algebraic expression, negative exponent]
---

Simplify, for $a, b \neq 0$,
$$
\left(\frac{a^2 b}{a b^3}\right)^{3} .
$$

:::soluzione
$\left(a\, b^{-2}\right)^3 = a^3 b^{-6} = \dfrac{a^3}{b^6}$.
:::
