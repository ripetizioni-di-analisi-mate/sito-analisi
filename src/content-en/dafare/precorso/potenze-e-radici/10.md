---
titolo: Ordering roots with different indices
teoria: [confronto-tra-radici, proprieta-delle-radici]
parole: [ordering, roots, common index]
---

Order from smallest to largest
$$
\sqrt[3]{2}, \qquad \sqrt[4]{3}, \qquad \sqrt{2} .
$$

:::soluzione
With common index $12$: $\sqrt[3]{2} = \sqrt[12]{16}$, $\sqrt[4]{3} = \sqrt[12]{27}$, $\sqrt{2} = \sqrt[12]{64}$, therefore $\sqrt[3]{2} < \sqrt[4]{3} < \sqrt{2}$.
:::
