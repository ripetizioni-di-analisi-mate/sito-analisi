---
titolo: Root of a square with a condition on the variable
teoria: [proprieta-delle-radici, radici]
parole: [root of a square, absolute value, literal radicals]
---

Simplify, for $x < \dfrac{1}{2}$,
$$
\sqrt{4x^{2} - 4x + 1} + 2x .
$$

:::soluzione
$1$, because $\sqrt{(2x - 1)^{2}} = |2x - 1| = 1 - 2x$ for $x < \dfrac{1}{2}$.
:::
