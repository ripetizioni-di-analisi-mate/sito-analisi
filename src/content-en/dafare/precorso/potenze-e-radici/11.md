---
titolo: Product and quotient of roots with different indices
teoria: [esponente-razionale, proprieta-delle-potenze]
parole: [rational exponent, roots with different indices, simplification]
---

Simplify, for $a > 0$,
$$
\frac{\sqrt{a} \cdot \sqrt[3]{a}}{\sqrt[6]{a}}
$$
and write the result with a single root.

:::soluzione
$a^{\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{3} - \frac{1}{6}} = a^{\frac{2}{3}} = \sqrt[3]{a^{2}}$.
:::
