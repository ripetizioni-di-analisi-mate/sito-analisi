---
titolo: Inequality with two logarithms and base one half
teoria: [disequazioni-logaritmiche, condizioni-di-esistenza]
parole: [logarithmic inequality, base less than 1, reversed direction]
---

Solve the inequality
$$
\log_{1/2} (x + 1) < \log_{1/2} (2x - 3) .
$$

:::soluzione
$\dfrac{3}{2} < x < 4$: the direction is reversed and gives $x + 1 > 2x - 3$, in a system with the domain condition $2x - 3 > 0$.
:::
