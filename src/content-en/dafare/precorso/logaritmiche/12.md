Solve the inequality
$$
\log_{\frac{1}{2}} (x + 1) < \log_{\frac{1}{2}} (2x - 3) .
$$

:::soluzione
$\dfrac{3}{2} < x < 4$: reversing the inequality gives $x + 1 > 2x - 3$, together with the domain restriction $2x - 3 > 0$.
:::
