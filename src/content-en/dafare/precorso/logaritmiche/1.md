---
titolo: Equation with one logarithm on each side
teoria: [equazioni-logaritmiche, condizioni-di-esistenza]
parole: [logarithmic equation, same base, domain conditions]
---

Solve the equation
$$
\log_2 (x + 5) = \log_2 (3x - 1) .
$$

:::soluzione
$x = 3$; the domain conditions require $x > \dfrac{1}{3}$ and are satisfied.
:::
