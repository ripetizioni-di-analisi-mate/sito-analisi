---
titolo: Equation with a solution to discard
teoria: [equazioni-logaritmiche, condizioni-di-esistenza]
parole: [logarithmic equation, domain conditions, discarded solution]
---

Solve the equation
$$
\log_5 (x^{2} - 9) = \log_5 (3x + 9) .
$$

:::soluzione
$x = 6$: the equality of the arguments gives $x = 6$ or $x = -3$, but for $x = -3$ the arguments vanish and the logarithms do not exist.
:::
