Solve the equation
$$
\log_5 (x^{2} - 9) = \log_5 (3x + 9) .
$$

:::soluzione
$x = 6$: the equality of the arguments gives $x = 6$ or $x = -3$, but for $x = -3$ the arguments vanish and the logarithms are undefined.
:::
