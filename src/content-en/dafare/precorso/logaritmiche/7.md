Solve the equation
$$
\log_3 (x + 2) - \log_3 (x - 4) = 1 .
$$

:::soluzione
$x = 7$, from $\dfrac{x + 2}{x - 4} = 3$, with the domain restriction $x > 4$ satisfied.
:::
