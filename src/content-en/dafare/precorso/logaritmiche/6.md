Solve the equation
$$
\log_2 x + \log_2 (x - 6) = 4 .
$$

:::soluzione
$x = 8$: from $x(x - 6) = 16$ we obtain $x = 8$ and $x = -2$, but $x = -2$ does not satisfy the domain conditions.
:::
