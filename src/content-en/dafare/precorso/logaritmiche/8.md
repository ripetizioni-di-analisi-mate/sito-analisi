titolo: Equation by substitution
teoria: [sostituzione-logaritmica]
parole: [logarithmic equation, substitution, quadratic]
---

Solve the equation
$$
(\log_3 x)^{2} - 4 \log_3 x + 3 = 0 .
$$

:::soluzione
$x = 3$ and $x = 27$: with $t = \log_3 x$ we obtain $t^{2} - 4t + 3 = 0$, that is $t = 1$ or $t = 3$.
:::
