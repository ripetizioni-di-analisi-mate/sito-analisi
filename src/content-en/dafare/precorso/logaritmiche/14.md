Solve the inequality
$$
(\log_3 x)^{2} - 3 \log_3 x + 2 > 0 .
$$

:::soluzione
$0 < x < 3 \ \vee \ x > 9$: with $t = \log_3 x$ one obtains $t < 1$ or $t > 2$.
:::
