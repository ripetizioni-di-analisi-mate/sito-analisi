---
titolo: Equation with logarithms to different bases
teoria: [cambio-di-base, logaritmo-uguale-k, condizioni-di-esistenza]
parole: [logarithmic equation, different bases, change of base]
---

Solve the equation
$$
\log_2 x + \log_8 x = 4 .
$$

:::soluzione
$x = 8$, from $\log_8 x = \dfrac{1}{3} \log_2 x$ and hence $\dfrac{4}{3} \log_2 x = 4$.
:::
