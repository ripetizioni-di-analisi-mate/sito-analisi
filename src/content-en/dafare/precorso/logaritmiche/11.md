---
titolo: Inequality with base one third
teoria: [disequazioni-logaritmo-numero, condizioni-di-esistenza]
parole: [logarithmic inequality, base less than 1, conditions of existence]
---

Solve the inequality
$$
\log_{\frac{1}{3}} (x - 2) \geq -1 .
$$

:::soluzione
$2 < x \leq 5$: reversing the inequality gives $x - 2 \leq 3$, together with the domain restriction $x - 2 > 0$.
:::
