---

Solve the inequality
$$
\log_{\frac{1}{3}} (x - 2) \geq -1 .
$$

:::soluzione
$2 < x \leq 5$: reversing the inequality gives $x - 2 \leq 3$, together with the domain restriction $x - 2 > 0$.
:::
