---

Write the equation of the line passing through $P(3, -1)$ and perpendicular to the line of equation $x - 2y + 4 = 0$.

:::soluzione
The given line has slope $\dfrac{1}{2}$, therefore the perpendicular line has slope $-2$: $y = -2x + 5$.
:::
