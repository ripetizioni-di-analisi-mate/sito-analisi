Write, in standard form and in general form, the equation of the circle with center $C(-1, 3)$ and radius $r = 2$.

:::soluzione
$(x + 1)^2 + (y - 3)^2 = 4$, that is $x^2 + y^2 + 2x - 6y + 6 = 0$.
:::
