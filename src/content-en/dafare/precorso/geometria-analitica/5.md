Find the vertex, the axis of symmetry and the intersections with the axes of the parabola
$$
y = x^2 - 6x + 5 .
$$

:::soluzione
Vertex $V(3, -4)$, axis $x = 3$, intersections $(1, 0)$, $(5, 0)$ and $(0, 5)$.
:::
