---
titolo: Lines through the origin tangent to a circle
teoria: [retta-e-circonferenza, distanza-punto-retta, circonferenza]

---

Determine the values of the real parameter $k$ for which the line $y = kx$ is tangent to the circle with equation
$$
(x - 4)^2 + y^2 = 4 .
$$

:::soluzione
$k = \pm\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$.
:::
