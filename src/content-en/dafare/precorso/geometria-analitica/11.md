---
titolo: Recognizing a conic with equal coefficients
teoria: [riconoscere-una-conica, completamento-dei-quadrati, circonferenza]

---

Recognize the conic of equation
$$
x^2 + y^2 + 4x - 2y + 1 = 0
$$
and find its elements.

:::soluzione
The coefficients of $x^2$ and $y^2$ are equal, and completing the squares gives $(x + 2)^2 + (y - 1)^2 = 4$: circle with center $C(-2, 1)$ and radius $r = 2$.
:::
