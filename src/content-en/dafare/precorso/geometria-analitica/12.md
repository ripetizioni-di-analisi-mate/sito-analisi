Identify the conic with equation
$$
4x^2 - y^2 - 8x - 4y - 4 = 0 ,
$$
reduce it to standard form and find its center, vertices and asymptotes.

:::soluzione
The quadratic coefficients have opposite signs, therefore it is a hyperbola. The reduction gives $(x - 1)^2 - \dfrac{(y + 2)^2}{4} = 1$: center $(1, -2)$, vertices $(0, -2)$ and $(2, -2)$, asymptotes $y + 2 = \pm 2(x - 1)$.
:::
