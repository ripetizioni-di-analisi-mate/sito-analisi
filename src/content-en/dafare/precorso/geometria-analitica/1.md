Given $A(-2, 1)$ and $B(4, 9)$, compute the distance $\overline{AB}$ and the midpoint of the segment $AB$.

:::soluzione
$\overline{AB} = \sqrt{36 + 64} = 10$ and $M(1, 5)$.
