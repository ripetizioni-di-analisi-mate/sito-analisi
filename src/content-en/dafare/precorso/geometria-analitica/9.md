Find the semi-axes, vertices and foci of the ellipse of equation
$$
\frac{x^2}{16} + \frac{y^2}{7} = 1 .
$$

:::soluzione
Semi-axes $a = 4$ and $b = \sqrt{7}$, vertices $(\pm 4, 0)$ and $(0, \pm\sqrt{7})$; from $c^2 = 16 - 7 = 9$ the foci are $(\pm 3, 0)$.
:::
