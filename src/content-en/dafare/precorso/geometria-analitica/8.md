Find the center and radius of the circle with equation
$$
x^2 + y^2 - 8x + 6y = 0 .
$$

:::soluzione
Completing the squares gives $(x - 4)^2 + (y + 3)^2 = 25$: center $C(4, -3)$ and radius $r = 5$.
:::
