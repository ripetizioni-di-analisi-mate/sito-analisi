Determine the equation of the parabola with vertical axis passing through the points $(0, -4)$, $(1, 0)$ and $(4, 0)$, and compute its vertex.

:::soluzione
$y = -x^2 + 5x - 4$, with vertex $V\left( \dfrac{5}{2}, \dfrac{9}{4} \right)$.
:::
