Find the vertices, foci and asymptotes of the hyperbola of equation
$$
\frac{x^2}{16} - \frac{y^2}{9} = 1 .
$$

:::soluzione
Vertices $(\pm 4, 0)$; from $c^2 = 16 + 9 = 25$ the foci are $(\pm 5, 0)$; asymptotes $y = \pm\dfrac{3}{4}x$.
:::
