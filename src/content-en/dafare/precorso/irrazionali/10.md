---
titolo: Inequality with a root greater than an expression
teoria: [disequazione-radice-maggiore]
parole: [irrational inequality, union of two systems]
---

Solve the inequality
$$
\sqrt{x + 6} > x .
$$

:::soluzione
$-6 \leq x < 3$: the union of the contributions $-6 \leq x < 0$ and $0 \leq x < 3$ of the two systems.
:::
