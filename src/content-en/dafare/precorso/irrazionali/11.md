---
titolo: Equation and inequality with cube roots
teoria: [indice-dispari]
parole: [cube root, odd index, cubing]
---

Solve the equation and the inequality
$$
\sqrt[3]{3x - 5} = 1 \qquad\text{and}\qquad \sqrt[3]{x + 4} < 2 .
$$

:::soluzione
The equation has solution $x = 2$; the inequality has solution $x < 4$.
:::
