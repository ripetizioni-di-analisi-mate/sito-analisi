---
titolo: Equation with two roots and a second-degree radicand
teoria: [equazione-due-radici]
parole: [irrational equation, equal radicands, second degree]
---

Solve the equation
$$
\sqrt{x^2 - 1} = \sqrt{3x + 3} .
$$

:::soluzione
$x = -1 \ \vee \ x = 4$. For $x = -1$ both radicands equal $0$.
:::
