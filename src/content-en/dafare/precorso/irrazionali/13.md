titolo: Irrational equation by substitution

---

Solve the equation
$$
2x - 3\sqrt{x} - 2 = 0 .
$$

:::soluzione
$x = 4$. With $t = \sqrt{x} \geq 0$ the equation $2t^2 - 3t - 2 = 0$ has roots $t = 2$ and $t = -\dfrac{1}{2}$, and the second is discarded.
:::
