---
titolo: Equation with a root and an expression on the right-hand side
teoria: [equazione-radice-uguale]
parole: [irrational equation, extraneous solution, condition on the right-hand side]
---

Solve the equation
$$
\sqrt{x + 5} = x - 1 .
$$

:::soluzione
$x = 4$. The value $x = -1$, produced by squaring, does not satisfy the condition $x - 1 \geq 0$ and is discarded.
:::
