Find the quotient and the remainder of the division of
$$
x^3 - 4x^2 + x + 6 \qquad \text{by} \qquad x - 2 .
$$

:::soluzione
$Q(x) = x^2 - 2x - 3$ and $R = 0$: the division is exact.
:::
