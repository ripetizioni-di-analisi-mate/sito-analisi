---
titolo: Multiplicity of a root with repeated synthetic division
teoria: [molteplicita-di-una-radice, ruffini, teorema-di-ruffini]
parole: [multiplicity of a root, triple root, synthetic division, factorization]
---

Determine the multiplicity of the root $2$ of the polynomial
$$
P(x) = x^4 - 5x^3 + 6x^2 + 4x - 8
$$
and factor it completely.

:::soluzione
The root $2$ is triple: $P(x) = (x - 2)^3 (x + 1)$.
:::
