---
titolo: Remainder without carrying out the division
teoria: [teorema-del-resto]
parole: [remainder theorem, remainder, evaluation]
---

Compute, without carrying out the divisions, the remainder of the division of
$$
P(x) = x^5 - 2x^3 + x - 4
$$
by $x - 1$ and by $x + 2$.

:::soluzione
$P(1) = -4$ and $P(-2) = -22$: the remainders are $-4$ and $-22$.
:::
