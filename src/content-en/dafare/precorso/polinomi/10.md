List the rational candidates and factor completely
$$
3x^3 - 2x^2 - 3x + 2 .
$$

:::soluzione
The candidates are $\pm 1$, $\pm 2$, $\pm\dfrac{1}{3}$, $\pm\dfrac{2}{3}$; the factorization is $(x - 1)(x + 1)(3x - 2)$.
:::
