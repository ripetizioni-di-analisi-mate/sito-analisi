Expand
$$
(2x - 5)^2 \qquad \text{and} \qquad (x + 3)^3 .
$$

:::soluzione
$(2x - 5)^2 = 4x^2 - 20x + 25$ and $(x + 3)^3 = x^3 + 9x^2 + 27x + 27$.
:::
