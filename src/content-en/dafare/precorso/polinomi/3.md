Factor
$$
x^3 + 8 \qquad \text{and} \qquad 27x^3 - 1 .
$$

:::soluzione
$x^3 + 8 = (x + 2)\left( x^2 - 2x + 4 \right)$ and $27x^3 - 1 = (3x - 1)\left( 9x^2 + 3x + 1 \right)$.
:::
