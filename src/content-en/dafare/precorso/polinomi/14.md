Simplify the fraction
$$
\frac{x^3 + 1}{x^2 - x - 2} ,
$$
stating the domain conditions.

:::soluzione
$\dfrac{x^2 - x + 1}{x - 2}$, with $x \neq -1$ and $x \neq 2$.
:::
