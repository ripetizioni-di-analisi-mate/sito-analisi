Factor completely
$$
x^4 - 13x^2 + 36 .
$$

:::soluzione
$x^4 - 13x^2 + 36 = \left( x^2 - 4 \right)\left( x^2 - 9 \right) = (x - 2)(x + 2)(x - 3)(x + 3)$.
:::
