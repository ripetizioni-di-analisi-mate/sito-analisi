Find the quotient and the remainder of the division of
$$
2x^4 + x^3 - 3x^2 + x - 1 \qquad \text{by} \qquad x^2 + 1 .
$$

:::soluzione
$Q(x) = 2x^2 + x - 5$ and $R = 4$.
:::
