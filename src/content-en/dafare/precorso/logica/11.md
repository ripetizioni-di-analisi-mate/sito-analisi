---
titolo: Sum of three consecutive integers
teoria: [dimostrazione-diretta]
parole: [direct proof, consecutive integers, multiples of 3]
---

Prove with a direct proof that the sum of three consecutive integers is a multiple of $3$.

:::soluzione
With $n$, $n + 1$, $n + 2$ the sum is $3n + 3 = 3(n + 1)$, three times the integer $n + 1$, therefore it is a multiple of $3$.
:::
