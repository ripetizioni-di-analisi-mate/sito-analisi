---
titolo: Condizione sufficiente tra multipli
teoria: [necessaria-sufficiente, implicazione]
parole: [condizione sufficiente, condizione necessaria, multipli]
---

Determine whether, for a natural number $n$, the condition "being a multiple of $9$" is necessary, sufficient, or necessary and sufficient for "being a multiple of $3$".

:::soluzione
Sufficient and not necessary: every multiple of $9$ can be written as $9k = 3 \cdot 3k$ and is a multiple of $3$, while $n = 6$ is a multiple of $3$ and the equality $6 = 9k$ has no solutions in the natural numbers.
:::
