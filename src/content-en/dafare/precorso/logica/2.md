---
titolo: Negation of an existential proposition
teoria: [negazione-dei-quantificatori]
parole: [negation, existential quantifier]
---

Write the negation of
$$
\exists n \in \mathbb{N} : n^2 = 10
$$
and determine which of the proposition and its negation is true.

:::soluzione
The negation is $\forall n \in \mathbb{N} : n^2 \neq 10$ and it is true: $3^2 = 9$, $4^2 = 16$, and the squares of the following natural numbers exceed $16$.
:::
