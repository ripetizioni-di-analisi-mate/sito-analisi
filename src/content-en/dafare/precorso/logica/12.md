---
titolo: Implication about an inequality by contrapositive
teoria: [dimostrazione-per-contronominale, inversa-contronominale]
parole: [proof by contrapositive, inequality, implication]
---

Prove by contrapositive that, for real $x$, if $x^2 + 2x < 0$ then $x < 0$.

:::soluzione
The contrapositive is "if $x \geq 0$ then $x^2 + 2x \geq 0$", which is true because for $x \geq 0$ the two terms $x^2$ and $2x$ are nonnegative; therefore the given implication is true.
:::
