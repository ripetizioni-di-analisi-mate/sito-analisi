---
titolo: Negation with two quantifiers
teoria: [quantificatori, negazione-dei-quantificatori]
parole: [negation, quantifiers, order of quantifiers]
---

Write the negation of
$$
\exists y \in \mathbb{R} \ \forall x \in \mathbb{R} : x^2 \geq y
$$
and determine which of the proposition and its negation is true.

:::soluzione
The negation is $\forall y \in \mathbb{R} \ \exists x \in \mathbb{R} : x^2 < y$. The original proposition is true: for $y = 0$, $x^2 \geq 0$ holds for every real $x$.
:::
