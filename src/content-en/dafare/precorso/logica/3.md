titolo: Negation of a quantified conjunction
teoria: [negazione-dei-quantificatori, leggi-di-de-morgan]

---

Write the negation of
$$
\forall x \in \mathbb{R} : x \geq 0 \ \land \ x^2 \geq 0
$$
and determine which of the proposition and its negation is true.

:::soluzione
The negation is $\exists x \in \mathbb{R} : x < 0 \lor x^2 < 0$ and it is true: for $x = -1$ the first alternative $x < 0$ is satisfied.
:::
