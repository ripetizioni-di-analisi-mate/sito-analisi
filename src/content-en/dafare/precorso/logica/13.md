---
titolo: Negation of a proposition with ε
teoria: [quantificatori, negazione-dei-quantificatori]

Write the negation of
$$
\forall \varepsilon > 0 \ \exists n \in \mathbb{N} : \frac{1}{n + 1} < \varepsilon
$$
and determine which of the proposition and its negation is true.

:::soluzione
The negation is $\exists \varepsilon > 0 \ \forall n \in \mathbb{N} : \dfrac{1}{n + 1} \geq \varepsilon$. The original proposition is true, because for every $\varepsilon > 0$ a natural number $n > \dfrac{1}{\varepsilon}$ satisfies $n + 1 > \dfrac{1}{\varepsilon}$, that is, $\dfrac{1}{n + 1} < \varepsilon$.
:::
