---
titolo: Negation of a universal proposition
teoria: [negazione-dei-quantificatori]
parole: [negation, universal quantifier, counterexample]
---

Write the negation of
$$
\forall x \in \mathbb{R} : x^2 \geq 1
$$
and determine which of the proposition and its negation is true.

:::soluzione
The negation is $\exists x \in \mathbb{R} : x^2 < 1$ and it is true: for $x = 0$ one has $x^2 = 0 < 1$.
:::
