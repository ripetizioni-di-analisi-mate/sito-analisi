---
titolo: Irrationality of a sum
teoria: [dimostrazione-per-assurdo]
parole: [proof by contradiction, irrational, contradiction]
---

Given that $\sqrt{2}$ is irrational, prove by contradiction that $1 + \sqrt{2}$ is irrational.

:::soluzione
If $1 + \sqrt{2}$ were a rational $q$, then $\sqrt{2} = q - 1$ would be a difference of rationals, therefore rational, contradicting the hypothesis; hence $1 + \sqrt{2}$ is irrational.
:::
