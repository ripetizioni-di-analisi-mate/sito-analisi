---
titolo: Necessary and sufficient condition with an equation
teoria: [necessaria-sufficiente]
parole: [necessary and sufficient condition, equivalence, equation]
---

Determine whether, for real $x$, the condition "$2x - 4 = 0$" is necessary, sufficient, or necessary and sufficient for "$x = 2$".

:::soluzione
Necessary and sufficient: $2x - 4 = 0 \iff 2x = 4 \iff x = 2$, therefore the two conditions are equivalent.
:::
