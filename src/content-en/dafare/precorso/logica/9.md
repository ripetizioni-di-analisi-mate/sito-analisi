---
titolo: Negation of a quantified implication
teoria: [negazione-dell-implicazione, negazione-dei-quantificatori]

---

Write the negation of
$$
\forall x \in \mathbb{R} : \ x > 0 \ \Longrightarrow\ x + 1 > 2
$$
and determine which of the proposition and its negation is true.

:::soluzione
The negation is $\exists x \in \mathbb{R} : x > 0 \land x + 1 \leq 2$ and it is true: for $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$ one has $x > 0$ and $x + 1 = \dfrac{3}{2} \leq 2$.
:::
