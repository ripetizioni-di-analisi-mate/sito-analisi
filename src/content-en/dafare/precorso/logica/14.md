---
titolo: Sufficient condition with a trinomial
teoria: [necessaria-sufficiente, implicazione]
parole: [sufficient condition, necessary condition, quadratic inequality]
---

Determine whether, for real $x$, the condition "$x^2 - 5x + 6 < 0$" is necessary, sufficient, or necessary and sufficient for "$2 < x < 4$".

:::soluzione
Sufficient and not necessary, because $x^2 - 5x + 6 < 0$ is equivalent to $2 < x < 3$, which implies $2 < x < 4$, while $x = \dfrac{7}{2}$ satisfies $2 < x < 4$ and gives $x^2 - 5x + 6 = \dfrac{3}{4} > 0$.
:::
