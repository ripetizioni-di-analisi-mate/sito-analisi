---
titolo: Contrapositive and converse of an implication about multiples
teoria: [inversa-contronominale, implicazione]
parole: [contrapositive, converse, multiples, counterexample]
---

Write the contrapositive and the converse of the implication "if $n$ is a multiple of $8$, then $n$ is a multiple of $4$" ($n$ natural) and determine the truth value of each.

:::soluzione
Contrapositive: "if $n$ has nonzero remainder in the division by $4$, then $n$ has nonzero remainder in the division by $8$", true because it is equivalent to the given implication. Converse: "if $n$ is a multiple of $4$, then $n$ is a multiple of $8$", false for $n = 12$.
:::
