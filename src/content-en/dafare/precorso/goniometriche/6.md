---
titolo: Equality of two sines
teoria: [riconducibili-a-elementari]
parole: [sin A equals sin B, families of solutions]
---

Solve the equation
$$
\sin 2x = \sin\left( x + \frac{\pi}{4} \right) .
$$

:::soluzione
From the two alternatives: $x = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + 2n\pi$ or $x = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + \dfrac{2n\pi}{3}$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$; the first family is contained in the second, therefore the solutions are $x = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + \dfrac{2n\pi}{3}$.
:::
