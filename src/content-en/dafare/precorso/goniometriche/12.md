Solve the inequality on $[0, 2\pi)$
$$
2\cos x + 1 > 0 .
$$

:::soluzione
The inequality is equivalent to $\cos x > -\dfrac{1}{2}$. Solution: $0 \leq x < \dfrac{2\pi}{3}$ or $\dfrac{4\pi}{3} < x < 2\pi$.
:::
