---
titolo: Equation with the double-angle formula for the cosine
teoria: [formule-di-addizione, secondo-grado-goniometriche, equazioni-elementari]
parole: [double angle, quadratic, sine]
---

Solve the equation
$$
\cos 2x = \sin x .
$$

:::soluzione
With $\cos 2x = 1 - 2\sin^2 x$ the equation becomes $2\sin^2 x + \sin x - 1 = 0$, which gives $\sin x = \dfrac{1}{2}$ or $\sin x = -1$. Solutions: $x = \dfrac{\pi}{6} + 2n\pi$, $x = \dfrac{5\pi}{6} + 2n\pi$ or $x = \dfrac{3\pi}{2} + 2n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$.
:::
