---
titolo: Elementary equation with no solutions
teoria: [equazioni-elementari, circonferenza-goniometrica]
parole: [equation with no solutions, sine, admissible values]
---

Solve the equation
$$
3\sin x = 4 .
$$

:::soluzione
The equation is equivalent to $\sin x = \dfrac{4}{3}$, and since $\dfrac{4}{3} > 1$ it has no solutions.
:::
