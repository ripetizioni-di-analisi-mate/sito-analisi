---
titolo: Equality of two cosines
teoria: [riconducibili-a-elementari]
parole: [cos A equals cos B, families of solutions]
---

Solve the equation
$$
\cos 3x = \cos x .
$$

:::soluzione
From the two alternatives: $x = n\pi$ or $x = \dfrac{n\pi}{2}$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$; the first family is contained in the second, therefore the solutions are $x = \dfrac{n\pi}{2}$.
:::
