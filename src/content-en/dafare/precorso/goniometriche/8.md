Solve the equation
$$
2\cos^2 x - 7\cos x + 3 = 0 .
$$

:::soluzione
The roots in $t = \cos x$ are $t = 3$, to be discarded, and $t = \dfrac{1}{2}$. Solutions: $x = \pm\dfrac{\pi}{3} + 2n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$.
:::
