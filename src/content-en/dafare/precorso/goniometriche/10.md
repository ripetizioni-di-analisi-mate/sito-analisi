titolo: Equazione lineare in seno e coseno

Solve the equation
$$
\sin x - \cos x = 1 .
$$

:::soluzione
Using the auxiliary angle method the equation becomes $\sqrt{2}\,\sin\left(x - \dfrac{\pi}{4}\right) = 1$. Solutions: $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2n\pi$ or $x = \pi + 2n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$.
:::
