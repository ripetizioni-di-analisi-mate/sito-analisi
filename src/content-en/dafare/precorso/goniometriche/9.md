---
titolo: Homogeneous equation of second degree
teoria: [omogenee-e-lineari, equazioni-elementari]
parole: [homogeneous equation, tangent, division by cosine squared]
---

Solve the equation
$$
3\sin^2 x - 4\sin x \cos x + \cos^2 x = 0 .
$$

:::soluzione
Dividing by $\cos^2 x$ gives $3\tan^2 x - 4\tan x + 1 = 0$, hence $\tan x = 1$ or $\tan x = \dfrac{1}{3}$. Solutions: $x = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + n\pi$ or $x = \arctan\dfrac{1}{3} + n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$.
:::
