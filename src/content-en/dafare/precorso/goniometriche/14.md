Solve on $[0, 2\pi)$ the inequality
$$
4\cos^2 x - 3 \geq 0 .
$$

:::soluzione
The inequality is equivalent to $\cos x \leq -\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ or $\cos x \geq \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$. Solution: $0 \leq x \leq \dfrac{\pi}{6}$, $\dfrac{5\pi}{6} \leq x \leq \dfrac{7\pi}{6}$ or $\dfrac{11\pi}{6} \leq x < 2\pi$.
:::
