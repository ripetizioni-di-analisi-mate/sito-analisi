titolo: Rational equation with the double-angle formula

Solve the equation
$$
\frac{\cos 2x}{\cos x - \sin x} = 0 .
$$

:::soluzione
With the condition $\cos x \neq \sin x$, that is, $x \neq \dfrac{\pi}{4} + n\pi$, the numerator vanishes for $x = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + \dfrac{n\pi}{2}$. Solutions: $x = \dfrac{3\pi}{4} + n\pi$, with $n \in \mathbb{Z}$.
:::
