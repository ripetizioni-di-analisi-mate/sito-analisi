---
titolo: Equation with an expression on the right-hand side and two solutions
teoria: [equazione-modulo-uguale-espressione]
parole: [absolute value equal to an expression, two acceptable solutions]
---

Solve the equation
$$
|2x - 5| = x + 1 .
$$

:::soluzione
$x = \dfrac{4}{3} \ \vee \ x = 6$. Both values satisfy the condition $x + 1 \geq 0$.
:::
