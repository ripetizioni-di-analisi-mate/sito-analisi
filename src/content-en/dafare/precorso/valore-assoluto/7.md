---
titolo: Inequality with absolute value less than a constant
teoria: [disequazioni-minore, modulo-come-distanza]
parole: [inequality with absolute value, interval, distance]
---

Solve the inequality
$$
|x - 2| < 5 .
$$

:::soluzione
$-3 < x < 7$: the points at a distance less than $5$ from $2$.
:::
