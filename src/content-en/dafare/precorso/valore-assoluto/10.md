---
titolo: Greater-than inequality with an expression on the right-hand side
teoria: [disequazione-modulo-espressione]
parole: [absolute value inequality, right-hand side with the variable, union]
---

Solve the inequality
$$
|x - 1| \geq 2x - 4 .
$$

:::soluzione
$x \leq 3$: the union of the two conditions $x \leq 3$ and $x \leq \dfrac{5}{3}$.
:::
