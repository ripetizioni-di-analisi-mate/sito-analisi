---
titolo: Equation with an expression on the right-hand side
teoria: [equazione-modulo-uguale-espressione]
parole: [absolute value equal to an expression, acceptability condition]
---

Solve the equation
$$
|x + 3| = 2x .
$$

:::soluzione
$x = 3$. The value $x = -1$, produced by the second system, does not satisfy the condition $2x \geq 0$ and is discarded.
:::
