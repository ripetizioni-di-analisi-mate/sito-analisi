---
titolo: Less-than inequality with an expression on the right-hand side
teoria: [disequazione-modulo-espressione]
parole: [absolute value inequality, right-hand side with the variable, system]
---

Solve the inequality
$$
|x + 1| < 3x - 3 .
$$

:::soluzione
$x > 2$: the intersection of the two conditions $x > 2$ and $x > \dfrac{1}{2}$.
:::
