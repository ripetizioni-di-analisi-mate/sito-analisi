Solve the equation
$$
x^{2} - 3|x| - 4 = 0 .
$$

:::soluzione
$x = -4 \ \vee \ x = 4$. With $t = |x| \geq 0$ the equation $t^{2} - 3t - 4 = 0$ has roots $t = 4$ and $t = -1$, and the second is discarded.
:::
