---
titolo: Inequality with two absolute values
teoria: [due-valori-assoluti]
parole: [study by regions, two absolute values, sum of absolute values]
---

Solve the inequality
$$
|x + 2| + |x - 4| \geq 8 .
$$

:::soluzione
$x \leq -3 \ \vee \ x \geq 5$. In the middle region $-2 \leq x < 4$ the left-hand side is constantly equal to $6$ and the inequality is never satisfied.
:::
