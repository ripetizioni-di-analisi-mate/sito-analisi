---
titolo: Equations with a zero constant and a negative constant
teoria: [equazioni-con-modulo]
parole: [absolute value equation, zero right-hand side, negative right-hand side]
---

Solve the equations
$$
|x + 4| = -3 \qquad\text{and}\qquad |7x - 21| = 0 .
$$

:::soluzione
The first equation has no solutions, because an absolute value is never negative; the second has solution $x = 3$.
:::
