How many H3 molecules occur in 15 canonical histone octamers?

:::soluzione
30.

Each canonical octamer contains two H3 copies, together with two H2A, two H2B and two H4. For 15 octamers the H3 count is $15\cdot2=30$. The value 120 would count all octamer proteins, since $15\cdot8=120$, rather than H3 alone.
:::
