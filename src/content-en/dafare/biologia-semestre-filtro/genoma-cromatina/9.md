After replication of a CpG site initially methylated on both strands, what state is expected before maintenance methylation?

:::soluzione
One methylated parental strand and one unmethylated new strand in each double helix.

Semiconservative replication gives each daughter molecule one parental and one newly synthesised strand. Parental methylation remains on the old strand, while the new strand must acquire it afterwards. A hemimethylated state therefore forms, recognised by systems that restore the pattern. Copying bases does not automatically copy methyl groups.
:::
