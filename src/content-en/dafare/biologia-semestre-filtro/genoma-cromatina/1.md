A diploid human somatic cell without abnormalities has completed S phase, but sister chromatids are still joined. How many chromosomes and nuclear DNA molecules does it contain?

:::soluzione
46 chromosomes and 92 DNA molecules.

S phase duplicates each DNA molecule, producing two sister chromatids. While sisters remain associated, each pair constitutes one duplicated chromosome. There are therefore 46 chromosomes and $46\cdot2=92$ molecules. The chromosome complement remains diploid; DNA content rises from $2C$ to $4C$.
:::
