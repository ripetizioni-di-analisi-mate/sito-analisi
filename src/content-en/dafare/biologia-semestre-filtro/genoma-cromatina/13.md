An Alu element does not encode its own reverse transcriptase. How can it generate new insertions under permissive conditions?

:::soluzione
By using proteins produced by other elements, such as LINE-1.

Alu is a SINE and is non-autonomous because it lacks a complete apparatus encoded by its own sequence. It can nevertheless use activities supplied by LINE-1. Autonomy describes the source of the required proteins, whereas mobility concerns the ability to generate insertions in the cellular context considered.
:::
