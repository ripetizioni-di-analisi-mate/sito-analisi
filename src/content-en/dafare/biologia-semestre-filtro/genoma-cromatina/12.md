A genomic element produces RNA; DNA is then synthesised from it and inserts at a new locus. How is this mechanism classified?

:::soluzione
Retrotransposition.

The path from DNA to RNA and then to a new DNA copy identifies an RNA intermediate followed by reverse transcription. This characterises retrotransposons. Chromosome replication instead copies DNA from a DNA template and does not necessarily create a new insertion at another locus.
:::
