A linear chromosome fragment retains origins and telomeres but loses its functional centromere. Which process is directly impaired?

:::soluzione
Normal segregation through attachment to the spindle.

The kinetochore assembles at the centromere and interacts with the spindle. Losing it prevents normal mechanical attachment during segregation. Replication origins may still allow replication, but copying a fragment does not guarantee that both daughter cells will receive it.
:::
