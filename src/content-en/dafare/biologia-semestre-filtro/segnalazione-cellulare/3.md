An intact epithelium shows increased paracellular tracer passage while communication through connexons remains normal. Which structure is the most direct candidate for the barrier defect?

:::soluzione
Tight junctions.

The tracer travels between cells, along a route controlled by the paracellular barrier. Tight junctions regulate this route. Connexons instead connect cytoplasms, and normal connexon function does not establish an intact extracellular barrier. The intact epithelium excludes large discontinuities as the cause within this model. Mechanical attachments can support the epithelium, but the structure directly regulating the paracellular route is the tight junction.
:::
