In a normal rod, a flash activates rhodopsin, transducin and PDE6. Which sequence describes the subsequent response?

:::soluzione
cGMP decreases, cation channels close and the membrane hyperpolarises.

PDE6 degrades cGMP, reducing the opening of cation channels that depend on this messenger. The inward current therefore decreases while the outward potassium current continues. The potential becomes more negative. Assigning cGMP synthesis to PDE6 would reverse the sign of the first step. The incorrect alternatives respectively reverse messenger degradation, the channel response or the final electrical sign.
:::
