A GPCR still binds its ligand but activates less G protein after GRK phosphorylation and arrestin binding. What does this finding describe?

:::soluzione
Receptor desensitisation.

The ligand is present and recognised, but coupling to the G protein decreases. This is desensitisation. GRK and arrestin may also contribute to internalisation, but a reduced response does not require every receptor to have already been removed from the surface. This is reduced transmission after recognition: it differs from loss of binding and has the opposite sign to more efficient coupling.
:::
