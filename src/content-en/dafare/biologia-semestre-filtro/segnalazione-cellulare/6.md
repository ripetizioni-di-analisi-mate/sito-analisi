After phospholipase C activation, which product reaches receptors on the endoplasmic reticulum membrane through the cytosol?

:::soluzione
IP3.

Phospholipase C cleaves PIP2 to form IP3 and DAG. IP3 is soluble and can diffuse to its channel receptors on the reticulum; DAG remains in the membrane. PIP3 is a lipid produced by PI3K and belongs to a different transformation of PIP2. PIP2 is the initial lipid substrate; DAG and PIP3 remain membrane-associated, unlike soluble IP3.
:::
