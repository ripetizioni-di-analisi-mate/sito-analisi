GDP-bound Ras encounters a functional GEF in the presence of cytosolic GTP. Which event does the GEF directly promote?

:::soluzione
GDP release followed by GTP binding.

A GEF facilitates nucleotide exchange by destabilising GDP binding. Available GTP can then occupy the site. The GEF does not directly convert bound GDP to GTP; acceleration of GTP hydrolysis is instead the characteristic function of a GAP. Stabilising GDP would have the opposite effect to GEF-promoted exchange.
:::
