NO reaches a vascular smooth-muscle cell. Which enzyme is directly activated in the canonical pathway that raises cGMP?

:::soluzione
Soluble guanylyl cyclase.

NO diffuses across the membrane and binds the haem group of soluble guanylyl cyclase. The enzyme produces cGMP from GTP. Adenylyl cyclase instead generates cAMP from ATP, changing both the target enzyme and the messenger specified in the question. A phosphodiesterase removes the cyclic nucleotide; the natriuretic-peptide receptor cyclase recognises a signal different from NO.
:::
