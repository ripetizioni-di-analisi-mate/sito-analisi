A cell secretes a factor that acts on nearby cells without using the bloodstream. How is this communication classified?

:::soluzione
Paracrine.

The criterion is the route taken by the signal. The factor is secreted and diffuses locally, making the communication paracrine. Endocrine signalling uses the circulation; in contact-dependent signalling, the ligand remains associated with the signalling cell. Response duration does not change this classification. The target is a neighbouring cell and the factor is free: neither tethering to the producer nor blood transport is required.
:::
