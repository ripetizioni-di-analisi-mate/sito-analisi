titolo: "The PIP3 balance"
teoria: ["insulina-pi3k"]
parole: ["The PIP3 balance"]
---

A cell produces PIP3 after an insulin signal. If PTEN activity increases while PI3K-dependent production stays unchanged, which change is favoured?

:::soluzione
A decrease in PIP3.

PTEN removes the phosphate at position 3 of the phosphoinositide, converting PIP3 to PIP2. With production unchanged, increasing PTEN activity favours a decrease in the PI3K product. The molecule remains a membrane lipid; it does not become the soluble IP3 generated by phospholipase C. The level depends on production and removal: considering PI3K alone would omit PTEN's opposing contribution.
:::
