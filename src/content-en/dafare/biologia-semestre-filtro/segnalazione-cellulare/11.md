In the linear EGF receptor → SOS → Ras → Raf → MEK → ERK pathway, a form of MEK active independently of Raf is introduced while Raf is blocked. What is expected if ERK is functional?

:::soluzione
Restoration of ERK activation.

MEK lies downstream of Raf and can activate ERK. Making MEK independent of Raf bypasses the upstream block in the stated model. Recovery is expected because ERK remains functional. An experimental conclusion would also require controls excluding alternative pathways and nonspecific effects. The introduced form is explicitly independent of Raf: it can restore the downstream segment without reactivating blocked upstream components.
:::
