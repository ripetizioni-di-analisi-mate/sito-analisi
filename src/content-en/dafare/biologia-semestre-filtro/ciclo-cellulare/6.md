A species has $2n=14$. Give chromosome, chromatid and DNA counts for one cell after S, after meiosis I and after meiosis II. Explain when ploidy changes.

:::soluzione
After S: 14 chromosomes, 28 chromatids, 4C. After meiosis I: 7 chromosomes, 14 chromatids, 2C. After meiosis II: 7 chromosomes, 7 chromatids, 1C. Ploidy falls from 2n to n in division I as homologues separate. Division II separates sisters without another S phase. Each count refers to one cell at that stage: summing all products would yield different totals and obscure the relevant change.
:::
