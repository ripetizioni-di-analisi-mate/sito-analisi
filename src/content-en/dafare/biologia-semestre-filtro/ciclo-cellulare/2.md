An Rb variant remains bound to E2F after mitogenic stimulation. In a second experiment E2F is made insensitive to Rb binding. In the simplified model, why can the second intervention restore S-gene expression without correcting Rb?

:::soluzione
The first defect maintains inhibition downstream of mitogenic signals. Making E2F independent of Rb binding can bypass that point: E2F promotes its targets while Rb remains altered. This reasoning concerns component order in a causal circuit. It does not establish restoration of every cellular function or completion of S phase. The experiment measures gene expression; replication and integrity require additional checks.
:::
