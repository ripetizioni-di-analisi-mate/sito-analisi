Two cells activate caspase 8 after Fas stimulation. BID blockade strongly reduces executioners in the first but has a modest effect in the second. Explain how this can occur without denying initial Fas activation in either cell.

:::soluzione
The first may depend more strongly on BID-linked mitochondrial amplification, while the second can activate executioners effectively through other downstream caspase-8 connections in the model. A shared initial signal does not impose identical downstream dependencies. The interpretation requires effective BID blockade and comparable conditions, with mitochondrial involvement measured. It does not justify claiming that the extrinsic pathway always bypasses mitochondria.
:::
