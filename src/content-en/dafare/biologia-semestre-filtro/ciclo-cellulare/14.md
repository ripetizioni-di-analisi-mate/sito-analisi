After damage, a treatment reduces S-phase entry and immediate apoptotic markers. Why are neither DNA repair nor lasting survival yet certain, and how can they be assessed separately?

:::soluzione
Arrest provides time for repair but does not establish its success; reduced apoptotic markers do not establish functional recovery. Residual damage requires an assay appropriate to the lesion, while viability and sustained survival require follow-up. The outcomes are separate: a cell can remain arrested with persistent damage or lose some apoptotic markers without avoiding another death outcome. Interpretation must follow what each measurement directly detects.
:::
