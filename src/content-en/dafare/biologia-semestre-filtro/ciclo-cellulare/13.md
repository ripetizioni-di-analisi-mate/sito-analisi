Initially a population is mainly annexin-V-positive and membrane-impermeant-dye-negative; later it is positive for both. Give a compatible interpretation and explain why this is not a unique proof of mechanism.

:::soluzione
This is compatible with progression from phosphatidylserine exposure with preserved membrane integrity to a later integrity-loss phase, as in apoptosis followed by secondary necrosis. Markers measure properties, not every pathway step. Morphology, caspase activity, controls and tracking the same cells help distinguish explanations. Populations may also be heterogeneous: two aggregate measurements do not prove that every cell followed the same timeline.
:::
