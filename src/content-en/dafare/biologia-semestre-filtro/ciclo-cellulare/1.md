A treatment reduces inhibitory CDK1 phosphorylation without changing cyclin B. Substrate phosphorylation also increases. Explain why this fits increased CDK1 activity and which control would help attribute the signal to it.

:::soluzione
Cyclin abundance need not increase for complex activity to change: removing an inhibitory phosphate can activate existing complexes. The substrate signal fits this prediction but could involve other kinases. Comparison with selective CDK1 inhibition, keeping substrate amount and reaction conditions equal, would help test attribution. Total substrate should also be measured: more protein could otherwise produce a stronger signal without increasing the phosphorylated fraction.
:::
