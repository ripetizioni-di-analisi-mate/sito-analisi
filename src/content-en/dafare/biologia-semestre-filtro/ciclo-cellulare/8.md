A gamete lacking one chromosome, written n-1, fuses with a normal n gamete. State the zygote complement and distinguish it from a change affecting the whole chromosome set.

:::soluzione
The sum is $(n-1)+n=2n-1$: one copy of a particular chromosome is missing relative to diploidy. This is monosomy, an aneuploidy. It is not haploidy because almost all other chromosomes remain paired, nor a whole-set change such as triploidy. The distinction concerns chromosome counting and does not alone predict viability or every biological effect.
:::
