In sample A, APC/C does not tag securin. In B, securin is ubiquitylated but the proteasome is blocked. Predict the shared effect on separase in the model and a measurement distinguishing the defects.

:::soluzione
In both samples securin can persist and keep separase inhibited, obstructing cohesion removal. A shared final effect does not mean the same blocking point. Measuring ubiquitylated securin distinguishes failure of tagging from failure of destruction after tagging; total protein abundance also matters. APC/C, proteasome and separase perform different actions: recognition and ubiquitylation, degradation, and cleavage of a cohesin subunit.
:::
