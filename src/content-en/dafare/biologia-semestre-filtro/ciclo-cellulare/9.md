Compare an arrested primary oocyte with an ovulated secondary oocyte: meiotic stage, ploidy and DNA content. Identify the event changing ploidy between them.

:::soluzione
The primary oocyte is arrested in prophase I at 2n,4C, having replicated DNA. The secondary is normally in metaphase II at n,2C. Meiosis I separated homologues and reduced ploidy between these stages. Sister chromatids remain joined in the secondary oocyte, so its haploidy does not imply 1C. Division II normally completes after activation associated with fertilisation.
:::
