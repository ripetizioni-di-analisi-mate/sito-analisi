A culture produces many differentiated cells but completely loses this capacity by the third passage. Is this sufficient to establish durable self-renewal? Propose a more informative assessment.

:::soluzione
No. The result fits progenitors that proliferate and exhaust. Cells retaining the original capacity must be followed over time and tested again for differentiated progeny production, controlling culture conditions. Many divisions in a short interval do not replace long-term self-renewal. Even a positive result would not automatically establish pluripotency; the range of generated cell types requires separate assessment.
:::
