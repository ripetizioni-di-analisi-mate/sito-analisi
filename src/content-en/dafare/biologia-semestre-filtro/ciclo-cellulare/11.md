In a model, high BCL2 reduces cytochrome c release. When cytochrome c is supplied directly to the cytosol of an extract with functional APAF1 and procaspase 9, the cascade activates. Which blocking position is supported?

:::soluzione
The result supports a block upstream of cytosolic cytochrome c, consistent with control of mitochondrial permeabilisation. Direct supply bypasses release and shows that the downstream system can function under extract conditions. It does not establish direct caspase-9 inhibition by BCL2. A bypass experiment localises a step, but artificial extract conditions need not reproduce every constraint of an intact cell.
:::
