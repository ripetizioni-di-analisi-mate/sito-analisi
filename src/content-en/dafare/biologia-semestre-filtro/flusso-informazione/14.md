In an artificial system, a tRNA recognising the GCU codon is charged with glycine instead of alanine. If it passes the checks and enters the ribosome, which amino acid can be incorporated at GCU?

:::soluzione
Glycine.

The anticodon allows the tRNA to recognise GCU, while the group attached to its 3′ end is glycine. The ribosome can therefore transfer the chain to that amino acid if the tRNA is accepted as stated. Amino acid–tRNA matching is ensured mainly by synthetases and their editing mechanisms. The ribosome does not convert glycine into the amino acid expected from the code.
:::
