A pre-mRNA contains three exons of 150, 90 and 210 nucleotides and two introns of 400 and 600 nucleotides. All exons are retained. What is the RNA length after splicing, excluding the cap and poly-A tail?

:::soluzione
450 nucleotides

Splicing removes the two introns and joins the three exons. The retained length is therefore 150 + 90 + 210 = 450 nucleotides. Intron lengths would be needed to calculate the precursor length, which is 1,450 nucleotides. Part of the exons might belong to UTRs: the question concerns mature RNA, so any untranslated regions are not subtracted.
:::
