---
titolo: "Origin of the poly-A tail"
teoria: ["maturazione-rna"]
parole: ["Origin of the poly-A tail"]
---

Two mRNAs from the same gene have identical exon sequences but poly-A tails of different lengths. Which explanation is compatible with normal processing and turnover?

:::soluzione
The tail is added enzymatically and can be shortened after synthesis.

Poly-A polymerase adds adenines after cleavage of the precursor's 3′ end. Deadenylases can subsequently shorten the tail. Two molecules with the same exons can therefore have different tails without any DNA difference. Splicing removes introns and joins exons; it does not produce the tail from the removed introns.
:::
