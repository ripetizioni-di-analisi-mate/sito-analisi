A DNA polymerase retains synthesis activity but loses only its proofreading exonuclease. Which direct consequence is expected?

:::soluzione
More errors escape the polymerase; synthesis remains 5′ → 3′.

Proofreading removes incorrect nucleotides from the chain end. If it is lost, more mismatches can persist during synthesis, although later systems may still repair them. The polymerisation site remains functional and continues adding nucleotides to the 3′-OH. Losing this control changes neither synthesis direction nor the primer requirement.
:::
