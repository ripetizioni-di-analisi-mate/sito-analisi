A large cytosolic protein accumulates in the nucleus after an NLS is added. The construct retains stability and folding. What does the comparison directly demonstrate?

:::soluzione
That NLS is sufficient to promote import of the construct.

The experiment adds a single targeting element and observes a localization change. It therefore supports the signal's sufficiency in the construct studied. Necessity in the natural protein would also require removal or mutation of its signal, with controls for possible folding effects.
:::
