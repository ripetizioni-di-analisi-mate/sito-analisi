Mitochondria supplied with substrates and oxygen receive an uncoupler. Which combination is expected compared with the control?

:::soluzione
A smaller proton gradient and less ATP produced per oxygen consumed.

The uncoupler allows protons to return through a pathway bypassing ATP synthase. More energy is dissipated, so yield decreases. Electron transfer can accelerate when the gradient offers less resistance; this distinguishes uncoupling from direct inhibition of the chain.
:::
