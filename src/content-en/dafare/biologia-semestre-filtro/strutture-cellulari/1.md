An ideal cell contains nonpenetrating solutes at $300\,\mathrm{mOsm/L}$. After transfer to a large bath of nonpenetrating solutes at $200\,\mathrm{mOsm/L}$, what equilibrium volume would it reach, neglecting regulation, lysis and pressures?

:::soluzione
$1.5V_i$

The amount of internal solute stays constant, so $C_iV_i=C_fV_f$. At equilibrium the internal concentration reaches that of the bath: $V_f=\dfrac{300}{200}V_i=1.5V_i$. Water enters because the bath is hypotonic. The result belongs to the stated ideal model, without volume regulation or membrane rupture.
:::
