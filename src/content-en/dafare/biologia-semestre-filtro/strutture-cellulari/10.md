A membrane glycoprotein receives sugars on a luminal domain in the reticulum. Where will that domain be after arrival at the plasma membrane?

:::soluzione
On the extracellular side.

Budding and fusion preserve domain orientation relative to the bilayer. The vesicle lumen becomes continuous with the external environment during exocytosis, so the luminal domain is exposed outside the cell. The cytosolic domain instead remains exposed to the cytosol.
:::
