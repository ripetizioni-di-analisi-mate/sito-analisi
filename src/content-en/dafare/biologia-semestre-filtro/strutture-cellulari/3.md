Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase completes $100$ cycles. How many net positive charges does it transfer outwards?

:::soluzione
$100$

Each cycle expels three Na⁺ and imports two K⁺. Net outward transfer is therefore $3-2=1$ positive charge per cycle. For $100$ cycles the result is $100$ net charges; $300$ counts only expelled sodium and ignores incoming potassium.
:::
