A protein with a presequence is destined for the mitochondrial matrix. Which sequence of steps is typical?

:::soluzione
TOM in the outer membrane, then TIM23 in the inner membrane.

The protein must cross two membranes. TOM provides outer entry for many cytosol-synthesized proteins; TIM23 participates in the presequence pathway towards the matrix. The inner-membrane potential and ATP-powered chaperones support transfer; the presequence is often removed after entry.
:::
