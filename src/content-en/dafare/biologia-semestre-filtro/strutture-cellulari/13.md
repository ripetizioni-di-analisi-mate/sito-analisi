Soluble lysosomal hydrolases are produced, but many are secreted after a defect in mannose-6-phosphate addition. Which process is impaired?

:::soluzione
Recognition for sorting from the Golgi towards endosomes.

Mannose-6-phosphate enables sorting receptors to recognize many hydrolases. If it is missing, enzymes can be synthesized and pass through the Golgi, but a fraction is directed towards secretion. The lysosome loses degradative activity without a general block of protein synthesis.
:::
