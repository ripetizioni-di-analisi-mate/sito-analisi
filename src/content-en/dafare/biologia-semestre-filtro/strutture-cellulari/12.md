In the classical LDL pathway, what happens to the receptor after it separates from cargo in the endosome?

:::soluzione
It recycles to the plasma membrane.

Endosomal acidification favours separation of LDL from its receptor. The receptor is selected for recycling and can participate in further uptake cycles; LDL proceeds towards degradative compartments. Distinguishing their fates avoids assigning cargo degradation to the receptor.
:::
