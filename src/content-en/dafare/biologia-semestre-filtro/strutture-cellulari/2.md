In a FRAP experiment, fluorescence in a small membrane region recovers only partly to a plateau, without new protein synthesis. Measurement photobleaching is controlled and the surrounding pool of fluorescent molecules is large. Which interpretation is supported?

:::soluzione
Mobile and relatively immobile fractions exist under the measured conditions.

Recovery results from lateral arrival of unbleached fluorescent molecules. The recovering fraction indicates mobility; incomplete recovery is compatible with constrained or very slow molecules within the observation window. The test concerns lateral movement and does not demonstrate protein transfer between leaflets.
:::
