For an imprinted gene expressed in the tissue studied, the maternal copy is silenced. Which copy normally supplies the transcript in that tissue?

:::soluzione
The paternal copy.

The assumption states that the maternally derived copy is silenced. The paternal copy therefore remains active if intact and normally regulated. Parental origin concerns the copy of the nuclear gene; it does not imply that the gene lies in mitochondrial DNA.
:::
