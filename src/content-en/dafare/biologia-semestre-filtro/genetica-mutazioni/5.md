Two Aa parents have a child with the dominant phenotype. Assuming complete dominance and penetrance, what is the probability that this child is Aa?

:::soluzione
$\dfrac23$

Before observation, AA, Aa and aa have probabilities one quarter, one half and one quarter. The dominant phenotype excludes aa and leaves a total probability of three quarters. Thus $P(\mathrm{Aa}\mid D)=\dfrac{\frac12}{\frac34}=\dfrac23$. One half would be the probability without the phenotype information.
:::
