An internal deletion removes four nucleotides from a translated region, far from the start codon. What effect on the downstream frame is expected?

:::soluzione
A shift in the reading frame.

Translation groups nucleotides in threes. Because four is not a multiple of three, the remaining sequence is divided into different triplets after the deletion. Whether the deleted number is even is irrelevant; what matters is the remainder after division by three.
:::
