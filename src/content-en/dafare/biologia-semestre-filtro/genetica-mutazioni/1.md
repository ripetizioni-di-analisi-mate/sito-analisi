Within the translated part of an mRNA, codon UAU becomes UAA. Given that UAU encodes tyrosine and UAA is a stop, how is the substitution classified?

:::soluzione
Nonsense.

The sequence retains its length, so the triplet frame remains unchanged. The new codon introduces a termination signal within the translated region. The variant is therefore nonsense and may shorten the product or promote transcript degradation, depending on its position.
:::
