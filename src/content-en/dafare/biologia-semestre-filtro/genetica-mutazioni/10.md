One Aa parent carries an autosomal dominant variant and the other is aa. Genotype Aa has 80% penetrance and aa does not show the trait. What is the probability that a child shows the trait?

:::soluzione
40%.

A child receives A with probability $\dfrac12$. Conditional on Aa, the trait appears with probability $0{,}80$. The product is $\dfrac12\cdot0{,}80=0{,}40$. Penetrance does not change the allele's transmission probability; it changes the phenotypic outcome among carriers.
:::
