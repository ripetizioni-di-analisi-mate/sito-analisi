In the usual model of human mitochondrial inheritance, a father carries a mitochondrial DNA variant and the mother does not. What transmission is expected through paternal mitochondrial DNA?

:::soluzione
None to the children in the model considered.

The usual model predicts inheritance of mitochondrial DNA from the mother. The father's variant therefore does not lead to transmission through his mitochondria. X-linked and Y-linked models follow nuclear chromosomes and have different rules, so they do not apply to this question.
:::
