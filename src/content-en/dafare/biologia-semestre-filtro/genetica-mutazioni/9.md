An AB/ab × ab/ab testcross produces 400 AB/ab, 400 ab/ab, 100 Ab/ab and 100 aB/ab offspring. What is the observed recombination frequency?

:::soluzione
20%.

The parental classes are AB and ab because they reproduce the double heterozygote's phase. The two recombinant classes together contain 200 of 1000 offspring. Thus $r=\dfrac{100+100}{1000}=0{,}20$. Using just one recombinant class would incorrectly halve the result.
:::
