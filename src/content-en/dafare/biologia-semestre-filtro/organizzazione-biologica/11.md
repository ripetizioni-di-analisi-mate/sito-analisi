In double-stranded DNA, guanine accounts for 20% of all bases. What percentage is adenine?

:::soluzione
30%.

Complementarity requires cytosine also to account for 20%. G and C therefore occupy 40% of the total; the remaining 60% is divided equally between A and T. Thus $A=\dfrac{60\%}{2}=30\%$. Equality between complementary bases applies to the complete double-stranded molecule, as specified in the question.
:::
