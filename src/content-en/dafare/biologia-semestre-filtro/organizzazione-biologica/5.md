A bacterium has an outer membrane containing lipopolysaccharide and a thin peptidoglycan layer. Which organisation is consistent with these findings?

:::soluzione
A Gram-negative-type envelope.

The combination of an outer membrane containing lipopolysaccharide and thin peptidoglycan characterises a Gram-negative envelope. Peptidoglycan lies between the plasma and outer membranes. In the typical Gram-positive model the layer is thick and that outer membrane is absent. A viral capsid is protein-based and lacks this cellular architecture.
:::
