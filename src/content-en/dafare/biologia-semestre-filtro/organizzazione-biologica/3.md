A negative-sense animal RNA virus depends on its own polymerase. Which activity must it provide in the virion to produce the first mRNAs?

:::soluzione
RNA-dependent RNA polymerase.

Negative-sense RNA is complementary to mRNA and must be copied into positive-sense RNA. An enzyme that synthesises RNA from RNA is therefore required. Ribosomes translate suitable mRNA but do not produce its complementary strand. The virus must carry the polymerase in the virion because its proteins cannot yet be synthesised from the negative-sense genome.
:::
