---

A plasmid passes from donor to recipient only when the cells can contact each other. The system depends on the donor’s conjugative proteins. Which route is involved?

:::soluzione
Conjugation.

Transfer depends on contact and a conjugative apparatus, so the findings identify conjugation. Donor and recipient may belong to different lineages, and transfer is horizontal. Vertical transmission would instead describe passage from a parent to daughter cells. Sensitivity to a barrier alone would be less conclusive without the information about conjugative proteins.
:::
