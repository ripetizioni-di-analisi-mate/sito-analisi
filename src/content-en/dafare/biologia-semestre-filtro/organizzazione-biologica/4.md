After treatment, a retrovirus-infected cell contains viral DNA but no integrated proviral DNA. If entry and reverse transcription are normal, which step is directly impaired?

:::soluzione
Insertion of viral DNA into the cellular genome.

The presence of viral DNA shows that the substrate for integration is available. The missing event is insertion into the cellular genome, a step involving integrase. Complete inhibition of reverse transcriptase would instead prevent production of that DNA. The data locate the step; alone they do not necessarily identify a single molecular target of the treatment.
:::
