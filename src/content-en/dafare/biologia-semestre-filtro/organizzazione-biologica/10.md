A molecule contains adenine and ribose, with no phosphate groups. How is it classified?

:::soluzione
Nucleoside.

A nitrogenous base and a sugar constitute a nucleoside. At least one phosphate group must be present to obtain a nucleotide. Adenine plus ribose forms adenosine; adding phosphate produces, for example, adenosine monophosphate. Classification follows the components actually present, without inferring phosphate merely because the base can occur in nucleic acids.
:::
