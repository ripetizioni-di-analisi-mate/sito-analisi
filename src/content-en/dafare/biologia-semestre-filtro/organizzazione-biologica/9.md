After heating, a protein loses activity and three-dimensional structure, but the chain retains its sequence and length. Which phenomenon is supported by the findings?

:::soluzione
Denaturation without extensive chain hydrolysis.

Heating has changed the spatial organisation required for function, while primary structure is preserved. This pattern is consistent with denaturation. Extensive proteolytic digestion would shorten chains by producing fragments. Loss of activity alone would be less informative: here, sequence and length measurements allow the two processes to be distinguished.
:::
