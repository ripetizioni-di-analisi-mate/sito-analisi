titolo: "Exceedance threshold for an exponential variable"

parole: ["exceedance threshold for an exponential variable"]
---

An exponential waiting time has rate $\dfrac13\,\mathrm{s}^{-1}$. Find the threshold exceeded with probability $\dfrac18$.

:::soluzione
$9\log 2\,\mathrm{s}$
:::
