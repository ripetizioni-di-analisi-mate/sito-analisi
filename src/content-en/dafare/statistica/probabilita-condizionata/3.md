Three lines produce $\dfrac{1}{2}$, $\dfrac{1}{4}$ and $\dfrac{1}{4}$ of all parts, respectively. Their rejection rates are $\dfrac{1}{10}$, $\dfrac{1}{5}$ and $\dfrac{2}{5}$. Calculate the probability that a part chosen at random from the combined output is rejected.

:::soluzione
$\dfrac{1}{5}$
:::
