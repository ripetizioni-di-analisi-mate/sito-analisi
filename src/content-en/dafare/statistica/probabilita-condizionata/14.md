Three groups of applications have shares $\dfrac{1}{2}$, $\dfrac{1}{3}$ and $\dfrac{1}{6}$. The probabilities of an application being flagged in the three groups are $\dfrac{1}{5}$, $\dfrac{3}{10}$ and $\dfrac{3}{5}$, respectively. Calculate the probability that a flagged application belongs to the second group.

:::soluzione
$\dfrac{1}{3}$
:::
