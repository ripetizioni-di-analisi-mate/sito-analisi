A website accounts for $75\%$ of all orders, with the rest placed at a counter. Complaints arise from $4\%$ of website orders and $12\%$ of counter orders. Given that an order generated a complaint, calculate the probability that it was placed at the counter.

:::soluzione
$\dfrac{1}{2}$
:::
