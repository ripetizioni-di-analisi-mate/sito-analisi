---
titolo: "Upper quantile of a sample mean"
teoria: ["normale-campione"]
parole: ["normally distributed sample mean"]
---

For an i.i.d. sample of $25$ observations from $N(40,25)$, find the threshold that the sample mean exceeds with probability $0.025$. Use $z_{0.975}\approx1.96$.

:::soluzione
$41.96$
:::
