Draw $16$ i.i.d. observations from a normal population. Find $P\left(\bar X_{16}-\mu>1.75\dfrac S4\right)$ using the cumulative distribution function $F_{15}(1.75)\approx0.95$ of $t_{15}$.

:::soluzione
$0.05$
:::
