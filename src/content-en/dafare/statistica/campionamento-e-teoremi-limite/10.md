---
titolo: "Tail probability for a mean using the central limit theorem"
teoria: ["tcl"]
parole: ["central limit theorem"]
---

An i.i.d. sample of $100$ observations comes from a population with mean $8$ and standard deviation $5$. Approximate $P(\bar X_{100}<7)$ using the central limit theorem and $\Phi(-2)\approx0.0228$.

:::soluzione
$0.0228$
:::
