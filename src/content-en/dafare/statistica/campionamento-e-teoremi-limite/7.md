---
titolo: "Tail probability for a nonnegative variable"
teoria: ["markov"]
parole: ["Markov"]
---

A nonnegative processing time has mean $9\,\mathrm{min}$. Use Markov's inequality to find an upper bound on the probability that processing takes at least $36\,\mathrm{min}$.

:::soluzione
$\dfrac{1}{4}$
:::
