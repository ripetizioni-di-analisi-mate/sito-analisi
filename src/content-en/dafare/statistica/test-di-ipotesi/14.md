Two independent normal samples each have $16$ observations. Their sample means are $15$ and $8$ and their sample standard deviations, calculated with the degrees-of-freedom correction, are $8$ and $6$. Calculate Welch's statistic for $H_0:\mu_1-\mu_2=2$.

:::soluzione

$2$

:::
