In $60$ independent trials the observed counts in three categories are $27$, $23$, and $10$. The null hypothesis assigns them probabilities $\dfrac12$, $\dfrac13$, and $\dfrac16$. Calculate Pearson's statistic.

:::soluzione

$0.75$

:::
