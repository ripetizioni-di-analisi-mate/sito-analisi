Six measurements had a reported mean of $14\,\mathrm{mm}$. One observation of $22\,\mathrm{mm}$ had been entered as $10\,\mathrm{mm}$. Find the corrected mean.

:::soluzione
$16\,\mathrm{mm}$
:::
