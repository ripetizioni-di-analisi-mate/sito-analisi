For $X,Y\in\{0,1\}$, the joint masses are $p_{00}=\dfrac1{10}$, $p_{01}=\dfrac2{10}$, and $p_{10}=\dfrac4{10}$. Calculate $P(X=1)$.

:::soluzione
$\dfrac7{10}$
:::
