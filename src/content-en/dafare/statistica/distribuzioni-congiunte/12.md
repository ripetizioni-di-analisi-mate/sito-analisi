Two departments handle fractions $\dfrac23$ and $\dfrac13$ of all orders. Their mean processing times are $6\,\mathrm{min}$ and $15\,\mathrm{min}$, respectively. Calculate the mean processing time for an order selected from all orders.

:::soluzione
$9\,\mathrm{min}$
:::
