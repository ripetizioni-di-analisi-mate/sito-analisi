---
titolo: "Lower confidence bound for a variance"
teoria: ["varianza"]
parole: ["variance", "confidence intervals"]
---

An independent sample of $6$ units from a normal population has $s^2=6$. Use $\chi^2_{5,0.95}=11.07$ and $\chi^2_{5,0.05}=1.145$ to find the lower endpoint of the $90\,\%$ confidence interval for the variance. Round to two decimal places.

:::soluzione
$2.71$
:::
