---
titolo: "t interval with an estimated standard deviation"
teoria: ["student"]
parole: ["Student’s t", "confidence intervals"]
---

A sample of $16$ independent observations from a normal population has mean $30$ and sample standard deviation $4$, calculated with the degrees-of-freedom correction. Use $t_{15,0.975}=2.131$ to calculate the two-sided $95\,\%$ interval.

:::soluzione
$[27.869,32.131]$
:::
