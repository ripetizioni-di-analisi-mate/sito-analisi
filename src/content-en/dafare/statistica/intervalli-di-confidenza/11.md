---
titolo: "Margin of error for paired differences"
teoria: ["appaiate"]
parole: ["paired", "confidence intervals"]
---

Sixteen independent units give sixteen normally distributed paired differences with sample standard deviation $8$, calculated with the degrees-of-freedom correction. Use $t_{15,0.975}=2.131$ to find the margin for the two-sided $95\,\%$ interval.

:::soluzione
$4.262$
:::
