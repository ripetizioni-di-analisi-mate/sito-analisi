A normal regression model with an intercept gives $n=10$, $b=-0.6$, and $SE(b)=0.2$. Test $H_0\colon\beta=0$ against a two-sided alternative at level $5\%$, using $t_{8,0.975}=2.306$.

:::soluzione
Reject $H_0$ since $T=-3$.
:::
