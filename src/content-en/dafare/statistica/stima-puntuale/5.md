titolo: "Method-of-moments estimate of a rate"
teoria: ["momenti"]
parole: ["method-of-moments estimate of a rate", "point estimation"]

Four waiting times from an exponential model with rate $\lambda>0$ are $2,3,7,8$ minutes. What is the numerical value of the method-of-moments estimate of $\lambda$, in $\mathrm{min}^{-1}$?

:::soluzione
$\dfrac15\,\mathrm{min}^{-1}$
:::
