---
titolo: "Interval of maximum likelihood estimates for a uniform distribution"
teoria: ["uniforme"]
parole: ["interval of maximum likelihood estimates for a uniform distribution", "point estimation"]
---

The independent observations $7,8,9$ come from a uniform distribution on $[\theta,\theta+4]$ with positive density at the endpoints. What is the set of maximum likelihood estimates of $\theta$?

:::soluzione
$[5,7]$
:::
