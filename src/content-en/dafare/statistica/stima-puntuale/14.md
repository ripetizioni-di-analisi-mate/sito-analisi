A Bernoulli model allows only $p=\dfrac14$ or $p=\dfrac34$. Three independent trials yield, in order, a success, a failure, and a success. Which value is the maximum likelihood estimate of $p$?

:::soluzione
$\dfrac{3}{4}$
:::
