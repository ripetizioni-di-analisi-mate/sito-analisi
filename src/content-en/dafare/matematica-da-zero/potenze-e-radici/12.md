---
titolo: Estimating the side length of a square
teoria: [radice-quadrata, stima-radice, numeri-irrazionali]
parole: [word problem, estimating a root, area of a square, approximation]
---

A square flower bed has an area of $130$ m². Find the two consecutive tenths of a meter between which its side length lies.

:::soluzione
Since $11.4^2 = 129.96$ and $11.5^2 = 132.25$, the side length $\sqrt{130}$ lies between $11.4$ m and $11.5$ m.
:::
