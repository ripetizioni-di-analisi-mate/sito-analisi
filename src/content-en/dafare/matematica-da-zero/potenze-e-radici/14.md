A sheet of paper is $1 \cdot 10^{-4}$ m thick. Find the height of a ream of $500$ sheets in meters and in centimeters.

:::soluzione
$500 \cdot 10^{-4} = 5 \cdot 10^{-2}$ m, or $5$ cm.
:::
