Write $0.00034$ and $0.0062$ in scientific notation and find the order of magnitude of each number.

:::soluzione
$0.00034 = 3.4 \cdot 10^{-4}$ has order of magnitude $10^{-4}$, and $0.0062 = 6.2 \cdot 10^{-3}$ has order of magnitude $10^{-2}$.
:::
