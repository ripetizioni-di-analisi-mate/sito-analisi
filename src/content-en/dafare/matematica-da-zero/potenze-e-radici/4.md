---
titolo: Rewriting powers using prime bases
teoria: [potenza-di-potenza, potenza-prodotto-quoziente, quoziente-stessa-base]
parole: [changing the base, factorization, power of a product, simplification]
---

Evaluate
$$
\frac{8^2 \cdot 3^6}{6^5} .
$$

:::soluzione
$\dfrac{2^6 \cdot 3^6}{2^5 \cdot 3^5} = 2 \cdot 3 = 6$.
:::
