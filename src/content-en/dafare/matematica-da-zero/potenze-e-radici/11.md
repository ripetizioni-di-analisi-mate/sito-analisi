titolo: Side length of a square with a given area
teoria: [radice-quadrata, quadrati-perfetti]
parole: [word problem, area of a square, side, perimeter, fencing]
---

A square terrace has an area of $196$ m². Find the length of a side and the number of meters of railing needed to enclose it on all four sides.

:::soluzione
Each side is $\sqrt{196} = 14$ m long, and $4 \cdot 14 = 56$ m of railing are needed.
:::
