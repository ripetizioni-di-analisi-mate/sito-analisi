titolo: Edge length of a cube with a given volume
teoria: [radice-cubica, cubi-perfetti]

A cube-shaped container has a volume of $512$ cm³. Find the length of an edge and the total area of its six faces.

:::soluzione
Each edge is $\sqrt[3]{512} = 8$ cm long, and the total area of the faces is $6 \cdot 8^2 = 384$ cm².
:::
