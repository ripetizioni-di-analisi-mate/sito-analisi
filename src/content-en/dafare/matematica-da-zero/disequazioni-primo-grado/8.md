---
titolo: A system with a negative coefficient
teoria: [sistema-disequazioni, schema-sistema, principi-equivalenza]
parole: [system of inequalities, reversing the inequality sign, interval]
---

Solve the system
$$
\begin{cases}
3 - x > 5 \\
2x + 7 \geq 1
\end{cases}
$$

:::soluzione
The solutions satisfy $-3 \leq x < -2$, giving the interval $[-3, -2)$.
:::
