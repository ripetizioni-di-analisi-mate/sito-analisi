titolo: Fencing a rectangular vegetable garden
teoria: [problemi-disequazioni, principi-equivalenza]
parole: [inequality word problems, perimeter, rectangle]

One side of a rectangular vegetable garden is three times as long as the other. There are $64$ m of fencing available to enclose it. What is the greatest possible length of the shorter side?

:::soluzione
If the shorter side has length $x$, the perimeter is $8x$, and $8x \leq 64$ gives $x \leq 8$, so the shorter side is at most $8$ m long.
:::
