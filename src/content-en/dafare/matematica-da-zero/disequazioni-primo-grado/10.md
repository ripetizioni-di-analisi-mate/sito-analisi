Solve the inequality
$$
\frac{x + 4}{3 - x} \geq 0 .
$$

:::soluzione
The solutions satisfy $-4 \leq x < 3$, giving the interval $[-4, 3)$. The value $3$ is excluded because it makes the denominator zero.
:::
