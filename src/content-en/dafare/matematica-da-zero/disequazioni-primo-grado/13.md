titolo: A taxi ride on a budget
teoria: [problemi-disequazioni, principi-equivalenza]

A taxi charges $4$ euros at the start of a ride plus $1.50$ euros per kilometer traveled. What is the greatest distance in kilometers that can be traveled for no more than $25$ euros?

:::soluzione
The inequality $4 + 1.5\,x \leq 25$ gives $x \leq 14$, so the greatest distance is $14$ km.
:::
