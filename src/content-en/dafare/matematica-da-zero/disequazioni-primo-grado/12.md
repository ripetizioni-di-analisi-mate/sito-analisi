At a copy shop, each photocopy costs $0.10$ euros. With a monthly pass costing $6$ euros, each photocopy costs $0.04$ euros. What is the smallest number of photocopies per month for which the pass saves money?

:::soluzione
The inequality $6 + 0.04\,x < 0.1\,x$ gives $x > 100$, so the pass saves money starting at $101$ photocopies per month.
:::
