---
titolo: An inequality with products and a negative coefficient
teoria: [principi-equivalenza, metodo-risoluzione]
parole: [inequality with products, reversing the inequality sign, linear inequality]
---

Solve the inequality
$$
3(2 - x) \geq 5 - (x - 7) .
$$

:::soluzione
The solutions satisfy $x \leq -3$, giving the interval $(-\infty, -3]$.
:::
