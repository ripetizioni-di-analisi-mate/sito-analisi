---
titolo: An inequality satisfied by every number
teoria: [sempre-mai-verificate]
parole: [inequality that is always true, real numbers, zero coefficient]
---

Solve the inequality
$$
3(x + 2) - x < 2(x + 4) .
$$

:::soluzione
The inequality always holds, so its solutions are all real numbers, that is, $\mathbb{R}$.
:::
