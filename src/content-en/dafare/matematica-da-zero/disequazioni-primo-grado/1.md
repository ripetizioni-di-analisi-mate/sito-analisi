---
titolo: Multiplying an inequality by a negative number
teoria: [proprieta-disuguaglianze]
parole: [inequality, negative number, reversing the inequality sign]
---

Multiply both sides of $4 < 7$ by $-2$ and write the resulting inequality with the correct symbol.

:::soluzione
$-8 > -14$. The inequality sign reverses because $-2$ is negative.
:::
