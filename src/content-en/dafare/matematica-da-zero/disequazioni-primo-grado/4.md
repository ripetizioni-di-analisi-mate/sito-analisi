Solve the inequality
$$
\frac{x - 1}{2} - \frac{x + 2}{5} > 1 .
$$

:::soluzione
The solutions satisfy $x > \dfrac{19}{3}$, giving the interval $\left( \dfrac{19}{3}, +\infty \right)$.
:::
