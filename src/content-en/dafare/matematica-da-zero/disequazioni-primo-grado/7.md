---
titolo: A system of two inequalities
teoria: [sistema-disequazioni, schema-sistema]
parole: [system of inequalities, graph of a system, interval]
---

Solve the system
$$
\begin{cases}
2x - 1 > 3 \\
x + 4 \leq 10
\end{cases}
$$

:::soluzione
The solutions satisfy $2 < x \leq 6$, giving the interval $(2, 6]$.
:::
