A pair of shoes costs $40$ euros. The price is increased by $15\%$, and then the new price is discounted by $15\%$. Find the final price and the percent change from the original price.

:::soluzione
The final price is $40 \cdot 1.15 \cdot 0.85 = 39.10$ euros, a decrease of $2.25\%$.
:::
