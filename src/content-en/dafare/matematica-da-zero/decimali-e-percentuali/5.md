Convert the fractions $\dfrac{7}{25}$ and $\dfrac{4}{9}$ to decimals, and state whether each decimal terminates or repeats.

:::soluzione
$\dfrac{7}{25} = 0.28$ is a terminating decimal, and $\dfrac{4}{9} = 0.\overline{4}$ is a pure repeating decimal.
:::
