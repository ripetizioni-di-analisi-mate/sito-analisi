Evaluate the expression
$$
0.\overline{6} \cdot 1.5 + 0.25 .
$$

:::soluzione
$\dfrac{2}{3} \cdot \dfrac{3}{2} + \dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{5}{4}$, or $1.25$.
:::
