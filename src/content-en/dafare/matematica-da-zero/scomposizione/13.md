A rectangle has area $x^2 + 2x - 15$ in $\mathrm{cm^2}$ and one side of length $x - 3$ in $\mathrm{cm}$, where $x > 3$. Find the length of the other side and calculate the perimeter for $x = 7$.

:::soluzione
The other side has length $x + 5$. For $x = 7$, the side lengths are $4\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the perimeter is $32\,\mathrm{cm}$.
:::
