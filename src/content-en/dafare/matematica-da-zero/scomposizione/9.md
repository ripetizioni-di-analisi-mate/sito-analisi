State the domain restrictions and simplify the fraction
$$
\frac{x^2 - 4}{x^2 - 2x} .
$$

:::soluzione
For $x \neq 0$ and $x \neq 2$, the fraction equals $\dfrac{x + 2}{x}$.
:::
