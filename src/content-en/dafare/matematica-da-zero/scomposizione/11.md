titolo: A floor covered by a rug
teoria: [differenza-di-quadrati]
parole: [word problems, difference of squares, areas, mental arithmetic]

The floor of a room is a square with side length $65\,\mathrm{dm}$. In the center is a square rug with side length $35\,\mathrm{dm}$. Use the difference of squares to find the area of the floor that is uncovered.

:::soluzione
$65^2 - 35^2 = 100 \cdot 30 = 3000\,\mathrm{dm^2}$.
:::
