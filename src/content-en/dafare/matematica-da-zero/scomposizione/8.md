Find the GCD and LCM of the polynomials $x^2 - 1$ and $x^2 + 2x + 1$.

:::soluzione
$\operatorname{MCD} = x + 1$ and $\operatorname{mcm} = (x - 1)(x + 1)^2$.
:::
