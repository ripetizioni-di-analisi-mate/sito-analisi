State the domain restrictions and calculate
$$
\frac{3}{x + 2} - \frac{1}{x - 2} .
$$

:::soluzione
For $x \neq -2$ and $x \neq 2$, the result is $\dfrac{2\,(x - 4)}{(x + 2)(x - 2)}$.
:::
