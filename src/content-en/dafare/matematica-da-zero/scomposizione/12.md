titolo: Side length of a square flower bed
teoria: [quadrato-di-binomio]
parole: [word problems, square of a binomial, perimeter, areas]
---

A square flower bed has area $x^2 + 8x + 16$ in $\mathrm{m^2}$, where $x > 0$. Write an expression for the side length and find the perimeter of the flower bed for $x = 6$.

:::soluzione
The side length is $x + 4$. For $x = 6$, the side length is $10\,\mathrm{m}$ and the perimeter is $40\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
