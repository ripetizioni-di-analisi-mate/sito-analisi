A bill of $300$ euros is split equally among $x$ friends, where $x$ is a natural number greater than zero. Write the difference between each person's share with $x$ friends and each person's share with $x + 5$ friends as a single rational expression, and evaluate it for $x = 20$.

:::soluzione
The difference is $\dfrac{300}{x} - \dfrac{300}{x + 5} = \dfrac{1500}{x\,(x + 5)}$ euros. For $x = 20$, it is $3$ euros.
:::
