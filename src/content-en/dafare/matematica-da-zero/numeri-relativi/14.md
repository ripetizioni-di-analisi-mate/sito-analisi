A trail starts at a beach at an elevation of $0$ m and climbs to a cave at an elevation of $+85$ m. From the same beach, a diver descends to a shipwreck at an elevation of $-37$ m. Find the elevation difference between the cave and the shipwreck, that is, the difference between the elevation of the cave and that of the shipwreck.

:::soluzione
$(+85) - (-37) = 85 + 37 = 122$, so the elevation difference is $122$ m.
:::
