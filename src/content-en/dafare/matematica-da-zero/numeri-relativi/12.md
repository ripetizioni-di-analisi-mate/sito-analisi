At a mountain hut, the temperature at $6$ in the morning is $-7$ °C. By $13$\:00 it rises by $12$ degrees, and by $22$\:00 it falls by $9$ degrees. Find the temperatures at $13$\:00 and $22$\:00 and the difference between the temperatures at $13$\:00 and $6$\:00.

:::soluzione
At $13$\:00 the temperature is $+5$ °C, and at $22$\:00 it is $-4$ °C. The difference is $(+5) - (-7) = 12$ degrees.
:::
