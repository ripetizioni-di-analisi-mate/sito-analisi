Anna's checking account has a balance of $+180$ euros. During one month, Anna receives a bank transfer of $450$ euros and pays $3$ installments of $160$ euros each and a bill of $95$ euros. Find the balance at the end of the month.

:::soluzione
$180 + 450 + 3 \cdot (-160) - 95 = 630 - 575 = 55$, so the balance is $+55$ euros.
:::
