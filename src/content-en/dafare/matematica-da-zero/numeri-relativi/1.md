titolo: Ordering low temperatures

On a winter day, the low temperatures in six cities are $-6$ °C, $+3$ °C, $-11$ °C, $0$ °C, $+8$ °C, and $-1$ °C. Arrange the temperatures in increasing order and identify the lowest.

:::soluzione
$-11 < -6 < -1 < 0 < +3 < +8$. The lowest temperature is $-11$ °C.
:::
