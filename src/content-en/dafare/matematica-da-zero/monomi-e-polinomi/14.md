titolo: Comparing a square and a rectangle

A square has side length $x$ centimeters, where $x > 3$. One side of a rectangle is $3$ cm longer than the side of the square, and the other is $3$ cm shorter. Find how much greater the area of the square is than the area of the rectangle.

:::soluzione
The rectangle has area $(x + 3)(x - 3) = x^2 - 9$, so the area of the square exceeds that of the rectangle by $9$ cm² for every value of $x$.
:::
