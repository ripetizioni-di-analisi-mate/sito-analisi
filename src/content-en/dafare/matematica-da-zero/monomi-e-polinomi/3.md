---
titolo: Simplifying a polynomial and finding its degree
teoria: [riduzione-dei-termini-simili, polinomio-ordinato, grado-di-un-polinomio]
parole: [like terms, polynomial in descending order, degree]
---

Simplify $2x^3 - 5x + 4x^2 - x^3 + 3x - 7$, write the terms in descending order, and state the degree of the polynomial.

:::soluzione
With like terms combined and the terms in descending order, the polynomial is $x^3 + 4x^2 - 2x - 7$. Its degree is $3$.
:::
