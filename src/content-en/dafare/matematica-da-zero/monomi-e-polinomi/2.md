titolo: The cost of a taxi ride
teoria: [espressione-letterale, valore-di-un-espressione]
parole: [word problem, algebraic expression, value, taxi]

A taxi ride costs $4$ euros at the start plus $2$ euros for each kilometer traveled. Write an expression for the cost of a ride of $k$ kilometers and evaluate it for a ride of $7$ kilometers.

:::soluzione
The cost is $4 + 2k$ euros. For $k = 7$, the ride costs $4 + 14 = 18$ euros.
:::
