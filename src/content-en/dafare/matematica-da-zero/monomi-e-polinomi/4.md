---
titolo: Product of two monomials with fractional coefficients
teoria: [prodotto-di-monomi, forma-normale]
parole: [product of monomials, coefficient, fractions]
---

Calculate the product $\left( -\dfrac{3}{2}a^3b \right) \cdot \left( \dfrac{4}{9}ab^2 \right)$.

:::soluzione
$-\dfrac{2}{3}a^4b^3$
:::
