A notebook costs $a$ euros and a pen costs $b$ euros. Marta buys $3$ notebooks and $2$ pens, and Luca buys $5$ notebooks and $1$ pen. Write an expression for their combined cost and evaluate it for $a = 3$ and $b = 2$.

:::soluzione
The combined cost is $8a + 3b$ euros. For $a = 3$ and $b = 2$, it is $24 + 6 = 30$ euros.
:::
