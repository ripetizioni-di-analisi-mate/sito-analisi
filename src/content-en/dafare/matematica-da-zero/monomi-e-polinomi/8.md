A rectangle has side lengths of $3x + 2$ and $2x - 1$ centimeters, where $x > \dfrac{1}{2}$. Write the area and perimeter of the rectangle as polynomials in $x$.

:::soluzione
The area is $A = (3x + 2)(2x - 1) = 6x^2 + x - 2$ cm² and the perimeter is $2p = 10x + 2$ cm.
:::
