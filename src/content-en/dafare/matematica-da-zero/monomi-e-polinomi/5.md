For $x \neq 0$ and $y \neq 0$, simplify $\left( -3x^2y \right)^2 : \left( 9x^3y \right) - 4xy$.

:::soluzione
The quotient is $\dfrac{9x^4y^2}{9x^3y} = xy$, so the expression equals $xy - 4xy = -3xy$.
:::
