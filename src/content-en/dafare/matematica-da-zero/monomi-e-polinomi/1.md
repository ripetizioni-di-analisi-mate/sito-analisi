---
titolo: Value of an expression with two variables
teoria: [valore-di-un-espressione, espressione-letterale]

---

Evaluate $a^2 - 2ab + 3$ for $a = -2$ and $b = 3$.

:::soluzione
The value of the expression is $(-2)^2 - 2 \cdot (-2) \cdot 3 + 3 = 4 + 12 + 3 = 19$.
:::
