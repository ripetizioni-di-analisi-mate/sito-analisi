A laundry offers two pricing plans. The first costs $35$ euros per month plus $25$ euros per rug cleaning, and the second costs $15$ euros per month plus $30$ euros per cleaning. For how many cleanings per month do the plans cost the same, and what is the cost then?

:::soluzione
With $4$ cleanings, the two plans cost the same amount, $135$ euros.
:::
