A group of $30$ people visits a museum. A full-price ticket costs $8$ euros and a reduced-price ticket costs $5$ euros. The group spends $201$ euros in all. How many full-price tickets and how many reduced-price tickets did the group buy?

:::soluzione
The group bought $17$ full-price tickets and $13$ reduced-price tickets.
:::
