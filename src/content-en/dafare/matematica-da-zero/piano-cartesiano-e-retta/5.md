Let $f(x) = 3x - 2$. Find $f(-1)$, $f(0)$, and $f(2)$, and determine whether $P(2, 5)$ lies on the graph of $f$.

:::soluzione
$f(-1) = -5$, $f(0) = -2$, and $f(2) = 4$. The point $P$ does not lie on the graph because $f(2) = 4 \neq 5$.
:::
