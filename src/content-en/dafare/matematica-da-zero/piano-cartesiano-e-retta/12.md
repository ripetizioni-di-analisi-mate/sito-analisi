A rental car costs a flat fee of $3$ euros plus $1.50$ euros per hour of use. Write the function that gives the cost $y$ for $x$ hours, and find the cost of renting the car for $8$ hours.

:::soluzione
$y = \dfrac{3}{2}x + 3$. For $8$ hours, the cost is $15$ euros.
:::
