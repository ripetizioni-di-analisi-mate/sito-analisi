titolo: The quadrant containing a point
teoria: [quadranti, coordinate]

---

For each of the points $A(-4, 2)$, $B(3, -5)$, $C(0, -2)$, and $D(-1, -1)$, identify which quadrant it lies in or which axis it lies on.

:::soluzione
$A$ is in quadrant II, $B$ is in quadrant IV, $C$ is on the $y$-axis, and $D$ is in quadrant III.
:::
