Sausages for a cookout come in packages of $8$ and buns in packages of $6$. The goal is to buy equal numbers of sausages and buns using whole packages. What is the smallest number of sausages needed, and how many packages of each kind are required?

:::soluzione
At least $\operatorname{mcm}(8, 6) = 24$ sausages and $24$ buns are needed, which means $3$ packages of sausages and $4$ packages of buns.
:::
