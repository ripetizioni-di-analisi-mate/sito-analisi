---
titolo: Two flashing lighthouses
teoria: [problemi-con-il-mcm, regola-mcm]
parole: [LCM problems, recurring events, lighthouses]
---

One lighthouse flashes every $8$ seconds and a second one every $14$ seconds. They both flash at this instant. In how many seconds will they next flash together?

:::soluzione
They will flash together again in $\operatorname{mcm}(8, 14) = 56$ seconds.
:::
