Determine whether $35$ and $72$ are coprime and calculate $\operatorname{mcm}(35, 72)$.

:::soluzione
The numbers $35$ and $72$ are coprime because $\operatorname{MCD}(35, 72) = 1$. Therefore, $\operatorname{mcm}(35, 72) = 35 \cdot 72 = 2\,520$.
:::
