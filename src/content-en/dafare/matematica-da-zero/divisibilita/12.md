A rectangular vegetable garden measures $42$ m by $30$ m. Posts are placed around its boundary, with one post at each corner. The spacing between posts is the same throughout and is as large as possible in whole meters. How far apart are adjacent posts, and how many posts are needed?

:::soluzione
The spacing is $\operatorname{MCD}(42, 30) = 6$ m. The perimeter is $144$ m, so $144 : 6 = 24$ posts are needed.
:::
