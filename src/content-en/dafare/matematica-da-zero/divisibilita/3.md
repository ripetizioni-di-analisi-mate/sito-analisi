Determine whether $7\,821$ is divisible by $3$, by $9$, and by $11$.

:::soluzione
The number $7\,821$ is divisible by $3$ and by $9$ because its digits add up to $18$. It is also divisible by $11$ because the digits in odd positions and those in even positions have the same sum, $9$.
:::
