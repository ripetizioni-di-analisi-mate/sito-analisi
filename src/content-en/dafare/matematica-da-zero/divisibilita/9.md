Calculate $\operatorname{MCD}(36, 60, 90)$ and $\operatorname{mcm}(36, 60, 90)$.

:::soluzione
$\operatorname{MCD}(36, 60, 90) = 6$ and $\operatorname{mcm}(36, 60, 90) = 180$.
:::
