titolo: Identical bouquets
teoria: [problemi-con-il-mcd, regola-mcd]

---

A florist has $72$ roses and $96$ tulips and wants to use all the flowers to make as many identical bouquets as possible. How many bouquets can the florist make, and how many flowers of each type will each bouquet contain?

:::soluzione
The florist makes $\operatorname{MCD}(72, 96) = 24$ bouquets, each with $3$ roses and $4$ tulips.
:::
