titolo: Writing an improper fraction as an integer plus a proper fraction
teoria: [tipi-di-frazioni, frazione-come-quoziente]

---

Write $\dfrac{23}{6}$ as the sum of an integer and a proper fraction, and identify the two consecutive integers it lies between.

:::soluzione
$\dfrac{23}{6} = 3 + \dfrac{5}{6}$, so the fraction lies between $3$ and $4$.
:::
