titolo: Discounted price
teoria: [frazione-di-un-numero, addizione-sottrazione]
parole: [discount, fraction of a number, price]

A sofa costs $420$ euros and is discounted by $\dfrac{2}{7}$ of its price. Find the price after the discount.

:::soluzione
The discount is $120$ euros, so the price after the discount is $300$ euros.
:::
