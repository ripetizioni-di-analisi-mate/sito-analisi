Arrange the fractions $\dfrac{3}{4}$, $\dfrac{5}{6}$, $\dfrac{2}{3}$, and $\dfrac{7}{12}$ in increasing order.

:::soluzione
$\dfrac{7}{12} < \dfrac{2}{3} < \dfrac{3}{4} < \dfrac{5}{6}$.
:::
