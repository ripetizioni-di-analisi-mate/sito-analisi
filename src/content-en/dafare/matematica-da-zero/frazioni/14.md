A family spends $\dfrac{1}{4}$ of its monthly salary of $2000$ euros on rent and then $\dfrac{2}{5}$ of what remains on groceries. Find the fraction of the salary left over and the amount in euros that it represents.

:::soluzione
The family has $\dfrac{3}{5} \cdot \dfrac{3}{4} = \dfrac{9}{20}$ of the salary left, or $900$ euros.
:::
