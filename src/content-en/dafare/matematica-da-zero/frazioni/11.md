titolo: Pages of a book
teoria: [frazione-di-un-numero]
parole: [fraction of a number, word problem, book]
---

A book has $320$ pages. Marco has read $\dfrac{5}{8}$ of the book. Find how many pages he has read and how many he has left to read.

:::soluzione
He has read $200$ pages and has $120$ left to read.
:::
