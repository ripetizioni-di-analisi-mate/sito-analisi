Determine which of the two fractions $\dfrac{7}{10}$ and $\dfrac{8}{11}$ is greater.

:::soluzione
$\dfrac{8}{11}$ is greater because $\dfrac{7}{10} = \dfrac{77}{110}$ and $\dfrac{8}{11} = \dfrac{80}{110}$.
:::
