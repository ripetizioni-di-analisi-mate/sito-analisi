---
titolo: A rational equation with an extraneous solution
teoria: [risoluzione-equazione-fratta, equazione-fratta]
parole: [rational equations, domain restrictions, extraneous solution]
---

Solve the equation
$$
\frac{x + 2}{x - 3} = \frac{5}{x - 3} .
$$

:::soluzione
The domain restriction is $x \neq 3$. The polynomial equation gives $x = 3$, which must be discarded, so the original equation has no solution.
:::
