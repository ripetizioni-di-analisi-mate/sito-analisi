titolo: Two cyclists riding toward each other

Two cyclists leave at the same time from two towns $60$ km apart and ride toward each other along the same road. The first travels at $18$ km/h and the second at $12$ km/h. How long does it take them to meet, and how many kilometers has each cyclist traveled?

:::soluzione
They meet after $2$ hours. The first cyclist has traveled $36$ km and the second $24$ km.
:::
