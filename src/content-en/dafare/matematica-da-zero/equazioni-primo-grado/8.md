---
titolo: A rational equation with two denominators
teoria: [equazione-fratta, risoluzione-equazione-fratta]
parole: [rational equations, domain restrictions, unknown in the denominator]
---

Solve the equation
$$
\frac{5}{x + 1} = \frac{3}{x - 1} .
$$

:::soluzione
With the domain restrictions $x \neq -1$ and $x \neq 1$, the equation has solution $x = 4$.
:::
