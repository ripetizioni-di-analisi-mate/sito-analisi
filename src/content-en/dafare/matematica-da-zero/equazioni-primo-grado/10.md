---
titolo: Rearranging a temperature formula
teoria: [formule-inverse]
parole: [rearranging formulas, degrees Celsius, degrees Fahrenheit]
---

The temperature in degrees Fahrenheit, $F$, is related to the temperature in degrees Celsius, $C$, by the formula $F = \dfrac{9}{5}\,C + 32$. Solve the formula for $C$ and find $C$ when $F = 77$.

:::soluzione
$C = \dfrac{5}{9}\,(F - 32)$. Substituting $F = 77$ gives $C = 25$ degrees Celsius.
:::
