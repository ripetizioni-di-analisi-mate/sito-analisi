---
titolo: Classifying an equation (1)
teoria: [tre-casi, uguaglianza-e-identita]
parole: [equation with infinitely many solutions, identity, equation with no solution]
---

Solve $3\,(x + 2) - x = 2\,(x + 3)$ and determine whether it has one solution, no solution, or infinitely many solutions.

:::soluzione
The equation is an identity, so every number is a solution.
:::
