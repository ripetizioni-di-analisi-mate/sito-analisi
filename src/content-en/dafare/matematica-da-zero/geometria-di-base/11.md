---
titolo: Converting between units of area
teoria: [unita-area]
parole: [units of measurement, square meters, square centimeters, unit conversions]
---

Express $3.2\ \text{m}^2$ in square centimeters and $45\,000\ \text{cm}^2$ in square meters.

:::soluzione
$3.2\ \text{m}^2 = 32\,000\ \text{cm}^2$ and $45\,000\ \text{cm}^2 = 4.5\ \text{m}^2$.
:::
