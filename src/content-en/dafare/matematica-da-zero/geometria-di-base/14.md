An aquarium shaped like a rectangular prism is $60$ cm long, $30$ cm wide, and $40$ cm high. Find how many liters of water it holds when full.

:::soluzione
The volume is $72\,000\ \text{cm}^3$, so the aquarium holds $72$ liters.
:::
