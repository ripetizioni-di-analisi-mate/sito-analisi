Find the circumference and area of a circle of radius $5$ cm, both in terms of $\pi$ and using $\pi \approx 3.14$.

:::soluzione
$C = 10\pi \approx 31.4$ cm and $A = 25\pi \approx 78.5\ \text{cm}^2$.
:::
