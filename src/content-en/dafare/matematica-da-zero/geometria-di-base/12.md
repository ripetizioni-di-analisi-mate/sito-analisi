titolo: Running laps around a field
teoria: [poligono-perimetro, area-rettangolo]

A rectangular field measures $15$ m by $8$ m. A runner completes $4$ full laps around the edge of the field. Find how many meters the runner covers.

:::soluzione
The perimeter of the field is $46$ m, so the runner covers $184$ m.
:::
