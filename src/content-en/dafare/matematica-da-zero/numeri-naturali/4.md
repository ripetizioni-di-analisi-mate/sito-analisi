titolo: Cartons of eggs
teoria: [divisione-con-resto]

A farmer collects $247$ eggs and packs them into cartons of $9$. How many full cartons does the farmer fill? How many eggs are left over?

:::soluzione
The farmer fills $27$ cartons and has $4$ eggs left over because $247 = 9 \cdot 27 + 4$.
:::
