Luca has $50$ euros. He buys $3$ bus tickets at $4$ euros each and $2$ sandwiches at $7$ euros each. Write and evaluate an expression for the amount of money he has left.

:::soluzione
$50 - (3 \cdot 4 + 2 \cdot 7) = 50 - 26 = 24$, so Luca has $24$ euros left.
:::
