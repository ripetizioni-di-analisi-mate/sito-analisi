---
titolo: A product using the associative property
teoria: [proprieta-commutativa, proprieta-associativa]
parole: [associative property, commutative property, mental arithmetic]
---

Group the factors to calculate the following product mentally:
$$
4 \cdot 17 \cdot 25 .
$$

:::soluzione
$17 \cdot (4 \cdot 25) = 17 \cdot 100 = 1\,700$.
:::
