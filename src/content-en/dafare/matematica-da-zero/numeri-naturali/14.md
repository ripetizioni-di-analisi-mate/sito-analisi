teoria: [divisione-con-resto]

A dinner has $155$ guests, and each table seats $6$. How many tables are needed? How many seats will be empty?

:::soluzione
Since $155 = 6 \cdot 25 + 5$, $26$ tables are needed, and $26 \cdot 6 - 155 = 1$ seat is left empty.
:::
