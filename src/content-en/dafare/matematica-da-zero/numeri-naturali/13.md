A store receives $6$ boxes of $35$ pens each. Over the course of a week, it sells $48$ pens and then receives another $25$ pens. How many pens does it have at the end of the week if it had none at the start?

:::soluzione
$6 \cdot 35 - 48 + 25 = 210 - 48 + 25 = 187$, so the store has $187$ pens.
:::
