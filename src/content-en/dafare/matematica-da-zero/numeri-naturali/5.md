---
titolo: Finding the dividend in division with remainder
teoria: [divisione-con-resto]
parole: [dividend, divisor, quotient, remainder]
---

In a division problem, the divisor is $8$, the quotient is $15$, and the remainder is $6$. Find the dividend.

:::soluzione
The dividend is $8 \cdot 15 + 6 = 126$.
:::
