Two containers of different shapes contain the same stationary liquid and are open to the same atmosphere. Both points are $8\,\mathrm m$ below their respective surfaces. How do the pressures compare?

- They are equal
- Pressure is higher in the wider vessel
- Pressure is higher in the vessel with greater volume
- Shape suffices to tell which is higher

:::soluzione
They are equal.
:::
