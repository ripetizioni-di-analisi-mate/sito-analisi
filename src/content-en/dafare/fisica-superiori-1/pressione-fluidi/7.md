In a liquid with $\rho=800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ the pressure increase from the surface is measured as $72000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$, what is the depth?

:::soluzione
$9\,\mathrm m$.
:::
