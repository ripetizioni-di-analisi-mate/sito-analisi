---

Two points in the same body of water are at depths $9$ and $11\,\mathrm m$. How much higher is the pressure at the latter? Use $\rho=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$.

:::soluzione
$20000\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
:::
