A stationary block on an incline has weight component $ 24\,\mathrm N$ down the slope. Maximum static friction is $ 32\,\mathrm N$. With no other forces parallel to the slope, what is the actual friction magnitude?

:::soluzione
$24\,\mathrm N$.
:::
