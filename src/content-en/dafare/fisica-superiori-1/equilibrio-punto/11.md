A block weighing $18\,\mathrm N$ is pushed against a vertical wall. The push has a component of $60\,\mathrm N$ towards the wall and a component of $6\,\mathrm N$ downwards. The coefficient of static friction is $0.30$. With these applied forces and the wall contact alone, can the block remain at rest?

- No, the maximum static friction is insufficient
- Yes, because friction can take any required value
- Yes, because the normal reaction always cancels the whole weight
- Yes, because maximum static friction is nonzero

:::soluzione
No, the maximum static friction is insufficient.
:::
