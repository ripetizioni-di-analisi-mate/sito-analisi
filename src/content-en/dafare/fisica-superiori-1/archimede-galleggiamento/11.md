parole: ["Extra immersed volume under added loads"]
---

A platform floats in water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. An $8\,\mathrm{kg}$ load is added and stays above the water. How much does the submerged volume increase if the platform still floats freely?

:::soluzione
$0.008\,\mathrm m^3$.
:::
