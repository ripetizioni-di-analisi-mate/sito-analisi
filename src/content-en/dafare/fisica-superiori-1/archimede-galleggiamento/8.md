A uniform block has volume $45\,\mathrm L$ and density $600\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. It floats in water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. How many litres are submerged?

:::soluzione
$27\,\mathrm L$.
:::
