A force $\vec F_1$ has components $(3;7)\,\mathrm N$. After adding a second force $\vec F_2$, the resultant must have components $(1;5)\,\mathrm N$. What is the signed vertical component $F_{2,y}$ of the second force?

:::soluzione
$-2\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
