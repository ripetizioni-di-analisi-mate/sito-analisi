A vector has components $(9;11)\,\mathrm m$. What components does the vector have after multiplication by 3?

- $(27;33)\,\mathrm m$
- $(12;14)\,\mathrm m$
- $(27;11)\,\mathrm m$
- $(9;33)\,\mathrm m$

:::soluzione
$(27;33)\,\mathrm m$.
:::
