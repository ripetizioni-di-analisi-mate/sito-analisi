Which description fits vectors $u=(8;0)\,\mathrm N$ and $v=(0;8)\,\mathrm N$?

- Perpendicular with equal magnitudes
- Equal
- Opposite
- Parallel and pointing the same way

:::soluzione
Perpendicular with equal magnitudes.
:::
