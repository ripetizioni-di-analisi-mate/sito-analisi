Mix $90\,\mathrm{cm}^3$ of liquid with density $1\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$ and $10\,\mathrm{cm}^3$ of liquid with density $2\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. Assuming additive volumes, what is the mixture density?

:::soluzione
$1.1\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.
:::
