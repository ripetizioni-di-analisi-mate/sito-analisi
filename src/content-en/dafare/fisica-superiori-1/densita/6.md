An object has external volume $13\,\mathrm{cm}^3$, an internal cavity of $2\,\mathrm{cm}^3$, and material density $10\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. Neglecting the mass of air, what is its mass?

:::soluzione
$110\,\mathrm{g}$.
:::
