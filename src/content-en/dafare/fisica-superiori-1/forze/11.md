While a crate slides on a horizontal floor, a vertical rope pulls it upwards with $60\,\mathrm N$. The crate weighs $460\,\mathrm N$, remains in contact with the floor and has a coefficient of kinetic friction of $0.20$. What is the magnitude of friction?

:::soluzione
$80\,\mathrm N$.
:::
