On a graph of mass in grams against volume in cubic centimetres, a line has numerical slope $8$. What is the unit of this slope?

- $\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $\mathrm{cm^3/g}$
- $\mathrm{g\,cm^3}$
- $\mathrm g$

:::soluzione
$\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.
:::
