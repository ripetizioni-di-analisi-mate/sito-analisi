A quantity is defined as the ratio of a mass of $11\,\mathrm{kg}$ to a volume in $\mathrm m^3$. What is its SI unit?

- $\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $\mathrm{kg\,m^3}$
- $\mathrm{m^3/kg}$
- $\mathrm{kg/m^2}$

:::soluzione
$\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.
:::
