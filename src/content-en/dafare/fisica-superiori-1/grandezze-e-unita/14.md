A rectangle has base $9\,\mathrm m$. Which expression has the dimensions of an area?

- $(9\,\mathrm m)(2\,\mathrm m)$
- $(9\,\mathrm m)+(2\,\mathrm m)$
- $(9\,\mathrm m)\div(2\,\mathrm m)$
- $(9\,\mathrm m)(2\,\mathrm s)$

:::soluzione
$(9\,\mathrm m)(2\,\mathrm m)$.
:::
