titolo: "Deduction by contraposition"
teoria: [contrapposta]
parole: ["logic", "deduction"]
---

If it rains, the field is wet. The field is not wet. What necessarily follows?

- It is raining
- It is not raining
- The field is covered
- It has stopped raining
- The ground absorbs all the rain

:::soluzione

Correct answer: B. It is not raining.

:::
