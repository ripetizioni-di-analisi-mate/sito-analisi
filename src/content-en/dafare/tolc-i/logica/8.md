---
titolo: "Existence of engineers"
teoria: [quantificatori]
parole: ["logic", "deduction"]
---

All engineers in the group know Python. Some people in the group know Python. Which conclusion is justified?

- At least one engineer belongs to the group
- Everyone in the group is an engineer
- Whether any engineers belong to the group is undetermined
- Nobody is an engineer
- Anyone who knows Python is necessarily an engineer

:::soluzione

Correct answer: C. Whether any engineers belong to the group is undetermined.

:::
