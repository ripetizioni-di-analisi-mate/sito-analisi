A gas absorbs $500\,\mathrm{J}$ of heat and does $200\,\mathrm{J}$ of work on its surroundings. What is its internal-energy change?

- $+700\,\mathrm{J}$
- $+300\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-300\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-700\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$

:::soluzione
B) $+300\,\mathrm{J}$
:::
