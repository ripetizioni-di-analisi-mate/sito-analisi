What is the speed of light in a transparent medium of index $n=1.5$? Take $c=3\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

- $4.5\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.5\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.5\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$

:::soluzione
E) $2\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
:::
