A net force of $12\,\mathrm{N}$ acts on a body of mass $3\,\mathrm{kg}$. What is the acceleration magnitude?

- $36\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

:::soluzione
C) $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
:::
