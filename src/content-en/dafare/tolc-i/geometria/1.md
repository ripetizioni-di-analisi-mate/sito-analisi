A right triangle has legs of $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $8\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the hypotenuse length?

- $10\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $14\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $\sqrt{14}\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $48\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $7\,\mathrm{cm}$

:::soluzione

Answer A: $10\,\mathrm{cm}$.

:::
