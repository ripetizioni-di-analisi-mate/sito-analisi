What are the center and radius of the circle $(x-2)^2+(y+1)^2=9$?

- Center $(-2,1)$, radius $3$
- Center $(2,-1)$, radius $3$
- Center $(2,1)$, radius $9$
- Center $(2,-1)$, radius $9$
- Center $(-2,-1)$, radius $3$

:::soluzione

Answer B: Center $(2,-1)$, radius $3$.

:::
