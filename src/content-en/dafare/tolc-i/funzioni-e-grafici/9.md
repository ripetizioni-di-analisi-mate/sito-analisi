What is the real domain of $\ln(x^2-4)$?

- $(-2,2)$
- $\mathbb R\setminus\{-2,2\}$
- $[2,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,-2)\cup(2,+\infty)$
- $\mathbb R$

:::soluzione

Correct answer: D. $(-\infty,-2)\cup(2,+\infty)$

:::
