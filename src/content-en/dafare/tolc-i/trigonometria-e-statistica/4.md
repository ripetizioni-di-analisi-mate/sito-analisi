Which expression equals $\sin(2x)$ for every real $x$?

- $2\sin x$
- $\sin^2x$
- $\cos^2x-\sin^2x$
- $2\sin x\cos x$
- $2\cos x$

:::soluzione

Correct answer: D. $2\sin x\cos x$

:::
