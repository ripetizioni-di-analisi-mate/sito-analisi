teoria: ["stevino"]
parole: ["Absolute pressure below a surface"]
---

A stationary liquid of density $\rho=860\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ has surface pressure $p_0=101300\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Find absolute pressure at depth $h=2.5\,\mathrm m$. Take $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

:::soluzione
$$p=p_0+\rho gh=(101300+860\cdot9.81\cdot2.5)\,\mathrm{Pa}\simeq1.22\cdot10^{5}\,\mathrm{Pa}$$
:::
