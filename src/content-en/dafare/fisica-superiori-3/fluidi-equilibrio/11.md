titolo: Absolute pressure below a surface
teoria: ["stevino"]
parole: ["Absolute pressure below a surface"]
---

A stationary liquid of density $\rho=880\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ has surface pressure $p_0=101300\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Find absolute pressure at depth $h=3\,\mathrm m$. Take $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

:::soluzione
$p=1.27\cdot10^{5}\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
:::
