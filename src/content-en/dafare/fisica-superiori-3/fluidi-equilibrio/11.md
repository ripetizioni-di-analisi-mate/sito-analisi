---

A chamber has a gauge pressure of $-18\,\mathrm{kPa}$ relative to the atmosphere pressing on the outside of its flat lid. The lid has area $0.25\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Taking the direction from inside to outside as positive, find the resultant of the pressure forces in newtons.

:::soluzione
$$F=p_{\rm rel}A=-18000\cdot0.25\,\mathrm{N}=-4500\,\mathrm{N}$$
:::
