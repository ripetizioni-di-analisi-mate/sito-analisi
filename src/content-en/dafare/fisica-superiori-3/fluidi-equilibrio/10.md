---
titolo: "An unknown thickness in two layers"
teoria: ["quota-colonna"]
parole: ["An unknown thickness in two layers"]
---

An open vessel contains oil of density $800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ above water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. The total liquid depth is $1.50\,\mathrm m$ and the gauge pressure at the bottom is $13800\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and find the thickness of the oil.

:::soluzione
$$h_o=\dfrac{\rho_a gH-p_{\rm rel}}{(\rho_a-\rho_o)g}=0.60\,\mathrm{m}$$
:::
