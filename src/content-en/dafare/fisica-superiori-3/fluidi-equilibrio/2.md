In stationary water of density $\rho=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$, find the pressure increase on descending by $h=1.1\,\mathrm m$. Take $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

:::soluzione
$$\Delta p=\rho gh=1000\cdot9.81\cdot1.1\,\mathrm{Pa}\simeq1.08\cdot10^{4}\,\mathrm{Pa}$$
:::
