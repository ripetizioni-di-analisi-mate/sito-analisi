An ideal hydraulic press has pistons at the same height, of areas $A_1=0.0023\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and $A_2=0.023\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The input force on the smaller one is $F_1=80\,\mathrm N$. Neglecting piston weights and friction, find the balanced force on the second piston.

:::soluzione
$F_2=800\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
