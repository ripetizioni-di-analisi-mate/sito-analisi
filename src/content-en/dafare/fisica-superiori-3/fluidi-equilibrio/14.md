A scale beneath a tank of water reads $53\,\mathrm N$. An object hangs from a separate support. It is fully immersed and held at rest without touching the tank or spilling water. The object displaces $0.0014\,\mathrm{m^3}$ of water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what force does the scale now read?

:::soluzione
$$F_{\rm b}=53\,\mathrm N+\rho gV=(53+14)\,\mathrm{N}=67\,\mathrm{N}$$
:::
