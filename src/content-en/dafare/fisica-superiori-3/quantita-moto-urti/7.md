parole: ["Final velocity in an elastic collision"]
---

A mass $m_1=5\,\mathrm{kg}$ with velocity $v_{1i}=4.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ collides elastically in one dimension with a stationary mass $m_2=6\,\mathrm{kg}$. With no external impulse find the final velocity of the second mass.

:::soluzione
$v_{2f}=4.09\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
