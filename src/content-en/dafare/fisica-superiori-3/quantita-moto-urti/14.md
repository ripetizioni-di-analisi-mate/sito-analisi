parole: ["Placing a third mass"]
---

On a track, a $1\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass sits at $-2\,\mathrm m$ and a $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass at $3\,\mathrm m$. At what coordinate $x$ should a $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass be placed so that the overall centre of mass is at $1\,\mathrm m$?

:::soluzione
Each mass contributes its weighted coordinate to the numerator. For the full system, the required numerator is $6\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m}$. The two fixed masses contribute $4\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m}$. The third mass must supply the remaining positive $2\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m}$.

$$
x=\frac{(1+2+3)\cdot1-1\cdot(-2)-2\cdot3}{3}\,\mathrm m=\frac{2}{3}\,\mathrm m.
$$

$x=\frac{2}{3}\,\mathrm m$.
:::
