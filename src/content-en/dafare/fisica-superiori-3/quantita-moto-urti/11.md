---
titolo: "Average force from the floor"
teoria: ["impulso"]
parole: ["Average force from the floor"]
---

A $0.50\,\mathrm{kg}$ ball strikes a floor at $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ downwards and leaves at $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$ upwards after $0.050\,\mathrm s$ of contact. Use $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and neglect air resistance. Find the magnitude of the average floor force.

:::soluzione
With upwards positive, the velocity changes by $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The average net force combines the upward floor force and the downward weight. To isolate the force from the floor, add the weight's magnitude to the $40\,\mathrm N$ found from the change in velocity.

$$
\overline{N}=m\frac{v_f-v_i}{\Delta t}+mg=\left(\frac{0.50\cdot(1-(-3))}{0.050}+0.50\cdot10\right)\,\mathrm N=45\,\mathrm N.
$$

$\overline{N}=45\,\mathrm N$.
:::
