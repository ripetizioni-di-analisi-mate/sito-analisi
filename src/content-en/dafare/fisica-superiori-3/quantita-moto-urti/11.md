An object of mass $m=0.6\,\mathrm{kg}$ and initial speed $v=8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ comes to rest in $\Delta t=0.18\,\mathrm s$. Find the magnitude of the mean net force.

:::soluzione
$F_m=26.7\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
