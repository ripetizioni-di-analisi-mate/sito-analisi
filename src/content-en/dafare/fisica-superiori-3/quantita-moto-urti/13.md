---
titolo: "Separation with a moving centre of mass"
teoria: ["centro-massa"]
parole: ["Separation with a moving centre of mass"]
---

Two carts of $2$ and $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ initially move together at $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the positive direction. An internal spring separates them. Their signed final velocities satisfy $v_1-v_2=8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the external impulse is zero. Find $v_2$ in the laboratory frame.

:::soluzione
Substitute $v_1=v_2+8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ into the momentum balance. The centre of mass keeps moving forwards while the second cart reverses. The first cart leaves at $5.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and offsets the negative contribution from the second.

$$
v_2=\left(\frac{(2+3)\cdot1-2\cdot8}{2+3}\right)\,\mathrm{m/s}=-2.2\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$

$v_2=-2.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
