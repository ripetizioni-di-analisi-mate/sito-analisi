A cart of mass $m=5\,\mathrm{kg}$ and speed $v=6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ strikes an identical stationary cart. They stick with no external impulse. Find the kinetic energy lost in the collision.

:::soluzione
$E_{persa}=45\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
