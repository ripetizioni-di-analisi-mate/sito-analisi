An initially stationary system splits with no external impulse into two bodies of masses $m_1=1.6\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $m_2=4.9\,\mathrm{kg}$. The first has velocity $v_1=3.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the positive direction. Find the component $v_2$.

:::soluzione
$v_2=-1.14\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
