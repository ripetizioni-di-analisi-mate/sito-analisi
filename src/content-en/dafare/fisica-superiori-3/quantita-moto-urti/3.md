An object of mass $m=0.5\,\mathrm{kg}$ and initial speed $v=7\,\mathrm{m/s}$ comes to rest in $\Delta t=0.16\,\mathrm s$. Find the magnitude of the mean net force.

:::soluzione
$F_m=21.9\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
