Water flows steadily through a real horizontal Venturi with no pumps. Pressure readings are $132\,\mathrm{kPa}$ at the inlet, $116\,\mathrm{kPa}$ in the throat and $128\,\mathrm{kPa}$ after the expansion. The inlet and expanded sections have equal areas and identical velocity profiles. What is the total mechanical energy loss per unit volume, expressed in kilopascals?

:::soluzione
$p_1-p_3=4\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
:::
