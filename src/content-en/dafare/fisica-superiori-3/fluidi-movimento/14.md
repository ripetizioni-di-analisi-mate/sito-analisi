titolo: Pressure at a different height and speed
teoria: ["bernoulli"]
parole: ["Pressure at a different height and speed"]
---

Ideal water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ flows steadily along a streamline. Section 1 has $p_1=200000\,\mathrm{Pa}$ and $v_1=1.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Section 2 is higher by $h=1.3\,\mathrm m$ and has $v_2=2.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. With no pumps or losses find $p_2$. Take $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

:::soluzione
$p_2=1.85\cdot10^{5}\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
:::
