Across a pipe section of $0.006\,\mathrm{m^2}$ the flow is entirely normal to the section and in the same direction. The speed is $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ across one third of the area and $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ across the rest. Find the total flow rate in litres per second.

:::soluzione
$Q=12\,\mathrm{L/s}$.
:::
