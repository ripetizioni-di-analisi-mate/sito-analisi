Water flows uniformly and perpendicularly through a section of area $A=0.0016\,\mathrm{m^2}$ at speed $v=1.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find the volume flow rate.

:::soluzione
$Q=0.00256\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$.
:::
