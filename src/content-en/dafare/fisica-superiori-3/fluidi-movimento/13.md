---
titolo: Exit speed from an open reservoir
teoria: ["torricelli"]
parole: ["Exit speed from an open reservoir"]
---

An open reservoir has a free surface much larger than a small hole at depth $h=1.3\,\mathrm m$. With equal air pressure on the surface and jet and negligible losses, find exit speed. Take $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

:::soluzione
$v=5.05\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
