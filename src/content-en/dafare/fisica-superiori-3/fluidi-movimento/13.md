A large closed tank discharges water into the air through a small hole $0.30\,\mathrm m$ below the free surface. The jet emerges at $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Use $\rho=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, with nearly constant level and negligible losses. By how many pascals does free-surface pressure exceed atmospheric pressure?

:::soluzione
$p_{\mathrm s}-p_{\mathrm{atm}}=9500\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
:::
