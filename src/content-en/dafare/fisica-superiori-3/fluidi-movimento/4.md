Ideal water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ travels steadily through a horizontal pipe. The speeds are $v_1=1.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_2=3.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$, with $v_2>v_1$. Find $p_1-p_2$.

:::soluzione
$\Delta p=5.64\cdot10^{3}\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
:::
