A Newtonian fluid of viscosity $\eta=0.056\,\mathrm{Pa\cdot s}$ has steady laminar flow in a long tube of radius $r=0.00106\,\mathrm m$ and length $L=1.0\,\mathrm m$. The pressure difference is $\Delta p=130\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Use Poiseuille's law to find the flow rate in cubic millimetres per second ($1\,\mathrm{mm^3}=10^{-9}\,\mathrm{m^3}$).

:::soluzione
$Q=1.15\,\mathrm{mm^3/s}$.
:::
