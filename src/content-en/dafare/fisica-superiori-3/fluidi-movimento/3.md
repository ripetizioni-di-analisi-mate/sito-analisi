An incompressible fluid flows steadily through a pipe with no leaks or branches. In the first section $A_1=0.0046\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and $v_1=1.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. In the second section $A_2=0.0023\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Find $v_2$.

:::soluzione
$v_2=3.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
