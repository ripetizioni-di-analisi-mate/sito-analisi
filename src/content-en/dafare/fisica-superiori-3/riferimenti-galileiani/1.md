The origins of two frames with parallel, consistently oriented axes coincide at $t=0$. The moving frame translates at $u=5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the positive direction. A point has relative coordinate $x'=6\,\mathrm m$ at time $t=4\,\mathrm s$. Find its coordinate in the fixed frame.

:::soluzione
$x=26\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
