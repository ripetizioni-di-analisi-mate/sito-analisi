A river is $d=50\,\mathrm m$ wide. A boat points perpendicular to the banks at relative speed $v_b=3.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The current has speed $u=1.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find the drift along the bank.

:::soluzione
$x=18.4\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
