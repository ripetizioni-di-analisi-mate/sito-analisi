A train travels at $u=15\,\mathrm{m/s}$. A passenger walks towards the front at $v’=1.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$ relative to the carriage. Find the velocity relative to the ground.

:::soluzione
$v=16.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
