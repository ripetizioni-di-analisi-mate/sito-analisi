An object has acceleration $a=4.9\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ relative to the ground. A cabin whose axes are parallel to and oriented the same way as the ground axes accelerates in the same direction with $A=1.3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the object's acceleration component relative to the cabin.

:::soluzione
$a’=3.6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
:::
