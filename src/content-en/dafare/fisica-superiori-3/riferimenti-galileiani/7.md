A body has acceleration $a=4.9\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ relative to the ground. A carriage with parallel axes accelerates in the same direction with $A=1.3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the component of the body's acceleration relative to the carriage.

:::soluzione
$a’=3.6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
:::
