A runner at $v_A=8.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ chases another at $v_B=3.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$, initially $d=45\,\mathrm m$ ahead. Both velocities are constant and in the same direction. Find the time to catch up, rounded to the nearest hundredth of a second.

:::soluzione
$t=9.18\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
