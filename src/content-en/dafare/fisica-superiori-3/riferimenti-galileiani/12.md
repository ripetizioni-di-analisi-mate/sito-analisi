---

At $t=0$ runner $A$ is $45.6\,\mathrm m$ behind $B$ and runs at $8.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. $B$ runs in the same direction at $5.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for the first $2.0\,\mathrm s$, then changes to $3.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. At what time does $A$ catch $B$? Round to the nearest hundredth of a second.

:::soluzione
$t=9.62\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
