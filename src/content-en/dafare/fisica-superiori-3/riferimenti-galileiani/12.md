A runner at $v_A=8.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ pursues another at $v_B=3.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$, initially $d=50\,\mathrm m$ ahead. Both speeds are constant and both runners move in the same direction. Find the catch-up time.

:::soluzione
$t=9.62\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
