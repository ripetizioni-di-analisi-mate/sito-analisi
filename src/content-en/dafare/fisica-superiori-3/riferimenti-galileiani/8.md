A carriage accelerates rightwards with magnitude $A=0.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. For a body of mass $m=5\,\mathrm{kg}$, find the magnitude of the apparent force due to translation.

:::soluzione
$F_{app}=4\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
