A moving walkway travels at $u=2.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. A person walks along it in the opposite direction with relative speed $w=0.7\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find the ground-velocity component, taking the walkway's direction as positive.

:::soluzione
$v=2.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
