parole: ["Coordinates with offset clocks"]
---

A moving frame has axes parallel to and pointing the same way as the ground axes. At ground time $t=0$ its origin is at $X_0=52\,\mathrm m$ and moves at $u=-3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Its clock reads $t'=t+2\,\mathrm s$. What is the origin's ground coordinate when its clock reads $7\,\mathrm s$?

:::soluzione
$x=37\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
