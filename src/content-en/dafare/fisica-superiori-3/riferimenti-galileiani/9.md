The origins of two frames coincide at $t=0$. The moving frame translates at $u=6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the positive direction. A point has moving-frame coordinate $x’=7\,\mathrm m$ at $t=5\,\mathrm s$. Find its coordinate in the stationary frame.

:::soluzione
$x=37\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
