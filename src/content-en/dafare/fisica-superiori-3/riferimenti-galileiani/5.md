A river is $45.0\,\mathrm m$ wide. The boat maintains a constant velocity relative to the water, perpendicular to the banks, and the uniform current is parallel to the banks. When it has travelled $30.0\,\mathrm m$ across the river, the boat is already $10.8\,\mathrm m$ downstream from its starting point. What is the drift distance on arrival?

:::soluzione
$x=16.2\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
