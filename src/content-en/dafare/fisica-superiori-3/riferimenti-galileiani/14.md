A drone has velocity $(1,2.5)\,\mathrm{m/s}$ relative to a ferry. The ferry has velocity $(0,3)\,\mathrm{m/s}$ relative to the water, and the water has velocity $(2,0)\,\mathrm{m/s}$ relative to the bank. The axes are parallel and point the same way. What is the drone's speed relative to the bank? Round to two decimal places.

:::soluzione
$v=6.26\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
