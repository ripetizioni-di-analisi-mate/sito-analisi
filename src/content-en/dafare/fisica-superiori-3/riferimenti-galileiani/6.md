The $x$-axis points downstream and the $y$-axis towards the opposite bank. A boat's velocity relative to the water is $(-1.05,\,4.20)\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The uniform current flows downstream at $4.20\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the magnitude of the boat's ground velocity?

:::soluzione
$v=5.25\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
