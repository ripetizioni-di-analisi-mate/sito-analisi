A boat's velocity relative to the water has magnitude $v_b=5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ perpendicular to the bank. The current parallel to the bank has magnitude $u=1.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find the magnitude of the velocity over the ground.

:::soluzione
$v=5.25\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
