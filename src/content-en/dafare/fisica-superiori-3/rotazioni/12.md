titolo: Angular acceleration from torque
teoria: ["dinamica-rotazione"]
parole: ["Angular acceleration from torque"]
---

A rigid body rotates about a fixed axis with moment of inertia $I=0.4\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$. The net axial torque is $M=3\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$. Find the angular acceleration.

:::soluzione
$\alpha=7.5\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$.
:::
