A rotor of radius $0.25\,\mathrm m$ and inertia $0.40\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ has a fixed axis. At the leftmost point, a $32\,\mathrm N$ force makes a $30^\circ$ angle with the outward radius and has a downward vertical component. At the rightmost point, a $4\,\mathrm N$ tangential force points downwards. Calculate $\alpha$ with anticlockwise rotation positive.

:::soluzione
$\alpha=7.5\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$.
:::
