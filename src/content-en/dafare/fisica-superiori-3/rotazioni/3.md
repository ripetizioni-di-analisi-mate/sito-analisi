---

A uniform solid disc has mass $m=5\,\mathrm{kg}$ and radius $R=0.26\,\mathrm m$. Find its moment of inertia about the central axis perpendicular to the disc.

:::soluzione
$I=0.169\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$.
:::
