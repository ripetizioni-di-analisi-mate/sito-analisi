Two flywheels on the same axis rotate at $-4$ and $+22\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ and have moments of inertia of $1.2$ and $0.8\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ respectively. An internal clutch engages until they rotate together. The angular impulse from external torques along the axis is negligible. What is the final signed angular velocity?

:::soluzione
$\omega_f=6.4\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
:::
