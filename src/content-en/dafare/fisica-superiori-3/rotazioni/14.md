A system changes from moment of inertia $I_i=3.2\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ to $I_f=1.4\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ with no external axial torque. It initially rotates at $\omega_i=2.8\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. Find $\omega_f$.

:::soluzione
$\omega_f=6.4\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
:::
