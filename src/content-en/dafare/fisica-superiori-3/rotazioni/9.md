A controller measures an initial angular velocity of $+6\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. For the first $2\,\mathrm s$ it applies $\alpha=+3\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$. Over the next $1\,\mathrm s$ it applies $\alpha=-1.5\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$. What is the final signed angular velocity?

:::soluzione
$\omega=10.5\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
:::
