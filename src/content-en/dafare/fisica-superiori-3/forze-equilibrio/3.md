An object of mass $m=5\,\mathrm{kg}$ slides on a fixed horizontal surface with coefficient of kinetic friction $\mu_d=0.13$. Its weight and the normal force are the only vertical forces. Find the magnitude of friction. Use $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

:::soluzione
$f_d=6.38\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
