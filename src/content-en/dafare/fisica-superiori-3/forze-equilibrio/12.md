parole: ["Stopping distance on an uphill slope"]
---

A block moves uphill on a ramp with initial speed $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The slope has $\sin\theta=0.60$ and $\cos\theta=0.80$. Use $\mu_d=0.25$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What distance $s$ does it travel along the ramp before its speed reaches zero?

:::soluzione
$s=1.00\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
