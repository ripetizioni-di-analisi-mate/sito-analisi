A mass $m=0.5\,\mathrm{kg}$ hangs at rest from an ideal vertical spring of spring constant $k=160\,\mathrm{N/m}$. Find the extension from the natural length. Use $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

:::soluzione
$x=0.0307\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
