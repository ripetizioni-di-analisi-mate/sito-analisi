An ideal lever of negligible weight experiences $F_1=35\,\mathrm N$ with moment arm $b_1=0.36\,\mathrm m$. A second force produces the opposite torque with moment arm $b_2=0.66\,\mathrm m$. Find $F_2$ for rotational equilibrium.

:::soluzione
$F_2=19.1\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
