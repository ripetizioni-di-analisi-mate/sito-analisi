An object of mass $m=5\,\mathrm{kg}$ is on a slope at $30^\circ$. Find the magnitude of the component of its weight along the slope. Use $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

:::soluzione
$P_{\parallel}=24.5\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
