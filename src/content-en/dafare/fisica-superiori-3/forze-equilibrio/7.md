A force of magnitude $F=16\,\mathrm N$ acts perpendicular to a lever at distance $b=0.26\,\mathrm m$ from its pivot. Find the magnitude of the torque.

:::soluzione
$M=4.16\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$.
:::
