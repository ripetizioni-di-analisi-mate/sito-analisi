A cart is connected to two walls by ideal horizontal springs. The left spring has $k_L=140\,\mathrm{N/m}$ and an extension of $0.050\,\mathrm m$. The right spring has $k_R=70\,\mathrm{N/m}$ and an extension of $0.020\,\mathrm m$. What is the component $F_{e,x}$ of the net spring force, taking rightwards as positive?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 linee="-3,-0.58 -3,1.0;3,-0.58 3,1.0;-3,0.16 -2.82,0.16 -2.67,0.33 -2.47,-0.01 -2.27,0.33 -2.07,-0.01 -1.87,0.33 -1.67,-0.01 -1.47,0.33 -1.27,-0.01 -1.07,0.33 -0.87,0.16 -0.52,0.16;0.52,0.16 0.85,0.16 1.07,0.33 1.27,-0.01 1.47,0.33 1.67,-0.01 1.87,0.33 2.07,-0.01 2.27,0.33 2.47,-0.01 2.67,0.33 2.82,0.16 3,0.16" poligoni="-.52,-.22 .52,-.22 .52,.56 -.52,.56" etichette="-2.2,.62,kₗ;2.08,.62,kᵣ" xmin=-3.32 xmax=3.32 ymin=-.76 ymax=1.2 titolo="The stretched springs pull the cart towards opposite anchors."}

:::soluzione
$F_{e,x}=-5.6\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
