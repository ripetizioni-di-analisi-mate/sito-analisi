parole: ["Outward pull at the threshold of release"]
---

A sled weighing $50\,\mathrm N$ slides in contact with a slope for which $\cos\theta=4/5$. An additional force pulls outwards perpendicular to the surface. What is its magnitude $F_{\max}$ at the threshold where the normal reaction becomes zero?

:::soluzione
$F_{\max}=40\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
