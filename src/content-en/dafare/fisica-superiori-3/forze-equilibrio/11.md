A sled slides over a fixed horizontal surface. The normal force on the first runner is $20\,\mathrm N$ and on the second it is $30\,\mathrm N$. Their kinetic friction coefficients are $0.10$ and $0.15$, respectively. What is the magnitude $f_d$ of the total friction force?

:::soluzione
$f_d=6.5\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
