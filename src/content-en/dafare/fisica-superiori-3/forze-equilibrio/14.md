A junction at rest supports a weight of $30\,\mathrm N$. The left string extends horizontally to the left. The right string rises to the right, with $\sin\theta=3/5$ and $\cos\theta=4/5$ for its angle above the horizontal. What is the tension $T_L$ in the left string?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 vettori="0,0,-1.6,0,T_L;0,0,1.6,1.2,T_R;0,0,0,-1.2,P" punti="0,0,pieno" segmenti="1.6,0,1.6,1.2;0,0,1.6,0" xmin=-2.08 xmax=2.15 ymin=-1.53 ymax=1.57 titolo="Only the right string has an upward component."}

:::soluzione
$T_L=40\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
