An object of mass $m=3\,\mathrm{kg}$ hangs at rest from two ideal symmetric strings making $30^\circ$ with the horizontal. Find the tension in each string. Use $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

:::soluzione
$T=29.4\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
