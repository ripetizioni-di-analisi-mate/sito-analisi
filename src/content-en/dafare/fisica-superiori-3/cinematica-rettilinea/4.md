An object starts with $v_0=5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and maintains acceleration $a=1.1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ for $t=4\,\mathrm s$. Calculate its final velocity.

:::soluzione
$v=9.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
