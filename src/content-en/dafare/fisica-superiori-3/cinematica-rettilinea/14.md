A vehicle has initial speed $v_0=14\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and brakes with constant deceleration magnitude $d=2.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Calculate its stopping distance, excluding reaction time.

:::soluzione
$s=35\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
