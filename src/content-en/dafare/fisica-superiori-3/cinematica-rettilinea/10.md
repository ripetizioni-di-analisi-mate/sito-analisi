In an object's velocity–time graph, a straight line begins at $+4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at $t=0$, crosses the axis at $t=2\,\mathrm s$ and reaches $-6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at $t=5\,\mathrm s$. What distance does the object travel in the first $5\,\mathrm s$?

:::soluzione
$13\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
