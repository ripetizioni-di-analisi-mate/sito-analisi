A cyclist rides $120\,\mathrm m$ in a straight line at $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and returns to her starting point along the same route without stopping. If her average speed over the full trip is $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is her constant speed on the return leg?

:::soluzione
$3\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
