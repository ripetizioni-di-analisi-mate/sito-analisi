A mass of $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ hangs from a vertical spring of stiffness $200\,\mathrm{N/m}$. The extension changes from $0.10$ to $0.26\,\mathrm m$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, find the change in the sum of elastic and gravitational potential energy.

:::soluzione
$$\Delta U=\bigl[100(0.26^2-0.10^2)-30(0.26-0.10)\bigr]\,\mathrm{J}=0.96\,\mathrm{J}$$
:::
