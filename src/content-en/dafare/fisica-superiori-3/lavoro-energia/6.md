titolo: Speed after a frictionless descent
teoria: ["bilancio-energia"]
parole: ["Speed after a frictionless descent"]
---

An object starts from rest and travels down a frictionless track with vertical drop $h=2.5\,\mathrm m$. Ignoring air resistance, find its final speed. Take $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

:::soluzione
$v=7\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
