titolo: Net work from a change in speed
teoria: ["energia-cinetica"]
parole: ["Net work from a change in speed"]
---

An object of mass $m=5\,\mathrm{kg}$ changes speed from $v_i=3.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $v_f=8.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Calculate the total work done by the forces.

:::soluzione
$$W_{\rm tot}=\dfrac12\cdot5(8.1^2-3.5^2)\,\mathrm{J}\simeq133\,\mathrm{J}$$
:::
