parole: ["Remaining braking distance"]
---

A vehicle brakes on level ground under a net force of constant magnitude. In the first $4.0\,\mathrm m$ its speed falls from $8.0$ to $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. If the same force acts until it stops, how many more metres does it travel? Round to two decimal places.

:::soluzione
$$d=4.0\cdot\dfrac{6^2}{8^2-6^2}\,\mathrm{m}\simeq5.14\,\mathrm{m}$$
:::
