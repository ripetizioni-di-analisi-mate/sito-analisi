An object of mass $m=6\,\mathrm{kg}$ changes speed from $v_i=4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $v_f=8.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Calculate the total work done by the forces.

:::soluzione
$W_{tot}=184\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
