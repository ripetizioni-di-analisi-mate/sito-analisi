parole: ["Energy in the train frame"]
---

An observer on the ground measures $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for a $6\,\mathrm{kg}$ trolley. A train moves in a straight line at $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the same direction. Find the trolley's kinetic energy in the train's frame.

:::soluzione
$$K=\dfrac12\cdot6(7-3)^2\,\mathrm{J}=48\,\mathrm{J}$$
:::
