teoria: ["orbite"]
parole: ["Speed in a circular orbit"]
---

A planet has gravitational parameter $\mu=GM=3.94\cdot10^{14}\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$. A satellite of negligible mass follows a circular orbit of radius $r=7800000\,\mathrm m$ from the centre. Find the orbital speed.

:::soluzione
$v=7.11\cdot10^{3}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
