parole: ["Field at a given radius"]
---

A spherical planet has surface field $g_0=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the field magnitude at distance $r=2.8R$ from its centre, where $R$ is its radius.

:::soluzione
$g_r=1.28\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
:::
