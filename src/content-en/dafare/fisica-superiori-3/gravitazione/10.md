At a probe’s position, two sources produce perpendicular fields of magnitudes $3g_*$ and $4g_*$, with $g_*=0.256\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the magnitude of the total field?

:::soluzione
$g_r=1.28\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
:::
