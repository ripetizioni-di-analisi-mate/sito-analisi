A planet has $\mu=GM=3.94\cdot10^{14}\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$. A satellite travels in a circular orbit of radius $r=7800000\,\mathrm m$. Find the period.

:::soluzione
$T=6.9\cdot10^{3}\,\mathrm{s}$.
:::
