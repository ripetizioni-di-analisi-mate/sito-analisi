A body of mass $m=180\,\mathrm{kg}$ is at distance $r=7800000\,\mathrm m$ from a planet with $\mu=GM=3.94\cdot10^{14}\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$. Find the potential energy with zero at infinity.

:::soluzione
$U=-9.09\cdot10^{9}\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
