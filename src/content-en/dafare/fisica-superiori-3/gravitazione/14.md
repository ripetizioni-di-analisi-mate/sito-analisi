A probe initially has $K_i=2.00\cdot10^9\,\mathrm J$ and $U_i=-6.00\cdot10^9\,\mathrm J$, with zero at infinity. Only gravity acts. When kinetic energy reaches $5.09\cdot10^9\,\mathrm J$, what is the potential energy?

:::soluzione
$U=-9.09\cdot10^{9}\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
