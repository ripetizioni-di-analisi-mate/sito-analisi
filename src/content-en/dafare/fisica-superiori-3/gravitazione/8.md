A satellite of mass $m=160\,\mathrm{kg}$ travels in a circular orbit of radius $r=7600000\,\mathrm m$ around a planet with $\mu=GM=3.93\cdot10^{14}\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$. Find the total orbital mechanical energy, with zero potential at infinity.

:::soluzione
$E=-4.14\cdot10^{9}\,\mathrm{J}$.
:::
