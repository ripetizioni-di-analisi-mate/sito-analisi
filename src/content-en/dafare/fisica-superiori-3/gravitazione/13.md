From centre distance $r=7200000\,\mathrm m$ for a planet with $\mu=GM=3.94\cdot10^{14}\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$, find the minimum escape speed, with no atmosphere or later thrust.

:::soluzione
$v_f=1.05\cdot10^{4}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
