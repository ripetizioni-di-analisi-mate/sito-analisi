A $70\,\mathrm{kg}$ probe in free fall towards a small asteroid has gravitational acceleration of magnitude $1.70\cdot10^{-8}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the magnitude of the force the probe exerts on the asteroid? Give the result in newtons.

:::soluzione
$F=1.19\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
