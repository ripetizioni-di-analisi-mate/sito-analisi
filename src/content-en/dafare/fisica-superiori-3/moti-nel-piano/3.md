A projectile has initial vertical velocity $v_{0y}=9\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Ignore drag and calculate the maximum rise above launch. Use $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

:::soluzione
$H=4.13\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
