A trolley follows the line $y=1-\dfrac43x$, with coordinates in metres. Over $4\,\mathrm{s}$ its horizontal coordinate decreases by $12\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the magnitude of its average velocity?

:::soluzione
$v_m=5\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
:::
