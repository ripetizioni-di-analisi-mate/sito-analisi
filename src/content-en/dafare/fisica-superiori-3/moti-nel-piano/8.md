In circular motion the tangential acceleration has magnitude $a_t=5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, and the centripetal acceleration has magnitude $a_c=6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the magnitude of the resultant acceleration.

:::soluzione
$a=7.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
:::
