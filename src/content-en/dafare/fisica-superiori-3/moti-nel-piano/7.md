An object travels round a circle of radius $r=2.6\,\mathrm{m}$ at constant speed $v=3.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find the magnitude of its centripetal acceleration.

:::soluzione
$a_c=4.71\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
:::
