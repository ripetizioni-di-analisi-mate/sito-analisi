A projectile has initial vertical velocity $v_{0y}=10\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Ignore drag and calculate the maximum rise above launch. Use $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

:::soluzione
$H=5.1\,\mathrm{m}$.
