In an oblique launch, a ball passes a reference height at $t=0.4\,\mathrm{s}$ and again at $t=1.2\,\mathrm{s}$. Use $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and neglect air resistance. How far above the reference height is its apex?

:::soluzione
$\Delta H=0.8\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
