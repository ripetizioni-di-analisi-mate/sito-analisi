Two disks on fixed, parallel axes touch at their rims and turn without slipping at the contact. The first has radius $0.40\,\mathrm{m}$ and the second radius $0.15\,\mathrm{m}$. The first has angular speed $6\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. What is the angular speed of the second?

:::soluzione
$\omega_2=16\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
:::
