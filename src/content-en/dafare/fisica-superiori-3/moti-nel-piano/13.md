A disk rotates uniformly anticlockwise. Two pictures $0.30\,\mathrm{s}$ apart show a mark first to the right of the centre and then at the opposite end of that diameter. A sensor records the mark crossing its initial position once between the pictures, excluding the endpoints. What is the angular speed, rounded to two decimal places?

:::soluzione
$\omega=10\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}\simeq31.42\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
:::
