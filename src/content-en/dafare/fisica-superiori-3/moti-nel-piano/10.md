A ball is launched horizontally from the vertical line $x=0$. Sensors record it at height $12\,\mathrm{m}$ when $x=2\,\mathrm{m}$ and at height $8\,\mathrm{m}$ when $x=6\,\mathrm{m}$. Gravity is uniform and air resistance is negligible. At what horizontal coordinate does it meet the floor at height zero?

:::soluzione
$R=10\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
