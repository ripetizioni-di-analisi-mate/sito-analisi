An object is launched at $45^\circ$ with initial speed $v_0=14\,\mathrm{m/s}$. It lands at the launch height. Ignore drag and calculate the range. Use $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

:::soluzione
$R=20\,\mathrm{m}$.
:::
