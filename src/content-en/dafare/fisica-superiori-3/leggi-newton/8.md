An object of mass $m=0.8\,\mathrm{kg}$ follows a circle of radius $r=1.6\,\mathrm m$ at constant speed $v=2.9\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find the magnitude of the net radial force.

:::soluzione
$F_r=4.21\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
