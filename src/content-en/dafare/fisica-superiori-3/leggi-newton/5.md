Two carts of masses $m_1=5\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $m_2=6\,\mathrm{kg}$ are connected by an ideal string on a smooth horizontal surface. A horizontal force $F=35\,\mathrm N$ pulls the system. Calculate their common acceleration.

:::soluzione
$a=3.18\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
:::
