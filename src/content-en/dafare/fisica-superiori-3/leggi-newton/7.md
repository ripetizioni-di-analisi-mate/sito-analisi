Two masses $m_1=1.6\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $m_2=3.9\,\mathrm{kg}$, with $m_2>m_1$, hang from an inextensible string over an ideal massless, frictionless pulley. Calculate the acceleration magnitude. Use $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

:::soluzione
$a=4.1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
:::
