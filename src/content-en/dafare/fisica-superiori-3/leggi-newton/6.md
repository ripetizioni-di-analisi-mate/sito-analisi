Two carts of masses $m_1=5\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $m_2=6\,\mathrm{kg}$ are joined by an ideal string on a smooth surface. A force $F=35\,\mathrm N$ pulls the first cart. Find the tension pulling the second.

:::soluzione
$T=19.1\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
