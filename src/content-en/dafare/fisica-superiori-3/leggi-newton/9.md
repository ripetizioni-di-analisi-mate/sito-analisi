The graph shows the signed velocity $v_x$ of a cart. The first segment runs from $-14\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in $3\,\mathrm s$. What is the component $a_x$ at $t=1\,\mathrm s$?

::grafico{linee="0,-14 3,0 5,4" punti="0,-14,pieno;3,0,pieno;5,4,pieno" xmin=0 xmax=5.5 ymin=-15.8 ymax=6 nomi="t (s),vₓ (m/s)" titolo="The velocity graph changes slope at 3 s."}

:::soluzione
$a_x=\dfrac{14}{3}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
:::
