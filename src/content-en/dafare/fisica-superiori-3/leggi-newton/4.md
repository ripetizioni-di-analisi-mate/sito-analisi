A person of mass $m=56\,\mathrm{kg}$ stands on a scale in a lift accelerating upwards at $a=0.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the normal force measured by the scale. Use $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

:::soluzione
$N=594\,\mathrm{N}$.
:::
