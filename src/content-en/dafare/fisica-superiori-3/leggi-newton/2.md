An object of mass $m=5\,\mathrm{kg}$ experiences $F_1=29\,\mathrm N$ to the right and $F_2=8\,\mathrm N$ to the left. Calculate its acceleration with right positive.

:::soluzione
$a=4.2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
:::
