Two carts at rest touch on a smooth horizontal track. $A$, with mass $6\,\mathrm{kg}$, is to the left of $B$, with mass $8\,\mathrm{kg}$. They are not attached. A force of $40\,\mathrm N$ to the right acts on $B$ alone. What is the component $a_B$ immediately afterwards, taking rightwards as positive?

:::soluzione
$a_B=5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
:::
