Three readings of the same length are $11.5\,\mathrm{cm}$, $11.7\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $11.4\,\mathrm{cm}$. Calculate the arithmetic mean and round to the nearest tenth of a centimetre.

:::soluzione
$\bar\ell=11.5\,\mathrm{cm}$.
:::
