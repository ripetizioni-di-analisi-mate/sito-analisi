A slot is $(31.0\pm0.4)\,\mathrm{mm}$ wide and the part that must fit into it is $(29.0\pm0.3)\,\mathrm{mm}$ wide. Assuming any pair of values from the two intervals is possible, what is the minimum clearance, meaning the slot width minus the part width?

:::soluzione
$1.3\,\mathrm{mm}$.
:::
