The same sensor records two positions, $32\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $44\,\mathrm{cm}$. Each reading includes the same additive calibration error with magnitude at most $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ and an individual error with magnitude at most $1\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the maximum uncertainty in their difference?

:::soluzione
$2\,\mathrm{cm}$.
:::
