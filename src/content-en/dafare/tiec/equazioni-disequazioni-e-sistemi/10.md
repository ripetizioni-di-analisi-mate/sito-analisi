---
titolo: Quadratic inequality with no constant term
teoria: [segno-trinomio]
parole: ["quadratic inequality", "values outside the roots"]
---

Which values satisfy $x^2 > 4x$?

- A) $x > 4$
- B) $0 < x < 4$
- C) $x < 0 \;\vee\; x > 4$
- D) $x < -4 \;\vee\; x > 0$

:::soluzione
Answer C. $x < 0 \;\vee\; x > 4$.
:::
