What are the solutions of $4x^2 - 9 = 0$?

- A) only $x = \dfrac{3}{2}$
- B) $x = \pm\dfrac{9}{4}$
- C) $x = \pm\dfrac{2}{3}$
- D) $x = \pm\dfrac{3}{2}$

:::soluzione
Answer D. $x = \pm\dfrac{3}{2}$.
:::
