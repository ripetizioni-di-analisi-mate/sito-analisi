The equation
$$
\frac{x + 3}{x - 1} = \frac{4}{x - 1}
$$
has which solutions?

- A) $x = 1$
- B) $x = -1$
- C) no real number
- D) every $x \neq 1$

:::soluzione
Answer C. No real number.
:::
