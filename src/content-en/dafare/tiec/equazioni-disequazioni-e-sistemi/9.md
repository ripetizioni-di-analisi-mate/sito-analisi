A phone plan costs $12$ euros per month plus $0.10$ euros per gigabyte of data. If $g \geq 0$ is the number of gigabytes used in a month, when is the monthly bill at most $20$ euros?

- A) $0 \leq g \leq 80$
- B) $0 \leq g \leq 200$
- C) $g \geq 80$
- D) $0 \leq g \leq 320$

:::soluzione
Answer A. $0 \leq g \leq 80$.
:::
