Which statement about the equation $3x^2 - 2x + 1 = 0$ is true?

- A) It has two distinct real roots.
- B) It has a double real root.
- C) It has no real roots.
- D) It has two real roots that are opposites.

:::soluzione
Answer C. It has no real roots.
:::
