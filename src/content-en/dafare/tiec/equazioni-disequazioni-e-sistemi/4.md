What are the solutions of $x^2 + 3x - 28 = 0$?

- A) $x = 4$ and $x = -7$
- B) $x = -4$ and $x = 7$
- C) $x = 4$ and $x = 7$
- D) $x = -4$ and $x = -7$

:::soluzione
Answer A. $x = 4$ and $x = -7$.
:::
