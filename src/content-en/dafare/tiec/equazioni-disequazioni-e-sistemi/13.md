The solution of the system
$$
\begin{cases}
2x + 3y = 12 \\
x - y = 1
\end{cases}
$$
is given by which choice?

- A) $(3;2)$
- B) $(2;3)$
- C) $(3;-2)$
- D) $(6;0)$

:::soluzione
Answer A. $(3;2)$.
:::
