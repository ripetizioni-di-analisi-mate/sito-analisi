The equation
$$
\frac{x}{x + 1} + \frac{1}{x - 1} = \frac{2}{x^2 - 1}
$$
has which solutions?

- A) $x = 1$ and $x = -1$
- B) only $x = 1$
- C) only $x = -1$
- D) no real number

:::soluzione
Answer D. No real number.
:::
