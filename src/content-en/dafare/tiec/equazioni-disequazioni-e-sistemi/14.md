A company sells each item for $30$ euros. Producing $q$ items costs $2000 + 10q$ euros. For which quantities does profit, defined as revenue minus cost, exceed $1000$ euros?

- A) $q > 100$
- B) $q > 150$
- C) $q > 50$
- D) $q > 75$

:::soluzione
Answer B. $q > 150$.
:::
