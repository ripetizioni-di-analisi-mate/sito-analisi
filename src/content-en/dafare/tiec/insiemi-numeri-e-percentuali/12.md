An item costs $80$ euros and is discounted twice, first by $20\%$ and then by $10\%$. What is its final price?

- A) $56$ euros
- B) $57.60$ euros
- C) $58.40$ euros
- D) $64$ euros

:::soluzione
Answer B, $57.60$ euros.
:::
