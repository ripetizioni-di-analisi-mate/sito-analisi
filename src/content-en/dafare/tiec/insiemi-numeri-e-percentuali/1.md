Given the sets $A = \{1, 3, 5, 7\}$ and $B = \{3, 4, 5, 6\}$, which of the following is $A \cup B$?

- A) $\{3, 5\}$
- B) $\{1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7\}$
- C) $\{1, 7\}$
- D) $\{1, 4, 6, 7\}$

:::soluzione
Answer B, $\{1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7\}$.
:::
