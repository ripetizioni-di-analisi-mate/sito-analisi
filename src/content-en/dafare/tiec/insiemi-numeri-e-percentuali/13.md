After a $25\%$ discount, a customer pays $63$ euros. What was the price before the discount?

- A) $78.75$ euros
- B) $88$ euros
- C) $84$ euros
- D) $47.25$ euros

:::soluzione
Answer C, $84$ euros.
:::
