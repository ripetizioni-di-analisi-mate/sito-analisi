Which fraction represents $2.\overline{4}$?

- A) $\dfrac{22}{9}$
- B) $\dfrac{8}{3}$
- C) $\dfrac{61}{25}$
- D) $\dfrac{11}{5}$

:::soluzione
Answer A, $\dfrac{22}{9}$.
:::
