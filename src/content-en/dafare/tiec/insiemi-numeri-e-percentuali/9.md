Let $a$ and $b$ be real numbers with $0 < a < b$. Which of the following inequalities is always true?

- A) $\dfrac{1}{a} > \dfrac{1}{b}$
- B) $a^2 > b^2$
- C) $-a < -b$
- D) $\dfrac{a}{b} > 1$

:::soluzione
Answer A, $\dfrac{1}{a} > \dfrac{1}{b}$.
:::
