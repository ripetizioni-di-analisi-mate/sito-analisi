A company's market share changes from $16\%$ to $12\%$. Which statement is true?

- A) Its share has decreased by $4\%$.
- B) Its share has fallen by $25$ percentage points.
- C) Its share has fallen by $4$ percentage points, a decrease of about $33.3\%$.
- D) Its share has fallen by $4$ percentage points, a decrease of $25\%$.

:::soluzione
Answer D. Its share has fallen by $4$ percentage points, a decrease of $25\%$.
:::
