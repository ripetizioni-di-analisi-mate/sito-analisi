A company checks its inventory every $6$ days, its cash on hand every $8$ days, and its machinery every $10$ days. All three checks took place today. How many days will pass before all three next take place on the same day?

- A) $24$ days
- B) $480$ days
- C) $60$ days
- D) $120$ days

:::soluzione
Answer D, $120$ days.
:::
