In the universal set $U = \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8\}$, let $A$ be the set of prime numbers in $U$. What is the complement of $A$ relative to $U$?

- A) $\{1, 4, 6, 8\}$
- B) $\{4, 6, 8\}$
- C) $\{1, 3, 5, 7\}$
- D) $\{2, 4, 6, 8\}$

:::soluzione
Answer A, $\{1, 4, 6, 8\}$.
:::
