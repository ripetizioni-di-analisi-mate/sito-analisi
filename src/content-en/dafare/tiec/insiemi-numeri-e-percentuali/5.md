Which of the following equals $0.2\overline{7}$?

- A) $\dfrac{3}{11}$
- B) $\dfrac{5}{18}$
- C) $\dfrac{27}{100}$
- D) $\dfrac{1}{4}$

:::soluzione
Answer B, $\dfrac{5}{18}$.
:::
