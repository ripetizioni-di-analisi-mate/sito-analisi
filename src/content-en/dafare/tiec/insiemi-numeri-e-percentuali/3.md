At a company with $50$ employees, $32$ speak English, $20$ speak Spanish, and $8$ speak neither language. How many employees speak both languages?

- A) $2$
- B) $18$
- C) $10$
- D) $12$

:::soluzione
Answer C, $10$.
:::
