Which of $\dfrac{5}{8}$, $\dfrac{3}{5}$, $\dfrac{7}{12}$, and $\dfrac{4}{7}$ is smallest?

- A) $\dfrac{5}{8}$
- B) $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- C) $\dfrac{7}{12}$
- D) $\dfrac{4}{7}$

:::soluzione
Answer D, $\dfrac{4}{7}$.
:::
