What is the difference $\dfrac{x}{x - 2} - \dfrac{4}{x^2 - 2x}$ for $x \neq 0$ and $x \neq 2$?

- A) $\dfrac{x - 4}{x^2 - 2x}$
- B) $\dfrac{x - 2}{x}$
- C) $\dfrac{x + 2}{x}$
- D) $\dfrac{x^2 + 4}{x(x - 2)}$

:::soluzione
Answer C. $\dfrac{x + 2}{x}$.
:::
