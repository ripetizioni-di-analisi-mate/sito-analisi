What is the quotient $\dfrac{a^2 - 4}{a} : \dfrac{a + 2}{a^2}$ for $a \neq 0$ and $a \neq -2$?

- A) $\dfrac{(a - 2)(a + 2)^2}{a^3}$
- B) $a(a - 2)$
- C) $\dfrac{a - 2}{a}$
- D) $a(a + 2)$

:::soluzione
Answer B. $a(a - 2)$.
:::
