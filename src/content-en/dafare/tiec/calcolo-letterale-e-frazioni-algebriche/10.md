What is the greatest common divisor of $x^2 - 1$, $x^2 + 2x + 1$, and $3x + 3$?

- A) $3(x - 1)(x + 1)^2$
- B) $x + 1$
- C) $3(x + 1)$
- D) $(x + 1)^2$

:::soluzione
Answer B. $x + 1$.
:::
