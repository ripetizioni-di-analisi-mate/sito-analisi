The polynomial $x^3 - 6x^2 + 11x - 6$ is divisible by $x - 1$. What is the quotient?

- A) $x^2 - 5x + 6$
- B) $x^2 - 7x + 18$
- C) $x^2 - 5x - 6$
- D) $x^3 - 5x^2 + 6x$

:::soluzione
Answer A. $x^2 - 5x + 6$.
:::
