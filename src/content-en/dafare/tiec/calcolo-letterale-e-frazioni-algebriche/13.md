A price of $p$ euros increases by a fraction $r$ of its value and then decreases by the same fraction $r$ of the new value, with $0 < r < 1$. What is the final price in euros?

- A) $p(1 - r^2)$
- B) $p$
- C) $p(1 - r)^2$
- D) $p(1 + r^2)$

:::soluzione
Answer A. $p(1 - r^2)$.
:::
