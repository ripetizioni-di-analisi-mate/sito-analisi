How does the quadratic $2x^2 + x - 6$ factor?

- A) $(2x + 3)(x - 2)$
- B) $\left(x - \dfrac{3}{2}\right)(x + 2)$
- C) $(2x - 3)(x - 2)$
- D) $(2x - 3)(x + 2)$

:::soluzione
Answer D. $(2x - 3)(x + 2)$.
:::
