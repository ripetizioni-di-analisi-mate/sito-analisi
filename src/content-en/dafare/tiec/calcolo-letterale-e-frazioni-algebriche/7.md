Which expression equals the polynomial $x^3 + 64$?

- A) $(x + 4)^3$
- B) $(x + 4)(x^2 + 4x + 16)$
- C) $(x + 4)(x^2 - 4x + 16)$
- D) $(x + 4)(x^2 - 8x + 16)$

:::soluzione
Answer C. $(x + 4)(x^2 - 4x + 16)$.
:::
