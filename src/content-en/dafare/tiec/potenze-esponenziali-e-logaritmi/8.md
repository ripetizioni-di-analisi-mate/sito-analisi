The number of customers using a service triples each year. After $t$ years, the number is $500 \cdot 3^{t}$. The number of customers exceeds $40\,500$ for:

- A) $t > 81$
- B) $t < 4$
- C) $t > 27$
- D) $t > 4$

:::soluzione
Answer D: $t > 4$.
:::
