The product $\left( 5 \cdot 10^{-3} \right) \cdot \left( 6 \cdot 10^{7} \right)$, written in scientific notation, is:

- A) $3 \cdot 10^{4}$
- B) $1.1 \cdot 10^{5}$
- C) $3 \cdot 10^{10}$
- D) $3 \cdot 10^{5}$

:::soluzione
Answer D: $3 \cdot 10^{5}$.
:::
