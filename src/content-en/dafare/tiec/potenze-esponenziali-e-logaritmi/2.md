The number $32^{-\frac{2}{5}}$ is equal to:

- A) $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- B) $-4$
- C) $4$
- D) $-\dfrac{64}{5}$

:::soluzione
Answer A: $\dfrac{1}{4}$.
:::
