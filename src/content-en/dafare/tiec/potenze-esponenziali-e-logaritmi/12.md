Which of the following functions is decreasing on all of $\mathbb{R}$?

- A) $y = 3^{x}$
- B) $y = \left( \dfrac{3}{2} \right)^{x}$
- C) $y = \left( \dfrac{2}{3} \right)^{x}$
- D) $y = \left( \dfrac{1}{2} \right)^{-x}$

:::soluzione
Answer C: $y = \left( \dfrac{2}{3} \right)^{x}$.
:::
