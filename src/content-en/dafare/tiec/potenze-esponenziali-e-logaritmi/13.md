The graph of the function $y = a^{x}$, with $a > 0$ and $a \neq 1$, passes through the point $\left( -2; \dfrac{1}{25} \right)$. The base $a$ equals:

- A) $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- B) $5$
- C) $-5$
- D) $25$

:::soluzione
Answer B: $5$.
:::
