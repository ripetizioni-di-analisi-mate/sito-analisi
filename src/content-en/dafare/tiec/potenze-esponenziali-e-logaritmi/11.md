The expression $\log_{2} \left( x^{2} - 9 \right)$ is defined for:

- A) $x > 3$
- B) $-3 < x < 3$
- C) $x \neq 3 \;\wedge\; x \neq -3$
- D) $x < -3 \;\vee\; x > 3$

:::soluzione
Answer D: $x < -3 \;\vee\; x > 3$.
:::
