The solutions of the equation $\log_{3} x + \log_{3} (x + 6) = 3$ are:

- A) $x = 3$
- B) $x = 3$ or $x = -9$
- C) $x = -9$
- D) $x = \dfrac{21}{2}$

:::soluzione
Answer A: $x = 3$.
:::
