teoria: [avvisi-e-messaggi]
parole: [announcement, out of order]
---

Read the notice and answer the question:

> This lift is out of order. Please use the stairs or the lift at the end of the corridor. We apologise for any inconvenience.

What does the notice say?

- A) The lift at the end of the corridor is broken.
- B) The stairs are closed.
- C) The lift will be repaired soon.
- D) This lift is not working.

:::soluzione
Answer D: This lift is not working.
:::
