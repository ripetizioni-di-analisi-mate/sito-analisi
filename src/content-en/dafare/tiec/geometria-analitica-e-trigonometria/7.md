Which center and radius does the circle $x^2 + y^2 + 8x - 2y + 8 = 0$ have?

- A) center $(4;-1)$ and radius $3$
- B) center $(-4;1)$ and radius $3$
- C) center $(-4;1)$ and radius $5$
- D) center $(-4;1)$ and radius $9$

:::soluzione
Answer B: center $(-4;1)$ and radius $3$.
:::
