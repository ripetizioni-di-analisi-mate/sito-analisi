For the circle $x^2 + y^2 - 6x + 2y - 6 = 0$, which statement describes the point $P(1;2)$?

- A) It lies on the circle.
- B) It is outside the circle.
- C) It is the center.
- D) It is inside the circle.

:::soluzione
Answer D: It is inside the circle.
:::
