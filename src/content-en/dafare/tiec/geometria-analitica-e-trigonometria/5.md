What is the equation of the line perpendicular to $y = 2x + 1$ through $P(2;3)$?

- A) $y = 2x - 1$
- B) $y = -2x + 7$
- C) $y = -\dfrac{1}{2}x + 4$
- D) $y = \dfrac{1}{2}x + 2$

:::soluzione
Answer C: $y = -\dfrac{1}{2}x + 4$.
:::
