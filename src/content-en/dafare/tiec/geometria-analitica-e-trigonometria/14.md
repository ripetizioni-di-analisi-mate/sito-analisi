An escalator in a shopping mall is $12$ m long and rises $6$ m. How long is its projection onto the floor?

- A) $6$ m
- B) $18$ m
- C) $6\sqrt{3}$ m
- D) $6\sqrt{2}$ m

:::soluzione
Answer C: $6\sqrt{3}$ m.
:::
