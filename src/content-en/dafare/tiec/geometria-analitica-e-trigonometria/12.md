Given $\cos x = -\dfrac{5}{13}$ and $180^\circ < x < 270^\circ$, what is $\tan x$?

- A) $-\dfrac{12}{5}$
- B) $\dfrac{5}{12}$
- C) $-\dfrac{12}{13}$
- D) $\dfrac{12}{5}$

:::soluzione
Answer D: $\dfrac{12}{5}$.
:::
