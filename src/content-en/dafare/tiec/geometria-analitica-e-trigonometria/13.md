What is the value of $\sin\dfrac{\pi}{6} + \cos\dfrac{\pi}{3} + \tan\dfrac{\pi}{4}$?

- A) $2$
- B) $1 + \sqrt{3}$
- C) $1$
- D) $\dfrac{3}{2}$

:::soluzione
Answer A: $2$.
:::
