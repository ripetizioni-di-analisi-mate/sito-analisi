One calling plan costs $10$ euros per month plus $0.20$ euros per minute, and a second plan costs $4$ euros per month plus $0.50$ euros per minute. For how many minutes are the monthly costs equal?

- A) $20$
- B) $12$
- C) $30$
- D) $200$

:::soluzione
Answer A: $20$ minutes.
:::
