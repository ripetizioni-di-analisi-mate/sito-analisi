At which points do the parabola $y = x^2 - 3x + 2$ and the line $y = x - 1$ intersect?

- A) $(1;0)$ and $(2;0)$
- B) $(-1;-2)$ and $(-3;-4)$
- C) $(1;0)$ and $(3;2)$
- D) Only $(2;1)$

:::soluzione
Answer C: $(1;0)$ and $(3;2)$.
:::
