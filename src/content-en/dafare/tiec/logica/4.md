“If the train is on time, Paolo arrives at the office before $9$.” Today Paolo arrived at the office at $9$\:$20$. Which conclusion is correct?

- A) The train was on time today.
- B) Paolo missed the train today.
- C) It cannot be determined whether the train was on time today.
- D) The train was not on time today.

:::soluzione
Answer D. The train was not on time today.
:::
