“Presenting the receipt is sufficient to receive a refund.” Given this statement, which of the following is true?

- A) Anyone who receives a refund presented the receipt.
- B) Anyone who does not present the receipt does not receive a refund.
- C) Anyone who does not receive a refund did not present the receipt.
- D) It is impossible to receive a refund without a receipt.

:::soluzione
Answer C. Anyone who does not receive a refund did not present the receipt.
:::
