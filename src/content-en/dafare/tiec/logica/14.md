Three pens and two notebooks cost $8$ euros. Two pens and three notebooks cost $9.50$ euros. How much does one notebook cost?

- A) $1$ euro
- B) $2$ euros
- C) $3.50$ euros
- D) $2.50$ euros

:::soluzione
Answer D, $2.50$ euros.
:::
