What is the negation of “If the flight is canceled, the airline refunds the ticket”?

- A) If the flight is not canceled, the airline does not refund the ticket.
- B) The flight is canceled and the airline does not refund the ticket.
- C) The flight is not canceled and the airline refunds the ticket.
- D) If the flight is canceled, the airline does not refund the ticket.

:::soluzione
Answer B. The flight is canceled and the airline does not refund the ticket.
:::
