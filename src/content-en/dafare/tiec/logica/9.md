Store X has more customers than store Y. Store Z has fewer customers than store X. Which of the following statements is necessarily true?

- A) X has more customers than both Y and Z.
- B) Y has more customers than Z.
- C) Z has more customers than Y.
- D) Y and Z have the same number of customers.

:::soluzione
Answer A. X has more customers than both Y and Z.
:::
