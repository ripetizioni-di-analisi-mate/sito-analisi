“No club member is a minor. All the players on the team are club members.” Which conclusion necessarily follows?

- A) Some players on the team are minors.
- B) No player on the team is a minor.
- C) All club members are players on the team.
- D) Some minors are club members.

:::soluzione
Answer B. No player on the team is a minor.
:::
