Read the following passage.

> In a hillside town, the share of residents over sixty-five rose from $20\%$ to $25\%$ in ten years. Over the same period the total population stayed nearly the same because the arrival of some families from elsewhere offset the decline in births.
>
> To meet the needs of an older population, the town decided to open a day center where people who live alone can spend the day in company and to make downtown sidewalks wheelchair accessible. It also asked the town’s family doctors to hold monthly information sessions on preventive care. The day center will be open Monday through Friday and run by a volunteer organization, with financial support from the town for rent and heating.

Which of the following statements is correct according to the passage?

- A) The proportion of residents over sixty-five increased by $5\%$
- B) The number of residents over sixty-five doubled
- C) The share of residents over sixty-five increased by $5$ percentage points, or one-quarter of its initial value
- D) The town’s population increased by $25\%$

:::soluzione
Answer C: The share of residents over sixty-five increased by $5$ percentage points, or one-quarter of its initial value.
:::
