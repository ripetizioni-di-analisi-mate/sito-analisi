Read the following passage.

> A generation ago, many workers learned a trade when young and practiced it the same way until retirement. Today software, machinery, and even the rules in many industries change within a few years. An administrative employee uses tools that did not exist ten years ago, and a mechanic needs to know how to read data from a car’s electronics.
>
> For this reason, businesses are increasingly organizing courses for employees, and some employment contracts provide for a minimum number of training hours each year. Job seekers also have a reason to keep up to date because employers increasingly ask at interviews which courses applicants have taken recently. Courses cover more than technical tools: many businesses also offer classes in foreign languages and organizing teamwork, skills that serve widely different jobs.

What is the main idea of the passage?

- A) Rapid changes in work make continuing education throughout one’s career increasingly important
- B) Mechanics need to know how to read data from a car’s electronics
- C) Employment contracts require too many hours of training
- D) Businesses prefer to hire young workers

:::soluzione
Answer A: Rapid changes in work make continuing education throughout one’s career increasingly important.
:::
