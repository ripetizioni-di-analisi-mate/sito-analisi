Read the following passage.

> A city divided an abandoned piece of land on the outskirts into sixty small garden plots, awarded every three years through a public application process. Residents who do not own other land suitable for cultivation may apply. Half the plots are assigned to people over sixty-five and half to families with children.
>
> Anyone assigned a plot pays a small annual fee covering irrigation water and agrees not to use chemicals. The plot holders have formed an association that organizes cleaning of the common areas and a spring open day when children from nearby schools visit the gardens. Vegetables, flowers, and herbs may be grown, but building sheds or fences other than those installed by the city is prohibited.

Which of the following statements is supported by the passage?

- A) Tending a garden plot improves older people’s health
- B) Thirty plots are assigned to people over sixty-five
- C) The plots are assigned permanently to successful applicants
- D) The annual fee covers irrigation water and tools

:::soluzione
Answer B: Thirty plots are assigned to people over sixty-five.
:::
