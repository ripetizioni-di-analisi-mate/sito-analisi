Read the following passage.

> For one month, cooks at a middle school cafeteria weighed the food left on students’ plates every day. The result surprised everyone: almost a third of the vegetables ended up in the trash, while nearly all the pasta was eaten.
>
> Working with the teachers, the cafeteria changed some recipes, serving vegetables in forms the students liked better, such as patties and savory pies. It also reduced portions of the less popular dishes. Three months later, another weighing showed that vegetable waste had fallen by half. When surveyed, students asked to suggest new recipes themselves. The school decided to repeat the weighing every October and display the results on a cafeteria poster. Families also received the new recipes so they could make them at home.

Which title best fits the passage?

- A) Food waste around the world
- B) Vegetable patties
- C) A school cafeteria measures leftovers and cuts waste
- D) Students do not eat pasta

:::soluzione
Answer C: A school cafeteria measures leftovers and cuts waste.
:::
