---
titolo: An unsupported inference about a survey of readers
teoria: [inferenza, controllo-dell-inferenza, eliminazione-delle-opzioni]
parole: [reading comprehension, unsupported inference, reading, e-books]
---

Read the following passage.

> In a survey in one province, a group of readers were asked what format they read books in. About a quarter of respondents said they mainly read e-books, while the others preferred print.
>
> People who travel daily by train or bus make up the majority of those who read electronically. They appreciate being able to carry many books on one device. Those who prefer print give the pleasure of turning pages and writing in the margins as their main reason. The survey did not ask how many books each respondent read or how long they had been reading. The authors note that many readers use both formats on different occasions and that the question asked only which format they used more often.

Which of the following conclusions is NOT supported by the passage?

- A) Most respondents prefer print books
- B) People who read e-books read more books than people who read in print
- C) People who travel daily by train or bus are among those who read e-books
- D) Some people who prefer print value the opportunity to write in the margins

:::soluzione
Answer B: People who read e-books read more books than people who read in print.
:::
