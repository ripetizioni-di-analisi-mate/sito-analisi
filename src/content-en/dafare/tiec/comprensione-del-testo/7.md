Read the following passage.

> Many small family businesses face a delicate transition when the founder retires. The founder’s children do not always want to continue the business. When they do, they must earn the trust of customers and suppliers used to dealing with their parent.
>
> The businesses that handle this stage best prepare for the changeover years in advance. The founder gradually brings the successor into the business, introduces them to key customers, and puts increasingly important decisions in their hands. When the change takes place suddenly, for example because of illness, the new owner finds themselves in charge of a business they only partly know. In many cases the founder stays on as an adviser after the handover, and customers with the closest ties to the founder continue to deal with both people for a time. The transition is considered complete when the successor makes the main decisions alone.

In the passage, what does “changeover” (the Italian word “ricambio”) mean?

- A) a replacement part for the company’s machinery
- B) an exchange of favors between businesses
- C) replacement of suppliers
- D) the transfer of leadership to a new person

:::soluzione
Answer D: The transfer of leadership to a new person.
:::
