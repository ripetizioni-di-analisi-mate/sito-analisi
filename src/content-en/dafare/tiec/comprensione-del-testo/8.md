Read the following passage.

> In the historic centers of some cities known for their art, many apartments have been converted into tourist accommodation and rented for a few days at a time. For owners this is often more profitable than renting to a family or student because the nightly rate is high and the apartments are occupied for much of the year.
>
> The trend affects local residents, however, because fewer homes are available to people who want to live in the center and their rents rise. Some local governments have decided to limit it by setting a maximum number of nights per year that an apartment can be rented to tourists. Other cities have chosen different approaches, such as incentives for owners who rent to residents for at least four years or restrictions applying only to certain streets in the historic center.

In the sentence “Some local governments have decided to limit it,” the pronoun “it” refers to:

- A) the nightly rate
- B) renting to a family or student
- C) the conversion of apartments into tourist accommodation
- D) the number of homes available to residents

:::soluzione
Answer C: The conversion of apartments into tourist accommodation.
:::
