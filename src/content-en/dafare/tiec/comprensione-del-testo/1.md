Read the following passage.

> For some years now, many cities have held Saturday morning markets in their squares where local farmers sell directly to customers. Shoppers often pay about the same as at the supermarket but get fruit and vegetables harvested only a few days earlier, and they can ask the grower how the produce was grown. For farmers the main advantage is different: by selling without intermediaries, they keep a larger share of the final price.
>
> These markets have a drawback, however. They take place once or twice a week at times when many people are at work. For this reason some growers have also arranged home delivery for orders. Delivery costs extra and requires a minimum order, so it is used mainly by large families and small neighborhood restaurants.

According to the passage, what is the main advantage of these markets for farmers?

- A) They sell at higher prices than supermarkets
- B) They keep a larger share of the final price
- C) They can tell customers how they grow their produce
- D) They sell their produce every day of the week

:::soluzione
Answer B: They keep a larger share of the final price.
:::
