Read the following passage.

> In recent years many towns have installed water kiosks, small stations connected to the public water supply that dispense filtered, chilled water, either still or sparkling. The water costs a few cents per liter, and users fill bottles brought from home. The town usually contracts a company to operate the kiosk, check the water quality regularly, and replace the filters.
>
> In towns that have installed a kiosk, waste collection operators have recorded fewer plastic bottles in the recycling. The most frequent users are families with children and older people living near the kiosk. People who live far away use it rarely because carrying many full bottles is hard work. Some towns have also installed a dispenser beside the kiosk that sells reusable glass bottles at cost.

What is the main purpose of the passage?

- A) To describe how water kiosks work, who uses them, and what effects they have had
- B) To persuade readers to stop buying bottled water
- C) To criticize towns that have not installed a water kiosk
- D) To compare water quality in different towns

:::soluzione
Answer A: To describe how water kiosks work, who uses them, and what effects they have had.
:::
