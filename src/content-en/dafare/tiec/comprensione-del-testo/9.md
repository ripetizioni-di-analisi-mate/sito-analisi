Read the following passage.

> A service company has introduced flexible hours for its office staff. Each employee can arrive between 7\:30 and 10\:00 a.m. and leave after completing eight hours of work, including the breaks provided in the contract.
>
> ___ most employees welcomed the change, office managers have had some difficulty scheduling meetings because not all colleagues are present for much of the morning. The company therefore set common hours from 10\:00 a.m. to 12\:00 noon, when everyone must be in the office, and asked that meetings be scheduled only during those two hours. After six months, an anonymous survey showed that most employees considered the common hours an acceptable compromise.

Which connective correctly completes the passage?

- A) Since
- B) Although
- C) Given that
- D) Because

:::soluzione
Answer B: Although.
:::
