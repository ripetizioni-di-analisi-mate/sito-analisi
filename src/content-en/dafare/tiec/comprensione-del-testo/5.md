Read the following passage.

> A city’s car-sharing service works as follows. Users reserve an available car by phone, unlock it with the app, and leave it in a public parking space within the service area at the end of the trip. They pay by the minute for driving, including fuel and insurance. There is no subscription, but to register, users must have held a driver’s license for at least one year.
>
> The cars cannot leave the service area, which includes downtown and nearby neighborhoods. The airport, twelve kilometers from the city, is outside it. The city has reserved some blue-marked spaces in its paid parking zones for shared cars, where they can park free. The price per minute is lower at night, and users who make a brief stop during their rental pay a reduced rate for the minutes spent parked.

It can be inferred from the passage that:

- A) someone who has held a driver’s license for six months cannot register for the service
- B) the shared cars are electric
- C) the service costs less than a taxi
- D) a shared car can be driven to the airport and left there

:::soluzione
Answer A: Someone who has held a driver’s license for six months cannot register for the service.
:::
