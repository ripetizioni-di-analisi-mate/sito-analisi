Read the following passage.

> A refurbished phone is a used device that a specialist company has inspected, repaired where necessary, and put back on sale with a warranty. It usually costs about one-third less than the new model. Buyers sometimes accept small cosmetic flaws, such as a scratch on the back, and get a device that works like new.
>
> The market for these phones is growing especially among students and families who need to buy several phones at once. The environment benefits too, since every refurbished phone sold avoids the production of a new device and the raw materials and energy that production requires. Some sellers also take customers’ old phones in trade and deduct their value from the price of the refurbished one. Customers can thus replace their phone for less money without keeping a device they no longer use at home.

Which of the following pieces of information appears in the passage?

- A) Refurbished phones cost one-third as much as new ones
- B) Refurbished phones are sold without a warranty
- C) Refurbished phones do not last as long as new ones
- D) A refurbished phone may have small cosmetic flaws

:::soluzione
Answer D: A refurbished phone may have small cosmetic flaws.
:::
