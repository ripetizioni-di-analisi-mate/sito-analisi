Read the following passage.

> For a month, a grocery store in a small town asked customers how they had traveled to the store. There were $400$ respondents. Of those, $45\%$ came on foot, $35\%$ by car, and the rest by bike. Nearly everyone who came by car said they lived outside town.
>
> The owner decided to install a bike rack in front of the store. The owner had noticed that more customers came by bike than expected and that up to then they had locked their bikes to lampposts, blocking the sidewalk. The rack holds ten bikes and was placed beside the entrance under the awning so they would be sheltered when it rains.

How many respondents came to the store by bike?

- A) $20$
- B) $140$
- C) $180$
- D) $80$

:::soluzione
Answer D: $80$.
:::
