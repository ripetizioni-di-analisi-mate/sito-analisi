---
titolo: "Exceeding the critical angle"
teoria: [snell]
parole: ["Exceeding the critical angle"]
---

Analyse a ray arriving from glass, $n_1=1.50$, towards air, $n_2=1.00$, at $60.0^\circ$ from the normal. First calculate the critical angle, then decide whether a propagating transmitted ray exists and find the reflected angle. Compare this case with incidence exactly at the critical angle.

:::soluzione
The critical angle is $\theta_c=\arcsin(\dfrac{1}{1.50})\simeq41.8^\circ$. At $60^\circ$, Snell would require $\sin\theta_t=1.50\sin60^\circ>1$, so no propagating transmitted ray exists. A grazing ray occurs only at the critical angle, not at all larger incidences; unchanged frequency does not require equal angles.

The reflected ray still makes $60.0^\circ$ with the normal. At the exact critical angle the refracted ray would be grazing; here incidence is larger and the propagating transmitted ray is absent. The geometrical description does not exclude the near-interface evanescent field predicted by wave theory.
:::
