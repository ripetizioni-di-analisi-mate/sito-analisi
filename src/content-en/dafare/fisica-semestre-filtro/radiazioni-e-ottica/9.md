---
titolo: "Inferring half-life from activity"
teoria: [decadimento-esponenziale]
parole: ["Inferring half-life from activity"]
---

An isolated single-radionuclide sample falls from $800$ to $100\,\mathrm{Bq}$ in $18.0\,\mathrm h$, with no production or physical removal. Infer half-life from the number of halvings and predict activity at $24.0\,\mathrm h$. Check the prediction using the eighteen-hour measurement.

:::soluzione
The final-to-initial ratio is $\dfrac{1}{8}$, corresponding to three halvings, so $T_{1/2}=\dfrac{18}{3}=6\,\mathrm h$. Dividing time by eight confuses the remaining fraction with the number of half-lives. Activity follows the same exponential as population because $\mathscr A=\lambda_dN$ with constant $\lambda_d$.

At twenty-four hours, four half-lives have elapsed, so $\mathscr A=\dfrac{800}{16}=50.0\,\mathrm{Bq}$. The same result follows by halving the $100\,\mathrm{Bq}$ measured at eighteen hours once more. Do not subtract a constant hourly loss: equal intervals remove a constant fraction.
:::
