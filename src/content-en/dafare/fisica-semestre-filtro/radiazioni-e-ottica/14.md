Construct the image of a real object $1.00\,\mathrm{cm}$ tall at $p=18.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ from a thin converging lens in air with $f=12.0\,\mathrm{cm}$. In the paraxial model take $q>0$ on the opposite side from the object. Derive position, magnification and signed height, then explain where a screen should be placed to receive the image.

:::soluzione
The conjugate-point equation gives $q=\dfrac{fp}{p-f}=36.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $M=-\dfrac{q}{p}=-2.00$. An object between $f$ and $2f$ produces a real, inverted, enlarged image. Adding reciprocals instead of subtracting gives $7.20\,\mathrm{cm}$; dropping the magnification sign gives an inconsistent orientation.

Image height is $h_i=Mh_o=-2.00\,\mathrm{cm}$: twice the magnitude and inverted. Since $q>0$, actual rays converge on the opposite side and a screen at $36.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ can receive the image in the ideal model. Signs of $q$ and $M$ describe distinct but consistent information.
:::
