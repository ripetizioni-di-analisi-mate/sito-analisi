An optical bench uses two filters in series, each transmitting $25.0\%$ at the beam frequency. Assume independent filters and negligible multiple reflections. Express power after each filter in terms of initial power and derive the final percentage. Discuss reversing their order and which assumptions would need checking if the measurement changed.

:::soluzione
The second filter transmits one quarter of the power already reduced by the first: $\mathcal T=(0.250)^2=0.0625$. Adding transmittances would give $50\%$ without describing two consecutive passages. Subtracting $75\%$ of initial power twice is also wrong: the second loss refers to the power reaching the second filter.

The transmittance product is commutative, and here both transmittances are equal: reversing them leaves $6.25\%$. This conclusion depends on independence and the absence of added contributions between elements. A measured change would require rechecking geometry, stability and model assumptions.
:::
