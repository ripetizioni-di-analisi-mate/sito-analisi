parole: ["Refraction angle"]
---

At an interface between transparent media with $n_1=1.00$ and $n_2=1.50$, a ray arrives from the first medium at $30.0^\circ$ to the normal. Set up Snell’s law and determine both transmitted-ray angles: to the normal and to the surface. Check the bending direction and discuss frequency.

:::soluzione
Snell gives $\sin\theta_t=(\dfrac{1}{1.50})\sin30^\circ=\dfrac{1}{3}$, so $\theta_t\simeq19.5^\circ$. Entering the higher-index medium bends the ray towards the normal. The complementary $70.5^\circ$ is the angle to the surface; reversing the index ratio instead predicts inconsistent bending away.

The angle to the surface is the complement, about $70.5^\circ$. The ray is closer to the normal because it enters the higher-index medium. Frequency stays unchanged: bending accompanies changes in speed and wavelength without requiring a change in individual photon energy.
:::
