A spectral measurement identifies a vacuum wavelength of $600\,\mathrm{nm}$. Determine oscillation frequency and period using $c=3.00\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Show the nanometre conversion and check the result by reconstructing the speed.

:::soluzione
Convert $600\,\mathrm{nm}=6.00\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm m$ and obtain $\nu=\dfrac c{\lambda_0}=5.00\cdot10^{14}\,\mathrm{Hz}$. The value $2.00\cdot10^{-15}$ would represent the period in seconds, not frequency. Multiplying speed by wavelength or confusing nano with micro produces the other incorrect orders of magnitude.

The period is the reciprocal: $T=\dfrac1\nu=2.00\cdot10^{-15}\,\mathrm s$. The product $\lambda_0\nu$ recovers $3.00\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$, checking nanometre conversion. Such a short period agrees with a very high optical frequency.
:::
