parole: ["Absorbed energy per unit mass"]
---

Analyse two depositions of the same absorbed ionizing energy, $2.00\,\mathrm{mJ}$: the first concerns $0.500\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the second twice that mass. Calculate and compare average doses in grays and milligrays, identifying the assumption that permits inverse scaling with mass.

:::soluzione
Dose is $D=\dfrac{E_{\mathrm{ass}}}{m}=\dfrac{2.00\cdot10^{-3}}{0.500}=0.00400\,\mathrm{J/kg}$. Since one gray equals one joule per kilogram, the result is $4.00\,\mathrm{mGy}$. Multiplying by mass or dropping the milli prefix changes the budget incorrectly; source activity is unnecessary when absorbed energy is already known.

With mass $1.00\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the same absorbed energy, dose would be $2.00\,\mathrm{mGy}$. Inverse proportionality holds because deposited energy is fixed. Physically doubling irradiated mass without specifying the resulting absorption would not by itself guarantee that conclusion.
:::
