A container holds $250\,\mathrm{mL}$. Express volume in litres, cubic centimetres and cubic metres. Explain why converting millilitres to cubic metres does not use the same factor as millimetres to metres.

:::soluzione
$250\,\mathrm{mL}=0.250\,\mathrm{L}=250\,\mathrm{cm^3}=2.50\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^3}$. Milli in millilitre applies to the litre, which is already one thousandth of a cubic metre. Conversion from millilitres to cubic metres therefore introduces $10^{-6}$. The millimetre is instead a length unit equal to $10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m}$: these are different quantities. The cubic-centimetre check cubes the centimetre conversion.
:::
