For displacements $\vec{a}=(3;4)\,\mathrm{m}$ and $\vec{b}=(-1;2)\,\mathrm{m}$, find sum and difference. Which vector added to the sum produces the zero vector?

:::soluzione
The sum is $(2;6)\,\mathrm{m}$ and the difference $(4;2)\,\mathrm{m}$. For subtraction reverse both signs of the second vector before adding. The opposite sum is $(-2;-6)\,\mathrm{m}$, cancelling it component by component. Equal magnitude alone is insufficient: direction must also be opposite. Sum magnitude is $\sqrt{40}\,\mathrm{m}$, not the sum of both magnitudes.
:::
