Obtain pressure dimensions and SI base units from force per area. Check whether $\rho v^2$ has the same dimensions. Does this match prove a pressure law?

:::soluzione
$[p]=ML^{-1}T^{-2}$ and the unit is $\mathrm{kg\,m^{-1}s^{-2}}$. Since $[\rho]=ML^{-3}$ and $[v^2]=L^2T^{-2}$, their product has the required dimension. It is a possible pressure-dimension term, but the match does not prove that it equals the system’s pressure. Numerical coefficients, flow conditions and further contributions remain undetermined. The physical model must supply them.
:::
