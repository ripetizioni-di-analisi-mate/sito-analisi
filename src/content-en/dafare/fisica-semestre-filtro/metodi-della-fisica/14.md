---
titolo: "Order in the cross product"
teoria: ["prodotti-vettori"]
parole: ["Order in the cross product"]
---

In right-handed Cartesian axes, let $\vec{a}=(2;0;0)$ and $\vec{b}=(0;3;0)$ be dimensionless vectors. Find $\vec{a}\times\vec{b}$ and $\vec{b}\times\vec{a}$. What if $\vec{b}$ were replaced by $(3;0;0)$?

:::soluzione
The first product is $(0;0;6)$: the right-hand rule turns $+x$ towards $+y$ to give $+z$. Reversing order gives $(0;0;-6)$, with equal magnitude and opposite direction. With the replacement vector, both factors would be parallel and the product zero. Multiplying magnitudes alone is insufficient: the angle’s sine and, for the vector result, the orientation convention are also required.
:::
