Calculate and normalize $(3.0\cdot10^{-6})(4.0\cdot10^3)$ and $3.0\cdot10^{-6}+4.0\cdot10^{-5}$. Explain why the same exponent operation cannot be used in both cases.

:::soluzione
The product is $12\cdot10^{-3}=1.2\cdot10^{-2}$: exponents add and the coefficient is then normalized. For the sum, rewrite the first term as $0.30\cdot10^{-5}$, obtaining $4.3\cdot10^{-5}$. Adding exponents in a sum is not a power law. The sum must exceed each positive term, whereas multiplication by a very small number can give a small product.
:::
