A $25\,\mathrm{mg}$ sample is divided without loss into five equal-mass portions. Express total mass in kilograms and micrograms, then each portion’s mass in micrograms.

:::soluzione
Total mass is $2.5\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{kg}=2.5\cdot10^4\,\mu\mathrm{g}$; each portion has mass $5.0\cdot10^3\,\mu\mathrm{g}$. Milligrams to kilograms involves two factors of one thousand, whereas milligrams to micrograms involves one in the opposite direction. Dividing before or after conversion gives the same result because both operations are linear. Adding the five portions recovers total mass.
:::
