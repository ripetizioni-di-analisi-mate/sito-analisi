Find the orders of magnitude of $7.0\cdot10^{-8}\,\mathrm{m}$ and $2.0\cdot10^{-8}\,\mathrm{m}$, choosing the nearest power on a logarithmic scale. Explain why equal written exponents do not require equal answers.

:::soluzione
The normalized-coefficient boundary is $\sqrt{10}\simeq3.16$. The first coefficient exceeds it, giving order $10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m}$. The second is smaller, giving $10^{-8}\,\mathrm{m}$. The coefficient matters as well as the scientific-notation exponent. The boundary corresponds to equal ratios relative to neighbouring powers, hence is geometric rather than arithmetic.
:::
