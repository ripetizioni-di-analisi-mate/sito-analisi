A $5.0\,\mathrm{N}$ force is at $60^\circ$ from positive $x$ in the first quadrant. Find components and reconstruct magnitude. What happens to magnitude if axes are rotated?

:::soluzione
$F_x=2.5\,\mathrm{N}$ and $F_y=5\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\,\mathrm{N}$. Both are positive and the vertical one is larger because direction is closer to the vertical axis. The squared sum is $25\,\mathrm{N^2}$, recovering magnitude. Rotating orthonormal axes changes components but not the physical vector or its magnitude. A component is therefore not independent of the axis choice.
:::
