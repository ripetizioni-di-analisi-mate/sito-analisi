Convert $\dfrac\pi4\,\mathrm{rad}$ to degrees and find sine and cosine. What arc does it subtend on a circle of radius $2.0\,\mathrm{cm}$? Check using the fraction of a turn.

:::soluzione
The angle is $45^\circ$ and both sine and cosine equal $\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$. Arc length is $s=r\theta=\dfrac\pi2\,\mathrm{cm}\simeq1.6\,\mathrm{cm}$. Forty-five degrees is one eighth of a turn, so dividing circumference $4\pi\,\mathrm{cm}$ by eight confirms the result. The arc/radius formula uses radians; the number forty-five cannot directly replace the angular value in that formula.
:::
