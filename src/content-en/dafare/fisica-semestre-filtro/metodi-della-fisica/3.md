parole: ["Density and the mass of a volume"]
---

A homogeneous liquid has density $1.06\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. Convert it to SI units and find the mass of $20\,\mathrm{cm^3}$, retaining intermediate digits. Check by also calculating in grams.

:::soluzione
The conversion-factor ratio is $\dfrac{10^{-3}}{10^{-6}}=10^3$, so $\rho=1060\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. Volume is $2.0\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{m^3}$ and unrounded mass is $m=0.0212\,\mathrm{kg}$. In the original units, $(1.06\,\mathrm{g/cm^3})(20\,\mathrm{cm^3})=21.2\,\mathrm{g}$ confirms the value. Final rounding would require the precision specified for volume: writing twenty alone does not establish that precision.
:::
