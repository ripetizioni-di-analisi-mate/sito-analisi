parole: ["Dividing without changing state"]
---

A homogeneous system is divided into two equal parts without a state change. Describe what happens to each part’s mass, volume, internal energy, temperature and density. Distinguish this operation from cooling.

:::soluzione
Mass, volume and internal energy halve under the model’s additive conditions. Temperature and density stay equal: system amount is reduced without changing its state. Mass-to-volume ratio is unchanged. In cooling, temperature changes despite being intensive: intensivity concerns varying the amount of matter at the same state, not an inability to vary during any process.
:::
