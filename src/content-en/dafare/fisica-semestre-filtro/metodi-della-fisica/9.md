A homogeneous sample has mass $0.24\,\mathrm{kg}$ and density $800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. Rearrange symbolically for volume and calculate it in cubic metres and millilitres. Check units before the value.

:::soluzione
From $\rho=\dfrac mV$ follows $V=\dfrac m\rho$. Units are $\dfrac{\mathrm{kg}}{\mathrm{kg/m^3}}=\mathrm{m^3}$, so the ratio has the required meaning. The result is $3.0\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^3}=300\,\mathrm{mL}$. Multiplying mass by density would not give volume. The reverse check multiplies the calculated volume by density, recovering $0.24\,\mathrm{kg}$.
:::
