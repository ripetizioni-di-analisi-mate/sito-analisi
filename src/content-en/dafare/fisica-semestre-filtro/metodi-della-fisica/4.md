Constant volume flow rate is $6.0\,\mathrm{L/min}$. Convert it to cubic metres per second and find volume transferred in $30\,\mathrm{s}$. Explain the check using a fraction of a minute.

:::soluzione
$Q=\dfrac{6.0\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^3}}{60\,\mathrm{s}}=1.0\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ and $V=Q\Delta t=3.0\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^3}=3.0\,\mathrm{L}$. Thirty seconds is half a minute, so half of six litres must pass. Converting the time in the flow-rate denominator requires division by sixty, whereas obtaining volume then requires multiplication by duration. Reversing these roles creates an error exposed by the check.
:::
