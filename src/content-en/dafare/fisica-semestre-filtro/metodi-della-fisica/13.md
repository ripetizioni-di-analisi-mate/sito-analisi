For dimensionless vectors $\vec{a}=(2;3)$ and $\vec{b}=(4;-1)$, calculate the dot product. Is their angle acute, right or obtuse? Set up its calculation without premature rounding.

:::soluzione
$\vec{a}\cdot\vec{b}=2\cdot4+3(-1)=5$. A positive value implies an acute angle for nonzero vectors. Magnitudes are $\sqrt{13}$ and $\sqrt{17}$, so $\cos\theta=\dfrac5{\sqrt{221}}$ and $\theta\simeq70.3^\circ$. The second vector’s negative component gives a negative contribution without making the total negative. Dot product divided by magnitudes must remain between minus one and one.
:::
