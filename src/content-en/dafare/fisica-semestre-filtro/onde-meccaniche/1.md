An oscillator completes $50$ cycles in $2.0\,\mathrm s$. Calculate frequency, period and cycle count in $0.30\,\mathrm s$, assuming constant frequency. Explain why period is not the total measurement duration.

:::soluzione
$f=\dfrac{50}{2.0\,\mathrm s}=25\,\mathrm{Hz}$ and $T=\dfrac{1}{f}=0.040\,\mathrm s$. In $0.30\,\mathrm s$, phase advances by $f\Delta t=7.5$ cycles: seven complete cycles plus half a cycle. Period measures one repetition; multiplying it by fifty reconstructs the observed two seconds. An arbitrary interval can contain a noninteger cycle count.
:::
