An oscillation has frequency $5.0\,\mathrm{Hz}$. Determine angular frequency and phase increase in $0.050\,\mathrm s$. State the elapsed fraction of a period.

:::soluzione
$\omega=2\pi f=10\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ and $\Delta\varphi=\omega\Delta t=\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. Since $T=0.20\,\mathrm s$, one quarter-period has elapsed. Frequency counts cycles, whereas angular frequency counts radians: one cycle corresponds to $2\pi$ radians. Confusing them would lose this factor.
:::
