Constant, uniformly distributed power of $2.0\,\mathrm W$ crosses $0.50\,\mathrm{m^2}$ perpendicular to propagation. Calculate intensity and energy transported through the whole section in $8.0\,\mathrm s$. Distinguish the units of the three quantities.

:::soluzione
$I=\dfrac{P}{S}=4.0\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$ and $E=P\Delta t=16\,\mathrm J$. Power means joules per second, intensity watts per square metre, and energy joules. Multiplying $I$ by area recovers $P$; including time recovers $E$. Without area, intensity could not be obtained from power alone.
:::
