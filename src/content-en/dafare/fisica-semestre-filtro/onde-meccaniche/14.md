A source approaches a stationary receiver along their joining line in a stationary medium. Source speed is below sound speed. Explain changes in front spacing ahead of the source, received frequency and each front’s speed in the medium.

:::soluzione
During one period the source advances by $u_sT$, leaving fronts ahead spaced by $(v-u_s)T$, less than $vT$. The receiver encounters more fronts per second and records higher frequency than emitted. Each front still travels at $v$ relative to the medium: the change comes from emission positions, not adding source speed to sound speed.
:::
