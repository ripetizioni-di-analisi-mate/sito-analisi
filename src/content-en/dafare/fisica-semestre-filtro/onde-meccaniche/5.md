For $y=(0.004\,\mathrm m)\cos[(2\pi\,\mathrm{m^{-1}})x-(20\pi\,\mathrm{s^{-1}})t]$, find amplitude, frequency, wavelength and propagation direction. Explain direction by following constant phase.

:::soluzione
$A=4\,\mathrm{mm}$, $f=\dfrac{\omega}{2\pi}=10\,\mathrm{Hz}$ and $\lambda=\dfrac{2\pi}{k}=1\,\mathrm m$. The condition $kx-\omega t=C$ gives increasing coordinate at velocity $\dfrac{\omega}{k}=10\,\mathrm{m/s}$, hence towards $+x$. The sign of local displacement does not determine wave direction; phase translation does.
:::
