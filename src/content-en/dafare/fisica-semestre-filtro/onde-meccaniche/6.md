With $k,\omega>0$, compare $A\cos(kx-\omega t)$ and $A\cos(kx+\omega t)$. State direction, speed and initial profile. Can a single photograph at $t=0$ distinguish them?

:::soluzione
The first travels towards $+x$, the second towards $-x$, both at speed $\dfrac{\omega}{k}$. At $t=0$ both have profile $A\cos(kx)$: one photograph cannot distinguish them. Constant phase gives respectively $x=+(\dfrac{\omega}{k})t+C$ and $x=-(\dfrac{\omega}{k})t+C$. Time information or local velocity direction at a non-turning point is required.
:::
