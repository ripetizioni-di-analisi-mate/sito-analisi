A pulse returns after $40\,\mu\mathrm s$ in a homogeneous medium with $v=1500\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Time is already corrected for instrument delays. Find distance to the stationary reflector along the straight path and one-way time.

:::soluzione
$d=\dfrac{v\Delta t}{2}=0.030\,\mathrm m=3.0\,\mathrm{cm}$. One-way time is $20\,\mu\mathrm s$. The product $v\Delta t=6.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ is total path, not depth. The check $\dfrac{2d}{v}$ reconstructs measured time. An incorrect assumed speed would change estimated distance even with an exact time measurement.
:::
