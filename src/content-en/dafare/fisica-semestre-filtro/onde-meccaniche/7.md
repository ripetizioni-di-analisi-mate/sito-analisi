Two coherent contributions have amplitudes $2$ and $4\,\mathrm{mm}$. Find total amplitude in phase and in antiphase. What range of amplitudes is obtained by varying phase continuously?

:::soluzione
In phase, $A_R=2+4=6\,\mathrm{mm}$; in antiphase, $A_R=|4-2|=2\,\mathrm{mm}$. Since $\cos\Delta\varphi$ ranges from $-1$ to $+1$, amplitude takes all values between $2$ and $6\,\mathrm{mm}$. The minimum is not zero because the contribution amplitudes differ.
:::
