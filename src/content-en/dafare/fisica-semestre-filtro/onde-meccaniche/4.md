teoria: ["parametri-onda"]
parole: ["One frequency, two media"]
---

A $680\,\mathrm{Hz}$ wave travels at $340\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Calculate wavelength. It then crosses a stationary interface into a medium with speed $1360\,\mathrm{m/s}$: determine transmitted frequency and wavelength.

:::soluzione
$\lambda_1=\dfrac{v_1}{f}=0.50\,\mathrm m$. Frequency remains $680\,\mathrm{Hz}$ because the stationary interface is driven at the source’s rhythm. In the second medium, $\lambda_2=\dfrac{v_2}{f}=2.0\,\mathrm m$. The wavelength ratio is four, equal to the speed ratio. Changing medium does not automatically multiply frequency.
:::
