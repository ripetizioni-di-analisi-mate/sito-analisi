A $0.25\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass oscillates without friction on a spring with $K=100\,\mathrm{N/m}$. Calculate period. How does it change if mass becomes nine times greater while the spring remains unchanged?

:::soluzione
$T=2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{m}{K}}=0.10\pi\,\mathrm s\simeq0.314\,\mathrm s$. Mass appears under a square root: $\dfrac{T_2}{T_1}=\sqrt9=3$, so $T_2\simeq0.942\,\mathrm s$. At fixed stiffness, greater inertia slows oscillation. Multiplying period by nine would incorrectly assume direct proportionality to mass.
:::
