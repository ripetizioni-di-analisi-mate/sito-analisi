A steady isotropic point source without absorption produces $9\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$ at $1\,\mathrm m$. Find intensity at $3\,\mathrm m$ and emitted power. Explain why decreasing intensity does not mean power loss.

:::soluzione
$I_2=9(\dfrac{1}{3})^2=1\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$. Power is $P=4\pi r_1^2I_1=36\pi\,\mathrm W$, equal to $4\pi r_2^2I_2$. The second sphere has nine times the area, offsetting intensity nine times smaller. The law requires isotropic propagation without absorption; it does not automatically describe a collimated beam.
:::
