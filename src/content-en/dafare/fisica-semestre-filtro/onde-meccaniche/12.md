---

Calculate level for $I=10^{-8}\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$ and $I_0=10^{-12}\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$. Then find level if intensity doubles. What would a level of $0\,\mathrm{dB}$ represent?

:::soluzione
$\dfrac{I}{I_0}=10^4$ gives $L_I=40\,\mathrm{dB}$. Doubling adds $10\log_{10}2\simeq3.01$ to the numerical level, giving $43.01\,\mathrm{dB}$. It does not double level. At $0\,\mathrm{dB}$ the ratio is one and intensity equals $I_0$: intensity is not zero.
:::
