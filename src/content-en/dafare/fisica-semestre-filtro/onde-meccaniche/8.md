Two coherent waves of equal frequency and amplitude reach a sensor in antiphase. Describe recorded displacement. Explain whether this proves that wave energy was destroyed and displacement is also zero elsewhere.

:::soluzione
The local contributions are equal and opposite at every instant, so the sensor records zero total displacement. The conclusion concerns the specified point and phase conditions. Elsewhere, different paths can give different phases and a nonzero sum. Local cancellation does not imply energy destruction: the complete field and fluxes must be considered; in a linear nondispersive medium, each pulse can reappear with its own shape after overlap.
:::
