A sinusoid on the same ideal string at unchanged tension halves its amplitude. Find the power ratio first at unchanged frequency, then if frequency doubles. State which formula factors remain constant.

:::soluzione
Using $P\propto\mu_\ell v f^2A^2$, the same string at unchanged tension keeps $\mu_\ell$ and $v$ fixed. First, $\dfrac{P_2}{P_1}=(\dfrac{1}{2})^2=\dfrac{1}{4}$. In the second case, the frequency factor $2^2$ cancels the amplitude factor and the ratio is one. The amplitude-squared rule alone requires all other factors unchanged.
:::
