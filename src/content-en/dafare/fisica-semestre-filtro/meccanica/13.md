---
titolo: "Separation of two initially stationary masses"
teoria: ["quantita-di-moto", "centro-di-massa", "energia-meccanica"]
parole: ["momentum", "centre of mass", "mechanical energy"]
---

Two carts of masses $1\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ are initially at rest on a smooth horizontal track. An internal spring releases $24\,\mathrm{J}$, entirely converted into kinetic energy, separating them. The $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ cart moves rightwards. Find both velocities and centre-of-mass velocity.

:::soluzione
$$
m_1v_1+m_2v_2=0\Rightarrow v_1=-3v_2.
$$
$$
\dfrac12(1)(9v_2^2)+\dfrac12(3)v_2^2=24\quad\text{(SI)}
\Rightarrow v_2=+2\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
$$
v_1=-6\,\mathrm{m/s},\qquad v_{\mathrm{CM}}=0.
$$

The spring exerts internal forces and total horizontal momentum remains zero. This requires opposite momenta, not equal speed magnitudes: the lighter cart is three times faster. Kinetic energy increases from zero to $24\,\mathrm{J}$ by consuming elastic energy; conserved momentum does not mean constant kinetic energy. Final energies are respectively $18$ and $6\,\mathrm{J}$, whose sum checks the given value. The centre of mass remains at rest because total momentum is zero throughout.
:::
