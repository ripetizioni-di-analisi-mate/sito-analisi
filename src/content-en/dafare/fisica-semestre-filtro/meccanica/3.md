---
titolo: "Pursuit starting from rest"
teoria: ["moti-rettilinei"]
parole: ["straight-line motion"]
---

At $t=0$, a walker is at $x=30\,\mathrm{m}$ and continues at a constant $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. A runner starts from rest at $x=0$ at the same instant and maintains acceleration $1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ until catching the walker. Find meeting time, meeting position and relative velocity at that instant.

:::soluzione
$$
x_C=30+2t,\qquad x_R=\dfrac12t^2\quad\text{(SI)}.
$$
$$
t^2-4t-60=0\Rightarrow t=10\,\mathrm{s}\ \text{or}\ t=-6\,\mathrm{s}.
$$
$$
x=50\,\mathrm{m},\qquad v_R=10\,\mathrm{m/s},\qquad v_R-v_C=8\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$

Equate positions, not velocities: a meeting requires the same point at the same instant. The negative root belongs to the mathematical extension before starting and must be rejected. At $10\,\mathrm{s}$ both position functions give $50\,\mathrm{m}$, checking the result. Velocities differ, however: the runner passes the walker at relative velocity $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The model assumes constant acceleration only over the specified interval, not indefinitely for a real runner.
:::
