titolo: "Checking rest on a ramp"

A $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ block is initially at rest on a fixed ramp with $\tan\theta=0.50$. Static friction coefficient is $0.60$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the normal force and friction required for rest, check whether rest is possible, and find the limiting angle above which it is impossible.

:::soluzione
$$
\cos\theta=\dfrac{2}{\sqrt5},\qquad \sin\theta=\dfrac1{\sqrt5}.
$$
$$
N=\dfrac{40}{\sqrt5}\,\mathrm{N}\approx17.9\,\mathrm{N},\qquad
f_s=\dfrac{20}{\sqrt5}\,\mathrm{N}\approx8.94\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
$$
f_s<\mu_sN\approx10.7\,\mathrm{N},\qquad
\theta_{\lim}=\arctan0.60\approx31.0^\circ.
$$

Friction magnitude equals the downslope weight component; friction itself points uphill to oppose the tendency to slide. Calculate it from equilibrium, then compare it with the available maximum. Do not impose $f_s=\mu_sN$ beforehand because the block is not at the threshold. Dividing $mg\sin\theta\le\mu_smg\cos\theta$ by $mg\cos\theta>0$ gives $\tan\theta\le\mu_s$. Mass cancels from the angular threshold but not from the force values.
:::
