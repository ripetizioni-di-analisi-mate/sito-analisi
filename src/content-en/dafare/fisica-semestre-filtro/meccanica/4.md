---
titolo: "Inferring height from a horizontal launch"
teoria: ["moti-nel-piano"]
parole: ["motion in a plane"]
---

A ball leaves a platform edge horizontally at $8.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and reaches the ground $24\,\mathrm{m}$ horizontally from the edge. Neglect air resistance and use $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find platform height and impact velocity components, with the vertical axis upwards.

:::soluzione
$$
t=\dfrac{24\,\mathrm{m}}{8\,\mathrm{m/s}}=3\,\mathrm{s},\qquad
h=\dfrac12gt^2=45\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
$$
v_x=8\,\mathrm{m/s},\qquad v_y=-gt=-30\,\mathrm{m/s},
\qquad |\vec v|=\sqrt{964}\,\mathrm{m/s}\approx31.0\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$

The constant horizontal component determines time first. Use the same time in vertical free fall, which starts with $v_{0y}=0$; do not use $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ as initial vertical velocity when finding height. Component $v_y$ is negative because the ball descends, whereas height is a positive distance. The energy check $v^2=v_0^2+2gh=64+900$ confirms final speed. The range formula for landing at launch height does not apply here.
:::
