---
titolo: "A force that changes sign"
teoria: ["lavoro-energia"]
parole: ["work and energy"]
---

A $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ cart travels from $x=0$ to $x=5\,\mathrm{m}$ on a smooth horizontal guide with initial velocity $+1\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Its only horizontal force is $+4\,\mathrm{N}$ for $0<x<2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $-2\,\mathrm{N}$ for $2<x<5\,\mathrm{m}$. Find where speed is greatest, its maximum value and final speed; check that the final point is reachable.

:::soluzione
$$
K_i=1\,\mathrm{J},\qquad W_1=8\,\mathrm{J},\qquad W_2=-6\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
$$
K(2\,\mathrm{m})=9\,\mathrm{J}\Rightarrow v_{\max}=3\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
$$
K_f=1+8-6=3\,\mathrm{J}\Rightarrow v_f=\sqrt3\,\mathrm{m/s}\approx1.73\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$

While force is positive, accumulated work and kinetic energy increase; after $x=2\,\mathrm{m}$ they decrease. The maximum therefore occurs at the force sign change, not necessarily at the end of the path. In the second section $K$ decreases linearly from $9$ to $3\,\mathrm{J}$ and remains positive: the cart does not stop before $x=5\,\mathrm{m}$. This check is needed because a formal balance to a position beyond the stopping point could assume a path the body never travels.
:::
