---
titolo: "Two blocks and an internal tension"
teoria: ["newton", "forze"]
parole: ["Newton laws", "forces"]
---

Two blocks of masses $m_A=2\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $m_B=3\,\mathrm{kg}$ are connected by an ideal taut string on a smooth horizontal plane. A horizontal $30\,\mathrm{N}$ force pulls $A$ away from $B$. Find common acceleration and tension, distinguishing the system equation from each block's equation.

:::soluzione
$$
30\,\mathrm{N}=(m_A+m_B)a\Rightarrow a=6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.
$$
$$
T=m_Ba=18\,\mathrm{N},\qquad
30\,\mathrm{N}-T=m_Aa=12\,\mathrm{N}.
$$

The inextensible string maintains the blocks' separation, so their accelerations along the plane are equal. In system $A+B$, the two tensions are internal, equal and opposite, and cancel: the external force accelerates the total mass. To find $T$, isolate $B$, on which tension is the only horizontal force. Setting $T=30\,\mathrm{N}$ would ignore the net force needed to accelerate $A$ as well. The equation for $A$ checks the result independently of the equation for $B$.
:::
