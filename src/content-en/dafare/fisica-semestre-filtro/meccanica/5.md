---
titolo: "Minimum period of constrained circular motion"
teoria: ["moti-nel-piano", "forze"]
parole: ["motion in a plane", "forces"]
---

A $0.25\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass rotates on a smooth horizontal table, attached to a string of length $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$. The string can withstand at most $5.0\,\mathrm{N}$. Find the maximum speed and minimum period compatible with an intact taut string. Check whether a period of $\dfrac\pi2\,\mathrm{s}$ is admissible.

:::soluzione
$$
\dfrac{mv^2}{r}\le F_{\max}\Rightarrow
v_{\max}=\sqrt{\dfrac{F_{\max}r}{m}}=\sqrt{10}\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
$$
T_{\min}=\dfrac{2\pi r}{v_{\max}}=\dfrac{\pi}{\sqrt{10}}\,\mathrm{s}\approx0.993\,\mathrm{s}.
$$
$$
T=\dfrac\pi2\,\mathrm{s}\Rightarrow v=2\,\mathrm{m/s},\qquad F_T=2\,\mathrm{N}<5\,\mathrm{N}.
$$

Tension is the only horizontal force and supplies radial acceleration. At fixed radius, a shorter period requires greater speed: the maximum-tension constraint therefore becomes a minimum-period constraint. Reversing the inequality would admit precisely the fastest motions, which load the string most strongly in this model. Weight and normal force balance vertically and are not added to radial tension. The specified period exceeds the minimum and requires only $2\,\mathrm{N}$, so it is admissible.
:::
