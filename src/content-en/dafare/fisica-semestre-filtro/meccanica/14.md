---
titolo: "Beam supported by an inclined cable"
teoria: ["leve", "newton"]
parole: ["levers", "Newton laws"]
---

A rigid horizontal beam of length $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ and negligible weight is hinged at its left end. An $80\,\mathrm{N}$ load acts downwards $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from the hinge. A cable at the right end pulls upwards and leftwards at $30^\circ$ to the horizontal. Find tension and hinge reaction components in static equilibrium.

:::soluzione
$$
(T\sin30^\circ)(2\,\mathrm{m})=(80\,\mathrm{N})(1.5\,\mathrm{m})
\Rightarrow T=120\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
$$
R_x=T\cos30^\circ=60\sqrt3\,\mathrm{N}\approx104\,\mathrm{N},\qquad
R_y=80\,\mathrm{N}-T\sin30^\circ=20\,\mathrm{N}.
$$

Choose rightwards and upwards positive. About the hinge, the cable's horizontal component has zero torque because its line of action passes through the pivot; only the vertical component produces torque. Equivalently, use perpendicular moment arm $2\sin30^\circ=1\,\mathrm{m}$ with the full tension, avoiding multiplying by sine twice. Then obtain reactions from the two force equations: $R_x$ points rightwards and $R_y$ upwards. Both opposing torques have magnitude $120\,\mathrm{N\,m}$.
:::
