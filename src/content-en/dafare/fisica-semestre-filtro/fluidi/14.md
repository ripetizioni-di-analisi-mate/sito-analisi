A capillary of radius $0.50\,\mathrm{mm}$ is in equilibrium with a large reservoir: $\gamma=0.050\,\mathrm{N/m}$, $\rho=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$, $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Gas pressure is common and meniscus volume negligible. Determine the level for zero and $120^\circ$ angles measured through the liquid; interpret the sign relative to the reservoir.

:::soluzione

$$
h_0=\dfrac{2\cdot0.050}{1000\cdot10\cdot0.00050}
=0.020\,\mathrm m=2.0\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
$$
h_{120}=h_0\cos120^\circ=-0.010\,\mathrm m=-1.0\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$

Level is positive upwards. Zero angle maximises the upward vertical tension component; an obtuse angle reverses its sign and produces depression. A negative value indicates a position below the reference, not a unit error. The formula describes final equilibrium, not continuous flow generated by the capillary.

:::
