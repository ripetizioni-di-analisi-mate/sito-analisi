---
titolo: "Force and stroke of a press"
teoria: [pascal-archimede]
parole: ["Force and stroke of a press"]
---

An ideal press has areas $5\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and $100\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. An additional force of $10\,\mathrm N$ acts on the small piston; the large one rises by $1.0\,\mathrm{cm}$. Neglect friction, piston weight and hydrostatic differences, with common external pressure. Determine large-piston force, small-piston stroke and work at both pistons.

:::soluzione

$$
F_2=10\dfrac{100}{5}=200\,\mathrm N,\quad
s_1=\dfrac{100}{5}\cdot1.0\,\mathrm{cm}=20\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
$$
W_1=10\cdot0.20=2.0\,\mathrm J,\qquad
W_2=200\cdot0.010=2.0\,\mathrm J.
$$

Pascal gives equal added pressures, while incompressibility conserves transferred volume. These are distinct constraints and both are needed. The area ratio increases force and reduces stroke by the same factor, leaving equal works in the ideal model. Convert lengths to metres for work calculations.

:::
