---
titolo: "Successive pressure drops"
teoria: [resistenze-idrauliche]
parole: ["Successive pressure drops"]
---

Two horizontal linear elements with resistances $2\cdot10^9$ and $3\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{Pa\,s/m^3}$ are in series. Total pressure difference is $1000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Find equivalent resistance, flow and pressure-drop division, verifying the node-to-node balance.

:::soluzione

$$
R_{\mathrm{eq}}=5\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{Pa\,s/m^3},\qquad
Q=\dfrac{1000}{5\cdot10^9}=2\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}.
$$
$$
\Delta p_1=400\,\mathrm{Pa},\qquad\Delta p_2=600\,\mathrm{Pa},\qquad
\Delta p_1+\Delta p_2=1000\,\mathrm{Pa}.
$$

In series the flow is shared because there are no branches or accumulation. Drops are proportional to resistances; the more resistive element requires the larger part of the total drop. Their sum reconstructs the given value as a check. Flow is $0.20\,\mathrm{mL/s}$ after volume conversion.

:::
