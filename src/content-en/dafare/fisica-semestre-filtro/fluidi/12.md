---
titolo: "Parallel branches and closing a path"
teoria: [resistenze-idrauliche]
parole: ["Parallel branches and closing a path"]
---

Two identical branches of resistance $4\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{Pa\,s/m^3}$ are in parallel under constant pressure difference $800\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Calculate equivalent resistance and flows. Then close one branch and predict the new total flow while maintaining the imposed pressure.

:::soluzione

$$
R_{\mathrm{eq}}=2\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{Pa\,s/m^3},\quad
Q_1=Q_2=\dfrac{800}{4\cdot10^9}=2\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}.
$$
$$
Q=4\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m^3/s},\qquad
Q'=2\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}.
$$

Each branch shares the same nodes and therefore the same driving pressure. Flows add and equivalent resistance is half the single-branch value. After closure, the remaining branch retains its flow because its pressure drop does not change. Total flow halves; there is no imposed total that must be redistributed here.

:::
