---

A homogeneous cube of density $600\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and side $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ floats with two horizontal faces in water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. Neglect air and surface tension. Find submerged fraction, emerged height and explain which part of the reasoning depends on the cubic shape.

:::soluzione

$$
\dfrac{V_{\mathrm{sub}}}{V}=\dfrac{600}{1000}=0.60,
\qquad h_{\mathrm{em}}=(1-0.60)\cdot10\,\mathrm{cm}=4.0\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$

Equal weight and buoyancy determine the volume ratio without requiring a cube. Converting volume into height instead uses constant horizontal cross section: the emerged $40\%$ of volume corresponds to $40\%$ of the side. For a sphere, cap volume would have to be related geometrically to height; simply multiplying diameter by the same fraction would not suffice.

:::
