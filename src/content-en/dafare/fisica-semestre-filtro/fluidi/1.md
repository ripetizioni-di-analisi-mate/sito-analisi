Design two supports to distribute a uniform normal force of $500\,\mathrm N$: the first has area $25\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and the second twice that area. Find pressures in pascals and explain why area conversion does not use the simple centimetre-to-metre factor.

:::soluzione

$$
A_1=25\cdot10^{-4}=0.0025\,\mathrm{m^2},\quad
p_1=\dfrac{500}{0.0025}=2.0\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{Pa},\quad
p_2=\dfrac{p_1}{2}=1.0\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}.
$$

Pressure is normal force divided by area. Each linear dimension contributes a factor $10^{-2}$, so area contributes $10^{-4}$. Total force does not change: the second support distributes it over more area. The check is $p_1A_1=p_2A_2=500\,\mathrm N$; pressure is not conserved when equal force is imposed on different areas.

:::
