---
titolo: "Energy in a horizontal narrowing"
teoria: [bernoulli]
parole: ["Energy in a horizontal narrowing"]
---

In a steady ideal model, an incompressible fluid of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ goes from $1$ to $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ along the same horizontal streamline, without pumps. Determine the static-pressure change and kinetic-energy gain of $1.0\,\mathrm L$ of fluid. Explain the connection between the results.

:::soluzione

$$
p_1-p_2=\dfrac12\cdot1000(3^2-1^2)=4000\,\mathrm{Pa}.
$$
$$
m=1000\cdot10^{-3}=1.0\,\mathrm{kg},\qquad
\Delta K=\dfrac12\cdot1.0(9-1)=4.0\,\mathrm J.
$$
$$
(p_1-p_2)\Delta V=4000\cdot10^{-3}=4.0\,\mathrm J.
$$

Static pressure decreases because net pressure work accelerates the fluid. Pressure difference times volume is exactly the kinetic-energy gain, since height is unchanged and there are no losses. Distinguish the positive decrease $p_1-p_2$ from the change $p_2-p_1$, which is negative.

:::
