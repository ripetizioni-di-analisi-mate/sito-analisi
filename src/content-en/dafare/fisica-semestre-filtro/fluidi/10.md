---
titolo: "Two constraints for the same pipe"
teoria: [poiseuille]
parole: ["Two constraints for the same pipe"]
---

A pipe is replaced by one of half the radius and unchanged length and viscosity. Poiseuille's assumptions hold in both cases. Compare two experiments: constant pressure difference and constant flow rate. Calculate resistance ratios and changes in the quantities not imposed.

:::soluzione

$$
\dfrac{R_2}{R_1}=\left(\dfrac{r_1}{r_2}\right)^4=16.
$$
$$
\Delta p_2=\Delta p_1:\quad\dfrac{Q_2}{Q_1}=\dfrac1{16};
\qquad Q_2=Q_1:\quad\dfrac{\Delta p_2}{\Delta p_1}=16.
$$

Resistance increases identically in both experiments because it depends on geometry and fluid. At constant pressure flow decreases; at constant flow the pump pressure increases. Continuity must not be used to impose equal flows across different experiments. The comparison remains ideal: regime changes or local losses would require additional contributions.

:::
