---
titolo: "Buoyancy and support of a submerged body"
teoria: [pascal-archimede]
parole: ["Buoyancy and support of a submerged body"]
---

A rigid object weighing $15\,\mathrm N$ hangs fully submerged in water and displaces $10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^3}$. Use $\rho=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Set up the force balance and find buoyancy and tension; predict the effect of lowering it without changing its volume or liquid density.

:::soluzione

$$
F_A=1000\cdot10\cdot10^{-3}=10\,\mathrm N,\qquad
T+F_A=15\,\mathrm N,\qquad T=5\,\mathrm N.
$$

Downward weight is balanced by upward buoyancy and tension. A spring balance would measure tension, not buoyancy or weight. Lowering the object raises absolute pressures around its surface but does not change the displaced water's weight: buoyancy and tension remain the same. Bottom contact and body compression are excluded.

:::
