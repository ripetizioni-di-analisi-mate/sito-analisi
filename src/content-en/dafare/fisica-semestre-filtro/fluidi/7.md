parole: ["Mean flow rate and accumulation"]
---

A collection measures $100\,\mathrm{mL}$ in $10\,\mathrm s$. Convert mean flow into SI units and litres per minute. If this inflow fed a container for the same interval with constant outflow $6\,\mathrm{mL/s}$, calculate accumulation, assuming an incompressible liquid.

:::soluzione

$$
Q=\dfrac{100}{10}=10\,\mathrm{mL/s}=10^{-5}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}=0.60\,\mathrm{L/min}.
$$
$$
\Delta V=100\,\mathrm{mL}-6\cdot10\,\mathrm{mL}=40\,\mathrm{mL}.
$$

Mean flow follows from total volume divided by time and does not require constant instantaneous flow. Accumulation only requires comparing total entering and exiting volumes over the same interval. The known outlet is constant, so its volume is flow times time. Flow equality cannot be imposed when the container is storing liquid.

:::
