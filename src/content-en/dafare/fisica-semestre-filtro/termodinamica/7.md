---
titolo: "Equilibrium with specified radiative power"
teoria: [trasmissione-calore]
parole: ["Equilibrium with specified radiative power"]
---

A grey surface of area $1.00\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and emissivity $0.80$ continuously receives $200\,\mathrm W$ from a heater. It exchanges energy only by radiation with large surroundings at $300\,\mathrm K$, with unit view factor and absorptivity equal to emissivity. Use $\sigma=5.67\cdot10^{-8}\,\mathrm{W\,m^{-2}K^{-4}}$. Find steady temperature and explain why ignoring radiation absorbed from the surroundings would give an inconsistent value.

:::soluzione
At steady state, internal energy is unchanged: net radiative loss must equal supplied power.
$$
200=0.80\sigma(T_s^4-300^4),\qquad
T_s=\left(300^4+\dfrac{200}{0.80\sigma}\right)^{\dfrac{1}{4}}
\simeq334.4\,\mathrm K.
$$
The surface is hotter than its surroundings, as required for net energy loss. Equating $200$ to emission alone would instead give about $257.7\,\mathrm K$: a surface colder than its surroundings would already absorb net radiant heat and could not remain steady at that temperature with its heater running. The correct balance therefore concerns emission minus absorption, not emission alone.
:::
