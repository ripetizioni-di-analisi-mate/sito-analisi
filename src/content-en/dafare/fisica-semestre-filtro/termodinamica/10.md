---
titolo: "Expansion against a finite pressure"
teoria: [trasformazioni, entropia]
parole: ["Expansion against a finite pressure"]
---

A closed ideal gas starts at $p_i=200\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and $V_i=10.0\,\mathrm L$ at $300\,\mathrm K$. A reservoir at $300\,\mathrm K$ ensures the same final temperature. The gas expands against constant $p_{\mathrm{ext}}=100\,\mathrm{kPa}$ to $V_f=20.0\,\mathrm L$, with only volume work. Find work, heat and total entropy change of gas plus reservoir. Compare work with a reversible isotherm between the same endpoints.

:::soluzione
Actual work uses external pressure:
$$
W=p_{\mathrm{ext}}(V_f-V_i)=1000\,\mathrm J.
$$
Since ideal-gas endpoints share a temperature, $\Delta U=0$ and $Q=W=1000\,\mathrm J$. Also $nRT=p_iV_i=2000\,\mathrm J$, so
$$
\Delta S_g=\dfrac{2000}{300}\ln2\simeq4.621\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}},
\qquad \Delta S_a=-\dfrac{1000}{300}\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}.
$$
The total is about $1.288\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}>0$. The reversible isotherm instead gives $W_{\mathrm{rev}}=2000\ln2\simeq1386\,\mathrm J$, exceeding actual work. Using the logarithm for actual work would confuse equilibrium internal pressure with imposed external pressure. Gas entropy change remains calculable from endpoints even when the actual process is irreversible.
:::
