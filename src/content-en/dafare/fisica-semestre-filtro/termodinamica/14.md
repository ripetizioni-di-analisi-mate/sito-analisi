---
titolo: "Two finite bodies reach equilibrium"
teoria: [calorimetria, entropia]
parole: ["Two finite bodies reach equilibrium"]
---

Two identical bodies, each of constant heat capacity $500\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$, initially have temperatures $300$ and $500\,\mathrm K$. They are placed in direct contact inside an isolated enclosure, with no work, reactions or phase changes. Find final temperature and total entropy change. Then assess: “Because energy is conserved, entropy must also be conserved.”

:::soluzione
The balance $500(T_f-300)+500(T_f-500)=0$ gives $T_f=400\,\mathrm K$. Use auxiliary reversible paths for the entropy changes:
$$
\Delta S_{\mathrm{tot}}=500\ln\dfrac{400}{300}+500\ln\dfrac{400}{500}
=500\ln\dfrac{16}{15}\simeq32.3\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}.
$$
The positive result describes entropy production during equilibration across finite temperature differences. The first law imposes conservation of combined energy; the second requires that isolated entropy not decrease and allows it to increase. These are not two forms of the same conservation law. The hot body's negative contribution is exceeded by the cold body's gain. The inverse process involving only these same bodies would give a negative sum and be impossible without additional external effects.
:::
