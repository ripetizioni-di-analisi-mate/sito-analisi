A cyclic device receives $1.00\,\mathrm{kW}$ of electrical power, entirely converted to work input, and extracts $3.00\,\mathrm{kW}$ from a source at $260\,\mathrm K$. It rejects heat only to surroundings at $300\,\mathrm K$. Find refrigerator COP, heat-pump COP and rejected power. Check the Carnot limit and calculate the reservoirs' total entropy-change rate.

:::soluzione
Energy balance gives $P_h=P_c+P_W=4.00\,\mathrm{kW}$. Thus
$$
\mathrm{COP}_{\mathrm R}=\dfrac{3}{1}=3,
\qquad \mathrm{COP}_{\mathrm H}=\dfrac{4}{1}=4
=\mathrm{COP}_{\mathrm R}+1.
$$
The reversible refrigerator limit is $\mathrm{COP}_{\mathrm R,C}=\dfrac{260}{300-260}=6.50$, above the stated value. Total entropy rate is
$$
\dot S_{\mathrm{tot}}=-\dfrac{3000}{260}+\dfrac{4000}{300}
\simeq1.79\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}s^{-1}}>0.
$$
The device respects both laws in the stated model. Its two COPs differ because the chosen useful effect changes, not the device. Power data require an entropy balance per unit time; confusing it with a change per cycle would leave cycle duration or frequency missing.
:::
