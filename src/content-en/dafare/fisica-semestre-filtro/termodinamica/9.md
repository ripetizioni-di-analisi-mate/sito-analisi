titolo: "Insufficient data to determine heat"
teoria: [primo-principio, trasformazioni]
parole: ["Insufficient data to determine heat"]
---

One mole of monatomic ideal gas goes from $300$ to $400\,\mathrm K$. Only these data and $R=8.314\,\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}K^{-1}}$ are known. A report claims that absorbed heat must be $1247\,\mathrm J$. Assess the claim: determine what is fixed and construct two processes, one isochoric and one mechanically reversible isobaric, with only volume work, to clarify the missing information.

:::soluzione
Internal energy change is fixed:
$$
\Delta U=n\dfrac32R\Delta T=1247.1\,\mathrm J.
$$
In the isochoric process, $W=0$, so $Q_V=1247.1\,\mathrm J$. In the isobaric process,
$$
W=nR\Delta T=831.4\,\mathrm J,
\qquad Q_p=\Delta U+W=2078.5\,\mathrm J.
$$
Both satisfy the specified temperatures but end at different volumes. The report equated heat with internal energy change without knowing work. Its claim would become valid by adding an isochoric constraint and excluding other work; it is not a general consequence of ideal behaviour alone. The data do not even specify a complete pair of states between which paths could be compared.
:::
