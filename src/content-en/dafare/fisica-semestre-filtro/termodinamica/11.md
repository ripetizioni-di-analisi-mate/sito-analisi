---
titolo: "Inferring gamma from a compression"
teoria: [gas-ideale, trasformazioni]
parole: ["Inferring gamma from a compression"]
---

A closed ideal gas has constant, initially unknown molar heat capacities. A reversible adiabatic compression reduces volume from $4.00$ to $1.00\,\mathrm L$ and raises temperature from $300$ to $600\,\mathrm K$. Initial pressure is $100\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and only volume work occurs. Infer $\gamma$, $C_V$ and $C_p$ in multiples of $R$, then work done by the gas.

:::soluzione
The reversible adiabatic law gives
$$
\dfrac{T_f}{T_i}=\left(\dfrac{V_i}{V_f}\right)^{\gamma-1},
\qquad 2=4^{\gamma-1},
\qquad \gamma=1+\dfrac{\ln2}{\ln4}=\dfrac32.
$$
From $C_p-C_V=R$ and $\gamma=\dfrac{C_p}{C_V}$, obtain $C_V=2R$ and $C_p=3R$. Also $nR=\dfrac{p_iV_i}{T_i}=\dfrac{400}{300}\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$. Therefore
$$
\Delta U=nC_V\Delta T=2\cdot\dfrac{400}{300}\cdot300=800\,\mathrm J,
\qquad W=-800\,\mathrm J.
$$
The negative sign denotes received work. No chemical identity needs assigning: the data determine capacities within the stated model. Without reversibility, temperature and volume ratios would not justify inferring $\gamma$ using this law.
:::
