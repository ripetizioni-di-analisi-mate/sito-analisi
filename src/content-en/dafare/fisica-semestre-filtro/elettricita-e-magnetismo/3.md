---
titolo: "Deflection between plates"
teoria: [campo-elettrico]
parole: ["deflection between plates"]
---

A positive particle of mass $m=1.0\cdot10^{-12}\,\mathrm{kg}$ and charge $q=2.0\cdot10^{-9}\,\mathrm C$ enters the origin with velocity $v_x=100\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_y=0$. For $0<x<L=0.020\,\mathrm m$, only a uniform field $E_y=500\,\mathrm{N/C}$ acts. Plates are at $y=\pm0.030\,\mathrm m$. Calculate transit time, deflection and exit velocity components, checking that no collision occurs. Neglect fringing, gravity and drag.

:::soluzione
Acceleration is $a_y=\dfrac{qE_y}{m}=1.0\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, while $a_x=0$. Thus $t=\dfrac{L}{v_x}=2.0\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm s$, $y=\dfrac{a_yt^2}{2}=0.020\,\mathrm m$ and $v_y=a_yt=200\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $v_x$ remains $100\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Deflection increases monotonically and at exit is less than $0.030\,\mathrm m$, so no earlier collision occurs. As an energy check, $\Delta K=\dfrac{m v_y^2}{2}=2.0\cdot10^{-8}\,\mathrm J=qE_yy$. The trajectory is parabolic whereas field lines are vertical.
:::
