A $3\,\Omega$ resistor is in series with a parallel pair of $6\,\Omega$ and $12\,\Omega$, powered by an ideal $21\,\mathrm V$ source. Find total and branch currents. Calculate each resistor’s power and check the source balance. Assume ideal wires and constant resistances.

:::soluzione
The parallel pair is $R_p=\dfrac{6\cdot12}{6+12}=4\,\Omega$, so $R_{\mathrm{eq}}=7\,\Omega$ and $I=3\,\mathrm A$. The series resistor drops $9\,\mathrm V$, leaving $12\,\mathrm V$ across the branches. Currents are $2\,\mathrm A$ through $6\,\Omega$ and $1\,\mathrm A$ through $12\,\Omega$, adding back to $I$. Powers are respectively $27\,\mathrm W$, $24\,\mathrm W$ and $12\,\mathrm W$. Their sum $63\,\mathrm W$ equals the source’s $21\cdot3\,\mathrm W$. Applying $21\,\mathrm V$ directly to each parallel branch would give an inconsistent balance by ignoring the initial drop.
:::
