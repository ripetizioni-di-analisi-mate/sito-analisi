---
titolo: "Where does the field vanish?"
teoria: [campo-elettrico]
parole: ["where does the field vanish?"]
---

Two stationary sources in vacuum are $Q_1=+4\,\mathrm{nC}$ at $x=0$ and $Q_2=+1\,\mathrm{nC}$ at $x=0.30\,\mathrm m$. Find the axial point where field vanishes, excluding the sources, and explain why no other axial point works. With $k_e=9.0\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$, also calculate potential at that point with zero at infinity.

:::soluzione
Outside the segment, both fields point the same way and cannot cancel. Inside, they oppose, requiring $\dfrac{4}{x^2}=\dfrac{1}{(0.30-x)^2}$. Distances are positive: $\dfrac{2}{x}=\dfrac{1}{0.30-x}$, giving $x=0.20\,\mathrm m$. The point is closer to the smaller charge, as expected. Potential is instead $V=9.0\cdot10^9\left[\dfrac{4\cdot10^{-9}}{0.20}+\dfrac{10^{-9}}{0.10}\right]=270\,\mathrm V$. Fields cancel vectorially, but positive potentials add: this does not contradict zero field.
:::
