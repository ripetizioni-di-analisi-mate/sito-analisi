titolo: "Piecewise flux and current sign"
teoria: [induzione]
parole: ["piecewise flux and current sign"]
---

A closed coil has $20$ turns, total resistance $4.0\,\Omega$ and negligible self-inductance. Flux per turn relative to an outward normal rises linearly from $0$ to $3.0\,\mathrm{mWb}$ between $t=0$ and $1.5\,\mathrm s$, remains constant until $3.0\,\mathrm s$, then falls linearly to $-3.0\,\mathrm{mWb}$ at $4.5\,\mathrm s$. Determine signed emf and current in each interval, taking counterclockwise as positive when viewed from the front. Exclude slope-change instants.

:::soluzione
The slopes are $+2.0\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{Wb/s}$, zero and $-4.0\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{Wb/s}$. Multiplying by $-N$ gives emfs $-0.040\,\mathrm V$, zero and $+0.080\,\mathrm V$. Dividing by $R$ gives $-0.010\,\mathrm A$, zero and $+0.020\,\mathrm A$: first clockwise, then absent, finally counterclockwise. In the third segment, total change is $-6.0\,\mathrm{mWb}$, not $-3.0\,\mathrm{mWb}$. Flux passing through zero does not reverse current while the slope stays negative. Change, rather than flux sign alone, determines induction.
:::
