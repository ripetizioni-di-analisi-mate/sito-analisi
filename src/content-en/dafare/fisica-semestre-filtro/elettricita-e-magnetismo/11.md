---
titolo: "Redistribution between capacitors"
teoria: [reti-condensatori]
parole: ["redistribution between capacitors"]
---

A $2.0\,\mu\mathrm F$ capacitor is at $12\,\mathrm V$ and a $4.0\,\mu\mathrm F$ capacitor is at $3.0\,\mathrm V$. After disconnecting the sources, positive plates are connected together and negative plates together through weakly resistive leads. Once equilibrium is reached, without external charge leakage, find final voltage, charges and loss of electrostatic energy. Neglect radiation.

:::soluzione
Conserved positive-node charge is $2\cdot12+4\cdot3=36\,\mu\mathrm C$. Parallel capacitance is $6\,\mu\mathrm F$, so $V_f=6\,\mathrm V$, with final charges $12\,\mu\mathrm C$ and $24\,\mu\mathrm C$. Initial energy is $\dfrac{2\cdot12^2}{2}+\dfrac{4\cdot3^2}{2}=162\,\mu\mathrm J$; final energy is $\dfrac{6\cdot6^2}{2}=108\,\mu\mathrm J$. The difference, $54\,\mu\mathrm J$, is dissipated during redistribution in the model’s resistive leads. Energy of the capacitors alone is not conserved, but the complete balance includes heat. Polarity matters: reversing one capacitor would change the algebraic charge on the nodes.
:::
