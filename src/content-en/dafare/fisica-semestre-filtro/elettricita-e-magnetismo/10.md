---
titolo: "Same dielectric, two constraints"
teoria: [condensatori]
parole: ["same dielectric, two constraints"]
---

Two identical capacitors of initial capacitance $C_0=2.0\,\mu\mathrm F$ are charged to $12\,\mathrm V$. One is disconnected; the other stays on an ideal $12\,\mathrm V$ source. A linear dielectric of $\varepsilon_r=3$ is fully inserted into each without leakage. Compare final charges, voltages and energies. For the connected case, calculate the source’s electrical work during insertion as constant voltage times additional supplied charge.

:::soluzione
Initially $q_0=24\,\mu\mathrm C$ and $U_0=144\,\mu\mathrm J$; both capacitances become $6.0\,\mu\mathrm F$. The isolated case retains $q=24\,\mu\mathrm C$, giving $4\,\mathrm V$ and energy $48\,\mu\mathrm J$. The connected case retains $12\,\mathrm V$, giving $q=72\,\mu\mathrm C$ and $U=432\,\mu\mathrm J$. The source supplies an additional $48\,\mu\mathrm C$ and does work $W_g=12\cdot48=576\,\mu\mathrm J$. This exceeds the capacitor’s energy increase, $288\,\mu\mathrm J$: the complete balance must include mechanical transfer to the dielectric. Simultaneously fixing charge and voltage would be incompatible with tripled capacitance.
:::
