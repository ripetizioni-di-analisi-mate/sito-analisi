The first row in a hall has $15$ seats, and each row has $3$ more seats than the preceding one. How many seats are in the eighteenth row?

- A) $66$
- B) $69$
- C) $63$
- D) $54$
- E) $51$

:::soluzione
Answer A: $66$.
:::
