Someone puts $1$ euro in a piggy bank on the first day and doubles the previous day’s deposit every day after that. How much is in the piggy bank after ten days?

- A) $1024$ euros
- B) $512$ euros
- C) $2047$ euros
- D) $511$ euros
- E) $1023$ euros

:::soluzione
Answer E: $1023$ euros.
:::
