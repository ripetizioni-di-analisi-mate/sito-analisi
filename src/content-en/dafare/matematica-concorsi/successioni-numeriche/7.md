What is the next term in the sequence $2, 5, 10, 17, 26, 37, \dots$?

- A) $48$
- B) $49$
- C) $51$
- D) $50$
- E) $47$

:::soluzione
Answer D: $50$, because the terms are the numbers $n^2 + 1$.
:::
