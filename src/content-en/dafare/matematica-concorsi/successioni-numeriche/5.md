What is the next term in the sequence $1, 4, 13, 40, \dots$?

- A) $120$
- B) $121$
- C) $67$
- D) $160$
- E) $123$

:::soluzione
Answer B: $121$, because each term is three times the preceding term plus $1$.
:::
