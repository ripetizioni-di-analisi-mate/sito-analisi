titolo: A missing-number table
teoria: [tabelle-numeriche]
parole: [table, missing number]
---

In the following table, the numbers in every row follow the same rule.
$$
\begin{array}{|c|c|c|}
\hline
6 & 2 & 10 \\ \hline
5 & 3 & 7 \\ \hline
8 & 5 & 11 \\ \hline
9 & 4 & ? \\ \hline
\end{array}
$$
What is the missing number?

- A) $13$
- B) $36$
- C) $14$
- D) $5$
- E) $22$

:::soluzione
Answer C: $14$, because the third number is twice the first minus the second.
:::
