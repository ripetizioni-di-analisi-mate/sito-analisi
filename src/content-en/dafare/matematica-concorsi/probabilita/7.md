An urn contains $2$ red balls and $3$ black balls. Three balls are drawn one at a time, with the ball returned after each draw. What is the probability that all three are black?

- A) $\dfrac{1}{10}$
- B) $\dfrac{27}{125}$
- C) $\dfrac{9}{25}$
- D) $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- E) $\dfrac{98}{125}$

:::soluzione
Answer B, $\left( \dfrac{3}{5} \right)^{3} = \dfrac{27}{125}$.
:::
