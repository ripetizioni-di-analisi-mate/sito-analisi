An urn contains $1$ red ball and $4$ white balls. Three balls are drawn one after another without replacement. What is the probability that the red ball appears on the third draw?

- A) $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- B) $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- C) $\dfrac{16}{125}$
- D) $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- E) $\dfrac{1}{60}$

:::soluzione
Answer D, $\dfrac{4}{5} \cdot \dfrac{3}{4} \cdot \dfrac{1}{3} = \dfrac{1}{5}$.
:::
