A bag contains $10$ tickets for a company raffle, including $3$ winning tickets. An employee draws two tickets at random, one after the other, without returning the first. What is the probability that at least one of the tickets wins?

- A) $\dfrac{7}{15}$
- B) $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- C) $\dfrac{8}{15}$
- D) $0.51$
- E) $\dfrac{1}{15}$

:::soluzione
Answer C, $1 - \dfrac{7}{10} \cdot \dfrac{6}{9} = 1 - \dfrac{7}{15} = \dfrac{8}{15}$.
:::
