From $8$ candidates, including Anna and Bruno, $3$ are chosen at random for an interview. What is the probability that both Anna and Bruno are chosen?

- A) $\dfrac{1}{28}$
- B) $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- C) $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- D) $\dfrac{9}{64}$
- E) $\dfrac{3}{28}$

:::soluzione
Answer E, $\dfrac{C_{6,1}}{C_{8,3}} = \dfrac{6}{56} = \dfrac{3}{28}$.
:::
