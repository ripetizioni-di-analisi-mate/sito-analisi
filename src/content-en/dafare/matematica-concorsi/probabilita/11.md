Five people, including Marco and Sara, line up in random order at a service counter. What is the probability that Marco and Sara stand next to each other?

- A) $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- B) $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- C) $\dfrac{1}{10}$
- D) $\dfrac{1}{20}$
- E) $\dfrac{3}{5}$

:::soluzione
Answer A, $\dfrac{2 \cdot 4!}{5!} = \dfrac{48}{120} = \dfrac{2}{5}$.
:::
