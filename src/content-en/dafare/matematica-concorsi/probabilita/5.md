Two fair six-sided dice are rolled. What is the probability that at least one of the numbers is even?

- A) $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- B) $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- C) $1$
- D) $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- E) $\dfrac{3}{4}$

:::soluzione
Answer E, $1 - \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot \dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{3}{4}$.
:::
