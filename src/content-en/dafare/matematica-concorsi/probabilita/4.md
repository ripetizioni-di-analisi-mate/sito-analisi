One card is drawn from a well-shuffled French-suited deck of $52$ cards. What is the probability that it is an ace or a spade?

- A) $\dfrac{17}{52}$
- B) $\dfrac{1}{52}$
- C) $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- D) $\dfrac{4}{13}$
- E) $\dfrac{1}{13}$

:::soluzione
Answer D, $\dfrac{4 + 13 - 1}{52} = \dfrac{16}{52} = \dfrac{4}{13}$.
:::
