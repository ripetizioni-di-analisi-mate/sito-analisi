A machine produces a defective part with probability $2.5\%$. What is the expected number of defective parts in a batch of $1200$ parts?

- A) $25$
- B) $300$
- C) $30$
- D) $3$
- E) $1170$

:::soluzione
Answer C, $1200 \cdot 0.025 = 30$.
:::
