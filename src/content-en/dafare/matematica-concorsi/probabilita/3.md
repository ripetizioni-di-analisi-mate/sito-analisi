One card is drawn from a well-shuffled Italian deck of $40$ cards. What is the probability that it is a face card (jack, knight, or king)?

- A) $\dfrac{1}{10}$
- B) $\dfrac{3}{10}$
- C) $\dfrac{3}{13}$
- D) $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- E) $\dfrac{3}{40}$

:::soluzione
Answer B, $\dfrac{12}{40} = \dfrac{3}{10}$.
:::
