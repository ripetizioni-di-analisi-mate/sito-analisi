In one year, an agency denied $18$ of the $120$ reimbursement requests it received. What is the estimated probability that a request is denied, based on the relative frequency?

- A) $18\%$
- B) $15\%$
- C) $12\%$
- D) $85\%$
- E) $1.5\%$

:::soluzione
Answer B, $\dfrac{18}{120} = 0.15 = 15\%$.
:::
