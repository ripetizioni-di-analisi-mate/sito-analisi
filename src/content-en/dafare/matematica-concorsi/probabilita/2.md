Two fair six-sided dice are rolled. What is the probability that the numbers add up to $10$?

- A) $\dfrac{1}{11}$
- B) $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- C) $\dfrac{1}{12}$
- D) $\dfrac{1}{18}$
- E) $\dfrac{2}{21}$

:::soluzione
Answer C, $\dfrac{3}{36} = \dfrac{1}{12}$.
:::
