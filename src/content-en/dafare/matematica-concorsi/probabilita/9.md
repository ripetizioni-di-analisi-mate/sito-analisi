A training course has $40$ participants. There are $16$ municipal employees, $6$ of whom are under $30$ years old, and $24$ national government employees. One municipal employee is chosen at random. What is the probability that this person is under $30$?

- A) $\dfrac{3}{10}$
- B) $\dfrac{3}{20}$
- C) $\dfrac{5}{8}$
- D) $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- E) $\dfrac{1}{2}$

:::soluzione
Answer D, $\dfrac{6}{16} = \dfrac{3}{8}$.
:::
