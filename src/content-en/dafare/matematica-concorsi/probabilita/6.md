Two office printers operate independently. On any given day, the first breaks down with probability $10\%$ and the second with probability $20\%$. What is the probability that neither printer breaks down that day?

- A) $0.72$
- B) $0.85$
- C) $0.98$
- D) $0.26$
- E) $0.02$

:::soluzione
Answer A, $0.9 \cdot 0.8 = 0.72$.
:::
