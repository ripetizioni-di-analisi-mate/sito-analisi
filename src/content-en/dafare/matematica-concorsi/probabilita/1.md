A fair six-sided die is rolled. What is the probability of rolling a prime number?

- A) $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- B) $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- C) $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- D) $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- E) $\dfrac{5}{6}$

:::soluzione
Answer A, $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
:::
