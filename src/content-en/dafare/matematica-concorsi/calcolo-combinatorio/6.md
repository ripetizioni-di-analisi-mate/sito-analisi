A questionnaire has $5$ questions. Each can be answered “yes,” “no,” or “I don’t know.” How many answer sequences are possible?

- A) $125$
- B) $15$
- C) $243$
- D) $60$
- E) $81$

:::soluzione
Answer C, $243$.
:::
