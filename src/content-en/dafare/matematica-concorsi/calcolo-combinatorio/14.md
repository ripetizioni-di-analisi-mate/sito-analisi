A code consists of $3$ digits from $0$ to $9$, with repetition allowed, and cannot contain the digit $0$. How many different codes can be formed?

- A) $504$
- B) $729$
- C) $84$
- D) $27$
- E) $81$

:::soluzione
Answer B, $729$.
:::
