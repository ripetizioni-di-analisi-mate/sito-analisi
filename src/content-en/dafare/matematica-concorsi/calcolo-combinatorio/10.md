A PIN has $4$ digits, each from $0$ to $9$. How many PINs contain at least one repeated digit?

- A) $5040$
- B) $10\,000$
- C) $6561$
- D) $3439$
- E) $4960$

:::soluzione
Answer E, $4960$.
:::
