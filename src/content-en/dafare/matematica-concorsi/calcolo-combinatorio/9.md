How many four-digit numbers can be formed using only the odd digits $1$, $3$, $5$, $7$, and $9$, with repetition allowed?

- A) $120$
- B) $625$
- C) $1024$
- D) $20$
- E) $3125$

:::soluzione
Answer B, $625$.
:::
