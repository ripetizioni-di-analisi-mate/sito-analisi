An exam committee is formed by choosing $3$ teachers from $5$ and $2$ outside experts from $4$. How many different committees can be formed?

- A) $16$
- B) $126$
- C) $60$
- D) $720$
- E) $30$

:::soluzione
Answer C, $60$.
:::
