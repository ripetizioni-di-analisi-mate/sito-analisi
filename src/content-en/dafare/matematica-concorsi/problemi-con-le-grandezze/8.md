Two cyclists leave at the same time from towns $54$ km apart and ride toward each other on the same road at constant speeds of $20$ km/h and $16$ km/h. How long does it take them to meet?

- A) $13$ h $30$ min
- B) $1$ h $50$ min
- C) $2$ h $42$ min
- D) $1$ h $30$ min
- E) $1$ h $20$ min

:::soluzione
Answer D. $1$ h $30$ min.
:::
