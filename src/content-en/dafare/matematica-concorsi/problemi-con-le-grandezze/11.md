One worker can install a fence in $9$ hours and a coworker can install it in $18$ hours. How long will they take to install the fence together if each works at a constant rate?

- A) $6$ hours
- B) $13.5$ hours
- C) $27$ hours
- D) $4.5$ hours
- E) $9$ hours

:::soluzione
Answer A. $6$ hours.
:::
