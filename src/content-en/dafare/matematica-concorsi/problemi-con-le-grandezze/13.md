You mix $4$ liters of a solution containing $30\%$ detergent with $6$ liters of a solution containing $15\%$ of the same detergent. What is the detergent concentration of the mixture?

- A) $22.5\%$
- B) $21\%$
- C) $45\%$
- D) $24\%$
- E) $2.1\%$

:::soluzione
Answer B. $21\%$.
:::
