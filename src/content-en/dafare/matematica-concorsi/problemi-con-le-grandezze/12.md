A faucet fills a tank in $5$ hours and a drain empties it in $20$ hours, both at constant rates. The tank starts empty. If the faucet and drain are opened at the same time, how long does the tank take to fill?

- A) $4$ h
- B) $15$ h
- C) $12$ h $30$ min
- D) $6$ h $40$ min
- E) $25$ h

:::soluzione
Answer D. $6$ h $40$ min.
:::
