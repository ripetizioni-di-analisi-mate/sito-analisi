A van leaves at $10{:}00$ and travels at a constant speed of $50$ km/h. At $10{:}12$, a motorcycle leaves the same point and follows the van along the same road at a constant speed of $80$ km/h. At what time does the motorcycle catch up with the van?

- A) $10{:}20$
- B) $10{:}24$
- C) $10{:}32$
- D) $10{:}45$
- E) $10{:}52$

:::soluzione
Answer C. At $10{:}32$.
:::
