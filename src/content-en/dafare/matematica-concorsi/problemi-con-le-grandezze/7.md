A cyclist travels $63$ km at an average speed of $18$ km/h. How long does the trip take?

- A) $3$ h $50$ min
- B) $3$ h $30$ min
- C) $3$ h $5$ min
- D) $4$ h
- E) $3$ h $15$ min

:::soluzione
Answer B. $3$ h $30$ min.
:::
