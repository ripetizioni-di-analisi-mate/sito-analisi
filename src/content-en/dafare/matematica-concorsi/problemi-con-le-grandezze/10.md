A bus travels a route at an average speed of $90$ km/h on the outbound trip and $60$ km/h on the return trip along the same route. What is its average speed for the entire trip?

- A) $75$ km/h
- B) $30$ km/h
- C) $70$ km/h
- D) $80$ km/h
- E) $72$ km/h

:::soluzione
Answer E. $72$ km/h.
:::
