A rectangular tank has inside dimensions of $60$ cm, $40$ cm, and $35$ cm. What is its capacity?

- A) $8.4$ liters
- B) $840$ liters
- C) $84$ liters
- D) $0.84$ liters
- E) $8400$ liters

:::soluzione
Answer C. $84$ liters.
:::
