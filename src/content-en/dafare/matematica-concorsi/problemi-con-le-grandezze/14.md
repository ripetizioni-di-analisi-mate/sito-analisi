A rectangular block of iron measures $10$ cm by $5$ cm by $4$ cm. The density of iron is $7.8\ \text{g/cm}^3$. What is the mass of the block?

- A) $15.6$ kg
- B) $0.156$ kg
- C) $1.56$ kg
- D) $2.56$ kg
- E) $156$ kg

:::soluzione
Answer C. $1.56$ kg.
:::
