At an exchange rate of $1$ euro $= 0.85$ pounds sterling, what is the value of $68$ pounds sterling in euros?

- A) $57.80$ euros
- B) $8$ euros
- C) $800$ euros
- D) $80$ euros
- E) $78.20$ euros

:::soluzione
Answer D. $80$ euros.
:::
