The table shows the $120$ applicants in a selection process by level of education and outcome.

| | High school diploma | College degree | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Qualified | $27$ | $18$ | $45$ |
| Did not qualify | $45$ | $30$ | $75$ |
| Total | $72$ | $48$ | $120$ |

*Among* applicants with a college degree, what percentage qualified?

- A) $37.5\%$
- B) $18\%$
- C) $40\%$
- D) $15\%$
- E) $62.5\%$

:::soluzione
Answer A: $37.5\%$.
:::
