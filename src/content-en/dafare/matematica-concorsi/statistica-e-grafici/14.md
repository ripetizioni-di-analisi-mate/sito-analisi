The graph shows the number of requests received by a municipal office in four consecutive quarters. What was the percent increase in requests from the second to the third quarter?

- A) $25\%$
- B) $10\%$
- C) $9.1\%$
- D) $12.5\%$
- E) $2.5\%$

::grafico{linee="1,200 2,250 3,275 4,240" punti="1,200,pieno;2,250,pieno;3,275,pieno;4,240,pieno" etichette="0.8,212,200;1.8,262,250;2.8,287,275;3.8,252,240" xmin=0 xmax=5 ymin=0 ymax=300 nomi="t,n" legenda=0 titolo="Number $n$ of requests in quarter $t$. The quarterly totals are $200$, $250$, $275$, and $240$"}

:::soluzione
Answer B: $10\%$.
:::
