The mean score on $5$ tests is $6.4$. After a sixth test, the mean of the $6$ scores is $6.5$. What was the score on the sixth test?

- A) $0.1$
- B) $6.5$
- C) $8$
- D) $7$
- E) $7.5$

:::soluzione
Answer D: $7$.
:::
