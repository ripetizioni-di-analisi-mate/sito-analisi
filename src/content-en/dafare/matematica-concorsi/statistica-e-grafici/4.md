A student passed three exams with grades of $21$, $27$, and $30$. The exams are worth $12$, $6$, and $6$ credits, respectively. What is the credit-weighted mean of the grades?

- A) $26$
- B) $24.75$
- C) $25.5$
- D) $27$
- E) $24$

:::soluzione
Answer B: $24.75$.
:::
