Over five days, a stock traded at $12.40$, $12.85$, $11.95$, $13.10$, and $12.60$ euros per share. What is the range of its price?

- A) $0.70$ euros
- B) $0.45$ euros
- C) $12.58$ euros
- D) $0.25$ euros
- E) $1.15$ euros

:::soluzione
Answer E: $1.15$ euros.
:::
