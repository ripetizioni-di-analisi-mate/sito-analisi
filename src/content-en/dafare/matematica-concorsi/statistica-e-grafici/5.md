The table shows the number of cars owned by each of the $20$ households in an apartment building.

| Cars | $1$ | $2$ | $3$ | $4$ | $5$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Households | $4$ | $6$ | $5$ | $3$ | $2$ |

What is the mean number of cars per household?

- A) $3$
- B) $4$
- C) $2.5$
- D) $10.6$
- E) $2.65$

:::soluzione
Answer E: $2.65$.
:::
