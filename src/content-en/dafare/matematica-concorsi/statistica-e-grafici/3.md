Over six days, a technician completed $11$, $16$, $9$, $22$, $14$, and $12$ service calls. What was the mean number of service calls per day?

- A) $13$
- B) $16.8$
- C) $14$
- D) $84$
- E) $15.5$

:::soluzione
Answer C: $14$.
:::
