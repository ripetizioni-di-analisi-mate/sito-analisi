In a survey of $600$ people, $75$ named the train as their usual means of transportation. In a pie chart of the responses, what angle does the sector for the train measure?

- A) $75°$
- B) $12.5°$
- C) $60°$
- D) $45°$
- E) $7.5°$

:::soluzione
Answer D: $45°$.
:::
