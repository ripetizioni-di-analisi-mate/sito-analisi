A courier has delivered $8$ packages with a mean weight of $15$ kg. If the courier also delivers a ninth package weighing $24$ kg, what is the mean weight of all $9$ packages?

- A) $19.5$ kg
- B) $16$ kg
- C) $17$ kg
- D) $15.5$ kg
- E) $18$ kg

:::soluzione
Answer B: $16$ kg.
:::
