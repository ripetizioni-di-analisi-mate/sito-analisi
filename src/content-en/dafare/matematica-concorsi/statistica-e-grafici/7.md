A store recorded the sizes of the T-shirts it sold one morning:

S, M, M, L, XL, M, L, L, M, S, L, M, XL, L, M.

What is the mode of the sizes sold?

- A) L
- B) $6$
- C) XL
- D) S
- E) M

:::soluzione
Answer E: M.
:::
