In a group of $40$ candidates, $6$ have previously taken a civil service exam. What is the relative frequency of candidates who have previously taken such an exam?

- A) $0.15$
- B) $0.6$
- C) $6$
- D) $0.06$
- E) $0.85$

:::soluzione
Answer A: $0.15$.
:::
