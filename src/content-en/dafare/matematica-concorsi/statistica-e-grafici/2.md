The chart shows the number of unused vacation days for $50$ employees. What percentage of employees have *at least* $3$ unused vacation days?

- A) $22\%$
- B) $26\%$
- C) $18\%$
- D) $44\%$
- E) $56\%$

There are $12$ employees with $1$ day, $16$ with $2$, $13$ with $3$, and $9$ with $4$"}

:::soluzione
Answer D: $44\%$.
:::
