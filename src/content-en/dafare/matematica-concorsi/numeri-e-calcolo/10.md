Which fraction equals the number $1.\overline{36}$?

- A) $\dfrac{15}{11}$
- B) $\dfrac{136}{99}$
- C) $\dfrac{41}{30}$
- D) $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- E) $\dfrac{34}{25}$

:::soluzione
Answer A: $\dfrac{15}{11}$.
:::
