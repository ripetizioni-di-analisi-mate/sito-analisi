At a hospital, Marco works the night shift every $6$ days and Luca every $10$ days. They are on duty together tonight. In how many days will they next be on duty together?

- A) $16$
- B) $30$
- C) $2$
- D) $60$
- E) $20$

:::soluzione
Answer B: $30$ days.
:::
