On a bus, $\dfrac{3}{7}$ of the seats are occupied, and there are $24$ occupied seats. How many seats are on the bus?

- A) $32$
- B) $72$
- C) $56$
- D) $42$
- E) $168$

:::soluzione
Answer C: $56$.
:::
