What is the value of the expression $\dfrac{2^5 \cdot 4^{-1}}{8^{2}}$?

- A) $\dfrac{1}{32}$
- B) $8$
- C) $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- D) $2$
- E) $\dfrac{1}{8}$

:::soluzione
Answer E: $\dfrac{1}{8}$.
:::
