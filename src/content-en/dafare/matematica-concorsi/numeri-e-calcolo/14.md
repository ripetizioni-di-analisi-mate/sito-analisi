A car uses gasoline at a constant rate of $1$ liter every $13$ km. Rounded to the nearest hundredth of a liter, how much gasoline does it use to travel $100$ km?

- A) $7.7$ liters
- B) $7.60$ liters
- C) $1.30$ liters
- D) $7.69$ liters
- E) $76.92$ liters

:::soluzione
Answer D: $7.69$ liters.
:::
