A prize of $2\,700$ euros is divided so that one employee receives $\dfrac{4}{9}$ of it. How much does the employee receive?

- A) $300$ euros
- B) $1\,200$ euros
- C) $1\,500$ euros
- D) $6\,075$ euros
- E) $1\,080$ euros

:::soluzione
Answer B: $1\,200$ euros.
:::
