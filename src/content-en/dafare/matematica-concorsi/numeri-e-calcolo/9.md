An employee spends $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of a paycheck on rent and $\dfrac{3}{4}$ of what remains on other expenses, leaving $250$ euros. How much was the paycheck?

- A) $3\,000$ euros
- B) $1\,000$ euros
- C) $1\,200$ euros
- D) $1\,500$ euros
- E) $750$ euros

:::soluzione
Answer D: $1\,500$ euros.
:::
