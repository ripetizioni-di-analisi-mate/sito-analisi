What is the value of the expression $\dfrac{\dfrac{5}{6} - \dfrac{1}{4}}{\dfrac{7}{8}}$?

- A) $\dfrac{49}{96}$
- B) $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- C) $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- D) $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- E) $\dfrac{16}{21}$

:::soluzione
Answer C: $\dfrac{2}{3}$.
:::
