How is the number $0.000\,000\,47$ written in scientific notation?

- A) $4.7 \cdot 10^{-6}$
- B) $4.7 \cdot 10^{-7}$
- C) $4.7 \cdot 10^{-5}$
- D) $4.7 \cdot 10^{7}$
- E) $4.7 \cdot 10^{-8}$

:::soluzione
Answer B: $4.7 \cdot 10^{-7}$.
:::
