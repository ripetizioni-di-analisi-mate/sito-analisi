The solution of the system
$$
\begin{cases}
2x + y = 7 \\
x - y = 5
\end{cases}
$$
is given by which choice?

- A) $x = 4$, $y = 1$
- B) $x = 4$, $y = -1$
- C) $x = 2$, $y = 3$
- D) $x = 6$, $y = 1$
- E) $x = -1$, $y = 4$

:::soluzione
Answer B. $x = 4$, $y = -1$.
:::
