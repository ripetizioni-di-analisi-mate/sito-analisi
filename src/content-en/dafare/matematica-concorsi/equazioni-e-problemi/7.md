Two employees spend a total of $50$ hours working on a case. The second works $7$ hours less than twice the number of hours the first works. How many hours does the first employee work?

- A) $19$
- B) $31$
- C) $25$
- D) $18$
- E) $21$

:::soluzione
Answer A. $19$ hours.
:::
