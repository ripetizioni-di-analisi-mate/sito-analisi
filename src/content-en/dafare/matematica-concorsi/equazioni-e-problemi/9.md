An exhibition sells $150$ tickets. Some are full-price tickets at $15$ euros each, and the rest are discounted tickets at $10$ euros each. Total ticket revenue is $1950$ euros. How many discounted tickets were sold?

- A) $90$
- B) $75$
- C) $60$
- D) $45$
- E) $130$

:::soluzione
Answer C. $60$.
:::
