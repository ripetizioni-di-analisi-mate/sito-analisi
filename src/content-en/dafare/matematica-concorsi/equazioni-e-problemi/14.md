A salesperson can choose between a fixed salary of $2000$ euros per month and a salary of $1200$ euros per month plus $40$ euros per contract signed. What is the smallest number of contracts per month for which the second option pays more?

- A) $20$
- B) $50$
- C) $80$
- D) $21$
- E) $30$

:::soluzione
Answer D. $21$ contracts.
:::
