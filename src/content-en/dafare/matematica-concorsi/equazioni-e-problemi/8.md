Three brothers divide an inheritance. Two of them each receive $\dfrac{3}{8}$ of the inheritance, and the third receives the remaining $15\,000$ euros. What is the total inheritance?

- A) $24\,000$ euros
- B) $40\,000$ euros
- C) $45\,000$ euros
- D) $60\,000$ euros
- E) $120\,000$ euros

:::soluzione
Answer D. $60\,000$ euros.
:::
