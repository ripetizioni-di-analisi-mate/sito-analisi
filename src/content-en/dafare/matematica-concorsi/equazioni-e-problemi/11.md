At a stationery store, $3$ reams of paper and $4$ pens cost $126$ euros, while $1$ ream of paper and $2$ pens cost $48$ euros. How much does a ream of paper cost?

- A) $9$ euros
- B) $39$ euros
- C) $18$ euros
- D) $24$ euros
- E) $30$ euros

:::soluzione
Answer E. $30$ euros.
:::
