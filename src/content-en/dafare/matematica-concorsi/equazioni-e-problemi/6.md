A grandfather is $66$ years old and his grandson is $12$. How many years ago was the grandfather ten times as old as his grandson?

- A) $4$
- B) $6$
- C) $54$
- D) $9$
- E) $8$

:::soluzione
Answer B. $6$ years ago.
:::
