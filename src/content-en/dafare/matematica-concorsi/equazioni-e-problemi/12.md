What are the solutions of $x^2 + 3x - 10 = 0$?

- A) $x = 2$ and $x = -5$
- B) $x = -2$ and $x = 5$
- C) $x = 4$ and $x = -10$
- D) $x = 1$ and $x = -10$
- E) the equation has no real solutions

:::soluzione
Answer A. $x = 2$ and $x = -5$.
:::
