An isosceles triangle has a base of $10$ cm and two equal sides of $13$ cm each. What is its altitude to the base?

- A) $8$ cm
- B) $12$ cm
- C) $18$ cm
- D) $\sqrt{194}$ cm
- E) $24$ cm

:::soluzione
Answer B. $12$ cm.
:::
