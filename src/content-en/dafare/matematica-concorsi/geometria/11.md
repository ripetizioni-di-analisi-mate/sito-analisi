A cone has base radius $6$ cm and height $8$ cm. What is its lateral surface area?

- A) $60\pi\ \text{cm}^2$
- B) $48\pi\ \text{cm}^2$
- C) $96\pi\ \text{cm}^2$
- D) $120\pi\ \text{cm}^2$
- E) $36\pi\ \text{cm}^2$

:::soluzione
Answer A. $60\pi\ \text{cm}^2$.
:::
