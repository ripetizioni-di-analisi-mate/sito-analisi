Using $\pi \approx 3.14$, what is the volume of a sphere with radius $3$ cm?

- A) $339.12\ \text{cm}^3$
- B) $37.68\ \text{cm}^3$
- C) $113.04\ \text{cm}^3$
- D) $11.304\ \text{cm}^3$
- E) $84.78\ \text{cm}^3$

:::soluzione
Answer C. $113.04\ \text{cm}^3$.
:::
