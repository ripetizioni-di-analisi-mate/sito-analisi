A rectangular park measures $80$ m by $150$ m. To go from one corner to the opposite corner, you can walk along two adjacent sides or take the straight diagonal path. How much distance does the diagonal save?

- A) $170$ m
- B) $230$ m
- C) $70$ m
- D) $60$ m
- E) $100$ m

:::soluzione
Answer D. $60$ m.
:::
