On a map drawn to a scale of $1 : 1000$, a rectangular plot measures $3$ cm by $4$ cm. What is its actual area?

- A) $1.2\ \text{m}^2$
- B) $120\ \text{m}^2$
- C) $12\,000\ \text{m}^2$
- D) $1200\ \text{m}^2$
- E) $12\ \text{m}^2$

:::soluzione
Answer D. $1200\ \text{m}^2$.
:::
