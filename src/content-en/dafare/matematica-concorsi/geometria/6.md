A rectangular garden measuring $40$ m by $25$ m is fenced along its entire boundary except for a gate $3$ m wide. How long is the fence?

- A) $127$ m
- B) $130$ m
- C) $62$ m
- D) $997$ m
- E) $133$ m

:::soluzione
Answer A. $127$ m.
:::
