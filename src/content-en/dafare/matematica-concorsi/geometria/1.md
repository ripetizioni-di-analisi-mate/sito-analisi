Two angles are supplementary, and one is four times the other. What is the measure of the larger angle?

- A) $36^\circ$
- B) $72^\circ$
- C) $144^\circ$
- D) $135^\circ$
- E) $160^\circ$

:::soluzione
Answer C. $144^\circ$.
:::
