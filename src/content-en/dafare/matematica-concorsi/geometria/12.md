A square pyramid has base side length $2.4$ m and height $1.5$ m. What is its volume?

- A) $8.64\ \text{m}^3$
- B) $2.88\ \text{m}^3$
- C) $1.44\ \text{m}^3$
- D) $4.32\ \text{m}^3$
- E) $3.6\ \text{m}^3$

:::soluzione
Answer B. $2.88\ \text{m}^3$.
:::
