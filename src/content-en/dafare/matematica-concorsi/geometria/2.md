The hands of a clock move continuously at constant rates. What is the smaller angle between the hour and minute hands at $3{:}30$?

- A) $75^\circ$
- B) $90^\circ$
- C) $105^\circ$
- D) $60^\circ$
- E) $85^\circ$

:::soluzione
Answer A. $75^\circ$.
:::
