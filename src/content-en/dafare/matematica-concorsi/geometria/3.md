What is the sum of the interior angles of a convex octagon?

- A) $1440^\circ$
- B) $1080^\circ$
- C) $900^\circ$
- D) $720^\circ$
- E) $1260^\circ$

:::soluzione
Answer B. $1080^\circ$.
:::
