What is the area of an equilateral triangle with side length $10$ cm?

- A) $50\ \text{cm}^2$
- B) $50\sqrt{3}\ \text{cm}^2$
- C) $25\sqrt{2}\ \text{cm}^2$
- D) $10\sqrt{3}\ \text{cm}^2$
- E) $25\sqrt{3}\ \text{cm}^2$

:::soluzione
Answer E. $25\sqrt{3}\ \text{cm}^2$.
:::
