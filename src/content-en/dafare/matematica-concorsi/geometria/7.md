A circle has circumference $31.4$ cm. Using $\pi \approx 3.14$, what area does it enclose?

- A) $314\ \text{cm}^2$
- B) $31.4\ \text{cm}^2$
- C) $78.5\ \text{cm}^2$
- D) $15.7\ \text{cm}^2$
- E) $157\ \text{cm}^2$

:::soluzione
Answer C. $78.5\ \text{cm}^2$.
:::
