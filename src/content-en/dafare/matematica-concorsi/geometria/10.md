The radius of a cylinder doubles and its height is halved. How does the new volume compare with the original?

- A) It is unchanged.
- B) It is half as large.
- C) It is four times as large.
- D) It is eight times as large.
- E) It is twice as large.

:::soluzione
Answer E. It is twice as large.
:::
