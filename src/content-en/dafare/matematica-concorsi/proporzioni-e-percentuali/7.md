An item costs $183$ euros including VAT at $22\%$. What is the taxable amount?

- A) $150$ euros
- B) $142.74$ euros
- C) $161$ euros
- D) $40.26$ euros
- E) $155$ euros

:::soluzione
Answer A. $150$ euros.
:::
