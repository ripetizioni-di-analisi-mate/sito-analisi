The unemployment rate rises from $8\%$ to $10\%$. Which statement is true?

- A) The rate increased by $2\%$
- B) The rate increased by $2$ percentage points, a relative increase of $25\%$
- C) The rate increased by $20\%$
- D) The rate increased by $25$ percentage points
- E) The rate increased by $10\%$

:::soluzione
Answer B. The rate increased by $2$ percentage points, a relative change of $25\%$.
:::
