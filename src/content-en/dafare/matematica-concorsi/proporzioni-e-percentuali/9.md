Three kilograms of cheese cost $5.40$ euros. How much do seven kilograms of the same cheese cost?

- A) $12.60$ euros
- B) $11.20$ euros
- C) $13.50$ euros
- D) $9.40$ euros
- E) $12.90$ euros

:::soluzione
Answer A. $12.60$ euros.
:::
