In a competitive examination, $12$ out of $80$ candidates pass the written test. What percentage of the candidates pass?

- A) $12\%$
- B) $15\%$
- C) $18\%$
- D) $6.7\%$
- E) $20\%$

:::soluzione
Answer B. $15\%$.
:::
