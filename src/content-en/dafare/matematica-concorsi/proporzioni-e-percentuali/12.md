A stock price rises by $30\%$ and then falls by $30\%$. How does the final price compare with the initial price?

- A) It stays the same
- B) It increases by $9\%$
- C) It decreases by $3\%$
- D) It decreases by $9\%$
- E) It decreases by $30\%$

:::soluzione
Answer D. It decreases by $9\%$.
:::
