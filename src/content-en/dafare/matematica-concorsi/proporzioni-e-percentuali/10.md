A sum of $840$ euros is divided in direct proportion to $1$, $2$, and $4$. What is the largest share?

- A) $420$ euros
- B) $480$ euros
- C) $560$ euros
- D) $240$ euros
- E) $336$ euros

:::soluzione
Answer B. $480$ euros.
:::
