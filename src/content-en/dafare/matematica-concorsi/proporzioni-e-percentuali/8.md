Eight workers complete a job in $15$ days. How long would twelve workers take to complete the same job at the same rate per worker?

- A) $22.5$ days
- B) $12$ days
- C) $10$ days
- D) $11$ days
- E) $9$ days

:::soluzione
Answer C. $10$ days.
:::
