An activity lasts 1 h 45 min. What is this duration in decimal hours?

- $1.45\,\mathrm h$
- $1.045\,\mathrm h$
- $1.75\,\mathrm h$
- $0.75\,\mathrm h$

## Explanation

The forty-five minutes are forty-five sixtieths of an hour. Add this fraction to the first complete hour.

$1+\frac{45}{60}=1.75\,\mathrm h$.
