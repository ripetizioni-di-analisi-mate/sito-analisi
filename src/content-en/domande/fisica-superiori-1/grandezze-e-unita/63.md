An activity lasts 1 h 45 min. What is this duration in decimal hours?

- $1.45\,\mathrm h$
- $1.045\,\mathrm h$
- $1.75\,\mathrm h$
- $0.75\,\mathrm h$

## Explanation

The $45\,\mathrm{min}$ are a fraction of an hour. Because one hour contains $60\,\mathrm{min}$, the ratio is $\dfrac{45}{60}=\dfrac34=0.75$. Add the whole hour to obtain $1.75\,\mathrm h$. Converting $0.75$ back to minutes by multiplying by sixty recovers the stated duration of forty-five minutes.
