A $40\,\mathrm{mL}$ measuring cup fills a container with constant cross section and base area $20\,\mathrm{cm}^2$. How much does the level rise with each pour?

- $800\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.002\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

A millilitre equals one cubic centimetre. Dividing the added volume by the base area gives the height in centimetres.

$\frac{40}{20}=2\,\mathrm{cm}$.
