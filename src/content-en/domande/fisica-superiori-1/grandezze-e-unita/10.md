Calculate $(5.5\cdot10^3)(9\cdot10^2)$ and choose its value.

- $14.5\cdot10^5$
- $49.5\cdot10^5$
- $49.5\cdot10^6$
- $49.5\cdot10^1$

## Explanation

The two coefficients and the two powers of ten can be grouped separately. Powers with the same base are multiplied by adding the exponents, because the factors of ten accumulate. The question asks for the product's value. For normalized notation, move the coefficient back into the range from $1$ to $10$ and compensate by adjusting the exponent.

The coefficient is $5.5\cdot9=49.5$ and the exponent is $3+2=5$.
