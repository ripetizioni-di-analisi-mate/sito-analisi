A rectangular box has edges of $2\,\mathrm{dm}$, $30\,\mathrm{cm}$, and $0.4\,\mathrm m$. What is its volume in litres?

- $240\,\mathrm L$
- $2.4\,\mathrm L$
- $9\,\mathrm L$
- $24\,\mathrm L$

## Explanation

Decimetres are convenient before multiplying. The three edges become $2\,\mathrm{dm}$, $3\,\mathrm{dm}$ and $4\,\mathrm{dm}$. The cuboid volume is $2\cdot3\cdot4=24\,\mathrm{dm}^3$. Since $1\,\mathrm{dm}^3=1\,\mathrm L$, the numerical value remains $24$. This unit choice avoids an additional conversion factor after the product.
