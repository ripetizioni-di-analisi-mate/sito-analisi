A rectangular box has edges of $2\,\mathrm{dm}$, $30\,\mathrm{cm}$, and $0.4\,\mathrm m$. What is its volume in litres?

- $240\,\mathrm L$
- $2.4\,\mathrm L$
- $9\,\mathrm L$
- $24\,\mathrm L$

## Explanation

Express all edges in decimetres to obtain cubic decimetres. Each cubic decimetre corresponds to one litre.

$2\cdot 3\cdot 4=24\,\mathrm L$.
