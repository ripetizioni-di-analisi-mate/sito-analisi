A conveyor belt moves at $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. How far would it move in kilometres over one hour?

- $\dfrac{5}{6}\,\mathrm{km}$
- $10.8\,\mathrm{km}$
- $0.18\,\mathrm{km}$
- $10800\,\mathrm{km}$

## Explanation

One hour contains $3600$ seconds. Multiplying the distance travelled in each second by the duration gives metres, then converted to kilometres.

$\frac{3\cdot 3600}{1000}=10.8\,\mathrm{km}$.
