A conveyor belt moves at $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. How far would it move in kilometres over one hour?

- $\dfrac{5}{6}\,\mathrm{km}$
- $10.8\,\mathrm{km}$
- $0.18\,\mathrm{km}$
- $10800\,\mathrm{km}$

## Explanation

The belt covers $3\,\mathrm m$ in each second. An hour contains $3600$ seconds, so the displacement is $3\cdot3600=10800\,\mathrm m$. Divide that number by $1000$ to express it in kilometres, giving $10.8\,\mathrm{km}$. Here the duration is fixed, so the result is a distance.
