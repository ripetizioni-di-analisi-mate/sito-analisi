Two suppliers report $4.8\cdot10^5$ and $7.2\cdot10^4$ items. Which figure is larger?

- $4.8\cdot10^5$
- $7.2\cdot10^4$
- They are equal
- It depends on the unit

## Explanation

Writing the second number with exponent five gives $0.72\cdot10^5$. With the same exponent, compare $4.8$ and $0.72$ directly.
