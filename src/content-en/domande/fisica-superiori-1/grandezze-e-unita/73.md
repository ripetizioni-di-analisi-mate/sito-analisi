Two suppliers report $4.8\cdot10^5$ and $7.2\cdot10^4$ items. Which figure is larger?

- $4.8\cdot10^5$
- $7.2\cdot10^4$
- They are equal
- It depends on the unit

## Explanation

Use the same power of ten to compare the two counts. The second value becomes $0.72\cdot10^5$, while the first is $4.8\cdot10^5$. Now compare $4.8$ with $0.72$. The first distributor reports the larger quantity even though the coefficient $7.2$ in the second original entry looked larger.
