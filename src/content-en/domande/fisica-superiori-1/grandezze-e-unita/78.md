A $0.5392\,\mathrm m$ rail holds a row of $350$ tiles of width $1.50\,\mathrm{mm}$. Adjacent tiles are separated by a gap of $40\,\mathrm{\mu m}$, and the row begins flush with one end. Treat the stated dimensions as exact. How much rail remains free at the other end?

- $0.20\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.24\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $14.2\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $13.96\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

There are $349$ gaps between the $350$ tiles. In millimetres, they occupy $349\cdot0.040=13.96$, and the tiles occupy $525$. The free length is $539.2-(525+13.96)=0.24\,\mathrm{mm}$. The positive value confirms that the row fits. Counting $350$ gaps would also subtract a space at the end of the row.
