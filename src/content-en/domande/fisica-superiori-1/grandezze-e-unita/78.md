What power of ten results from cubing $10^{-2}$?

- $10^{-8}$
- $10^{-6}$
- $10^{-1}$
- $10^6$

## Explanation

Cubing means multiplying three copies of the number. Adding the exponents gives $-2-2-2=-6$.
