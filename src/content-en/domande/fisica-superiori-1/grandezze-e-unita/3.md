A sheet of card has area $50\,\mathrm{{cm}}^2$. What is the area in square metres?

- $0.5\,\mathrm m^2$
- $0.05\,\mathrm m^2$
- $0.005\,\mathrm m^2$
- $500000\,\mathrm m^2$

## Explanation

A square centimetre is the area of a square with side $10^{-2}\,\mathrm m$. The conversion factor occurs twice in the product of the sides, giving $10^{-4}$. That factor applies to the entire area measurement. Expressing it in square metres tells how many unit squares with a one-metre side would have the same area as the card.

Since $1\,\mathrm{cm}^2=10^{-4}\,\mathrm m^2$, the result is $A=0.005\,\mathrm m^2$.
