A sheet of metal has mass $m$ and area $A$. Which expression gives mass per unit area?

- $\dfrac{m}{A}$
- $mA$
- $\dfrac{A}{m}$
- $\dfrac{m}{A^2}$

## Explanation

To find the mass per unit area, distribute the total mass $m$ over the area $A$. The requested ratio is $\dfrac{m}{A}$, in $\mathrm{kg/m^2}$ when using SI units. Its reciprocal, $\dfrac{A}{m}$, would answer a different question: how much area corresponds to each kilogram.
