A sheet of metal has mass $m$ and area $A$. Which expression gives mass per unit area?

- $\dfrac{m}{A}$
- $mA$
- $\dfrac{A}{m}$
- $\dfrac{m}{A^2}$

## Explanation

Mass per unit area requires dividing mass by area. In SI this quantity is expressed in $\mathrm{kg/m^2}$.
