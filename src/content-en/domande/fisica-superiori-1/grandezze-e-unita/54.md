A full tank has volume $0.15\,\mathrm m^3$. How many $25\,\mathrm L$ cans can it fill completely?

- $3750$
- $6$
- $0.006$
- $0.6$

## Explanation

The tank holds $0.15\cdot1000=150\,\mathrm L$. One full container uses $25$, so $\dfrac{150}{25}=6$. In general count only full containers, even when the quotient has a fractional part. Here $6\cdot25=150$, so all the water fits and nothing remains in the tank.
