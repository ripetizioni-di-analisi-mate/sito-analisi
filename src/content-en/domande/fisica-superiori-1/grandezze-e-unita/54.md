A full tank has volume $0.15\,\mathrm m^3$. How many $25\,\mathrm L$ cans can it fill completely?

- $3750$
- $6$
- $0.006$
- $0.6$

## Explanation

Convert the available volume to litres. The number of full cans is the total volume divided by the capacity of one can.

$\frac{0.15\cdot 1000}{25}=6$.
