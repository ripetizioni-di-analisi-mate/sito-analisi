A drawing is enlarged by multiplying all lengths by $3$. By what factor do its areas increase?

- $3$
- $9$
- $27$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

Area contains two linear dimensions. A factor of three applies to each length, so multiply two equal factors.

$3^{2}=9$.
