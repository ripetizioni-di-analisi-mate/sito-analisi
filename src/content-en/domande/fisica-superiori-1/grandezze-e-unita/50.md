A drawing is enlarged by multiplying all lengths by $3$. By what factor do its areas increase?

- $3$
- $9$
- $27$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

In a uniform enlargement every corresponding segment becomes three times as long. A square of side one therefore becomes a square of side three, which can hold nine original squares. The same factor applies to areas elsewhere in the drawing because the linear factor appears in both directions of the plane.
