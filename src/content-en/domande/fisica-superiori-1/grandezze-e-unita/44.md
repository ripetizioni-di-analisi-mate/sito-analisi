A mark on one sample spans $2500\,\mathrm{\mu m}$ and a second sample measures $2\,\mathrm{mm}$. By what fraction of the second measurement does the first exceed it?

- $0.2$
- $1.25$
- $0.5$
- $0.25$

## Explanation

The first measurement is $2.5\,\mathrm{mm}$. Its excess is $0.5\,\mathrm{mm}$, which is compared with the second measurement of $2\,\mathrm{mm}$.

$\frac{\frac{2500}{1000}-2}{2}=0.25$.
