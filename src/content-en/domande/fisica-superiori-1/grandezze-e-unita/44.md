The first measured length is $2500\,\mathrm{\mu m}$ and the second is $2\,\mathrm{mm}$. By what fraction of the second length is the first longer?

- $0.2$
- $1.25$
- $0.5$
- $0.25$

## Explanation

The first measurement of $2500\,\mathrm{\mu m}$ equals $2.5\,\mathrm{mm}$. Its excess over the second sample is $2.5-2=0.5\,\mathrm{mm}$. Compare that excess with the second measurement to obtain $\dfrac{0.5}{2}=0.25$. The denominator must be the reference sample because the fraction expresses the excess relative to it.
