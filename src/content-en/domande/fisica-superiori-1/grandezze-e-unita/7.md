An experiment lasts 8 hours and 45 minutes. How long does it last in seconds?

- $525\,\mathrm s$
- $28845\,\mathrm s$
- $31500\,\mathrm s$
- $845\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

An hour contains sixty minutes and every minute lasts sixty seconds. The first term therefore uses the factor $60^2=3600$ and the second uses $60$. Both terms count seconds, so their sum covers the whole duration. Converting the two contributions back separately recovers the units in the question.

The total duration is $t=8\cdot3600+45\cdot60=31500\,\mathrm s$.
