Which of these expressions is normalized scientific notation for $5200000$?

- $52\cdot10^6$
- $0.52\cdot10^6$
- $5.2\cdot10^6$
- $5.2\cdot10^{-6}$

## Explanation

Normalized form requires a coefficient whose absolute value is at least $1$ and below $10$. Moving the decimal point after the $5$ gives $5.2$, which must be multiplied by $10^6$ to recover $5200000$. The expression $52\cdot10^5$ also represents the number, but its coefficient is outside the normalized range.
