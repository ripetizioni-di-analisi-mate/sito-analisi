A meter stores only the mantissa $4.8$ and the integer exponent of a distance expressed in metres. The distance lies between $3$ and $7$ millimetres. Which exponent must be restored?

- $-2$
- $3$
- $-3$
- $-4$

## Explanation

One millimetre is $10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m}$. The exponent $-3$ gives $4.8\,\mathrm{mm}$, which lies within the interval. Increasing or decreasing the exponent by one would multiply or divide the distance by ten.
