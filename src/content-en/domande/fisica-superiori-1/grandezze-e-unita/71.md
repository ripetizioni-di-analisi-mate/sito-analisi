Which of these expressions is normalized scientific notation for $5200000$?

- $52\cdot10^5$
- $0.52\cdot10^7$
- $5.2\cdot10^6$
- $5.2\cdot10^{-6}$

## Explanation

Equivalent expressions may have different coefficients. Normalized notation requires a coefficient from one inclusive to ten exclusive.
