A calculation gives the result $0.0045\cdot10^{8}$. What is the same quantity in normalized scientific notation?

- $4.5\cdot10^{11}$
- $45\cdot10^{4}$
- $4.5\cdot10^{5}$
- $4.5\cdot10^{4}$

## Explanation

The coefficient $0.0045$ is less than $1$, so the expression is not normalized. Moving the decimal point three places to the right turns the coefficient into $4.5$, that is it multiplies it by $10^3$. To leave the number unchanged the power must be divided by $10^3$, so the exponent drops from $8$ to $5$:
$$
0.0045\cdot10^{8}=4.5\cdot10^{-3}\cdot10^{8}=4.5\cdot10^{5}.
$$
The expression $45\cdot10^4$ represents the same number, but its coefficient is greater than $10$. The value $4.5\cdot10^{11}$ comes from increasing the exponent instead of decreasing it.
