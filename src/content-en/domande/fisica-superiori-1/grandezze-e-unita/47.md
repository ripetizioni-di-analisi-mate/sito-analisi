A rectangle has sides of $40\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $1.5\,\mathrm m$. What is its area in square metres?

- $60\,\mathrm m^2$
- $\dfrac{4}{15}\,\mathrm m^2$
- $0.6\,\mathrm m^2$
- $1.9\,\mathrm m^2$

## Explanation

Convert the first side to metres and multiply by the second side. The product of the units gives square metres.

$\frac{40}{100}\cdot 1.5=0.6\,\mathrm m^2$.
