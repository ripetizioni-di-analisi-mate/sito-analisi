A rectangle has sides of $40\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $1.5\,\mathrm m$. What is its area in square metres?

- $60\,\mathrm m^2$
- $\dfrac{4}{15}\,\mathrm m^2$
- $0.6\,\mathrm m^2$
- $1.9\,\mathrm m^2$

## Explanation

The rectangle's area is its base multiplied by its height. Convert $40\,\mathrm{cm}$ to $0.40\,\mathrm m$ because the other length is already in metres. The product $(0.40\,\mathrm m)(1.5\,\mathrm m)$ gives $0.60\,\mathrm m^2$. Once the units match, the two metre factors make the square unit of area explicit.
