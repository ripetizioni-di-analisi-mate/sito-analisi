A cell is $25\,\mathrm{\mu m}$ wide. What is its width in millimetres?

- $0.25\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.025\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $25000\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.000025\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

One millimetre contains a thousand micrometres because the exponents for milli and micro differ by three. The width in millimetres is therefore a small fraction of a millimetre.

$\frac{25}{1000}=0.025\,\mathrm{mm}$.
