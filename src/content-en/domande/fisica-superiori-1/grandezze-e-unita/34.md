A cell is $25\,\mathrm{\mu m}$ wide. What is its width in millimetres?

- $0.25\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.025\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $25000\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.000025\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

A millimetre is $10^{-3}\,\mathrm m$ and a micrometre $10^{-6}\,\mathrm m$. Their ratio is therefore $10^3$ micrometres per millimetre. Divide the original reading by $1000$ to obtain $\dfrac{25}{1000}=0.025\,\mathrm{mm}$. A result below one millimetre is consistent with the original value of only $25$ micrometres.
