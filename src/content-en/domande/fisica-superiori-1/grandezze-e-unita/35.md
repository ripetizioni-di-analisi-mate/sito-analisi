How many pulses lasting $250\,\mathrm{\mu s}$ occupy one millisecond with no gaps between them?

- $0.25$
- $40$
- $4$
- $250000$

## Explanation

Express one millisecond as a thousand microseconds. The ratio of the total duration to the duration of one pulse gives the required count.

$\frac{1000}{250}=4$.
