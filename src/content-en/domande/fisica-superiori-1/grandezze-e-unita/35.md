How many pulses lasting $250\,\mathrm{\mu s}$ occupy one millisecond with no gaps between them?

- $0.25$
- $40$
- $4$
- $250000$

## Explanation

Because the pulses follow each other without gaps, each fills a span of $250\,\mathrm{\mu s}$. The available time equals $1000\,\mathrm{\mu s}$. The number of spans is $\dfrac{1000}{250}=4$. The time units cancel in the ratio, leaving a dimensionless count of four pulses.
