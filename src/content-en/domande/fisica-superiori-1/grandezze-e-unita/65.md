A test has four $0.8\,\mathrm s$ acquisitions and three $600\,\mathrm{ms}$ pauses. How long does the full sequence last?

- $5.0\,\mathrm s$
- $5.6\,\mathrm s$
- $3.2\,\mathrm s$
- $2.6\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The four acquisitions take $4\cdot0.8=3.2\,\mathrm s$. Since $600\,\mathrm{ms}=0.6\,\mathrm s$, the three pauses add $3\cdot0.6=1.8\,\mathrm s$. Their sum is $5.0\,\mathrm s$. A fourth pause would add another $0.6\,\mathrm s$ and bring the total to $5.6\,\mathrm s$.
