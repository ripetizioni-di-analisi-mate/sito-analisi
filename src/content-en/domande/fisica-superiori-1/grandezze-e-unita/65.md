A test consists of four $0.8\,\mathrm s$ recordings and three $120\,\mathrm{ms}$ gaps. How long is the whole sequence?

- $3.56\,\mathrm s$
- $3.68\,\mathrm s$
- $6.8\,\mathrm s$
- $1.16\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The recording occurs four times. Each of the three gaps is $0.120\,\mathrm s$, so every term of the sum is in seconds.

$4\cdot 0.8+\frac{3\cdot 120}{1000}=3.56\,\mathrm s$.
