A square sheet has area $0.16\,\mathrm m^2$. What is the length of each side in centimetres?

- $16\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $160\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $40\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The side measures $0.4\,\mathrm m$ because its square is $0.16\,\mathrm m^2$. Its centimetre value is a hundred times its metre value.

$40=40\,\mathrm{cm}$.
