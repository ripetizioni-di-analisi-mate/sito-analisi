A square sheet has area $0.16\,\mathrm m^2$. What is the length of each side in centimetres?

- $16\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $160\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $40\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

If $s$ is the positive side length, $s^2=0.16\,\mathrm m^2$. Take the positive square root to obtain $0.4\,\mathrm m$, because $0.4\cdot0.4=0.16$. There are one hundred centimetres in each metre, so the side measures $40\,\mathrm{cm}$. As a check, $(40\,\mathrm{cm})^2$ gives $1600\,\mathrm{cm}^2=0.16\,\mathrm m^2$.
