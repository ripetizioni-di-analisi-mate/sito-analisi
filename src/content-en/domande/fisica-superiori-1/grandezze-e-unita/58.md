The edge of a cube is halved. What fraction of the original volume does the smaller cube have?

- $0.5$
- $0.125$
- $0.25$
- $8$

## Explanation

Volume is the product of three equal lengths. Each length is multiplied by one half, so the factor occurs three times.

$\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{3}=0.125$.
