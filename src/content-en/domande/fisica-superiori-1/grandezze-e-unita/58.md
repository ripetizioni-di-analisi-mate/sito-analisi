The edge of a cube is halved. What fraction of the original volume does the smaller cube have?

- $0.5$
- $0.125$
- $0.25$
- $8$

## Explanation

The original cube has volume $s^3$. Substituting the new edge $s/2$ gives $\dfrac{s^3}{2^3}$. The factors $s^3$ cancel in the ratio to the original volume, leaving $\dfrac18=0.125$. The halving acts in three directions, so volume decreases by a greater factor than the side length.
