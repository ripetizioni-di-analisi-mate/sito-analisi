Pressure is force divided by area. A force of $300\,\mathrm N$ is distributed over $50\,\mathrm{cm}^2$. What is the ratio in $\mathrm{N/m^2}$?

- $6\,\mathrm{N/m^2}$
- $600\,\mathrm{N/m^2}$
- $60000\,\mathrm{N/m^2}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{N/m^2}$

## Explanation

The force is already expressed in newtons. The area it acts on is $50\cdot10^{-4}=0.0050\,\mathrm m^2$. Dividing the force by this area gives $\dfrac{300}{0.0050}=60000\,\mathrm{N/m^2}$. The high value is consistent with the small area: the same force spread across one square metre would give a lower pressure.
