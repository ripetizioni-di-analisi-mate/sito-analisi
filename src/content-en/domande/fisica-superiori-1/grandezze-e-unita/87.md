Pressure is force divided by area. A force of $300\,\mathrm N$ is distributed over $50\,\mathrm{cm}^2$. What is the ratio in $\mathrm{N/m^2}$?

- $6\,\mathrm{N/m^2}$
- $600\,\mathrm{N/m^2}$
- $60000\,\mathrm{N/m^2}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{N/m^2}$

## Explanation

The force is already given in newtons. Convert the denominator to square metres with the factor $10^{-4}$ and then divide.

$\frac{300}{50\cdot 10^{-4}}=60000\,\mathrm{N/m^2}$.
