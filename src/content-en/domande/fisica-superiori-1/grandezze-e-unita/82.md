The exponent of $a\cdot10^n$ is $-5$, and the number is multiplied by one thousand. What exponent does the new normalized form have if $a$ stays the same?

- $-8$
- $-2$
- $8$
- $-15$

## Explanation

A factor of one thousand is $10^3$. Multiplying the two powers of ten leaves the coefficient unchanged and adds three to the exponent.

$-5+3=-2$.
