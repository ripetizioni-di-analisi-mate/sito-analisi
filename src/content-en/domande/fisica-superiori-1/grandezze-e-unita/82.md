The exponent of $a\cdot10^n$ is $-5$, and the number is multiplied by one thousand. What exponent does the new normalized form have if $a$ stays the same?

- $-8$
- $-2$
- $8$
- $-15$

## Explanation

The new value is one thousand times the original one. One thousand is $10^3$, so $(a\cdot10^{-5})\cdot10^3=a\cdot10^{-2}$. The change is entirely in the power because $a$ stays at its original value. As a check, the exponent increases by three even though both powers here are factors below one.
