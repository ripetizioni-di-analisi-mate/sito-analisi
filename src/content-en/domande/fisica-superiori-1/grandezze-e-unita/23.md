Distance and time obey $d=k t^2$. What is the unit of $k$ if the data use SI units?

- $\mathrm{s^2/m}$
- $\mathrm{m\,s^2}$
- $\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Solving for $k$ gives distance divided by time squared. The denominator's unit is therefore squared.
