Distance and time obey $d=k t^2$. What is the unit of $k$ if the data use SI units?

- $\mathrm{s^2/m}$
- $\mathrm{m\,s^2}$
- $\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Distance is the product of $k$ and the square of the time. To isolate $k$, divide $d$ by $t^2$. In SI the numerator gives metres and the denominator seconds squared. Multiplying $\mathrm{m/s^2}$ by $\mathrm s^2$ checks that the formula indeed returns metres.
