A hollow cube holds exactly $27\,\mathrm L$ when full. How long is an inner edge in centimetres?

- $30\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $3\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $27\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $270\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Twenty-seven litres equal $27\,\mathrm{dm^3}$. The edge is $3\,\mathrm{dm}$ because $3^3=27$. Multiply by ten to express the edge in centimetres.

$30=30\,\mathrm{cm}$.
