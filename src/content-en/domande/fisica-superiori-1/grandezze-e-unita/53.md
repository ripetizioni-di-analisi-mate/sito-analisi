A hollow cube holds exactly $27\,\mathrm L$ when full. How long is an inner edge in centimetres?

- $30\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $3\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $27\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $270\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Capacity refers to the inside of the cube, whose volume is $27\,\mathrm L=27\,\mathrm{dm}^3$. To find the internal edge, seek the length whose cube gives that volume. Since $3^3=27$, the edge is $3\,\mathrm{dm}=30\,\mathrm{cm}$. The wall thickness does not enter this calculation.
