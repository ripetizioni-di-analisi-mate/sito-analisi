A product gives $14.4\cdot10^{-3}$. Which coefficient and exponent rewrite it in normalized form?

- $1.44$ and $-2$
- $1.44$ and $-4$
- $14.4$ and $-3$
- $1.44$ and $3$

## Explanation

Divide $14.4$ by ten to bring the coefficient into the required range. Recover the factor of ten by increasing the exponent from $-3$ to $-2$.
