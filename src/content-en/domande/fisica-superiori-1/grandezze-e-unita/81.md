Two displacements along an axis are $x_A=-0.42\cdot10^{-2}\,\mathrm m$ and $x_B=-4.3\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm m$. What is the difference $x_A-x_B$?

- $1\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm m$
- $-1\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm m$
- $8.5\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm m$
- $1\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

To compare signed displacements, express $x_A$ with the same power of ten as $x_B$. This gives $x_A=-4.2\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm m$. The difference between the coefficients is $-4.2-(-4.3)=0.1$. Thus the result is $0.1\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm m=1\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm m$. The sign is positive because $x_A>x_B$.
