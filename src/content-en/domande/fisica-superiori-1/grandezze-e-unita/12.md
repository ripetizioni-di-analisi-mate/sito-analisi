A liquid dose has volume $8\,\mathrm{{mL}}$. How many litres does it contain?

- $8000\,\mathrm L$
- $0.08\,\mathrm L$
- $0.000008\,\mathrm L$
- $0.008\,\mathrm L$

## Explanation

The prefix milli modifies the unit that follows, which here is the litre. One millilitre is one thousandth of a litre, so the number of millilitres is multiplied by $10^{-3}$. The new numerical value is less than one because the dose is only a fraction of a one-litre container's capacity.

The dose has volume $V=8\cdot10^{-3}=0.008\,\mathrm L$.
