The relation $Q=I t$ connects charge, current, and time. If $I$ is in amperes and $t$ in seconds, which combination gives the unit of $Q$?

- $\mathrm{A\,s}$
- $\mathrm{A/s}$
- $\mathrm{s/A}$
- $\mathrm{A\,s^2}$

## Explanation

The law multiplies current by duration. The charge unit is therefore formed by multiplying amperes and seconds.
