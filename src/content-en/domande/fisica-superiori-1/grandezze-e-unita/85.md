A heater transfers $0.9\,\mathrm{kJ}$ uniformly in $30\,\mathrm s$ to a plate of area $600\,\mathrm{cm}^2$. What is the average power transferred per square metre?

- $500\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $50\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $0.05\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $15000\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$

## Explanation

Average power is energy divided by elapsed time. With $0.9\,\mathrm{kJ}=900\,\mathrm J$, this gives $900\div30=30\,\mathrm W$, since $1\,\mathrm W=1\,\mathrm{J/s}$. The area is $600\cdot10^{-4}=0.06\,\mathrm m^2$. Dividing $30\,\mathrm W$ by the area gives $500\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$. As a check, $(500\,\mathrm{W/m^2})(0.06\,\mathrm m^2)=30\,\mathrm W$ gives back the total power.
