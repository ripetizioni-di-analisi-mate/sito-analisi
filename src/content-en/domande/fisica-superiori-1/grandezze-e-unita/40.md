A $4\,\mathrm m$ rope loses a $125\,\mathrm{cm}$ piece. What percentage of its original length remains?

- $31.25\,\%$
- $320\,\%$
- $220\,\%$
- $68.75\,\%$

## Explanation

Convert the original length to $400\,\mathrm{cm}$ and subtract the $125\,\mathrm{cm}$ cut off. This leaves $275\,\mathrm{cm}$. Compare the remainder with the whole cord, giving $\dfrac{275}{400}=0.6875=68.75\%$. The ratio $\dfrac{125}{400}$ would instead give the fraction of the cord removed.
