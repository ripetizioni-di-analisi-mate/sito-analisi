A $4\,\mathrm m$ rope loses a $125\,\mathrm{cm}$ piece. What percentage of its original length remains?

- $31.25\,\%$
- $320\,\%$
- $220\,\%$
- $68.75\,\%$

## Explanation

Find the remaining part by subtracting the cut centimetres from the original length in centimetres. Multiply the ratio to the original length by a hundred.

$\frac{400-125}{400}=0.6875=68.75\,\%$.
