A conveyor carries $4500\,\mathrm{g/min}$. What is the mass flow rate in kilograms per second?

- $270\,\mathrm{kg/s}$
- $75\,\mathrm{kg/s}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{kg/s}$
- $0.075\,\mathrm{kg/s}$

## Explanation

Divide the numerator in grams by one thousand. The same mass passes in sixty seconds, so divide again by sixty.

$\frac{\frac{4500}{1000}}{60}=0.075\,\mathrm{kg/s}$.
