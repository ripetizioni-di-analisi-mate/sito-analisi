A conveyor carries $4500\,\mathrm{g/min}$. What is the mass flow rate in kilograms per second?

- $270\,\mathrm{kg/s}$
- $75\,\mathrm{kg/s}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{kg/s}$
- $0.075\,\mathrm{kg/s}$

## Explanation

In one minute the belt carries $4500\,\mathrm g=4.5\,\mathrm{kg}$. Distributing this mass over $60$ seconds gives $\dfrac{4.5}{60}=0.075\,\mathrm{kg/s}$. The reverse check multiplies the flow by $60\,\mathrm s$ and recovers $4.5\,\mathrm{kg}$. Dividing by time gives the average mass carried in just one second.
