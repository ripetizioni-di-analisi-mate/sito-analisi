Cubes with $1\,\mathrm{cm}$ edges are assembled into a cube with a $1\,\mathrm{dm}$ edge. How many small cubes are needed?

- $100$
- $10$
- $10000$
- $1000$

## Explanation

Ten cubes fit along each direction. Multiply the three counts to obtain the total in three dimensions.

$10^{3}=1000$.
