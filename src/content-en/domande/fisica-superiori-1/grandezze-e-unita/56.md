A tank has a horizontal cross-sectional area of $250\,\mathrm{cm}^2$ up to $4\,\mathrm{cm}$ above the bottom and $500\,\mathrm{cm}^2$ above that level. How high do $2.5\,\mathrm L$ of water reach?

- $5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $10\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $3\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $7\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The first section holds $250\cdot4=1000\,\mathrm{cm}^3$. From $2.5\,\mathrm L=2500\,\mathrm{cm}^3$, this leaves $1500\,\mathrm{cm}^3$ for the upper section. Dividing by $500\,\mathrm{cm}^2$ gives $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ above the step in cross-section. Add the $4\,\mathrm{cm}$ already filled to measure the level from the bottom.
