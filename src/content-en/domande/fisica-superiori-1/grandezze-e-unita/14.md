A rectangle has base $9\,\mathrm m$. Which expression has the dimensions of an area?

- $(9\,\mathrm m)+(2\,\mathrm m)$
- $(9\,\mathrm m)(2\,\mathrm m)$
- $(9\,\mathrm m)\div(2\,\mathrm m)$
- $(9\,\mathrm m)(2\,\mathrm s)$

## Explanation

The area of a rectangle requires a base and a height, both lengths. Multiplying two factors measured in metres gives square metres. Adding two lengths gives metres, while taking their ratio cancels the length units. Multiplying a length by a time would give a quantity with dimensions different from area.

The product of the units is $\mathrm m\cdot\mathrm m=\mathrm m^2$.
