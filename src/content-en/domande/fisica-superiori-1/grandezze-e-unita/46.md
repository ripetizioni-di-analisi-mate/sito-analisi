A graph of volume in a beaker has the points $(0\,\mathrm{min};40\,\mathrm{mL})$ and $(3\,\mathrm{min};220\,\mathrm{mL})$, joined by a straight line. What is the flow rate in millilitres per second?

- $60\,\mathrm{mL/s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{mL/s}$
- $\dfrac{11}{9}\,\mathrm{mL/s}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{mL/s}$

## Explanation

The gradient measures the increase in volume, so subtract the initial reading. In $3\,\mathrm{min}=180\,\mathrm s$, $220-40=180\,\mathrm{mL}$ entered. The ratio of the two changes is $1\,\mathrm{mL/s}$. Dividing the final volume by time would count the $40\,\mathrm{mL}$ already present before the trial.
