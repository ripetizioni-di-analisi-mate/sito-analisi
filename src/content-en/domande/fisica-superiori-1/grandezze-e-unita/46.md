Plotting the same length in millimetres vertically and centimetres horizontally gives a straight line. What is its numerical slope?

- $0.1$
- $10$
- $100$
- $1000$

## Explanation

Each horizontal centimetre corresponds to ten vertical millimetres. The ratio of these changes is the numerical slope of the line.
