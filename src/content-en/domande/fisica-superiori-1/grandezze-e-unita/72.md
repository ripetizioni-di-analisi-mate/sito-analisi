What is $-3.2\cdot10^{-4}$ in ordinary decimal notation?

- $-0.0032$
- $0.00032$
- $-32000$
- $-0.00032$

## Explanation

The negative exponent divides by ten thousand, moving the decimal point left. The sign comes from the coefficient and remains negative.

$-3.2\cdot 10^{-4}=-0.00032$.
