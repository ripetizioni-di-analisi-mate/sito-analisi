What is $-3.2\cdot10^{-4}$ in ordinary decimal notation?

- $-0.0032$
- $0.00032$
- $-32000$
- $-0.00032$

## Explanation

The factor $10^{-4}$ means dividing the magnitude by ten thousand. The digits $3$ and $2$ move four places to the right relative to the fixed decimal point, or equivalently the point moves left relative to the digits. The product is negative because the coefficient is negative. Its ordinary decimal form is $-0.00032$.
