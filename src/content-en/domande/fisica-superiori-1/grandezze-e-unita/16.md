Which of the four units is an SI base unit?

- The newton
- The square metre
- The litre
- The kilogram

## Explanation

The kilogram is the SI base unit for mass. It belongs to the chosen set from which many other units are built. For example, a newton has the form $\mathrm{kg\,m/s^2}$ because a force is obtained by multiplying mass by acceleration. It is therefore a derived unit.
