Given that $F$ is a force and $d$ is a length, which expression can have the unit $\mathrm{N\,m}$?

- $F+d$
- $\dfrac{F}{d}$
- $Fd$
- $\dfrac{d}{F}$

## Explanation

The symbol $\mathrm{N\,m}$ combines a force factor and a length factor by multiplication. The quantity $F$ provides the factor in newtons and $d$ the factor in metres, so the required product is $Fd$. Dividing $F$ by $d$ would instead give force per metre.
