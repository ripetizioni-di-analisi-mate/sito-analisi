Given that $F$ is a force and $d$ is a length, which expression can have the unit $\mathrm{N\,m}$?

- $F+d$
- $\dfrac{F}{d}$
- $Fd$
- $\dfrac{d}{F}$

## Explanation

A product of units comes from multiplying the two quantities. The newton comes from $F$ and the metre from $d$.
