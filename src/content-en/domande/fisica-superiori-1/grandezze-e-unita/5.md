A jug contains $4.5\,\mathrm L$. What is the volume in cubic metres?

- $0.0045\,\mathrm m^3$
- $0.045\,\mathrm m^3$
- $4500\,\mathrm m^3$
- $0.0000045\,\mathrm m^3$

## Explanation

A litre equals one cubic decimetre. A decimetre is one tenth of a metre, so the product of the three dimensions gives $(10^{-1})^3=10^{-3}$. Each litre is counted as one thousandth of a cubic metre. The result should be far below one cubic metre, which has a capacity of a thousand litres.

From $1\,\mathrm L=10^{-3}\,\mathrm m^3$ it follows that $V=0.0045\,\mathrm m^3$.
