A recording starts at 11:58:50 and ends at 12:01:05. How long is it in seconds?

- $135\,\mathrm s$
- $75\,\mathrm s$
- $175\,\mathrm s$
- $65\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

From 11:58:50 to 11:59:00, $10\,\mathrm s$ pass. Then one minute passes until 12:00:00. From noon to 12:01:05 there are another $65\,\mathrm s$. Adding the consecutive intervals gives $10+60+65=135\,\mathrm s$. Splitting the elapsed time at the hour boundary avoids treating minutes and seconds as decimal digits.
