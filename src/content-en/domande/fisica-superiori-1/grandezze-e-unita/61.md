A recording starts at 11:58:50 and ends at 12:01:05. How long is it in seconds?

- $135\,\mathrm s$
- $75\,\mathrm s$
- $175\,\mathrm s$
- $125\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

Ten seconds and a full minute elapse before noon. Another sixty-five seconds elapse after noon.

$60-50+60+60+5=135\,\mathrm s$.
