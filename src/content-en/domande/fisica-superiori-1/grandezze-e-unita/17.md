A $7\,\mathrm{kg}$ body is taken to a location with weaker gravity. Which quantity remains $7\,\mathrm{kg}$?

- Its mass
- Its weight
- Its volume expressed in newtons
- The gravitational field

## Explanation

The $7\,\mathrm{kg}$ measure the body's mass, and that mass stays the same when it moves to a different place. Weight, given by $P=mg$, is measured in newtons. The local value of $g$ changes and $m$ stays the same, so only the weight decreases.
