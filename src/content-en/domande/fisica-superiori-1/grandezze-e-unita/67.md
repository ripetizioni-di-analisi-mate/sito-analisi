In a test the maximum allowed reaction time is $0.25\,\mathrm s$. A contestant reacts in $280\,\mathrm{ms}$. By how much does this exceed the limit?

- $255\,\mathrm{ms}$
- $250\,\mathrm{ms}$
- $30\,\mathrm{ms}$
- $530\,\mathrm{ms}$

## Explanation

To compare the two times, they are expressed in the same unit. Since $1\,\mathrm s=1000\,\mathrm{ms}$, the limit is $0.25\cdot1000\,\mathrm{ms}=250\,\mathrm{ms}$. The reaction time is greater than the limit, so the excess is $280\,\mathrm{ms}-250\,\mathrm{ms}=30\,\mathrm{ms}$.
