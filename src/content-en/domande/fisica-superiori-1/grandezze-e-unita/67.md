The maximum permitted delay is $0.25\,\mathrm s$. A sensor responds $280\,\mathrm{ms}$ late. By how much does it exceed the limit?

- $255\,\mathrm{ms}$
- $250\,\mathrm{ms}$
- $30\,\mathrm{ms}$
- $530\,\mathrm{ms}$

## Explanation

Express the limit as $250\,\mathrm{ms}$. The difference from the actual delay gives the excess in milliseconds.

$280-0.25\cdot 1000=30\,\mathrm{ms}$.
