The maximum permitted delay is $0.25\,\mathrm s$. A sensor responds $280\,\mathrm{ms}$ late. By how much does it exceed the limit?

- $255\,\mathrm{ms}$
- $250\,\mathrm{ms}$
- $30\,\mathrm{ms}$
- $530\,\mathrm{ms}$

## Explanation

To compare the delay with the limit, use milliseconds, already used for the sensor. The limit of $0.25\,\mathrm s$ equals $250\,\mathrm{ms}$. The measurement is larger, so the excess is $280-250=30\,\mathrm{ms}$. The ratio of the two times would answer a relative question and would not give a delay in milliseconds.
