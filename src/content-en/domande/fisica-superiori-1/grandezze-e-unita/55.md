A rectangular tank has a base area of $0.50\,\mathrm m^2$. It receives $25\,\mathrm L$. How many centimetres deep is the water?

- $1.25\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $50\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.05\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The tank has a constant cross-section, so every extra centimetre of height adds the same volume. Write $V=Ah$ and convert $25\,\mathrm L$ to $0.025\,\mathrm m^3$. Dividing by $0.50\,\mathrm m^2$ gives $0.05\,\mathrm m=5\,\mathrm{cm}$. The ratio's units leave a single length dimension, as the question requires.
