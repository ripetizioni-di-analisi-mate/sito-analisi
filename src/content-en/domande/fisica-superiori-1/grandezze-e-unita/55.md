A rectangular tank has a base area of $0.50\,\mathrm m^2$. It receives $25\,\mathrm L$. How many centimetres deep is the water?

- $1.25\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $50\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.05\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Convert the volume to cubic metres and divide by the base area. The ratio gives a height in metres, then converted to centimetres.

$\frac{\frac{25}{1000}}{0.5}\cdot 100=5\,\mathrm{cm}$.
