What is the difference $1.04\cdot10^6-9.6\cdot10^5$?

- $-8560000$
- $-85.6$
- $80000$
- $2000000$

## Explanation

With a common factor of $10^6$, subtract $0.96$ from $1.04$. The difference is much smaller than the original numbers.

$1.04\cdot 10^{6}-9.6\cdot 10^{5}=80000$.
