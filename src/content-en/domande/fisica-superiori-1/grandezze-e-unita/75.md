What is the difference $1.04\cdot10^6-9.6\cdot10^5$?

- $-8560000$
- $-85.6$
- $80000$
- $2000000$

## Explanation

The difference is easier to compute with a common exponent. Write $9.6\cdot10^5=0.96\cdot10^6$. Subtracting the coefficients gives $1.04-0.96=0.08$, and hence a difference of $0.08\cdot10^6=80000$. It is positive and much smaller than the two original values, which have the same order of magnitude.
