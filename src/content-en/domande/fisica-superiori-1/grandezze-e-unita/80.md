The measurement $3200\,\mathrm{mm}$ is to be written in scientific notation in metres. What is the normalized form?

- $3.2\cdot10^3\,\mathrm m$
- $3.2\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm m$
- $32\cdot10^0\,\mathrm m$
- $3.2\cdot10^0\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The value in millimetres is $3.2\cdot10^3$. Each millimetre equals $10^{-3}\,\mathrm m$, so changing to metres adds $-3$ to the previous exponent. This gives $3.2\cdot10^0\,\mathrm m$. A direct check agrees: one thousand millimetres make a metre, so $3200\,\mathrm{mm}$ are a little over three metres.
