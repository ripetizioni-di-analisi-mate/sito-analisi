A plan has a scale of $1:100$. What real area in square metres corresponds to $6\,\mathrm{cm}^2$ on the plan?

- $6\,\mathrm m^2$
- $0.06\,\mathrm m^2$
- $60000\,\mathrm m^2$
- $0.0006\,\mathrm m^2$

## Explanation

The scale multiplies every length by one hundred. Area increases by $100^2$ in square centimetres and is then converted to square metres.

$6\cdot 100^{2}\cdot 10^{-4}=6\,\mathrm m^2$.
