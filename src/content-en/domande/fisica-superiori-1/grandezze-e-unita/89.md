A plan has a scale of $1:100$. What real area in square metres corresponds to $6\,\mathrm{cm}^2$ on the plan?

- $6\,\mathrm m^2$
- $0.06\,\mathrm m^2$
- $60000\,\mathrm m^2$
- $0.0006\,\mathrm m^2$

## Explanation

Every centimetre on the map corresponds to $100\,\mathrm{cm}=1\,\mathrm m$ in reality. A square of $1\,\mathrm{cm}^2$ therefore represents a square of $1\,\mathrm m^2$. The region of $6\,\mathrm{cm}^2$ corresponds to $6\,\mathrm m^2$. This uses the scale in two directions because area combines two lengths by multiplication.
