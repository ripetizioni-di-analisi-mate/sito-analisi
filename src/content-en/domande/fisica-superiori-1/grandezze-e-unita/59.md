A large cube is cut into eight identical cubes, with two along each edge. What happens to the total surface area after separating them?

- It is halved
- It stays the same
- It doubles
- It increases eightfold

## Explanation

Each smaller cube has one quarter of the original cube's area. Add eight quarters, including the newly exposed internal faces.
