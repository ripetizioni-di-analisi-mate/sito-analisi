A large cube is cut into eight identical cubes, with two along each edge. What happens to the total surface area after separating them?

- It is halved
- It stays the same
- It doubles
- It increases eightfold

## Explanation

The surface area of a small cube with half the edge length is a quarter of the original, because two lengths are halved in each face. Separating all eight small cubes allows their surface areas to be added, giving $8\cdot\dfrac14=2$ times the original surface area. Faces that previously touched are now exposed.
