Is a $600\,\mathrm{mm}^2$ plate larger or smaller than $4\,\mathrm{cm}^2$, and by how much?

- Smaller by $2\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- Larger by $56\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- The two areas are equal
- Larger by $2\,\mathrm{cm}^2$

## Explanation

Because a centimetre contains ten millimetres, a square of side one centimetre contains one hundred small squares of $1\,\mathrm{mm}^2$. The plate's area therefore equals $\dfrac{600}{100}=6\,\mathrm{cm}^2$. Subtracting $4\,\mathrm{cm}^2$ leaves $2\,\mathrm{cm}^2$. The plate has the larger area because the difference in that order is positive.
