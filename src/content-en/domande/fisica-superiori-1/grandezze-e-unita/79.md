A measurement is reported as $0.072\cdot10^6$. What exponent is obtained after normalization?

- $8$
- $5$
- $4$
- $3$

## Explanation

To normalize $0.072\cdot10^6$, multiply the coefficient by one hundred to change it from $0.072$ to $7.2$. To preserve the total value, compensate by dividing the power of ten by one hundred. The result is $7.2\cdot10^4$. Its exponent is therefore $6-2=4$ because the coefficient grew by two powers of ten.
