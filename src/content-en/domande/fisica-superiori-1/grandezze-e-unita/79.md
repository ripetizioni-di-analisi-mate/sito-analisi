A measurement is written as $0.072\cdot10^6$. What exponent is obtained after normalization?

- $8$
- $5$
- $4$
- $3$

## Explanation

Multiply the coefficient by a hundred to obtain $7.2$. Compensate by dividing the power of ten by a hundred.

$6-2=4$.
