A dispenser divides $2\,\mathrm L$ of liquid among $330\,\mathrm{mL}$ bottles. After filling six bottles, how much is left?

- $1670\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $20\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $1980\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $350\,\mathrm{mL}$

## Explanation

The initial volume is two thousand millilitres. Each full bottle removes $330\,\mathrm{mL}$, so subtract six equal contributions.

$2000-6\cdot 330=20\,\mathrm{mL}$.
