A dispenser divides $2\,\mathrm L$ of liquid among $330\,\mathrm{mL}$ bottles. After filling six bottles, how much is left?

- $1670\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $20\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $1980\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $350\,\mathrm{mL}$

## Explanation

The dispenser starts with $2\,\mathrm L=2000\,\mathrm{mL}$. The six bottles remove $6\cdot330=1980\,\mathrm{mL}$. The difference $2000-1980=20\,\mathrm{mL}$ is the liquid left. The remainder is positive, so there is enough for six bottles, and below $330\,\mathrm{mL}$, so a seventh bottle would remain partly empty.
