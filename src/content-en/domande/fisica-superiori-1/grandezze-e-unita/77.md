An area is the product of two equal sides of length $3\cdot10^{-2}\,\mathrm m$. What is the area in square metres?

- $0.0009\,\mathrm m^2$
- $0.0003\,\mathrm m^2$
- $0.09\,\mathrm m^2$
- $90000\,\mathrm m^2$

## Explanation

The area is the product of two equal lengths. Squaring $3\cdot10^{-2}$ makes the coefficient $3^2=9$ and the power $10^{-4}$ because two exponents equal to $-2$ are added. The two metre factors give $\mathrm m^2$. The result is $9\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm m^2=0.0009\,\mathrm m^2$.
