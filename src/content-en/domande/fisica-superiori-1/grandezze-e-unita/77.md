An area is the product of two equal sides of length $3\cdot10^{-2}\,\mathrm m$. What is the area in square metres?

- $0.0009\,\mathrm m^2$
- $0.0003\,\mathrm m^2$
- $0.09\,\mathrm m^2$
- $90000\,\mathrm m^2$

## Explanation

When squaring, square both the coefficient and the power of ten. The linear unit is squared as well.

$\left(3\cdot 10^{-2}\right)^{2}=0.0009\,\mathrm m^2$.
