What is the normalized form of $\dfrac{2\cdot10^7}{8\cdot10^2}$?

- $2.5\cdot10^5$
- $2.5\cdot10^6$
- $4\cdot10^5$
- $2.5\cdot10^4$

## Explanation

The ratio of the coefficients is $0.25$, and the exponents give $7-2=5$. Raising the coefficient to $2.5$ requires reducing the exponent by one.
