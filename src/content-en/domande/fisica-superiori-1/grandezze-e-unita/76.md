What is the normalized form of $\dfrac{2\cdot10^7}{8\cdot10^2}$?

- $2.5\cdot10^5$
- $2.5\cdot10^6$
- $4\cdot10^5$
- $2.5\cdot10^4$

## Explanation

Divide the coefficients and subtract the exponents because the common base is ten. This gives $\dfrac28\cdot10^{7-2}=0.25\cdot10^5$. The coefficient is below $1$. Multiplying it by ten and dividing the power by ten leaves the product unchanged and gives $2.5\cdot10^4$, with a coefficient in the required range.
