What does the number $12$ in $l=12\,\mathrm{cm}$ represent?

- How many metres make up the length
- The name of the quantity
- How many one-centimetre units make up the length
- The absolute uncertainty of the measurement

## Explanation

The number depends on the chosen unit. Here it counts one-centimetre units along the measured length.
