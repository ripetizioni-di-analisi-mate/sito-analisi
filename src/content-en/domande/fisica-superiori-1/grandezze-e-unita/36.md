A laboratory sorts samples of mass $500\,\mathrm{mg}$, $0.004\,\mathrm{kg}$, and $0.7\,\mathrm g$. Which is lightest?

- The $0.004\,\mathrm{kg}$ sample
- The $0.7\,\mathrm g$ sample
- They all have the same mass
- The $500\,\mathrm{mg}$ sample

## Explanation

Expressing all masses in grams gives $500\,\mathrm{mg}=0.500\,\mathrm g$ and $0.004\,\mathrm{kg}=4\,\mathrm g$. The third sample already has a stated mass of $0.7\,\mathrm g$. The first is the lightest because $0.500<0.7<4$. The numerical values reveal the order only after we choose a common unit.
