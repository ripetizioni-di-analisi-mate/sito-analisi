Which expression uses the SI kilogram symbol with the correct spacing?

- $5\mathrm{kg}$
- $5\,\mathrm{Kg}$
- $5\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $5\,\mathrm{kgs}$

## Explanation

The symbol $\mathrm{kg}$ uses lowercase letters and stays the same for plural values. A space separates it from the numerical value.
