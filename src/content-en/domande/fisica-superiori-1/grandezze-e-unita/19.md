The formula $d=d_0+bt+ct^2$ gives a distance in metres when $t$ is measured in seconds. Which units must $b$ and $c$ have, respectively?

- $\mathrm m,\ \mathrm m$
- $\mathrm{m/s^2},\ \mathrm{m/s}$
- $\mathrm{m/s}$ and $\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $\mathrm{s/m},\ \mathrm{s^2/m}$

## Explanation

Because the sum gives a distance, both $bt$ and $ct^2$ must be lengths. In the first product, the unit of $b$ must cancel one second. In the second, the unit of $c$ must cancel two seconds multiplied together. Both terms then have metres as their unit.
