The data value $7\,\mathrm{km}$ was transcribed as $7\,\mathrm{cm}$. By what factor must the recorded measurement be multiplied to restore the original length?

- $100000$
- $10$
- $100$
- $1000$

## Explanation

To recover the original length, compare the two measurements in the same unit. The first is $7\cdot10^3\,\mathrm m$, while the recorded one is $7\cdot10^{-2}\,\mathrm m$. The $7$ and the metres cancel in the ratio, leaving $10^5$. The factor exceeds one, as needed to turn the too-small entry into the original value.
