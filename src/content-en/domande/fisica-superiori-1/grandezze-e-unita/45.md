The data value $7\,\mathrm{km}$ was transcribed as $7\,\mathrm{cm}$. By what factor must the recorded measurement be multiplied to restore the original length?

- $100000$
- $10$
- $100$
- $1000$

## Explanation

The numerical values are the same. The ratio of the lengths is the ratio of the units: one thousand metres divided by one hundredth of a metre.

$\frac{10^{3}}{10^{-2}}=100000$.
