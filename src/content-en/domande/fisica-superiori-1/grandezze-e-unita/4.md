A sample occupies $65\,\mathrm{{cm}}^3$. What is its volume in cubic metres?

- $0.65\,\mathrm m^3$
- $0.065\,\mathrm m^3$
- $0.0065\,\mathrm m^3$
- $0.000065\,\mathrm m^3$

## Explanation

A cubic centimetre is the volume of a cube with side $10^{-2}\,\mathrm m$. Multiplying the three sides gives three factors of $10^{-2}$, yielding $10^{-6}$. The value in cubic metres therefore contains one millionth of a cubic metre for each cubic centimetre of the sample.

Therefore $V=65\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm m^3=0.000065\,\mathrm m^3$.
