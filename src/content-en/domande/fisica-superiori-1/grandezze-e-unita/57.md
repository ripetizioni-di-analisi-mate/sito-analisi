A rectangular film is folded along a line of symmetry. The two halves coincide on the plane, and the material keeps its original dimensions. By what factor does the area covered change?

- $0.5$
- $0.25$
- $2$
- $1$

## Explanation

The fold places one half of the sheet on top of the other. The halves cover the same part of the plane, so the covered region is only half the original area. The material's overall surface area stays the same because both layers are still present. The question concerns the footprint, whose factor is $\dfrac12=0.5$.
