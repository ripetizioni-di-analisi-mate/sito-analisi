A rectangular film is folded exactly in half. By what factor does the area covered on the plane change if the film is not stretched?

- $0.5$
- $0.25$
- $2$
- $1$

## Explanation

The fold halves one dimension of the covered region and leaves the other unchanged. The product of the dimensions is halved.

$\frac{1}{2}=0.5$.
