A volume $V$ is collected over a time $t$. Which unit can express the flow rate $V/t$?

- $\mathrm{s/m^3}$
- $\mathrm{m^3\,s}$
- $\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\mathrm{m^3/s}$

## Explanation

The flow rate describes the volume collected during one second. The volume is measured in cubic metres and the collection time in seconds, so the ratio has units of $\mathrm{m^3/s}$. Multiplying the constant rate by a duration in seconds then gives a volume in cubic metres again.
