A volume $V$ is collected over a time $t$. Which unit can express the flow rate $V/t$?

- $\mathrm{s/m^3}$
- $\mathrm{m^3\,s}$
- $\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\mathrm{m^3/s}$

## Explanation

The calculation is a ratio. Volume contributes cubic metres, and duration goes in the denominator in seconds.
