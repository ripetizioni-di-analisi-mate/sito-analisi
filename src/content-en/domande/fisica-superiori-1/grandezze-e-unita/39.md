A map represents $2\,\mathrm{km}$ with $4\,\mathrm{cm}$. How many real metres does one centimetre on the map represent?

- $8000\,\mathrm m$
- $0.5\,\mathrm m$
- $500\,\mathrm m$
- $50\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

First convert the real distance to metres. Dividing two thousand metres by the four centimetres on the map gives the real distance per centimetre.

$\frac{2\cdot 1000}{4}=500\,\mathrm m$.
