A map represents $2\,\mathrm{km}$ with $4\,\mathrm{cm}$. How many real metres does one centimetre on the map represent?

- $8000\,\mathrm m$
- $0.5\,\mathrm m$
- $500\,\mathrm m$
- $50\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The $4\,\mathrm{cm}$ on the map represent $2\,\mathrm{km}=2000\,\mathrm m$ in the real world. The map scale assigns the same real length to every centimetre. Dividing $2000$ by $4$ gives $500\,\mathrm m$ on the ground per centimetre on the map. The ratio links measurements in two different spaces.
