What is the number $0.00009$ in normalized scientific notation?

- $9\cdot10^{-5}$
- $9\cdot10^{5}$
- $9\cdot10^{-4}$
- $9\cdot10^{-6}$

## Explanation

The normalized coefficient must be at least $1$ and less than $10$. Move the decimal point five places to obtain a coefficient between $1$ and $10$. The change multiplies the number by $10^5$, so it is compensated by the factor $10^{-5}$. Their product gives the original value, which is less than one.

Moving the decimal point five places to the right produces $9$, so the exponent is $-5$.
