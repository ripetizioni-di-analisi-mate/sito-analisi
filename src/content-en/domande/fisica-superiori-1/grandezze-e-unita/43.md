On scale A, twelve divisions cover $3\,\mathrm{cm}$. A measurement spans eight divisions. What is the length in millimetres?

- $2\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $32\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $20\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $45\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

First find the length of one division by dividing the total length by twelve. Then multiply by the number of divisions spanned by the measurement.

$\frac{3\cdot 10}{12}\cdot 8=20\,\mathrm{mm}$.
