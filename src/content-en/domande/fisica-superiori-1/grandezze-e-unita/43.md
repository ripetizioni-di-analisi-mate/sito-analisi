On scale A, twelve divisions cover $3\,\mathrm{cm}$. A measurement spans eight divisions. What is the length in millimetres?

- $2\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $32\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $20\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $45\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The full scale segment measures $3\,\mathrm{cm}=30\,\mathrm{mm}$. Each division covers $\dfrac{30}{12}=2.5\,\mathrm{mm}$. Eight divisions therefore measure $8\cdot2.5=20\,\mathrm{mm}$. A second check is that eight divisions are two-thirds of twelve, so the reading is two-thirds of $30\,\mathrm{mm}$.
