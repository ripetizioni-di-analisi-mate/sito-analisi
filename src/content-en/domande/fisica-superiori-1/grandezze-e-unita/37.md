Does a $2.4\,\mathrm m$ pipe fit without protruding in a $235\,\mathrm{cm}$ case?

- No, it protrudes by $5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- Yes, there is $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ left
- No, it protrudes by $50\,\mathrm{cm}$
- Yes, the lengths are equal

## Explanation

The pipe measures $240\,\mathrm{cm}$. The positive difference $240-235$ gives the protruding part in centimetres.
