Two cyclists start at the same point and ride in the same direction for $3.5\,\mathrm{min}$. A maintains $18\,\mathrm{km/h}$ and B $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. How far apart are they at the end?

- B is $210\,\mathrm m$ ahead
- A is $210\,\mathrm m$ ahead
- B is $3.5\,\mathrm m$ ahead
- B is $1260\,\mathrm m$ ahead

## Explanation

Express both speeds in metres per second. For A the value is $\dfrac{18\cdot1000}{3600}=5$, so B gains $1\,\mathrm m$ every second. The $3.5\,\mathrm{min}$ last $210\,\mathrm s$, giving a gain of $210\,\mathrm m$. The product $6\cdot210$ would give the entire distance travelled by B.
