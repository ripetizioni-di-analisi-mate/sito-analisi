A square panel has a side of $9\,\mathrm m$. What is its area in square centimetres?

- $900\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $8100\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $810000\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $90000\,\mathrm{cm}^2$

## Explanation

The question asks for an area in square centimetres. First convert the side using a hundred centimetres per metre. Then multiply the converted length by itself, including its unit. This gives a factor of $100^2$ compared with the numerical value of the area in square metres.

The side measures $900\,\mathrm{cm}$, so $A=(900)^2=810000\,\mathrm{cm}^2$.
