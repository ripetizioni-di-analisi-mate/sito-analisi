What is the speed in metres per second of a cyclist travelling at $18\,\mathrm{km/h}$?

- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $64.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $300\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

In one hour the cyclist travels $18\,\mathrm{km}=18000\,\mathrm m$. The same hour contains $3600\,\mathrm s$, so the distance travelled in one second is $\dfrac{18000}{3600}=5$. The seconds factor stays in the denominator because speed expresses distance travelled per unit time.
