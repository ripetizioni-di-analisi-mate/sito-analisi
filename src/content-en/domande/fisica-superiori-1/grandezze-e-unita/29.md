What is the speed in metres per second of a cyclist travelling at $18\,\mathrm{km/h}$?

- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $64.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $300\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Convert kilometres into metres and hours into seconds. The duration appears in the denominator of the ratio.

$\frac{18\cdot 1000}{3600}=5\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
