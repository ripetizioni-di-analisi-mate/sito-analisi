Which notation distinguishes a millisecond from a metre multiplied by a second?

- $\mathrm{m\,s}$ for a millisecond, $\mathrm{ms}$ for the product
- $\mathrm{ms}$ for a millisecond, $\mathrm{m\,s}$ for the product
- $\mathrm{ms}$ for both
- $\mathrm{m/s}$ for both

## Explanation

In $\mathrm{ms}$ the first letter is the milli prefix applied to seconds, so the duration is one thousandth of a second. In $\mathrm{m\,s}$ the space separates two complete symbols. The first denotes metres and the second seconds. In this case the units are multiplied.
