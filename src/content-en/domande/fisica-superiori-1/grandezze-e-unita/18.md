Which notation distinguishes a millisecond from a metre multiplied by a second?

- $\mathrm{m\,s}$ for a millisecond, $\mathrm{ms}$ for the product
- $\mathrm{ms}$ for a millisecond, $\mathrm{m\,s}$ for the product
- $\mathrm{ms}$ for both
- $\mathrm{m/s}$ for both

## Explanation

The prefix and the unit are joined in milliseconds. In the product a space or dot separates the two unit symbols.
