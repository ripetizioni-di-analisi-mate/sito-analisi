An experimental reading is $0.97\,\mathrm m$, and the reference is $100\,\mathrm{cm}$. By how many millimetres is the reading below the reference?

- $3\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $-30\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $903\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $30\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The reference of $100\,\mathrm{cm}$ equals $1000\,\mathrm{mm}$, and the measurement of $0.97\,\mathrm m$ equals $970\,\mathrm{mm}$. Subtract the reading from the reference because the question asks by how much the reading falls short. The difference is $1000-970=30\,\mathrm{mm}$. This also corresponds to three centimetres on the reference scale.
