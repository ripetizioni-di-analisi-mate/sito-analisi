An experimental reading is $0.97\,\mathrm m$, and the reference is $100\,\mathrm{cm}$. By how many millimetres is the reading below the reference?

- $3\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $-30\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $903\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $30\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The difference must combine quantities stated in the same unit. The reference is one thousand millimetres and the reading is nine hundred and seventy millimetres.

$100\cdot 10-0.97\cdot 1000=30\,\mathrm{mm}$.
