An assembly needs three $40\,\mathrm{cm}$ tubes and one $850\,\mathrm{mm}$ tube. What total length should be bought in metres?

- $2.05\,\mathrm m$
- $1.25\,\mathrm m$
- $3.75\,\mathrm m$
- $9.7\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The first contribution contains three equal centimetre lengths. The final tube is counted once, in millimetres. Convert both contributions to metres.

$\frac{3\cdot 40}{100}+\frac{850}{1000}=2.05\,\mathrm m$.
