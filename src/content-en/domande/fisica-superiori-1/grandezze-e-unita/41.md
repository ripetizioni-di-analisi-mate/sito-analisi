An assembly needs three $40\,\mathrm{cm}$ tubes and one $850\,\mathrm{mm}$ tube. What total length should be bought in metres?

- $2.05\,\mathrm m$
- $1.25\,\mathrm m$
- $3.75\,\mathrm m$
- $9.7\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Each $40\,\mathrm{cm}$ pipe is $0.40\,\mathrm m$, so the three identical pipes contribute $1.20\,\mathrm m$. The $850\,\mathrm{mm}$ pipe contributes $0.850\,\mathrm m$ once. Adding the two contributions in the same unit gives $2.05\,\mathrm m$. The count of each type determines how much it contributes to the sum.
