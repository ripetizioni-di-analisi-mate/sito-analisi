The formulas $A=bh$ and $A=3bh$ both describe a quantity in $\mathrm m^2$. What does dimensional analysis establish?

- The second is correct for every rectangle
- Both have the dimensions of area
- The first has the dimensions of length
- The formulas always give the same number

## Explanation

The factor $3$ is dimensionless and leaves the unit of the product unchanged. Geometry determines which coefficient is correct.
