The formulas $A=bh$ and $A=3bh$ both describe a quantity in $\mathrm m^2$. What does dimensional analysis establish?

- The second is correct for every rectangle
- Both have the dimensions of area
- The first has the dimensions of length
- The formulas always give the same number

## Explanation

In both proposals the product $bh$ has the dimension of length squared. The coefficient $3$ is a pure number and leaves the unit unchanged. The dimensional check therefore allows both. For a rectangle, geometry determines that its area is the product of base and height taken once.
