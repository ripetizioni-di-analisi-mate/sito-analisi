A model proposes $t=\dfrac{d}{v}$, with $d$ in metres and $v$ in metres per second. Which unit results from the ratio?

- $\mathrm s$
- $\mathrm{s^{-1}}$
- $\mathrm{m^2/s}$
- $\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Distance contributes metres. Speed is distance travelled per second, so its reciprocal has units of $\mathrm{s/m}$. In the ratio $\dfrac{d}{v}$, multiply metres by $\mathrm{s/m}$ and cancel the two metre factors. Seconds remain, which is consistent with the duration the model is intended to provide.
