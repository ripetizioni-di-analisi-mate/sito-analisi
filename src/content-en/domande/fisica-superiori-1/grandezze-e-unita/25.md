A model proposes $t=\dfrac{d}{v}$, with $d$ in metres and $v$ in metres per second. Which unit results from the ratio?

- $\mathrm s$
- $\mathrm{s^{-1}}$
- $\mathrm{m^2/s}$
- $\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Dividing by metres per second is equivalent to multiplying by seconds per metre. The metres cancel, leaving seconds.
