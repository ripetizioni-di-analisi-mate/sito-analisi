The total length of a square's boundary is $80\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its area in square metres?

- $0.04\,\mathrm m^2$
- $0.64\,\mathrm m^2$
- $0.16\,\mathrm m^2$
- $0.2\,\mathrm m^2$

## Explanation

The perimeter is made of four equal sides. Divide the perimeter in metres by four and square the side length.

$\left(\frac{\frac{80}{100}}{4}\right)^{2}=0.04\,\mathrm m^2$.
