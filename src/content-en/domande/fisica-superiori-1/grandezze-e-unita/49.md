The total length of a square's boundary is $80\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its area in square metres?

- $0.04\,\mathrm m^2$
- $0.64\,\mathrm m^2$
- $0.16\,\mathrm m^2$
- $0.2\,\mathrm m^2$

## Explanation

The $80\,\mathrm{cm}$ measure the entire boundary, which has four equal sides. Each side is therefore $\dfrac{80}{4}=20\,\mathrm{cm}=0.20\,\mathrm m$. Square one side length to obtain an area of $(0.20)^2\,\mathrm m^2=0.04\,\mathrm m^2$. Squaring the perimeter would use a length four times larger than the required one.
