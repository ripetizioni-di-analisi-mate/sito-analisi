A quantity is defined as the ratio of a mass of $11\,\mathrm{kg}$ to a volume in $\mathrm m^3$. What is its SI unit?

- $\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $\mathrm{kg\,m^3}$
- $\mathrm{m^3/kg}$
- $\mathrm{kg/m^2}$

## Explanation

A derived unit follows the operation defining the quantity. The mass stays in the numerator, in kilograms, and the volume goes in the denominator, in cubic metres. The ratio gives a mass per unit volume. Changing the sample's mass would change the number while keeping the same unit.

Dividing mass by volume gives the unit $\dfrac{\mathrm{kg}}{\mathrm m^3}$.
