A test requires a cable at least $2.1\,\mathrm m$ long. Which of these cables is the shortest that meets the requirement?

- $174\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2110\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm m$
- $13.2\,\mathrm{dm}$

## Explanation

Express all lengths in metres to compare them. The $174\,\mathrm{cm}$ correspond to $1.74\,\mathrm m$, and the $13.2\,\mathrm{dm}$ correspond to $1.32\,\mathrm m$. Both are below the $2.1\,\mathrm m$ minimum. The eligible lengths are $2.110\,\mathrm m$ and $2.5\,\mathrm m$. The shorter is $2110\,\mathrm{mm}$.
