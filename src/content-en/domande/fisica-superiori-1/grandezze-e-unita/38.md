A test needs a cable at least $2.1\,\mathrm m$ long. Which of these cables is the shortest that meets the requirement?

- $208\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2110\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $2.2\,\mathrm m$
- $20.9\,\mathrm{dm}$

## Explanation

The lengths in metres are $2.110$, $2.08$, $2.2$, and $2.09$. Among those at least $2.1$, the smallest is $2.110$.
