Which factor multiplies a value in millimetres to obtain the corresponding value in centimetres?

- $0.1$
- $10$
- $0.01$
- $0.001$

## Explanation

One centimetre contains ten millimetres. To count complete or fractional groups of ten, divide the original value by ten.

$\frac{1}{10}=0.1$.
