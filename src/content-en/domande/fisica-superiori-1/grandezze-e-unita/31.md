A pump transfers $120\,\mathrm{L/min}$. How many millilitres per second does it transfer?

- $0.002\,\mathrm{mL/s}$
- $7200000\,\mathrm{mL/s}$
- $2000\,\mathrm{mL/s}$
- $120000\,\mathrm{mL/s}$

## Explanation

Replace each litre with one thousand millilitres. Replace each minute with sixty seconds, which remain in the denominator.

$\frac{120\cdot 1000}{60}=2000\,\mathrm{mL/s}$.
