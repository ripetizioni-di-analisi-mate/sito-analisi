A pump transfers $120\,\mathrm{L/min}$. How many millilitres per second does it transfer?

- $0.002\,\mathrm{mL/s}$
- $7200000\,\mathrm{mL/s}$
- $2000\,\mathrm{mL/s}$
- $120000\,\mathrm{mL/s}$

## Explanation

The $120\,\mathrm L$ collected in one minute equal $120000\,\mathrm{mL}$. The minute contains $60$ seconds, and each second contributes equally at a constant flow rate. The volume per second is therefore $\dfrac{120000}{60}=2000\,\mathrm{mL/s}$. The volume and time conversions act on different parts of the ratio.
