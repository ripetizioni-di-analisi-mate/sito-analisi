A measurement system must take $300$ readings, each lasting $0.4\,\mathrm s$. How many minutes do all readings take?

- $120\,\mathrm{min}$
- $2\,\mathrm{min}$
- $12.5\,\mathrm{min}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{min}$

## Explanation

Multiply the count by the duration of one reading to find the total duration. Then divide the seconds by sixty.

$\frac{300\cdot 0.4}{60}=2\,\mathrm{min}$.
