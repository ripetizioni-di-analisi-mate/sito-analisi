A measurement system must take $300$ readings, each lasting $0.4\,\mathrm s$. How many minutes do all readings take?

- $120\,\mathrm{min}$
- $2\,\mathrm{min}$
- $12.5\,\mathrm{min}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{min}$

## Explanation

Add $300$ equal durations, each of $0.4\,\mathrm s$. Since every reading occupies that same interval, the subtotal is $300\cdot0.4=120\,\mathrm s$. Dividing the number of seconds by $60$ gives $2\,\mathrm{min}$. The result uses the time required for every individual reading as specified in the question.
