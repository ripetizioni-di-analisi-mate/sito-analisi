A scale reads $700\,\mathrm g$. What is the mass in kilograms?

- $7\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.7\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $700000\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $70\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

A kilogram contains a thousand grams. Divide the value in grams by one thousand because each gram is a thousandth of a kilogram. The scale gives a mass reading. A weight force would be expressed in newtons and would also require the strength of the gravitational field.

Since $1000\,\mathrm g=1\,\mathrm{kg}$, the mass is $0.7\,\mathrm{kg}$.
