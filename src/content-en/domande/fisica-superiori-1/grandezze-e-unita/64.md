What fraction of an hour is a $900\,\mathrm s$ break?

- $15$
- $4$
- $0.9$
- $0.25$

## Explanation

To find the fraction, express both durations in the same unit. One hour contains $3600\,\mathrm s$.

$\frac{900}{3600}=0.25$.
