A recorder saves $9$ readings at equal intervals of $250\,\mathrm{ms}$. What is the elapsed time from the first reading to the last?

- $2.25\,\mathrm s$
- $0.25\,\mathrm s$
- $20\,\mathrm s$
- $2\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The first reading is the initial instant of the interval requested. The other $8$ readings each end one interval. One interval is a quarter of a second, so the duration is $8\cdot250\,\mathrm{ms}=2000\,\mathrm{ms}=2\,\mathrm s$. Multiplying by $9$ would include an interval before the first reading or after the last.
