A $3\,\mathrm m$ length must be cut into $47\,\mathrm{cm}$ pieces. What length remains after cutting the largest possible number of whole pieces?

- $65\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $18\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $29\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $253\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Six pieces fit in $300\,\mathrm{cm}$ because seven would require $329\,\mathrm{cm}$. Subtract the length of the six whole pieces.

$300-6\cdot 47=18\,\mathrm{cm}$.
