A $3\,\mathrm m$ length must be cut into $47\,\mathrm{cm}$ pieces. What length remains after cutting the largest possible number of whole pieces?

- $65\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $18\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $29\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $253\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The original piece measures $300\,\mathrm{cm}$. Six pieces of $47\,\mathrm{cm}$ require $282\,\mathrm{cm}$, whereas seven would require $329\,\mathrm{cm}$. Six is therefore the maximum number of complete pieces. Subtract the $282\,\mathrm{cm}$ already assigned, leaving $18\,\mathrm{cm}$, which is indeed shorter than another piece.
