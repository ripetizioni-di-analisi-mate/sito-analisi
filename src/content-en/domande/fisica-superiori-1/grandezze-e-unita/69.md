Five identical oscillations take $1\,\mathrm{min}$ and $15\,\mathrm s$. What is the period of one oscillation?

- $15\,\mathrm s$
- $75\,\mathrm s$
- $27\,\mathrm s$
- $23\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The total duration contains five periods. In seconds it is $60+15$, so take one fifth of that duration.

$\frac{60+15}{5}=15\,\mathrm s$.
