Five identical oscillations take $1\,\mathrm{min}$ and $15\,\mathrm s$. What is the period of one oscillation?

- $15\,\mathrm s$
- $75\,\mathrm s$
- $27\,\mathrm s$
- $23\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The total duration is $60+15=75\,\mathrm s$. The five oscillations are equal, so each occupies one fifth of this time. The period is $\dfrac{75}{5}=15\,\mathrm s$. Multiplying the period by five recovers the original duration. The period measures a single cycle, whereas the reading covers five cycles.
