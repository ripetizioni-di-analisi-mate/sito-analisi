What area in square metres corresponds to $0.03\,\mathrm{km}^2$ of land?

- $30\,\mathrm m^2$
- $0.00000003\,\mathrm m^2$
- $30000\,\mathrm m^2$
- $300\,\mathrm m^2$

## Explanation

One square kilometre is a square with a side of $1000\,\mathrm m$. Its area is $(1000)^2\,\mathrm m^2=10^6\,\mathrm m^2$. Taking $0.03$ of that area gives $30000\,\mathrm m^2$. The multiplier is one million because converting kilometres affects both sides of the reference square.
