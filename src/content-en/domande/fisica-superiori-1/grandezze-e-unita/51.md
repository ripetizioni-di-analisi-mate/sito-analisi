What area in square metres corresponds to $0.03\,\mathrm{km}^2$ of land?

- $30\,\mathrm m^2$
- $0.00000003\,\mathrm m^2$
- $30000\,\mathrm m^2$
- $300\,\mathrm m^2$

## Explanation

Each side of a reference square changes by a factor of one thousand. For an area unit, square the factor.

$0.03\cdot 1000^{2}=30000\,\mathrm m^2$.
