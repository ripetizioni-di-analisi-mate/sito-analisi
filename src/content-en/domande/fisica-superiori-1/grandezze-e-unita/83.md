A distance measures $0.00080\,\mathrm m$. Which normalized form preserves both significant figures in the reading?

- $8\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm m$
- $8.0\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm m$
- $8.0\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm m$
- $8.00\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The zeroes before eight locate the decimal point. The zero after eight is part of the precision expressed in the reading and is retained in the coefficient.
