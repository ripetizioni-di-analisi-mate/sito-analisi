A distance is reported as $0.00080\,\mathrm m$. How many significant figures should the coefficient have in normalized scientific notation for the same measurement?

- $1$
- $3$
- $2$
- $5$

## Explanation

Moving the decimal point four places to the right gives the coefficient $8.0$, so the measurement is $8.0\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm m$. The leading zeros locate the decimal point. The zero after the eight is a measured digit, so there are two significant figures. Writing only $8$ would omit that information.
