A source emits 18 sounds in $3\,\mathrm s$. At a constant frequency, how many sounds does it emit per second?

- $54\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $6\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $18\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

Frequency is a count divided by the duration in seconds. The unit $\mathrm{Hz}$ denotes one event per second.

$\frac{18}{3}=6\,\mathrm{Hz}$.
