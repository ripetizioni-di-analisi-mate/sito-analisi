A source emits 18 sounds in $3\,\mathrm s$. At a constant frequency, how many sounds does it emit per second?

- $54\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $6\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $18\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

Frequency counts events per unit time. Since $18$ sounds are observed in $3\,\mathrm s$, the average count in one second is $\dfrac{18}{3}=6$. The stated constant frequency lets us interpret this as the rate maintained during the trial. One event per second corresponds to $1\,\mathrm{Hz}$.
