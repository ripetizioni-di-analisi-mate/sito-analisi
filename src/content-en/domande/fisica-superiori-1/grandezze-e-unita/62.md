An experiment lasts $2.40\,\mathrm h$, expressed in decimal hours. What is the duration in minutes?

- $160\,\mathrm{min}$
- $144\,\mathrm{min}$
- $240\,\mathrm{min}$
- $120.40\,\mathrm{min}$

## Explanation

Two full hours give $120\,\mathrm{min}$. The fraction of an hour is $0.40$, or $0.40\cdot60=24\,\mathrm{min}$. Their sum is $144\,\mathrm{min}$. The digits after the decimal point represent hundredths of an hour. Reading them as forty minutes would give a different duration because an hour contains sixty minutes.
