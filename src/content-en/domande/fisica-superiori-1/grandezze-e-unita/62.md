An experiment lasts $2.35\,\mathrm h$. What is the duration in minutes?

- $155\,\mathrm{min}$
- $141\,\mathrm{min}$
- $235\,\mathrm{min}$
- $120.35\,\mathrm{min}$

## Explanation

The decimal notation counts hours and fractions of an hour. Multiply the whole value by sixty minutes per hour.

$2.35\cdot 60=141\,\mathrm{min}$.
