$A$ is area, $l$ is length, and $t$ is time. Which expression has the dimensions of length?

- $Al$
- $\dfrac{A}{t}$
- $lt$
- $\dfrac{A}{l}$

## Explanation

Area contributes two factors of length. Dividing by one length cancels a factor, yielding a length.
