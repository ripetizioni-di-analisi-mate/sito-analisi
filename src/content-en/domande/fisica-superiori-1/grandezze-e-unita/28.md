$A$ is area, $l$ is length, and $t$ is time. Which expression has the dimensions of length?

- $Al$
- $\dfrac{A}{t}$
- $lt$
- $\dfrac{A}{l}$

## Explanation

Area $A$ contributes two length factors and length $l$ contributes one. In the quotient $\dfrac{A}{l}$, one of the two factors in $A$ cancels the factor in $l$, leaving metres. In a rectangle, dividing the area by the base does indeed give the height.
