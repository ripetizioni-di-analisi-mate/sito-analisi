The length of a coil is found by multiplying the number of turns by the length of one turn. What unit does the count of whole turns have?

- It is in metres
- It is in square metres
- It is in seconds
- It is dimensionless

## Explanation

A turn is an event to be counted, so the number of turns is dimensionless. For example, five turns of $2\,\mathrm m$ each give $5\cdot2=10\,\mathrm m$. The metre unit comes from the length of a single turn. The count says how many times this distance contributes to the total.
