The length of a coil is found by multiplying the number of turns by the length of one turn. What unit does the count of whole turns have?

- It is in metres
- It is in square metres
- It is in seconds
- It is dimensionless

## Explanation

The number of turns is a count. The unit of the product comes entirely from the length of one turn.
