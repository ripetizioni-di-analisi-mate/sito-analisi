A value in metres obtained from centimetres was multiplied by $10^{-2}$ a second time. What factor corrects the final result?

- $0.01$
- $10$
- $10000$
- $100$

## Explanation

The first conversion already produced metres. Cancel the second factor by multiplying by its reciprocal.

$\frac{1}{10^{-2}}=100$.
