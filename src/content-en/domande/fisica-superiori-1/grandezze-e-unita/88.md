A value in metres obtained from centimetres was multiplied by $10^{-2}$ a second time. What factor corrects the final result?

- $0.01$
- $10$
- $10000$
- $100$

## Explanation

The first conversion from centimetres to metres already introduced the required factor $10^{-2}$. The subsequent step applied the factor again to the value already in metres. To undo just that last multiplication, use its reciprocal $\dfrac{1}{10^{-2}}=100$. The product of the erroneous factor and its reciprocal equals one.
