A stopwatch reads $0.35\,\mathrm s$ at the start and $1200\,\mathrm{ms}$ at the end. What time interval did it measure?

- $1.2\,\mathrm s$
- $1.55\,\mathrm s$
- $11.65\,\mathrm s$
- $0.85\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

Elapsed time is the final reading minus the initial reading. Convert the final reading from milliseconds into seconds.

$\frac{1200}{1000}-0.35=0.85\,\mathrm s$.
