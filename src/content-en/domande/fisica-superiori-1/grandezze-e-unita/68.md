A stopwatch reads $0.35\,\mathrm s$ at the start and $1200\,\mathrm{ms}$ at the end. What time interval did it measure?

- $1.2\,\mathrm s$
- $1.55\,\mathrm s$
- $11.65\,\mathrm s$
- $0.85\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The final reading of $1200\,\mathrm{ms}$ equals $1.200\,\mathrm s$. The stopwatch already read $0.35\,\mathrm s$ at the start of the requested interval. Subtract that from the final reading to obtain $1.200-0.35=0.85\,\mathrm s$. Adding the resulting duration to the initial reading exactly recovers the stop reading.
