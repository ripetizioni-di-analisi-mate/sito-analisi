How long, in seconds, is a time interval of $30\,\mathrm{ms}$?

- $30000\,\mathrm s$
- $0.3\,\mathrm s$
- $0.00003\,\mathrm s$
- $0.03\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The symbol $\mathrm{ms}$ denotes the millisecond, that is, the second preceded by the prefix milli, which stands for $10^{-3}$. So one millisecond equals $10^{-3}\,\mathrm s$. Therefore the interval lasts $t=30\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm s=0.03\,\mathrm s$.
