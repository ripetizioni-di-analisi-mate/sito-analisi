A route is $6.3\cdot10^3\,\mathrm m$ long. Another $7\cdot10^2\,\mathrm m$ is added. What is its total length in metres?

- $13300\,\mathrm m$
- $7000\,\mathrm m$
- $1330000\,\mathrm m$
- $6370\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The second term is $0.7\cdot10^3\,\mathrm m$. The coefficients can be added once they share the same power of ten.

$6.3\cdot 10^{3}+7\cdot 10^{2}=7000\,\mathrm m$.
