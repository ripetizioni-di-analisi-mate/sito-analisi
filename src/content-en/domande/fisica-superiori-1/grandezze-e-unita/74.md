A route is $6.3\cdot10^3\,\mathrm m$ long. Another $7\cdot10^2\,\mathrm m$ is added. What is its total length in metres?

- $13300\,\mathrm m$
- $7000\,\mathrm m$
- $1330000\,\mathrm m$
- $5600\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The $7\cdot10^2\,\mathrm m$ extension equals $0.7\cdot10^3\,\mathrm m$. After factoring out the common $10^3$, add the coefficients $6.3+0.7=7.0$. The total is $7.0\cdot10^3\,\mathrm m=7000\,\mathrm m$. The same calculation in ordinary metres adds $700$ to the original $6300$.
