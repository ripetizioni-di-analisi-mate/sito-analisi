How many consecutive intervals of $50\,\mathrm{ms}$ are contained in one minute?

- $1.2$
- $1200$
- $20$
- $3000$

## Explanation

One minute lasts $60\,\mathrm s$ and one second contains $1000\,\mathrm{ms}$, so one minute lasts $60000\,\mathrm{ms}$. The number of intervals is the ratio of the total duration to the duration of one interval, both expressed in the same unit. Therefore there are $\dfrac{60000}{50}=1200$ intervals.
