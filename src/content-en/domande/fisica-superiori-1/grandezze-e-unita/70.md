A recorder stores a value every $50\,\mathrm{ms}$. If each interval ends with a recording, how many recordings occur in one minute?

- $1.2$
- $1200$
- $20$
- $3000$

## Explanation

One minute contains $60000\,\mathrm{ms}$. Divide that duration into intervals of $50\,\mathrm{ms}$ to get $\dfrac{60000}{50}=1200$ complete intervals. The conditions specify one recording at the end of each interval. Thus the number of recordings equals the quotient, and counting starts after the first interval without an additional reading at time zero.
