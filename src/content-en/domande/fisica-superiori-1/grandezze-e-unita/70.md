A recorder stores a value every $50\,\mathrm{ms}$. If each interval ends with a recording, how many recordings occur in one minute?

- $1.2$
- $1200$
- $20$
- $3000$

## Explanation

A minute is sixty thousand milliseconds. With one reading at the end of each interval, the count is the ratio of the two durations.

$\frac{60\cdot 1000}{50}=1200$.
