A and B have equal masses. A's volume is one third of B's volume. How many times B's density is A's density?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $1$
- $3$
- $9$

## Explanation

At equal mass, density is inversely proportional to volume. One third of the volume gives three times the density.
