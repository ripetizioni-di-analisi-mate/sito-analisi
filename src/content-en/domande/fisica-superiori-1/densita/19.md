A and B have equal masses. A's volume is one third of B's volume. How many times B's density is A's density?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $1$
- $3$
- $9$

## Explanation

The numerator is the same mass for both samples and cancels in the ratio of densities. This gives $\dfrac{\rho_A}{\rho_B}=\dfrac{V_B}{V_A}$. Since $V_B=3V_A$, A contains the same mass in the smaller volume and has three times the density of B.
