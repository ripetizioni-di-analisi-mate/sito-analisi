A $300\,\mathrm g$ block of density $5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$ loses $10\,\mathrm{cm}^3$ of material. What mass remains?

- $250\,\mathrm g$
- $290\,\mathrm g$
- $350\,\mathrm g$
- $50\,\mathrm g$

## Explanation

Density converts the removed volume into the mass of the cut piece. Subtract that mass from the initial value.

$300-5\cdot 10=250\,\mathrm g$.
