A $300\,\mathrm{g}$ block of density $5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$ loses $10\,\mathrm{cm}^3$ of material. What mass remains?

- $250\,\mathrm{g}$
- $290\,\mathrm{g}$
- $350\,\mathrm{g}$
- $50\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

The removed $10\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ have the density of the whole block. They contain five grams per cubic centimetre, or $50\,\mathrm{g}$ altogether. Starting from $300\,\mathrm{g}$ leaves $250\,\mathrm{g}$. As a check, the initial volume was $60\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ and the final volume is $50\,\mathrm{cm^3}$, still at five grams per cubic centimetre.
