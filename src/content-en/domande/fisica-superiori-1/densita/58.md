Equal masses of $60\,\mathrm g$ from materials at $2$ and $3\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$ are combined with additive volumes. What is the mean density?

- $2.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $2.4\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$

## Explanation

At equal masses the volumes differ. Find each part's volume from its density and divide total mass by the sum of volumes.

$\frac{60+60}{\frac{60}{2}+\frac{60}{3}}=2.4\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.
