An empty shell has mean density $2\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$ and wall density $5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. What fraction of the external volume is material?

- $2.5$
- $0.6$
- $0.4$
- $10$

## Explanation

Set the two expressions for mass equal. The volume ratio equals mean density divided by wall density.

$\frac{2}{5}=0.4$.
