An empty shell has mean density $2\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$ and wall density $5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. What fraction of the external volume is material?

- $2.5$
- $0.6$
- $0.4$
- $10$

## Explanation

The empty shell has the same mass in both density calculations. In the ratio $\dfrac{\rho_{\mathrm{media}}}{\rho_{\mathrm{parete}}}$, that mass cancels. The result is $\dfrac{V_m}{V_e}=\dfrac25=0.4$. The material therefore occupies $40\,\%$ of the external volume, leaving $60\,\%$ empty.
