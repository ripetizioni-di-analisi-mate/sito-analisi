An $8\,\mathrm{cm}^2$ region is cut out of a $0.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ thick sheet with density $6\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. What mass is removed?

- $48\,\mathrm g$
- $24\,\mathrm g$
- $4\,\mathrm g$
- $96\,\mathrm g$

## Explanation

The cutout runs through the thickness. Its area times thickness gives the volume to multiply by density.

$8\cdot 0.5\cdot 6=24\,\mathrm g$.
