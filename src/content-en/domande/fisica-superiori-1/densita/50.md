Two devices measure the same small cube. They agree on its mass. The second assigns twice the length to every edge. What density does the second device calculate relative to the first?

- One half
- One eighth
- Twice as much
- Eight times as much

## Explanation

The volume of the cube is the cube of its edge length. Doubling all three dimensions multiplies the estimated volume by eight. Dividing the same mass by that volume gives one eighth of the original density.
