A plate is $0.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ thick and has density $6\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. A region of area $8\,\mathrm{cm}^2$ is cut out. What mass is removed?

- $48\,\mathrm{g}$
- $24\,\mathrm{g}$
- $4\,\mathrm{g}$
- $96\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

Consider just the part that is cut out. Its volume is its $8\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ area times the common thickness of $0.5\,\mathrm{cm}$, giving $4\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. Each cubic centimetre has mass $6\,\mathrm{g}$. Multiplying gives $24\,\mathrm{g}$, the amount by which the sheet's mass decreases.
