A stone has density $4\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$ and mass $28\,\mathrm g$. What does a cylinder starting at $35\,\mathrm{mL}$ read after full submersion?

- $7\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $28\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $147\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $42\,\mathrm{mL}$

## Explanation

Mass divided by density gives the stone's volume. Add this to the initial reading in millilitres.

$35+\frac{28}{4}=42\,\mathrm{mL}$.
