A stone has density $4\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$ and mass $28\,\mathrm{g}$. The initial reading of the graduated cylinder is $35\,\mathrm{mL}$. What does it read after the stone is fully submerged?

- $7\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $28\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $147\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $42\,\mathrm{mL}$

## Explanation

The mass of $28\,\mathrm{g}$ amounts to four grams per cubic centimetre, so the volume is $\dfrac{28}{4}=7\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. For full submersion this is seven millilitres of displaced liquid. The scale starts at 35 millilitres, so the final reading is seven millilitres above 35.
