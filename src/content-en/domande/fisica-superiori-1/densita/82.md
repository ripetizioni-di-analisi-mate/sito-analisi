Two components occupy $25\%$ and $75\%$ of an additive volume. How much does mean density increase if the first material is replaced with a material $2\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$ denser?

- $1.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $2\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $8\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$

## Explanation

The first density has weight one quarter in the mean. The mean increases by only one quarter of the change.

$0.25\cdot 2=0.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.
