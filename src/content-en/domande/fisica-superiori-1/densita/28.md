Use a density of $1200\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ with a volume of $2.5\,\mathrm{L}$. What is the mass in kilograms?

- $3000\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $480\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $30\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $3\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The density is given per cubic metre, so convert the volume to $0.0025\,\mathrm{m^3}$ before multiplying. This gives $1200\cdot0.0025=3\,\mathrm{kg}$. A second way is to use $1.2\,\mathrm{kg/L}$. Multiplying this by $2.5\,\mathrm{L}$ gives the same mass.
