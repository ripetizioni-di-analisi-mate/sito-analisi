Use a density of $1200\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ with a volume of $2.5\,\mathrm L$. What is the mass in kilograms?

- $3000\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $480\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $30\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $3\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Convert litres to cubic metres to cancel the density's volume unit. Then multiply.

$\frac{1200\cdot 2.5}{1000}=3\,\mathrm{kg}$.
