A $42\,\mathrm{g}$ solid is submerged with an attached bubble. How does density calculated from its apparent volume compare with the solid's density?

- It is lower
- It is higher
- It is equal
- It is negative

## Explanation

The cylinder measures the liquid displaced by both the solid and the attached bubble. The mass used remains the solid's $42\,\mathrm{g}$. Dividing this mass by a volume that is too large underestimates the material's density. Removing the bubble eliminates the extra contribution to the volume.
