A $42\,\mathrm g$ solid is submerged with an attached bubble. How does density calculated from its apparent volume compare with the solid's density?

- It is lower
- It is higher
- It is equal
- It is negative

## Explanation

The bubble adds to the displaced volume, while the mass used is only the solid's. A larger denominator gives a smaller ratio.
