A homogeneous sphere is replaced by a sphere of the same material with twice the diameter. By what factor does mass change?

- $4$
- $2$
- $8$
- $0.5$

## Explanation

Doubling diameter doubles radius. Volume contains the radius cubed, and equal density gives the same factor for mass.

$2^{3}=8$.
