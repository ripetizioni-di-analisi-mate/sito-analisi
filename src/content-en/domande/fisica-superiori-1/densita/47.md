A sheet of area $100\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ has a $2\,\mathrm{mm}$ core with density $2.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. Each of its two faces has a $0.5\,\mathrm{mm}$ coating with density $4\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. Ignoring the edges, what is its total mass?

::grafico{assi=0 xmin=-1 xmax=12 ymin=-0.5 ymax=3.6 poligoni="0,0.5 9,0.5 9,2.5 0,2.5;0,0 9,0 9,0.5 0,0.5;0,2.5 9,2.5 9,3 0,3" etichette="9.8,0.25,0.5 mm;9.8,1.5,2 mm;9.8,2.75,0.5 mm" titolo="Cross-section of the three layers. Each coating is 0.5 mm thick."}

- $70\,\mathrm{g}$
- $130\,\mathrm{g}$
- $90\,\mathrm{g}$
- $900\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

Convert the thicknesses to centimetres before finding each layer's volume. The core occupies $100\cdot0.2=20\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ and has mass $50\,\mathrm{g}$. Each face adds $5\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ of coating, or $20\,\mathrm{g}$. There are two coatings, so add $40\,\mathrm{g}$ to the mass of the core.
