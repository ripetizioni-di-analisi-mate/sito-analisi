A mixture with additive volumes contains liquids at $1$ and $3\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. The first is three quarters of the volume. To which density is the mixture density closer?

- To $3\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- It is equally far from both
- To $1\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- It exceeds both

## Explanation

The mean density weights each value by its share of the volume. The first contributes three quarters and the second one quarter, so $\rho_{\mathrm{mix}}=1\cdot\dfrac34+3\cdot\dfrac14=1.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. This lies half a gram per cubic centimetre from $1$ and one and a half grams per cubic centimetre from $3$.
