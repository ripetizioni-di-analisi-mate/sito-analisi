A mixture with additive volumes contains liquids at $1$ and $3\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. The first is three quarters of the volume. To which density is the mixture density closer?

- To $3\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- It is equally far from both
- To $1\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- It exceeds both

## Explanation

The first liquid has three quarters of the weight in the average. The resulting density is $0.75\cdot1+0.25\cdot3=1.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.
