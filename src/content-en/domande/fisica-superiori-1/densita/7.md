A hollow object has mass $36\,\mathrm{g}$ and external volume $23\,\mathrm{cm}^3$. What is its mean density based on external volume?

- $\dfrac{23}{36}\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $828\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $\dfrac{36}{23}\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $2\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$

## Explanation

The question specifies external volume, which includes the cavity. Divide the total mass by that volume. The ratio describes mean mass per cubic centimetre of occupied space and may be considerably below the wall material's density. The latter would require the volume of the wall alone.

Thus $\rho_{media}=\dfrac{36}{23}=\dfrac{36}{23}\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.
