A mixture uses liquids at $1$ and $4\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$ with positive additive volumes. Which result is incompatible with that model?

- $1.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $3.8\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$

## Explanation

A weighted mean with positive weights lies between the two original densities. The value $4.5$ exceeds them both.
