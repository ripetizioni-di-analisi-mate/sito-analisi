A mixture uses liquids at $1$ and $4\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$ with positive additive volumes. Which result is incompatible with that model?

- $1.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $3.8\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$

## Explanation

With positive additive volumes, the two contributions form a weighted mean with positive weights that add to one. The result lies strictly between $1$ and $4\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. The value $4.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$ exceeds the upper limit, so it cannot follow from the stated data and model.
