A bar with unchanged material and cross section is bent into a ring without changing the material volume. What happens to the material's mass-to-volume ratio?

- It rises because the middle is empty
- It stays the same
- It falls because the middle is empty
- It is zero because the ring is closed

## Explanation

The requested ratio concerns the bar's material. The space enclosed in the centre does not alter the volume of the bar itself.
