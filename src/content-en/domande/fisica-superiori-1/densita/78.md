An irregular, nonporous solid dissolves in water. Its volume is to be measured with a graduated cylinder. Which procedure satisfies the assumptions of displacement measurement?

- Half-submerge it in water and double the rise in level.
- Fully submerge it in a liquid in which it stays intact, avoiding bubbles and spills.
- Use water and read the volume after the sample has dissolved.
- Read the liquid volume before inserting the sample and use that reading alone.

## Explanation

The difference between readings measures the volume of liquid displaced by the intact sample. If the sample dissolves, the final reading describes a solution and the displacement model no longer applies. A compatible liquid preserves the sample, so its submersion accounts for the change in volume.
