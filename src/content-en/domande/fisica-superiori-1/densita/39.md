A $70\,\mathrm g$ cup holds $50\,\mathrm{cm}^3$ of liquid. By how much does using the $120\,\mathrm g$ total instead of the liquid mass raise the calculated density?

- $2.4\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $\dfrac{5}{7}\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $1.4\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $\dfrac{12}{7}\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$

## Explanation

The volume is still the liquid's. The unremoved tare adds seventy grams in the numerator, altering the ratio by that mass per unit volume.

$\frac{70}{50}=1.4\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.
