A material of density $2.8\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$ is made into $14\,\mathrm g$ pieces. What volume do three pieces occupy?

- $5\,\mathrm{cm}^3$
- $15\,\mathrm{cm}^3$
- $117.6\,\mathrm{cm}^3$
- $0.6\,\mathrm{cm}^3$

## Explanation

The total mass includes three pieces. Dividing by density gives their total volume in cubic centimetres.

$\frac{3\cdot 14}{2.8}=15\,\mathrm{cm}^3$.
