Each piece has a mass of $14\,\mathrm{g}$ and is made of a material with density $2.8\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. What volume do three pieces occupy?

- $5\,\mathrm{cm}^3$
- $15\,\mathrm{cm}^3$
- $117.6\,\mathrm{cm}^3$
- $0.6\,\mathrm{cm}^3$

## Explanation

Three pieces of $14\,\mathrm{g}$ require a total mass of $42\,\mathrm{g}$. To convert this mass to a volume, divide by the mass of one cubic centimetre, which is $2.8\,\mathrm{g}$. The quotient is $15\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. This is equivalent to finding $5\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ for one piece and adding the volumes of all three pieces.
