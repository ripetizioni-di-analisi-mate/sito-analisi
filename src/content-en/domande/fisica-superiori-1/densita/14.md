An empty container has mass $38\,\mathrm g$. With $18\,\mathrm{cm}^3$ of liquid inside, its total mass is $74\,\mathrm g$. What is the liquid's density?

- $\dfrac{37}{9}\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $2\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $\dfrac{19}{9}\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$

## Explanation

The scale reads the container and liquid together. The empty container already contributes part of the total, called the tare, which must be subtracted before finding the ratio. The stated volume belongs to the liquid alone, so use the liquid mass alone. Adding the tare to the resulting mass checks the procedure.

The liquid mass is $74-38=36\,\mathrm g$, so $\rho=\dfrac{36}{18}=2\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.
