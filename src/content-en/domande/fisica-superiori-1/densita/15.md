A density is $4.2\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. What does the number $4.2$ express in these units?

- The volume in cubic centimetres of one gram
- The total mass of any sample
- The mass in grams corresponding to one cubic centimetre
- The weight in newtons per cubic centimetre

## Explanation

The denominator of the unit gives the reference volume. A $1\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ portion of the homogeneous material therefore has mass $4.2\,\mathrm{g}$. Two cubic centimetres would have mass $8.4\,\mathrm{g}$, since there are twice as many units of volume. The density would stay the same.
