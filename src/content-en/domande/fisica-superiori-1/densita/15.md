A density is $4.2\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. What does the number $4.2$ express in these units?

- The volume in cubic centimetres of one gram
- The total mass of any sample
- The mass in grams corresponding to one cubic centimetre
- The weight in newtons per cubic centimetre

## Explanation

The cubic centimetre is in the denominator. The ratio gives the mass corresponding to a unit of volume.
