What additional information is needed to determine the mean density of a $0.3\,\mathrm{kg}$ sample?

- The volume of the same sample
- Only its colour
- The mass of a different container
- The length of a container unrelated to the sample

## Explanation

The ratio uses two properties of the same sample. The known mass must be divided by its volume.
