A sample keeps the same mass. Its initial volume is between $40$ and $44\,\mathrm{cm^3}$, and its final volume is between $24$ and $32\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. If the endpoints can be combined freely, what is the smallest possible value of $\dfrac{\rho_f}{\rho_i}$?

- $\dfrac{42}{28}$
- $\dfrac{44}{24}$
- $\dfrac{32}{40}$
- $\dfrac{40}{32}$

## Explanation

The same mass cancels in the density ratio, so $\dfrac{\rho_f}{\rho_i}=\dfrac{V_i}{V_f}$. A positive ratio is minimised by using the smallest numerator and the largest denominator. These endpoints are $40$ and $32\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. The minimum ratio exceeds one, so the density must increase.
