The mass-to-volume ratio is $4\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. How much mass does an extra $2.5\,\mathrm{cm}^3$ of the same material add?

- $1.6\,\mathrm g$
- $6.5\,\mathrm g$
- $0.625\,\mathrm g$
- $10\,\mathrm g$

## Explanation

At fixed density, any extra volume contributes the density times that volume. The cubic units cancel.

$4\cdot 2.5=10\,\mathrm g$.
