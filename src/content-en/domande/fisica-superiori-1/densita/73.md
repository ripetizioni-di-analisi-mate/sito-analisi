Two total-mass lines for different liquids are parallel but have different intercepts. What does this indicate about the model?

- Equal densities and different tares
- Different densities and equal tares
- Equal masses for every volume
- Always equal volumes even with different masses

## Explanation

Parallel lines have the same slope. The intercept is the reading at zero volume, so it distinguishes the empty containers.
