Two total-mass lines for different liquids are parallel but have different intercepts. What does this indicate about the model?

- Equal densities and different tares
- Different densities and equal tares
- Equal masses for every volume
- Always equal volumes even with different masses

## Explanation

Parallel lines have the same slope. For each extra cubic centimetre, both scales record the same mass increase, so the densities match in the model. At zero volume, the two readings are different. Those readings are for the empty containers, so their tare masses differ.
