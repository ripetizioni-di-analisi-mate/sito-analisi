A cylinder goes from $42$ to $55\,\mathrm{mL}$ with a body and hook immersed. The hook alone displaces $3\,\mathrm{mL}$. What is the body's volume?

- $13\,\mathrm{cm}^3$
- $52\,\mathrm{cm}^3$
- $10\,\mathrm{cm}^3$
- $16\,\mathrm{cm}^3$

## Explanation

The increase has two contributions, the body and the hook. Subtract the initial reading and the hook's own displacement.

$55-42-3=10\,\mathrm{cm}^3$.
