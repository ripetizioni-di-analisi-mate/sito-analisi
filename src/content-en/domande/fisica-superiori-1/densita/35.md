A cylinder goes from $42$ to $55\,\mathrm{mL}$ with a body and hook immersed. The hook alone displaces $3\,\mathrm{mL}$. What is the body's volume?

- $13\,\mathrm{cm}^3$
- $52\,\mathrm{cm}^3$
- $10\,\mathrm{cm}^3$
- $16\,\mathrm{cm}^3$

## Explanation

The reading rises by $55-42=13\,\mathrm{mL}$. This increase includes both the object and the hook. Since the hook alone displaces $3\,\mathrm{mL}$, the object accounts for $13-3=10\,\mathrm{mL}$. A millilitre equals a cubic centimetre. The correction assumes the same part of the hook is submerged in both trials.
