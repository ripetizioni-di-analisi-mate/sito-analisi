A homogeneous sample has mass $270\,\mathrm g$ and volume $90\,\mathrm{cm}^3$. What is its density?

- $3\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $24300\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $180\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$

## Explanation

Mass measures the amount of matter in the sample and volume gives the space occupied. Divide the first value by the second, because density is the mass per cubic centimetre. As a check, multiply the resulting density by the volume to recover the original mass.

The calculation gives $\rho=\dfrac{270}{90}=3\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.
