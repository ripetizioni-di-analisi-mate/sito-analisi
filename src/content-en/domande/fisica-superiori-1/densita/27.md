A $0.3\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass occupies $75\,\mathrm{mL}$. What is its density in $\mathrm{g/cm^3}$?

- $0.004\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $22500\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $4\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$

## Explanation

A millilitre is one cubic centimetre, so the volume becomes $75\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. The mass of $0.3\,\mathrm{kg}$ is $300\,\mathrm{g}$. Dividing $300$ by $75$ gives four grams per cubic centimetre. The product $4\cdot75=300$ also checks that mass and volume were converted to the required units.
