A $0.3\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass occupies $75\,\mathrm{mL}$. What is its density in $\mathrm{g/cm^3}$?

- $0.004\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $22500\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $4\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$

## Explanation

Convert the mass to three hundred grams and the volume to seventy-five cubic centimetres. Their ratio gives the requested density.

$\frac{0.3\cdot 1000}{75}=4\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.
