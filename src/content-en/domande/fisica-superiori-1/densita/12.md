A homogeneous cube has side $8\,\mathrm{cm}$ and density $2\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. What is its mass?

- $16\,\mathrm{g}$
- $128\,\mathrm{g}$
- $512\,\mathrm{g}$
- $1024\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

For a cube multiply three copies of the edge length. Applying the same operation to the unit gives cubic centimetres. Density is the mass of each cubic centimetre, so its product with volume gives the total mass. Doubling the edge would multiply the mass by eight.

It follows that $m=\rho l^3=2\cdot8^3=1024\,\mathrm{g}$.
