An object has external volume $25\,\mathrm{cm}^3$ and contains $20\,\mathrm{cm}^3$ of material. What percentage of the external volume is cavity?

- $80\,\%$
- $125\,\%$
- $5\,\%$
- $20\,\%$

## Explanation

The cavity is the difference between outside and material volumes. Divide by the full external volume to obtain the percentage.

$\frac{25-20}{25}=0.2=20\,\%$.
