An object has external volume $25\,\mathrm{cm}^3$ and contains $20\,\mathrm{cm}^3$ of material. What percentage of the external volume is cavity?

- $80\,\%$
- $125\,\%$
- $5\,\%$
- $20\,\%$

## Explanation

The cavity is the part of the external volume not occupied by the material. Subtract the material's $20\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ from the external $25\,\mathrm{cm^3}$, leaving $5\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. This is $\dfrac{5}{25}=\dfrac15=20\,\%$ of the external volume. The denominator is the whole object's external volume because that is the reference for the percentage.
