A graduated cylinder reads $20\,\mathrm{mL}$ with liquid alone, $24\,\mathrm{mL}$ with the first solid fully submerged, and $31\,\mathrm{mL}$ with both solids fully submerged. The solids are nonporous. What is the ratio of the second solid's volume to the first's?

- $2.75$
- $1.75$
- $\dfrac{31}{24}$
- $\dfrac{4}{7}$

## Explanation

The first submersion adds $24-20=4\,\mathrm{mL}$. The second starts at 24 millilitres because the first solid is already in the cylinder. The second thus adds $31-24=7\,\mathrm{mL}$. The requested ratio of the second to the first is $\dfrac{7}{4}=1.75$, the same as the ratio of the increases.
