Two nonporous solids are submerged one after the other. The readings are $20$, $24$, and $31\,\mathrm{mL}$. What is the volume ratio of the second solid to the first?

- $2.75$
- $1.75$
- $\dfrac{31}{24}$
- $\dfrac{4}{7}$

## Explanation

The first difference isolates the first solid. The second isolates the second solid because the intermediate reading already includes the first solid's volume.

$\frac{31-24}{24-20}=1.75$.
