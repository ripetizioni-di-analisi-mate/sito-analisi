A closed quantity of liquid is warmed with no loss, and its volume increases. How does its mean density change?

- It decreases
- It increases
- It stays the same
- It becomes equal to the mass

## Explanation

The closed system retains the mass of the stated amount of liquid. That same mass occupies a greater volume, so the ratio $\rho=\dfrac{m}{V}$ decreases. If the volume doubled, for example, each unit of volume would contain half as much mass on average.
