A closed quantity of liquid is warmed with no loss, and its volume increases. How does its mean density change?

- It decreases
- It increases
- It stays the same
- It becomes equal to the mass

## Explanation

The liquid's mass is conserved. Mass divided by volume decreases when the positive denominator increases.
