Four identical sheets, each $60\,\mathrm g$ and $20\,\mathrm{cm}^3$, are stacked without gaps. What is the density of the stack as one object?

- $12\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $0.75\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $320\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $3\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$

## Explanation

The stack's mass and volume are four times those of a single sheet. The common factor disappears in the ratio.

$\frac{4\cdot 60}{4\cdot 20}=3\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.
