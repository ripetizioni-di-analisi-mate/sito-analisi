Four plates, each with a mass of $60\,\mathrm{g}$ and a volume of $20\,\mathrm{cm}^3$, are stacked with a gap of $10\,\mathrm{cm}^3$ between each pair of adjacent plates. If the mass of the air and supports is negligible, what is the average density of the stack including the gaps?

- $3\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $8\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $\dfrac{6}{11}\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $\dfrac{24}{11}\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$

## Explanation

The four plates have a total mass of $240\,\mathrm{g}$. They occupy $80\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ and form three gaps, which add $30\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ to the volume. The required average density is therefore $\dfrac{240}{110}=\dfrac{24}{11}\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. Using only the volume of the plates would give the density of their material.
