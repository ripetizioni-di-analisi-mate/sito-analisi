The total mass of a container with liquid obeys $m=60+3V$, with $m$ in grams and $V$ in cubic centimetres. What does $60$ represent?

- The empty container's mass in grams
- Density in grams per cubic centimetre
- Maximum volume in cubic centimetres
- The mass of one cubic centimetre of liquid

## Explanation

To read the tare, set $V=0$, because a zero liquid volume leaves the container empty. The equation then gives $m=60\,\mathrm{g}$. The coefficient $3$ belongs to the part that grows with volume and tells us how many grams each cubic centimetre of liquid adds.
