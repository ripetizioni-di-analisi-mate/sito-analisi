The graph uses volume in litres and mass in kilograms. A line has slope $0.8$. What is the density in SI units?

- $0.0008\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $1.25\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $0.8\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

The slope is kilograms per litre. A cubic metre contains a thousand litres, so the value per cubic metre is a thousand times larger.

$0.8\cdot 1000=800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.
