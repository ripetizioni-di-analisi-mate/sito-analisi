The graph uses volume in litres and mass in kilograms. A line has slope $0.8$. What is the density in SI units?

- $0.0008\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $1.25\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $0.8\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

The slope is in kilograms per litre, because those are the units on the two axes. It is $0.8\,\mathrm{kg/L}$. A cubic metre contains a thousand litres, each corresponding to $0.8\,\mathrm{kg}$. A cubic metre therefore has mass $800\,\mathrm{kg}$, and the SI density is $800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.
