A liquid's total mass line is $m=35+2V$ in grams and cubic centimetres. What volume gives a total reading of $75\,\mathrm g$?

- $37.5\,\mathrm{cm}^3$
- $20\,\mathrm{cm}^3$
- $40\,\mathrm{cm}^3$
- $55\,\mathrm{cm}^3$

## Explanation

Subtract the initial container mass before dividing by slope. The difference is the liquid's mass.

$\frac{75-35}{2}=20\,\mathrm{cm}^3$.
