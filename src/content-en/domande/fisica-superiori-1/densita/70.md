A liquid's total mass line is $m=35+2V$ in grams and cubic centimetres. What volume gives a total reading of $75\,\mathrm{g}$?

- $37.5\,\mathrm{cm}^3$
- $20\,\mathrm{cm}^3$
- $40\,\mathrm{cm}^3$
- $55\,\mathrm{cm}^3$

## Explanation

The scale reads a total mass of $75\,\mathrm{g}$. Of this, $35\,\mathrm{g}$ belong to the empty container. The liquid accounts for $40\,\mathrm{g}$, at density $2\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$ given by the slope. Dividing $40$ by $2$ gives $20\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. Substitution into the equation gives back $35+2\cdot20=75\,\mathrm{g}$.
