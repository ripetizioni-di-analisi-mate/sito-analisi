With mass on the horizontal axis and volume on the vertical axis, the slope is $0.25\,\mathrm{cm^3/g}$. What is the density?

- $0.25\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $25\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $0.0625\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $4\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$

## Explanation

With mass on the horizontal axis, the slope gives the volume corresponding to one gram. Density asks for the inverse relationship, the mass corresponding to one cubic centimetre. Take the reciprocal of the slope, including its units. The result is $\dfrac{1}{0.25}=4\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.
