With mass on the horizontal axis and volume on the vertical axis, the slope is $0.25\,\mathrm{cm^3/g}$. What is the density?

- $0.25\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $25\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $0.0625\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $4\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$

## Explanation

The swapped axes give volume per mass. Density is the reciprocal, which also reverses the ratio's unit.

$\frac{1}{0.25}=4\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.
