Equal volumes of liquids of density $1.2$ and $1.8\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$ are mixed. With additive volumes, what density results?

- $1.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $3\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $2.16\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $0.3\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$

## Explanation

The initial volumes are equal. Calling both $V$, the total mass is $1.2V+1.8V$ grams. Additive volumes give a final volume of $2V$, so the ratio is the arithmetic mean $\dfrac{1.2+1.8}{2}=1.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. This mean applies here because the liquids contribute equal shares of the volume.
