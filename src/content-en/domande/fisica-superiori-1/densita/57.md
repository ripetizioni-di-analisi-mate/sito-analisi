Equal volumes of liquids of density $1.2$ and $1.8\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$ are mixed. With additive volumes, what density results?

- $1.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $3\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $2.16\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $0.3\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$

## Explanation

The same volume weights both densities. It cancels from the ratio, leaving the average of the two values.

$\frac{1.2+1.8}{2}=1.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.
