Sample A has twice the mass and four times the volume of B. What is the density ratio $\rho_A/\rho_B$?

- $8$
- $0.5$
- $2$
- $0.25$

## Explanation

The mass factor goes in the numerator and the volume factor in the denominator. The sample with more mass is less dense.

$\frac{2}{4}=0.5$.
