Sample A has twice the mass and four times the volume of B. What is the density ratio $\dfrac{\rho_A}{\rho_B}$?

- $8$
- $0.5$
- $2$
- $0.25$

## Explanation

The comparison is of mass per unit of volume, so both scale factors matter. In sample A the numerator doubles and the denominator quadruples relative to B. Thus $\dfrac{\rho_A}{\rho_B}=\dfrac{2}{4}=0.5$. The relative increase in volume is larger than that in mass, so A has the lower density.
