A block is compressed until its volume is halved, with no loss of mass. What percentage of its initial density does it now have?

- $50\,\%$
- $150\,\%$
- $200\,\%$
- $100\,\%$

## Explanation

The mass stays fixed, so compare the densities using the inverse ratio of the volumes. If the final volume is $\dfrac12$ of the initial volume, the final density is $2$ times the original. The final value is $200\,\%$ of the initial value. The increase counts only what is added to the initial value and is $100\,\%$.
