A block is compressed to $80\%$ of its initial volume with no loss of mass. What percentage of its initial density does it now have?

- $80\,\%$
- $120\,\%$
- $125\,\%$
- $20\,\%$

## Explanation

The final volume introduces a factor $0.8$ in the denominator. Density is therefore multiplied by its reciprocal.

$\frac{100\,\%}{0.8}=125\,\%$.
