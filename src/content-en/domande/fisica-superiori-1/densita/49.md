A rectangular plate measures $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ by $5\,\mathrm{cm}$, is $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ thick, and has mass $60\,\mathrm{g}$. What is its density?

- $3\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $0.3\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$

## Explanation

The plate's area is $10\cdot5=50\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. Its thickness is $0.4\,\mathrm{cm}$, so it occupies $20\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. Dividing $60\,\mathrm{g}$ by $20\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ gives a density of $3\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. Using the thickness in millimetres directly would give a numerical volume ten times too large.
