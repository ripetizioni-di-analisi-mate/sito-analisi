A rectangular plate measures $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ by $5\,\mathrm{cm}$, is $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ thick, and has mass $60\,\mathrm g$. What is its density?

- $3\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $0.3\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$

## Explanation

Thickness in centimetres is $0.4$. The product of all three dimensions gives volume in the correct unit for the ratio.

$\frac{60}{10\cdot 5\cdot 0.4}=3\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.
