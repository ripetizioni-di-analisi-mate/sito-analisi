A closed jar filled with liquid has a total mass of $212\,\mathrm{g}$. The jar with the liquid and lid removed has a mass of $65\,\mathrm{g}$. The lid alone has a mass of $20\,\mathrm{g}$. What is the mass of the liquid?

- $147\,\mathrm{g}$
- $127\,\mathrm{g}$
- $192\,\mathrm{g}$
- $167\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

The jar and lid have a combined mass of $65+20=85\,\mathrm{g}$. This is their total tare. Subtracting it from $212\,\mathrm{g}$ gives $127\,\mathrm{g}$ of liquid. The sum $127+65+20=212\,\mathrm{g}$ reproduces the balance reading.
