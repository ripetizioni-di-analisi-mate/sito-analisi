A sealed jar with liquid has total mass $212\,\mathrm g$. The empty jar and lid have masses $65$ and $12\,\mathrm g$, respectively. What is the liquid's mass?

- $147\,\mathrm g$
- $135\,\mathrm g$
- $200\,\mathrm g$
- $159\,\mathrm g$

## Explanation

The reading includes three contributions. Subtract both container parts to isolate the required mass.

$212-65-12=135\,\mathrm g$.
