Express $0.002\,\mathrm{g/mm^3}$ in $\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. What is the numerical value?

- $0.000002\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $0.02\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $2\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $0.2\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$

## Explanation

Each centimetre contains ten millimetres. A cubic centimetre contains $10^3=1000$ cubes with sides of one millimetre. To find the mass in each cubic centimetre, multiply the mass in each cubic millimetre by a thousand. The result is $0.002\cdot1000=2\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.
