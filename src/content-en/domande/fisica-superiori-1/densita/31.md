Express $0.002\,\mathrm{g/mm^3}$ in $\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. What is the numerical value?

- $0.000002\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $0.02\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $2\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $0.2\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$

## Explanation

One cubic centimetre contains a thousand cubic millimetres. The mass per cubic centimetre therefore contains a thousand contributions of the original value.

$0.002\cdot 1000=2\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.
