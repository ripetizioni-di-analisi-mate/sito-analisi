On a graph of mass in grams against volume in cubic centimetres, line A rises faster than line B on the same scales. What follows?

- B has greater density
- The densities are equal
- A has greater density
- The slope concerns volume alone

## Explanation

Slope measures mass gained per unit increase in volume. The steeper line therefore has the higher density.
