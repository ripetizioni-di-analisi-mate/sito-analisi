On a graph of mass in grams against volume in cubic centimetres, line A rises faster than line B on the same scales. What follows?

- B has greater density
- The densities are equal
- A has greater density
- The slope concerns volume alone

## Explanation

The slope gives the increase in mass for a fixed increase in volume. The scales of the graphs match, so the steeper line corresponds to a greater increase in mass per cubic centimetre. This ratio is density, which is greater for A regardless of the mass read at any single point.
