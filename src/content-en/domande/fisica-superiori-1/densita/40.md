A container reads $98\,\mathrm g$ with $20\,\mathrm{mL}$ and $126\,\mathrm g$ with $40\,\mathrm{mL}$ of the same liquid. What is the liquid's density?

- $3.15\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $4.9\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $0.7\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $1.4\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$

## Explanation

Use the increase in mass corresponding to the increase in volume. The container's mass appears in both readings and cancels.

$\frac{126-98}{40-20}=1.4\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.
