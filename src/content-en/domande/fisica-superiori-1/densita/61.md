A $100\,\mathrm{cm}^3$ mixture has density $1.6\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. Adding $20\,\mathrm{cm}^3$ of another liquid gives density $1.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. What was the added liquid's density if volumes are additive?

- $1\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $9\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $8\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $0.1\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$

## Explanation

Find the mass before and after adding the liquid. The difference belongs to the added volume, so divide by that volume.

$\frac{120\cdot 1.5-100\cdot 1.6}{20}=1\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.
