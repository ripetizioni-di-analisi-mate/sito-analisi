Mix $10$, $20$, and $30\,\mathrm{cm}^3$ of liquids at $1$, $2$, and $3\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$, respectively. With additive volumes, what is the overall density?

- $\dfrac{7}{3}\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $2\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $140\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $0.1\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$

## Explanation

Find and add the three masses using density times volume. The denominator is the sum of the three volumes.

$\frac{10\cdot 1+20\cdot 2+30\cdot 3}{10+20+30}=\dfrac{7}{3}\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.
