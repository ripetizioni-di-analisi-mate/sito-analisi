A density of $3\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$ was obtained for a $45\,\mathrm g$ sample. If volume was underestimated by $3\,\mathrm{cm}^3$, what corrected density is obtained?

- $3\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $3.75\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $6\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$

## Explanation

The initial data imply a volume of fifteen. Add the volume correction and then compute the ratio again.

$\frac{45}{\frac{45}{3}+3}=2.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.
