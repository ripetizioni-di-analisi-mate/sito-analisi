With no tare, a reading at three times the volume has only twice the mass. Which property is incompatible with both exact readings?

- Positive mass at both volumes
- Mass increasing with volume
- Constant density over the interval
- Lower density at the second point

## Explanation

At constant density, mass is directly proportional to volume. With tare excluded, tripling the volume would triple the mass. The data give only double the mass, so the new mass-to-volume ratio is $\dfrac23$ of the initial one. The two different ratios are incompatible with constant density over the interval.
