Mass stays unchanged during mixing, but the final volume exceeds the initial total. How is density calculated with additive volumes biased?

- It is too low
- It is correct
- It is too high
- It becomes zero

## Explanation

The assumption gives a smaller denominator than the measured one. The same mass divided by smaller volume yields greater density.
