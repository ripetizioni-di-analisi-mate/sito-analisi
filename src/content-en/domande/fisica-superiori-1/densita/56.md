A shell with an open hole is submerged and liquid completely fills the cavity without bubbles. What volume does the shell exclude from the liquid?

- The whole outside volume, including the cavity
- The cavity volume
- Twice the outside volume
- The wall material's volume

## Explanation

The liquid enters the cavity through the hole, so it can occupy the entire empty internal space. Only the solid wall excludes liquid. The displaced volume equals the volume of the material. It would be greater if the hole were sealed and the cavity remained dry.
