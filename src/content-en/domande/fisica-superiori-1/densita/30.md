Mass in grams was converted to kilograms, and volume remains in cubic centimetres. Which unit does the computed ratio have?

- $\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $\mathrm{kg/cm^3}$
- $\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $\mathrm{cm^3/kg}$

## Explanation

The units of a ratio come from the units actually used in each measurement. After conversion the mass is in kilograms. Volume remains in cubic centimetres, so the ratio is in kilograms per cubic centimetre. To obtain SI units, convert the cubic centimetres to cubic metres as well.
