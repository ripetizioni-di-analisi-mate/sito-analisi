Mass in grams was converted to kilograms, and volume remains in cubic centimetres. Which unit does the computed ratio have?

- $\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $\mathrm{kg/cm^3}$
- $\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $\mathrm{cm^3/kg}$

## Explanation

The ratio uses kilograms for mass and cubic centimetres for volume. Reaching SI requires a further conversion.
