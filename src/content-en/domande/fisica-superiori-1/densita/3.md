A sample has a scale reading of $135\,\mathrm{g}$ and density $9\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. What is its volume?

- $1215\,\mathrm{cm}^3$
- $\dfrac{1}{15}\,\mathrm{cm}^3$
- $15\,\mathrm{cm}^3$
- $126\,\mathrm{cm}^3$

## Explanation

The scale gives total mass and density tells how many grams belong to a cubic centimetre. Dividing mass by that value gives the number of cubic centimetres in the sample. Multiplying the resulting volume by the mass of each unit volume checks the result.

Thus $V=\dfrac{m}{\rho}=\dfrac{135}{9}=15\,\mathrm{cm}^3$.
