A sphere of material at $2\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$ has volume $24\,\mathrm{cm}^3$. A smaller homogeneous sphere of volume $6\,\mathrm{cm}^3$ is made by removing material. How many grams were removed?

- $48\,\mathrm g$
- $12\,\mathrm g$
- $36\,\mathrm g$
- $18\,\mathrm g$

## Explanation

The difference of volumes belongs to the removed part. Multiply it by material density to obtain its mass.

$\left(24-6\right)\cdot 2=36\,\mathrm g$.
