A hole with constant cross-section is cut through a uniform sheet of thickness $5\,\mathrm{mm}$ and density $6\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. The mass drops from $120$ to $108\,\mathrm{g}$. What is the area of the hole?

- $2\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $5\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $4\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $12\,\mathrm{cm^2}$

## Explanation

The difference between weighings is $12\,\mathrm{g}$. The removed material has the sheet's density, so it occupied $\dfrac{12}{6}=2\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. The hole passes through the entire $0.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ thickness, so divide the volume by this thickness. The final units are square centimetres, as required for an area.
