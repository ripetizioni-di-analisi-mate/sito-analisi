Positive mass and volume are bounded by $18$ and $22\,\mathrm{g}$ and by $4$ and $5\,\mathrm{cm}^3$. What is the highest possible density if the bounds can combine independently?

- $4.4\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $3.6\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $5.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$

## Explanation

For positive volume, raising the mass increases density, and reducing the volume increases it further. To maximise the ratio, pair the largest mass, $22\,\mathrm{g}$, with the smallest volume, $4\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. The question allows this combination, which gives $\dfrac{22}{4}=5.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.
