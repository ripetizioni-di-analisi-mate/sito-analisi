Positive mass and volume are bounded by $18$ and $22\,\mathrm g$ and by $4$ and $5\,\mathrm{cm}^3$. What is the highest possible density if the bounds can combine independently?

- $4.4\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $3.6\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $5.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$

## Explanation

The ratio grows with the numerator and falls with the denominator. The upper bound uses the largest mass and smallest volume.

$\frac{22}{4}=5.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.
