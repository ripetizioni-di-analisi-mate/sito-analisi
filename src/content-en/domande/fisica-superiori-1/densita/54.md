Several objects have the same external volume and are made of the same material. The air in their cavities has negligible mass. Which object has the lowest mean density?

- The one with a thicker wall everywhere
- The one with the larger empty cavity
- The completely solid one
- They all have the same mean density

## Explanation

For objects with equal external volumes and the same material, a larger cavity leaves less volume for the wall. At the same material density, that smaller volume of material has less mass. Both mean-density ratios use the same external volume, so the object with more empty space has the lower mean density.
