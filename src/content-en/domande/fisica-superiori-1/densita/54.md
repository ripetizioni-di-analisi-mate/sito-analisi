With the same outer shape and wall material, which object has the lower mean density?

- The one with a thicker wall everywhere
- The one with the larger empty cavity
- The completely solid one
- They all have the same mean density

## Explanation

The external denominator is the same. A larger cavity leaves less material and lowers the numerator mass.
