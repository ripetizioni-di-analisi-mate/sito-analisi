A graph uses $2\,\mathrm{cm}^3$ per horizontal square and $5\,\mathrm{g}$ per vertical square. The line rises six squares over three horizontal squares. What is density?

- $2\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $0.8\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $30\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$

## Explanation

The six vertical squares represent $6\cdot5=30\,\mathrm{g}$. The three horizontal squares represent $3\cdot2=6\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. The slope in physical units is therefore $\dfrac{30}{6}=5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. The ratio of the square counts alone is two, but that does not account for the different scales on the axes.
