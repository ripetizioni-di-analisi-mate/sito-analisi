A graph uses $2\,\mathrm{cm}^3$ per horizontal square and $5\,\mathrm g$ per vertical square. The line rises six squares over three horizontal squares. What is density?

- $2\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $0.8\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $30\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$

## Explanation

Convert the square counts using their respective scales. Then divide the increase in mass by the increase in volume.

$\frac{6\cdot 5}{3\cdot 2}=5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.
