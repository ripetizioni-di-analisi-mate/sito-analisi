Which pair describes equal-density samples with different masses?

- $12\,\mathrm g$ in $4\,\mathrm{cm}^3$ and $18\,\mathrm g$ in $4\,\mathrm{cm}^3$
- $12\,\mathrm g$ in $6\,\mathrm{cm}^3$ and $18\,\mathrm g$ in $3\,\mathrm{cm}^3$
- $12\,\mathrm g$ in $3\,\mathrm{cm}^3$ and $18\,\mathrm g$ in $9\,\mathrm{cm}^3$
- $12\,\mathrm g$ in $4\,\mathrm{cm}^3$ and $18\,\mathrm g$ in $6\,\mathrm{cm}^3$

## Explanation

Both ratios in the first pair equal three. The same comparison gives different values for the other pairs.
