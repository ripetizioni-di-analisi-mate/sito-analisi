A uniform sheet with area $60\,\mathrm{cm}^2$ and mass $48\,\mathrm{g}$ has density $4\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. What is its thickness in millimetres?

- $2\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.2\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $32\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $720\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

Dividing mass by density gives a sheet volume of $\dfrac{48}{4}=12\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. A uniform sheet's volume is its area times thickness. Divide $12\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ by $60\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ to obtain $0.2\,\mathrm{cm}$. Converting to millimetres gives the requested thickness of $2\,\mathrm{mm}$.
