A uniform sheet with area $60\,\mathrm{cm}^2$ and mass $48\,\mathrm g$ has density $4\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. What is its thickness in millimetres?

- $2\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.2\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $32\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $720\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

First find volume by dividing mass by density. Volume divided by area gives thickness, initially in centimetres.

$\frac{\frac{48}{4}}{60}\cdot 10=2\,\mathrm{mm}$.
