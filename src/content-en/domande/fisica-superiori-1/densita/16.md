A quarter of the volume is removed from a homogeneous block weighing $240\,\mathrm{g}$ and occupying $80\,\mathrm{cm}^3$. What is the final-to-initial density ratio under the same conditions?

- $0.75$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $0.25$
- $1$

## Explanation

The initial density is $\dfrac{240}{80}=3\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. The removed part occupies $20\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ and has mass $60\,\mathrm{g}$ because the material is homogeneous. There are $180\,\mathrm{g}$ in $60\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ left, giving the same density of $3\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. The ratio of the two equal densities is one.
