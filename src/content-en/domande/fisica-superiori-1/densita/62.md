Two liquids with total mass $90\,\mathrm g$ initially occupied $40$ and $50\,\mathrm{mL}$. The measured final volume is $84\,\mathrm{mL}$. What density results?

- $1\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $\dfrac{15}{14}\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $\dfrac{14}{15}\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $\dfrac{15}{29}\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$

## Explanation

The final reading gives the actual denominator. Adding the initial volumes would assume that mixing leaves total volume unchanged.

$\frac{90}{84}=\dfrac{15}{14}\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.
