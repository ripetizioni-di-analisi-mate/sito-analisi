A block fills a cylinder of fixed cross section without gaps. Compression halves its height and mass stays the same. How does density change?

- It doubles
- It halves
- It becomes one quarter
- It stays the same

## Explanation

With fixed cross-sectional area, volume changes with height. Halving the denominator volume doubles the ratio at fixed mass.
