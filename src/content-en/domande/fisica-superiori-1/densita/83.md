In the ideal graph $m=\rho V$, a point lies below the line at its measured $V$. What does that mean about its measured mass?

- It is above the predicted mass at that volume
- It must be zero
- It is below the predicted mass at that volume
- It agrees with the predicted mass

## Explanation

The point's vertical coordinate is smaller than the line's value at the same horizontal coordinate. The vertical axis represents mass.
