In the ideal graph $m=\rho V$, a point lies below the line at its measured $V$. What does that mean about its measured mass?

- It is above the predicted mass at that volume
- It must be zero
- It is below the predicted mass at that volume
- It agrees with the predicted mass

## Explanation

Fix the volume read on the horizontal axis. At that volume the line's vertical coordinate is the predicted mass. If the measured point lies below the line, its vertical coordinate is smaller than predicted. The discrepancy shows that the measurement and prediction differ, but on its own it does not identify the source.
