Which density in grams per cubic centimetre equals $840\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$?

- $0.84\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $840000\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $8.4\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $0.00084\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$

## Explanation

For the same density, the number in $\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ is a thousand times the number in $\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. Divide $840$ by a thousand to obtain $0.84\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. Check it on one litre, which contains a thousand cubic centimetres. At $0.84\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$, a litre has mass $840\,\mathrm{g}$.
