Which density in grams per cubic centimetre equals $840\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$?

- $0.84\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $840000\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $8.4\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $0.00084\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$

## Explanation

One gram per cubic centimetre represents a thousand kilograms per cubic metre. Reverse the conversion by dividing by a thousand.

$\frac{840}{1000}=0.84\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.
