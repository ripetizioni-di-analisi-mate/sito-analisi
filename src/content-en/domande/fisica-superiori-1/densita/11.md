A sheet has area $80\,\mathrm{cm}^2$, thickness $1\,\mathrm{mm}$, and density $5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. What is its mass?

- $400\,\mathrm{g}$
- $4000\,\mathrm{g}$
- $40\,\mathrm{g}$
- $16\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

The sheet's volume is its area times its thickness. Because the area is in square centimetres, express the thickness in centimetres first. Multiplying the resulting volume by density converts cubic centimetres to grams. As a check, a thicker sheet of the same area and material would have greater mass.

The volume is $V=80\cdot0.1=8\,\mathrm{cm}^3$, so $m=5V=40\,\mathrm{g}$.
