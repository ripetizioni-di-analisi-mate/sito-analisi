A laboratory density is $0.6\,\mathrm{g/mL}$. What is it in $\mathrm{mg/mL}$?

- $600\,\mathrm{mg/mL}$
- $0.0006\,\mathrm{mg/mL}$
- $60\,\mathrm{mg/mL}$
- $600000\,\mathrm{mg/mL}$

## Explanation

Volume is already in millilitres. Replace each gram in the numerator with a thousand milligrams.

$0.6\cdot 1000=600\,\mathrm{mg/mL}$.
