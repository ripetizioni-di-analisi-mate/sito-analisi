A material has density $9\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. What is the mass of $23\,\mathrm{cm}^3$ of this material?

- $\dfrac{23}{9}\,\mathrm g$
- $207\,\mathrm g$
- $\dfrac{9}{23}\,\mathrm g$
- $32\,\mathrm g$

## Explanation

Density gives the mass contained in each cubic centimetre. Volume counts the cubic centimetres present, so multiply density by volume to find the mass. The cubic centimetre in the density denominator cancels the cubic centimetre in the volume, leaving grams.

Therefore $m=\rho V=9\cdot23=207\,\mathrm g$.
