To obtain density $1.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$ without mass loss, what measured final volume must a $120\,\mathrm g$ mixture have?

- $180\,\mathrm{cm}^3$
- $0.0125\,\mathrm{cm}^3$
- $118.5\,\mathrm{cm}^3$
- $80\,\mathrm{cm}^3$

## Explanation

Density refers to the actual volume after mixing. Recover that volume by dividing total mass by density.

$\frac{120}{1.5}=80\,\mathrm{cm}^3$.
