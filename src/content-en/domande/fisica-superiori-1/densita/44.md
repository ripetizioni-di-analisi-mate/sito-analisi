The level in a cylinder of constant cross-sectional area $4\,\mathrm{cm}^2$ rises by $1.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ after full submersion. What volume is displaced?

- $\dfrac{8}{3}\,\mathrm{cm}^3$
- $0.375\,\mathrm{cm}^3$
- $5.5\,\mathrm{cm}^3$
- $6\,\mathrm{cm}^3$

## Explanation

The rise in liquid level is the height of a layer. Its volume is cross-sectional area times height.

$4\cdot 1.5=6\,\mathrm{cm}^3$.
