In a cylinder with constant cross-sectional area $5\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, the liquid rises by $1.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ when a solid is fully submerged. What volume is displaced?

- $\dfrac{10}{3}\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $0.3\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $6.5\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{cm^3}$

## Explanation

The cross-section is constant, so the displaced volume corresponds to a layer of area $5\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and height $1.5\,\mathrm{cm}$. This gives $5\cdot1.5=7.5\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. The units also agree, as length times area gives volume. The result applies to the whole solid because it is fully submerged.
