Only half of a solid's volume is immersed, and its total mass is used in the ratio. How does the apparent density compare to the actual density?

- Half as large
- Twice as large
- The same
- Four times as large

## Explanation

The displaced volume is half the total volume. Dividing the same mass by half the volume doubles the result.
