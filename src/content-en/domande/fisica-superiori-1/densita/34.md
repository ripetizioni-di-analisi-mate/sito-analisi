Only half of a solid's volume is immersed, and its total mass is used in the ratio. How does the apparent density compare to the actual density?

- Half as large
- Twice as large
- The same
- Four times as large

## Explanation

The rise in level comes from the submerged volume, which here is $\dfrac V2$. The mass used is still the mass of the whole solid. This gives $\dfrac{m}{\frac{V}{2}}=\dfrac{2m}{V}$, twice the true density. The two measurements refer to different parts of the sample, which is why the ratio is distorted.
