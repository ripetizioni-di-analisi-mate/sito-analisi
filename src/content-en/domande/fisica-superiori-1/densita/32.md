Which sample is denser, $900\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ or $0.95\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$?

- The first sample
- The values are equal
- The units describe incompatible quantities
- The second sample

## Explanation

The second value equals $950\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$, fifty more than the first in the same unit.
