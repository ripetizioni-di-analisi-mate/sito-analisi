Which sample is denser, $900\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ or $0.95\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$?

- The first sample
- The values are equal
- The units describe incompatible quantities
- The second sample

## Explanation

Express both densities in the same unit. The value $0.95\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$ is $950\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. Comparing it with $900\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ shows that the second sample has 50 more kilograms per cubic metre. This compares mass at equal volume, regardless of the sizes of the samples.
