A displacement measurement includes a $2\,\mathrm{mL}$ bubble. The observed increase is $8\,\mathrm{mL}$ and the solid's mass is $18\,\mathrm{g}$. What corrected density is obtained?

- $3\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $2.25\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $1.8\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $9\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$

## Explanation

The difference of $8\,\mathrm{mL}$ includes the bubble's $2\,\mathrm{mL}$ contribution. Subtracting it shows that the solid displaces $6\,\mathrm{mL}$, or $6\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. Use only this volume with the solid's $18\,\mathrm{g}$ mass. The corrected density is therefore $\dfrac{18}{6}=3\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.
