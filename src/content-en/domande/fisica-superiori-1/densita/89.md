A displacement measurement includes a $2\,\mathrm{mL}$ bubble. The observed increase is $8\,\mathrm{mL}$ and the solid's mass is $18\,\mathrm g$. What corrected density is obtained?

- $3\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $2.25\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $1.8\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $9\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$

## Explanation

The eight millilitres include both the solid and the bubble. Subtract the bubble to find the correct denominator.

$\frac{18}{8-2}=3\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.
