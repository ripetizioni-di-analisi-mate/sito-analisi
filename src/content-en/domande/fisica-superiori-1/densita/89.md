A gauge shows only that the volume of a $24\,\mathrm{g}$ sample exceeds $8\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. Which conclusion about its density is certain?

- It is less than $3\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.
- It is exactly $3\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.
- It exceeds $3\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.
- It is less than $1\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.

## Explanation

Dividing $24$ by $8$ would give $3$. The actual volume is greater, so the mass-to-volume ratio must be below $3$. A volume greater than $24\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ would be needed to impose the bound $1$.
