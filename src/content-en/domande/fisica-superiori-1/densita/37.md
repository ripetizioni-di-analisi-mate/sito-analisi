During full submersion, $2\,\mathrm{mL}$ of water spills out of the cylinder. How is final minus initial corrected to obtain the solid's volume?

- Add $2\,\mathrm{mL}$
- Subtract $2\,\mathrm{mL}$
- Divide by two
- Multiply by two

## Explanation

The final reading is missing the two millilitres that left the cylinder. The difference between final and initial readings is therefore the object's volume reduced by the spill. Add the lost two millilitres to recover the actual volume. Check the sign by considering the higher final reading that there would have been without the spill.
