During full submersion, $2\,\mathrm{mL}$ of water spills out of the cylinder. How is final minus initial corrected to obtain the solid's volume?

- Add $2\,\mathrm{mL}$
- Subtract $2\,\mathrm{mL}$
- Divide by two
- Multiply by two

## Explanation

The spill lowers the final reading by two millilitres compared with submersion without a spill. The correction restores that volume.
