A shell has external volume $40\,\mathrm{cm}^3$, half of it an empty cavity. What fraction of the wall's density is its mean density?

- $1$
- $2$
- $0.5$
- $0.25$

## Explanation

Only the half occupied by material gives mass. The mean density is calculated over the whole external volume.

$\frac{1}{2}=0.5$.
