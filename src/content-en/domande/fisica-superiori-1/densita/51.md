A shell has external volume $40\,\mathrm{cm}^3$, half of it an empty cavity. What fraction of the wall's density is its mean density?

- $1$
- $2$
- $0.5$
- $0.25$

## Explanation

The material occupies $20\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ out of the $40\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ external volume. With the cavity empty, only these $20\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ contribute mass. The mean density based on external volume divides that mass by twice the volume of the material. It is therefore half the density of the wall.
