Which unit would be appropriate for the reciprocal of a density in $\mathrm{g/cm^3}$?

- $\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $\mathrm{cm^3/g}$
- $\mathrm{g\,cm^3}$
- $\mathrm{g/cm^2}$

## Explanation

Density is mass per unit volume and is measured here in grams per cubic centimetre. Its reciprocal is volume per unit mass. Swapping the numerator and denominator of the unit gives cubic centimetres per gram. Multiplying the two units cancels them and leaves one.
