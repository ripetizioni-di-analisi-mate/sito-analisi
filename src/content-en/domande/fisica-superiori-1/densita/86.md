Which unit would be appropriate for the reciprocal of a density in $\mathrm{g/cm^3}$?

- $\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $\mathrm{cm^3/g}$
- $\mathrm{g\,cm^3}$
- $\mathrm{g/cm^2}$

## Explanation

The reciprocal swaps numerator and denominator. It gives the volume corresponding to one unit of mass, the gram.
