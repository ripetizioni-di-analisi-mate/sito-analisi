A nonporous irregular stone has known mass. What further measurement finds its density without modelling its shape?

- The initial liquid volume alone
- The empty cylinder's mass
- The liquid volume increase with the stone fully submerged
- The stone's maximum length

## Explanation

The final-minus-initial difference measures the solid's volume directly under fully submerged conditions.
