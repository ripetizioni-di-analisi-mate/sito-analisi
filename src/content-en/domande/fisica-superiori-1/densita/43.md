A nonporous irregular stone has known mass. What further measurement finds its density without modelling its shape?

- The initial liquid volume alone
- The empty cylinder's mass
- The liquid volume increase with the stone fully submerged
- The stone's maximum length

## Explanation

Density is found by dividing mass by the object's volume. An irregular shape makes a simple geometrical formula unsuitable. With the stone fully submerged and without bubbles, the difference between the two cylinder readings gives the required volume. The ratio can then be calculated.
