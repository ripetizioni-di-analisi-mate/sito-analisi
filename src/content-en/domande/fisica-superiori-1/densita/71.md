A homogeneous material gives $m=20\,\mathrm g$ at $V=10\,\mathrm{cm}^3$ and is modelled by a line through the origin. Which additional pair confirms the same slope?

- $V=16\,\mathrm{cm}^3$ and $m=20\,\mathrm g$
- $V=32\,\mathrm{cm}^3$ and $m=16\,\mathrm g$
- $V=16\,\mathrm{cm}^3$ and $m=32\,\mathrm g$
- $V=20\,\mathrm{cm}^3$ and $m=60\,\mathrm g$

## Explanation

The slope is two mass units per volume unit. The first new pair preserves this ratio exactly.
