A material's density is $1.8\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. What is it in SI units?

- $1.8\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $0.0018\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $1800000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $1800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

Density is a ratio, so the conversions act in the numerator and denominator. A gram is one thousandth of a kilogram, and a cubic centimetre is one millionth of a cubic metre. Their conversion factors have a ratio of one thousand, which multiplies the old numerical value.

Since $1\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$, the density is $\rho=1800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.
