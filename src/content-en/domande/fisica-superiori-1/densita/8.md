Two samples each have volume $18\,\mathrm{cm}^3$ and their densities are $2$ and $11\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. By how much is the second sample's mass greater?

- $234\,\mathrm{g}$
- $9\,\mathrm{g}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{g}$
- $162\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

The two samples have the same volume. Subtract the first density from the second and multiply the difference by the common volume. This is equivalent to finding both masses separately. The positive sign confirms that the second sample has greater mass.

The calculation gives $\Delta m=(11-2)\cdot18=162\,\mathrm{g}$.
