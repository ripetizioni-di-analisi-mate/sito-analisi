Two cubes of the same material have edges of $2$ and $6\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the larger-to-smaller mass ratio?

- $3$
- $27$
- $9$
- $108$

## Explanation

The factor of three applies in every direction. Volume scales with the cube of edge length, and mass increases in the same ratio.

$\left(\frac{6}{2}\right)^{3}=27$.
