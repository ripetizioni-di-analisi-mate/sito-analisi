Two cubes of the same material have edges of $2$ and $6\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the larger-to-smaller mass ratio?

- $3$
- $27$
- $9$
- $108$

## Explanation

The material is the same, so the mass ratio equals the volume ratio. The edge grows from $2$ to $6\,\mathrm{cm}$, a factor of three. This factor appears in each of the three dimensions, so the volume increases by a factor of $3^3=27$. The mass increases by the same factor.
