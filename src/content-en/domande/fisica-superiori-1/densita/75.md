Two readings are $(V,m)=(5,32)$ and $(15,56)$, in cubic centimetres and grams. What total mass is predicted at zero volume by the linear model?

- $32\,\mathrm{g}$
- $24\,\mathrm{g}$
- $20\,\mathrm{g}$
- $37\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

The readings differ by $56-32=24\,\mathrm{g}$ over an increase in volume of $15-5=10\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. The slope is $2.4\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. The first $5\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ of liquid therefore contribute $12\,\mathrm{g}$. Subtracting this from the reading of $32\,\mathrm{g}$ gives a tare of $20\,\mathrm{g}$, the mass at zero volume.
