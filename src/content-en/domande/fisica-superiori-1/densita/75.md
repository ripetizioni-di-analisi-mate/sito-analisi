Two readings are $(V,m)=(5,32)$ and $(15,56)$, in cubic centimetres and grams. What total mass is predicted at zero volume by the linear model?

- $32\,\mathrm g$
- $24\,\mathrm g$
- $20\,\mathrm g$
- $37\,\mathrm g$

## Explanation

The slope is $\dfrac{56-32}{15-5}$. To return to zero volume, subtract the mass of the first five cubic centimetres from thirty-two.

$32-\frac{56-32}{15-5}\cdot 5=20\,\mathrm g$.
