Which relation converts directly from $\mathrm{g/cm^3}$ to $\mathrm{kg/L}$?

- Multiply the number by a hundred
- Numerical values are equal
- Divide the number by a thousand
- Multiply the number by a thousand

## Explanation

Converting grams to kilograms divides the numerical mass by a thousand. Converting cubic centimetres to litres does the same to the numerical volume. The two factors cancel in the ratio, so the same numerical value expresses the density in both units.
