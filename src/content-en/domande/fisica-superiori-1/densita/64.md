A homogeneous $80\,\mathrm{mL}$ mixture has density $1.25\,\mathrm{g/mL}$. If $20\,\mathrm{mL}$ is removed, how much mass remains?

- $100\,\mathrm g$
- $25\,\mathrm g$
- $125\,\mathrm g$
- $75\,\mathrm g$

## Explanation

Homogeneity gives the remaining liquid the same density. Multiply this density by the remaining volume.

$\left(80-20\right)\cdot 1.25=75\,\mathrm g$.
