A container holding $30\,\mathrm{mL}$ of liquid of density $1.2\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$ has total mass $86\,\mathrm g$. What is the empty container's mass?

- $50\,\mathrm g$
- $122\,\mathrm g$
- $36\,\mathrm g$
- $56\,\mathrm g$

## Explanation

Find the liquid's mass from density and volume. Subtracting it from the total leaves the empty container's mass.

$86-1.2\cdot 30=50\,\mathrm g$.
