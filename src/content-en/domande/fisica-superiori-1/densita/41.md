A container holding $30\,\mathrm{mL}$ of liquid of density $1.2\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$ has total mass $86\,\mathrm{g}$. What is the empty container's mass?

- $50\,\mathrm{g}$
- $122\,\mathrm{g}$
- $36\,\mathrm{g}$
- $56\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

The liquid occupies $30\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ and has mass $1.2\cdot30=36\,\mathrm{g}$. The tare is what remains when this mass is subtracted from the total reading. Thus $86-36=50\,\mathrm{g}$. If the tare is $50\,\mathrm{g}$, adding the $36\,\mathrm{g}$ of liquid reproduces the stated weighing.
