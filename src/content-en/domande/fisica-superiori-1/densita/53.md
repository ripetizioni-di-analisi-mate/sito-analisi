An object of external volume $35\,\mathrm{cm}^3$ has a $10\,\mathrm{cm}^3$ cavity. The cavity is opened and filled with liquid of density $1.4\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. How much mass is added, neglecting the displaced air?

- $14\,\mathrm g$
- $49\,\mathrm g$
- $35\,\mathrm g$
- $\dfrac{50}{7}\,\mathrm g$

## Explanation

The liquid occupies the cavity. Multiply by that volume because the wall is not replaced.

$10\cdot 1.4=14\,\mathrm g$.
