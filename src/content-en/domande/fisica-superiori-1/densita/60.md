To make $50\,\mathrm{cm}^3$ of a $1.4\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$ mixture, use liquids at $1$ and $2\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. With additive volumes, how much of the denser liquid is used?

- $30\,\mathrm{cm}^3$
- $70\,\mathrm{cm}^3$
- $25\,\mathrm{cm}^3$
- $20\,\mathrm{cm}^3$

## Explanation

Starting with all the less dense liquid, each cubic centimetre replaced adds one gram. This builds the desired density.

$\frac{50\cdot \left(1.4-1\right)}{2-1}=20\,\mathrm{cm}^3$.
