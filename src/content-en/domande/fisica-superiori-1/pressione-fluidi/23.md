A pad presses on a surface with friction. Which quantity enters the pressure calculation on the flat face?

- The tangential friction component
- The pad's speed
- The normal component of contact force
- The magnitude of normal plus friction in all cases

## Explanation

The block can press into the plane and experience friction along the plane at the same time. Pressure measures just the force perpendicular to that plane, spread over the area of contact. Thus the magnitude of the resultant of normal force and friction is not the force to put in the numerator.
