Why is it a unit error to divide $40\,\mathrm N$ by $20\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and state $2\,\mathrm{Pa}$?

- To obtain pascals one must multiply pressure by area
- The numerical value two is in $\mathrm{N/cm^2}$
- The newton must be converted to kilograms
- Two pascals are two square metres per newton

## Explanation

Direct division gives $2\,\mathrm{N/cm^2}$. A square centimetre equals $10^{-4}\,\mathrm m^2$, hence $2\,\mathrm{N/cm^2}=20000\,\mathrm{N/m^2}=20000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Simply replacing newtons per square centimetre with pascals would leave the numerical value unchanged even though the reference area in the denominator changed.
