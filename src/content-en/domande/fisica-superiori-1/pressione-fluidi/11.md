In an ideal hydraulic press the pistons are at the same elevation. The small piston has area $2\,\mathrm{cm}^2$ and receives an additional force of $8\,\mathrm N$. The other piston has area $20\,\mathrm{cm}^2$. What additional force does the liquid exert on the larger piston?

- $8\,\mathrm N$
- $0.8\,\mathrm N$
- $80\,\mathrm N$
- $160\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The added load causes a pressure increase of $\dfrac{8}{2}\,\mathrm{N/cm^2}=4\,\mathrm{N/cm^2}$. The increase is transmitted to the large piston. Multiplying by $20\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ gives $80\,\mathrm N$. The output force is greater because that same pressure acts over ten times the area.
