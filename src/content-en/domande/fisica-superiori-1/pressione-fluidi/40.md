In water, one point is $7\,\mathrm m$ below the surface. Another point has $20\,\mathrm{kPa}$ lower pressure. If the gradient is $10\,\mathrm{kPa/m}$, what is the second depth?

- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The stated gradient gives two kilopascals per fifth of a metre. A drop of twenty kilopascals means rising two metres, so depth decreases.

$$
h_2=7\,\mathrm m-\frac{20\,\mathrm{kPa}}{10\,\mathrm{kPa/m}}=5\,\mathrm m.
$$
