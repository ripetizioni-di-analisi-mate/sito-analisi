The first layer contributes $5\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and pressure rises by $14\,\mathrm{kPa}$ from surface to bottom. What is the second layer's contribution?

- $5\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $14\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $19\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $9\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

Both increases add on the path to the bottom. Subtract the first increase from the total to obtain the missing one.

$$
\Delta p_2=(14-5)\,\mathrm{kPa}=9\,\mathrm{kPa}.
$$
