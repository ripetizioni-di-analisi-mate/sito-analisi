The first layer contributes $5\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and pressure rises by $14\,\mathrm{kPa}$ from surface to bottom. What is the second layer's contribution?

- $5\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $14\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $19\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $9\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

The path starts at the surface, crosses the first layer and then the second. The first accounts for five of the fourteen kilopascals of increase from the surface to the bottom. The remaining nine belong to the second segment. Both contributions are positive because each layer is traversed going down from the preceding level.

$$
\Delta p_2=(14-5)\,\mathrm{kPa}=9\,\mathrm{kPa}.
$$
