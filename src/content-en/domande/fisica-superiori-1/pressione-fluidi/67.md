What allows comparison of pressure at an oil-water interface in one arm with a water point at the same elevation in the other arm?

- Oil always having the same density as water
- Equal volumes of liquid
- Pressure continuity at the flat interface and the connected water
- The free surfaces having equal levels for different liquids

## Explanation

At a flat interface pressure is continuous across the boundary. In connected water the same level identifies another point at equal pressure.
