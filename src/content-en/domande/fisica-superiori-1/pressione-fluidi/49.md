An open vessel's gauge pressure uses atmosphere as reference. What is the reading exactly at the surface, neglecting surface tension?

- Zero
- Atmospheric pressure
- Bottom pressure
- A negative value equal to atmosphere

## Explanation

At the open surface the water and air have the same pressure at their boundary, neglecting surface tension. The sensor's reference is that same air, so the difference is zero. An absolute sensor at the same point would read atmospheric pressure, and a relative sensor deeper down would read a positive increase.
