An open vessel's gauge pressure uses atmosphere as reference. What is the reading exactly at the surface, neglecting surface tension?

- Zero
- Atmospheric pressure
- Bottom pressure
- A negative value equal to atmosphere

## Explanation

The interface is exposed to the reference pressure itself. The difference from that reference is zero.
