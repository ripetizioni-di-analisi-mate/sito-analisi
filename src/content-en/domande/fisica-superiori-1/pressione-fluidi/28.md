What is a pressure of $24500\,\mathrm{Pa}$ expressed in kilopascals?

- $245\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $2.45\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $0.0245\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $24.5\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

The prefix kilo stands for $10^3$, so $1\,\mathrm{kPa}=1000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. To convert from pascals to kilopascals one therefore divides by $1000$. It follows that
$$
24500\,\mathrm{Pa}=\frac{24500}{1000}\,\mathrm{kPa}=24.5\,\mathrm{kPa}.
$$
