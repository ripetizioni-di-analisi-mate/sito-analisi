A sensor gives $24500\,\mathrm{Pa}$ and the report requires kilopascals. What value should be written?

- $245\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $2.45\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $0.0245\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $24.5\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

A kilopascal is one thousand pascals. Divide the number by a thousand when using the larger unit.

$$
24500\,\mathrm{Pa}=24.5\,\mathrm{kPa}.
$$
