A sensor gives $24500\,\mathrm{Pa}$ and the report requires kilopascals. What value should be written?

- $245\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $2.45\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $0.0245\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $24.5\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

One kilopascal contains one thousand pascals. To express $24500\,\mathrm{Pa}$ in that unit, count the complete groups of one thousand and the fraction of a group left over. The result is $24.5\,\mathrm{kPa}$. The inverse conversion, multiplying by one thousand, recovers the original reading without changing the pressure.

$$
24500\,\mathrm{Pa}=24.5\,\mathrm{kPa}.
$$
