A barometer has residual pressure $2\,\mathrm{kPa}$ above its column. The contribution $\rho gh$ is $95\,\mathrm{kPa}$. What is external pressure?

- $93\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $95\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $190\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $97\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

Base pressure equals top pressure plus the increase along the column. The residual gas pressure is given and contributes to the result.

$$
p_{\rm est}=(2+95)\,\mathrm{kPa}=97\,\mathrm{kPa}.
$$
