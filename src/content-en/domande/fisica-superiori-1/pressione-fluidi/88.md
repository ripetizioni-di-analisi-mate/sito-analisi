A barometer has residual pressure $15\,\mathrm{kPa}$ above its column. The contribution $\rho gh$ is $80\,\mathrm{kPa}$. What is external pressure?

- $65\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $80\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $160\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $95\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

Start at the top, where there is already a pressure of $15\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Descending the column adds $80\,\mathrm{kPa}$. This reaches the height of the reservoir surface, where atmospheric pressure is $95\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Interpreting the reading as though there were a vacuum above the column would omit the $15\,\mathrm{kPa}$ from the gas.

$$
p_{\rm est}=(15+80)\,\mathrm{kPa}=95\,\mathrm{kPa}.
$$
