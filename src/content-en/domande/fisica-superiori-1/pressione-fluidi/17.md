A small flat wall element rotates around a fixed point in a resting liquid. What distinguishes local pressure from the force on a very small element?

- Pressure at the point stays the same while the normal force changes direction
- Pressure rotates like a vector and force stays fixed
- Both vanish on a vertical wall
- The force always points in the direction of gravity

## Explanation

Rotating the face about the same point preserves the depth of that point. Since the liquid is at rest, the pressure there has the same value for every orientation. The force changes direction because it is normal to the face. On a small element of fixed area its magnitude remains $pA$.
