A small flat wall element rotates around a fixed point in a resting liquid. What distinguishes local pressure from the force on a very small element?

- Pressure at the point stays the same while the normal force turns
- Pressure rotates like a vector and force stays fixed
- Both vanish on a vertical wall
- Force always points along gravity

## Explanation

Local pressure is a scalar. The force acts on the face and follows its normal as the face rotates.
