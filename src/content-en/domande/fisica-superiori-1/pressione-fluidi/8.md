From the surface to a point at $9\,\mathrm m$, pressure rises by $81000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$, what constant density does the liquid have?

- $90\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $9000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{900}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $900\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

Comparing the surface with the submerged point eliminates the initial pressure. The increase is $\rho gh$, so first divide by depth to find $9000\,\mathrm{Pa/m}$. Dividing that gradient by $10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$ gives $900\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. This density is lower than the usual density of water.
