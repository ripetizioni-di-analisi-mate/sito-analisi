What is the slope unit on a graph of pressure in pascals against depth in metres?

- $\mathrm{Pa\,m}$
- $\mathrm{m/Pa}$
- $\mathrm{Pa/m}$
- $\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

Slope is the difference of ordinates divided by the difference of abscissas. Here it is pressure per unit length.
