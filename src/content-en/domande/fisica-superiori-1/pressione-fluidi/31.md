What is the slope unit on a graph of pressure in pascals against depth in metres?

- $\mathrm{Pa\,m}$
- $\mathrm{m/Pa}$
- $\mathrm{Pa/m}$
- $\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The gradient is the pressure increase divided by the change in depth. The vertical axis is in pascals and the horizontal axis in metres, so the result is in $\mathrm{Pa/m}$. For example, a pressure increase of $6000\,\mathrm{Pa}$ over two metres gives a gradient of $3000\,\mathrm{Pa/m}$.
