Two water points are separated along an inclined $5\,\mathrm m$ pipe rising $3\,\mathrm m$ from A to B. With $\rho=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$, what is $p_A-p_B$?

- $30\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $-30\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $50\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $15\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

The pressure difference follows from the vertical separation along gravity. The diagonal length describes the pipe, but the three-metre rise is the elevation difference. A is deeper, so A minus B is positive.

$$
p_A-p_B=1000\cdot10\cdot3\,\mathrm{Pa}=30\,\mathrm{kPa}.
$$
