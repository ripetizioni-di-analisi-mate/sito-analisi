A pipe with several bends is full of water at rest. The points A, B and C of the pipe are at elevations of $2$, $5$ and $1\,\mathrm m$, respectively. At A the pressure equals atmospheric pressure, which is treated as constant. What is the sign of the gauge pressure at B and at C?

- Negative at B, positive at C
- Positive at both
- Positive at B, negative at C
- Negative at both

## Explanation

By Stevin's law, the pressure in a liquid at rest decreases going up and increases going down. Point B is higher than A, so the pressure at B is lower than atmospheric pressure and its gauge pressure is negative. Point C is lower than A, therefore the pressure at C is higher than atmospheric pressure and its gauge pressure is positive.
