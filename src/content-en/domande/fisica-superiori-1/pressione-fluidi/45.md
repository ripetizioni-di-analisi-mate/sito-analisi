A pipe with several bends is full of water at rest. The sensors at A, B and C are at elevations of $2$, $5$ and $1\,\mathrm m$, respectively. At A, the pressure equals atmospheric pressure, which is treated as constant. What is the sign of the gauge pressure relative to the atmosphere at the other two points?

- Negative at B, positive at C
- Positive at both
- Positive at B, negative at C
- Negative at both

## Explanation

Pressure decreases with elevation in the liquid. B is above A, so its pressure is below atmospheric pressure. C is below A, so its pressure is higher than atmospheric pressure. The sign at each sensor follows from its final elevation relative to A.
