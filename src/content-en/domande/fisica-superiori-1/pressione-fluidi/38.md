Two liquids at the same $g$ are plotted as absolute pressure versus depth from the same atmosphere. Where can the lines coincide if their densities differ?

- At every depth
- Only at the surface
- Always at one metre
- Never, not even at the surface

## Explanation

They have the same intercept and different slopes. At any positive depth the denser liquid has the larger hydrostatic term.
