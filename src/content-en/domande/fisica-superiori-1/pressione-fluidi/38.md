Two liquids at the same $g$ are plotted as absolute pressure versus depth from the same atmosphere. Where can the lines coincide if their densities differ?

- At every depth
- Only at the surface
- Always at one metre
- Never, not even at the surface

## Explanation

At the surface both pressures are atmospheric, so the lines share the intercept at zero depth. Below the surface they rise with different gradients. Starting from the same point, two lines with distinct gradients stay different at every positive depth. The upper line corresponds to the denser liquid.
