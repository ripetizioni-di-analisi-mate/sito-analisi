One arm is open to atmosphere at $100\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and the water surface on the gas side is $0.2\,\mathrm m$ lower. At gradient $10\,\mathrm{kPa/m}$, what is the gas's absolute pressure?

- $98\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $102\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $2\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $100\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

The lower surface identifies the gas at higher pressure. Add two hydrostatic kilopascals to the known atmosphere.

$$
p_{\rm gas}=100\,\mathrm{kPa}+(10\,\mathrm{kPa/m})(0.2\,\mathrm m)=102\,\mathrm{kPa}.
$$
