A flat plate rests on two pads of area $0.010$ and $0.030\,\mathrm m^2$. The average contact pressures on the two pads are equal and the total normal load is $240\,\mathrm N$. How much of the load does the smaller pad support?

- $120\,\mathrm N$
- $180\,\mathrm N$
- $60\,\mathrm N$
- $240\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Equal pressure on the two pads means that the load on each pad is proportional to its area. The smaller pad accounts for one quarter of the total area, so it supports one quarter of $240\,\mathrm N$, or $60\,\mathrm N$. Both pads exert an average pressure of $6000\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
