A liquid with $\rho=900\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ shows a pressure increase of $18\,\mathrm{kPa}$ over a descent of $4\,\mathrm m$. What value of $g$ is implied?

- $5\,\mathrm{N/kg}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{N/kg}$
- $50\,\mathrm{N/kg}$

## Explanation

The measured slope is density times field strength. Divide the slope by density to isolate the local field.

$$
g=\frac{18000}{900\cdot4}\,\mathrm{N/kg}=5\,\mathrm{N/kg}.
$$
