A tank contains oil over water. The combined thickness is $2.0\,\mathrm m$, and the pressure at the bottom exceeds the surface pressure by $17.6\,\mathrm{kPa}$. The gradient is $7\,\mathrm{kPa/m}$ in oil and $10\,\mathrm{kPa/m}$ in water. How thick is the oil layer?

- $1.2\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $0.8\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $1.76\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

With two metres of water the increase would be $20\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Replacing one metre of water with one metre of oil reduces the increase by $3\,\mathrm{kPa}$. A reduction of $2.4\,\mathrm{kPa}$ therefore requires $0.8\,\mathrm m$ of oil. Check that $7\cdot0.8+10\cdot1.2=17.6$ in kilopascals.
