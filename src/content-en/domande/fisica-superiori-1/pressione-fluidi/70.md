One arm is sealed above the liquid and its surface pressure rises. The other stays open. Which free surface can fall when the same liquid reaches equilibrium?

- The open arm, because it is at lower pressure
- The one under the higher surface pressure
- Both must fall by equal amounts
- Neither, unequal surface pressures always give equal levels

## Explanation

At a common elevation the pressures must agree. The side with higher surface pressure therefore needs a shorter column above that level.
