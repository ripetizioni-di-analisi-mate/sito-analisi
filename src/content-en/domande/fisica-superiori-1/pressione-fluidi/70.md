Two communicating arms contain the same liquid with both surfaces initially at the same level. One arm is sealed and the pressure above its surface increases, while the other stays open. The liquid volume remains constant. Which surface falls?

- The open arm, because it is at lower pressure
- The surface in the sealed arm, because its surface pressure is higher
- Both must fall by equal amounts
- Neither, unequal surface pressures always give equal levels

## Explanation

To compare the branches, choose a height in the liquid below both surfaces. Pressure must be the same there. The starting pressure in the closed branch has increased, so a shorter liquid column is needed to compensate for it. The free surface of that branch is the lower one.
