A large piston needs to rise $2\,\mathrm{cm}$. Its area is eight times the small piston's. What small-piston stroke displaces the same volume?

- $16\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The volume displaced by the small piston must equal the volume made available by the large piston. For every centimetre of travel, the large piston makes room for eight times the volume displaced by one centimetre of travel at the small piston. The small piston therefore needs $16\,\mathrm{cm}$ of travel to match the large piston's two centimetres.

$$
x_1=\frac{A_2}{A_1}x_2=8\cdot2\,\mathrm{cm}=16\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
