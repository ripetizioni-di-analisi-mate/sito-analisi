A large piston needs to rise $2\,\mathrm{cm}$. Its area is eight times the small piston's. What small-piston stroke displaces the same volume?

- $16\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Displaced volume is conserved. A cross-section that is eight times smaller must travel eight times farther.

$$
x_1=\frac{A_2}{A_1}x_2=8\cdot2\,\mathrm{cm}=16\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
