Gauge pressure rises although the absolute sensor at the same point stays constant. How can the reference pressure change?

- It decreases
- It increases
- It is always unchanged
- It must drop to zero

## Explanation

The relative reading subtracts the reference pressure from the absolute pressure. The absolute value stays fixed, so increasing the difference requires a decrease in the reference. If the reference falls by $2\,\mathrm{kPa}$, the relative reading increases by exactly $2\,\mathrm{kPa}$ even though the actual pressure at the point stays the same.
