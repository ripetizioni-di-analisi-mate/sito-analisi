Gauge pressure rises although the absolute sensor at the same point stays constant. How can the reference pressure change?

- It decreases
- It increases
- It is always unchanged
- It must drop to zero

## Explanation

Gauge pressure is absolute pressure minus reference. Subtracting a smaller reference increases the difference at fixed absolute pressure.
