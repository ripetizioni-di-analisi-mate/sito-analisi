Before a change, two points in a press liquid are at different elevations. After four kilopascals are added at the surface, what happens to their hydrostatic difference if elevations stay fixed?

- It grows by four kilopascals
- It falls by four kilopascals
- It stays unchanged
- It always disappears

## Explanation

The imposed change is shared, whereas the difference $\rho g\Delta h$ depends on the unchanged vertical separation.
