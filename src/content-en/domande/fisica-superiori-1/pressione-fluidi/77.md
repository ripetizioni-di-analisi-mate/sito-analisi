A tank contains oil over water and pressure is measured at a point still in the oil. How much water enters the hydrostatic sum from the surface to that point?

- None, because the path stops above the water
- All water down to the bottom
- Always half the water thickness
- The water portion set by total volume without using heights

## Explanation

Pressure increase is counted along the vertical path from surface to point. Deeper layers are not on that path.
