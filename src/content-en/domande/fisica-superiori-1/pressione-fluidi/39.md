The density of a uniform liquid can be found from the slope of pressure against depth. To estimate that slope, pressure is measured at two depths. Depth uncertainty is negligible and each pressure reading has the same maximum uncertainty, and their errors can have opposite signs. Which pair of depths gives the smallest maximum uncertainty in the estimate?

- $0.40$ and $0.90\,\mathrm m$
- $1.40$ and $2.40\,\mathrm m$
- $0.40$ and $2.40\,\mathrm m$
- $0.90$ and $2.40\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

In the worst case, the magnitudes of the two pressure errors add. This margin is the same for every pair. The slope calculation divides it by the vertical separation, so its effect on the estimate is smallest at the greatest separation of $2\,\mathrm m$.
