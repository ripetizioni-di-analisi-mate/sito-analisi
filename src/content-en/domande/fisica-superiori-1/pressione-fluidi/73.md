A barrier cuts off every connection between two water vessels. Which communicating-vessel condition has failed?

- The continuous liquid connection between the two arms
- The existence of gravity
- The constant density in each vessel
- The definition of absolute pressure

## Explanation

Equal pressure at the same elevation refers to a single connected body of fluid. With separated volumes, their surface conditions may differ.
