A barrier cuts off every connection between two water vessels. Which communicating-vessel condition has failed?

- The continuous liquid connection between the two arms
- The existence of gravity
- The constant density in each vessel
- The definition of absolute pressure

## Explanation

The barrier interrupts the continuous liquid path between the vessels. From that point equal heights in different vessels need not have equal pressures. For example, with both open to the atmosphere, adding water to only one gives different depths below the respective surfaces at the same height.
