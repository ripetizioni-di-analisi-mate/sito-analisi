The liquid surfaces in a U-tube manometer are at the same elevation. What does this tell us about the gas pressures in the two arms? The liquid is uniform and gas density is negligible.

- Both gases are under vacuum
- The right side has double the pressure
- The gas pressures are equal
- The liquid is weightless

## Explanation

The two surfaces are at the same height in a connected liquid, so the pressures applied by the two gases are equal. The level difference is zero, as is the pressure difference that the instrument indicates. Both absolute gas pressures may be positive because the instrument measures only their difference.
