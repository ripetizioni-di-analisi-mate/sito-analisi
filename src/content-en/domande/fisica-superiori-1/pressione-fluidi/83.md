What do equal levels in a U-tube manometer indicate if its liquid is the same and gas densities are negligible?

- Both gases are under vacuum
- The right side has double the pressure
- The gas pressures are equal
- The liquid is weightless

## Explanation

Pressure in the connected liquid is equal at equal elevation. Both gases touch surfaces at that elevation, so their pressures are equal.
