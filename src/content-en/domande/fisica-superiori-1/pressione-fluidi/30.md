Two instruments measure the pressure at the same submerged point in a sealed tank. The pressure is $24\,\mathrm{kPa}$ relative to the outside air and $18\,\mathrm{kPa}$ relative to the gas above the liquid. How does the gas pressure compare with the pressure of the outside air?

- The gas pressure is $6\,\mathrm{kPa}$ below the outside air pressure
- The gas pressure is $6\,\mathrm{kPa}$ above the outside air pressure
- The gas pressure is $18\,\mathrm{kPa}$ above the outside air pressure
- The gas pressure is $42\,\mathrm{kPa}$ above the outside air pressure

## Explanation

Both instruments measure the same physical pressure by subtracting a different reference. Subtracting the second reading from the first cancels the pressure at the point, leaving $p_{\mathrm{gas}}-p_{\mathrm{atm}}=(24-18)\,\mathrm{kPa}=6\,\mathrm{kPa}$. The smaller reading corresponds to the higher reference pressure.
