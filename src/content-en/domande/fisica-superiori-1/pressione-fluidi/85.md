A denser manometer liquid is used for the same gas pressure difference. How does the level difference change?

- It decreases
- It increases
- It stays unchanged
- It vanishes for any higher density

## Explanation

Level difference is pressure difference divided by $\rho g$. Greater density enlarges the denominator and reduces the height needed.
