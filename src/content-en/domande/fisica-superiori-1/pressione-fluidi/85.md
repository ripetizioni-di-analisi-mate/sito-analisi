A U-tube manometer must measure pressure differences up to $3\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Its scale ends at a level difference of $0.25\,\mathrm m$. Neglecting gas density and using $g=10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$, what is the minimum liquid density?

- $1200\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.
- $750\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.
- $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.
- $1500\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.

## Explanation

At the threshold the maximum level difference supports exactly $3000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Solving $\rho g\Delta h=3000\,\mathrm{Pa}$ gives $\dfrac{3000}{10\cdot0.25}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}=1200\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. A less dense liquid would need a greater level difference for the same pressure and would exceed the end of the scale.
