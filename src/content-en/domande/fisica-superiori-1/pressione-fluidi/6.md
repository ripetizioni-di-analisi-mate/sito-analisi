A water tank is open to atmospheric pressure $100000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. At depth $8\,\mathrm m$, what is the absolute pressure? Use $\rho=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$.

- $80000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $180000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $20000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $108000\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

Add the $80000\,\mathrm{Pa}$ from the water column to the surface pressure. The total is $180000\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
