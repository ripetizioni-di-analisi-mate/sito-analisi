A water tank is open to atmospheric pressure $100000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. At depth $8\,\mathrm m$, what is the absolute pressure? Use $\rho=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$.

- $80000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $180000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $20000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $108000\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

At the free surface the liquid is already at the atmospheric pressure of $100000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Going down by $8\,\mathrm m$ adds $1000\cdot10\cdot8\,\mathrm{Pa}=80000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Their sum, $180000\,\mathrm{Pa}$, is the absolute pressure. A reading relative to the surface would contain only the increase caused by the water column.
