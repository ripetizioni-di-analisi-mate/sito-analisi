Two level pistons have different diameters. Force advantage uses their areas. What is wrong with using the diameter ratio directly?

- Use only the reciprocal of the smaller diameter
- Area is independent of diameter
- The force ratio is always one
- Circular pistons require the square of the diameter ratio

## Explanation

The common $\pi/4$ factor in the areas cancels, leaving the squared diameter ratio.
