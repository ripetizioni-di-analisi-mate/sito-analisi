In a liquid with $\rho=800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ the pressure increase from the surface is measured as $72000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$, what is the depth?

- $90\,\mathrm m$
- $7200\,\mathrm m$
- $9\,\mathrm m$
- $0.9\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Each metre of that liquid adds $8000\,\mathrm{Pa}$ to the surface pressure because its gradient is density times field strength. To recover depth, divide the measured pressure increase by the amount gained in one metre. This gives $h=\dfrac{72000}{8000}\,\mathrm m=9\,\mathrm m$, without needing the absolute surface pressure.
