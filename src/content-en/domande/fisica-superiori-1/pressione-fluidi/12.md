In an ideal press at the same elevation, the large piston has four times the area of the small one. What added force on the small piston gives $96\,\mathrm N$ of added force on the large piston?

- $96\,\mathrm N$
- $384\,\mathrm N$
- $48\,\mathrm N$
- $24\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

At equal added pressure, forces are proportional to area. The small piston needs $96/4=24\,\mathrm N$.
