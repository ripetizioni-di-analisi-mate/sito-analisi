In an ideal press at the same elevation, the large piston has four times the area of the small one. What added force on the small piston gives $96\,\mathrm N$ of added force on the large piston?

- $96\,\mathrm N$
- $384\,\mathrm N$
- $48\,\mathrm N$
- $24\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

At the same height, compare the forces due to the same change in pressure. The large piston has four times the area, so reaching $96\,\mathrm N$ requires a quarter as much force at the small piston. This is $\dfrac{96}{4}\,\mathrm N=24\,\mathrm N$. Multiplying the input force by the area ratio recovers the desired output.
