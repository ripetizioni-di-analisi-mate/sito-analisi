A water manometer is connected to two gases. Surface A is $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ lower than B. With $g=10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$ and $\rho=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$, how do their pressures compare?

- A exceeds B by $1200\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- B exceeds A by $1200\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- A exceeds B by $12\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- They are equal

## Explanation

Start at surface B and descend in the liquid to elevation A. Pressure rises along this path, so gas A must exert the higher pressure. Converting twelve centimetres to metres gives twelve hundred pascals of difference. The comparison uses the full separation of the surfaces.

$$
p_A=p_B+\rho g\Delta h=p_B+1000\cdot10\cdot0.12\,\mathrm{Pa}.
$$
