A press piston of area $3\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ receives added force $9\,\mathrm N$. What pressure increase is transmitted in pascals?

- $3\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $30000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $3000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $270000\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The input area is $3\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm m^2$. Dividing force by area gives $30000\,\mathrm{N/m^2}$, or $30000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Alternatively, nine newtons over three square centimetres gives $3\,\mathrm{N/cm^2}$, the same pressure. In the Pascal model this increase is transmitted through the liquid, rather than the nine-newton force itself.

$$
\Delta p=\frac{9}{3\cdot10^{-4}}\,\mathrm{Pa}=30000\,\mathrm{Pa}.
$$
