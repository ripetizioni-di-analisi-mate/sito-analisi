A press piston of area $3\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ receives added force $9\,\mathrm N$. What pressure increase is transmitted in pascals?

- $3\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $30000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $3000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $270000\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

Divide the input force by its area in square metres. Pascal's principle transmits that value through the incompressible fluid.

$$
\Delta p=\frac{9}{3\cdot10^{-4}}\,\mathrm{Pa}=30000\,\mathrm{Pa}.
$$
