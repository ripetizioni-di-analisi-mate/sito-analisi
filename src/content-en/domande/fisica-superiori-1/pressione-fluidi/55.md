The larger piston has greater force. What remains equal between ideal level pistons after adding a load?

- The force increase
- Piston displacement
- The pressure increase
- Piston area

## Explanation

The pressure change introduced by the load is transmitted through the liquid and has the same value at both pistons. Multiplying it by the area gives the added force. The large piston can thus have a greater force while having exactly the same added load per unit area.
