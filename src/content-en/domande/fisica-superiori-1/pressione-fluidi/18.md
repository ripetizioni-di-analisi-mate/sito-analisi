A sensor connected to a piston has the correct scale and a constant zero offset. It reads $3\,\mathrm{kPa}$ under a normal load of $4\,\mathrm N$ and $9\,\mathrm{kPa}$ under $16\,\mathrm N$. What is the piston area?

- $2\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $20\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $15\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $40\,\mathrm{cm^2}$

## Explanation

Subtracting the readings eliminates the zero offset. The increase of $12\,\mathrm N$ produces a change of $6000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Dividing the change in force by the change in pressure gives $A=0.0020\,\mathrm m^2=20\,\mathrm{cm^2}$.
