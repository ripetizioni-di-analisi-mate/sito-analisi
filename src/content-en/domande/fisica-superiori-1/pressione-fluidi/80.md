Two vessels have the same layer thicknesses of two liquids and their bottom pressures are compared. In a hypothetical static model keeping the layers separate, does changing their order alter the total hydrostatic increment?

- Yes, the denser liquid loses its contribution if it is on top
- Yes, it always doubles
- No, because only the lower layer matters
- No, both contributions add in either order

## Explanation

The bottom sum is $\rho_1gh_1+\rho_2gh_2$ regardless of order. The depth graph, however, would have its slopes in a different order.
