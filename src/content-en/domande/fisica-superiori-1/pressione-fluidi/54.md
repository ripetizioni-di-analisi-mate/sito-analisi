In a closed circuit with incompressible liquid, an input piston of area $4\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ moves in by $9\,\mathrm{cm}$. Two output pistons of areas $6$ and $12\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ are attached to a crossbar that gives them equal travel. How far does each move?

- $6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $3\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The input displaces $36\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. If the common output travel is $x$, the two pistons together accommodate $(6+12)x\,\mathrm{cm^3}$, with $x$ in centimetres. Equating the volumes gives $x=2$. The pistons move the same distance, and the larger piston accommodates twice the volume.
