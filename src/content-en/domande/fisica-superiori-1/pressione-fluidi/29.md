Which equivalence expresses the pascal in terms of newtons?

- $1\,\mathrm{Pa}=1\,\mathrm{N/m^2}$
- $1\,\mathrm{Pa}=1\,\mathrm N$
- $1\,\mathrm{Pa}=1\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $1\,\mathrm{Pa}=1\,\mathrm{m^2/N}$

## Explanation

The unit comes from normal force divided by an area. Force is in newtons and area in square metres.
