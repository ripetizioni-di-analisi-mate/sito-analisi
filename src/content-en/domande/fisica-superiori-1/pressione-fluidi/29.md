Which equivalence expresses the pascal in terms of newtons?

- $1\,\mathrm{Pa}=1\,\mathrm{N/m^2}$
- $1\,\mathrm{Pa}=1\,\mathrm N$
- $1\,\mathrm{Pa}=1\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $1\,\mathrm{Pa}=1\,\mathrm{m^2/N}$

## Explanation

Uniform pressure is the magnitude of the normal force divided by the area. Force is measured in newtons and area in square metres, so $1\,\mathrm{Pa}=1\,\mathrm{N/m^2}$. For example, one newton uniformly spread over one square metre gives one pascal. The same force across half a square metre gives two pascals.
