A vertical plate $0.40\,\mathrm m$ wide extends from $0.30$ to $0.90\,\mathrm m$ below the water surface. The air pressure on the other face equals the pressure at the water surface. With a hydrostatic gradient of $10\,\mathrm{kPa/m}$, what is the magnitude of the resultant of the pressure forces on the two faces?

- $720\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $2160\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $1440\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $2880\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

The air exerts the same pressure on the water surface and on the dry face. Its pressure cancels when the two face pressures are subtracted. The pressure difference varies linearly from $3$ to $9\,\mathrm{kPa}$. The width is constant, so every pair of symmetric strips has a mean pressure of $6\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Multiplying by the area $0.40\cdot0.60\,\mathrm m^2$ gives $1440\,\mathrm N$.
