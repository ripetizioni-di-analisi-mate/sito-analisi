Gravity halves at fixed external pressure and liquid density. If an ideal barometer initially read $0.7\,\mathrm m$, what height does it now read?

- $0.35\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.7\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.1\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The product of field strength and height must stay unchanged. Halving the field requires twice the height to support the given pressure.

$$
h_2=h_1\frac{g_1}{g_2}=0.7\cdot2\,\mathrm m=1.4\,\mathrm m.
$$
