Gravity halves at fixed external pressure and liquid density. If an ideal barometer initially read $0.7\,\mathrm m$, what height does it now read?

- $0.35\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.7\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.1\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

With the field strength halved, a metre of the same column supports half as much pressure as before. The outside pressure is unchanged, so twice the height is required. The height increases from $0.7$ to $1.4\,\mathrm m$. The product of the new height and the new field strength equals its original value.

$$
h_2=h_1\frac{g_1}{g_2}=0.7\cdot2\,\mathrm m=1.4\,\mathrm m.
$$
