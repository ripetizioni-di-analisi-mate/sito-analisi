At uniform equal pressure, which face receives a greater force, one of $2\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ or one of $6\,\mathrm{cm^2}$?

- The $2\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ face, with three times the force
- The forces are equal
- Equal pressure means opposite forces
- The $6\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ face, with three times the force

## Explanation

The two faces have the same load per unit area, so force increases in proportion to available area. The $6\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ face is three times as large as the $2\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ face. It therefore receives three times the force despite being under exactly the same pressure.
