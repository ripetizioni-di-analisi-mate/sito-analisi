Two arms contain water with gradient $10\,\mathrm{kPa/m}$. Surface pressure above A is $3\,\mathrm{kPa}$ greater than above B. What level difference develops?

- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{10}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $30\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

B requires a taller column to balance A's higher surface pressure. Three kilopascals correspond to three decimetres of water.

$$
\Delta h=\frac{3\,\mathrm{kPa}}{10\,\mathrm{kPa/m}}=0.3\,\mathrm m.
$$
