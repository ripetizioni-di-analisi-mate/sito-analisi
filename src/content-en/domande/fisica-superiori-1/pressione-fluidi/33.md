Four absolute-pressure sensors in a water tank read $105$, $115$, $120$ and $135\,\mathrm{kPa}$ at depths of $0.50$, $1.50$, $2.50$ and $3.50\,\mathrm m$, respectively. The water is at rest, the gradient is $10\,\mathrm{kPa/m}$ and exactly one sensor is out of calibration. At what depth is that sensor?

- $2.50\,\mathrm m$
- $0.50\,\mathrm m$
- $1.50\,\mathrm m$
- $3.50\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Subtracting the hydrostatic contribution from each reading gives the same surface pressure of $100\,\mathrm{kPa}$ for three sensors. At $2.50\,\mathrm m$, the calculation gives $120-25=95$ in kilopascals. At that depth the reading consistent with the other three is $125\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
