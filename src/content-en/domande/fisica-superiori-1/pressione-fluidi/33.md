Two points in oil of density $800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ differ by $24\,\mathrm{kPa}$ in pressure. With $g=10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$, what is their vertical separation?

- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.003\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.92\,\mathrm{m}$
- $30\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Subtracting the pressures removes their common surface value. Each metre adds eight kilopascals, so twenty-four kilopascals correspond to three metres. The graph shows one pair of depths separated by this amount. The measurement does not specify the individual depths.

$$
\Delta h=\frac{24000\,\mathrm{Pa}}{800\cdot10\,\mathrm{Pa/m}}=3\,\mathrm m.
$$
