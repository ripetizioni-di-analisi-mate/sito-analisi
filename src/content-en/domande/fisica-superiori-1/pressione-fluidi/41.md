When deriving the law from an imaginary column with vertical sides, what contribution from the sides is absent from the vertical balance?

- The vertical component of side pressure forces
- Force at the bottom
- The column weight
- Force on the upper surface

## Explanation

The forces from the liquid on a wall are normal to the surface. Since the sides of the imaginary volume are vertical, their normals are horizontal and the side forces have zero vertical component. The vertical balance therefore contains the forces on the two bases and the column's weight.
