In an ideal hydraulic model the large piston gives more force and travels less far. How do the force-times-displacement products compare for constant added forces?

- The large product is always double
- They are equal without losses
- The small product is always greater
- Both are zero because fluid is incompressible

## Explanation

For each piston the force times the stroke is the pressure change times the displaced volume. The liquid transmits the same pressure increase, and the displaced volume is shared because the liquid is incompressible. The products therefore have equal magnitudes. The larger force is offset by the shorter stroke.
