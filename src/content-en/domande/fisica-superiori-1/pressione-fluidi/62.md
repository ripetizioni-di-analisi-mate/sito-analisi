In an ideal hydraulic model the large piston gives more force and travels less far. How do the force-times-displacement products compare for constant added forces?

- The large product is always double
- They are equal without losses
- The small product is always greater
- Both are zero because fluid is incompressible

## Explanation

The force ratio equals the area ratio. The displacement ratio is its reciprocal, so the ratios cancel in the product.
