A pressure is $9\,\mathrm{N/cm^2}$. What is it in pascals?

- $900\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $0.0009\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $9\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $90000\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

A square centimetre contains two factors of length and equals $10^{-4}\,\mathrm m^2$. This unit is in the denominator of pressure, so substitution gives $\dfrac{9}{10^{-4}}\,\mathrm{N/m^2}=90000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. The number increases because it now expresses the force corresponding to a much larger reference area.
