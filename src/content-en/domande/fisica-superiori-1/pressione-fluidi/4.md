A pressure is $9\,\mathrm{N/cm^2}$. What is it in pascals?

- $900\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $0.0009\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $9\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $90000\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

One square centimetre is $10^{-4}\,\mathrm m^2$ and is in the denominator. The result is $9\cdot10^4\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
