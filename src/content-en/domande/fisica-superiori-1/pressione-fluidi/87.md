If an ideal barometer is tilted, what length determines the atmospheric balance?

- The length along the tube
- Tube diameter
- The vertical height of the column
- Horizontal distance from the reservoir

## Explanation

Pressure depends on the weight above a level, so the equation uses the vertical distance between the top of the column and the reservoir surface. If the column extended one metre along a tube inclined at $30^\circ$ to the horizontal, that would correspond to half a vertical metre. Tilting changes the distance along the tube for the same difference in height.
