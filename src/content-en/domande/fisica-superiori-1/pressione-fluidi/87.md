If an ideal barometer is tilted, what length determines the atmospheric balance?

- The length along the tube
- Tube diameter
- The vertical height of the column
- Horizontal distance from the reservoir

## Explanation

In $\rho gh$ height measures separation along gravity. A tilted tube can have a longer column at the same vertical height.
