The circular pistons in an ideal press have radii $8$ and $16\,\mathrm{cm}$. At equal elevations, what larger-to-smaller force ratio gives the same pressure change?

- $4$
- $2$
- $0.25$
- $8$

## Explanation

The common pressure rise cancels in the force ratio. The remaining area ratio is $\dfrac{\pi(16\,\mathrm{cm})^2}{\pi(8\,\mathrm{cm})^2}=4$. The radii are squared because they measure lengths, whereas pressure acts on surfaces. Taking the linear ratio would underestimate the force multiplication by a factor of two.
