A membrane has the same uniform pressure on both sides. What resultant force follows?

- Twice either pressure force
- Zero, because the two contributions oppose each other
- A force always directed inward
- A force numerically equal to the pressure in pascals

## Explanation

Each face receives a force equal to pressure multiplied by its area. The two areas match and the pressures agree, so the magnitudes are equal. The face normals point in opposite directions. The forces cancel in the resultant even though each has a nonzero magnitude.
