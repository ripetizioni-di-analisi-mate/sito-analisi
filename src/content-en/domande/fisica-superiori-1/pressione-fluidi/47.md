A gauge reads $+18\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and atmospheric pressure is $99\,\mathrm{kPa}$. What would an absolute sensor show at the same point?

- $81\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $18\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $117\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $99\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

The positive sign means the gas exceeds atmospheric pressure by eighteen kilopascals. Add the difference to the reference to obtain absolute pressure.

$$
p_{\rm ass}=99\,\mathrm{kPa}+18\,\mathrm{kPa}=117\,\mathrm{kPa}.
$$
