A gauge reads $+18\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and atmospheric pressure is $99\,\mathrm{kPa}$. What would an absolute sensor show at the same point?

- $81\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $18\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $117\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $99\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

The relative reading is positive, so the point is $18\,\mathrm{kPa}$ above the surrounding pressure. The absolute sensor is referenced to a vacuum and must also include the atmosphere's $99\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Subtracting $99\,\mathrm{kPa}$ converts the result back to the given relative reading.

$$
p_{\rm ass}=99\,\mathrm{kPa}+18\,\mathrm{kPa}=117\,\mathrm{kPa}.
$$
