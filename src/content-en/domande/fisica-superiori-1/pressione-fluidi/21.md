A flat base has regions of area $0.03$ and $0.01\,\mathrm m^2$. Their respective uniform pressures are $2000$ and $6000\,\mathrm{Pa}$, acting the same way. What is the average pressure on the base?

- $3000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $4000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $8000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $2000\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

In each region its own area links force and pressure. The forces are both sixty newtons and point the same way. Divide the total force of one hundred and twenty newtons by total area for the mean pressure. It is closer to two thousand pascals, the pressure on the larger region.

$$
p_{\rm media}=\frac{2000\cdot0.03+6000\cdot0.01}{0.04}\,\mathrm{Pa}=3000\,\mathrm{Pa}.
$$
