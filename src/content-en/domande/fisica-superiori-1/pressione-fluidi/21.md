Two adjacent regions on the same face of a plate are under uniform pressures of $2$ and $8\,\mathrm{kPa}$. The required average pressure across the entire face is $6\,\mathrm{kPa}$. What fraction of the area must be under the pressure of $8\,\mathrm{kPa}$?

- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

If the whole face were exposed to the lower pressure, the average would be $2\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Applying the higher pressure over a fraction $x$ of the area adds $6x\,\mathrm{kPa}$ to the average. To reach $6\,\mathrm{kPa}$, an increase of $4\,\mathrm{kPa}$ is needed, so $x=\dfrac{2}{3}$.
