Atmospheric pressure falls at an ideal barometer. The liquid density and the gravitational field stay constant. How does the column height change?

- It decreases
- It increases
- It stays unchanged
- It decreases only in a tube of constant cross-section

## Explanation

The column supports the difference between atmospheric pressure and the negligible top pressure. If atmospheric pressure falls at fixed density and field strength, the column must be shorter. For example, a pressure one tenth lower requires a height one tenth lower because the hydrostatic gradient stays the same.
