Atmospheric pressure falls at an ideal barometer with unchanged liquid density. What happens to column height?

- It decreases
- It increases
- It stays unchanged
- It becomes the tube diameter

## Explanation

The balance gives $h=p_{\rm atm}/(\rho g)$. A decrease in the numerator with fixed denominator decreases the height.
