What is the reference of a sensor that reads zero when exposed to an ideal vacuum?

- The pressure just outside the container
- Absolute pressure with zero at vacuum
- The bottom of the liquid
- Sea level as zero elevation

## Explanation

An absolute scale takes vacuum as zero. A relative scale starts at the chosen reference pressure.
