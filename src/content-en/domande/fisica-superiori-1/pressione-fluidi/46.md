What is the reference of a sensor that reads zero when exposed to an ideal vacuum?

- The pressure just outside the container
- Absolute pressure with zero at vacuum
- The bottom of the liquid
- Sea level as zero elevation

## Explanation

In an ideal vacuum the absolute pressure is zero. This sensor therefore uses a vacuum as its zero and gives an absolute reading. A sensor zeroed at atmospheric pressure would instead give a negative vacuum reading, whose magnitude equals the atmospheric pressure taken as its reference.
