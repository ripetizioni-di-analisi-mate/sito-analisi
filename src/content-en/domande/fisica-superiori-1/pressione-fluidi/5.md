In water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ at depth $8\,\mathrm m$, what is the pressure relative to the surface? Take $g=10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$.

- $80000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $8000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $180000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $0.0008\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The question uses the surface as its reference, so only the pressure added by the column enters. In water the gradient is $1000\cdot10\,\mathrm{Pa/m}=10000\,\mathrm{Pa/m}$. The vertical depth of $8\,\mathrm m$ gives $80000\,\mathrm{Pa}$ above the surface pressure. The absolute pressure would also require the pressure acting on the surface.
