In water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ at depth $8\,\mathrm m$, what is the pressure relative to the surface? Take $g=10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$.

- $80000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $8000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $180000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $0.0008\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The increase from the surface is the hydrostatic term alone, $1000\cdot10\cdot8=80000\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
