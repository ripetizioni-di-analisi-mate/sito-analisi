When can one constant slope $\rho g$ be used for the entire pressure-versus-depth graph?

- Whenever the vessels are cylindrical
- When fluid and density change halfway down
- When density and field are uniform through the resting liquid interval
- Only if absolute pressure is zero at every depth

## Explanation

For a single straight line, every metre of depth must add the same pressure increase. This holds for a uniform liquid in a constant gravitational field. Two layers with different densities in the same field each have their own increase per metre, so the gradient changes at the interface.
