When can one constant slope $\rho g$ be used for the entire pressure-versus-depth graph?

- Whenever the vessels are cylindrical
- When fluid and density change halfway down
- When density and field are uniform through the resting liquid interval
- Only if absolute pressure is zero at every depth

## Explanation

The height coefficient is field times density. If those quantities change along the path, the slope is no longer a single value.
