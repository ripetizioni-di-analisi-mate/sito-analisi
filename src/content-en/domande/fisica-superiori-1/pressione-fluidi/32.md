When measuring deeper in the same uniform liquid with unchanged surface pressure, how does the point move on a graph of absolute pressure against depth?

- Right and downward
- Vertically at the same depth
- Onto a different horizontal line
- Right and upward along the same line

## Explanation

Depth and pressure rise together with positive slope $\rho g$. The unchanged conditions give the same line.
