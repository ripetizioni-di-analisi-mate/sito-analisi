A normal force of $160\,\mathrm N$ is uniformly distributed over $0.02\,\mathrm m^2$. What is the pressure?

- $8000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $8\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $3.2\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $80\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The $160\,\mathrm N$ force is already perpendicular to the face, so all of it contributes to the pressure. Divide it by $0.02\,\mathrm m^2$ to get the load per unit area. The result is $8000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Multiplying by the same area recovers the given $160\,\mathrm N$.
