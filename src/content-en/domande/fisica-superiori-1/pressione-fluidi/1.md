A normal force of $160\,\mathrm N$ is uniformly distributed over $0.02\,\mathrm m^2$. What is the pressure?

- $8000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $8\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $3.2\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $80\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

Divide force by area. The ratio $160/0.02=8000$ is measured in pascals.
