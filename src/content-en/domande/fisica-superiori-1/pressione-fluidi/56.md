The large piston is replaced by one of double the area while the small piston and input load stay fixed. How does the pressure increase change in the ideal press?

- It doubles
- It halves
- It quadruples
- It stays unchanged

## Explanation

The small piston's force and area are fixed, so their ratio is unchanged. The force exerted by that pressure on the new larger area changes.
