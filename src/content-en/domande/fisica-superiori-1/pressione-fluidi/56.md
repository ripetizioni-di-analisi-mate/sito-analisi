The large piston is replaced by one of double the area while the small piston and input load stay fixed. How does the pressure increase change in the ideal press?

- It doubles
- It halves
- It quadruples
- It stays unchanged

## Explanation

The added pressure is set by the force on the small piston divided by its area. Neither input quantity changes when the output piston is replaced. The added output force doubles because the same pressure increase now acts on a surface twice as large.
