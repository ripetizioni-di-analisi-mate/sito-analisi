Pressure varies across a flat wall. What do you get by dividing the total normal force by the area?

- Always the maximum pressure
- Always the minimum pressure
- The wall's unit normal
- The mean pressure on the wall

## Explanation

The result is a mean pressure. A uniform pressure at that value would give the same total normal force on the wall. The ratio accounts for regions that occupy more area. That single number cannot recover the highest or lowest local pressure on the face.
