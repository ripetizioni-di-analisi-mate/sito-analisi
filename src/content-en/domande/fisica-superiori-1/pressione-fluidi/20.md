Pressure varies across a flat wall. What do you get by dividing the total normal force by the area?

- Always the maximum pressure
- Always the minimum pressure
- The wall's unit normal
- The mean pressure on the wall

## Explanation

The ratio gives the equivalent uniform value that would produce the same total force over the same area.
