A flat wall of area $0.02\,\mathrm m^2$ separates two gases with uniform pressures $118000$ and $110000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. What is the magnitude of the resultant gas force?

- $2360\,\mathrm N$
- $160\,\mathrm N$
- $8000\,\mathrm N$
- $320\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The pressure difference is $8000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Multiplying by area gives $160\,\mathrm N$ directed from higher to lower pressure.
