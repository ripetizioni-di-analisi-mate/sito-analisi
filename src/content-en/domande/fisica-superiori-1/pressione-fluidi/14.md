A flat wall of area $0.02\,\mathrm m^2$ separates two gases with uniform pressures $118000$ and $110000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. What is the magnitude of the resultant gas force?

- $2360\,\mathrm N$
- $160\,\mathrm N$
- $8000\,\mathrm N$
- $320\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

On opposite faces the pressures exert forces in opposite directions. Since the area is the same, subtract the pressures first to obtain $118000-110000=8000$ in pascals. Multiplying by $0.02\,\mathrm m^2$ gives $160\,\mathrm N$, directed towards the lower-pressure gas. Adding the two magnitudes would treat the forces as if they pointed in the same direction.
