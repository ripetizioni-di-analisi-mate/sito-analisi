At equal depth in two open liquids, pressure is greater in A. With the same field and air pressure, what does this compare?

- Vessel width, which is greater in A
- Total volume, which is greater in A
- Density, which is higher in A
- Depth, which is assumed different

## Explanation

The last two factors in $\rho gh$ are shared. At common surface pressure, the difference reflects density.
