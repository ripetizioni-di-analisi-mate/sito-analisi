Two uniform liquids at rest are held in vessels open to the same atmosphere. The gravitational field is the same and the pressure at equal depths is higher in liquid A. Which property of the liquids explains the difference?

- Vessel width, which is greater in A
- Total volume, which is greater in A
- Density, which is higher in A
- Depth, which is assumed different

## Explanation

With the same atmosphere, the two pressures start from the same surface value. At the same positive depth and with the same gravitational field strength, a larger increase requires more mass per unit volume. Thus A contains the denser liquid. The total amount of liquid in either vessel is irrelevant to this comparison.
