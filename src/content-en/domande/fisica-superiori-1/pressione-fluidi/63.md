A horizontal sealed piston of area $0.020\,\mathrm m^2$ and weight $400\,\mathrm N$ slides without friction in a vertical cylinder. The gas below it is at $106\,\mathrm{kPa}$ absolute. What absolute pressure of the gas above the piston keeps it in equilibrium?

- $20\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $126\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $86\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $66\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

The gas below pushes upward. The resultant of the two pressure forces must balance the downward weight, so the lower pressure must exceed the upper pressure by $\dfrac{400\,\mathrm N}{0.020\,\mathrm m^2}=20\,\mathrm{kPa}$. This gives $(106-20)\,\mathrm{kPa}=86\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
