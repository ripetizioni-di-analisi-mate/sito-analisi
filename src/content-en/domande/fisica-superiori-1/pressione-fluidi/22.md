To keep mean pressure at or below $5000\,\mathrm{Pa}$, what minimum area must support $120\,\mathrm N$?

- $0.0024\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $0.024\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $0.24\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $\dfrac{125}{3}\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

The limiting area makes force divided by area exactly $5000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. The area $0.024\,\mathrm m^2$ reaches that value. A smaller area would increase the ratio and exceed the limit, while a larger area would keep it below the limit.

$$
A\geq\frac{120}{5000}\,\mathrm m^2=0.024\,\mathrm m^2.
$$
