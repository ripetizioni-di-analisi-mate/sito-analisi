Two points in the same body of water are at depths $9$ and $11\,\mathrm m$. How much higher is the pressure at the latter? Use $\rho=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$.

- $20000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $90000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $110000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The depth difference is $2\,\mathrm m$. The common surface pressure cancels, giving $20000\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
