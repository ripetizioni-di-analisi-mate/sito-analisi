Two points in the same body of water are at depths $9$ and $11\,\mathrm m$. How much higher is the pressure at the latter? Use $\rho=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$.

- $20000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $90000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $110000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The two points share a surface pressure. It cancels in the difference, leaving the weight per unit area of the column between the points. The second point is $(11-9)\,\mathrm m=2\,\mathrm m$ deeper, so its pressure exceeds that at the first by $1000\cdot10\cdot2\,\mathrm{Pa}=20000\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
