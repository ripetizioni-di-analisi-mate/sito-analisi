A pressure of $0.6\,\mathrm{kPa}$ is shown in $\mathrm{N/cm^2}$ on another instrument. What number should appear?

- $0.06$
- $0.6$
- $6$
- $60$

## Explanation

At $600\,\mathrm{N/m^2}$, one square metre receives $600\,\mathrm N$. One square centimetre is one ten-thousandth of that area and therefore receives $0.06\,\mathrm N$. The two instruments report the same physical pressure, but their numerical readings refer to different unit areas.

$$
600\,\mathrm{Pa}=600\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{N/cm^2}=0.06\,\mathrm{N/cm^2}.
$$
