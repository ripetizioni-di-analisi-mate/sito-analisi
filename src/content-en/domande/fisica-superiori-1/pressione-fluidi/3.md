A force of $320\,\mathrm N$ produces uniform pressure $16000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Over what area does it act?

- $50\,\mathrm m^2$
- $0.2\,\mathrm m^2$
- $0.02\,\mathrm m^2$
- $0.002\,\mathrm m^2$

## Explanation

Area is the unknown in $pA=F$. Pressure specifies force per unit area, so the ratio of total force to pressure gives $A=\dfrac{320}{16000}\,\mathrm m^2=0.02\,\mathrm m^2$. A larger area with the same force would give a lower pressure than the one stated.
