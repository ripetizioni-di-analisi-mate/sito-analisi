A force of $320\,\mathrm N$ produces uniform pressure $16000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Over what area does it act?

- $50\,\mathrm m^2$
- $0.2\,\mathrm m^2$
- $0.02\,\mathrm m^2$
- $0.002\,\mathrm m^2$

## Explanation

Solve for $A=F/p$. Dividing $320\,\mathrm N$ by $16000\,\mathrm{Pa}$ gives $0.02\,\mathrm m^2$.
