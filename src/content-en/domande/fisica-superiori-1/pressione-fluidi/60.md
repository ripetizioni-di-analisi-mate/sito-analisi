A flat plate of area $0.040\,\mathrm m^2$ separates gases A and B. In A the absolute pressure is $92\,\mathrm{kPa}$. In B the pressure relative to the surroundings is $-5\,\mathrm{kPa}$, and the surroundings are at $102\,\mathrm{kPa}$ absolute. What is the force component along the normal directed from A to B?

- $200\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $3880\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $-400\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $-200\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

First convert B to the absolute scale. Its pressure is $(102-5)\,\mathrm{kPa}=97\,\mathrm{kPa}$, higher than A's. The force from A is positive and the force from B negative, so the result is $(92-97)\cdot1000\cdot0.040\,\mathrm N=-200\,\mathrm N$. The sign shows that the plate is pushed towards A.
