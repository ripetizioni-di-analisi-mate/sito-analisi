A rectangular vertical plate separates water and gas. On the water side the absolute pressure increases linearly from $80\,\mathrm{kPa}$ at the top edge to $120\,\mathrm{kPa}$ at the bottom edge. On the gas side the pressure is uniform. What absolute gas pressure makes the resultant of the pressure forces zero?

- $80\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $100\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $120\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $40\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

Horizontal strips of equal height have equal area. Because the pressure varies linearly, strips equally far from the centre have a mean pressure of $100\,\mathrm{kPa}$. The whole plate therefore has the same mean. Uniform pressure of $100\,\mathrm{kPa}$ on the other face gives the same total force in the opposite direction.
