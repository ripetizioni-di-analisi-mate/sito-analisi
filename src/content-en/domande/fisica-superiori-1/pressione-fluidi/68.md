Two open columns exert the same pressure at the comparison level. The water column is $9\,\mathrm{cm}$ high at density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. The other column is $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ high. What is its density?

- $250\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $900\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $\dfrac{4000}{3}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $750\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

Surface pressure and field are shared. To have the same density-times-height product, the taller column must contain the less dense liquid.

$$
\rho_2=\frac{1000\cdot9}{12}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}=750\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}.
$$
