A uniform pressure of $9000\,\mathrm{Pa}$ acts on $0.03\,\mathrm m^2$. What perpendicular force does it produce?

- $270000\,\mathrm N$
- $270\,\mathrm N$
- $9000\,\mathrm N$
- $27\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The pressure is uniform, so each part of the surface receives the same load per square metre. The pressure is $9000\,\mathrm{N/m^2}$. Multiplying by the $0.03\,\mathrm m^2$ on which it acts cancels the square metres and leaves $270\,\mathrm N$. The force is normal to the face.
