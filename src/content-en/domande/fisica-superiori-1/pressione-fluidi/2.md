A uniform pressure of $9000\,\mathrm{Pa}$ acts on $0.03\,\mathrm m^2$. What perpendicular force does it produce?

- $270000\,\mathrm N$
- $270\,\mathrm N$
- $9000\,\mathrm N$
- $27\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Since a pascal is a newton per square metre, the product $9000\cdot0.03=270$ is in newtons.
