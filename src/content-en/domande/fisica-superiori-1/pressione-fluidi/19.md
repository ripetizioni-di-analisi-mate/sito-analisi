A press shares $180\,\mathrm N$ equally over three pads of area $0.01\,\mathrm m^2$ each. What is the pressure on one pad?

- $18000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $600\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $6000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $3000\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

Each pad receives one third of the load because the sharing is equal. Divide that share by one pad's area.

$$
p=\frac{180/3}{0.01}\,\mathrm{Pa}=6000\,\mathrm{Pa}.
$$
