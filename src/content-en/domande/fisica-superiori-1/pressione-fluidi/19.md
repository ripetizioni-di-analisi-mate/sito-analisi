A press shares $180\,\mathrm N$ equally over three pads of area $0.01\,\mathrm m^2$ each. What is the pressure on one pad?

- $18000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $600\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $6000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $3000\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The load is explicitly shared equally, so each pad carries $60\,\mathrm N$. To find the local pressure, divide by the area of that pad. The same value is obtained from $180\,\mathrm N$ divided by the total area of $0.03\,\mathrm m^2$, since all three pads have the same area and carry the same load.

$$
p=\frac{180/3}{0.01}\,\mathrm{Pa}=6000\,\mathrm{Pa}.
$$
