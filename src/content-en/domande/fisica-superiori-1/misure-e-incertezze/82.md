The symmetric interval $[2;6]\,\mathrm{cm}$ is multiplied by the exact factor $-2$. What is the new ordered interval?

- $[-4;-12]\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $[-12;-4]\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $[4;12]\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $[-8;0]\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

A negative multiplier reverses the order. The lower bound comes from six and the upper bound from two.
