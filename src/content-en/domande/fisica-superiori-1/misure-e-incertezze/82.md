The symmetric interval $[2;6]\,\mathrm{cm}$ is multiplied by the exact factor $-2$. What is the new ordered interval?

- $[-4;-12]\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $[-12;-4]\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $[4;12]\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $[-8;0]\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Multiplying the endpoints gives $-2\cdot2=-4$ and $-2\cdot6=-12$. The factor is negative, so the order reverses: the new minimum is $-12\,\mathrm{cm}$. The ordered interval is $[-12;-4]\,\mathrm{cm}$. In terms of position on the scale, the value with larger magnitude is farther to the left here.
