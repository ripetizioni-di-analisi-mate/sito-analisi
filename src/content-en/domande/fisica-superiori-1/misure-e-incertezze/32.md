Which series, with the same mean as the others, has the largest half-range?

- $9$, $10$, $11$
- $10$, $10$, $10$
- $9.5$, $10$, $10.5$
- $8$, $10$, $12$

## Explanation

The series are centred at the same value. The first one's endpoints are four units apart, farther than all the others.
