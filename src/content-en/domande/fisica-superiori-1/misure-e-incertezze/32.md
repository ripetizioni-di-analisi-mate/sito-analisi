Which series, with the same mean as the others, has the largest half-range?

- $9$, $10$, $11$
- $10$, $10$, $10$
- $9.5$, $10$, $10.5$
- $8$, $10$, $12$

## Explanation

The half-range depends on the distance between the largest and smallest readings. The series $8$, $10$ and $12$ has a range of $12-8=4$, greater than the other choices. Dividing by two gives a half-range of $2$. The common mean makes it possible to compare how far the readings extend on either side.
