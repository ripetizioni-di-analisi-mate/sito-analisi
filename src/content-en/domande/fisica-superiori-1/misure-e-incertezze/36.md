Which expression has symmetric interval $[3.4;4.2]\,\mathrm s$?

- $(3.8\pm0.8)\,\mathrm s$
- $(4.2\pm0.4)\,\mathrm s$
- $(3.4\pm0.8)\,\mathrm s$
- $(3.8\pm0.4)\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The centre of a symmetric interval is its midpoint, here $\dfrac{3.4+4.2}{2}=3.8\,\mathrm s$. The uncertainty is half the total distance, $\dfrac{4.2-3.4}{2}=0.4\,\mathrm s$. The expression $(3.8\pm0.4)\,\mathrm s$ recovers both endpoints by adding the radius to the centre or subtracting it.
