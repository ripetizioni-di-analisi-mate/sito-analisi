Which expression has symmetric interval $[3.4;4.2]\,\mathrm s$?

- $(3.8\pm0.8)\,\mathrm s$
- $(4.2\pm0.4)\,\mathrm s$
- $(3.4\pm0.8)\,\mathrm s$
- $(3.8\pm0.4)\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The midpoint is $3.8$ and the width is $0.8$. The uncertainty is half the width.
