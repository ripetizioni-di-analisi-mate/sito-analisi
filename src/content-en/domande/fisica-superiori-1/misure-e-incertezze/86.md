A square has side $(20\pm2)\,\mathrm{cm}$. How much does the nominal area exceed its exact lower bound?

- $80\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $76\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $84\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $4\,\mathrm{cm}^2$

## Explanation

The smallest side is eighteen centimetres. Subtract its square from the nominal area of four hundred square centimetres.

$20^{2}-\left(20-2\right)^{2}=76\,\mathrm{cm}^2$.
