A square has side length $(20\pm4)\,\mathrm{cm}$. By how much does its nominal area exceed the exact lower bound?

- $160\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $144\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $176\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $16\,\mathrm{cm^2}$

## Explanation

The nominal area is $20^2=400\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. For positive lengths, the square is smallest at the lowest possible side length of $16\,\mathrm{cm}$. The minimum area is $16^2=256\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, so the difference is $400-256=144\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. The linear estimate of $2\cdot20\cdot4=160\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ would omit the quadratic term.
