A scale has an affine response between $20$ and $100\,\mathrm g$. It reads $45\,\mathrm g$ for a $20\,\mathrm g$ reference and $165\,\mathrm g$ for a $100\,\mathrm g$ reference. What mass corresponds to a reading of $105\,\mathrm g$?

- $70\,\mathrm g$
- $90\,\mathrm g$
- $105\,\mathrm g$
- $60\,\mathrm g$

## Explanation

The sensitivity is $\dfrac{165-45}{100-20}=1.5$, so the reading also contains an offset of $45-1.5\cdot20=15\,\mathrm g$. Invert the formula by subtracting the offset before dividing by the sensitivity. This gives $\dfrac{105-15}{1.5}\,\mathrm g=60\,\mathrm g$, which lies between the two references used to estimate the calibration.
