A scale reads $5\%$ above every nonzero reference tested and shows zero when empty. What correction recovers the reference for a reading of $42\,\mathrm g$ under this model?

- $44.1\,\mathrm g$
- $37\,\mathrm g$
- $41.95\,\mathrm g$
- $40\,\mathrm g$

## Explanation

The model multiplies the reference by $1.05$. Divide by the same factor to undo its effect.

$\frac{42}{1.05}=40\,\mathrm g$.
