An intervention narrows each centred interval without shifting its centre. What can be said about two intervals that were initially disjoint?

- They touch at an endpoint
- They remain disjoint
- They always overlap
- Their union becomes a single interval

## Explanation

The new intervals are subsets of the earlier ones. Neither can reach values that were outside its original interval.
