A manual stopwatch is used to estimate the period of an oscillation. If the absolute uncertainty in starting and stopping the watch stays about the same, what reduces its relative effect?

- Time many oscillations together and divide by their count
- Time an even shorter part of a cycle
- Change the unit to milliseconds
- Round each time to the nearest whole second

## Explanation

Measure the time $T$ for $n$ oscillations and divide by $n$ to estimate the period. If the overall start-and-stop uncertainty is $\Delta T$, the period estimate has uncertainty $\dfrac{\Delta T}{n}$. The relative uncertainty is $\dfrac{\Delta T}{T}$. At fixed $\Delta T$, timing more cycles increases $T$ and reduces that ratio.
