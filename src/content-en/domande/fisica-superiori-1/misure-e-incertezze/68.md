Which result reports the central value to decimal places consistent with the stated uncertainty of $0.3\,\mathrm{cm}$?

- $(12.45\pm0.3)\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $(12.4567\pm0.3)\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $(12\pm0.3)\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $(12.4\pm0.3)\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The final digit of the uncertainty $0.3\,\mathrm{cm}$ is in the tenths place. The central value should therefore also end at tenths. The form $(12.4\pm0.3)\,\mathrm{cm}$ uses that place for both parts. The selected final digit makes clear which decimal place the measurement result is reported to.
