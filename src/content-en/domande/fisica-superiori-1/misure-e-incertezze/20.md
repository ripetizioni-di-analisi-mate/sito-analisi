A display changes in steps of $0.01\,\mathrm s$. Its manufacturer states an uncertainty of $0.03\,\mathrm s$. What uncertainty is used under that specification?

- $0.01\,\mathrm s$
- $0.005\,\mathrm s$
- $0.04\,\mathrm s$
- $0.03\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The display can show two readings separated by $0.01\,\mathrm s$, which is its resolution. The experiment follows the manufacturer's stated uncertainty of $0.03\,\mathrm s$. This is larger than the display step and is the value to attach to the reading under the chosen protocol.
