A display changes in steps of $0.01\,\mathrm s$. Its manufacturer states an uncertainty of $0.03\,\mathrm s$. What uncertainty is used under that specification?

- $0.01\,\mathrm s$
- $0.005\,\mathrm s$
- $0.04\,\mathrm s$
- $0.03\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The display step describes resolution. The requested measurement specification assigns an uncertainty of three hundredths.

$0.03=0.03\,\mathrm s$.
