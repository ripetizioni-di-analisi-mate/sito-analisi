A reading equal to the current mean is added to a series of measurements. What happens to its mean and half-range?

- The mean rises and the half-range stays unchanged
- The mean stays unchanged and the half-range halves
- Both stay unchanged
- The mean halves and the half-range rises

## Explanation

If the mean is $\bar x$ across $n$ readings, their sum is $n\bar x$. Adding one reading equal to $\bar x$ gives $(n+1)\bar x$, so the new mean is still $\bar x$. The new reading lies between the minimum and maximum. Since neither endpoint changes, the half-range also stays the same.
