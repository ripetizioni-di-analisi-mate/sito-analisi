A stable sample produces increasing readings as the instrument warms up. Which check best addresses the observed pattern?

- Only change the unit in which readings are recorded
- Halve the uncertainty by convention
- Compare the same reference before and after thermal stabilization
- Discard the time order of the readings

## Explanation

The sample is stable, so an ordered change in readings during warm-up suggests a change in the instrument. Measure the same reference before and after thermal stabilisation, keeping the other conditions fixed. The instrument's temperature changes while the reference mass stays the same. The difference between readings reveals possible thermal drift.
