The absolute uncertainty is $0.02\,\mathrm{mm}$ and the central value is $7\,\mathrm{mm}$. Which form reports the central value to the same decimal place as the uncertainty?

- $7.0\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $7\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $7.00\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $7.000\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The zero in the hundredths place indicates the position of the last reported digit. The numerical value remains seven.
