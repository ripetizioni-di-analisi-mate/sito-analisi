The absolute uncertainty is $0.02\,\mathrm{mm}$ and the central value is $7\,\mathrm{mm}$. To which decimal place should the central value be reported?

- To tenths of a millimetre
- To whole millimetres
- To hundredths of a millimetre
- To thousandths of a millimetre

## Explanation

The last digit of $0.02\,\mathrm{mm}$ is in the hundredths place. Write the centre as $7.00\,\mathrm{mm}$, with two digits after the decimal point. The value lies at the same point on the scale. The final zero indicates the decimal place used to report the measurement.
