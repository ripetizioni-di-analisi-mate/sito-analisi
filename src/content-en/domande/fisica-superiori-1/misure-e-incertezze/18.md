An object spans from $2.8$ to $9.3\,\mathrm{cm}$ on a ruler. How long is it?

- $9.3\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $6.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $12.1\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $3.7\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The ruler gives positions along the scale. Length is the interval between the two positions.

$9.3-2.8=6.5\,\mathrm{cm}$.
