An object spans from $2.8$ to $9.3\,\mathrm{cm}$ on a ruler. How long is it?

- $9.3\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $6.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $12.1\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $3.7\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The ruler displays the coordinates of both endpoints. Because the first is at $2.8\,\mathrm{cm}$, the final reading of $9.3\,\mathrm{cm}$ includes the part of the scale between zero and that first endpoint. The object's length is the difference $9.3-2.8=6.5\,\mathrm{cm}$. Moving both endpoints along the ruler would leave that difference unchanged.
