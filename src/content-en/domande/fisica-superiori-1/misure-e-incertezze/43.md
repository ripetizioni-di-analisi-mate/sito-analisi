Three instruments give intervals $[0;4]$, $[2;7]$, and $[5;8]\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is their common intersection?

- $[2;4]\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $[5;7]\,\mathrm{cm}$
- It is empty
- $[0;8]\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

A shared value would have to satisfy the greatest lower limit, $5\,\mathrm{cm}$, and the smallest upper limit, $4\,\mathrm{cm}$. These conditions are incompatible, so the intersection is empty. The second interval overlaps each of the other two separately without producing a reading common to all three.
