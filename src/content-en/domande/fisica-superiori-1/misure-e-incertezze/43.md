Three instruments give intervals $[0;4]$, $[2;7]$, and $[5;8]\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is their common intersection?

- $[2;4]\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $[5;7]\,\mathrm{cm}$
- It is empty
- $[0;8]\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

A common value would have to be at least five and at most four. These two conditions cannot hold simultaneously.
