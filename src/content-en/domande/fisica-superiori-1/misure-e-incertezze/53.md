Rods $(20\pm1)$ and $(10\pm2)\,\mathrm{cm}$ are placed end to end. What is the conservative upper bound of their combined length?

- $33\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $30\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $32\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $29\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

For the greatest possible sum, take both rods at their upper endpoints and add those endpoints.

$20+1+10+2=33\,\mathrm{cm}$.
