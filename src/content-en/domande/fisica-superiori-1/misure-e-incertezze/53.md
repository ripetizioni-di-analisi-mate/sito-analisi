Rods of length $(20\pm4)$ and $(10\pm5)\,\mathrm{cm}$ are placed end to end. If the endpoints can occur together, what is the upper bound for total length?

- $39\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $30\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $35\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $34\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The two rods can have lengths of $20+4=24\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $10+5=15\,\mathrm{cm}$ at the same time. Total length increases when either rod gets longer, so the upper bound is $24+15=39\,\mathrm{cm}$. Adding both uncertainties to the nominal $30\,\mathrm{cm}$ gives the same endpoint.
