A thermometer displays only multiples of $0.2\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. Two readings are averaged. Which mean is possible near $20\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$?

- $20.05\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $20.1\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $20.15\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $20.25\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$

## Explanation

The sum of two multiples of $0.2$ is also a multiple of $0.2$. Dividing by two gives a multiple of $0.1$. Readings of $20.0$ and $20.2$ give exactly $20.1$.
