The required tolerance is $2\%$ of $50\,\mathrm{mm}$. What maximum absolute uncertainty is permitted?

- $25\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $1\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $100\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.02\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

A relative tolerance of $2\%$ allows at most two parts in one hundred of the nominal length. The absolute value is $0.02\cdot50\,\mathrm{mm}=1\,\mathrm{mm}$. In a symmetric interval centred at $50\,\mathrm{mm}$, this allows values between $49$ and $51\,\mathrm{mm}$. The requested uncertainty is the one-millimetre radius, not the full width of that band.
