The required tolerance is $2\%$ of $50\,\mathrm{mm}$. What maximum absolute uncertainty is permitted?

- $25\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $1\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $100\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.02\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

Two parts per hundred of the nominal value correspond to multiplying by $0.02$. The result is a length.

$\frac{50\cdot 2}{100}=1\,\mathrm{mm}$.
