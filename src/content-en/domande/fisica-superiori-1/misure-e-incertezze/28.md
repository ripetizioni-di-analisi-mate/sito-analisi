Adding one measurement changes the observed endpoints from $[8;12]$ to $[8;16]\,\mathrm{cm}$. By how much does the half-range increase?

- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The minimum is unchanged and the maximum rises by four centimetres. The half-range increases by half that shift.

$\frac{16-8-\left(12-8\right)}{2}=2\,\mathrm{cm}$.
