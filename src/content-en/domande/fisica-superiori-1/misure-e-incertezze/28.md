Adding one measurement changes the observed endpoints from $[8;12]$ to $[8;16]\,\mathrm{cm}$. By how much does the half-range increase?

- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Before the final reading, the range was $12-8=4\,\mathrm{cm}$. Afterwards it is $16-8=8\,\mathrm{cm}$. The respective half-ranges are $2$ and $4\,\mathrm{cm}$, so the increase is $2\,\mathrm{cm}$. The other readings may affect the mean, but the stated extremes suffice to find the half-range.
