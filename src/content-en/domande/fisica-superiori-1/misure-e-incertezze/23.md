The sorted readings are $3$, $4$, $5$, $6$ and $7\,\mathrm g$. Only the last changes, to $12\,\mathrm g$. What happens to the mean and the median, the third reading in order?

- Both increase by $1\,\mathrm g$.
- The mean increases by $5\,\mathrm g$ and the median stays the same.
- The mean increases by $1\,\mathrm g$ and the median stays the same.
- The mean stays the same and the median increases by $1\,\mathrm g$.

## Explanation

The last reading contributes $12-7=5\,\mathrm g$ more to the sum. There are still five values, so the mean rises by $\dfrac55=1\,\mathrm g$. The changed reading remains last in the new sorted series. Its third entry is still $5\,\mathrm g$, so the median is unchanged.
