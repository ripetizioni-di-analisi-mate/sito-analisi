The readings of a mass are $4$, $4$, $5$, and $7\,\mathrm g$. What is their mean?

- $\dfrac{16}{3}\,\mathrm g$
- $5.5\,\mathrm g$
- $5\,\mathrm g$
- $3\,\mathrm g$

## Explanation

The four-gram reading occurs twice, so it contributes twice to the sum of the four observations.

$\frac{4+4+5+7}{4}=5\,\mathrm g$.
