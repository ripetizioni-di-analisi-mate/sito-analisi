A mass is $m=(110\pm9.9)\,\mathrm g$. What is its percentage uncertainty?

- $0.09\,\%$
- $900\,\%$
- $\dfrac{100}{9}\,\%$
- $9\,\%$

## Explanation

The absolute uncertainty and the central value are both in grams. Dividing the first number by the second gives a dimensionless fraction. Multiply that fraction by one hundred to express how much uncertainty there is per hundred parts of the central value. Grams apply to the measurement and absolute uncertainty.

The calculation gives $\varepsilon=\dfrac{9.9}{110}=0.09=9\%$.
