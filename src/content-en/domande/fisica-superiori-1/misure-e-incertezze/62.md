A product uses one exact factor and another with $4\%$ relative uncertainty. What is the relative uncertainty of the product?

- $0\,\%$
- $4\,\%$
- $8\,\%$
- $2\,\%$

## Explanation

Multiplying by an exact factor scales the value and its absolute uncertainty in the same proportion.

$\varepsilon_{\mathrm{prodotto}}=4\,\%$.
