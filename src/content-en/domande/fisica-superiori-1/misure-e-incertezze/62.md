A product uses one exact positive factor and another with $4\%$ relative uncertainty. What is the relative uncertainty of the product?

- $0\,\%$
- $4\,\%$
- $8\,\%$
- $2\,\%$

## Explanation

The exact positive factor $k$ multiplies the central value $x_m$ and the absolute uncertainty $\Delta x$. In the new ratio $\dfrac{k\Delta x}{kx_m}$, $k$ cancels, leaving the original relative uncertainty of $4\%$. The interval in physical units can therefore grow or shrink without changing its width relative to the centre.
