A displacement is measured as $(0.0\pm0.2)\,\mathrm{cm}$. How should the relative ratio defined for positive central values be interpreted?

- It equals zero
- It is $20\%$
- It is always $100\%$
- It is undefined

## Explanation

The ratio would have a zero denominator. The reading still has an absolute uncertainty of two tenths of a centimetre.
