A displacement is measured as $(0.0\pm0.2)\,\mathrm{cm}$. How should the relative ratio defined for positive central values be interpreted?

- It equals zero
- It is $20\%$
- It is always $100\%$
- It is undefined

## Explanation

Relative uncertainty would divide $0.2\,\mathrm{cm}$ by the central value $0.0\,\mathrm{cm}$. Division by zero is undefined, so the result cannot be expressed as a percentage of that centre. The absolute uncertainty remains well defined, and the reading describes the interval from $-0.2$ to $0.2\,\mathrm{cm}$ around zero.
