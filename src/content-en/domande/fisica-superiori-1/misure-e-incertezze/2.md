The extreme readings of a length are $42$ and $58\,\mathrm{mm}$. Estimate the uncertainty with the half-range.

- $16\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $8\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $50\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $42\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

Consider the distance between the extreme readings. The requested rule takes half this distance, the radius of the interval with those endpoints. The result uses the same unit as the measurements. Additional uncertainty sources, such as resolution, would require separate data.

The calculation gives $\Delta l=\dfrac{58-42}2=8\,\mathrm{mm}$.
