A square has side $(5\pm1)\,\mathrm{cm}$. How much does the exact upper area bound exceed the nominal area?

- $10\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $11\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $1\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $9\,\mathrm{cm}^2$

## Explanation

The nominal area is five squared. The upper bound is six squared, including the squared deviation term.

$\left(5+1\right)^{2}-5^{2}=11\,\mathrm{cm}^2$.
