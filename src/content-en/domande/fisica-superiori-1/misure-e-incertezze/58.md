Two measured lengths are $L=(12\pm1)\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $W=(6\pm1)\,\mathrm{mm}$. All combinations are allowed in the interval model. What are the bounds of $L/W$?

- $\left[\dfrac{13}{7};\dfrac{11}{5}\right]$
- $\left[\dfrac{11}{7};\dfrac{13}{5}\right]$
- $\left[\dfrac{11}{6};\dfrac{13}{6}\right]$
- $\left[\dfrac{12}{7};\dfrac{12}{5}\right]$

## Explanation

With a positive denominator the ratio increases with $L$ and decreases with $W$. Its minimum therefore uses $L=11$ and $W=7$, while its maximum uses $L=13$ and $W=5$. The units cancel. Using both minimum inputs would give a possible case without attaining the lower bound of the ratio.
