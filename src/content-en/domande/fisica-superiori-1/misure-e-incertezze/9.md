A frame's side is found by subtracting two readings whose uncertainties are $0.8$ and $1\,\mathrm{mm}$. Under the conservative rule, what is the uncertainty of the difference?

- $1.8\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.2\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $1\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.8\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The difference is greatest when the first reading is above its centre and the second is below its centre. Subtracting the second negative deviation makes it a positive contribution, so the magnitudes add. The same sum gives the deviation toward the lower endpoint.

Thus $\Delta l=0.8+1=1.8\,\mathrm{mm}$.
