Three readings are $15$, $16$, $17\,\mathrm g$. The correction adds $0.4\,\mathrm g$ to each. What is the new mean?

- $\dfrac{242}{15}\,\mathrm g$
- $16.4\,\mathrm g$
- $15.6\,\mathrm g$
- $17.2\,\mathrm g$

## Explanation

Each reading receives the same contribution. Computing the mean divides the total contribution by three.

$\frac{15+16+17}{3}+0.4=16.4\,\mathrm g$.
