Three readings are $15$, $16$ and $17\,\mathrm g$. A correction adds $4\,\mathrm g$ to each reading. What is the new mean?

- $\dfrac{52}{3}\,\mathrm g$
- $20\,\mathrm g$
- $12\,\mathrm g$
- $24\,\mathrm g$

## Explanation

The original mean is $\dfrac{15+16+17}{3}=16\,\mathrm g$. The correction raises each reading by $4\,\mathrm g$, so the sum grows by $3\cdot4=12\,\mathrm g$. Dividing that increase by three shows that the mean rises by $4\,\mathrm g$. The new mean is therefore $20\,\mathrm g$.
