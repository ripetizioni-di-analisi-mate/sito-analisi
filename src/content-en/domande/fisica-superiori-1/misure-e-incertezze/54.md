Subtract $(3\pm0.5)\,\mathrm m$ from $(9\pm1)\,\mathrm m$. What is the lower bound of the difference?

- $5.5\,\mathrm m$
- $4.5\,\mathrm m$
- $6\,\mathrm m$
- $7.5\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The difference is smallest when the first term is minimized and the largest second term is subtracted.

$9-1-\left(3+0.5\right)=4.5\,\mathrm m$.
