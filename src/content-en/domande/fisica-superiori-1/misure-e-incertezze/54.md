Subtract $(3\pm0.5)\,\mathrm m$ from $(9\pm1)\,\mathrm m$. Assume every pair of values from the two intervals is possible. What is the lower bound of the difference?

- $5.5\,\mathrm m$
- $4.5\,\mathrm m$
- $6\,\mathrm m$
- $7.5\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The difference is smallest when the first term is at its lower endpoint and the second is at its upper endpoint. These endpoints can occur together. Subtract $3+0.5=3.5\,\mathrm m$ from $9-1=8\,\mathrm m$ to obtain $4.5\,\mathrm m$. Subtracting the lower endpoint of the second term would give a larger difference.
