A manufacturer's uncertainty specification is $\pm(0.1+0.01x)$ grams for a reading with numerical value $x$ in grams. What uncertainty does it assign to $30\,\mathrm g$?

- $0.1\,\mathrm g$
- $0.3\,\mathrm g$
- $0.4\,\mathrm g$
- $0.11\,\mathrm g$

## Explanation

The fixed term contributes $0.1\,\mathrm g$. For a numerical reading of $x=30$, the reading-dependent term is $0.01\cdot30=0.3\,\mathrm g$. The protocol adds the two terms and assigns $0.4\,\mathrm g$. The $\pm$ sign means that this value applies on each side of the reading, without doubling it.
