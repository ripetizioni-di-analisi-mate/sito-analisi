A manufacturer's uncertainty specification is $\pm(0.1+0.01x)$ grams for a reading with numerical value $x$ in grams. What uncertainty does it assign to $30\,\mathrm g$?

- $0.1\,\mathrm g$
- $0.3\,\mathrm g$
- $0.4\,\mathrm g$
- $0.11\,\mathrm g$

## Explanation

The specification has a fixed term and a term increasing with the reading. Substitute the reading and add the terms.

$0.1+0.01\cdot 30=0.4\,\mathrm g$.
