A force meter with a maximum reading of $20\,\mathrm N$ has divisions of $0.2\,\mathrm N$, and the protocol assigns half a division as the absolute uncertainty. What is the smallest positive force for which the relative uncertainty is at most $1\%$?

- $1\,\mathrm N$
- $10\,\mathrm N$
- $20\,\mathrm N$
- $0.1\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Half a division sets $\Delta F=0.1\,\mathrm N$. We need a positive force $F$ satisfying $\dfrac{\Delta F}{F}\leq0.01$. Solving for $F$ gives $F\geq\dfrac{0.1}{0.01}\,\mathrm N=10\,\mathrm N$. At larger readings the absolute uncertainty stays the same, but the fraction it represents gets smaller.
