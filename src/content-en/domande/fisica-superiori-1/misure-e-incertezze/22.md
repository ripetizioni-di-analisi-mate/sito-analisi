A ruler has marks every $2\,\mathrm{mm}$. The protocol assigns a full division to each reading. What reading uncertainty is reported in centimetres?

- $0.1\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.2\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $20\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The rule uses the full smallest division. A centimetre contains ten millimetres, so two millimetres equal two tenths of a centimetre.

$\frac{2}{10}=0.2\,\mathrm{cm}$.
