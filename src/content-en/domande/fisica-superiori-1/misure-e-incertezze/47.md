A mass has relative uncertainty $0.04$ and absolute uncertainty $2\,\mathrm g$. What is the central mass?

- $0.08\,\mathrm g$
- $0.02\,\mathrm g$
- $50\,\mathrm g$
- $0.5\,\mathrm g$

## Explanation

Relative uncertainty equals absolute uncertainty divided by the central value. Divide the absolute by the relative to recover the centre.

$\frac{2}{0.04}=50\,\mathrm g$.
