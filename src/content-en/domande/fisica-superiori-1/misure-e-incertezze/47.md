A mass has relative uncertainty $0.04$ and absolute uncertainty $2\,\mathrm g$. What is the central mass?

- $0.08\,\mathrm g$
- $0.02\,\mathrm g$
- $50\,\mathrm g$
- $0.5\,\mathrm g$

## Explanation

For a positive central mass, relative uncertainty is $\varepsilon=\dfrac{\Delta m}{m}$. Multiplying by $m$ and dividing by $\varepsilon$ gives $m=\dfrac{\Delta m}{\varepsilon}$. The values yield $\dfrac{2}{0.04}=50\,\mathrm g$. Relative uncertainty is dimensionless, so the mass keeps the numerator's unit of grams.
