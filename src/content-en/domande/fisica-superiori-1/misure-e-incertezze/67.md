A protocol rounds an uncertainty of $0.061\,\mathrm s$ upward to hundredths. What does it report?

- $0.06\,\mathrm s$
- $0.061\,\mathrm s$
- $0.07\,\mathrm s$
- $0.1\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The first hundredth at or above the original value is $0.07$. This preserves a conservative estimate.

$0.07=0.07\,\mathrm s$.
