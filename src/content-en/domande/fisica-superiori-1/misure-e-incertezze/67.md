A protocol rounds an uncertainty of $0.061\,\mathrm s$ upward to hundredths. What does it report?

- $0.06\,\mathrm s$
- $0.061\,\mathrm s$
- $0.07\,\mathrm s$
- $0.1\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The uncertainty of $0.061\,\mathrm s$ lies between $0.06$ and $0.07\,\mathrm s$. The protocol requires the smallest value in hundredths that is at least the calculated one. Use $0.07\,\mathrm s$. The lower value would be closer but would narrow the estimated interval, whereas this rule preserves a conservative bound.
