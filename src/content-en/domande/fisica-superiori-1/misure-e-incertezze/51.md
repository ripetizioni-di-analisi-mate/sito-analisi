Two estimates have the same relative uncertainty of $5\%$. Their central values are $20$ and $80\,\mathrm g$. What is the ratio of the larger absolute uncertainty to the smaller?

- $0.25$
- $60$
- $4$
- $5$

## Explanation

The relative factor $0.05$ multiplies both central values to produce their absolute uncertainties. The $20\,\mathrm g$ reading has uncertainty $1\,\mathrm g$ and the $80\,\mathrm g$ reading has uncertainty $4\,\mathrm g$. The larger-to-smaller ratio is $\dfrac41=4$. The common percentage cancels because it appears once in each term of the ratio.
