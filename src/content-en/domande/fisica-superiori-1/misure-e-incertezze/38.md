To accept a part, its entire measurement interval must lie between $30$ and $34\,\mathrm{mm}$, including the endpoints. The measurement is $(31\pm2)\,\mathrm{mm}$. What follows?

- The part must be accepted because its central value is allowed.
- The intervals overlap, and the lower endpoint of the measurement lies outside the tolerance band.
- The measurement allows no permitted value.
- The entire measurement interval lies in the allowed band.

## Explanation

The measurement allows values from $29$ to $33\,\mathrm{mm}$. The section from $30$ to $33\,\mathrm{mm}$ overlaps the allowed band. However, the rule also applies to the lower endpoint of $29\,\mathrm{mm}$, which is outside the specification. The decision compares both endpoints as well as the central value.
