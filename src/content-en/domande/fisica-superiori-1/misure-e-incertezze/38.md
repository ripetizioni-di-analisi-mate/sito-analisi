Does the interval for $(50\pm2)\,\mathrm g$ contain a known reference of $53\,\mathrm g$?

- Yes, the difference is less than two
- No, the reference is above the upper endpoint
- Yes, the interval has total width four
- No, the reference is below the lower endpoint

## Explanation

The largest value in the interval is $50+2=52\,\mathrm g$. The reference is above that endpoint.
