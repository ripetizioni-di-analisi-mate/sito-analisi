Disjoint intervals are $[2;3]$ and $[3.5;4]\,\mathrm{mm}$. How far apart are they along the scale?

- $2\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $1\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The two intervals are disjoint, and the one starting at $3.5\,\mathrm{mm}$ lies to the right. The smallest gap is therefore between $3$, the right endpoint of the first, and $3.5$, the left endpoint of the second. It is $3.5-3=0.5\,\mathrm{mm}$. Choosing a farther endpoint would include part of that interval in the distance.
