Disjoint intervals are $[2;3]$ and $[3.5;4]\,\mathrm{mm}$. How far apart are they along the scale?

- $2\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $1\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

Find the distance between the two nearest endpoints: the right edge of the first and the left edge of the second.

$3.5-3=0.5\,\mathrm{mm}$.
