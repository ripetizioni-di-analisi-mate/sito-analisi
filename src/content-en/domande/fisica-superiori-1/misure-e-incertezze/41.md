Measurements centred at $5$ and $7\,\mathrm{cm}$ have uncertainty $0.6\,\mathrm{cm}$ and an unknown uncertainty, respectively. What minimum uncertainty makes them compatible?

- $1.4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2.6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.6\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The intervals just meet when the one on the right reaches the left interval's upper endpoint.

$7-\left(5+0.6\right)=1.4\,\mathrm{cm}$.
