Measurements centred at $5$ and $7\,\mathrm{cm}$ have uncertainty $0.6\,\mathrm{cm}$ and an unknown uncertainty, respectively. What minimum uncertainty makes them compatible?

- $1.4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2.6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.6\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The left-hand interval ends at $5+0.6=5.6\,\mathrm{cm}$. The left endpoint of the second measurement is $7-\Delta$. The intervals are separate while it stays above $5.6\,\mathrm{cm}$. At $\Delta=7-5.6=1.4\,\mathrm{cm}$, the closed intervals touch exactly at $5.6\,\mathrm{cm}$. This makes $1.4\,\mathrm{cm}$ the smallest uncertainty required.
