An object has mass $55\,\mathrm g$ with relative uncertainty $9\%$. What is the absolute uncertainty?

- $4.95\,\mathrm g$
- $495\,\mathrm g$
- $\dfrac{55}{9}\,\mathrm g$
- $0.09\,\mathrm g$

## Explanation

A percentage counts parts per hundred. Divide the percentage number by one hundred and multiply by the central mass. The fraction is dimensionless and the product is in grams. Dividing the result by the mass recovers the relative uncertainty stated in the question.

Therefore $\Delta m=55\cdot\dfrac{9}{100}=4.95\,\mathrm g$.
