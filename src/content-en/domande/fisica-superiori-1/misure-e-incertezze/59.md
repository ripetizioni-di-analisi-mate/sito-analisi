A cube's edge has a relative uncertainty of $2\%$. What is the conservative linear estimate for its volume?

- $2\,\%$
- $8\,\%$
- $6\,\%$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}\,\%$

## Explanation

The cube's volume is $s^3$, where $s$ is its edge length. To first order, a small relative change in $s$ is amplified by a factor of three. With $2\%$ this gives $3\cdot2\%=6\%$. The expansion of $(1+\varepsilon)^3$ also contains higher powers of $\varepsilon$, which the requested linear estimate omits.
