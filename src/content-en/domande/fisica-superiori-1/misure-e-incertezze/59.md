A cube's edge has a relative uncertainty of $2\%$. What is the conservative linear estimate for its volume?

- $2\,\%$
- $8\,\%$
- $6\,\%$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}\,\%$

## Explanation

Volume contains three identical edge factors. At first order each contributes the same relative uncertainty.

$3\cdot2\,\%=6\,\%$.
