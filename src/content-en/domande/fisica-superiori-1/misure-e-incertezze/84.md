Two absolute uncertainties of $5$ and $3\,\mathrm{mm}$ enter a conservative model. What uncertainty results if the second input is multiplied by two as a single term $2b$?

- $8\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $16\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $13\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $11\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The first reading can contribute a deviation of $5\,\mathrm{mm}$. The second appears as $2b$, so a deviation of $3\,\mathrm{mm}$ in $b$ gives $6\,\mathrm{mm}$ in the total. In the conservative case, the deviations can point the same way and give $5+6=11\,\mathrm{mm}$. Both copies of $b$ refer to the same reading and the same deviation.
