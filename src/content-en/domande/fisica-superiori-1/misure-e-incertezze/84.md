Two absolute uncertainties of $5$ and $3\,\mathrm{mm}$ enter a conservative model. What uncertainty results if the second input is multiplied by two as a single term $2b$?

- $8\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $16\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $13\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $11\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The factor of two doubles a deviation of the second input. Add this new magnitude to the first.

$5+2\cdot 3=11\,\mathrm{mm}$.
