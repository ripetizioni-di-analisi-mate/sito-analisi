Both endpoints of a segment are read with the same additive zero offset. What effect does this offset have on their difference?

- It is added twice
- It remains once with a positive sign
- It cancels
- It remains once with a negative sign

## Explanation

If the zero offset is $b$, the readings at the endpoints are $x_1+b$ and $x_2+b$. Their difference is $(x_2+b)-(x_1+b)=x_2-x_1$. Cancellation occurs because the same $b$ appears in both readings and is subtracted once. Any offset that changed between the endpoints would require a separate check.
