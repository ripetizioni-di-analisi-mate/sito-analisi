For $x>0$, let $z=\dfrac{1}{x^2}$. If the relative uncertainty in $x$ is $3\,\%$, what is the first-order estimate of the relative uncertainty in $z$?

- $6\,\%$
- $3\,\%$
- $-6\,\%$
- $9\,\%$

## Explanation

For $z=x^{-2}$, the sign of the exponent says that $z$ decreases when $x$ increases. The estimate concerns the magnitude of the change, so to first order it uses $|-2|=2$. With $3\%$ for the base, this gives $2\cdot3\%=6\%$. The positive value gives the size of the change, and the exponent's sign describes its direction.
