A positive dimensionless quantity follows $z=1/x^2$. If $x$ has $3\%$ uncertainty, what is the first-order estimate for $z$?

- $6\,\%$
- $3\,\%$
- $-6\,\%$
- $9\,\%$

## Explanation

The power has exponent $-2$ and uncertainty is a nonnegative magnitude. Multiply by the exponent's absolute value.

$2\cdot3\,\%=6\,\%$.
