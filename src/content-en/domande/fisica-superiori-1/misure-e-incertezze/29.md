Every reading in a series is $3\,\mathrm g$ too large because of a fixed offset. What happens to the half-range after correcting them?

- It stays the same
- It falls by $3\,\mathrm g$
- It rises by $3\,\mathrm g$
- It is halved

## Explanation

Correcting an additive deviation of $3\,\mathrm g$ shifts every reading down by the same amount. The new difference between its maximum and minimum is $(x_{\max}-3)-(x_{\min}-3)=x_{\max}-x_{\min}$. Because the same deviation cancels from both endpoints, half that difference stays the same. The series changes position without changing its width.
