For the same instrument, the rule changes from using a full division to using a half division. What changes?

- The physical spacing of marks is halved
- The range doubles
- The assigned uncertainty is halved
- The resolution step doubles

## Explanation

The estimation rule changes while the physical properties of the instrument's scale stay the same.
