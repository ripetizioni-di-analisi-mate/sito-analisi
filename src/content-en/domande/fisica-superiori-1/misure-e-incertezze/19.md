For the same instrument, the rule changes from using a full division to using a half division. What changes?

- The physical spacing of marks is halved
- The range doubles
- The assigned uncertainty is halved
- The resolution step doubles

## Explanation

If a division measures $d$, the first rule assigns an absolute uncertainty of $d$ and the second gives $\dfrac d2$. The assigned uncertainty is therefore halved. The marks remain in the same positions because the instrument is unchanged. The change concerns the rule used to attach uncertainty to its reading.
