For reference masses of $20$ and $40\,\mathrm g$, the balance reads $22$ and $44\,\mathrm g$, respectively. Which description fits both observations?

- The offset is always $2\,\mathrm g$
- The offset is always $4\,\mathrm g$
- Both readings are exact
- The reading is $1.1$ times the reference mass

## Explanation

The ratios of reading to reference are $\dfrac{22}{20}=1.1$ and $\dfrac{44}{40}=1.1$. The same scale factor describes both readings. To correct them, divide each by $1.1$. The differences $22-20=2\,\mathrm g$ and $44-40=4\,\mathrm g$ are unequal, so a constant zero offset does not explain both observations.
