The $20$ and $40\,\mathrm g$ reference masses read $22$ and $44\,\mathrm g$. Which description fits both observations?

- The offset is always $2\,\mathrm g$
- The offset is always $4\,\mathrm g$
- Both readings are exact
- The reading is multiplied by $1.1$

## Explanation

Both ratios of reading to reference equal $1.1$. The two differences are two and four grams.
