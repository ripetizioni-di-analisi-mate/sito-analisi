A measured mass is $0.4\,\mathrm{kg}$ with an absolute uncertainty of $6\,\mathrm g$. What is the percentage relative uncertainty?

- $1500\,\%$
- $0.015\,\%$
- $\dfrac{200}{3}\,\%$
- $1.5\,\%$

## Explanation

The relative uncertainty is the ratio of the absolute uncertainty to the central value, both expressed in the same unit. The central value is therefore converted to grams, $0.4\,\mathrm{kg}=400\,\mathrm g$. The ratio is
$$
\varepsilon=\frac{6\,\mathrm g}{400\,\mathrm g}=0.015 ,
$$
which multiplied by one hundred gives $1.5\,\%$. The value $1500\,\%$ comes from the ratio of the numbers $6$ and $0.4$, which mixes grams and kilograms.
