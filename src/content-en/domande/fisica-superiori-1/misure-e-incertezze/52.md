A $0.4\,\mathrm{kg}$ reading has absolute uncertainty of $6\,\mathrm g$. What is its percentage uncertainty?

- $1500\,\%$
- $0.015\,\%$
- $\dfrac{200}{3}\,\%$
- $1.5\,\%$

## Explanation

Convert the central value $0.4\,\mathrm{kg}$ to $400\,\mathrm g$ so it can be compared with the uncertainty of $6\,\mathrm g$. The ratio $\dfrac{6}{400}=0.015$ has no unit and represents $1.5\%$. Directly using the numbers $6$ and $0.4$ would mix grams and kilograms and give an incorrect percentage.
