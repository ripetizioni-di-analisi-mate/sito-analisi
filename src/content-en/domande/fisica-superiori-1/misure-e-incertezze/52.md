A $0.4\,\mathrm{kg}$ reading has absolute uncertainty of $6\,\mathrm g$. What is its percentage uncertainty?

- $1500\,\%$
- $0.015\,\%$
- $\dfrac{200}{3}\,\%$
- $1.5\,\%$

## Explanation

The central value is four hundred grams. Form the ratio in grams and multiply it by one hundred.

$\frac{6}{0.4\cdot 1000}=0.015=1.5\,\%$.
