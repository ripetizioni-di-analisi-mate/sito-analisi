Each washer has a mass of exactly $4\,\mathrm g$. A box contains only washers, and the mass of its contents lies between $26$ and $30\,\mathrm g$. How many washers does it contain?

- $6$
- $8$
- No number is consistent with the measurement.
- $7$

## Explanation

The possible masses are whole-number multiples of four grams. The only multiple in the interval is $28\,\mathrm g$, so there are seven washers.
