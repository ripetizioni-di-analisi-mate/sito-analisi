The centre is $10$ and the lower endpoint is $8\,\mathrm s$. What is the upper endpoint for a symmetric interval?

- $12\,\mathrm s$
- $2\,\mathrm s$
- $18\,\mathrm s$
- $10\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The lower endpoint is $10-8=2\,\mathrm s$ to the left of the centre. A symmetric interval has the same radius to the right, so its upper endpoint is $10+2=12\,\mathrm s$. Taking the midpoint of $8$ and $12\,\mathrm s$ recovers $10\,\mathrm s$, as the stated centre requires.
