The centre is $10$ and the lower endpoint is $8\,\mathrm s$. What is the upper endpoint for a symmetric interval?

- $12\,\mathrm s$
- $2\,\mathrm s$
- $18\,\mathrm s$
- $11\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The lower bound is two seconds from the centre. The upper bound is the same distance above the centre.

$10+\left(10-8\right)=12\,\mathrm s$.
