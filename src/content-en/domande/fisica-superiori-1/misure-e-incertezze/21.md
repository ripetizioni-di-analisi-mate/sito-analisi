A force of $80\,\mathrm N$ must be measured with divisions no greater than $1\,\mathrm N$. Which scale meets both requirements?

- Range $100\,\mathrm N$ with division $0.5\,\mathrm N$
- Range $50\,\mathrm N$ with division $0.1\,\mathrm N$
- Range $200\,\mathrm N$ with division $5\,\mathrm N$
- Range $60\,\mathrm N$ with division $2\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

To read $80\,\mathrm N$, the instrument's range must reach at least that value. To distinguish changes of one newton, each division must be at most $1\,\mathrm N$. The $100\,\mathrm N$ dial with $0.5\,\mathrm N$ divisions meets both inequalities. Fine spacing is useful only if the measurement lies within the range.
