A force of $80\,\mathrm N$ must be measured with divisions no greater than $1\,\mathrm N$. Which scale meets both requirements?

- Range $100\,\mathrm N$ with division $0.5\,\mathrm N$
- Range $50\,\mathrm N$ with division $0.1\,\mathrm N$
- Range $200\,\mathrm N$ with division $5\,\mathrm N$
- Range $60\,\mathrm N$ with division $2\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The range must include eighty newtons and the step must be no greater than one newton.
