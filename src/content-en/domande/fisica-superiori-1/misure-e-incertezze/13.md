Round $18.45\,\mathrm{cm}$ to the nearest tenth, rounding upward in a tie when the following digit is 5.

- $18.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $18.4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $18.45\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $18.6\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Keep one digit after the decimal point. The first discarded digit is in the hundredths place. It is five and later digits are zero, so the value is halfway between adjacent tenths. The specified rule selects the higher tenth.

Since it is $5$, the rounded value is $18.5\,\mathrm{cm}$.
