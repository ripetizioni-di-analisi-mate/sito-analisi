Round $0.35\,\mathrm{cm}$ to the nearest tenth. If it is halfway between two tenths, choose the greater one.

- $0.4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.3\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.35\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The adjacent tenths are $0.3\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $0.4\,\mathrm{cm}$. Both lie $0.05\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the given value, so apply the stated convention and choose $0.4\,\mathrm{cm}$. The form $0.35\,\mathrm{cm}$ still reports the hundredths and gives the value before rounding.
