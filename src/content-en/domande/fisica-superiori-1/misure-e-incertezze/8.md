Two separately measured lengths have absolute uncertainties $0.8$ and $0.9\,\mathrm{cm}$. Under the conservative rule for a sum, what is the uncertainty of the sum?

- $0.1\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.72\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.9\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $1.7\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

To obtain the upper bound of the sum, take both inputs at their upper endpoints. The two positive deviations add. The lower bound differs from the central value by the same amount, with both deviations directed downward.

The calculation gives $\Delta L=0.8+0.9=1.7\,\mathrm{cm}$.
