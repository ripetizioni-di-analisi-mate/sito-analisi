A measurement is $(0.6\pm0.03)\,\mathrm m$. What percentage uncertainty is obtained after converting it to centimetres?

- $5\,\%$
- $0.05\,\%$
- $0.0005\,\%$
- $500\,\%$

## Explanation

The conversion gives a centre of $60\,\mathrm{cm}$ and an uncertainty of $3\,\mathrm{cm}$. Their ratio is unchanged.

$\frac{3}{60}=0.05=5\,\%$.
