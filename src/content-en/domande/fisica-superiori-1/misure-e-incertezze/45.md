A measurement is $(0.6\pm0.03)\,\mathrm m$. What percentage uncertainty is obtained after converting it to centimetres?

- $5\,\%$
- $0.05\,\%$
- $0.0005\,\%$
- $500\,\%$

## Explanation

In centimetres the central value is $60\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the uncertainty is $3\,\mathrm{cm}$. Their ratio is $\dfrac{3}{60}=0.05=5\%$. The ratio is the same in metres because the conversion factor multiplies both numerator and denominator. Changing the unit alters the numerical value of absolute uncertainty, while the percentage stays the same.
