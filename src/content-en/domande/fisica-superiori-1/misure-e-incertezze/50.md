A clock displays $r=1.20t+b$ for actual time $t$, with the same $b$ throughout the trial. Its start and stop readings differ by $24\,\mathrm s$. What is the actual duration?

- $24\,\mathrm s$
- $20\,\mathrm s$
- $28.8\,\mathrm s$
- $\dfrac{50}{3}\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

Subtracting the readings makes the offset $b$ appear with opposite signs, so it cancels. The coefficient $1.20$ multiplies both times, so it remains in front of their difference. The actual duration is $\dfrac{24}{1.20}\,\mathrm s=20\,\mathrm s$. Taking a difference removes a constant offset while retaining a scale error.
