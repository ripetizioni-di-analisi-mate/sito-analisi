Absolute uncertainty is halved and the positive central value is unchanged. By what factor is relative uncertainty multiplied?

- $2$
- $0.5$
- $1$
- $0.25$

## Explanation

Absolute uncertainty is the numerator of the ratio. The denominator is constant, so the ratio is also halved.

$\frac{1}{2}=0.5$.
