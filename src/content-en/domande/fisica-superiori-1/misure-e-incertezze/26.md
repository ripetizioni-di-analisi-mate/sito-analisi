Two measurements have a mean of $8\,\mathrm s$. Six more have a mean of $16\,\mathrm s$. What is the mean of all eight measurements, with equal weight per measurement?

- $12\,\mathrm s$
- $14\,\mathrm s$
- $10\,\mathrm s$
- $3\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The first two measurements contribute $2\cdot8=16\,\mathrm s$, and the other six contribute $6\cdot16=96\,\mathrm s$. The total is $112\,\mathrm s$, so the mean is $\dfrac{112}{8}=14\,\mathrm s$. The result is closer to $16\,\mathrm s$ because six of the eight measurements belong to the second group.
