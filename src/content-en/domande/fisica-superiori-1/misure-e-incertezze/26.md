Three measurements have mean $10\,\mathrm s$, and five others have mean $14\,\mathrm s$. What is the mean of all eight with equal weight for each measurement?

- $12\,\mathrm s$
- $12.5\,\mathrm s$
- $11.5\,\mathrm s$
- $3\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The two means describe groups of different sizes. Multiply each mean by its count to recover the sums.

$\frac{3\cdot 10+5\cdot 14}{8}=12.5\,\mathrm s$.
