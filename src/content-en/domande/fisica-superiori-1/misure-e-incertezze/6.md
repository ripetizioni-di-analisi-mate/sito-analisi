An analogue ruler's smallest division is $0.8\,\mathrm{{cm}}$. Using half a division as the reading uncertainty, what uncertainty should be assigned?

- $0.8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $1.6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.08\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The problem specifies half of the smallest division for one reading. Divide the division size by two and keep its unit. The smallest division is the interval between two marks, so distinguish the scale step from the uncertainty assigned when using it.

Thus $\Delta l=\dfrac{0.8}2=0.4\,\mathrm{cm}$.
