A spring scale goes up to $20\,\mathrm N$ and has $0.5\,\mathrm N$ divisions. Which reading exceeds its range?

- $20.5\,\mathrm N$
- $19.5\,\mathrm N$
- $0.5\,\mathrm N$
- $2.0\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The range specifies the upper limit of the scale. The half-newton resolution concerns the gap between distinguishable readings.
