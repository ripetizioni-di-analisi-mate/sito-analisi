A spring scale has a maximum mark of $20\,\mathrm N$ and divisions of $0.5\,\mathrm N$. Which force exceeds its measurement range?

- $20.5\,\mathrm N$
- $18\,\mathrm N$
- $0.5\,\mathrm N$
- $2.0\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The measurement range ends at the highest value marked on the dial. A force of $20.5\,\mathrm N$ exceeds that maximum by $0.5\,\mathrm N$, so measuring it requires another instrument. The $0.5\,\mathrm N$ divisions indicate the spacing between the marks already on the scale.
