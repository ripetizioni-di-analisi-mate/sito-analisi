Three readings in centimetres are $2$, $3$ and $7$. Which symmetric interval has the smallest width while containing all three?

- $(4\pm2.5)\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $(4.5\pm2.5)\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $(4\pm3)\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $(5\pm3)\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

An interval containing $2$ and $7$ must be at least $7-2=5\,\mathrm{cm}$ wide. Its midpoint is $\dfrac{2+7}{2}=4.5$, and half the minimum width is $2.5\,\mathrm{cm}$. The reading $3$ is also included. Using the mean of the three readings as the centre would require a wider interval.
