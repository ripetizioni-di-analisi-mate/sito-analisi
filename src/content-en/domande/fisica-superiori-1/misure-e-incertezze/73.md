A scale reads $52.0$, $51.9$, and $52.1\,\mathrm g$ for a $50\,\mathrm g$ reference. Which description is supported?

- Small spread and a positive mean offset
- Large spread with a correct mean
- Zero mean offset and identical readings
- The resolution is proven to be two grams

## Explanation

The mean reading is $52.0\,\mathrm g$ and the half-range is $\dfrac{52.1-51.9}{2}=0.1\,\mathrm g$. The reference is $50\,\mathrm g$, so the mean is shifted upwards by $2\,\mathrm g$. These two values describe different features of the trial. The readings are close together while their centre lies above the reference.
