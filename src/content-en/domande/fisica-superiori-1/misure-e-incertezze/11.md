Mass and volume have relative uncertainties $8\%$ and $10\%$. Under the conservative first-order rule for $\rho=m/V$, what is the relative uncertainty of density?

- $80\,\%$
- $2\,\%$
- $18\,\%$
- $9\,\%$

## Explanation

The ratio grows if the numerator increases and the denominator decreases. In the conservative case the two deviations therefore act in the same direction on the result. The linear approximation adds the relative uncertainties. With large deviations, dividing the appropriate endpoints gives the bounds directly.

Therefore $\varepsilon_\rho\simeq8\%+10\%=18\%$.
