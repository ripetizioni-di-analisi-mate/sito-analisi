An observer looks straight at an analogue scale and then views it from the side. Which step reduces the parallax error?

- Always add one division to the reading
- Replace centimetres with millimetres
- Add the two readings without averaging
- Look straight at the scale along its normal

## Explanation

The pointer is slightly in front of the plane of the scale marks. Viewed from the side, its apparent position can shift relative to the mark beneath it. Placing the eye directly in front of the pointer, along the normal to the scale, reduces this perspective shift. Use the same viewing position for readings that are to be compared.
