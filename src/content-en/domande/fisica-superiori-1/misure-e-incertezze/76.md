An observer alternates between looking straight at a scale and looking from the side. Which step controls the parallax error?

- Always add one division to the reading
- Replace centimetres with millimetres
- Add the two readings without averaging
- Look straight at the scale along its normal

## Explanation

The viewing angle changes the apparent alignment of pointer and marks. Looking straight at the scale avoids this perspective shift.
