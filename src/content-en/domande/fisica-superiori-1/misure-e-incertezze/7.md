There are $10$ equal intervals between $0$ and $50\,\mathrm N$ on a scale. What is the value of each division?

- $50\,\mathrm N$
- $500\,\mathrm N$
- $5\,\mathrm N$
- $\dfrac{50}{11}\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The scale span is the final value minus the initial value. Divide this span by the number of equal intervals. The marks bound those intervals, so counting the marks at both endpoints as well would give one too many.

One division equals $\dfrac{50}{10}=5\,\mathrm N$.
