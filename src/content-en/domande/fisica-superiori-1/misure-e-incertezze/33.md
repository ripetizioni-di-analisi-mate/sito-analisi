Readings of $12$, $13$, $14\,\mathrm{mm}$ are transcribed in centimetres. What is the new numerical mean?

- $1.3\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $13\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $3.9\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.1\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Dividing every reading by ten converts the lengths to centimetres. The same factor multiplies the mean.

$\frac{12+13+14}{3\cdot 10}=1.3\,\mathrm{cm}$.
