Readings of $12$, $13$, $14\,\mathrm{mm}$ are transcribed in centimetres. What is the new numerical mean?

- $1.3\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $13\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $3.9\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.1\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The mean of the three readings is $\dfrac{12+13+14}{3}=13\,\mathrm{mm}$. Divide that by ten to obtain $1.3\,\mathrm{cm}$. Converting the individual readings first changes all three terms by the same factor. This is why the mean of the converted values equals the converted mean.
