A scale from $20$ to $60\,\mathrm N$ has eleven marks, including endpoints. What is each interval worth?

- $\dfrac{40}{11}\,\mathrm N$
- $6\,\mathrm N$
- $4\,\mathrm N$
- $40\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Eleven marks enclose ten spaces. Divide the difference of the endpoint values by ten.

$\frac{60-20}{10}=4\,\mathrm N$.
