A uniform scale from $20$ to $60\,\mathrm N$ has nine tick marks, including both endpoints. How much does each interval represent?

- $\dfrac{40}{9}\,\mathrm N$
- $6\,\mathrm N$
- $5\,\mathrm N$
- $40\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The nine marks include the two ends, so they enclose eight intervals. The endpoint values differ by $60-20=40\,\mathrm N$, spread equally across those intervals. Each interval is $\dfrac{40}{8}=5\,\mathrm N$. Starting at $20\,\mathrm N$ and adding $5\,\mathrm N$ eight times gives $60\,\mathrm N$.
