A measured change is $(-2.0\pm0.4)\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the upper endpoint?

- $-2.4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2.4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-1.6\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Add the uncertainty to obtain the upper endpoint even when the centre is negative. The new value is less negative.

$-2+0.4=-1.6\,\mathrm{cm}$.
