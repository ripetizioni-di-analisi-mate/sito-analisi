A measured change is $(-2.0\pm0.4)\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the upper endpoint?

- $-2.4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2.4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-1.6\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

To find the upper endpoint, move along the scale towards increasing numbers. Start at $-2.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ and add $0.4\,\mathrm{cm}$ to obtain $-1.6\,\mathrm{cm}$. The negative sign of the centre does not change this rule. Subtracting the uncertainty would give $-2.4\,\mathrm{cm}$, the lower value located to the left on the scale.
