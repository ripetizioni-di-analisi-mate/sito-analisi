Add $0.26$ and $0.26\,\mathrm m$ and round the result to a tenth. What is the value if rounding is postponed until after addition?

- $0.5\,\mathrm m$
- $0.6\,\mathrm m$
- $0.52\,\mathrm m$
- $0.4\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The exact sum is $0.52\,\mathrm m$, which rounds to $0.5$. Rounding the two addends first would give a different result.

$0.5=0.5\,\mathrm m$.
