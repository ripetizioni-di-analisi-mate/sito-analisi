Add $0.26$ and $0.26\,\mathrm m$ and round the result to a tenth. What is the value if rounding is postponed until after addition?

- $0.5\,\mathrm m$
- $0.6\,\mathrm m$
- $0.3\,\mathrm m$
- $0.4\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Keep the hundredths until addition is complete, giving $0.26+0.26=0.52\,\mathrm m$. The nearest tenth is $0.5\,\mathrm m$. If both terms were rounded to $0.3\,\mathrm m$ first, their sum would be $0.6\,\mathrm m$, because the two rounding changes would act in the same direction.
