A rectangle's base and height have relative uncertainties $8\%$ and $9\%$. Using conservative linear propagation, what is the relative uncertainty of its area?

- $72\,\%$
- $17\,\%$
- $1\,\%$
- $8.5\,\%$

## Explanation

Area is the product of two positive dimensions. In the conservative first-order model, each relative deviation enters as an addend. The estimate leaves out the product of the two relative deviations. Exact bounds would instead use products of the corresponding length endpoints.

Therefore $\varepsilon_A\simeq8\%+9\%=17\%$.
