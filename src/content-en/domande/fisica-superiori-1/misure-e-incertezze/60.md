For $A=2x$, measure $x=(10\pm0.6)\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the absolute uncertainty in $A$?

- $0.6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.3\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2.4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The endpoints of $x$ are $9.4$ and $10.6\,\mathrm{cm}$. Multiplying them by two gives $18.8$ and $21.2\,\mathrm{cm}$, symmetric around $20\,\mathrm{cm}$. The uncertainty is therefore $21.2-20=1.2\,\mathrm{cm}$. Doubling the uncertainty assigned to $x$ gives the same answer because the distances from the centre also double.
