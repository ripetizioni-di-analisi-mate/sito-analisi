For $A=2x$, measure $x=(10\pm0.6)\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the absolute uncertainty in $A$?

- $0.6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.3\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2.4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Multiplying the measurement by two doubles both the centre and the distances of the endpoints from it.

$2\cdot 0.6=1.2\,\mathrm{cm}$.
