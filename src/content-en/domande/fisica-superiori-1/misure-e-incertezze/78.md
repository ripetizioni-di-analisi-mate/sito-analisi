A measurement's precision improves: its centre stays $5\,\mathrm g$ and the uncertainty falls from $1$ to $0.2\,\mathrm g$. Where is the $5.7\,\mathrm g$ reference?

- Inside both
- Inside the first interval and outside the second
- Outside both
- Inside the second and outside the first

## Explanation

The first upper endpoint is six grams and the second is $5.2\,\mathrm g$. The reference lies between them.
