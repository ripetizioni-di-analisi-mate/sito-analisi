Two measurements have the same central value of $5\,\mathrm g$ and respective uncertainties of $1$ and $0.2\,\mathrm g$. Which of the two intervals contains the reference value of $5.7\,\mathrm g$?

- Inside both
- Inside the first interval and outside the second
- Outside both
- Inside the second and outside the first

## Explanation

With uncertainty of $1\,\mathrm g$, the interval is $[4;6]\,\mathrm g$ and contains $5.7\,\mathrm g$. With uncertainty of $0.2\,\mathrm g$, it becomes $[4.8;5.2]\,\mathrm g$, and the reference lies above the upper endpoint. The centre stayed the same. The change in the conclusion is due to the narrower interval.
