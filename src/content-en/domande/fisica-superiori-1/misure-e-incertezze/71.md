An empty scale reads $-2\,\mathrm g$. It reads $48\,\mathrm g$ for an object. What mass is recovered assuming the same additive offset?

- $46\,\mathrm g$
- $48\,\mathrm g$
- $50\,\mathrm g$
- $24\,\mathrm g$

## Explanation

The reading is two grams below the actual value. Subtracting the negative offset adds two grams.

$48-\left(-2\right)=50\,\mathrm g$.
