For readings of $8$, $8$, $10$, and $14\,\mathrm{cm}$, which lies outside the interval centred at the mean with radius equal to the half-range?

- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $10\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $14\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $11\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The mean of the four readings is $\dfrac{8+8+10+14}{4}=10\,\mathrm{cm}$. The half-range is $\dfrac{14-8}{2}=3\,\mathrm{cm}$, so the interval centred on the mean is $[7;13]\,\mathrm{cm}$. The reading of $14\,\mathrm{cm}$ exceeds its upper endpoint. The three-centimetre radius would enclose all the data if centred at $11\,\mathrm{cm}$, the midpoint of the extremes.
