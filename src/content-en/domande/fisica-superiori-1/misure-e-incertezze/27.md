For readings of $8$, $8$, $10$, and $14\,\mathrm{cm}$, which lies outside the interval centred at the mean with radius equal to the half-range?

- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $10\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $14\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $11\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The mean is $10$ and the half-range is $3$. The interval from $7$ to $13$ excludes the highest reading.

$14=14\,\mathrm{cm}$.
