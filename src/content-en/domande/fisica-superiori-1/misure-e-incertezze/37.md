Convert $(8.4\pm0.2)\,\mathrm{cm}$ into millimetres. What is the resulting expression?

- $(84\pm2)\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $(84\pm0.2)\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $(8.4\pm2)\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $(0.84\pm0.02)\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

Both the central value and the absolute uncertainty measure a length, so each numerical value changes by a factor of $10$ in millimetres. The new form is $(84\pm2)\,\mathrm{mm}$. As a check, the endpoints of $8.2$ and $8.6\,\mathrm{cm}$ become $82$ and $86\,\mathrm{mm}$, each at a distance of $2\,\mathrm{mm}$ from the new centre.
