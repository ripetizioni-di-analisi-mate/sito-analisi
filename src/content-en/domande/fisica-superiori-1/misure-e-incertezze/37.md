Convert $(8.4\pm0.2)\,\mathrm{cm}$ into millimetres. What is the resulting expression?

- $(84\pm2)\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $(84\pm0.2)\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $(8.4\pm2)\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $(0.84\pm0.02)\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The unit applies to both centre and uncertainty. In millimetres both values are ten times their original numbers.
