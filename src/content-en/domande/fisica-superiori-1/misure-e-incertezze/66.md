The calculated central value is $17.836\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the final uncertainty is $0.05\,\mathrm{cm}$. Round the central value to the nearest value at the same decimal place as the uncertainty. What is the size of the difference introduced by rounding?

- $0.006\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.004\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.036\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.05\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The uncertainty is given to the hundredths place. The value $17.836\,\mathrm{cm}$ is closer to $17.84\,\mathrm{cm}$ than to $17.83\,\mathrm{cm}$. Rounding therefore gives $17.84\,\mathrm{cm}$, a difference of $17.840-17.836=0.004\,\mathrm{cm}$. A difference of $0.006\,\mathrm{cm}$ would result from truncation.
