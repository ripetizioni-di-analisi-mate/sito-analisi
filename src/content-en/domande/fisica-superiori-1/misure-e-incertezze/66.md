If the final uncertainty is $0.05\,\mathrm{cm}$, what central value is reported for $17.836\,\mathrm{cm}$ with rounding to nearest?

- $17.83\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $17.84\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $17.8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $17.836\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The uncertainty is in hundredths. Round the centre to hundredths using the six in the thousandths place.

$17.84=17.84\,\mathrm{cm}$.
