Two positive quantities each have a relative uncertainty of $25\,\%$. If their two upper endpoints are compatible, by what percentage does the maximum product exceed the nominal product?

- $50\,\%$
- $25\,\%$
- $6.25\,\%$
- $56.25\,\%$

## Explanation

The maximum product uses both factors at their upper endpoints, $1.25$ times their nominal values. The product grows by a factor of $1.25^2=1.5625$, an increase of $56.25\,\%$. Adding the two uncertainties gives $50\,\%$ because it leaves out the product of the two deviations, which is $6.25\,\%$.
