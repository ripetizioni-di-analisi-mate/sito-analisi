With two relative uncertainties of $10\%$, what is the exact upper deviation of a positive product as a percentage?

- $20\,\%$
- $10\,\%$
- $1\,\%$
- $21\,\%$

## Explanation

Multiply the two upper factors $1.1$ and subtract one. The product of the linear deviations also appears.

$(1+0.1)^2-1=0.21=21\,\%$.
