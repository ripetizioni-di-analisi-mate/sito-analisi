An interval runs from $6.18$ to $6.58\,\mathrm{cm}$. It is to be reported with centre $6.4\,\mathrm{cm}$ and uncertainty in tenths of a centimetre, retaining all original values. What is the smallest uncertainty?

- $0.2\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.3\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.1\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.4\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

From the new centre to the lower endpoint the distance is $6.40-6.18=0.22\,\mathrm{cm}$. On the other side it is $6.58-6.40=0.18\,\mathrm{cm}$. The radius must reach the farther value, so round $0.22\,\mathrm{cm}$ up to the next tenth. A radius of $0.2\,\mathrm{cm}$ would start at $6.2\,\mathrm{cm}$ and miss part of the original interval.
