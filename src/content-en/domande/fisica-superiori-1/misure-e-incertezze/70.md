Round $1.251\,\mathrm s$ to the nearest tenth. What is the result?

- $1.2\,\mathrm s$
- $1.3\,\mathrm s$
- $1.25\,\mathrm s$
- $1.26\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The number exceeds $1.25$, so it is closer to $1.3$ than to $1.2$.

$1.3=1.3\,\mathrm s$.
