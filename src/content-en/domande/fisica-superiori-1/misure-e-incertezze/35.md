A reading is $(7.2\pm0.3)\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the total width of the interval?

- $0.3\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $6.9\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The uncertainty is the distance from each endpoint to the centre. The entire interval contains two such distances.

$2\cdot 0.3=0.6\,\mathrm{cm}$.
