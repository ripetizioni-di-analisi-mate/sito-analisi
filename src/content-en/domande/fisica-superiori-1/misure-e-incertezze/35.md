A reading is $(7.2\pm0.3)\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the total width of the interval?

- $0.3\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $6.9\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The uncertainty of $0.3\,\mathrm{cm}$ extends on each side of the central value. The lower endpoint is $7.2-0.3=6.9\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the upper one $7.2+0.3=7.5\,\mathrm{cm}$. The full width is $7.5-6.9=0.6\,\mathrm{cm}$, equal to two interval radii. The value after the $\pm$ sign is the distance from the centre to only one endpoint.
