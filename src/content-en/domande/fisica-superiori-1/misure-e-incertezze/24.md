Two readings have mean $15\,\mathrm{cm}$. One is $13\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the other?

- $2\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $28\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $16\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $17\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The sum must be twice the mean. Subtract the one known reading to get the missing one.

$2\cdot 15-13=17\,\mathrm{cm}$.
