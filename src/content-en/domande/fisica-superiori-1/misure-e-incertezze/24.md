Two readings have mean $15\,\mathrm{cm}$. One is $13\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the other?

- $2\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $28\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $26\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $17\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Two readings with mean $15\,\mathrm{cm}$ must add to $2\cdot15=30\,\mathrm{cm}$. Subtracting the known reading of $13\,\mathrm{cm}$ leaves $17\,\mathrm{cm}$ for the other. One check is that $13$ is two centimetres below the mean and $17$ is two above it. With just two readings, those distances from the centre balance.
