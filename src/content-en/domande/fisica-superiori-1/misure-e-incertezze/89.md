A positive distance's absolute uncertainty doubles while its central value triples. By what factor does the relative uncertainty change?

- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $1.5$
- $6$
- $1$

## Explanation

In the fraction defining relative uncertainty, multiply the numerator by two and the denominator by three.

$\frac{2}{3}=\dfrac{2}{3}$.
