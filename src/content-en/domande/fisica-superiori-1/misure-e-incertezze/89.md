A positive distance's absolute uncertainty doubles while its central value triples. By what factor does the relative uncertainty change?

- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $1.5$
- $6$
- $1$

## Explanation

For a positive distance the relative ratio is $\dfrac{\Delta x}{x_m}$. The numerator doubles and the denominator triples, so $\dfrac{2\Delta x}{3x_m}=\dfrac23\cdot\dfrac{\Delta x}{x_m}$. The relative uncertainty falls even though the absolute one rises, because the value it is measured against grows by a still larger factor.
