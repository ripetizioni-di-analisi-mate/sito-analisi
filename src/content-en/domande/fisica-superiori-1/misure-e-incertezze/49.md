Which reading has smaller relative uncertainty, $(200\pm4)\,\mathrm{mm}$ or $(30\pm1)\,\mathrm{mm}$?

- The $200\,\mathrm{mm}$ reading
- The $30\,\mathrm{mm}$ reading
- They have the same relative uncertainty
- The temperature is needed

## Explanation

The first assigned absolute uncertainty is larger, but it is compared with a much larger length. The two ratios are $\dfrac4{200}=0.02$ and $\dfrac1{30}\simeq0.0333$. The lower percentage therefore belongs to the reading centred at $200\,\mathrm{mm}$. The comparison concerns the fraction of the measurement rather than just the widths of the intervals.
