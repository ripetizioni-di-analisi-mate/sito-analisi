In the conservative sum of two lengths, one uncertainty is $2\,\mathrm{mm}$ and the other $0.3\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the combined uncertainty in millimetres?

- $5\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $2.3\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $1\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $6\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

Add the absolute deviations after converting them. Three tenths of a centimetre correspond to three millimetres.

$2+0.3\cdot 10=5\,\mathrm{mm}$.
