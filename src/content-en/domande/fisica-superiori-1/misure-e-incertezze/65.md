In the conservative sum of two lengths, one uncertainty is $2\,\mathrm{mm}$ and the other $0.3\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the combined uncertainty in millimetres?

- $5\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $2.3\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $1\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $6\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

In the conservative model, both terms can deviate to the extreme in the same direction, so add the absolute uncertainties. Convert $0.3\,\mathrm{cm}$ to $3\,\mathrm{mm}$ and add the first contribution of $2\,\mathrm{mm}$. The result is $5\,\mathrm{mm}$. Conversion is necessary before combining the interval widths.
