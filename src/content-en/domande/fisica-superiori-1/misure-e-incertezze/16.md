The $2$ and $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ marks enclose six equal spaces. What is the value at the fourth mark after $2\,\mathrm{cm}$?

- $2\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $4.4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $7\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

From $2$ to $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ there are $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ split into six equal gaps. Each is $\dfrac36=0.5\,\mathrm{cm}$. The fourth mark after the initial one is four gaps away, at $2+4\cdot0.5=4\,\mathrm{cm}$. Add the displacement to the initial reading because the part of the scale in use starts at a mark other than zero.
