The $2$ and $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ marks enclose six equal spaces. What is the value at the fourth mark after $2\,\mathrm{cm}$?

- $2\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $4.4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $7\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Each gap represents $0.5\,\mathrm{cm}$. Add four gaps to the initial reading.

$2+\frac{4\cdot \left(5-2\right)}{6}=4\,\mathrm{cm}$.
