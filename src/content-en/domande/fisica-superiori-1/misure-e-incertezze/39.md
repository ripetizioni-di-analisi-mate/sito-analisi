What point is shared by intervals $[4;6]$ and $[6;8]\,\mathrm{cm}$?

- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $5\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The first interval ends at $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the second starts at that same value. Both intervals are closed, so each contains its endpoint at $6\,\mathrm{cm}$. Any smaller value is outside the second interval and any larger one is outside the first. The only common value is therefore $6\,\mathrm{cm}$.
