What point is shared by intervals $[4;6]$ and $[6;8]\,\mathrm{cm}$?

- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $5\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Closed intervals include their endpoints. The first's upper endpoint is the second's lower endpoint.

$6=6\,\mathrm{cm}$.
