For $R=x/y$, suppose $x\in[4;6]$ and $y\in[2;3]$. What is the minimum ratio?

- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $2$
- $3$
- $0.75$

## Explanation

A positive ratio decreases with a smaller numerator or larger denominator. Use $x=4$ and $y=3$.

$\frac{4}{3}=\dfrac{4}{3}$.
