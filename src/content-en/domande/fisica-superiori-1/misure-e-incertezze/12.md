A square's side has relative uncertainty $8\%$. What is the first-order relative uncertainty of its area?

- $8\,\%$
- $64\,\%$
- $4\,\%$
- $16\,\%$

## Explanation

Area is the side squared. If the side is multiplied by $1+\varepsilon_l$, the area is multiplied by $(1+\varepsilon_l)^2$. Expansion gives two equal linear terms and a quadratic term. The first-order estimate keeps only twice the relative uncertainty in the side.

The power rule gives $\varepsilon_A\simeq2\varepsilon_l=16\%$.
