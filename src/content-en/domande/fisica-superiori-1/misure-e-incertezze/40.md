What is the width of the intersection of $[10.1;10.7]$ and $[10.4;11.0]\,\mathrm{cm}$?

- $0.9\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.3\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

A shared value must satisfy both lower limits, so it must be at least $10.4\,\mathrm{cm}$. It must also respect both upper limits, so it can be no higher than $10.7\,\mathrm{cm}$. The common portion is $[10.4;10.7]\,\mathrm{cm}$, with length $10.7-10.4=0.3\,\mathrm{cm}$.
