What is the width of the intersection of $[10.1;10.7]$ and $[10.4;11.0]\,\mathrm{cm}$?

- $0.9\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.3\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The intersection starts at the larger lower endpoint and ends at the smaller upper endpoint.

$10.7-10.4=0.3\,\mathrm{cm}$.
