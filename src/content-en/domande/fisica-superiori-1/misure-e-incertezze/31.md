The half-range is $0.8\,\mathrm{mm}$ and the protocol gives instrumental uncertainty of $0.05\,\mathrm{cm}$. What larger value is assigned in millimetres?

- $0.5\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $1.3\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.8\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.3\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The instrumental uncertainty is $0.5\,\mathrm{mm}$. The larger quantity is therefore the half-range.

$0.8=0.8\,\mathrm{mm}$.
