The half-range is $0.8\,\mathrm{mm}$ and the protocol gives instrumental uncertainty of $0.05\,\mathrm{cm}$. What larger value is assigned in millimetres?

- $0.5\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $1.3\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.8\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.3\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The choice requires comparing two distances in the same unit. The instrumental uncertainty is $0.05\,\mathrm{cm}=0.5\,\mathrm{mm}$, while the half-range is already $0.8\,\mathrm{mm}$. The protocol asks for the larger value, so assign $0.8\,\mathrm{mm}$. Adding the two would implement a different method from the one specified for this trial.
