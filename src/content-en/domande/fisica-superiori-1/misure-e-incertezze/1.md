Three readings of a length are $24$, $32$, and $40\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is their arithmetic mean?

- $32\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $24\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $40\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $96\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Each reading has equal weight. Add the three readings and divide by three. The extremes are equally far from the middle reading, one above it and one below it, so their shifts cancel and the mean equals the middle reading.

It follows that $\bar l=\dfrac{24+32+40}3=32\,\mathrm{cm}$.
