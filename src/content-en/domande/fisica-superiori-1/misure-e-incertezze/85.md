For $V=Ah$, positive values satisfy $A\in[3;4]\,\mathrm m^2$ and $h\in[2;3]\,\mathrm m$. All combinations are allowed. Which pair of endpoints gives the minimum volume?

- $A=3\,\mathrm m^2$ and $h=2\,\mathrm m$
- $A=4\,\mathrm m^2$ and $h=3\,\mathrm m$
- $A=4\,\mathrm m^2$ and $h=2\,\mathrm m$
- $A=3\,\mathrm m^2$ and $h=3\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The volume is the product of the area and the height. For positive values, increasing one quantity while holding the other fixed increases the product. Because the endpoints may be combined, the minimum uses $3\,\mathrm m^2$ and $2\,\mathrm m$. The resulting volume would be $6\,\mathrm m^3$. Increasing either value above its minimum would make the volume grow.
