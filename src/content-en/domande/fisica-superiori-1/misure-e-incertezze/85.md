For $V=Ah$, positive values satisfy $A\in[3;4]\,\mathrm m^2$ and $h\in[2;3]\,\mathrm m$. Which pair of endpoints gives the minimum volume?

- $A=3\,\mathrm m^2$ and $h=2\,\mathrm m$
- $A=4\,\mathrm m^2$ and $h=3\,\mathrm m$
- $A=4\,\mathrm m^2$ and $h=2\,\mathrm m$
- $A=3\,\mathrm m^2$ and $h=3\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The volume increases with either input when the other is positive. Minimize both to make the volume as small as possible.
