A measurement is $l=(120\pm0.9)\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its relative uncertainty as a fraction?

- $108$
- $\dfrac{400}{3}$
- $0.0075$
- $0.9$

## Explanation

Relative uncertainty is the ratio of the interval's half-width to the central value. Both lengths are in centimetres, so the unit cancels. The result gives the fraction of the central value represented by the uncertainty. Expressing it as a percentage would require multiplying by one hundred.

The ratio is $\varepsilon=\dfrac{0.9}{120}=0.0075$.
