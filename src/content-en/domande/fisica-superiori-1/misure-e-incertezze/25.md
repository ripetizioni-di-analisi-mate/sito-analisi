A reading of $32\,\mathrm g$ is added to three readings with a mean of $20\,\mathrm g$. What is the new mean?

- $23\,\mathrm g$
- $26\,\mathrm g$
- $32\,\mathrm g$
- $92\,\mathrm g$

## Explanation

The initial mean summarizes three readings, so their sum was $3\cdot20=60\,\mathrm g$. The fourth reading brings the sum to $92\,\mathrm g$, and the new mean is $\dfrac{92}{4}=23\,\mathrm g$. The added reading is $12\,\mathrm g$ above the original mean. That difference is distributed across four readings, so the mean rises by $3\,\mathrm g$.
