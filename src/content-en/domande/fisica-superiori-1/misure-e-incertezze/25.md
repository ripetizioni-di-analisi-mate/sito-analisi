A $24\,\mathrm g$ reading is added to three readings with mean $20\,\mathrm g$. What is the new mean?

- $21\,\mathrm g$
- $22\,\mathrm g$
- $24\,\mathrm g$
- $84\,\mathrm g$

## Explanation

The original three readings total sixty grams. Divide the new total by four.

$\frac{3\cdot 20+24}{4}=21\,\mathrm g$.
