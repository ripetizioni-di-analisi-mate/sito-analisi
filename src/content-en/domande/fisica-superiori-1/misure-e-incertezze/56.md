A rectangle has a base in $[2;3]$ and height in $[4;6]\,\mathrm m$. What is its largest possible area?

- $8\,\mathrm m^2$
- $12\,\mathrm m^2$
- $9\,\mathrm m^2$
- $18\,\mathrm m^2$

## Explanation

With positive factors, area increases as either dimension increases. Multiply the two upper endpoints.

$3\cdot 6=18\,\mathrm m^2$.
