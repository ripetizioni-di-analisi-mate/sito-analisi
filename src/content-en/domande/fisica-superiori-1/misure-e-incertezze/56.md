The sides $a$ and $b$ of a rectangle may vary from $2$ to $6\,\mathrm{cm}$, with the exact constraint $a+b=8\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its maximum area?

- $36\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $12\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $24\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $16\,\mathrm{cm}^2$

## Explanation

The constraint gives $b=8-a$ for lengths expressed in centimetres. Their numerical product is $a(8-a)=16-(a-4)^2$. The square being subtracted is at least zero, and $a=4$ is allowed, so the maximum occurs with both sides at $4\,\mathrm{cm}$. Choosing both upper endpoints would violate the constraint on their sum.
