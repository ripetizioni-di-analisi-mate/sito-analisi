Each of two symmetric cords has tension $25\,\mathrm N$ to hold a weight of $30\,\mathrm N$. What is each horizontal component's magnitude?

- $15\,\mathrm{N}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N}$
- $25\,\mathrm{N}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Each cord carries half the weight vertically, or fifteen newtons. Pythagoras gives the other component with twenty-five newtons as the hypotenuse.

$$
T_x=\sqrt{25^2-15^2}\,\mathrm N=20\,\mathrm N.
$$

The calculation gives $\sqrt{25^2-15^2}\,\mathrm N=20\,\mathrm N$. The horizontal components have this same magnitude and opposite directions, while the two vertical contributions add to $30\,\mathrm N$.
