Two vertical springs A and B are fixed at the same height and hold the same ring at rest. In their linear range both are $16\,\mathrm{cm}$ long. Their natural lengths are $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ for A and $14\,\mathrm{cm}$ for B. Their spring constants are $100$ and $200\,\mathrm{N/m}$, respectively. Which spring pulls harder, and by how much?

- B, by $2\,\mathrm N$.
- They pull with the same magnitude.
- A, by $2\,\mathrm N$.
- B, by $4\,\mathrm N$.

## Explanation

The lengths at rest are the same, while the extensions are $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ for A and $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ for B. Using metres, the spring law gives $100\cdot0.06\,\mathrm N=6\,\mathrm N$ for A and $200\cdot0.02\,\mathrm N=4\,\mathrm N$ for B. A pulls harder because its greater extension makes up for its lower stiffness.
