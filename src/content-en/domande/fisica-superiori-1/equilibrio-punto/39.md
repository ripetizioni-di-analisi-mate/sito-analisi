Two vertical springs mounted at the same height have equal unloaded lengths and stiffnesses $100$ and $300\,\mathrm{N/m}$. They support a point weighing $24\,\mathrm N$. What is the force of the stiffer spring?

- $6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$
- $18\,\mathrm{N}$
- $24\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The mounts impose the same deformation. The two upward forces add to the weight, which lets us solve for $x$. The three-times-stiffer spring exerts three times the force, or three quarters of the total weight. The other spring supplies the remaining six newtons.

$$
\begin{aligned}x&=\frac{24}{100+300}\,\mathrm m=0.06\,\mathrm m\\[6pt]F_2&=300\cdot0.06\,\mathrm N=18\,\mathrm N.\end{aligned}
$$
