A rope parallel to a smooth ramp holds a block. A meter in the rope and a sensor for the normal force show the same magnitude. What follows about the height and horizontal base of the ramp?

- The height is twice the base.
- The height equals the base.
- The height is half the base.
- The comparison depends on the block's mass.

## Explanation

The tension measures the component of weight along the ramp. The normal force measures the magnitude of the perpendicular component. In the projection triangle the two legs are proportional to the height and base of the ramp. If the two measured magnitudes agree, those two sides of the ramp triangle must also agree. The weight is a common factor.
