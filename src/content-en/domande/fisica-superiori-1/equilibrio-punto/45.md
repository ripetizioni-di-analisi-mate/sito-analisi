A rope parallel to a smooth ramp balances the component of weight along the ramp. A second device pulls the block outwards, perpendicular to the ramp, with a force of $12\,\mathrm N$. The contact sensor reads a normal force of $20\,\mathrm N$. At what outward pull would the sensor read zero, at the contact threshold?

- $32\,\mathrm N$
- $8\,\mathrm N$
- $20\,\mathrm N$
- $44\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The outward pull and the normal force point in the same direction. In the first measurement they add to $32\,\mathrm N$ and balance the weight component perpendicular to the ramp. When the normal force reaches zero, the pull must balance that same component on its own. The required pull is therefore $32\,\mathrm N$.
