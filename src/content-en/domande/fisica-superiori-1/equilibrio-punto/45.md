A block has weight component $32\,\mathrm N$ into the plane. A guide also exerts $9\,\mathrm N$ perpendicular to and into the plane. What normal reaction is required for equilibrium?

- $41\,\mathrm{N}$
- $23\,\mathrm{N}$
- $32\,\mathrm{N}$
- $9\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

On the perpendicular axis, weight and the guide both press into the plane. The outward normal reaction balances their sum. The check uses axes rotated with the plane, so any forces parallel to it do not affect the result.

$$
N-32\,\mathrm N-9\,\mathrm N=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad N=41\,\mathrm N.
$$
