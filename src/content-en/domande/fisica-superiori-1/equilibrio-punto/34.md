A vertical spring holding a body at rest is $18\,\mathrm{cm}$ long. A small weight is removed within the linear range. What data determine the decrease in length?

- Only the initial length and direction of weight
- The weight removed and stiffness
- Only the natural length
- The field strength without the removed weight

## Explanation

Subtract the two equations $kx=P$. The change in length is the change in weight divided by stiffness. The natural length cancels.

The removed weight and the stiffness are needed. The starting length of $18\,\mathrm{cm}$ then gives the final length by subtraction once the change is expressed in centimetres.
