The angle of two symmetric cords is given from the vertical rather than the horizontal. What factor gives the upward component of each tension?

- The cosine of that angle
- The sine of that angle
- Twice its sine
- The reciprocal of its cosine

## Explanation

The vertical component is the side adjacent to the angle measured from vertical. Multiply the tension magnitude by the cosine.

If the angle to the vertical is zero, the entire tension supports the weight. The cosine is one at that endpoint and falls to zero when the rope is horizontal.
