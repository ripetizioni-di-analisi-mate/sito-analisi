Where should the tension arrow point when a rope runs to a support above and left of the point?

- Leftward and upward along the rope
- Downward and rightward
- Straight up only
- Perpendicular to the rope

## Explanation

A taut rope pulls from the point toward its attachment. Its arrow has both a leftward and an upward component.
