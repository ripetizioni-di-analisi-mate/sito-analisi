Two cords can only pull upward. The point weighs $25\,\mathrm N$, but the first cord is measured at $30\,\mathrm N$. What follows for the second?

- The second must pull up with $5\,\mathrm N$
- It would need negative tension along the assumed direction, so the assumptions are incompatible
- The second is $55\,\mathrm N$
- The second can be tightened regardless of the load

## Explanation

The first cord already supplies more support than needed. A second upward pull cannot reduce that total.

$$
T_2=25\,\mathrm N-30\,\mathrm N=-5\,\mathrm N.
$$

The balance formally gives $T_2=(25-30)\,\mathrm N=-5\,\mathrm N$. The sign would imply a downward push, which the second rope from above cannot exert. A different force is needed or the data must be corrected.
