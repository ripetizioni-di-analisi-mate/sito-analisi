A balancing force has magnitude $13\,\mathrm N$ and horizontal component of magnitude $5\,\mathrm N$. What is its vertical component's magnitude?

- $12\,\mathrm{N}$
- $8\,\mathrm{N}$
- $13\,\mathrm{N}$
- $18\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The axes are perpendicular. Subtract the known component's square from the total's square, then take the square root to recover the vertical magnitude.

$$
E_y=\sqrt{13^2-5^2}\,\mathrm N=12\,\mathrm N.
$$
