An equilibrant has magnitude $13\,\mathrm N$ and a horizontal component of magnitude $12\,\mathrm N$. What is the magnitude of its vertical component?

- $5\,\mathrm N$
- $1\,\mathrm N$
- $13\,\mathrm N$
- $25\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The components are perpendicular, so the square of the total magnitude equals the sum of the squared components. This gives $E_y^2=(13^2-12^2)\,\mathrm{N^2}=25\,\mathrm{N^2}$. The positive square root gives the magnitude of $5\,\mathrm N$. The sign would require knowing whether the vertical component points upwards or downwards.
