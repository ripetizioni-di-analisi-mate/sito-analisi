On a slope the parallel component of weight is $ 45\,\mathrm N$ and maximum static friction is $ 27\,\mathrm N$. Can the block remain stationary with no additional forces?

- Yes, because friction can take any required value
- Yes, because the normal reaction always cancels the whole weight
- No, the maximum static friction is insufficient
- Yes, because maximum static friction is nonzero

## Explanation

Rest would require $45\,\mathrm N$ of uphill friction, beyond the $27\,\mathrm N$ limit. These forces alone cannot keep the body stationary.
