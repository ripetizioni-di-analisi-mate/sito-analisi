The mass held by an ideal vertical spring in its linear range doubles, with $g$ constant. What happens to extension from the natural length?

- It halves
- It is unchanged
- It doubles
- It becomes four times larger

## Explanation

Weight doubles in the same field. The condition $kx=P$ relates extension to weight through the unchanged spring constant.

Writing $x=\dfrac{mg}{k}$ shows that the numerator doubles while the denominator is unchanged. The deformation doubles. Adding it to the natural length gives the spring's total length.
