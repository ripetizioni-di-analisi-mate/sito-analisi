A solution assumes uphill friction and finds a negative component. Its magnitude is below the limit and contact is maintained. What follows?

- Rest is possible with downhill friction
- Rest is impossible whenever friction is negative
- The static coefficient is negative
- The normal reaction points downhill

## Explanation

The component sign points opposite the positive sense. Static admissibility depends on the magnitude, which is below the limit.

The negative component means downhill friction when the positive axis points uphill. The stated limit confirms that the contact can indeed supply the calculated force in that direction.
