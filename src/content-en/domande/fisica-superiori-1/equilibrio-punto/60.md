A ring weighing $100\,\mathrm N$ is supported by two inclined ropes. The horizontal and vertical fractions of the left tension are $\dfrac45$ and $\dfrac35$. For the right rope they are $\dfrac35$ and $\dfrac45$. Which pair gives the left and right tensions, in that order?

- $(62.5;62.5)\,\mathrm N$
- $(80;60)\,\mathrm N$
- $(100;100)\,\mathrm N$
- $(60;80)\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Horizontal cancellation requires $4T_\mathrm s=3T_\mathrm d$. The vertical components must add to $100\,\mathrm N$, giving $3T_\mathrm s+4T_\mathrm d=500\,\mathrm N$. Substituting $T_\mathrm d=\dfrac43 T_\mathrm s$ gives $60\,\mathrm N$ on the left and $80\,\mathrm N$ on the right. Equal magnitudes would leave a nonzero horizontal component.
