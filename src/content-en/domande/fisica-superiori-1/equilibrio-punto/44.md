A second ramp is as high as the first but has twice the sloping length. How do the tensions compare for a parallel rope holding the same weight on either smooth ramp?

- On the second it is twice as large
- They are equal
- On the second it is four times larger
- On the second it is half the first

## Explanation

Tension equals $P h/\ell$. With common weight and height, doubling the sloping length halves the ratio.
