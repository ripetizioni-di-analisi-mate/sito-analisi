A spring with $k=160\,\mathrm{N/m}$ has been tested up to $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ extension. What is the greatest weight for which its equilibrium stays within the verified range?

- $3.2\,\mathrm{N}$
- $16\,\mathrm{N}$
- $800\,\mathrm{N}$
- $8\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The declared limit is the maximum extension. Within the linear range, equilibrium load increases with extension, so the maximum is at the endpoint.

$$
P_{\max}=160\cdot0.05\,\mathrm N=8\,\mathrm N.
$$
