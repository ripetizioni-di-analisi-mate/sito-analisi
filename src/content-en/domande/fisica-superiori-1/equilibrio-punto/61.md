A table exerts $37\,\mathrm N$ of normal reaction on a stationary block weighing $50\,\mathrm N$. The only other vertical force is an upward cord tension. What is it?

- $13\,\mathrm{N}$
- $37\,\mathrm{N}$
- $50\,\mathrm{N}$
- $87\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The table supports part of the weight. The cord supplies the rest because both upward components must add to fifty newtons.

$$
T=(50-37)\,\mathrm N=13\,\mathrm N.
$$
