A second taut cord from above is added to a load which previously hung from a single cord. The load and its static state remain the same. What follows about the vertical component of the first cord's tension?

- It still equals weight if the second carries some of it
- It doubles automatically
- Its direction reverses
- It decreases if the second supplies a positive vertical component

## Explanation

The vertical contributions must sum to the same weight. Adding a positive share reduces the part the first support must provide.
