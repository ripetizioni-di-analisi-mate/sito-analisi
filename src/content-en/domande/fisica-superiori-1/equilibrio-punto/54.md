On a ramp the weight contributes $14\,\mathrm N$ downhill and a rope pulls uphill with $8\,\mathrm N$. With no additional push the normal force is $25\,\mathrm N$, and $\mu_s=0.20$. What is the smallest push perpendicular to and towards the ramp that makes rest possible?

- $1\,\mathrm N$
- $5\,\mathrm N$
- $6\,\mathrm N$
- $30\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Along the ramp, $(14-8)\,\mathrm N=6\,\mathrm N$ of uphill friction is required. The new push is perpendicular to the ramp, so it increases the normal force while leaving the required friction unchanged. To reach a limit of $6\,\mathrm N$ requires $N=\dfrac{6}{0.20}\,\mathrm N=30\,\mathrm N$. The initial normal force is $25\,\mathrm N$, so the additional push is $5\,\mathrm N$.
