Which force has zero component in the uphill direction of a non-adhesive inclined plane?

- The weight of a body on an inclined ramp
- The normal reaction
- A rope parallel to the ramp
- Friction along the ramp when present

## Explanation

The normal reaction is perpendicular to the ramp by definition, whereas the uphill axis is parallel to it. Its projection is zero.
