Each of two symmetric cords can hold up to $22\,\mathrm N$. They form $30^\circ$ with the horizontal. What is the maximum supported weight?

- $11\,\mathrm{N}$
- $44\,\mathrm{N}$
- $33\,\mathrm{N}$
- $22\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Each cord can supply at most eleven newtons of vertical support. Those contributions add, while the horizontal components are opposite.

$$
P_{\max}=2(22\,\mathrm N)\sin30^\circ=22\,\mathrm N.
$$

Use $\sin30^\circ=\dfrac12$, so each tension contributes half its magnitude vertically. The total reaches $22\,\mathrm N$ when both ropes reach their own limit.
