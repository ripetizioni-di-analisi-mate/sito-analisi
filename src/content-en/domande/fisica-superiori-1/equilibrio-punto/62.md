A $40\,\mathrm N$ block rests on a horizontal plane. An oblique rope supplies $12\,\mathrm N$ upward and $5\,\mathrm N$ horizontally, with the latter balanced. What normal reaction acts?

- $35\,\mathrm{N}$
- $28\,\mathrm{N}$
- $48\,\mathrm{N}$
- $52\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The vertical balance determines the normal reaction and includes only the vertical part of the tension. The horizontal component belongs in the other balance.

$$
N=P-T_y=(40-12)\,\mathrm N=28\,\mathrm N.
$$

The vertical equation is $N+12\,\mathrm N-40\,\mathrm N=0$, giving $N=28\,\mathrm N$. The rope reduces the load on the surface by $12\,\mathrm N$, the component that supports part of the weight.
