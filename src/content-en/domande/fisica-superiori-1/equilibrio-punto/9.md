The downslope component of a block's weight on a smooth incline is $ 24\,\mathrm N$. A cord parallel to the incline holds the block still. What tension is required?

- $24\,\mathrm N$
- $40\,\mathrm N$
- $32\,\mathrm N$
- $0\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Along the incline, the cord balances the weight component. Tension points uphill and has magnitude $24\,\mathrm N$.
