At fixed weight the inclination increases from $20^\circ$ to $40^\circ$. What happens to the magnitudes of the two components?

- Parallel increases and perpendicular decreases
- Both increase
- Parallel decreases and perpendicular increases
- Both remain unchanged

## Explanation

In this interval the sine increases and the cosine decreases. Multiply the weight by these respective factors to obtain the components.
