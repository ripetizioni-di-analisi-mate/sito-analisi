Three arrows are arranged head-to-tail and return to their starting point. What does this construction certify for a particle?

- Each force has zero magnitude
- The forces are all perpendicular
- The forces sum to zero
- Their sum is as long as the drawn perimeter

## Explanation

The vector sum goes from the start of the first arrow to the final endpoint. Because the two points coincide, the sum is zero.

Closure cancels both the horizontal and vertical displacements in the construction. That is enough for the balance of a particle. For an extended body, the moments would also have to be checked.
