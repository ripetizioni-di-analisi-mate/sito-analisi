A block rests on a ramp and its parallel rope exactly counters the downhill component of weight. What is the actual friction?

- It is uphill at its limit
- It is zero
- It is downhill at its limit
- It always equals the normal reaction

## Explanation

The non-friction forces already sum to zero along the plane. No extra tangential contribution is required.
