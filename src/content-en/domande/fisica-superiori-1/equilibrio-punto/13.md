Two symmetric cords hold a point weighing $ 64\,\mathrm N$. The vertical component of each tension is four fifths of its magnitude. What is the tension in one cord?

- $40\,\mathrm N$
- $32\,\mathrm N$
- $64\,\mathrm N$
- $80\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Each tension must contribute $32\,\mathrm N$ vertically. Dividing the vertical contribution by $4/5$ gives $40\,\mathrm N$.
