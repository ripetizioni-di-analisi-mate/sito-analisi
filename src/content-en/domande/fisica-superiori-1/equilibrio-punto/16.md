How do you check whether a positive force along $x$ and a negative one along $y$ balance on their own?

- They balance if their magnitudes agree
- They balance because their signs are opposite
- It is enough to move the origin
- They do not balance because each axis retains a nonzero force

## Explanation

Forces on perpendicular axes contribute to separate sums. Opposite signs do not cancel across different axes.
