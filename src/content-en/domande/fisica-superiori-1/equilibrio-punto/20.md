In a problem about a hanging hook, its diagram includes the force with which the hook pulls the rope. How should that arrow be treated?

- It is the ceiling's normal reaction on the hook
- It must be added to the tension on the hook
- It should always be replaced with the rope's weight
- It belongs in the rope's diagram

## Explanation

The rope receives that force. For the hook, use the force of the rope on the hook, directed toward the support.
