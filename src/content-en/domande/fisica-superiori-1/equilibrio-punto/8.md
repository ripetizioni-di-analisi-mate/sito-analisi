A body weighs $ 40\,\mathrm N$ and its weight component parallel to the smooth plane is $ 24\,\mathrm N$. With no other perpendicular forces, what is the normal reaction?

- $24\,\mathrm N$
- $40\,\mathrm N$
- $16\,\mathrm N$
- $32\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The weight is the hypotenuse of the triangle of perpendicular components. The component normal to the plane has magnitude $\sqrt{40^2-24^2}\,\mathrm N=32\,\mathrm N$, balanced by the normal reaction.

Use the positive square root because the question asks for a magnitude. The weight component points into the slope, while the normal force points away from it with the same magnitude.
