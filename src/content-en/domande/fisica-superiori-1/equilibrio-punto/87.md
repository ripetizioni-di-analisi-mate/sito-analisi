Weight contributes $16\,\mathrm N$ downhill. Static friction is limited to $6\,\mathrm N$ and a rope pulls uphill. What is the largest rope tension compatible with rest?

- $10\,\mathrm{N}$
- $16\,\mathrm{N}$
- $22\,\mathrm{N}$
- $6\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

At the largest tension the block would tend to slide uphill. Friction therefore acts downhill at its limit and adds to the downslope weight component.

$$
T_{\max}=(16+6)\,\mathrm N=22\,\mathrm N.
$$
