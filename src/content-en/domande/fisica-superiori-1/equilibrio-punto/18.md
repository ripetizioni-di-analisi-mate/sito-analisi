Two opposing pulls act on a body constrained by a smooth horizontal guide. Its weight and normal force balance. The force meter on the right shows between $9$ and $11\,\mathrm N$ to the right. The one on the left shows between $7$ and $8\,\mathrm N$ to the left. What do these intervals guarantee about the resultant?

- The resultant can be zero.
- The resultant always points to the right.
- The resultant always points to the left.
- The direction of the resultant changes depending on the values within the intervals.

## Explanation

Taking right as positive, the resultant is the difference between the two magnitudes. The smallest difference occurs with the smallest rightward force and the largest leftward force. It is $(9-8)\,\mathrm N=1\,\mathrm N$. Even for this combination the component is positive, so equilibrium is ruled out for every value in the given intervals.
