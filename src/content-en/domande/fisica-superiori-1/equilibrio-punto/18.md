Two ropes pull a point with components $(8;6)\,\mathrm N$ and $(-3;-2)\,\mathrm N$. Which vector balances both forces when added?

- $(5;4)\,\mathrm N$
- $(-5;-4)\,\mathrm N$
- $(-11;-8)\,\mathrm N$
- $(-5;4)\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Add the horizontal components first and the vertical components separately, since the axes are independent. The resultant has components of five and four newtons, both positive. The new force must oppose each with equal magnitude. As a check, the three components sum to zero on both axes.

$$
\vec E=-[(8;6)+(-3;-2)]\,\mathrm N=(-5;-4)\,\mathrm N.
$$
