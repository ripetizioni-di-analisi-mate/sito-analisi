A body of mass $ 9\,\mathrm{kg}$ hangs at rest from a spring with $k=900\,\mathrm{N/m}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$, what is the extension?

- $1\,\mathrm m$
- $0.1\,\mathrm m$
- $0.01\,\mathrm m$
- $10\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Equilibrium gives $kx=mg=90\,\mathrm N$. Dividing by $900\,\mathrm{N/m}$ gives $0.1\,\mathrm m$.
