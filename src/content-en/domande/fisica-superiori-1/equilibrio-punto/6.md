A body of mass $ 9\,\mathrm{kg}$ hangs at rest from a spring with $k=900\,\mathrm{N/m}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$, what is the extension?

- $1\,\mathrm m$
- $0.1\,\mathrm m$
- $0.01\,\mathrm m$
- $10\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Equilibrium gives $kx=mg=90\,\mathrm N$. Dividing by $900\,\mathrm{N/m}$ gives $0.1\,\mathrm m$.

For a spring in equilibrium, the extension is measured from its natural length. Dividing newtons by newtons per metre leaves metres. The result is $10\,\mathrm{cm}$, which at the given stiffness gives back $90\,\mathrm N$.
