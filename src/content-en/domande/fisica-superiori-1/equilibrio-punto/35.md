A vertical spring indicator descends a further $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ when $0.6\,\mathrm N$ of weight is added. What is its stiffness?

- $15\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $0.15\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $150\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $600\,\mathrm{N/m}$

## Explanation

The difference between two equilibrium weights is $k$ times the change in extension. Four millimetres equal $0.004\,\mathrm m$.

$$
k=\frac{0.6\,\mathrm N}{0.004\,\mathrm m}=150\,\mathrm{N/m}.
$$

Dividing the increase in force by the increase in length gives $\dfrac{0.6\,\mathrm N}{0.004\,\mathrm m}=150\,\mathrm{N/m}$. The absolute initial position is not needed to determine this slope.
