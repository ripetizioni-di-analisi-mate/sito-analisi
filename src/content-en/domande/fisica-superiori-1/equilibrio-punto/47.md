A rope initially pulls uphill more strongly than the parallel weight component, then slackens until it pulls less strongly. If the block remains still, how does friction change direction?

- First uphill, then downhill
- Always uphill
- First downhill, then uphill
- Always downhill

## Explanation

Friction balances the tangential resultant of the other forces. Initially the uphill rope wins, later the downhill weight component does.
