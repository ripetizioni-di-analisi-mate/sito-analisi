The same $25\,\mathrm N$ block is tested on two slopes with $\mu_s=0.30$. In each trial a parallel rope pulls uphill with $15\,\mathrm N$. In trial A, $\sin\alpha=\dfrac35$, and in trial B, $\sin\alpha=\dfrac45$. In which trials is rest possible?

- B only
- A and B
- A only
- Neither A nor B

## Explanation

In A, weight along the slope is $25\cdot\dfrac35\,\mathrm N=15\,\mathrm N$, already balanced by the rope. In B it is $20\,\mathrm N$, so $5\,\mathrm N$ of uphill friction is needed. The normal force in B is $25\cdot\dfrac35\,\mathrm N=15\,\mathrm N$, and available friction is $4.5\,\mathrm N$. Only the first trial meets the static limit.
