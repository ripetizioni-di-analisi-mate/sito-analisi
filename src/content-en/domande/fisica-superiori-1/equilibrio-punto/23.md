A load modelled as a point is held by two vertical cords. Only its weight is known. What does equilibrium determine about tensions?

- The difference of the tensions
- Each share, always half the weight
- The sum of the two tensions
- The zero sum of the upward tensions

## Explanation

There is one independent vertical equation in two unknowns. Weight fixes the total, but another datum is needed to find the individual values.
