In all three trials the weight has a $12\,\mathrm N$ component down the ramp. The rope pulls uphill with the tensions shown in the table, and the block stays at rest in every trial. In which trials does static friction point downhill?

| Trial | Tension (N) |
| --- | --- |
| A | 13 |
| B | 9 |
| C | 12 |

- Only in B.
- In A and C.
- Only in A.
- In all three.

## Explanation

Taking uphill as positive, the balance is $f_x=12\,\mathrm N-T$. In A this gives $-1\,\mathrm N$, so friction points downhill. In B it gives $+3\,\mathrm N$, uphill. In C the other forces along the ramp already balance, and friction is zero. The direction of friction depends on the combined effect of the rope and weight.
