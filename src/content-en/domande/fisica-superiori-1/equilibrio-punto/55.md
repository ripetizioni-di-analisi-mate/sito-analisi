Can two perfectly horizontal cords by themselves hold a suspended load with positive weight at rest?

- Yes, with equal tensions
- Yes, with sufficiently large tensions
- No, because they supply no vertical component
- Yes, if the cords pull opposite ways

## Explanation

Whatever the magnitudes of the horizontal tensions, their vertical components are zero. The downward weight is left unbalanced.

The vertical balance would give $0-P=0$, which is impossible for $P>0$. A solution needs another upward force or a setup in which the ropes are inclined.
