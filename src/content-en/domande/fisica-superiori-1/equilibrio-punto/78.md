A vertical wall must support a $16\,\mathrm N$ weight by static friction. There are no other vertical forces and $\mu_s=0.5$. What minimum normal load makes equilibrium possible?

- $8\,\mathrm{N}$
- $32\,\mathrm{N}$
- $16\,\mathrm{N}$
- $64\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Find the normal reaction that lets maximum friction reach the weight. Since the coefficient is one half, available friction is half the normal load. The threshold is therefore twice the weight, shown by the intersection in the graph.

$$
0.5N\geq16\,\mathrm N\quad\Longrightarrow\quad N_{\min}=32\,\mathrm N.
$$
