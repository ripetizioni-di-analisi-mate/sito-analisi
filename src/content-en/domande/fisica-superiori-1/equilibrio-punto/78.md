A wall sensor measures a downward friction force of $3\,\mathrm N$ on a stationary block. The block weighs $24\,\mathrm N$. An angled push holds it against the wall with a horizontal component of $35\,\mathrm N$. With $\mu_s=0.10$ and up taken as positive, what vertical component of the push produces this reading?

- $+21\,\mathrm N$
- $+27\,\mathrm N$
- $+24\,\mathrm N$
- $+3\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Weight and friction both point downwards. The push must balance their sum, so $S_y=(24+3)\,\mathrm N=+27\,\mathrm N$. The sensor reading is consistent with rest because the normal force is $35\,\mathrm N$, which allows up to $3.5\,\mathrm N$ of friction. The vertical push exceeds the weight by exactly the measured $3\,\mathrm N$.
