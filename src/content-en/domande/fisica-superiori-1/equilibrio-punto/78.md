A block of weight $24\,\mathrm N$ is at rest against a vertical wall, pressed by an oblique push whose horizontal component is $35\,\mathrm N$. The wall exerts on the block a friction force of $3\,\mathrm N$ directed downwards. With $\mu_s=0.10$ and upwards taken as positive, what is the vertical component $S_y$ of the push?

- $+21\,\mathrm N$
- $+27\,\mathrm N$
- $+24\,\mathrm N$
- $+3\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Along the vertical act the weight and the friction force, both directed downwards, and the component $S_y$ of the push. Vertical equilibrium gives $S_y-24\,\mathrm N-3\,\mathrm N=0$, so $S_y=+27\,\mathrm N$. Horizontal equilibrium gives $N=35\,\mathrm N$, therefore the maximum static friction is $0.10\cdot35\,\mathrm N=3.5\,\mathrm N$. Since $3\,\mathrm N\leq3.5\,\mathrm N$, the block can remain at rest with the given friction force.
