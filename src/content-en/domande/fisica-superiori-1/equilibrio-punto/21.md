Two devices pull a ring in opposite directions along a smooth horizontal guide. Its weight and normal force balance. The graph gives the magnitudes of the two pulls. At what time is the resultant force on the ring zero?

::grafico{curve="t,3+2*t,0,4;t,9-t,0,4" legenda="\text{Pull to the right};\text{Pull to the left}" punti="0,3,pieno;0,9,pieno;4,11,pieno;4,5,pieno" xmin=0 xmax=4.4 ymin=0 ymax=13 nomi="t (s),F (N)" titolo="The rightward pull rises from 3 to 11 N. The leftward pull drops from 9 to 5 N over the first 4 s."}

- $2\,\mathrm s$
- $1\,\mathrm s$
- $3\,\mathrm s$
- $4\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The two horizontal forces point in opposite directions, so they cancel when their magnitudes agree. Find the crossing of the two lines and read its time coordinate, $2\,\mathrm s$. At that instant each pull measures $7\,\mathrm N$. Before then the leftward pull is greater. Afterwards the rightward pull is greater.
