A body of weight $ 40\,\mathrm N$ is on a smooth inclined plane. The ratio of the height to the sloping length is three fifths. What is the component of weight down the plane?

- $32\,\mathrm N$
- $40\,\mathrm N$
- $24\,\mathrm N$
- $64\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The height-to-length ratio gives the sine of the ramp angle. Multiplying the weight by $3/5$ gives $24\,\mathrm N$ down the slope.

The fraction three fifths is less than one, so the component has smaller magnitude than the weight. If the axis points uphill, the same component is written as $-24\,\mathrm N$.
