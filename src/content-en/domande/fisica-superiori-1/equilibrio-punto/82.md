A point weighing $18\,\mathrm N$ is stationary against the ceiling under a $30\,\mathrm N$ upward push. What is the downward normal reaction?

- $18\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$
- $30\,\mathrm{N}$
- $48\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The ceiling reaction and the weight both point downward. Subtracting weight from the push leaves twelve newtons for the reaction to balance.

$$
30\,\mathrm N-18\,\mathrm N-N=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad N=12\,\mathrm N.
$$
