Two vertical cords support a load of weight $ 80\,\mathrm N$. The tension in the first is $ 24\,\mathrm N$. What is the tension in the second?

- $56\,\mathrm N$
- $104\,\mathrm N$
- $80\,\mathrm N$
- $24\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The first cord carries $24\,\mathrm N$ of the $80\,\mathrm N$ weight. The second therefore has tension $56\,\mathrm N$.
