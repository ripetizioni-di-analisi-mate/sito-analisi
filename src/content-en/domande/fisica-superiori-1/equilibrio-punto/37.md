A ring on a horizontal guide tends to move left. Where must the fixed end of a taut spring lie along the guide to balance it?

- To the right of the ring
- To the left of the ring
- At the ring itself
- Where the spring pushes left

## Explanation

A stretched spring pulls the ring toward its mounting point. The required force points right to oppose the initial tendency.

For the spring force to have a positive component to the right, the attachment must lie to the right of the ring. A left attachment would pull the ring in the direction of the original resultant.
