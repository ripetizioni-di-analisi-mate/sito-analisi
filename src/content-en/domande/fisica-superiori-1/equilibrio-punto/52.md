Two blocks on the same ramp differ in weight and have the same static coefficient. With only weight, normal reaction, and friction, why can the same rest condition apply?

- The normal reaction is the same for different masses
- Friction is always zero on a ramp
- The parallel component never depends on weight
- Weight appears in both the parallel component and the normal reaction and cancels

## Explanation

The inequality is $P\sin\alpha\leq\mu_sP\cos\alpha$. Dividing by the positive weight leaves a comparison of angle and coefficient.
