For the same spring in its linear range, the table gives four equilibrium lengths under different loads. Exactly one length reading is wrong. Which load corresponds to it?

| Load (N) | Length (cm) |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | 12 |
| 4 | 14 |
| 6 | 17 |
| 8 | 18 |

- $6\,\mathrm N$
- $2\,\mathrm N$
- $4\,\mathrm N$
- $8\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

In the linear range, equal increases in load give equal increases in length. The lengths measured at $2$, $4$ and $8\,\mathrm N$ increase by one centimetre per newton. The same rule gives $16\,\mathrm{cm}$ under a load of $6\,\mathrm N$. The recorded length of $17\,\mathrm{cm}$ is the only point off the line.
