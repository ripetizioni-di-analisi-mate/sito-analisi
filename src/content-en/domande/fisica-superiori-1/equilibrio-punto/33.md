A vertical spring with $k=250\,\mathrm{N/m}$ supports a $20\,\mathrm N$ weight at rest. If it is $34\,\mathrm{cm}$ long under load, what is its natural length?

- $26\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $42\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.26\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The spring force at rest has the same magnitude as weight. Dividing weight by stiffness determines the extension in metres. Convert it to centimetres before subtracting it from the measured length. The resulting natural length is smaller than the loaded length.

$$
\begin{aligned}x&=\frac{20}{250}\,\mathrm m=8\,\mathrm{cm}\\[6pt]L_0&=(34-8)\,\mathrm{cm}=26\,\mathrm{cm}.\end{aligned}
$$
