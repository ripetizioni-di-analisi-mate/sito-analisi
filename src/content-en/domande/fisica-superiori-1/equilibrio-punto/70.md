In a plane diagram the resultant has two zero components. Why is its angle to the horizontal unnecessary?

- The angle is always $45^\circ$
- The zero vector has no defined direction
- The resultant is horizontal and cannot be zero
- The resultant is vertical by definition

## Explanation

Two zero components already fix the whole vector. With zero magnitude there is no arrow to orient relative to the axis.
