A point is acted on by $54\,\mathrm N$ of weight, an upward vertical cord, and a cord to the floor pulling down with $12\,\mathrm N$. How much must the first cord pull?

- $66\,\mathrm{N}$
- $42\,\mathrm{N}$
- $54\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

A cord pulls toward its anchor. The lower cord adds to weight, so the upper cord balances both downward contributions.

$$
T=54\,\mathrm N+12\,\mathrm N=66\,\mathrm N.
$$
