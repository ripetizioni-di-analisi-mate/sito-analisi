What distinguishes sustained rest from uniform straight-line motion if both have zero resultant force?

- The initial velocity
- The value of one balanced force
- The origin of the axes
- The choice to model the body as a point

## Explanation

A zero resultant preserves the previous velocity. An initially stationary point stays at rest; otherwise it continues with the given velocity.

For example, a cart that already has nonzero velocity continues changing position even with zero acceleration. Knowing only the force balance does not determine its initial velocity.
