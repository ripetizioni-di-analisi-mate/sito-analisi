A point is pulled $ 27\,\mathrm N$ to the right and $ 36\,\mathrm N$ upward. What is the magnitude of the balancing force?

- $63\,\mathrm N$
- $45\,\mathrm N$
- $9\,\mathrm N$
- $972\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The balancing force has the same magnitude as the resultant and opposite component signs. Pythagoras gives $\sqrt{27^2+36^2}\,\mathrm N=45\,\mathrm N$.

The two given forces are perpendicular. The equilibrant is therefore longer than either leg but shorter than their sum. Its direction, if requested, has both components negative.
