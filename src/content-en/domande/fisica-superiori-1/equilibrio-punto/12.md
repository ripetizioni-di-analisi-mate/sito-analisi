With no other forces, a block must stay at rest on an incline. Its weight components have magnitude $ 27\,\mathrm N$ parallel and $ 36\,\mathrm N$ perpendicular to the plane. What minimum static coefficient is required?

- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{7}$
- $0.25$
- $0.75$

## Explanation

At the threshold, maximum friction equals the required magnitude. The ratio of the parallel component to the normal reaction is $\dfrac{27}{36}=0.75$.
