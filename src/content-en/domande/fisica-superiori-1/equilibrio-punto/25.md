Three cords originally share a total load of $81\,\mathrm N$ equally. A small portion is transferred from one cord to the other two without changing total mass. What is the new sum of the vertical tensions?

- $81\,\mathrm{N}$
- $27\,\mathrm{N}$
- $54\,\mathrm{N}$
- $108\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The body's vertical balance involves its total weight. Redistribution changes individual values while their sum remains the weight at rest.

$$
T_1+T_2+T_3=P=81\,\mathrm N.
$$

In the initial configuration all three tensions are $27\,\mathrm N$. After redistributing the load these individual values may change, while their vertical components must still add to $81\,\mathrm N$.
