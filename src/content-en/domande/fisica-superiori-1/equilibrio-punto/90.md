If the push into a vertical wall drops to zero and there is no adhesion, what static friction magnitude can the model supply?

- The full weight for any mass
- Zero, because the normal load vanishes
- Infinite, because the contact is smooth
- Twice the weight

## Explanation

Horizontally the normal reaction follows the push and is zero with no other actions. Thus the limit $\mu_sN$ is also zero.
