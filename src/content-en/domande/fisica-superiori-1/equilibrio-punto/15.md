A ring receives $12\,\mathrm N$ to the right and $9\,\mathrm N$ downward. What magnitude must a third force have to balance the ring?

- $3\,\mathrm{N}$
- $21\,\mathrm{N}$
- $15\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The resultant of the first two forces has a positive horizontal and negative vertical component. The balancing force reverses both signs. When finding its magnitude, the components are the legs of a right triangle. A magnitude of fifteen newtons is consistent with twelve newtons on one axis and nine on the other.

$$
\vec E=(-12;9)\,\mathrm N,\qquad E=\sqrt{12^2+9^2}\,\mathrm N=15\,\mathrm N.
$$
