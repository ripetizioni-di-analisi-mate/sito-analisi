Only the positive sense of the horizontal axis is reversed. What happens to the physical equilibrium condition for a ring?

- It becomes impossible
- The vertical components reverse signs
- It stays the same because all horizontal components reverse signs together
- The resultant always acquires a positive magnitude

## Explanation

The horizontal sum is multiplied by $-1$. A zero sum remains zero, and the vertical sum is unaffected.

The physical forces keep the same directions. Only the convention for assigning signs to components changes, so the new axis describes the same state of the particle.
