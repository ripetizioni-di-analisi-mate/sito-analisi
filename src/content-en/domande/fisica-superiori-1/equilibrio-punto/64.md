A block rests against a vertical wall under a horizontal push. Which force can balance weight with no other vertical actions?

- The wall's normal reaction
- The push into the wall
- Downward friction from the wall
- Upward friction from the wall

## Explanation

The normal reaction is horizontal because the surface is vertical. Friction is tangential to the wall and can act vertically upward.
