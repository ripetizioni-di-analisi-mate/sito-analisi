An object of mass $ 9\,\mathrm{kg}$ hangs at rest from an ideal vertical cord. With $g=10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$, what is the tension?

- $9\,\mathrm N$
- $0.9\,\mathrm N$
- $90\,\mathrm N$
- $180\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The only forces are downward weight and upward tension. The tension equals $mg=9\cdot10\,\mathrm N=90\,\mathrm N$.

Taking up as positive, the vertical sum is $T-P$. The stationary load requires $T-P=0$, while both horizontal components are already zero. The result is a force in newtons.
