An ideal spring without preload holds a sample at rest. Once the load is removed and a new static state is reached, what extension does the model predict?

- Twice the previous extension
- Zero if the spring's own weight is neglected
- The previous extension with reversed sign
- A permanent deformation equal to the natural length

## Explanation

There is no final load. The condition $kx=0$ and positive stiffness imply zero extension.
