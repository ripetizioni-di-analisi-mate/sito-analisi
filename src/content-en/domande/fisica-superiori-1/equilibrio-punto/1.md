A ring is pulled to the right by $ 40\,\mathrm N$ and left by $ 16\,\mathrm N$. What magnitude must a third horizontal force have for equilibrium?

- $24\,\mathrm N$
- $56\,\mathrm N$
- $16\,\mathrm N$
- $0\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The given forces give $40-16=24\,\mathrm N$ to the right. A force of the same magnitude directed left cancels their sum.

Taking right as positive, the balance is $40\,\mathrm N-16\,\mathrm N+F_{3,x}=0$. This gives $F_{3,x}=-24\,\mathrm N$. The question asks for the magnitude, so choose the positive value and use the sign only to determine direction.
