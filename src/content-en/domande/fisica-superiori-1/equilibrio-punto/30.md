Two vertical ties from the ceiling support a ring. A third tie attached to the floor pulls the ring vertically downwards. The tension of the lower tie increases by $12\,\mathrm N$ and the two upper tensions are adjusted to keep the ring at rest. How does the sum of the upper tensions change?

- $-12\,\mathrm N$
- $+12\,\mathrm N$
- $0\,\mathrm N$
- $+24\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The upper pulls must balance both the weight and the pull towards the floor. The ring has the same weight in both situations. Subtracting the two equilibrium equations leaves only the increase in the lower pull, so the sum of the upper tensions must rise by $12\,\mathrm N$. The balance leaves the division between the supports open.
