A push of $50\,\mathrm N$ holds a block against a wall. If $\mu_s=0.4$, what is the greatest weight friction can support?

- $20\,\mathrm{N}$
- $50\,\mathrm{N}$
- $125\,\mathrm{N}$
- $25\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The push equals the wall's normal reaction. Maximum friction is the coefficient times that reaction and equals weight at the threshold.

$$
P_{\max}=\mu_sS=0.4\cdot50\,\mathrm N=20\,\mathrm N.
$$
