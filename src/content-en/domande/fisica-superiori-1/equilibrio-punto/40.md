With a horizontal plane, weight and the normal reaction are the only forces. What are the weight components at zero inclination?

- Parallel component equal to weight and zero perpendicular component
- Both equal to weight
- Both zero
- Zero parallel component and perpendicular component equal to the weight

## Explanation

At zero angle, $\sin 0=0$ and $\cos 0=1$. The ramp is horizontal, so all the weight is perpendicular to the plane.

The parallel balance already holds because the weight pulls neither right nor left. In the other balance the normal force supplies the full magnitude of the weight upwards.
