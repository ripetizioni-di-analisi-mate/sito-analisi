A point weighing $100\,\mathrm N$ is held on a frictionless ramp with ratio $h/\ell=0.8$. What is the magnitude of its perpendicular reaction with no other normal contributions?

- $80\,\mathrm{N}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N}$
- $60\,\mathrm{N}$
- $100\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

From the geometry $h/\ell$ is the sine of the angle. Pythagoras gives the positive cosine. The normal reaction balances the weight component given by that cosine.

$$
N=100\sqrt{1-0.8^2}\,\mathrm N=60\,\mathrm N.
$$
