A block is held at rest against a vertical wall. Weight and friction are the only vertical forces. What happens to actual friction if the horizontal push increases and the block stays at rest?

- It increases by the same amount as the push
- It vanishes
- It remains equal to weight
- It turns horizontal

## Explanation

The push determines the normal reaction and therefore the limit. Vertical balance requires the same friction magnitude because the weight has not changed.

With no other vertical forces, the tangential reaction must be $f_s=P$ in both trials. Increasing the horizontal push widens the margin between that value and the limit.
