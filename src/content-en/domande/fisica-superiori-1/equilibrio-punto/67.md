If the push against a vertical wall increases and the block stays at rest with weight as the only other vertical force, what happens to actual friction?

- It increases by the same amount as the push
- It vanishes
- It remains equal to weight
- It turns horizontal

## Explanation

The push determines the normal reaction and therefore the limit. Vertical balance requires the same friction magnitude because the weight has not changed.
