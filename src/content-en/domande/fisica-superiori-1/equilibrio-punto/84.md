On a ramp the weight has a $14\,\mathrm N$ downhill component, the normal force is $20\,\mathrm N$ and $\mu_s=0.20$. A parallel rope pulls uphill with $10\,\mathrm N$, and the block is at rest. In a second state of rest, what tension gives static friction of the same magnitude in the opposite direction?

- $14\,\mathrm N$
- $10\,\mathrm N$
- $4\,\mathrm N$
- $18\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Taking uphill as positive, the starting friction is $(14-10)\,\mathrm N=+4\,\mathrm N$. Reversing the direction at the same magnitude requires $f_x=-4\,\mathrm N$. The balance $f_x=14\,\mathrm N-T$ gives $T=18\,\mathrm N$. The static limit is $0.20\cdot20\,\mathrm N=4\,\mathrm N$. Both directions are possible right at the limit.
