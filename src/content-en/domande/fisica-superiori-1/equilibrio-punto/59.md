A horizontal plane supports a weight while an added vertical push acts downward. What is the sign of that push if upward is positive?

- Positive, like the normal reaction
- Zero because it is external
- Negative, like weight
- It depends on the coordinate origin

## Explanation

A component is negative if it points opposite the positive axis. The downward push contributes to the same side of the balance as weight.
