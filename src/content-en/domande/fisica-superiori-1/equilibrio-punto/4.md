A load weighing $ 96\,\mathrm N$ is held by two vertical cords that share the load equally. What is the tension in each?

- $96\,\mathrm N$
- $192\,\mathrm N$
- $24\,\mathrm N$
- $48\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The equal tensions give $2T=96\,\mathrm N$ from the vertical balance. Each cord carries $48\,\mathrm N$.
