A cylinder is pressed against a horizontal ceiling by an upward force. Which way can the ceiling's normal reaction act?

- Vertically upward
- Horizontally leftward
- Tangential to the ceiling
- Vertically downward

## Explanation

The ceiling is horizontal and the body is below it. Its normal reaction is perpendicular and points out of the surface toward the body, hence downward.
