A point is on a non-adhesive horizontal support, and the applied upward force exceeds its weight. If weight, that force, and the normal reaction are the only actions, can it stay at rest in contact?

- No, a downward normal reaction would be needed
- Yes, because the upward normal reaction can grow without limit
- Yes, because weight always equals the normal reaction
- No, because the normal reaction is parallel to the support

## Explanation

The vertical sum vanishes only if the normal reaction equals weight minus pull. A simple support can push upward. A negative value would represent a downward force, which is incompatible with the stated support.

$$
N=P-T<0.
$$
