A body of weight $25\,\mathrm N$ is already at rest on a horizontal floor with no adhesion. A vertical rope pulls it upwards with exactly $25\,\mathrm N$. Weight, tension and the normal force are the only forces. What does the model allow?

- It can remain at rest in contact with zero normal force.
- It can remain at rest only with a $25\,\mathrm N$ upward normal force.
- Separation is unavoidable as soon as the normal force reaches zero.
- The floor must pull the body downwards.

## Explanation

The tension and weight already balance, so the required normal force is zero. Contact without adhesion allows zero normal force as a limiting case. Since the body starts at rest with zero resultant, it can remain in its original position with its surface still touching the floor.
