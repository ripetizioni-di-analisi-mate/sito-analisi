Which relation checks two weight components on perpendicular ramp axes?

- $P_\parallel+P_\perp=P$ in all cases
- $P_\parallel P_\perp=P$
- $P_\perp=P$ on every ramp
- $P_\parallel^2+P_\perp^2=P^2$

## Explanation

The projections form perpendicular legs. The weight magnitude is the hypotenuse, so the squares of the components add.

In symbols, $P_\parallel^2+P_\perp^2=P^2$. This checks the magnitudes. Assigning the signs as well requires checking which directions the weight points along the chosen axes.
