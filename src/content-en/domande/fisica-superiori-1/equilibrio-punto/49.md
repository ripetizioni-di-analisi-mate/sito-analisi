On a ramp the normal reaction is $25\,\mathrm N$ and $\mu_s=0.4$. The downslope weight component is $7\,\mathrm N$. How much extra downhill push can still allow rest?

- $3\,\mathrm{N}$
- $7\,\mathrm{N}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N}$
- $17\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The push adds to the weight component. Friction can counter their sum up to ten newtons, leaving only three newtons of extra push.

$$
S_{\max}=\mu_s N-P_\parallel=(0.4\cdot25-7)\,\mathrm N=3\,\mathrm N.
$$
