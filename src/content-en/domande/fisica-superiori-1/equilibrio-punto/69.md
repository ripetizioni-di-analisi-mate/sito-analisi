A horizontal push of $80\,\mathrm N$ presses a stationary $20\,\mathrm N$ block against a vertical wall. What minimum static coefficient allows the wall to support the block?

- $0.25$
- $4$
- $0.2$
- $0.5$

## Explanation

Horizontally, the normal reaction balances the push. Weight is the only other vertical force, so friction carries all of it. At the threshold, maximum friction reaches the required twenty newtons. The minimum coefficient is their ratio to the normal reaction and has no units.

$$
N=80\,\mathrm N,\quad f=20\,\mathrm N,\quad\mu_{s,\min}=\frac{20}{80}=0.25.
$$
