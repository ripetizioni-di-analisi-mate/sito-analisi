A point receives $8\,\mathrm N$ westward, $12\,\mathrm N$ eastward, and $3\,\mathrm N$ eastward. What is the balancing component with east positive?

- $7\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-7\,\mathrm{N}$
- $23\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-23\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The signed sum leaves seven newtons eastward. The balancing force must cancel the sum and therefore has a negative component.

$$
E_x=-(-8+12+3)\,\mathrm N=-7\,\mathrm N.
$$
