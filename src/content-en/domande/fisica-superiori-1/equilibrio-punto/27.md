A vertical rope from the ceiling supports box A. A second vertical rope joins A to box B below it. A small weight of $6\,\mathrm N$ is moved from A to B. The boxes are at rest before and after the change. How do the tensions in the upper rope and in the rope between the boxes change, respectively?

- Both increase by $6\,\mathrm N$.
- The first drops by $6\,\mathrm N$ and the second stays the same.
- The first stays the same and the second increases by $6\,\mathrm N$.
- The first stays the same and the second drops by $6\,\mathrm N$.

## Explanation

Taking the two boxes together, the upper rope supports the same total weight before and after the change. The rope between the boxes supports only the lower one. Since B receives the small load, its weight increases by $6\,\mathrm N$, and its supporting rope must provide that much additional tension.
