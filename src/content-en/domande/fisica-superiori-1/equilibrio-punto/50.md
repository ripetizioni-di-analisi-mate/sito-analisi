A test with known coefficient gives $f_{\mathrm{rich}}=\mu_sN$. What description of rest is allowed by the model?

- Friction must already be kinetic
- The body can be at rest at the threshold
- The body has lost contact
- Rest always requires a strict inequality

## Explanation

Static equilibrium uses $\leq$. Equality is the boundary beyond which that state of rest cannot continue.
