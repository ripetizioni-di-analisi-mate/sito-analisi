Two forces on a point have resultant $(27;36)\,\mathrm N$. What additional force balances them?

- $(27;36)\,\mathrm N$
- $(-27;-36)\,\mathrm N$
- $(-27;36)\,\mathrm N$
- $(36;-27)\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Reverse both signs so that the sum vanishes along both $x$ and $y$. The required force is $(-27;-36)\,\mathrm N$.
