Two forces on a point have resultant $(27;36)\,\mathrm N$. What additional force balances them?

- $(27;36)\,\mathrm N$
- $(-27;-36)\,\mathrm N$
- $(-27;36)\,\mathrm N$
- $(36;-27)\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Reverse both signs so that the sum vanishes along both $x$ and $y$. The required force is $(-27;-36)\,\mathrm N$.

Along $x$, cancel a positive residual of $27\,\mathrm N$. Along $y$ the residual to cancel is $36\,\mathrm N$. Reversing just one component would leave an unbalanced force on the other axis.
