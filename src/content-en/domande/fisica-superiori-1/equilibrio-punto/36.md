A ring on a smooth horizontal guide is attached to an ideal spring aligned with the guide. The spring can be compressed and its fixed end is to the right of the ring. The spring is $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ long, its natural length is $16\,\mathrm{cm}$, and $k=200\,\mathrm{N/m}$. What external horizontal force holds the ring at rest?

- $8\,\mathrm N$ to the left.
- $32\,\mathrm N$ to the right.
- $24\,\mathrm N$ to the left.
- $8\,\mathrm N$ to the right.

## Explanation

The spring is compressed by $4\,\mathrm{cm}$, or $0.04\,\mathrm m$. It pushes the ring leftwards, away from the fixed end. The elastic force has magnitude $200\cdot0.04\,\mathrm N=8\,\mathrm N$. The guide is smooth, so equilibrium along it requires an external force of the same magnitude to the right.
