A ring of weight $60\,\mathrm N$ is supported by two vertical ropes pulling upwards. Rope A can carry at most $30\,\mathrm N$ and rope B at most $45\,\mathrm N$. The load can be redistributed. What is the full range of allowable tensions in rope A?

- From $0$ to $45\,\mathrm N$.
- From $15$ to $45\,\mathrm N$.
- From $30$ to $45\,\mathrm N$.
- From $15$ to $30\,\mathrm N$.

## Explanation

The tensions must add up to $60\,\mathrm N$. Rope B can carry at most $45\,\mathrm N$, so A must carry at least $15\,\mathrm N$. The upper endpoint of $30\,\mathrm N$ comes from A's own limit. Between these endpoints, the remaining load assigned to B is always positive and within its limit.
