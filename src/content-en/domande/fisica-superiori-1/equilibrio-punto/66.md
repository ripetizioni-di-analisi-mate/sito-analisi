A ring of weight $58\,\mathrm N$ is pulled up and to the left by a rope with tension $30\,\mathrm N$, and up and to the right by a rope with tension $50\,\mathrm N$. The horizontal and vertical factors are $\dfrac45$ and $\dfrac35$ for the left rope, and $\dfrac35$ and $\dfrac45$ for the right. What extra force is needed for equilibrium?

- $6\,\mathrm N$ to the right.
- $6\,\mathrm N$ to the left.
- $18\,\mathrm N$ upwards.
- No additional force.

## Explanation

The upward components are $18$ and $40\,\mathrm N$, adding to the weight. Horizontally the left rope pulls with $24\,\mathrm N$ and the right rope with $30\,\mathrm N$. That leaves $6\,\mathrm N$ to the right. A leftward force of that magnitude is therefore needed, with no additional vertical component.
