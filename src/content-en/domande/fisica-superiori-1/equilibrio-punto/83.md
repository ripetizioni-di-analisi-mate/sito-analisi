A smooth ring can slide on a vertical guide. Which reaction component can the guide supply in the frictionless model?

- Vertical along the guide
- Any vertical force without limit
- Horizontal, perpendicular to the guide
- A couple on the point

## Explanation

The ring can slide along the guide and there is no tangential friction force. The guide reacts perpendicular to its length.
