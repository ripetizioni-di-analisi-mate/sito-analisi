A stationary block on an incline has weight component $ 24\,\mathrm N$ down the slope. Maximum static friction is $ 32\,\mathrm N$. With no other forces parallel to the slope, what is the actual friction magnitude?

- $32\,\mathrm N$
- $24\,\mathrm N$
- $8\,\mathrm N$
- $0\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The required magnitude matches the parallel weight component, $24\,\mathrm N$. Since the limit is $32\,\mathrm N$, the uphill friction can indeed be $24\,\mathrm N$.

There is an $8\,\mathrm N$ margin below the limit. If friction nevertheless reached its maximum of $32\,\mathrm N$, the balance would leave an $8\,\mathrm N$ uphill resultant, which is incompatible with rest.
