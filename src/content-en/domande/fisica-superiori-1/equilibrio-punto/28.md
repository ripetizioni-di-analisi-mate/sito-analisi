A hanging load has mass $4\,\mathrm{kg}$. With $g=9\,\mathrm{N/kg}$ it is supported by two vertical cords, one carrying $25\%$ of the load. How much does the other carry?

- $9\,\mathrm{N}$
- $18\,\mathrm{N}$
- $36\,\mathrm{N}$
- $27\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Total weight is thirty-six newtons. If one cord carries one quarter, the other carries three quarters.

$$
T_2=\frac34(4\cdot9)\,\mathrm N=27\,\mathrm N.
$$
