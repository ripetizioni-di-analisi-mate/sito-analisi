A block is stationary and the applied tangential force gradually rises from zero. What describes the graph of static friction magnitude against the pull until sliding begins?

- A horizontal segment at the threshold for all pulls
- A segment with slope one from the origin
- A line with slope $\mu_s$ against the applied pull
- A curve falling toward zero

## Explanation

In static equilibrium, friction matches the magnitude of the tangential force it balances. The equality holds from the origin to the threshold, so the slope against the pull is one.
