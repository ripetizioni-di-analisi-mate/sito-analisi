A spring with natural length $ 18\,\mathrm{cm}$ reaches a length of $ 22\,\mathrm{cm}$. With $k=400\,\mathrm{N/m}$, what is the magnitude of its elastic force?

- $88\,\mathrm N$
- $1600\,\mathrm N$
- $16\,\mathrm N$
- $4\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The extension is the difference $22-18=4\,\mathrm{cm}$. The law uses this change, or $0.04\,\mathrm m$. With $k=400\,\mathrm{N/m}$ the force is $16\,\mathrm N$.
