A rope pulls a block diagonally upward while its horizontal component is kept fixed. What happens to the static margin if only the vertical component increases, while contact remains?

- It increases because the weight rises
- It stays fixed because the limit depends only on the horizontal component
- It becomes infinite at detachment
- It decreases because the normal reaction falls

## Explanation

The required friction is determined by the unchanged horizontal component. Increasing the vertical support reduces the normal load and therefore the available threshold.

For weight $P$ and upward component $T_y$, the normal force is $P-T_y$ until contact is lost. Increasing $T_y$ reduces the difference between the maximum and required friction.
