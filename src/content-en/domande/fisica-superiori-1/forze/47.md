Two springs give parallel linear segments on a graph of force against total length, but their horizontal intercepts differ. What are their properties?

- Different stiffnesses but the same natural length
- The same force at each length, with different stiffnesses
- The same stiffness but different natural lengths
- The same natural length and stiffness

## Explanation

Parallel lines have equal slopes, so the stiffnesses match. The horizontal coordinates at which force is zero differ, giving different natural lengths.

An equal further extension of either spring requires the same increase in force. But if the two springs have the same total length, the deformation of each is measured from its own intercept.
