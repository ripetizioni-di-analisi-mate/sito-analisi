The same box is held stationary first on a shallow ramp and then on a steeper one. The gravitational field is the same in both trials. How should the two arrows representing the box's weight be drawn at the same scale?

- Vertically downward and equal in length
- Perpendicular to their respective ramps and equal in length
- Vertically downward, with the longer arrow on the steeper ramp
- Parallel to their respective ramps, with the longer arrow on the steeper ramp

## Explanation

Weight is the gravitational force on the box. Since its mass $m$ and the field strength $g$ stay the same, its magnitude $P=mg$ is the same in both trials. The slope changes the components of weight along and perpendicular to the ramp. The arrow for the full weight remains vertically downward.
