To measure $\mu_d$, a box is dragged at constant speed across a horizontal plane. The rope is horizontal and the plane supports all its weight. Which ratio is needed?

- Pull during sliding divided by weight
- Weight divided by the pull during sliding
- Pull at the onset of motion divided by weight
- Pull during sliding divided by speed

## Explanation

At constant speed, the horizontal resultant is zero and the pull equals the magnitude of friction during sliding. The horizontal cord leaves the normal reaction equal to the weight, which supplies the denominator.
