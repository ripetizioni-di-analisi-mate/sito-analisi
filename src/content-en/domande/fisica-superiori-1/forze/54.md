A crate slides across a level surface with a coefficient of kinetic friction of $0.25$. The kinetic friction is $12\,\mathrm{N}$ before an object is removed from the crate and $8\,\mathrm{N}$ afterward. In both trials, weight and the normal force are the only external vertical forces on the crate and its contents. How much does the removed object weigh?

- $4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $16\,\mathrm{N}$
- $32\,\mathrm{N}$
- $48\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

In both trials the normal force supports the full weight on the surface. Since $f_d=\mu_dN$ with the same coefficient, the decrease in friction is one quarter of the decrease in the normal force. The $(12-8)\,\mathrm{N}=4\,\mathrm{N}$ reduction in friction thus corresponds to $\dfrac{4\,\mathrm{N}}{0.25}=16\,\mathrm{N}$ of weight removed.
