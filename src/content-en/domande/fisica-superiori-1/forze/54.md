For the same contact at the same normal load, the static limit is $16\,\mathrm N$ and the kinetic friction is $12\,\mathrm N$. By what percentage is the kinetic coefficient smaller than the static coefficient?

- $75\,\%$
- $25\,\%$
- $\dfrac{100}{3}\,\%$
- $4\,\%$

## Explanation

The ratio of the coefficients can be read from the forces because the normal reaction is shared. The decrease is four newtons out of sixteen. The denominator is the force associated with the static reference. Dividing by twelve would measure a percentage change relative to a different reference.

$$
\frac{\mu_s-\mu_d}{\mu_s}=\frac{16-12}{16}=0.25=25\%.
$$
