A hand holds a ring attached to the right end of a horizontal spring stationary. The other end is fixed. Along the axis pointing to the right, only the hand and the spring exert forces on the ring. In its linear range, the spring has $k=200\,\mathrm{N/m}$ and exerts a force with component $F_{e,x}=-6\,\mathrm{N}$. What are the deformation $x$ and the component of the force from the hand, in that order?

- $-3\,\mathrm{cm},\ -6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $+3\,\mathrm{cm},\ -6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{cm},\ +6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $+3\,\mathrm{cm},\ +6\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The spring pulls to the left, so its right end is extended from its resting position. Hooke's law gives $x=\dfrac{6\,\mathrm{N}}{200\,\mathrm{N/m}}=0.03\,\mathrm{m}=3\,\mathrm{cm}$. Since the end is stationary, the hand must balance the spring's component of $-6\,\mathrm{N}$ with a component of $+6\,\mathrm{N}$.
