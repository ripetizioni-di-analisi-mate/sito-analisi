Two carts connected by an ideal string move to the right. A towing force of $18\,\mathrm N$ acts to the right on the first cart. The horizontal resistive forces are $3\,\mathrm N$ and $5\,\mathrm N$ to the left, one on each cart. The string transmits a tension of $7\,\mathrm N$. What is the net horizontal force on the two-cart system, taking right as positive?

- $+3\,\mathrm N$
- $+17\,\mathrm N$
- $+10\,\mathrm N$
- $+18\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The system boundary contains both carts and the string. Tension enters the individual balances with opposite signs, so it cancels in the sum. The external forces give $R_x=(18-3-5)\,\mathrm N=+10\,\mathrm N$. The positive sign means that the resultant points in the towing direction.
