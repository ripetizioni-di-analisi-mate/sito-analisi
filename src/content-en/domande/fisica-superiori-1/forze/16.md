A stationary magnet on a stand attracts a suspended paper clip without touching it. How is the force of the magnet on the clip classified?

- A normal reaction
- Static friction
- The elastic force of the clip
- A force acting at a distance

## Explanation

A magnetic interaction exists between the magnet and clip even without contact. Normal and frictional forces require surfaces in contact.

The magnet's support can exert a normal force on the magnet itself. That force belongs to a different pair of bodies and does not change the classification of the attraction acting on the paper clip.
