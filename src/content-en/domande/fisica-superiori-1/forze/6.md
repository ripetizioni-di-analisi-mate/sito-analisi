A spring extends by $ 9\,\mathrm{cm}$ under a force of $ 27\,\mathrm N$. What is its spring constant in the linear range?

- $3\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $300\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $30\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $3000\,\mathrm{N/m}$

## Explanation

Since $9\,\mathrm{cm}=0.09\,\mathrm m$, the relation $k=F_e/x$ gives $27\,\mathrm N$ divided by $0.09\,\mathrm m$. This is $300\,\mathrm{N/m}$.
