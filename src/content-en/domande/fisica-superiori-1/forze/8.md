A horizontal spring is compressed by moving its right end $ 8\,\mathrm{cm}$ to the left. In which direction does the spring push that end?

- To the left
- Always upward
- The force is zero because the spring is compressed
- To the right

## Explanation

The moving end lies to the left of its unloaded position. The compressed spring exerts a restoring force toward that position, hence to the right.

With the positive axis pointing right, the deformation is $x=-8\,\mathrm{cm}$. Since $k$ is positive, the component $-kx$ is positive. This determines the direction without knowing the stiffness.
