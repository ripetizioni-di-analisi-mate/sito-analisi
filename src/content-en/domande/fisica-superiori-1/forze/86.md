To estimate a gradient from readings with the same uncertainty, which two measurements should be chosen within a reliably linear interval?

- Two almost coincident measurements
- Two measurements well separated along the interval
- Two measurements both outside the linear interval
- Two measurements with the same elongation and different forces

## Explanation

A slope is a ratio of differences. Increasing the distance between readings makes the differences larger relative to reading uncertainty, provided the model remains linear throughout the interval.

Two readings at the same horizontal coordinate would make the denominator zero. A pair farther apart on the horizontal axis reduces the ratio's sensitivity to the resolution of that axis.
