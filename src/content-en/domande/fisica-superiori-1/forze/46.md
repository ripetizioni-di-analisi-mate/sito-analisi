After the applied force is removed, a spring stays longer than before. Which condition of the elastic model has failed?

- The horizontal orientation of the axis on a graph
- Return to the natural length after unloading
- The definition of the newton
- Positivity of the measured length

## Explanation

Permanent deformation means the length does not return to its initial value when the load is removed. A reversible elastic model does not describe the whole cycle.
