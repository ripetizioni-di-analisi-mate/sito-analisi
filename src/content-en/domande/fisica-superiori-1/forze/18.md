An empty bag hanging from an ideal force gauge weighs $20\,\mathrm{N}$. With the samples inside, it weighs $80\,\mathrm{N}$. The local field strength is $g=10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$. What is the mass of the samples alone?

- $2\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $60\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The gauge supports both the bag and the samples. Subtracting the empty-bag reading from the full-bag reading isolates the weight of the samples. The difference is $(80-20)\,\mathrm{N}=60\,\mathrm{N}$. Since each kilogram weighs $10\,\mathrm{N}$ in that location, the samples have a mass of $6\,\mathrm{kg}$.
