A block is pressed against a horizontal plane. The threshold tangential pull rises from $8$ to $12\,\mathrm N$ as the normal reaction increases by $10\,\mathrm N$. What is $\mu_s$ in the constant-coefficient model?

- $0.4$
- $2.5$
- $0.8$
- $1.2$

## Explanation

The limit depends linearly on the normal reaction. Taking the difference between the measured states eliminates the unknown initial normal reaction and uses its change alone.

$$
\mu_s=\frac{\Delta f_{s,\max}}{\Delta N}=\frac{12-8}{10}=0.4.
$$

With the calculated coefficient, the two normal forces would be $20\,\mathrm N$ and $30\,\mathrm N$. Their difference of $10\,\mathrm N$ reproduces the stated increase between the trials.
