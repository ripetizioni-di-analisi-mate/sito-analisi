A plate is held stationary between two moving horizontal belts. The upper belt moves to the right, with a normal force of $20\,\mathrm N$ and $\mu_d=0.35$. The lower belt moves to the left, with a normal force of $45\,\mathrm N$ and $\mu_d=0.20$. What horizontal component of the applied force keeps the plate stationary, taking right as positive?

- $-2\,\mathrm N$
- $+16\,\mathrm N$
- $+2\,\mathrm N$
- $-16\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Consider sliding relative to the plate at each contact. The upper belt drags it rightwards with $0.35\cdot20\,\mathrm N=7\,\mathrm N$, and the lower belt pulls it leftwards with $0.20\cdot45\,\mathrm N=9\,\mathrm N$. The sum is $-2\,\mathrm N$, so a force of $+2\,\mathrm N$ keeps the plate at rest.
