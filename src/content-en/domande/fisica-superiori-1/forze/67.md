A block slides on a surface and experiences $15\,\mathrm N$ of kinetic friction. The normal reaction is halved while the surfaces and sliding remain the same. What is the new friction magnitude?

- $30\,\mathrm{N}$
- $15\,\mathrm{N}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The kinetic coefficient belongs to the same pair of surfaces and remains constant. The kinetic magnitude is directly proportional to the normal reaction, so it is halved as well.

$$
f'_d=\mu_d\frac N2=\frac{f_d}{2}=7.5\,\mathrm N.
$$
