The natural length of a spring is $20\,\mathrm{cm}$. A spring of $k=250\,\mathrm{N/m}$ is first $18\,\mathrm{cm}$ and then $22\,\mathrm{cm}$ long. With positive direction toward extension, what is the change in $F_{e,x}$?

- $10\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-10\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-5\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The two-centimetre compression produces a positive component of five newtons. The same extension produces a negative component of five newtons. The change is the final component minus the initial one.

$$
\Delta F_{e,x}=(-5-5)\,\mathrm N=-10\,\mathrm N.
$$

Subtracting the initial component from the final one gives $(-5-5)\,\mathrm N=-10\,\mathrm N$. The change is negative because the force switches from the extension direction to the opposite direction.
