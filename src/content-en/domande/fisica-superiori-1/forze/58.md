A resting object is pulled horizontally by a force below its static limit. An extra vertical downward load is applied without changing the pull. What happens to the limit and actual friction?

- The limit and actual friction increase by the same amount
- The limit increases and actual friction is unchanged
- The limit stays the same and actual friction increases
- The limit falls and actual friction vanishes

## Explanation

The vertical load increases the normal reaction and hence the limit. The force to balance along the surface is still the original horizontal pull, so the actual required friction is unchanged.

A normal-force increase of $\Delta N$ raises the limit by $\mu_s\Delta N$ if the coefficient stays constant. The available margin grows without altering the horizontal balance.
