Maximum static friction is $ 16\,\mathrm N$ with normal reaction $ 80\,\mathrm N$. What is the static friction coefficient?

- $5$
- $0.2$
- $0.02$
- $2$

## Explanation

At the threshold, $f_{s,\max}=\mu_s N$. The coefficient is therefore the ratio $\dfrac{16\,\mathrm N}{80\,\mathrm N}=0.2$. The matching units cancel.

The coefficient means that at the threshold the contact supplies twenty percent of the normal force in the tangential direction. Without a threshold measurement, a trial at rest would establish only a lower bound.
