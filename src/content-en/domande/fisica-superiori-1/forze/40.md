A plot of pulling force versus total length shows a straight line meeting the length axis at $11\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is read at the intercept?

- The maximum permissible extension
- The spring constant
- The force measurement error
- The natural length

## Explanation

The horizontal intercept is the length at which the force is zero. In the unpreloaded model it is the natural length; the spring constant is the slope.
