An elastic force has a component of $-7\,\mathrm N$, and an assumed normal reaction evaluates to $-7\,\mathrm N$ as a magnitude. How should these signs be interpreted?

- The component can point in the negative direction, while the magnitude signals an assumption to revisit
- Both signs are forbidden
- Both are automatically possible for a simple contact
- The two values must always be added

## Explanation

The sign of a component is defined by the axis orientation. A simple constraint's magnitude must be nonnegative, so this value shows that the constraint cannot act as assumed.

Reversing the axis changes the sign of the spring-force component. For the support, check instead whether the body loses contact or whether another force is missing from the diagram.
