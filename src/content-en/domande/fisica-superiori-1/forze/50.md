An ideal cord is slack while its end touches a crate. What tension does it transmit along the presumed cord direction?

- Tension equal to the crate's weight
- Zero tension
- Negative tension, because the cord pushes
- Tension equal to the crate's normal reaction

## Explanation

A cord transmits a pull when taut. In the slack state there is no tension along it; any supporting force must come from other contacts.
