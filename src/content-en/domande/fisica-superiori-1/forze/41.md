The slope of a graph using extension in centimetres and force in newtons is $4\,\mathrm{N/cm}$. What is the same stiffness in SI units?

- $400\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $0.04\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $40\,\mathrm{N/m}$

## Explanation

A centimetre is one hundredth of a metre. Because length is in the denominator, expressing the same change in force per metre multiplies the numerical value by one hundred.

$$
4\,\mathrm{N/cm}=\frac{4\,\mathrm N}{0.01\,\mathrm m}=400\,\mathrm{N/m}.
$$
