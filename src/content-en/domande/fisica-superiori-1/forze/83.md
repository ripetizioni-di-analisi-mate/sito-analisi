One push is $6\,\mathrm N$ northward and another is $6\,\mathrm N$ eastward. If both act on one point, what can be deduced without evaluating the exact magnitude?

- The resultant is zero
- The resultant points west
- The resultant points northeast
- The resultant points vertically

## Explanation

Both components are positive along the eastward and northward axes and have the same magnitude. The resultant lies along the bisector of that quadrant.

Equal components place the direction at $45^\circ$ to both east and north. The intermediate arrow can be constructed by translating one force to the tip of the other.
