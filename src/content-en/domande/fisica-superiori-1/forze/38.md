A horizontal spring's moving end is at its natural position while held still by a support. What contribution does an ideal unpreloaded spring make along that direction?

- A force of magnitude $k$
- No elastic force
- A force of magnitude $kL_0$
- A restoring force to the left, independent of $L_0$

## Explanation

The length equals $L_0$, so the deformation is zero. Hooke's law assigns zero force to the unloaded natural state.

The support can hold the end in place while other bodies exert forces on it. This does not change the spring force, which depends on the deformation of the spring at that position.
