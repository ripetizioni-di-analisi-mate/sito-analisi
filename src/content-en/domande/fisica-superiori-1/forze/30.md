Under a steady pull of $12\,\mathrm{N}$, a device needs its spring to extend by at least $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ and at most $4\,\mathrm{cm}$. All four candidate springs operate in their linear range under the test load. Which stiffness meets both conditions?

- $200\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $400\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $800\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{N/m}$

## Explanation

Under the same pull, a stiffer spring extends less. From $k=\dfrac{F}{x}$, the $4\,\mathrm{cm}$ limit requires $k\geq300\,\mathrm{N/m}$ and the $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ limit requires $k\leq600\,\mathrm{N/m}$. The $400\,\mathrm{N/m}$ spring lies within this range and extends by $0.03\,\mathrm{m}$ under the test load.
