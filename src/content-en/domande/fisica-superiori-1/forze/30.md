An ideal spring with $k=200\,\mathrm{N/m}$ extends by $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ at its allowed maximum load. Starting from a load of $3\,\mathrm N$, by how much can the load still increase?

- $10\,\mathrm{N}$
- $7\,\mathrm{N}$
- $3\,\mathrm{N}$
- $13\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ measurement is taken from the natural length, so it gives the total permitted load. The initial load already uses part of that range. Subtract $3\,\mathrm N$ from the $10\,\mathrm N$ limit. As a check using lengths, the starting extension is $1.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ and another $3.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ remains available.

$$
\begin{aligned}F_{\max}&=200\cdot0.05\,\mathrm N=10\,\mathrm N\\[6pt]\Delta F&=(10-3)\,\mathrm N=7\,\mathrm N.\end{aligned}
$$
