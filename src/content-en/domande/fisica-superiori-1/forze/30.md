A spring working in its linear range is subject to a pull of $12\,\mathrm{N}$. Its extension must be at least $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ and at most $4\,\mathrm{cm}$. Which stiffness meets both conditions?

- $200\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $400\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $800\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{N/m}$

## Explanation

By Hooke's law the extension is $x=\dfrac{F}{k}$, so under the same pull a stiffer spring extends less. The condition $x\leq0.04\,\mathrm m$ gives $k\geq\dfrac{12}{0.04}\,\mathrm{N/m}=300\,\mathrm{N/m}$. The condition $x\geq0.02\,\mathrm m$ gives $k\leq\dfrac{12}{0.02}\,\mathrm{N/m}=600\,\mathrm{N/m}$. Therefore the stiffness of $400\,\mathrm{N/m}$ meets both conditions, with an extension of $0.03\,\mathrm m$.
