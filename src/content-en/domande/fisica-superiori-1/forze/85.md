Near a surface, $g$ is taken constant. In which comparison should that assumption be revisited directly?

- Between locations on different celestial bodies
- Between two samples in the same vessel
- Between consecutive weighings on the same table
- Between two masses at the same height in the same location

## Explanation

The approximation assumes that the field changes little across the region of interest. Moving to another celestial body changes that region substantially and requires the new local field.
