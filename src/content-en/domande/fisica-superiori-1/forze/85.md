The same $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ sample is weighed in two locations with a calibrated spring scale. The readings are $(19.8\pm0.1)\,\mathrm N$ and $(19.3\pm0.1)\,\mathrm N$. The stated uncertainties are bounds on measurement error. In the model $P=mg$, can the same value of $g$ fit both readings?

- No, the two weight intervals do not overlap
- Yes, because both measurements use the same sample
- Yes, because the two intervals meet at the mean of the readings
- Yes, because equal error bounds imply equal weights

## Explanation

A single value of $g$ would give a single weight since the mass is the same. The first interval has lower endpoint $19.7\,\mathrm N$, while the second has upper endpoint $19.4\,\mathrm N$. Since $19.7>19.4$, no value of the weight fits both measurements.
