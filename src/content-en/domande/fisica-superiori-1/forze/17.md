In the force diagram of a door being pushed by a person, which arrow is due to the hand?

- The force of the hand on the door
- The force of the door on the hand
- The weight of the hand
- The force of the floor on the person

## Explanation

The diagram contains only actions received by the selected body. The body here is the door, and the hand exerts a contact force on it.

Any forces from the hinges also belong in the door's diagram. The arrow due to the hand is distinct because it refers to a different contact.
