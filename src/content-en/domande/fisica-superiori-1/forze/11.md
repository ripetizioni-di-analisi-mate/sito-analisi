While a crate slides on a horizontal floor, a vertical rope pulls it upwards with $60\,\mathrm N$. The crate weighs $460\,\mathrm N$, remains in contact with the floor and has a coefficient of kinetic friction of $0.20$. What is the magnitude of friction?

- $92\,\mathrm N$
- $104\,\mathrm N$
- $80\,\mathrm N$
- $12\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The floor carries the portion of the weight that the rope does not support. Vertically, $N+60\,\mathrm N-460\,\mathrm N=0$, so $N=400\,\mathrm N$. The surfaces slide, and friction has magnitude $0.20\cdot400\,\mathrm N=80\,\mathrm N$. Using the full weight as the normal force would assign the floor the $60\,\mathrm N$ carried by the rope.
