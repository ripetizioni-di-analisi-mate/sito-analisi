A body slides on a plane with normal reaction $ 400\,\mathrm N$ and kinetic friction coefficient $0.2$. What is the magnitude of friction?

- $400\,\mathrm N$
- $200\,\mathrm N$
- $80\,\mathrm N$
- $2000\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The surfaces slide, so use the kinetic coefficient. The frictional force is $0.2\cdot400\,\mathrm N=80\,\mathrm N$ and opposes the relative sliding.
