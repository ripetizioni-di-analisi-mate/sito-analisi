A spring scale reads $4\,\mathrm N$ for a $500\,\mathrm g$ object. What is the local field in newtons per kilogram?

- $8\,\mathrm{N/kg}$
- $0.008\,\mathrm{N/kg}$
- $2\,\mathrm{N/kg}$
- $125\,\mathrm{N/kg}$

## Explanation

Express the mass in kilograms before dividing the force, since the field is requested per kilogram. Half a kilogram weighs four newtons, so one kilogram in the same field weighs eight.

$$
m=0.5\,\mathrm{kg},\qquad g=\frac{4}{0.5}\,\mathrm{N/kg}=8\,\mathrm{N/kg}.
$$
