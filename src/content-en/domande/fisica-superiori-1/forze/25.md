A spring scale has an unknown fixed offset and is otherwise linear. For samples of $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $5\,\mathrm{kg}$ measured separately, it reads $23\,\mathrm N$ and $53\,\mathrm N$. What is the local value of $g$?

- $10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$
- $11.5\,\mathrm{N/kg}$
- $15\,\mathrm{N/kg}$
- $30\,\mathrm{N/kg}$

## Explanation

The offset is identical in both trials, so subtracting the readings eliminates it. An extra $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ produces an extra $30\,\mathrm N$, giving $g=\dfrac{53-23}{5-2}\,\mathrm{N/kg}=10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$. Substituting this value into the first sample's reading gives an offset of $3\,\mathrm N$.
