Two springs have the same extension but transmit different forces. Which observation identifies the stiffer one?

- It has the longer natural length
- It exerts the greater force magnitude
- Its force arrow points in the direction of extension
- Its force is zero at that extension

## Explanation

For the same deformation, force magnitude is directly proportional to $k$. The spring that supplies the larger restoring force therefore has the larger stiffness.

The common extension is measured relative to each spring's natural length. Equal final lengths alone would not guarantee that the two deformations are equal.
