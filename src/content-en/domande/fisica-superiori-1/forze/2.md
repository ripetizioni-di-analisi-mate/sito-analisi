An object has weight $ 90\,\mathrm N$ at a location where $g=10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$. What is its mass?

- $90\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $9\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $900\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.9\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The given weight is $P=90\,\mathrm N$. To isolate the mass, divide the force by the local field $g=10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$. The result is $9\,\mathrm{kg}$.

Dividing force by force per unit mass gives a mass, since $\mathrm N/(\mathrm{N/kg})=\mathrm{kg}$. Multiplying the recovered mass by the field gives the original $90\,\mathrm N$ as a check.
