A stationary block is pulled horizontally with $ 27\,\mathrm N$. Maximum static friction is $ 45\,\mathrm N$ and there are no other horizontal forces. What is the actual magnitude of friction?

- $45\,\mathrm N$
- $27\,\mathrm N$
- $18\,\mathrm N$
- $0\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

To stay at rest, the block requires $27\,\mathrm N$ of friction opposite the pull. Since this requirement lies below the $45\,\mathrm N$ limit, static equilibrium is possible.
