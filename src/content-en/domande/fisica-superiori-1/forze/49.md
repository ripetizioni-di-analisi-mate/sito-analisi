A smooth surface is inclined to the horizontal. In what direction is the normal reaction on the contacting box oriented?

- Perpendicular to and pointing out of the plane
- Vertically upward in every case
- Parallel to the plane and uphill
- Parallel to the plane and downhill

## Explanation

The normal is defined by the contact geometry. An inclined surface has a perpendicular direction tilted from vertical. The support reaction points out of the surface.
