A ring is pulled horizontally in opposite directions by two stretched springs, each initially exerting a tension of $8\,\mathrm N$. The left spring has $k_\mathrm s=100\,\mathrm{N/m}$ and the right spring has $k_\mathrm d=200\,\mathrm{N/m}$. The ring is displaced $1\,\mathrm{cm}$ to the right and both springs stay in their linear ranges. What is the component of the resultant spring force, taking right as positive?

::grafico{assi=0 linee="-4,-0.3 -4,0.3;4,-0.3 4,0.3;-4,0 -3.5,0 -3,0.24 -2.5,-0.24 -2,0.24 -1.5,-0.24 -1,0.24 -0.5,0 0,0;0,0 0.5,0 1,0.24 1.5,-0.24 2,0.24 2.5,-0.24 3,0.24 3.5,0 4,0" vettori="0,0.67,1.1,0.67,u" punti="0,0,pieno,O" xmin=-4.5 xmax=4.5 ymin=-0.6 ymax=1.1 titolo="The supports stay fixed. The displacement u is measured from O to the right."}

- $+3\,\mathrm N$
- $-1\,\mathrm N$
- $-15\,\mathrm N$
- $-3\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The displacement stretches the left spring and shortens the right one. The leftward pull increases by $1\,\mathrm N$ and the rightward pull decreases by $2\,\mathrm N$. Thus $F_x=(8-2)\,\mathrm N-(8+1)\,\mathrm N=-3\,\mathrm N$. The resultant pulls the ring back towards equilibrium.
