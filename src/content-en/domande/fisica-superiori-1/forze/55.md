A body is at rest on a horizontal support with no adhesion. It is subject to its weight, the contact force from the support and a vertical upward pull of magnitude equal to its weight. What characterizes the contact at that instant?

- The normal reaction is twice the weight
- The support must pull downward
- The normal reaction is zero
- The upward pull increases the maximum friction

## Explanation

The pull alone balances the weight, so the support supplies no additional vertical force. The zero normal reaction is the boundary case of contact with no mutual pressing.

Increasing tension further would require the surface to support the body by pulling downwards. A simple support cannot supply that force, so beyond the threshold the model changes.
