A trial with a horizontal pull of $21\,\mathrm N$ leaves a body at rest with normal reaction $70\,\mathrm N$. What lower bound does this impose on $\mu_s$?

- $0.07$
- $0.21$
- $0.3$
- $\dfrac{10}{3}$

## Explanation

The stationary body requires twenty-one newtons of friction, but the test need not reach the threshold. The static limit must be at least as large as the required friction.

$$
\mu_sN\geq21\,\mathrm N\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\mu_s\geq\frac{21}{70}=0.3.
$$
