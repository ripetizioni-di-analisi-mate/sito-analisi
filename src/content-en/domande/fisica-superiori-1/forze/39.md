The line shows the pull $F$ predicted by a spring model as a function of extension $x$. The extension is set to $x=5\,\mathrm{cm}$, and the pull is measured with an instrument whose error has a magnitude of at most $1\,\mathrm{N}$. Which reading is consistent with the model?

::grafico{f="2*x" nomi="x (cm),F (N)" punti="5,10,pieno" etichette="5.1,10.72,A" segmenti="5,0,5,10;0,10,5,10" xmin=0 xmax=6.4 ymin=0 ymax=13 titolo="Point A marks the model prediction at an extension of 5 cm."}

- $7\,\mathrm{N}$
- $8\,\mathrm{N}$
- $11\,\mathrm{N}$
- $13\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The vertical guide at $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ meets the line at $10\,\mathrm{N}$. Since the allowed error is at most $1\,\mathrm{N}$, compatible readings range from $9\,\mathrm{N}$ to $11\,\mathrm{N}$, inclusive. The $11\,\mathrm{N}$ reading is at the upper limit. The $8\,\mathrm{N}$ reading differs from the prediction by $2\,\mathrm{N}$, exceeding the allowed error.
