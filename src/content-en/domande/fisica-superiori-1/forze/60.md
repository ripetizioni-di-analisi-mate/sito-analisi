A box weighing $52\,\mathrm N$ rests on a table. A box weighing $20\,\mathrm N$ rests on top of it and is pulled vertically upwards with $12\,\mathrm N$. Both are stationary. Which pair gives the normal force from the table followed by the normal force between the boxes?

- $(72;20)\,\mathrm N$
- $(60;32)\,\mathrm N$
- $(40;8)\,\mathrm N$
- $(60;8)\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

For the upper box alone, the rope supports $12\,\mathrm N$ of its $20\,\mathrm N$ weight. The contact therefore transmits the remaining $8\,\mathrm N$. The table must support the lower box's weight plus this load, or $(52+8)\,\mathrm N=60\,\mathrm N$. Both normal forces are positive, consistent with both contacts being maintained.
