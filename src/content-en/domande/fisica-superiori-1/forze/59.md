A stationary block weighing $60\,\mathrm N$ has $\mu_s=0.4$. A vertical cord pulls upward with $10\,\mathrm N$. What is the greatest horizontal pull compatible with rest?

- $24\,\mathrm{N}$
- $28\,\mathrm{N}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

First calculate the normal reaction, since the vertical cord carries part of the weight. The coefficient times the fifty-newton normal reaction gives the threshold horizontal pull.

$$
F_{\max}=\mu_s(P-T)=0.4(60-10)\,\mathrm N=20\,\mathrm N.
$$

At the limit, friction matches the largest sustainable pull in magnitude and opposes it. A greater horizontal pull would require more friction than the contact can supply.
