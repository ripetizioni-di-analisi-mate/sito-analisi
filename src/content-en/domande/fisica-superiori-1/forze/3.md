A sample of mass $ 8\,\mathrm{kg}$ weighs $ 48\,\mathrm N$ on a celestial body. What is the local value of $g$?

- $48\,\mathrm{N/kg}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}\,\mathrm{N/kg}$
- $6\,\mathrm{N/kg}$
- $384\,\mathrm{N/kg}$

## Explanation

The field measures gravitational force per unit mass. Dividing the weight of $48\,\mathrm N$ by the mass of $8\,\mathrm{kg}$ gives $6\,\mathrm{N/kg}$.
