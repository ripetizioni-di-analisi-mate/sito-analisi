Sole A has $\mu_s=0.6$ and sole B has $\mu_s=0.4$. If they must have the same friction threshold and A has a $20\,\mathrm N$ normal reaction, what normal reaction is required on B?

- $20\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$
- $50\,\mathrm{N}$
- $30\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

A lower coefficient requires a larger normal load to reach the same threshold. For contact B the product must still be twelve newtons, the threshold at contact A.

$$
\mu_{s,A}N_A=0.6\cdot20\,\mathrm N=12\,\mathrm N,\qquad N_B=\frac{12}{0.4}\,\mathrm N=30\,\mathrm N.
$$
