A spring pulls a block to the left with $8\,\mathrm N$. A rope pulls it to the right with adjustable magnitude $F$. The maximum static friction is $3\,\mathrm N$. For what range of $F$ can the block remain at rest?

- $0\leq F\leq3\,\mathrm N$
- $0\leq F\leq11\,\mathrm N$
- $8\,\mathrm N\leq F\leq11\,\mathrm N$
- $5\,\mathrm N\leq F\leq11\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Rest requires a frictional component $f_x=8\,\mathrm N-F$. Its magnitude must stay within the $3\,\mathrm N$ limit, giving $5\,\mathrm N\leq F\leq11\,\mathrm N$. At the lower end friction acts in the direction of the rope. At the upper end it acts against the rope. Friction is zero when the two pulls match.
