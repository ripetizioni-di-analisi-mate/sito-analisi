Two horizontal forces of $11\,\mathrm N$ and $7\,\mathrm N$ act to the left and right respectively. With rightward positive, what is the total component?

- $4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $18\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-18\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The oriented axis gives the leftward force a negative component. The sign of the sum gives the resultant's direction; its magnitude is $4\,\mathrm N$.

$$
R_x=(-11+7)\,\mathrm N=-4\,\mathrm N.
$$

The signed calculation is $R_x=(-11+7)\,\mathrm N=-4\,\mathrm N$. If the axis pointed left instead, the same physical arrow would have component $+4\,\mathrm N$.
