A student obtains $\mu_s=2$ from exact threshold measurements. Which statement about the form of the model alone is correct?

- The ratio is dimensionless and the formula itself imposes no upper bound of one
- The coefficient is in newtons when it exceeds one
- Every coefficient greater than one must be inverted
- The result is impossible because all force magnitudes must be below the normal reaction

## Explanation

The static coefficient is a ratio of force magnitudes. The units cancel, and a ratio greater than one describes a tangential threshold above the normal reaction, provided the measurements support the model.
