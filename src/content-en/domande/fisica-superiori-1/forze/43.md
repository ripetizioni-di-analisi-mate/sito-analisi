A graph has force in newtons on the horizontal axis and extension in metres on the vertical axis. Its slope is $0.004\,\mathrm{m/N}$. What is the stiffness?

- $0.004\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $25\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $250\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{N/m}$

## Explanation

The measured slope is deformation divided by force. Stiffness takes those quantities in the opposite order, so it is the reciprocal of the slope.

$$
k=\frac{1}{0.004\,\mathrm{m/N}}=250\,\mathrm{N/m}.
$$
