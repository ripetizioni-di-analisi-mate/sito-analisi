An object has mass $ 9\,\mathrm{kg}$. Using $g=10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$, what is its weight?

- $90\,\mathrm N$
- $9\,\mathrm N$
- $0.9\,\mathrm N$
- $19\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Weight acts downward and has magnitude $P=mg$. Multiply $9\,\mathrm{kg}$ by $10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$ to obtain $90\,\mathrm N$. Kilograms cancel in the product.
