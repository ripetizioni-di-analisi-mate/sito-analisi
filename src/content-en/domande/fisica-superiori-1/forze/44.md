The instrument measuring forces erroneously adds $2\,\mathrm N$ to every reading for a linear spring. What feature remains unchanged in the resulting line?

- The zero-reading point
- The vertical intercept of the line
- The force reading at every extension
- The slope of the line

## Explanation

The same additive error affects both ordinates and cancels when they are subtracted. Individual ordinates and the intercept change.
