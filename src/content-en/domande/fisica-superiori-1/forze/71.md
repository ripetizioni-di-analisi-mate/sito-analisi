A student calculates weight by adding mass directly to field strength. Which check detects the mistake before doing arithmetic?

- Check the resolution of the balance
- Convert the field strength from $\mathrm{N/kg}$ to $\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- Check whether the two terms have the same unit
- Round both values to the same decimal place

## Explanation

Addition requires quantities of the same dimension. Kilograms and newtons per kilogram represent different quantities; their product gives newtons and can represent weight.

For example, a $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ body in a field of $10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$ weighs $20\,\mathrm N$. The field value means ten newtons for each of the two kilograms.
