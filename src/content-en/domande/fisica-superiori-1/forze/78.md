A plate compresses a spring of stiffness $100\,\mathrm{N/m}$ from its natural length. After the first $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ of travel, it meets a second, unloaded spring of stiffness $200\,\mathrm{N/m}$ mounted in parallel. Both springs respond linearly. What is the magnitude of their total force at $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ of travel?

- $9\,\mathrm N$
- $5\,\mathrm N$
- $3\,\mathrm N$
- $7\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The first spring is active throughout the travel and is compressed by $0.03\,\mathrm m$. The second is compressed only during the centimetre after contact. Both springs push on the plate in the same direction. Adding the magnitudes gives $(100\cdot0.03+200\cdot0.01)\,\mathrm N=5\,\mathrm N$.
