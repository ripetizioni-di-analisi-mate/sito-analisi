A block of mass $ 8\,\mathrm{kg}$ stands at rest on a table. An additional force of $ 16\,\mathrm N$ pushes it vertically downward. With $g=10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$, what is the normal reaction?

- $64\,\mathrm N$
- $80\,\mathrm N$
- $16\,\mathrm N$
- $96\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The weight is $80\,\mathrm N$ and the $16\,\mathrm N$ push acts in the same vertical direction. The upward normal reaction balances both, giving $96\,\mathrm N$.

Taking up as positive, equilibrium gives $N-80\,\mathrm N-16\,\mathrm N=0$. The support reading exceeds the weight by $16\,\mathrm N$ because it also balances the extra push.
