In a scale diagram, a $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ arrow represents $12\,\mathrm N$. What changes if the entire drawing is enlarged by a factor of two?

- The physical magnitude represented by the arrow
- The sense of the force
- The SI unit of magnitude
- The drawing length representing one newton

## Explanation

Enlarging the drawing doubles every drawing length without changing the forces represented. The scale goes from $3/12$ to $6/12$ drawing centimetres per newton.

A $6\,\mathrm N$ force would be drawn with an arrow $1.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ long before enlargement and $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ long afterwards. Both drawing lengths represent the same force magnitude.
