A resting block is pulled first to the right and then to the left by the same magnitude, below threshold in both trials. How do the static friction forces compare?

- Equal magnitudes and the same direction
- Zero magnitude in the second trial
- It necessarily reaches the threshold in the second trial
- Equal magnitudes and opposite directions

## Explanation

Friction balances the pull in each trial. Reversing the pull reverses the required direction while its magnitude stays unchanged.

Taking right as positive, the first trial requires a negative friction component and the second a positive one. The limit is unchanged between trials if the normal load stays the same.
