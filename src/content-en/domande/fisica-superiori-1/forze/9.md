A block has a normal reaction of $ 180\,\mathrm N$ and static friction coefficient $0.3$. What is the maximum magnitude of static friction?

- $54\,\mathrm N$
- $180\,\mathrm N$
- $600\,\mathrm N$
- $540\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The static limit is $\mu_s N=0.3\cdot180\,\mathrm N=54\,\mathrm N$. It gives the greatest frictional force the contact can exert without slipping.
