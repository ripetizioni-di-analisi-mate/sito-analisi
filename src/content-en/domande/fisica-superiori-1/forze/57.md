A rough level surface exerts a total contact force of magnitude $13\,\mathrm{N}$ on a stationary box. The component perpendicular to the surface has magnitude $12\,\mathrm{N}$. What is the magnitude of the static friction component?

- $5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$
- $25\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The contact force is the vector sum of the normal force and static friction, which are perpendicular. Their magnitudes form the legs of a right triangle, and the total force magnitude is the hypotenuse. By the Pythagorean theorem, $f_s=\sqrt{13^2-12^2}\,\mathrm{N}=5\,\mathrm{N}$. Direct subtraction of magnitudes would require components along the same line.
