A block stays at rest while two horizontal cords pull with equal magnitudes in opposite directions. The table is rough. What value must static friction have?

- It is zero
- It is at the maximum
- It equals the magnitude of one pull
- It is twice the magnitude of one pull

## Explanation

The two horizontal components of tension already cancel. A nonzero horizontal friction component would spoil the force balance. The roughness permits static friction when it is needed, but this situation requires zero friction.

$$
T-T+f_x=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad f_x=0.
$$
