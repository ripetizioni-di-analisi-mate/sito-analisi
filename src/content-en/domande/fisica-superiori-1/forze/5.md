An ideal spring with $k=800\,\mathrm{N/m}$ exerts a force of magnitude $ 40\,\mathrm N$. What is the magnitude of its extension?

- $0.05\,\mathrm m$
- $20\,\mathrm m$
- $0.2\,\mathrm m$
- $0.002\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

In the linear range, divide the force magnitude by the spring constant. The quotient $\dfrac{40}{800}=0.05$ is measured in metres, equivalent to five centimetres.

As a reverse check, an extension of $0.05\,\mathrm m$ multiplied by $800\,\mathrm{N/m}$ gives the measured magnitude of $40\,\mathrm N$. The magnitude is enough here because the question specifies extension.
