A stationary box weighing $90\,\mathrm N$ is pushed down by $18\,\mathrm N$ and pulled up by $30\,\mathrm N$. What is the normal reaction of the horizontal surface?

- $108\,\mathrm{N}$
- $102\,\mathrm{N}$
- $78\,\mathrm{N}$
- $138\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The push and weight have the same sign because both act downward. The cord supports $30\,\mathrm N$ of the total downward load of $108\,\mathrm N$. The surface supports the rest. Adding the $78\,\mathrm N$ normal reaction to the $30\,\mathrm N$ tension checks that the upward forces total $108\,\mathrm N$.

$$
N+30\,\mathrm N-18\,\mathrm N-90\,\mathrm N=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad N=78\,\mathrm N.
$$
