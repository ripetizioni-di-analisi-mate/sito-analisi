A box weighing $12\,\mathrm{N}$ rests on a surface. A hand pulls it vertically upward with $15\,\mathrm{N}$. The surface has no adhesive. Which prediction is physically possible?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="-1.1,0 1.1,0 1.1,1.25 -1.1,1.25" linee="-2.6,0 2.6,0;0,1.25 0,2.85;-0.13,2.6 0,2.85 0.13,2.6;0,0.75 0,-0.53;-0.13,-0.28 0,-0.53 0.13,-0.28" etichette="0.2,2.25,pull 15 N;0.2,-0.45,weight 12 N" xmin=-3 xmax=3 ymin=-0.95 ymax=3.25 legenda=0 titolo="The box on the support and the two known forces"}

- The surface exerts $3\,\mathrm{N}$ downward.
- The normal force remains $12\,\mathrm{N}$ as the box lifts off.
- The box lifts off and the normal force becomes zero.
- The box remains at rest with a normal force of $27\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

Maintaining equilibrium would require an extra $3\,\mathrm{N}$ downward. The surface can only push the box upward while they touch. The pull exceeds the weight and the box lifts off.
