The perpendicular components of the resultant on a sled are $F_x=3\,\mathrm N$ and $F_y=-4\,\mathrm N$. What is its magnitude?

- $1\,\mathrm{N}$
- $5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $7\,\mathrm{N}$
- $25\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The component magnitudes are the legs of a right triangle, and the resultant is the hypotenuse. The negative sign of $F_y$ indicates that the vertical component points opposite to the positive $y$-direction. Squaring that component gives a positive value.

$$
R=\sqrt{3^2+(-4)^2}\,\mathrm N=5\,\mathrm N.
$$
