A sensor measures the two perpendicular components of a force, $F_x=3\,\mathrm N$ and $F_y=-4\,\mathrm N$. What is the magnitude of the resultant in the coordinate plane?

- $1\,\mathrm{N}$
- $5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $7\,\mathrm{N}$
- $25\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The two contributions are perpendicular, so the component magnitudes are the legs of a right triangle. The negative sign of $F_y$ points the arrow into the lower half-plane and disappears when squared.

$$
R=\sqrt{3^2+(-4)^2}\,\mathrm N=5\,\mathrm N.
$$
