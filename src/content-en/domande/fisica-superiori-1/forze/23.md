Two samples are transferred from a location with field $g_1$ to one with field $g_2$. Which ratio is unchanged for the samples at a common location?

- The ratio of the first sample's weight to the second sample's mass
- The ratio of a sample's weight to its mass
- The ratio of the two weights
- The ratio of the first sample's weights at the two locations

## Explanation

Both masses are multiplied by the same $g$ when the samples are at the same site. The common factor cancels, leaving a ratio that depends only on their masses.

$$
\frac{P_1}{P_2}=\frac{m_1g}{m_2g}=\frac{m_1}{m_2}.
$$

The comparison is between the two weights at the same site, so the same field appears in numerator and denominator. The weight of an individual sample can change when it is moved.
