A linear spring of stiffness $125\,\mathrm{N/m}$ is extended from $1$ to $5\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the increase in elastic force magnitude?

- $6.25\,\mathrm{N}$
- $5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1.25\,\mathrm{N}$
- $500\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The spring constant measures the change in force per unit deformation. The added deformation here is $4\,\mathrm{cm}$, so use that change in the product instead of the final five-centimetre extension.

$$
\Delta F=k\Delta x=125\cdot0.04\,\mathrm N=5\,\mathrm N.
$$
