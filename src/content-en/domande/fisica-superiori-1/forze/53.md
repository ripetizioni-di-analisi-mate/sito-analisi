A box weighing $50\,\mathrm N$ rests on a table with a second $18\,\mathrm N$ box on it. What is the normal reaction of the table on the bottom box?

- $68\,\mathrm{N}$
- $32\,\mathrm{N}$
- $50\,\mathrm{N}$
- $18\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The top box transfers a downward load of eighteen newtons to the lower box. The table supports this load and the weight of the bottom box. The same value follows by isolating both boxes as one system.

$$
N=(50+18)\,\mathrm N=68\,\mathrm N.
$$
