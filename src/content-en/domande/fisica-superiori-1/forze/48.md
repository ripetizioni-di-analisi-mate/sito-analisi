A block weighing $15\,\mathrm{N}$ is held stationary against a rough vertical wall by a horizontal push perpendicular to the wall. The coefficient of static friction is $0.50$. Weight, the push and contact with the wall are the only forces on the block. What is the minimum magnitude of the push?

- $7.5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $15\,\mathrm{N}$
- $45\,\mathrm{N}$
- $30\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Horizontally, the normal force balances the push. Vertically, friction must be $15\,\mathrm{N}$ upward to support the weight. At the minimum push, friction reaches its limiting value, $0.50N=15\,\mathrm{N}$. Thus the normal force, and hence the push, is $30\,\mathrm{N}$. A smaller push would give a static limit below the weight.
