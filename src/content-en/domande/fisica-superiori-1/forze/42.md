A rectangular block starts sliding across a level floor when the horizontal pull reaches $12\,\mathrm{N}$. The test is repeated with the block turned onto a face with three times the area, made of the same material. In both trials the block stays on the chosen face, and the coefficient of static friction is the same. What threshold does the Coulomb model predict in the second trial?

- $36\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The pull is horizontal in both trials, so the floor supports the full weight of the block and the normal force stays the same. At the threshold, the model gives $f_{s,\max}=\mu_s N$. Since both $\mu_s$ and $N$ stay the same, the threshold remains $12\,\mathrm{N}$. The apparent contact area does not enter the product in this model.
