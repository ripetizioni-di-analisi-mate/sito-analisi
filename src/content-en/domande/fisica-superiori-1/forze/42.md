A block starts at rest with $N=35\,\mathrm N$ and $\mu_s=0.4$. A horizontal cord pulls it with $9\,\mathrm N$. How many newtons of margin remain before slipping?

- $14\,\mathrm{N}$
- $5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $9\,\mathrm{N}$
- $23\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The static limit depends on the contact and the normal load. Actual friction balances the $9\,\mathrm N$ pull because $9<14$. Reaching the threshold requires another $5\,\mathrm N$ of pull. The normal reaction stays unchanged because the cord is horizontal.

$$
\begin{aligned}f_{s,\max}&=0.4\cdot35\,\mathrm N=14\,\mathrm N\\[6pt]\Delta F&=(14-9)\,\mathrm N=5\,\mathrm N.\end{aligned}
$$
