Contacts A and B have $(N,\mu_s)=(40\,\mathrm N,0.3)$ and $(60\,\mathrm N,0.2)$ respectively. What is the static-limit difference A minus B?

- $12\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The first contact's higher coefficient is offset by its lower normal reaction. Compare the products: both give twelve newtons.

$$
f_{s,\max,A}-f_{s,\max,B}=(0.3\cdot40-0.2\cdot60)\,\mathrm N=0.
$$
