A block of mass $ 8\,\mathrm{kg}$ remains at rest on a table while a cord pulls it upward with $ 24\,\mathrm N$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$, what is the normal reaction?

- $56\,\mathrm N$
- $104\,\mathrm N$
- $80\,\mathrm N$
- $24\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The cord supports $24\,\mathrm N$ of the $80\,\mathrm N$ weight. The normal reaction supplies the remaining $80-24=56\,\mathrm N$. The positive result is consistent with continued contact.

Adding the calculated reaction to the tension reconstructs the entire downward load. A normal force of $80\,\mathrm N$, added to the rope's pull, would leave an upward net force.
