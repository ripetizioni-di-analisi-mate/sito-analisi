On a graph of the elastic component $F_{e,x}$ against $x$, with positive $x$ for extension, what sign must the slope of an ideal spring have?

- Negative
- Positive
- Zero
- It changes sign on crossing the origin

## Explanation

The elastic component always acts in the direction opposite to deformation, giving $-kx$. The single slope is $-k$, with $k>0$.

Increasing $x$ by a positive amount changes $F_{e,x}$ by $-k\Delta x$, which is negative. The ratio of the two changes stays the same even when the interval crosses the origin.
