An object slides left relative to a stationary surface. With normal reaction and surfaces fixed, what change in kinetic friction does the model predict if the speed doubles, still to the left?

- Double the magnitude toward the right
- Same magnitude toward the left
- Half the magnitude toward the right
- Same magnitude toward the right

## Explanation

In this model, the kinetic magnitude is $\mu_dN$ and contains no speed term. Its direction still opposes the leftward sliding.
