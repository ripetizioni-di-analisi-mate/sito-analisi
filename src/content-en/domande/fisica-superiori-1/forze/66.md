A block weighing $18\,\mathrm{N}$ is pressed upward against a horizontal ceiling by a vertical push of $30\,\mathrm{N}$. The block is stationary and is acted on only by its weight, the push and the normal force from the ceiling. What are the magnitude and direction of the normal force?

- $12\,\mathrm{N}$ upward
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$ downward
- $48\,\mathrm{N}$ downward
- $30\,\mathrm{N}$ downward

## Explanation

The block touches the underside of the ceiling, so the normal force on the block points downward. Weight acts in the same direction. Since the block is stationary, the upward push balances both forces. The normal force supplies the difference between the push and the weight, $(30-18)\,\mathrm{N}=12\,\mathrm{N}$.
