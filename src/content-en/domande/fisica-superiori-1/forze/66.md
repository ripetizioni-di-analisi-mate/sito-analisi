An object weighing $40\,\mathrm N$ is assumed to remain stationary on a non-adhesive horizontal plane while a cord pulls it vertically upward with $45\,\mathrm N$. What does the check show?

- The normal reaction is $5\,\mathrm N$ upward
- Equilibrium with that contact is impossible
- The normal reaction is $85\,\mathrm N$ upward
- The normal reaction acts with $5\,\mathrm N$ downward

## Explanation

For an upward normal reaction, the equation demands a negative value. A non-adhesive surface can only push outward from the surface. A five-newton downward force would require another constraint. The stated forces do not allow equilibrium with support contact.

$$
N=(40-45)\,\mathrm N=-5\,\mathrm N.
$$
