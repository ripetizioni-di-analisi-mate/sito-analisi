Two readings with the same spring scale give different weights for an identical reference mass. Which observation rules out an additive zero shift as the cause of the difference?

- The unloaded reading is zero in both locations
- Both readings use newtons
- The samples have the same colour
- The reference mass is far from the spring

## Explanation

An unloaded reading checks the zero at each location. If the check passes, an additive zero offset is ruled out as the source of the difference.
