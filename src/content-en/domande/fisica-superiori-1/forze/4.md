An ideal spring has $k=160\,\mathrm{N/m}$ and an extension $x=0.2\,\mathrm m$ within its elastic limit. What is the magnitude of its elastic force?

- $800\,\mathrm N$
- $160\,\mathrm N$
- $3.2\,\mathrm N$
- $32\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The deformation is positive and is measured in metres. Hooke's law gives $F_e=kx=160\cdot0.2\,\mathrm N=32\,\mathrm N$. The force tends to shorten the spring.

If the direction of extension is positive, the spring-force component is $-32\,\mathrm N$. Choose the positive option because the question asks for the magnitude, represented by the arrow's length in a scale diagram.
