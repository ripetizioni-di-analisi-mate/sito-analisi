A box weighing $64\,\mathrm N$ lies on a horizontal surface. A rope pulls with upward component $16\,\mathrm N$ and horizontal component $30\,\mathrm N$, balanced by friction. What is the normal reaction?

- $80\,\mathrm{N}$
- $30\,\mathrm{N}$
- $98\,\mathrm{N}$
- $48\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The horizontal component balances friction on the other axis. The vertical balance includes the weight, the upward rope component, and the normal reaction, so subtract sixteen newtons from the weight.

$$
N=(64-16)\,\mathrm N=48\,\mathrm N.
$$
