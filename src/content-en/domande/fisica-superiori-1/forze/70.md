To estimate a sample's weight, two groups use $g=9.81$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$. They obtain slightly different values. What makes the results comparable?

- Replace the mass by the magnitude of the normal reaction
- State the value of $g$ used and the precision of the result
- Use different units for the two results
- Assume that $g$ depends on the mass

## Explanation

The difference originates in the model parameter. Stating the adopted value and precision lets a reader distinguish an approximation effect from a calculation error.
