As a van starts moving to the right and speeds up, a parcel remains stationary relative to the level cargo floor. The floor is the parcel's only contact. What horizontal force does the floor exert on the parcel as the van starts moving?

- Kinetic friction to the right
- Static friction to the left
- Static friction to the right
- Kinetic friction to the left

## Explanation

The parcel and the floor move forward together, so their surfaces do not slide relative to each other and friction is static. As the van starts moving, the floor tends to move forward underneath the parcel, which would otherwise lag behind. Friction on the parcel acts to the right and makes it move with the van.
