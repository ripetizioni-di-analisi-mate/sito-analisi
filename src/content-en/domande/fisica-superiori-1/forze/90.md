A spring scale graduated in newtons is held horizontally between two supports, with equal $18\,\mathrm N$ pulls on its ends. What force does it measure if ideal and at rest?

- $0\,\mathrm N$
- $18\,\mathrm N$
- $36\,\mathrm N$
- $9\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The resultant on the scale is zero, but each end transmits an eighteen-newton pull to the spring. The reading measures the internal tension, not the external resultant.
