Two straight lines show weight $P$ against mass $m$ at sites A and B. What is the ratio of the local fields $g_A$ and $g_B$?

::grafico{f="9*x;6*x" nomi="m (kg),P (N)" punti="2,18,pieno,A;2,12,pieno,B" segmenti="2,0,2,18" legenda="\text{site A};\text{site B}" xmin=0 xmax=3 ymin=0 ymax=28 titolo="At a mass of 2 kg the weights are 18 N in A and 12 N in B."}

- $\dfrac23$
- $3$
- $1$
- $\dfrac32$

## Explanation

The lines pass through the origin, so their slopes are the ratios of weight to mass. The two marked points have the same horizontal coordinate of $2\,\mathrm{kg}$. This coordinate cancels in the ratio of the slopes, leaving $\dfrac{18}{12}=\dfrac32$. Line A assigns more newtons to each kilogram than line B.
