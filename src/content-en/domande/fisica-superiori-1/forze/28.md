On a graph of $P$ against $m$, two straight lines pass through the origin and line A is steeper than line B. What follows about the two fields?

- Field B is larger
- The fields are equal
- A negative mass is needed to compare them
- Field A is larger

## Explanation

Each slope is the increase in weight divided by the increase in mass, namely $g$. For the same increase in mass, line A gives a greater increase in weight.
