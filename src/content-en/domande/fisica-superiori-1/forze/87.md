A person lifts a crate that was resting on a table and holds it motionless, completely off the surface. Which force from the table disappears from its force diagram?

- The weight of the crate
- The force of the hand
- The table's normal reaction
- The force of the gravitational field

## Explanation

The table's normal reaction is a contact force and vanishes when contact is broken. The weight remains and the hand supports the crate.
