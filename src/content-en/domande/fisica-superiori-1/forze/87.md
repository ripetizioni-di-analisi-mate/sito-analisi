A person lifts a crate that was resting on a table and holds it motionless in the air. Which statement describes the forces acting on the crate in this final position?

- The crate's weight becomes zero
- The force of the hand exceeds the weight
- The table's normal reaction is zero and the hand balances the weight
- The table's normal reaction remains equal to the weight because the crate is motionless

## Explanation

The table's normal reaction is a contact force and vanishes when contact is broken. The weight remains and the hand supports the crate.

While the box is held at rest in the air, the vertical component of the hand's force balances its entire weight. Before contact was lost, the table could provide part of that support.
