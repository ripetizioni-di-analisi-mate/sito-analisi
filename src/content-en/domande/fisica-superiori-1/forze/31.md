A student substitutes $g=9.81$ into the weight formula. Which unit must accompany that value if weight is expressed in newtons and mass in kilograms?

- $\mathrm{kg/N}$
- $\mathrm{N\cdot kg}$
- $\mathrm{N/kg}$
- $\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Find the unit of $g$ by dividing the unit of weight by the unit of mass. This check matches the definition of field as force per unit mass.

Stating the unit distinguishes the field from the force on the sample. The field value alone gives the weight per kilogram, rather than the weight of every sample.
