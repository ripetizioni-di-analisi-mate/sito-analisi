A student substitutes $g=9.81$ into the weight formula without a unit. Which unit must it have if weight is in newtons and mass in kilograms?

- $\mathrm{kg/N}$
- $\mathrm{N\cdot kg}$
- $\mathrm{N/kg}$
- $\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Find the unit of $g$ by dividing the unit of weight by the unit of mass. This check matches the definition of field as force per unit mass.
