A spring with $k=75\,\mathrm{N/m}$ can be compressed by at most $8\,\mathrm{cm}$. What opposing force along the axis is needed to hold the end at the limiting compression?

- $0.6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $9.375\,\mathrm{N}$
- $600\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

To hold the end still, the applied force must balance the elastic push. Substitute the limiting compression in metres into the elastic relation to find the required magnitude.

$$
F=75\cdot0.08\,\mathrm N=6\,\mathrm N.
$$
