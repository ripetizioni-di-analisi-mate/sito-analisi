A cord can sustain at most $55\,\mathrm N$ and pulls upward on a $70\,\mathrm N$ load on a horizontal plane. What is the smallest normal reaction consistent with an intact cord and a stationary load?

- $15\,\mathrm{N}$
- $55\,\mathrm{N}$
- $70\,\mathrm{N}$
- $125\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Normal reaction plus tension must equal the weight. The normal reaction is smallest when the tension is largest. It remains positive, so the support still carries some load.

$$
N_{\min}=P-T_{\max}=(70-55)\,\mathrm N=15\,\mathrm N.
$$

A tension below its maximum would leave more load on the surface. Reducing the normal force to zero would require the full $70\,\mathrm N$ in tension, beyond the rope's limit.
