A load has a gravitational weight of $15\,\mathrm N$ in two experiments, with half the mass in the second. If the first field is $4\,\mathrm{N/kg}$, what is the second field?

- $2\,\mathrm{N/kg}$
- $8\,\mathrm{N/kg}$
- $4\,\mathrm{N/kg}$
- $60\,\mathrm{N/kg}$

## Explanation

To keep the product $mg$ unchanged when mass is halved, the field must double. The common weight makes it unnecessary to find either mass separately.

$$
m_1g_1=\frac{m_1}{2}g_2\quad\Longrightarrow\quad g_2=2g_1.
$$
