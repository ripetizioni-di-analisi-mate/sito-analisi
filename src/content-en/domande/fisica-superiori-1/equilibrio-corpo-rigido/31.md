A beam has an unknown reaction passing through support A. Why take moments about A first?

- The reaction at A is always zero
- Every other force vanishes
- The arm of the reaction at A vanishes
- Moments about the support have no units

## Explanation

The reaction need not vanish, but its line passes through the reference. The moment equation therefore has one fewer unknown.
