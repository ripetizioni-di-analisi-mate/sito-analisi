Equal loads on opposite sides have arms $0.5$ and $0.7\,\mathrm m$. The total moment has magnitude $4\,\mathrm{N\,m}$. What is each load?

- $20\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N}$
- $40\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The moments have opposite signs and a common force magnitude. The difference of products is the force times the difference of the arms.

$$
F=\frac{4}{0.7-0.5}\,\mathrm N=20\,\mathrm N.
$$
