The two lines of action of a couple are moved farther apart while both forces stay fixed. What happens to resultant and moment?

- The resultant increases and the moment stays zero
- Both magnitudes increase
- Both vanish
- The resultant stays zero and the moment magnitude increases

## Explanation

The opposite vectors cancel regardless of their application points. The force–distance product grows as the separation increases. For $F=10\,\mathrm N$, widening the separation from $0.2$ to $0.5\,\mathrm m$ raises the moment from $2$ to $5\,\mathrm{N\,m}$. The two force vectors still add to zero.
