Three equal masses sit at $x=0$, $x=2\,\mathrm m$, and $x=7\,\mathrm m$. What is their weighted mean coordinate?

- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

All masses are equal, so the mass factor cancels. The result is the ordinary mean of the three coordinates. The three positions are not evenly spaced. The mean is $\dfrac{0+2+7}{3}\,\mathrm m=3\,\mathrm m$, whereas the midpoint between the extreme positions would be $3.5\,\mathrm m$.

$$
x_G=\frac{0+2+7}{3}\,\mathrm m=3\,\mathrm m.
$$
