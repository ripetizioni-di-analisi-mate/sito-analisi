The pivot is moved closer to a fixed load while a perpendicular effort is applied at the same point on the opposite side. What happens to the needed effort?

- It increases
- It decreases
- It stays unchanged
- It depends only on the pivot weight

## Explanation

The load arm decreases and the effort arm increases. Their ratio falls so the same load needs less effort.
