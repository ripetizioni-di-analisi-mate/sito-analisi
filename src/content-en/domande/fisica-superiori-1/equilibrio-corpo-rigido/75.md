A body weighs $80\,\mathrm N$ and its centre of gravity is $0.15\,\mathrm m$ horizontally from its tipping edge. The horizontal push available is $30\,\mathrm N$ and friction suffices. What minimum height reaches the threshold?

- $0.15\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.45\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.25\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The weight's restoring moment is twelve newton metres. With fixed horizontal force, overturning moment grows with height. The threshold is where the two products are equal. A lower point of application would produce insufficient moment.

$$
h_{\min}=\frac{80\,\mathrm N\cdot0.15\,\mathrm m}{30\,\mathrm N}=0.4\,\mathrm m.
$$
