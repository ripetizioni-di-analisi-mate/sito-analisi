An $80\,\mathrm N$ box rests on two level supports $0.50\,\mathrm m$ apart. The vertical line through its centre of mass passes midway between them. A $20\,\mathrm N$ force pushes horizontally rightwards $0.75\,\mathrm m$ above the surface. Friction prevents slipping. What are the left and right vertical reactions?

- $(40\,\mathrm N,\ 40\,\mathrm N)$
- $(70\,\mathrm N,\ 10\,\mathrm N)$
- $(10\,\mathrm N,\ 70\,\mathrm N)$
- $(20\,\mathrm N,\ 60\,\mathrm N)$

## Explanation

About the left support, both the weight and the horizontal push produce clockwise torques. Friction acts at the level of the supports and has zero moment arm. The right reaction balances both torques, while the two reactions still add to the weight. Both are positive, so both supports remain loaded.

$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{\mathrm R}\cdot0.50\,\mathrm m
&= 80\,\mathrm N\cdot0.25\,\mathrm m+20\,\mathrm N\cdot0.75\,\mathrm m\\[6pt]
R_{\mathrm R}&=70\,\mathrm N\\[6pt]
R_{\mathrm L}&=80\,\mathrm N-70\,\mathrm N=10\,\mathrm N.
\end{aligned}
$$
