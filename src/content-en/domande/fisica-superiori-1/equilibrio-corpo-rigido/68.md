The moments of three forces are computed about a different origin for each. Why does that sum not check equilibrium?

- Moments of different forces can never be added
- Each force needs its own centre of mass
- The sum works only for horizontal forces
- Moments must use a common reference point

## Explanation

The moment equation is about one reference point. Measure every moment arm from that same point.
