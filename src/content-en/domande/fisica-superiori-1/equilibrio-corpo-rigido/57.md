A light beam is supported at A and B, $5\,\mathrm m$ apart. A $100\,\mathrm N$ load can move between the supports. A can supply up to $70\,\mathrm N$ and B up to $60\,\mathrm N$. Which range of distances $a$ from A satisfies both limits?

- $1.5\,\mathrm m\leq a\leq3\,\mathrm m$
- $0\leq a\leq3\,\mathrm m$
- $1.5\,\mathrm m\leq a\leq5\,\mathrm m$
- $2\,\mathrm m\leq a\leq3.5\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Moving the load towards B increases the reaction at B and decreases the reaction at A. B’s limit sets a maximum distance. To meet A’s limit, B must support at least thirty newtons, which gives a minimum distance as well. The positions satisfying both limits are allowed.

$$
\begin{aligned}
R_B&=(20\,\mathrm{N/m})a,\qquad R_A=100\,\mathrm N-R_B\\[6pt]
R_A\leq70\,\mathrm N&\quad\Longrightarrow\quad R_B\geq30\,\mathrm N\\[6pt]
30\,\mathrm N&\leq R_B\leq60\,\mathrm N\\[6pt]
1.5\,\mathrm m&\leq a\leq3\,\mathrm m.
\end{aligned}
$$
