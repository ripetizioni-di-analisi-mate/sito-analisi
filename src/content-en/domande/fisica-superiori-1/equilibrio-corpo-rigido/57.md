A light $5\,\mathrm m$ beam supports $100\,\mathrm N$ between its supports. B can supply a reaction of at most $40\,\mathrm N$. What is the greatest distance from A to the load?

- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Moving the weight away from A increases its moment about A and therefore the B reaction required. The limit at B determines the largest arm. Then A carries sixty newtons and the load is still between the supports.

$$
100a\leq40\cdot5\quad\Longrightarrow\quad a_{\max}=2\,\mathrm m.
$$
