What distinguishes joules from newton metres used for moments, although they have the same base units?

- For a moment the metre is always squared
- The joule measures force and the newton metre measures mass
- They are synonyms in every formula
- They describe different quantities, energy and moment

## Explanation

Moment describes the turning effect of a force at a moment arm. Energy uses joules for a different physical quantity. For a moment, the length is perpendicular to the line of action and the result has a turning direction. Work uses the actual displacement and the force component along that displacement.
