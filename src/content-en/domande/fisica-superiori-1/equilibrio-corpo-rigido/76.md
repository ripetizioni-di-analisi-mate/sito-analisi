How can reactions found from moments about A be checked without solving the equations again?

- Check only that both reactions are positive
- Simply compare the two reactions for equality
- Check that every arm numerically equals the span
- Check vertical balance and zero moment about B

## Explanation

A solution must give zero resultant and zero moment about every point. Summing moments about B gives a check distinct from the original setup. If $R_A$ is wrong, taking moments about B gives it a lever arm equal to the full span. The check can then reveal the error even when the moment equation about A was set up correctly.
