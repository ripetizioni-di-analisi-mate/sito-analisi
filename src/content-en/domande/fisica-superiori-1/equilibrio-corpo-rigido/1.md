A force of $ 80\,\mathrm N$ acts with perpendicular moment arm $0.2\,\mathrm m$ about a pivot. What is the moment magnitude?

- $16\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $400\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $80\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $1.6\,\mathrm{N\,m}$

## Explanation

Multiply the force by the perpendicular distance to obtain the magnitude. Here $80\cdot0.2=16\,\mathrm{N\,m}$. The same lever arm can be written as $20\,\mathrm{cm}$. Multiplying in centimetres gives $1600\,\mathrm{N\,cm}$, equivalent to $16\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ once the length is converted.
