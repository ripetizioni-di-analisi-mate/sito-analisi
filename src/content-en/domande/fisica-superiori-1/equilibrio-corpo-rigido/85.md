A bar of constant cross-section consists of two uniform halves, with the right half denser than the left. Which way is its centre of mass shifted from the midpoint?

- To the right
- To the left
- It must stay at the midpoint
- Outside the bar on the left

## Explanation

For the same volume, the right half contains more mass. Its contribution to moments about the midpoint is larger, shifting the centre to the right. The centres of the two uniform halves lie at equal distances from the midpoint. The right half has greater mass, so its moment about the midpoint is larger.
