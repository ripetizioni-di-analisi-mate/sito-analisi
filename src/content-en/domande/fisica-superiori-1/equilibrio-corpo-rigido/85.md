A bar of constant cross-section is denser in its right half. Which way is its centre of mass shifted from the midpoint?

- To the right
- To the left
- It must stay at the midpoint
- Outside the bar on the left

## Explanation

For the same volume, the right half contains more mass. Its contribution to moments about the midpoint is larger, shifting the centre to the right.
