A block weighs $100\,\mathrm N$ with a centred centre of gravity and a base $0.4\,\mathrm m$ wide. A horizontal push acts at height $0.4\,\mathrm m$ and the static coefficient is $0.3$. Which threshold does an increasing push reach first?

- Tipping at $50\,\mathrm N$
- Tipping at $30\,\mathrm N$
- Both thresholds are at $50\,\mathrm N$
- Sliding at $30\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Check the friction and moment limits separately while contact is at rest. The horizontal push leaves the normal reaction equal to weight. The friction limit is reached before the moments permit tipping from rest.

$$
\begin{aligned}F_{\mathrm{sl}}&=0.3\cdot100\,\mathrm N=30\,\mathrm N\\[6pt]F_{\mathrm{tip}}&=\frac{100\cdot0.2}{0.4}\,\mathrm N=50\,\mathrm N.\end{aligned}
$$
