A board carrying a rigid block is tilted slowly. The centre of mass is $0.40\,\mathrm m$ above the board measured perpendicularly to it. Its perpendicular projection is $0.12\,\mathrm m$ from the downhill edge. The static friction coefficient is $0.50$. At what value of $\tan\theta$ does the block reach the first threshold for motion?

- $0.12$
- $0.4$
- $0.5$
- $0.3$

## Explanation

The vertical weight line reaches the edge when $0.40\tan\theta=0.12$, giving $\tan\theta=0.30$. Sliding requires $\tan\theta=\mu_s=0.50$, because the ratio of the tangential to normal component of weight is $\tan\theta$. The smaller of the two values is reached first, so the first threshold is for tipping.
