A light beam of length $3\,\mathrm m$ is supported at ends A and B. A load of $ 96\,\mathrm N$ is $1\,\mathrm m$ from A. What is the reaction at B?

- $96\,\mathrm N$
- $64\,\mathrm N$
- $32\,\mathrm N$
- $288\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Moments about A give $3R_B=96\cdot1$ in newton metres. Hence $R_B=32\,\mathrm N$. The load is one third of the span from A, so B carries one third of the weight. That leaves $96-32=64\,\mathrm N$ at A, consistent with its being closer to the load.
