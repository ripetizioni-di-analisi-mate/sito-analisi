The graph shows how torque magnitude varies with force at two points A and B on a handle. At both points the force is perpendicular to the handle. What is the ratio $\dfrac{b_A}{b_B}$ of the moment arms?

::grafico{f="0.5*x;0.25*x" punti="16,8,pieno,A;16,4,pieno,B" segmenti="16,0,16,8;0,8,16,8;0,4,16,4" xmin=0 xmax=20 ymin=0 ymax=11 nomi="F (N),|M| (N·m)" legenda=0 titolo="A and B mark the readings for the two configurations at the same force of 16 N"}

- $1$
- $2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $4$

## Explanation

The slope of each line is the moment arm because torque equals force times moment arm. At the same force of sixteen newtons, the readings are eight newton-metres at A and four at B. Dividing the torques at the same force gives the ratio of moment arms.

$$
\frac{b_A}{b_B}=\frac{|M_A|}{|M_B|}=\frac{8\,\mathrm{N\,m}}{4\,\mathrm{N\,m}}=2.
$$
