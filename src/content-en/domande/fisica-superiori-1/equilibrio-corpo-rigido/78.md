A lever needs a moment of $60\,\mathrm{N\,m}$. The effort's moment arm increases from $1$ to $2\,\mathrm m$. By how many newtons does the required force decrease?

- $15\,\mathrm{N}$
- $30\,\mathrm{N}$
- $60\,\mathrm{N}$
- $90\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

For fixed moment, force and arm are inversely proportional. Doubling the arm halves the force from sixty to thirty newtons. Subtract the two readings to obtain the decrease. They are two points on the curve of the same moment.

$$
\Delta F=\left(\frac{60}{1}-\frac{60}{2}\right)\mathrm N=30\,\mathrm N.
$$
