A beam has vertical reactions $34$ and $26\,\mathrm N$. One external load weighs $40\,\mathrm N$ and the only other vertical load is the beam itself. What does the beam weigh?

- $6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N}$
- $40\,\mathrm{N}$
- $60\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The upward reactions support all downward forces. Subtract the known load from the total measured at the supports to isolate the beam weight.

$$
P_{\mathrm{trave}}=(34+26-40)\,\mathrm N=20\,\mathrm N.
$$
