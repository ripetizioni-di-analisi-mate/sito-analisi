A flat cutout is freely suspended first from hole A and then from hole B. Each time, the vertical through the suspension point is drawn on the cutout. The two distinct lines intersect. Which point do they identify?

- The midpoint of segment AB.
- The hole that supports the greater weight.
- The cutout's centre of gravity.
- The centre of the smallest circle containing the cutout.

## Explanation

In equilibrium the weight exerts zero torque about the suspension point. The centre of gravity must therefore lie on each of the two verticals drawn on the body. Their intersection satisfies both conditions.
