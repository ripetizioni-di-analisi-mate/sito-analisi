Masses of $2$ and $6\,\mathrm{kg}$ are at positions $-3$ and $+1\,\mathrm m$. What is their centre of gravity coordinate?

- $-1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The smaller mass has a longer arm to the left. The two products have equal magnitudes and opposite signs, so the origin is the centre.

$$
x_G=\frac{2(-3)+6(1)}{2+6}\,\mathrm m=0.
$$
