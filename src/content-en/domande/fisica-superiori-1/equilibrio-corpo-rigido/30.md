A coplanar force system has a purely vertical resultant. The total moment is $+18\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ about A and $+6\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ about B, which is $2\,\mathrm m$ to the right of A. With anticlockwise and upward taken as positive, what is the vertical component $R_y$ of the resultant?

- $-6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$
- $24\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Moving the reference point from A to B reduces the horizontal coordinate of every point of application by $2\,\mathrm m$. The total moment therefore changes by $-2R_y$ in consistent units. Since $6-18=-12$, the result is $R_y=+6\,\mathrm N$. The moment decreases to the right because the resultant points upwards.
