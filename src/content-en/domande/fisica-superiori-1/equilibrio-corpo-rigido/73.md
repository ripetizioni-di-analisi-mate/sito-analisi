In an ideal lever, both forces are tangent to their circular paths and the lever reduces the effort needed for the load. For a fixed small turn, how does the effort point's travel compare with the load point's travel?

- The effort point travels farther
- The effort point travels less far
- The effort point stays still
- The two points always travel the same distance

## Explanation

With tangent forces, the effort arm is longer. At the same turning angle the arc length is proportional to the distance from the pivot. If the effort point is three times farther from the pivot, its arc is three times as long for any shared angle. The force advantage comes with greater travel.
