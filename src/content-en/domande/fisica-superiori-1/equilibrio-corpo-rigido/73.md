A lever reduces the effort needed for a load. For a fixed small turn, how does the effort point's travel compare with the load point's travel?

- The effort point travels farther
- The effort point travels less far
- The effort point stays still
- The two points always travel the same distance

## Explanation

With tangent forces, the effort arm is longer. At the same turning angle the arc length is proportional to the distance from the pivot.
