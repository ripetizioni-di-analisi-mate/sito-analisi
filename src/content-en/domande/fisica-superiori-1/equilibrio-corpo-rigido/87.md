A force of $18\,\mathrm N$ acts at $60^\circ$ to a $0.2\,\mathrm m$ crank. What perpendicular force at the same distance gives the same moment?

- $9\,\mathrm{N}$
- $18\,\mathrm{N}$
- $9 \sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $18 \sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The perpendicular force has the full crank length as its arm. The equivalent force is therefore the perpendicular component of the original force.

$$
F_\perp=18\sin60^\circ\,\mathrm N=9\sqrt3\,\mathrm N.
$$
