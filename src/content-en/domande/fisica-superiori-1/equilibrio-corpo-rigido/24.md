A $12\,\mathrm N$ force acts rightwards along a horizontal line $0.30\,\mathrm m$ above the pivot $O$. An $8\,\mathrm N$ upward force is added along a vertical line to the right of $O$. The required net torque is $+0.40\,\mathrm{N\,m}$, taking anticlockwise as positive. How far from $O$ must the vertical line pass?

- $0.40\,\mathrm m$
- $0.05\,\mathrm m$
- $0.30\,\mathrm m$
- $0.50\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The horizontal force produces a clockwise torque, and its moment arm is its height above the pivot. The vertical force on the right produces an anticlockwise torque. It must offset $3.60\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ and leave another positive $0.40$, so its torque is $4.00\,\mathrm{N\,m}$. Dividing by eight gives the moment arm.

$$
\begin{aligned}
8\,\mathrm N\cdot b-12\,\mathrm N\cdot0.30\,\mathrm m&=+0.40\,\mathrm{N\,m}\\[6pt]
b&=\frac{(3.60+0.40)\,\mathrm{N\,m}}{8\,\mathrm N}=0.50\,\mathrm m.
\end{aligned}
$$
