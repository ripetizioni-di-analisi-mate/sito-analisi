A horizontal bar receives $10\,\mathrm N$ downward $0.2\,\mathrm m$ left of O and $5\,\mathrm N$ downward $0.3\,\mathrm m$ right of O. With anticlockwise positive, what is the total moment?

- $-0.5\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $3.5\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{N\,m}$

## Explanation

On the left, the downward push tends to lift the right end and makes a positive anticlockwise moment. On the right, a downward force has the opposite effect. Subtract the products, leaving a small anticlockwise excess.

$$
\sum M_O=(10\cdot0.2-5\cdot0.3)\,\mathrm{N\,m}=+0.5\,\mathrm{N\,m}.
$$
