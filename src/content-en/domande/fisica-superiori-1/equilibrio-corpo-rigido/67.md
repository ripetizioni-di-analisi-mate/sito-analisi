A block would tip at $28\,\mathrm N$ and its normal reaction is $70\,\mathrm N$. What minimum static coefficient prevents sliding before that threshold?

- $0.25$
- $0.7$
- $0.4$
- $2.5$

## Explanation

The sliding threshold must be at least twenty-eight newtons. Thus the minimum coefficient is the tipping push divided by the normal reaction. The condition is $\mu_s\cdot70\geq28$, so $\mu_s\geq0.4$. At the limiting value the thresholds coincide. With a lower coefficient, friction reaches its maximum before the tipping force is reached.

$$
\mu_s\geq\frac{28}{70}=0.4.
$$
