Two masses of $ 9$ and $ 18\,\mathrm{kg}$ are attached to a light rod at $x=0$ and $x=3\,\mathrm m$. Where is their centre of gravity?

- $2\,\mathrm m$
- $1.5\,\mathrm m$
- $1\,\mathrm m$
- $3\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The weighted mean is $\dfrac{9\cdot0+18\cdot3}{9+18}\,\mathrm m=2\,\mathrm m$. It lies closer to the mass that is twice as large.
