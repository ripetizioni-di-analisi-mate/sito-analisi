A light lever has arms $0.3$ and $0.9\,\mathrm m$. The longer arm carries $16\,\mathrm N$. What load on the other side balances it?

- $16\,\mathrm{N}$
- $32\,\mathrm{N}$
- $\dfrac{16}{3}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $48\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The load acts at one third of the arm. It must exert three times the force for the same moment.

$$
F=\frac{16\cdot0.9}{0.3}\,\mathrm N=48\,\mathrm N.
$$
