A perpendicular force is doubled while its arm is halved. How does the moment magnitude change?

- It doubles
- It halves
- It is unchanged
- It quadruples

## Explanation

The two changes multiply $Fb$ by $2$ and $\dfrac{1}{2}$. The factors multiply to one. For example, $20\,\mathrm N$ at $0.4\,\mathrm m$ and $40\,\mathrm N$ at $0.2\,\mathrm m$ both produce $8\,\mathrm{N\,m}$. The second arrangement compensates for its shorter arm by doubling the force.
