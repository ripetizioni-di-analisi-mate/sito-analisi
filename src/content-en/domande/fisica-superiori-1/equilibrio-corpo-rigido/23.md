An upward force is moved from a point left of the pivot to a symmetric point on the right. What happens to its moment?

- Same sign and half the magnitude
- It vanishes
- Same magnitude and opposite sign
- Double the magnitude and the same sign

## Explanation

The arm has the same length at either point. The side of the pivot changes, reversing the turning direction. For an upward force, $F_y>0$ and $M_O=xF_y$. Moving it from $x=-b$ to $x=+b$ changes the moment from negative $-bF_y$ to positive $+bF_y$.
