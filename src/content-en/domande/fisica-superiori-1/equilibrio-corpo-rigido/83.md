Two supports stand $4\,\mathrm m$ apart. Loads of $20$ and $40\,\mathrm N$ act $1$ and $2\,\mathrm m$ from A. Neglecting the beam, what is their combined moment magnitude about A?

- $60\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $80\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $100\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $140\,\mathrm{N\,m}$

## Explanation

Both forces point down on the same side of A, producing the same turning direction. Add their respective products. The contribution magnitudes are $20\cdot1=20$ and $40\cdot2=80\,\mathrm{N\,m}$. Together they give $100\,\mathrm{N\,m}$. With anticlockwise positive, the signed moment would be negative.

$$
M=(20\cdot1+40\cdot2)\,\mathrm{N\,m}=100\,\mathrm{N\,m}.
$$
