Masses of $1$ and $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ are attached to a light rod at $x=0$ and $x=4\,\mathrm m$ respectively. What is the centre of gravity coordinate?

- $1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The mass at the origin contributes to total mass but has zero arm. The right mass contributes twelve kilogram-metres. Dividing by four kilograms gives a position between the points, closer to the heavier mass.

$$
x_G=\frac{1\cdot0+3\cdot4}{1+3}\,\mathrm m=3\,\mathrm m.
$$
