A mass is added exactly at a system's original centre of gravity. What happens to the centre's position?

- It always moves to the right
- It does not change
- It moves toward the most distant mass
- The coordinate always halves

## Explanation

The added mass has zero arm about the original centre. The total moment about that point remains zero. The total mass increases, but the added weight acts on the same line as the initial equivalent weight. Both can therefore be represented by a single force acting at the original point.
