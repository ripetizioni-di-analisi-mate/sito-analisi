Can a single added nonzero force balance a lone couple on a free body?

- Yes, if its arm is zero
- No, it would introduce a nonzero resultant
- Yes, if it points toward the centre of gravity
- Yes, if it matches one force in the couple

## Explanation

The original couple has zero resultant. A single force changes the resultant even if its moment cancels the original moment.
