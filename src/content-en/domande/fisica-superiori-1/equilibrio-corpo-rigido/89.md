For a vertical force on a horizontal beam, move the reference vertically while keeping its horizontal coordinate. What happens to the arm?

- It stays unchanged
- It increases by the vertical shift
- It always drops to zero
- It becomes the direct distance to the application point

## Explanation

The arm is horizontal distance to a vertical line. Moving the reference parallel to that line leaves the distance unchanged. For a vertical force $M_O=xF_y$. Moving O vertically changes only the vertical separation from the point of application. The relative horizontal coordinate $x$ and the product stay the same.
