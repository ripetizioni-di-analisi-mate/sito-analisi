A beam on supports without anchors gives $R_B<0$ when B is assumed upward. What does this signal?

- Simple contact at B cannot supply the required force
- Always take the absolute value of the reaction at B
- The beam has negative mass
- The force at B is automatically sufficient

## Explanation

The negative reaction would require a downward restraint. A support that only pushes up cannot provide it.
