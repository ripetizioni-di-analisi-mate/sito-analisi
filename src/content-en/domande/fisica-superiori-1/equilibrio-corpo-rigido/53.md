A beam on supports without anchors gives $R_B<0$ when B is assumed upward. What does this signal?

- Simple contact at B cannot supply the required force
- Always take the absolute value of the reaction at B
- The reaction at B can be set to zero with the reaction at A unchanged
- A simple support at B can supply the downward force

## Explanation

The negative reaction would require a downward restraint. A support that only pushes up cannot provide it. For a simple support the minimum reaction is zero, at loss of contact. Sustaining equilibrium with the calculated negative reaction would require a restraint capable of holding the beam down.
