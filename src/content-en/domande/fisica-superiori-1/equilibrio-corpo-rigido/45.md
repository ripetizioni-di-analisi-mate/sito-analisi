Two horizontal $20\,\mathrm N$ forces form a couple on a plate. The force at $A$ points right, and the one at $B$ points left. $B$ is $0.40\,\mathrm m$ to the right and $0.30\,\mathrm m$ above $A$. Taking anticlockwise as positive, what is the torque of the couple?

- $+6\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $+8\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $+10\,\mathrm{N\,m}$

## Explanation

Take A as the reference point, so the first force produces zero torque. The upper force pointing left tends to turn the plate anticlockwise. Its moment arm is the height difference between the horizontal lines. The forty-centimetre separation along their direction does not contribute.

$$
M_A=+20\,\mathrm N\cdot0.30\,\mathrm m=+6\,\mathrm{N\,m}.
$$
