A horizontal weightless rod, $2\,\mathrm m$ long, is hinged at the left. A $75\,\mathrm N$ weight hangs $0.80\,\mathrm m$ from the hinge. At the right end, a taut cable has components $T_x=-\dfrac45T$ and $T_y=\dfrac35T$. What is the magnitude of the horizontal component of the hinge reaction?

- $30\,\mathrm{N}$
- $40\,\mathrm{N}$
- $50\,\mathrm{N}$
- $75\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The vertical component of the cable force balances the moment of the weight, so $2T_y=75\cdot0.80$ and $T_y=30\,\mathrm N$. This gives the tension $T=50\,\mathrm N$. Since the cable pulls leftwards with $40\,\mathrm N$, horizontal balance requires an equally large hinge reaction to the right.
