An $8\,\mathrm m$ beam between A and B carries its own central weight of $50\,\mathrm N$. An additional load of $30\,\mathrm N$ is placed directly above A. What is the new reaction at B?

- $40\,\mathrm{N}$
- $25\,\mathrm{N}$
- $55\,\mathrm{N}$
- $80\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The added load has zero arm about A. The moment that B must balance stays unchanged while A carries the extra thirty newtons.

$$
R_B=\frac{50\cdot4+30\cdot0}{8}\,\mathrm N=25\,\mathrm N.
$$
