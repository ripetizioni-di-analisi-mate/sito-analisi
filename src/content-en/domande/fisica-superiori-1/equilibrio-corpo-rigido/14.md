A uniform rod is $ 16\,\mathrm{cm}$ long with one end at the origin. At what distance is its centre of gravity?

- $16\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

By symmetry of the mass distribution, the centre is halfway along the rod, or $8\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the origin.
