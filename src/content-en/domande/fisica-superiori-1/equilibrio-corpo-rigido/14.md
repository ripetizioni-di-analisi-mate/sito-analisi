A uniform rod is $ 16\,\mathrm{cm}$ long with one end at the origin. At what distance is its centre of gravity?

- $16\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

By symmetry of the mass distribution, the centre is halfway along the rod, or $8\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the origin. Matching portions to the left and right of the midpoint have the same mass at equal distances. Their moments cancel in pairs, so the $16\,\mathrm{cm}$ length is divided by two.
