Two blocks have equal weights and centred centres of gravity but different base widths. A horizontal push acts at equal height. Which base gives a higher tipping threshold with sufficient friction?

- The wider base
- The narrower base
- They are equal
- It depends only on the choice of units

## Explanation

A wider base increases the restoring arm from weight to the edge. A larger pushing moment is therefore needed at the same height.
