A force of $ 80\,\mathrm N$ gives a moment of magnitude $ 24\,\mathrm{N\,m}$. What is the moment arm?

- $\dfrac{10}{3}\,\mathrm m$
- $3\,\mathrm m$
- $0.3\,\mathrm m$
- $30\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Dividing $24\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ by $80\,\mathrm N$ gives the perpendicular distance $0.3\,\mathrm m$.
