An ideal lever balances $ 54\,\mathrm N$ with perpendicular arm $0.2\,\mathrm m$ from the pivot using $ 27\,\mathrm N$ on the other side. What is the second force's moment arm?

- $0.1\,\mathrm m$
- $0.2\,\mathrm m$
- $0.4\,\mathrm m$
- $2\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The known moment is $54\cdot0.2=10.8\,\mathrm{N\,m}$. Dividing by $27\,\mathrm N$ gives the required $0.4\,\mathrm m$ arm.
