An object rests on the ground with the vertical through its centre exactly on the edge. What is its weight moment about that edge?

- Maximum clockwise
- Maximum anticlockwise
- Equal to its weight in newtons
- Zero

## Explanation

The weight line passes through the edge, so its arm is zero. This marks the boundary between the two possible moment directions. In the product $Pb$, the weight $P$ remains nonzero and only the lever arm $b$ vanishes. Once its vertical line passes beyond the edge, the weight has a moment that encourages toppling.
