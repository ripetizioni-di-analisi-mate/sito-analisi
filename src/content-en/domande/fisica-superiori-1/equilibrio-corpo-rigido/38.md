An effort arm of $0.72\,\mathrm m$ is to provide mechanical advantage 9. How long must the load arm be?

- $0.09\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.08\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.72\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6.48\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Advantage is effort arm divided by load arm. Advantage nine requires a load arm one ninth as long.

$$
b_r=\frac{b_m}{9}=0.08\,\mathrm m.
$$
