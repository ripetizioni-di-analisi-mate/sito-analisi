On a $0.40\,\mathrm m$ handle, the applied force can be at most $30\,\mathrm N$. The allowed direction makes an angle between $0^\circ$ and $30^\circ$ with the line segment from the pivot to the point of application. What is the largest possible moment in magnitude?

- $3\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $10.4\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N\,m}$

## Explanation

Both the force and its lever arm must be maximised. Between $0^\circ$ and $30^\circ$ the sine increases, so the largest lever arm is $0.40\sin30^\circ=0.20\,\mathrm m$. At $30\,\mathrm N$ the product is $6\,\mathrm{N\,m}$. A moment of $12\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ would require a right angle, which is outside the permitted range.
