A vertical force of $25\,\mathrm N$ acts at end A of a horizontal spanner. Bolt O is $0.3\,\mathrm m$ from A. What is the moment magnitude?

- $25\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $75\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{N\,m}$

## Explanation

The line of action is vertical and OA is horizontal. Thus OA is the perpendicular moment arm, as shown. Multiply distance by force. The product of newtons and metres gives the unit of moment.

$$
M=25\,\mathrm N\cdot0.3\,\mathrm m=7.5\,\mathrm{N\,m}.
$$
