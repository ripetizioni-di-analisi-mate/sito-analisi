Two moments are $ 45\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ anticlockwise and $ 18\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ clockwise. Taking anticlockwise as positive, what is the total moment?

- $27\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $63\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $-27\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N\,m}$

## Explanation

Give $45$ a positive sign and $18\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ a negative sign. The total is $27\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ anticlockwise. Numerically, $\sum M=45-18=27\,\mathrm{N\,m}$. Adding the magnitudes gives $63$, which would correspond to two moments acting in the same direction.
