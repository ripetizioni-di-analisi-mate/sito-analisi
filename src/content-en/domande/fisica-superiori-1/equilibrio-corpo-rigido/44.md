Two anticlockwise couples exert $5$ and $8\,\mathrm{N\,m}$. A third clockwise couple leaves $2\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ anticlockwise. What is the magnitude of the third couple?

- $2\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $13\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $11\,\mathrm{N\,m}$

## Explanation

The third couple reduces the anticlockwise total from thirteen to two. Their difference is the clockwise contribution.

$$
M_3=(5+8-2)\,\mathrm{N\,m}=11\,\mathrm{N\,m}.
$$
