A light symmetric beam carries equal loads at equal distances from its centre. How do the end reactions compare?

- The left reaction is greater
- The right reaction is greater
- One of the end reactions is always zero
- They are equal

## Explanation

The load arrangement is symmetric about midspan. Their total moment is that of a single load at the centre, so the reactions share it equally.
