Can a bar with mass concentrated at its ends have a centre of gravity in an empty region?

- No, it must coincide with actual matter
- Yes, because it is an equivalent position of the resultant weight
- No, because empty space has an opposite weight
- Yes, only if the bar can never have weight

## Explanation

The centre is the location where the total weight produces the correct moment. For equal end masses it is their midpoint, even if no matter occupies that location.
