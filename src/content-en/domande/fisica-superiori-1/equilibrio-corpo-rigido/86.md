Two containers share the same horizontal centre coordinate and base. Weight is added above the centre to one container, leaving the horizontal weight arm unchanged. Under a push at the same height, how does the weight moment about the edge change?

- Its magnitude decreases
- Its magnitude increases
- The moment vanishes because the centre rises
- Its magnitude never changes

## Explanation

The vertical weight line has horizontal arm from the edge. At fixed arm, larger weight increases the restoring product. If the weight increases from $P$ to $2P$ at the same lever arm $b$, the moment magnitude increases from $Pb$ to $2Pb$. The height of the centre of mass does not determine the arm of a vertical force.
