A couple gives a clockwise moment. Both forces are reversed without moving their lines. What changes?

- The moment becomes anticlockwise with the same magnitude
- The resultant becomes nonzero
- The moment becomes zero
- The moment magnitude doubles

## Explanation

The two vectors remain opposite and their separation is unchanged. Both turning tendencies reverse, so the moment reverses. The magnitude stays $Fd$ because the force and separation are unchanged. With anticlockwise positive, reversing both vectors changes the moment from $-Fd$ to $+Fd$.
