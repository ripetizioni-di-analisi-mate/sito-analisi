A load on a light beam moves from A toward B between the supports. How do the reactions change?

- Both increase
- Both decrease
- A decreases and B increases
- The sum grows but neither individual reaction changes

## Explanation

The load moment about A increases, so $R_B$ must rise. Total weight is fixed, so $R_A=P-R_B$ falls. At A the load's lever arm is initially zero, so B starts with zero reaction. When the load reaches B itself, that support carries its full weight.
