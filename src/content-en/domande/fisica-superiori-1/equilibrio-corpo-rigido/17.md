To find the arm of a diagonal force, what should be drawn from the pivot?

- The perpendicular to the line of action
- The shortest segment to the arrowhead
- A line parallel to the force
- Any vertical line

## Explanation

The arm is the distance from the pivot to the line. The perpendicular segment reaching the line gives that distance. The perpendicular may meet the line beyond the point at which the force is drawn. Use the whole line because moving the vector along it leaves the moment unchanged.
