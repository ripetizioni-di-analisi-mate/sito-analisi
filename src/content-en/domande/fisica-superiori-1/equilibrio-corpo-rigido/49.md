Two supports are $4\,\mathrm m$ apart. A light beam has one $60\,\mathrm N$ load and B measures $45\,\mathrm N$. How far from A is the load?

- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The moments about A link reaction B to the unknown load position. B supports three quarters of the weight, so the distance from A is three quarters of the span.

$$
a=\frac{R_B L}{P}=\frac{45\cdot4}{60}\,\mathrm m=3\,\mathrm m.
$$
