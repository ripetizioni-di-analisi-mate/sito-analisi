A vertical force on the right of a pivot gives moment $-6\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ with anticlockwise positive. Its arm is $0.5\,\mathrm m$. What vertical component produces the moment?

- $12\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{N}$
- $3\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The rightward position is positive, so the negative moment sign comes from the force. Divide the moment by the arm.

$$
F_y=\frac{-6\,\mathrm{N\,m}}{+0.5\,\mathrm m}=-12\,\mathrm N.
$$
