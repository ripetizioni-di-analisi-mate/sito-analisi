A lever is initially horizontal, with its handle $0.60\,\mathrm m$ from the pivot and the load $0.20\,\mathrm m$ away on the other side. During rotation the handle drops by $0.18\,\mathrm m$ vertically. What is the vertical rise of the load's attachment point?

- $0.03\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.12\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.06\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.54\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The two points turn through the same angle on opposite sides of the pivot. Starting from the horizontal, the magnitude of each height change is the distance from the pivot times the sine of that angle. The load is one third as far from the pivot as the handle, so it rises by $\dfrac{0.18}{3}\,\mathrm m=0.06\,\mathrm m$.
