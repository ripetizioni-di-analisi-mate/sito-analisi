A lever carries $24\,\mathrm N$ at $0.5\,\mathrm m$ right of the pivot and its own weight $10\,\mathrm N$ acts $0.2\,\mathrm m$ to the right. What downward force $0.4\,\mathrm m$ left of the pivot balances it?

- $30\,\mathrm{N}$
- $40\,\mathrm{N}$
- $35\,\mathrm{N}$
- $14\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Both weights on the right turn the lever clockwise, so their moment magnitudes add. The required load is on the other side and produces an anticlockwise moment. The lever's own weight contributes two newton metres, so omitting it would underestimate the required force by five newtons.

$$
F=\frac{24\cdot0.5+10\cdot0.2}{0.4}\,\mathrm N=35\,\mathrm N.
$$
