A weightless horizontal lever is attached to an ideal hinge at $O$. It experiences an anticlockwise couple of $9\,\mathrm{N\,m}$. A vertical force can be applied at $P$, $0.30\,\mathrm m$ to the right of $O$. Which forces at $P$ and $O$ satisfy both balance conditions?

- $30\,\mathrm N$ upwards at $P$ and $30\,\mathrm N$ downwards at $O$
- $15\,\mathrm N$ downwards at $P$ and $15\,\mathrm N$ upwards at $O$
- $30\,\mathrm N$ downwards at $P$ and zero reaction at $O$
- $30\,\mathrm N$ downwards at $P$ and $30\,\mathrm N$ upwards at $O$

## Explanation

The force at P must produce clockwise torque. On the right it does so by pointing downwards. The initial couple supplies no net force, so the hinge must cancel the new force at P in full. Its reaction passes through O and leaves the torque balance unchanged.

$$
\begin{aligned}
F_{P,y}&=-\frac{9\,\mathrm{N\,m}}{0.30\,\mathrm m}=-30\,\mathrm N\\[6pt]
R_{O,y}&=-F_{P,y}=+30\,\mathrm N.
\end{aligned}
$$
