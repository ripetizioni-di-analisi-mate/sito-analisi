A box rests on a horizontal surface. Its centre of mass is a horizontal distance $b=0.24\,\mathrm m$ from the edge it is pushed towards. The static friction coefficient is $0.30$. At what height $h$ must a horizontal push act for the sliding and tipping thresholds to coincide?

- $0.8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.24\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.25\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Before motion, the normal reaction equals the weight $P$, so the sliding threshold is $0.30P$. The tipping threshold follows from $Fh=Pb$. Equating the two forces cancels $P$ and gives $h=\dfrac{0.24}{0.30}\,\mathrm m=0.80\,\mathrm m$. Above this height the tipping threshold is reached first.
