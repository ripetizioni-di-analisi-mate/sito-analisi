A $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ system initially has centre of gravity at $x=1\,\mathrm m$. An additional $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ is placed at $x=3\,\mathrm m$. What is the new coordinate?

- $1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

For moments, the original system can be replaced by its total mass at its centre. The two equivalent masses are now equal, so the new centre is halfway between their positions.

$$
x_G=\frac{4\cdot1+4\cdot3}{8}\,\mathrm m=2\,\mathrm m.
$$
