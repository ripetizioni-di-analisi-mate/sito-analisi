The initial centre of gravity lies at $x=1\,\mathrm m$ for a total mass of $4\,\mathrm{kg}$. An additional $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ is placed at $x=3\,\mathrm m$. What is the new coordinate?

- $1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

For moments, the original system can be replaced by its total mass at its centre. The two equivalent masses are now equal, so the new centre is halfway between their positions. The numerator is $4\cdot1+4\cdot3=16\,\mathrm{kg\,m}$ and the denominator is $8\,\mathrm{kg}$. The centre moves just one metre because the added mass is half the new system.

$$
x_G=\frac{4\cdot1+4\cdot3}{8}\,\mathrm m=2\,\mathrm m.
$$
