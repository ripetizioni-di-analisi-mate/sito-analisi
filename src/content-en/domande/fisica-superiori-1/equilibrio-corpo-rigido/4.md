A force of $ 45\,\mathrm N$ acts along a line passing through the pivot. What is its moment about that pivot?

- $45\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $-45\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N\,m}$

## Explanation

The pivot lies on the line of action, so the perpendicular distance is zero. The moment vanishes even though the force is $45\,\mathrm N$.
