Two equal and opposite parallel forces of $ 160\,\mathrm N$ have lines of action separated by $0.1\,\mathrm m$. What is the magnitude of the couple's moment?

- $0\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $160\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $32\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $16\,\mathrm{N\,m}$

## Explanation

The arm for a couple is the distance between the lines. The result is $160\cdot0.1=16\,\mathrm{N\,m}$.
