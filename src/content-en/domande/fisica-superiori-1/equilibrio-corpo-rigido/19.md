On a rod, a force has perpendicular component $6\,\mathrm N$ and parallel component $8\,\mathrm N$. It acts $1.5\,\mathrm m$ from the pivot. What is its moment magnitude?

- $12\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $21\,\mathrm{N\,m}$

## Explanation

The parallel component passes through the pivot and has zero arm. The perpendicular part uses the full distance and supplies the only contribution.

$$
M=F_\perp r=6\cdot1.5\,\mathrm{N\,m}=9\,\mathrm{N\,m}.
$$
