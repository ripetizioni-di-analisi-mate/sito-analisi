A crank of length $0.25\,\mathrm m$ must produce a moment of magnitude $15\,\mathrm{N\,m}$. What is the minimum force if the direction is freely chosen?

- $60\,\mathrm{N}$
- $3.75\,\mathrm{N}$
- $15\,\mathrm{N}$
- $30\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Minimize the force by using the greatest available moment arm. The entire crank length is the arm only when force is perpendicular. An oblique force would shorten the arm and require a greater magnitude.

$$
F_{\min}=\frac{M}{r}=\frac{15\,\mathrm{N\,m}}{0.25\,\mathrm m}=60\,\mathrm N.
$$
