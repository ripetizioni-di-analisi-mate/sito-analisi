A uniform $5\,\mathrm m$ beam weighs $40\,\mathrm N$ and rests on A and B at its ends. With a single added downward load, the measured reactions are $R_A=44\,\mathrm N$ and $R_B=56\,\mathrm N$. How far from A does the added load act?

- $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The reactions support $100\,\mathrm N$ in total. Subtracting the beam's own weight leaves $60\,\mathrm N$ for the load. Taking moments about A gives $56\cdot5=40\cdot2.5+60x$. Hence $60x=180$, so $x=3\,\mathrm m$. The load is beyond the midpoint, consistent with the larger reaction at B.
