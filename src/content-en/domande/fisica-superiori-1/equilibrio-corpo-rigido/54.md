A uniform beam $4\,\mathrm m$ long weighs $40\,\mathrm N$ and rests on supports at its ends A and B. Two downward loads act with magnitudes $60\,\mathrm N$ at $1\,\mathrm m$ from A and $20\,\mathrm N$ at $3\,\mathrm m$ from A. What is the vertical reaction $R_B$?

- $30\,\mathrm{N}$
- $50\,\mathrm{N}$
- $70\,\mathrm{N}$
- $120\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Apply the beam's own weight at its midpoint. All three loads give clockwise moments about A, so add their weight–distance products. Reaction B counters the moment four metres away. The vertical balance gives seventy newtons at A and checks the total load of one hundred and twenty.

$$
R_B=\frac{40\cdot2+60\cdot1+20\cdot3}{4}\,\mathrm N=50\,\mathrm N.
$$
