A uniform beam weighing $ 32\,\mathrm N$ is supported at its ends with an additional load $ 64\,\mathrm N$ at its centre. What is each reaction?

- $32\,\mathrm N$
- $96\,\mathrm N$
- $64\,\mathrm N$
- $48\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Both weights act at the centre and add to $96\,\mathrm N$. The equal reactions are therefore $48\,\mathrm N$ each.
