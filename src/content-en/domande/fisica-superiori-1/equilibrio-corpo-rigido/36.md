A $30\,\mathrm N$ weight acts $0.4\,\mathrm m$ to the right of a pivot. An upward rope acts farther right at $0.6\,\mathrm m$. What is the balancing tension, neglecting lever weight?

- $30\,\mathrm{N}$
- $45\,\mathrm{N}$
- $50\,\mathrm{N}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Forces on the same side of a pivot can produce opposite moments when their directions differ. Weight tends to lower the right side. The rope tends to lift it and has the longer arm, so it needs fewer newtons than the weight.

$$
T\cdot0.6=30\cdot0.4\quad\Longrightarrow\quad T=20\,\mathrm N.
$$
