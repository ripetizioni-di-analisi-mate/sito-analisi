An upward force of $24\,\mathrm N$ acts on a rod at $x=0$, and a downward force of $36\,\mathrm N$ acts at $x=2\,\mathrm m$. At what coordinate $d$, possibly beyond both points of application, must the reference point be placed to obtain zero total moment?

- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

With the new reference point, the horizontal coordinates of the points of application are $-d$ and $2-d$. The equation $24(-d)-36(2-d)=0$ gives $12d=72$, so $d=6\,\mathrm m$. The two moments cancel about that point, while a downward resultant of $12\,\mathrm N$ remains.
