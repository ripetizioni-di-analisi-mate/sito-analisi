A force has a moment of magnitude $ 16\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ with moment arm $0.2\,\mathrm m$. What is the force magnitude?

- $3.2\,\mathrm N$
- $80\,\mathrm N$
- $16\,\mathrm N$
- $800\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The arm is known, so divide the moment by $0.2\,\mathrm m$. The force is $80\,\mathrm N$. Check the result by reconstructing the moment. A force of $80\,\mathrm N$ at one fifth of a metre produces $16\,\mathrm{N\,m}$. Multiplying the two given values would produce a unit other than newtons.
