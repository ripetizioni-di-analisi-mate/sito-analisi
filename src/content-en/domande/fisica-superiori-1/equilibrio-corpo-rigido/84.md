An ideal plier arm has effort arm $18\,\mathrm{cm}$ and load arm $3\,\mathrm{cm}$. Its handle receives $12\,\mathrm N$. With the forces normal to the lever, what force does that half apply to the object?

- $2\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$
- $36\,\mathrm{N}$
- $72\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Isolate one half and balance the effort and load moments about the hinge. The effort arm is six times the load arm, so the force that half exerts on the object has six times the magnitude of the handle force.

$$
F_r=\frac{12\cdot18}{3}\,\mathrm N=72\,\mathrm N.
$$
