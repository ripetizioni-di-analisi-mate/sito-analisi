For one half of an ideal pair of pliers, during the same small rotation about the pivot the handle travels through an arc of $15\,\mathrm{mm}$ and the jaw through an arc of $5\,\mathrm{mm}$. The object pushes on the jaw with $54\,\mathrm N$. If both forces are tangent to their arcs, what handle force is needed for equilibrium?

- $162\,\mathrm N$
- $54\,\mathrm N$
- $6\,\mathrm N$
- $18\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

For the same rotation angle, the arcs are proportional to their distances from the pivot. Because the two forces are tangential, those distances are also their moment arms. The handle’s moment arm is three times longer and it must oppose the jaw’s torque. Its force is one third of the load.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{b_{\mathrm m}}{b_{\mathrm r}}&=\frac{15\,\mathrm{mm}}{5\,\mathrm{mm}}=3\\[6pt]
F_{\mathrm m}&=54\,\mathrm N\cdot\frac{1}{3}=18\,\mathrm N.
\end{aligned}
$$
