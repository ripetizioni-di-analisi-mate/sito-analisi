Two anticlockwise moments are $3$ and $7\,\mathrm{N\,m}$, and a clockwise moment is $8\,\mathrm{N\,m}$. With anticlockwise positive, what is the remaining moment?

- $2\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $18\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $-18\,\mathrm{N\,m}$

## Explanation

Add the contributions that turn the same way. The only clockwise contribution is negative, leaving a positive total.

$$
M=(3+7-8)\,\mathrm{N\,m}=2\,\mathrm{N\,m}.
$$
