A system of forces in a plane has a nonzero resultant and total moment. Each point of application and its force are rotated by $180^\circ$ about pivot O while the axes stay fixed with their origin at O. What happens to the resultant and the total moment about O?

- The resultant reverses direction and the moment is unchanged
- The resultant reverses direction and the moment changes sign
- Both the resultant and the moment are unchanged
- The resultant is unchanged and the moment reverses sign

## Explanation

The rotation reverses both components of each force, so the resultant reverses direction. The coordinates of the point of application also change sign. Each product in the moment has two factors that change sign, so every individual contribution stays the same and so does the sum.

$$
M'_O=(-x)(-F_y)-(-y)(-F_x)=xF_y-yF_x=M_O.
$$
