A $48\,\mathrm N$ load acts downwards $0.25\,\mathrm m$ to the right of the pivot of a weightless horizontal lever. A force $\vec F$ acts $0.50\,\mathrm m$ to the right of the pivot. The pivot is frictionless and can provide a horizontal reaction of at most $10\,\mathrm N$ in magnitude. Which components of $\vec F$ allow equilibrium? The $x$ axis points right and the $y$ axis points up.

- $F_x=6\,\mathrm N$ and $F_y=20\,\mathrm N$
- $F_x=14\,\mathrm N$ and $F_y=24\,\mathrm N$
- $F_x=8\,\mathrm N$ and $F_y=24\,\mathrm N$
- $F_x=8\,\mathrm N$ and $F_y=48\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The horizontal component passes through the pivot and has zero moment. The moment balance gives $0.50 F_y=0.25\cdot48$, so $F_y=24\,\mathrm N$. The horizontal reaction must be $-F_x$. Of the two forces with the required vertical component, only the one with $F_x=8\,\mathrm N$ stays within the $10\,\mathrm N$ limit. The vertical component of the reaction is $48-24=24\,\mathrm N$.
