The pivot and load stay fixed and the effort is applied obliquely, with the magnitude found for a right-angle application. What happens?

- The moment is unchanged at any angle
- The effort moment increases
- The effort moment is smaller and insufficient
- The load weight vanishes

## Explanation

The perpendicular application yields the greatest arm. Tilting the force reduces the effective component, so the original moment balance fails.
