A push applied at right angles to the end of a handle is redirected to $30^\circ$ to the handle without changing its magnitude or point. What happens to the moment magnitude?

- It doubles
- It stays unchanged
- It vanishes
- It halves

## Explanation

Initially the full force contributes. The new perpendicular component is half its magnitude because $\sin30^\circ=\dfrac{1}{2}$. The distance $r$ from the pivot to the point of application stays fixed. Only the effective arm changes, from $r\sin90^\circ=r$ to $r\sin30^\circ=\dfrac{r}{2}$, so its product with the force changes by the same factor.
