A push applied at right angles to the end of a handle is redirected to $30^\circ$ to the handle without changing its magnitude or point. What happens to the moment magnitude?

- It doubles
- It stays unchanged
- It vanishes
- It halves

## Explanation

Initially the full force contributes. The new perpendicular component is half its magnitude because $\sin30^\circ=1/2$.
