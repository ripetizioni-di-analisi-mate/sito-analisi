To obtain a moment of $9\,\mathrm{N\,m}$, two opposite $30\,\mathrm N$ forces are applied. What separation between their lines is required?

- $0.15\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The couple formula uses the magnitude of one force. Both forces are already accounted for by the distance between their lines. This gives $d=\dfrac{9\,\mathrm{N\,m}}{30\,\mathrm N}=0.3\,\mathrm m$. Taking moments on the first line, the whole $9\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ comes from the lever arm of the other force.

$$
d=\frac{9\,\mathrm{N\,m}}{30\,\mathrm N}=0.3\,\mathrm m.
$$
