A lever has two downward forces on opposite sides of its pivot. What determines which way it tends to rotate?

- Only the larger force
- Comparing each force times its own arm
- Only the longer arm
- The sum of the force magnitudes

## Explanation

The products give moments in opposite directions. A smaller force may produce a larger moment when it acts with a sufficiently long arm. For example, $20\,\mathrm N$ at $0.2\,\mathrm m$ gives $4\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ and $10\,\mathrm N$ at $0.5\,\mathrm m$ gives $5$. The second moment is larger. If the products are equal, the total moment is zero.
