A lever has two downward forces on opposite sides of its pivot. What determines which way it tends to rotate?

- Only the larger force
- Comparing each force times its own arm
- Only the longer arm
- The sum of the force magnitudes

## Explanation

The products give moments in opposite directions. The larger moment wins, which can belong to the smaller force if its arm is longer.
