A $30\,\mathrm N$ upward force acts along a line $20\,\mathrm{cm}$ to the right of pivot $O$. It is replaced with a force of the same magnitude pointing horizontally to the right. Where must the new line pass to preserve the magnitude and sign of the torque about $O$?

- $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ above $O$
- $20\,\mathrm{cm}$ above $O$
- $20\,\mathrm{cm}$ below $O$
- $40\,\mathrm{cm}$ below $O$

## Explanation

The initial force tends to turn the body anticlockwise and has a twenty-centimetre moment arm. At the same force magnitude the same moment arm is needed to reproduce the torque magnitude. A force pushing to the right produces anticlockwise torque when its line passes below the pivot.
