Three nonzero forces act on lines that all meet at O. Which balance is automatic about O?

- The forces have zero resultant
- The vertical components add to zero
- Every force has zero magnitude
- The total moment is zero

## Explanation

Each line of action passes through O and has zero arm. Determining the resultant still requires adding the vectors. The three zero moments add to $0\,\mathrm{N\,m}$, regardless of the force magnitudes. The resultant can still be nonzero if, for example, two of the forces point the same way and the third is weaker.
