Three nonzero forces act on lines that all meet at O. Which balance is automatic about O?

- The forces have zero resultant
- All weights are equal
- Every force has zero magnitude
- The total moment is zero

## Explanation

Each line of action passes through O and has zero arm. Determining the resultant still requires adding the vectors.
