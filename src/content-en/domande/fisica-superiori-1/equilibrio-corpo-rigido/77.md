A box on a flat base has its centre of gravity nearer the right edge. With sufficient friction to prevent sliding, in which direction is the tipping threshold lower at a fixed pushing height?

- Toward the right, because weight has a shorter arm about the right edge
- Toward the left, because the centre lies farther right
- Equally in both directions, because weight is the same
- Toward the left, because a longer arm restores less

## Explanation

The edge on the push side is the reference at the threshold. A shorter weight arm gives a smaller restoring moment. At fixed weight and height, the threshold is $\dfrac{Pb}{h}$. The centre of mass is closer to the right edge, so $b$ is smaller for a push towards that side.
