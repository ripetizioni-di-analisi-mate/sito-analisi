A box on a flat base has its centre of gravity nearer the right edge. In which direction is it less stable at a fixed pushing height?

- Toward the right, because weight has a shorter arm about the right edge
- Toward the left, because the centre lies farther right
- Equally in both directions, because weight is the same
- Toward the left, because a longer arm restores less

## Explanation

The edge on the push side is the reference at the threshold. A shorter weight arm gives a smaller restoring moment.
