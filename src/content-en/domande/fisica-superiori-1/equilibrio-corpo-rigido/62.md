An object rests on a horizontal surface. The vertical through its centre of gravity passes through the interior of its support base. What is possible under its weight and the reaction from the surface alone?

- Weight necessarily causes tipping
- A support resultant inside the base can balance weight
- The support resultant must act outside the base
- The normal resultant must act downward

## Explanation

Weight and the upward reaction can share the same line. The contact region can contain the required line of action. The total reaction must equal the weight and share its vertical line of action. Shifting it towards an edge at the same magnitude would introduce a moment about the centre of mass.
