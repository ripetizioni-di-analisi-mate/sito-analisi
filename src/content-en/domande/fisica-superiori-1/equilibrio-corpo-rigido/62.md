The vertical through an object's centre of gravity still falls inside its flat support base. What is possible with only weight and support?

- Weight necessarily causes tipping
- A support resultant inside the base can balance weight
- The support resultant must act outside the base
- The normal resultant must act downward

## Explanation

Weight and the upward reaction can share the same line. The contact region can contain the required line of action.
