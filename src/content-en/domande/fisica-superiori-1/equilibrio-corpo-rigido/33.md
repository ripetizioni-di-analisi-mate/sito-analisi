A light lever carries $60\,\mathrm N$ at $0.2\,\mathrm m$ left of O and a downward force $0.6\,\mathrm m$ to the right adjusted for equilibrium. What is the vertical pivot reaction?

- $80\,\mathrm{N}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N}$
- $40\,\mathrm{N}$
- $60\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

First balance the moments about the pivot to obtain the force on the right. Then the pivot balances both downward vertical forces. The upward reaction exceeds either load and still gives zero moment about the pivot.

$$
\begin{aligned}F&=\frac{60\cdot0.2}{0.6}\,\mathrm N=20\,\mathrm N\\[6pt]R&=(60+20)\,\mathrm N=80\,\mathrm N.\end{aligned}
$$
