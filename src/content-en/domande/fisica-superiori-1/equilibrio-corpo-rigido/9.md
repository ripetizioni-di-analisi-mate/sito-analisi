A couple of forces of magnitude $ 80\,\mathrm N$ separated by $0.2\,\mathrm m$ acts alone on a rigid body. Which description is correct?

- Zero resultant and nonzero total moment
- Equilibrium because the resultant is zero
- Zero moment because the forces are opposite
- Resultant equal to the sum of the magnitudes

## Explanation

The vectors cancel in the resultant. The product $80\cdot0.2$ gives a moment of $16\,\mathrm{N\,m}$, so the moment balance fails. For a free body, a zero force sum rules out acceleration of the centre of mass. The moment of $16\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ still tends to rotate the body.
