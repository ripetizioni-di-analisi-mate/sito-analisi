The coordinate origin is moved $2\,\mathrm m$ right for the same system. What changes?

- The physical centre moves right by $2\,\mathrm m$
- The masses are multiplied by two
- The centre's coordinate falls by $2\,\mathrm m$ while its physical position stays fixed
- The new coordinate is always zero

## Explanation

Every coordinate decreases by the same amount. The weighted mean decreases by the same amount, but none of the bodies move.
