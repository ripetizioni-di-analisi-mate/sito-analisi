A system has zero resultant and moment $7\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ anticlockwise about A. What is its moment about another point B?

- The same anticlockwise moment
- The same clockwise moment
- Zero moment
- A moment that is always halved

## Explanation

The change between references is the moment associated with the resultant. Since it is zero, the total moment is independent of the point. The moment stays at $7\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ anticlockwise. Moving the reference can alter each individual lever arm, but the changes in their contributions add to zero.
