An ideal lever balances $ 80\,\mathrm N$ with arm $0.2\,\mathrm m$ using a force on the opposite side with arm $0.5\,\mathrm m$. What is that force?

- $80\,\mathrm N$
- $32\,\mathrm N$
- $200\,\mathrm N$
- $16\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The moments have opposite signs and equal magnitudes. The known moment is $80\cdot0.2=16\,\mathrm{N\,m}$, so the required force is $16/0.5=32\,\mathrm N$.
