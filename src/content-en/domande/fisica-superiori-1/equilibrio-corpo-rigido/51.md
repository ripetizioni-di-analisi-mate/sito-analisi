A light $6\,\mathrm m$ beam is supported at ends A and B. A load of $90\,\mathrm N$ is $2\,\mathrm m$ from A. What is the vertical reaction difference $R_A-R_B$?

- $60\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-30\,\mathrm{N}$
- $30\,\mathrm{N}$
- $90\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The moments about A contain only the load and reaction B. Reaction A supplies the rest of the weight support, or sixty newtons. Subtracting shows that the support closer to the load carries thirty newtons more.

$$
\begin{aligned}R_B&=\frac{90\cdot2}{6}\,\mathrm N=30\,\mathrm N\\[6pt]R_A-R_B&=(90-30)-30\,\mathrm N=30\,\mathrm N.\end{aligned}
$$
