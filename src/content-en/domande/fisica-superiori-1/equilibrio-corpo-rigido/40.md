Two opposite parallel forces have the same line of action. How does the system differ from a couple with nonzero moment?

- The resultant is the sum of the magnitudes
- One force must be vertical
- The moment doubles
- The separation is zero and the moment vanishes

## Explanation

The resultant is zero in both force arrangements. In this case, the arm from either force to the other's line is zero.
