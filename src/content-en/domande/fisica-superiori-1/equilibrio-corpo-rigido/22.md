The sign convention is reversed from anticlockwise positive to clockwise positive. What changes for a system already in equilibrium?

- The total becomes nonzero
- Individual signs reverse and the total remains zero
- The resultant gains a vertical component
- The arms double

## Explanation

The convention multiplies every moment by $-1$, so their sum is multiplied by $-1$ and remains zero.
