The sign convention is reversed from anticlockwise positive to clockwise positive. What changes for a system already in equilibrium?

- The total becomes nonzero
- Nonzero moments change sign and the total remains zero
- The resultant gains a vertical component
- The arms double

## Explanation

The convention multiplies every moment by $-1$, so their sum is multiplied by $-1$ and remains zero. For example, $+5$ and $-5\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ become $-5$ and $+5\,\mathrm{N\,m}$. The numerical signs change, while the two physical turning effects still cancel.
