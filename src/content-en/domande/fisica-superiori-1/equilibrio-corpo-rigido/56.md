Both masses on a light bar are doubled without moving them. What happens to the centre of gravity?

- Its coordinate always doubles
- It moves to the midpoint of the masses
- It moves toward the lighter mass
- It stays at the same coordinate

## Explanation

Both numerator and denominator of the weighted mean double. Their ratio is unchanged.
