Both masses on a light bar are doubled without moving them. What happens to the centre of gravity?

- Its coordinate always doubles
- It moves to the midpoint of the masses
- It moves toward the lighter mass
- It stays at the same coordinate

## Explanation

Both numerator and denominator of the weighted mean double. Their ratio is unchanged. In $x_G=\dfrac{m_1x_1+m_2x_2}{m_1+m_2}$, the new ratio multiplies both numerator and denominator by two. Total weight increases while the equivalent point of application stays fixed.
