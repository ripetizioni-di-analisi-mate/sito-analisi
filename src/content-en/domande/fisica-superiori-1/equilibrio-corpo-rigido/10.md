A horizontal uniform beam of weight $ 160\,\mathrm N$ is supported at its ends. With no other loads, what is each vertical reaction?

- $160\,\mathrm N$
- $80\,\mathrm N$
- $320\,\mathrm N$
- $40\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The beam's weight acts at the centre and the reactions are equal. Each is half of $160\,\mathrm N$, or $80\,\mathrm N$. Let $L$ be the distance between the supports. Taking moments about the first gives $R_BL=160\,\dfrac{L}{2}$. The total load is $R_A+R_B=160\,\mathrm N$, so the first support also carries $80\,\mathrm N$.
