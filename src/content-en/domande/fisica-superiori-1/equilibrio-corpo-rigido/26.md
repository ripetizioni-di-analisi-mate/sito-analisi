What check is missing if only horizontal and vertical force balance is known for a planar rigid body?

- The sum of force magnitudes
- The sum of the moments about a point
- The number of applied forces
- The sum of the lever arm lengths

## Explanation

The translational resultant is already zero. The remaining condition concerns turning, represented by the sum of moments. Consider two equal and opposite vertical forces. Their sum is zero along both axes, but their distinct lines of action produce a moment that still needs to be checked.
