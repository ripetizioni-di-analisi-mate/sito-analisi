Equal and opposite couples act in different parts of one rigid bar. What total moment do they give about an arbitrary point?

- Twice one couple's moment
- It depends on how far apart the regions are
- Zero only at the midpoint
- Zero

## Explanation

Each couple has a moment independent of its reference point. Their assigned moments oppose, so they cancel. Moving a couple along the bar leaves its moment unchanged because its resultant is zero. The different positions therefore do not affect the sum of the two opposite contributions.
