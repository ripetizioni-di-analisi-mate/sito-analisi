A horizontal bar extends from $x=0$ to $x=8\,\mathrm m$. An upward force of $8\,\mathrm N$ acts at $x=0$ and a downward force of $12\,\mathrm N$ acts at $x=2\,\mathrm m$. An upward force of $4\,\mathrm N$ is added to cancel the resultant. At which coordinate must it act to cancel the moment as well?

- $1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The vertical sum is already $8-12+4=0$. Taking moments about $x=0$, the first force has zero lever arm, the second contributes $-24\,\mathrm{N\,m}$, and the third contributes $4x$ in newton metres. The condition $4x=24$ requires $x=6\,\mathrm m$. The three forces then satisfy both balance conditions.
