A displacement arrow has components $(-6;8)\,\mathrm m$. An arrow on the same line must point the other way and have magnitude $15\,\mathrm m$. What is its horizontal component?

- $-9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The initial magnitude is $\sqrt{(-6)^2+8^2}\,\mathrm m=10\,\mathrm m$. To reach $15\,\mathrm m$ the scale factor must have magnitude $\dfrac{15}{10}$. The new arrow must also face the opposite way, so the factor is $-1.5$. The horizontal component is $(-1.5)\cdot(-6)\,\mathrm m=9\,\mathrm m$.
