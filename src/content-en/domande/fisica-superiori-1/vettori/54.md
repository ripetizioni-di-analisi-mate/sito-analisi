On an axis positive to the right, walk $4\,\mathrm m$ right and $9\,\mathrm m$ left. What is the signed displacement?

- $5\,\mathrm m$
- $-5\,\mathrm m$
- $13\,\mathrm m$
- $-13\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The second section is negative. The resultant points left because its contribution exceeds the rightward one.

$4-9=-5\,\mathrm m$.
