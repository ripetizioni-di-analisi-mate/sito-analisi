From $\vec u=(-6;8)\,\mathrm m$, construct $\vec v=\tfrac12\vec u$. What are v's components?

- $(3;-4)\,\mathrm m$
- $(-12;16)\,\mathrm m$
- $(-6;4)\,\mathrm m$
- $(-3;4)\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Halve both contributions. A positive scalar, even one below one, preserves each sign.

The values stay in the quadrant with negative horizontal and positive vertical component. With a common tail, the new tip is halfway from the origin to the old tip.
