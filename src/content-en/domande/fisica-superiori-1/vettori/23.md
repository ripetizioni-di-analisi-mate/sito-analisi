The vertical axis points down and the horizontal axis right. What are the components of a displacement three metres right and two metres up?

- $(3;2)\,\mathrm m$
- $(-3;2)\,\mathrm m$
- $(3;-2)\,\mathrm m$
- $(-2;3)\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The convention assigns positive signs to right and down. Moving up is opposite the positive vertical direction.

If upwards were chosen as positive, only the second sign would change. The magnitude, calculated from the squares of three and two, would stay the same.
