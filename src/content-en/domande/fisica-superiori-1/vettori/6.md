A vector has perpendicular components $27$ and $36\,\mathrm N$. What is its magnitude?

- $63\,\mathrm N$
- $45\,\mathrm N$
- $9\,\mathrm N$
- $972\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The components are the legs of a right triangle. Magnitude is the hypotenuse, so add the squares of the two legs. The positive root turns squared magnitude into a length. The result exceeds either leg but is smaller than their sum because the direct path is shorter.

By Pythagoras, $|u|=\sqrt{(27)^2+(36)^2}=45\,\mathrm N$.

The three sides are multiples of $3$, $4$ and $5$ by a factor of nine. The value $63\,\mathrm N$ would add the legs rather than give the straight segment represented by the arrow.
