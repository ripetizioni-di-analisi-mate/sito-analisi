One arrow points five metres right, another eight metres left. The chosen axis is positive to the left. What component results?

- $-3\,\mathrm m$
- $13\,\mathrm m$
- $3\,\mathrm m$
- $-13\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Left is positive under the stated convention. Add eight to minus five to obtain a positive component.

$8-5=3\,\mathrm m$.
