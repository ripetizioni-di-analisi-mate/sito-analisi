Given $u_x=9\,\mathrm m$ and $v_x=-11\,\mathrm m$, what is the $x$ component of $u-v$?

- $-2\,\mathrm m$
- $2\,\mathrm m$
- $20\,\mathrm m$
- $-20\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Subtracting a vector means adding its opposite. On the horizontal axis alone, reverse the sign of the second vector's component and add it to the first. The originally negative component becomes positive. Adding the second vector's component to the result checks that the first component is recovered.

Therefore $(u-v)_x=9-(-11)=20\,\mathrm m$.
