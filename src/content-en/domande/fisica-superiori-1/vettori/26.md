The origin is moved while both axes remain parallel and point the same ways. How do displacement components between two fixed points change?

- Both reverse sign
- They stay the same
- They double
- They become the endpoint coordinates

## Explanation

The same offset is subtracted from both coordinates on each axis. It cancels when making the difference.
