Vector $u$ has components $(9;-11)\,\mathrm N$. What are the components of $-u$?

- $(9;11)\,\mathrm N$
- $(-9;-11)\,\mathrm N$
- $(11;-9)\,\mathrm N$
- $(-9;11)\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The opposite arrow has the same length but points the other way along the same line. This reverses the effect along each axis, so both signs change. Each pair of corresponding components adds to zero, confirming that the vectors cancel.

Changing both signs gives $(-9;11)\,\mathrm N$.
