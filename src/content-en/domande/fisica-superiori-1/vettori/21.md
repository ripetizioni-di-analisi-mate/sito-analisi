With right and up positive, where does $(0;-6)\,\mathrm m$ point?

- Vertically downward
- Horizontally right
- Vertically upward
- Its orientation is undefined because one component is zero

## Explanation

The zero horizontal component leaves a vertical arrow. The negative vertical component indicates down.

An arrow with components $(6;0)\,\mathrm m$ would have the same magnitude, but would point right. The positions of the two values in the pair specify different axes.
