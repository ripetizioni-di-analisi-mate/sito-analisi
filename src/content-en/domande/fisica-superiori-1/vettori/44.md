What is the magnitude of $(0;-8)\,\mathrm N$?

- $-8\,\mathrm N$
- $64\,\mathrm N$
- $0\,\mathrm N$
- $8\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The horizontal component is zero. The square root of the vertical component's square gives its absolute value.

$8=8\,\mathrm N$.
