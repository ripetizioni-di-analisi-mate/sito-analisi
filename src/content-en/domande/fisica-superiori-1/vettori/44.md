On a line with right positive, a cart first moves $4\,\mathrm m$ left. At the end of its second segment it has travelled a total distance of $15\,\mathrm m$ and is to the right of its starting point. What is its total signed displacement?

- $11\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-7\,\mathrm{m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m}$
- $7\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The total distance adds the lengths of the two segments. The second is $15-4=11\,\mathrm m$ long. It must point right, since the cart ends to the right of its starting point. The change in position is therefore $(-4+11)\,\mathrm m=7\,\mathrm m$, using a negative sign for the first segment.
