A $6\,\mathrm m$ displacement is followed by an $8\,\mathrm m$ displacement. The straight-line distance from the starting point to the endpoint is $10\,\mathrm m$. What angle do the two displacements form?

- $0^\circ$
- $180^\circ$
- $45^\circ$
- $90^\circ$

## Explanation

The two legs and the direct segment form a triangle. Their lengths satisfy $6^2+8^2=10^2$, so by the converse of Pythagoras the angle between the $6$ and $8\,\mathrm m$ sides is a right angle. The angle between the two displacements is supplementary to that interior angle and is also $90^\circ$.
