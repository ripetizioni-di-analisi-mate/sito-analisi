A $6\,\mathrm m$ northward displacement is followed by $8\,\mathrm m$ eastward. What would the net magnitude be if only the second sense were reversed?

- $14\,\mathrm m$
- $2\,\mathrm m$
- $8\,\mathrm m$
- $10\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The contributions remain perpendicular. Changing the horizontal sign does not affect its square in Pythagoras.

$10=10\,\mathrm m$.
