In a tip-to-tail path, the first arrow goes from O to A and the second from A to B. Where is the resultant drawn?

- From B to O
- From A to O
- From B to A
- From O to B

## Explanation

The resultant gives the displacement of the two arrows together. The overall path starts at O and ends at B.
