Vectors $\vec u=(5;5)$ and $\vec v=(2;1)$ are drawn from a common starting point. What is the magnitude of the vector joining their tips?

- $7$
- $25$
- $5$
- $1$

## Explanation

The coordinate differences are three and four. The tip-to-tip vector has the length of their hypotenuse, five.

In the opposite direction the components are $(-3;-4)$. The magnitude remains five because the squares are unchanged. The question asks for a distance, so no direction needs choosing.
