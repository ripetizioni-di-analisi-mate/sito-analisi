Three vectors sum to zero. The first is $(3;0)$ and the second $(0;-4)$. What is the magnitude of the third?

- $5$
- $7$
- $1$
- $25$

## Explanation

The first two give $(3;-4)$ and the third is $(-3;4)$. Its magnitude is the square root of twenty-five.
