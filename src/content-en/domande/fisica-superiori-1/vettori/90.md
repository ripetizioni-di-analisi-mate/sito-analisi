Two vectors have equal horizontal components, and A's vertical component is $3\,\mathrm m$ more than B's. What is the magnitude of A minus B?

- $0\,\mathrm m$
- $3\,\mathrm m$
- $6\,\mathrm m$
- $9\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The horizontal components cancel in the subtraction. Only the vertical difference remains, whose magnitude is three metres.

This gives $\vec A-\vec B=(0;3)\,\mathrm m$, so $\lVert\vec A-\vec B\rVert=3\,\mathrm m$.

The difference is a vertical vector with a positive component of three metres in the axis convention used. Its magnitude equals the absolute value of that component.
