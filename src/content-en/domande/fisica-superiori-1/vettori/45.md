Compare the lengths of $(6;0)$ and $(3;4)$. Which is longer?

- $(6;0)$
- $(3;4)$
- They have the same length
- Comparison needs an angle

## Explanation

The first magnitude is six. The second is the square root of $3^2+4^2=25$, which is five.

You can compare the squared magnitudes directly, $36$ versus $25$, because taking the square root preserves their order for nonnegative values.
