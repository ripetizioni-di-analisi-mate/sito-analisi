A vector has magnitude $10\,\mathrm N$ and horizontal component $8\,\mathrm N$. What information determines whether the vertical component is $+6$ or $-6\,\mathrm N$?

- The unit used for magnitude
- Whether the arrow goes up or down in the chosen axes
- The horizontal location of the point of application
- The fact that magnitude is positive

## Explanation

The squares determine only its vertical size. The arrow's vertical sense selects the sign.
