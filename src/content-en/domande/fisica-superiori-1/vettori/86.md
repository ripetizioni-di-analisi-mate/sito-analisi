Adding $(5;-4)\,\mathrm N$ cancels two forces. What resultant did those two forces have before the third was added?

- $(5;-4)\,\mathrm N$
- $(-5;4)\,\mathrm N$
- $(-5;-4)\,\mathrm N$
- $(0;0)\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The added force is the opposite of the previous sum. Reverse both signs to recover the resultant.

Adding the two pairs used in the reasoning gives $(-5+5;4-4)=(0;0)$. Changing only the horizontal sign would instead leave a nonzero vertical component.
