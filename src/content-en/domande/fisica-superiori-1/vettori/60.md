A resultant is $(0;8)\,\mathrm N$. How must its equilibrant point?

- Horizontally in the positive sense
- Vertically in the positive sense
- Horizontally in the negative sense
- Vertically in the negative sense

## Explanation

The equilibrant keeps the horizontal component at zero and opposes eight vertical newtons. Its vertical component is negative.
