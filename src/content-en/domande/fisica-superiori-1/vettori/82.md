The resultant magnitude is $3\,\mathrm N$ and one vector has magnitude $7\,\mathrm N$. Which magnitude for the other is impossible?

- $4\,\mathrm N$
- $2\,\mathrm N$
- $7\,\mathrm N$
- $10\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The second magnitude must be at least $7-3=4$ for a resultant of three to be possible. Two is too small.

$2=2\,\mathrm N$.
