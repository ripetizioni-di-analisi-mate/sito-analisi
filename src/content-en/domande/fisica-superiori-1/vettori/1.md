Two vectors have horizontal components $u_x=9\,\mathrm N$ and $v_x=11\,\mathrm N$. What is the horizontal component of their sum?

- $20\,\mathrm N$
- $-2\,\mathrm N$
- $99\,\mathrm N$
- $\dfrac{9}{11}\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Follow only the motion along the horizontal axis. The first vector contributes its horizontal component, and the second is added to it. Both are positive, so the sum lies in the positive sense. The vertical components would be needed for the other component or the whole vector's magnitude.

Thus $(u+v)_x=u_x+v_x=9+11=20\,\mathrm N$.
