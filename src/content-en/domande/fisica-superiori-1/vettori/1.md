Two vectors have horizontal components $u_x=9\,\mathrm N$ and $v_x=11\,\mathrm N$. What is the horizontal component of their sum?

- $20\,\mathrm N$
- $-2\,\mathrm N$
- $99\,\mathrm N$
- $\dfrac{9}{11}\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The resultant's horizontal component is the sum of the two vectors' horizontal components. Both are positive, so the projection of the resultant points in the positive direction along the horizontal axis.

Thus $(u+v)_x=u_x+v_x=9+11=20\,\mathrm N$.

Both contributions point the same way, so $9+11=20$ exceeds either term. The horizontal projection alone, however, does not determine the magnitude of the whole resultant.
