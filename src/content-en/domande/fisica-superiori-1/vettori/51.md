Two vectors have magnitudes $5$ and $2\,\mathrm N$. Between what bounds can their sum's magnitude vary?

- From $0$ to $7\,\mathrm N$
- From $2$ to $5\,\mathrm N$
- From $3$ to $7\,\mathrm N$
- From $7$ to $10\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The largest sum uses matching senses. The greatest cancellation uses opposite senses and leaves the difference of the magnitudes.

If the arrows are perpendicular, the magnitude is $\sqrt{5^2+2^2}\,\mathrm N=\sqrt{29}\,\mathrm N$. This lies between three and seven, as expected for an intermediate orientation.
