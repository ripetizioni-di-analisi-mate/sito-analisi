Which of these quantities requires information about orientation to be fully described?

- An object's mass
- An activity's duration
- A container's volume
- Displacement from A to B

## Explanation

The same displacement magnitude can lead to many points. Orientation selects the endpoint for a fixed start.

For example, six metres east and six metres north have the same magnitude. They give different endpoints relative to A because the line followed changes.
