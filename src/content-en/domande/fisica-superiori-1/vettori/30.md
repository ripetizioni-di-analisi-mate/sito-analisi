Which description fits a vector with positive horizontal component and zero vertical component?

- It must point north
- It points along the positive horizontal direction
- It has zero magnitude
- It points up and right

## Explanation

One nonzero component fixes the line orientation along its axis. Its positive sign chooses the positive sense.
