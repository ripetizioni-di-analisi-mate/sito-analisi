Forces $(4;1)$ and $(-2;5)\,\mathrm N$ are to be cancelled by a third force. What components must it have?

- $(2;6)\,\mathrm N$
- $(-2;6)\,\mathrm N$
- $(-2;-6)\,\mathrm N$
- $(-4;-1)\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

First add both forces to obtain $(2;6)$. Reverse both component signs to cancel them.
