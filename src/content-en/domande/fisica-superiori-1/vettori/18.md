Which property is guaranteed for the zero vector?

- It points right
- Both components are zero
- Its magnitude is one
- It is inclined at forty-five degrees

## Explanation

Zero magnitude means the tip coincides with the tail. Both position differences are zero.

From $\sqrt{u_x^2+u_y^2}=0$ both squares must vanish, since they are nonnegative. Just one zero component would still allow an arrow along the other axis.
