Which operation reverses a nonzero vector's sense while preserving its magnitude?

- Multiply by $2$
- Translate the arrow parallel to itself
- Multiply by zero
- Multiply by $-1$

## Explanation

The opposite has both component signs reversed. Their squares stay the same, hence the magnitude does too.

For $\vec u=(2;-3)$, the factor $-1$ gives $(-2;3)$. Both arrows have magnitude $\sqrt{13}$ and lie on opposite sides of their shared tail.
