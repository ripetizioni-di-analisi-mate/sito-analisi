Two components of the same displacement measure $300\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $4\,\mathrm m$. What is its magnitude in metres?

- $7\,\mathrm m$
- $304\,\mathrm m$
- $25\,\mathrm m$
- $5\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

First convert the component given in centimetres to metres. The two consistent lengths are the legs of the five-metre triangle.

The magnitude is $\sqrt{3^2+4^2}\,\mathrm m=5\,\mathrm m$.

A result near three hundred metres would contradict the scales because $300\,\mathrm{cm}=3\,\mathrm m$. The magnitude five exceeds either leg and is below their sum of seven.
