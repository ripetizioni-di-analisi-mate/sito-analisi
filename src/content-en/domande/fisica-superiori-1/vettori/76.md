Two components of the same displacement measure $300\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $4\,\mathrm m$. What is its magnitude in metres?

- $7\,\mathrm m$
- $304\,\mathrm m$
- $25\,\mathrm m$
- $5\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

First convert the horizontal component to three metres. The two consistent lengths are the legs of the five-metre triangle.

$5=5\,\mathrm m$.
