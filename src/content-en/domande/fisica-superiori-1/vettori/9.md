Two collinear forces are $40\,\mathrm N$ to the right and $16\,\mathrm N$ to the left. What is the magnitude of the resultant?

- $24\,\mathrm N$
- $56\,\mathrm N$
- $-24\,\mathrm N$
- $640\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Assign a positive sign to the rightward force and a negative sign to the leftward one. The resultant component is a difference of the magnitudes. The rightward force is larger, so the balance points right. Its magnitude is the absolute value of this balance in the same force unit.

The magnitude is $40-16=24\,\mathrm N$.

With right positive, the component is $+24\,\mathrm N$ because the forty-newton force is larger. The magnitude stays at twenty-four even if left is taken as positive.
