Along an axis, $u=-5\,\mathrm m$ and $v=-2\,\mathrm m$. What component has $u-v$?

- $-7\,\mathrm m$
- $3\,\mathrm m$
- $-3\,\mathrm m$
- $5\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Subtracting the second displacement adds two in the positive sense. The result stays negative because the first displacement is minus five.

$-5-\left(-2\right)=-3\,\mathrm m$.

For the inverse check, add the result to the displacement that was subtracted. This gives $(-3)+(-2)=-5$, matching the first given displacement.
