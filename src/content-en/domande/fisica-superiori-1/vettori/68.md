Two arrows start at a common origin O and point to A and B. Which arrow of the parallelogram represents their sum?

- The side from O to A
- The side from O to B
- The segment from A to B
- The diagonal from O to the opposite vertex

## Explanation

Reach the opposite vertex by following one arrow and then a translated copy of the other. The diagonal gives the total displacement.

The first path follows O–A and then a copy of O–B. The other follows O–B and then a copy of O–A. Both end at the same opposite vertex.
