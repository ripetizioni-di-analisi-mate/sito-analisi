A vector $u=(8;-10)\,\mathrm N$ is multiplied by $-2$. What is the result?

- $(16;-20)\,\mathrm N$
- $(-16;20)\,\mathrm N$
- $(-8;10)\,\mathrm N$
- $(6;-12)\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Multiplication by minus two acts once on the horizontal component and once on the vertical. The initially positive component becomes negative and the other becomes positive. This is a half turn of the arrow together with doubling its length. The multiplier is dimensionless, so components remain in newtons.

The calculation gives $-2u=(-16;20)\,\mathrm N$.
