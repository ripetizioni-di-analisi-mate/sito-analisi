What value of $k$ makes $k(2;-3)=(-6;9)$?

- $3$
- $-2$
- $-3$
- $4.5$

## Explanation

The first component requires a factor of minus three. Applying it to the second gives nine, verifying the choice.

$\frac{-6}{2}=-3$.
