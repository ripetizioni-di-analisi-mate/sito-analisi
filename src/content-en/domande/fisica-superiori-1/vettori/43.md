The vector $\vec v$ has components $(5;-12)\,\mathrm m$. What is the magnitude of the vector $-3\vec v$?

- $13\,\mathrm m$
- $-39\,\mathrm m$
- $39\,\mathrm m$
- $51\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The magnitude of $\vec v$ is obtained from the components with Pythagoras' theorem, so
$$
\lVert\vec v\rVert=\sqrt{5^2+(-12)^2}\,\mathrm m=\sqrt{169}\,\mathrm m=13\,\mathrm m .
$$
Multiplying a vector by a number $k$ multiplies its magnitude by $\lvert k\rvert$. Here $\lvert-3\rvert=3$, so the magnitude of $-3\vec v$ is $3\cdot13\,\mathrm m=39\,\mathrm m$. The minus sign only reverses the direction, and indeed the components $(-15;36)\,\mathrm m$ again give $\sqrt{225+1296}\,\mathrm m=39\,\mathrm m$. The value $51\,\mathrm m$ comes from adding the absolute values of the components instead of using Pythagoras' theorem.
