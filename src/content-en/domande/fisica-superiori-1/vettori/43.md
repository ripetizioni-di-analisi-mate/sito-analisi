On a grid, a robot may move one square right and one up in a single step, or one right and one down. It starts at $(0,0)$. Which point can it reach in four steps?

- $(4,3)$
- $(3,2)$
- $(4,2)$
- $(4,5)$

## Explanation

Each step has horizontal component $+1$, so the final horizontal coordinate is $4$. Three steps up and one down give height $3-1=2$. A sum of four components equal to $+1$ or $-1$ is always even and cannot exceed four.
