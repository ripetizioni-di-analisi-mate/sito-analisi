What is the magnitude of the vector with components $(-3;-4)\,\mathrm m$?

- $7\,\mathrm m$
- $-5\,\mathrm m$
- $5\,\mathrm m$
- $25\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The component squares are nine and sixteen. They add to twenty-five, whose positive square root is the magnitude.

The magnitude is $\sqrt{(-3)^2+(-4)^2}\,\mathrm m=5\,\mathrm m$.

Although the tip lies in the quadrant with both coordinates negative, the length must be positive. Using the signs of the components as the sign of the magnitude would give an impossible result.
