If $\vec u+\vec v=(7;1)$ and $\vec u=(2;4)$, which pair gives $\vec v$?

- $(9;5)$
- $(5;-3)$
- $(-5;3)$
- $(2;-4)$

## Explanation

Subtracting u's components from the resultant leaves the unknown vector's components. The differences are $7-2$ and $1-4$.
