A plot uses $2\,\mathrm m$ per horizontal square and $0.5\,\mathrm m$ per vertical square. An arrow goes three squares right and four down. What are its components?

- $(3;-4)\,\mathrm m$
- $(6;-2)\,\mathrm m$
- $(6;2)\,\mathrm m$
- $(2;-6)\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The horizontal scale converts three squares to six metres. The vertical scale converts four squares to two metres with a downward sign.

Counting squares would give $(3;-4)$, but those are not changes in metres. Convert each axis using its own factor before comparing the two lengths.
