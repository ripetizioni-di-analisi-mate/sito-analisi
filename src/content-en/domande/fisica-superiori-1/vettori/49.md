What is the squared magnitude of the resultant of perpendicular displacements of $2$ and $5\,\mathrm m$?

- $29\,\mathrm m^2$
- $49\,\mathrm m^2$
- $21\,\mathrm m^2$
- $10\,\mathrm m^2$

## Explanation

The perpendicular arrows form the two legs. The resultant's square is the sum of their squares.

$2^{2}+5^{2}=29\,\mathrm m^2$.
