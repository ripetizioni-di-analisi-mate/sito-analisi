The components are $4$ and $4\,\mathrm m$. Which formula gives magnitude without approximation?

- $8\,\mathrm m$
- $16\,\mathrm m$
- $4\sqrt2\,\mathrm m$
- $4\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The squares add to $16+16=32$. Taking the square factor $16$ outside the root gives $4\sqrt2$.
