Two nonzero vectors have zero resultant. What is required of their magnitudes?

- They are equal
- One is twice the other
- One is smaller than one
- Their magnitudes have opposite signs

## Explanation

To reach zero magnitude, the difference of the two magnitudes must be zero. The two vectors also point opposite ways.

Equal magnitudes are necessary but insufficient. Two equal arrows pointing the same way add to twice their length, so the arrows must also point in opposite directions.
