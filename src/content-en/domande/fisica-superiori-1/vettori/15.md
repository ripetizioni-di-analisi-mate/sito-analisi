A vector is horizontal and points left. How are the line orientation and sense distinguished?

- Vertical line and leftward sense
- Horizontal line and upward sense
- Horizontal line and leftward sense
- Vertical line and downward sense

## Explanation

Horizontal identifies the line the arrow lies on. Left picks one of the two ways along that line.
