Two vectors have zero sum and the first is $u=(9;11)\,\mathrm N$. What is the second?

- $(9;11)\,\mathrm N$
- $(-9;-11)\,\mathrm N$
- $(9;-11)\,\mathrm N$
- $(-9;11)\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

A resultant is zero when both components vanish. To satisfy the horizontal condition, take the opposite of the first vector's horizontal component. For the vertical condition do the same with its other component. The new vector is the opposite, of equal length and pointing the other way along the same line.

The second vector must be $v=-u=(-9;-11)\,\mathrm N$.
