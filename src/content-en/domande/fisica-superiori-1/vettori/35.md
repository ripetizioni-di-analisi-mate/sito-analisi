In the figure each horizontal unit represents $2\,\mathrm m$ and each vertical unit $1\,\mathrm m$. What is the physical magnitude of the displacement from A to B?

::grafico{uguale=1 xmin=-1 xmax=7 ymin=-1 ymax=8 nomi="X,Y" vettori="1,1,3,4" punti="1,1,pieno;3,4,pieno,B" etichette="0.73,0.74,A;2.22,2.52,AB" titolo="The axes show unit counts, which must be converted to metres."}

- $7\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The drawing shows two units to the right and three upwards. The physical components are $2\cdot2\,\mathrm m=4\,\mathrm m$ and $3\cdot1\,\mathrm m=3\,\mathrm m$. Apply the scales before using Pythagoras. The magnitude is $\sqrt{4^2+3^2}\,\mathrm m=5\,\mathrm m$, rather than the sum of the two legs.
