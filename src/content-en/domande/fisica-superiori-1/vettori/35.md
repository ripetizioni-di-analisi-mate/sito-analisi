What happens to the resultant when the order of two displacements is swapped while each keeps its own components?

- The resultant always reverses
- Its magnitude becomes the difference of their magnitudes
- The result stays the same, although the intermediate point can change
- The intermediate point is always the same as well

## Explanation

Component sums are independent of order. The first section drawn, however, can end at a different point.
