Which description fits vectors $u=(8;0)\,\mathrm N$ and $v=(0;8)\,\mathrm N$?

- Perpendicular with equal magnitudes
- Equal
- Opposite
- Parallel and pointing the same way

## Explanation

The first vector has only a nonzero horizontal component. The second has only a nonzero vertical component. Their lines are perpendicular. The nonzero components have equal sizes, which give their respective magnitudes. Equal magnitudes can therefore occur for differently oriented arrows.

Both have magnitude $8\,\mathrm N$ and their directions differ.
