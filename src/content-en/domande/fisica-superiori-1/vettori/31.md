Forces $(4;-3)$ and $(-2;-5)\,\mathrm N$ act at the same point. What are the resultant components?

- $(6;2)\,\mathrm N$
- $(2;8)\,\mathrm N$
- $(2;-8)\,\mathrm N$
- $(-8;2)\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Add the two horizontal terms and add the two vertical terms. Both vertical contributions are negative, so their balance is negative.

Horizontally the forces oppose each other and the positive component prevails. Vertically both point downwards, so the negative component has magnitude $3+5=8\,\mathrm N$.
