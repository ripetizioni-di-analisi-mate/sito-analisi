Forces $(4;-3)$ and $(-2;-5)\,\mathrm N$ act at the same point. What are the resultant components?

- $(6;2)\,\mathrm N$
- $(2;8)\,\mathrm N$
- $(2;-8)\,\mathrm N$
- $(-8;2)\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Add the two horizontal terms and add the two vertical terms. Both vertical contributions are negative, so their balance is negative.
