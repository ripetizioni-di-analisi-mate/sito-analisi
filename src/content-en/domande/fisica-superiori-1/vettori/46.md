Two vectors have components $(3;-4)$ and $(-3;-4)$. What follows about their magnitudes?

- The positive horizontal component gives a greater magnitude
- They are equal
- The negative horizontal component gives a greater magnitude
- Both magnitudes are negative

## Explanation

Only the horizontal sign changes and its square stays nine. The sum of squares is therefore the same.

Geometrically, reflecting one tip in the vertical axis gives the other. The reflection changes its position relative to the axis while keeping its distance from the origin.
