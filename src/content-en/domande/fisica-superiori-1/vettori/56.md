On one line, a resultant comes from forces of $12$ and $7\,\mathrm N$. It measures $19\,\mathrm N$. How are their senses related?

- They point opposite ways
- They are perpendicular
- Both necessarily point right
- They point the same way

## Explanation

Nineteen is the sum of the magnitudes. Reaching that maximum requires the two forces to point the same way, whichever way it is.

Opposite directions would give magnitude $12-7=5\,\mathrm N$. Nineteen is exactly the same-direction limit, so the absolute direction along the line is not needed.
