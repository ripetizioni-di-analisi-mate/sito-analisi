A person returns to the starting point along the same line after a $6\,\mathrm m$ outward section. What distance and displacement magnitude result?

- Distance $6\,\mathrm m$ and magnitude $6\,\mathrm m$
- Distance $12\,\mathrm m$ and magnitude zero
- Distance zero and magnitude $12\,\mathrm m$
- Both distance and magnitude zero

## Explanation

Outward and return sections each measure six metres. The endpoint equals the start, so displacement is zero.

The return leg has the same magnitude as the outward leg and the opposite direction. The arrows add to zero, while distance adds two positive lengths.
