Displacement is $(2;-5)\,\mathrm m$ and the endpoint is $B=(8;1)\,\mathrm m$. What was the starting point?

- $(6;6)\,\mathrm m$
- $(10;-4)\,\mathrm m$
- $(6;-4)\,\mathrm m$
- $(8;1)\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Subtract the displacement from the endpoint coordinates. Subtracting $-5$ from the vertical value gives six at the starting point.
