Three points are $A=(1;2)$, $B=(3;3)$, and $C=(6;1)$. What is the net displacement from A to B and then C?

- $(2;1)$
- $(3;-2)$
- $(5;-1)$
- $(6;1)$

## Explanation

Add the two displacements or subtract starting position from ending position. Point B cancels out of the total.
