With a shared tail, which endpoints does $\vec v-\vec u$ connect?

- From the tip of u to the tip of v
- From the tip of v to the tip of u
- From the origin to the tip of v
- From the tip of u back to the origin

## Explanation

The difference asks which arrow must be added to u to obtain v. It starts at the tip of the first and ends at the second.
