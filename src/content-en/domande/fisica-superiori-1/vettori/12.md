In the Cartesian plane, $A=(8;10)\,\mathrm m$ and $B=(32;20)\,\mathrm m$. What are the components of the vector from A to B?

- $(-24;-10)\,\mathrm m$
- $(40;30)\,\mathrm m$
- $(32;20)\,\mathrm m$
- $(24;10)\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The vector describes the difference between the finishing point and starting point. Subtract A's horizontal coordinate from B's and repeat for the vertical coordinate. The results are changes, rather than the final position's coordinates. Adding them to A's coordinates reaches B exactly.

Therefore $AB=(32-8;20-10)=(24;10)\,\mathrm m$.
