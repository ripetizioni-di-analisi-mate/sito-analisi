A vector has components $(9;11)\,\mathrm m$. What components does the vector have after multiplication by 3?

- $(27;33)\,\mathrm m$
- $(12;14)\,\mathrm m$
- $(27;11)\,\mathrm m$
- $(9;33)\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The same multiplier acts on both components, keeping horizontal and vertical distinct. The positive scalar triples the arrow's length while preserving its sense. Their ratio therefore stays the same, confirming the same tilt in the plane.

Its components become $(3\cdot9;3\cdot11)=(27;33)\,\mathrm m$.

Since the factor three is positive, the arrow stays in the same quadrant and triples in magnitude. The component ratio remains $\dfrac{33}{27}=\dfrac{11}{9}$.
