A vector has magnitude $7\,\mathrm N$ and horizontal component $-7\,\mathrm N$. What is its vertical component?

- $7\,\mathrm N$
- $-7\,\mathrm N$
- $0\,\mathrm N$
- $14\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The horizontal component accounts for the entire squared magnitude. There is no remaining contribution to the vertical square.

$0=0\,\mathrm N$.
