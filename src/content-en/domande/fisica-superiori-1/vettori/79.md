A force with components $(12;-5)\,\mathrm N$ acts on a ring. A single force of magnitude $12\,\mathrm N$ may be added, pointing either directly left or directly up. What is the smallest achievable magnitude of the resultant?

- $0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $7\,\mathrm{N}$
- $5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $13\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Pointing left cancels the $12\,\mathrm N$ component and leaves only $-5\,\mathrm N$ vertically. Pointing up leaves the horizontal component of $12\,\mathrm N$, so the magnitude is at least twelve. The horizontal choice gives the minimum of $5\,\mathrm N$. Cancelling both components would require a diagonal force.
