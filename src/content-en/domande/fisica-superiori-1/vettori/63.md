Two equal forces are applied at different points on a bar. What information distinguishes their physical representations beyond the vector?

- Their magnitude
- The newton unit
- Their point of application
- Their component signs

## Explanation

Equal vectors encode the same magnitude and orientation. The location where each is applied is separate information.

For example, a force perpendicular to a bar applied near its hinge and the same force applied farther away affect rotation differently even though their components are identical.
