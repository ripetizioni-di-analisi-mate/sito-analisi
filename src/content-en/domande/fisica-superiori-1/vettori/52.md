Two arrows have lengths $8$ and $3\,\mathrm m$. Which value cannot be the magnitude of their sum?

- $5\,\mathrm m$
- $8\,\mathrm m$
- $11\,\mathrm m$
- $4\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The magnitudes differ by five, which is the minimum. A magnitude of four would require more cancellation than opposite senses can provide.

$4=4\,\mathrm m$.
