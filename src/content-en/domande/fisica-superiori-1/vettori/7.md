A vector has magnitude $40\,\mathrm m$ and horizontal component $24\,\mathrm m$. What is the magnitude of its vertical component?

- $16\,\mathrm m$
- $64\,\mathrm m$
- $32\,\mathrm m$
- $40\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The squared magnitude is the sum of the squared components. Subtracting the squared horizontal component isolates the vertical square. Its positive square root gives the requested magnitude. The actual vertical sign would require knowing whether the arrow points up or down.

Therefore $|u_y|=\sqrt{(40)^2-(24)^2}=32\,\mathrm m$.

Check that $\sqrt{24^2+32^2}\,\mathrm m=40\,\mathrm m$. The question asks for the magnitude of one component. These data do not determine whether the arrow rises or falls.
