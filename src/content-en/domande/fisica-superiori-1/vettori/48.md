A reported magnitude is $4\,\mathrm N$ with a horizontal component of $5\,\mathrm N$. What follows for perpendicular axes?

- The vertical component is one
- The vertical component is minus one
- The magnitude must be negative
- The data are incompatible

## Explanation

A leg's squared length cannot exceed the hypotenuse's square. Five is larger than the stated total magnitude.

Substitution would give $u_y^2=4^2-5^2=-9\,\mathrm{N^2}$. No real component has a negative square, so choosing a different sign cannot resolve the discrepancy.
