A cart starts from $A=(2;1)\,\mathrm m$. It repeats the same displacement three times and reaches $B=(-4;13)\,\mathrm m$. What is the vertical component of one displacement?

- $12\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $13\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Find the full change in vertical coordinate by subtracting the initial height from the final one. It is $13-1=12\,\mathrm m$. Each displacement has the same components, so the total vertical change is three times that of one repetition. Dividing by three gives $4\,\mathrm m$ in the positive vertical direction.
