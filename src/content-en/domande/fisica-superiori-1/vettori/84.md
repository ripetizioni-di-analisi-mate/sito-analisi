Starting at $x=-3\,\mathrm m$, make signed displacements $+7$ and $-2\,\mathrm m$. What is the final position?

- $5\,\mathrm m$
- $-8\,\mathrm m$
- $6\,\mathrm m$
- $2\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Add the net displacement to the starting position. The displacement balance is five positive metres.

$-3+7-2=2\,\mathrm m$.
