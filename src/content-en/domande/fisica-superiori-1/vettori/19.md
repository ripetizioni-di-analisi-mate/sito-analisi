Two arrows are perpendicular and each has magnitude five. What follows?

- They are the same vector
- They are opposite vectors
- They have equal magnitude and different line orientations
- They have the same line orientation

## Explanation

Equal magnitude compares only length. Perpendicularity distinguishes the lines along which the arrows point.
