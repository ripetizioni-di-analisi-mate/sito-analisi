A person goes from A to B and then from B to C. They return from C to A along the direct segment. How many metres do they travel in total?

::grafico{uguale=1 xmin=-1 xmax=12 ymin=-1 ymax=15 nomi="x (m),y (m)" vettori="1,1,6,1,AB;6,1,6,13,BC" segmenti="1,1,6,13" punti="1,1,pieno,A;6,1,pieno,B;6,13,pieno,C" titolo="A is (1; 1), B is (6; 1) and C is (6; 13). The return segment is diagonal."}

- $17\,\mathrm{m}$
- $13\,\mathrm{m}$
- $30\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The first two segments measure $6-1=5\,\mathrm m$ and $13-1=12\,\mathrm m$. The return is the triangle's hypotenuse, $\sqrt{5^2+12^2}\,\mathrm m=13\,\mathrm m$ long. Adding all three lengths gives $30\,\mathrm m$. Total displacement is zero because the route closes at A.
