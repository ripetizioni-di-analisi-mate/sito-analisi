A motion has three alternating sections, $5$ metres forward, $7$ backward, and $2$ forward. What total distance is travelled?

- $14\,\mathrm m$
- $0\,\mathrm m$
- $2\,\mathrm m$
- $7\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Distance counts all three sections and adds their lengths. The signed resultant can instead vanish.

$5+7+2=14\,\mathrm m$.

Taking forwards as positive, the signed sum would instead be $5-7+2=0$. This means a return to the starting point and does not cancel the fourteen metres travelled.
