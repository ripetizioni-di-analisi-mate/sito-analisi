What displacement is obtained by adding $200\,\mathrm{cm}$ to the right and $3\,\mathrm m$ upward?

- $(200;3)\,\mathrm m$
- $(2;3)\,\mathrm m$
- $(5;0)\,\mathrm m$
- $(2;-3)\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Two hundred centimetres equal two metres. The contributions are perpendicular, so they enter separate coordinates.

The component pairs are $(2;0)\,\mathrm m$ and $(0;3)\,\mathrm m$. In the same unit they give a diagonal arrow reaching the point two metres right and three up.
