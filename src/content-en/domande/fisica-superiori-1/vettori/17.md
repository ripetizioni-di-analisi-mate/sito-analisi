A displacement arrow is shifted five squares without rotating it. How does the represented vector change?

- It stays the same
- Its magnitude increases by five
- Its sense reverses
- Its vertical component vanishes

## Explanation

Translation shifts tail and tip in the same way. The differences of their coordinates stay unchanged.

If all horizontal coordinates increase by the same amount, the endpoint difference stays the same. The same subtraction applies to the vertical coordinates.
