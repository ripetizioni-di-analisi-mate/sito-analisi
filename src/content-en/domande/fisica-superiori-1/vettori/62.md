A diagonal resultant has both components nonzero. If a single horizontal force is proposed to cancel it, which component remains after the best horizontal correction?

- The horizontal component of the resultant
- The vertical component of the resultant
- Neither component
- The sum of both components as one horizontal force

## Explanation

A horizontal force can cancel the contribution on its own axis. Its vertical component is zero, so the other balance is unchanged.

For example, adding $(-3;0)\,\mathrm N$ to the resultant $(3;4)\,\mathrm N$ gives $(0;4)\,\mathrm N$. The horizontal component vanishes, but the constraint leaves four newtons vertically.
