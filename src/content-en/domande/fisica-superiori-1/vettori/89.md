Two paths in a plane share their start and endpoint. One is straight and the other deviates. What is certainly equal for both paths?

- The total displacement vector
- The distance travelled
- The duration
- The number of turns

## Explanation

Displacement depends only on the starting and ending positions. The shape of the route can change the distance.
