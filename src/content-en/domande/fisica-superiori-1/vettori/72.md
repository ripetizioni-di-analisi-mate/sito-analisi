For components $(6;9)$, what positive scalar gives a horizontal component of $2$?

- $3$
- $\dfrac{2}{9}$
- $12$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

Divide the desired component by its previous value. The same factor reduces the vertical component to three.

$\frac{2}{6}=\dfrac{1}{3}$.
