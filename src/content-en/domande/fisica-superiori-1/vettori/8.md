Two collinear forces of $24$ and $16\,\mathrm N$ both point right. What is the magnitude of the resultant?

- $8\,\mathrm N$
- $384\,\mathrm N$
- $0\,\mathrm N$
- $40\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Both forces lie on the same line. Taking right as positive, both components are positive and add. Since the result is positive, its magnitude equals its component's numerical value and the arrow still points right. Two aligned vectors do not require a right triangle.

Thus $R=24+16=40\,\mathrm N$.

On a line the largest possible magnitude is $24+16=40\,\mathrm N$, which occurs in this configuration. Opposite directions would leave $8\,\mathrm N$ instead.
