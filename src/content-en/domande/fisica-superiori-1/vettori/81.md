The greatest magnitude of a two-vector sum is $12\,\mathrm N$ and its least is $4\,\mathrm N$. What is the larger of the two magnitudes?

- $8\,\mathrm N$
- $4\,\mathrm N$
- $16\,\mathrm N$
- $12\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The maximum adds the magnitudes and the minimum subtracts the smaller from the larger. Adding these results gives twice the larger.

$\frac{12+4}{2}=8\,\mathrm N$.
