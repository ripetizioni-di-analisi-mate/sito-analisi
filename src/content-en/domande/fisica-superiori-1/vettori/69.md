A traveller goes from O to A, from A to B, and from B to O. What is known about the arrow sum even when their magnitudes are unknown?

- It is the zero vector
- Its magnitude is the first section's length
- It is the vector from O to A
- It must be parallel to the second arrow and nonzero

## Explanation

The last tip returns to the first tail. The path gives no net change either horizontally or vertically.
