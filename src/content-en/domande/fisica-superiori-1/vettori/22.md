With right and up positive, what are the component signs of an arrow pointing up and left?

- $u_x>0$ and $u_y<0$
- $u_x<0$ and $u_y>0$
- $u_x<0$ and $u_y<0$
- $u_x=0$ and $u_y>0$

## Explanation

Moving left decreases the horizontal coordinate. Moving up increases the vertical coordinate.

With the tail at the origin, the tip is therefore in the quadrant with negative horizontal coordinates and positive vertical coordinates. These signs do not determine the arrow's length.
