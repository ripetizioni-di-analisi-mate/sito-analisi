All coordinates in a displacement plot are converted from metres to centimetres. What happens to the numerical components?

- Both are multiplied by one hundred without changing sign
- Only the horizontal is multiplied by one hundred
- Both are divided by one hundred
- Both reverse sign

## Explanation

A metre contains a hundred centimetres along either axis. The positive factor applies to both components.

For example, $(-2;3)\,\mathrm m$ becomes $(-200;300)\,\mathrm{cm}$. The numerical magnitude also multiplies by one hundred, while the arrow represented stays the same.
