If coordinates are written vertical first and horizontal second, how is the vector $(2;-6)$ rewritten?

- $(-2;6)$
- $(2;6)$
- $(-6;2)$
- $(6;-2)$

## Explanation

The physical arrow stays the same. Move the negative vertical value to the first position of the pair.
