A horizontal vector and a nonzero vertical vector are added. Which horizontal component does the resultant have?

- The magnitude of the vertical vector
- The difference of their magnitudes
- Twice the horizontal component
- That of the horizontal vector

## Explanation

The vertical vector contributes zero to the horizontal sum. Only the first vector's horizontal component remains.
