Two nonzero vectors, one horizontal and one vertical, are added. What is the horizontal component of the resultant?

- The magnitude of the vertical vector
- The difference of their magnitudes
- Twice the horizontal component
- That of the horizontal vector

## Explanation

The vertical vector contributes zero to the horizontal sum. Only the first vector's horizontal component remains.

If the contributions were two metres right and three metres up, the final horizontal coordinate would increase by two even though the overall displacement was diagonal.
