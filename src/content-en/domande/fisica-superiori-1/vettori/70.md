Under what condition is $\vec u-\vec v$ zero?

- Only their magnitudes agree
- Both components of u equal those of v
- The vectors are nonzero opposites
- The vectors are nonzero and perpendicular

## Explanation

The difference is zero when each component subtraction gives zero. Equal lengths alone do not enforce that condition.

For example, $(3;4)$ and $(4;3)$ both have magnitude five. Their difference is $(-1;1)$, however, because the condition must be checked for each component separately.
