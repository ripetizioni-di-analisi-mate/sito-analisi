A sensor starts at $(5;2)\,\mathrm m$ and moves by $(-3;4)\,\mathrm m$. Where does it arrive?

- $(8;-2)\,\mathrm m$
- $(-3;4)\,\mathrm m$
- $(5;6)\,\mathrm m$
- $(2;6)\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Final position is initial position plus displacement. The results are $5-3=2$ and $2+4=6$.

The starting point is not the origin. Its horizontal coordinate is five and its vertical coordinate is two. Reading $(-3;4)$ directly would therefore give the change rather than the endpoint.
