An object starts from the position $(5;2)\,\mathrm m$ and undergoes the displacement $(-3;4)\,\mathrm m$. In which position does it end up?

- $(8;-2)\,\mathrm m$
- $(-3;4)\,\mathrm m$
- $(5;6)\,\mathrm m$
- $(2;6)\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The final position is the sum of the initial position and the displacement, component by component. The final $x$-coordinate is therefore $5+(-3)=2$ and the final $y$-coordinate is $2+4=6$. Hence the object ends up at the position $(2;6)\,\mathrm m$.
