Which property holds for $-\tfrac12\vec u$ if $\vec u$ is nonzero?

- Opposite sense and half the magnitude
- Opposite sense and double the magnitude
- Same sense and half the magnitude
- Perpendicular line and half the magnitude

## Explanation

The minus sign reverses the arrow. The factor's absolute value, one half, determines the new magnitude.
