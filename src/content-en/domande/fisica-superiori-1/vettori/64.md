A and B both have vertical coordinate $7$, but their horizontal coordinate goes from $9$ to $4$. What horizontal component has the vector from A to B?

- $5$
- $9$
- $4$
- $-5$

## Explanation

The component uses final minus initial horizontal coordinate. The arrow is horizontal and its sign is negative.

$4-9=-5$.

The equal vertical coordinates give zero vertical displacement. With $x$ pointing right, the negative sign in $4-9=-5$ corresponds to motion to the left.
