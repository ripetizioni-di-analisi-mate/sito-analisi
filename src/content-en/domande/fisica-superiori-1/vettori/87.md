Three forces are $(1;3)$, $(2;-1)$, and $(-3;1)\,\mathrm N$. Which check for a zero resultant fails?

- Only the vertical sum is zero
- Both sums are zero
- Only the horizontal sum is zero, and the vertical sum remains positive
- Neither sum is zero

## Explanation

The horizontal components give $1+2-3=0$. The vertical components give $3-1+1=3$ and still require correction.
