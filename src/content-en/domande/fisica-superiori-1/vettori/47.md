An arrow has magnitude $13\,\mathrm m$, horizontal component $5\,\mathrm m$, and points down. What is its vertical component?

- $12\,\mathrm m$
- $-8\,\mathrm m$
- $-12\,\mathrm m$
- $-18\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Subtracting the squares gives $13^2-5^2=144$. The vertical magnitude is twelve and pointing down gives it a negative sign.

$-12=-12\,\mathrm m$.
