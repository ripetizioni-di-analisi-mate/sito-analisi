A displacement has magnitude $13\,\mathrm m$ and horizontal component $5\,\mathrm m$. The starting elevation is $10\,\mathrm m$ and the final elevation lies between $15$ and $25\,\mathrm m$. What is the final elevation?

- $-2\,\mathrm m$
- $10\,\mathrm m$
- $22\,\mathrm m$
- $18\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

By Pythagoras, the change in elevation has magnitude $\sqrt{13^2-5^2}\,\mathrm m=12\,\mathrm m$. The two candidate elevations are $10+12=22\,\mathrm m$ and $10-12=-2\,\mathrm m$. Only $22\,\mathrm m$ lies in the given interval, so the change is positive. The value $12\,\mathrm m$ describes the change in elevation.
