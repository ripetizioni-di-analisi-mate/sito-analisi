Construct $\vec w=2\vec u-\vec v$ with $\vec u=(1;4)$ and $\vec v=(3;2)$. Which pair results?

- $(1;-6)$
- $(-1;6)$
- $(5;10)$
- $(-2;2)$

## Explanation

The product $2\vec u$ gives $(2;8)$. Subtract $(3;2)$ from that scaled pair.

The negative horizontal component shows that the doubled initial contribution of two is outweighed by the three being subtracted. The vertical component stays positive because eight exceeds two.
