Two vectors have vertical components $u_y=8\,\mathrm N$ and $v_y=-10\,\mathrm N$. What is the vertical component of their sum?

- $18\,\mathrm N$
- $-2\,\mathrm N$
- $2\,\mathrm N$
- $80\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The two terms are signed components. Add the negative component with its sign, cancelling part of the positive one. A negative balance means the vertical result points opposite the positive axis, while its magnitude along that axis is the absolute value.

The calculation gives $(u+v)_y=8+(-10)=-2\,\mathrm N$.
