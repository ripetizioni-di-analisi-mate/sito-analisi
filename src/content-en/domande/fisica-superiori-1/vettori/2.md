A force $\vec F_1$ has components $(3;7)\,\mathrm N$. After adding a second force $\vec F_2$, the resultant must have components $(1;5)\,\mathrm N$. What is the signed vertical component $F_{2,y}$ of the second force?

- $2\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{N}$
- $5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-7\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

To reach the required resultant, the vertical component must change from $7$ to $5\,\mathrm N$. The added force therefore supplies $(5-7)\,\mathrm N=-2\,\mathrm N$. The sign shows that it reduces the upward contribution. Cancelling the first force's vertical component completely would instead leave no vertical resultant.
