On a rightward-oriented axis, the successive displacements are $32\,\mathrm m$ right, $16\,\mathrm m$ left, and $8\,\mathrm m$ right. What is the signed net displacement?

- $56\,\mathrm m$
- $-24\,\mathrm m$
- $24\,\mathrm m$
- $8\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The first and final contributions increase the coordinate, while the middle one decreases it. Adding all three with these signs gives the displacement from start to finish. Total distance would be larger because every section adds positively. The question asks for the signed change in position.

Therefore $s=32-16+8=24\,\mathrm m$.
