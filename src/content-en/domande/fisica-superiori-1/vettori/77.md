Vector $(3;4)\,\mathrm m$ becomes $(6;4)\,\mathrm m$. How much does its squared magnitude change?

- $27\,\mathrm m^2$
- $3\,\mathrm m^2$
- $9\,\mathrm m^2$
- $36\,\mathrm m^2$

## Explanation

The vertical component stays the same. Subtract the square of the previous horizontal component from the square of the new one.

$6^{2}+4^{2}-\left(3^{2}+4^{2}\right)=27\,\mathrm m^2$.
