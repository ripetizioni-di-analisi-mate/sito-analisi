A vector is $(3;0)$. What vertical component can a scalar multiple of it have?

- $3$
- $0$
- $-3$
- $6$

## Explanation

Any number multiplied by the zero second component gives zero. The copy stays on the same line or becomes zero.

Multiplying by a negative number can change the sign of the first component. The product in the second position stays zero, so no vertical displacement appears.
