Two forces of $6$ and $2\,\mathrm N$ point left. What single force can cancel their sum?

- $8\,\mathrm N$ to the right
- $4\,\mathrm N$ to the right
- $8\,\mathrm N$ to the left
- $6\,\mathrm N$ up

## Explanation

The two leftward forces point the same way, so add their magnitudes. Cancellation needs equal magnitude pointing right.
