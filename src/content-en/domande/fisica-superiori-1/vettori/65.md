Travel from $A=(3;2)$ to $B=(7;8)$ and then return directly to A. Which pair describes the return vector?

- $(-4;-6)$
- $(4;6)$
- $(3;2)$
- $(-7;-8)$

## Explanation

The return starts at B. Subtracting B from A gives the two differences with signs opposite to the outward journey.

Check the return by adding $(4;6)+(-4;-6)=(0;0)$. The overall vector of the closed trip is therefore zero even though the segment was travelled twice.
