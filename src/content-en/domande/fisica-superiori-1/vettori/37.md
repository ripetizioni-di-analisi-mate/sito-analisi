Three displacements are $(2;5)$, $(-3;4)$, and $(5;-9)\,\mathrm m$. Along which axis does their resultant lie?

- Along the positive horizontal axis
- Along the negative horizontal axis
- Along the positive vertical axis
- The resultant is zero

## Explanation

The vertical components sum to $5+4-9=0$. The horizontal components sum to $2-3+5=4$, which is positive.

Zero in the second component identifies the axis. Positive four in the first sets the direction, so the arrow is not zero even though the vertical contributions cancel.
