Two vectors each have magnitude $6\,\mathrm N$. Which arrangement gives the minimum possible magnitude of their sum?

- Vectors along one line pointing in the same sense
- Perpendicular vectors
- Vectors at forty-five degrees
- Vectors along one line pointing in opposite senses

## Explanation

Opposite alignment gives full cancellation because neither vector has greater magnitude. The minimum is zero.
