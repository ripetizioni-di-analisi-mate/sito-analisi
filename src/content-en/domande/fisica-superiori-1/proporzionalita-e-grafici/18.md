The line describes the first two readings. Point P is a third reading. What signed vertical change would bring P onto the line at the same horizontal coordinate?

::grafico{f="2*x+2" punti="1,4,pieno;2,6,pieno;4,11,pieno,P" xmin=0 xmax=5 ymin=0 ymax=13 nomi="x (cm),F (N)" titolo="P has horizontal coordinate 4 cm and vertical coordinate 11 N."}

- $1\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-1\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{N}$
- $2\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Between the first two readings force rises from $4$ to $6\,\mathrm N$ over one centimetre, so the slope is $2\,\mathrm{N/cm}$. At $4\,\mathrm{cm}$ the line predicts $6+2\cdot(4-2)=10\,\mathrm N$. The requested change is predicted minus observed, namely $10-11=-1\,\mathrm N$. The residual, defined as measured value minus predicted value, is instead $11-10=1\,\mathrm N$, so the requested change is the opposite of the residual.
