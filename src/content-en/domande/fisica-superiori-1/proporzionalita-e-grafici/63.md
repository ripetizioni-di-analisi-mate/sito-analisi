For $x>0$, what positive horizontal coordinate do the graphs $y=x^2$ and $y=4x$ share?

- $2$
- $1$
- $4$
- $16$

## Explanation

At the intersection the two rules are equal. Division by $x$ is allowed in the positive domain and gives $x=4$.

The same equation also has solution $x=0$, but that coordinate is excluded. At $x=4$ both graphs give a vertical coordinate of sixteen.
