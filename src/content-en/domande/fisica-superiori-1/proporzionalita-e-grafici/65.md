The table gives two reliable readings and a third one, at $x=9$, to check. The first two are exact and follow inverse proportion. What signed correction must be applied to the third $y$ value to give the same product?

| $x$ | $y$ |
| ---: | ---: |
| 2 | 18 |
| 3 | 12 |
| 9 | 5 |

- $-1$
- $1$
- $-4$
- $4$

## Explanation

The first products are $2\cdot18=3\cdot12=36$. At the third point, $y=\dfrac{36}{9}=4$ is required to preserve that product. The correction is the required value minus the reading, giving $4-5=-1$. A correction of four units would confuse the final value with its change.
