Horizontal values $2$, $4$, and $6$ give vertical values $8$, $16$, and $24$. What relation fits this table?

- The ratio $y/x$ always equals 4
- The product $xy$ always equals 4
- The slope is 8
- The differences of vertical values are negative

## Explanation

Each vertical value is four times its horizontal value. A rise of eight over two horizontal units confirms slope four.
