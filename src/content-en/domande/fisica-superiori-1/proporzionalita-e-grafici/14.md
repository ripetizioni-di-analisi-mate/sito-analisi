On a graph of mass in grams against volume in cubic centimetres, a line has numerical slope $8$. What is the unit of this slope?

- $\mathrm{cm^3/g}$
- $\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $\mathrm{g\,cm^3}$
- $\mathrm g$

## Explanation

Take differences of vertical values in grams and horizontal values in cubic centimetres. Dividing the first difference by the second gives the slope. The units undergo the same division. In this graph the ratio gives the increase in mass per unit volume.

The unit is $\dfrac{\mathrm g}{\mathrm{cm}^3}$.

A slope of eight means that along the line an increase in volume of $1\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ corresponds to $8\,\mathrm g$ of additional mass. Swapping the axes would give reciprocal units.
