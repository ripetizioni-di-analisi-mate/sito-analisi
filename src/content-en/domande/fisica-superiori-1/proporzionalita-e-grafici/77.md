The total mass of a container and liquid follows $m=50+2V$, with $m$ in grams and $V$ in cubic centimetres. A second empty container has $10\,\mathrm g$ less mass and holds the same liquid. What is the new equation?

- $m=40+2V$
- $m=50+12V$
- $m=40-2V$
- $m=60+2V$

## Explanation

Changing the container affects the starting mass. The slope, which describes mass added by the liquid, stays the same.
