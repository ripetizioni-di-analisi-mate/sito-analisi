Which line describes two directly proportional quantities on a domain that includes the origin?

- $y=0.5x$
- $y=0.5x+4$
- $y=4-0.5x$
- $y=4$

## Explanation

The direct function consists of one term, the horizontal value times a fixed coefficient. It therefore passes through the origin.
