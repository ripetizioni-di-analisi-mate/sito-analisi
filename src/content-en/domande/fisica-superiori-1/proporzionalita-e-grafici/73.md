A pump with constant flow fills two containers one after the other, without a pause. It puts $8\,\mathrm L$ into the first and $12\,\mathrm L$ into the second. The combined filling time is $15\,\mathrm{min}$. How long does the second container take to fill?

- $9\,\mathrm{min}$
- $6\,\mathrm{min}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{min}$
- $22.5\,\mathrm{min}$

## Explanation

At a constant flow rate, the share of the total time equals the share of the total volume delivered. The second container receives $\dfrac{12}{8+12}=\dfrac35$ of the volume, so it takes $\dfrac35\cdot15=9\,\mathrm{min}$. The ratio $\dfrac{12}{8}$ instead compares the second container's time with the first container's time.
