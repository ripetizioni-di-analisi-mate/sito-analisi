A vessel loses water at a constant rate. It contains $20\,\mathrm L$ at $2\,\mathrm{min}$ and $14\,\mathrm L$ at $5\,\mathrm{min}$. Suppose a second outlet draining $1\,\mathrm{L/min}$ were opened at $t=2\,\mathrm{min}$ and remained open throughout that interval. At what time would $11\,\mathrm L$ remain? Use the same clock.

- $3\,\mathrm{min}$
- $6.5\,\mathrm{min}$
- $5\,\mathrm{min}$
- $7\,\mathrm{min}$

## Explanation

Losing $6\,\mathrm L$ in three minutes gives an outflow of $2\,\mathrm{L/min}$. The second outlet makes the total loss $3\,\mathrm{L/min}$. Dropping from $20$ to $11\,\mathrm L$ takes $3\,\mathrm{min}$ after opening. The clock was already at two minutes, so the requested time is $5\,\mathrm{min}$.
