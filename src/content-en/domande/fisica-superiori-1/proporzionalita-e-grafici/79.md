A line has vertical intercept $-6$ and crosses the horizontal axis at $x=3$. Which rule follows?

- $y=-2x-6$
- $y=2x+6$
- $y=2x-6$
- $y=3x-6$

## Explanation

Starting at $-6$ requires an increase of six in three steps. The slope is two and the intercept is given.
