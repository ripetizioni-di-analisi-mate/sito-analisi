How far above $y=4x$ is the line $y=4x+7$ at the same horizontal coordinate?

- $4$
- $11$
- $7$
- $28$

## Explanation

Subtracting the models at the same horizontal coordinate cancels the $4x$ contribution. The difference of intercepts remains.
