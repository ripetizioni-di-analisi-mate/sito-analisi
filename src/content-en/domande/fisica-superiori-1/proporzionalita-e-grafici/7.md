A line passes through $(1;10)$ and $(4;43)$. What is its slope?

- $10.75$
- $33$
- $11$
- $\dfrac{1}{11}$

## Explanation

Slope is the increase in vertical value divided by the increase in horizontal value. Subtract the first vertical value from the second and keep the same order for the horizontal values. This describes the same move in both directions. A positive result describes an increasing line and gives its rise per horizontal unit.

The calculation gives $m=\dfrac{43-10}{4-1}=11$.
