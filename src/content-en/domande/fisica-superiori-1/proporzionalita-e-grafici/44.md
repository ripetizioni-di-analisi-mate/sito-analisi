Readings at $x=2$ and $x=6$ are $11$ and $19$. Assuming an affine model, what is the reading at their midpoint?

- $8$
- $13$
- $19$
- $15$

## Explanation

Half the vertical change has occurred halfway across the horizontal interval in an affine model. Take the mean of the two vertical values.

$\frac{11+19}{2}=15$.

The vertical gap is eight. Covering half the four horizontal units adds half that gap, namely four, to eleven.
