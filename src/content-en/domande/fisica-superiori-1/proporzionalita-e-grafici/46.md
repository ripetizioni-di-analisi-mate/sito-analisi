A starts with $8\,\mathrm L$, fills at $2\,\mathrm{L/min}$ and has capacity $12\,\mathrm L$. B starts empty, fills at $3\,\mathrm{L/min}$ and has capacity $24\,\mathrm L$. After capacity is reached, excess water flows out. When do the two volumes held first become equal?

- $2\,\mathrm{min}$
- $4\,\mathrm{min}$
- $8\,\mathrm{min}$
- $12\,\mathrm{min}$

## Explanation

A reaches capacity after $\dfrac{12-8}{2}\,\mathrm{min}=2\,\mathrm{min}$. At that time B holds $6\,\mathrm L$, so it is still behind. A then stays at $12\,\mathrm L$ while B reaches that volume after $\dfrac{12}{3}\,\mathrm{min}=4\,\mathrm{min}$. Intersecting the unrestricted lines would not describe the first actual equality.
