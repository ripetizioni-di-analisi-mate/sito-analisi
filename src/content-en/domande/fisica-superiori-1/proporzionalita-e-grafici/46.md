Two containers each receive $3\,\mathrm{L/min}$ before reaching capacity. A starts with four more litres than B. When do their volumes match in this model?

- After four minutes
- Never during the modelled interval
- After three minutes
- Immediately before filling starts

## Explanation

The two lines are parallel. Their identical increases leave a four-litre gap at every modelled time.
