A distance-versus-duration plot has slope $60\,\mathrm{m/min}$. What is the same slope with time in seconds?

- $60\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3600\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

One minute contains sixty seconds. The distance gained per second is one sixtieth of the amount per minute.

$\frac{60}{60}=1\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
