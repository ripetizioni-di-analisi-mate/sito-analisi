An affine reading passes through $(2;9)$ and $(5;18)$. What vertical value does it give at $x=0$?

- $9$
- $4.5$
- $18$
- $3$

## Explanation

Find slope from the differences between the two points. Subtract the first point's horizontal contribution from its vertical value.

$9-\frac{18-9}{5-2}\cdot 2=3$.
