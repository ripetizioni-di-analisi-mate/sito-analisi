The line $y=8x+q$ passes through the point $(2;26)$. What is its intercept $q$?

- $26$
- $18$
- $6$
- $10$

## Explanation

The given vertical value contains the contribution that grows with the horizontal coordinate and the fixed contribution. Moving the proportional part to the other side of the equation leaves only the intercept. The model uses it as the value at zero. Adding back the proportional part at the given coordinate checks it.

Substituting the point gives $q=(26)-8\cdot2=10$.
