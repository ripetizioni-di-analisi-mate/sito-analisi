A graph of the volume collected in litres against time in seconds is a straight line with slope $0.4\,\mathrm{L/s}$. If the axes are swapped and each quantity keeps its original unit, what is the new slope?

- $0.4\,\mathrm{s/L}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s/L}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{s/L}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{s/L}$

## Explanation

The original slope measures litres added per second. With time on the vertical axis, the slope measures seconds per litre added. The numerator and denominator swap, so $\dfrac{1}{0.4}=2.5\,\mathrm{s/L}$. A change of $1\,\mathrm L$ takes the time needed to collect two and a half increments of $0.4\,\mathrm L$.
