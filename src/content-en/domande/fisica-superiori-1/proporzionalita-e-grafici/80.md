A straight line plots volume in litres against time in seconds. What is its slope unit?

- $\mathrm{s/L}$
- $\mathrm{L\,s}$
- $\mathrm s$
- $\mathrm{L/s}$

## Explanation

Divide the vertical change by the horizontal change. The numerator and denominator units follow that order.
