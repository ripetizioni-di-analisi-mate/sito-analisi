A linear drain law predicts $V=10-5t$ litres. What is the first time it reaches the physical limit of an empty container?

- $t=5$
- $t=2$
- $t=10$
- $t=-2$

## Explanation

Physical volume can fall to zero. The line reaches zero after two time units and would predict negative volumes afterward.
