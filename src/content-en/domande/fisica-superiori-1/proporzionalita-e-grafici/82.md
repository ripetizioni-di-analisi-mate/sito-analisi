In the law $y=x^2$, the average slope from $x=2$ to $x=5$ equals the average slope from $x=1$ to another horizontal coordinate $b>1$. What is $b$?

- $4$
- $6$
- $5$
- $7$

## Explanation

The slope from $2$ to $5$ is $\dfrac{25-4}{5-2}=7$. From horizontal coordinate $1$ to coordinate $b$ it is $\dfrac{b^2-1}{b-1}=b+1$ because $b>1$. Hence $b+1=7$ and $b=6$. The intervals have different widths, but their endpoint coordinates have the same sum.
