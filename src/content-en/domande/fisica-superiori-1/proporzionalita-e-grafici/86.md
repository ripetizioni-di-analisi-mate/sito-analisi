A test measures $y=5$ at $x=0$. Which hypothesis cannot fit that exact observation?

- An affine relationship with intercept 5
- Direct proportionality $y=kx$
- A constant value of 5
- A curve $y=x^2+5$

## Explanation

In the direct model any coefficient times zero gives zero. The other models allow a vertical value of five at that coordinate.
