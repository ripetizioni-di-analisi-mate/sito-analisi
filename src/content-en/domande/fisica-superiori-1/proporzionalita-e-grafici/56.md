A square's side increases from $3$ to $4\,\mathrm{cm}$. How much does area grow?

- $1\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $16\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $2\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $7\,\mathrm{cm}^2$

## Explanation

Area is the square of the side length. Subtract the initial square from the final square.

$4^{2}-3^{2}=7\,\mathrm{cm}^2$.
