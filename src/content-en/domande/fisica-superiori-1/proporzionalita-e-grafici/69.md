An affine law predicts $y=3x+1$. Measurements at $x=2$ and $x=6$ have residuals $-2$ and $+2$, calculated as observed minus predicted. What is the slope of the line through the two measurements?

- $4$
- $2$
- $3$
- $5$

## Explanation

The predictions are $7$ and $19$. Adding the respective residuals gives observations of $5$ and $21$. The horizontal separation is four, so the slope is $\dfrac{21-5}{6-2}=4$. The second residual is larger than the first, which makes the slope steeper than the model's.
