A test's measurements cover times from 2 to 6 minutes. Which prediction would be extrapolation?

- A prediction at 9 minutes
- A prediction at 3 minutes
- A prediction at 4 minutes
- A prediction at 5 minutes

## Explanation

Nine minutes is beyond the observed range. Predicting there assumes the model stays valid beyond the measured interval.
