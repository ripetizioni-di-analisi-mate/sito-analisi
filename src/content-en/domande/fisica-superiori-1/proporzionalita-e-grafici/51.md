In $y=12+4x$, the horizontal value doubles from $x=3$ to $x=6$. By what factor does the vertical value change?

- $2$
- $4$
- $1.5$
- $0.5$

## Explanation

The proportional contribution doubles while the intercept remains twelve. The total values are thirty-six and twenty-four.

$\frac{12+4\cdot 6}{12+4\cdot 3}=1.5$.

The values are $24$ and $36$, giving a ratio of $\dfrac{36}{24}=1.5$. A zero intercept would instead make the vertical coordinate double with the horizontal one.
