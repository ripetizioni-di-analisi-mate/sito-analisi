Tank A holds $8$ litres and gains $2\,\mathrm{L/min}$. Tank B starts empty and gains $6\,\mathrm{L/min}$. When do they reach the same volume?

- $2\,\mathrm{min}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{min}$
- $4\,\mathrm{min}$
- $32\,\mathrm{min}$

## Explanation

The initial gap closes at B's flow rate minus A's. Divide eight by that difference in rates.

$\frac{8}{6-2}=2\,\mathrm{min}$.

Vessel B closes the initial eight-litre gap by four litres per minute. After two minutes A holds $8+2\cdot2=12\,\mathrm L$ and B holds $6\cdot2=12\,\mathrm L$.
