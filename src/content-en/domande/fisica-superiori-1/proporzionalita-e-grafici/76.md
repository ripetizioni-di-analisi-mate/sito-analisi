A pump delivers $3\,\mathrm{L/min}$ into a container and $1\,\mathrm{L/min}$ flows out through a hole. The container initially holds $5\,\mathrm L$. How much water does it hold after four minutes?

- $17\,\mathrm L$
- $21\,\mathrm L$
- $7\,\mathrm L$
- $13\,\mathrm L$

## Explanation

Each minute $3\,\mathrm L$ flow in and $1\,\mathrm L$ flows out, so the volume increases by $3-1=2\,\mathrm L$ per minute. The volume therefore depends on time through an affine relationship, with slope $2\,\mathrm{L/min}$ and initial value $5\,\mathrm L$.

$$
V(t)=5\,\mathrm L+2\,\mathrm{L/min}\cdot t .
$$

For $t=4\,\mathrm{min}$ this gives $V=5+2\cdot4=13\,\mathrm L$. The value $17\,\mathrm L$ comes from using only the pump's flow rate, without subtracting the flow through the hole.
