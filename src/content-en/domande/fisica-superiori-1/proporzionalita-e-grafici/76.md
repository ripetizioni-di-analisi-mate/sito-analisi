A sensor follows the law $y=2x$ and displays $y$ rounded to the nearest integer. It shows $7$. Which value of $x$ is consistent with the reading?

- $3.1$
- $3.9$
- $7.0$
- $3.6$

## Explanation

For $x=3.6$, the unrounded value is $7.2$, which rounds to $7$. The other inputs produce $6.2$, $7.8$ and $14$, respectively.
