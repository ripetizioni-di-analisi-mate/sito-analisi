A pump supplies $3\,\mathrm{L/min}$ and a leak lets $1\,\mathrm{L/min}$ out. Starting with $5\,\mathrm L$, how much is in the container after four minutes?

- $17\,\mathrm L$
- $21\,\mathrm L$
- $7\,\mathrm L$
- $13\,\mathrm L$

## Explanation

The effective slope is inflow minus outflow. Apply it for four minutes and add the starting volume.

$5+\left(3-1\right)\cdot 4=13\,\mathrm L$.
