A test spring follows $F=kx$ with $k=90\,\mathrm{N/m}$. What extension does a force $18\,\mathrm N$ produce?

- $5\,\mathrm m$
- $1.8\,\mathrm m$
- $0.2\,\mathrm m$
- $0.02\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The spring law is supplied for its proportional range, with coefficient force per unit length. Extension is the quantity multiplied by that coefficient under these conditions. Divide the force by the coefficient to isolate it; metres remain when the units cancel. Substituting the extension recovers the stated force.

It follows that $x=\dfrac{F}{k}=\dfrac{18}{90}=0.2\,\mathrm m$.
