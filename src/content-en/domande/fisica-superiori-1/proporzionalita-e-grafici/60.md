A side changes from $l$ to $1.10l$. What percentage increase occurs in the square's area?

- $10\,\%$
- $20\,\%$
- $121\,\%$
- $21\,\%$

## Explanation

The area factor is $1.10$ squared, or $1.21$. Subtract the original part before expressing the increase as a percentage.

$1.10^2-1=0.21=21\,\%$.
