The side of a square goes from $l$ to $1.25l$. By what percentage does its area increase?

- $25\,\%$
- $50\,\%$
- $156.25\,\%$
- $56.25\,\%$

## Explanation

The factor $1.25$ applies to both dimensions. The square of the new side divided by the square of the old side gives $1.25^2=1.5625$. The new area is $156.25\,\%$ of the initial area. Subtract the original $100\,\%$ to get the increase of $56.25\,\%$.
