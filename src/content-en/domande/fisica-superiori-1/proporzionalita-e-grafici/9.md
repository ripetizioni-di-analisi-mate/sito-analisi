A container initially has $11\,\mathrm L$ and takes in $9\,\mathrm{L/min}$. How much liquid does it contain after $3\,\mathrm{min}$, with no losses?

- $38\,\mathrm L$
- $27\,\mathrm L$
- $20\,\mathrm L$
- $60\,\mathrm L$

## Explanation

The original volume is the constant term in the model. The product of flow rate and time gives only the added liquid. Add the two quantities because both are volumes and there are no losses. The result exceeds the initial amount because the incoming flow rate is positive.

The result is $V=11+9\cdot3=38\,\mathrm L$.

In three minutes $27\,\mathrm L$ are added to the initial eleven. The total volume divided by time is not the flow rate because it includes those initial litres.
