Which law is higher for $t>3$, if $V_A=9+t$ and $V_B=3+3t$?

- $V_A$
- They are equal for all $t$
- $V_B$
- They alternate every three time units

## Explanation

The lines meet at $t=3$. Afterward B grows faster because its slope is three rather than one.
