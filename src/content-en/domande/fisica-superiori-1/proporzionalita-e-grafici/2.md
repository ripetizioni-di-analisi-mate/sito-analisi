Collected liquid volume follows $V=qt$ at a constant flow rate $q=8\,\mathrm{L/min}$. What volume is collected in $10\,\mathrm{min}$?

- $1.25\,\mathrm L$
- $80\,\mathrm L$
- $18\,\mathrm L$
- $0.8\,\mathrm L$

## Explanation

Flow rate gives the number of litres collected each minute and stays constant. To find the total, take the same contribution for every minute, so multiply. The time in minutes cancels the minutes in the denominator of the rate. The origin represents no time elapsed and no volume collected.

The law gives $V=8\cdot10=80\,\mathrm L$.

Multiplying $\mathrm{L/min}$ by $\mathrm{min}$ cancels the minutes, leaving litres. The model counts the volume collected over the interval independently of any initial volume already present.
