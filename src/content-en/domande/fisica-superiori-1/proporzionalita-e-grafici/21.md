A vertical line in the $(x;y)$ plane passes through two points with different vertical coordinates. Which coordinate is shared?

- The horizontal coordinate $x$
- The vertical coordinate $y$
- Both coordinates
- The vertical slope expressed as zero

## Explanation

Moving vertically keeps the horizontal position fixed. The value of $x$ is the same for both points.

For example, $(2;4)$ and $(2;7)$ lie on the same vertical line. Only the height read on the vertical axis changes between them.
