A measurement is $(7.8\pm0.3)\,\mathrm N$. The model predicts $8.0\,\mathrm N$. What does the interval alone show?

- The prediction exceeds the maximum
- The prediction is below the minimum
- The model is proven for every observation
- The prediction lies within the interval

## Explanation

The interval runs from $7.5$ to $8.1\,\mathrm N$. Eight lies between these endpoints for this observation.

The distance between the central reading and the prediction is $0.2\,\mathrm N$, less than the margin of $0.3\,\mathrm N$. This reading alone is compatible with the prediction.
