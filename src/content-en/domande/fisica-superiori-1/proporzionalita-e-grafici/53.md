A scale measures the increase $s$ in the side length of a square from an initial side of $2\,\mathrm{cm}$. The area $A$ is plotted vertically. Lengths are given in centimetres. Which new horizontal variable is directly proportional to the area?

- $u=(s+2)^2$
- $u=s^2$
- $u=s+2$
- $u=s^2+4$

## Explanation

The area uses the full side length, so $A=(s+2)^2$. With $u=(s+2)^2$, the rule becomes $A=u$. Choosing $u=s^2+4$ would omit the $4s$ term that appears when the square is expanded. That choice gives the right area at $s=0$, whereas for $s>0$ it underestimates the area.
