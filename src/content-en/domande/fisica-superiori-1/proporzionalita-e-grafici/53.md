For $y=3x^2$, by what factor does halving positive $x$ multiply $y$?

- $0.25$
- $0.5$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

The factor one half occurs in both factors in the square. The coefficient three is unchanged.

$\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{2}=0.25$.
