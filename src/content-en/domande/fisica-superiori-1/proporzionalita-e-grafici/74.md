In $Ah=c$, area is in square centimetres and thickness in centimetres. What unit does $c$ have?

- $\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $\mathrm{cm}^3$
- $\mathrm{cm}$
- $\mathrm{cm}^{-1}$

## Explanation

The constant is area times thickness. Square centimetres times centimetres gives cubic centimetres, a volume unit.
