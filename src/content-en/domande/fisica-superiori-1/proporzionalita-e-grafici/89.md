For the same input $x$, two sensors report $y_A=2x+4$ and $y_B=3x+1$. If only reading $y_A$ is known, which expression gives reading $y_B$?

- $\dfrac32y_A-5$
- $\dfrac23y_A-5$
- $\dfrac32y_A+1$
- $\dfrac32y_A+5$

## Explanation

The first rule gives $x=\dfrac{y_A-4}{2}$. Substitution into the second gives $y_B=\dfrac32(y_A-4)+1=\dfrac32y_A-5$. The slope ratio determines the coefficient of $y_A$. To check the intercepts, use $x=0$, where reading $y_A=4$ must correspond to $y_B=1$.
