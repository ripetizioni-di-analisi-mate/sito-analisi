Two quantities $y_A$ and $y_B$ depend on the same quantity $x$ according to the laws $y_A=2x+4$ and $y_B=3x+1$. Which expression gives $y_B$ as a function of $y_A$?

- $\dfrac32y_A-5$
- $\dfrac23y_A-5$
- $\dfrac32y_A+1$
- $\dfrac32y_A+5$

## Explanation

From the first law, $x$ is obtained as a function of $y_A$, namely $x=\dfrac{y_A-4}{2}$. Substituting this expression into the second law gives
$$
y_B=3\cdot\frac{y_A-4}{2}+1=\frac32y_A-6+1=\frac32y_A-5.
$$
The coefficient of $y_A$ is therefore the ratio of the slopes of the two laws, $\dfrac32$.
