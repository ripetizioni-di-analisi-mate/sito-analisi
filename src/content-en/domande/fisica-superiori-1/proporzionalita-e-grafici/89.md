Two pricing plans are $y_A=4+2x$ and $y_B=1+3x$. At which $x$ are their costs equal?

- $3$
- $1$
- $4$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

A's starting cost exceeds B's by three. A saves one per unit, so after three units the two effects balance.

$\frac{4-1}{3-2}=3$.
