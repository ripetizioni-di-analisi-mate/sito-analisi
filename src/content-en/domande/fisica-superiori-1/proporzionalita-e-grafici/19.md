A mass-versus-volume table and a plot of volume against mass use the same observations. How is $V=4\,\mathrm{cm}^3$, $m=12\,\mathrm g$ plotted with mass horizontal?

- $(4;12)$
- $(12;12)$
- $(12;4)$
- $(4;4)$

## Explanation

The horizontal coordinate is now mass and the vertical one is volume. The same observation therefore swaps coordinates.
