The model $y=3x+5$ is measured from a new horizontal origin, with $u=x-2$. What is the new intercept at $u=0$?

- $11$
- $5$
- $-1$
- $3$

## Explanation

The new origin $u=0$ corresponds to $x=2$. The line's value there is the new intercept.

$3\cdot 2+5=11$.

In terms of $u$, the law becomes $y=3(u+2)+5=3u+11$. Translation changes the intercept because it shifts the point from which the horizontal coordinate is measured.
