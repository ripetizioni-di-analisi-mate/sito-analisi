With $xy$ constant, $x$ rises by $25\%$. By what percentage does $y$ fall?

- $20\,\%$
- $25\,\%$
- $80\,\%$
- $125\,\%$

## Explanation

The new $x$ is $1.25$ times its previous value. Dividing the old $y$ by that factor gives eighty per cent.

$1-\frac{1}{1.25}=0.2=20\,\%$.
