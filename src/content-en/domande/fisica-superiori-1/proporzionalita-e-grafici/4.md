Two positive quantities are inversely proportional. When $x=8$, an observation gives $y=10$. What is the constant $c$ in the rule $y=\dfrac{c}{x}$?

- $1.25$
- $0.8$
- $18$
- $80$

## Explanation

The product is constant when two positive quantities are inversely proportional. Multiply the first and second coordinates to find that product. The factors change for other pairs but must give the same value. Their ratio would instead characterize a direct-proportionality prediction.

Therefore $c=xy=8\cdot10=80$.
