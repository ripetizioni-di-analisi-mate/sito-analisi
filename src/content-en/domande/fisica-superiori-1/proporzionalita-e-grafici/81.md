Two graphs with different vertical origins show the same final reading. Which quantity should be compared to check equality?

- The vertical value read on the scales
- The number of squares from the page edge
- The angle on the page
- A distance in centimetres from the page centre

## Explanation

Different origins shift positions on the page. The labels give the physical value without using an absolute paper position.
