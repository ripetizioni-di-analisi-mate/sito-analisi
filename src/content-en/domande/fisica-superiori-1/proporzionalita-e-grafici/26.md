In a positive direct relationship, $x$ falls from $20$ to $15$ and $y$ was $60$. What is the new $y$?

- $80$
- $45$
- $55$
- $900$

## Explanation

The new-to-old horizontal ratio is three quarters. In a direct law, the same factor multiplies the vertical value.

$\frac{60\cdot 15}{20}=45$.

The new horizontal coordinate is $\dfrac{15}{20}=\dfrac34$ of the old one. The same factor takes sixty to forty-five. The decrease in the vertical coordinate is fifteen, rather than the five-unit decrease in the horizontal one.
