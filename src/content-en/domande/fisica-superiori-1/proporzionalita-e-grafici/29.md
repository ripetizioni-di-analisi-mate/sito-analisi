A table for direct proportionality gives $(x;y)=(7;21)$. How much does $y$ increase if $x$ increases by two units?

- $6$
- $3$
- $42$
- $23$

## Explanation

The pair gives a slope of three. A two-unit increase gives twice that slope as the vertical change.

$\frac{21}{7}\cdot 2=6$.
