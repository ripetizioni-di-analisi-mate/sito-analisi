A horizontal scale goes from $10$ to $30\,\mathrm s$ over five equal squares. What time is three squares past the first mark?

- $12\,\mathrm s$
- $28\,\mathrm s$
- $18\,\mathrm s$
- $22\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The scale starts at ten seconds. Add three times the value of one square, found from the full span.

$10+\frac{3\cdot \left(30-10\right)}{5}=22\,\mathrm s$.
