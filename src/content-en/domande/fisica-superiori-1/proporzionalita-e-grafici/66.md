Exact data $(2;12)$, $(3;8)$, and $(6;4)$ suggest which law?

- $y=6x$
- $y=24/x$
- $y=12-2x$
- $y=24/x^2$

## Explanation

Each pair's product is twenty-four. This is the fixed numerator of the inverse law that fits the data.
