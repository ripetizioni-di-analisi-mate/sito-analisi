In $xy=c$, switch $x$ from metres to centimetres and keep $y$ in the same unit. What factor changes the numerical value of $c$?

- $0.01$
- $100$
- $1$
- $10000$

## Explanation

The value of $x$ is multiplied by a hundred and $y$ stays fixed. Since $c$ is the product, the factor enters once.

With the new variable $u=100x$, we have $uy=100xy=100c$. The physical quantity can stay the same because the unit used to express the product changes.
