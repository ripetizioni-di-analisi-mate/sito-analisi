The model $y=4x^2$ uses metres for $x$ and litres for $y$. What is the unit of the coefficient four?

- $\mathrm{L/m}$
- $\mathrm{m^2/L}$
- $\mathrm{L\,m^2}$
- $\mathrm{L/m^2}$

## Explanation

Divide the vertical quantity by the square of the horizontal quantity. The area unit therefore goes in the denominator.
