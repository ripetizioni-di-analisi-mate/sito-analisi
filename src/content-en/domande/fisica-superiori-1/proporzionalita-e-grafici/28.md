A direct reading has coefficient $k=2\,\mathrm{N/cm}$. Which statement interprets that coefficient?

- Force at zero is 2 N
- Two centimetres always correspond to 1 N
- Force doubles every centimetre
- Force rises by 2 N for each centimetre horizontally

## Explanation

Slope is vertical change divided by the corresponding horizontal change. Its value is two newtons per centimetre.
