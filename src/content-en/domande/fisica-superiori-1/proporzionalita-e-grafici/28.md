A pump delivers $0.18\,\mathrm{L/s}$. On a graph of the delivered volume in millilitres against time in minutes, what is the slope?

- $180\,\mathrm{mL/min}$
- $3\,\mathrm{mL/min}$
- $10.8\,\mathrm{mL/min}$
- $10800\,\mathrm{mL/min}$

## Explanation

Since $0.18\,\mathrm L=180\,\mathrm{mL}$, the rate is $180\,\mathrm{mL/s}$. One minute contains $60\,\mathrm s$, so it collects $180\cdot60=10800\,\mathrm{mL}$. The coefficient read from the new line is $10800\,\mathrm{mL/min}$. The number $10.8$ would express the same flow rate with litres in the numerator.
