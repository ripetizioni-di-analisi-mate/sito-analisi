Three observation-minus-prediction residuals are $-1$, $-1$, and $-1\,\mathrm L$. What constant adjustment to the model cancels all three?

- Add one litre to every prediction
- Multiply the predictions by minus one
- Subtract three litres from every prediction
- Subtract one litre from every prediction

## Explanation

Every observation is one litre below prediction. Lowering each prediction by one litre makes it match the data.

A negative residual means the prediction is too high. Lowering every prediction by one litre gives residual $-1-(-1)=0$ at all three points.
