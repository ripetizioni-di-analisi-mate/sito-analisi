A fixed route takes $12$ minutes at mean speed $3\,\mathrm{km/h}$. What speed is needed to reduce the time to $9$ minutes?

- $4\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $2.25\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $9\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{km/h}$

## Explanation

Distance is speed times duration. Keeping the product fixed multiplies speed by the ratio of the old time to the new time.

$\frac{3\cdot 12}{9}=4\,\mathrm{km/h}$.
