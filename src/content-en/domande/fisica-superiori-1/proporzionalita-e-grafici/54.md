A law $y=2x^2$ is used for $x\geq0$. Which $x$ gives $y=18$?

- $9$
- $3$
- $36$
- $6$

## Explanation

Dividing by two gives the horizontal coordinate squared, nine. Take the nonnegative root because of the stated domain.

The algebraic solution $-3$ has the same square but is excluded by $x\geq0$. The positive value checks out because $2\cdot3^2=18$.
