If $y$ is directly proportional to $x$ with positive coefficient, what holds when $x=0$ is in the domain?

- $y=1$
- $y=k$
- $y=0$
- $y$ is undefined

## Explanation

The model multiplies the horizontal value by a fixed coefficient. Multiplying zero gives zero.
