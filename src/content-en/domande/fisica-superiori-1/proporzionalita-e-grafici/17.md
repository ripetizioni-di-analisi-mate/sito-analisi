The same relation is drawn first with $x$ in metres and then with $x$ in centimetres. The vertical unit stays fixed. By what factor is the numerical slope multiplied?

- $0.01$
- $100$
- $1$
- $0.1$

## Explanation

The numerical horizontal difference becomes one hundred times larger. Divide the ratio with the same vertical difference by one hundred.

$\frac{1}{100}=0.01$.

For example, ten newtons over two metres give five newtons per metre. The same ten newtons over two hundred centimetres give a numerical value one hundredth as large.
