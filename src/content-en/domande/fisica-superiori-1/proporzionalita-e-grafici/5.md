The law is $y=\dfrac{48}x$, with $x>0$. What is $y$ when $x=3$?

- $16$
- $144$
- $45$
- $0.0625$

## Explanation

In the inverse law the new horizontal value goes in the denominator. The numerator is the constant product preserved for every allowed pair. Divide it by the given horizontal value. Multiplying the result by the horizontal value recovers the constant product and checks the calculation.

The calculation gives $y=\dfrac{48}3=16$.

The check is $3\cdot16=48$. At a horizontal coordinate greater than three, dividing forty-eight by a larger number would give a smaller output.
