A quantity follows $y=c/x^2$ with $c>0$ and positive $x$. If $x$ is multiplied by $8$, what is the ratio $y_2/y_1$?

- $0.015625$
- $0.125$
- $8$
- $64$

## Explanation

The new horizontal value is squared in the denominator. The denominator is multiplied by the square of the factor, while the numerator is unchanged. The new vertical value is the previous one divided by that square. The ratio is less than one because the positive horizontal value increased.

Therefore $\dfrac{y_2}{y_1}=\dfrac1{8^2}=0.015625$.

The new value is $\dfrac{1}{64}$ of the original. The decimal is a dimensionless ratio. Multiply this fraction by the initial value of $y$ to obtain the final value.
