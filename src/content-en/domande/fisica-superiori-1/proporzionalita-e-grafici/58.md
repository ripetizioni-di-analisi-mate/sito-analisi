The law $y=k/x^2$ gives $y=16$ at $x=1$. What does it predict at $x=4$?

- $4$
- $1$
- $64$
- $256$

## Explanation

The horizontal value is squared in the denominator. Four times the horizontal value makes the denominator sixteen times greater.

$\frac{16}{4^{2}}=1$.
