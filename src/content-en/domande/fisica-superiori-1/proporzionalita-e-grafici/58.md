A probe follows the rule $y=\dfrac{36}{x^2}$. The input lies in $2\leq x\leq3$. What are the smallest and largest possible values of $y$, in that order?

- $12$ and $18$
- $4$ and $9$
- $6$ and $9$
- $144$ and $324$

## Explanation

For positive $x$, the squared denominator grows with $x$, so $y$ falls. At the right endpoint, $\dfrac{36}{3^2}=4$ is the minimum. At the left endpoint, $\dfrac{36}{2^2}=9$ is the maximum. While the values of $x$ increase from left to right, the values of $y$ decrease.
