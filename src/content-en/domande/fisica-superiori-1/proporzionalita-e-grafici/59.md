For an inverse-square model, $y$ must fall to one ninth of its initial value. By what factor is positive $x$ multiplied?

- $9$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$

## Explanation

The denominator must become nine times larger. Since it contains the square of the horizontal value, triple that value.

With $x_2=3x_1$ we get $x_2^2=9x_1^2$. In the inverse law this ninefold increase in the denominator reduces the vertical value to one ninth.
