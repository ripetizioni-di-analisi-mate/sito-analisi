Moving from $x=1$ to $x=5$ raises a reading from $4$ to $20$. Under an affine law, how many one-unit steps give a total increase of 12?

- $0.75$
- $3$
- $48$
- $0.6$

## Explanation

The slope is sixteen divided by four. Divide the target increase by the increase per step.

$\frac{12}{\frac{20-4}{5-1}}=3$.
