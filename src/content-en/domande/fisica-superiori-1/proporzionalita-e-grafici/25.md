Two direct laws are $y_A=3x$ and $y_B=5x$. What is $y_B-y_A$ at $x=4$?

- $8$
- $2$
- $20$
- $32$

## Explanation

At the same horizontal coordinate, the values differ by that coordinate times the difference of their coefficients.

$\left(5-3\right)\cdot 4=8$.
