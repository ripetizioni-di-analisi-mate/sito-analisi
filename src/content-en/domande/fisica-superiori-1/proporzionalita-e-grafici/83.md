The graph of $y=cx^2$ is replotted after converting metres to centimetres on the $x$ axis. What factor changes the numerical value of $c$?

- $0.01$
- $100$
- $0.0001$
- $10000$

## Explanation

The new horizontal value is one hundred times larger. Its square is ten thousand times larger, so divide the numerical coefficient by ten thousand.

$\frac{1}{100^{2}}=0.0001$.

With the new coordinate $u=100x$, write $y=c(\dfrac{u}{100})^2$. The two conversion factors inside the square give a divisor of $10000$ for the numerical coefficient.
