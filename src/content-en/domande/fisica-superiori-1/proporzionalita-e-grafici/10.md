A graph is given by $y=80-8x$. At what value of $x$ does it meet the horizontal axis?

- $80$
- $10$
- $18$
- $-10$

## Explanation

The vertical coordinate vanishes on the horizontal axis. Set the expression for that coordinate to zero. The constant term is positive and the slope is negative, so a positive horizontal coordinate is needed to offset the initial term. Substitution into the expression gives a zero vertical value.

Setting $0=80-8x$ gives $x=10$.

Ten cancels the constant term because $80-8\cdot10=0$. The point is $(10;0)$. The pair $(0;80)$ instead represents the vertical-axis intersection.
