A fixed amount of liquid forms layers with minimum allowed depth $0.5\,\mathrm{cm}$. At $30\,\mathrm{cm}^3$, what is the maximum area?

- $15\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $\dfrac{1}{60}\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $29.5\,\mathrm{cm}^2$
- $60\,\mathrm{cm}^2$

## Explanation

At fixed volume, area increases as thickness decreases. The maximum uses the permitted minimum thickness.

$\frac{30}{0.5}=60\,\mathrm{cm}^2$.

The ratio $\dfrac{30}{0.5}$ has units of area because it divides cubic centimetres by centimetres. A larger area would require thickness below the permitted limit.
