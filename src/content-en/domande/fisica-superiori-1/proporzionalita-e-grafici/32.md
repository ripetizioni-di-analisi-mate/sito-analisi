A total time of $90\,\mathrm s$ is split into equal intervals with no gaps. If their number goes from 3 to 9, by what factor does each duration change?

- $3$
- $6$
- $10$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

The number of intervals times their duration is fixed. Tripling the count makes each interval one third as long.

$\frac{3}{9}=\dfrac{1}{3}$.
