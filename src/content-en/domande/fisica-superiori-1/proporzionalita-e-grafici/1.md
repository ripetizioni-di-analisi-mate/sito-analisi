Two quantities follow $y=cx$. A measurement gives $x=8$ and $y=80$. What is the value of $c$?

- $10$
- $640$
- $0.1$
- $72$

## Explanation

In the direct model, the vertical value is the horizontal value times a coefficient. The given nonzero horizontal value allows that coefficient to be found by division. The ratio measures the vertical increase for a single unit on the horizontal axis. Using it in the law recovers the observed pair.

The calculation gives $c=\dfrac{y}{x}=\dfrac{80}{8}=10$.

Substituting $c=10$ gives $y=10\cdot8=80$. That product is the check. Multiplying eighty by eight instead would treat the observations as an inverse proportion.
