To spread $48\,\mathrm{cm}^3$ of material, reduce its base from $12$ to $8\,\mathrm{cm}^2$. What increase in thickness is required?

- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Find both thicknesses using volume divided by area. The thickness at the smaller area is the greater one.

$\frac{48}{8}-\frac{48}{12}=2\,\mathrm{cm}$.

The two thicknesses are $4\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $6\,\mathrm{cm}$, since $12\cdot4=8\cdot6=48\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. The requested increase is their difference of two centimetres.
