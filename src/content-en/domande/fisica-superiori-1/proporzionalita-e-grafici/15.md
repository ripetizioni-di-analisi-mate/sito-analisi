In a plot of volume against time, which coordinate gives volume at three minutes?

- The horizontal coordinate at vertical coordinate 3
- The slope of the segment from the origin
- The vertical coordinate at horizontal coordinate 3
- A distance in paper centimetres

## Explanation

Time is the horizontal variable and volume the vertical one. Fix the time and read the vertical coordinate.

Start at three on the time axis, move vertically until reaching the curve, and read the corresponding value on the volume axis.
