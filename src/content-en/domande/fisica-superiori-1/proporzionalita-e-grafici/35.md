A positive table contains $(1;8)$, $(2;4)$, and $(4;3)$. What does the product of the coordinates tell you?

- All pairs have product 8
- The first pair has product 9
- Only the first two pairs fit the inverse model with constant 8
- All pairs are directly proportional with coefficient 8

## Explanation

The first two products are eight. The third pair gives twelve, distinguishing the table from the inverse model.

If the first two readings are reliable, at $x=4$ the inverse model predicts $y=2$. The discrepancy is vertical because the observed value is three.
