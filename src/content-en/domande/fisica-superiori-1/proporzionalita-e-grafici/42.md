A numerical signal passes through two devices. A doubles the input and B adds three units. For the same input, how much larger is the output for B followed by A than for A followed by B?

- $6$
- $3$
- $0$
- It depends on the input.

## Explanation

For input $x$, B followed by A gives $2(x+3)=2x+6$. Reversing the order gives $2x+3$. Their difference is $3$ for any $x$.
