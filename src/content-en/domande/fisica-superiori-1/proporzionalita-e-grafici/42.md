A line passes through $(2;10)$ and $(5;4)$. What is its slope when moving from the second point to the first?

- $2$
- $-2$
- $-0.5$
- $5$

## Explanation

Reversing the direction changes the signs of both differences. Their ratio remains negative and unchanged.

$\frac{10-4}{2-5}=-2$.
