A tap delivers $24\,\mathrm L$ in eight minutes at a constant rate. How many minutes does it take to go from $9$ to $24\,\mathrm L$ at the same rate?

- $8\,\mathrm{min}$
- $3\,\mathrm{min}$
- $5\,\mathrm{min}$
- $15\,\mathrm{min}$

## Explanation

The flow rate is $24$ divided by eight. Divide only the required increase in volume by that rate.

$\frac{24-9}{\frac{24}{8}}=5\,\mathrm{min}$.
