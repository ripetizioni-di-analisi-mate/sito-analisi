Which observation at $x=3$ distinguishes $y=12-2x$ from $y=16/x$, which already agree at $x=2$ and $x=4$?

- $y=6$ fits only the second
- $y=8$ fits both
- $y=6$ fits only the first
- $y=4$ fits both

## Explanation

The affine model gives $12-2\cdot3=6$. The inverse model gives $\dfrac{16}{3}$, a different value.

The value $6$ differs from $\dfrac{16}{3}$ by $\dfrac23$. Multiplying six by the new horizontal coordinate three gives eighteen, rather than sixteen.
