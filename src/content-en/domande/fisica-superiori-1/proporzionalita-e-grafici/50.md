A correction subtracts $2\,\mathrm N$ from every vertical reading on a force-versus-length line. What happens to the slope?

- It decreases by two units
- It remains the same
- It increases by two units
- It becomes zero

## Explanation

The correction appears in both vertical values used for a difference. It cancels in the slope's numerator.

The line shifts down by two newtons at every point. Neither the vertical separation of any two readings nor the horizontal interval changes, so the ratio of differences is identical.
