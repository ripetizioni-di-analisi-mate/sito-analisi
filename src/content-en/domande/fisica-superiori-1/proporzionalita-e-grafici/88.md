Volume first grows four litres per minute and then stays fixed. Which sequence of slopes represents the two phases?

- $0$ then $4\,\mathrm{L/min}$
- $4$ then $-4\,\mathrm{L/min}$
- $4$ then $4\,\mathrm{L/min}$
- $4$ then $0\,\mathrm{L/min}$

## Explanation

During filling, volume increases by four per minute. In the constant section the vertical change over any interval is zero.

The first segment rises because the volume changes by four litres each minute. Once the volume stops changing, an additional minute produces zero increase as well.
