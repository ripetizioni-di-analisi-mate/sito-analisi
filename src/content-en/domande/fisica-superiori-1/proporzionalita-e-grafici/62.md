Measurements follow $y=k/x^2$. Which change makes $y$ directly proportional to the new horizontal coordinate for $x>0$?

- $u=x^2$
- $u=1/x^2$
- $u=x$
- $u=1/x$

## Explanation

The new variable must include the whole factor multiplying $k$. Choosing the reciprocal square gives $y=ku$.
