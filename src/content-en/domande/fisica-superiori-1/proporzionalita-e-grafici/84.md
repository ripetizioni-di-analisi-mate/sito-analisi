For $y=x^2$ and $x\geq0$, what average slope runs from $x=1$ to $x=4$?

- $9$
- $4$
- $15$
- $5$

## Explanation

Find both vertical values by squaring the horizontal values. Divide the vertical difference by the horizontal separation.

$\frac{4^{2}-1^{2}}{4-1}=5$.

The ratio is $\dfrac{16-1}{4-1}=\dfrac{15}{3}=5$. This is the slope of the secant over the whole interval, rather than a coefficient shared by all segments of the parabola.
