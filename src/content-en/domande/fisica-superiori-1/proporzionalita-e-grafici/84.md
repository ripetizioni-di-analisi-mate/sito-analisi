For $y=x^2$ and $x\geq0$, what average slope runs from $x=1$ to $x=4$?

- $9$
- $4$
- $15$
- $5$

## Explanation

Find both vertical values by squaring the horizontal values. Divide the vertical difference by the horizontal separation.

$\frac{4^{2}-1^{2}}{4-1}=5$.
