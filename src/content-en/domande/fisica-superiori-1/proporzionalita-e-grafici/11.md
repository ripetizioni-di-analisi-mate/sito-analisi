A table contains the pairs $(1;8), (2;16), (3;24)$. Which model describes all of the data?

- $y=8\div x$
- $y=x+8$
- $y=8x$
- $y=8x^2$

## Explanation

For direct proportionality, every vertical value must be its horizontal value times the same number. Divide each vertical value by its own horizontal value to obtain the same coefficient. The resulting law passes through each listed pair, and the origin belongs to its model line.

All ratios are $8$, so the points satisfy $y=8x$.

Dividing the vertical coordinates by their corresponding horizontal coordinates gives $\dfrac81=\dfrac{16}{2}=\dfrac{24}{3}$. The readings share the same coefficient, and the resulting law reproduces all three.
