A constant-product model relates positive $x$ and $y$. If $x$ is halved, what happens to $y$?

- It halves
- It doubles
- It quadruples
- It stays the same

## Explanation

Halving one factor requires doubling the other for their product to stay the same.

If $x$ becomes $\dfrac{x}{2}$, the product must stay at $xy$. Multiplying the other factor by two is what cancels the division applied to the first.
