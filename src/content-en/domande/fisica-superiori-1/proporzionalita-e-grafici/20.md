A curve falls three squares while moving two squares right. Each square is $5\,\mathrm N$ vertically and $1.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ horizontally. What average slope follows?

- $5\,\mathrm{N/cm}$
- $-1.5\,\mathrm{N/cm}$
- $-0.45\,\mathrm{N/cm}$
- $-5\,\mathrm{N/cm}$

## Explanation

The vertical change is negative, minus fifteen newtons. The horizontal change is positive, three centimetres.

$\frac{-3\cdot 5}{2\cdot 1.5}=-5\,\mathrm{N/cm}$.

The vertical change is $-3\cdot5=-15\,\mathrm N$. The horizontal change is $2\cdot1.5=3\,\mathrm{cm}$. The signed ratio describes a decrease of five newtons per centimetre over the interval.
