A model gives $10\,\mathrm L$ and the observation is $10.4\,\mathrm L$. What is observation minus prediction?

- $-0.4\,\mathrm L$
- $20.4\,\mathrm L$
- $0.4\,\mathrm L$
- $1.04\,\mathrm L$

## Explanation

The residual measures the difference in the vertical unit. It is positive because the observation exceeds the prediction.

$10.4-10=0.4\,\mathrm L$.
