A variable $y$ follows $y=cx^2$. At $x=2$, its value is $12$. What value does it take at $x=3$?

- $18$
- $108$
- $27$
- $17$

## Explanation

Find $c$ by dividing twelve by two squared. Multiply that same coefficient by three squared.

$\frac{12}{2^{2}}\cdot 3^{2}=27$.

The ratio of the horizontal coordinates is $\dfrac32$, so the vertical value is multiplied by $\dfrac94$. Applied to twelve, this factor gives twenty-seven.
