A quantity follows $y=cx^2$ with $c>0$ and positive initial $x$. If $x$ becomes $8$ times its initial value, by what factor does $y$ increase?

- $8$
- $16$
- $1$
- $64$

## Explanation

The horizontal quantity is squared. Substituting its new value gives two copies of the multiplying factor in the square. The law's coefficient is the same before and after, so the ratio of vertical values is the square of the given factor.

Indeed $y_2=c(8x_1)^2=64y_1$.
