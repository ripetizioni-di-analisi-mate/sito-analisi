The graph shows the volume in a vessel with no leaks. The pump is off during the horizontal segment. What is the pump's average flow rate over the intervals when it is on?

::grafico{segmenti="0,3,2,11;2,11,4,11;4,11,6,15" punti="0,3,pieno;2,11,pieno;4,11,pieno;6,15,pieno" xmin=0 xmax=7 ymin=0 ymax=18 nomi="t (min),V (L)" etichette="0.4,2.7,3 L;2.7,12.2,11 L;5.88,16.25,15 L" titolo="There is one rise before the pause and another after the pump restarts."}

- $3\,\mathrm{L/min}$
- $2\,\mathrm{L/min}$
- $4\,\mathrm{L/min}$
- $6\,\mathrm{L/min}$

## Explanation

The first two minutes add $11-3=8\,\mathrm L$ and the final two add $15-11=4\,\mathrm L$. The pump runs for four minutes and supplies $12\,\mathrm L$ in all, so the requested average is $\dfrac{12}{4}\,\mathrm{L/min}=3\,\mathrm{L/min}$. Including the pause in the denominator would give the average over the entire experiment.
