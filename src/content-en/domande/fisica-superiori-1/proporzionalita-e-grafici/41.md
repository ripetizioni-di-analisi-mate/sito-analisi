A container starts with $12\,\mathrm L$ and loses two litres per minute. When does it become empty under this model?

- $6\,\mathrm{min}$
- $24\,\mathrm{min}$
- $10\,\mathrm{min}$
- $14\,\mathrm{min}$

## Explanation

Volume reaches zero when twelve litres have left. Divide volume by the outlet flow rate.

$\frac{12}{2}=6\,\mathrm{min}$.
