The observations are $(1;2)$, $(2;8)$, and $(3;18)$. Which transformation of the horizontal coordinate gives a line through the origin?

- Use $u=x^2$
- Use $u=1/x$
- Use $u=x+1$
- Use $u=x-1$

## Explanation

The vertical values are twice one, four, and nine. These are the squares of the three horizontal values, giving a constant ratio with $u$.

The new pairs are $(1;2)$, $(4;8)$ and $(9;18)$. Each vertical coordinate is twice the transformed horizontal coordinate, so all lie on $y=2u$.
