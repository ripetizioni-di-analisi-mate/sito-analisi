A pump at a constant flow rate collects twice the expected volume. What timing change explains this at the same flow rate?

- The time halved
- The time quadrupled
- The time did not change
- The time doubled

## Explanation

At fixed flow rate, the factor multiplying time also multiplies the collected volume.
