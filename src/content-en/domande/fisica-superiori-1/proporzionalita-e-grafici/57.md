For $y=x^2$, how does the increase in $y$ from $x=2$ to $x=3$ compare with that from $x=1$ to $x=2$?

- It is greater by 2 units
- It is equal
- It is smaller by 2 units
- It is exactly double

## Explanation

The two changes to compare are $9-4$ and $4-1$. The vertical increases are five and three.

The horizontal coordinate changes by one unit in each interval, but the vertical increases are three and five. The average slope therefore differs between the intervals.
