Two models make the same prediction at $x=2$. Which observation can distinguish them?

- Recalculate both at $x=2$
- Remove the units
- A measurement where their predictions differ
- Replace each measurement with one model's prediction

## Explanation

A comparison is needed where the models make different predictions. Observing the shared point alone cannot distinguish the laws.

Repeating the measurement at $x=2$ may reduce the uncertainty of the reading, but the predictions still coincide there. A different coordinate allows a difference to be observed.
