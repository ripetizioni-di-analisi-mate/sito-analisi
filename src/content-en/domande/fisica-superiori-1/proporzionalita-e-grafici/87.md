A prediction at $x=4$ is $20$ and the reading is $18$. What is the relative residual compared with prediction, as a percentage?

- $10\,\%$
- $-\dfrac{100}{9}\,\%$
- $-10\,\%$
- $90\,\%$

## Explanation

Subtract prediction from observation and divide by prediction, the requested reference. The ratio is negative.

$\frac{18-20}{20}=-0.1=-10\,\%$.
