A $20\,\mathrm L$ container starts with $8\,\mathrm L$ and receives three litres per minute. For how long can the filling line describe its volume before overflowing?

- $\dfrac{20}{3}\,\mathrm{min}$
- $4\,\mathrm{min}$
- $\dfrac{8}{3}\,\mathrm{min}$
- $\dfrac{28}{3}\,\mathrm{min}$

## Explanation

Available space is capacity minus starting content. Divide this space by the flow rate to find when it runs out.

$\frac{20-8}{3}=4\,\mathrm{min}$.

The equation $8+3t=20$ gives $t=4\,\mathrm{min}$. After that time, extending the line for the contents would predict values above the available capacity.
