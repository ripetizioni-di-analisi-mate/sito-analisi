The rule is $xy=72$ and $y$ goes from $4$ to $6$. How much does $x$ decrease?

- $6$
- $12$
- $36$
- $-6$

## Explanation

Calculate the horizontal value at each vertical value. In the inverse model, the larger vertical value has the smaller horizontal value.

$\frac{72}{4}-\frac{72}{6}=6$.
