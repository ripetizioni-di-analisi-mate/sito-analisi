A model has slope $-4\,\mathrm{L/min}$. What change does it give over two further minutes of operation?

- $8\,\mathrm L$
- $-4\,\mathrm L$
- $-2\,\mathrm L$
- $-8\,\mathrm L$

## Explanation

Multiply the rate by the elapsed time in the same time unit. The negative sign describes loss of volume.

$-4\cdot 2=-8\,\mathrm L$.

The product $(-4)\cdot2$ stays negative because duration is positive. Finding the final volume would also require the starting volume, to which this change would be added.
