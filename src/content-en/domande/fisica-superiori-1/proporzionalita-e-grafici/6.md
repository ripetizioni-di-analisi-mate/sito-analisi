The law is $xy=108$ with $x,y>0$. What value of $x$ corresponds to $y=4$?

- $432$
- $27$
- $104$
- $\dfrac{1}{27}$

## Explanation

The equation gives the constant product and one of its factors. Since the supplied vertical value is positive, division by it does not lose any solutions. It gives the horizontal factor which, multiplied by the vertical one, recovers the required product.

Therefore $x=\dfrac{108}4=27$.

The solution lies in the positive domain. Check that $27\cdot4=108$. Multiplying the constant by the vertical coordinate would instead give too large a product.
