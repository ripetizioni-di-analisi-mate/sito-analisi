For the model $y=12/x$ with $x>0$, which input is impossible?

- $x=1$
- $x=0$
- $x=2$
- $x=12$

## Explanation

Zero is outside the domain because it would be in the denominator and make the ratio undefined.

Even in the form $xy=12$, substituting $x=0$ would give a zero product for any finite $y$. Thus no vertical coordinate satisfies the law there.
