For a uniform block partially floating with air buoyancy neglected, what density comparison is required?

- It exceeds the liquid density
- The block density is below the liquid density
- It is always exactly equal
- Liquid density must be zero

## Explanation

The immersed fraction is less than one. In the model, it equals body density divided by liquid density.

The density ratio must therefore be less than one. Equality would mean full submersion, and a denser body would require more volume than is available.
