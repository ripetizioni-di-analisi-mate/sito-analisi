A sealed float of mass $20\,\mathrm{kg}$ has outer volume $60\,\mathrm L$. For margin, $10\,\mathrm L$ must remain above water. In water of density $1\,\mathrm{kg/L}$, what maximum payload can be carried above water?

- $20\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $40\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $30\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $50\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The requirement leaves at most fifty litres submerged, supporting fifty kilograms total. Twenty kilograms are already used by the empty float. Thirty kilograms remain for payload. Ignoring the margin gives forty kilograms at full immersion, which fails the requirement.

$$
m_{c,\max}=(60-10)\,\mathrm L\cdot1\,\mathrm{kg/L}-20\,\mathrm{kg}=30\,\mathrm{kg}.
$$
