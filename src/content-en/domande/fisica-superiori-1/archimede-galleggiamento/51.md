A platform is built from two identical sealed modules. Each module has mass $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ and can displace at most $12\,\mathrm L$ of water. A $14\,\mathrm{kg}$ box stays dry and its load is balanced at the centre. In water of density $1\,\mathrm{kg/L}$, which setups can support the box while floating freely?

- Both one module and two modules together
- One module, while the two modules together sink
- Two modules together, while one module is insufficient
- Neither setup

## Explanation

Two modules must support $(14+6)\,\mathrm{kg}=20\,\mathrm{kg}$. They displace $20\,\mathrm L$ out of the available $24\,\mathrm L$. With one module, the box and module have mass $(14+3)\,\mathrm{kg}=17\,\mathrm{kg}$. This would require $17\,\mathrm L$, beyond the $12\,\mathrm L$ that the single module can displace.
