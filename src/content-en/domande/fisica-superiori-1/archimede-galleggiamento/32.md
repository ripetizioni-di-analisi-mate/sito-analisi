In a vessel of constant cross-sectional area $0.3\,\mathrm m^2$ the level rises $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ when a body is immersed. How many litres does it displace?

- $0.006\,\mathrm L$
- $0.6\,\mathrm L$
- $60\,\mathrm L$
- $6\,\mathrm L$

## Explanation

The immersed part takes up space previously available to liquid. At constant cross-section this equals the volume of the new two-centimetre layer.

$$
V_i=(0.3\,\mathrm m^2)(0.02\,\mathrm m)=0.006\,\mathrm m^3=6\,\mathrm L.
$$
