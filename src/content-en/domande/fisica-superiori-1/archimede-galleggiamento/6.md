A body floats freely at rest in water without bottom contact and weighs $80\,\mathrm N$. What is its buoyant force?

- $8\,\mathrm N$
- $80\,\mathrm N$
- $160\,\mathrm N$
- $0\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Without other constraints only weight and buoyancy act. Their balance requires $F_A=80\,\mathrm N$.

The density of water determines how much of it must be displaced to produce that force. As long as the body floats freely, the given weight determines the magnitude of buoyancy.
