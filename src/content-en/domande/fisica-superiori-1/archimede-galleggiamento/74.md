Where can a vertical string be anchored to stop a fully immersed body that would rise?

- Always above the body
- Below the body
- On the body with no external anchor
- Exactly above its centre of gravity at the surface

## Explanation

The constraint needs a downward force. A taut string pulls toward its anchor, which must therefore be below the body.

A string anchored above would pull upwards, in the same direction as the initial net force. Anchoring it below lets the tension oppose the excess buoyancy.
