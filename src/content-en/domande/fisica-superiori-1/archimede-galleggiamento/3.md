A body displaces $9\,\mathrm L$ of liquid and experiences buoyancy of $81\,\mathrm N$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$, what is the liquid's density?

- $90\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $9000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $900\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{900}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

Convert nine litres to $0.009\,\mathrm m^3$, then divide $81\,\mathrm N$ by $gV_i$. The density is $900\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.
