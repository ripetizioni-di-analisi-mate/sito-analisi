A platform of total mass $60\,\mathrm{kg}$ floats first in liquid of density $1\,\mathrm{kg/L}$ and then in liquid of density $1.2\,\mathrm{kg/L}$. How much does immersed volume decrease?

- $12\,\mathrm L$
- $50\,\mathrm L$
- $60\,\mathrm L$
- $10\,\mathrm L$

## Explanation

The displaced liquid mass is sixty kilograms in both tests. In the denser liquid the same mass occupies less space. The first test gives sixty litres and the second fifty. The graph shows inverse dependence on density when mass stays fixed.

$$
\Delta V_i=\left(\frac{60}{1}-\frac{60}{1.2}\right)\mathrm L=10\,\mathrm L.
$$
