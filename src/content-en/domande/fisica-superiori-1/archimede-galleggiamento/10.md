A rigid watertight float has maximum outer volume $80\,\mathrm L$ and mass $32\,\mathrm{kg}$. In water of density $1\,\mathrm{kg/L}$, what added mass, held out of the water, can it support up to complete immersion?

- $80\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $48\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $32\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $112\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

At complete immersion the displaced water has mass $80\,\mathrm{kg}$. Removing the float's own $32\,\mathrm{kg}$ leaves $48\,\mathrm{kg}$ of payload.
