An upright cylinder of constant cross-section is $25\,\mathrm{cm}$ tall and $9\,\mathrm{cm}$ protrudes above water. With $\rho_f=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$, what is its mean density?

- $640\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $360\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $625\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $900\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

In an upright cylinder, heights are proportional to volumes because they multiply the same area. Subtract the exposed height to obtain sixteen immersed centimetres. Mean density is water density times the immersed fraction of sixteen twenty-fifths.

$$
\rho_c=\rho_f\frac{h_i}{h}=1000\frac{25-9}{25}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}=640\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}.
$$
