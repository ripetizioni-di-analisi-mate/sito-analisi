A floating platform has horizontal cross-section $0.20\,\mathrm{m^2}$ below a step and $0.40\,\mathrm{m^2}$ above it. Before loading, the waterline is $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ below the step. After adding a dry load, the submerged height increases by $5\,\mathrm{cm}$. The platform keeps floating freely without tilting and water density is $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. What mass was added?

::grafico{assi=0 poligoni="-1,-5 1,-5 1,0 2,0 2,5 -2,5 -2,0 -1,0" segmenti="-2.7,-2,2.3,-2;-2.7,3,2.3,3" etichette="2.48,-1.88,I;2.48,3.18,F;-.58,-3.55,A₁;-.58,1.32,A₂" xmin=-2.85 xmax=2.88 ymin=-5.3 ymax=5.28 titolo="I and F mark the waterlines relative to the platform before and after loading."}

- $10\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $16\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The first two centimetres add $0.20\cdot0.02\,\mathrm{m^3}=0.004\,\mathrm{m^3}$ of submerged volume. The next three use the larger cross-section and add $0.40\cdot0.03\,\mathrm{m^3}=0.012\,\mathrm{m^3}$. This gives $0.016\,\mathrm{m^3}$ of extra water displaced. Those sixteen litres of water have the same mass as the added load, $16\,\mathrm{kg}$.
