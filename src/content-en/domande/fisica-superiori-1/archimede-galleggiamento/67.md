A design carries $28\,\mathrm{kg}$ of structure and $20\,\mathrm{kg}$ of dry payload. In water of density $1\,\mathrm{kg/L}$ at least $6\,\mathrm L$ must remain above water. What minimum outer volume is needed?

- $42\,\mathrm L$
- $48\,\mathrm L$
- $54\,\mathrm L$
- $60\,\mathrm L$

## Explanation

Forty-eight submerged litres support the total mass. Add the six litres required above the surface.

$$
V_{\rm tot,\min}=\frac{28+20}{1}\,\mathrm L+6\,\mathrm L=54\,\mathrm L.
$$

This is the smallest volume that allows the required margin. With only forty-eight litres of external volume, the platform would reach full submersion at the specified load.
