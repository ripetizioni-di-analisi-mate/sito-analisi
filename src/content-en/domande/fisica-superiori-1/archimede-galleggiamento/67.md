A design carries $28\,\mathrm{kg}$ of structure and $20\,\mathrm{kg}$ of dry payload. In water of density $1\,\mathrm{kg/L}$ at least $5\,\mathrm L$ must remain above water. What minimum outer volume is needed?

- $43\,\mathrm L$
- $48\,\mathrm L$
- $53\,\mathrm L$
- $58\,\mathrm L$

## Explanation

Forty-eight submerged litres support the total mass. Add the five litres required above the surface.

$$
V_{\rm tot,\min}=\frac{28+20}{1}\,\mathrm L+5\,\mathrm L=53\,\mathrm L.
$$
