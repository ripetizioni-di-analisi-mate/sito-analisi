A submerged body is held at rest by a bottom string of tension $7\,\mathrm N$. Its weight is $11\,\mathrm N$. What is its buoyancy?

- $4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $7\,\mathrm{N}$
- $18\,\mathrm{N}$
- $11\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Weight and bottom-line tension both act downward. Buoyancy is the only upward contribution and balances their sum.

$$
F_A=P+T=(11+7)\,\mathrm N=18\,\mathrm N.
$$

The condition differs from a string attached above. Here the seven newtons act in the same direction as the eleven newtons of weight, increasing the required buoyancy.
