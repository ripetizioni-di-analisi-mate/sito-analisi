In an experiment a fully submerged object compresses at greater depth while liquid density stays fixed. Which change invalidates the rigid-body argument for constant buoyancy?

- Pressure rises equally across the entire wall
- Weight changes from newtons to pascals
- Displaced volume decreases
- Field must decrease by definition

## Explanation

The body's occupied volume enters the buoyancy product directly. Compression at fixed density reduces that product.

Even though pressure is higher at greater depth, buoyancy in this model is the weight of the volume actually displaced. With less volume, less liquid mass is displaced.
