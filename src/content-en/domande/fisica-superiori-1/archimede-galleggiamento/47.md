A ship floating at rest experiences buoyancy $6000\,\mathrm N$. What is its weight if weight and buoyancy are the only forces?

- $3000\,\mathrm N$
- $12000\,\mathrm N$
- $6000\,\mathrm N$
- $0\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Rest requires zero vertical resultant. The measured buoyancy is the only upward force and matches weight.
