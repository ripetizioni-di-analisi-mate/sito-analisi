A sealed float initially has half its external volume submerged in water of density $1\,\mathrm{kg/L}$. After adding a dry load of $0.30\,\mathrm{kg}$, the submerged fraction rises from one half to three quarters. What is the external volume of the float?

- $0.30\,\mathrm L$
- $0.60\,\mathrm L$
- $0.90\,\mathrm L$
- $1.20\,\mathrm L$

## Explanation

The added load requires another $0.30\,\mathrm L$ of displaced water. The increase in submerged fraction equals one quarter of the total volume. If one quarter is $0.30\,\mathrm L$, the full volume is $4\cdot0.30\,\mathrm L=1.20\,\mathrm L$. The calculation compares the two immersions of the same float.
