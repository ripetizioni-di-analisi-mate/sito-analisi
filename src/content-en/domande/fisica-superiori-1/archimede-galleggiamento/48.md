The same block is $72\%$ submerged in water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and $90\%$ submerged in oil. What is the oil density?

- $720\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $900\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $1250\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

The body's mean density is fluid density times immersed fraction in each test. Equate the water product with the oil product. A greater fraction must be immersed in oil, so its calculated density is below water density.

$$
\rho_{\rm olio}=\frac{0.72\cdot1000}{0.90}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}=800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}.
$$
