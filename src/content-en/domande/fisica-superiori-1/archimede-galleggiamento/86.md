When fully immersed, two watertight bodies have volumes $4$ and $7\,\mathrm L$ in the same fluid. What is the ratio of larger to smaller buoyancy?

- $\dfrac{4}{7}$
- $1.75$
- $3$
- $1$

## Explanation

Both bodies are in the same liquid and gravitational field. The ratio of their buoyant forces therefore depends only on the submerged volumes, which here equal the respective external volumes.

$$
\frac{F_{A,2}}{F_{A,1}}=\frac{7}{4}=1.75.
$$

In the same liquid each litre gives the same contribution to buoyancy. Seven litres give more than four, so the requested ratio must exceed one.
