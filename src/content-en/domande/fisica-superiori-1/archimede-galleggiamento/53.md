A body is suspended in water by a line from above. What prevents us from always concluding $F_A=P$?

- Tension is another term in the balance
- Buoyancy points down in water
- A submerged body always has zero weight
- Water pressure does not increase with depth

## Explanation

The condition $F_A=P$ requires only those two forces. A taut upper line supplies extra support.
