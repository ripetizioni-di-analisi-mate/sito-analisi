An air bubble in resting water excludes some water. What forces produce the buoyant resultant on the bubble?

- Kinetic friction always directed upward
- Gravitational forces from air on water
- Water-pressure forces on its surface
- Tensions in an unseen cord

## Explanation

Buoyancy sums the surrounding medium's pressure forces. A bubble also replaces part of its surrounding fluid when it occupies a volume.
