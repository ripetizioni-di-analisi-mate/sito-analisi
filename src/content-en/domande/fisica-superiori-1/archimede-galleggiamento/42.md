A prism of volume $10\,\mathrm L$ and mean density $600\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ floats in water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. How much volume is above the water?

- $6\,\mathrm L$
- $4\,\mathrm L$
- $0.4\,\mathrm L$
- $16\,\mathrm L$

## Explanation

The density ratio gives six tenths immersed. The question asks for the other portion, four tenths. Apply that fraction to the ten-litre total. The volume check adds six submerged litres to four exposed litres to recover the prism's total.

$$
\begin{aligned}V_i&=10\frac{600}{1000}\,\mathrm L=6\,\mathrm L\\[6pt]V_e&=(10-6)\,\mathrm L=4\,\mathrm L.\end{aligned}
$$
