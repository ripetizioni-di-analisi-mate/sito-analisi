An oblique line pulls a float toward the bottom. Which part of its tension enters vertical balance, provided horizontal balance is also satisfied?

- The full magnitude for every angle
- Only its horizontal component
- The downward vertical component
- The tension treated as upward

## Explanation

Equilibrium is written using force projections. Vertically use the component along weight and buoyancy.
