An object of density $900\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ rests at the interface between oil of density $800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. The entire object is in the two liquids, clear of the container. What fraction of its volume is in the water?

- $\dfrac9{10}$
- $\dfrac19$
- $\dfrac45$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

If the fraction in water is $f$, the displaced mass per unit volume is $1000f+800(1-f)$. Equilibrium requires this to equal $900$, so $200f=100$ and $f=\tfrac12$.
