A spring scale reads $75\,\mathrm N$ in air and $60\,\mathrm N$ after the body is completely immersed, with negligible air buoyancy. By what percentage did the reading fall?

- $15\,\%$
- $25\,\%$
- $80\,\%$
- $20\,\%$

## Explanation

The reading falls by $75-60=15\,\mathrm N$, which is the apparent loss of weight due to the buoyant force of the liquid. The percentage decrease is calculated with respect to the initial reading, so the drop is divided by $75\,\mathrm N$.

$$
\frac{75-60}{75}\cdot100\,\%=\frac{15}{75}\cdot100\,\%=20\,\%.
$$

The reading has therefore fallen by $20\,\%$. The value $25\,\%$ comes from comparing the drop with the final reading of $60\,\mathrm N$, while $80\,\%$ is the ratio of the final reading to the initial one.
