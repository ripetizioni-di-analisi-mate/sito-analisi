A scale reads $75\,\mathrm N$ in air and $60\,\mathrm N$ after complete immersion, with negligible air buoyancy. By what percentage did the reading fall?

- $15\,\%$
- $25\,\%$
- $80\,\%$
- $20\,\%$

## Explanation

The reference for the percentage drop is the initial reading. A drop of fifteen newtons is one fifth of seventy-five.

$$
\frac{75-60}{75}\cdot100\%=20\%.
$$

The fifteen-newton drop is twenty per cent of the reading in air. Using sixty newtons in the denominator would compare the drop with the final reading.
