A watertight platform of mass $16\,\mathrm{kg}$ and external volume $35\,\mathrm L$ floats in water of density $1\,\mathrm{kg/L}$. A $9.5\,\mathrm{kg}$ load hangs below it and is fully submerged. The load displaces $2.5\,\mathrm L$ of water. What volume of the platform remains above water?

- $9.5\,\mathrm L$
- $12\,\mathrm L$
- $14.5\,\mathrm L$
- $23\,\mathrm L$

## Explanation

The whole system must displace $16+9.5=25.5$ litres. The load displaces $2.5$ of those litres, so the platform displaces only $23$. The platform has an external volume of $35\,\mathrm L$ and a submerged volume of $23\,\mathrm L$. This leaves $12\,\mathrm L$ above the surface. Subtracting all $25.5\,\mathrm L$ would wrongly assign the load's displacement to the platform.
