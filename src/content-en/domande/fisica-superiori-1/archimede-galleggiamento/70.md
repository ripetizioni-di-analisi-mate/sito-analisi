A float has $35\,\mathrm L$ immersed and $15\,\mathrm L$ exposed in water of density $1\,\mathrm{kg/L}$. How much additional dry mass can it carry before complete immersion?

- $20\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $15\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $35\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $50\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The added capacity comes from the volume still above water, not the part already immersed. Fifteen litres support fifteen extra kilograms.

$$
\Delta m_{\max}=(1\,\mathrm{kg/L})(15\,\mathrm L)=15\,\mathrm{kg}.
$$
