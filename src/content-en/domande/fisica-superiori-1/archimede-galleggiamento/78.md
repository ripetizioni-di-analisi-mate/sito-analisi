Before a body is immersed, a vessel needs another $0.8\,\mathrm L$ of water to reach its outlet. With the body held immersed clear of the vessel, $3.2\,\mathrm L$ is collected from the outlet. At equilibrium, what buoyant force acts on the body with $\rho_f=1\,\mathrm{kg/L}$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 linee="0,4 0,0 4,0 4,4;4,2.8 5.1,2.8 5.1,2.2;4.6,0.85 4.6,0 6.2,0 6.2,0.85;0,2.8 4,2.8;4.6,0.43 6.2,0.43" poligoni="1.3,1.8 2.7,1.8 2.7,3.25 1.3,3.25" vettori="5.1,2.04,5.1,0.94," etichette="1.98,2.2,body;5.39,0.24,collected" xmin=-0.6 xmax=6.8 ymin=-0.4 ymax=4.15 titolo="The final water level reaches the outlet. The cup collects 3.2 L."}

- $32\,\mathrm N$
- $40\,\mathrm N$
- $24\,\mathrm N$
- $8\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The first $0.8\,\mathrm L$ displaced raises the water level to the outlet. Any further water displaced flows out and is collected. Add the volume displaced before overflow and the volume collected. The buoyant force equals the weight of the resulting four litres of water.

$$
\begin{aligned}
V_i&=(0.8+3.2)\,\mathrm L=4.0\,\mathrm L,\\[6pt]
F_A&=(1\,\mathrm{kg/L})(4.0\,\mathrm L)(10\,\mathrm{N/kg})=40\,\mathrm N.
\end{aligned}
$$
