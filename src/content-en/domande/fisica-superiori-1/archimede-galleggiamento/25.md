What unit follows from density times volume times field when using $\mathrm{kg/m^3}$, $\mathrm m^3$, and $\mathrm{N/kg}$?

- $\mathrm N$
- $\mathrm{kg}$
- $\mathrm{Pa}$
- $\mathrm{N/m^3}$

## Explanation

Cubic metres and kilograms cancel between numerators and denominators. The result is a force in newtons.
