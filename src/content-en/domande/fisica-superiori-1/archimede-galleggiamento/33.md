A float weighs $70\,\mathrm N$ in water where each displaced litre weighs $10\,\mathrm N$. What volume is submerged in free equilibrium?

- $7\,\mathrm L$
- $0.7\,\mathrm L$
- $70\,\mathrm L$
- $700\,\mathrm L$

## Explanation

Without ropes or supports, buoyancy must equal weight. Divide seventy newtons by the contribution per litre. The graph intersection at seven litres gives exactly the required force. The result is admissible if at least seven litres of displacement are available.

$$
V_i=\frac{70\,\mathrm N}{10\,\mathrm{N/L}}=7\,\mathrm L.
$$
