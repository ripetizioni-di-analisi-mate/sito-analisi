A rigid, sealed prism is slowly lowered into water without touching the bottom. The graph shows buoyancy against the downward displacement $d$ from first contact with the water. From $d=4\,\mathrm{cm}$ onwards, buoyancy is constant as the prism continues down. What happens at that point?

::grafico{linee="0,0 4,20 8,20" punti="4,20,pieno" segmenti="4,0,4,20" xmin=0 xmax=9 ymin=0 ymax=25 nomi="d (cm),Fₐ (N)" titolo="Buoyancy as the prism is lowered from first contact with the water."}

- The top face enters the water too
- The lower face touches the bottom
- Water pressure stops increasing with depth
- The prism starts to emerge

## Explanation

On the rising segment the prism enters the water and displaces more and more volume. The line becomes horizontal when the top face also goes under. The displaced volume is then the full volume of the prism. The object keeps descending while buoyancy stays the same.
