At the same liquid and mass, a new float has twice the old one's external volume. How does its immersed fraction change in free flotation?

- It halves
- It doubles
- It stays the same
- It becomes greater than one

## Explanation

Mass fixes the same displaced volume in both cases. That volume is a smaller fraction because the total doubles.

If $V_i$ is the same, the fraction changes from $\dfrac{V_i}{V}$ to $\dfrac{V_i}{2V}$. For example, an initial fraction of sixty per cent would become thirty per cent.
