An upper string is slack and the body stays at rest without supports. What balance must already hold for buoyancy and weight?

- Equal magnitudes, opposite directions
- Both point up
- Buoyancy always exceeds weight
- Weight always exceeds buoyancy

## Explanation

A slack rope exerts no tension. The two remaining forces must give zero vertical resultant.

If buoyancy and weight had different magnitudes, one would be greater and the net force would be nonzero. A slack string cannot offset that difference.
