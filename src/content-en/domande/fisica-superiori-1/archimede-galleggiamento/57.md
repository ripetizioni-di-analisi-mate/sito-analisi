A float of mass $18\,\mathrm{kg}$ has maximum usable immersed volume $40\,\mathrm L$. In a liquid of density $0.9\,\mathrm{kg/L}$, how much dry payload is available?

- $18\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $22\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $36\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $54\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The usable volume displaces thirty-six kilograms of the given liquid. That mass is total capacity, including the float. Subtract the unloaded float mass to get eighteen kilograms available. The same volume of denser water would allow a larger payload.

$$
m_c=40\,\mathrm L\cdot0.9\,\mathrm{kg/L}-18\,\mathrm{kg}=18\,\mathrm{kg}.
$$
