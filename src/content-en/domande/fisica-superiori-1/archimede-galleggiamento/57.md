A sealed float of mass $18\,\mathrm{kg}$ and external volume $45\,\mathrm L$ must carry a dry load of $18\,\mathrm{kg}$. At least $5\,\mathrm L$ of the float must stay above the liquid. What is the minimum liquid density?

- $0.90\,\mathrm{kg/L}$
- $0.80\,\mathrm{kg/L}$
- $0.45\,\mathrm{kg/L}$
- $1.11\,\mathrm{kg/L}$

## Explanation

The submerged volume can be at most $40\,\mathrm L$. That volume of liquid must have the same mass as the float and its load together. In a less dense liquid, displacing that mass takes more than forty litres of submerged volume. At $0.80\,\mathrm{kg/L}$ the entire float would be submerged.

$$
\rho_\mathrm{min}=\frac{(18+18)\,\mathrm{kg}}{(45-5)\,\mathrm L}=0.90\,\mathrm{kg/L}.
$$
