A block with total volume $40\,\mathrm L$ floats with $60\%$ of its volume immersed. What volume is above the surface?

- $16\,\mathrm L$
- $24\,\mathrm L$
- $40\,\mathrm L$
- $1.6\,\mathrm L$

## Explanation

The exposed part is forty percent of the same total volume. It equals $0.4\cdot40\,\mathrm L=16\,\mathrm L$.

The twenty-four submerged litres plus the sixteen above water reconstruct the block's forty litres. Taking sixty per cent would give the volume on the wrong side of the surface.
