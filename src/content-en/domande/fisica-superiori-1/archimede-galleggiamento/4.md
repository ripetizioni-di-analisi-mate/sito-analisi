A body weighing $270\,\mathrm N$ hangs at rest from a spring scale. When fully submerged, it experiences buoyancy of $90\,\mathrm N$. What force does the scale read?

- $360\,\mathrm N$
- $270\,\mathrm N$
- $90\,\mathrm N$
- $180\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Buoyancy supplies $90\,\mathrm N$ upward. The string tension supplies the remaining $270-90=180\,\mathrm N$.
