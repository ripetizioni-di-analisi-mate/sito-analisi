Equal dry loads are added to two floats with enough volume available, one in water and one in a less dense liquid. Which needs a larger increase in submerged volume?

- Water
- They are equal because the mass is the same
- There is no increase in either case
- The less dense liquid

## Explanation

Equal added weight requires equal extra displaced-liquid mass. Fixed mass occupies greater volume in the less dense liquid.
