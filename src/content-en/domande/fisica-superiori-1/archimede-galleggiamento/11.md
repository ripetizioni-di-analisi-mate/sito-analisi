A platform floats in water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. An $8\,\mathrm{kg}$ load is added and stays above the water. How much does the submerged volume increase if the platform still floats freely?

- $8\,\mathrm m^3$
- $0.08\,\mathrm m^3$
- $0.008\,\mathrm m^3$
- $8000\,\mathrm m^3$

## Explanation

The displaced water mass increases by eight kilograms. Dividing by density gives $\Delta V_i=0.008\,\mathrm m^3$.

The difference is eight litres because each litre supports one kilogram. The platform's initial displaced volume cancels when the two floating balances before and after loading are subtracted.
