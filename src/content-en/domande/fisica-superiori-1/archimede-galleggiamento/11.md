A platform floats in water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. A mass of $8\,\mathrm{kg}$ is added. How much does immersed volume increase while it still floats freely?

- $8\,\mathrm m^3$
- $0.08\,\mathrm m^3$
- $0.008\,\mathrm m^3$
- $8000\,\mathrm m^3$

## Explanation

The displaced water mass increases by eight kilograms. Dividing by density gives $\Delta V_i=0.008\,\mathrm m^3$.
