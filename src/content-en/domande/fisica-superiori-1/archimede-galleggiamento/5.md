An object weighs $180\,\mathrm N$ in air and the scale reads $108\,\mathrm N$ when it is fully immersed and at rest. Neglect air buoyancy. What is the liquid's buoyancy?

- $72\,\mathrm N$
- $288\,\mathrm N$
- $108\,\mathrm N$
- $180\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The apparent loss measures the part of the weight supported by liquid. It equals $180-108=72\,\mathrm N$.

In air the string supported the full weight. In water, the measured one hundred and eight newtons still come from the string, while the liquid supports the difference. The weight remains one hundred and eighty newtons.
