A submerged body weighing $55\,\mathrm N$ has $15\,\mathrm N$ of upward tension and $6\,\mathrm N$ of tension to the bottom. With both strings vertical, what buoyancy gives rest?

- $34\,\mathrm{N}$
- $61\,\mathrm{N}$
- $76\,\mathrm{N}$
- $46\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Balance the two directions separately. The lower line adds to downward weight and the upper line adds to upward buoyancy.

$$
F_A=55\,\mathrm N+6\,\mathrm N-15\,\mathrm N=46\,\mathrm N.
$$

As a check, forty-six newtons of buoyancy plus fifteen of upper tension give sixty-one newtons upwards. Fifty-five newtons of weight plus six of lower tension give the same total downwards.
