A solid is completely submerged in a liquid whose density exactly equals its mean density. What can the upper scale read at ideal equilibrium?

- The full weight
- Twice the weight
- Zero, because buoyancy and weight already balance
- A negative vertical tension

## Explanation

The body and its displaced liquid volume have the same mass. Their forces are equal and opposite, leaving no load for the line.

Weight and buoyancy already give a zero net force, as required for the body to stay at rest. In a uniform liquid the same condition holds at every depth of full submersion.
