A block on the bottom receives $30\,\mathrm N$ buoyancy even while supported, as given by the model, and weighs $44\,\mathrm N$. What upward reaction is needed?

- $30\,\mathrm{N}$
- $44\,\mathrm{N}$
- $74\,\mathrm{N}$
- $14\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The bottom supports the weight remaining after the given buoyancy contribution. The positive reaction is consistent with non-adhesive contact.

$$
N=P-F_A=(44-30)\,\mathrm N=14\,\mathrm N.
$$
