Why does pressure force on the lower face of a submerged cube exceed the pressure force on its top face?

- Because water pushes only upward
- Because surface pressure is negative
- Because the lower face is deeper and the areas are equal
- Because the cube's top has zero area

## Explanation

Both faces have the same area $A$. Pressure increases with depth, so the upward force on the lower face is larger than the downward force on the upper face. Both pressures include the same free-surface pressure, which cancels when their force magnitudes are subtracted.
