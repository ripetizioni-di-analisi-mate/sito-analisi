Whose weight directly determines the buoyant force on a submerged stone?

- Always the stone's weight
- The weight of all liquid in the vessel
- Always the stone's apparent weight
- The displaced liquid's weight

## Explanation

The pressure resultant replaces the support that held the volume of liquid now excluded by the object. Its weight therefore sets the buoyancy.
