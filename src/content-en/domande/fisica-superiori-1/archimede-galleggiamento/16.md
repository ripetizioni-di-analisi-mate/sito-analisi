Whose weight directly determines the buoyant force on a submerged stone?

- Always the stone's weight
- The weight of all liquid in the vessel
- Always the stone's apparent weight
- The displaced liquid's weight

## Explanation

Before the stone was introduced, pressure forces on the fluid at rest balanced its weight. The pressure forces have the same resultant over the same submerged surface, so the weight of the displaced fluid enters the calculation. The stone's own weight is used to predict its motion after release.
