A model boat displaces $12\,\mathrm L$ while floating freely in liquid of density $0.85\,\mathrm{kg/L}$. What is its total mass?

- $12\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $10.2\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.85\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $\dfrac{240}{17}\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Equate boat weight to displaced liquid weight. The shared gravitational field cancels, leaving equal masses.

$$
m=\rho_f V_i=0.85\cdot12\,\mathrm{kg}=10.2\,\mathrm{kg}.
$$
