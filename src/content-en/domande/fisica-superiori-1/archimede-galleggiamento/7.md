A uniform body of density $900\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ floats in water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. What fraction of its volume is immersed?

- $\dfrac{10}{9}$
- $0.1$
- $0.9$
- $0.09$

## Explanation

In the balance $\rho_f V_i=\rho_c V$, the volume ratio equals the density ratio. The fraction is $\dfrac{900}{1000}=0.9$.

In percentage terms, ninety per cent is submerged and ten per cent is above water. Both densities are in the same units, so their ratio is dimensionless.
