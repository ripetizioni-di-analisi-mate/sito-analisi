A waterproof object weighs $50\,\mathrm N$ and has an external volume of $4\,\mathrm L$. A spring scale above it holds it at rest while partly submerged. The scale has a capacity of $30\,\mathrm N$. If each litre of displaced water weighs $10\,\mathrm N$, what minimum submerged volume keeps the reading within capacity?

- $1\,\mathrm L$
- $3\,\mathrm L$
- $4\,\mathrm L$
- $2\,\mathrm L$

## Explanation

At the limit the scale supports $30\,\mathrm N$ of the $50\,\mathrm N$ weight. The water must supply $20\,\mathrm N$, the weight of two litres. A smaller submerged volume gives less buoyancy and the scale would exceed its capacity. The object has enough volume to submerge the required two litres.
