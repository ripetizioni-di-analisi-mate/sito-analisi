Two differently shaped, watertight, non-porous bodies are submerged fully in the same uniform liquid without touching the bottom. Both have outer volume $8\,\mathrm L$. How do their buoyant forces compare?

- They are equal
- The heavier body has greater buoyancy
- The wider body has greater buoyancy whatever its shape
- The denser body has greater buoyancy

## Explanation

Both exclude equal volumes of liquid in the same field. The buoyancy law therefore gives equal magnitudes.
