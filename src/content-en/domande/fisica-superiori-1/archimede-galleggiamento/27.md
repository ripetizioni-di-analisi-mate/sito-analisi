Object A weighs $60\,\mathrm N$ in air and object B weighs $45\,\mathrm N$, with negligible air buoyancy. Hung motionless one at a time and fully immersed in the same liquid, both give a spring scale reading of $30\,\mathrm N$. What is the ratio of A's volume to B's volume?

- $1$
- $0.5$
- $2$
- $1.33$

## Explanation

The liquid supports $(60-30)\,\mathrm N=30\,\mathrm N$ on A and $(45-30)\,\mathrm N=15\,\mathrm N$ on B. In the same liquid, buoyancy is proportional to submerged volume. The ratio is $30/15=2$. Equal readings give equal tensions, while the different weights require different contributions from the liquid.
