An object at rest underwater weighs $20\,\mathrm N$ and receives $32\,\mathrm N$ of buoyancy. A slanted line pulls it towards the bottom. The magnitude of the vertical component is three fifths of the tension. A second, horizontal line balances the horizontal component. What is the magnitude of the tension in the slanted line?

- $12\,\mathrm N$
- $7.2\,\mathrm N$
- $32\,\mathrm N$
- $20\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The vertical balance needs $(32-20)\,\mathrm N=12\,\mathrm N$ downwards. These twelve newtons are three fifths of the tension. One fifth is therefore $4\,\mathrm N$ and the full magnitude is $20\,\mathrm N$. The second line balances only the horizontal effect and does not enter the vertical sum.
