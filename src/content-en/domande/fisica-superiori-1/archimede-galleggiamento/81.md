A rigid shell is fully submerged in two trials with no spillage. With its cavity closed, the rise in water level indicates $12\,\mathrm L$ of displacement. With the cavity open and filled by water from the tank, the rise indicates $8\,\mathrm L$. What fraction of the external volume is occupied by the cavity?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

With the cavity closed, the body excludes all $12\,\mathrm L$ of its external volume. When open, tank water fills the cavity and only the walls exclude water. The cavity has volume $(12-8)\,\mathrm L=4\,\mathrm L$, so the requested fraction is $\dfrac{4}{12}=\dfrac{1}{3}$.
