The same cube is rotated while entirely submerged in a uniform liquid. It touches no wall and its outer volume stays fixed. What happens to the buoyancy magnitude?

- It becomes zero
- It stays the same
- It doubles after half a turn
- It is greater only with a horizontal edge

## Explanation

Some face pressure components change, but displaced liquid weight stays fixed. The vertical resultant has the same magnitude.
