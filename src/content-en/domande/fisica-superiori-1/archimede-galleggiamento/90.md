The same cube is fully submerged in a uniform liquid, away from the bottom and walls. Two orientations are compared with the cube and liquid at rest. What happens to the magnitude of the buoyant force if the external volume stays the same?

- It becomes zero
- It stays the same
- It doubles after half a turn
- It is greater only with a horizontal edge

## Explanation

The vertical components of the pressure forces on individual faces change, while the displaced liquid's weight remains fixed. Their sum gives the same buoyant force magnitude.

The fluid density, the gravitational field and the submerged volume stay the same as the cube turns. The situation would differ if part of the cube emerged.
