A submerged body without bottom contact has buoyancy $30\,\mathrm N$ and weight $20\,\mathrm N$. A cord to the bottom holds it at rest. What is its tension?

- $10\,\mathrm{N}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N}$
- $30\,\mathrm{N}$
- $50\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Buoyancy tends to lift the body. The bottom cord adds downward tension to weight. The line supplies only the ten-newton difference. Weight plus tension reaches thirty newtons and balances buoyancy.

$$
F_A-P-T=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad T=(30-20)\,\mathrm N=10\,\mathrm N.
$$
