A formal calculation gives a submerged fraction of $1.2$ for an object. How is it interpreted?

- Free flotation is impossible with those data
- Twenty percent remains above water
- The body stays suspended at every depth
- The fraction should be rounded to one

## Explanation

Immersed volume cannot exceed the total. Even fully submerged, buoyancy falls below the required weight.
