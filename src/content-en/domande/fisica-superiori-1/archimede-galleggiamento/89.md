Objects A and B are fully immersed in the same liquid. Their volumes are equal but they weigh $40$ and $60\,\mathrm N$. With both upper lines taut, by how much do their tensions differ?

- $20\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N}$
- $100\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Equal volumes give equal buoyancy. Subtracting tensions leaves the difference of weights, twenty newtons.

$$
T_B-T_A=(60-F_A)-(40-F_A)=20\,\mathrm N.
$$
