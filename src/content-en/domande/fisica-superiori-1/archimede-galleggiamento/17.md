The displaced liquid is collected and its mass is $1.8\,\mathrm{kg}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$, what is buoyancy without knowing the liquid volume?

- $18\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1.8\,\mathrm{N}$
- $9\,\mathrm{N}$
- $180\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Mass and field directly give the displaced liquid's weight. Density and volume do not need to be reconstructed separately.

$$
F_A=(1.8\,\mathrm{kg})(10\,\mathrm{N/kg})=18\,\mathrm N.
$$
