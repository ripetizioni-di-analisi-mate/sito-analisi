A body weighs $45\,\mathrm N$ in air with negligible air buoyancy and reads $30\,\mathrm N$ in a liquid when fully immersed. What fraction of its weight is supplied by buoyancy?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $3$
- $0.5$

## Explanation

The fifteen-newton difference is the support from liquid. Compare this share with the full forty-five-newton weight.

$$
\frac{F_A}{P}=\frac{45-30}{45}=\frac13.
$$

The other two thirds are supported by the string. You can also obtain that fraction by comparing the thirty-newton reading with the forty-five-newton initial weight.
