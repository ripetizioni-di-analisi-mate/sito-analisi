Only buoyancy is measured in a liquid of unknown density. Field $g$ is known. What is determined directly by the measurement?

- The product $\rho_f V_i$
- Only $V_i$ uniquely
- Only $\rho_f$ uniquely
- Body mass in every situation

## Explanation

Dividing force by field gives displaced liquid mass. A second datum is needed to separate density and volume.

For example, the same displaced liquid mass may occupy less volume in salt water than in fresh water. The two buoyancy readings alone would give the same product.
