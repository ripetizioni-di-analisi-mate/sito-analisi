A body displaces $8\,\mathrm L$ of water. With $\rho=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$, what is the buoyant force?

- $80\,\mathrm N$
- $8\,\mathrm N$
- $8000\,\mathrm N$
- $80000\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Eight litres equal $0.008\,\mathrm m^3$. The displaced liquid has mass $8\,\mathrm{kg}$ and weight $80\,\mathrm N$, the buoyant-force magnitude.
