An object crosses two resting liquid layers and displaces $3\,\mathrm L$ of oil at $0.8\,\mathrm{kg/L}$ plus $2\,\mathrm L$ of water at $1\,\mathrm{kg/L}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$, what is total buoyancy?

- $40\,\mathrm{N}$
- $50\,\mathrm{N}$
- $24\,\mathrm{N}$
- $44\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The displaced weight splits into two fluids of different density. The displaced masses are two point four and two kilograms. Add their weights.

$$
F_A=(3\cdot0.8+2\cdot1)10\,\mathrm N=44\,\mathrm N.
$$
