A submerged prism has horizontal faces of area $0.03\,\mathrm m^2$. The top pressure is $104000\,\mathrm{Pa}$ and the bottom pressure is $108000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. The sides are vertical. What is the buoyant force?

- $120\,\mathrm{N}$
- $3240\,\mathrm{N}$
- $3120\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4000\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The lower face gets an upward force and the upper face a downward force. With equal areas, the pressures can be subtracted before multiplying. The sides contribute no vertical force. The one-hundred-and-twenty-newton resultant is upward because pressure is greater at the deeper face.

$$
F_A=(108000-104000)\,\mathrm{Pa}\cdot0.03\,\mathrm m^2=120\,\mathrm N.
$$
