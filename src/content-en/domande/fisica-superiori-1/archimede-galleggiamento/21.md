A rigid prism with vertical sides is at rest and fully submerged in water, clear of the walls. A piston increases the water pressure by the same amount everywhere. The water density stays fixed and the two horizontal faces have equal areas. How do the forces on those faces and the buoyant force change?

- Both face force magnitudes increase and buoyancy stays the same
- Both face force magnitudes and buoyancy increase
- Only the force magnitude on the lower face increases
- Both face force magnitudes and buoyancy stay the same

## Explanation

Each face receives the same increase in force because the pressure increment and the area are equal. The increase on the upper face acts downwards and the one on the lower face upwards. These increments cancel in the resultant, leaving the original buoyant force.
