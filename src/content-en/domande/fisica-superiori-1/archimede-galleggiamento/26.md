Buoyancy can be used to infer $V_i$. When is that also the complete external volume?

- When half the body is above the surface
- When a rigid watertight body is fully immersed
- Whenever it floats freely
- Whenever the liquid is less dense than water

## Explanation

Displaced volume is the part excluding fluid. It is the whole outer volume when immersion is complete and fluid cannot enter.
