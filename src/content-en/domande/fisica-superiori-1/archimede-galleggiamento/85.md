A rigid body already fully immersed is moved deeper into a uniform liquid. What happens to the pressure difference across its two horizontal faces at fixed orientation?

- It stays fixed because the vertical separation of the faces stays fixed
- It doubles for every extra metre
- It always vanishes
- It becomes atmospheric pressure

## Explanation

Both pressures grow by the same amount. Their difference is $\rho g$ times the face separation, which the rigid body keeps fixed.
