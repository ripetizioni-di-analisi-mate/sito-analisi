An immersed body experiences buoyancy of $64\,\mathrm N$ in oil of density $800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$, what is its immersed volume?

- $8\,\mathrm m^3$
- $0.008\,\mathrm m^3$
- $0.08\,\mathrm m^3$
- $0.0008\,\mathrm m^3$

## Explanation

The submerged volume is $\dfrac{F_A}{\rho_f g}$. Substituting the given values gives $\dfrac{64}{800\cdot10}\,\mathrm m^3=0.008\,\mathrm m^3$.

To check the volume, write it as eight litres. The oil weighs eight newtons per litre, so eight litres give exactly sixty-four newtons of buoyancy.
