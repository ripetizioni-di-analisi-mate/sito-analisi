An immersed body experiences buoyancy of $64\,\mathrm N$ in oil of density $800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$, what is its immersed volume?

- $8\,\mathrm m^3$
- $0.008\,\mathrm m^3$
- $0.08\,\mathrm m^3$
- $0.0008\,\mathrm m^3$

## Explanation

The volume is $F_A/(\rho_f g)$. The ratio $64/(800\cdot10)=0.008$ is in cubic metres.
