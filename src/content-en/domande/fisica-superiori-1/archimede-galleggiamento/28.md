A buoyant force of $18\,\mathrm N$ in liquid of density $900\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ with $g=10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$ implies a volume. How many litres?

- $0.002\,\mathrm L$
- $0.2\,\mathrm L$
- $20\,\mathrm L$
- $2\,\mathrm L$

## Explanation

SI units give two thousandths of a cubic metre. Each thousandth is one litre, giving two litres.

$$
V_i=\frac{18}{900\cdot10}\,\mathrm m^3=2\,\mathrm L.
$$
