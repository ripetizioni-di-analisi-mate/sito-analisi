Two bodies in the same liquid float with submerged fractions $0.4$ and $0.6$. What is the ratio of the second body's density to the first?

- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $1.5$
- $0.2$
- $2$

## Explanation

Each fraction is a density relative to the same liquid. The common liquid density cancels in the body density ratio.

$$
\frac{\rho_2}{\rho_1}=\frac{0.6}{0.4}=1.5.
$$

The second body is denser, as indicated by the value greater than one. The reverse ratio would compare the first body with the second and give two thirds.
