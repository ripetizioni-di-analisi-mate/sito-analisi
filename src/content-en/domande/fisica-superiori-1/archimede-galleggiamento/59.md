A freely floating body has $7\,\mathrm L$ below and $3\,\mathrm L$ above the water. What is its mean density if the water density is $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$?

- $300\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $700\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $\dfrac{7000}{3}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

The fraction's denominator is total volume, ten litres. The float's mean density is seven tenths of water density.

$$
\rho_c=1000\frac{7}{7+3}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}=700\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}.
$$

The volume ratio is seven tenths, so the density must be below that of water. Putting the volume above the surface in the numerator in place of the submerged volume would give three tenths of the water density.
