A float has $7\,\mathrm L$ below and $3\,\mathrm L$ above the water. What is its mean density if water density is $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$?

- $300\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $700\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $\dfrac{7000}{3}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

The fraction's denominator is total volume, ten litres. The float's mean density is seven tenths of water density.

$$
\rho_c=1000\frac{7}{7+3}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}=700\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}.
$$
