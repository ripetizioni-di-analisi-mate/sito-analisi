A freely floating object and an object held fully immersed are transferred to the same denser liquid. Which has unchanged buoyancy when gravity stays fixed?

- Only the one held submerged
- Both
- Neither in any case
- The freely floating object

## Explanation

The free float adjusts its volume to keep buoyancy equal to weight. The other has fixed submerged volume and receives more buoyancy.
