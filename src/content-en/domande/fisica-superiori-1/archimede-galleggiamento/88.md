Two rigid bodies are transferred from the same liquid into a denser one. The first floats freely before and after the transfer. The second is held fully submerged in both tests. On which body is the buoyant force from the liquid unchanged if gravity is fixed and air buoyancy is negligible?

- Only the one held submerged
- Both
- Neither in any case
- The freely floating object

## Explanation

The free float adjusts its volume to keep buoyancy equal to weight. The other has fixed submerged volume and receives more buoyancy.

Higher density reduces the volume needed to support the same weight of the float. The restrained body still excludes the same volume, increasing the mass it displaces.
