A rigid hollow container is fully submerged. Its cavity fills with water through holes, and the water inside can flow freely to and from the surrounding water. What volume does the container now displace?

- The entire outer volume
- Only the cavity volume
- The wall material's volume, excluding the flooded cavity
- The sealed volume, whatever the cavity

## Explanation

Because water fills the cavity, the solid displaces only the volume of its walls. For example, with an external volume of $4\,\mathrm L$ and a flooded cavity of $1\,\mathrm L$, the displaced volume is $3\,\mathrm L$. A sealed cavity of the same size would also displace that internal litre.
