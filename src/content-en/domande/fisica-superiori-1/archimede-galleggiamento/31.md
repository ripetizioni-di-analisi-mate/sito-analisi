A rigid hollow container fills completely with water through holes, and the inside water communicates freely with the outside. What volume now excludes water?

- The entire outer volume
- Only the cavity volume
- The wall material's volume, excluding the flooded cavity
- The sealed volume, whatever the cavity

## Explanation

Water occupies the cavity and so that part is not displaced. The excluded volume is the space taken by the solid material.
