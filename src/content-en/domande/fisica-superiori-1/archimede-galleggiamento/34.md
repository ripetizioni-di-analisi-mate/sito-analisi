To recover displaced volume using only collected overflow, what initial condition is needed in the overflow-vessel method?

- The vessel must be empty
- The initial level must already reach the overflow outlet
- The body must be denser than every fluid
- The vessel must be square

## Explanation

If the initial level is below the outlet, some displaced volume fills the gap and is missing from the collected liquid.
