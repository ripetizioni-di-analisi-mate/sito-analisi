A body of mean density $0.72\,\mathrm{kg/L}$ floats in liquid of density $0.9\,\mathrm{kg/L}$. What percentage of its volume is exposed?

- $8\,\%$
- $72\,\%$
- $20\,\%$
- $80\,\%$

## Explanation

The density ratio is eight tenths and gives the immersed part. Take one minus that ratio and convert to a percentage.

$$
100\left(1-\frac{0.72}{0.9}\right)\%=20\%.
$$
