Two fully submerged bodies at rest are connected only by a taut vertical line. The upper one weighs $22\,\mathrm N$ and receives $30\,\mathrm N$ of buoyancy. The lower one weighs $24\,\mathrm N$ and receives $16\,\mathrm N$ of buoyancy. What is the tension in the line between them?

- $0\,\mathrm N$
- $16\,\mathrm N$
- $8\,\mathrm N$
- $24\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

On the upper body, the line pulls downwards to balance $(30-22)\,\mathrm N=8\,\mathrm N$. On the lower body the line pulls upwards with the same magnitude, balancing $(24-16)\,\mathrm N=8\,\mathrm N$. For the two bodies taken together, the line forces are internal and cancel.
