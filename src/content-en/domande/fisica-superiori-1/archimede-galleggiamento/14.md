A rigid body fully submerged in incompressible water is lowered $8\,\mathrm m$ while staying clear of the bottom. Density and $g$ stay constant. How does buoyancy change?

- It increases by the same factor as absolute pressure
- It stays unchanged
- It decreases because weight grows
- It becomes zero if the body does not float

## Explanation

The body was already fully submerged. Density, field, and displaced volume stay constant, so buoyancy is unchanged.
