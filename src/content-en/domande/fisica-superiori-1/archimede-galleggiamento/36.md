An object weighing $54\,\mathrm N$ can displace at most $5\,\mathrm L$ of water. Each litre of water weighs $10\,\mathrm N$. Is any submerged volume in equilibrium without constraints?

- Yes, with $4.6\,\mathrm L$ immersed
- Yes, with $5\,\mathrm L$ immersed
- Yes, using $5.4\,\mathrm L$ immersed
- No, maximum buoyancy is $50\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

Before accepting a calculated volume, check the maximum available. The volume formally needed would be five point four litres, beyond the external volume. Even fully submerged the body lacks four newtons of support, so those forces alone cannot maintain rest.

$$
F_{A,\max}=5\cdot10\,\mathrm N=50\,\mathrm N<54\,\mathrm N.
$$
