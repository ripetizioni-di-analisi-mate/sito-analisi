Two rigid, sealed objects have the same weight. Object A floats with some volume above the water. Object B is released from rest while fully submerged in the same water and starts to sink. Which object has the greater external volume?

- B, because it goes fully under water
- They have equal volumes because they weigh the same
- It depends on how deep B was released
- A, because it already displaces enough water with only part of its volume

## Explanation

The submerged part of A displaces water whose weight equals the shared weight of the objects. B sinks because even its fully submerged volume displaces water of lower weight. The full volume of B is therefore smaller even than the submerged part of A.
