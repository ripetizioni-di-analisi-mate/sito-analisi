A spring scale reads less after the stationary sample is immersed. Which force is directly tracked by the change in the scale reading?

- The sample's gravitational weight
- Gravitational force per unit mass
- The tension indicated by the instrument
- The pressure force on the sample’s lower face

## Explanation

Upward buoyancy now supports some of the load. The string can exert less tension while weight stays the same.
