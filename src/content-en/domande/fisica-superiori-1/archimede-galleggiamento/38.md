A sample hangs from above as immersion slowly increases. How does tension change while the line stays taut and the liquid is uniform?

- It increases
- It decreases
- It always stays the same
- It becomes weight multiplied by volume

## Explanation

Displaced volume and buoyancy increase. Weight stays fixed, leaving a smaller share for the string.
