A rigid sample hangs from a string and is lowered slowly into a uniform liquid. How does tension change as the submerged volume increases and the string stays taut?

- It increases
- It decreases
- It always stays the same
- It becomes weight multiplied by volume

## Explanation

Displaced volume and buoyancy increase. Weight stays fixed, leaving a smaller share for the string.

Once the body is fully submerged, lowering it farther leaves the displaced volume and hence the reading unchanged. Before full submersion, tension may already reach zero and the string can go slack.
