A bottom line restraining a submerged body lighter than displaced liquid is cut. With all other forces initially unchanged, which way does the resultant point immediately afterward?

- Downward
- Upward
- Horizontal
- It stays zero

## Explanation

Removing the downward line force leaves buoyancy larger than weight. The resultant points up.
