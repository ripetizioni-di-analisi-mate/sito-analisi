An upright block of constant section has $18\,\mathrm{cm}$ immersed and $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ exposed in water. In a new liquid its immersed fraction falls to $0.6$. What height is exposed?

- $5.4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $9.6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $14.4\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Total height remains twenty-four centimetres. Four tenths of that height is exposed in the new equilibrium because cross-section is constant.

$$
h_e=(18+6)(1-0.6)\,\mathrm{cm}=9.6\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$

To check the sum, calculate the new submerged height, $0.6\cdot24\,\mathrm{cm}=14.4\,\mathrm{cm}$. The two heights add to the original twenty-four centimetres.
