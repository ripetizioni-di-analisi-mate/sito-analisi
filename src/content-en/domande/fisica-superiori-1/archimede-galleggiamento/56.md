Which geometric condition ensures that a float's submerged height fraction equals its submerged volume fraction at every waterline?

- For every shape, because height and volume are always proportional
- Only if water density is zero
- When half the volume is above water, whatever the shape
- When the horizontal cross-section is constant throughout the height in that orientation

## Explanation

At constant area, multiply height by that area for volume. If cross-section varies, use the actual volumes of the two parts.

For constant area, $V_i=Ah_i$ and $V=Ah$. The ratio then matches the height ratio at any level because the same area cancels.
