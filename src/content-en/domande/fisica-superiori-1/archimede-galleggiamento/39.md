In a uniform liquid of density $850\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ an object of volume $0.012\,\mathrm m^3$ stays suspended fully submerged without ropes. What mass has it in the ideal model?

- $1.02\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $10.2\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $102\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Free suspension requires weight and buoyancy magnitudes to match. In full immersion the displaced volume is the whole external volume. Dividing the equality by field strength makes the mass equal to the displaced fluid mass. The body's mean density equals the liquid density.

$$
m=\rho_f V_i=(850\,\mathrm{kg/m^3})(0.012\,\mathrm m^3)=10.2\,\mathrm{kg}.
$$
