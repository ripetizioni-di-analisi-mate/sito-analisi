A sealed body stays suspended without strings in a liquid of density $0.80\,\mathrm{kg/L}$ while fully submerged. Some internal ballast is removed and the body is resealed with the same external volume. At its new equilibrium at the surface, $3.0\,\mathrm L$ is above the liquid. How much mass was removed?

- $3.0\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $3.75\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $2.4\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.8\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Initially the displaced liquid had the same mass as the body. The body now displaces $3.0\,\mathrm L$ less and is still in equilibrium. The supported mass therefore fell by $0.80\cdot3.0\,\mathrm{kg}=2.4\,\mathrm{kg}$. This is the mass of ballast that was removed.
