A watertight object of volume $5\,\mathrm L$ is held fully submerged first in water of density $1\,\mathrm{kg/L}$ and then in oil of density $0.8\,\mathrm{kg/L}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$, how much does buoyancy decrease?

- $0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N}$
- $50\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Full immersion displaces the same volume in both tests. The mass of displaced fluid changes, falling by one kilogram here. Multiply by the field to find the buoyancy change. During free flotation volume could adjust, whereas here it is fixed.

$$
\Delta F_A=5\,\mathrm L\cdot10\,\mathrm{N/kg}\cdot(1-0.8)\,\mathrm{kg/L}=10\,\mathrm N.
$$
