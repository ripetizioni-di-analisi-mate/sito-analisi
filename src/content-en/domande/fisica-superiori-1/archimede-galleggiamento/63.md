In liquid A a buoyant force of $18\,\mathrm N$ is measured for $2\,\mathrm L$ submerged. In liquid B the buoyant force is $32\,\mathrm N$ for $4\,\mathrm L$ submerged. The gravitational field is the same. What is the density ratio A divided by B?

- $0.5625$
- $2$
- $1.125$
- $0.8889$

## Explanation

Compare buoyancy per litre. In A it is $18/2\,\mathrm{N/L}=9\,\mathrm{N/L}$, and in B it is $32/4\,\mathrm{N/L}=8\,\mathrm{N/L}$. With the same gravity, the density ratio is $9/8=1.125$. The measured total buoyancy is larger in B because that trial displaces more liquid.
