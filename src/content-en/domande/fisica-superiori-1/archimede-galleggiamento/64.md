A $6\,\mathrm{kg}$ payload is removed from a platform floating in water of density $1\,\mathrm{kg/L}$. How many litres does immersed volume decrease?

- $0.6\,\mathrm L$
- $12\,\mathrm L$
- $60\,\mathrm L$
- $6\,\mathrm L$

## Explanation

Removing weight reduces required displaced mass by the same amount. One kilogram of water corresponds to one litre.

$$
\Delta V_i=\frac{6\,\mathrm{kg}}{1\,\mathrm{kg/L}}=6\,\mathrm L.
$$
