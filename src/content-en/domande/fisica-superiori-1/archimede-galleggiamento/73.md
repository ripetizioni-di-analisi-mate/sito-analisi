A float's new cargo itself enters the water. What assumption changes in the simplified calculation using only the float's submerged volume?

- The cargo can itself displace water and contribute buoyancy
- The cargo loses its weight
- Water density vanishes
- The float's buoyancy always doubles

## Explanation

Using only the float's volume assumes dry cargo. If the cargo displaces water, include its own submerged volume.
