During hydrostatic weighing the specimen accidentally touches the bottom. Why can't the reading be interpreted using $T=P-F_A$?

- Weight always changes on contact
- Liquid density becomes the body's density
- The scale now measures absolute pressure
- A new contact force enters the vertical balance

## Explanation

The bottom can contribute a support force. The difference between weight and tension then has another contribution besides buoyancy.
