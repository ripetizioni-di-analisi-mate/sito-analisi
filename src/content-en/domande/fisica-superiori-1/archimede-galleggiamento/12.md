A boat with total mass $800\,\mathrm{kg}$ floats first in fresh water and then in denser salt water. At the same mass, what happens to buoyancy?

- It increases because the liquid is denser
- It decreases because less volume is immersed
- It becomes zero in salt water
- It remains equal to the boat's weight

## Explanation

Buoyancy equals the weight in both equilibria and stays the same. A smaller volume suffices in salt water.

The displaced liquid must have a mass of $800\,\mathrm{kg}$ in both tests. Each litre of salt water carries more mass, so the same mass takes up fewer litres of it.
