Two tanks containing the same water have constant horizontal cross-sections. Tank A has area $300\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and its level rises by $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ when an object is immersed. Tank B has area $100\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and its level rises by $4\,\mathrm{cm}$ with another object. The objects hang clear of the bottom. There is no spillage and one litre of water weighs $10\,\mathrm N$. What is the buoyant force in A minus the buoyant force in B?

- $-2\,\mathrm N$
- $20\,\mathrm N$
- $10\,\mathrm N$
- $2\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

In A the water displaced is $300\cdot2\,\mathrm{cm^3}=600\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. In B it is $100\cdot4\,\mathrm{cm^3}=400\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. The difference is $200\,\mathrm{cm^3}=0.20\,\mathrm L$, giving $2\,\mathrm N$ of buoyancy. The level rises less in A, where each centimetre represents more displaced water.
