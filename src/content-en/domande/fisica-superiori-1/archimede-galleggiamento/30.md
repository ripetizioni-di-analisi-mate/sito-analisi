An object weighs $50\,\mathrm N$ and hangs from a spring scale in a uniform liquid. When a quarter of its volume is submerged, the scale reads $45\,\mathrm N$. The object is slowly lowered until three quarters of its volume is submerged. What does the scale read now?

- $45\,\mathrm N$
- $35\,\mathrm N$
- $15\,\mathrm N$
- $65\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

With a quarter submerged, the liquid supports $(50-45)\,\mathrm N=5\,\mathrm N$. At three quarters the displaced volume triples, so buoyancy rises to $15\,\mathrm N$. The scale reads $(50-15)\,\mathrm N=35\,\mathrm N$. The weight stays fixed while the part supported by the liquid changes.
