Part of a body seals against the bottom, with no liquid underneath it. What assumption should be checked before applying the usual buoyancy formula directly?

- That water has no density
- That fluid pressure acts on the entire immersed boundary assumed by the model
- That body mass equals its weight
- That all pressure acts horizontally

## Explanation

The usual model sums pressures over the immersed boundary. A sealed bottom contact can remove a pressure contribution and introduce a contact force.

The force expected on a wetted lower face may be absent. In this experiment, identify which surfaces are touching the liquid before applying a formula for the net force.
