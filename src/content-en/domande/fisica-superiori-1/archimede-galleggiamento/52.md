A boat of fixed mass moves to an area with weaker gravity and continues floating in the same liquid. What happens to immersed volume?

- It decreases with field strength
- It increases with field strength
- It vanishes
- It remains the same

## Explanation

Weight and buoyancy both contain the factor $g$. The flotation balance sets volume from unchanged mass and liquid density.

The submerged volume is the mass of the boat divided by the liquid density. Weaker gravity reduces both forces, without changing the quotient.
