The first cell of `a` retains the first sample, and every subsequent cell contains the original sample minus its predecessor. For example, `{3, 5, -2, 5}` represents `{3, 8, 6, 11}`. Which loop reconstructs the four samples in the same array? Assume the sums are representable.

- `for (int i=3; i>0; --i) a[i] += a[i-1];`
- `for (int i=1; i<4; ++i) a[i] -= a[i-1];`
- `for (int i=0; i<3; ++i) a[i] += a[i+1];`
- `for (int i=1; i<4; ++i) a[i] += a[i-1];`

## Explanation

Before processing index $i$, the previously reconstructed sample is needed in `a[i-1]`. Moving right gives $5+3=8$, then $-2+8=6$, and finally $5+6=11$. In the opposite direction the preceding cell may still hold a difference.
