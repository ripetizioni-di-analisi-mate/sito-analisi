The maximum of each row of `m` is found and the two maxima are added. What is the result?

```c
int m[2][3] = {{3, 1, 4}, {-2, -1, -5}};
```

- $3$
- $4$
- $2$
- $0$

## Explanation

The first row has maximum $4$. In the second row, compare $-2$, $-1$ and $-5$. The greatest value is $-1$, so the sum is $4+(-1)=3$. Initializing the maximum to zero would fail for the second row because every cell is negative.
