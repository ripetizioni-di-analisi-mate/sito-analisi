What sequence of values does `a` hold at the end?

```c
int a[5] = {2, 4, 6, 8, 10};
for (int i = 4; i > 0; --i) {
    a[i] = a[i - 1];
}
```

- `2, 2, 2, 2, 2`
- `4, 6, 8, 10, 10`
- `2, 2, 4, 6, 8`
- `2, 4, 6, 8, 10`

## Explanation

The right-to-left traversal reads the previous cell before it is changed. Destinations $4$ down to $1$ therefore receive $8$, $6$, $4$ and $2$ in that order. Cell $0$ remains $2$. Copying in the opposite direction would propagate the first value.
