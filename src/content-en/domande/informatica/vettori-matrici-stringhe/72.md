A scanner visits the rows of matrix `m[3][4]` from top to bottom. It moves left to right on even rows and in the opposite direction on odd rows. The first cell has visit number zero. Which cell has visit number $6$?

- `m[1][2]`
- `m[1][1]`
- `m[0][2]`
- `m[2][1]`

## Explanation

Visits $0$ through $3$ belong to the first row. On row $1$, visit $4$ starts at column $3$, visit $5$ reaches column $2$, and visit $6$ reaches column $1$. The remainder on dividing by four counts steps within the row, which here is traversed from the right.
