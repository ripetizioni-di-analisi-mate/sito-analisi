In an adjacency matrix, `g[u][v]` equals $1$ if there is a direct step from `u` to `v`. How many intermediate indices `k` allow a walk from $0$ to $3$ in exactly two steps?

```c
int g[4][4] = {{0,1,1,0}, {0,0,0,1},
               {1,0,0,1}, {0,0,0,0}};
```

- $1$
- $3$
- $0$
- $2$

## Explanation

Row $0$ shows that indices $1$ and $2$ are reachable in one step. In column $3$, both row $1$ and row $2$ contain $1$. Both two-step walks, through $1$ and through $2$, therefore exist even though the direct connection from $0$ to $3$ is absent.
