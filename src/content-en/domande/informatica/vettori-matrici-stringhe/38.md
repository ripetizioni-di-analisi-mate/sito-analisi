For `s = "abcxba"` of length $6$, compare in order the characters at indices `i` and `5-i` for `i` from $0$ to $2$. What is the left index of the first pair with different characters?

- $3$
- $1$
- $2$
- $0$

## Explanation

The pairs at $0$ and $5$ and at $1$ and $4$ are `'a'` with `'a'` and `'b'` with `'b'`, respectively. The next pair uses $2$ and $3$ and compares `'c'` with `'x'`. The first left index that fails is therefore $2$.
