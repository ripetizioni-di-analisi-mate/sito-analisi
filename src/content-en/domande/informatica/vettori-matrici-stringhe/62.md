In the grid below, the top middle cell is replaced by the sum of its original neighbours that share an edge. Directions outside the grid are ignored. Which value is written?

```c
int g[2][3] = {{2, 7, 4}, {6, 3, 8}};
```

- $12$
- $9$
- $5$
- $13$

## Explanation

Cell `g[0][1]` has $2$ on its left, $4$ on its right, and $3$ below it. Moving up would leave the grid. The required sum is $2+4+3=9$, excluding the central value $7$ and diagonal neighbours.
