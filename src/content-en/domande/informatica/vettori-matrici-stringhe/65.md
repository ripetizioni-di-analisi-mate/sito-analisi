Table `P[r][c]` holds the sum of entries in the first `r` rows and first `c` columns, with both upper bounds excluded. Given `P[4][5]=38`, `P[1][5]=9`, `P[4][2]=13`, and `P[1][2]=4`, what is the sum over rows `[1,4)` and columns `[2,5)`?

- $24$
- $16$
- $20$
- $12$

## Explanation

From the total $38$, subtract the upper strip with sum $9$ and the left strip with sum $13$. Their common rectangle, with sum $4$, has now been subtracted twice. Add it back once to obtain $38-9-13+4=20$.
