Two bookings cover the index intervals `[0,3)` and `[2,5)`. Starting with six zero-initialized cells at indices 0 through 5, add $1$ at the beginning and subtract $1$ at the exclusive end of each interval. Which index from $0$ through $4$ has two active bookings when the values are accumulated from the left?

- $3$
- $4$
- $2$
- $1$

## Explanation

The increments at indices $0$ and $2$ bring the total to $1$ and then $2$. The first booking ends before index $3$, where the total drops back to $1$. The sequence of counts over the five indices is therefore $1,1,2,1,1$.
