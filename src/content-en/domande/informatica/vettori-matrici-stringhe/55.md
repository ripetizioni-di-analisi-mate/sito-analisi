Coefficient `c[j]` multiplies the degree-`j` power in a polynomial. What value does this evaluation produce at `x = -2`?

```c
int c[] = {2, 3, 0, 1};
int p = 0;
for (int j = 3; j >= 0; --j)
    p = -2 * p + c[j];
```

- $-6$
- $-12$
- $0$
- $12$

## Explanation

The loop visits coefficients from the highest degree down to the constant term. After successive iterations, `p` equals $1$, $-2$, $7$, and $-12$. Multiplication by $-2$ comes before each addition and accounts for the degree of the coefficient.
