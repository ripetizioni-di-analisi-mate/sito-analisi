Assume `<string.h>` is available. Which property of the result of `strcmp("tram", "tra")` is guaranteed by C17 on every conforming implementation?

- It is zero.
- It is exactly $1$.
- It is strictly positive.
- It is strictly negative.

## Explanation

The first three characters agree. The first string then has the nonzero character for the letter `m`, while the second has its zero terminator. Comparing them as unsigned characters makes the result positive. The interface specifies the sign and leaves its magnitude unspecified.
