In the array `{2, 2, 5, 1, 3, 3, 7, 0}`, what is the length of the longest contiguous nondecreasing run?

- $4$
- $5$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

A run ends only when the next value is smaller than its predecessor. The decreases are from $5$ to $1$ and from $7$ to $0$. The three runs have lengths $3,4,1$, and both consecutive occurrences of $3$ belong to the middle run.
