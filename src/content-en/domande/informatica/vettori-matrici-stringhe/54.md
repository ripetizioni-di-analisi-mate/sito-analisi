Two arrays of four codes use only values from $0$ to $3$. For each code `v`, compute `d[v]` as its frequency in the first array minus its frequency in the second. This gives `{0, -2, 1, 1}`. What is the minimum number of single-element replacements in the first array needed to match the frequencies?

- $3$
- $1$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

The first array is missing two occurrences of code $1$, with one extra $2$ and one extra $3$. Each replacement can supply at most one missing occurrence. Replacing one $2$ and one $3$ by two $1$s attains the lower bound of two replacements.
