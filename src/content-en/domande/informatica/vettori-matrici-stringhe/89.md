Characters at indices $1$ and $2$ of `s` are deleted by shifting the suffix to the left with `memmove`, which permits overlapping ranges. What is the minimum byte count needed to transfer the terminator as well? Assume `<string.h>` is available.

```c
char s[] = "abcdef";
/* memmove(s + 1, s + 3, ...); */
```

- $5$
- $2$
- $3$
- $4$

## Explanation

The suffix begins at index $3$ with the letter `d`. The data to move are `d`, `e`, `f`, and the final null, giving four C bytes. Copying only the three letters would leave an old letter at index $4$ and the new string would not end immediately after `f`.
