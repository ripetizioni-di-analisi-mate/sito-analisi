Which strings do `a` and `b` contain, in order, after the final statement?

```c
char a[] = "go";
char b[] = "go";
a[0] = 'n';
```

- `no`, `no`
- `no`, `go`
- `go`, `no`
- `go`, `go`

## Explanation

The declarations create two distinct arrays and initialize each with a copy of `go` and the final null. The assignment changes only `a[0]` to `'n'`. The null in `a` stays in place, so the first string is `no` and the second is still `go`.
