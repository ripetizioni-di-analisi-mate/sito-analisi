The matrix product `C = A B` is formed. What is entry `C[1][0]` for the following arrays?

```c
int A[2][3] = {{1, 0, 2}, {2, -1, 3}};
int B[3][2] = {{1, 5}, {4, 0}, {-1, 2}};
```

- $-8$
- $-5$
- $11$
- $5$

## Explanation

Use row $1$ of `A`, namely $2,-1,3$, and column $0$ of `B`, namely $1,4,-1$. The pairwise products are $2,-4,-3$. Their sum is $-5$. The column index selects the fixed column of the second matrix.
