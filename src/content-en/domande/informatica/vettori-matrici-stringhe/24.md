For `int m[4][3]`, number the cells from zero in row-major storage order. Which valid cell has linear number $9$?

- `m[3][0]`
- `m[2][3]`
- `m[3][1]`
- `m[0][3]`

## Explanation

Each row contains $3$ integers. Dividing $9$ by $3$ gives quotient $3$ and remainder $0$. The quotient gives the row and the remainder gives the column, so the cell is `m[3][0]`. Column index $3$ is outside every row.
