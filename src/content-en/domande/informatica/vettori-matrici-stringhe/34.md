Which string starts at `word[0]` after the fragment?

```c
char word[7] = "strada";
word[2] = word[5];
word[5] = '\0';
```

- `stada`
- `staad`
- `strad`
- `staada`

## Explanation

Cell $5$ initially contains `'a'`. The first assignment copies it to position $2$, which previously held `'r'`. The second writes the null at position $5$. Cells $0$ to $4$ therefore form `staad`, with two consecutive `'a'` characters.
