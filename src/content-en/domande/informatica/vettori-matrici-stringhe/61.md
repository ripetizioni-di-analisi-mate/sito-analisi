Matrix `a` has two rows and three columns. It is rotated clockwise through a quarter turn so that its top row becomes the rightmost column of `b`. Where does cell `a[1][2]` end up?

- `b[2][1]`
- `b[2][0]`
- `b[0][1]`
- `b[1][2]`

## Explanation

The original columns become rows of the new matrix, so column $2$ gives row $2$. Original row $0$ goes to the right and row $1$ to the left. The new column is therefore $0$, giving `b[2][0]`.
