Variable `sum` holds the sum of the three cells at indices `i-3, i-2, i-1`, with `3 <= i < n`. Which assignment moves the window one cell to the right? All entries and intermediate results fit in `int`.

- `sum = sum - a[i-3] + a[i-1];`
- `sum = sum - a[i] + a[i-3];`
- `sum = sum - a[i-3] + a[i];`
- `sum = sum - a[i-2] + a[i];`

## Explanation

The new window covers $i-2,i-1,i$. The cells at indices $i-2$ and $i-1$ remain in the sum exactly once each. The outgoing term is therefore `a[i-3]` and the incoming one is `a[i]`.
