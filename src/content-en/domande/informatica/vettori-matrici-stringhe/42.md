The sum of an array’s elements over index range $[0,6)$ is $12$. The sum over $[0,2)$ is $4$, and the sum over $[4,6)$ is $-1$. What is the sum over $[2,4)$?

- $7$
- $8$
- $17$
- $9$

## Explanation

The three ranges partition $[0,6)$ because each shared boundary is included only in the range to its right. With $S$ the middle sum, $4+S+(-1)=12$. Thus $S=12-4+1=9$.
