With `<string.h>` available, what do `buf[3]` and `buf[4]` contain afterward?

```c
char buf[] = "ABCDE";
strncpy(buf, "xy", 4);
```

- `'D'` and `'E'`
- `'y'` and `'E'`
- `'\0'` and `'\0'`
- `'\0'` and `'E'`

## Explanation

The source has two visible characters. With a count of $4$, `strncpy` writes `x` and `y` into the first two cells and pads the next two with null characters. Cell $4$ lies beyond the four destination cells involved and retains the letter `E`.
