In the fragment, `j` is the length of the prefix of `p` matched in order in `s`, possibly skipping characters of `s`. What is its final value?

```c
char s[] = "cabd", p[] = "cadb";
int j = 0;
for (int i = 0; s[i] != '\0' && p[j] != '\0'; ++i)
    if (s[i] == p[j]) ++j;
```

- $1$
- $4$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

The first two positions match `c` and `a`. The following `b` is skipped because the expected character at that point is `d`. The final `d` brings `j` to $3$. Completing `cadb` would require another `b` later in the string.
