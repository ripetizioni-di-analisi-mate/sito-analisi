Given `int a[2][3]`, execute `b[j][i] = a[i][j]` for every cell of `a`. Which declaration of `b` has the minimum capacity to hold all the assignments?

- `int b[2][3];`
- `int b[3][2];`
- `int b[2][2];`
- `int b[3][3];`

## Explanation

Index `j` takes values $0$, $1$ and $2$ and becomes the row index of `b`. Index `i` takes $0$ and $1$ and becomes the column index. At least three rows and two columns are needed, so the minimum capacity is $3\cdot2=6$ integers.
