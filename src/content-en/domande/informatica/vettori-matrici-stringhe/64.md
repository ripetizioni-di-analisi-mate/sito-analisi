A window two rows tall and three columns wide must stay entirely inside a four-row, five-column matrix. How many different cells may serve as its top-left corner with the orientation fixed?

- $9$
- $6$
- $12$
- $15$

## Explanation

The starting row index can be $0$, $1$, or $2$, since the following row is also needed. The starting column index can likewise be $0$, $1$, or $2$, since two further columns are needed. These choices are independent, giving three times three, or $9$ positions.
