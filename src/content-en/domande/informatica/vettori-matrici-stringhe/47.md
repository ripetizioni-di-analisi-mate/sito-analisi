A frequency array contains `{1, 2, 0, 3, 1}`. The entry at index `v` counts observations equal to `v`. What is the median of the seven observations?

- $3$
- $2$
- $4$
- $1$

## Explanation

The median is the fourth observation in increasing order. Values $0$ and $1$ together occupy the first three positions. Value $2$ has frequency zero, so positions four through six are occupied by $3$.
