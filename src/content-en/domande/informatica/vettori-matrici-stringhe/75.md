A three-by-four matrix initially has only two zero entries, at `[0][1]` and `[2][1]`. In one logical step, every cell in the row or column of an original zero is set to zero. How many cells, including the two original ones, are zero at the end?

- $12$
- $9$
- $10$
- $8$

## Explanation

Rows $0$ and $2$ become entirely zero, contributing $8$ cells. Both original zeros identify the same column $1$. Only the cell in row $1$ of that column has not yet been counted, bringing the total to $9$.
