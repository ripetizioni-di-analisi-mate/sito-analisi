Only the segment at indices `[1, 5)` of `{9, 2, 4, 7, 8, 1}` is to be reversed. Which index pairs should be swapped to do so while touching each cell in that segment exactly once?

- `(1,2)` and `(3,4)`
- `(1,5)` and `(2,4)`
- `(0,4)` and `(1,3)`
- `(1,4)` and `(2,3)`

## Explanation

The segment indices are $1,2,3,4$. Reversal pairs the first with the last and the second with the next-to-last, so the block $2,4,7,8$ becomes $8,7,4,2$. Indices $0$ and $5$ keep the values outside the segment.
