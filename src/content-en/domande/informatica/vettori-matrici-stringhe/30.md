Which declaration immediately produces a C string containing exactly the characters of `abc`, followed by a terminator inside the array?

- `char s[3] = "abc";`
- `char s[4] = {'a', 'b', 'c'};`
- `char s[5] = {'a', 'b', 'c', 'a', 'b'};`
- `char s[4] = {'a', 'b', 'c', 'd'};`

## Explanation

With three initializers in a four-cell array, the first omitted cell, `s[3]`, receives zero. A capacity of three cells fits the characters of `abc` and leaves no room for the terminator. The other two lists fill every position with non-null characters.
