Calculate `m[0][1] + m[1][1]` after initialization.

```c
int m[2][3] = {{9}, {2, 4}};
```

- $6$
- $2$
- $4$
- $13$

## Explanation

Each inner list initializes one row. In row $0$, the two cells after $9$ receive zero. In row $1$, $2$ and $4$ occupy the first two cells. The sum therefore uses $0$ and $4$, giving $4$.
