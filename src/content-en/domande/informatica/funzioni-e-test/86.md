An API represents prices as whole cents. The function `add_tax(c)` must add 10% of the price **rounded down to a whole number of cents**. For `c == 29`, which oracle can be worked out by hand without duplicating the implementation code?

- 3 cents
- 31 cents
- 29 cents
- 32 cents

## Explanation

Ten percent of 29 cents is 2.9 cents. The required whole-cent tax is therefore 2 cents and the total is 29 + 2 = 31. Rounding to the nearest cent would instead give a tax of 3 cents and a total of 32.
