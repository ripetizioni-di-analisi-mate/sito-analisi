The implementation is exactly one of `a-b`, the absolute value of `a-b`, and `a+b`. Only one call on integers from 0 through 9 is allowed, and its numeric result is observed. Which pair distinguishes all three implementations?

- `(5,2)`
- `(2,5)`
- `(0,4)`
- `(3,3)`

## Explanation

For (2,5) the three results are −3, 3 and 7 respectively. Each value identifies a single implementation. With (0,4), for example, both the absolute difference and the sum would give 4, leaving two possibilities.
