A suite can choose among four tests. Test A detects only defect 1, B detects 1 and 2, C detects 2 and 3, and D detects only 2. Defects are introduced individually. Which of the listed test pairs can detect each of the three?

- B and C
- C and D
- A and B
- B and D

## Explanation

Defect 3 occurs only in the coverage of C. Among the listed pairs that include C, adding B also covers defect 1, whereas adding D leaves 1 undetected. The union of the defects detected by B and C therefore comprises the entire required set.
