The postcondition of $f$ permits $2\leq z\leq9$, where $z$ is the result. The precondition of $g$ requires $0\leq z\leq8$. Which value is allowed by the first contract and violates the second?

- $2$
- $5$
- $9$
- $8$

## Explanation

The composition is guaranteed if every value permitted by the first postcondition is accepted by the second contract. The values $2$, $5$, and $8$ also satisfy $z\leq8$. The value $9$ is permitted by the first interval and exceeds the maximum of the second. It is therefore a counterexample to a guarantee based only on the contracts.
