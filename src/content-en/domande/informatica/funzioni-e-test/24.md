A contract accepts integers $n\geq3$ whose mathematical cost $2n+5$ is at most $30$. What is the greatest admissible $n$?

- $13$
- $11$
- $15$
- $12$

## Explanation

The cost inequality gives $2n\leq25$. The greatest admissible integer is therefore $12$, which also satisfies $n\geq3$. As a check, at $n=12$ the cost is $29$, while at $n=13$ it rises to $31$.
