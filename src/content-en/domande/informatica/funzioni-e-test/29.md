The integer domain runs from $0$ to $20$. The bands change at $10$ and $15$, both of which belong to the following band. Which set contains the two adjacent integers on opposite sides of each threshold?

- $\{9,11,14,16\}$
- $\{0,10,15,20\}$
- $\{9,10,14,15\}$
- $\{10,11,15,16\}$

## Explanation

The first transition is between `9` and `10`, because `10` is the first value in the next band. The second is between `14` and `15`. Testing both immediate sides of the two thresholds therefore requires exactly those four values.
