A transfer function must call `check` and, only if it succeeds, `send`, in that order and exactly once each when applicable. Test doubles record calls in a log that starts empty. Which pair of logs checks the entire interaction for a successful and a failed check?

- Success `send, check` and failure `check`.
- Success `check, send` and failure `check, send`.
- Success `check, send` and failure `check`.
- Success `check, send, send` and failure `check`.

## Explanation

Success must show the check followed by exactly one send. Failure must show the check alone. The complete log also distinguishes a duplicate send or reversed order, defects that an observation of the final result alone might conceal.
