The specification of `mid(lo,hi)` requires the average rounded down when `0 <= lo && lo <= hi`. Which `int` expression works over the **entire** domain, including `hi == INT_MAX`?

- `lo / 2 + hi / 2`
- `lo + (hi - lo) / 2`
- `(lo + hi) / 2`
- `hi - (hi - lo) / 2`

## Explanation

The difference is between 0 and `hi`, and adding half of it to `lo` gives a value no greater than `hi`. This yields the lower midpoint. The direct sum can exceed `INT_MAX`, separate divisions lose a unit for two odd endpoints, and subtracting from `hi` rounds up when the difference is odd.
