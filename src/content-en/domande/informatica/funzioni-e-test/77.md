In the decision `alarm = smoke || heat;`, both inputs are 0 or 1. Tests must demonstrate that changing **only** `heat` can change `alarm`. Which test pair shows this?

- `(smoke,heat) = (0,1), (1,0)`
- `(smoke,heat) = (0,0), (1,1)`
- `(smoke,heat) = (1,0), (1,1)`
- `(smoke,heat) = (0,0), (0,1)`

## Explanation

With no smoke, the decision follows the heat sensor directly. Holding `smoke` at 0 while changing `heat` from 0 to 1 therefore produces both outcomes. Holding `smoke` at 1 would leave the alarm active regardless of heat.
