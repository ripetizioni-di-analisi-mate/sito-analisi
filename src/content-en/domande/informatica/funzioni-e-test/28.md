For integers from $16$ to $21$, the specification uses different rules depending on parity and on the comparison `x >= 18`. What is the minimum number of one-input tests covering all combinations that occur?

- $2$
- $3$
- $6$
- $4$

## Explanation

The threshold divides the values into two bands, each containing at least one even and one odd value. Values $16$ and $17$ represent the two parities below the threshold. Values $18$ and $19$ represent them from the threshold upward. There are four nonempty classes, and each call covers one.
