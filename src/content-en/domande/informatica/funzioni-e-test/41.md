For a call in the domain, the postcondition requires an integer $y$ from $1$ to $4$ with $3y\geq7$. What is the smallest result compatible with the contract?

- $2$
- $3$
- $4$
- $5$

## Explanation

Admissible results must be integers from $1$ to $4$ and satisfy $3y\geq7$. At $y=2$ the product is $6$, which rules out both $2$ and all smaller values. At $y=3$ the product is $9$, which satisfies the bound. The value $4$ is admissible and greater than $3$.
