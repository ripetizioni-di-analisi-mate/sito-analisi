A stateless function accepts precisely the integer intervals `(lo,hi)` satisfying `0 <= lo && lo <= hi && hi <= 4`. Each test makes one call on one pair. How many calls are needed for exhaustive testing of the domain?

- $10$
- $15$
- $25$
- $20$

## Explanation

Fixing `lo` at 0, 1, 2, 3 and 4 gives respectively 5, 4, 3, 2 and 1 choices for `hi`. Their sum is 15. Counting only intervals with distinct endpoints would omit the five singleton cases admitted by the contract.
