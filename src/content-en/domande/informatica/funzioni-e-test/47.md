A function must apply three distinct fees to integer levels 0, 1 and 2. The designer proposes `int fee(_Bool level);`, and the body can observe only the parameter. What problem arises in C17?

- Level 2 arrives as 0 because it is even.
- Calling the function with 2 always requires a diagnostic.
- Level 2 is preserved until the first comparison in the body.
- Levels 1 and 2 both arrive as 1.

## Explanation

Conversion to the parameter type takes place before the body is entered. Zero becomes 0, and every nonzero value becomes 1. The interface has already lost the information needed to distinguish the second fee from the third.
