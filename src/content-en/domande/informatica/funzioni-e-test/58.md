A register shows 4 occupied slots. The contract for `reserve(k)` requires failure without changing the register if the total capacity of 6 would be exceeded. Which oracle checks both parts of the contract after `reserve(3)`?

- Success status and 4 occupied slots.
- Failure status regardless of the register.
- Failure status and 4 slots still occupied.
- Failure status and 6 occupied slots.

## Explanation

The request would bring the total to 7, above the limit of 6. The contract therefore requires failure and no change, checked by comparing occupancy with its initial value of 4. Checking the status code alone would miss a partial update.
