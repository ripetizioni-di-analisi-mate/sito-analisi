Two stacks grow from opposite ends of the same array of $9$ cells. The left stack occupies indices $0$ through $2$ inclusive, and the right stack occupies $6$ through $8$ inclusive. Without popping, how many additional pushes in total can be divided between the stacks before the array is full?

- $2$
- $3$
- $6$
- $4$

## Explanation

Each stack occupies three cells, so six cells are in use. The free indices are $3,4,5$. Each push from either end consumes one of those three shared free cells.
