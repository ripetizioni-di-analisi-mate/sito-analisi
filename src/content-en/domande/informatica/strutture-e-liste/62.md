A function promises an independent list copy. It allocates fresh nodes into a temporary chain and publishes its head only after the entire operation succeeds. Allocation of the fourth node fails after three successful allocations. Which action meets the contract and accounts for the allocated memory?

- Publish the three temporary nodes as a complete copy
- Free the three temporary nodes, retain the original and report failure
- Report failure and discard the temporary head
- Free the first three nodes of the original

## Explanation

The temporary chain owns three separate allocations, none of which has been handed to the caller. On failure the function can therefore free all three. The original nodes still belong to their owner, and the destination must retain its previous state.
