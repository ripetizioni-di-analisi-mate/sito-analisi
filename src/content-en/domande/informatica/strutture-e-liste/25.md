`head` designates a list of four live automatic nodes. After this fragment, how many nodes are reachable from `head` and `old`, respectively? Include `<stddef.h>`.

```c
struct Node *old = head;
head = old->next;
old->next = NULL;
```

- $(4,1)$
- $(3,1)$
- $(3,4)$
- $(1,3)$

## Explanation

`old` designates the first of the four nodes, and its successor is the second node. Moving `head` to that successor leaves three nodes reachable from the new head. Assigning `NULL` to the successor of `old` then isolates a one-node chain.
