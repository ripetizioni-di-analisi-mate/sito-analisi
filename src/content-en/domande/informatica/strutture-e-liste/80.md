A valid linked queue contains $n\ge 6$ nodes and stores only the front and rear pointers, with forward `next` links. To read the fifth item in FIFO order starting from the front, how many moves to a successor are needed, counting the first item as position one?

- $n-1$
- $1$
- $4$
- $5$

## Explanation

The front pointer already designates the item in position one. One move reaches the second item and four reach the fifth. The rear pointer simplifies appending but does not provide direct access to an arbitrary interior position.
