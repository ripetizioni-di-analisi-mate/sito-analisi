Three distinct live nodes have links `A.next = &B`, `B.next = &C` and `C.next = &A`. The head is `&A`. How many distinct nodes are encountered, and is `NULL` reached by following only `next`?

- Three distinct nodes and arrival at `NULL`.
- Two distinct nodes and arrival at `NULL`.
- Two distinct nodes and no arrival at `NULL`.
- Three distinct nodes and no arrival at `NULL`.

## Explanation

Following the head visits nodes `A`, `B` and `C`. The successor of `C` is `A` again, so traversal repeats the same path. There are three distinct nodes, and the chain violates the condition that it end at a null pointer.
