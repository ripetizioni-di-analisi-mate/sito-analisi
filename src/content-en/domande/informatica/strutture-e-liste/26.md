The head of an owning list points to a dynamically allocated node. Only that node should be freed and the rest must stay available. Which procedure meets both conditions?

- Save the successor, update the head and free the old node.
- Free the head and then read its successor.
- Free the successor while holding the same head.
- Clear the head and free the now-null pointer.

## Explanation

Removal must preserve the link to the rest of the list and free the outgoing node. Reading the successor requires the original node to remain live. After saving the link, the head can move and the old node can be released.
