Two distinct stacks contain `A,B,C` and `X` respectively, listed bottom to top. All items from the first are moved to the second by popping the source top and immediately pushing it onto the destination. What is the destination stack’s final order from bottom to top?

- `X,C,B,A`
- `A,B,C,X`
- `C,B,A,X`
- `X,A,B,C`

## Explanation

Popping the source yields `C`, then `B`, then `A`. Every push adds an item above the existing ones and leaves `X` at the bottom. The final bottom-to-top order is therefore `X,C,B,A`.
