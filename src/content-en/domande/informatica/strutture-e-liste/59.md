Two borrowed head pointers allow the chains `A→B→D→E` and `C→D→E` to be read. Equal letters denote the same live node and no nodes are freed. How many distinct physical nodes are reachable from the two heads together?

- $7$
- $2$
- $5$
- $4$

## Explanation

The separate traversals count four and three positions. The two nodes in the shared suffix, `D` and `E`, occur in both traversals. The number of distinct objects is therefore $4+3-2=5$.
