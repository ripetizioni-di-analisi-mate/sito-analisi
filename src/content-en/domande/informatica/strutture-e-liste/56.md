The list `1→2→3→4→5` is traversed in order, saving the successor before each move. Every odd-keyed node is detached and inserted at the head of an initially empty list. Even-keyed nodes retain their original order. What are the final odd-key list and the remaining list, respectively?

- `5→3→1` and `2→4`
- `1→3→5` and `2→4`
- `5→3→1` and `4→2`
- `1→3→5` and `4→2`

## Explanation

The odd keys are visited in the order $1,3,5$. Each head insertion places the latest node first, reversing that sequence. The two even-keyed nodes retain their relative positions in the original list.
