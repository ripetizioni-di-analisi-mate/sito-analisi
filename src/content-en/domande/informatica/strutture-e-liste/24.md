The chain of live nodes is `a`, `b`, `c`. A new node is inserted immediately after `b`, preserving existing order. What address does the new node’s `next` member receive?

- `&a`
- `&b`
- `NULL`
- `&c`

## Explanation

Inserting after `b` preserves its previous outbound link as the link leaving the new node. The old successor of `b` is `c`, so the new node points to `c`. The link of `b` changes to point to the new node.
