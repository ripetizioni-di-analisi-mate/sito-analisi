A stack of capacity $3$ contains $4,7$ from bottom to top. An attempt is made to push $9$, then $2$, and then one element is popped. A push on a full stack leaves it unchanged. What pair gives the removed value and the final length?

- $(2,2)$
- $(9,3)$
- $(7,2)$
- $(9,2)$

## Explanation

The first push changes the occupied prefix from $4,7$ to $4,7,9$. At three elements the stack is full, so the next attempt leaves it unchanged. The LIFO pop returns $9$ and reduces the length to $2$.
