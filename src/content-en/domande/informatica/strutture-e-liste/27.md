`q` is a nonempty owning queue. Type `Queue` contains pointers to the first and last nodes. What holds immediately after `Queue copy = q;`?

- The copy automatically allocates a second chain.
- The pointer members in the copy become null.
- Copying transfers ownership and clears the original endpoints.
- The endpoints of the two structures designate the same nodes.

## Explanation

Copy initialization preserves each pointer field’s value. Two distinct queue variables can therefore refer to the same chain. Release responsibility must belong to an owner specified by the interface.
