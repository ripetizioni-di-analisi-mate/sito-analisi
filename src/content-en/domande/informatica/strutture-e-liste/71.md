A bracket checker pushes each opening symbol. On a closing symbol, its type must match the opening symbol on top. While processing `([)]`, at which position, counting from one, can the checker first prove that the pairing is invalid?

- $2$
- $3$
- $4$
- $1$

## Explanation

The first two opening symbols are accepted. At the third position, `)` requires `(` on top, but the latest unmatched opening is `[`. The total numbers of opening and closing symbols can match even when this local condition fails.
