Two sorted lists are merged. The left list contains `L2` and `L4`, with keys $2$ and $4$. The right list contains `R2` and `R3`, with keys $2$ and $3$. On equal keys the first node of the left list is chosen. Other nodes retain their order within their own list. What is the output order?

- `L2, L4, R2, R3`
- `L2, R3, R2, L4`
- `L2, R2, R3, L4`
- `R2, L2, R3, L4`

## Explanation

The first comparison is a tie, so `L2` is emitted. The remaining heads have key $4$ on the left and key $2$ on the right. The merge takes `R2`, then `R3`, before appending `L4` as the last remaining node.
