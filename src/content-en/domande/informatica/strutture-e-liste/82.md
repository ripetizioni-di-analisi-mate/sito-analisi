A circular queue uses indices $0$ through $7$, has `first` equal to $6$, and contains four items. An inspector looks at physical index $1$. What is its zero-based logical position, or is it a free cell?

- Free cell
- Position $5$
- Position $1$
- Position $3$

## Explanation

Starting at `first`, the four occupied indices in FIFO order are $6,7,0,1$. Reaching the inspected index takes three forward moves from the first item. Its logical position is therefore $3$.
