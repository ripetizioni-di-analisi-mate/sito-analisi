Live nodes `a`, `b` and `c` form the chain `a→b→c`. A variable `Node **slot = &a.next;` holds the address of the link leaving `a`. The statement `*slot = b.next;` executes without changing the head or freeing any nodes. Which sequence is now visited from the head?

- `b→c`
- `a→c`
- `a→b`
- `a→b→c`

## Explanation

Dereferencing `slot` designates member `a.next`. The value read from `b.next` is the address of `c`, which is written into that member. The head still points to `a`, and the very next step reaches `c`.
