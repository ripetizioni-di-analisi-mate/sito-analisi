A circular queue of capacity $4$ has `first == 3` and `used == 4`. What position is available for an insertion that preserves all data?

- $3$, the position of the first value.
- $0$, the index after wrapping to the start.
- No free position.
- $2$, the position of the last value.

## Explanation

The four elements occupy indices $3$, $0$, $1$ and $2$, reaching capacity. An index obtained by applying the modulus anyway would point to an occupied cell. Inserting while preserving the four data values requires a position to become free.
