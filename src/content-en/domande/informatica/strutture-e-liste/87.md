A circular queue with a `used` counter has capacity one, may use its single cell and starts empty. Insertion into a full queue fails without replacing the value. A program tries to enqueue `A`, then `B`, then dequeues. What value is returned and what is `used` after the dequeue?

- `B` and $0$
- `A` and $1$
- No value and $0$
- `A` and $0$

## Explanation

The first insertion fills the only cell and raises the counter to one. The attempt to add `B` finds the queue full and retains `A`. Dequeuing returns `A` and reduces the counter to zero, while the only possible physical index remains zero.
