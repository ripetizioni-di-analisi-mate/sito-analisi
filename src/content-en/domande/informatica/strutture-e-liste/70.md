Symbols `A`, `B` and `C` must enter a single stack in that order. The top may be popped at any time between pushes or after the final push. Which output order is impossible?

- `B, A, C`
- `A, C, B`
- `C, A, B`
- `C, B, A`

## Explanation

To make `C` the first output, all three symbols must be pushed before the first pop. The symbol below `C` is then `B`, and the symbol below `B` is `A`. Under these conditions the only output starting with `C` continues with `B, A`.
