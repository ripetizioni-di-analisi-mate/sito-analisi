A circular queue of capacity $5$ has `first` equal to $3$ and four items. Physical cells $0,1,2,3,4$ contain `C,D,X,A,B` respectively, where `X` is stale. The queue is copied into a larger array with new `first` set to zero. Which values should occupy its first four indices?

- `D,C,B,A`
- `A,B,C,D`
- `C,D,A,B`
- `C,D,X,A`

## Explanation

To preserve FIFO order, visit the old indices $3,4,0,1$. Their contents give `A,B,C,D`. Cell $2$ is outside the four logical positions, so its stale value is left out.
