Consider the mathematical map $k\mapsto(2+k)\bmod6$ on all nonnegative integers. Which pair contains two values with the same image?

- $(0,5)$
- $(3,6)$
- $(1,8)$
- $(1,7)$

## Explanation

Two values of $k$ give the same remainder when their difference is a multiple of $6$. In pair $(1,7)$ the difference is $6$ and both remainders are $3$. Differences $5$, $3$ and $7$ in the other pairs leave different remainders.
