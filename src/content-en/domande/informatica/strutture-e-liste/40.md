A circular queue of capacity $5$ has `first == 4` and `used == 3`. Two dequeues are followed by one enqueue. Which physical indices hold the final two elements in logical order?

- $(0,1)$
- $(1,0)$
- $(4,0)$
- $(1,2)$

## Explanation

The initial positions are $4$, $0$ and $1$. The two dequeues remove positions $4$ and $0$, leaving position $1$. With first index $1$ and length $1$, the enqueue uses $(1+1)\bmod5=2$.
