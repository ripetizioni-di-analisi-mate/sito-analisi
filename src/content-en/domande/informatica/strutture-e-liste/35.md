A stack contains $7$, $1$, $9$ from bottom to top. Two pops are performed. What is the first result minus the second?

- $2$
- $-8$
- $-6$
- $8$

## Explanation

The initial top is $9$. After removing it, the top becomes $1$. The two pops therefore return $9$ and $1$ in order, and the requested difference is $9-1=8$.
