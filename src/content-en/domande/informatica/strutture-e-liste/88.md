A full circular queue of capacity $5$ contains `A,B,C,D,E` in that order. A rotation dequeues the front and immediately re-enqueues the same item at the rear. This step is repeated twelve times. Which item is at the front afterwards?

- `C`
- `D`
- `E`
- `B`

## Explanation

Each pair of operations preserves the length and advances the front by one position in the original sequence. Five rotations restore the initial order. Twelve rotations are equivalent to two, taking the front from `A` to `B` and then to `C`.
