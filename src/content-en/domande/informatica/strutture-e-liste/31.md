A valid linked queue contains keys $7$, $5$, $3$ from front to rear. What are the keys at `front` and `rear` after one successful dequeue?

- $(5,5)$
- $(5,3)$
- $(7,3)$
- $(3,5)$

## Explanation

FIFO removal detaches the initial node with key $7$. Its successor has key $5$ and becomes the first node. Since two nodes remain, the rear still designates the previous last node, whose key is $3$.
