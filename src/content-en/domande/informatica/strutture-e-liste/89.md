An initially empty container is known to implement either LIFO or FIFO. Every proposed operation succeeds. If only removed values can be observed, which test distinguishes the two disciplines with certainty?

- Insert $2$, remove, insert $7$, remove
- Insert $7$, insert $7$, remove once
- Insert $2$ and remove once
- Insert $2$, insert $7$, remove once

## Explanation

At least two distinguishable items must be present simultaneously. After inserting $2$ and then $7$, the first removal yields $7$ for LIFO and $2$ for FIFO. If the values are identical or only one item is present at a removal, the observations coincide.
