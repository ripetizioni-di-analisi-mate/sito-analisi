A filter on a linear list must remove every zero-key node, including consecutive ones. `slot` points to the link leading to the node under examination. When that node is removed, its successor’s address is assigned to `*slot`. Where should `slot` point for the very next check, without scanning the retained nodes again?

- Always at the head, even if the filter was in the middle
- Two links farther along
- At the same link, which has just been updated
- At the next link inside the node just removed

## Explanation

After removal the updated link leads to the first node still to be checked, or to `NULL`. Keeping `slot` in place also checks a possible second consecutive zero. Advancing to the next link is appropriate when the current node is retained.
