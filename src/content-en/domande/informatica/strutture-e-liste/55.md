During list reversal, `prev` designates the chain `B→A→NULL` and `cur` designates the disjoint chain `C→D→NULL`. The steps `next = cur->next; cur->next = prev; prev = cur; cur = next;` run in that order. Which chains do `prev` and `cur` now represent?

- `C→B→A` and `D`
- `C→D` and `B→A`
- `B→A` and `C→D`
- `D→C→B→A` and the empty chain

## Explanation

The initial save preserves the address of `D`. Rewriting the link in `C` places `C` in front of the already reversed prefix. The last two assignments update the two chain heads, leaving `D` as the first node still to process.
