A circular queue of capacity $7$ contains four elements and has `first == 6`. What is the physical index of logical position $2$, counting from logical position zero?

- $2$
- $6$
- $8$
- $1$

## Explanation

Logical position $0$ uses index $6$. The next logical step uses $0$ after wrapping to the start. The following step uses $1$, consistent with $(6+2)\bmod7=1$.
