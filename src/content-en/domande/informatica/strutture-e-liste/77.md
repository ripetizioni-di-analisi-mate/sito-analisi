Two nonempty linked queues own disjoint chains `P→Q` and `R→S`. The whole second queue is to be moved to the rear of the first in constant time, leaving the second queue empty. After linking `Q` to `R`, which endpoints should the first queue hold?

- Front `P` and rear `Q`
- Front `P` and rear `S`
- Front `R` and rear `S`
- Front `Q` and rear `R`

## Explanation

The new order is `P→Q→R→S`. The front remains the original front of the first queue, and its new rear comes from the second before that queue’s endpoints are cleared. The link in `Q` keeps both chains reachable.
