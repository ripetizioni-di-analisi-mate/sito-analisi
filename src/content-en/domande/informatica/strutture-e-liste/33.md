An array stack `data[5]` has `used == 3`, where `used` counts its elements. Which expression designates the top value?

- `data[used]`
- `data[4]`
- `data[used - 1]`
- `data[0]`

## Explanation

The occupied prefix of three elements uses indices $0$, $1$ and $2$. The top is the last of those positions, so its index is `used - 1`. Index `used` instead denotes the first cell available for another push.
