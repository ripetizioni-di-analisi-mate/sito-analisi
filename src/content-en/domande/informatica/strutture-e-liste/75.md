A postfix evaluator pushes numbers. For each operator it pops the right operand first and the left operand second. What value remains on top after the sequence `8 3 2 * -`?

- $2$
- $-2$
- $18$
- $10$

## Explanation

Multiplication pops $2$ and $3$ and replaces them with $6$. The stack now has $8$ below $6$. Subtraction therefore uses $8$ as the left operand and $6$ as the right operand, giving $8-6=2$.
