A job queue contains `A` with $3$ units of work left, `B` with $1$, and `C` with $2$, in that order. Each turn removes the front job, processes one unit, and returns the job to the rear only if work remains. Which job finishes first, and on which turn?

- `B` on turn $2$
- `A` on turn $1$
- `A` on turn $3$
- `C` on turn $2$

## Explanation

On the first turn `A` goes from three units to two and joins the rear behind `C`. The front is now `B`, which needs only the one unit supplied on the second turn. `B` is therefore the first job to leave permanently.
