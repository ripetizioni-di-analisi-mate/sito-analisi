A singly linked list starts with two nodes and stores only its head. Each append starts at the head and follows `next` to the last node. How many moves from a node to its non-null successor are needed in total for four consecutive appends? Count only moves made while searching for the last node.

- $10$
- $4$
- $14$
- $6$

## Explanation

A chain of length $n$ requires $n-1$ moves from the head to its last node. The lengths before the four appends are $2,3,4,5$. This gives $1+2+3+4=10$ moves, excluding the writes that link in the new nodes.
