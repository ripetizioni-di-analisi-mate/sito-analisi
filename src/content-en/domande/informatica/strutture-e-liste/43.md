A circular queue of capacity $6$ has `first == 4` and length between $0$ and $6$. For which lengths does $(4+\mathrm{used})\bmod6$ equal $4$? The number is calculated for the full state as well.

- $\{0,6\}$
- $\{0\}$
- $\{6\}$
- $\{2,4\}$

## Explanation

Equality of the two indices requires `used` to be congruent to zero modulo $6$. The only multiples in the permitted range are $0$ and $6$, representing an empty and a full queue, respectively.
