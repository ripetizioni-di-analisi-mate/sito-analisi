A stack and a queue are initially empty and receive $4$, $10$, $6$, $1$ in order. All insertions succeed. Remove two elements from each structure. What pair gives the values returned by the second removal, stack first and queue second?

- $(10,6)$
- $(1,4)$
- $(10,10)$
- $(6,10)$

## Explanation

The stack removes values from most recent to least recent, so its first two outputs are $1$ and $6$. The queue uses insertion order, so its first two outputs are $4$ and $10$. Taking the second value in each sequence gives $(6,10)$.
