On a particular C17 implementation, `sizeof(struct Rec)` is $12$ bytes and member `value` starts $4$ bytes from the beginning of each record. An array `struct Rec a[5]` starts at byte $100$. At what byte address does `a[3].value` begin in this model?

- $136$
- $144$
- $140$
- $116$

## Explanation

Three complete array elements precede the fourth record. Each occupies $12$ bytes, including any padding. The record therefore starts at $100+3\cdot12=136$. Adding the member offset gives $136+4=140$.
