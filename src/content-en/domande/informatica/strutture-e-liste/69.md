An initially empty stack must complete every operation successfully. `+` means push and `−` means pop. The sequence is `+ + − + + − − +`. What is the smallest sufficient capacity?

- $4$
- $5$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

The occupancies after successive operations are $1,2,1,2,3,2,1,2$. Capacity must cover the maximum intermediate occupancy, which is $3$. Using only the final length would suggest $2$ and cause the fifth operation to fail.
