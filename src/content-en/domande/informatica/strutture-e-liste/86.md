A circular queue uses only two indices in a five-cell array. `read` points to the first item and `write` to the next insertion cell. Equal indices mean empty, so one cell must always remain free. With `read = 3` and `write = 2`, how many items are present, and can another be inserted without dequeuing?

- Four items, insertion possible
- Five items, insertion impossible
- One item, insertion possible
- Four items, insertion impossible

## Explanation

Starting at $3$ and stopping before the write index visits $3,4,0,1$, or four items. Inserting would advance `write` from $2$ to $3$, making it equal to `read`. Under the stated convention the queue is therefore already full.
