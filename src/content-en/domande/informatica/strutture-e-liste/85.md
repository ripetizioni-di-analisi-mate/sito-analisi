The physical cells of a circular queue of capacity $4$ contain `K,L,M,N` in index order. The logical sequence must be exactly `N,K`. Which `(first, used)` pair represents this queue?

- $(3,1)$
- $(3,2)$
- $(0,2)$
- $(2,2)$

## Explanation

The requested first item is `N` at index $3$, so `first` must be $3$. The next item is at index $(3+1)\bmod4=0$, which holds `K`. The requested sequence ends there and has length two.
