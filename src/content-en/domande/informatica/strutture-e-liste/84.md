A circular queue of capacity $10$ holds seven items with `first` equal to $7$. Sending them in FIFO order without rearranging them can use two physically contiguous spans of the array. What are the span lengths in transmission order?

- $3$ and $4$
- $4$ and $3$
- $2$ and $5$
- $7$ and $0$

## Explanation

The span starting at index $7$ covers $7,8,9$, so it holds three items before the boundary. The remaining $7-3=4$ items occupy $0,1,2,3$. Reversing the spans would change where the front appears in the transmission.
