With successful output, what does `printf("[%08.3d]", 12);` produce? Each `·` represents one space.

- `[00000012]`
- `[012·····]`
- `[······12]`
- `[·····012]`

## Explanation

In decimal integer conversion, precision 3 requires at least three digits, giving `012`. The explicit precision overrides the field’s `0` flag. Five leading spaces bring the width up to eight.
