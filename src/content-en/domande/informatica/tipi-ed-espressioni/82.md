The numeric locale is C and decimal conversion rounds correctly to nearest with ties to even. Both arguments are exact. What text does the call produce?

```c
printf("%.1f %.1f", 2.25, 2.75);
```

- `2.3 2.7`
- `2.2 2.8`
- `2.3 2.8`
- `2.2 2.7`

## Explanation

With one decimal place, 2.25 is halfway between 2.2 and 2.3. The choice is 2.2 because its retained last digit is even. Similarly, 2.75 is halfway between 2.7 and 2.8, but here the endpoint with an even last digit is 2.8.
