Assume `INT_MIN == -INT_MAX - 1` and `UINT_MAX > INT_MAX`. A function receives an `int x < 0` and must obtain its mathematical magnitude as an `unsigned int`, even for `INT_MIN`. Which expression is always defined and correct?

- `0u - (unsigned int)x`
- `UINT_MAX - (unsigned int)x`
- `(unsigned int)(-x)`
- `(unsigned int)x`

## Explanation

Converting `x` to `unsigned int` selects its representative modulo the number of values of that type. Subtracting it from zero in the same type gives the representative of its mathematical opposite. Under the assumptions, this magnitude fits even when `x` is the minimum. Computing `-x` as an `int` first would produce an out-of-range result at that minimum.
