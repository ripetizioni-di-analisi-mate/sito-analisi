On a C17 implementation with `INT_MAX == 32767`, what is the type of the unsuffixed decimal constant `32768`?

- `int`
- `unsigned int`
- `long long`
- `long`

## Explanation

For an unsuffixed decimal integer constant, C17 tries `int`, `long` and `long long` in order. Since $32768$ exceeds the largest `int` value and fits within the guaranteed minimum range of `long`, the first suitable type is `long`.
