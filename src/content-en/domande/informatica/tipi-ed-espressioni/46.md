A reader receives the character for the digit six. Which expression produces the integer 6 even if the execution character set is not ASCII?

- `'6' / '0'`
- `'6' - 48`
- `(int)'6'`
- `'6' - '0'`

## Explanation

C17 guarantees that the codes for the digits from `'0'` through `'9'` are consecutive in increasing order. Six positions separate `'6'` from `'0'`, so subtraction yields 6. Subtracting 48 would rely on a property of a particular encoding.
