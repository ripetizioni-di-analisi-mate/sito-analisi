A price in cents is a nonnegative `int n`. Before calculating `12 * n` as an `int`, which check accepts exactly the values whose product is representable and is itself defined even at the maximum value of `n`?

- `n <= INT_MAX - 12`
- `n < INT_MAX / 12`
- `n <= INT_MAX / 12`
- `12 * n <= INT_MAX`

## Explanation

Dividing the positive maximum by 12 gives the largest integer that can be multiplied by 12 without exceeding that maximum. Equality must be allowed. A check that multiplies first would perform the very operation that may be out of range.
