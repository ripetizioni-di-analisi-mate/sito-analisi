Determine the decimal value of `total` in C17.

```c
int total = 0x1F - 011 + 5;
```

- $25$
- $27$
- $15$
- $45$

## Explanation

The `0x` prefix identifies a hexadecimal constant, so `0x1F` is $16+15=31$. The leading zero in `011` identifies an octal constant, equal to $8+1=9$. Addition and subtraction group from the left, giving $(31-9)+5=27$.
