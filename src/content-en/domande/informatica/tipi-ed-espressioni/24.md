For an `int` value `a`, `a / 6` is $-4$ and `a % 6` is $-2$. What is the value of `a`?

- $-22$
- $-26$
- $26$
- $-24$

## Explanation

Every defined integer division satisfies $a=qb+r$, where $q$ is the quotient and $r$ the remainder. Substitute quotient $-4$, divisor $6$ and remainder $-2$. This gives $a=(-4)\cdot6-2=-26$. The remainder has magnitude less than $6$ and the same sign as the dividend.
