On an implementation with binary floating-point arithmetic, what decimal value does the C17 constant `0x1.8p+2` denote?

- `6.0`
- `24.0`
- `384.0`
- `3.6`

## Explanation

The `0x1.8` part means one plus eight sixteenths, or 1.5. The exponent after `p` denotes a power of two. Multiplying 1.5 by four therefore gives exactly 6.
