In the C locale, with successful output, what characters does `printf("[%*.*f]", -7, 2, 3.5);` produce? Each `·` denotes one space. Assume 3.5 is exactly representable.

- `[3.50···]`
- `[3.50000]`
- `[···-3.5]`
- `[···3.50]`

## Explanation

The first asterisk receives width −7, which requests left alignment in a seven-character field. The second receives precision 2, producing `3.50`. The four characters of the number leave three spaces on the right before the closing bracket.
