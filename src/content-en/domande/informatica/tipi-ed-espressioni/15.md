Assume `UCHAR_MAX <= INT_MAX`. What is the value of `result` in this C17 fragment?

```c
unsigned char amount = 4;
int result = amount - 9;
```

- $-5$
- $5$
- `UCHAR_MAX - 4`
- `UINT_MAX - 4u`

## Explanation

Since `int` represents the whole range of `unsigned char`, promotion converts `amount` to an `int` with value $4$. The other operand already has type `int`. Subtraction is therefore signed, and $4-9=-5$ lies within the range of the type.
