What is the value of `result`? Assume a C17 Annex F implementation in round-to-nearest mode.

```c
double result = (double)(5 / 2) + (double)5 / 2;
```

- $4.0$
- $4.5$
- $5.0$
- $2.5$

## Explanation

In the first term, the division is between two `int` values, so it gives $2$ before the cast. In the second term, the cast makes the dividend a `double`, so the division gives $\dfrac{5}{2}=2.5$. Adding the two `double` values yields $4.5$.
