- `UINT_MAX - 2u`
- `UINT_MAX - 1u`
- `UINT_MAX - 3u`
- `3u`

## Explanation

Both operands have type `unsigned int`. If $M$ is the number of values in that type, the result is congruent to $2-5=-3$ modulo $M$. Its representative in the range from $0$ through $M-1$ is $M-3$, the maximum $M-1$ minus $2$.
