The commas inside the parentheses are comma operators. What are the values of `x` and `y` after the second declaration?

```c
int x = 3;
int y = (x += 1, x *= 2, x - 3);
```

- `8, 8`
- `8, 5`
- `3, 5`
- `5, 5`

## Explanation

The first expression assigns 4 to `x` before the second starts. The second doubles 4 and stores 8. The final subtraction computes 5 as the value of the full expression, without assigning it to `x`.
