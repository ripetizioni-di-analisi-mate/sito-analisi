Assume `UCHAR_MAX == 255`. A reader proposes `unsigned char b = 0; int r = sscanf("300", "%hhu", &b);` and intends to check `r` for range failure. Which conclusion follows from C17?

- The out-of-range conversion has undefined behavior
- `r == EOF` is guaranteed
- `r == 1` and `b == 44` are guaranteed
- `r == 0` and `b == 0` are guaranteed

## Explanation

The digit sequence matches the format, but 300 is not representable in the destination object. C17 specifies undefined behavior for this case of formatted input. Its return count therefore cannot serve as a range check. An ordinary integer assignment to `unsigned char` follows a different rule.
