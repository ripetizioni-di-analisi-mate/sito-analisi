Determine `result` in C17.

```c
int result = 100 / 5 / 2 + 7 / 3 * 3;
```

- $17$
- $16$
- $56$
- $12$

## Explanation

The multiplicative operators group from the left. The first term is `(100 / 5) / 2`, so it gives $20$ divided by $2$, namely $10$. In the second term, dividing $7$ by $3$ gives the integer quotient $2$, and the subsequent multiplication gives $6$. The sum is $16$.
