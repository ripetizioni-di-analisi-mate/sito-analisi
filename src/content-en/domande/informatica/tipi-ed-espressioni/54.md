After the C17 call, what are `n` and `next`? The initial number contains three digits, but the conversion specifies a width.

```c
int n = 0;
char next = '?';
sscanf("835;", "%2d%c", &n, &next);
```

- `83, ';'`
- `835, ';'`
- `83, '5'`
- `8, '3'`

## Explanation

`%2d` can consume at most two characters of the numeric field and converts `83`. The remaining input is `5;`. The `%c` conversion reads the next available character, the digit `'5'`, without skipping it.
