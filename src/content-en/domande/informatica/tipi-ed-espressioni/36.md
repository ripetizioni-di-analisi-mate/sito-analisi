The write with `<stdio.h>` succeeds. What characters does `printf` produce?

```c
printf("[%.4d]", -6);
```

- `[-006]`
- `[0006]`
- `[-6]`
- `[-0006]`

## Explanation

With `d`, precision $4$ requires at least four digits. The numeric part of $-6$ has one digit, so three zeros are added. The sign is written before the four digits, and the brackets are copied from the format string.
