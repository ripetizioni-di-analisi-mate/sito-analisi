Two readings from a switch panel are stored as `unsigned int`. The previous reading is `old = 0x0Du` and the current reading is `now = 0x16u`. Which expression and result identify exactly the bits that went from zero to one?

- `now ^ old`, yielding `0x1B`
- `old & ~now`, yielding `0x09`
- `now & old`, yielding `0x04`
- `now & ~old`, yielding `0x12`

## Explanation

A qualifying bit must be one in `now` and zero in `old`. The bitwise complement of `old` tests the second condition and AND requires both. Bits 3, 2 and 0 were on, and bits 4, 2 and 1 are on now. Only bits 4 and 1 qualify, giving $16+2=18$. XOR would also include bits that were switched off.
