In the C locale a reader encounters the text `12+5`. What values do `r`, `left`, and `right` take? There is no whitespace between the fields.

```c
int left = -1, right = -1;
int r = sscanf("12+5", "%d%d", &left, &right);
```

- `1, 12, -1`
- `1, 17, -1`
- `0, -1, -1`
- `2, 12, 5`

## Explanation

The first decimal conversion reads 12 and stops before the plus sign, which cannot continue the field already in progress. The second conversion recognizes `+5` as an integer with an optional sign. The two fields can therefore both be assigned even with no whitespace separator.
