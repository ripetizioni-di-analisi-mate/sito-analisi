What type does the expression `8u + 2.5f` have in C17, before any conversion to a destination type?

- `double`
- `long double`
- `unsigned int`
- `float`

## Explanation

`2.5f` has type `float`. The other operand is an unsigned integer, and the expression has no higher-ranking floating type, so the usual arithmetic conversions convert `8u` to `float`. The `+` operator produces a value of that common type.
