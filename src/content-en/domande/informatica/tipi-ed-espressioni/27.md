Determine `result` after the declarations.

```c
int n = 4;
int first = ++n;
int second = n++;
int result = 10 * first + second + n;
```

- $61$
- $60$
- $50$
- $62$

## Explanation

The pre-increment brings `n` to $5$ and produces $5$ for `first`. The post-increment still produces $5$ for `second` and brings `n` to $6$ before the last declaration. The three contributions are $50$, $5$ and $6$, giving $61$.
