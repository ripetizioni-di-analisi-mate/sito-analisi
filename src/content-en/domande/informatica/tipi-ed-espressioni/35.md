The write with `<stdio.h>` succeeds. What characters does this C17 call emit? Each `·` stands for one space.

```c
printf("|%5d|", -12);
```

- `|···-12|`
- `|-12··|`
- `|··-12|`
- `|·-12|`

## Explanation

The sign and two digits occupy three characters in the field. Its minimum width is $5$, so two spaces are added on the left. The vertical bars are ordinary format-string text and enclose the field.
