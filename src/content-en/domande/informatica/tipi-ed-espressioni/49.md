Two C17 implementations have `UINT_MAX == 4294967295U`. Machine A has `LONG_MAX == 2147483647L`, while B has `LONG_MAX == 9223372036854775807L`. What is the value of `-1L < 1U` on A and B, respectively?

- `0, 0`
- `0, 1`
- `1, 0`
- `1, 1`

## Explanation

The rank of `long` exceeds that of `unsigned int`. On A the signed type cannot cover the full unsigned range, so both operands become `unsigned long`, and converted −1 is the maximum of that type. On B, `long` covers the entire range of `unsigned int`, so `1U` is converted to `long` and −1 is less than 1.
