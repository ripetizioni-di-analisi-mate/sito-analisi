The line containing two readings is expected to have a literal separator. What are the final values of `r`, `a`, and `b`?

```c
int a = 0, b = 99;
int r = sscanf("12;7", "%d,%d", &a, &b);
```

- `1, 12, 0`
- `1, 12, 99`
- `2, 12, 7`
- `0, 0, 99`

## Explanation

The first conversion assigns 12 to `a`. The format then requires a comma, while the available character is a semicolon. The mismatch stops reading before the second assignment, so `b` retains 99 and the count is 1.
