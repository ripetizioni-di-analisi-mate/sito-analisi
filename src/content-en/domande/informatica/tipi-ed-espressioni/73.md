A programmer wants to test whether the bit with weight 2 is clear. With `unsigned int flags = 1u;`, what values are produced, in order, by `flags & 2u == 0u` and `(flags & 2u) == 0u`?

- `1, 0`
- `0, 0`
- `0, 1`
- `1, 1`

## Explanation

Equality binds more tightly than bitwise AND. Without parentheses, `2u == 0u` first produces zero, followed by `1u & 0`. With parentheses the requested bit is extracted first. Since `1u & 2u` is zero, the final comparison is true.
