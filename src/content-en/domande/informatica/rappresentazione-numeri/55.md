Two five-bit unsigned registers start at $28$. If an operation produces a value outside $[0,31]$, the first register clamps it to the nearer endpoint. The second keeps the remainder modulo $32$. We add $9$ and then subtract $6$ in both. What are their final values, respectively?

- $(25,5)$
- $(31,31)$
- $(31,25)$
- $(25,31)$

## Explanation

In the first register the sum $37$ is clamped to $31$. The subtraction acts on that already clamped value and produces $25$. In the second register $37$ is reduced to the remainder $5$. Subtracting $6$ gives $-1$, whose representative between $0$ and $31$ is $31$.
