A $5$-bit two's-complement word represents $-11$ and is sign-extended to $9$ bits. By how much does the unsigned value of the word increase?

- $480$
- $512$
- $496$
- $32$

## Explanation

Sign extension preserves the signed value $-11$. For a negative word of $n$ bits, the unsigned value is obtained by adding $2^n$ to the signed value. This gives $32-11=21$ in five bits and $512-11=501$ in nine bits. The increase is $501-21=480$.
