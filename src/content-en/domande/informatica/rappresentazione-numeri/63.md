A six-bit word represents $-13$ in two’s complement. A circuit shifts it one position to the right, discards the rightmost bit and copies the sign bit into the vacated position. Which integer does it now represent?

- $25$
- $-26$
- $-6$
- $-7$

## Explanation

The encoding of $-13$ is the unsigned encoding of $64-13=51$, namely $110011_2$. The stated shift produces $111001_2$, whose value is $57-64=-7$. The discarded bit is one. Indeed, $-13=2\cdot(-7)+1$, so the resulting quotient is the one rounded downward.
