A teaching format splits a word into fields $s\mid E\mid F$ of one, three and two bits. All exponents are ordinary. The value is $(-1)^s(1.F)_2\,2^{E-3}$, where $E$ is read as unsigned. What value does $1\mid010\mid11$ represent?

- $\dfrac{7}{8}$
- $-7$
- $-\dfrac{7}{8}$
- $-\dfrac{3}{8}$

## Explanation

The bit $s=1$ makes the result negative. The field $010_2$ equals $2$, so the effective exponent is $2-3=-1$. The significand includes the implicit leading one and equals $1+\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{4}=\dfrac{7}{4}$. Multiplying by $2^{-1}$ and applying the sign gives $-\dfrac{7}{8}$.
