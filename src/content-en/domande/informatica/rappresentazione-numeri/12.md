- $0.1101_2$
- $0.1010_2$
- $0.1011_2$
- $0.0111_2$

## Explanation

The decimal fraction equals $\dfrac{6875}{10000}=\dfrac{11}{16}$. Since $16=2^4$, the value can be written with four fractional positions, and the numerator $11$ determines the bits. The decomposition $11=8+2+1$ gives

$$
\frac{11}{16}=\frac{8}{16}+\frac{2}{16}+\frac{1}{16}=2^{-1}+2^{-3}+2^{-4}.
$$

The positions with weights $2^{-1}$, $2^{-3}$, and $2^{-4}$ therefore contain the digit $1$, giving $0.1011_2$.
