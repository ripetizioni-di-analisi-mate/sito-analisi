Only positive normalized binary representations are allowed. The maximum exponent is $4$, and the precision $p$ includes the leading bit. Which configuration represents $\dfrac{11}{128}$ exactly?

- $p=3$, minimum exponent $-4$.
- $p=4$, minimum exponent $-4$.
- $p=4$, minimum exponent $-3$.
- $p=5$, minimum exponent $-3$.

## Explanation

The numerator $11$ is $1011_2$, and the denominator is $2^7$. Normalization gives

$$
\frac{11}{128}=1.011_2\cdot 2^{-4}.
$$

The last significant digit is $1$, so at least four digits are needed to preserve the exact value. The exponent $-4$ must lie in the allowed range. Among the available configurations, both conditions hold for $p=4$ and a minimum exponent of $-4$.
