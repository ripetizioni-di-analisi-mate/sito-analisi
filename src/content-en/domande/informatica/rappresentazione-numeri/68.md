The value $-\dfrac{19}{16}$ is converted to a fixed-point grid with two fractional bits and sufficient range. Converter A rounds toward $-\infty$ and B toward zero. Which values do they store, respectively?

- $\left(-1,-1\right)$
- $\left(-\dfrac{5}{4},-1\right)$
- $\left(-\dfrac{5}{4},-\dfrac{5}{4}\right)$
- $\left(-1,-\dfrac{5}{4}\right)$

## Explanation

The spacing is $\dfrac{1}{4}$, so the neighbors are $-\dfrac{5}{4}=-\dfrac{20}{16}$ and $-1=-\dfrac{16}{16}$. The first is below the input and is selected by A. The second is closer to zero and is selected by B. The comparison uses the ordering of negative numbers.
