- $111010_2$
- $110110_2$
- $111100_2$
- $101110_2$

## Explanation

When dividing by $2$, the remainder is the digit in position $0$ and the quotient contains the higher-order digits. The process repeats on each quotient until it reaches zero.

$$
\begin{aligned}
58 &= 2\cdot29+0 &\quad 7 &= 2\cdot3+1\\
29 &= 2\cdot14+1 & 3 &= 2\cdot1+1\\
14 &= 2\cdot7+0 & 1 &= 2\cdot0+1
\end{aligned}
$$

Reading the remainders from last to first gives the six-bit representation $111010_2$. Indeed, $32+16+8+2=58$.
