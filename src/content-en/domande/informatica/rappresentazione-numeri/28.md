In a fixed-point format with five fractional bits, the underlying integer is increased by $1$. Assuming the new integer is still representable, by how much does the fixed-point value increase?

- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{16}$
- $\dfrac{1}{32}$
- $\dfrac{1}{64}$

## Explanation

If $I$ is the underlying integer, the fixed-point value is $I\,2^{-5}$. Increasing $I$ by one gives $(I+1)2^{-5}$. The difference is therefore $((I+1)-I)2^{-5}=2^{-5}=\dfrac{1}{32}$, regardless of $I$.
