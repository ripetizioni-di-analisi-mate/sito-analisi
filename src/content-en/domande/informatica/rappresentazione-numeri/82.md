The sum $a\oplus b$ is rounded to nearest with three binary significant digits, unrestricted exponents and ties to an even significand. What values do $(1\oplus\dfrac{1}{8})\oplus\dfrac{1}{8}$ and $1\oplus(\dfrac{1}{8}\oplus\dfrac{1}{8})$ give, respectively?

- $\left(1,\dfrac{5}{4}\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac{5}{4},\dfrac{5}{4}\right)$
- $(1,1)$
- $\left(\dfrac{5}{4},1\right)$

## Explanation

In the range from $1$ to $2$ the spacing is $\dfrac{1}{4}$. The sum $1+\dfrac{1}{8}$ is halfway between $1$ and $\dfrac{5}{4}$ and rounds back to $1$, whose significand ends in zero. This happens twice in the first grouping. In the second grouping the two eighths give exactly one quarter, which adds to $1$ to give exactly $\dfrac{5}{4}$.
