Compute the exact sum $1.01_2+2^{-4}$ and round it to the nearest normalized binary value with three significant digits, including the leading digit. What is the result?

- $1.00_2$
- $1.10_2$
- $1.11_2$
- $1.01_2$

## Explanation

Between $1$ and $2$, the format has spacing $2^{-2}=\dfrac{1}{4}$. The values on either side of the exact sum are $1.01_2$ and $1.10_2$. The sum is $\dfrac{1}{16}$ from the first and $\dfrac{1}{4}-\dfrac{1}{16}=\dfrac{3}{16}$ from the second. The first value, $1.01_2$, is therefore the result.
