A format contains zero and numbers of either sign with magnitude $1.b_1b_2b_3$ in base two multiplied by $2^e$, where $e$ is an integer from $-2$ through $3$. How many distinct mathematical values does it contain?

- $193$
- $97$
- $49$
- $96$

## Explanation

For each exponent the three explicit bits give $2^3=8$ significands. There are six exponents and their magnitude ranges are disjoint, giving $48$ distinct positive values. The sign adds equally many negative values. Counting zero once brings the total to $2\cdot48+1=97$.
