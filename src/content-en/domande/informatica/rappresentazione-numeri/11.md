## Explanation

Sign extension prepends one copy of the leftmost bit for each additional position. The leftmost bit of the original word is $1$, so both new bits on the left are $1$. The extended word is $11101101_2$. Indeed, the original word has value $45-64=-19$, and the extended word has value $237-256=-19$.
