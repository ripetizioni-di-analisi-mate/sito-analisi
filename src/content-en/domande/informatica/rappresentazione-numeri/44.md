The following additions use $5$-bit two's complement, and both operands are representable. Which addition produces signed overflow with a carry-out of $0$?

- $12+5$
- $(-12)+(-5)$
- $(-12)+5$
- $7+8$

## Explanation

The five-bit signed range is $[-16,15]$. The sum $12+5=17$ exceeds $15$ and the two codes, interpreted as unsigned, add up to $17<32$, so signed overflow occurs and the carry-out is $0$. The sum $(-12)+(-5)=-17$ also lies outside the range, but the unsigned codes add up to $20+27=47\geq32$ and the carry-out is $1$. The other two sums, $-7$ and $15$, are both representable as signed values.
