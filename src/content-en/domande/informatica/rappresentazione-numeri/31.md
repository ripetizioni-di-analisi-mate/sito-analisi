The word $0111_2$ has two fractional bits and the word $011_2$ has one. Both use unsigned fixed-point representation. What is their sum as a $6$-bit unsigned word with two fractional bits?

- $001010_2$
- $010100_2$
- $010001_2$
- $001101_2$

## Explanation

The words represent $\dfrac{7}{4}$ and $\dfrac{3}{2}$. To express the sum in units of $\dfrac{1}{4}$, rewrite the second value as $\dfrac{6}{4}$.

$$
\frac{7}{4}+\frac{6}{4}=\frac{13}{4}.
$$

The integer to encode is therefore $13$. Since $13=8+4+1$, its six-bit representation is $001101_2$.
