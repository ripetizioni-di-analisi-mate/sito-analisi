A controller must decide whether a long unsigned binary word represents a multiple of $3$. Positions are numbered from zero on the right. Which test works for every word?

- The number of ones in even positions minus the number of ones in odd positions is a multiple of $3$.
- The total number of ones is a multiple of $3$.
- The final two digits are both zero.
- The counts of ones in even and odd positions must be equal.

## Explanation

Since $2$ is congruent to $-1$ modulo $3$, the powers of $2$ alternate between the residues $1,-1,1,-1,\ldots$. The word has the same residue as the difference between the two counts. A nonzero difference can also pass the test. For example, $10101_2=21$ has three ones in even positions and none in odd positions.
