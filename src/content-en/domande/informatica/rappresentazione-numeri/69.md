A sensor rounds to the nearest point on a binary fixed-point grid. All readings lie between the grid endpoints. What is the smallest number $f$ of fractional bits that guarantees an absolute error of at most $\dfrac{1}{100}$ for every reading?

- $6$
- $7$
- $8$
- $5$

## Explanation

Adjacent points are $2^{-f}$ apart. The worst case is a midpoint, at distance $2^{-(f+1)}$ from either neighbor. With $f=5$ that distance is $\dfrac{1}{64}$, which exceeds the tolerance. With $f=6$ it falls to $\dfrac{1}{128}$, so the sixth bit is both sufficient and necessary.
