A normalized binary format has three significant digits, including the leading $1$. The smallest positive normal value is $2^{-2}$. Subnormal values between zero and that minimum are added with the same spacing as normal numbers in $[2^{-2},2^{-1})$. How many strictly positive values are added, and which is the largest?

- $3$ values, largest $\dfrac{3}{16}$
- $4$ values, largest $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $7$ values, largest $\dfrac{7}{32}$
- $2$ values, largest $\dfrac{1}{8}$

## Explanation

The two digits after the leading one divide the normal interval into four steps. The spacing is therefore $2^{-2}\cdot 2^{-2}=\dfrac{1}{16}$. Below the smallest normal value, which is $\dfrac{4}{16}$, the strictly positive multiples are $\dfrac{1}{16}$, $\dfrac{2}{16}$, and $\dfrac{3}{16}$. Neither zero nor the smallest normal number is a new positive subnormal value.
