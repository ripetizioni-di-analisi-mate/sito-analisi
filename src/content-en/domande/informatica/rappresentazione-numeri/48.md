The first three digits $213$ of a base-$4$ integer are still legible. Exactly two further digits followed. What is the smallest decimal interval containing every possible value?

- $[21300,21333]$
- $[624,640]$
- $[624,639]$
- $[156,171]$

## Explanation

The prefix has value $2\cdot16+1\cdot4+3=39$. The two missing positions multiply the prefix’s contribution by $4^2=16$. The suffix can represent any integer from $0$ through $15$, including both endpoints. The possible values therefore run from $39\cdot16=624$ to $624+15=639$.
