A receiver reads hexadecimal digits from the left and can store only the remainder of the prefix modulo $31$. Its current remainder is $12$, and the next digit is $\mathrm D$. Which remainder should it store?

- $19$
- $1$
- $9$
- $25$

## Explanation

Appending a digit on the right multiplies the prefix by $16$ and then adds the digit’s value, here $13$. The prefix can be replaced by its remainder because the discarded contribution is still a multiple of $31$ after multiplication. The new remainder is that of $12\cdot16+13=205$, namely $205-6\cdot31=19$.
