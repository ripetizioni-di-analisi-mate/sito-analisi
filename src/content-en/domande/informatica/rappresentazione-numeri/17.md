A $7$-bit field reserves $17$ distinct words to indicate service states. How many words remain available to encode data?

- $111$
- $110$
- $112$
- $47$

## Explanation

Each of the seven positions has two independent choices, giving $2^7=128$ possible words. Subtracting the $17$ reserved words leaves $128-17=111$ available words. The all-zero word is one of the $128$ bit patterns, so $127$ is the maximum unsigned value and $128$ is the number of words.
