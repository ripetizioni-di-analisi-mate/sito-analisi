A seven-bit two’s-complement word represents $x$. All seven bits are flipped and the new word is read in the same format. Which formula gives the new value even for $x=0$ and for the minimum representable value?

- $x-1$
- $-x$
- $127-x$
- $-1-x$

## Explanation

When adding the values of the two words, each position contributes its weight exactly once because the two bits are complementary. The signed weights sum to $-64+32+16+8+4+2+1=-1$. If the original value is $x$, the value of the complemented word must therefore be $-1-x$.
