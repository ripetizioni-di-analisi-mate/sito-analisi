An archive rounds the integers $48$ and $49$ to the nearest representable binary value, with ties to an even significand. Exponents are unrestricted. What is the smallest precision $p\geq2$, including the leading digit, at which the two readings remain distinguishable?

- $4$
- $5$
- $6$
- $7$

## Explanation

At five digits the spacing in $[32,64)$ is $2$. The value $49$ is midway between $48$ and $50$ and rounds to $48$, whose representation $1.1000_2\cdot2^5$ ends in a zero significand bit. At lower precision $48$ is still representable and the spacing is larger. At six digits the spacing is $1$, so both integers are exact.
