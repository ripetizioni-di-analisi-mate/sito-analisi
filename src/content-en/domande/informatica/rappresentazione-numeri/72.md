Three contributions each equal $\dfrac{3}{16}$. A rounds every contribution to the nearest quarter and then adds. B adds the exact contributions and rounds the total to the nearest quarter. By how much does A’s result exceed B’s?

- $\dfrac{3}{16}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{1}{16}$

## Explanation

Each contribution lies $\dfrac{1}{16}$ from $\dfrac{1}{4}$ and $\dfrac{3}{16}$ from zero, so A obtains three quarters. B’s exact sum is $\dfrac{9}{16}$, which lies $\dfrac{1}{16}$ from $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $\dfrac{3}{16}$ from $\dfrac{3}{4}$. B obtains one half. The difference is therefore $\dfrac{3}{4}-\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{1}{4}$.
