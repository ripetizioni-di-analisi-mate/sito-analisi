## Explanation

In base two, the place values increase from right to left according to powers of $2$. Numbering the positions from zero on the right, the digits equal to $1$ occupy positions $5$, $3$, $2$, and $0$, so

$$
101101_2=2^5+2^3+2^2+2^0=32+8+4+1=45.
$$

The digit in position $4$ is $0$, so its weight of $16$ contributes nothing to the sum.
