For each of the $8$-bit words $10010110_2$ and $11010110_2$, let $U$ be the unsigned value and let $S$ be the two's-complement value. Which relation holds for both?

- $U-S=256$
- $U-S=128$
- $S-U=256$
- $U+S=256$

## Explanation

In both words, the leftmost bit is $1$. Its weight is $+128$ in the unsigned interpretation and $-128$ in two's complement. The other bits have the same weights, so their contributions cancel in the difference. This gives $U-S=128-(-128)=256$.
