To construct an interval containing $x=-\dfrac{11}{10}$, we round once toward $-\infty$ and once toward $+\infty$. The format uses normalized binary numbers of either sign with three significant digits, including the leading one, and unrestricted exponents. Which interval results?

- $\left[-\dfrac{3}{2},-\dfrac{5}{4}\right]$
- $\left[-\dfrac{5}{4},-1\right]$
- $\left[-1,-\dfrac{7}{8}\right]$
- $\left[-\dfrac{9}{8},-1\right]$

## Explanation

For magnitudes between $1$ and $2$ the format spacing is $\dfrac{1}{4}$. The adjacent magnitudes surrounding $\dfrac{11}{10}$ are $1$ and $\dfrac{5}{4}$. Negation reverses their order, so the lower neighbor is $-\dfrac{5}{4}$ and the upper one is $-1$. These are the bounds produced by the two directed roundings.
