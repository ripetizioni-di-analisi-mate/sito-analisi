Let $b$ be an integer base at least $4$. For which value of $b$ does $132_b=42_{10}$ hold?

- $4$
- $5$
- $6$
- $7$

## Explanation

The numeral $132_b$ has value $b^2+3b+2$ because the positions have weights $b^2$, $b$, and $1$. Factoring gives $(b+1)(b+2)=42$. The consecutive factors $6$ and $7$ have product $42$, so $b+1=6$ and $b=5$. The product is strictly increasing for $b\geq4$, so the base is unique.
