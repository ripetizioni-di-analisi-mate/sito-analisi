In a ten-bit word, the three most significant bits form the field $101_2$. The remaining seven bits contain the unsigned value $\mathrm{12}_{16}$. What is the hexadecimal value of the word?

- $512_{16}$
- $152_{16}$
- $292_{16}$
- $052_{16}$

## Explanation

The upper field has value $5$ and has seven positions to its right, giving a contribution of $5\cdot2^7=640$. The lower field has value $18$, so the total is $658=292_{16}$. Concatenating the hexadecimal digits directly would give the lower field eight positions instead of its allocated seven.
