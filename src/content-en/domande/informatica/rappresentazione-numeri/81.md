Compare two procedures for $x=\dfrac{33}{32}$ and $y=1$. A rounds $x$ and $y$ separately and then subtracts them. B subtracts the exact values and rounds the difference. The format uses round to nearest, four binary significant digits, zero and unrestricted exponents. What results do A and B produce?

- $\left(0,\dfrac{1}{32}\right)$
- $\left(0,0\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac{1}{8},\dfrac{1}{32}\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac{1}{32},0\right)$

## Explanation

Between $1$ and $2$ the spacing is $\dfrac{1}{8}$. The value $\dfrac{33}{32}$ is only $\dfrac{1}{32}$ from $1$, so A subtracts equal values and obtains zero. The exact difference in B is $\dfrac{1}{32}=2^{-5}$. A power of two is representable with a single nonzero significant bit, so B retains it.
