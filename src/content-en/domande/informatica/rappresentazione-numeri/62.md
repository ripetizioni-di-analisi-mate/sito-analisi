The positions of the octet $01100100_2$ are numbered from $7$ to $0$ from the left. The field in positions $5$ through $2$ inclusive is a four-bit two’s-complement integer. What is its value?

- $-6$
- $-7$
- $-28$
- $9$

## Explanation

Separating the fields gives $01\;1001\;00$. The first bit of the middle field has weight $-8$ because that field has width four. Its last bit contributes $1$, so its value is $-8+1=-7$. The initial bit of the octet belongs to another field and does not determine the sign of this value.
