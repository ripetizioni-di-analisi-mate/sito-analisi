## Explanation

The required word has an unsigned value congruent to $-37$ modulo $2^8$. The only representative between $0$ and $255$ is $256-37=219$.

$$
219=128+64+16+8+2+1=11011011_2.
$$

The leftmost bit is $1$, so the two's-complement interpretation subtracts $256$ from the unsigned value and gives $219-256=-37$.
