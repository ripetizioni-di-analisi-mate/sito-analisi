With four binary significant digits including the leading one and unrestricted exponents, consider integers in $[32,64)$. How many are both multiples of $3$ and exactly representable?

- $2$
- $8$
- $11$
- $3$

## Explanation

In this range the exponent is $5$, so adjacent representable numbers are $2^{5-(4-1)}=4$ apart. Multiples of four that are also multiples of three are precisely the multiples of twelve. Those in the given interval are $36$, $48$ and $60$, giving a total of three.
