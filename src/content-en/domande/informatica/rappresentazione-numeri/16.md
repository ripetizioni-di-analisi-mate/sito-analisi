What is the exact representation of $0.\mathrm{6C}_{16}$ with eight binary digits after the point?

- $0.11000110_2$
- $0.11011000_2$
- $0.01100011_2$
- $0.01101100_2$

## Explanation

The relation $16=2^4$ pairs each hexadecimal digit with a group of four bits, including digits after the point. The digit $6$ gives $0110_2$, and the digit $\mathrm C$ has value $12$, giving $1100_2$. The first group fills fractional positions $1$ through $4$ and the second fills positions $5$ through $8$, so the representation is $0.01101100_2$. The leading zero in the first group preserves the place values of the other digits.
