An archive must store every measurement $\dfrac{k}{8}$ exactly for integers $-120\leq k\leq119$. It uses a single two’s-complement fixed-point word and will reserve all unused codes for status signals. At the minimum width, how many codes can be reserved?

- $16$
- $15$
- $8$
- $0$

## Explanation

There are $119-(-120)+1=240$ distinct measurements, so seven bits with $128$ configurations are insufficient. Eight bits with three fractional positions cover all the required underlying integers because $[-120,119]$ lies within $[-128,127]$. Eight codes remain below the lower limit and eight above the upper one, giving $16$ status signals.
