For unsigned integers $a,b$ from $0$ through $255$, we want to predict the carry out of $a+b$ before forming the sum. Which test is correct for every input, including when the sum is exactly $255$?

- $a\geq255-b$
- $a> b$
- $a>255-b$
- $a>127$ and $b>127$

## Explanation

A carry first appears at a sum of $256$. Thus the condition is $a+b>255$, equivalent to $a>255-b$. The difference $255-b$ always fits in the input range. With $a=200$ and $b=55$ the threshold is met exactly, and the value $255$ still fits in the word.
