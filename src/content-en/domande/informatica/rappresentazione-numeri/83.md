Round $\dfrac{37}{32}$ to nearest in two ways using unrestricted binary exponents and ties to an even significand. A rounds directly to three significant digits. B rounds to four digits and then to three. Both precisions count the leading digit. What are the two results?

- $\left(1,1\right)$
- $\left(1,\dfrac{5}{4}\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac{5}{4},1\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac{5}{4},\dfrac{5}{4}\right)$

## Explanation

At three digits the midpoint between $1$ and $\dfrac{5}{4}$ is $\dfrac{9}{8}=\dfrac{36}{32}$. The input is above the midpoint, so A selects $\dfrac{5}{4}$. At four digits the nearest value is $\dfrac{9}{8}$ itself. B therefore encounters a tie in its second step and selects $1$, the candidate with an even last digit.
