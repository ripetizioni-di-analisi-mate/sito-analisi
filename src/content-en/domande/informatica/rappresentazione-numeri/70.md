An unsigned octet with value $K$ encodes the measurement $t=\dfrac{K-64}{8}$. The check accepts $-\dfrac{3}{2}\leq t\leq\dfrac{9}{4}$, including both endpoints. How many octet codes are accepted?

- $29$
- $30$
- $32$
- $31$

## Explanation

Multiplying the interval by $8$ gives $-12\leq K-64\leq18$. Adding back the offset $64$ yields $52\leq K\leq82$. Both endpoints are valid octet codes and are included by the check. The number of integers is therefore $82-52+1=31$.
