The three leftmost bits are discarded from each eight-bit word. For how many of the $256$ words does this preserve both the unsigned value and, separately, the two’s-complement value?

- $16$
- $8$
- $32$
- $64$

## Explanation

To preserve the unsigned value, the three discarded bits must be zero. The original value is then nonnegative and at most $31$. After truncation the bit of weight $16$ becomes the sign bit. It must also be zero, or the new signed value would be negative. The other four bits are free, giving $2^4=16$ words.
