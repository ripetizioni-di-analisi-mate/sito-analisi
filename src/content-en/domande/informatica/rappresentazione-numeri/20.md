How many strictly negative even integers can be represented in $6$-bit two's complement?

- $15$
- $31$
- $32$
- $16$

## Explanation

Negative integers require the leftmost bit to be $1$. Parity depends on the rightmost bit because all the other weights, including $-32$, are even. Setting the rightmost bit to $0$ leaves four free bits, giving $2^4=16$ distinct words. In decimal, these are the even integers from $-32$ through $-2$.
