Round $1.00111_2\cdot2^1$ to the nearest representable value in a normalized binary format with four significant digits, including the leading digit. What is the result?

- $1.001_2\cdot2^1$
- $1.011_2\cdot2^1$
- $1.010_2\cdot2^1$
- $1.010_2\cdot2^2$

## Explanation

The two adjacent values are $1.001_2\cdot2^1$ and $1.010_2\cdot2^1$. At this exponent, the spacing between them is $2^{1-3}=\dfrac{1}{4}$. The bits after the fourth significant digit place the exact value $2^1(2^{-4}+2^{-5})=\dfrac{3}{16}$ above the lower candidate. The distance to the upper candidate is $\dfrac{1}{4}-\dfrac{3}{16}=\dfrac{1}{16}$, so rounding gives $1.010_2\cdot2^1$.
