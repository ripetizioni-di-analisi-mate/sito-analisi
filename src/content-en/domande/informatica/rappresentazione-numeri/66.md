To convert a fraction to binary, each step doubles the remainder and extracts its integer part. After three steps the bits produced are $101$, and the remainder to be doubled at the next step is $\dfrac{3}{5}$. What was the original fraction?

- $\dfrac{13}{20}$
- $\dfrac{7}{10}$
- $\dfrac{49}{40}$
- $\dfrac{5}{8}$

## Explanation

The prefix contributes $\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{8}=\dfrac{5}{8}$. The three steps scaled the remainder by a factor of $2^3$, so its contribution to the original value is $\dfrac{3}{5}\cdot\dfrac{1}{8}=\dfrac{3}{40}$. Adding gives $\dfrac{25}{40}+\dfrac{3}{40}=\dfrac{7}{10}$.
