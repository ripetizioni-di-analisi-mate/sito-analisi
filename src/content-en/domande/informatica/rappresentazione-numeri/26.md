Which decimal value has an exact binary representation with at most three digits after the point?

- $0.2$
- $0.35$
- $0.45$
- $0.375$

## Explanation

With three fractional binary digits, each value is an integer multiple of $2^{-3}=\dfrac{1}{8}$. A number therefore has such a representation if its product with $8$ is an integer. The product $8\cdot0.375$ equals $3$, whereas the other products are $1.6$, $2.8$, and $3.6$. The exact value is therefore $0.375=0.011_2$.
