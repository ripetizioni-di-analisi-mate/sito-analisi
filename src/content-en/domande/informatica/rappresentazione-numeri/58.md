A circuit computes the mathematical difference between two independent six-bit two’s-complement samples. What is the smallest two’s-complement width that holds every possible difference?

- $6$ bits
- $8$ bits
- $12$ bits
- $7$ bits

## Explanation

Each sample lies between $-32$ and $31$. The largest difference is $31-(-32)=63$, while the smallest is $-32-31=-63$. Six bits reach only as high as $31$. The seven-bit interval from $-64$ through $63$ includes both extremes.
