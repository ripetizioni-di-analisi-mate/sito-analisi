In a packed decimal encoding, each digit from 0 to 9 occupies four bits. For example, $59$ is stored as $59_{16}$. Adding $59_{16}$ and $08_{16}$ as binary words gives $61_{16}$. Which corrected word represents the decimal sum in the same format?

- $61_{16}$
- $6\mathrm{B}_{16}$
- $67_{16}$
- $71_{16}$

## Explanation

The units digits give $9+8=17$. The units digit must be $7$, and a carry of $1$ raises the tens digit from $5$ to $6$. The binary units field carried at $16$, while a decimal digit carries at $10$. Adding the difference $16-10=6$ to the raw result $61_{16}$ gives $67_{16}$.
