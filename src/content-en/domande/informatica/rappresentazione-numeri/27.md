Expand $\dfrac{1}{5}$ in base two. What prefix consists of the first four digits after the point before rounding?

- $0.0101_2$
- $0.0010_2$
- $0.0001_2$
- $0.0011_2$

## Explanation

At each step, double the remaining fractional part. The integer part of the product is the next digit, so

$$
\begin{aligned}
2\cdot\tfrac{1}{5}&=0+\tfrac{2}{5}\\[3pt]
2\cdot\tfrac{2}{5}&=0+\tfrac{4}{5}\\[3pt]
2\cdot\tfrac{4}{5}&=1+\tfrac{3}{5}\\[3pt]
2\cdot\tfrac{3}{5}&=1+\tfrac{1}{5}.
\end{aligned}
$$

The digits in the order of the steps are $0$, $0$, $1$, and $1$. The requested prefix is $0.0011_2$.
