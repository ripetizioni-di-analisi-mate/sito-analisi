A base-$7$ code was transcribed as $2541_7$ instead of $2451_7$. By how much does the recorded value exceed the correct value?

- $56$
- $42$
- $6$
- $7$

## Explanation

The outer digits are unchanged. The digit in the $7^2$ position increases by one, while the digit in the $7$ position decreases by one. The recorded value therefore exceeds the correct one by $49-7=42$. The change in each digit must be weighted according to its position.
