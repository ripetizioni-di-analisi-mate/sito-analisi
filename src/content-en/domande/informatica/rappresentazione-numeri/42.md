What is the minimum number of digits after the decimal point needed to write the binary number $0.11111_2$ exactly?

- $4$
- $5$
- $6$
- $7$

## Explanation

The number is $\dfrac{16+8+4+2+1}{32}=\dfrac{31}{32}$. The fraction is already in lowest terms and its denominator is $32=2^5$. For a representation with $m$ digits after the decimal point, the denominator in lowest terms must divide $10^m=2^m5^m$, so $m\geq5$. Indeed, $\dfrac{31}{32}=\dfrac{96875}{100000}=0.96875$.
