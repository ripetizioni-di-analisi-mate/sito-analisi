In a normalized binary format with four significant digits and an unrestricted exponent, let $d_-$ be the distance from $8$ to the next lower representable number and $d_+$ the distance from $8$ to the next higher one. What is the pair $(d_-,d_+)$?

- $(1,1)$
- $\left(1,\dfrac{1}{2}\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac{1}{2},1\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac{1}{2},\dfrac{1}{2}\right)$

## Explanation

The number $8$ is the start of the interval for exponent $3$. The next higher representable number is $1.001_2\cdot2^3=9$, so $d_+=1$. The next lower one is $1.111_2\cdot2^2=\dfrac{15}{2}$, the last number with exponent $2$. Therefore $d_-=8-\dfrac{15}{2}=\dfrac{1}{2}$.
