The words are added in $4$-bit two's complement. The input words are $1101_2$ and $0101_2$. Which pair describes the carry out of the fourth bit and whether signed overflow occurs?

- Carry $0$, no overflow.
- Carry $1$, no overflow.
- Carry $0$, overflow.
- Carry $1$, overflow.

## Explanation

Adding the bit patterns as unsigned values gives $13+5=18=10010_2$, so the carry out of the four-bit word is $1$. In two's complement, $1101_2$ equals $13-16=-3$, while $0101_2$ equals $5$. The exact sum is $-3+5=2$, which belongs to $[-8,7]$. The carry is therefore $1$ and there is no signed overflow.
