- $\mathrm{B9D}_{16}$
- $\mathrm{B6B}_{16}$

## Explanation

Since $16=2^4$, each group of four bits corresponds to one hexadecimal digit. The three groups are read in the given order.

$$
\begin{aligned}
1011_2 &= 8+2+1=11=\mathrm{B}_{16}\\[3pt]
0110_2 &= 4+2=6\\[3pt]
1101_2 &= 8+4+1=13=\mathrm{D}_{16}
\end{aligned}
$$

The hexadecimal representation is therefore $\mathrm{B6D}_{16}$.
