Three unsigned octets must sum to zero modulo $256$. The first two are $\mathrm{9A}_{16}$ and $\mathrm{6D}_{16}$. Which third octet completes the check?

- $07_{16}$
- $08_{16}$
- $\mathrm{F9}_{16}$
- $\mathrm{F8}_{16}$

## Explanation

The first two octets have values $154$ and $109$, so their sum is $263$, leaving remainder $7$. The third must contribute $256-7=249=\mathrm{F9}_{16}$. Checking the full sum gives $154+109+249=512$, exactly twice the modulus.
