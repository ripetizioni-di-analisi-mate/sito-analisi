Interpret $10000000_2$ in $8$-bit two's complement and calculate the negation of its value. Which statement describes whether the negation is representable?

- The negation is $127$ and is representable.
- The negation is $128$ and is representable.
- The negation is $128$ and exceeds the maximum.
- The negation is $0$ and is representable.

## Explanation

The sign bit is the only bit equal to $1$, so the word represents $-2^7=-128$. The exact negation is $+128$. The maximum in $8$-bit two's complement is $2^7-1=127$, so at least $9$ bits are needed to represent the negation.
