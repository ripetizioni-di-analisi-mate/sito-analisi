In the $8$-bit unsigned model, adding a word of value $x$ and a word of value $87$ stores $16$. What value does the first word represent?

- $71$
- $169$
- $185$
- $103$

## Explanation

The stored sum is the remainder modulo $256$, so $x+87\equiv16\pmod{256}$. Since $0\leq x\leq255$, the sum $x+87$ lies between $87$ and $342$. The only number in this interval with remainder $16$ is $16+256=272$. Therefore $x=272-87=185$.
