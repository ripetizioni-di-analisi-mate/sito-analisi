In fixed point, the stored integer $-7$ has three fractional bits and the stored integer $6$ has two. Their product is stored exactly in a sufficiently wide format with four fractional bits. Which integer must be stored in the destination format?

- $-3$
- $-84$
- $-42$
- $-21$

## Explanation

The two factors are $-\dfrac{7}{8}$ and $\dfrac{6}{4}$. The product of the integers is $-42$ and the denominator of the product is $2^{3+2}=32$. The destination uses denominator $16$, so the numerator must be divided by two. This gives $-\dfrac{42}{32}=-\dfrac{21}{16}$ with no loss of precision.
