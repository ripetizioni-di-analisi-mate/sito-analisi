## Explanation

Because the word uses $8$-bit two's complement, its leftmost bit has weight $-2^7=-128$. The remaining seven bits have the usual positive binary place values.

$$
-128+64+32+8+2+1=-128+107=-21.
$$

The value $235$ comes from giving the first bit a weight of $+128$, as in the unsigned interpretation.
