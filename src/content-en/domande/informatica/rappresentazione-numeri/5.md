## Explanation

The maximum unsigned value with $n$ bits is $2^n-1$ because each position holds a digit of at most $1$. With $9$ bits, the maximum is $2^9-1=511$, so $750$ is out of range. With $10$ bits, the maximum is $2^{10}-1=1023$, so $750$ is in range. Since the maximum increases with $n$, the minimum width is $10$ bits.
