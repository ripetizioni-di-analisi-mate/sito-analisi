A $16$-bit binary counter advances from $\mathrm{6FFF}_{16}$ to the next value. How many bit positions change?

- $4$
- $16$
- $12$
- $13$

## Explanation

The last three hexadecimal digits are all $\mathrm F$, so the twelve trailing ones become zeros. The carry increments the leading digit from $6$ to $7$. In binary it changes from $0110$ to $0111$, changing just one further bit. The total is $12+1=13$.
