In the $8$-bit unsigned word $00110101_2$, set the bit to $1$ at position $6$, with positions numbered from $0$ at the right. All other bits stay the same. By how much does the value increase?

- $32$
- $128$
- $64$
- $117$

## Explanation

The bit at position $6$ is initially $0$ and has weight $2^6$. Setting it to $1$ changes its contribution from $0\cdot2^6$ to $1\cdot2^6$. The other contributions cancel when the two values are subtracted. The value increases by $2^6=64$. The modified word has the new value $53+64=117$.
