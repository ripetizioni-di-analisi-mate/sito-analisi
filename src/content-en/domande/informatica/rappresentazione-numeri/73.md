An archive must store both a positive factor $x$ and its reciprocal exactly, using a finite number of fractional bits in each case and sufficient space for the integer part. Which factor permits this?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $8$
- $\dfrac{5}{16}$
- $\dfrac{3}{8}$

## Explanation

A reduced rational number has a finite binary expansion when its denominator is a power of two. For the reciprocal, the original numerator must satisfy that condition as well. For $x=8$, both $8$ and $\dfrac{1}{8}$ satisfy the criterion. The other reciprocals have a factor of $3$ or $5$ in their reduced denominators.
