Format A is unsigned fixed point with five total bits and three fractional bits. Format B is normalized binary floating point with three significant digits, including the leading digit, and unrestricted exponents. Which number is exactly representable in both formats?

- $0.0111_2$
- $1.011_2$
- $1.01_2$
- $10.11_2$

## Explanation

Format A represents multiples of $\dfrac{1}{8}$ up to $\dfrac{31}{8}$. The number $0.0111_2=\dfrac{7}{16}$ lies outside this grid and is therefore excluded. The numbers $1.011_2$ and $10.11_2$ require four significant digits in normalized form. The number $1.01_2$ equals $\dfrac{10}{8}$ and has three significant digits, so it fits both formats.
