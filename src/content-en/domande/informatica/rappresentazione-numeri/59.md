A subtractor computes $a-b$ on four-bit two’s-complement words and keeps the low four bits. For which pair does the resulting word represent a positive value even though the mathematical difference is negative?

- $a=-3,\ b=4$
- $a=-6,\ b=5$
- $a=6,\ b=-5$
- $a=-7,\ b=-2$

## Explanation

Four-bit signed values range from $-8$ through $7$. The difference $-6-5=-11$ is below the minimum. The low four bits represent its remainder $5$ modulo $16$, whose sign bit is zero. In contrast, $-3-4=-7$ and $-7-(-2)=-5$ fit, while $6-(-5)$ is positive before reduction.
