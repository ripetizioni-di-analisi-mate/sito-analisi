An eight-bit counter advances by one per pulse and returns to zero after $255$. Two successive readings are $241$ and $11$. Given that between $260$ and $300$ pulses arrived, how many were there?

- $286$
- $282$
- $270$
- $294$

## Explanation

The first passage from $241$ to $11$ takes $256-241+11=26$ pulses. Each full revolution adds another $256$ pulses without changing the final reading. The possible counts are therefore $26$, $282$, $538$, and so on. The supplied range selects $282$.
