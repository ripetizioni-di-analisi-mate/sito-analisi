Round $1.0001_2$ and $1.1011_2$ to four significant binary digits, including the leading digit. Choose the nearest value, breaking ties by choosing the one with an even last significant digit. What are the results in the same order?

- $1.001_2$ and $1.110_2$
- $1.000_2$ and $1.110_2$
- $1.000_2$ and $1.101_2$
- $1.001_2$ and $1.101_2$

## Explanation

In both numbers the first discarded bit is $1$ and all subsequent bits are $0$, so the two candidates are equally far from the exact value. For $1.0001_2$, the candidates are $1.000_2$ and $1.001_2$, so the choice is $1.000_2$, which ends in $0$. For $1.1011_2$, the candidates are $1.101_2$ and $1.110_2$, so the choice is $1.110_2$, which ends in $0$.
