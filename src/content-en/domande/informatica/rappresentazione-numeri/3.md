## Explanation

In base sixteen, the digit $\mathrm{A}$ has value $10$. Since it is the second digit from the right, its weight is $16$.

$$
\mathrm{3A7}_{16}=3\cdot16^2+10\cdot16+7=768+160+7=935.
$$

The weight $16^2$ applies to the leading digit $3$. The final digit $7$ has weight $16^0=1$.
