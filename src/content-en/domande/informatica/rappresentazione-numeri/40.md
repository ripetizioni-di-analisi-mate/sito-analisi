An $n$-bit word, with $n\geq2$, consists entirely of $1$ bits. Let $U$ be its unsigned value and $S$ its two's-complement value. What are these values?

- $U=2^n$, $S=-1$.
- $U=2^n-1$, $S=-2^{n-1}$.
- $U=2^n-1$, $S=2-2^{n-1}$.
- $U=2^n-1$, $S=-1$.

## Explanation

The unsigned interpretation adds every power from $2^0$ to $2^{n-1}$, so $U=2^n-1$. In the signed interpretation, the leftmost bit has weight $-2^{n-1}$. The remaining bits contribute $2^{n-1}-1$, so $S=-2^{n-1}+(2^{n-1}-1)=-1$.
