- $90$
- $5.625$
- $11.25$
- $2.8125$

## Explanation

As an unsigned integer, $01011010_2$ equals $64+16+8+2=90$. Since the fixed binary point leaves four fractional positions, every place value of the integer is divided by $2^4$.

$$
\frac{90}{2^4}=\frac{90}{16}=\frac{45}{8}=5.625.
$$

Writing the same bits as $0101.1010_2$ gives the same value because $5+2^{-1}+2^{-3}=5.625$.
