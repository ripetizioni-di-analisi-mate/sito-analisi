## Explanation

The integer sum is $93+49=142$, so it exceeds the maximum of $127$ representable in $8$-bit two's complement. The eight least significant bits have unsigned value $142=128+8+4+2$. The leftmost bit is $1$, so the two's-complement value is

$$
142-2^8=142-256=-114.
$$

Overflow occurs because the exact sum lies outside the representable range.
