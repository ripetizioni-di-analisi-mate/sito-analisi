What is the normalized binary form of $0.001011_2$ with the leading significant digit in the units position?

- $1.011_2\cdot2^{-3}$
- $1.011_2\cdot2^{-2}$
- $1.101_2\cdot2^{-3}$
- $1.011_2\cdot2^3$

## Explanation

The first $1$ to the right of the binary point is in position $-3$, which has weight $2^{-3}$. Factoring out that weight expresses the remaining terms relative to the new leading bit.

$$
2^{-3}+2^{-5}+2^{-6}
=2^{-3}(1+2^{-2}+2^{-3}).
$$

The significand is therefore $1.011_2$ and the exponent is $-3$.
