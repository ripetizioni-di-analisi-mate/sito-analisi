Round $1.1111_2\cdot2^{-2}$ to the nearest representable value in a normalized binary format with four significant digits, including the leading digit. The exponent is unrestricted. In a tie, choose the candidate with an even last significant digit. Which representation results?

- $1.000_2\cdot2^{-1}$
- $1.111_2\cdot2^{-2}$
- $1.000_2\cdot2^{-2}$
- $1.000_2\cdot2^{-3}$

## Explanation

The exact value is $\dfrac{31}{64}$. It lies halfway between $1.111_2\cdot2^{-2}=\dfrac{30}{64}$ and $1.000_2\cdot2^{-1}=\dfrac{32}{64}$. The second candidate ends in $0$, so it is selected in the tie. At exponent $-2$, adding one unit in the last place to $1.111_2$ gives $10.000_2$, so normalization raises the exponent to $-1$.
