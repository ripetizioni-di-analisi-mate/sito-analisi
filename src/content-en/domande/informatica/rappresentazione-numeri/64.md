A six-bit two’s-complement integer $x$ is doubled, keeping only the low six result bits. For which initial values does the new word represent the exact signed value $2x$?

- $-16\leq x\leq15$
- $-15\leq x\leq15$
- $-32\leq x\leq31$
- $-16\leq x\leq16$

## Explanation

The six-bit signed range is $-32$ through $31$. Exact doubling requires $-32\leq2x\leq31$. Dividing by two and requiring $x$ to be an integer gives the interval from $-16$ through $15$. The value $-16$ must be included because its double is exactly the representable minimum.
