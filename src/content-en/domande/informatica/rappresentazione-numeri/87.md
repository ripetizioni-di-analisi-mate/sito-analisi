With four-bit two’s complement we want to compute $6+5-7$ exactly, checking the range after every operation. In which of the two evaluations, A given by $(6+5)-7$ and B by $6+(5-7)$, are all intermediate results representable?

- In both.
- In neither.
- Only in B.
- Only in A.

## Explanation

The final mathematical result is $4$, within the range from $-8$ through $7$. However, A first computes $6+5=11$, which already exceeds the maximum at the intermediate step. B first obtains $5-7=-2$ and then $6+(-2)=4$. Both values B must store lie within the range.
