Consider the $12$-bit words whose unsigned values, written with three hexadecimal digits, begin with $\mathrm{D}$. Which values do the same words represent in two's complement?

- $[-1024,-769]\cap\mathbb{Z}$
- $[-768,-512]\cap\mathbb{Z}$
- $[3328,3583]\cap\mathbb{Z}$
- $[-768,-513]\cap\mathbb{Z}$

## Explanation

The unsigned values range from $\mathrm{D00}_{16}=13\cdot16^2=3328$ to $\mathrm{DFF}_{16}=3328+255=3583$. Since $\mathrm{D}=1101_2$, the leftmost bit of each twelve-bit word is $1$. The signed interpretation therefore subtracts $4096$ from each value. The endpoints become $3328-4096=-768$ and $3583-4096=-513$.
