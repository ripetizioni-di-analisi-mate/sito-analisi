In a normalized binary format with four significant digits, including the leading digit, and unrestricted integer exponents, what is the smallest positive integer without an exact representation?

- $15$
- $16$
- $17$
- $18$

## Explanation

The integers from $1$ to $15$ have at most four digits in their binary integer representations, so each is representable. The number $16$ is a power of two and has normalized representation $1.000_2\cdot2^4$. The number $17$ requires $1.0001_2\cdot2^4$, with a $1$ in the fifth significant position. Therefore the smallest positive integer without an exact representation is $17$.
