The lines are `7`, `-3`, `12x` and `10`. Only lines with a complete decimal integer are accepted. `total` is the sum and `count` is the number of valid lines. Both have type `int`. What is `total / count`?

- $6$
- $5$
- $4$
- $14$

## Explanation

Line `12x` is discarded because of the trailing character. Thus $7-3+10=14$ over $3$ lines. Integer division truncates the quotient toward zero and gives $4$ because $14=3\cdot4+2$.
