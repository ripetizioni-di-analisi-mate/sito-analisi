A file is empty. In order, `fprintf(file, "%d", 3)`, `fprintf(file, "%d\n", 5)` and closing succeed. What text appears before the first newline?

- `3 5`
- `35`
- `3`
- `53`

## Explanation

The first call transmits digit `3` and its format has no separator. The second appends `5` and then the first newline. The text before that newline is therefore `35`.
