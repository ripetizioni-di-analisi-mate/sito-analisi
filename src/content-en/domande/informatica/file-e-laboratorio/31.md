In the C locale, an integer is accepted when only whitespace follows the digits. The strings `"+6\t"`, `"6  z"` and `" -0 "` are examined in order. How many are accepted?

- $2$
- $3$
- $1$
- $0$

## Explanation

The first suffix is an allowed tab. In the second, the letter `z` remains after the spaces, so a partial conversion is insufficient to accept the whole string. The third yields signed zero with one space remaining. There are $2$ accepted strings.
