A valid line contains exactly two integers separated by one semicolon. Spaces around the fields are allowed. How many of `"4;5"`, `"4 5"`, `"4;5;6"`, `"4; -2"` and `"4;"` are valid?

- $1$
- $3$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

The valid strings are `"4;5"` and `"4; -2"`. The second string lacks the separator, the third leaves an extra field, and the last lacks a second integer. The count is $2$. Success of the first conversion alone is insufficient to validate the line.
