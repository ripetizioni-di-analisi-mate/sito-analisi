A stream delivers two valid lines and one complete invalid line, in that order. The next attempt returns `NULL` on error before delivering a line. The program stops. What are the counts of valid and complete invalid lines already examined, in that order?

- `2, 2`
- `0, 0`
- `3, 1`
- `2, 1`

## Explanation

The first two lines increment the valid count and the third increments the complete invalid count. The later error delivers no line. Thus the partial counts are $2$ and $1$, while the overall read outcome is still an error.
