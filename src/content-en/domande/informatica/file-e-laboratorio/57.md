The stream supplies the text `"7\nXYZ\n"`. A successful call to `fscanf(f, "%d", &n)` is immediately followed by `fgets(buf, 16, f)`. What string does this first `fgets` obtain?

- `"XYZ\n"`
- `""`
- `"\n"`
- `"7\n"`

## Explanation

The decimal conversion ends before the separator after digit 7. The line read starts on that newline, stores it and appends a null terminator. The line containing XYZ will be available on the next call.
