A lab assignment requires the exact CSV report on `stdout` and an explanation of rejected lines on `stderr`. A test captures the two streams separately. With one valid record of value 8 and one rejected line, which check distinguishes incorrect routing from a calculation error?

- Compare `stdout` with the expected report alone and verify the diagnostic in the `stderr` capture.
- Check only the combined number of newlines across the two captures.
- Concatenate both streams and look only for digit 8.
- Check only that a character was emitted to at least one stream.

## Explanation

The specification assigns different destinations to the data and the messages. Separate checks reveal both a wrong total and a diagnostic accidentally placed in the CSV. Combining streams or merely counting separators would discard the information about destination.
