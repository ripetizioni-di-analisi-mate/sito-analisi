A per-category report requires the pair (valid record count, total). Only categories A and B, a single integer value from −5 to 5, and no extra fields are allowed. The lines are `A -3`, `A 3 x`, `B 0`, `A 2`, and `Z 5`. Which report should the end-to-end test produce?

- A `(2,1)`, B `(1,0)`
- A `(2,-1)`, B `(0,0)`
- A `(2,-1)`, B `(1,0)`
- A `(3,2)`, B `(1,0)`

## Explanation

The valid readings for A are −3 and 2, giving count 2 and total −1. The extra field invalidates the entire line containing 3, and Z is an unknown category. For B the zero contributes to the count while leaving the total at zero.
