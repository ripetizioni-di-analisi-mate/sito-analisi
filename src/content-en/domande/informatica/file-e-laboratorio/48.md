A seekable binary stream opened with `"r+b"` contains bytes A, B, C. A call to `fgetc` has just read A without encountering end-of-file. B must be overwritten with X. Which C17 sequence, assuming success, follows the rules for changing direction?

- `fputc('X', f);`
- `fseek(f, 0L, SEEK_SET); fputc('X', f);`
- `clearerr(f); fputc('X', f);`
- `fseek(f, 0L, SEEK_CUR); fputc('X', f);`

## Explanation

After an input operation that did not encounter end-of-file, changing to output requires a positioning operation. On this binary stream, a zero offset from the current position leaves it at the second byte. Seeking to the beginning would overwrite A instead.
