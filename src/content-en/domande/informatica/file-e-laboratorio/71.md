Two teams must exchange the value 300. Format T requires ASCII digits followed by LF; format B requires exactly two unsigned octets, most significant first. Which pair of hex sequences meets the two specifications?

- T `03 00 00 0A`, B `01 2C`
- T `33 30 30 0A`, B `01 2C`
- T `33 30 30 0A`, B `2C 01`
- T `33 30 30 00`, B `01 2C`

## Explanation

In ASCII the digits 3, 0 and 0 have hexadecimal codes 33, 30 and 30; LF is 0A. In binary, 300 is 256 + 44, so the two octets are 01 and 2C. A C null terminator is not part of format T.
