The valid records are `(B, -7)`, `(A, 2)`, `(B, -9)` and `(B, -2)`. What is the maximum measurement for category `B`?

- $0$
- $2$
- $-7$
- $-2$

## Explanation

The first matching value is $-7$ and sets the running maximum. $-9$ is smaller, while $-2$ is greater and becomes the maximum. Value $2$ belongs to category `A`. Starting at zero would give a value absent from the measurements considered.
