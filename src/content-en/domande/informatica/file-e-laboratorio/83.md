A lab program counts level changes between consecutive records. The first block of a file ends at level 2 and contains one change. The second starts at level 5 and contains two internal changes. Both blocks are nonempty. How many changes are in their concatenation?

- $2$
- $4$
- $3$
- $5$

## Explanation

The internal changes contribute 1 + 2 = 3. Concatenation also produces the pair formed by the last record of the first block and the first record of the second. Since 2 and 5 differ, this pair contributes a fourth change.
