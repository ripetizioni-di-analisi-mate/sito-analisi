A lab task counts values **strictly greater than the previous valid value**. Nonnumeric lines are skipped without resetting the previous value. The lines are `4`, `X`, `7`, `6`, and `8`. The first value initialises the state. How many increases are recorded?

- $1$
- $3$
- $4$
- $2$

## Explanation

The sequence to compare is 4, 7, 6, 8. The X line does not replace the previous valid reading, so the transition from 4 to 7 is an increase. From 7 to 6 there is a decrease and from 6 to 8 a second increase.
