A lab program must find the mean of all valid readings across two files. The first contains three readings totalling 12; the second contains one reading totalling 8. What is the combined mean?

- $5$
- $10$
- $6$
- $4$

## Explanation

The partial totals add to 20 and the counts add to 4. Their ratio is 20 divided by 4, or 5. Taking the unweighted mean of the two file means would give 6 because it would assign the same weight to groups of different sizes.
