Only the first valid record for each identifier is accepted. The `(id, value)` records are `(4, 2)`, `(4, 8)`, `(7, 1)`, `(2, 3)` and `(7, 6)`, all valid. What is the sum of the accepted values?

- $17$
- $20$
- $11$
- $6$

## Explanation

Identifier $4$ contributes $2$ on its first appearance. Identifiers $7$ and $2$ contribute $1$ and $3$, respectively. The later appearances of $4$ and $7$ are duplicates and are ignored. The sum is $2+1+3=6$.
