Among records with the maximum value, the **numerically smallest identifier** must be chosen regardless of file order. The current candidate is `(id=8, value=13)`. Which incoming record must replace it specifically because of the tie rule?

- `(id=3, value=13)`
- `(id=2, value=12)`
- `(id=9, value=13)`
- `(id=8, value=12)`

## Explanation

The primary rule maximises the value, so only records with value 13 enter the tie comparison. Of the two candidates with that value, identifier 3 improves on the current 8 whereas 9 would be worse. An even smaller identifier cannot compensate for a lower value.
