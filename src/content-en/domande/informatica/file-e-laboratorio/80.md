A log supplies 800 integers, each between −100 and 100. The final mathematical total is zero. On a machine with `INT_MAX == 32767`, which argument justifies using a `long` accumulator throughout the scan?

- Every prefix has absolute total at most 80000, a representable C17 `long` bound even without using the final-total information.
- The number of valid lines always equals the maximum partial total.
- Since the final result is zero, each prefix must be zero.
- Integer sums are automatically calculated in `long` when input comes from a file.

## Explanation

After at most 800 additions of terms of absolute value at most 100, the absolute value of any partial sum is at most 80000. This conservative bound fits the guaranteed range of `long`. For 400 readings of 100 followed by 400 of −100, the total returns to zero after reaching 40000, beyond the limit of this `int`.
