In the C locale, with `end` declared as `char *`, what does `strtol(" -031  ", &end, 10)` return?

- $-31$
- $-25$
- $31$
- $0$

## Explanation

The conversion skips the leading space and recognizes the minus sign. The third argument fixes base $10$, so `031` has value $31$. The space after the digits ends the numeric sequence and the result is $-31$.
