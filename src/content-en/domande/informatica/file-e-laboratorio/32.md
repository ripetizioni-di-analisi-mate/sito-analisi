In the C locale, `"12, -8"` is read as two integers separated by a comma. Use base-$10$ `strtol` for the first field, consume the comma and use `strtol` for the second field. What is the sum?

- $4$
- $12$
- $20$
- $-4$

## Explanation

The first conversion stops at the comma and gives $12$. After passing the separator, the second skips the space and reads $-8$. The sum is $12-8=4$. The comma belongs to neither integer.
