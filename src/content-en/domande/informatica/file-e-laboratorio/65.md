A log identifies sensors by the **decimal value** of the first field. Leading zeros are permitted, and readings must be summed per sensor. The records are `"09 4"`, `"9 3"` and `"10 5"`. What should the report show for sensor 9?

- A total of 3 from one reading.
- A total of 12 from three readings.
- A total of 7 from two readings.
- A total of 4 from one reading.

## Explanation

With base-ten conversion both 09 and 9 identify sensor 9. The associated readings are 4 and 3, giving a total of 7. Grouping by literal comparison of the field text would incorrectly split this single identifier into two groups.
