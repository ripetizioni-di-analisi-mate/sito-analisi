A file contains the text `ABCDE F`. Using two distinct four-`char` arrays, two successful consecutive calls to `fscanf(f, "%3s", buffer)` each read one string. What are the two results?

- `"ABCD"` and `"E"`
- `"ABC"` and `"DE"`
- `"AB"` and `"CDE"`
- `"ABC"` and `"F"`

## Explanation

Width 3 limits the first conversion to three assigned characters in addition to the terminator written into the array. The remainder of the word stays in the stream. The second call therefore starts at D and stops at the space after E, leaving F for a later read.
