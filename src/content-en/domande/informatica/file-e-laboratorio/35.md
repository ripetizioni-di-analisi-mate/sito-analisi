On a writable stream, what does the successful call `fprintf(file, "%s:%02d\n", "R", 7)` return?

- $4$
- $6$
- $5$
- $3$

## Explanation

`%s` writes `R` and the next character of the format is the separator. `%02d` writes `07` because it requests a minimum two-character width with zero padding. The newline is then transmitted. The count is $1+1+2+1=5$ characters.
