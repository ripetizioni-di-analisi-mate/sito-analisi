The records are `(A, 4)`, `(A, -5)`, `(B, 2)` and `(B, 7)`. A sum is maintained per category. What is the final `B` total minus the `A` total?

- $10$
- $8$
- $-10$
- $9$

## Explanation

Category `A` has total $4-5=-1$. Category `B` has total $2+7=9$. The difference in the requested order is $9-(-1)=10$. A category total can be negative.
