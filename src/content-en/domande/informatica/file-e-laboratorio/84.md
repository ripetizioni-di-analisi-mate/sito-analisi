Two scanners must produce identical results on complete records. The first receives text in one-byte chunks, the second receives the entire text at once. What property should a test verify when both scans process `"12\n34\n"`?

- The second recognises just one record because it receives a single chunk.
- The first recognises four numeric records because it receives four separate digits.
- Both recognise two numeric records totalling 46.
- The total depends on how many times the reader is called.

## Explanation

In the stated format a newline delimits each record. A chunk boundary may fall inside a reading, so the scanner must preserve the prefix until the separator arrives. Both scans must reconstruct 12 and 34 and obtain their sum of 46.
