A direct-mapped cache has four lines and eight-byte blocks. The block number is $4t+i$, where $t$ is the tag and $i$ is the line index. Line $2$ is valid, has tag $3$ and holds the requested byte at offset $5$. What is the byte’s address?

- $117$
- $61$
- $19$
- $112$

## Explanation

First reconstruct the block number, $4\cdot3+2=14$. Block $14$ starts at address $14\cdot8=112$. The offset picks out the requested byte within that block, giving address $112+5=117$.
