A peripheral permits a single transfer only if every requested byte is present and the requested addresses are consecutive. The only populated ranges are $[20,30)$ and $[32,40)$. Which read meets the rule?

- $8$ bytes starting at $33$
- $2$ bytes starting at $29$
- $8$ bytes starting at $32$
- $4$ bytes starting at $28$

## Explanation

A read of $n$ bytes from $a$ uses addresses $a$ through $a+n-1$. Eight bytes from $32$ end at $39$, still in the second range. The reads starting at $28$ and $29$ include missing byte $30$, while eight bytes from $33$ reach missing byte $40$.
