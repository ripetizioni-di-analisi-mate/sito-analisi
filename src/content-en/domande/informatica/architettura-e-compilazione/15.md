In a unified-address-space model, a cell can be reached during instruction fetch and during a data read. What determines the role of its bits on a given access?

- Only the numerical value in the cell
- The operation that interprets the bits
- The sign of its address
- Only the position of the cell

## Explanation

Instruction fetch supplies the sequence to the decoding mechanism. A read performed as a data operation supplies the same bits as a value. In this model the access determines which interpretation applies.
