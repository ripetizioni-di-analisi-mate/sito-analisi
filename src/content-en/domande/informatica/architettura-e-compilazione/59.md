A device stores $3$ octets. When they are read as an unsigned integer, which sequence in increasing address order gives the same value under big-endian and little-endian interpretations?

- `2A 00 2A`
- `2A 2A 00`
- `00 2A 2A`
- `00 2A 01`

## Explanation

Reversing the three octets leaves the middle one at weight $256$. If the end octets are $p$ and $q$, the difference between the interpretations is $(p-q)(256^2-1)$. Equality therefore requires matching ends, as in `2A 00 2A`.
