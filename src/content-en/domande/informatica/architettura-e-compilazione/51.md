`A` holds $6$. A machine copies `A` to cell `x` with `STORE x` and then reloads `A` from `x`. Only the final state is recorded, with both `x` and `A` equal to $6$. Which integers are possible as the initial contents of `x`?

- All representable integers except $6$
- Any integer the cell can hold
- Only $6$
- Only $0$

## Explanation

The first instruction writes $6$ before the old contents of `x` are read. The following load therefore sees $6$ for every initial state. A cell that already held $6$ produces the recorded trace as well.
