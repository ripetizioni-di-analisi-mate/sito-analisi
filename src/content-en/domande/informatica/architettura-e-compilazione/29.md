Two independent trials start with an empty direct-mapped cache with four lines and four-byte blocks aligned from byte $0$. Trial U reads $5,6,7,4$. Trial V reads $0,4,8,12$. A miss loads the requested block. How many more hits does U produce than V?

- $3$
- $4$
- $1$
- $0$

## Explanation

In trial U every address is in block $1$. The first read loads it and the following three find it. Trial V visits each of blocks $0,1,2,3$ once, so every read misses. The difference in hit counts is $3-0=3$.
