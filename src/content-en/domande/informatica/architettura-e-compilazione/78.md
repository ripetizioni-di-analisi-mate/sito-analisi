Three object files take $2$, $5$ and $4$ seconds to produce, respectively. The three jobs are independent and a system with three processors starts all of them at time zero. Linking starts only when every object is ready and takes $3$ seconds. Ignoring other costs, when is the executable ready?

- After $8$ seconds
- After $5$ seconds
- After $7$ seconds
- After $14$ seconds

## Explanation

The three object-generation jobs overlap. The last finishes at time $5$, which determines when linking can start. The executable is ready after $5+3=8$ seconds. Summing all three compilation times would describe a serial build.
