On a trace of one hundred sequential accesses, cache A takes $1\,\mathrm{ns}$ per lookup and incurs $20$ misses. Cache B takes $2\,\mathrm{ns}$ and incurs $10$ misses. Both have the same additional penalty $P$ per miss. At what value of $P$ are their total times equal?

- $20\,\mathrm{ns}$
- $1\,\mathrm{ns}$
- $10\,\mathrm{ns}$
- $5\,\mathrm{ns}$

## Explanation

The hundred lookups cost $100\,\mathrm{ns}$ in A and $200\,\mathrm{ns}$ in B. B makes up the difference by avoiding ten misses. At the break-even point $10P=100\,\mathrm{ns}$, so $P=10\,\mathrm{ns}$ and both caches take $300\,\mathrm{ns}$.
