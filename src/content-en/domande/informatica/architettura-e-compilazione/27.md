Eight reads run sequentially and six hit. Every read looks up the cache for $3\,\mathrm{ns}$, and a miss takes another $20\,\mathrm{ns}$. Find the total time for all eight reads.

- $58\,\mathrm{ns}$
- $40\,\mathrm{ns}$
- $64\,\mathrm{ns}$
- $24\,\mathrm{ns}$

## Explanation

There are eight lookups, costing $8\cdot3=24\,\mathrm{ns}$. The two misses add $2\cdot20=40\,\mathrm{ns}$. The total time is $24+40=64\,\mathrm{ns}$. Dividing by eight would instead give the mean per access.
