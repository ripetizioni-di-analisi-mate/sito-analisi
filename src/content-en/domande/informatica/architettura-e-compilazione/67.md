A benchmark has two phases. All $20$ reads in the first phase miss. The second phase has $80$ reads, three quarters of which hit. What percentage of all reads hit?

- $75\%$
- $37.5\%$
- $40\%$
- $60\%$

## Explanation

The first phase contributes zero hits and the second contributes $80\cdot\dfrac{3}{4}=60$. Across the hundred reads, the hit rate is therefore $60\%$. Taking the unweighted average of the two phase rates, $37.5\%$, would give the phases equal weight despite their different numbers of reads.
