A bus always transfers aligned groups of four bytes, $[0,4)$, $[4,8)$ and so on. To serve a read it fetches each group containing at least one requested byte exactly once. How many bytes cross the bus to deliver the six bytes at addresses $3$ through $8$ inclusive?

- $24$
- $6$
- $12$
- $8$

## Explanation

Byte $3$ requires group $[0,4)$, bytes $4$ through $7$ are in the second group, and byte $8$ requires the third. Thus $3\cdot4=12$ bytes cross the bus. The recipient uses only six of them, which affects transfer efficiency.
