## Explanation

In little-endian order the byte at the lower address has weight $1$. The value of `0x34` contributes $52$, while `0x12` contributes $18\cdot256$. The sum is $4660$, written `0x1234`. Byte order applies to the whole word, so the digits within each byte keep their order.
