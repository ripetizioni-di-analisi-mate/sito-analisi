A teaching machine uses $10$-bit addresses. Each combination identifies one physically present four-byte word, and distinct words occupy disjoint areas. What is the capacity in bytes?

- $1024$
- $4096$
- $4100$
- $16384$

## Explanation

The address width gives $2^{10}=1024$ distinct words. Each word occupies four bytes and the areas are disjoint, so the capacity in bytes is $1024\cdot4=4096$. The addressed unit is a word.
