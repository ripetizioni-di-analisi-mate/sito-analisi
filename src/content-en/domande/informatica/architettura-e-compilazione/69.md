Of $200$ reads, $150$ find their block in the first-level cache. Only the remaining reads consult the second level, which satisfies four out of every five requests it receives. How many of the $200$ reads reach main memory?

- $40$
- $160$
- $10$
- $50$

## Explanation

The second level receives $200-150=50$ requests. One in five of those continues to main memory, giving $\dfrac{50}{5}=10$ reads. The second-level fraction applies to the requests that actually reach that level.
