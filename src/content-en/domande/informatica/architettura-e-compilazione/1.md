- $8192\,\mathrm{byte}$
- $4096\,\mathrm{byte}$
- $13\,\mathrm{byte}$
- $65536\,\mathrm{byte}$

## Explanation

Each of the $13$ bits has two possible values, so there are $2^{13}=8192$ distinct addresses. Addresses and bytes are in one-to-one correspondence, giving a capacity of $8192\,\mathrm{byte}$. The address width supplies the exponent.
