A relocation field stores the difference in bytes between the address of symbol `dest` and a base `ref`. Before final layout, `dest` is at $160$ and `ref` at $132$. The linker shifts both addresses forward by $64$ bytes. What should the field contain after the shift?

- $-28$
- $28$
- $224$
- $92$

## Explanation

The new addresses are $224$ and $196$. Subtracting them in the specified order gives $224-196=28$. The shared shift cancels in the subtraction, so a field relative to that base retains its original value.
