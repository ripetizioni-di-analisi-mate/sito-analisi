A machine uses unsigned integer addresses. Its map includes decimal address $767$ and no higher address. A total of $300$ bytes are physically present. What is the minimum address width that can express all positions in the map?

- $9$
- $10$
- $8$
- $11$

## Explanation

With $9$ bits the maximum is $2^9-1=511$, below $767$. With $10$ bits the maximum is $2^{10}-1=1023$, which covers all required addresses. Representability depends on the map’s highest address, even though the amount of physical memory is smaller.
