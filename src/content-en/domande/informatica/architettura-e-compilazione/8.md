## Explanation

The miss fraction is $1-\dfrac{3}{4}=\dfrac{1}{4}$. The lookup cost is paid on every access, while the additional cost applies only to misses. The mean time is therefore $2+\dfrac{1}{4}\cdot40=12\,\mathrm{ns}$.
