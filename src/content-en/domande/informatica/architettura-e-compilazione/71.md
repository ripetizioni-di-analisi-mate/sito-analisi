A cache already holds the byte block $[8,16)$, while block $[16,24)$ is absent. The blocks use different lines. For a single four-byte read starting at address $14$, the controller looks up each required block once in increasing address order. What sequence of results does it obtain?

- Miss, then hit
- Hit, then miss
- A single hit
- A single miss

## Explanation

The request covers bytes $14$, $15$, $16$ and $17$. The first two are in the resident block and produce one hit. The other two are in the next block and require a single fill following the miss.
