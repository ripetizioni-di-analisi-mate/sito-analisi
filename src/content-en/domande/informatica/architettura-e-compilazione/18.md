The counter starts at $0$. `LOAD` loads the accumulator from the named cell, `ADD` adds that cell’s value to the accumulator and `STORE` writes the accumulator into the cell. These instructions advance the counter by one. `JZ a` sets the counter to $a$ if the accumulator is zero and otherwise increments it. Initially $M[20]=2$, $M[21]=-1$ and $M[22]=0$. How many instructions execute, including `HALT`?

```text
0 LOAD 20
1 ADD 21
2 STORE 20
3 JZ 6
4 LOAD 22
5 JZ 0
6 HALT
```

- $10$
- $12$
- $11$
- $7$

## Explanation

On the first pass, cell $20$ falls from $2$ to $1$. All six instructions from $0$ to $5$ execute, and the last returns the counter to $0$ because loading $M[22]$ set the accumulator to zero. On the second pass, cell $20$ falls to zero, so $0,1,2,3,6$ execute. The total is $6+5=11$.
