A C17 configuration contains `#define FLAG 0`. A header encloses the declaration `int a;` between `#ifdef FLAG` and `#endif`, then encloses `int b;` between `#if FLAG` and `#endif`. Which declarations survive preprocessing?

- Both
- Only `int a;`
- Neither
- Only `int b;`

## Explanation

`#ifdef` checks whether a macro definition exists, and `FLAG` is defined. `#if` expands the macro and evaluates its numerical value, which here is zero. The first group’s text remains and the second group is discarded.
