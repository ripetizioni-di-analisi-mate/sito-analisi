With a GCC-compatible driver, `cc -std=c17 -E model.c -o result.o` succeeds. What material is in `result.o`?

- Assembly listing
- Relocatable object
- Linked executable
- Text resulting from preprocessing

## Explanation

The `-E` option tells the driver to stop after preprocessing. `-o result.o` assigns the output name. The chosen suffix is unusual here, and the contents remain the text produced by the preprocessor.
