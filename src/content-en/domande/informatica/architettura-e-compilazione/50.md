Register `PC` is $8$ both before and after a step. In this model, `HALT` preserves `PC` and `A` and sets the halted bit. `JZ 8` can also leave `PC` unchanged when `A` is zero, in which case the machine remains active. Before the step the machine was active and `A` was zero. Which observation after the step distinguishes the two cases with certainty without inspecting the code?

- The value of `A`
- The contents of the last data cell read before the step
- The address of the instruction just executed
- The value of the halted bit

## Explanation

Both instructions execute at the same address and preserve the accumulator. The branch allows another fetch at address $8$, whereas `HALT` sets the halted bit. That bit distinguishes the two states even though the observed `PC` is the same.
