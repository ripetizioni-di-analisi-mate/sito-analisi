A sensor sends two octets, most significant first. A C17 program receives them separately as `unsigned int` values `a` and `b` between $0$ and $255$. Which expression reconstructs the transmitted value on any C17 machine, regardless of its in-memory byte order?

- `a * 16u + b`
- `b * 256u + a`
- `a * 256u + b`
- `a + b`

## Explanation

The first octet has weight $256$ and the second has weight one. The expression works with the two values already read, and its maximum is $255\cdot256+255=65535$, representable in `unsigned int` in C17. Octets `0x01` and `0x02` give $258$.
