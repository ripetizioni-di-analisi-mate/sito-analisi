Starting with an empty cache, a benchmark reads the first byte of each of $12$ distinct blocks exactly once. Each miss transfers exactly $32$ bytes from memory, and other transfers are ignored. What fraction of the transferred bytes does the program actually request?

- $\dfrac{1}{32}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{12}$
- $\dfrac{12}{32}$

## Explanation

Twelve blocks are loaded for a total of $12\cdot32=384$ bytes. The program requests twelve bytes, one per block. Dividing $12$ by $384$ gives $\dfrac{1}{32}$. Every fill also supplies the thirty-one neighbouring bytes.
