An unsigned word has four eight-bit bytes. In increasing address order it contains `0x10`, `0x22`, `0x00`, `0x01`. What is the value of the word in little-endian order?

- `0x10220001`
- `0x01002210`
- `0x01220010`
- `0x01001022`

## Explanation

In the hexadecimal notation of the number, write the bytes from most to least significant. In little-endian order this goes from highest to lowest address, giving `01 00 22 10`. The internal zero remains because it occupies the whole position of weight $256^2$.
