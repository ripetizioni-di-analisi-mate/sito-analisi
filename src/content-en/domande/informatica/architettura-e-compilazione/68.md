Twenty-five sequential reads must finish in at most $118\,\mathrm{ns}$. Each lookup takes $2\,\mathrm{ns}$, and each miss adds $9\,\mathrm{ns}$. What is the largest allowable integer number of misses?

- $6$
- $7$
- $13$
- $8$

## Explanation

Lookups consume $25\cdot2=50\,\mathrm{ns}$ even if all reads hit. This leaves $68\,\mathrm{ns}$ for penalties. Seven misses consume $63$ of those nanoseconds, giving $113\,\mathrm{ns}$ in total. Eight misses would take the total to $122\,\mathrm{ns}$, beyond the deadline.
