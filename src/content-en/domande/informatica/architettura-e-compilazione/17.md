In an integer-cell model, `LOAD` loads the named cell into the accumulator, `STORE` writes the accumulator to the named cell and `ADD` adds the data currently in the named cell to the accumulator. The instructions run in order. Given initial values $M[30]=5$, $M[31]=2$ and $M[32]=9$, find the final value of $M[32]$.

```text
LOAD 30
STORE 31
ADD 31
STORE 32
```

- $7$
- $10$
- $5$
- $9$

## Explanation

After loading, the accumulator is $5$. The first write updates $M[31]$ to $5$ and leaves the accumulator unchanged. Addition therefore reads $5$ from cell $31$ and gives $5+5=10$. The second write copies $10$ into cell $32$.
