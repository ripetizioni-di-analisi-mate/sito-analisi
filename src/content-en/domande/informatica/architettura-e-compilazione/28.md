Half the accesses hit. The total time for a hit is $2\,\mathrm{ns}$ and the total time for a miss, including the lookup, is $38\,\mathrm{ns}$. What is the mean time?

- $21\,\mathrm{ns}$
- $20\,\mathrm{ns}$
- $19\,\mathrm{ns}$
- $40\,\mathrm{ns}$

## Explanation

The two outcomes occur equally often, so the mean is $\dfrac{2+38}{2}=20\,\mathrm{ns}$. The value $38$ already includes the lookup for a miss. Adding $2$ again would change the stated cost.
