Byte-addressed memory is present exactly in ranges `[0x000, 0x280)` and `[0x300, 0x400)`. Addresses outside the two ranges are empty. How many bytes are physically present?

- $1024$
- $640$
- $896$
- $897$

## Explanation

The first range has length $(280)_{16}=640$ bytes. The second has length $(100)_{16}=256$ bytes. Because they are disjoint, add their lengths to obtain $640+256=896$ bytes. The $128$-byte gap between the ranges is excluded.
