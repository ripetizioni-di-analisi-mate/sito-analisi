A direct-mapped cache has four lines numbered from zero. Memory starts at byte $0$ and is divided into eight-byte blocks. The line is the remainder of the block number divided by four. What is the pair of line and within-block offset, in that order, for address $54$?

- $(2,6)$
- $(6,2)$
- $(2,2)$
- $(3,6)$

## Explanation

Block $6$ starts at $6\cdot8=48$ and contains $54$. The offset is therefore $54-48=6$. Dividing block number $6$ by $4$ gives remainder $2$, which is the line. The pair is $(2,6)$.
