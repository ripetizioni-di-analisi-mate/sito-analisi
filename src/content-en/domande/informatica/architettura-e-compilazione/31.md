What value does `value` hold after macro expansion and integer initialization?

```c
#define OFFSET 2 + 3
int value = 4 * OFFSET;
```

- $20$
- $9$
- $11$
- $14$

## Explanation

Replacement produces `int value = 4 * 2 + 3;`. The preprocessor inserts the macro’s tokens into the expression. Multiplication takes precedence over addition, giving $4\cdot2+3=11$. The value $20$ would require parentheses around `2 + 3`.
