An ordinary build takes $3\,\mathrm{s}$ to preprocess, $8\,\mathrm{s}$ to compile to assembly and $2\,\mathrm{s}$ to assemble each rebuilt source. Linking takes $15\,\mathrm{s}$. Two objects are up to date, a third must be rebuilt and the executable must be linked again. The operations run sequentially and other costs are negligible. How long does this build take?

- $13\,\mathrm{s}$
- $41\,\mathrm{s}$
- $54\,\mathrm{s}$
- $28\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The affected source goes through the three stages to produce its object, taking $3+8+2=13\,\mathrm{s}$. The other two objects are reused. Linking runs once and adds $15\,\mathrm{s}$, so the total is $13+15=28\,\mathrm{s}$.
