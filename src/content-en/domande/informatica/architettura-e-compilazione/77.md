A header contains only `#define WIDTH BASE`. Before including it, `a.c` defines `BASE` as $3$ and `b.c` defines it as $8$. After the include, the files respectively define `int a(void){return WIDTH;}` and `int b(void){return WIDTH;}`. The translation units are compiled separately and linked. What is `b() - a()`?

- $11$
- $-5$
- $5$
- $0$

## Explanation

The header is preprocessed in each translation unit with that unit’s macro definitions. Consequently the body of `a` returns $3$ and the body of `b` returns $8$. Linking makes both functions available, and the caller computes $8-3=5$.
