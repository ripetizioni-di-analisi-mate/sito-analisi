The project objects contain external references to `base`, `delta`, `edge` and `print`. Two separate objects use the name `delta`. The linker inputs provide one ordinary definition of `base` and one of `print`. Neither of the other names has a definition. How many distinct names remain without definitions?

- $3$
- $4$
- $1$
- $2$

## Explanation

Remove `base` and `print` from the set of four required names, since their definitions are available. This leaves `delta` and `edge`. Both uses of `delta` require a definition of the same name, contributing one entry to the name count.
