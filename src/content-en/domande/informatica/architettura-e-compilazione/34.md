One object file is produced for each source `a.c`, `b.c`, `c.c` and `d.c`. Their relevant dependencies are as follows.

| Source | Relevant dependencies |
|---|---|
| `a.c` | Directly includes `p.h` and `q.h` |
| `b.c` | Includes `p.h` |
| `c.c` | Includes `local.h`, which includes `q.h` |
| `d.c` | Includes neither, even indirectly |

The system recompiles a source once if at least one of its dependencies changes. Both `p.h` and `q.h` change. How many objects are rebuilt?

- $2$
- $4$
- $3$
- $1$

## Explanation

The change to `p.h` affects `a.c` and `b.c`. The change to `q.h` affects `a.c` and `c.c`, including through `local.h`. The union contains three source files. `a.c` counts once even though two of its dependencies changed.
