Over $100$ sequential reads the mean time is $11\,\mathrm{ns}$. Each lookup takes $1\,\mathrm{ns}$, and only a miss adds $40\,\mathrm{ns}$. How many misses occurred?

- $10$
- $20$
- $75$
- $25$

## Explanation

The mean includes $1\,\mathrm{ns}$ for the lookup common to every access. This leaves $11-1=10\,\mathrm{ns}$ per access as the mean penalty contribution. The miss fraction is $\dfrac{10}{40}=\dfrac{1}{4}$, giving $100\cdot\dfrac{1}{4}=25$ misses.
