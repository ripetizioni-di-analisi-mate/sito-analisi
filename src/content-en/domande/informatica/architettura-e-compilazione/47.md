A machine with exact integer arithmetic executes `LOAD x`, `ADD y`, `JZ fine`. The first two instructions load the contents of `x` and add the contents of `y`. The trace shows that the branch to `fine` was taken because the accumulator was zero. Cell `y` held $-7$. What did `x` contain?

- $0$
- $7$
- $14$
- $-7$

## Explanation

The branch reveals the value after the addition. If $v$ is the value loaded from `x`, the trace gives $v+(-7)=0$, hence $v=7$. Substituting $7$ in the trace takes the accumulator from $7$ to zero as required.
