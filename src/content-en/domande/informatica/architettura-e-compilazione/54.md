Two descriptions of the same physical memory list the byte-address ranges $[64,96)$ and $[80,112)$. An address occurring in both descriptions denotes the same byte. How many distinct bytes are in their union?

- $16$
- $48$
- $64$
- $32$

## Explanation

Each interval contains $32$ bytes. Addresses $80$ through $95$ inclusive, or $16$ bytes, appear twice in the sum. The union therefore contains $32+32-16=48$ bytes, comprising addresses $64$ through $111$ inclusive.
