A four-byte unsigned word with eight-bit bytes is stored in little-endian order starting at $a$. Only the byte at address $a+2$ changes, from `0x1A` to `0x1B`. By how much does the word increase?

- $256$
- $16\,777\,216$
- $1$
- $65\,536$

## Explanation

The byte increases by one. In little-endian order offset zero has weight one, so offset two has weight $256^2$. The other bytes cancel when the two word values are subtracted, leaving $1\cdot256^2=65\,536$.
