The first free byte of memory is `0x12D`. A four-byte register must start at an address divisible by four. The aim is to leave as few bytes unused before the register as possible. Which pair gives the padding size and the register’s starting address?

- $0$ bytes, `0x12D`
- $2$ bytes, `0x12F`
- $3$ bytes, `0x130`
- $4$ bytes, `0x131`

## Explanation

The remainder of `0x12D` on division by four is one. The three bytes `0x12D`, `0x12E` and `0x12F` precede the first free address with zero remainder, `0x130`. The register’s four bytes are counted starting at that address.
