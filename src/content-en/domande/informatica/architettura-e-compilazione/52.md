On an accumulator machine, `LOAD` reads a cell, `ADD` adds a cell’s contents and `STORE` writes the accumulator to a cell. Cells `x`, `y` and `z` are distinct, and arithmetic is exact on integers. Which sequence always produces the same final accumulator and memory as `LOAD x; ADD y; STORE z`?

- `LOAD x; STORE z; ADD y`
- `LOAD z; ADD y; STORE x`
- `LOAD y; STORE x; ADD x`
- `LOAD y; ADD x; STORE z`

## Explanation

Both reads take place before the write. Reversing their order gives $y+x=x+y$, and `z` is still the only cell written. Moving `STORE z` earlier would store just one operand even if the accumulator later contained the correct sum.
