Two builds use the same original source but potentially different settings. The files saved immediately after preprocessing contain `int border = 4;` in the first build and `int border = 9;` in the second. Which conclusion is already established by this comparison?

- The linker modified both preprocessed files
- The difference must originate in the assembler
- The compiler inputs already differ in their initializers
- The two executables must print different values

## Explanation

The comparison is made before assembly generation and linking. It directly shows a difference in the tokens that will be compiled. Predicting a difference in output would also require knowing whether and how the program uses `border`.
