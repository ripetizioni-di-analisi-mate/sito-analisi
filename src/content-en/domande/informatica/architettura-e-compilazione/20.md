An unsigned two-byte word, with eight bits per byte, has value $783$. It is stored in big-endian order starting at address $a$. What is the decimal value of the byte at address $a+1$?

- $3$
- $15$
- $48$
- $0$

## Explanation

Dividing by $256$ gives $783=3\cdot256+15$. The quotient is the most significant byte and occupies address $a$ in big-endian order. The remainder is the least significant byte, at $a+1$, with value $15$.
