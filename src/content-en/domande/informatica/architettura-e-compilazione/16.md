A teaching machine runs the following program from address $0$. `LOAD` loads the specified cell into the accumulator, `ADD` adds its contents and `STORE` writes the accumulator into the cell. These instructions advance by one cell. `JZ` jumps to its operand if the accumulator is zero and otherwise advances by one cell. `HALT` stops. Initially $M[11]=6$, $M[12]=-6$, $M[13]=5$ and $M[14]=9$. What is the final value of $M[14]$?

```text
0 LOAD 11
1 ADD 12
2 JZ 4
3 LOAD 13
4 STORE 14
5 HALT
```

- $5$
- $6$
- $9$
- $0$

## Explanation

`LOAD 11` sets the accumulator to $6$. After `ADD 12` it contains $6+(-6)=0$. The jump condition holds, so execution continues at cell $4$ with the accumulator still zero. `STORE 14` writes zero to the requested cell.
