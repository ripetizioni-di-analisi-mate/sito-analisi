For a $16$-element array, every call of length $n>1$ inspects all its $n$ elements once and then calls the routine on the two equal halves. Leaves of length one inspect no elements. How many inspections are performed in total?

- $32$
- $31$
- $64$
- $80$

## Explanation

The internal node lengths are $16$, $8$, $4$ and $2$, spread over four levels. At each level the segments partition the original array, so the sum of their lengths is always $16$. The leaves add no inspections. The total is $4\cdot16=64$.
