## Explanation

The path $5,4,3,2,1$ reaches a leaf with five active records, counting both endpoints. Each child call decreases its argument by at least one, and recursion stops at $0$ or $1$. A branch that subtracts two cannot increase that maximum. Each child also completes its own subtree before the next child starts.
