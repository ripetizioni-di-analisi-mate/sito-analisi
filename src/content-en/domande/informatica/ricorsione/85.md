In recursive binary search on a nondecreasing array over $[l,r)$, after reading `a[m]` we learn that the key is greater than `a[m]`. Which interval should the child receive to retain all remaining candidate positions and strictly reduce the length even when $r-l=1$?

- $[m+1,r)$
- $[l,m)$
- $[m,r)$
- $[m+1,r-1)$

## Explanation

The array is assumed sorted in nondecreasing order. All positions up to $m$ have values at most `a[m]`, so they can be excluded. If $m<r-1$, position $r-1$ remains a candidate and must be part of the child interval. If the segment contained only $m$, the new left endpoint equals $r$ and the child receives the empty base interval.
