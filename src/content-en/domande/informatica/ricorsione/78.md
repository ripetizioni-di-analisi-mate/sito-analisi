To process a list of $n$ elements, A passes a reference to its suffix without copying it. B copies the entire suffix remaining after the first element before each descent and stops at an empty list. Starting at $n=6$, how many elements does B copy in total?

- $12$
- $6$
- $15$
- $21$

## Explanation

The first list has six elements, but the copied suffix contains five. Subsequent copies contain four, three, two, one and zero elements respectively. The total copied is $5+4+3+2+1+0=15$. The two routines can have the same depth while doing different amounts of work at each step.
