Two recursions split blocks of eight elements down to individual leaves. A always peels one element from the rest. B always splits into equal halves. Each finishes one child before starting the other. What are the maximum logical depths of A and B, counting roots and leaves?

- $(8,8)$
- $(8,4)$
- $(15,15)$
- $(7,3)$

## Explanation

A’s longest branch traverses lengths $8,7,6,5,4,3,2,1$, so eight frames are simultaneously active. In B the path lengths are $8,4,2,1$, giving four frames. Both strategies produce eight leaves, but the number of leaves does not determine the longest path.
