A routine recursively visits a triangle of digits. From a digit one may descend to the digit at the same index or the next one on its right in the following row. The rows are `[4]`, `[8,3]`, `[1,1,9]`. What is the largest sum along a path from the root to the final row?

- $21$
- $12$
- $13$
- $16$

## Explanation

The final row returns the value of each cell directly. From the node $8$ only the two ones can be reached, so its best result is $9$. From the node $3$ the $9$ can be reached, giving $12$. The root adds $4$ to the larger of the two results and returns $16$.
