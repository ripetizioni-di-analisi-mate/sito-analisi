- $\dfrac{n(n-1)}{2}$
- $n^2$
- $\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}$
- $\dfrac{(n+1)(n+2)}{2}$

## Explanation

At $n=0$ the return is zero, as the formula requires. For $n>0$, the induction hypothesis gives $\dfrac{(n-1)n}{2}$ for the child call. The return adds $n$, so $n+\dfrac{(n-1)n}{2}=\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}$. The relation holds across the given domain.
