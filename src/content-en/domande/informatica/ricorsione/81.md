A routine must compute the maximum gain over a nonempty prefix of a sequence of gains and losses, namely the largest sum of any nonempty prefix. Assume $n\ge 2$. For the first $n-1$ elements, a child has returned the total $s$ and the greatest sum $m$ of a nonempty prefix. On appending the final element $v$, which new pair should be returned?

- $(s+v,\max(m,s+v))$
- $(\max(s,v),\max(m,s+v))$
- $(s+v,m+v)$
- $(s+v,\max(m,v))$

## Explanation

The new total is $s+v$. Existing prefixes do not change, so their best sum is still $m$. The only previously unexamined prefix contains all $n$ elements and has sum $s+v$. Comparing these two candidates suffices even when $v$ is negative.
