A tail recursion advances along the Fibonacci sequence. State $(r,a,b)$ stores two consecutive terms and the remaining number $r$ of advances. At $r=0$ it returns $a$. Starting at $(5,0,1)$, which simultaneous update respects the meaning of the three fields?

- $(r-1,b,2b)$
- $(r-1,a,a+b)$
- $(r-1,b,a+b)$
- $(r-1,a+b,b)$

## Explanation

If $a=F_k$ and $b=F_{k+1}$, the next pair must be $(F_{k+1},F_{k+2})=(b,a+b)$. The sum uses both old values. Five updates give $(1,1)$, $(1,2)$, $(2,3)$, $(3,5)$ and $(5,8)$. At the base, the first value, $5$, is returned.
