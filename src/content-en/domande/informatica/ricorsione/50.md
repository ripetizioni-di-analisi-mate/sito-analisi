Three disks numbered from smallest (1) to largest (3) must move from peg A to peg C. To move $n>0$ disks, the procedure first moves the top $n-1$ to the spare peg, transfers disk $n$ to the destination, then moves the $n-1$ from the spare peg to the destination. The base case $n=0$ makes no move. Which transfer is sixth, counting from one?

- Disk 2 from B to C.
- Disk 1 from B to A.
- Disk 3 from A to C.
- Disk 2 from A to B.

## Explanation

The first subproblem takes three transfers to move disks 1 and 2 from A to B. The fourth transfer moves disk 3 from A to C. Two disks remain to be moved from B to C, with A as the spare peg. On step five disk 1 goes from B to A and frees disk 2, which moves from B to C on step six.
