We want to decide whether an array of positive weights has a subset summing to $t$. When $t>0$ the first weight may be taken or skipped. Under which condition is it correct to prune the branch taking that weight, regardless of the array suffix?

- The first weight exceeds $t$.
- The first weight is odd and $t$ is even.
- The first weight equals $t$.
- The first weight is below $t$ and the suffix is nonempty.

## Explanation

If the weight exceeds the target, taking it already gives a partial sum greater than $t$. Every later weight is positive, so no continuation of that branch can undo the excess. If the weight equaled $t$, taking it would immediately give a solution with an empty suffix selection.
