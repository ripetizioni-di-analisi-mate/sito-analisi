An expression tree contains constant leaves, unary operators and binary operators. The recursive contract assigns depth one to a constant and counts the number of nodes on the longest root-to-leaf path. At a binary node whose children have depths $p,q$, which formula proves the contract step?

- $1+\max(p,q)$
- $1+p+q$
- $\max(p,q)$
- $1+\min(p,q)$

## Explanation

Every path from the current node continues into just one child. A path through the first has at most $1+p$ nodes, and one through the second has at most $1+q$. A longest path in the deeper child attains the larger bound. The current node contributes exactly one position.
