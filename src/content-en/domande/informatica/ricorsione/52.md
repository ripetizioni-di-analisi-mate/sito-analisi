A recursive check reads the two ends of a word. Different ends return false. Matching ends trigger a check of the interior. Zero or one character returns true without comparisons. Which shortest example returns false after exactly two comparisons?

- `abaca`
- `abcb`
- `aba`
- `abca`

## Explanation

Two pairs of characters are required, so the word needs at least four characters. In `abca` the two outer `a` characters match. The inner pair `b`, `c` fails the second comparison. A three-character word can compare only one pair because its central character is already a base case.
