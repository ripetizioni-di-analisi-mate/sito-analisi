In a memory model a call with argument $n$ reserves exactly $n$ cells and retains them until it returns. It starts at $n=16$, makes a single call with $n/2$, and stops descending at $n=1$, which also reserves one cell. How many cells are occupied at the peak?

- $31$
- $80$
- $16$
- $30$

## Explanation

The maximum occurs just after the base call has been entered. The calls of lengths $16,8,4,2$ are still active and the last one of length $1$ has joined them. The number of cells is $16+8+4+2+1=31$. The depth is five, but the model’s frames have different sizes.
