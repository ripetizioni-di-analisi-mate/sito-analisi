A function splits a half-open interval $[l,r)$ into $[l,m)$ and $[m,r)$, where $m=l+\lfloor(r-l)/2\rfloor$. It makes both calls outside the base case. What is the minimal length condition for handling directly every case in which splitting would leave a child as large as the parent?

- $r-l\leq2$
- Only $r=l$
- $r-l\leq1$
- Only $r-l=1$

## Explanation

At length zero both children coincide with the empty interval. At length one, $m=l$ and the right child coincides with the whole interval. For lengths of at least two we have $l<m<r$, so both children are strictly shorter. An interval of length two can be split.
