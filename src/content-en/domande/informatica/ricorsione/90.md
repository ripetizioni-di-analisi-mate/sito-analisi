Two recursions concatenate the labels visited in a binary tree. A produces `ABDEC` by visiting node, left and right. B produces `DBEAC` by visiting left, node and right. Every label occurs exactly once. What are the left and right children, respectively, of the node labeled B?

- D and C.
- E and D.
- D and E.
- A and C.

## Explanation

The first traversal starts with A, so A is the root. In the second traversal the labels before A are `DBE`, precisely its left subtree. The first traversal enters that subtree at B. In the second traversal D precedes B and E follows it, so D is its left child and E its right child.
