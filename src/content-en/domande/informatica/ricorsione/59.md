An explicit stack initially holds the root. It replaces a recursion that records the node, visits the whole left subtree, then visits the right subtree. When popping a node with two children, in what order should the children be pushed to reproduce the visits?

- Only the left child, because its descendants will recreate the right one.
- Right first, then left.
- Either order, even when the children have different labels.
- Left first, then right.

## Explanation

A stack pops the most recently pushed node first. Pushing the right child first leaves it waiting beneath the left child. Each descendant of the left child is in turn pushed above the right child. The right child will therefore be popped only after the entire left subtree has been visited.
