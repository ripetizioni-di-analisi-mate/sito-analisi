A log records `+` on entry to each invocation and `-` immediately before it returns. Initially no calls are active. The complete log is `++-++---`. How many calls entered, and what was the maximum depth?

- $4$ entries, depth $4$.
- $3$ entries, depth $4$.
- $4$ entries, depth $3$.
- $8$ entries, depth $2$.

## Explanation

The four `+` signs count four entries. Finding the depth requires tracking the balance of entries minus returns. After each character the balance is $1,2,1,2,3,2,1,0$. Its maximum is three and is reached before the final three consecutive returns.
