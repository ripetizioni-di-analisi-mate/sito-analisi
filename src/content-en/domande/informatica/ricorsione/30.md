In a recursive function `unsigned f(unsigned n, unsigned acc)`, which form puts the call in tail position in the source code? Consider the branch with `n > 0u`.

- `return 1u + f(n - 1u, acc);`
- `return f(n - 1u, acc + 1u);`
- `return 1u * f(n - 1u, acc);`
- `return (f(n - 1u, acc), acc);`

## Explanation

In the direct return, the accumulator update is already part of the argument. Once the child returns, its value goes straight to the caller. The forms with addition or multiplication leave an operation to perform on that result. With the comma operator, `acc` is returned after the call has been evaluated.
