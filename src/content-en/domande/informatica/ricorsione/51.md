The base of a recursion on states $0,1,2,3$ is $0$. The other transitions are $1\to3$, $2\to0$, $3\to1$. What is the largest domain of initial states for which the specification can promise to reach the base?

- $\{0,2\}$
- $\{0,1,2\}$
- $\{2\}$
- $\{0,1,2,3\}$

## Explanation

Starting at $0$ already reaches the base. Starting at $2$ takes one transition. States $1$ and $3$ form a closed cycle, and neither leads to $0$. A domain containing either state would therefore promise termination for a trace that never reaches the base.
