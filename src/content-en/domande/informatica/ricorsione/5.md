## Explanation

Each call with a positive argument starts two calls whose argument is one smaller. The root with argument $3$ therefore gives $1$, $2$, $4$, and $8$ nodes on the four levels. The total is $1+2+4+8=15$. The function's returned value counts the eight leaves because each base case contributes one.
