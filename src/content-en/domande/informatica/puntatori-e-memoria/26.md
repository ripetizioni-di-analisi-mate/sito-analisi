Calculate `end - p` in the fragment shown.

```c
char s[] = "uno";
char *p = s + 2;
const char *end = s + sizeof s;
```

- $4$
- $2$
- $3$
- $1$

## Explanation

The literal `"uno"` has three visible characters plus the terminator. Thus `sizeof s` equals $4$. The pointers designate the one-past position at index $4$ and the cell at index $2$, respectively. Their difference is $4-2=2$ elements.
