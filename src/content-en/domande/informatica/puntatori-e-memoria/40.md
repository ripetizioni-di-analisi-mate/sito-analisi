A block for four `int` elements obtained with `malloc` received writes only at indices $0$ and $3$. Its capacity increases to seven after successful `realloc` with a positive representable size. How many elements hold an assigned value immediately after this?

- $7$
- $5$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

The expansion preserves the contents of the region for the first four integers. Within that region, values were stored only at indices $0$ and $3$. `realloc` does not initialize the added region. Thus only two elements hold assigned values.
