Reserve space with `int **p = calloc(4, sizeof *p);`. Assume `<stdlib.h>` is included, the total size is representable and the call succeeds. Which statement is guaranteed before assigning values to pointer cells?

- All bits have indeterminate values.
- Only the first cell has all bits zero.
- All four cells are guaranteed representations of null pointers.
- All bits in the block are zero.

## Explanation

When `calloc` succeeds, it initializes all bits in the region to zero. C17 does not require a null pointer representation to consist entirely of zero bits. To obtain null pointers, assign `NULL` to cells of pointer type.
