`p` and `q` point to elements of the same array. The result of `p - q` is representable and may be negative. With `<stddef.h>` and `<stdio.h>` included, which statement prints that result directly with the matching format?

- `printf("%td", p - q);`
- `printf("%zu", p - q);`
- `printf("%p", p - q);`
- `printf("%u", p - q);`

## Explanation

The difference between the two pointers has type `ptrdiff_t`, a signed integer type. The `t` length modifier with the `d` conversion matches that type. The `%zu` format expects a `size_t` argument, used for sizes such as the result of `sizeof`.
