Given `int a[4] = {2, 4, 6, 8};`, a loop starts at `a + 1` and advances while `p != last`. What value should `last` have to visit indices $1$, $2$ and $3$?

- `a + 4`
- `a + 1`
- `a + 3`
- `a + 2`

## Explanation

The final iteration must start at index $3$. Its increment moves the pointer to index $4$. Because the loop tests the boundary before reading, `a + 4` stops it exactly after that iteration.
