In the fragment, with `<stdlib.h>` included, suppose both allocations succeed and the product is representable. What is the program state at the end?

```c
int *p = malloc(sizeof *p);
int *fresh = malloc(2 * sizeof *fresh);
int *old = p;
p = fresh;
free(old);
```

- Two live blocks, one for each pointer.
- One live block, owned by `old`.
- One live block, owned by `p`.
- No live block.

## Explanation

The two successful allocations create two distinct blocks. After `p = fresh`, the variable `p` designates the later block. `old` retains the address of the first and allows it to be released. One live block remains, designated by `p`.
