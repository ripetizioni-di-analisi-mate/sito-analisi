What value does reading `*p` give after the declarations and assignment?

```c
int x = 4;
const int *p = &x;
int *q = &x;
*q = 11;
```

- $11$
- $15$
- $4$
- $7$

## Explanation

The qualification in `const int *p` restricts modification through `p`. The object `x` was declared as `int` and remains modifiable through `q`. Since both pointers designate it, reading `*p` after the write gives $11$.
