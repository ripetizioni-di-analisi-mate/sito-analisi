A function stores `n` measurements in a buffer and always reserves one additional cell for an end marker. The capacity is eight cells and `n` is six. How many more measurements can be appended before capacity must grow?

- $7$
- $2$
- $1$
- $0$

## Explanation

Appending one measurement uses seven data cells and places the marker in the eighth. A second measurement would require nine cells altogether. Under this contract the number of additional measurement slots is $8-6-1=1$.
