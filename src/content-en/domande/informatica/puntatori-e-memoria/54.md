An image is divided into rows of four `unsigned short` elements. Assume `sizeof(unsigned short) == 2` and that allocation succeeds. How many bytes does the call request?

```c
#include <stdlib.h>

unsigned short (*row)[4] = malloc(5 * sizeof *row);
```

- $20$
- $40$
- $8$
- $10$

## Explanation

`row` points to an array of four elements. Consequently `sizeof *row` is $4\cdot 2=8$ bytes. The call requests five such rows, or 40 bytes. The size of the pointer variable does not enter the product.
