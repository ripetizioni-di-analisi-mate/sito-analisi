With `<string.h>` included, `memmove` shifts the first three samples right by one position. What does the buffer contain afterwards?

```c
int a[] = {2, 5, 8, 9, 7};
memmove(a + 1, a, 3 * sizeof *a);
```

- $(5,8,9,9,7)$
- $(2,2,5,8,7)$
- $(2,2,2,2,7)$
- $(2,5,8,2,7)$

## Explanation

The original values at positions 0, 1, and 2 are copied into positions 1, 2, and 3. The effect of `memmove` is as if the copy went through a temporary buffer, even when the ranges overlap. The two positions outside the destination retain 2 and 7.
