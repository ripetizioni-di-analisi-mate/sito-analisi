Two pointers are swapped before one of the objects is updated. What are the final values of `(x,y)`?

```c
int x = 2, y = 7;
int *left = &x, *right = &y;
int *t = left;
left = right;
right = t;
*left -= *right;
```

- $(-5,7)$
- $(7,2)$
- $(5,2)$
- $(2,5)$

## Explanation

`t` saves the address of `x`. After the swap, `left` points to `y` and `right` to `x`, while the integers still hold their initial values. The final statement writes $7-2=5$ into `y` and leaves `x` equal to 2.
