Let `p` own a block for four integers obtained with `malloc` and initialized to $(10,5,16,3)$. With `<stddef.h>` and `<stdlib.h>` included, assume `realloc` succeeds and its size is representable. What is the value of `next[2]` at the end?

```c
int *view = p + 2;
size_t index = (size_t)(view - p);
int *next = realloc(p, 6 * sizeof *p);
next[index] += 3;
```

- $8$
- $16$
- $19$
- $13$

## Explanation

Before resizing, `index` receives $2$, a difference measured in elements. The new block preserves the four previous integers, so `next[index]` is $16$ before the write. The increment adds $3$ and produces $16+3=19$ at index $2$.
