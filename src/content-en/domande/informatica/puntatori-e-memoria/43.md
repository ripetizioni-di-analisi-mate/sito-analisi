`p` is the value returned by `malloc` for a live block with space for three integers. With `<stdlib.h>` included, which argument to `free` ends the lifetime of that block?

- `p + 1`
- `p + 3`
- `&p`
- `p`

## Explanation

`free` requires the starting address of the block returned by the allocation. That is the value of `p`. The displacements by one and three elements designate an internal position and the excluded boundary, respectively, while `&p` is the address of the pointer variable itself.
