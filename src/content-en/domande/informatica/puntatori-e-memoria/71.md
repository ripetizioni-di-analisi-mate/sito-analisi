On a system with `SIZE_MAX == 65535`, one block of bytes must hold a 15-byte header followed by 24-byte records. What is the largest record count for which the total size is representable?

- $2730$
- $2729$
- $2731$
- $2735$

## Explanation

Subtracting the 15-byte header leaves $65535-15=65520$ bytes. Dividing by 24 gives exactly 2730 records. One more record would bring the total to $65535+24$, above the largest representable size.
