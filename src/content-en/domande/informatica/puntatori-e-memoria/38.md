Let `p` be the starting pointer of a live block obtained with `malloc`, and let `int *q = p;`. After `free(p);`, which single command can make `q` null without reading its previous value?

- `q = q;`
- `int x = *q;`
- `free(q);`
- `q = NULL;`

## Explanation

After `free(p)` the block’s lifetime ends. The value of `q` that designated it is indeterminate. The direct assignment `q = NULL;` replaces that value without reading it. The other commands attempt a read or another release through the old reference.
