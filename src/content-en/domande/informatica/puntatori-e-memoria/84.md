Two implementations have 8-bit bytes and provide `uint16_t`. One is little-endian and the other big-endian. To store `0x1234` as two bytes ordered `0x12, 0x34` on both, which choice is portable under these assumptions?

- Always copy the two representation bytes in reverse address order.
- Convert the number’s address to `void *` before copying its two representation bytes.
- Always copy the two representation bytes in increasing address order.
- Store the value of the high bits first and then the low bits, extracted with a shift and mask.

## Explanation

Given value `v`, the expression `v >> 8` yields 18 and `v & 0xff` yields 52. Writing them into successive positions produces `0x12, 0x34`. The procedure operates on the numerical value, so it works with both stated representations.
