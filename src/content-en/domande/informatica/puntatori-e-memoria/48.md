What are the final values of `first` and `a[1]` after the two increment expressions?

```c
int a[] = {4, 9, 16};
int *p = a;
int first = *p++;
(*p)++;
```

- $(9,10)$
- $(4,9)$
- $(4,10)$
- $(5,9)$

## Explanation

In `*p++` the postfix increment applies to `p`. The expression uses its old address, so `first` receives 4 and `p` advances to the second element. The parentheses in `(*p)++` make the increment apply to the pointed-to integer, which changes from 9 to 10. The final pair is therefore $(4,10)$.
