On a machine where `sizeof(unsigned long)` is $8$, take `unsigned long a[8];`, `p = a + 6` and `q = p - 3`, with pointers of the corresponding type. What is the signed displacement of `q` relative to `p`, expressed mathematically in C bytes?

- $-3$
- $-32$
- $-24$
- $24$

## Explanation

The move from `p` to `q` is $-3$ elements. Because each element takes $8$ C bytes, the signed displacement is $-3\cdot8=-24$ C bytes. The sign comes from describing `q` relative to `p`.
