In the same twelve-sample array, one view covers indices from 2 inclusive to 9 exclusive, and another covers 6 inclusive to 11 exclusive. How many elements do they share?

- $7$
- $3$
- $4$
- $5$

## Explanation

The intersection begins at the larger start index, 6, and ends at the smaller exclusive bound, 9. It includes indices 6, 7, and 8. The two bounding pointers are therefore $9-6=3$ elements apart.
