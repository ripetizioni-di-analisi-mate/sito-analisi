A dynamic block holds 10, 20, 30, 40, and 50 in order. Its last two values must become the first two cells of the resulting block, and the capacity must shrink to two. Assume `realloc` succeeds. Which order preserves both required values?

- Copy cells 3 and 4 into cells 0 and 1, then shrink the block.
- Swap just cells 0 and 4, then shrink the block.
- Shrink the block on the assumption that its last two cells are kept.
- Copy cells 0 and 1 into cells 3 and 4, then shrink the block.

## Explanation

A successful shrink preserves the initial bytes up to the new size. To retain 40 and 50, they must already occupy the first two cells at the time of the request. Copying them into the front puts the required values in the prefix that survives the shrink.
