In a model where `SIZE_MAX` equals $65535$, a capacity of $1020$ elements increases by $5$. Each element takes $64$ C bytes. What are the two results of checking representability in `size_t`, first for the new element count and then for the requested bytes?

- The sum exceeds the limit.
- Both the sum and the product fit.
- The new element count equals the initial capacity.
- The sum fits, and the product exceeds the limit.

## Explanation

The increase gives $1020+5=1025$ elements, within the limit $65535$. Then compare $1025$ with $\left\lfloor\dfrac{65535}{64}\right\rfloor=1023$. The requested capacity exceeds this threshold because $1025\cdot64=65600$ C bytes. The second check stops the request.
