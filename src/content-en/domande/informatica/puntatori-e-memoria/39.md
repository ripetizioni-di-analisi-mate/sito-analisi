A four-integer block `p` obtained with `malloc` holds $(11,6,21,9)$. With `<stdlib.h>` included, execute `int *next = realloc(p, 2 * sizeof *p);`. Assume a representable size and success. What is the sum of the elements in the returned two-integer block?

- $15$
- $47$
- $27$
- $17$

## Explanation

The new capacity is two integers, so on success `realloc` preserves the prefix containing elements at indices $0$ and $1$. The new array holds $11$ and $6$. The requested sum is $11+6=17$.
