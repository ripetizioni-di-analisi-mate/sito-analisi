An iterative merge sort starts from ten singleton runs. On every round it merges adjacent pairs from the left and leaves an unpaired run for the next round. Which run lengths remain just after two rounds?

- `{4,3,3}`
- `{5,5}`
- `{4,4,2}`
- `{8,2}`

## Explanation

The first round pairs ten singletons to produce five runs of length two. The second pairs the first four runs, forming two runs of four entries. The fifth run retains length two.
