An array of $n\ge2$ entries has its first $n-1$ already increasing and its last smaller than every other entry. Insertion sort compares each new key with its predecessors from right to left and stops at the first predecessor less than or equal to it. What is the tight order of the total number of key comparisons on this family?

- $\Theta(1)$
- $\Theta(n)$
- $\Theta(n^2)$
- $\Theta(n\log n)$

## Explanation

Within the first $n-1$ entries each insertion stops at its first comparison, contributing $n-2$ comparisons. The final entry crosses the whole prefix and requires $n-1$ comparisons. The total is $2n-3$, which is linear despite the long final insertion.
