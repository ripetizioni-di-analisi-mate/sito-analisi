This scan has one additional writable cell at `a[n]`, separate from its $n$ data entries. If the key is absent from the data, how many comparisons between `a[i]` and `key` are evaluated?

```c
a[n] = key;
size_t i = 0;
while (a[i] != key) ++i;
```

- $n-1$ for every $n>0$
- $2n+2$
- $n+1$
- $n$

## Explanation

The first $n$ comparisons find unequal values. Upon reaching index $n$, the scan also compares the stopping value just written, and the test becomes false. The cost is $n+1$, including $n=0$, when only the extra cell is compared.
