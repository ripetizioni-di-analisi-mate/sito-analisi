A stability test observes records with a key and a unique identifier. The output is already known to be a permutation sorted by key. Which additional condition is necessary and sufficient to tell whether the result is stable, even when keys repeat?

- All output keys are distinct.
- The first key is the minimum.
- The identifiers increase globally.
- Within every equal-key group, the original indices of the output records increase.

## Explanation

Stability concerns record pairs equivalent under the key. Once a group is contiguous, its original indices must appear in the order in which the input was traversed. Checking that indices increase within each group tests precisely that condition.
