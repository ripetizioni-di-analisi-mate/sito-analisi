What zero-based index does `lower_bound` return for key $6$ in the sequence $\langle 1,3,5,8,12,19\rangle$?

- $2$
- $4$
- $6$
- $3$

## Explanation

The first three values are less than $6$, and the next is $8$. The first position with key at least $6$ is therefore $3$. Inserting $6$ at that position places it between $5$ and $8$ and preserves the order.
