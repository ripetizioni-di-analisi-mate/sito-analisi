What is the tight asymptotic order of the cost $3n^2+7n+2$ for $n\geq1$?

- $\Theta(n)$
- $\Theta(n^2)$
- $\Theta(n^3)$
- $\Theta(n\log n)$

## Explanation

For every $n\geq1$, the cost is at least $3n^2$. Since $n\leq n^2$ and $1\leq n^2$, it is also at most $(3+7+2)n^2=12n^2$. The two bounds with positive constants give a tight quadratic order.
