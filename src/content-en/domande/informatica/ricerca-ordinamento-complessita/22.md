How many copies of $4$ lie between the first index with value at least $4$ and the first index with value greater than $4$ in the sorted sequence $\langle 1,4,4,4,7,9\rangle$?

- $2$
- $3$
- $4$
- $5$

## Explanation

The first position with key at least $4$ is $1$. The first with key greater than $4$ is $4$. Equal keys therefore occupy the half-open interval $[1,4)$. Its length is $4-1=3$.
