A deterministic sorter distinguishes five distinct keys using only comparisons with two outcomes. It must distinguish all $5!$ possible input orders. Which integer lower bound on worst-case comparisons follows by counting leaves of its decision tree?

- At least $120$
- At least $6$, with $7$ not implied
- At least $7$
- At least $24$

## Explanation

A binary tree with at most $h$ comparisons on each path has at most $2^h$ leaves. Distinguishing the orders needs at least $120$ leaves. Since $2^6=64<120$ and $2^7=128$, some path must contain at least seven comparisons.
