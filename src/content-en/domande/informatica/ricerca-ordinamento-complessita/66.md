A selection-sort variant must put the suffix’s first minimum at its head while preserving the relative order of the other records. Which operation achieves this even when records between the head and the minimum have equal keys?

- Copy the minimum over the head, leaving its original cell unchanged.
- Reverse the entire segment from the head through the minimum.
- Remove the minimum and shift the preceding suffix records one cell to the right.
- Swap the head and the first minimum.

## Explanation

The records between the head and the minimum all move one place together, preserving their internal order. Because the selected minimum is the first, extracting it does not pass an earlier equal record. A long-distance swap could instead move the former head past an equal key.
