A C17 comparison must return a negative, zero, or positive integer according to the order of two `int` values `x,y`. Which expression meets the specification over the entire range, including `INT_MIN` and `INT_MAX`?

- `x <= y`
- `x - y`
- `(x > y) - (x < y)`
- `(x < y) - (x > y)`

## Explanation

Each relational comparison produces $0$ or $1$. The proposed difference gives $-1$, $0$, or $1$ for $x<y$, $x=y$, or $x>y$, respectively, and is always representable. Subtracting the operands themselves can exceed the limits of `int`.
