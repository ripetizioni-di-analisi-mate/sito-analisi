Merge sort divides a run of length $m$ into runs of lengths $\lfloor \dfrac{m}{2}\rfloor$ and $\lceil \dfrac{m}{2}\rceil$. Each merge copies exactly $m$ entries back. For five entries, how many copy-back writes are made altogether?

- $8$
- $15$
- $10$
- $12$

## Explanation

The top split produces runs of lengths $2$ and $3$. Sorting the first costs two writes. Within the run of three, its two-entry child costs two writes and its merge costs three, for a subtotal of five. The final merge costs another five, giving $2+5+5=12$.
