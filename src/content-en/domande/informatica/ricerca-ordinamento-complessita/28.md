How many predecessor shifts does insertion sort perform on $\langle 4,1,3,2\rangle$? Exclude the final writes of the keys.

- $3$
- $5$
- $6$
- $4$

## Explanation

Each shift moves past a preceding element larger than the key being inserted. In the initial sequence, $4$ precedes three smaller elements, and $3$ precedes $2$. These four inversions each require one shift. Other comparisons do not cause shift writes.
