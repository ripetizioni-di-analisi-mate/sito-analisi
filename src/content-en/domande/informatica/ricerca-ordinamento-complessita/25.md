What is the largest length for which a standard `lower_bound` using the integer midpoint performs at most $3$ key comparisons in the worst case?

- $7$
- $3$
- $8$
- $15$

## Explanation

The longest branch reduces length $m$ to half rounded down. From $7$ it gives $3$, $1$ and $0$, so three comparisons suffice. From $8$ the worst case gives $4$, $2$, $1$ and $0$. Every length at least $8$ requires at least four comparisons along this branch.
