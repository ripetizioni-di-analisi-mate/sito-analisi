Insertion sort orders $(3,A),(5,B),(3,C)$ by key and shifts only predecessors with strictly larger key. What is the final order of the tags?

- `A, C, B`
- `C, A, B`
- `A, B, C`
- `B, A, C`

## Explanation

To insert $(3,C)$, the algorithm shifts $(5,B)$, which has a larger key. At the comparison with $(3,A)$, the strict inequality is false, so the existing record stays before the new one. The final tag order is `A, C, B`.
