A predicate on nonnegative indices is false below a boundary $p$ and true from $p$ onward, with $p>1$. Probe indices $1,2,4,8,\ldots$ until the first true result, then use binary search in the resulting interval. What is the tight worst-case order of the number of probes for boundaries up to $P$, with $P\ge2$?

- $\Theta(\log P)$
- $\Theta(P\log P)$
- $\Theta(P)$
- $\Theta(\log\log P)$

## Explanation

Doubling reaches or passes $p$ in a number of probes proportional to $\log p$. The remaining interval has length at most proportional to $p$, so binary search also takes at most a constant multiple of $\log p$ probes. For boundaries near $P$, the first phase alone supplies the matching lower bound.
