After a phase taking $\Theta(n)$ operations, a procedure performs exactly $n$ independent searches. Each makes exactly $\log_2 n$ comparisons, where $n$ is a power of two and $n\ge2$. What is the tight total cost if operations have unit cost and each search takes constant work per comparison?

- $\Theta(n+\log n)$
- $\Theta(n^2)$
- $\Theta(\log n)$
- $\Theta(n\log n)$

## Explanation

Searches make $n\log_2 n$ comparisons in total because the per-search cost is incurred $n$ times. Preparation adds a linear term. As $n$ grows, the search term dominates the initial phase.
