Selection sort orders $\langle 5,4,3,2,1\rangle$ and swaps only when the minimum is not already in the current position. How many swaps does it perform?

- $0$
- $2$
- $4$
- $10$

## Explanation

The first minimum is $1$, and the first swap gives $\langle 1,4,3,2,5\rangle$. The second minimum, $2$, gives $\langle 1,2,3,4,5\rangle$. On all remaining passes the minimum is already at the start of the suffix. Under the stated condition there are two swaps.
