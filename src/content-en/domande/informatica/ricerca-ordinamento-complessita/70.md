Records must be sorted by department and, within each department, by date, both in ascending order. Only stable single-key sorts are available. In what order should the two passes be performed?

- Department first, then date.
- Two passes on department only.
- Two passes on date only.
- Date first, then department.

## Explanation

The first pass puts records in date order. The second groups them by department and preserves the existing relative order within each department. The final pass therefore establishes the primary key.
