A linear scan searches for a key at one of $6$ positions in an array of distinct keys. The positions are equally likely. How many element-to-key comparisons does it make on average if it stops at the match?

- $3.5$
- $3$
- $6$
- $21$

## Explanation

Success at the first position costs one comparison. At the sixth it costs six. Since all positions have equal probability, the mean is $\dfrac{1+2+3+4+5+6}{6}=\dfrac{21}{6}=3.5$. An unsuccessful search is excluded by the assumptions.
