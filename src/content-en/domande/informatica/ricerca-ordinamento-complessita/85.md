A program costs exactly $n^2$ for all measured sizes from $1$ to $100$, but costs exactly $101n$ for $n>100$. What is the tight asymptotic order of this cost function?

- $\Theta(1)$
- $\Theta(n^2)$
- $\Theta(n)$
- $\Theta(n\log n)$

## Explanation

A tight bound requires inequalities to hold beyond some threshold. Choosing threshold $101$, the ratio of cost to $n$ is always $101$. The first hundred values do not affect those asymptotic bounds.
