A merge sort on $8$ elements always splits into equal halves down to singletons and merges every pair of children. How many merges take place in total?

- $3$
- $4$
- $7$
- $8$

## Explanation

At the base there are eight singleton runs, and no merge takes place inside them. Four pairs of singleton runs merge to form four length-$2$ runs. Two merges then form the two length-$4$ runs, followed by one final merge. The total is $4+2+1=7$.
