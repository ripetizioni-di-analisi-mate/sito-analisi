Compare two algorithms on `{9,8,1,2}`. Insertion sort has just processed position $1$. Selection sort has just put the global minimum at position $0$. Which statement distinguishes the guarantees for the two prefixes at this point?

- The insertion prefix is internally sorted. The first selection key is already in its final position.
- Neither prefix has an order property.
- Both prefixes already contain the smallest values in the entire array.
- The insertion prefix necessarily keeps the old head in first position.

## Explanation

Insertion turns the prefix $9,8$ into $8,9$, while $1$ is still in the suffix. Selection puts $1$ first after examining all candidates. Subsequent selection passes act only on the remaining suffix.
