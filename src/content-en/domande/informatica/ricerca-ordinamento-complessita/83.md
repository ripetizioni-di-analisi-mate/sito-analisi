For $n\ge1$, an operation counter gives $T(n)=n^2$ for even $n$ and $T(n)=n$ for odd $n$. Which statement about its asymptotic bounds is correct?

- $T\in O(n^2)$ and $T\notin\Theta(n^2)$
- $T\in\Theta(n^2)$
- $T\in\Theta(1)$
- $T\in O(n)$

## Explanation

The cost never exceeds $n^2$, so the quadratic upper bound holds. On odd sizes, however, $\dfrac{T(n)}{n^2}=\dfrac{1}{n}$ tends to zero. No positive constant can bound that ratio from below for all sufficiently large sizes.
