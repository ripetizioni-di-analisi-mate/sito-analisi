Merge two nonempty sorted runs of lengths $m$ and $n$. Compare the front keys and, once a run is exhausted, copy the remainder without comparisons. What is the exact maximum number of key comparisons for every $m,n\geq1$?

- $m+n$
- $m+n-1$
- $mn$
- $m+n+1$

## Explanation

Every comparison emits one element, and the merge stops comparing once one run is exhausted. This occurs after at most $m+n-1$ emissions, since only one element remains then. The bound is attained if the two largest values belong to different runs. Both runs are still nonempty before those two values are compared.
