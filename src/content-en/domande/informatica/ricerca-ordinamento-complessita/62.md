Two adjacent out-of-order keys are swapped, $x$ on the left and $y$ on the right with $x>y$. An inversion is a pair of positions with the larger key on the left. By how much does the total inversion count decrease?

- $x-y$ for every pair
- $1$
- The number of entries to the right of $y$
- $2$

## Explanation

The pair $x,y$ ceases to be an inversion. Every other key was already before both or after both and stays on that side. Its combined contributions with $x$ and $y$ are preserved, so the decrease is exactly one.
