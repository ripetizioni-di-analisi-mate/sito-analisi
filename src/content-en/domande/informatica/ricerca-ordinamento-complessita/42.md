Two procedures have costs $n^2$ and $5n+10$ respectively. What is the smallest positive integer $n$ for which the first cost strictly exceeds the second?

- $7$
- $5$
- $6$
- $8$

## Explanation

For $1\leq n\leq6$, $n^2\leq6n\leq5n+10$. So on that range the quadratic cost is at most the linear one. At $n=7$ we have $49>45$, making $7$ the first crossover.
