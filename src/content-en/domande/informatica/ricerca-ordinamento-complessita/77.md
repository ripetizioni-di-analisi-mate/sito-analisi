When merging two sorted runs, the remaining left run is `{4,6,9}` and the remaining right run is `{3,7}`. How many cross-run inversions can the algorithm record immediately when it emits the $3$, assuming the left run preceded the right one in the input?

- $2$
- $3$
- $6$
- $1$

## Explanation

The $3$ is less than the left front value $4$. Since the left run is ordered, it is also less than the other two remaining left entries. All three preceded it in the original sequence, yielding inversions $(4,3),(6,3),(9,3)$.
