A `lower_bound` has interval $[4,12)$ and the comparison `a[mid] < key` is true. How long is the interval after the update?

- $4$
- $3$
- $7$
- $8$

## Explanation

The midpoint is $4+4=8$. Since the value at that position is less than the search key, sorted order excludes all indices up to and including $8$. The update moves `lo` to $9$, leaving $[9,12)$ of length $12-9=3$.
