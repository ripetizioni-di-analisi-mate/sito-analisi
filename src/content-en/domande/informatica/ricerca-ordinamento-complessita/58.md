Four parcels of weights $3,1,4,2$ must be loaded in this order over at most two trips. Each trip holds a contiguous segment. Feasibility is monotone in the integer capacity per trip. What is the first feasible capacity?

- $10$
- $4$
- $6$
- $5$

## Explanation

With capacity $5$, the first trip can hold $3+1$ but cannot add the next $4$. The remaining parcels weigh $4+2=6$, too much for the second trip even if the split is moved earlier. At capacity $6$, both segments $3,1$ and $4,2$ fit. By monotonicity, capacities below $5$ were insufficient as well.
