Three sorted runs of lengths $2$, $3$, and $9$ must be merged two at a time. Each merge writes all its participating entries once. What is the smallest total number of writes needed to obtain a single run?

- $26$
- $19$
- $14$
- $23$

## Explanation

The second merge necessarily includes all $14$ entries. Minimize the first merge by combining $2+3=5$ entries. The total is $5+14=19$. Starting with the run of length $9$ would write more entries before the final merge.
