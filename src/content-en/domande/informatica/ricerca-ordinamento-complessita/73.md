A custom rule says A precedes B, B precedes C, and C precedes A. Every pair can be compared. Which property prevents these preferences from defining a consistent strict order?

- The number of records must be even.
- Duplicate keys are required.
- Transitivity fails.
- The entries have no labels.

## Explanation

From the first two relations, transitivity would require A to precede C. The last preference requires the opposite direction. Every linear arrangement of the three records violates at least one of the relations required by the comparator.
