An insertion sort skips the final write of the saved key if its index has not changed. On `{1,4,3,5}`, count only writes to array cells, including shifts of predecessors. How many writes are performed?

- $2$
- $3$
- $1$
- $4$

## Explanation

Keys $4$ and $5$ are encountered at their correct positions, so their passes make no array writes. The pass for $3$ moves the $4$ one cell to the right and writes the $3$ into the vacated cell. These are two distinct writes.
