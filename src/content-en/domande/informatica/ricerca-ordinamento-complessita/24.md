In a `lower_bound` the interval is $[4,5)$ and the value at position $4$ exceeds `key`. What position is returned at the end?

- $3$
- $5$
- $4$
- $6$

## Explanation

The integer half of $5-4$ is $0$, so the midpoint is $4$. The false comparison moves `hi` to $4$ and leaves `lo` unchanged. The endpoints coincide and the resulting position is $4$. Index $5$ was the excluded endpoint of the segment to visit.
