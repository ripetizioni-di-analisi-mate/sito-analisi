Only the three smallest of ten distinct keys are needed in order. Selection sort is stopped after placing the first three, scanning the full candidate suffix on each pass. How many key comparisons are saved relative to a complete sort?

- $24$
- $45$
- $7$
- $21$

## Explanation

The first three passes use $9+8+7=24$ comparisons. Seven entries remain, so finishing selection would take $6+5+4+3+2+1=21$ comparisons. Those are the comparisons avoided by stopping early.
