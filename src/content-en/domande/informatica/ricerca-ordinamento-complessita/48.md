A linear search stops on success. Requests are for A with probability $0.5$, B with $0.2$, C with $0.1$, and an absent key with $0.2$. Each record occurs once. What is the minimum mean number of comparisons achievable by rearranging the three records?

- $1.8$
- $1.2$
- $2.2$
- $2$

## Explanation

Unsuccessful requests always cost three comparisons. Placing the most frequent successful requests first minimizes the mean, giving A, B, C. The mean is $0.5+2\cdot0.2+3\cdot0.1+3\cdot0.2=1.8$.
