For each $n\geq2$, the array holds $n$ distinct keys in decreasing order. Insertion sort uses `j > 0 && a[j - 1] > key`. What is the exact number of key comparisons?

- $\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}$
- $n-1$
- $n^2$
- $\dfrac{n(n-1)}{2}$

## Explanation

At pass $i$, with $1\leq i<n$, the key being inserted is smaller than all $i$ members of the prefix. There are $i$ successful key comparisons. When `j` reaches zero, the first part of the guard is false and no further keys are compared. Adding the passes gives $1+\cdots+(n-1)=\dfrac{n(n-1)}2$.
