On the same records, X makes 20 comparisons and four writes, while Y makes 12 comparisons and eight writes. A comparison costs one unit and a write costs $w$ units, where $w$ is a positive integer. What is the smallest $w$ for which X costs strictly less?

- $4$
- $2$
- $1$
- $3$

## Explanation

The total costs are $20+4w$ and $12+8w$. The first is smaller when $8<4w$, equivalently $w>2$. At $w=2$ they tie, so the first integer weight favouring X is $3$.
