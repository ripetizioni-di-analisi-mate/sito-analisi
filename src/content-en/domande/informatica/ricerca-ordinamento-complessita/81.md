A level-by-level scan performs $1,2,4,\ldots,n$ visits on successive levels, where $n=2^k$. Count all visits and exclude control tests. What is the exact total?

- $n\log_2 n$
- $n+1$ for every $n$
- $2n-1$
- $n^2$

## Explanation

Let $S$ denote the total. Doubling it gives the sum $2+4+\cdots+2n$. Subtracting $S$ cancels all intermediate terms and leaves $2n-1$. The number of levels is logarithmic, but the work per level varies.
