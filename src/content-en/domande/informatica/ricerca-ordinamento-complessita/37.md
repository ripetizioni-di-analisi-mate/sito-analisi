Merge $\langle 2,4,12\rangle$ and $\langle 3,5\rangle$. Once one run is exhausted, how many elements are copied without further key comparisons?

- $1$
- $0$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

The comparisons emit $2$, $3$, $4$ and $5$ in order. The right run is then exhausted. Only $12$ remains in the left run and is copied without comparison to other keys. The copied remainder has length $1$.
