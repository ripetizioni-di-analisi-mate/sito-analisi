We want the multiset intersection of two sorted sequences. Equal front entries produce one copy and advance both cursors, while unequal fronts advance only the smaller one. What is the output for `{1,2,2,2,5}` and `{2,2,3,5,5}`?

- `{2,2,5}`
- `{2,5}`
- `{2,2,2,5,5}`
- `{1,2,3,5}`

## Explanation

One copy is emitted only while both runs still have a matching copy. The three occurrences of $2$ on the left can match only two on the right. For $5$ the left run limits the number of pairs to one, giving output $2,2,5$.
