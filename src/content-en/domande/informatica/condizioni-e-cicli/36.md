What is the final value of `digit_sum`?

```c
int number = 309, digit_sum = 0;
while (number > 0) {
    digit_sum += number % 10;
    number /= 10;
}
```

- $9$
- $30$
- $12$
- $3$

## Explanation

The remainder modulo $10$ obtains the least significant digit, and integer division removes that digit. The values of `number` at the loop head are $309$, $30$, $3$ and $0$. The first three body executions add $9$, $0$ and $3$ in order. The sum is $9+0+3=12$.
