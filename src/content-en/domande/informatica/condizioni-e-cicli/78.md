The desired postcondition of a loop with guard `i < 4` is `s == 10`. The proposed invariant states only `0 <= i && i <= 4 && s >= 0`. Which state `(i, s)` shows that the invariant and the false guard alone are insufficient?

- `(3, 0)`
- `(5, 0)`
- `(4, 0)`
- `(4, 10)`

## Explanation

A counterexample to the implication must satisfy the invariant, make the guard false and violate `s == 10`. The pair $(4,0)$ meets all three requirements. It need not be reachable in any particular program. Its existence shows that the stated conditions alone allow that value of `s`.
