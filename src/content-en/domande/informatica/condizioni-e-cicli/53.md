A `switch` on an `int` already contains `case 3 + 3:`. Which additional label in the same `switch` violates the requirement for distinct values?

- `case 5 / 2:`
- `case 7U:`
- `case 2 * 3:`
- `case 2 << 1:`

## Explanation

Case constant expressions are evaluated before execution. Both `3 + 3` and `2 * 3` have value 6, so they would designate the same dispatch value. The expression `2 << 1` gives 4, `5 / 2` gives 2 and `7U` gives 7.
