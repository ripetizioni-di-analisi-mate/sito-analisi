Let `n` be a nonnegative `int`. Which quantity is a nonnegative integer variant for every permitted initial value?

```c
while (n > 0) {
    n /= 2;
}
```

- $-n$
- $1$
- $n$
- $n\bmod 2$

## Explanation

When the guard is true, `n` is a positive integer. Integer division by $2$ produces a nonnegative value strictly below the starting value. This satisfies both requirements for an integer variant. Parity can remain unchanged, for example in the transition from $4$ to $2$.
