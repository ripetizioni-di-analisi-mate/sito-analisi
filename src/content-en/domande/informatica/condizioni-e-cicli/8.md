## Explanation

Post-increment gives the comparison the previous value of `i` and updates `i` during evaluation of the guard. The comparisons use $0$, $1$, $2$, $3$ and $4$. The last comparison is false, but its post-increment has already occurred. The body executes four times and `i` ends at $5$.
