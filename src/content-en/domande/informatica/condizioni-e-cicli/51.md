A two-desk queue directs the customer to the less busy desk. What is the tuple `(left, right, ticket)` after the assignment?

```c
int left = 3, right = 2;
int ticket = (left <= right) ? left++ : right++;
```

- `(3, 3, 3)`
- `(3, 2, 2)`
- `(4, 3, 2)`
- `(3, 3, 2)`

## Explanation

The test `3 <= 2` is false, so only `right++` is evaluated. Post-increment supplies the previous value 2 to `ticket` and raises `right` to 3. The `left` counter stays at 3 because its increment belongs to the other alternative.
