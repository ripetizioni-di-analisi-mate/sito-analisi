What is `s` on exit?

```c
int s = 0;
for (int i = 1, j = 8; i < j; ++i, j -= 2) {
    s += j - i;
}
```

- $7$
- $11$
- $14$
- $12$

## Explanation

The first body execution uses the pair $(1,8)$ and adds $7$. The update increments `i` and subtracts $2$ from `j`. The next pairs are therefore $(2,6)$ and $(3,4)$, adding $4$ and $1$ respectively. The pair $(4,2)$ fails the guard. The total is $7+4+1=12$.
