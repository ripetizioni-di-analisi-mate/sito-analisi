## Explanation

For each `i`, the addition takes place only for `j` from zero to `i - 1`. The `break` exits the inner loop before the contribution with `j == i`. The three rows give $10$, $20+21=41$ and $30+31+32=93$. The outer loop continues between rows, so the total is $10+41+93=144$.
