A power algorithm keeps $p\,b^e$ equal to the required result. If the current exponent is `e = 2*t + 1`, the new `b` will be `b*b` and the new `e` will be `t`. How should `p` change using the old values? All products are representable.

- `p = p;`
- `p = p * b * b;`
- `p = p + b;`
- `p = p * b;`

## Explanation

For an odd exponent the unpaired factor can be separated by writing $p\,b^{2t+1}=(p\,b)(b^2)^t$. After halving the exponent and squaring the base, $(b^2)^t$ represents every paired factor. Multiplying the accumulator by the old base preserves the remaining factor.
