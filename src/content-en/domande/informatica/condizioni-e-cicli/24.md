Let the two `int` objects `x` and `count` be initialized to $0$. Use body `{ ++count; }` with guard `x > 0`. What are the final `count` values for the two loops?

- $1$ with `while` and $0$ with `do while`.
- $0$ with both.
- $0$ with `while` and $1$ with `do while`.
- $1$ with both.

## Explanation

The guard `x > 0` is false whenever it is evaluated because `x` is $0$ and the body modifies only `count`. In `while` the test precedes the first body execution and the count remains $0$. In `do while` the body precedes the first test and adds one, so the count is $1$.
