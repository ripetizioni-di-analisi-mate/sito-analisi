## Explanation

The `continue` statement skips the addition when the remainder on division by $3$ is zero. In the interval from $1$ to $8$, the excluded values are therefore $3$ and $6$. The loop still increments `i` after each skip. The sum of all values is $36$, so the required sum is $36-3-6=27$.
