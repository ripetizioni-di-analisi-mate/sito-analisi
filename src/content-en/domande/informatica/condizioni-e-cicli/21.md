Which type is permitted for the expression `mode` in `switch (mode)`?

- `float`
- `double`
- `long double`
- `int`

## Explanation

C17 requires an integer controlling expression for `switch` and applies integer promotions before comparing its value with the case labels. `int` is the integer type among those listed. The other three are real floating-point types.
