A controller receives an integer hour `h` from `0` through `23`. The night period starts at 22 inclusive and ends at 6 exclusive. Which condition classifies every hour correctly?

- `h > 22 || h <= 6`
- `h >= 22 || h < 6`
- `h >= 22 && h < 6`
- `h >= 6 && h < 22`

## Explanation

Midnight splits the period into two intervals. The first contains 22 and 23, and the second contains integers from 0 to 5. Membership in either interval is sufficient, so the guards are joined by logical OR. Checking the endpoints confirms that 22 is included and 6 is excluded.
