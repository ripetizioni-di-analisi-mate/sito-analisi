What is the final pair `(a, b)`?

```c
int a = 15, b = 2;
while (a > b) {
    a -= 3;
    ++b;
}
```

- $(3,6)$
- $(6,5)$
- $(4,6)$
- $(0,7)$

## Explanation

The initial difference between `a` and `b` is $13$. Each body execution subtracts $3$ from `a` and adds $1$ to `b`, so the difference falls by $4$. After three steps it is still $1$, and the fourth step takes it to $-3$. The final values are $15-4\cdot3=3$ and $2+4=6$.
