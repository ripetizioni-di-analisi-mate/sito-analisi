## Explanation

The first operand of `&&` tests whether `d` differs from zero. Since `d` equals $0$, the comparison is false. Under short-circuit evaluation, that false result determines the conjunction. The expression `18 / d > 2` is not evaluated, so the fragment assigns $0$ to `ok` without performing a division.
