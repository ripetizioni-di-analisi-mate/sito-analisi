What does the omitted condition represent in `for (int i = 0; ; ++i)`?

- An always-false guard.
- A guard true only on the first iteration.
- A guard that tests whether the counter is zero.
- An always-true guard.

## Explanation

In C17 an omitted controlling expression in a `for` is replaced by a nonzero integer constant. Thus the header test alone permits every iteration. A `break` in the body can still terminate the loop when its condition holds.
