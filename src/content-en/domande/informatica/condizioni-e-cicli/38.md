Let `N` be a nonnegative `int`. Which additional condition makes `remaining >= 0` an invariant at every guard test?

```c
int remaining = N;
while (remaining > 0) {
    remaining -= 2;
}
```

- `N` is greater than zero.
- `N` is a multiple of $3$.
- `N` is at most $1$.
- `N` is even.

## Explanation

The update subtracts $2$ and preserves parity. For a nonnegative even starting value, every positive value in the sequence is at least $2$, so the next value remains nonnegative. The final test occurs with `remaining` equal to $0$. For an odd starting value the transition from $1$ to $-1$ makes the required relation false at the final test.
