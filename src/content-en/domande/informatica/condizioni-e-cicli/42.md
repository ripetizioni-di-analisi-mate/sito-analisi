A loop terminates because guard $G$ is false. Invariant $I$ holds at every test and evaluating $G$ leaves the state unchanged. Which implication, once established, yields postcondition $Q$?

- $I\land G\implies Q$
- $I\land\lnot G\implies Q$
- $Q\implies I$
- $G\implies Q$

## Explanation

The loop stops because the guard is false, and the invariant also holds at the final test. Because testing the guard leaves the state unchanged, both $I$ and $\lnot G$ hold on exit. To conclude the postcondition, $Q$ must follow from those two properties. An implication with a true guard instead concerns states in which the body is entered.
