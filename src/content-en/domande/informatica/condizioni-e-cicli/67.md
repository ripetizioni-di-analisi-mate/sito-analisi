A controller toggles its phase at every test. The guard uses the comma operator. What is the final pair `(i, phase)`?

```c
int i = 0, phase = 0;
while ((phase = !phase, i < 3)) ++i;
```

- `(1, 0)`
- `(3, 0)`
- `(0, 1)`
- `(3, 1)`

## Explanation

The comma operator first evaluates the assignment to `phase` and then the comparison `i < 3`. Only the second operand supplies the guard value. The body advances for indices 0, 1 and 2. The phase also toggles on the final test at index 3, so four toggles return it to zero.
