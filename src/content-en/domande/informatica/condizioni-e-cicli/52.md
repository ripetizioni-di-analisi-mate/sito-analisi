The programmer expected `total` to accumulate all the indices. With the punctuation shown, what value does it get?

```c
int i, total = 0;
for (i = 0; i < 4; ++i);
    total += i;
```

- `0`
- `6`
- `4`
- `10`

## Explanation

The semicolon directly after the `for` header is a null statement and forms the entire loop body. The counter reaches 4, making the guard false. Only then does the next statement add 4 to `total` once. Indentation does not affect this syntax.
