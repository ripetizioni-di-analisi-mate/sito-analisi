A dispenser accepts an item while total spending remains within the budget, including the limit itself. Which single test distinguishes the correct guard `spent + cost <= budget` from the variant `spent + cost < budget`? Each option gives `(spent, cost, budget)`.

- `(6, 3, 10)`
- `(6, 5, 10)`
- `(0, 4, 10)`
- `(6, 4, 10)`

## Explanation

The two guards differ only at the equality boundary. With spending 6 and cost 4, the total is exactly 10. The inclusive guard accepts the purchase and the strict guard rejects it. In the other tests the total is either below or above the budget, so the two versions make the same decision.
