Variables `a` and `b` hold consecutive Fibonacci terms. The pair `(a, b)` should become `(b, a+b)`. Which statement completes the body after the first assignment, assuming every result is representable?

```c
b = a + b;
/* next statement */
```

- `a = b - a;`
- `a = a + b;`
- `a = b;`
- `b = b - a;`

## Explanation

Call the old values $A,B$. After the first assignment `b` equals $A+B$, while `a` still equals $A$. The difference `b - a` therefore recovers $B$ without changing the new sum. For example, $(2,3)$ becomes $(2,5)$ and then $(3,5)$.
