What is the final value of `n`?

```c
int n = 1;
int result = 1 || ++n == 2;
```

- $2$
- $1$
- $0$
- $3$

## Explanation

The left operand of `||` is $1$, so it is already true. Short-circuit evaluation skips the entire right operand, including `++n`. The disjunction returns $1$ and `n` retains its initial value $1$. The comparison with $2$ belongs to the skipped operand.
