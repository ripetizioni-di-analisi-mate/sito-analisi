A small ball is pushed up an inclined plane, and its position along the plane is $x=3+4t-t^2$, with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. What is its acceleration?

- $-2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $+2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

In the position equation, the coefficient of $t^2$ is $\dfrac{1}{2}a$. Here that coefficient is $-1$, so $\dfrac{1}{2}a=-1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, and therefore $a=-2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The value $-1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ reads the coefficient of $t^2$ as the acceleration. The value $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ is actually the initial velocity, $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and $+2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ drops the sign.
