Velocity decreases linearly from $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in $4\,\mathrm{s}$. What total distance is travelled?

- $8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Velocity reaches zero after $3\,\mathrm{s}$. The triangles have area magnitudes of $9\,\mathrm{m}$ and $1\,\mathrm{m}$, which are added for distance.
