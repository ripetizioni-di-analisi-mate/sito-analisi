An object's velocity changes from $6$ m/s to $18$ m/s in $2$ s. What is its average acceleration?

- $12\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $24\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Average acceleration is the change in velocity divided by the elapsed time. The change is $12$ meters per second. Dividing by $2$ seconds gives $6$ meters per second squared.
