Two robots start from rest at the same point. A accelerates at $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ for $2\,\mathrm{s}$ and B at $1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ for $4\,\mathrm{s}$. What is true at the end of their respective intervals?

- B finishes faster than A
- They have equal final velocities but different displacements
- A covers four times B’s distance
- They have equal displacements but different final velocities

## Explanation

In both cases $\dfrac{at^2}{2}=8$ metres. The products $at$ are $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ respectively.
