A cart is launched along an inclined laboratory track; the graph shows its velocity along the track between $0$ and $5\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the displacement during this interval?

::grafico{f="6-2*x+0*sqrt(x*(5-x))" area="6-2*x;0;0;3|0;6-2*x;3;5" punti="0,6,pieno;3,0,pieno;5,-4,pieno" segmenti="5,0,5,-4" xmin=-1 xmax=6 ymin=-6 ymax=8 nomi="t,v" titolo="Velocity of the cart in $\mathrm{m/s}$ and time in seconds"}

- $13\,\mathrm{m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The velocity is zero at $t=3\,\mathrm{s}$, so the area splits into two triangles. The triangle above the axis has base $3\,\mathrm{s}$ and height $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$, so it contributes $+9\,\mathrm{m}$. The triangle below the axis has base $2\,\mathrm{s}$ and height $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$, so it contributes $-4\,\mathrm{m}$. The displacement is the signed sum, $9\,\mathrm{m}-4\,\mathrm{m}=5\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $13\,\mathrm{m}$ is the distance traveled, and $9\,\mathrm{m}$ counts only the motion up the track.
