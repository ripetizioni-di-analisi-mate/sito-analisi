A vehicle follows $v=12-3t$ in SI units, including after any reversal. Taking right as positive, when is it moving left and increasing its speed?

- Never
- For $t>4\,\mathrm{s}$
- Only at $t=4\,\mathrm{s}$
- For $0<t<4\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Acceleration is always negative. After $4\,\mathrm{s}$ velocity is negative too, so the two point in the same direction and speed increases.
