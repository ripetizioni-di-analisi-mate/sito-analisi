A trolley has position $x=5t-t^2$ in SI units. What is its average velocity from $t=1$ s to $t=4$ s?

- $0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Both endpoint positions are $4\,\mathrm{m}$. Their difference is zero, so the average velocity is zero even though the trolley reverses within the interval.
