A bowling ball and a small glass marble are dropped from rest at the same instant from the same height of $2\,\mathrm{m}$. Air resistance is negligible for both; use $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Which statement is true?

- The bowling ball arrives first, because it has more mass
- They reach the ground at the same instant
- The marble arrives first, because it is smaller
- The bowling ball arrives first, because it is larger

## Explanation

With negligible air resistance, both objects are in free fall and have the same acceleration $g$. The equations $v=gt$ and $h=\dfrac{1}{2}gt^2$ contain neither the mass nor the size of the object. The two falls therefore have the same motion, and the objects reach the ground **at the same instant**, after $t=\sqrt{\dfrac{2h}{g}}\approx0.64\,\mathrm{s}$.
