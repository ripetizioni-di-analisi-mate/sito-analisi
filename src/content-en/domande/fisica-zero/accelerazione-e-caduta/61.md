A high-speed train arriving at a station is moving at $72\,\mathrm{km/h}$ and stops in $40\,\mathrm{s}$ with constant acceleration. The axis points in the direction of motion. What is the acceleration?

- $-1.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $+0.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-0.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The initial velocity in meters per second is $\dfrac{72}{3.6}\,\mathrm{m/s}=20\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The final velocity is zero, so
$$
a=\frac{0-20\,\mathrm{m/s}}{40\,\mathrm{s}}=-0.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.
$$
The negative sign is consistent with braking in the positive direction. The value $-1.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ uses kilometers per hour without converting, and $+0.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ drops the sign.
