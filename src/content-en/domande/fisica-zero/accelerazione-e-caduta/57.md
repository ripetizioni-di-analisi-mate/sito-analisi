A trolley accelerates from rest at $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ for $2\,\mathrm{s}$, then maintains the resulting velocity for another $2\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the total displacement?

- $12\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $24\,\mathrm{m}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

In the first segment it reaches $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and covers $6\,\mathrm{m}$. In the next it covers $6\cdot2=12$ metres, giving $18\,\mathrm{m}$ in all.
