The graph shows the velocity of a moped accelerating uniformly for $4\,\mathrm{s}$. What is its displacement during this interval?

::grafico{f="2+x+0*sqrt(x*(4-x))" area="2+x;0;0;4" punti="0,2,pieno;4,6,pieno" segmenti="4,0,4,6" xmin=-1 xmax=5 ymin=-1 ymax=8 nomi="t,v" titolo="Velocity of the moped in $\mathrm{m/s}$ and time in seconds"}

- $24\,\mathrm{m}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The displacement is the signed area between the graph and the time axis. Here the region is a trapezoid with heights $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and base $4\,\mathrm{s}$, entirely above the axis, so
$$
\Delta x=\frac{2\,\mathrm{m/s}+6\,\mathrm{m/s}}{2}\cdot4\,\mathrm{s}=16\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
The value $24\,\mathrm{m}$ uses only the final velocity, as if the graph were horizontal. The value $8\,\mathrm{m}$ is the area of just the triangle above the rectangle of height $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
