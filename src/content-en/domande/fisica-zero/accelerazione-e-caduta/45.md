A freely falling coin passes a $2\,\mathrm{m}$ tall window from top to bottom in $0.2\,\mathrm{s}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what was its speed at the top edge?

- $11\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Average speed is $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the speed change is $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. With constant acceleration the average lies halfway between the endpoints, so the first speed is $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
