On a velocity–time graph, the region under the graph is a triangle with base $7$ s and height $4$ m/s, entirely above the time axis. What is the displacement?

- $14\,\mathrm{m}$
- $28\,\mathrm{m}$
- $11\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3.5\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The displacement is the area under the velocity–time graph. The area of a triangle is half the product of its base and height. Therefore $\Delta s=\dfrac{7\cdot4}{2}=14$ meters.
