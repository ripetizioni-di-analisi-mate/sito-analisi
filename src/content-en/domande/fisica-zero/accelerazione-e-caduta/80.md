A spreadsheet estimates distance each second using the speed at the start of the second multiplied by $1\,\mathrm{s}$. If the positive velocity rises continuously, how does this affect the total?

- The total is too small
- It is exact if the motion starts at the origin
- The total is too large
- The result is negative

## Explanation

Each rectangle uses the smallest velocity in its interval. Its area lies below the velocity–time graph, so their sum underestimates the displacement.
