A tennis player tosses the ball straight up for a serve with an initial velocity of $9.81\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Neglecting air resistance, with $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, how long does it take for the ball to reach its highest point?

- $2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $96.2\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

With the axis pointing upward, the velocity is $v=v_0-gt$. At the highest point the velocity is zero, so the time to reach it is
$$
t_s=\frac{v_0}{g}=\frac{9.81\,\mathrm{m/s}}{9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}}=1\,\mathrm{s}.
$$
The value $2\,\mathrm{s}$ is the time to return to the launch height, $\dfrac{2v_0}{g}$. The value $96.2\,\mathrm{s}$ multiplies the velocity by $g$ instead of dividing.
