Positions of $0\,\mathrm{m}$, $1\,\mathrm{m}$, $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and $9\,\mathrm{m}$ are logged at $1\,\mathrm{s}$ intervals. What constant acceleration fits all four readings if the object starts from rest?

- $1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The data fit $x=t^2$ in SI units. Starting from rest, the coefficient of $t^2$ is half the acceleration, so $a=2$ metres per second squared.
