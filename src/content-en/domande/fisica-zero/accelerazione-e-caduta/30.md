Two runners start side by side at the same instant. Anna runs at a constant velocity of $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$; Bruno starts from rest with a constant acceleration of $1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Which statement is true at time $t=10\,\mathrm{s}$?

- Anna is $25\,\mathrm{m}$ ahead
- Bruno is ahead and has the same velocity as Anna
- They are side by side and have the same velocity
- They are side by side again, and Bruno is faster than Anna

## Explanation

Anna covers $5\,\mathrm{m/s}\cdot10\,\mathrm{s}=50\,\mathrm{m}$. Bruno, starting from rest, covers $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}\cdot(10\,\mathrm{s})^2=50\,\mathrm{m}$, so the two are **side by side** again. Bruno's velocity is $v=at=10\,\mathrm{m/s}$, so at that instant it is twice Anna's. The two velocities are equal at $t=5\,\mathrm{s}$, when Anna is $12.5\,\mathrm{m}$ ahead.
