A motorcycle is moving with velocity $v_0$ and begins to accelerate with constant acceleration $a$ when the stopwatch reads $2\,\mathrm{s}$. Which expression gives its velocity for $t\geq 2\,\mathrm{s}$, with $t$ read on the stopwatch?

- $v=v_0+at$
- $v=v_0+a\,(t+2\,\mathrm{s})$
- $v=v_0+a\,(t-2\,\mathrm{s})$
- $v=v_0+at-2\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

When the accelerated motion begins at a time $t_0$ other than zero, the velocity equation contains the elapsed time $t-t_0$. Here $t_0=2\,\mathrm{s}$, so $v=v_0+a\,(t-2\,\mathrm{s})$, which correctly gives $v_0$ at $t=2\,\mathrm{s}$. The expression $v=v_0+at$ would also count the two seconds during which the motorcycle was not accelerating. The expression $v_0+at-2\,\mathrm{s}$ subtracts a time from a velocity, so its units are inconsistent.
