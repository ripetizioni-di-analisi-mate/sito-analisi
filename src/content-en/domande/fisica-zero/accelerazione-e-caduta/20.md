Two balls are released simultaneously from rest, one $3\,\mathrm{m}$ above the other. How does their separation change before either hits the ground, with no air resistance?

- It vanishes after one second
- It remains $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- It doubles in the first second
- It grows as time squared

## Explanation

The balls start with the same velocity and undergo the same acceleration. Their displacements agree at every instant, preserving the initial difference in height.
