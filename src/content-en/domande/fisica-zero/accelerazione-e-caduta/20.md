An object starts from rest with a constant acceleration of $2$ meters per second squared. What is its velocity after $5$ s?

- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $25\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The velocity equation is $v=v_0+at$. The object starts from rest, so $v_0=0$. After $5$ seconds, its velocity is $v=2\cdot5=10$ meters per second.
