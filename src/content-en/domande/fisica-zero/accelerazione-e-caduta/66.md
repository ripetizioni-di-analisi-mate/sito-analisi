A van brakes uniformly to a stop at a red light and then stays at rest for a few seconds. What shape does its velocity–time graph have, with the axis pointing in the direction of motion?

- A segment that descends and continues below the time axis
- A horizontal stretch above the time axis
- A segment that descends to the time axis, then a stretch along the axis
- A segment that rises to a maximum value, then a horizontal stretch

## Explanation

During uniform braking the velocity is positive and decreases linearly, so the graph is a descending segment. When the van stops, the velocity is zero and the segment reaches the time axis. The van then stays at rest, so at later times the velocity is zero and the graph lies along the axis. A segment continuing below the axis would describe a van that moves backward after stopping.
