The proposed height law for a falling object is $y=30-5t^2$ metres. Which measurement would indicate that the clock started after release and this law needs changing for that clock?

- A decrease in height with time
- Increasing fall distance after the first second
- Initial height $30\,\mathrm{m}$ and zero velocity
- A downward velocity at clock time zero

## Explanation

The proposed law lacks a term proportional to $t$, so it gives zero initial velocity. A downward starting velocity requires a $v_i t$ term.
