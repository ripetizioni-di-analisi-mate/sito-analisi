The graph shows the position of a cyclist starting from rest at a traffic light, during the first $3\,\mathrm{s}$. Which statement about the motion is true?

::grafico{f="x^2+0*sqrt(x*(3-x))" punti="0,0,pieno;1,1,pieno;2,4,pieno;3,9,pieno" segmenti="1,0,1,1;2,0,2,4;3,0,3,9" xmin=-1 xmax=4 ymin=-0.5 ymax=9.5 nomi="t,x" titolo="Position of the cyclist in meters and time in seconds"}

- The cyclist's velocity is constant
- The cyclist's velocity decreases with time
- The cyclist's velocity increases with time
- The cyclist stays at rest until $t=1\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

From the graph we read positions $0$, $1$, $4$, and $9\,\mathrm{m}$ at times $0$, $1$, $2$, and $3\,\mathrm{s}$. The displacements in the three successive seconds are therefore $1$, $3$, and $5\,\mathrm{m}$. In equal time intervals the cyclist covers longer and longer distances, so the velocity **increases**. With constant velocity, the position–time graph would be a straight line segment. Between $0$ and $1\,\mathrm{s}$ the position changes, so the cyclist is not at rest.
