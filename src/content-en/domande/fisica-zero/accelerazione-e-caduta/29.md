A car’s velocities measured every $2\,\mathrm{s}$ are $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$, $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$, starting at $0\,\mathrm{s}$. Which prediction at $6\,\mathrm{s}$ fits constant acceleration?

- $18\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $13\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $11\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $17\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Velocity rises by $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in each interval. Continuing that pattern for another $2\,\mathrm{s}$ takes it from $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $13\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
