An object starts from rest with a constant acceleration of $2$ meters per second squared. What is its velocity after $6$ s?

- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $36\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $24\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The velocity equation is $v=v_0+at$. The object starts from rest, so $v_0=0$. After $6$ seconds, its velocity is $v=2\cdot6=12$ meters per second.
