During braking, readings of $(x,v^2)$ in SI units form a straight line of slope $-8$. What is the acceleration along the road?

- $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-16\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The relation $v^2=v_i^2+2a(x-x_i)$ shows that these axes give a slope of $2a$. Halving $-8$ yields $-4$ metres per second squared.
