A trolley initially at rest is recorded at $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ after $1\,\mathrm{s}$ and $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ after $2\,\mathrm{s}$. Which conclusion follows?

- The trolley stayed at rest in the second interval
- Average acceleration over the two seconds is $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- Velocity decreased in the second interval
- Acceleration was not constant over the two seconds

## Explanation

Velocity increased by $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the first second and $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the second. Different changes in equal intervals rule out constant acceleration.
