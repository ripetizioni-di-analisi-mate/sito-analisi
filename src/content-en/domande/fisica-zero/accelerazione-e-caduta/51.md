Two objects have the same position and velocity at one instant. One continues at constant velocity while the other accelerates forwards at $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is their separation $3\,\mathrm{s}$ later?

- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The common initial velocity contributes equally to both displacements. Their difference is $\dfrac{2\cdot3^2}{2}=9$ metres.
