A baseball is thrown straight up at $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and caught at the same height. With negligible air resistance and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what are the displacement $\Delta y$ and the distance traveled during the flight?

- $\Delta y=0$ and distance $90\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\Delta y=90\,\mathrm{m}$ and distance $90\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\Delta y=0$ and distance $45\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\Delta y=45\,\mathrm{m}$ and distance $90\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The ball returns to its starting height, so the displacement is $\Delta y=0$. The maximum height above the launch point is $h_{\max}=\dfrac{v_0^2}{2g}=\dfrac{900}{20}\,\mathrm{m}=45\,\mathrm{m}$. The ball rises $45\,\mathrm{m}$ and falls $45\,\mathrm{m}$, so the distance traveled is $90\,\mathrm{m}$. The pair with distance $45\,\mathrm{m}$ counts only the ascent.
