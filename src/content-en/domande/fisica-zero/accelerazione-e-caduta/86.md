Two candidate motion laws are $x_1=2+t+t^2$ and $x_2=2+3t+t^2$ in SI units. Which measurement immediately distinguishes them at the initial instant?

- Initial acceleration
- Initial acceleration direction
- Initial velocity
- Initial position

## Explanation

Both models have the same constant and quadratic terms. The linear coefficient is the initial velocity, respectively $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
