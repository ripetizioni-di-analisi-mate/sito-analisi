A trolley moves at $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and brakes at a constant $-2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ until it stops. How much braking distance does it need?

- $36\,\mathrm{m}$
- $72\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $24\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Losing $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ takes $6\,\mathrm{s}$. Average speed during braking is $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$, giving a distance of $36\,\mathrm{m}$.
