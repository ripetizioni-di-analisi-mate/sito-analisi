A diver drops from rest from a platform $3.2\,\mathrm{m}$ above the water. Neglecting air resistance and using $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, with what speed does the diver enter the water?

- $64\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $32\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5.7\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The time is not given, so we use $v^2-v_0^2=2a\Delta x$ with the axis pointing downward, $v_0=0$, $a=g$, and $\Delta x=3.2\,\mathrm{m}$:
$$
v^2=2\cdot10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}\cdot3.2\,\mathrm{m}=64\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}.
$$
The speed is therefore $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The value $64\,\mathrm{m/s}$ omits the square root, and $5.7\,\mathrm{m/s}$ omits the factor of $2$.
