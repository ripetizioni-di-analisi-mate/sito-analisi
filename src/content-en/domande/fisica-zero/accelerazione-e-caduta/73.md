A sensor reads $a=0$ for a full second. Which position–time graph must describe that second for an object initially moving?

- A horizontal line
- A straight segment with nonzero slope
- A parabola with its vertex at the origin
- A curve of increasing slope

## Explanation

Zero acceleration preserves the initial velocity. A nonzero constant velocity produces a sloping straight position–time line.
