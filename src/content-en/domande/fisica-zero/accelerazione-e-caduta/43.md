Two balls leave the same height with equal speeds, one upwards and the other downwards. How do their impact speeds compare in the absence of air resistance?

- They are equal
- The downward-launched ball is faster
- The upward-launched ball is faster
- They depend on the masses

## Explanation

Both satisfy $v_f^2=v_0^2+2gh$ with the same $v_0^2$ and $h$. Their flight times differ, while the final speeds agree.
