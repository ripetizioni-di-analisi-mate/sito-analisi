An object falls with acceleration $g$ and a device measures depth $h$ below the release point. Which axes give a straight line suitable for estimating $g$ for a drop from rest?

- $h^2$ vertically and $t$ horizontally
- $h$ vertically and $\sqrt{t}$ horizontally
- $h$ vertically and $t^2$ horizontally
- $h$ vertically and $t$ horizontally

## Explanation

The relation $h=\dfrac{g}{2}t^2$ is linear in $t^2$. With these axes the slope equals half of $g$.
