A boy throws a ball straight up at $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and catches it at the same height from which he threw it. The axis points upward and air resistance is negligible. What is the ball's velocity when it returns to his hand?

- $v=+12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $v=0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $v=-24\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $v=-12\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The ball returns to the launch height at $t_r=\dfrac{2v_0}{g}$. Substituting into the velocity equation gives
$$
v=v_0-g\,\frac{2v_0}{g}=-v_0=-12\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
The ball therefore returns with the same speed as at launch, directed downward. The value $+12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ has the right magnitude but describes upward motion; zero velocity occurs at the highest point.
