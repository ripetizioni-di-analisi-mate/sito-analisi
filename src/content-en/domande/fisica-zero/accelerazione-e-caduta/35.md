A ball is thrown downwards at $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ from a high platform. Taking $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ with negligible air resistance, how far does it drop in its first second?

- $10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Initial velocity contributes $5\,\mathrm{m}$ and gravity adds another $5\,\mathrm{m}$. Both contributions point downwards.
