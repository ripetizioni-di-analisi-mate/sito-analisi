A bicycle starts from rest on a bike path and covers $18\,\mathrm{m}$ with a constant acceleration of $1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is its velocity at the end of this stretch?

- $36\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The time is not given, so we use the relation $v^2-v_0^2=2a\Delta x$. With $v_0=0$ this gives
$$
v^2=2\cdot1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}\cdot18\,\mathrm{m}=36\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}.
$$
The bicycle moves in the positive direction, so $v=6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The value $36\,\mathrm{m/s}$ omits the square root, and $4.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ omits the factor of $2$.
