A ball falls from rest for time $T$ before reaching the ground. At what fraction of $T$ does it reach half its impact speed, with no air resistance?

- $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

In a fall from rest speed is proportional to time. Half the final speed is therefore reached halfway through the fall time.
