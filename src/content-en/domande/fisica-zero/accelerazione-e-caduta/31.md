A car travels at $24$ m/s and brakes with a constant acceleration of $-4$ meters per second squared. How long does it take to stop?

- $24\,\mathrm{s}$
- $96\,\mathrm{s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

When the car stops, its final velocity is zero. Solving the equation $0=24-4\Delta t$ gives $\Delta t=\dfrac{24}{4}$. Therefore the car stops after $6$ seconds.
