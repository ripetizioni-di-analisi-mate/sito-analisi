A marble is released from rest on an inclined plane and rolls down with constant acceleration. In the first second it travels $0.2\,\mathrm{m}$. How many meters does it travel between $t=1\,\mathrm{s}$ and $t=2\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $0.2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.8\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The marble starts from rest, so $\Delta x=\dfrac{1}{2}at^2$. For $t=1\,\mathrm{s}$ we have $\dfrac{1}{2}a\cdot1\,\mathrm{s^2}=0.2\,\mathrm{m}$, so $a=0.4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. After $2\,\mathrm{s}$ the displacement from the start is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot0.4\cdot4\,\mathrm{m}=0.8\,\mathrm{m}$. In the second second the marble therefore travels $0.8\,\mathrm{m}-0.2\,\mathrm{m}=0.6\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $0.8\,\mathrm{m}$ is the total displacement during the first two seconds.
