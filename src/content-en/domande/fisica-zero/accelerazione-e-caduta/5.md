A robot starts at the $5\,\mathrm{m}$ mark at $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and accelerates in that direction at $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ for $3\,\mathrm{s}$. Which coordinate does it reach?

- $15\,\mathrm{m}$
- $29\,\mathrm{m}$
- $11\,\mathrm{m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The initial velocity contributes $6\,\mathrm{m}$ and acceleration adds $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot2\cdot3^2=9$ metres. Adding both to the initial position gives $20\,\mathrm{m}$.
