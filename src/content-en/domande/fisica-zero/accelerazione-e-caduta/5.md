An object falls from rest for $3$ s. Neglecting air resistance and taking $g=10$ meters per second squared, what is its final speed?

- $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $45\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $90\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

In free fall from rest, the speed is $v=gt$. Substitute $g=10$ and $t=3$. The final speed is therefore $30$ meters per second.
