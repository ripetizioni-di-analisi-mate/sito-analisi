The graph shows the velocity of a car in city traffic between $0$ and $7\,\mathrm{s}$. In which interval is the car's displacement greatest?

::grafico{f="min(2*x,4,4-4*(x-4)/3)+0*sqrt(x*(7-x))" punti="0,0,pieno;2,4,pieno;4,4,pieno;7,0,pieno" segmenti="2,0,2,4;4,0,4,4" xmin=-1 xmax=8 ymin=-1 ymax=6 nomi="t,v" titolo="Velocity of the car in $\mathrm{m/s}$ and time in seconds"}

- Between $0$ and $2\,\mathrm{s}$
- Between $4$ and $7\,\mathrm{s}$
- Between $2$ and $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- The displacement is the same in all three intervals

## Explanation

In each interval, the displacement is the area under the graph. Between $0$ and $2\,\mathrm{s}$ there is a triangle with area $\dfrac{2\cdot4}{2}\,\mathrm{m}=4\,\mathrm{m}$. Between $2$ and $4\,\mathrm{s}$ there is a rectangle with area $2\cdot4\,\mathrm{m}=8\,\mathrm{m}$. Between $4$ and $7\,\mathrm{s}$ there is a triangle with area $\dfrac{3\cdot4}{2}\,\mathrm{m}=6\,\mathrm{m}$. The greatest displacement is therefore between $2$ and $4\,\mathrm{s}$, even though the last interval is the longest.
