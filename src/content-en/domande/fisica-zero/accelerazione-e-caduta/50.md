A measurement gives an acceleration of $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. For constant acceleration along the positive axis, which statement explains the unit?

- Velocity after one second is always $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- Velocity increases by $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ each second
- Speed is constant at $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- Position always increases by $3\,\mathrm{m}$ in one second

## Explanation

The extra second in the denominator refers to the time interval over which velocity changes. Each second therefore adds $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to the previous velocity.
