A stone is dropped from a bridge and hits the water after $2\,\mathrm{s}$. Neglecting air resistance and using $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the stone's speed just before it hits the water?

- $9.81\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $39.24\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $19.62\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4.9\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

In **free fall** from rest, with the axis pointing downward, the velocity is $v=gt$. So
$$
v=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}\cdot2\,\mathrm{s}=19.62\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
The value $9.81\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is the velocity after just one second. The value $39.24\,\mathrm{m/s}$ squares the time. The value $4.9\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is $\dfrac{1}{2}g$, the coefficient in the equation for the distance fallen.
