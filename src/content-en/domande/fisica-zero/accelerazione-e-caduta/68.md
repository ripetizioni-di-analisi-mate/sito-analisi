A sphere speeds up from $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in one region and from $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $14\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in a second region of the same length. Which has the larger constant acceleration magnitude?

- Both have equal acceleration
- The first region
- The accelerations have opposite signs
- The second region

## Explanation

For equal displacements compare the changes in squared speed. These are $64-4=60$ and $196-64=132$, so the second acceleration is larger.
