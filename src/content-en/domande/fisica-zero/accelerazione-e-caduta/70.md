A trolley must stop within $12\,\mathrm{m}$. It travels at $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and takes $2\,\mathrm{s}$ to stop once uniform braking begins. What is the longest delay before braking if it keeps travelling at $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ during the delay?

- $1\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Braking takes $4\cdot2=8$ metres. That leaves $4\,\mathrm{m}$ for the delay, covered at $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in half a second.
