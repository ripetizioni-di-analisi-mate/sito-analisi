A drop of water falls from rest from a gutter $5\,\mathrm{m}$ above the ground. Neglecting air resistance and using $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, how long does it take to reach the ground?

- $1\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.71\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

From $h=\dfrac{1}{2}gt^2$ we get $t^2=\dfrac{2h}{g}$. Substituting the data gives
$$
t^2=\frac{2\cdot5\,\mathrm{m}}{10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}}=1\,\mathrm{s^2}.
$$
The time is positive, so $t=1\,\mathrm{s}$. The value $0.5\,\mathrm{s}$ divides the height by $g$ without the factor of $2$ and without the square root, and $0.71\,\mathrm{s}$ takes the square root but omits the factor of $2$.
