An object falls from rest for $4$ s. Neglecting air resistance and taking $g=10$ meters per second squared, what is its final speed?

- $80\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $40\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $160\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

In free fall from rest, the speed is $v=gt$. Substitute $g=10$ and $t=4$. The final speed is therefore $40$ meters per second.
