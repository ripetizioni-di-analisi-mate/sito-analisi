A vehicle continues at $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for $1\,\mathrm{s}$ after a stop signal, then brakes uniformly to rest in another $2\,\mathrm{s}$. How far from the signal does it stop?

- $10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m}$
- $30\,\mathrm{m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

It travels $10\,\mathrm{m}$ during the reaction time. Average speed while braking is $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$, which adds another $10\,\mathrm{m}$.
