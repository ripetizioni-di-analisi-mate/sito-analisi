A ball is thrown upwards at the same speed on two airless planets. Gravity on planet B is half that on A. How does the height gained change?

- It halves on B
- It doubles on B
- It quadruples on B
- It stays the same

## Explanation

The height gain is $\dfrac{v_0^2}{2g}$. At fixed launch speed halving $g$ doubles the height gained.
