On a velocity–time graph, the region under the graph is a triangle with base $6$ s and height $4$ m/s, entirely above the time axis. What is the displacement?

- $24\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The displacement is the area under the velocity–time graph. The area of a triangle is half the product of its base and height. Therefore $\Delta s=\dfrac{6\cdot4}{2}=12$ meters.
