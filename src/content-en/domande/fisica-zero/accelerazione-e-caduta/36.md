In an acceleration test, a sports car must go from rest to $60\,\mathrm{m/s}$ over a $900\,\mathrm{m}$ stretch of track, with constant acceleration. What acceleration is needed?

- $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0.067\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The two velocities and the displacement are known, so the relation $v^2-v_0^2=2a\Delta x$ gives
$$
a=\frac{v^2-v_0^2}{2\Delta x}=\frac{(60\,\mathrm{m/s})^2-0}{2\cdot900\,\mathrm{m}}=\frac{3600}{1800}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.
$$
The value $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ omits the factor of $2$ in the denominator, and $1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ uses it twice. The value $0.067\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ divides the velocity by the distance without squaring it.
