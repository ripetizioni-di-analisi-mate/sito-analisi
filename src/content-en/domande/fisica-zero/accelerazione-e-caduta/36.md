A stopwatch starts when a falling ball passes through a ring at $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Taking downwards as positive, what initial velocity applies to the subsequent segment?

- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The segment begins at the ring crossing. Its initial velocity is therefore the velocity the ball already has at that instant.
