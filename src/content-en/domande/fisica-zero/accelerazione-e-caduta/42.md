An object falls from rest for $7$ s. With $g=10$ meters per second squared and negligible air resistance, what distance does it travel?

- $490\,\mathrm{m}$
- $245\,\mathrm{m}$
- $70\,\mathrm{m}$
- $35\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The distance fallen is $h=\dfrac{1}{2}gt^2$. The object starts from rest and its acceleration is constant. Therefore $h=5\cdot7^2=245$ meters.
