A roller coaster car enters a straight downhill stretch, $4\,\mathrm{m}$ long, with velocity $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and covers it with a constant acceleration of $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is its velocity at the end of the stretch?

- $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $25\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The time is not given, so we use the relation $v^2-v_0^2=2a\Delta x$, which gives
$$
v^2=v_0^2+2a\Delta x=(3\,\mathrm{m/s})^2+2\cdot2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}\cdot4\,\mathrm{m}=9\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}+16\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}=25\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}.
$$
The car moves in the positive direction, so $v=5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The value $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$ adds the square root of $16$ to the initial velocity, that is, it adds the velocities instead of their squares. The value $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ ignores the initial velocity, and $25\,\mathrm{m/s}$ omits the square root.
