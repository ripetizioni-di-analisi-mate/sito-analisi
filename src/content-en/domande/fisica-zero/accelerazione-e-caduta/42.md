A ball is launched upwards at $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ from a height of $2\,\mathrm{m}$. What maximum height does it reach with $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and no air resistance?

- $12\,\mathrm{m}$
- $7\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The ascent adds $\dfrac{10^2}{2\cdot10}=5$ metres to the initial height. The peak is therefore at $7\,\mathrm{m}$ above the chosen reference.
