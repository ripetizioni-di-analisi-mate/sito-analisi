A vehicle moves along a straight road with $x=4+3t-2t^2$ in SI units. What is its acceleration?

- $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

In the position law the coefficient of $t^2$ is $\dfrac{a}{2}$. Here it is $-2$, so acceleration is $-4$ metres per second squared.
