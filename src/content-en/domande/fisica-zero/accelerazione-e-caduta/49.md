The graph shows a runner's velocity during a $4\,\mathrm{s}$ acceleration, with velocity in $\mathrm{m/s}$ and time in seconds. What does the shaded area represent?

::grafico{f="3+0.5*x+0*sqrt(x*(4-x))" area="3+0.5*x;0;0;4" punti="0,3,pieno;4,5,pieno" segmenti="4,0,4,5" xmin=-1 xmax=5 ymin=-1 ymax=7 nomi="t,v" titolo="Velocity of the runner and the region between the graph and the time axis"}

- The runner's displacement between $0$ and $4\,\mathrm{s}$, in meters
- The runner's acceleration, in $\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- The velocity reached at $4\,\mathrm{s}$, in $\mathrm{m/s}$
- The time taken by the runner, in seconds

## Explanation

The area of a region on the velocity–time graph is the product of a velocity and a time. Its units are $\mathrm{m/s}\cdot\mathrm{s}=\mathrm{m}$, so the area represents a **displacement**. Here the region is a trapezoid with heights $3$ and $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and base $4\,\mathrm{s}$, and it corresponds to $16\,\mathrm{m}$. The acceleration, by contrast, is the slope of the segment, and the final velocity is the vertical coordinate of its right endpoint.
