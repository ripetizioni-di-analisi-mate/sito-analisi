An object starts with velocity $5$ m/s and accelerates at a constant rate of $2$ meters per second squared for $3$ s. What is its final velocity?

- $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $11\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The change in velocity is $a\Delta t=2\cdot3=6$ meters per second. Add this change to the initial velocity. The final velocity is therefore $11$ meters per second.
