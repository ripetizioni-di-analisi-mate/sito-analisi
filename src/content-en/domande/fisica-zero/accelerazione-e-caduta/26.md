A velocity–time graph is a horizontal line at $v=-3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What acceleration does it represent?

- $0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Acceleration is the graph’s slope. A horizontal line has zero slope even when it lies below the time axis.
