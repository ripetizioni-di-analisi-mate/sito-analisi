An object falls from rest for $6$ s. Neglecting air resistance and taking $g=10$ meters per second squared, what is its final speed?

- $180\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $360\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $60\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

In free fall from rest, the speed is $v=gt$. Substitute $g=10$ and $t=6$. The final speed is therefore $60$ meters per second.
