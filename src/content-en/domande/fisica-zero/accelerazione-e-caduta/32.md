A trolley goes from $0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in $4\,\mathrm{s}$. Which additional information lets us conclude that its displacement was $16\,\mathrm{m}$?

- It started at the origin
- Velocity increased linearly with time
- Its mass was $1\,\mathrm{kg}$
- The stopwatch read zero at the start

## Explanation

For a linear increase the average velocity is the mean of the endpoints, $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. This justifies multiplying $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ by $4\,\mathrm{s}$.
