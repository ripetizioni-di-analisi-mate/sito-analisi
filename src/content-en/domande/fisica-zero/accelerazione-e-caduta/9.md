A vehicle goes from $54\,\mathrm{km/h}$ to rest in $5\,\mathrm{s}$. What is its average acceleration, taking its initial direction as positive?

- $-10.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-15\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The initial speed is $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Thus velocity changes by $-15$ metres per second in $5\,\mathrm{s}$, giving $-3$ metres per second squared.
