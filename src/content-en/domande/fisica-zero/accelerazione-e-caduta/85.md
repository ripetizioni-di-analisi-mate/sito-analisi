A ball is dropped into a well. The time until impact is heard is used as if it were entirely fall time. How is the calculated depth affected if sound takes an appreciable time to travel back up?

- It is always halved
- It is underestimated
- It is overestimated
- It is exact because sound travels upwards

## Explanation

The recorded time includes both the fall and the return of the sound. Using too long a time in $h=\dfrac{gt^2}{2}$ gives too large a depth.
