A car travels at $28$ m/s and brakes with a constant acceleration of $-4$ meters per second squared. How long does it take to stop?

- $28\,\mathrm{s}$
- $112\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.75\,\mathrm{s}$
- $7\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

When the car stops, its final velocity is zero. Solving the equation $0=28-4\Delta t$ gives $\Delta t=\dfrac{28}{4}$. Therefore the car stops after $7$ seconds.
