A ball thrown upwards from a balcony lands below its launch height. How do its ascent time and its descent time from the peak compare, neglecting air resistance?

- Ascent takes longer
- Descent always takes exactly twice as long
- Descent takes longer
- They are equal for any final height

## Explanation

Returning from the peak to launch height takes as long as the ascent. The additional stretch to the ground adds to the descent time.
