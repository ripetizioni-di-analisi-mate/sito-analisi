A camera follows an object dropped from rest. Its distances in the first three equal time intervals are in the ratio 1, 3, 5. What number belongs to the fourth interval?

- $16$
- $7$
- $8$
- $9$

## Explanation

Cumulative distances are proportional to $1^2,2^2,3^2,4^2$. The fourth interval distance is proportional to $4^2-3^2=7$.
