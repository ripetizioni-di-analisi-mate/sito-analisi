An object is launched upward at $60$ m/s. With $g=10$ meters per second squared and negligible air resistance, how long does it take to reach its maximum height?

- $12\,\mathrm{s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{s}$
- $60\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

At the highest point the vertical velocity is zero. The velocity equation is $v=60-10\Delta t$. Setting $v=0$ gives $\Delta t=6$ seconds.
