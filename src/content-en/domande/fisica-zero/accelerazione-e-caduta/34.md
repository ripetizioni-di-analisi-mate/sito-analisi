A toy car is pushed up a sloping driveway and then rolls back. Its position along the driveway is $x=6t-t^2$, with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. What is the farthest position up the driveway that it reaches?

- $x=9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $x=3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $x=18\,\mathrm{m}$
- $x=36\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Comparing with the position equation gives $v_0=6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $\dfrac{1}{2}a=-1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, so $a=-2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and the velocity is $v=6-2t$. The car stops moving up when $v=0$, that is, at $t=3\,\mathrm{s}$. At that instant the position is
$$
x=6\cdot3-3^2=9\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
The value $3\,\mathrm{m}$ confuses the position with the instant at which it is reached. The value $18\,\mathrm{m}$ ignores the term $-t^2$.
