Two spheres are dropped from rest from heights $H$ and $4H$ in the same gravitational field without air resistance. What is the ratio of the second fall time to the first?

- $16$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $4$
- $2$

## Explanation

Fall time is proportional to the square root of height. The square root of four is two.
