The velocity of a skateboard rolling down a ramp is measured at $t=0$, $1$, $2$, and $3\,\mathrm{s}$. Which sequence of values corresponds to motion with constant acceleration?

- $2$, $4$, $8$, $16\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1$, $4$, $9$, $16\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10$, $8$, $5$, $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3$, $7$, $11$, $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

In uniformly accelerated motion, the velocity changes by the same amount in equal time intervals. In the sequence $3$, $7$, $11$, $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$ the velocity increases by $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ each second, so the acceleration is constant and equal to $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. In the sequence $2$, $4$, $8$, $16$ the increases are $2$, $4$, and $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and in $1$, $4$, $9$, $16$ they are $3$, $5$, and $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$. In the decreasing sequence the decreases are $2$, $3$, and $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$, which also differ from one another.
