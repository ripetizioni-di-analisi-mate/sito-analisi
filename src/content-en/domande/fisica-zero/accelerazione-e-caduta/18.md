Two identical objects fall in air, one with a parachute and one without. Which observation shows that constant-$g$ free fall no longer describes one of them?

- It was initially at rest
- Its descent takes positive time
- Its height decreases
- Its speed becomes constant while it keeps descending

## Explanation

Constant velocity implies zero acceleration. Air resistance must therefore be included to describe that interval.
