Velocity changes from $-12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in $2\,\mathrm{s}$. Which pair describes the average acceleration and the change in speed?

- Negative acceleration and increasing speed
- Negative acceleration and decreasing speed
- Positive acceleration and increasing speed
- Positive acceleration and decreasing speed

## Explanation

Signed velocity increases by $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$, while its magnitude falls from $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The two comparisons concern different quantities.
