An object starts from rest with a constant acceleration of $2$ meters per second squared. What is its velocity after $3$ s?

- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The velocity equation is $v=v_0+at$. The object starts from rest, so $v_0=0$. After $3$ seconds, its velocity is $v=2\cdot3=6$ meters per second.
