A ball is thrown upwards at $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and negligible air resistance, how long does it take to reach its highest point?

- $3\,\mathrm{s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $30\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The vertical velocity is zero at the top. Gravity removes $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ of upward velocity each second, so the ascent takes $3\,\mathrm{s}$.
