Four objects start from rest along a straight line. Which of the following motions has the greatest average acceleration?

- A bicycle from $0$ to $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in $2\,\mathrm{s}$
- A car from $0$ to $72\,\mathrm{km/h}$ in $8\,\mathrm{s}$
- A train from $0$ to $36\,\mathrm{km/h}$ in $20\,\mathrm{s}$
- A runner from $0$ to $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in $4\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

To compare the average accelerations, all velocities are expressed in $\mathrm{m/s}$, dividing kilometers per hour by $3.6$. The car reaches $\dfrac{72}{3.6}\,\mathrm{m/s}=20\,\mathrm{m/s}$, so it has $a_m=\dfrac{20}{8}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=2.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The train reaches $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$, so it has $a_m=\dfrac{10}{20}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=0.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The runner has $a_m=\dfrac{8}{4}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and the bicycle $a_m=\dfrac{6}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Therefore the greatest average acceleration is the bicycle's. Without the conversion the car would seem to have $\dfrac{72}{8}=9$, a value that mixes kilometers per hour and seconds.
