A ball passes balcony height at $2\,\mathrm{s}$ on its way up and $6\,\mathrm{s}$ on its way down. With no air resistance, when does it reach its highest point?

- $2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Constant-gravity motion is symmetric in time about the peak. The peak lies halfway between the crossings of the same height, at $4\,\mathrm{s}$.
