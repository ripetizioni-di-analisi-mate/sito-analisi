An object starts from rest with a constant acceleration of $2$ meters per second squared. What is its velocity after $7$ s?

- $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $14\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $49\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $28\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The velocity equation is $v=v_0+at$. The object starts from rest, so $v_0=0$. After $7$ seconds, its velocity is $v=2\cdot7=14$ meters per second.
