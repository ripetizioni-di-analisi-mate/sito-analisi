An object is launched upward at $50$ m/s. With $g=10$ meters per second squared and negligible air resistance, how long does it take to reach its maximum height?

- $5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{s}$
- $50\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

At the highest point the vertical velocity is zero. The velocity equation is $v=50-10\Delta t$. Setting $v=0$ gives $\Delta t=5$ seconds.
