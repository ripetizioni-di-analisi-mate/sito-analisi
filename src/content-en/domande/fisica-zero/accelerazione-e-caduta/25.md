A car brakes from $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ with an acceleration of $-3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and stays at rest once stopped. Where is it relative to the braking point after $5\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-7.5\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Braking lasts $2\,\mathrm{s}$ with an average velocity of $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The car then remains at the $6\,\mathrm{m}$ mark throughout the next $3\,\mathrm{s}$.
