A car accelerates uniformly from $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$. When its speed reaches $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what fractions of the time and distance have elapsed?

- Half the time and less than half the distance
- Less than half the time and half the distance
- Half the time and half the distance
- Half the time and more than half the distance

## Explanation

Velocity changes linearly, so $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$ occurs halfway in time. Average speed is lower in the first half than in the second, so less distance is covered before halfway.
