A flowerpot falls from rest from the roof of a $30\,\mathrm{m}$ building. Neglecting air resistance and using $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, how many meters does it fall between $t=1\,\mathrm{s}$ and $t=2\,\mathrm{s}$ after it leaves the roof?

- $19.6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4.9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $14.7\,\mathrm{m}$
- $9.8\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The distance fallen from the release point is $h=\dfrac{1}{2}gt^2$. At $t=1\,\mathrm{s}$ it is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot9.81\cdot1\,\mathrm{m}\approx4.9\,\mathrm{m}$, and at $t=2\,\mathrm{s}$ it is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot9.81\cdot4\,\mathrm{m}\approx19.6\,\mathrm{m}$. The pot is still falling, because $19.6\,\mathrm{m}$ is less than the height of the building. In the requested interval it therefore falls
$$
\frac{1}{2}\cdot9.81\cdot(4-1)\,\mathrm{m}\approx14.7\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
The value $19.6\,\mathrm{m}$ is the total distance during the first two seconds.
