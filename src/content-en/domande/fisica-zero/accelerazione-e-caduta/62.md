A ball falling from a roof is photographed at equal time intervals. Which sequence of successive spacings, in centimetres, is consistent with constant downward acceleration throughout?

- $4,7,10,13$
- $4,7,7,13$
- $4,10,11,19$
- $4,8,16,32$

## Explanation

At constant acceleration the distances in equal consecutive intervals grow by equal amounts. The sequence that increases by $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ each time meets this condition.
