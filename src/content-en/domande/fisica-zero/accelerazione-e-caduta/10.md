An object's velocity changes from $4$ m/s to $12$ m/s in $2$ s. What is its average acceleration?

- $8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Average acceleration is the change in velocity divided by the elapsed time. The change is $8$ meters per second. Dividing by $2$ seconds gives $4$ meters per second squared.
