An object falls from rest for $1.5\,\mathrm{s}$ before reaching the ground. Neglect air resistance and take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. How far does it fall in that interval?

- $22.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $11.25\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Distance is $\dfrac{gt^2}{2}$. Substitution gives $5\cdot1.5^2=11.25$ metres.
