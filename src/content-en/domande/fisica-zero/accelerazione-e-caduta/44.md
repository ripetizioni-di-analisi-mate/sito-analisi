A sphere is released inside a lift travelling upwards at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Immediately after release, before gravity appreciably changes its velocity, which way does it move relative to the building?

- It is instantaneously at rest
- Downwards
- Upwards
- Horizontally

## Explanation

Before release the sphere shares the lift’s velocity. Removing the support produces no jump in velocity, so the sphere initially continues upwards.
