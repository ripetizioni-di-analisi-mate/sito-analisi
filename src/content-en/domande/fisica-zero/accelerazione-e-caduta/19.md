An object's velocity changes from $5$ m/s to $15$ m/s in $2$ s. What is its average acceleration?

- $10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Average acceleration is the change in velocity divided by the elapsed time. The change is $10$ meters per second. Dividing by $2$ seconds gives $5$ meters per second squared.
