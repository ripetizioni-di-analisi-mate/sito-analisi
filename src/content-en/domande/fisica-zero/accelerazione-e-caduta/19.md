Two drops leave the same point from rest $1\,\mathrm{s}$ apart. Once both are in free fall in a vacuum with $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, how does their separation change?

- It grows by $5\,\mathrm{m}$ every two seconds
- It remains $5\,\mathrm{m}$
- It grows by $10\,\mathrm{m}$ each second
- It shrinks by $10\,\mathrm{m}$ each second

## Explanation

At the second release the first drop is already moving down at $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Equal accelerations preserve that velocity difference, so the gap grows at $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
