Two vehicles accelerate along the same axis. The second vehicle’s velocity–time graph is the first graph shifted up by $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Over $4\,\mathrm{s}$, by how much do their signed displacements differ?

- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $7\,\mathrm{m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The strip between the graphs has constant height $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Its area over $4\,\mathrm{s}$ is $12\,\mathrm{m}$, regardless of the graphs’ common shape.
