A trolley goes from $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $16\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in $6\,\mathrm{s}$ along the positive axis. What is its average acceleration?

- $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Velocity changes by $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Dividing by $6\,\mathrm{s}$ gives an average gain of $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ each second.
