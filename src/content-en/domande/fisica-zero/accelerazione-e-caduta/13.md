A regional train leaves the station from rest with a constant acceleration of $0.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is its velocity after $40\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $80\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $400\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

With constant acceleration, the velocity equation $v=v_0+at$ applies. The train starts from rest, so $v_0=0$ and
$$
v=0.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}\cdot40\,\mathrm{s}=20\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
The value $80\,\mathrm{m/s}$ divides the time by the acceleration. The value $400\,\mathrm{m/s}$ uses $\dfrac{1}{2}at^2$, which is a displacement, and $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ includes a factor of $\dfrac{1}{2}$ that does not appear in the velocity equation.
