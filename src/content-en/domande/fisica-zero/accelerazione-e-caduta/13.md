A car travels at $16$ m/s and brakes with a constant acceleration of $-4$ meters per second squared. How long does it take to stop?

- $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $16\,\mathrm{s}$
- $64\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

When the car stops, its final velocity is zero. Solving the equation $0=16-4\Delta t$ gives $\Delta t=\dfrac{16}{4}$. Therefore the car stops after $4$ seconds.
