A ball is thrown vertically upwards at $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and returns to its launch height. With no air resistance and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what total distance does it travel?

- $10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The height gained is $\dfrac{10^2}{2\cdot10}=5$ metres. Ascent and descent both contribute to distance, giving $10\,\mathrm{m}$.
