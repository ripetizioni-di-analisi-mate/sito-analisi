Two electric cars accelerate uniformly along the same straight road. On the velocity–time graphs, A's line starts at $0$ and is steeper than B's line, which starts at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Which statement is certainly true?

- A's velocity is greater than B's at every instant
- A covers more distance than B in every interval
- The two cars have the same acceleration
- A's acceleration is greater than B's

## Explanation

On a velocity–time graph, the slope of the line measures the acceleration. A's line is steeper, so **A's acceleration is greater**. At the initial instant A is at rest and B is moving at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$, so A is not faster at every instant. For the same reason, at first the area under B's line is larger, and B covers more distance.
