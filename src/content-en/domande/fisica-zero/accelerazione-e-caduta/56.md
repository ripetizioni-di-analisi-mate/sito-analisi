An acceleration rises uniformly from zero to $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ in $3\,\mathrm{s}$. By how much does velocity increase?

- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Velocity change is the area on an acceleration–time graph. The triangle has base $3\,\mathrm{s}$ and height $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, giving $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
