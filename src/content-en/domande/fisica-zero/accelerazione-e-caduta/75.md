A stone is dropped from rest from the edge of a cliff and hits the sea after $3\,\mathrm{s}$. Neglecting air resistance and using $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, how high is the cliff above sea level?

- $29.4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $88.3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $44.1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $14.7\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The distance fallen in free fall from rest is $h=\dfrac{1}{2}gt^2$. With $t=3\,\mathrm{s}$ this gives
$$
h=\frac{1}{2}\cdot9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}\cdot9\,\mathrm{s^2}\approx44.1\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
The value $29.4\,\mathrm{m}$ computes $gt$, which is a velocity. The value $88.3\,\mathrm{m}$ omits the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$, and $14.7\,\mathrm{m}$ does not square the time.
