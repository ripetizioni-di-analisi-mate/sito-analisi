An object falls from rest for $4$ s. With $g=10$ meters per second squared and negligible air resistance, what distance does it travel?

- $160\,\mathrm{m}$
- $40\,\mathrm{m}$
- $80\,\mathrm{m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The distance fallen is $h=\dfrac{1}{2}gt^2$. The object starts from rest and its acceleration is constant. Therefore $h=5\cdot4^2=80$ meters.
