A trolley increases its velocity uniformly from $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. How long does the increase take?

- $6\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $9\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

It must gain $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at a rate of $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ each second. Dividing gives $4\,\mathrm{s}$.
