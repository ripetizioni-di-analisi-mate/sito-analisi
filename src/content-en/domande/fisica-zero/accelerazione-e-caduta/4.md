A subway train starts from rest at one station, reaches $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and stops at the next station. From departure to stop, $90\,\mathrm{s}$ elapse. What is the average acceleration over the whole trip?

- $0.17\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0.33\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Average acceleration depends only on the initial velocity, the final velocity, and the elapsed time. The train starts at rest and ends at rest, so $v_f-v_i=0$. Therefore
$$
a_m=\frac{0-0}{90\,\mathrm{s}}=0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.
$$
The maximum velocity of $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$ refers to an instant within the trip and does not enter the calculation. The value $0.17\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ divides that velocity by $90\,\mathrm{s}$.
