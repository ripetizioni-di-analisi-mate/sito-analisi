A car travels at $12$ m/s and brakes with a constant acceleration of $-4$ meters per second squared. How long does it take to stop?

- $12\,\mathrm{s}$
- $48\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.75\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

When the car stops, its final velocity is zero. Solving the equation $0=12-4\Delta t$ gives $\Delta t=\dfrac{12}{4}$. Therefore the car stops after $3$ seconds.
