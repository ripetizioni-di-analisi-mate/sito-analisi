A sprinter leaves the starting blocks and reaches a velocity of $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in $3\,\mathrm{s}$. Which expression correctly states the sprinter's average acceleration?

- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m\cdot s}$
- $27\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Average acceleration is the change in velocity divided by the time, so its unit is $\mathrm{m/s}$ divided by $\mathrm{s}$, that is, $\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The sprinter starts from rest, so the change in velocity is $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the ratio is $\dfrac{9}{3}=3$. The correct expression is therefore $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, which means that on average the velocity increases by $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ each second. The value $27\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ multiplies the velocity by the time instead of dividing.
