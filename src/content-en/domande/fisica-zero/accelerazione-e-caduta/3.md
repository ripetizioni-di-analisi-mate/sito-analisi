An object starts at $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and accelerates at $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ in that direction for $5\,\mathrm{s}$. What is its final velocity?

- $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $13\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The gain in velocity is $2\cdot5=10$ metres per second. Adding the initial velocity gives $13\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
