An object starts from rest and has a constant acceleration of $2$ meters per second squared. What is its displacement in the first $3$ s?

- $18\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

For zero initial velocity, the displacement is $\Delta s=\dfrac{1}{2}at^2$. The factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$ cancels the factor of two from $a=2$. Therefore the displacement is $3^2=9$ meters.
