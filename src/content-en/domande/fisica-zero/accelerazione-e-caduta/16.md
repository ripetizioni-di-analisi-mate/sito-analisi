A toy train runs on a straight track with initial velocity $v_0=-3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and constant acceleration $a=+1.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, maintained for $4\,\mathrm{s}$. What happens during this interval?

- It slows down, stops at $t=2\,\mathrm{s}$, and then moves in the positive direction
- Its speed in the negative direction increases for all $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- It slows down and stops at exactly $t=4\,\mathrm{s}$
- It always moves in the positive direction, faster and faster

## Explanation

The velocity equation is $v=-3+1.5\,t$, with $v$ in $\mathrm{m/s}$ and $t$ in seconds. Velocity and acceleration initially have opposite signs, so the train slows down. The velocity is zero when $1.5\,t=3$, that is, at $t=2\,\mathrm{s}$. After that instant the velocity is positive and increasing, so the train moves in the positive direction, reaching $v=3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at $t=4\,\mathrm{s}$.
