A capsule falls in a vacuum with the sensor’s positive axis pointing downwards. Which velocity law applies after release from rest?

- $v=-gt$
- $v=g$
- $v=gt$
- $v=\dfrac{gt^2}{2}$

## Explanation

Acceleration points along the positive axis. Starting from zero, the signed velocity therefore increases by $g$ each second.
