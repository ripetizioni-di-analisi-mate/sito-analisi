A falling sphere reappears in a photograph $0.1\,\mathrm{s}$ later. Which pair of data is sufficient to find its average speed between the pictures even if acceleration varies?

- The actual distance travelled and elapsed time
- Only the endpoint accelerations
- Initial velocity alone and elapsed time
- The sphere’s mass and elapsed time

## Explanation

Average speed is defined as distance divided by elapsed time. This definition also applies when acceleration varies.
