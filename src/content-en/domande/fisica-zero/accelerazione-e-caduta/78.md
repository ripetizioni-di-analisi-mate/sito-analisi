A ball falls for $3\,\mathrm{s}$ from rest. Another is thrown downwards from the same height $1\,\mathrm{s}$ later. What launch speed makes it arrive together with the first if $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and air resistance is negligible?

- $12.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $22.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The first ball falls $45\,\mathrm{m}$. In the second ball’s $2\,\mathrm{s}$, gravity accounts for $20\,\mathrm{m}$ and its launch speed must account for the other $25\,\mathrm{m}$, requiring $12.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
