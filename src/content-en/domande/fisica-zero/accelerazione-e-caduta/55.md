An acceleration graph shows $+3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ for $2\,\mathrm{s}$ and $-1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ for $4\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the total change in velocity?

- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The signed areas are $+6$ and $-4$ metres per second. Their sum leaves a $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ increase.
