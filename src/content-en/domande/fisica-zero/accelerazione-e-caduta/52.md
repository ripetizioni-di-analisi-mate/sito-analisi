A trolley starts from rest at $x=0$ with acceleration $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Another passes the same point at that instant at a constant $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. When does the first trolley next catch the second?

- $1\,\mathrm{s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The positions in metres are $t^2$ and $4t$. Equality has two solutions, $t=0$ and $t=4$, and the later meeting is at $4\,\mathrm{s}$.
