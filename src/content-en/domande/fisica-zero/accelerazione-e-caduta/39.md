An object starts from rest and has a constant acceleration of $2$ meters per second squared. What is its displacement in the first $7$ s?

- $98\,\mathrm{m}$
- $14\,\mathrm{m}$
- $49\,\mathrm{m}$
- $7\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

For zero initial velocity, the displacement is $\Delta s=\dfrac{1}{2}at^2$. The factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$ cancels the factor of two from $a=2$. Therefore the displacement is $7^2=49$ meters.
