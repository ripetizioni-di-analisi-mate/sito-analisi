A ball passes a window going upwards at $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. How long until its second pass at the window, with $g=8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and no air resistance?

- $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

On the return pass the velocity is $-8$ metres per second. The full change is $-16$ metres per second, which takes $2\,\mathrm{s}$ at $-8$ metres per second squared.
