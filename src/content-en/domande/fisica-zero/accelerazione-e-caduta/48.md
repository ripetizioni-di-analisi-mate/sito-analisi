In a warehouse, a forklift starts from rest and backs up; the positive direction of the axis is the forward direction. The graph shows its velocity during the first $3\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the displacement?

::grafico{f="-2*x/3+0*sqrt(x*(3-x))" area="0;-2*x/3;0;3" punti="0,0,pieno;3,-2,pieno" segmenti="3,0,3,-2" xmin=-1 xmax=4 ymin=-3 ymax=1 nomi="t,v" titolo="Velocity of the forklift in $\mathrm{m/s}$ and time in seconds"}

- $+3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-1.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The region between the graph and the time axis is a triangle with base $3\,\mathrm{s}$ and height $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$, lying below the axis. Regions below the axis contribute negatively, so
$$
\Delta x=-\frac{3\,\mathrm{s}\cdot2\,\mathrm{m/s}}{2}=-3\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
The negative sign indicates that the forklift moved backward. The value $+3\,\mathrm{m}$ is the distance traveled, and $-6\,\mathrm{m}$ forgets to divide by $2$.
