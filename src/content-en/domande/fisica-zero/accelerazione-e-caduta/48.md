Two sensors measure a trolley’s velocity using axes pointing in opposite directions. How are the accelerations they calculate from two synchronised readings related?

- They always have the same sign
- They have equal magnitude and opposite signs when nonzero
- One has twice the magnitude
- Both must be zero

## Explanation

Reversing the axis reverses both velocity signs. Their difference also reverses, while the elapsed time is unchanged.
