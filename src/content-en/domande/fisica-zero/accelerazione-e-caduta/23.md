A trolley travels at $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and must stop in exactly $8\,\mathrm{m}$ with constant acceleration. What deceleration magnitude is required?

- $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

At the stop $v^2=0$. From $v_0^2=2|a|s$ we obtain $|a|=\dfrac{64}{16}=4$ metres per second squared.
