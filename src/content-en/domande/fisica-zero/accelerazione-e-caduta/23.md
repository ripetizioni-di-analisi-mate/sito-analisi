An object falls from rest for $5$ s. Neglecting air resistance and taking $g=10$ meters per second squared, what is its final speed?

- $125\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $250\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $50\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

In free fall from rest, the speed is $v=gt$. Substitute $g=10$ and $t=5$. The final speed is therefore $50$ meters per second.
