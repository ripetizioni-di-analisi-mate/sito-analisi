A key is released from rest relative to the floor of a freely falling lift. Before impact and neglecting air resistance, how does the key accelerate relative to the floor?

- It accelerates towards the ceiling at $g$
- It accelerates towards the floor at $g$
- It has zero relative acceleration
- It accelerates towards the floor at $2g$

## Explanation

Relative to the ground the key and floor both accelerate downwards at $g$. Their difference in acceleration is therefore zero.
