A bunch of keys thrown straight up rises to $1.25\,\mathrm{m}$ above its starting point and then falls back to the same level, where it is caught. Neglecting air resistance and using $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, how long does the whole flight last?

- $0.5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The descent from the highest point is a fall from rest through $1.25\,\mathrm{m}$, so it lasts
$$
t=\sqrt{\frac{2h}{g}}=\sqrt{\frac{2\cdot1.25}{10}}\,\mathrm{s}=\sqrt{0.25}\,\mathrm{s}=0.5\,\mathrm{s}.
$$
The ascent lasts as long as the descent, so the whole flight lasts $2\cdot0.5\,\mathrm{s}=1\,\mathrm{s}$. The value $0.5\,\mathrm{s}$ is the ascent alone or the descent alone. The value $0.25\,\mathrm{s}$ stops at $t^2$ without taking the square root.
