On a trolley’s velocity–time graph one point has $v=0$. What observation immediately before and after would distinguish a genuine reversal from stopping and resuming in the same direction?

- Equal measurement intervals
- Velocities with opposite signs
- Equal speed magnitudes
- Two positive positions

## Explanation

The sign of velocity gives direction along the axis. Opposite signs on either side of the stop show that direction has changed.
