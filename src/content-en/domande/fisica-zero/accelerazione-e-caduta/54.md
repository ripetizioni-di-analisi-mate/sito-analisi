An object crosses the origin going left at $-5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Its constant acceleration is $+1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. On which side of the origin is it when it reverses?

- Left of the origin
- At the origin
- Right of the origin
- Anywhere, because the mass is unknown

## Explanation

Until its velocity reaches zero the object continues leftwards. It therefore reverses after moving away from the origin on the negative side.
