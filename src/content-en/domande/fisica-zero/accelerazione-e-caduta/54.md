The graph shows the velocity of a remote-controlled car moving along an axis, between $0$ and $4\,\mathrm{s}$. Which description of the motion matches the graph?

::grafico{f="-2+x+0*sqrt(x*(4-x))" punti="0,-2,pieno;2,0,pieno;4,2,pieno" xmin=-1 xmax=5 ymin=-3 ymax=3 nomi="t,v" titolo="Velocity of the remote-controlled car in $\mathrm{m/s}$ and time in seconds"}

- It moves in the positive direction while slowing down, stops at $2\,\mathrm{s}$, then turns back
- It always moves in the negative direction, faster and faster
- It stays at rest until $2\,\mathrm{s}$ and then starts off in the positive direction
- It moves in the negative direction while slowing down, stops at $2\,\mathrm{s}$, then moves in the positive direction faster and faster

## Explanation

Until $2\,\mathrm{s}$ the graph is below the axis, so the velocity is negative and the car moves in the negative direction. In that stretch the magnitude of the velocity goes from $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to zero, so the car slows down. At $2\,\mathrm{s}$ the velocity is zero. Afterward the graph is above the axis and increasing, so the car moves in the positive direction faster and faster. The slope is always positive, so the acceleration stays positive over the whole interval.
