An object falls from rest without air resistance. It drops $20\,\mathrm{m}$ in the first $2\,\mathrm{s}$. How far does it drop between $2\,\mathrm{s}$ and $4\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $60\,\mathrm{m}$
- $40\,\mathrm{m}$
- $80\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Cumulative fall distance is proportional to time squared. At $4\,\mathrm{s}$ it is four times $20\,\mathrm{m}$, so the next interval adds $80-20=60$ metres.
