An object starts from rest and has a constant acceleration of $2$ meters per second squared. What is its displacement in the first $5$ s?

- $25\,\mathrm{m}$
- $50\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

For zero initial velocity, the displacement is $\Delta s=\dfrac{1}{2}at^2$. The factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$ cancels the factor of two from $a=2$. Therefore the displacement is $5^2=25$ meters.
