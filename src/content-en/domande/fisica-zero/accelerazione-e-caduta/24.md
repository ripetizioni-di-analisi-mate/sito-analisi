A vehicle brakes from $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at constant acceleration. What fraction of the total distance needed to stop from $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$ has it already covered?

- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

At half speed one quarter of the original braking distance remains, since stopping distance depends on speed squared. Three quarters has therefore been covered.
