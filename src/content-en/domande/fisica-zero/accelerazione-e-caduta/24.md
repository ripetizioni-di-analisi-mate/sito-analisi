An object falls from rest for $5$ s. With $g=10$ meters per second squared and negligible air resistance, what distance does it travel?

- $250\,\mathrm{m}$
- $50\,\mathrm{m}$
- $25\,\mathrm{m}$
- $125\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The distance fallen is $h=\dfrac{1}{2}gt^2$. The object starts from rest and its acceleration is constant. Therefore $h=5\cdot5^2=125$ meters.
