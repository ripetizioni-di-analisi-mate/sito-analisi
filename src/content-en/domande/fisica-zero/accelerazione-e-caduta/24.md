When the light turns green, a car starts from rest with a constant acceleration of $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. How many meters does it travel in the first $4\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $12\,\mathrm{m}$
- $24\,\mathrm{m}$
- $48\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

By the **position equation** for constant acceleration, the displacement is $x-x_0=v_0t+\dfrac{1}{2}at^2$. The car starts from rest, so $v_0=0$ and
$$
x-x_0=\frac{1}{2}\cdot3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}\cdot(4\,\mathrm{s})^2=24\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
The velocity stays positive, so the displacement equals the distance traveled. The value $48\,\mathrm{m}$ omits the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$, and the values $12$ and $6\,\mathrm{m}$ do not square the time.
