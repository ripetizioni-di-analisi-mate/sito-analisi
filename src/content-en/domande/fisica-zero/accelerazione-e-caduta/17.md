The graph shows the velocity of a go-kart on a straight stretch during the first $4\,\mathrm{s}$. What is its acceleration?

::grafico{f="1+2*x+0*sqrt(x*(4-x))" punti="0,1,pieno;4,9,pieno" segmenti="4,0,4,9;0,9,4,9" xmin=-1 xmax=5 ymin=-1 ymax=11 nomi="t,v" titolo="Velocity of the go-kart in $\mathrm{m/s}$ and time in seconds"}

- $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2.25\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The velocity–time graph is a straight line segment, so the acceleration is constant and equals the slope. The velocity goes from $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in $4\,\mathrm{s}$, so
$$
a=\frac{9\,\mathrm{m/s}-1\,\mathrm{m/s}}{4\,\mathrm{s}}=2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.
$$
The value $2.25\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ divides only the final velocity by the time, without subtracting the initial value. The value $9\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ reads the final vertical coordinate as if it were the acceleration.
