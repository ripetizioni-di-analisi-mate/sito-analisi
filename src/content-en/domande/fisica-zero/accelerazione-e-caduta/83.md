A juggler throws a club straight up and catches it at the same height from which it was thrown. Air resistance is negligible. How do the club's ascent time and descent time compare?

- They are equal
- The descent takes longer than the ascent
- The descent takes less time than the ascent
- It depends on the club's mass

## Explanation

With the axis pointing upward, the height is $y=v_0t-\dfrac{1}{2}gt^2$, which is zero again at $t_r=\dfrac{2v_0}{g}$. The highest point is reached at $t_s=\dfrac{v_0}{g}$, exactly halfway through the flight. The descent therefore lasts $t_r-t_s=\dfrac{v_0}{g}$, **the same time** as the ascent. The mass does not appear in either expression.
