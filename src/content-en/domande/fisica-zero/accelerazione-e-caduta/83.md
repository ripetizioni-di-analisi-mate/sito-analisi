Two vehicles brake side by side from $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The first stops in $2\,\mathrm{s}$ and the second in $4\,\mathrm{s}$. For uniform braking, how much farther does the second travel?

- $10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $30\,\mathrm{m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Both mean speeds are $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The second vehicle’s extra $2\,\mathrm{s}$ therefore correspond to $10\,\mathrm{m}$ more.
