A ball is thrown vertically from ground level and returns after $4\,\mathrm{s}$. With no air resistance and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what was its initial upward velocity?

- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $40\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The ascent takes half the flight time, or $2\,\mathrm{s}$. Gravity removes the initial upward velocity in that time, so it was $10\cdot2=20$ metres per second.
