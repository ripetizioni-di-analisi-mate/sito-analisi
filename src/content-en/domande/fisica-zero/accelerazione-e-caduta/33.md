An object falls from rest for $6$ s. With $g=10$ meters per second squared and negligible air resistance, what distance does it travel?

- $180\,\mathrm{m}$
- $360\,\mathrm{m}$
- $60\,\mathrm{m}$
- $30\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The distance fallen is $h=\dfrac{1}{2}gt^2$. The object starts from rest and its acceleration is constant. Therefore $h=5\cdot6^2=180$ meters.
