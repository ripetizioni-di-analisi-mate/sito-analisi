A robot vacuum moves along a straight hallway. At time $t=0$ it is $2\,\mathrm{m}$ before the origin, with velocity $0.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the positive direction and constant acceleration $0.1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Which position equation describes it, with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds?

- $x=2+0.5\,t+0.05\,t^2$
- $x=-2+0.5\,t+0.05\,t^2$
- $x=-2+0.5\,t+0.1\,t^2$
- $x=-2+0.05\,t+0.5\,t^2$

## Explanation

The robot is before the origin, so its initial position is negative, $x_0=-2\,\mathrm{m}$. In the position equation $x=x_0+v_0t+\dfrac{1}{2}at^2$, the coefficient of $t^2$ is half the acceleration, that is, $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot0.1=0.05$. Therefore $x=-2+0.5\,t+0.05\,t^2$. The equation with $+2$ places the robot after the origin, and the one with $0.1\,t^2$ omits the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
