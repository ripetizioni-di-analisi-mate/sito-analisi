A sensor records the velocity component along a fixed axis. Its reading changes from $+2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $+10\,\mathrm{m/s}$, then back to $+2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is necessarily true of the average acceleration over the entire interval?

- It is zero
- It is negative because the last phase was braking
- It is positive because velocity increased
- It is $8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Final and initial velocities agree, so the overall change is zero. The accelerations in the individual phases can still be nonzero.
