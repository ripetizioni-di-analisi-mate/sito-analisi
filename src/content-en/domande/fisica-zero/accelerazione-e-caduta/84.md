A ball thrown upwards slows during its ascent. What shape does its speed–time graph have around the highest point, with no air resistance?

- A horizontal zero line throughout descent
- A line continuing below the axis
- Two straight segments forming a V with a zero minimum
- A parabola peaking at the top

## Explanation

Signed velocity passes linearly through zero. Taking its magnitude reflects the negative portion above the axis, forming a V.
