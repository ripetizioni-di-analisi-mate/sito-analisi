An object falls from rest for $3$ s. With $g=10$ meters per second squared and negligible air resistance, what distance does it travel?

- $90\,\mathrm{m}$
- $45\,\mathrm{m}$
- $30\,\mathrm{m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The distance fallen is $h=\dfrac{1}{2}gt^2$. The object starts from rest and its acceleration is constant. Therefore $h=5\cdot3^2=45$ meters.
