A point starts from rest with a constant acceleration of $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. When has it travelled $16\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{s}$
- $16\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

For these data distance in metres is $t^2$, with $t$ in seconds. The positive square root of 16 gives $4\,\mathrm{s}$ after the start.
