A cable car cabin starts from rest and reaches a velocity of $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in $1$ minute and $20$ seconds. What is its average acceleration?

- $0.4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0.1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0.13\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0.067\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Acceleration is measured in $\mathrm{m/s^2}$, so the time must be expressed in seconds, and $1$ minute and $20$ seconds is $60\,\mathrm{s}+20\,\mathrm{s}=80\,\mathrm{s}$. The change in velocity is $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$, so
$$
a_m=\frac{8\,\mathrm{m/s}}{80\,\mathrm{s}}=0.1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.
$$
The value $0.4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ uses only the $20$ seconds, and the value $0.13\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ uses only the minute. The value $0.067\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ reads the duration as $120\,\mathrm{s}$.
