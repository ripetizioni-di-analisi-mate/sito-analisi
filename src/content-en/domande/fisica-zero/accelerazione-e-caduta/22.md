Two uniform braking manoeuvres have the same deceleration magnitude. If the starting speed doubles, how does braking distance change?

- It doubles
- It halves
- It quadruples
- It stays the same

## Explanation

Braking distance is $\dfrac{v_0^2}{2|a|}$. The numerator quadruples while the denominator stays fixed.
