A car travels at $20$ m/s and brakes with a constant acceleration of $-4$ meters per second squared. How long does it take to stop?

- $20\,\mathrm{s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $80\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.25\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

When the car stops, its final velocity is zero. Solving the equation $0=20-4\Delta t$ gives $\Delta t=\dfrac{20}{4}$. Therefore the car stops after $5$ seconds.
