A shopping cart moves along a supermarket aisle, on an axis with a chosen positive direction. For which of the following pairs of velocity and acceleration, taken at the same instant, does the cart's speed increase?

- $v=-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $a=-2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $v=+4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $a=-2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $v=-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $a=+2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $v=+4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $a=0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Speed is the magnitude of the velocity, and it increases when velocity and acceleration have the same sign. In the pair $v=-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$, $a=-2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ the two signs agree, so the velocity becomes more and more negative and its magnitude grows. In the two pairs with opposite signs, the magnitude of the velocity decreases. With $a=0$ the velocity stays constant.
