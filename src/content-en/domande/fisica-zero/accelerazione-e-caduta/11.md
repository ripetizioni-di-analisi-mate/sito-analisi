An object starts from rest with a constant acceleration of $2$ meters per second squared. What is its velocity after $4$ s?

- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The velocity equation is $v=v_0+at$. The object starts from rest, so $v_0=0$. After $4$ seconds, its velocity is $v=2\cdot4=8$ meters per second.
