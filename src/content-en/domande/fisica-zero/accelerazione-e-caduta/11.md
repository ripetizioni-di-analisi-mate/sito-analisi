An object is dropped from rest $20\,\mathrm{m}$ above the ground. How fast is it moving just before impact if $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and air resistance is negligible?

- $40\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $200\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Time is not given. The relation $v^2=2gh$ gives $v^2=400$ in SI units, so the speed is $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
