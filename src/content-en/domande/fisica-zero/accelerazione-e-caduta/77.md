A ball is thrown upwards at $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and no air resistance, for how long is it more than $15\,\mathrm{m}$ above launch level?

- $1\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Relative height in metres is $20t-5t^2$. It equals $15\,\mathrm{m}$ at $1\,\mathrm{s}$ and $3\,\mathrm{s}$ and exceeds $15\,\mathrm{m}$ between those crossings, a duration of $2\,\mathrm{s}$.
