An object's velocity changes from $7$ m/s to $21$ m/s in $2$ s. What is its average acceleration?

- $7\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $14\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $28\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Average acceleration is the change in velocity divided by the elapsed time. The change is $14$ meters per second. Dividing by $2$ seconds gives $7$ meters per second squared.
