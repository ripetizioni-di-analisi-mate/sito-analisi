A ball passes a window on its way up at $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is its velocity as it passes the same window on the way down, taking upwards as positive and neglecting air resistance?

- $-12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Between crossings at the same height the change in $v^2$ is zero. The speed remains $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the direction reverses.
