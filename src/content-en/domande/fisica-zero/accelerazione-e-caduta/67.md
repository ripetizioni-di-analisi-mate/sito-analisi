A student extends a uniform braking velocity–time line beyond its zero crossing and predicts reverse motion. What information would justify that extension?

- That the initial velocity was positive
- That acceleration retains its direction and value after the stop
- That the road was straight before stopping
- That braking distance was known

## Explanation

A braking model specifies motion until rest. Extending it requires a force that continues to produce the same acceleration even as the vehicle begins moving again.
