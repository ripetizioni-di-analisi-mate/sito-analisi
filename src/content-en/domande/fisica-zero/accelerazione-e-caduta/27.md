Two balls leave a horizontal table at the same instant. One is dropped from rest and the other rolls off the edge with purely horizontal velocity. With a level floor and negligible air resistance, which hits the floor first?

- The dropped ball, because its path is shorter
- They land together
- The horizontally launched ball, because its initial speed is greater
- The comparison depends on the horizontal speed

## Explanation

Both start at the same height with zero vertical velocity and have the same vertical acceleration. Their heights therefore agree at each instant even though their horizontal positions differ.
