The position of a bus on a straight road is $x=20+5t+0.5\,t^2$, with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. What does the term $5t$ represent?

- The position of the bus at the initial instant
- The contribution to the displacement due to the acceleration
- The velocity of the bus at time $t$
- The displacement the bus would have if it kept its initial velocity

## Explanation

Comparing with the position equation $x=x_0+v_0t+\dfrac{1}{2}at^2$ gives $x_0=20\,\mathrm{m}$ and $v_0=5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The term $v_0t=5t$ is therefore the displacement the bus would have if it kept its initial velocity. The contribution of the acceleration is the term $0.5\,t^2$, while $20$ is the initial position. The velocity at time $t$ is $v=5+t$, which differs from $5t$.
