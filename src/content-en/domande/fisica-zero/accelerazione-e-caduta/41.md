An object falls from rest for $7$ s. Neglecting air resistance and taking $g=10$ meters per second squared, what is its final speed?

- $70\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $245\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $490\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

In free fall from rest, the speed is $v=gt$. Substitute $g=10$ and $t=7$. The final speed is therefore $70$ meters per second.
