An object is thrown upwards at $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ from $15\,\mathrm{m}$ above the ground. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and no air resistance, when does it reach the ground?

- $3\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Height in metres is $15+10t-5t^2$. Setting this to zero gives roots of $-1$ and $3$ seconds. The time after launch is $3\,\mathrm{s}$.
