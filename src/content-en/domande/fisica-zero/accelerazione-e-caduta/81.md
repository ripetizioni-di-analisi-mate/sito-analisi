A soccer ball is kicked straight up with a velocity of $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Neglecting air resistance and using $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what maximum height does it reach above the launch point?

- $40\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $200\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The maximum height above the launch point is $h_{\max}=\dfrac{v_0^2}{2g}$, obtained by substituting the ascent time into the height equation. So
$$
h_{\max}=\frac{(20\,\mathrm{m/s})^2}{2\cdot10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}}=\frac{400}{20}\,\mathrm{m}=20\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
The value $40\,\mathrm{m}$ omits the factor of $2$ in the denominator. The value $2\,\mathrm{m}$ is the ascent time, $2\,\mathrm{s}$, written in meters, and $200\,\mathrm{m}$ divides by $2$ but not by $g$.
