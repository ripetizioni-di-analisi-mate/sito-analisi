An object starts from rest and reaches speed $V$ over time $T$. Which idealised velocity profile gives the largest displacement, treating very rapid changes as instantaneous?

- It increases linearly from zero to $V$
- It reaches $V$ immediately and keeps it until $T$
- It stays still for half the time and then maintains $V$
- It stays still until the last instant and then reaches $V$

## Explanation

Displacement is the area under velocity. Maintaining the maximum for the full interval gives the rectangle $VT$, larger than the alternatives.
