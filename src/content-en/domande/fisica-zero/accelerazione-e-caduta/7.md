Velocity rises linearly from $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in $4\,\mathrm{s}$. What displacement is given by the trapezium under the velocity–time graph?

::grafico{linee="0,2 4,10" poligoni="0,0 4,0 4,10 0,2" punti="0,2;4,10" xmin=0 xmax=4.8 ymin=0 ymax=12 nomi="t,v" legenda=0 titolo="Velocity in metres per second against time in seconds."}

- $40\,\mathrm{m}$
- $24\,\mathrm{m}$
- $48\,\mathrm{m}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The average velocity is the mean of the endpoint values, or $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. In $4\,\mathrm{s}$ the displacement is therefore $24\,\mathrm{m}$.
