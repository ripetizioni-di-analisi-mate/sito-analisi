Constant acceleration is measured along a track, but initial position is unknown. What can still be predicted from initial velocity and duration?

- Final distance from the origin in every case
- Displacement along the track
- Absolute final coordinate
- The height of each track end

## Explanation

The formula $\Delta x=v_i t+\dfrac{at^2}{2}$ uses only the available data. Converting displacement to a final coordinate would also require initial position.
