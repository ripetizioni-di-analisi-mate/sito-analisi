Leaving a highway toll booth, a car moves at constant velocity for $2\,\mathrm{s}$ and then accelerates, as the graph shows. What is the displacement during the $4\,\mathrm{s}$ shown?

::grafico{f="max(4,2*x)+0*sqrt(x*(4-x))" area="max(4,2*x);0;0;4" punti="0,4,pieno;2,4,pieno;4,8,pieno" segmenti="2,0,2,4;4,0,4,8" xmin=-1 xmax=5 ymin=-2 ymax=10 nomi="t,v" titolo="Velocity of the car in $\mathrm{m/s}$ and time in seconds"}

- $16\,\mathrm{m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $32\,\mathrm{m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The area under the graph splits into two parts. Between $0$ and $2\,\mathrm{s}$ there is a rectangle with area $4\,\mathrm{m/s}\cdot2\,\mathrm{s}=8\,\mathrm{m}$. Between $2$ and $4\,\mathrm{s}$ there is a trapezoid with area $\dfrac{4\,\mathrm{m/s}+8\,\mathrm{m/s}}{2}\cdot2\,\mathrm{s}=12\,\mathrm{m}$. The total displacement is therefore $8\,\mathrm{m}+12\,\mathrm{m}=20\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $16\,\mathrm{m}$ treats the velocity as $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ throughout, and $32\,\mathrm{m}$ as $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ throughout.
