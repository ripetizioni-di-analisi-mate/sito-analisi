A ball passes a mark on the way up at $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Another mark is $8\,\mathrm{m}$ higher. Does it reach the second mark, with $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and no air resistance?

- Yes, it arrives exactly at rest
- Yes, it rises another $12\,\mathrm{m}$
- No, it rises only another $7.2\,\mathrm{m}$
- Yes, it arrives at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The greatest remaining height gain is $\dfrac{12^2}{2\cdot10}=7.2$ metres. The turning point therefore lies below the second mark.
