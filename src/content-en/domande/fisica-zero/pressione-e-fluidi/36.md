A lid is held down by the difference between atmospheric pressure and the lower pressure inside a jar. A small valve connecting the inside to the outside is opened. Why is the lid easier to lift after waiting?

- Incoming air reduces the pressure difference
- The weight of the outside air disappears
- The area of the lid increases
- The mass of the lid decreases

## Explanation

Air enters until the pressures on both sides match. This removes the pressure contribution to the closing force.
