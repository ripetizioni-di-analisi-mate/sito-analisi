For the still water of a lake, with atmospheric pressure $p_0$ at the surface, the absolute pressure $p$ is graphed as a function of the depth $h$. What does the graph look like?

- An increasing straight line through the origin
- A decreasing straight line that starts at the value $p_0$ for $h=0$
- A horizontal straight line at height $p_0$
- An increasing straight line that starts at the value $p_0$ for $h=0$

## Explanation

By Stevin's law, $p=p_0+\rho gh$, with $\rho$ and $g$ constant. Hence $p$ is a linear function of $h$ with positive slope $\rho g$, and the graph is an increasing straight line. For $h=0$ we get $p=p_0$, so the line starts at the value $p_0$; the graph of the relative pressure $\rho gh$ is the one that passes through the origin.
