A tank contains $0.4\,\mathrm{m}$ of water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ beneath $0.2\,\mathrm{m}$ of oil of density $800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, how much higher is bottom pressure than the oil surface pressure?

- $5.6\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $1.6\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $2.4\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

The water layer adds $4\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and the oil layer $1.6\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Following the vertical column downwards adds these contributions.
