A body displaces $7$ liters of water with a density of $1000$ kilograms per cubic meter. With $g=10$ meters per second squared, what is the buoyant force?

- $70\,\mathrm{N}$
- $70000\,\mathrm{N}$
- $7\,\mathrm{N}$
- $700\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The buoyant force equals the weight of the displaced liquid. The volume is $0.007$ cubic meters, so $F_A=1000\cdot10\cdot0.007$. This gives $F_A=70$ newtons.
