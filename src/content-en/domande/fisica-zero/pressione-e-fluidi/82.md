A pure ice cube floats in fresh water and melts completely. Neglect evaporation and changes in the density of liquid water. What happens to the water level?

- It falls by the initially exposed volume divided by the container area
- It rises by the full original volume of ice divided by the container area
- It rises by the initially exposed volume divided by the container area
- It stays at the same height

## Explanation

Before melting, the ice displaces water equal to its own mass. The meltwater has the same mass and density as the previously displaced water and therefore occupies exactly that volume.
