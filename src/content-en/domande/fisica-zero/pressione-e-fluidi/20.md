At the zoo, an elephant weighing $50000\,\mathrm{N}$ stands still on all four legs. Each foot touches the ground over an area of $0.125\,\mathrm{m^2}$. What is the average pressure under the feet?

- $400000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $100000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $25000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $12500\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The weight is distributed over the four feet, that is, over a total area of $4\cdot0.125\,\mathrm{m^2}=0.5\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The average pressure is the ratio of the weight to this area. Therefore, $p=\dfrac{50000}{0.5}\,\mathrm{Pa}=100000\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
