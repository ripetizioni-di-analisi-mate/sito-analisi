A sample has a density of $1.3\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. Which value expresses the same density for masses in kilograms and volumes in cubic metres?

- $1.3\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $0.0013\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $130\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $1300\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

One gram is $10^{-3}$ kilograms, while one cubic centimetre is $10^{-6}$ cubic metres. The ratio of the conversion factors is one thousand.
