A liquid sample has a mass of $0.48\,\mathrm{kg}$ and a volume of $0.60\,\mathrm{L}$. What is its density in SI units?

- $800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $0.8\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $80\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $1250\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

The volume is $0.00060\,\mathrm{m^3}$. Density is $\rho=\dfrac{0.48}{0.00060}=800$ kilograms per cubic metre.
