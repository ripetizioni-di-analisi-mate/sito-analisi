A confined liquid connects three identical pistons at the same height. A pump maintains a gauge pressure of $6\,\mathrm{kPa}$. What gauge pressure reaches each piston at equilibrium?

- $18\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $2\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

Static pressure is shared at the same elevation. The number of pistons affects the total area on which a force is exerted, while the pressure stays at $6\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
