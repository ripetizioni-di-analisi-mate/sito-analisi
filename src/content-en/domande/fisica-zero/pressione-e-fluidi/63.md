In an ideal hydraulic press with the pistons at the same height, the large piston supports a load much greater than the force applied to the small piston. Which quantity has the same value under the two pistons?

- The force exerted by the liquid
- The displacement during operation
- The area of the contact surface
- The pressure increase in the liquid

## Explanation

By Pascal's principle, a pressure increase applied to the confined liquid is transmitted to every part of it, so the pressure increase is the same under both pistons. The forces are this pressure times the areas, so they are different. The displacements also differ, because the small piston moves through a larger distance.
