A glass marble is completely immersed, in turn, in four liquids at rest: alcohol ($790\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$), olive oil ($920\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$), water ($1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$), and seawater ($1030\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$). In which liquid is the buoyant force on the marble greatest?

- In the alcohol
- In the olive oil
- In the seawater
- The force is the same in all of them, because the marble is the same

## Explanation

The buoyant force is $F_A=\rho_f gV_{\mathrm{imm}}$. The marble is always completely submerged, so $V_{\mathrm{imm}}$ is its volume in every case, and $g$ is also the same. Therefore, the buoyant force is greatest in the densest liquid, that is, in the seawater.
