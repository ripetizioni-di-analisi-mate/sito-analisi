A vertical cylinder of constant cross-section is lowered slowly into water, from first contact until fully submerged and then deeper. How does buoyancy vary with the lowering distance if the cylinder never touches the vessel?

- It is constant from first contact
- It rises linearly until full immersion and then stays constant
- It keeps rising linearly after full immersion
- It falls as the cylinder enters the water

## Explanation

During entry the submerged volume grows as base area times submerged height. After full immersion it stays equal to the cylinder's volume.
