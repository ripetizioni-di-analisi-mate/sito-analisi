The same buoy floats in the same liquid on two planets. Gravity on the second is half that on the first. Assuming unchanged densities and shape, how do buoyancy in newtons and the submerged fraction change?

- Buoyancy halves and the submerged fraction stays the same
- Both halve
- Buoyancy stays the same and the submerged fraction doubles
- Both stay the same

## Explanation

In the equilibrium condition $\rho_{\ell}gV_{\rm sub}=mg$, gravity cancels, so the submerged volume stays the same. Weight in newtons and hence buoyancy both halve.
