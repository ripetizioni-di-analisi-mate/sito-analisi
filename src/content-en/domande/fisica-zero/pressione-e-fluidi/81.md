A wrench with a mass of $0.5\,\mathrm{kg}$ and a volume of $0.0001\,\mathrm{m^3}$ hangs at rest from a spring scale, completely submerged in water with a density of $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and not touching the bottom. With $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what does the scale read?

- $5.886\,\mathrm{N}$
- $3.924\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4.905\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.981\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The weight of the wrench is $mg=0.5\cdot9.81\,\mathrm{N}=4.905\,\mathrm{N}$. The wrench is completely submerged, so the buoyant force is $F_A=1000\cdot9.81\cdot0.0001\,\mathrm{N}=0.981\,\mathrm{N}$. The scale reads the apparent weight $T=mg-F_A$, so $T=4.905\,\mathrm{N}-0.981\,\mathrm{N}=3.924\,\mathrm{N}$.
