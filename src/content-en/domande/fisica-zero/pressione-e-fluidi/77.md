A water tank rests on a scale. An object hung from a separate stand is immersed without touching the tank or causing overflow. Its buoyancy is $4\,\mathrm{N}$. By how much does the scale's force reading increase?

- It depends only on the object's total weight
- $0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $8\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The water pushes the object up with $4\,\mathrm{N}$. The object pushes the water down with an equal $4\,\mathrm{N}$ reaction, which reaches the scale.
