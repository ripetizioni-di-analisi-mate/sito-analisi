An ice cube with a density of $920\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ floats at rest in a glass of water with a density of $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. What percentage of the cube's volume is underwater?

- $8\%$
- $9.2\%$
- $92\%$
- $108.7\%$

## Explanation

For a homogeneous body floating at rest, $\dfrac{V_{\mathrm{imm}}}{V}=\dfrac{\rho_c}{\rho_f}$. Hence $\dfrac{V_{\mathrm{imm}}}{V}=\dfrac{920}{1000}=0.92$. Therefore, $92\%$ of the cube's volume is underwater and only $8\%$ sticks out.
