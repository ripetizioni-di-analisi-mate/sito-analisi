The formulas for liquids in static equilibrium hold for a liquid of uniform density in which every part is at rest. Which of these situations do they apply to?

- The water coming out of a garden hose
- The water in an aquarium with the pump switched off
- The water in a waterfall
- The water in a flooding river

## Explanation

A fluid is in static equilibrium when every part of it is at rest in the chosen frame of reference. In the aquarium with the pump off, the water is at rest, so the formulas for liquids in equilibrium apply. In the garden hose, the waterfall, and the river, the water is flowing, so these formulas do not necessarily describe those situations.
