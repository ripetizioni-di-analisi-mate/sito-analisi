An open tank contains still water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. Atmospheric pressure is $100\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the absolute pressure at a depth of $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $8\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $108\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $80\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $180\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

The water column adds $1000\cdot10\cdot0.80=8000$ pascals, or $8\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Absolute pressure also includes the $100\,\mathrm{kPa}$ at the surface.
