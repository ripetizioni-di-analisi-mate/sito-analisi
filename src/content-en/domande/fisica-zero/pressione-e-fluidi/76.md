A buoy at rest is tilted and released. At that instant its weight and buoyancy have equal magnitudes and act along two different vertical lines. What initial effect do they produce?

- Upward translation without rotation
- No effect because the two forces cancel in every respect
- Downward translation without rotation
- Rotation without acceleration of the centre of mass

## Explanation

The net force is zero. The lines of action are separated, so the forces form a couple with a nonzero torque that can rotate the buoy.
