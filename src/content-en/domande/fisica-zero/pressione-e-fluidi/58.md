In an ideal press without friction or significant height changes the small piston receives $20\,\mathrm{J}$ of work. The large piston exerts ten times the force. How much work can it deliver to the load?

- $20\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $2\,\mathrm{J}$
- $200\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Ten times the force comes with one tenth of the travel. The product of force and displacement remains $20\,\mathrm{J}$.
