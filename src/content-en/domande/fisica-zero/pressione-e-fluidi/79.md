A fresh egg placed in a glass of tap water sinks to the bottom. If a lot of salt is dissolved in the water, the same egg rises and floats. Which statement explains this?

- Salt water is denser than the egg, so when fully submerged the buoyant force exceeds the weight
- The salt reduces the mass of the egg
- The salt increases the volume of the egg
- In salt water the buoyant force points downward

## Explanation

The weight and volume of the egg do not change. In tap water the egg is denser than the liquid, so its weight exceeds the buoyant force even when fully submerged, and the egg sinks. Dissolving salt raises the density of the liquid above that of the egg, so the buoyant force $\rho_f gV$ at full immersion becomes greater than the weight, and the egg rises until it floats.
