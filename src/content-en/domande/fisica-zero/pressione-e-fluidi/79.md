A closed hull of mass $12\,\mathrm{kg}$ can displace at most $20\,\mathrm{L}$ of water before becoming completely submerged. At a water density of $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$, what cargo mass takes it exactly to that limit?

- $8\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $32\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The $20\,\mathrm{L}$ of displaced water can support a total mass of $20\,\mathrm{kg}$. Subtracting the hull mass leaves $8\,\mathrm{kg}$ for cargo.
