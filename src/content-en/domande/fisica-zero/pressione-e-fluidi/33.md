A gauge referred to the outside air reads $-25\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Local atmospheric pressure is $100\,\mathrm{kPa}$. What is the absolute pressure in the vessel?

- $75\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $125\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $25\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $-25\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

A negative gauge reading indicates pressure below the atmosphere. The sum $100+(-25)$ gives an absolute pressure of $75\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
