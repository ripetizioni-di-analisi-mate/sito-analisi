The bottom of a swimming pool is $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$ below the surface of the water, which has a density of $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. With $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the relative pressure on the bottom with respect to the surface?

- $2500\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $124525\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $24525\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $2452.5\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

By Stevin's law, the pressure increase relative to the surface is $\rho gh$. Hence $p_{\mathrm{rel}}=1000\cdot9.81\cdot2.5\,\mathrm{Pa}=24525\,\mathrm{Pa}$. The atmospheric pressure at the surface is not added, because the question asks for the relative pressure.
