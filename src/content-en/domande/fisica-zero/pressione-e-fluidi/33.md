A body weighs $60$ N in air and experiences an upward buoyant force of $18$ N in a liquid. What is its apparent weight?

- $42\,\mathrm{N}$
- $78\,\mathrm{N}$
- $18\,\mathrm{N}$
- $60\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

For a body supported in equilibrium, the apparent weight equals the weight minus the buoyant force. Subtract $18$ from $60$. The apparent weight is therefore $42$ newtons.
