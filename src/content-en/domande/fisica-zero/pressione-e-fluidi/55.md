Two points at different elevations in a confined liquid initially have pressures of $105\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and $112\,\mathrm{kPa}$. A piston adds $8\,\mathrm{kPa}$ without appreciably changing elevations or density. What is their final pressure difference?

- $23\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $15\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $7\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

The increase is transmitted to both points. The pressures become $113\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and $120\,\mathrm{kPa}$, keeping the original $7\,\mathrm{kPa}$ difference.
