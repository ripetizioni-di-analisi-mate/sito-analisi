Underwater in a bucket there is a brick held up by a string, with its two largest faces horizontal. On which face does the water exert the greatest pressure force?

- On the top face, with a downward force
- On the bottom face, with an upward force
- On the two largest faces, with forces of equal magnitude
- On the side faces, with upward forces

## Explanation

The two largest faces have the same area. The bottom face is deeper, so by Stevin's law the pressure on it is greater. Therefore, the force on the bottom face, which points upward, exceeds the force on the top face, and the difference between the two is the buoyant force. The forces on the side faces are horizontal.
