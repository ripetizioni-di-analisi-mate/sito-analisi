Two uniform bodies of equal mass are fully immersed in the same liquid. A is twice as dense as B. What is the buoyancy on A relative to B?

- Half as large
- One quarter as large
- The same
- Twice as large

## Explanation

At equal mass the denser body has half the volume. Fully immersed, A therefore displaces half as much liquid as B.
