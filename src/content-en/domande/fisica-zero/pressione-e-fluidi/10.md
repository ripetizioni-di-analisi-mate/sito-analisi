An ideal press has pistons of areas $5\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and $50\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ at the same height. External pressures are equal and piston weights and friction are negligible. What force on the small piston balances $700\,\mathrm{N}$ on the large one?

- $70\,\mathrm{N}$
- $7000\,\mathrm{N}$
- $350\,\mathrm{N}$
- $140\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The added pressure is the same at both pistons. The small piston requires one tenth of the force because it has one tenth of the area.
