During a step, a perpendicular force of $300\,\mathrm{N}$ acts on the stiletto heel of a shoe, which touches the floor over an area of $1\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. What is the average pressure of the heel on the floor?

- $3\cdot10^{2}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $3\cdot10^{4}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $3\cdot10^{8}\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $3\cdot10^{6}\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

One square centimeter equals $0.0001\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The average pressure is the ratio of the perpendicular force to the area, so $p=\dfrac{300}{0.0001}\,\mathrm{Pa}=3000000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. In scientific notation the pressure is $3\cdot10^{6}\,\mathrm{Pa}$; the very small area makes the pressure very large even with a moderate force.
