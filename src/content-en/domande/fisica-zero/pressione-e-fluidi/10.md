A perpendicular force of $80$ N acts on an area of $4$ square meters. What is the average pressure?

- $320\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $20\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $80\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $0.8\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The average pressure is $p=\dfrac{F_\perp}{A}$. Divide the force by the area over which it acts. Therefore, $p=\dfrac{80}{4}=20$ pascals.
