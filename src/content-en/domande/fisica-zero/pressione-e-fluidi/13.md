A uniform block of density $750\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ floats freely in water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. Neglecting air buoyancy, what percentage of its volume is submerged?

- $25\%$
- $75\%$
- $100\%$
- $50\%$

## Explanation

The block weighs as much as the displaced water. The submerged fraction is $\dfrac{750}{1000}=0.75$.
