The surface pressure is $100000$ Pa and the pressure due to the liquid is $4000$ Pa. What is the absolute pressure?

- $104000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $4000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $96000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $100000\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The absolute pressure is the sum of the surface pressure and the increase due to the liquid. Calculate $100000+4000$. The absolute pressure is therefore $104000$ pascals.
