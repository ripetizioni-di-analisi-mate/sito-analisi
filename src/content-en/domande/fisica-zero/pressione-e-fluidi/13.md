A brick measuring $24\,\mathrm{cm}\times12\,\mathrm{cm}\times6\,\mathrm{cm}$ can be placed on a table on any of its three different faces. In which position is the average pressure on the table greatest?

- Resting on the $24\,\mathrm{cm}\times12\,\mathrm{cm}$ face
- Resting on the $12\,\mathrm{cm}\times6\,\mathrm{cm}$ face
- Resting on the $24\,\mathrm{cm}\times6\,\mathrm{cm}$ face
- The pressure is the same in all three positions

## Explanation

In every position, the force on the table is the weight of the brick, which does not change. Since $p=\dfrac{F}{A}$, the pressure is greatest when the contact area is smallest. The three faces have areas of $288\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, $144\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, and $72\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, so the greatest pressure occurs when the brick rests on the $12\,\mathrm{cm}\times6\,\mathrm{cm}$ face.
