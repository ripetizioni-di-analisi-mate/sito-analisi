The surface pressure is $100000$ Pa and the pressure due to the liquid is $3000$ Pa. What is the absolute pressure?

- $3000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $97000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $100000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $103000\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The absolute pressure is the sum of the surface pressure and the increase due to the liquid. Calculate $100000+3000$. The absolute pressure is therefore $103000$ pascals.
