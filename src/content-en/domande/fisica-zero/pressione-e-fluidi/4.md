A can contains $3\,\mathrm{L}$ of a uniform liquid of density $900\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. What is the mass of the liquid alone?

- $2700\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.3\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $3.9\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $2.7\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The volume is $0.003\,\mathrm{m^3}$. From $m=\rho V$, the mass is $900\cdot0.003=2.7$ kilograms.
