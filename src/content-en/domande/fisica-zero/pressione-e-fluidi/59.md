A car is raised with a hydraulic jack. The piston that is pushed has a much smaller area than the piston that supports the car. How do the displacements of the two pistons compare during the lift?

- The small piston moves through a larger distance than the large one
- Both pistons move through the same distance
- The large piston moves through a larger distance than the small one
- The small piston stays still and only the large one moves

## Explanation

The liquid keeps its volume, so the volume that leaves the small cylinder enters the large cylinder. The same volume corresponds to a longer distance in a cylinder of smaller area. Therefore, the small piston moves through a larger distance; this is why the increase in force is consistent with conservation of energy.
