The circular pistons of an ideal press have diameters of $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $6\,\mathrm{cm}$. At equal elevation and external pressure, by what factor is force amplified from the smaller to the larger piston?

- 9
- 27
- 6
- 3

## Explanation

The force ratio equals the area ratio. Circle area is proportional to diameter squared, so the factor is $3^2=9$.
