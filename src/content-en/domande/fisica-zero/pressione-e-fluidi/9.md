A lead ball and an aluminum ball have the same mass, $100\,\mathrm{g}$. Lead has a density of $11300\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and aluminum $2700\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. Which statement about the volumes of the two balls is true?

- The aluminum ball has the larger volume
- The lead ball has the larger volume
- The two balls have the same volume
- The volumes cannot be compared without knowing the diameters

## Explanation

From the definition of density, $V=\dfrac{m}{\rho}$. The two balls have the same mass, so the one made of the less dense material has the larger volume. Since aluminum is less dense than lead, the aluminum ball has the larger volume.
