An open tank has a horizontal base of area $0.050\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and contains water to a depth of $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$. With density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what force on the base is due to the liquid over and above atmospheric pressure?

- $150\,\mathrm{N}$
- $600\,\mathrm{N}$
- $3000\,\mathrm{N}$
- $15\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The excess pressure is $1000\cdot10\cdot0.30=3000$ pascals and is uniform across the base. Multiplication by $0.050\,\mathrm{m^2}$ gives $150\,\mathrm{N}$.
