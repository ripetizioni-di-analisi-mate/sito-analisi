A kitchen tabletop measures $1.2\,\mathrm{m}$ by $0.8\,\mathrm{m}$. The air above it is at a pressure of $100000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. What is the magnitude of the force that the air exerts on the top face of the tabletop?

- $9600\,\mathrm{N}$
- $960\,\mathrm{N}$
- $960000\,\mathrm{N}$
- $96000\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The area of the tabletop is $1.2\cdot0.8\,\mathrm{m^2}=0.96\,\mathrm{m^2}$. For uniform pressure, the force is $F=pA$. Therefore, $F=100000\cdot0.96\,\mathrm{N}=96000\,\mathrm{N}$, perpendicular to the tabletop, that is, downward.
