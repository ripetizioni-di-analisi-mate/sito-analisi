A support foot must carry $800\,\mathrm{N}$ while keeping the mean pressure at or below $20\,\mathrm{kPa}$. What is the smallest permissible contact area?

- $0.004\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $0.016\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $0.040\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $0.40\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

The area must satisfy $A\geq\dfrac{F}{p_{\max}}$. The lower bound is $\dfrac{800}{20000}=0.040$ square metres.
