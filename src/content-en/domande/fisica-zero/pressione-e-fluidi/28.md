A perpendicular force of $120$ N acts on an area of $4$ square meters. What is the average pressure?

- $480\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $120\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $30\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The average pressure is $p=\dfrac{F_\perp}{A}$. Divide the force by the area over which it acts. Therefore, $p=\dfrac{120}{4}=30$ pascals.
