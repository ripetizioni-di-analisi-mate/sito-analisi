A uniform pressure of $6$ Pa acts on an area of $3$ square meters. What is the magnitude of the perpendicular force?

- $18\,\mathrm{N}$
- $2\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $9\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The definition of pressure gives $F=pA$. Multiply the pressure by the area. The magnitude of the force is therefore $18$ newtons.
