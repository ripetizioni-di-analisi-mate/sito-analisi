One $2\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ region of a plate is uniformly pressed at $10\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and a second $6\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ region at $30\,\mathrm{kPa}$. What is the mean pressure over the whole plate?

- $40\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $25\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $15\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

The forces on the two regions add. Dividing by total area gives the weighted mean $\dfrac{10\cdot2+30\cdot6}{8}=25$ kilopascals.
