The air exerts a downward force of tens of thousands of newtons on the top face of a tabletop, and the table stays intact. Which statement explains this?

- Air pressure acts only on moving bodies
- The air also presses on the bottom face of the tabletop, with an almost equal upward force
- The force of the air is parallel to the tabletop
- Atmospheric pressure is zero in a closed room

## Explanation

The air surrounds the tabletop and presses on all of its faces. On the bottom face the pressure is practically the same as on top, and the force is perpendicular to the tabletop and points upward. Therefore, the two forces have almost equal magnitudes and opposite directions, and their resultant is very small.
