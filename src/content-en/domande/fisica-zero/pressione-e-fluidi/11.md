A skier weighing $720\,\mathrm{N}$ stands still on level ground on two skis. Each ski rests on the snow over an area of $0.18\,\mathrm{m^2}$. What is the average pressure on the snow?

- $4000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $2000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $259.2\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The skier is at rest on level ground, so the perpendicular force on the snow equals his weight. This force is distributed over both skis, that is, over a total area of $2\cdot0.18\,\mathrm{m^2}=0.36\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Therefore, $p=\dfrac{720}{0.36}\,\mathrm{Pa}=2000\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
