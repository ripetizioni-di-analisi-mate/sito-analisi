A uniform pressure of $4$ Pa acts on an area of $3$ square meters. What is the magnitude of the perpendicular force?

- $\dfrac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.75\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$
- $7\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The definition of pressure gives $F=pA$. Multiply the pressure by the area. The magnitude of the force is therefore $12$ newtons.
