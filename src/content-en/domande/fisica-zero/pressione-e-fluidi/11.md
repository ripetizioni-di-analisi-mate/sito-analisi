An object displaces $0.0015\,\mathrm{m^3}$ of still water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what buoyant force does it experience?

- $150\,\mathrm{N}$ upwards
- $15\,\mathrm{N}$ upwards
- $1.5\,\mathrm{N}$ upwards
- $15\,\mathrm{N}$ downwards

## Explanation

The displaced water has mass $1.5\,\mathrm{kg}$. The buoyant force equals its weight in magnitude, $15\,\mathrm{N}$, and acts upwards.
