An ideal barometer has a nearly perfect vacuum above its column. At the same atmospheric pressure and gravity, how tall would the column be with a liquid of twice the density?

- Four times as tall
- Twice as tall
- The same height
- Half as tall

## Explanation

Equilibrium requires $p_{\rm atm}=\rho gh$. When density doubles, height must halve to keep this product unchanged.
