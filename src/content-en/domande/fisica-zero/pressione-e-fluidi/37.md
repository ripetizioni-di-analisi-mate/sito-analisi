A perpendicular force of $140$ N acts on an area of $4$ square meters. What is the average pressure?

- $35\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $560\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $140\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $1.4\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The average pressure is $p=\dfrac{F_\perp}{A}$. Divide the force by the area over which it acts. Therefore, $p=\dfrac{140}{4}=35$ pascals.
