A diver is $10\,\mathrm{m}$ deep in the sea. Seawater has a density of $1030\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$, and the atmospheric pressure at the surface is $100000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. With $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the absolute pressure at the diver's depth?

- $101043\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $201043\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $110300\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $100000\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

By Stevin's law, $p=p_0+\rho gh$. The increase due to the water is $\rho gh=1030\cdot9.81\cdot10\,\mathrm{Pa}=101043\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Therefore, $p=100000\,\mathrm{Pa}+101043\,\mathrm{Pa}=201043\,\mathrm{Pa}$, about twice the atmospheric pressure.
