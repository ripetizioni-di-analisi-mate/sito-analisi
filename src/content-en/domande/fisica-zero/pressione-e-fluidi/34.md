A capsule maintains constant absolute pressure. As it is transported, atmospheric pressure falls by $8\,\mathrm{kPa}$. How does a gauge referred to the surrounding air change?

- It increases by $8\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- It always halves
- It stays constant
- It decreases by $8\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

The gauge reads $p_{\rm int}-p_{\rm atm}$. For fixed internal pressure, subtracting a smaller atmospheric value increases the difference.
