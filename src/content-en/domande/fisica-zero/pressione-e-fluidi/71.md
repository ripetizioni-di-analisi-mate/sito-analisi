A suction cup seals against the bottom of a tank. Water does not reach its contact face. Why is its outer volume insufficient to find the resultant water force on the cup?

- The base cancels water density
- Gravity changes sign at the base
- Water pressure acts only on the wetted surfaces
- All water forces become horizontal

## Explanation

The usual fully submerged buoyancy result assumes fluid pressure around the body. Here there is no water pressure on the sealed face.
