A stone hangs from a string, completely submerged in a bucket of water without touching the bottom. The buoyant force on the stone is $2.943\,\mathrm{N}$. With $\rho_f=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the volume of the stone?

- $0.3\,\mathrm{L}$
- $3\,\mathrm{L}$
- $2.943\,\mathrm{L}$
- $0.03\,\mathrm{L}$

## Explanation

The stone is completely submerged, so the immersed volume equals its volume. From $F_A=\rho_f gV$ we get $V=\dfrac{F_A}{\rho_f g}=\dfrac{2.943}{9810}\,\mathrm{m^3}=0.0003\,\mathrm{m^3}$. Since one cubic meter contains $1000$ liters, the stone has a volume of $0.3\,\mathrm{L}$.
