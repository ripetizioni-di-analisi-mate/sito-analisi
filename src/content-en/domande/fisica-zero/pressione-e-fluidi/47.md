A watering can has a body $32\,\mathrm{cm}$ tall and a spout, connected near the bottom, whose opening is $25\,\mathrm{cm}$ above the bottom. Holding the can upright, up to what height can it be filled with water without water coming out of the spout?

- $25\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $32\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $28.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $7\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The body of the watering can and the spout are communicating vessels containing the same water, and atmospheric pressure acts on both free surfaces. Therefore, in equilibrium the water reaches the same level in the body and in the spout. The spout ends $25\,\mathrm{cm}$ above the bottom, so above this level water comes out of the spout, even though the body is $32\,\mathrm{cm}$ tall.
