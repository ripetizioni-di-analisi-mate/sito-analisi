In an open tank the surface pressure is $100\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and pressure rises by $10\,\mathrm{kPa}$ per metre of depth. The probe shows absolute pressure, but its display stays at $120\,\mathrm{kPa}$ for every value above $120\,\mathrm{kPa}$. At which two positions will it show the same reading?

- At $1\,\mathrm{m}$ and $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- At the surface and $1\,\mathrm{m}$
- At $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- At the surface and $2\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

At $2\,\mathrm{m}$ the actual pressure reaches the instrument limit of $120\,\mathrm{kPa}$. At $3\,\mathrm{m}$ the actual pressure is $130\,\mathrm{kPa}$, but the display remains at $120\,\mathrm{kPa}$. At the two shallower points the readings are $100\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and $110\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
