The surface pressure is $100000$ Pa and the pressure due to the liquid is $5000$ Pa. What is the absolute pressure?

- $5000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $105000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $95000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $100000\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The absolute pressure is the sum of the surface pressure and the increase due to the liquid. Calculate $100000+5000$. The absolute pressure is therefore $105000$ pascals.
