A rectangular aquarium is full of still water. What are the direction and sense of the force that the water exerts on one of the vertical glass walls?

- Horizontal, perpendicular to the glass, and pointing outward
- Vertical and pointing downward
- Parallel to the glass and pointing downward
- Horizontal, perpendicular to the glass, and pointing inward

## Explanation

A fluid in equilibrium exerts a force on a wall perpendicular to that wall. The wall is vertical, so the force is horizontal. The water presses on the glass from inside the aquarium, so the force points outward.
