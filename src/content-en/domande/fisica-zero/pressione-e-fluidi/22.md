The walls of a closed box are made of material of density $2400\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and occupy one quarter of its outer volume. The mass of the air inside is negligible. What is the average density of the whole box?

- $2400\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $1800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $600\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $9600\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

For outer volume $V$, the walls occupy $\dfrac V4$ and have mass $2400\dfrac V4$ in SI units. Dividing by $V$ gives $600\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.
