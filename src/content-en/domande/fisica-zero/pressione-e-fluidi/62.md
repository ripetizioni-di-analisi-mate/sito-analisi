A workshop press has two circular pistons at the same height, with diameters of $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $10\,\mathrm{cm}$. A force of $80\,\mathrm{N}$ is applied to the small piston. Neglecting friction and the weights of the pistons, what force can be balanced on the large piston?

- $2000\,\mathrm{N}$
- $400\,\mathrm{N}$
- $3.2\,\mathrm{N}$
- $80\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The area of a circle of diameter $d$ is $\pi\dfrac{d^2}{4}$, so the areas are in the ratio of the squares of the diameters. The large diameter is $5$ times the small one, so the large area is $5^2=25$ times the small area. By Pascal's principle, $F_2=F_1\dfrac{A_2}{A_1}=80\cdot25\,\mathrm{N}=2000\,\mathrm{N}$.
