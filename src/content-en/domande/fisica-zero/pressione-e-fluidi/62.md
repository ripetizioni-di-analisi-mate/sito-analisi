An ideal press requires $150\,\mathrm{kPa}$ gauge to lift a load. The hose is rated to $200\,\mathrm{kPa}$ gauge and the seal to $120\,\mathrm{kPa}$ gauge. What follows?

- Both components are within their ratings
- The required pressure exceeds the seal rating
- Only the hose rating is exceeded
- The two pressure ratings add

## Explanation

The added pressure reaches each component. The required $150\,\mathrm{kPa}$ exceed $120\,\mathrm{kPa}$ even though they are below the hose rating.
