Two open tanks have the same water depth and horizontal bases of areas $A$ and $3A$. How does the force due to excess hydrostatic pressure on the second base compare?

- It is three times as large
- It is nine times as large
- It is one third as large
- It is the same

## Explanation

Equal depth gives equal gauge pressure. The product $p_{\rm rel}A$ is three times larger over three times the area.
