A uniform pressure of $7$ Pa acts on an area of $3$ square meters. What is the magnitude of the perpendicular force?

- $\dfrac{7}{3}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $21\,\mathrm{N}$
- $\dfrac{3}{7}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The definition of pressure gives $F=pA$. Multiply the pressure by the area. The magnitude of the force is therefore $21$ newtons.
