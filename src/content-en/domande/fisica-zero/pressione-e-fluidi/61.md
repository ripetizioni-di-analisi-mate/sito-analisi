A horizontal piston of area $0.020\,\mathrm{m^2}$ weighs $100\,\mathrm{N}$ and supports a further load of $300\,\mathrm{N}$. Atmospheric pressure acts above. What gauge pressure beneath balances it without friction?

- $10\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $5\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $15\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

Gauge pressure supports both the piston and the load. The required force is $400\,\mathrm{N}$, giving $p=\dfrac{400}{0.020}=20000$ pascals.
