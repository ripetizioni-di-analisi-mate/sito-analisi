A perpendicular force of $100$ N acts on an area of $4$ square meters. What is the average pressure?

- $400\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $100\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $25\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $1\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The average pressure is $p=\dfrac{F_\perp}{A}$. Divide the force by the area over which it acts. Therefore, $p=\dfrac{100}{4}=25$ pascals.
