A parcel always exerts the same perpendicular force of $60\,\mathrm{N}$ on a shelf, while its contact area $A$ changes because different bases are used. Which graph describes the average pressure $p$ as a function of $A$?

- A curve that falls as $A$ increases and approaches the $A$ axis without touching it
- A straight line rising through the origin
- A horizontal straight line
- A straight line that falls and crosses the $A$ axis

## Explanation

The average pressure is $p=\dfrac{60}{A}$, with $A$ in square meters and $p$ in pascals. When $A$ doubles, $p$ halves, so the pressure decreases as the area increases. Since $p$ stays positive for every area, the graph never crosses the $A$ axis and gets closer and closer to it; it is a branch of a hyperbola.
