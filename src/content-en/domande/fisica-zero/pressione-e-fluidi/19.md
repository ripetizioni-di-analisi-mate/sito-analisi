A stone is completely submerged without trapped bubbles in a measuring cylinder. The reading increases from $45\,\mathrm{mL}$ to $62\,\mathrm{mL}$ without overflow. What volume should be used to calculate the stone's density?

- $62\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $17\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $107\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $45\,\mathrm{cm^3}$

## Explanation

The increase in the reading is the displaced volume, $62-45=17$ millilitres. Each millilitre equals one cubic centimetre.
