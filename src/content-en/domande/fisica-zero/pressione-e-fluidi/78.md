A rowboat with two people on board has a total mass of $300\,\mathrm{kg}$ and floats at rest on a freshwater lake with a density of $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. What is the volume of the immersed part of the boat?

- $0.3\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $0.03\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $2.943\,\mathrm{m^3}$

## Explanation

The boat floats at rest, so the buoyant force equals the weight, $\rho_f gV_{\mathrm{imm}}=mg$. Canceling $g$ gives $V_{\mathrm{imm}}=\dfrac{m}{\rho_f}$. Therefore, $V_{\mathrm{imm}}=\dfrac{300}{1000}\,\mathrm{m^3}=0.3\,\mathrm{m^3}$.
