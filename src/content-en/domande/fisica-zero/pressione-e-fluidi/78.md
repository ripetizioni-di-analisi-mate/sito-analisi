A barge with vertical sides has a waterline area of $4\,\mathrm{m^2}$. An extra $200\,\mathrm{kg}$ is loaded. How much farther does it sink in water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ while remaining afloat?

- $20\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $50\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The additional displaced volume is $\dfrac{200}{1000}=0.20$ cubic metres. Dividing by $4\,\mathrm{m^2}$ gives a downward displacement of $0.05\,\mathrm{m}$.
