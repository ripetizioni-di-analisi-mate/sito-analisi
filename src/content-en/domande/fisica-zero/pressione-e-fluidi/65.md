A class wants to test whether added pressure is transmitted far from a piston. Which comparison will test this in a confined liquid at rest?

- Measure only the horizontal separation of the probes
- Measure the total liquid mass once
- Compare before-and-after increases on two fixed probes, one near and one far
- Compare only the piston areas

## Explanation

Subtract each probe's initial reading from its final reading. Comparing these changes isolates the applied excess pressure even if the original pressures differ.
