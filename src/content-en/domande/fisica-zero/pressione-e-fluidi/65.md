A child holds a soccer ball completely underwater in a swimming pool. The ball has a volume of $5\,\mathrm{L}$, and the water has a density of $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. With $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the buoyant force on the ball?

- $5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4.905\,\mathrm{N}$
- $49.05\,\mathrm{N}$
- $49050\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The ball is completely submerged, so the immersed volume is its full volume, $5\,\mathrm{L}=0.005\,\mathrm{m^3}$. The buoyant force is $F_A=\rho_f gV_{\mathrm{imm}}$. Therefore, $F_A=1000\cdot9.81\cdot0.005\,\mathrm{N}=49.05\,\mathrm{N}$, directed upward.
