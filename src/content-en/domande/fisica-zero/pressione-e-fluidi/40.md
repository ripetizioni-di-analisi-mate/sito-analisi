A probe reads $94\,\mathrm{kPa}$ absolute at $1\,\mathrm{m}$ and $112\,\mathrm{kPa}$ absolute at $3\,\mathrm{m}$ depth in the same still uniform liquid. What is the surface pressure?

- $76\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $103\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $85\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $94\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

The pressure rises $18\,\mathrm{kPa}$ across $2\,\mathrm{m}$, giving $9\,\mathrm{kPa/m}$. Moving up from the first point to the surface subtracts $9\,\mathrm{kPa}$ from $94\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
