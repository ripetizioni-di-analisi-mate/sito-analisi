The surface pressure is $100000$ Pa and the pressure due to the liquid is $7000$ Pa. What is the absolute pressure?

- $7000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $93000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $100000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $107000\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The absolute pressure is the sum of the surface pressure and the increase due to the liquid. Calculate $100000+7000$. The absolute pressure is therefore $107000$ pascals.
