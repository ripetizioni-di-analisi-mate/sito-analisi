A container is filled with water to the brim. An object hanging from a string is completely immersed in it, and $150\,\mathrm{g}$ of water overflow into a saucer. With $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the buoyant force on the object?

- $1.47\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.15\,\mathrm{N}$
- $14.7\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1471.5\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The water that overflowed is the water displaced by the object. The magnitude of the buoyant force equals the weight of the displaced fluid. Therefore, $F_A=0.150\cdot9.81\,\mathrm{N}\approx1.47\,\mathrm{N}$.
