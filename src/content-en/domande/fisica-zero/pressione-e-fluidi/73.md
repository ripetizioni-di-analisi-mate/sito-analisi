A rigid object already fully submerged is lowered by $1\,\mathrm{m}$ in a uniform liquid. What happens to the pressure at a point on its surface and to its total buoyancy?

- Pressure increases and buoyancy stays the same
- Both increase
- Pressure stays the same and buoyancy increases
- Both decrease

## Explanation

All pressures increase by the same hydrostatic increment. Displaced volume and liquid density stay fixed, so buoyancy is unchanged.
