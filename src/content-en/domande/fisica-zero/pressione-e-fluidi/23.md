Three litres of a liquid of density $500\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ are mixed with $1\,\mathrm{L}$ of a liquid of density $1100\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$, with no volume change on mixing. What is the final density?

- $1600\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $500\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $650\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

The two masses are $1.5\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $1.1\,\mathrm{kg}$. Dividing their total of $2.6\,\mathrm{kg}$ by the combined volume of $0.004\,\mathrm{m^3}$ gives $650\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.
