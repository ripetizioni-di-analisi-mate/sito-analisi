A small pressure sensor is held still at one point in a swimming pool, at a depth of $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$. It is rotated so that its membrane faces first upward, then sideways, and finally downward. How does the measured pressure change?

- It is greatest with the membrane facing upward
- It is greatest with the membrane facing downward
- It stays the same in all three orientations
- It is zero with the membrane facing sideways

## Explanation

At a given point in a fluid in equilibrium, the pressure has the same value in all directions. The sensor stays at the same point, and only the orientation of the membrane changes. Therefore, the measured pressure is the same; only the direction of the force on the membrane changes, and it is always perpendicular to the membrane.
