A body displaces $5$ liters of water with a density of $1000$ kilograms per cubic meter. With $g=10$ meters per second squared, what is the buoyant force?

- $50000\,\mathrm{N}$
- $5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $50\,\mathrm{N}$
- $500\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The buoyant force equals the weight of the displaced liquid. The volume is $0.005$ cubic meters, so $F_A=1000\cdot10\cdot0.005$. This gives $F_A=50$ newtons.
