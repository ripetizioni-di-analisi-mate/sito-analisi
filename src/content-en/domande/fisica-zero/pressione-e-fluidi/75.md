A cube with an edge of $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ is held still underwater, with its horizontal top face $30\,\mathrm{cm}$ below the surface. With $\rho=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the resultant of the water's pressure forces on the cube?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="0,0 4,0 4,5 0,5" linee="1.5,1 2.5,1 2.5,2 1.5,2 1.5,1;-0.3,5 4.3,5" segmenti="3,5,3,2;2.8,2,2.8,1" etichette="3.55,3.5,30 cm;3.35,1.5,10 cm" xmin=-0.5 xmax=4.8 ymin=-0.3 ymax=5.5 titolo="Submerged cube: the top face is $30\,\mathrm{cm}$ below the surface, and the edge is $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ long."}

- $29.43\,\mathrm{N}$ downward
- $39.24\,\mathrm{N}$ upward
- $68.67\,\mathrm{N}$ upward
- $9.81\,\mathrm{N}$ upward

## Explanation

By Stevin's law, the relative pressure is $1000\cdot9.81\cdot0.30\,\mathrm{Pa}=2943\,\mathrm{Pa}$ on the top face and $1000\cdot9.81\cdot0.40\,\mathrm{Pa}=3924\,\mathrm{Pa}$ on the bottom face. Each face has an area of $0.10\cdot0.10\,\mathrm{m^2}=0.01\,\mathrm{m^2}$, so the forces are $29.43\,\mathrm{N}$ downward and $39.24\,\mathrm{N}$ upward; atmospheric pressure acts on both faces and its contribution cancels. The forces on the side faces balance, so the resultant is $39.24\,\mathrm{N}-29.43\,\mathrm{N}=9.81\,\mathrm{N}$ upward, which is the buoyant force.
