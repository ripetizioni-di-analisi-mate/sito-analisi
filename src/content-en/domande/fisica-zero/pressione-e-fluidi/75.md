A rigid float is moved from fresh water to a denser liquid and settles without taking in liquid. How do its buoyancy and submerged volume change?

- Buoyancy stays the same and submerged volume decreases
- Both decrease
- Buoyancy increases and volume stays the same
- Both increase

## Explanation

Unchanged weight fixes buoyancy at equilibrium. The denser liquid needs less displaced volume to supply that same force.
