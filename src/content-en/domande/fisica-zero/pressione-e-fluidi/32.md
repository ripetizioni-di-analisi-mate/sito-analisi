A body displaces $6$ liters of water with a density of $1000$ kilograms per cubic meter. With $g=10$ meters per second squared, what is the buoyant force?

- $60000\,\mathrm{N}$
- $6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $600\,\mathrm{N}$
- $60\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The buoyant force equals the weight of the displaced liquid. The volume is $0.006$ cubic meters, so $F_A=1000\cdot10\cdot0.006$. This gives $F_A=60$ newtons.
