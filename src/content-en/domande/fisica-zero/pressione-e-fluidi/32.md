The barometer hanging in the hallway at home reads $101000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. What does this value indicate?

- The relative pressure of the air with respect to the atmosphere
- The pressure difference between the hallway and the kitchen
- The density of the air in the hallway
- The absolute pressure of the air, measured relative to a vacuum

## Explanation

The relative pressure of the room air with respect to the atmosphere is $p-p_0$, and it is $0\,\mathrm{Pa}$ because the room air is the atmosphere itself. A value close to $100000\,\mathrm{Pa}$ is therefore measured relative to a vacuum. Hence the barometer indicates the absolute pressure of the air; a density would be measured in $\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.
