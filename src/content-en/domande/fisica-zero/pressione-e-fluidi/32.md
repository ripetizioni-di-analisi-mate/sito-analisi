A vertical partition of area $0.020\,\mathrm{m^2}$ separates gases at uniform pressures of $110\,\mathrm{kPa}$ on the left and $90\,\mathrm{kPa}$ on the right. What net force do the gases exert?

- $4000\,\mathrm{N}$ to the right
- $400\,\mathrm{N}$ to the right
- $400\,\mathrm{N}$ to the left
- $2200\,\mathrm{N}$ to the right

## Explanation

Pressure on the left exceeds pressure on the right by $20\,\mathrm{kPa}$, so the resulting force points to the right. Multiplying $20000\,\mathrm{Pa}$ by $0.020\,\mathrm{m^2}$ gives $400\,\mathrm{N}$.
