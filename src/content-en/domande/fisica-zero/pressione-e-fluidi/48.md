A small sealed vessel and its liquid are in ideal free fall. In the vessel frame after transients, neglecting capillarity and tidal effects, what pressure gradient is due to apparent weight?

- The same as in the stationary laboratory
- Upwards with the same magnitude as before
- Zero
- Twice the stationary-laboratory value

## Explanation

The vessel and fluid accelerate together. Their effective gravity in this frame is zero, so it produces no top-to-bottom pressure difference.
