A tube contains two immiscible liquids with densities of 800 and 1100 kilograms per cubic metre. They are allowed to settle, with wall effects negligible. Which arrangement is stable?

- The $800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ liquid at the bottom
- Either order has equal stability
- The $1100\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ liquid at the bottom
- The denser liquid centred within both layers

## Explanation

An equal volume of the denser liquid weighs more. If placed above the lighter one, it tends to sink under gravity.
