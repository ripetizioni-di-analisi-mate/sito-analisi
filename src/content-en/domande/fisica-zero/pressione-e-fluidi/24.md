A rubber ball is held still underwater by a string tied to the bottom of a tank. How are the forces that the water exerts on the various points of the ball's surface directed?

- At every point perpendicular to the surface and pointing into the ball
- All vertical and pointing upward
- All vertical and pointing downward
- At every point tangent to the surface of the ball

## Explanation

A fluid in equilibrium exerts forces perpendicular to any surface it acts on. The surface of the ball is curved, so the direction of the force changes from point to point. The water presses from outside, so every force points into the ball. The resultant of all these forces is the buoyant force, which points upward.
