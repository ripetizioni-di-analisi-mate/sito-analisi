A body weighs $50$ N in air and experiences an upward buoyant force of $15$ N in a liquid. What is its apparent weight?

- $65\,\mathrm{N}$
- $15\,\mathrm{N}$
- $50\,\mathrm{N}$
- $35\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

For a body supported in equilibrium, the apparent weight equals the weight minus the buoyant force. Subtract $15$ from $50$. The apparent weight is therefore $35$ newtons.
