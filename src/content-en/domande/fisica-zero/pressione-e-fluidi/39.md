In a fluid at rest, a graph of pressure against depth has slope $8\,\mathrm{kPa/m}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what density does it indicate?

- $8000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $80\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $1250\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

The slope is $\rho g$, equal to $8000\,\mathrm{Pa/m}$. Dividing by $g$ gives $800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.
