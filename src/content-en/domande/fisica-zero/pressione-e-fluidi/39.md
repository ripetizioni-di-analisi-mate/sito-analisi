In a deep fryer, seed oil with a density of $900\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ is at rest and $8\,\mathrm{cm}$ deep. With $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the relative pressure of the oil on the bottom?

- $784.8\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $72\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $706.32\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $70632\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The depth in meters is $0.08\,\mathrm{m}$. By Stevin's law, the relative pressure on the bottom is $\rho gh$, using the density of the oil rather than that of water. Hence $p_{\mathrm{rel}}=900\cdot9.81\cdot0.08\,\mathrm{Pa}=706.32\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
