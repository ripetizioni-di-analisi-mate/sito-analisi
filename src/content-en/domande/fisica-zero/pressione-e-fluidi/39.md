In water with a density of $1000$ kilograms per cubic meter, what is the pressure increase at a depth of $7$ m? Use $g=10$ meters per second squared.

- $7000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $700\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $70000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $700000\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The hydrostatic pressure law gives the increase $\Delta p=\rho gh$. The surface pressure would be added only to obtain absolute pressure. Therefore, $\Delta p=1000\cdot10\cdot7=70000$ pascals.
