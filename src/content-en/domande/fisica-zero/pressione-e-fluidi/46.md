The graph shows the relative pressure $p_{\mathrm{rel}}$ in an alcohol at rest as a function of the depth $h$. The line passes through the point with $h=0.50\,\mathrm{m}$ and $p_{\mathrm{rel}}=3924\,\mathrm{Pa}$. With $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the density of the alcohol?

::grafico{f="7.848*x" xmin=0 xmax=1 ymin=0 ymax=9 nomi="h (m),pᵣₑₗ (kPa)" punti="0.5,3.924,pieno" segmenti="0.5,0,0.5,3.924;0,3.924,0.5,3.924" titolo="Relative pressure in the alcohol, in kilopascals; the marked point has $h=0.50\,\mathrm{m}$ and $p_{\mathrm{rel}}=3.924\,\mathrm{kPa}$."}

- $7848\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $400\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $80\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

By Stevin's law, $p_{\mathrm{rel}}=\rho gh$, so the graph is a straight line through the origin. Substituting the coordinates of the point gives $3924=\rho\cdot9.81\cdot0.50$. Therefore,
$$
\rho=\frac{3924}{9.81\cdot0.50}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}=800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}.
$$
