A transparent tube measures $50\,\mathrm{cm}$ along its sloping axis, but its endpoints differ in height by $30\,\mathrm{cm}$. Which distance determines their pressure difference for fluid at rest?

- $80\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $20\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $30\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $50\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Gravity acts vertically. The height difference in $\rho g\Delta h$ is $30\,\mathrm{cm}$, regardless of tube length.
