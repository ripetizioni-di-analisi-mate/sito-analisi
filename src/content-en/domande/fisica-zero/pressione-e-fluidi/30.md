In flight, the air in an airplane cabin is at $80000\,\mathrm{Pa}$, while the outside air is at $30000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. A window has an area of $0.08\,\mathrm{m^2}$. What is the magnitude of the net force of the two pressures on the window?

- $4000\,\mathrm{N}$
- $6400\,\mathrm{N}$
- $2400\,\mathrm{N}$
- $8800\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The cabin air pushes the window outward with a force of $80000\cdot0.08\,\mathrm{N}=6400\,\mathrm{N}$. The outside air pushes it inward with a force of $30000\cdot0.08\,\mathrm{N}=2400\,\mathrm{N}$. The two forces point in opposite directions, so the net force is $6400\,\mathrm{N}-2400\,\mathrm{N}=4000\,\mathrm{N}$, directed outward.
