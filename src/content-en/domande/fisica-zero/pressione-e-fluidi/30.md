In water with a density of $1000$ kilograms per cubic meter, what is the pressure increase at a depth of $6$ m? Use $g=10$ meters per second squared.

- $6000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $60000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $600\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $600000\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The hydrostatic pressure law gives the increase $\Delta p=\rho gh$. The surface pressure would be added only to obtain absolute pressure. Therefore, $\Delta p=1000\cdot10\cdot6=60000$ pascals.
