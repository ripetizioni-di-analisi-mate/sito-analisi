A hydraulic circuit contains still water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. Gauge pressure below the lower piston is $50\,\mathrm{kPa}$. The upper piston is $2\,\mathrm{m}$ higher and has area $0.010\,\mathrm{m^2}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what load can it balance if its own weight is negligible?

- $700\,\mathrm{N}$
- $200\,\mathrm{N}$
- $300\,\mathrm{N}$
- $500\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Pressure falls by $20\,\mathrm{kPa}$ over the $2\,\mathrm{m}$ rise, leaving $30\,\mathrm{kPa}$ gauge under the upper piston. The product $30000\cdot0.010$ gives $300\,\mathrm{N}$.
