Two syringes at the same height are connected by a thin tube, and the system is full of water. Syringe A has a plunger with an area of $1\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, syringe B one with an area of $4\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. Two people push the plungers and the water stays at rest. Neglecting friction, which statement is true?

- The person pushing on A exerts a larger force
- The two people exert the same force
- The water stays at rest only if nobody pushes
- The person pushing on B exerts a larger force

## Explanation

By Pascal's principle, the additional pressure is the same under both plungers, so $\dfrac{F_A}{1\,\mathrm{cm^2}}=\dfrac{F_B}{4\,\mathrm{cm^2}}$. It follows that $F_B=4F_A$. Therefore, the larger plunger, that of B, requires a larger force.
