In a mercury barometer, the mercury column stands $0.76\,\mathrm{m}$ above the free surface in the dish, with a vacuum above the column. Mercury has a density of $13600\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. With $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the atmospheric pressure that supports the column?

- $10336\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $1013962\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $101396\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $7456\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

There is a vacuum above the column, so the pressure at its top is zero. By Stevin's law, at the point of the column level with the free surface in the dish, the pressure is $\rho gh=13600\cdot9.81\cdot0.76\,\mathrm{Pa}\approx101396\,\mathrm{Pa}$. This point is at the same level as the surface in the dish, on which the atmosphere acts, so the atmospheric pressure is about $101396\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
