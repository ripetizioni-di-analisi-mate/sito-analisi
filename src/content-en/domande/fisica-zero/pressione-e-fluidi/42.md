A body weighs $70$ N in air and experiences an upward buoyant force of $21$ N in a liquid. What is its apparent weight?

- $91\,\mathrm{N}$
- $49\,\mathrm{N}$
- $21\,\mathrm{N}$
- $70\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

For a body supported in equilibrium, the apparent weight equals the weight minus the buoyant force. Subtract $21$ from $70$. The apparent weight is therefore $49$ newtons.
