One arm of a U-tube is open to the atmosphere and the other is connected to a gas. At equilibrium the liquid level is lower on the gas side. Which comparison is correct?

- The absolute gas pressure is zero
- Gas pressure exceeds atmospheric pressure
- The pressures are equal
- Gas pressure is lower than atmospheric pressure

## Explanation

At the elevation of the gas-side interface, the other arm is below its open surface. The pressure there exceeds atmospheric pressure and equals the gas pressure.
