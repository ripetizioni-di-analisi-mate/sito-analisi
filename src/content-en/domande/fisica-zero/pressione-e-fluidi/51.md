A vertical rectangular window of constant width extends from the water surface to a depth at which the gauge pressure is $6\,\mathrm{kPa}$. What is the mean gauge pressure over the window?

- $1.5\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $3\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

In uniform water, gauge pressure rises linearly from zero to $6\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Over a constant-width wall the mean is the average of the endpoint pressures.
