A bathtub is filled with still water up to $40\,\mathrm{cm}$ above the drain, which is closed by a horizontal plug with an area of $0.0012\,\mathrm{m^2}$. With $\rho=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the force of the water on the plug in addition to the contribution of atmospheric pressure?

- $0.48\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4.71\,\mathrm{N}$
- $47.09\,\mathrm{N}$
- $124.71\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

By Stevin's law, the relative pressure on the plug is $\rho gh=1000\cdot9.81\cdot0.40\,\mathrm{Pa}=3924\,\mathrm{Pa}$. The force due to this pressure is $F=p_{\mathrm{rel}}A$. Therefore, $F=3924\cdot0.0012\,\mathrm{N}\approx4.71\,\mathrm{N}$, directed downward.
