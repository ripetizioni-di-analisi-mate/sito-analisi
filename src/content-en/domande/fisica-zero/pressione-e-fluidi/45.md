A U-tube holds water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. The left surface stands $0.20\,\mathrm{m}$ below the right one, which is open to the atmosphere. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what gauge pressure must the gas at left exert to maintain that difference?

- $+2\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $+20\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

At the level of the left surface, pressure in the other arm exceeds atmospheric pressure by $1000\cdot10\cdot0.20=2000$ pascals. The gas must provide the same excess pressure.
