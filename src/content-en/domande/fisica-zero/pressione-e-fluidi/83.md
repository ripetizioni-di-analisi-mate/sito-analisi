A stone denser than water is aboard a boat floating in a tank. It is lowered to the bottom, fully submerged. What happens to the water level once the boat and water have settled?

- It stays the same because stone mass is conserved
- It rises because the stone is now fully wet
- It stays the same because stone volume is unchanged
- It falls because the stone made the boat displace more water

## Explanation

In the boat the stone displaced its own mass of water. On the bottom it displaces only its own volume, which is smaller because the stone is denser than water.
