A sample lowered slowly into a liquid gains $8\,\mathrm{N}$ of buoyancy for each additional litre immersed. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the liquid density?

- $80\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $1250\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $8000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

Each litre displaces $\dfrac{8}{10}=0.8$ kilograms of liquid. One cubic metre contains one thousand litres and therefore has a mass of $800\,\mathrm{kg}$.
