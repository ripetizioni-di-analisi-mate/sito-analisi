A buoy with a volume of $0.04\,\mathrm{m^3}$ floats in a lake with one quarter of its volume underwater. The water has a density of $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. With $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the buoyant force on the buoy?

- $392.4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $294.3\,\mathrm{N}$
- $98.1\,\mathrm{N}$
- $9.81\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The buoyant force formula uses the volume of the immersed part only, $V_{\mathrm{imm}}=\dfrac{0.04}{4}\,\mathrm{m^3}=0.01\,\mathrm{m^3}$. Hence $F_A=1000\cdot9.81\cdot0.01\,\mathrm{N}=98.1\,\mathrm{N}$. Since the buoy floats at rest, this value is also its weight.
