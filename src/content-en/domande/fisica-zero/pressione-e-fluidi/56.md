A hydraulic cylinder contains an air bubble. At the start of a stroke the input piston travels visibly before the output piston moves. Which property of the bubble can explain the delay?

- The bubble doubles the piston area
- Air can compress and take up part of the displaced volume
- The bubble makes the absolute pressure zero
- Air cancels gravity

## Explanation

During the stroke some of the input volume goes into compressing the air. That portion does not immediately move the output piston.
