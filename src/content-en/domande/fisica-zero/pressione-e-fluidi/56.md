In a car's braking system, the pedal pushes with a force of $100\,\mathrm{N}$ on a small piston with an area of $2\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ that presses on the brake fluid. Neglecting friction and differences in height, what pressure increase reaches the pistons at the wheels?

- $500000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $50\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $5000000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $50000\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The area of the small piston is $2\cdot0.0001\,\mathrm{m^2}=0.0002\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The pressure increase produced in the fluid is $\dfrac{F}{A}=\dfrac{100}{0.0002}\,\mathrm{Pa}=500000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. By Pascal's principle, this increase is transmitted to every part of the confined fluid, so it also reaches the pistons at the wheels.
