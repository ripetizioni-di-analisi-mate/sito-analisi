A sensor sits in a jet leaving a nozzle at varying speed. A student proposes finding its pressure from height alone using the hydrostatic relation. Which condition should be checked first?

- That the stream can be seen
- That the vessel is transparent
- That the nozzle is circular
- That the fluid being modelled is in static equilibrium

## Explanation

The hydrostatic relation follows from force balance in fluid elements at rest. An accelerating jet also requires information about its motion.
