The pascal is the SI unit of pressure. Which combination of units is equal to $1\,\mathrm{Pa}$?

- $1\,\mathrm{N\cdot m^2}$
- $1\,\mathrm{N/m^2}$
- $1\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $1\,\mathrm{N/cm^2}$

## Explanation

Pressure is the ratio of a perpendicular force to an area, $p=\dfrac{F}{A}$. In the SI, force is measured in newtons and area in square meters. Therefore, $1\,\mathrm{Pa}=1\,\mathrm{N/m^2}$, which is the pressure of a $1\,\mathrm{N}$ force distributed uniformly over $1\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The unit $\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ is the unit of density.
