A town gets its water from an open reservoir on a hill, with the water surface $40\,\mathrm{m}$ above the town square. A vertical transparent tube, open at the top and connected to the water mains in the square, runs up the front of a $60\,\mathrm{m}$ building. With the water at rest, how high above the square does the water rise in the tube?

- $40\,\mathrm{m}$
- $60\,\mathrm{m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $100\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The reservoir, the mains, and the tube form communicating vessels with the same water at rest, and atmospheric pressure acts on the free surfaces. Therefore, the water in the tube reaches the same level as the surface in the reservoir, that is, $40\,\mathrm{m}$ above the square. The height of the building does not matter, because the tube is open and the water stops at that level.
