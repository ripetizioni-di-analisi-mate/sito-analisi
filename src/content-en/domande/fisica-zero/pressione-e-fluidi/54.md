A small pressure probe immersed in still liquid is turned through a quarter turn without changing its centre or depth. What happens to the reading if probe size is negligible?

- It changes sign
- It vanishes when the face is vertical
- It stays the same
- It halves

## Explanation

Pressure at a point is a scalar and is the same in every direction. Rotation changes the direction of the force on the sensor face.
