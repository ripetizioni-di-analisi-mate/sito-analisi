An object of density $950\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ rests fully submerged across the boundary between oil of density $800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. What fraction of its volume is in the water?

- $95\%$
- $75\%$
- $50\%$
- $25\%$

## Explanation

If $f$ is the fraction in water, the displaced mass per unit volume of the object is $1000f+800(1-f)$. Setting this equal to 950 gives $f=0.75$.
