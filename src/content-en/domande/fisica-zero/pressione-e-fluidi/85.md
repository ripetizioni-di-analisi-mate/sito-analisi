A homogeneous plastic toy fish for an aquarium has the same density as water. It is carried by hand to mid-depth, completely underwater, and released at rest. What happens?

- It stays at rest at mid-depth, in equilibrium
- It rises until it floats at the surface
- It sinks to the bottom
- It keeps rising and sinking

## Explanation

At full immersion, the buoyant force is $\rho_f gV$ and the weight is $\rho_c gV$. The two densities are equal, so the weight and the buoyant force have the same magnitude and the net force is zero. Therefore, the toy fish, released at rest, stays in equilibrium where it is.
