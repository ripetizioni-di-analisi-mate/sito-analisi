A uniform pressure of $6000\,\mathrm{Pa}$ acts on one flat face of area $0.015\,\mathrm{m^2}$. What is the magnitude of the force on this face alone?

- $900\,\mathrm{N}$
- $9\,\mathrm{N}$
- $400\,\mathrm{N}$
- $90\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

For one face under uniform pressure, $F=pA$. Multiplying $6000\cdot0.015$ gives $90\,\mathrm{N}$.
