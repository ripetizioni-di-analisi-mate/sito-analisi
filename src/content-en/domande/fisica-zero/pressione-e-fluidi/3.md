A jar contains $0.72\,\mathrm{kg}$ of honey, which has a density of $1440\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. How many liters does the honey occupy?

- $2\,\mathrm{L}$
- $0.05\,\mathrm{L}$
- $5\,\mathrm{L}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{L}$

## Explanation

From the definition of density, $V=\dfrac{m}{\rho}$. Hence $V=\dfrac{0.72}{1440}\,\mathrm{m^3}=0.0005\,\mathrm{m^3}$. Since one cubic meter contains $1000$ liters, the honey occupies $0.5\,\mathrm{L}$.
