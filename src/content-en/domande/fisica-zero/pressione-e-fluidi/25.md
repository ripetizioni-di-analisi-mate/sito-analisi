The gauge used to inflate bicycle tires measures the relative pressure with respect to the atmosphere, which is at $100000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. On a flat tire with the valve open, the gauge reads $0\,\mathrm{Pa}$. What is the absolute pressure of the air in the tire?

- $100000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $0\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $200000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $50000\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The relative pressure is $p_{\mathrm{rel}}=p-p_0$, where $p_0$ is the atmospheric pressure. A reading of zero means $p-p_0=0$, so $p=p_0$. Therefore, the air in the tire has an absolute pressure of $100000\,\mathrm{Pa}$, the same as the outside air.
