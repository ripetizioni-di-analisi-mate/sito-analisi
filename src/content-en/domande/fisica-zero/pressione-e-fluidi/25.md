A brick measures 2, 3 and 6 centimetres along its edges. Which face should rest on a table to minimise the mean contact pressure?

- Any face gives the same pressure
- The $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ by $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ face
- The $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ by $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ face
- The $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ by $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ face

## Explanation

The weight does not change with orientation. The largest area is $18\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, giving the lowest ratio of weight to area.
