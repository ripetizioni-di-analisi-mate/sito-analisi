A plastic bottle is full of still water and closed with its cap. It is squeezed by hand, and the water pressure just below the cap increases by $2000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. By how much does the water pressure on the bottom of the bottle increase?

- $0\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $2000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $4000\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The water in the closed bottle is a confined liquid in equilibrium. By Pascal's principle, a pressure increase applied to the liquid is transmitted to every part of it. Therefore, the pressure on the bottom also increases by $2000\,\mathrm{Pa}$, while the difference due to depth remains the one given by Stevin's law.
