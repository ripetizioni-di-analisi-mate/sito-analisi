An ideal press full of incompressible liquid has an output piston four times the input area. The input advances $12\,\mathrm{cm}$. How far does the output move if there is no leakage?

- $3\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $48\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $6\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The displaced volumes are equal, so $A_1s_1=A_2s_2$. With four times the area, the output moves one quarter of $12\,\mathrm{cm}$.
