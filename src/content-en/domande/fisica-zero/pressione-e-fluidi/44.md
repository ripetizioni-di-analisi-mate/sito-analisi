A homogeneous body with a density of $700$ kilograms per cubic meter floats in water with a density of $1000$ kilograms per cubic meter. What fraction of its volume is submerged?

- $\dfrac{10}{7}$
- $0.3$
- $0.07$
- $0.7$

## Explanation

In equilibrium, the weight equals the buoyant force in magnitude. Therefore, $\rho_cVg=\rho_\ell V_i g$. Simplifying gives $\dfrac{V_i}{V}=\dfrac{700}{1000}=0.7$.
