Two open vessels connected at their bases contain the same liquid at rest. One vessel is much wider than the other. Neglecting capillarity, how do their free surfaces settle?

- At the same elevation
- Higher in the narrow vessel
- At the level corresponding to half the volume of each vessel
- Higher in the wide vessel

## Explanation

Atmospheric pressure acts on both surfaces. Equal pressure at the connection requires columns of equal height.
