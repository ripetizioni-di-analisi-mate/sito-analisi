A bottle of sparkling water is closed and standing upright. The gas above the liquid has an absolute pressure of $150000\,\mathrm{Pa}$, while the atmosphere outside is at $100000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. With $\rho=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the absolute pressure $20\,\mathrm{cm}$ below the surface of the liquid?

- $101962\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $1962\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $151962\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $51962\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

In Stevin's law $p=p_0+\rho gh$, the term $p_0$ is the pressure on the surface of the liquid. The bottle is closed, so the gas at $150000\,\mathrm{Pa}$ acts on the surface, not the outside atmosphere. The increase due to the water is $1000\cdot9.81\cdot0.20\,\mathrm{Pa}=1962\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Therefore, $p=150000\,\mathrm{Pa}+1962\,\mathrm{Pa}=151962\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
