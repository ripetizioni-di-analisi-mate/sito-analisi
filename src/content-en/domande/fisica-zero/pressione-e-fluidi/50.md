Two open tanks share the same atmospheric pressure. One point is $4\,\mathrm{m}$ deep in water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$, the other $5\,\mathrm{m}$ deep in oil of density $800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. How do their absolute pressures compare?

- The oil pressure is greater because the point is deeper
- The water pressure is greater because water is denser
- They are equal
- The oil pressure is twice as great

## Explanation

The products of density and depth are $1000\cdot4$ and $800\cdot5$, both 4000 in SI units. The same atmospheric pressure is then added.
