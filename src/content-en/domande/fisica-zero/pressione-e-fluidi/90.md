An apple with a mass of $0.2\,\mathrm{kg}$ floats at rest in a basin of water, with about one quarter of its volume above the water. With $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the buoyant force on the apple?

- $1.962\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.2\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1.47\,\mathrm{N}$
- $19.62\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The apple floats at rest and is acted on only by its weight and the buoyant force, neglecting the buoyant force of the air. Hence the buoyant force equals the weight, $F_A=mg=0.2\cdot9.81\,\mathrm{N}=1.962\,\mathrm{N}$. The fraction of the volume above the water is not needed for this calculation.
