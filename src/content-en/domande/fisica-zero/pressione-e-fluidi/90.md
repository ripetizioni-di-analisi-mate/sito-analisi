Two unknown liquids must be ranked by density. A spring scale, a thread and a single solid denser than both liquids are available. Which test ranks the liquids without knowing the solid's mass or volume?

- Weigh the dry solid before each immersion: the higher dry weight identifies the denser liquid
- Fully immerse the suspended solid in each: the liquid with the lower reading is denser
- Fully immerse the suspended solid in each: the liquid with the higher reading is denser
- Half immerse it in the first and fully immerse it in the second: the lower reading always identifies the denser liquid

## Explanation

At equal weight and displaced volume, higher density produces greater buoyancy and reduces thread tension. Full immersion in both liquids makes the readings comparable.
