A balloon of volume $5\,\mathrm{m^3}$ contains gas of density $0.2\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. Outside air has density $1.2\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. What combined weight of envelope and payload lets it remain suspended, with $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and negligible envelope volume?

- $10\,\mathrm{N}$
- $50\,\mathrm{N}$
- $70\,\mathrm{N}$
- $60\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The displaced air weighs $1.2\cdot5\cdot10=60$ newtons. The gas inside weighs $10\,\mathrm{N}$, leaving $50\,\mathrm{N}$ of the buoyancy to support the envelope and payload.
