A wooden raft has a volume of $0.5\,\mathrm{m^3}$ and a mass of $150\,\mathrm{kg}$ and floats on a freshwater river with a density of $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. What is the largest mass that can be loaded before the raft is completely underwater?

- $500\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $150\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $650\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $350\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The maximum buoyant force occurs at full immersion and equals $\rho_f gV$, the weight of $1000\cdot0.5\,\mathrm{kg}=500\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water. To float, the weight of the raft and load must not exceed this force, so the total mass is at most $500\,\mathrm{kg}$. Therefore, the maximum load is $500\,\mathrm{kg}-150\,\mathrm{kg}=350\,\mathrm{kg}$.
