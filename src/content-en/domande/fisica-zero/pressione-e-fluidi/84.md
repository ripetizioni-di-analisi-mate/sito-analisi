Two solid blocks of equal volume and densities $500\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and $900\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ are bonded with no gaps. They float together in water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. What percentage of their total volume is submerged?

- $90\%$
- $70\%$
- $50\%$
- $140\%$

## Explanation

Equal volumes give an average density equal to the arithmetic mean, $700\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. The equilibrium submerged fraction is the average density divided by the water density.
