A homogeneous wooden board floats at rest in a pond with $70\%$ of its volume underwater. The water has a density of $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. What is the density of the wood?

- $700\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $300\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $1429\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $70\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

For a homogeneous body floating at rest, $\dfrac{V_{\mathrm{imm}}}{V}=\dfrac{\rho_c}{\rho_f}$, so $\rho_c=\rho_f\dfrac{V_{\mathrm{imm}}}{V}$. The immersed fraction is $0.70$. Therefore, $\rho_c=1000\cdot0.70\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}=700\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.
