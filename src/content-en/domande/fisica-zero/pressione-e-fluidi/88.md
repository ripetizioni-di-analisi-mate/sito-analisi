A solid sample on a spring scale weighs $50\,\mathrm{N}$ in air and has an apparent weight of $30\,\mathrm{N}$ when fully submerged in water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. Neglecting air buoyancy, what is the sample's density?

- $2500\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $400\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $1500\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $600\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

The $20\,\mathrm{N}$ difference is the weight of a water volume equal to the sample volume. At equal volumes the density ratio equals the weight ratio, $\dfrac{50}{20}=2.5$.
