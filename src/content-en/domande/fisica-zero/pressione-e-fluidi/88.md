A stone hanging from a spring scale gives a reading of $6\,\mathrm{N}$ in air and $4\,\mathrm{N}$ when it is at rest and completely submerged in water with a density of $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$, not touching the bottom. What is the density of the stone?

- $1500\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $3000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $2000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $333\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

The difference between the two readings is the buoyant force, $F_A=6\,\mathrm{N}-4\,\mathrm{N}=2\,\mathrm{N}$. The mass of the stone is $m=\dfrac{6\,\mathrm{N}}{g}$, and the buoyant force gives the volume $V=\dfrac{F_A}{\rho_f g}=\dfrac{2\,\mathrm{N}}{1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}\cdot g}$. In the ratio $\dfrac{m}{V}$ the factor $g$ cancels, so
$$
\rho=\frac{6\cdot1000}{2}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}=3000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}.
$$
