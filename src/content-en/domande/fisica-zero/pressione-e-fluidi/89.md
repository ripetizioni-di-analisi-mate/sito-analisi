A vessel full of water to the brim is on a scale. An external stand holds an object fully submerged without touching the vessel. Overflow falls clear of the scale. How does the reading change from before immersion?

- It decreases by the weight of the spilled water
- It increases by the sum of the object's weight and the spilled water's weight
- It stays the same
- It increases by the object's weight

## Explanation

The vessel loses the weight of the displaced water. The object exerts a downward force on the remaining water equal to the buoyancy, precisely the lost weight. The effects cancel.
