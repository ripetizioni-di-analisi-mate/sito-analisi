Four small homogeneous objects are completely immersed, one at a time, in a bowl of olive oil with a density of $920\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$, and released from rest at mid-depth. Which of them rises and floats?

- A grape, with a density of $1080\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- A coin, with a density of $8900\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- A small pebble, with a density of $2600\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- A small piece of fir wood, with a density of $450\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

A completely immersed object is acted on by its weight $\rho_c gV$, pointing downward, and by the buoyant force $\rho_f gV$, pointing upward. If the density of the object is less than that of the oil, the buoyant force exceeds the weight and the object rises until it floats. Of the four objects, only the fir wood has a density less than that of the oil, because $450\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ is less than $920\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. The other three objects are denser than the oil, so their weight exceeds the buoyant force and they sink to the bottom.
