A body weighs $30$ N in air and experiences an upward buoyant force of $9$ N in a liquid. What is its apparent weight?

- $39\,\mathrm{N}$
- $21\,\mathrm{N}$
- $9\,\mathrm{N}$
- $30\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

For a body supported in equilibrium, the apparent weight equals the weight minus the buoyant force. Subtract $9$ from $30$. The apparent weight is therefore $21$ newtons.
