A science book gives the density of seawater as $1.03\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. What is this density in $\mathrm{kg/m^3}$?

- $1030\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $1.03\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $103\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $10300\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

One gram is one thousandth of a kilogram, so $1.03\,\mathrm{g}=0.00103\,\mathrm{kg}$. One cubic meter contains $100\cdot100\cdot100=1000000$ cubic centimeters, so $1\,\mathrm{cm^3}=0.000001\,\mathrm{m^3}$. Therefore,
$$
\rho=\frac{0.00103}{0.000001}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}=1030\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}.
$$
