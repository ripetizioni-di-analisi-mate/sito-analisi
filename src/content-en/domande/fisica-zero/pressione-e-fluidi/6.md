In still water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$, how much higher is the pressure at $3\,\mathrm{m}$ depth than at the surface? Take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $130000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $300\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $3000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $30000\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The hydrostatic pressure increase is $\rho gh$. The product $1000\cdot10\cdot3$ is $30000\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
