A perpendicular force of $60$ N acts on an area of $4$ square meters. What is the average pressure?

- $15\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $240\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $60\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $0.6\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The average pressure is $p=\dfrac{F_\perp}{A}$. Divide the force by the area over which it acts. Therefore, $p=\dfrac{60}{4}=15$ pascals.
