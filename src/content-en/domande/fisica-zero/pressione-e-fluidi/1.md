A press applies a normal force of $240\,\mathrm{N}$ to a $0.080\,\mathrm{m^2}$ base. What is the mean pressure on the base?

- $300\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $1920\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $19.2\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $3000\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

Mean pressure equals the normal force divided by the area. This gives $\dfrac{240}{0.080}=3000$ pascals.
