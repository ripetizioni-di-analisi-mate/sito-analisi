A kitchen bottle holds $0.75\,\mathrm{L}$ of olive oil with a density of $920\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. What is the mass of the oil, not counting the bottle?

- $690\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $6.9\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.69\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.069\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

One liter is one thousandth of a cubic meter, so $0.75\,\mathrm{L}=0.00075\,\mathrm{m^3}$. From the definition of density, the mass is $m=\rho V$. Therefore, $m=920\cdot0.00075\,\mathrm{kg}=0.69\,\mathrm{kg}$.
