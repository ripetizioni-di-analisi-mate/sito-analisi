In still oil of density $800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ a probe reads $12\,\mathrm{kPa}$ above the surface pressure. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, how far below the surface is the probe?

- $9.6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.15\,\mathrm{m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Rearrange the pressure relation as $h=\dfrac{\Delta p}{\rho g}$. The difference is $12000\,\mathrm{Pa}$ and each metre adds $8000\,\mathrm{Pa}$, giving a depth of $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$.
