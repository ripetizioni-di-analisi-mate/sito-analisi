A homogeneous body with a density of $300$ kilograms per cubic meter floats in water with a density of $1000$ kilograms per cubic meter. What fraction of its volume is submerged?

- $\dfrac{10}{3}$
- $0.7$
- $0.03$
- $0.3$

## Explanation

In equilibrium, the weight equals the buoyant force in magnitude. Therefore, $\rho_cVg=\rho_\ell V_i g$. Simplifying gives $\dfrac{V_i}{V}=\dfrac{300}{1000}=0.3$.
