A bedroom measures $4\,\mathrm{m}$ by $5\,\mathrm{m}$ and is $3\,\mathrm{m}$ high. The air inside has a density of $1.2\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. What is the mass of the air in the room?

- $50\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $72\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $7.2\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $720\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The volume of the room is $4\cdot5\cdot3\,\mathrm{m^3}=60\,\mathrm{m^3}$. From the definition of density, the mass is $m=\rho V$. Therefore, $m=1.2\cdot60\,\mathrm{kg}=72\,\mathrm{kg}$, a mass comparable to that of an adult.
