A homogeneous body with a density of $600$ kilograms per cubic meter floats in water with a density of $1000$ kilograms per cubic meter. What fraction of its volume is submerged?

- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $0.4$
- $0.6$
- $0.06$

## Explanation

In equilibrium, the weight equals the buoyant force in magnitude. Therefore, $\rho_cVg=\rho_\ell V_i g$. Simplifying gives $\dfrac{V_i}{V}=\dfrac{600}{1000}=0.6$.
