An ideal vessel can be evacuated to vacuum while the surroundings remain at $100\,\mathrm{kPa}$ absolute. What is the lowest gauge pressure relative to the surroundings?

- $0\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $100\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $-200\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $-100\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

Zero is the lower limit for the absolute gas pressure. Subtracting the atmospheric $100\,\mathrm{kPa}$ gives a gauge pressure of $-100\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
