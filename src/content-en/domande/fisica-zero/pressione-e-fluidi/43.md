In a U-tube open on both sides, the oil column is $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ above the interface and the water in the other arm is $9\,\mathrm{cm}$ above that same elevation. If water has density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$, what is the density of the oil?

- $750\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $1330\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $250\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

At the common elevation pressures match, so $\rho_o\cdot12=1000\cdot9$. The height ratio gives $750\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.
