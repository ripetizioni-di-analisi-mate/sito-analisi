In an ideal hydraulic press, the area of the second piston is $5$ times that of the first. If a force of $7$ N is applied to the first, what force is produced on the second?

- $1.4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $7\,\mathrm{N}$
- $35\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The pressure transmitted to the two pistons is the same. The force is therefore proportional to the area. Since the area increases by a factor of $5$, the force on the second piston is $35$ newtons.
