A cuboid is fully submerged with equal horizontal top and bottom faces. Why does the resultant vertical pressure force point upwards?

- Pressure on the bottom face exceeds pressure on the top face
- Pressure at the top face is zero
- All side faces push upwards
- Pressure acts only on the lower half

## Explanation

The bottom face lies deeper. With equal areas its upward force exceeds the downward force on the upper face.
