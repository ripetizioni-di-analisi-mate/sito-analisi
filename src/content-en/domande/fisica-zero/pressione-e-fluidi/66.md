A wooden board is held still underwater by a string, tilted at $30^\circ$ to the horizontal. What are the direction and sense of the buoyant force on the board?

- Perpendicular to the board, upward
- Vertical, upward
- Parallel to the board, upward
- Vertical, downward

## Explanation

The buoyant force is the resultant of the water's pressure forces on the surface of the body, and its magnitude is the weight of the displaced water. The deeper parts of the surface experience greater pressures, while the horizontal components of the pressure forces cancel out. Therefore, the resultant is vertical and points upward, whatever the tilt of the board.
