A rigid uniform body has the same density as its surrounding uniform liquid. It is fully submerged, lowered slowly to a deeper position and released from rest. What does the model predict without contact or currents?

- It must emerge until half its volume is exposed
- It can stay in equilibrium at the new level
- It must return to its first level
- It must fall to the bottom

## Explanation

Equal densities and full immersion make weight and buoyancy equal at every level. After displacement there is no vertical restoring force.
