A standing person has a volume of $0.07\,\mathrm{m^3}$ and is surrounded by air with a density of $1.2\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. With $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the buoyant force of the air on the person?

- $0.82\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.084\,\mathrm{N}$
- $8.2\,\mathrm{N}$
- $686.7\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The buoyant force acts in every fluid, including air. The person is entirely surrounded by air, so $F_A=1.2\cdot9.81\cdot0.07\,\mathrm{N}\approx0.82\,\mathrm{N}$. This force is very small compared with the person's weight, which is why the buoyant force of air is usually neglected.
