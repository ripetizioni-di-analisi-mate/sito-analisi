A body displaces $4$ liters of water with a density of $1000$ kilograms per cubic meter. With $g=10$ meters per second squared, what is the buoyant force?

- $40000\,\mathrm{N}$
- $40\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $400\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The buoyant force equals the weight of the displaced liquid. The volume is $0.004$ cubic meters, so $F_A=1000\cdot10\cdot0.004$. This gives $F_A=40$ newtons.
