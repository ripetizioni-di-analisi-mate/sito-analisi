The bottom of a jam jar has an area of $40\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. What is this area in square meters?

- $0.4\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $0.04\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $0.004\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $0.0004\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

One meter contains one hundred centimeters, so one square meter contains $100\cdot100=10000$ square centimeters. Therefore, $1\,\mathrm{cm^2}=0.0001\,\mathrm{m^2}$. It follows that $40\,\mathrm{cm^2}=40\cdot0.0001\,\mathrm{m^2}=0.004\,\mathrm{m^2}$.
