An aquarium travels on a train moving uniformly in a straight line. Its water has settled and is at rest relative to the train. In which frame can ordinary hydrostatics be applied directly to the stationary water?

- Only in a freely falling frame
- In the train frame, which is inertial in this model
- In no frame because the train is moving
- Only once the train returns to a station

## Explanation

Uniform straight-line motion preserves an inertial frame. In the train frame the liquid is at rest under ordinary gravity.
