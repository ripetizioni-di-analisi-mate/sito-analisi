A sealed bottle contains a little water and air. It is tilted and allowed to settle. Which description matches the behaviour of the two fluids?

- The air maintains a rigid shape of its own
- Both keep their previous shapes
- The water expands to occupy the entire bottle
- Water keeps approximately the same volume and air fills the remaining space

## Explanation

Water changes shape to fit its container. The gas occupies the available region above the new free surface.
