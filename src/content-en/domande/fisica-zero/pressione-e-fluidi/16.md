One wheel of a parked car supports a force of $3000\,\mathrm{N}$. Neglecting the stiffness of the tire walls, assume that the average pressure of the tire on the asphalt equals the relative pressure of the air inside it, that is, $200000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. What is the contact area between that wheel and the asphalt?

- $0.15\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $0.0015\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $0.015\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $0.06\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

By the assumption in the question, the average pressure on the asphalt is $p=200000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. The definition of average pressure $p=\dfrac{F}{A}$ gives $A=\dfrac{F}{p}$. Hence $A=\dfrac{3000}{200000}\,\mathrm{m^2}=0.015\,\mathrm{m^2}$. This area equals $150\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and refers to a single wheel.
