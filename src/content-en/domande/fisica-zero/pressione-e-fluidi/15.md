Two probes are at the same elevation in one connected basin of still uniform water. One is under a pier and the other is far away. With the same pressure at the free surface, how do their pressures compare?

- It is greater farther from the pier
- It is greater under the pier because it is shaded
- They depend on the horizontal separation of the probes
- They are equal

## Explanation

In a fluid at rest, hydrostatic pressure differences depend on elevation differences. The two probes have zero elevation difference.
