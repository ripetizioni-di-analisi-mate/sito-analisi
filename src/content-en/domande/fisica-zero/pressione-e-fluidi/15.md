A cubic concrete block with an edge of $0.5\,\mathrm{m}$ and a density of $2400\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ sits in a garden. With $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what average pressure does the block exert on the ground?

- $1200\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $11772\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $2943\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $23544\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The volume of the block is $0.5^3\,\mathrm{m^3}=0.125\,\mathrm{m^3}$, so its mass is $m=\rho V=2400\cdot0.125\,\mathrm{kg}=300\,\mathrm{kg}$. Its weight is $mg=300\cdot9.81\,\mathrm{N}=2943\,\mathrm{N}$ and acts on the base, whose area is $0.5^2\,\mathrm{m^2}=0.25\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Therefore, $p=\dfrac{2943}{0.25}\,\mathrm{Pa}=11772\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
