A body weighs $40$ N in air and experiences an upward buoyant force of $12$ N in a liquid. What is its apparent weight?

- $52\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$
- $28\,\mathrm{N}$
- $40\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

For a body supported in equilibrium, the apparent weight equals the weight minus the buoyant force. Subtract $12$ from $40$. The apparent weight is therefore $28$ newtons.
