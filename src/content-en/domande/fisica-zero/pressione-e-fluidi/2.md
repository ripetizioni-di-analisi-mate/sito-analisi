A uniform pressure of $3$ Pa acts on an area of $3$ square meters. What is the magnitude of the perpendicular force?

- $1\,\mathrm{N}$
- $9\,\mathrm{N}$
- $6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The definition of pressure gives $F=pA$. Multiply the pressure by the area. The magnitude of the force is therefore $9$ newtons.
