While calculating the pressure from $50\,\mathrm{N}$ on $25\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, a student uses $0.25\,\mathrm{m^2}$. How does the answer change when the area is corrected?

- It stays the same
- It becomes one hundred times smaller
- It becomes ten times larger
- It becomes one hundred times larger

## Explanation

The correct area is $0.0025\,\mathrm{m^2}$, one hundredth of the value used. At the same force the pressure therefore rises from $200\,\mathrm{Pa}$ to $20000\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
