A cork stopper has a mass of $6\,\mathrm{g}$ and a volume of $25\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. What is the density of the cork in $\mathrm{kg/m^3}$?

- $0.24\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $240\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $24\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $2400\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

One gram is one thousandth of a kilogram, so $6\,\mathrm{g}=0.006\,\mathrm{kg}$. One cubic meter contains $100\cdot100\cdot100=1000000$ cubic centimeters, so $25\,\mathrm{cm^3}=0.000025\,\mathrm{m^3}$. Density is the ratio of mass to volume, therefore
$$
\rho=\frac{0.006}{0.000025}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}=240\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}.
$$
