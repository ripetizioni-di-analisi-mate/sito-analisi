An open vessel flares outwards at the top. Its liquid weighs $100\,\mathrm{N}$, but gauge pressure gives a $70\,\mathrm{N}$ force at the horizontal base. Which force supports the remaining $30\,\mathrm{N}$ in the liquid's balance?

- An upward force from a vacuum
- A viscous tangential force in the stationary fluid
- The vertical component of the sloping walls' forces
- The horizontal component of the base force

## Explanation

The sloping wall reactions have vertical components. Using gauge pressures, those components supplement the floor force and bring the total to the $100\,\mathrm{N}$ weight.
