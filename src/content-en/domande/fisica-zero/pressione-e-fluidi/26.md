A homogeneous body with a density of $500$ kilograms per cubic meter floats in water with a density of $1000$ kilograms per cubic meter. What fraction of its volume is submerged?

- $2$
- $0.5$
- $0.05$
- $1.5$

## Explanation

In equilibrium, the weight equals the buoyant force in magnitude. Therefore, $\rho_cVg=\rho_\ell V_i g$. Simplifying gives $\dfrac{V_i}{V}=\dfrac{500}{1000}=0.5$.
