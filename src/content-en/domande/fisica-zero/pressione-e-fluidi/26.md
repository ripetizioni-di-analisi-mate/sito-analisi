A sealed ball is in the mountains, where the atmospheric pressure is $70000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. The air inside the ball has an absolute pressure of $100000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. What is the relative pressure of the air in the ball with respect to the outside atmosphere?

- $170000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $70000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $-30000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $30000\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The relative pressure is the difference between the absolute pressure and the reference pressure, here the local atmospheric pressure. Hence $p_{\mathrm{rel}}=100000\,\mathrm{Pa}-70000\,\mathrm{Pa}=30000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. The value is positive because the air in the ball is at a higher pressure than the outside air.
