A hand exerts a force with a $30\,\mathrm{N}$ normal component and a $40\,\mathrm{N}$ tangential component on a plate. Over $0.010\,\mathrm{m^2}$, what mean pressure does the hand exert?

- $4\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $5\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $7\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $3\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

Pressure uses the perpendicular component. The ratio $\dfrac{30}{0.010}=3000$ pascals equals $3\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
