The surface pressure is $100000$ Pa and the pressure due to the liquid is $6000$ Pa. What is the absolute pressure?

- $6000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $94000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $106000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $100000\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The absolute pressure is the sum of the surface pressure and the increase due to the liquid. Calculate $100000+6000$. The absolute pressure is therefore $106000$ pascals.
