The normal load on a support doubles and its contact area triples. What fraction of the original mean pressure results?

- $\dfrac16$
- $6$
- $\dfrac32$
- $\dfrac23$

## Explanation

In $p=\dfrac FA$ the numerator doubles while the denominator triples. The new pressure is $\dfrac23p$.
