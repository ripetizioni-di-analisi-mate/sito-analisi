A suction cup with an area of $20\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ is stuck to the bathroom tiles. Pulling it off perpendicularly takes a force of $120\,\mathrm{N}$, equal to the net force of the pressures of the outside air and of the air inside the cup. What is the difference between the outside pressure and the inside pressure?

- $6\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $600000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $60000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $6000\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The area of the suction cup is $20\cdot0.0001\,\mathrm{m^2}=0.002\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The net force of the two pressures is the pressure difference $\Delta p$ multiplied by the area, $F=\Delta p\,A$. Therefore, $\Delta p=\dfrac{120}{0.002}\,\mathrm{Pa}=60000\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
