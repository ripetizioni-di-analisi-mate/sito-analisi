Half the volume of uniform oil in a bottle is poured out at unchanged temperature. How do the mass and density of the remaining oil change?

- Mass stays the same and density halves
- Mass halves and density stays the same
- Both halve
- Density doubles and mass halves

## Explanation

Removing half the sample halves both mass and volume. Their common factor cancels in the density ratio.
