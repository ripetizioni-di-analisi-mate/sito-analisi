In water with a density of $1000$ kilograms per cubic meter, what is the pressure increase at a depth of $5$ m? Use $g=10$ meters per second squared.

- $50000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $5000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $500\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $500000\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The hydrostatic pressure law gives the increase $\Delta p=\rho gh$. The surface pressure would be added only to obtain absolute pressure. Therefore, $\Delta p=1000\cdot10\cdot5=50000$ pascals.
