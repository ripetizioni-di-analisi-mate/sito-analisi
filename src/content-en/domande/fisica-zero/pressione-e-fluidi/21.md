A suitcase weighing $150\,\mathrm{N}$ rests on the floor on a rectangular side measuring $50\,\mathrm{cm}$ by $20\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the average pressure on the floor?

- $0.15\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $15000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $150\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $1500\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

In meters the sides measure $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$ and $0.20\,\mathrm{m}$, so the contact area is $0.50\cdot0.20\,\mathrm{m^2}=0.10\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The force perpendicular to the floor is the weight of the suitcase. Therefore, $p=\dfrac{150}{0.10}\,\mathrm{Pa}=1500\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
