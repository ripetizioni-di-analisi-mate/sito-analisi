A hiker walks on fresh snow first in boots and then in snowshoes, which are wide frames strapped under the boots. In snowshoes he sinks much less. Which statement explains this?

- The snowshoes reduce the hiker's weight
- The force on the snow is the same and is spread over a larger area, so the pressure is lower
- The snowshoes increase the pressure on the snow and pack it down
- With snowshoes, the force on the snow becomes parallel to the surface

## Explanation

The force the hiker exerts on the snow is his weight, both in boots and in snowshoes. The average pressure is $p=\dfrac{F}{A}$, so for the same force a larger area gives a lower pressure. Snowshoes have a much larger contact area than boot soles, so the pressure on the snow decreases and the hiker sinks less.
