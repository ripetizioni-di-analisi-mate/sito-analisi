In water with a density of $1000$ kilograms per cubic meter, what is the pressure increase at a depth of $4$ m? Use $g=10$ meters per second squared.

- $4000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $400\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $400000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $40000\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The hydrostatic pressure law gives the increase $\Delta p=\rho gh$. The surface pressure would be added only to obtain absolute pressure. Therefore, $\Delta p=1000\cdot10\cdot4=40000$ pascals.
