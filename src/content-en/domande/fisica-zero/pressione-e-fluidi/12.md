A $5\,\mathrm{kg}$ body of volume $0.002\,\mathrm{m^3}$ hangs at rest completely immersed in water without touching the vessel. With water density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what does the spring scale read?

- $30\,\mathrm{N}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N}$
- $70\,\mathrm{N}$
- $50\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The weight is $50\,\mathrm{N}$ and buoyancy is $1000\cdot10\cdot0.002=20$ newtons. For equilibrium the support supplies the remaining $30\,\mathrm{N}$.
