A person with a mass of $70\,\mathrm{kg}$ wades into a pool up to the neck and feels lighter. What has changed compared with when she was out of the water?

- Her mass has decreased
- The upward buoyant force of the water acts on her, while her mass and weight are unchanged
- Her weight has decreased, because water reduces the acceleration due to gravity
- Her mass has increased, because the body absorbs water

## Explanation

Mass is a property of the body and does not depend on the fluid around it, and the weight $mg$ stays the same because $g$ does not change. In the water, the buoyant force also acts, pointing upward. Therefore, the bottom of the pool must exert a smaller force to support her, and this is what the person feels.
