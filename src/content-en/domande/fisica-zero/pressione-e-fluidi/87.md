A floating hydrometer has a fixed mass and a thin cylindrical stem. All the marks lie on the stem. How should the marks be spaced for equal increments in liquid density?

- Farther apart at higher densities
- Closer at higher densities
- Equally spaced
- All at the same height because weight is fixed

## Explanation

Submerged volume is $\dfrac{m}{\rho}$. For the same increase in density, the volume change is smaller at a higher starting density. With a constant stem area, the spacing between marks therefore decreases.
