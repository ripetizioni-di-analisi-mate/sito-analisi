An empty cylinder has mass $100\,\mathrm{g}$. Filled to the same mark with water and then with a second liquid, its total masses are $300\,\mathrm{g}$ and $260\,\mathrm{g}$. What is the second liquid's density relative to water?

- $60\%$
- $80\%$
- $125\%$
- $65\%$

## Explanation

Subtracting the tare gives $200\,\mathrm{g}$ of water and $160\,\mathrm{g}$ of the other liquid. At equal volumes the density ratio is $\dfrac{160}{200}=0.80$.
