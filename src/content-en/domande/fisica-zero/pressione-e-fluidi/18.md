A person weighing $780\,\mathrm{N}$ lies on a mattress, touching it over an area of $0.65\,\mathrm{m^2}$. What is the average pressure on the mattress?

- $507\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $120\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $1200\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $12000\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

Lying down, the person presses on the mattress with a perpendicular force equal to her weight. The average pressure is the ratio of this force to the contact area. Therefore, $p=\dfrac{780}{0.65}\,\mathrm{Pa}=1200\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
