A fully submerged body experiences $18\,\mathrm{N}$ of buoyancy and weighs $12\,\mathrm{N}$. A cord anchored to the bottom holds the body still. What is the cord tension?

- $6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $30\,\mathrm{N}$
- $18\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Without the cord the resultant would be $6\,\mathrm{N}$ upwards. The cord therefore exerts $6\,\mathrm{N}$ downwards on the body.
