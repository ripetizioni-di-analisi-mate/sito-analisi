A cone floats with its tip down. The waterline is halfway up its height and the submerged portion is a similar cone. What fraction of its volume is submerged?

- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac18$

## Explanation

The linear dimensions of the submerged portion are half those of the full cone. Volumes of similar solids scale as the cube of that ratio, so the fraction is $\left(\dfrac12\right)^3$.
