A small stone statue hangs from a spring scale that reads $12\,\mathrm{N}$ in air. With the statue at rest and completely submerged in water, not touching the bottom, the scale reads $8.5\,\mathrm{N}$. What is the buoyant force on the statue?

- $20.5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $3.5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $8.5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

In air, neglecting the buoyant force of the air, the scale reads the weight, $mg=12\,\mathrm{N}$. In water it reads the apparent weight $T=mg-F_A$. Hence $F_A=mg-T=12\,\mathrm{N}-8.5\,\mathrm{N}=3.5\,\mathrm{N}$.
