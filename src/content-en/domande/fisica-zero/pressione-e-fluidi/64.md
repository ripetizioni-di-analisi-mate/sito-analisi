The input piston of a press displaces $60\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ of fluid while the output piston opens up $48\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. Rigid pipes, incompressible liquid and absence of bubbles rule out storage. What percentage of the input volume leaked out?

- $80\%$
- $20\%$
- $12\%$
- $25\%$

## Explanation

The missing volume is $60-48=12$ cubic centimetres. Relative to the $60\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ input, the loss is $\dfrac{12}{60}=0.20$.
