The arm of an excavator is driven by a hydraulic cylinder. The oil presses on the piston of the cylinder with a relative pressure of $1\cdot10^{7}\,\mathrm{Pa}$, and the piston has an area of $0.01\,\mathrm{m^2}$. What force does the oil exert on the piston, beyond that due to the atmosphere?

- $1\cdot10^{9}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1\cdot10^{3}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1\cdot10^{7}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1\cdot10^{5}\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

For uniform pressure, the force on the piston face is $F=pA$. Hence $F=1\cdot10^{7}\cdot0.01\,\mathrm{N}=1\cdot10^{5}\,\mathrm{N}$, that is, $100000\,\mathrm{N}$. The force is perpendicular to the face of the piston on which the oil presses.
