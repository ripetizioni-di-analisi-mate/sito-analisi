Two points in the same liquid at rest are at the same elevation, and the density is uniform. The liquid has a density of $500$ kilograms per cubic meter. How do the pressures at the two points compare?

- The pressure is higher at the point closer to the wall
- The pressure is lower in the wider container
- They are equal
- They depend only on the total volume

## Explanation

In a homogeneous liquid at rest, the pressure difference depends on the difference in elevation. Here the difference in elevation is zero. The two pressures are therefore equal.
