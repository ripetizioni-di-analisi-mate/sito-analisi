While cooking, the valve of a pressure cooker keeps the steam at a relative pressure of $80000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. The atmospheric pressure in the kitchen is $100000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. What is the absolute pressure of the steam in the cooker?

- $20000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $80000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $180000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $100000\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

From the definition $p_{\mathrm{rel}}=p-p_0$, we get $p=p_0+p_{\mathrm{rel}}$. Hence $p=100000\,\mathrm{Pa}+80000\,\mathrm{Pa}=180000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Absolute pressure is measured relative to a vacuum, so it also includes the pressure of the atmosphere.
