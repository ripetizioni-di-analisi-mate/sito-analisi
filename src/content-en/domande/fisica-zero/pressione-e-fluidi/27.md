A machine weighs $900\,\mathrm{N}$. Its left support carries two thirds of the weight and has a contact area of $0.020\,\mathrm{m^2}$. What is the mean pressure beneath that support?

- $45\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $90\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $15\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $30\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

The force on the support is $\dfrac23\cdot900=600$ newtons. Dividing this force by its own area gives $30000\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
