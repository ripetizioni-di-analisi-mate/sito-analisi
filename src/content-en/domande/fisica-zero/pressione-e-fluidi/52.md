A U-tube with two identical open branches contains still water, with the surfaces $20\,\mathrm{cm}$ above the bottom of the tube. More water is poured into the left branch only, and the water is allowed to come to rest. What are the final levels?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 linee="0,3 0,0 3,0 3,3;1,3 1,1 2,1 2,3" poligoni="0,0 3,0 3,2 2,2 2,1 1,1 1,2 0,2" etichette="0.5,2.5,p₀;2.5,2.5,p₀;-0.75,1,20 cm" segmenti="-0.2,0,-0.2,2" xmin=-1.2 xmax=3.6 ymin=-0.4 ymax=3.4 titolo="U-tube before the addition: both surfaces are $20\,\mathrm{cm}$ above the bottom."}

- Higher than before and at the same level in both branches
- Higher in the left branch and unchanged in the right one
- Higher in the left branch and lower in the right one
- Unchanged in both branches

## Explanation

After the addition the water comes back to rest, the branches are connected, and atmospheric pressure acts on both surfaces. Therefore, the levels again reach the same height. The added water is shared between the two branches, so both levels end up higher than $20\,\mathrm{cm}$.
