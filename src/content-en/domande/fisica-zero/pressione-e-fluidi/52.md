A rectangular vertical gate runs from the water surface downwards. Atmospheric pressure acts on the other side. Where does the horizontal resultant act relative to mid-height?

- Exactly at mid-height
- Above mid-height
- Below mid-height
- At the upper edge

## Explanation

Equal strips placed symmetrically about mid-height carry different forces. The lower strip carries the greater force, putting the resultant below the geometric centre.
