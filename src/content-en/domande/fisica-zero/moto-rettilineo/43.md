In a tennis serve, the ball leaves the racket at $162\,\mathrm{km/h}$. What is this speed in meters per second?

- $583.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.7\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $162\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $45\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Since $1\,\mathrm{km}=1000\,\mathrm{m}$ and $1\,\mathrm{h}=3600\,\mathrm{s}$, we have $1\,\mathrm{km/h}=\dfrac{1000\,\mathrm{m}}{3600\,\mathrm{s}}=\dfrac{1}{3.6}\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Therefore the value in kilometers per hour is divided by $3.6$. Hence the speed of the ball is $\dfrac{162}{3.6}\,\mathrm{m/s}=45\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
