Two lines on the same position–time graph cross at $(7,12)$, with seconds and metres as units. What does the point show?

- Both move at $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for $7\,\mathrm{s}$
- Their separation after $7\,\mathrm{s}$ is $12\,\mathrm{m}$
- Both are at the $12\,\mathrm{m}$ mark after $7\,\mathrm{s}$
- Both travel exactly $12\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The horizontal coordinate gives one time and the vertical coordinate one position. The point belongs to both lines, so it describes a meeting.
