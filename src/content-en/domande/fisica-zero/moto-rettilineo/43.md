An object travels $7$ m to the right and $14$ m to the left. What is the total distance traveled?

- $7\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-7\,\mathrm{m}$
- $21\,\mathrm{m}$
- $14\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The distance traveled is the sum of the lengths of the individual legs, regardless of direction. The calculation is $7+14$. Therefore, the total distance is $21$ meters.
