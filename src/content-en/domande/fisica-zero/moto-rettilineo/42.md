At $4\,\mathrm{s}$, A’s position–time line is above B’s, but B’s slope is steeper. With both slopes positive, what is true at $4\,\mathrm{s}$?

- B is farther ahead and faster
- A is farther ahead and faster
- Their velocities are equal
- A is farther ahead and B is faster

## Explanation

Line height gives position. Slope gives velocity, so the position and speed comparisons favour different objects.
