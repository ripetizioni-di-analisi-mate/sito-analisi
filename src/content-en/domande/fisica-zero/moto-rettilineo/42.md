An object moves $7$ m to the right and then $7$ m to the left. What is its total displacement?

- $14\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $7\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-14\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The object ends up back at its initial position. The difference between the final and initial positions is therefore zero. The total displacement is $0$ meters, while the distance traveled is $14$ meters.
