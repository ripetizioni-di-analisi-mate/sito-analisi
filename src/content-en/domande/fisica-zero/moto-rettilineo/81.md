A belt carries parcels at the same speed. Consecutive parcel centres pass a sensor every $3\,\mathrm{s}$. If the belt speed doubles and centre spacing stays fixed, what will the interval between passes be?

- $6\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.75\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The interval is the time needed to cover the centre spacing. At fixed spacing, doubling speed halves that time.
