A subway train starts from rest at a station, reaches a certain speed, keeps it for a few hundred meters of straight tunnel, and finally brakes to a stop at the next station. In which phase can the motion be described as uniform motion in a straight line?

- In the middle stretch, traveled at constant velocity
- While leaving the first station
- While braking before the second station
- Over the whole trip between the two stations

## Explanation

**Uniform motion in a straight line** requires the velocity to stay constant over the interval studied. While leaving, the velocity increases, and while braking, it decreases, so the model does not apply to these phases. In the middle stretch the velocity is constant, so the model describes the motion. Over the whole trip the velocity changes, so the model does not apply to the entire route either.
