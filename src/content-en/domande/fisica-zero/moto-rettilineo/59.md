Two cars pass the same sign $3\,\mathrm{s}$ apart, both at a constant $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the same direction. What is their separation when the second car reaches the sign?

- $120\,\mathrm{m}$
- $60\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The first car has travelled for $3\,\mathrm{s}$ since passing the sign. By the time the second arrives, the first is $60\,\mathrm{m}$ ahead.
