Along a straight street there are, in this order, the school, the pharmacy, and the movie theater. Which choice of reference frame gives all three buildings a position greater than or equal to zero?

- Origin at the pharmacy and positive direction from the pharmacy toward the movie theater
- Origin at the school and positive direction from the school toward the movie theater
- Origin at the school and positive direction from the pharmacy toward the school
- Origin at the movie theater and positive direction from the pharmacy toward the movie theater

## Explanation

The position of a point is zero at the origin and is positive if the point lies, relative to the origin, on the side of the positive direction. With the origin at the school and the positive direction toward the movie theater, the pharmacy and the movie theater lie on the positive side, so they have positive positions, while the school has position zero. With the origin at the pharmacy, the school and the movie theater lie on opposite sides, so one of them has a negative position whichever direction is chosen. In the other two choices the positive direction points away from the other two buildings, so these have negative positions.
