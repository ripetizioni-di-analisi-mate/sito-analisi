A $60\,\mathrm{m}$ train crosses a $140\,\mathrm{m}$ bridge at $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$. How much time passes between the front entering and the rear leaving the bridge?

- $6\,\mathrm{s}$
- $14\,\mathrm{s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

At the end the front is $60\,\mathrm{m}$ beyond the bridge. It has covered $140+60=200$ metres since entering, which takes $20\,\mathrm{s}$ at $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
