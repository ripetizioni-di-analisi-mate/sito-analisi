An object travels $6$ m to the right and $12$ m to the left. What is the total distance traveled?

- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The distance traveled is the sum of the lengths of the individual legs, regardless of direction. The calculation is $6+12$. Therefore, the total distance is $18$ meters.
