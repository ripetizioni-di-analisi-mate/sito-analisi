A container ship in uniform motion in a straight line covers $12\,\mathrm{km}$ in $40\,\mathrm{min}$. How many kilometers does it cover in $1\,\mathrm{h}$ $40\,\mathrm{min}$?

- $30\,\mathrm{km}$
- $24\,\mathrm{km}$
- $4.8\,\mathrm{km}$
- $36\,\mathrm{km}$

## Explanation

In uniform motion $\Delta x=v\,\Delta t$ with constant $v$, so displacements are proportional to time intervals. The velocity of the ship is $v=\dfrac{12\,\mathrm{km}}{40\,\mathrm{min}}=0.3\,\mathrm{km/min}$. The new time interval is $1\,\mathrm{h}$ $40\,\mathrm{min}$, that is, $100\,\mathrm{min}$. Therefore the ship covers $0.3\,\mathrm{km/min}\cdot100\,\mathrm{min}=30\,\mathrm{km}$.
