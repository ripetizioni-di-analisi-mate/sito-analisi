Four marbles move along parallel straight grooves according to the following position equations, with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. Which marble has a horizontal line as its position-time graph?

- $x=6$
- $x=6t$
- $x=6-t$
- $x=1+6t$

## Explanation

A horizontal line on a position-time graph corresponds to a position that is the same at every instant. In the equation $x=6$ the position is $6\,\mathrm{m}$ whatever the value of $t$, so the marble is at rest and the graph is horizontal. The other equations contain a term in $t$, so the position changes over time and the line is slanted.
