A camera records 20 frames per second. A trolley’s front enters the picture at frame 10 and leaves at frame 30. How much time elapses?

- $0.5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

There are $30-10=20$ frame intervals between the images. At twenty intervals per second this corresponds to $1\,\mathrm{s}$.
