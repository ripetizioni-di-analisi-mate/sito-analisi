A ladybug moves back and forth along the straight edge of a table for one minute. At the end, its displacement is $-8\,\mathrm{cm}$. Which value could its distance traveled have?

- $-8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $20\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The distance traveled is a sum of positive lengths, so it is never negative. Moreover, the ladybug covers at least the distance between its starting point and its end point, which is $|\Delta x|=8\,\mathrm{cm}$. Therefore the distance traveled is greater than or equal to $8\,\mathrm{cm}$. Of the values offered, only $20\,\mathrm{cm}$ satisfies both conditions.
