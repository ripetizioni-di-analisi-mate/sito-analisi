An object moves uniformly with $s_0=2$ m and $v=4$ m/s. What is its position after $5$ s?

- $22\,\mathrm{m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $30\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The position equation is $s=s_0+vt$. Substitute the initial position and the elapsed time. Therefore, $s=2+4\cdot5=22$ meters.
