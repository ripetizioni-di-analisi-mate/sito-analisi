Two trolleys move towards one another along a track. They start $15\,\mathrm{m}$ apart and their constant speeds are $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. How long until they meet?

- $3\,\mathrm{s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $15\,\mathrm{s}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Each trolley reduces the gap, which shrinks by $1+2=3$ metres each second. Closing $15\,\mathrm{m}$ therefore takes $5\,\mathrm{s}$.
