Two riverboats travel along a straight stretch of river with the position equations $x_A=1200-6t$ and $x_B=4t$, with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. Which statement is correct?

- Boat B is faster, because its velocity is positive and A's is negative
- The two boats are equally fast, because both are in uniform motion
- Boat B is faster, because its position increases over time
- Boat A is faster, because the magnitude of its velocity is $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

In the position equation $x=x_0+vt$ the coefficient of $t$ is the velocity, so $v_A=-6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_B=4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The sign of the velocity indicates only the direction of motion, while the magnitude indicates how many meters the object covers each second. Boat A covers $6\,\mathrm{m}$ each second and boat B covers $4$. Therefore boat A is faster, even though it moves in the negative direction.
