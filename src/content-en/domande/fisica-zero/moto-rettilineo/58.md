At 10:00 a boat is $6\,\mathrm{km}$ west of a port and is travelling east at $12\,\mathrm{km/h}$. A ferry leaves the port westbound at 10:20 at $12\,\mathrm{km/h}$. When do they meet?

- 10:25
- 10:40
- 10:15
- 10:30

## Explanation

In $20\,\mathrm{min}$ the boat covers $4\,\mathrm{km}$. When the ferry leaves, $2\,\mathrm{km}$ remain. At a closing speed of $24\,\mathrm{km/h}$ that gap closes in $5\,\mathrm{min}$.
