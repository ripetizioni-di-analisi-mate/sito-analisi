An optical bench is graduated in centimetres. The slider follows $x=20+5t$, with $x$ in cm and $t$ in s. What is its velocity in m/s?

- $0.05\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The coefficient of $t$ means $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ per second. Each centimetre is one hundredth of a metre, giving $0.05\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
