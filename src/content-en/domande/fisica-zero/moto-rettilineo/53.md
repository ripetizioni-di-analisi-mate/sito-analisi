A drone starts moving at the instant $t_0=4\,\mathrm{s}$ from position $x_0=-6\,\mathrm{m}$, and from then on travels along a straight line at a constant velocity of $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is its position at the instant $t=10\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $19\,\mathrm{m}$
- $21\,\mathrm{m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The drone moves only from the instant $t_0=4\,\mathrm{s}$, so up to $t=10\,\mathrm{s}$ it travels for $t-t_0=6\,\mathrm{s}$. The position equation with the start at the instant $t_0$ gives
$$
x=x_0+v(t-t_0)=-6\,\mathrm{m}+2.5\,\mathrm{m/s}\cdot6\,\mathrm{s}=9\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
