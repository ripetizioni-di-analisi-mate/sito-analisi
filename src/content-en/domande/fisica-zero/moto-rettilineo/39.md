Which of these motions, each lasting one minute, has zero average velocity and positive average speed?

- A car that stays stopped at a traffic light for the whole minute
- A freight elevator that goes up from the ground floor to the third floor
- The plunger of a syringe pushed all the way in and brought back to its initial position
- A cyclist who always pedals in the same direction on a straight road

## Explanation

The average velocity is zero when the overall displacement is zero, that is, when the object ends where it started. The average speed is positive when the distance traveled is positive, that is, when the object has moved. The syringe plunger moves and returns to its initial position, so it satisfies both conditions. The stopped car also has zero average speed, while the freight elevator and the cyclist have a nonzero displacement.
