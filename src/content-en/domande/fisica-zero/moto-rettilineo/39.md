Two distinct position–time lines are parallel throughout the observation. Which event is ruled out during that interval?

- The objects having equal velocities
- The two objects meeting
- Motion towards decreasing positions
- A separation of $3\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Parallel lines have the same slope and a constant nonzero vertical separation. The two objects therefore occupy different positions at every instant.
