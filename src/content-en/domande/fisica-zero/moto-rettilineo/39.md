A speed is given as $126$ km/h. What is it in meters per second?

- $453.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $126\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $35\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

To convert from km/h to m/s, divide by $3.6$. The calculation is $\dfrac{126}{3.6}$. Therefore, the speed is $35$ meters per second.
