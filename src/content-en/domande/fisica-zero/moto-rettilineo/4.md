Along a straight track, a traffic light is chosen as the origin and the positive direction points from the traffic light toward the streetcar depot. A streetcar is at $x=-40\,\mathrm{m}$. Which statement is correct?

- The streetcar is $40\,\mathrm{m}$ from the traffic light, on the depot side
- The streetcar is moving away from the depot
- The streetcar is $40\,\mathrm{m}$ beyond the depot
- The streetcar is $40\,\mathrm{m}$ from the traffic light, on the side opposite the depot

## Explanation

Position indicates where the object is relative to the origin. The value $40\,\mathrm{m}$ is therefore the distance of the streetcar from the traffic light. The negative sign indicates that the streetcar is in the negative direction, that is, on the side opposite the depot. The position at a single instant does not tell us which way the streetcar is moving, so the data do not show that it is moving away from the depot.
