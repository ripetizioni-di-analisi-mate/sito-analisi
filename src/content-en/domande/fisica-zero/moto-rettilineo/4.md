A sensor records $x=4$ m at $2\,\mathrm{s}$ and $x=28$ m at $10\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the average velocity between the readings?

- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The displacement is $24\,\mathrm{m}$ and the elapsed time is $8\,\mathrm{s}$. This gives an average of $3\,\mathrm{m}$ in the positive direction per second.
