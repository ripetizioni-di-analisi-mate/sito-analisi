An object moves uniformly with $s_0=2$ m and $v=3$ m/s. What is its position after $5$ s?

- $15\,\mathrm{m}$
- $25\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $17\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The position equation is $s=s_0+vt$. Substitute the initial position and the elapsed time. Therefore, $s=2+3\cdot5=17$ meters.
