On a straight bike path, two friends set off at the same instant: Anna from position $x=0\,\mathrm{m}$ with velocity $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and Bruno from position $x=900\,\mathrm{m}$ with velocity $-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. How long does it take them to meet?

- $100\,\mathrm{s}$
- $900\,\mathrm{s}$
- $180\,\mathrm{s}$
- $225\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The position equations are $x_A=5t$ and $x_B=900-4t$, with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. The two friends meet when they occupy the **same position at the same instant**, that is, when $5t=900-4t$. Therefore $9t=900$ and $t=100\,\mathrm{s}$. At that instant both are at $x=500\,\mathrm{m}$.
