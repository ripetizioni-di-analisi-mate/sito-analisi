Extending two buses’ distinct position–time lines on the same road gives an intersection at $t=-2$ s. The model is valid only from $t=0$. What can be concluded?

- They will meet $2\,\mathrm{s}$ later
- The model predicts no meetings for $t\ge0$
- The buses certainly met $2\,\mathrm{s}$ earlier
- They occupy the same position from time zero

## Explanation

Two distinct lines intersect at most once. The only solution lies outside the model’s interval and cannot establish that a past meeting took place.
