A runner travels at constant speed for $8\,\mathrm{s}$ and covers $12\,\mathrm{m}$ in the first $3\,\mathrm{s}$. What fraction of the total distance remains at $3\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{5}{8}$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$

## Explanation

Five of the eight seconds remain. At constant speed the remaining fraction of the time is also the remaining fraction of the distance.
