On the position-time graph of a remote-controlled car in uniform motion, the line passes through the points $(1\,\mathrm{s},\,-4\,\mathrm{m})$ and $(5\,\mathrm{s},\,8\,\mathrm{m})$. What is the velocity of the car?

- $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The velocity is the change in position divided by the change in time between two points on the line. The position changes by $8\,\mathrm{m}-(-4\,\mathrm{m})=12\,\mathrm{m}$ and the time by $5\,\mathrm{s}-1\,\mathrm{s}=4\,\mathrm{s}$. Therefore
$$
v=\frac{12\,\mathrm{m}}{4\,\mathrm{s}}=3\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
