A vehicle is at $8\,\mathrm{m}$ at $3\,\mathrm{s}$ and has been travelling at $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Where was it at time zero?

- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $14\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-14\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The coordinate decreased by $6\,\mathrm{m}$ in three seconds. Recovering the earlier position therefore requires adding $6\,\mathrm{m}$ to the final reading.
