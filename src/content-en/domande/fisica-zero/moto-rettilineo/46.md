The conveyor belt at a supermarket checkout moves $12\,\mathrm{m}$ every minute. What is its speed in meters per second?

- $720\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac{10}{3}\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The belt covers $12\,\mathrm{m}$ in one minute, that is, in $60\,\mathrm{s}$. Therefore its speed is
$$
v=\frac{12\,\mathrm{m}}{60\,\mathrm{s}}=0.2\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
The factor $3.6$ applies only to converting between kilometers per hour and meters per second, so it is not used here.
