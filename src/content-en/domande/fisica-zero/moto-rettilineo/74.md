The position-time graph describes a canoe moving at constant velocity along a straight canal. Which position equation describes the motion, with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds?

::grafico{f="-4+2*x" punti="0,-4;6,8" segmenti="0,8,6,8;6,0,6,8" xmin=0 xmax=7 ymin=-6 ymax=10 nomi="t,x" legenda=0 titolo="Position $x$ of the canoe, in meters, as a function of time $t$, in seconds."}

- $x=2-4t$
- $x=-4+2t$
- $x=-4+\dfrac{1}{2}t$
- $x=4+2t$

## Explanation

The height of the line at $t=0$ is the initial position, so $x_0=-4\,\mathrm{m}$. Between $t=0\,\mathrm{s}$ and $t=6\,\mathrm{s}$ the position goes from $-4\,\mathrm{m}$ to $8\,\mathrm{m}$, so
$$
v=\frac{8\,\mathrm{m}-(-4\,\mathrm{m})}{6\,\mathrm{s}-0\,\mathrm{s}}=2\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
Substituting into the position equation $x=x_0+vt$ gives $x=-4+2t$.
