A cyclist is at the $6\,\mathrm{km}$ mark on a straight road. For $15\,\mathrm{min}$ the cyclist may travel in either direction at up to $12\,\mathrm{km/h}$. What interval of coordinates is reachable?

- From $3\,\mathrm{km}$ to $9\,\mathrm{km}$
- Only from $6\,\mathrm{km}$ to $9\,\mathrm{km}$
- From $4\,\mathrm{km}$ to $8\,\mathrm{km}$
- From $0\,\mathrm{km}$ to $12\,\mathrm{km}$

## Explanation

In a quarter of an hour the displacement magnitude cannot exceed $3\,\mathrm{km}$. Subtracting and adding that value to $6\,\mathrm{km}$ gives the interval endpoints.
