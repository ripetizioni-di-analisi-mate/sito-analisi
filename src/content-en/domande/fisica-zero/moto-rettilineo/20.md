An object's displacement is $60$ m in $4$ s. What is its average velocity?

- $240\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac{1}{15}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $60\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Average velocity is displacement divided by the time interval. Substitution gives $v_m=\dfrac{60}{4}$. Therefore, $v_m=15$ meters per second.
