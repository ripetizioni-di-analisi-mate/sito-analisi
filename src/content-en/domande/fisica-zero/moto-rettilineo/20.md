A scooter travels from kilometre marker 1.2 to marker 0.8 without turning. By how much does its odometer increase?

- $40\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-400\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2000\,\mathrm{m}$
- $400\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The coordinate decreases by $0.4\,\mathrm{km}$. The odometer records path length, so its reading increases by $400\,\mathrm{m}$.
