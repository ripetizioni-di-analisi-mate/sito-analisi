A moka pot is put on the stove when the kitchen timer reads $3\,\mathrm{min}$ $40\,\mathrm{s}$, and the coffee starts to come out when the timer reads $7\,\mathrm{min}$ $10\,\mathrm{s}$. How much time has passed?

- $270\,\mathrm{s}$
- $210\,\mathrm{s}$
- $430\,\mathrm{s}$
- $650\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

We convert both instants to seconds using $1\,\mathrm{min}=60\,\mathrm{s}$. The initial instant is $3\cdot60\,\mathrm{s}+40\,\mathrm{s}=220\,\mathrm{s}$ and the final instant is $7\cdot60\,\mathrm{s}+10\,\mathrm{s}=430\,\mathrm{s}$. The time interval is the difference between the two instants, so $\Delta t=430\,\mathrm{s}-220\,\mathrm{s}=210\,\mathrm{s}$, that is, $3\,\mathrm{min}$ $30\,\mathrm{s}$.
