Two objects are $60$ m apart and move toward each other at speeds of $6$ m/s and $6$ m/s. How long does it take them to meet?

- $10\,\mathrm{s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $60\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The distance between the objects decreases at a rate equal to the sum of their speeds, or $12$ meters per second. The time is $\dfrac{60}{12}$. Therefore, they meet after $5$ seconds.
