One person stands still on a moving walkway. Another walks beside it in the same direction and at the same speed as the walkway. How does their separation change?

- It grows at the walkway speed
- It stays constant
- It becomes zero after one second
- It shrinks at the walkway speed

## Explanation

Both people have the same velocity relative to the floor. They make equal displacements in each interval, leaving their separation unchanged.
