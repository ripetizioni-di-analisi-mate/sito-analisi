Choose a law for total distance $d$ accumulated by time $t$ at constant speed $u$, starting from a zeroed counter. Which expression has the required units and initial value?

- $d=ut^2$
- $d=u+t$
- $d=ut$
- $d=\dfrac{u}{t}$

## Explanation

Initial distance is zero and speed multiplied by time has units of length. The product $ut$ satisfies both requirements.
