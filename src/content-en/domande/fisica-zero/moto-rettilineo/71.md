A hiker must arrive by 10:30. The final route requires $35\,\mathrm{min}$ of walking and another $10\,\mathrm{min}$ of waiting. What is the latest possible departure time?

- 10:05
- 09:55
- 09:45
- 09:35

## Explanation

The time required is $35+10=45$ minutes. Counting back $45\,\mathrm{min}$ from 10:30 gives 09:45.
