The position-time graph describes the motion of a dog along a straight trail. Its doghouse is at position $x=2\,\mathrm{m}$. Which description matches the graph?

::grafico{linee="0,8 4,2 7,2" punti="0,8;4,2" xmin=0 xmax=8 ymin=0 ymax=9 nomi="t,x" legenda=0 titolo="Position $x$ of the dog, in meters, as a function of time $t$, in seconds."}

- The dog approaches the doghouse at constant velocity, reaches it, and stays there
- The dog moves away from the doghouse and then stops at $8\,\mathrm{m}$
- The dog approaches the doghouse more and more slowly until it stops
- The dog goes down a slope and then continues on level ground

## Explanation

Between $0\,\mathrm{s}$ and $4\,\mathrm{s}$ the graph is a falling segment, so the position decreases at constant velocity and the dog approaches the doghouse. At the instant $4\,\mathrm{s}$ the position is $2\,\mathrm{m}$, that is, the dog has reached the doghouse. After $4\,\mathrm{s}$ the graph is horizontal at a height of $2\,\mathrm{m}$, so the dog stays at rest at the doghouse. An approach that gets slower and slower would correspond to a curved line, and the shape of the graph does not represent the profile of the ground.
