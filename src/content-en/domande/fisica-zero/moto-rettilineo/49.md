We want to check that $54\,\mathrm{km/h}$ equals $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Which calculation is correct?

- $54\cdot\dfrac{3600\,\mathrm{s}}{1000\,\mathrm{m}}$
- $54\cdot\dfrac{1000\,\mathrm{m}}{60\,\mathrm{s}}$
- $54\cdot\dfrac{100\,\mathrm{m}}{3600\,\mathrm{s}}$
- $54\cdot\dfrac{1000\,\mathrm{m}}{3600\,\mathrm{s}}$

## Explanation

One kilometer per hour is one kilometer traveled in one hour. Since $1\,\mathrm{km}=1000\,\mathrm{m}$ and $1\,\mathrm{h}=3600\,\mathrm{s}$, we have $1\,\mathrm{km/h}=\dfrac{1000\,\mathrm{m}}{3600\,\mathrm{s}}$. Therefore
$$
54\,\mathrm{km/h}=54\cdot\frac{1000\,\mathrm{m}}{3600\,\mathrm{s}}=\frac{54000}{3600}\,\mathrm{m/s}=15\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
