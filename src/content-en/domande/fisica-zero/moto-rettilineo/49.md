A trolley advances $3\,\mathrm{m}$ every $2\,\mathrm{s}$. A strip photograph records an image every half second. What spacing separates consecutive images for uniform motion?

- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.75\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Half a second is one quarter of the recorded interval. In uniform motion displacement is proportional to time, so consecutive images are a quarter of $3\,\mathrm{m}$ apart.
