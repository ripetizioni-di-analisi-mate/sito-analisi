An object moves from position $s_i=3$ m to position $s_f=12$ m. What is its displacement?

- $9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Displacement is the final position minus the initial position. Substitution gives $\Delta s=12-3$. Therefore, the displacement is $9$ meters.
