A suitcase rests on the luggage rack of a train traveling along a straight track. Which statement about its state of motion is correct?

- The suitcase is at rest relative to both the car and the tracks
- The suitcase is moving relative to the car and at rest relative to the tracks
- The suitcase is at rest relative to the car and moving relative to the tracks
- The suitcase is moving relative to both the car and the tracks

## Explanation

Whether an object is moving depends on the chosen **reference frame**. The suitcase always occupies the same spot on the rack, so it is at rest relative to the car. The car moves along the track and carries the suitcase with it, so relative to the tracks the position of the suitcase changes over time. Therefore the suitcase is at rest relative to the car and moving relative to the tracks.
