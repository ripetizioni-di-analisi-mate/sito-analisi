A lift goes from level $x=+6$ m to level $x=-2$ m. The axis points upwards. What is its signed displacement?

- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Subtract the initial position from the final position. The result is $-2-6=-8$, so the displacement points downwards.
