A tortoise moves $3\,\mathrm{m}$ in $2\,\mathrm{min}$. A student calculates its average velocity by dividing the two numbers and writes $v_m=\dfrac{3}{2}=1.5$. Which unit belongs next to $1.5$?

- $\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\mathrm{m\cdot min}$
- $\mathrm{min/m}$
- $\mathrm{m/min}$

## Explanation

The average velocity is a displacement divided by a time interval, so its unit is a unit of length divided by a unit of time. The student divided meters by minutes, so the result is $1.5\,\mathrm{m/min}$. In meters per second, the same velocity is $\dfrac{3\,\mathrm{m}}{120\,\mathrm{s}}=0.025\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
