A position–time line crosses the time axis at $t=6$ s with a negative slope. What does this crossing mean?

- The clock resets at each reversal
- The velocity is zero at $6\,\mathrm{s}$
- The object crosses the origin towards decreasing coordinates
- The object stops for $6\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

On the time axis the ordinate, which is position, is zero. The negative slope shows that the crossing is towards decreasing coordinates.
