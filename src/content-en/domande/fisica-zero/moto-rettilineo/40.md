An object moves uniformly with $s_0=2$ m and $v=7$ m/s. What is its position after $5$ s?

- $35\,\mathrm{m}$
- $45\,\mathrm{m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $37\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The position equation is $s=s_0+vt$. Substitute the initial position and the elapsed time. Therefore, $s=2+7\cdot5=37$ meters.
