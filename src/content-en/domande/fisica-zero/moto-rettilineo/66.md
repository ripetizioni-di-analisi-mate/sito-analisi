The position-time graph describes a car backing out of a straight driveway at constant velocity. What is the velocity of the car?

::grafico{linee="0,12 6,0" punti="0,12;6,0" xmin=0 xmax=7 ymin=0 ymax=13 nomi="t,x" legenda=0 titolo="Position $x$ of the car, in meters, as a function of time $t$, in seconds."}

- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-0.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The velocity in uniform motion is found from two points on the graph by dividing the change in position by the change in time. From the graph, the car is at $12\,\mathrm{m}$ at the instant $0\,\mathrm{s}$ and at $0\,\mathrm{m}$ at the instant $6\,\mathrm{s}$. Therefore
$$
v=\frac{0\,\mathrm{m}-12\,\mathrm{m}}{6\,\mathrm{s}-0\,\mathrm{s}}=-2\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
The negative sign corresponds to the falling line.
