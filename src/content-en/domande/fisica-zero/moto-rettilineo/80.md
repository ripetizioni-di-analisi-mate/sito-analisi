A shuttle travels along a corridor from $0\,\mathrm{m}$ to $20\,\mathrm{m}$ at $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. A second sensor is at $25\,\mathrm{m}$. What prediction is supported by this description alone?

- The second sensor is not reached during the described run
- The second sensor triggers at $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- The shuttle must reverse afterwards
- The second sensor triggers at $5\,\mathrm{s}$ during this run

## Explanation

The given model ends at the $20\,\mathrm{m}$ mark. Extending it to $25\,\mathrm{m}$ would require information about the subsequent motion.
