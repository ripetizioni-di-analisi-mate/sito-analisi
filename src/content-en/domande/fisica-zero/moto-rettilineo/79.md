A person stays at a stop from 8:02 until 8:06. A bus passes through the stop at 8:04. How many meeting instants are there if the bus passes that position only once?

- One
- Four
- Every instant of the wait
- Two

## Explanation

The person occupies the same position throughout the wait. The bus occupies it only at 8:04, which falls within that interval.
