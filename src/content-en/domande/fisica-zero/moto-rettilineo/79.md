Two cars leave a gas station at the same instant and travel in opposite directions along a straight road, one at $25\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the other at $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$, at constant velocities. How far apart are they after $40\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $1800\,\mathrm{m}$
- $200\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{m}$
- $800\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

We place the origin at the gas station and take the direction of the first car as positive. The position equations are $x_1=25t$ and $x_2=-20t$, with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. At the instant $40\,\mathrm{s}$ the positions are therefore $x_1=1000\,\mathrm{m}$ and $x_2=-800\,\mathrm{m}$. Hence the distance between the two cars is $1000\,\mathrm{m}-(-800\,\mathrm{m})=1800\,\mathrm{m}$.
