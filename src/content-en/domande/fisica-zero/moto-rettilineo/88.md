Two fixed marks on the same straight riverbank are $100\,\mathrm{m}$ apart. A boat moves parallel to the bank at $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ relative to the water against a current of $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. How much time passes between the boat drawing level with the first and then the second mark?

- $100\,\mathrm{s}$
- $25\,\mathrm{s}$
- $50\,\mathrm{s}$
- $12.5\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Relative to the bank the boat gains $6-2=4$ metres each second. Covering the $100\,\mathrm{m}$ between fixed marks therefore takes $25\,\mathrm{s}$.
