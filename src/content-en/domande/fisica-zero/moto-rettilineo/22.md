A flight lasts $2\,\mathrm{h}$ $15\,\mathrm{min}$. What is this duration in seconds?

- $8100\,\mathrm{s}$
- $7215\,\mathrm{s}$
- $135\,\mathrm{s}$
- $9000\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

We convert the hours and the minutes separately. Since $1\,\mathrm{h}=3600\,\mathrm{s}$, two hours equal $7200\,\mathrm{s}$. Since $1\,\mathrm{min}=60\,\mathrm{s}$, fifteen minutes equal $900\,\mathrm{s}$. Therefore the flight lasts $7200\,\mathrm{s}+900\,\mathrm{s}=8100\,\mathrm{s}$.
