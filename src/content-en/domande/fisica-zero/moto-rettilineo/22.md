A shuttle leaves at 23:58 and arrives at 00:07 the next day. How long is the trip?

- $9\,\mathrm{min}$
- $69\,\mathrm{min}$
- $5\,\mathrm{min}$
- $51\,\mathrm{min}$

## Explanation

There are $2\,\mathrm{min}$ before midnight and another $7\,\mathrm{min}$ afterwards. The elapsed time is therefore $9\,\mathrm{min}$.
