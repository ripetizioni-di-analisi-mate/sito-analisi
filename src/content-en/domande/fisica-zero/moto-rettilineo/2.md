A person walks from the $1\,\mathrm{m}$ mark to the $9\,\mathrm{m}$ mark and back to the $3\,\mathrm{m}$ mark. What distance does an ideal distance counter record?

- $14\,\mathrm{m}$
- $11\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The outward leg is $8\,\mathrm{m}$ long and the return leg is $6\,\mathrm{m}$ long. The counter adds both lengths, giving $14\,\mathrm{m}$.
