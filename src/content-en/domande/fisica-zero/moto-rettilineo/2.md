An object's displacement is $36$ m in $4$ s. What is its average velocity?

- $144\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $36\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Average velocity is displacement divided by the time interval. Substitution gives $v_m=\dfrac{36}{4}$. Therefore, $v_m=9$ meters per second.
