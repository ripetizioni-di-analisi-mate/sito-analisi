In a straight hallway, the origin is at the door and the positive direction points from the door toward the window. A table is at $x=4\,\mathrm{m}$ and the window is at $x=7\,\mathrm{m}$. If the origin is moved to the window and the positive direction is left unchanged, what is the position of the table?

- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $11\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-11\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The table is $7\,\mathrm{m}-4\,\mathrm{m}=3\,\mathrm{m}$ from the window. The table is on the door side, that is, in the negative direction from the window. Since the window is the new origin, the position of the table is therefore negative. Hence in the new reference frame the table is at $x=-3\,\mathrm{m}$, while its distance from the window is still $3\,\mathrm{m}$.
