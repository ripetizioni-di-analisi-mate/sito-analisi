A billiard ball starts at position $x=0.3\,\mathrm{m}$, hits the cushion at $x=1.8\,\mathrm{m}$, and bounces back to $x=0.9\,\mathrm{m}$, always along the same line. What is its displacement?

- $2.4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-0.9\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

To calculate the displacement, the initial and final positions are enough, even though the ball changed direction. Therefore
$$
\Delta x=0.9\,\mathrm{m}-0.3\,\mathrm{m}=0.6\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
The distance traveled, on the other hand, is $1.5\,\mathrm{m}+0.9\,\mathrm{m}=2.4\,\mathrm{m}$, because it includes the trip to the cushion and the return.
