A ball follows a curve and returns to the straight line from which it started. What determines whether the entire motion is rectilinear?

- Only the endpoints need to lie on one line
- The horizontal projection must increase
- Every position must lie on a single straight line
- The initial and final speeds must match

## Explanation

The trajectory includes the intermediate positions. A curved section therefore prevents the whole path from being a straight line.
