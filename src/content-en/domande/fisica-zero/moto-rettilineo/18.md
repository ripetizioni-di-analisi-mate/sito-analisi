On a position-time graph, a straight line passes through $(0\,\mathrm s,2\,\mathrm m)$ and $(4\,\mathrm s,18\,\mathrm m)$. What is the velocity?

- $16\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The velocity is the slope of the line on the position-time graph. The calculation is $\dfrac{18-2}{4-0}$. Therefore, the velocity is $4$ meters per second.
