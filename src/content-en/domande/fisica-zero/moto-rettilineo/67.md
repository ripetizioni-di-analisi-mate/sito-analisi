The graph shows the positions of two skaters, A and B, who start together from the same point on a straight rink. Which statement is correct?

::grafico{f="2*x;x" xmin=0 xmax=6 ymin=0 ymax=13 nomi="t,x" legenda="A;B" titolo="Positions $x$ of the two skaters, in meters, as a function of time $t$, in seconds."}

- B is faster than A, because B's line is lower
- A and B have the same velocity, because they start from the same point
- A is faster than B, because A's line is steeper
- B is faster than A, because B takes longer to reach the same position

## Explanation

On a position-time graph, velocity is the change in position divided by the change in time, so it corresponds to the slope of the line. In the same time interval, A's position increases more than B's, so A's line is steeper. Consequently A is faster than B. The common starting point concerns only the initial positions, not the velocities.
