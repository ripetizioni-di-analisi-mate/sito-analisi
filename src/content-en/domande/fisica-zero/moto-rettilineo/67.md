Two straight-line journeys are plotted on graphs printed with different scales. Both lines make the same angle to the horizontal. How can their velocities be compared?

- Calculate each slope using that graph’s axis scales
- Compare the changes in position after the same number of horizontal grid squares, without reading the scales
- Measure only their heights at the page midpoint
- Compare only the printed angles

## Explanation

A centimetre on paper can represent different times and distances on the two graphs. Comparable velocities require changes expressed in the same physical units.
