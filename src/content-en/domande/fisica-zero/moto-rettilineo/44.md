On a graph each vertical square represents $5\,\mathrm{m}$ and each horizontal square $2\,\mathrm{s}$. A line rises two squares over a run of five squares. What is the velocity?

- $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The rise is $2\cdot5=10$ metres and the interval is $5\cdot2=10$ seconds. The slope in physical units is therefore $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
