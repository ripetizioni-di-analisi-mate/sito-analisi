A weather station measures a wind speed of $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is this speed in kilometers per hour?

- $\dfrac{25}{6}\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $900\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $54\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $5.4\,\mathrm{km/h}$

## Explanation

One meter per second corresponds to $3600\,\mathrm{m}$ in one hour, that is, to $3.6\,\mathrm{km/h}$. Therefore, to convert from meters per second to kilometers per hour, we multiply the value by $3.6$. Hence the wind blows at $15\cdot3.6\,\mathrm{km/h}=54\,\mathrm{km/h}$.
