The print head carriage of a printer moves along its straight rail at a constant velocity $v=-4\,\mathrm{cm/s}$. What is its displacement between the instants $t=3\,\mathrm{s}$ and $t=8\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $20\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-32\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The interval lasts $\Delta t=8\,\mathrm{s}-3\,\mathrm{s}=5\,\mathrm{s}$. In uniform motion, the displacement is the velocity times the time interval, so
$$
\Delta x=-4\,\mathrm{cm/s}\cdot5\,\mathrm{s}=-20\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
The negative sign indicates that the carriage moves in the negative direction of the rail.
