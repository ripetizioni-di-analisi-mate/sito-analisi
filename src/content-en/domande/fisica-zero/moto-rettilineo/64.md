A trolley crosses the origin at $4\,\mathrm{s}$ while travelling right at $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. If time is on the vertical axis and position on the horizontal axis, what is the slope of its line?

- $4\,\mathrm{s/m}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}\,\mathrm{s/m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{s/m}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{s/m}$

## Explanation

With the axes exchanged, the slope is elapsed time divided by displacement. The trolley takes one third of a second for each metre.
