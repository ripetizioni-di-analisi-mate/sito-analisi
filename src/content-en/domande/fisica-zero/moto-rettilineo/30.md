A runner covers $1800\,\mathrm{m}$ in $8\,\mathrm{min}$ and then stands still for $2\,\mathrm{min}$ at a traffic light. What is her average speed over the entire $10\,\mathrm{min}$ interval?

- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.75\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $180\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $225\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The **average speed** is the distance traveled divided by the total time, which includes the time spent at rest. The total time is $10\,\mathrm{min}=600\,\mathrm{s}$. Therefore
$$
u_m=\frac{1800\,\mathrm{m}}{600\,\mathrm{s}}=3\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
Leaving out the stop would give $\dfrac{1800\,\mathrm{m}}{480\,\mathrm{s}}=3.75\,\mathrm{m/s}$, which is the average speed over the $8\,\mathrm{min}$ of running only.
