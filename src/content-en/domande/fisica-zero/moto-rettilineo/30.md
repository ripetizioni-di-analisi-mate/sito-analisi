A trolley moves forwards for $2\,\mathrm{s}$ at $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and backwards for $3\,\mathrm{s}$ at $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Taking forwards as positive, what is its average velocity?

- $1.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The displacements are $+10$ and $-3$ metres. Dividing their sum of $7\,\mathrm{m}$ by $5\,\mathrm{s}$ gives $1.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
