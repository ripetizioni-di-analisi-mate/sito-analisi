A speed is given as $108$ km/h. What is it in meters per second?

- $388.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $108\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

To convert from km/h to m/s, divide by $3.6$. The calculation is $\dfrac{108}{3.6}$. Therefore, the speed is $30$ meters per second.
