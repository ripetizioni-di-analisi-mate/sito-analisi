Two airport shuttles move along the same straight lane in the same direction. At $t=0$, shuttle A is at $x=0\,\mathrm{m}$ with velocity $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and shuttle B is at $x=10\,\mathrm{m}$ with velocity $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The equation $2t=10+3t$ has the solution $t=-10\,\mathrm{s}$. What can we conclude for $t\geq0$?

- The two shuttles do not meet
- The two shuttles meet after $10\,\mathrm{s}$
- Shuttle A catches B after $10\,\mathrm{s}$, at $x=20\,\mathrm{m}$
- The two shuttles meet at the instant $t=0$

## Explanation

The position equations hold for $t\geq0$, so a meeting time is acceptable only if it is not negative. The only solution of the equation is $t=-10\,\mathrm{s}$, so for $t\geq0$ the two shuttles never occupy the same position at the same instant. The result is consistent with the data, because B starts ahead of A and is faster, so the distance between the two shuttles increases.
