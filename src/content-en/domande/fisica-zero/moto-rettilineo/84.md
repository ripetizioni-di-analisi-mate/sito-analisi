A runner makes five trips between two lines $20\,\mathrm{m}$ apart, alternating direction and starting at the first line. Where is the runner after the fifth trip?

- Halfway between the lines
- At the first line
- At the second line
- $100\,\mathrm{m}$ from the first line

## Explanation

Each pair of trips returns the runner to the first line. After four trips the runner is back there, and the fifth ends at the second line.
