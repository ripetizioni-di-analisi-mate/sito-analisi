A waiter walks from the counter to a table $8\,\mathrm{m}$ away and back to the counter, taking $10\,\mathrm{s}$ in all. What are the waiter's average speed and average velocity?

- $u_m=0.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_m=0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $u_m=1.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_m=1.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $u_m=0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_m=1.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $u_m=1.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_m=0\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The distance traveled includes the trip there and back, so $s=16\,\mathrm{m}$ and $u_m=\dfrac{16\,\mathrm{m}}{10\,\mathrm{s}}=1.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The waiter returns to the starting point, so the displacement is zero. Consequently the average velocity $v_m=\dfrac{\Delta x}{\Delta t}$ is also $0\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
