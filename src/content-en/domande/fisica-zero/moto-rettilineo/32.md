Three cyclists cover the same distance in $8\,\mathrm{min}$, $10\,\mathrm{min}$ and $12\,\mathrm{min}$. Which cyclist definitely has the greatest average speed?

- The cyclist who takes $12\,\mathrm{min}$
- The cyclist who takes $10\,\mathrm{min}$
- The cyclist who takes $8\,\mathrm{min}$
- All three equally

## Explanation

The distance is fixed and average speed is inversely proportional to elapsed time. The shortest time therefore gives the highest average speed.
