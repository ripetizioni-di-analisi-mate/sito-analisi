An amusement park train moves along a straight track, and its position is recorded every second starting at $t=0$. Which of these sequences of positions is consistent with uniform motion in a straight line?

- $0\,\mathrm{m}$, $3\,\mathrm{m}$, $6\,\mathrm{m}$, $9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m}$, $1\,\mathrm{m}$, $4\,\mathrm{m}$, $9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m}$, $3\,\mathrm{m}$, $5\,\mathrm{m}$, $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m}$, $3\,\mathrm{m}$, $3\,\mathrm{m}$, $6\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

In **uniform motion in a straight line**, the object undergoes equal displacements in equal time intervals. In the sequence $0$, $3$, $6$, $9$ meters the displacement is $3\,\mathrm{m}$ every second, so it is consistent with a constant velocity of $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. In the other sequences, the displacements from one second to the next change. For example, in the sequence $0$, $1$, $4$, $9$ meters the displacements are $1\,\mathrm{m}$, $3\,\mathrm{m}$, and $5\,\mathrm{m}$, so the velocity increases.
