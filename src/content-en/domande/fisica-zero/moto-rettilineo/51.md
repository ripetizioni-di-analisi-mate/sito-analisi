A runner travels $10\,\mathrm{m}$ along a straight track and then stands on a treadmill aligned with it. For $3\,\mathrm{s}$ the runner stays in the same place relative to the ground. How much does the ground-frame distance increase in those $3\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $13\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

In the ground frame the runner stays at one point for those three seconds. The path length in that frame is therefore zero.
