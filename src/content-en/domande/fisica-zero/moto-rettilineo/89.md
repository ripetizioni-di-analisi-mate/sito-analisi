A logger saves one position per minute and shows a trolley at $0\,\mathrm{m}$ in every sample. Which observation would directly prove that it moved between two samples?

- A more precise clock without further position measurements
- An intermediate reading of $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- Another reading of $0\,\mathrm{m}$ at the end of the minute
- The coincidence of the first and last coordinates

## Explanation

A nonzero intermediate position proves that the trolley left the origin. Equal endpoint readings are consistent with rest and also with an outward and return journey.
