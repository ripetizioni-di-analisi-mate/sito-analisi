Two scooters, A and B, travel in the same direction along a straight road, and the graph shows their positions over time. Which statement is correct?

::grafico{f="2*x;6+0.5*x" xmin=0 xmax=7 ymin=0 ymax=13 nomi="t,x" legenda="A;B" titolo="Positions $x$ of the two scooters, in meters, as a function of time $t$, in seconds."}

- A is always ahead of B
- At the instant $4\,\mathrm{s}$ A and B have the same velocity
- B is faster than A
- Before $4\,\mathrm{s}$ B is ahead of A, and after $4\,\mathrm{s}$ A is ahead of B

## Explanation

On a position-time graph, the scooter ahead is the one with the greater position, that is, the one whose line is higher. For $t<4\,\mathrm{s}$, B's line lies above A's, so B is ahead. At the instant $4\,\mathrm{s}$ the lines intersect, so the two scooters are at the same position. A's line is steeper, so A is faster and after $4\,\mathrm{s}$ it is ahead of B.
