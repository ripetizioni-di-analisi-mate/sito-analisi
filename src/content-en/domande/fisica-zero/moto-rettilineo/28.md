A vehicle travels forwards for two equal time intervals at $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is its average speed?

- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

With equal durations, distances are proportional to speeds. Dividing the combined distance by twice the duration gives the arithmetic mean, $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
