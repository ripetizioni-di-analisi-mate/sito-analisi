An object moves from position $s_i=6$ m to position $s_f=24$ m. What is its displacement?

- $30\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-18\,\mathrm{m}$
- $24\,\mathrm{m}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Displacement is the final position minus the initial position. Substitution gives $\Delta s=24-6$. Therefore, the displacement is $18$ meters.
