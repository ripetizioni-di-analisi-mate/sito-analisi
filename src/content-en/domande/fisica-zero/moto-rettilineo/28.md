A bucket is lowered into a well on a rope. The axis is vertical, with the origin at the rim of the well and upward as the positive direction. In $12\,\mathrm{s}$ the bucket moves from $x=0\,\mathrm{m}$ to $x=-18\,\mathrm{m}$. What is its average velocity?

- $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-\dfrac{2}{3}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-18\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-1.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The displacement is $\Delta x=-18\,\mathrm{m}-0\,\mathrm{m}=-18\,\mathrm{m}$. The **average velocity** is the displacement divided by the time interval, so
$$
v_m=\frac{-18\,\mathrm{m}}{12\,\mathrm{s}}=-1.5\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
The negative sign indicates that the bucket moved downward, that is, in the negative direction of the axis.
