The trolley of a tower crane moves along the straight jib at a constant velocity of $0.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ starting at $t=0$. At $t=12\,\mathrm{s}$ it is at $x=19\,\mathrm{m}$. Where was it at $t=0$?

- $13\,\mathrm{m}$
- $25\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $18.5\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

From the position equation $x=x_0+vt$ we get $x_0=x-vt$. In $12\,\mathrm{s}$ the trolley moves $0.5\,\mathrm{m/s}\cdot12\,\mathrm{s}=6\,\mathrm{m}$ in the positive direction. Therefore the initial position is $x_0=19\,\mathrm{m}-6\,\mathrm{m}=13\,\mathrm{m}$.
