A van leads a car by $30\,\mathrm{m}$ and travels at $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The car follows at $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$. If both maintain their speeds, when will the car overtake?

- It never happens under these conditions
- After $15\,\mathrm{s}$
- After $2.5\,\mathrm{s}$
- After $3\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The leading vehicle is $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ faster. Its lead grows by $2\,\mathrm{m}$ each second and stays positive.
