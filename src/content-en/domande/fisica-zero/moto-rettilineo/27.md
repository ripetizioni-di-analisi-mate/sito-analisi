A trolley is at $1\,\mathrm{m}$, $5\,\mathrm{m}$ and $11\,\mathrm{m}$ at times $0\,\mathrm{s}$, $2\,\mathrm{s}$ and $5\,\mathrm{s}$. What follows from these samples alone?

- The trolley certainly moved at $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at every instant
- The trolley reversed between $2\,\mathrm{s}$ and $5\,\mathrm{s}$
- The average velocity increased in the second interval
- The data are consistent with a constant velocity of $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Both interval slopes are $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Discrete readings also allow speed changes between samples if the trolley still reaches the recorded marks on time.
