Two timekeepers follow a sailing race. The first starts a stopwatch at the starting gun, while the second starts one $20\,\mathrm{s}$ earlier. A boat rounds the buoy when the first stopwatch reads $80\,\mathrm{s}$. What does the second stopwatch read at that instant?

- $60\,\mathrm{s}$
- $80\,\mathrm{s}$
- $100\,\mathrm{s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The second stopwatch was started $20\,\mathrm{s}$ before the first, so at every instant it reads $20\,\mathrm{s}$ more. Therefore, when the first reads $80\,\mathrm{s}$, the second reads $100\,\mathrm{s}$. The two stopwatches use different instants as $t=0$ and therefore assign different values to the same instant. Time intervals measured with either one, however, are the same.
