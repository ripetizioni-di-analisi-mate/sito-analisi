On a position-time graph, a straight line passes through $(0\,\mathrm s,2\,\mathrm m)$ and $(4\,\mathrm s,22\,\mathrm m)$. What is the velocity?

- $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $22\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The velocity is the slope of the line on the position-time graph. The calculation is $\dfrac{22-2}{4-0}$. Therefore, the velocity is $5$ meters per second.
