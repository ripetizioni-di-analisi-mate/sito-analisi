Along a straight highway, the positive direction is east. A motorcycle travels at a constant velocity $v=-20\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Which statement is correct?

- Every second the velocity of the motorcycle decreases by $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- Every second the motorcycle moves $20\,\mathrm{m}$ west
- The motorcycle is $20\,\mathrm{m}$ west of the origin
- Every second the motorcycle moves $20\,\mathrm{m}$ east

## Explanation

In uniform motion with velocity $v$, the displacement each second is $v\cdot1\,\mathrm{s}$. With $v=-20\,\mathrm{m/s}$, the displacement each second is therefore $-20\,\mathrm{m}$. The negative sign indicates the direction opposite the positive one, that is, west. The velocity is constant, so it does not decrease over time, and its value does not tell where the motorcycle is at a given instant.
