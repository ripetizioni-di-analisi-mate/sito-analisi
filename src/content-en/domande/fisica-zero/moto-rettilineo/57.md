A trolley starts at the $12\,\mathrm{m}$ mark moving right at $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. A second starts simultaneously at the $0\,\mathrm{m}$ mark moving right. At what speed must it travel to catch the first exactly $3\,\mathrm{s}$ later?

- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

After $3\,\mathrm{s}$ the first trolley reaches $18\,\mathrm{m}$. The second must cover the full $18\,\mathrm{m}$ in that time, requiring $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
