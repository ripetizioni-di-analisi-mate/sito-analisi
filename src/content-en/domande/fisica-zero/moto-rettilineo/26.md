A student holds a plank for $1\,\mathrm{min}$ $48\,\mathrm{s}$. Expressed in minutes, how long does the exercise last?

- $1.48\,\mathrm{min}$
- $0.8\,\mathrm{min}$
- $108\,\mathrm{min}$
- $1.8\,\mathrm{min}$

## Explanation

Since $1\,\mathrm{min}=60\,\mathrm{s}$, the $48\,\mathrm{s}$ correspond to $\dfrac{48}{60}\,\mathrm{min}=0.8\,\mathrm{min}$. Adding the whole minute gives $1\,\mathrm{min}+0.8\,\mathrm{min}=1.8\,\mathrm{min}$. The value $1.48\,\mathrm{min}$ would come from writing the seconds after the decimal point, as if one minute contained $100\,\mathrm{s}$.
