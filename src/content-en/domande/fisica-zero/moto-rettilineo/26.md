Two objects are $50$ m apart and move toward each other at speeds of $5$ m/s and $5$ m/s. How long does it take them to meet?

- $10\,\mathrm{s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{s}$
- $50\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The distance between the objects decreases at a rate equal to the sum of their speeds, or $10$ meters per second. The time is $\dfrac{50}{10}$. Therefore, they meet after $5$ seconds.
