Positions recorded one second apart are $0,4,4,1$ metres. For how long is the object certainly at rest if the graph is straight between successive readings?

::grafico{linee="0,0 1,4 2,4 3,1" punti="0,0;1,4;2,4;3,1" xmin=0 xmax=3.4 ymin=0 ymax=5 nomi="t,x" legenda=0 titolo="Position in metres against time in seconds."}

- $3\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Position is unchanged between the second and third readings. Since the connecting segment is straight, the slope stays zero throughout that second.
