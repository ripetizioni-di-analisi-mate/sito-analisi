Two cars leave the same tollbooth together and arrive together at the same parking lot, along the same straight road. One of them stops for a few minutes to get gas and then makes up the time by driving faster. Which statement is certainly true?

- The car that stopped has a smaller average velocity
- The car that did not stop traveled at constant velocity
- The two cars have the same average velocity
- The two cars had the same velocity at every instant

## Explanation

The average velocity depends only on the overall displacement and the time interval. The two cars start from the same point and arrive at the same point, so they have the same displacement. They leave and arrive at the same instants, so the trip takes the same time. Consequently their average velocities are equal, even though the two motions differ along individual parts of the route.
