A $20\,\mathrm{m}$ train takes $4\,\mathrm{s}$ to pass a fixed signal completely at constant speed. What is its speed?

- $80\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

From the arrival of its front until its rear passes the signal, the train advances by its own length. It therefore covers $20\,\mathrm{m}$ in $4\,\mathrm{s}$.
