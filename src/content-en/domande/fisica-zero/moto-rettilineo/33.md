An object moves $6$ m to the right and then $6$ m to the left. What is its total displacement?

- $0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The object ends up back at its initial position. The difference between the final and initial positions is therefore zero. The total displacement is $0$ meters, while the distance traveled is $12$ meters.
