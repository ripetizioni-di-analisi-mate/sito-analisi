On a position-time graph, a straight line passes through $(0\,\mathrm s,2\,\mathrm m)$ and $(4\,\mathrm s,30\,\mathrm m)$. What is the velocity?

- $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $28\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac{1}{7}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The velocity is the slope of the line on the position-time graph. The calculation is $\dfrac{30-2}{4-0}$. Therefore, the velocity is $7$ meters per second.
