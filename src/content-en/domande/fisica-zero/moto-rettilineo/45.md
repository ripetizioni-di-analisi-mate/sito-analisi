A shuttle travelling at constant velocity is at $2\,\mathrm{m}$ at $1\,\mathrm{s}$ and $14\,\mathrm{m}$ at $7\,\mathrm{s}$. Without finding its initial position, where is it halfway in time between the readings?

- $12\,\mathrm{m}$
- $7\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

A straight line divides displacement in proportion to time. At the midpoint in time the shuttle is halfway between $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $14\,\mathrm{m}$.
