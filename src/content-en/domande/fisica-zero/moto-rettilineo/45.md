Along the straight avenue of a park pass a horse at $35\,\mathrm{km/h}$, a cyclist at $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$, a skater at $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and a maintenance vehicle at $30\,\mathrm{km/h}$. Which of them is moving fastest?

- The cyclist
- The horse
- The skater
- The maintenance vehicle

## Explanation

To compare the speeds, we must express them all in the same unit, for example in kilometers per hour. We therefore multiply the speeds in meters per second by $3.6$. The cyclist goes $9\cdot3.6\,\mathrm{km/h}=32.4\,\mathrm{km/h}$ and the skater $8\cdot3.6\,\mathrm{km/h}=28.8\,\mathrm{km/h}$. Hence the fastest is the horse, at $35\,\mathrm{km/h}$.
