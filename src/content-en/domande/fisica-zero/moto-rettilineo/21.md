A robot ends $6\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right of its starting point after travelling $14\,\mathrm{m}$ in all. How many metres did it travel leftwards?

- $10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $7\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Rightward and leftward distances add to $14\,\mathrm{m}$ and differ by $6\,\mathrm{m}$. Subtracting these totals gives $8\,\mathrm{m}$, twice the leftward distance.
