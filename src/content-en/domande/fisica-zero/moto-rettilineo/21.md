A speed is given as $90$ km/h. What is it in meters per second?

- $25\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $324\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $90\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

To convert from km/h to m/s, divide by $3.6$. The calculation is $\dfrac{90}{3.6}$. Therefore, the speed is $25$ meters per second.
