In a ski race, an athlete starts when the official clock reads $t=12\,\mathrm{s}$ and crosses the finish line when it reads $1\,\mathrm{min}$ $47\,\mathrm{s}$. Which statement is correct?

- The run lasts $107\,\mathrm{s}$
- The run lasts $119\,\mathrm{s}$
- The athlete crosses the finish line at the instant $t=95\,\mathrm{s}$
- The run lasts $95\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The clock reading at the finish is an **instant**, while the time taken is a **time interval**. The finishing instant is $1\,\mathrm{min}$ $47\,\mathrm{s}$, that is, $t_f=107\,\mathrm{s}$. The time interval is the final instant minus the initial instant, so $\Delta t=107\,\mathrm{s}-12\,\mathrm{s}=95\,\mathrm{s}$. Therefore the run lasts $95\,\mathrm{s}$ and the finish occurs at the instant $107\,\mathrm{s}$.
