At kilometre 27 a sign reads “station $4\,\mathrm{km}$ ahead”. The station lies along the straight road in the direction of decreasing kilometre marks. At which mark is it?

- $-4\,\mathrm{km}$
- $31\,\mathrm{km}$
- $4\,\mathrm{km}$
- $23\,\mathrm{km}$

## Explanation

The sign gives a positive distance. Converting that to a coordinate requires the direction, so subtract $4\,\mathrm{km}$ from $27\,\mathrm{km}$.
