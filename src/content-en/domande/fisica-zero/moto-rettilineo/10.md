A position–time line passes through $(0,4)$ and $(3,13)$, with time in seconds and position in metres. Which equation fits both readings?

- $x=4+3t$
- $x=3t$
- $x=4+9t$
- $x=3+4t$

## Explanation

The position at time zero is $4\,\mathrm{m}$. The $9\,\mathrm{m}$ rise over $3\,\mathrm{s}$ gives a slope of $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$, so the coefficient of time is 3.
