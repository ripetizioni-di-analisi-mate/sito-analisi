In a park, a dog runs along a straight path. It starts at position $x=2\,\mathrm{m}$, runs to $x=14\,\mathrm{m}$ to get a ball, and comes back to $x=5\,\mathrm{m}$. What distance has it traveled?

- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $21\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The **distance traveled** is the sum of the lengths of the individual legs, each counted as positive. On the way out, the dog covers $14\,\mathrm{m}-2\,\mathrm{m}=12\,\mathrm{m}$. On the way back, it covers $14\,\mathrm{m}-5\,\mathrm{m}=9\,\mathrm{m}$. Therefore the distance traveled is $12\,\mathrm{m}+9\,\mathrm{m}=21\,\mathrm{m}$, while the displacement is only $5\,\mathrm{m}-2\,\mathrm{m}=3\,\mathrm{m}$.
