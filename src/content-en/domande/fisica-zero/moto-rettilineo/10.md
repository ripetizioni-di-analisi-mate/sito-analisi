An object moves from position $s_i=4$ m to position $s_f=16$ m. What is its displacement?

- $20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{m}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Displacement is the final position minus the initial position. Substitution gives $\Delta s=16-4$. Therefore, the displacement is $12$ meters.
