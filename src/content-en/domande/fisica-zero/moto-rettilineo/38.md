A position–time graph has two consecutive rising straight segments. The rise per second is twice as large on the second. What changes at the join?

- Position doubles instantaneously
- The object reverses direction
- The object stops for an interval
- Speed increases while direction stays the same

## Explanation

Both slopes are positive. The larger magnitude of the second slope means the object moves faster in the same direction.
