An object's displacement is $84$ m in $4$ s. What is its average velocity?

- $336\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $21\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac{1}{21}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $84\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Average velocity is displacement divided by the time interval. Substitution gives $v_m=\dfrac{84}{4}$. Therefore, $v_m=21$ meters per second.
