A crab moves sideways along the straight water's edge. It starts at position $x=1\,\mathrm{m}$, and over $15\,\mathrm{s}$ its average velocity is $v_m=-0.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is its position at the end of the interval?

- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

From the definition of average velocity, $\Delta x=v_m\,\Delta t$. Therefore the displacement of the crab is $\Delta x=-0.2\,\mathrm{m/s}\cdot15\,\mathrm{s}=-3\,\mathrm{m}$. The final position is the initial position plus the displacement, so $x_f=1\,\mathrm{m}+(-3\,\mathrm{m})=-2\,\mathrm{m}$.
