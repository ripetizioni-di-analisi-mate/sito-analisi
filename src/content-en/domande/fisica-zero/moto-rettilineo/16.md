A new origin lies $7\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right of the old one. Both axes use the same unit and direction. What is the new coordinate of an object previously at $x=4$ m?

- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-11\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $11\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The point is $3\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left of the new origin. Subtracting the location of the new origin gives $x'=4-7=-3$ metres.
