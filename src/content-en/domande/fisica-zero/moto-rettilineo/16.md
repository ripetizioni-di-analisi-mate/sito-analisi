On a straight road, the positive direction is north. A cyclist has a displacement of $-300\,\mathrm{m}$ between 9:00 and 9:02. Which statement is certainly true?

- Between 9:00 and 9:02 the cyclist traveled exactly $300\,\mathrm{m}$
- Between 9:00 and 9:02 the cyclist always pedaled south
- At 9:02 the cyclist is $300\,\mathrm{m}$ farther south than at 9:00
- At 9:02 the cyclist is at position $x=-300\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The displacement compares only the final position with the initial position. The value $-300\,\mathrm{m}$ therefore says that at 9:02 the cyclist is $300\,\mathrm{m}$ farther south than at 9:00. The displacement does not tell whether the cyclist changed direction during the two minutes, so the distance traveled may exceed $300\,\mathrm{m}$. Moreover, the final position also depends on the initial position, which is not given.
