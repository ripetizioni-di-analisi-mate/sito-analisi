An object travels $4$ m to the right and $8$ m to the left. What is the total distance traveled?

- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The distance traveled is the sum of the lengths of the individual legs, regardless of direction. The calculation is $4+8$. Therefore, the total distance is $12$ meters.
