Two runners travel along the same straight road without stopping or turning back. Runner A passes position $x=50\,\mathrm{m}$ at the instant $t=8\,\mathrm{s}$, and runner B passes the same position at the instant $t=10\,\mathrm{s}$. Which statement is certainly true?

- A and B meet at position $50\,\mathrm{m}$
- A and B meet at the instant $t=9\,\mathrm{s}$
- A and B do not meet at position $50\,\mathrm{m}$
- B is faster than A

## Explanation

Two objects meet when they occupy the **same position at the same instant**. Neither runner stops or turns back, so each occupies the position $50\,\mathrm{m}$ at only one instant. A and B occupy that position at two different instants, so they do not meet at that point. The data do not give the velocities of the two runners, so they do not allow us to determine who is faster or whether they meet somewhere else.
