A robot travels from A to B at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and returns at $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ with no stop. For what fraction of the total time is it heading towards A?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

For the same distance, returning at half speed takes twice as long. Of the three equal portions of travel time, two are spent returning.
