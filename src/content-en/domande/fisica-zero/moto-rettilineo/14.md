One cyclist starts at $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Six seconds later a second leaves the same point at $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the same direction. How long does the chase last after the second cyclist sets off, at constant speeds?

- $2.4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The initial lead is $2\cdot6=12$ metres. The second cyclist gains $3\,\mathrm{m}$ each second and catches the first in $4\,\mathrm{s}$.
