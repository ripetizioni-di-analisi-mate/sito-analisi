In a warehouse, a forklift starts at position $x=0\,\mathrm{m}$ along a straight aisle. It moves forward $30\,\mathrm{m}$, backs up $12\,\mathrm{m}$, and finally moves forward another $5\,\mathrm{m}$. What is its final position?

- $47\,\mathrm{m}$
- $13\,\mathrm{m}$
- $23\,\mathrm{m}$
- $35\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Moving forward gives positive displacements and backing up gives a negative displacement. The overall displacement is the sum of the three displacements, so $\Delta x=30\,\mathrm{m}-12\,\mathrm{m}+5\,\mathrm{m}=23\,\mathrm{m}$. Since the forklift starts at the origin, its final position is $x=0\,\mathrm{m}+23\,\mathrm{m}=23\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $47\,\mathrm{m}$ is instead the distance traveled.
