Two people walk at constant velocities along one road. An observer boards a train moving parallel to it. Which statement about their meeting remains valid in the new frame?

- Their individual velocities must keep their signs
- The numerical meeting coordinate must stay the same
- They are together at the same meeting event
- The train must stop to observe the meeting

## Explanation

Changing frames shifts both coordinates by the same amount at that instant. Equal coordinates therefore remain equal even if the numerical value changes.
