On a position-time graph, the lines representing two cars in the same lane are parallel and distinct. What can we conclude?

- The cars have the same velocity and meet after some time
- The cars have the same velocity and never meet
- The cars have opposite velocities
- The cars have different velocities and meet once

## Explanation

The velocity of each car corresponds to the slope of its line, so two parallel lines indicate the same velocity. The lines are distinct and parallel, so they have no points in common. A common point would mean the same position at the same instant, so the two cars never meet and the distance between them stays constant.
