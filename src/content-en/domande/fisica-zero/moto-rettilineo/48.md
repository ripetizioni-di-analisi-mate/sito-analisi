A high-speed train covers $1\,\mathrm{km}$ of straight track in $10\,\mathrm{s}$. What is its average velocity in kilometers per hour?

- $100\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $36\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $360\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $6\,\mathrm{km/h}$

## Explanation

In meters per second, the average velocity is $v_m=\dfrac{1000\,\mathrm{m}}{10\,\mathrm{s}}=100\,\mathrm{m/s}$. To convert to kilometers per hour, we multiply by $3.6$. Therefore the average velocity of the train is $100\cdot3.6\,\mathrm{km/h}=360\,\mathrm{km/h}$.
