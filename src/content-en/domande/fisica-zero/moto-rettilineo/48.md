A robot follows $x=3t$ in metres from $0\,\mathrm{s}$ to $4\,\mathrm{s}$ and then stops. What is its position at $9\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $12\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $27\,\mathrm{m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

At the end of the moving segment the robot is at $3\cdot4=12$ metres. It retains this position throughout the next five seconds.
