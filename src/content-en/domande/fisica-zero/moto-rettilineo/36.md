A person walks from the front door to the newsstand and back to the front door along a straight street. One day the person stops for five minutes to chat at the newsstand and walks as usual for the rest of the trip. What effect does the stop have on the average velocity and the average speed of the whole trip?

- The average speed and the average velocity both decrease
- The average speed and the average velocity both stay the same
- The average speed stays the same and the average velocity decreases
- The average speed decreases and the average velocity stays zero

## Explanation

The person returns to the front door, so the displacement is zero with or without the stop. Therefore the average velocity $v_m=\dfrac{\Delta x}{\Delta t}$ is zero in both cases. The distance traveled stays the same, while the total time increases by five minutes, because it also includes the time spent standing still. Consequently the average speed $u_m=\dfrac{s}{\Delta t}$ decreases.
