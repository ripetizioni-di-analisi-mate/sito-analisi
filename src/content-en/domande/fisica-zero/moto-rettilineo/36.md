A walker takes $5\,\mathrm{min}$ per kilometre. At that pace, how long will $400\,\mathrm{m}$ take?

- $1.25\,\mathrm{min}$
- $2\,\mathrm{min}$
- $12.5\,\mathrm{min}$
- $4\,\mathrm{min}$

## Explanation

Four hundred metres is two fifths of a kilometre. At constant pace the trip takes the same fraction of $5\,\mathrm{min}$, or $2\,\mathrm{min}$.
