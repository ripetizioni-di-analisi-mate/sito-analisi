On a position-time graph, a straight line passes through $(0\,\mathrm s,2\,\mathrm m)$ and $(4\,\mathrm s,26\,\mathrm m)$. What is the velocity?

- $24\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $26\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The velocity is the slope of the line on the position-time graph. The calculation is $\dfrac{26-2}{4-0}$. Therefore, the velocity is $6$ meters per second.
