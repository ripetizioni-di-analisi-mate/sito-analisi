A speedometer reads $72\,\mathrm{km/h}$. How many metres does the vehicle cover each second at this speed?

- $36\,\mathrm{m}$
- $72\,\mathrm{m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $259.2\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

One hour represents 72,000 metres of travel and contains 3,600 seconds. The distance in one second is therefore $20\,\mathrm{m}$.
