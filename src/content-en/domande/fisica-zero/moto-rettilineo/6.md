A coach sets the origin at the starting line of the $100$-meter dash and measures distances in meters. What other choice is needed for the position $x=-2\,\mathrm{m}$ to indicate a specific point on the track?

- The positive direction of the axis
- The speed of the athlete
- The duration of the race
- The mass of the athlete

## Explanation

A **reference frame** for motion along a straight line consists of an origin, a unit of length, and a positive direction. With the origin and the unit, we know that the point is $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from the starting line. However, there are two points at this distance, one on each side of the line. The positive direction determines which side the negative positions are on, so it identifies the point.
