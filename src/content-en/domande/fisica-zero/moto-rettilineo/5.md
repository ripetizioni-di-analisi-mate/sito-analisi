To study a car's trip along $3\,\mathrm{km}$ of straight highway, we follow a single point on the car, such as the center of the hood. Why is this simplification acceptable?

- The car always keeps the same speed
- The mass of the car is negligible
- The dimensions of the car are negligible compared with the distance traveled
- The highway has a fixed positive direction

## Explanation

A car is a few meters long, while the stretch being studied is $3\,\mathrm{km}$, or $3000\,\mathrm{m}$, long. The dimensions of the car are therefore negligible compared with the distance involved. Hence the position of a single point gives, to a good approximation, the location of the whole car. The speed and the mass of the car play no role in this choice.
