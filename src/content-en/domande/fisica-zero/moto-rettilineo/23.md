A bus leaves at 2:40 p.m. and arrives at its destination at 4:10 p.m. the same day. How long is the trip, in hours?

- $1.3\,\mathrm{h}$
- $1.7\,\mathrm{h}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{h}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{h}$

## Explanation

From 2:40 to 3:40 one hour passes, and from 3:40 to 4:10 another $30\,\mathrm{min}$ pass. The trip therefore lasts $1\,\mathrm{h}$ $30\,\mathrm{min}$. Thirty minutes is half an hour, or $0.5\,\mathrm{h}$, so the trip lasts $1.5\,\mathrm{h}$.
