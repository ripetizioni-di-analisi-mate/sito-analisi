Two stopwatches start at different times but run at the same rate. The second always reads $7\,\mathrm{s}$ more. What correction is needed when comparing their measured durations for the same run?

- Add $14\,\mathrm{s}$ to the first duration
- Halve the second duration
- Subtract $7\,\mathrm{s}$ from the second duration
- No correction

## Explanation

The offset appears in both endpoint readings on the second stopwatch. It cancels when the readings are subtracted.
