Two light gates are $2\,\mathrm{m}$ apart. The same trolley passes at constant speed, but only the second sensor triggers $0.1\,\mathrm{s}$ late. How is the speed computed from the raw readings affected?

- It is underestimated
- It is exact because the distance is known
- It is overestimated
- Its sign reverses

## Explanation

The delay increases the recorded travel time. Dividing the correct distance by too long a time gives too small a speed.
