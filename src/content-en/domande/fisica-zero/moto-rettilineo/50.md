A sprinter runs the $100$-meter dash at an average speed of $36\,\mathrm{km/h}$. How long does it take?

- $2.8\,\mathrm{s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.36\,\mathrm{s}$
- $360\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

In meters per second, the average speed is $u_m=\dfrac{36}{3.6}\,\mathrm{m/s}=10\,\mathrm{m/s}$. From the definition of average speed, $\Delta t=\dfrac{s}{u_m}$. Therefore
$$
\Delta t=\frac{100\,\mathrm{m}}{10\,\mathrm{m/s}}=10\,\mathrm{s}.
$$
