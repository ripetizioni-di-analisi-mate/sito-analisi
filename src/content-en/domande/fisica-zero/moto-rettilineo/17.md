Two objects are $40$ m apart and move toward each other at speeds of $4$ m/s and $4$ m/s. How long does it take them to meet?

- $5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{s}$
- $40\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The distance between the objects decreases at a rate equal to the sum of their speeds, or $8$ meters per second. The time is $\dfrac{40}{8}$. Therefore, they meet after $5$ seconds.
