A toy train moves along a straight track and undergoes a displacement of $-45\,\mathrm{cm}$, ending at position $x=30\,\mathrm{cm}$. Where did it start?

- $-15\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $75\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $15\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-75\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

From the definition of displacement, $\Delta x=x_f-x_i$, so $x_i=x_f-\Delta x$. Substituting the data gives
$$
x_i=30\,\mathrm{cm}-(-45\,\mathrm{cm})=75\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
The result is consistent with the sign of the displacement, because the train moved in the negative direction, from $75\,\mathrm{cm}$ to $30\,\mathrm{cm}$.
