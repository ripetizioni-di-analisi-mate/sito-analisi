An object moves from position $s_i=7$ m to position $s_f=28$ m. What is its displacement?

- $21\,\mathrm{m}$
- $35\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-21\,\mathrm{m}$
- $28\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Displacement is the final position minus the initial position. Substitution gives $\Delta s=28-7$. Therefore, the displacement is $21$ meters.
