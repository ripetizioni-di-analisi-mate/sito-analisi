An athlete aims to cover $600\,\mathrm{m}$ in $120\,\mathrm{s}$. The first $300\,\mathrm{m}$ take $75\,\mathrm{s}$. What average speed is required over the remaining $300\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac{20}{3}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

There are $45\,\mathrm{s}$ left. Dividing the remaining $300\,\mathrm{m}$ by $45\,\mathrm{s}$ gives $\dfrac{20}{3}$ metres per second.
