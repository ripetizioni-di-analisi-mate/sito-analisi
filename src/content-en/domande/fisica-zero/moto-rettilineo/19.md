An object moves from position $s_i=5$ m to position $s_f=20$ m. What is its displacement?

- $25\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-15\,\mathrm{m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Displacement is the final position minus the initial position. Substitution gives $\Delta s=20-5$. Therefore, the displacement is $15$ meters.
