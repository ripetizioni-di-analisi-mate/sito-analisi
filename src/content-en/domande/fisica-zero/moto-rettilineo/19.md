To time a van passing a gate, an observer records when its front and then its rear cross the gate. Which information would a point-particle model discard?

- The direction along the road
- The stopwatch’s starting reading
- The van’s length
- The gate’s position

## Explanation

The two crossings refer to different ends of the vehicle. A point model merges those ends, so it loses the distance between them.
