A cyclist rides from the café to the bakery along a straight street. Positions are measured first with the origin at the café and then with the origin at the church, on the same street and with the same positive direction. Which quantity has the same value in both reference frames?

- The displacement of the cyclist
- The initial position of the cyclist
- The final position of the cyclist
- The position of the church

## Explanation

When the origin is moved, every position changes by the same amount, namely the distance between the two origins with the appropriate sign. The initial position, the final position, and the position of the church therefore change value. The displacement is the difference between two positions, so the amount added to both cancels in the subtraction. Consequently the displacement has the same value in both reference frames.
