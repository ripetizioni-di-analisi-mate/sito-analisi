An airport baggage belt carries a backpack whose position equation is $x=2-0.5t$, with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. Which statement is correct?

- The backpack starts at $0.5\,\mathrm{m}$ and moves in the positive direction at $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- The backpack starts at $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and moves in the negative direction at $0.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- The backpack starts at the origin and moves in the negative direction at $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- The backpack starts at $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and moves in the positive direction at $0.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

We compare the given equation with the form $x=x_0+vt$ of the position equation for uniform motion. The constant term is the initial position, so $x_0=2\,\mathrm{m}$. The coefficient of $t$ is the velocity, so $v=-0.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The negative sign indicates that the backpack moves in the negative direction of the axis.
