Two people start at the same time from opposite ends A and B of a straight $90\,\mathrm{m}$ path and walk towards each other at constant speeds. The first walks twice as fast as the second. How far has the slower person walked when they meet?

- $45\,\mathrm{m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m}$
- $60\,\mathrm{m}$
- $30\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

With equal travel times the distances are in a one-to-two ratio. Divide the $90\,\mathrm{m}$ into three equal parts, one of which belongs to the slower walker.
