A bee flies along the straight edge of a flower bed, from position $x=7\,\mathrm{m}$ to position $x=-2\,\mathrm{m}$. What is its displacement?

- $-9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-5\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The **displacement** is the final position minus the initial position. Therefore
$$
\Delta x=x_f-x_i=-2\,\mathrm{m}-7\,\mathrm{m}=-9\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
The negative sign indicates that the bee moved in the negative direction of the axis.
