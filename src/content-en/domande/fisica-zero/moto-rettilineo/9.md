A shuttle stays at $x=2$ m until the clock reads $5\,\mathrm{s}$ and then travels forwards at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Where is it when the clock reads $9\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $38\,\mathrm{m}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m}$
- $22\,\mathrm{m}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The shuttle has been moving for $9-5=4$ seconds. Add the initial $2\,\mathrm{m}$ to the $16\,\mathrm{m}$ travelled.
