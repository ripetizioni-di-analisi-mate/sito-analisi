Entry and exit times for a tunnel are read from different clocks. What must be checked before subtracting the readings to find the transit time?

- That the vehicle keeps constant speed
- That both displays show the same number of decimal places
- That the entry speed is known
- That the clocks are synchronised

## Explanation

An unknown clock offset would be included in the difference. Synchronisation puts the two events on the same time scale.
