A passenger walks towards the rear of a train at $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ relative to the carriage while the train moves forwards at $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the passenger’s velocity relative to the stationary platform, taking forwards as positive?

- $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The carriage advances $8\,\mathrm{m}$ over the ground in one second. During that second the passenger moves back $2\,\mathrm{m}$ inside the carriage, giving a net advance of $6\,\mathrm{m}$ relative to the platform.
