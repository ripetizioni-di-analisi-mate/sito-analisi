A speed is given as $72$ km/h. What is it in meters per second?

- $259.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $72\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

To convert from km/h to m/s, divide by $3.6$. The calculation is $\dfrac{72}{3.6}$. Therefore, the speed is $20$ meters per second.
