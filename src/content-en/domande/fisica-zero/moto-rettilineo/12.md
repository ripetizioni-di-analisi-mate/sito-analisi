A person walks $90\,\mathrm{m}$ and returns to the starting point. The entire trip lasts $150\,\mathrm{s}$. Which pair gives average velocity followed by average speed, in m/s?

- $(0,\ 0.6)$
- $(0.6,\ 1.2)$
- $(1.2,\ 0)$
- $(0,\ 1.2)$

## Explanation

Returning to the start makes the net displacement zero. The total distance is $180\,\mathrm{m}$, which divided by $150\,\mathrm{s}$ gives $1.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
