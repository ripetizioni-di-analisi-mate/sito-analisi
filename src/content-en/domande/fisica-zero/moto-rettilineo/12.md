In which of these motions is the distance traveled equal to the magnitude of the displacement?

- A drawer pulled all the way open and then pushed halfway shut
- A shopping cart pushed along a straight aisle, always in the same direction
- An elevator that goes up to the third floor and then down to the first
- A tennis player who runs to the net and back to the baseline

## Explanation

The distance traveled equals $|\Delta x|$ when the object moves along the line without changing direction. The shopping cart always moves forward in the same direction, so the length of its path equals the distance between the starting point and the end point. The drawer, the elevator, and the tennis player instead reverse direction. For these objects, the stretch covered backward adds to the distance traveled and subtracts from the displacement, so $s>|\Delta x|$.
