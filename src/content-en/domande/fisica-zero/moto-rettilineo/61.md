A bicycle is $5\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left of the origin at $t=0$ and moves to the right at a constant speed of $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The positive direction is to the right. Which position equation describes the motion, with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds?

- $x=5+4t$
- $x=-5-4t$
- $x=4-5t$
- $x=-5+4t$

## Explanation

The initial position is to the left of the origin, that is, in the negative direction, so $x_0=-5\,\mathrm{m}$. The bicycle moves to the right, so its velocity is positive and equals $v=4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Substituting into the position equation $x=x_0+vt$ gives $x=-5+4t$.
