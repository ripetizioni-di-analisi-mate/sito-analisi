The law $x=2t-8$ uses metres and seconds. In which interval is the point strictly left of the origin, for $t\ge0$?

- $0\le t<4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $t>8\,\mathrm{s}$
- $t=4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $t>4\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Left of the origin the coordinate is negative. The condition $2t-8<0$ is equivalent to $t<4$ within the stated time domain.
