An ambulance travels at a constant speed of $90\,\mathrm{km/h}$ for $20\,\mathrm{s}$ along a straight road. How many meters does it travel?

- $500\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1800\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6480\,\mathrm{m}$
- $50\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

To combine the speed with a time in seconds, the speed must be expressed in meters per second. Therefore $90\,\mathrm{km/h}=\dfrac{90}{3.6}\,\mathrm{m/s}=25\,\mathrm{m/s}$. In uniform motion, the displacement is the velocity times the time interval, so the ambulance travels $25\,\mathrm{m/s}\cdot20\,\mathrm{s}=500\,\mathrm{m}$.
