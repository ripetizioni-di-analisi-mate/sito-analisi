The position-time graph of a cart moving on a horizontal track is a rising slanted segment. Which statement is correct?

- The cart moves at constant velocity in the positive direction
- The cart is climbing an inclined track
- The cart is at rest
- The velocity of the cart increases over time

## Explanation

The position-time graph shows position as a function of time and does not depict the trajectory. A straight-line segment corresponds to uniform motion, so the velocity of the cart is constant. The segment is rising, so the position increases over time and the velocity is positive. The slant of the graph therefore indicates the velocity, not the slope of the track.
