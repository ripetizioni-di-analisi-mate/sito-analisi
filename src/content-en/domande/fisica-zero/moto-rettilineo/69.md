A commuter averages the speeds of several legs without weighting them. What condition would make this procedure exact for any set of speeds?

- Every leg is travelled in the same direction
- All legs cover equal distance
- The speeds on successive legs increase in equal increments
- All legs take equal time

## Explanation

Each leg’s speed contributes to average speed in proportion to its duration. Equal durations give equal weights.
