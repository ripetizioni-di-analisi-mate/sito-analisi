A tractor moves at constant velocity along a straight dirt road: at the instant $2\,\mathrm{s}$ it is at $x=7\,\mathrm{m}$, and at the instant $6\,\mathrm{s}$ it is at $x=19\,\mathrm{m}$. Where does its position-time graph meet the position axis, that is, where was the tractor at $t=0$?

- $7\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-5\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The velocity is
$$
v=\frac{19\,\mathrm{m}-7\,\mathrm{m}}{6\,\mathrm{s}-2\,\mathrm{s}}=3\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
Between $t=0$ and $t=2\,\mathrm{s}$ the tractor therefore moves $3\,\mathrm{m/s}\cdot2\,\mathrm{s}=6\,\mathrm{m}$. Hence at $t=0$ it was at $x_0=7\,\mathrm{m}-6\,\mathrm{m}=1\,\mathrm{m}$, which is the height of the line at $t=0$.
