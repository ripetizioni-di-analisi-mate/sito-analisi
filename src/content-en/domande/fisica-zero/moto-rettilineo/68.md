A robot travels along a line at $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ from second 3 to second 9. What is the area of the rectangle under this segment of the velocity–time graph?

- $12\,\mathrm{m}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $24\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The base is the elapsed $6\,\mathrm{s}$ and the height is $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Their product is $12\,\mathrm{m}$ and represents displacement.
