A light gate switches on when a stopwatch reads $1\,\mathrm{min}$ $20\,\mathrm{s}$ and off when it reads $2\,\mathrm{min}$ $5\,\mathrm{s}$. How long is it on?

- $85\,\mathrm{s}$
- $125\,\mathrm{s}$
- $45\,\mathrm{s}$
- $75\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The readings correspond to $80\,\mathrm{s}$ and $125\,\mathrm{s}$. Subtracting the first reading gives a duration of $45\,\mathrm{s}$.
