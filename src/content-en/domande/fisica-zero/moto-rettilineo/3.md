A speed is given as $54$ km/h. What is it in meters per second?

- $194.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $54\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

To convert from km/h to m/s, divide by $3.6$. The calculation is $\dfrac{54}{3.6}$. Therefore, the speed is $15$ meters per second.
