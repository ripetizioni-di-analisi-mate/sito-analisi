Which of these motions has a straight-line trajectory?

- The tip of the minute hand of a wall clock
- A Ferris wheel car during one revolution
- A ball chipped toward the goal
- An apple that comes off a branch and falls, with no wind

## Explanation

Motion is **rectilinear** when the trajectory, that is, the set of points the object occupies, lies along a straight line. The apple starts from rest and falls downward with no sideways push, so all the points it occupies lie on a vertical line. The tip of the minute hand and the Ferris wheel car instead travel along circles. The chipped ball rises and then falls along a curved path. Therefore only the apple has a straight-line trajectory.
