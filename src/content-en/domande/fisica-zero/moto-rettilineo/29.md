A delivery van passes position $x=120\,\mathrm{m}$ when the clock reads $t=20\,\mathrm{s}$, and position $x=480\,\mathrm{m}$ when it reads $t=80\,\mathrm{s}$. What is its average velocity over this interval?

- $4.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The displacement is $\Delta x=480\,\mathrm{m}-120\,\mathrm{m}=360\,\mathrm{m}$. The time interval is the final instant minus the initial instant, so $\Delta t=80\,\mathrm{s}-20\,\mathrm{s}=60\,\mathrm{s}$. Therefore
$$
v_m=\frac{360\,\mathrm{m}}{60\,\mathrm{s}}=6\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
