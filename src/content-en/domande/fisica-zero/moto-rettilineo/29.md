An object's displacement is $72$ m in $4$ s. What is its average velocity?

- $18\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $288\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac{1}{18}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $72\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Average velocity is displacement divided by the time interval. Substitution gives $v_m=\dfrac{72}{4}$. Therefore, $v_m=18$ meters per second.
