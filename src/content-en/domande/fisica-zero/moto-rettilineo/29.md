A shuttle covers half its route at $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the other half at $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the average speed with no stops?

- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Take two legs of $6\,\mathrm{m}$ each. They take $2\,\mathrm{s}$ and $1\,\mathrm{s}$, so $12\,\mathrm{m}$ is covered in $3\,\mathrm{s}$.
