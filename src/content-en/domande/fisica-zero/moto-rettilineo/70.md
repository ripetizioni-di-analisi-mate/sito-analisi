The position-time graph describes the motion of a child on a scooter along a straight sidewalk. What distance does the child travel during the $10\,\mathrm{s}$ shown?

::grafico{linee="0,0 4,8 6,8 10,0" punti="0,0;4,8;6,8;10,0" xmin=0 xmax=11 ymin=0 ymax=9 nomi="t,x" legenda=0 titolo="Position $x$ of the child, in meters, as a function of time $t$, in seconds."}

- $16\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $24\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

On the first stretch, the child goes from $0\,\mathrm{m}$ to $8\,\mathrm{m}$, so he covers $8\,\mathrm{m}$. Between $4\,\mathrm{s}$ and $6\,\mathrm{s}$ the graph is horizontal, so the child is at rest. On the last stretch he returns from $8\,\mathrm{m}$ to $0\,\mathrm{m}$ and covers another $8\,\mathrm{m}$. The distance traveled is therefore $8\,\mathrm{m}+8\,\mathrm{m}=16\,\mathrm{m}$, while the overall displacement is zero.
