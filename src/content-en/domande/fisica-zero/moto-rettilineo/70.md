An app gives only the magnitude of average velocity, the same for two runs along a line. They last $3\,\mathrm{min}$ and $9\,\mathrm{min}$. What is the ratio of the displacement magnitudes, second run to first?

- $9$
- $1$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

Displacement magnitude equals the magnitude of average velocity multiplied by duration. With the same average-velocity magnitude, tripling the duration triples the product.
