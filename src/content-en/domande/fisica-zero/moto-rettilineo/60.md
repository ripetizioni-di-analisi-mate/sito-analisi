A cart moves along a straight track at a constant velocity of $0.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. At the instant $t_0=10\,\mathrm{s}$ it is at position $x_0=2\,\mathrm{m}$. At what instant does it reach position $x=14\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $t=30\,\mathrm{s}$
- $t=45\,\mathrm{s}$
- $t=35\,\mathrm{s}$
- $t=40\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

From the position equation $x=x_0+v(t-t_0)$ we obtain the time spent moving, $t-t_0=\dfrac{x-x_0}{v}$. Therefore
$$
t-t_0=\frac{14\,\mathrm{m}-2\,\mathrm{m}}{0.4\,\mathrm{m/s}}=30\,\mathrm{s}.
$$
The cart was at $x_0$ at the instant $10\,\mathrm{s}$, so it reaches position $14\,\mathrm{m}$ at the instant $t=10\,\mathrm{s}+30\,\mathrm{s}=40\,\mathrm{s}$.
