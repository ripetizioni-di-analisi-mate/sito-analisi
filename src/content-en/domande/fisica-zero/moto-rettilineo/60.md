Two shuttles must reach their terminal at the same time. A travels $12\,\mathrm{km}$ at $24\,\mathrm{km/h}$ and B travels $3\,\mathrm{km}$ at $12\,\mathrm{km/h}$. When should B depart relative to A?

- At the same instant
- $15\,\mathrm{min}$ earlier
- $30\,\mathrm{min}$ later
- $15\,\mathrm{min}$ later

## Explanation

A’s trip takes $30\,\mathrm{min}$ and B’s takes $15\,\mathrm{min}$. For a joint arrival, B must depart when A has $15\,\mathrm{min}$ left.
