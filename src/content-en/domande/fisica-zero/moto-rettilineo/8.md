Along the straight edge of a swimming pool, the origin is at the ladder. The lifeguard is at $x=-6\,\mathrm{m}$ and a swimmer is at $x=4\,\mathrm{m}$. Which statement is correct?

- The swimmer is farther from the ladder than the lifeguard
- The lifeguard is farther from the ladder than the swimmer
- The lifeguard and the swimmer are the same distance from the ladder
- The lifeguard and the swimmer are on the same side of the ladder

## Explanation

The absolute value of the position is the distance from the origin, while the sign tells which side of the origin the point is on. The lifeguard is $|-6\,\mathrm{m}|=6\,\mathrm{m}$ from the ladder and the swimmer is $4\,\mathrm{m}$ away, so the lifeguard is farther. The two positions have opposite signs, so the two people are on opposite sides of the ladder.
