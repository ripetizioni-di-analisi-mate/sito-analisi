An object starts at $x=24$ m at $t=0$ and maintains a velocity of $-6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. When does it cross the origin?

- $144\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $18\,\mathrm{s}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The position in metres is $24-6t$. It is zero at the origin, so it takes $4\,\mathrm{s}$ to cover the initial gap of $24\,\mathrm{m}$.
