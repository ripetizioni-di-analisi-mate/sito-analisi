A mail carrier leaves the post office, at $x=0\,\mathrm{m}$, at $t=0$ and walks at $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ toward a house at $x=900\,\mathrm{m}$. At $t=150\,\mathrm{s}$ the resident leaves the house and walks toward the mail carrier at $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$ along the same straight street. At what instant do they meet?

- $t=350\,\mathrm{s}$
- $t=300\,\mathrm{s}$
- $t=450\,\mathrm{s}$
- $t=200\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The mail carrier's position equation is $x_P=2t$, with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. The resident starts from $900\,\mathrm{m}$ at the instant $150\,\mathrm{s}$ and moves in the negative direction, so for $t\geq150\,\mathrm{s}$ the resident's position equation is $x_R=900-(t-150)$. They meet when $2t=900-(t-150)$, that is, $2t=1050-t$. Therefore $3t=1050$ and $t=350\,\mathrm{s}$, which comes after $150\,\mathrm{s}$ and is therefore acceptable.
