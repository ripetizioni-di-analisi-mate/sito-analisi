Can a trolley have a positive average velocity even if it travels left for most of the time, taking right as positive?

- No, the sign always follows the longer time
- Yes, only if the two distances are equal
- No, any reversal makes the average zero
- Yes, if its rightward distance exceeds its leftward distance

## Explanation

A short fast rightward run can cover more distance than a long slow leftward run. The sign of average velocity follows the net displacement.
