A van appears stationary in a photograph taken from a vehicle beside it. Which extra observation would measure its average velocity relative to the road?

- Its positions relative to two road marks and their passing times
- A single measurement of the van’s distance from a road sign
- A single reading of lateral separation
- Two positions relative to the vehicle beside it without knowing that vehicle’s motion

## Explanation

The marks belong to the road frame. Their coordinates and passing times give displacement and elapsed time in that frame, and hence the average velocity.
