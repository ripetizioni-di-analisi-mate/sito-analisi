An object travels $105$ m at a constant velocity of $7$ m/s. How long does it take?

- $15\,\mathrm{s}$
- $735\,\mathrm{s}$
- $7\,\mathrm{s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

For uniform motion, $\Delta s=v\Delta t$. Divide the displacement by the velocity. Therefore, $\Delta t=\dfrac{105}{7}=15$ seconds.
