A trolley goes from $x=-10$ m to $x=-2$ m without turning. How does its distance from the origin change during the trip?

- It stays constant because both coordinates are negative
- It increases while the coordinate increases
- It decreases while the coordinate increases
- It decreases while the coordinate decreases

## Explanation

The coordinate magnitude falls from $10\,\mathrm{m}$ to $2\,\mathrm{m}$. Its algebraic value changes from $-10$ to $-2$ and therefore increases.
