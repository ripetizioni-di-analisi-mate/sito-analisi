A trolleybus travels along a straight avenue toward the station at a constant speed of $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Observer A chooses the direction toward the station as positive, while observer B chooses the opposite direction. What velocities do they assign to the trolleybus?

- $+10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for both A and B
- $-10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for A and $+10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for B
- $+10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for A and $0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for B
- $+10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for A and $-10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for B

## Explanation

The sign of the velocity indicates whether the object moves in the positive or the negative direction of the chosen axis. For A the trolleybus moves in the positive direction, so its velocity is $+10\,\mathrm{m/s}$. For B the same motion is in the negative direction, so the velocity is $-10\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The magnitude is the same for both observers, because the two reference frames differ only in direction.
