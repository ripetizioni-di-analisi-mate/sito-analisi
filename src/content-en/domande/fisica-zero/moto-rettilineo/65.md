A graph labelled total distance travelled drops from $12\,\mathrm{m}$ to $9\,\mathrm{m}$, although the counter is working and never resets. Why is this incompatible with its label?

- Accumulated path length can only increase or stay constant
- The initial reading must always be zero
- An object cannot move towards smaller coordinates
- Distance must increase at a constant rate

## Explanation

A return leg still adds its length to the total. Position can decrease, while distance already travelled remains in the total.
