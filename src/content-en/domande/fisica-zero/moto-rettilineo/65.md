The position-time graph describes the motion of a robot vacuum along a straight hallway. During which time interval is the robot at rest?

::grafico{linee="0,0 2,6 5,6 8,0" punti="0,0;2,6;5,6;8,0" xmin=0 xmax=9 ymin=0 ymax=7 nomi="t,x" legenda=0 titolo="Position $x$ of the robot, in meters, as a function of time $t$, in seconds."}

- Between $0\,\mathrm{s}$ and $2\,\mathrm{s}$
- Between $5\,\mathrm{s}$ and $8\,\mathrm{s}$
- Between $2\,\mathrm{s}$ and $5\,\mathrm{s}$
- Only at the instant $8\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

On a position-time graph, a horizontal stretch means that the position does not change, so the object is at rest. Between $2\,\mathrm{s}$ and $5\,\mathrm{s}$ the graph stays at a height of $6\,\mathrm{m}$, so the robot is at rest at that position. On the other two stretches the position changes over time. At the instant $8\,\mathrm{s}$ the robot is at the origin, but a zero position only tells where the robot is, not that the robot is at rest.
