In a relay race, the clock is reset to zero when the first runner hands the baton to the second. On that clock, the first runner had started at the instant $t=-48\,\mathrm{s}$. How long did the first leg last?

- $48\,\mathrm{s}$
- $-48\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $96\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The first runner's leg begins at the instant $t_i=-48\,\mathrm{s}$ and ends at the handoff, $t_f=0\,\mathrm{s}$. The time interval is the final instant minus the initial instant, so $\Delta t=0\,\mathrm{s}-(-48\,\mathrm{s})=48\,\mathrm{s}$. A negative instant simply indicates a moment before the clock was reset, while the time interval is positive.
