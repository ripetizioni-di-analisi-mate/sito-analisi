When does the total distance travelled equal the magnitude of displacement for a whole journey along a line?

- It keeps the same speed even when reversing
- It keeps the same direction of travel, possibly stopping
- It finishes at rest
- The return leg takes less time than the outward one

## Explanation

Every section of travel must contribute with the same sign to displacement. A stop adds no distance and leaves this condition intact.
