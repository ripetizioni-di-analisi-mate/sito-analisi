An object travels $5$ m to the right and $10$ m to the left. What is the total distance traveled?

- $15\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The distance traveled is the sum of the lengths of the individual legs, regardless of direction. The calculation is $5+10$. Therefore, the total distance is $15$ meters.
