A walk consists of $40\,\mathrm{m}$ in $20\,\mathrm{s}$ followed by a $5\,\mathrm{s}$ stop. What is the average speed from the start until the end of the stop?

- $0.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The stop brings the elapsed time to $25\,\mathrm{s}$. The distance remains $40\,\mathrm{m}$, so the average speed is $\dfrac{40}{25}=1.6$ metres per second.
