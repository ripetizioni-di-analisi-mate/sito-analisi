A swimmer starts at one end of a $25\,\mathrm{m}$ pool, swims to the opposite end, and returns to the starting point. What are the swimmer's displacement and distance traveled?

- $\Delta x=50\,\mathrm{m}$ and $s=50\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\Delta x=25\,\mathrm{m}$ and $s=50\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\Delta x=0\,\mathrm{m}$ and $s=50\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\Delta x=0\,\mathrm{m}$ and $s=0\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The swimmer finishes at the starting point, so the final position equals the initial position and $\Delta x=0\,\mathrm{m}$. The distance traveled, on the other hand, adds the lengths of the two legs, each $25\,\mathrm{m}$ long. Therefore $s=25\,\mathrm{m}+25\,\mathrm{m}=50\,\mathrm{m}$.
