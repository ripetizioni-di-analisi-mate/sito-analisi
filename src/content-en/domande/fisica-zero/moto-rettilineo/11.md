An object's displacement is $48$ m in $4$ s. What is its average velocity?

- $192\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac{1}{12}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $48\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Average velocity is displacement divided by the time interval. Substitution gives $v_m=\dfrac{48}{4}$. Therefore, $v_m=12$ meters per second.
