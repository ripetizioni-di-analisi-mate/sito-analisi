A sensor records every displacement and every elapsed time as twice its true value. By what factor does the velocity calculated from those readings differ from the true velocity?

- $2$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $4$

## Explanation

The same scale factor appears in the numerator and denominator of displacement divided by time. Cancelling it recovers the true velocity.
