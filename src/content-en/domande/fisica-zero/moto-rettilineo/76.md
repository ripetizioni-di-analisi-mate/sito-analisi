The position-time graph describes a young man jogging along the straight shore of a lake. What is his average velocity over the interval from $0\,\mathrm{s}$ to $8\,\mathrm{s}$?

::grafico{linee="0,0 4,10 8,4" punti="0,0;4,10;8,4" xmin=0 xmax=9 ymin=0 ymax=11 nomi="t,x" legenda=0 titolo="Position $x$ of the jogger, in meters, as a function of time $t$, in seconds."}

- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.25\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The average velocity depends only on the overall displacement and the time interval. From the graph, the position at the instant $0\,\mathrm{s}$ is $0\,\mathrm{m}$ and the position at the instant $8\,\mathrm{s}$ is $4\,\mathrm{m}$, so $\Delta x=4\,\mathrm{m}$. Therefore
$$
v_m=\frac{4\,\mathrm{m}}{8\,\mathrm{s}}=0.5\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
The value $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is instead the average speed, which uses the distance traveled, $10\,\mathrm{m}+6\,\mathrm{m}=16\,\mathrm{m}$.
