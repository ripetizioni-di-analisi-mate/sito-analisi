On a straight road, a truck travels at $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and is $300\,\mathrm{m}$ ahead of a car. What constant velocity must the car have, in the same direction, to catch the truck in $60\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $17\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

We place the origin at the car's initial position. The position equations are $x_C=vt$ for the car and $x_T=300+12t$ for the truck, with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. The car catches the truck at the instant $60\,\mathrm{s}$ if $60v=300+12\cdot60=1020$. Therefore
$$
v=\frac{1020\,\mathrm{m}}{60\,\mathrm{s}}=17\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
