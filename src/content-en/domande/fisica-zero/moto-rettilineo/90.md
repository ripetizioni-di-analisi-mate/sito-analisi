A trolley travels back and forth between marks $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $8\,\mathrm{m}$ on one track. After it has covered the whole stretch at least once, which description gives its trajectory?

- A line as long as the sum of all passes
- The graph of position against time
- The segment from $2\,\mathrm{m}$ to $8\,\mathrm{m}$
- Only the two end marks

## Explanation

A trajectory is the set of positions visited. Repeated passes increase the total distance travelled while leaving the set of visited points equal to that segment.
