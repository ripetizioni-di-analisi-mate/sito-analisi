An object travels $3$ m to the right and $6$ m to the left. What is the total distance traveled?

- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The distance traveled is the sum of the lengths of the individual legs, regardless of direction. The calculation is $3+6$. Therefore, the total distance is $9$ meters.
