In a building, each floor is $3\,\mathrm{m}$ high. The ground-floor level is chosen as the origin and upward as the positive direction. What is the position of the floor of the second basement level?

- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The floor of the second basement level is two floors below the ground floor. Its distance from the origin is therefore $2\cdot3\,\mathrm{m}=6\,\mathrm{m}$. Since the point is below the origin and the positive direction is upward, its position is negative. Hence the position is $x=-6\,\mathrm{m}$.
