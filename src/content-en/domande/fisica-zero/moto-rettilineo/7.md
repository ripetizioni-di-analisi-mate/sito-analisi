A trolley starts at the $-5\,\mathrm{m}$ mark and maintains a velocity of $+3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Which mark does it reach after $4\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $-17\,\mathrm{m}$
- $7\,\mathrm{m}$
- $17\,\mathrm{m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The trolley advances $12\,\mathrm{m}$. Adding this displacement to the starting position gives $-5+12=7$ metres.
