A washing machine starts at 10:47 a.m., and the selected program lasts $95\,\mathrm{min}$. At what time does the wash end?

- At 12:22 p.m.
- At 11:22 a.m.
- At 11:42 a.m.
- At 12:42 p.m.

## Explanation

Ninety-five minutes is $60\,\mathrm{min}+35\,\mathrm{min}$, that is, $1\,\mathrm{h}$ $35\,\mathrm{min}$. Adding one hour to 10:47 a.m. gives 11:47 a.m. From 11:47 to 12:00 there are $13\,\mathrm{min}$, so $22$ of the $35\,\mathrm{min}$ remain after noon. Therefore the wash ends at 12:22 p.m.
