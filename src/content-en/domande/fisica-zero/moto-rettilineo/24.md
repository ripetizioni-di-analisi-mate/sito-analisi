An object moves $5$ m to the right and then $5$ m to the left. What is its total displacement?

- $10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The object ends up back at its initial position. The difference between the final and initial positions is therefore zero. The total displacement is $0$ meters, while the distance traveled is $10$ meters.
