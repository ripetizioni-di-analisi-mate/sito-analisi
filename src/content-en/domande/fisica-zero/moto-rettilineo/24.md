A bicycle’s distance counter starts at zero and later reads $600\,\mathrm{m}$. Without knowing the endpoint, what can be said about the displacement magnitude?

- At least $600\,\mathrm{m}$
- At most $600\,\mathrm{m}$
- Exactly $300\,\mathrm{m}$
- Exactly $600\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Every segment contributes its length to distance. Opposite segments cancel in displacement, so its magnitude can only be less than or equal to $600\,\mathrm{m}$.
