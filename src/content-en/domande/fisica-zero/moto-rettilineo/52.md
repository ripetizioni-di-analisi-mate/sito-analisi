A car travels at $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$. A sign gives a speed limit of $60\,\mathrm{km/h}$. By how much is the car below the limit?

- $4\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $6\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $45\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $15\,\mathrm{km/h}$

## Explanation

Express the car’s speed in kilometres per hour. It is $15\cdot3.6=54$ kilometres per hour, leaving a margin of $6\,\mathrm{km/h}$.
