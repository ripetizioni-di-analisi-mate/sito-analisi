A race finishes at the $100\,\mathrm{m}$ mark. A runner is at the $99\,\mathrm{m}$ mark when the clock shows $14\,\mathrm{s}$ and at the $103\,\mathrm{m}$ mark when it shows $15\,\mathrm{s}$. Assuming uniform motion between readings, how long after the first reading does the runner finish?

- $0.25\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.75\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Between readings the runner covers $4\,\mathrm{m}$ in $1\,\mathrm{s}$. The finish is $1\,\mathrm{m}$ beyond the first position, one quarter of the total distance. At constant speed the runner takes one quarter of the second, or $0.25\,\mathrm{s}$.
