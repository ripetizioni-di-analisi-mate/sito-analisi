Droplets form on cold glass as humid room air passes it. Taking the vapour that forms the drops as the system, which description gives the change and the sign of the heat?

- Vaporisation with heat absorbed.
- Condensation with heat released.
- Condensation with heat absorbed.
- Melting with heat released.

## Explanation

The water changes from vapour to liquid. Condensation transfers energy out of the chosen system, so its heat transfer is negative.
