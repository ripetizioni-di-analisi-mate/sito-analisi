A heater delivers a constant net thermal power of $50\,\mathrm{W}$ to a sample with a heat capacity of $500\,\mathrm{J/K}$. The sample stays in one phase. How long does it take to warm by $6\,\mathrm{K}$?

- $60\,\mathrm{s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{s}$
- $300\,\mathrm{s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The energy required is $500\cdot 6=3000$ joules. At $50\,\mathrm{J}$ each second, the time is $\dfrac{3000}{50}=60$ seconds.
