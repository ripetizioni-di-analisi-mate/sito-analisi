An object absorbs $4200$ J and has a heat capacity of $200$ J/K. By how much does its temperature rise?

- $840000\,\mathrm{K}$
- $4200\,\mathrm{K}$
- $\dfrac{7}{3}\,\mathrm{K}$
- $21\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

The heat capacity satisfies $Q=C\Delta T$. Therefore $\Delta T=\dfrac{Q}{C}$. This gives $\Delta T=\dfrac{4200}{200}=21$ kelvins.
