A heater delivers $12\,\mathrm{kJ}$ to a bath and the sample stores $9\,\mathrm{kJ}$. What percentage of the supplied energy remains in the sample?

- $75\,\%$
- $25\,\%$
- $133\,\%$
- $3\,\%$

## Explanation

The required fraction is the sample’s energy divided by the energy supplied, so $\dfrac{9}{12}=0.75$, or $75\,\%$.
