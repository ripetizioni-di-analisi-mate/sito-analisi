A down jacket keeps its wearer warm on a winter day. Which statement correctly describes its effect?

- It produces energy and transfers it to the body
- It reduces the energy the body transfers to the cold air
- It stops the cold of the air from getting to the body
- It increases the specific heat capacity of the body

## Explanation

The body is warmer than the winter air, so it transfers energy to its surroundings. The down jacket is an insulating material that reduces this transfer, just as an insulated container reduces the heat transfer from a hot liquid to the outside. The body therefore loses energy more slowly. Heat flows spontaneously from the object at the higher temperature to the one at the lower temperature, so there is no "cold" getting in.
