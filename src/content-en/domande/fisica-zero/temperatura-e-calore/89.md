A water bottle has an ideal wall that lets heat pass but prevents mass transfer. Which observation alone is consistent with this description when the bottle is placed in a warmer bath?

- The absence of mass transfer alone forces the temperature to stay constant.
- The water loses mass for each joule received by the definition of heat.
- The water mass remains constant while its temperature rises.
- The water gains mass because heat carries water through the wall.

## Explanation

Energy can cross the wall because of the temperature difference. Since material remains confined, the water can warm without a change in mass.
