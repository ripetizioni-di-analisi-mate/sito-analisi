A plate and its holder have heat capacities of $300\,\mathrm{J/K}$ and $200\,\mathrm{J/K}$. They stay at the same temperature. How much energy do they store together for a common rise of $4\,\mathrm{K}$?

- $1.2\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $2\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $0.4\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $0.8\,\mathrm{kJ}$

## Explanation

Both parts warm up, so their heat capacities add to $500\,\mathrm{J/K}$. Multiplying by $4\,\mathrm{K}$ gives $2000\,\mathrm{J}$.
