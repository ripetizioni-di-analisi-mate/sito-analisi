A sample of mass $6$ kg has a specific heat capacity of $300$ joules per kilogram per kelvin. What is its heat capacity?

- $50\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $0.02\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $306\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $1800\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

The heat capacity is $C=mc$. It depends on the mass of the object and the specific heat capacity of the material. Therefore $C=6\cdot300=1800$ joules per kelvin.
