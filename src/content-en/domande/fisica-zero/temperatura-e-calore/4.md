A cup of tea at $80\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ is forgotten on a desk in a room whose temperature stays constant at $22\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. What is the temperature of the tea after many hours?

- $51\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $58\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $18\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $22\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

The tea is warmer than the air in the room, so it transfers energy to its surroundings and cools. The transfer continues as long as there is a temperature difference. The room temperature stays constant, so given enough time the tea reaches **thermal equilibrium** with its surroundings at $22\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. The value $51\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ is the average of the two initial temperatures, and it would be obtained only if the room had the same heat capacity as the tea.
