An object absorbs $1800$ J and has a heat capacity of $200$ J/K. By how much does its temperature rise?

- $360000\,\mathrm{K}$
- $1800\,\mathrm{K}$
- $1\,\mathrm{K}$
- $9\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

The heat capacity satisfies $Q=C\Delta T$. Therefore $\Delta T=\dfrac{Q}{C}$. This gives $\Delta T=\dfrac{1800}{200}=9$ kelvins.
