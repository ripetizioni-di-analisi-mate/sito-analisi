How much heat must $0.30\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water receive to warm from $10\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ to $40\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ without losses? Use a constant specific heat capacity of $4180\,\mathrm{J/(kg\cdot K)}$.

- $125.4\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $50.16\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $12.54\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $37.62\,\mathrm{kJ}$

## Explanation

The rise is $30\,\mathrm{K}$. Multiplying mass, specific heat capacity and temperature rise gives $0.30\cdot 4180\cdot 30=37620$ joules, or $37.62\,\mathrm{kJ}$.
