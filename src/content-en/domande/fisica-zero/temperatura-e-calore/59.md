Water with a mass of $0.50\,\mathrm{kg}$, at $90\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$, is poured into a $0.50\,\mathrm{kg}$ aluminum pot that is at $20\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. Neglecting heat transfer to the air, about what common temperature do they reach? Use $c_a=4180\,\mathrm{J/(kg\cdot K)}$ and $c_{\mathrm{Al}}=900\,\mathrm{J/(kg\cdot K)}$.

- $55\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $90\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $78\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $32\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

The pot takes part in the exchange, so its heat is one of the two terms in the balance. The heat capacities are $C_a=0.50\cdot4180\,\mathrm{J/K}=2090\,\mathrm{J/K}$ and $C_{\mathrm{Al}}=0.50\cdot900\,\mathrm{J/K}=450\,\mathrm{J/K}$. Therefore,
$$
t_e=\frac{2090\cdot90+450\cdot20}{2090+450}=\frac{197\,100}{2540}\approx78 .
$$
The common temperature is about $78\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$, close to the initial temperature of the water, because for equal masses water has a much larger heat capacity. The value $55\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$, the simple average, ignores the difference between the specific heat capacities.
