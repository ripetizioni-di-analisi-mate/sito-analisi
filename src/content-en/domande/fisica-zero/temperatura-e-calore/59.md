During a complete melting experiment, an ideal heater is changed from $80\,\mathrm{W}$ to $160\,\mathrm{W}$ for a second run with the same mass, pressure and initial state. How do plateau duration and energy absorbed in melting compare?

- Twice the duration, the same energy.
- Half the duration, the same energy.
- Half the duration and half the energy.
- The same duration, twice the energy.

## Explanation

Complete melting requires the same $mL_f$. Delivering that energy at twice the power takes half the time.
