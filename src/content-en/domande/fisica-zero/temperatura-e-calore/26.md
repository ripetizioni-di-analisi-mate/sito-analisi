In three successive intervals a block absorbs $600\,\mathrm{J}$, releases $850\,\mathrm{J}$ and absorbs $100\,\mathrm{J}$. What is its signed net heat intake?

- $1550\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-150\,\mathrm{J}$
- $150\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-350\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Taking energy entering as positive gives $600-850+100=-150$ joules. Overall, the block has released $150\,\mathrm{J}$.
