A single-phase sample receives two identical energy pulses with no losses. The first raises its temperature by $6\,\mathrm{K}$ and the second by $3\,\mathrm{K}$. What do the results show about the average heat capacities in the two intervals?

- The second is half the first.
- The second is twice the first.
- They are equal.
- The second is negative.

## Explanation

Average heat capacity is energy received divided by temperature rise. In the second interval the numerator is the same and the denominator halves, so the capacity doubles.
