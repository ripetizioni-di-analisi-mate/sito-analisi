A temperature rises by $7$ degrees Celsius. What is the increase in kelvins?

- $280.15\,\mathrm{K}$
- $7\,\mathrm{K}$
- $266\,\mathrm{K}$
- $70\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

The Kelvin scale differs from the Celsius scale by an additive constant. When two temperatures are subtracted, the constant cancels. Therefore a rise of $7$ degrees Celsius equals a rise of $7$ kelvins.
