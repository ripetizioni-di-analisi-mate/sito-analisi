A pot holds $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water for pasta. The water absorbs $627\,000\,\mathrm{J}$ and reaches $100\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$, not yet boiling. What was its initial temperature? Use $c_a=4180\,\mathrm{J/(kg\cdot K)}$ and neglect the pot.

- $75\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $25\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $175\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $-50\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

The specific heat equation gives the temperature increase
$$
\Delta T=\frac{Q}{mc_a}=\frac{627\,000\,\mathrm{J}}{2.0\,\mathrm{kg}\cdot4180\,\mathrm{J/(kg\cdot K)}}=75\,\mathrm{K}.
$$
The increase is the final temperature minus the initial temperature, so the initial temperature is $100-75=25\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. The value $75\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ confuses the increase with the starting temperature.
