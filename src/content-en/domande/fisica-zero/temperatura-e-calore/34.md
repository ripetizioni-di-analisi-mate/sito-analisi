Two equal masses of water at $60$ and $80$ degrees Celsius are mixed without heat loss or a change of state. What is the final temperature in degrees Celsius?

- $140$
- $70$
- $60$
- $80$

## Explanation

The masses are equal and have the same specific heat capacity. The energy balance therefore gives the arithmetic mean of the two temperatures. This gives $\dfrac{60+80}{2}=70$ degrees Celsius.
