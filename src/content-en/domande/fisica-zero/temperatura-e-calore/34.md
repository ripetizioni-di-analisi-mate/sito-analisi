A homogeneous block is cut into four equal pieces without changing its material. For one piece, how do specific heat capacity and heat capacity compare with those of the original block?

- Same specific heat capacity, one quarter of the heat capacity.
- Both stay the same.
- One quarter of the specific heat capacity, same heat capacity.
- Both fall to one quarter.

## Explanation

Specific heat capacity is a property of the material under the stated conditions. Heat capacity is the product $mc$, so it decreases in proportion to the mass.
