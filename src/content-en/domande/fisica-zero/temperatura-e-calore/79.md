During a run on a hot day, sweat evaporates from the skin. Why does this cool the body?

- The sweat transfers cold to the skin as it evaporates
- Evaporation lowers the specific heat capacity of the skin
- The vapor that forms releases energy to the skin
- The evaporating water absorbs energy from the skin in order to turn into vapor

## Explanation

Evaporation is a change from liquid to vapor, and a liquid that vaporizes absorbs energy, as the relation $Q=mL_v$ with $Q>0$ shows. This energy is taken from the objects in contact with the liquid, in particular from the skin. The skin therefore releases energy and its temperature tends to drop. Evaporation occurs at the surface of the sweat even though its temperature is far below the boiling point.
