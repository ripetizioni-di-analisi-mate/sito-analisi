Two identical samples receive the same $100\,\mathrm{W}$ radiation beam for $30\,\mathrm{s}$. The first absorbs $60\,\%$ of the incident energy and the second $20\,\%$. By how much do their absorbed energies differ?

- $1.8\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $2.4\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $0.4\,\mathrm{kJ}$

## Explanation

Each sample receives $3000\,\mathrm{J}$ of incident energy. The absorbed fractions differ by 0.40, giving an energy difference of $0.40\cdot 3000=1200$ joules.
