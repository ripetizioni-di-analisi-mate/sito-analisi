Identical wet and dry cloths cover two probes at the same initial temperature as the surrounding air. Both receive the same flow of unsaturated air and there is no other energy source. Which probe initially cools by evaporation?

- The dry-cloth probe, because air can pass through it more easily.
- The wet-cloth probe, because evaporating water draws energy from the cloth and probe.
- Both equally, because the airflow is the same.
- Neither, because water must reach its boiling point to evaporate.

## Explanation

Water in the wet cloth can evaporate below its boiling point. The energy for vaporisation initially comes from the cloth and probe. The dry cloth contains no water to evaporate.
