Two beakers contain equal masses of water, one at $60\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ and one at $20\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. The water is mixed in an insulated vessel of negligible heat capacity. With constant specific heat capacity, what is the common temperature?

- $80\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $30\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $40\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $20\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

The portions have equal heat capacities. Each kelvin lost by the hot water raises the cold water by one kelvin, so the final reading is halfway between $20\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ and $60\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$.
