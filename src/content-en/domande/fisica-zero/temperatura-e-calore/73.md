A closed bottle of water, just taken out of the refrigerator, in a humid kitchen, becomes covered with droplets on its outer surface. What change of state produces the droplets?

- Condensation of the water vapor in the air
- Evaporation of the water inside the bottle
- Melting of a layer of ice formed on the bottle
- Freezing of the water vapor on the cold surface

## Explanation

The droplets are liquid water forming on the cold surface. The change from vapor to liquid is called **condensation**, the reverse of vaporization. The water vapor in the air in contact with the cold bottle releases energy and becomes liquid. The water in the bottle stays inside, because the container is closed.
