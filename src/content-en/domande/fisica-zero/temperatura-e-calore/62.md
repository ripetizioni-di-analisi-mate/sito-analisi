Pure water boils steadily at $93\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ at a mountain site. The thermometer has been checked. Which local quantity is needed to compare the result with the $100\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ listed in tables?

- The heater power setting.
- Atmospheric pressure.
- The mass of water in the pot.
- The specific heat capacity of the pot.

## Explanation

Boiling temperature depends on the pressure acting on the liquid. The tabulated $100\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ refers to standard atmospheric pressure.
