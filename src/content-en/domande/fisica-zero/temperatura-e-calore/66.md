An ice cube tray holds $0.20\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water already at $0\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. In the freezer, the water freezes completely while staying at $0\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. What is the heat $Q$ transferred by the water? Use $L_f=334\,000\,\mathrm{J/kg}$.

- $+66\,800\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-6680\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-452\,000\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-66\,800\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

When it freezes, the water releases the same energy it would absorb in melting, that is, $mL_f=0.20\cdot334\,000\,\mathrm{J}=66\,800\,\mathrm{J}$. The energy leaves the water, so for the water the heat is negative. Therefore, $Q=-66\,800\,\mathrm{J}$, and it is the freezer that removes this energy from the water. The value $-452\,000\,\mathrm{J}$ results from mistakenly using the latent heat of vaporization.
