On a pure liquid’s temperature-versus-heat graph, the boiling plateau runs from $20\,\mathrm{kJ}$ to $100\,\mathrm{kJ}$. When the meter reads $40\,\mathrm{kJ}$, what fraction of the mass present at the start of the plateau has vaporised?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$

## Explanation

The full plateau uses $80\,\mathrm{kJ}$. Only $20\,\mathrm{kJ}$ has entered since its start, so the fraction vaporised is $\dfrac{20}{80}$ of the initial mass.
