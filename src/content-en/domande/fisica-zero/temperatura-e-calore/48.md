Two blocks initially at $20\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ and $70\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ have positive heat capacities. In an insulated system with no phase changes, which final reading signals an error even if their masses are unknown?

- $65\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $75\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $25\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $45\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

The equilibrium temperature is a positively weighted average of the initial values and must lie between them. A reading of $75\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ exceeds even the hotter block’s initial temperature.
