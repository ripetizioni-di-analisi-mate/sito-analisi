A body at $27\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ is brought to twice its absolute temperature. For this calculation use $T_K=t_C+273$. What Celsius temperature does it reach?

- $600\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $150\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $327\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $54\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

The initial absolute temperature is $300\,\mathrm{K}$. Twice that value is $600\,\mathrm{K}$, which in this approximation corresponds to $600-273=327$ degrees Celsius.
