A sample of mass $5$ kg has a specific heat capacity of $200$ joules per kilogram per kelvin. How much heat is needed to raise its temperature by $3$ K without a change of state?

- $3000\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{J}$
- $\dfrac{1000}{3}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $600\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

With no change of state, use $Q=mc\Delta T$. Multiply $5$, $200$, and $3$. Therefore the heat required is $3000$ joules.
