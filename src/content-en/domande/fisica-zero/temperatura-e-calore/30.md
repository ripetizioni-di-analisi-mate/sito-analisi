A sample of mass $6$ kg has a specific heat capacity of $200$ joules per kilogram per kelvin. How much heat is needed to raise its temperature by $3$ K without a change of state?

- $1200\,\mathrm{J}$
- $3600\,\mathrm{J}$
- $400\,\mathrm{J}$
- $600\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

With no change of state, use $Q=mc\Delta T$. Multiply $6$, $200$, and $3$. Therefore the heat required is $3600$ joules.
