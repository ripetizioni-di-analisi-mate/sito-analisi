In an entrance hall at $15\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$, a wooden door with a metal handle has been in place for days. When touched, the handle feels colder than the wood. Which statement correctly describes the situation?

- The handle is at a lower temperature than the wood, because metal is a cold material
- The wood is at a higher temperature than the handle, because it holds the heat of the room
- The handle and the wood are at the same temperature, but the metal transfers energy from the hand more quickly
- The handle transfers cold to the hand more quickly than the wood

## Explanation

The door and the handle have been in the same surroundings for days, so they are in thermal equilibrium with the air and are both at $15\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. How an object feels to the touch also depends on how quickly the skin transfers energy to it. Metal can transfer energy faster than wood, so the skin in contact with the handle cools more quickly. A thermometer brought into equilibrium with each object would give the same reading.
