A thermometer reads $45\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ before cooling and $12\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ afterwards. What is the signed change in kelvins?

- $-33\,\mathrm{K}$
- $-57\,\mathrm{K}$
- $57\,\mathrm{K}$
- $33\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

The change is the final reading minus the initial reading, so $12-45=-33$. A Celsius degree and a kelvin have the same interval size.
