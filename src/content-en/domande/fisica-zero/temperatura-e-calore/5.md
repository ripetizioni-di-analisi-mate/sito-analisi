An iron sample gives $21.6\,\mathrm{kJ}$ to an insulated water bath. No other body exchanges energy. With absorbed heat positive, what are the signed heats of the iron and the water, in that order?

- $-21.6\,\mathrm{kJ}$, $+21.6\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $-21.6\,\mathrm{kJ}$, $-21.6\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $+21.6\,\mathrm{kJ}$, $-21.6\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $+21.6\,\mathrm{kJ}$, $+21.6\,\mathrm{kJ}$

## Explanation

The iron loses energy, so its heat is negative. All that energy enters the water and the two signed heat transfers sum to zero.
