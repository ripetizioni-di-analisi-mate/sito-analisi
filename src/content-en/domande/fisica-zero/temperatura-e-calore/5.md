A sample of mass $3$ kg has a specific heat capacity of $300$ joules per kilogram per kelvin. What is its heat capacity?

- $900\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $100\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $0.01\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $303\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

The heat capacity is $C=mc$. It depends on the mass of the object and the specific heat capacity of the material. Therefore $C=3\cdot300=900$ joules per kelvin.
