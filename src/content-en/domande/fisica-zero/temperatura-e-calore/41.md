A sample of mass $7$ kg has a specific heat capacity of $300$ joules per kilogram per kelvin. What is its heat capacity?

- $2100\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $\dfrac{300}{7}\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $\dfrac{7}{300}\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $307\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

The heat capacity is $C=mc$. It depends on the mass of the object and the specific heat capacity of the material. Therefore $C=7\cdot300=2100$ joules per kelvin.
