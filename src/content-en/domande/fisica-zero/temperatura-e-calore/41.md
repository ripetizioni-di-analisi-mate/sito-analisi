To distinguish two materials, equal insulated masses receive equal net powers. One temperature curve rises at $2\,\mathrm{K/min}$ and the other at $5\,\mathrm{K/min}$. Which sample has the greater specific heat capacity?

- The one rising at $2\,\mathrm{K/min}$.
- The one rising at $5\,\mathrm{K/min}$.
- Whichever has the greater Celsius reading, regardless of slope.
- The two values must be equal.

## Explanation

Both samples receive the same energy each minute. The more slowly warming sample needs more energy per kilogram per kelvin.
