A pure material melts in equilibrium at $64\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ under constant pressure. The heater continues to supply energy while both crystals and liquid are present. What changes during this stage?

- The liquid fraction increases.
- Temperature rises in proportion to the energy supplied.
- The total sample mass falls by the mass that melts.
- The ratio of liquid mass to solid mass stays fixed.

## Explanation

While the phases coexist at the specified pressure, the melting temperature is fixed. The heat absorbed therefore converts an increasing fraction of the solid into liquid.
