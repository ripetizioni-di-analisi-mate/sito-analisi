A radiator heats the air in a room. Which sentence uses the words heat and internal energy correctly?

- The air in the room contains more heat than the radiator
- The heat of the radiator is its temperature
- Internal energy is the energy that flows from the radiator to the air
- The radiator transfers heat to the air, and the internal energy of the air increases

## Explanation

**Heat** is energy transferred because of a temperature difference, so it describes a flow of energy from one object to another. **Internal energy**, on the other hand, is a property of an object in its current state. The radiator is warmer than the air and transfers energy to it as heat; therefore, the internal energy of the air increases. The sentences that speak of heat contained in an object, or that call the transferred energy internal energy, confuse the two concepts.
