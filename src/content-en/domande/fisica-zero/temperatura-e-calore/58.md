A $0.50\,\mathrm{kg}$ sample remains at its melting temperature for $200\,\mathrm{s}$ while absorbing a constant net power of $100\,\mathrm{W}$. It melts completely during that interval. What is its specific latent heat?

- $100\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$
- $10\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$
- $40\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$

## Explanation

The melting stage absorbs $100\cdot 200=20000$ joules. Dividing $20\,\mathrm{kJ}$ by $0.50\,\mathrm{kg}$ gives $40\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$.
