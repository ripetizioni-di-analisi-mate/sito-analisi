A sample shows a melting plateau of $2\,\mathrm{min}$ and a boiling plateau of $12\,\mathrm{min}$. The same entire mass changes phase at the same net power in both. What is $\dfrac{L_v}{L_f}$?

- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $14$
- $10$
- $6$

## Explanation

The absorbed energies are proportional to duration because the power is unchanged. The masses are equal as well, so the ratio of latent heats is $\dfrac{12}{2}=6$.
