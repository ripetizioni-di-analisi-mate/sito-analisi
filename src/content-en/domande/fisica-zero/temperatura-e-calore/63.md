Steam and liquid water are both at the boiling temperature. At that pressure, which portion delivers more energy to a cooler surface if the masses are equal and both end as liquid water at the same temperature?

- They release equal energy because their initial temperatures match.
- The liquid, because it starts denser.
- The steam releases energy only after cooling below the final temperature.
- The steam, because it condenses first.

## Explanation

Both masses release the same energy during their final cooling as liquids. The steam also releases latent heat as it condenses.
