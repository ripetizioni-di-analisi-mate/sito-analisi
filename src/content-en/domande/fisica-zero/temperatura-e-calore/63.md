Ice cubes with a total mass of $0.050\,\mathrm{kg}$, already at $0\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$, are put into a drink. How much energy do the cubes absorb to melt completely? Use $L_f=334\,000\,\mathrm{J/kg}$.

- $1670\,\mathrm{J}$
- $16\,700\,\mathrm{J}$
- $167\,000\,\mathrm{J}$
- $334\,000\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The cubes are already at the melting point, so all the heat absorbed goes into the change of state, and $Q=mL_f$. Therefore,
$$
Q=0.050\,\mathrm{kg}\cdot334\,000\,\mathrm{J/kg}=16\,700\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
During melting, the temperature of the cubes stays at $0\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$, and the energy is taken from the drink, which cools. The value $334\,000\,\mathrm{J}$ is the heat needed to melt one kilogram of ice.
