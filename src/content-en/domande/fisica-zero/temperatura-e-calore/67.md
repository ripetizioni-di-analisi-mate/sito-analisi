An open vessel on a balance contains water already boiling. Over $100\,\mathrm{s}$ its mass drops by $10\,\mathrm{g}$ solely because steam escapes. With $L_v=2260$ kJ/kg, what average power went into vaporisation?

- $226\,\mathrm{W}$
- $2260\,\mathrm{W}$
- $2.26\,\mathrm{W}$
- $22.6\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

Vaporising $0.010\,\mathrm{kg}$ absorbs $22.6\,\mathrm{kJ}$. Dividing $22600\,\mathrm{J}$ by $100\,\mathrm{s}$ gives $226\,\mathrm{W}$.
