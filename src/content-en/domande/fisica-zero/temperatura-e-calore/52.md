Water at $70\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ and water at $20\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ are poured into an insulated pitcher, in unknown amounts. Which of these values can be the common final temperature?

- $15\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $75\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $35\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $90\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

The system is isolated, so the energy released by the hot water is absorbed by the cold water. The hot water cools and the cold water warms, so the final temperature lies between $20\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ and $70\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. Of the values offered, only $35\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ lies in this interval. The exact value depends on the masses, which are not known here.
