Two identical vessels are placed in a room at $20\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$, one with water at $5\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ and one with water at $55\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. If identical insulating jackets are fitted, how do the initial heat transfers with the room compare with those of the uninsulated vessels?

- The cold water starts cooling and the hot water starts warming.
- Only the cooling of the hot water slows down.
- Both the warming of the cold water and the cooling of the hot water slow down.
- The cold water warms faster and the hot water cools more slowly.

## Explanation

The jacket impedes energy transfer in either direction. Energy flows from the room to the cold water and from the hot water to the room, at lower rates than for the uninsulated vessels.
