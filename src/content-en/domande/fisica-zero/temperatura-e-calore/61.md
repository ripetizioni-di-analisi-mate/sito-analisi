A saucer of water at $22\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ slowly loses mass without bubbling. Which process explains this observation in air?

- Condensation from the air.
- Boiling throughout the liquid.
- Evaporation from the surface.
- Freezing at the bottom.

## Explanation

Some molecules leave the surface even below the boiling temperature. Their departure reduces the mass of water left in the saucer.
