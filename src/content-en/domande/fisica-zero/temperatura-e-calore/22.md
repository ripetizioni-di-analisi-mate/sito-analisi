An aluminum pan weighing $0.80\,\mathrm{kg}$ is taken off the stove at $180\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ and cools on the countertop to $30\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. What is the heat $Q$ transferred by the pan? Use $c_{\mathrm{Al}}=900\,\mathrm{J/(kg\cdot K)}$.

- $+108\,000\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-129\,600\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-21\,600\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-108\,000\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The pan does not change state, so $Q=mc\Delta T$ applies. The change is the final temperature minus the initial temperature, so $\Delta T=30-180=-150\,\mathrm{K}$. Therefore,
$$
Q=0.80\cdot900\cdot(-150)\,\mathrm{J}=-108\,000\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
The negative sign indicates that the pan **releases** $108\,000\,\mathrm{J}$ to its surroundings.
