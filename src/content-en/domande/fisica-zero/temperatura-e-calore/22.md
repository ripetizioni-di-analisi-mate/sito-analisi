An object absorbs $3000$ J and has a heat capacity of $200$ J/K. By how much does its temperature rise?

- $600000\,\mathrm{K}$
- $15\,\mathrm{K}$
- $3000\,\mathrm{K}$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

The heat capacity satisfies $Q=C\Delta T$. Therefore $\Delta T=\dfrac{Q}{C}$. This gives $\Delta T=\dfrac{3000}{200}=15$ kelvins.
