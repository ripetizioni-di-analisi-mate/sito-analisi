While cooling, a sensor intended for water and ice suddenly displays $-4\,\mathrm{K}$. What should be checked before calculating any heat transfer?

- Whether the water has a mass below one kilogram.
- Whether two phases in the vessel have the same temperature.
- The sign of the latent heat of fusion.
- The scale or sensor operation, because the reading is below absolute zero.

## Explanation

For ordinary water and ice, absolute temperature is nonnegative. A reading of $-4\,\mathrm{K}$ therefore points to a unit or measurement problem.
