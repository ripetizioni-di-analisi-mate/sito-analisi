A temperature is $70$ degrees Celsius. What is it in kelvins?

- $343.15\,\mathrm{K}$
- $343\,\mathrm{K}$
- $-203\,\mathrm{K}$
- $203.15\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

The conversion formula is $T=t_C+273.15$, so add $273.15$ to the Celsius value. The result is $343.15$ kelvins.
