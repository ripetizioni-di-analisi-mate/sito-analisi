On a graph, heat absorbed is on the vertical axis and temperature on the horizontal axis. A straight segment from $12\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ to $18\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ rises from $4\,\mathrm{kJ}$ to $7\,\mathrm{kJ}$. What heat capacity follows from its slope?

::grafico{linee="12,4 18,7" punti="12,4;18,7" xmin=10 xmax=20 ymin=0 ymax=8 nomi="T,Q" legenda=0 titolo="Heat absorbed in kilojoules against temperature in degrees Celsius."}

- $0.5\,\mathrm{kJ/K}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{kJ/K}$
- $2\,\mathrm{kJ/K}$
- $3\,\mathrm{kJ/K}$

## Explanation

The slope is determined by the differences between the points. Heat increases by $3\,\mathrm{kJ}$ over $6\,\mathrm{K}$, giving a heat capacity of $0.5\,\mathrm{kJ/K}$.
