One end of a metal rod is immersed in warm water, and the rest is suspended in insulated surroundings. Energy reaches the dry end without bulk transport of metal. What is the transfer along the rod called?

- Condensation.
- Convection.
- Evaporation.
- Conduction.

## Explanation

Energy travels through regions of the solid at different temperatures while the material remains macroscopically at rest. This is conduction.
