Wet laundry hung on a balcony at $25\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ dries in a few hours. What change of state does the water in the laundry undergo?

- Evaporation from the surface, at a temperature below the boiling point
- Boiling, because the water turns into vapor
- Condensation, because the water passes into the air
- Boiling at $25\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$, because the pressure is lower outdoors

## Explanation

The water changes from liquid to vapor, so this is a vaporization. Evaporation occurs at the surface of a liquid even at temperatures below the boiling point, while boiling occurs throughout the liquid as vapor bubbles form. No bubbles form in the laundry at $25\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$, so the process is **evaporation**.
