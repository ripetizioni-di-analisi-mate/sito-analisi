A sample warms from $20\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ to $30\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ without changing phase. Its heat capacity stays between $200\,\mathrm{J/K}$ and $300\,\mathrm{J/K}$ throughout. Which interval must contain the energy absorbed?

- From 20 to $30\,\mathrm{kJ}$.
- From 5 to $6\,\mathrm{kJ}$.
- From 0.2 to $0.3\,\mathrm{kJ}$.
- From 2 to $3\,\mathrm{kJ}$.

## Explanation

Each kelvin takes between $200\,\mathrm{J}$ and $300\,\mathrm{J}$. Over the full $10\,\mathrm{K}$ rise the corresponding energy bounds are $2000\,\mathrm{J}$ and $3000\,\mathrm{J}$.
