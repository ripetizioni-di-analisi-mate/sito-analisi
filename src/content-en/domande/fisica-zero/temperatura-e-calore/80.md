Maintaining a box at a constant temperature takes $30\,\mathrm{W}$ with its lid on and $50\,\mathrm{W}$ with the lid off. The surroundings and other losses are unchanged. How much energy loss does the lid prevent in $10\,\mathrm{min}$?

- $20\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $48\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $30\,\mathrm{kJ}$

## Explanation

The lid reduces the required power by $20\,\mathrm{W}$. Over $600\,\mathrm{s}$ the energy difference is $20\cdot 600=12000$ joules.
