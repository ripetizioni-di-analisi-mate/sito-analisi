Two equal masses of water at $70$ and $90$ degrees Celsius are mixed without heat loss or a change of state. What is the final temperature in degrees Celsius?

- $160$
- $70$
- $80$
- $90$

## Explanation

The masses are equal and have the same specific heat capacity. The energy balance therefore gives the arithmetic mean of the two temperatures. This gives $\dfrac{70+90}{2}=80$ degrees Celsius.
