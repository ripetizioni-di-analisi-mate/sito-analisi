A teaspoon and a ladle are made of the same steel, and the ladle has ten times the mass. Which statement correctly compares the two utensils?

- They have the same heat capacity, and the ladle has ten times the specific heat capacity
- They have the same specific heat capacity, and the ladle has ten times the heat capacity
- The ladle has ten times the specific heat capacity and ten times the heat capacity
- They have the same specific heat capacity and the same heat capacity

## Explanation

Specific heat capacity is a property of the substance, so it is the same for two objects made of the same steel. The heat capacity of a homogeneous object, on the other hand, is $C=mc$, which depends on both the substance and the mass. The ladle has the same specific heat capacity and ten times the mass, so its heat capacity is ten times that of the teaspoon.
