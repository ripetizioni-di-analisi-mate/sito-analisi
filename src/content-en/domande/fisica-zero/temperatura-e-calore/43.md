A plate has a constant heat capacity of $200\,\mathrm{J/K}$. It warms by $8\,\mathrm{K}$ and returns to its initial temperature, staying in the same phase and exchanging only heat. What is the sum of the magnitudes of the two heat transfers?

- $0\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $1.6\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $3.2\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $0.8\,\mathrm{kJ}$

## Explanation

Heating and cooling each transfer $1600\,\mathrm{J}$ in magnitude. The magnitudes therefore sum to $3200\,\mathrm{J}$, although the signed sum is zero.
