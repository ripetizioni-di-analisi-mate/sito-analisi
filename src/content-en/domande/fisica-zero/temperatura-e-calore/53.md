A lab wants to determine the specific heat capacity of a hot sample by mixing it with water. The masses, the water’s specific heat capacity and both initial temperatures are known. What further reading completes the balance in an ideal vessel?

- Only the starting time.
- The outside temperature with the door shut.
- The common equilibrium temperature.
- The water depth alone, with no vessel geometry.

## Explanation

The common temperature determines both the sample’s cooling and the water’s warming. Equal heat transfers in magnitude then leave only the sample’s specific heat capacity unknown.
