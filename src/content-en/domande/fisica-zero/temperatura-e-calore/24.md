A probe rounds to the nearest Celsius degree. Its display can read $1\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ both before and after a slight warming. Which pair of actual temperatures demonstrates this without a halfway case?

- $1.1\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$, $1.8\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $0.2\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$, $1.1\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $0.1\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$, $2.1\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $0.7\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$, $1.2\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

Both $0.7\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ and $1.2\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ lie less than half a degree from $1\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. The actual rise of $0.5\,\mathrm{K}$ can therefore go undetected on the display.
