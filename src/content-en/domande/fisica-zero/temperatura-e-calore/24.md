How much energy is needed to melt $5$ kg of a substance already at its melting point if its latent heat is $10000$ J/kg?

- $2000\,\mathrm{J}$
- $5000\,\mathrm{J}$
- $10005\,\mathrm{J}$
- $50000\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

During the change of state, use $Q=mL$. The substance is already at its melting point, so no additional heating term is needed. The heat required is $50000$ joules.
