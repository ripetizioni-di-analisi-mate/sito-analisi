Three identical windows each lose $20\,\mathrm{W}$ to the outdoors under the same conditions. Ideal panels stop all loss through two windows. How much heat still passes through the windows in $1\,\mathrm{min}$?

- $3.6\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $0.020\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $2.4\,\mathrm{kJ}$

## Explanation

Only one window continues losing energy at $20\,\mathrm{W}$. In $60\,\mathrm{s}$ it loses $20\cdot 60=1200$ joules.
