The display of a laboratory oven reads $433.15\,\mathrm{K}$. The oven temperature is then increased by $40\,\mathrm{K}$. What temperature does the oven reach, in degrees Celsius?

- $200\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $473.15\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $160\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $120\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

The initial temperature in degrees Celsius is $433.15-273.15=160\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. An increase of $40\,\mathrm{K}$ is also an increase of $40\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$, because the intervals on the two scales are the same size. Therefore, the final temperature is $160+40=200\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. The value $473.15\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ results from adding $40$ to the reading in kelvins without then converting it.
