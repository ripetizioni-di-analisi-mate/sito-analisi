An object absorbs $2400$ J and has a heat capacity of $200$ J/K. By how much does its temperature rise?

- $12\,\mathrm{K}$
- $480000\,\mathrm{K}$
- $2400\,\mathrm{K}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

The heat capacity satisfies $Q=C\Delta T$. Therefore $\Delta T=\dfrac{Q}{C}$. This gives $\Delta T=\dfrac{2400}{200}=12$ kelvins.
