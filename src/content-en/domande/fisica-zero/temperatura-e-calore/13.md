During boiling, $67.8\,\mathrm{kJ}$ is absorbed solely to convert water to steam. With a specific latent heat of vaporisation of $2260\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$, what mass vaporises?

- $300\,\mathrm{g}$
- $30\,\mathrm{g}$
- $150\,\mathrm{g}$
- $3\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

Each kilogram requires $2260\,\mathrm{kJ}$, so the mass is $\dfrac{67.8}{2260}=0.030$ kilograms, or $30\,\mathrm{g}$.
