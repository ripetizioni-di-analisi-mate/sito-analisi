A temperature is $60$ degrees Celsius. What is it in kelvins?

- $333\,\mathrm{K}$
- $-213\,\mathrm{K}$
- $213.15\,\mathrm{K}$
- $333.15\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

The conversion formula is $T=t_C+273.15$, so add $273.15$ to the Celsius value. The result is $333.15$ kelvins.
