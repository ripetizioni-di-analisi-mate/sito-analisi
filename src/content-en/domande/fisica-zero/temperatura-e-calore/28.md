A bucket and a glass contain liquid water at the same temperature. Which measurement determines which portion of water needs more energy for the same temperature rise without losses?

- The mass of water in each.
- The masses of the empty containers.
- Heater power without measuring the duration of each run.
- Only the common initial temperature of the two samples.

## Explanation

For the same substance and temperature change, $c\Delta  T$ is the same. The heat required is therefore proportional to the mass of water.
