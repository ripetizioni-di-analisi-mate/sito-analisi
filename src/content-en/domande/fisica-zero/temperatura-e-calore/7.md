Two equal masses of water at $30$ and $50$ degrees Celsius are mixed without heat loss or a change of state. What is the final temperature in degrees Celsius?

- $80$
- $30$
- $40$
- $50$

## Explanation

The masses are equal and have the same specific heat capacity. The energy balance therefore gives the arithmetic mean of the two temperatures. This gives $\dfrac{30+50}{2}=40$ degrees Celsius.
