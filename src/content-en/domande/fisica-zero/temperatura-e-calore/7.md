The thermostat of a household freezer is set to $-18\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. What temperature in kelvins does this correspond to?

- $291.15\,\mathrm{K}$
- $255.15\,\mathrm{K}$
- $-255.15\,\mathrm{K}$
- $-291.15\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

To convert from degrees Celsius to kelvins, add $273.15$ to the numerical value in degrees Celsius. The value is negative, so the sum amounts to subtracting $18$ from $273.15$, which gives
$$
T_K=-18+273.15=255.15 .
$$
The freezer is therefore at $255.15\,\mathrm{K}$. The value $291.15\,\mathrm{K}$ results from adding $18$ instead of subtracting it.
