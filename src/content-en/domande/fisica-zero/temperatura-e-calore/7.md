An object with a constant heat capacity of $900\,\mathrm{J/K}$ absorbs $4500\,\mathrm{J}$ without changing phase. By how much does its temperature rise?

- $50\,\mathrm{K}$
- $4\,\mathrm{K}$
- $0.2\,\mathrm{K}$
- $5\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

Each kelvin requires $900\,\mathrm{J}$. The temperature therefore rises by $\dfrac{4500}{900}=5$ kelvins.
