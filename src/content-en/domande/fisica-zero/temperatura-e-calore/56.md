A bag contains $300\,\mathrm{g}$ of pure ice at $0\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. At standard pressure it absorbs $33.4\,\mathrm{kJ}$ without losses. With $L_f=334$ kJ/kg, how much ice remains?

- $100\,\mathrm{g}$
- $300\,\mathrm{g}$
- $200\,\mathrm{g}$
- $0\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

The heat melts $\dfrac{33.4}{334}=0.10$ kilograms, or $100\,\mathrm{g}$. Subtracting that amount from the initial $300\,\mathrm{g}$ leaves $200\,\mathrm{g}$.
