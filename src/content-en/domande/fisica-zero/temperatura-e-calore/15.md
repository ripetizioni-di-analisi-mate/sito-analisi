The same ideal probe equilibrates first with block A and then with block B, reading $18\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ each time. The blocks retain their temperatures during the measurements. What happens when A and B first touch?

- A heats B because it was measured first.
- Both receive a net gain of heat from each other.
- The net heat transfer is zero.
- The larger block heats the smaller one.

## Explanation

By the zeroth law, two bodies in equilibrium with the same thermometer at the same reading have equal temperatures. Contact therefore produces no net transfer driven by a temperature difference.
