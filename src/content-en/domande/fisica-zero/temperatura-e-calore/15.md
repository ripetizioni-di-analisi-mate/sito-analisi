How much energy is needed to melt $4$ kg of a substance already at its melting point if its latent heat is $10000$ J/kg?

- $2500\,\mathrm{J}$
- $4000\,\mathrm{J}$
- $40000\,\mathrm{J}$
- $10004\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

During the change of state, use $Q=mL$. The substance is already at its melting point, so no additional heating term is needed. The heat required is $40000$ joules.
