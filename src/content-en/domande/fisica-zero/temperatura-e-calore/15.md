An athlete places an ice pack on a sore knee. How does energy transfer take place between the knee and the pack?

- Cold flows from the pack to the knee
- Energy flows from the pack to the knee, which is at a higher temperature
- Energy flows from the knee to the pack, which is at a lower temperature
- There is no energy transfer until all the ice has melted

## Explanation

Heat is energy transferred because of a temperature difference. In ordinary thermal contact, energy flows spontaneously from the object at the higher temperature to the one at the lower temperature. The knee is warmer than the pack, so it transfers energy to the pack and cools. For the knee the heat transferred is therefore negative, while for the pack it is positive.
