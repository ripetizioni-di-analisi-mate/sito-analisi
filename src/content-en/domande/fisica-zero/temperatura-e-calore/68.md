A wax data sheet lists “$160\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$” for melting. Which operation directly gives an energy with the correct units?

- Divide by the mass melted in kilograms.
- Divide by an absolute temperature.
- Multiply by the mass melted in kilograms.
- Multiply only by the temperature change in kelvins.

## Explanation

Kilograms of mass cancel the kilograms in the denominator. Kilojoules remain, giving the energy required for that amount of melting.
