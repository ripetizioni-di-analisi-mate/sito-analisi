The reservoir of a steam iron holds $0.050\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water at $10\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$, which is turned completely into vapor at $100\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$, at standard atmospheric pressure. How much energy does the water absorb? Use $c_a=4180\,\mathrm{J/(kg\cdot K)}$ and $L_v=2\,260\,000\,\mathrm{J/kg}$.

- $113\,000\,\mathrm{J}$
- $18\,810\,\mathrm{J}$
- $131\,810\,\mathrm{J}$
- $94\,190\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The process has two stages. First the liquid water warms from $10\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ to $100\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ and absorbs $mc_a\Delta T=0.050\cdot4180\cdot90\,\mathrm{J}=18\,810\,\mathrm{J}$. Then it vaporizes at constant temperature and absorbs $mL_v=0.050\cdot2\,260\,000\,\mathrm{J}=113\,000\,\mathrm{J}$. Therefore, the total heat is $18\,810+113\,000=131\,810\,\mathrm{J}$. The value $113\,000\,\mathrm{J}$ leaves out the heating that comes before boiling.
