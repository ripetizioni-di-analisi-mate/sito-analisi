A temperature is $50$ degrees Celsius. What is it in kelvins?

- $323\,\mathrm{K}$
- $-223\,\mathrm{K}$
- $323.15\,\mathrm{K}$
- $223.15\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

The conversion formula is $T=t_C+273.15$, so add $273.15$ to the Celsius value. The result is $323.15$ kelvins.
