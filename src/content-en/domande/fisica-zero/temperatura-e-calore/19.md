A hot stone is dropped into a bucket of water. Until equilibrium is reached, the stone releases $2400\,\mathrm{J}$, while the water absorbs $2100\,\mathrm{J}$. How much energy did the bucket and the surrounding air absorb in total?

- $4500\,\mathrm{J}$
- $300\,\mathrm{J}$
- $2400\,\mathrm{J}$
- $2100\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The energy released by the stone goes to the objects in thermal contact with it. The water receives $2100\,\mathrm{J}$ of it, so the rest goes to the bucket and the air. This remainder is $2400-2100=300\,\mathrm{J}$. The bucket and the air therefore absorb $300\,\mathrm{J}$, and the system made up of the stone and the water is not isolated.
