A thermometer has a constant additive offset. In melting ice at $0\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ it reads $+4\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. If it subsequently reads $12\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$, what is the actual temperature?

- $48\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $8\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $16\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $3\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

The reading is always four degrees above the actual temperature. Subtracting this offset from the new reading gives $8\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$.
