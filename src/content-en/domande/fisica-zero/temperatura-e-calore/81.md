A probe logs one reading every $30\,\mathrm{s}$. A pure substance at constant pressure takes $8\,\mathrm{s}$ to melt completely. What could hide the plateau in the recorded data?

- The entire melting stage can occur between consecutive readings.
- A single reading at the melting point is enough to establish the plateau duration.
- The absence of two equal readings proves that the sample did not absorb latent heat.
- The two points recorded before and after melting must have the same temperature.

## Explanation

The recording interval is longer than the plateau. The thermometer may therefore sample just before melting and again only after it is over.
