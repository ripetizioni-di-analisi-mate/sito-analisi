A temperature rises by $4$ degrees Celsius. What is the increase in kelvins?

- $277.15\,\mathrm{K}$
- $269\,\mathrm{K}$
- $4\,\mathrm{K}$
- $40\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

The Kelvin scale differs from the Celsius scale by an additive constant. When two temperatures are subtracted, the constant cancels. Therefore a rise of $4$ degrees Celsius equals a rise of $4$ kelvins.
