In an insulated system $0.30\,\mathrm{kg}$ of warm aluminium touches $0.60\,\mathrm{kg}$ of cold iron. Their constant specific heat capacities are $900\,\mathrm{J/(kg\cdot K)}$ and $450\,\mathrm{J/(kg\cdot K)}$, respectively. How do the magnitudes of their temperature changes compare?

- Only the iron changes temperature.
- The aluminium changes twice as much.
- They are equal.
- The iron changes twice as much.

## Explanation

Each heat capacity is $270\,\mathrm{J/K}$. All the heat lost by the aluminium enters the iron, so the two temperature changes have equal magnitudes.
