On a winter night, the air temperature in a vegetable garden goes from $14\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ at six in the evening to $-2\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ at dawn. What is the temperature change?

- $16\,\mathrm{K}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{K}$
- $-16\,\mathrm{K}$
- $12\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

The temperature change is the final value minus the initial value. Here $\Delta T=-2-14=-16$, so $\Delta T=-16\,\mathrm{K}$. The negative sign indicates that the air has cooled. The value $-12\,\mathrm{K}$ results from subtracting $2$ from $14$, that is, from treating the final temperature as if it were positive.
