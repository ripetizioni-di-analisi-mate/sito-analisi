Two bodies with equal heat capacities reach $28\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ in an ideal insulated vessel. One started at $10\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. What was the initial temperature of the other body?

- $18\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $46\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $56\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $38\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

The first body warms by $18\,\mathrm{K}$. Since the capacities are equal, the second must have cooled by the same $18\,\mathrm{K}$, starting at $46\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$.
