A probe reads $300.15\,\mathrm{K}$. What should a Celsius thermometer in thermal equilibrium with the probe read?

- $27\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $573.3\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $300.15\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $-27\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

Subtract 273.15 from the value in kelvins to obtain the Celsius reading. The result is $27\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$.
