A temperature rises by $3$ degrees Celsius. What is the increase in kelvins?

- $276.15\,\mathrm{K}$
- $3\,\mathrm{K}$
- $270\,\mathrm{K}$
- $30\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

The Kelvin scale differs from the Celsius scale by an additive constant. When two temperatures are subtracted, the constant cancels. Therefore a rise of $3$ degrees Celsius equals a rise of $3$ kelvins.
