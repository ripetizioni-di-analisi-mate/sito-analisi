In a pot of water heated from below, the portions of water near the bottom rise and those at the surface sink. What does this circulation depend on?

- On conduction in the metal of the pot, which pushes the water upward
- On the electromagnetic waves emitted by the flame
- On the density differences between the warmer water and the cooler water
- On the heat contained in the water at the bottom

## Explanation

The motion of portions of liquid that carry energy is **convection**, and this circulation depends on density differences and on the conditions in the fluid. The water near the bottom warms more and has a different density from the cooler water at the surface. Therefore, portions of the water move, and energy is carried throughout the pot.
