Before a bath is stirred, a calibrated probe reads $19\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ at the top and $24\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ at the bottom. Both readings are reproducible and are taken before the bath changes. Which conclusion is justified by these measurements alone?

- After stirring the bath must be at the temperature measured at the bottom.
- The whole bath is uniform at the mean of the two readings.
- Temperature must vary linearly at every intermediate depth.
- The two sampled regions of the bath differ in temperature.

## Explanation

The readings refer to different regions and show that the bath is not at a uniform temperature at that time. More information is needed to recover the depth profile or predict the energy-weighted average.
