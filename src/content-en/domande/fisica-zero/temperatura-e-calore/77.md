To make fishing sinkers, $0.50\,\mathrm{kg}$ of lead already at its melting point is melted, and the lead absorbs $11\,500\,\mathrm{J}$ to melt completely. What is the specific latent heat of fusion of lead?

- $5750\,\mathrm{J/kg}$
- $2300\,\mathrm{J/kg}$
- $23\,000\,\mathrm{J/kg}$
- $230\,000\,\mathrm{J/kg}$

## Explanation

The lead is already at its melting point, so all the heat goes into the change of state, and $Q=mL_f$. The mass is nonzero, so
$$
L_f=\frac{Q}{m}=\frac{11\,500\,\mathrm{J}}{0.50\,\mathrm{kg}}=23\,000\,\mathrm{J/kg}.
$$
The unit $\mathrm{J/kg}$ gives the energy needed to melt one kilogram of lead. The value $5750\,\mathrm{J/kg}$ results from multiplying by the mass instead of dividing.
