A wall absorbs radiation at $90\,\mathrm{W}$ and emits radiation at $110\,\mathrm{W}$, with no other transfers. What is the net incoming radiative power?

- $200\,\mathrm{W}$
- $20\,\mathrm{W}$
- $-200\,\mathrm{W}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

Subtract emitted power from received power. The balance $90-110=-20$ watts means the wall has a net energy loss.
