How much energy is needed to melt $3$ kg of a substance already at its melting point if its latent heat is $10000$ J/kg?

- $\dfrac{10000}{3}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $30000\,\mathrm{J}$
- $3000\,\mathrm{J}$
- $10003\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

During the change of state, use $Q=mL$. The substance is already at its melting point, so no additional heating term is needed. The heat required is $30000$ joules.
