A quarter kilogram of water at $15\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ absorbs $20900\,\mathrm{J}$. With a constant specific heat capacity of $4180\,\mathrm{J/(kg\cdot K)}$ and no phase change, what temperature does it reach?

- $95\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $5\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $35\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $20\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

The water has a heat capacity of $1045\,\mathrm{J/K}$, so the rise is $\dfrac{20900}{1045}=20$ kelvins. Adding that rise to the initial $15\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ gives $35\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$.
