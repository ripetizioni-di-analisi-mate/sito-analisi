The two faces of a conducting slab are maintained at $60\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ and $20\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. The temperature at every point stops changing with time. What can be said about transfer through the slab?

- Heat flows from the cold face to the hot face.
- Heat can continue to flow from the hot face to the cold face.
- The flow must reverse every second.
- The two faces must be at the same temperature.

## Explanation

A constant local temperature is compatible with energy entering and leaving at equal rates. The maintained temperature difference across the slab sustains conduction.
