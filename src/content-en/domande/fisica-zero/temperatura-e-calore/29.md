A temperature rises by $6$ degrees Celsius. What is the increase in kelvins?

- $6\,\mathrm{K}$
- $279.15\,\mathrm{K}$
- $267\,\mathrm{K}$
- $60\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

The Kelvin scale differs from the Celsius scale by an additive constant. When two temperatures are subtracted, the constant cancels. Therefore a rise of $6$ degrees Celsius equals a rise of $6$ kelvins.
