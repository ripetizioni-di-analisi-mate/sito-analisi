A metal tile and a wooden tile have been at $18\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ for hours. A warmer hand feels the metal as colder. Which measurement distinguishes this sensation from their initial temperature?

- The temperature of the hand before either contact, without any measurement during contact.
- The thickness of the tiles without measuring transfer to the skin.
- The air temperature measured after removing the hand.
- The initial rate at which the skin loses energy to each tile.

## Explanation

The tiles start at the same temperature. Metal can draw energy from the skin more rapidly, so the rate of transfer on contact matters.
