Two equal masses of water at $40$ and $60$ degrees Celsius are mixed without heat loss or a change of state. What is the final temperature in degrees Celsius?

- $100$
- $40$
- $60$
- $50$

## Explanation

The masses are equal and have the same specific heat capacity. The energy balance therefore gives the arithmetic mean of the two temperatures. This gives $\dfrac{40+60}{2}=50$ degrees Celsius.
