Sensor A reads $275\,\mathrm{K}$ and sensor B reads $5\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. The two bodies are brought into contact. In which direction does energy spontaneously flow at first?

- From B to A.
- There is zero net flow.
- The direction depends only on the masses.
- From A to B.

## Explanation

B is at $278.15\,\mathrm{K}$, above A at $275\,\mathrm{K}$. On ordinary thermal contact, heat initially flows from the warmer body to the cooler one.
