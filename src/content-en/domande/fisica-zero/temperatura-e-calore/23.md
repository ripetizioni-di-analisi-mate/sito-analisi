A sample of mass $5$ kg has a specific heat capacity of $300$ joules per kilogram per kelvin. What is its heat capacity?

- $60\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $\dfrac{1}{60}\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $1500\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $305\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

The heat capacity is $C=mc$. It depends on the mass of the object and the specific heat capacity of the material. Therefore $C=5\cdot300=1500$ joules per kelvin.
