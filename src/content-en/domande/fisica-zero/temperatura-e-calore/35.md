An insulated water-mixing experiment is repeated with both masses doubled and the initial temperatures unchanged. The vessel has negligible heat capacity. How do the final temperature and the amount of heat exchanged between the portions change?

- Same final temperature, half the heat exchanged.
- Twice the final temperature, same heat exchanged.
- Both final temperature and heat exchanged stay the same.
- Same final temperature, twice the heat exchanged.

## Explanation

The factor of two cancels in the weighted mean. The temperature changes are therefore identical, while each portion has twice the mass and exchanges twice as much energy.
