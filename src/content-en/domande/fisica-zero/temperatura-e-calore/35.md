An object releases $600$ J. What is the heat $Q$ if heat absorbed by the object is taken as positive?

- $600\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-600\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-60\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The sign of the heat depends on the direction of energy transfer relative to the object. When heat is released, energy leaves the object. Under the stated convention, $Q=-600$ joules.
