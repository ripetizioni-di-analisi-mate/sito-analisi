Two equal masses of water at $50$ and $70$ degrees Celsius are mixed without heat loss or a change of state. What is the final temperature in degrees Celsius?

- $60$
- $120$
- $50$
- $70$

## Explanation

The masses are equal and have the same specific heat capacity. The energy balance therefore gives the arithmetic mean of the two temperatures. This gives $\dfrac{50+70}{2}=60$ degrees Celsius.
