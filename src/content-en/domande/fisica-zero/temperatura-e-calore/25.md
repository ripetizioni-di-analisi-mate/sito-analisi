A report says that a stationary, isolated block “contains $200\,\mathrm{J}$ of heat”. Which quantity describes the microscopic energy associated with the state of the block?

- Specific heat capacity.
- Thermal power.
- Heat capacity.
- Internal energy.

## Explanation

Microscopic motion and interactions contribute to internal energy. Heat refers to energy exchanged between systems because of a temperature difference.
