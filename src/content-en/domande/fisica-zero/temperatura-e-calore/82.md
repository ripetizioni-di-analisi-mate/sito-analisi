An experiment is intended to measure the effect of exposed surface area on evaporation. Which pair of runs best isolates that variable?

- Same water mass, temperature, humidity and airflow, with different exposed areas.
- Water and another liquid in different rooms.
- Same surface area, with hot water in one run and cold water in the other.
- Identical vessels, one in sunlight and one in darkness.

## Explanation

Keeping the substance, temperature and air conditions the same makes a difference in mass-loss rate attributable to the change in exposed area.
