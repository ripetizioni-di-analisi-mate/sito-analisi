Between the Sun and Earth's atmosphere there is an almost empty space. How does energy from the Sun reach Earth?

- By conduction through space
- By convection of the gases between the Sun and Earth
- By conduction and convection together
- By radiation, that is, by electromagnetic waves

## Explanation

Conduction takes place through a material, and convection requires the motion of a fluid, so both need matter. The space between the Sun and Earth is almost empty, so these two modes cannot carry solar energy. In **radiation**, energy is carried by electromagnetic waves, which can also travel through a vacuum, and this is how energy from the Sun reaches Earth.
