A radiator contains hot water. Energy crosses its solid metal wall and is then carried toward the ceiling by moving air. Which mechanisms describe these two stages, in order?

- Conduction then convection.
- Conduction and radiation.
- Radiation then freezing.
- Convection then conduction.

## Explanation

Energy crosses the stationary wall by conduction. In the next stage warm air transports energy as it moves, so the mechanism is convection.
