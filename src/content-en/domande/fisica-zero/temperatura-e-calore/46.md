Water at $80\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ is to be mixed with water at $20\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ to obtain $35\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. With no losses and the same constant specific heat capacity, how much cold water is needed per kilogram of hot water?

- $4\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $1\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.333\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $3\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The hot water loses $45\,\mathrm{K}$ and the cold water gains $15\,\mathrm{K}$. Equal heat transfers in magnitude require three times as much cold water as hot water.
