The software of a weather station converts every reading to kelvins. If the value $T_K$ in kelvins is plotted against the value $t_C$ in degrees Celsius, with $t_C$ on the horizontal axis, what graph is obtained?

- A line with slope $1$ that crosses the vertical axis at $273.15$
- A line through the origin with slope $273.15$
- A line through the origin with slope $1$
- A line with slope $-1$ that crosses the vertical axis at $273.15$

## Explanation

The conversion is $T_K=t_C+273.15$, a linear function of $t_C$. The coefficient of $t_C$ is $1$, so the slope of the line is $1$, and an increase of one degree Celsius corresponds to an increase of one kelvin. For $t_C=0$ we get $T_K=273.15$; therefore, the line crosses the vertical axis at $273.15$ and does not pass through the origin.
