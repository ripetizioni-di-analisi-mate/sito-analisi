How much heat melts $0.12\,\mathrm{kg}$ of pure ice already at $0\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ at standard atmospheric pressure? Use a specific latent heat of fusion of $334\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$.

- $2783\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $40.08\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $334\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $4.008\,\mathrm{kJ}$

## Explanation

The ice is already at its melting point, so all the energy goes into the phase change. Multiplying $0.12\cdot 334$ gives $40.08\,\mathrm{kJ}$.
