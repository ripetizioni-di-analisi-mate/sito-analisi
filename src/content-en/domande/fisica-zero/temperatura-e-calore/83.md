A metal spoon is left in a bowl of hot broth, and after a while the end of the handle, outside the broth, is also warm. How does energy travel along the handle?

- By convection, with the metal moving along the handle
- By transport of broth inside the handle
- By radiation, because metal is a good conductor
- By conduction along the metal, without movement of the material

## Explanation

The handle is outside the broth but is part of the same piece of metal. In **conduction**, energy moves through a material from the warmer parts to the cooler parts, without bulk movement of the material. The metal of the spoon does not move, so convection, which requires the motion of a fluid, does not describe how energy travels along the handle.
