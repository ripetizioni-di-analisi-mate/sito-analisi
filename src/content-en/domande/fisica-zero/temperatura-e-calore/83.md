One probe always reads $2\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ above the true temperature. Another is accurate. Both follow the same body before and after heating. How do the measured temperature changes compare?

- The first change is $2\,\mathrm{K}$ smaller.
- The changes are equal.
- The first change is twice the second.
- The first change is $2\,\mathrm{K}$ greater.

## Explanation

The same offset is added to both readings from the first probe. When the initial reading is subtracted from the final reading, the offset cancels.
