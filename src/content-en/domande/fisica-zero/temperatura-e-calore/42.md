In a car parked in the sun, the dashboard, with a heat capacity of $4000\,\mathrm{J/K}$, absorbs $60\,000\,\mathrm{J}$ in one hour. Neglecting the energy the dashboard releases to the air, by how much does its temperature rise?

- $1.5\,\mathrm{K}$
- $0.067\,\mathrm{K}$
- $150\,\mathrm{K}$
- $15\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

For an object of heat capacity $C$, $Q=C\Delta T$. The heat capacity is nonzero, so the heat is divided by $C$
$$
\Delta T=\frac{Q}{C}=\frac{60\,000\,\mathrm{J}}{4000\,\mathrm{J/K}}=15\,\mathrm{K}.
$$
The temperature of the dashboard therefore rises by $15\,\mathrm{K}$, that is, by $15\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. The value $0.067\,\mathrm{K}$ results from dividing the heat capacity by the heat.
