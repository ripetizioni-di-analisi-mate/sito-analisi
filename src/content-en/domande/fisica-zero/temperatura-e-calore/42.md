A solid stays in one phase. The first $10\,\mathrm{K}$ rise takes $2\,\mathrm{kJ}$, and the next $10\,\mathrm{K}$ takes $3\,\mathrm{kJ}$. What follows for the full $20\,\mathrm{K}$ rise?

- It takes $4\,\mathrm{kJ}$.
- It takes $2.5\,\mathrm{kJ}$.
- It takes $5\,\mathrm{kJ}$.
- It takes $6\,\mathrm{kJ}$.

## Explanation

The intervals are consecutive, so their actual heat inputs add. Using either value twice would ignore the change in heat capacity.
