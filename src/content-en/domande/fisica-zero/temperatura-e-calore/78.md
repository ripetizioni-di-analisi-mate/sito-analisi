A vacuum separates the polished walls of a double-walled vessel, but a metal bridge crosses the gap. Which change specifically reduces heat loss along that bridge?

- Further lower the pressure in the gap while leaving the bridge unchanged.
- Replace the bridge with a less conductive support of the same shape.
- Polish the facing walls more thoroughly without changing the bridge.
- Increase the cross-sectional area of the bridge without changing its material or length.

## Explanation

The bridge provides a solid conduction path even though the rest of the gap is empty. A less conductive material reduces transfer along that path.
