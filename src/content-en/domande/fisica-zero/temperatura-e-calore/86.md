A glass bottle full of hot water stands on an indoor windowsill. Which description of the energy transfer from the water to the air in the room is correct?

- The energy passes through the glass by convection, because the glass moves
- The energy passes to the air only by radiation, because glass is a solid
- The energy does not pass to the air until the water boils
- The energy passes through the glass by conduction and then also passes to the air by convection

## Explanation

In the glass wall the material does not move, so energy passes through it by **conduction**. The air in contact with the outer wall warms and moves, so it also carries energy by **convection**. In a real situation several modes of transfer can act together, and radiation is only one of them. The transfer takes place as long as there is a temperature difference, with no need for boiling.
