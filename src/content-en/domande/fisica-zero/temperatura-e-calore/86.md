Water and its vessel have a combined heat capacity of $500\,\mathrm{J/K}$. A constant $10\,\mathrm{W}$ heat loss continues while the heater is on. What heater power, entirely converted to heat, gives a rise of $3\,\mathrm{K/min}$?

- $35\,\mathrm{W}$
- $15\,\mathrm{W}$
- $10\,\mathrm{W}$
- $25\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

The rise requires $500\cdot 3=1500$ joules per minute, or $25\,\mathrm{W}$ net. The heater must also replace the $10\,\mathrm{W}$ loss, bringing the required input to $35\,\mathrm{W}$.
