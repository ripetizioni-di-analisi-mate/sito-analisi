The technical data sheet for a window glass lists the number $840$ next to the entry "specific heat capacity." Which unit should go with that number?

- $\mathrm{J/K}$
- $\mathrm{J/kg}$
- $\mathrm{J\cdot kg\cdot K}$
- $\mathrm{J/(kg\cdot K)}$

## Explanation

The specific heat capacity is the heat needed to raise the temperature of one kilogram of a substance by one kelvin. It is therefore obtained by dividing an energy by a mass and by a temperature change, so its unit is $\mathrm{J/(kg\cdot K)}$. The unit $\mathrm{J/K}$ belongs to the heat capacity of a whole object, while $\mathrm{J/kg}$ is the unit of latent heats. The glass therefore requires $840\,\mathrm{J}$ to warm one kilogram of material by $1\,\mathrm{K}$.
