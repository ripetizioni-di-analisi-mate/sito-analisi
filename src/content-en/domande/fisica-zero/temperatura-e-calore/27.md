An externally driven stirrer raises the temperature of water in a thermally insulated vessel. What transfer accounts for the water’s energy gain?

- Latent heat without a phase change.
- A shift of the Celsius zero.
- Heat entering through the insulated walls.
- Mechanical work done by the blades on the water.

## Explanation

Thermal insulation makes external heat transfer negligible. The blades transfer energy by doing work, and stirring can increase the water’s internal energy.
