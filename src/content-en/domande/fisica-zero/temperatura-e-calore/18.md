A drop of water has a heat capacity of $4\,\mathrm{J/K}$. To disturb its temperature as little as possible, which initially warmer probe should be used if all probes start at the same temperature?

- $0.04\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $40\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $0.4\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $4\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

For a given temperature change, the probe with the smallest heat capacity gives the least energy to the drop. Of the stated values, $0.04\,\mathrm{J/K}$ is the smallest.
