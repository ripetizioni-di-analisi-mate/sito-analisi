A bathtub holding $150\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water and a cup holding $0.25\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water are both at $40\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$, and both cool down to $20\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. Which statement is correct?

- The bathtub releases more energy than the cup, because it has a larger mass
- The bathtub and the cup release the same energy, because they have the same initial temperature
- The bathtub and the cup release the same energy, because they undergo the same temperature change
- The cup releases more energy, because it cools more quickly

## Explanation

Temperature alone does not determine how much energy an object transfers. For the same substance and the same temperature change, the equation $Q=mc\Delta T$ shows that the heat transferred is proportional to the mass. The two samples of water undergo the same change of $-20\,\mathrm{K}$, and the mass of the bathtub water is $600$ times that of the cup. Therefore, the bathtub releases $600$ times as much energy.
