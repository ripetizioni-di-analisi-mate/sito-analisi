An insulated vessel of negligible heat capacity receives $0.30\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water at $80\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ and $0.70\,\mathrm{kg}$ at $20\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. Assuming constant specific heat capacity, what temperature follows from the heat balance?

- $26\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $62\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $50\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $38\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

The common specific heat capacity cancels. The temperature in degrees Celsius is $\dfrac{0.30\cdot 80+0.70\cdot 20}{0.30+0.70}=38$.
