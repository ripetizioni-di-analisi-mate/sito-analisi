A temperature is $40$ degrees Celsius. What is it in kelvins?

- $313\,\mathrm{K}$
- $313.15\,\mathrm{K}$
- $-233\,\mathrm{K}$
- $233.15\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

The conversion formula is $T=t_C+273.15$, so add $273.15$ to the Celsius value. The result is $313.15$ kelvins.
