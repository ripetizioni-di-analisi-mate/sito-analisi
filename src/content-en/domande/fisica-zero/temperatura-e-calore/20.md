A temperature rises by $5$ degrees Celsius. What is the increase in kelvins?

- $278.15\,\mathrm{K}$
- $268\,\mathrm{K}$
- $50\,\mathrm{K}$
- $5\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

The Kelvin scale differs from the Celsius scale by an additive constant. When two temperatures are subtracted, the constant cancels. Therefore a rise of $5$ degrees Celsius equals a rise of $5$ kelvins.
