A steel spoon and a brick each receive $1000\,\mathrm{J}$ of heat, without a change of state. Which statement about their temperature change is correct?

- It is the same for both objects, because they receive the same energy
- It is larger for the brick, because the brick has a larger mass
- It depends only on the material, not on the mass of the objects
- It depends on the mass and the specific heat capacity of each object

## Explanation

The specific heat equation gives $\Delta T=\dfrac{Q}{mc}$. For the same $Q$, the temperature change therefore depends on the product of mass and specific heat capacity, that is, on the heat capacity of the object. The spoon and the brick have different masses and materials, so in general they undergo different changes. For the same material, a larger mass gives a smaller change.
