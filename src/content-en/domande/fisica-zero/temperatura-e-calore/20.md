A thermometer column grows linearly with temperature. It is $20\,\mathrm{mm}$ long at $0\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ and $100\,\mathrm{mm}$ long at $100\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. What temperature corresponds to a length of $40\,\mathrm{mm}$?

- $25\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $40\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $20\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $50\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

The calibration span is $80\,\mathrm{mm}$. The reading is $20\,\mathrm{mm}$ above the zero mark, one quarter of the span, so it corresponds to $25\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$.
