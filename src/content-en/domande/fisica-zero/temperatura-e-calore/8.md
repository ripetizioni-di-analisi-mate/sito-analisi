In a laboratory, a sample is stored in liquid nitrogen, which boils at $77\,\mathrm{K}$ at atmospheric pressure. What is this temperature in degrees Celsius?

- $350.15\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $196.15\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $-196.15\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $-77\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

To convert from kelvins to degrees Celsius, subtract $273.15$ from the value in kelvins. This gives $t_C=77-273.15=-196.15$, so liquid nitrogen boils at $-196.15\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. The result is negative because $77\,\mathrm{K}$ is a much lower temperature than the melting point of ice, which corresponds to $273.15\,\mathrm{K}$. The value $350.15\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ results from adding $273.15$ instead of subtracting it.
