A homogeneous $0.70\,\mathrm{kg}$ sample absorbs $12600\,\mathrm{J}$ and warms by $20\,\mathrm{K}$ without changing phase. What is its mean specific heat capacity?

- $441\,\mathrm{J/(kg\cdot K)}$
- $630\,\mathrm{J/(kg\cdot K)}$
- $900\,\mathrm{J/(kg\cdot K)}$
- $18000\,\mathrm{J/(kg\cdot K)}$

## Explanation

Divide the heat by the product of mass and temperature change to get $\dfrac{12600}{0.70\cdot 20}=900$ joules per kilogram per kelvin.
