A temperature is $30$ degrees Celsius. What is it in kelvins?

- $303.15\,\mathrm{K}$
- $303\,\mathrm{K}$
- $-243\,\mathrm{K}$
- $243.15\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

The conversion formula is $T=t_C+273.15$, so add $273.15$ to the Celsius value. The result is $303.15$ kelvins.
