A freezer reads $-25\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. What temperature should be marked on a diagram calibrated in kelvins?

- $25\,\mathrm{K}$
- $248.15\,\mathrm{K}$
- $298.15\,\mathrm{K}$
- $-25\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

Zero Celsius corresponds to $273.15\,\mathrm{K}$. Moving $25\,\mathrm{K}$ below this reference gives $248.15\,\mathrm{K}$.
