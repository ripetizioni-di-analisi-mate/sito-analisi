A clinical thermometer is placed under the armpit, and the instructions say to wait three minutes before reading the temperature. Why is this wait necessary?

- The body must reach the initial temperature of the thermometer
- All the heat contained in the body must pass into the thermometer
- The thermometer must exchange energy with the body until they reach thermal equilibrium
- The skin must cool down to room temperature

## Explanation

A thermometer shows its own temperature. In contact with the body, it exchanges energy with the body until the two temperatures are equal, that is, until they reach **thermal equilibrium**, and this exchange takes some time. In an ideal measurement, the thermometer changes the temperature of the body by a negligible amount. Therefore, after the wait, the reading is the temperature of the body.
