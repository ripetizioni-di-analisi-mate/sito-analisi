An object absorbs $3600$ J and has a heat capacity of $200$ J/K. By how much does its temperature rise?

- $720000\,\mathrm{K}$
- $3600\,\mathrm{K}$
- $18\,\mathrm{K}$
- $2\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

The heat capacity satisfies $Q=C\Delta T$. Therefore $\Delta T=\dfrac{Q}{C}$. This gives $\Delta T=\dfrac{3600}{200}=18$ kelvins.
