A thermal store must absorb $18\,\mathrm{kJ}$ with a temperature rise of at most $5\,\mathrm{K}$. Its material has a constant specific heat capacity of $600\,\mathrm{J/(kg\cdot K)}$. What minimum mass meets this limit?

- $6\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.6\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $3\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $30\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Each kilogram absorbs $3000\,\mathrm{J}$ for a rise of $5\,\mathrm{K}$. Dividing $18000\,\mathrm{J}$ by that energy per kilogram gives $6\,\mathrm{kg}$. A larger mass would warm less.
