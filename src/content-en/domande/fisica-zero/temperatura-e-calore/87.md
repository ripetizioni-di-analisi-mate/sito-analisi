A sample cools for $5\,\mathrm{min}$. Its outward heat-transfer rate falls linearly from $40\,\mathrm{W}$ to $0\,\mathrm{W}$ throughout that interval. How much energy does it release?

- $3\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $0.2\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kJ}$

## Explanation

The linear decrease gives an average power of $20\,\mathrm{W}$. In $300\,\mathrm{s}$ the sample releases $20\cdot 300=6000$ joules.
