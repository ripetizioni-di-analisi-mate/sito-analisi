Many pots have a metal body and wooden or plastic handles. What is the reason, from a thermal point of view?

- Wood and plastic transfer cold to the hand
- Wood and plastic block radiation from the flame
- Wood and plastic transfer energy by conduction more slowly than metal
- Wood and plastic have zero specific heat capacity

## Explanation

The handle is attached to the body of the pot, which is hot, and energy travels along it by conduction. Metal transfers energy faster than wood and plastic. With a wooden or plastic handle, energy therefore reaches the end held by the hand slowly, and that end stays at a lower temperature.
