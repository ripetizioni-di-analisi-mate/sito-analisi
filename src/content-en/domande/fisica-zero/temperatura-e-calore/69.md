At standard pressure, a transparent container of pure water reads $0\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. What phase information remains undetermined from temperature alone?

- The difference between this temperature and $0\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$.
- The melting temperature at the stated pressure.
- The Celsius value displayed.
- The fraction of mass present as ice.

## Explanation

Liquid and solid can coexist in different proportions at the melting point. A temperature reading does not give the mass of either phase.
