In a room heated by a wood stove in one corner, warm air rises above the stove and cooler air sinks along the opposite wall, until the whole room warms up. Which mode of heat transfer describes this circulation?

- Conduction
- Convection
- Radiation
- Evaporation

## Explanation

**Convection** involves the motion of a fluid, that is, a liquid or gas, which carries energy with it. Air is a gas, and portions of it move from the stove to the rest of the room, so the energy is carried by the motion of the air. In conduction the material does not move, while radiation takes place through electromagnetic waves; therefore, neither describes the circulation of the air.
