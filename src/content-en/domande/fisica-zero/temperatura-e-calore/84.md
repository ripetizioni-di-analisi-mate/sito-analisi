A temperature-controlled test box must absorb a specified external heat input of $50\,\mathrm{kJ}$ while keeping its contents at $0\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ with melting ice. With $L_f=334$ kJ/kg, which available ice mass is the smallest sufficient choice?

- $100\,\mathrm{g}$
- $300\,\mathrm{g}$
- $160\,\mathrm{g}$
- $50\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

The requirement is at least $\dfrac{50}{334}$ kilograms, about $150\,\mathrm{g}$. Of the available masses, $160\,\mathrm{g}$ is the smallest above that threshold.
