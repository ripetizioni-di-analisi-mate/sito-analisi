A calorimeter already contains water at $20\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. When hot water is added, its walls warm along with the water. Compared with a vessel of zero heat capacity, how does the final temperature change for the same water masses and initial temperatures?

- It always equals the added water’s temperature.
- It is higher.
- It is identical for every vessel.
- It is lower.

## Explanation

The initially cool walls absorb some of the available energy. This adds heat capacity on the cooler side of the balance.
