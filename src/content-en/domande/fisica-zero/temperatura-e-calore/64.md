While pasta is cooking, $0.30\,\mathrm{kg}$ of the water in the pot, already at $100\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$, turns into vapor at standard atmospheric pressure. How much energy does this water absorb to vaporize? Use $L_v=2\,260\,000\,\mathrm{J/kg}$.

- $67\,800\,\mathrm{J}$
- $678\,000\,\mathrm{J}$
- $100\,200\,\mathrm{J}$
- $2\,260\,000\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The water is already at its boiling point, so the heat absorbed goes only into vaporization, and $Q=mL_v$ is used. Therefore,
$$
Q=0.30\,\mathrm{kg}\cdot2\,260\,000\,\mathrm{J/kg}=678\,000\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
The value $100\,200\,\mathrm{J}$ results from mistakenly using the latent heat of fusion, which concerns the change from solid to liquid.
