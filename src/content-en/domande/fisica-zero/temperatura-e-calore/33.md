In an experiment, some of the measured heat also warms the vessel. A student assigns all the heat to the sample and calculates $c=\dfrac{Q}{m\Delta  T}$. How is the estimate affected?

- It becomes negative even while the sample warms.
- It is exact because the final temperature is shared.
- It is too low.
- It is too high.

## Explanation

The numerator includes the energy absorbed by the vessel. With the same mass and temperature rise, that extra energy overestimates the specific heat capacity.
