A sample of mass $4$ kg has a specific heat capacity of $300$ joules per kilogram per kelvin. What is its heat capacity?

- $75\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $1200\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $\dfrac{1}{75}\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $304\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

The heat capacity is $C=mc$. It depends on the mass of the object and the specific heat capacity of the material. Therefore $C=4\cdot300=1200$ joules per kelvin.
