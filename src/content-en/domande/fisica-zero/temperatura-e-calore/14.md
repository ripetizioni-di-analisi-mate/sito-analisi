At standard atmospheric pressure, $0.080\,\mathrm{kg}$ of ice at $0\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ is to become liquid water at $25\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. Neglecting losses, which expression gives the heat in kJ when $L_f=334$ in kJ/kg and $c=4.18$ in kJ/(kg·K)?

- $0.080(334-4.18\cdot 25)$
- $0.080(334+4.18\cdot 25)$
- $0.080\cdot 334$
- $0.080\cdot 4.18\cdot 25$

## Explanation

The ice absorbs energy as it melts. The water also absorbs energy as its temperature rises by $25\,\mathrm{K}$, so the two terms add to about $35.1\,\mathrm{kJ}$.
