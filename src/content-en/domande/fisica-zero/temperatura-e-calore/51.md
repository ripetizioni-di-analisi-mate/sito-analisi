A portion of water at $10\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ is combined with one at $30\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$, then a third portion at $80\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ is added. Under ideal insulated conditions, how does the final result change if only the order of addition changes?

- It is higher when the cold water is added last.
- It always equals the temperature of the second portion.
- The final temperature stays the same.
- It is higher when the hot water is added last.

## Explanation

With unchanged masses and initial temperatures, the initial total energy and the total heat capacity are unchanged. The final balance includes all three portions of water.
