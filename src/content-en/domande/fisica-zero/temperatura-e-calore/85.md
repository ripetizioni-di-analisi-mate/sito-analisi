A thermos has a double wall with a gap from which almost all the air has been removed. Why does the tea inside stay hot for many hours?

- The gap reduces conduction and convection to the outside
- The thermos produces energy that keeps the tea hot
- The glass of the thermos has a very large specific heat capacity
- The gap increases the specific heat capacity of the tea

## Explanation

Conduction and convection require a material, so in an almost empty space these two modes of transfer are greatly reduced. The tea therefore releases energy to its surroundings very slowly, and its temperature drops only a little. The thermos supplies no energy to the tea, and it approximates the isolated system used in mixing problems.
