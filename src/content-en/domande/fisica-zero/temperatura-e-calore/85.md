Warm water and $10\,\mathrm{g}$ of ice at $0\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ are put in an insulated vessel. Cooling the initial water to $0\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ would release $5.00\,\mathrm{kJ}$. Melting the ice requires $3.34\,\mathrm{kJ}$ and the heat capacity of all the water after melting is $0.830\,\mathrm{kJ/K}$. What is the final temperature?

- $4\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $6\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $2\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $0\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

After all the ice melts, $5.00-3.34=1.66$ kilojoules remain to warm all the water. Dividing by $0.830\,\mathrm{kJ/K}$ gives a rise of $2\,\mathrm{K}$ above $0\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$.
