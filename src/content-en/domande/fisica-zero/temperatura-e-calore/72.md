Dye reveals that water heated from below rises on one side and descends on the other. Which observation distinguishes convection from conduction alone?

- The vessel continues to receive energy from the heater.
- Moving portions of fluid carry energy with them.
- The temperature near the bottom exceeds that at the surface.
- Energy is also measured crossing the solid wall.

## Explanation

The dye follows the bulk motion of the water. Energy travels with the fluid, which is the defining feature of convection.
