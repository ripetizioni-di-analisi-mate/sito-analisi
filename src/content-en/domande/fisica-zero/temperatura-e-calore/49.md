Two samples in a calorimeter exchange $-800\,\mathrm{J}$ and $+650\,\mathrm{J}$ respectively. The system comprising the samples and the vessel walls is insulated. How much heat did the walls absorb?

- $1450\,\mathrm{J}$
- $150\,\mathrm{J}$
- $725\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-150\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Together the samples have released $150\,\mathrm{J}$. Since the complete system is insulated, the missing energy in the balance enters the walls.
