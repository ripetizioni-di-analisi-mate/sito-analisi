A person sits one meter from a very hot radiator, and their face receives energy by radiation. Which statement about this transfer is correct?

- Only the radiator emits radiation, because it is warmer than the person
- The radiator and the person both emit and absorb radiation, and the net transfer carries energy from the radiator to the person
- The radiation reaches the face because the air between the radiator and the person carries it
- The net transfer carries energy from the person to the radiator, because the person also emits radiation

## Explanation

In **radiation**, energy is carried by electromagnetic waves, which can also travel through a vacuum, so the air is not needed to carry it. Every object emits and absorbs radiation, so the person also sends radiation toward the radiator. The net transfer depends on both objects. Since the radiator is at a higher temperature than the person, the net energy flows from the radiator to the person, and the face warms up.
