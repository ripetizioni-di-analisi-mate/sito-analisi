Air in an insulating panel is split into many small closed cells instead of occupying one large cavity. Which transport mechanism is directly hindered by dividing the space?

- Conduction within the solid cell walls.
- Propagation of all electromagnetic waves.
- Radiative exchange between all opposing solid surfaces.
- Large-scale convective circulation of the air.

## Explanation

The cell walls interrupt the paths along which air could circulate. Energy can still cross the materials that form the panel.
