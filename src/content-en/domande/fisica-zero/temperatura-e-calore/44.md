Two experiments take $1.2\,\mathrm{kJ}$ and $2.4\,\mathrm{kJ}$ to raise the same vessel by $4\,\mathrm{K}$, first empty and then filled with a liquid. What is the heat capacity of the liquid alone?

- $600\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $300\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $1200\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $900\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

The liquid accounts for the extra $1200\,\mathrm{J}$. Dividing that energy by the common rise of $4\,\mathrm{K}$ gives $300\,\mathrm{J/K}$.
