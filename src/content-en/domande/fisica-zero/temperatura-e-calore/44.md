An object releases $700$ J. What is the heat $Q$ if heat absorbed by the object is taken as positive?

- $700\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-70\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-700\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The sign of the heat depends on the direction of energy transfer relative to the object. When heat is released, energy leaves the object. Under the stated convention, $Q=-700$ joules.
