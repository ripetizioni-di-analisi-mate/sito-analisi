A heat meter already shows $1.2\,\mathrm{kJ}$ before a sample is heated and $5.2\,\mathrm{kJ}$ afterwards. What energy belongs in the balance for this run alone?

- $1.2\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $6.4\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $5.2\,\mathrm{kJ}$

## Explanation

The meter accumulates energy readings. The run contributes the increase in the total, so $5.2-1.2=4$ kilojoules.
