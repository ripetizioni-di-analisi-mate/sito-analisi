On wet asphalt a car brakes with locked wheels, which skid on the road with $\mu_d=0.5$. Only friction acts horizontally. Using $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and the axis pointing in the direction of motion, what is the acceleration of the car?

- $+5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-0.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-50\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Kinetic friction opposes the sliding, so with the axis in the direction of motion the horizontal net force is $-\mu_dmg$. Dividing by the mass gives
$$
a=-\mu_dg=-0.5\cdot10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=-5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.
$$
The mass of the car cancels, so the result does not depend on it. The negative sign indicates that the car slows down.
