A toy water rocket pushes water downward through its nozzle and rises. Which force pushes the rocket upward?

- The force of the rocket on the expelled water
- The force of the air below the rocket
- The force of the expelled water on the rocket
- The weight of the water left in the rocket

## Explanation

The rocket pushes the water downward, so by Newton's third law the water pushes the rocket upward with a force of equal magnitude. Of the two forces in the interaction, the one exerted by the water acts on the rocket. Therefore the force of the water on the rocket lifts it, when it exceeds the rocket's weight. The force of the rocket on the water acts on the water and accelerates it downward.
