A $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ book lies on a level table with a $1\,\mathrm{kg}$ book on top. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, which pair gives the inter-book contact force and the table normal force, respectively?

- $10\,\mathrm{N}$ and $20\,\mathrm{N}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N}$ and $30\,\mathrm{N}$
- $30\,\mathrm{N}$ and $10\,\mathrm{N}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N}$ and $30\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The contact between books supports only the upper book. The table supports both books together, so it carries their total weight of $30\,\mathrm{N}$.
