A scale supports a $60\,\mathrm{kg}$ person in a lift moving upwards but slowing at $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what force does it read?

- $600\,\mathrm{N}$
- $720\,\mathrm{N}$
- $480\,\mathrm{N}$
- $120\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Acceleration points downwards, so $mg-N=m\cdot2$. The normal force is $120\,\mathrm{N}$ below the weight, giving $480\,\mathrm{N}$.
