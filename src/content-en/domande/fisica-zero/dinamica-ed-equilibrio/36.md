A swimmer at rest against the edge of the pool pushes off with his feet and starts moving. Which force sets the swimmer in motion?

- The force of the feet on the edge
- The force of the edge on the swimmer's feet
- The swimmer's weight
- The push of the water on the edge

## Explanation

The feet push on the edge, so by **Newton's third law** the edge exerts on the feet a force of equal magnitude and opposite direction. The motion of the swimmer depends on the forces that act on him. The force of the feet acts on the edge, so it does not accelerate the swimmer. The force of the edge on the feet, directed toward the middle of the pool, is what changes his velocity.
