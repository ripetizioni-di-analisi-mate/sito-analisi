A player’s shoe and a ball interact briefly. Which comparison holds for their contact forces during the interaction?

- They always point in the same direction
- The ball feels more force because it accelerates more
- Their magnitudes are equal at every instant
- They are equal only after separation

## Explanation

The forces belong to one interaction and act on different bodies. Newton’s third law gives equal magnitudes and opposite directions at every instant of contact.
