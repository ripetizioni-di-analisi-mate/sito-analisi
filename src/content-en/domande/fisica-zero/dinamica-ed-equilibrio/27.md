A car is traveling north on a straight road and brakes. What is the direction of its acceleration while braking?

- South
- North
- East
- The acceleration is zero

## Explanation

While braking, the speed decreases, so the change in velocity is opposite to the velocity itself. The car moves north, so the acceleration points south. By Newton's second law, the net force on the car also points south. The car keeps moving north until it stops.
