A trolley initially at rest on a track is subjected to two opposite horizontal forces of $6\,\mathrm{N}$ for $3\,\mathrm{s}$, with no other horizontal force. What is its displacement during that time?

- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The opposing forces sum to zero. With zero acceleration and zero initial velocity the trolley remains at rest.
