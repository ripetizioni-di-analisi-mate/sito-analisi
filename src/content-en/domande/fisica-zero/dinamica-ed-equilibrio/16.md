A kicked ball rolls across the grass and slows down until it stops. What can be inferred about the net force on the ball while it rolls?

- It points opposite to the direction of motion
- It points in the direction of motion and decreases
- It is zero
- It is vertical, pointing downward

## Explanation

The speed of the ball decreases, so the ball has an acceleration opposite to its motion. By Newton's second law, the net force points in the same direction as the acceleration. Therefore the net force points opposite to the motion, and it comes from the resistive forces of the grass and the air. A zero net force would instead keep the velocity constant.
