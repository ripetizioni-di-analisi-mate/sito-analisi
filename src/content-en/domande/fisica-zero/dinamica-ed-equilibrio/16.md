A body of mass $2$ kg is subject to a force of $24$ N to the right and a force of $8$ N to the left. What is the magnitude of its acceleration?

- $16\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $32\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The net force has magnitude $24-8=16$ newtons and points to the right. Divide by the mass of $2$ kg. Therefore the magnitude of the acceleration is $8$ meters per second squared.
