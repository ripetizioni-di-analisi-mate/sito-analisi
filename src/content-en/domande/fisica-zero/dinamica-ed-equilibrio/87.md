A constant pull accelerates a trolley on a smooth track. For the same measured mass and acceleration, a rough track requires $3\,\mathrm{N}$ more pulling force. What does the difference measure if other effects are identical?

- The change in its net force
- The trolley’s change in weight
- The entire pull on the rough track
- The extra resistance of the rough track

## Explanation

Equal mass and acceleration require the same resultant. The additional pull therefore balances the extra $3\,\mathrm{N}$ of resistance.
