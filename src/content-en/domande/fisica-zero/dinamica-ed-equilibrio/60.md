A crane lowers a $500\,\mathrm{kg}$ pallet of bricks that, starting from rest, accelerates downward at $1.2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Using $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the tension in the cable?

- $4305\,\mathrm{N}$
- $5505\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4905\,\mathrm{N}$
- $600\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

With the positive axis upward, the acceleration has component $a=-1.2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Newton's second law gives $T-mg=ma$, so
$$
T=m(g+a)=500\,\mathrm{kg}\cdot(9.81-1.2)\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=4305\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
The tension is less than the weight, $4905\,\mathrm{N}$, because the net force points downward.
