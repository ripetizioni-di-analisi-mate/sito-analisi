A $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ object changes from $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to the right in $3\,\mathrm{s}$. What was the magnitude of its average net force?

- $4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $2\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Average acceleration is $\dfrac{7-1}{3}=2$ metres per second squared. Multiplication by the constant mass gives $4\,\mathrm{N}$.
