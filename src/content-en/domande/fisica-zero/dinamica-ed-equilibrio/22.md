A bowling ball, already released by the player, moves in a straight line at constant velocity along the horizontal lane; friction and air resistance are negligible. Which forces appear in the free-body diagram of the ball?

- The weight, the normal force, and a forward force that keeps it moving
- The weight, the normal force, and the push of the player's hand
- Only the weight
- The weight and the normal force

## Explanation

The external forces on the ball are the forces that other bodies exert on it at the instant considered. Earth exerts the weight and the lane exerts the normal force. The hand no longer touches the ball, so its push does not appear in the diagram. The ball moves at constant velocity, so by Newton's second law the net force is zero and no forward force is needed. The weight and the normal force therefore have the same magnitude and balance each other.
