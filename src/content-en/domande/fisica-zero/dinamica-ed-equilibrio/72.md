A box rests on an incline. The downslope weight component is $12\,\mathrm{N}$, the normal force is $20\,\mathrm{N}$, and a rope pulls uphill with $18\,\mathrm{N}$. What static friction is needed, assuming sufficient grip?

- $18\,\mathrm{N}$ downhill
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$ uphill
- $6\,\mathrm{N}$ downhill
- $6\,\mathrm{N}$ uphill

## Explanation

Without friction $6\,\mathrm{N}$ would remain uphill. Equilibrium therefore requires $6\,\mathrm{N}$ downhill, against the tendency to slip.
