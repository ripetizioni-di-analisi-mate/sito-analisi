A net force of $30$ N acts on a mass of $3$ kg. What is its acceleration?

- $90\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0.1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $30\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Newton's second law gives $F_\mathrm{ris}=ma$. Solving for the acceleration gives $a=\dfrac{F_\mathrm{ris}}{m}$. Therefore $a=\dfrac{30}{3}=10$ meters per second squared.
