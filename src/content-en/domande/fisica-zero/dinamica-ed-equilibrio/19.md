A box resting on a conveyor belt moves in a straight line at a constant $0.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$, with zero net force. What distance does it travel in $40\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $80\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $40\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The net force is zero, so by Newton's first law the velocity of the box remains constant. The motion is therefore uniform and straight, and the distance is the product of speed and time,
$$
s=0.5\,\mathrm{m/s}\cdot 40\,\mathrm{s}=20\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
The value $80\,\mathrm{m}$ comes from dividing the time by the speed instead of multiplying them.
