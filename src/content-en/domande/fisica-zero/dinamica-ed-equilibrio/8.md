A $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass hangs motionless from an ideal vertical string. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what tension does a spring scale in the string show?

- $40\,\mathrm{N}$
- $80\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The string supports the full weight. With zero acceleration, tension and weight have equal magnitudes, so the scale reads $40\,\mathrm{N}$.
