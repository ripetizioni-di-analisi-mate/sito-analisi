A shopping cart receives a push of $15\,\mathrm{N}$ to the right and one of $40\,\mathrm{N}$ to the left; the vertical forces balance. With the $x$-axis pointing to the left, what is the component $R_x$ of the net force?

- $-25\,\mathrm{N}$
- $55\,\mathrm{N}$
- $25\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-55\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

With the positive axis pointing to the left, the leftward push has component $+40\,\mathrm{N}$ and the rightward push has component $-15\,\mathrm{N}$. Therefore
$$
R_x=40\,\mathrm{N}-15\,\mathrm{N}=25\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
The positive sign indicates that the net force points to the left. With the axis pointing to the right, one would obtain $-25\,\mathrm{N}$, which describes the same force.
