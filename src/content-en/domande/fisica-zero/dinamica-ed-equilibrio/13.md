The frame fixed to the ground is treated as inertial. Which of the following frames can be treated as inertial with the same approximation?

- A bus that is braking
- A car accelerating at a green light
- A train traveling along a straight track at constant velocity
- A spinning merry-go-round

## Explanation

A frame that moves at constant velocity, without rotating, relative to an inertial frame is also inertial. The train on the straight track at constant velocity satisfies this condition, so it is inertial like the ground. The braking bus and the accelerating car have an acceleration relative to the ground, so they are accelerating frames. The merry-go-round rotates, so it is not an inertial frame either.
