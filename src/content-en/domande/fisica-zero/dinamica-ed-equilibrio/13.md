A $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ trolley starts from rest on a frictionless track. A constant horizontal pull of $6\,\mathrm{N}$ acts for $4\,\mathrm{s}$. How far does the trolley travel during that time?

- $48\,\mathrm{m}$
- $24\,\mathrm{m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Newton’s second law gives $a=3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The distance from rest is therefore $\dfrac{3\cdot4^2}{2}=24$ metres.
