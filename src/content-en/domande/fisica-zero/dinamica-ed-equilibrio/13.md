A body of mass $4$ kg hangs at rest from a single vertical string. With $g=10$ meters per second squared, what is the tension in the string?

- $40\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $80\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The forces on the body are the downward weight and the upward tension. In equilibrium their magnitudes are equal. Therefore $T=mg=40$ newtons.
