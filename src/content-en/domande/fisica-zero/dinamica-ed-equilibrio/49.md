A light rope passes over an ideal pulley. The direction changes by 90° between two segments of this rope. What stays the same on both sides of the pulley?

- The entire tension vector with all its components
- The sum of the two tension vectors, which is always zero
- The tension magnitude
- The vertical component of each tension

## Explanation

An ideal pulley redirects the rope while preserving tension magnitude. Forces act along their respective segments and thus have different directions.
