A mass of $7$ kg has an acceleration of $3$ meters per second squared. What is the magnitude of the net force?

- $\dfrac{7}{3}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $21\,\mathrm{N}$
- $\dfrac{3}{7}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The net force is the product of mass and acceleration. Substituting gives $F_\mathrm{ris}=7\cdot3$. Therefore its magnitude is $21$ newtons.
