A bench rests on two scales that measure vertical force. A child, the only load, changes seats and sits still again without touching the floor. The left scale now reads $30\,\mathrm{N}$ more than before. How does the right scale reading change?

- It drops by $30\,\mathrm{N}$.
- It rises by $30\,\mathrm{N}$.
- It stays the same because the child’s weight is unchanged.
- It drops by $15\,\mathrm{N}$ because there are two scales.

## Explanation

Compare the two states at rest. The sum of the readings balances the weight of the same bench and child, so it stays constant. The increase on the left requires an equal decrease on the right.
