A passenger and a bus floor accelerate together forwards at $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ without slipping. The passenger has a mass of $50\,\mathrm{kg}$ and foot friction is the only horizontal force. What is its magnitude?

- $500\,\mathrm{N}$
- $50\,\mathrm{N}$
- $25\,\mathrm{N}$
- $100\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

To accelerate with the bus the passenger needs a net force of $50\cdot2=100$ newtons. Static friction supplies that force.
