The same horizontal net force is applied first to an empty wheelbarrow and then to the same wheelbarrow full of soil. Which statement is true?

- The full wheelbarrow has the larger acceleration
- The empty wheelbarrow has the larger acceleration
- The two accelerations are equal, because the force is the same
- The full wheelbarrow stays at rest

## Explanation

By Newton's second law, the acceleration is $a=\dfrac{R}{m}$. The net force is the same in both cases, so the acceleration is larger for the smaller mass. The empty wheelbarrow has the smaller mass, so it accelerates more. The full wheelbarrow still accelerates, because a nonzero net force acts on it.
