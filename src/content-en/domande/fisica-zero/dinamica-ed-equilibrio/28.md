Two skaters push off each other briefly. The force between their hands varies during contact. Its impulse on skater A is $+6\,\mathrm{N\,s}$. What is the impulse of the same interaction on skater B, using the same axis?

- $-6\,\mathrm{N\,s}$
- $+6\,\mathrm{N\,s}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N\,s}$
- It depends on the ratio of the skaters’ masses.

## Explanation

At every part of the contact, the two hand forces have equal magnitudes, opposite directions and the same duration. Their impulses, which add the force contributions over time, are therefore opposite. The masses affect the velocity changes but not this relation.
