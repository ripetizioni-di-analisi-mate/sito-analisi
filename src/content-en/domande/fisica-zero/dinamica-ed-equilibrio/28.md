A net force of $36$ N acts on a mass of $3$ kg. What is its acceleration?

- $108\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{12}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $36\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Newton's second law gives $F_\mathrm{ris}=ma$. Solving for the acceleration gives $a=\dfrac{F_\mathrm{ris}}{m}$. Therefore $a=\dfrac{36}{3}=12$ meters per second squared.
