A $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ crate rests on the ground. An upward rope pull increases to $25\,\mathrm{N}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what happens once the pull reaches $25\,\mathrm{N}$?

- The normal force remains $20\,\mathrm{N}$ upwards
- The crate stays still with a $45\,\mathrm{N}$ normal force
- The normal force becomes $5\,\mathrm{N}$ downwards
- The crate lifts and the normal force becomes zero

## Explanation

The weight is $20\,\mathrm{N}$ and the ground can only push upwards. The pull exceeds the weight, so contact is lost and the normal force vanishes.
