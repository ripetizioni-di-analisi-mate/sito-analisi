A bus brakes and a bottle lying on its floor rolls forward. How is this explained in the ground frame?

- The floor pushes the bottle forward during braking
- The bottle tends to keep its velocity while the bus slows down
- Braking produces a forward force that acts on the bottle
- The bottle accelerates forward relative to the ground

## Explanation

The ground is the inertial frame in which the laws of dynamics hold. Only very small horizontal forces act on the bottle, so its velocity relative to the ground stays almost the same as before braking. The bus, on the other hand, slows down, so the bottle ends up moving forward relative to the floor. In the frame of the bus, which is accelerating, the laws written for the ground do not hold in the same form.
