A sled moving at $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ slows solely under kinetic friction on a level surface. With $\mu_d=0.4$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, how long does it take to stop?

- $0.5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Dividing friction by mass gives a deceleration of $\mu_dg=4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Losing $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ therefore takes $2\,\mathrm{s}$.
