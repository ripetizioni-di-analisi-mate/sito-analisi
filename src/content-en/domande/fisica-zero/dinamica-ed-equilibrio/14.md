The normal force is $40$ N and the coefficient of kinetic friction is $0.2$. What is the magnitude of the kinetic frictional force?

- $200\,\mathrm{N}$
- $8\,\mathrm{N}$
- $40\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.8\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the kinetic frictional force is $F_a=\mu_dN$. Multiply $0.2$ by $40$. Therefore $F_a=8$ newtons.
