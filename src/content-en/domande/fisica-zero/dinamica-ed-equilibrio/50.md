Which of the following bodies is in equilibrium?

- A car starting at a green light
- An elevator rising at constant velocity
- A ball thrown upward, at the highest point of its path
- A cyclist braking before an intersection

## Explanation

A body is in equilibrium when the net force on it is zero, that is, when its acceleration is zero. The elevator rising at constant velocity has zero acceleration, so it is in dynamic equilibrium. The car starting and the cyclist braking change their velocity, so they have an acceleration. At the highest point the ball has zero velocity, but its weight acts on it, so its acceleration is $g$ downward.
