An ideal movable pulley supports a load at rest with two vertical segments of the same rope. The load weighs $80\,\mathrm{N}$. What is the rope tension?

- $160\,\mathrm{N}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N}$
- $40\,\mathrm{N}$
- $80\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Each segment pulls upwards on the pulley with tension $T$. Load equilibrium requires $2T=80$ newtons, so $T=40$ newtons.
