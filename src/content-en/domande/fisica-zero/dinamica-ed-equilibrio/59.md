A block remains at rest under a $12\,\mathrm{N}$ horizontal push with a normal force of $40\,\mathrm{N}$. What follows about $\mu_s$ from this test alone?

- $\mu_s\ge0.30$
- $\mu_s<0.30$
- $\mu_s=0.30$ exactly
- $\mu_s=0$

## Explanation

For the block to stay still, maximum friction must be at least $12\,\mathrm{N}$. This gives a lower bound without proving the threshold has been reached.
