A cat is sleeping on a cushion. According to Newton's third law, which force forms the pair of forces of the same interaction with the cat's weight?

- The force with which the cat pulls on Earth
- The normal force of the cushion on the cat
- The force of the cat on the cushion
- The weight of the cushion

## Explanation

The cat's weight is the gravitational pull that Earth exerts on the cat. By Newton's third law, in the same interaction the cat exerts on Earth a force of equal magnitude, pointing upward. The normal force of the cushion also acts on the cat and balances the weight, but it belongs to the interaction between the cat and the cushion. The two forces of a third-law pair always act on different bodies.
