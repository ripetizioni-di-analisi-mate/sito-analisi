A block is held at rest against a rough vertical wall by a horizontal push. If the push increases while the block stays at rest, what happens to the actual friction magnitude?

- It becomes equal to the normal force
- It always grows in proportion to the push
- It remains equal to the weight
- It falls to zero

## Explanation

Vertically the unchanged weight still requires the same upward friction for equilibrium. Only the maximum available friction increases.
