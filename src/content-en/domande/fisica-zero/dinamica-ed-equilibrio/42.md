The normal force is $140$ N and the coefficient of static friction is $0.3$. What is the maximum static friction?

- $140\,\mathrm{N}$
- $42\,\mathrm{N}$
- $\dfrac{1400}{3}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $21\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Static friction can have a magnitude of up to $\mu_sN$. Its maximum value is $0.3\cdot140$. Therefore the maximum is $42$ newtons.
