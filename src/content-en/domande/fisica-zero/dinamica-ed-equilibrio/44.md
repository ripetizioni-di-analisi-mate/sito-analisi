A $5\,\mathrm{kg}$ block rests on a frictionless 30° incline, held by a string parallel to the slope. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and $\sin30^\circ=0.5$, what tension is needed?

- $12.5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $50\,\mathrm{N}$
- $25\,\mathrm{N}$
- $43.3\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The string balances the component of weight along the slope. It is $mg\sin30^\circ=25$ newtons.
