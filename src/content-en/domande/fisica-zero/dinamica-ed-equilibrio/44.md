Two friends stand at rest on two skateboards, side by side; the one with mass $40\,\mathrm{kg}$ pushes the one with mass $60\,\mathrm{kg}$ with a force of $120\,\mathrm{N}$. Friction is negligible. What is the magnitude of the acceleration of the one who pushes?

- $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

By Newton's third law, the person being pushed exerts a force of $120\,\mathrm{N}$ in the opposite direction on the person who pushes. This is the only horizontal force on the one who pushes, so by Newton's second law
$$
a=\frac{120\,\mathrm{N}}{40\,\mathrm{kg}}=3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.
$$
The value $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ is instead the acceleration of the $60\,\mathrm{kg}$ friend.
