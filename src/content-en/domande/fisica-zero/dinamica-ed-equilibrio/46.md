A $90\,\mathrm{N}$ laboratory model is supported at rest by two vertical strings. Each string is rated for at most $60\,\mathrm{N}$. The load sharing is unknown. What condition on the first tension $T_1$ is equivalent to both strings staying within their ratings?

- $0\,\mathrm{N}\le T_1\le60\,\mathrm{N}$
- $60\,\mathrm{N}\le T_1\le90\,\mathrm{N}$
- $30\,\mathrm{N}\le T_1\le60\,\mathrm{N}$
- $T_1=45\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Equilibrium gives the second tension as $T_2=90\,\mathrm{N}-T_1$. The first rating requires $T_1\le60\,\mathrm{N}$. The second requires $90\,\mathrm{N}-T_1\le60\,\mathrm{N}$, or $T_1\ge30\,\mathrm{N}$. Unequal sharing can still meet both limits.
