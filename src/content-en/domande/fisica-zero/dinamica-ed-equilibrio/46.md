A $12\,\mathrm{kg}$ microwave oven rests on a horizontal countertop, and only its weight and the force of the countertop act on it. Using $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the normal force?

- $117.72\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1.22\,\mathrm{N}$
- $235.44\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The oven is at rest, so the net vertical force is zero. Taking upward as positive gives $N-mg=0$, so
$$
N=mg=12\,\mathrm{kg}\cdot9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=117.72\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
The value $12\,\mathrm{N}$ confuses mass with weight, which is measured in newtons.
