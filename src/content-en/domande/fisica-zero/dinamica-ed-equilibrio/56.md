A driver's hands exert two forces of equal magnitude and opposite direction on the steering wheel: one on the top of the rim, to the right, and one on the bottom of the rim, to the left. Which statement is true?

- The net force is zero and the wheel rotates, because a body that can rotate also requires conditions on the torques
- The net force is zero, so the wheel stays still
- The net force has twice the magnitude of each force
- The net force is nonzero, because the forces are applied at different points

## Explanation

The two forces have equal magnitudes and opposite directions, so their vector sum is zero. The condition of zero net force, however, concerns a particle. The steering wheel is an extended body that can rotate, and the two forces, applied at different points, make it turn. Describing the rotation therefore requires additional conditions on the torques of the forces.
