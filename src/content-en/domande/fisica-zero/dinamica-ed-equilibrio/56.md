An object is compared with standard masses on an equal-arm balance on Earth and on the Moon. Neglecting air, what happens to the measured mass?

- It falls in proportion to the weight
- It becomes zero without an atmosphere
- It increases because gravity is weaker
- It remains the same

## Explanation

Local gravity multiplies both the unknown mass and the reference masses. It cancels in the balance condition.
