A $50\,\mathrm{kg}$ wardrobe is at rest on a horizontal floor, with a coefficient of static friction $\mu_s=0.4$. Using $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the maximum static friction?

- $20\,\mathrm{N}$
- $125\,\mathrm{N}$
- $200\,\mathrm{N}$
- $500\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Only the weight and the normal force act vertically on the wardrobe, so $N=mg=50\,\mathrm{kg}\cdot10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=500\,\mathrm{N}$. The maximum **static friction** is
$$
F_{s,\max}=\mu_sN=0.4\cdot500\,\mathrm{N}=200\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
The value $20\,\mathrm{N}$ comes from multiplying $\mu_s$ by the mass instead of by the normal force.
