A wooden block slides on a table first on its broad face and then on its narrow face. In the constant-coefficient model, at the same weight and with no other vertical forces, what happens to friction?

- It halves if area halves
- It doubles if area halves
- Its magnitude stays the same
- It vanishes on the narrow face

## Explanation

The model uses the product of the coefficient and the normal force. Both are unchanged under the stated conditions, so friction stays unchanged.
