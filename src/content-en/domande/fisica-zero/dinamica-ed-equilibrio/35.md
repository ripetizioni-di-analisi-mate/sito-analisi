A person stands on a scale calibrated in newtons inside a lift. It reads $600\,\mathrm{N}$ at rest. What does it read while travelling upwards at constant velocity?

- $600\,\mathrm{N}$
- $300\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1200\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Constant velocity still means zero acceleration. The scale’s support therefore continues to balance the $600\,\mathrm{N}$ weight.
