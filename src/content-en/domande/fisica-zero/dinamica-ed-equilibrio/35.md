During a stroke, a ping-pong ball of mass $2.7\,\mathrm{g}$ receives a net force of $0.054\,\mathrm{N}$. What is its acceleration?

- $0.02\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $200\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

A newton equals $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s^2}$, so the mass must be expressed in kilograms, $m=2.7\,\mathrm{g}=0.0027\,\mathrm{kg}$. By Newton's second law,
$$
a=\frac{0.054\,\mathrm{N}}{0.0027\,\mathrm{kg}}=20\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.
$$
The value $0.02\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ comes from dividing by $2.7$ without converting grams to kilograms.
