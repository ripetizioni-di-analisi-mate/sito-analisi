The normal force is $60$ N and the coefficient of static friction is $0.3$. What is the maximum static friction?

- $60\,\mathrm{N}$
- $18\,\mathrm{N}$
- $200\,\mathrm{N}$
- $9\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Static friction can have a magnitude of up to $\mu_sN$. Its maximum value is $0.3\cdot60$. Therefore the maximum is $18$ newtons.
