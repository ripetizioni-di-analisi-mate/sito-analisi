Two forces act on a small boat in the horizontal plane: the push of the current, $30\,\mathrm{N}$ east, and the push of the wind, $40\,\mathrm{N}$ north, as shown in the figure. What is the magnitude of the net force of these two forces?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="-0.4,-0.4 0.4,-0.4 0.4,0.4 -0.4,0.4" vettori="0,0,3,0,30 N;0,0,0,4,40 N" riferimento="-3,-1.6,0,1.2,east,north" xmin=-3.6 xmax=4.6 ymin=-2 ymax=4.6 titolo="Horizontal forces on the boat seen from above; one unit in the diagram represents $10\,\mathrm{N}$"}

- $50\,\mathrm{N}$
- $70\,\mathrm{N}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N}$
- $35\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Take the $x$-axis pointing east and the $y$-axis pointing north. The components of the net force are then $R_x=30\,\mathrm{N}$ and $R_y=40\,\mathrm{N}$. Since the two components are perpendicular, the magnitude follows from the Pythagorean theorem,
$$
R=\sqrt{(30\,\mathrm{N})^2+(40\,\mathrm{N})^2}=50\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
The sum $70\,\mathrm{N}$ would hold only for two forces with the same direction.
