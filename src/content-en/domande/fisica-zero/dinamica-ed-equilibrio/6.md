A book rests on a horizontal table standing on the floor. Treat the book and the table together as one system and neglect air. Which forces belong in its external-force diagram?

- The book’s weight and the table’s normal force on the book.
- Both weights, the floor’s force and both contact forces between the book and table.
- The book’s force on the table and the table’s force on the floor.
- The book’s weight, the table’s weight and the floor’s support force on the table.

## Explanation

The system boundary surrounds both the book and the table. Their contact forces on one another are internal. Earth exerts both weights and the floor supports the table from outside that boundary, giving three external forces.
