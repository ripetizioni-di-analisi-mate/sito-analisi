On a level stretch of snow, the coefficient of kinetic friction between skis and snow is $0.05$. If a $70\,\mathrm{kg}$ skier glides along that stretch, what is the frictional force on the skis, using $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$?

- $3.5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $35\,\mathrm{N}$
- $350\,\mathrm{N}$
- $700\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The snow is horizontal and only the weight and the normal force act vertically, so $N=mg=700\,\mathrm{N}$. In the **kinetic friction** model, the magnitude of friction is
$$
F_d=\mu_dN=0.05\cdot700\,\mathrm{N}=35\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
The value $700\,\mathrm{N}$ is the normal force, and $3.5\,\mathrm{N}$ comes from multiplying $\mu_d$ by the mass.
