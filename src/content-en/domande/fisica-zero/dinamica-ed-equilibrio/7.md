A sled is pulled forward by two dogs, with $60\,\mathrm{N}$ and $40\,\mathrm{N}$; the other forces balance. A student draws these two arrows in the free-body diagram of the sled, together with the arrow of the net force $\vec R$, and then adds all three arrows. Which statement about the net force on the sled is correct?

- It is $100\,\mathrm{N}$ forward
- It is $200\,\mathrm{N}$ forward
- It is $20\,\mathrm{N}$ forward
- It is $50\,\mathrm{N}$ forward

## Explanation

The net force is the vector sum of the forces applied to the sled. The two pulls point in the same direction, so $R=60\,\mathrm{N}+40\,\mathrm{N}=100\,\mathrm{N}$ forward. The arrow of $\vec R$ represents this sum and not an additional interaction, so it must not be added to the two pulls again. By adding $\vec R$ as well, the student obtains $200\,\mathrm{N}$, which counts the same forces twice.
