A hand presses down with $7\,\mathrm{N}$ on a $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ box resting on a horizontal surface. Taking $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what force magnitude does the surface exert on the box?

- $27\,\mathrm{N}$
- $7\,\mathrm{N}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N}$
- $13\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Weight and the hand’s force both point downwards. For equilibrium the surface exerts $20+7=27$ newtons upwards.
