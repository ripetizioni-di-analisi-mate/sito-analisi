A body of mass $2$ kg is subject to a force of $18$ N to the right and a force of $6$ N to the left. What is the magnitude of its acceleration?

- $12\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $24\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The net force has magnitude $18-6=12$ newtons and points to the right. Divide by the mass of $2$ kg. Therefore the magnitude of the acceleration is $6$ meters per second squared.
