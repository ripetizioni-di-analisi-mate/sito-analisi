A block slides down an incline at constant velocity under just gravity and contact. Given $\sin\theta=0.6$ and $\cos\theta=0.8$, what acceleration down the slope will it have if friction is removed, taking $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$?

- $10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Friction initially balanced the tangential component of weight. Removing it leaves that component alone, producing $g\sin\theta=6$ metres per second squared.
