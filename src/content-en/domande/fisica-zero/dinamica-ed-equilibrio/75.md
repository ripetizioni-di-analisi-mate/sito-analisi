To hold a $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ brick still while marking it with a pencil, someone presses it against the horizontal table with a vertical force of $10\,\mathrm{N}$. With $\mu_s=0.5$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the maximum static friction between the table and the brick?

- $10\,\mathrm{N}$
- $15\,\mathrm{N}$
- $5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $30\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The brick is at rest vertically, so the normal force balances the weight and the push of the hand, $N=mg+F=20\,\mathrm{N}+10\,\mathrm{N}=30\,\mathrm{N}$. The maximum static friction is therefore
$$
\mu_sN=0.5\cdot30\,\mathrm{N}=15\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
The value $10\,\mathrm{N}$ comes from using $N=mg$, that is, ignoring the push of the hand.
