The normal force is $70$ N and the coefficient of kinetic friction is $0.2$. What is the magnitude of the kinetic frictional force?

- $14\,\mathrm{N}$
- $350\,\mathrm{N}$
- $70\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1.4\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the kinetic frictional force is $F_a=\mu_dN$. Multiply $0.2$ by $70$. Therefore $F_a=14$ newtons.
