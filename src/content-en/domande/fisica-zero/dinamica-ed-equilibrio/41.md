A $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ object is pressed against a vertical wall with a horizontal force of $50\,\mathrm{N}$. It is at rest and there are no other horizontal forces. What is the wall’s normal-force magnitude?

- $20\,\mathrm{N}$
- $50\,\mathrm{N}$
- $80\,\mathrm{N}$
- $30\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The normal force is horizontal and must balance the applied push. Weight acts vertically and does not enter this balance.
