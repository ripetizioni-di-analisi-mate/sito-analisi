Two sleds of different masses slide at the same speed on level ground with the same $\mu_d$. If friction alone acts horizontally, which stops first?

- The lighter one stops first because it has less inertia
- Neither stops if their masses differ
- They stop at the same time
- The heavier one stops first because friction is greater

## Explanation

Friction and inertia are both proportional to mass. Deceleration is $\mu_d g$ for both, so equal initial speeds give equal stopping times.
