A climber hangs from a vertical rope. In which of the following situations is the tension in the rope greatest?

- While at rest
- While rising at constant velocity
- While being pulled up with an upward acceleration
- While being lowered with a downward acceleration

## Explanation

With the axis upward, Newton's second law gives $T-mg=ma$, so $T=m(g+a)$. When the climber is at rest or rises at constant velocity, $a=0$, so the tension equals the weight. With a downward acceleration, $a$ is negative and the tension is less than the weight. The tension is therefore greatest when the acceleration points upward.
