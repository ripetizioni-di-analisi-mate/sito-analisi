Two ideal spring scales are placed in series in a taut, light rope at rest. One reads $18\,\mathrm{N}$. What does the other read?

- $9\,\mathrm{N}$
- $36\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $18\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

A light rope segment in equilibrium transmits the same tension at both ends. Both scales therefore measure $18\,\mathrm{N}$.
