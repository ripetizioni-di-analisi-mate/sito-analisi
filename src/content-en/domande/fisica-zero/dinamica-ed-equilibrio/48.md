An $8\,\mathrm{kg}$ backpack rests on the floor and is pulled upward with a vertical force of $30\,\mathrm{N}$, without being lifted. Using $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the normal force of the floor on the backpack?

- $108.48\,\mathrm{N}$
- $78.48\,\mathrm{N}$
- $30\,\mathrm{N}$
- $48.48\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The weight acts downward on the backpack, while the force of the hand and the normal force act upward. The backpack stays at rest, so taking upward as positive gives $N+F-mg=0$. Therefore
$$
N=mg-F=78.48\,\mathrm{N}-30\,\mathrm{N}=48.48\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
The value $108.48\,\mathrm{N}$ comes from giving the force of the hand the wrong direction.
