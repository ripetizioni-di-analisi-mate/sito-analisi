A coin on a perfectly smooth table in a railway carriage appears to slide backwards when the train accelerates forwards. Which description applies in the ground frame during the short initial interval?

- The coin’s weight reverses
- The coin retains its horizontal velocity
- The coin acquires the train’s acceleration
- The table exerts a backward horizontal force on the coin

## Explanation

The smooth surface exerts no horizontal force. Relative to the ground the coin coasts while the carriage accelerates beneath it.
