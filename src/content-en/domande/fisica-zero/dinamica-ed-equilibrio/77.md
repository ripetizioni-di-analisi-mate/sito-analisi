A chair is at rest on a horizontal floor and nobody pushes it. What is the static frictional force of the floor on the chair?

- It equals $\mu_sN$
- It equals $\mu_dN$
- It is zero
- It equals the weight of the chair

## Explanation

Static friction adjusts to the force needed to prevent sliding, up to the maximum value $\mu_sN$. No horizontal force other than friction acts on the chair, and the chair is at rest, so the horizontal net force is zero. Therefore the static frictional force is also zero. The value $\mu_sN$ is only the maximum available.
