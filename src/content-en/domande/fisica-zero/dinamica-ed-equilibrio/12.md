A space probe, very far from any planet or star, travels with its engines off and no appreciable force acts on it. How does it move relative to an inertial frame?

- In a straight line at constant velocity
- It slows down until it stops
- It stops as soon as the engines are turned off
- It accelerates in the direction of motion

## Explanation

No appreciable force acts on the probe, so the net force is zero. By the **law of inertia**, its velocity remains constant in magnitude and direction. Therefore the probe continues in a straight line at the velocity it had when the engines were turned off. Slowing down would require a net force opposite to the motion, and there is none here.
