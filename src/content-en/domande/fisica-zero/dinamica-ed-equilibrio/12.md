An $8\,\mathrm{kg}$ sled moves at constant velocity on a horizontal surface with $\mu_d=0.15$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The pull is horizontal. What is its magnitude?

- $68\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{N}$
- $80\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Kinetic friction is $0.15\cdot80=12$ newtons. Maintaining constant velocity requires a pull of the same magnitude.
