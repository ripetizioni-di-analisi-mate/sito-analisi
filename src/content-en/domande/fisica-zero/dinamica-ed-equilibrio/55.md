A $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ bucket is being lowered by a rope rated at $30\,\mathrm{N}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what maximum upward acceleration can the rope produce to slow its descent?

- $10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $25\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

At maximum tension the upward net force is $30-20=10$ newtons. Dividing by $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ gives the limit of $5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
