A bunch of helium balloons is tied to a $0.5\,\mathrm{kg}$ toy lying on the floor and pulls it vertically upward with a force of $6\,\mathrm{N}$. Using $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what happens to the toy?

- It stays on the floor and the normal force is $1.095\,\mathrm{N}$ downward
- It stays on the floor and the normal force is $10.905\,\mathrm{N}$
- It leaves the floor and accelerates upward
- It stays on the floor and the normal force is zero

## Explanation

The weight of the toy is $0.5\,\mathrm{kg}\cdot9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=4.905\,\mathrm{N}$. If the toy stayed on the floor at rest, equilibrium would give $N=4.905\,\mathrm{N}-6\,\mathrm{N}=-1.095\,\mathrm{N}$. A negative value of $N$ indicates that the assumption of contact is not satisfied, because the floor can only push the toy. Therefore the toy leaves the floor, and the net force $6\,\mathrm{N}-4.905\,\mathrm{N}=1.095\,\mathrm{N}$ upward makes it accelerate upward.
