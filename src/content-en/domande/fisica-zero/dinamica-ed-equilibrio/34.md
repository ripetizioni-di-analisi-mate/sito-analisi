The graph shows the velocity $v$, in meters per second, of a $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ cart moving along a straight track, as a function of time $t$, in seconds. What is the net force on the cart?

::grafico{curve="t,2*t,0,3.5" nomi="t,v" legenda=0 punti="3,6,pieno" segmenti="3,0,3,6;0,6,3,6" xmin=-0.5 xmax=4 ymin=-0.8 ymax=8 titolo="Velocity of the cart: $t$ in seconds, $v$ in meters per second"}

- $1\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$
- $2\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The graph is a straight line that goes from $v=0$ at $t=0$ to $v=6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at $t=3\,\mathrm{s}$, so the acceleration is constant. Its slope gives
$$
a=\frac{6\,\mathrm{m/s}}{3\,\mathrm{s}}=2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.
$$
By Newton's second law, the net force is $R=ma=2\,\mathrm{kg}\cdot2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=4\,\mathrm{N}$. The value $2\,\mathrm{N}$ confuses the acceleration with the force.
