Two identical blocks are on a frictionless surface. The first is pushed away from the second and no string joins them. Neglecting mutual gravity, what force does the first exert on the second after separation?

- It equals the external push
- It equals one block’s weight
- It equals half the external push
- It is zero

## Explanation

Contact can transmit compression while the surfaces touch. Once separated, the blocks have none of the stated interactions between them.
