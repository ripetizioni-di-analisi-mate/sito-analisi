A body of mass $2$ kg is subject to a force of $36$ N to the right and a force of $12$ N to the left. What is the magnitude of its acceleration?

- $24\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $48\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The net force has magnitude $36-12=24$ newtons and points to the right. Divide by the mass of $2$ kg. Therefore the magnitude of the acceleration is $12$ meters per second squared.
