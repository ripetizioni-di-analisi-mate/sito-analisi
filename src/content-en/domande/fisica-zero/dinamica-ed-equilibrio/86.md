A trolley moves right at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ on a smooth track. A net force of $10\,\mathrm{N}$ acts leftwards for $1\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the largest mass for which its final velocity is zero or leftwards?

- $4\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $10\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $40\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Stopping requires a leftward velocity change of at least $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Thus $\dfrac{10\cdot1}{m}$ must be at least 4, giving $m\le2.5$ kilograms.
