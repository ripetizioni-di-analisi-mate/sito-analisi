As an incline is slowly tilted, a block begins to slip when $\tan\theta=0.40$. Only gravity and contact forces act. What is the static-friction coefficient?

- $0.16$
- $2.5$
- $0.80$
- $0.40$

## Explanation

At the threshold $mg\sin\theta=\mu_s mg\cos\theta$. Dividing by the normal component of weight gives $\mu_s=\tan\theta=0.40$.
