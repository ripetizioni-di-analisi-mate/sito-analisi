A box rests on the bed of a truck that accelerates forward on a level road, and the box does not slide relative to the truck bed. What is the direction of the static frictional force on the box?

- Backward, opposite to the motion of the truck
- Downward
- It has no direction, because friction is zero
- Forward, in the direction of the acceleration

## Explanation

The box does not slide on the truck bed, so relative to the ground it has the same acceleration as the truck, pointing forward. By Newton's second law, the net force on the box then points forward. The only horizontal force on the box is the friction of the truck bed, so static friction points forward. Without this force, the box would keep its velocity and the truck bed would slide under it.
