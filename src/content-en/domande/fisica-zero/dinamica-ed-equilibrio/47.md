Two masses of $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ hang at rest in series, with the $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass on top and ideal vertical strings. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, by how much does the top string tension exceed the intervening string tension?

- $20\,\mathrm{N}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N}$
- $50\,\mathrm{N}$
- $30\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The lower string and the upper body’s weight both pull down on that body. Equilibrium makes the tension difference equal to the upper body’s own weight, $20\,\mathrm{N}$.
