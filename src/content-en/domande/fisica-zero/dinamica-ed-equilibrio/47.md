A $0.5\,\mathrm{kg}$ block of butter is at rest on a kitchen scale, and a finger presses it down with a vertical force of $3\,\mathrm{N}$. Using $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the normal force of the scale on the block?

- $7.905\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4.905\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1.905\,\mathrm{N}$
- $3\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The weight and the push of the finger act downward on the block, and the normal force acts upward. The block is at rest, so taking upward as positive gives $N-mg-F=0$. Therefore
$$
N=mg+F=0.5\,\mathrm{kg}\cdot9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}+3\,\mathrm{N}=7.905\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
The value $4.905\,\mathrm{N}$ is the weight alone, that is, it ignores the push of the finger.
