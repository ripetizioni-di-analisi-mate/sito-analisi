A person standing in a train releases an object without pushing it. The train moves at constant velocity and air resistance is negligible. What horizontal force must act to maintain the object’s horizontal train velocity after release?

- No horizontal force
- A force equal to its previous vertical support
- A horizontal force equal to its weight
- A constant force along the train’s direction

## Explanation

The object already has that horizontal velocity at release. By Newton’s first law it keeps it when the horizontal resultant is zero.
