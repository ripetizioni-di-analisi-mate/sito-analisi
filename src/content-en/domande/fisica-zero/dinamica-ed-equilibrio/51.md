Standing still on a bathroom scale, a person places her hands on a nearby sink and presses down on it. The scale reads the normal force on her feet. What happens to the scale reading?

- It increases, because the hands push downward
- It remains equal to the person's weight
- It decreases, because the sink pushes the hands upward
- It drops to zero

## Explanation

The hands press down on the sink, so by Newton's third law the sink pushes the hands upward with a force $F$. The forces on the person are then the weight downward, the normal force $N$ of the scale, and the force $F$ upward. The person is at rest, so $N+F-mg=0$ and $N=mg-F$. The normal force, and with it the scale reading, is therefore less than the weight.
