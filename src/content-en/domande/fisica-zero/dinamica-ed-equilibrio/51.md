A $60\,\mathrm{N}$ lamp hangs from two identical symmetric strings, each 60° from vertical. What is the tension in each, given $\cos60^\circ=0.5$?

- $30\,\mathrm{N}$
- $15\,\mathrm{N}$
- $120\,\mathrm{N}$
- $60\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The horizontal components cancel. Each vertical contribution is $T\cos60^\circ$, so their sum is $T$ and equals $60\,\mathrm{N}$.
