A pull sensor reads $12\,\mathrm{N}$ while a $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ trolley accelerates at $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ along the pull. What is the magnitude of the only other horizontal force, a resistance?

- $8\,\mathrm{N}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The required resultant is $2\cdot4=8$ newtons. Resistance therefore subtracts $12-8=4$ newtons from the pull.
