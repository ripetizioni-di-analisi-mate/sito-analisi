A body of mass $5$ kg is at rest on a horizontal surface. Only its weight and the normal force act on it. With $g=10$ meters per second squared, what is the normal force?

- $50\,\mathrm{N}$
- $5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $500\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The body is in vertical equilibrium. The normal force therefore has the same magnitude as the weight. Since $P=mg$, we obtain $N=50$ newtons.
