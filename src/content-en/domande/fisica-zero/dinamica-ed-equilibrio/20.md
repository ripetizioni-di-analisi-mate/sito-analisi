An electric scooter with its rider has a total mass of $75\,\mathrm{kg}$ and receives a horizontal net force of $150\,\mathrm{N}$. What is its acceleration?

- $0.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $225\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

By **Newton's second law**, the net force is the product of mass and acceleration, so the acceleration is the ratio of net force to mass,
$$
a=\frac{R}{m}=\frac{150\,\mathrm{N}}{75\,\mathrm{kg}}=2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.
$$
The value $0.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ comes from inverting the ratio, that is, dividing the mass by the force.
