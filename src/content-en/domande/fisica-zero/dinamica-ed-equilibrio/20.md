A mass of $5$ kg has an acceleration of $3$ meters per second squared. What is the magnitude of the net force?

- $\dfrac{5}{3}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $8\,\mathrm{N}$
- $15\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The net force is the product of mass and acceleration. Substituting gives $F_\mathrm{ris}=5\cdot3$. Therefore its magnitude is $15$ newtons.
