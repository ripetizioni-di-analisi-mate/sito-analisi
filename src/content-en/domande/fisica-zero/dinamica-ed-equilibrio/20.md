A graph shows horizontal net force vertically and acceleration horizontally. A trolley’s data lie on a line through the origin and $(2\,\mathrm{m/s^2},6\,\mathrm{N})$. What does its slope measure?

- A velocity of $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- A mass of one third of a kilogram
- A mass of $3\,\mathrm{kg}$
- A constant force of $3\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The relation $F=ma$ is a straight line of slope $m$. Dividing $6\,\mathrm{N}$ by $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ therefore gives $3\,\mathrm{kg}$.
