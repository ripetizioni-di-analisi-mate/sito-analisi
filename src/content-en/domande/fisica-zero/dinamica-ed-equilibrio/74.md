A block is launched uphill on a rough slope and is still moving upwards. How do the weight component and kinetic friction combine along the slope?

- Friction points uphill and weight downhill
- Both point uphill
- They cancel for every angle
- Both point downhill

## Explanation

Friction opposes uphill slipping and points downhill. The weight component also points downhill, so their magnitudes add.
