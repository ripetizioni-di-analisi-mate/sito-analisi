A coefficient of static friction is measured for tires on dry asphalt. Which of the following ways of writing its value is correct?

- $\mu_s=0.9$
- $\mu_s=0.9\,\mathrm{N}$
- $\mu_s=0.9\,\mathrm{N/kg}$
- $\mu_s=0.9\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The maximum static friction is $F_{s,\max}=\mu_sN$, where $F_{s,\max}$ and $N$ are both forces measured in newtons. So $\mu_s=\dfrac{F_{s,\max}}{N}$ is the ratio of two forces. The units cancel, so the coefficient has no units and is written $\mu_s=0.9$.
