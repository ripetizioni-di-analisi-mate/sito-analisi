A body of mass $3$ kg hangs at rest from a single vertical string. With $g=10$ meters per second squared, what is the tension in the string?

- $0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $3\,\mathrm{N}$
- $60\,\mathrm{N}$
- $30\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The forces on the body are the downward weight and the upward tension. In equilibrium their magnitudes are equal. Therefore $T=mg=30$ newtons.
