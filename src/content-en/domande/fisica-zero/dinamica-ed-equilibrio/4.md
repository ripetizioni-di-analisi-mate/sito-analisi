A crate moves at constant velocity. A $9\,\mathrm{N}$ resistance to the left and a pulling force are its only horizontal forces. What is the pulling force?

- $9\,\mathrm{N}$ to the right
- $9\,\mathrm{N}$ to the left
- $0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $18\,\mathrm{N}$ to the right

## Explanation

Constant velocity means zero acceleration. The net horizontal force must therefore vanish, so the pull balances the resistance.
