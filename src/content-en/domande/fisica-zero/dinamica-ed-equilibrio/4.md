Two people push a broken-down car east along a straight road, one with $250\,\mathrm{N}$ and the other with $180\,\mathrm{N}$. What is the net force of the two pushes?

- $70\,\mathrm{N}$ east
- $430\,\mathrm{N}$ east
- $215\,\mathrm{N}$ east
- $430\,\mathrm{N}$ west

## Explanation

Take east as the positive direction. The two pushes have components $+250\,\mathrm{N}$ and $+180\,\mathrm{N}$, so
$$
R_x=250\,\mathrm{N}+180\,\mathrm{N}=430\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
Since the component is positive, the net force points east. The difference $70\,\mathrm{N}$ would be correct only for two pushes in opposite directions.
