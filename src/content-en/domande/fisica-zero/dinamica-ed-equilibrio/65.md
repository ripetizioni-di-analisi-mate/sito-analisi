A crate weighing $100\,\mathrm{N}$ slides right. Its rope pulls with components $30\,\mathrm{N}$ right and $20\,\mathrm{N}$ up. With $\mu_d=0.25$ and a level surface, what is the horizontal net-force magnitude?

- $20\,\mathrm{N}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N}$
- $5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $30\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The pull unloads the contact, reducing the normal force to $80\,\mathrm{N}$ and friction to $20\,\mathrm{N}$. The horizontal resultant is $30-20=10$ newtons to the right.
