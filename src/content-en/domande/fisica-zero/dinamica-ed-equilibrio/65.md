An $8\,\mathrm{kg}$ bucket hangs from a rope that passes over an ideal pulley, and a person holds the other end of the rope still. Using $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, with what force does the person pull on the rope?

- $39.24\,\mathrm{N}$
- $78.48\,\mathrm{N}$
- $156.96\,\mathrm{N}$
- $8\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The bucket is at rest, so the tension in the rope equals its weight, $T=8\,\mathrm{kg}\cdot9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=78.48\,\mathrm{N}$. The rope and the pulley are ideal, so the tension has the same value along the whole rope. Therefore the person pulls the free end with $78.48\,\mathrm{N}$. The pulley changes the direction of the force, while its magnitude stays the same.
