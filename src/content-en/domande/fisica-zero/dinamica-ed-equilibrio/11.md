A $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ block starts from rest under a horizontal pull of $24\,\mathrm{N}$. On the level surface $\mu_s=0.5$, $\mu_d=0.25$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is its acceleration just after it starts moving?

- $1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The pull exceeds the $20\,\mathrm{N}$ static threshold. During sliding the friction is $10\,\mathrm{N}$, giving $\dfrac{24-10}{4}=3.5$ metres per second squared.
