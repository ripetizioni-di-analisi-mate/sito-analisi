A mass of $4$ kg has an acceleration of $3$ meters per second squared. What is the magnitude of the net force?

- $\dfrac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.75\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$
- $7\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The net force is the product of mass and acceleration. Substituting gives $F_\mathrm{ris}=4\cdot3$. Therefore its magnitude is $12$ newtons.
