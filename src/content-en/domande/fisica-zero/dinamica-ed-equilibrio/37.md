A person starts walking on a horizontal floor. Which external force makes the person accelerate forward?

- The friction of the floor on the soles, pointing forward
- The force of the soles on the floor, pointing backward
- The force of the leg muscles
- The normal force of the floor

## Explanation

The foot pushes the floor backward with a tangential force, so by Newton's third law the floor exerts on the foot a tangential force pointing forward, that is, a frictional force. This force acts on the person and is horizontal, so it is the force that accelerates the person forward. The force of the muscles is internal to the body and is not one of the external forces. The normal force is vertical, so it does not contribute to the horizontal acceleration.
