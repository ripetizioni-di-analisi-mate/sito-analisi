A person in free fall together with a scale stays near its platform without pressing on it. What does an ideal scale read before any impact?

- The person’s weight
- $0\,\mathrm{N}$
- Twice the weight
- Weight minus the scale’s mass

## Explanation

Both the person and platform already accelerate at $g$ under gravity. No normal force is needed for that motion and the contact sensor reads zero.
