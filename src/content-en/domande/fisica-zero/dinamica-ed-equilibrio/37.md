A net force of $42$ N acts on a mass of $3$ kg. What is its acceleration?

- $14\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $126\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{14}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $42\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Newton's second law gives $F_\mathrm{ris}=ma$. Solving for the acceleration gives $a=\dfrac{F_\mathrm{ris}}{m}$. Therefore $a=\dfrac{42}{3}=14$ meters per second squared.
