A box starts together with a van on its level cargo bed. If $\mu_s=0.3$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the van’s maximum horizontal acceleration without slipping?

- $7\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0.3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The box requires $ma$ and the maximum available friction is $\mu_s mg$. Dividing by mass gives $a\le\mu_s g=3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
