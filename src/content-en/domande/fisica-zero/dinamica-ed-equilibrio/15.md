A suitcase is pulled across a horizontal floor at constant velocity. The only horizontal resistive force is friction, $35\,\mathrm{N}$. What is the horizontal pulling force?

- $0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $70\,\mathrm{N}$
- $45\,\mathrm{N}$
- $35\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The suitcase moves at constant velocity, so its acceleration is zero. By Newton's second law, the net force is then zero. Only the pull and friction act horizontally, so their magnitudes are equal and the pull is $35\,\mathrm{N}$. A larger pull would produce an acceleration, and the velocity would not remain constant.
