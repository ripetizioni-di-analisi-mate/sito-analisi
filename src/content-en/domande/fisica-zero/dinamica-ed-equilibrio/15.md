A skater moves on an ideal frictionless surface. A brief horizontal push ends, leaving only weight and the normal force. What happens to the skater’s velocity?

- It gradually reverses
- It immediately returns to zero
- It keeps increasing because of the earlier push
- It keeps the value already reached

## Explanation

Weight and the normal force balance, so the net force is zero. In an inertial frame the skater retains the velocity already acquired.
