The normal force is $80$ N and the coefficient of static friction is $0.3$. What is the maximum static friction?

- $80\,\mathrm{N}$
- $\dfrac{800}{3}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $24\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Static friction can have a magnitude of up to $\mu_sN$. Its maximum value is $0.3\cdot80$. Therefore the maximum is $24$ newtons.
