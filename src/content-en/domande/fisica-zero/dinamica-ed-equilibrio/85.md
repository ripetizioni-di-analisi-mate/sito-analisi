A test trolley experiences net forces along one line of $+4$ N for $3\,\mathrm{s}$ and $-6$ N for $2\,\mathrm{s}$. With constant mass, how do its final and initial velocities compare?

- The final velocity always has the opposite sign
- They are equal
- The final velocity is always more negative
- The final velocity is always zero

## Explanation

The signed force–time products are $+12$ and $-12$ newton seconds. Dividing by the same mass gives changes in velocity that cancel.
