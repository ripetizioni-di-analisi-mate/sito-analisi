A $25\,\mathrm{kg}$ sandbag is dragged at constant velocity across a horizontal floor with $\mu_d=0.4$. Using $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what horizontal pulling force is needed?

- $250\,\mathrm{N}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N}$
- $62.5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $100\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The bag moves at constant velocity, so the horizontal net force is zero and the pull has the same magnitude as the kinetic friction. The normal force is $N=mg=250\,\mathrm{N}$, so
$$
F=\mu_dN=0.4\cdot250\,\mathrm{N}=100\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
The value $250\,\mathrm{N}$ is the weight of the bag, which in this case is balanced by the normal force.
