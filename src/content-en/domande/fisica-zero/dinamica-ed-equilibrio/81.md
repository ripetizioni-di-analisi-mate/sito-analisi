A box is dragged across a surface at constant velocity by a horizontal spring stretched $2\,\mathrm{cm}$. The spring constant is 100 N/m. What is the kinetic friction magnitude?

- $50\,\mathrm{N}$
- $200\,\mathrm{N}$
- $2\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.02\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The extension is $0.02\,\mathrm{m}$, so the spring pull is $100\cdot0.02=2$ newtons. Constant velocity requires equal friction in the opposite direction.
