A vertical rope with appreciable mass supports a stationary load. Comparing the section near the ceiling with the one just above the load, which has greater tension?

- The one near the ceiling
- The one just above the load
- They are equal for any rope mass
- Both tensions are zero

## Explanation

The upper section also carries the weight of the rope below it. The lower section carries almost only the load.
