Two boxes of books are stacked on the floor: the top one has a mass of $3\,\mathrm{kg}$, the bottom one $5\,\mathrm{kg}$. Using $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the normal force of the floor on the bottom box?

- $49.05\,\mathrm{N}$
- $29.43\,\mathrm{N}$
- $78.48\,\mathrm{N}$
- $19.62\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The top box is at rest, so the bottom box supports it with a force equal to its weight, $3\,\mathrm{kg}\cdot9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=29.43\,\mathrm{N}$. By Newton's third law, the top box presses down on the bottom box with $29.43\,\mathrm{N}$. The bottom box is also at rest, so
$$
N=29.43\,\mathrm{N}+5\,\mathrm{kg}\cdot9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=78.48\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
The value $49.05\,\mathrm{N}$ ignores the box resting on top.
