Two people pull the ends of a rope in opposite directions, each with a force of $100\,\mathrm{N}$, and the rope stays at rest. What is the tension in the rope?

- $100\,\mathrm{N}$
- $200\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $50\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The tension is the magnitude of the force that the rope exerts at each end. Each person pulls the rope with $100\,\mathrm{N}$, so by Newton's third law the rope pulls each person with $100\,\mathrm{N}$. Therefore the tension is $100\,\mathrm{N}$. The value $0\,\mathrm{N}$ is instead the net force of the two forces applied to the rope, which is zero because the rope is at rest.
