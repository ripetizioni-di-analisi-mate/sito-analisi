A crate initially rests with a maximum static friction of $12\,\mathrm{N}$ and kinetic friction of $8\,\mathrm{N}$. The pull slowly reaches $13\,\mathrm{N}$, then drops to $10\,\mathrm{N}$ while the crate is sliding. What happens just after the reduction?

- It continues to accelerate along the pull
- It stops as soon as the pull falls below $12\,\mathrm{N}$
- It maintains exactly constant velocity
- It slows because the pull was reduced

## Explanation

Sliding has already begun, so friction is $8\,\mathrm{N}$. A $2\,\mathrm{N}$ net force remains along the pull and speed continues to rise.
