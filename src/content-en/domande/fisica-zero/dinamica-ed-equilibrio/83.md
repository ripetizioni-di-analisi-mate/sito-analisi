A plate slides along a bar counter at $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and only friction acts horizontally, with $\mu_d=0.2$. Using $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, after how much time does the plate stop?

- $10\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

With the axis in the direction of motion, the acceleration due to friction alone is $a=-\mu_dg=-2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The equation $v=v_0+at$ with $v=0$ gives $0=2\,\mathrm{m/s}-2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}\cdot t$. Therefore
$$
t=\frac{2\,\mathrm{m/s}}{2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}}=1\,\mathrm{s}.
$$
The value $10\,\mathrm{s}$ comes from dividing the speed by $\mu_d$ without multiplying by $g$.
