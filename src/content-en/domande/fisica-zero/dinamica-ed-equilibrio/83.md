A $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ object is rising through air. At one instant air resistance is $6\,\mathrm{N}$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the acceleration magnitude during ascent at that instant?

- $13\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $7\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

During ascent weight and drag both point downwards. The net force is $26\,\mathrm{N}$, producing $13\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ on the $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass.
