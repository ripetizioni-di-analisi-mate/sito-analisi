A crate rests on a level surface, with weight and normal force its only vertical forces. If the mass doubles and the materials stay the same, what happens to maximum static friction?

- It doubles
- It stays the same
- It quadruples
- It halves

## Explanation

Normal force equals weight and therefore doubles. With the same coefficient, the product $\mu_s N$ doubles too.
