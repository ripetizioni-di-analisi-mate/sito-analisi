A vase is at rest on a horizontal table. A book is placed on top of the vase and stays at rest. What happens to the normal force of the table on the vase?

- It remains equal to the weight of the vase
- It decreases
- It increases by an amount equal to the weight of the book
- It increases by twice the weight of the book

## Explanation

The book is at rest, so the vase supports it with a force equal to the weight of the book. By Newton's third law, the book presses down on the vase with a force of the same magnitude. The vase is at rest, so the normal force of the table must balance both the weight of the vase and this new force. The normal force therefore increases by exactly the weight of the book.
