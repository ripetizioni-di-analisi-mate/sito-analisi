A block slides on a level surface under a $10\,\mathrm{N}$ pull. An extra $20\,\mathrm{N}$ downward push is added while the block continues sliding in the same direction with $\mu_d=0.25$. By how much does friction increase?

- $10\,\mathrm{N}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The downward push increases the normal force by $20\,\mathrm{N}$. Friction changes by $\mu_d\Delta N=0.25\cdot20=5$ newtons irrespective of its initial value.
