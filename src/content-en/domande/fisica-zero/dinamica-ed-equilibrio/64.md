A dog pulls on its leash, which stays taut, and its owner holds it back. What is the direction of the force of the leash on the dog's collar?

- Along the leash, from the owner's hand toward the collar
- Vertical, downward
- Perpendicular to the leash
- Along the leash, from the collar toward the owner's hand

## Explanation

A taut string exerts on the body attached to it a force along the string, directed away from the body. The leash is attached to the collar, so its force on the collar is directed along the leash. The leash can only pull the collar, so the force points from the collar toward the owner's hand. This is the force that holds the dog back.
