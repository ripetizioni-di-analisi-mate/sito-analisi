A skydiver with equipment has a mass of $80\,\mathrm{kg}$ and descends vertically at constant velocity. Using $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the resistive force of the air?

- $80\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1569.6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $784.8\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The velocity is constant, so the acceleration is zero and the skydiver is in dynamic equilibrium. The vertical forces are the weight downward and the air resistance upward, so their magnitudes are equal. The weight is
$$
mg=80\,\mathrm{kg}\cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=784.8\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
Therefore the air resistance is $784.8\,\mathrm{N}$.
