A body of mass $4$ kg moves in an inertial frame and the net force on it is zero. How does its velocity change?

- It remains constant
- It immediately drops to zero
- It increases at a constant rate
- It decreases at a constant rate

## Explanation

Newton's second law gives zero acceleration when the net force is zero. Zero acceleration means constant velocity. The body can be at rest or move in a straight line at constant speed.
