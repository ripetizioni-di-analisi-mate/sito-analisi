An object of positive mass has zero instantaneous velocity but accelerates to the right. What is true of its net force in an inertial frame?

- It points left to keep the object at rest
- It is nonzero and points right
- Its direction depends only on position
- It is zero because velocity is zero

## Explanation

Newton’s second law relates net force to instantaneous acceleration. Zero velocity at that instant does not cancel the stated acceleration.
