A person pushes a cabinet across the floor. Which of the following forces is an external force acting on the cabinet?

- The force of the cabinet on the person
- The force of the floor on the person's feet
- The person's weight
- The push of the person on the cabinet

## Explanation

The external forces on the cabinet are the forces that other bodies exert on the cabinet. The push of the person is exerted on the cabinet, so it is one of its external forces. The force of the cabinet on the person and the force of the floor on the feet act on the person, so they belong to the diagram of the person. The person's weight also acts on the person and not on the cabinet.
