A vertical string raises a $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ bucket with an upward acceleration of $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Taking $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and neglecting air resistance, what is the tension?

- $30\,\mathrm{N}$
- $36\,\mathrm{N}$
- $24\,\mathrm{N}$
- $6\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Taking upwards as positive gives $T-mg=ma$. Tension supplies the $30\,\mathrm{N}$ needed to balance weight plus the $6\,\mathrm{N}$ of upward net force.
