A constant $5\,\mathrm{N}$ force acts to the right for $2\,\mathrm{s}$ on a $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ object, with no other horizontal force. What is the change in velocity even if the object was initially moving left?

- $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to the right
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to the left
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to the right
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to the right

## Explanation

The force produces $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ to the right. Over $2\,\mathrm{s}$ the accumulated change is $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in that direction, independent of initial velocity.
