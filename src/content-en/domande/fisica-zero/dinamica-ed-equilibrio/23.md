A kitchen spring scale measures forces in newtons. Which combination of SI units is equivalent to $1\,\mathrm{N}$?

- $1\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s^2}$
- $1\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s^2}$
- $1\,\mathrm{kg/s^2}$

## Explanation

Newton's second law $R=ma$ relates force to the product of a mass and an acceleration. Mass is measured in $\mathrm{kg}$ and acceleration in $\mathrm{m/s^2}$, so force is measured in $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s^2}$. The newton is defined by this very relation, so $1\,\mathrm{N}=1\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s^2}$. The unit $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$ is that of a mass times a velocity.
