The normal force is $50$ N and the coefficient of kinetic friction is $0.2$. What is the magnitude of the kinetic frictional force?

- $250\,\mathrm{N}$
- $50\,\mathrm{N}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the kinetic frictional force is $F_a=\mu_dN$. Multiply $0.2$ by $50$. Therefore $F_a=10$ newtons.
