A ball hangs on a string inside a bus accelerating forwards. It settles at a constant angle relative to the bus. Which side of the suspension point’s vertical line does the ball occupy?

- Behind the suspension point
- Directly below the suspension point
- On whichever side its initial velocity points towards
- Ahead of the suspension point

## Explanation

Tension points from the ball towards its support and needs a forward component to accelerate the ball. The ball must therefore hang behind the support.
