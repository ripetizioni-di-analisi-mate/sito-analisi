A toy chest stays at rest on the floor while one child pushes it with $40\,\mathrm{N}$ to the right and another with $25\,\mathrm{N}$ to the left. What is the static frictional force on the chest?

- $15\,\mathrm{N}$ to the right
- $65\,\mathrm{N}$ to the left
- $15\,\mathrm{N}$ to the left
- $0\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

With the axis to the right, the two pushes give $40\,\mathrm{N}-25\,\mathrm{N}=15\,\mathrm{N}$. The chest is at rest, so the horizontal net force is zero and the static friction $F_x$ satisfies $15\,\mathrm{N}+F_x=0$. Therefore $F_x=-15\,\mathrm{N}$, that is, the frictional force is $15\,\mathrm{N}$ to the left. Friction opposes the tendency of the chest to move to the right.
