For which of the following questions can a car be represented as a particle?

- Whether it tips over on a sharp bend
- How many turns each wheel makes in one kilometer
- Where the jack should be placed to lift it
- How long it takes to travel $120\,\mathrm{km}$ on a highway

## Explanation

The **particle** model applies when the shape and rotation of the body have a negligible effect on the problem. Over a distance of $120\,\mathrm{km}$, the dimensions of the car, a few meters, are negligible, so it is enough to follow the position of one of its points. Tipping over depends on the points where the forces act, so it requires the dimensions of the car. The number of turns concerns the rotation of the wheels and the position of the jack concerns the shape of the body, so in these two cases the model is not enough either.
