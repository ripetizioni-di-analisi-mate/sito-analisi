A net force of $18$ N acts on a mass of $3$ kg. What is its acceleration?

- $6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $54\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Newton's second law gives $F_\mathrm{ris}=ma$. Solving for the acceleration gives $a=\dfrac{F_\mathrm{ris}}{m}$. Therefore $a=\dfrac{18}{3}=6$ meters per second squared.
