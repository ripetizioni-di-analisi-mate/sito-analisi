A $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ trolley experiences $15\,\mathrm{N}$ to the right and $6\,\mathrm{N}$ to the left. The vertical forces balance. What is its acceleration taking right as positive?

- $9\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $7\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The horizontal resultant is $15-6=9$ newtons. Dividing by $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ gives an acceleration of $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ to the right.
