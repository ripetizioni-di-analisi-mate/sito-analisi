A rope supporting a rising bucket breaks. What is the net force immediately afterwards if air resistance is neglected?

- The previous tension, upwards
- Weight alone, downwards
- The sum of weight and the previous tension
- Zero while the bucket continues upwards

## Explanation

Breaking the rope removes the interaction that transmitted tension. Gravity remains even though the bucket is still moving upwards for a short time.
