A car travels along a straight stretch of highway at a constant $90\,\mathrm{km/h}$. Which statement about the forces acting on the car is true?

- The driving force exceeds the resistive forces
- The net force points in the direction of motion
- No force acts on the car
- The net force is zero

## Explanation

The car moves in a straight line at constant velocity, so its acceleration is zero. By Newton's second law, the net force is also zero. Several forces still act on the car, namely the weight, the normal force of the road, the driving force, and the resistive forces, and they balance. A driving force larger than the resistive forces would make the speed increase.
