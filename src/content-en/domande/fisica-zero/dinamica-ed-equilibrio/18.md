A hand exerts a force of $4$ N on a cart. On which body does the reaction force exerted by the cart act?

- On the cart, in the same direction
- On the hand
- On the cart, in the opposite direction
- Only on Earth

## Explanation

The action and reaction forces are part of the same interaction. They act on two different bodies with equal magnitudes and opposite directions. The reaction force exerted by the cart therefore acts on the hand.
