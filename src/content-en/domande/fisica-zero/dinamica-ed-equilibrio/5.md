Two initially stationary trolleys of $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $6\,\mathrm{kg}$ push apart on a frictionless track. If the first accelerates at $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the second one’s acceleration magnitude at that instant?

- $12\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The force on the first has magnitude $12\,\mathrm{N}$. By Newton’s third law the second feels an equally large force, producing $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ on its double mass.
