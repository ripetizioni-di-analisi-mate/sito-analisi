The normal force is $30$ N and the coefficient of kinetic friction is $0.2$. What is the magnitude of the kinetic frictional force?

- $6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $150\,\mathrm{N}$
- $30\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.6\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the kinetic frictional force is $F_a=\mu_dN$. Multiply $0.2$ by $30$. Therefore $F_a=6$ newtons.
