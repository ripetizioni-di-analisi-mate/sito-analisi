Three children pull a toy cart with three horizontal ropes, as shown in the figure: two pull to the right with $24\,\mathrm{N}$ and $18\,\mathrm{N}$, the third pulls to the left with $30\,\mathrm{N}$. The vertical forces balance. What is the net force on the cart?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="-0.6,-0.5 0.6,-0.5 0.6,0.5 -0.6,0.5" vettori="0.6,0.25,4.6,0.25,24 N;0.6,-0.25,3.6,-0.25,18 N;-0.6,0,-5.6,0,30 N" xmin=-6.2 xmax=5.4 ymin=-1.3 ymax=1.3 titolo="Horizontal forces on the cart; one unit in the diagram represents $6\,\mathrm{N}$"}

- $12\,\mathrm{N}$ to the left
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$ to the right
- $72\,\mathrm{N}$ to the right
- $0\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Take the axis to the right, so the two rightward forces have positive components and the leftward force has a negative component. The sum of the components is
$$
R_x=24\,\mathrm{N}+18\,\mathrm{N}-30\,\mathrm{N}=12\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
Since $R_x$ is positive, the net force is $12\,\mathrm{N}$ to the right. The value $72\,\mathrm{N}$ comes from adding the magnitudes without taking the directions into account.
