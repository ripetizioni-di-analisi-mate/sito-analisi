A crate slides across a horizontal floor and, once the pull is removed, slows down because of friction until it stops. What happens after it stops, if no other horizontal forces act on the crate?

- It stays at rest
- It moves backward, pushed by friction
- It keeps moving at constant velocity
- It oscillates back and forth until friction vanishes

## Explanation

The kinetic friction model holds only while the surfaces slide relative to each other. After the crate stops, we return to the static case. No other horizontal forces act on the crate, so the static friction required is zero and the crate stays at rest. The braking model therefore does not describe any spontaneous backward motion.
