A boy drags a $10\,\mathrm{kg}$ surfboard across the sand with a horizontal force, and the board accelerates at $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The sand exerts a resistive force of $20\,\mathrm{N}$. What is the pulling force?

- $15\,\mathrm{N}$
- $35\,\mathrm{N}$
- $5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Take the axis in the direction of motion and let $F$ be the pulling force. Newton's second law gives
$$
F-20\,\mathrm{N}=10\,\mathrm{kg}\cdot1.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=15\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
Therefore $F=15\,\mathrm{N}+20\,\mathrm{N}=35\,\mathrm{N}$. The value $15\,\mathrm{N}$ is the net force, which equals the pull only when there are no resistive forces.
