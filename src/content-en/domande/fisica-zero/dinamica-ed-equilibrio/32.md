Blocks of $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ touch on a frictionless surface. A $10\,\mathrm{N}$ force pushes the $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ block into the other one. What common acceleration do they have?

- $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3.3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

For the two-block system the contact forces are internal. The $10\,\mathrm{N}$ external force accelerates a total mass of $5\,\mathrm{kg}$.
