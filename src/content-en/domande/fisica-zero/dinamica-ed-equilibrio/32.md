The normal force is $60$ N and the coefficient of kinetic friction is $0.2$. What is the magnitude of the kinetic frictional force?

- $300\,\mathrm{N}$
- $60\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the kinetic frictional force is $F_a=\mu_dN$. Multiply $0.2$ by $60$. Therefore $F_a=12$ newtons.
