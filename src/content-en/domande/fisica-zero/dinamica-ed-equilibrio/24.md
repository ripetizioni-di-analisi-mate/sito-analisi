A canoe with its paddler, of total mass $120\,\mathrm{kg}$, receives a forward push of $90\,\mathrm{N}$ from the paddle stroke, while the water exerts $30\,\mathrm{N}$ backward. The vertical forces balance. What is the acceleration of the canoe?

- $1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0.75\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Take the axis forward, so the horizontal net force is
$$
R_x=90\,\mathrm{N}-30\,\mathrm{N}=60\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
By Newton's second law, the acceleration is the net force divided by the mass,
$$
a_x=\frac{60\,\mathrm{N}}{120\,\mathrm{kg}}=0.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.
$$
The value $0.75\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ comes from using the push alone and ignoring the resistance of the water.
