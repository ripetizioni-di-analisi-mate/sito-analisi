The normal force is $100$ N and the coefficient of static friction is $0.3$. What is the maximum static friction?

- $100\,\mathrm{N}$
- $\dfrac{1000}{3}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $15\,\mathrm{N}$
- $30\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Static friction can have a magnitude of up to $\mu_sN$. Its maximum value is $0.3\cdot100$. Therefore the maximum is $30$ newtons.
