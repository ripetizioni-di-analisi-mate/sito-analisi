Two perpendicular horizontal forces of $6\,\mathrm{N}$ and $8\,\mathrm{N}$ act on a $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ trolley on a smooth surface. What is its acceleration magnitude?

- $5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $7\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

By the Pythagorean theorem the net force has magnitude $10\,\mathrm{N}$. Dividing by mass gives $5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
