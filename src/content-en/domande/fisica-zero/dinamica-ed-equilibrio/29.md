A mass of $6$ kg has an acceleration of $3$ meters per second squared. What is the magnitude of the net force?

- $18\,\mathrm{N}$
- $2\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $9\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The net force is the product of mass and acceleration. Substituting gives $F_\mathrm{ris}=6\cdot3$. Therefore its magnitude is $18$ newtons.
