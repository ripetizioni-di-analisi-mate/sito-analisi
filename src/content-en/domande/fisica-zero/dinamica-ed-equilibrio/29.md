A book is balanced on a table. Which force is the Newton’s third-law partner of the book’s weight?

- The gravitational attraction of the book on Earth
- The table’s normal force on the book
- The book’s contact force on the table
- The weight of the table

## Explanation

Weight is Earth’s attraction on the book. The reciprocal force is the book’s attraction on Earth, whereas the normal force belongs to a contact interaction.
