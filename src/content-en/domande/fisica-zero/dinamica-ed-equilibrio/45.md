A $0.4\,\mathrm{kg}$ ball falls toward the ground. Which statement about the force that the ball exerts on Earth is true?

- It is zero, because the ball is much smaller than Earth
- Its magnitude is much smaller than the ball's weight
- It has the same magnitude as the ball's weight and gives Earth a negligible acceleration
- It has the same magnitude and the same direction as the ball's weight

## Explanation

The weight of the ball and the force of the ball on Earth are the two forces of the same interaction. By Newton's third law, they therefore have equal magnitudes and opposite directions. Earth, however, has an enormous mass, so by Newton's second law its acceleration is negligible. The ball, which has a small mass, instead receives a clearly visible acceleration.
