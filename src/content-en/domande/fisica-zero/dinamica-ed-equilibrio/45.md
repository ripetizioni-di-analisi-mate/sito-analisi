A block slides on a smooth straight incline with no additional constraints. What perpendicular acceleration enters the force balance while the block stays on the incline?

- The full acceleration measured along the path
- Zero
- $g$
- $g\sin\theta$ down the slope

## Explanation

The constraint keeps the distance from the plane zero. Acceleration normal to the plane is therefore zero and the reaction balances that weight component.
