A box on a rough surface is initially at rest with no applied horizontal force. The static coefficient is positive. What is the friction magnitude?

- The box’s weight
- $0\,\mathrm{N}$
- Always $\mu_d N$
- Always $\mu_s N$

## Explanation

No force tends to move the box horizontally. Zero friction is therefore required for equilibrium despite the positive available limit.
