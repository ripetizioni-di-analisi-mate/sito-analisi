A force gauge measures a net force of $18\,\mathrm{N}$ on an object accelerating at $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is its inertial mass?

- $6\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $15\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $54\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Solving $F=ma$ for mass means dividing force by the measured acceleration. The quotient is $6\,\mathrm{kg}$.
