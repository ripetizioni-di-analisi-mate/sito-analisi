A waiter walks at constant velocity carrying a cup on a tray. Which force should not be drawn in the free-body diagram of the cup?

- The weight of the cup
- The normal force of the tray on the cup
- The force of the cup on the tray
- The weight and the normal force, because they balance

## Explanation

The free-body diagram of the cup contains only the forces that act on the cup. The weight is exerted by Earth on the cup and the normal force is exerted by the tray on the cup, so both appear. The force of the cup on the tray acts on the tray instead, so it belongs to the diagram of the tray. Two forces that balance remain in the diagram, because each one corresponds to an interaction.
