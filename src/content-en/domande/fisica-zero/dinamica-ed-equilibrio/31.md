A ball thrown vertically upward is rising; air resistance is negligible and the $y$-axis points upward. With $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the acceleration $a_y$ of the ball while it rises?

- $a_y=+9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $a_y=-9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $a_y=0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $a_y=-4.9\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

After the throw, the only force on the ball is its weight, which points downward, so $R_y=-mg$. By Newton's second law $R_y=ma_y$, so $a_y=-g=-9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The negative sign indicates a downward acceleration, opposite to the velocity during the rise. This is why the ball slows down while it rises, even though it moves upward.
