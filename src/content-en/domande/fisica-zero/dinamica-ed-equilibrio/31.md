Two trolleys push against each other. A student adds both mutual contact forces to find the resultant on trolley A alone. Which correction is appropriate?

- Keep both with half their original magnitudes
- Transfer the force A exerts on B onto A
- Keep both but reverse both arrows
- Keep only the force exerted on A by B

## Explanation

A single-body force diagram contains the forces acting on that body. The force exerted by A acts on B.
