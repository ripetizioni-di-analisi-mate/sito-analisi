Blocks of $1\,\mathrm{kg}$, $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ touch in that order on a smooth surface. The first is pushed towards the others by $12\,\mathrm{N}$. What is the contact-force magnitude between the first and second blocks?

- $6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$
- $2\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The common acceleration is $\dfrac{12}{6}=2$ metres per second squared. The first pushes the remaining two blocks, a $5\,\mathrm{kg}$ group, so the contact force is $10\,\mathrm{N}$.
