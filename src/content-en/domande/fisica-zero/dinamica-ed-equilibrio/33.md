The normal force is $120$ N and the coefficient of static friction is $0.3$. What is the maximum static friction?

- $36\,\mathrm{N}$
- $120\,\mathrm{N}$
- $400\,\mathrm{N}$
- $18\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Static friction can have a magnitude of up to $\mu_sN$. Its maximum value is $0.3\cdot120$. Therefore the maximum is $36$ newtons.
