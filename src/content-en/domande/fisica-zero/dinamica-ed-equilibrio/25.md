A body of mass $2$ kg is subject to a force of $30$ N to the right and a force of $10$ N to the left. What is the magnitude of its acceleration?

- $10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $40\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The net force has magnitude $30-10=20$ newtons and points to the right. Divide by the mass of $2$ kg. Therefore the magnitude of the acceleration is $10$ meters per second squared.
