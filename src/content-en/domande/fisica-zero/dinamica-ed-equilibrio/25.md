A moped with its rider, of mass $200\,\mathrm{kg}$, starts from rest with a constant net force of $400\,\mathrm{N}$ in the direction of motion. What speed does it reach after $5\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

By Newton's second law, the acceleration is
$$
a=\frac{400\,\mathrm{N}}{200\,\mathrm{kg}}=2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.
$$
The net force is constant, so the acceleration is also constant and the equation $v=v_0+at$ applies. Since the moped starts from rest, $v_0=0$, and therefore $v=2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}\cdot5\,\mathrm{s}=10\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
