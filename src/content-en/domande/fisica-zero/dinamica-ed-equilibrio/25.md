Two horizontal forces of $5\,\mathrm{N}$ each act on an object, but their angle is unspecified. Which interval contains all possible net-force magnitudes?

- From $5\,\mathrm{N}$ to $10\,\mathrm{N}$
- Only $10\,\mathrm{N}$
- From $0\,\mathrm{N}$ to $5\,\mathrm{N}$
- From $0\,\mathrm{N}$ to $10\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Opposite directions cancel and identical directions add. Intermediate angles give magnitudes between those two extremes.
