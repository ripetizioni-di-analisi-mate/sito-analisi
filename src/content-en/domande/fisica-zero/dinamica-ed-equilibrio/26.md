A technician wants to predict whether a tall crate will tip when pushed near the top. What information is lost if the crate is modelled as a point particle?

- The total mass
- The position of the force’s line of action relative to the base
- Units of the applied force
- Direction of the translational net force

## Explanation

Tipping depends on force lever arms about the supporting edge. A point-particle model cannot distinguish different application points.
