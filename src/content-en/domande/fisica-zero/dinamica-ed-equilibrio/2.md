What net force must act on a $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass to produce an acceleration of $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ to the left?

- $1.6\,\mathrm{N}$ to the left
- $10\,\mathrm{N}$ to the left
- $10\,\mathrm{N}$ to the right
- $6.5\,\mathrm{N}$ to the left

## Explanation

The magnitude of the net force is $4\cdot2.5=10$ newtons. Newton’s second law gives it the direction of the acceleration.
