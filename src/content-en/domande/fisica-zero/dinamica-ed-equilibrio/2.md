A mass of $3$ kg has an acceleration of $3$ meters per second squared. What is the magnitude of the net force?

- $1\,\mathrm{N}$
- $9\,\mathrm{N}$
- $6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The net force is the product of mass and acceleration. Substituting gives $F_\mathrm{ris}=3\cdot3$. Therefore its magnitude is $9$ newtons.
