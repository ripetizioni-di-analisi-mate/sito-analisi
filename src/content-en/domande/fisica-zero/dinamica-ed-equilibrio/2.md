A lamp hangs at rest from the ceiling on a cable. Which external forces act on the lamp?

- Its weight, the force of the cable, and the force of the ceiling
- Only its weight
- Its weight and the force of the lamp on the cable
- Its weight and the force of the cable

## Explanation

External forces are the forces that other bodies exert on the lamp. Earth exerts the weight, and the cable, which is in contact with the lamp, pulls it upward. The ceiling touches the cable and not the lamp, so its force acts on the cable. The force of the lamp on the cable also acts on the cable, so it is not one of the forces on the lamp.
