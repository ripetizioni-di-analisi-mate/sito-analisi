A $5\,\mathrm{kg}$ block rests on a level surface. A slowly increased horizontal pull of $15\,\mathrm{N}$ just starts it sliding. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is $\mu_s$?

- $3.0$
- $0.30$
- $0.15$
- $0.75$

## Explanation

At the threshold the pull equals maximum static friction. The normal force is $50\,\mathrm{N}$, giving a coefficient of $\dfrac{15}{50}=0.30$.
