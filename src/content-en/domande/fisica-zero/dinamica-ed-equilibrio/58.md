A $25\,\mathrm{kg}$ punching bag hangs at rest from a single vertical chain. Using $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the tension in the chain?

- $25\,\mathrm{N}$
- $2.55\,\mathrm{N}$
- $245.25\,\mathrm{N}$
- $490.5\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Only the **tension** upward and the weight downward act on the bag. The bag is at rest, so taking upward as positive gives $T-mg=0$. Therefore
$$
T=mg=25\,\mathrm{kg}\cdot9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=245.25\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
The value $2.55\,\mathrm{N}$ comes from dividing the mass by $g$ instead of multiplying.
