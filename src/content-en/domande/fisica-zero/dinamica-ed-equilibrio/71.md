A $5\,\mathrm{kg}$ box at rest on a horizontal table starts to slide when the horizontal push exceeds $15\,\mathrm{N}$. Using $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the coefficient of static friction?

- $3$
- $0.03$
- $0.15$
- $0.3$

## Explanation

The box starts to slide when the push exceeds the maximum static friction, so $\mu_sN=15\,\mathrm{N}$. Only the weight and the normal force act vertically, so $N=5\,\mathrm{kg}\cdot10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=50\,\mathrm{N}$. Then
$$
\mu_s=\frac{15\,\mathrm{N}}{50\,\mathrm{N}}=0.3.
$$
The coefficient is a pure number, because it is the ratio of two forces.
