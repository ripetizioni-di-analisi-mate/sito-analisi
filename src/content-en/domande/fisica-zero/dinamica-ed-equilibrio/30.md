A cart moves to the left on a horizontal track and, from a certain instant on, receives a constant net force pointing to the right. How does the motion continue?

- It reverses its direction of motion immediately
- It continues to the left at constant velocity
- It slows down, stops, and then moves to the right
- It slows down, stops, and stays at rest

## Explanation

By Newton's second law, the acceleration points in the direction of the net force, so it points to the right. The initial velocity is to the left, so the cart slows down until its velocity becomes zero. The net force still points to the right after the cart stops, so the cart starts moving to the right. The direction of the acceleration depends on the forces, while the direction of motion also depends on the initial velocity.
