A skater pushes a wall that does not visibly move and glides away on smooth ice. How can the wall exert a force while remaining at rest?

- Newton’s third law applies only to moving objects
- The wall exerts a force only after the skater moves
- The wall’s supports can balance the skater’s force on it
- The wall always exerts more force than it receives

## Explanation

The reciprocal force exists during contact. The wall’s acceleration depends on all forces on it, including forces from its supports.
