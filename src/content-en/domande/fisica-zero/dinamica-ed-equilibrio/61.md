A box initially at rest relative to the ground is placed on a conveyor moving right. Before the box catches up with the belt, which way does friction on the box point?

- It alternates direction continuously
- Left, because the belt moves right
- It is zero because the box starts from rest
- Right, along the belt motion

## Explanation

Relative to the belt, the bottom of the box slips left. Kinetic friction on the box therefore points right and increases its ground speed.
