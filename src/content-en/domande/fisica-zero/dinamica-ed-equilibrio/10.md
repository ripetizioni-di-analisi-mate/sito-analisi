A stationary crate is pulled horizontally with $8\,\mathrm{N}$ to the right. Its maximum static friction is $20\,\mathrm{N}$ and there are no other horizontal forces. What friction actually acts?

- $12\,\mathrm{N}$ to the left
- $8\,\mathrm{N}$ to the right
- $20\,\mathrm{N}$ to the left
- $8\,\mathrm{N}$ to the left

## Explanation

The pull is below the threshold, so equilibrium is possible. Static friction takes the value needed to balance the $8\,\mathrm{N}$ pull.
