Two horses pull a cart along a straight, level road. The resistive forces total $500\,\mathrm{N}$ backward, and the horizontal net force on the cart is $300\,\mathrm{N}$ forward. What is the total pull of the horses?

- $200\,\mathrm{N}$
- $800\,\mathrm{N}$
- $300\,\mathrm{N}$
- $500\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Take the axis forward and let $F$ be the total pull. The horizontal components are $+F$ and $-500\,\mathrm{N}$, so $R_x=F-500\,\mathrm{N}$. Since $R_x=300\,\mathrm{N}$, we obtain
$$
F=300\,\mathrm{N}+500\,\mathrm{N}=800\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
The pull therefore exceeds the resistive forces by $300\,\mathrm{N}$, that is, by the value of the net force.
