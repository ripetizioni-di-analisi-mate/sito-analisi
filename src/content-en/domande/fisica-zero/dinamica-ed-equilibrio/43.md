A body of mass $2$ kg is subject to a force of $42$ N to the right and a force of $14$ N to the left. What is the magnitude of its acceleration?

- $28\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $56\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $14\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $7\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The net force has magnitude $42-14=28$ newtons and points to the right. Divide by the mass of $2$ kg. Therefore the magnitude of the acceleration is $14$ meters per second squared.
