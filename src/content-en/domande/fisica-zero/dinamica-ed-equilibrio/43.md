A hammer strikes a nail and, during the impact, exerts a force of $400\,\mathrm{N}$ downward on it. What force does the nail exert on the hammer at the same instant?

- $400\,\mathrm{N}$ downward
- $0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $200\,\mathrm{N}$ upward
- $400\,\mathrm{N}$ upward

## Explanation

The hammer and the nail interact, so by Newton's third law $\vec F_{\text{nail}\to\text{hammer}}=-\vec F_{\text{hammer}\to\text{nail}}$. The force of the nail on the hammer therefore has the same magnitude, $400\,\mathrm{N}$, and the opposite direction, that is, upward. This force is what slows the hammer down during the impact.
