A surface exerts a $40\,\mathrm{N}$ normal force and a $30\,\mathrm{N}$ horizontal friction force on a block. What is the magnitude of the total contact force?

- $10\,\mathrm{N}$
- $50\,\mathrm{N}$
- $70\,\mathrm{N}$
- $40\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Normal force and friction are perpendicular. Their resultant magnitude is $\sqrt{40^2+30^2}=50$ newtons.
