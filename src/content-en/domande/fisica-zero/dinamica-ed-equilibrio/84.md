A baseball player dives and slides along the ground toward the north to reach the base. What is the direction of the frictional force of the ground on the player?

- South
- North
- Upward
- Downward

## Explanation

The player slides along the ground, so kinetic friction acts. This force is tangent to the ground and opposes the relative sliding. The sliding is toward the north, so the frictional force on the player points south. This force is what slows the player down until he stops.
