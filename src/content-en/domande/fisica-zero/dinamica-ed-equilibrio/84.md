A ball falls through air and eventually reaches a constant falling speed. How do weight and drag compare in that regime?

- Weight exceeds drag because the ball is descending
- Drag vanishes because speed is constant
- Both forces point downwards
- Equal magnitudes and opposite directions

## Explanation

At constant velocity acceleration is zero. The air must therefore exert an upward force balancing the downward weight.
