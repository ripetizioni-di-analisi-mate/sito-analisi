A light rod can push or pull along its axis. In which situation could an ideal string replace it without changing equilibrium?

- The rod exerts a transverse force at both ends
- The rod supports a load by compression alone
- The rod is pulling its two ends towards one another
- The rod holds the two points apart by pushing

## Explanation

A taut string transmits an axial pull. It can therefore replace an element in tension and goes slack if required to push.
