A sled slows from $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in $2\,\mathrm{s}$ solely through friction on level ground. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what kinetic-friction coefficient fits the observations?

- $0.20$
- $0.60$
- $0.40$
- $2.0$

## Explanation

Deceleration magnitude is $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. For friction alone on level ground $|a|=\mu_d g$, giving a coefficient of 0.20.
