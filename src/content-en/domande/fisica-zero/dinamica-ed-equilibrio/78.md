A laboratory moves at constant straight-line velocity relative to ground assumed inertial. Inside it, a freely moving object is observed at constant velocity. What does this imply for the laboratory frame?

- The laboratory must be stationary relative to the ground
- The object must have zero mass
- The object has zero velocity relative to the ground
- Newton’s first law holds in this frame too

## Explanation

Two frames in uniform translation relative to each other measure the same acceleration. If one is inertial, so is the other.
