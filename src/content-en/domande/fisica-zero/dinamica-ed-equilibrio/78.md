A horizontal push of $100\,\mathrm{N}$ is applied, one at a time, to four crates at rest on different horizontal floors. Using $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, which crate starts to slide?

- $m=30\,\mathrm{kg}$, $\mu_s=0.4$
- $m=20\,\mathrm{kg}$, $\mu_s=0.6$
- $m=50\,\mathrm{kg}$, $\mu_s=0.3$
- $m=40\,\mathrm{kg}$, $\mu_s=0.2$

## Explanation

A crate starts to slide when the push exceeds the maximum static friction $\mu_smg$. For the four crates this maximum is $0.2\cdot400\,\mathrm{N}=80\,\mathrm{N}$, $0.4\cdot300\,\mathrm{N}=120\,\mathrm{N}$, $0.6\cdot200\,\mathrm{N}=120\,\mathrm{N}$, and $0.3\cdot500\,\mathrm{N}=150\,\mathrm{N}$. Only for the $40\,\mathrm{kg}$ crate with $\mu_s=0.2$ is the maximum less than $100\,\mathrm{N}$, so it is the only one that slides. The others stay at rest with a frictional force of $100\,\mathrm{N}$.
