A magnet is stuck to the vertical door of a refrigerator. What is the direction of the normal force of the door on the magnet?

- Horizontal, from the door outward
- Vertical, upward
- Vertical, downward
- Horizontal, into the door

## Explanation

The normal force is always perpendicular to the contact surface. The door is vertical, so the normal force is horizontal. A supporting surface pushes the body and does not pull it, so the force points from the door outward. The force that holds the magnet up along the vertical is instead the static friction with the door.
