Two symmetric strings support the same sign. Their attachments are moved so that the strings become nearly horizontal while the sign stays in equilibrium. How does the tension change?

- It vanishes because the strings are nearly horizontal
- It always stays at half the weight
- It decreases because two strings share the weight
- It increases because the vertical fraction decreases

## Explanation

The same weight still needs support. Each string supplies a smaller vertical fraction of its tension, so the required magnitude increases.
