A trolley gains $14\,\mathrm{J}$ of kinetic energy while resistance does $-6\,\mathrm{J}$ of work. The motor provides the only other contribution to the work. How much work does it do?

- $8\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{J}$
- $20\,\mathrm{J}$
- $14\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The work contributions must sum to $14\,\mathrm{J}$. The motor must supply $20\,\mathrm{J}$ to replace the $6\,\mathrm{J}$ lost and leave the observed increase.
