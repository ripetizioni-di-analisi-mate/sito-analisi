A book of mass $1.5\,\mathrm{kg}$ rests on a shelf $2\,\mathrm{m}$ above the floor. The top of a desk $0.8\,\mathrm{m}$ high is chosen as the zero level of potential energy. With $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the gravitational potential energy of the book?

- $29.4\,\mathrm{J}$
- $17.7\,\mathrm{J}$
- $11.8\,\mathrm{J}$
- $41.2\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The height $h$ must be measured from the chosen zero level, that is, from the top of the desk. The book is $2\,\mathrm{m}-0.8\,\mathrm{m}=1.2\,\mathrm{m}$ above the desk, so $h=1.2\,\mathrm{m}$. Therefore,
$$
U=mgh=1.5\cdot9.81\cdot1.2\,\mathrm{J}\approx17.7\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
The value $29.4\,\mathrm{J}$ is the potential energy relative to the floor.
