A constant force of $7$ N acts in the direction of a displacement of $4$ m. How much work does it do?

- $28\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1.75\,\mathrm{J}$
- $11\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-28\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

When the force and displacement point in the same direction, the work is $W=F\Delta s$. Multiplying $7$ N by $4$ m therefore gives $W=28$ joules.
