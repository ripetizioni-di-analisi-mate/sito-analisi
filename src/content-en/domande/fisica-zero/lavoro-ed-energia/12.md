An ideal spring of stiffness $400\,\mathrm{N/m}$ is compressed $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ from its natural length. How much elastic energy does it store?

- $20\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0.50\,\mathrm{J}$
- $10\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The compression is $0.05\,\mathrm{m}$. Squaring this displacement gives $U_e=\dfrac12\cdot400\cdot(0.05)^2=0.50$ joules.
