A mass of $2$ kg moves at $4$ m/s. What is its kinetic energy?

- $32\,\mathrm{J}$
- $8\,\mathrm{J}$
- $4\,\mathrm{J}$
- $16\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The kinetic energy is $K=\dfrac{1}{2}mv^2$. With a mass of $2$ kg, the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$ cancels. Therefore, $K=4^2=16$ joules.
