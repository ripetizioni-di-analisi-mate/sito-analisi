A slider travels $7\,\mathrm{m}$ over a stationary surface. A constant friction force of $4\,\mathrm{N}$ opposes its motion. How much work does friction do on the slider?

- $-28\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $28\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Friction has a negative component along the displacement, so its work is $-4\cdot7=-28$ joules.
