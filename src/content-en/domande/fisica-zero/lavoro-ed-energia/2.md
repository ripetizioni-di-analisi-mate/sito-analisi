While the owner walks $5\,\mathrm{m}$ in a straight line, the dog on the leash pulls backward with a constant force of $30\,\mathrm{N}$, parallel to the displacement and in the opposite direction. How much work does the force of the leash do on the owner?

- $150\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-150\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The force of the leash is parallel to the displacement but points in the opposite direction, so its work is $W=-Fs$. The product of the magnitudes is $30\,\mathrm{N}\cdot5\,\mathrm{m}=150\,\mathrm{J}$. Therefore, the work done by the force of the leash is **$-150\,\mathrm{J}$**. The negative sign indicates that this force takes energy away from the owner's motion.
