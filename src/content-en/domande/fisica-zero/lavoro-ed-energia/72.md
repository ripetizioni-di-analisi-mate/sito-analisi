A trolley crosses a level unpowered track section. Its entry kinetic energy lies between $48\,\mathrm{J}$ and $52\,\mathrm{J}$ and its exit energy between $39\,\mathrm{J}$ and $41\,\mathrm{J}$. What is the smallest loss compatible with these readings?

- $11\,\mathrm{J}$
- $13\,\mathrm{J}$
- $7\,\mathrm{J}$
- $9\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Choose the lowest possible entry energy and highest possible exit energy to minimise the loss. Their difference is $48-41=7$ joules.
