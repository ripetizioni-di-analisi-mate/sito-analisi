A package slides down a loading ramp with friction; besides gravity and friction, only the normal force from the ramp acts on it. The package's mechanical energy at the bottom is less than at the top. What quantity is this decrease equal to?

- The work done by gravity
- The package's initial gravitational potential energy
- The package's final kinetic energy
- The magnitude of the work done by friction

## Explanation

The normal force is perpendicular to the motion and does zero work, so friction is the only force that does work besides gravity. In this case $E_f-E_i=W_a$. Since $W_a$ is negative, the mechanical energy decreases by $-W_a$, that is, by **the magnitude of the work done by friction**. The work done by gravity, by contrast, is already included in the change in potential energy.
