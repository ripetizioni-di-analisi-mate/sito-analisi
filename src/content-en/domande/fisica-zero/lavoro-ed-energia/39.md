A mass of $2$ kg moves at $7$ m/s. What is its kinetic energy?

- $98\,\mathrm{J}$
- $14\,\mathrm{J}$
- $49\,\mathrm{J}$
- $7\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The kinetic energy is $K=\dfrac{1}{2}mv^2$. With a mass of $2$ kg, the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$ cancels. Therefore, $K=7^2=49$ joules.
