For a pendant lamp, one student measures heights from the floor and another from the ceiling. Then the lamp is lowered by $20\,\mathrm{cm}$. Which statement about the gravitational potential energy of the lamp computed by the two students is true?

- They find the same value of $U$
- Only the calculation with the zero at the floor is correct
- They find different values of $U$, but the same change in $U$ when the lamp is lowered
- They find different values of $U$ and also different changes when the lamp is lowered

## Explanation

The value of $U=mgh$ depends on the zero level, because the height $h$ is measured from that level. Relative to the ceiling the lamp has a negative height, relative to the floor a positive one, so the two values of $U$ differ. When the lamp is lowered by $20\,\mathrm{cm}$, both heights decrease by $0.20\,\mathrm{m}$. Therefore, the change in potential energy, $-mg\cdot0.20\,\mathrm{m}$, is **the same** for the two students.
