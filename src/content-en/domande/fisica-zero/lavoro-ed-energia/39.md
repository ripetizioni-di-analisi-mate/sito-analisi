Gravitational potential energy is zero at bridge level. A $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ bucket has potential energy $-60\,\mathrm{J}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, where is the bucket relative to the bridge?

- $20\,\mathrm{m}$ below
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$ below
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$ above
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$ below

## Explanation

Dividing the energy by the $30\,\mathrm{N}$ weight gives a height of $-2\,\mathrm{m}$, placing the bucket below the bridge.
