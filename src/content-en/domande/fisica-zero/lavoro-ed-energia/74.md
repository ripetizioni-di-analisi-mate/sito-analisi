On an ideal pulley an $8\,\mathrm{kg}$ load rises $1\,\mathrm{m}$ while a $5\,\mathrm{kg}$ counterweight descends $1\,\mathrm{m}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, how much does the combined gravitational potential energy increase?

- $-30\,\mathrm{J}$
- $80\,\mathrm{J}$
- $30\,\mathrm{J}$
- $130\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The load gains $80\,\mathrm{J}$ while the counterweight loses $50\,\mathrm{J}$. The combined change is $80-50=30$ joules.
