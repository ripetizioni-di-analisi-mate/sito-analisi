A $0.8\,\mathrm{kg}$ book is pushed along a tabletop and released at $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$; from then on, it is slowed only by a constant kinetic friction force of $2\,\mathrm{N}$. How far does it slide before stopping?

- $0.45\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.8\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

When the book is released, its kinetic energy is $K_i=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot0.8\cdot1.5^2\,\mathrm{J}=0.9\,\mathrm{J}$. The table is level, so the potential energy does not change, and the loss of kinetic energy equals the magnitude of the work done by friction, $fs$. The book stops, so $fs=0.9\,\mathrm{J}$. We get
$$
s=\frac{0.9\,\mathrm{J}}{2\,\mathrm{N}}=0.45\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
