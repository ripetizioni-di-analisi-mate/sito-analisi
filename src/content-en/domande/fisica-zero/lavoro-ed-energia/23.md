On a force–position graph the horizontal force component is $8\,\mathrm{N}$ from 0 to $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $-3\,\mathrm{N}$ from 2 to $6\,\mathrm{m}$. The body moves towards increasing position. How much work does this force do?

::grafico{linee="0,8 2,8;2,-3 6,-3" xmin=0 xmax=6.8 ymin=-5 ymax=10 nomi="x,F" legenda=0 titolo="Force component in newtons against position in metres."}

- $28\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{J}$
- $4\,\mathrm{J}$
- $16\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The two rectangles have opposite signed areas. Their sum is $8\cdot2-3\cdot4=4$ joules.
