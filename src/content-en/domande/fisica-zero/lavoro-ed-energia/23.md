A child is swinging on a playground swing. At which points of the swing is the child's kinetic energy zero?

- At the lowest point of the swing
- Halfway between the lowest point and a highest point
- At no point, because the swing is always moving
- At the two highest points of the swing

## Explanation

The kinetic energy $K=\dfrac{1}{2}mv^2$ is zero only when the speed is zero. At the two highest points the swing reverses its direction of motion, so it is momentarily at rest there. Therefore, the kinetic energy is zero **at the two highest points**. At the lowest point the speed is greatest, and so is the kinetic energy.
