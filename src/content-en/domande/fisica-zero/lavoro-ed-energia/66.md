A block keeps sliding on a level surface. At distances of 0, 1 and 2 metres its kinetic energies are 20, 17 and 12 joules respectively. What do these readings imply about a model with constant friction as the only horizontal force?

- Unequal losses over equal distances rule out this model for the exact data
- The readings require zero friction
- Decreasing energy verifies the model
- Gravity must do positive work

## Explanation

The loss is $3\,\mathrm{J}$ in the first metre and $5\,\mathrm{J}$ in the second. Constant resistance would remove the same energy in each metre.
