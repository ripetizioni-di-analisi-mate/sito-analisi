Over a short interval a machine receives $90\,\mathrm{J}$ from outside, delivers $110\,\mathrm{J}$ of work and loses no heat. How must its previously stored energy have changed?

- It decreased by $110\,\mathrm{J}$
- It increased by $20\,\mathrm{J}$
- It decreased by $20\,\mathrm{J}$
- It stayed constant

## Explanation

The output exceeds the new input by $20\,\mathrm{J}$. The energy balance requires those $20\,\mathrm{J}$ to come from storage.
