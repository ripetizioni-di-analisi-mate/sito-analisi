A pump for watering a vegetable garden draws a power of $1.2\,\mathrm{kW}$ from the electrical grid and delivers a useful power of $900\,\mathrm{W}$ to the water. What is its efficiency?

- $75\%$
- $133\%$
- $25\%$
- $7.5\%$

## Explanation

For average powers measured over the same interval, the efficiency is $\eta=\dfrac{P_{\mathrm{utile}}}{P_{\mathrm{ass}}}$. The two powers must be in the same unit, so $P_{\mathrm{ass}}=1200\,\mathrm{W}$. Therefore,
$$
\eta=\frac{900}{1200}=0.75=75\%.
$$
The value $133\%$ would come from swapping numerator and denominator, and it is impossible because it exceeds $100\%$.
