A body initially has $15$ J of kinetic energy. The net work done on it is $10$ J. What is its final kinetic energy?

- $5\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-5\,\mathrm{J}$
- $150\,\mathrm{J}$
- $25\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The work–energy theorem gives $K_f-K_i=W_\mathrm{ris}$. Add the work to the initial energy. This gives $K_f=15+10=25$ joules.
