A van of mass $1500\,\mathrm{kg}$ reverses across a courtyard at $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$, that is, it moves in the direction opposite to the one in which it usually travels. What is its kinetic energy?

- $-3000\,\mathrm{J}$
- $3000\,\mathrm{J}$
- $6000\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-1500\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The kinetic energy is $K=\dfrac{1}{2}mv^2$, where $v$ is the speed, that is, the magnitude of the velocity. The magnitude does not depend on the direction of motion, so in reverse it is still $v=2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Therefore,
$$
K=\frac{1}{2}\cdot1500\cdot2^2\,\mathrm{J}=3000\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
The kinetic energy of a moving body is positive, whatever the direction in which it moves. The value $6000\,\mathrm{J}$ would come from forgetting the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
