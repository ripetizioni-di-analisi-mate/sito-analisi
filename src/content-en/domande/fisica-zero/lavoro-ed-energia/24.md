The force along the direction of motion increases linearly from $2\,\mathrm{N}$ to $10\,\mathrm{N}$ over $3\,\mathrm{m}$. How much work does it do?

- $30\,\mathrm{J}$
- $6\,\mathrm{J}$
- $36\,\mathrm{J}$
- $18\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The area under the force–position graph is a trapezium. The force averaged over distance is $\dfrac{2+10}{2}=6$ newtons, giving $18\,\mathrm{J}$ of work.
