A body initially has $9\,\mathrm{J}$ of kinetic energy. All forces together do $6\,\mathrm{J}$ of work during a displacement. What is its final kinetic energy?

- $15\,\mathrm{J}$
- $6\,\mathrm{J}$
- $54\,\mathrm{J}$
- $3\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Net work is the change in kinetic energy. Adding that change to the initial value gives $9+6=15$ joules.
