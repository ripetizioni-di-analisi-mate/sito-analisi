A soccer player kicks a ball of mass $0.4\,\mathrm{kg}$, which leaves at $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the kinetic energy of the ball just after the kick?

- $160\,\mathrm{J}$
- $4\,\mathrm{J}$
- $80\,\mathrm{J}$
- $8\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The kinetic energy of a body of mass $m$ moving at speed $v$ is $K=\dfrac{1}{2}mv^2$. The speed must be squared, so $v^2=400\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}$. Therefore,
$$
K=\frac{1}{2}\cdot0.4\cdot400\,\mathrm{J}=80\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
The value $160\,\mathrm{J}$ would come from forgetting the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
