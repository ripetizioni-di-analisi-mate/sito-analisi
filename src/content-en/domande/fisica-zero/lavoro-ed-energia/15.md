A body initially has $12$ J of kinetic energy. The net work done on it is $8$ J. What is its final kinetic energy?

- $4\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{J}$
- $20\,\mathrm{J}$
- $96\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The work–energy theorem gives $K_f-K_i=W_\mathrm{ris}$. Add the work to the initial energy. This gives $K_f=12+8=20$ joules.
