A constant force of $5$ N acts in the direction of a displacement of $4$ m. How much work does it do?

- $1.25\,\mathrm{J}$
- $9\,\mathrm{J}$
- $20\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

When the force and displacement point in the same direction, the work is $W=F\Delta s$. Multiplying $5$ N by $4$ m therefore gives $W=20$ joules.
