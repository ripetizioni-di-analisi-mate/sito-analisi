A person pushes a trolley $4\,\mathrm{m}$ horizontally with constant force components of $6\,\mathrm{N}$ forwards and $8\,\mathrm{N}$ downwards. How much work does the person do?

- $32\,\mathrm{J}$
- $40\,\mathrm{J}$
- $24\,\mathrm{J}$
- $56\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

There is no vertical displacement. Only the forward component contributes, doing $6\cdot4=24$ joules of work.
