The energy of an ideal spring is measured at several positive extensions. Which plot should be a straight line through the origin with numerical slope equal to half the spring constant in SI units?

- $U_e^2$ against $x$
- $U_e$ against $x^2$
- $U_e$ against $x$
- $x$ against $U_e$

## Explanation

The relation $U_e=\dfrac{k}{2}x^2$ is linear in $x^2$. Its slope in this plot is $\dfrac{k}{2}$.
