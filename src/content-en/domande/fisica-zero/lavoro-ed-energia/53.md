A bag hangs from a vertical ideal spring and stays at rest in equilibrium with the spring stretched $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ beyond its natural length. Which statement about the spring's elastic potential energy, with zero at the natural length, is true?

- It is zero, because the bag is in equilibrium
- It is negative, because the spring force points upward
- It is zero, because the bag is at rest
- It is positive, because the spring is stretched

## Explanation

The elastic potential energy is $U_e=\dfrac{1}{2}kx^2$, with $x$ the deformation from the natural length. Here $x=0.10\,\mathrm{m}$, so $U_e=\dfrac{1}{2}k\cdot0.01\,\mathrm{m^2}$, which is a **positive** number. Equilibrium means that the spring force balances the weight and says nothing about the value of the energy. The elastic energy is zero only when the spring is at its natural length.
