A stone falls from a bridge toward the river, with negligible air resistance. Which statement about its kinetic energy and its gravitational potential energy during the fall is true?

- Both energies increase
- The potential energy decreases and the kinetic energy increases by the same amount
- The kinetic energy increases and the potential energy stays constant
- Both energies decrease

## Explanation

Only gravity does work on the stone, so the mechanical energy $K+U$ stays constant. The stone goes down, so its potential energy $U=mgh$ decreases. Since the sum $K+U$ does not change, the kinetic energy increases **by the same amount** by which the potential energy decreases.
