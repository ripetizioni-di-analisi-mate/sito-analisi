A sled descends at constant speed. Gravity does $60\,\mathrm{J}$ of work and friction does $-45\,\mathrm{J}$. What work does the control rope do, given that no other force does work?

- $-15\,\mathrm{J}$
- $15\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-105\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Constant speed requires zero net work. Gravity and friction supply $15\,\mathrm{J}$ together, so the rope must remove $15\,\mathrm{J}$.
