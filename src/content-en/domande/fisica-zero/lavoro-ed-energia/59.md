A coconut comes off a palm tree and falls from rest from a height of $7.2\,\mathrm{m}$. With negligible air resistance and $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, at what speed does it reach the sand?

- $11.9\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $141\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $70.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Only gravity does work, so mechanical energy is conserved. With the zero level at the sand, the initial energy is all potential and the final energy is all kinetic, so $mgh=\dfrac{1}{2}mv^2$. The mass cancels, and we get
$$
v=\sqrt{2gh}=\sqrt{2\cdot9.81\cdot7.2}\,\mathrm{m/s}\approx11.9\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
The value $141$ is $2gh$, that is, $v^2$ rather than the speed.
