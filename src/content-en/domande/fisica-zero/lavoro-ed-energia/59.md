An object falls from rest $12\,\mathrm{m}$ above the ground without air resistance. Taking $U=0$ at ground level, at what height are kinetic and potential energy equal?

- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

When $K=U$, each equals half the original energy. Since $U=mgh$ is proportional to height, that height is half of $12\,\mathrm{m}$.
