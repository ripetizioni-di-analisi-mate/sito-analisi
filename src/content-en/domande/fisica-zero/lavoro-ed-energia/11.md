A ball falls $1.8\,\mathrm{m}$ from rest. Neglecting air resistance and taking $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is its final speed?

- $18\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $36\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Lost gravitational potential energy becomes kinetic energy. Mass cancels from $mgh=\dfrac12mv^2$, so $v=\sqrt{2\cdot10\cdot1.8}=6$ metres per second.
