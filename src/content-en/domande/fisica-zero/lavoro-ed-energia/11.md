A skydiver descends vertically with the parachute open, and the parachute exerts an upward force on the skydiver. During the descent, what is the sign of the work done by this force?

- Negative
- Positive
- Zero
- Positive or negative depending on the speed

## Explanation

During the descent, the skydiver's displacement is downward. The force of the parachute is upward, so it has the same line of action as the displacement and points the opposite way. A force opposite to the displacement does work $W=-Fs$, so the work done by the force of the parachute is **negative**. The sign depends on the direction of the force relative to the displacement, not on the value of the speed.
