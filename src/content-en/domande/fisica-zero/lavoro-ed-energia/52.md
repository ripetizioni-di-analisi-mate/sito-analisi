Two ideal springs A and B start unstretched. B is twice as stiff as A. Each is stretched slowly to the same final force. How do their stored energies compare?

- B stores four times as much
- B stores half as much as A
- The energies are equal
- B stores twice as much

## Explanation

At the same force B stretches half as far. Work is half the product of final force and extension, so B stores half as much energy.
