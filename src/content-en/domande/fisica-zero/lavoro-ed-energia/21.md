A magnet exerts a constant $3\,\mathrm{N}$ force to the right on a slider. The slider moves $2\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right and returns to its starting point. What total work does the magnet do?

- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $6\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{J}$
- $12\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The outward work is $6\,\mathrm{J}$ and the return work is $-6\,\mathrm{J}$. The force keeps its direction, so the two contributions cancel.
