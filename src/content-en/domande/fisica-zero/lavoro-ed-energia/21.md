A mass of $2$ kg moves at $5$ m/s. What is its kinetic energy?

- $25\,\mathrm{J}$
- $50\,\mathrm{J}$
- $10\,\mathrm{J}$
- $5\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The kinetic energy is $K=\dfrac{1}{2}mv^2$. With a mass of $2$ kg, the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$ cancels. Therefore, $K=5^2=25$ joules.
