During a race, a runner of mass $60\,\mathrm{kg}$ has a kinetic energy of $1920\,\mathrm{J}$. What is the runner's speed?

- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $64\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $32\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5.7\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

From $K=\dfrac{1}{2}mv^2$, multiplying by $2$ and dividing by $m$ gives
$$
v^2=\frac{2K}{m}=\frac{2\cdot1920}{60}\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}=64\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}.
$$
The speed is the positive square root of $v^2$, so $v=8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The value $64$ is $v^2$, not the speed.
