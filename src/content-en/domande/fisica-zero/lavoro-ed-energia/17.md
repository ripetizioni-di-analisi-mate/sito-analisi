Over a complete cycle a small motor receives $30\,\mathrm{J}$ and transfers $24\,\mathrm{J}$ to its load. What percentage of the input becomes useful work?

- $20\%$
- $80\%$
- $60\%$
- $125\%$

## Explanation

The load receives a fraction $\dfrac{24}{30}=0.8$ of the input. Multiplication by one hundred gives an efficiency of $80\%$.
