A machine takes in $40$ J and delivers $32$ J of useful energy. What is its efficiency?

- $0.8$
- $1.25$
- $0.2$
- $8$

## Explanation

Efficiency is the ratio of useful energy output to energy input. Evaluating $\eta=\dfrac{32}{40}$ therefore gives an efficiency of $0.8$, or $80\%$.
