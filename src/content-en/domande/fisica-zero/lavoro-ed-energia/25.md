A sensor records a constant horizontal force of $8\,\mathrm{N}$ on a trolley for $2\,\mathrm{s}$. Which further measurement is sufficient to determine the work done by this force?

- The trolley's mass alone
- The sensor's initial temperature
- The duration to more decimal places
- The signed displacement in the direction of the force

## Explanation

Work by a constant force is the force times the signed displacement along it. Elapsed time alone does not determine that displacement.
