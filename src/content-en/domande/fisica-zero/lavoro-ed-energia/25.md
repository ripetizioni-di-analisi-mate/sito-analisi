A machine does $100$ J of work in $4$ s. What is its average power?

- $25\,\mathrm{W}$
- $400\,\mathrm{W}$
- $100\,\mathrm{W}$
- $1\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

Average power is work divided by time. Evaluating $P=\dfrac{100}{4}$ therefore gives an average power of $25$ watts.
