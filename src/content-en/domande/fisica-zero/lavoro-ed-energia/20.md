A constant force of $5$ N acts opposite to a displacement of $4$ m. How much work does it do?

- $20\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-5\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The work is $W=F\Delta s\cos180^\circ$. The cosine is $-1$ because the force and the displacement point in opposite directions. Therefore, $W=-20$ joules.
