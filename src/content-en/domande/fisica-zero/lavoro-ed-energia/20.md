Riding downhill, a cyclist goes from $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and the mass of cyclist and bicycle does not change. By what number is the kinetic energy multiplied?

- $3$
- $6$
- $9$
- $27$

## Explanation

The kinetic energy is $K=\dfrac{1}{2}mv^2$, and the mass stays the same. The speed goes from $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$, so it is multiplied by $3$. Since the speed appears squared, the kinetic energy is multiplied by $3^2=9$. Indeed, $\dfrac{1}{2}m\cdot144$ is nine times $\dfrac{1}{2}m\cdot16$.
