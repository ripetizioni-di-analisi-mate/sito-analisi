A hand holds a suitcase still with a force of $50\,\mathrm{N}$ for $12\,\mathrm{s}$. How much mechanical work does the hand do on the suitcase?

- $12\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $600\,\mathrm{J}$
- $50\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The point where the force acts stays still. Its displacement remains zero throughout the hold, so the hand does zero work on the case.
