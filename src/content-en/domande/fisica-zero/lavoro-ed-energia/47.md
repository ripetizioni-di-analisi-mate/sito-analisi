A traveler slowly lifts a suitcase at constant speed up to the luggage rack of the train. How do the work done by gravity and the work done by the force of the hand on the suitcase compare?

- They are both positive
- They are both negative
- The work done by gravity is zero, because the suitcase moves slowly
- They are opposite and have the same magnitude

## Explanation

Only gravity and the force of the hand do work on the suitcase. The speed is constant, so by the **work–energy theorem** the net work is zero. The suitcase goes up, so gravity does negative work. It follows that the work done by the hand is positive and has the same magnitude as the work done by gravity.
