A spring with a natural length of $20\,\mathrm{cm}$ is stretched to $25\,\mathrm{cm}$. In $U_e=\dfrac12kx^2$, with $k$ in newtons per metre, what value of $x$ should be used?

- $0.25\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.05\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Deformation is the change from natural length. The difference is $5\,\mathrm{cm}$, or $0.05\,\mathrm{m}$.
