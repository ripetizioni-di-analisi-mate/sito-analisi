A motor converts $80\%$ of its input to shaft work. A transmission delivers $75\%$ of that work to the load. What is the overall efficiency?

- $155\%$
- $60\%$
- $45\%$
- $77.5\%$

## Explanation

For every $100\,\mathrm{J}$ entering the motor, $80\,\mathrm{J}$ reach the transmission. The load receives $0.75\cdot80=60$ joules, or $60\%$ of the original input.
