The motor of a small construction crane delivers an average mechanical power of $1.5\,\mathrm{kW}$. How long does it take to do $9000\,\mathrm{J}$ of work?

- $6\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.17\,\mathrm{s}$
- $6000\,\mathrm{s}$
- $13.5\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The power must be expressed in watts, so $P_m=1.5\,\mathrm{kW}=1500\,\mathrm{W}$. From the definition $P_m=\dfrac{W}{\Delta t}$ we get $\Delta t=\dfrac{W}{P_m}$. Therefore,
$$
\Delta t=\frac{9000\,\mathrm{J}}{1500\,\mathrm{W}}=6\,\mathrm{s}.
$$
The value $6000\,\mathrm{s}$ would come from dividing by $1.5$ without converting the kilowatts.
