A belt accelerates a box to the right without slipping. In the floor frame the box moves to the right. What is the sign of the work done on the box by static friction from the belt?

- Zero because the surfaces do not slip
- Positive
- Negative
- Alternately positive and negative

## Explanation

The horizontal force from the belt accelerates the box to the right. The contact point on the box also moves right in the floor frame, so the work is positive.
