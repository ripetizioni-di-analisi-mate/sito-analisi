A small ball is released from rest on a smooth U-shaped track, $40\,\mathrm{cm}$ above the lowest point. Air resistance is negligible. How high does it rise on the other side of the track?

- Up to $20\,\mathrm{cm}$
- Up to $80\,\mathrm{cm}$
- Up to $40\,\mathrm{cm}$
- It does not rise and stops at the lowest point

## Explanation

The track is smooth, so the normal force does zero work and mechanical energy is conserved. At the start the ball is at rest, so its energy is all potential. At the highest point on the other side the ball is again at rest, so there too the energy is all potential. The two potential energies are equal, so the ball rises **up to $40\,\mathrm{cm}$**.
