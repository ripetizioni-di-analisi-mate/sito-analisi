An advertisement for a motor claims that, for every $500\,\mathrm{J}$ of energy taken in, the motor delivers $600\,\mathrm{J}$ of useful energy, with no change in stored energy. Which statement about this motor is true?

- It is impossible, because the efficiency would be greater than $1$
- It is possible, with an efficiency of $120\%$
- It is possible, with an efficiency of $83\%$
- It is possible, if the motor runs slowly

## Explanation

The claimed efficiency would be $\eta=\dfrac{600}{500}=1.2$. For a machine whose stored energy does not change, $0\leq\eta\leq1$, because the useful energy is a portion of the input energy. Therefore, an efficiency of $1.2$ is **impossible**, whatever the operating speed. The value $83\%$ would come from the inverted ratio $\dfrac{500}{600}$.
