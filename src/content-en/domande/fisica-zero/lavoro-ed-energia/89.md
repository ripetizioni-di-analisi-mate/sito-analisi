On a level road a vehicle must overcome $200\,\mathrm{N}$ of resistance while travelling steadily at $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The motor converts $80\%$ of its input power to useful power. What input power is required?

- $1250\,\mathrm{W}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{W}$
- $400\,\mathrm{W}$
- $800\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

The steady pull balances resistance, so useful power is $200\cdot5=1000$ watts. The required input is $\dfrac{1000}{0.80}=1250$ watts.
