A mass of $4$ kg is $3$ m above the chosen zero level. With $g=10$ meters per second squared, what is its gravitational potential energy?

- $120\,\mathrm{J}$
- $12\,\mathrm{J}$
- $40\,\mathrm{J}$
- $240\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Near Earth's surface we use $U=mgh$. The height is measured from the reference level. Therefore, $U=4\cdot10\cdot3=120$ joules.
