A motor delivers a constant useful power of $120\,\mathrm{W}$ to raise $12\,\mathrm{kg}$ by $4\,\mathrm{m}$. Initial and final speeds are equal and losses are negligible. Taking $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, how long does the lift take?

- $0.4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $40\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The only energy change is the increase $mgh=480$ joules. The motor supplies $120\,\mathrm{J}$ per second, so the lift takes $4\,\mathrm{s}$.
