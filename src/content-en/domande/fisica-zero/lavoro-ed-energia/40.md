For the same lift, the zero of gravitational potential energy is moved from the floor to the ceiling. Which calculated quantity keeps its original value?

- The final potential energy
- The initial potential energy
- The difference between final and initial potential energy
- The final height measured from the new level

## Explanation

Moving the reference adds the same constant to both energies. It cancels when the initial value is subtracted from the final value.
