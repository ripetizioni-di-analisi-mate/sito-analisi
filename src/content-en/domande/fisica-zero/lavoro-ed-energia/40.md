A mass of $7$ kg is $3$ m above the chosen zero level. With $g=10$ meters per second squared, what is its gravitational potential energy?

- $21\,\mathrm{J}$
- $70\,\mathrm{J}$
- $420\,\mathrm{J}$
- $210\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Near Earth's surface we use $U=mgh$. The height is measured from the reference level. Therefore, $U=7\cdot10\cdot3=210$ joules.
