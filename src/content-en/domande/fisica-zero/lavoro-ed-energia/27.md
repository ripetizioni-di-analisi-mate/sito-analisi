A skater of mass $50\,\mathrm{kg}$ is at rest on the ice. A teammate pushes him, and by the end of the push the net work done by the forces on the skater is $400\,\mathrm{J}$. At what speed does the skater move?

- $16\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The skater starts from rest, so $K_i=0$. By the **work–energy theorem**, $K_f=K_i+W_{\mathrm{tot}}=400\,\mathrm{J}$. From $\dfrac{1}{2}mv^2=400\,\mathrm{J}$ we get
$$
v^2=\frac{2\cdot400}{50}\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}=16\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}.
$$
Therefore, the speed is $v=4\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
