A force gauge and a ruler give a product of $2500\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$ for a pull parallel to the path. What is this work in kilojoules?

- $0.25\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $25\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $2500\,\mathrm{kJ}$

## Explanation

One newton multiplied by one metre corresponds to one joule. Dividing $2500\,\mathrm{J}$ by one thousand gives $2.5\,\mathrm{kJ}$.
