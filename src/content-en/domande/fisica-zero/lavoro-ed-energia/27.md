A body's potential energy changes from $30$ J to $10$ J in the absence of friction. If it starts from rest, what is its final kinetic energy?

- $40\,\mathrm{J}$
- $10\,\mathrm{J}$
- $20\,\mathrm{J}$
- $30\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

In the absence of friction, mechanical energy is conserved. The decrease in potential energy becomes kinetic energy. Therefore, $K_f=30-10=20$ joules.
