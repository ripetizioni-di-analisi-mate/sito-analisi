On the highway, a car of mass $1000\,\mathrm{kg}$ travels at $72\,\mathrm{km/h}$. What is its kinetic energy?

- $2.0\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{J}$
- $2.6\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{J}$
- $4.0\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1.0\cdot10^4\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

To get the energy in joules, the speed must be expressed in meters per second. Since $1\,\mathrm{km/h}$ corresponds to $\dfrac{1}{3.6}\,\mathrm{m/s}$, we have $v=\dfrac{72}{3.6}\,\mathrm{m/s}=20\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Therefore,
$$
K=\frac{1}{2}mv^2=\frac{1}{2}\cdot1000\cdot20^2\,\mathrm{J}=200\,000\,\mathrm{J}=2.0\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
The value $2.6\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{J}$ would come from using $72$ without converting the speed.
