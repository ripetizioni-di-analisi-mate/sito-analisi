A machine does $80$ J of work in $4$ s. What is its average power?

- $320\,\mathrm{W}$
- $80\,\mathrm{W}$
- $0.8\,\mathrm{W}$
- $20\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

Average power is work divided by time. Evaluating $P=\dfrac{80}{4}$ therefore gives an average power of $20$ watts.
