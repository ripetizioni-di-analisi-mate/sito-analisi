A machine does $60\,\mathrm{J}$ of work in $4\,\mathrm{s}$. Its power varies during the test. What is its average power?

- $4\,\mathrm{W}$
- $15\,\mathrm{W}$
- $240\,\mathrm{W}$
- $60\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

Average power uses the total work and the whole time interval. Their ratio $\dfrac{60}{4}$ is $15\,\mathrm{W}$.
