A bucket full of water sits at the bottom of a well, $4\,\mathrm{m}$ below the rim. If the zero level of potential energy is the rim of the well, what is the sign of the bucket's gravitational potential energy?

- Positive
- Negative
- It is zero
- It depends on the mass of the bucket

## Explanation

The height $h$ in $U=mgh$ is measured upward from the zero level. The bucket is $4\,\mathrm{m}$ below the rim, so $h=-4\,\mathrm{m}$. Since $m$ and $g$ are positive, the product $mgh$ has the sign of $h$. Therefore, the bucket's potential energy is **negative**, whatever its mass.
