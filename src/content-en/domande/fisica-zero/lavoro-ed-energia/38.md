A platform rises $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$ carrying a box that stays in contact. The platform's normal force on the box is $40\,\mathrm{N}$. In the floor frame, what is the sum of the work done by the normal force on the box and by its reaction on the platform?

- $40\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{J}$
- $20\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Both contact points rise $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$. The forces point in opposite directions, doing $20\,\mathrm{J}$ on the box and $-20\,\mathrm{J}$ on the platform. Their signed sum is zero.
