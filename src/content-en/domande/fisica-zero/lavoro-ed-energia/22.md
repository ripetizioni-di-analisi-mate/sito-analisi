The kinetic energy $K$ of a bowling ball of fixed mass is plotted against its speed $v$, for $v\geq0$. What does the graph look like?

- A straight line through the origin
- An arc of a parabola starting at the origin and rising ever less steeply
- A horizontal straight line
- An arc of a parabola starting at the origin and rising ever more steeply

## Explanation

With fixed mass, $K=\dfrac{1}{2}mv^2$ is a constant number multiplied by $v^2$. The graph of a function of the form $cv^2$ with $c>0$ is an arc of a parabola with its vertex at the origin. For $v=0$ we have $K=0$, so the graph starts at the origin. Since equal increases in $v$ produce ever larger increases in energy, the arc **rises ever more steeply**.
