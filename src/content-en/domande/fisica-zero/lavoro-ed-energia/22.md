A block slides $3\,\mathrm{m}$ out and $3\,\mathrm{m}$ back along a fixed track. The sliding friction has a constant magnitude of $2\,\mathrm{N}$. How much internal energy do the block and track gain from friction altogether?

- $-12\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $12\,\mathrm{J}$
- $6\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Friction opposes the motion on both legs. It dissipates $2\,\mathrm{J}$ per metre of travel, giving $2(3+3)=12$ joules overall.
