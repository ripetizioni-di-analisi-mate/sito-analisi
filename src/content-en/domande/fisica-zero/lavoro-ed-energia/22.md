A mass of $5$ kg is $3$ m above the chosen zero level. With $g=10$ meters per second squared, what is its gravitational potential energy?

- $15\,\mathrm{J}$
- $150\,\mathrm{J}$
- $50\,\mathrm{J}$
- $300\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Near Earth's surface we use $U=mgh$. The height is measured from the reference level. Therefore, $U=5\cdot10\cdot3=150$ joules.
