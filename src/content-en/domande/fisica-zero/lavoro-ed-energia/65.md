A sliding block and its surface gain a combined $50\,\mathrm{J}$ of internal energy from friction. The block gains $18\,\mathrm{J}$. Neglecting exchanges with the surroundings, how much does the surface gain?

- $68\,\mathrm{J}$
- $32\,\mathrm{J}$
- $18\,\mathrm{J}$
- $25\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The balance includes both bodies. Subtracting the measured gain of the block from the total dissipated energy gives $50-18=32$ joules.
