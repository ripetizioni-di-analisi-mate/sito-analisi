Two motors rated at $60\%$ and $90\%$ efficiency can perform the same $180\,\mathrm{J}$ lift. How much input energy is saved by using the second?

- $180\,\mathrm{J}$
- $54\,\mathrm{J}$
- $30\,\mathrm{J}$
- $100\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The respective inputs are $\dfrac{180}{0.60}=300$ and $\dfrac{180}{0.90}=200$ joules. Their difference gives a saving of $100\,\mathrm{J}$.
