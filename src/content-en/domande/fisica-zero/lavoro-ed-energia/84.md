A blender is labeled with a power of $500$ watts. Which combination of units is one watt equal to?

- $\mathrm{J\cdot s}$
- $\mathrm{J/s}$
- $\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $\mathrm{N/s}$

## Explanation

Average power is the energy transferred divided by the time taken, $P_m=\dfrac{W}{\Delta t}$. Energy is measured in joules and time in seconds, so power is measured in joules per second. Therefore, **$1\,\mathrm{W}=1\,\mathrm{J/s}$**. The combination $\mathrm{N\cdot m}$ is the joule, the unit of energy rather than of power.
