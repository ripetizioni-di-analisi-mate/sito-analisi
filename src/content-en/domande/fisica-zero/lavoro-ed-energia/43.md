A drone of mass $1.2\,\mathrm{kg}$ hovers above a lawn. Relative to the lawn, its gravitational potential energy is $235.44\,\mathrm{J}$. With $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, at what height is the drone?

- $24\,\mathrm{m}$
- $40\,\mathrm{m}$
- $196\,\mathrm{m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

From $U=mgh$, dividing both sides by $mg$ gives the height:
$$
h=\frac{U}{mg}=\frac{235.44}{1.2\cdot9.81}\,\mathrm{m}=\frac{235.44}{11.772}\,\mathrm{m}=20\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
The value $24\,\mathrm{m}$ would come from dividing by $g$ without dividing by the mass.
