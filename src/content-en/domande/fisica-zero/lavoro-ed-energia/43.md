A machine does $140$ J of work in $4$ s. What is its average power?

- $560\,\mathrm{W}$
- $140\,\mathrm{W}$
- $35\,\mathrm{W}$
- $1.4\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

Average power is work divided by time. Evaluating $P=\dfrac{140}{4}$ therefore gives an average power of $35$ watts.
