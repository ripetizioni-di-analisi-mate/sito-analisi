A ball falls in a vacuum. If the chosen system consists only of the ball, how does gravity enter its kinetic-energy balance?

- As internal friction in the ball
- As an external force doing work
- As a force doing zero work
- As a heat transfer

## Explanation

Earth lies outside the chosen system and pulls on the ball. The work done by that pull increases the ball's kinetic energy.
