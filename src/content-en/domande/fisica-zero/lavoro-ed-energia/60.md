A juggler throws a ball straight up at $4.9\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Neglecting air resistance and with $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, how high does the ball rise above the release point?

- $1.22\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.45\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Only gravity does work, so mechanical energy is conserved. At the highest point the ball is momentarily at rest, so all the initial kinetic energy has become potential energy, $\dfrac{1}{2}mv^2=mgh$. The mass cancels, and we get
$$
h=\frac{v^2}{2g}=\frac{4.9^2}{2\cdot9.81}\,\mathrm{m}\approx1.22\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
The value $2.45\,\mathrm{m}$ would come from forgetting the factor $2$ in the denominator.
