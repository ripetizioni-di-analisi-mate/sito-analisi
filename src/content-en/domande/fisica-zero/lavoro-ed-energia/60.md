On a fixed frictionless track, a sled starts to the right with total mechanical energy of $18\,\mathrm{J}$. Before its destination, where potential energy is $5\,\mathrm{J}$, it must pass over a hump whose crest has potential energy of $20\,\mathrm{J}$. What happens?

- It always reaches the destination because the potential energy there is below $18\,\mathrm{J}$
- The hump raises its total mechanical energy to $20\,\mathrm{J}$
- It stops before the crest where potential energy reaches $18\,\mathrm{J}$
- It crosses the crest with $2\,\mathrm{J}$ of kinetic energy

## Explanation

As the sled climbs it loses kinetic energy. When potential energy reaches $18\,\mathrm{J}$, kinetic energy vanishes. Continuing towards the crest would require negative kinetic energy.
