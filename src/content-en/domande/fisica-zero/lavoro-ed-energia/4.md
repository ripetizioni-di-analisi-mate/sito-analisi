A crate moves $5\,\mathrm{m}$ along a level floor under a horizontal pull of $20\,\mathrm{N}$ and an opposing friction force of $6\,\mathrm{N}$. All other forces are vertical. What is the net work?

- $70\,\mathrm{J}$
- $14\,\mathrm{J}$
- $100\,\mathrm{J}$
- $130\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The pull does $100\,\mathrm{J}$ and friction does $-30\,\mathrm{J}$. The vertical forces do zero work, leaving a total of $70\,\mathrm{J}$.
