A mass of $3$ kg is $3$ m above the chosen zero level. With $g=10$ meters per second squared, what is its gravitational potential energy?

- $9\,\mathrm{J}$
- $30\,\mathrm{J}$
- $180\,\mathrm{J}$
- $90\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Near Earth's surface we use $U=mgh$. The height is measured from the reference level. Therefore, $U=3\cdot10\cdot3=90$ joules.
