At the bus stop, a person holds a grocery bag without moving it for two minutes, and at the end their arm is tired. Which statement about the mechanical work done by the hand on the bag during those two minutes is true?

- It is positive, because the arm gets tired
- It is zero, because the bag does not move
- It is negative, because the hand opposes the weight
- It grows the longer the wait lasts

## Explanation

The work of a force requires a displacement of its point of application. The bag stays at rest for the whole two minutes, so the point where the hand's force is applied does not move. Therefore, the mechanical work done by the hand on the bag is **zero**. The tired arm is due to the energy the body uses to keep the muscles contracted, which is not transferred to the bag.
