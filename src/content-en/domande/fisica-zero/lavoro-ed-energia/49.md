A spring is stretched slowly from its natural length. A student multiplies the final force by the extension and obtains $8\,\mathrm{J}$. What is the actual external work for an ideal spring?

- $8\,\mathrm{J}$
- $4\,\mathrm{J}$
- $16\,\mathrm{J}$
- $2\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The force rises linearly from zero. Its average over the extension is half the final value, so the triangular area is $4\,\mathrm{J}$.
