At the gym, a resistance band that behaves like an ideal spring with constant $150\,\mathrm{N/m}$ is stretched by $0.4\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the magnitude of the restoring force that the band exerts on the hand?

- $60\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$
- $375\,\mathrm{N}$
- $30\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

An ideal spring exerts a restoring force of magnitude $F_e=kx$, where $x$ is the extension from the natural length. With $k=150\,\mathrm{N/m}$ and $x=0.4\,\mathrm{m}$ we get
$$
F_e=150\cdot0.4\,\mathrm{N}=60\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
The value $12\,\mathrm{N}$ is the number given by $\dfrac{1}{2}kx^2$, that is, the elastic energy in joules, which is a different quantity.
