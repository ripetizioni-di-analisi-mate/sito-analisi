A student carries an $8\,\mathrm{kg}$ backpack from the ground floor up three floors in a building where each floor is $3\,\mathrm{m}$ high. With $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, by how much does the backpack's gravitational potential energy increase?

- $942\,\mathrm{J}$
- $706\,\mathrm{J}$
- $235\,\mathrm{J}$
- $72\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Going up three floors from the ground floor, the change in height is $3\cdot3\,\mathrm{m}=9\,\mathrm{m}$. The increase in potential energy is $mg$ times the change in height, so
$$
\Delta U=8\cdot9.81\cdot9\,\mathrm{J}\approx706\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
The value $942\,\mathrm{J}$ would come from counting four floors instead of three.
