A $1\,\mathrm{m}$ tall rigid crate is slowly tipped onto its side. Its centre of mass drops from $0.5\,\mathrm{m}$ to $0.2\,\mathrm{m}$. Its mass is $10\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. How much gravitational potential energy does it lose?

- $30\,\mathrm{J}$
- $100\,\mathrm{J}$
- $50\,\mathrm{J}$
- $70\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Use the centre of mass, which drops $0.3\,\mathrm{m}$. The potential energy lost is $10\cdot10\cdot0.3=30$ joules.
