A mass of $2$ kg moves at $3$ m/s. What is its kinetic energy?

- $18\,\mathrm{J}$
- $6\,\mathrm{J}$
- $9\,\mathrm{J}$
- $3\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The kinetic energy is $K=\dfrac{1}{2}mv^2$. With a mass of $2$ kg, the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$ cancels. Therefore, $K=3^2=9$ joules.
