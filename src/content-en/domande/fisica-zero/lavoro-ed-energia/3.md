A box moves $5\,\mathrm{m}$ horizontally across a fixed surface. The surface exerts a vertical normal force of $80\,\mathrm{N}$. How much work does this force do?

- $16\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-400\,\mathrm{J}$
- $400\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The box has no vertical displacement. The force component along its path is zero, so the normal force does zero work.
