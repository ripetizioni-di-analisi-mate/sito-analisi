A $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ sled starts from rest. The net work done by all forces during a push is $49\,\mathrm{J}$. What speed does it reach?

- $49\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $14\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Starting from rest gives zero initial kinetic energy, so the final energy is $49\,\mathrm{J}$. With $m=2$ in SI units, $v^2=49$, giving a speed of $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
