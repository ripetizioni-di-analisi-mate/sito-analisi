To move a sofa $3\,\mathrm{m}$ across the living room floor, a person pushes it with a constant horizontal force in the direction of motion and does $450\,\mathrm{J}$ of work. What is the magnitude of the force?

- $1350\,\mathrm{N}$
- $150\,\mathrm{N}$
- $75\,\mathrm{N}$
- $300\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The force is constant and points in the direction of the displacement, so $W=Fs$. Dividing both sides by $s$ gives the force:
$$
F=\frac{W}{s}=\frac{450\,\mathrm{J}}{3\,\mathrm{m}}=150\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
The result is in newtons, because a joule divided by a meter is a newton.
