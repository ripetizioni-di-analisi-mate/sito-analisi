A machine does $60$ J of work in $4$ s. What is its average power?

- $240\,\mathrm{W}$
- $60\,\mathrm{W}$
- $15\,\mathrm{W}$
- $0.6\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

Average power is work divided by time. Evaluating $P=\dfrac{60}{4}$ therefore gives an average power of $15$ watts.
