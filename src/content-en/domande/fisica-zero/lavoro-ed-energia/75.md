A winch pulls horizontally with a steady force of $150\,\mathrm{N}$ while its load moves at $0.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ along the pull. What power does the pulling force deliver?

- $150\,\mathrm{W}$
- $60\,\mathrm{W}$
- $375\,\mathrm{W}$
- $600\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

In one second the load travels $0.4\,\mathrm{m}$. The work done in that second is $150\cdot0.4=60$ joules, so the power is $60\,\mathrm{W}$.
