A coin is slid across a level table and, after a few centimeters, stops because of friction. What becomes of its initial kinetic energy?

- It becomes gravitational potential energy of the coin
- It becomes kinetic energy of the table
- It increases the internal energy of the coin and the table
- It disappears without turning into other forms

## Explanation

The table is level, so the coin's gravitational potential energy does not change. Friction does negative work, and the coin's mechanical energy decreases to zero. The energy transferred through friction **increases the internal energy of the coin and the table**, which warm up slightly. When this transformation is included, the overall energy balance still holds.
