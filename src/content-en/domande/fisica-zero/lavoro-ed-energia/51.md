An ideal spring stores $0.75\,\mathrm{J}$ at a deformation of $0.10\,\mathrm{m}$. What is its spring constant?

- $15\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $75\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $150\,\mathrm{N/m}$

## Explanation

Rearranging $U_e=\dfrac12kx^2$ gives $k=\dfrac{2U_e}{x^2}$. Dividing 1.50 by 0.01 gives $150\,\mathrm{N/m}$.
