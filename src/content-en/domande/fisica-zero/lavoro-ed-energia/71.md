Inside an insulated container a wheel slows to rest through internal friction. The bearings and wheel belong to the system, and there is no external work. Which quantity of the entire system stays constant?

- The sum of mechanical and internal energies
- The wheel's kinetic energy alone
- Mechanical energy alone
- Internal energy alone

## Explanation

Friction transfers energy from the motion to internal energy in the wheel and bearings. With no energy exchanged across the system boundary, the total stays constant.
