The joule is the unit of work. Which combination of units is one joule equal to?

- $\mathrm{N/m}$
- $\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $\mathrm{N\cdot s}$
- $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$

## Explanation

For a constant force in the direction of the displacement, the work is $W=Fs$, that is, the product of a force and a length. Force is measured in newtons and length in meters, so work is measured in $\mathrm{N\cdot m}$. One joule is the work done by a force of $1\,\mathrm{N}$ acting over $1\,\mathrm{m}$ in the direction of the displacement, so **$1\,\mathrm{J}=1\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$**. The combination $\mathrm{N/m}$ is the ratio of a force to a length, while $\mathrm{N\cdot s}$ is the product of a force and a time.
