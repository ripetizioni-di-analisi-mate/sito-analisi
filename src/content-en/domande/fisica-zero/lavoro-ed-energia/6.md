A body of mass $5\,\mathrm{kg}$ has $90\,\mathrm{J}$ of kinetic energy. What is its speed?

- $36\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

From $K=\dfrac12 mv^2$, the squared speed is $\dfrac{2K}{m}=36$ in SI units. Its positive square root is $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
