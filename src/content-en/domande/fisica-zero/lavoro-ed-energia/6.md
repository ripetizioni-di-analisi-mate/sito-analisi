A body initially has $9$ J of kinetic energy. The net work done on it is $6$ J. What is its final kinetic energy?

- $3\,\mathrm{J}$
- $15\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{J}$
- $54\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The work–energy theorem gives $K_f-K_i=W_\mathrm{ris}$. Add the work to the initial energy. This gives $K_f=9+6=15$ joules.
