An electric winch with an efficiency of $80\%$ delivers $2400\,\mathrm{J}$ of useful energy to lift a boat onto its trailer. Assuming no change in the energy stored in the winch, how much energy is transferred in non-useful forms?

- $480\,\mathrm{J}$
- $3000\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1920\,\mathrm{J}$
- $600\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

From the definition of efficiency, the energy taken in is
$$
E_{\mathrm{ass}}=\frac{E_{\mathrm{utile}}}{\eta}=\frac{2400\,\mathrm{J}}{0.80}=3000\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
The energy stored in the winch does not change, so the part of the input energy that is not useful is transferred in other forms. Therefore, the energy in non-useful forms is $3000\,\mathrm{J}-2400\,\mathrm{J}=600\,\mathrm{J}$. The value $480\,\mathrm{J}$ would come from taking $20\%$ of the useful energy instead of the input energy.
