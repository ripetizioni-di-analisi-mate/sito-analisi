A mains-powered motor raises a known mass at constant speed through a known height. The value of $g$ is known. Which reading completes a measurement of lifting efficiency?

- Electrical energy drawn during this same lift
- Total cable length
- Pulley diameter
- Only the motor's final temperature

## Explanation

Useful work is already determined by $mgh$. Efficiency requires the input energy for that same interval.
