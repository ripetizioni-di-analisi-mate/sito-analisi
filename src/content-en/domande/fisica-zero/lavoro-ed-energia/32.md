Two $1\,\mathrm{kg}$ sliders move at $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in opposite directions. What is their total kinetic energy before they meet?

- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $2\,\mathrm{J}$
- $4\,\mathrm{J}$
- $8\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Each slider has $\dfrac12\cdot1\cdot2^2=2$ joules. Kinetic energies are scalars and add even when the velocities point in opposite directions.
