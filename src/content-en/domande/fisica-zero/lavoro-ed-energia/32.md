A spring with $k=600$ N/m is stretched by $0.2$ m. What is its elastic energy?

- $24\,\mathrm{J}$
- $120\,\mathrm{J}$
- $60\,\mathrm{J}$
- $12\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The elastic energy is $U_e=\dfrac{1}{2}k(\Delta\ell)^2$. The square of $0.2$ is $0.04$. Therefore, $U_e=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot600\cdot0.04=12$ joules.
