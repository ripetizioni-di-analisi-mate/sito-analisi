A body's potential energy changes from $24$ J to $8$ J in the absence of friction. If it starts from rest, what is its final kinetic energy?

- $32\,\mathrm{J}$
- $16\,\mathrm{J}$
- $8\,\mathrm{J}$
- $24\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

In the absence of friction, mechanical energy is conserved. The decrease in potential energy becomes kinetic energy. Therefore, $K_f=24-8=16$ joules.
