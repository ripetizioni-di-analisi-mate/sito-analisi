In the formula $K=\dfrac{1}{2}mv^2$, mass is in kilograms and speed is in meters per second. Which combination of base units, then, expresses kinetic energy?

- $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s^2}$
- $\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s^2}$

## Explanation

The factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$ is a pure number and has no units. The mass carries the unit $\mathrm{kg}$, while $v^2$ carries $\left(\mathrm{m/s}\right)^2=\mathrm{m^2/s^2}$. Therefore, kinetic energy is measured in **$\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s^2}$**, which is the joule. Indeed, $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s^2}$ is the newton, and a newton times a meter gives $\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s^2}$ again.
