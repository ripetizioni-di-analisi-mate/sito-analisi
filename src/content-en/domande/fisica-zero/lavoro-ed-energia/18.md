A body starts from rest with $18\,\mathrm{J}$ of potential energy. At a second point its potential energy is $6\,\mathrm{J}$. If only conservative forces do work, what kinetic energy has it acquired?

- $18\,\mathrm{J}$
- $24\,\mathrm{J}$
- $6\,\mathrm{J}$
- $12\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The sum of kinetic and potential energy stays at $18\,\mathrm{J}$. At the second point $6\,\mathrm{J}$ remain as potential energy, leaving $12\,\mathrm{J}$ as kinetic energy.
