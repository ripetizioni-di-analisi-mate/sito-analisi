A flowerpot of mass $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ sits on a balcony railing, $6\,\mathrm{m}$ above the sidewalk. Taking the sidewalk as the zero level and $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the gravitational potential energy of the pot?

- $177\,\mathrm{J}$
- $18\,\mathrm{J}$
- $88\,\mathrm{J}$
- $29\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Near Earth's surface, the gravitational potential energy is $U=mgh$, with $h$ measured upward from the zero level. The pot is at $h=6\,\mathrm{m}$ above the sidewalk, so
$$
U=3\cdot9.81\cdot6\,\mathrm{J}=176.58\,\mathrm{J}\approx177\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
The value $18\,\mathrm{J}$ would come from forgetting the factor $g$.
