A body's potential energy changes from $36$ J to $12$ J in the absence of friction. If it starts from rest, what is its final kinetic energy?

- $48\,\mathrm{J}$
- $12\,\mathrm{J}$
- $36\,\mathrm{J}$
- $24\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

In the absence of friction, mechanical energy is conserved. The decrease in potential energy becomes kinetic energy. Therefore, $K_f=36-12=24$ joules.
