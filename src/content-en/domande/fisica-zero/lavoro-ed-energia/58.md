A hand presses with its palm on the end of a spring resting against a wall and compresses it. In what direction is the force that the spring exerts on the hand?

- Toward the wall, in the same direction as the compression
- None, because the force is zero while the hand is at rest
- Outward from the spring, opposite to the compression
- Perpendicular to the axis of the spring

## Explanation

The spring force is a restoring force, that is, it tends to bring the spring back to its natural length. The compressed spring tends to lengthen, so it pushes the hand along its axis, away from the wall. Therefore, the force on the hand points **opposite to the compression**. Its magnitude is $kx$, which is not zero even when the hand is at rest.
