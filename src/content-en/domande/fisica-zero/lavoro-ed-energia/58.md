On a smooth fixed track a body slows from $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ under gravity and the normal force alone. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, how has its height changed?

- It rose $0.2\,\mathrm{m}$
- It rose $2.8\,\mathrm{m}$
- It fell $1.4\,\mathrm{m}$
- It rose $1.4\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Lost kinetic energy becomes gravitational potential energy. The height increases by $\dfrac{8^2-6^2}{2\cdot10}=1.4$ metres.
