A constant force of $4$ N acts in the direction of a displacement of $4$ m. How much work does it do?

- $1\,\mathrm{J}$
- $16\,\mathrm{J}$
- $8\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-16\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

When the force and displacement point in the same direction, the work is $W=F\Delta s$. Multiplying $4$ N by $4$ m therefore gives $W=16$ joules.
