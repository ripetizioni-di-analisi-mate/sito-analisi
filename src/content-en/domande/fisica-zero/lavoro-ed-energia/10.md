A $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass is raised from a height of $1\,\mathrm{m}$ to a height of $5\,\mathrm{m}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, how much work does gravity do on the mass?

- $-100\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-120\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-80\,\mathrm{J}$
- $80\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The weight acts downwards while the mass rises $4\,\mathrm{m}$. The work done by gravity is $mg(h_i-h_f)=20(1-5)=-80$ joules.
