A crate is dragged across a warehouse floor by a horizontal rope. The forces on the crate are the tension in the rope, friction from the floor, the weight, and the normal force from the floor. Which of these forces does zero work during the displacement?

- The tension in the rope
- Friction from the floor
- They all do nonzero work
- The normal force from the floor

## Explanation

The crate moves horizontally. The normal force from the floor is vertical, so it is perpendicular to the displacement. A force perpendicular to the displacement does zero work, so **the normal force does zero work**. The tension is horizontal in the direction of motion and does positive work, while friction opposes the motion and does negative work. The weight, which is vertical, also does zero work.
