A hoist continuously delivers $600\,\mathrm{W}$ of useful power. Each $20\,\mathrm{kg}$ crate must rise $3\,\mathrm{m}$ with equal initial and final speeds. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and no losses or pauses, how many crates can it lift in $1\,\mathrm{min}$?

- 60
- 180
- 10
- 20

## Explanation

Each crate requires $600\,\mathrm{J}$. In one minute the hoist delivers $600\cdot60=36000$ joules, enough for 60 crates.
