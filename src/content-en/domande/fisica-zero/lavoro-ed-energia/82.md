During a bike ride, a cyclist delivers an average mechanical power of $200\,\mathrm{W}$ to the pedals for $30$ minutes. How much work does she do in this interval?

- $6.0\cdot10^3\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1.2\cdot10^4\,\mathrm{J}$
- $3.6\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{J}$
- $3.6\cdot10^4\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

From the definition $P_m=\dfrac{W}{\Delta t}$, multiplying by $\Delta t$ gives $W=P_m\,\Delta t$. The time must be expressed in seconds, so $\Delta t=30\cdot60\,\mathrm{s}=1800\,\mathrm{s}$. Therefore,
$$
W=200\,\mathrm{W}\cdot1800\,\mathrm{s}=360\,000\,\mathrm{J}=3.6\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
The value $6.0\cdot10^3\,\mathrm{J}$ would come from leaving the time in minutes.
