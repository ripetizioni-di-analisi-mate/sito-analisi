A spring initially storing $18\,\mathrm{J}$ returns to natural length and pushes a stationary $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ trolley on a level track. During release $2\,\mathrm{J}$ are dissipated. What is the final speed?

- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The trolley gains $18-2=16$ joules of kinetic energy. With $m=2$ in SI units, $v=\sqrt{16}=4$ metres per second.
