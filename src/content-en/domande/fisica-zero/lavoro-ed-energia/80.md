The same child slides twice, starting from rest, down the same playground slide: the first time the slide is dry and friction is not negligible, the second time it is wet and friction is negligible. In which case does the child reach the bottom faster?

- With the dry slide
- With the wet slide
- At the same speed in both cases
- It depends only on the child's mass

## Explanation

In both descents the drop in height is the same, so the potential energy lost is the same. With the wet slide friction is negligible, and all the potential energy lost becomes kinetic energy. With the dry slide friction does negative work, so the mechanical energy at the bottom is smaller. Consequently, the child reaches the bottom faster **with the wet slide**.
