A motor's meter records consumption of $0.5\,\mathrm{kWh}$. How much energy is this in kilojoules?

- $3600\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $30\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $500\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $1800\,\mathrm{kJ}$

## Explanation

A kilowatt is a kilojoule per second and an hour contains $3600\,\mathrm{s}$. The energy is therefore $0.5\cdot3600=1800$ kilojoules.
