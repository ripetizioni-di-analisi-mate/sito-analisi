On a track with a signed coordinate, a sensor reports a mass of $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ and kinetic energy of $16\,\mathrm{J}$. Which signed velocities are consistent with the reading?

- Only $+4$ metres per second
- $+4$ or $-4$ metres per second
- $+8$ or $-8$ metres per second
- Only $-16$ metres per second

## Explanation

Kinetic energy depends on $v^2$, which is 16 in SI units here. Either direction gives the same square.
