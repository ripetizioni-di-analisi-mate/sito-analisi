A constant force of $6$ N acts in the direction of a displacement of $4$ m. How much work does it do?

- $1.5\,\mathrm{J}$
- $10\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-24\,\mathrm{J}$
- $24\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

When the force and displacement point in the same direction, the work is $W=F\Delta s$. Multiplying $6$ N by $4$ m therefore gives $W=24$ joules.
