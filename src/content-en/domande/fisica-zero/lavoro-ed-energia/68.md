A motor puts $40\,\mathrm{J}$ of work into a system while friction dissipates $9\,\mathrm{J}$ of mechanical energy. Its initial mechanical energy is $20\,\mathrm{J}$. What is the final value?

- $31\,\mathrm{J}$
- $51\,\mathrm{J}$
- $69\,\mathrm{J}$
- $11\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Add the motor input and subtract the dissipated energy from the initial value. The balance gives $20+40-9=51$ joules.
