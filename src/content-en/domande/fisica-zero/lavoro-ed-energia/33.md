A body initially has $18$ J of kinetic energy. The net work done on it is $12$ J. What is its final kinetic energy?

- $30\,\mathrm{J}$
- $6\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{J}$
- $216\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The work–energy theorem gives $K_f-K_i=W_\mathrm{ris}$. Add the work to the initial energy. This gives $K_f=18+12=30$ joules.
