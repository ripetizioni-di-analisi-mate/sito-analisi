Two identical carts roll along a level track at the same speed. The first is stopped by a constant braking force in $2\,\mathrm{m}$, the second by another constant braking force in $4\,\mathrm{m}$. Which statement about the net work done by the forces on the two carts is true?

- The net work on the first cart is twice as large
- The net work is the same for the two carts
- The net work on the second cart is twice as large
- The net work is zero for both

## Explanation

By the **work–energy theorem**, the net work is $W_{\mathrm{tot}}=K_f-K_i$. The carts have the same mass and the same initial speed, hence the same $K_i$, and both end at rest, so $K_f=0$. Therefore, the net work is $-K_i$ for both. The different stopping distances show that the two braking forces have different magnitudes, while the work they do is the same.
