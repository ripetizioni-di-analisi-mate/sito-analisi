A technician plots $K$ in joules against $v^2$ in square metres per second squared. The line passes through the origin and has numerical slope 1.5. What is the mass?

- $3\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.75\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

In $K=\dfrac m2v^2$, the coefficient of $v^2$ equals half the mass. Twice 1.5 is 3, giving a mass of $3\,\mathrm{kg}$.
