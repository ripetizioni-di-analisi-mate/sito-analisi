An ideal spring goes slowly from $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ of compression to $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ of extension. What is the overall change in elastic energy?

- It equals twice the initial energy
- It is positive because the spring ends in tension
- It is negative because the spring passes through its natural length
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The deformation changes sign, yet its square is the same at both endpoints. Passing through zero energy en route leaves equal initial and final energies.
