A $10\,\mathrm{kg}$ trolley moves at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. On the level track the only force doing work is a constant resistance of $20\,\mathrm{N}$. How far does the trolley travel before stopping?

- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The trolley must lose $\dfrac12\cdot10\cdot4^2=80$ joules. The resistance removes $20\,\mathrm{J}$ per metre, so the stopping distance is $4\,\mathrm{m}$.
