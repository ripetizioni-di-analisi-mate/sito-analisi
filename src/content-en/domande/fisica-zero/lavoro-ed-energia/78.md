A bartender slides a tray along the level counter toward a customer. Over the $0.8\,\mathrm{m}$ the tray travels, its mechanical energy decreases by $1.2\,\mathrm{J}$ because of kinetic friction, which is constant. What is the magnitude of the friction force?

- $0.96\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.67\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.75\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Friction is the only force that does work, because the weight and the normal force are perpendicular to the level counter. So the decrease in mechanical energy equals the magnitude of the work done by friction, $fs=1.2\,\mathrm{J}$. Therefore,
$$
f=\frac{1.2\,\mathrm{J}}{0.8\,\mathrm{m}}=1.5\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
The value $0.96\,\mathrm{N}$ would come from multiplying the two quantities instead of dividing them.
