A drive can deliver at most $100\,\mathrm{W}$. A manoeuvre requires at least $450\,\mathrm{J}$ of useful work. What is the shortest time compatible with this limit?

- $4.5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2.25\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.22\,\mathrm{s}$
- $45\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

At most $100\,\mathrm{J}$ can be delivered each second. The lower bound is $\dfrac{450}{100}=4.5$ seconds, attained by maintaining maximum power.
