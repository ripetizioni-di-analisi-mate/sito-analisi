A $10\,\mathrm{kg}$ crate is taken from the ground floor up one floor, $3\,\mathrm{m}$ higher, once with a vertical freight elevator and once along a ramp $15\,\mathrm{m}$ long. How does the work done by gravity compare in the two cases?

- It is larger in magnitude along the ramp, because the path is longer
- It is the same
- It is larger in magnitude with the elevator, because the climb is vertical
- Along the ramp the work done by gravity is positive

## Explanation

The work done by gravity is $W_P=mg(h_i-h_f)$, so it depends only on the initial and final heights. In both cases the crate starts on the ground floor and ends $3\,\mathrm{m}$ higher, so the change in height is the same. Consequently, the two amounts of work are **the same**, and both are negative because the crate goes up. The length of the ramp does not appear in the formula.
