A body initially has $21$ J of kinetic energy. The net work done on it is $14$ J. What is its final kinetic energy?

- $7\,\mathrm{J}$
- $35\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-7\,\mathrm{J}$
- $294\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The work–energy theorem gives $K_f-K_i=W_\mathrm{ris}$. Add the work to the initial energy. This gives $K_f=21+14=35$ joules.
