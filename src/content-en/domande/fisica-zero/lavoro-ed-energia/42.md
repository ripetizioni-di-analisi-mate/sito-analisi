Raising a $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ sample slowly by $3\,\mathrm{m}$ on a planet increases its potential energy by $72\,\mathrm{J}$. What is the local gravitational acceleration, assumed uniform?

- $18\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $24\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The change is $mg\Delta h$. Dividing 72 by $4\cdot3$ gives $6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
