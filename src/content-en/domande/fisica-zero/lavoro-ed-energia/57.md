A simple pendulum reaches the end of its swing with the string 30 degrees from the vertical. It is momentarily at rest. Which statement is correct?

- Its kinetic energy is zero and gravity has a tangential component
- Its mechanical energy must be zero
- Its tangential acceleration is zero
- Its weight is zero

## Explanation

At the turning point $v=0$. Gravity still has a component along the arc towards its lowest point, which starts the bob moving back.
