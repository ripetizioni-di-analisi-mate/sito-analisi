A sled of mass $20\,\mathrm{kg}$, at rest on smooth level snow, is pulled by a constant horizontal force of $50\,\mathrm{N}$ through $4\,\mathrm{m}$. Friction is negligible. What speed does the sled reach?

- $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.16\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4.47\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The weight and the normal force are vertical and do zero work, so the net work is that of the pulling force, $W_{\mathrm{tot}}=50\cdot4=200\,\mathrm{J}$. The sled starts from rest, so by the **work–energy theorem** $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot20\cdot v^2=200\,\mathrm{J}$. We get
$$
v=\sqrt{\frac{2\cdot200}{20}}\,\mathrm{m/s}=\sqrt{20}\,\mathrm{m/s}\approx4.47\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
