A trolley starts with $12\,\mathrm{J}$ of kinetic energy. A report gives the combined work of all forces over the section as $-20\,\mathrm{J}$. Which check reveals an inconsistency?

- Work must equal the initial energy
- Every resistive force must do positive work
- A moving body must accelerate
- The calculated final energy would be $-8\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The work–energy theorem would give $12-20=-8$ joules. With positive mass, $\dfrac12mv^2$ is always greater than or equal to zero.
