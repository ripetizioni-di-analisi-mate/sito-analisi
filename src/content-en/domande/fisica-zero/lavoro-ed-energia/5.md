A trolley modelled as a particle has mass $6\,\mathrm{kg}$ and speed $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is its kinetic energy?

- $24\,\mathrm{J}$
- $96\,\mathrm{J}$
- $48\,\mathrm{J}$
- $12\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The squared speed is $4^2=16$ in SI units. Multiplying by half the mass gives $K=3\cdot16=48$ joules.
