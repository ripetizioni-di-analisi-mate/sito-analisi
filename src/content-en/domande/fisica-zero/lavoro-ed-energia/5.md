A rolling suitcase is pulled $15\,\mathrm{m}$ along a level sidewalk by a constant force of $40\,\mathrm{N}$ in the direction of motion. The wheels experience a constant resistive force of $10\,\mathrm{N}$ opposite to the motion, and the weight and the normal force are vertical. What is the net work done on the suitcase?

- $450\,\mathrm{J}$
- $750\,\mathrm{J}$
- $600\,\mathrm{J}$
- $30\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The net work is the sum of the work done by all the forces, each with its own sign. The pulling force points in the direction of motion, so it does $40\cdot15=600\,\mathrm{J}$. The resistive force opposes the motion, so it does $-10\cdot15=-150\,\mathrm{J}$. The weight and the normal force are perpendicular to the displacement, so they do zero work. We get
$$
W_{\mathrm{tot}}=600\,\mathrm{J}-150\,\mathrm{J}=450\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
