An app plots cumulative work $W$ against position $x$. Between $1\,\mathrm{m}$ and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ the graph is straight and $W$ rises from $7\,\mathrm{J}$ to $19\,\mathrm{J}$. What is the force component along $x$?

- $3\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$
- $6.5\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Use the change in work over the section. The ratio $\dfrac{19-7}{4-1}=4$ gives the force in newtons.
