A particle moves without losses with a total mechanical energy of $12\,\mathrm{J}$. At which point could it have a nonzero speed?

- Where $U=7$ joules
- Where $U=15$ joules
- Where $U=12$ joules
- Where $U=20$ joules

## Explanation

The relation $K=12-U$ requires $U<12$ for $K>0$. At $7\,\mathrm{J}$ of potential energy, $5\,\mathrm{J}$ are available for motion.
