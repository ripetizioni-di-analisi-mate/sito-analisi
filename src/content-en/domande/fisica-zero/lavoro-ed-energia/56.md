For an ideal spring, $F_e=kx$, with the force in newtons and the deformation in meters. In what unit is the spring constant $k$ measured?

- $\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $\mathrm{J}$
- $\mathrm{N}$
- $\mathrm{N/m}$

## Explanation

From $F_e=kx$, dividing by $x$ gives $k=\dfrac{F_e}{x}$. Force is measured in newtons and deformation in meters, so $k$ is measured in **newtons per meter**, $\mathrm{N/m}$. This value tells how many newtons are needed for each meter of deformation. The product $\mathrm{N\cdot m}$, by contrast, is the joule, the unit of work or energy.
