During a test a motor delivers $10\,\mathrm{W}$ for $6\,\mathrm{s}$, then $30\,\mathrm{W}$ for $2\,\mathrm{s}$. How much mechanical energy does it deliver in total?

- $320\,\mathrm{J}$
- $80\,\mathrm{J}$
- $120\,\mathrm{J}$
- $160\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The intervals contribute $10\cdot6=60$ and $30\cdot2=60$ joules respectively. The energies delivered add.
