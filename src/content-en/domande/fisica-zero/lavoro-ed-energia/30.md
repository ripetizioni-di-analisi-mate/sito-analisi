Trolleys A and B have equal kinetic energies. B has four times the mass of A. How do their speeds compare?

- $v_B=v_A$
- $v_B=\dfrac{v_A}{4}$
- $v_B=2v_A$
- $v_B=\dfrac{v_A}{2}$

## Explanation

Equal energies require $m_Av_A^2=4m_Av_B^2$. Taking positive speeds gives $v_B=\dfrac{v_A}{2}$.
