A mass of $2$ kg moves at $6$ m/s. What is its kinetic energy?

- $72\,\mathrm{J}$
- $36\,\mathrm{J}$
- $12\,\mathrm{J}$
- $6\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The kinetic energy is $K=\dfrac{1}{2}mv^2$. With a mass of $2$ kg, the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$ cancels. Therefore, $K=6^2=36$ joules.
