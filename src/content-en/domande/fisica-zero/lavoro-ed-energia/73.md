A $5\,\mathrm{kg}$ crate is launched at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ across the level floor of a garage and slides $2\,\mathrm{m}$, slowed by a constant kinetic friction force of $8\,\mathrm{N}$. What is its speed after these $2\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $9.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4.7\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The floor is level, so the potential energy does not change, and the change in mechanical energy equals the change in kinetic energy. At the start $K_i=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot5\cdot4^2\,\mathrm{J}=40\,\mathrm{J}$, and friction does $W_a=-8\cdot2=-16\,\mathrm{J}$. Therefore, $K_f=40\,\mathrm{J}-16\,\mathrm{J}=24\,\mathrm{J}$. From $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot5\cdot v^2=24$ we get
$$
v=\sqrt{\frac{48}{5}}\,\mathrm{m/s}=\sqrt{9.6}\,\mathrm{m/s}\approx3.1\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
