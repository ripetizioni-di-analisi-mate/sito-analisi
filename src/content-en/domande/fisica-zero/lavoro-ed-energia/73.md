Two identical loaded sleds start at the same speed on different level surfaces. One stops in $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and the other in $5\,\mathrm{m}$. Sliding friction is constant during each test. What is the ratio of the first friction force to the second?

- $\dfrac{25}{4}$
- $\dfrac25$
- $\dfrac52$
- $1$

## Explanation

Both lose the same kinetic energy, so $F_1\cdot2=F_2\cdot5$. The shorter stopping distance corresponds to the greater force.
