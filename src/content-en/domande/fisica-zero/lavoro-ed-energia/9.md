The graph shows the force, parallel to the motion and in the same direction, that a conveyor belt exerts on a package as a function of the package's displacement. How much work does the force do over the first $5\,\mathrm{m}$?

::grafico{f="20" xmin=0 xmax=7 ymin=0 ymax=40 nomi="s,F" segmenti="5,0,5,20" titolo="Force of the belt on the package, with $s$ in meters and $F$ in newtons."}

- $4\,\mathrm{J}$
- $100\,\mathrm{J}$
- $25\,\mathrm{J}$
- $50\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The graph shows a constant force of $20\,\mathrm{N}$ over the entire displacement. A constant force in the direction of the displacement does work $W=Fs$. Therefore, over the first $5\,\mathrm{m}$,
$$
W=20\,\mathrm{N}\cdot5\,\mathrm{m}=100\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
This product is the area of the rectangle between the line $F=20\,\mathrm{N}$ and the $s$ axis, from $s=0$ to $s=5\,\mathrm{m}$.
