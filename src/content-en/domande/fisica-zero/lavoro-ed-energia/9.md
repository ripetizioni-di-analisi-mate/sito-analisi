A $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ backpack rests on a shelf $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ above the floor. Taking gravitational potential energy to be zero at floor level and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the backpack's potential energy?

- $60\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-60\,\mathrm{J}$
- $40\,\mathrm{J}$
- $6\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The height relative to the chosen level is positive. This gives $U=mgh=4\cdot10\cdot1.5=60$ joules.
