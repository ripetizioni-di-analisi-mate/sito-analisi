A body's potential energy changes from $18$ J to $6$ J in the absence of friction. If it starts from rest, what is its final kinetic energy?

- $12\,\mathrm{J}$
- $24\,\mathrm{J}$
- $6\,\mathrm{J}$
- $18\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

In the absence of friction, mechanical energy is conserved. The decrease in potential energy becomes kinetic energy. Therefore, $K_f=18-6=12$ joules.
