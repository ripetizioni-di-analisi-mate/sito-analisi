A plant receives $100\,\mathrm{J}$, producing $40\,\mathrm{J}$ of work, $35\,\mathrm{J}$ of heat recovered for a building and $25\,\mathrm{J}$ of waste heat. The contract counts both work and building heat as useful. What is the efficiency?

- $75\%$
- $40\%$
- $35\%$
- $25\%$

## Explanation

The specified useful outputs add to $40+35=75$ joules. Compared with $100\,\mathrm{J}$ of input this gives $75\%$ efficiency.
