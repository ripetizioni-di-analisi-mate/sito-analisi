Over a $2\,\mathrm{m}$ displacement to the right, the potential energy associated with a constant conservative force falls from $11\,\mathrm{J}$ to $5\,\mathrm{J}$. What is the horizontal component of the force?

- $3\,\mathrm{N}$ to the right
- $8\,\mathrm{N}$ to the right
- $6\,\mathrm{N}$ to the left
- $3\,\mathrm{N}$ to the left

## Explanation

The force does $11-5=6$ joules of work. Positive work over $2\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right requires a rightward component of $3\,\mathrm{N}$.
