A body's potential energy changes from $42$ J to $14$ J in the absence of friction. If it starts from rest, what is its final kinetic energy?

- $28\,\mathrm{J}$
- $56\,\mathrm{J}$
- $14\,\mathrm{J}$
- $42\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

In the absence of friction, mechanical energy is conserved. The decrease in potential energy becomes kinetic energy. Therefore, $K_f=42-14=28$ joules.
