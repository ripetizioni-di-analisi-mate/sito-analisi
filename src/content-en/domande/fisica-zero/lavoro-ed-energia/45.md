In a warehouse, four packages are lifted, each starting from the floor. Which lift produces the largest increase in gravitational potential energy?

- A $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ package lifted $1\,\mathrm{m}$
- A $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ package lifted $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- A $1\,\mathrm{kg}$ package lifted $5\,\mathrm{m}$
- A $7\,\mathrm{kg}$ package lifted $0.5\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The increase in potential energy is $mg\,\Delta h$, where $\Delta h$ is the rise. The factor $g$ is the same for all the packages, so it is enough to compare the products $m\,\Delta h$. They are $2\cdot3=6$, $4\cdot1=4$, $1\cdot5=5$, and $7\cdot0.5=3.5$, in $\mathrm{kg\cdot m}$. Therefore, the largest increase comes from **the $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ package lifted $3\,\mathrm{m}$**.
