A trolley descends while compressing a spring. Gravitational potential energy falls by $30\,\mathrm{J}$ and elastic potential energy rises by $12\,\mathrm{J}$. All other forces do zero work. How does kinetic energy change?

- It increases by $18\,\mathrm{J}$
- It decreases by $18\,\mathrm{J}$
- It increases by $42\,\mathrm{J}$
- It decreases by $42\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The net potential-energy change is $-30+12=-18$ joules. Conservation requires the opposite change in kinetic energy.
