A bowling ball and a tennis ball are dropped from rest from a window at the same moment and from the same height. Air resistance is negligible. How do their speeds compare when they reach the ground?

- The bowling ball's speed is greater
- The tennis ball's speed is greater
- They are equal
- They depend on the ratio of the masses of the two balls

## Explanation

For each ball, mechanical energy is conserved, so $mgh=\dfrac{1}{2}mv^2$. The mass appears on both sides and cancels, so $v=\sqrt{2gh}$. The two balls fall from the same height, so they reach the ground at **the same speed**. Their kinetic energies, however, are different, because the bowling ball has the larger mass.
