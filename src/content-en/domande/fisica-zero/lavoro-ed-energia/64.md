A block on a level track loses $2\,\mathrm{J}$ per metre travelled on rough material. Readings either side of a partly smooth section give kinetic energies of $18\,\mathrm{J}$ and $12\,\mathrm{J}$. How much rough track did it cross?

- $9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The smooth section leaves the energy unchanged. The measured $6\,\mathrm{J}$ loss corresponds to three metres of rough material.
