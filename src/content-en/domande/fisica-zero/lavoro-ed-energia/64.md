A roller-coaster car has a mechanical energy of $50\,\mathrm{kJ}$, and friction and air resistance are negligible. At a certain point of the track, its gravitational potential energy is $32\,\mathrm{kJ}$. What is its kinetic energy there?

- $18\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $82\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $32\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $50\,\mathrm{kJ}$

## Explanation

Without friction, the mechanical energy $E=K+U$ is conserved along the whole track. So at every point $K=E-U$. At the point considered,
$$
K=50\,\mathrm{kJ}-32\,\mathrm{kJ}=18\,\mathrm{kJ}.
$$
The value $82\,\mathrm{kJ}$ would come from adding the two energies instead of subtracting them.
