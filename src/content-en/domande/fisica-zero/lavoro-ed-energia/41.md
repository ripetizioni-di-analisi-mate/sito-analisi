Two fixed tracks connect the same points A and B. One is longer and has a short uphill section. The same block travels from A to B on each track. How do the works done by gravity alone compare?

- More work is done on the track without an uphill section
- More work is done on the longer track
- They depend on the descent time
- They are equal

## Explanation

Work by gravity is $mg(h_A-h_B)$. Both tracks share the same endpoint heights, so intermediate rises and falls cancel.
