An apple comes off a branch and falls to the ground. Which statement about the work done by gravity and the apple's gravitational potential energy during the fall is true?

- Gravity does negative work, because the potential energy decreases
- Gravity does positive work and the potential energy increases
- Gravity does positive work, equal to the decrease in potential energy
- Gravity does zero work, because the apple starts from rest

## Explanation

The work done by gravity is $W_P=mg(h_i-h_f)=U_i-U_f$. During the fall $h_i>h_f$, so $W_P$ is positive. At the same time $U_f<U_i$, that is, the potential energy decreases. Therefore, gravity does **positive work, equal to the decrease in potential energy**. The zero initial speed has no effect on the work done by gravity, which depends only on the change in height.
