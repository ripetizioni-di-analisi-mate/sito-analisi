A spring with $k=700$ N/m is stretched by $0.2$ m. What is its elastic energy?

- $14\,\mathrm{J}$
- $28\,\mathrm{J}$
- $140\,\mathrm{J}$
- $70\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The elastic energy is $U_e=\dfrac{1}{2}k(\Delta\ell)^2$. The square of $0.2$ is $0.04$. Therefore, $U_e=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot700\cdot0.04=14$ joules.
