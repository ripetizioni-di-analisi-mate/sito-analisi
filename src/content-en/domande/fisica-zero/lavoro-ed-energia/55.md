The spring bumper of a toy cart, treated as an ideal spring with constant $800\,\mathrm{N/m}$, stores $1\,\mathrm{J}$ of elastic energy during a collision with a wall. By how much is the spring compressed at that moment?

- $0.25\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $3.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.125\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

From $U_e=\dfrac{1}{2}kx^2$, multiplying by $2$ and dividing by $k$ gives
$$
x^2=\frac{2U_e}{k}=\frac{2\cdot1}{800}\,\mathrm{m^2}=0.0025\,\mathrm{m^2}.
$$
The compression is the positive square root, so $x=0.05\,\mathrm{m}=5\,\mathrm{cm}$. The value $0.25\,\mathrm{cm}$ corresponds to reading $x^2$ as if it were $x$.
