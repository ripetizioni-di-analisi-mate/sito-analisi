A spring is loaded and unloaded slowly over the same deformation range. Which observation indicates that some work was dissipated over the cycle?

- The initial and final spring energies are equal
- More work is supplied during loading than is returned during unloading
- The extension changes during the test
- The peak force is positive

## Explanation

The spring ends in its starting state and speeds are negligible. The difference between input and returned work measures the energy dissipated over the cycle.
