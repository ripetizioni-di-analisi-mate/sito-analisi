A machine receives $100\,\mathrm{J}$ in a first cycle at $90\%$ efficiency and $300\,\mathrm{J}$ in a second cycle at $50\%$. What is its combined efficiency?

- $70\%$
- $60\%$
- $80\%$
- $45\%$

## Explanation

The useful outputs are $90\,\mathrm{J}$ and $150\,\mathrm{J}$. With total input of $400\,\mathrm{J}$ the combined efficiency is $\dfrac{240}{400}=0.60$.
