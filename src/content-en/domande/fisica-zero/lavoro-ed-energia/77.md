A crane delivers $200\,\mathrm{W}$ for $3\,\mathrm{s}$ and then stands still for $2\,\mathrm{s}$ with zero useful power. What is its average useful power over the full five seconds?

- $200\,\mathrm{W}$
- $40\,\mathrm{W}$
- $100\,\mathrm{W}$
- $120\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

The total work is $600\,\mathrm{J}$. The observation interval includes the pause, so average power is $\dfrac{600}{5}=120$ watts.
