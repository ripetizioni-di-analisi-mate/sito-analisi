A $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ parcel rests on the floor of a train moving steadily at $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ relative to a station. What are its kinetic energies relative to the train and the station, respectively?

- $9\,\mathrm{J}$ and $9\,\mathrm{J}$
- $9\,\mathrm{J}$ and $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$ and $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$ and $9\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The parcel is at rest relative to the train. Its speed relative to the station is $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$, giving $\dfrac12\cdot2\cdot3^2=9$ joules.
