A bowling ball rolls down the lane with a kinetic energy of $30\,\mathrm{J}$. Along one stretch, the net work done by the forces on the ball is $-12\,\mathrm{J}$. What is the ball's kinetic energy at the end of the stretch?

- $42\,\mathrm{J}$
- $18\,\mathrm{J}$
- $30\,\mathrm{J}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The **work–energy theorem** gives $K_f-K_i=W_{\mathrm{tot}}$, so $K_f=K_i+W_{\mathrm{tot}}$. The net work enters with its own sign, so
$$
K_f=30\,\mathrm{J}+(-12\,\mathrm{J})=18\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
The value $42\,\mathrm{J}$ would come from ignoring the negative sign of the work.
