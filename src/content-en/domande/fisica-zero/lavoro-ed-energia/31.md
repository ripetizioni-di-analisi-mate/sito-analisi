A mass of $6$ kg is $3$ m above the chosen zero level. With $g=10$ meters per second squared, what is its gravitational potential energy?

- $18\,\mathrm{J}$
- $60\,\mathrm{J}$
- $180\,\mathrm{J}$
- $360\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Near Earth's surface we use $U=mgh$. The height is measured from the reference level. Therefore, $U=6\cdot10\cdot3=180$ joules.
