A machine delivers $900\,\mathrm{J}$ of useful energy at $60\%$ efficiency. Its stored energy is the same at the end of the cycle as at the start. How much energy went into other forms?

- $540\,\mathrm{J}$
- $360\,\mathrm{J}$
- $600\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1500\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The useful $900\,\mathrm{J}$ represent $60\%$ of the input, which is $\dfrac{900}{0.60}=1500$ joules. The remaining $600\,\mathrm{J}$ left in other forms.
