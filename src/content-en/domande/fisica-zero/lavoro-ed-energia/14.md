A constant force of $100\,\mathrm{N}$ acts on a crate in four different situations. In which situation does the force do the most work?

- Force opposite to the motion, displacement of $8\,\mathrm{m}$
- Force perpendicular to the motion, displacement of $10\,\mathrm{m}$
- Force in the direction of motion, displacement of $5\,\mathrm{m}$
- Force in the direction of motion, displacement of $4\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

We compute the work in the four cases. With the force in the direction of motion, $W=Fs$, which gives $500\,\mathrm{J}$ for $5\,\mathrm{m}$ and $400\,\mathrm{J}$ for $4\,\mathrm{m}$. With the force opposite to the motion, the work is $W=-Fs=-800\,\mathrm{J}$, while with the perpendicular force the work is zero. Therefore, the most work is **$500\,\mathrm{J}$**, with the force in the direction of motion over $5\,\mathrm{m}$.
