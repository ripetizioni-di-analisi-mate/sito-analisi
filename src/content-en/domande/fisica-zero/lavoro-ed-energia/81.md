A freight elevator lifts $200\,\mathrm{kg}$ of material at constant speed through $6\,\mathrm{m}$ in $12\,\mathrm{s}$. Neglecting friction and with $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what average mechanical power does the motor deliver?

- $100\,\mathrm{W}$
- $491\,\mathrm{W}$
- $981\,\mathrm{W}$
- $1962\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

At constant speed the kinetic energy does not change, so the work done by the motor equals the increase in potential energy of the load:
$$
W=mgh=200\cdot9.81\cdot6\,\mathrm{J}=11\,772\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
The average power is the work divided by the time taken, so
$$
P_m=\frac{11\,772\,\mathrm{J}}{12\,\mathrm{s}}=981\,\mathrm{W}.
$$
