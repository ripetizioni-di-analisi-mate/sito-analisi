A motor's cumulative work is 0, 4, 13 and 15 joules at times 0, 1, 2 and 3 seconds respectively. During which one-second interval is its mean power greatest?

- From 2 to 3 seconds
- It is the same in all three intervals
- From 0 to 1 second
- From 1 to 2 seconds

## Explanation

The successive work increments are 4, 9 and 2 joules. With equal durations the largest increment gives the greatest mean power.
