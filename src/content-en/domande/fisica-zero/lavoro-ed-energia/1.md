At the supermarket, a person pushes a shopping cart with a constant horizontal force of $25\,\mathrm{N}$, in the direction of motion, through $8\,\mathrm{m}$. How much work does the push do?

- $200\,\mathrm{J}$
- $3.1\,\mathrm{J}$
- $33\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-200\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The push is constant and points in the direction of the displacement, so its work is positive and equals $W=Fs$. We multiply the magnitude of the force by the distance traveled:
$$
W=25\,\mathrm{N}\cdot8\,\mathrm{m}=200\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}.
$$
Since $1\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}=1\,\mathrm{J}$, the push does **$200\,\mathrm{J}$** of work.
