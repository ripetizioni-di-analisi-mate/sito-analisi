A constant force of $3$ N acts in the direction of a displacement of $4$ m. How much work does it do?

- $12\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0.75\,\mathrm{J}$
- $7\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

When the force and displacement point in the same direction, the work is $W=F\Delta s$. Multiplying $3$ N by $4$ m therefore gives $W=12$ joules.
