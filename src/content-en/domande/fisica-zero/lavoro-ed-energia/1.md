A rope pulls a crate with a constant force of $15\,\mathrm{N}$. Its attachment point moves $6\,\mathrm{m}$ in the direction of the pull. How much work does the rope do?

- $-90\,\mathrm{J}$
- $21\,\mathrm{J}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{J}$
- $90\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Force and displacement point in the same direction, so the work is positive. Multiplying their magnitudes gives $15\cdot6=90$ joules.
