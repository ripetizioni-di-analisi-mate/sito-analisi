A $0.5\,\mathrm{kg}$ ball thrown from a low balcony starts at a height of $1\,\mathrm{m}$, rises to $4\,\mathrm{m}$, and then lands on a low wall at a height of $2\,\mathrm{m}$. With $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, how much work does gravity do over the whole path?

- $4.9\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-24.5\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-4.9\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-14.7\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The work done by gravity depends only on the initial and final heights, not on the path. The ball starts at $h_i=1\,\mathrm{m}$ and ends at $h_f=2\,\mathrm{m}$, so
$$
W_P=mg(h_i-h_f)=0.5\cdot9.81\cdot(1-2)\,\mathrm{J}\approx-4.9\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
The negative work during the $3\,\mathrm{m}$ rise and the positive work during the $2\,\mathrm{m}$ descent together give the same result.
