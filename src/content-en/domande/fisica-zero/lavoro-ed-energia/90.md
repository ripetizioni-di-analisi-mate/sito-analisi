A device must deliver $60\,\mathrm{J}$ of useful work in $2\,\mathrm{s}$. Which source meets both the energy and power requirements, assuming ideal conversion and power adjustable up to the stated maximum?

- $80\,\mathrm{J}$ available, $40\,\mathrm{W}$ maximum
- $50\,\mathrm{J}$ available, $25\,\mathrm{W}$ maximum
- $100\,\mathrm{J}$ available, $20\,\mathrm{W}$ maximum
- $40\,\mathrm{J}$ available, $80\,\mathrm{W}$ maximum

## Explanation

The source must store at least $60\,\mathrm{J}$ and be able to deliver at least $\dfrac{60}{2}=30$ watts. Only the $80\,\mathrm{J}$ supply with a $40\,\mathrm{W}$ limit meets both conditions.
