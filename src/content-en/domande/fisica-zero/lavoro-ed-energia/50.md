It takes $3\,\mathrm{J}$ to stretch an ideal spring slowly from zero to $2\,\mathrm{cm}$. How much additional work is required to go from $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ to $4\,\mathrm{cm}$?

- $9\,\mathrm{J}$
- $12\,\mathrm{J}$
- $6\,\mathrm{J}$
- $3\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Doubling the extension quadruples the total energy from $3\,\mathrm{J}$ to $12\,\mathrm{J}$. The increase after the first $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ is therefore $9\,\mathrm{J}$.
