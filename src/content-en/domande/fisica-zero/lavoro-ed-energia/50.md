When an object of weight $2\,\mathrm{N}$ is hung from the hook of a spring scale, its ideal spring stretches by $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the system stays at rest. What is the spring constant?

- $0.4\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $0.1\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $40\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $80\,\mathrm{N/m}$

## Explanation

The object is at rest, so the spring force balances the weight and equals $F_e=2\,\mathrm{N}$. For an ideal spring $F_e=kx$, with $x=0.05\,\mathrm{m}$. Therefore,
$$
k=\frac{F_e}{x}=\frac{2\,\mathrm{N}}{0.05\,\mathrm{m}}=40\,\mathrm{N/m}.
$$
The value $0.4\,\mathrm{N/m}$ would come from dividing by $5$ without converting the centimeters.
