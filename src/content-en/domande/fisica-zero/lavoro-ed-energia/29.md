A bicycle and rider now travel at half their previous speed. For the same mass, what fraction of their initial translational kinetic energy remains?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $2$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

For fixed mass, energy is proportional to speed squared. The factor is therefore $\left(\dfrac12\right)^2=\dfrac14$.
