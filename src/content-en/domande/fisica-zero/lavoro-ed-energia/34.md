A machine does $120$ J of work in $4$ s. What is its average power?

- $480\,\mathrm{W}$
- $30\,\mathrm{W}$
- $120\,\mathrm{W}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

Average power is work divided by time. Evaluating $P=\dfrac{120}{4}$ therefore gives an average power of $30$ watts.
