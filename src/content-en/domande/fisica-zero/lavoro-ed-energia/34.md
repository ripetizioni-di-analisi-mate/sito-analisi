When calculating kinetic energy at $18\,\mathrm{km/h}$, a student uses 18 for the speed in metres per second. By what factor is the energy overestimated?

- 3.6
- 36
- 1.8
- 12.96

## Explanation

The correct speed is $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The entered value is larger by a factor of 3.6, which is squared in the energy formula, giving $3.6^2=12.96$.
