At a water park, two slides start from the same platform and end in the same pool, with the same drop in height: one is steep and short, the other long and spiral-shaped. Friction and air resistance are negligible. Starting from rest, which slide gives the greater speed at the bottom?

- The steep slide
- The spiral slide, because the path is longer
- It cannot be determined without knowing the lengths of the slides
- Both give the same speed

## Explanation

Without friction, only gravity does work on the slides, because the normal force is perpendicular to the motion. So mechanical energy is conserved and $\dfrac{1}{2}mv^2=mgh$, where $h$ is the drop in height. The drop is the same for both slides, so the speed at the bottom is **the same**. The shape and length of the path change the time of descent, but not the final speed.
