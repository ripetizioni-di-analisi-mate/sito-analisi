A ball dropped from rest at $1\,\mathrm{m}$ rebounds to $0.64\,\mathrm{m}$. Both heights are measured from the centre's position at impact. Neglecting air resistance, what fraction of its initial mechanical energy is lost in the collision?

- $64\%$
- $36\%$
- $80\%$
- $20\%$

## Explanation

At each highest point the ball is at rest, so the energy ratio equals the height ratio, 0.64. The lost fraction is $1-0.64=0.36$.
