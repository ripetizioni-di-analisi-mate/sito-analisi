A trolley returns to the same height and speed after every circuit. The motor does $18\,\mathrm{J}$ of work per circuit and friction is the only loss. How much energy is dissipated over five circuits?

- $3.6\,\mathrm{J}$
- $18\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $90\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Mechanical energy returns to its initial value after every circuit, so all motor work offsets friction. Five circuits dissipate $5\cdot18=90$ joules.
