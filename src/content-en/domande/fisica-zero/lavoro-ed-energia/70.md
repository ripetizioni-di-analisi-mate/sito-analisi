A seagull of mass $0.3\,\mathrm{kg}$ flies at $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at a height of $20\,\mathrm{m}$ above the beach. With the zero level at the beach and $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is its mechanical energy?

- $73.9\,\mathrm{J}$
- $58.9\,\mathrm{J}$
- $15\,\mathrm{J}$
- $88.9\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The mechanical energy is the sum of kinetic and potential energy, $E=K+U$. The kinetic energy is $K=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot0.3\cdot10^2\,\mathrm{J}=15\,\mathrm{J}$, and the potential energy is $U=0.3\cdot9.81\cdot20\,\mathrm{J}=58.86\,\mathrm{J}$. Therefore,
$$
E=15\,\mathrm{J}+58.86\,\mathrm{J}\approx73.9\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
The value $88.9\,\mathrm{J}$ would come from computing the kinetic energy without the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
