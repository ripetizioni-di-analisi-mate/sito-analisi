A kitchen's electric oven has two heating elements, of $20\,\Omega$ and $30\,\Omega$, connected in parallel. What is the equivalent resistance?

- $50\,\Omega$
- $25\,\Omega$
- $10\,\Omega$
- $12\,\Omega$

## Explanation

For two resistors in parallel, $R_{\mathrm{eq}}=\dfrac{R_1R_2}{R_1+R_2}$. Therefore
$$
R_{\mathrm{eq}}=\frac{20\cdot30}{20+30}\,\Omega=\frac{600}{50}\,\Omega=12\,\Omega.
$$
The result is smaller than each of the two resistances, as is always the case for a parallel connection.
