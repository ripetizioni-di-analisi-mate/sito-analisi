A black box containing only resistors draws $0.5\,\mathrm{A}$ from an ideal $8\,\mathrm{V}$ source. A single equivalent resistor can replace it. Which information is certainly preserved by the replacement?

- The number of internal current paths.
- The number of internal nodes.
- The current drawn from the source at the same $8\,\mathrm{V}$.
- The separate power of each internal component.

## Explanation

Equivalence is defined by terminal behaviour. At $8\,\mathrm{V}$ the replacement reproduces the total $0.5\,\mathrm{A}$ even though its internal structure differs.
