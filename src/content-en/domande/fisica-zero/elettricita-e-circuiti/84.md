An ideal voltmeter measures the voltage between two nodes. Why does it need two connections even though it draws negligible current?

- The second lead must close the load current path in series.
- Voltage is a difference between the potentials of two points.
- The two leads must split the load current equally.
- The voltage is the internal drop along one lead and the second lead cancels it.

## Explanation

The reading compares one node’s potential with the other’s. The second lead provides the reference for that difference even when measurement current is zero.
