Two lamps, which can be treated as ohmic resistors of $500\,\Omega$ and $1000\,\Omega$, are connected in parallel to the same $230\,\mathrm{V}$ line. Which of the two absorbs more power?

- The $500\,\Omega$ lamp
- The $1000\,\Omega$ lamp
- They absorb the same power
- The one closer to the outlet

## Explanation

In parallel, the two lamps have the same voltage, $230\,\mathrm{V}$. At the same voltage, the power is $P_e=\dfrac{V^2}{R}$, so it is larger where the resistance is smaller. Therefore the $500\,\Omega$ lamp absorbs more power, about $106\,\mathrm{W}$, compared with about $53\,\mathrm{W}$ for the other.
