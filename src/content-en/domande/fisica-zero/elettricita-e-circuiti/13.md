An $18\,\mathrm{W}$ device runs for $20\,\mathrm{min}$. Which pair expresses its energy in joules and kilowatt-hours?

- $360\,\mathrm{J}$, $0.006\,\mathrm{kWh}$
- $21600\,\mathrm{J}$, $0.006\,\mathrm{kWh}$
- $21600\,\mathrm{J}$, $6\,\mathrm{kWh}$
- $360\,\mathrm{J}$, $0.36\,\mathrm{kWh}$

## Explanation

In $1200\,\mathrm{s}$ the energy is $18\cdot 1200=21600$ joules. Multiplying $0.018\,\mathrm{kW}$ by one third of an hour gives the same energy as $0.006\,\mathrm{kWh}$.
