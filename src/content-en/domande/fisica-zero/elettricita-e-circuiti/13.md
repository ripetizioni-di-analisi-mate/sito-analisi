A flashlight consists of a battery, a bulb, and a switch connected in a single path. With the flashlight on, the switch is opened. What happens to the current?

- It becomes zero only between the switch and the bulb
- It becomes zero throughout the circuit
- It is halved
- It stays the same, but the bulb does not use it

## Explanation

A steady current flows only if the conducting path is closed. The open switch breaks the only path in the flashlight, so the current **becomes zero at every point** of the circuit, even in the wires far from the switch. Therefore the bulb goes out.
