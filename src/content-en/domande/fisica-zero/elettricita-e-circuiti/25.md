Two identical resistors of $10$ ohms each are connected in parallel. What is their equivalent resistance?

- $5\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $20\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $10\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{\Omega}$

## Explanation

For two identical resistors in parallel, the current splits equally between the two branches. The equivalent resistance is half the resistance of each branch. Therefore $R_\mathrm{eq}=\dfrac{10}{2}=5$ ohms.
