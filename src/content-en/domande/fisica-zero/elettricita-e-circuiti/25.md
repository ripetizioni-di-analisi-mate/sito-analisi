During a DC charge, the cable of an electric car carries a constant current of $32\,\mathrm{A}$. Which expression gives the same value of the current?

- $32\,\mathrm{C/s}$
- $32\,\mathrm{C\cdot s}$
- $32\,\mathrm{J/C}$
- $32\,\mathrm{J/s}$

## Explanation

Current is the charge passing through a cross section divided by the time, so the ampere is equivalent to one coulomb per second, $1\,\mathrm{A}=1\,\mathrm{C/s}$. Therefore $32\,\mathrm{A}$ corresponds to $32\,\mathrm{C}$ passing through the cross section every second. One joule per coulomb is the volt, the unit of voltage, and one joule per second is the watt, the unit of power.
