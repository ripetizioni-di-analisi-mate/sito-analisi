In an electric scooter, a battery powers the motor and the headlight through cables. Which component acts as the source?

- The motor
- The connecting cables
- The battery
- The headlight

## Explanation

The source transfers energy to the charges, while a load receives electrical energy. The **battery** supplies energy to the charges moving around the circuit, so it is the source. The motor and the headlight receive electrical energy and are therefore loads, while the cables are conductors that connect the components.
