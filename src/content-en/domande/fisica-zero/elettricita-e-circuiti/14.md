A resistor carries $3$ A and has $12$ V across its terminals. What is its resistance?

- $36\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $4\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $12\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{\Omega}$

## Explanation

Rearranging Ohm's law gives $R=\dfrac{V}{I}$. Divide $12$ by $3$. The resistance is therefore $4$ ohms.
