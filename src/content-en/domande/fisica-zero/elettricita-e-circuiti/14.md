A $12\,\Omega$ ohmic resistor runs across $12\,\mathrm{V}$ for $2\,\mathrm{h}$. How much energy does it dissipate in kilowatt-hours if its resistance stays constant?

- $24\,\mathrm{kWh}$
- $0.288\,\mathrm{kWh}$
- $0.024\,\mathrm{kWh}$
- $0.006\,\mathrm{kWh}$

## Explanation

The power is $\dfrac{12^2}{12}=12$ watts. Two hours gives $24\,\mathrm{Wh}$, or $0.024\,\mathrm{kWh}$.
