A lamp rated at $6\,\mathrm{V}$ and $3\,\mathrm{W}$ can be modelled as a constant resistor under the stated conditions. What resistance must an equivalent load provide at the rated point?

- $18\,\Omega$
- $12\,\Omega$
- $2\,\Omega$
- $0.5\,\Omega$

## Explanation

From $P=\dfrac{V^2}{R}$ it follows that $R=\dfrac{6^2}{3}=12$ ohms. That load draws $3\,\mathrm{W}$ when supplied at $6\,\mathrm{V}$.
