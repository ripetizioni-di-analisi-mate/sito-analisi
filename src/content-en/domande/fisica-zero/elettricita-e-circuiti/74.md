When running, an electric tabletop grill connected to the $230\,\mathrm{V}$ line draws a current of $8\,\mathrm{A}$. What power does it receive?

- $28.75\,\mathrm{W}$
- $1840\,\mathrm{W}$
- $238\,\mathrm{W}$
- $184\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

The power absorbed by a load is the voltage across it multiplied by the current through it, $P_e=VI$. Therefore
$$
P_e=230\,\mathrm{V}\cdot8\,\mathrm{A}=1840\,\mathrm{W}.
$$
The grill thus receives $1840\,\mathrm{J}$ of energy every second.
