At a junction $0.8\,\mathrm{A}$ arrives through the supply wire and $1.1\,\mathrm{A}$ leaves through one branch. There is only one other branch and the currents are steady. What are the direction and magnitude of current in this other branch?

- No current flows in the second branch.
- Away from the junction, with magnitude $1.9\,\mathrm{A}$.
- Toward the junction, with magnitude $0.3\,\mathrm{A}$.
- Away from the junction, with magnitude $0.3\,\mathrm{A}$.

## Explanation

The known outgoing current exceeds the known incoming current by $0.3\,\mathrm{A}$. The second branch must therefore supply the difference to the junction.
