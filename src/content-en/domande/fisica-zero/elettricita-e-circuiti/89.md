A pair of earbuds charges at $5\,\mathrm{V}$ with a constant current of $200\,\mathrm{mA}$ for $1$ hour. How much energy does it receive, in watt-hours?

- $1000\,\mathrm{Wh}$
- $60\,\mathrm{Wh}$
- $0.04\,\mathrm{Wh}$
- $1\,\mathrm{Wh}$

## Explanation

In amperes, the current is $200\,\mathrm{mA}=0.2\,\mathrm{A}$, so the power is $P_e=VI=5\,\mathrm{V}\cdot0.2\,\mathrm{A}=1\,\mathrm{W}$. At constant power, the energy in one hour is therefore
$$
E=1\,\mathrm{W}\cdot1\,\mathrm{h}=1\,\mathrm{Wh}.
$$
