A $4\,\Omega$ resistor links the midpoints of two identical parallel branches, each formed by two equal series resistors. With an ideal battery, what current flows through the midpoint link?

- Half of each branch current.
- $0\,\mathrm{A}$
- The full battery current.
- A current determined only by the $4\,\Omega$ resistance.

## Explanation

By symmetry the two midpoints are at the same potential. The voltage across the link is therefore $0\,\mathrm{V}$, giving $0\,\mathrm{A}$ through its $4\,\Omega$ resistance.
