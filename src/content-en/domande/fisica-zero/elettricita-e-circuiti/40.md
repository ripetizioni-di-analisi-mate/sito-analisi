A car's rear window defroster, supplied with $12\,\mathrm{V}$, carries $15\,\mathrm{A}$. What is its resistance under these conditions?

- $180\,\Omega$
- $1.25\,\Omega$
- $3\,\Omega$
- $0.8\,\Omega$

## Explanation

The resistance of a component is the ratio of the voltage across it to the current through it. Therefore
$$
R=\frac{V}{I}=\frac{12\,\mathrm{V}}{15\,\mathrm{A}}=0.8\,\Omega.
$$
The resistance is less than one ohm; indeed, the current in amperes is larger than the voltage in volts.
