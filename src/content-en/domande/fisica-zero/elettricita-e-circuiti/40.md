A resistor of $7$ ohms carries a current of $2$ A. What is the voltage across its terminals?

- $3.5\,\mathrm{V}$
- $9\,\mathrm{V}$
- $28\,\mathrm{V}$
- $14\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

For an ohmic resistor, $V=RI$ holds. Multiply $7$ by $2$. The voltage is therefore $14$ volts.
