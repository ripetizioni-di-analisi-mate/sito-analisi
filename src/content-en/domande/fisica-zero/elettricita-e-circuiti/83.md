In which of these household appliances is the conversion of electrical energy to internal energy by Joule heating the main purpose of its operation?

- The fan
- The clothes iron
- The blender
- The vacuum cleaner

## Explanation

In Joule heating, the electrical energy received by a resistor is converted to internal energy, and the resistor heats up. In the **clothes iron**, this heating is the very purpose of the appliance, since the hot soleplate is what irons the clothes. In the fan, the blender, and the vacuum cleaner, the electrical energy mainly runs a motor, and the heating is a side effect.
