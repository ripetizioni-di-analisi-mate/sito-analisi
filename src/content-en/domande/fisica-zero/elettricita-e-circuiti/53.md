A bicycle bulb works properly with $6\,\mathrm{V}$ across it and $0.5\,\mathrm{A}$. It is to be powered by an ideal $9\,\mathrm{V}$ battery, with a resistor connected in series. What resistance must the resistor have?

- $18\,\Omega$
- $12\,\Omega$
- $6\,\Omega$
- $1.5\,\Omega$

## Explanation

In series the voltages add up, so the resistor must have $9\,\mathrm{V}-6\,\mathrm{V}=3\,\mathrm{V}$ across it. In series the current is the same, so the resistor also carries $0.5\,\mathrm{A}$. Therefore
$$
R=\frac{3\,\mathrm{V}}{0.5\,\mathrm{A}}=6\,\Omega.
$$
