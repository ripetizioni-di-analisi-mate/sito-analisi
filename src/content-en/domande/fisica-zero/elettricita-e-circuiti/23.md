A circuit with one ideal battery and one resistor has a break in its return wire. After transients die out, where can a steady current flow?

- Nowhere along the single path.
- Only inside the resistor.
- Only in the section before the break.
- Only in the section after the resistor.

## Explanation

The break removes the closed path. In steady state the current is consequently zero throughout the branch.
