A resistor carries $3$ A and has $15$ V across its terminals. What is its resistance?

- $45\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $15\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $5\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $0.2\,\mathrm{\Omega}$

## Explanation

Rearranging Ohm's law gives $R=\dfrac{V}{I}$. Divide $15$ by $3$. The resistance is therefore $5$ ohms.
