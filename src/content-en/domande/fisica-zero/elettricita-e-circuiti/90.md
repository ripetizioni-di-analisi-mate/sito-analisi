During a trial voltage and current change together. For half the time they are $2\,\mathrm{V}$ and $1\,\mathrm{A}$, and for the other half $6\,\mathrm{V}$ and $3\,\mathrm{A}$. What is the mean power over the trial?

- $4\,\mathrm{W}$
- $20\,\mathrm{W}$
- $10\,\mathrm{W}$
- $8\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

The two intervals have powers of $2\,\mathrm{W}$ and $18\,\mathrm{W}$. Equal durations give their mean of $10\,\mathrm{W}$. Multiplying the separately averaged voltage and current would instead give $8\,\mathrm{W}$.
