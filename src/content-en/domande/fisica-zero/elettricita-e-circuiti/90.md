A mug warmer contains two resistors connected in parallel, of $12\,\Omega$ and $6\,\Omega$, across which an ideal source maintains $12\,\mathrm{V}$. What total power do the two resistors absorb?

- $36\,\mathrm{W}$
- $8\,\mathrm{W}$
- $12\,\mathrm{W}$
- $24\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

In parallel, each resistor has $12\,\mathrm{V}$ across it. The powers absorbed are therefore
$$
\frac{(12\,\mathrm{V})^2}{12\,\Omega}=12\,\mathrm{W},\qquad \frac{(12\,\mathrm{V})^2}{6\,\Omega}=24\,\mathrm{W}.
$$
The total power is their sum, so it is $36\,\mathrm{W}$. The same value follows from the equivalent resistance, $4\,\Omega$, with $\dfrac{(12\,\mathrm{V})^2}{4\,\Omega}=36\,\mathrm{W}$.
