A resistor is mounted in parallel with another on a circuit board. The board is disconnected from the supply and the two branches are the only paths between the terminals. How can an ohmmeter measure the first resistor alone?

- Place an ideal wire across the second resistor during measurement.
- Disconnect one end of the second branch and measure across the first resistor.
- Leave both branches connected and reverse the leads to remove the second branch’s contribution.
- Leave both branches connected and measure across the second resistor.

## Explanation

An ohmmeter sends a small current between its terminals. With both branches connected it reads their parallel equivalent. Opening the second branch makes the test current pass through the first resistor alone.
