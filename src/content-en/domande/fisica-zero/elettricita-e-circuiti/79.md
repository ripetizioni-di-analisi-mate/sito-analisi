Every evening an electric toothbrush is used for $2$ minutes, absorbing a constant power of $2\,\mathrm{W}$. How many joules does it receive in one evening?

- $240\,\mathrm{J}$
- $4\,\mathrm{J}$
- $14\,400\,\mathrm{J}$
- $120\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The energy comes out in joules when the power is in watts and the time in seconds. The time of use is $2\cdot60\,\mathrm{s}=120\,\mathrm{s}$, so
$$
E=P_e\Delta t=2\,\mathrm{W}\cdot120\,\mathrm{s}=240\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
