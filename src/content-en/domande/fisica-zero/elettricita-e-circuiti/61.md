An ohmic resistor keeps its resistance while its current rises from $0.20\,\mathrm{A}$ to $0.60\,\mathrm{A}$. By what factor does its dissipated power increase?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $6$
- $3$
- $9$

## Explanation

A resistor’s power is $RI^2$. The current triples, so with unchanged resistance the power is multiplied by nine.
