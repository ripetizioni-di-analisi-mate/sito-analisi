On a boat, an ideal $12\,\mathrm{V}$ battery powers a navigation light of $6\,\Omega$ and a spotlight of $12\,\Omega$ in parallel, both ohmic. What total current leaves the battery?

- $\dfrac{2}{3}\,\mathrm{A}$
- $2\,\mathrm{A}$
- $1\,\mathrm{A}$
- $3\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

In parallel, each load has the battery voltage, $12\,\mathrm{V}$, across it. The branch currents are therefore $\dfrac{12\,\mathrm{V}}{6\,\Omega}=2\,\mathrm{A}$ and $\dfrac{12\,\mathrm{V}}{12\,\Omega}=1\,\mathrm{A}$. The total current is the sum of the branch currents, so it is $3\,\mathrm{A}$.
