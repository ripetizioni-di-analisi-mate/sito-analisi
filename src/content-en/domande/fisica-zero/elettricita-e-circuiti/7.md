A $300\,\Omega$ ohmic resistor at constant temperature is connected across $6\,\mathrm{V}$. What reading should an ammeter display in milliamperes?

- $20\,\mathrm{mA}$
- $1800\,\mathrm{mA}$
- $2\,\mathrm{mA}$
- $50\,\mathrm{mA}$

## Explanation

The current is $\dfrac{6}{300}=0.020$ amperes. Since one ampere is a thousand milliamperes, the reading is $20\,\mathrm{mA}$.
