A plastic ruler and a wool cloth, initially neutral, are rubbed together without touching any other object. The ruler acquires a negative charge. What can be said about the total charge of the ruler and the cloth after rubbing?

- It is negative, like the ruler's charge
- It is still zero
- It is positive, like the cloth's charge
- It has increased in absolute value

## Explanation

The ruler and the cloth exchange charge only with each other, so they form a system that cannot exchange charge with its surroundings. By **conservation of charge**, the total charge of the system keeps its initial value, which is zero. The ruler has a negative charge, so the cloth has a positive charge with the same absolute value.
