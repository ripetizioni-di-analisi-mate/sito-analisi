Two identical metal spheres on insulating supports touch and, by symmetry, share their total charge of $-6\,\mathrm{\mu C}$ equally. After separation, what charge does each have?

- $+3\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{\mu C}$

## Explanation

Total charge is conserved and symmetry requires equal shares. Each sphere therefore has half of $-6\,\mathrm{\mu C}$, namely $-3\,\mathrm{\mu C}$.
