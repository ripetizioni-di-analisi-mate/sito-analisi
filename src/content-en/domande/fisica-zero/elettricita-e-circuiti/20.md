A constant current of $5$ A flows for $4$ s. How much charge passes through a cross section of the wire?

- $1.25\,\mathrm{C}$
- $9\,\mathrm{C}$
- $0.8\,\mathrm{C}$
- $20\,\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

For a constant current, the charge is $Q=I\Delta t$. Multiply $5$ by $4$. A charge of $20$ coulombs therefore passes through the wire.
