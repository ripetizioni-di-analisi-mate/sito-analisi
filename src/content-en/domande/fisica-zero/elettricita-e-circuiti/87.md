In a room, a $2000\,\mathrm{W}$ space heater and a $300\,\mathrm{W}$ halogen lamp run at the same time, connected in parallel to the same $230\,\mathrm{V}$ line, which is treated as an ideal source. What current flows in the common cable that supplies them?

- $8.7\,\mathrm{A}$
- $10\,\mathrm{A}$
- $1.3\,\mathrm{A}$
- $20\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

The two appliances are in parallel, so they have the same voltage, and the current in the common cable is the sum of the branch currents. Hence the total power is also the sum of the powers, $2000\,\mathrm{W}+300\,\mathrm{W}=2300\,\mathrm{W}$. From $P_e=VI$ we get
$$
I=\frac{2300\,\mathrm{W}}{230\,\mathrm{V}}=10\,\mathrm{A}.
$$
