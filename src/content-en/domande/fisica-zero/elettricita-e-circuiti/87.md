A nominal $10\,\Omega$ resistor can differ from its rated value by $20\,\%$. Connected across an ideal $12\,\mathrm{V}$ source, which interval gives the full range of possible currents?

- From $0.8\,\mathrm{A}$ to $1.2\,\mathrm{A}$.
- From $8\,\mathrm{A}$ to $12\,\mathrm{A}$.
- From $1\,\mathrm{A}$ to $1.5\,\mathrm{A}$.
- From $1.2\,\mathrm{A}$ to $2.4\,\mathrm{A}$.

## Explanation

The resistance can range from $8\,\Omega$ to $12\,\Omega$. Since $I=\dfrac{12}{R}$, minimum current is $1\,\mathrm{A}$ and maximum current is $1.5\,\mathrm{A}$.
