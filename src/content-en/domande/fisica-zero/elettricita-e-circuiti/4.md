A resistor of $3$ ohms carries a current of $2$ A. What is the voltage across its terminals?

- $1.5\,\mathrm{V}$
- $5\,\mathrm{V}$
- $12\,\mathrm{V}$
- $6\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

For an ohmic resistor, $V=RI$ holds. Multiply $3$ by $2$. The voltage is therefore $6$ volts.
