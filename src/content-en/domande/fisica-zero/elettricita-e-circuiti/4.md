A steady current of $25\,\mathrm{mA}$ crosses a wire section for $2\,\mathrm{min}$. How much charge passes?

- $50\,\mathrm{C}$
- $0.05\,\mathrm{C}$
- $3000\,\mathrm{C}$
- $3\,\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

The current is $0.025\,\mathrm{A}$ and the time is $120\,\mathrm{s}$. The charge is their product, $0.025\cdot 120=3$ coulombs.
