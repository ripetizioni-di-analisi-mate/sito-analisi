The display of a kitchen scale draws a current of $0.045\,\mathrm{A}$. What is this current in milliamperes?

- $4.5\,\mathrm{mA}$
- $45\,\mathrm{mA}$
- $450\,\mathrm{mA}$
- $0.045\,\mathrm{mA}$

## Explanation

The prefix milli means one thousandth, so $1\,\mathrm{mA}=10^{-3}\,\mathrm{A}$ and one ampere contains $1000\,\mathrm{mA}$. To convert amperes to milliamperes, we therefore multiply by $1000$. This gives $0.045\,\mathrm{A}=0.045\cdot1000\,\mathrm{mA}=45\,\mathrm{mA}$.
