A constant current of $6$ A flows for $4$ s. How much charge passes through a cross section of the wire?

- $24\,\mathrm{C}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{C}$
- $10\,\mathrm{C}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}\,\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

For a constant current, the charge is $Q=I\Delta t$. Multiply $6$ by $4$. A charge of $24$ coulombs therefore passes through the wire.
