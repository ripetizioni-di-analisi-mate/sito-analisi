The resistor of a waffle iron has a resistance of $8\,\Omega$ under operating conditions. Which pair of measurements, voltage across it and current through it, is consistent with this value?

- $16\,\mathrm{V}$ and $2\,\mathrm{A}$
- $4\,\mathrm{V}$ and $2\,\mathrm{A}$
- $8\,\mathrm{V}$ and $8\,\mathrm{A}$
- $2\,\mathrm{V}$ and $16\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

Resistance is the ratio of the voltage across the resistor to the current through it, and $1\,\Omega=1\,\mathrm{V/A}$. For the pair $16\,\mathrm{V}$ and $2\,\mathrm{A}$, the ratio is $\dfrac{16\,\mathrm{V}}{2\,\mathrm{A}}=8\,\Omega$, so this pair is **consistent**. The other pairs give $2\,\Omega$, $1\,\Omega$, and $\dfrac{1}{8}\,\Omega$, so they do not correspond to a resistance of $8\,\Omega$.
