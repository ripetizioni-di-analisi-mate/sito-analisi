Two ohmic resistors in series have voltage drops of $8\,\mathrm{V}$ and $2\,\mathrm{V}$ respectively. Without knowing the current, what ratio $\dfrac{P_1}{P_2}$ of dissipated powers follows?

- $16$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $4$
- $2$

## Explanation

Both carry the same current. In the ratio of powers $VI$ the current cancels, leaving the voltage ratio of four.
