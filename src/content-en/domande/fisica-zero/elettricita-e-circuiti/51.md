In a circuit, two bulbs A and B are connected in series to a battery, and bulb A glows more brightly than B. How do the currents through the two bulbs compare?

- They are equal
- Larger in bulb A
- Larger in bulb B
- Zero in bulb B

## Explanation

Two components in series carry the same current, because there are no branches between them and, in the steady state, charge does not accumulate. The currents in A and B are therefore **equal**. The difference in brightness depends on the voltage across each bulb, and hence on the power each one absorbs.
