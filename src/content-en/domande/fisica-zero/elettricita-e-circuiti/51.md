Two resistive lamps in parallel are powered by an ideal voltage source. One filament breaks. In steady state, how does the other lamp’s brightness change if it depends on the power drawn?

- It doubles because it takes both previous currents.
- It becomes zero because both paths are broken.
- It always halves.
- It stays the same.

## Explanation

The intact lamp remains directly across the same voltage. With unchanged resistance, it therefore draws the same power.
