The battery of an electric bicycle stores $0.5\,\mathrm{kWh}$ of energy. What is this energy in joules?

- $500\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1\,800\,000\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1800\,\mathrm{J}$
- $30\,000\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

One kilowatt-hour corresponds to $3\,600\,000\,\mathrm{J}$, because $1\,\mathrm{kW}=1000\,\mathrm{W}$ and $1\,\mathrm{h}=3600\,\mathrm{s}$. Therefore
$$
0.5\,\mathrm{kWh}=0.5\cdot3\,600\,000\,\mathrm{J}=1\,800\,000\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
