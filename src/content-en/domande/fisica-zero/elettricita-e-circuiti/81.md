An ideal battery powers two identical parallel loads. In a redrawing, symbols are moved and wires lengthened while all node connections stay the same. Which electrical measurement distinguishes the drawings in the ideal-wire model?

- No current or voltage measurement on the components.
- The voltage of the branch drawn higher up.
- Power rises in proportion to the length on paper.
- The current of the branch drawn longer.

## Explanation

Connections and component parameters determine the ideal circuit. Redrawing preserves the nodes and hence all corresponding electrical measurements.
