A fixed resistor is tested at $4\,\mathrm{V}$ and at $8\,\mathrm{V}$. To dissipate the same energy, how long should the $8\,\mathrm{V}$ trial last compared with the $4\,\mathrm{V}$ trial?

- One quarter as long.
- The same time.
- Twice as long.
- Half as long.

## Explanation

Doubling the voltage quadruples $\dfrac{V^2}{R}$. Keeping power times time constant therefore requires one quarter of the time.
