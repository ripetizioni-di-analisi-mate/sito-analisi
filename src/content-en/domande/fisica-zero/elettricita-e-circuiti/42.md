A lamp filament’s resistance rises as it heats up. Starting with a cold lamp, a student wants to measure its room-temperature resistance. Which procedure minimises error from self-heating?

- Apply a small current and promptly measure both voltage and current.
- Average several readings after prolonged operation at full power.
- Apply the largest available voltage to obtain a larger signal.
- Supply rated power and wait until the lamp glows steadily.

## Explanation

A small current dissipates little power in the filament. A prompt reading also limits energy deposited before measurement. The measured voltage divided by the measured current therefore approximates the cold resistance.
