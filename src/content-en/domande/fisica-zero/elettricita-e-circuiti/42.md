On the resistor of a model railway, these pairs of values are measured: $3\,\mathrm{V}$ with $0.25\,\mathrm{A}$, $6\,\mathrm{V}$ with $0.50\,\mathrm{A}$, $9\,\mathrm{V}$ with $0.75\,\mathrm{A}$. What is its resistance?

- $\dfrac{1}{12}\,\Omega$
- $24\,\Omega$
- $12\,\Omega$
- $36\,\Omega$

## Explanation

For the three pairs, the ratio of voltage to current is
$$
\frac{3}{0.25}=12,\qquad \frac{6}{0.50}=12,\qquad \frac{9}{0.75}=12.
$$
The ratio is the same for all the measurements, so the resistor is ohmic over this range. Therefore its resistance is $12\,\Omega$.
