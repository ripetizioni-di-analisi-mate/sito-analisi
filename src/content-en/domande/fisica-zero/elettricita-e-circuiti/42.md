Two resistors of $7$ ohms and $14$ ohms are connected in series. What is their equivalent resistance?

- $\dfrac{14}{3}\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $21\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $98\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $7\,\mathrm{\Omega}$

## Explanation

Resistors in series carry the same current. Their voltages add, so their resistances also add. This gives $R_\mathrm{eq}=7+14=21$ ohms.
