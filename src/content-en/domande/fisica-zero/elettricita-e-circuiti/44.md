A device is supplied with $70$ V and carries $2$ A. What is the electrical power?

- $35\,\mathrm{W}$
- $70\,\mathrm{W}$
- $280\,\mathrm{W}$
- $140\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

Electrical power is $P=VI$. Multiply the voltage by the current. The device therefore absorbs $140$ watts.
