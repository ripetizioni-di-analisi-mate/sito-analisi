An ohmic resistor in an alarm clock circuit has $3\,\mathrm{V}$ across it when it carries $0.1\,\mathrm{A}$. At the same temperature, what voltage is needed for it to carry $0.25\,\mathrm{A}$?

- $1.2\,\mathrm{V}$
- $0.75\,\mathrm{V}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{V}$
- $30\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

From the first measurement, the resistance is $R=\dfrac{3\,\mathrm{V}}{0.1\,\mathrm{A}}=30\,\Omega$. The resistor is ohmic and the temperature is the same, so the resistance remains $30\,\Omega$. Therefore
$$
V=RI=30\,\Omega\cdot0.25\,\mathrm{A}=7.5\,\mathrm{V}.
$$
