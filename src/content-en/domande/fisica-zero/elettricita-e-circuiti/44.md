In a series circuit supplied at $15\,\mathrm{V}$, the first two resistors have drops of $4\,\mathrm{V}$ and $6\,\mathrm{V}$. There is only one other resistor and the wires are ideal. What is its voltage drop?

- $5\,\mathrm{V}$
- $15\,\mathrm{V}$
- $10\,\mathrm{V}$
- $25\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

The three drops must add to $15\,\mathrm{V}$. This leaves $15-4-6=5$ volts for the last resistor.
