Resistors of $2\,\Omega$, $3\,\Omega$ and $6\,\Omega$ are available. Exactly two are to be used in parallel to obtain $2\,\Omega$. Which pair works?

- $3\,\Omega$ and $6\,\Omega$
- No pair.
- $2\,\Omega$ and $3\,\Omega$
- $2\,\Omega$ and $6\,\Omega$

## Explanation

A parallel pair containing a $2\,\Omega$ branch would give less than $2\,\Omega$. For $3\,\Omega$ and $6\,\Omega$, $\dfrac{1}{3}+\dfrac{1}{6}=\dfrac{1}{2}$, giving the required equivalent.
