An air conditioner absorbs a constant power of $1.5\,\mathrm{kW}$ and runs for $4$ hours. How much energy does it absorb, in kilowatt-hours?

- $6\,\mathrm{kWh}$
- $0.375\,\mathrm{kWh}$
- $6000\,\mathrm{kWh}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{kWh}$

## Explanation

At constant power, the energy is $E=P_e\Delta t$. If the power is in kilowatts and the time in hours, the energy is in kilowatt-hours. Therefore
$$
E=1.5\,\mathrm{kW}\cdot4\,\mathrm{h}=6\,\mathrm{kWh}.
$$
