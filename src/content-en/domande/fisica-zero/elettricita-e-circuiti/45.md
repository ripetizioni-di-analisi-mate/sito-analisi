A device draws a constant power of $7$ W for $20$ s. How much energy does it absorb?

- $140\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0.35\,\mathrm{J}$
- $27\,\mathrm{J}$
- $\dfrac{20}{7}\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

At constant power, the energy is $E=P\Delta t$. Multiply $7$ by $20$. The energy absorbed is therefore $140$ joules.
