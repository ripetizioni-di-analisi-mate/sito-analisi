A potentiometer has a uniform straight resistive track of $100\,\Omega$ between ends A and B. The wiper C is one quarter of the way from A to B. With the potentiometer disconnected and negligible contact resistance, which terminal pair provides $75\,\Omega$?

- No pair, because the wiper does not change the total resistance of the track.
- A and B.
- C and B.
- A and C.

## Explanation

Track resistance is proportional to length because the track is uniform. Three quarters of the track, or $75\,\Omega$, remain from C to B. A to C gives $25\,\Omega$ and A to B gives the full $100\,\Omega$.
