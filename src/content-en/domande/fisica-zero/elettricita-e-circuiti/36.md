Two different bulbs are connected, one at a time, to the same ideal $4.5\,\mathrm{V}$ battery with ideal wires. Which quantity has the same value in both cases?

- The current in the bulb
- The power absorbed by the bulb
- The energy received by the bulb in one minute
- The voltage across the bulb

## Explanation

The battery is ideal, so it maintains $4.5\,\mathrm{V}$ across its terminals whichever bulb is connected. With ideal wires, the voltage across the bulb equals the voltage across the terminals, so **the voltage** is $4.5\,\mathrm{V}$ in both cases. The two bulbs have different resistances, so the current, the power, and the energy received in one minute differ from one bulb to the other.
