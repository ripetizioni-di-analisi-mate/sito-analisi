An $8\,\Omega$ ohmic resistor is to carry $0.25\,\mathrm{A}$. What voltage is required across it?

- $0.03125\,\mathrm{V}$
- $8.25\,\mathrm{V}$
- $32\,\mathrm{V}$
- $2\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

The required voltage is the product $RI$. Multiplying $8\,\Omega$ by $0.25\,\mathrm{A}$ gives $2\,\mathrm{V}$.
