A device draws a constant power of $6$ W for $20$ s. How much energy does it absorb?

- $0.3\,\mathrm{J}$
- $26\,\mathrm{J}$
- $\dfrac{10}{3}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $120\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

At constant power, the energy is $E=P\Delta t$. Multiply $6$ by $20$. The energy absorbed is therefore $120$ joules.
