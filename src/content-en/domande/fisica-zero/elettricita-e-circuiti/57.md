A shop's illuminated sign consists of $20$ identical bulbs connected in series. The sign is supplied with $230\,\mathrm{V}$ and carries $0.25\,\mathrm{A}$. What is the resistance of each bulb under these conditions?

- $920\,\Omega$
- $46\,\Omega$
- $11.5\,\Omega$
- $18\,400\,\Omega$

## Explanation

The equivalent resistance of the sign is $R_{\mathrm{eq}}=\dfrac{230\,\mathrm{V}}{0.25\,\mathrm{A}}=920\,\Omega$. In series, the equivalent resistance is the sum of the resistances, and the $20$ bulbs are identical. Therefore each one has resistance
$$
R=\frac{920\,\Omega}{20}=46\,\Omega.
$$
