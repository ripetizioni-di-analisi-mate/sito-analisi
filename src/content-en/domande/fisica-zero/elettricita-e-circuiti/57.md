Four identical $8\,\Omega$ resistors form two parallel branches, each containing two resistors in series. What is the total equivalent resistance?

- $32\,\Omega$
- $2\,\Omega$
- $16\,\Omega$
- $8\,\Omega$

## Explanation

Each branch has a resistance of $16\,\Omega$. Two equal branches in parallel halve that value, giving an equivalent of $8\,\Omega$.
