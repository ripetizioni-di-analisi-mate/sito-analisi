A constant current of $3$ A flows for $4$ s. How much charge passes through a cross section of the wire?

- $0.75\,\mathrm{C}$
- $12\,\mathrm{C}$
- $7\,\mathrm{C}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

For a constant current, the charge is $Q=I\Delta t$. Multiply $3$ by $4$. A charge of $12$ coulombs therefore passes through the wire.
