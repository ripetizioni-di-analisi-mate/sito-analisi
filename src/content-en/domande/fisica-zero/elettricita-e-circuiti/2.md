Two initially neutral spheres are isolated from all other objects. After an exchange the first measures $-7\,\mathrm{\mu C}$. What is the final charge of the second?

- $+14\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $0\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $+7\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $-7\,\mathrm{\mu C}$

## Explanation

Their initial total charge is zero and is conserved. The second sphere must therefore balance the first sphere’s $-7\,\mathrm{\mu C}$ with $+7\,\mathrm{\mu C}$.
