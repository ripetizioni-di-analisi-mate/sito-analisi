Two balloons are rubbed in the same way on the same wool sweater and acquire charges of the same sign. When they hang from two threads close to each other, what do they do?

- They attract each other
- They repel each other
- They stay still because they have the same charge
- They attract each other only after touching

## Explanation

The two balloons have charges of the same sign. Two bodies with charges of the same sign repel each other, so the balloons **repel each other** and move apart. Attraction occurs between bodies with charges of opposite signs.
