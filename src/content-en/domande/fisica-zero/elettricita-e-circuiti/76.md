The wires in the power cable of a welding machine have a total resistance of $0.1\,\Omega$ and carry $10\,\mathrm{A}$. What power is converted to internal energy in the wires of the cable?

- $1\,\mathrm{W}$
- $100\,\mathrm{W}$
- $20\,\mathrm{W}$
- $10\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

For an ohmic conductor, the absorbed power can be written as $P_e=RI^2$. Therefore
$$
P_e=0.1\,\Omega\cdot(10\,\mathrm{A})^2=0.1\cdot100\,\mathrm{W}=10\,\mathrm{W}.
$$
This power is converted to internal energy of the wires by **Joule heating**, which is why a cable carrying a large current gets warm.
