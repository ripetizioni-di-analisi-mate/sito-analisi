An ideal source maintains $12\,\mathrm{V}$. Its total current rises from $0.6\,\mathrm{A}$ to $0.9\,\mathrm{A}$ when one resistor is added in parallel across the entire network. What is the added resistance?

- $40\,\Omega$
- $13.3\,\Omega$
- $4\,\Omega$
- $20\,\Omega$

## Explanation

The existing branches keep the same voltage, so the new branch alone draws the extra $0.3\,\mathrm{A}$. Its resistance is $\dfrac{12}{0.3}=40$ ohms.
