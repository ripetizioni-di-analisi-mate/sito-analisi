Two identical $10\,\Omega$ resistors are available for building a small terrarium heater. Which way of connecting the two resistors gives the smaller equivalent resistance?

- In series
- Both connections give the same value
- In parallel
- It depends on the voltage of the source

## Explanation

In series the resistances add up, giving $20\,\Omega$. In parallel, however, $R_{\mathrm{eq}}=\dfrac{10\cdot10}{10+10}\,\Omega=5\,\Omega$. Therefore the smaller equivalent resistance is obtained by connecting the resistors **in parallel**. The resistances are constant, so the comparison holds for any source voltage.
