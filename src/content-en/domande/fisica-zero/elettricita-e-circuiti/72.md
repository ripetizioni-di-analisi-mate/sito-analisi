A device converts $25\,\%$ of its $8\,\mathrm{W}$ electrical input to light and the remainder to heat. How much thermal energy does it release in $10\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $6\,\mathrm{J}$
- $20\,\mathrm{J}$
- $60\,\mathrm{J}$
- $80\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The thermal share is $75\,\%$ of $8\,\mathrm{W}$, or $6\,\mathrm{W}$. Sustained for $10\,\mathrm{s}$, that power releases $60\,\mathrm{J}$ of thermal energy.
