At a steady-state junction $1.2\,\mathrm{A}$ arrives from the left and $0.3\,\mathrm{A}$ from below. A current of $0.4\,\mathrm{A}$ leaves to the right. What current must leave through the fourth wire?

- $1.9\,\mathrm{A}$
- $1.1\,\mathrm{A}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{A}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

The incoming currents total $1.5\,\mathrm{A}$. For outgoing currents to match, the fourth wire must carry $1.5-0.4=1.1$ amperes.
