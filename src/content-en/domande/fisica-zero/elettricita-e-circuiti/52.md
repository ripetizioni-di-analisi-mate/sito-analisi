A small decorative light consists of two resistors in series powered by an ideal battery. A third resistor is added in series, with the same battery. What happens to the current in the circuit?

- It decreases
- It increases
- It stays the same
- It becomes zero

## Explanation

In a series connection, the equivalent resistance is the sum of the resistances, so adding the third resistor increases $R_{\mathrm{eq}}$. The ideal battery maintains the same voltage, and the current is $I=\dfrac{V}{R_{\mathrm{eq}}}$. Therefore the current **decreases**. The path remains closed, so the current remains nonzero.
