On an apartment's electricity bill, the consumption for one month is given as $250\,\mathrm{kWh}$. Which quantity does this value express?

- The average power absorbed during the month
- The current drawn during the month
- The energy absorbed during the month
- The voltage of the line

## Explanation

The kilowatt-hour is the energy corresponding to a power of one kilowatt maintained for one hour. It is therefore a unit of energy, because it also includes the operating time. Hence $250\,\mathrm{kWh}$ indicates **the energy absorbed** by the appliances in the apartment during the month. Power is measured instead in watts or kilowatts, current in amperes, and voltage in volts.
