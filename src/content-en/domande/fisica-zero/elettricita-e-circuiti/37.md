A charge of $42$ C passes through a cross section of a wire in $3$ s. What is the average current?

- $14\,\mathrm{A}$
- $126\,\mathrm{A}$
- $42\,\mathrm{A}$
- $\dfrac{1}{14}\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

The average current is the charge transferred divided by the time. This gives $I=\dfrac{42}{3}$. The current is therefore $14$ amperes.
