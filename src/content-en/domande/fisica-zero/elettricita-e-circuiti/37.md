A component at controlled temperature draws $1\,\mathrm{A}$ at $3\,\mathrm{V}$ and $1.5\,\mathrm{A}$ at $6\,\mathrm{V}$. Which conclusion follows from the data?

- Voltage doubles, so the current also doubled.
- Both points have a constant resistance of $4\,\Omega$.
- The voltage-to-current ratio changes from $3\,\Omega$ to $4\,\Omega$.
- Both points have a constant resistance of $3\,\Omega$.

## Explanation

The ratios are $\dfrac{3}{1}=3$ and $\dfrac{6}{1.5}=4$ ohms. The points rule out one constant resistance over this range.
