A record gives current $I=5$ without a unit. If the number were in milliamperes rather than amperes, how would that change the energy found from $E=VI\Delta  t$ at fixed voltage and time?

- It would be one millionth as large.
- It would stay the same.
- It would be one thousandth as large.
- It would be a thousand times larger.

## Explanation

Five milliamperes is $0.005\,\mathrm{A}$. Energy in the stated formula is proportional to current, so it too falls to one thousandth.
