The ohmic resistor of a foot warmer absorbs $10\,\mathrm{W}$ when it has $6\,\mathrm{V}$ across it. What power does it absorb with $12\,\mathrm{V}$ across it, if the resistance stays constant?

- $40\,\mathrm{W}$
- $20\,\mathrm{W}$
- $10\,\mathrm{W}$
- $5\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

For an ohmic resistor, $P_e=\dfrac{V^2}{R}$ with $R$ constant. Doubling the voltage makes $V^2$ four times larger, so the power also quadruples and becomes $4\cdot10\,\mathrm{W}=40\,\mathrm{W}$. The same value follows from finding the resistance, $R=\dfrac{(6\,\mathrm{V})^2}{10\,\mathrm{W}}=3.6\,\Omega$, and then
$$
P_e=\frac{(12\,\mathrm{V})^2}{3.6\,\Omega}=40\,\mathrm{W}.
$$
