An ideal $1.5\,\mathrm{V}$ battery has just been taken out of a remote control and is not connected to anything. What are the voltage across its terminals and the current through it?

- $0\,\mathrm{V}$ and $0\,\mathrm{A}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{V}$ and $0\,\mathrm{A}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{V}$ and $1.5\,\mathrm{A}$
- $0\,\mathrm{V}$ and $1.5\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

An ideal battery maintains its stated voltage across its terminals even when the circuit is open, so the voltage is $1.5\,\mathrm{V}$. Without a closed path there is no steady current, so the current is zero. Voltage and current indeed describe two distinct aspects of a circuit.
