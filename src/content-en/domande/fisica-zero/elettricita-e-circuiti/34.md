Two identical resistors of $12$ ohms each are connected in parallel. What is their equivalent resistance?

- $24\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $6\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $12\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $3\,\mathrm{\Omega}$

## Explanation

For two identical resistors in parallel, the current splits equally between the two branches. The equivalent resistance is half the resistance of each branch. Therefore $R_\mathrm{eq}=\dfrac{12}{2}=6$ ohms.
