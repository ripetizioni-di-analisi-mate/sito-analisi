The potentials of nodes A and B relative to the same reference are $+7\,\mathrm{V}$ and $-2\,\mathrm{V}$. What is $V_A-V_B$?

- $+9\,\mathrm{V}$
- $-9\,\mathrm{V}$
- $-5\,\mathrm{V}$
- $+5\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

Subtract B’s potential from A’s. The difference is $7-(-2)=9$ volts.
