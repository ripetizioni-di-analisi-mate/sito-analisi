A $12\,\mathrm{V}$ supply is rated for a maximum total current of $1\,\mathrm{A}$. Two parallel devices already draw $0.35\,\mathrm{A}$ and $0.40\,\mathrm{A}$. What maximum power remains for a third device at the same $12\,\mathrm{V}$?

- $9\,\mathrm{W}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{W}$
- $12\,\mathrm{W}$
- $3\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

The remaining current is $1-0.35-0.40=0.25$ amperes. At $12\,\mathrm{V}$ this corresponds to $12\cdot 0.25=3$ watts.
