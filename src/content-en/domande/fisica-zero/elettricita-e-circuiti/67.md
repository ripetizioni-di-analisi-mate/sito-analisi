For a food warmer, an equivalent resistance of $4\,\Omega$ is wanted, obtained by connecting a $12\,\Omega$ resistor and a second resistor in parallel. What resistance must the second resistor have?

- $8\,\Omega$
- $16\,\Omega$
- $6\,\Omega$
- $3\,\Omega$

## Explanation

In parallel, $\dfrac{1}{R_{\mathrm{eq}}}=\dfrac{1}{R_1}+\dfrac{1}{R_2}$. Therefore
$$
\frac{1}{R_2}=\frac{1}{4\,\Omega}-\frac{1}{12\,\Omega}=\frac{3-1}{12\,\Omega}=\frac{1}{6\,\Omega}.
$$
So the second resistor must have $R_2=6\,\Omega$.
