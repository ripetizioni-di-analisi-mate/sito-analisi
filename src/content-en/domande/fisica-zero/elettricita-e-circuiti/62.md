Two ohmic elements of $3\,\Omega$ and $6\,\Omega$ are compared in two trials. Both have the same current in the first trial and the same voltage in the second. Which element dissipates more power in each trial?

- The powers are equal in both trials.
- First the $6\,\Omega$ element, then the $3\,\Omega$ element.
- Always the $6\,\Omega$ element.
- Always the $3\,\Omega$ element.

## Explanation

At fixed current $P=I^2R$ increases with resistance. At fixed voltage $P=\dfrac{V^2}{R}$ decreases as resistance increases.
