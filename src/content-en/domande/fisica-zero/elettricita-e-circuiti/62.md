In a kitchen, the refrigerator and a lamp are connected in parallel to the same $230\,\mathrm{V}$ line, which is treated as an ideal source. While the refrigerator is running, what is the voltage across the lamp?

- $230\,\mathrm{V}$
- $115\,\mathrm{V}$
- $460\,\mathrm{V}$
- A value below $230\,\mathrm{V}$ that depends on the refrigerator

## Explanation

Two loads in parallel have their ends connected to the same pair of points, so they have the same voltage across them. In the model, the line is treated as an ideal source that maintains $230\,\mathrm{V}$ across its terminals. Therefore the voltage across the lamp is $230\,\mathrm{V}$, however the refrigerator is operating.
