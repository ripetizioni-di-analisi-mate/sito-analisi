A device is supplied with $40$ V and carries $2$ A. What is the electrical power?

- $80\,\mathrm{W}$
- $20\,\mathrm{W}$
- $40\,\mathrm{W}$
- $160\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

Electrical power is $P=VI$. Multiply the voltage by the current. The device therefore absorbs $80$ watts.
