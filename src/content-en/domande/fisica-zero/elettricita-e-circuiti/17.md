An atom contains seven protons and seven electrons. Which description explains why it is neutral, regardless of the number of neutrons?

- Each proton carries two negative charges.
- Its positive and negative charges cancel.
- It contains no charged particles.
- The neutrons cancel the charge of each electron.

## Explanation

Each proton and electron has a charge of equal magnitude and opposite sign. Equal numbers of each give a total of zero.
