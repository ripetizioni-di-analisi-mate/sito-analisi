A travel bottle warmer is connected to a car battery, which is treated as an ideal constant-voltage source. Its resistance is constant, and it has been on for a few minutes. What shape does the graph of the current $I$ as a function of time $t$ have over this interval?

- A straight line through the origin
- A horizontal segment
- A segment sloping downward
- A curve that rises more and more rapidly

## Explanation

The voltage and the resistance are constant, so the current $I=\dfrac{V}{R}$ has the same value at every instant. The bottle warmer is therefore in a steady state, and the graph of $I$ as a function of $t$ is a **horizontal segment**. A straight line through the origin describes instead the charge $q$ that has passed, which at constant current grows in proportion to time.
