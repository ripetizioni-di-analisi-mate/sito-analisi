Three resistors of $2\,\Omega$, $3\,\Omega$ and $7\,\Omega$ form a single path between A and B with no branches. What equivalent resistance is seen from A and B?

- $7\,\Omega$
- $12\,\Omega$
- $4\,\Omega$
- $42\,\Omega$

## Explanation

The same current passes through all three resistors. The voltage drops add, so the resistances add as well, giving $12\,\Omega$.
