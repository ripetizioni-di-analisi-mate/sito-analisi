Two identical resistors of $14$ ohms each are connected in parallel. What is their equivalent resistance?

- $28\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $14\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $7\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $3.5\,\mathrm{\Omega}$

## Explanation

For two identical resistors in parallel, the current splits equally between the two branches. The equivalent resistance is half the resistance of each branch. Therefore $R_\mathrm{eq}=\dfrac{14}{2}=7$ ohms.
