Particle A repels particle B. B attracts C. All three have nonzero net charge and interact only electrostatically as point charges. How do A and C interact?

- They exert no electric force.
- They repel.
- They attract only if they have equal mass.
- They attract.

## Explanation

Repulsion means A and B have the same sign. C has the opposite sign to B and hence to A, so A and C attract.
