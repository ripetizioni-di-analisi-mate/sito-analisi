Two identical resistors of $8$ ohms each are connected in parallel. What is their equivalent resistance?

- $16\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $8\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $2\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $4\,\mathrm{\Omega}$

## Explanation

For two identical resistors in parallel, the current splits equally between the two branches. The equivalent resistance is half the resistance of each branch. Therefore $R_\mathrm{eq}=\dfrac{8}{2}=4$ ohms.
