The wires of a home's electrical system are made of copper. Which charged particles move along the wires when there is a current in the system?

- Protons
- Whole copper atoms
- Electrons
- The charges in the plastic sheath

## Explanation

A conductor contains charges that can move through the material. In metals such as copper, these charges are **electrons**. The current in the wires is therefore due to the directed motion of electrons, while the plastic sheath covering them is an insulator.
