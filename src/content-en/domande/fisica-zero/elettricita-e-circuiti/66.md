A device uses $60\,\mathrm{W}$ for $10\,\mathrm{min}$ and then remains on standby at $3\,\mathrm{W}$ for $50\,\mathrm{min}$. What is its average power over the full hour?

- $31.5\,\mathrm{W}$
- $10\,\mathrm{W}$
- $63\,\mathrm{W}$
- $12.5\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

The first interval contributes $10\,\mathrm{Wh}$ and the second $2.5\,\mathrm{Wh}$. Dividing the total $12.5\,\mathrm{Wh}$ by one hour gives a mean power of $12.5\,\mathrm{W}$.
