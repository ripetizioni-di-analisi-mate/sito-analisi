An electric hot plate and a toaster are connected in parallel to the same line. The common cable carries $13\,\mathrm{A}$, and the hot plate carries $8.7\,\mathrm{A}$. What current passes through the toaster?

- $21.7\,\mathrm{A}$
- $4.3\,\mathrm{A}$
- $6.5\,\mathrm{A}$
- $13\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

Charge does not accumulate at the junction, so the current in the common cable is the sum of the currents in the two branches, $I=I_1+I_2$. Therefore the current in the toaster is
$$
I_2=13\,\mathrm{A}-8.7\,\mathrm{A}=4.3\,\mathrm{A}.
$$
