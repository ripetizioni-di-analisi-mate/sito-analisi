A label gives only $0.6\,\mathrm{kWh}$. What information is missing to find the average power drawn during use?

- The maximum current that the terminals can carry.
- The price of energy per kilowatt-hour.
- Only the rated voltage of the device.
- The duration of the operating interval.

## Explanation

A kilowatt-hour measures energy. Dividing energy in kilowatt-hours by duration in hours gives average power in kilowatts.
