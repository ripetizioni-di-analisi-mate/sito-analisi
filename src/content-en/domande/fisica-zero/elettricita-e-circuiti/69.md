## Explanation

The two $6\,\Omega$ resistors are in parallel, so they are equivalent to $\dfrac{6\cdot6}{6+6}\,\Omega=3\,\Omega$. This combination is in series with the $3\,\Omega$ resistor, so the total resistance between $A$ and $B$ is $3\,\Omega+3\,\Omega=6\,\Omega$. The $3\,\Omega$ resistor is in the section with no branches, so it carries the whole current,
$$
I=\frac{12\,\mathrm{V}}{6\,\Omega}=2\,\mathrm{A}.
$$
