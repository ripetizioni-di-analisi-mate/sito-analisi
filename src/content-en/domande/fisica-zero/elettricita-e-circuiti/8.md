A positively charged rod is brought near two charged balls hanging from insulating threads. Ball A is attracted, and ball B is repelled. What are the signs of the charges on A and B?

- A positive, B negative
- A negative, B positive
- Both positive
- Both negative

## Explanation

Charges of opposite signs attract each other, so ball A, which is attracted by the positive rod, has a **negative** charge. Charges of the same sign repel each other, so ball B, which is repelled by the rod, has a **positive** charge.
