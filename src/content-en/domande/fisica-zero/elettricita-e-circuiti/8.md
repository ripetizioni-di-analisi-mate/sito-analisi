A device is supplied with $30$ V and carries $2$ A. What is the electrical power?

- $15\,\mathrm{W}$
- $30\,\mathrm{W}$
- $120\,\mathrm{W}$
- $60\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

Electrical power is $P=VI$. Multiply the voltage by the current. The device therefore absorbs $60$ watts.
