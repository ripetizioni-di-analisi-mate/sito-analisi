A component measures $12\,\mathrm{V}$ and $0.40\,\mathrm{A}$ at the same instant. What is its resistance at this operating point?

- $30\,\Omega$
- $4.8\,\Omega$
- $0.033\,\Omega$
- $3\,\Omega$

## Explanation

The voltage-to-current ratio is $\dfrac{12}{0.40}=30$ volts per ampere, or $30\,\Omega$. This value describes the component under the measured conditions.
