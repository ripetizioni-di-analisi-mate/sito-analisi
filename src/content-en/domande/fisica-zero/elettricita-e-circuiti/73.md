An ideal source delivers $20\,\mathrm{W}$ to a circuit containing only three resistors. Two dissipate $8\,\mathrm{W}$ and $5\,\mathrm{W}$ respectively. What does the third dissipate?

- $7\,\mathrm{W}$
- $13\,\mathrm{W}$
- $17\,\mathrm{W}$
- $33\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

In steady state the supplied power is distributed among the three resistors. The remaining share is $20-8-5=7$ watts.
