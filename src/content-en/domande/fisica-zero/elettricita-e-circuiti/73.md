The ceiling bulbs in a hallway are connected in parallel to the same line, which is treated as an ideal constant-voltage source. The filament of one of them breaks. What happens to the other bulbs?

- They stay on with the same brightness
- They all go out
- They stay on and glow more brightly
- They stay on and glow less brightly

## Explanation

In a parallel connection, each bulb is in a separate branch, with its ends connected to the same two points of the line. The broken filament opens only its own branch, so the other bulbs still have the same voltage across them. Therefore each one carries the same current as before and **stays on with the same brightness**.
