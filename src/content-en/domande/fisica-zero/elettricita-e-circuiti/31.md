A resistor of $6$ ohms carries a current of $2$ A. What is the voltage across its terminals?

- $3\,\mathrm{V}$
- $8\,\mathrm{V}$
- $12\,\mathrm{V}$
- $24\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

For an ohmic resistor, $V=RI$ holds. Multiply $6$ by $2$. The voltage is therefore $12$ volts.
