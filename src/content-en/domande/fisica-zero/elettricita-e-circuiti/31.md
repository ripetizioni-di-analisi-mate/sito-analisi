While $0.5\,\mathrm{C}$ passes through the bulb of a flashlight, the bulb receives $1.5\,\mathrm{J}$. What is the voltage across the bulb?

- $0.75\,\mathrm{V}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}\,\mathrm{V}$
- $3\,\mathrm{V}$
- $2\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

The voltage across a load is the energy received divided by the charge passing through it. Therefore
$$
V=\frac{W}{q}=\frac{1.5\,\mathrm{J}}{0.5\,\mathrm{C}}=3\,\mathrm{V}.
$$
Each coulomb passing through the bulb thus corresponds to $3\,\mathrm{J}$ received.
