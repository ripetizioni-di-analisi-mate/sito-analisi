A current signal rises linearly from $0\,\mathrm{A}$ to $4\,\mathrm{A}$ in $6\,\mathrm{s}$. How much charge passes during that interval on the current–time graph?

::grafico{linee="0,0 6,4" poligoni="0,0 6,0 6,4" punti="6,4" xmin=0 xmax=7 ymin=0 ymax=5 nomi="t,I" legenda=0 titolo="Current in amperes against time in seconds."}

- $24\,\mathrm{C}$
- $6\,\mathrm{C}$
- $4\,\mathrm{C}$
- $12\,\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

The area under the graph is a triangle with a base of $6\,\mathrm{s}$ and a height of $4\,\mathrm{A}$. Half their product gives $12\,\mathrm{C}$.
