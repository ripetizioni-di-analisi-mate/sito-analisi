A real battery is marked $9\,\mathrm{V}$ open circuit but supplies $7\,\mathrm{V}$ at its terminals while operating. The load carries $0.4\,\mathrm{A}$. What value should be used for the power actually received by the load?

- $0.8\,\mathrm{W}$
- $17.5\,\mathrm{W}$
- $2.8\,\mathrm{W}$
- $3.6\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

The voltage while current flows is $7\,\mathrm{V}$. The load therefore receives $7\cdot 0.4=2.8$ watts. The open-circuit reading applies to a different operating condition.
