While brewing a coffee, which takes $30\,\mathrm{s}$, an espresso machine receives $36\,000\,\mathrm{J}$ of electrical energy. What power does the machine absorb, assuming it is constant?

- $1\,080\,000\,\mathrm{W}$
- $72\,000\,\mathrm{W}$
- $120\,\mathrm{W}$
- $1200\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

At constant power, the energy received is $E=P_e\Delta t$. Dividing both sides by $\Delta t$ gives $P_e=\dfrac{E}{\Delta t}$. Therefore
$$
P_e=\frac{36\,000\,\mathrm{J}}{30\,\mathrm{s}}=1200\,\mathrm{W}.
$$
The machine thus receives $1200\,\mathrm{J}$ every second.
