In a school laboratory, four small spheres are prepared. For each one, the total positive charge and the total negative charge are given. Which sphere is electrically neutral?

- $+3\,\mathrm{\mu C}$ and $-3\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $+3\,\mathrm{\mu C}$ and $-2\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $+4\,\mathrm{\mu C}$ and $-2\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $0\,\mathrm{\mu C}$ and $-3\,\mathrm{\mu C}$

## Explanation

A sphere is neutral when its charges add up to zero. For the sphere with $+3\,\mathrm{\mu C}$ and $-3\,\mathrm{\mu C}$ the sum is zero, so this sphere is **neutral**. The other sums are $+1\,\mathrm{\mu C}$, $+2\,\mathrm{\mu C}$, and $-3\,\mathrm{\mu C}$, so the other spheres have a nonzero net charge.
