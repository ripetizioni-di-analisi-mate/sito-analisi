Two resistors of $3$ ohms and $6$ ohms are connected in series. What is their equivalent resistance?

- $2\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $9\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $18\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $3\,\mathrm{\Omega}$

## Explanation

Resistors in series carry the same current. Their voltages add, so their resistances also add. This gives $R_\mathrm{eq}=3+6=9$ ohms.
