An equivalent positive charge of $3\,\mathrm{C}$ passes through a load in the conventional current direction across a constant $9\,\mathrm{V}$ drop. How much energy does the load receive?

- $27\,\mathrm{J}$
- $3\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0.333\,\mathrm{J}$
- $12\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Each coulomb transfers $9\,\mathrm{J}$. For $3\,\mathrm{C}$ the total energy is $3\cdot 9=27$ joules.
