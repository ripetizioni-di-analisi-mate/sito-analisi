An oriented measurement gives $V_A-V_B=+4$ volts. The voltmeter leads are swapped without altering the circuit. What changes?

- The reading reverses sign and becomes $-4\,\mathrm{V}$.
- The voltage magnitude doubles.
- The resistance of the component.
- The physical direction of circuit current.

## Explanation

Swapping the leads reverses the order of the potentials in the difference. The meter therefore reads $V_B-V_A=-4$ volts while the circuit conditions stay the same.
