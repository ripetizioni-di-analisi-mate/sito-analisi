A car's windshield wiper motor, powered at $12\,\mathrm{V}$, carries a constant current of $2\,\mathrm{A}$ for $3\,\mathrm{s}$. By first finding the charge that passes, how much energy does the motor receive?

- $24\,\mathrm{J}$
- $8\,\mathrm{J}$
- $18\,\mathrm{J}$
- $72\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

At constant current, the charge that passes is $q=I\Delta t=2\,\mathrm{A}\cdot3\,\mathrm{s}=6\,\mathrm{C}$. The energy received by a load is the charge passing through it multiplied by the voltage across it. Therefore
$$
W=qV=6\,\mathrm{C}\cdot12\,\mathrm{V}=72\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
