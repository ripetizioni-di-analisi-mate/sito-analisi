An ammeter is to measure the current in one resistor in a branched circuit. How should an ideal ammeter be connected so that this very current passes through it?

- In parallel with the resistor.
- Between two points of the same wire without opening the branch.
- In series with the source before all the branches.
- In series in the resistor’s branch.

## Explanation

A series connection leaves no branching between the meter and resistor, so their currents match. An ideal meter’s zero resistance leaves the branch current unchanged.
