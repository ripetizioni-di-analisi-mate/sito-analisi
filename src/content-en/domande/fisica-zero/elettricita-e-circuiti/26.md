A device is supplied with $50$ V and carries $2$ A. What is the electrical power?

- $25\,\mathrm{W}$
- $100\,\mathrm{W}$
- $50\,\mathrm{W}$
- $200\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

Electrical power is $P=VI$. Multiply the voltage by the current. The device therefore absorbs $100$ watts.
