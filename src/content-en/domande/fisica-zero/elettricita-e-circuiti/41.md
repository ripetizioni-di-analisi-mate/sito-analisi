The graph shows the voltage $V$ as a function of the current $I$ for the ohmic resistors A and B of two hand warmers. Which resistor has the larger resistance?

::grafico{f="5*x;2*x" xmin=0 xmax=3 ymin=0 ymax=12 nomi="I,V" legenda=0 etichette="1.75,10.6,A;2.75,4.4,B" titolo="Voltage $V$ in volts as a function of current $I$ in amperes for resistors A and B"}

- B, because at the same voltage it carries a larger current
- They have the same resistance, because both lines pass through the origin
- It cannot be determined without knowing the temperature
- A, because at the same current it requires a higher voltage

## Explanation

At each point of the graph, the resistance is the ratio of voltage to current. At the same current, for example $I=2\,\mathrm{A}$, line A gives $10\,\mathrm{V}$ and line B gives $4\,\mathrm{V}$. Therefore $R_A=5\,\Omega$ and $R_B=2\,\Omega$, and the larger resistance belongs to **A**, the steeper line. A larger current at the same voltage indicates a smaller resistance instead.
