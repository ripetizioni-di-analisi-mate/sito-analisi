The voltage applied to an ohmic resistor increases by $50\,\%$, while a replacement resistor has twice the original resistance. What is the ratio of new current to original current?

- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

In the current ratio, voltage contributes a factor of 1.5 and resistance contributes a factor of 2 in the denominator. The new current is therefore $75\,\%$ of the initial value.
