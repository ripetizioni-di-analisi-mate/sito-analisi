A resistor carries $3$ A and has $21$ V across its terminals. What is its resistance?

- $7\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $63\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $21\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $\dfrac{1}{7}\,\mathrm{\Omega}$

## Explanation

Rearranging Ohm's law gives $R=\dfrac{V}{I}$. Divide $21$ by $3$. The resistance is therefore $7$ ohms.
