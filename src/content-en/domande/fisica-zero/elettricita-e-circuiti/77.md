With an ideal $6\,\mathrm{V}$ source and a $10\,\Omega$ resistor, the current must be between $0.15\,\mathrm{A}$ and $0.20\,\mathrm{A}$ inclusive. Which one of the available resistors should be added in series?

- $15\,\Omega$
- $22\,\Omega$
- $35\,\Omega$
- $47\,\Omega$

## Explanation

The current ceiling requires at least $30\,\Omega$ in total, while the current floor allows at most $40\,\Omega$. Subtracting the existing $10\,\Omega$ leaves a required range of $20\,\Omega$ to $30\,\Omega$. Only $22\,\Omega$ among the choices is in that range.
