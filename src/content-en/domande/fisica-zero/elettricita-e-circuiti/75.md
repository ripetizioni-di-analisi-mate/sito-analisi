The battery connections are reversed in a circuit containing only ohmic resistors. With unchanged voltage magnitude, what happens to the signed current and the heat produced per second in a resistor?

- Current becomes zero and heating increases.
- Both reverse sign.
- Current retains its sign and heating doubles.
- Current reverses sign and dissipated power stays the same.

## Explanation

Reversing the voltage reverses the current. Heat produced per second is $RI^2$ and depends on the square, so it stays the same.
