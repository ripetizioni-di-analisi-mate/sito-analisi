A component receives $48\,\mathrm{J}$ of electrical energy as $4\,\mathrm{C}$ passes through it. What constant voltage is across the component?

- $44\,\mathrm{V}$
- $192\,\mathrm{V}$
- $12\,\mathrm{V}$
- $0.083\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

Voltage is energy transferred per unit charge. The ratio $\dfrac{48}{4}=12$ joules per coulomb equals $12\,\mathrm{V}$.
