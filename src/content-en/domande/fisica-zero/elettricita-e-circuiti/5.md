A resistor carries $3$ A and has $9$ V across its terminals. What is its resistance?

- $3\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $27\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $9\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}\,\mathrm{\Omega}$

## Explanation

Rearranging Ohm's law gives $R=\dfrac{V}{I}$. Divide $9$ by $3$. The resistance is therefore $3$ ohms.
