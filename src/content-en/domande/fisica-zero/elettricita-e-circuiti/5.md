A tiny dust grain is electrically neutral. The charge of all its protons is $+2\,\mathrm{\mu C}$. What is the total charge of its electrons?

- $+2\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $0\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{\mu C}$

## Explanation

A body is neutral when its charges add up to zero. The charges of the grain are those of its protons and those of its electrons, so $+2\,\mathrm{\mu C}+q_e=0$. Therefore the total charge of the electrons is $q_e=-2\,\mathrm{\mu C}$. The grain thus contains many charged particles even though its total charge is zero.
