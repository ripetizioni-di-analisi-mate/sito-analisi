Three conducting spheres can exchange charge with each other. A wire connects one sphere to Earth and this is their only external connection. For which system can we require total charge to stay at its initial value throughout the exchange?

- The three spheres together with the connecting wire and Earth.
- Only the sphere connected to Earth.
- Only the two spheres without a direct earth wire.
- The three spheres taken on their own.

## Explanation

Charge can enter or leave the spheres through the earth wire. Including the wire and Earth makes that transfer internal, and by assumption no other charge crosses the system boundary.
