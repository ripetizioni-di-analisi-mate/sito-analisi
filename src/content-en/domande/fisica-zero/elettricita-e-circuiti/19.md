A charge of $30$ C passes through a cross section of a wire in $3$ s. What is the average current?

- $90\,\mathrm{A}$
- $30\,\mathrm{A}$
- $10\,\mathrm{A}$
- $0.1\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

The average current is the charge transferred divided by the time. This gives $I=\dfrac{30}{3}$. The current is therefore $10$ amperes.
