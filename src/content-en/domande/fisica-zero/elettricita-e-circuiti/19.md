While a smartphone is charging, $1800\,\mathrm{C}$ pass through a cross section of the cable in $15$ minutes. What is the average current in the cable?

- $2\,\mathrm{A}$
- $120\,\mathrm{A}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{A}$
- $30\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

The average current is the charge passing through the cross section divided by the time interval, expressed in seconds. Since $15\,\mathrm{min}=15\cdot60\,\mathrm{s}=900\,\mathrm{s}$, we have
$$
I=\frac{1800\,\mathrm{C}}{900\,\mathrm{s}}=2\,\mathrm{A}.
$$
So, on average, $2\,\mathrm{C}$ pass through the cross section every second.
