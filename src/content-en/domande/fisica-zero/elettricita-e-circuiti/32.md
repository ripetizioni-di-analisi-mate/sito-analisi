An ideal AA battery maintains $1.5\,\mathrm{V}$ across its terminals and powers a small load. Which statement describes what this voltage means for the load?

- $1.5\,\mathrm{C}$ pass through it for each joule received
- It receives $1.5\,\mathrm{J}$ every second
- $1.5\,\mathrm{C}$ pass through it every second
- It receives $1.5\,\mathrm{J}$ for each coulomb passing through it

## Explanation

Voltage is the energy that can be transferred per unit charge, and $1\,\mathrm{V}=1\,\mathrm{J/C}$. A voltage of $1.5\,\mathrm{V}$ therefore means that each coulomb passing through the load corresponds to $1.5\,\mathrm{J}$ received. The energy received in one second is a power, and the charge passing in one second is a current.
