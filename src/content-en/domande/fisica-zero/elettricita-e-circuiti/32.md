A resistor carries $3$ A and has $18$ V across its terminals. What is its resistance?

- $54\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $18\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $6\,\mathrm{\Omega}$

## Explanation

Rearranging Ohm's law gives $R=\dfrac{V}{I}$. Divide $18$ by $3$. The resistance is therefore $6$ ohms.
