A current has zero average over a $10\,\mathrm{s}$ interval. Which situation is still possible?

- A net negative charge of $10\,\mathrm{C}$ passed.
- The current was positive at every instant of the interval.
- A net positive charge of $10\,\mathrm{C}$ passed.
- Equal amounts of charge passed in opposite directions at different times.

## Explanation

Zero mean current implies zero net charge transfer. The current may have changed direction so that opposite transfers cancelled.
