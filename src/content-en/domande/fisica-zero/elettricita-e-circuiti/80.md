While it is running, a blender absorbs a power of $600\,\mathrm{W}$. Which statement about the running blender is correct?

- It receives $600\,\mathrm{J}$ in total, however long it runs
- It receives $600\,\mathrm{J}$ every second
- It receives $600\,\mathrm{J}$ every hour
- $600\,\mathrm{C}$ pass through it every second

## Explanation

Power is the energy received per unit time, and one watt corresponds to one joule per second. A power of $600\,\mathrm{W}$ therefore means that the blender receives $600\,\mathrm{J}$ **every second** it runs. The total energy also depends on how long it runs, while coulombs per second measure a current.
