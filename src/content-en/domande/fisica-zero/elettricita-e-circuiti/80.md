Paths A and B connect the same two nodes of a DC circuit. Along path A the voltage drops in the chosen direction are $2\,\mathrm{V}$ and $5\,\mathrm{V}$. What must be the total voltage drop in that same direction along B?

- $0\,\mathrm{V}$
- $10\,\mathrm{V}$
- $7\,\mathrm{V}$
- $3\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

Potential difference depends on the two end nodes. The drops along A add to $7\,\mathrm{V}$, so any path between the same nodes has that same directed difference.
