Two identical ohmic elements are connected in series. One fails open, breaking the path completely. With the main switch closed and an ideal battery, which voltage measurement locates the fault?

- Zero volts across both.
- The full battery voltage across the intact element.
- Half the voltage across each element.
- The full battery voltage across the open element.

## Explanation

Current is zero, so the intact element’s drop $RI$ is zero. The available voltage thus appears across the open element.
