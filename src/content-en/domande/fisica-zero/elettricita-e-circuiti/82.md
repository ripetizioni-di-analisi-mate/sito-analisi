A laptop charger absorbs a constant power of $20\,\mathrm{W}$. How long does it take to absorb $0.1\,\mathrm{kWh}$ of energy?

- $2\,\mathrm{h}$
- $0.005\,\mathrm{h}$
- $5\,\mathrm{h}$
- $200\,\mathrm{h}$

## Explanation

Since $1\,\mathrm{kWh}=1000\,\mathrm{Wh}$, the energy is $100\,\mathrm{Wh}$. From $E=P_e\Delta t$ we get $\Delta t=\dfrac{E}{P_e}$. Therefore
$$
\Delta t=\frac{100\,\mathrm{Wh}}{20\,\mathrm{W}}=5\,\mathrm{h}.
$$
