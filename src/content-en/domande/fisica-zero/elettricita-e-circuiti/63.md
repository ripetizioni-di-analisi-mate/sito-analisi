A $100\,\Omega$ component may dissipate at most $0.25\,\mathrm{W}$. What is the greatest DC voltage magnitude allowed by the ohmic model?

- $5\,\mathrm{V}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{V}$
- $25\,\mathrm{V}$
- $50\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

The condition $V^2\le PR$ gives $V^2\le 0.25\cdot 100=25$ in square volts. Taking the positive square root gives $5\,\mathrm{V}$.
