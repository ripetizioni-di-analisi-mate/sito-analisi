In an amplifier circuit, a $1000\,\Omega$ resistor is connected in parallel with a $10\,\Omega$ resistor. Approximately what is the equivalent resistance?

- Slightly more than $10\,\Omega$
- About $505\,\Omega$
- $1010\,\Omega$
- Slightly less than $10\,\Omega$

## Explanation

In parallel, $\dfrac{1}{R_{\mathrm{eq}}}=\dfrac{1}{10\,\Omega}+\dfrac{1}{1000\,\Omega}$, the sum of two positive terms. Hence $\dfrac{1}{R_{\mathrm{eq}}}$ exceeds $\dfrac{1}{10\,\Omega}$, and $R_{\mathrm{eq}}$ is less than $10\,\Omega$. The second term is very small compared with the first, so $R_{\mathrm{eq}}$ stays close to $10\,\Omega$. Indeed, the calculation gives
$$
R_{\mathrm{eq}}=\frac{10\cdot1000}{10+1000}\,\Omega\approx9.9\,\Omega.
$$
