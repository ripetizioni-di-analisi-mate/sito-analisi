A $4\,\Omega$ resistor is in series with the parallel combination of $12\,\Omega$ and $6\,\Omega$. A constant $12\,\mathrm{V}$ is applied across the whole network. What current passes through the $6\,\Omega$ resistor?

- $1.5\,\mathrm{A}$
- $2\,\mathrm{A}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{A}$
- $1\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

The parallel pair is equivalent to $4\,\Omega$. The two $4\,\Omega$ sections in series split the voltage equally, so the $6\,\Omega$ branch has $6\,\mathrm{V}$ across it and carries $1\,\mathrm{A}$.
