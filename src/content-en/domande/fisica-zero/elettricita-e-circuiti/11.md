A constant current of $4$ A flows for $4$ s. How much charge passes through a cross section of the wire?

- $1\,\mathrm{C}$
- $8\,\mathrm{C}$
- $16\,\mathrm{C}$
- $17\,\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

For a constant current, the charge is $Q=I\Delta t$. Multiply $4$ by $4$. A charge of $16$ coulombs therefore passes through the wire.
