In a clothes dryer, a sweater, a shirt, and a sock, initially neutral, rub against one another. Assume the three garments exchange charge only with one another. In the end, the sweater has charge $+5\,\mathrm{\mu C}$ and the shirt $-2\,\mathrm{\mu C}$. What is the charge of the sock?

- $+3\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $-7\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $+7\,\mathrm{\mu C}$

## Explanation

The three garments exchange charge only with one another, so their total charge is conserved. They all start out neutral, so the three charges must also add up to zero at the end. Hence $+5\,\mathrm{\mu C}-2\,\mathrm{\mu C}+q=0$, which gives $q=-3\,\mathrm{\mu C}$.
