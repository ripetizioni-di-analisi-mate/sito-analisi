An energy meter rises from $128.5\,\mathrm{kWh}$ to $130.0\,\mathrm{kWh}$. At a flat rate of 0.20 euro per kilowatt-hour and with no fixed charges, what does the interval cost?

- 0.30 euro
- 0.03 euro
- 26 euros
- 1.50 euros

## Explanation

Consumption is the difference between the final and initial readings, or $1.5\,\mathrm{kWh}$. Multiplying by the unit price gives thirty cents.
