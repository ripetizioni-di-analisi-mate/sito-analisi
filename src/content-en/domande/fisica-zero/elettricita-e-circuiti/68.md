A bird is perched on a wire of a power line, with its feet $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ apart. Treating the wire as an ideal wire, what is the voltage between the two points of the wire touched by its feet?

- The voltage of the line
- Half the voltage of the line
- $0\,\mathrm{V}$
- A voltage that increases with the height of the wire

## Explanation

In an ideal wire, the voltage between two points on the same stretch is zero, so those points have the same potential. Both feet touch the same wire, so the voltage between the two contact points is **zero**. The voltage of the line exists between two different wires instead.
