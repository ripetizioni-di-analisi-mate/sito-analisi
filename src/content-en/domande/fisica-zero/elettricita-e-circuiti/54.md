Resistor A is connected between nodes X and Y. Resistor B connects Y and Z, but X and Z are directly joined by an ideal wire. How are A and B connected?

- In separate circuits.
- Their connection cannot be determined without their resistances.
- In parallel.
- In series because they share the label Y.

## Explanation

The ideal wire makes X and Z one electrical node. Each resistor therefore has one end at Y and the other at the common X–Z node.
