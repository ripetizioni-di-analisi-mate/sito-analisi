A string of balcony lights consists of three sections in series, connected to an ideal $12\,\mathrm{V}$ source. There are $3\,\mathrm{V}$ across the first section and $5\,\mathrm{V}$ across the second. What is the voltage across the third section?

- $4\,\mathrm{V}$
- $20\,\mathrm{V}$
- $8\,\mathrm{V}$
- $2\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

In a series connection, the total voltage is the sum of the voltages across the individual sections. Hence $3\,\mathrm{V}+5\,\mathrm{V}+V_3=12\,\mathrm{V}$. Therefore
$$
V_3=12\,\mathrm{V}-8\,\mathrm{V}=4\,\mathrm{V}.
$$
