In an old string of Christmas tree lights, all connected in series, the filament of one bulb breaks. What happens to the other bulbs?

- They stay on with the same brightness
- They all go out
- They stay on and glow more brightly
- Only those after the broken bulb go out

## Explanation

In a series connection, the bulbs form a single path with no branches. The broken filament opens this path, so the current becomes zero throughout the circuit. Therefore **all the bulbs go out**, including those before the broken one.
