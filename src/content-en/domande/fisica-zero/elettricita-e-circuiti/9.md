A glass rod is rubbed with a silk cloth, with no exchange of charge with other bodies. Both were initially neutral, and at the end the glass has a positive net charge. Which description of the exchange is correct?

- The glass gave electrons to the silk
- The glass received electrons from the silk
- The glass and the silk both gave away electrons
- New positive charges were created in the glass

## Explanation

An initially neutral body that loses electrons acquires a positive net charge. The glass has become positive, so it **gave away electrons**. The glass and the silk exchange charge only with each other, so their total charge stays zero and the electrons lost by the glass went to the silk. The same law rules out the creation of new charges and the loss of electrons by both bodies.
