A $5\,\Omega$ and a $10\,\Omega$ resistor are in series across an ideal $12\,\mathrm{V}$ battery. What is the voltage drop across the $10\,\Omega$ resistor?

- $8\,\mathrm{V}$
- $4\,\mathrm{V}$
- $6\,\mathrm{V}$
- $12\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

The total resistance is $15\,\Omega$ and the common current is $0.8\,\mathrm{A}$. The $10\,\Omega$ resistor therefore drops $10\cdot 0.8=8$ volts.
