A device draws a constant power of $3$ W for $20$ s. How much energy does it absorb?

- $60\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0.15\,\mathrm{J}$
- $23\,\mathrm{J}$
- $\dfrac{20}{3}\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

At constant power, the energy is $E=P\Delta t$. Multiply $3$ by $20$. The energy absorbed is therefore $60$ joules.
