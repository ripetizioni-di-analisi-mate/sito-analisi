The resistance of an electric kettle is $46\,\Omega$ and is taken to be constant during operation. If the kettle is supplied with $230\,\mathrm{V}$, what current passes through it?

- $0.2\,\mathrm{A}$
- $5\,\mathrm{A}$
- $10\,580\,\mathrm{A}$
- $50\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

The resistance is constant, so **Ohm's first law** applies, $V=RI$. Dividing both sides by $R$ gives $I=\dfrac{V}{R}$. Therefore
$$
I=\frac{230\,\mathrm{V}}{46\,\Omega}=5\,\mathrm{A}.
$$
