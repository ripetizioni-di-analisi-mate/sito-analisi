A constant current of $7$ A flows for $4$ s. How much charge passes through a cross section of the wire?

- $1.75\,\mathrm{C}$
- $28\,\mathrm{C}$
- $11\,\mathrm{C}$
- $\dfrac{4}{7}\,\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

For a constant current, the charge is $Q=I\Delta t$. Multiply $7$ by $4$. A charge of $28$ coulombs therefore passes through the wire.
