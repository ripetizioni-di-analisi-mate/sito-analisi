On a graph with current vertically and voltage horizontally, an ohmic resistor’s line rises from $0\,\mathrm{A}$ to $0.5\,\mathrm{A}$ as voltage increases by $2\,\mathrm{V}$. What does its slope of 0.25 amperes per volt represent?

- Resistance in ohms.
- Energy per coulomb.
- The reciprocal of the resistance.
- Power in watts.

## Explanation

With these axes the slope is $\dfrac{I}{V}=\dfrac{1}{R}$. The corresponding resistor therefore has a resistance of $4\,\Omega$.
