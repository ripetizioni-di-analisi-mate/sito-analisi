Walking on a nylon carpet, a person builds up a charge of $-0.8\,\mathrm{\mu C}$ on their body. What is this charge in coulombs?

- $-8\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{C}$
- $-8\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{C}$
- $-8\cdot10^{5}\,\mathrm{C}$
- $-8\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

The prefix micro means one millionth, so $1\,\mathrm{\mu C}=10^{-6}\,\mathrm{C}$. Therefore
$$
-0.8\,\mathrm{\mu C}=-0.8\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{C}=-8\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{C}.
$$
The sign stays negative because the conversion changes only the unit.
