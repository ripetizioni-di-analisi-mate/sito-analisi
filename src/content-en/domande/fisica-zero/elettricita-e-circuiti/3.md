A net charge of $15\,\mathrm{C}$ crosses a section in the chosen direction in $3\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the average current?

- $12\,\mathrm{A}$
- $45\,\mathrm{A}$
- $5\,\mathrm{A}$
- $0.2\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

Average current is charge passing through the section divided by time. The ratio $\dfrac{15}{3}=5$ coulombs per second equals $5\,\mathrm{A}$.
