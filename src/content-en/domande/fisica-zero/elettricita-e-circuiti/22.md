A resistor of $5$ ohms carries a current of $2$ A. What is the voltage across its terminals?

- $2.5\,\mathrm{V}$
- $10\,\mathrm{V}$
- $7\,\mathrm{V}$
- $20\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

For an ohmic resistor, $V=RI$ holds. Multiply $5$ by $2$. The voltage is therefore $10$ volts.
