Across a section, $2\,\mathrm{C}$ of positive charge moves right and $3\,\mathrm{C}$ in magnitude of negative charge moves left. What is the net conventional charge transfer to the right?

- $-1\,\mathrm{C}$
- $+5\,\mathrm{C}$
- $+1\,\mathrm{C}$
- $-5\,\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

Negative charge moving left contributes like positive charge moving right. The two directed contributions add to $+5\,\mathrm{C}$.
