An $8\,\Omega$ resistor is in parallel with an unknown positive resistance. Which measured equivalent resistance rules out that connection?

- $10\,\Omega$
- $2\,\Omega$
- $4\,\Omega$
- $6\,\Omega$

## Explanation

Each positive-resistance branch adds current at the same voltage. The equivalent must therefore be below $8\,\Omega$, which rules out $10\,\Omega$.
