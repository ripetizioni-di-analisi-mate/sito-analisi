In a door alarm, an ideal $9\,\mathrm{V}$ battery powers two resistors in series, of $100\,\Omega$ and $200\,\Omega$. What current passes through the resistors?

- $0.09\,\mathrm{A}$
- $0.045\,\mathrm{A}$
- $0.135\,\mathrm{A}$
- $0.03\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

The resistors are in series, so $R_{\mathrm{eq}}=100\,\Omega+200\,\Omega=300\,\Omega$. The current is the same in both resistors and follows from Ohm's law applied to the equivalent resistor. Therefore
$$
I=\frac{9\,\mathrm{V}}{300\,\Omega}=0.03\,\mathrm{A},
$$
that is, $30\,\mathrm{mA}$.
