A charge of $36$ C passes through a cross section of a wire in $3$ s. What is the average current?

- $108\,\mathrm{A}$
- $36\,\mathrm{A}$
- $\dfrac{1}{12}\,\mathrm{A}$
- $12\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

The average current is the charge transferred divided by the time. This gives $I=\dfrac{36}{3}$. The current is therefore $12$ amperes.
