In the direction of conventional current a resistor receives $6\,\mathrm{J}$ from each coulomb passing through. In one second $2\,\mathrm{C}$ enters. How many coulombs leave in the same second in steady state?

- $12\,\mathrm{C}$
- $0\,\mathrm{C}$
- $2\,\mathrm{C}$
- $3\,\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

In steady state the resistor does not accumulate charge. It converts energy while all the charge entering during that interval leaves again.
