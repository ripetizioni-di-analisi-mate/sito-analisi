A toy's circuit contains three resistors connected in series, of $4\,\Omega$, $6\,\Omega$, and $10\,\Omega$. What is the equivalent resistance?

- $240\,\Omega$
- $10\,\Omega$
- $20\,\Omega$
- $2\,\Omega$

## Explanation

In a series connection, the resistors carry the same current and the voltages add up. Therefore the equivalent resistance is the sum of all the resistances,
$$
R_{\mathrm{eq}}=(4+6+10)\,\Omega=20\,\Omega.
$$
