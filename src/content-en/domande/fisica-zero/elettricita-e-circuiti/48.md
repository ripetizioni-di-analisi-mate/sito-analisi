Two identical $6\,\Omega$ resistors are connected to the same two nodes. Which single resistor would draw the same total current at the same voltage?

- $3\,\Omega$
- $36\,\Omega$
- $12\,\Omega$
- $6\,\Omega$

## Explanation

The branches share the voltage and draw equal currents. Total current is twice that of one branch, so the equivalent resistance is half of $6\,\Omega$.
