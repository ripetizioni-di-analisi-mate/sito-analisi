Several pairs of voltage and current values are measured on the ohmic resistor of a charger, held at constant temperature. What shape does the graph of the voltage $V$ as a function of the current $I$ have?

- A horizontal line
- A straight line through the origin
- A straight line that does not pass through the origin
- A curve that gets steeper and steeper

## Explanation

For an ohmic conductor, $V=RI$ with $R$ constant, so voltage and current are directly proportional. The graph of a direct proportion is a **straight line through the origin**, because zero current corresponds to zero voltage. The slope of the line depends on the value of the resistance.
