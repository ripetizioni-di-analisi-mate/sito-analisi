An unknown resistor is in series with $3\,\Omega$ and $5\,\Omega$. The battery supplies $28\,\mathrm{V}$ and the measured current is $2\,\mathrm{A}$. What is the unknown resistance?

- $14\,\Omega$
- $22\,\Omega$
- $8\,\Omega$
- $6\,\Omega$

## Explanation

The source measurements give a total resistance of $14\,\Omega$. Subtracting the known contributions leaves $14-3-5=6$ ohms.
