The circuit of an aquarium heater contains two resistors in series, of $2\,\Omega$ and $10\,\Omega$, powered by an ideal $24\,\mathrm{V}$ source. What is the voltage across the $10\,\Omega$ resistor?

- $4\,\mathrm{V}$
- $12\,\mathrm{V}$
- $20\,\mathrm{V}$
- $2.4\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

The equivalent resistance of the series combination is $2\,\Omega+10\,\Omega=12\,\Omega$, so the current is $I=\dfrac{24\,\mathrm{V}}{12\,\Omega}=2\,\mathrm{A}$. The same current passes through the $10\,\Omega$ resistor, so the voltage across it is
$$
V=10\,\Omega\cdot2\,\mathrm{A}=20\,\mathrm{V}.
$$
The voltage across the $2\,\Omega$ resistor is $4\,\mathrm{V}$, and indeed the two voltages add up to $24\,\mathrm{V}$.
