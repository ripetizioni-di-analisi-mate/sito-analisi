A $12\,\Omega$ branch is to be joined by another resistor so that their parallel equivalent is $4\,\Omega$. What should the second resistance be?

- $16\,\Omega$
- $3\,\Omega$
- $8\,\Omega$
- $6\,\Omega$

## Explanation

Subtract the conductances to obtain $\dfrac{1}{R}=\dfrac{1}{4}-\dfrac{1}{12}=\dfrac{1}{6}$. Taking the reciprocal gives $6\,\Omega$.
