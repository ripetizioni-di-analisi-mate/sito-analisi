A $2000\,\mathrm{W}$ vacuum cleaner runs for $6$ minutes, while a $10\,\mathrm{W}$ lamp stays on for $10$ hours. Which of the two appliances absorbs more energy?

- The lamp
- The vacuum cleaner
- They absorb the same energy
- It cannot be determined without knowing the voltages

## Explanation

The energy depends on both the power and the operating time, according to $E=P_e\Delta t$. Six minutes is $0.1\,\mathrm{h}$, so the vacuum cleaner absorbs $2000\,\mathrm{W}\cdot0.1\,\mathrm{h}=200\,\mathrm{Wh}$. The lamp absorbs $10\,\mathrm{W}\cdot10\,\mathrm{h}=100\,\mathrm{Wh}$. Therefore **the vacuum cleaner** absorbs more energy, twice as much as the lamp.
