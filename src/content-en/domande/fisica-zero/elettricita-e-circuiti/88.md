A circuit contains two series resistors. A real voltmeter of finite resistance is connected across the second. With an ideal constant-voltage source, how does the second resistor’s voltage change compared with the circuit without the meter?

- It increases.
- It decreases.
- It becomes negative without changing the lead orientation.
- It is identical for every meter resistance.

## Explanation

Putting the meter in parallel lowers the resistance of the second section. The first section then takes a larger share of the total voltage, leaving a smaller share for the second.
