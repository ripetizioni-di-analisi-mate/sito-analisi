A car's $12\,\mathrm{V}$ battery powers the starter motor. During one start, $50\,\mathrm{C}$ pass through the starter motor. How much energy does it receive?

- $600\,\mathrm{J}$
- $62\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0.24\,\mathrm{J}$
- $6000\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Voltage is the energy transferred per unit charge, so the energy received by the starter motor is $W=qV$. Each coulomb corresponds to $12\,\mathrm{J}$, so
$$
W=50\,\mathrm{C}\cdot12\,\mathrm{V}=600\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
