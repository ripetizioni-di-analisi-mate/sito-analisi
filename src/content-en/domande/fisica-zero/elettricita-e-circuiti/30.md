In the chosen direction the current is $+2\,\mathrm{A}$ for $3\,\mathrm{s}$, followed by $-1\,\mathrm{A}$ for $4\,\mathrm{s}$. What net charge passes through the section over the whole sequence?

- $-2\,\mathrm{C}$
- $+2\,\mathrm{C}$
- $+10\,\mathrm{C}$
- $+7\,\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

The first interval contributes $+6\,\mathrm{C}$ and the second $-4\,\mathrm{C}$. Their signed sum is a net charge of $+2\,\mathrm{C}$.
