Two ohmic lines A and B are plotted on the same axes, with voltage vertically and current horizontally. A lies above B at every positive current. Which resistor draws less current when both receive the same positive voltage?

- A
- Their switch-on time is required.
- B
- They always draw the same current.

## Explanation

A needs more voltage for the same current, so it has the greater resistance. At a common voltage, $\dfrac{V}{R}$ is smaller for A.
