In a doorbell circuit, an ohmic resistor of $470\,\Omega$ carries a current of $20\,\mathrm{mA}$. What is the voltage across it?

- $9400\,\mathrm{V}$
- $23.5\,\mathrm{V}$
- $9.4\,\mathrm{V}$
- $0.94\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

Since $1\,\mathrm{mA}=10^{-3}\,\mathrm{A}$, the current is $0.020\,\mathrm{A}$. The resistor is ohmic, so Ohm's law $V=RI$ holds. Therefore
$$
V=470\,\Omega\cdot0.020\,\mathrm{A}=9.4\,\mathrm{V}.
$$
