A device supplied at $6\,\mathrm{V}$ carries a steady $0.20\,\mathrm{A}$. What electrical power does it absorb?

- $0.12\,\mathrm{W}$
- $30\,\mathrm{W}$
- $6.2\,\mathrm{W}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

At constant voltage and current, power is $P=VI$. The product $6\cdot 0.20$ gives $1.2\,\mathrm{W}$.
