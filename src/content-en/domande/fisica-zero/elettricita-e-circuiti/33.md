A source transfers $90\,\mathrm{J}$ while maintaining a constant $9\,\mathrm{V}$. The current log reads $2\,\mathrm{A}$ throughout. How long did the transfer last?

- $45\,\mathrm{s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

At $9\,\mathrm{V}$ the charge transferred is $\dfrac{90}{9}=10$ coulombs. A current of $2\,\mathrm{A}$ transfers that charge in $5\,\mathrm{s}$.
