A desk fan is powered at $5\,\mathrm{V}$ by a computer's USB port and receives $20\,\mathrm{J}$ of energy. How much charge has passed through it?

- $100\,\mathrm{C}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{C}$
- $4\,\mathrm{C}$
- $15\,\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

For a load, $W=qV$, where $V$ is the voltage across it. Dividing both sides by $V$ gives $q=\dfrac{W}{V}$. Therefore
$$
q=\frac{20\,\mathrm{J}}{5\,\mathrm{V}}=4\,\mathrm{C}.
$$
