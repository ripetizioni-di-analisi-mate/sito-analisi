Two resistors of $6$ ohms and $12$ ohms are connected in series. What is their equivalent resistance?

- $18\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $4\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $72\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $6\,\mathrm{\Omega}$

## Explanation

Resistors in series carry the same current. Their voltages add, so their resistances also add. This gives $R_\mathrm{eq}=6+12=18$ ohms.
