Mobile electrons in a copper wire have an average drift velocity from right to left. What is the direction of conventional current?

- From right to left.
- Perpendicular to the wire.
- In neither direction because the copper is neutral.
- From left to right.

## Explanation

The convention uses the direction in which positive charge would move. For negative carriers the conventional direction is therefore opposite to their drift.
