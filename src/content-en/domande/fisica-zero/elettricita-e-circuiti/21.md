The motor of a cordless drill carries a constant current of $1.5\,\mathrm{A}$. How long does it take for $90\,\mathrm{C}$ to pass through a cross section of its internal wire?

- $135\,\mathrm{s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{s}$
- $600\,\mathrm{s}$
- $60\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

At constant current, $q=I\Delta t$. Dividing both sides by $I$ gives $\Delta t=\dfrac{q}{I}$. Therefore
$$
\Delta t=\frac{90\,\mathrm{C}}{1.5\,\mathrm{A}}=60\,\mathrm{s},
$$
that is, one minute.
