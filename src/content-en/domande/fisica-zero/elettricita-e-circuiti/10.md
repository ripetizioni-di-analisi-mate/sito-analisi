A charge of $24$ C passes through a cross section of a wire in $3$ s. What is the average current?

- $72\,\mathrm{A}$
- $8\,\mathrm{A}$
- $24\,\mathrm{A}$
- $0.125\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

The average current is the charge transferred divided by the time. This gives $I=\dfrac{24}{3}$. The current is therefore $8$ amperes.
