A plastic straw has a net charge of $-3\,\mathrm{\mu C}$. Which statement about the charges in the straw is true?

- It contains only negative charges
- The positive charges outweigh the negative ones
- The positive and negative charges cancel out
- The negative charges outweigh the positive ones

## Explanation

The net charge is the sum of the positive charges of the protons and the negative charges of the electrons. The sum is $-3\,\mathrm{\mu C}$, so the negative charge exceeds the positive charge in absolute value, and **the negative charges outweigh the positive ones**. Protons are still present, because they are part of the matter of the straw.
