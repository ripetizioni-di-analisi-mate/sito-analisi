Two branches of $4\,\Omega$ and $12\,\Omega$ are in parallel across an ideal $12\,\mathrm{V}$ source. What fraction of the total current passes through the $4\,\Omega$ branch?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

The common voltage produces $3\,\mathrm{A}$ in the $4\,\Omega$ branch and $1\,\mathrm{A}$ in the other. The $4\,\Omega$ branch therefore carries $3\,\mathrm{A}$ of the total $4\,\mathrm{A}$.
