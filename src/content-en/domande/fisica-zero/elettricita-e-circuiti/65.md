A $10\,\mathrm{W}$ lamp and a $40\,\mathrm{W}$ lamp both operate at their ratings. For how many hours must the $10\,\mathrm{W}$ lamp run to use as much energy as the $40\,\mathrm{W}$ lamp does in $30\,\mathrm{min}$?

- $2\,\mathrm{h}$
- $4\,\mathrm{h}$
- $0.125\,\mathrm{h}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{h}$

## Explanation

The $40\,\mathrm{W}$ lamp uses $20\,\mathrm{Wh}$ in half an hour. At $10\,\mathrm{W}$ it takes $2\,\mathrm{h}$ to use the same $20\,\mathrm{Wh}$.
