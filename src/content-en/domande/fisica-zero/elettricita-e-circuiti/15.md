A desk lamp is powered by an ideal constant-voltage source and has a constant resistance. Which situation is described by the steady-state model?

- The exact instant the switch is closed
- The lamp after ten minutes of operation, with stable current and voltage
- The brief changes just after the switch is closed
- The instant the switch is opened to turn it off

## Explanation

A circuit is in a steady state when currents and voltages keep the same value over time. While the switch is being closed or opened, and during the brief changes that follow closing, these quantities change, so those instants are excluded from the model. Ten minutes after the lamp is switched on, the current and the voltage are stable, so the lamp is **in a steady state**.
