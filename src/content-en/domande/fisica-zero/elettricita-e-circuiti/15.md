Two resistors of $4$ ohms and $8$ ohms are connected in series. What is their equivalent resistance?

- $\dfrac{8}{3}\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $32\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $12\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $4\,\mathrm{\Omega}$

## Explanation

Resistors in series carry the same current. Their voltages add, so their resistances also add. This gives $R_\mathrm{eq}=4+8=12$ ohms.
