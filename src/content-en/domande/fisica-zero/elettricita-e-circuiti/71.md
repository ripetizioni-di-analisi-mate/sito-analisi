A rechargeable cell can supply $600\,\mathrm{mAh}$ of usable charge before cutoff. A sensor draws a constant $30\,\mathrm{mA}$. Neglecting other losses, how long can it operate?

- $18000\,\mathrm{h}$
- $0.05\,\mathrm{h}$
- $2\,\mathrm{h}$
- $20\,\mathrm{h}$

## Explanation

A milliampere-hour is current times time. Dividing the available $600\,\mathrm{mAh}$ by $30\,\mathrm{mA}$ leaves $20\,\mathrm{h}$.
