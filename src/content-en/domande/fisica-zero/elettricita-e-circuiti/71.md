The heater in a heated glove has two elements, of $4\,\Omega$ and $12\,\Omega$, connected in parallel. A total current of $2\,\mathrm{A}$ passes through them. What is the voltage across the parallel combination?

- $6\,\mathrm{V}$
- $32\,\mathrm{V}$
- $8\,\mathrm{V}$
- $24\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

The equivalent resistance of the parallel combination is $\dfrac{4\cdot12}{4+12}\,\Omega=\dfrac{48}{16}\,\Omega=3\,\Omega$. The total current of $2\,\mathrm{A}$ passes through the equivalent resistor, so the voltage across the combination is
$$
V=R_{\mathrm{eq}}I=3\,\Omega\cdot2\,\mathrm{A}=6\,\mathrm{V}.
$$
Indeed, with $6\,\mathrm{V}$ the branches carry $1.5\,\mathrm{A}$ and $0.5\,\mathrm{A}$, which add up to $2\,\mathrm{A}$.
