In a kitchen, someone wants to complete the circuit between a battery terminal and a bulb using an object at hand. With which object does the bulb light up?

- A plastic straw
- A wooden toothpick
- A steel spoon
- A rubber band

## Explanation

For a current to flow, the path must be closed by conducting materials. Steel is a metal, so it contains electrons that can move, and the **spoon** completes the circuit. Plastic, dry wood, and rubber are insulators, so they impede the movement of charges, and with them the bulb stays off.
