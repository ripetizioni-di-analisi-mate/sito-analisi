A device draws a constant power of $4$ W for $20$ s. How much energy does it absorb?

- $0.2\,\mathrm{J}$
- $80\,\mathrm{J}$
- $24\,\mathrm{J}$
- $5\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

At constant power, the energy is $E=P\Delta t$. Multiply $4$ by $20$. The energy absorbed is therefore $80$ joules.
