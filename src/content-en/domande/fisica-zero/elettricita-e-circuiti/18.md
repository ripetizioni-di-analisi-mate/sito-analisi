A neutral grain becomes charged solely by losing 5 electrons. If the elementary charge magnitude is $1.6\cdot 10^{-19}$ coulombs, what charge does the grain acquire?

- $+3.2\cdot 10^{-20}\,\mathrm{C}$
- $-8.0\cdot 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}$
- $+8.0\cdot 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}$
- $+5.0\cdot 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

Losing one electron raises the net charge by one elementary charge. Multiplying by five gives $+8.0\cdot 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}$.
