Two ideal batteries are both rated for $2\,\mathrm{Ah}$ of charge. One delivers $3\,\mathrm{V}$ and the other a steady $6\,\mathrm{V}$ throughout discharge. How do their available energies compare?

- The $3\,\mathrm{V}$ battery offers twice the energy.
- The $6\,\mathrm{V}$ battery offers twice the energy.
- Their energies are equal because their charges are equal.
- Both have $2\,\mathrm{J}$ of energy.

## Explanation

Energy equals charge times voltage. At equal available charge, doubling voltage doubles the energy from $6\,\mathrm{Wh}$ to $12\,\mathrm{Wh}$.
