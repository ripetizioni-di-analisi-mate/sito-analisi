## Explanation

The type of connection depends only on the points to which the components are connected. Resistor $R_1$ has one end connected to $A$ and the other to $B$. Resistor $R_2$ also has one end connected to $A$, by a short wire, and the other connected to $B$, by a long wire that runs along the bottom of the drawing. In ideal wires length does not matter, so the two resistors have their ends connected to the same two points and are **in parallel**.
