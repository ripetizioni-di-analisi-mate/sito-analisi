The graph shows the charge $q$ passing through a cross section of the cable of a $12\,\mathrm{V}$ portable refrigerator as a function of time $t$. What is the current in the cable?

::grafico{f="2*x" xmin=0 xmax=8 ymin=0 ymax=16 nomi="t,q" legenda=0 punti="5,10,pieno" segmenti="5,0,5,10;0,10,5,10" titolo="Charge $q$ in coulombs as a function of time $t$ in seconds"}

- $2\,\mathrm{A}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{A}$
- $10\,\mathrm{A}$
- $50\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

The graph is a straight line through the origin, so the charge increases by the same amount every second and the current is constant. From the marked point, $10\,\mathrm{C}$ pass in $5\,\mathrm{s}$. Therefore
$$
I=\frac{10\,\mathrm{C}}{5\,\mathrm{s}}=2\,\mathrm{A}.
$$
