An ideal voltmeter is connected across an otherwise disconnected ideal $9\,\mathrm{V}$ battery. Which pair gives its reading and the current it draws?

- $9\,\mathrm{V}$, $9\,\mathrm{A}$
- $9\,\mathrm{V}$, $0\,\mathrm{A}$
- $0\,\mathrm{V}$, $0\,\mathrm{A}$
- $0\,\mathrm{V}$, $9\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

An ideal battery maintains its terminal voltage even in an open circuit. An ideal voltmeter has infinite resistance, so it reads $9\,\mathrm{V}$ while drawing no current.
