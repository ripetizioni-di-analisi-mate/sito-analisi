Two resistors of $5$ ohms and $10$ ohms are connected in series. What is their equivalent resistance?

- $\dfrac{10}{3}\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $50\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $5\,\mathrm{\Omega}$
- $15\,\mathrm{\Omega}$

## Explanation

Resistors in series carry the same current. Their voltages add, so their resistances also add. This gives $R_\mathrm{eq}=5+10=15$ ohms.
