Two identical resistors are available to make the largest and then the smallest possible equivalent resistance, using both each time. What is the ratio of the largest to the smallest?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $4$
- $1$
- $2$

## Explanation

Series gives $2R$ while parallel gives $\dfrac{R}{2}$. The ratio of those values is four.
