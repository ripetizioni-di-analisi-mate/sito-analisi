A balloon and a child's hair are initially neutral. When the balloon is rubbed on the hair, some electrons move from the hair to the balloon. What net charge does the hair then have?

- Negative
- Zero
- Positive
- Positive or negative, depending on how many electrons moved

## Explanation

The hair was neutral, so its positive and negative charges added up to zero. During rubbing, the hair loses electrons, which carry negative charge. The sum of its charges therefore becomes positive, and the hair acquires a **positive** net charge. The balloon, which received those electrons, has a negative net charge instead.
