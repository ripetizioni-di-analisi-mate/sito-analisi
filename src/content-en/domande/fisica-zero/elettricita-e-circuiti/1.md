A charge of $18$ C passes through a cross section of a wire in $3$ s. What is the average current?

- $6\,\mathrm{A}$
- $54\,\mathrm{A}$
- $18\,\mathrm{A}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

The average current is the charge transferred divided by the time. This gives $I=\dfrac{18}{3}$. The current is therefore $6$ amperes.
