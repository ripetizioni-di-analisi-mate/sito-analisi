Two small objects have measured charges of $-4\,\mathrm{\mu C}$ and $+9\,\mathrm{\mu C}$. What is the total charge of the two-object system?

- $-5\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $+5\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $-13\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $+13\,\mathrm{\mu C}$

## Explanation

Charges add with their signs. The sum is $-4+9=+5$ microcoulombs, so the net charge is positive.
