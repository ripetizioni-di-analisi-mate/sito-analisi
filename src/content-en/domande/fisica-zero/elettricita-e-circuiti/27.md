A device draws a constant power of $5$ W for $20$ s. How much energy does it absorb?

- $0.25\,\mathrm{J}$
- $25\,\mathrm{J}$
- $100\,\mathrm{J}$
- $4\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

At constant power, the energy is $E=P\Delta t$. Multiply $5$ by $20$. The energy absorbed is therefore $100$ joules.
