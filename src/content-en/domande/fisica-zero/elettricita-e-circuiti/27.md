Nodes A and B are directly connected by an ideal wire carrying current. What does an ideal voltmeter connected from A to B read?

- A voltage increasing with the length of the ideal wire.
- $0\,\mathrm{V}$
- The full battery voltage in every circuit.
- A reading expressed in amperes.

## Explanation

An ideal wire has zero resistance, so its ends are at the same potential even while carrying current. Their voltage difference is $0\,\mathrm{V}$.
