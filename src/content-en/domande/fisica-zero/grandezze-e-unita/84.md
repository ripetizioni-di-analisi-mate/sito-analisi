A vehicle uses $6\,\mathrm{L}$ per $100\,\mathrm{km}$. What is its consumption in millilitres per kilometre?

- $600\,\mathrm{mL/km}$
- $6\,\mathrm{mL/km}$
- $60\,\mathrm{mL/km}$
- $0.06\,\mathrm{mL/km}$

## Explanation

Six litres is $6000\,\mathrm{mL}$ per hundred kilometres. Divide by one hundred to express consumption per kilometre.
