A hair has a diameter of $0.00007\,\mathrm{m}$. How is this value written in scientific notation?

- $7\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $7\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $7\cdot10^{5}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $7\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The coefficient, between $1$ and $10$, is $7$. Going from $7$ to $0.00007$ moves the decimal point five places to the left, which means dividing by $10^5$. Dividing by $10^5$ is the same as multiplying by $10^{-5}$. Therefore, the diameter of the hair is $7\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{m}$.
