A tank has inside dimensions of $20\,\mathrm{cm}$, $15\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $8\,\mathrm{cm}$. How many litres does it hold when filled to the rim?

- $0.24\,\mathrm{L}$
- $240\,\mathrm{L}$
- $24\,\mathrm{L}$
- $2.4\,\mathrm{L}$

## Explanation

The inside volume is $20\cdot 15\cdot 8=2400\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. Each litre occupies $1000\,\mathrm{cm^3}$, giving a capacity of $2.4\,\mathrm{L}$.
