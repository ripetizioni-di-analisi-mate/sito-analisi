An area measures $4$ square meters. What is its value in square centimeters?

- $40000\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $400\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $4000\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $4000000\,\mathrm{cm^2}$

## Explanation

Each side of a one-meter square measures $100$ centimeters. Its area is therefore $100^2=10000$ square centimeters. An area of $4$ square meters equals $40000$ square centimeters.
