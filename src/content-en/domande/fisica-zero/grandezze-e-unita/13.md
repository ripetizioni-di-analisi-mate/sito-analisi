The label on a phone charger reads $2\,\mathrm{A}$. Which quantity does this value describe?

- Luminous intensity
- Amount of substance
- Electric current
- Thermodynamic temperature

## Explanation

In the International System of Units, the symbol $\mathrm{A}$ stands for the ampere. The ampere is the base unit of electric current. Luminous intensity is measured in candelas, amount of substance in moles, and thermodynamic temperature in kelvins. Therefore, $2\,\mathrm{A}$ describes **an electric current**.
