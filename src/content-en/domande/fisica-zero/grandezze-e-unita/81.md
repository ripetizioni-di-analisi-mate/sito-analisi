In a table of materials, each row gives the density of a substance. Which of the following units is a unit of density?

- $\mathrm{g\cdot cm^3}$
- $\mathrm{cm^3/g}$
- $\mathrm{kg/m^2}$
- $\mathrm{g/cm^3}$

## Explanation

Density is the ratio of a mass to a volume, $\rho=\dfrac{m}{V}$. Its unit is therefore a unit of mass divided by a unit of volume. Of the options given, only $\mathrm{g/cm^3}$ has this form. The unit $\mathrm{kg/m^2}$ has an area in the denominator, while $\mathrm{cm^3/g}$ is the unit of the inverse ratio, a volume divided by a mass.
