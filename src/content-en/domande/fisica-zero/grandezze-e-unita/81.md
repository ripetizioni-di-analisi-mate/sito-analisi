A belt travels at $18\,\mathrm{km/h}$. How many metres does it cover in one second at this constant speed?

- $5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $64.8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

In an hour the belt covers $18000\,\mathrm{m}$, and an hour contains $3600\,\mathrm{s}$. In one second it covers $\dfrac{18000}{3600}=5\,\mathrm{m}$.
