A tablet contains $250\,\mathrm{mg}$ of active ingredient. How many grams is that?

- $2.5\,\mathrm{g}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{g}$
- $25\,\mathrm{g}$
- $0.025\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

Since $1\,\mathrm{g}=1000\,\mathrm{mg}$, one milligram is one thousandth of a gram. Therefore, $250\,\mathrm{mg}=\dfrac{250}{1000}\,\mathrm{g}=0.25\,\mathrm{g}$. The tablet contains a quarter of a gram of active ingredient.
