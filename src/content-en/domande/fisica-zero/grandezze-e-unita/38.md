A time interval lasts $7$ minutes. How long is it in seconds?

- $700\,\mathrm{s}$
- $420\,\mathrm{s}$
- $7\,\mathrm{s}$
- $\dfrac{7}{60}\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

One minute contains $60$ seconds. Multiply the number of minutes by $60$. The duration is therefore $420$ seconds.
