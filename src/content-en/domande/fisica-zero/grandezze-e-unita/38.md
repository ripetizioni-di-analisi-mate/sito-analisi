Two rectangles measure $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ by $8\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $4\,\mathrm{cm}$ by $4\,\mathrm{cm}$. Which requires more wire to go once around it?

- The first, by $4\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- The second, by $4\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- The first, by $8\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- They require the same length.

## Explanation

The perimeters are $2(2+8)=20\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $2(4+4)=16\,\mathrm{cm}$. Equal areas do not imply equal perimeters.
