An empty tank receives $5\,\mathrm{L/min}$ and loses $2\,\mathrm{L/min}$ from the start. If both rates stay constant, after how many minutes will it contain $18\,\mathrm{L}$?

- $9\,\mathrm{min}$
- $6\,\mathrm{min}$
- $3.6\,\mathrm{min}$
- $2.57\,\mathrm{min}$

## Explanation

Each minute the tank gains $5-2=3\,\mathrm{L}$. The required time is $\dfrac{18}{3}=6$ minutes.
