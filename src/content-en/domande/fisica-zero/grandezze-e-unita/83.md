The average distance between Earth and the Moon is about $384\,000\,\mathrm{km}$. How is it written in scientific notation?

- $3.84\cdot10^3\,\mathrm{km}$
- $3.84\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{km}$
- $3.84\cdot10^4\,\mathrm{km}$
- $3.84\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{km}$

## Explanation

In scientific notation the coefficient is at least $1$ and less than $10$, so the coefficient is $3.84$. Going from $3.84$ to $384\,000$ moves the decimal point five places to the right, which means multiplying by $10^5$. Therefore, the distance is written as $3.84\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{km}$.
