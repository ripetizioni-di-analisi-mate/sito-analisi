An object with a mass of $36$ kg has a density of $3$ kilograms per cubic meter. What is its volume?

- $108\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{12}\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $36\,\mathrm{m^3}$

## Explanation

Solving the density formula for volume gives $V=\dfrac{m}{\rho}$. Substituting the given values gives $V=\dfrac{36}{3}$. The volume is therefore $12$ cubic meters.
