A box can carry up to $1.6\,\mathrm{kg}$ of contents and can fit at most five jars. Each full jar has mass $450\,\mathrm{g}$. How many can you pack within both limits?

- $3$
- $2$
- $5$
- $4$

## Explanation

Three jars have a mass of $1350\,\mathrm{g}$, while four have a mass of $1800\,\mathrm{g}$. The $1600\,\mathrm{g}$ load restriction takes effect before the space restriction.
