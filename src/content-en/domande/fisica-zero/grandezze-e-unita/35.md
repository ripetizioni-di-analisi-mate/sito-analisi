To get to work, a person takes $0.75\,\mathrm{h}$ by bus or $50\,\mathrm{min}$ by bike. How many more minutes does the bike take?

- $25\,\mathrm{min}$
- $49.25\,\mathrm{min}$
- $5\,\mathrm{min}$
- $15\,\mathrm{min}$

## Explanation

To compare the two durations, express both in minutes. Since an hour contains $60$ minutes, $0.75\,\mathrm{h}=0.75\cdot60\,\mathrm{min}=45\,\mathrm{min}$. The bike therefore takes $50\,\mathrm{min}-45\,\mathrm{min}=5\,\mathrm{min}$ longer. Comparing only the numbers $0.75$ and $50$, without their units, would not describe the two durations.
