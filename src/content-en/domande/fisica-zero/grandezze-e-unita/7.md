To measure one side of a table without a tape measure, a rod $0.5\,\mathrm{m}$ long is used. The rod fits along the side exactly four and a half times. How long is the side?

- $2.25\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Measuring the side means counting how many times it contains the rod, which acts as the unit of measurement. The side contains the rod $4.5$ times, so its length is $4.5$ times the length of the rod. Therefore,
$$
\ell=4.5\cdot0.5\,\mathrm{m}=2.25\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
The number $4.5$ counts the rods that fit along the side, so it is multiplied by the length of the rod to obtain meters.
