A material has a density of $800$ kilograms per cubic meter. What is the mass of $3$ cubic meters of this material?

- $\dfrac{800}{3}\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.00375\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $2400\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $240\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Rearranging $\rho=\dfrac{m}{V}$ gives $m=\rho V$. Therefore, multiply $800$ by $3$. The mass is $2400$ kilograms.
