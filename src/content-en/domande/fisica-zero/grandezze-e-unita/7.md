A container has an internal volume of $0.007\,\mathrm{m^3}$. How many cubic centimetres does it contain?

- $70000\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $7000\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $70\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $700\,\mathrm{cm^3}$

## Explanation

Each of the three dimensions gains a factor of $100$ when expressed in centimetres. The volume is $0.007\cdot 100^3=7000\,\mathrm{cm^3}$.
