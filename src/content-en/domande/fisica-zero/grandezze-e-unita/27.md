A wheel completes $5$ turns without slipping as it travels $3.5\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the distance around its rim?

- $70\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $17.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $350\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $175\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Without slipping, the wheel advances by one circumference per turn. Divide $3.5\,\mathrm{m}$ by five, then express $0.7\,\mathrm{m}$ as $70\,\mathrm{cm}$.
