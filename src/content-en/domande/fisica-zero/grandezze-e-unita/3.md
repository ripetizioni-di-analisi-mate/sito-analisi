A scale can support $2\,\mathrm{kg}$. With an $1850\,\mathrm{g}$ parcel on it, how many more grams can be added?

- $850\,\mathrm{g}$
- $1850\,\mathrm{g}$
- $150\,\mathrm{g}$
- $350\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

Express the capacity in grams, $2\,\mathrm{kg}=2000\,\mathrm{g}$. The remaining capacity is $2000-1850=150\,\mathrm{g}$.
