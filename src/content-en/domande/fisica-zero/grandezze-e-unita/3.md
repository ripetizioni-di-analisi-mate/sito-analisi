A mass is $750$ g. What is its value in kilograms?

- $750\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.075\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.75\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

One kilogram contains $1000$ grams. To convert grams to kilograms, divide by $1000$. The mass is therefore $0.75$ kilograms.
