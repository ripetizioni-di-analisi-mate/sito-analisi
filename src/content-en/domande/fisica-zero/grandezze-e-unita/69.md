An open faucet delivers $12\,\mathrm{L}$ of water per minute. How many minutes does it take to fill a $0.06\,\mathrm{m^3}$ bucket?

- $5\,\mathrm{min}$
- $50\,\mathrm{min}$
- $0.005\,\mathrm{min}$
- $0.72\,\mathrm{min}$

## Explanation

First, express the volume of the bucket in liters. Since $1\,\mathrm{m^3}=1000\,\mathrm{L}$, we have $0.06\,\mathrm{m^3}=60\,\mathrm{L}$. Each minute $12\,\mathrm{L}$ flows in, so the time needed is $\dfrac{60}{12}\,\mathrm{min}=5\,\mathrm{min}$.
