A vessel holding $20\,\mathrm{mL}$ of oil has a total mass of $68\,\mathrm{g}$. With $50\,\mathrm{mL}$ of the same oil, its total mass is $92\,\mathrm{g}$. What is the oil's density?

- $3.4\,\mathrm{g/mL}$
- $1.84\,\mathrm{g/mL}$
- $0.8\,\mathrm{g/mL}$
- $1.25\,\mathrm{g/mL}$

## Explanation

Subtracting the readings eliminates the vessel's mass. The change of $30\,\mathrm{mL}$ corresponds to $24\,\mathrm{g}$, so $\rho =\dfrac{24}{30}=0.8\,\mathrm{g/mL}$.
