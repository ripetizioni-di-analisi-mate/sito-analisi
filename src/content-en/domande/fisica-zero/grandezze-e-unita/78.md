The graph shows the mass of samples of two uniform materials, A and B, as a function of their volume. Which statement is true?

::grafico{curve="t,2*t,0,4.5;t,0.5*t,0,5.5" xmin=-0.5 xmax=5.5 ymin=-0.5 ymax=9 nomi="V,m" legenda=0 punti="3,6,pieno;4,2,pieno" segmenti="3,0,3,6;0,6,3,6;4,0,4,2;0,2,4,2" etichette="3.7,8,A;4.8,1.6,B" titolo="Mass m in grams as a function of volume V in cubic centimeters, for materials A and B."}

- B has a greater density than A
- A and B have the same density
- A has a greater density than B
- The density of A increases with the volume of the sample

## Explanation

For each material, the density is the ratio of mass to volume, that is, the mass of one cubic centimeter. From the graph, a sample of A with $V=3\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ has a mass of $6\,\mathrm{g}$, so $\rho_A=2\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. A sample of B with $V=4\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ has a mass of $2\,\mathrm{g}$, so $\rho_B=0.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. Therefore, **A has a greater density** than B, and its line is steeper. Along each line the ratio $\dfrac{m}{V}$ stays constant, so the density does not depend on the volume of the sample.
