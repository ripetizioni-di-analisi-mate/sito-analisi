A pump delivers exactly $12$ drops per minute. Operating continuously, how many minutes does it take to deliver $90$ drops at this average rate?

- $9\,\mathrm{min}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{min}$
- $6\,\mathrm{min}$
- $12.5\,\mathrm{min}$

## Explanation

Each minute corresponds to twelve drops. The time for ninety drops is the ratio $\dfrac{90}{12}=7.5$ minutes.
