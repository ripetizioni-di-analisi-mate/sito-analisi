An area measures $6$ square meters. What is its value in square centimeters?

- $600\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $6000\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $60000\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $6000000\,\mathrm{cm^2}$

## Explanation

Each side of a one-meter square measures $100$ centimeters. Its area is therefore $100^2=10000$ square centimeters. An area of $6$ square meters equals $60000$ square centimeters.
