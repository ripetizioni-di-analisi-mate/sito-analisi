A ready meal must be heated in the microwave for $3\,\mathrm{min}$ and $20\,\mathrm{s}$. How many seconds is that?

- $200\,\mathrm{s}$
- $320\,\mathrm{s}$
- $180\,\mathrm{s}$
- $230\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

One minute contains $60$ seconds, so $3\,\mathrm{min}=3\cdot60\,\mathrm{s}=180\,\mathrm{s}$. The remaining $20\,\mathrm{s}$ is added to this. Therefore, the total time is $180\,\mathrm{s}+20\,\mathrm{s}=200\,\mathrm{s}$.
