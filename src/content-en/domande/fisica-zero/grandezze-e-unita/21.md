On a worksheet $a$ denotes a length and $S$ an area. Which expression can represent another length?

- $\dfrac{a}{S}$
- $\dfrac{S}{a}$
- $S\cdot a$
- $S+a$

## Explanation

Dividing area by length leaves length, for example $\dfrac{\mathrm{m^2}}{\mathrm{m}}=\mathrm{m}$. Addition would require quantities of the same dimension.
