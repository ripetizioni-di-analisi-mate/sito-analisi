A mass is $1250$ g. What is its value in kilograms?

- $1.25\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $1250\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.125\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $12.5\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

One kilogram contains $1000$ grams. To convert grams to kilograms, divide by $1000$. The mass is therefore $1.25$ kilograms.
