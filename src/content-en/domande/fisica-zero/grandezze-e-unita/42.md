An object has a mass of $14$ kg and a volume of $7$ cubic meters. What is its density?

- $98\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $2\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $7\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

Density is the ratio of mass to volume. Substituting gives $\rho=\dfrac{14}{7}$. The density is therefore $2$ kilograms per cubic meter.
