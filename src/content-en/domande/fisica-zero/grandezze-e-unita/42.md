An empty bowl placed on a kitchen scale gives a reading of $180\,\mathrm{g}$. After some sugar is poured into the bowl, the scale reads $615\,\mathrm{g}$. What is the mass of the sugar?

- $795\,\mathrm{g}$
- $615\,\mathrm{g}$
- $435\,\mathrm{g}$
- $535\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

The second reading includes both the mass of the bowl and the mass of the sugar. The mass of the bowl is given by the first reading, so the mass of the sugar is the difference between the two readings. Therefore, $m=615\,\mathrm{g}-180\,\mathrm{g}=435\,\mathrm{g}$. This is an indirect measurement, obtained from two direct measurements read on the scale.
