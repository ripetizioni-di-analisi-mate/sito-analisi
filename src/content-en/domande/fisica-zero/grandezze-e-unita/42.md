One litre of paint covers $8\,\mathrm{m^2}$. How many litres are needed for two coats on $12\,\mathrm{m^2}$, assuming the same coverage per coat?

- $6\,\mathrm{L}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{L}$
- $3\,\mathrm{L}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{L}$

## Explanation

Two coats are equivalent to covering $24\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Divide the total area by the coverage, $\dfrac{24}{8}=3$ litres.
