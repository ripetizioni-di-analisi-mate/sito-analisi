To measure the volume of a dry sponge, it is completely submerged in a graduated cylinder containing water. The sponge absorbs some of the water. How does the volume obtained from the difference between the final and initial readings compare with the true volume?

- It is less than the true volume of the sponge
- It is greater than the true volume of the sponge
- It is equal to the true volume of the sponge
- It is equal to the initial volume of water

## Explanation

The immersion method requires that the object not absorb water. The sponge contains empty spaces that fill with water from the cylinder once it is submerged. This water moves into the sponge, so the level rises less than it would for a solid object of the same volume. Therefore, the difference between the readings is **less than the true volume** of the sponge.
