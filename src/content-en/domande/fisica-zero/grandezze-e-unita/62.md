What volume is occupied by a solid block with mass $156\,\mathrm{g}$ and density $7.8\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$?

- $200\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $20\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $1216.8\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $0.05\,\mathrm{cm^3}$

## Explanation

Each cubic centimetre has mass $7.8\,\mathrm{g}$. The number of cubic centimetres in the block is $\dfrac{156}{7.8}=20$.
