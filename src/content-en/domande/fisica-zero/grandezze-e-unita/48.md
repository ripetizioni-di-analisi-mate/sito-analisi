A uniform coating has volume $12\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ and covers $300\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. How thick is the layer in millimetres?

- $0.4\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $4\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.04\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

Divide volume by area to obtain $\dfrac{12}{300}=0.04\,\mathrm{cm}$. Multiplying by ten gives $0.4\,\mathrm{mm}$.
