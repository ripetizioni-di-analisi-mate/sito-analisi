You want to turn $7\,\mathrm{km}$ into metres by multiplying by a single factor equal to one. Which ratio should you use?

- $\dfrac{1\,\mathrm{m}}{1000\,\mathrm{km}}$
- $\dfrac{1000\,\mathrm{km}}{1\,\mathrm{m}}$
- $\dfrac{1000\,\mathrm{m}}{1\,\mathrm{km}}$
- $\dfrac{1\,\mathrm{km}}{1000\,\mathrm{m}}$

## Explanation

Put the starting unit in the denominator so it cancels. The numerator must be equivalent to one kilometre, so it is $1000\,\mathrm{m}$.
