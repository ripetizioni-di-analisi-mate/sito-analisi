The rectangular top of a desk is measured in decimeters, and its length and width are multiplied. In which unit is the resulting area?

- $\mathrm{dm}$
- $\mathrm{dm^2}$
- $\mathrm{dm^3}$
- $\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

The area of a rectangle is the product of two lengths expressed in the same unit. Multiplying decimeters by decimeters gives the square decimeter, so the area is in $\mathrm{dm^2}$. Expressing it in $\mathrm{m^2}$ would require a conversion, because $1\,\mathrm{m^2}=100\,\mathrm{dm^2}$. The cubic decimeter, $\mathrm{dm^3}$, is a unit of volume.
