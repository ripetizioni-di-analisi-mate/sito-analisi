An area measures $5$ square meters. What is its value in square centimeters?

- $500\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $50000\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $5000\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $5000000\,\mathrm{cm^2}$

## Explanation

Each side of a one-meter square measures $100$ centimeters. Its area is therefore $100^2=10000$ square centimeters. An area of $5$ square meters equals $50000$ square centimeters.
