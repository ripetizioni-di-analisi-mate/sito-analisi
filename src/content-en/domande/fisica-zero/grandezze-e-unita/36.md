Ten identical beads have a combined mass of $2.5\,\mathrm{g}$. How many beads of the same kind are needed for $6\,\mathrm{g}$?

- $40$
- $24$
- $15$
- $6$

## Explanation

One bead has mass $\dfrac{2.5}{10}=0.25\,\mathrm{g}$. Divide the target mass by the mass of one bead, $\dfrac{6}{0.25}=24$.
