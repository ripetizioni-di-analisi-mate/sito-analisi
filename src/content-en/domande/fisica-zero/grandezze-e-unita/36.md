A length is $120$ mm. What is its value in centimeters?

- $1200\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $120\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

One centimeter contains $10$ millimeters. Therefore, divide the numerical value by $10$. The length is $12$ centimeters.
