A worksheet proposes four formulas using only density $\rho $ and length $l$. Which expression has the dimensions of mass?

- $\dfrac{l^3}{\rho} $
- $\dfrac{\rho}{l}$
- $\rho  l^3$
- $\rho  l^2$

## Explanation

Density has dimensions of mass divided by length cubed. Multiplication by $l^3$ cancels the three powers of length and leaves mass.
