The mass of a van is given in four different forms. Which of them is in scientific notation?

- $42\cdot10^2\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $4.2\cdot10^3\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.42\cdot10^4\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $420\cdot10^1\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

All four forms equal $4200\,\mathrm{kg}$. A number is in scientific notation when it has the form $a\cdot10^n$, where $a$ is at least $1$ and less than $10$ and $n$ is an integer. The coefficients $42$ and $420$ are greater than $10$, while $0.42$ is less than $1$. Therefore, the only form in scientific notation is $4.2\cdot10^3\,\mathrm{kg}$.
