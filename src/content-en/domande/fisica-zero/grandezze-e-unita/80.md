One event lasts $6\cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{s}$ and another lasts $6\cdot 10^{-9}\,\mathrm{s}$. How many orders of magnitude separate the durations?

- $3$
- $6$
- $9$
- $12$

## Explanation

The coefficients are equal, so the ratio is $10^{-3-(-9)}=10^6$. A factor of ten to the sixth power corresponds to six orders of magnitude.
