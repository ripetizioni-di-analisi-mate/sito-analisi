Measurements of a brick give a mass of $2.4\,\mathrm{kg}$ and a volume of $1.5\,\mathrm{dm^3}$. What is the average density of the brick in $\mathrm{kg/m^3}$?

- $1.6\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $160\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $1600\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $3600\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

Since $1\,\mathrm{m^3}=1000\,\mathrm{dm^3}$, the volume of the brick is $1.5\,\mathrm{dm^3}=0.0015\,\mathrm{m^3}$. The average density is the ratio of mass to volume. Therefore,
$$
\rho=\frac{2.4\,\mathrm{kg}}{0.0015\,\mathrm{m^3}}=1600\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}.
$$
The quotient $\dfrac{2.4}{1.5}=1.6$ is the density in $\mathrm{kg/dm^3}$, and multiplying it by $1000$ gives the same result.
