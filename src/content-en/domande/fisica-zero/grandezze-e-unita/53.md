A closed box has an outer side of $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ and walls $1\,\mathrm{cm}$ thick on all six faces. What is the volume of its cubic cavity?

- $8\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $1331\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $1728\,\mathrm{cm^3}$

## Explanation

Subtract two wall thicknesses in each dimension, $12-2\cdot 1=10\,\mathrm{cm}$. The cavity therefore has volume $10^3=1000\,\mathrm{cm^3}$.
