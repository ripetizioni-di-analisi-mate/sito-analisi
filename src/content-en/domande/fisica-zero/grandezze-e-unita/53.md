A camera manufacturer states that the sensor has an area of $864\,\mathrm{mm^2}$. How many square centimeters is that?

- $86.4\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $0.864\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $8.64\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $8640\,\mathrm{cm^2}$

## Explanation

Since $1\,\mathrm{cm}=10\,\mathrm{mm}$, a square with sides $1\,\mathrm{cm}$ long has an area of $10\,\mathrm{mm}\cdot10\,\mathrm{mm}=100\,\mathrm{mm^2}$. Therefore, $1\,\mathrm{cm^2}=100\,\mathrm{mm^2}$, and converting to square centimeters requires dividing by $100$. The area of the sensor is therefore $\dfrac{864}{100}\,\mathrm{cm^2}=8.64\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. Dividing by $10$ would account for the length factor only once, while for an area it appears twice.
