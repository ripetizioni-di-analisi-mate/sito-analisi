You know only that a rectangular floor covers $6\,\mathrm{m^2}$. To determine exactly how many whole $20\,\mathrm{cm}$ square tiles fit in rows parallel to the edges without cutting, what extra information is needed?

- The same floor area expressed in square centimetres.
- The perimeter of one tile.
- The length of at least one side of the floor.
- The combined area of both faces of a tile.

## Explanation

One side and the area determine the other side. Count how many whole tiles fit along each side and multiply the counts, excluding leftover strips.
