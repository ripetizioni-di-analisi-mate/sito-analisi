A water tank holds $1.2\cdot10^4\,\mathrm{L}$ of water. How many cubic meters is that?

- $12\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $120\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $12\,000\,\mathrm{m^3}$

## Explanation

The expression $1.2\cdot10^4$ means $1.2\cdot10\,000=12\,000$, so the tank holds $12\,000\,\mathrm{L}$. One cubic meter contains $1000\,\mathrm{L}$. Therefore, the volume is $\dfrac{12\,000}{1000}\,\mathrm{m^3}=12\,\mathrm{m^3}$.
