A person runs $2.5\,\mathrm{km}$ and then walks another $800\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the total distance in kilometers?

- $10.5\,\mathrm{km}$
- $2.58\,\mathrm{km}$
- $802.5\,\mathrm{km}$
- $3.3\,\mathrm{km}$

## Explanation

The two distances are added after expressing them in the same unit. Since $1\,\mathrm{km}=1000\,\mathrm{m}$, we have $800\,\mathrm{m}=0.8\,\mathrm{km}$. The total distance is therefore $2.5\,\mathrm{km}+0.8\,\mathrm{km}=3.3\,\mathrm{km}$.
