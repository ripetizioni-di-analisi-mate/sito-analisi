A feature measures $0.000004\,\mathrm{m}$. Which unit gives it a numerical value between one and ten?

- Nanometre
- Millimetre
- Centimetre
- Micrometre

## Explanation

A micrometre is $10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m}$. Dividing $4\cdot 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m}$ by that unit gives the number $4$.
