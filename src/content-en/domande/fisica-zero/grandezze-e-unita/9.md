A table is measured with a ruler, giving a reading of $152\,\mathrm{cm}$; this is then written as $1.52\,\mathrm{m}$ using $1\,\mathrm{m}=100\,\mathrm{cm}$. Which statement is true?

- The factor $100$ and the reading $152\,\mathrm{cm}$ are both exact
- The factor $100$ has limited precision, while the reading $152\,\mathrm{cm}$ is exact
- The factor $100$ and the reading $152\,\mathrm{cm}$ both have limited precision
- The factor $100$ is exact, while the reading $152\,\mathrm{cm}$ has limited precision

## Explanation

The relationship $1\,\mathrm{m}=100\,\mathrm{cm}$ follows from the meaning of the prefix centi, so the factor $100$ is exact. The reading on the ruler, however, depends on the instrument and on how it is lined up with the edge of the table. Therefore, the measurement $152\,\mathrm{cm}$ has limited precision. Converting to meters changes how the value is written, but it does not make the measurement more precise.
