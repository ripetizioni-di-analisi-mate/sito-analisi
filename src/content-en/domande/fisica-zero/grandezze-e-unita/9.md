A sample has mass $75\,\mathrm{g}$ and volume $30\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. What is its mean density?

- $2.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $0.4\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $45\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $105\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$

## Explanation

Density is mass per unit volume. Divide $75$ by $30$ to obtain $2.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.
