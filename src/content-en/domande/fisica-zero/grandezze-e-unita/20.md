A time interval lasts $5$ minutes. How long is it in seconds?

- $500\,\mathrm{s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $\dfrac{1}{12}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $300\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

One minute contains $60$ seconds. Multiply the number of minutes by $60$. The duration is therefore $300$ seconds.
