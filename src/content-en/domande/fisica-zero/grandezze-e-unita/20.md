Three pieces, each $60\,\mathrm{cm}$ long, are cut from a $10\,\mathrm{m}$ roll of tape. How much tape is left on the roll?

- $9.4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8.2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $9.82\,\mathrm{m}$
- $7\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Together the three pieces are $3\cdot60\,\mathrm{cm}=180\,\mathrm{cm}$ long. Since $100\,\mathrm{cm}=1\,\mathrm{m}$, we have $180\,\mathrm{cm}=1.8\,\mathrm{m}$. Therefore, $10\,\mathrm{m}-1.8\,\mathrm{m}=8.2\,\mathrm{m}$ of tape is left on the roll.
