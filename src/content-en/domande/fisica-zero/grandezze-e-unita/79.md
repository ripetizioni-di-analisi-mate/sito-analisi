A square face has side $3\cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m}$. What is its area in square metres?

- $9\cdot 10^{-8}\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $6\cdot 10^{-8}\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $3\cdot 10^{-8}\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $9\cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

Square both the coefficient and the power of ten. This gives $3^2\cdot 10^{2(-4)}=9\cdot 10^{-8}\,\mathrm{m^2}$.
