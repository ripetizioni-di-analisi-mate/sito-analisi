A room is $5\,\mathrm{m}$ long, $4\,\mathrm{m}$ wide, and $3\,\mathrm{m}$ high. The air inside has a density of $1.2\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. What is the mass of the air in the room?

- $50\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.02\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $72\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $7.2\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The volume of the room is $V=5\cdot4\cdot3\,\mathrm{m^3}=60\,\mathrm{m^3}$. From the definition of density, $m=\rho\,V$. The mass of the air is therefore $m=1.2\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}\cdot60\,\mathrm{m^3}=72\,\mathrm{kg}$. The air in the room thus has a mass comparable to that of an adult.
