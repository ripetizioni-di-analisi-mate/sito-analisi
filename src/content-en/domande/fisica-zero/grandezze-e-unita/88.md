A product sheet lists the thickness of a notebook as $10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m}$. How many centimeters is that?

- $0.01\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $1\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $100\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.1\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

A negative exponent gives the reciprocal of the positive power, so $10^{-2}=\dfrac{1}{100}=0.01$. The notebook is therefore $0.01\,\mathrm{m}$ thick. Since $1\,\mathrm{m}=100\,\mathrm{cm}$, we have $0.01\,\mathrm{m}=0.01\cdot100\,\mathrm{cm}=1\,\mathrm{cm}$.
