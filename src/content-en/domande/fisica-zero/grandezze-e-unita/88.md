A $1\,\mathrm{L}$ vessel is filled to the rim with identical marbles, with air in the gaps. Which conclusion about the volume of glass is certain?

- It is less than $1\,\mathrm{L}$.
- It is exactly $1\,\mathrm{L}$.
- It is greater than $1\,\mathrm{L}$.
- It is exactly $0.5\,\mathrm{L}$ for every arrangement.

## Explanation

The internal space is shared by glass and air. Subtracting a positive volume of air from one litre leaves less than a litre of glass.
