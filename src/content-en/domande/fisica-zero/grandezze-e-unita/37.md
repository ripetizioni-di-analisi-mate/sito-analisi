A cake recipe calls for $350\,\mathrm{g}$ of flour. How many kilograms is that?

- $3.5\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.35\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.035\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $350\,000\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

One kilogram contains $1000$ grams, so converting from grams to kilograms requires dividing by $1000$. This gives $\dfrac{350}{1000}\,\mathrm{kg}=0.35\,\mathrm{kg}$. The numerical value decreases because the kilogram is a larger unit than the gram.
