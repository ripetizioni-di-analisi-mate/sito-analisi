Two sealed parcels occupy different volumes. What can establish which has greater mass without knowing the materials?

- Choose the parcel with the longest side.
- Choose the parcel that displaces more water when sealed and fully submerged.
- Measure the density of only one parcel and compare only the external volumes.
- Put one on each pan of an equal-arm balance.

## Explanation

An equal-arm balance compares masses directly in the same location. Dimensions alone leave the masses of the two contents undetermined.
