A kitchen scale displays masses in grams. What is the base unit of mass in the International System of Units?

- The gram
- The milligram
- The kilogram
- The metric ton

## Explanation

The International System of Units defines seven base units, and the base unit of mass is the kilogram, symbol $\mathrm{kg}$. The gram is a submultiple of the kilogram, because $1\,\mathrm{kg}=1000\,\mathrm{g}$. The scale therefore uses a unit suited to the small masses found in a kitchen, while the SI base unit is **the kilogram**.
