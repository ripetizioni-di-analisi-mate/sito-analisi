Which row gives both $560000\,\mathrm{m}$ and $0.0036\,\mathrm{kg}$ in normalised scientific notation?

- $5.6\cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{m}$, $3.6\cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $5.6\cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{m}$, $3.6\cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $5.6\cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{m}$, $3.6\cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $56\cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{m}$, $0.36\cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The normalised coefficient is at least one and less than ten. Move the decimal point five places left for the first value and three places right for the second.
