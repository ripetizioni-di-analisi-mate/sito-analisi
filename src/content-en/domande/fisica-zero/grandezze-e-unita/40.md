A shopping bag holds $1.2\,\mathrm{kg}$ of apples, $450\,\mathrm{g}$ of bread, and $800\,\mathrm{g}$ of cheese. What is the total mass in kilograms?

- $1.25\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $1251.2\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $2.45\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $24.5\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The masses are added after expressing them in the same unit. Since $1\,\mathrm{kg}=1000\,\mathrm{g}$, we have $450\,\mathrm{g}=0.45\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $800\,\mathrm{g}=0.8\,\mathrm{kg}$. The total mass is therefore $1.2\,\mathrm{kg}+0.45\,\mathrm{kg}+0.8\,\mathrm{kg}=2.45\,\mathrm{kg}$.
