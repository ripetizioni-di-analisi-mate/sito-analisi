An area measures $7$ square meters. What is its value in square centimeters?

- $700\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $7000\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $7000000\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $70000\,\mathrm{cm^2}$

## Explanation

Each side of a one-meter square measures $100$ centimeters. Its area is therefore $100^2=10000$ square centimeters. An area of $7$ square meters equals $70000$ square centimeters.
