A $50\,\mathrm{cm}$ by $40\,\mathrm{cm}$ panel has a square hole of side $10\,\mathrm{cm}$. What area remains to be painted on one face?

- $1500\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $2300\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $1900\,\mathrm{cm^2}$

## Explanation

A full face would have area $50\cdot 40=2000\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. The hole takes up $10^2=100\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, leaving $1900\,\mathrm{cm^2}$.
