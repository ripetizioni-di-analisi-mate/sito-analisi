An object with a mass of $30$ kg has a density of $3$ kilograms per cubic meter. What is its volume?

- $90\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $0.1\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $30\,\mathrm{m^3}$

## Explanation

Solving the density formula for volume gives $V=\dfrac{m}{\rho}$. Substituting the given values gives $V=\dfrac{30}{3}$. The volume is therefore $10$ cubic meters.
