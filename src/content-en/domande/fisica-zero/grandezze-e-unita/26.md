A $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ line on a plan represents a $12\,\mathrm{m}$ corridor. How many actual centimetres does one centimetre on the plan represent?

- $40\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $400\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $3600\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Twelve metres is $1200\,\mathrm{cm}$. Divide the actual length by the three centimetres on the plan to find $400\,\mathrm{cm}$ per drawn centimetre.
