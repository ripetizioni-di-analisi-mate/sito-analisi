A pencil rests on a ruler graduated in centimeters, with its ends lined up with the marks shown in the figure. How long is the pencil?

- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $11\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $14\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $9\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The length read on a ruler is the distance between the two marks lined up with the ends of the object. The back end of the pencil is at the $3$ mark and the tip is at the $11$ mark. Therefore, the pencil is $\ell=11\,\mathrm{cm}-3\,\mathrm{cm}=8\,\mathrm{cm}$ long. The reading $11\,\mathrm{cm}$ would give the length of the pencil only if its other end were at the zero mark.
