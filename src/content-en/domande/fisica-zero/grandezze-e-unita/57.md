A fish tank shaped like a rectangular prism has internal dimensions of $50\,\mathrm{cm}$, $30\,\mathrm{cm}$, and $40\,\mathrm{cm}$. How many liters of water does it hold when full?

- $600\,\mathrm{L}$
- $6\,\mathrm{L}$
- $60\,\mathrm{L}$
- $60\,000\,\mathrm{L}$

## Explanation

The volume of a rectangular prism is the product of its three dimensions. This gives $V=50\cdot30\cdot40\,\mathrm{cm^3}=60\,000\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. Since $1\,\mathrm{L}=1\,\mathrm{dm^3}=1000\,\mathrm{cm^3}$, divide by $1000$. Therefore, the full tank holds $60\,\mathrm{L}$ of water.
