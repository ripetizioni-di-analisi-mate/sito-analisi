A canister is one quarter full. Adding $9\,\mathrm{L}$ brings it to three quarters full without losses. What is its total capacity?

- $18\,\mathrm{L}$
- $27\,\mathrm{L}$
- $36\,\mathrm{L}$
- $12\,\mathrm{L}$

## Explanation

The rise from one quarter to three quarters represents half the capacity. If half holds nine litres, the full canister holds eighteen.
