A smartphone is $8\,\mathrm{mm}$ thick. What is its thickness in meters?

- $0.08\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8000\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.008\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

One millimeter is one thousandth of a meter, that is, $1\,\mathrm{mm}=0.001\,\mathrm{m}$. The thickness is therefore $8\cdot0.001\,\mathrm{m}=0.008\,\mathrm{m}$. The numerical value decreases because the meter is a larger unit than the millimeter.
