An object with a mass of $24$ kg has a density of $3$ kilograms per cubic meter. What is its volume?

- $8\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $72\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $0.125\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $24\,\mathrm{m^3}$

## Explanation

Solving the density formula for volume gives $V=\dfrac{m}{\rho}$. Substituting the given values gives $V=\dfrac{24}{3}$. The volume is therefore $8$ cubic meters.
