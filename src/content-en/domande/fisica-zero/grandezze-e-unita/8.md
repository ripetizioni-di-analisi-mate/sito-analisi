A student writes in a notebook: "the desk is $1.2$ long." What is missing for the measurement to be written completely?

- The unit of measurement
- The numerical value
- The name of the instrument used
- The mass of the desk

## Explanation

A measurement is written as a numerical value followed by a unit. The student's sentence contains the number $1.2$, but the unit is missing. Without a unit, it is impossible to know whether the desk is $1.2$ meters long, $1.2$ decimeters long, or some other length. Therefore, completing the measurement requires **the unit of measurement**, for example $1.2\,\mathrm{m}$.
