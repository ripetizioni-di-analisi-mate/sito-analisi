The bottle contains $1250\,\mathrm{mL}$. Which pair of labels can describe the same contents?

- $12.5\,\mathrm{L}$ and $1250\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $1.25\,\mathrm{L}$ and $125\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $0.125\,\mathrm{L}$ and $125\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $1.25\,\mathrm{L}$ and $1250\,\mathrm{cm^3}$

## Explanation

A thousand millilitres make one litre, and a millilitre occupies one cubic centimetre. Apply each equivalence to $1250\,\mathrm{mL}$.
