An object with a mass of $18$ kg has a density of $3$ kilograms per cubic meter. What is its volume?

- $54\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m^3}$

## Explanation

Solving the density formula for volume gives $V=\dfrac{m}{\rho}$. Substituting the given values gives $V=\dfrac{18}{3}$. The volume is therefore $6$ cubic meters.
