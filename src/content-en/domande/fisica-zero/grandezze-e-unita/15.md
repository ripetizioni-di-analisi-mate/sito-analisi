A store sells hardwood flooring at a fixed price per $\mathrm{m^2}$. What does the symbol $\mathrm{m^2}$ represent?

- The area of a square with sides $1\,\mathrm{m}$ long
- A length of $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- The volume of a cube with edges $1\,\mathrm{m}$ long
- A meter divided by a meter

## Explanation

The symbol $\mathrm{m^2}$ means a meter multiplied by a meter, so it is a unit of area. A square meter is the area of a square with sides $1\,\mathrm{m}$ long. The exponent $2$ indicates the product of two lengths, while the volume of a cube is measured in $\mathrm{m^3}$. Therefore, the price of the flooring refers to each square one meter on a side covered by the floor.
