An object has a mass of $8$ kg and a volume of $4$ cubic meters. What is its density?

- $32\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $2\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

Density is the ratio of mass to volume. Substituting gives $\rho=\dfrac{8}{4}$. The density is therefore $2$ kilograms per cubic meter.
