Two identical bottles each contain $1\,\mathrm{L}$ of liquid: one holds water, with a density of $1.0\,\mathrm{kg/L}$, and the other holds oil, with a density of $0.80\,\mathrm{kg/L}$. Which statement about the two liquids is true?

- The oil has a greater mass than the water
- The two liquids have the same mass
- The water has a greater mass than the oil
- The oil occupies a greater volume than the water

## Explanation

The mass of a liquid is the product of its density and its volume. The two liquids have the same volume of $1\,\mathrm{L}$, so the one with the greater density has the greater mass. The water has a mass of $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the oil $0.80\,\mathrm{kg}$. Therefore, **the water has a greater mass** than the oil.
