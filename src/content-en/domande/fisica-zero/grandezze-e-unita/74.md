Two sections measure $2.4\cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{m}$ and $6\cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the total distance?

- $3\cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8.4\cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.44\cdot 10^6\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Use a common exponent by writing $6\cdot 10^2=0.6\cdot 10^3$. The sum becomes $(2.4+0.6)\cdot 10^3=3\cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{m}$.
