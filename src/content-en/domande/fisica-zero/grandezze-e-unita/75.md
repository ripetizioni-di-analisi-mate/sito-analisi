One kilogram of feathers is compared with one kilogram of iron. Which of the two occupies the greater volume?

- The feathers, because they have a lower density
- The iron, because it has a higher density
- They occupy the same volume, because they have the same mass
- The feathers, because they have a smaller mass

## Explanation

From the definition of density, $V=\dfrac{m}{\rho}$. The two masses are equal, so the volume is greater for the material with the lower density. Feathers have a much lower density than iron. Therefore, for the same mass, **the feathers occupy the greater volume**.
