Four granules have the diameters listed below. Which diameter is the smallest?

- $9\cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8\cdot 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\cdot 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.2\cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Express the lengths in millionths of a metre. The coefficients for $9\cdot 10^{-7}$, $2\cdot 10^{-6}$, $1.2\cdot 10^{-5}$ and $8\cdot 10^{-6}$ metres are $0.9$, $2$, $12$ and $8$, respectively. The minimum is $0.9$.
