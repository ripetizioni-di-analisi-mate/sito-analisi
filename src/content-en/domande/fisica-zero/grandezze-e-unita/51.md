An impermeable solid is submerged with an air bubble attached. If all the displacement is attributed to the solid, what error occurs?

- The bubble does not change the displaced volume because it contains air.
- The solid's measured volume is too small.
- Whether the error is positive or negative depends on the density of the solid material.
- The solid's measured volume is too large.

## Explanation

The bubble also takes up space and displaces water. The change in reading includes both the solid's volume and the bubble's volume.
