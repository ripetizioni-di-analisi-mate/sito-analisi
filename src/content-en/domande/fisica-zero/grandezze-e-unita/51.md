A square rug has an area of $2.25\,\mathrm{m^2}$. How long is each side?

- $0.5625\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.125\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The area of a square is the product of the side with itself. We therefore look for the length that, multiplied by itself, gives $2.25$. Since $1.5\cdot1.5=2.25$, each side of the rug is $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ long.
