A full jar has a mass of $480\,\mathrm{g}$ on a scale. The same empty jar has a mass of $135\,\mathrm{g}$. What is the mass of its contents?

- $480\,\mathrm{g}$
- $615\,\mathrm{g}$
- $345\,\mathrm{g}$
- $135\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

The full reading includes the jar and its contents. Subtract the tare to obtain $480-135=345\,\mathrm{g}$.
