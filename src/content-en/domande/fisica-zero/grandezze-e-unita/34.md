A washing machine cycle lasts $1.5\,\mathrm{h}$. How many seconds is that?

- $90\,\mathrm{s}$
- $540\,\mathrm{s}$
- $54\,000\,\mathrm{s}$
- $5400\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Since $1\,\mathrm{h}=3600\,\mathrm{s}$, the number of hours is multiplied by $3600$. This gives $1.5\cdot3600\,\mathrm{s}=5400\,\mathrm{s}$. The same value is obtained by going through minutes, because $1.5\,\mathrm{h}=90\,\mathrm{min}$ and $90\cdot60\,\mathrm{s}=5400\,\mathrm{s}$.
