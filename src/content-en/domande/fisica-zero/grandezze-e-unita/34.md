A material has a density of $800$ kilograms per cubic meter. What is the mass of $6$ cubic meters of this material?

- $\dfrac{400}{3}\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $4800\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.0075\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $480\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Rearranging $\rho=\dfrac{m}{V}$ gives $m=\rho V$. Therefore, multiply $800$ by $6$. The mass is $4800$ kilograms.
