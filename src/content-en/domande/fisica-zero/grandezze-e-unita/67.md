An object has an external volume of $50\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ and mass $240\,\mathrm{g}$. Its shell material has density $6\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. Neglecting the mass of air, what is the internal empty volume?

- $90\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $10\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $50\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $40\,\mathrm{cm^3}$

## Explanation

The shell material takes up $\dfrac{240}{6}=40\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. Empty space accounts for the remaining part of the external $50\,\mathrm{cm^3}$, namely $10\,\mathrm{cm^3}$.
