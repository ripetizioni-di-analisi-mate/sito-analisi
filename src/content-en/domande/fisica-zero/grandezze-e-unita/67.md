A wooden beam shaped like a rectangular prism is $2\,\mathrm{m}$ long, $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ wide, and $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ high. What is its volume in cubic meters?

- $0.01\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $0.1\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $0.001\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $100\,\mathrm{m^3}$

## Explanation

The three dimensions are expressed in meters before multiplying them. We have $10\,\mathrm{cm}=0.1\,\mathrm{m}$ and $5\,\mathrm{cm}=0.05\,\mathrm{m}$. The volume is therefore $V=2\cdot0.1\cdot0.05\,\mathrm{m^3}=0.01\,\mathrm{m^3}$. In cubic centimeters, the same volume is $200\cdot10\cdot5\,\mathrm{cm^3}=10\,000\,\mathrm{cm^3}$.
