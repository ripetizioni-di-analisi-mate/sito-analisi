A container holds $3$ liters. What is this volume in cubic meters?

- $0.003\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $3000\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $0.03\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m^3}$

## Explanation

One liter equals one cubic decimeter. One cubic meter contains $1000$ liters. The volume is therefore $\dfrac{3}{1000}=0.003$ cubic meters.
