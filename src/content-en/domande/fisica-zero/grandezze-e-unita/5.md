A timer shows $2.4\,\mathrm{h}$. What should someone enter in separate hours and minutes fields?

- 2 h and 15 min
- 2 h and 24 min
- 2 h and 4 min
- 2 h and 40 min

## Explanation

The two hours are whole hours. Convert the remaining fraction by calculating $0.4\cdot 60=24$ minutes.
