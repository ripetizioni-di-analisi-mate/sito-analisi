A swimmer's training log contains the entries $400\,\mathrm{m}$ and $400\,\mathrm{s}$. What do they represent?

- A length and a duration
- Two equal lengths
- Two equal durations
- The same quantity written in two different units

## Explanation

The symbol $\mathrm{m}$ stands for the meter, the unit of length, so $400\,\mathrm{m}$ is the distance swum. The symbol $\mathrm{s}$ stands for the second, the unit of time, so $400\,\mathrm{s}$ is a duration. The two measurements have the same number, but the units show that they refer to different quantities. Therefore, the entries represent **a length and a duration**.
