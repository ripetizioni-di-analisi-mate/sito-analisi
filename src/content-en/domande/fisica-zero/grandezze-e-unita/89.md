The ratio of the lengths of two rods is $2.5$ when both are measured in metres. What is it when both are expressed in centimetres?

- $2.5$
- $25$
- $0.025$
- $250$

## Explanation

The conversion multiplies both lengths by one hundred. The common factor cancels in the ratio, leaving its value at $2.5$.
