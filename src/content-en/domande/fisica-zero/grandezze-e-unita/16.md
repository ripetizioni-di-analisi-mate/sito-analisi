A database requires values in SI base units. Which symbol belongs in the mass field?

- $\mathrm{kg}$
- $\mathrm{N}$
- $\mathrm{g}$
- $\mathrm{mg}$

## Explanation

The SI uses the kilogram as its base unit of mass. Grams and milligrams are submultiples of the kilogram. Newtons measure force.
