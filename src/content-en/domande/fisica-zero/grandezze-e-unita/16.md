A material has a density of $800$ kilograms per cubic meter. What is the mass of $4$ cubic meters of this material?

- $200\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.005\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $320\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $3200\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Rearranging $\rho=\dfrac{m}{V}$ gives $m=\rho V$. Therefore, multiply $800$ by $4$. The mass is $3200$ kilograms.
