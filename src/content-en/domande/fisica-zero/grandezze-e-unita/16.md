A person is $1.74\,\mathrm{m}$ tall. What is this height in centimeters?

- $17.4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $1740\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.0174\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $174\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Since $1\,\mathrm{m}=100\,\mathrm{cm}$, the meter is replaced by its value in centimeters. This gives $1.74\cdot100\,\mathrm{cm}=174\,\mathrm{cm}$. The numerical value increases because the centimeter is a smaller unit than the meter.
