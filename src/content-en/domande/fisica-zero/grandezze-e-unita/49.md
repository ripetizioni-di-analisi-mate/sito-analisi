The water level in a measuring cylinder rises from $46\,\mathrm{mL}$ to $63\,\mathrm{mL}$ when a stone is fully submerged, with no bubbles or overflow. What volume does the stone occupy?

- $63\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $46\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $17\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $109\,\mathrm{cm^3}$

## Explanation

The difference $63-46=17\,\mathrm{mL}$ is the volume of water displaced. For a fully submerged object it equals its volume, or $17\,\mathrm{cm^3}$.
