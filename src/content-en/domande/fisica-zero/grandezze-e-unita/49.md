The edge of a square handkerchief, measured all the way around, is $1.6\,\mathrm{m}$ long. What is the area of the handkerchief?

- $2.56\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $0.4\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $0.16\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $0.64\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

The edge measured all the way around is the perimeter, that is, the sum of the four equal sides of the square. The side is therefore $\dfrac{1.6}{4}\,\mathrm{m}=0.4\,\mathrm{m}$ long. The area of a square is the product of the side with itself, so $A=0.4\cdot0.4\,\mathrm{m^2}=0.16\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The value $2.56$ would be obtained by multiplying the perimeter by itself, but the perimeter is not the side of the square.
