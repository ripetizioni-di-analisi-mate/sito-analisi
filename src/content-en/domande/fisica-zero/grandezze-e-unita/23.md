A container holds $5$ liters. What is this volume in cubic meters?

- $5000\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $0.05\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $0.005\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m^3}$

## Explanation

One liter equals one cubic decimeter. One cubic meter contains $1000$ liters. The volume is therefore $\dfrac{5}{1000}=0.005$ cubic meters.
