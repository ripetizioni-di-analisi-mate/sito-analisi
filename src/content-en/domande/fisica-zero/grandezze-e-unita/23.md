The sign at the start of a hiking trail gives a length of $4\,\mathrm{hm}$. How many meters is that?

- $4000\,\mathrm{m}$
- $400\,\mathrm{m}$
- $40\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.4\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The symbol $\mathrm{h}$ stands for the prefix hecto, which equals $100$. One hectometer therefore contains $100$ meters. So the trail is $4\cdot100\,\mathrm{m}=400\,\mathrm{m}$ long.
