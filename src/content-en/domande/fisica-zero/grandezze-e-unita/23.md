You have $1\,\mathrm{m}$ of cord and need $18\,\mathrm{cm}$ pieces. Ignoring the width of the cut, what is the maximum number of whole pieces?

- $5$
- $4$
- $6$
- $8$

## Explanation

Five pieces use $90\,\mathrm{cm}$, leaving ten. A sixth would require $108\,\mathrm{cm}$, more than the available $100\,\mathrm{cm}$.
