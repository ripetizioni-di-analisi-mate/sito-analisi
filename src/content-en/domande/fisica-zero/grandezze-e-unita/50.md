The floor of a rectangular room measuring $3\,\mathrm{m}$ by $2\,\mathrm{m}$ is to be covered with square tiles $50\,\mathrm{cm}$ on a side, without cutting any. How many tiles are needed?

- $6$
- $12$
- $24$
- $240$

## Explanation

The area of the floor is $3\cdot2\,\mathrm{m^2}=6\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Each tile has sides $50\,\mathrm{cm}=0.5\,\mathrm{m}$ long, so it covers $0.5\cdot0.5\,\mathrm{m^2}=0.25\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Therefore, $\dfrac{6}{0.25}=24$ tiles are needed. The same number is obtained by counting $6$ tiles along the $3\,\mathrm{m}$ side and $4$ along the $2\,\mathrm{m}$ side.
