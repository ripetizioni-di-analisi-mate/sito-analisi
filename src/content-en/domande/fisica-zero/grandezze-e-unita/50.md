A waterproof cube of volume $40\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ and mass $30\,\mathrm{g}$ floats in water. Its submerged part displaces a mass of water equal to the mass of the cube. If $1\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ of water has a mass of $1\,\mathrm{g}$, how much of the cube’s volume lies above the waterline?

- $30\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $40\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $10\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $70\,\mathrm{cm^3}$

## Explanation

To displace $30\,\mathrm{g}$ of water, $30\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ of the cube must be submerged. Its volume above the surface is therefore $40-30=10$ cubic centimetres.
