An experiment lasts $18\,\mathrm{min}$, including a $90\,\mathrm{s}$ break. What fraction of the total time is spent on the break?

- $\dfrac{11}{12}$
- $\dfrac{1}{12}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$

## Explanation

Eighteen minutes corresponds to $1080\,\mathrm{s}$. The fraction is $\dfrac{90}{1080}=\dfrac{1}{12}$ and has no unit.
