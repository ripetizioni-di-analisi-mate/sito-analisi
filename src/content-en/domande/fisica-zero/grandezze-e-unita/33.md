An object has a mass of $12$ kg and a volume of $6$ cubic meters. What is its density?

- $2\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $72\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

Density is the ratio of mass to volume. Substituting gives $\rho=\dfrac{12}{6}$. The density is therefore $2$ kilograms per cubic meter.
