A graduated cylinder contains $200\,\mathrm{mL}$ of water. After a stone that does not absorb water is completely submerged, the level rises to $260\,\mathrm{mL}$. What is the volume of the stone?

- $460\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $260\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $200\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $60\,\mathrm{cm^3}$

## Explanation

The increase in the reading corresponds to the space occupied by the submerged stone. The final reading includes both the water and the stone, so the initial reading is subtracted. This gives $V=(260-200)\,\mathrm{mL}=60\,\mathrm{mL}$. Since $1\,\mathrm{mL}=1\,\mathrm{cm^3}$, the stone has a volume of $60\,\mathrm{cm^3}$.
