A material's density is $2.7\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. What value should be entered in software that uses $\mathrm{kg/m^3}$?

- $0.0027\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $27\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $270\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $2700\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

One gram equals $10^{-3}\,\mathrm{kg}$ and one cubic centimetre equals $10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^3}$. The ratio gains a factor of $\dfrac{10^{-3}}{10^{-6}}=1000$.
