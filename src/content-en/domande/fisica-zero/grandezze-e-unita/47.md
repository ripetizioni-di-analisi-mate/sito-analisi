A $5\,\mathrm{m^2}$ terrace receives $2\,\mathrm{mm}$ of uniform rainfall. How many litres could you collect without losses?

- $100\,\mathrm{L}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{L}$
- $1\,\mathrm{L}$
- $10\,\mathrm{L}$

## Explanation

The depth is $0.002\,\mathrm{m}$. The volume is $5\cdot 0.002=0.010\,\mathrm{m^3}$, equivalent to $10\,\mathrm{L}$.
