A tablecloth has an area of $1.2\,\mathrm{m^2}$. What is its area in square centimeters?

- $120\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $1200\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $1\,200\,000\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $12\,000\,\mathrm{cm^2}$

## Explanation

Since $1\,\mathrm{m}=100\,\mathrm{cm}$, a square with sides $1\,\mathrm{m}$ long has an area of $100\,\mathrm{cm}\cdot100\,\mathrm{cm}=10\,000\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. For areas, the length conversion factor therefore appears twice. So $1.2\,\mathrm{m^2}=1.2\cdot10\,000\,\mathrm{cm^2}=12\,000\,\mathrm{cm^2}$.
