A ream of $500$ identical sheets of paper is $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ thick. How thick is one sheet?

- $1\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.1\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.01\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

A single sheet is too thin to measure with a ruler, so the thickness of the whole ream is measured and divided by the number of sheets. Since $1\,\mathrm{cm}=10\,\mathrm{mm}$, the ream is $50\,\mathrm{mm}$ thick. Then one sheet is
$$
\frac{50\,\mathrm{mm}}{500}=0.1\,\mathrm{mm}
$$
thick. This is an indirect measurement, obtained from a direct measurement by a division.
