A sheet has an area of $3600\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. What is its area in square metres?

- $36\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $0.0036\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $3.6\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $0.36\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

A square with sides of one metre contains $100\cdot 100=10000$ square centimetres. Divide $3600$ by $10000$.
