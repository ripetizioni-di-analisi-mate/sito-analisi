An object has a mass of $6$ kg and a volume of $3$ cubic meters. What is its density?

- $18\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $2\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $3\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

Density is the ratio of mass to volume. Substituting gives $\rho=\dfrac{6}{3}$. The density is therefore $2$ kilograms per cubic meter.
