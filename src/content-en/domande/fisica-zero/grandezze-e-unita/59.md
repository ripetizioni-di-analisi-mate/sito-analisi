A soda can is labeled $33\,\mathrm{cL}$. How many milliliters does it contain?

- $33\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $3300\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $330\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $3.3\,\mathrm{mL}$

## Explanation

The prefix centi equals $0.01$, so $1\,\mathrm{cL}=0.01\,\mathrm{L}$ and $33\,\mathrm{cL}=0.33\,\mathrm{L}$. One liter contains $1000$ milliliters. Therefore, the can contains $0.33\cdot1000\,\mathrm{mL}=330\,\mathrm{mL}$.
