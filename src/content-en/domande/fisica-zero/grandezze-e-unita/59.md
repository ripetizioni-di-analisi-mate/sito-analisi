You need to carry $3.2\,\mathrm{L}$ of water in $750\,\mathrm{mL}$ bottles. What is the minimum number of bottles if the last may be partly full?

- $6$
- $3$
- $4$
- $5$

## Explanation

Four bottles hold $3000\,\mathrm{mL}$, less than the required $3200\,\mathrm{mL}$. The remaining $200\,\mathrm{mL}$ require a fifth bottle.
