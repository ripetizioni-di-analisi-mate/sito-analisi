A light flashes at regular intervals of $0.4\,\mathrm{s}$. How much time passes between the first and sixth flashes?

- $1.6\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2.4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3.0\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Six flashes enclose five intervals. Multiply five by the duration of each interval, $5\cdot 0.4=2.0\,\mathrm{s}$.
