A rope $6\,\mathrm{m}$ long is cut into $8$ equal pieces. How long is each piece in centimeters?

- $48\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $750\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $75\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Since $1\,\mathrm{m}=100\,\mathrm{cm}$, the rope is $600\,\mathrm{cm}$ long. The $8$ pieces are equal, so each one is $\dfrac{600}{8}\,\mathrm{cm}$ long. Therefore, each piece measures $75\,\mathrm{cm}$.
