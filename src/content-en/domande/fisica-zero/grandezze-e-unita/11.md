An oven's timer beeps for twelve seconds. Which way of writing this duration follows the SI rules?

- $12\,\mathrm{sec}$
- $12\,\mathrm{s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{S}$
- $\mathrm{s}\,12$

## Explanation

The symbol for the second is $\mathrm{s}$, lowercase and without abbreviations such as sec. The symbol follows the number, separated by a space, and stays the same in the plural. Therefore, the correct form is $12\,\mathrm{s}$.
