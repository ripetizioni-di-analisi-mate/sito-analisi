You cut $85\,\mathrm{cm}$ off a $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$ cable. How much cable remains, expressed in metres?

- $0.165\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3.35\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.65\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.415\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The cut section measures $0.85\,\mathrm{m}$. Subtract lengths in the same unit to get $2.50-0.85=1.65\,\mathrm{m}$.
