A time interval lasts $4$ minutes. How long is it in seconds?

- $400\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $240\,\mathrm{s}$
- $\dfrac{1}{15}\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

One minute contains $60$ seconds. Multiply the number of minutes by $60$. The duration is therefore $240$ seconds.
