A stack of $80$ identical sheets is $8\,\mathrm{mm}$ tall. Ignoring air gaps, how thick is one sheet in micrometres?

- $1000\,\mathrm{\mu m}$
- $640\,\mathrm{\mu m}$
- $100\,\mathrm{\mu m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{\mu m}$

## Explanation

Divide the height by the number of sheets, $\dfrac{8}{80}=0.1\,\mathrm{mm}$. A millimetre contains a thousand micrometres, so one sheet measures $100$ micrometres.
