Four bike routes have these lengths: $0.8\,\mathrm{km}$, $750\,\mathrm{m}$, $60\,000\,\mathrm{cm}$, and $9000\,\mathrm{dm}$. Which route is the longest?

- The $0.8\,\mathrm{km}$ route
- The $9000\,\mathrm{dm}$ route
- The $750\,\mathrm{m}$ route
- The $60\,000\,\mathrm{cm}$ route

## Explanation

To compare the lengths, express them all in meters. We have $0.8\,\mathrm{km}=800\,\mathrm{m}$, $60\,000\,\mathrm{cm}=600\,\mathrm{m}$, and $9000\,\mathrm{dm}=900\,\mathrm{m}$. The largest value is $900\,\mathrm{m}$, so the longest route is the $9000\,\mathrm{dm}$ one. The largest number, $60\,000$, belongs to the shortest route, because the centimeter is the smallest of the units used.
