A shelf drawing shows only $48$ next to an edge. What information is needed to cut a piece of the same length?

- The precision with which the number was printed.
- The size of the permitted uncertainty, without specifying any unit.
- The desired number of significant figures for the cut.
- The length unit used for the dimension.

## Explanation

The same number could mean $48\,\mathrm{mm}$ or $48\,\mathrm{cm}$. The unit identifies the physical length represented.
