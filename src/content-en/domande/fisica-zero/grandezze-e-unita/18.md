A half-marathon course is $21.1\,\mathrm{km}$ long. How many meters is that?

- $21\,100\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2110\,\mathrm{m}$
- $211\,000\,\mathrm{m}$
- $211\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The prefix kilo means one thousand, so $1\,\mathrm{km}=1000\,\mathrm{m}$. Therefore, $21.1$ is multiplied by $1000$, which moves the decimal point three places to the right. The course is $21\,100\,\mathrm{m}$ long.
