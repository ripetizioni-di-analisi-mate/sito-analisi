A trail curves on a map. Which procedure retains the bends when measuring the distance along the trail?

- Join only the endpoints with a ruler and apply the scale.
- Add the horizontal and vertical distances between the endpoints and apply the scale.
- Measure only the horizontal projection of the trail and apply the scale.
- Lay a thread along the route, straighten it and apply the map scale.

## Explanation

The thread follows the route and retains its length when straightened without stretching. The scale then converts map distance into ground distance.
