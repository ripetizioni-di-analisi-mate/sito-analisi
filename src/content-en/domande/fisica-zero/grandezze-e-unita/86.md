A grain of sand has a mass of $0.3\,\mathrm{mg}$. What is its mass in kilograms, written in scientific notation?

- $3\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $3\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $3\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $3\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Since $1\,\mathrm{g}=1000\,\mathrm{mg}$, we have $0.3\,\mathrm{mg}=0.0003\,\mathrm{g}$. Since $1\,\mathrm{kg}=1000\,\mathrm{g}$, dividing by $1000$ again gives $0.0000003\,\mathrm{kg}$. Going from $3$ to $0.0000003$ moves the decimal point seven places to the left. Therefore, the mass of the grain is $3\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{kg}$.
