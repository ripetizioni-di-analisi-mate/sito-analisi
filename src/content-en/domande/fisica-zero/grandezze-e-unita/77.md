A sensor records a length in metres first as $1.2\cdot 10^3$ and then as $1.20\cdot 10^3$. What is the difference in the notation?

- The second measurement is ten times larger.
- The second notation has one fewer significant figure.
- The same numerical value, with one extra significant figure in the second notation.
- The first measurement is a hundred times larger.

## Explanation

The coefficients $1.2$ and $1.20$ represent the same number. The explicit trailing zero shows that the hundredths place of the coefficient was also reported.
