A $900\,\mathrm{mL}$ vessel contains $820\,\mathrm{mL}$ of water. An object of volume $130\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ is held fully submerged. How much water overflows?

- $210\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $130\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $50\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $80\,\mathrm{mL}$

## Explanation

The initial free space is $900-820=80\,\mathrm{mL}$. The object displaces $130\,\mathrm{mL}$, so $130-80=50\,\mathrm{mL}$ rises past the rim.
