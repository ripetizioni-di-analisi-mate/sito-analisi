A circular lid has diameter $10\,\mathrm{cm}$. Using $A=\pi  r^2$, what is the area of one face?

- $25\pi \,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $10\pi \,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $5\pi \,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $100\pi \,\mathrm{cm^2}$

## Explanation

The formula requires the radius, half the diameter. Substitute $r=5\,\mathrm{cm}$ to obtain $\pi \cdot 5^2=25\pi \,\mathrm{cm^2}$.
