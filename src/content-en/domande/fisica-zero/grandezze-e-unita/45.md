A rectangular kitchen floor measures $4\,\mathrm{m}$ by $3.5\,\mathrm{m}$. What is its area?

- $7.5\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $140\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $28\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $14\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

The area of a rectangle is the product of its base and height, expressed in the same unit. Both sides are already in meters, so $A=4\cdot3.5\,\mathrm{m^2}=14\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The unit is the square meter because two lengths in meters are multiplied.
