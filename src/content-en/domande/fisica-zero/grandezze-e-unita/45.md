A length is $140$ mm. What is its value in centimeters?

- $14\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $1400\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $140\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $1.4\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

One centimeter contains $10$ millimeters. Therefore, divide the numerical value by $10$. The length is $14$ centimeters.
