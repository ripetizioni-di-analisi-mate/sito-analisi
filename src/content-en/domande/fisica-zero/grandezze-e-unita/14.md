A bottle contains $0.75\,\mathrm{L}$ of liquid with density $1.2\,\mathrm{kg/L}$. What is the mass of the liquid alone?

- $0.90\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $1.95\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $1.60\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.625\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Each litre has a mass of $1.2\,\mathrm{kg}$. Multiply by the fraction of a litre present, $1.2\cdot 0.75=0.90\,\mathrm{kg}$.
