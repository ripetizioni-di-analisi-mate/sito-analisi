A container holds $4$ liters. What is this volume in cubic meters?

- $4000\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $0.004\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $0.04\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m^3}$

## Explanation

One liter equals one cubic decimeter. One cubic meter contains $1000$ liters. The volume is therefore $\dfrac{4}{1000}=0.004$ cubic meters.
