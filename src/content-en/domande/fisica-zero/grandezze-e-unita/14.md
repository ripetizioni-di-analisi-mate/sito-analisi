A laboratory thermometer displays temperature in kelvins. Which of the following units is an SI base unit?

- The kelvin
- The liter
- The hour
- The kilometer

## Explanation

The seven SI base units are the meter, kilogram, second, kelvin, ampere, mole, and candela. The liter is a unit of volume equal to $1\,\mathrm{dm^3}$, so it comes from the meter. The hour is a unit used alongside the SI, and the kilometer is a multiple of the meter formed with a prefix. Therefore, the only base unit in the list is **the kelvin**.
