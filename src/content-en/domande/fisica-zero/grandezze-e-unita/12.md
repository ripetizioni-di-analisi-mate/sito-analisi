For a school's five-a-side soccer field, the following are known: the length of the field, its area, the mass of the ball, and the duration of a game. Which of these is a derived quantity?

- The length of the field
- The area of the field
- The mass of the ball
- The duration of the game

## Explanation

Length, mass, and time each have an SI base unit: the meter, the kilogram, and the second. The area of the field is obtained by multiplying two lengths, so it is a **derived quantity**. Its SI unit is the square meter, $\mathrm{m^2}$, which is a meter multiplied by a meter.
