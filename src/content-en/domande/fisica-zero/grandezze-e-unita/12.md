A rectangular rug is $2.4\,\mathrm{m}$ long and $150\,\mathrm{cm}$ wide. How many square metres does it cover?

- $7.8\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $0.36\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $3.6\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $36\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

The width is $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$. Multiplying the sides in the same unit gives $2.4\cdot 1.5=3.6\,\mathrm{m^2}$.
