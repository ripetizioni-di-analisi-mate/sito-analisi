Cardstock is labelled $300\,\mathrm{g/m^2}$. What is the mass of a piece with an area of $0.20\,\mathrm{m^2}$?

- $60\,\mathrm{g}$
- $6\,\mathrm{g}$
- $300\,\mathrm{g}$
- $1500\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

The label assigns $300\,\mathrm{g}$ to each square metre. Multiply by the area of the piece, $300\cdot 0.20=60\,\mathrm{g}$.
