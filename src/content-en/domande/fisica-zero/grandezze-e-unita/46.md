A rectangular sheet of paper has sides of $21\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $0.3\,\mathrm{m}$. What is its area in square centimeters?

- $630\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $6.3\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $63\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $6300\,\mathrm{cm^2}$

## Explanation

The two sides are expressed in the same unit before multiplying them. Since $1\,\mathrm{m}=100\,\mathrm{cm}$, the second side is $0.3\cdot100\,\mathrm{cm}=30\,\mathrm{cm}$. The area is therefore $A=21\cdot30\,\mathrm{cm^2}=630\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. The product $21\cdot0.3=6.3$ would mix centimeters and meters and would not give an area in a well-defined unit.
