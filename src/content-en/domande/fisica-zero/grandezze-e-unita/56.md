Two straight-sided cylinders have base areas of $30\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and $50\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. All water is poured without loss from the first, where it is $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ deep, into the empty second cylinder. How deep is it there?

- $10\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $15\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $3\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The volume is conserved and equals $30\cdot 10=300\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. In the second container the depth is $\dfrac{300}{50}=6\,\mathrm{cm}$.
