A company makes rectangular acoustic panels in four sizes. Which size has an area of $1\,\mathrm{m^2}$?

- $50\,\mathrm{cm}$ by $50\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m}$ by $10\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$ by $50\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $100\,\mathrm{cm}$ by $50\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

For each size, express the sides in meters and multiply them. The $2\,\mathrm{m}$ by $50\,\mathrm{cm}$ panel has sides $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $0.5\,\mathrm{m}$, so its area is $2\cdot0.5\,\mathrm{m^2}=1\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The other sizes have areas of $0.25\,\mathrm{m^2}$, $0.1\,\mathrm{m^2}$, and $0.5\,\mathrm{m^2}$. A rectangle can therefore have the same area as a square with $1\,\mathrm{m}$ sides even when its sides are not $1\,\mathrm{m}$ long.
