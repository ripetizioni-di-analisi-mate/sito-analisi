A roll contains $3\cdot 10^3$ equal sections, each $4\cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m}$ long. What is the total length?

- $1.2\cdot 10^1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.2\cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $7\cdot 10^1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.2\cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Multiply the coefficients and add the exponents to get $12\cdot 10^1\,\mathrm{m}$. Normalising gives $1.2\cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{m}$.
