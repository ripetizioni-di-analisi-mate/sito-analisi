Ice has a density of $0.92\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. What is this density in $\mathrm{kg/m^3}$?

- $92\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $920\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $9200\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $0.00092\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

Since $1\,\mathrm{g}=0.001\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $1\,\mathrm{cm^3}=0.000001\,\mathrm{m^3}$, it follows that
$$
1\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}=\frac{0.001\,\mathrm{kg}}{0.000001\,\mathrm{m^3}}=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}.
$$
Therefore, $0.92\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}=0.92\cdot1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}=920\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. One cubic meter of ice thus has a mass of $920\,\mathrm{kg}$.
