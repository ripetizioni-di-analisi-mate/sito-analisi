Two unknown solids both have a density of $2.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$ within the instruments' resolution. Which conclusion is justified?

- Their volumes must be equal.
- The measured density fits both and another property is needed to identify them with certainty.
- They must both have mass $2.5\,\mathrm{g}$.
- They certainly have the same chemical composition.

## Explanation

Density establishes the ratio between mass and volume. Different materials may have equal ratios or ratios too close to distinguish by the measurement.
