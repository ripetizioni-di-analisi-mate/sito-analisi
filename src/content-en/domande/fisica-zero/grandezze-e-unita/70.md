A small wooden cube has a mass of $12\,\mathrm{g}$ and a volume of $20\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. What is its density?

- $240\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $6\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $0.6\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$

## Explanation

Density is the ratio of mass to volume, $\rho=\dfrac{m}{V}$. Substituting the data gives $\rho=\dfrac{12\,\mathrm{g}}{20\,\mathrm{cm^3}}=0.6\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. Therefore, each cubic centimeter of that wood has a mass of $0.6\,\mathrm{g}$.
