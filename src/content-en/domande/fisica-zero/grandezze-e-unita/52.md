The floor plan of a studio apartment has the shape shown in the figure. What is the floor area?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 xmin=-1.6 xmax=9.6 ymin=-1.2 ymax=5 poligoni="0,0 8,0 8,2 5,2 5,4 0,4" etichette="3.6,-0.6,8 m;-1.4,2,4 m;2.1,4.5,5 m;8.3,1,2 m" titolo="Top view of the studio apartment; all corners are right angles."}

- $32\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $40\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $26\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

A vertical segment divides the figure into two rectangles. The first rectangle has sides $5\,\mathrm{m}$ and $4\,\mathrm{m}$, so its area is $20\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The second has base $8\,\mathrm{m}-5\,\mathrm{m}=3\,\mathrm{m}$ and height $2\,\mathrm{m}$, so its area is $6\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Therefore, the floor area is $20\,\mathrm{m^2}+6\,\mathrm{m^2}=26\,\mathrm{m^2}$.
