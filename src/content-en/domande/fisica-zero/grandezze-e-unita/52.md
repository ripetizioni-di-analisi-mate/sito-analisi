For which sample is direct immersion in water unreliable for measuring its original volume because the sample changes during the procedure?

- An impermeable steel cylinder.
- An undamaged glazed ceramic marble.
- A solid glass sphere.
- A sugar cube that dissolves in the water.

## Explanation

Displacement requires the sample to retain its volume during immersion. A dissolving sugar cube loses its shape and mixes with the water.
