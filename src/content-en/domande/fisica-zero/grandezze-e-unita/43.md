A material has a density of $800$ kilograms per cubic meter. What is the mass of $7$ cubic meters of this material?

- $\dfrac{800}{7}\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.00875\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $5600\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $560\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Rearranging $\rho=\dfrac{m}{V}$ gives $m=\rho V$. Therefore, multiply $800$ by $7$. The mass is $5600$ kilograms.
