An outline on a grid encloses $14$ whole cells and $8$ half cells. Each whole cell covers $4\,\mathrm{mm^2}$. What area estimate do you obtain?

- $88\,\mathrm{mm^2}$
- $72\,\mathrm{mm^2}$
- $56\,\mathrm{mm^2}$
- $36\,\mathrm{mm^2}$

## Explanation

The eight half cells make four whole ones. Multiply $14+4=18$ equivalent cells by $4\,\mathrm{mm^2}$.
