A windowpane is $3\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m}$ thick, and a glass tabletop is $2\cdot10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m}$ thick. Which statement is true?

- The windowpane is thicker, because $3>2$
- The tabletop is thicker, at $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ compared with $3\,\mathrm{mm}$
- The two panes have the same thickness
- The windowpane is thicker, at $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ compared with $2\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

We have $10^{-3}=0.001$ and $10^{-2}=0.01$. The windowpane is therefore $3\cdot0.001\,\mathrm{m}=0.003\,\mathrm{m}=3\,\mathrm{mm}$ thick. The tabletop is $2\cdot0.01\,\mathrm{m}=0.02\,\mathrm{m}=2\,\mathrm{cm}$ thick, or $20\,\mathrm{mm}$. Therefore, **the tabletop is thicker**. The exponents are different, so the thicknesses are compared after writing both in the same unit.
