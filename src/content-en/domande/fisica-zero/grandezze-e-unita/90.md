A sealed container has mass $480\,\mathrm{g}$ both before and after two liquids are mixed inside it. What further measurement is needed to tell whether the liquid volumes added exactly?

- Determine only the mass of the empty container.
- Compare the final mixture volume with the sum of the initial volumes.
- Measure only the maximum capacity of the container.
- Reweigh the same closed container after mixing.

## Explanation

The weighings check mass conservation. To test volume additivity, compare the final volume with the sum of the initial volumes under the same conditions.
