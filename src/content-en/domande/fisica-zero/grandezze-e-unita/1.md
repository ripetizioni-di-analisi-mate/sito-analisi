A backpack can be described by several properties. Which of these is a physical quantity?

- The brand of the backpack
- The country where it was made
- Whether it is comfortable
- The mass of the backpack

## Explanation

A physical quantity is a property that can be measured by comparing it with a unit of measurement. The mass of the backpack is measured with a balance and expressed in kilograms, so it is a physical quantity. The brand and the country of manufacture describe the backpack, but there is no unit to compare them with. Comfort depends on the person carrying the backpack and is not expressed as a number followed by a unit. Therefore, the only physical quantity in the list is **the mass of the backpack**.
