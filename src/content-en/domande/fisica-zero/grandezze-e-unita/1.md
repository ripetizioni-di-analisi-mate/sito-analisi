A ribbon is $2.75\,\mathrm{m}$ long. What is its length in centimetres?

- $27.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2750\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.0275\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $275\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

A metre contains $100\,\mathrm{cm}$. The same length therefore contains $2.75\cdot 100=275$ centimetres.
