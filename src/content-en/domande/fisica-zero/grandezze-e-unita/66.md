A car's fuel tank has a capacity of $45\,\mathrm{L}$. Which quantity does this value express?

- A mass
- An area
- A volume
- A length

## Explanation

The liter is the volume of a cube with edges one decimeter long, so it is a unit of volume. The capacity of the tank is the internal space available for fuel. Therefore, $45\,\mathrm{L}$ expresses **a volume**, equal to $45\,\mathrm{dm^3}$. The mass of the fuel it holds also depends on the fuel's density.
