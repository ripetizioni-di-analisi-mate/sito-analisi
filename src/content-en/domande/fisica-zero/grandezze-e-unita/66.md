Two solid blocks occupy $10\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ and $30\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ and have densities of $2\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$ and $4\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$, respectively. What is their combined mass divided by their combined volume?

- $3\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $3.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $6\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$

## Explanation

Their masses are $20\,\mathrm{g}$ and $120\,\mathrm{g}$. Divide the sum by the total volume, $\dfrac{140}{40}=3.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.
