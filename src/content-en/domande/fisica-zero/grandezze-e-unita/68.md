A sealed sample keeps the same mass while its volume increases from $80\,\mathrm{mL}$ to $100\,\mathrm{mL}$. What percentage of the initial density is the final density?

- $80\,\mathrm{\%}$
- $20\,\mathrm{\%}$
- $100\,\mathrm{\%}$
- $125\,\mathrm{\%}$

## Explanation

Write the density ratio as $\dfrac{\dfrac{m}{100}}{\dfrac{m}{80}}$. The mass cancels, leaving $\dfrac{80}{100}=0.8$, or eighty percent.
