A rectangular tub with an internal base of $50\,\mathrm{cm}$ by $40\,\mathrm{cm}$ is filled with $30\,\mathrm{L}$ of water. How high does the water reach?

- $1.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $150\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.015\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $15\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The water forms a rectangular prism with the same base as the tub, so its volume is the base area multiplied by the height. The base area is $50\cdot40\,\mathrm{cm^2}=2000\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. Since $1\,\mathrm{L}=1000\,\mathrm{cm^3}$, the volume of the water is $30\,000\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. Therefore, the height is $h=\dfrac{30\,000}{2000}\,\mathrm{cm}=15\,\mathrm{cm}$.
