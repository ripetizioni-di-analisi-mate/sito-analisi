A container holds $7$ liters. What is this volume in cubic meters?

- $0.007\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $7000\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $0.07\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $7\,\mathrm{m^3}$

## Explanation

One liter equals one cubic decimeter. One cubic meter contains $1000$ liters. The volume is therefore $\dfrac{7}{1000}=0.007$ cubic meters.
