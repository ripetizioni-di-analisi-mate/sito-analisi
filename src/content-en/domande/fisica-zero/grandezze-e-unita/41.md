A tennis match starts at $15{:}50$ and ends at $17{:}25$ on the same day. How long does it last in minutes?

- $95\,\mathrm{min}$
- $135\,\mathrm{min}$
- $175\,\mathrm{min}$
- $85\,\mathrm{min}$

## Explanation

From $15{:}50$ to $16{:}00$ is $10\,\mathrm{min}$. From $16{:}00$ to $17{:}00$ is one hour, or $60\,\mathrm{min}$, and from $17{:}00$ to $17{:}25$ is another $25\,\mathrm{min}$. The time interval is therefore $10+60+25=95\,\mathrm{min}$. Subtracting $17.25-15.50$ as if they were decimal numbers would give a wrong result, because an hour contains $60$ minutes.
