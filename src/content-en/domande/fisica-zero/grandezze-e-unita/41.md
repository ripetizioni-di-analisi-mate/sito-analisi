An open-top cube-shaped box has side $4\,\mathrm{cm}$. How much paper covers the outside of the bottom and walls once, without overlap?

- $80\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $16\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $64\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $96\,\mathrm{cm^2}$

## Explanation

The base and four walls make five faces. Each is $4^2=16\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, so $5\cdot 16=80\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ is needed.
