A container holds $6$ liters. What is this volume in cubic meters?

- $6000\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $0.06\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $0.006\,\mathrm{m^3}$

## Explanation

One liter equals one cubic decimeter. One cubic meter contains $1000$ liters. The volume is therefore $\dfrac{6}{1000}=0.006$ cubic meters.
