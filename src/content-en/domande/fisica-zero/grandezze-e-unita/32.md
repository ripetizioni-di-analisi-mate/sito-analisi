A bag of flour sits on one pan of a two-pan balance. The standard masses $500\,\mathrm{g}$, $200\,\mathrm{g}$, $200\,\mathrm{g}$, and $50\,\mathrm{g}$ are placed on the other pan, and the balance reaches equilibrium. What is the mass of the bag?

- $750\,\mathrm{g}$
- $1900\,\mathrm{g}$
- $950\,\mathrm{g}$
- $475\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

A two-pan balance measures a mass by comparing it with standard masses. When the two pans are in equilibrium, the mass of the bag equals the total mass of the standard masses on the other pan. There are four standard masses, and the $200\,\mathrm{g}$ one appears twice. Therefore, the mass of the bag is $500\,\mathrm{g}+200\,\mathrm{g}+200\,\mathrm{g}+50\,\mathrm{g}=950\,\mathrm{g}$.
