A device starts on Tuesday at 18:00 and runs for $0.5$ days. When does it switch off?

- Wednesday at 12:00
- Wednesday at 18:00
- Wednesday at 06:00
- Tuesday at 23:00

## Explanation

Half a day is twelve hours. The first six take you to midnight and the following six to 06:00 on Wednesday.
