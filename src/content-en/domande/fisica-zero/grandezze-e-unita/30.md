Signal A leaves at $0\,\mathrm{ms}$ and takes $0.015\,\mathrm{s}$ to arrive. B leaves at $4\,\mathrm{ms}$ and takes $9\,\mathrm{ms}$. Which arrives first, and by how much?

- A, $4\,\mathrm{ms}$
- A, $2\,\mathrm{ms}$
- B, $6\,\mathrm{ms}$
- B, $2\,\mathrm{ms}$

## Explanation

A arrives $15\,\mathrm{ms}$ after the time origin. B arrives at $4+9=13\,\mathrm{ms}$, so it precedes A by two milliseconds.
