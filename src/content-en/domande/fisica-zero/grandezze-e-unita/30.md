A train trip takes $1.2\,\mathrm{h}$. How long is that in hours and minutes?

- $1\,\mathrm{h}$ and $12\,\mathrm{min}$
- $1\,\mathrm{h}$ and $20\,\mathrm{min}$
- $1\,\mathrm{h}$ and $2\,\mathrm{min}$
- $1\,\mathrm{h}$ and $30\,\mathrm{min}$

## Explanation

The whole-number part $1$ is one full hour. The decimal part $0.2$ is a fraction of an hour, so it is converted to minutes by multiplying by $60$. This gives $0.2\cdot60\,\mathrm{min}=12\,\mathrm{min}$. Therefore, the trip takes $1\,\mathrm{h}$ and $12\,\mathrm{min}$.
