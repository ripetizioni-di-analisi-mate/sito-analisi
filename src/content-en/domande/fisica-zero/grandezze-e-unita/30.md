A mass is $1500$ g. What is its value in kilograms?

- $1500\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.15\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $15\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

One kilogram contains $1000$ grams. To convert grams to kilograms, divide by $1000$. The mass is therefore $1.5$ kilograms.
