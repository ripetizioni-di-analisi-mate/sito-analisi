You need $600\,\mathrm{mL}$ of solution containing $5\,\mathrm{\%}$ component A by volume. Assume volumes add. What volume of A is needed?

- $120\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $30\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $570\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $300\,\mathrm{mL}$

## Explanation

Five percent means a fraction of $0.05$ of the final volume. Multiply $0.05\cdot 600=30\,\mathrm{mL}$.
