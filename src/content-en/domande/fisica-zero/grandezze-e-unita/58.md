An inflatable backyard pool holds $2.4\,\mathrm{m^3}$ of water. How many liters is that?

- $240\,\mathrm{L}$
- $24\,000\,\mathrm{L}$
- $2.4\,\mathrm{L}$
- $2400\,\mathrm{L}$

## Explanation

A liter is the volume of one cubic decimeter. Since $1\,\mathrm{m}=10\,\mathrm{dm}$, we have $1\,\mathrm{m^3}=10\cdot10\cdot10\,\mathrm{dm^3}=1000\,\mathrm{dm^3}=1000\,\mathrm{L}$. The pool therefore holds $2.4\cdot1000\,\mathrm{L}=2400\,\mathrm{L}$.
