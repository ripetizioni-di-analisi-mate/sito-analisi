A uniform chocolate bar is broken into two equal halves. How does the density of one half compare with that of the whole bar?

- It is half as large
- It is the same
- It is twice as large
- It is one quarter as large

## Explanation

One half has half the mass of the bar and half its volume. Density is the ratio $\dfrac{m}{V}$, and dividing both the numerator and the denominator by $2$ does not change the ratio. Therefore, the density of one half **is the same** as that of the whole bar. The density of a uniform object depends only on the material it is made of.
