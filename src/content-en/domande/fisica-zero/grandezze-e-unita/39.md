A square plate has an area of $144\,\mathrm{mm^2}$. How long is its side in centimetres?

- $1.2\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $14.4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.12\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The side is the square root of the area, or $12\,\mathrm{mm}$. Ten millimetres make one centimetre, giving a side of $1.2\,\mathrm{cm}$.
