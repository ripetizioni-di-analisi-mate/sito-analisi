A sign at the entrance to a bridge gives a vehicle limit of $3.5\,\mathrm{t}$. What does this value express?

- A mass of $350\,\mathrm{kg}$
- A mass of $35\,000\,\mathrm{kg}$
- A mass of $3500\,\mathrm{kg}$
- A mass of $3.5\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The symbol $\mathrm{t}$ stands for the metric ton, which is a unit of mass. One metric ton equals $1000\,\mathrm{kg}$, so $3.5\,\mathrm{t}=3.5\cdot1000\,\mathrm{kg}=3500\,\mathrm{kg}$. Therefore, the sign indicates that vehicles may have a mass of at most $3500\,\mathrm{kg}$.
