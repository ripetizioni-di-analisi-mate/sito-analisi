A mass is $1750$ g. What is its value in kilograms?

- $1750\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.175\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $1.75\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $17.5\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

One kilogram contains $1000$ grams. To convert grams to kilograms, divide by $1000$. The mass is therefore $1.75$ kilograms.
