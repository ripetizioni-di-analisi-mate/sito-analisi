The battery of a smartwatch lasts one full day, that is, $24\,\mathrm{h}$. How many seconds is that, written in scientific notation?

- $8.64\cdot10^3\,\mathrm{s}$
- $8.64\cdot10^4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.44\cdot10^3\,\mathrm{s}$
- $8.64\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Since $1\,\mathrm{h}=3600\,\mathrm{s}$, the duration is $24\cdot3600\,\mathrm{s}=86\,400\,\mathrm{s}$. In scientific notation the coefficient is at least $1$ and less than $10$, so the coefficient is $8.64$. Going from $8.64$ to $86\,400$ moves the decimal point four places to the right, which means multiplying by $10^4$. Therefore, one day lasts $8.64\cdot10^4\,\mathrm{s}$. The number $1440$, by contrast, counts the minutes in a day.
