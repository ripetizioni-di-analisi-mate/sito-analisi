A homogeneous block of density $4\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$ is cut into pieces with one quarter and three quarters of its mass. What is the density of the smaller piece?

- $16\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $4\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $1\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $3\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$

## Explanation

Both mass and volume in the smaller piece become one quarter of their initial values. Their ratio stays at $4\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.
