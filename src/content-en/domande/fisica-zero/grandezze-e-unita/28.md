A recording starts at 23:52 and ends at 00:17 that same night. How many minutes does it last?

- $15\,\mathrm{min}$
- $25\,\mathrm{min}$
- $65\,\mathrm{min}$
- $35\,\mathrm{min}$

## Explanation

Eight minutes elapse before midnight. Seventeen more elapse after midnight, giving a total of $8+17=25$ minutes.
