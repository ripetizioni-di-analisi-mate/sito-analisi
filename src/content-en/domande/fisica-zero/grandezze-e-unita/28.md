In a $4\times400\,\mathrm{m}$ relay, each of four athletes runs $400\,\mathrm{m}$. How many kilometers does the team cover in total?

- $16\,\mathrm{km}$
- $0.16\,\mathrm{km}$
- $1.6\,\mathrm{km}$
- $1600\,\mathrm{km}$

## Explanation

The team covers $400\,\mathrm{m}$ four times, which is $1600\,\mathrm{m}$. Since $1000\,\mathrm{m}=1\,\mathrm{km}$, converting to kilometers requires dividing by $1000$. The total distance is therefore $1.6\,\mathrm{km}$.
