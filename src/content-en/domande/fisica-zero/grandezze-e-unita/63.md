Sample A has mass $18\,\mathrm{g}$ in $6\,\mathrm{cm^3}$, and B has mass $35\,\mathrm{g}$ in $14\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. Which is denser, and by how much?

- B, $0.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- A, $1.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- B, $2.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- A, $0.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$

## Explanation

The masses per cubic centimetre are $\dfrac{18}{6}=3$ for A and $\dfrac{35}{14}=2.5$ for B. Subtraction gives A's advantage of $0.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.
