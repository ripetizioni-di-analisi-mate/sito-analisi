A cube-shaped container is to hold exactly $1\,\mathrm{L}$. How long must its internal edge be?

- $10\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $1\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $100\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The liter is the volume of a cube with edges one decimeter long, that is, $1\,\mathrm{L}=1\,\mathrm{dm^3}$. One decimeter is $10\,\mathrm{cm}$, so the internal edge must measure $10\,\mathrm{cm}$. Indeed, $10\cdot10\cdot10\,\mathrm{cm^3}=1000\,\mathrm{cm^3}=1000\,\mathrm{mL}=1\,\mathrm{L}$.
