The volume of a brick has been measured in cubic centimeters. By what number must the value be divided to express it in cubic meters?

- $100$
- $1\,000\,000$
- $1000$
- $10\,000$

## Explanation

One meter contains $100$ centimeters. For a volume, the length conversion factor appears three times, once for each dimension. Therefore, $1\,\mathrm{m^3}=100\cdot100\cdot100\,\mathrm{cm^3}=1\,000\,000\,\mathrm{cm^3}$, and converting to cubic meters requires dividing by $1\,000\,000$. For example, a $2000\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ brick has a volume of $0.002\,\mathrm{m^3}$.
