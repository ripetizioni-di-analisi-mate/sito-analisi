A seller says every full one-litre bottle contains one kilogram of liquid. Which piece of information can test that claim for a particular liquid?

- The liquid's density under the measurement conditions.
- Only the pressure at which the liquid is bottled.
- The mass of the empty bottle.
- The internal capacity of the empty bottle.

## Explanation

A litre measures volume. The relation $m=\rho  V$ assigns one kilogram to a litre only when the density is $1\,\mathrm{kg/L}$.
