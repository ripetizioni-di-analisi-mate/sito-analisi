An object with a mass of $42$ kg has a density of $3$ kilograms per cubic meter. What is its volume?

- $126\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{14}\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $42\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $14\,\mathrm{m^3}$

## Explanation

Solving the density formula for volume gives $V=\dfrac{m}{\rho}$. Substituting the given values gives $V=\dfrac{42}{3}$. The volume is therefore $14$ cubic meters.
