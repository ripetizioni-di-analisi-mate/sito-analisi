A kitchen timer is set in minutes. Which statement about the minute is true?

- It is the SI base unit of time
- It is a multiple of the second written with a prefix
- It is an SI base unit, like the hour
- It is a unit used alongside the SI, while the SI unit of time is the second

## Explanation

The SI base unit of time is the second. The minute and the hour are units used alongside the SI, with $1\,\mathrm{min}=60\,\mathrm{s}$ and $1\,\mathrm{h}=3600\,\mathrm{s}$. The factor $60$ is not a power of ten, while prefixes indicate factors such as $1000$, $100$, or $0.001$. Therefore, the minute is **a unit used alongside the SI**.
