An area measures $3$ square meters. What is its value in square centimeters?

- $300\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $3000\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $3000000\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $30000\,\mathrm{cm^2}$

## Explanation

Each side of a one-meter square measures $100$ centimeters. Its area is therefore $100^2=10000$ square centimeters. An area of $3$ square meters equals $30000$ square centimeters.
