A door is $0.8\,\mathrm{m}$ wide. If its width is expressed in meters, centimeters, millimeters, and kilometers, in which unit is the numerical value largest?

- In centimeters
- In millimeters
- In meters
- In kilometers

## Explanation

Changing to a smaller unit increases the numerical value, while the width stays the same. We have $0.8\,\mathrm{m}=80\,\mathrm{cm}=800\,\mathrm{mm}=0.0008\,\mathrm{km}$. The millimeter is the smallest of the four units, so the largest number, $800$, is obtained **in millimeters**.
