An observation lasts $4$ minutes and $12$ seconds. How many seconds should the stopwatch record?

- $212\,\mathrm{s}$
- $720\,\mathrm{s}$
- $412\,\mathrm{s}$
- $252\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Four minutes give $4\cdot 60=240\,\mathrm{s}$. Add the final twelve seconds to obtain $252\,\mathrm{s}$.
