Luca writes $640\,\mathrm{mm}=6.40\,\mathrm{m}$. What must he divide his result in metres by to correct it?

- $10$
- $100$
- $1000$
- $0.1$

## Explanation

A metre contains a thousand millimetres, so $640\,\mathrm{mm}=0.640\,\mathrm{m}$. Luca's result is ten times the correct value.
