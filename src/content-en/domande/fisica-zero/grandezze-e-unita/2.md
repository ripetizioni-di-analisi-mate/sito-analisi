To find the surface area of a rectangular kitchen tile, the two sides are measured with a tape measure and then multiplied. Which of the following is an indirect measurement?

- The length of the longer side read on the tape measure
- The length of the shorter side read on the tape measure
- The mass of the tile read on the scale
- The area of the tile

## Explanation

A direct measurement is obtained by comparing the quantity with a suitable instrument, such as a tape measure or a scale. The two sides are read on the tape measure and the mass is read on the scale, so these are direct measurements. The area is not read on an instrument; it is obtained by multiplying the two sides. Therefore, the area of the tile is an **indirect measurement**.
