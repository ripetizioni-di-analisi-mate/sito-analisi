Two samples have the same mass. The first material is twice as dense as the second. How does the volume of the first compare with the volume of the second?

- It is twice as large.
- It is equal.
- It is one quarter as large.
- It is half as large.

## Explanation

At fixed mass, $V=\dfrac{m}{\rho} $. Doubling the denominator halves the volume needed to contain the same mass.
