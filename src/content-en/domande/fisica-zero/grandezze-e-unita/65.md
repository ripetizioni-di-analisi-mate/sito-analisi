An ice cube has edges $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ long. What is its volume in milliliters?

- $9\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $270\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $27\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $2.7\,\mathrm{mL}$

## Explanation

A cube is a rectangular prism whose three dimensions are equal, so its volume is the product of three edges. This gives $V=3\cdot3\cdot3\,\mathrm{cm^3}=27\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. Since $1\,\mathrm{mL}=1\,\mathrm{cm^3}$, the volume of the ice cube is $27\,\mathrm{mL}$.
