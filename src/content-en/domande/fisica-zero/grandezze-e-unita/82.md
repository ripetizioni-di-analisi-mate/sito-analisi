A dispenser delivers $200\,\mathrm{mL/min}$ for thirty seconds and then releases one final $25\,\mathrm{mL}$ pulse. How many millilitres does it deliver in total?

- $100\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $75\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $125\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $225\,\mathrm{mL}$

## Explanation

Thirty seconds is half a minute, so the first stage delivers $100\,\mathrm{mL}$. Add the volume of the final pulse to get $125\,\mathrm{mL}$.
