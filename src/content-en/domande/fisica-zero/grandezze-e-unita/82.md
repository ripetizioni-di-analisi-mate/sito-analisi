A plastic bottle full of water is placed in the freezer. The water turns into ice, its mass stays the same, and its volume increases. What happens to the density?

- It increases
- It stays the same
- It doubles
- It decreases

## Explanation

Density is the ratio $\rho=\dfrac{m}{V}$. The mass in the numerator stays the same, while the volume in the denominator increases. A fraction with the same numerator and a larger denominator has a smaller value. Therefore, the density **decreases**, and indeed ice has a density of about $0.92\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$, lower than that of water.
