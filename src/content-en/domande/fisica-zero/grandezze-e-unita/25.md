A rectangular field has the side lengths shown in the figure. What is its perimeter in meters?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 xmin=-2.2 xmax=7 ymin=-1.2 ymax=4.8 poligoni="0,0 6,0 6,4 0,4" etichette="2.6,-0.6,60 m;-2,2,0.04 km" titolo="Top view of the rectangular field, with the lengths of two adjacent sides."}

- $200\,\mathrm{m}$
- $120.08\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2400\,\mathrm{m}$
- $100\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Before adding the sides, express them in the same unit. Since $1\,\mathrm{km}=1000\,\mathrm{m}$, the short side is $0.04\cdot1000\,\mathrm{m}=40\,\mathrm{m}$ long. The perimeter of a rectangle is the sum of its four sides, which is twice the sum of two adjacent sides. The perimeter is therefore $2\cdot(60+40)\,\mathrm{m}=200\,\mathrm{m}$.
