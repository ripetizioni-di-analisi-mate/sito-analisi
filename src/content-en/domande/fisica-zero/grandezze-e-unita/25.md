A material has a density of $800$ kilograms per cubic meter. What is the mass of $5$ cubic meters of this material?

- $4000\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $160\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.00625\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $400\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Rearranging $\rho=\dfrac{m}{V}$ gives $m=\rho V$. Therefore, multiply $800$ by $5$. The mass is $4000$ kilograms.
