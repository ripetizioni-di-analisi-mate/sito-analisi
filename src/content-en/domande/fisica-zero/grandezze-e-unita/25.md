The start of a ruler is broken. The ends of a pencil line up with the $3.4\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $16.1\,\mathrm{cm}$ marks. How long is it?

- $19.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $16.1\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $12.7\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $9.3\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The marks are positions measured from the same origin. Subtract the starting position from the final one, $16.1-3.4=12.7\,\mathrm{cm}$.
