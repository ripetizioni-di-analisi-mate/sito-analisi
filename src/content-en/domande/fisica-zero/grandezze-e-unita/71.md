A $10\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ bar contains only two solid materials with densities of $2$ and $5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. Its mass is $32\,\mathrm{g}$ and the volumes add. What volume does the denser material occupy?

- $8\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $4\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $2.4\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $6\,\mathrm{cm^3}$

## Explanation

If the bar were all light material, its mass would be $20\,\mathrm{g}$. Each cubic centimetre replaced by the denser material adds $5-2=3\,\mathrm{g}$, so $\dfrac{32-20}{3}=4\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ is required.
