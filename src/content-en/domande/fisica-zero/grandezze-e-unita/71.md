A bottle contains $2\,\mathrm{L}$ of olive oil, which has a density of $0.92\,\mathrm{kg/L}$. What is the mass of the oil?

- $0.46\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $18.4\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.184\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $1.84\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

From the definition $\rho=\dfrac{m}{V}$, the mass is the product of density and volume, $m=\rho\,V$. The density is in $\mathrm{kg/L}$ and the volume is in liters, so the units are compatible. Therefore, $m=0.92\,\mathrm{kg/L}\cdot2\,\mathrm{L}=1.84\,\mathrm{kg}$.
