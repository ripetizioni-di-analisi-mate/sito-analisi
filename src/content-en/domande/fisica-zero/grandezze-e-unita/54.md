A model tank has all lengths equal to one fifth of the actual tank's lengths. What fraction of the actual volume does the model occupy?

- $\dfrac{1}{125}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{25}$
- $\dfrac{1}{15}$

## Explanation

The change of scale applies to all three dimensions. The volume fraction is $(\dfrac{1}{5})^3=\dfrac{1}{125}$.
