A wall to be painted measures $4\,\mathrm{m}$ by $2.7\,\mathrm{m}$. One liter of paint covers $6\,\mathrm{m^2}$. How many liters are needed for one coat?

- $1.8\,\mathrm{L}$
- $0.56\,\mathrm{L}$
- $64.8\,\mathrm{L}$
- $1.1\,\mathrm{L}$

## Explanation

The area of the wall is $4\cdot2.7\,\mathrm{m^2}=10.8\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Each liter covers $6\,\mathrm{m^2}$, so the number of liters is the number of times $6\,\mathrm{m^2}$ fits into $10.8\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Therefore, $\dfrac{10.8}{6}\,\mathrm{L}=1.8\,\mathrm{L}$ of paint is needed.
