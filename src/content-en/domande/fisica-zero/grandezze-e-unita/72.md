Honey has a density of $1.4\,\mathrm{kg/L}$. What volume, in liters, do the $700\,\mathrm{g}$ of honey in a jar occupy?

- $0.5\,\mathrm{L}$
- $0.98\,\mathrm{L}$
- $2\,\mathrm{L}$
- $500\,\mathrm{L}$

## Explanation

The density is given in $\mathrm{kg/L}$, so the mass is expressed in kilograms, giving $700\,\mathrm{g}=0.7\,\mathrm{kg}$. From the definition $\rho=\dfrac{m}{V}$, we get $V=\dfrac{m}{\rho}$. The volume is therefore $V=\dfrac{0.7}{1.4}\,\mathrm{L}=0.5\,\mathrm{L}$.
