Two sensors follow $y_A=2x+1$ and $y_B=7-x$. At which horizontal coordinate do they have the same reading?

::grafico{linee="0,1 3,7;0,7 6,1" etichette="2.9,7.4,A;5.3,2.4,B" xmin=0 xmax=6 ymin=0 ymax=8 nomi="x,y" legenda=0 titolo="The two sensor response lines."}

- $6$
- $2$
- $5$
- $3$

## Explanation

The ordinates agree where the lines cross. From $2x+1=7-x$ obtain $3x=6$, hence $x=2$.
