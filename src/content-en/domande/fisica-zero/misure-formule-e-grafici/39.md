The length of a trail is measured as $(1500\pm30)\,\mathrm{m}$ and then rewritten in kilometers. What happens to the relative uncertainty?

- It becomes one thousand times smaller
- It becomes one thousand times larger
- It stays the same, $2\%$
- It cannot be calculated after the change of unit

## Explanation

In meters the relative uncertainty is $\dfrac{30}{1500}=0.02$, that is, $2\%$. In kilometers the measurement becomes $(1.500\pm0.030)\,\mathrm{km}$, because the value and the uncertainty are both divided by one thousand. Their ratio is $\dfrac{0.030}{1.500}=0.02$. Therefore the relative uncertainty **stays the same**.
