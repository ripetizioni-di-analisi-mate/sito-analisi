A measurement is reported as $(140\pm2)$ cm. What is the relative uncertainty?

- $70$
- $2$
- $\dfrac{1}{70}$
- $\dfrac{1}{7}$

## Explanation

Relative uncertainty is the ratio of the absolute uncertainty to the measured value. The two quantities must be expressed in the same unit. This gives $\dfrac{2}{140}=\dfrac{1}{70}$, and the units cancel.
