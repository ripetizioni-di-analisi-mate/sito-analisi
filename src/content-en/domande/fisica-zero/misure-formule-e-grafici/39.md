A standard measured once per minute gives $20.0$, $20.2$, $20.4$ and $20.6\,\mathrm{g}$ in that order. What check makes most sense before replacing the series with a mean?

- Use the median as proof that the response is stable over time.
- Increase only the number of digits reported for the mean.
- Divide the half-range by the number of readings and declare that drift is negligible.
- Check whether the zero or measurement conditions change over time.

## Explanation

The steady increase follows time order. Drift in the instrument or conditions could produce this sequence, and a single mean would conceal it.
