At time $2\,\mathrm{s}$, a measurement gives $y=5.0\pm 0.4$. A curve with no uncertainty of its own predicts $5.6$. By how much does the prediction exceed the top of the error bar?

- $1.0$
- $0.4$
- $0.2$
- $0.6$

## Explanation

The bar reaches $5.0+0.4=5.4$. The prediction exceeds this limit by $5.6-5.4=0.2$.
