Which of these pairs consists of directly proportional quantities?

- The area of a disk and its radius
- The circumference of a bicycle wheel and its radius
- The time to travel $10\,\mathrm{km}$ and the speed
- The mass of a jar full of honey and the volume of the honey

## Explanation

The circumference is $C=2\pi r$, so the ratio $\dfrac{C}{r}=2\pi$ is constant and the two quantities are directly proportional. The area of a disk is $\pi r^2$, so its ratio to the radius is $\pi r$, which changes with the radius. The time over a fixed distance is inversely proportional to the speed. The mass of the full jar also includes the mass of the glass, so its ratio to the volume of honey is not constant.
