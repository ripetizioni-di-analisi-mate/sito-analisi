Heart rate is $f=\dfrac{n}{t}$, where $n$ is the number of beats counted in the time $t$. At rest, a person has $f=80$ beats per minute. How many beats are counted in $15\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $1200$
- $20$
- $5.3$
- $320$

## Explanation

Multiplying both sides of $f=\dfrac{n}{t}$ by $t$ gives $n=ft$. The rate is given in beats per minute, so the time must be expressed in minutes: $15\,\mathrm{s}=0.25\,\mathrm{min}$. Therefore
$$
n=80\,\frac{\text{beats}}{\mathrm{min}}\cdot0.25\,\mathrm{min}=20.
$$
The value $1200$ would result from multiplying $80$ by $15$ without converting the seconds to minutes.
