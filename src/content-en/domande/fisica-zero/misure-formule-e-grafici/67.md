On the temperature-time graph, a straight segment joins $(1\,\mathrm{min}, 12\,\mathrm{K})$ and $(4\,\mathrm{min}, 18\,\mathrm{K})$. What is the increase in temperature per minute?

::grafico{linee="1,12 4,18" punti="1,12;4,18" xmin=0 xmax=5 ymin=0 ymax=20 nomi="t,T" legenda=0 titolo="Time in minutes and temperature in kelvin."}

- $0.5\,\mathrm{K/min}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{K/min}$
- $6\,\mathrm{K/min}$
- $2\,\mathrm{K/min}$

## Explanation

The vertical change is $6\,\mathrm{K}$ and the horizontal change is $3\,\mathrm{min}$. The slope is $\dfrac{6}{3}=2\,\mathrm{K/min}$.
