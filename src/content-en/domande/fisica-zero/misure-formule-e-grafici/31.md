The mean of four readings is $12\,\mathrm{s}$. The first three are $8$, $13$ and $11$ seconds. What is the fourth?

- $16\,\mathrm{s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The sum of the readings must be $4\cdot 12=48\,\mathrm{s}$. The first three add to $32\,\mathrm{s}$, leaving $16\,\mathrm{s}$ for the fourth.
