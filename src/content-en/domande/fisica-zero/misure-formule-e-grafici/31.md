A mass is reported as $(600\pm3)$ g. What is the upper endpoint of the associated interval?

- $597\,\mathrm{g}$
- $600\,\mathrm{g}$
- $603\,\mathrm{g}$
- $6\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

Add the uncertainty to the central value to find the upper endpoint. This gives $600+3$. Therefore the endpoint is $603$ grams.
