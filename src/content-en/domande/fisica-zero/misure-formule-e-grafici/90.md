A burning candle has length $L=20-2t$, with $L$ in centimeters and $t$ in hours. After how many hours is the candle $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ long?

- $6\,\mathrm{h}$
- $4\,\mathrm{h}$
- $16\,\mathrm{h}$
- $10\,\mathrm{h}$

## Explanation

Setting $L=12$ in the formula gives $12=20-2t$. Adding $2t$ to both sides and subtracting $12$ from both sides gives $2t=8$. Dividing by $2$ gives $t=4$, so the candle is $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ long after $4\,\mathrm{h}$. The value $10\,\mathrm{h}$ is instead the time after which the candle is completely burned, that is, $L=0$.
