A recorder stores only $(0\,\mathrm{s},1\,\mathrm{V})$ and $(4\,\mathrm{s},1\,\mathrm{V})$. Software joins them with a horizontal segment. What can you say about the voltage after two seconds without additional assumptions?

::grafico{linee="0,1 4,1" punti="0,1;4,1" xmin=0 xmax=5 ymin=0 ymax=3 nomi="t,V" legenda=0 titolo="The endpoints are the only recorded readings."}

- It was at least $1\,\mathrm{V}$ because no recorded value is lower.
- The value at two seconds is not determined by the two readings.
- It was certainly $1\,\mathrm{V}$ because the segment is horizontal.
- It was at most $1\,\mathrm{V}$ because no recorded value exceeds it.

## Explanation

The segment shows a choice of interpolation. A transient pulse between acquisitions could have left both recorded readings unchanged.
