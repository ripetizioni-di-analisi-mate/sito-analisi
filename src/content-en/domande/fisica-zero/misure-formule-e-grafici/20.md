The sign in front of an underpass gives a maximum height of $3.80\,\mathrm{m}$. The height of a van is $(3.75\pm0.03)\,\mathrm{m}$. Which statement about the upper endpoint of the associated interval is correct?

- It is $3.81\,\mathrm{m}$, so it exceeds $3.80\,\mathrm{m}$
- It is $3.75\,\mathrm{m}$, so it does not exceed $3.80\,\mathrm{m}$
- It is $3.78\,\mathrm{m}$, so it does not exceed $3.80\,\mathrm{m}$
- It is $3.83\,\mathrm{m}$, so it exceeds $3.80\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The upper endpoint is found by adding the uncertainty to the measured value:
$$
h_{\max}=(3.75+0.03)\,\mathrm{m}=3.78\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
Since $3.78\,\mathrm{m}<3.80\,\mathrm{m}$, even the highest value of the associated interval stays below the limit on the sign. The value $3.75\,\mathrm{m}$ is the central value, not the upper endpoint.
