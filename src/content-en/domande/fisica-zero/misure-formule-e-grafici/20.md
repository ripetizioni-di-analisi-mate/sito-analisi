A scale reads zero when empty and $80\,\mathrm{g}$ for a $100\,\mathrm{g}$ standard. If its response is proportional, what actual mass corresponds to a reading of $120\,\mathrm{g}$?

- $150\,\mathrm{g}$
- $180\,\mathrm{g}$
- $100\,\mathrm{g}$
- $96\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

The reading is $\dfrac{80}{100}=0.8$ times the actual mass. Divide the reading by $0.8$ to invert the relation, giving $\dfrac{120}{0.8}=150\,\mathrm{g}$.
