On a scale with an assigned instrumental uncertainty of $1\,\mathrm{g}$, a sandwich is weighed three times: $125\,\mathrm{g}$, $125\,\mathrm{g}$, and $126\,\mathrm{g}$. The problem specifies using the larger of the half-range and the instrumental uncertainty as the uncertainty. How is the measurement written?

- $(125.3\pm0.5)\,\mathrm{g}$
- $(126\pm1)\,\mathrm{g}$
- $(376\pm1)\,\mathrm{g}$
- $(125\pm1)\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

The mean is $\dfrac{125+125+126}{3}\,\mathrm{g}\approx125.3\,\mathrm{g}$. The half-range is $\dfrac{126-125}{2}\,\mathrm{g}=0.5\,\mathrm{g}$, which is less than the instrumental uncertainty of $1\,\mathrm{g}$. Therefore, according to the specified rule, the uncertainty is $1\,\mathrm{g}$. The measured value is rounded to the same place as the uncertainty, that is, to the units, so the measurement is $(125\pm1)\,\mathrm{g}$.
