A measurement interval extends from $7.4\,\mathrm{mL}$ to $8.2\,\mathrm{mL}$. Which central value and symmetric uncertainty describe it?

- $(8.2\pm 0.8)\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $(7.8\pm 0.4)\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $(7.4\pm 0.4)\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $(7.8\pm 0.8)\,\mathrm{mL}$

## Explanation

The centre is the mean of the endpoints, $\dfrac{7.4+8.2}{2}=7.8$. The uncertainty is half the width, $\dfrac{8.2-7.4}{2}=0.4\,\mathrm{mL}$.
