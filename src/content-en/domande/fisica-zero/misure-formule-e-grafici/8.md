A measuring jug has a mark every $25\,\mathrm{mL}$. How many scale intervals separate the $100\,\mathrm{mL}$ mark from the $200\,\mathrm{mL}$ mark?

- $5$
- $25$
- $100$
- $4$

## Explanation

The difference between the two readings is $200\,\mathrm{mL}-100\,\mathrm{mL}=100\,\mathrm{mL}$. Each interval is $25\,\mathrm{mL}$, so the number of intervals is
$$
\frac{100\,\mathrm{mL}}{25\,\mathrm{mL}}=4.
$$
Counting the two end marks, five marks are involved, because the intervals are the spaces between consecutive marks.
