In each trial you set the voltage in volts and measure the current in amperes. You want to plot the response against the controlled variable. How do you label the axes?

- Horizontal current (A), vertical voltage (V).
- Horizontal current (V), vertical voltage (A).
- Horizontal voltage (V), vertical current (A).
- Horizontal voltage (A), vertical current (V).

## Explanation

The controlled input goes on the horizontal axis. Put the response on the vertical axis and give each quantity its unit.
