A stone with a mass of $50\,\mathrm{g}$ is placed in a graduated cylinder containing water, and the level rises from $50\,\mathrm{mL}$ to $70\,\mathrm{mL}$. What is the density of the stone?

- $0.71\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $1.0\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$
- $0.4\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$

## Explanation

The volume of the stone equals the rise in the water level, that is, $70\,\mathrm{mL}-50\,\mathrm{mL}=20\,\mathrm{mL}=20\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. Density is the ratio of mass to volume, so
$$
\rho=\frac{50\,\mathrm{g}}{20\,\mathrm{cm^3}}=2.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}.
$$
The value $0.71\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$ would result from using the final reading $70\,\mathrm{mL}$ as the volume, which also includes the water.
