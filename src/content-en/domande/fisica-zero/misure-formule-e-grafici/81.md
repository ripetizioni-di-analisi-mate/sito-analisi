Two inputs differ by $0.4\,\mathrm{cm}$. The display needs a difference of at least $1\,\mathrm{V}$ between outputs, and $\Delta V=k\Delta x$. What is the minimum positive $k$ that makes the inputs distinguishable?

- $2.5\,\mathrm{V/cm}$
- $0.4\,\mathrm{V/cm}$
- $1\,\mathrm{V/cm}$
- $4\,\mathrm{V/cm}$

## Explanation

Require $k\cdot 0.4\ge 1$. Dividing by $0.4\,\mathrm{cm}$ gives $k\ge 2.5\,\mathrm{V/cm}$.
