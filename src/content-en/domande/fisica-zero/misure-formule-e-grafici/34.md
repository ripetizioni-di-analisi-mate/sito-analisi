For the same apple, a kitchen scale gives the masses $182\,\mathrm{g}$, $186\,\mathrm{g}$, and $184\,\mathrm{g}$. With the mean as the value and the half-range as the uncertainty, what is the percentage uncertainty, rounded to the tenths?

- $1.1\%$
- $2.2\%$
- $0.5\%$
- $11\%$

## Explanation

The mean is $\dfrac{182+186+184}{3}\,\mathrm{g}=184\,\mathrm{g}$. The half-range is $\dfrac{186-182}{2}\,\mathrm{g}=2\,\mathrm{g}$. The relative uncertainty is therefore
$$
u_r=\frac{2\,\mathrm{g}}{184\,\mathrm{g}}\approx0.0109.
$$
Multiplying by $100$ gives about $1.1\%$. The value $2.2\%$ would result from using the difference between the largest and smallest readings without dividing it by two.
