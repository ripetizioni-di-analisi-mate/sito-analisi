Three readings are $5$, $8$ and $11\,\mathrm{s}$. Which additional reading leaves the half-range unchanged?

- $13\,\mathrm{s}$
- $7\,\mathrm{s}$
- $15\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The half-range depends only on the extremes. $7\,\mathrm{s}$ lies between $5$ and $11\,\mathrm{s}$, so neither extreme changes.
