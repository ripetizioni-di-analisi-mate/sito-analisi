A measurement is reported as $(120\pm2)$ cm. What is the relative uncertainty?

- $60$
- $\dfrac{1}{60}$
- $2$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$

## Explanation

Relative uncertainty is the ratio of the absolute uncertainty to the measured value. The two quantities must be expressed in the same unit. This gives $\dfrac{2}{120}=\dfrac{1}{60}$, and the units cancel.
