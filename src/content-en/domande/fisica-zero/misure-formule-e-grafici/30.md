The endpoints of a rod read $2.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $12.0\,\mathrm{cm}$. The ruler adds the same unknown offset of up to $0.1\,\mathrm{cm}$ to both readings, with no other errors. How much does this offset contribute to the uncertainty in the length?

- $0.05\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.2\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.1\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Length is the difference between the positions. Subtracting two readings increased by the same amount cancels that amount exactly.
