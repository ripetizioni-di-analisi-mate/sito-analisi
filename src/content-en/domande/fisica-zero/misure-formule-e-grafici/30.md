A bottle contains $750\,\mathrm{mL}$ of liquid detergent with a mass of $900\,\mathrm{g}$. This pair of values is to be plotted on a graph that shows volume in liters on the horizontal axis and mass in kilograms on the vertical axis. What are the horizontal and vertical coordinates of the point?

- Horizontal coordinate $750$ and vertical coordinate $900$
- Horizontal coordinate $7.5$ and vertical coordinate $9$
- Horizontal coordinate $0.75$ and vertical coordinate $900$
- Horizontal coordinate $0.75$ and vertical coordinate $0.9$

## Explanation

The coordinates of the point are the values of the two quantities expressed in the units shown on the axes. Since $1\,\mathrm{L}=1000\,\mathrm{mL}$, the volume is $\dfrac{750}{1000}\,\mathrm{L}=0.75\,\mathrm{L}$. Since $1\,\mathrm{kg}=1000\,\mathrm{g}$, the mass is $\dfrac{900}{1000}\,\mathrm{kg}=0.9\,\mathrm{kg}$. Therefore the point has horizontal coordinate $0.75$ and vertical coordinate $0.9$. The numbers $750$ and $900$ would be the coordinates of the point only with the axes in milliliters and grams.
