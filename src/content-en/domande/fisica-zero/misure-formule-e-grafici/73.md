The floor of a room is covered exactly with identical square tiles, without cutting any of them. What is the relationship between the number of tiles needed and the area of one tile?

- They are directly proportional
- They are inversely proportional
- The number of tiles does not depend on the area of one tile
- The number of tiles increases when the area of one tile increases

## Explanation

The number of tiles $N$ multiplied by the area $a$ of one tile gives the area of the floor, $Na=A$. The floor area is fixed, so the product $Na$ is constant. Therefore the two quantities are **inversely proportional**. With tiles of twice the area, for example, half as many are needed.
