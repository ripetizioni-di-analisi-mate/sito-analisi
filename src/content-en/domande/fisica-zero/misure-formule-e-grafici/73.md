A point lies three divisions left of the origin and two above it. Each division is two horizontal units or five vertical units. What are its coordinates?

- $(-3,2)$
- $(6,10)$
- $(-6,10)$
- $(-10,6)$

## Explanation

Leftward gives a negative abscissa, so $x=-3\cdot 2=-6$. Upward gives a positive ordinate, so $y=2\cdot 5=10$.
