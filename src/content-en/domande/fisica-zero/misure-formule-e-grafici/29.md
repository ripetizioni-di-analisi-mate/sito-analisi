Two students time the same fall of a ball from a balcony. The first student's readings are $1.9\,\mathrm{s}$, $2.0\,\mathrm{s}$, and $2.1\,\mathrm{s}$; the second student's readings are $1.6\,\mathrm{s}$, $2.0\,\mathrm{s}$, and $2.4\,\mathrm{s}$. Which statement is true?

- The two series have the same half-range and different means
- The second series has a larger mean than the first
- The two series have the same mean, and the second has a larger half-range
- The two series have the same mean and the same half-range

## Explanation

Both series add up to $6.0\,\mathrm{s}$, so they have the same mean, $2.0\,\mathrm{s}$. The half-range of the first series is $\dfrac{2.1-1.9}{2}\,\mathrm{s}=0.1\,\mathrm{s}$. The half-range of the second is $\dfrac{2.4-1.6}{2}\,\mathrm{s}=0.4\,\mathrm{s}$. Therefore the second series has the same mean and a **larger half-range**, that is, its readings are more spread out.
