The possible durations for A range from $8$ to $11\,\mathrm{s}$, and for B from $13$ to $15\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the least amount by which B exceeds A?

- $2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $7\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

To minimise the difference, use the smallest B and the largest A. The guaranteed margin is $13-11=2\,\mathrm{s}$.
