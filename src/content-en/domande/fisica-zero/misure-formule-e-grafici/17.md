From left to right a scale shows $80$ and then $60$ four equal intervals apart. What is the value of the third mark to the right of $80$?

- $65$
- $95$
- $75$
- $55$

## Explanation

Each step to the right decreases the reading by $\dfrac{80-60}{4}=5$. After three steps it reads $80-3\cdot 5=65$.
