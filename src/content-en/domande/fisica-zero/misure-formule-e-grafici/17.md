Two thermometers placed in the same oven read $(180\pm5)\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ and $(192\pm5)\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. Which statement about the intervals associated with the two measurements is true?

- They have no values in common
- They share the values from $185\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ to $187\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- They share only the value $186\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- The second interval contains the first

## Explanation

The first interval runs from $175\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ to $185\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. The second runs from $187\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ to $197\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. The upper endpoint of the first, $185\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$, is less than the lower endpoint of the second, $187\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. Therefore the two intervals **have no values in common**, and the values between $185\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ and $187\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ belong to neither of them.
