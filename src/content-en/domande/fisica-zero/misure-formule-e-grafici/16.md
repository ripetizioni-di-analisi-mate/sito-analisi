The interval associated with a measurement of the length of a cutting board runs from $39.6\,\mathrm{cm}$ to $40.4\,\mathrm{cm}$. How is the measurement written with its absolute uncertainty?

- $(40.0\pm0.4)\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $(40.0\pm0.8)\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $(39.6\pm0.4)\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $(40.4\pm0.8)\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The measured value is the center of the interval, namely
$$
\frac{39.6+40.4}{2}\,\mathrm{cm}=40.0\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
The absolute uncertainty is the distance from the center to each endpoint, so it equals $40.4\,\mathrm{cm}-40.0\,\mathrm{cm}=0.4\,\mathrm{cm}$. Therefore the measurement is $(40.0\pm0.4)\,\mathrm{cm}$. The value $0.8\,\mathrm{cm}$ is the width of the interval, twice the uncertainty.
