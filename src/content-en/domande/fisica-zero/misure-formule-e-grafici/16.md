A thermometer is marked from $-10$ to $50$ degrees Celsius. Its column rises past the last mark. What can you record without extrapolating?

- The temperature is exactly $51$ degrees Celsius.
- The temperature is at most $50$ degrees Celsius.
- The temperature is exactly $50$ degrees Celsius.
- The temperature exceeds $50$ degrees Celsius.

## Explanation

The last mark gives a threshold. Beyond that mark the instrument assigns no graduated numerical value to the column position.
