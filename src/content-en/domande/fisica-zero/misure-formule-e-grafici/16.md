The formula is $A=bh$. If $A=24$ square meters and $b=4$ m, what is $h$?

- $96\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Divide the area by the positive base length. This gives $h=\dfrac{A}{b}=\dfrac{24}{4}$. The units simplify to meters, so $h=6$ m.
