For equally spaced inputs $0,1,2,3$, a table shows outputs $2,5,8,11$. Which output at $x=4$ continues the same successive increments?

- $17$
- $22$
- $14$
- $16$

## Explanation

The output rises by three at every step. Adding the same increment to the final value gives $11+3=14$.
