The height of a basil seedling measured on the first three days is $2\,\mathrm{cm}$, $4\,\mathrm{cm}$, and $6\,\mathrm{cm}$. Which statement is justified by these data?

- On the hundredth day the seedling will be $200\,\mathrm{cm}$ tall
- The data are consistent with direct proportionality over the three observed days and by themselves say nothing about the following days
- The data prove that the seedling grows $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ per day forever
- The data are not consistent with direct proportionality

## Explanation

For the three pairs, the ratio of height to day is $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ per day, so the data are consistent with direct proportionality. A limited set of points, however, does not prove that the same law holds outside the observed interval. Therefore the data do not allow a prediction of the height on the hundredth day.
