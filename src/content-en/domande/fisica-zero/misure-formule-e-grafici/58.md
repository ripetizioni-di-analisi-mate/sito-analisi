The average speed of a train is $v=\dfrac{d}{t}$, where $d$ is the distance traveled and $t$ the time taken. A train travels $150\,\mathrm{km}$ at an average speed of $100\,\mathrm{km/h}$. How long does it take?

- $0.67\,\mathrm{h}$
- $15\,000\,\mathrm{h}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{h}$
- $50\,\mathrm{h}$

## Explanation

Multiplying both sides of $v=\dfrac{d}{t}$ by $t$ gives $vt=d$. The speed is positive, so dividing by $v$ gives $t=\dfrac{d}{v}$. Substituting the data,
$$
t=\frac{150\,\mathrm{km}}{100\,\mathrm{km/h}}=1.5\,\mathrm{h}.
$$
The value $0.67\,\mathrm{h}$ would result from dividing the speed by the distance.
