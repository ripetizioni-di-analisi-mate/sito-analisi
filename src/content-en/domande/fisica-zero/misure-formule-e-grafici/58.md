A filled vessel has total mass $m=t+\rho  V$. Two readings at different volumes use the same vessel. Which operation eliminates the tare $t$ without weighing it?

- Subtract the two equations side by side.
- Multiply each equation by its respective mass.
- Add the two total masses.
- Add them and divide by two.

## Explanation

The same tare is an additive term in both equations. Subtraction gives $m_2-m_1=\rho (V_2-V_1)$, which contains only differences.
