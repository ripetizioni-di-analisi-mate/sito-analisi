A nail is lying on a ruler marked in millimeters. The head of the nail lines up with the $3.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ mark and the tip with the $7.6\,\mathrm{cm}$ mark. How long is the nail?

- $7.6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $4.6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $10.6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $3.8\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The nail does not start at the zero mark, so its length is the difference between the two readings. This gives
$$
\ell=7.6\,\mathrm{cm}-3.0\,\mathrm{cm}=4.6\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
The reading $7.6\,\mathrm{cm}$ would give the length only if the head of the nail were at the zero mark.
