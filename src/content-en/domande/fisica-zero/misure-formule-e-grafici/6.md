A parcel has a measured mass of $(250\pm 5)\,\mathrm{g}$. What is the percentage uncertainty?

- $20\,\mathrm{\%}$
- $0.02\,\mathrm{\%}$
- $2\,\mathrm{\%}$
- $5\,\mathrm{\%}$

## Explanation

Form the ratio $\dfrac{5}{250}=0.02$. Multiply it by one hundred to express it as a percentage.
