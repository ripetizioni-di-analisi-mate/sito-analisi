The motion of a cart is plotted on graph paper. On the horizontal axis one square represents $2\,\mathrm{s}$, and on the vertical axis one square represents $5\,\mathrm{m}$. Starting from the origin, where is the point $t=6\,\mathrm{s}$, $x=20\,\mathrm{m}$ drawn?

- $3$ squares to the right and $4$ up
- $6$ squares to the right and $20$ up
- $4$ squares to the right and $3$ up
- $12$ squares to the right and $100$ up

## Explanation

On the horizontal axis each square represents $2\,\mathrm{s}$, so $6\,\mathrm{s}$ corresponds to $\dfrac{6}{2}=3$ squares. On the vertical axis each square represents $5\,\mathrm{m}$, so $20\,\mathrm{m}$ corresponds to $\dfrac{20}{5}=4$ squares. Therefore the point is $3$ squares to the right and $4$ up. The scales of the two axes are different, and each keeps equal intervals for equal increments.
