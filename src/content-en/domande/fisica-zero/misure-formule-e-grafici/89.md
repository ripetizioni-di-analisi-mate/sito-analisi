A signal returns to the same value every $4\,\mathrm{s}$ and varies within each cycle. You always sample it at $0,4,8,12\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the main limitation of the table?

- It samples the same phase every time and can make a varying signal look constant.
- The readings prove that the signal never varies.
- The maximum must occur exactly halfway through the table.
- The period becomes $12\,\mathrm{s}$ because the observations end there.

## Explanation

All sampling times differ by multiples of the period. Adding readings at other phases of the cycle would allow the variation to be detected.
