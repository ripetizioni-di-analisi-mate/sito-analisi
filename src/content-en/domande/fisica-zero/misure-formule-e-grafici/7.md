Measurements A and B are $(40\pm 1)\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $(100\pm 2)\,\mathrm{cm}$. Which has the smaller relative uncertainty?

- B, at $2\,\mathrm{\%}$.
- Both are $2\,\mathrm{\%}$.
- A, at $2.5\,\mathrm{\%}$.
- A, at $1\,\mathrm{\%}$.

## Explanation

The ratio is $\dfrac{1}{40}=0.025$ for A and $\dfrac{2}{100}=0.02$ for B. The latter corresponds to $2\,\mathrm{\%}$ and is smaller.
