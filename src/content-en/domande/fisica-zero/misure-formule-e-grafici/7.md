The formula is $A=bh$. If $A=18$ square meters and $b=3$ m, what is $h$?

- $54\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Divide the area by the positive base length. This gives $h=\dfrac{A}{b}=\dfrac{18}{3}$. The units simplify to meters, so $h=6$ m.
