A coach wants to time a $100\,\mathrm{m}$ sprint, which lasts about $12\,\mathrm{s}$, to the nearest hundredth of a second. Which stopwatch is suitable?

- Range $60\,\mathrm{s}$ and resolution $0.1\,\mathrm{s}$
- Range $60\,\mathrm{s}$ and resolution $0.01\,\mathrm{s}$
- Range $10\,\mathrm{s}$ and resolution $0.01\,\mathrm{s}$
- Range $1\,\mathrm{h}$ and resolution $1\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The stopwatch must satisfy two conditions at the same time:
$$
\begin{cases}
\text{range}\ge 12\,\mathrm{s}\\
\text{resolution}\le 0.01\,\mathrm{s}
\end{cases}
$$
The instrument with a range of $10\,\mathrm{s}$ has the required resolution, but the sprint exceeds its measuring range. The instruments with resolutions of $0.1\,\mathrm{s}$ and $1\,\mathrm{s}$ cannot distinguish hundredths. Therefore only the stopwatch with a range of $60\,\mathrm{s}$ and a resolution of $0.01\,\mathrm{s}$ is suitable.
