A length is reported as $(60\pm1)$ cm. What is the lower endpoint of the associated interval?

- $61\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $60\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $59\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Subtract the uncertainty from the central value to find the lower endpoint. This gives $60-1$. Therefore the endpoint is $59$ centimeters.
