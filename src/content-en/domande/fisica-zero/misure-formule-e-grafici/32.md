Two trials average $10\,\mathrm{s}$ and six more average $14\,\mathrm{s}$. By how many seconds does the mean of all eight trials exceed the unweighted mean $\dfrac{10+14}{2}$?

- $0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Recover the two totals, $2\cdot 10=20\,\mathrm{s}$ and $6\cdot 14=84\,\mathrm{s}$. The combined mean is $\dfrac{20+84}{8}=13\,\mathrm{s}$. This is one second above the unweighted mean of twelve.
