A signal reads $0,2,8$ at times $0,1,2\,\mathrm{s}$. By how much does the mean slope in the second interval exceed the mean slope over the whole period?

- $6$ units per second
- $0$ units per second
- $2$ units per second
- $4$ units per second

## Explanation

In the second interval the change is $8-2=6$ in one second. Over both intervals the mean slope is $\dfrac{8}{2}=4$ per second, so the excess is two.
