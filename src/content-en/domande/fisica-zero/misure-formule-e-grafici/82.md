A bathtub already contains $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ of water when the tap is opened, and the level rises by $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ per minute: $h=10+2t$, with $h$ in centimeters and $t$ in minutes. Which statement is true?

- The level $h$ and the time $t$ are directly proportional, because the graph is a straight line
- The level $h$ and the time $t$ are inversely proportional
- The level $h$ and the time $t$ are not directly proportional, because the ratio $\dfrac{h}{t}$ changes
- The rise in level $h-10$ is inversely proportional to the time

## Explanation

For $t=1$ we have $h=12$ and $\dfrac{h}{t}=12$; for $t=2$ we have $h=14$ and $\dfrac{h}{t}=7$. The ratio changes, so level and time **are not directly proportional**, even though the graph is a straight line. The line crosses the vertical axis at $10$, which is the initial level. It is the rise in level, $h-10=2t$, that is directly proportional to the time.
