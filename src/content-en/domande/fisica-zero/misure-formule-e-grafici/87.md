A point has input $4\pm 0.5$ and an output known exactly to equal $9$. What are the endpoints of its uncertainty segment in the $(x,y)$ plane?

- $(3.5,8.5)$ and $(4.5,9.5)$
- $(3.5,9)$ and $(4.5,9)$
- $(4,9)$ and $(4.5,9.5)$
- $(4,8.5)$ and $(4,9.5)$

## Explanation

Only $x$ varies, from $4-0.5$ to $4+0.5$. Keeping $y=9$ gives a horizontal segment.
