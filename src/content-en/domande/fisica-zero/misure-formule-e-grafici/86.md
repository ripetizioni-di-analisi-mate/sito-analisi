A database lists pairs as (mass in grams, volume in millilitres). One sample is stored as $(18,6)$. Where does the point go on a plot with volume horizontal and mass vertical?

- $(18,6)$
- $(3,1)$
- $(1,3)$
- $(6,18)$

## Explanation

Read the pair according to the database labels. The volume six goes on the horizontal axis and the mass eighteen on the vertical axis.
