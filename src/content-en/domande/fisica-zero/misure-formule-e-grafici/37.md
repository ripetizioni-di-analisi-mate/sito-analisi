Three readings of a time interval are $6$ s, $7$ s, and $8$ s. What is their arithmetic mean?

- $7\,\mathrm{s}$
- $21\,\mathrm{s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The mean is the sum of the readings divided by their number. The sum is $21$ seconds, and there are three readings. Therefore the mean is $7$ seconds.
