In a gym, four weights have stated masses whose uncertainties were estimated using the same rule. Which one has the smallest relative uncertainty?

- $(2.0\pm0.1)\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $(20.0\pm0.5)\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $(50\pm2)\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $(10.0\pm0.3)\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The uncertainties are comparable, so the ratios of absolute uncertainty to measured value are compared. This gives $\dfrac{0.5}{20.0}=0.025$, $\dfrac{0.1}{2.0}=0.05$, $\dfrac{2}{50}=0.04$, and $\dfrac{0.3}{10.0}=0.03$. The smallest ratio is $0.025$, that is, $2.5\%$. Therefore the $(20.0\pm0.5)\,\mathrm{kg}$ weight has the **smallest relative uncertainty**, even though its absolute uncertainty is not the smallest.
