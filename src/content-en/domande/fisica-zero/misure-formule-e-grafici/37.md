A certified standard is $50.0\,\mathrm{g}$. A scale repeatedly reads $52.0\,\mathrm{g}$ with very little scatter. Which feature do the data reveal?

- A resolution necessarily equal to $2\,\mathrm{g}$.
- Certified accuracy from internal agreement alone.
- Large scatter and no bias.
- Good repeatability and a bias from the reference.

## Explanation

The readings cluster together but lie about two grams from the reference. The first observation concerns repeatability and the second concerns accuracy.
