A measurement is reported as $(80\pm2)$ cm. What is the relative uncertainty?

- $40$
- $2$
- $0.25$
- $0.025$

## Explanation

Relative uncertainty is the ratio of the absolute uncertainty to the measured value. The two quantities must be expressed in the same unit. This gives $\dfrac{2}{80}=\dfrac{1}{40}$, and the units cancel.
