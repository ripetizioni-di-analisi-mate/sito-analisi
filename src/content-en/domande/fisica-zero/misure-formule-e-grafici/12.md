A person's height is reported as $h=(180\pm1)\,\mathrm{cm}$. What are the endpoints of the interval associated with the measurement?

- From $179\,\mathrm{cm}$ to $181\,\mathrm{cm}$
- From $178\,\mathrm{cm}$ to $182\,\mathrm{cm}$
- From $180\,\mathrm{cm}$ to $181\,\mathrm{cm}$
- From $179.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ to $180.5\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The endpoints are found by subtracting the absolute uncertainty from the measured value and adding it. This gives
$$
h_{\min}=(180-1)\,\mathrm{cm}=179\,\mathrm{cm},
\qquad
h_{\max}=(180+1)\,\mathrm{cm}=181\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
Therefore the associated interval runs from $179\,\mathrm{cm}$ to $181\,\mathrm{cm}$ and has a width of $2\,\mathrm{cm}$.
