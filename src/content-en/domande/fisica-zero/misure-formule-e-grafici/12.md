A rectangle with base $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ and height $8\,\mathrm{cm}$ is redrawn with the same area and a $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ base. What is its new height?

- $2\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $16\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The initial area is $3\cdot 8=24\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. To preserve it, the new base times the height must remain $24$, so $h=\dfrac{24}{6}=4\,\mathrm{cm}$.
