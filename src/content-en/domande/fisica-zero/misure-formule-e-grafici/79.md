An electric scooter moves at constant speed. A table gives the distance traveled: $300\,\mathrm{m}$ after $1\,\mathrm{min}$, $600\,\mathrm{m}$ after $2\,\mathrm{min}$, $900\,\mathrm{m}$ after $3\,\mathrm{min}$. Assuming the same relationship also holds between the measurements, what distance corresponds to $2.5\,\mathrm{min}$?

- $750\,\mathrm{m}$
- $600\,\mathrm{m}$
- $900\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1500\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

For the three pairs, the ratio of distance to time is $300\,\mathrm{m/min}$, so the data are consistent with the direct proportionality $d=300\,t$. The problem assumes that the relationship also holds between the measurements, so it can be used for $t=2.5\,\mathrm{min}$:
$$
d=300\,\mathrm{m/min}\cdot2.5\,\mathrm{min}=750\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
The value $1500\,\mathrm{m}$ would result from multiplying $2.5$ by the distance at $2\,\mathrm{min}$.
