A detector reports only $s<2$ whenever the signal is below threshold. The model predicts $s=1.5$ for that trial. What follows from the reading?

- The measured signal must equal zero.
- The measurement-minus-prediction residual is exactly $0.5$.
- The prediction is compatible, but the exact residual cannot be calculated.
- The prediction is ruled out because it differs from two.

## Explanation

The reading supplies only an upper bound, and $1.5$ satisfies it. The actual value below the threshold is unknown.
