A length is reported as $(50\pm1)$ cm. What is the lower endpoint of the associated interval?

- $51\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $50\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $49\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Subtract the uncertainty from the central value to find the lower endpoint. This gives $50-1$. Therefore the endpoint is $49$ centimeters.
