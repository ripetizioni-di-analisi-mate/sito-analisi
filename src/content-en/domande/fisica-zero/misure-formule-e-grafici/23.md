A digital thermometer rounds to the nearest degree Celsius and displays $20$ degrees. Which difference between the true temperature and the reading could be hidden by rounding?

- $+0.3$ degrees Celsius
- $+0.8$ degrees Celsius
- $+1.4$ degrees Celsius
- $-0.8$ degrees Celsius

## Explanation

Before rounding, values strictly between $19.5$ and $20.5$ give $20$. Of the choices, only $20.3$ belongs to that interval.
