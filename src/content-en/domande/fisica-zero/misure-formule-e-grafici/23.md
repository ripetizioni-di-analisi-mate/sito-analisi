The length of the same long jump is measured three times with a measuring tape: $4.10\,\mathrm{m}$, $4.35\,\mathrm{m}$, and $4.20\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the half-range of the readings?

- $0.25\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.075\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.05\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.125\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The half-range uses the largest and smallest readings, which are $4.35\,\mathrm{m}$ and $4.10\,\mathrm{m}$. Their difference is $0.25\,\mathrm{m}$. Therefore
$$
s=\frac{4.35-4.10}{2}\,\mathrm{m}=0.125\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
Using the first and last readings in the order they were written would give $0.05\,\mathrm{m}$, because the last reading is not the largest.
