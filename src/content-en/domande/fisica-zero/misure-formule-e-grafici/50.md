A rectangle’s base can range from $9\,\mathrm{cm}$ to $11\,\mathrm{cm}$ and its height from $4\,\mathrm{cm}$ to $6\,\mathrm{cm}$. Each extreme of one measurement can occur with any allowed value of the other. How wide is the range of possible areas, meaning maximum area minus minimum area?

- $15\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $50\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $30\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $4\,\mathrm{cm^2}$

## Explanation

For positive side lengths, increasing either factor increases the area. The minimum is $9\cdot4=36\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and the maximum is $11\cdot6=66\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. Their difference is $66-36=30\,\mathrm{cm^2}$.
