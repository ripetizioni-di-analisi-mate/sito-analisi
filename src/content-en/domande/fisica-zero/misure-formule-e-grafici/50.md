A running route is $5.2384\,\mathrm{km}$ long. What is this distance in meters, rounded to the nearest meter?

- $524\,\mathrm{m}$
- $52\,384\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5238\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5.238\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Since $1\,\mathrm{km}=1000\,\mathrm{m}$, multiplying by $1000$ gives $5238.4\,\mathrm{m}$. The conversion is exact, so it introduces no uncertainty. To round to the nearest meter, look at the first decimal digit, which is $4$, less than $5$. Therefore the distance is $5238\,\mathrm{m}$.
