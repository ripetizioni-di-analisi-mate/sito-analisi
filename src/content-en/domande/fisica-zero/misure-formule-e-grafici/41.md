A length is reported as $(70\pm1)$ cm. What is the lower endpoint of the associated interval?

- $69\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $71\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $70\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Subtract the uncertainty from the central value to find the lower endpoint. This gives $70-1$. Therefore the endpoint is $69$ centimeters.
