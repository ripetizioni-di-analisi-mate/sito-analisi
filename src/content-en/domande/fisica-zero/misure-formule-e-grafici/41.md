A sensor measures $80\,\mathrm{kPa}$ with a relative uncertainty of $2.5\,\mathrm{\%}$ of the reading. What is the absolute uncertainty?

- $2.5\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $0.2\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $32\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $2\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

Convert the percentage to $0.025$. Multiply by the reading to obtain $0.025\cdot 80=2\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
