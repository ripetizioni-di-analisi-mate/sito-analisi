A shelf holds twelve jars of jam, each with a mass of $0.36\,\mathrm{kg}$. Keeping all digits in the intermediate steps, what is the total mass rounded to the nearest tenth of a kilogram?

- $4.3\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $4.8\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $3.6\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $43\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

First multiply the mass of one jar by the number of jars:
$$
12\cdot0.36\,\mathrm{kg}=4.32\,\mathrm{kg}.
$$
The hundredths digit is $2$, less than $5$, so the result rounded to the tenths is $4.3\,\mathrm{kg}$. Rounding $0.36\,\mathrm{kg}$ to $0.4\,\mathrm{kg}$ first would give $4.8\,\mathrm{kg}$, because the rounding error would be multiplied by twelve.
