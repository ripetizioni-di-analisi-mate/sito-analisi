For positive values rounded to tenths, exact halfway cases round upward. Which interval produces $3.7$?

- $3.65<x\le 3.75$
- $3.65\le x<3.75$
- $3.60\le x<3.70$
- $3.70\le x<3.80$

## Explanation

The lower boundary $3.65$ rounds up to $3.7$. The upper boundary $3.75$ already rounds up to $3.8$, so it is excluded.
