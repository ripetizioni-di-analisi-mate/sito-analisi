The mass of a packet of spices is reported as $0.0350\,\mathrm{kg}$. How many significant digits does this value have?

- $5$
- $3$
- $4$
- $2$

## Explanation

Significant digits are counted starting from the first nonzero digit, which is $3$. The zeros before it only indicate the position of the decimal point. The final zero, written after the $5$, expresses the precision with which the number is reported, so it is significant. Therefore the significant digits are $3$, $5$, and $0$, that is, **three**.
