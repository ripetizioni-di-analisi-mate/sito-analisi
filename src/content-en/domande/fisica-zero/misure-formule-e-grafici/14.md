A bag of cement has a mass $m=(25.0\pm0.3)\,\mathrm{kg}$. Which of these values belongs to the interval associated with the measurement?

- $25.4\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $24.6\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $24.8\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $25.5\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The endpoints of the associated interval are $(25.0-0.3)\,\mathrm{kg}=24.7\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $(25.0+0.3)\,\mathrm{kg}=25.3\,\mathrm{kg}$. The value $24.8\,\mathrm{kg}$ lies between $24.7\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $25.3\,\mathrm{kg}$, so it belongs to the interval. The values $25.4\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $25.5\,\mathrm{kg}$ exceed the upper endpoint. The value $24.6\,\mathrm{kg}$ is less than the lower endpoint.
