A length is reported as $(40\pm1)$ cm. What is the lower endpoint of the associated interval?

- $41\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $39\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $40\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Subtract the uncertainty from the central value to find the lower endpoint. This gives $40-1$. Therefore the endpoint is $39$ centimeters.
