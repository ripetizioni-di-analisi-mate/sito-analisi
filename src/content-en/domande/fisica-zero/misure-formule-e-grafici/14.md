A straight line passes through $(2,8)$ and $(4,16)$. What ordinate corresponds to an abscissa of $3$?

::grafico{linee="2,8 4,16" punti="2,8;4,16" xmin=0 xmax=5 ymin=0 ymax=20 nomi="x,y" legenda=0 titolo="Segment between the two measured points."}

- $14$
- $10$
- $12$
- $24$

## Explanation

Three is halfway between two and four. On a straight line the ordinate halfway across is also the mean of the two ordinates, $\dfrac{8+16}{2}=12$.
