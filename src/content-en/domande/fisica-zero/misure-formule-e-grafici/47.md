The mean duration of one swing of a wall clock's pendulum, calculated from several measurements, is $1.8462\,\mathrm{s}$, and the uncertainty has already been reported as $0.03\,\mathrm{s}$. How is the result written with consistent digits?

- $(1.8462\pm0.03)\,\mathrm{s}$
- $(1.8\pm0.03)\,\mathrm{s}$
- $(1.84\pm0.03)\,\mathrm{s}$
- $(1.85\pm0.03)\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The uncertainty $0.03\,\mathrm{s}$ goes to the hundredths, so the value is rounded to the hundredths. The third decimal digit of $1.8462$ is $6$, so the hundredths digit changes from $4$ to $5$. The result is $(1.85\pm0.03)\,\mathrm{s}$. The version with $1.84$ truncates the number instead of rounding it, and the version with $1.8462$ reports digits finer than the uncertainty.
