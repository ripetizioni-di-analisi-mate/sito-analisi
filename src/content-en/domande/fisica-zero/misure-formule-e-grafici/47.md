A reading of $9.98\,\mathrm{m}$ must be rounded to two significant figures. A student submits $1.00\cdot10^1\,\mathrm{m}$. Which comment describes the result accurately?

- The exponent must be zero because rounding cannot change the order of magnitude.
- The numerical value is right, but the coefficient’s last zero indicates one extra significant figure.
- The result has the required precision because only nonzero digits are significant.
- The coefficient should be truncated before carrying, so the rounded value must remain below ten.

## Explanation

The third significant digit is eight, producing a carry into the tens place. At two significant figures, the result is $1.0\cdot10^1\,\mathrm{m}$. The submitted form has the same numerical value, but its coefficient retains three significant figures.
