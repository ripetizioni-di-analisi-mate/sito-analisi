The mass on a package of pasta is given as $(500\pm4)\,\mathrm{g}$. How wide is the interval associated with the measurement?

- $4\,\mathrm{g}$
- $2\,\mathrm{g}$
- $8\,\mathrm{g}$
- $16\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

The associated interval runs from $(500-4)\,\mathrm{g}=496\,\mathrm{g}$ to $(500+4)\,\mathrm{g}=504\,\mathrm{g}$. Its width is the difference between the endpoints, so
$$
504\,\mathrm{g}-496\,\mathrm{g}=8\,\mathrm{g}.
$$
The width is therefore twice the absolute uncertainty.
