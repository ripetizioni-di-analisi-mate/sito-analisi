To weigh a parcel of about $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ you want to distinguish changes of $2\,\mathrm{g}$. Which scale meets both requirements?

- Capacity $500\,\mathrm{g}$, resolution $0.01\,\mathrm{g}$.
- Capacity $10\,\mathrm{kg}$, resolution $10\,\mathrm{g}$.
- Capacity $2\,\mathrm{kg}$, resolution $0.1\,\mathrm{g}$.
- Capacity $5\,\mathrm{kg}$, resolution $1\,\mathrm{g}$.

## Explanation

The parcel must fit within the capacity and a two-gram change must be readable. The five-kilogram scale with one-gram steps meets both conditions.
