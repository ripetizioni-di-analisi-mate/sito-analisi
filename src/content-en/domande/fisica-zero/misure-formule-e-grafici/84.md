The graph shows the volume of water used by a shower as a function of time; the two marked points correspond to $t=2\,\mathrm{min}$, $V=20\,\mathrm{L}$ and $t=4\,\mathrm{min}$, $V=40\,\mathrm{L}$, and the line passes through the origin. After how many minutes has the shower used $70\,\mathrm{L}$?

::grafico{f="10*x" xmin=0 xmax=8 ymin=0 ymax=80 nomi="t,V" punti="2,20;4,40" titolo="Time $t$ in minutes and volume $V$ of water used by the shower in liters."}

- $700\,\mathrm{min}$
- $7\,\mathrm{min}$
- $3.5\,\mathrm{min}$
- $14\,\mathrm{min}$

## Explanation

The line passes through the origin, so volume and time are directly proportional, with constant $\dfrac{20\,\mathrm{L}}{2\,\mathrm{min}}=10\,\mathrm{L/min}$. From the relationship $V=10\,t$ we get $t=\dfrac{V}{10}$. Therefore for $70\,\mathrm{L}$ we obtain
$$
t=\frac{70\,\mathrm{L}}{10\,\mathrm{L/min}}=7\,\mathrm{min}.
$$
On the graph, the point of the line with vertical coordinate $70$ does indeed have horizontal coordinate $7$.
