The models $y=x$ and $y=x^2$ agree at $x=0$ and $x=1$. With similar absolute noise, at which of the following positive inputs do their predictions differ most?

- $2$
- $1$
- $3$
- $4$

## Explanation

Compare $\lvert x^2-x\rvert$ at the four inputs. For $1,2,3,4$ the differences are $0,2,6,12$, so the greatest separation occurs at four.
