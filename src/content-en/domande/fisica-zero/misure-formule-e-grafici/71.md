Pieces of different lengths are cut from a coil of copper wire, and each piece is weighed. Which graph represents mass as a function of length?

- A straight line that crosses the vertical axis above the origin
- A branch of a hyperbola that approaches the axes
- A straight line through the origin
- A horizontal line

## Explanation

The wire is the same, so every centimeter has the same mass and the ratio of mass to length is constant. Mass and length are therefore directly proportional, $m=k\ell$. The graph of $m=k\ell$ is a **straight line through the origin**, because zero length corresponds to zero mass. A line that crosses the vertical axis above the origin would describe a mass already present at zero length.
