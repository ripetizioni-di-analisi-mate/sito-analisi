Two graphs show the same points in the same units. Only the vertical scale on the paper is stretched. What changes?

- The physical values on the horizontal axis are halved.
- The sign of the slope becomes negative.
- An increasing line looks steeper, but the ratio of the physical changes stays the same.
- The slope in physical units always doubles.

## Explanation

The physical coordinates of the points do not change. The distance in centimetres on the page does change, altering the drawn angle.
