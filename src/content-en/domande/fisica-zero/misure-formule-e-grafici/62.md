A boat's sail is a triangle with base $b$ and height $h$, with area $A=\dfrac{bh}{2}$. Which expression gives the base in terms of the area and the height?

- $b=\dfrac{A}{2h}$
- $b=\dfrac{Ah}{2}$
- $b=\dfrac{2A}{h}$
- $b=\dfrac{2h}{A}$

## Explanation

Multiplying both sides of $A=\dfrac{bh}{2}$ by $2$ gives $2A=bh$. The height is positive, so dividing by $h$ gives
$$
b=\frac{2A}{h}.
$$
With $A=6\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and $h=4\,\mathrm{m}$, this gives $b=3\,\mathrm{m}$, and indeed $\dfrac{3\cdot4}{2}\,\mathrm{m^2}=6\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The expression $\dfrac{A}{2h}$ would give $0.75\,\mathrm{m}$, which does not satisfy the original formula.
