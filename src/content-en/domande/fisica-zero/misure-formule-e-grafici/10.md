Three readings of a time interval are $3$ s, $4$ s, and $5$ s. What is their arithmetic mean?

- $12\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The mean is the sum of the readings divided by their number. The sum is $12$ seconds, and there are three readings. Therefore the mean is $4$ seconds.
