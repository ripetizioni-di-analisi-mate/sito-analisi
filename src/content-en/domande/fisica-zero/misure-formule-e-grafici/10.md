A rectangle has area $48\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and height $6\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its base?

- $42\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $288\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $16\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Starting from $A=bh$, divide both sides by $h$. Substitution gives $b=\dfrac{48}{6}=8\,\mathrm{cm}$.
