Two sensors give rising readings during the same hours. Which test would help determine whether quantity A directly affects B?

- Collect more days of data with both quantities still varying together without controls.
- Calculate only the similarity of the two already observed curves.
- Change A together with a third quantity that could affect B.
- Change A in a controlled way and measure B while holding other relevant conditions fixed.

## Explanation

Simultaneous trends can also arise from a common cause. A controlled change of A lets you test how B responds with other conditions held fixed.
