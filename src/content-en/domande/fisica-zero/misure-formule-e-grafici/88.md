A graph shows the outdoor temperature measured at $8$, $12$, and $16$ hours, and the three points are joined by two line segments. What do the segments represent?

- Other measurements taken between the times
- The uncertainty of the three measurements
- The mean temperature of the day
- A proposed trend between one measurement and the next

## Explanation

Each point represents a measured pair of values, namely a time and the temperature recorded. There are only three measurements, so the values along the segments were not measured. Therefore the segments represent a choice of how to connect the points, that is, a proposed trend between the measurements. Using them to read the temperature at $10$ hours requires assuming that the relationship also holds between the points.
