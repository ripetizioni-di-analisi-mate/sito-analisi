A luggage scale measures a suitcase of $20\,\mathrm{kg}$ with a stated uncertainty of $3\%$. What is the absolute uncertainty?

- $6\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.6\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.06\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $3\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

An uncertainty of $3\%$ corresponds to a relative uncertainty $u_r=0.03$. From the definition $u_r=\dfrac{u}{m}$, multiplying both sides by the mass gives $u=u_r\,m$. Therefore
$$
u=0.03\cdot20\,\mathrm{kg}=0.6\,\mathrm{kg}.
$$
The value $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ confuses the percentage with an absolute uncertainty.
