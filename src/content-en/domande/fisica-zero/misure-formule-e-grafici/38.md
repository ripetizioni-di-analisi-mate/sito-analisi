Durations for the same trial are $4.0$, $4.1$, $3.9$ and $9.5$ seconds. What is a justified first step before removing the last reading?

- Delete it solely because it raises the mean.
- Treat it immediately as a new typical duration and disregard the other three.
- Check notes and procedure for a documented cause.
- Replace it by the mean of the other three before checking the log.

## Explanation

The gap from the other trials flags a reading for investigation. A transcription error or an interrupted trial may justify a documented correction or exclusion.
