An empty cup has mass $4\,\mathrm{g}$. A liquid of density $1.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$ is gradually added. Where does the graph of total mass against added volume meet the vertical axis?

- $1.5\,\mathrm{g}$
- $0\,\mathrm{g}$
- $6\,\mathrm{g}$
- $4\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

On the vertical axis the added volume is zero. The total mass is then just the cup's mass, $4\,\mathrm{g}$.
