A mass is reported as $(400\pm3)$ g. What is the upper endpoint of the associated interval?

- $403\,\mathrm{g}$
- $397\,\mathrm{g}$
- $400\,\mathrm{g}$
- $6\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

Add the uncertainty to the central value to find the upper endpoint. This gives $400+3$. Therefore the endpoint is $403$ grams.
