A length is reported as $(30\pm1)$ cm. What is the lower endpoint of the associated interval?

- $29\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $31\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $30\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Subtract the uncertainty from the central value to find the lower endpoint. This gives $30-1$. Therefore the endpoint is $29$ centimeters.
