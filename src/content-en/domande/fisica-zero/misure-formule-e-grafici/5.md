Your readings are $24.2$, $24.5$ and $24.8$ centimetres. What is the half-range?

- $0.6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.3\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $24.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.15\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Use only the maximum and minimum. Their difference is $0.6\,\mathrm{cm}$, and halving it gives $0.3\,\mathrm{cm}$.
