A bottle contains $460\,\mathrm{g}$ of olive oil, which has a density of $\rho=0.92\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. What is the volume of the oil?

- $423\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $500\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $0.002\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $50\,\mathrm{cm^3}$

## Explanation

From the formula $\rho=\dfrac{m}{V}$, dividing by the density, which is positive, gives $V=\dfrac{m}{\rho}$. Substituting the data gives
$$
V=\frac{460\,\mathrm{g}}{0.92\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}}=500\,\mathrm{cm^3}.
$$
The gram cancels and the cubic centimeter remains, which is the unit of a volume. The value $423\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ would result from multiplying the mass by the density.
