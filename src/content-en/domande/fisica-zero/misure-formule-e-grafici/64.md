The rectangular screen of a television has area $A=bh$. To find the height, both sides are divided by the base $b$. What condition makes this operation valid?

- The base $b$ is nonzero
- The height $h$ is nonzero
- The area $A$ is greater than the base $b$
- The base and the height have the same measure

## Explanation

Dividing by a number is possible only if that number is nonzero. The operation divides by $b$, so the condition concerns the base. For a real screen the base is a positive length, so the condition is satisfied and the result is $h=\dfrac{A}{b}$. Conditions on the height or on a comparison between area and base play no role in the division.
