For the same input of $4$ units, a sensor that follows $y=kx$ in both modes first gives $10$ and then $6$. What is the ratio of its new coefficient to its original coefficient?

- $0.4$
- $2.5$
- $0.6$
- $1.67$

## Explanation

The coefficients are $\dfrac{6}{4}$ and $\dfrac{10}{4}$. The common input cancels in their ratio, leaving $\dfrac{6}{10}=0.6$.
