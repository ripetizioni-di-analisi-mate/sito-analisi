The scale of a kitchen measuring cup starts at zero and consists of $20$ equal intervals of $5\,\mathrm{mL}$ each. What is the range of the measuring cup?

- $100\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $25\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $4\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $20\,\mathrm{mL}$

## Explanation

The scale starts at zero, so the range is the value of the last mark. Twenty intervals of $5\,\mathrm{mL}$ cover
$$
20\cdot5\,\mathrm{mL}=100\,\mathrm{mL}.
$$
Therefore the range is $100\,\mathrm{mL}$, while $5\,\mathrm{mL}$ is the scale division.
