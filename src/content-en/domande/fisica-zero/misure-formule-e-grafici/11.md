A homogeneous material has mass $15\,\mathrm{g}$ in $5\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. What is the mass of $12\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ of the same material?

- $36\,\mathrm{g}$
- $60\,\mathrm{g}$
- $22\,\mathrm{g}$
- $4\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

The constant ratio is $\dfrac{15}{5}=3\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$. For twelve cubic centimetres the mass is $3\cdot 12=36\,\mathrm{g}$.
