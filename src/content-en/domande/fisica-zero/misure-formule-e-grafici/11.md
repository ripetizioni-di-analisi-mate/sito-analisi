In a series of measurements, the minimum is $40$ cm and the maximum is $44$ cm. What is the half-range?

- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $1\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $42\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The half-range is half the difference between the maximum and minimum. The difference is $4$ centimeters. Therefore the half-range is $2$ centimeters.
