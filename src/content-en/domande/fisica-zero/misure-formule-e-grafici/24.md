Timing the start and end of a sequence has a combined uncertainty of $0.2\,\mathrm{s}$. You time $20$ identical oscillations and divide the time by twenty. What uncertainty does this contribution give for one period?

- $0.1\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.01\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.2\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The number of oscillations is known exactly. Divide the uncertainty on the total time by twenty as well, giving $\dfrac{0.2}{20}=0.01\,\mathrm{s}$.
