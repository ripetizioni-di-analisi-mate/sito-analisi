Between the $50$ mL and $70$ mL marks there are $10$ equal intervals. What does one interval represent?

- $10\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $20\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $1\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $2\,\mathrm{mL}$

## Explanation

The difference between the two readings is $20$ milliliters. Divide this difference by the number of intervals, $10$. Therefore one interval represents $2$ milliliters.
