A wheel of cheese has a mass $m=(320\pm4)\,\mathrm{g}$. How is the same measurement written in kilograms?

- $(0.320\pm0.004)\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $(0.320\pm4)\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $(0.32\pm0.04)\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $(3.20\pm0.04)\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Since $1\,\mathrm{kg}=1000\,\mathrm{g}$, converting from grams to kilograms means dividing by $1000$. The conversion applies to both the measured value and the uncertainty, because the uncertainty has the same unit as the quantity. This gives $\dfrac{320}{1000}\,\mathrm{kg}=0.320\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $\dfrac{4}{1000}\,\mathrm{kg}=0.004\,\mathrm{kg}$. Therefore the measurement is $(0.320\pm0.004)\,\mathrm{kg}$.
