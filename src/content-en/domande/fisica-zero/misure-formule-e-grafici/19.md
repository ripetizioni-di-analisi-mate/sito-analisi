An empty scale reads $-3\,\mathrm{g}$. With a sample it reads $47\,\mathrm{g}$. If the error is a constant offset, what signed quantity must you add to the sample reading?

- $-6\,\mathrm{g}$
- $+6\,\mathrm{g}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{g}$
- $+3\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

At zero load the scale underreads by three grams. Subtract the signed offset, $47-(-3)=50\,\mathrm{g}$.
