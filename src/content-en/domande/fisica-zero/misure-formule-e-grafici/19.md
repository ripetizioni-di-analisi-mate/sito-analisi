Three readings of a time interval are $4$ s, $5$ s, and $6$ s. What is their arithmetic mean?

- $15\,\mathrm{s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The mean is the sum of the readings divided by their number. The sum is $15$ seconds, and there are three readings. Therefore the mean is $5$ seconds.
