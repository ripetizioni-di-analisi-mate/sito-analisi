A mass is reported as $(300\pm3)$ g. What is the upper endpoint of the associated interval?

- $297\,\mathrm{g}$
- $300\,\mathrm{g}$
- $6\,\mathrm{g}$
- $303\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

Add the uncertainty to the central value to find the upper endpoint. This gives $300+3$. Therefore the endpoint is $303$ grams.
