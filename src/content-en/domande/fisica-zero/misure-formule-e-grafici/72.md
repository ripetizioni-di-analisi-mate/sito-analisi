The graph shows the time $t$ needed to fill a jerrycan from a tap as a function of the tap's flow rate $Q$. The measured points are $(2,12)$, $(4,6)$, and $(6,4)$. Which relationship describes the data?

::grafico{f="24/x" xmin=0 xmax=8 ymin=0 ymax=16 nomi="Q,t" punti="2,12;4,6;6,4" titolo="Flow rate $Q$ in liters per minute and filling time $t$ in minutes."}

- $t=24Q$
- $t=24-2Q$
- $t=\dfrac{Q}{24}$
- $t=\dfrac{24}{Q}$

## Explanation

For the three points, the product of flow rate and time is $2\cdot12=4\cdot6=6\cdot4=24$. The product is constant, so the quantities are inversely proportional and $t=\dfrac{24}{Q}$. The product $24\,\mathrm{L}$ is the volume of the jerrycan. The relationship $t=24-2Q$ gives $20$ for $Q=2$, so it does not pass through the first point.
