An axis labelled linear has equally spaced marks labelled $0,1,2,4,5$. Which adjacent pair reveals the inconsistency?

- $0$ and $1$
- $4$ and $5$
- $2$ and $4$
- $1$ and $2$

## Explanation

On a linear axis equal gaps must represent equal increments. The step from two to four is twice as large as the others.
