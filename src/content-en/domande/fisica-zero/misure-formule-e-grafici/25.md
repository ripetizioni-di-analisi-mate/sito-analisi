A length is $(1.20\pm 0.03)\,\mathrm{m}$. Which entry reports it correctly in centimetres?

- $(1.20\pm 3)\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $(120\pm 3)\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $(120\pm 30)\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $(120\pm 0.03)\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The unit change multiplies both the value and its absolute uncertainty by one hundred. They become $120\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $3\,\mathrm{cm}$, respectively.
