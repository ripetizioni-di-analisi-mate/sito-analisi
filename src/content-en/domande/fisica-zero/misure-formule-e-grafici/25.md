The formula is $A=bh$. If $A=30$ square meters and $b=5$ m, what is $h$?

- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $150\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Divide the area by the positive base length. This gives $h=\dfrac{A}{b}=\dfrac{30}{5}$. The units simplify to meters, so $h=6$ m.
