A mass is reported as $(500\pm3)$ g. What is the upper endpoint of the associated interval?

- $497\,\mathrm{g}$
- $503\,\mathrm{g}$
- $500\,\mathrm{g}$
- $6\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

Add the uncertainty to the central value to find the upper endpoint. This gives $500+3$. Therefore the endpoint is $503$ grams.
