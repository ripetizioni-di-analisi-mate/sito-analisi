In a cylinder, the bottom of the water's concave meniscus is at $6.0\,\mathrm{mL}$ and the edge touching the glass is at $8.0\,\mathrm{mL}$. The procedure calls for the bottom of the meniscus. What do you record?

- $6.0\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $7.0\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $8.0\,\mathrm{mL}$

## Explanation

The curved surface crosses more than one mark. The procedure specifies the lowest point, so record six millilitres without subtracting the edge height.
