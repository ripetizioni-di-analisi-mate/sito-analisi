A cyclist rides four laps of the same track and times each lap: $62\,\mathrm{s}$, $64\,\mathrm{s}$, $63\,\mathrm{s}$, and $65\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the mean lap time?

- $84.7\,\mathrm{s}$
- $254\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $63.5\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The arithmetic mean is the sum of the readings divided by their number. The sum of the four times is $254\,\mathrm{s}$. Therefore
$$
\overline{t}=\frac{254\,\mathrm{s}}{4}=63.5\,\mathrm{s}.
$$
Dividing by three instead of four would give $84.7\,\mathrm{s}$, a value larger than every reading.
