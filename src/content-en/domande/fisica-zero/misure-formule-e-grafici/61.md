For square plates with sides $1,2,3,4\,\mathrm{cm}$, the areas are $1,4,9,16\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. Which change immediately distinguishes this relation from direct proportion?

- The area increases when the side increases.
- Doubling the side from $1$ to $2$ quadruples the area.
- All listed values are positive.
- The first point has equal coordinates.

## Explanation

Under direct proportion, doubling the input must double the output. Here the factor of four reveals a different dependence.
