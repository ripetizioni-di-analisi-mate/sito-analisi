A round pizza has an area $A=1256\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. The area of a circle is $A=\pi r^2$; use $\pi\approx3.14$. What is the radius of the pizza?

- $400\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $200\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $20\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $40\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Dividing both sides of $A=\pi r^2$ by $\pi$ gives $r^2=\dfrac{A}{\pi}$. Substituting the data,
$$
r^2=\frac{1256\,\mathrm{cm^2}}{3.14}=400\,\mathrm{cm^2}.
$$
The radius is positive, so it is the square root of this value, namely $r=20\,\mathrm{cm}$. The value $400$ is $r^2$, not the radius, while $40\,\mathrm{cm}$ is the diameter.
