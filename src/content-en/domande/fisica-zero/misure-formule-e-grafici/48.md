Which way of writing the length of a screwdriver has a value and an uncertainty with mutually consistent digits?

- $(14.63\pm0.2)\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $(15\pm0.2)\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $(14.6\pm0.237)\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $(14.6\pm0.2)\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The measured value is rounded to the same decimal place as the reported uncertainty, and the uncertainty is usually reported with one significant digit. In $(14.6\pm0.2)\,\mathrm{cm}$ both numbers go to the tenths, so the notation is consistent. In $(14.63\pm0.2)\,\mathrm{cm}$ the value has one more digit than the uncertainty, while in $(15\pm0.2)\,\mathrm{cm}$ it has one fewer. The uncertainty $0.237\,\mathrm{cm}$ has three significant digits, more than the rule allows.
