The mean of the measurements is $8.736\,\mathrm{g}$. The protocol gives the already rounded uncertainty as $0.02\,\mathrm{g}$ and requires the central value to end at the same decimal place. How should the mean be reported?

- I round to hundredths: the third decimal rounds the second one up, giving $8.74\,\mathrm{g}$.
- I keep every digit shown by the calculation because the mean uses all the readings.
- To reach the required place, I drop the third decimal digit regardless of its value.
- I round the mean to one significant figure because the uncertainty has one significant figure.

## Explanation

The uncertainty sets the hundredth of a gram as the last place to report. The third decimal of the mean is six, so the three in the hundredths place rounds up to four. The reported mean is $8.74\,\mathrm{g}$.
