A meter specifies a bound of $2\,\mathrm{\%}$ of the reading plus $0.1\,\mathrm{V}$. At a reading of $10.0\,\mathrm{V}$, what is the total bound?

- $0.1\,\mathrm{V}$
- $0.3\,\mathrm{V}$
- $0.2\,\mathrm{V}$
- $0.02\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

The percentage term is $0.02\cdot 10.0=0.2\,\mathrm{V}$. Add the stated fixed term, $0.2+0.1=0.3\,\mathrm{V}$.
