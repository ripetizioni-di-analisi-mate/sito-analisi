The mean temperature for a week, calculated by a weather station, is $18.47\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. What is it rounded to the nearest unit?

- $19\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $18\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $18.5\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $20\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

To round to the nearest unit, look at the first decimal digit, which is $4$. The digit is less than $5$, so the units digit stays $8$ and the following digits are dropped. Therefore the rounded temperature is $18\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. The value $19\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ comes from rounding twice, first to $18.5\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ and then to the unit, which changes the result.
