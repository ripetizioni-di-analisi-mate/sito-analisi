A rectangular-box aquarium contains $60\,\mathrm{L}$ of water, that is, $60\,\mathrm{dm^3}$. Its bottom is a rectangle measuring $5\,\mathrm{dm}$ by $3\,\mathrm{dm}$. Using $V=abc$, what height does the water reach?

- $12\,\mathrm{dm}$
- $20\,\mathrm{dm}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{dm}$
- $4\,\mathrm{dm}$

## Explanation

In the formula $V=abc$, the letters $a$ and $b$ are the sides of the bottom and $c$ is the height of the water. The sides are positive, so we divide by their product:
$$
c=\frac{V}{ab}=\frac{60\,\mathrm{dm^3}}{5\,\mathrm{dm}\cdot3\,\mathrm{dm}}=4\,\mathrm{dm}.
$$
A cubic decimeter divided by a square decimeter gives a decimeter, which is a length. The value $12\,\mathrm{dm}$ would result from dividing only by $5\,\mathrm{dm}$.
