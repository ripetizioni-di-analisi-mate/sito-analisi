A model gives two mathematical solutions, $t=2\,\mathrm{s}$ and $t=8\,\mathrm{s}$, for the same signal. The instrument recorded only from $5$ to $10\,\mathrm{s}$. Which solution describes the recorded measurement?

- $8\,\mathrm{s}$
- Both.
- $2\,\mathrm{s}$
- Neither.

## Explanation

Apply the experimental constraint as well as the equation. Eight seconds lies within the recording window, while two seconds is earlier.
