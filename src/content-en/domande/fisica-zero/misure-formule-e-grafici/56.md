A student uses time $t$, length $L$ and speed $v$. Which equation can add the indicated terms without incompatible units?

- $L_{\rm tot}=L+t$
- $L_{\rm tot}=L+vt$
- $L_{\rm tot}=L+\dfrac{v}{t}$
- $L_{\rm tot}=L+v$

## Explanation

The product $vt$ has dimensions of length. It can therefore be added to $L$ to obtain a total length.
