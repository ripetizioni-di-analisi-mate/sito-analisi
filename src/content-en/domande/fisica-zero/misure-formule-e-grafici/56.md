The edge of a rectangular rug has length $P=2(b+h)$, where $b$ is the base and $h$ the height. Which expression gives the height in terms of $P$ and $b$?

- $h=P-2b$
- $h=\dfrac{P-b}{2}$
- $h=\dfrac{P}{2b}$
- $h=\dfrac{P}{2}-b$

## Explanation

Dividing both sides of $P=2(b+h)$ by $2$ gives $\dfrac{P}{2}=b+h$. Then subtracting $b$ from both sides gives
$$
h=\frac{P}{2}-b.
$$
With $P=10\,\mathrm{m}$ and $b=3\,\mathrm{m}$, this gives $h=2\,\mathrm{m}$, and indeed $2\cdot(3+2)\,\mathrm{m}=10\,\mathrm{m}$. The expression $P-2b$ would give $4\,\mathrm{m}$, twice the height.
