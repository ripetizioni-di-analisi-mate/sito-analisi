Three readings of a time interval are $5$ s, $6$ s, and $7$ s. What is their arithmetic mean?

- $18\,\mathrm{s}$
- $7\,\mathrm{s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The mean is the sum of the readings divided by their number. The sum is $18$ seconds, and there are three readings. Therefore the mean is $6$ seconds.
