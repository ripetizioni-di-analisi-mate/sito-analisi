Two readings of the same object are $(10\pm 2)\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $(13\pm 2)\,\mathrm{cm}$. Which interval agrees with both?

- From $8$ to $15\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- From $12$ to $15\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- From $10$ to $13\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- From $11$ to $12\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

The individual intervals run from $8$ to $12$ and from $11$ to $15$ centimetres. Their only common part is $11$ to $12\,\mathrm{cm}$.
