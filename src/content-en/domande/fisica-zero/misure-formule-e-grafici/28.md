The length of a table is measured three times and recorded as $1.52\,\mathrm{m}$, $151\,\mathrm{cm}$, and $1.53\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the mean length?

- $152\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $51.4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $456\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $1.52\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The readings must be expressed in the same unit before the mean is calculated. In centimeters they are $152\,\mathrm{cm}$, $151\,\mathrm{cm}$, and $153\,\mathrm{cm}$. Therefore
$$
\overline{\ell}=\frac{152+151+153}{3}\,\mathrm{cm}=152\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
Adding the numbers as written, without converting them, would give about $51.4$, a meaningless value.
