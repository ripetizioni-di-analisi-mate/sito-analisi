A lab has measured only $x=2,y=6$. Which alternative model, different from $y=3x$, agrees with that measurement?

- $y=2x+1$
- $y=x+3$
- $y=x+4$
- $y=x^2$

## Explanation

Substitute $x=2$. Among the alternatives, only $2+4=6$ works. A shared point allows multiple models that further measurements can distinguish.
