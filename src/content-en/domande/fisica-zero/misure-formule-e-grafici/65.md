The volume $V$ of gasoline used by a scooter is directly proportional to the distance $d$ traveled, according to the relationship $V=kd$. After $120\,\mathrm{km}$ the scooter has used $3\,\mathrm{L}$. What is the constant of proportionality $k$?

- $40\,\mathrm{km/L}$
- $0.025\,\mathrm{L/km}$
- $0.025\,\mathrm{km/L}$
- $360\,\mathrm{L\cdot km}$

## Explanation

The distance is positive, so dividing both sides of $V=kd$ by $d$ gives $k=\dfrac{V}{d}$. Substituting the data gives
$$
k=\frac{3\,\mathrm{L}}{120\,\mathrm{km}}=0.025\,\mathrm{L/km}.
$$
The constant is a volume divided by a distance, so it is measured in liters per kilometer. The value $40\,\mathrm{km/L}$ is the inverse ratio $\dfrac{d}{V}$, that is, the distance traveled on one liter, and not the constant of the relationship $V=kd$.
