In an experiment $p=\dfrac{k}{V}$ with $V>0$. Which horizontal coordinate produces a straight line through the origin if the vertical coordinate is $p$?

- $V^2$
- $\dfrac{1}{V}$
- $\sqrt V$
- $V$

## Explanation

Setting $x=\dfrac{1}{V}$ turns the relation into $p=kx$. The theoretical line has slope $k$ and zero intercept.
