The volume of a wooden block is found with the rearranged formula $V=\dfrac{m}{\rho}$, with the mass in kilograms and the density in $\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. In what unit is the volume obtained?

- $\mathrm{kg^2/m^3}$
- $\mathrm{kg}$
- $\dfrac{1}{\mathrm{m^3}}$
- $\mathrm{m^3}$

## Explanation

In the formula, the units are divided just like the numbers. This gives
$$
\frac{\mathrm{kg}}{\mathrm{kg/m^3}}=\mathrm{kg}\cdot\frac{\mathrm{m^3}}{\mathrm{kg}}=\mathrm{m^3}.
$$
The kilogram cancels, so the volume is obtained in cubic meters, as it must be for a volume. The unit $\mathrm{kg^2/m^3}$ would result from multiplying the mass by the density.
