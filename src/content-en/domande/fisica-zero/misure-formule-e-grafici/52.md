The area of a disc is $A=\pi r^2$. If $A=49\pi \,\mathrm{cm^2}$, what physically admissible radius is obtained?

- $49\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $14\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-7\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $7\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Dividing by $\pi $ gives $r^2=49\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. A radius is a nonnegative length, so take the positive root.
