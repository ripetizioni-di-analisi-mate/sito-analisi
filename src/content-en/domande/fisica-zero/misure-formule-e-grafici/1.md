Three readings of a time interval are $2$ s, $3$ s, and $4$ s. What is their arithmetic mean?

- $3\,\mathrm{s}$
- $9\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The mean is the sum of the readings divided by their number. The sum is $9$ seconds, and there are three readings. Therefore the mean is $3$ seconds.
