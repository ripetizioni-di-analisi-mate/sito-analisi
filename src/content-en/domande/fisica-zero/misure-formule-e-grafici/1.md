Two scale marks $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ apart enclose six equal intervals. What does each interval represent?

- $0.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $3\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.05\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Six intervals together span three centimetres. Divide the span by the number of intervals, $\dfrac{3}{6}=0.5\,\mathrm{cm}$.
