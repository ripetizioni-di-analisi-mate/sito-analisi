A recipe calls for $320\,\mathrm{g}$ of pasta for four people. If the amount of pasta is directly proportional to the number of people, how much pasta is needed for six people?

- $213\,\mathrm{g}$
- $1920\,\mathrm{g}$
- $480\,\mathrm{g}$
- $640\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

The two quantities are directly proportional, so the ratio of the mass of pasta to the number of people is constant:
$$
k=\frac{320\,\mathrm{g}}{4}=80\,\mathrm{g}.
$$
For six people the mass is $6\cdot80\,\mathrm{g}=480\,\mathrm{g}$. The value $213\,\mathrm{g}$ would result from treating the quantities as inversely proportional, that is, giving less pasta to more people.
