The water level rises to $6\,\mathrm{cm}$, remains there from minute two to minute five, then falls. What does the horizontal part of the graph show?

::grafico{linee="0,2 2,6 5,6 7,3" punti="2,6;5,6" xmin=0 xmax=7 ymin=0 ymax=8 nomi="t,h" legenda=0 titolo="Time in minutes and level in centimetres."}

- The level is zero for three minutes.
- The time interval of the segment is zero.
- The level is $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ and its net change over those three minutes is zero.
- The level rises $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ every minute.

## Explanation

The starting and ending ordinates of the horizontal segment coincide at six centimetres. Their difference is zero even though the water has positive depth.
