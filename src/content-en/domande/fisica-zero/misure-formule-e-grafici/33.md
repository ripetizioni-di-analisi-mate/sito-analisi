The mean of five measurements was $20\,\mathrm{g}$. One reading was copied as $30\,\mathrm{g}$ when it should have been $20\,\mathrm{g}$. By how much should the mean decrease?

- $5\,\mathrm{g}$
- $10\,\mathrm{g}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{g}$
- $2\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

The sum of the five readings falls by $10\,\mathrm{g}$. The divisor stays at five, so the mean falls by $\dfrac{10}{5}=2\,\mathrm{g}$.
