The time to slide down a water slide is measured three times. The result, with the mean as the value and the half-range as the uncertainty, is $(12.0\pm0.3)\,\mathrm{s}$. Which set of three readings produces this result?

- $11.8\,\mathrm{s}$, $12.0\,\mathrm{s}$, $12.2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $11.7\,\mathrm{s}$, $12.0\,\mathrm{s}$, $12.3\,\mathrm{s}$
- $11.7\,\mathrm{s}$, $12.3\,\mathrm{s}$, $12.6\,\mathrm{s}$
- $12.0\,\mathrm{s}$, $12.3\,\mathrm{s}$, $12.6\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

For the set $11.7\,\mathrm{s}$, $12.0\,\mathrm{s}$, $12.3\,\mathrm{s}$ the sum is $36.0\,\mathrm{s}$, so the mean is $12.0\,\mathrm{s}$. The half-range is $\dfrac{12.3-11.7}{2}\,\mathrm{s}=0.3\,\mathrm{s}$, so this set gives $(12.0\pm0.3)\,\mathrm{s}$. The set with extremes $11.8\,\mathrm{s}$ and $12.2\,\mathrm{s}$ has a mean of $12.0\,\mathrm{s}$ but a half-range of $0.2\,\mathrm{s}$. The two sets that reach $12.6\,\mathrm{s}$ have means of $12.2\,\mathrm{s}$ and $12.3\,\mathrm{s}$.
