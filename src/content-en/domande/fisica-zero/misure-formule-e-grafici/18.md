A technician wants a scale from $0$ to $2\,\mathrm{mL}$ in steps of $0.25\,\mathrm{mL}$. How many marks are needed, including both endpoints?

- $8$
- $10$
- $9$
- $7$

## Explanation

The number of intervals is $\dfrac{2}{0.25}=8$. Nine marks are needed because the eight gaps also have a starting endpoint.
