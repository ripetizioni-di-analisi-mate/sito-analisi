A weighing reported as $m=(72.4\pm0.1)\,\mathrm{kg}$ was taken with a bathroom scale. Which statement correctly describes this notation?

- The true mass is exactly $72.4\,\mathrm{kg}$
- The true mass certainly lies between $72.3\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $72.5\,\mathrm{kg}$
- The relative uncertainty of the measurement is $0.1\,\mathrm{kg}$
- The measured value is $72.4\,\mathrm{kg}$, and the associated interval runs from $72.3\,\mathrm{kg}$ to $72.5\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The $\pm$ notation gives the measured value, $72.4\,\mathrm{kg}$, and the absolute uncertainty, $0.1\,\mathrm{kg}$. Subtracting and adding the uncertainty gives the associated interval, from $72.3\,\mathrm{kg}$ to $72.5\,\mathrm{kg}$. The meaning of the interval depends on how the uncertainty was evaluated, so the notation alone does not guarantee that it certainly contains the true value. The value $0.1\,\mathrm{kg}$ has a unit, so it is the absolute uncertainty, not the relative one.
