A rectangular frame has perimeter $P=2(b+h)$. If $P=50\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $h=9\,\mathrm{cm}$, what is its base?

- $32\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $41\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $20.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $16\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Halving the perimeter gives $b+h=25\,\mathrm{cm}$. Subtract the height to obtain $b=25-9=16\,\mathrm{cm}$.
