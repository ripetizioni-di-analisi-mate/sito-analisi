A calculation gives $0.148\,\mathrm{cm}$ as the uncertainty in the width of a tile. If the uncertainty is reported with a single significant digit, what value is written?

- $0.15\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.2\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.148\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.1\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The first nonzero digit of $0.148$ is $1$, in the tenths place. To keep a single significant digit, round to the tenths by looking at the next digit, which is $4$. The digit is less than $5$, so the tenths digit stays $1$. Therefore the reported uncertainty is $0.1\,\mathrm{cm}$; the value $0.2\,\mathrm{cm}$ would result from first rounding to $0.15$ and then rounding again.
