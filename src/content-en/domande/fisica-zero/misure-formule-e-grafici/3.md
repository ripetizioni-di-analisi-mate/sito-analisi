A cylinder has $0.4\,\mathrm{mL}$ divisions. It reads $7.2\,\mathrm{mL}$, and the protocol assigns half a division as the uncertainty. Which result should be recorded?

- $(7.2\pm 0.4)\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $(7.2\pm 0.1)\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $(7.2\pm 0.2)\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $(7.2\pm 0.8)\,\mathrm{mL}$

## Explanation

The central reading remains $7.2\,\mathrm{mL}$. Half of $0.4\,\mathrm{mL}$ is $0.2\,\mathrm{mL}$, which follows the uncertainty sign.
