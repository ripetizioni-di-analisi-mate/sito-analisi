A measurement is reported as $(60\pm2)$ cm. What is the relative uncertainty?

- $30$
- $2$
- $\dfrac{1}{30}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

Relative uncertainty is the ratio of the absolute uncertainty to the measured value. The two quantities must be expressed in the same unit. This gives $\dfrac{2}{60}=\dfrac{1}{30}$, and the units cancel.
