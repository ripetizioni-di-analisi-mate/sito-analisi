A digital clinical thermometer displays $36.8\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. As the temperature rises, the display changes to $36.9\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ and then to $37.0\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$, with no values in between. What is the resolution of the thermometer?

- $1\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $0.01\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $36.8\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $0.1\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

The resolution is the smallest change in temperature that the instrument can distinguish. Consecutive readings differ by $0.1\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$, and no intermediate values appear. Therefore the resolution is $0.1\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. The value $36.8\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ is a reading and does not describe the display increment.
