A device has a constant absolute uncertainty of $0.3\,\mathrm{mL}$. What minimum positive reading gives a relative uncertainty of at most $2\,\mathrm{\%}$?

- $15\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $60\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $6\,\mathrm{mL}$

## Explanation

Require $\dfrac{0.3}{V}\le 0.02$. For positive volume the condition becomes $V\ge \dfrac{0.3}{0.02}=15\,\mathrm{mL}$.
