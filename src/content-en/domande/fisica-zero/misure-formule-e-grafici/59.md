The weather forecast for an American city gives $50$ degrees Fahrenheit. The relationship with degrees Celsius is $t_F=1.8\,t_C+32$. What temperature in degrees Celsius does this correspond to?

- $10\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $45.6\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $-4.2\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $122\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

To find $t_C$, first subtract $32$ from both sides, which gives $t_F-32=1.8\,t_C$. Then divide by $1.8$, so
$$
t_C=\frac{t_F-32}{1.8}=\frac{50-32}{1.8}=10.
$$
The temperature is therefore $10\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. The value $122\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ would result from applying the original formula to the number $50$ as if it were already in degrees Celsius.
