For inputs $1,2,4$, which output triple fits $y=kx$ with a single constant $k$?

- $3,6,9$
- $3,6,12$
- $3,2,1$
- $3,5,9$

## Explanation

Check each output divided by its input. For $3,6,12$ the three ratios all equal three, while in the other triples at least one ratio differs.
