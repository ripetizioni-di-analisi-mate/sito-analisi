A person stands on a platform that measures weight and reads $686\,\mathrm{N}$. Weight is $P=mg$, with $g=9.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the person's mass?

- $70\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $6723\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.014\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $676\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The acceleration $g$ is positive, so dividing both sides of $P=mg$ by $g$ gives $m=\dfrac{P}{g}$. Substituting the data gives
$$
m=\frac{686\,\mathrm{N}}{9.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}}=70\,\mathrm{kg}.
$$
A newton divided by $\mathrm{m/s^2}$ gives a kilogram, the unit of mass. The value $6723\,\mathrm{kg}$ would result from multiplying instead of dividing.
