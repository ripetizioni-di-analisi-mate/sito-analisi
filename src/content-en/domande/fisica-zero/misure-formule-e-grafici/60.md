Three pairs follow a law $xy=k$. Which of these four pairs breaks the law shared by the other three?

- $(6,3)$
- $(3,8)$
- $(4,6)$
- $(2,12)$

## Explanation

The three products $2\cdot 12=3\cdot 8=4\cdot 6=24$ agree. Instead $6\cdot 3=18$, so this pair breaks the constant-product pattern.
