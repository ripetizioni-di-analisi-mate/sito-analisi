A pump delivers $2\,\mathrm{L/min}$ for the first three minutes and $5\,\mathrm{L/min}$ for the next minute. What volume corresponds to the area under its flow-rate-versus-time graph?

::grafico{linee="0,2 3,2;3,5 4,5" xmin=0 xmax=4.5 ymin=0 ymax=6 nomi="t,Q" legenda=0 titolo="Time in minutes and flow rate in litres per minute."}

- $20\,\mathrm{L}$
- $14\,\mathrm{L}$
- $11\,\mathrm{L}$
- $7\,\mathrm{L}$

## Explanation

The area of each rectangle is rate times duration. The contributions are $2\cdot 3=6\,\mathrm{L}$ and $5\cdot 1=5\,\mathrm{L}$, for a total of eleven litres.
