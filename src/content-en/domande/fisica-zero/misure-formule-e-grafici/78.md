A bucket is filled with water one liter at a time and weighed after each addition. The measurements, shown in the graph, are $V=1\,\mathrm{L}$, $m=1.4\,\mathrm{kg}$; $V=2\,\mathrm{L}$, $m=2.4\,\mathrm{kg}$; $V=3\,\mathrm{L}$, $m=3.4\,\mathrm{kg}$. Which formula, with $V$ in liters and $m$ in kilograms, describes the data?

::grafico{xmin=0 xmax=4 ymin=0 ymax=5 nomi="V,m" punti="1,1.4;2,2.4;3,3.4" titolo="Water volume $V$ in liters and total mass $m$ of the bucket in kilograms."}

- $m=1.4\,V$
- $m=0.4+V$
- $m=V$
- $m=0.4\,V$

## Explanation

Each added liter increases the mass by $1\,\mathrm{kg}$, so the term that depends on the volume is $1\cdot V$. Removing one liter from the first measurement leads to the empty bucket, which therefore has a mass of $1.4\,\mathrm{kg}-1\,\mathrm{kg}=0.4\,\mathrm{kg}$. The formula is then $m=0.4+V$, and the graph of this relationship is a straight line that crosses the vertical axis at $0.4$. The formula $m=1.4\,V$ fits the first point, but for $V=2$ it gives $2.8$ instead of $2.4$.
