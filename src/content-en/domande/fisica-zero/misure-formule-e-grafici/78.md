For three measurements, model A has absolute residuals of $1,1,4$ and model B has $2,2,1$. Choose the model with the smaller sum of absolute residuals. Which is selected and with what sum?

- A, $2$
- B, $2$
- B, $5$
- A, $6$

## Explanation

The sum is $1+1+4=6$ for A and $2+2+1=5$ for B. The stated criterion therefore favours B.
