A school laboratory balance has an absolute uncertainty of $2\,\mathrm{g}$ on every weighing. What is the smallest mass that can be weighed with a relative uncertainty of no more than $1\%$?

- $200\,\mathrm{g}$
- $20\,\mathrm{g}$
- $50\,\mathrm{g}$
- $2000\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

The relative uncertainty is $u_r=\dfrac{u}{m}$, with $u=2\,\mathrm{g}$. The condition $u_r\le0.01$ becomes $\dfrac{2\,\mathrm{g}}{m}\le0.01$. Since $m$ is positive, we can multiply by $m$ and divide by $0.01$, which gives
$$
m\ge\frac{2\,\mathrm{g}}{0.01}=200\,\mathrm{g}.
$$
Therefore the smallest mass is $200\,\mathrm{g}$, and larger masses give an even smaller relative uncertainty.
