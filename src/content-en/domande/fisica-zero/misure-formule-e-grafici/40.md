A mass is reported as $(700\pm3)$ g. What is the upper endpoint of the associated interval?

- $697\,\mathrm{g}$
- $700\,\mathrm{g}$
- $6\,\mathrm{g}$
- $703\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

Add the uncertainty to the central value to find the upper endpoint. This gives $700+3$. Therefore the endpoint is $703$ grams.
