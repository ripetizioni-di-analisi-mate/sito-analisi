A length is reported as $(18.6\pm 0.2)\,\mathrm{cm}$. What are the endpoints of its stated interval?

- $18.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $18.7\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $18.4\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $18.8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $18.2\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $19.0\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $18.6\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $18.8\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Subtract and add the uncertainty to the central value. The limits are $18.6-0.2=18.4$ and $18.6+0.2=18.8$ centimetres.
