A kitchen scale has a range of $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ and a resolution of $1\,\mathrm{g}$. Which of these objects has a mass that exceeds the measuring range of the scale?

- A melon of $2.8\,\mathrm{kg}$
- A teaspoon of salt of $5\,\mathrm{g}$
- A bag of potatoes of $3.5\,\mathrm{kg}$
- A mug of $350\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

The range is the largest mass the scale can measure, so its measuring range extends to $3\,\mathrm{kg}$. The bag of potatoes has a mass of $3.5\,\mathrm{kg}$, which is greater than the range, so it exceeds the measuring range. The melon and the mug have masses less than $3\,\mathrm{kg}$. The teaspoon of salt has a mass of $5\,\mathrm{g}$, which the scale can distinguish because its resolution is $1\,\mathrm{g}$.
