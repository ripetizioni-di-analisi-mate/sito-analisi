A roll of aluminum foil is $30\,\mathrm{m}$ long, with an uncertainty of $15\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the percentage uncertainty in the length?

- $0.5\%$
- $50\%$
- $5\%$
- $0.05\%$

## Explanation

The value and the uncertainty must be expressed in the same unit. Since $15\,\mathrm{cm}=0.15\,\mathrm{m}$, the relative uncertainty is
$$
u_r=\frac{0.15\,\mathrm{m}}{30\,\mathrm{m}}=0.005.
$$
Multiplying by $100$ gives $0.5\%$. Dividing $15$ by $30$ without converting would give $50\%$, a value one hundred times too large.
