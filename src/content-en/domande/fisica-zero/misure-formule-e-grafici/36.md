A scale with a $1\,\mathrm{g}$ display step shows $82\,\mathrm{g}$ in every repetition. What follows?

- The observed spread is zero, but the resolution limit remains.
- The last digit can be reported to arbitrarily fine precision without further information.
- The mass is known with zero uncertainty.
- The repeated readings alone show that the scale is correctly calibrated.

## Explanation

The maximum equals the minimum, so the half-range is zero. The display still assigns the same value to a range of nearby masses.
