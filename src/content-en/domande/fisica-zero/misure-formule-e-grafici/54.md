A water bottle contains $0.96\,\mathrm{L}$ of water. What is the volume rounded to the nearest tenth of a liter?

- $0.9\,\mathrm{L}$
- $1.1\,\mathrm{L}$
- $0.1\,\mathrm{L}$
- $1.0\,\mathrm{L}$

## Explanation

To round to the tenths, look at the hundredths digit, which is $6$. The digit is at least $5$, so the tenths digit increases by one. The tenths digit is $9$, so increasing it produces a whole unit, and the result is $1.0\,\mathrm{L}$. The zero after the decimal point indicates that the value is reported to the tenths.
