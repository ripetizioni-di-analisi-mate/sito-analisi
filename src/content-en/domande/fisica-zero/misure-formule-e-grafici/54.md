For $q=\dfrac{a}{b}$ with nonzero values a student proposes $b=\dfrac{q}{a}$. Which numerical test disproves the proposal?

- $a=6,b=2$ gives $q=3$ and $\dfrac{q}{a}=0.5\ne 2$.
- $a=4,b=1$ gives $\dfrac{q}{a}=1$.
- $a=9,b=1$ gives $\dfrac{q}{a}=1$.
- $a=b=1$ gives $\dfrac{q}{a}=1$.

## Explanation

A counterexample must contradict the proposed formula. With $a=6$ and $b=2$, $\dfrac{q}{a}$ gives one half instead of the starting value two, whereas for $b=1$ the two expressions agree.
