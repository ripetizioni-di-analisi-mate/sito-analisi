A bicycle odometer records a ride of $(25.0\pm0.5)\,\mathrm{km}$. What is the percentage uncertainty of the measurement?

- $0.5\%$
- $5\%$
- $2\%$
- $50\%$

## Explanation

The relative uncertainty is the ratio of the absolute uncertainty to the measured value:
$$
u_r=\frac{0.5\,\mathrm{km}}{25.0\,\mathrm{km}}=0.02.
$$
The units cancel, so $u_r$ is a pure number. Multiplying by $100$ gives the percentage uncertainty, $2\%$. The value $0.5\%$ confuses the absolute uncertainty in kilometers with a percentage.
