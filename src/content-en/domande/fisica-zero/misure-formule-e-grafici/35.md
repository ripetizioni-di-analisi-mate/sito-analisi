Four readings, $2$, $2$, $2$ and $10\,\mathrm{s}$, have equal weight. What is the midpoint of the extremes minus the arithmetic mean?

- $0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The midpoint of the extremes is $\dfrac{2+10}{2}=6\,\mathrm{s}$. The mean counts repeated entries and is $\dfrac{16}{4}=4\,\mathrm{s}$, giving a difference of $2\,\mathrm{s}$.
