A measurement is reported as $(100\pm2)$ cm. What is the relative uncertainty?

- $0.02$
- $50$
- $2$
- $0.2$

## Explanation

Relative uncertainty is the ratio of the absolute uncertainty to the measured value. The two quantities must be expressed in the same unit. This gives $\dfrac{2}{100}=\dfrac{1}{50}$, and the units cancel.
