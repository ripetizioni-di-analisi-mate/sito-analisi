When you look at an angle at a pointer just above a scale, the reading changes as you move your head. How do you reduce this error?

- Repeat the reading several times from the same oblique viewing angle.
- Keep your eye aligned with the zero mark even when the pointer moves.
- Look straight at the scale, with your eye in line with the pointer.
- Use a magnifying lens while keeping the same oblique viewing angle.

## Explanation

The gap between pointer and scale produces an apparent displacement when viewed obliquely. Looking straight on aligns the pointer with the mark underneath.
