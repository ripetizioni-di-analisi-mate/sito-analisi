On a graph, the horizontal axis shows the time of day in hours and the vertical axis shows the outdoor temperature in degrees Celsius. At $14$ hours (2 p.m.), the thermometer reads $22\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. Which point represents this measurement?

- $(22,14)$
- $(14,0)$
- $(0,22)$
- $(14,22)$

## Explanation

The first coordinate of a point is the value on the horizontal axis, that is, the time, and the second is the value on the vertical axis, that is, the temperature. The time is $14$ and the temperature is $22\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. Therefore the point is $(14,22)$. The point $(22,14)$ swaps the two quantities and would represent $14\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ at $22$ hours.
