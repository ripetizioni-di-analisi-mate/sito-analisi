A model predicts $4.5\,\mathrm{V}$ for a trial measuring $4.1\,\mathrm{V}$. Defining the residual as measurement minus prediction, what is its value?

- $-0.2\,\mathrm{V}$
- $-0.4\,\mathrm{V}$
- $0.4\,\mathrm{V}$
- $8.6\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

Follow the specified order of subtraction, $4.1-4.5=-0.4\,\mathrm{V}$. The negative sign places the measurement below the curve.
