A sensor gives $y=3x$ up to and including $x=4$, then remains at $12$. For $x=6$, what is the extrapolated straight-line value minus the actual reading?

::grafico{linee="0,0 4,12 7,12" punti="4,12" xmin=0 xmax=7 ymin=0 ymax=20 nomi="x,y" legenda=0 titolo="Linear response followed by saturation."}

- $2$
- $18$
- $6$
- $12$

## Explanation

Extending the line would give $3\cdot 6=18$. The saturated sensor reads twelve, giving a difference of $18-12=6$.
