The length of a bike path, rounded to the nearest tenth of a kilometer with values exactly halfway rounded up, is $7.5\,\mathrm{km}$. Which of these values could be the length before rounding?

- $7.44\,\mathrm{km}$
- $7.56\,\mathrm{km}$
- $7.55\,\mathrm{km}$
- $7.45\,\mathrm{km}$

## Explanation

Rounding $7.45$ to the tenths, look at the hundredths digit, which is $5$. Values exactly halfway are rounded up, so $7.45$ becomes $7.5$. The value $7.44$ has hundredths digit $4$, so it becomes $7.4$. The values $7.55$ and $7.56$ have tenths digit $5$ and a following digit of at least $5$, so they become $7.6$.
