A sensor follows $s=s_0+vt$ with $s_0=3\,\mathrm{m}$ and $v=2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. After how long does it read $15\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $6\,\mathrm{s}$
- $9\,\mathrm{s}$
- $24\,\mathrm{s}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

First isolate the displacement, $s-s_0=12\,\mathrm{m}$. Then divide by the speed, $t=\dfrac{12}{2}=6\,\mathrm{s}$.
