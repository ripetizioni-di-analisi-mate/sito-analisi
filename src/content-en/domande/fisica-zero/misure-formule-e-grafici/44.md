An instrument with a $100\,\mathrm{N}$ full scale specifies uncertainty of $1\,\mathrm{\%}$ of full scale. For a $20\,\mathrm{N}$ reading, what percentage of the reading is that?

- $20\,\mathrm{\%}$
- $0.2\,\mathrm{\%}$
- $1\,\mathrm{\%}$
- $5\,\mathrm{\%}$

## Explanation

One percent of full scale is $1\,\mathrm{N}$ at any reading. Relative to $20\,\mathrm{N}$ that corresponds to $100\cdot \dfrac{1}{20}=5\,\mathrm{\%}$.
