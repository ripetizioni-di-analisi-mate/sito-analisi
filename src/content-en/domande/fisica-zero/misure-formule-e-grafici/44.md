A swimmer's time in the $50\,\mathrm{m}$ freestyle, calculated from the video recording, is $52.365\,\mathrm{s}$. Rounding values exactly halfway up, what is this time to the nearest hundredth of a second?

- $52.36\,\mathrm{s}$
- $52.4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $52.37\,\mathrm{s}$
- $52.3\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

To round to the hundredths, look at the third decimal digit, which is $5$. The digit is at least $5$, so the hundredths digit increases by one, from $6$ to $7$. The following digits are dropped. Therefore the rounded time is $52.37\,\mathrm{s}$; the values $52.4\,\mathrm{s}$ and $52.3\,\mathrm{s}$ are rounded to the tenths, not to the hundredths.
