A pin must have a diameter between $9.8$ and $10.2$ millimetres inclusive. Which measurement has its whole interval within the specification?

- $(10.2\pm 0.1)\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $(10.0\pm 0.1)\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $(9.8\pm 0.1)\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $(10.0\pm 0.3)\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The interval centred on $10.0$ with uncertainty $0.1$ runs from $9.9$ to $10.1$ millimetres. Each other interval extends beyond at least one limit.
