A watermelon has a mass $m=(6.0\pm0.1)\,\mathrm{kg}$. What unit does the relative uncertainty of this measurement have?

- The kilogram
- The kilogram squared
- The reciprocal of the kilogram
- None; it is a pure number

## Explanation

The relative uncertainty is the ratio $u_r=\dfrac{u}{m}=\dfrac{0.1\,\mathrm{kg}}{6.0\,\mathrm{kg}}$. The numerator and denominator are both expressed in kilograms, so the unit cancels. Therefore the relative uncertainty is a **pure number**, approximately $0.017$. The kilogram is instead the unit of the absolute uncertainty.
