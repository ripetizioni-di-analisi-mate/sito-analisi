A position is reported as $(0.0\pm 0.2)\,\mathrm{cm}$ relative to an origin. Can $\dfrac{u}{\lvert x\rvert}$ be calculated using that central value?

- Yes, the ratio is $0$.
- Yes, the ratio is $0.2$.
- Yes, the ratio is $1$.
- No, the denominator is zero.

## Explanation

The absolute uncertainty remains $0.2\,\mathrm{cm}$. Relative uncertainty would require dividing it by the magnitude of zero, an undefined operation.
