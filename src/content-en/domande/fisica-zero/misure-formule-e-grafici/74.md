The air trapped in a syringe with its tip sealed is compressed slowly at constant temperature. Under these conditions, the pressure and volume of the air are inversely proportional. If the volume is reduced to one third, what happens to the pressure?

- It triples
- It is reduced to one third
- It stays the same
- It increases by three units

## Explanation

In inverse proportionality, the product $pV$ remains constant. If the volume becomes $\dfrac{V}{3}$, the pressure must become $3p$, because $3p\cdot\dfrac{V}{3}=pV$. Therefore the pressure **triples**. A reduction of the pressure to one third would correspond to direct proportionality.
