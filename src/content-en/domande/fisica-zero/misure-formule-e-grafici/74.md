You have measurements at $2,4,6,8\,\mathrm{s}$. Which prediction uses the model outside the observed interval?

- The prediction at $3\,\mathrm{s}$.
- The prediction at $5\,\mathrm{s}$.
- The prediction at $7\,\mathrm{s}$.
- The prediction at $10\,\mathrm{s}$.

## Explanation

The observed times cover two to eight seconds. A prediction at ten seconds extrapolates and requires the model to hold beyond the last measurement.
