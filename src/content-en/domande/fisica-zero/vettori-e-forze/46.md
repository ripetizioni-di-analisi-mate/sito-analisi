A ship is towed by two tugboats whose cables are arranged symmetrically. With the $x$-axis along the direction of travel, the forces of the cables have components, in newtons, $(300,\,400)$ and $(300,\,-400)$. Which description of the resultant is correct?

- Along the $x$-axis, in the positive direction, with magnitude $1000\,\mathrm{N}$
- Along the $x$-axis, in the positive direction, with magnitude $600\,\mathrm{N}$
- Slanted, with components $(600,\,800)\,\mathrm{N}$
- Along the $y$-axis, with magnitude $800\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The $x$-components add up to $R_x=300\,\mathrm{N}+300\,\mathrm{N}=600\,\mathrm{N}$. The $y$-components are opposite, so $R_y=400\,\mathrm{N}-400\,\mathrm{N}=0\,\mathrm{N}$. Therefore the resultant lies along the $x$-axis, in the positive direction, and has magnitude $600\,\mathrm{N}$. Each cable pulls with $\sqrt{300^2+400^2}\,\mathrm{N}=500\,\mathrm{N}$, but the sum of the magnitudes, $1000\,\mathrm{N}$, differs from the magnitude of the resultant because the two cables are not parallel.
