An object is dropped and loses contact with the hand. Neglecting air, which force arrow remains immediately after release?

- No force until the object reaches an appreciable speed.
- An upward support force from the hand equal to the weight.
- A downward push from the hand.
- Weight directed downward.

## Explanation

Release removes the hand's contact force. The gravitational interaction with Earth continues from the initial instant.
