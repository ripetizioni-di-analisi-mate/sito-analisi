Two children pull a sled across the snow. In newtons, their forces have components $(3,\,1)$ and $(5,\,5)$. What is the magnitude of the resultant?

- $14\,\mathrm{N}$
- $100\,\mathrm{N}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N}$
- $6\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The resultant is found by adding the components separately, so $R_x=3\,\mathrm{N}+5\,\mathrm{N}=8\,\mathrm{N}$ and $R_y=1\,\mathrm{N}+5\,\mathrm{N}=6\,\mathrm{N}$. The two components are perpendicular, so
$$
R=\sqrt{8^2+6^2}\,\mathrm{N}=\sqrt{100}\,\mathrm{N}=10\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
