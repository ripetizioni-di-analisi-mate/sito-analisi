A moving walkway carries a person $4\,\mathrm{m}$ east relative to the floor. During that interval the person walks $3\,\mathrm{m}$ west relative to the walkway. Where are they relative to their starting place on the floor?

- $3\,\mathrm{m}$ west.
- $7\,\mathrm{m}$ east.
- $1\,\mathrm{m}$ west.
- $1\,\mathrm{m}$ east.

## Explanation

Combine the person's displacement relative to the walkway with the walkway's displacement relative to the floor. Taking east as positive gives $-3+4=1\,\mathrm{m}$.
