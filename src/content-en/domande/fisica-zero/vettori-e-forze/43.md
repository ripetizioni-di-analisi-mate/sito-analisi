A hand pulls a rope, and the rope pulls the hand with an equal opposite force. Why do those two arrows alone fail to give the resultant on the hand?

- One of the two acts only when the hand is moving.
- Opposite forces cannot be drawn on the same axis.
- The two forces have different magnitudes by definition.
- Only the force exerted by the rope acts on the hand.

## Explanation

To form the resultant on a body, list only forces acting on that body. The force exerted by the hand acts on the rope.
