A spring with spring constant $k=700$ N/m is stretched by $0.02$ m. What is the magnitude of the spring force?

- $1400\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.14\,\mathrm{N}$
- $14\,\mathrm{N}$
- $700\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Hooke's law gives the magnitude $F=k\Delta\ell$. The extension is expressed in meters. Therefore $F=700\cdot0.02=14$ newtons.
