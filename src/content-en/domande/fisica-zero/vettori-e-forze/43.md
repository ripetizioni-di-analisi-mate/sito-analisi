With no wind, a raindrop undergoes a displacement with components $A_x=0\,\mathrm{m}$ and $A_y=-2\,\mathrm{m}$, with the $y$-axis pointing up. Which description of the displacement is correct?

- Horizontal, pointing left, magnitude $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- Vertical, pointing up, magnitude $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- Vertical, pointing down, magnitude $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- Vertical, pointing down, magnitude $-2\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The horizontal component is zero, so the arrow has no part along the $x$-axis and lies along the vertical. The component $A_y$ is negative, so the arrow points opposite to the positive direction of the $y$-axis, that is, down. The magnitude is $\sqrt{0^2+(-2)^2}\,\mathrm{m}=2\,\mathrm{m}$, a positive value.
