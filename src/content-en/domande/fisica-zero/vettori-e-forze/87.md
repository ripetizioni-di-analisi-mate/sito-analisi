A student plots $F$ against the total length $l$ of a stretched spring and forces the line through the graph origin. How should the horizontal axis be changed if force remains vertical and a line through the origin is desired?

- Use $\dfrac{l}{l_0}$ as the horizontal coordinate.
- Only change the length axis from metres to centimetres.
- Use $l-l_0$ as the horizontal coordinate.
- Use $l+l_0$ as the horizontal coordinate.

## Explanation

The tensile force vanishes at the natural length $l_0$. Shifting the origin makes the horizontal coordinate the extension, giving $F=k(l-l_0)$.
