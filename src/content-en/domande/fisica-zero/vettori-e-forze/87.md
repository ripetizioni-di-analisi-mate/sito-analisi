The data sheet of a toy spring gives a spring constant of $2\,\mathrm{N/cm}$. What is the spring constant in newtons per meter?

- $0.02\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $2000\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $200\,\mathrm{N/m}$

## Explanation

The value $2\,\mathrm{N/cm}$ means that the spring force increases by $2\,\mathrm{N}$ for each centimeter of extension. Since $1\,\mathrm{cm}=0.01\,\mathrm{m}$,
$$
2\,\mathrm{N/cm}=\frac{2\,\mathrm{N}}{0.01\,\mathrm{m}}=200\,\mathrm{N/m}.
$$
One meter contains one hundred centimeters, so the value in newtons per meter is one hundred times the value in newtons per centimeter.
