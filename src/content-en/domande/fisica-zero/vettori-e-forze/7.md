Two displacements have components $(5,-2)\,\mathrm{m}$ and $(-2,6)\,\mathrm{m}$. What are the components of the total displacement?

- $(3,4)\,\mathrm{m}$
- $(7,4)\,\mathrm{m}$
- $(3,8)\,\mathrm{m}$
- $(7,-8)\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Add the horizontal and vertical components separately. They are $5+(-2)=3\,\mathrm{m}$ and $-2+6=4\,\mathrm{m}$.
