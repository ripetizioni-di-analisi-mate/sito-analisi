A freezer display reads $-18\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. Which statement about this quantity is correct?

- It is a vector quantity, because the minus sign indicates a sense
- It is the magnitude of a vector pointing down
- It is a scalar quantity, and the minus sign is part of the temperature value
- It is a vector quantity with magnitude $18\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

Temperature is described by a number and its unit, so it is a **scalar** quantity. The value $-18\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ indicates a temperature $18$ degrees below zero on the Celsius scale. The minus sign belongs to the number and does not specify any direction or sense.
