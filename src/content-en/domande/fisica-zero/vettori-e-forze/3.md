Two hands push a cart with $7\,\mathrm{N}$ and $5\,\mathrm{N}$, both to the right. What is the magnitude of their resultant?

- $35\,\mathrm{N}$
- $2\,\mathrm{N}$
- $6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The vectors have the same direction and point the same way. Add their magnitudes, $7+5=12\,\mathrm{N}$.
