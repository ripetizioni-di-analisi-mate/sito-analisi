Two perpendicular forces have magnitudes of $9$ N and $12$ N. What is the magnitude of the resultant?

- $21\,\mathrm{N}$
- $3\,\mathrm{N}$
- $15\,\mathrm{N}$
- $75\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The components are perpendicular, so the Pythagorean theorem applies. This gives $F=\sqrt{(9)^2+(12)^2}$. The magnitude is $15$ newtons.
