In a building, one elevator rises $6\,\mathrm{m}$ while another descends $6\,\mathrm{m}$. What do the two displacements have in common?

- Magnitude and direction; their senses are opposite
- Magnitude, direction, and sense
- Only the sense
- Direction and sense; their magnitudes differ

## Explanation

Both displacements lie along the vertical, so they have the same **direction**. Both measure $6\,\mathrm{m}$, so they have the same magnitude. The first points up and the second points down, so their **senses** are opposite.
