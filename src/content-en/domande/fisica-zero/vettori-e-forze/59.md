Two spring scales measure perpendicular force components. The horizontal component is positive, while the vertical component is compatible with both $+0.1\,\mathrm{N}$ and $-0.1\,\mathrm{N}$. Which directional statement is certain?

- It definitely points up and right.
- It definitely points down and right.
- It definitely lies on the horizontal axis.
- The arrow points right, but which side of the horizontal it lies on is unresolved.

## Explanation

The positive horizontal component places the arrow in the right half of the plane. The uncertain vertical sign leaves the upper or lower half unresolved.
