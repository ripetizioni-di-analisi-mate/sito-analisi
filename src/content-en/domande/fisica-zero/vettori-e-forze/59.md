A force gauge held horizontally is pulled to the right and reads $3\,\mathrm{N}$. What information does the scale of the instrument provide?

- The magnitude, direction, and sense of the force
- The mass of the body pulling, in kilograms
- The spring constant of the spring inside
- The magnitude of the force; the direction and sense depend on how the instrument is oriented and pulled

## Explanation

A **force gauge** measures the magnitude of a force, and its scale is marked in newtons. The reading of $3\,\mathrm{N}$ therefore gives the magnitude. The direction and sense follow from the orientation of the instrument and the side from which it is pulled, in this case horizontal and to the right.
