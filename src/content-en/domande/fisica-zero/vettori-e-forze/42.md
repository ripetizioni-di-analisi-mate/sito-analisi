In two tests you push a door with the same perpendicular force, first near the hinge and then near the handle. Which feature of the drawing distinguishes the turning effect?

- The length of the arrow at the same drawing scale and force magnitude.
- The force magnitude, which must be greater near the handle.
- The angle between the push and the door surface.
- The distance from the hinge to the line of action.

## Explanation

The arrows have the same magnitude and orientation, but the one at the handle passes farther from the hinge. This larger distance increases the turning effect of the same force.
