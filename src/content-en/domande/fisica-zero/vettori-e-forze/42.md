A metal ring is pulled by three ropes. The first two exert forces with components, in newtons, $(4,\,1)$ and $(2,\,-5)$. What components must the force of the third rope have for the resultant on the ring to be zero?

- $(-6,\,4)\,\mathrm{N}$
- $(6,\,-4)\,\mathrm{N}$
- $(-6,\,-4)\,\mathrm{N}$
- $(-2,\,4)\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The first two forces add up to $(4+2,\,1-5)\,\mathrm{N}=(6,\,-4)\,\mathrm{N}$. The resultant of the three forces is zero when the third force is the opposite vector of this sum, that is, when it has the same components with the signs changed. Therefore the third force has components $(-6,\,4)\,\mathrm{N}$.
