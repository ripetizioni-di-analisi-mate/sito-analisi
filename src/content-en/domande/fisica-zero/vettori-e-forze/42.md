A body weighs $70$ N at a location where $g=10$ meters per second squared. What is its mass?

- $700\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $7\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $70\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.7\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Rearranging $P=mg$ gives $m=\dfrac{P}{g}$. Substituting the values gives $\dfrac{70}{10}$. The mass is therefore $7$ kilograms.
