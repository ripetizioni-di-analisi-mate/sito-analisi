On the scale of a school force gauge there are four equal intervals between the $4\,\mathrm{N}$ mark and the $5\,\mathrm{N}$ mark. The pointer stops on the third mark past $4\,\mathrm{N}$. What value does the instrument read?

- $7\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.75\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4.75\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4.25\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The difference between the two numbered marks is $1\,\mathrm{N}$, split into four equal intervals, so each interval is $\dfrac{1\,\mathrm{N}}{4}=0.25\,\mathrm{N}$. Three intervals amount to $0.75\,\mathrm{N}$. Therefore the reading is $4\,\mathrm{N}+0.75\,\mathrm{N}=4.75\,\mathrm{N}$.
