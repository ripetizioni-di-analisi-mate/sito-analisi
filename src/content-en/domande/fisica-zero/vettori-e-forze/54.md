On another planet an equal-arm balance balances an object against a $200\,\mathrm{g}$ standard. Both are in the same nonzero uniform field. What is known about the object?

- It has less mass than the standard if the field is weaker than Earth's.
- It has mass $200\,\mathrm{g}$ regardless of local $g$.
- It weighs $200\,\mathrm{N}$ regardless of the field.
- Its mass is unknown until $g$ is measured.

## Explanation

With equal arms, balance requires equal weights. The common local factor cancels from $m_1g=m_2g$, giving equal masses.
