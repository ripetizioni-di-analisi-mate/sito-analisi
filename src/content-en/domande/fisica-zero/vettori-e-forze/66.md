A graph has force on the horizontal axis and extension on the vertical axis. Its slope is $0.025\,\mathrm{m/N}$. What stiffness does it represent?

::grafico{linee="0,0 4,10" punti="4,10" xmin=0 xmax=5 ymin=0 ymax=14 nomi="F,x" legenda=0 titolo="Force in newtons and extension in centimetres."}

- $25\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $40\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $0.025\,\mathrm{N/m}$

## Explanation

With these axes the slope is extension divided by force, or $\dfrac{1}{k}$. Invert the value, $k=\dfrac{1}{0.025}=40\,\mathrm{N/m}$.
