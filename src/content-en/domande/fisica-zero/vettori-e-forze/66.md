A skateboard is at rest on a sloped ramp. What are the direction and sense of the skateboard's weight?

- Perpendicular to the ramp, pointing into the ramp
- Parallel to the ramp, pointing toward the bottom of the ramp
- Vertical, pointing up
- Vertical, pointing down

## Explanation

Weight is the gravitational force that Earth exerts on the skateboard. Near Earth's surface this force is directed vertically downward, whatever surface the body rests on. Hence the slope of the ramp does not change the direction of the weight.
