Someone asks where a bakery is and is told “$300\,\mathrm{m}$ from here.” What information is missing to locate the bakery?

- The magnitude of the displacement
- The direction and sense of the displacement
- The mass of the person
- The time needed to get there

## Explanation

The displacement from the person to the bakery is a vector quantity. The answer gives only its magnitude, $300\,\mathrm{m}$. Every point $300\,\mathrm{m}$ away from the person fits that description, so locating the bakery also requires the **direction** and the **sense**, for example “along this street, toward the square.”
