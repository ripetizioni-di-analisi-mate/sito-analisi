Choose east as positive. Displacement A is $9\,\mathrm{m}$ west. What are the components of A and its opposite, in that order?

- $(-9,-9)\,\mathrm{m}$
- $(+9,+9)\,\mathrm{m}$
- $(+9,-9)\,\mathrm{m}$
- $(-9,+9)\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

West has a negative component on this axis. The opposite vector has the same magnitude and points east, so its component changes sign.
