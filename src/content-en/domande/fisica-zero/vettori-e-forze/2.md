Two forces point in opposite directions and have magnitudes of $9$ N and $3$ N. What is the magnitude of the resultant?

- $12\,\mathrm{N}$
- $6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $27\,\mathrm{N}$
- $3\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

For collinear forces pointing in opposite directions, subtract the smaller magnitude from the larger one. The resultant points in the direction of the larger force. Its magnitude is $9-3=6$ newtons.
