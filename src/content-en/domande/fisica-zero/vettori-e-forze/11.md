An object weighs $3.5\,\mathrm{N}$ where $g=10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$. What is its mass?

- $0.35\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $3.5\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $2.86\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $35\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Rearrange $P=mg$ to get $m=\dfrac{P}{g}$. The ratio $\dfrac{3.5}{10}$ is $0.35\,\mathrm{kg}$.
