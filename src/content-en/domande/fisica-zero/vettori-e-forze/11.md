A weather report gives four pieces of data for the same city. Which one describes a vector quantity?

- Wind of $20\,\mathrm{km/h}$ from the north
- Temperature of $14\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- Relative humidity of $70\,\%$
- Rainfall: $5\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The wind data include a value, $20\,\mathrm{km/h}$, and an orientation, from north to south. A quantity described by a magnitude, a direction, and a sense is a **vector** quantity. Temperature, humidity, and rainfall are each described by a single number with its unit, so they are scalar quantities.
