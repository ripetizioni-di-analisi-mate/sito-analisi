Two forces point in opposite directions and have magnitudes of $12$ N and $4$ N. What is the magnitude of the resultant?

- $16\,\mathrm{N}$
- $48\,\mathrm{N}$
- $8\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

For collinear forces pointing in opposite directions, subtract the smaller magnitude from the larger one. The resultant points in the direction of the larger force. Its magnitude is $12-4=8$ newtons.
