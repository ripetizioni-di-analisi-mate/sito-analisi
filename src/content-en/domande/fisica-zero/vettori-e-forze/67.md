A laboratory balance manual lists the value $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Which equivalent form is used to calculate weight?

- $9.81\,\mathrm{N}$
- $9.81\,\mathrm{kg/N}$
- $9.81\,\mathrm{N/kg}$
- $9.81\,\mathrm{N\cdot kg}$

## Explanation

In the relation $P=mg$, the weight is in newtons and the mass is in kilograms. Dividing the weight by the mass gives $g=\dfrac{P}{m}$, which is therefore measured in newtons per kilogram. Hence $9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ is equivalent to $9.81\,\mathrm{N/kg}$ when calculating weight, and each kilogram of mass has a weight of $9.81\,\mathrm{N}$.
