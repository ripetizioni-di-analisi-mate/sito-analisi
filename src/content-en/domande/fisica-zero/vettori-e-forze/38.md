Two forces point in opposite directions and have magnitudes of $21$ N and $7$ N. What is the magnitude of the resultant?

- $28\,\mathrm{N}$
- $14\,\mathrm{N}$
- $147\,\mathrm{N}$
- $7\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

For collinear forces pointing in opposite directions, subtract the smaller magnitude from the larger one. The resultant points in the direction of the larger force. Its magnitude is $21-7=14$ newtons.
