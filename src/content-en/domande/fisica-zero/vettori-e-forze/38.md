A force has magnitude $6\,\mathrm{N}$. A sensor reports a horizontal component of $-8\,\mathrm{N}$. Without any other measurements, what can you conclude?

- The vertical component must be $14\,\mathrm{N}$.
- The negative sign resolves the apparent inconsistency.
- The data are inconsistent because the component magnitude exceeds the force magnitude.
- The vertical component must be $2\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

A projection cannot be longer than the arrow itself. Here $8>6$, regardless of the vertical direction.
