Two perpendicular axes $x$ and $y$ are marked out on a lawn. A drone takes off from the origin and lands at the point with coordinates $(-6\,\mathrm{m},\,8\,\mathrm{m})$. What is the magnitude of the displacement from the takeoff point to the landing point?

- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $14\,\mathrm{m}$
- $100\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The displacement runs from the origin to the landing point, so its components are $A_x=-6\,\mathrm{m}$ and $A_y=8\,\mathrm{m}$. By the Pythagorean theorem, its magnitude is
$$
A=\sqrt{(-6)^2+8^2}\,\mathrm{m}=\sqrt{36+64}\,\mathrm{m}=10\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
The negative sign of $A_x$ does not affect the magnitude, because $(-6)\cdot(-6)=36$.
