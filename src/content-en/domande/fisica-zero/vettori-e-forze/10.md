An object has mass $1.20\,\mathrm{kg}$. With $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is its weight magnitude to three significant figures?

- $8.18\,\mathrm{N}$
- $11.8\,\mathrm{N}$
- $118\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1.20\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Calculate $P=mg=1.20\cdot 9.81=11.772\,\mathrm{N}$. To three significant figures this rounds to $11.8\,\mathrm{N}$.
