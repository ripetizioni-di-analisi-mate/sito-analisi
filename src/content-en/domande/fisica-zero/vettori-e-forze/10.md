Two forces of $4$ N and $4$ N act along the same line and point the same way. What is the magnitude of the resultant?

- $0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $8\,\mathrm{N}$
- $16\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The two forces point the same way, so their magnitudes add. The resultant points the same way as well. Its magnitude is $4+4=8$ newtons.
