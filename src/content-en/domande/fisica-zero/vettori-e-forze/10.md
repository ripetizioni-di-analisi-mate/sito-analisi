In the figure, arrow $P$ represents the weight of a flower pot, and each unit on the axes corresponds to $10\,\mathrm{N}$. Which description of the force is correct?

::grafico{vettori="1,0,1,-3,P" uguale=1 xmin=-4 xmax=4 ymin=-4 ymax=1 titolo="The weight of the pot; each unit on the axes corresponds to $10\,\mathrm{N}$."}

- Magnitude $3\,\mathrm{N}$, vertical direction, pointing down
- Magnitude $30\,\mathrm{N}$, vertical direction, pointing up
- Magnitude $30\,\mathrm{N}$, vertical direction, pointing down
- Magnitude $-30\,\mathrm{N}$, vertical direction, pointing down

## Explanation

The arrow is $3$ units long and each unit represents $10\,\mathrm{N}$, so the magnitude is $3\cdot10\,\mathrm{N}=30\,\mathrm{N}$. The arrow lies along the vertical, so its direction is vertical. Its tip points down, and this is the sense of the force. A magnitude is nonnegative, so $-30\,\mathrm{N}$ cannot express it.
