A vector has magnitude $6$ N and makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with the positive $x$-axis. What is its $x$-component?

- $6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $3 \sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $3\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The horizontal component is $F_x=F\cos60^\circ$. The cosine of $60^\circ$ is $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Therefore $F_x=3$ newtons.
