The wind pushes on a boat's sail with a horizontal force of $120\,\mathrm{N}$. The drawing uses a scale in which $1\,\mathrm{cm}$ represents $40\,\mathrm{N}$. How long is the arrow that represents the force?

- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.33\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $3\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $30\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Each centimeter of the drawing represents $40\,\mathrm{N}$. The length of the arrow is therefore found by dividing the force by the **scale**:
$$
\ell=\frac{120\,\mathrm{N}}{40\,\mathrm{N/cm}}=3\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
The arrow is horizontal and points the way the wind pushes the sail.
