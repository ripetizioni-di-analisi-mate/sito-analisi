A cart experiences $3\,\mathrm{N}$ rightward, $12\,\mathrm{N}$ leftward and $4\,\mathrm{N}$ rightward. With right positive, what is the resultant component?

- $-19\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $19\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The two rightward pushes total $7\,\mathrm{N}$. Include the leftward force with its sign to obtain $7-12=-5\,\mathrm{N}$.
