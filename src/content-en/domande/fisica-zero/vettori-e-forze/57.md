An unloaded spring scale reads $0.8\,\mathrm{N}$. In a trial it reads $3.8\,\mathrm{N}$. If the zero error is constant and additive, how much force is actually applied?

- $0.8\,\mathrm{N}$
- $3\,\mathrm{N}$
- $3.8\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4.6\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The indication includes the force and the unloaded offset. Subtract $0.8\,\mathrm{N}$ from the reading to obtain $3.0\,\mathrm{N}$.
