The package of an adhesive hook states the maximum force the hook can withstand. In which SI unit is this force expressed?

- Kilogram, $\mathrm{kg}$
- Newton, $\mathrm{N}$
- Newton per meter, $\mathrm{N/m}$
- Newton per kilogram, $\mathrm{N/kg}$

## Explanation

Force is a vector quantity, and its SI unit is the **newton**, with symbol $\mathrm{N}$. The kilogram measures mass. The newton per meter is the unit of the spring constant, while the newton per kilogram is the unit of $g$ used to calculate weight.
