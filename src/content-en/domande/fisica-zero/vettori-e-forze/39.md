Two perpendicular forces have magnitudes of $21$ N and $28$ N. What is the magnitude of the resultant?

- $49\,\mathrm{N}$
- $7\,\mathrm{N}$
- $35\,\mathrm{N}$
- $175\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The components are perpendicular, so the Pythagorean theorem applies. This gives $F=\sqrt{(21)^2+(28)^2}$. The magnitude is $35$ newtons.
