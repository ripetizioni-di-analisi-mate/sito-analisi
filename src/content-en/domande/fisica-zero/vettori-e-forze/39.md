An actuator pushes with $3\,\mathrm{N}$ east for two seconds and then with $9\,\mathrm{N}$ west for one second. What is the mean component over the full interval, taking east as positive and weighting by duration?

- $-6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-1\,\mathrm{N}$
- $5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Multiplying the signed forces by their durations gives $3\cdot 2=6$ and $-9\cdot 1=-9$ newton seconds. Divide their sum by three seconds to obtain $-1\,\mathrm{N}$.
