The guy line of a camping tent pulls the pole to the left and down. With the $x$-axis pointing right and the $y$-axis pointing up, what are the signs of the components of the force?

- $F_x<0$ and $F_y<0$
- $F_x>0$ and $F_y<0$
- $F_x<0$ and $F_y>0$
- $F_x>0$ and $F_y>0$

## Explanation

Part of the force points to the left, opposite to the positive direction of the $x$-axis, so $F_x$ is negative. Part of it points down, opposite to the positive direction of the $y$-axis, so $F_y$ is negative as well.
