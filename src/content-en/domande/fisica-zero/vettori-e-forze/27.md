A runner completes a closed $400\,\mathrm{m}$ track and returns exactly to the starting line. What are the distance travelled and displacement magnitude, in that order?

- $0\,\mathrm{m}$, $400\,\mathrm{m}$
- $400\,\mathrm{m}$, $400\,\mathrm{m}$
- $400\,\mathrm{m}$, $0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m}$, $0\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Distance counts the whole route, so it is one full lap. Displacement connects only the initial and final positions, which coincide.
