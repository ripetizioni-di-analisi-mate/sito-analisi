The resultant of two forces is $5$ N to the right. What third force would make the total resultant zero?

- $5$ N to the right
- $10$ N to the left
- $5$ N to the left
- A force of zero

## Explanation

For equilibrium, the vector sum must be zero. The required force has the same magnitude as the resultant and points in the opposite direction. A force of $5$ N to the left is therefore needed.
