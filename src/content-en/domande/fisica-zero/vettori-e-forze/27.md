Two people drag a heavy table using two horizontal ropes perpendicular to each other: one pulls with $72\,\mathrm{N}$, the other with $96\,\mathrm{N}$. What is the magnitude of the resultant of the two forces?

- $168\,\mathrm{N}$
- $24\,\mathrm{N}$
- $84\,\mathrm{N}$
- $120\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The two forces are perpendicular, so the magnitude of the resultant is given by $R=\sqrt{F_1^2+F_2^2}$. We have $72^2=5184$ and $96^2=9216$, and their sum is $14\,400$. Since $120\cdot120=14\,400$, the resultant has magnitude $120\,\mathrm{N}$.
