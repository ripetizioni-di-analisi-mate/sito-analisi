A sled dog pulls a sled with a force of $90\,\mathrm{N}$ toward the north. A second force, parallel to the first, also acts on the sled, and the resultant of the two is $30\,\mathrm{N}$ toward the north. What is the second force?

- $60\,\mathrm{N}$ toward the south
- $60\,\mathrm{N}$ toward the north
- $120\,\mathrm{N}$ toward the south
- $30\,\mathrm{N}$ toward the south

## Explanation

Choose an axis whose positive direction is north. The component of the dog's force is $+90\,\mathrm{N}$ and that of the resultant is $+30\,\mathrm{N}$. Since $R_x=F_{1x}+F_{2x}$, the second component is
$$
F_{2x}=30\,\mathrm{N}-90\,\mathrm{N}=-60\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
Therefore the second force has magnitude $60\,\mathrm{N}$ and points **south**.
