Two forces of $5$ N and $4$ N act along the same line and point the same way. What is the magnitude of the resultant?

- $1\,\mathrm{N}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N}$
- $9\,\mathrm{N}$
- $13\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The two forces point the same way, so their magnitudes add. The resultant points the same way as well. Its magnitude is $5+4=9$ newtons.
