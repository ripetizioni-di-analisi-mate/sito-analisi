A displacement points along $(3,4)$. Which unitless pair points the same way and has magnitude one?

- $(0.6,0.8)$
- $(-0.6,-0.8)$
- $(0.3,0.4)$
- $(3,4)$

## Explanation

The original pair has magnitude $\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=5$. Divide both components by five to preserve the direction and obtain unit magnitude.
