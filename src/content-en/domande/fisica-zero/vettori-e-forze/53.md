Two people stand at opposite points on a spherically symmetric planet. Neglecting rotation, how do the weights of two objects at their feet point relative to space?

- Parallel in the same direction toward a universal down.
- Along the same line, in opposite directions toward the centre.
- Both tangent to the local surface.
- Both radially outward.

## Explanation

Local down points toward the centre of the planet. From opposite ends of a diameter the arrows toward the centre point opposite ways.
