In which of the following situations is it reasonable to describe the body with the point particle model?

- A door turning about its hinge
- A wrench loosening a bolt
- A car traveling from Naples to Rome, studied over the whole trip
- A bicycle wheel spinning about its hub

## Explanation

In the **point particle** model, the size of the body is neglected for the problem under study. A car is a few meters long, while the trip from Naples to Rome covers hundreds of kilometers, so the position of a single point is enough to describe the journey. For the door, the wrench, and the wheel, the rotation of the body matters, so their size and the points of application of the forces cannot be neglected.
