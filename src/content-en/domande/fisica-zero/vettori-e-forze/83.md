A spring scale weighing $2\,\mathrm{N}$ hangs vertically from a rope and supports a stationary $6\,\mathrm{N}$ load from its lower hook. What force must the upper rope exert on the scale?

- $8\,\mathrm{N}$
- $2\,\mathrm{N}$
- $6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The scale experiences its own weight and the lower-hook tension downward. The upper rope must balance their sum, $2+6=8\,\mathrm{N}$.
