A student stretches the spring of a toy several times, measuring the spring force and the extension each time and staying within the range where Hooke's law holds. Plotting force as a function of extension, which graph does the student obtain?

- A horizontal straight line
- A straight line through the origin
- A straight line crossing the force axis above the origin
- A curve that rises more and more steeply

## Explanation

Within the range where **Hooke's law** holds, the force is $F_e=k\,\Delta\ell$, with $k$ constant. When the extension is zero the force is also zero, so the graph passes through the origin. The force is proportional to the extension, so the points lie on a straight line with slope $k$.
