Two forces of $7$ N and $4$ N act along the same line and point the same way. What is the magnitude of the resultant?

- $11\,\mathrm{N}$
- $3\,\mathrm{N}$
- $28\,\mathrm{N}$
- $15\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The two forces point the same way, so their magnitudes add. The resultant points the same way as well. Its magnitude is $7+4=11$ newtons.
