Three vectors of equal magnitude leave the same point at angles $0^{\circ}$, $120^{\circ}$ and $240^{\circ}$. What is their sum?

::grafico{vettori="0,0,2,0;0,0,-1,1.732;0,0,-1,-1.732" xmin=-2 xmax=3 ymin=-2.5 ymax=2.5 uguale=1 legenda=0 titolo="Three outward forces at intervals of 120 degrees."}

- The zero vector.
- A vector of triple magnitude along the first.
- A vector of double magnitude along the third.
- A vector of the same magnitude along the second.

## Explanation

Drawing the three vectors head to tail forms a closed equilateral triangle. The arrow from start to finish therefore has zero magnitude.
