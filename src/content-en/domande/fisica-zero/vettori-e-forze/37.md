A cat in a garden moves $5\,\mathrm{m}$ east and then $7\,\mathrm{m}$ north. Without computing the exact value, between which values does the magnitude of its overall displacement lie?

- Between $0\,\mathrm{m}$ and $5\,\mathrm{m}$
- Between $5\,\mathrm{m}$ and $7\,\mathrm{m}$
- Between $7\,\mathrm{m}$ and $12\,\mathrm{m}$
- More than $12\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The displacements are perpendicular, so $R^2=5^2+7^2=74$. Since $74$ is greater than $7^2=49$, the magnitude $R$ is greater than $7\,\mathrm{m}$. Since $74$ is less than $12^2=144$, the magnitude is less than $12\,\mathrm{m}$. The exact value is $\sqrt{74}\,\mathrm{m}\approx8.6\,\mathrm{m}$.
