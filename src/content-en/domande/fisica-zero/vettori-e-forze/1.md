Four pieces of information are written down while baking a cake. Which one describes a vector quantity?

- The oven temperature, $180\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- The mass of the flour, $250\,\mathrm{g}$
- The baking time, $35\,\mathrm{min}$
- Moving the baking pan $40\,\mathrm{cm}$ to the right, from the counter to the oven

## Explanation

A vector quantity is described by a magnitude, a direction, and a sense. The displacement of the pan is given by a magnitude, $40\,\mathrm{cm}$, and an orientation, to the right, so it is a **vector** quantity. Temperature, mass, and duration are each described by a number with its unit of measurement, so they are scalar quantities.
