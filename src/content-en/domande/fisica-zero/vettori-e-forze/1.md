A $15\,\mathrm{N}$ force is drawn at a scale of $3\,\mathrm{N}$ per centimetre on the page. How long should the arrow be?

- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $45\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $3\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Each centimetre represents three newtons. The number of centimetres required is $\dfrac{15}{3}=5$.
