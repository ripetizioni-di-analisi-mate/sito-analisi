Two forces of $3$ N and $4$ N act along the same line and point the same way. What is the magnitude of the resultant?

- $7\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$
- $11\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The two forces point the same way, so their magnitudes add. The resultant points the same way as well. Its magnitude is $3+4=7$ newtons.
