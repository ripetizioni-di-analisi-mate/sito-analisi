A stationary object extends a vertical spring of $k=200\,\mathrm{N/m}$ by $3\,\mathrm{cm}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$, what is its mass?

- $6\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.6\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.06\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

At equilibrium, weight equals the spring-force magnitude, $P=200\cdot 0.03=6\,\mathrm{N}$. Dividing by ten newtons per kilogram gives $0.6\,\mathrm{kg}$.
