A cart moves along a straight horizontal guide at constant nonzero velocity. What resultant of all forces is consistent with this motion?

- A nonzero force perpendicular to the guide.
- $0\,\mathrm{N}$
- A constant force against the motion.
- A constant force along the motion.

## Explanation

Constant velocity means there is no change in velocity. The net force is therefore zero even as the cart moves.
