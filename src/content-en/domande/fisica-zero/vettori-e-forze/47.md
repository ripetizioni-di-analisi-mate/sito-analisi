The figure shows two forces $A$ and $B$ applied to the same hook; each unit on the axes corresponds to $1\,\mathrm{N}$. What is the magnitude of the resultant?

::grafico{vettori="0,0,4,-1,A;0,0,2,9,B" uguale=1 xmin=-3 xmax=9 ymin=-2 ymax=10 titolo="Two forces applied to the hook; each unit on the axes corresponds to $1\,\mathrm{N}$."}

- about $13.3\,\mathrm{N}$
- $14\,\mathrm{N}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N}$
- $100\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

From the figure, the components are $A=(4,\,-1)\,\mathrm{N}$ and $B=(2,\,9)\,\mathrm{N}$. Adding the components gives $R_x=6\,\mathrm{N}$ and $R_y=8\,\mathrm{N}$. The components of the resultant are perpendicular, so
$$
R=\sqrt{36+64}\,\mathrm{N}=10\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
The sum of the magnitudes of the two forces, about $13.3\,\mathrm{N}$, is a different quantity.
