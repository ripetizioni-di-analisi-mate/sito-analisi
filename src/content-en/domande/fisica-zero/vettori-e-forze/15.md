A body weighs $40$ N at a location where $g=10$ meters per second squared. What is its mass?

- $400\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $40\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.4\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Rearranging $P=mg$ gives $m=\dfrac{P}{g}$. Substituting the values gives $\dfrac{40}{10}$. The mass is therefore $4$ kilograms.
