In a tug-of-war, the team on the right pulls with $1350\,\mathrm{N}$ and the team on the left pulls with $1500\,\mathrm{N}$. With the axis pointing to the right, what is the component of the resultant of the two forces on the rope?

- $+150\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-2850\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-150\,\mathrm{N}$
- $+2850\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The force of the right-hand team has component $+1350\,\mathrm{N}$, while the force of the left-hand team has component $-1500\,\mathrm{N}$. The component of the resultant is therefore the sum of the signed components,
$$
R_x=1350\,\mathrm{N}-1500\,\mathrm{N}=-150\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
The negative sign shows that the resultant points to the left.
