A ring is pulled leftward by a spring extended $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ with $k=100\,\mathrm{N/m}$ and rightward by one extended $1\,\mathrm{cm}$ with $k=200\,\mathrm{N/m}$. What is the net force on the ring?

- $0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $5\,\mathrm{N}$ left.
- $1\,\mathrm{N}$ right.
- $1\,\mathrm{N}$ left.

## Explanation

The left spring pulls with $100\cdot 0.03=3\,\mathrm{N}$ and the right spring with $200\cdot 0.01=2\,\mathrm{N}$. One newton remains leftward.
