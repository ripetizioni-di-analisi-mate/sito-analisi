A crate of fruit with a mass of $10\,\mathrm{kg}$ sits on the floor. With a vertical $y$-axis pointing up and $g=9.81\,\mathrm{N/kg}$, what is the $y$-component of the crate's weight?

- $+98.1\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-10\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-9.81\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-98.1\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the weight is $P=10\,\mathrm{kg}\cdot9.81\,\mathrm{N/kg}=98.1\,\mathrm{N}$. The weight points down, opposite to the positive direction of the $y$-axis. Therefore its component is $P_y=-98.1\,\mathrm{N}$.
