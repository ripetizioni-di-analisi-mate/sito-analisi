A push is meant to point along positive $x$, but its components are $(10,2)\,\mathrm{N}$. The alignment criterion is $\dfrac{\lvert F_y\rvert}{F_x}\le 0.1$. What decision do you make?

- It passes because $F_x>0$ alone is enough.
- It fails because the ratio is $5$.
- It passes because the ratio is $0.02$.
- It fails the check because the ratio is $0.2$.

## Explanation

The transverse component has magnitude two newtons compared with ten longitudinally. The ratio $\dfrac{2}{10}=0.2$ is above the one-tenth threshold.
