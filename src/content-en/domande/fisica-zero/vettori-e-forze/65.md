A lunar rover has a mass of $210\,\mathrm{kg}$. On the Moon, $g=1.62\,\mathrm{N/kg}$. What is the magnitude of the rover's weight on the Moon?

- $2060.1\,\mathrm{N}$
- $129.6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $34.02\,\mathrm{N}$
- $340.2\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Weight depends on the value of $g$ at the location of the body. On the Moon, therefore,
$$
P=mg=210\,\mathrm{kg}\cdot1.62\,\mathrm{N/kg}=340.2\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
The value $2060.1\,\mathrm{N}$ is obtained with $g=9.81\,\mathrm{N/kg}$ and is the rover's weight on Earth, where its mass is still $210\,\mathrm{kg}$.
