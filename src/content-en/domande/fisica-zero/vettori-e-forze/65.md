A spring is $20\,\mathrm{cm}$ long under $3\,\mathrm{N}$ and $26\,\mathrm{cm}$ under $6\,\mathrm{N}$. Its natural length is unknown. What is its linear stiffness?

- $100\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $50\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $23.1\,\mathrm{N/m}$

## Explanation

Subtracting the measurements eliminates the natural length. Three extra newtons produce $0.06\,\mathrm{m}$ of extra extension, so $k=\dfrac{3}{0.06}=50\,\mathrm{N/m}$.
