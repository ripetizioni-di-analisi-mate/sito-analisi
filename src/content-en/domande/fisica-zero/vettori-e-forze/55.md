The weight of a bag, about $30\,\mathrm{N}$, is to be measured with a force gauge. Which of the following instruments can make the measurement?

- Capacity $10\,\mathrm{N}$, divisions of $0.1\,\mathrm{N}$
- Capacity $20\,\mathrm{N}$, divisions of $0.2\,\mathrm{N}$
- Capacity $25\,\mathrm{N}$, divisions of $0.1\,\mathrm{N}$
- Capacity $50\,\mathrm{N}$, divisions of $0.5\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The **capacity** is the maximum value the instrument can measure. A force of about $30\,\mathrm{N}$ exceeds capacities of $10\,\mathrm{N}$, $20\,\mathrm{N}$, and $25\,\mathrm{N}$, so those force gauges cannot measure it. The only suitable instrument is the one with a capacity of $50\,\mathrm{N}$, even though its divisions are coarser.
