A light spring scale is stationary. Two people pull its ends with $8\,\mathrm{N}$ each in opposite directions along the scale. What does it read?

- $8\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $16\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The scale measures the tension transmitted through it, equal to the force at either end. The zero net external force keeps the scale stationary.
