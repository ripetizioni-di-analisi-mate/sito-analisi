A spring with spring constant $k=200$ N/m exerts a force of magnitude $10$ N. What is the extension?

- $2000\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.05\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The rearranged formula is $\Delta\ell=\dfrac{F}{k}$. Substituting the values gives $\dfrac{10}{200}$. The extension is therefore $0.05$ meters.
