An arrow of magnitude $8\,\mathrm{m}$ points exactly northeast, halfway between north and east. What is its eastward component?

- $4\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

By symmetry each component equals $a$. From $2a^2=8^2$ obtain $a=\dfrac{8}{\sqrt{2}}=4\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{m}$.
