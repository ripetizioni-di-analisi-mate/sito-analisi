In a park, a boy runs $8\,\mathrm{m}$ east and then $6\,\mathrm{m}$ south. Which statement about his run is correct?

- The distance traveled and the magnitude of the displacement are both $14\,\mathrm{m}$
- The distance traveled is $14\,\mathrm{m}$ and the magnitude of the displacement is $10\,\mathrm{m}$
- The distance traveled is $10\,\mathrm{m}$ and the magnitude of the displacement is $14\,\mathrm{m}$
- The distance traveled is $14\,\mathrm{m}$ and the magnitude of the displacement is $2\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The **distance traveled** is the length of the path, so it equals $8\,\mathrm{m}+6\,\mathrm{m}=14\,\mathrm{m}$. The overall displacement is the arrow from the starting point to the end point. Since east and south are perpendicular directions, its magnitude is $\sqrt{8^2+6^2}\,\mathrm{m}=\sqrt{100}\,\mathrm{m}=10\,\mathrm{m}$.
