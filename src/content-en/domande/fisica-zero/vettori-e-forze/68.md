You need to detect $2\,\mathrm{N}$ using a linear spring. Extension must be at least $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ to be readable and at most $4\,\mathrm{cm}$. Which stiffness works?

- $20\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $200\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $80\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $40\,\mathrm{N/m}$

## Explanation

With $k=80\,\mathrm{N/m}$, extension is $\dfrac{2}{80}=0.025\,\mathrm{m}$, or $2.5\,\mathrm{cm}$. The springs rated twenty and forty exceed four centimetres, while the one rated two hundred reaches only one.
