A wall hook can hold objects whose weight does not exceed $50\,\mathrm{N}$. Approximately what is the maximum mass of an object that can hang from the hook, with $g=9.81\,\mathrm{N/kg}$?

- about $5.1\,\mathrm{kg}$
- about $490\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $50\,\mathrm{kg}$
- about $0.51\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Dividing both sides of $P=mg$ by $g$ gives $m=\dfrac{P}{g}$. With the maximum weight of $50\,\mathrm{N}$ we find
$$
m=\frac{50\,\mathrm{N}}{9.81\,\mathrm{N/kg}}\approx5.1\,\mathrm{kg}.
$$
An object with a larger mass has a weight greater than $50\,\mathrm{N}$.
