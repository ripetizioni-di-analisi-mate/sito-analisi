A spring is stretched between two fixed supports. Heating it increases its natural length, which remains shorter than the distance between the supports. Its stiffness stays the same. How does the force the spring exerts on the supports change?

- It still pulls the supports towards each other, with greater force.
- It still pulls the supports towards each other, with less force.
- The force stays the same because the distance between the supports is fixed.
- The spring starts pushing the supports apart.

## Explanation

The actual length is fixed. As the natural length increases, the difference between the two lengths becomes smaller but remains positive. This difference sets the tension in Hooke’s law, so the pull weakens.
