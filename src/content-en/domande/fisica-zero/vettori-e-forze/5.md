On a trail map, $1\,\mathrm{cm}$ corresponds to $50\,\mathrm{m}$. The arrow from a mountain hut to a spring is $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ long. What is the magnitude of the displacement from the hut to the spring?

- $3000\,\mathrm{m}$
- $56\,\mathrm{m}$
- $300\,\mathrm{m}$
- $30\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Each centimeter of the map represents $50\,\mathrm{m}$ of ground. The arrow is $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ long, so it represents
$$
6\cdot50\,\mathrm{m}=300\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
This value is the **magnitude** of the displacement; its direction and sense are read from the orientation of the arrow on the map.
