A trail takes you $9\,\mathrm{m}$ east and then $12\,\mathrm{m}$ north. What is the magnitude of the displacement from start to finish?

- $15\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $21\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The two sections are the legs of a right triangle. The straight-line distance is $\sqrt{9^2+12^2}=\sqrt{225}=15\,\mathrm{m}$.
