A mass of $3$ kg is at a location where $g=10$ meters per second squared. What is its weight?

- $30\,\mathrm{N}$
- $3\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.3\,\mathrm{N}$
- $300\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The weight is given by $P=mg$. Multiply the mass of $3$ kg by the acceleration of $10$ meters per second squared. Therefore $P=30$ newtons.
