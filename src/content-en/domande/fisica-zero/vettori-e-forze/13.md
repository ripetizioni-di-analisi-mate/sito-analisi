A spring of natural length $16\,\mathrm{cm}$ has constant $200\,\mathrm{N/m}$. It is stretched with $8\,\mathrm{N}$ in the linear regime. What is the final length?

- $20\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $24\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The extension is $\dfrac{8}{200}=0.04\,\mathrm{m}$, or $4\,\mathrm{cm}$. Add it to the natural length, $16+4=20\,\mathrm{cm}$.
