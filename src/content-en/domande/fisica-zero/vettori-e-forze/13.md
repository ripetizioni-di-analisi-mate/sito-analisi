In a supermarket, an axis is chosen along an aisle, with its positive direction toward the checkout. A cart undergoes a displacement with component $+4\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the component of the opposite vector of this displacement?

- $+4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $+0.25\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The **opposite vector** has the same magnitude and direction as the displacement and the opposite sense. The displacement points toward the checkout, so the opposite vector points away from the checkout, that is, in the negative direction of the axis. Therefore the component of the opposite vector is $-4\,\mathrm{m}$.
