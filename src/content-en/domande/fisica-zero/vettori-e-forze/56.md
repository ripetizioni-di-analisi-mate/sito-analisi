A vertical spring scale has a $12\,\mathrm{N}$ capacity. Its attached hanger already weighs $2\,\mathrm{N}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$, what maximum mass can you add slowly?

- $1.2\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.2\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $1.4\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $1\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The remaining capacity is $12-2=10\,\mathrm{N}$. Divide that weight by the gravitational field strength, $\dfrac{10}{10}=1\,\mathrm{kg}$.
