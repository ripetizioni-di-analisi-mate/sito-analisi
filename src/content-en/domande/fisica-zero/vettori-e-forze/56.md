Which of the following situations shows a force that deforms a body without setting it in motion?

- A soccer player kicks a ball at rest
- The wind pushes a sailboat at rest by the pier, which moves away
- A hand squeezes a sponge resting in the sink
- A person pushes a cart at rest, which starts to move

## Explanation

A force can change the motion of a body or deform it. The sponge stays put in the sink and changes shape, so the force of the hand produces a **deformation**. The ball, the boat, and the cart go from rest to motion, so in those cases the force changes their motion.
