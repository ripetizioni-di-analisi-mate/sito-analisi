Two parallel springs between the same plates have stiffnesses of $30$ and $70\,\mathrm{N/m}$. If both extend $5\,\mathrm{cm}$, what is the total elastic-force magnitude?

- $5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $2\,\mathrm{N}$
- $3.5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The extensions are equal and the forces point the same way. Add them to obtain $(30+70)\cdot 0.05=5\,\mathrm{N}$.
