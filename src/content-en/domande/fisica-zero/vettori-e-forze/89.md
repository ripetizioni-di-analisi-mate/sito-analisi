A mass on a vertical spring passes through the equilibrium position while moving upward. At that instant, ignoring friction, how do weight and spring force compare?

- Weight vanishes at the equilibrium crossing.
- The spring force vanishes because the resultant is zero.
- They have equal magnitudes and opposite directions.
- The spring force is greater simply because the velocity is upward.

## Explanation

At the equilibrium position the deformation produces a force equal and opposite to weight. This equality depends on position and holds even while the mass passes through.
