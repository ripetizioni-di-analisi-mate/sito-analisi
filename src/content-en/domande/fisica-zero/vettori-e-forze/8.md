A spring with spring constant $k=200$ N/m exerts a force of magnitude $6$ N. What is the extension?

- $1200\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.03\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The rearranged formula is $\Delta\ell=\dfrac{F}{k}$. Substituting the values gives $\dfrac{6}{200}$. The extension is therefore $0.03$ meters.
