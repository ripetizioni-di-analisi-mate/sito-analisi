Two forces each have magnitude $6\,\mathrm{N}$. What additional condition guarantees zero resultant when they act on a point?

- Along the same line in opposite senses.
- An angle of $60^{\circ}$ between the forces.
- Perpendicular directions.
- Along the same line in the same sense.

## Explanation

Cancelling a vector requires its opposite. Equal magnitudes are given, so the forces must lie along the same line and point opposite ways.
