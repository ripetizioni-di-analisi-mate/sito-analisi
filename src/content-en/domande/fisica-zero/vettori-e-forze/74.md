From the natural position, extensions of $1,2,3,4\,\mathrm{cm}$ require forces of $2,4,6,10\,\mathrm{N}$. At what extension is a departure from the initial linear law first observed?

::grafico{punti="1,2;2,4;3,6;4,10" xmin=0 xmax=5 ymin=0 ymax=12 nomi="x,F" legenda=0 titolo="Extension in centimetres and force in newtons."}

- $1\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $3\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The first three points follow $2\,\mathrm{N}$ per centimetre. At four centimetres the prediction is $8\,\mathrm{N}$, while the measurement is $10\,\mathrm{N}$.
