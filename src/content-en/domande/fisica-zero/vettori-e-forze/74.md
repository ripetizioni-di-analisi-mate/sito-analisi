With $g=9.81\,\mathrm{N/kg}$, which of the following objects has a weight of about $1\,\mathrm{N}$?

- An apple of $100\,\mathrm{g}$
- A watermelon of $10\,\mathrm{kg}$
- A grain of rice of $0.02\,\mathrm{g}$
- A water bottle of $1\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The weight of an object of mass $m$ is $mg$. For the apple this gives $0.1\,\mathrm{kg}\cdot9.81\,\mathrm{N/kg}\approx0.98\,\mathrm{N}$, that is, about $1\,\mathrm{N}$. The $1\,\mathrm{kg}$ bottle weighs $9.81\,\mathrm{N}$, the watermelon about $98\,\mathrm{N}$, and the grain of rice about $0.0002\,\mathrm{N}$.
