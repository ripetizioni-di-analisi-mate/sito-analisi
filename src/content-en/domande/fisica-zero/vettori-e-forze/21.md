The push of the paddle and the drag of the water act on a canoe along the same line. With the positive direction of the axis toward the bow, the component of the resultant is $+12\,\mathrm{N}$. If the axis is chosen instead with its positive direction toward the stern, what are the component and the magnitude of the resultant?

- Component $-12\,\mathrm{N}$, magnitude $12\,\mathrm{N}$
- Component $+12\,\mathrm{N}$, magnitude $12\,\mathrm{N}$
- Component $-12\,\mathrm{N}$, magnitude $-12\,\mathrm{N}$
- Component $+12\,\mathrm{N}$, magnitude $-12\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The resultant is the same force in both cases, pointing toward the bow with magnitude $12\,\mathrm{N}$. With the new axis the bow lies in the negative direction, so the component becomes $-12\,\mathrm{N}$. The magnitude does not depend on the choice of axis and is nonnegative, so it remains $12\,\mathrm{N}$.
