Two perpendicular forces have magnitudes of $15$ N and $20$ N. What is the magnitude of the resultant?

- $25\,\mathrm{N}$
- $35\,\mathrm{N}$
- $5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $125\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The components are perpendicular, so the Pythagorean theorem applies. This gives $F=\sqrt{(15)^2+(20)^2}$. The magnitude is $25$ newtons.
