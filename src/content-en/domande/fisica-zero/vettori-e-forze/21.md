A force has components $(4,-7)\,\mathrm{N}$ with east and north positive. Now choose west and north as positive. What are the components of the same force?

- $(7,-4)\,\mathrm{N}$
- $(4,7)\,\mathrm{N}$
- $(-4,-7)\,\mathrm{N}$
- $(-4,7)\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The horizontal axis reverses, so the horizontal component changes sign. The north axis keeps its orientation, so the second component stays $-7\,\mathrm{N}$.
