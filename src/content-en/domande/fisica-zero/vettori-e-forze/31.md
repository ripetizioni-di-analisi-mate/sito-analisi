A warehouse worker walks the three straight legs shown in the figure, with the $x$-axis pointing east and the $y$-axis pointing north; each unit on the axes is $1\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the magnitude of his overall displacement?

::grafico{vettori="0,0,7,0,A;7,0,7,8,B;7,8,6,8,C" uguale=1 xmin=-1 xmax=9 ymin=-1 ymax=9 nomi="east,north" titolo="The three legs walked inside the warehouse; each unit on the axes is $1\,\mathrm{m}$."}

- $16\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $14\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The three legs are $7\,\mathrm{m}$ east, $8\,\mathrm{m}$ north, and $1\,\mathrm{m}$ west. Along the $x$-axis the components add up to $7\,\mathrm{m}-1\,\mathrm{m}=6\,\mathrm{m}$, while along the $y$-axis they add up to $8\,\mathrm{m}$. The two components of the sum are perpendicular, so
$$
R=\sqrt{6^2+8^2}\,\mathrm{m}=10\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
The value $16\,\mathrm{m}$ is the distance traveled, that is, the sum of the lengths of the three legs.
