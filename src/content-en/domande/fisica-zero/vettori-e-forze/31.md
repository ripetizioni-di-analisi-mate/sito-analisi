Two vectors of magnitudes $8\,\mathrm{N}$ and $3\,\mathrm{N}$ can rotate freely in a plane. What is the full range of possible magnitudes of their sum?

- From $5$ to $11\,\mathrm{N}$ inclusive.
- From $3$ to $8\,\mathrm{N}$ inclusive.
- From $0$ to $11\,\mathrm{N}$ inclusive.
- Only $5\,\mathrm{N}$ or $11\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

Opposite directions give the minimum, $8-3=5\,\mathrm{N}$. Matching directions give the maximum, $8+3=11\,\mathrm{N}$, with intermediate values as one vector rotates.
