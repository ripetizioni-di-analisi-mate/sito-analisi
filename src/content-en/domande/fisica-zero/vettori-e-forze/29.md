Arrows A and B start at the same origin. Which vector is represented by an arrow from A's tip to B's tip?

::grafico{vettori="0,0,2,3,A;0,0,4,1,B;2,3,4,1" xmin=-1 xmax=5 ymin=-1 ymax=4 uguale=1 nomi="x,y" legenda=0 titolo="Two arrows with a common origin and the connection between their tips."}

- $\vec B-\vec A$
- $-\vec A-\vec B$
- $\vec A+\vec B$
- $\vec A-\vec B$

## Explanation

To go from A's tip to B's tip, first return to the origin along $-\vec A$, then follow $\vec B$. The sum is $\vec B-\vec A$.
