In the figure, vectors $A$ and $B$ represent two successive displacements of a robotic lawn mower, placed tip to tail. Which of the arrows $P$, $Q$, $S$, $T$ represents the overall displacement?

::grafico{vettori="0,1,4,1,A;4,1,4,4,B;6,1,10,4,P;16,4,12,1,Q;5,-4,1,-1,S;7,-3,14,-3,T" uguale=1 xmin=-1 xmax=17 ymin=-5 ymax=5 titolo="Vectors $A$ and $B$ placed tip to tail, and four arrows $P$, $Q$, $S$, $T$."}

- Arrow $P$
- Arrow $Q$
- Arrow $S$
- Arrow $T$

## Explanation

By the **tip-to-tail rule**, the sum runs from the tail of $A$ to the tip of $B$. Vector $A$ moves $4$ units to the right and $B$ moves $3$ units up, so the sum moves $4$ units to the right and $3$ units up. Arrow $P$ makes exactly this move, so it represents the overall displacement. Arrow $Q$ points the opposite way, $S$ points to the left, and $T$ is horizontal with length $4+3=7$, so none of these three represents the sum.
