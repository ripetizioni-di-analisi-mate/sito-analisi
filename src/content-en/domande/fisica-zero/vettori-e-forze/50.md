An object weighs $18\,\mathrm{N}$ on planet A. Planet B's gravitational field is one third as strong as A's. What happens to mass and weight?

- Both mass and weight fall to one third.
- Mass stays the same and weight becomes $6\,\mathrm{N}$.
- Mass falls to one third and weight stays $18\,\mathrm{N}$.
- Mass stays the same and weight becomes $54\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

Mass is a property of the sample. Only $g$ changes in $P=mg$, so the new weight is $\dfrac{18}{3}=6\,\mathrm{N}$.
