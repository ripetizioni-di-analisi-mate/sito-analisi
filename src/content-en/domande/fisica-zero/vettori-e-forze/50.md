A box is dragged across the floor with a rope. With the $x$-axis horizontal and the $y$-axis vertical, the force of the rope has components $(4\,\mathrm{N},\,3\,\mathrm{N})$ and the displacement of the box has components $(2\,\mathrm{m},\,0\,\mathrm{m})$. A student adds the components of the two vectors and writes $R_x=6$ and $R_y=3$. Which statement is correct?

- The sum is correct and the result is measured in newtons
- The sum is correct and the result is measured in meters
- The two vectors are not parallel, so their combined magnitude is found with the Pythagorean theorem
- The sum has no meaning, because the components refer to different physical quantities

## Explanation

Adding vectors by components means adding components along the same axis that refer to the same physical quantity. Force is measured in newtons and displacement in meters, so $4\,\mathrm{N}$ and $2\,\mathrm{m}$ are values of different quantities. Therefore the sum $4+2$ has no physical meaning, whatever unit it is written in. For the same reason, combining the two vectors with the Pythagorean theorem has no meaning. Only vectors of the same quantity are added together, for example two forces or two displacements.
