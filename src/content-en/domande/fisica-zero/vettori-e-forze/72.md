On an imaginary planet, the weights of objects of different mass are measured. The graph shows the magnitude of the weight $P$, in newtons, as a function of the mass $m$, in kilograms. What is $g$ on that planet?

::grafico{f="4*x" xmin=0 xmax=6 ymin=0 ymax=25 punti="5,20,pieno" segmenti="5,0,5,20;0,20,5,20" nomi="m (kg),P (N)" legenda=0 titolo="Weight as a function of mass; the highlighted point has $m=5\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $P=20\,\mathrm{N}$."}

- $100\,\mathrm{N/kg}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{N/kg}$
- $9.81\,\mathrm{N/kg}$
- $4\,\mathrm{N/kg}$

## Explanation

The graph is a straight line through the origin, in agreement with the relation $P=mg$. The highlighted point has $m=5\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $P=20\,\mathrm{N}$. Dividing the weight by the mass gives
$$
g=\frac{20\,\mathrm{N}}{5\,\mathrm{kg}}=4\,\mathrm{N/kg}.
$$
The value $9.81\,\mathrm{N/kg}$ applies near Earth's surface, while the data in the graph refer to a different place.
