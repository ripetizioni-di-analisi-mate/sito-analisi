Two elastic cords are arranged in parallel, but the second remains slack until their common displacement reaches $4\,\mathrm{cm}$. At $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ the first cord, with stiffness $100\,\mathrm{N/m}$, has been stretching from zero. What is the total restoring-force magnitude?

- $4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $2\,\mathrm{N}$
- $6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

At two centimetres the second cord is still slack and carries no tension. The first provides $100\cdot 0.02=2\,\mathrm{N}$, which is the entire force.
