A probe at rest weighs a frame carrying a load. The spring scale reads $12\,\mathrm{N}$, then $18\,\mathrm{N}$ after $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ are added to the same frame. The frame and any zero offset are unchanged. What is the local gravitational field strength?

- $6\,\mathrm{N/kg}$
- $9\,\mathrm{N/kg}$
- $3\,\mathrm{N/kg}$
- $15\,\mathrm{N/kg}$

## Explanation

Subtracting the first reading from the second cancels the initial load and any common additive offset. The $6\,\mathrm{N}$ increase is the weight of the additional $2\,\mathrm{kg}$, giving a field strength of $\dfrac{6}{2}=3\,\mathrm{N/kg}$.
