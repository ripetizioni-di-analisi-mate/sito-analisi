A force $\vec{A}$ with components $(2\,\mathrm{N},\,3\,\mathrm{N})$ acts on a piece of furniture, with the $x$-axis horizontal and the $y$-axis vertical. Which force $\vec{B}$, added to $\vec{A}$, gives a horizontal resultant?

- $(1\,\mathrm{N},\,-3\,\mathrm{N})$
- $(-2\,\mathrm{N},\,3\,\mathrm{N})$
- $(-2\,\mathrm{N},\,0\,\mathrm{N})$
- $(3\,\mathrm{N},\,2\,\mathrm{N})$

## Explanation

A force is horizontal when its $y$-component is zero and its $x$-component is nonzero. With $\vec{B}=(1\,\mathrm{N},\,-3\,\mathrm{N})$ the sum is $(3\,\mathrm{N},\,0\,\mathrm{N})$, so the resultant is horizontal. With the other forces the sum is $(0,\,6)\,\mathrm{N}$ or $(0,\,3)\,\mathrm{N}$, which are vertical, or $(5,\,5)\,\mathrm{N}$, which is slanted.
