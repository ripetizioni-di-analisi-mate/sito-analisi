Under $1\,\mathrm{N}$, a linear spring extends by a positive amount less than $1\,\mathrm{mm}$, too small to read. What bound on stiffness follows?

- $k>1000\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $k=0\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $k=1000\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $k<1000\,\mathrm{N/m}$

## Explanation

The denominator in $k=\dfrac{F}{\Delta l}$ is positive and less than $0.001\,\mathrm{m}$. The ratio therefore exceeds $\dfrac{1}{0.001}=1000\,\mathrm{N/m}$.
