The return spring of a garden gate stretches by $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ when it exerts a spring force of $9\,\mathrm{N}$. Assuming Hooke's law holds, what is the spring constant?

- $150\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $54\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $0.54\,\mathrm{N/m}$

## Explanation

The spring constant is found by dividing the force by the extension, $k=\dfrac{F_e}{\Delta\ell}$. The extension in meters is $0.06\,\mathrm{m}$, so
$$
k=\frac{9\,\mathrm{N}}{0.06\,\mathrm{m}}=150\,\mathrm{N/m}.
$$
This value corresponds to $1.5\,\mathrm{N}$ of force for each centimeter of extension.
