After legs with components $(4,1)\,\mathrm{km}$ and $(-1,3)\,\mathrm{km}$, a boat must return straight to its starting point. What displacement must it make now?

- $(-3,-4)\,\mathrm{km}$
- $(5,-2)\,\mathrm{km}$
- $(-5,2)\,\mathrm{km}$
- $(3,4)\,\mathrm{km}$

## Explanation

The first two legs add to $(3,4)\,\mathrm{km}$. To close the route the last leg must cancel both components.
