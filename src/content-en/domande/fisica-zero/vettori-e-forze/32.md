A mass of $6$ kg is at a location where $g=10$ meters per second squared. What is its weight?

- $6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $600\,\mathrm{N}$
- $60\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The weight is given by $P=mg$. Multiply the mass of $6$ kg by the acceleration of $10$ meters per second squared. Therefore $P=60$ newtons.
