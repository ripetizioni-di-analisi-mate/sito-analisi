A linear spring of natural length $25\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $k=160\,\mathrm{N/m}$ allows at most an $8\,\mathrm{\%}$ tensile extension relative to its natural length. What is the force limit?

- $32\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12.8\,\mathrm{N}$
- $40\,\mathrm{N}$
- $3.2\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The allowed extension is $0.08\cdot 25=2\,\mathrm{cm}$, or $0.02\,\mathrm{m}$. The limiting force is $160\cdot 0.02=3.2\,\mathrm{N}$.
