A spring with spring constant $k=400$ N/m is stretched by $0.02$ m. What is the magnitude of the spring force?

- $800\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.08\,\mathrm{N}$
- $400\,\mathrm{N}$
- $8\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Hooke's law gives the magnitude $F=k\Delta\ell$. The extension is expressed in meters. Therefore $F=400\cdot0.02=8$ newtons.
