A robot vacuum moves along a hallway: it goes forward $3.2\,\mathrm{m}$, back $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$, and forward again $0.8\,\mathrm{m}$. Taking forward as positive, what is the component of its overall displacement?

- $+5.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $+0.9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $+2.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $+3.9\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The forward displacements have positive components and the backward one has a negative component. Adding the three components gives
$$
R_x=3.2\,\mathrm{m}-1.5\,\mathrm{m}+0.8\,\mathrm{m}=2.5\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
The robot therefore ends up $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$ ahead of its starting point, even though it traveled $5.5\,\mathrm{m}$ in all.
