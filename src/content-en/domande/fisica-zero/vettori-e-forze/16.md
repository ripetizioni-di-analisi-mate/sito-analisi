An arrow runs from $(-2,5)$ to $(4,1)$ on a grid in metres. What are the components of the displacement?

::grafico{vettori="-2,5,4,1" punti="-2,5;4,1" xmin=-3 xmax=5 ymin=0 ymax=6 nomi="x,y" uguale=1 legenda=0 titolo="Tail and tip on a grid in metres."}

- $(-6,4)\,\mathrm{m}$
- $(6,-4)\,\mathrm{m}$
- $(4,1)\,\mathrm{m}$
- $(2,6)\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Subtract the tail coordinate from the tip coordinate on each axis. This gives $4-(-2)=6$ and $1-5=-4$ metres.
