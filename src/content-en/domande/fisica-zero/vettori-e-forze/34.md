A city block is a square with sides of $100\,\mathrm{m}$. A runner starts at one corner of the block and runs along three consecutive sides. What is the magnitude of his displacement?

- $300\,\mathrm{m}$
- $100\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m}$
- about $141\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Consider, for example, the legs $100\,\mathrm{m}$ east, $100\,\mathrm{m}$ north, and $100\,\mathrm{m}$ west. Along the east-west direction the components add up to $100\,\mathrm{m}-100\,\mathrm{m}=0\,\mathrm{m}$, while along the north-south direction they add up to $100\,\mathrm{m}$. Hence the runner ends at the corner next to the starting corner, and his displacement has magnitude $100\,\mathrm{m}$. The distance traveled, on the other hand, is $300\,\mathrm{m}$.
