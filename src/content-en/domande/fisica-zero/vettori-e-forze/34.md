A spring with spring constant $k=600$ N/m is stretched by $0.02$ m. What is the magnitude of the spring force?

- $1200\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.12\,\mathrm{N}$
- $600\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Hooke's law gives the magnitude $F=k\Delta\ell$. The extension is expressed in meters. Therefore $F=600\cdot0.02=12$ newtons.
