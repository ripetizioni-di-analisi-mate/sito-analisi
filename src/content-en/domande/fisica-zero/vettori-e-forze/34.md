Three forces have components $(4,3)\,\mathrm{N}$, $(-1,-7)\,\mathrm{N}$ and $(0,q)\,\mathrm{N}$. What must $q$ be for the resultant to point horizontally right?

- $7\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $3\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The total horizontal component is already positive, $4-1=3\,\mathrm{N}$. Set the vertical component to zero with $3-7+q=0$, giving $q=4\,\mathrm{N}$.
