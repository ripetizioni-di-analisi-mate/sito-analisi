An anchored buoy at sea is acted on by three horizontal forces: $4\,\mathrm{N}$ toward the north, $4\,\mathrm{N}$ toward the south, and $3\,\mathrm{N}$ toward the east. What is the resultant of the three forces?

- $11\,\mathrm{N}$ toward the east
- $5\,\mathrm{N}$ toward the northeast
- The resultant is zero
- $3\,\mathrm{N}$ toward the east

## Explanation

The northward and southward forces are collinear, have the same magnitude, and point in opposite senses, so their sum is zero. Only the $3\,\mathrm{N}$ force toward the east remains. Therefore the **resultant** has magnitude $3\,\mathrm{N}$ and points east.
