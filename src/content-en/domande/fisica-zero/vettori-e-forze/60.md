A cart moves leftward and experiences a horizontal braking force. Its velocity component on the chosen axis is positive. What is the sign of the braking-force component?

- Positive.
- Zero.
- Negative.
- It can be decided only if the mass is known.

## Explanation

Because leftward motion has a positive component, the positive axis points left. The braking force points right and has a negative component.
