A book with a mass of $1.5\,\mathrm{kg}$ is put into a backpack with a mass of $4\,\mathrm{kg}$. What is the magnitude of the total weight of the backpack with the book, with $g=9.81\,\mathrm{N/kg}$?

- about $39\,\mathrm{N}$
- about $54\,\mathrm{N}$
- about $15\,\mathrm{N}$
- $5.5\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Mass is a scalar quantity, so the total mass is $4\,\mathrm{kg}+1.5\,\mathrm{kg}=5.5\,\mathrm{kg}$. The total weight is therefore
$$
P=5.5\,\mathrm{kg}\cdot9.81\,\mathrm{N/kg}=53.955\,\mathrm{N},
$$
that is, about $54\,\mathrm{N}$, directed vertically downward.
