You want to check whether a spring scale is linear from $0$ to $10\,\mathrm{N}$. Which procedure could reveal a deviation halfway along the scale?

- Apply several known forces across the range and compare each reading with its reference.
- Repeat only the $10\,\mathrm{N}$ load many times.
- Only check that the pointer returns to zero after unloading.
- Check only zero and full scale.

## Explanation

A scale can be correct at both ends and wrong between them. Intermediate standards test precisely that part of the scale.
