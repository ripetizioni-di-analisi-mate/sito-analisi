A force gauge has a capacity of $2\,\mathrm{N}$, and its scale, from $0\,\mathrm{N}$ to $2\,\mathrm{N}$, is divided into $40$ equal intervals. What is the value of one interval of the scale?

- $0.5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.05\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.025\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The scale covers $2\,\mathrm{N}$, divided into $40$ equal parts. Each interval is therefore
$$
\frac{2\,\mathrm{N}}{40}=0.05\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
This is the difference between readings on consecutive marks.
