A vertical spring operates in its linear regime and is already extended by its hanger. Adding a $100\,\mathrm{g}$ standard moves the pointer down $4\,\mathrm{mm}$. Replace the standard with an unknown mass and the pointer moves $10\,\mathrm{mm}$ below the hanger-only reading. What is the unknown mass?

- $150\,\mathrm{g}$
- $40\,\mathrm{g}$
- $400\,\mathrm{g}$
- $250\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

Changes from the hanger-only reading eliminate the tare. In the same field and linear regime mass scales with added extension, $100\cdot \dfrac{10}{4}=250\,\mathrm{g}$.
