A spring hangs from the ceiling, and its lower end is pulled down by $5\,\mathrm{cm}$. The spring constant is $120\,\mathrm{N/m}$ and Hooke's law holds. With a vertical axis pointing up, what is the component of the spring force on the lower end?

- $-6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $+6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $+600\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-600\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the force is $F_e=120\,\mathrm{N/m}\cdot0.05\,\mathrm{m}=6\,\mathrm{N}$. The end has been moved down, and the spring force tends to restore the natural length, so it points up. Since the axis points up, the component is $+6\,\mathrm{N}$.
