Which of the following totals is always found by adding the two given values, with no further information?

- The magnitude of the displacement of someone who walks $1.5\,\mathrm{km}$ and then $0.5\,\mathrm{km}$
- The magnitude of the resultant of two pushes of $15\,\mathrm{N}$ and $5\,\mathrm{N}$ on the same cart
- The magnitude of the displacement of an elevator that moves $15\,\mathrm{m}$ and then $5\,\mathrm{m}$
- The mass of a bag holding a $1.5\,\mathrm{kg}$ sack and a $0.5\,\mathrm{kg}$ bottle

## Explanation

Mass is a scalar quantity, so the total mass is the sum of the masses, $1.5\,\mathrm{kg}+0.5\,\mathrm{kg}=2\,\mathrm{kg}$. Displacements and forces are vector quantities, so the magnitude of their sum also depends on their directions and senses. For example, an elevator that rises $15\,\mathrm{m}$ and then descends $5\,\mathrm{m}$ ends up $10\,\mathrm{m}$ from its starting point, not $20\,\mathrm{m}$.
