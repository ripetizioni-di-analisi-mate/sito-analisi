The scale from $2\,\mathrm{N}$ to $4\,\mathrm{N}$ has ten equal intervals. What does the seventh mark after $2\,\mathrm{N}$ indicate in the increasing direction?

- $4.8\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1.4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $3.4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $2.7\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Each interval is $\dfrac{4-2}{10}=0.2\,\mathrm{N}$. Add seven intervals to the starting mark, $2+7\cdot 0.2=3.4\,\mathrm{N}$.
