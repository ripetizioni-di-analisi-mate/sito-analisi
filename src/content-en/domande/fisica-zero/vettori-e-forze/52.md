A spring scale displays kilograms by dividing force by $10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$. On a planet with $g=4\,\mathrm{N/kg}$, what mass does it display for an actual $5\,\mathrm{kg}$ sample at rest?

- $20\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $12.5\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $2\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $5\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The sample produces a load of $5\cdot 4=20\,\mathrm{N}$. The calibration divides by ten, so the displayed value is $2\,\mathrm{kg}$.
