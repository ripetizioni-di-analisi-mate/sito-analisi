A door is pushed with a horizontal force of $20\,\mathrm{N}$, perpendicular to the door, once near the hinge and once at the handle. The magnitude, direction, and sense of the force are the same in both cases. What distinguishes the two pushes?

- The magnitude, which becomes smaller near the hinge
- The point of application, which changes the turning effect on the door
- The sense, which reverses near the hinge
- Nothing, because the two pushes have the same effect

## Explanation

The two pushes have the same magnitude, direction, and sense, so they are described by the same vector. The door turns about the hinge, so its size matters for the problem. In this case the **point of application** must also be specified, and the same push has a different turning effect near the hinge than at the handle.
