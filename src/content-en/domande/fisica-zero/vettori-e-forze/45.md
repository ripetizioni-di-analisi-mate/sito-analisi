A ball on a taut cord moves right while the anchor point is directly above it. Which way does the cord's force on the ball point at that instant?

- Leftward against the motion.
- Downward away from the anchor.
- Rightward along the motion.
- Upward along the cord.

## Explanation

A taut cord pulls the body along the cord toward the other end. The direction of velocity does not determine the direction of tension.
