Four marbles roll across a table, with displacements whose components, in centimeters, are listed below. Which displacement has the largest magnitude?

- $(3,\,4)$
- $(4,\,-4)$
- $(0,\,5)$
- $(-7,\,0)$

## Explanation

The magnitude is $\sqrt{A_x^2+A_y^2}$. This gives $\sqrt{49}=7$ for $(-7,\,0)$, $\sqrt{25}=5$ for $(3,\,4)$, $\sqrt{32}\approx5.7$ for $(4,\,-4)$, and $\sqrt{25}=5$ for $(0,\,5)$. Therefore the largest magnitude, $7\,\mathrm{cm}$, belongs to the displacement $(-7,\,0)$, even though its $x$-component is negative.
