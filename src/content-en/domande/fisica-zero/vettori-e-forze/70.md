Two light springs in series, of stiffness $40$ and $120\,\mathrm{N/m}$, are stretched in equilibrium. What is their combined stiffness?

- $30\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $80\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $60\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $160\,\mathrm{N/m}$

## Explanation

For the same force $F$, the extensions are $\dfrac{F}{40}$ and $\dfrac{F}{120}$. Their sum is $\dfrac{F}{30}$, so the system acts like a $30\,\mathrm{N/m}$ spring.
