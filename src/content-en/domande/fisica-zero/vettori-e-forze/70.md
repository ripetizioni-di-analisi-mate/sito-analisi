A car tows a trailer by a tow hitch, and the trailer is treated as a point particle. Which list contains all the information needed to describe the force that the hitch exerts on the trailer?

- The magnitude in newtons and the mass of the trailer
- The magnitude, the direction, the sense, and the body on which the force acts
- The magnitude and the direction
- The direction, the sense, and the speed of the car

## Explanation

Force is a vector quantity, so its description includes the magnitude, the direction, and the sense. Describing a force also requires specifying the body on which it acts, in this case the trailer. The trailer is treated as a point particle, so its size does not matter and the point of application need not be given. The mass of the trailer and the speed of the car are not part of the description of the force.
