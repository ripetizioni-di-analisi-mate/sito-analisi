Two sensors report that the resultant has $F_x=F_y=0$. The apparatus can also exert forces along a third perpendicular axis. What else is needed to conclude that the resultant is zero?

- Verify that $F_x+F_y=0$.
- Verify that the magnitude of its projection onto the plane is zero.
- Verify that $F_z=0$ too.
- Repeat the reading with the two sensors rotated only within their original plane.

## Explanation

The first two readings rule out a component in the sensor plane. A force perpendicular to that plane would remain invisible to both sensors.
