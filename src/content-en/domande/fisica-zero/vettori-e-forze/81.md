A horizontal spring is attached to a wall on its right. The left end of the spring is pulled to the left, and the spring stretches. Which way does the spring force exerted by the spring on its left end point?

- To the left, the way it is pulled
- To the right, toward the wall
- Vertically downward
- To the right or to the left, depending on the value of $k$

## Explanation

The spring force tends to restore the spring to its natural length. The left end has been moved to the left, away from the wall, so the force that restores the natural length points to the right, toward the wall. The value of $k$ determines the magnitude of the force, not its sense.
