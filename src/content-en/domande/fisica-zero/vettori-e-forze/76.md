The spring of a hanging kitchen scale has a natural length of $8\,\mathrm{cm}$. When a ladle is hung from it, its length becomes $11.5\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the extension of the spring?

- $11.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $3.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $19.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.35\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The **extension** is the difference between the length of the stretched spring and its natural length, $\Delta\ell=\ell-\ell_0$. Substituting gives $\Delta\ell=11.5\,\mathrm{cm}-8\,\mathrm{cm}=3.5\,\mathrm{cm}$. The final length, $11.5\,\mathrm{cm}$, is a different measurement from the extension.
