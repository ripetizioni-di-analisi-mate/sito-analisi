An unloaded spring is $12.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ long. After a heavy load, it is fully unloaded and allowed to settle, but remains $12.8\,\mathrm{cm}$ long. Which observation establishes a permanent change within a resolution of $0.1\,\mathrm{cm}$?

- Its length grew while the force was applied.
- The force decreased during unloading.
- Its zero-load length increased by $0.8\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- The force during the test exceeded the force that had initially stretched the spring.

## Explanation

Compare the initial and final states under the same zero-load conditions. The remaining difference, $12.8-12.0=0.8\,\mathrm{cm}$, is clearly resolvable.
