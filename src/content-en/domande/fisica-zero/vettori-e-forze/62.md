A spring is fixed on the left. Its right end is pushed leftward from its natural position and held there. Which way does the spring push the hand holding it?

- Rightward, toward the end's natural position.
- Leftward, along the imposed displacement.
- Its direction depends on the numerical value of the positive spring constant.
- Neither way because the end is stationary.

## Explanation

Compression places the end left of its natural position. The restoring force opposes that deformation and therefore acts rightward on the hand.
