On a kitchen scale, a smartphone reads $200\,\mathrm{g}$. With $g=9.81\,\mathrm{N/kg}$, what weight does Earth exert on the phone?

- $1.962\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1962\,\mathrm{N}$
- $20.4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.2\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The value of $g$ is given in newtons per kilogram, so the mass is written in kilograms, and $200\,\mathrm{g}=0.2\,\mathrm{kg}$. Therefore
$$
P=0.2\,\mathrm{kg}\cdot9.81\,\mathrm{N/kg}=1.962\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
Using grams without converting would give $1962\,\mathrm{N}$, a value a thousand times too large.
