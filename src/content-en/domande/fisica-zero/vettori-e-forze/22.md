A vector has magnitude $10$ N and makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with the positive $x$-axis. What is its $x$-component?

- $10\,\mathrm{N}$
- $5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $5 \sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The horizontal component is $F_x=F\cos60^\circ$. The cosine of $60^\circ$ is $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Therefore $F_x=5$ newtons.
