You describe the same force first with horizontal and vertical axes and then with perpendicular axes rotated through $30^{\circ}$. Which quantity always retains the same value?

- The algebraic sum of the two components.
- The force magnitude.
- The component along the first axis.
- The angle measured from the first axis.

## Explanation

Rotating the axes changes the reference used for angles and projections. The physical force and the length of the arrow representing it stay the same.
