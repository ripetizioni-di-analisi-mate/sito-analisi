The figure shows two horizontal forces $F_1$ and $F_2$ acting on a shopping cart; each unit on the axes corresponds to $5\,\mathrm{N}$. With the $x$-axis pointing to the right, what is the component of the resultant?

::grafico{vettori="0,1,4,1,F1;0,-1,-6,-1,F2" uguale=1 xmin=-7 xmax=5 ymin=-2 ymax=2 titolo="Two horizontal forces on the cart; each unit on the axes corresponds to $5\,\mathrm{N}$."}

- $+10\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-10\,\mathrm{N}$
- $+50\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Arrow $F_1$ is $4$ units long and points to the right, so its component is $4\cdot5\,\mathrm{N}=+20\,\mathrm{N}$. Arrow $F_2$ is $6$ units long and points to the left, so its component is $-30\,\mathrm{N}$. The component of the resultant is $20\,\mathrm{N}-30\,\mathrm{N}=-10\,\mathrm{N}$, and the resultant points to the left.
