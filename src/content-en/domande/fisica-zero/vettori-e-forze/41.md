A mass of $7$ kg is at a location where $g=10$ meters per second squared. What is its weight?

- $70\,\mathrm{N}$
- $7\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.7\,\mathrm{N}$
- $700\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The weight is given by $P=mg$. Multiply the mass of $7$ kg by the acceleration of $10$ meters per second squared. Therefore $P=70$ newtons.
