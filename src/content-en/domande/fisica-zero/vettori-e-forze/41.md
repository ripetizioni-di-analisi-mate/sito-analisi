The figure shows, from above, the force $F$ that a cue exerts on a billiard ball; each unit on the axes corresponds to $1\,\mathrm{N}$. What are the components of the force?

::grafico{vettori="0,0,-4,3,F" uguale=1 xmin=-6 xmax=2 ymin=-1 ymax=5 titolo="Force of the cue on the ball, seen from above; each unit on the axes corresponds to $1\,\mathrm{N}$."}

- $F_x=4\,\mathrm{N}$, $F_y=3\,\mathrm{N}$
- $F_x=-4\,\mathrm{N}$, $F_y=3\,\mathrm{N}$
- $F_x=3\,\mathrm{N}$, $F_y=-4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $F_x=-3\,\mathrm{N}$, $F_y=4\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The components are read from the projections of the arrow onto the two axes. The tip lies $4$ units to the left of the origin, so $F_x=-4\,\mathrm{N}$. The tip lies $3$ units above the origin, so $F_y=3\,\mathrm{N}$. The sign of $F_x$ shows that part of the force points in the negative direction of the $x$-axis.
