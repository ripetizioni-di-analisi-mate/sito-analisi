In a drawing, a force acts at $(2,3)$ and points horizontally left. Which equation describes its line of action?

::grafico{vettori="2,3,-1,3,F" punti="2,3" xmin=-2 xmax=4 ymin=0 ymax=5 nomi="x,y" legenda=0 titolo="Point of application and the leftward force."}

- $x=2$
- $x=-2$
- $y=3$
- $y=-3$

## Explanation

The line passes through the point of application and is parallel to the force. The ordinate stays constant along a horizontal line and here equals three.
