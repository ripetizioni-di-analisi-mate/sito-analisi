A bucket full of water, with a mass of $8\,\mathrm{kg}$, is pulled up from a well by a vertical rope that exerts an upward force of $100\,\mathrm{N}$. Considering only the weight and the force of the rope, what is the resultant on the bucket, with $g=9.81\,\mathrm{N/kg}$?

- $21.52\,\mathrm{N}$ upward
- $178.48\,\mathrm{N}$ upward
- $92\,\mathrm{N}$ upward
- $21.52\,\mathrm{N}$ downward

## Explanation

The weight of the bucket has magnitude $8\,\mathrm{kg}\cdot9.81\,\mathrm{N/kg}=78.48\,\mathrm{N}$ and points down. With a vertical axis pointing up, the components are $+100\,\mathrm{N}$ for the rope and $-78.48\,\mathrm{N}$ for the weight. Their sum is $+21.52\,\mathrm{N}$, so the resultant has magnitude $21.52\,\mathrm{N}$ and points up.
