A sack of potatoes has a mass of $5\,\mathrm{kg}$. What is the magnitude of its weight, with $g=9.81\,\mathrm{N/kg}$?

- $49.05\,\mathrm{N}$
- $5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.51\,\mathrm{N}$
- $14.81\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the weight is given by $P=mg$. Substituting the data gives
$$
P=5\,\mathrm{kg}\cdot9.81\,\mathrm{N/kg}=49.05\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
The **weight** is a force directed vertically downward, while $5\,\mathrm{kg}$ is the mass of the sack.
