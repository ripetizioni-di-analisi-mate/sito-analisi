A spring goes from its $18\,\mathrm{cm}$ natural length to $15\,\mathrm{cm}$. With $\Delta  l=l-l_0$, which pair gives the signed deformation and its type?

- $-3\,\mathrm{cm}$, compression
- $+15\,\mathrm{cm}$, tension
- $-15\,\mathrm{cm}$, compression
- $+3\,\mathrm{cm}$, tension

## Explanation

The difference is $15-18=-3\,\mathrm{cm}$. The final length is below the natural length, so the spring is compressed.
