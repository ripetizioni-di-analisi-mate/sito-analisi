A gym chest expander contains a spring with spring constant $k=250\,\mathrm{N/m}$ that obeys Hooke's law. The spring is stretched by $40\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the magnitude of the spring force?

- $100\,\mathrm{N}$
- $10\,000\,\mathrm{N}$
- $6.25\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The spring constant is given in newtons per meter, so the extension enters Hooke's law in meters, and $40\,\mathrm{cm}=0.4\,\mathrm{m}$. By **Hooke's law**,
$$
F_e=k\,\Delta\ell=250\,\mathrm{N/m}\cdot0.4\,\mathrm{m}=100\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
Using centimeters would give $10\,000\,\mathrm{N}$, a value a hundred times too large.
