A light lever in a spring scale triples the pointer travel for the same spring extension, without affecting the spring. The new dial is long enough for the entire travel and keeps the same physical spacing between marks. What happens to force per division and to the spring’s maximum permitted load?

- Both the force per division and the spring’s maximum load become one third as large.
- The force per division triples and the spring’s maximum load stays the same.
- The force per division becomes one third as large and the spring’s maximum load stays the same.
- Force per division stays the same and the spring’s maximum load triples.

## Explanation

For the same force increase, the pointer crosses three times as many divisions. Each division therefore represents one third of the original force increment. The lever magnifies the reading, while the spring reaches its elastic limit at the same load.
