Two tugboats act on a barge along the same line. With the axis pointing east, the component of their resultant is $R_x=-400\,\mathrm{N}$. Which description of the resultant is correct?

- Magnitude $-400\,\mathrm{N}$, pointing east
- Magnitude $400\,\mathrm{N}$, pointing east
- Magnitude $-400\,\mathrm{N}$, pointing west
- Magnitude $400\,\mathrm{N}$, pointing west

## Explanation

The magnitude of the resultant is the absolute value of its component, $R=|-400\,\mathrm{N}|=400\,\mathrm{N}$. The component is negative, so the resultant points opposite to the positive direction of the axis. Since the positive direction is east, the resultant points **west**.
