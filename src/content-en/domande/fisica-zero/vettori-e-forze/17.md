A displacement has components $(-3,2)\,\mathrm{m}$. You draw its tail at $(5,4)\,\mathrm{m}$. Where must its tip be?

- $(2,6)\,\mathrm{m}$
- $(8,2)\,\mathrm{m}$
- $(5,-1)\,\mathrm{m}$
- $(-3,2)\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Start at the tail and add the components to the corresponding coordinates. The tip is at $(5-3,4+2)=(2,6)\,\mathrm{m}$.
