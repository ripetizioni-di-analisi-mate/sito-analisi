A spring with spring constant $k=200$ N/m exerts a force of magnitude $8$ N. What is the extension?

- $0.04\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1600\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The rearranged formula is $\Delta\ell=\dfrac{F}{k}$. Substituting the values gives $\dfrac{8}{200}$. The extension is therefore $0.04$ meters.
