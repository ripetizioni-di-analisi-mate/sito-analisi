A replacement spring for a garden tool exerts a spring force of $3\,\mathrm{N}$ when stretched by $2\,\mathrm{cm}$. Hooke's law holds for extensions up to $10\,\mathrm{cm}$. What spring force does the spring exert when stretched by $5\,\mathrm{cm}$?

- $6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{N}$
- $15\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

By Hooke's law, the spring constant is
$$
k=\frac{3\,\mathrm{N}}{0.02\,\mathrm{m}}=150\,\mathrm{N/m}.
$$
An extension of $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ lies within the range of validity, so $k$ stays the same. Therefore $F_e=150\,\mathrm{N/m}\cdot0.05\,\mathrm{m}=7.5\,\mathrm{N}$.
