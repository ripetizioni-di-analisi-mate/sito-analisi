Two sensors aligned with different diagonals are calibrated to give the combinations $F_x+F_y=10\,\mathrm{N}$ and $F_x-F_y=4\,\mathrm{N}$. What reconstructed vertical component do they give?

- $14\,\mathrm{N}$
- $3\,\mathrm{N}$
- $6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $7\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Subtract the second equation from the first to eliminate $F_x$. This gives $2F_y=10-4=6\,\mathrm{N}$, hence $F_y=3\,\mathrm{N}$.
