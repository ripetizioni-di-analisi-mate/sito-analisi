A crane lifts a load $9\,\mathrm{m}$ vertically and then moves it $1200\,\mathrm{cm}$ horizontally. What is the magnitude of the overall displacement of the load?

- $21\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1200\,\mathrm{m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Since $1\,\mathrm{m}=100\,\mathrm{cm}$, the horizontal displacement is $12\,\mathrm{m}$. The vertical and horizontal displacements are perpendicular, so by the Pythagorean theorem
$$
R=\sqrt{(9\,\mathrm{m})^2+(12\,\mathrm{m})^2}=\sqrt{225}\,\mathrm{m}=15\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
Combining $9$ and $1200$ without converting the units would give a meaningless value.
