Two symmetric cables each pull with magnitude $T$. Their horizontal components cancel and each cable has upward component $0.6T$. Which $T$ gives a vertical resultant of $24\,\mathrm{N}$?

- $14.4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$
- $40\,\mathrm{N}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The two relevant components add to $0.6T+0.6T=1.2T$. Setting $1.2T=24\,\mathrm{N}$ gives $T=20\,\mathrm{N}$.
