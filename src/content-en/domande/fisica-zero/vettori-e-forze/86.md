A rigid plate translates without rotating on two vertical springs of stiffness $k$ and $3k$. Their additional compressions are equal. What fraction of the added load does the stiffer spring support?

- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

With the same extra compression, the added forces are in the ratio one to three. The stiffer spring supplies three of the four parts of the total.
