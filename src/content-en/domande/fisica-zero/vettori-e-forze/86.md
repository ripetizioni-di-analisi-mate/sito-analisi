The spring of a folding clothes drying rack has a natural length of $15\,\mathrm{cm}$ and is stretched to $18\,\mathrm{cm}$. Its spring constant is $300\,\mathrm{N/m}$ and Hooke's law holds. What is the magnitude of the spring force?

- $9\,\mathrm{N}$
- $54\,\mathrm{N}$
- $900\,\mathrm{N}$
- $45\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The extension is $\Delta\ell=18\,\mathrm{cm}-15\,\mathrm{cm}=3\,\mathrm{cm}$, that is, $0.03\,\mathrm{m}$. By Hooke's law,
$$
F_e=k\,\Delta\ell=300\,\mathrm{N/m}\cdot0.03\,\mathrm{m}=9\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
The spring force points so as to bring the spring back to its length of $15\,\mathrm{cm}$.
