A body weighs $30$ N at a location where $g=10$ meters per second squared. What is its mass?

- $300\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $3\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $30\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.3\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Rearranging $P=mg$ gives $m=\dfrac{P}{g}$. Substituting the values gives $\dfrac{30}{10}$. The mass is therefore $3$ kilograms.
