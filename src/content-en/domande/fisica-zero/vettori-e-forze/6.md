A point experiences $7\,\mathrm{N}$ rightward and $24\,\mathrm{N}$ upward. With which positive or negative coordinate direction does the resultant make the smallest angle?

- Upward vertical.
- Leftward horizontal.
- Rightward horizontal.
- Downward vertical.

## Explanation

Both components are positive, so the arrow points up and right. Its vertical component exceeds the horizontal one, placing the resultant closer to the vertical.
