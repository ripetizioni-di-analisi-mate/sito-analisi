A dog pulls on its leash. Which of the following values *cannot* be the magnitude of the force the dog exerts on the leash?

- $20\,\mathrm{N}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $150\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The **magnitude** of a vector is its nonnegative value, expressed with its unit. A negative number such as $-20\,\mathrm{N}$ can be the component of a force along an axis, when the force points in the negative direction. As a magnitude, however, $-20\,\mathrm{N}$ is not allowed. The other three values are positive, so they can be magnitudes of a force.
