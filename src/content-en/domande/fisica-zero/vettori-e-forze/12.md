A spring of constant $120\,\mathrm{N/m}$ stretches from its $14\,\mathrm{cm}$ natural length to $19\,\mathrm{cm}$. In the Hooke regime, what is the magnitude of its elastic force?

- $600\,\mathrm{N}$
- $22.8\,\mathrm{N}$
- $16.8\,\mathrm{N}$
- $6\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The extension is $19-14=5\,\mathrm{cm}$, or $0.05\,\mathrm{m}$. The force magnitude is $120\cdot 0.05=6\,\mathrm{N}$.
