Two perpendicular forces have magnitudes of $12$ N and $16$ N. What is the magnitude of the resultant?

- $28\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $100\,\mathrm{N}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The components are perpendicular, so the Pythagorean theorem applies. This gives $F=\sqrt{(12)^2+(16)^2}$. The magnitude is $20$ newtons.
