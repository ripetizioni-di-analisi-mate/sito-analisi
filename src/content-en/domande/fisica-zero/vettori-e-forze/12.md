An elevator goes down from the fourth floor to the first floor, with a displacement of $9\,\mathrm{m}$. A vertical axis is chosen with its positive direction upward. What is the component of the displacement along this axis?

- $9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The displacement has magnitude $9\,\mathrm{m}$ and points down. The positive direction of the axis is up, so the displacement points opposite to the positive direction. Therefore its **component** is negative and equals $-9\,\mathrm{m}$.
