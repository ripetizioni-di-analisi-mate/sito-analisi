A skate rests on a smooth slope that rises to the right. In which direction does the normal force of the slope on the skate act?

- Parallel to the slope, downhill.
- Perpendicular to the surface, upward and leftward.
- Perpendicular to the slope, downward and rightward.
- Vertically upward.

## Explanation

The smooth surface pushes the skate along its outward perpendicular. For the stated slope that direction points upward and leftward.
