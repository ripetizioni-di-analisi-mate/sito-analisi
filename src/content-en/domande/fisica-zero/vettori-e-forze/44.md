A warehouse robot makes three successive displacements, with components in meters $(2,\,0)$, $(0,\,3)$, and $(-5,\,1)$. What is the magnitude of the overall displacement?

- $11\,\mathrm{m}$
- $7\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $25\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Adding the components along each axis gives $R_x=(2+0-5)\,\mathrm{m}=-3\,\mathrm{m}$ and $R_y=(0+3+1)\,\mathrm{m}=4\,\mathrm{m}$. The magnitude of the sum is
$$
R=\sqrt{(-3)^2+4^2}\,\mathrm{m}=5\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
The robot therefore ends $3\,\mathrm{m}$ in the negative direction of the $x$-axis and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ in the positive direction of the $y$-axis from its starting point.
