A spring with spring constant $k=200$ N/m exerts a force of magnitude $14$ N. What is the extension?

- $2800\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.7\,\mathrm{m}$
- $7\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.07\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The rearranged formula is $\Delta\ell=\dfrac{F}{k}$. Substituting the values gives $\dfrac{14}{200}$. The extension is therefore $0.07$ meters.
