You have drawn the arrow for displacement A. After adding B using the head-to-tail rule, which two points do you join to draw the resultant?

::grafico{vettori="0,0,3,1,A;3,1,4,3,B" xmin=-1 xmax=5 ymin=-1 ymax=4 uguale=1 nomi="x,y" legenda=0 titolo="Two displacements drawn in succession."}

- The head of A to the head of B, in that order.
- The head of B to the tail of A, in that order.
- The tail of B to the tail of A, in that order.
- The tail of A to the head of B, in that order.

## Explanation

Place B's tail at A's head. The total arrow runs from the start of the first displacement to the end of the last.
