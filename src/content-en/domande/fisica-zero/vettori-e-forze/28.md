Two forces of $6$ N and $4$ N act along the same line and point the same way. What is the magnitude of the resultant?

- $2\,\mathrm{N}$
- $24\,\mathrm{N}$
- $14\,\mathrm{N}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The two forces point the same way, so their magnitudes add. The resultant points the same way as well. Its magnitude is $6+4=10$ newtons.
