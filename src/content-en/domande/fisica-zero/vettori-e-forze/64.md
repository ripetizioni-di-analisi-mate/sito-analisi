A force of $1.8\,\mathrm{N}$ produces a $12\,\mathrm{mm}$ extension in a linear spring. What is the spring constant in newtons per metre?

- $15\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $216\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $150\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $0.15\,\mathrm{N/m}$

## Explanation

Convert the deformation to metres, $12\,\mathrm{mm}=0.012\,\mathrm{m}$. Force divided by extension is $\dfrac{1.8}{0.012}=150\,\mathrm{N/m}$.
