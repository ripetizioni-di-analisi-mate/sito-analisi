An apple is near Earth's surface. Which description of its weight is correct?

- It is a force exerted by Earth on the apple, directed vertically downward
- It is a force exerted by the apple on Earth, directed vertically downward
- It is a force exerted by Earth on the apple, directed vertically upward
- It is a property of the apple measured in kilograms

## Explanation

**Weight** is the gravitational force that Earth exerts on a body, so it acts on the apple and is exerted by Earth. Near Earth's surface it is directed vertically downward. The property of the apple measured in kilograms is its mass, which is a different quantity from its weight.
