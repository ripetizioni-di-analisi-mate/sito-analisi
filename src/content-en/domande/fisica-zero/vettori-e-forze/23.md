On a grid with equal scales, which vector results from rotating $(2,1)$ a quarter-turn counterclockwise?

::grafico{vettori="0,0,2,1,A" xmin=-3 xmax=3 ymin=-3 ymax=3 uguale=1 nomi="x,y" legenda=0 titolo="Initial arrow before the quarter turn."}

- $(1,2)$
- $(-2,-1)$
- $(1,-2)$
- $(-1,2)$

## Explanation

Rightward turns upward, and upward turns left. The two rightward units become two upward units, while the upward unit becomes one leftward unit.
