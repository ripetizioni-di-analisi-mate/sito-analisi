An ant walks along the straight edge of a table: it moves forward $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ and then back $30\,\mathrm{mm}$. What is the magnitude of its overall displacement?

- $15\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $18\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $42\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $9\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Components can be added only when they are expressed in the same unit. Since $1\,\mathrm{cm}=10\,\mathrm{mm}$, the backward move of $30\,\mathrm{mm}$ corresponds to $3\,\mathrm{cm}$. Taking forward as positive, the components are $+12\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $-3\,\mathrm{cm}$, whose sum is $+9\,\mathrm{cm}$. Therefore the overall displacement has magnitude $9\,\mathrm{cm}$ and points forward.
