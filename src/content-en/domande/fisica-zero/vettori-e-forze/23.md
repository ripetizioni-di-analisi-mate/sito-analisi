A mass of $5$ kg is at a location where $g=10$ meters per second squared. What is its weight?

- $5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $50\,\mathrm{N}$
- $500\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The weight is given by $P=mg$. Multiply the mass of $5$ kg by the acceleration of $10$ meters per second squared. Therefore $P=50$ newtons.
