The same package is weighed with a very precise force gauge, first at one of the poles, where $g\approx9.83\,\mathrm{N/kg}$, and then at the equator, where $g\approx9.78\,\mathrm{N/kg}$. Which statement is correct?

- The weight is greater at the equator, and the mass is the same in both places
- The weight is the same in both places, and the mass is greater at the pole
- The weight is greater at the pole, and the mass is the same in both places
- Weight and mass are the same in both places

## Explanation

Mass is a property of the package, so it is the same at the pole and at the equator. The weight has magnitude $P=mg$, so for the same mass it is greater where $g$ is greater. Since $9.83>9.78$, the package weighs more at the pole, even though the difference is small.
