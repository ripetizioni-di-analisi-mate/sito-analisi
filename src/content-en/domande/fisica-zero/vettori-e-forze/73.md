Two springs have equal $k$ and natural lengths of $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $20\,\mathrm{cm}$. Each is extended by $10\,\mathrm{\%}$ of its own length. What is the ratio of the force on the second to that on the first?

- $1$
- $0.5$
- $4$
- $2$

## Explanation

The absolute extensions are $1\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $2\,\mathrm{cm}$. Because $k$ is equal, the force ratio equals the ratio of extensions, namely two.
