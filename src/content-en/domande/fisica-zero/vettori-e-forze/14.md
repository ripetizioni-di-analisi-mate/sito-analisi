A mass of $4$ kg is at a location where $g=10$ meters per second squared. What is its weight?

- $4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $40\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $400\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The weight is given by $P=mg$. Multiply the mass of $4$ kg by the acceleration of $10$ meters per second squared. Therefore $P=40$ newtons.
