Two people push a broken-down car along a straight road, both pushing the same way. One pushes with $250\,\mathrm{N}$ and the other with $300\,\mathrm{N}$. What is the magnitude of the resultant of the two pushes?

- $550\,\mathrm{N}$
- $50\,\mathrm{N}$
- $275\,\mathrm{N}$
- $390\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The two forces are collinear and point the same way. If the positive direction of the axis is the direction of the pushes, the components are $+250\,\mathrm{N}$ and $+300\,\mathrm{N}$. The component of the resultant is their sum, $550\,\mathrm{N}$, so the **resultant** has magnitude $550\,\mathrm{N}$ and points the same way as the pushes.
