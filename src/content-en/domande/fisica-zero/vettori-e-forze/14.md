Under the same $4\,\mathrm{N}$ force, spring A extends $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ and B extends $5\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is $\dfrac{k_A}{k_B}$ in the linear regime?

- $1$
- $10$
- $2.5$
- $0.4$

## Explanation

At equal load, $k=\dfrac{F}{\Delta l}$ is inversely proportional to extension. The ratio is $\dfrac{k_A}{k_B}=\dfrac{5}{2}=2.5$.
