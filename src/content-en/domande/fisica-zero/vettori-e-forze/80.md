A toy spider hangs from the ceiling on a spring with a natural length of $20\,\mathrm{cm}$ and a spring constant of $50\,\mathrm{N/m}$. The spring is stretched and exerts a spring force of $4\,\mathrm{N}$. Assuming Hooke's law holds, how long is the spring?

- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $28\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $20.08\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

By Hooke's law, the extension is
$$
\Delta\ell=\frac{F_e}{k}=\frac{4\,\mathrm{N}}{50\,\mathrm{N/m}}=0.08\,\mathrm{m}=8\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
The spring is stretched, so its length exceeds its natural length. Therefore its length is $\ell=\ell_0+\Delta\ell=20\,\mathrm{cm}+8\,\mathrm{cm}=28\,\mathrm{cm}$.
