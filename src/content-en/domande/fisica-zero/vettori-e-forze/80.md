Two identical springs of natural length $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ connect a ring to walls $30\,\mathrm{cm}$ apart. The ring slides along the line between them, both springs are taut, and there are no other horizontal forces. How far from the left wall does it balance?

- $20\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $15\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $10\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The opposing tensions must have equal magnitudes, and the springs have the same stiffness. Equal extensions are required, so the ring must divide the thirty centimetres equally.
