A gust moves a kite $5\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left and up, in a vertical plane. With the $x$-axis pointing right and the $y$-axis pointing up, which pair of components can describe this displacement?

- $(3\,\mathrm{m},\,-4\,\mathrm{m})$
- $(-3\,\mathrm{m},\,4\,\mathrm{m})$
- $(-3\,\mathrm{m},\,-4\,\mathrm{m})$
- $(-1\,\mathrm{m},\,4\,\mathrm{m})$

## Explanation

A displacement to the left has $A_x<0$ and a displacement upward has $A_y>0$, which leaves the pairs $(-3,\,4)$ and $(-1,\,4)$. For the first the magnitude is $\sqrt{9+16}\,\mathrm{m}=5\,\mathrm{m}$, while for the second it is $\sqrt{17}\,\mathrm{m}\approx4.1\,\mathrm{m}$. Therefore only $(-3\,\mathrm{m},\,4\,\mathrm{m})$ describes the displacement.
