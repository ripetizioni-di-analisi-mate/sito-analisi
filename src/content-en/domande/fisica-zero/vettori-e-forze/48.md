One arrow in a drawing represents $3\,\mathrm{N}$ and another $4\,\mathrm{m}$. Can they be added head to tail to obtain one physical resultant?

- Yes, if the arrows have equal lengths on the page.
- Yes, if they are made parallel before addition.
- No, they represent quantities with different dimensions.
- Yes, the resultant always has magnitude five.

## Explanation

Physical vector addition requires quantities of the same kind. A force cannot be added to a displacement, whatever scales are used to draw them.
