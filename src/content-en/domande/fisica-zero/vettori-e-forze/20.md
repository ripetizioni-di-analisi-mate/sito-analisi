A swimmer swims one length of a $25\,\mathrm{m}$ pool and then returns to the wall where he started. What is the magnitude of his overall displacement?

- $50\,\mathrm{m}$
- $25\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-50\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

With the axis pointing in the direction of the first length, the outbound displacement has component $+25\,\mathrm{m}$ and the return displacement has component $-25\,\mathrm{m}$. The sum of the components is $0\,\mathrm{m}$, so the overall displacement is **zero**. The value $50\,\mathrm{m}$ is the distance traveled, which measures the length of the path.
