Two forces point in opposite directions and have magnitudes of $15$ N and $5$ N. What is the magnitude of the resultant?

- $20\,\mathrm{N}$
- $75\,\mathrm{N}$
- $5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

For collinear forces pointing in opposite directions, subtract the smaller magnitude from the larger one. The resultant points in the direction of the larger force. Its magnitude is $15-5=10$ newtons.
