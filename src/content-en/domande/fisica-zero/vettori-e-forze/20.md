A taut cable pulls a hook along the unit direction vector $(-0.8,0.6)$. Two sensors measure the horizontal and vertical components of the cable force alone. Which pair of readings is consistent with the cable?

- $(-8,6)\,\mathrm{N}$
- $(8,-6)\,\mathrm{N}$
- $(-6,8)\,\mathrm{N}$
- $(-8,-6)\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The force must be a positive multiple of the unit vector. Opposite component signs alone do not suffice. Multiplying both components by $10\,\mathrm{N}$ gives $(-8,6)\,\mathrm{N}$. Swapping their magnitudes changes the slope, and a negative multiplier reverses the direction.
