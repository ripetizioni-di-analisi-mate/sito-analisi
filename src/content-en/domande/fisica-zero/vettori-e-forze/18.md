Vector $\vec a$ has components $(2,-5)\,\mathrm{m}$. What are the components of $-3\vec a$?

- $(-1,-8)\,\mathrm{m}$
- $(-6,-15)\,\mathrm{m}$
- $(-6,15)\,\mathrm{m}$
- $(6,-15)\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Multiply each component by negative three. The magnitude triples and the direction reverses, giving $(-6,15)\,\mathrm{m}$.
