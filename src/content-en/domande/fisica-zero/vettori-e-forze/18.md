A crate on the floor is pulled by two ropes lying along the same line, with forces of $6\,\mathrm{N}$ and $8\,\mathrm{N}$. What are the possible magnitudes of the resultant of the two forces?

- $14\,\mathrm{N}$ or $2\,\mathrm{N}$
- Only $14\,\mathrm{N}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N}$ or $14\,\mathrm{N}$
- Only $2\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The forces are collinear, so they can point the same way or opposite ways. If they point the same way, the components are $+6\,\mathrm{N}$ and $+8\,\mathrm{N}$, and the resultant has magnitude $14\,\mathrm{N}$. If they point opposite ways, the components are $+6\,\mathrm{N}$ and $-8\,\mathrm{N}$, their sum is $-2\,\mathrm{N}$, and the resultant has magnitude $2\,\mathrm{N}$. The value $10\,\mathrm{N}$ corresponds to two perpendicular forces, which are not collinear.
