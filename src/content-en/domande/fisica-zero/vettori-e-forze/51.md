An apple hangs from a branch. Which of the following forces acts on the apple without any contact between the two interacting bodies?

- The force of the stem holding it
- Its weight, exerted by Earth
- The push of the hand picking it
- The force of the moving air striking it

## Explanation

Earth exerts a gravitational force on the apple, its **weight**, even though the two bodies do not touch. The stem, the hand, and the air are in contact with the apple, so their forces are contact forces. Therefore weight is the only force in the list that acts at a distance.
