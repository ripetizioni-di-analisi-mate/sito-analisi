A has mass $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ where $g=6\,\mathrm{N/kg}$. B has mass $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ where $g=10\,\mathrm{N/kg}$. By how many newtons does B's weight exceed A's?

- $30\,\mathrm{N}$
- $24\,\mathrm{N}$
- $6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Calculate the weights separately, $P_A=4\cdot 6=24\,\mathrm{N}$ and $P_B=3\cdot 10=30\,\mathrm{N}$. Their difference is $30-24=6\,\mathrm{N}$.
