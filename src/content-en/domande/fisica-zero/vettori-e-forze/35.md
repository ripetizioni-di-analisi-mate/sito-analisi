A spring with spring constant $k=200$ N/m exerts a force of magnitude $12$ N. What is the extension?

- $2400\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.06\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The rearranged formula is $\Delta\ell=\dfrac{F}{k}$. Substituting the values gives $\dfrac{12}{200}$. The extension is therefore $0.06$ meters.
