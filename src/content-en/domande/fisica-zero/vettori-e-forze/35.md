An existing push is $(3,1)\,\mathrm{N}$. A second actuator must bring the resultant to $(2,5)\,\mathrm{N}$. What force must it supply?

- $(-3,-1)\,\mathrm{N}$
- $(-1,4)\,\mathrm{N}$
- $(5,6)\,\mathrm{N}$
- $(1,-4)\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Subtract the existing force from the desired one. The missing contribution is $(2-3,5-1)=(-1,4)\,\mathrm{N}$.
