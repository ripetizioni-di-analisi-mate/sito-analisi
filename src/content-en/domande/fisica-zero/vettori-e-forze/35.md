A ball is kicked twice in a row across a grass field. In which case is the magnitude of the overall displacement given by $R=\sqrt{A^2+B^2}$?

- $5\,\mathrm{m}$ north and then $2\,\mathrm{m}$ south
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$ north and then $2\,\mathrm{m}$ northeast
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$ north and then $2\,\mathrm{m}$ north
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$ north and then $2\,\mathrm{m}$ west

## Explanation

The relation $R=\sqrt{A^2+B^2}$ comes from the Pythagorean theorem and holds when the two vectors are **perpendicular**. North and west form a right angle, so in that case the relation applies and gives $R=\sqrt{29}\,\mathrm{m}$. Displacements north and south, or twice north, are collinear, and the northeast direction makes a $45^\circ$ angle with north. Therefore the relation does not apply in the other cases.
