A robot must finish $7\,\mathrm{m}$ north of its start after two straight sections totalling $7\,\mathrm{m}$. Which route is possible?

- Equal sections northeast and northwest.
- Both sections point north.
- North first and then east, with positive lengths.
- South first and then north, with positive lengths.

## Explanation

The required straight-line distance already equals the whole available length. Any deviation reduces the total northward projection and prevents the robot from reaching the target.
