A body weighs $60$ N at a location where $g=10$ meters per second squared. What is its mass?

- $6\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $600\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $60\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.6\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Rearranging $P=mg$ gives $m=\dfrac{P}{g}$. Substituting the values gives $\dfrac{60}{10}$. The mass is therefore $6$ kilograms.
