Three people move a couch along a straight hallway, each exerting a force parallel to the hallway. In which case is the resultant of the three forces zero?

- $50\,\mathrm{N}$ forward, $20\,\mathrm{N}$ forward, $30\,\mathrm{N}$ backward
- $50\,\mathrm{N}$ forward, $20\,\mathrm{N}$ backward, $30\,\mathrm{N}$ backward
- $50\,\mathrm{N}$ forward, $20\,\mathrm{N}$ backward, $30\,\mathrm{N}$ forward
- $50\,\mathrm{N}$ backward, $20\,\mathrm{N}$ backward, $30\,\mathrm{N}$ backward

## Explanation

Take forward as the positive direction. The forces of $50\,\mathrm{N}$ forward, $20\,\mathrm{N}$ backward, and $30\,\mathrm{N}$ backward have components $+50\,\mathrm{N}$, $-20\,\mathrm{N}$, and $-30\,\mathrm{N}$, whose sum is $0\,\mathrm{N}$, so their resultant is **zero**. For the other sets the sum of the components is $+40\,\mathrm{N}$, $+60\,\mathrm{N}$, and $-100\,\mathrm{N}$, so the resultant is not zero.
