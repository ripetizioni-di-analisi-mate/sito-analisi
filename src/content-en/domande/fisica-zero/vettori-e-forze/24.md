A $10\,\mathrm{N}$ force makes an angle of $60^{\circ}$ above the positive horizontal. Given $\cos 60^{\circ}=0.5$, what is the horizontal component?

- $5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N}$
- $8.66\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The component adjacent to the angle is the magnitude times the cosine. This gives $10\cdot 0.5=5\,\mathrm{N}$ with a positive sign.
