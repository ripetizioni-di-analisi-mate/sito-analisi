A body weighs $50$ N at a location where $g=10$ meters per second squared. What is its mass?

- $500\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $50\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $5\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Rearranging $P=mg$ gives $m=\dfrac{P}{g}$. Substituting the values gives $\dfrac{50}{10}$. The mass is therefore $5$ kilograms.
