A tourist walks $120\,\mathrm{m}$ east along a street and then $50\,\mathrm{m}$ north along a side street. What is the magnitude of her overall displacement?

- $130\,\mathrm{m}$
- $170\,\mathrm{m}$
- $70\,\mathrm{m}$
- $85\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

East and north form a right angle, so the two displacements are perpendicular. The tip-to-tail rule produces a right triangle, and the overall displacement is its hypotenuse. By the **Pythagorean theorem**,
$$
R=\sqrt{(120\,\mathrm{m})^2+(50\,\mathrm{m})^2}=\sqrt{16\,900}\,\mathrm{m}=130\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
