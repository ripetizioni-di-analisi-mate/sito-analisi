A spring with spring constant $k=500$ N/m is stretched by $0.02$ m. What is the magnitude of the spring force?

- $10\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.1\,\mathrm{N}$
- $500\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Hooke's law gives the magnitude $F=k\Delta\ell$. The extension is expressed in meters. Therefore $F=500\cdot0.02=10$ newtons.
