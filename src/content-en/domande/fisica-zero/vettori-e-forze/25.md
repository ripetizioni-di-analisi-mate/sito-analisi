A force has magnitude $13\,\mathrm{N}$ and horizontal component $5\,\mathrm{N}$. It points downward. What is the vertical component, with upward positive?

- $12\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-18\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-8\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The vertical magnitude is $\sqrt{13^2-5^2}=12\,\mathrm{N}$. Because the vector points down, its signed component is $-12\,\mathrm{N}$.
