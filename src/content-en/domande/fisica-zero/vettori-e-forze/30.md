Two forces of $5\,\mathrm{N}$ each act on the same ball. Three configurations are compared: forces with the same direction and the same sense (I), perpendicular forces (II), and forces with the same direction and opposite senses (III). Which ordering of the magnitudes of the resultant is correct?

- $R_{\mathrm{II}}>R_{\mathrm{I}}>R_{\mathrm{III}}$
- $R_{\mathrm{I}}=R_{\mathrm{II}}>R_{\mathrm{III}}$
- $R_{\mathrm{I}}>R_{\mathrm{II}}>R_{\mathrm{III}}$
- $R_{\mathrm{I}}>R_{\mathrm{III}}>R_{\mathrm{II}}$

## Explanation

When the forces point the same way their components add, so $R_{\mathrm{I}}=10\,\mathrm{N}$. For perpendicular forces the Pythagorean theorem applies, and $R_{\mathrm{II}}=\sqrt{25+25}\,\mathrm{N}=\sqrt{50}\,\mathrm{N}\approx7.1\,\mathrm{N}$. When they point opposite ways the components are $+5\,\mathrm{N}$ and $-5\,\mathrm{N}$, so $R_{\mathrm{III}}=0\,\mathrm{N}$. The correct ordering is therefore $R_{\mathrm{I}}>R_{\mathrm{II}}>R_{\mathrm{III}}$.
