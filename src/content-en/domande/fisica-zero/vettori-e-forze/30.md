A courier travels $3\,\mathrm{km}$ east and then $2\,\mathrm{km}$ north. Another starts at the same point and takes the same two displacements in reverse order. What coincides?

- Neither point, because the order is reversed.
- Both points, because the paths are identical.
- The turning point coincides, while the endpoint differs.
- The endpoint coincides, while the turning point differs.

## Explanation

Both finish three kilometres east and two north of the start. The first reaches the eastern side first, while the second reaches the northern side first.
