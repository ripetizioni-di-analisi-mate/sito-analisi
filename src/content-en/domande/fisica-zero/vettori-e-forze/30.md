Two perpendicular forces have magnitudes of $18$ N and $24$ N. What is the magnitude of the resultant?

- $42\,\mathrm{N}$
- $30\,\mathrm{N}$
- $6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $150\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The components are perpendicular, so the Pythagorean theorem applies. This gives $F=\sqrt{(18)^2+(24)^2}$. The magnitude is $30$ newtons.
