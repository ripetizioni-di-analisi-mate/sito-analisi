If two legs have equal distances and different speeds, which contributes more time to the trip?

- The one with higher speed
- They contribute equal time
- The one travelled last
- The one with lower speed

## Explanation

For fixed distance, duration is inversely proportional to speed. This is why the average speed is closer to the lower value.

For two $60\,\mathrm m$ legs at $3$ and $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$, the times are $20$ and $10\,\mathrm s$. The lower speed is held for twice as long.
