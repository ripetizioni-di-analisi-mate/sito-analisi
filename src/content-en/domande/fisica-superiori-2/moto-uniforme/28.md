A person covers $42\,\mathrm m$ in $14\,\mathrm s$, stops, and then continues in the same direction for $21\,\mathrm m$ in another $14\,\mathrm s$. The average speed for the whole journey is $1.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. How long is the stop?

- $14\,\mathrm s$
- $21\,\mathrm s$
- $28\,\mathrm s$
- $7\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The two legs total $63\,\mathrm m$. At the given average speed, the complete duration is $\dfrac{63}{1.8}\,\mathrm s=35\,\mathrm s$. The moving legs use $14+14=28\,\mathrm s$. The stop lasts the difference, $35-28=7\,\mathrm s$, because it adds time to the average without adding distance.
