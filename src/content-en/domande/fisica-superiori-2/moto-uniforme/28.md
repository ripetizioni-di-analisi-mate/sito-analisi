A pedestrian covers $42\,\mathrm{m}$ in $14\,\mathrm{s}$, stops for $7\,\mathrm{s}$ and continues in the same direction for $21\,\mathrm{m}$ in $14\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the mean velocity, taking the direction of travel as positive?

- $2.25\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The stop counts towards the total elapsed time. Motion is in the same direction throughout, so displacement is 63 m.

$$
v_m=\frac{42+21}{14+7+14}\,\mathrm{m/s}=1.8\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
