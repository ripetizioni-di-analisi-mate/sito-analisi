A table gives velocities in m/s and durations in minutes. What is needed before calculating displacements in metres?

- Divide all velocities by 60
- Convert the durations to seconds
- Multiply both time and velocity by 60
- Convert each duration to hours and use it directly

## Explanation

In the product of velocity and duration, the time units in the numerator and denominator must agree. Seconds then cancel, leaving metres.

For example, maintaining $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for $2\,\mathrm{min}$ gives $4\cdot120=480\,\mathrm m$. Multiplying by the number two directly would count only two seconds.
