Two objects $220\,\mathrm{m}$ apart set off towards each other at speeds $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$. How long until they meet? Choose the closest value.

- $14.667\,\mathrm{s}$
- $27.5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $31.429\,\mathrm{s}$
- $29.333\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The first object closes the gap by $8\,\mathrm{m}$ per second and the second closes it by a further $7\,\mathrm{m}$ per second. Add the speeds because the objects move towards each other. At the calculated time the gap is zero.

$$
\begin{aligned}
t_I &= \frac{220}{8+7}\,\mathrm{s} \simeq 14.667\,\mathrm{s}
\end{aligned}
$$
