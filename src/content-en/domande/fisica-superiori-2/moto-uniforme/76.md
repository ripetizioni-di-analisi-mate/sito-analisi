A graph shows a slanting straight line. To determine whether motion is uniform, what information is needed first?

- The colour of the line
- The area on the screen below the line
- The quantities plotted on the axes
- The number of points drawn on the line

## Explanation

A slanting line on a position–time graph gives constant velocity. On a velocity–time graph it gives changing velocity.
