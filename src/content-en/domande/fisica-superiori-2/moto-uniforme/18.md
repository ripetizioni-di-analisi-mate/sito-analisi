A belt moving at $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ carries a box to $x=9\,\mathrm{m}$ after $4\,\mathrm{s}$. Where did the box start?

- $9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $17\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Work backwards by subtracting the displacement from the final coordinate.

$$
x_0=9-2\cdot4=1\,\mathrm{m}.
$$

Each of the four seconds adds $2\,\mathrm m$, so the motion covers $8\,\mathrm m$. The starting coordinate is $9-8=1\,\mathrm m$. Moving another $8\,\mathrm m$ from that coordinate recovers the final reading.
