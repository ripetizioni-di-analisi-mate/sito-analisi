A vehicle maintains a speed of $108\,\mathrm{km/h}$. Express this value in $\mathrm{m/s}$.

- $108\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $388.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Convert the kilometre in the numerator to 1000 metres and the hour in the denominator to 3600 seconds. The ratio of the conversion factors is $\dfrac{1000}{3600}$, so the numerical value decreases. Travelling for one hour at the converted speed would reproduce the distance in kilometres given by the initial speed.

$$
\begin{aligned}
u &= 108\,\frac{\mathrm{km}}{\mathrm{h}} = \frac{108\cdot1000}{3600}\,\mathrm{m/s} \\[6pt]
&= 30\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
