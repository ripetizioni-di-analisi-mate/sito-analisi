Which two values are always sufficient to determine the average velocity over an interval?

- Distance and duration
- First position and final velocity
- Signed displacement and duration
- Final position and starting time

## Explanation

The required quantity is a ratio: signed displacement in the numerator and positive duration in the denominator.

For example, a signed displacement of $-12\,\mathrm m$ in $4\,\mathrm s$ gives $-3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. An unsigned distance of $12\,\mathrm m$ would not distinguish motion in the positive direction from motion in the opposite direction.
