Consider data recorded at the endpoints of an interval. Which pair always determines the average velocity?

- Distance and duration
- First position and final velocity
- Signed displacement and duration
- Final position and starting time

## Explanation

The required quantity is a ratio: signed displacement in the numerator and positive duration in the denominator.
