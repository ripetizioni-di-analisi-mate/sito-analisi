Two robots move at $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the same direction. The faster one starts behind and catches up in $6\,\mathrm{s}$. What was the initial gap?

- $30\,\mathrm{m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m}$
- $42\,\mathrm{m}$
- $54\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Because both move in the same direction, the second gains 5 m each second for 6 s.

$$
d=(7-2)\cdot6=30\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
