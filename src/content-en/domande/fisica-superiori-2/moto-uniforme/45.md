Initially A is at $x=0$ and B at $x=20\,\mathrm{m}$. A moves towards B at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and B remains at rest. How long does A need to reach B?

- $5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $16\,\mathrm{s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

B stays at coordinate 20 m. Set the expression for A equal to that constant coordinate.

$$
4t=20\quad\Longrightarrow\quad t=5\,\mathrm{s}.
$$

Thus $4t=20$ and $t=5\,\mathrm s$. B's position graph is horizontal. Where it crosses A's rising line, the graph fixes both the time and their common position.
