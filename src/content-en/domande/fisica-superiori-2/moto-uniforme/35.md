A trolley is at $x=2\,\mathrm m$ at 9:00 and at $x=14\,\mathrm m$ one minute later. It travelled along the line and may have paused or reversed direction. What is the shortest total distance consistent with the data?

- $16\,\mathrm m$
- $0\,\mathrm m$
- $12\,\mathrm m$
- $6\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Going from $2$ to $14$ metres requires travelling at least the difference between the coordinates. Moving in one direction attains the $12$-metre minimum. Any reversal adds distance.
