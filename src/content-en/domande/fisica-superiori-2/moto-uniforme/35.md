Two equal legs are travelled at $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and at an unknown speed. The average speed is $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the speed on the second leg?

- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

For equal distances, add the reciprocals of the speeds to determine time in terms of one leg’s length. Invert the remaining reciprocal to find the second speed.

$$
\frac{1}{u_2}=\frac{2}{4}-\frac{1}{6}=\frac13\,\mathrm{s/m}.
$$
