An object travels at a positive speed on one leg and at a higher speed on a second leg. Both durations are positive. Where does average speed lie?

- Strictly between the two speeds
- Below both speeds
- Above both speeds
- Always equal to the higher speed

## Explanation

The average weights each speed by the time spent in that phase. Both weights are positive and add to the total duration.

An average equal to the lower speed would require the second stage to last no time. Likewise, the positive duration of the first stage keeps the average below the higher speed.
