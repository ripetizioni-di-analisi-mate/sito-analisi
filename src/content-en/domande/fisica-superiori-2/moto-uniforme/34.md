A cyclist covers one stage at $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and then another at $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$, with no stops. Her average speed is $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What fraction of the total time does she spend on the second stage?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{6}{7}$

## Explanation

Let $p$ be the fraction of time at the higher speed. The first stage takes the fraction $1-p$. Weighting the speeds by the time fractions gives $3(1-p)+7p=6$, so $p=\dfrac{3}{4}$. The average being closer to $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$ agrees with the second stage lasting longer.
