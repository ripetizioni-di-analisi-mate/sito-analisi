An athlete travels at $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for $8\,\mathrm{s}$ and at $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for another $8\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the average speed?

- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Each speed lasts for half the time. The two distances are 24 m and 56 m, so divide 80 m by 16 s.

$$
v_m=\frac{3\cdot8+7\cdot8}{8+8}\,\mathrm{m/s}=5\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
