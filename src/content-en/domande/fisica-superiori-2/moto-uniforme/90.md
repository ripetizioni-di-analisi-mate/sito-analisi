Two legs are performed in the opposite order without changing their velocities or durations. What happens to overall average speed?

- It increases when the faster leg comes last
- It is unchanged
- It changes sign
- It always becomes the arithmetic mean

## Explanation

Total distance sums the same leg lengths and total time sums the same durations. Changing the order of addition does not alter the ratio.

The instant when the body changes speed does change. Intermediate positions can differ, but at the end both the accumulated distance and the elapsed time are the same.
