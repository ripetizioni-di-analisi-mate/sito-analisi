A door moves from $x=-3\,\mathrm{m}$ at $6\,\mathrm{s}$ to $x=5\,\mathrm{m}$ at $10\,\mathrm{s}$. What is its average velocity?

- $0.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Duration is the difference between the two clock readings. Displacement is positive because the coordinate increases.

$$
v_m=\frac{5-(-3)}{10-6}\,\mathrm{m/s}=2\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
