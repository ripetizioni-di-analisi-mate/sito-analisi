Two sensors register an exact displacement of $12\,\mathrm m$. The first shows the correct time, $5\,\mathrm s$. The second shows $9\,\mathrm s$, but may have recorded the passage with a delay of between $0$ and $1\,\mathrm s$. What is the greatest compatible average velocity?

- $2.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The second passage can have occurred between $8$ and $9\,\mathrm s$. The shortest duration is therefore $8-5=3\,\mathrm s$. Since the displacement is positive and fixed, the maximum average velocity uses the smallest denominator and is $\dfrac{12}{3}\,\mathrm{m/s}=4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The value $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ corresponds to no delay.
