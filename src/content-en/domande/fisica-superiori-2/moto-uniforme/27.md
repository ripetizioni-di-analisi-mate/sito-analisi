A cyclist rides a straight stretch of $12\,\mathrm m$. The cyclist starts at time $5\,\mathrm s$ and arrives at a time between $8\,\mathrm s$ and $9\,\mathrm s$. What is the greatest average velocity consistent with these data?

- $2.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Average velocity is the displacement divided by the duration of the motion. The displacement is fixed, so the average velocity is greatest when the duration is smallest. The shortest duration corresponds to arrival at $8\,\mathrm s$ and is $8-5=3\,\mathrm s$.

$$
v_{\max}=\frac{12}{3}\,\mathrm{m/s}=4\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$

With arrival at $9\,\mathrm s$ the duration would be $4\,\mathrm s$, giving the smallest average velocity, $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
