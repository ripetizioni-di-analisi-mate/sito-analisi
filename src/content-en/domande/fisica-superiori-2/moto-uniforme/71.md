In uniform motion, position changes from positive to negative. How does velocity change at the crossing?

- It stays constant and negative
- It becomes zero
- It changes from positive to negative
- It changes from negative to positive

## Explanation

Position passes through zero while decreasing. The coefficient of time remains constant.
