A trolley moves along a straight track and may reverse its direction of motion. It starts at $x=4\,\mathrm m$, during the motion it passes through both $x=-3\,\mathrm m$ and $x=9\,\mathrm m$, in either order, and stops at $x=8\,\mathrm m$. What is the smallest distance it can have travelled?

- $4\,\mathrm m$
- $12\,\mathrm m$
- $20\,\mathrm m$
- $28\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The trolley must reach both $x=-3\,\mathrm m$ and $x=9\,\mathrm m$, so it travels at least the $12\,\mathrm m$ between these two points, plus the stretch from the start to the first point reached and the stretch from the second point to the finish. If it reaches $x=-3\,\mathrm m$ first, the smallest distance is $(7+12+1)\,\mathrm m=20\,\mathrm m$. If it reaches $x=9\,\mathrm m$ first, the smallest distance is $(5+12+11)\,\mathrm m=28\,\mathrm m$. The smallest distance travelled is therefore $20\,\mathrm m$. The overall displacement is $(8-4)\,\mathrm m=4\,\mathrm m$ instead, because it depends only on the initial and final positions.
