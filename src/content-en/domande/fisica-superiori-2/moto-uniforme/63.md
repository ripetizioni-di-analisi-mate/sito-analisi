A camera records a robot along a track at $x=4\,\mathrm m$ initially, at $x=-3\,\mathrm m$ during the motion and at $x=5\,\mathrm m$ at the end. The robot may reverse between images. What is the smallest total distance consistent with the three images?

- $1\,\mathrm m$
- $7\,\mathrm m$
- $15\,\mathrm m$
- $22\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Reaching the intermediate position takes at least $|-3-4|\,\mathrm m=7\,\mathrm m$. Between the second and third images at least $|5-(-3)|\,\mathrm m=8\,\mathrm m$ are needed. Their sum is $15\,\mathrm m$, achievable in two legs without further reversals. Comparing only the endpoints gives $1\,\mathrm m$ and misses the intermediate passage.
