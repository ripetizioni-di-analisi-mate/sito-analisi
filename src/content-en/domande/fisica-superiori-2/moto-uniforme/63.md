A student estimates distance travelled from one initial and one final photograph. What information makes this estimate exact?

- The object moved a positive distance in each direction
- The axis is in kilometres instead of metres
- Between the photographs the object moved only in one direction or stayed still
- The clock started at zero

## Explanation

If the direction stays the same, the path length equals the magnitude of the difference between the two coordinates.
