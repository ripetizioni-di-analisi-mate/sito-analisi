A robot moves from $x=-4\,\mathrm{m}$ to $x=7\,\mathrm{m}$ and turns back to $x=3\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the total path length?

- $15\,\mathrm{m}$
- $7\,\mathrm{m}$
- $11\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The first leg covers 11 m. The return leg is 4 m. Both lengths contribute positively to the total distance.

$$
d=|7-(-4)|+|3-7|=15\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
