A robot starts at $x=-4\,\mathrm m$ in the positive direction and then reverses once. It travels $18\,\mathrm m$ in total and its displacement is $+6\,\mathrm m$. At which coordinate does it reverse?

- $8\,\mathrm m$
- $2\,\mathrm m$
- $14\,\mathrm m$
- $-10\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Let $a$ be the outward distance and $b$ the return distance. Then $a+b=18$ and $a-b=6$, with distances in metres. Adding gives $a=12\,\mathrm m$. The robot turns at $-4+12=8\,\mathrm m$. The coordinate $2\,\mathrm m$ is the endpoint, reached after the return leg.
