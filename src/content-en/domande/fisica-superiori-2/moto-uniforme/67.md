Why is the numerical value of a speed in km/h greater than its value in m/s?

- One kilometre is shorter than one metre
- An hour is shorter than a second
- One metre per second equals 3.6 kilometres per hour
- The physical speed increases during conversion

## Explanation

At 1 m/s the distance covered in 3600 s is 3600 m, or 3.6 km. The same physical speed is therefore 3.6 km/h.
