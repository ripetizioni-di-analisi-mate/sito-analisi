A train moves at $90\,\mathrm{km/h}$. How far does it travel in $40\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $3600\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{m}$
- $900\,\mathrm{m}$
- $400\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

First convert the speed to metres per second. Multiplying by seconds cancels the denominator and leaves metres.

$$
d=\frac{90}{3.6}\cdot40\,\mathrm{m}=1000\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
