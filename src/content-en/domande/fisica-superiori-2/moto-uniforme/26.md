A person walks $60\,\mathrm{m}$ east, $80\,\mathrm{m}$ west and $25\,\mathrm{m}$ east. What is the displacement with east positive?

- $-5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $165\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

East contributes positively and west negatively. The sum includes every leg.

$$
\Delta x=60-80+25=5\,\mathrm{m}.
$$

The eastward legs total $85\,\mathrm m$, five more than the $80\,\mathrm m$ travelled west. The actual distance is $165\,\mathrm m$. Displacement accounts for the cancellation between the two directions and points east.
