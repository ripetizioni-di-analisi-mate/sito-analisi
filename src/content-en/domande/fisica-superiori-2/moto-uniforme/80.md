On the position–time graph of two objects, line A crosses the time axis while line B is elsewhere at that instant. What happens?

- A catches B at the origin
- Both objects stop
- A crosses the origin without meeting B
- A and B have the same velocity

## Explanation

A crossing the time axis only means $x_A=0$. A meeting requires the intersection of the two lines.

When A passes through zero, read B's vertical value at the same time. Its nonzero value makes the two positions distinct even though both motions are observed on the same axis.
