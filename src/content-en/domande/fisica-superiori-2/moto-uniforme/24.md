In one reference frame a stone is at $x=17\,\mathrm{m}$. A new origin is placed at the old position $x=12\,\mathrm{m}$ with the same orientation. What is the stone’s new coordinate?

- $17\,\mathrm{m}$
- $29\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The new coordinate measures position from the new origin. Subtract 12 m from the old coordinate.

$$
x^\prime=17-12=5\,\mathrm{m}.
$$

The stone stays at the same physical point. On the new scale it lies five metres in the positive direction from the origin. Changing the origin alone preserves displacements and velocities.
