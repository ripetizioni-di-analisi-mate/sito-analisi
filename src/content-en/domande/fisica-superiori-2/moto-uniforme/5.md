Two objects $200\,\mathrm{m}$ apart set off towards each other at speeds $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. How long until they meet? Choose the closest value.

- $15.385\,\mathrm{s}$
- $28.571\,\mathrm{s}$
- $33.333\,\mathrm{s}$
- $30.769\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The first object closes the gap by $7\,\mathrm{m}$ per second and the second closes it by a further $6\,\mathrm{m}$ per second. Add the speeds because the objects move towards each other. At the calculated time the gap is zero.

$$
\begin{aligned}
t_I &= \frac{200}{7+6}\,\mathrm{s} \simeq 15.385\,\mathrm{s}
\end{aligned}
$$

The separation to close is the initial distance between both people. It differs from the distance travelled by either person alone.
