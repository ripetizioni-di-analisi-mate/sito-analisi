The positive direction of an axis is reversed while its origin stays fixed. What happens to a non-zero displacement?

- Its magnitude doubles
- Its sign stays the same
- It reverses sign
- It becomes zero

## Explanation

Both the initial and the final positions reverse sign. Their difference therefore reverses sign.

For example, a change from $2$ to $7\,\mathrm m$ becomes one from $-2$ to $-7\,\mathrm m$. The change goes from $+5$ to $-5\,\mathrm m$ and keeps its magnitude.
