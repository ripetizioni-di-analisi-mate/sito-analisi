An object starts at $x_A=0$ with velocity $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and a second starts at $x_B=12\,\mathrm{m}$ with velocity $-1\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at the same time. How far has the second travelled when they meet?

- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The separation decreases by 4 m each second. They meet after 3 s and the second object travels one metre each second.

$$
d_B=1\cdot\frac{12}{3+1}\,\mathrm{m}=3\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
