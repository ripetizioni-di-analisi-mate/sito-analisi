Two trolleys start together at $x_A=0$ and $x_B=24\,\mathrm m$ with velocities $3$ and $-1\,\mathrm{m/s}$, respectively. After passing each other they keep their velocities. How much time elapses between the two instants when they are $6\,\mathrm m$ apart?

- $1.5\,\mathrm s$
- $3\,\mathrm s$
- $6\,\mathrm s$
- $12\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The signed difference $x_B-x_A=24-4t$ goes from $+6\,\mathrm m$ before passing to $-6\,\mathrm m$ afterwards. That is a change of magnitude $12\,\mathrm m$ at a rate of $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The elapsed time is therefore $\dfrac{12}{4}\,\mathrm s=3\,\mathrm s$. Using only the positive sign would find just the earlier instant.
