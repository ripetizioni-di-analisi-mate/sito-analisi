A velocity–time graph has two rectangles of equal area, one above and one below the axis. What follows?

- The distance travelled is zero
- Velocity is positive throughout
- The final position must be the origin
- The total displacement is zero

## Explanation

The rectangles contribute equal magnitudes with opposite signs. Displacement is zero, while distance adds both positive areas.
