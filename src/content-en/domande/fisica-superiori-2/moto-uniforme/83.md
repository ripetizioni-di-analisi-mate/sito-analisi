A vehicle has the velocity–time graph shown. After $t=6\,\mathrm s$ it keeps the velocity of the last stage. How many more seconds does it need to return to its starting position?

::grafico{linee="0,6 2,6;2,-1 6,-1" segmenti="2,0,2,6;2,0,2,-1;6,0,6,-1" xmin=0 xmax=7 ymin=-2 ymax=8 nomi="t (s),v (m/s)" titolo="Velocity is 6 m/s from 0 to 2 s and −1 m/s from 2 to 6 s."}

- $2\,\mathrm s$
- $4\,\mathrm s$
- $12\,\mathrm s$
- $8\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The positive area is $6\cdot2=12\,\mathrm m$ and the negative area already accumulated is $(-1)\cdot4=-4\,\mathrm m$. After $6\,\mathrm s$, $8\,\mathrm m$ of positive displacement remain. At $-1\,\mathrm{m/s}$ another $8\,\mathrm s$ are needed to cancel it. This duration is measured from the end of the graph.
