A position–time line passes through $(2\,\mathrm{s},8\,\mathrm{m})$ and $(5\,\mathrm{s},20\,\mathrm{m})$. What is its slope?

- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6.67\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Take the changes vertically and horizontally. The slope has units of velocity.

$$
v=\frac{20-8}{5-2}\,\mathrm{m/s}=4\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$

Between the points the coordinate increases by $12\,\mathrm m$ in $3\,\mathrm s$. Swapping the points reverses both differences. Their ratio stays positive at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
