A runner covers $180\,\mathrm{m}$ at $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and another $180\,\mathrm{m}$ at $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the average speed?

- $7.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $14.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $7.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The first leg takes $30\,\mathrm{s}$ and the second $20\,\mathrm{s}$. Average speed is the ratio of $360\,\mathrm{m}$ to $50\,\mathrm{s}$. Because the durations differ, the slower leg carries more weight and brings the result closer to the lower speed.

$$
\begin{aligned}
t_1 &= \frac{180}{6}\,\mathrm{s} \simeq 30\,\mathrm{s} \\[6pt]
t_2 &= \frac{180}{9}\,\mathrm{s} \simeq 20\,\mathrm{s} \\[6pt]
v_m &= \frac{2\cdot 180}{\frac{180}{6}+\frac{180}{9}}\,\mathrm{m/s}\simeq 7.2\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
