The graph of $v(t)$ is horizontal at $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ from $t=0$ to $t=4\,\mathrm{s}$ and at $-10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ from $4$ to $6\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the average velocity?

- $-5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6.67\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The two rectangle areas, +20 m and −20 m, cancel. The displacement is zero even though the object travelled 40 m.

$$
v_m=\frac{5\cdot4-10\cdot2}{6}\,\mathrm{m/s}=0.
$$

Dividing zero displacement by a positive duration gives $v_m=0$. The average speed would be $\dfrac{40}{6}\,\mathrm{m/s}$ because its numerator adds the absolute areas.
