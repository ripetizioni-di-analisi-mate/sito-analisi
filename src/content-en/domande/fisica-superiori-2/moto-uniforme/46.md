A conveyor carries a box at $15\,\mathrm{cm/s}$ for $6\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the displacement in metres along the direction of motion?

- $90\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.15\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The product gives 90 cm. Since 100 cm equals 1 m, the displacement is 0.90 m.

$$
\Delta x=15\cdot6\,\mathrm{cm}=\frac{90}{100}\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
