A robot starts at $x=4\,\mathrm{m}$, moves at $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for $2\,\mathrm{s}$, and stops. At what position does it remain?

- $10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

While moving, the position increases by 6 m. During the stop, velocity and further displacement are zero.

$$
x=4+3\cdot2=10\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
