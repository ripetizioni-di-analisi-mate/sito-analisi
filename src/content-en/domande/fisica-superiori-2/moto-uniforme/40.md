A hare leads by $54\,\mathrm{m}$ and runs at $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. A dog moving in the same direction at constant speed catches it after $9\,\mathrm{s}$. What was the dog’s speed?

- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Closing a 54 m lead in 9 s requires a relative speed of 6 m/s. Add this to the hare’s speed.

$$
u=6+\frac{54}{9}=12\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$

In nine seconds the hare travels another $54\,\mathrm m$. The dog must therefore cover $108\,\mathrm m$, the sum of the initial lead and the hare's further displacement.
