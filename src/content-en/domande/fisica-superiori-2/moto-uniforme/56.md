For the first half of the time an object travels at $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$; for the second half it returns in the opposite direction at $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the magnitude of its average velocity?

- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Give the second leg a negative sign. Since the times are equal, the average velocity is the arithmetic mean of the signed velocities.

$$
|v_m|=\left|\frac{6+(-2)}{2}\right|\,\mathrm{m/s}=2\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
