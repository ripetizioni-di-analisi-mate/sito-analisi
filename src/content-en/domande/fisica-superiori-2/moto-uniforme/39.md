Two objects start from $x=0$ in the same direction. The first leaves at $t=0$ at $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$; the second leaves at $t=4\,\mathrm{s}$ at $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What does the common clock read at the meeting after the second departure?

- $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

During the wait, the first gains 12 m. The second closes the gap at 2 m/s and takes 6 s, which must be added to the 4 s already elapsed.

$$
3t=5(t-4)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad t=10\,\mathrm{s}.
$$
