Two legs have equal duration and different velocities in the same direction. How is average velocity found?

- By taking the arithmetic mean of the two velocities
- By adding the velocities without dividing
- By always taking the smaller velocity
- By subtracting the second velocity from the first

## Explanation

Each velocity applies for the same number of seconds. The denominator is twice the time for one leg.
