On the position–time graph of a uniformly moving cart, position rises by $9\,\mathrm{m}$ every three seconds. What is the position change over $10\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $30\,\mathrm{m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $90\,\mathrm{m}$
- $27\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The constant slope corresponds to three metres per second. The change over ten seconds is therefore 30 m.

$$
\Delta x=\frac{9}{3}\cdot10\,\mathrm{m}=30\,\mathrm{m}.
$$

The change is the same whichever coordinate starts those ten seconds. Finding the final position would also require the vertical value at the start of that interval.
