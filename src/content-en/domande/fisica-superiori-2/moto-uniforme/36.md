A trip lasts $4\,\mathrm{s}$ at $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $6\,\mathrm{s}$ at $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What constant speed would cover the same distance in the same time?

- $4.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The intervals have different durations. The total distance is the 8 m of the first leg plus the 42 m of the second.

$$
u=\frac{2\cdot4+7\cdot6}{4+6}\,\mathrm{m/s}=5\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$

The equivalent constant speed is therefore $\dfrac{50}{10}\,\mathrm{m/s}=5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The simple arithmetic mean gives $4.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ because it weights both periods equally, although the higher speed lasts longer.
