A cyclist travels at $18\,\mathrm{km/h}$. How many metres does the cyclist cover in $3\,\mathrm{min}$?

- $54\,\mathrm{m}$
- $300\,\mathrm{m}$
- $540\,\mathrm{m}$
- $900\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The speed is 5 m/s and the time is 180 s. Multiply the two quantities after converting both to SI units.

$$
d=\frac{18}{3.6}\cdot(3\cdot60)\,\mathrm{m}=900\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
