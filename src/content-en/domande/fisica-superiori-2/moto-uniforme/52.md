Two sensors are $24\,\mathrm m$ apart. A trolley passes them at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The first logs its crossing one second late, the second two seconds late. What interval separates the logged times?

- $6\,\mathrm s$
- $5\,\mathrm s$
- $9\,\mathrm s$
- $7\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The journey takes $\dfrac{24}{4}=6$ seconds. If the first crossing occurs at time zero, the sensors log times of $1$ and $6+2=8$ seconds. Their difference is $7$ seconds.

Theory — [Position at constant velocity](/en/fisica-superiori-2/moto-uniforme/teoria#uniforme).
