A uniform-motion model passes through readings $(0\,\mathrm s,-4\,\mathrm m)$ and $(6\,\mathrm s,14\,\mathrm m)$. An intermediate measurement at $t=4\,\mathrm s$ gives $x=12\,\mathrm m$. What is measured position minus predicted position at that time?

- $-4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The two readings used by the model give a slope of $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. At $4\,\mathrm s$, the model predicts $-4+3\cdot4=8\,\mathrm m$. The measurement exceeds the prediction by $12-8=4\,\mathrm m$. On the position-time graph, this is the signed vertical separation between the line and the measurement.
