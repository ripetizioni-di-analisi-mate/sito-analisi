A sledge moves at $-5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for $3\,\mathrm{s}$ and then at $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for $6\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the average speed?

- $-0.33333\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The displacement on the first leg is $-15\,\mathrm{m}$ and on the second it is $12\,\mathrm{m}$. Their signed sum is $-3\,\mathrm{m}$. The distance is the sum of the magnitudes, giving $27\,\mathrm{m}$. For the required average, divide the distance by $9\,\mathrm{s}$, the duration of the whole interval.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta x_1 &= -5\cdot 3 = -15\,\mathrm{m} \\[6pt]
\Delta x_2 &= 2\cdot 6 = 12\,\mathrm{m} \\[6pt]
d &= \lvert -15\rvert + \lvert 12\rvert = 27\,\mathrm{m} \\[6pt]
v_m &= \frac{27}{9}\,\mathrm{m/s}\simeq 3\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
