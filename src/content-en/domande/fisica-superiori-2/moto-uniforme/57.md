An object is at rest for $8\,\mathrm{s}$ and then moves forward at $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for $4\,\mathrm{s}$. What is its average speed?

- $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The distance travelled is 36 m, and the duration is 12 s because it includes the stop.

$$
v_m=\frac{0\cdot8+9\cdot4}{8+4}\,\mathrm{m/s}=3\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
