On a velocity–time graph, what are the units of a rectangle’s area, with a time base and velocity height?

- Metres per second
- Seconds squared
- Metres
- Metres per second squared

## Explanation

Multiplying seconds by metres per second cancels the time unit. The area therefore measures displacement.
