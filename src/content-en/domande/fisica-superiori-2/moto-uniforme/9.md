A conveyor belt carries a parcel at constant velocity from $t=0$ to $t=10\,\mathrm s$. The parcel is at $22\,\mathrm m$ when $t=6\,\mathrm s$ and $34\,\mathrm m$ when $t=10\,\mathrm s$. What is its initial coordinate $x_0$?

- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m}$
- $22\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The slope between the two readings is $\dfrac{34-22}{10-6}\,\mathrm{m/s}=3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The displacement in the first $6\,\mathrm s$ is therefore $18\,\mathrm m$. To find the intercept, subtract that displacement from the coordinate at the first reading. This gives $x_0=22-18=4\,\mathrm m$.

To check, start at $4\,\mathrm m$ and cover another $30\,\mathrm m$ in ten seconds. This recovers the second reading without using the first recorded position as the origin.
