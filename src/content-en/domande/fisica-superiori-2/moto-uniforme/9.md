A cart has initial position $15\,\mathrm{m}$ and constant velocity $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What coordinate does it occupy after $10\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $85\,\mathrm{m}$
- $70\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-55\,\mathrm{m}$
- $155\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Use the uniform-motion equation because the velocity is constant. The interval lasts $10\,\mathrm{s}$. The product of the velocity and this duration gives displacement $70\,\mathrm{m}$. The coordinate increases by $7\,\mathrm{m}$ each second. It starts at $15\,\mathrm{m}$, so the final coordinate $85\,\mathrm{m}$ includes both the known position and the change.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta t &= 10-0 = 10\,\mathrm{s} \\[6pt]
\Delta x &= v\Delta t = (7)\cdot 10 = 70\,\mathrm{m} \\[6pt]
x_f &= x_0+\Delta x = 15+(70) = 85\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
