A cyclist travels $350\,\mathrm{m}$ in a straight line at constant speed $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$. How long does it take? Choose the closest value.

- $3150\,\mathrm{s}$
- $38.889\,\mathrm{s}$
- $47.889\,\mathrm{s}$
- $19.444\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Both quantities are already in SI units. At $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$ the cyclist covers $9\,\mathrm{m}$ each second, so divide the distance by the speed to get $38.889\,\mathrm{s}$. Multiplying the exact time by $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$ gives the original distance $350\,\mathrm{m}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta t &= \frac{d}{u} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{350}{9}\,\mathrm{s} \\[6pt]
&\simeq 38.889\,\mathrm{s}
\end{aligned}
$$

The selected value rounds to the nearest thousandth of a second. The exact quotient is $\dfrac{350}{9}\,\mathrm s$.
