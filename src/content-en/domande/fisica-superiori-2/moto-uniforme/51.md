Two trains $60\,\mathrm{m}$ and $90\,\mathrm{m}$ long travel on parallel tracks at $8$ and $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in opposite directions. How long elapses from the fronts aligning to the rears aligning?

- $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{s}$
- $150\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Between alignments the relative position must change by the sum of the lengths. Add the speeds because the directions are opposite.

$$
\Delta t=\frac{60+90}{8+7}\,\mathrm{s}=10\,\mathrm{s}.
$$

The trains move past one another at $8+7=15\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The relative distance is $60+90=150\,\mathrm m$. Dividing that distance by the relative speed gives $10\,\mathrm s$.
