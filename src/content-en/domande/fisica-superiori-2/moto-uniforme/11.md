An object moves from $75\,\mathrm{m}$ to $15\,\mathrm{m}$ in $9\,\mathrm{s}$. What is its signed average velocity? Choose the closest value.

- $6.6667\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.6667\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-6.6667\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The endpoint positions and the duration are enough to find the average velocity. Subtract the initial position from the final position to get $-60\,\mathrm{m}$, then divide by $9\,\mathrm{s}$. The quotient is negative since the final coordinate is lower than the initial coordinate. Average speed would additionally require the distance along the entire route.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta x &= 15-75 = -60\,\mathrm{m} \\[6pt]
v_m &= \frac{\Delta x}{\Delta t} = \frac{-60}{9}\,\mathrm{m/s} \simeq -6.6667\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
