Two position readings in uniform motion are $3\,\mathrm m$ and $11\,\mathrm m$, separated by exactly $2\,\mathrm s$. Each true coordinate can differ from its reading by up to $1\,\mathrm m$. What is the smallest velocity consistent with the data?

- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

To minimise the difference, take the first coordinate at its largest possible value, $4\,\mathrm m$, and the second at its smallest, $10\,\mathrm m$. Both values are allowed, so the minimum difference is $6\,\mathrm m$. Dividing by the elapsed time gives $v_{\min}=3\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

Shifting both readings by one metre in the same direction would keep the central difference of $8\,\mathrm m$.
