When does the magnitude of displacement equal the distance travelled along the axis?

- When all non-zero displacements have the same direction
- When the route includes at least one reversal
- When the object returns to its starting point after travelling a positive distance
- When the interval lasts one second

## Explanation

In one direction every moving leg has the same sign. The magnitude of the sum equals the sum of the magnitudes. A stop adds zero to both.
