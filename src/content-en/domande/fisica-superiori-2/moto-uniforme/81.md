The position of B is $x_B=b+v(t-t_B)$ for $t\geq t_B$. What does $b$ represent?

- B’s coordinate at its own departure
- B’s coordinate at $t=0$ for every $t_B$
- The duration since A started
- The relative velocity

## Explanation

At $t=t_B$ the velocity term is zero. The coordinate is $b$.

The term $t-t_B$ counts the seconds since B started. If the clock already shows a positive time when B starts, $b$ is the position at that instant. The value at time zero could be outside the domain.
