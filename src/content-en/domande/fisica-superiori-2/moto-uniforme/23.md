Two trolleys move in the positive direction along the same axis. The first travels at $36\,\mathrm{km/h}$ for $1\,\mathrm{min}$ and the second at $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for $90\,\mathrm s$. What is the difference between their displacements, first minus second?

- $-60\,\mathrm m$
- $360\,\mathrm m$
- $60\,\mathrm m$
- $1140\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The first speed is $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the first interval lasts $60\,\mathrm s$. The displacements are therefore $10\cdot60=600\,\mathrm m$ and $6\cdot90=540\,\mathrm m$. The requested difference is $60\,\mathrm m$. Comparing only the speeds would compare distance per second, whereas the two durations differ.
