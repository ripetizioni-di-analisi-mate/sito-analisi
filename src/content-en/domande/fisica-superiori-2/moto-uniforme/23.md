A vehicle maintains a speed of $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Express this value in $\mathrm{km/h}$.

- $12\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $3.3333\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $43.2\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $4.32\,\mathrm{km/h}$

## Explanation

Convert both the distance and the time. There are 3600 seconds in an hour, so the distance in metres is 3600 times the distance covered in one second. Dividing by 1000 expresses the same distance in kilometres. The result in kilometres per hour is the distance in kilometres that would be travelled in one hour.

$$
\begin{aligned}
u &= 12\,\frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}}=\frac{12}{1000}\cdot3600\,\mathrm{km/h} \\[6pt]
&= 43.2\,\mathrm{km/h}
\end{aligned}
$$
