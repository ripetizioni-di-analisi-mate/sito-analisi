A car maintains $v=-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ along a straight line. In one interval its position decreases by $44\,\mathrm{m}$. How long is the interval?

- $-11\,\mathrm{s}$
- $44\,\mathrm{s}$
- $11\,\mathrm{s}$
- $176\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The change in position is negative, as is the velocity. Dividing two negative quantities gives a positive duration.

$$
\Delta t=\frac{-44}{-4}\,\mathrm{s}=11\,\mathrm{s}.
$$
