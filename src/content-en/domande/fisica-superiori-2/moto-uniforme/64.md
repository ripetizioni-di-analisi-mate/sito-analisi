The average velocity is negative. Which conclusion is certain?

- The object was initially at rest
- Instantaneous velocity was negative throughout
- Average speed is negative
- Final position is lower than initial position

## Explanation

Elapsed time is positive. The sign of the quotient therefore depends on the sign of displacement, which compares only the two endpoint positions.

There may still have been a stage in the positive direction. The calculation depends on the net change between the two endpoints, so a negative average velocity does not fix the direction at each instant.
