A sledge moves at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for $3\,\mathrm{s}$ and then at $-5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for $6\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the signed average velocity?

- $4.6667\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The displacement on the first leg is $12\,\mathrm{m}$ and on the second it is $-30\,\mathrm{m}$. Their signed sum is $-18\,\mathrm{m}$. The distance is the sum of the magnitudes, giving $42\,\mathrm{m}$. For the required average, divide the displacement by $9\,\mathrm{s}$, the duration of the whole interval.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta x_1 &= 4\cdot 3 = 12\,\mathrm{m} \\[6pt]
\Delta x_2 &= -5\cdot 6 = -30\,\mathrm{m} \\[6pt]
\Delta x &= (12)+(-30) = -18\,\mathrm{m} \\[6pt]
v_m &= \frac{-18}{9}\,\mathrm{m/s}\simeq -2\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
