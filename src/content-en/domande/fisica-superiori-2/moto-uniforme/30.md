A cart starts at $x=0$ at $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and reverses at $t=4\,\mathrm s$. From then on it moves at $-1\,\mathrm{m/s}$. How much time elapses between the two passages through $x=4\,\mathrm m$?

- $2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

On the outward leg, $2t=4$ gives $t=2\,\mathrm s$. At the reversal the cart is at $8\,\mathrm m$. Returning to $4\,\mathrm m$ takes another $4\,\mathrm m$ at a speed of $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The second passage is therefore at $t=8\,\mathrm s$ and the interval is $6\,\mathrm s$.

Both times are readings of the same clock. Subtracting $t=2\,\mathrm s$ from $t=8\,\mathrm s$ includes the last part of the outward leg and the first part of the return.
