Which observation, assumed exact, rules out uniform motion along a line?

- The initial position is negative
- Equal consecutive time intervals give different displacements
- The object crosses the origin
- Signed velocity is negative

## Explanation

In uniform motion multiplying the constant velocity by the same duration always gives the same displacement.

For example, displacements of $6\,\mathrm m$ in two seconds and $8\,\mathrm m$ in the next two give different average slopes. One straight line cannot have both slopes.
