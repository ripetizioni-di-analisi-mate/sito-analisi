An object has a negative coordinate. What does this alone imply?

- It is moving in the negative direction
- It is moving away from the origin
- It is on the side of the origin opposite the positive direction
- Its velocity is zero

## Explanation

The sign of a single coordinate places the object relative to the origin. Motion requires a comparison between times.
