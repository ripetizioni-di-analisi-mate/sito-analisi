Two cyclists travel in the same direction at $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The slower cyclist is $28\,\mathrm{m}$ ahead of the faster one.The first mentioned starts from coordinate $31\,\mathrm{m}$ in the positive direction. What is the meeting coordinate?

- $80\,\mathrm{m}$
- $52\,\mathrm{m}$
- $38\,\mathrm{m}$
- $101\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The initial gap is $28\,\mathrm{m}$ and shrinks by $4\,\mathrm{m}$ each second because both cyclists move in the same direction. Their relative speed is therefore the difference of their speeds. At the meeting the first cyclist's lead is zero. The first object travels $21\,\mathrm{m}$, so add that distance to its initial position. Its endpoint is $52\,\mathrm{m}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
t_I &= \frac{28}{7-3}\,\mathrm{s} \simeq 7\,\mathrm{s} \\[6pt]
x_I &= 31+3\cdot\frac{28}{4} = 52\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
