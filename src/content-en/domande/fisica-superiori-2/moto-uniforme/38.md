Two trolleys move at constant velocities in the same direction. At $t=0$ they are at $x_A=0$ and $x_B=15\,\mathrm m$. At $t=4\,\mathrm s$ their coordinates are $x_A=16\,\mathrm m$ and $x_B=23\,\mathrm m$. B leaves the straight section at $t=5\,\mathrm s$. What is their separation at that instant?

- $0\,\mathrm m$
- $5\,\mathrm m$
- $7\,\mathrm m$
- $15\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

B's lead shrinks from $15$ to $23-16=7\,\mathrm m$ in $4\,\mathrm s$, a decrease of $2\,\mathrm m$ per second. During the next second it falls to $7-2=5\,\mathrm m$. A is therefore still behind when B leaves the straight section. Extending the decrease would require a model for the subsequent route.
