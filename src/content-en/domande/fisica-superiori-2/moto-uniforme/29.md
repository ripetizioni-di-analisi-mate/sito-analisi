The graph shows a sled's position over time. A sensor records displacement only during sections with a speed of at least $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What total displacement does it record?

::grafico{linee="0,0 3,-6 5,-6 9,6" punti="0,0,pieno;3,-6,pieno;5,-6,pieno;9,6,pieno" xmin=0 xmax=10 ymin=-8 ymax=8 nomi="t (s),x (m)" titolo="The graph falls from 0 to −6 m in 3 s, stays flat until 5 s and rises to 6 m in 4 s."}

- $12\,\mathrm m$
- $18\,\mathrm m$
- $6\,\mathrm m$
- $0\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The first section has slope $\dfrac{-6}{3}\,\mathrm{m/s}=-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$, so its magnitude is below the threshold. The horizontal section has zero slope. The last slope is $\dfrac{6-(-6)}{9-5}\,\mathrm{m/s}=3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Only this section is recorded. Its position increases by $12\,\mathrm m$, so the sensor reports a positive displacement.
