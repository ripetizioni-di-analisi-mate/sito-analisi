The graph shows a sled's position over time. What is the sled's displacement in the section where the magnitude of its velocity is greatest?

::grafico{linee="0,0 3,-6 5,-6 9,6" punti="0,0,pieno;3,-6,pieno;5,-6,pieno;9,6,pieno" xmin=0 xmax=10 ymin=-8 ymax=8 nomi="t (s),x (m)" titolo="The graph falls from 0 to −6 m in 3 s, stays flat until 5 s and rises to 6 m in 4 s."}

- $12\,\mathrm m$
- $18\,\mathrm m$
- $6\,\mathrm m$
- $-6\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

In each straight section of the graph the velocity is the slope. In the first section it is $\dfrac{-6-0}{3}\,\mathrm{m/s}=-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$, in the second it is zero and in the third it is $\dfrac{6-(-6)}{9-5}\,\mathrm{m/s}=3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The magnitude of the velocity is therefore greatest in the third section. In that section the position goes from $-6\,\mathrm m$ to $6\,\mathrm m$, so the displacement is $12\,\mathrm m$.
