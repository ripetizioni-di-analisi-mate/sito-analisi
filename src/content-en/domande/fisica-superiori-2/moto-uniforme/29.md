A sledge moves at $-3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for $4\,\mathrm{s}$ and then at $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for $9\,\mathrm{s}$. How far does it travel in total?

- $30\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The displacement on the first leg is $-12\,\mathrm{m}$ and on the second it is $18\,\mathrm{m}$. Their signed sum is $6\,\mathrm{m}$. The distance is the sum of the magnitudes, giving $30\,\mathrm{m}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta x_1 &= -3\cdot 4 = -12\,\mathrm{m} \\[6pt]
\Delta x_2 &= 2\cdot 9 = 18\,\mathrm{m} \\[6pt]
d &= \lvert -12\rvert + \lvert 18\rvert = 30\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
