A $50\,\mathrm{m}$ train crosses a $100\,\mathrm{m}$ tunnel at $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the time from the front entering to the rear exiting?

- $5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{s}$
- $15\,\mathrm{s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

When the front reaches the exit, the rear must still advance by the length of the train. The front therefore moves by 150 m in total.

$$
\Delta t=\frac{100+50}{10}\,\mathrm{s}=15\,\mathrm{s}.
$$

The front alone takes $10\,\mathrm s$ to travel from one end to the other. The remaining $50\,\mathrm m$ then take another $5\,\mathrm s$. The total time is $15\,\mathrm s$.
