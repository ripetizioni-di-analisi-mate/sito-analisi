A cart has initial position $12\,\mathrm{m}$ and constant velocity $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What coordinate does it occupy after $8\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $60\,\mathrm{m}$
- $48\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-36\,\mathrm{m}$
- $108\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Use the uniform-motion equation because the velocity is constant. The interval lasts $8\,\mathrm{s}$. The product of the velocity and this duration gives displacement $48\,\mathrm{m}$. The coordinate increases by $6\,\mathrm{m}$ each second. It starts at $12\,\mathrm{m}$, so the final coordinate $60\,\mathrm{m}$ includes both the known position and the change.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta t &= 8-0 = 8\,\mathrm{s} \\[6pt]
\Delta x &= v\Delta t = (6)\cdot 8 = 48\,\mathrm{m} \\[6pt]
x_f &= x_0+\Delta x = 12+(48) = 60\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
