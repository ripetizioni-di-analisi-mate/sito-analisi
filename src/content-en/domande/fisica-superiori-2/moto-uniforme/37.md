Two objects $36\,\mathrm{m}$ apart set off towards each other at speeds $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$.The first mentioned starts from coordinate $5\,\mathrm{m}$ in the positive direction. What is the meeting coordinate?

- $29\,\mathrm{m}$
- $65\,\mathrm{m}$
- $11\,\mathrm{m}$
- $41\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The first object closes the gap by $4\,\mathrm{m}$ per second and the second closes it by a further $2\,\mathrm{m}$ per second. Add the speeds because the objects move towards each other. At the calculated time the gap is zero. The first object travels $24\,\mathrm{m}$, so add that distance to its initial position. Its endpoint is $29\,\mathrm{m}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
t_I &= \frac{36}{4+2}\,\mathrm{s} \simeq 6\,\mathrm{s} \\[6pt]
x_I &= 5+4\cdot\frac{36}{6} = 29\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
