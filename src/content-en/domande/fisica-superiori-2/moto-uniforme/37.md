Two carts A and B move on two tracks parallel to the $x$ axis, both in the positive direction. At time zero A is at $x_A=0$ and B is at $x_B=12\,\mathrm m$. Their constant velocities are $4$ and $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$, respectively. During which interval do the two position coordinates differ by at most $4\,\mathrm m$?

- $[4;8]\,\mathrm s$
- $[0;4]\,\mathrm s$
- $[2;6]\,\mathrm s$
- $[6;10]\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

B's lead decreases by two metres each second and turns negative after A passes it. Use the absolute value of this difference to measure the separation along the axis. At four seconds B is four metres ahead, and at eight seconds A is four metres ahead. The threshold holds at all times in between.

$$
\left|12\,\mathrm m-(2\,\mathrm{m/s})t\right|\leq4\,\mathrm m\qquad\Longleftrightarrow\qquad 4\,\mathrm s\leq t\leq8\,\mathrm s.
$$
