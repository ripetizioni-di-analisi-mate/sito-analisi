An object moves from $x_i=70\,\mathrm m$ to $x_f=14\,\mathrm m$ in $8\,\mathrm s$. What is its signed average velocity?

- $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.75\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-7\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The endpoint positions and the duration are enough to find the average velocity. Subtract the initial position from the final position to get $-56\,\mathrm{m}$, then divide by $8\,\mathrm{s}$. The quotient is negative since the final coordinate is lower than the initial coordinate. Average speed would additionally require the distance along the entire route.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta x &= 14-70 = -56\,\mathrm{m} \\[6pt]
v_m &= \frac{\Delta x}{\Delta t} = \frac{-56}{8}\,\mathrm{m/s} \simeq -7\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
