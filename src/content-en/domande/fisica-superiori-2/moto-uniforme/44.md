The average velocity from $t=2\,\mathrm{s}$ to $t=7\,\mathrm{s}$ is $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. At $t=2\,\mathrm{s}$ the position is $x=8\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the position at $t=7\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $-6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Even for varying motion, average velocity directly relates displacement to the elapsed 5 s. Add the change of −10 m to the initial 8 m.

$$
x_f=8+(-2)(7-2)=-2\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
