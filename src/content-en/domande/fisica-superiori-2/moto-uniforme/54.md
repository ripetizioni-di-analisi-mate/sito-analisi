On a velocity–time graph a horizontal line is at $v=-3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The object starts at $x=2\,\mathrm{m}$. Where is it after $4\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $-12\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $14\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The signed area of the rectangle is −12 m. Add the initial position to obtain the final coordinate.

$$
x_f=2+(-3)\cdot4=-10\,\mathrm{m}.
$$

The negative velocity reduces the position by three metres each second. From the initial $2\,\mathrm m$, the drop of $12\,\mathrm m$ therefore takes the body to $-10\,\mathrm m$.
