A uniform-motion table gives $(t,x)=(1,4),(3,10),(5,x_5)$ in SI units. What is $x_5$?

- $13\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The coordinate rises by 6 m from 1 s to 3 s. The intervals have equal duration and velocity is constant, so it rises by another 6 m.

$$
x_5=10+(10-4)=16\,\mathrm{m}.
$$

The time step between successive rows is $2\,\mathrm s$. The ratio $\dfrac62\,\mathrm{m/s}$ confirms that the graph keeps its slope of $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
