A position–time graph is redrawn with a different vertical scale, making the line look steeper. What determines the velocity?

- The number of pixels in height
- The ratio of changes read in the units of the axes
- The apparent angle with the edge of the screen
- Only the vertical intercept

## Explanation

The physical values are the changes in coordinate and in time. The picture can stretch while representing the same numerical changes.

If two points differ by $6\,\mathrm m$ and $2\,\mathrm s$, the ratio is $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at any scale. Measuring the line's angle on the page does not recover those units.
