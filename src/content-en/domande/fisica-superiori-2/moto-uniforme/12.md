At $t=0$, cart A starts from $x=0$ at $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Cart B starts from $x=24\,\mathrm m$ with velocity $-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and stops at $t=3\,\mathrm s$. At what time does A reach B?

- $3\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Extending B's first segment would give $2t=24-4t$ and $t=4\,\mathrm s$, outside that segment's interval. After stopping, B stays at $24-4\cdot 3=12\,\mathrm m$. Solving $2t=12$ now gives $t=6\,\mathrm s$. This time falls within the interval when B is stationary.

When B stops, A is still at $6\,\mathrm m$. It needs three more seconds to close the $6\,\mathrm m$ gap.
