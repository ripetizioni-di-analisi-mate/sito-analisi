A train travels at $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for $6\,\mathrm{s}$. On a second leg lasting $4\,\mathrm{s}$ it continues in the same direction. To average $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what speed is needed on the second leg?

- $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The required total distance is 70 m in 10 s. Subtract the first leg’s 30 m, leaving 40 m to be covered in 4 s.

$$
u_2=\frac{7(6+4)-5\cdot6}{4}\,\mathrm{m/s}=10\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
