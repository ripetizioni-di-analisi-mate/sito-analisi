A sensor records a cart at $-2\,\mathrm{m}$ at time $5\,\mathrm{s}$. Its constant velocity is $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Where is it at $10\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-22\,\mathrm{m}$
- $38\,\mathrm{m}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Use the uniform-motion equation because the velocity is constant. The interval lasts $5\,\mathrm{s}$. The product of the velocity and this duration gives displacement $20\,\mathrm{m}$. The coordinate increases by $4\,\mathrm{m}$ each second. It starts at $-2\,\mathrm{m}$, so the final coordinate $18\,\mathrm{m}$ includes both the known position and the change.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta t &= 10-5 = 5\,\mathrm{s} \\[6pt]
\Delta x &= v\Delta t = (4)\cdot 5 = 20\,\mathrm{m} \\[6pt]
x_f &= x_0+\Delta x = -2+(20) = 18\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
