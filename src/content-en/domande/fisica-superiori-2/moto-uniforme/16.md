A cart moves in a straight line at constant velocity. A sensor records $x=-3\,\mathrm m$ at $2\,\mathrm s$ and $x=9\,\mathrm m$ at $6\,\mathrm s$. Which additional reading $(t;x)$, with $t$ in seconds and $x$ in metres, is consistent with those two records?

- $(0;-6)$
- $(4;1)$
- $(9;12)$
- $(5;6)$

## Explanation

The two readings determine the slope of the position–time graph. Twelve metres in four seconds gives a velocity of three metres per second. To check a new reading, start from minus three metres at two seconds and add the displacement over the relevant interval. At five seconds the cart is at six metres.

$$
v=\frac{9-(-3)}{6-2}\,\mathrm{m/s}=3\,\mathrm{m/s},\qquad x(5\,\mathrm s)=[-3+3(5-2)]\,\mathrm m=6\,\mathrm m.
$$
