What can be deduced from zero displacement at the end of a journey?

- The object never moved
- The final position equals the initial position
- The distance travelled must be zero
- Both positions must be the origin

## Explanation

Displacement is final position minus initial position. A zero difference leaves open both the intermediate positions and the choice of origin.
