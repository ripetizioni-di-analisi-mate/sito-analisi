The equation is $x=18-6t$, with $x$ in metres and $t$ in seconds, for $t\geq0$. When does the object cross the origin?

- $3\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{s}$
- $18\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The crossing requires position zero. The positive time found lies in the domain.

$$
18-6t=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad t=\frac{18}{6}\,\mathrm{s}=3\,\mathrm{s}.
$$

Before the calculated instant, for example at $t=2\,\mathrm s$, the position is still $6\,\mathrm m$. A second after the crossing it is already $-6\,\mathrm m$. The zero coordinate marks passage through the origin. The velocity stays constant throughout.
