The calculated average speed on a straight route is less than $|v_m|$. What does this reveal?

- The data or calculation are inconsistent
- The object turned back at least once
- The object travelled in the negative direction
- Both quantities must be negative

## Explanation

Distance is at least the magnitude of displacement. Dividing both quantities by the same positive time preserves the inequality.
