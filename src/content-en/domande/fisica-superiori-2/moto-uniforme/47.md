An object travels at $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for $2\,\mathrm{s}$, then reverses at constant velocity for $5\,\mathrm{s}$ and returns to its starting point. What is its velocity on the second leg?

- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The first displacement is +10 m and the second must cancel it. Divide −10 m by 5 s to find the velocity.

$$
v_2=\frac{-5\cdot2}{5}\,\mathrm{m/s}=-2\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$

The negative sign represents motion back along the axis. The check $5\cdot2+(-2)\cdot5=0$ gives zero displacement after both legs. The total path length is $20\,\mathrm m$.
