A piecewise linear position–time graph changes slope without any jumps. What condition does the model satisfy?

- Velocity is constant over the whole interval
- The end position of each leg is the starting position of the next
- The object is at rest at every slope change
- Distance travelled is always zero

## Explanation

Continuity of position means that the legs meet at the same point. The slope may change in a piecewise motion model.
