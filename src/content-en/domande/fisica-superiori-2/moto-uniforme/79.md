The position equation gives $t=-2\,\mathrm{s}$, but the objects begin moving at $t=0$. How should this result be interpreted?

- The objects meet at $t=2\,\mathrm{s}$
- It lies outside the model domain and gives no meeting after departure
- The objects always meet twice
- The objects meet exactly at the origin

## Explanation

The sign of the time carries information. Taking its magnitude would generally give a different pair of positions.

For example, $x_A=2t$ and $x_B=-2+t$ are equal at $t=-2\,\mathrm s$. At $t=2\,\mathrm s$ their coordinates are $4$ and $0\,\mathrm m$, so changing the sign does not give a meeting.
