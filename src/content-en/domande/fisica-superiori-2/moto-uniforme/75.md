A position–time graph remains horizontal at $x=5\,\mathrm{m}$. What does it say about the object?

- It moves at 5 m/s
- It returns to the origin
- Displacement increases by 5 m each second
- It is at rest five metres from the origin

## Explanation

The coordinate does not change. Its slope, and therefore its velocity, is zero.
