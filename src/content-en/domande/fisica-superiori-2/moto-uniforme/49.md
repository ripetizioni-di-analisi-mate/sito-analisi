An $80\,\mathrm{m}$ train moves at $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$. How long does it take to pass a stationary person completely? Choose the closest value.

- $80\,\mathrm{s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{s}$
- $13.333\,\mathrm{s}$
- $6.6667\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Between the front and the rear passing the person, the train moves by its own length. Divide that length by speed.

$$
\Delta t=\frac{80}{12}\,\mathrm{s}=\frac{20}{3}\,\mathrm{s}.
$$
