On a position–time graph a line decreases but remains above the time axis. What are the signs of position and velocity?

- Positive position and negative velocity
- Positive position and positive velocity
- Negative position and positive velocity
- Negative position and negative velocity

## Explanation

Positive height gives the position. Negative slope gives the velocity.

A change from $10$ to $6\,\mathrm m$ in $2\,\mathrm s$ illustrates the two signs. Both positions are positive, while the velocity is $\dfrac{6-10}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s}=-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ because the graph's vertical value falls over time.
