The stop between two forward legs is lengthened while the legs stay the same. What happens to average speed?

- It increases
- It stays the same
- It becomes negative
- It decreases

## Explanation

Total distance stays the same while total time increases. A positive distance divided by a longer time gives a smaller quotient.
