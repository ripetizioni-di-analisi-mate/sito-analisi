A clock reads $2\,\mathrm{min}$ after motion began. How far has an object travelling at $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ moved?

- $360\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $180\,\mathrm{m}$
- $60\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Two minutes contain 120 seconds. The speed is three metres for each of those seconds.

$$
d=3\cdot(2\cdot60)\,\mathrm{m}=360\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
