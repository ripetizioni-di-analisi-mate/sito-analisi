A cart has initial position $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and constant velocity $-5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What coordinate does it occupy after $2\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $-10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $13\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-7\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Use the uniform-motion equation because the velocity is constant. The interval lasts $2\,\mathrm{s}$. The product of the velocity and this duration gives displacement $-10\,\mathrm{m}$. The negative slope means that in this interval the cart moves towards decreasing coordinates. The final value is $-7\,\mathrm{m}$. The distance travelled is $10\,\mathrm{m}$ because the direction stays the same.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta t &= 2-0 = 2\,\mathrm{s} \\[6pt]
\Delta x &= v\Delta t = (-5)\cdot 2 = -10\,\mathrm{m} \\[6pt]
x_f &= x_0+\Delta x = 3+(-10) = -7\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
