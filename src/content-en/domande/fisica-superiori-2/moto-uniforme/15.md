The first light gate is at $10\,\mathrm m$, the second at $-2\,\mathrm m$. A trolley travels at $-3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and passes the second gate when the clock reads $8\,\mathrm s$. When did it pass the first gate?

- $12\,\mathrm s$
- $2\,\mathrm s$
- $4\,\mathrm s$
- $8\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The displacement between the gates is $-2-10=-12\,\mathrm m$. Dividing by the velocity $-3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ gives a positive duration of $4\,\mathrm s$. The second reading occurs at $8\,\mathrm s$, so the first occurs at $8-4=4\,\mathrm s$. The duration links the two clock readings.
