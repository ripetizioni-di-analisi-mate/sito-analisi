How many meetings do two particles have if their position functions agree over a whole interval?

- They occupy the same position at every time in the interval
- One meeting at the start
- Two meetings, one at each endpoint
- None, because the velocities are equal

## Explanation

The functions assign the same coordinate to every time in the interval. The meeting condition holds at every instant.

For example, if both follow $x=2+3t$ on the same interval, their lines overlap. Equality holds at every time under consideration and does not require one isolated intersection.
