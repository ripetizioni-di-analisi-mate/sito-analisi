If the origin shifts by three metres and the axis keeps its orientation, what happens to displacement?

- It always increases by three metres
- It reverses sign
- It is unchanged
- It becomes zero

## Explanation

The initial and final coordinates change by the same amount. In the displacement formula the origin-shift terms cancel.

For example, from $2$ to $7\,\mathrm m$ the change is $5\,\mathrm m$. Moving the origin $3\,\mathrm m$ in the positive direction gives readings of $-1$ and $4\,\mathrm m$.
