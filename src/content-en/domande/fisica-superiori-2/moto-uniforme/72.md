Two position–time lines have the same slope but different vertical intercepts. What do they describe?

- The same initial position and different velocities
- The same velocity and different initial positions
- Opposite velocities
- A collision at every time

## Explanation

The slope is the signed velocity, while the intercept is the position at the chosen time zero.

For example, $x_A=3t+1$ and $x_B=3t+6$ both increase by three metres per second. The vertical gap of five metres stays constant, so the graphs are parallel.
