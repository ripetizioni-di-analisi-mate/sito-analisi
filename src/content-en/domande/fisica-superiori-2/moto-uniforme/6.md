Two cyclists travel in the same direction at $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The slower cyclist is $70\,\mathrm{m}$ ahead of the faster one. How long until they meet?

- $10\,\mathrm{s}$
- $14\,\mathrm{s}$
- $5.8333\,\mathrm{s}$
- $28\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The initial gap is $70\,\mathrm{m}$ and shrinks by $5\,\mathrm{m}$ each second because both cyclists move in the same direction. Their relative speed is therefore the difference of their speeds. At the meeting the first cyclist's lead is zero.

$$
\begin{aligned}
t_I &= \frac{70}{12-7}\,\mathrm{s} \simeq 14\,\mathrm{s}
\end{aligned}
$$
