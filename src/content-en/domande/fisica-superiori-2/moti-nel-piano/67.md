In a horizontal launch without air at $u_0>0$, how does speed change as time increases?

- It increases
- It stays constant
- It decreases to zero
- It oscillates between two values

## Explanation

The horizontal component remains $u_0$, and the vertical component has magnitude $gt$. The speed is therefore $\sqrt{u_0^2+g^2t^2}$. Before impact, the second term under the square root increases with time and the first stays the same. The speed therefore increases.
