In a horizontal launch without air at $u_0>0$, how does speed change as time increases?

- It increases
- It stays constant
- It decreases to zero
- It oscillates between two values

## Explanation

The horizontal component is fixed while the magnitude of the vertical component grows. The sum of their squares grows, hence the square root grows.
