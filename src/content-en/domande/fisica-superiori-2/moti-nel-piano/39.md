A point has tangential speed $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ while rotating at $\omega=3\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. How far is it from the centre?

- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Divide the tangential speed by angular speed. A radian describes a length ratio, so the quotient is in metres.

$$
r=\frac{6}{3}\,\mathrm{m}=2\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
