Two different paths have the same endpoints. Which quantity is necessarily identical?

- Travel time
- Total distance
- The displacement vector
- Velocity at each point

## Explanation

Displacement comes from subtracting the two positions. The intermediate path and times concern different quantities.

Between points separated by six metres horizontally and eight vertically, the direct segment is ten metres long. Two sections along the axes measure fourteen metres. Both connect the same endpoints and give the same displacement.
