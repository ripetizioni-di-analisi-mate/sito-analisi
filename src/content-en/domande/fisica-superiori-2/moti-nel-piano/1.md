An object has perpendicular velocity components $v_x=15\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_y=20\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Find its speed.

- $25\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $35\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Draw the two components as the legs of a right triangle. Their signs give the direction and quadrant of the vector. Since the legs are perpendicular, add their squared magnitudes to find the square of the diagonal length. Its positive square root gives $25\,\mathrm{m/s}$. As a check, it exceeds either single component magnitude and does not exceed their sum.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\lvert\vec v\rvert &= \sqrt{(15)^2+(20)^2}\,\mathrm{m/s} \\[6pt]
&\simeq 25\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
