What angular magnitude corresponds to a quarter of a turn?

- $\pi$ radians
- $2\pi$ radians
- $\frac14$ radian
- $\frac{\pi}{2}$ radians

## Explanation

One full turn is $2\pi$ radians. Divide that angle by four to obtain the answer.

One full turn measures $2\pi\,\mathrm{rad}$. A quarter covers one fourth of that, so the magnitude is $\dfrac{2\pi}{4}\,\mathrm{rad}$. Assigning a sign also requires a convention and the direction of rotation.
