What angle is swept in a quarter turn?

- $\pi$ radians
- $2\pi$ radians
- $\frac14$ radian
- $\frac{\pi}{2}$ radians

## Explanation

One full turn is $2\pi$ radians. Divide that angle by four to obtain the answer.
