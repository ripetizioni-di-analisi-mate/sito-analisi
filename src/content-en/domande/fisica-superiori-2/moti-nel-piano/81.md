Which expression has units of centripetal acceleration?

- $\omega^2 r$
- $\omega r$
- $\frac{r}{\omega^2}$
- $r^2\omega$

## Explanation

Squaring angular velocity gives inverse time squared. Multiplying by a length gives metres per second squared.

A radian comes from the ratio of two lengths, so dimensionally $\omega$ has inverse time units. Its square multiplied by a radius gives length divided by time squared.
