Angular speed is $6\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. What is the period? Choose the closest value.

- $3\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.16667\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.33333\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.66667\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

One revolution requires an angle of $2\pi$. Divide that angle by the angular rate to find the duration.

$$
T=\frac{2\pi}{6\pi}\,\mathrm{s}=\frac13\,\mathrm{s}.
$$

The period is the time needed to sweep through $2\pi\,\mathrm{rad}$. At $6\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ this is $\dfrac13\,\mathrm s$. Multiplying the exact period by the angular speed recovers the angle of one turn.
