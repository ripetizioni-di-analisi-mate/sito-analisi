A ball is launched horizontally from a height of $60\,\mathrm m$ at $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$, starting from $x=0$. What is the magnitude of its vertical velocity component when it crosses the plane $x=36\,\mathrm m$? Use $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and neglect air resistance.

- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $45\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The horizontal equation gives $t=\dfrac{36}{12}\,\mathrm s=3\,\mathrm s$. The drop is $\dfrac12\cdot10\cdot3^2\,\mathrm m=45\,\mathrm m$. The ball is still $15\,\mathrm m$ above the ground and remains in flight. With up as positive, $v_y=-gt=-30\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The requested magnitude is $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
