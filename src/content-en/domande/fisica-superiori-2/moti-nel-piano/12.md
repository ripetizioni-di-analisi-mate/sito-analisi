A ball is launched horizontally and is still in flight after $3\,\mathrm{s}$. Ignoring air resistance, what is the magnitude of the vertical component of its velocity then? Use $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $-30\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $60\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Evaluate the components at the same time $3\,\mathrm{s}$. The vertical equation needs no horizontal component. With upward positive, velocity along $y$ starts at zero and decreases by $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ each second. The negative sign means the ball is falling. The question asks for magnitude, $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The total speed would also require the horizontal component.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v_y &= 0-(10)\cdot 3=-30\,\mathrm{m/s} \\[6pt]
\lvert v_y\rvert &= 30\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
