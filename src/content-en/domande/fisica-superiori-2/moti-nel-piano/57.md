A point has constant velocities along both axes. What is its path if at least one component is non-zero?

- A circle
- A parabola in every case
- A straight line
- A sequence of disconnected points

## Explanation

The velocity keeps both magnitude and direction. Displacement over each interval is proportional to the same vector.
