In uniform rotation, which quantity is shared by two points fixed to the same disk?

- Tangential speed at any radius
- Angular velocity
- Centripetal acceleration at any radius
- Arc length in one revolution

## Explanation

Points fixed to the disk sweep the same angle in the same time. The linear quantities also depend on distance from the axis.
