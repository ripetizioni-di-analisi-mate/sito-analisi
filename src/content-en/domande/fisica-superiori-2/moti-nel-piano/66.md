If the vertical axis is positive downward, what is the sign of the vertical component of acceleration for a launch without air resistance?

- Negative
- Zero
- It depends on horizontal velocity
- Positive

## Explanation

Gravity acts downward, in the same direction as the chosen axis. The acceleration component is therefore $a_y=+g$. In a horizontal launch the vertical velocity starts at zero. As the ball flies it becomes positive on this axis and its magnitude grows.
