When reading the horizontal projection over a whole revolution, what feature reveals the period?

- The maximum horizontal coordinate
- The vertical intercept alone
- The tangent slope at an arbitrary point
- The time after which the same phase of the cycle returns

## Explanation

The projected position and its direction of change repeat together. The period measures the time for that repetition.
