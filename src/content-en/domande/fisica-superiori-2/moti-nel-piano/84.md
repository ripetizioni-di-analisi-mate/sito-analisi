The graph shows the horizontal coordinate of a point in uniform circular motion. How much time elapses from the first crossing of $x=0$ with decreasing projection to the next crossing in the same direction?

::grafico{f="3*cos(PI*x/3)" xmin=0 xmax=8.5 ymin=-4 ymax=4 punti="1.5,0,pieno;4.5,0,pieno;7.5,0,pieno" etichette="1.51,0.67,1.5;4.35,-0.77,4.5;7.55,0.72,7.5" nomi="t (s),x (m)" titolo="The projection crosses zero downwards at 1.5 s, upwards at 4.5 s and downwards at 7.5 s."}

- $3\,\mathrm s$
- $4.5\,\mathrm s$
- $7.5\,\mathrm s$
- $6\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The first downward crossing occurs at $1.5\,\mathrm s$. At $4.5\,\mathrm s$ the coordinate returns to zero, but this time it is increasing. The next downward crossing is at $7.5\,\mathrm s$. The same phase returns after $7.5-1.5=6\,\mathrm s$, the period of rotation.
