A point follows $x=10+15t$ and $y=20+18t^2$ in SI units. What is the magnitude of its displacement between $t=0$ and $t=2\,\mathrm s$?

- $102\,\mathrm m$
- $78\,\mathrm m$
- $30\,\mathrm m$
- $39\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Evaluate both equations at each endpoint. The initial position is $(10;20)\,\mathrm m$ and the final position is $(40;92)\,\mathrm m$. Subtraction gives components $(30;72)\,\mathrm m$. The magnitude is $\sqrt{30^2+72^2}\,\mathrm m=78\,\mathrm m$. Using the final pair on its own would give the distance from the origin at the end of the observation.
