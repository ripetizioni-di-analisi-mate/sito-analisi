A point completes one full turn with constant tangential speed. What is its average acceleration over the full revolution?

- It is $\dfrac{v^2}{r}$ towards the initial centre
- It equals $\omega$
- It has maximum magnitude
- Zero

## Explanation

At the end the velocity has recovered its initial magnitude and direction. Dividing the zero vector difference by the positive period gives zero.
