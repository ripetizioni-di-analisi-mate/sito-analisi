A point moves in a circle of radius $0.5\,\mathrm{m}$ at frequency $5\,\mathrm{Hz}$. Find its tangential speed. Choose the closest value.

- $7.854\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $31.416\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $15.708\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The distance in one revolution is the circumference $2\pi r$. The point makes 5 revolutions each second, so multiply that length by the frequency. Instantaneous velocity points along the tangent and has magnitude $15.708\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Another point at twice the radius on the same disk would make the same number of revolutions per second and travel twice the distance in each revolution.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v &= 2\pi r f=2\pi\cdot 0.5\cdot 5\,\mathrm{m/s} \\[6pt]
&\simeq 15.708\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
