A point $0.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from the axis covers a $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ arc. What is the swept angle in radians?

- $3\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $0.75\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $6.2832\,\mathrm{rad}$

## Explanation

The angle in radians is the ratio of arc length to radius. Length units cancel in this ratio.

$$
\theta=\frac{1.5}{0.5}\,\mathrm{rad}=3\,\mathrm{rad}.
$$

One radian corresponds to an arc as long as the radius. The given arc contains three such lengths, so the angle is $3\,\mathrm{rad}$. It is slightly less than half a turn, which measures $\pi\,\mathrm{rad}$.
