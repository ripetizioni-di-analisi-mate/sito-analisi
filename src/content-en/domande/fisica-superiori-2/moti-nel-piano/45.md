Two bends have radii $10$ and $40\,\mathrm{m}$. The vehicles have the same speed. What is the ratio of acceleration in the first to the second?

- $4$
- $0.25$
- $2$
- $16$

## Explanation

The common speed squared cancels when dividing the accelerations. The inverse radius ratio remains.

$$
\frac{a_1}{a_2}=\frac{r_2}{r_1}=\frac{40}{10}=4.
$$
