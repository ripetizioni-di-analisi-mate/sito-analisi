For a horizontal launch without air from the same height, how does greater initial speed affect the curvature of $y(x)$?

- The $x^2$ coefficient always doubles
- The trajectory becomes exactly horizontal
- The intercept changes but curvature remains fixed
- The magnitude of the $x^2$ coefficient is smaller

## Explanation

The expression $y=h-\frac{g}{2u_0^2}x^2$ shows that the quadratic coefficient is inversely proportional to $u_0^2$.
