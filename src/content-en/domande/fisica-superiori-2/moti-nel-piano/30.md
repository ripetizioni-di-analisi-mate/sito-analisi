A wheel has one mark on its rim. A camera takes two photographs per second. In every photograph the mark is in the same position. Which of these constant rotation frequencies is consistent with this observation?

- $1\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $4\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $3\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $5\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

There are $0.5$ seconds between photographs. At four revolutions per second the wheel completes two full revolutions in that time. Each other frequency gives a half-integer number of revolutions, so the mark would appear on the opposite side.
