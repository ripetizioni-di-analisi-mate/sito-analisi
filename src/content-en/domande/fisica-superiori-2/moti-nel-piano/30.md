A disk makes $180$ revolutions in one minute. What is the frequency in SI units?

- $180\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $3\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $60\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $0.33333\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

Express time in seconds in the denominator, so the ratio counts revolutions per second.

$$
f=\frac{180}{60}\,\mathrm{Hz}=3\,\mathrm{Hz}.
$$
