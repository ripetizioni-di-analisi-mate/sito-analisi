A ball is launched horizontally at $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ from $125\,\mathrm{m}$ above the ground. Use $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and neglect air resistance. What is the horizontal range?

- $15\,\mathrm{m}$
- $60\,\mathrm{m}$
- $30\,\mathrm{m}$
- $42.426\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

With the vertical axis positive upward, initial vertical velocity is zero. Height drops from $125\,\mathrm{m}$ to zero, so the fall equation gives the positive time $5\,\mathrm{s}$. There is no horizontal acceleration. Velocity $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is unchanged throughout the already calculated duration, giving range $30\,\mathrm{m}$. The plot shows the trajectory from launch to ground contact in the same coordinates as the given data.

$$
\begin{aligned}
t_f &= \sqrt{\frac{2\cdot 125}{10}}\,\mathrm{s}\simeq 5\,\mathrm{s} \\[6pt]
R &= 6\cdot\sqrt{\frac{2\cdot125}{10}}\,\mathrm{m}\simeq 30\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
