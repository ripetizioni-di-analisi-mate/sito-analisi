A rotation has period $0.8\,\mathrm{s}$. How long does one quarter of a turn take?

- $0.2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.8\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.25\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

In uniform rotation equal fractions of a turn take equal fractions of the period. One quarter of the full period is $0.2\,\mathrm{s}$.

$$
\Delta t=\frac{0.8}{4}\,\mathrm{s}=0.2\,\mathrm{s}.
$$
