A uniform rotation has period $0.8\,\mathrm{s}$. How long does one quarter of a turn take?

- $0.2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.8\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.25\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

In uniform rotation, equal fractions of a turn take equal intervals. A quarter turn takes $\dfrac{0.8}{4}\,\mathrm s=0.2\,\mathrm s$. The radius affects the length of the arc, while the duration for any fraction of a turn is determined by the period.
