What are the units on a trajectory graph of $y$ against $x$?

- Metres on both axes
- Seconds on both axes
- Seconds horizontally and metres vertically
- Metres horizontally and metres per second vertically

## Explanation

A trajectory shows locations in space. A coordinate against time graph, by contrast, contains a time axis.
