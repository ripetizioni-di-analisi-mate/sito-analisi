Rotation direction is reversed at the same speed. At the same point what changes?

- Only the direction of centrifugal acceleration
- The radius becomes negative
- Velocity becomes radial
- The direction of tangent velocity, with acceleration still inward

## Explanation

The tangent is traversed in the opposite direction. Centripetal acceleration depends on radius and squared speed and still points towards the centre.

At the same point, the tangent line is the same. The velocity arrow reverses along that line. With the same radius and speed, $v^2/r$ and the inward direction both stay the same.
