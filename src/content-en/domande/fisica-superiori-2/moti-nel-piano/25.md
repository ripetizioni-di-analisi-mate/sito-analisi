A ball leaves a $15\,\mathrm{m}$ platform horizontally. After $1\,\mathrm{s}$ what is its height if $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and air resistance is negligible?

- $10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The drop in one second is five metres. Subtract that loss of height from the starting height.

$$
y=15-\frac12\cdot10\cdot1^2=10\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
