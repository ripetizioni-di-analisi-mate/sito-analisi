A wheel turns with period $3\,\mathrm{s}$ from a fixed starting point. After $10\,\mathrm{s}$ what fraction of a revolution has it moved beyond the last full turn? Choose the closest value.

- $0.66667$
- $3$
- $1$
- $0.33333$

## Explanation

Three complete revolutions take nine seconds. The remaining second is one third of the period.

$$
N-\lfloor N\rfloor=\frac{10}{3}-3=\frac13.
$$

Three full turns take $9\,\mathrm s$. The remaining second is a third of the three seconds per revolution. The phase has advanced by $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\,\mathrm{rad}$ from the start of the final turn.
