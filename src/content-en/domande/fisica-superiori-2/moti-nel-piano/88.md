In circular motion, how does the direction of average velocity over half a turn compare with that of the initial instantaneous velocity?

- Both are radial towards the centre
- Average velocity is along the chord, instantaneous velocity along the tangent
- Both give the same tangent at every point
- Average velocity follows the arc

## Explanation

The average uses the vector joining the endpoints, along a diameter over half a turn. Instantaneous velocity is parallel to the local tangent.
