A point is directly above the centre. Which way does centripetal acceleration point?

- Upwards
- Rightwards
- Downwards
- Leftwards

## Explanation

Inward acceleration points from the point to the centre, which is below it. Rotation direction changes the tangent direction, while this radius stays the same.

With the centre at the origin, the vertical coordinate of the highest point is $+r$. The vector from that point to the centre has a negative vertical component. Velocity is horizontal and its direction depends on the rotation.
