A point is directly above the centre. Which way does centripetal acceleration point?

- Upwards
- Rightwards
- Downwards
- Leftwards

## Explanation

Inward acceleration points from the point to the centre, which is below it. Rotation direction changes the tangent direction, while this radius stays the same.
