A point starts from $(2,1)$ with constant velocity $(3,-1)\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is its $y$ coordinate when $x=11\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $-3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The x equation gives the time of three seconds. Use that same duration in the y equation.

$$
t=\frac{11-2}{3}\,\mathrm{s}=3\,\mathrm{s},\qquad y=1-3=-2\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
