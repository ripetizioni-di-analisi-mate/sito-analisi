Doubling horizontal launch speed from a fixed height, how does range change without air resistance?

- It quadruples
- It stays the same
- It doubles
- It halves

## Explanation

Flight time depends on height and $g$. In the same time, double the horizontal speed covers twice the distance.

A ball that remains airborne for two seconds travels six metres at a horizontal speed of $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. At $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ it covers twelve metres. Changing the horizontal speed preserves the time needed to fall through the given height.
