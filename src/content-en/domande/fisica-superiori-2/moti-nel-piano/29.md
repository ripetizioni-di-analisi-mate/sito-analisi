A marble launched horizontally from a height of $10\,\mathrm{m}$ falls $5\,\mathrm{m}$ in $1\,\mathrm{s}$. Ignoring air resistance, how far has it fallen at $1.2\,\mathrm{s}$ while still in flight?

- $7.2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The vertical drop from rest varies with time squared. Multiplying the time by 1.2 multiplies the height change by 1.44.

$$
s=5\cdot1.2^2\,\mathrm{m}=7.2\,\mathrm{m}.
$$

The ratio of the times gives a factor $1.2^2=1.44$ for the drop. The five metres therefore grow to $7.2\,\mathrm m$. The remaining height of $2.8\,\mathrm m$ confirms that the ball has not reached the ground.
