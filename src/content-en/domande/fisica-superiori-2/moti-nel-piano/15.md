A robot moves across the floor from the point $(1;3)$ to the point $(4;7)$, with coordinates in metres. What is the magnitude of its displacement?

- $7\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The displacement components are the differences between corresponding coordinates, that is $4-1=3\,\mathrm m$ and $7-3=4\,\mathrm m$. The two components are perpendicular, so the magnitude follows from Pythagoras' theorem.

$$
d=\sqrt{(4-1)^2+(7-3)^2}\,\mathrm{m}=5\,\mathrm{m}.
$$

The magnitude of the displacement is therefore $5\,\mathrm m$. The coordinates of the final point alone give the robot's position relative to the origin instead.
