A robot moves from $(1,3)$ to $(4,7)$ in metres. What is its displacement magnitude?

- $7\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The legs are the coordinate differences, 3 m and 4 m. Pythagoras gives the magnitude of the vector joining the points.

$$
d=\sqrt{(4-1)^2+(7-3)^2}\,\mathrm{m}=5\,\mathrm{m}.
$$

The coordinates go from $(1;3)$ to $(4;7)$, so the displacement components are $3$ and $4\,\mathrm m$. The segment is $5\,\mathrm m$ long. The final coordinates alone would measure position relative to the origin.
