What does angular velocity in rad/s measure?

- Angle swept per unit time
- Length travelled per unit time
- Seconds per revolution
- The radius of the path

## Explanation

The numerator gives an angle. Multiply by radius to convert to speed along the circumference.

For example, $\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ means half a turn in one second. In two seconds the angle is $2\pi\,\mathrm{rad}$, one complete turn. The quantity describes angle and can be the same for points at different radii.
