A point $1.2\,\mathrm{m}$ from the centre moves at $4\pi\,\mathrm{m/s}$. How many turns does it complete in $6\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $5$
- $6$
- $12$
- $10$

## Explanation

Divide the distance travelled in six seconds by the circumference. The factor $\pi$ cancels.

$$
N=\frac{4\pi\cdot6}{2\pi\cdot1.2}=10.
$$
