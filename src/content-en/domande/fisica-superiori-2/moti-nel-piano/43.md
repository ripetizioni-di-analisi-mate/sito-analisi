A platform rotates at $\omega=3\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. What is $a_c$ at distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from the axis?

- $6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $36\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

With angular speed known, use its square times the radius directly. The result is a positive magnitude.

$$
a_c=3^2\cdot2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=18\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.
$$

At the stated radius, the speed is also $3\cdot2\,\mathrm{m/s}=6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The alternative calculation $\dfrac{6^2}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ gives $18\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The acceleration vector points towards the centre at every instant.
