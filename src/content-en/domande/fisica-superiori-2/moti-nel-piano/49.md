A ball starts horizontally at $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and negligible air, what is its speed after $1.2\,\mathrm{s}$ if still airborne?

- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $13\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $17\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The vertical component is −12 m/s and the horizontal component remains 5 m/s. The speed combines both through Pythagoras.

$$
u=\sqrt{5^2+(-10\cdot1.2)^2}\,\mathrm{m/s}=13\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
