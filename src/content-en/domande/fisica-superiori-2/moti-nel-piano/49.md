A ball starts horizontally at $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and negligible air, what is its speed after $1.2\,\mathrm{s}$ if still airborne?

- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $13\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $17\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

After $1.2\,\mathrm s$, the vertical component is $-12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the horizontal component is still $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The speed is $\sqrt{5^2+12^2}\,\mathrm{m/s}=13\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The vertical component’s sign shows downward motion. Both contributions to the square of speed are positive.
