A disk rotates uniformly about its centre. What is the tangential speed of the centre?

- It is zero
- It equals the rim speed
- It is maximal
- It diverges

## Explanation

The disk rotates about its centre. The centre stays in place and $r=0$ in $v=\omega r$.
