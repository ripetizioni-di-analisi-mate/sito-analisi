A disk rotates uniformly about its centre. What is the tangential speed of the centre?

- It is zero
- It equals the rim speed
- It is maximal
- It diverges

## Explanation

The centre lies on the axis of rotation and stays in the same position. In the formula $v=\omega r$ its radius is $r=0$, so its speed is zero. As the distance from the axis increases, the length of the arc travelled per turn also increases.
