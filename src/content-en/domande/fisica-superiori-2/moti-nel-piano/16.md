Speed is $13\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_x=5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The object moves north of the east axis. What is $v_y$, with north positive?

- $-12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Subtract the squared known component from the squared speed. The sign is positive because the vertical component points north.

$$
v_y=\sqrt{13^2-5^2}\,\mathrm{m/s}=12\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
