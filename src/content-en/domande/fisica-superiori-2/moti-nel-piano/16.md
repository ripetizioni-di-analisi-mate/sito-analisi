Speed is $13\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_x=5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The $x$ axis points east and the $y$ axis points north. The north–south component points north. What is $v_y$?

- $-12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

With perpendicular axes, the square of speed is the sum of the squares of the components. This gives $v_y^2=13^2-5^2=144$ in SI units. The two roots are $+12$ and $-12\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Since the $y$ axis and the component point north, choose $v_y=12\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
