The period changes from $T$ to $2T$. What effect does this have on frequency?

- It doubles
- It halves
- It quadruples
- It stays unchanged

## Explanation

Frequency is the reciprocal of period. Doubling the denominator halves the quotient.

Doubling the time per turn means completing half as many turns in the same time. For example, changing from $0.25$ to $0.50\,\mathrm s$ per turn changes the frequency from $4$ to $2\,\mathrm{Hz}$.
