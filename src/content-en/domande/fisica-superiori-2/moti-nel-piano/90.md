Two wheels of different radii complete the same number of turns in the same time. Which quantity matches?

- Distance travelled by the rim points
- Average tangential speed of the rim points
- Radius
- Average frequency

## Explanation

Average frequency divides the count by duration. The rim distances include the length of a revolution, which depends on radius.
