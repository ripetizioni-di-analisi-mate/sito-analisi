Two wheels of different radii complete the same number of turns in the same time. Which quantity matches?

- Distance travelled by the rim points
- Average tangential speed of the rim points
- Radius
- Average frequency

## Explanation

The average frequency is $f=\dfrac{N}{\Delta t}$, where $N$ is the number of revolutions and $\Delta t$ the elapsed time. The two values are the same for both wheels. The length of an individual turn depends on radius. Different radii therefore give different distances along the rim.
