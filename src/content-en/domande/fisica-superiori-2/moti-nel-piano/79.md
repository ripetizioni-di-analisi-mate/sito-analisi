At fixed speed, what change in $a_c$ is needed for a gentler bend?

- A greater magnitude
- A smaller magnitude
- No change
- A negative magnitude

## Explanation

A gentler bend has greater radius. Dividing the same squared speed by a larger radius produces a smaller value.

For example, at $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ a radius of $6\,\mathrm m$ gives $6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Taking a curve of radius $12\,\mathrm m$ at the same speed halves the result. The numerator $v^2$ is the same.
