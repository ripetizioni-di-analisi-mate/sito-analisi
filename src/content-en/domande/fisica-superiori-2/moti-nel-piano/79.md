At fixed speed, what change in $a_c$ is needed for a gentler bend?

- A greater magnitude
- A smaller magnitude
- No change
- A negative magnitude

## Explanation

A gentler bend has greater radius. Dividing the same squared speed by a larger radius produces a smaller value.
