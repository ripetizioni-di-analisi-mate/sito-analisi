A wheel rotates steadily at $120$ revolutions per minute. A camera takes $30$ evenly spaced photographs per second. How many intervals between consecutive photographs last one revolution?

- $2$
- $10$
- $15$
- $60$

## Explanation

Dividing by $60$ gives $f=2\,\mathrm{Hz}$. One revolution therefore takes $\dfrac12\,\mathrm s$. There are thirty camera intervals per second, so fifteen elapse in half a second. The requested count is the time intervals between exposures. Counting both the starting and ending photographs would add one image to that interval count.
