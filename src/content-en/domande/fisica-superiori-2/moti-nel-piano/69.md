A tachometer reads revolutions per minute. Which conversion gives frequency in hertz?

- Multiply by 60
- Divide by $2\pi$ only
- Divide by 60
- Change only the unit symbol

## Explanation

The revolutions over a minute are distributed across its 60 seconds. Hertz gives the count for one second.
