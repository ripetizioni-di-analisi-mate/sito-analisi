What angle do instantaneous velocity and centripetal acceleration form in uniform rotation?

- $0^\circ$
- $90^\circ$
- $180^\circ$
- $45^\circ$ at every point

## Explanation

Velocity is tangent, and acceleration is radial. The tangent is perpendicular to the radius at the point.

The tangent to the circle is perpendicular to the radius at the point of contact. Velocity follows the tangent and centripetal acceleration follows the radius towards the centre. Reversing the rotation preserves perpendicularity.
