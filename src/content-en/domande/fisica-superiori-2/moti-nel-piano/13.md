A wheel makes 72 revolutions in $6\,\mathrm{s}$ at constant frequency. What is the frequency in hertz?

- $12\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $6\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $24\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

The duration used is $6\,\mathrm{s}$. Hertz counts revolutions in one second, so divide the number of revolutions by that duration. This gives $12\,\mathrm{Hz}$. Multiplying that figure by $6\,\mathrm{s}$ recovers the 72 observed revolutions. The period would instead be duration divided by revolutions.

$$
\begin{aligned}
f &= \frac{N}{\Delta t} = \frac{72}{6}\,\mathrm{Hz} \\[6pt]
&\simeq 12\,\mathrm{Hz}
\end{aligned}
$$
