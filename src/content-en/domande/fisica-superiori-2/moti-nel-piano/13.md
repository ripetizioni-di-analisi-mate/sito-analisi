A reference radius on a uniformly rotating wheel sweeps through an angle of magnitude $6\pi\,\mathrm{rad}$ in $0.25\,\mathrm s$. What is the frequency?

- $12\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $3\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $6\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $24\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

Each turn corresponds to $2\pi\,\mathrm{rad}$, so $\dfrac{6\pi}{2\pi}=3$ is the number of turns. The measurement took a quarter of a second, so the frequency is $\dfrac{3}{0.25}\,\mathrm{Hz}=12\,\mathrm{Hz}$. Convert the angle in radians to turns before expressing the count per second.
