A point rotates with frequency $4\,\mathrm{Hz}$. What is its angular speed? Choose the closest value.

- $4\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $25.133\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $12.566\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $50.265\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

Each revolution changes the angle by $2\pi$ radians. Multiply by four revolutions in one second to find the angular rate.

$$
\omega=2\pi\cdot4\,\mathrm{rad/s}=8\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}.
$$

Four turns in one second correspond to four angles of $2\pi\,\mathrm{rad}$. This gives $8\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. To check, keeping that rate for a quarter of a second gives an angle of $2\pi\,\mathrm{rad}$.
