What remains constant along $x$ during a horizontal launch without air resistance?

- The horizontal coordinate
- The horizontal velocity component
- Total speed
- The vertical velocity component

## Explanation

Gravity acts vertically in the model. Acceleration along x is zero, so velocity along x is unchanged.

The horizontal acceleration component is zero. The ball therefore covers equal horizontal distances in equal times. Gravity changes the vertical component, so the magnitude of the full velocity can vary.
