To locate an object using two position equations, which readings are combined?

- Initial $x$ and final $y$
- $x$ and $y$ at the same instant
- Only the two initial coordinates
- Horizontal velocity and vertical coordinate

## Explanation

The two coordinates must describe the same instantaneous state. Readings at different times generally do not give an occupied position.

With $x(t)=2+t$ and $y(t)=3t$ in SI units, the position at $t=1\,\mathrm s$ is $(3;3)\,\mathrm m$. Pairing $x(1)$ with $y(2)$ would use one coordinate from each of two different times.
