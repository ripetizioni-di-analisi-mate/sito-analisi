A boat moves at $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ towards the opposite bank and drifts at $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ along the bank. The river is $24\,\mathrm{m}$ wide. How far downstream does it drift during the crossing?

- $8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $24\,\mathrm{m}$
- $36\,\mathrm{m}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The cross-river component takes 8 s to cover the width. In the same time the component along the bank produces the downstream displacement.

$$
d_\parallel=2\cdot\frac{24}{3}\,\mathrm{m}=16\,\mathrm{m}.
$$

The across-river component of $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ determines the eight seconds needed to cross the river. In that time the current carries the boat $2\cdot8=16\,\mathrm m$ downstream. This drift accumulates throughout the crossing.
