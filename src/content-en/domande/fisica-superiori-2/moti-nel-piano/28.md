The vertical velocity of a ball launched horizontally reaches $-12\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What time has elapsed with $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and air resistance ignored?

- $0.83333\,\mathrm{s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{s}$
- $-1.2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The component changes by −10 m/s per second and starts from zero. The quotient of the two negative values is positive.

$$
t=\frac{-12}{-10}\,\mathrm{s}=1.2\,\mathrm{s}.
$$

With up as positive, the component changes by $-10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ every second. Going from zero to $-12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ thus takes a little over one second. The time is positive because the quotient uses two negative quantities.
