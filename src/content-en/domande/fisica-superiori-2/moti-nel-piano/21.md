A motion has $x=3t$, $y=2t^2$ in SI units. What is $y$ when $x=6\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

From $x=6\,\mathrm{m}$ it follows that $t=2\,\mathrm{s}$. Square that time in the second equation.

$$
y=2\cdot2^2\,\mathrm{m}=8\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
