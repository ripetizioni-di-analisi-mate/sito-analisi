Two balls are launched simultaneously and horizontally from the same height with different horizontal speeds and reach the same floor. Which arrives first, neglecting air?

- The one with the higher initial speed
- The one with the lower initial speed
- They arrive at the same time
- Necessarily the heavier one

## Explanation

The two balls start with zero vertical velocity and have the same vertical acceleration. They reach a floor at the same height after equal times. If that time is two seconds, horizontal speeds of $2$ and $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ yield ranges of $4$ and $10\,\mathrm m$.
