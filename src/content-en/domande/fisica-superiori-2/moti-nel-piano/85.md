An experiment launches two balls from the same height and the faster horizontal launch stays in the air longer. Which check directly probes the chapter model?

- Divide both times by mass
- Include x velocity in the unit of $g$
- Check that both start horizontally and land at the same level
- Count the spins after impact

## Explanation

A horizontal launch and equal height drop ensure the same vertical equation. A change in these data can change flight time.
