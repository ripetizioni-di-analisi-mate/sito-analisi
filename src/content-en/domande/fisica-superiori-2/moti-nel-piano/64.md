How do the $x(t)$ graph and the trajectory differ in a horizontal launch with $g>0$ and $u_0>0$?

- Both are straight lines
- Both are arcs of a parabola
- The first is a straight line, the second an arc of a parabola
- The first is a circle and the second a straight line

## Explanation

The horizontal motion is uniform. The trajectory also includes height, which is quadratic in time and therefore in the x coordinate.

In $x(t)$ the horizontal axis measures time. The trajectory instead uses horizontal position. Substituting $t=\dfrac{x}{u_0}$ into the vertical equation produces an $x^2$ term and hence a parabolic curve.
