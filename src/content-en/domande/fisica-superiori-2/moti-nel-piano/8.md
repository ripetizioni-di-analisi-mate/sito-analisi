An object moves around a circle of radius $6\,\mathrm{m}$ at constant speed $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the magnitude of the centripetal acceleration? Choose the closest value.

- $4.08\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $16.3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-8.17\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $8.17\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

At constant speed the velocity vector still changes direction. Acceleration points towards the centre and has magnitude equal to speed squared divided by radius. The squared speed from the data is $49\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}$. Dividing by $6\,\mathrm{m}$ gives $8.17\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Check that a length factor cancels and leaves metres per second squared.

$$
\begin{aligned}
a_c&=\frac{v^2}{r}=\frac{7^2}{6}\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \\[6pt]
&\simeq 8.17\,\mathrm{m/s^2}
\end{aligned}
$$
