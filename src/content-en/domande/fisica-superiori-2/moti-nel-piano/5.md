A wheel makes $60$ revolutions in $6\,\mathrm s$ at a constant rate. What is its frequency?

- $10\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $5\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $20\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $-10\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

The duration used is $6\,\mathrm{s}$. Hertz counts revolutions in one second, so divide the number of revolutions by that duration. This gives $10\,\mathrm{Hz}$. Multiplying that figure by $6\,\mathrm{s}$ recovers the 60 observed revolutions. The period would instead be duration divided by revolutions.

$$
\begin{aligned}
f &= \frac{N}{\Delta t} = \frac{60}{6}\,\mathrm{Hz} \\[6pt]
&\simeq 10\,\mathrm{Hz}
\end{aligned}
$$
