A horizontal launch at $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ gives a range of $9\,\mathrm{m}$. Use $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and neglect air. What was the launch height?

- $15\,\mathrm{m}$
- $30\,\mathrm{m}$
- $90\,\mathrm{m}$
- $45\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The horizontal component gives 3 s. Use that time for the vertical drop from rest.

$$
h=\frac12\cdot 10\left(\frac93\right)^2\,\mathrm{m}=45\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
