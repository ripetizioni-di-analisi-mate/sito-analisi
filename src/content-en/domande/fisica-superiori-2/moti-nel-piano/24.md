A ball leaves a $20\,\mathrm m$ high ledge horizontally at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. A horizontal platform at height $15\,\mathrm m$ extends from $x=3\,\mathrm m$ to $x=6\,\mathrm m$, measured from the vertical line through the launch. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and negligible air resistance, at which horizontal coordinate does the ball first contact a surface?

- $3\,\mathrm m$
- $6\,\mathrm m$
- $8\,\mathrm m$
- $4\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The height falls by $5\,\mathrm m$ at $\sqrt{\dfrac{2\cdot5}{10}}\,\mathrm s=1\,\mathrm s$. The ball is then $4\,\mathrm m$ from the launch vertical. That coordinate lies over the platform because it is between $3$ and $6\,\mathrm m$. The ball hits the platform before the floor. The range would be $8\,\mathrm m$ without the platform.
