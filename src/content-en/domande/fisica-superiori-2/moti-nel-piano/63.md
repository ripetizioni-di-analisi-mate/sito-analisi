At fixed initial speed the launch height is quadrupled. How does range on the same level floor change, ignoring air?

- It doubles
- It quadruples
- It stays the same
- It halves

## Explanation

Fall time is proportional to the square root of height. Range is proportional to that time because horizontal speed is constant.
