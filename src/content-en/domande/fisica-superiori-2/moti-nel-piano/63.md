At fixed initial speed the launch height is quadrupled. How does range on the same level floor change, ignoring air?

- It doubles
- It quadruples
- It stays the same
- It halves

## Explanation

Fall time is proportional to the square root of height. Range is proportional to that time because horizontal speed is constant.

The fall time contains the square root of the height, so the factor of four becomes a factor of two. The horizontal speed is the same and the range grows in the same proportion as the time.
