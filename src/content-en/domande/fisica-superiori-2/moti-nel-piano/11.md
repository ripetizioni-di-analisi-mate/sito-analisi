A ball is launched horizontally from the vertical line $x=0$. After $2\,\mathrm s$ a camera records $x=14\,\mathrm m$ and height $160\,\mathrm m$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and negligible air resistance, how far horizontally from the launch does it reach the ground at height zero?

- $14\,\mathrm m$
- $28\,\mathrm m$
- $42\,\mathrm m$
- $84\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The horizontal reading gives $u_0=\dfrac{14}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s}=7\,\mathrm{m/s}$. In two seconds the height drops by $\dfrac12\cdot10\cdot2^2\,\mathrm m=20\,\mathrm m$, so the starting height was $180\,\mathrm m$. The vertical equation gives $t_f=\sqrt{\dfrac{2\cdot180}{10}}\,\mathrm s=6\,\mathrm s$. Multiplying the horizontal velocity by the entire flight time gives $R=42\,\mathrm m$.
