For a point $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from the centre the frequency is $0.5\,\mathrm{Hz}$. What is the centripetal acceleration? Choose the closest value.

- $3.14\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $4.93\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $39.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $9.87\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The frequency corresponds to $\pi$ radians per second. Square this value and multiply by the radius.

$$
a_c=(2\pi\cdot0.5)^2\cdot1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=\pi^2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.
$$

Half a revolution per second corresponds to $\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. Since $r=1\,\mathrm m$, squaring the angular speed gives the numerical value $\pi^2$. The closest option expresses this acceleration in metres per second squared.
