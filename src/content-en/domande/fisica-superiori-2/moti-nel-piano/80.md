At fixed angular speed, how does $a_c$ change towards the rim of a disk?

- It increases with radius
- It decreases with radius
- It stays fixed
- It reverses sign every half turn

## Explanation

In $\omega^2r$ the angular factor is fixed. The product increases with radius.
