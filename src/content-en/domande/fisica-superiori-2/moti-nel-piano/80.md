At fixed angular speed, how does $a_c$ change towards the rim of a disk?

- It increases with radius
- It decreases with radius
- It stays fixed
- It reverses sign every half turn

## Explanation

In $\omega^2r$ the angular factor is fixed. The product increases with radius.

The relation $a_c=\omega^2r$ shows that at fixed angular speed, doubling the radius doubles the acceleration. The tangential speed also increases, because the outer point covers a longer arc in the same time.
