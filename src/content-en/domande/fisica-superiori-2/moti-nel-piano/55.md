In orthonormal Cartesian axes, what is preserved if the two components of a vector are interchanged?

- Its magnitude
- Its direction in every case
- Its quadrant in every case
- Its direction along x in every case

## Explanation

The sum of squares is unchanged by the swap. The ordered components determine the orientation.

For example, $(3;-2)$ and $(-2;3)$ both have magnitude $\sqrt{13}$. Swapping changes the arrow's quadrant in this case. The sum of the squares stays the same because their order does not matter.
