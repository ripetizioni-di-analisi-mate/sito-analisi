An object follows a circular path at $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ with $a_c=16\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the radius?

- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Isolate the radius in the centripetal formula. The numerator is speed squared, in $\mathrm{m^2/s^2}$.

$$
r=\frac{8^2}{16}\,\mathrm{m}=4\,\mathrm{m}.
$$

The radius is $4\,\mathrm m$. At that distance the angular speed is $\dfrac{8}{4}\,\mathrm{rad/s}=2\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. The alternative relation $\omega^2r$ gives $16\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and checks both the arithmetic and the unit.
