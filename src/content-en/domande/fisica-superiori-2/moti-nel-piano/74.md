After one full turn, what is true of distance and displacement?

- Both are zero
- Both equal the circumference
- Distance is zero and displacement is the radius
- Distance is the circumference and the displacement vector is zero

## Explanation

The point returns to its starting position, so the coordinate difference is zero. It has travelled the entire circle in the meantime.

The endpoint coincides with the starting point, so the displacement vector is zero. The point still covers every part of the circumference, giving distance $2\pi r$. The average velocity over the turn is zero.
