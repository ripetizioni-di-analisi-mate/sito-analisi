At the same radius two points have frequencies $3\,\mathrm{Hz}$ and $1\,\mathrm{Hz}$. What is the ratio $\dfrac{a_{c1}}{a_{c2}}$?

- $3$
- $9$
- $0.33333$
- $6$

## Explanation

At fixed radius acceleration is proportional to frequency squared. The factor of three must be squared.

$$
\frac{a_{c1}}{a_{c2}}=\left(\frac31\right)^2=9.
$$
