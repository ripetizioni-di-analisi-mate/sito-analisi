Two point particles $A$ and $B$ move in the plane according to $A(t)=(2+t;\,1+2t)$ and $B(t)=(11-2t;\,10-t)$, with coordinates in metres and time in seconds. At what time do the two particles meet?

- $1\,\mathrm s$
- $3\,\mathrm s$
- $4\,\mathrm s$
- $9\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The two particles meet when both coordinates coincide at the same time. Equating the horizontal coordinates gives $2+t=11-2t$, so $3t=9$ and $t=3\,\mathrm s$. At that time the vertical coordinates are $1+2\cdot3=7$ and $10-3=7$, so they also coincide. At $t=3\,\mathrm s$ both particles are therefore at $(5;7)$, in metres.
