A marble starts horizontally at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ from a height of $25\,\mathrm{m}$. Ignoring air with $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is its height when $x=8\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The x coordinate gives two seconds of flight. Height falls by twenty metres in that interval.

$$
y=25-\frac12\cdot10\left(\frac84\right)^2=5\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
