Two robots with a common clock follow $A(t)=(2+t;1+2t)$ and $B(t)=(11-2t;10-t)$, in SI units. When do they meet?

- $1\,\mathrm s$
- $3\,\mathrm s$
- $4\,\mathrm s$
- $9\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

Equating horizontal coordinates gives $2+t=11-2t$, so $t=3\,\mathrm s$. The vertical coordinates must coincide at that same time. Here $1+2\cdot3=10-3=7$, in metres. Both robots are at $(5;7)\,\mathrm m$. One equal coordinate would only put them on the same line in the plane and still require checking the second.
