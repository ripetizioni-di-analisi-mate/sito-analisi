A disk rotates uniformly and has four equally spaced marks around its rim. A sensor produces a pulse as each mark passes. Consecutive pulses are $0.12\,\mathrm s$ apart. How long does a complete revolution take?

- $0.12\,\mathrm s$
- $0.24\,\mathrm s$
- $0.03\,\mathrm s$
- $0.48\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

Between consecutive marks the disk turns through a quarter of a revolution. Seeing the same mark again takes four pulse intervals, so $T=4\cdot0.12\,\mathrm s=0.48\,\mathrm s$. The four marks produce four pulses per turn. One interval measures the time from one mark to the next.
