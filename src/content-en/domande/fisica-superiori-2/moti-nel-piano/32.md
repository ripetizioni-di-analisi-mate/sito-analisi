Eight revolutions take $12\,\mathrm{s}$ at uniform rotation. How long does one revolution last?

- $0.66667\,\mathrm{s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

All revolutions take the same time. Divide the total time by eight to get the period.

$$
T=\frac{12}{8}\,\mathrm{s}=1.5\,\mathrm{s}.
$$
