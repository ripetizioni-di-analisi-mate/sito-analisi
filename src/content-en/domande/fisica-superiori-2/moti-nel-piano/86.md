A vector $\vec v$ has $v_y=0$ and $v_x<0$. What direction and magnitude does it have?

- Positive x direction, magnitude $v_x$
- Positive y direction, magnitude zero
- Negative x direction, magnitude $-v_x$
- Negative x direction, magnitude $v_x$

## Explanation

The only non-zero component is negative along x. Its magnitude is its absolute value, so $-v_x>0$.
