A wheel has constant frequency $2.5\,\mathrm{Hz}$. How many revolutions does it make in $12\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $24$
- $12$
- $30$
- $4.8$

## Explanation

Frequency counts revolutions per second. Multiplying by 12 s turns the rate into the total count.

$$
N=2.5\cdot12=30.
$$

The wheel makes five turns every two seconds, consistent with the given frequency. Twelve seconds contain six two-second intervals, so $6\cdot5=30$ turns are completed. The result is a count without units.
