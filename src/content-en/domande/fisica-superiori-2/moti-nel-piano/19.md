In SI units $x=4t$ and $y=3+2t$. What is the displacement magnitude from $t=1\,\mathrm{s}$ to $t=3\,\mathrm{s}$? Choose the closest value.

- $12\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8.94\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The duration is 2 s for each component. The initial offset along y cancels when taking the difference between endpoints.

$$
d=\sqrt{(4\cdot2)^2+(2\cdot2)^2}\,\mathrm{m}=\sqrt{80}\,\mathrm{m}.
$$

Over the two seconds the horizontal coordinate grows by $8\,\mathrm m$ and the vertical one by $4\,\mathrm m$. The magnitude is $\sqrt{80}\,\mathrm m$. The constant $3$ in the vertical law shifts both positions by the same amount and cancels in the displacement.
