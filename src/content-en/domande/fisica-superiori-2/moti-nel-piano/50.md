With east and north positive, what direction do $v_x<0$ and $v_y>0$ indicate?

- North-east
- North-west
- South-west
- South-east

## Explanation

The x component points west and the y component north. The diagonal lies in the quadrant containing both.

The negative horizontal component means instantaneous motion towards the west. The positive vertical component means north. A pair such as $(-3;4)\,\mathrm{m/s}$ has those two signs and points into the north-west quadrant.
