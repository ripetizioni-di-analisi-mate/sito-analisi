Two marks on a disk are $0.2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $0.6\,\mathrm{m}$ from the centre. The first has speed $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the second speed?

- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.67\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The disk has the same angular speed at both points. Tangential speed is proportional to distance from the centre.

$$
u_2=2\frac{0.6}{0.2}\,\mathrm{m/s}=6\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$

The second point is three times farther from the centre. Both sweep the same angle each second, so the second covers three times the arc length. One can also find $\omega=\dfrac{2}{0.2}\,\mathrm{rad/s}=10\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ and multiply by $0.6\,\mathrm m$.
