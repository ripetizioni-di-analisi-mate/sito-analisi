An object has zero acceleration along x and negative acceleration along y. What follows?

- Both velocity components stay fixed
- The x coordinate is always zero
- Speed is always constant
- The component $v_x$ is constant and $v_y$ decreases

## Explanation

Acceleration specifies separate changes. Along x the change is zero, while along y it is towards decreasing coordinates.

A negative acceleration along $y$ reduces that component of velocity. If the component was initially positive, it can still describe upward motion for a while. The sign of acceleration describes the change in velocity.
