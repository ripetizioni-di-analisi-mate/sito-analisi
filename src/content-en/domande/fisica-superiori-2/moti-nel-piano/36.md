A wheel of radius $0.4\,\mathrm{m}$ makes half a turn. What is the length of the arc travelled by a point on the rim? Choose the closest value.

- $2.5133\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.62832\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.2566\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

A half turn has angle $\pi$ radians. Multiply it by the radius to find the arc length.

$$
s=0.4\pi\,\mathrm{m}=\frac{2\pi}{5}\,\mathrm{m}.
$$

This length is measured along the rim. The endpoints are separated by the diameter, $0.8\,\mathrm m$. The required arc is half the circumference, $0.4\pi\,\mathrm m$, about $1.2566\,\mathrm m$.
