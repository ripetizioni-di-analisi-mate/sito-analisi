A first point moves along a curve at $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ with radius $2\,\mathrm m$. A second moves at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ with radius $8\,\mathrm m$. What is the ratio $a_{c2}/a_{c1}$?

- $4$
- $2$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $1$

## Explanation

The second point has twice the speed, so the square in the numerator increases by a factor of four. The denominator radius increases by the same factor. The acceleration ratio is one. Calculating the magnitudes separately gives $\dfrac{2^2}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and $\dfrac{4^2}{8}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, both equal to $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
