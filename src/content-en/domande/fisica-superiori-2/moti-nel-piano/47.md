A point traces an arc at $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ with radius $2\,\mathrm{m}$. A second moves at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ with radius $8\,\mathrm{m}$. What is $a_{c2}$?

- $1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The second speed is double and the radius four times as large. Both numerator and denominator of the acceleration formula increase by a factor of four.

$$
a_{c2}=\frac{4^2}{8}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.
$$
