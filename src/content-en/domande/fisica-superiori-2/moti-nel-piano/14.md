A point on the rim of a wheel in uniform circular motion starts at a mark. It first reaches the diametrically opposite point after $\dfrac{1}{12}\,\mathrm s$. How long does one complete turn take?

- $\dfrac{1}{12}\,\mathrm s$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}\,\mathrm s$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}\,\mathrm s$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The first passage through the opposite point completes half a turn. At constant angular velocity the second half takes the same time. The period is therefore $2\cdot\dfrac{1}{12}\,\mathrm s=\dfrac{1}{6}\,\mathrm s$. The first-passage condition rules out any complete turns before that time.
