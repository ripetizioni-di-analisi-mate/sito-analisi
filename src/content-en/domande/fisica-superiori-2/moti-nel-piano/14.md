A platform rotates at constant frequency $6\,\mathrm{Hz}$. How long does it take to complete one revolution? Choose the closest value.

- $0.33333\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.16667\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.083333\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The point makes 6 revolutions per second. The time for one revolution is the reciprocal $0.16667\,\mathrm{s}$. With constant frequency, each revolution takes the same time. Multiply the period by 1 for the requested number of revolutions. Duration times frequency recovers that count.

$$
\begin{aligned}
T &= \frac1{6}\,\mathrm{s}\simeq 0.16667\,\mathrm{s} \\[6pt]
\Delta t &= 1\cdot \frac1{6}\,\mathrm{s}\simeq 0.16667\,\mathrm{s}
\end{aligned}
$$
