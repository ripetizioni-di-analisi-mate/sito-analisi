Just before an impact, with right and up positive, a ball has $v_x=6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_y=-8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Which description gives the velocity's direction and inclination from the horizontal?

- Right and above the horizontal
- Right and below the horizontal by less than $45^\circ$
- Right and below the horizontal by more than $45^\circ$
- Left and below the horizontal

## Explanation

The sign of $v_x$ gives right and the sign of $v_y$ gives down. The arrow tilts by more than $45^\circ$ from the horizontal because the magnitude of its vertical component is $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and its horizontal component is $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. At $45^\circ$ the projections would be equally long. The signs and the comparison of magnitudes answer different parts of the question.
