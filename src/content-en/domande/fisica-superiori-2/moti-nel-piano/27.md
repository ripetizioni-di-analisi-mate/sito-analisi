At impact the velocity components are $v_x=6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_y=-8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the impact speed?

- $14\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The negative sign gives the downward direction. The magnitude combines the two perpendicular legs through their squares.

$$
u=\sqrt{6^2+(-8)^2}\,\mathrm{m/s}=10\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
