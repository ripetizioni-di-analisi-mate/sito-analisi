A hiker moves $10\,\mathrm{m}$ east, $8\,\mathrm{m}$ south and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ west. How far is she from the start?

- $10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $14\,\mathrm{m}$
- $22\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

First combine the east and west legs along their common axis. The legs of the resultant diagonal measure 6 m and 8 m.

$$
d=\sqrt{(10-4)^2+(-8)^2}\,\mathrm{m}=10\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
