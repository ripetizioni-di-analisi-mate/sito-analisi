A marble leaves horizontally at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ from a height of $20\,\mathrm{m}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and negligible air, when does it hit the ground?

- $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The vertical component starts at zero. Height determines the flight time irrespective of velocity along x.

$$
t=\sqrt{\frac{2\cdot20}{10}}\,\mathrm{s}=2\,\mathrm{s}.
$$
