For a path of radius $9\,\mathrm{m}$, $a_c=4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the speed?

- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $36\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.66667\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Multiply acceleration by radius to obtain speed squared. Its positive square root is measured in m/s.

$$
u=\sqrt{4\cdot9}\,\mathrm{m/s}=6\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$

The product of radius and acceleration is $36\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}$. Its nonnegative square root is $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Speed is a magnitude. The direction around the circle would require additional information.
