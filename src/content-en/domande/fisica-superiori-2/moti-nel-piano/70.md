In anticlockwise circular motion a point is to the right of the centre. Which way does its instantaneous velocity point?

- Towards the centre
- Upwards along the tangent
- Downwards along the tangent
- Radially outwards

## Explanation

The tangent is vertical at that point. Anticlockwise motion leads from the right side of the circle towards its upper half.
