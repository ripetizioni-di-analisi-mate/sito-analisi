With perpendicular axes and both $v_x,v_y$ non-zero, how do $\lvert\vec v\rvert$ and $\lvert v_x\rvert$ compare?

- The total magnitude is smaller
- They are always equal
- The total magnitude is greater
- It depends on the sign of $v_y$

## Explanation

A strictly positive square is added to the square of $v_x$. The square root is greater than the magnitude of $v_x$.
