At the instant of horizontal launch, what vertical velocity component is assigned?

- Zero
- $-g$
- $u_0$
- It depends on mass

## Explanation

The initial vector is horizontal and has zero vertical projection. Gravity generates a vertical component after launch.

The initial arrow is horizontal. The vertical component then changes with constant acceleration. With up positive and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, after one second in flight the component is already $-10\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
