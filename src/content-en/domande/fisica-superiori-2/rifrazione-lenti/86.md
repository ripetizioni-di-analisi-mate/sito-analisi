A virtual image produced by a converging lens has magnification $+3$ for $p=12\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find focal length.

- $18\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $9\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $24\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-18\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Use $q=-Mp$ to obtain the signed image distance from the positive magnification. Keep the sign of $q$ when calculating the focal length. The result is positive and exceeds the object distance, as required for a converging lens that produces an enlarged virtual image.

$$
q=-36\,\mathrm{cm},\qquad f=\frac{12(-36)}{12-36}\,\mathrm{cm}=18\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
