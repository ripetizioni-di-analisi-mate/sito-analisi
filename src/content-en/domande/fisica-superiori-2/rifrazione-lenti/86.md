A virtual image produced by a converging lens has magnification $+3$ for $p=12\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find focal length.

- $18\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $9\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $24\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-18\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

For the thin lens use signed distances in the same unit. The sign of $q$ gives the side and the sign of $M$ gives orientation.

$$
q=-36\,\mathrm{cm},\qquad f=\frac{12(-36)}{12-36}\,\mathrm{cm}=18\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
