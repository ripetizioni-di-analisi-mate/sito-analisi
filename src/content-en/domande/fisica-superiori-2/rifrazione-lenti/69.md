For a thin lens q is positive. Which side can hold a screen to receive the image?

- The same side as the real object.
- Any plane gives the same focus.
- Neither side, since positive q indicates a virtual image.
- The side opposite the real object in the image plane.

## Explanation

In the convention positive q is a physical meeting of rays on the output side. A screen in that plane receives the beam.
