Which situation calls for caution with the thin-lens paraxial model?

- A thick lens and rays far from the axis.
- A thin lens and rays near the axis.
- Negligible thickness compared with other distances.
- The same medium on both sides and small angles.

## Explanation

A thick lens physically separates the two refracting surfaces. A ray far from the axis can also meet them at angles too large for the paraxial model. Using one plane in those cases can predict the image position poorly.
