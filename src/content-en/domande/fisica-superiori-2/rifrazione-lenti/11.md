A protractor laid along a glass surface reads $50^\circ$ between the surface and an incident ray in air. The indices of air and glass are $1.00$ and $1.50$. What is the angle of refraction from the normal? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $30.71^\circ$
- $40^\circ$
- $25.374^\circ$
- $50^\circ$

## Explanation

The $50^\circ$ reading uses the surface as its reference. The complementary angle of $40^\circ$ is measured from the normal. Use this second value in Snell’s law to get $\theta_2=\arcsin\!\left(\dfrac{\sin40^\circ}{1.50}\right)\simeq25.374^\circ$. Using the first value directly would give a ray direction different from the one measured.
