Light goes from air with $n_1=1$ into glass with $n_2=1.5$. The angle of incidence from the normal is $40^\circ$. What is the refracted angle? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $40{}^\circ$.
- $50.748{}^\circ$.
- $25.374{}^\circ$.
- $12.687{}^\circ$.

## Explanation

Angles are measured from the normal. In Snell’s law isolate the sine of $\theta_2$, then take the inverse sine. The next medium has a larger index, so the refracted angle is smaller than the incident angle.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\theta_2 &= \frac{180 \arcsin{\left(\frac{n_1 \sin{\left(\frac{\pi \alpha}{180} \right)}}{n_2} \right)}}{\pi} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{180 \arcsin{\left(\frac{\left(1\right) \sin{\left(\frac{\pi \left(40\right)}{180} \right)}}{\left(1.5\right)} \right)}}{\pi} \\[6pt]
&\simeq 25.374{}^\circ
\end{aligned}
$$
