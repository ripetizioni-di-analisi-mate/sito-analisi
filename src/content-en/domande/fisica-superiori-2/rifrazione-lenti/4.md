Light goes from a material of refractive index $1.8$ into air of refractive index $1$. What is the critical angle, measured from the normal in the incident medium? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $56.3^\circ$.
- $16.9^\circ$.
- $90.0^\circ$.
- $33.7^\circ$.

## Explanation

The initial index is larger. The critical angle is the incidence yielding a limiting ray along the interface. Set $\sin\theta_2=1$ in Snell’s law and obtain $\theta_c=\arcsin(\dfrac{n_2}{n_1})$.

The index ratio is about $0.556$. Read its inverse sine in degrees. At larger incidence angles the law would require a transmitted sine above one.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\sin\theta_c &= \frac{n_2}{n_1} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{1}{1.8}\simeq 0.556 \\[6pt]
\theta_c &= \arcsin(0.556)\simeq 33.7^\circ
\end{aligned}
$$
