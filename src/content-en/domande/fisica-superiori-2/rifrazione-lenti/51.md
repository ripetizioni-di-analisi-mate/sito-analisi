The same light enters glass of higher index than the incident medium. What happens to speed and wavelength?

- Both increase.
- Speed decreases and wavelength increases.
- Both decrease.
- Speed increases and wavelength is unchanged.

## Explanation

A higher index means a shorter distance travelled in one second. Since the number of cycles per second is unchanged, the distance travelled during a single cycle also decreases. That distance is the wavelength.
