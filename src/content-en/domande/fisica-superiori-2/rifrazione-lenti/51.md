The same light enters glass of higher index than the incident medium. What happens to speed and wavelength?

- Both increase.
- Speed decreases and wavelength increases.
- Both decrease.
- Speed increases and wavelength is unchanged.

## Explanation

Speed varies inversely with index. With frequency unchanged, wavelength follows speed and also decreases.
