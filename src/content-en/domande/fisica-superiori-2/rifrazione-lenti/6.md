A thin diverging lens has focal length $f=-9\,\mathrm{cm}$ and a real object at $p=27\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the signed image distance? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $6.75\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $-6.75\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $-13.5\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $-3.375\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

A diverging lens has $f<0$. Subtracting $1/p>0$ from $1/f<0$ gives $1/q<0$. The image point is on the object side and is virtual.

$$
\begin{aligned}
q &= \frac{f p}{- f + p} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{\left(-9\right) \left(27\right)}{- \left(-9\right) + \left(27\right)} \\[6pt]
&\simeq -6.75\,\mathrm{cm}
\end{aligned}
$$
