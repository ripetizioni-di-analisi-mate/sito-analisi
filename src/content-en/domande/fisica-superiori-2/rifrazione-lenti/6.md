A real point source is $27\,\mathrm{cm}$ from a diverging lens in air. The focal length has magnitude $9\,\mathrm{cm}$. Set the lens centre at coordinate zero and orient the axis in the direction of light. What is the image coordinate? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $6.75\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $-6.75\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $-13.5\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $-3.375\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

A diverging lens has $f<0$. Subtracting $\dfrac{1}{p}>0$ from $\dfrac{1}{f}<0$ gives $\dfrac{1}{q}<0$. The image point is on the object side and is virtual.

The magnitude of the image distance is below $9\,\mathrm{cm}$, so the virtual image is between the lens centre and the focus on the object side. A positive value would instead indicate outgoing rays meeting.

$$
\begin{aligned}
q &= \frac{f p}{- f + p} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{\left(-9\right) \left(27\right)}{- \left(-9\right) + \left(27\right)} \\[6pt]
&\simeq -6.75\,\mathrm{cm}
\end{aligned}
$$
