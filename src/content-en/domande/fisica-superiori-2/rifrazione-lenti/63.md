With a fixed first index greater than the second, the second index is increased but stays smaller. How does the critical angle change?

- It decreases.
- It always stays the same.
- It must become zero.
- It increases.

## Explanation

Increasing only the outer index raises its ratio to the first index. The arcsine is increasing over this range. The threshold moves closer to $90^\circ$, so exceeding it takes a more oblique incidence.
