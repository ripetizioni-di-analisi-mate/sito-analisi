With a fixed first index greater than the second, the second index is increased but stays smaller. How does the critical angle change?

- It decreases.
- It always stays the same.
- It must become zero.
- It increases.

## Explanation

The ratio of final to initial index increases. Inverse sine is increasing between zero and one.
