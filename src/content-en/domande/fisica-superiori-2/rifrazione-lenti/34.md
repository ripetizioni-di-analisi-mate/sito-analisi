A thin converging lens has $f=14\,\mathrm{cm}$ and a real object at $p=7\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find the signed image position.

- $14\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-14\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-7\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $21\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

For the thin lens use signed distances in the same unit. The sign of $q$ gives the side and the sign of $M$ gives orientation.

$$
q=\frac{14\cdot7}{7-14}\,\mathrm{cm}=-14\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
