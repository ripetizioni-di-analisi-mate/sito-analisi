A thin converging lens of focal length $14\,\mathrm{cm}$ forms a virtual image at $q=-14\,\mathrm{cm}$. Rays from the same point should now leave parallel to one another. How much farther from the lens should the point move along the axis?

- $3.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $7\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $14\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $21\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The initial position gives $\dfrac{1}{p}=\dfrac{1}{14}+\dfrac{1}{14}$ in inverse centimetres because the reciprocal of $q$ is negative. The object was therefore $7\,\mathrm{cm}$ away. For parallel rays, its new distance must equal the $14\,\mathrm{cm}$ focal length. It moves $7\,\mathrm{cm}$ farther away, the difference between the two positions.
