A converging lens of $f=15\,\mathrm{cm}$ forms a real image of equal height magnitude. What is the separation between object and screen?

- $30\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $60\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $45\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $15\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

For the thin lens use signed distances in the same unit. The sign of $q$ gives the side and the sign of $M$ gives orientation.

$$
p=q=2f=30\,\mathrm{cm},\qquad p+q=60\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
