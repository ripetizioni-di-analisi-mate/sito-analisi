Two light components have vacuum wavelengths of $600\,\mathrm{nm}$ and $480\,\mathrm{nm}$. Their respective indices in a material are $1.50$ and $1.60$. What is the difference between their wavelengths in the material?

- $50\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $75\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $100\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $150\,\mathrm{nm}$

## Explanation

Each component keeps its own frequency, but the index depends on frequency. Divide each vacuum wavelength by its own index. The resulting wavelengths are $400\,\mathrm{nm}$ and $300\,\mathrm{nm}$, giving a difference of $100\,\mathrm{nm}$. Subtracting first and dividing by a single index would give a different result.
