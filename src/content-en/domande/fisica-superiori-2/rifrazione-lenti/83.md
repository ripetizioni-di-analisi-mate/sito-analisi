A monochromatic beam passes from the first medium to the second. The ratio of speeds $v_2/v_1$ is $0.75$. In the first medium $\lambda_1=640\,\mathrm{nm}$. What wavelength is obtained in the second?

- $640\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $853\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $480\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $160\,\mathrm{nm}$

## Explanation

The same $c$ and frequency apply across the stationary interface. The product $nv$ is $c$, and $n\lambda$ is the vacuum wavelength.

$$
\lambda_2=\frac{v_2}{v_1}\lambda_1=0.75\cdot640\,\mathrm{nm}=480\,\mathrm{nm}.
$$
