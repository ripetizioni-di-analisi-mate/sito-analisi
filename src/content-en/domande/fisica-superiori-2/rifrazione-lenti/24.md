In air $n_1=1$ and $\sin\theta_1=0.6$. In the second medium $\sin\theta_2=0.4$. Find the second index.

- $0.66666667$
- $1$
- $1.2$
- $1.5$

## Explanation

Angles are measured from the normal to the interface. The law preserves the product of index and the sine of the angle across the two media.

$$
n_2=\frac{1\cdot0.6}{0.4}=1.5.
$$
