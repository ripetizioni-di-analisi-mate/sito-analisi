In air $n_1=1$ and $\sin\theta_1=0.6$. In the second medium $\sin\theta_2=0.4$. Find the second index.

- $0.67$
- $1$
- $1.2$
- $1.5$

## Explanation

The known product is $1\cdot0.6=0.6$. Getting the same value with a sine of $0.4$ requires an index above one. Since the sines are already given, the angles do not have to be calculated.

$$
n_2=\frac{1\cdot0.6}{0.4}=1.5.
$$
