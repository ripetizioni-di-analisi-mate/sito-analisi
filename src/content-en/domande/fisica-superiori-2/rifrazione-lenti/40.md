A thin lens forms an image with $M=0.40$ from a real object at $p=24\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find the signed image position.

- $9.6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-60\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-9.6\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

For the thin lens use signed distances in the same unit. The sign of $q$ gives the side and the sign of $M$ gives orientation.

$$
q=-Mp=-0.40\cdot24\,\mathrm{cm}=-9.6\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
