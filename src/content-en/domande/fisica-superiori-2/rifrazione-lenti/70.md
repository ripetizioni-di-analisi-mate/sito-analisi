A real object lies at p equal to the lens’s positive focal length. What happens after the lens?

- The rays emerge parallel.
- The image lies at q equal to zero.
- The image is exactly at the output focus.
- Magnification is one half at every height.

## Explanation

The reciprocal of image distance is zero. This corresponds to parallel rays without a finite meeting.
