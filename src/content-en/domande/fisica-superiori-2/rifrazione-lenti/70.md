A point on the object is in the input focal plane of a thin converging lens, so $p=f$. How do rays from that point leave the lens?

- The rays emerge parallel.
- The image lies at q equal to zero.
- The image is exactly at the output focus.
- Magnification is one half at every height.

## Explanation

In $\dfrac{1}{q}=\dfrac{1}{f}-\dfrac{1}{p}$ the two terms cancel because $p=f$. The rays from that point emerge parallel to each other, so there is no plane at a finite distance on the output side where they meet.
