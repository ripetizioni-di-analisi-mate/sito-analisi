Light has vacuum wavelength $550\,\mathrm{nm}$. What is its wavelength in glass of refractive index $1.5$? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $825\,\mathrm{nm}$.
- $366.667\,\mathrm{nm}$.
- $550\,\mathrm{nm}$.
- $183.333\,\mathrm{nm}$.

## Explanation

Frequency stays the same on entering glass. Since $\lambda=v/\nu$ and $v=c/n$, wavelength in the material is $\lambda_0/n$. The quotient keeps the unit of the input.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda &= \frac{\lambda_0}{n} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{\left(550\right)}{\left(1.5\right)} \\[6pt]
&\simeq 366.667\,\mathrm{nm}
\end{aligned}
$$
