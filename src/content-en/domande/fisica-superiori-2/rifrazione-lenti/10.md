Monochromatic light has wavelength $440\,\mathrm{nm}$ in a medium where its speed is $2.4\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. It crosses a stationary boundary into glass where its speed is $2.0\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is its new wavelength? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $293.333\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $366.667\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $440\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $550\,\mathrm{nm}$

## Explanation

The frequency stays the same at the boundary. Since $\lambda$ is speed divided by frequency in each medium, wavelength changes in the same proportion as speed. Thus $\lambda_2=440\dfrac{2.0}{2.4}\,\mathrm{nm}\simeq366.667\,\mathrm{nm}$. The ratio is below one, which confirms that the new wavelength is shorter than the initial one.
