The same monochromatic beam travels through two media. In the first it has $\lambda_1=420\,\mathrm{nm}$ and $n_1=1.4$. What is $\lambda_2$ in the second medium, with $n_2=1.75$?

- $336\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $420\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $525\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $240\,\mathrm{nm}$

## Explanation

The vacuum wavelength can be found first: $420\cdot1.4\,\mathrm{nm}=588\,\mathrm{nm}$. Dividing by the second medium’s index gives the required value. The second index is higher, so the wavelength has to be smaller.

$$
\lambda_2=420\frac{1.4}{1.75}\,\mathrm{nm}=336\,\mathrm{nm}.
$$
