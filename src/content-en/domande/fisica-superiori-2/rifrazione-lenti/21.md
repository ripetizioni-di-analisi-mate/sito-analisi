The same monochromatic beam travels through two media. In the first it has $\lambda_1=420\,\mathrm{nm}$ and $n_1=1.4$. What is $\lambda_2$ in the second medium, with $n_2=1.75$?

- $336\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $420\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $525\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $240\,\mathrm{nm}$

## Explanation

The same $c$ and frequency apply across the stationary interface. The product $nv$ is $c$, and $n\lambda$ is the vacuum wavelength.

$$
\lambda_2=420\frac{1.4}{1.75}\,\mathrm{nm}=336\,\mathrm{nm}.
$$
