Which quantity stays unchanged across a stationary boundary between transparent materials?

- Speed in all cases.
- Wavelength in all cases.
- Frequency.
- Refractive index.

## Explanation

The field on the two sides has the same time dependence at the stationary boundary. The same number of cycles passes per second, even though the spacing between consecutive peaks changes in the new medium.
