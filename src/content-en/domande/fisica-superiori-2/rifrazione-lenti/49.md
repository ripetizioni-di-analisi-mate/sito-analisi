Which quantity stays unchanged across a stationary boundary between transparent materials?

- Speed in all cases.
- Wavelength in all cases.
- Frequency.
- Refractive index.

## Explanation

Transmitted oscillations retain the source frequency across the boundary. Speed and wavelength can change.
