A thin lens forms a real image at $q=18\,\mathrm{cm}$ of a real object at $p=40\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its signed transverse magnification? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $0.45$.
- $-2.22222$.
- $-0.45$.
- $2.22222$.

## Explanation

Magnification is $M=-q/p$. Here $q$ and $p$ are positive, so $M$ is negative. Its magnitude is the height ratio, and the sign indicates an inverted image.

$$
\begin{aligned}
M &= - \frac{q}{p} \\[6pt]
&= - \frac{\left(18\right)}{\left(40\right)} \\[6pt]
&\simeq -0.45
\end{aligned}
$$
