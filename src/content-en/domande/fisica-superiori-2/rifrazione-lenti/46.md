Two thin lenses in contact in air have optical powers $+8$ and $-3\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$. Find the equivalent focal length in centimetres.

- $20\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $50\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The first lens’s positive power exceeds the magnitude of the second lens’s negative power. The system remains converging, with five dioptres. The reciprocal is two tenths of a metre, which is twenty centimetres.

$$
f=\frac{1}{8-3}\,\mathrm m=0.20\,\mathrm m=20\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
