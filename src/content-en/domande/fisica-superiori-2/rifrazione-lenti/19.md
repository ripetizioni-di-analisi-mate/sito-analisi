A wavelength in glass is $350\,\mathrm{nm}$ with $n=1.6$. What is its vacuum value?

- $350\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $218.75\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $560\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $480\,\mathrm{nm}$

## Explanation

The same $c$ and frequency apply across the stationary interface. The product $nv$ is $c$, and $n\lambda$ is the vacuum wavelength.

$$
\lambda_0=n\lambda=1.6\cdot350\,\mathrm{nm}=560\,\mathrm{nm}.
$$
