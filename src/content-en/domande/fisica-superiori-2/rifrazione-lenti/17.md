A ray crosses $60\,\mathrm{cm}$ of glass with $n=1.50$ at normal incidence. With $c=3.0\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$, find the time spent in glass.

- $3\,\mathrm{ns}$
- $2\,\mathrm{ns}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{ns}$
- $0.3\,\mathrm{ns}$

## Explanation

The same $c$ and frequency apply across the stationary interface. The product $nv$ is $c$, and $n\lambda$ is the vacuum wavelength.

$$
t=\frac{0.60\cdot1.50}{3.0\cdot10^8}\,\mathrm s=3.0\,\mathrm{ns}.
$$
