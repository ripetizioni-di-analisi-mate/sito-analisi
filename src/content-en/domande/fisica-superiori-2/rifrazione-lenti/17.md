A ray crosses $60\,\mathrm{cm}$ of glass with $n=1.50$ at normal incidence. With $c=3.0\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$, find the time spent in glass.

- $3\,\mathrm{ns}$
- $2\,\mathrm{ns}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{ns}$
- $0.3\,\mathrm{ns}$

## Explanation

At normal incidence the path in glass equals its thickness. Convert sixty centimetres to metres and use $v=2.0\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Dividing distance by this speed gives three billionths of a second. Converting the result gives three nanoseconds.

$$
t=\frac{0.60\cdot1.50}{3.0\cdot10^8}\,\mathrm s=3.0\,\mathrm{ns}.
$$
