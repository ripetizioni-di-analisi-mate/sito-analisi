Add a weaker diverging lens to a stronger converging lens, with the thin lenses in contact. What happens to the resulting positive focal length?

- It always decreases.
- It increases because positive power decreases.
- It always becomes negative.
- It is unchanged regardless of the added power.

## Explanation

The overall power stays positive because the converging lens is stronger. The second lens reduces this value without changing its sign. The reciprocal of a smaller positive number is greater, so the output focus moves farther away.
