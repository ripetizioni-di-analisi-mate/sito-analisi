Along a normal a pulse crosses $0.30\,\mathrm m$ of water with $n=1.33$ and $0.20\,\mathrm m$ of air with $n=1$. With $c=3.0\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the total duration to the nearest hundredth of a nanosecond?

- $1.67\,\mathrm{ns}$
- $0.67\,\mathrm{ns}$
- $2.00\,\mathrm{ns}$
- $6\,\mathrm{ns}$

## Explanation

Each section has its own speed. Add individual travel times along the normal.

$$
t=\frac{1.33\cdot0.30+1\cdot0.20}{3.0\cdot10^8}\,\mathrm s\simeq2.00\,\mathrm{ns}.
$$
