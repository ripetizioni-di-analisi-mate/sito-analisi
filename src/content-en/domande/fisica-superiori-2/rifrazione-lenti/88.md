Along a normal, a pulse crosses $0.30\,\mathrm m$ of water of index $n=1.33$ and $0.20\,\mathrm m$ of air of index $n=1$. Neglect dispersion and use $c=3.0\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the total time to the nearest hundredth of a nanosecond?

- $1.67\,\mathrm{ns}$
- $0.67\,\mathrm{ns}$
- $2.00\,\mathrm{ns}$
- $6\,\mathrm{ns}$

## Explanation

Calculate each travel time by multiplying the segment length by its index and dividing by $c$. The water segment takes $1.33\,\mathrm{ns}$ and the air segment about $0.667\,\mathrm{ns}$. Add the travel times before rounding to the nearest hundredth of a nanosecond.

$$
t=\frac{1.33\cdot0.30+1\cdot0.20}{3.0\cdot10^8}\,\mathrm s\simeq2.00\,\mathrm{ns}.
$$
