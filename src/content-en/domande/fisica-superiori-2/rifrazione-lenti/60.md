Which ordering of indices is needed for a critical angle of total internal reflection?

- The initial index must be greater.
- The final index must be greater.
- The indices must be equal.
- Any two indices suffice.

## Explanation

At the threshold the incident sine is the final index divided by the initial one. For a threshold below ninety degrees, the ratio is less than one.
