Which ordering of indices is needed for a critical angle of total internal reflection?

- The initial index must be greater.
- The final index must be greater.
- The indices must be equal.
- Any two indices suffice.

## Explanation

At the threshold the transmitted sine is one, and the incident sine is the ratio of final index to initial index. That ratio must be below one to allow incidence angles above the threshold. The initial index therefore has to be higher.
