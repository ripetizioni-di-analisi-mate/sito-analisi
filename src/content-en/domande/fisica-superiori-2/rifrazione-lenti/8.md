A thin converging lens has focal length $0.3\,\mathrm m$. What is its optical power? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $0.3\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$.
- $-3.33333\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$.
- $6.66667\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$.
- $3.33333\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$.

## Explanation

Optical power is the reciprocal of focal length expressed in metres. A converging lens has positive focal length and therefore positive power.

$$
\begin{aligned}
D &= \frac{1}{f} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{1}{\left(0.3\right)} \\[6pt]
&\simeq 3.33333\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}
\end{aligned}
$$
