A thin converging lens has focal length $0.3\,\mathrm m$. What is its optical power? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $0.3\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$.
- $-3.3\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$.
- $6.7\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$.
- $3.3\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$.

## Explanation

Optical power is the reciprocal of focal length expressed in metres. A converging lens has positive focal length and therefore positive power.

The result gives the power for a focal length of three tenths of a metre. Entering $30$ as though it were a distance in metres would reduce the result by a factor of one hundred.

$$
\begin{aligned}
D &= \frac{1}{f} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{1}{0.3}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}} \\[6pt]
&\simeq 3.3\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}
\end{aligned}
$$
