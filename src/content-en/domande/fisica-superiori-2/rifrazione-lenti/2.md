Light has vacuum wavelength $520\,\mathrm{nm}$. What is its wavelength in glass of refractive index $1.5$? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $780\,\mathrm{nm}$.
- $346.667\,\mathrm{nm}$.
- $520\,\mathrm{nm}$.
- $173.333\,\mathrm{nm}$.

## Explanation

Frequency stays the same on entering glass. Since $\lambda=\dfrac{v}{\nu}$ and $v=\dfrac{c}{n}$, wavelength in the material is $\dfrac{\lambda_0}{n}$. The quotient keeps the unit of the input.

The same number of oscillations passes in a second in both measurements. In glass a single oscillation occupies a shorter space, two thirds of its length in vacuum.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda &= \frac{\lambda_0}{n} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{\left(520\right)}{\left(1.5\right)} \\[6pt]
&\simeq 346.667\,\mathrm{nm}
\end{aligned}
$$
