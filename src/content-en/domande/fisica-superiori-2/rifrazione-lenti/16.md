Two materials have indices $1.25$ and $1.50$. What is the ratio of speed in the first medium to speed in the second?

- $0.83333333$
- $0.25$
- $1.5$
- $1.2$

## Explanation

The same $c$ and frequency apply across the stationary interface. The product $nv$ is $c$, and $n\lambda$ is the vacuum wavelength.

$$
\frac{v_1}{v_2}=\frac{c/n_1}{c/n_2}=\frac{1.50}{1.25}=1.20.
$$
