Two materials have indices $1.25$ and $1.50$. What is the ratio of speed in the first medium to speed in the second?

- $0.83$
- $0.25$
- $1.50$
- $1.20$

## Explanation

The speed is higher in the medium with lower index. The common $c$ cancels in the two quotients, and the indices enter in reverse order. The result $1.20$ means the speed in the first medium is twenty per cent higher than the second.

$$
\frac{v_1}{v_2}=\frac{\dfrac{c}{n_1}}{\dfrac{c}{n_2}}=\frac{1.50}{1.25}=1.20.
$$
