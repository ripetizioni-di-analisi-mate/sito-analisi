A thin diverging lens has focal length $f=-10\,\mathrm{cm}$ and a real object at $p=30\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the signed image distance? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $7.5\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $-7.5\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $-15\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $-3.75\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

A diverging lens has $f<0$. Subtracting $1/p>0$ from $1/f<0$ gives $1/q<0$. The image point is on the object side and is virtual.

$$
\begin{aligned}
q &= \frac{f p}{- f + p} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{\left(-10\right) \left(30\right)}{- \left(-10\right) + \left(30\right)} \\[6pt]
&\simeq -7.5\,\mathrm{cm}
\end{aligned}
$$
