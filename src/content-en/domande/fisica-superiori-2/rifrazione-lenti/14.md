A thin lens has power $-10\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$. Its image of a real object is upright and one quarter as tall as the object. What is the signed image distance in centimetres?

- $+7.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-7.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-10\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-30\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The reciprocal of the power gives $f=-0.10\,\mathrm m=-10\,\mathrm{cm}$. Since the image is upright, $M=+\dfrac{1}{4}$, so $p=-4q$. In the lens equation $\dfrac{1}{p}+\dfrac{1}{q}=\dfrac{3}{4q}$. This gives $q=\dfrac{3}{4} f=-7.5\,\mathrm{cm}$. The negative sign agrees with a virtual image on the object side.
