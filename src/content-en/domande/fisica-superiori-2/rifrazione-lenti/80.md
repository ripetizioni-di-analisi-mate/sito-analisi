Two thin lenses in contact in air have equal power magnitudes and opposite signs. What follows from their sum?

- Twice the positive power.
- Twice the negative power.
- Zero equivalent power.
- Zero equivalent focal length.

## Explanation

The converging and diverging contributions to power are opposite. In the contact model a parallel incident beam stays parallel on exit. Zero power does not correspond to a finite focal length.
