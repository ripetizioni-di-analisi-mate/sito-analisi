In the diagram object O is $27\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the lens and the exit focus F is $9\,\mathrm{cm}$ away. How far from the lens does image I form?

::grafico{assi=0 linee="-30.115385,0 16.615385,0;0,-5.2 0,5.2" vettori="-27,0,-27,4;-27,4,0,4;-27,4,0,0;0,4,13.5,-2;0,0,13.5,-2;13.5,0,13.5,-2" punti="9,0,pieno,F;-9,0,pieno,F′;13.5,-2,pieno,I;-27,4,pieno,O" xmin=-33.230769 xmax=19.730769 ymin=-5.72 ymax=5.72 titolo="O and I lie on opposite sides of the lens."}

- $18\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $13.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-13.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $9\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The object is beyond two focal lengths. The ray arrows physically converge and the result is on the output side. Expect a distance between one and two focal lengths.

$$
q=\frac{9\cdot27}{27-9}\,\mathrm{cm}=13.5\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
