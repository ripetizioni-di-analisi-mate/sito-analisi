An object and screen are fixed $48\,\mathrm{cm}$ apart. A thin converging lens of focal length $9\,\mathrm{cm}$ gives a sharp image when it is $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the object. How far towards the screen should the lens move to find the second sharp position?

::grafico{assi=0 linee="0,0 48,0;0,-2 0,2;48,-2 48,2;12,-2 12,2" punti="0,0,pieno,O;12,0,pieno,A;48,0,pieno,S" xmin=-4 xmax=53 ymin=-4 ymax=4 titolo="The first sharp image is obtained with the lens at A. Object O and screen S stay fixed."}

- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $24\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $36\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $48\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

In the first position $p=12\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $q=(48-12)\,\mathrm{cm}=36\,\mathrm{cm}$. The lens equation uses the sum of their reciprocals, so swapping the distances preserves focus. In the second position $p=36\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $q=12\,\mathrm{cm}$. The lens moves by the difference between the two values of $p$, or $24\,\mathrm{cm}$.
