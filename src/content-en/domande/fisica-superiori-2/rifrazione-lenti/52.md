The glass index measured with red light is used to estimate violet light very precisely. What needs checking?

- Whether the sound frequency is higher.
- Whether glass changes mass when light enters.
- Whether the index differs at the two frequencies.
- Whether index should be measured in joules.

## Explanation

If the glass is dispersive, its index depends on frequency. Two colours can have different values even in the same sample. Snell’s law therefore needs the value measured at the violet ray’s frequency.
