A ray strikes the interface between air and glass perpendicularly. Which direction does the transmitted ray take?

- It travels along the surface.
- It necessarily turns by 45°.
- It continues along the normal.
- It cannot be transmitted.

## Explanation

The incident sine is zero. Snell’s law therefore gives zero transmitted sine and angle.
