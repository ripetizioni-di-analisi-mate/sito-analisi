A ray strikes the interface between air and glass perpendicularly. Which direction does the transmitted ray take?

- It travels along the surface.
- It necessarily turns by 45°.
- It continues along the normal.
- It cannot be transmitted.

## Explanation

In Snell’s law, the transmitted sine is the incident sine multiplied by the ratio of the indices. The incident sine is zero here, so the transmitted sine and angle are also zero. The glass can still reduce the speed and wavelength.
