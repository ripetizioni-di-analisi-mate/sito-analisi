A transparent material has refractive index $1.6$. Use $c=3\cdot10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the speed of light in the material? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $1.875\cdot10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $4.8\cdot10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $3\cdot10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $9.375\cdot10^{7}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

The refractive index is the ratio $n=\dfrac{c}{v}$. To isolate $v$, divide vacuum speed by the dimensionless index. Since $n>1$ for the stated material, $v<c$.

Light travels a shorter distance in the same time in the medium. As a check, multiplying $1.875$ by $1.6$ recovers the coefficient $3$ of the speed in vacuum.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v &= \frac{c}{n} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{\left(3\cdot10^{8}\right)}{\left(1.6\right)} \\[6pt]
&\simeq 1.875\cdot10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
