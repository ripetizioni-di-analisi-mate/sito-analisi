A thin converging lens has focal length $f=10\,\mathrm{cm}$. A real object is at $p=30\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find the signed image distance in the paraxial approximation. Choose the value closest to the result.

- $15\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $-15\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $7.5\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $20\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

Focal length is positive and the real object is outside the focal distance. Subtract $1/p$ from $1/f$ and take the reciprocal. The positive value of $q$ puts the image on the output side.

$$
\begin{aligned}
q &= \frac{f p}{- f + p} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{\left(10\right) \left(30\right)}{- \left(10\right) + \left(30\right)} \\[6pt]
&\simeq 15\,\mathrm{cm}
\end{aligned}
$$
