A thin converging lens of focal length $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ must form a screen image of a real object with half the object's height in magnitude. How far from the lens should the screen be placed?

- $15\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $30\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $10\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-15\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The screen catches a real image, so $q>0$ and $M=-0.50$. The relation $M=-\dfrac{q}{p}$ gives $p=2q$. Substituting into the lens equation yields $\dfrac{1}{f}=\dfrac{1}{2q}+\dfrac{1}{q}=\dfrac{3}{2q}$. The solution is therefore $q=15\,\mathrm{cm}$. The distance twice as large, $30\,\mathrm{cm}$, belongs to the object.
