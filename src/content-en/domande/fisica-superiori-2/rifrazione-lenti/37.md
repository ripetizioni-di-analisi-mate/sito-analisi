A diverging lens gives $q=-8\,\mathrm{cm}$ for $p=24\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is $f$?

- $-12\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-16\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

For the thin lens use signed distances in the same unit. The sign of $q$ gives the side and the sign of $M$ gives orientation.

$$
f=\frac{24\cdot(-8)}{24-8}\,\mathrm{cm}=-12\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
