A diverging lens gives $q=-8\,\mathrm{cm}$ for $p=24\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is $f$?

- $-12\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-16\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The reciprocal of the virtual distance is negative and has larger magnitude than the object term. The resulting focal length must therefore be negative. This agrees with the diverging lens stated in the question.

$$
f=\frac{24\cdot(-8)}{24-8}\,\mathrm{cm}=-12\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
