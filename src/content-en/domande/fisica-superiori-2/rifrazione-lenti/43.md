A thin converging lens has focal length $12\,\mathrm{cm}$. The object is moved from $p=36$ to $p=24\,\mathrm{cm}$. How far must the screen move away from the lens to keep the image in focus?

- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $18\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Moving the object towards the focus lowers $p-f$. Both image distances remain positive, but change from eighteen to twenty-four centimetres. The requested difference is positive, so the screen moves farther away from the lens.

$$
q_i=18\,\mathrm{cm},\qquad q_f=24\,\mathrm{cm},\qquad \Delta q=6\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
