A thin converging lens has focal length $12\,\mathrm{cm}$. The object is moved from $p=36$ to $p=24\,\mathrm{cm}$. How far must the screen move away from the lens to keep the image in focus?

- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $18\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

For the thin lens use signed distances in the same unit. The sign of $q$ gives the side and the sign of $M$ gives orientation.

$$
q_i=18\,\mathrm{cm},\qquad q_f=24\,\mathrm{cm},\qquad \Delta q=6\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
