A ray in the higher-index material has an incidence below the critical angle. What does the model say about propagation in the second medium?

- Total internal reflection is mandatory.
- A transmitted direction exists.
- The transmitted angle is always zero.
- The transmitted ray stays in the first material.

## Explanation

Incidence below the threshold gives a transmitted sine below one. A valid direction in the second medium therefore exists. The law gives the direction and does not rule out some light also being reflected.
