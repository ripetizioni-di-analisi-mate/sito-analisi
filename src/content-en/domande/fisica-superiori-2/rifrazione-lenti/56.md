Two materials have the same index for the light used. What direction does Snell’s law predict for transmission?

- The angle from the normal stays the same.
- The angle doubles.
- There is total internal reflection at every angle.
- Direction turns by 90°.

## Explanation

The indices cancel. Within the range of angles measured from the normal, equal sines imply equal angles.
