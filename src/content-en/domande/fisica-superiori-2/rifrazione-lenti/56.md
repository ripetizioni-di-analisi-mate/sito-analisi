Two materials have the same index for the light used. What direction does Snell’s law predict for transmission?

- The angle from the normal stays the same.
- The angle doubles.
- There is total internal reflection at every angle.
- Direction turns by 90°.

## Explanation

The equal indices cancel in the formula. The sines are equal, and in the relevant angle range that means equal angles. The transmitted ray keeps the same direction even though the material changes.
