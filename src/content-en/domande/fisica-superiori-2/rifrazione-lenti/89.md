From one medium to another the critical angle is 30°. Both indices are multiplied by the same positive factor. What is the new critical angle?

- It depends on the sum of the indices.
- 60° in every case.
- Zero for any factor greater than one.
- 30°.

## Explanation

The threshold depends on the second index divided by the first. Multiplying both by the same factor leaves that ratio unchanged. Its inverse sine stays the same even though the individual indices of the two media change.
