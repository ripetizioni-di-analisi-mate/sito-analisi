What are the units of refractive index?

- It is dimensionless.
- Metres per second.
- Nanometres.
- Dioptres.

## Explanation

Both vacuum speed and speed in the medium are measured in metres per second. Dividing one by the other cancels all units. The index is a pure ratio and can be compared directly with one.
