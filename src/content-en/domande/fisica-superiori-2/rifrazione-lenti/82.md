Two thin lenses are separated by a significant distance. Why can the sum of powers for lenses in contact fail?

- Both focal lengths become zero.
- The beam changes position and width between the lenses.
- Lens separation is always dimensionless.
- Each individual power changes unit.

## Explanation

An oblique ray changes height between the two lenses. The bend imposed by the second lens depends on the height at which the ray meets it. Separating them therefore adds a geometric quantity absent from the model for lenses in contact.
