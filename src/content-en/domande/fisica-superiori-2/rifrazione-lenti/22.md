Light in glass with $n=1.5$ has wavelength $400\,\mathrm{nm}$. With $c=3.0\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is its frequency?

- $7.5\cdot10^{14}\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $5.0\cdot10^{14}\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $3.3\cdot10^{14}\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $500000\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

In the glass the speed is $2.0\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and one wavelength is $400\cdot10^{-9}\,\mathrm m$. Their ratio gives cycles per second. Using $c$ with the wavelength measured in glass would give a frequency that is too high.

$$
\nu=\frac{3.0\cdot10^8}{1.5\cdot400\cdot10^{-9}}\,\mathrm{Hz}=5.0\cdot10^{14}\,\mathrm{Hz}.
$$
