An object of height $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ gives an image with signed height $-1.5\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is $M$?

- $0.3$
- $-3.3333333$
- $-0.3$
- $0.7$

## Explanation

For the thin lens use signed distances in the same unit. The sign of $q$ gives the side and the sign of $M$ gives orientation.

$$
M=\frac{-1.5}{5}=-0.30.
$$
