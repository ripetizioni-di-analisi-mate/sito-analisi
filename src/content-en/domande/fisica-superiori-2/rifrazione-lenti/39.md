An object of height $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ gives an image with signed height $-1.5\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is $M$?

- $0.3$
- $-3.3333333$
- $-0.3$
- $0.7$

## Explanation

The image height already includes its sign, so put no second minus sign into the height ratio. The magnitude says the image is three tenths the object’s height. The sign places its top below the optical axis.

$$
M=\frac{-1.5}{5}=-0.30.
$$
