A ray passes from a medium of higher index to one of lower index at a nonzero incidence angle below the critical angle. How does the transmitted ray turn relative to the normal?

- The transmitted ray bends towards the normal.
- The transmitted ray bends away from the normal.
- The transmitted ray changes frequency at the boundary.
- The transmitted ray must be parallel to the normal.

## Explanation

When the index decreases, the transmitted sine has to rise to preserve the product. Incidence below the threshold ensures that the new sine stays below one. The ray enters the second medium at a larger angle from the normal.
