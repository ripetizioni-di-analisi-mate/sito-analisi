A ray makes an angle φ with the surface. What angle goes into Snell’s law as incidence?

- $\varphi+90^\circ$
- $180^\circ-\varphi$
- $2\varphi$
- $90^\circ-\varphi$

## Explanation

The normal is perpendicular to the surface. Use the complementary angle.
