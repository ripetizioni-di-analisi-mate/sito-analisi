A ray makes an angle φ with the surface. What angle goes into Snell’s law as incidence?

- $\varphi+90^\circ$
- $180^\circ-\varphi$
- $2\varphi$
- $90^\circ-\varphi$

## Explanation

A surface and its normal meet at a right angle. If the protractor reads $65^\circ$ from the surface, the ray’s incidence is $25^\circ$ from the normal. This second reading is the one used in the sine in Snell’s law.
