A converging lens has an object between one and two focal lengths. Where is the screen relative to the lens?

- On the object side, between one and two focal lengths.
- Exactly at the centre.
- At the object in the lens plane.
- On the output side, beyond two focal lengths.

## Explanation

The denominator p minus f is positive and less than f. The expression for q is positive and exceeds two focal lengths.
