Light goes from air with $n_1=1$ into glass with $n_2=1.5$. The angle of incidence from the normal is $35^\circ$. What is the refracted angle? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $35{}^\circ$.
- $44.9629{}^\circ$.
- $22.4814{}^\circ$.
- $11.2407{}^\circ$.

## Explanation

Angles are measured from the normal. In Snell’s law isolate the sine of $\theta_2$, then take the inverse sine. The next medium has a larger index, so the refracted angle is smaller than the incident angle.

The refracted sine is about $0.382$. To compare it with incidence, take the inverse sine and read the result in degrees. The roughly $22.5^\circ$ is measured from the same normal.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\theta_2 &= \frac{180 \arcsin{\left(\frac{n_1 \sin{\left(\frac{\pi \alpha}{180} \right)}}{n_2} \right)}}{\pi} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{180 \arcsin{\left(\frac{\left(1\right) \sin{\left(\frac{\pi \left(35\right)}{180} \right)}}{\left(1.5\right)} \right)}}{\pi} \\[6pt]
&\simeq 22.4814{}^\circ
\end{aligned}
$$
