A $90\,\mathrm{cm}$ slab with $n=1.50$ replaces the same path in vacuum at normal incidence. How much delay does it add with $c=3.0\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$?

- $3\,\mathrm{ns}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{ns}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{ns}$
- $6\,\mathrm{ns}$

## Explanation

Over $0.90\,\mathrm m$ light would take $3.0\,\mathrm{ns}$ in vacuum. In glass the speed is reduced by the index factor and the time rises to $4.5\,\mathrm{ns}$. The delay is the difference between these times because the same path segment is replaced.

$$
\Delta t=\frac{0.90(1.50-1)}{3.0\cdot10^8}\,\mathrm s=1.5\,\mathrm{ns}.
$$
