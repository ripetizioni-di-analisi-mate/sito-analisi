Two identical thin lenses in contact in air give an equivalent power of $-8\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$. What is the signed focal length of each lens?

- $25\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-12.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-50\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-25\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

With two identical lenses, total power is twice the power of one. Each therefore contributes negative four dioptres. The reciprocal is negative with magnitude one quarter of a metre, which converts to twenty-five centimetres.

$$
D_1=D_2=-4\,\mathrm{m^{-1}},\qquad f=\frac1{-4}\,\mathrm m=-25\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
