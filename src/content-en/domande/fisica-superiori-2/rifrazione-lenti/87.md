Two identical thin lenses in contact in air give an equivalent power of $-8\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$. What is the signed focal length of each lens?

- $25\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-12.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-50\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-25\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Optical power is the reciprocal of the signed focal length in metres. For lenses in contact the powers add in the stated model.

$$
D_1=D_2=-4\,\mathrm{m^{-1}},\qquad f=\frac1{-4}\,\mathrm m=-25\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
