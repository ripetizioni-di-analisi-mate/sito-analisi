A student adds the reciprocal of p in centimetres to the reciprocal of q in metres. What is the problem?

- Lens distances are always in seconds.
- The numbers use different inverse-length units and need a common unit.
- Reciprocals are dimensionless by definition.
- Multiplication must be used in every formula.

## Explanation

Terms to be added must share the same unit. It is simplest to convert the distances before taking their reciprocals.
