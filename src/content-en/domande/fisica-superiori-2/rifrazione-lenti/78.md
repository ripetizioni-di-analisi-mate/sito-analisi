For the same sign, which lens has the greater power magnitude?

- The one with the larger focal-length magnitude.
- The one with the smaller focal-length magnitude.
- The one with infinite focal length.
- It depends only on object length.

## Explanation

In magnitude, the power is one divided by the focal length in metres. For lenses of the same sign, a focal magnitude of $0.25\,\mathrm m$ corresponds to four dioptres in magnitude, while $0.50\,\mathrm m$ corresponds to two.
