A converging lens forms a real image three times the object height in magnitude at $q=60\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find $f$.

- $15\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $20\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $30\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $40\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The image distance is three times the object distance because the magnification magnitude is three. The object is therefore $20\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the lens. Use both positive distances in the reciprocals to obtain the focal length.

$$
\begin{aligned}p&=\frac{60}{3}\,\mathrm{cm}=20\,\mathrm{cm}\\f&=\frac{20\cdot60}{20+60}\,\mathrm{cm}=15\,\mathrm{cm}.\end{aligned}
$$
