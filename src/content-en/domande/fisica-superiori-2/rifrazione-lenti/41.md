A converging lens forms a real image three times the object height in magnitude at $q=60\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find $f$.

- $15\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $20\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $30\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $40\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

For the thin lens use signed distances in the same unit. The sign of $q$ gives the side and the sign of $M$ gives orientation.

$$
\begin{aligned}p&=\frac{60}{3}\,\mathrm{cm}=20\,\mathrm{cm}\\f&=\frac{20\cdot60}{20+60}\,\mathrm{cm}=15\,\mathrm{cm}.\end{aligned}
$$
