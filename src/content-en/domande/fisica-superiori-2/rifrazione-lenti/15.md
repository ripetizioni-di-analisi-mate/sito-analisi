Light travels at $2.4\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in a material. With $c=3.0\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the index?

- $0.8$
- $1.5$
- $1.25$
- $1.2$

## Explanation

The same $c$ and frequency apply across the stationary interface. The product $nv$ is $c$, and $n\lambda$ is the vacuum wavelength.

$$
n=\frac{3.0\cdot10^8}{2.4\cdot10^8}=1.25.
$$
