Light travels at $2.4\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in a material. With $c=3.0\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the index?

- $0.8$
- $1.5$
- $1.25$
- $2.4$

## Explanation

The speed in the medium is four fifths of the speed in vacuum. The index takes the ratio in the opposite order, so it is five quarters. The speed units cancel. An index above one is consistent with the stated speed being below the speed in vacuum.

$$
n=\frac{3.0\cdot10^8}{2.4\cdot10^8}=1.25.
$$
