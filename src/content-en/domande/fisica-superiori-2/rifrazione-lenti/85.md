Light goes from index $1.8$ to $1.2$ with $\sin\theta_1=0.8$. What formal sine value follows from Snell’s law?

- $0.8$
- $1.2$
- $0.66666667$
- $0.53333333$

## Explanation

The law gives the value that transmission would require, rather than an angle. Since a sine cannot reach $1.2$, there is no transmitted ray in the second medium in the ideal model. Reflection keeps the ray in the first medium.

$$
\frac{n_1}{n_2}\sin\theta_1=\frac{1.8}{1.2}\cdot0.8=1.2.
$$
