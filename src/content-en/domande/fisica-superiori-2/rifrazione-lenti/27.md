A ray in a medium of index $1.50$ enters air and refracts at $30^\circ$ to the normal. Find the sine of the incident angle.

- $0.5$
- $0.75$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

The given angle is after the ray enters air, so multiply that sine by the index of air before dividing by the initial index. The result is below $\sin30^\circ$, consistent with a smaller initial angle.

$$
\sin\theta_1=\frac{1\cdot\sin30^\circ}{1.50}=\frac{1}{3}.
$$
