A ray in a medium of index $1.50$ enters air and refracts at $30^\circ$ to the normal. Find the sine of the incident angle.

- $0.5$
- $0.75$
- $0.33333333$
- $0.66666667$

## Explanation

Angles are measured from the normal to the interface. The law preserves the product of index and the sine of the angle across the two media.

$$
\sin\theta_1=\frac{1\cdot\sin30^\circ}{1.50}=\frac13.
$$
