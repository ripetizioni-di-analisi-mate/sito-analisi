A formal calculation gives a transmitted sine greater than one. What does that mean at an ideal boundary between transparent materials?

- There is total internal reflection.
- The transmitted angle exceeds 90°.
- The angle should be doubled.
- The ray always continues along the normal.

## Explanation

A sine such as $1.05$ represents no real angle. It signals the end of the branch with propagating transmission. At an ideal interface reflection remains in the initial medium, with its angle to the normal equal to incidence.
