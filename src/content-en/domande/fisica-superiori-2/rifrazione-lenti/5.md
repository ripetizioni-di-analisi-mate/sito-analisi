A thin converging lens has focal length $f=9\,\mathrm{cm}$. A real object is at $p=25.2\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find the signed image distance in the paraxial approximation. Choose the value closest to the result.

- $14\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $-14\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $6.6\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $16.2\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

Focal length is positive and the real object is outside the focal distance. Subtract $\dfrac{1}{p}$ from $\dfrac{1}{f}$ and take the reciprocal. The positive value of $q$ puts the image on the output side.

The object is also beyond twice the focal length. The image is therefore smaller as well as real. The magnitude of image distance divided by object distance is below one.

$$
\begin{aligned}
q &= \frac{f p}{p-f} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{9\cdot25.2}{25.2-9}\,\mathrm{cm} \\[6pt]
&= 14\,\mathrm{cm}
\end{aligned}
$$
