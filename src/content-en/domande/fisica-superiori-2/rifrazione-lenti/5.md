A thin converging lens has focal length $f=9\,\mathrm{cm}$. A real object is at $p=25.2\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find the signed image distance in the paraxial approximation. Choose the value closest to the result.

- $14\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $-14\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $6.63158\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $16.2\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

Focal length is positive and the real object is outside the focal distance. Subtract $1/p$ from $1/f$ and take the reciprocal. The positive value of $q$ puts the image on the output side.

$$
\begin{aligned}
q &= \frac{f p}{- f + p} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{\left(9\right) \left(25.2\right)}{- \left(9\right) + \left(25.2\right)} \\[6pt]
&\simeq 14\,\mathrm{cm}
\end{aligned}
$$
