For a converging lens the object lies beyond twice the focal length. What image property applies?

- It is virtual, upright and enlarged.
- It is virtual and reduced.
- It is real, inverted and reduced.
- It is real and always larger.

## Explanation

When $p>2f$, the formula places $q$ between one and two focal lengths. Since $q$ is positive and less than $p$, the ratio with its minus sign gives a negative magnification of magnitude below one.
