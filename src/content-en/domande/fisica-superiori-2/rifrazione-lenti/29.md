Light goes from a material of index $2$ to air of index $1$. What is the critical angle?

- $30{}^\circ$
- $60{}^\circ$
- $90{}^\circ$
- $45{}^\circ$

## Explanation

At the critical angle, the transmitted sine is one. The threshold exists if the ray starts in the higher-index medium.

$$
\theta_c=\arcsin\frac12=30^\circ.
$$
