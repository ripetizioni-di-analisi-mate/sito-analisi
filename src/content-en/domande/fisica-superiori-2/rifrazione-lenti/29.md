A laboratory measures $\sin i$ and $\sin r$ for monochromatic light passing from air into a liquid. The three data cards give the pairs A $(0.30,0.20)$, B $(0.45,0.30)$ and C $(0.60,0.45)$. Two cards refer to the same liquid and one to a different liquid. Neglecting measurement error, which card should be set aside?

- Card C.
- Card A.
- Card B.
- All three cards describe the same liquid.

## Explanation

By Snell's law, $\dfrac{\sin i}{\sin r}$ is constant for a given pair of media. For A and B the ratio is $\dfrac32$. For C it is $\dfrac{60}{45}=\dfrac43$. Although the ray bends towards the normal in every trial, card C requires a different refractive index.
