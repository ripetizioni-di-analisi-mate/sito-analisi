Light goes from a material of index $2$ to air of index $1$. What is the critical angle?

- $30{}^\circ$
- $60{}^\circ$
- $90{}^\circ$
- $45{}^\circ$

## Explanation

With initial index two and final index one, the incidence sine must be one half when the transmitted sine reaches one. The critical angle lies in the initial medium. Exceeding it gives total internal reflection.

$$
\theta_c=\arcsin\frac{1}{2}=30^\circ.
$$
