In the paraxial construction, which ray of a thin lens continues along the same line?

- The ray through the optical centre.
- Every ray from the top.
- Only a ray at a large aperture.
- Every parallel ray even with finite focal length.

## Explanation

In the thin-lens model a ray through the optical centre keeps its direction if the outer medium is the same on both sides. This ray directly links the object to the point where the image is sought in the drawing.
