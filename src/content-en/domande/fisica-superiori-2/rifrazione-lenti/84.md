In the diagram the first material has index $1$ and the second $\sqrt2$. The incident ray makes $45^\circ$. What is the magnitude of its change of direction after transmission?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 linee="-3.4,0 3.4,0" segmenti="0,-3.05,0,3.05" vettori="-1.979899,1.979899,0,0;0,0,1.4,-2.424871" xmin=-3.8 xmax=3.8 ymin=-3.2 ymax=3.2 etichette="-2.4,2.6,n₁;2.4,-2.6,n₂" titolo="The dashed line is the normal. The ray enters the second material."}

- $30{}^\circ$
- $15{}^\circ$
- $45{}^\circ$
- $75{}^\circ$

## Explanation

Snell’s law gives a transmitted sine of one half. The propagation angles are both referred to the normal direction pointing across the boundary.

$$
|\theta_1-\theta_2|=45^\circ-30^\circ=15^\circ.
$$
