For a diverging lens of $f=-15\,\mathrm{cm}$ the image is at $q=-10\,\mathrm{cm}$. How far away is the real object?

- $25\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $30\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $150\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

For the thin lens use signed distances in the same unit. The sign of $q$ gives the side and the sign of $M$ gives orientation.

$$
p=\frac{(-15)(-10)}{-10-(-15)}\,\mathrm{cm}=30\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
