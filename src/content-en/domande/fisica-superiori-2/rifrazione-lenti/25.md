A ray enters a material of index $\sqrt2$ from air at incidence $45^\circ$. What is the refracted angle from the normal?

- $30{}^\circ$
- $45{}^\circ$
- $60{}^\circ$
- $90{}^\circ$

## Explanation

Angles are measured from the normal to the interface. The law preserves the product of index and the sine of the angle across the two media.

$$
\sin\theta_2=\frac{\sin45^\circ}{\sqrt2}=\frac12,\qquad\theta_2=30^\circ.
$$
