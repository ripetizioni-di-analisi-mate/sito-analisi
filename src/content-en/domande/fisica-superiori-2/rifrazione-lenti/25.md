A ray enters a material of index $\sqrt2$ from air at incidence $45^\circ$. What is the refracted angle from the normal?

- $30{}^\circ$
- $45{}^\circ$
- $60{}^\circ$
- $90{}^\circ$

## Explanation

Substituting $\sin45^\circ=\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$ cancels the factor $\sqrt2$. In the first quadrant a sine of one half corresponds to $30^\circ$. The refracted ray bends towards the normal, as required by a higher index than air.

$$
\sin\theta_2=\frac{\sin45^\circ}{\sqrt2}=\frac{1}{2},\qquad\theta_2=30^\circ.
$$
