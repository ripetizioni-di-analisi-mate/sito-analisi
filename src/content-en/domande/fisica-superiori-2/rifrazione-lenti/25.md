A transparent object is almost invisible when immersed in a liquid. Neglect absorption and surface roughness. Which comparison of refractive indices explains why even oblique rays change direction very little?

- The object's and liquid's indices are nearly equal.
- The index of the liquid is always exactly one.
- The object's index is much greater than the liquid's.
- The product of the indices must be zero.

## Explanation

In Snell's law the ratio of the indices sets the ratio of the sines of the angles. When the indices are nearly equal, the ray crosses the boundary with very little deflection.
