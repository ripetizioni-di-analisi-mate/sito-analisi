What direction does the transmitted ray approach at the ideal critical angle?

- Normal to the surface.
- Equal to 45° for any index.
- Opposite the incoming ray along the same line.
- Parallel to the surface.

## Explanation

The transmitted sine is one and its angle from the normal is $90^\circ$. The direction therefore lies along the surface. A small decrease in incidence takes the ray into the outer medium at an angle below ninety degrees.
