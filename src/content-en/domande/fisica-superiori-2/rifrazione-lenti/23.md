A right isosceles triangular prism of index $1.50$ is in air. A ray enters perpendicular to one leg and meets the hypotenuse at an incidence angle of $45^\circ$. Follow the ray reflected at the hypotenuse that then exits the other leg. What angle does the final ray make with the initial ray?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 linee="0,0 0,4 4,0 0,0" segmenti="1.53,1.13,2.87,2.47" vettori="-1.4,1.8,2.2,1.8;2.2,1.8,2.2,-1.2" etichette="-1.15,2.16,incident;2.53,-0.76,emerging;2.88,2.52,normal" xmin=-1.75 xmax=4.8 ymin=-1.5 ymax=4.35 titolo="The ray enters along the normal to the first leg and meets the hypotenuse of the prism."}

- $45^\circ$
- $0^\circ$
- $90^\circ$
- $135^\circ$

## Explanation

The ray reaches the hypotenuse without bending on entry. The critical angle into air is about $41.8^\circ$, so at $45^\circ$ total internal reflection occurs. The propagation directions make an angle of $180^\circ-2\cdot45^\circ=90^\circ$ with one another. The ray leaves the other leg along its normal and keeps the $90^\circ$ deflection.
