In air the ray makes $30^\circ$ with the normal. The glass has $n=1.50$. What is the sine of the refracted angle?

- $0.5$
- $0.75$
- $0.33333333$
- $0.66666667$

## Explanation

Angles are measured from the normal to the interface. The law preserves the product of index and the sine of the angle across the two media.

$$
\sin\theta_2=\frac{\sin30^\circ}{1.50}=\frac13.
$$
