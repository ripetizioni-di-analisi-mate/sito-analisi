A ray from a lower-index material strikes a higher-index material at an oblique angle. What happens to the angle from the normal?

- It increases.
- It decreases.
- It remains identical for every case.
- It becomes 90° for every incidence.

## Explanation

To keep the product of index and sine constant, the higher index goes with a smaller sine. Between zero and ninety degrees, sine increases with angle. A smaller sine therefore means a direction closer to the normal.
