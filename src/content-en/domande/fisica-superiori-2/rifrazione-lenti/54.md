A ray from a lower-index material strikes a higher-index material at an oblique angle. What happens to the angle from the normal?

- It increases.
- It decreases.
- It remains identical for every case.
- It becomes 90° for every incidence.

## Explanation

The larger index multiplies the transmitted sine. To give the same product as at incidence, the sine needs to be smaller.
