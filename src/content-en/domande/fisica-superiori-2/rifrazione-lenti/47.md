Two thin lenses are in contact in air. The required equivalent power is $+2\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$ and the first lens has power $+6\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$. What power must the second lens have?

- $-4\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $-8\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$

## Explanation

The final power must be four dioptres lower than the initial one. The added lens in contact must therefore have negative power. Adding the two signed values gives the required two dioptres.

$$
D_2=(2-6)\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}=-4\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}.
$$
