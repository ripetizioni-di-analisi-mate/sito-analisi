A ray crosses a parallel-sided slab with the same medium outside both sides. What can change even while the outgoing direction stays parallel?

- Frequency at each crossing.
- The final angle necessarily.
- The lateral position of the emerging ray.
- Speed in the final outside medium, which is the same.

## Explanation

At oblique incidence with different indices, the ray bends on entry. It then reaches the second face at a different point from where the original line would meet it. On leaving, it resumes its original direction from that new point.
