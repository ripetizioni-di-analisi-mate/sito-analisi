A converging lens of $f=8\,\mathrm{cm}$ has an object at $p=10\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find the screen distance for the focused image.

- $40\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $18\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $16\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $20\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

For the thin lens use signed distances in the same unit. The sign of $q$ gives the side and the sign of $M$ gives orientation.

$$
q=\frac{8\cdot10}{10-8}\,\mathrm{cm}=40\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
