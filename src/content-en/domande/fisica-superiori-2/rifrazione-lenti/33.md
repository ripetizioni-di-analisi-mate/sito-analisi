A converging lens of $f=8\,\mathrm{cm}$ has an object at $p=10\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find the screen distance for the focused image.

- $40\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $18\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $16\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $20\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The object is just beyond the focal length. The denominator for $q$ is only $2\,\mathrm{cm}$, so the screen is far from the lens. The value is positive, and the enlarged image can be caught on the output side.

$$
q=\frac{8\cdot10}{10-8}\,\mathrm{cm}=40\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
