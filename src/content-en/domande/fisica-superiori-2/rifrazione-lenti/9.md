A transparent material has refractive index $1.7$. Use $c=3\cdot10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the speed of light in the material? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $1.76471\cdot10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $5.1\cdot10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $3\cdot10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $8.82353\cdot10^{7}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

The refractive index is the ratio $n=c/v$. To isolate $v$, divide vacuum speed by the dimensionless index. Since $n>1$ for the stated material, $v<c$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v &= \frac{c}{n} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{\left(3\cdot10^{8}\right)}{\left(1.7\right)} \\[6pt]
&\simeq 1.76471\cdot10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
