A pulse travels at normal incidence through $0.60\,\mathrm m$ of index $1.40$ and then $0.40\,\mathrm m$ of index $2.15$. Neglect dispersion and use $c=3\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is its average speed over the full metre? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $1.76471\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.14\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.40\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.00\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Find each time by dividing its distance by its speed. Then add the times to get $t=\dfrac{0.60\cdot1.40+0.40\cdot2.15}{3\cdot10^8}\,\mathrm s$. The numerator is $1.70$. Dividing the full metre by that time gives the required speed. It lies between the speeds in the individual segments.
