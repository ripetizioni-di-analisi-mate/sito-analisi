A lens has focal length $+20\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find the power in dioptres.

- $0.05\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $-5\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$

## Explanation

The focal length of $20\,\mathrm{cm}$ must first be written as two tenths of a metre. Its reciprocal is five per metre, with the positive sign of a converging lens. Taking the reciprocal in centimetres would give different units.

$$
D=\frac{1}{0.20\,\mathrm m}=5\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}.
$$
