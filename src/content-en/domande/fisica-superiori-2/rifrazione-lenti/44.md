A lens has focal length $+20\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find the power in dioptres.

- $0.05\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $-5\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$

## Explanation

Optical power is the reciprocal of the signed focal length in metres. For lenses in contact the powers add in the stated model.

$$
D=\frac{1}{0.20\,\mathrm m}=5\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}.
$$
