For the same monochromatic light the speed is $1.50\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in medium A and $2.85\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in medium B. What is the critical angle for light travelling from A to B, measured from the normal? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $58.2^\circ$
- $22.5^\circ$
- $45.0^\circ$
- $31.8^\circ$

## Explanation

Medium A has the higher index because light travels more slowly there. At the threshold the sine of the transmitted angle is one. The index ratio $\dfrac{n_B}{n_A}$ equals the inverse speed ratio $\dfrac{v_A}{v_B}$. Thus $\theta_c=\arcsin\!\left(\dfrac{1.50}{2.85}\right)\simeq31.8^\circ$. The complementary angle would be measured from the surface.
