Light goes from a material of refractive index $1.9$ into air of refractive index $1$. What is the critical angle, measured from the normal in the incident medium? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $58.2431{}^\circ$.
- $15.8784{}^\circ$.
- $90{}^\circ$.
- $31.7569{}^\circ$.

## Explanation

The initial index is larger. The critical angle is the incidence yielding a limiting ray along the interface. Set $\sin\theta_2=1$ in Snell’s law and obtain $\theta_c=\arcsin(n_2/n_1)$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\theta_c &= \frac{180 \arcsin{\left(\frac{n_2}{n_1} \right)}}{\pi} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{180 \arcsin{\left(\frac{\left(1\right)}{\left(1.9\right)} \right)}}{\pi} \\[6pt]
&\simeq 31.7569{}^\circ
\end{aligned}
$$
