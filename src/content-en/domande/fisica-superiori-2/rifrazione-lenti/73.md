How is a converging lens used as a magnifier to see an upright image?

- Put the object beyond twice the focal length.
- Put the object at the focus for a finite image.
- Always use a screen in any position.
- Put the object inside the focal length.

## Explanation

For $0<p<f$, the denominator $p-f$ is negative. The rays emerge diverging, and their backward extensions meet on the object side. The magnification is $M=\dfrac{f}{f-p}$, positive and greater than one. Its sign gives an upright image, and its magnitude shows enlargement.
