A ray makes $30^\circ$ with the surface before entering a medium of index $1.50$ from air. Round the refracted angle from the normal to a tenth.

- $19.5{}^\circ$
- $35.3{}^\circ$
- $54.7{}^\circ$
- $60{}^\circ$

## Explanation

Angles are measured from the normal to the interface. The law preserves the product of index and the sine of the angle across the two media.

$$
\theta_2=\arcsin\!\left(\frac{\sin60^\circ}{1.50}\right)\simeq35.3^\circ.
$$
