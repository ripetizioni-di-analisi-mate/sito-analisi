A ray in glass of index $1.50$ makes $30^\circ$ with the surface. In two tests the outer medium is air of index $1.00$ or water of index $\dfrac{4}{3}$. In which test does total internal reflection occur?

- In both tests.
- Only in the test with air.
- Only in the test with water.
- In neither test.

## Explanation

Incidence from the normal is $60^\circ$. Into air, the required sine is $1.50\sin60^\circ\simeq1.30$, above one. Into water it is $\dfrac{3\cdot1.50}{4}\sin60^\circ\simeq0.974$, below one. Only for air does the law give no outward propagating direction. The initial medium and incidence are the same in both tests.
