From glass of $n=1.5$ to air, incidence is $60^\circ$ from the normal. Total internal reflection occurs. What is the reflected angle from the normal?

- $30{}^\circ$
- $90{}^\circ$
- $42{}^\circ$
- $60{}^\circ$

## Explanation

Reflection sends the ray back into the glass. The incident and reflected rays make equal angles with the normal on opposite sides of it. The sixty degrees describe the reflection without requiring a transmitted angle.

$$
\theta_r=\theta_i=60^\circ.
$$
