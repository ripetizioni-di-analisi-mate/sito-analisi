A lens has an object at $p=16\,\mathrm{cm}$ and a real image at $q=48\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its focal length?

- $16\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $24\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $48\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Both distances are positive, so the reciprocal of the focal length is their positive sum. The common denominator is $48\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the numerator is four. The focal length is below both distances, as expected for a sum of reciprocals.

$$
f=\frac{16\cdot48}{16+48}\,\mathrm{cm}=12\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
