A lens has an object at $p=16\,\mathrm{cm}$ and a real image at $q=48\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its focal length?

- $16\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $24\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $48\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

For the thin lens use signed distances in the same unit. The sign of $q$ gives the side and the sign of $M$ gives orientation.

$$
f=\frac{16\cdot48}{16+48}\,\mathrm{cm}=12\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
