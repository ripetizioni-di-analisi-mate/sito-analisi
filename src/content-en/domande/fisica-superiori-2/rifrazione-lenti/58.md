Reverse a path that was transmitted across two materials. What property of the law recovers the initial ray?

- The law uses only the second index.
- The angle to the normal always changes by 90°.
- Swapping both indices and angles preserves equality.
- The incident sine is always zero.

## Explanation

The two sides of the equation have the same form. Swapping them describes the reverse path.
