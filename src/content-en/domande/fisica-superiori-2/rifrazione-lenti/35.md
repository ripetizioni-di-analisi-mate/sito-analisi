A converging lens of focal length $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ gives a real image at $q=18\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find the object distance.

- $6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $30\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $36\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $9\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

For the thin lens use signed distances in the same unit. The sign of $q$ gives the side and the sign of $M$ gives orientation.

$$
p=\frac{12\cdot18}{18-12}\,\mathrm{cm}=36\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
