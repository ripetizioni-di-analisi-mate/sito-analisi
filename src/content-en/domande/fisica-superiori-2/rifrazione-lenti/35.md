A converging lens of focal length $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ gives a real image at $q=18\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find the object distance.

- $6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $30\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $36\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $9\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Subtract the reciprocal of the image distance from that of the focal length. Since $q>f$, the reciprocal of $p$ is positive. Here $q$ is between one and two focal lengths, and the object is indeed beyond two focal lengths.

$$
p=\frac{12\cdot18}{18-12}\,\mathrm{cm}=36\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
