A converging lens forms real images while the object moves away along the axis. What distance does the image position approach for a very distant object?

- Twice the focal length in all cases.
- The optical centre.
- The focal length on the output side.
- The object distance itself.

## Explanation

As the object distance increases, its reciprocal gets smaller. In the relation that determines $q$, almost only the reciprocal focal length remains. Rays from a distant object arrive almost parallel and gather close to the output focus.
