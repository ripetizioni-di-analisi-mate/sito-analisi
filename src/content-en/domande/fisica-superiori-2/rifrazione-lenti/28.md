From air a ray enters a parallel-sided slab at $40^\circ$ from the normal. It leaves the other face into air. What is the emerging angle from the normal?

- $0{}^\circ$
- $20{}^\circ$
- $50{}^\circ$
- $40{}^\circ$

## Explanation

The two normals are parallel. The segment inside can follow a different angle, but the final sine from the normal returns to its entry value. The outgoing line may still be displaced sideways.

$$
\sin\theta_{\mathrm{uscita}}=\sin40^\circ,\qquad\theta_{\mathrm{uscita}}=40^\circ.
$$
