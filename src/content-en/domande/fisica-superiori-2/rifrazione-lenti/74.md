What image does an isolated diverging lens give with a real object in the thin-lens model?

- Real, inverted and enlarged.
- Real and upright.
- Virtual but always the same size as the object.
- Virtual, upright and reduced.

## Explanation

The negative value of $f$ puts $q$ on the object side. In magnitude $q$ is smaller than $p$ because the denominator adds $p$ and the magnitude of $f$. The image is therefore upright and smaller than the object.
