Two thin lenses of focal lengths $+50$ and $-25\,\mathrm{cm}$ are placed in contact in air. What is their combined power?

- $2\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$

## Explanation

Convert the focal lengths to metres before taking their reciprocals. The first lens gives two dioptres and the second gives negative four. The lens with shorter focal length in magnitude dominates, so the lenses in contact are diverging overall.

$$
D=\frac{1}{0.50\,\mathrm m}+\frac{1}{-0.25\,\mathrm m}=-2\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}.
$$
