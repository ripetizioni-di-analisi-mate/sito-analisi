Two thin lenses of focal lengths $+50$ and $-25\,\mathrm{cm}$ are placed in contact in air. What is their combined power?

- $2\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$

## Explanation

Optical power is the reciprocal of the signed focal length in metres. For lenses in contact the powers add in the stated model.

$$
D=\frac{1}{0.50\,\mathrm m}+\frac{1}{-0.25\,\mathrm m}=-2\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}.
$$
