Light has wavelength $480\,\mathrm{nm}$ in vacuum and $320\,\mathrm{nm}$ in the medium. What index follows?

- $0.66666667$
- $1.3333333$
- $2$
- $1.5$

## Explanation

The same $c$ and frequency apply across the stationary interface. The product $nv$ is $c$, and $n\lambda$ is the vacuum wavelength.

$$
n=\frac{480}{320}=1.5.
$$
