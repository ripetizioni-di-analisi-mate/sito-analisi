Light has wavelength $480\,\mathrm{nm}$ in vacuum and $320\,\mathrm{nm}$ in the medium. What index follows?

- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $2$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$

## Explanation

At equal frequency, the wavelength decreases in the same proportion as speed. For the index, put the vacuum wavelength in the numerator. The result is one and a half, since $320\,\mathrm{nm}$ is two thirds of $480\,\mathrm{nm}$. The nanometres cancel in the ratio.

$$
n=\frac{480}{320}=\frac{3}{2}.
$$
