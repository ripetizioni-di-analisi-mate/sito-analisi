The lens has negative optical power. What property matches the model?

- It is always converging.
- It is diverging.
- It has positive finite focal length.
- It always has the same direction as a prism.

## Explanation

The reciprocal of a negative quantity remains negative. The signed focal length is therefore negative. A beam entering parallel leaves diverging, and its extensions pass through the focus on the object side.
