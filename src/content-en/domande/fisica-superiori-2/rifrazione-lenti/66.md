A ray parallel to the axis crosses a converging lens in the thin model. Where does it pass after the lens?

- Through a virtual focus behind the object.
- Always through the optical centre.
- Along the axis without crossing it.
- Through the focus on the output side.

## Explanation

Another incoming parallel ray at a higher or lower height is directed to the same point on the axis. That point is the output focus. The rule applies in the paraxial approximation and supplies one line for the image construction.
