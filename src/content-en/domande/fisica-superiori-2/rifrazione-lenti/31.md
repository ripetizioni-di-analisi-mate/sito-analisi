A critical angle of $30^\circ$ into air is measured. What is the index of the initial medium?

- $0.5$
- $1$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

At the critical angle, the transmitted sine is one. The threshold exists if the ray starts in the higher-index medium.

$$
n_1=\frac{1}{\sin30^\circ}=2.
$$
