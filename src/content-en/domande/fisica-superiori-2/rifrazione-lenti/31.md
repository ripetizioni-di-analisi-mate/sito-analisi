A critical angle of $30^\circ$ into air is measured. What is the index of the initial medium?

- $0.5$
- $1$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

At the threshold the ray in air grazes the surface. Taking air to have index one reduces the law to $n_1\sin30^\circ=1$. The initial medium must compensate for the sine factor of one half.

$$
n_1=\frac{1}{\sin30^\circ}=2.
$$
