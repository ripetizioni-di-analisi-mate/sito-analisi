A ray starts in a material of index $2$ with critical angle $45^\circ$ towards a second medium. What is the second index to the nearest hundredth?

- $0.71$
- $1.41$
- $2.83$
- $1$

## Explanation

At the critical angle, the transmitted sine is one. The threshold exists if the ray starts in the higher-index medium.

$$
n_2=2\sin45^\circ=\sqrt2\simeq1.41.
$$
