A ray starts in a material of index $2$ with critical angle $45^\circ$ towards a second medium. What is the second index to the nearest hundredth?

- $0.71$
- $1.41$
- $2.83$
- $1$

## Explanation

The sine of $45^\circ$ is $\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$. Multiplying it by the initial index two gives $\sqrt2$. The new index is lower than the initial one, as required for a critical angle to exist.

$$
n_2=2\sin45^\circ=\sqrt2\simeq1.41.
$$
