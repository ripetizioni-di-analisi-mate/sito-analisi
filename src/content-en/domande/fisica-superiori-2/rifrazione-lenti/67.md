An incident ray parallel to the axis passes through a thin diverging lens. Where does the backward extension of the outgoing ray pass?

- Through a real focus on the opposite side.
- Through the focus on the object side.
- Through any object position.
- The extension cannot be drawn.

## Explanation

The ray moves away from the axis on the output side. Following it forward does not bring it to the axis. Extending its line back towards the source, however, puts the intersection at the focus on the object side.
