A lens has power $-2.5\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$. What is the focal length in centimetres?

- $-40\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $40\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-0.4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-25\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Optical power is the reciprocal of the signed focal length in metres. For lenses in contact the powers add in the stated model.

$$
f=\frac1{-2.5}\,\mathrm m=-0.40\,\mathrm m=-40\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
