A block slides down a smooth $30^\circ$ slope from rest. After $2\,\mathrm{s}$ what is its speed with $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$?

- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $17.321\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Acceleration down the slope is 5 m/s². Multiply by duration to find velocity from rest.

$$
v=(10\sin30^\circ)\cdot2\,\mathrm{m/s}=10\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$

The angle and $g$ are fixed, so the acceleration along the plane stays constant. The final velocity points downhill.
