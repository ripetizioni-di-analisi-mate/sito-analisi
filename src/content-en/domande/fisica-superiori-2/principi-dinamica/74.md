A horizontal push alone exceeds maximum static friction. Which conclusion fits the model?

- The contact cannot maintain rest
- The block stays at rest by exceeding the static-friction limit
- The normal force automatically vanishes
- Kinetic friction must exactly equal the push

## Explanation

Zero net force would need friction above its limit. The condition for remaining at rest therefore fails.
