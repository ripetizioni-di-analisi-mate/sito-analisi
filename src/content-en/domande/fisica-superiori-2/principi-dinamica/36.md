A $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ block remains in contact with a smooth $60^\circ$ slope. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the normal force?

- $10\,\mathrm{N}$
- $40\,\mathrm{N}$
- $34.641\,\mathrm{N}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The normal force balances only the perpendicular projection of weight. That projection uses the cosine of the slope angle.

$$
N=4\cdot10\cos60^\circ\,\mathrm{N}=20\,\mathrm{N}.
$$

The block has zero acceleration perpendicular to the surface while it follows the plane. The parallel component of weight still acts along the ramp.
