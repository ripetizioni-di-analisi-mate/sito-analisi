A $5\,\mathrm{kg}$ block slides rightwards and is pushed by $25\,\mathrm{N}$ in that direction. With $\mu_d=0.2$ on horizontal ground and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is its rightward acceleration?

- $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $7\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Friction is 10 N and acts oppositely. Calculate acceleration after subtracting this contribution from the push.

$$
a_x=\frac{25-0.2\cdot5\cdot10}{5}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.
$$

The net force is $15\,\mathrm N$ to the right. Because the block is already sliding, the current relative motion determines which coefficient to use.
