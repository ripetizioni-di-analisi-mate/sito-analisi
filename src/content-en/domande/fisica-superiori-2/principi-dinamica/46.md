A block at rest on a level surface has normal force $80\,\mathrm{N}$ and $\mu_s=0.3$. What is the greatest horizontal push that can be balanced without sliding?

- $8\,\mathrm{N}$
- $24\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$
- $80\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Static friction adjusts to the push up to a limit. The maximum push it can balance is therefore coefficient times normal force.

$$
F_{\max}=\mu_s N=0.3\cdot80\,\mathrm{N}=24\,\mathrm{N}.
$$

A push of $20\,\mathrm N$ could be balanced by $20\,\mathrm N$ of friction. The $24\,\mathrm N$ value is reached at the threshold.
