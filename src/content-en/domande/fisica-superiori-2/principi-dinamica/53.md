A moving cart on a smooth track has zero net force. What happens in the inertial track frame?

- It stops immediately
- It reverses after a time
- It maintains the same velocity vector
- It increases its speed linearly

## Explanation

With zero net force Newton’s first law preserves the velocity vector. The initial value determines what velocity is maintained.
