A model vehicle follows a curve of radius $4\,\mathrm{m}$ at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ under a radial net force of $12\,\mathrm{N}$. What is its mass?

- $1\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $3\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Speed squared divided by radius gives 4 m/s². To find mass divide net force by that acceleration magnitude.

$$
m=\frac{12}{\frac{4^2}{4}}\,\mathrm{kg}=3\,\mathrm{kg}.
$$

Putting the mass back into the second law gives $3\cdot4\,\mathrm N=12\,\mathrm N$. The acceleration depends only on the specified path and speed.
