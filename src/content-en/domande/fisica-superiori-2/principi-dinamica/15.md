Three horizontal pushes of $4$, $7$ and $9\,\mathrm{N}$ act on the same object. The first two point right and the third left. What is the net force with right positive?

- $-2\,\mathrm{N}$
- $16\,\mathrm{N}$
- $2\,\mathrm{N}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Add signed force components. The rightward pushes give 11 N together, then subtract the opposing contribution.

$$
F_x=4+7-9=2\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
