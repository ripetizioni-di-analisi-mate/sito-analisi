An object of mass $5\,\mathrm{kg}$ follows a circle of radius $6\,\mathrm{m}$ at constant speed $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the magnitude of the radial net force? Choose the closest value.

- $20.417\,\mathrm{N}$
- $81.667\,\mathrm{N}$
- $40.833\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-40.833\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Speed is constant even while the vector changes direction. Radial acceleration is speed squared divided by radius, giving $8.1667\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The second law multiplies this by mass, yielding $40.833\,\mathrm{N}$ towards the centre. This is the required total component and may come from different interactions depending on the problem. Other components cancel in uniform circular motion.

$$
\begin{aligned}
a_c &= \frac{7^2}{6}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}\simeq 8.1667\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \\[6pt]
F_R &= ma_c \simeq 40.833\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
