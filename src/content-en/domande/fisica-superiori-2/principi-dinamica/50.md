What does an arrow in a free-body diagram of a book represent?

- The force the book exerts on another object
- The book’s position at every time
- Always a velocity
- A force acting on the book

## Explanation

The diagram isolates the book. Each arrow is therefore a contribution to the sum of forces on that book.
