A $5\,\mathrm{kg}$ object rests on a horizontal surface with no other vertical forces. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the vertical net force?

- $50\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-50\,\mathrm{N}$
- $100\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Weight and normal force act on the same object. Rest means zero acceleration, so the net force is zero too.

$$
F_{Ry}=50-50=0\,\mathrm{N}.
$$

Weight and the normal force both have magnitude $50\,\mathrm N$. The zero result refers to their vector sum.
