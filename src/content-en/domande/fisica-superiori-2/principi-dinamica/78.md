During a bend at constant speed, where does the horizontal net force point?

- Outwards
- Along the tangent in the direction of motion
- Towards the centre of the bend
- Net force is necessarily zero

## Explanation

Centripetal acceleration points radially inward. By the second law, net force points the same way.
