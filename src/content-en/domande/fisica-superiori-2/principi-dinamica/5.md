A block of mass $6\,\mathrm{kg}$ slides on a horizontal surface with kinetic friction coefficient $0.25$. Use $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the magnitude of friction.

- $15\,\mathrm{N}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $30\,\mathrm{N}$
- $75\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The block is sliding, so use the kinetic coefficient. First find the normal force by adding the vertical forces, where acceleration is zero. Weight is $60\,\mathrm{N}$ and the upwards pull in addition to the normal force is $0\,\mathrm{N}$, so the contact supplies $60\,\mathrm{N}$. The coefficient is dimensionless, so the product is in newtons. Friction points against sliding.

$$
\begin{aligned}
N &= mg-F_y=60-0=60\,\mathrm{N} \\[6pt]
f_d &= \mu_d N=0.25\cdot 60\,\mathrm{N}=15\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
