Which way does acceleration point for an object of positive mass and non-zero net force?

- In the direction of the net force
- Opposite to the net force
- Always along velocity
- Always downward

## Explanation

By the second law, acceleration is net force multiplied by the positive reciprocal of mass.

Dividing by the mass changes the magnitude and preserves the direction. The instantaneous velocity may point elsewhere, as it does along the tangent on a bend.
