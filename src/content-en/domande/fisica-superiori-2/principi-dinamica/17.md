A cart has a rightward net force of $21\,\mathrm{N}$ that produces acceleration $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the mass?

- $7\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $3\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $18\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $63\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Isolate mass by dividing net force by acceleration. A force of 21 N gives three metres per second squared, so the mass is 7 kg.

$$
m=\frac{21}{3}\,\mathrm{kg}=7\,\mathrm{kg}.
$$

The two arrows must point the same way for a positive mass. The ratio is measured in kilograms because $\mathrm N=\mathrm{kg\,m/s^2}$.
