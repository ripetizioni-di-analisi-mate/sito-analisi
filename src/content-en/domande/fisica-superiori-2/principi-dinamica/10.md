A $5\,\mathrm{kg}$ sample rests on a scale in a lift. The scale exerts an upward normal force of $65\,\mathrm N$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the acceleration component when upwards is positive?

- $-3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $13\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $23\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The sample experiences the upward normal force and its $50\,\mathrm N$ weight downwards. The net force is $(65-50)\,\mathrm N=15\,\mathrm N$, so $a_y=\dfrac{15}{5}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The positive sign shows that the acceleration is upwards. The lift could also be moving downwards as it slows.
