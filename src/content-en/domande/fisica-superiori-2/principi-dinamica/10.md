An object of mass $7\,\mathrm{kg}$ has an acceleration magnitude of $3.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the magnitude of its net force.

- $12.25\,\mathrm{N}$
- $24.5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $49\,\mathrm{N}$
- $73.5\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The given acceleration is caused by all forces together. Newton’s second law gives net force by multiplying $7\,\mathrm{kg}$ by $3.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The product of the units is the newton. The sum of forces points in the same direction as acceleration. The relation determines net force and leaves the separate contributions undetermined.

$$
\begin{aligned}
F_R &= ma=7\cdot(3.5)\,\mathrm{N} = 24.5\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
