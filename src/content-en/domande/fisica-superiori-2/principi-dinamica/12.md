On a smooth horizontal surface, two parallel springs pull the same $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ block to the left. Their spring constants are $120\,\mathrm{N/m}$ and $180\,\mathrm{N/m}$ and both are extended by $4\,\mathrm{cm}$ within their linear ranges. There are no other horizontal forces. What is the magnitude of the acceleration at that instant?

- $2.4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3.6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Both restoring forces point to the left, so their magnitudes add. With $4\,\mathrm{cm}=0.04\,\mathrm m$, the horizontal net force has magnitude $(120+180)\cdot0.04\,\mathrm N=12\,\mathrm N$. Dividing by the $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass gives $6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. This value describes the stated instant because the forces change as the extension changes.
