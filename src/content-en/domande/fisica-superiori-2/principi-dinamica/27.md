A spring has $k=300\,\mathrm{N/m}$. By how much is it extended if its restoring force is $15\,\mathrm{N}$ in the linear range?

- $0.2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.05\,\mathrm{m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Isolate displacement in the linear law. Dividing newtons by newtons per metre gives metres.

$$
x=\frac{15}{300}\,\mathrm{m}=0.05\,\mathrm{m}.
$$

The result is equivalent to $5\,\mathrm{cm}$. For a spring of the same stiffness, doubling the extension would double the restoring force within the linear model.
