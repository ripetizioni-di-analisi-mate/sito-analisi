On a smooth ramp the magnitude of the gravitational acceleration component along the plane is $6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, with $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is $\sin\alpha$?

- $0.8$
- $0.6$
- $1.6667$
- $0.36$

## Explanation

The down-slope component is $g\sin\alpha$. Its ratio to $g$ directly gives sine, a dimensionless number.

$$
\sin\alpha=\frac{6}{10}=0.6.
$$

The value lies between zero and one, as expected for a ramp at an acute angle. With the surface smooth, the parallel acceleration comes from weight.
