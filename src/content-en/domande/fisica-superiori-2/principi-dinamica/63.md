Why does a third-law pair not add to zero in a free-body diagram for one object?

- Both arrows always point the same way
- The forces occur at different times
- The reaction is not a force
- The forces act on two different objects

## Explanation

The diagram contains the force received by the chosen object. The other belongs to the diagram of the object it interacts with.
