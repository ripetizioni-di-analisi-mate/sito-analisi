On a planet a $5\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass weighs $20\,\mathrm{N}$. What is the local gravitational acceleration magnitude?

- $0.25\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $100\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Weight is a force and local gravity is that force divided by mass. Newtons per kilogram equal metres per second squared.

$$
g=\frac{20}{5}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.
$$

The same value gives the weight per kilogram on that planet. Multiplying by the stated mass again recovers the measured $20\,\mathrm N$.
