On smooth ice, a $40\,\mathrm{kg}$ skater pushes another with $80\,\mathrm{N}$ eastward. What is the first skater's acceleration magnitude?

- $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $80\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The first gets 80 N westward by Newton’s third law. Divide by the first skater’s own mass for acceleration.

$$
a=\frac{80}{40}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.
$$
