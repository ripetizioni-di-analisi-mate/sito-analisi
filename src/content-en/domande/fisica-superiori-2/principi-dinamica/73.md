A small push is increased on a resting block without reaching the threshold. What change follows?

- The normal force vanishes
- The block must have kinetic friction
- Static friction increases to balance the greater push
- The static coefficient becomes the push

## Explanation

While the block is at rest, horizontal net force is zero. The contact supplies actual friction equal and opposite to the push.
