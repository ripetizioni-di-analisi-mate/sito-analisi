The magnitude of the only horizontal push on a resting block is increased without reaching the static friction limit. What happens?

- The normal force vanishes
- The block must have kinetic friction
- Static friction increases to balance the greater push
- The static coefficient becomes the push

## Explanation

While the block is at rest, horizontal net force is zero. The contact supplies actual friction equal and opposite to the push.

While the force needed stays below the threshold, friction follows the push. The coefficient and normal force determine how much friction the contact can supply.
