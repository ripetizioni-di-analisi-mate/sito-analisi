An ideal spring's force has magnitude $6\,\mathrm{N}$ at extension $0.02\,\mathrm{m}$. What is its magnitude at $0.035\,\mathrm{m}$, in the linear range?

- $10.5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $9\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The force ratio equals the displacement ratio because the spring constant is unchanged. The new displacement is 1.75 times the original.

$$
F_2=6\frac{0.035}{0.02}\,\mathrm{N}=10.5\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
