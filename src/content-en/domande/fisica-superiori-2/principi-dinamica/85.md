An object has zero velocity at one instant but non-zero net force. Which statement is correct?

- Acceleration is necessarily zero
- Mass is necessarily zero
- The first law requires it to stay at rest
- Acceleration is non-zero at that instant

## Explanation

The second law involves mass and net force, regardless of the velocity at that particular instant.
