Which pair follows the third law during contact between hand and wall?

- Force of hand on wall and weight of wall
- Weight of hand and force of wall on hand
- Force of hand on wall and force of wall on hand
- Two equal forces on the wall

## Explanation

The acting and receiving objects are interchanged in the same contact interaction. That gives the two distinct objects.

The hand's force belongs on the wall's diagram and the wall's force belongs on the hand's. The magnitudes are equal and the directions are opposite.
