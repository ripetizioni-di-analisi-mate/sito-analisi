What is the partner of Earth's gravitational force on a book?

- The table's force on the book
- The book's gravitational force on Earth
- The book's force on the table
- Net force on the book

## Explanation

Exchange the acting and receiving objects while retaining the same interaction. The gravitational pair acts on Earth and the book.

The normal force has a different origin, the contact with the table. To find the partner of the weight, keep the gravitational interaction and swap the two bodies.
