On a level surface a block is lifted vertically until contact is lost. Which friction-model quantity between floor and block vanishes?

- The block's mass
- The block's weight
- The normal force and therefore the contact friction force
- Gravitational acceleration

## Explanation

Without contact the surface exerts no normal force. The friction limit depends on that force, so the contribution from that surface vanishes.

Weight still acts after separation. To determine acceleration, add weight and any other forces that continue to act on the block.
