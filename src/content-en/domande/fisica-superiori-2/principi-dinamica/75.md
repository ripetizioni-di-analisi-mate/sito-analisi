Why is it convenient to choose an axis parallel to an inclined plane?

- Weight has zero magnitude
- The normal force has zero projection on that axis
- The normal force becomes parallel to the motion
- Acceleration disappears

## Explanation

The normal force is perpendicular to the plane. On the slope axis, only components of other forces along the slope remain.
