A $6\,\mathrm{kg}$ block slides on a smooth $30^\circ$ slope with $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the weight component along the slope?

- $5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $60\,\mathrm{N}$
- $30\,\mathrm{N}$
- $51.962\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Weight is 60 N and its parallel projection is that magnitude times the sine of the inclination.

$$
P_\parallel=60\sin30^\circ\,\mathrm{N}=30\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
