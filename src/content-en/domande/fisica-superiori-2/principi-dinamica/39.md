A $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ ball travels in a circle of radius $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and has radial net force $24\,\mathrm{N}$. What is its speed?

- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $36\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Divide force by mass to get 12 m/s². Multiply by radius for speed squared, then take the positive square root.

$$
v=\sqrt{\frac{24\cdot3}{2}}\,\mathrm{m/s}=6\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
