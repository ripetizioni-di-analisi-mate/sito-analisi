Are normal force and weight on a block on a smooth incline opposite vectors?

- Yes, they are always equal and opposite
- Yes, both lie along the slope
- They are always the same vector
- They point in different directions because weight is vertical and the normal is perpendicular to the plane

## Explanation

The normal force balances only the component of weight perpendicular to the surface. The component along the slope remains in the resultant.

If the plane is at $30^\circ$, weight has a component along the ramp equal to half its magnitude. That component determines the downhill acceleration.
