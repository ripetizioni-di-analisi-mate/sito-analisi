On a horizontal surface two carts are joined by a taut ideal string. Cart $A$, of mass $3\,\mathrm{kg}$, is behind cart $B$, of mass $5\,\mathrm{kg}$. Cart $B$ is pulled to the right with $32\,\mathrm N$. During motion, each cart experiences a $4\,\mathrm N$ resistance to the left. What is the tension in the string?

- $13\,\mathrm N$
- $9\,\mathrm N$
- $19\,\mathrm N$
- $24\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

For the two carts together, tension is internal and its contributions cancel. The external forces give $a=\dfrac{32-4-4}{3+5}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. On $A$ alone the string must overcome the resistance and produce that acceleration, so $T-4\,\mathrm N=(3)(3)\,\mathrm N$ and $T=13\,\mathrm N$.
