A cart of mass $7\,\mathrm{kg}$ experiences a horizontal force of $80\,\mathrm{N}$ to the right and $15\,\mathrm{N}$ to the left. Find the acceleration with right positive. Choose the closest value.

- $9.2857\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-9.2857\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $13.571\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $11.429\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

In the ground frame, take the horizontal force components on the cart. The rightward push enters with a positive sign, and the leftward one with a negative sign. Their difference is $65\,\mathrm{N}$. Newton’s second law divides this net force by the mass $7\,\mathrm{kg}$. Acceleration is $9.2857\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and points with the stronger force. The initial velocity does not enter this calculation.

$$
\begin{aligned}
F_{Rx} &= 80-15 = 65\,\mathrm{N} \\[6pt]
a_x &= \frac{65}{7}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}\simeq 9.2857\,\mathrm{m/s^2}
\end{aligned}
$$
