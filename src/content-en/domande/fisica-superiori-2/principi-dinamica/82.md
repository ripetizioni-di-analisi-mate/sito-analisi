If a string breaks while an object moves in a circle on a frictionless level surface, which direction does it initially move afterwards?

- Radially inward
- Radially outward
- Vertically
- Tangent to the circle at the point of breaking

## Explanation

At breakage velocity is tangent and the horizontal force changing its direction disappears. Horizontal velocity retains the direction it already had.
