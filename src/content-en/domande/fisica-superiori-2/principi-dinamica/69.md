If an ideal spring goes from extension to an equal amount of compression, how does signed force change?

- Its magnitude doubles
- It reverses sign with the same magnitude
- It stays the same including sign
- It becomes zero

## Explanation

The product $kx$ changes sign because $x$ changes sign. The extra factor −1 gives a reversed force direction.

For example, at $x=+0.02\,\mathrm m$ the force is negative, while at $x=-0.02\,\mathrm m$ it is positive. Its magnitude $k|x|$ stays the same.
