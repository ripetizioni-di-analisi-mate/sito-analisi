A $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ block slides rightwards on level ground with $\mu_d=0.25$, $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and no horizontal push. What is its acceleration with right positive?

- $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-2.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Normal force balances the 40 N weight. Kinetic friction is 10 N to the left, so the horizontal net force is negative.

$$
a_x=-\frac{0.25\cdot40}{4}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=-2.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.
$$
