A block moves on a smooth surface attached to an ideal horizontal spring. It passes to the right through the position where the spring has its natural length. What are its velocity and acceleration at that instant?

- Both zero
- Velocity to the right and zero acceleration
- Zero velocity and acceleration to the left
- Both to the right

## Explanation

At the natural position $x=0$, so the spring exerts $F_e=-kx=0$. Weight and normal force balance, making the net force and acceleration zero at that instant. The velocity still points to the right because the block is passing through that position. Soon the spring deforms and the acceleration changes.
