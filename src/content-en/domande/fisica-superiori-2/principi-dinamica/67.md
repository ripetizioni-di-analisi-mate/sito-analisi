Which quantity determines the direction of the restoring force for a linear spring with $F_e=-kx$?

- The sign of the block's velocity
- The sign of displacement from natural length
- The duration of motion
- The magnitude of its mass

## Explanation

The law contains signed displacement. The minus sign makes force point opposite to the displacement.
