Does zero net force imply that every force is zero?

- Forces can balance while having non-zero magnitudes
- Yes, every force must vanish
- No, the net force must equal the weight
- Yes, but only if the object has positive mass

## Explanation

Two forces of equal magnitude and opposite direction on one object add to zero. The individual contributions still exist.
