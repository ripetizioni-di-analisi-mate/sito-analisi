A truck and a light cart touch. How do the magnitudes of their mutual forces compare at the same instant?

- The truck's force is greater
- The cart's force is greater
- It depends only on the accelerations
- They are equal

## Explanation

The third law compares the two forces in the interaction. Different masses can produce different accelerations despite equal force magnitudes.
