Two identical blocks slide on the same level surface. A load on the second doubles its total weight. What is the ratio of the second friction magnitude to the first?

- $1$
- $0.5$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

Normal force doubles with weight in the absence of other vertical forces. Since the kinetic coefficient is the same, friction doubles too.

$$
\frac{f_{d2}}{f_{d1}}=\frac{\mu_d(2N)}{\mu_d N}=2.
$$
