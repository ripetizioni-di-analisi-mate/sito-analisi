An object of mass $6\,\mathrm{kg}$ has an acceleration magnitude of $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the magnitude of its net force.

- $9\,\mathrm{N}$
- $18\,\mathrm{N}$
- $36\,\mathrm{N}$
- $54\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The given acceleration is caused by all forces together. Newton’s second law gives net force by multiplying $6\,\mathrm{kg}$ by $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The product of the units is the newton. The sum of forces points in the same direction as acceleration. The relation determines net force and leaves the separate contributions undetermined.

$$
\begin{aligned}
F_R &= ma=6\cdot(3)\,\mathrm{N} = 18\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
