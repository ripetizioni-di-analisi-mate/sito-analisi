A cart is pushed by a varying force. When can the second law use the net force measured at one instant?

- To find velocity a minute later directly
- To find acceleration at that instant
- Only if force is always zero
- Only if the cart is already stopped

## Explanation

The second law is an instantaneous relation. Finding a future velocity requires the force history and initial condition.

If the net force changes after the measurement, the acceleration changes too. A force measured at one instant can therefore be used directly only for that instant.
