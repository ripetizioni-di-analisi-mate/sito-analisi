An ideal spring with spring constant $180\,\mathrm{N/m}$ is displaced $0.05\,\mathrm{m}$ from its natural length in the linear range. Find the magnitude of the spring force.

- $-9\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $18\,\mathrm{N}$
- $9\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Hooke’s law holds in the linear range. The minus sign shows restoring action: force points opposite displacement from natural length. Spring constant times the displacement magnitude gives $9\,\mathrm{N}$. Length units cancel, leaving newtons. On the graph the origin is the natural-length state and P is the stated displacement.

$$
\begin{aligned}
F_e &= -kx=-180(0.05)\,\mathrm{N}=-9\,\mathrm{N} \\[6pt]
\lvert F_e\rvert &= 9\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
