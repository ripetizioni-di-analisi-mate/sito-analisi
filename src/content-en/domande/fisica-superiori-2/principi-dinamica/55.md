A bicycle travels at constant velocity along a straight, level road. Resistive forces oppose its motion. How do forward push and resistance compare horizontally?

- Push is always greater
- Same magnitude and opposite directions
- Resistance is always greater
- Both necessarily vanish

## Explanation

Constant velocity means zero acceleration. The sum of the horizontal components must be zero.

The drive force points along the motion and the resistive forces point the other way. A greater drive force would give a forward net force and increase the speed.
