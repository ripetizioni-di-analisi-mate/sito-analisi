A bicycle travels at constant velocity on a real road despite resistance. How do forward push and resistance compare horizontally?

- Push is always greater
- Same magnitude and opposite directions
- Resistance is always greater
- Both necessarily vanish

## Explanation

Constant velocity means zero acceleration. The sum of the horizontal components must be zero.
