A block weighing $100\,\mathrm{N}$ is pressed against a horizontal surface by an extra $20\,\mathrm{N}$ downward force. With $\mu_s=0.25$, what is maximum static friction?

- $30\,\mathrm{N}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N}$
- $25\,\mathrm{N}$
- $40\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The surface supports both the weight and the extra push. The limit is one quarter of the 120 N normal force.

$$
f_{s,\max}=0.25(100+20)\,\mathrm{N}=30\,\mathrm{N}.
$$

The $30\,\mathrm N$ value sets the largest tangential push that can be balanced. With no tangential push, the actual friction would be zero.
