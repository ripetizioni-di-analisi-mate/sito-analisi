Two blocks of unequal mass slide along the same smooth slope. How do their parallel accelerations compare?

- They are equal
- The heavier block has greater acceleration
- The lighter block has greater acceleration
- It depends on the colour of the surface

## Explanation

The weight component and inertia are proportional to the same mass. Dividing cancels the mass factor.
