An object weighs $90\,\mathrm{N}$ where $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. At a second location $g=2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What does it weigh there?

- $18\,\mathrm{N}$
- $9\,\mathrm{N}$
- $45\,\mathrm{N}$
- $90\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Mass is 9 kg at both locations. The second weight uses that mass times the new local gravity.

$$
P_2=\frac{90}{10}\cdot2\,\mathrm{N}=18\,\mathrm{N}.
$$

The second field has one fifth of the first field's strength. The weight therefore drops to one fifth when the same object is moved.
