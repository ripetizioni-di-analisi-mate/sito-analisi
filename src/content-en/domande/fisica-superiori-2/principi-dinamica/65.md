During a jump on rigid ground, which force transmits the vertical push to the body?

- The body's downward force on the ground
- The body's gravitational force on Earth
- The ground's upward force on the body
- An added force called inertia

## Explanation

The body pushes down on the ground. The ground pushes up on the body, which is the contact force to include for the body.

During contact, the upward normal force can exceed weight and produce an upward net force. The ground's normal force drops to zero after take-off.
