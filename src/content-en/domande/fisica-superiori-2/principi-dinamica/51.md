With horizontal and vertical axes, which arrows contribute to horizontal net force?

- The magnitudes of all vertical forces
- Weight only
- Only the largest force
- The horizontal projections of every force

## Explanation

Net force is a vector sum. Along one axis, add only the components on it, with their signs.

An angled force can contribute to both axes. Vertical weight has zero horizontal component because it is perpendicular to that axis.
