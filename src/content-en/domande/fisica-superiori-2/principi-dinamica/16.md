Two perpendicular forces have magnitudes $6$ and $8\,\mathrm{N}$. What is the magnitude of their resultant?

- $2\,\mathrm{N}$
- $8\,\mathrm{N}$
- $14\,\mathrm{N}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Since the two directions are perpendicular, apply Pythagoras to the components of the sum. The magnitude exceeds each leg.

$$
\lvert\vec F_R\rvert=\sqrt{6^2+8^2}\,\mathrm{N}=10\,\mathrm{N}.
$$

The value would be $14\,\mathrm N$ for aligned forces pointing the same way. Here the two contributions make the legs of a triangle, so adding their magnitudes would overestimate the net force.
