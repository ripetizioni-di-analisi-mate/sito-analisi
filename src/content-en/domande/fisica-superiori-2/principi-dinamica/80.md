A ball swings on a string over a frictionless plane. In the horizontal force sum, how should centripetal force be treated?

- Add it to tension as a separate force
- It equals weight
- It is a tangent force on the string

## Explanation

Draw the actual physical interactions. Inward tension already is the net force that changes the direction of velocity.
