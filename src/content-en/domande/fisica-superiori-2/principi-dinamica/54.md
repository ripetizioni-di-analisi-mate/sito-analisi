What indicates that axes fixed to a car are unsuitable as an inertial frame?

- The car has large mass
- The car accelerates relative to the ground
- The wheels have equal radii
- The car is on high ground

## Explanation

Axes attached to an accelerating object accelerate relative to the ground frame, treated as inertial here.
