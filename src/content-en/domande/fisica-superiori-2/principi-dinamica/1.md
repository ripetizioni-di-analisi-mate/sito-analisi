A cart of mass $6\,\mathrm{kg}$ experiences a horizontal force of $70\,\mathrm{N}$ to the right and $13\,\mathrm{N}$ to the left. Find the acceleration with right positive.

- $9.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-9.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $13.833\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $11.667\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

In the ground frame, take the horizontal force components on the cart. The rightward push enters with a positive sign, and the leftward one with a negative sign. Their difference is $57\,\mathrm{N}$. Newton’s second law divides this net force by the mass $6\,\mathrm{kg}$. Acceleration is $9.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and points with the stronger force. The initial velocity does not enter this calculation.

$$
\begin{aligned}
F_{Rx} &= 70-13 = 57\,\mathrm{N} \\[6pt]
a_x &= \frac{57}{6}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}\simeq 9.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}
\end{aligned}
$$
