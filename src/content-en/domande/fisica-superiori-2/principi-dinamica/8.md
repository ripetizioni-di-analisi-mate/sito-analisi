A block initially at rest is pushed with $6\,\mathrm{N}$ to the right. Maximum static friction is $20\,\mathrm{N}$ and the block stays at rest. What is the actual friction magnitude?

- $20\,\mathrm{N}$
- $26\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $6\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

At rest the horizontal net force is zero. The known pushes add with sign to $6\,\mathrm{N}$ to the right. The contact must supply exactly that magnitude to the left. Since $6\,\mathrm{N}$ is below the limit $20\,\mathrm{N}$, rest is consistent. The limit specifies the contact's capacity. Force balance selects the actual value.

$$
\begin{aligned}
F_{Rx}&=6-0-f_s=0 \\[6pt]
f_s&=6\,\mathrm{N}<20\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
