Which measurement determines net force rather than one of many forces acting on an object?

- Initial position alone
- Mass and the acceleration vector
- Instantaneous speed alone
- Distance travelled in one revolution alone

## Explanation

Mass times acceleration gives the sum of forces. Individual forces require additional information about interactions.

A $5\,\mathrm N$ net force to the right can come from a single push or from opposing pushes of $9\,\mathrm N$ and $4\,\mathrm N$.
