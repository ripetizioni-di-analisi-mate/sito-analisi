A load weighs $50\,\mathrm{N}$ and has a $10\,\mathrm{N}$ upward pull while remaining in contact and sliding horizontally. With $\mu_d=0.3$, what is friction magnitude?

- $3\,\mathrm{N}$
- $15\,\mathrm{N}$
- $18\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The pull supplies 10 N of the upward force needed to balance weight. The surface supplies 40 N, so use that normal force for friction.

$$
f_d=0.3(50-10)\,\mathrm{N}=12\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
