Two identical cars travel around bends of different radii at the same constant speed. Which needs the greater net force magnitude?

- The one in the gentler bend
- The forces are always equal
- The one with smaller mass
- The one in the tighter bend

## Explanation

A tighter bend has a smaller radius. Speed squared divided by radius increases, so net force increases.
