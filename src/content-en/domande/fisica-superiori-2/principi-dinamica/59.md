For constant mass, the net force is doubled without changing direction. How does acceleration change?

- It halves
- Its magnitude doubles and direction stays the same
- Only its direction changes
- It quadruples

## Explanation

Mass remains the proportionality factor in the second law. The acceleration vector scales in the same way as force.
