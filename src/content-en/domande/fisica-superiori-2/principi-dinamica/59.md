A $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ cart experiences a horizontal net force of $4\,\mathrm N$ in the first $2\,\mathrm s$, with right positive. In the next $2\,\mathrm s$ the net force changes to $-2\,\mathrm N$. The initial velocity is $-1\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the velocity at the end of the $4\,\mathrm s$?

::grafico{linee="0,4 2,4;2,-2 4,-2" punti="2,4,vuoto;2,-2,pieno" xmin=0 xmax=4.4 ymin=-4 ymax=6 nomi="t (s),F (N)" titolo="The net force changes at 2 s. Each interval lasts two seconds."}

- $-1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The net force gives two constant accelerations, $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and $-1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Multiplying each by its interval duration gives velocity changes of $+4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Add both changes to the initial velocity, giving $v=(-1+4-2)\,\mathrm{m/s}=1\,\mathrm{m/s}$, to the right.
