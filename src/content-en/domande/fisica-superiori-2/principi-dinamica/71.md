A block slides leftward on a fixed mat. Which way does kinetic friction on the block point?

- To the right
- To the left
- Upwards
- Perpendicular to the surface

## Explanation

Kinetic friction opposes relative motion at the surface. The mat is fixed and the block slides leftward.

The block slides left relative to the carpet, so friction on the block points right. If this were the only horizontal force, the block would slow down.
