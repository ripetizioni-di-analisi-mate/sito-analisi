A $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ object has net force components $6$ and $8\,\mathrm{N}$. What is its acceleration magnitude?

- $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $7\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Find the net-force magnitude by Pythagoras and divide by mass. Equivalently, acceleration components are 3 and 4 m/s².

$$
a=\frac{\sqrt{6^2+8^2}}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.
$$

Dividing both components by the same positive factor preserves the vector direction. Both routes therefore give the same magnitude.
