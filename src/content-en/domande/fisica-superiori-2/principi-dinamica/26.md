A linear spring extends by $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ with a restoring-force magnitude of $12\,\mathrm{N}$. What is its spring constant?

- $4\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $400\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $40\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $0.36\,\mathrm{N/m}$

## Explanation

Spring constant is the force magnitude divided by displacement. Converting first gives a displacement of 0.03 m.

$$
k=\frac{12}{0.03}\,\mathrm{N/m}=400\,\mathrm{N/m}.
$$

The units are newtons per metre because the ratio divides a force by a length. Expressed per centimetre, the value would be $4\,\mathrm{N/cm}$.
