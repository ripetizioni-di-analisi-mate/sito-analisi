How does the normal force on a block on a smooth slope change when inclination increases from zero to an acute angle, at fixed mass?

- It increases
- It stays equal to weight
- It decreases
- It reverses sign

## Explanation

Normal force balances $mg\cos\alpha$. Cosine decreases as the acute angle rises, so the magnitude decreases.
