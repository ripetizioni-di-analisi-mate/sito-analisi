Mass is $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ and speed $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Radial net force is $16\,\mathrm{N}$. What is the radius?

- $1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Isolate radius as mass times squared speed divided by radial force. The resulting units are metres.

$$
r=\frac{2\cdot8^2}{16}\,\mathrm{m}=8\,\mathrm{m}.
$$

At the same mass and speed, a smaller radial force would be possible on a larger-radius arc. The radius is a positive distance.
