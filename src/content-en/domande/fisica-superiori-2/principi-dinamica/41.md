An object on a level platform moves at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at radius $2\,\mathrm{m}$. What minimum $\mu_s$ is needed if friction is the only horizontal force and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$?

- $0.8$
- $0.2$
- $0.4$
- $8$

## Explanation

Friction must supply acceleration 8 m/s². The limit is $\mu_smg$, so dividing by mass gives $\mu_s g$.

$$
\mu_s\geq\frac{4^2}{2\cdot10}=0.8.
$$
