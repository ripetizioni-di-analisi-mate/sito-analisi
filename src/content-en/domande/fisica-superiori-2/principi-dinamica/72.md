What is the actual static friction on a resting block with no horizontal pushes on a level surface?

- Always $\mu_sN$
- Equal to weight
- Zero
- Equal to normal force

## Explanation

No other force tends to slide the block. Zero horizontal net force therefore requires zero actual friction.
