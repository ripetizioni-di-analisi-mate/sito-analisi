An object experiences $14\,\mathrm{N}$ rightward and $20\,\mathrm{N}$ leftward. What extra force with right positive gives zero horizontal net force?

- $6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $14\,\mathrm{N}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The existing forces give six newtons leftward. The extra force has the same magnitude and opposite direction.

$$
F_x=-(14-20)\,\mathrm{N}=6\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
