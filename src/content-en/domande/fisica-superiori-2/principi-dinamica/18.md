A $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ object accelerates rightward at $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. One of its two horizontal forces is $5\,\mathrm{N}$ leftward. What is the other force with right positive?

- $3\,\mathrm{N}$
- $13\,\mathrm{N}$
- $5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $8\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Net force is 8 N to the right. The required force must balance the five opposing newtons and leave the remaining eight.

$$
F-5=4\cdot2\quad\Longrightarrow\quad F=13\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
