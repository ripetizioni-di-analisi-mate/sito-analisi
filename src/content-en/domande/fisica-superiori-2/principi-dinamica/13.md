A $5\,\mathrm{kg}$ block is initially at rest on a horizontal surface. The static and kinetic friction coefficients are $0.32$ and $0.20$ respectively. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, a horizontal $20\,\mathrm N$ push is applied to the right. What is the acceleration after applying the push, with right positive?

- $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The normal force is $50\,\mathrm N$, so the static limit is $0.32\cdot50\,\mathrm N=16\,\mathrm N$. The $20\,\mathrm N$ push exceeds that limit and the block slides to the right. Friction is then kinetic and has magnitude $0.20\cdot50\,\mathrm N=10\,\mathrm N$. This gives $a_x=\dfrac{20-10}{5}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
