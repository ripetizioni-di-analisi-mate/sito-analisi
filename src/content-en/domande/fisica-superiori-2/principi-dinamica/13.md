A block of mass $7\,\mathrm{kg}$ slides on a horizontal surface with kinetic friction coefficient $0.3$. Use $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the magnitude of friction.

- $21\,\mathrm{N}$
- $10.5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $42\,\mathrm{N}$
- $91\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The block is sliding, so use the kinetic coefficient. First find the normal force by adding the vertical forces, where acceleration is zero. Weight is $70\,\mathrm{N}$ and the upwards pull in addition to the normal force is $0\,\mathrm{N}$, so the contact supplies $70\,\mathrm{N}$. The coefficient is dimensionless, so the product is in newtons. Friction points against sliding.

$$
\begin{aligned}
N &= mg-F_y=70-0=70\,\mathrm{N} \\[6pt]
f_d &= \mu_d N=0.3\cdot 70\,\mathrm{N}=21\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
