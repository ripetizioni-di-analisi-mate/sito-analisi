A conveyor belt starts moving right under a stationary package. Just after start-up, which way does friction tend to draw the package?

- To the left
- Perpendicular to the belt
- To the right
- Always opposite the belt's motion

## Explanation

Relative to the belt the package tends to slide left. Friction opposes that relative motion and drags the package right.

Find the sign by comparing the velocities at the contact. The parcel can initially be at rest relative to the ground and already move left relative to the belt.
