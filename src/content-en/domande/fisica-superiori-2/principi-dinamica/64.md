Two carts pushing one another are considered as a single system. How do the mutual forces contribute to the net force on the system?

- They cancel in the system sum
- They double the net force
- They necessarily make the whole system rotate
- They give a net force equal to mass

## Explanation

The forces on both carts are included. The internal pair has equal magnitudes and opposite directions, so its total contribution is zero.

The sum of external forces determines how the system's overall motion changes. For either cart on its own, the push from the other is external again.
