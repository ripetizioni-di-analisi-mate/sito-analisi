On a horizontal surface, when does $N=mg$ follow directly?

- With zero vertical acceleration and no other vertical force
- Always, even when the object is airborne
- Even when a rope pulls the object upwards
- Only when the object accelerates upwards

## Explanation

If vertical acceleration is absent the vertical sum is zero. With only weight and normal force, their magnitudes agree.
