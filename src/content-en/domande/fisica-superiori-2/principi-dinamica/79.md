Which interaction can supply the centripetal net force to a car turning on a level road?

- Vertical weight alone
- Vertical normal force alone
- Tangential velocity alone
- Friction between road and tyres

## Explanation

The required net force is horizontally inward. Road contact can supply that component through static friction.

Without sideways skidding, the contact must be able to supply $m\dfrac{v^2}{r}$ towards the centre. If that requirement exceeds the static limit, the car cannot follow the intended bend.
