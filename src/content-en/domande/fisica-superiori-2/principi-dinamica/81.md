At fixed mass and radius, if speed doubles, how does the required force in a bend change?

- It doubles
- It halves
- It quadruples
- It stays the same

## Explanation

The term $v^2$ multiplies mass and is divided by radius. Doubling speed is therefore squared.

If the first trial requires $10\,\mathrm N$, doubling speed requires $40\,\mathrm N$ at the same mass and on the same path.
