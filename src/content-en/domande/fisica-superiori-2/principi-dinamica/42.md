The same $12\,\mathrm{N}$ net force acts on $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ and on $6\,\mathrm{kg}$ in two trials. What is the ratio of the first acceleration to the second?

- $1$
- $2$
- $0.5$
- $4$

## Explanation

At equal force, divide by the mass in each trial. The smaller mass accelerates twice as much.

$$
\frac{a_1}{a_2}=\frac{6}{3}=2.
$$
