A constant net force of $12\,\mathrm{N}$ acts on $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ for $2\,\mathrm{s}$ starting from rest. What speed is acquired in the direction of the force?

- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $24\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The second law gives 4 m/s². Starting at rest, two seconds at that acceleration produce eight metres per second.

$$
v=\frac{12}{3}\cdot2\,\mathrm{m/s}=8\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
