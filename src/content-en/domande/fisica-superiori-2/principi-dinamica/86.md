Two perpendicular forces of equal magnitude act on a particle. What angle does the resultant make with either force?

- $45^\circ$
- $90^\circ$
- $0^\circ$
- $180^\circ$

## Explanation

Vector addition gives a diagonal with equal components. That diagonal bisects the right angle.
