A lift moves downwards and slows with an acceleration of magnitude $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. A scale inside it exerts a normal force of $72\,\mathrm N$ on a sample. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the magnitude of the sample's weight?

- $90\,\mathrm N$
- $72\,\mathrm N$
- $60\,\mathrm N$
- $12\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

While the lift travels downwards and slows, the acceleration points upwards. With that direction positive, $72\,\mathrm N-mg=ma_y$. This gives $m=\dfrac{72}{10+2}\,\mathrm{kg}=6\,\mathrm{kg}$ and hence $P=60\,\mathrm N$. The scale exerts more than the weight because the net force must accelerate the sample upwards.
