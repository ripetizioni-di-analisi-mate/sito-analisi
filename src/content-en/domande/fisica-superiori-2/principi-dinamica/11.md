An object of mass $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ is where the acceleration due to gravity is $9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the magnitude of its weight?

- $14.715\,\mathrm{N}$
- $58.86\,\mathrm{N}$
- $29.43\,\mathrm{N}$
- $7.3575\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Weight is the gravitational force on the object. If it were the only force, the object would accelerate with magnitude $9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Apply the second law to that situation to express weight as mass times gravitational acceleration. The value $29.43\,\mathrm{N}$ is in newtons, while the mass remains $3\,\mathrm{kg}$. The direction is vertically down. Changing location could change weight while mass stays the same.

$$
\begin{aligned}
P &= mg=3\cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{N} = 29.43\,\mathrm{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
