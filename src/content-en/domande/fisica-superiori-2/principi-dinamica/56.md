In an inertial laboratory two objects have zero net force. One is at rest and the other moving. What happens to their states?

- They reach the same velocity
- Both stop
- Each keeps its own initial velocity
- All velocities double

## Explanation

The law of inertia constrains the change of velocity. Different initial values can therefore persist.
