A puck slides on a frictionless horizontal platform. In the inertial ground frame, the horizontal net force on the puck is zero. The platform accelerates to the right at $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the puck's acceleration relative to the platform?

- $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ to the right
- Zero in every frame
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ to the left
- It depends on the puck's mass

## Explanation

The puck has zero horizontal acceleration relative to the ground, so its velocity in that frame is constant. The platform gains velocity to the right. Subtracting the platform's acceleration from the puck's, with right positive, gives $(0-2)\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=-2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The platform frame is accelerating.
