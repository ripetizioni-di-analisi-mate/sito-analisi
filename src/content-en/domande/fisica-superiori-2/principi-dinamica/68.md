On a graph of signed spring force against displacement $x$, what slope describes an ideal spring?

- $-k$
- $+k$
- $x$
- Zero for every spring

## Explanation

The linear relation $F_e=-kx$ has slope $-k$. Zero force corresponds to $x=0$.
