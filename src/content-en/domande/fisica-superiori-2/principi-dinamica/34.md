A block at rest receives opposing pushes of $18$ and $11\,\mathrm{N}$. The static limit is $10\,\mathrm{N}$. What friction is needed to remain still?

- $10\,\mathrm{N}$
- $7\,\mathrm{N}$
- $11\,\mathrm{N}$
- $29\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The pushes give 7 N net. The contact can provide 7 N oppositely because the limit is higher.

$$
f_s=18-11=7\,\mathrm{N}<10\,\mathrm{N}.
$$

Friction points in the direction of the $11\,\mathrm N$ push. If it already had its maximum value of $10\,\mathrm N$, the horizontal net force would no longer be zero.
