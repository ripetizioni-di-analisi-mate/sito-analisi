A smooth ramp has height, base and sloping side in the ratio $3:4:5$. A $5\,\mathrm{kg}$ block is pushed horizontally towards the higher side with a force of $20\,\mathrm N$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the acceleration component along the plane, taking uphill as positive?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 linee="-3,-2.25 3,2.25" vettori="0,0,0,-2.5,P;0,0,1,0,F" punti="0,0,pieno" xmin=-3.5 xmax=3.5 ymin=-3 ymax=2.8 titolo="Project the weight and the push along the ramp."}

- $2.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-2.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Along the ramp, the push contributes $20\cdot\dfrac45\,\mathrm N=16\,\mathrm N$ uphill. Weight contributes $50\cdot\dfrac35\,\mathrm N=30\,\mathrm N$ downhill. The normal force has no parallel component. Thus $a_x=\dfrac{16-30}{5}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=-2.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The negative sign indicates a downhill acceleration.
