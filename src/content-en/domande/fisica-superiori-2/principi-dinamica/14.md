An object slides on a smooth plane inclined at $35^\circ$ above the horizontal. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the acceleration along the plane? Choose the closest value.

- $8.1915\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $5.7358\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-5.7358\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Choose a downhill axis along the plane. The normal force is perpendicular to the plane and contributes nothing along that axis. Weight projects along the axis as $mg\sin\alpha$, because the parallel leg of the component triangle is opposite the slope angle. Divide by $m$ in the second law, leaving $g\sin\alpha$. The result $5.7358\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ is below $g$, since the angle is less than a right angle.

$$
\begin{aligned}
a &= g\sin\alpha=10\sin35^\circ\,\mathrm{m/s^2}\simeq 5.7358\,\mathrm{m/s^2}
\end{aligned}
$$
