A block has a normal force of $60\,\mathrm{N}$ on a surface. During sliding, friction is $18\,\mathrm{N}$. What is its kinetic coefficient?

- $0.3$
- $3.3333$
- $0.6$
- $0.9$

## Explanation

The coefficient is the tangential friction force divided by the normal force. The force units cancel.

$$
\mu_d=\frac{18}{60}=0.3.
$$
