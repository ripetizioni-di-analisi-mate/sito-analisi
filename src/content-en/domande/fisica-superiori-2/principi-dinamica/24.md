An object weighs $60\,\mathrm{N}$ where $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is its mass?

- $10\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $60\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $600\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

To get mass, divide the gravitational force by the acceleration it would produce in free fall.

$$
m=\frac{60}{10}\,\mathrm{kg}=6\,\mathrm{kg}.
$$
