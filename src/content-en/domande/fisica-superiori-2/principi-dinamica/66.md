The same sample moves to a place with a lower $g$. Which quantity stays the same?

- Weight
- Gravitational force exerted by the new body
- Gravitational acceleration
- Mass

## Explanation

Mass measures the sample’s inertia. Weight is mass times local $g$ and therefore changes.

A two-pan balance compares weights in the same field, so the gravitational factor is common. A spring scale measures the weight force directly.
