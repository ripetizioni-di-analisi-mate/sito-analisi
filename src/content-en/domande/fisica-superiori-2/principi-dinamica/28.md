With extension positive, a spring of $k=160\,\mathrm{N/m}$ is compressed by $0.05\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the signed spring force?

- $-8\,\mathrm{N}$
- $16\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-16\,\mathrm{N}$
- $8\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Compression gives a negative coordinate relative to natural length. Restoring force is opposite, hence positive.

$$
F_e=-160(-0.05)\,\mathrm{N}=8\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
