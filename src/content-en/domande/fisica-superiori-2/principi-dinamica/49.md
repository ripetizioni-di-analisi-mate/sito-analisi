A $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ block slows from $10$ to $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ over $3\,\mathrm{s}$ at constant acceleration. What longitudinal net force acts if the initial direction is positive?

- $8\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-8\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-24\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Velocity changes by −6 m/s in three seconds. Divide for acceleration, then multiply by mass.

$$
F_R=4\frac{4-10}{3}\,\mathrm{N}=-8\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
