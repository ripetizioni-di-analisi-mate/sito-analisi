Two objects of $20$ and $40\,\mathrm{kg}$ push each other horizontally on a frictionless surface. What is the ratio of their acceleration magnitudes, first to second?

- $0.5$
- $2$
- $1$
- $4$

## Explanation

The mutual forces have equal magnitudes. Their acceleration ratio is therefore the inverse mass ratio.

$$
\frac{a_1}{a_2}=\frac{\frac{F}{20}}{\frac{F}{40}}=\frac{40}{20}=2.
$$

The common factor $F$ cancels because it is the same contact-force magnitude. The accelerations point in opposite directions, and the ratio compares their magnitudes.
