A vehicle starts at $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and covers $54\,\mathrm{m}$ in the next $6\,\mathrm{s}$ with constant acceleration. What is the acceleration? Choose the closest value.

- $0.17\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0.33\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-0.33\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The initial velocity contributes $8\cdot6=48\,\mathrm m$. The measured displacement exceeds that amount by $6\,\mathrm m$. The position equation therefore gives $\dfrac12a\cdot6^2=6$, so $a=\dfrac13\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. This is approximately $0.33\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The positive sign agrees with the observed displacement, which exceeds the displacement at the initial velocity over the whole interval.
