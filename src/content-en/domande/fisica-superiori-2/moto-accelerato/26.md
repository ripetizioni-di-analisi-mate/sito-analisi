A vehicle starts at $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and covers $54\,\mathrm{m}$ in the next $6\,\mathrm{s}$ with constant acceleration. What is the acceleration? Choose the closest value.

- $0.16667\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0.33333\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-0.33333\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

At the initial velocity alone the vehicle would cover 48 m. The extra six metres are half the acceleration times the square of elapsed time.

$$
a=\frac{2(54-8\cdot6)}{6^2}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=\frac13\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.
$$
