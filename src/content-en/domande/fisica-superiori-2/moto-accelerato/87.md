After doubling $v_0$ at fixed $a$ and time, how does the displacement $v_0t+\frac12at^2$ change?

- It always doubles
- It increases by $\frac12at^2$
- It always quadruples
- It changes by $v_0t$, with $v_0$ the original value

## Explanation

Only the linear contribution of the initial term doubles. The acceleration term stays the same.
