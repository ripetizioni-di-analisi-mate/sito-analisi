After doubling $v_0$ at fixed $a$ and time, how does the displacement $v_0t+\frac12at^2$ change?

- It always doubles
- It increases by $\frac12at^2$
- It always quadruples
- It changes by $v_0t$, with $v_0$ the original value

## Explanation

The original displacement is $v_0t+\dfrac12at^2$. Doubling $v_0$ changes the first term to $2v_0t$. Acceleration and time determine the second term, which remains equal. Subtracting the two displacements gives the change $v_0t$, with the sign of the original velocity.
