A vehicle travels $21\,\mathrm{m}$ starting at $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and accelerating in the same direction at $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is its final speed? Choose the closest value.

- $67\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $109\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10.44\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Acceleration and displacement are known, so use $v_f^2=v_0^2+2a\Delta x$. This gives $v_f^2=5^2+2\cdot2\cdot21=109$ in SI units. The speed is the positive square root, $\sqrt{109}\,\mathrm{m/s}$, or about $10.44\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The velocity is also positive because the vehicle accelerates in its initial direction.
