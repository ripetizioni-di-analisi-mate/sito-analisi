A vehicle travels $21\,\mathrm{m}$ starting at $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and accelerating in the same direction at $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is its final speed? Choose the closest value.

- $67\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $109\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10.44\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Find the square of the final velocity by adding 84 to the initial square. Speed is the positive square root of the result.

$$
u_f=\sqrt{5^2+2\cdot2\cdot21}\,\mathrm{m/s}=\sqrt{109}\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
