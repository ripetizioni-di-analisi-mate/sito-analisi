An object is thrown upward at $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and neglect air resistance. How far above the launch point does it rise?

- $90\,\mathrm{m}$
- $22.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-45\,\mathrm{m}$
- $45\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

While rising, velocity is positive and acceleration points down. Height is greatest when velocity first reaches zero, after $3\,\mathrm{s}$. Set final velocity to zero in the relation without time to get the height gain $45\,\mathrm{m}$. The height gain is positive because the apex is above the launch point, while the acceleration is negative.

$$
\begin{aligned}
t_M &= \frac{30}{10}\,\mathrm{s}=3\,\mathrm{s} \\[6pt]
\Delta h &= \frac{30^2}{2\cdot 10}\,\mathrm{m}=45\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
