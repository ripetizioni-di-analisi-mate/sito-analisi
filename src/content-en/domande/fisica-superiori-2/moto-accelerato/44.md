A stone is dropped from rest $45\,\mathrm{m}$ above ground. Use $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and ignore air resistance. What is the impact speed?

- $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $45\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $90\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Choosing down as positive makes both acceleration and displacement positive. The relation without time gives the square of the speed.

$$
u=\sqrt{2\cdot10\cdot45}\,\mathrm{m/s}=30\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
