A stone is dropped from rest $45\,\mathrm{m}$ above ground. Use $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and ignore air resistance. What is the impact speed?

- $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $45\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $90\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Choose downward as positive. The displacement is $45\,\mathrm m$ and the acceleration is $10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. With zero initial velocity, $v_f^2=2\cdot10\cdot45=900$ in SI units. The positive square root gives the speed $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Taking the axis upwards would change the sign of the velocity. The position equation provides a check. The fall takes three seconds, during which the object gains thirty metres per second.
