A ball rises from the ground at $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Neglecting air and with $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, how long does it remain in the air before returning to the ground?

- $2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Height above the ground is $20t-5t^2$ in metres. At ground level it is zero, and the equation factors as $5t(4-t)=0$. The root $t=0$ is the launch. The root $t=4\,\mathrm s$ is the return and therefore the time spent in the air.
