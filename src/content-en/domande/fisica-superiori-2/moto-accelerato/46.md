A ball rises from the ground at $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Neglecting air and with $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, how long does it remain in the air before returning to the ground?

- $2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Set height equal to zero. One solution is the departure. The other zero is the return time.

$$
y=20t-5t^2=5t(4-t)=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad t=4\,\mathrm{s}.
$$
