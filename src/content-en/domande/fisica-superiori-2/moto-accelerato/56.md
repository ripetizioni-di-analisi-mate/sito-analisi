With negative velocity and constant negative acceleration, how does speed change?

- It decreases
- It increases
- It remains constant
- It immediately becomes zero

## Explanation

Negative acceleration subtracts a positive amount from a velocity that is already negative. The velocity becomes more negative and moves further from zero. For example, changing from $-3$ to $-5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ increases the speed from $3$ to $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$, while the motion stays in the negative direction.
