With constant acceleration, when is the mean of the endpoint velocities zero?

- When acceleration is negative
- When initial position is zero
- When the endpoint velocities cancel in sign and magnitude
- When the object keeps moving in the same direction at positive velocity

## Explanation

The mean of the endpoint velocities is $\dfrac{v_i+v_f}{2}$. It is zero when the sum is zero, so $v_f=-v_i$. Changing from $+5$ to $-5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is one example. Over that interval, with constant acceleration, the net displacement is zero even though the object has moved.
