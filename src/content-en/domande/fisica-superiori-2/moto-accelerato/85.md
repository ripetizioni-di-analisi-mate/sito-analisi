A trolley leaves a fixed starting position at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and maintains an acceleration of $-2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ even after it reverses. How much time passes before it returns to its starting position?

- $0\,\mathrm s$
- $2\,\mathrm s$
- $4\,\mathrm s$
- $8\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

Returning to the starting position requires zero displacement, so set $4t-t^2=0$. Factoring gives $t(4-t)=0$. The root $0$ is the departure and the root $4\,\mathrm s$ is the return. At $2\,\mathrm s$ only the velocity is zero and the trolley is furthest from its starting position.
