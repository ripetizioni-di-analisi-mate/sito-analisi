Motion follows $v=v_0+at$ for $t\geq0$ and acceleration has the same sign as $v_0$. Can the object reverse?

- Yes, at $t=\frac{v_0}{a}$
- Always after a second
- No, velocity keeps its initial sign
- Yes, when position is zero

## Explanation

The velocity change has the same sign as the initial velocity at every positive time. Their sum cannot vanish in the interval.
