On a piecewise velocity–time graph, which area gives the distance travelled when the graph crosses zero?

- The signed sum of the areas
- Only the area above the axis
- The sum of the magnitudes above and below the axis
- Only the area below the axis

## Explanation

Each area relative to the time axis gives a signed displacement. For example, a positive area of $7\,\mathrm m$ and a negative area of $-4\,\mathrm m$ give a net displacement of $3\,\mathrm m$. Distance counts both legs travelled and equals $7+4=11\,\mathrm m$.
