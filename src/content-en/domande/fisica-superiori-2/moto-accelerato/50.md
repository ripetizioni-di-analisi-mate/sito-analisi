A vertical launch reaches its apex after $1.5\,\mathrm{s}$. Using $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and neglecting air resistance, what was its launch speed?

- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

While rising, each second subtracts 10 m/s from velocity. Reaching zero at 1.5 s implies an initial value of 15 m/s.

$$
v_0=g\,t_M=10\cdot1.5\,\mathrm{m/s}=15\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
