A vertical launch reaches its apex after $1.5\,\mathrm{s}$. Using $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and neglecting air resistance, what was its launch speed?

- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

At the highest point, vertical velocity is zero. Taking upward as positive, the initial velocity must balance the decrease of $gt$ accumulated in $1.5\,\mathrm s$. This gives $v_0=10\cdot1.5=15\,\mathrm{m/s}$. It is positive and also equals the initial speed.
