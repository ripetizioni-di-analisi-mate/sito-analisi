An object moves with $v_0=2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $a=2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is its displacement from $t=1\,\mathrm{s}$ to $t=3\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Subtract the positions at the two endpoints. By the first endpoint velocity has already changed since $t=0$.

$$
\Delta x=(2\cdot3+3^2)-(2\cdot1+1^2)=12\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
