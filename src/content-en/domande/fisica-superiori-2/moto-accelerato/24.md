An object moves with $v_0=2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $a=2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is its displacement from $t=1\,\mathrm{s}$ to $t=3\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Between $1$ and $3\,\mathrm s$, the velocity goes from $2+2\cdot1=4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $2+2\cdot3=8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. With constant acceleration, the average is $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$, so the displacement over $2\,\mathrm s$ is $12\,\mathrm m$. The interval before $1\,\mathrm s$ lies outside the calculation.
