A tracked object starts at $x_0=-5\,\mathrm{m}$ with $v_0=3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $a=2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is its position at $t=4\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $28\,\mathrm{m}$
- $11\,\mathrm{m}$
- $23\,\mathrm{m}$
- $39\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Add the initial-velocity and acceleration contributions to the known coordinate. Over four seconds they are 12 m and 16 m.

$$
x=-5+3\cdot4+\frac{2\cdot4^2}{2}\,\mathrm{m}=23\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
