A tracked object starts at $x_0=-5\,\mathrm{m}$ with $v_0=3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $a=2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is its position at $t=4\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $28\,\mathrm{m}$
- $11\,\mathrm{m}$
- $23\,\mathrm{m}$
- $39\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The position equation adds the initial position, the contribution from the initial velocity and the contribution from acceleration. At $4\,\mathrm s$ these terms are $-5$, $3\cdot4=12$ and $\dfrac12\cdot2\cdot4^2=16$, in metres. The position is $23\,\mathrm m$. The displacement from the starting point is $28\,\mathrm m$.
