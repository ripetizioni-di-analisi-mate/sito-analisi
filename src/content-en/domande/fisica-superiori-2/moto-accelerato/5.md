A vehicle travels at $28\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and brakes with a constant acceleration of magnitude $6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the distance travelled from braking to rest. Choose the closest value.

- $65.333\,\mathrm{m}$
- $130.67\,\mathrm{m}$
- $32.667\,\mathrm{m}$
- $34\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Choose the direction of travel as positive. Velocity decreases by $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ each second and reaches zero after $4.6667\,\mathrm{s}$. The graph during braking forms a triangle of height $28\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and base $4.6667\,\mathrm{s}$. Its area is $65.333\,\mathrm{m}$. Isolating displacement in the relation without time, with final velocity zero, gives the same result.

$$
\begin{aligned}
t_a &= \frac{v_0}{b}=\frac{28}{6}\,\mathrm{s}\simeq 4.6667\,\mathrm{s} \\[6pt]
s_f &= \frac{v_0^2}{2b}=\frac{28^2}{2\cdot 6}\,\mathrm{m}\simeq 65.333\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
