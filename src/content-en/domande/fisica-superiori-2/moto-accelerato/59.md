What does the unit $\mathrm{m/s^2}$ mean?

- Metres travelled each second
- Metres per second of velocity change per second
- Seconds needed to travel one metre
- Square metres travelled in one second

## Explanation

Acceleration divides a change in velocity by a time interval. Thus m/s is divided by s.
