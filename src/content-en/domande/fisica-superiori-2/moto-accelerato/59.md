What does the unit $\mathrm{m/s^2}$ mean?

- Metres travelled each second
- Metres per second of velocity change per second
- Seconds needed to travel one metre
- Square metres travelled in one second

## Explanation

Acceleration is a velocity change, in metres per second, divided by an interval in seconds. At constant $a=2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, velocity increases by $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ every second. The unit describes how quickly velocity changes along the axis.
