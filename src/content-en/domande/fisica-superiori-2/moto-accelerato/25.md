Starting from rest with constant acceleration $8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, how much time is needed to cover $36\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $3\,\mathrm{s}$
- $9\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

From rest distance is proportional to time squared. Divide by half the acceleration and take the positive square root.

$$
36=4t^2\quad\Longrightarrow\quad t=3\,\mathrm{s}.
$$
