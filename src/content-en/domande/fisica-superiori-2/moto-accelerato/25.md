Starting from rest with constant acceleration $8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, how much time is needed to cover $36\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $3\,\mathrm{s}$
- $9\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The initial velocity is zero, so the displacement in metres is $\dfrac12\cdot8t^2=4t^2$. Setting $4t^2=36$ gives $t^2=9$. The time elapsed since departure is positive, so $t=3\,\mathrm s$. Substitution gives back $4\cdot3^2=36\,\mathrm m$. The other root gives an instant before departure on the mathematical extension of the parabola. The specified motion begins at time zero.
