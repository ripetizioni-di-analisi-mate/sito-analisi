A cart with constant acceleration $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ has displacement $40\,\mathrm{m}$ over $5\,\mathrm{s}$. What was its initial velocity?

- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Acceleration contributes 25 m. The remaining 15 m come from the initial velocity over five seconds.

$$
v_0=\frac{40-\frac12\cdot2\cdot5^2}{5}\,\mathrm{m/s}=3\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
