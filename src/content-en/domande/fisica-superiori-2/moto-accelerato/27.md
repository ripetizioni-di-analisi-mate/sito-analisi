A cart with constant acceleration $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ has displacement $40\,\mathrm{m}$ over $5\,\mathrm{s}$. What was its initial velocity?

- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Over $5\,\mathrm s$, acceleration alone contributes $\dfrac12\cdot2\cdot5^2=25\,\mathrm m$. Of the given $40\,\mathrm m$, the remaining $15\,\mathrm m$ are the $v_0t$ term. Dividing those $15\,\mathrm m$ by the duration of $5\,\mathrm s$ gives $v_0=3\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
