A driver takes $0.6\,\mathrm{s}$ to react while the car keeps $25\,\mathrm{m/s}$. How far does it travel before braking?

- $25\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $41.667\,\mathrm{m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The reaction phase has constant speed. The required distance is the rectangle area without the later braking triangle.

$$
s_r=25\cdot0.6\,\mathrm{m}=15\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
