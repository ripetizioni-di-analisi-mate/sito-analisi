A driver takes $0.6\,\mathrm{s}$ to react while the car keeps $25\,\mathrm{m/s}$. How far does it travel before braking?

- $25\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $41.667\,\mathrm{m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

During the reaction interval, the model assigns a constant velocity of $25\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The distance is therefore $25\cdot0.6=15\,\mathrm m$. It is a rectangle on the velocity-time graph, of height $25\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and width $0.6\,\mathrm s$. Braking begins at the end of the rectangle.
