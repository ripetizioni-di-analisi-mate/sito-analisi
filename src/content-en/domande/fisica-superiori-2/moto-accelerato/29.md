A cart goes from $+8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at constant acceleration over $6\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the net displacement?

- $12\,\mathrm{m}$
- $24\,\mathrm{m}$
- $36\,\mathrm{m}$
- $48\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Under constant acceleration, the endpoint velocities give the average $\dfrac{8-4}{2}=2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The net displacement is therefore $2\cdot6=12\,\mathrm m$. The motion after reversal makes a negative contribution because the trolley moves back along the same axis.
