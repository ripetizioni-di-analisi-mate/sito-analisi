A cart goes from $+8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at constant acceleration over $6\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the net displacement?

- $12\,\mathrm{m}$
- $24\,\mathrm{m}$
- $36\,\mathrm{m}$
- $48\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The positive area before reversal and the negative area after it both contribute to displacement. With constant acceleration the mean is 2 m/s.

$$
\Delta x=\frac{8+(-4)}{2}\cdot6=12\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
