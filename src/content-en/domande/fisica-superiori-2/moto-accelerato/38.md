A test track allows $40\,\mathrm{m}$ to stop from $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$, with no reaction time. What constant acceleration magnitude stops the car exactly at the end?

- $10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Use the equation without time at final velocity zero. The magnitude is initial speed squared divided by twice the distance.

$$
b=\frac{20^2}{2\cdot40}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.
$$
