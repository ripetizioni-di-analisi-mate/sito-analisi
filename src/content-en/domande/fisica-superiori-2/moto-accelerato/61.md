A rising and a falling line intersect on a velocity–time graph. What is certainly equal at the intersection?

- The accelerations
- The velocities at that instant
- The positions
- The distances travelled from the origin

## Explanation

A point on the graph has time as its horizontal coordinate and velocity as its vertical coordinate. At the intersection the velocities are therefore equal at the same instant. The slopes have opposite signs and give different accelerations. The ordinates alone do not tell whether the positions coincide.
