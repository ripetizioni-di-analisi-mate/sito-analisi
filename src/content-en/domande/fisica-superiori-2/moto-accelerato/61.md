A rising and a falling line intersect on a velocity–time graph. What is certainly equal at the intersection?

- The accelerations
- The velocities at that instant
- The positions
- The distances travelled from the origin

## Explanation

The shared ordinate gives the velocity of both objects. Positions would require initial conditions and displacements.
