With constant non-zero $a$, what form does the position graph $x(t)$ have?

- An always increasing straight line
- A circle
- A parabola
- A horizontal line

## Explanation

The position equation contains the term $\dfrac12at^2$. With constant nonzero $a$, the quadratic coefficient remains nonzero and the graph is a parabola. Velocity is the slope of this curve. The slope changes at a steady rate because acceleration is constant.
