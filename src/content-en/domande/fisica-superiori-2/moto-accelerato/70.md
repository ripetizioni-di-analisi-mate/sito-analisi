Which condition guarantees that the arithmetic mean of $v_i,v_f$ gives the average velocity over the interval?

- When only the two readings are known, for any motion
- When $v(t)$ varies linearly over the interval
- When velocity is positive, regardless of its equation
- When distance exceeds the magnitude of displacement

## Explanation

When velocity varies linearly, displacement is the area of a trapezoid with signed bases. Dividing that area by the elapsed time gives the arithmetic mean of the endpoint velocities. With a curved or segmented profile, the two endpoint readings can stay the same while the area between them changes.
