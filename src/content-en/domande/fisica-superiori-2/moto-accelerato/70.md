When can the arithmetic mean of $v_i,v_f$ be used for average velocity over the interval?

- When only the two readings are known, for any motion
- When $v(t)$ varies linearly over the interval
- When velocity is positive, regardless of its equation
- When distance exceeds the magnitude of displacement

## Explanation

For a straight line, the mean of the two bases gives the trapezoid’s average height. A curve with the same endpoints can enclose a different area.
