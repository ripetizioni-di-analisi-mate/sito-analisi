A vehicle goes from $22$ to $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in $3\,\mathrm{s}$ of uniform braking, then keeps braking in the same way. How much longer does it take to stop?

- $3\,\mathrm{s}$
- $5.5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Velocity drops by 12 m/s in three seconds, or 4 m/s each second. The next phase starts at the remaining 10 m/s.

$$
t_{\mathrm{res}}=\frac{10}{4}\,\mathrm{s}=2.5\,\mathrm{s}.
$$
