The $v(t)$ graph for stopping has a positive horizontal leg followed by a slanting leg down to zero. Which phases do they represent?

- Braking, then reaction
- Reaction, then braking
- Two braking phases of equal intensity
- Reaction, then increasing speed

## Explanation

During the reaction, the driver has yet to begin braking and the model keeps the initial velocity. This is the horizontal segment. When braking begins, the slope becomes negative and the velocity decreases. The point where the line reaches the time axis represents the stop.
