An object has initial velocity $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and constant acceleration $5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the signed velocity after $6\,\mathrm{s}$.

- $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $37\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-23\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The acceleration gives a change of $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ each second. Over $6\,\mathrm{s}$ the total change is $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Adding it to the initial velocity gives $37\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The same velocity is the ordinate at the end of the velocity–time line.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta v &= (5)\cdot 6 = 30\,\mathrm{m/s} \\[6pt]
v_f &= 7+(30)=37\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
