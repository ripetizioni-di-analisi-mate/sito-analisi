A cart changes from $3$ to $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$ during a positive displacement of $18\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the constant acceleration?

- $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0.33333\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The change in the squared velocities is 72. Twice the displacement is 36 m. Their ratio has units of acceleration.

$$
a=\frac{9^2-3^2}{2\cdot18}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.
$$
