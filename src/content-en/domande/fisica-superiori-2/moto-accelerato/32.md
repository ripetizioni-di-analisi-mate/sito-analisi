A cart changes from $3$ to $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$ during a positive displacement of $18\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the constant acceleration?

- $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0.33333\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The relation without time gives $2a\Delta x=v_f^2-v_0^2$. The right-hand side is $9^2-3^2=72$ in SI units. Dividing by $2\cdot18=36$ gives $a=2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The acceleration is positive, in agreement with the increase in velocity along the displacement.
