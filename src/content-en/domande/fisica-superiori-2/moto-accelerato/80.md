Two objects of different mass are dropped from the same height at the same instant, with negligible air resistance. Which lands first?

- They arrive together
- The heavier one
- The lighter one
- Density must be known

## Explanation

With negligible air resistance both objects have the same acceleration $g$. They are released from the same height and both start with zero velocity. The fall takes $\sqrt{\dfrac{2h}{g}}$ for each object, so they hit the ground together. Mass does not enter the conditions of this model.
