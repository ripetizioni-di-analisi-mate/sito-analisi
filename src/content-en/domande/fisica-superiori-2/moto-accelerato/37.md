A car travelling at $18\,\mathrm{m/s}$ stops $6\,\mathrm{s}$ after uniform braking begins. What distance is covered while braking?

- $54\,\mathrm{m}$
- $108\,\mathrm{m}$
- $36\,\mathrm{m}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The area under the decreasing line is a triangle. The mean velocity while braking is half the initial speed.

$$
s_f=\frac{18\cdot6}{2}\,\mathrm{m}=54\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
