A car travelling at $18\,\mathrm{m/s}$ stops $6\,\mathrm{s}$ after uniform braking begins. What distance is covered while braking?

- $54\,\mathrm{m}$
- $108\,\mathrm{m}$
- $36\,\mathrm{m}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

During braking the velocity decreases linearly from $18$ to $0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The average velocity is therefore $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Multiplying by the $6\,\mathrm s$ of braking gives $54\,\mathrm m$. The product is the area of the triangle beneath the velocity-time graph. Using the initial velocity for all six seconds would give twice that distance, the area of the rectangle enclosing the triangle.
