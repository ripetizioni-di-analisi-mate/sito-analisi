What does the $at$ term in $v=v_0+at$ represent?

- The total displacement
- The initial velocity
- The signed change in velocity
- The area on the velocity–time graph

## Explanation

Acceleration gives velocity change per second. Multiplying it by elapsed time gives the total change.
