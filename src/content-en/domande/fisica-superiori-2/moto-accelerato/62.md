What does the $at$ term in $v=v_0+at$ represent?

- The total displacement
- The initial velocity
- The signed change in velocity
- The area on the velocity–time graph

## Explanation

Subtracting $v_0$ from both sides gives $v-v_0=at$. The product is the velocity change accumulated over elapsed time, with the sign of $a$. If $a=-2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and $t=3\,\mathrm s$, for example, the final velocity is $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ below the initial value.
