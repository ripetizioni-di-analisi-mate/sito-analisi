A trolley travels at $24\,\mathrm{m/s}$ when it starts braking at $a=-6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. After $2\,\mathrm s$ it continues braking at $a=-3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ until it stops. How far does it travel from the beginning of braking?

- $60\,\mathrm m$
- $48\,\mathrm m$
- $96\,\mathrm m$
- $36\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

After $2\,\mathrm s$ the velocity has dropped to $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The first stage has an average velocity of $18\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and covers $36\,\mathrm m$. The second stage takes $\dfrac{12}{3}=4\,\mathrm s$ at an average velocity of $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$, so it covers $24\,\mathrm m$. The two distances add to $60\,\mathrm m$.
