Why does a negative value of $v_f^2$ show the data do not describe the requested point?

- Signed velocity is always positive
- Speed squared measures time
- The square of a real velocity is non-negative
- Negative acceleration cannot exist

## Explanation

The square of any real velocity is greater than or equal to zero. If the data give $v_f^2<0$, the point cannot be reached under the stated assumptions, or one measurement is inconsistent with the others. A point with $v_f^2=0$ may be a stop or a reversal.
