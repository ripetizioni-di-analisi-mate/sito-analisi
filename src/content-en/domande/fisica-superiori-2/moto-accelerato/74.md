If a vertical launch reaches the same height going up and down, how do the two speeds compare in the air-free model?

- The upward speed is double
- The downward speed is double
- Both are always zero
- They are equal

## Explanation

In the relation $v^2=v_0^2-2g(y-y_0)$, the same height $y$ gives the same value of $v^2$. The two speeds are equal, while their signs are opposite because one crossing is on the way up and the other on the way down. The comparison assumes negligible air resistance.
