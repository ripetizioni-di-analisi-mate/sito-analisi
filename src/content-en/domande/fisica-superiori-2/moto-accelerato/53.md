An object travels $30\,\mathrm{m}$ in $5\,\mathrm{s}$ under constant acceleration without reversal. The initial velocity is $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the final velocity?

- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The average velocity is 6 m/s. The arithmetic mean of the two endpoint velocities must give this value.

$$
v_f=2\frac{30}{5}-2=10\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
