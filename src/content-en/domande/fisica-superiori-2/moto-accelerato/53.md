Two sensors measure the motion of the same trolley. At $t=0$ the first records a position of $4\,\mathrm m$. The second measures a velocity of $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at $t=0$ and $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at $t=2\,\mathrm s$. Which position at $t=2\,\mathrm s$ is consistent with constant acceleration?

- $4\,\mathrm m$
- $16\,\mathrm m$
- $0\,\mathrm m$
- $8\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

With constant acceleration the velocity readings give an average of $\dfrac{-2+6}{2}=2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The signed displacement is therefore $2\cdot2=4\,\mathrm m$. Adding it to the initial position gives the required position, $4+4=8\,\mathrm m$. The average already accounts for the short interval in the negative direction.
