Over $3\,\mathrm{s}$ an object goes from $+6$ to $-6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the signed average acceleration?

- $0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The initial and final speeds match, whereas the velocities are opposite. The change in velocity is −12 m/s.

$$
a_m=\frac{-6-6}{3}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=-4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.
$$
