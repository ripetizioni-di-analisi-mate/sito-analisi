Over $3\,\mathrm{s}$ an object goes from $+6$ to $-6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the signed average acceleration?

- $0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The initial velocity is $+6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the final velocity is $-6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The change is therefore $-6-(+6)=-12\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Dividing by $3\,\mathrm s$ gives $-4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The speeds are equal at the two instants, while the velocities point in opposite directions.
