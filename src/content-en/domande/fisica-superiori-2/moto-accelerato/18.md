A trolley moves with constant acceleration along a line. It reverses direction at $t=4\,\mathrm s$. Which observation must hold at $t=3\,\mathrm s$ and $t=5\,\mathrm s$?

- It has the same velocity and different positions.
- It occupies the same position and has opposite velocities.
- Its accelerations are opposite.
- It is at rest at both times.

## Explanation

Measured from the reversal, the velocity is $v=a(t-4)$ with time in seconds. The two times give opposite velocities. The signed areas under the velocity–time graph from $3$ to $4$ and from $4$ to $5$ cancel, so the net displacement is zero.
