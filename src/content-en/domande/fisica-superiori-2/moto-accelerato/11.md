A cart has initial velocity $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and constant acceleration $6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is its signed displacement in $7\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $49\,\mathrm{m}$
- $343\,\mathrm{m}$
- $196\,\mathrm{m}$
- $147\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The initial-velocity term contributes $49\,\mathrm{m}$. Acceleration changes the velocity linearly, giving the signed area of a triangle of base $7\,\mathrm{s}$. This second contribution is $147\,\mathrm{m}$. Add them for the displacement. As a check, multiply the mean of the two endpoint velocities, $28\,\mathrm{m/s}$, by the duration.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta x &= v_0t+\frac12at^2 \\[6pt]
&= (7)\cdot 7+\frac{(6)\cdot 7^2}{2}\,\mathrm{m} \\[6pt]
&= 196\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
