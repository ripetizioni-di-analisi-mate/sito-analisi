For the first $2\,\mathrm{s}$, a trolley maintains a constant velocity of $-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Over the next $5\,\mathrm{s}$ its velocity changes from $-4$ to $14\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the signed average acceleration over the two stages together?

- $\dfrac{18}{7}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $\dfrac{18}{5}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $\dfrac{10}{7}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-\dfrac{18}{7}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The total change is $14-(-4)=18\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The two stages together last $2+5=7\,\mathrm{s}$, so the average acceleration is $\dfrac{18}{7}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The initial stage contributes to the duration even though the velocity stays constant. Dividing by $5\,\mathrm{s}$ would give the average for the second stage alone.
