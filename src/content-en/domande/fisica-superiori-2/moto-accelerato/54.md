Which plot directly indicates constant non-zero acceleration?

- A horizontal line on a velocity–time plot
- A slanting line on a velocity–time plot
- A slanting line on a position–time plot
- A horizontal line on a position–time plot

## Explanation

On a velocity-time graph, acceleration is the slope of the line. A sloping straight line has the same slope at every point, and that slope is nonzero. A horizontal line on the same graph would describe constant velocity. On a position-time graph the slope of a straight line gives velocity.
