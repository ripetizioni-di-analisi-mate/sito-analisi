A sensor measures average acceleration $-3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ for $5\,\mathrm{s}$ and a final velocity of $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What was the initial velocity?

- $-13\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $17\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The velocity change is $-3\cdot5=-15\,\mathrm{m/s}$ because the duration is positive and the average acceleration is negative. Since $v_f=v_i-15$ in SI units, the initial reading is $v_i=2+15=17\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Subtracting $15$ from the initial reading recovers the final velocity.
