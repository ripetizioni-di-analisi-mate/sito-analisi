A sensor measures average acceleration $-3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ for $5\,\mathrm{s}$ and a final velocity of $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What was the initial velocity?

- $-13\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $17\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The change is −15 m/s. Work backwards by subtracting it from the final velocity.

$$
v_i=v_f-a_m\Delta t=2-(-3)\cdot5=17\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
