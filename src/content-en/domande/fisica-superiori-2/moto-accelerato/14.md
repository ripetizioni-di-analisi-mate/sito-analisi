In a vehicle stopping model, its speed remains $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$ during the reaction. It then brakes with a constant deceleration magnitude of $5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the maximum reaction time if the vehicle must stop within $55\,\mathrm m$ from the moment the signal is seen?

- $2.75\,\mathrm s$
- $0.75\,\mathrm s$
- $3\,\mathrm s$
- $2\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

Braking alone requires $\dfrac{20^2}{2\cdot5}=40\,\mathrm m$. At most $55-40=15\,\mathrm m$ remain for the constant-speed stage before braking. The corresponding duration is $\dfrac{15}{20}=0.75\,\mathrm s$. Each additional second of reaction would increase the total distance by $20\,\mathrm m$.
