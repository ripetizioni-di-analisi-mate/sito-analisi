On a downhill section velocity changes from $-12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ between $t=2\,\mathrm{s}$ and $t=6\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the mean acceleration?

- $-2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $1.3333\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The change is positive even though both velocities are negative. The duration is four seconds.

$$
a_m=\frac{-4-(-12)}{6-2}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.
$$
