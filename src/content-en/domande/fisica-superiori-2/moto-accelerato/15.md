On a downhill section velocity changes from $-12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ between $t=2\,\mathrm{s}$ and $t=6\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the mean acceleration?

- $-2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $1.33\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The readings are $6-2=4\,\mathrm s$ apart. The velocity increases by $-4-(-12)=8\,\mathrm{m/s}$, so the ratio is $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The positive sign describes the increase in signed velocity. At both readings the object is travelling in the negative direction. The endpoint readings suffice for this average even if acceleration took different values between the two instants.
