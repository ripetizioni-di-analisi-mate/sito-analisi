A stone is thrown vertically upwards at $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is its velocity one second after the apex, before reaching the ground, taking up as positive and ignoring air?

- $-10\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

At the highest point the velocity is zero. With upward taken as positive, the acceleration remains $-10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. In one second it produces a change of $-10\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The requested velocity therefore has magnitude $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and a negative sign because the stone is falling then.
