A cart leaves the origin with $v_0=-12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and acceleration $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. When is it momentarily at rest?

- $-4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $9\,\mathrm{s}$
- $36\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

In SI units the equation is $v=-12+3t$. At the instant when the trolley is at rest, $v=0$. Setting $3t=12$ gives $t=4\,\mathrm s$. If the same acceleration continues, the velocity becomes positive just after that instant.
