A cart leaves the origin with $v_0=-12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and acceleration $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. When is it momentarily at rest?

- $-4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $9\,\mathrm{s}$
- $36\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Rest means zero velocity. Positive acceleration cancels the negative initial velocity in four seconds.

$$
-12+3t=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad t=4\,\mathrm{s}.
$$
