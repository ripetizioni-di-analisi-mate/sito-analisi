An object starts and ends at the same non-zero speed but reverses its direction along the axis. What is true of its velocity change?

- The change has magnitude twice the speed
- The change is zero
- The change equals the initial speed
- It depends only on initial position

## Explanation

The endpoint velocities have equal magnitudes and opposite signs. Subtracting them doubles the magnitude.
