If the axis orientation is reversed, what happens to the non-zero components $v$ and $a$?

- Only $a$ reverses sign
- Only $v$ reverses sign
- Both double
- Both reverse sign with magnitudes unchanged

## Explanation

Reversing the axis replaces each velocity component with its opposite. The difference between two velocities also changes sign and, since the elapsed time is the same, so does acceleration. The object's speed and the magnitude of its acceleration are independent of this choice.
