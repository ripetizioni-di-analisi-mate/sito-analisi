If the axis orientation is reversed, what happens to the non-zero components $v$ and $a$?

- Only $a$ reverses sign
- Only $v$ reverses sign
- Both double
- Both reverse sign with magnitudes unchanged

## Explanation

Velocity reverses sign because it is measured in the new direction. Its change reverses sign too, so acceleration does as well.
