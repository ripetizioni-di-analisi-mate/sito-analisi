A cart has initial velocity $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and constant acceleration $5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is its signed displacement in $6\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $36\,\mathrm{m}$
- $216\,\mathrm{m}$
- $126\,\mathrm{m}$
- $90\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The initial-velocity term contributes $36\,\mathrm{m}$. Acceleration changes the velocity linearly, giving the signed area of a triangle of base $6\,\mathrm{s}$. This second contribution is $90\,\mathrm{m}$. Add them for the displacement. As a check, multiply the mean of the two endpoint velocities, $21\,\mathrm{m/s}$, by the duration.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta x &= v_0t+\frac12at^2 \\[6pt]
&= (6)\cdot 6+\frac{(5)\cdot 6^2}{2}\,\mathrm{m} \\[6pt]
&= 126\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
