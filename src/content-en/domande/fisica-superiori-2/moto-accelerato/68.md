From rest, at fixed positive constant acceleration, how does displacement change when time triples?

- It triples
- It halves
- It is multiplied by nine
- It stays the same

## Explanation

From rest, the displacement equation contains only $\dfrac12at^2$. At the same acceleration, substituting $3t$ for $t$ multiplies the term by $3^2=9$. The velocity reached grows only by a factor of three. The elapsed time also triples, giving a factor of nine in their product.
