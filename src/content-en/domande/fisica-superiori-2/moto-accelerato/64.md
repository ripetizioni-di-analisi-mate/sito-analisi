A line $v(t)$ crosses the axis from positive to negative values. If motion continues as shown, what happens to the object?

- It stops forever
- It necessarily passes through the origin
- It reverses direction
- It starts moving faster in the positive direction

## Explanation

Before the line crosses the time axis, the velocity is positive. At the crossing it is zero, then it becomes negative. The sign change shows that the object reverses direction along the same axis. At that instant the position-time graph has zero slope.
