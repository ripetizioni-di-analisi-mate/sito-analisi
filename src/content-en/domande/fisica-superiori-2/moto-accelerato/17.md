A trolley moves along a straight track. Its velocity changes from $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ with an average acceleration of $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. How long is the time interval?

- $3\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The change in velocity is $\Delta v=10-(-2)=12\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Average acceleration is the change in velocity divided by the duration, so the duration is $\Delta t=\dfrac{\Delta v}{a_m}$.

$$
\Delta t=\frac{12}{4}\,\mathrm s=3\,\mathrm s.
$$

If the minus sign of the initial velocity were ignored, the change would be $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the duration $2\,\mathrm s$.
