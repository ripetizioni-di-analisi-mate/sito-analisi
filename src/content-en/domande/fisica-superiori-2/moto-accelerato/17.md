A detector reads $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at the start and $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at the end. The mean acceleration is $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. How many seconds separate the readings?

- $3\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The velocity changes by $10-(-2)=12\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Average acceleration is the change divided by the duration, so $\Delta t=\dfrac{12}{4}=3\,\mathrm s$. The units of velocity divided by the units of acceleration simplify to a unit of time. To check, start from the first reading, add the total increase and verify that the result agrees with the second reading.
