A detector reads $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at the start and $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at the end. The mean acceleration is $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. How many seconds separate the readings?

- $3\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Subtract the negative initial velocity in the numerator. A change of twelve metres per second at that mean rate requires three seconds.

$$
\Delta t=\frac{10-(-2)}{4}\,\mathrm{s}=3\,\mathrm{s}.
$$
