A ball is thrown up at $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ from an initial height of $6\,\mathrm{m}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and negligible air, what is the maximum height above ground?

- $7.2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $13.2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $14.4\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

At the apex velocity is zero. The gain above the launch point is 7.2 m and must be added to the initial height.

$$
y_M=6+\frac{12^2}{2\cdot10}\,\mathrm{m}=13.2\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
