A ball is thrown up at $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ from an initial height of $6\,\mathrm{m}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and negligible air, what is the maximum height above ground?

- $7.2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $13.2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $20.4\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

At the highest point the velocity is zero. With constant $g$, the height gained is $\dfrac{12^2}{2\cdot10}=7.2\,\mathrm m$. The object already starts $6\,\mathrm m$ above the ground, so the highest height is $6+7.2=13.2\,\mathrm m$. The gain alone is measured from the launch point. The time to the top gives another check. The ball reaches its highest point after one and two tenths of a second, and the position equation gives the same height.
