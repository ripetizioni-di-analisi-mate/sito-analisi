An object is thrown straight down at $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ from a platform $30\,\mathrm{m}$ above ground. Ignore air resistance and use $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. When does it hit the ground?

- $2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2.4495\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

With down positive, distance is $5t+5t^2$. The other root is negative and precedes the throw.

$$
5t+5t^2=30\quad\Longrightarrow\quad (t-2)(t+3)=0,\quad t=2\,\mathrm{s}.
$$
