An object is thrown straight down at $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ from a platform $30\,\mathrm{m}$ above ground. Ignore air resistance and use $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. When does it hit the ground?

- $2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2.45\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Taking downward as positive, the displacement of $30\,\mathrm m$ satisfies $5t+5t^2=30$. Dividing by $5$ and factoring gives $(t-2)(t+3)=0$. The root consistent with elapsed time since launch is $2\,\mathrm s$. The root $-3\,\mathrm s$ comes before the launch.
