A cart with $v=6-2t$ in SI units moves from $t=0$ to $t=5\,\mathrm{s}$. How far does it travel in total?

- $5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $13\,\mathrm{m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Direction reverses at 3 s. The positive triangle has area 9 m and the later one has magnitude 4 m. Add both magnitudes for distance.

$$
d=\frac{3\cdot6}{2}+\frac{2\cdot4}{2}=13\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
