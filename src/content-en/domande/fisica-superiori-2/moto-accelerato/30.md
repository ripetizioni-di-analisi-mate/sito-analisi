A cart with $v=6-2t$ in SI units moves from $t=0$ to $t=5\,\mathrm{s}$. How far does it travel in total?

- $5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $13\,\mathrm{m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The velocity is zero at $t=3\,\mathrm s$. Before that point the area is $\dfrac12\cdot3\cdot6=9\,\mathrm m$. Afterwards it is negative with magnitude $\dfrac12\cdot2\cdot4=4\,\mathrm m$. Distance includes both legs and equals $9+4=13\,\mathrm m$. Subtracting the two areas would give five metres. That value gives the trolley's position relative to its starting point after it travels part of the way back.
