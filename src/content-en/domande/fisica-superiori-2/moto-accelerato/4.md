A vehicle travels at $28\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the positive direction and brakes with constant acceleration $a=-6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. How long does it take to brake to rest? Choose the closest value.

- $9.33\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2.33\,\mathrm{s}$
- $168\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4.67\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Choose the direction of travel as positive. Velocity decreases by $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ each second and reaches zero after $4.67\,\mathrm{s}$. The required time is the first instant at which the velocity reaches zero. The equation describes the braking interval only.

$$
\begin{aligned}
t_a &= \frac{v_0}{b}=\frac{28}{6}\,\mathrm{s}\simeq 4.67\,\mathrm{s}
\end{aligned}
$$
