If limited grip reduces deceleration magnitude, at the same initial speed and reaction time what happens to stopping distance?

- It decreases
- It increases
- It stays the same
- It becomes zero

## Explanation

Reaction distance stays the same because speed and reaction time are unchanged. Braking distance alone is $s_f=\dfrac{v_0^2}{2b}$. Reducing $b$ makes the denominator smaller and increases the distance needed. The total distance grows by the same amount as the braking distance.
