If limited grip reduces deceleration magnitude, at the same initial speed and reaction time what happens to stopping distance?

- It decreases
- It increases
- It stays the same
- It becomes zero

## Explanation

Reaction distance stays the same. With a smaller deceleration magnitude, dividing the squared speed by that value gives more braking distance.
