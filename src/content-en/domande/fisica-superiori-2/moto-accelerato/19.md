An object has $v=-7\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at time $4\,\mathrm{s}$. With constant $a=2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is its velocity at $9\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $11\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Apply the equation to the five seconds since the first reading. The positive change of 10 m/s exceeds the initial magnitude of 7 m/s.

$$
v_f=-7+2(9-4)=3\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
