An object has $v=-7\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at time $4\,\mathrm{s}$. With constant $a=2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is its velocity at $9\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $11\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The interval from the known velocity reading lasts $9-4=5\,\mathrm s$. Velocity increases by $2\cdot5=10\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Starting from $-7\,\mathrm{m/s}$ gives $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The final positive sign shows that the velocity crossed zero between the two readings.
