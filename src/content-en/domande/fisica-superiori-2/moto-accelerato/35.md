Initial speed is $72\,\mathrm{km/h}$ and the magnitude of deceleration is $5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the braking time?

- $14.4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $40\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

First convert the speed to 20 m/s. Each second takes off 5 m/s until zero is reached.

$$
t_a=\frac{\frac{72}{3.6}}{5}\,\mathrm{s}=4\,\mathrm{s}.
$$
