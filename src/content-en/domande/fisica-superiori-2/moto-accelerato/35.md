Initial speed is $72\,\mathrm{km/h}$ and the magnitude of deceleration is $5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the braking time?

- $14.4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $40\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Convert the initial speed to metres per second by dividing $72$ by $3.6$, giving $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The magnitude decreases by $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ each second, so coming to rest takes $\dfrac{20}{5}=4\,\mathrm s$. The conversion puts velocity and acceleration in a consistent system of units. The reading gives a speed. After choosing an axis along the motion, use the positive magnitude of the deceleration in the denominator.
