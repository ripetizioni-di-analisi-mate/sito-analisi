At one-second intervals a sensor records positions of $0$, $3$, $8$ and $15\,\mathrm m$. The motion continues with the same constant acceleration. What will the next position be?

- $22\,\mathrm m$
- $30\,\mathrm m$
- $24\,\mathrm m$
- $18\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The displacements in successive seconds are $3$, $5$ and $7$ metres. Constant acceleration increases each one by the same amount, here $2$ metres. The next displacement is $9$ metres, giving a position of $15+9=24$ metres.
