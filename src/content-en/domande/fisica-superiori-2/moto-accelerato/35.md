A trolley moves along a straight line with constant acceleration. At one-second intervals it occupies the positions $0$, $3$, $8$ and $15\,\mathrm m$. What position does it occupy one second after the last one?

- $22\,\mathrm m$
- $30\,\mathrm m$
- $24\,\mathrm m$
- $18\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The displacements in the three intervals are $3$, $5$ and $7\,\mathrm m$. With constant acceleration $a$ the displacements in consecutive intervals of duration $T$ always differ by $aT^2$. Here the difference is $2\,\mathrm m$ with $T=1\,\mathrm s$, so $a=2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Therefore the displacement in the next second is $7\,\mathrm m+2\,\mathrm m=9\,\mathrm m$, and the position becomes $15\,\mathrm m+9\,\mathrm m=24\,\mathrm m$. The value $22\,\mathrm m$ is obtained by repeating the displacement of the last second, as in uniform motion.
