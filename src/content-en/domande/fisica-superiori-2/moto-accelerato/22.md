An athlete starts from rest, accelerates at $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ for $4\,\mathrm{s}$, then maintains the attained velocity for $3\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the final velocity?

- $10.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The increase occurs over the first four seconds. When acceleration becomes zero, the resulting velocity is unchanged.

$$
v_f=0+1.5\cdot4+0\cdot3=6\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
