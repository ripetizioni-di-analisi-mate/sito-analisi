Velocity rises linearly from $4$ to $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ over $7\,\mathrm{s}$. What displacement follows from the trapezoid area?

- $28\,\mathrm{m}$
- $84\,\mathrm{m}$
- $112\,\mathrm{m}$
- $56\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

On the velocity-time graph the starting and ending heights are $4$ and $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The trapezoid's average height is $\dfrac{4+12}{2}=8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Its width is $7\,\mathrm s$, so its area is $8\cdot7=56\,\mathrm m$. The entire trapezoid is above the axis and contributes positively.
