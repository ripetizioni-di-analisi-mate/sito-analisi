Velocity rises linearly from $4$ to $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ over $7\,\mathrm{s}$. What displacement follows from the trapezoid area?

- $28\,\mathrm{m}$
- $84\,\mathrm{m}$
- $112\,\mathrm{m}$
- $56\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The mean of the two endpoint velocities is 8 m/s. The trapezoid area is that value times seven seconds.

$$
\Delta x=\frac{4+12}{2}\cdot7\,\mathrm{m}=56\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
