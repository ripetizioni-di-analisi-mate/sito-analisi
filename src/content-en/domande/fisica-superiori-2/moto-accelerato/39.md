Uniform braking with magnitude $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ covers $50\,\mathrm{m}$ before rest. What was the initial speed?

- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $40\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $200\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Before braking the square of the speed must cancel the deceleration term over fifty metres. Take the positive root.

$$
u_0=\sqrt{2\cdot4\cdot50}\,\mathrm{m/s}=20\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
