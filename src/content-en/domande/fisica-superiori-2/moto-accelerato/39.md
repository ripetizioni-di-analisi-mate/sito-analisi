Uniform braking with magnitude $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ covers $50\,\mathrm{m}$ before rest. What was the initial speed?

- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $40\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $200\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Braking distance satisfies $s_f=\dfrac{v_0^2}{2b}$ because the final velocity is zero. Substituting the data gives $v_0^2=2\cdot4\cdot50=400$ in SI units. Speed is a magnitude and corresponds to the positive square root, $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
