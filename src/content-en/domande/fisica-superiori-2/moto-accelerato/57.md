A positive velocity decreases but remains positive over an interval. What is the sign of the average acceleration?

- Negative
- Positive
- Zero
- It is set by the initial position

## Explanation

Along the oriented axis the final velocity is below the initial one, so $v_f-v_i<0$. Time elapsed is positive, making the quotient negative. Remaining in the positive direction only means that both velocities are positive. The sign of the average acceleration depends on how the two readings change.
