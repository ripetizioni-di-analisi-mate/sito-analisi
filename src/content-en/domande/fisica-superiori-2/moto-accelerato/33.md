A car slows from $15$ to $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$ with $a=-3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What distance does it travel during this reduction in speed?

- $24\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-24\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $48\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The numerator and denominator of the relation are both negative, so displacement is positive.

$$
\Delta x=\frac{9^2-15^2}{2(-3)}\,\mathrm{m}=24\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
