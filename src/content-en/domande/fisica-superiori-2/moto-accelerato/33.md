A car slows from $15$ to $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$ with $a=-3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What distance does it travel during this reduction in speed?

- $24\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-24\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $48\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The velocity falls by $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$, so the reduction takes $\dfrac{6}{3}=2\,\mathrm s$. At constant acceleration the average velocity during the stage is $\dfrac{15+9}{2}=12\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Velocity stays positive and the distance travelled is $12\cdot2=24\,\mathrm m$.
