Two carts with the same constant non-zero acceleration have different initial velocities. What happens to their velocity difference?

- It vanishes as time grows
- It reverses sign every second
- It equals the acceleration
- It stays constant

## Explanation

At each common instant both initial velocities gain the same term $at$. Subtracting the two velocity equations cancels those terms. The difference therefore remains equal to its initial value. On a velocity-time graph the two lines are parallel, with the same vertical separation throughout.
