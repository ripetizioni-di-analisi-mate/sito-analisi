At the same initial speed a vehicle halves reaction time while braking is unchanged. Which distance halves?

- Only the reaction distance
- Only the braking distance
- The total distance in every case
- Both distances

## Explanation

Reaction distance is the product $v_0t_r$. Since $v_0$ is unchanged, halving the time halves this distance. Braking alone starts at the same speed and uses the same deceleration, so it covers the same distance as before. The total distance decreases only by the distance saved during the reaction.
