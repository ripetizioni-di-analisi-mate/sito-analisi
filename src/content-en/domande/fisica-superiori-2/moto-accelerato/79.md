What is the acceleration at the highest point of a ball thrown upwards, with air resistance ignored?

- Zero
- $g$ upwards
- $g$ downwards
- It depends on the initial velocity

## Explanation

The ball has zero velocity at the apex. Acceleration is still gravitational because the same model applies at that instant.
