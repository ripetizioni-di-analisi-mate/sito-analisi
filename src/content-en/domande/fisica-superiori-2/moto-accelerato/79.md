What is the acceleration at the highest point of a ball thrown upwards, with air resistance ignored?

- Zero
- $g$ upwards
- $g$ downwards
- It depends on the initial velocity

## Explanation

At the highest point, vertical velocity is zero. Acceleration is the rate of change of velocity, and in this model it equals $g$ downwards at every instant. That is why velocity is positive just before the highest point and negative just after it when upward is taken as positive.
