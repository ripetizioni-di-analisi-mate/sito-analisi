One ball is thrown up and another down from the same height at the same initial speed. Ignoring air, which has greater speed on reaching the ground?

- The one thrown upwards
- The one thrown downwards
- Neither, their impact speeds match
- It depends on the masses

## Explanation

With no air resistance, the square of the final velocity follows from $v_f^2=v_0^2+2a\Delta y$. Both launches start with the same speed and have the same displacement to the ground, so the two terms are equal. The final speeds agree even though the flight durations differ.
