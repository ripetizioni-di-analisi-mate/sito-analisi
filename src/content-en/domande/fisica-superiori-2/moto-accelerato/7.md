An object is released from rest $125\,\mathrm{m}$ above the ground. Neglecting air resistance and taking $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, when does it reach the ground?

- $2.5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $15\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Take height as positive upward. The acceleration is therefore negative, and height starts at $125\,\mathrm{m}$ and then decreases. Impact occurs at zero height. The equation gives two opposite roots; choose the positive one because motion is observed after release.

$$
\begin{aligned}
y &= 125-\frac{10}{2}t^2=0 \\[6pt]
t &= \sqrt{\frac{2\cdot 125}{10}}\,\mathrm{s}\simeq 5\,\mathrm{s}
\end{aligned}
$$
