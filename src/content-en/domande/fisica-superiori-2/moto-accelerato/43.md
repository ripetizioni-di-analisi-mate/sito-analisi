An object falls from rest for $2\,\mathrm{s}$ before impact. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and negligible air resistance, what was its initial height?

- $10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $40\,\mathrm{m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The object starts from rest and travels downwards by its original height. With acceleration of magnitude $g$, the equation gives $h=\dfrac12gt^2=\dfrac12\cdot10\cdot2^2=20\,\mathrm m$. The duration is squared because the velocity increases throughout the fall.
