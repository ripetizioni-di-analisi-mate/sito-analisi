An object falls from rest for $2\,\mathrm{s}$ before impact. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and negligible air resistance, what was its initial height?

- $10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $40\,\mathrm{m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Height decreases by half the product of gravity and time squared. This decrease equals the initial height above the ground.

$$
h=\frac12\cdot10\cdot2^2=20\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
