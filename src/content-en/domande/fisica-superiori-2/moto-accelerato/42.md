Reaction lasts $1\,\mathrm{s}$ at $16\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Uniform braking then lasts $4\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the velocity $3\,\mathrm{s}$ after detection?

- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

At the required time the car is halfway through the four seconds of braking, since the first second was reaction time. Velocity decreases linearly.

$$
v=16-\frac{16}{4}(3-1)=8\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
