Reaction lasts $1\,\mathrm{s}$ at $16\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Braking then lasts $4\,\mathrm{s}$ at constant acceleration until the vehicle stops. What is the velocity $3\,\mathrm{s}$ after detection?

- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Braking starts $1\,\mathrm s$ after the signal is seen. At the requested instant, braking has lasted $3-1=2\,\mathrm s$. To fall from $16$ to $0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in $4\,\mathrm s$, the acceleration is $-4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Over two seconds the velocity falls by $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$, leaving $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The vehicle stops five seconds after the signal is seen, after the requested instant. The braking equation for the sloping segment therefore still applies.
