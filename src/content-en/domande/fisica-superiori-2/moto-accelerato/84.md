For a fall modelled with constant acceleration a negative height relative to ground is calculated. Which interpretation is correct?

- The object continues underground according to the same physical law
- The object's mass is negative
- The chosen $g$ is necessarily wrong
- The requested time is after impact if the ground is at zero height

## Explanation

Zero height represents the ground and marks contact. If an equation for a fall from above the ground is extended to give a negative height, the time used is beyond contact. Describing what happens next requires a model of the impact or subsequent motion.
