A trolley moving in the positive direction brakes at constant acceleration until it stops. In the first $2\,\mathrm s$ of braking, it moves $36\,\mathrm m$. At the end of those $2\,\mathrm s$ its velocity is $14\,\mathrm{m/s}$. How much time elapses from the beginning of braking to rest?

- $3.5\,\mathrm s$
- $4\,\mathrm s$
- $8.5\,\mathrm s$
- $5.5\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

Over the first two seconds the average velocity is $\dfrac{36}{2}=18\,\mathrm{m/s}$. It is the mean of the initial velocity and $14\,\mathrm{m/s}$, so $v_0=22\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The slope is $\dfrac{14-22}{2}=-4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Dropping from $22$ to $0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ takes $\dfrac{22}{4}=5.5\,\mathrm s$.
