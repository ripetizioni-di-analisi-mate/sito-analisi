A trolley with constant acceleration moves through a displacement of $14\,\mathrm m$ in the first $2\,\mathrm s$ and $26\,\mathrm m$ in the next $2\,\mathrm s$. What is its velocity at $t=4\,\mathrm s$?

- $16\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $13\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

For equal intervals, the difference in displacement is $aT^2$. Since $26-14=12\,\mathrm m$ and $T=2\,\mathrm s$, the acceleration is $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. For the first stage, $14=2v_0+\dfrac12\cdot3\cdot2^2$, so $v_0=4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. After four seconds the velocity is $4+3\cdot4=16\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
