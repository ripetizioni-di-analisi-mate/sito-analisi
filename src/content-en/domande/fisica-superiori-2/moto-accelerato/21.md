On a first leg velocity rises from $4$ to $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Keeping this acceleration for another $3\,\mathrm{s}$, what velocity is reached?

- $18\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The second phase starts at the velocity where the first finishes, 12 m/s. The next three seconds add 6 m/s.

$$
v_f=12+2\cdot3=18\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
