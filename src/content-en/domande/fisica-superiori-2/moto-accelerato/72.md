Which item need not be known when constant acceleration, displacement and initial velocity are given and the final speed is sought?

- Displacement
- Acceleration
- Initial velocity
- Duration

## Explanation

The equation $v_f^2=v_0^2+2a\Delta x$ eliminates time. It gives the squared final velocity.
