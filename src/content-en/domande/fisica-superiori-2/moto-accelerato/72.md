Which item need not be known when constant acceleration, displacement and initial velocity are given and the final speed is sought?

- Displacement
- Acceleration
- Initial velocity
- Duration

## Explanation

The relation $v_f^2=v_0^2+2a\Delta x$ contains only the three given quantities and the square of the final velocity. When the right-hand side is nonnegative, the positive square root gives the required magnitude. Choosing the sign of the velocity would also require knowing the direction of motion.
