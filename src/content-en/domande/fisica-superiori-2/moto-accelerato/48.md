A ball launches upward from ground at $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and negligible air, when does it first reach $25\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The height in metres is $30t-5t^2$. Setting $30t-5t^2=25$ gives $(t-1)(t-5)=0$. Both times lie within the flight. The first is $1\,\mathrm s$, while the ball is still rising towards its highest point, reached after $\dfrac{30}{10}=3\,\mathrm s$.
