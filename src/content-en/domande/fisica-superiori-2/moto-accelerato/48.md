A ball launches upward from ground at $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and negligible air, when does it first reach $25\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The ball reaches the height on the way up and on the way down. Choose the first positive root, before the apex at 3 s.

$$
30t-5t^2=25\quad\Longrightarrow\quad (t-1)(t-5)=0,\quad t=1\,\mathrm{s}.
$$
