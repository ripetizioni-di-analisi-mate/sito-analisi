At one point $v^2=16\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}$ is known. Velocity is in the direction opposite the positive axis. What is its signed value?

- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-16\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The square gives the magnitude $\sqrt{16}=4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The question assigns the direction opposite to the positive axis, so the component is $-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The square alone is compatible with both directions, and the directional information selects the root.
