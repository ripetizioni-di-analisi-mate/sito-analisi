At one point $v^2=16\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}$ is known. Velocity is in the direction opposite the positive axis. What is its signed value?

- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-16\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The square root gives the magnitude 4 m/s. The direction tells us to assign the negative sign.

$$
v=-\sqrt{16}\,\mathrm{m/s}=-4\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
