If $a<0$ and $v_0>0$, which point on the position–time graph corresponds to $v=0$ when it is reached?

- A minimum with a horizontal tangent
- A maximum with a horizontal tangent
- The vertical intercept in every case
- A discontinuity of position

## Explanation

The slope of the position graph is velocity. Under negative acceleration, velocity starts positive, reaches zero and becomes negative if the motion continues. The curve first rises and then falls. The point with zero velocity is therefore a maximum with a horizontal tangent.
