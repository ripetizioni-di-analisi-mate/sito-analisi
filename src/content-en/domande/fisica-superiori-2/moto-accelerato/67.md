If $a<0$ and $v_0>0$, which point on the position–time graph corresponds to $v=0$ when it is reached?

- A minimum with a horizontal tangent
- A maximum with a horizontal tangent
- The vertical intercept in every case
- A discontinuity of position

## Explanation

Velocity changes from positive to negative. Position first increases and then decreases, with zero slope at the reversal.
