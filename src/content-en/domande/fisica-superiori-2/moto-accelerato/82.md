An object is thrown up, and down is chosen as positive. What are the signs of initial velocity and acceleration?

- Both positive
- Both negative
- Positive initial velocity, negative acceleration
- Negative initial velocity, positive acceleration

## Explanation

Taking downward as positive makes the upward launch velocity negative. Gravity points downwards, so its acceleration is positive. During the rise the negative velocity approaches zero. Beyond the highest point the velocity is positive and describes the downward part of the motion.
