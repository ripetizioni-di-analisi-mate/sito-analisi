An object starts from rest with $a=2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. How much distance does it cover during the fourth second alone?

- $7\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The fourth second runs from $t=3$ to $t=4$. Subtract the total distance after three seconds from the total after four.

$$
\Delta x=4^2-3^2=7\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
