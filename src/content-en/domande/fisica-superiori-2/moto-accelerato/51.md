An object starts from rest with $a=2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. How much distance does it cover during the fourth second alone?

- $7\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The fourth second runs from $t=3$ to $t=4\,\mathrm s$. Displacement from the start is $t^2$ in metres, so at the two boundaries it is $9$ and $16\,\mathrm m$. The velocity is positive and the distance travelled during the fourth second alone is $16-9=7\,\mathrm m$.
