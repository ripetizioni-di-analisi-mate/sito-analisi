For a fall from rest, initial height is quadrupled with $g$ unchanged. How does the fall time change?

- It doubles
- It quadruples
- It halves
- It remains the same

## Explanation

In a fall from rest, height is proportional to the square of time because $h=\dfrac12gt^2$. If the height becomes four times its initial value, the time is multiplied by $\sqrt4=2$. The model uses the same value of $g$ before and after the change in height.
