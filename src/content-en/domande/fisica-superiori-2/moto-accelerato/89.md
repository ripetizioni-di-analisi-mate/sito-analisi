In $x(t)=x_0+v_0t+\frac12at^2$, how do you check the units of the third term?

- Multiply m/s² by s² to get metres
- Divide m/s² by s²
- Add time to velocity
- The result is in newtons

## Explanation

The factor one half is dimensionless. Squared time cancels the squared seconds in acceleration.
