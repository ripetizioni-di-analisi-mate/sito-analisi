In $x(t)=x_0+v_0t+\frac12at^2$, how do you check the units of the third term?

- Multiply m/s² by s² to get metres
- Divide m/s² by s²
- Add time to velocity
- The result is in newtons

## Explanation

Acceleration has unit $\mathrm{m/s^2}$, and time squared has unit $\mathrm{s^2}$. In the product the squared seconds cancel, leaving metres. The factor $\dfrac12$ is dimensionless. The third term can therefore be added to the other two terms, which are also signed lengths.
