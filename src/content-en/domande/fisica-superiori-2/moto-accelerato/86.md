How can tabulated velocity data be checked for consistency with constant acceleration?

- Compare only the initial and final velocities
- Compare velocity changes divided by their corresponding durations
- Compare positions with squared velocities
- Use only the maximum velocity

## Explanation

For data linear in time, the ratio of velocity change to duration stays the same even for unequal intervals.
