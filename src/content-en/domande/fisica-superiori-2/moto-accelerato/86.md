A sensor records exactly $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at $1\,\mathrm s$ and $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at $3\,\mathrm s$. If acceleration remains constant, which third reading at $6\,\mathrm s$ is consistent with the first two?

- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The slope fixed by the first two readings is $\dfrac{2-(-2)}{3-1}=2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. There are $3\,\mathrm s$ between the second and third readings. Velocity therefore increases by $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to reach $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Reusing the earlier change would ignore that the second interval is longer.
