At $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $b=5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ the measured distance from detection to rest is $60\,\mathrm{m}$. What reaction time does the model give?

- $1\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Braking alone requires $\dfrac{20^2}{2\cdot5}=40\,\mathrm m$. Of the measured $60\,\mathrm m$, the remaining $20\,\mathrm m$ are travelled before braking. In that stage the velocity is constant at $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$, so the reaction time is $\dfrac{20}{20}=1\,\mathrm s$.
