At $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $b=5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ the measured distance from detection to rest is $60\,\mathrm{m}$. What reaction time does the model give?

- $1\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Braking takes 40 m. The remaining twenty metres are the reaction distance at 20 m/s.

$$
t_r=\frac{60-\frac{20^2}{2\cdot5}}{20}\,\mathrm{s}=1\,\mathrm{s}.
$$
