An initial positive velocity of $18\,\mathrm{km/h}$ increases under $a=3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What velocity in m/s is reached after $2\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $11\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $24\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The initial velocity of $18\,\mathrm{km/h}$ equals $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. During the stated $2\,\mathrm s$, acceleration adds $3\cdot2=6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Since the initial velocity and the change point in the same direction, the resulting velocity is $5+6=11\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The conversion applies to the initial velocity. The increase obtained from the acceleration and duration is already in the required units, so the two contributions can be added.
