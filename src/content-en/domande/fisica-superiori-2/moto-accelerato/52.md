An initial positive velocity of $18\,\mathrm{km/h}$ increases under $a=3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What velocity in m/s is reached after $2\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $11\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $24\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Conversion gives 5 m/s at the start. In two seconds acceleration adds 6 m/s in the same units.

$$
v=\frac{18}{3.6}+3\cdot2=11\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
