How is the motion described when $a=0$ and $v_0$ is non-zero?

- Position stays constant
- Velocity becomes zero after a second
- Position is proportional to time squared
- Velocity stays at $v_0$ and position is linear in time

## Explanation

With $a=0$, the acceleration terms vanish in both equations. This gives $v=v_0$ and $x=x_0+v_0t$. Since $v_0$ is nonzero, the position changes by the same amount over any intervals of equal duration. The position-time graph is a sloping straight line.
