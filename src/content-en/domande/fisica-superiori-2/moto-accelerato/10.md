An object has initial velocity $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and constant acceleration $6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Find the signed velocity after $7\,\mathrm{s}$.

- $42\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $50\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-34\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $14\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The acceleration gives a change of $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ each second. Over $7\,\mathrm{s}$ the total change is $42\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Adding it to the initial velocity gives $50\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The same velocity is the ordinate at the end of the velocity–time line.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta v &= (6)\cdot 7 = 42\,\mathrm{m/s} \\[6pt]
v_f &= 8+(42)=50\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
