An object starts with a velocity of $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$. With constant acceleration, it moves through a displacement of $140\,\mathrm m$ over the next $4\,\mathrm s$. What is its velocity at the end of the interval?

- $35\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $50\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $70\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Dividing displacement by duration gives the average velocity, $\dfrac{140}{4}=35\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Since acceleration is constant, this equals the mean of the endpoint velocities. Thus $20+v_f=2\cdot35$ and $v_f=50\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The value $35\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is the velocity at the midpoint of the interval.
