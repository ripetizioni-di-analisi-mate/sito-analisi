A body of mass $6\,\mathrm{kg}$ is at signed height $5\,\mathrm{m}$ relative to the zero level. What is its gravitational potential energy with $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$?

- $-300\,\mathrm{J}$
- $150\,\mathrm{J}$
- $600\,\mathrm{J}$
- $300\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Mass times $g$ gives the weight magnitude $60\,\mathrm{N}$. Multiply again by the signed height $5\,\mathrm{m}$. The result $300\,\mathrm{J}$ is relative to the chosen zero level. A height below it gives a negative value without implying negative kinetic energy. Shifting the reference adds the same constant to each point’s energy, while the difference between two positions is unchanged.

$$
\begin{aligned}
U_g &= mgh=6\cdot 10\cdot(5)\,\mathrm{J}=300\,\mathrm{J}
\end{aligned}
$$
