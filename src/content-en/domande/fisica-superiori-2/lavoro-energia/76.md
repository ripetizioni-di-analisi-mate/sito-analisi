A crate slides on a fixed floor with friction. What does friction work do to the mechanical energy of the crate?

- It decreases it
- It increases it
- It leaves it unchanged in every displacement
- It sets it equal to mass

## Explanation

Friction opposes displacement and does negative work. The mechanical balance loses the corresponding amount. If a box on a horizontal section loses $15\,\mathrm J$ to friction, this energy transfers to other forms, for instance to the surfaces' internal energy.
