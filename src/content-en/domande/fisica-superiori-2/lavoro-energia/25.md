Net work on a $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ cart is $36\,\mathrm{J}$. It starts from rest. What is its final speed?

- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $36\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Initial kinetic energy is zero. All net work equals final kinetic energy, from which speed follows. For $m=2\,\mathrm{kg}$, the numerical value of $v_f^2$ is $36$ in SI units. The square root gives $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the speed is positive.

$$
v=\sqrt{\frac{2\cdot36}{2}}\,\mathrm{m/s}=6\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
