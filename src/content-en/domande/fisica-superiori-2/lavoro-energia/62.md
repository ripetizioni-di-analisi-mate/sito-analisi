Two objects of equal mass have opposite velocities with equal magnitude. Which comparison is correct?

- One energy is negative
- The rightward object has more energy
- Both energies are zero
- Their kinetic energies are equal

## Explanation

The squares use the speeds. The two masses and magnitudes agree, so the energies agree.
