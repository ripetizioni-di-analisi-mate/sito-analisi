Two carts of equal mass each gain $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in speed. The first goes from $2$ to $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the second from $5$ to $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the ratio of the net work on the second to the net work on the first?

- $1$
- $4$
- $\dfrac12$
- $2$

## Explanation

Net work depends on the difference of the squared speeds. Because the masses are equal, the ratio only compares these differences. The first gives $12$ and the second $24$. For the same speed increase, more work is required on the initially faster cart.

$$
\frac{W_2}{W_1}=\frac{7^2-5^2}{4^2-2^2}=\frac{24}{12}=2.
$$
