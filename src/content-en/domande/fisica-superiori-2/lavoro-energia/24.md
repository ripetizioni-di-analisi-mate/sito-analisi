At the same speed of $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$, a cart with its load has kinetic energy $150\,\mathrm J$ and the empty cart has $50\,\mathrm J$. What is the mass of the load alone?

- $4\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Because the speed is the same, removing the load reduces the energy solely through the change in mass. The $100\,\mathrm J$ difference is the kinetic energy of the load alone. Dividing by half the squared speed gives $8\,\mathrm{kg}$. The cart's contribution was already subtracted in the difference.

$$
m_c=\frac{2(150-50)}{5^2}\,\mathrm{kg}=8\,\mathrm{kg}.
$$
