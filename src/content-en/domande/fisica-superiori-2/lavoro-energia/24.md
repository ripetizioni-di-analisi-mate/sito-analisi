An object moving at $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ has kinetic energy $100\,\mathrm{J}$. What is its mass?

- $4\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $16\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Mass is twice the energy divided by the square of speed. Five squared is 25.

$$
m=\frac{2\cdot100}{5^2}\,\mathrm{kg}=8\,\mathrm{kg}.
$$
