Two ramps of different slopes connect the same initial and final heights. How does the work of weight compare for the same object?

- It is greater on the longer ramp
- It is greater on the steeper ramp
- It depends on the travel time
- It is equal on both ramps

## Explanation

The work of weight near the ground is $mg(h_i-h_f)$. The two heights and the shared factors give the same value.
