A sled travels down a $5\,\mathrm m$ ramp and then back up to its starting point. Its final speed equals its initial speed. A constant resistance of $6\,\mathrm N$ opposes the motion in both sections. The towing force, gravity and resistance are the only forces doing work. How much total work does the towing force do?

- $0\,\mathrm J$
- $30\,\mathrm J$
- $-60\,\mathrm J$
- $60\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Gravity does zero work over the closed path. The resistance removes $30\,\mathrm J$ on the outward journey and another $30\,\mathrm J$ on the return. The work–energy theorem requires zero net work because the speeds at the endpoints are equal. The tow therefore supplies $60\,\mathrm J$.

$$
W_T=-[-6(5+5)]\,\mathrm J=60\,\mathrm J.
$$
