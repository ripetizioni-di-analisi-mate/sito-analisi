A parallel-force graph against distance has one region above the axis and one below with equal geometric area. What is total work?

- The positive sum of both areas
- The magnitude of the lower area alone
- Zero
- Twice the upper area

## Explanation

For work, area below the axis enters negatively. The two equal magnitudes cancel. If each region has magnitude $12\,\mathrm J$, the contributions are $+12$ and $-12\,\mathrm J$. The total geometric area would be $24\,\mathrm J$, but the work is zero.
