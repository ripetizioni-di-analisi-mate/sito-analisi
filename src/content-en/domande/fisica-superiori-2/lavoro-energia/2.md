A cart of $6\,\mathrm{kg}$ has speed $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is its kinetic energy?

- $73.5\,\mathrm{J}$
- $147\,\mathrm{J}$
- $294\,\mathrm{J}$
- $441\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Kinetic energy depends on mass and the square of speed. Square $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$ before multiplying by half the mass. The value $147\,\mathrm{J}$ is non-negative, as expected from positive mass and squared velocity. In the product of units, kilograms times squared metres per squared second equals joules. A cart of the same mass moving twice as fast would have four times this energy.

$$
\begin{aligned}
K &= \frac12 mv^2 = \frac{6\cdot 7^2}{2}\,\mathrm{J} \\[6pt]
&= 147\,\mathrm{J}
\end{aligned}
$$
