A parallel force increases linearly from $4$ to $10\,\mathrm{N}$ over $3\,\mathrm{m}$. What is its work?

- $12\,\mathrm{J}$
- $21\,\mathrm{J}$
- $18\,\mathrm{J}$
- $30\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The trapezoid bases are the endpoint forces. Their mean, 7 N, times three metres gives the work.

$$
W=\frac{4+10}{2}\cdot3\,\mathrm{J}=21\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
