A cart starts from rest and drops through $30\,\mathrm{m}$ without friction. What is its speed at the bottom with $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$? Choose the closest value.

- $24.495\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $12.247\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $48.99\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $73.485\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Mechanical energy is conserved without friction because weight is represented by potential energy and the normal force from the stationary surface does no work. The loss in height energy is $mgh$ and becomes an increase in kinetic energy. Add it to the initial kinetic energy from speed $0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The same mass appears on both sides and cancels. The positive square root gives $24.495\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v_f^2 &= v_0^2+2gh = 0^2+2\cdot10\cdot30 \\[6pt]
v_f &= \sqrt{0^2+2\cdot10\cdot30}\,\mathrm{m/s}\simeq 24.495\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$
