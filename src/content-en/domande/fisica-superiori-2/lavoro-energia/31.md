A $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ object drops $4\,\mathrm{m}$, with $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is its change in gravitational potential energy?

- $80\,\mathrm{J}$
- $20\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-80\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Final minus initial height is −4 m. Multiplication by mass and gravity keeps the negative sign. The change is $2\cdot10\cdot(-4)\,\mathrm J=-80\,\mathrm J$. Gravity does positive work during the descent. The question asks for the change in potential energy.

$$
\Delta U_g=2\cdot10(-4)\,\mathrm{J}=-80\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
