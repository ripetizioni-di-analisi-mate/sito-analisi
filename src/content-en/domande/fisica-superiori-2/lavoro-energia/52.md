A pump takes in $600\,\mathrm{J}$ and transfers $70\%$ usefully. How much energy is not useful output?

- $120\,\mathrm{J}$
- $420\,\mathrm{J}$
- $180\,\mathrm{J}$
- $300\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Take the complement to one hundred percent to find the non-useful share. It is 30 percent of the input.

$$
E_\mathrm{loss}=(1-0.70)\,600\,\mathrm{J}=180\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
