An object moves $4\,\mathrm m$ to the right and then returns to its starting point. A constant force of $12\,\mathrm N$ always points right. Friction has magnitude $3\,\mathrm N$ and opposes the motion in both sections. What is the sum of the work done by these two forces?

- $-24\,\mathrm J$
- $0\,\mathrm J$
- $-12\,\mathrm J$
- $+24\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The contributions of the constant force are opposite because the displacement reverses while the force stays the same. Friction reverses direction along with the motion, so its work stays negative. Each friction contribution is $-12\,\mathrm J$, for a total of $-24\,\mathrm J$.

$$
W=(12\cdot4-12\cdot4-3\cdot4-3\cdot4)\,\mathrm J=-24\,\mathrm J.
$$
