A push does $50\,\mathrm{J}$ over a displacement of $4\,\mathrm{m}$. What is its component along the displacement?

- $12.5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N}$
- $50\,\mathrm{N}$
- $200\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Work gives the projection times distance directly. Dividing by four metres gives the component in newtons.

$$
F_\parallel=\frac{50}{4}\,\mathrm{N}=12.5\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
