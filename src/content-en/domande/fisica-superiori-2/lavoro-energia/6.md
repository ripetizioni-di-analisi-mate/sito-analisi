An ideal spring of constant $180\,\mathrm{N/m}$ is compressed by $0.25\,\mathrm{m}$. How much elastic potential energy does it store?

- $2.8125\,\mathrm{J}$
- $5.625\,\mathrm{J}$
- $11.25\,\mathrm{J}$
- $22.5\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Measure displacement from the natural length. The force needed to load the spring slowly grows from zero to $45\,\mathrm{N}$. Its work is the triangular area with base $0.25\,\mathrm{m}$ and height equal to the final force. The factor one half appears because the force did not have its final value for the entire displacement. Equal amounts of compression and extension give the same energy because displacement is squared.

$$
\begin{aligned}
U_e &= \frac12 kx^2 = \frac{180(-0.25)^2}{2}\,\mathrm{J} \\[6pt]
&= 5.625\,\mathrm{J}
\end{aligned}
$$
