An external push does positive work and gravity is already included through $U_g$. If no other force does work, how does $E_m$ change?

- It always decreases
- It stays unchanged
- It increases by the work of the push
- It changes by twice the push's work

## Explanation

With weight represented by potential energy, the mechanical change equals work by other forces. Only the push remains here. If the push does $30\,\mathrm J$ of work, $E_m$ increases by $30\,\mathrm J$. The change in $K$ also depends on how much the height changes.
