Two objects have equal kinetic energy. The second has four times the first one's mass. What is the ratio of the first speed to the second?

- $2$
- $4$
- $0.5$
- $1$

## Explanation

To offset the mass factor four, squared speed is one quarter. The speed ratio is the square root of four. Equal energies give $m_1v_1^2=m_2v_2^2$. Thus, for equal energy the lighter object must move faster. The requested ratio is $2$.

$$
\frac{v_1}{v_2}=\sqrt{\frac{m_2}{m_1}}=2.
$$
