A frictionless cart drops $6\,\mathrm{m}$ starting at $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is its final speed with $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$? Choose the closest value.

- $10.954\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $11.136\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The final speed squared includes the initial squared speed and the gravity contribution. Omitting the former would apply only from rest.

$$
v_f=\sqrt{2^2+2\cdot10\cdot6}\,\mathrm{m/s}=\sqrt{124}\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
