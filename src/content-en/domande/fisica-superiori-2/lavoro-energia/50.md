A machine gives $300\,\mathrm{J}$ of useful output at $75\%$ efficiency. How much energy does it take in?

- $225\,\mathrm{J}$
- $300\,\mathrm{J}$
- $400\,\mathrm{J}$
- $525\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Output is 75 percent of the input. Divide output by 0.75 to recover the input.

$$
E_i=\frac{300}{0.75}\,\mathrm{J}=400\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
