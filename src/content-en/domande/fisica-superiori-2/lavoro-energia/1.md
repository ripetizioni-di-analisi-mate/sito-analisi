A constant force of $30\,\mathrm{N}$ makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with a straight-line displacement of $6\,\mathrm{m}$. Find its work.

- $90\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-90\,\mathrm{J}$
- $180\,\mathrm{J}$
- $45\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Work uses the component of force parallel to displacement. The angle is measured between these vectors, so use cosine. The signed component is $15\,\mathrm{N}$. Multiplying by $6\,\mathrm{m}$ gives $90\,\mathrm{J}$. The perpendicular component has no displacement in its direction and contributes zero. The sign of the result agrees with the projection's direction relative to the motion.

$$
\begin{aligned}
F_\parallel &= 30\cos60^\circ\,\mathrm{N}\simeq 15\,\mathrm{N} \\[6pt]
W &= F_\parallel s \simeq 90\,\mathrm{J}
\end{aligned}
$$
