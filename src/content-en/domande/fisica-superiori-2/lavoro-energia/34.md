A spring with $k=200\,\mathrm{N/m}$ stores $4\,\mathrm{J}$. What is its displacement magnitude?

- $0.1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.04\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.4\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Isolate displacement squared. Double energy and divide by stiffness to get $0.04\,\mathrm{m^2}$, then take the positive square root. The magnitude is $0.20\,\mathrm m$. The positive root describes the size of the deformation. Extension and compression of this magnitude store the same energy.

$$
x=\sqrt{\frac{2\cdot4}{200}}\,\mathrm{m}=0.2\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
