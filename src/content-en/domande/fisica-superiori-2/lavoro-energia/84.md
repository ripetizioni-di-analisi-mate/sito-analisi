With positive energy input and no release of stored energy, what range can the efficiency fraction take?

- From zero to one inclusive
- Any positive number, including greater than one
- From −1 to zero
- From one to a hundred

## Explanation

Useful output is a non-negative part of input. Their ratio therefore lies between zero and one. At zero there is no useful output. At one all the input energy is useful. In this balance there are no additional sources that could make the output exceed the input.
