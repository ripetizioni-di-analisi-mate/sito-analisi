A $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ object starts at $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Over one leg net work is $-36\,\mathrm{J}$. What is the final speed?

- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Initial energy is 100 J and negative work brings it to 64 J. With this mass $K=v^2$ in SI units, so speed is 8 m/s. The work gives the physical difference between the energies, measured in joules. The speed is found afterwards, from the square root that restores the units of speed.

$$
v_f=\sqrt{\frac{2(100-36)}{2}}\,\mathrm{m/s}=8\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
