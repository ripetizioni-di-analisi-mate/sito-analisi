An ideal pendulum descends and rises without friction. At which position is its speed greatest?

- At the greatest height
- It has the same speed at each position
- At the lowest height of the path
- Whenever the string is horizontal, in every case

## Explanation

Total energy stays constant. At the lowest height $U_g$ is least, so the kinetic share is greatest. As the pendulum rises, gravitational energy increases and speed falls. At the lowest height, the opposite applies because the loss of gravitational energy is greatest.
