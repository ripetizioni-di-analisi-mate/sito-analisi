Two engines supply the same useful work in different times. Which has greater average power?

- The one taking more time
- They must have equal power
- The one taking less time
- The one losing more energy, irrespective of duration

## Explanation

Average power divides the same positive work by time. A smaller denominator gives a larger quotient. For the same positive work, reducing duration increases the energy transferred per second on average. The times being compared must cover all the useful work.
