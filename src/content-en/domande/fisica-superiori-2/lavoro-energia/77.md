When gravitational potential energy is included in $K+U_g$, where does the work of weight appear?

- As a separate term also added to the other side
- Always with the sign of $\Delta U_g$
- It is always zero
- In the change in $U_g$ with opposite sign

## Explanation

The work of weight is already represented by $-\Delta U_g$. Adding it as other work would count it twice. During descent $U_g$ decreases and gravity does positive work. The opposite signs link two descriptions of the same interaction. Use one representation in the balance.
