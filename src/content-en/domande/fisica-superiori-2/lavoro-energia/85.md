When can efficiency be found as the ratio of two average powers?

- When one is in joules and the other in watts
- Only when durations necessarily differ
- When one measures useful output and the other input over the same interval
- Always even with input and output reversed

## Explanation

Dividing both energies by the same duration leaves their ratio unchanged. Different durations do not cancel this way. Useful power averaged only during lifting and input power averaged over a longer period refer to different intervals. Their ratio does not generally represent the efficiency.
