When can efficiency be found as the ratio of two average powers?

- When one is in joules and the other in watts
- Only when durations necessarily differ
- When both cover the same duration, one useful output and one input
- Always even with input and output reversed

## Explanation

Dividing both energies by the same duration leaves their ratio unchanged. Different durations do not cancel this way.
