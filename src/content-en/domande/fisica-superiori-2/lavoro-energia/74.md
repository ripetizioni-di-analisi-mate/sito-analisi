A body starts from rest and slides down a smooth track. Which data determine its final speed from the balance of weight and kinetic energy?

- Height drop and mass alone
- Path length and mass
- Height drop and $g$
- Travel time and mass

## Explanation

The height contribution $mgh$ equals $\frac12mv^2$. Mass occurs on both sides and cancels from the result.
