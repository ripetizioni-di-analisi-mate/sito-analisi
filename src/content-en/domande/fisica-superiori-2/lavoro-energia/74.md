A cart enters one of two frictionless tracks at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Both end lower than the entry point. The highest point of track A is $0.60\,\mathrm m$ above the entry. That of track B is $1.20\,\mathrm m$ higher. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and no other forces doing work, which track can it complete?

- Both
- B only
- A only
- Neither

## Explanation

The entry speed allows a rise of at most $0.80\,\mathrm m$. Find that value by converting all the initial kinetic energy into potential energy at the highest reachable point. The cart gets over the rise on A. On B it stops before the crest even though the endpoint is lower.

$$
h_{\max}=\frac{4^2}{2\cdot10}\,\mathrm m=0.80\,\mathrm m.
$$
