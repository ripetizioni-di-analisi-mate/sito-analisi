Can an object below the chosen height zero have positive kinetic energy?

- No, the two energies must have the same sign
- Yes, if its speed is non-zero
- No, a negative position rules out motion
- Only if the mass is negative

## Explanation

Height energy can be negative relative to a reference. Kinetic energy depends on speed squared and positive mass. For example, with $m=1\,\mathrm{kg}$, $h=-2\,\mathrm m$, $v=2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, the energies are $U_g=-20\,\mathrm J$ and $K=2\,\mathrm J$. They describe different aspects.
