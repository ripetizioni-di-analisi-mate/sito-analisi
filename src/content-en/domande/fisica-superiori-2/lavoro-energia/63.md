A push does positive work while the object slows down. What conclusion is certain?

- The push does negative work
- Net work by all forces is negative
- No other force does work
- Net work must be zero

## Explanation

The decrease in speed reduces kinetic energy. Other forces must do negative work with a greater magnitude than the push’s work. For example, if the push supplies $10\,\mathrm J$ and braking removes $15\,\mathrm J$, the object loses a total of $5\,\mathrm J$ of kinetic energy.
