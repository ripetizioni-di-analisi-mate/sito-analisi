The work of a constant force is negative and displacement is non-zero. What follows about its projection?

- It definitely points forward
- It is perpendicular to the displacement
- It points opposite to the displacement
- Its magnitude is always zero

## Explanation

Displacement magnitude is positive. The sign of the product comes from the component of force in that direction. The angle between force and displacement exceeds $90^\circ$ when the cosine is negative. The opposing component can coexist with a perpendicular component, which does no work.
