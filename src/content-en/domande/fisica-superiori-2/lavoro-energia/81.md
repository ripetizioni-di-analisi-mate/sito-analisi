Two motors lift the same load through the same height. Each lifts at constant speed, and there is no other resistance on the load. Motor A draws $800\,\mathrm W$ at $75\%$ efficiency. Motor B draws $1000\,\mathrm W$ at $50\%$ efficiency. Which takes less time?

- Motor B
- The times are equal
- The mass is also needed to compare the times
- Motor A

## Explanation

Useful power is the input power multiplied by the efficiency. A supplies $600\,\mathrm W$ and B $500\,\mathrm W$. The load requires the same useful energy in both trials, so A takes less time. The higher input power of B does not settle the comparison.

$$
\mathcal P_{u,A}=800\cdot0.75\,\mathrm W,\qquad \mathcal P_{u,B}=1000\cdot0.50\,\mathrm W.
$$
