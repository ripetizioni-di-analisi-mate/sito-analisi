The parallel force component is $5\,\mathrm{N}$ for the first $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and $-3\,\mathrm{N}$ for the next $2\,\mathrm{m}$. How much work does it do?

- $20\,\mathrm{J}$
- $26\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-14\,\mathrm{J}$
- $14\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The rectangle areas are 20 J and −6 J. Their sum accounts for the sign change of the parallel force.

$$
W=5\cdot4-3\cdot2=14\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
