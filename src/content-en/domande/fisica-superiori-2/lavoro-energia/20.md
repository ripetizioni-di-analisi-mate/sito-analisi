A constant horizontal force of $12\,\mathrm N$ points right. The object follows a semicircular arc from A to B. B is $3\,\mathrm m$ to the right of A and at the same height. How much work does the force do along the arc?

- $18\pi\,\mathrm J$
- $0\,\mathrm J$
- $-36\,\mathrm J$
- $36\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The force is fixed and horizontal. Its work on each piece depends on that piece's horizontal displacement. Adding the pieces cancels the intermediate positions and leaves the total horizontal displacement of $3\,\mathrm m$. This gives $36\,\mathrm J$. The arc length does not determine the work.

$$
W=(12\,\mathrm N)(3\,\mathrm m)=36\,\mathrm J.
$$
