During lifting a $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass, $U_g$ increases by $160\,\mathrm{J}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, how far does it rise?

- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m}$
- $40\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Energy increase divided by weight gives height change. Joules divided by newtons give metres. The weight is $40\,\mathrm N$, so the rise is $\dfrac{160}{40}=4$ metres. The positive sign is consistent with lifting the object.

$$
\Delta h=\frac{160}{4\cdot10}\,\mathrm{m}=4\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
