A cart moves from $x=0$ to $x=4\,\mathrm m$. It enters with $10\,\mathrm J$ of kinetic energy. The component of the resultant force along the axis increases linearly from $-6$ to $+6\,\mathrm N$. At which point is kinetic energy lowest?

- $x=2\,\mathrm m$
- $x=0\,\mathrm m$
- $x=4\,\mathrm m$
- $x=3\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Over the first $2\,\mathrm m$ the resultant force is negative and the kinetic energy drops by $6\,\mathrm J$. After $x=2\,\mathrm m$, the force becomes positive and the cart gains energy. The minimum is therefore at the point where the force changes sign and equals $4\,\mathrm J$.

$$
K(2\,\mathrm m)=\left(10-\frac{6\cdot2}{2}\right)\,\mathrm J=4\,\mathrm J.
$$
