A parallel force rises linearly from $0$ to $12\,\mathrm{N}$ over $5\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the work?

- $30\,\mathrm{J}$
- $12\,\mathrm{J}$
- $60\,\mathrm{J}$
- $24\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The area is a triangle. Its mean height over the displacement is 6 N, so work is 6 N times 5 m.

$$
W=\frac12\cdot5\cdot12\,\mathrm{J}=30\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
