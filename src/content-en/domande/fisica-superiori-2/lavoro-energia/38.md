A ball is launched upwards at $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Ignoring air resistance with $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, how much height does it gain before coming momentarily to rest?

- $10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

At the apex kinetic energy is zero. Initial kinetic energy becomes the entire increase in height energy. Writing $\dfrac12m\cdot10^2=mg\Delta h$ cancels the mass and gives $\Delta h=5\,\mathrm m$. The velocity is momentarily zero at the top. Gravity still acts.

$$
\Delta h=\frac{10^2}{2\cdot10}\,\mathrm{m}=5\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
