A rope at $60^\circ$ to the displacement supplies $36\,\mathrm{J}$ over $3\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the pull magnitude?

- $6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$
- $24\,\mathrm{N}$
- $36\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The 36 J over 3 m requires 12 N along motion. Cosine is one half, so the full magnitude is twice the projection. Check the factor by substituting $24\,\mathrm N$ into the work formula. $24\,\mathrm N$ times $3\,\mathrm m$ times one half gives the specified $36\,\mathrm J$.

$$
F=\frac{36}{3\cos60^\circ}\,\mathrm{N}=24\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
