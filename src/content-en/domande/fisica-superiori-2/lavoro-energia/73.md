On a stationary smooth ramp a cart experiences weight and normal force. Why does the normal force leave total mechanical energy unchanged?

- It has the same direction as velocity
- It increases the height energy
- It always cancels weight
- It is perpendicular to displacement and does no work

## Explanation

The path remains tangent to the ramp during contact. The perpendicular normal transfers no mechanical energy in this model.
