On a stationary smooth ramp a cart experiences weight and normal force. Why does the normal force leave total mechanical energy unchanged?

- It has the same direction as velocity
- It increases the height energy
- It always cancels weight
- It is perpendicular to displacement and does no work

## Explanation

The path remains tangent to the ramp during contact. The perpendicular normal transfers no mechanical energy in this model. The normal force can change the direction of motion. Its dot product with a small tangential displacement is zero, so it does not change kinetic energy through its own work.
