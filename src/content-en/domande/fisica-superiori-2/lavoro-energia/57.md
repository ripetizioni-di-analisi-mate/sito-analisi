Two equal forces act over the same displacement. The first is parallel in the direction of travel, the second at $60^\circ$. How do their works compare?

- They are equal
- The second is twice the first
- The second is negative
- The first is twice the second

## Explanation

The first projection is its full magnitude. The second projection is half because cosine is one half. Both forces act over the same distance, which cancels in the ratio. The first transfers twice the energy even though the two force vectors have the same magnitude.
