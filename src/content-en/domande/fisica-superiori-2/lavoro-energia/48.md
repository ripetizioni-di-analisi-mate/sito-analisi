An engine delivers $600\,\mathrm{J}$ in $2\,\mathrm{s}$ and then $400\,\mathrm{J}$ over $6\,\mathrm{s}$. What is average power over the full interval?

- $125\,\mathrm{W}$
- $100\,\mathrm{W}$
- $183.33\,\mathrm{W}$
- $250\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

Add the two work values and their durations. Taking the arithmetic mean of the phase powers would give both phases equal time weighting.

$$
\mathcal P_m=\frac{600+400}{2+6}\,\mathrm{W}=125\,\mathrm{W}.
$$
