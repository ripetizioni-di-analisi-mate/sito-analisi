For a linear spring compare $x=+a$ and $x=-a$ with $a>0$. How do the energies compare?

- Compression energy is negative
- They are equal and positive
- Extension energy is negative
- One is zero

## Explanation

The displacement square is $a^2$ in both cases. For a spring with $k>0$, both energies are positive. The spring forces in the two configurations are opposite, while the energies are equal. Both configurations require the same work to load the spring from its natural length.
