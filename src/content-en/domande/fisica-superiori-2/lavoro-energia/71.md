What area explains the factor of one half in $U_e$ during slow loading of an ideal spring from zero?

- A rectangle at the final force height
- A triangle below the increasing force
- A region under the mass curve
- The area of an energy–time graph

## Explanation

The loading force starts at zero and grows linearly. The triangular area is half the product of base and final height. The base is the deformation and the height is the final force. The factor comes from the geometry because the average force over the interval is half the final force.
