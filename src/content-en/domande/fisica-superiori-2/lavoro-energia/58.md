If a displacement is reversed while the force stays unchanged, what happens to its non-zero work?

- It doubles
- It reverses sign at the same magnitude
- It stays the same
- It vanishes

## Explanation

Projection in the new direction changes sign. Displacement magnitude stays the same, so work reverses sign. For example, work of $+20\,\mathrm J$ becomes $-20\,\mathrm J$. On an outward and return trip over the same segment, the two contributions from the force cancel.
