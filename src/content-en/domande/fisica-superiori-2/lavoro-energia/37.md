A $k=200\,\mathrm{N/m}$ spring relaxes from $0.2\,\mathrm{m}$ of compression to its natural length. What work does the spring force do?

- $4\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $8\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The spring’s energy falls from 4 J to zero. Spring work is the opposite of the change in spring energy. The force pushes the end along its motion. Its projection starts at $40\,\mathrm N$ and falls to zero, so the area of the triangle gives $4\,\mathrm J$.

$$
W_e=U_{e,i}-U_{e,f}=\frac12\cdot200(0.2)^2\,\mathrm{J}=4\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
