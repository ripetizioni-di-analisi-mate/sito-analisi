A $7\,\mathrm{kg}$ box has gravitational potential energy $280\,\mathrm J$ at A. Point B is $2\,\mathrm m$ higher. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and the same reference, what is $U_g$ at B?

- $140\,\mathrm J$
- $280\,\mathrm J$
- $560\,\mathrm J$
- $420\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The positive change in height gives an increase of $140\,\mathrm J$. Add this change to the potential energy at A. The sum is $420\,\mathrm J$. Changing the reference level between A and B would make the difference of the two energies cease to represent the change in energy.

$$
U_{g,B}=(280+7\cdot10\cdot2)\,\mathrm J=420\,\mathrm J.
$$
