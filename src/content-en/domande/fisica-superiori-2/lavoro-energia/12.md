A body of mass $7\,\mathrm{kg}$ is at signed height $6\,\mathrm{m}$ relative to the zero level. What is its gravitational potential energy with $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$?

- $-420\,\mathrm{J}$
- $210\,\mathrm{J}$
- $840\,\mathrm{J}$
- $420\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Mass times $g$ gives the weight magnitude $70\,\mathrm{N}$. Multiply again by the signed height $6\,\mathrm{m}$. The result $420\,\mathrm{J}$ is relative to the chosen zero level. A height below it gives a negative value without implying negative kinetic energy. Shifting the reference adds the same constant to each point’s energy, while the difference between two positions is unchanged.

$$
\begin{aligned}
U_g &= mgh=7\cdot 10\cdot(6)\,\mathrm{J}=420\,\mathrm{J}
\end{aligned}
$$
