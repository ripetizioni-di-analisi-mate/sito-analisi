The graph shows a cart's kinetic energy. From A to B it rises from $10$ to $25\,\mathrm J$ in $2\,\mathrm s$. From B to C it stays constant for $2\,\mathrm s$. From C to D it falls to $9\,\mathrm J$ in $1\,\mathrm s$. In which section is the magnitude of the net average power greatest?

::grafico{linee="0,10 2,25 4,25 5,9" punti="0,10,pieno,A;2,25,pieno,B;4,25,pieno,C;5,9,pieno,D" xmin=-0.3 xmax=5.75 ymin=0 ymax=30 nomi="t (s),K (J)" titolo="The cart’s kinetic energy across the three sections."}

- A–B
- B–C
- The magnitude is the same in all three sections
- C–D

## Explanation

Divide the change in kinetic energy by the duration to find the net average power. The values are $7.5\,\mathrm W$, zero and $-16\,\mathrm W$. Energy is lost in C–D, but the magnitude is greater because a larger change takes place in less time.

$$
\mathcal P_{CD}=\frac{9-25}{1}\,\mathrm W=-16\,\mathrm W.
$$
