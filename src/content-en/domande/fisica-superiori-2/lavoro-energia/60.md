What information is needed to find work from a graph of $F_\parallel(x)$?

- The initial slope alone
- The final ordinate alone
- The arithmetic mean of the two $x$ coordinates
- The signed area over the displacement interval

## Explanation

Each interval contributes force times length. Adding the contributions measures the signed area.
