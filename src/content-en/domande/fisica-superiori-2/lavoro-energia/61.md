At constant mass speed triples. How does kinetic energy change?

- It becomes nine times as large
- It triples
- It doubles
- It stays the same

## Explanation

Speed is squared. The increase factor therefore enters the multiplication twice. With mass unchanged, $\dfrac12m(3v)^2=9K$. The increase concerns the energy needed to bring the object from rest to the new speed, which becomes nine times as large.
