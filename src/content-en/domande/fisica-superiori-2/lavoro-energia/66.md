What stays the same when the zero height changes without moving the object?

- Every numerical value of $U_g$
- Each point's coordinate measured from zero
- Work done by weight between two fixed positions
- Always potential energy at every point

## Explanation

The same constant is added to both energies. Their difference is unchanged, so the work of weight is unchanged.
