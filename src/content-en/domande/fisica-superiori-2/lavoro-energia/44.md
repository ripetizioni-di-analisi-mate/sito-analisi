A machine does work at average power $300\,\mathrm{W}$ for $4\,\mathrm{s}$. How much energy does it transfer?

- $75\,\mathrm{J}$
- $300\,\mathrm{J}$
- $600\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1200\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Three hundred watts is an average of three hundred joules per second. Multiply this rate by the duration. The work is $300\,\mathrm W\cdot4\,\mathrm s=1200\,\mathrm J$. The average is sufficient even if the machine changes power during the interval. It is based on the total work over that interval.

$$
E=300\cdot4\,\mathrm{J}=1200\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
