Friction is the only force doing work on a $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ object slowing from $6$ to $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ over $8\,\mathrm{m}$. What is its constant magnitude?

- $4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $16\,\mathrm{N}$
- $8\,\mathrm{N}$
- $64\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Kinetic energy decreases by 64 J. Opposing friction does that magnitude of work over 8 m.

$$
f=\frac{\frac12\cdot4(6^2-2^2)}{8}\,\mathrm{N}=8\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
