A crane lifts $20\,\mathrm{kg}$ vertically at constant speed $0.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the useful lifting power?

- $10\,\mathrm{W}$
- $50\,\mathrm{W}$
- $100\,\mathrm{W}$
- $400\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

At constant velocity the lift force balances weight of 200 N. In one second the load rises half a metre. The product $200\cdot0.5\,\mathrm W=100\,\mathrm W$ is the gravitational energy gained each second. Gravity supplies opposite power, so the net power on the load is zero.

$$
\mathcal P=mgv=20\cdot10\cdot0.5\,\mathrm{W}=100\,\mathrm{W}.
$$
