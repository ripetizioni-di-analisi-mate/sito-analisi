A horizontal $40\,\mathrm{N}$ pull maintains a crate at $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the direction of the force. What is the pull's power?

- $40\,\mathrm{W}$
- $120\,\mathrm{W}$
- $13.333\,\mathrm{W}$
- $0\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

Each second displacement is 3 m along the force. Joules transferred in one second are the product of these values. This gives $40\cdot3\,\mathrm W=120\,\mathrm W$. The calculated power is that of the towing force. At constant speed, the resultant may have zero power because other forces cancel it.

$$
\mathcal P=Fv=40\cdot3\,\mathrm{W}=120\,\mathrm{W}.
$$
