Net work on an object of fixed mass is zero. What follows from the work–kinetic energy theorem?

- Initial and final speeds are equal
- Speed is zero at every instant
- Velocity direction cannot change
- Every individual force does zero work

## Explanation

The change in $K$ is zero. The theorem compares the endpoints, and one speed is compatible with different directions. Initial and final velocities with the same magnitude can even point in opposite directions. In between, the object may have slowed down and then regained its initial speed.
