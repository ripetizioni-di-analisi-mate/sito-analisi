During one cycle a device receives $100\,\mathrm J$ from a socket and supplies $120\,\mathrm J$ of useful work. Which observation makes this balance consistent with conservation of energy?

- An internal battery gains $20\,\mathrm J$.
- An internal battery loses $30\,\mathrm J$ and $10\,\mathrm J$ is dissipated.
- The device dissipates $20\,\mathrm J$ as the only other change.
- All internal stores return to their initial values and there are no other energy inputs.

## Explanation

The socket and battery together supply $100+30=130\,\mathrm J$. This accounts for $120\,\mathrm J$ of useful work and $10\,\mathrm J$ dissipated. Comparing the output with the socket alone leaves out the battery.
