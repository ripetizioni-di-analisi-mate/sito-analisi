A machine draws $500\,\mathrm{W}$ and provides useful power $350\,\mathrm{W}$ during the same interval. What is its efficiency as a number from zero to one?

- $0.3$
- $0.7$
- $0.5$
- $0.71429$

## Explanation

Both average powers refer to the same duration. Divide useful power by input power to get the fraction directly.

$$
\eta=\frac{350}{500}=0.7.
$$
