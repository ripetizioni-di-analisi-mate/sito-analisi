A sled starts from rest and descends a ramp. Friction removes $36\%$ of the gravitational potential energy lost during the descent. What fraction of the frictionless final speed does the sled retain?

- $0.80$
- $0.64$
- $0.36$
- $1.25$

## Explanation

This leaves $64\%$ of the energy that would all become kinetic on the frictionless ramp. The energy ratio is therefore $0.64$. For equal mass, take the square root to obtain the speed ratio. The speed is reduced by $20\%$.

$$
\frac{v}{v_0}=\sqrt{0.64}=0.80.
$$
