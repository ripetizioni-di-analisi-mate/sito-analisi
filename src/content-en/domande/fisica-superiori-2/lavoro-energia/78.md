Two descents of equal height start from rest. On one friction does negative work and on the other friction is absent. Where is final speed greater if both reach the bottom?

- On the descent without friction
- On the descent with friction
- It is always equal
- It depends only on the length of the previous climb

## Explanation

Weight supplies the same energy in both cases. Friction removes some final kinetic energy on the first route.
