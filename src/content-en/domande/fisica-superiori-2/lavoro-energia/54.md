A lift descends through $2\,\mathrm m$ at constant speed. A $5\,\mathrm{kg}$ suitcase remains on its floor. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, how much work does the normal force from the floor do on the suitcase?

- $-100\,\mathrm J$
- $+100\,\mathrm J$
- $0\,\mathrm J$
- $-50\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Constant speed means the normal force has the same magnitude as the weight. The normal force points up while the displacement points down. Its work is therefore $-100\,\mathrm J$. Gravity does the opposite work, so the net work is zero, consistent with constant speed.

$$
W_N=-5\cdot10\cdot2\,\mathrm J=-100\,\mathrm J.
$$
