An object moves horizontally and its weight is vertical. What work does weight do?

- Zero
- The positive product of weight and distance
- The negative product of weight and distance
- The increase in kinetic energy in every case

## Explanation

Force and displacement are perpendicular. The cosine of a right angle is zero, so the product is zero.
