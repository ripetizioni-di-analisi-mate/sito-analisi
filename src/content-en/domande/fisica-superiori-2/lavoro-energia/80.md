What is the difference between joules and watts in an engine balance?

- Both measure energy alone
- A joule measures energy and a watt energy per unit time
- A joule measures power and a watt energy
- A watt measures energy per unit length

## Explanation

Divide the joules of work by seconds to obtain watts. The measurements answer how much energy is transferred and how quickly. A constant power of $40\,\mathrm W$ over $3\,\mathrm s$ transfers $120\,\mathrm J$. Time connects the two quantities. Over a longer interval, the same power transfers more energy.
