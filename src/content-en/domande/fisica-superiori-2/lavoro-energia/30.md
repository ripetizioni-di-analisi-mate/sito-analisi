A $5\,\mathrm{kg}$ object rises from $h=1$ to $h=6\,\mathrm{m}$ with $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the work done by weight?

- $250\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-250\,\mathrm{J}$
- $300\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-300\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Height increases by five metres, increasing potential energy. Work by weight is the opposite of that increase. The weight has magnitude $50\,\mathrm N$ and points downwards. The vertical displacement points upwards, so $W_g=-50\cdot5\,\mathrm J=-250\,\mathrm J$.

$$
W_g=-mg(h_f-h_i)=-5\cdot10(6-1)\,\mathrm{J}=-250\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
