A spring of $k=100\,\mathrm{N/m}$ moves slowly from $x=0.1$ to $x=0.3\,\mathrm{m}$. How much does its energy increase?

- $2\,\mathrm{J}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{J}$
- $5\,\mathrm{J}$
- $4\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The endpoint energies are 0.5 J and 4.5 J. Their difference also equals the trapezoid area under the loading force over that interval.

$$
\Delta U_e=\frac12\cdot100(0.3^2-0.1^2)\,\mathrm{J}=4\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
