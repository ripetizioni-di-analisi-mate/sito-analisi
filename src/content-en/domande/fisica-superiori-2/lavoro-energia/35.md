A spring stores $0.8\,\mathrm{J}$ when extended by $0.1\,\mathrm{m}$. What is its spring constant?

- $16\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $80\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $160\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $320\,\mathrm{N/m}$

## Explanation

The square of 0.1 m is 0.01 m². Twice the energy divided by it gives stiffness in newtons per metre. This gives $k=\dfrac{1.6}{0.01}\,\mathrm{N/m}=160\,\mathrm{N/m}$. With that constant, the force at the given deformation is $16\,\mathrm N$ and the corresponding triangular area is indeed $0.8\,\mathrm J$.

$$
k=\frac{2\cdot0.8}{0.1^2}\,\mathrm{N/m}=160\,\mathrm{N/m}.
$$
