A $k=200\,\mathrm{N/m}$ spring compressed by $0.2\,\mathrm{m}$ launches a $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ object on a smooth surface. What is its speed at the spring's natural length if it starts at rest?

- $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The object starts at rest. The initial 4 J of elastic energy becomes kinetic energy. Elastic potential energy reaches zero at natural length. For mass $2\,\mathrm{kg}$, this gives $v^2=4$ in SI units, hence $v=2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The work of the release can be checked from the area under the force curve.

$$
v=\sqrt{\frac{200(0.2)^2}{2}}\,\mathrm{m/s}=2\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
