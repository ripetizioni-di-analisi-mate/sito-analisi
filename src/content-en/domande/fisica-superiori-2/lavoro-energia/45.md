At average useful power $250\,\mathrm{W}$, how long is needed for $1000\,\mathrm{J}$ of useful work?

- $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{s}$
- $25\,\mathrm{s}$
- $250\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Each second provides 250 J on average. Divide the total required by 250.

$$
\Delta t=\frac{1000}{250}\,\mathrm{s}=4\,\mathrm{s}.
$$
