A machine takes in $1800\,\mathrm{J}$ and supplies $1224\,\mathrm{J}$ of useful energy. What is its efficiency as a percentage?

- $32\,\mathrm{\%}$
- $34\,\mathrm{\%}$
- $147.06\,\mathrm{\%}$
- $68\,\mathrm{\%}$

## Explanation

Put useful energy in the numerator and input energy for the same process in the denominator. The quotient is a dimensionless fraction. Multiply by one hundred to express it as a percentage. The difference between input and useful output is $576\,\mathrm{J}$. As a check, the useful and non-useful percentages of the same input add to one hundred percent.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\eta &= \frac{1224}{1800} = 0.68 \\[6pt]
\eta_\% &= 68\,\%
\end{aligned}
$$
