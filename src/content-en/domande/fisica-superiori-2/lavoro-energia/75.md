An object passes two distinct points of a smooth track at the same height, with no motor. How do the speeds compare?

- They always depend on the local track shape
- The second is always greater
- One must be zero
- They are equal

## Explanation

The height energy is the same. Conservation of $K+U_g$ therefore gives equal kinetic energy for the same mass.
