A $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ object is $8\,\mathrm{m}$ above the floor. A new zero level is $10\,\mathrm{m}$ above the floor. What is $U_g$ relative to the new level with $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$?

- $60\,\mathrm{J}$
- $240\,\mathrm{J}$
- $300\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-60\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The new signed height is negative, −2 m. The same weight multiplies this new height. The calculation is $30\cdot(8-10)\,\mathrm J=-60\,\mathrm J$. A negative value means that the object lies below the reference level chosen for gravitational energy.

$$
U_g=3\cdot10(8-10)\,\mathrm{J}=-60\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
