A machine works in two stages. In the first it draws $300\,\mathrm J$ at $90\%$ efficiency. In the second it draws $700\,\mathrm J$ at $50\%$ efficiency. What is the efficiency over the whole cycle?

- $70\%$
- $62\%$
- $78\%$
- $45\%$

## Explanation

The first stage supplies $270\,\mathrm J$ of useful energy and the second $350\,\mathrm J$. Add these outputs and compare them with the total input of $1000\,\mathrm J$. The efficiency is closer to $50\%$ because the second stage draws more of the energy.

$$
\eta=\frac{0.90\cdot300+0.50\cdot700}{300+700}=0.62.
$$
