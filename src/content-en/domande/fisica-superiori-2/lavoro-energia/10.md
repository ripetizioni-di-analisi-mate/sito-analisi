A cart enters a horizontal track with kinetic energy $100\,\mathrm J$. A motor does $160\,\mathrm J$ of work on the cart and a brake then does $-36\,\mathrm J$. What is the final kinetic energy if the other forces do no work?

- $124\,\mathrm J$
- $224\,\mathrm J$
- $260\,\mathrm J$
- $296\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The first transfer brings the kinetic energy to $260\,\mathrm J$. This is the energy at the start of braking. The negative work removes $36\,\mathrm J$, leaving $224\,\mathrm J$. The value $124\,\mathrm J$ is the overall change from entry, which is a separate quantity from the final energy. Both amounts of work act on the same cart in successive phases.

$$
K_f=(100+160-36)\,\mathrm J=224\,\mathrm J.
$$
