An object completes a closed path near Earth's surface. How much work does weight do in the constant-$g$ model?

- Always positive
- Zero
- Always negative
- Weight times total distance

## Explanation

Final height equals initial height. Gravitational potential-energy change is zero, so work is zero as well. Gravity can do positive work downwards and negative work upwards. On returning to the starting height, the gravitational contributions cancel exactly.
