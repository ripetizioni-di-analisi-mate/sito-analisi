An object completes a closed path near Earth's surface. How much work does weight do in the constant-$g$ model?

- Always positive
- Zero
- Always negative
- Weight times total distance

## Explanation

Final height equals initial height. Gravitational potential-energy change is zero, so work is zero as well.
