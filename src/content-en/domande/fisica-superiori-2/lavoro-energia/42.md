A $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ cart starts from rest and drops $5\,\mathrm{m}$. Friction does $-64\,\mathrm{J}$. What is its final speed, with $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$?

- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The loss of height energy supplies 100 J. Friction removes 64 from mechanical energy, leaving 36 J of final kinetic energy.

$$
v_f=\sqrt{\frac{2(100-64)}{2}}\,\mathrm{m/s}=6\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
