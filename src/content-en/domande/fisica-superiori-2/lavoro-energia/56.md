A person holds an object at rest with an upward force. How much mechanical work does that force do on the object?

- Positive because the force is non-zero
- Zero, because there is no displacement
- Negative because weight acts down
- Equal to the object's weight

## Explanation

Work on the object requires displacement. The energy used by the person holding it concerns a different system.
