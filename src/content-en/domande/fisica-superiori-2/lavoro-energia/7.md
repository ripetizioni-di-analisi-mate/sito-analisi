An engine does $1400\,\mathrm{J}$ of useful work in $8\,\mathrm{s}$. What is its average useful power?

- $87.5\,\mathrm{W}$
- $350\,\mathrm{W}$
- $175\,\mathrm{W}$
- $525\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

The total useful work over the interval is known, so find its rate per unit time. Divide $1400\,\mathrm{J}$ by $8\,\mathrm{s}$. The resulting unit is joules per second, or watts. This average gives the constant power that would deliver the same useful work in the same time. Multiplying it by the duration recovers the given work.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal P_m &= \frac{W}{\Delta t} = \frac{1400}{8}\,\mathrm{W} \\[6pt]
&= 175\,\mathrm{W}
\end{aligned}
$$
