Displacement magnitude doubles in the same spring's linear range. How does elastic energy change?

- It doubles
- It stays the same
- It halves
- It quadruples

## Explanation

Stiffness stays fixed and displacement is squared. The factor of two therefore gives a factor of four. If one deformation stores $2\,\mathrm J$, doubling its magnitude gives $8\,\mathrm J$. Both deformations need to be measured from the natural length for the comparison.
