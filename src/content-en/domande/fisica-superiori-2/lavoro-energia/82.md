A force is perpendicular to the object's velocity at one instant. What is that force's power at that instant?

- Zero
- The positive product of the magnitudes
- The negative product of the magnitudes
- Always equal to kinetic energy

## Explanation

At that instant there is no force component along velocity. Parallel force times speed is zero. The result concerns that force at that instant. A perpendicular force can change the velocity direction, but its power is zero because the parallel projection vanishes.
