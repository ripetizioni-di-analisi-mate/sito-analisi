Two devices are connected in series, and all the useful output from the first is the input to the second. The first usefully transfers $60\%$ of its input and the second $70\%$. What is the overall efficiency?

- $42\%$
- $65\%$
- $30\%$
- $70\%$

## Explanation

The second device receives the useful energy from the first. Of the initial $100\,\mathrm J$, the second receives $60\,\mathrm J$ and supplies $42\,\mathrm J$ of useful output. Each efficiency applies to the input of its own stage. The loss in the second stage is $18\,\mathrm J$ of the original $100\,\mathrm J$.

$$
\eta=0.60\cdot0.70=0.42.
$$
