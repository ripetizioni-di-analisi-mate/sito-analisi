What does an efficiency of $70\%$ mean for a machine?

- Seventy percent of energy input is useful output
- It uses 70 joules whatever the input
- Seventy percent of output is loss
- The engine is off for 70 percent of the time

## Explanation

The denominator is input energy. For every hundred joules in, seventy are useful.
