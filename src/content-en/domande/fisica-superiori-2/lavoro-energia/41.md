A $1\,\mathrm{kg}$ object drops $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from rest and compresses a $k=200\,\mathrm{N/m}$ spring on the level at the bottom. What is maximum compression with $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and negligible friction throughout? Choose the closest value.

- $0.63246\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.44721\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Departure and maximum compression each have zero velocity. All the lost height energy is stored in the spring, so it equals 40 J.

$$
x=\sqrt{\frac{2\cdot1\cdot10\cdot4}{200}}\,\mathrm{m}=\sqrt{\frac25}\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
