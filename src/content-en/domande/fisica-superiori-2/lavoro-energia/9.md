A constant force of $35\,\mathrm{N}$ makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with a straight-line displacement of $7\,\mathrm{m}$. Find its work.

- $122.5\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-122.5\,\mathrm{J}$
- $245\,\mathrm{J}$
- $61.25\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Work uses the component of force parallel to displacement. The angle is measured between these vectors, so use cosine. The signed component is $17.5\,\mathrm{N}$. Multiplying by $7\,\mathrm{m}$ gives $122.5\,\mathrm{J}$. The perpendicular component has no displacement in its direction and contributes zero. The sign of the result agrees with the projection's direction relative to the motion.

$$
\begin{aligned}
F_\parallel &= 35\cos60^\circ\,\mathrm{N}\simeq 17.5\,\mathrm{N} \\[6pt]
W &= F_\parallel s \simeq 122.5\,\mathrm{J}
\end{aligned}
$$
