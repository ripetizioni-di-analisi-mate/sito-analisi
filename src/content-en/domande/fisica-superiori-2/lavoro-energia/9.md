A cart moves along a track. The component of the force along the motion is $35\,\mathrm N$ over $4\,\mathrm m$ and $-7\,\mathrm N$ over the next $2.5\,\mathrm m$. How much work does this force do over both sections?

::grafico{linee="0,35 4,35;4,-7 6.5,-7" area="35;0;0;4|0;-7;4;6.5" segmenti="4,0,4,35;4,0,4,-7;6.5,0,6.5,-7" etichette="6.07,-11.4,6.5 m" xmin=-0.3 xmax=7.12 ymin=-13.5 ymax=42 nomi="x (m),F (N)" titolo="The height in each section is the force component along the motion."}

- $122.5\,\mathrm J$
- $157.5\,\mathrm J$
- $140\,\mathrm J$
- $-17.5\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Read the component as the height of each rectangle. The first section gives $140\,\mathrm J$. The second removes $17.5\,\mathrm J$ because the force points opposite to the motion. The signed sum is $122.5\,\mathrm J$. The two distances contribute separately. Subtract the second rectangle because it lies below the axis.

$$
W=[35\cdot4+(-7)\cdot2.5]\,\mathrm J=122.5\,\mathrm J.
$$
