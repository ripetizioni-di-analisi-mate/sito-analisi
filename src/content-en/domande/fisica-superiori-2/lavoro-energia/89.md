Why can $(v_f-v_i)^2$ not represent the change in squared speed while braking?

- It always equals $v_f^2-v_i^2$
- It is non-negative while $v_f^2-v_i^2$ can be negative
- Its units differ from $v_f^2-v_i^2$
- It is always zero during braking

## Explanation

Net work needs the difference between energies of two states. While braking the final energy is below the initial energy.
