A $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ object goes from $+4$ to $-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ along one axis. What is its net work between the two states?

- $24\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-24\,\mathrm{J}$
- $48\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The velocities are opposite but have equal squares. Their kinetic energies agree and the change is zero. The final direction can change even when the net work is zero. During a reversal, the kinetic energy can first decrease and then return to its initial value.

$$
W_\mathrm{tot}=\frac12\cdot3\bigl[(-4)^2-4^2\bigr]\,\mathrm{J}=0.
$$
