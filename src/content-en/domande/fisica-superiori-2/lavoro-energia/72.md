If two springs reach the same displacement magnitude, which stores more energy?

- The one with smaller spring constant
- They always store equal energy
- It depends only on the final speed of the object pushed
- The one with greater spring constant

## Explanation

At fixed displacement, energy is proportional to $k$. A stiffer spring needs more force throughout loading.
