Two ideal springs in the linear regime reach the same deformation magnitude. Which stores more energy?

- The one with smaller spring constant
- They always store equal energy
- It depends only on the final speed of the object pushed
- The one with greater spring constant

## Explanation

At fixed displacement, energy is proportional to $k$. A stiffer spring needs more force throughout loading. If $k$ doubles, the triangular area under the loading force doubles as well. The base stays the same because both springs end at the same deformation.
