An engine takes in energy at $1.2\,\mathrm{kW}$ and runs for $5\,\mathrm{s}$. What is its input in joules?

- $6\,\mathrm{J}$
- $240\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1200\,\mathrm{J}$
- $6000\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

A kilowatt is one thousand watts. The rate is therefore 1200 J per second, which is multiplied by duration. The product $1200\,\mathrm W\cdot5\,\mathrm s$ gives $6000\,\mathrm J$. The seconds cancel the time in the rate unit. Omitting the conversion would make the result a thousand times too small.

$$
E=1.2\cdot1000\cdot5\,\mathrm{J}=6000\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
