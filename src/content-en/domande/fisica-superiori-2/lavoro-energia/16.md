A $30\,\mathrm{N}$ force does $45\,\mathrm{J}$ over $3\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the cosine of the angle between force and displacement?

- $2$
- $0.33333$
- $-0.5$
- $0.5$

## Explanation

The product of the magnitudes is 90 J. The work divided by that product gives the relative projection, one half. The ratio is $\dfrac{45}{90}=0.50$. A force along the displacement would give the maximum work. Here the value is half the maximum because the projection is half its possible maximum.

$$
\cos\theta=\frac{45}{30\cdot3}=\frac12.
$$
