An object goes down a ramp and loses $120\,\mathrm{J}$ of potential energy. It gains $75\,\mathrm{J}$ of kinetic energy. What is the work of friction if it is the only other force doing work?

- $45\,\mathrm{J}$
- $195\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-45\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-195\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Mechanical change adds the kinetic gain and the potential loss. Their negative net difference equals friction work.

$$
W_f=\Delta E_m=75-120=-45\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
