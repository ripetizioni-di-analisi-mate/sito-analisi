A rope pulls $25\,\mathrm{N}$ along the displacement and friction is $10\,\mathrm{N}$ oppositely. What is their total work over $6\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $60\,\mathrm{J}$
- $90\,\mathrm{J}$
- $150\,\mathrm{J}$
- $210\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The rope does 150 J and friction −60 J. Add both with their signs, as for the projections they come from. This gives $150-60=90\,\mathrm J$. The same result follows by multiplying the horizontal resultant of $15\,\mathrm N$ by the $6\,\mathrm m$ travelled.

$$
W_\mathrm{tot}=(25-10)\cdot6\,\mathrm{J}=90\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
