An object moves $5\,\mathrm{m}$ while a constant $8\,\mathrm{N}$ force points oppositely. What is its work?

- $40\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-40\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-13\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The angle between the vectors is $180^\circ$, so the projection along the motion is $-8\,\mathrm{N}$. Multiplying by distance retains the negative sign.

$$
W=8\cdot5\cos180^\circ\,\mathrm{J}=-40\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
