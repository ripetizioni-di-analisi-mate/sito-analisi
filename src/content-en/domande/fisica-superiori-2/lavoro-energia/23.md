A $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass has kinetic energy $54\,\mathrm{J}$. What is its speed?

- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $36\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Double the energy and divide by mass to get speed squared. Take the positive square root.

$$
v=\sqrt{\frac{2\cdot54}{3}}\,\mathrm{m/s}=6\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
