A machine takes in $900\,\mathrm{J}$ at $60\%$ efficiency. How much useful energy does it deliver?

- $540\,\mathrm{J}$
- $360\,\mathrm{J}$
- $600\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1500\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Sixty percent corresponds to factor 0.60. Useful output is that factor times the input.

$$
E_u=0.6\cdot900\,\mathrm{J}=540\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
