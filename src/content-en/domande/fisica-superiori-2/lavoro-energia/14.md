A spring is attached to a block on a horizontal track and initially stores $4\,\mathrm J$ of elastic energy. The block starts and finishes at rest. Over the displacement, a hand does $7\,\mathrm J$ of work and friction does $-2\,\mathrm J$. What is the final elastic energy?

- $5\,\mathrm J$
- $9\,\mathrm J$
- $11\,\mathrm J$
- $13\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The balance includes the spring's energy. The work done by the hand and friction increases the mechanical energy by $5\,\mathrm J$. The kinetic energy is zero at both endpoints, so the entire increase is in elastic energy. Adding it to the initial $4\,\mathrm J$ gives $9\,\mathrm J$. Gravity and the normal force do no work over the horizontal displacement.

$$
U_{e,f}=(4+7-2)\,\mathrm J=9\,\mathrm J.
$$
