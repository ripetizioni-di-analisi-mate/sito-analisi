A particle starts from rest. A calculation would give negative net work before a final state of positive mass. What does that reveal?

- The particle has negative kinetic energy
- Its mass becomes zero
- The data or signs do not fit that evolution
- The particle can have observable imaginary speed

## Explanation

From rest $K_i=0$. Negative net work would imply $K_f<0$, impossible for positive mass and real speed. Such a balance can mean that a later phase is unreachable. An object starting from rest can proceed only if the net work allows a nonnegative kinetic energy.
