During mixing, part of the heat leaves the container for the surroundings. How does the balance for the samples alone change?

- Their sum stays zero simply because energy is lost.
- The specific heat capacity of water becomes zero.
- The sum of their contributions is not necessarily zero.
- The final temperature is always an arithmetic mean.

## Explanation

The samples no longer form an isolated system. Energy transferred outward enters the balance with its sign.
