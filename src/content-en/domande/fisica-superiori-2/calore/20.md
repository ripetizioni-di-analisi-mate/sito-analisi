A $0.8\,\mathrm{kg}$ block absorbs $3.2\,\mathrm{kJ}$ and warms by $5\,\mathrm K$. What specific heat capacity does this give?

- $640\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$
- $4000\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$
- $80\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$
- $800\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$

## Explanation

The model applies within a single phase. The sample’s capacity is the product $mc$, which links the temperature interval to signed heat.

$$
c=\frac{3200}{0.8\cdot5}\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}=800\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}.
$$
