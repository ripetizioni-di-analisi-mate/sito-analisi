A piece of copper is cooled in water in an insulated vessel. Why is the mean of the two masses insufficient?

- The masses must be in kelvins.
- Final temperature uses heat capacities, which also depend on specific heat capacities.
- Copper will absorb heat if it starts hotter.
- Heat capacity is equal for all materials.

## Explanation

Comparing the responses of copper and water requires the products $m_1c_1$ and $m_2c_2$. Masses alone work when the specific heat capacities are equal.
