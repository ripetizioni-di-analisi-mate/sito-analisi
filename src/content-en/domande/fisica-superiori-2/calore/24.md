In a graph of $\Delta T$ against $Q$ the slope is $0.004\,\mathrm{K/J}$. What is $C$?

- $400\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $0.004\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $25\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $250\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

This graph gives kelvins per joule, the temperature rise produced by one joule. Heat capacity gives the energy needed for a one-kelvin rise. It is therefore the reciprocal of the slope. The reciprocal of $0.004$ is 250, and the units become joules per kelvin.

$$
C=\frac{1}{0.004}\,\mathrm{J/K}=250\,\mathrm{J/K}.
$$
