In a graph of $\Delta T$ against $Q$ the slope is $0.004\,\mathrm{K/J}$. What is $C$?

- $400\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $0.004\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $25\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $250\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

With $\Delta T$ on the vertical axis, $\Delta T=Q/C$. The slope is the reciprocal of heat capacity.

$$
C=\frac{1}{0.004}\,\mathrm{J/K}=250\,\mathrm{J/K}.
$$
