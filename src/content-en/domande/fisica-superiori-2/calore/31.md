An object at $70\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ cools by $30\,\mathrm K$ while the other warms by $10\,\mathrm K$ on reaching equilibrium. With insulation, what is the ratio of the cool object’s heat capacity to the warm object’s?

- $0.33333333$
- $1$
- $3$
- $9$

## Explanation

For an insulated system exchanging only heat internally, the signed contributions add to zero. Use positive capacities and signed changes.

$$
\frac{C_{\mathrm c}}{C_{\mathrm h}}=\frac{30}{10}=3.
$$
