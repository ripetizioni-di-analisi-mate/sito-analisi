A sample starts at $20\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ and has a constant heat capacity $C=750\,\mathrm{J/K}$. If it absorbs $15\,\mathrm{kJ}$, what temperature does it reach within the same phase?

- $40\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $20\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $35\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $-20\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$

## Explanation

The sample stays in the same phase. Dividing 15,000 joules by its heat capacity gives a rise of 20 kelvins. A difference of 20 kelvins corresponds to a change of 20 degrees Celsius. Add this change to the initial reading of 20 degrees Celsius.

$$
T_f=20\,{}^\circ\mathrm C+\frac{15000}{750}\,\mathrm K=40\,{}^\circ\mathrm C.
$$
