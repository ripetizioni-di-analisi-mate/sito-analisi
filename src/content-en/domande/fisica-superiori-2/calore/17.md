At $20\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ a sample has $C=750\,\mathrm{J/K}$. If it absorbs $15\,\mathrm{kJ}$, what temperature does it reach within the same phase?

- $40\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $20\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $35\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $-20\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$

## Explanation

The model applies within a single phase. The sample’s capacity is the product $mc$, which links the temperature interval to signed heat.

$$
T_f=20\,{}^\circ\mathrm C+\frac{15000}{750}\,\mathrm K=40\,{}^\circ\mathrm C.
$$
