A mass of water cools by $8\,\mathrm K$ and then warms by $3\,\mathrm K$. With constant $C=840\,\mathrm{J/K}$, what is the net heat transfer?

- $4200\,\mathrm J$
- $-4200\,\mathrm J$
- $-9240\,\mathrm J$
- $-6720\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The model applies within a single phase. The sample’s capacity is the product $mc$, which links the temperature interval to signed heat.

$$
Q_{\mathrm{net}}=840(-8+3)\,\mathrm J=-4200\,\mathrm J.
$$
