A mass of water cools by $8\,\mathrm K$ and then warms by $3\,\mathrm K$. With constant $C=840\,\mathrm{J/K}$, what is the net heat transfer?

- $4200\,\mathrm J$
- $-4200\,\mathrm J$
- $-9240\,\mathrm J$
- $-6720\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The heat capacity stays the same during both stages, so it can be factored out when adding the heat transfers. The sample loses eight kelvins and gains three, giving a total change of $-5\,\mathrm K$. The negative result means that more heat is released in the first stage than is absorbed in the second.

$$
Q_{\mathrm{net}}=840(-8+3)\,\mathrm J=-4200\,\mathrm J.
$$
