A mass of water of $0.5\,\mathrm{kg}$ warms by $18\,\mathrm K$ without changing phase. Use $c=4200\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$. How much heat does it absorb? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $37800\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $75600\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $18900\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $151200\,\mathrm{J}$.

## Explanation

The water stays in the same phase and $c$ has the stated value. The product $mc$ is the sample heat capacity. Multiply it by $\Delta T$ and obtain a positive result because the sample warms.

$$
\begin{aligned}
Q &= \Delta T c m \\[6pt]
&= \left(18\right) \left(4200\right) \left(0.5\right) \\[6pt]
&\simeq 37800\,\mathrm{J}
\end{aligned}
$$
