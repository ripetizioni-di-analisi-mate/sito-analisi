Two parts reach the same final temperature with no phase changes. What condition on their initial temperatures allows a single $\Delta T$ and the sum of their heat capacities to be used?

- When their initial temperatures also coincide.
- Whenever final temperatures coincide.
- When both capacities are positive.
- When the masses add to one kilogram.

## Explanation

The same final reading from different origins gives different changes. The common factor is available only when the two changes are equal.
