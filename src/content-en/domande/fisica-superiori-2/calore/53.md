A $200\,\mathrm{J/K}$ calorimeter holds water of heat capacity $1800\,\mathrm{J/K}$. The system warms by $6\,\mathrm K$. How much energy does the calorimeter alone gain?

- $12000\,\mathrm J$
- $10800\,\mathrm J$
- $200\,\mathrm J$
- $1200\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The parts undergo the same temperature change, so their products $C_i\Delta T$ add and $\Delta T$ can be factored out.

$$
Q_{\mathrm{cal}}=200\cdot6\,\mathrm J=1200\,\mathrm J.
$$
