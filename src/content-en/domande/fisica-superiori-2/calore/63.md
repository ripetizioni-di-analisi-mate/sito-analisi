If $m$ is in kilograms and $\Delta T$ in kelvins, which unit is assigned to $c$?

- $\mathrm{J/K}$
- $\mathrm{kg\,K/J}$
- $\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$
- $\mathrm W$

## Explanation

In $Q=mc\Delta T$, heat is measured in joules. To find the specific heat capacity, divide by the sample’s mass in kilograms and its temperature rise in kelvin. The unit is therefore $\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$. Joules per kelvin refer to the heat capacity of the whole sample, which already includes its mass.
