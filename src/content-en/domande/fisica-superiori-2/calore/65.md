If the specific heat capacity rises noticeably over the range considered, what will the graph of $Q$ versus $\Delta T$ generally look like when heating a fixed mass?

- The slope rises.
- The slope remains exactly constant.
- The slope falls to zero.
- The graph is always horizontal.

## Explanation

At fixed mass, the local slope measures the extra energy needed for a small temperature increase. It is approximately $mc$ over the interval considered. If $c$ rises, adding one kelvin in the hotter part takes more energy than adding one near the start. The graph therefore becomes steeper.
