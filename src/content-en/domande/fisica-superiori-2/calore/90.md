A layer of very high conductivity is added in series with insulation, at fixed area and thickness. Why might it have little effect on power?

- Its resistance is infinite.
- Its resistance $d/(kA)$ is small compared with the insulation.
- Power never depends on the material.
- Resistances subtract for different materials.

## Explanation

If the added resistance is much smaller than the existing one, their sum changes little. A fixed total temperature drop gives similar power.
