A wall of area $8\,\mathrm{m^2}$ transfers $120\,\mathrm W$. What power is conducted per square metre?

- $960\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $120\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $60\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $15\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$

## Explanation

The total power passes through eight square metres. Power divided by area gives the mean power per unit area, so divide $120\,\mathrm W$ by $8\,\mathrm{m^2}$. If heat passes uniformly through the wall, this value applies to each square metre.

$$
\frac{P}{A}=\frac{120}{8}\,\mathrm{W/m^2}=15\,\mathrm{W/m^2}.
$$
