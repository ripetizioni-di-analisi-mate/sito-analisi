Two objects at the same temperature contain different amounts of matter. What can we conclude about net heat transfer when we touch them together?

- It is always towards the smaller body.
- It measures the difference between their masses.
- Its value equals both internal energies.
- At equilibrium it is zero.

## Explanation

Spontaneous net heat flow requires a temperature difference. The objects can have different internal energies.
