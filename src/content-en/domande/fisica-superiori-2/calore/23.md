A graph of $Q$ versus $\Delta T$ passes through the origin and $(8\,\mathrm K,2400\,\mathrm J)$. What heat capacity does it show?

- $19200\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $0.0033333333\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $300\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $2400\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

The vertical axis gives the energy received and the horizontal axis gives the temperature rise. Since the line passes through the origin, divide 2400 joules by 8 kelvins to find its slope. This slope is the heat capacity of the whole sample and tells us how many joules are required for a one-kelvin rise.

$$
C=\frac{2400}{8}\,\mathrm{J/K}=300\,\mathrm{J/K}.
$$
