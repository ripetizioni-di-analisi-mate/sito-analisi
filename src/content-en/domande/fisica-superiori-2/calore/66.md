A homogeneous block is cut into two equal halves. How do the specific heat capacity and heat capacity of each half change?

- Both halve.
- Specific heat capacity halves and heat capacity is unchanged.
- Both double.
- The specific heat capacity is unchanged and heat capacity halves.

## Explanation

The same substance retains $c$ under the same conditions. The sample capacity is $mc$, so it halves with mass.
