A homogeneous block is cut into two equal halves. How do the specific heat capacity and heat capacity of each half change?

- Both halve.
- Specific heat capacity halves and heat capacity is unchanged.
- Both double.
- The specific heat capacity is unchanged and heat capacity halves.

## Explanation

Both halves are made from the same material and retain the same specific heat capacity under the same conditions. In $C=mc$, only the mass changes. If warming the block by one kelvin takes $600\,\mathrm J$, warming either half by the same amount takes $300\,\mathrm J$.
