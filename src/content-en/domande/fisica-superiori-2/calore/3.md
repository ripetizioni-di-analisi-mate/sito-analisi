An object of mass $1\,\mathrm{kg}$ has specific heat capacity $900\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$. Find its heat capacity. Choose the value closest to the result.

- $1800\,\mathrm{J/K}$.
- $450\,\mathrm{J/K}$.
- $900\,\mathrm{J/K}$.
- $3600\,\mathrm{J/K}$.

## Explanation

Specific heat capacity is given per kilogram. Multiplying by the whole mass gives the heat capacity of the entire object, in joules per kelvin.

$$
\begin{aligned}
C &= c m \\[6pt]
&= \left(900\right) \left(1\right) \\[6pt]
&\simeq 900\,\mathrm{J/K}
\end{aligned}
$$
