A mass of water of $0.6\,\mathrm{kg}$ warms by $20\,\mathrm K$ without changing phase. Use $c=4200\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$. How much heat does it absorb? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $50400\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $100800\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $25200\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $201600\,\mathrm{J}$.

## Explanation

The water stays in the same phase and $c$ has the stated value. The product $mc$ is the sample heat capacity. Multiply it by $\Delta T$ and obtain a positive result because the sample warms.

$$
\begin{aligned}
Q &= \Delta T c m \\[6pt]
&= \left(20\right) \left(4200\right) \left(0.6\right) \\[6pt]
&\simeq 50400\,\mathrm{J}
\end{aligned}
$$
