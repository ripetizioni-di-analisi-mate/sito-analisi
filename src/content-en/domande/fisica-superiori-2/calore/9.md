A heater supplies $9000\,\mathrm J$ to a sample and its container. Their combined heat capacity is $600\,\mathrm{J/K}$ and their common temperature increases by $12\,\mathrm K$. Neither undergoes a phase change. How much energy is lost to the surroundings?

- $1800\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $7200\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $9000\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $3600\,\mathrm{J}$.

## Explanation

The system consists of both bodies, so use their combined heat capacity to find their increase in energy. The heater reading gives the energy supplied. Subtract the energy stored from the input to find the energy transferred to the surroundings. This gives $9000-600\cdot12=1800\,\mathrm J$.
