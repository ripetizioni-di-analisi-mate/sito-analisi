Two objects have the same $C=600\,\mathrm{J/K}$. The first has $c=300\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$ and the second $c=600\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$. What is $\dfrac{m_1}{m_2}$?

- $0.5$
- $1$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

For each object, the mass is $m=\dfrac Cc$. The first has half the specific heat capacity of the second, so it needs twice the mass to have the same heat capacity. The ratio compares two masses in the same units and is therefore dimensionless.

$$
\frac{m_1}{m_2}=\frac{C/c_1}{C/c_2}=\frac{c_2}{c_1}=2.
$$
