Two objects have the same $C=600\,\mathrm{J/K}$. The first has $c=300\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$ and the second $c=600\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$. What is $m_1/m_2$?

- $0.5$
- $1$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

At fixed heat capacity, a smaller specific heat capacity is compensated by more mass.

$$
\frac{m_1}{m_2}=\frac{C/c_1}{C/c_2}=\frac{c_2}{c_1}=2.
$$
