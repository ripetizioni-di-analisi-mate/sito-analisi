A hot cup transfers energy to a cold saucer. Treat the cup and saucer together as isolated from their surroundings. What is the sign of the heat exchanged for each object?

- Negative for the cup, positive for the saucer.
- Positive for both.
- Negative for both.
- Positive for the cup, negative for the saucer.

## Explanation

The same transfer leaves the first system and enters the second. The convention assigns opposite signs to the two contributions.
