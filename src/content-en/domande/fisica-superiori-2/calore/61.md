A hot cup transfers energy to a cold saucer. Treat the cup and saucer together as isolated from their surroundings. What is the sign of the heat exchanged for each object?

- Negative for the cup, positive for the saucer.
- Positive for both.
- Negative for both.
- Positive for the cup, negative for the saucer.

## Explanation

Heat flows spontaneously from the warmer cup to the cooler saucer. In the cup's balance the transfer crosses its boundary outwards and is negative. The same transfer enters the saucer and is therefore positive. For the isolated system as a whole, the two contributions cancel.
