A device transfers $36\,\mathrm{kJ}$ in $120\,\mathrm s$. Its efficiency is $0.60$. What is the rated power?

- $180\,\mathrm W$
- $300\,\mathrm W$
- $3000\,\mathrm W$
- $500\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

The product $\eta P$ gives useful power. Energy transferred to the body is useful power times duration when the power is constant.

$$
P=\frac{36000}{0.60\cdot120}\,\mathrm W=500\,\mathrm W.
$$
