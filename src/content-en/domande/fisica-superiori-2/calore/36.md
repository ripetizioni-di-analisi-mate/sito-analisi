A device delivers $36\,\mathrm{kJ}$ to its load in $120\,\mathrm s$. Its efficiency is $0.60$. What is the device’s average input power?

- $180\,\mathrm W$
- $300\,\mathrm W$
- $3000\,\mathrm W$
- $500\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

Dividing the 36,000 joules delivered to the load by the time gives 300 watts of useful power. This is 60 per cent of the average input power, so divide by $0.60$ to recover the input. Over 120 seconds the input is 60 kilojoules, of which 36 reach the load.

$$
P=\frac{36000}{0.60\cdot120}\,\mathrm W=500\,\mathrm W.
$$
