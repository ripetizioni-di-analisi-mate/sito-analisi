In an insulated vessel of negligible heat capacity, $0.20\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water at $80\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ is mixed with $0.30\,\mathrm{kg}$ at $20\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. After equilibrium, $0.25\,\mathrm{kg}$ of the mixture is removed and replaced with $0.25\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water at $20\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. What is the new equilibrium temperature?

- $26\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $44\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $38\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $32\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$

## Explanation

The first balance gives $\dfrac{0.20\cdot80+0.30\cdot20}{0.50}\,{}^\circ\mathrm C=44\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. Removing half the mixture reduces the mass. The water left behind is still at the same temperature. In the second balance the masses are equal, so the final temperature is the mean of $44$ and $20\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$, or $32\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$.
