Mix $0.30\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water at $80\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ and $0.70\,\mathrm{kg}$ at $20\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ in an insulated container of negligible heat capacity. Find the common temperature.

- $50\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $62\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $28\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $38\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$

## Explanation

For an insulated system exchanging only heat internally, the signed contributions add to zero. Use positive capacities and signed changes.

$$
T_f=\frac{0.30\cdot80+0.70\cdot20}{0.30+0.70}\,{}^\circ\mathrm C=38\,{}^\circ\mathrm C.
$$
