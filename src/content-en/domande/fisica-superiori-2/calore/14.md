A sample with heat capacity $600\,\mathrm{J/K}$ must warm by $30\,\mathrm K$. It first receives useful power of $180\,\mathrm W$ for $50\,\mathrm s$. The heater then switches to an input of $300\,\mathrm W$ with efficiency $0.75$. The sample stays in the same phase. How long does heating take in total?

- $40\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $90\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $80\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $100\,\mathrm{s}$.

## Explanation

The required energy is $600\cdot30=18000\,\mathrm J$. The first stage supplies $9000\,\mathrm J$, leaving the same amount still to be delivered. In the second stage the body receives $225\,\mathrm W$ and takes $\dfrac{9000}{225}\,\mathrm s=40\,\mathrm s$. The requested duration is the sum of the two intervals because the useful power changes halfway through the energy transfer.
