A sample of mass $0.5\,\mathrm{kg}$ and specific heat capacity $500\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$ absorbs $3000\,\mathrm J$ without changing phase. What is its temperature increase? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $24\,\mathrm{K}$.
- $12\,\mathrm{K}$.
- $6\,\mathrm{K}$.
- $48\,\mathrm{K}$.

## Explanation

Divide the energy absorbed by the energy needed by the sample for each kelvin. Its heat capacity is $mc$, so $\Delta T=\dfrac{Q}{mc}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta T &= \frac{Q}{c m} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{\left(3000\right)}{\left(500\right) \left(0.5\right)} \\[6pt]
&\simeq 12\,\mathrm{K}
\end{aligned}
$$
