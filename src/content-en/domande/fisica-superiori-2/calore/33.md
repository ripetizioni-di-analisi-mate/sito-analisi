Two portions of water cool and warm from $65$ and $25\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ respectively to $45\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. With insulation and negligible container capacity, what is the ratio of hot mass to cool mass?

- $1$
- $2$
- $0.5$
- $4$

## Explanation

The two samples exchange equal amounts of energy. The hot sample drops from 65 to 45 degrees Celsius, and the cold one rises from 25 to 45. The magnitudes of the temperature changes are equal, and so are the specific heat capacities. The energy balance therefore requires equal masses.

$$
\frac{m_{\mathrm{calda}}}{m_{\mathrm{fredda}}}=\frac{45-25}{65-45}=1.
$$
