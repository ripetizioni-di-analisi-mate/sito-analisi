Two portions of water cool and warm from $65$ and $25\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ respectively to $45\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. With insulation and negligible container capacity, what is the ratio of hot mass to cool mass?

- $1$
- $2$
- $0.5$
- $4$

## Explanation

The equal intervals cancel in the balance. The mass ratio is one.

$$
\frac{m_{\mathrm{calda}}}{m_{\mathrm{fredda}}}=\frac{45-25}{65-45}=1.
$$
