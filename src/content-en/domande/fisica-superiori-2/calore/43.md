A wall conducts $25\,\mathrm W$ in steady state. What energy passes through in $4$ minutes?

- $100\,\mathrm J$
- $1500\,\mathrm J$
- $6000\,\mathrm J$
- $2400\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

In steady state the conducted power stays constant in time. The 25 watts represent 25 joules transferred each second. Four minutes last 240 seconds, so multiply the power by 240. The product gives the energy that crossed the wall during the interval.

$$
Q=25\,\mathrm W\cdot240\,\mathrm s=6000\,\mathrm J.
$$
