A wall conducts $25\,\mathrm W$ in steady state. What energy passes through in $4$ minutes?

- $100\,\mathrm J$
- $1500\,\mathrm J$
- $6000\,\mathrm J$
- $2400\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Express duration in seconds because one watt is one joule per second.

$$
Q=25\,\mathrm W\cdot240\,\mathrm s=6000\,\mathrm J.
$$
