Two parts of heat capacities $180$ and $320\,\mathrm{J/K}$ are in equilibrium. They warm together by $4\,\mathrm K$. How much energy is absorbed?

- $2000\,\mathrm J$
- $1280\,\mathrm J$
- $720\,\mathrm J$
- $500\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The parts undergo the same temperature change, so their products $C_i\Delta T$ add and $\Delta T$ can be factored out.

$$
Q=(180+320)\cdot4\,\mathrm J=2000\,\mathrm J.
$$
