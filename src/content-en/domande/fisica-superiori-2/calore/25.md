Two parts of heat capacities $180$ and $320\,\mathrm{J/K}$ are in equilibrium. They warm together by $4\,\mathrm K$. How much energy is absorbed?

- $2000\,\mathrm J$
- $1280\,\mathrm J$
- $720\,\mathrm J$
- $500\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Both parts warm together and undergo the same increase of four kelvins. Add their heat capacities before multiplying by this rise. Calculating the contributions separately gives $720\,\mathrm J$ for the first part and $1280\,\mathrm J$ for the second. Adding these contributions gives the same total energy.

$$
Q=(180+320)\cdot4\,\mathrm J=2000\,\mathrm J.
$$
