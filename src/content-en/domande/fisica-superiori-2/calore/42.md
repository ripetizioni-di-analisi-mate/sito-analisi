For $60\,\mathrm s$ a load receives energy at $500\,\mathrm W$ and transfers energy to the air at $100\,\mathrm W$. What net energy does it gain?

- $36000\,\mathrm J$
- $24000\,\mathrm J$
- $30000\,\mathrm J$
- $6000\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The two powers have opposite signs for the load. Multiplying their difference by time gives the net transfer.

$$
Q_{\mathrm{net}}=(500-100)\cdot60\,\mathrm J=24000\,\mathrm J.
$$
