A sample continuously receives $300\,\mathrm W$. The ambient temperature is $20\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. The sample loses $12\,\mathrm W$ to the air for each kelvin above ambient and remains in the same phase. At what temperature does it settle?

- $25\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $45\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $55\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $65\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$

## Explanation

The temperature settles when the sample’s energy stops increasing. The input power then equals the heat loss rate. This requires $\dfrac{300\,\mathrm W}{12\,\mathrm{W/K}}=25\,\mathrm K$ above ambient. Adding the rise to the ambient temperature gives $45\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. Below this value the loss is smaller than the input.
