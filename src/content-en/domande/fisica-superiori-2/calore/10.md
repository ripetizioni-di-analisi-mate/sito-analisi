A sample of mass $0.6\,\mathrm{kg}$ and specific heat capacity $500\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$ absorbs $3500\,\mathrm J$ without changing phase. What is its temperature increase? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $23.3333\,\mathrm{K}$.
- $11.6667\,\mathrm{K}$.
- $5.83333\,\mathrm{K}$.
- $46.6667\,\mathrm{K}$.

## Explanation

Divide the energy absorbed by the energy needed by the sample for each kelvin. Its heat capacity is $mc$, so $\Delta T=\dfrac{Q}{mc}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta T &= \frac{Q}{c m} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{\left(3500\right)}{\left(500\right) \left(0.6\right)} \\[6pt]
&\simeq 11.6667\,\mathrm{K}
\end{aligned}
$$
