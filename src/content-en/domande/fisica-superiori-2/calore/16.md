A sample releases $5.4\,\mathrm{kJ}$ as it cools by $12\,\mathrm K$. What is its heat capacity?

- $-450\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $64800\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $450\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

The model applies within a single phase. The sample’s capacity is the product $mc$, which links the temperature interval to signed heat.

$$
C=\frac{-5400}{-12}\,\mathrm{J/K}=450\,\mathrm{J/K}.
$$
