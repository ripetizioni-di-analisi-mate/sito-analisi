A sample releases $5.4\,\mathrm{kJ}$ as it cools by $12\,\mathrm K$. What is its heat capacity?

- $-450\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $64800\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $450\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

For the sample, the heat released is $Q=-5400\,\mathrm J$, and cooling gives $\Delta T=-12\,\mathrm K$. Heat capacity is the ratio of these two signed quantities. Both are negative, so the ratio is positive. Each kelvin of cooling corresponds to a release of 450 joules.

$$
C=\frac{-5400}{-12}\,\mathrm{J/K}=450\,\mathrm{J/K}.
$$
