Both faces of a uniform wall with no internal heat sources are kept at the same temperature. What does the one-dimensional steady-state model predict?

- Infinite power.
- The same power as when they differ by 10 K.
- No net power between the faces.
- Flow from the farther face.

## Explanation

Without internal heat sources, the steady temperature profile of a uniform wall with constant conductivity is linear. If the two temperatures match, the line is horizontal and its gradient is zero. Even for a highly conductive material, $P=0$, because there is no temperature difference to drive energy transfer between the faces.
