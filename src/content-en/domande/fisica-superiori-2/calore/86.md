If both faces of a wall in steady conditions are held at the same temperature, what does the one-dimensional model predict?

- Infinite power.
- The same power as when they differ by 10 K.
- No net power between the faces.
- Flow from the farther face.

## Explanation

The temperature difference in the conduction law is zero. In the absence of internal sources the model gives zero power.
