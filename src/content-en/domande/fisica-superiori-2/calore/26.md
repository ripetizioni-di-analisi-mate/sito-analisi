A composite object has heat capacity $840\,\mathrm{J/K}$. The first part contributes $240\,\mathrm{J/K}$. What does the second contribute?

- $1080\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $600\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $420\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $240\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

The parts undergo the same temperature change, so their products $C_i\Delta T$ add and $\Delta T$ can be factored out.

$$
C_2=(840-240)\,\mathrm{J/K}=600\,\mathrm{J/K}.
$$
