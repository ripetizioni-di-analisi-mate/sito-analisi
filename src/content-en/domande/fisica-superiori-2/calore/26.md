A composite object has heat capacity $840\,\mathrm{J/K}$. The first part contributes $240\,\mathrm{J/K}$. What does the second contribute?

- $1080\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $600\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $420\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $240\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

The value of $840\,\mathrm{J/K}$ is the energy needed to raise the complete object's temperature by one kelvin. Of these joules per kelvin, 240 come from the first part. Subtracting gives the contribution of the second part. The two contributions must add back to the total heat capacity.

$$
C_2=(840-240)\,\mathrm{J/K}=600\,\mathrm{J/K}.
$$
