A wall transfers $40\,\mathrm W$ in steady state across a $20\,\mathrm K$ difference. A layer is added in series to halve the power at the same temperature difference across the outer faces. How much resistance is added?

- $1\,\mathrm{K/W}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{K/W}$
- $2\,\mathrm{K/W}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{K/W}$

## Explanation

The initial data give a resistance of $\dfrac{20}{40}=0.50\,\mathrm{K/W}$. With the same temperature difference, halving the power requires doubling the total resistance. Resistances in series add, so the difference between the required resistance and the initial resistance is exactly the resistance to add.

$$
R_{\mathrm{add}}=\frac{20}{20}-\frac{20}{40}\,\mathrm{K/W}=0.50\,\mathrm{K/W}.
$$
