A wall transfers $40\,\mathrm W$ across $20\,\mathrm K$. Power is to be halved by adding a layer in series. How much resistance is added?

- $1\,\mathrm{K/W}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{K/W}$
- $2\,\mathrm{K/W}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{K/W}$

## Explanation

Power is common to layers in series. Their resistances add because their temperature drops add.

$$
R_{\mathrm{add}}=\frac{20}{20}-\frac{20}{40}\,\mathrm{K/W}=0.50\,\mathrm{K/W}.
$$
