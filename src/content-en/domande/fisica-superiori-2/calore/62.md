A student applies $Q=mc\Delta T$ directly while a sample boils. Which assumption must be checked?

- That heat is measured in joules.
- That the sample stays in the same phase.
- That temperature change always has positive sign.
- That mass must always be expressed in grams.

## Explanation

The formula relates heat to a temperature change within one phase. During boiling at fixed pressure, some of the liquid turns into vapour while the temperature can stay constant. For the phase-change stage, use the latent heat multiplied by the mass that vaporises.
