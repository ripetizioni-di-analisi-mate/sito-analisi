A thermal bridge occupies $10\%$ of a wall’s area. For the same temperature difference, the bridge conducts $50\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$ and the rest of the wall conducts $10\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$. Lateral heat flows are negligible. What fraction of the total power passes through the thermal bridge?

- $\dfrac{1}{10}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{5}{14}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

Divide the total wall area into ten equal parts. One part is the bridge, so it contributes $5\,\mathrm W$ per square metre of wall. The other nine parts together contribute $9\,\mathrm W$. The bridge carries $\dfrac{5}{5+9}=\dfrac{5}{14}$ of the total. Both the power per unit area and the area occupied matter.
