At equal area, thickness and face temperatures, which material conducts less power?

- The one of higher conductivity.
- The denser one, regardless of conductivity.
- The one of lower conductivity.
- Always the one of lower specific heat capacity.

## Explanation

In the steady-state equation the other factors are fixed and $P$ is directly proportional to $k$.
