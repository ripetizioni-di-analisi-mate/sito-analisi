Water absorbs $21\,\mathrm{kJ}$ over $10\,\mathrm K$ without changing phase. What mass is present if $c=4200\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$?

- $5\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $2\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.05\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The model applies within a single phase. The sample’s capacity is the product $mc$, which links the temperature interval to signed heat.

$$
m=\frac{21000}{4200\cdot10}\,\mathrm{kg}=0.5\,\mathrm{kg}.
$$
