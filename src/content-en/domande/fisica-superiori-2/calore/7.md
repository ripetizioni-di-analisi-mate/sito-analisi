A plane wall has area $6\,\mathrm{m^2}$, thickness $0.05\,\mathrm m$ and thermal conductivity $0.04\,\mathrm{W/(m\,K)}$. Its faces differ in temperature by $18\,\mathrm K$. What is the power transferred by conduction in steady state? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $0.216\,\mathrm{W}$.
- $14.4\,\mathrm{W}$.
- $86.4\,\mathrm{W}$.
- $172.8\,\mathrm{W}$.

## Explanation

In steady state the power is the same through every cross-section of the wall. It is proportional to the area and temperature difference and inversely proportional to the thickness. Energy flows from the warm face to the cool face.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal P &= \frac{A \Delta T \lambda}{d} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{\left(6\right) \left(18\right) \left(0.04\right)}{\left(0.05\right)} \\[6pt]
&\simeq 86.4\,\mathrm{W}
\end{aligned}
$$
