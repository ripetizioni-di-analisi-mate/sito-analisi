Two devices have the same rated power. The first has efficiency $0.80$ and the second $0.50$. What is the ratio $t_1/t_2$ to supply the same useful heat?

- $1.6$
- $1$
- $0.625$
- $0.3$

## Explanation

The product $\eta P$ gives useful power. Energy transferred to the body is useful power times duration when the power is constant.

$$
\frac{t_1}{t_2}=\frac{0.50}{0.80}=0.625.
$$
