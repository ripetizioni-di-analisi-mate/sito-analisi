Two devices have the same rated power. The first has efficiency $0.80$ and the second $0.50$. What is the ratio $\dfrac{t_1}{t_2}$ to supply the same useful heat?

- $1.6$
- $1$
- $0.625$
- $0.3$

## Explanation

The two devices must deliver the same amount of useful heat. At the same rated power, this requires $\eta_1 t_1=\eta_2 t_2$. The time ratio therefore has the second device's efficiency in the numerator. It is less than one because the more efficient device delivers useful energy faster.

$$
\frac{t_1}{t_2}=\frac{0.50}{0.80}=0.625.
$$
