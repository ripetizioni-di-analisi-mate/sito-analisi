Temperature across a wall falls linearly from $30$ to $14\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ over $8\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the magnitude of the average gradient in kelvins per metre?

- $2\,\mathrm{K/m}$
- $16\,\mathrm{K/m}$
- $200\,\mathrm{K/m}$
- $50\,\mathrm{K/m}$

## Explanation

The temperature drop is 16 kelvins. Divide by $0.08\,\mathrm m$, the thickness in the requested units, to express the result per metre. The gradient directed from the warm to the cold face is negative. The question asks for its magnitude, so use the positive value.

$$
|\nabla T|=\frac{30-14}{0.08}\,\mathrm{K/m}=200\,\mathrm{K/m}.
$$
