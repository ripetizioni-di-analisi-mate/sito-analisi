With properties and area unchanged, what is the effect of reversing two layers in series at fixed outer temperatures?

- Total power always doubles.
- Total power is unchanged, but the interface temperature can change.
- Total resistance is halved.
- The power changes sign.

## Explanation

The sum of the resistances is independent of order. The drop between the warm face and the interface is across the first layer and depends on its resistance.
