A wall in steady state consists of two layers in series. Both external faces are brought to temperatures $7\,\mathrm K$ lower. The wall is allowed to reach its new steady state and its thermal resistances remain constant. How does the interface temperature change?

- $0\,\mathrm K$
- $-7\,\mathrm K$
- $+7\,\mathrm K$
- $-14\,\mathrm K$

## Explanation

Both face temperatures fall by the same amount. The total temperature drop stays fixed, and with constant resistances, so does the power. The drop from the hot face to the interface therefore also stays fixed. Since its starting temperature falls by $7\,\mathrm K$, the interface temperature falls by $7\,\mathrm K$ too.
