A calorimeter starts at the final temperature reached by the samples. What is its heat contribution in the final balance?

- Zero because its temperature change is zero.
- Equal to its heat capacity expressed in joules.
- Always negative.
- Always positive.

## Explanation

With constant heat capacity, the net contribution is heat capacity times the temperature change. Even if exchanges occurred along the way, the final and initial readings are equal.
