A calorimeter exchanges only heat and starts at the final temperature reached by the samples. What is its heat contribution in the final balance?

- Zero because its temperature change is zero.
- Equal to its heat capacity expressed in joules.
- Always negative.
- Always positive.

## Explanation

With constant heat capacity and heat transfer alone, the calorimeter’s net contribution is $C_0(T_f-T_0)$. If its final temperature is its initial temperature, this term is zero. It may have absorbed energy at one stage and returned it at another, provided the vessel’s transfers sum to zero.
