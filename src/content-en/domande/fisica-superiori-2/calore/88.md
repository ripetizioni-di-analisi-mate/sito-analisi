Three layers with no side losses are crossed by the same power. Which relation gives their total resistance?

- $R=R_1+R_2+R_3$.
- $R=R_1R_2R_3$.
- $R=R_1-R_2+R_3$.
- $R=R_1/R_3$.

## Explanation

Power is common to layers in series. Their resistances add because their temperature drops add.
