Three layers with no side losses are crossed by the same power. Which relation gives their total resistance?

- $R=R_1+R_2+R_3$.
- $R=R_1R_2R_3$.
- $R=R_1-R_2+R_3$.
- $R=\dfrac{R_1}{R_3}$.

## Explanation

Heat passes through the layers in succession and each carries the same power $P$. The overall drop is therefore $\Delta T=P(R_1+R_2+R_3)$. The factor multiplying $P$ is the total resistance. For two side-by-side paths the temperature drop is shared, and their powers add.
