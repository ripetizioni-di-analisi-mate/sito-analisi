A heater’s only input is constant electric power. Its estimated efficiency is $1.20$. What does this check show?

- Physical efficiency may be any number greater than one.
- The data or balance boundary need checking.
- Useful power must be zero.
- Input must be expressed in kelvins.

## Explanation

The estimate would mean $120\,\mathrm J$ of useful output for every $100\,\mathrm J$ of electrical input. If the apparatus stores or releases energy during the test, that change must be included. If it returns to its initial state and the input is only electrical, the ratio is incompatible with the balance. Recheck the measurements and system boundary.
