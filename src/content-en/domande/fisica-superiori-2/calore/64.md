Two objects have the same heat capacity $C$ and receive the same $Q$. Which relation between their temperature rises holds under the model conditions?

- $\Delta T_1=2\Delta T_2$.
- $\Delta T_1=-\Delta T_2$.
- $\Delta T_1=\Delta T_2$.
- $\Delta T_1+\Delta T_2=0$.

## Explanation

The model applies within a single phase. The sample’s capacity is the product $mc$, which links the temperature interval to signed heat.
