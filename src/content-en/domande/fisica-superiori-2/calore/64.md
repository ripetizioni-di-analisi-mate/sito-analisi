Two objects have the same heat capacity $C$ and receive the same $Q$. Which relation between their temperature rises holds under the model conditions?

- $\Delta T_1=2\Delta T_2$.
- $\Delta T_1=-\Delta T_2$.
- $\Delta T_1=\Delta T_2$.
- $\Delta T_1+\Delta T_2=0$.

## Explanation

With the same heat capacity, each kelvin requires the same amount of energy for both bodies. It follows that $\Delta T_1=\dfrac{Q}{C}=\Delta T_2$. Their final temperatures may still differ. If one starts ten degrees hotter, that gap remains after the two equal increases.
