A $400\,\mathrm g$ sample with $c=600\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$ warms by $12\,\mathrm K$. What heat does it absorb?

- $288\,\mathrm J$
- $2880000\,\mathrm J$
- $2880\,\mathrm J$
- $240\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The model applies within a single phase. The sample’s capacity is the product $mc$, which links the temperature interval to signed heat.

$$
Q=0.400\cdot600\cdot12\,\mathrm J=2880\,\mathrm J.
$$
