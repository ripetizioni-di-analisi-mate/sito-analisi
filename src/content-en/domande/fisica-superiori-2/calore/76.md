What quantity is represented by the area of a rectangle on a graph of constant useful power versus time?

- $Q=P_{\mathrm u}\Delta t$.
- $t=P_{\mathrm u}Q$.
- $C=P_{\mathrm u}\Delta t$.
- $k=P_{\mathrm u}/\Delta t$.

## Explanation

The product $\eta P$ gives useful power. Energy transferred to the body is useful power times duration when the power is constant.
