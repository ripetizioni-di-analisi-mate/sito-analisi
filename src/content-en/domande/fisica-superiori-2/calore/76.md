What quantity is represented by the area of a rectangle on a graph of constant useful power versus time?

- $Q=P_{\mathrm u}\Delta t$.
- $t=P_{\mathrm u}Q$.
- $C=P_{\mathrm u}\Delta t$.
- $k=\dfrac{P_{\mathrm u}}{\Delta t}$.

## Explanation

The rectangle’s height is the useful power and its width is the duration. Watts are joules per second, so the area is measured in joules and represents the energy received. For example, an interval at $200\,\mathrm W$ lasting $15\,\mathrm s$ corresponds to $3000\,\mathrm J$.
