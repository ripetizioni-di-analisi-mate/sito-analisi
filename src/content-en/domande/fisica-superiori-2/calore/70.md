The warm object has a far smaller heat capacity than the cold object in an insulated system. Which initial temperature is the final value closer to?

- The temperature of the cold object.
- The temperature of the warm object.
- Absolute zero in every case.
- Always halfway between them.

## Explanation

The common temperature lies closer to the cold body’s starting temperature because that body has the larger heat capacity. The heat released by the hot body equals the heat absorbed by the cold one. If the cold body’s heat capacity is ten times larger, its temperature changes by one tenth as much.
