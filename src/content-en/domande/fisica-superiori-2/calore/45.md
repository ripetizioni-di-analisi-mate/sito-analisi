A panel of conductivity $0.08\,\mathrm{W/(m\,K)}$ and area $2\,\mathrm{m^2}$ conducts $20\,\mathrm W$ across a $15\,\mathrm K$ difference. What is its thickness?

- $0.12\,\mathrm m$
- $0.06\,\mathrm m$
- $0.24\,\mathrm m$
- $1.2\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

In steady state without lateral losses, heat transfer through the wall depends on area, conductivity and the temperature gradient.

$$
d=\frac{0.08\cdot2\cdot15}{20}\,\mathrm m=0.12\,\mathrm m.
$$
