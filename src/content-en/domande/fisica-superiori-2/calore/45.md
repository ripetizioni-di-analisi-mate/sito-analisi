A panel of conductivity $0.08\,\mathrm{W/(m\,K)}$ and area $2\,\mathrm{m^2}$ conducts $20\,\mathrm W$ across a $15\,\mathrm K$ difference. What is its thickness?

- $0.12\,\mathrm m$
- $0.06\,\mathrm m$
- $0.24\,\mathrm m$
- $1.2\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

For the same material, area and temperature difference, a thicker wall transfers less power. So divide the product $kA\Delta T$ by the stated power to obtain the thickness. The ratio has dimensions of length, and $0.12\,\mathrm m$ is 12 centimetres.

$$
d=\frac{0.08\cdot2\cdot15}{20}\,\mathrm m=0.12\,\mathrm m.
$$
