An insulated vessel of heat capacity $800\,\mathrm{J/K}$ at $20\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ receives a sample of heat capacity $200\,\mathrm{J/K}$ at $70\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. What is the final temperature?

- $45\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $60\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $30\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $25\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$

## Explanation

For an insulated system exchanging only heat internally, the signed contributions add to zero. Use positive capacities and signed changes.

$$
T_f=\frac{800\cdot20+200\cdot70}{1000}\,{}^\circ\mathrm C=30\,{}^\circ\mathrm C.
$$
