How does energy reach Earth from the Sun through a vacuum?

- By convection through the vacuum.
- By conduction through the vacuum.
- By radiation.
- Only through a solid wall.

## Explanation

Electromagnetic waves travel through a vacuum. Conduction and convection require matter.
