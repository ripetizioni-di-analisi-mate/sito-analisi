How does energy reach Earth from the Sun through a vacuum?

- By convection through the vacuum.
- By conduction through the vacuum.
- By radiation.
- Only through a solid wall.

## Explanation

Between the Sun and Earth, energy crosses regions almost empty of matter as electromagnetic waves. This transfer is radiation. In the atmosphere, air currents can then redistribute energy that has been absorbed. Air movement describes a later transfer within the medium, while convection does not occur during the crossing through a vacuum.
