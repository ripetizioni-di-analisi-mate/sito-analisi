A notebook records the heat and temperature of the same sample. Which pair of units is consistent?

- Kelvins and joules.
- Joules and kelvins.
- Joules and watts.
- Watts and kelvins.

## Explanation

Heat is energy transferred during an exchange and its SI unit is the joule. Temperature determines the direction of spontaneous heat transfer between bodies in contact and is measured in kelvins in the SI. The correct pair therefore matches joules with kelvins.
