A notebook records the heat and temperature of the same sample. Which pair of units is consistent?

- Kelvins and joules.
- Joules and kelvins.
- Joules and watts.
- Watts and kelvins.

## Explanation

Heat measures transferred energy in joules. Temperature on the absolute scale uses kelvins.
