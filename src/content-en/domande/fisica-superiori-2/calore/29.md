In an insulated balance, $1\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water at $18\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ is brought to $28\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ with water at $68\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. How much hot water is needed if the container has negligible heat capacity?

- $0.25\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.2\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

For an insulated system exchanging only heat internally, the signed contributions add to zero. Use positive capacities and signed changes.

$$
m_{\mathrm{h}}=1\cdot\frac{28-18}{68-28}\,\mathrm{kg}=0.25\,\mathrm{kg}.
$$
