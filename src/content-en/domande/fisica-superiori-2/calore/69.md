Two samples without phase changes have positive $C_1$ and $C_2$. If insulated and starting at $T_{1,i}$ and $T_{2,i}$, which equation gives their balance?

- $(C_1+C_2)T_f=0$.
- $C_1(T_f-T_{1,i})=C_2(T_f-T_{2,i})$.
- $T_f=T_{1,i}+T_{2,i}$.
- $C_1(T_f-T_{1,i})+C_2(T_f-T_{2,i})=0$.

## Explanation

For an insulated system exchanging only heat internally, the signed contributions add to zero. Use positive capacities and signed changes.
