Two samples with constant heat capacities $C_1>0$ and $C_2>0$ start at temperatures in kelvin with $0<T_{1,i}<T_{2,i}$. They exchange heat only with each other and stay in the same phase. Which equation expresses the energy balance at the equilibrium temperature $T_f$?

- $(C_1+C_2)T_f=0$.
- $C_1(T_f-T_{1,i})=C_2(T_f-T_{2,i})$.
- $T_f=T_{1,i}+T_{2,i}$.
- $C_1(T_f-T_{1,i})+C_2(T_f-T_{2,i})=0$.

## Explanation

The first sample warms, so $Q_1=C_1(T_f-T_{1,i})>0$. The second cools, so $Q_2=C_2(T_f-T_{2,i})<0$. The cold sample receives all the heat released by the hot sample, so $Q_1+Q_2=0$. The signed terms can be added directly without introducing a second minus sign.
