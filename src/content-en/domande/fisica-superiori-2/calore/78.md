What expression goes into the balance if the calorimeter starts at its own temperature $T_0$ and has a heat capacity $C_0$?

- $C_0(T_f-T_0)$.
- $C_0(T_0-T_f)$.
- $C_0+T_f$.
- $T_0C_0^2$.

## Explanation

Each component in the balance has its own starting temperature. For the vessel the change is $T_f-T_0$, so its term is $C_0(T_f-T_0)$. If it goes from $45$ to $35\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ with $C_0=100\,\mathrm{J/K}$, the term is $-1000\,\mathrm J$. The sign signals energy released by the calorimeter.
