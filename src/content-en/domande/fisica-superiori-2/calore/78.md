What expression goes into the balance for a calorimeter of heat capacity $C_0$ starting at its own temperature $T_0$?

- $C_0(T_f-T_0)$.
- $C_0(T_0-T_f)$.
- $C_0+T_f$.
- $T_0C_0^2$.

## Explanation

The parts undergo the same temperature change, so their products $C_i\Delta T$ add and $\Delta T$ can be factored out.
