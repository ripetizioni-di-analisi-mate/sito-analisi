Two insulated samples have heat capacities $C_1=300$ and $C_2=900\,\mathrm{J/K}$. The first starts at $80\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ and the second at $20\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. What is the magnitude of the heat released by the first?

- $4500\,\mathrm J$
- $13500\,\mathrm J$
- $18000\,\mathrm J$
- $40500\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

For an insulated system exchanging only heat internally, the signed contributions add to zero. Use positive capacities and signed changes.

$$
\begin{aligned}T_f&=\frac{300\cdot80+900\cdot20}{1200}\,{}^\circ\mathrm C=35\,{}^\circ\mathrm C\\|Q_1|&=300(80-35)\,\mathrm J=13500\,\mathrm J.\end{aligned}
$$
