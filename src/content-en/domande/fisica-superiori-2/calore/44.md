A wall of area $2\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and thickness $0.10\,\mathrm m$ conducts $40\,\mathrm W$ across $20\,\mathrm K$. What conductivity follows?

- $1\,\mathrm{W/(m\,K)}$
- $10\,\mathrm{W/(m\,K)}$
- $0.2\,\mathrm{W/(m\,K)}$
- $0.1\,\mathrm{W/(m\,K)}$

## Explanation

In steady state without lateral losses, heat transfer through the wall depends on area, conductivity and the temperature gradient.

$$
k=\frac{40\cdot0.10}{2\cdot20}\,\mathrm{W/(m\,K)}=0.10\,\mathrm{W/(m\,K)}.
$$
