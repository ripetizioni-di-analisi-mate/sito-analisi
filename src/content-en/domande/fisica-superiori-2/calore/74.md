An insulated system has three components that exchange only heat. The first component releases $8400\,\mathrm J$ and the calorimeter absorbs $2400\,\mathrm J$. What is the signed heat for the second component?

- $+8400\,\mathrm J$
- $+6000\,\mathrm J$
- $-6000\,\mathrm J$
- $+10800\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The three heats must sum to zero in the insulated system. The first term is $-8400\,\mathrm J$ because the body loses energy. The calorimeter contributes $+2400\,\mathrm J$. The second component must therefore absorb $6000\,\mathrm J$. Assigning it all $8400\,\mathrm J$ would count the calorimeter’s share twice.
