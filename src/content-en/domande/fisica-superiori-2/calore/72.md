With initial readings of $20$ and $50$ °C a calculation gives $57$ °C with insulation and no phase changes. What check detects the error?

- The final temperature must always be zero.
- The heat released by either body is positive.
- Always use the higher temperature.
- With positive weights the mean lies between the initial readings.

## Explanation

With a final temperature of $57\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$, both bodies would warm up. The terms $C_1\Delta T_1$ and $C_2\Delta T_2$ would both be positive, making their sum positive. An isolated system requires a zero sum. A result above both readings signals an error in the calculation or the stated conditions.
