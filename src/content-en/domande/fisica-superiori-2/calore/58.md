A calorimeter of heat capacity $210\,\mathrm{J/K}$ is replaced in the balance by an equivalent mass of water. With $c=4200\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$, what mass represents the same capacity?

- $0.5\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.05\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.2\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Find the amount of water that absorbs the same number of joules per kelvin as the calorimeter. Set the calorimeter's heat capacity equal to the mass of water multiplied by its specific heat capacity. The ratio has units of kilograms and gives a mass of 50 grams.

$$
m_{\mathrm{eq}}=\frac{210}{4200}\,\mathrm{kg}=0.05\,\mathrm{kg}.
$$
