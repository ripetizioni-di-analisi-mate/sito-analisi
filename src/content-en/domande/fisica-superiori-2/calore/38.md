The efficiency is $75\%$ and the container receives $30\,\mathrm{kJ}$. How much energy is supplied to the heater?

- $22.5\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $40\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $30\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $10\,\mathrm{kJ}$

## Explanation

The product $\eta P$ gives useful power. Energy transferred to the body is useful power times duration when the power is constant.

$$
E_{\mathrm{in}}=\frac{30}{0.75}\,\mathrm{kJ}=40\,\mathrm{kJ}.
$$
