The efficiency is $75\%$ and the container receives $30\,\mathrm{kJ}$. How much energy is supplied to the heater?

- $22.5\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $40\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $30\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $10\,\mathrm{kJ}$

## Explanation

The container receives 75 per cent of the energy supplied. The 30 kilojoules therefore represent three quarters of the total. One quarter is 10 kilojoules, so four quarters are 40 kilojoules. Subtracting the lost 10 kilojoules from the input gives the energy received by the container.

$$
E_{\mathrm{in}}=\frac{30}{0.75}\,\mathrm{kJ}=40\,\mathrm{kJ}.
$$
