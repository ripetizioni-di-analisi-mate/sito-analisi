Sample A has $c_A=400\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$ and B of equal mass has $c_B=1200\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$. Both absorb the same energy. What is the ratio $\dfrac{\Delta T_A}{\Delta T_B}$?

- $3$
- $0.33333333$
- $1$
- $9$

## Explanation

The samples have equal masses and absorb the same energy. Equating their products $mc\Delta T$ leaves $c_A\Delta T_A=c_B\Delta T_B$. The temperature changes are therefore in the inverse ratio of the specific heat capacities. A needs less energy per kelvin and therefore warms by more.

$$
\frac{\Delta T_A}{\Delta T_B}=\frac{c_B}{c_A}=\frac{1200}{400}=3.
$$
