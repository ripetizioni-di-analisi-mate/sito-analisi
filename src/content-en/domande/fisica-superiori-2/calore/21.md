Sample A has $c_A=400\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$ and B of equal mass has $c_B=1200\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$. Both absorb the same energy. What is the ratio $\Delta T_A/\Delta T_B$?

- $3$
- $0.33333333$
- $1$
- $9$

## Explanation

At fixed mass and energy, temperature rise varies as $1/c$.

$$
\frac{\Delta T_A}{\Delta T_B}=\frac{c_B}{c_A}=\frac{1200}{400}=3.
$$
