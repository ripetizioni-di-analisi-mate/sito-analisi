A $750\,\mathrm W$ heater has efficiency $0.80$. What is its useful power?

- $150\,\mathrm W$
- $750\,\mathrm W$
- $600\,\mathrm W$
- $937.5\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

The product $\eta P$ gives useful power. Energy transferred to the body is useful power times duration when the power is constant.

$$
P_{\mathrm u}=0.80\cdot750\,\mathrm W=600\,\mathrm W.
$$
