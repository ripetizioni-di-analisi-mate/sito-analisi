A $750\,\mathrm W$ heater has efficiency $0.80$. What is its useful power?

- $150\,\mathrm W$
- $750\,\mathrm W$
- $600\,\mathrm W$
- $937.5\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

Efficiency is the ratio of useful to rated power. Of each input joule, $0.80$ joules reach the intended load. A rated input of 750 joules per second therefore gives 600 useful joules per second. The difference of 150 watts is the power that follows other paths.

$$
P_{\mathrm u}=0.80\cdot750\,\mathrm W=600\,\mathrm W.
$$
