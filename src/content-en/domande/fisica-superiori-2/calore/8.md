A mass of water of $5\,\mathrm{kg}$ and a calorimeter of heat capacity $600\,\mathrm{J/K}$ are initially in equilibrium. How much heat is needed to warm both by $14\,\mathrm K$? Use $c=4200\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$ and neglect heat losses. Choose the value closest to the result.

- $30240\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $8400\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $151200\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $302400\,\mathrm{J}$.

## Explanation

The water and calorimeter share the same temperature change. Their heat transfers add, so first add their heat capacities and then multiply by the interval.

$$
\begin{aligned}
Q &= \Delta T \left(C + c m\right) \\[6pt]
&= \left(14\right) \left(\left(600\right) + \left(4200\right) \left(5\right)\right) \\[6pt]
&\simeq 302400\,\mathrm{J}
\end{aligned}
$$
