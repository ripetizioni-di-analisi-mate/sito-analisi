Three objects of equal heat capacity start at $10$, $25$ and $70\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. The system is insulated. Find the common temperature.

- $30\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $52.5\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $105\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $35\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$

## Explanation

For an insulated system exchanging only heat internally, the signed contributions add to zero. Use positive capacities and signed changes.

$$
T_f=\frac{10+25+70}{3}\,{}^\circ\mathrm C=35\,{}^\circ\mathrm C.
$$
