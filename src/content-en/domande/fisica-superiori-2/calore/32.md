Three objects of equal heat capacity start at $10$, $25$ and $70\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. The system is insulated. Find the common temperature.

- $30\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $52.5\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $105\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $35\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$

## Explanation

Add the three temperature changes, each multiplied by the same heat capacity. The sum of these contributions is zero in the insulated system. Cancelling the common heat capacity gives the arithmetic mean. At 35 degrees Celsius, the first two bodies warm by 25 and 10 kelvins respectively, while the third cools by 35.

$$
T_f=\frac{10+25+70}{3}\,{}^\circ\mathrm C=35\,{}^\circ\mathrm C.
$$
