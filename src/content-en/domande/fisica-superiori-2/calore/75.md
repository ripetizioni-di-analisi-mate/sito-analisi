For a heater with efficiency $\eta<1$, why is $\eta P$ used to calculate the time needed to warm the water?

- Because $P$ always measures absolute temperature.
- Because only that fraction of the power reaches the load.
- Because the water receives more power than enters the heater.
- Because $\eta$ is in seconds.

## Explanation

The time depends on how much energy reaches the water each second. With efficiency $0.75$ and a rated power of $400\,\mathrm W$, $300\,\mathrm J$ reaches the water every second. Divide the energy needed by all the water by that useful power. Dividing by the rated watts would include losses and give too short a time.
