Why does heating time for water use $\eta P$ for a heater with efficiency $\eta<1$?

- Because $P$ always measures absolute temperature.
- Because only that fraction of the power reaches the load.
- Because the water receives more power than enters the heater.
- Because $\eta$ is in seconds.

## Explanation

Divide the energy needed by the water by the useful power. Dividing it by rated power would give too short a time.
