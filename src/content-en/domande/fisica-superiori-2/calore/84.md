A heater is switched on suddenly on one face of a wall. Why is it too soon to use the steady-state equation at that instant?

- The internal profile is still changing and the wall may be storing energy.
- The thickness no longer has units.
- Conduction is possible only when the faces have the same temperature.
- The wall necessarily changes phase.

## Explanation

In steady state, temperature at each point stays constant. At start-up, more energy can enter than leave.
