A $0.30\,\mathrm{kg}$ block with $c=900\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$ cools to $30\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$, releasing $5400\,\mathrm J$. What was its initial temperature?

- $10\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $50\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $20\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $60\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$

## Explanation

The block has heat capacity $0.30\cdot900=270\,\mathrm{J/K}$. It releases energy, so use $Q=-5400\,\mathrm J$ in its balance. Dividing by the heat capacity gives a change of $-20\,\mathrm K$. The final temperature is known, so subtract the signed change to recover the initial temperature.

$$
T_i=T_f-\frac{Q}{mc}=30\,{}^\circ\mathrm C-\frac{-5400}{0.30\cdot900}\,\mathrm K=50\,{}^\circ\mathrm C.
$$
