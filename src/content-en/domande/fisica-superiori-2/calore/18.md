A $0.30\,\mathrm{kg}$ block with $c=900\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$ cools to $30\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$, releasing $5400\,\mathrm J$. What was its initial temperature?

- $10\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $50\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $20\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $60\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$

## Explanation

The model applies within a single phase. The sample’s capacity is the product $mc$, which links the temperature interval to signed heat.

$$
T_i=T_f-\frac{Q}{mc}=30\,{}^\circ\mathrm C-\frac{-5400}{0.30\cdot900}\,\mathrm K=50\,{}^\circ\mathrm C.
$$
