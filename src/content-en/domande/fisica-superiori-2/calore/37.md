A $500\,\mathrm W$ element runs for $40\,\mathrm s$ and loses $6\,\mathrm{kJ}$ outside. What energy reaches the sample?

- $14000\,\mathrm J$
- $20000\,\mathrm J$
- $26000\,\mathrm J$
- $6000\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Input energy is power times time. Subtract the energy leaving the sample system.

$$
Q_{\mathrm u}=500\cdot40\,\mathrm J-6000\,\mathrm J=14000\,\mathrm J.
$$
