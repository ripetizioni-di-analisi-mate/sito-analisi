A liquid and its calorimeter initially have the same temperature. They absorb $10\,\mathrm{kJ}$ together and reach equilibrium. Their heat capacities are $900$ and $100\,\mathrm{J/K}$. What fraction of the energy goes to the calorimeter?

- $0.11111111$
- $0.1$
- $0.9$
- $0.5$

## Explanation

The parts undergo the same temperature change, so their products $C_i\Delta T$ add and $\Delta T$ can be factored out.

$$
\frac{Q_{\mathrm{cal}}}{Q_{\mathrm{tot}}}=\frac{100}{900+100}=0.10.
$$
