Mix $6\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water at $20\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}$ and $7\,\mathrm{kg}$ at $60\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}$. The system is insulated and the container has negligible heat capacity. What is the final temperature? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $41.5385\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}$.
- $40\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}$.
- $38.4615\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}$.
- $20.7692\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}$.

## Explanation

The hot and cold contributions to the balance have opposite signs. The specific heat capacity of water is a common factor in the two terms. The final temperature is a mass-weighted mean, closer to the temperature of the larger mass.

$$
\begin{aligned}
T_e &= \frac{T_1 m_1 + T_2 m_2}{m_1 + m_2} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{\left(20\right) \left(6\right) + \left(60\right) \left(7\right)}{\left(6\right) + \left(7\right)} \\[6pt]
&\simeq 41.5385\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}
\end{aligned}
$$
