In liquid circulating in a saucepan, portions of warm and cool water move. Which mechanism describes transport by this motion?

- Radiation through a vacuum.
- Conduction alone without movement of matter.
- Freezing.
- Convection.

## Explanation

Water heated near the bottom can rise and carry energy upwards. Cooler portions can sink and sustain the circulation. This transfer through movement of matter is convection. Conduction can also occur at the contact between the liquid and the pan wall, where it does not describe the movement of water portions.
