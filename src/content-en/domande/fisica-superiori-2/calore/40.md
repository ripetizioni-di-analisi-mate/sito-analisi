A heater delivers $240\,\mathrm W$ of useful power for $25\,\mathrm s$ to a block with $C=300\,\mathrm{J/K}$. How much does its temperature rise?

- $5\,\mathrm K$
- $8\,\mathrm K$
- $6000\,\mathrm K$
- $20\,\mathrm K$

## Explanation

Use the useful power, already given in joules per second. In 25 seconds the block receives $6000\,\mathrm J$. Heat capacity gives the energy needed for each kelvin, so divide by $300\,\mathrm{J/K}$ to convert the accumulated energy into the block's temperature rise.

$$
\Delta T=\frac{240\cdot25}{300}\,\mathrm K=20\,\mathrm K.
$$
