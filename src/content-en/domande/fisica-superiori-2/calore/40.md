A heater delivers $240\,\mathrm W$ of useful power for $25\,\mathrm s$ to a block with $C=300\,\mathrm{J/K}$. How much does its temperature rise?

- $5\,\mathrm K$
- $8\,\mathrm K$
- $6000\,\mathrm K$
- $20\,\mathrm K$

## Explanation

The product $\eta P$ gives useful power. Energy transferred to the body is useful power times duration when the power is constant.

$$
\Delta T=\frac{240\cdot25}{300}\,\mathrm K=20\,\mathrm K.
$$
