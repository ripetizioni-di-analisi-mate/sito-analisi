Mix $5\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water at $20\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ and $6\,\mathrm{kg}$ at $60\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. The system is insulated and the container has negligible heat capacity. What is the final temperature? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $41.8\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$.
- $35\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$.
- $48.2\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$.
- $20.9\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$.

## Explanation

The hot and cold contributions to the balance have opposite signs. The specific heat capacity of water is a common factor in the two terms. The final temperature is a mass-weighted mean, closer to the temperature of the larger mass.

$$
\begin{aligned}
T_e &= \frac{T_1 m_1 + T_2 m_2}{m_1 + m_2} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{20\cdot5 + 60\cdot6}{5 + 6}\,{}^\circ\mathrm C \\[6pt]
&\simeq 41.8\,{}^\circ\mathrm C
\end{aligned}
$$
