A heater delivers $150\,\mathrm W$ for $30\,\mathrm s$, then $300\,\mathrm W$ for another $20\,\mathrm s$. What is the total useful energy?

- $10500\,\mathrm J$
- $22500\,\mathrm J$
- $9000\,\mathrm J$
- $6000\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Power is constant within each interval, so the energy for that interval is the product of its power and duration. The first contribution is 4500 joules and the second is 6000. Add the energies from the two intervals, since the two power levels occur at different times.

$$
Q_{\mathrm u}=(150\cdot30+300\cdot20)\,\mathrm J=10500\,\mathrm J.
$$
