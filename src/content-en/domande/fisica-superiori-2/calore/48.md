A wall is replaced by one with three times the area and twice the thickness, with conductivity and temperature difference unchanged. What is the ratio of the new conducted power to the original power?

- $0.67$
- $6$
- $1.5$
- $1$

## Explanation

The material and temperature drop are the same, so their factors cancel in the ratio. Tripling the area multiplies the total power by three, while doubling the thickness divides it by two. The combined effect is a power one and a half times the original.

$$
\frac{P_2}{P_1}=\frac{A_2}{A_1}\frac{d_1}{d_2}=3\cdot\frac12=1.5.
$$
