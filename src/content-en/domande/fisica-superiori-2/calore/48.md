A wall is replaced by one with three times the area and twice the thickness, with conductivity and temperature difference unchanged. What is the ratio of the new conducted power to the original power?

- $0.66666667$
- $6$
- $1.5$
- $1$

## Explanation

In steady state without lateral losses, heat transfer through the wall depends on area, conductivity and the temperature gradient.

$$
\frac{P_2}{P_1}=\frac{A_2}{A_1}\frac{d_1}{d_2}=3\cdot\frac12=1.5.
$$
