In two layers in series the first has the larger temperature drop. What does that imply at the same steady power?

- The first layer has smaller thermal resistance.
- Power in the first layer must be greater.
- The first layer has greater thermal resistance.
- The first face is at zero temperature.

## Explanation

For each layer $\Delta T_i=PR_i$. The same power produces a greater drop across a larger resistance.
