A heater delivers a constant useful power of $120\,\mathrm W$ to a sample. The graph shows its temperature as it melts. How much energy does the sample absorb during the melting interval alone?

::grafico{linee="0,18 25,30 75,30 95,39" punti="25,30,pieno;75,30,pieno" segmenti="25,15,25,30;75,15,75,30" etichette="26,17,25 s;76,17,75 s" xmin=0 xmax=99 ymin=15 ymax=42 nomi="t (s),T (°C)" titolo="The temperature stays at 30 °C from 25 s to 75 s while the sample melts."}

- $3.0\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $9.0\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $11.4\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $6.0\,\mathrm{kJ}$

## Explanation

Melting occupies the horizontal stretch between $25\,\mathrm s$ and $75\,\mathrm s$. The sample absorbs $120\,\mathrm J$ each second even while its temperature stays constant. Multiplying by the $50\,\mathrm s$ duration gives $6000\,\mathrm J=6.0\,\mathrm{kJ}$. The first $25\,\mathrm s$ heat the solid and are excluded.
