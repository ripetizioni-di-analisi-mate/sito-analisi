In a graph of $Q$ versus $\Delta T$, the line for A is steeper. If the samples receive the same positive heat transfer, which warms less?

- B, because it has greater heat capacity.
- A, because it has smaller heat capacity.
- They are equal regardless of the slopes.
- A, because it has greater heat capacity.

## Explanation

The steeper line represents more energy per kelvin. With the same energy, A has the smaller interval.
