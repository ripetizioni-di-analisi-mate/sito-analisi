A layer of resistance $0.30\,\mathrm{K/W}$ in series with a second layer touches the hot surface at $28\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. Power is $30\,\mathrm W$. What is the interface temperature?

- $9\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $37\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $19\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $25\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$

## Explanation

From the warm face to the interface, the heat crosses only the first layer. Its temperature drop is the stated resistance multiplied by 30 watts, or 9 kelvins. Since the interface lies towards the cold face, subtract these 9 kelvins from 28 degrees Celsius.

$$
T_{\mathrm I}=28\,{}^\circ\mathrm C-30\cdot0.30\,\mathrm K=19\,{}^\circ\mathrm C.
$$
