An object with $C=100\,\mathrm{J/K}$ starts at $20\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. It is brought into contact with a second object with $C=300\,\mathrm{J/K}$ until both reach $50\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. What was the initial temperature of the second object if the system is insulated?

- $40\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $60\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $80\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $140\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$

## Explanation

For an insulated system exchanging only heat internally, the signed contributions add to zero. Use positive capacities and signed changes.

$$
T_{2,i}=50\,{}^\circ\mathrm C+\frac{100}{300}\cdot(50-20)\,\mathrm K=60\,{}^\circ\mathrm C.
$$
