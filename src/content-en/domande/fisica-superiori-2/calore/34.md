An object with $C=100\,\mathrm{J/K}$ starts at $20\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. It is brought into contact with a second object with $C=300\,\mathrm{J/K}$ until both reach $50\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. What was the initial temperature of the second object if the system is insulated?

- $40\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $60\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $80\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $140\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$

## Explanation

The first body warms by 30 kelvins and absorbs $3000\,\mathrm J$. To supply this energy, the second body cools by 10 kelvins because its heat capacity is $300\,\mathrm{J/K}$. Its initial temperature is therefore 10 degrees Celsius above the final temperature shared by both bodies.

$$
T_{2,i}=50\,{}^\circ\mathrm C+\frac{100}{300}\cdot(50-20)\,\mathrm K=60\,{}^\circ\mathrm C.
$$
