A wall of thickness $0.20\,\mathrm m$ has $k=0.05\,\mathrm{W/(m\,K)}$ and area $4\,\mathrm{m^2}$. What is its thermal resistance?

- $1\,\mathrm{K/W}$
- $4\,\mathrm{K/W}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{K/W}$
- $0.04\,\mathrm{K/W}$

## Explanation

In steady state without lateral losses, heat transfer through the wall depends on area, conductivity and the temperature gradient.

$$
R=\frac{0.20}{0.05\cdot4}\,\mathrm{K/W}=1\,\mathrm{K/W}.
$$
