Two layers of resistance $0.20$ and $0.60\,\mathrm{K/W}$ are in series between surfaces differing by $24\,\mathrm K$. What power passes through?

- $30\,\mathrm W$
- $40\,\mathrm W$
- $120\,\mathrm W$
- $80\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

In steady state the same power crosses both layers. Their temperature drops add, so the total resistance is $0.80\,\mathrm{K/W}$. Dividing 24 kelvins by that resistance gives 30 watts. At that power the individual layers cause drops of 6 and 18 kelvins respectively.

$$
P=\frac{24}{0.20+0.60}\,\mathrm W=30\,\mathrm W.
$$
