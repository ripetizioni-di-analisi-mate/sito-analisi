A metal spoon stays still in hot soup and its handle warms. Which mechanism transfers energy along the metal?

- Bulk convection of the solid metal.
- Conduction.
- Radiation through a vacuum inside the spoon.
- Mass transfer from soup to handle.

## Explanation

The end in the soup is hotter than the handle. Interactions within the metal transfer energy along the spoon, so the handle warms while it remains still. This is conduction. Currents in the soup can bring energy to its surface, but they occur within the fluid surrounding the spoon.
