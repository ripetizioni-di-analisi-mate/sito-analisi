A real object is $p=27\,\mathrm{cm}$ from a concave mirror of focal length $f=9\,\mathrm{cm}$. Using the paraxial approximation, find the signed image distance. Choose the value closest to the result.

- $-13.5\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $6.75\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $13.5\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $18\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

Isolate $1/q$ by subtracting $1/p$ from $1/f$. Since the real object is beyond the focus, $1/q$ and $q$ are positive. That sign indicates a real image in front of the mirror.

$$
\begin{aligned}
q &= \frac{f p}{- f + p} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{\left(9\right) \left(27\right)}{- \left(9\right) + \left(27\right)} \\[6pt]
&\simeq 13.5\,\mathrm{cm}
\end{aligned}
$$

The image is closer to the vertex than the centre of curvature, which is eighteen centimetres away. Because the image distance is half the object distance, the magnification magnitude is below one.
