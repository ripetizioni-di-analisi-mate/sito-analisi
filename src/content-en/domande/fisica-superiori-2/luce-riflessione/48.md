When is the straight-ray model suitable?

- Always, even for very narrow slits.
- Only for reflections in a gas.
- Only if the ray is stationary.
- In a uniform medium away from obstacles comparable in size to the wavelength.

## Explanation

In a uniform medium the geometric model represents direction and sense. A small slit reveals wave behaviour.

In a uniform medium the index of refraction does not change along the path. For openings much wider than the wavelength, straight-line propagation is a good approximation.
