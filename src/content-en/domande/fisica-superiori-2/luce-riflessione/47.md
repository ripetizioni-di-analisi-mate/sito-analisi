For a concave mirror with focal length $10\,\mathrm{cm}$, what distance gives a real image of the same height magnitude as the object?

- $20\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $10\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $40\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

A real image with the same height magnitude must have magnification $-1$. This requires $p=q$. Both reciprocal terms in the equation are equal, so their sum is twice either term. The common distance is twice the focal length, at the centre of curvature.

$$
q=p,\qquad \frac1{10}=\frac2p,\qquad p=20\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
