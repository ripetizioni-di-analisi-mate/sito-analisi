For a concave mirror with focal length $10\,\mathrm{cm}$, what distance gives a real image of the same height magnitude as the object?

- $20\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $10\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $40\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

In the paraxial model, use the reciprocal equation with signed distances. The sign of $q$ distinguishes real and virtual images, and $M=-q/p$ gives orientation.

$$
q=p,\qquad \frac1{10}=\frac2p,\qquad p=20\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
