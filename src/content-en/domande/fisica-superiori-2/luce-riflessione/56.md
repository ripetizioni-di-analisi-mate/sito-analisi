The opening is made much wider while screen and object stay fixed. What geometrical effect is expected?

- Sharpness always increases without limit.
- The image becomes upright by symmetry.
- More rays from each point hit different regions and the image blurs.
- The screen plane turns.

## Explanation

The ideal pinhole selects one direction per object point. A wide opening admits rays from that same point in different directions.

The rays pass through different parts of the opening and spread over the screen rather than reaching one geometrical point. Each object point gives a larger patch.
