A pulse of light covers $1800\,\mathrm m$ in a vacuum. Use $c=3\cdot10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$. How long does it take? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $1.2\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $6\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $3\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $2.4\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{s}$.

## Explanation

The path is in a vacuum and speed is constant. Time is distance divided by $c$, in metres divided by metres per second.

$$
\begin{aligned}
t &= \frac{d}{c} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{\left(1800\right)}{\left(3\cdot10^{8}\right)} \\[6pt]
&\simeq 6\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{s}
\end{aligned}
$$
