For two objects both beyond the focus of the same concave mirror, which one has the image farther from the vertex?

- The one farther from the focus in all cases.
- The one closer to the focus.
- Both give the same distance.
- Neither gives a real image.

## Explanation

As positive p approaches f from above, the reciprocal of q approaches zero on the positive side. Distance q increases.
