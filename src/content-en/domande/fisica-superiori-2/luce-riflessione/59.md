A ray strikes a mirror in air. Which property is always required by the law of reflection?

- The normal is parallel to the mirror.
- The reflected ray always goes behind the mirror.
- The incident and reflected angles are always complementary.
- The incident ray, reflected ray and normal are coplanar.

## Explanation

The law of reflection requires coplanarity and equal angles to the normal.

At oblique incidence, the normal and the incident ray determine the plane for the outgoing ray. The two arms lie on opposite sides of the normal and make equal angles with it.
