A ray strikes a mirror in air. Which arrangement is consistent with the law of reflection?

- The normal is parallel to the mirror.
- The reflected ray always goes behind the mirror.
- The incident and reflected angles are always complementary.
- The incident ray, reflected ray and normal are coplanar.

## Explanation

The law of reflection requires coplanarity and equal angles to the normal.
