An object and its plane-mirror image are separated by $4.6\,\mathrm m$. How far from the mirror is the object?

- $4.6\,\mathrm m$
- $9.2\,\mathrm m$
- $2.3\,\mathrm m$
- $1.15\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The image lies at the symmetric point across the reflecting plane. Distances are measured from that plane.

$$
p=\frac{4.6}{2}\,\mathrm m=2.3\,\mathrm m.
$$
