An object and its plane-mirror image are separated by $4.6\,\mathrm m$. How far from the mirror is the object?

- $4.6\,\mathrm m$
- $9.2\,\mathrm m$
- $2.3\,\mathrm m$
- $1.15\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The object and image are symmetric, and the line between them is perpendicular to the mirror. The plane meets that line halfway. The stated $4.6\,\mathrm m$ includes the part in front of the plane and the part behind it, so the object distance is half the given value.

$$
p=\frac{4.6}{2}\,\mathrm m=2.3\,\mathrm m.
$$
