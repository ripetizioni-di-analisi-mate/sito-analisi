A spherical-mirror calculation combines p in centimetres and f in metres without conversion. What check exposes the problem?

- Any numerical values can be added.
- The equation always requires masses in kilograms.
- The reciprocal of a length is always measured in kelvins.
- Express both reciprocals in the same inverse length unit.

## Explanation

The terms in the equation can be added only after using the same unit of distance.
