The position graph shows a pulse returning to its source. What distance has it travelled by the end?

::grafico{linee="0,0 2.5,750 5,0" punti="2.5,750,pieno" xmin=0 xmax=5.5 ymin=0 ymax=850 nomi="t (μs),x (m)" titolo="Position measured from the source."}

- $750\,\mathrm m$
- $1500\,\mathrm m$
- $0\,\mathrm m$
- $2250\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

In the graph, the coordinate along the path increases from zero to 750 metres and then returns to zero. The pulse covers the same distance in each direction.

$$
s=(750+750)\,\mathrm m=1500\,\mathrm m.
$$

The slope is negative on the return leg because the coordinate decreases. Path length adds the two segment lengths, while final position minus initial position is zero.
