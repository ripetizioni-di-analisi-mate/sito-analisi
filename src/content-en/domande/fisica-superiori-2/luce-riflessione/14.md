A concave spherical mirror has radius of curvature $70\,\mathrm{cm}$. In the paraxial approximation, what is its focal length? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $140\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $35\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $70\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $17.5\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

For axial rays close to the axis, the focus is about halfway from the vertex to the centre of curvature. A concave mirror has a positive focal length in this convention.

$$
\begin{aligned}
f &= \frac{R}{2} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{\left(70\right)}{2} \\[6pt]
&\simeq 35\,\mathrm{cm}
\end{aligned}
$$
