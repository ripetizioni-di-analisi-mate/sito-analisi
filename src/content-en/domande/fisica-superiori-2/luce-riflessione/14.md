A concave mirror forms a real image on a screen. The image is half as tall as the object. The object and screen are in front of the mirror on the same line, with the screen between the object and the mirror. Their separation is $52.5\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the focal length $f$ in the paraxial model?

- $26.25\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $35\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $52.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $70\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The image has $|M|=\dfrac12$, so $q=\dfrac p2$. Since $p-q=52.5\,\mathrm{cm}$, this gives $p=105\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $q=52.5\,\mathrm{cm}$. In the mirror equation, the reciprocal of $q$ is twice the reciprocal of $p$. The focal length is therefore $\dfrac p3=35\,\mathrm{cm}$, positive as expected for a concave mirror.
