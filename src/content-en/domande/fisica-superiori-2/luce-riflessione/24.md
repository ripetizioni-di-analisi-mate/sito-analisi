A screen $20\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the pinhole receives an image of magnitude $10\,\mathrm{cm}$. If the object is $100\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the pinhole, what is its height?

- $2\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $20\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $100\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $50\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The two right triangles with vertex at the aperture are similar. The ratio of vertical sides equals the ratio of axial distances.

$$
h_o=10\frac{100}{20}\,\mathrm{cm}=50\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
