For a convex spherical mirror a real object is at $p=36\,\mathrm{cm}$ and focal length is $f=-12\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the transverse magnification in the paraxial model?

- $-0.25$
- $0.33333333$
- $0.25$
- $4$

## Explanation

First find the virtual image position. The denominator $p-f$ equals the object distance plus the focal-length magnitude. The signed distance is negative. The minus sign in the magnification then gives a positive number below one, as expected for an upright, reduced image.

$$
\begin{aligned}q&=\frac{-12\cdot36}{36+12}\,\mathrm{cm}=-9\,\mathrm{cm}\\M&=-\frac{-9}{36}=0.25.\end{aligned}
$$
