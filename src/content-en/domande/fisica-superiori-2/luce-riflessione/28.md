Two plane mirrors meet at a right angle. A ray in the plane perpendicular to their line of intersection reflects once from each mirror, as shown. What angle is formed by the initial and final propagation vectors?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 linee="0,0 0,2.65;0,0 5.9,0" vettori="3,2,0,1;0,1,3,0;3,0,5.7,0.9" punti="0,1,pieno;3,0,pieno" xmin=-0.6 xmax=6.3 ymin=-0.6 ymax=3 titolo="The ray reflects first from the vertical side and then from the horizontal side."}

- $0^\circ$
- $45^\circ$
- $90^\circ$
- $180^\circ$

## Explanation

With axes along the two sides shown, each reflection reverses the component perpendicular to its mirror and preserves the parallel component, because the angle of reflection equals the angle of incidence and the two rays lie on opposite sides of the normal. The first mirror reverses one component and the second reverses the other. The final vector is the opposite of the initial vector, giving an angle of $180^\circ$.
