The reflected ray makes $25^\circ$ with the surface. What is the angle of incidence measured from the normal?

- $25{}^\circ$
- $50{}^\circ$
- $115{}^\circ$
- $65{}^\circ$

## Explanation

The angles from the normal are equal for the two arms. The surface is perpendicular to the normal.

$$
\theta_i=\theta_r=90^\circ-25^\circ=65^\circ.
$$
