The real object and real inverted image of a concave mirror have equal height magnitude. What relation holds between p and f?

- $p=2f$
- $p=f$
- $p=f/2$
- $p=4f$

## Explanation

Equal magnitudes give q equal to p. The equation gives the focal reciprocal equal to twice the reciprocal of p.
