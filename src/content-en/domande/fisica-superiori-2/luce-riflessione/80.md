A concave mirror projects a real image onto a screen. Half of the reflecting aperture is covered, leaving a paraxial region that still receives rays from every point of the object. How does the image change?

- It stays complete and in the same position, with reduced brightness
- Half of the image disappears on the covered side
- The whole image shifts towards the mirror
- The whole image becomes smaller at the same distance

## Explanation

Rays from the same object point still converge at the position predicted by the equation. Covering the mirror removes some rays from each beam, while the object and image distances stay the same. The screen receives less light without losing half of the figure.
