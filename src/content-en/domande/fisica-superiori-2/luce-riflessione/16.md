Two pulses leave the same point along the same line in opposite directions. What is their separation after $4\,\mathrm{\mu s}$, taking $c=3\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$?

- $1200\,\mathrm m$
- $600\,\mathrm m$
- $0\,\mathrm m$
- $2400\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

After the stated time, each pulse is $1200\,\mathrm m$ from the origin. As they are on opposite half-lines, the segment between them passes through the origin and includes both distances. If they had travelled in the same direction, the positions would instead coincide.

$$
s=2\cdot3\cdot10^8\cdot4\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm m=2400\,\mathrm m.
$$
