To give a real image at $q=12\,\mathrm{cm}$ from an object at $p=24\,\mathrm{cm}$, what focal length is needed?

- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $36\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

In the paraxial model, use the reciprocal equation with signed distances. The sign of $q$ distinguishes real and virtual images, and $M=-q/p$ gives orientation.

$$
f=\frac{24\cdot12}{24+12}\,\mathrm{cm}=8\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
