To give a real image at $q=12\,\mathrm{cm}$ from an object at $p=24\,\mathrm{cm}$, what focal length is needed?

- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $36\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The object and the real image are both in front of the mirror, so both reciprocal terms are positive. Their sum is the reciprocal of the focal length. The positive result is consistent with a concave mirror, and its magnitude is smaller than both stated distances.

$$
f=\frac{24\cdot12}{24+12}\,\mathrm{cm}=8\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
