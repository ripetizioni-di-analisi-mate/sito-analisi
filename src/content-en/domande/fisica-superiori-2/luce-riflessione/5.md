An object is $1\,\mathrm m$ from a plane mirror. How far is the object from its image? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $2\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $1\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $0.5\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

The image is the point symmetric to the object about the mirror plane. It is as far behind as the object is in front, so the separation is the sum of those two distances.

$$
\begin{aligned}
s &= 2 d \\[6pt]
&= 2 \left(1\right) \\[6pt]
&\simeq 2\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$

The normal line joining the two points crosses the mirror perpendicularly. The mirror divides the segment into two halves of one metre each.
