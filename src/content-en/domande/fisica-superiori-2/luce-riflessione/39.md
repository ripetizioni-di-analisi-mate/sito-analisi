For a concave mirror $p=12\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $f=8\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find the signed image position.

- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $24\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-24\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The object is farther than one focal length and closer than two. The denominator $p-f$ is positive and small, so the position is real and beyond the centre of curvature. The value $24\,\mathrm{cm}$ is indeed greater than the $16\,\mathrm{cm}$ from vertex to centre.

$$
q=\frac{8\cdot12}{12-8}\,\mathrm{cm}=24\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
