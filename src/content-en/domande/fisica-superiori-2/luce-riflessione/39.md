For a concave mirror $p=12\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $f=8\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find the signed image position.

- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $24\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-24\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

In the paraxial model, use the reciprocal equation with signed distances. The sign of $q$ distinguishes real and virtual images, and $M=-q/p$ gives orientation.

$$
q=\frac{8\cdot12}{12-8}\,\mathrm{cm}=24\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
