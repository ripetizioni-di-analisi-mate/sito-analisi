A concave mirror forms a sharp image of a small source on a screen. The source is moved to the original image position and the screen to the original source position. What does the paraxial model predict?

- The image necessarily becomes virtual.
- All reflected rays emerge parallel.
- A sharp image forms on the screen again.
- The focal length of the same mirror doubles.

## Explanation

The rays can retrace their original paths in reverse. The object and image distances also enter the mirror equation symmetrically, so swapping them preserves focus.
