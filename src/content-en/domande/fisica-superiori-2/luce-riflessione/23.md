An object $32\,\mathrm{cm}$ tall is $160\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the pinhole. Where must the screen go for an image of magnitude $4.8\,\mathrm{cm}$?

- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $48\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $24\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $6\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The two right triangles with vertex at the aperture are similar. The ratio of vertical sides equals the ratio of axial distances.

$$
q=160\frac{4.8}{32}\,\mathrm{cm}=24\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
