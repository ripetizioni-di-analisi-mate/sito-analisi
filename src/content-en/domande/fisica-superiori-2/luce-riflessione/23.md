A $32\,\mathrm{cm}$ tall object stays fixed in front of the hole in a pinhole camera. Moving the screen $15\,\mathrm{cm}$ farther from the hole increases the image height in magnitude by $3\,\mathrm{cm}$. How far from the hole is the object?

- $40\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $80\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $160\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $240\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

With the object fixed, the ratio of the projected height magnitude to screen distance is constant. Similar triangles give the same ratio for the changes between the two screen positions. Each extra centimetre of screen distance adds one fifth of a centimetre to the image height. Multiply the object's height by five to find its distance from the hole.

$$
p=h_o\frac{\Delta q}{\Delta |h_i|}=32\,\mathrm{cm}\,\frac{15\,\mathrm{cm}}{3\,\mathrm{cm}}=160\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
