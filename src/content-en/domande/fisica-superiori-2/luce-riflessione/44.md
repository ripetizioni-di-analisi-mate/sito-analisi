A convex mirror gives $M=+0.20$ for an object at $p=40\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is $q$?

- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-200\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-8\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

In the paraxial model, use the reciprocal equation with signed distances. The sign of $q$ distinguishes real and virtual images, and $M=-q/p$ gives orientation.

$$
q=-Mp=-0.20\cdot40\,\mathrm{cm}=-8\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
