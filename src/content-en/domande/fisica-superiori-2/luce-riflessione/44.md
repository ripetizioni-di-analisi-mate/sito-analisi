A convex mirror gives $M=+0.20$ for an object at $p=40\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is $q$?

- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-200\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-8\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Positive magnification gives an upright image. To obtain the distance, multiply it by the object distance and change sign, as required by $q=-Mp$. The magnitude of $q$ is one fifth of $p$, and its sign places the image behind the mirror.

$$
q=-Mp=-0.20\cdot40\,\mathrm{cm}=-8\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
