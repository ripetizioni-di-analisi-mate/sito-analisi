A ray makes an angle of $30^\circ$ with a plane mirror’s surface. What is its angle of reflection from the normal? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $30{}^\circ$.
- $90{}^\circ$.
- $180{}^\circ$.
- $60{}^\circ$.

## Explanation

First find the angle from the normal by subtracting the given angle $\varphi$ from the surface from $90^\circ$. The law of reflection gives that same angle from the normal for the reflected ray.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\theta_r &= 90^\circ - \varphi \\[6pt]
&= 90^\circ - 30^\circ \\[6pt]
&= 60^\circ
\end{aligned}
$$

The requested angle uses the normal. Measuring the reflected ray from the surface would instead give thirty degrees again.
