A measured time includes a known detector delay in addition to light travel. How is the travel time alone found?

- $t_{\mathrm{volo}}=t_{\mathrm{misura}}+t_{\mathrm{ritardo}}$
- $t_{\mathrm{volo}}=2t_{\mathrm{misura}}$
- $t_{\mathrm{volo}}=t_{\mathrm{ritardo}}$
- $t_{\mathrm{volo}}=t_{\mathrm{misura}}-t_{\mathrm{ritardo}}$

## Explanation

Subtract the contribution that is not due to propagation. Travel time can then be multiplied by speed.
