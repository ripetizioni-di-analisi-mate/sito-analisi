In a pinhole camera the object and screen distances from the pinhole increase by $50\%$ and $20\%$ respectively. By what factor does image magnitude change?

- $1.25$
- $0.3$
- $0.8$
- $1.8$

## Explanation

The two right triangles with vertex at the aperture are similar. The ratio of vertical sides equals the ratio of axial distances.

$$
\frac{|h_{i,f}|}{|h_{i,i}|}=\frac{1.20}{1.50}=0.80.
$$
