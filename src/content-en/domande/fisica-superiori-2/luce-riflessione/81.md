In a convex mirror diagram, how is the point from which reflected rays appear to come found?

- Extend them only forward beyond the object.
- Intersect normals drawn parallel to the surface.
- Look for the highest point of the object.
- Extend the reflected rays backwards.

## Explanation

The actual rays diverge in front of the mirror. Their line extensions behind give a virtual intersection.
