A pulse leaves at time zero and travels straight in vacuum. One probe records it $600\,\mathrm m$ away and another records it at $1800\,\mathrm m$, with $c=3\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the interval between the signals?

- $2\,\mathrm{\mu s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{\mu s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{\mu s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{\mu s}$

## Explanation

In a vacuum path length divided by time is $c$. For several legs, add their path lengths, distinguishing path from displacement.

$$
\Delta t=\frac{1800-600}{3\cdot10^8}\,\mathrm s=4\,\mathrm{\mu s}.
$$
