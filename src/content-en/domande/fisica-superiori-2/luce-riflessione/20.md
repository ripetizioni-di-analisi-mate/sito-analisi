A pulse in a vacuum leaves the source, passes a sensor at $600\,\mathrm m$ and reflects $1800\,\mathrm m$ from the source. It passes the same sensor again before returning to the source. With $c=3\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$, how much time elapses between the two sensor readings?

- $2\,\mathrm{\mu s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{\mu s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{\mu s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{\mu s}$

## Explanation

Between the records, the pulse travels twice along the segment from sensor to reflector. Each segment is $(1800-600)\,\mathrm m=1200\,\mathrm m$, so $\Delta t=\dfrac{2400}{3\cdot10^8}\,\mathrm s=8\,\mathrm{\mu s}$. The path from the source to the sensor occurs before the first record.
