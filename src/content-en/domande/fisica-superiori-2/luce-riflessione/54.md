The top of an object lies above the axis of a pinhole camera. Where does it appear on the screen?

- Below the axis.
- Above the axis at the same height.
- In the aperture plane at every distance.
- Always outside the screen.

## Explanation

A ray passes from the upper point through the aperture and continues along the same line. Its height on the far side has the opposite sign.

The opening is the centre of projection. Moving the screen farther away puts the ray farther below the axis, increasing the magnitude of the image height.
