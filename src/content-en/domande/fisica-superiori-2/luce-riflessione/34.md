An observer and an object are both in front of the mirror on the same normal line, respectively $1.5\,\mathrm m$ and $0.8\,\mathrm m$ from the plane. How far from the observer does the image appear?

- $0.7\,\mathrm m$
- $2.3\,\mathrm m$
- $1.6\,\mathrm m$
- $1.5\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The image is $0.8\,\mathrm m$ behind the mirror. The observer and the image lie on the same normal, so the segment between them crosses the plane and equals the sum of both distances from it. The reflected rays appear to come from the point behind the mirror.

$$
d_{\mathrm{obs}}=(1.5+0.8)\,\mathrm m=2.3\,\mathrm m.
$$
