On one normal line, the observer stands $1.5\,\mathrm m$ from the mirror and the object $0.8\,\mathrm m$ in front of it. How far from the observer does the image appear?

- $0.7\,\mathrm m$
- $2.3\,\mathrm m$
- $1.6\,\mathrm m$
- $1.5\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The image lies at the symmetric point across the reflecting plane. Distances are measured from that plane.

$$
d_{\mathrm{oss}}=(1.5+0.8)\,\mathrm m=2.3\,\mathrm m.
$$
