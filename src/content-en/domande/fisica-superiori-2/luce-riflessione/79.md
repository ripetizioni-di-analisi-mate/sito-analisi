A mirror gives positive object distance $p$ and magnification $M=-3$. What is $q$ in terms of $p$?

- $q=-3p$
- $q=\dfrac{p}{3}$
- $q=-\dfrac{p}{3}$
- $q=3p$

## Explanation

The magnification equation $M=-\dfrac{q}{p}$ gives $q=-Mp$. Substituting $M=-3$ yields $q=3p$.

The magnification magnitude says the image is three times as tall. Its negative sign indicates an inverted figure and requires positive image distance when $p>0$.
