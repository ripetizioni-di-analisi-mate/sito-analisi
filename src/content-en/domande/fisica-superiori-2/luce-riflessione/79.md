A mirror gives positive object distance p and magnification M = −3. What is q in terms of p?

- $q=-3p$
- $q=p/3$
- $q=-p/3$
- $q=3p$

## Explanation

Isolate the minus sign along with p in the magnification equation. The distance obtained is positive.
