A parallel beam reflects in many directions from a rough wall. What is the cause in the geometrical model?

- The speed of light in vacuum is different for each ray.
- Each angle to the normal is fixed at 90°.
- Rough walls create a common real focus.
- Local normals point in different directions.

## Explanation

Each small segment reflects according to its own normal. Nonparallel normals yield nonparallel reflected rays.

A ray striking one tilted facet sees a different normal from a neighbouring ray. The equal-angle law holds at each facet and gives different outgoing directions across the beam.
