An object approaches a stationary plane mirror along its normal at $0.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. At what rate does the distance between the object and its image decrease?

- $0.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.15\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The image lies at the symmetric point across the reflecting plane. Distances are measured from that plane. The distance between object and image is twice the distance of the object from the mirror, so it decreases at twice the speed; call this speed $u$.

$$
u=2\cdot0.3\,\mathrm{m/s}=0.6\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$

Each second, the object moves $0.3\,\mathrm m$ closer to the plane, and the image does the same on the other side. Both changes contribute to their closing separation.
