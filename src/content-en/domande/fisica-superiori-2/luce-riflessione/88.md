An object approaches a stationary plane mirror along its normal at $0.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. At what rate does the distance between the object and its image decrease?

- $0.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.15\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The image lies at the symmetric point across the reflecting plane. Distances are measured from that plane.

$$
|\dot s|=2\cdot0.3\,\mathrm{m/s}=0.6\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
