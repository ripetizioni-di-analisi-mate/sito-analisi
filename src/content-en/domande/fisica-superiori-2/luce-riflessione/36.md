A stationary object is in front of a plane mirror which moves $0.25\,\mathrm m$ backwards parallel to its original plane, away from the object. How much further from the object does the image move?

- $0.25\,\mathrm m$
- $0.125\,\mathrm m$
- $1\,\mathrm m$
- $0.5\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The image lies at the symmetric point across the reflecting plane. Distances are measured from that plane.

$$
\Delta s=2\Delta p=2\cdot0.25\,\mathrm m=0.50\,\mathrm m.
$$
