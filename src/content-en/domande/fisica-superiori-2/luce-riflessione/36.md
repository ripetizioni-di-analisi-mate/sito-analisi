A stationary object is in front of a plane mirror. The mirror moves $0.25\,\mathrm m$ away along its normal, keeping the same orientation. How much does the distance between the image and the object increase?

- $0.25\,\mathrm m$
- $0.125\,\mathrm m$
- $1\,\mathrm m$
- $0.5\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

As the plane moves back, the object's distance from the new plane increases by $0.25\,\mathrm m$. The image must be equally far away on the other side of the new plane. Both halves of the separation grow. This change describes the image relative to the stationary object.

$$
\Delta s=2\Delta p=2\cdot0.25\,\mathrm m=0.50\,\mathrm m.
$$
