A concave spherical mirror has radius of curvature $60\,\mathrm{cm}$. In the paraxial approximation, what is its focal length? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $120\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $30\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $60\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $15\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

For axial rays close to the axis, the focus is about halfway from the vertex to the centre of curvature. A concave mirror has a positive focal length in this convention.

$$
\begin{aligned}
f &= \frac{R}{2} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{\left(60\right)}{2} \\[6pt]
&\simeq 30\,\mathrm{cm}
\end{aligned}
$$

The thirty centimetres are measured from the vertex towards the centre of curvature. A screen collecting a parallel axial beam goes at that position.
