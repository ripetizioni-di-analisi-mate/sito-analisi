What happens to reflected rays in the model when an on-axis object is at the focus of a concave mirror?

- They leave parallel.
- They all return to the focal point.
- They always form an image in the vertex plane.
- They stop at the surface.

## Explanation

With p equal to f, the reciprocal difference is zero. The rays do not meet at a finite distance.

Two different rays leave in the same direction in the model. A screen at finite distance can intercept them at different points, without forming a concentrated image of the object point.
