The object is between vertex and focus of a concave mirror. What image does the model give?

- Real, inverted and reduced.
- Real and equal in size.
- Virtual and inverted.
- Virtual, upright and enlarged.

## Explanation

If the object is closer than the focus, $p-f<0$ and the image has $q<0$. The magnification is $M=\dfrac{f}{f-p}$. The denominator is positive and smaller than $f$, so $M>1$. The ray extensions meet behind the mirror and the image height stays positive.
