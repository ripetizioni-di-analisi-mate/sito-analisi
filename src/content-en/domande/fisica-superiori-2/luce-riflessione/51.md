A beam spreads after leaving a slit comparable in width to the wavelength. Which model is needed to explain it?

- Two parallel plane mirrors.
- A wave model including diffraction.
- Straight rays passing only through the centre.
- A model of thermal expansion.

## Explanation

Diffraction becomes significant when aperture width and wavelength are comparable.
