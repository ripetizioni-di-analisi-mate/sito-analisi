A’s vacuum path is $1.2\,\mathrm{km}$ longer than B’s. With $c=3\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$, how much later does A arrive?

- $4\,\mathrm{\mu s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{\mu s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{\mu s}$
- $0.004\,\mathrm{\mu s}$

## Explanation

In a vacuum path length divided by time is $c$. For several legs, add their path lengths, distinguishing path from displacement.

$$
\Delta t=\frac{1200}{3\cdot10^8}\,\mathrm s=4\,\mathrm{\mu s}.
$$
