A point source illuminates a disc of diameter $8\,\mathrm{cm}$ located $20\,\mathrm{cm}$ from it. The screen lies $60\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the source and is parallel to the disc. What is the shadow diameter?

- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $16\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $24\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $32\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Rays grazing the edges of the disc mark the shadow boundaries. The projection triangles have their vertex at the source and parallel bases. The screen is three times as far from the source as the disc, so the projected diameter is three times the disc diameter.

$$
d_{\mathrm{shadow}}=8\,\mathrm{cm}\cdot\frac{60}{20}=24\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
