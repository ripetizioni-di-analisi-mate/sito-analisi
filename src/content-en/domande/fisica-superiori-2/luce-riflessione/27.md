A point source illuminates a disc of diameter $8\,\mathrm{cm}$ located $20\,\mathrm{cm}$ from it. The screen lies $60\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the source and is parallel to the disc. What is the shadow diameter?

- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $16\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $24\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $32\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The two right triangles with vertex at the aperture are similar. The ratio of vertical sides equals the ratio of axial distances.

$$
d_{\mathrm{ombra}}=8\,\mathrm{cm}\cdot\frac{60}{20}=24\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
