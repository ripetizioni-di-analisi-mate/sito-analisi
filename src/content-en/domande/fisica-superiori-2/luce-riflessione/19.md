In a one-way test a pulse covers $450\,\mathrm m$ in $1.50\,\mathrm{\mu s}$. What speed follows?

- $300000\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6.75\cdot10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\cdot10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.5\cdot10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The experiment measures one outward trip, so use the $450\,\mathrm m$ path directly. The time must be in seconds, giving $1.50\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm s$. The ratio has units of $\mathrm{m/s}$ and represents the speed along the measured path.

$$
v=\frac{450}{1.50\cdot10^{-6}}\,\mathrm{m/s}=3\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
