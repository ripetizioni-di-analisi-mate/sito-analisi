In a one-way test a pulse covers $450\,\mathrm m$ in $1.50\,\mathrm{\mu s}$. What speed follows?

- $300000\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6.75\cdot10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\cdot10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.5\cdot10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

In a vacuum path length divided by time is $c$. For several legs, add their path lengths, distinguishing path from displacement.

$$
v=\frac{450}{1.50\cdot10^{-6}}\,\mathrm{m/s}=3\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
