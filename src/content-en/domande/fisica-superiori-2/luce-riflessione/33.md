The object is $1.4\,\mathrm m$ from the plane and moves $0.20\,\mathrm m$ closer. What is the new object-to-image separation?

- $2.4\,\mathrm m$
- $1.2\,\mathrm m$
- $2.0\,\mathrm m$
- $2.8\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

After moving, the object is $1.20\,\mathrm m$ in front of the plane. Its symmetric point lies the same distance behind. Add the two segments, which meet at the mirror. Compared with the initial separation, each segment has shortened by $0.20\,\mathrm m$.

$$
s=2(1.4-0.20)\,\mathrm m=2.4\,\mathrm m.
$$
