The object is $1.4\,\mathrm m$ from the plane and moves $0.20\,\mathrm m$ closer. What is the new object-to-image separation?

- $2.4\,\mathrm m$
- $1.2\,\mathrm m$
- $2.6\,\mathrm m$
- $2.8\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The image lies at the symmetric point across the reflecting plane. Distances are measured from that plane.

$$
s=2(1.4-0.20)\,\mathrm m=2.4\,\mathrm m.
$$
