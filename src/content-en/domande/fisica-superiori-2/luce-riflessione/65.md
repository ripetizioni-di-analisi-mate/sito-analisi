Two object points on the same normal are at different distances in front of the plane. Which image point is further behind?

- The image of the nearer object point.
- Both are at the same distance.
- The image of the more distant object point.
- It cannot be found without a focal length.

## Explanation

Reflection preserves the magnitude of the coordinate perpendicular to the plane. A larger magnitude in front gives a larger magnitude behind.

To compare positions, always measure from the plane. An object point one metre in front produces an image one metre behind, while an object point farther away produces an image farther behind.
