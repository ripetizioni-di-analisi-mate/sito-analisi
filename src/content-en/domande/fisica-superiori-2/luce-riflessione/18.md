A target in vacuum is $1.5\,\mathrm{km}$ away. How long does the round trip take with $c=3\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$?

- $5\,\mathrm{\mu s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{\mu s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{\mu s}$
- $0.01\,\mathrm{\mu s}$

## Explanation

The timer runs from emission until the pulse returns. The target is $1500\,\mathrm m$ away, so the complete path is $3000\,\mathrm m$. Divide that distance by the speed of light in a vacuum. The time for a single leg would be half as long.

$$
t=\frac{2\cdot1500}{3\cdot10^8}\,\mathrm s=10\,\mathrm{\mu s}.
$$
