A target in vacuum is $1.5\,\mathrm{km}$ away. How long does the round trip take with $c=3\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$?

- $5\,\mathrm{\mu s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{\mu s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{\mu s}$
- $0.01\,\mathrm{\mu s}$

## Explanation

In a vacuum path length divided by time is $c$. For several legs, add their path lengths, distinguishing path from displacement.

$$
t=\frac{2\cdot1500}{3\cdot10^8}\,\mathrm s=10\,\mathrm{\mu s}.
$$
