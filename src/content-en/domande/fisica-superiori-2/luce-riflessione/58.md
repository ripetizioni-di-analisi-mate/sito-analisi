A ray arrives along the normal to a plane mirror. How does it leave?

- It leaves along the surface.
- It returns along that line in the opposite direction.
- It continues behind the mirror in the same direction.
- It leaves at 45° to the normal.

## Explanation

Incidence and reflection both make zero angle to the normal. The reflected arrow points back into the initial medium.
