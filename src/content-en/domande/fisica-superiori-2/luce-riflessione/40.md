A concave mirror has $f=12\,\mathrm{cm}$ and an object at $p=6\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its signed image distance?

- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The object lies between the focus and the vertex, making $p-f$ negative. Thus $q$ must be negative. The reflected rays diverge in front of the mirror and only their extensions meet. At half the focal length, the image distance from the plane has magnitude equal to the focal length.

$$
q=\frac{12\cdot6}{6-12}\,\mathrm{cm}=-12\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
