A concave mirror has $f=12\,\mathrm{cm}$ and an object at $p=6\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its signed image distance?

- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

In the paraxial model, use the reciprocal equation with signed distances. The sign of $q$ distinguishes real and virtual images, and $M=-q/p$ gives orientation.

$$
q=\frac{12\cdot6}{6-12}\,\mathrm{cm}=-12\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
