A concave mirror has a fixed focal length. What point does the image position approach as a real object moves far away?

- The centre of curvature.
- The vertex plane behind the mirror.
- The real focus.
- An infinite distance for every f.

## Explanation

The reciprocal of p becomes small compared with the focal reciprocal. The reciprocal of q approaches that of f.
