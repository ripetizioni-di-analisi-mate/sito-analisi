A concave spherical mirror focuses paraxial rays parallel to its axis $22\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the vertex. What is the magnitude of its radius of curvature?

- $44\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $11\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $22\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $66\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

For a spherical mirror in the paraxial model, the focus is halfway to the centre of curvature. The sign distinguishes the side.

$$
R\simeq2f=44\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
