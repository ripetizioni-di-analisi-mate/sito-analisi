A concave spherical mirror focuses paraxial rays parallel to its axis $22\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the vertex. What is the magnitude of its radius of curvature?

- $44\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $11\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $22\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $66\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Parallel rays near the axis meet at the focus, so the measured $22\,\mathrm{cm}$ is the focal-length magnitude. In the paraxial approximation, the focus is halfway from the vertex to the centre of curvature. Double the measured distance to obtain the radius.

$$
R\simeq2f=44\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
