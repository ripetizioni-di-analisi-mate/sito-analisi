A screen is moved from $30$ to $45\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the aperture while the object stays still. If the initial image magnitude is $8\,\mathrm{cm}$, what is the final magnitude?

- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $24\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $5.3333333\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The object and pinhole stay in the same places. On the more distant screen, the magnitude of the image height scales with distance from the pinhole. Moving from $30$ to $45\,\mathrm{cm}$ multiplies the initial height by $1.5$. The image remains inverted in both screen positions.

$$
|h_{i,2}|=8\frac{45}{30}\,\mathrm{cm}=12\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
