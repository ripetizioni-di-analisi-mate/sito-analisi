From which line is the angle of incidence measured at a mirror?

- From the tangent to the surface.
- From the local normal at the point of incidence.
- From the direction of any reflected ray.
- From the vertical axis of the room.

## Explanation

The reflection law compares angles to the normal at the point struck.

The surface itself is perpendicular to the normal. If an angle is measured from the surface, take its complement before applying the equality of incidence and reflection.
