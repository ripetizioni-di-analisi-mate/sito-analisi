A pulse goes towards a target in vacuum and returns after $6\,\mathrm{\mu s}$. Use $c=3\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. How far away is the target?

- $1800\,\mathrm m$
- $450\,\mathrm m$
- $900\,\mathrm m$
- $600\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The measured time includes two segments of equal length, one from the source to the target and one on the way back. Both are travelled at speed $c$ in a vacuum. Speed times time gives the entire trip. Halve it to obtain the target distance.

$$
d=\frac{3\cdot10^8\cdot6\cdot10^{-6}}2\,\mathrm m=900\,\mathrm m.
$$
