A concave mirror receives paraxial rays parallel to the principal axis. Where does a screen go to collect them?

- Behind the vertex at any distance.
- At the centre of curvature for every ray.
- At the distant source itself.
- In front of the vertex at the focal plane.

## Explanation

The real focus is where the reflected parallel beam meets. A screen in the focal plane intercepts that meeting.

The focal plane is perpendicular to the axis and passes through the real focus. A screen placed a little earlier or later intercepts a wider section of the beam.
