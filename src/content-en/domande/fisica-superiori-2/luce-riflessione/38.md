A convex mirror has radius of curvature of magnitude $54\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its signed focal length?

- $27\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-27\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-54\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-108\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

For a spherical mirror in the paraxial model, the focus is halfway to the centre of curvature. The sign distinguishes the side.

$$
f\simeq-\frac{54}{2}\,\mathrm{cm}=-27\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
