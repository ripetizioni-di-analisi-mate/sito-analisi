A convex mirror has radius of curvature of magnitude $54\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its signed focal length?

- $27\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-27\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-54\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-108\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Half the radius of curvature gives the focal-length magnitude, twenty-seven centimetres. For a convex mirror the reflected rays diverge and the focus is the apparent intersection behind the vertex plane. In the adopted sign convention, a position behind the mirror is negative.

$$
f\simeq-\frac{54}{2}\,\mathrm{cm}=-27\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
