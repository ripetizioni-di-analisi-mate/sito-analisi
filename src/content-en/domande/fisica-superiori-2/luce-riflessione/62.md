Why cannot a screen behind a plane mirror receive the image of a real object in front?

- Physical reflected rays stay in front, and only backward extensions meet behind.
- The image behind always has zero height.
- The plane mirror has a finite real focus in front.
- Ray arrows pass behind without reflecting.

## Explanation

Construct the image point by symmetry. It is virtual because the reflected beam does not pass through it.
