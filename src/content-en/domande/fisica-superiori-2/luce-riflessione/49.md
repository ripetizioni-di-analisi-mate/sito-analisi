What does the arrow on a ray in a diagram indicate?

- The direction of travel along the ray.
- Intensity in watts.
- Object depth.
- Lens aperture.

## Explanation

The segment shows the geometrical direction. The arrow distinguishes the two senses along it.

At a mirror, the incident segment points towards the reflection point and the reflected segment points outwards. The arrows show the directions of travel even when the rays lie on the same line.
