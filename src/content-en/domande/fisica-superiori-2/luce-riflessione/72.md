For a concave mirror with the object beyond the centre of curvature, which regime occurs?

- A virtual, upright and enlarged image behind the vertex.
- A real image beyond the centre and equal in size.
- A real, inverted and reduced image between focus and centre.
- No finite image.

## Explanation

For a concave mirror, write $q=f+\dfrac{f^2}{p-f}$. If $p>2f$, the denominator exceeds $f$. The second term is positive and less than $f$, so $f<q<2f$. The image is real and inverted. Since $q<p$, the magnification magnitude is below one.
