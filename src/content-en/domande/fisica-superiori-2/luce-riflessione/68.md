Why state the paraxial model when using a spherical mirror focal length?

- Because all far-off-axis rays diverge from zero.
- Because the focus depends on mirror mass.
- Because a single focus approximates rays near the axis.
- Because only unreflected rays have a direction.

## Explanation

Rays far from the axis meet differently inclined parts of the surface and can cross the axis at different points.

For rays at small aperture, the intersection is approximated halfway along the radius of curvature. Including marginal rays shifts the intersection and reduces the accuracy of the single-focus model.
