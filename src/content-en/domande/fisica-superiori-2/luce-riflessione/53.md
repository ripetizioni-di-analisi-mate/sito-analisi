To draw the geometrical edge of the shadow of an obstacle with a point source, which rays are used?

- Rays from the source that just graze the edge.
- Only the ray aimed at the opaque centre.
- Rays that leave the screen and do not go towards the source.
- Rays parallel to every edge.

## Explanation

The grazing rays separate directions that meet the obstacle from directions that pass it.

On a screen beyond the obstacle, the intersections with these rays bound the shadow. A point inside it lines up with the source through the opaque obstacle, which blocks the corresponding ray.
