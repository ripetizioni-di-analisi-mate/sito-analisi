The mirror turns by $8^\circ$ in the ray plane about the point of incidence. The incident ray stays fixed. How much does the reflected ray turn?

- $8{}^\circ$
- $4{}^\circ$
- $16{}^\circ$
- $32{}^\circ$

## Explanation

The point of incidence and the incoming ray stay fixed. The normal rotates, while still bisecting the opening between the arms. The reflected arm changes orientation by twice the change in the normal, so it rotates through sixteen degrees.

$$
\Delta\theta_r=2\cdot8^\circ=16^\circ.
$$
