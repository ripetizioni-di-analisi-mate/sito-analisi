A parallel beam strikes a smooth plane surface. Which property of the incident and reflected beams holds?

- All rays return towards one finite focus.
- Reflected directions are random.
- The reflected beam is also parallel.
- All reflected rays are perpendicular to the plane even at oblique incidence.

## Explanation

Local normals have the same orientation. Every ray undergoes the same angular transformation.
