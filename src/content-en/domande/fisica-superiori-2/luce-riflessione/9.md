The timer records a pulse returning to the detector at the source $7\,\mathrm{\mu s}$ after emission. The circuit adds a fixed delay of $1\,\mathrm{\mu s}$. In a vacuum, with $c=3\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the total path length $s$ of the pulse?

- $1800\,\mathrm m$
- $2100\,\mathrm m$
- $900\,\mathrm m$
- $2400\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The reading includes an interval after the signal has already arrived. The flight lasts $(7-1)\,\mathrm{\mu s}=6\,\mathrm{\mu s}$, so $s=3\cdot10^8\cdot6\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm m=1800\,\mathrm m$. The path includes both the outward and return lengths. Half that value would give the distance to the target.
