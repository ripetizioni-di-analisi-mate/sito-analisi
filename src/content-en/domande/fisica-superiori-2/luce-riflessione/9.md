Light travels in a vacuum for $6\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm s$. Use $c=3\cdot10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What distance does it cover? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $1800\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $3600\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $900\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $7200\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

In the model light travels $c$ metres each second. Multiplying by the time in seconds gives the path in metres. The given time is one way.

$$
\begin{aligned}
s &= c t \\[6pt]
&= \left(3\cdot10^{8}\right) \left(6\cdot10^{-6}\right) \\[6pt]
&\simeq 1800\,\mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
