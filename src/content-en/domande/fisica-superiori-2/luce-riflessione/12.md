For a plane mirror, the angle between the incoming and outgoing propagation vectors is $70^\circ$. What is the angle of reflection $\theta_r$ measured from the normal?

- $35^\circ$
- $70^\circ$
- $110^\circ$
- $55^\circ$

## Explanation

The incoming vector points towards the mirror. Its arm traced outwards from the point of incidence points the other way. The opening between the two arms is therefore $180^\circ-70^\circ=110^\circ$. The normal divides it into two equal parts, and the angle of reflection is $\dfrac{110^\circ}{2}=55^\circ$.
