A ray makes an angle of $35^\circ$ with a plane mirror’s surface. What is its angle of reflection from the normal? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $35{}^\circ$.
- $70{}^\circ$.
- $27.5{}^\circ$.
- $55{}^\circ$.

## Explanation

First find the angle from the normal by subtracting the given angle from the surface from $90^\circ$. The law of reflection gives that same angle from the normal for the reflected ray.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\theta_r &= 90 - A \\[6pt]
&= 90 - \left(35\right) \\[6pt]
&\simeq 55{}^\circ
\end{aligned}
$$
