Where is the centre of curvature of a concave mirror?

- Behind the vertex plane in the unlit medium.
- At the vertex for every ray.
- On the screen wherever it is placed.
- On the front side containing the real focus.

## Explanation

The sphere centre is on the reflecting inner side. The paraxial focus is between the centre and the vertex.
