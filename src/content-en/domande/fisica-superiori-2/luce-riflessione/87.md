A ray arrives at $58^\circ$ from the normal. What is the angle between the surface and the reflected ray?

- $58{}^\circ$
- $116{}^\circ$
- $148{}^\circ$
- $32{}^\circ$

## Explanation

The angles from the normal are equal for the two arms. The surface is perpendicular to the normal.

$$
\varphi=90^\circ-58^\circ=32^\circ.
$$

The reflected ray retains the angle of fifty-eight degrees to the normal. When the reference changes from the normal to the surface, the complementary angles must add to a right angle.
