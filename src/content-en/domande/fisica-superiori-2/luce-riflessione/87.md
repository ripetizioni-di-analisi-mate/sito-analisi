An incident ray is $58^\circ$ from the normal. What is the angle between the surface and the reflected ray?

- $58{}^\circ$
- $116{}^\circ$
- $148{}^\circ$
- $32{}^\circ$

## Explanation

The angles from the normal are equal for the two arms. The surface is perpendicular to the normal.

$$
\varphi=90^\circ-58^\circ=32^\circ.
$$
