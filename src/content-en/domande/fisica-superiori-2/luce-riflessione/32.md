The incident ray arrives at $40^\circ$ from the normal. What is the angle between the incoming and outgoing propagation vectors?

- $80{}^\circ$
- $40{}^\circ$
- $50{}^\circ$
- $100{}^\circ$

## Explanation

From the point of incidence, the arm towards the source and the outgoing arm form an angle of $80^\circ$. The incoming vector points opposite to the arm towards the source. Comparing the two propagation arrows therefore uses the supplement of that opening.

$$
\psi=180^\circ-2\cdot40^\circ=100^\circ.
$$
