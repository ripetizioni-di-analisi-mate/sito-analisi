The incident ray arrives at $40^\circ$ from the normal. What is the angle between the incoming and outgoing propagation vectors?

- $80{}^\circ$
- $40{}^\circ$
- $50{}^\circ$
- $100{}^\circ$

## Explanation

The incoming propagation vector points towards the mirror. The requested angle is therefore supplementary to the opening between the two ray arms.

$$
\psi=180^\circ-2\cdot40^\circ=100^\circ.
$$
