What does a negative focal length mean for a convex mirror?

- The real focus lies in front at a negative height.
- The virtual focus is behind the mirror.
- The centre of curvature coincides with the vertex.
- The distance has zero magnitude.

## Explanation

The convention assigns negative signs to positions behind the vertex plane. The reflected rays diverge from an apparent point.
