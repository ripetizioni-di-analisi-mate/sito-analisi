An object is $27\,\mathrm{cm}$ from a convex mirror of focal length $f=-9\,\mathrm{cm}$. A camera is $30\,\mathrm{cm}$ in front of the mirror, nearly on the axis. How far from the camera is the reflected image on which it must be focused?

- $23.25\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $6.75\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $57\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $36.75\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

From the mirror equation $\dfrac{1}{q}=\dfrac{1}{f}-\dfrac{1}{p}$ we get
$$
\frac{1}{q}=-\frac{1}{9}-\frac{1}{27}=-\frac{4}{27}\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}},
$$
so $q=-6.75\,\mathrm{cm}$. The negative sign indicates a virtual image, $6.75\,\mathrm{cm}$ behind the mirror. The reflected rays entering the camera diverge as if they came from that point, so the camera must focus on the image. Since the camera and the image are on opposite sides of the mirror, their distance is $30\,\mathrm{cm}+6.75\,\mathrm{cm}=36.75\,\mathrm{cm}$. The value $23.25\,\mathrm{cm}$ is obtained by wrongly placing the image in front of the mirror.
