A convex mirror has focal length $f=-9\,\mathrm{cm}$ and a real object is at $p=27\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find the signed image distance using the convention in the theory. Choose the value closest to the result.

- $6.75\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $-13.5\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $-3.375\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $-6.75\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

For the convex mirror the focal length is negative. Subtracting the positive reciprocal of $p$ from the negative reciprocal of $f$ gives a negative reciprocal for $q$. The image is behind the mirror.

$$
\begin{aligned}
q &= \frac{f p}{- f + p} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{\left(-9\right) \left(27\right)}{- \left(-9\right) + \left(27\right)} \\[6pt]
&\simeq -6.75\,\mathrm{cm}
\end{aligned}
$$
