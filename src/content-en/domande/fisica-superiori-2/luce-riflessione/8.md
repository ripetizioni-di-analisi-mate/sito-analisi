An object $27$ cm from a convex mirror of focal length $-9$ cm forms a virtual image $6.75$ cm behind the mirror. A camera is $30$ cm in front of the mirror, nearly on the axis. At what apparent distance should it focus to photograph the reflected image, ignoring its small lateral offset?

- $23.25$ cm
- $6.75$ cm
- $57$ cm
- $36.75$ cm

## Explanation

The reflected rays entering the camera diverge as if they came from the image point behind the mirror. Camera and image are on opposite sides, so their apparent separation is $30+6.75=36.75$ cm.
