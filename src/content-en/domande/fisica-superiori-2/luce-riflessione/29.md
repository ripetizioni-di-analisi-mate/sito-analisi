For incidence $32^\circ$ from the normal, what is the angle between the two ray arms above the mirror?

- $64{}^\circ$
- $32{}^\circ$
- $58{}^\circ$
- $116{}^\circ$

## Explanation

The angles from the normal are equal for the two arms. The surface is perpendicular to the normal.

$$
\theta_{\mathrm{rami}}=\theta_i+\theta_r=64^\circ.
$$
