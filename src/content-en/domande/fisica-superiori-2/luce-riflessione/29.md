For incidence $32^\circ$ from the normal, what is the angle between the two ray arms above the mirror?

- $64{}^\circ$
- $32{}^\circ$
- $26^\circ$
- $116{}^\circ$

## Explanation

The normal lies between the two arms traced from the point of incidence into the medium above the plane. Both make $32^\circ$ with that line, so add the two angles to get the opening. The requested angle is between the arms drawn outwards from the same origin.

$$
\theta_{\mathrm{between}}=\theta_i+\theta_r=64^\circ.
$$
