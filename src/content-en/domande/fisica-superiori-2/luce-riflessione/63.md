An object moves parallel to a plane mirror while keeping a fixed distance from the plane. How does its image move?

- It moves parallel to the plane by the same vector.
- It moves only closer to the plane.
- It moves away from the plane by the translation distance.
- It stays still.

## Explanation

Reflection affects only the coordinate normal to the plane. The parallel change is carried over unchanged to the image.

For example, a movement of $0.3\,\mathrm m$ upwards is reproduced in the same direction and by the same amount. Both points remain at their original distance from the plane.
