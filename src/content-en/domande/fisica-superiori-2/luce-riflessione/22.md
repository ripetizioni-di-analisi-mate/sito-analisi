In a pinhole camera the object is $150\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the aperture and the screen $30\,\mathrm{cm}$ away. What fraction of object height is the image magnitude?

- $5$
- $0.2$
- $0.33$
- $0.4$

## Explanation

The line from the top of the object to the opening continues across the axis. The triangles are similar, so the required fraction is the screen-to-opening distance divided by the object-to-opening distance. The screen is closer, so a fraction below one is expected.

$$
\frac{|h_i|}{h_o}=\frac{30}{150}=\frac15.
$$
