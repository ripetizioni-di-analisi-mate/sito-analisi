In a pinhole camera the object is $150\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the aperture and the screen $30\,\mathrm{cm}$ away. What fraction of object height is the image magnitude?

- $5$
- $0.2$
- $0.33333333$
- $0.4$

## Explanation

The two right triangles with vertex at the aperture are similar. The ratio of vertical sides equals the ratio of axial distances.

$$
\frac{|h_i|}{h_o}=\frac{30}{150}=\frac15.
$$
