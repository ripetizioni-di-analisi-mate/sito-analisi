What physical meaning does negative M have in the mirror model?

- The image length is physically negative.
- The image necessarily has greater magnitude.
- The image is inverted relative to the object.
- The object is beyond the vertical axis.

## Explanation

M is a signed ratio of heights. Magnitude compares sizes and sign describes orientation.
