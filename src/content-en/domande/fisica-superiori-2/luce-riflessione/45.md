A convex mirror forms $q=-6\,\mathrm{cm}$ for an object at $p=18\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the focal length?

- $-9\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $9\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-18\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-4.5\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

In the paraxial model, use the reciprocal equation with signed distances. The sign of $q$ distinguishes real and virtual images, and $M=-q/p$ gives orientation.

$$
f=\frac{18\cdot(-6)}{18-6}\,\mathrm{cm}=-9\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
