A convex mirror forms $q=-6\,\mathrm{cm}$ for an object at $p=18\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the focal length?

- $-9\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $9\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-18\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-4.5\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The virtual image has negative signed distance. To find the focal length, add the positive reciprocal of $p$ to the negative reciprocal of $q$. The second has larger magnitude because the image is closer to the plane. The sum is negative, as expected for a convex mirror.

$$
f=\frac{18\cdot(-6)}{18-6}\,\mathrm{cm}=-9\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
