Two light pulses leave the same point in the same direction in vacuum, $2.0\,\mathrm{\mu s}$ apart in time. Using $c=3.0\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what distance separates them after the second pulse leaves?

- $600\,\mathrm m$
- $1200\,\mathrm m$
- $300\,\mathrm m$
- $0\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

In a vacuum path length divided by time is $c$. For several legs, add their path lengths, distinguishing path from displacement.

$$
s=c\Delta t=3.0\cdot10^8\cdot2.0\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm m=600\,\mathrm m.
$$
