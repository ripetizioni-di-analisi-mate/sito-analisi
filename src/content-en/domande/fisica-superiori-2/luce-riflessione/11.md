In a pinhole camera the screen is $24\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the opening. In the object plane $120\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the opening, point $A$ is at height $+35\,\mathrm{cm}$ and point $B$ is at height $-15\,\mathrm{cm}$ relative to the axis. What is the vertical distance $h$ between their images?

::grafico{assi=0 linee="-120,0 24,0;-120,-20 -120,43;24,-15 24,15" vettori="-120,35,24,-7;-120,-15,24,3" punti="-120,35,pieno;-120,-15,pieno;0,0,pieno;24,-7,pieno;24,3,pieno" etichette="-127,39,A;-127,-10,B;-8,3,F;25,-10,A′;25,6,B′" xmin=-134 xmax=42 ymin=-23 ymax=49 titolo="The rays from A and B pass through F and reach opposite sides of the axis."}

- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $7\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $10\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $50\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The triangles at the opening give a scale factor of $\dfrac{24}{120}=\dfrac15$. The ray from $A$ crosses from above the axis to below it and reaches height $-7\,\mathrm{cm}$. The ray from $B$ reaches $+3\,\mathrm{cm}$. Their heights have opposite signs, so the distance between them is $(3-(-7))\,\mathrm{cm}=10\,\mathrm{cm}$.
