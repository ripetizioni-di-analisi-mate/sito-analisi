A real object $4\,\mathrm{cm}$ tall is at $p=40\,\mathrm{cm}$ and has a real image at $q=15\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its signed image height?

- $1.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-0.375\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-1.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-10.667\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

In the paraxial model, use the reciprocal equation with signed distances. The sign of $q$ distinguishes real and virtual images, and $M=-q/p$ gives orientation.

$$
h_i=-\frac{15}{40}\cdot4\,\mathrm{cm}=-1.5\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
