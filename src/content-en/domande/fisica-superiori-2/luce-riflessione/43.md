A real object $4\,\mathrm{cm}$ tall is at $p=40\,\mathrm{cm}$ and has a real image at $q=15\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its signed image height?

- $1.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-0.375\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-1.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $-10.667\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The magnification magnitude is the ratio of $15$ to $40$ and multiplies the object height. Because $p$ and $q$ are both positive, the minus sign in the magnification equation makes image height negative. The object's tip above the axis corresponds to an image tip below it.

$$
h_i=-\frac{15}{40}\cdot4\,\mathrm{cm}=-1.5\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
