Screen distance remains fixed while object distance from the pinhole changes from $180$ to $120\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the growth factor in projected height?

- $1.5$
- $0.67$
- $0.5$
- $3$

## Explanation

Object height and screen distance stay fixed, so only the denominator of the image-height expression changes. The ratio of new height to initial height is the inverse ratio of the distances, with $180$ on top and $120$ below. Moving the object closer increases the image magnitude.

$$
\frac{|h_{i,2}|}{|h_{i,1}|}=\frac{180}{120}=1.5.
$$
