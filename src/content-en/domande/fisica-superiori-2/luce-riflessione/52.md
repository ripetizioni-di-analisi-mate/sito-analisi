The Sun is not a point source. What feature of a shadow follows from its angular extent?

- A boundary that is always infinitely thin.
- A region receiving light from part of the source.
- The absence of any shadow.
- An image that must be upright.

## Explanation

From a penumbra some parts of the source are hidden and other parts visible.

At that point in the shadow only part of the Sun's disc remains visible. The more of the disc one can see, the more light reaches the point. In full shadow the entire disc is hidden.
