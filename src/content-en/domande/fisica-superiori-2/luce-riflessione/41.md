A mirror with $f=9\,\mathrm{cm}$ projects a real image at $q=18\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the object distance?

- $18\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $9\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $27\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $6\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The screen receives a real image at two focal lengths. Subtracting the reciprocal of $q$ from that of $f$ gives the reciprocal of $p$. This yields $p=q$. Object and image share the same axial position and have opposite transverse orientations.

$$
p=\frac{9\cdot18}{18-9}\,\mathrm{cm}=18\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
