A mirror with $f=9\,\mathrm{cm}$ projects a real image at $q=18\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the object distance?

- $18\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $9\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $27\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $6\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

In the paraxial model, use the reciprocal equation with signed distances. The sign of $q$ distinguishes real and virtual images, and $M=-q/p$ gives orientation.

$$
p=\frac{9\cdot18}{18-9}\,\mathrm{cm}=18\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
