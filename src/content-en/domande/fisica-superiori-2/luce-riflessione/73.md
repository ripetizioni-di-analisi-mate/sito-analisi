For a concave mirror and p between f and 2f, where does the real image form?

- Behind the vertex.
- Between vertex and focus.
- Beyond the centre of curvature.
- Exactly at the focus for all p.

## Explanation

For $f<p<2f$, the denominator $p-f$ is positive and less than $f$. In $q=f+\dfrac{f^2}{p-f}$, the second term is therefore greater than $f$. This gives $q>2f$, beyond the centre of curvature. The positive sign shows that a screen in front of the mirror can receive the image.
