For a concave mirror and p between f and 2f, where does the real image form?

- Behind the vertex.
- Between vertex and focus.
- Beyond the centre of curvature.
- Exactly at the focus for all p.

## Explanation

In q equal to fp divided by p minus f, the denominator is positive but less than f. The result exceeds two focal lengths.
