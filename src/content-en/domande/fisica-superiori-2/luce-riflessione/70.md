With mirror type unchanged, the magnitude of R halves. How does the magnitude of f change?

- $|f|$ doubles.
- $|f|$ halves.
- $|f|$ quadruples.
- $|f|$ is unchanged.

## Explanation

In the paraxial model the magnitudes are proportional. Changing R by a factor changes the magnitude of f by that factor.

For example, going from a radius of $60$ to $30\,\mathrm{cm}$ changes the focal-length magnitude from $30$ to $15\,\mathrm{cm}$. The sign stays the one associated with the mirror type.
