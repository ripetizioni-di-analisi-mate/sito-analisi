Points $A$ and $B$ in front of a plane mirror are $0.48\,\mathrm m$ and $0.96\,\mathrm m$ from the plane respectively. Their perpendicular projections onto the mirror are $1.92\,\mathrm m$ apart. What is the geometric distance $s$ from $A$ to the image of $B$?

- $2.4\,\mathrm m$
- $1.44\,\mathrm m$
- $1.92\,\mathrm m$
- $3.36\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The image of $B$ lies behind the plane. The difference between the perpendicular coordinates is therefore $(0.48+0.96)\,\mathrm m=1.44\,\mathrm m$. The parallel component stays at $1.92\,\mathrm m$. The two legs are perpendicular, so $s=\sqrt{1.44^2+1.92^2}\,\mathrm m=2.4\,\mathrm m$.
