In a diagram the incident and reflected arms above the plane are $150^\circ$ apart. What is the angle of a ray from the plane?

- $75{}^\circ$
- $15{}^\circ$
- $30{}^\circ$
- $60{}^\circ$

## Explanation

The angles from the normal are equal for the two arms. The surface is perpendicular to the normal.

$$
\varphi=90^\circ-\frac{150^\circ}{2}=15^\circ.
$$
