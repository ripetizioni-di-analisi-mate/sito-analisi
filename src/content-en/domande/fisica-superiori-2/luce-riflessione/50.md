A ray is deflected and returns to its starting point. Which quantity can be positive even though the final position equals the starting position?

- The magnitude of final displacement.
- The total path length.
- The distance between starting point and end point.
- The final vertical coordinate if the origin was the start.

## Explanation

Path length includes every leg travelled. Displacement magnitude measures only the separation between start and finish.
