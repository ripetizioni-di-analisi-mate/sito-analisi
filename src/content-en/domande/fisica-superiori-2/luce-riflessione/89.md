A concave mirror with $f=16\,\mathrm{cm}$ has an object inside its focal length at $p=8\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is M?

- $-2$
- $0.5$
- $2$
- $-0.5$

## Explanation

In the paraxial model, use the reciprocal equation with signed distances. The sign of $q$ distinguishes real and virtual images, and $M=-q/p$ gives orientation.

$$
\begin{aligned}q&=\frac{16\cdot8}{8-16}\,\mathrm{cm}=-16\,\mathrm{cm}\\ M&=-\frac{-16}{8}=2.\end{aligned}
$$
