Which property holds for the image of a real object in a convex mirror in the paraxial model?

- It is always real at the focal plane.
- It is always real and enlarged.
- It is virtual and reduced.
- It is inverted and always the same size as the object.

## Explanation

Writing $f=-|f|$ gives magnification $M=\dfrac{|f|}{p+|f|}$. For a real object, the denominator exceeds the numerator, and the ratio lies between zero and one. The image is upright and reduced. Since $q=-Mp$, the image lies behind the mirror and is virtual.
