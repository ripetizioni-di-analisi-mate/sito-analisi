A mirror image has positive q. What test with a screen is possible?

- Place a screen behind to catch the extensions.
- No screen can receive a real image.
- A screen belongs at the focus for every object position.
- A screen in front at the image plane can receive the rays.

## Explanation

A positive sign indicates a physical meeting of rays in front of the mirror. A suitable screen can intercept it.
