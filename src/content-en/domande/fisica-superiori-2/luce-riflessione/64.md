An upright object faces a vertical plane mirror. What are the image height and orientation?

- The same height and upright.
- Half the height and inverted.
- Double the height and upright.
- The same height and inverted.

## Explanation

Reflection in the plane changes the coordinate perpendicular to it. Vertical height remains unchanged.
