Object and screen distances from the aperture are both doubled. What is the ratio of new to old projected height magnitude?

- $2$
- $1$
- $4$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

The ratio q over p is unchanged. Similar triangles preserve the height ratio.

In the formula, both the numerator and denominator of the scale factor are multiplied by two. Their ratio, and hence the magnitude of the image height, stays the same.
