A pinhole camera has its screen $14\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the pinhole. An object $180\,\mathrm{cm}$ tall is $280\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the pinhole. What is the magnitude of the image height? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $3600\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $4.5\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $9\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $18\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

The triangles with vertex at the pinhole are similar. Use the ratio of screen distance to object distance. The vertical sides lie on opposite sides of the axis, so the image is inverted.

$$
\begin{aligned}
h &= \frac{H q}{p} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{\left(180\right) \left(14\right)}{\left(280\right)} \\[6pt]
&\simeq 9\,\mathrm{cm}
\end{aligned}
$$
