A convex mirror with focal length $f=-20\,\mathrm{cm}$ forms an image at $q=-8\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find the object distance.

- $13.333333\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $20\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

In the paraxial model, use the reciprocal equation with signed distances. The sign of $q$ distinguishes real and virtual images, and $M=-q/p$ gives orientation.

$$
p=\frac{(-20)(-8)}{-8-(-20)}\,\mathrm{cm}=\frac{40}{3}\,\mathrm{cm}\simeq13.33\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
