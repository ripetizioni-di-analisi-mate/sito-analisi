A convex mirror with focal length $f=-20\,\mathrm{cm}$ forms an image at $q=-8\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find the object distance.

- $\dfrac{40}{3}\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $20\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

To isolate $p$, subtract the reciprocal of $q$ from that of $f$. The reciprocal of $q$ is negative and larger in magnitude because the image is closer to the vertex than the focus. The difference is positive, as required for a real object in front of the mirror.

$$
p=\frac{(-20)(-8)}{-8-(-20)}\,\mathrm{cm}=\frac{40}{3}\,\mathrm{cm}\simeq13.33\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
