Which comparison decides if ice remains when it starts at zero in warmer water in an insulated vessel of negligible heat capacity?

- $T_a$ and $L_f$.
- $m_a$ and $c_a$.
- $m_a c_a(T_a-0)$ and $m_gL_f$.
- $m_a c_a T_a$ and $m_g c_g T_a$ in every case.

## Explanation

The water can release energy by cooling to zero. The available amount is $m_a c_a(T_a-0)$. The threshold to melt all the ice already at zero is $m_gL_f$. If the first amount is smaller, ice remains at equilibrium and the temperature is zero.
