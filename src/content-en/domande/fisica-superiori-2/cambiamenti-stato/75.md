Which comparison decides if ice remains when it starts at zero in warmer water in an insulated vessel of negligible heat capacity?

- $T_a$ and $L_f$.
- $m_a$ and $c_a$.
- $m_a c_a(T_a-0)$ and $m_gL_f$.
- $m_a c_a T_a$ and $m_g c_g T_a$ in every case.

## Explanation

Compare two energies. The first can be released on cooling to zero, and the second is needed for complete melting.
