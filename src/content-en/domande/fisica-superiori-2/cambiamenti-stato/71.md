A liquid cools to the freezing point and then freezes. How do the two terms combine if $Q$ is positive inward?

- One is positive and the other zero.
- Both are positive.
- They always have equal magnitude and opposite signs.
- Both are negative and add.

## Explanation

In both stages the sample releases energy. The first term uses the negative temperature change and the second has the sign of freezing.
