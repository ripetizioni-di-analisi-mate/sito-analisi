A liquid cools to the freezing point and then freezes. How do the two terms combine if $Q$ is positive inward?

- One is positive and the other zero.
- Both are positive.
- They always have equal magnitude and opposite signs.
- Both are negative and add.

## Explanation

The liquid loses energy as its temperature falls, so the contribution found with its specific heat capacity is negative. During freezing the sample gives up additional heat, $-mL_f$. The two terms have the same sign and their sum gives the heat transfer for the sample.
