A pure sample melts with the same useful power in two trials. The second uses twice the starting mass at zero. How does the plateau duration change?

- It halves.
- It remains equal at the same temperature.
- It doubles.
- It quadruples.

## Explanation

The energy required for melting is proportional to mass. With the same energy-versus-time slope, it takes twice as long.
