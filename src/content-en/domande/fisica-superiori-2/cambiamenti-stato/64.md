A pure sample melts with the same useful power in two trials. The second uses twice the starting mass at zero. How does the plateau duration change?

- It halves.
- It remains equal at the same temperature.
- It doubles.
- It quadruples.

## Explanation

At the same pressure, the latent heat per kilogram is the same in both trials. The second mass is twice as large, so melting takes twice the energy. Since the same useful energy is supplied each second, the plateau against time lasts twice as long.
