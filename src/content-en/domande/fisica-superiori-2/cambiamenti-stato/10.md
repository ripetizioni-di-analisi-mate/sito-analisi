Supply $20040\,\mathrm J$ to ice at $0\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}$ as it melts. Use $L_f=334000\,\mathrm{J/kg}$ and assume enough ice is available. What mass melts? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $0.12\,\mathrm{kg}$.
- $0.06\,\mathrm{kg}$.
- $0.03\,\mathrm{kg}$.
- $0.24\,\mathrm{kg}$.

## Explanation

Energy is supplied during melting. Divide joules by the latent heat in joules per kilogram to obtain the melted mass in kilograms. There is enough ice for the result to be possible.

$$
\begin{aligned}
m &= \frac{Q}{L} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{\left(20040\right)}{\left(334000\right)} \\[6pt]
&\simeq 0.06\,\mathrm{kg}
\end{aligned}
$$
