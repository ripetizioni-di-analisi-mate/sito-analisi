How can boiling be distinguished from evaporation in a pan?

- Any liquid losing mass necessarily forms bubbles.
- The liquid becomes solid at the bottom.
- Vapour bubbles form within the liquid as well.
- The surface alone always sets the melting point.

## Explanation

Evaporation starts at the surface. Boiling produces vapour throughout the bulk at the pressure and temperature of the boiling point.
