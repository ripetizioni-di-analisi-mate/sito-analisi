How can boiling be distinguished from evaporation in a pan?

- Any liquid losing mass necessarily forms bubbles.
- The liquid becomes solid at the bottom.
- Vapour bubbles form within the liquid as well.
- The surface alone always sets the melting point.

## Explanation

During boiling, vapour also forms within the liquid and bubbles grow as they rise to the surface. During evaporation, molecules leave from the free surface. Boiling is therefore indicated by growing vapour bubbles inside the liquid at the relevant temperature and pressure.
