Ice of mass $0.5\,\mathrm{kg}$, already at $0\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}$, absorbs constant power $400\,\mathrm W$ without heat loss. Use $L_f=334000\,\mathrm{J/kg}$. How long does complete melting take? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $835\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $417.5\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $208.75\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $1670\,\mathrm{s}$.

## Explanation

The melting threshold is $0.50\cdot334000\,\mathrm J=167000\,\mathrm J$. The ice receives $400\,\mathrm J$ each second. Dividing the threshold by this useful power gives $417.5\,\mathrm s$. Since the ice starts at zero, the entire calculated time is spent on the phase change.

$$
\begin{aligned}
t &= \frac{L m}{P} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{\left(334000\right) \left(0.5\right)}{\left(400\right)} \\[6pt]
&\simeq 417.5\,\mathrm{s}
\end{aligned}
$$
