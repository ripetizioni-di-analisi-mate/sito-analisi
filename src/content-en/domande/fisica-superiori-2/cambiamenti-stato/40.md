Complete melting requires $33.4\,\mathrm{kJ}$. The first $60\,\mathrm s$ receive $250\,\mathrm W$, then useful power rises to $400\,\mathrm W$. Find the total time.

- $46\,\mathrm s$
- $83.5\,\mathrm s$
- $133\,\mathrm s$
- $106\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The first $60\,\mathrm s$ supplies $250\cdot60\,\mathrm J=15\,\mathrm{kJ}$. This leaves $18.4\,\mathrm{kJ}$. At $400\,\mathrm W$ that takes $46\,\mathrm s$. The requested time starts when power is first applied, so add both intervals to obtain $106\,\mathrm s$. Dividing all the energy by $400\,\mathrm W$ would describe a different trial using the higher power from the start.

$$
t_{\mathrm{tot}}=60\,\mathrm s+\frac{33400-250\cdot60}{400}\,\mathrm s=106\,\mathrm s.
$$
