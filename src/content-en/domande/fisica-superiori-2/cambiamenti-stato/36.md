To finish melting $0.10\,\mathrm{kg}$ from zero, a heater runs for $100\,\mathrm s$. What is its useful power if $L_f=334000\,\mathrm{J/kg}$?

- $33.4\,\mathrm W$
- $3340\,\mathrm W$
- $100\,\mathrm W$
- $334\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

Useful power is joules delivered each second. On completion, the energy supplied reaches the threshold for the stages included.

$$
P=\frac{0.10\cdot334000}{100}\,\mathrm W=334\,\mathrm W.
$$
