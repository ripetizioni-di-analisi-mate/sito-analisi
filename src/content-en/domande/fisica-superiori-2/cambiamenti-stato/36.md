To melt $0.10\,\mathrm{kg}$ of ice already at zero, a heater runs for $100\,\mathrm s$. What is its useful power if $L_f=334000\,\mathrm{J/kg}$?

- $33.4\,\mathrm W$
- $3340\,\mathrm W$
- $100\,\mathrm W$
- $334\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

Melting requires $0.10\cdot334000\,\mathrm J=33400\,\mathrm J$. This energy is delivered in $100\,\mathrm s$. The ratio is $334\,\mathrm{J/s}$, or $334\,\mathrm W$. This is the power transferred to the ice. With losses present, the heater could draw greater electrical power. The same value is $0.334\,\mathrm{kJ/s}$, useful when the latent heat is given in kilojoules per kilogram.

$$
P=\frac{0.10\cdot334000}{100}\,\mathrm W=334\,\mathrm W.
$$
