How much heat takes $0.10\,\mathrm{kg}$ of ice from $-15\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ to its melting point without melting any? Use $c_g=2100\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$.

- $6300\,\mathrm J$
- $33400\,\mathrm J$
- $3150\,\mathrm J$
- $36550\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Separate sections with changing temperature from constant-temperature sections with a phase change. The sum covers the entire path.

$$
Q=0.10\cdot2100\cdot15\,\mathrm J=3150\,\mathrm J.
$$
