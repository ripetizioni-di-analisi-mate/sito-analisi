A $0.080\,\mathrm{kg}$ ice sample at zero receives $33.4\,\mathrm{kJ}$. With $L_f=334\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$, what energy is left after all the ice has melted?

- $26.72\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $33.4\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $6.68\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $60.12\,\mathrm{kJ}$

## Explanation

The phase change takes place at its transition temperature. The contribution is $m_{\mathrm{tr}}L$ in magnitude, with a sign set by the transfer direction.

$$
Q_{\mathrm{resto}}=(33.4-0.080\cdot334)\,\mathrm{kJ}=6.68\,\mathrm{kJ}.
$$
