A $0.080\,\mathrm{kg}$ ice sample at zero receives $33.4\,\mathrm{kJ}$. With $L_f=334\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$, what energy is left after all the ice has melted?

- $26.72\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $33.4\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $6.68\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $60.12\,\mathrm{kJ}$

## Explanation

Melting the whole sample takes $0.080\cdot334\,\mathrm{kJ}=26.72\,\mathrm{kJ}$. This threshold is below the available energy. Subtract $26.72\,\mathrm{kJ}$ from $33.4\,\mathrm{kJ}$, leaving $6.68\,\mathrm{kJ}$. Past the threshold the sample is already liquid and further energy can raise its temperature.

$$
Q_{\mathrm{resto}}=(33.4-0.080\cdot334)\,\mathrm{kJ}=6.68\,\mathrm{kJ}.
$$
