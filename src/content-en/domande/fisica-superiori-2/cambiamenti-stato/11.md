A $0.030\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass of water already at $100\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ must vaporise at normal atmospheric pressure. Only $50\%$ of the energy supplied by the source reaches the sample. With $L_v=2260000\,\mathrm{J/kg}$, what energy $Q$ must the source supply?

- $67800\,\mathrm J$
- $33900\,\mathrm J$
- $135600\,\mathrm J$
- $271200\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The water absorbs $0.030\cdot2260000\,\mathrm J=67800\,\mathrm J$ because it is already at boiling point. This amount is only $50\%$ of the input energy. The source must supply twice that energy, or $135600\,\mathrm J$. The other $67800\,\mathrm J$ is transferred elsewhere.
