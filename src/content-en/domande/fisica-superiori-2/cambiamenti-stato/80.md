Once all the ice has melted, what mass is used for further heating of the liquid phase?

- Initial liquid-water mass plus melted mass.
- Only the original ice mass.
- Only the original liquid water.
- Initial liquid-water mass minus all the melted mass.

## Explanation

The meltwater is now in the same phase as the original water. Every portion of liquid contributes to the combined heat capacity.
