Once all the ice has melted, what mass is used for further heating of the liquid phase?

- Initial liquid-water mass plus melted mass.
- Only the original ice mass.
- Only the original liquid water.
- Initial liquid-water mass minus all the melted mass.

## Explanation

After melting, both the initial water and the melted ice are liquid. They warm by the same temperature change on reaching the final equilibrium state. The sensible heat term therefore uses the sum of the two masses. Using one mass alone would overestimate the temperature rise.
