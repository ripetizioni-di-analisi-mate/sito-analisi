Start with ice below zero and finish with water above zero at normal pressure. What is the order of contributions?

- Melt, warm solid, warm liquid.
- Warm liquid, melt, warm solid.
- Warm solid, melt, warm liquid.
- Use only the vaporisation term.

## Explanation

The ice first reaches zero by warming as a solid. At the melting point, further energy changes its phase and temperature stays constant while ice remains. Once all the mass is liquid, use the specific heat capacity of water for the stage up to the final temperature.
