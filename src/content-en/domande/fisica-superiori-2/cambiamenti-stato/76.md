Warm water and ice at zero degrees Celsius are mixed in an insulated vessel of negligible heat capacity. Cooling the warm water down to zero would release exactly the energy needed to melt all the ice. What is the final state?

- Only liquid water at zero.
- Ice and water above zero.
- Only ice at zero.
- Liquid water at ten degrees.

## Explanation

The energy available melts even the last bit of ice. After the change there is no remaining energy to raise temperature.
