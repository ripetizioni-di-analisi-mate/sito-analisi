Of $0.30\,\mathrm{kg}$ of ice at zero, $20\%$ melts. What energy is absorbed with $L_f=334\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$?

- $100.2\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $80.16\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $66.8\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $20.04\,\mathrm{kJ}$

## Explanation

The melted portion is $20\%$ of $0.30\,\mathrm{kg}$, or $0.060\,\mathrm{kg}$. Only this mass enters the latent heat term. Multiplying it by $334\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$ gives $20.04\,\mathrm{kJ}$. At zero, $0.240\,\mathrm{kg}$ of ice remains and the rest is water. Using the whole block would instead give $100.2\,\mathrm{kJ}$, the value needed for complete melting.

$$
Q=0.20\cdot0.30\cdot334\,\mathrm{kJ}=20.04\,\mathrm{kJ}.
$$
