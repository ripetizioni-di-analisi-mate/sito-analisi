Of $0.30\,\mathrm{kg}$ of ice at zero, $20\%$ melts. What energy is absorbed with $L_f=334\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$?

- $100.2\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $80.16\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $66.8\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $20.04\,\mathrm{kJ}$

## Explanation

The phase change takes place at its transition temperature. The contribution is $m_{\mathrm{tr}}L$ in magnitude, with a sign set by the transfer direction.

$$
Q=0.20\cdot0.30\cdot334\,\mathrm{kJ}=20.04\,\mathrm{kJ}.
$$
