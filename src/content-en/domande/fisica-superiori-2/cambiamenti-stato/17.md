At the end of the melting plateau a $0.12\,\mathrm{kg}$ sample has absorbed $36\,\mathrm{kJ}$. If the solid phase vanished just then, what $L_f$ follows?

- $300\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$
- $30\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$
- $4.32\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$
- $3600\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$

## Explanation

The disappearance of the solid phase shows that the energy along the plateau converted all $0.12\,\mathrm{kg}$. Divide the measured energy, $36\,\mathrm{kJ}$, by that mass. The result is $300\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$. This is energy per kilogram and applies at the pressure of the experiment.

$$
L_f=\frac{36}{0.12}\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}=300\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}.
$$
