Some of the initial ice remains alongside liquid water. What final temperature follows from the equilibrium model at normal pressure?

- The arithmetic mean of the initial temperatures.
- One hundred degrees Celsius.
- A value above the water’s initial temperature.
- Zero degrees Celsius.

## Explanation

Coexistence of the two phases fixes equilibrium temperature at the melting point. The masses determine how much remains solid.
