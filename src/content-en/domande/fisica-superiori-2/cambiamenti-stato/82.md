A tray of water at room temperature slowly loses mass with no bubbling. Which process fits the observation?

- Boiling throughout the volume.
- Freezing.
- Frost deposition as the only process.
- Evaporation at the surface.

## Explanation

At the surface, some molecules can leave the water and enter the air even at room temperature. Gradual mass loss without bubbles in the liquid is consistent with this mechanism. The process is evaporation. If vapour bubbles formed within the liquid, that would be evidence of boiling.
