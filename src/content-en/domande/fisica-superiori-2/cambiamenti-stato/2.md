Ice at $0\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}$ receives $16700\,\mathrm J$ as it melts. Use $L_f=334000\,\mathrm{J/kg}$ and assume enough ice is available. What mass melts? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $0.1\,\mathrm{kg}$.
- $0.05\,\mathrm{kg}$.
- $0.025\,\mathrm{kg}$.
- $0.2\,\mathrm{kg}$.

## Explanation

Energy enters while the ice is at its melting point. The ratio of $16700\,\mathrm J$ to $334000\,\mathrm{J/kg}$ gives the kilograms that can become liquid. This gives $0.050\,\mathrm{kg}$, or $50\,\mathrm g$. Multiplying by the latent heat recovers the supplied energy. If less ice were available, melting would finish before the sample absorbed all the available energy.

$$
\begin{aligned}
m &= \frac{Q}{L} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{\left(16700\right)}{\left(334000\right)} \\[6pt]
&\simeq 0.05\,\mathrm{kg}
\end{aligned}
$$
