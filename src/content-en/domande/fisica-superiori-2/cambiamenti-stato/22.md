A mass of $15\,\mathrm g$ of vapour deposits directly as frost on a cold plate. The specific latent heat for this transition under the test conditions is $2830\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$. What is $Q$ for the water that changes phase, taking absorbed heat as positive?

- $42.45\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $-42.45\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $-4.245\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $-424.5\,\mathrm{kJ}$

## Explanation

Frost forms when vapour changes directly to solid. The system releases energy to the plate during this transition, so the heat transfer for the water is negative. Convert $15\,\mathrm g$ to $0.015\,\mathrm{kg}$ and multiply by the stated latent heat. This gives $-42.45\,\mathrm{kJ}$.
