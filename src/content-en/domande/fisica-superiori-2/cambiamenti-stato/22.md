Freeze $50\,\mathrm g$ of water already at zero with $L_f=334\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$. What is the signed heat for the water?

- $16.7\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $-16.7\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $-1.67\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $-16700\,\mathrm{kJ}$

## Explanation

The phase change takes place at its transition temperature. The contribution is $m_{\mathrm{tr}}L$ in magnitude, with a sign set by the transfer direction.

$$
Q=-0.050\cdot334\,\mathrm{kJ}=-16.7\,\mathrm{kJ}.
$$
