Ice of mass $0.6\,\mathrm{kg}$, already at $0\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}$, absorbs constant power $450\,\mathrm W$ without heat loss. Use $L_f=334000\,\mathrm{J/kg}$. How long does complete melting take? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $890.667\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $445.333\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $222.667\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $1781.33\,\mathrm{s}$.

## Explanation

The energy needed for complete melting is $mL_f$. Power in watts is the number of joules absorbed per second. Dividing energy by power gives time.

$$
\begin{aligned}
t &= \frac{L m}{P} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{\left(334000\right) \left(0.6\right)}{\left(450\right)} \\[6pt]
&\simeq 445.333\,\mathrm{s}
\end{aligned}
$$
