A mass of steam of $0.05\,\mathrm{kg}$ condenses at $100\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}$ at normal atmospheric pressure. Use $L_v=2.26\cdot10^{6}\,\mathrm{J/kg}$. What is the magnitude of the energy released? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $226000\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $56500\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $452000\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $113000\,\mathrm{J}$.

## Explanation

Condensation is the reverse of vaporisation. The signed heat for the sample is negative. The question asks for its magnitude, so the answer is $mL_v$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
|Q| &= L m \\[6pt]
&= \left(2.26\cdot10^{6}\right) \left(0.05\right) \\[6pt]
&\simeq 113000\,\mathrm{J}
\end{aligned}
$$
