A mass of water boils and the steam is then heated further. Which specific heat capacity applies once no liquid remains?

- Always that of liquid water.
- That of ice.
- Latent heat in place of any specific heat capacity.
- That of steam under the stated conditions.

## Explanation

Once the liquid phase has disappeared, further energy can raise the temperature of the gas. Use the specific heat capacity of vapour under the stated conditions in that section. Latent heat applies to the boiling stage, when liquid and vapour coexist.
