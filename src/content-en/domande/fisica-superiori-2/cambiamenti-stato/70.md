A mass of water boils and the steam is then heated further. Which specific heat capacity applies once no liquid remains?

- Always that of liquid water.
- That of ice.
- Latent heat in place of any specific heat capacity.
- That of steam under the stated conditions.

## Explanation

After the change is complete, a different single phase is present. A temperature rise relates energy to the specific heat capacity of that phase.
