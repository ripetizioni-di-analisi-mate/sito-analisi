After warming cold ice, energy supply stops exactly when it reaches zero. What has formed in the model?

- It has all melted.
- Only vapour has formed.
- Its temperature already exceeds zero.
- The sample has reached the melting point and is still solid.

## Explanation

The energy supplied up to zero is the amount given by the specific heat capacity of ice. Reaching that temperature makes melting possible, but converting any mass takes the corresponding latent heat as well. Since the source stops at the first threshold, ice remains at the melting temperature.
