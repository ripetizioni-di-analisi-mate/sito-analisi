After warming cold ice, energy supply stops exactly when it reaches zero. What has formed in the model?

- It has all melted.
- Only vapour has formed.
- Its temperature already exceeds zero.
- The sample has reached the melting point and is still solid.

## Explanation

That warming uses only specific heat capacity. Further energy is required to melt a positive amount of mass.
