Ice and water in a glass have the same chemical composition. Why are they two phases?

- They must be at different temperatures.
- They have different physical properties.
- They always have equal masses.
- They consist of different elements.

## Explanation

Composition identifies which molecules are present. Both parts of the glass contain water molecules, but in ice they form a rigid structure and in liquid water they can move past one another. There are therefore two regions with different physical properties, separated by the boundary between ice and liquid water.
