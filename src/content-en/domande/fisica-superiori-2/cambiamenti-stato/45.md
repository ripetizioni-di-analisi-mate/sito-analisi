In an insulated vessel of negligible heat capacity, warm water is mixed with ice at zero degrees Celsius. Cooling the original warm water all the way to zero would release $75.2\,\mathrm{kJ}$. Melting all the ice takes $33.4\,\mathrm{kJ}$ and the total liquid mass after melting is $1\,\mathrm{kg}$. Use $c_a=4180\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$ and find the final temperature.

- $10\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $17.99\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $7.99\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $0\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$

## Explanation

First compare the heat water can release down to zero with that needed to melt the ice. The comparison determines which phases remain.

$$
T_f=0\,{}^\circ\mathrm C+\frac{(75.2-33.4)\cdot1000}{4180}\,\mathrm K=10\,{}^\circ\mathrm C.
$$
