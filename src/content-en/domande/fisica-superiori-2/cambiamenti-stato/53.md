What conditions are used in the model of melting at a fixed temperature?

- Only constant mass.
- Arbitrarily changing pressure.
- Pure substance, stated pressure and phase equilibrium.
- Gas as the only phase.

## Explanation

The model fixes pressure because each pressure has its own equilibrium conditions. For a pure substance, solid and liquid can then coexist at one temperature. While both phases are present and close to equilibrium, the heat transfer changes the liquid fraction and temperature remains constant.
