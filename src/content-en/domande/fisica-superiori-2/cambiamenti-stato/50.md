Dry ice changes directly from solid to gas. What is the transition called?

- Melting.
- Condensation.
- Freezing.
- Sublimation.

## Explanation

Dry ice loses mass because some of the solid turns to gas. The direct transition between these phases is called sublimation. Identify the initial and final phases to recognise it. If a liquid appeared first, the first transition in that path would instead be melting.
