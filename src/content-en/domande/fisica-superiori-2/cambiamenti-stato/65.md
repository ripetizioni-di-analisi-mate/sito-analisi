A temperature graph is almost flat while a thermostat holds liquid water far from its transition. What is missing to attribute the section to latent heat?

- Evidence of a phase change.
- A number measured in joules.
- A time axis.
- A mass greater than a kilogram.

## Explanation

A thermostat can hold the temperature constant by adjusting the energy supplied or removed. An almost steady temperature can therefore occur in water that remains liquid. A conclusion that phase change is occurring requires evidence such as a second phase appearing and a change in the mass converted.
