During boiling, water loses mass at a rate of $0.5\,\mathrm{g/s}$. With $L_v=2260\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$, how much power is used for vaporisation?

- $113\,\mathrm W$
- $2260\,\mathrm W$
- $1130\,\mathrm W$
- $1.13\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

Useful power is joules delivered each second. On completion, the energy supplied reaches the threshold for the stages included.

$$
P=0.0005\cdot2260000\,\mathrm W=1130\,\mathrm W.
$$
