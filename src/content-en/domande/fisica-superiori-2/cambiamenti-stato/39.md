During boiling, water loses mass at a rate of $0.5\,\mathrm{g/s}$. With $L_v=2260\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$, how much power is used for vaporisation?

- $113\,\mathrm W$
- $2260\,\mathrm W$
- $1130\,\mathrm W$
- $1.13\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

Each second, $0.0005\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water turns to vapour. Each kilogram takes $2260\,\mathrm{kJ}$, so the power going into the phase change is $1.13\,\mathrm{kJ/s}$. Converting kilojoules to joules gives $1130\,\mathrm W$. Any heat losses would increase the power required from the source.

$$
P=0.0005\cdot2260000\,\mathrm W=1130\,\mathrm W.
$$
