With $c_g=2100\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$ and $L_f=334\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$, how many kilojoules take $0.10\,\mathrm{kg}$ from ice at $-10\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ to water at zero?

- $33.4\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $2.1\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $37.6\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $35.5\,\mathrm{kJ}$

## Explanation

Separate sections with changing temperature from constant-temperature sections with a phase change. The sum covers the entire path.

$$
Q=0.10\cdot2100\cdot10\,\mathrm J+0.10\cdot334\,\mathrm{kJ}=35.5\,\mathrm{kJ}.
$$
