Which unit must $L$ have if energy is in joules and mass in kilograms?

- $\mathrm{J/K}$
- $\mathrm{J/kg}$
- $\mathrm{W/kg}$
- $\mathrm{kg/J}$

## Explanation

Mass multiplies L to produce energy, so its unit is the energy divided by the mass.
