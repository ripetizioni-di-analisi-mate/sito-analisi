Which unit must $L$ have if energy is in joules and mass in kilograms?

- $\mathrm{J/K}$
- $\mathrm{J/kg}$
- $\mathrm{W/kg}$
- $\mathrm{kg/J}$

## Explanation

Specific latent heat is the energy per kilogram that changes phase. Divide the energy in joules by the mass in kilograms to obtain $\mathrm{J/kg}$. In an ideal transition the temperature remains constant, so kelvins do not appear in this unit. Specific heat capacity in a single phase instead uses $\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$, because it also measures a rise in temperature.
