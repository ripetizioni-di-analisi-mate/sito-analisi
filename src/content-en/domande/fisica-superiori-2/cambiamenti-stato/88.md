Melting takes $125\,\mathrm s$ with $400\,\mathrm W$ of useful power. A second device melts the same mass in $100\,\mathrm s$. What useful power does it supply?

- $320\,\mathrm W$
- $400\,\mathrm W$
- $500\,\mathrm W$
- $5\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

The first melting process uses $400\cdot125\,\mathrm J=50000\,\mathrm J$. The second converts the same mass under the same conditions, so it must transfer the same amount of energy. Delivering it in $100\,\mathrm s$ requires $500\,\mathrm W$ of useful power. The faster device supplies more energy each second. The melting temperature is the same in both trials. What changes is the duration of the plateau.

$$
P_2=\frac{400\cdot125}{100}\,\mathrm W=500\,\mathrm W.
$$
