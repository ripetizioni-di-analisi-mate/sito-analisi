Melting takes $125\,\mathrm s$ with $400\,\mathrm W$ of useful power. A second device melts the same mass in $100\,\mathrm s$. What useful power does it supply?

- $320\,\mathrm W$
- $400\,\mathrm W$
- $500\,\mathrm W$
- $5\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

Useful power is joules delivered each second. On completion, the energy supplied reaches the threshold for the stages included.

$$
P_2=\frac{400\cdot125}{100}\,\mathrm W=500\,\mathrm W.
$$
