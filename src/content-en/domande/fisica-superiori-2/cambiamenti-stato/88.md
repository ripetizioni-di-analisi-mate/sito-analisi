A block of ice at $0\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ receives a useful power of $400\,\mathrm W$ from a heater and melts completely in $125\,\mathrm s$. A second block of ice of the same mass, also at $0\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$, melts completely in $100\,\mathrm s$ with another heater. What useful power does the second heater supply?

- $320\,\mathrm W$
- $400\,\mathrm W$
- $500\,\mathrm W$
- $100\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

Melting the first block requires the energy $Q=400\cdot125\,\mathrm J=50000\,\mathrm J$. The second block has the same mass and is at the same temperature, so melting it requires the same energy. Power is the energy supplied divided by the time, therefore
$$
P_2=\frac{50000}{100}\,\mathrm W=500\,\mathrm W.
$$
