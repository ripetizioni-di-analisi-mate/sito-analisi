If a tabulated latent heat is positive, why can freezing have a negative heat transfer?

- Mass is negative during cooling.
- L is a magnitude and the sign comes from the direction of transfer.
- The tabulated latent heat always becomes zero.
- The sign depends on the joule units.

## Explanation

The sample releases heat while forming the solid. The magnitude per kilogram is the same as for the reverse transition.
