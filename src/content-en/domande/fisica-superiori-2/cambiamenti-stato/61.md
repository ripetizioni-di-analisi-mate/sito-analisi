What is the slope of temperature versus heat in a phase with constant mass $m$ and specific heat capacity $c$?

- $mc$
- $mL$
- $1/c$
- $1/(mc)$

## Explanation

Isolate the temperature interval in Q equal to mc times the change, giving the temperature rise per unit of energy.
