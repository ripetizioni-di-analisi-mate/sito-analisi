In a phase with constant mass $m$ and specific heat capacity $c$, temperature is plotted against $Q$, taken as positive when the sample absorbs heat. What is the slope?

- $mc$
- $mL$
- $\dfrac{1}{c}$
- $\dfrac{1}{mc}$

## Explanation

On a single-phase section, the energy transferred is $Q=mc\Delta T$. The slope of $T$ plotted against $Q$ is the temperature rise per unit of energy. It is therefore $\dfrac{1}{mc}$. The unit of the slope is kelvins per joule.
