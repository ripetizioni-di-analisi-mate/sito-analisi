During condensation of $0.025\,\mathrm{kg}$ of steam at $100\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ with $L_v=2260\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$, how much energy do the surroundings receive?

- $56.5\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $-56.5\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $565\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $5.65\,\mathrm{kJ}$

## Explanation

The phase change takes place at its transition temperature. The contribution is $m_{\mathrm{tr}}L$ in magnitude, with a sign set by the transfer direction.

$$
Q_{\mathrm{ambiente}}=0.025\cdot2260\,\mathrm{kJ}=56.5\,\mathrm{kJ}.
$$
