During condensation of $0.025\,\mathrm{kg}$ of steam at $100\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ with $L_v=2260\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$, how much energy do the surroundings receive?

- $56.5\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $-56.5\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $565\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $5.65\,\mathrm{kJ}$

## Explanation

Each kilogram that condenses at this temperature releases $2260\,\mathrm{kJ}$. The mass here is $0.025\,\mathrm{kg}$, so the transfer has magnitude $56.5\,\mathrm{kJ}$. This is an output for the vapour and an input for the surroundings. Only condensation is counted because subsequent cooling of the water is outside the stated process.

$$
Q_{\mathrm{ambiente}}=0.025\cdot2260\,\mathrm{kJ}=56.5\,\mathrm{kJ}.
$$
