A mass of ice of $0.6\,\mathrm{kg}$, already at $0\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}$, melts completely at normal atmospheric pressure. Use $L_f=334000\,\mathrm{J/kg}$. How much heat does it absorb? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $200400\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $400800\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $100200\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $801600\,\mathrm{J}$.

## Explanation

The ice starts at the melting point. All the energy goes into the phase change, so multiply $L_f$ by the converted mass. The sign is positive because the sample absorbs energy.

$$
\begin{aligned}
Q &= L m \\[6pt]
&= \left(334000\right) \left(0.6\right) \\[6pt]
&\simeq 200400\,\mathrm{J}
\end{aligned}
$$
