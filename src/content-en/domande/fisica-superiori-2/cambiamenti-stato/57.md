For a mass $m$ condensing at a given pressure, what is the signed heat for that mass itself?

- $Q=mL_v$.
- $Q=-mL_v$.
- $Q=-mL_f$.
- $Q=0$.

## Explanation

Condensation reverses boiling and releases to the surroundings a magnitude of m times the latent heat of vaporisation.
