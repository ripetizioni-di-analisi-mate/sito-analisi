A mass $m$ of vapour condenses at constant pressure. What is the signed heat transferred to the sample during condensation alone?

- $Q=mL_v$.
- $Q=-mL_v$.
- $Q=-mL_f$.
- $Q=0$.

## Explanation

As vapour condenses, it becomes liquid at the equilibrium temperature. The latent heat $L_v$ gives the magnitude transferred per kilogram and is multiplied by $m$. Since energy leaves the sample during condensation, the transfer is negative and $Q=-mL_v$.
