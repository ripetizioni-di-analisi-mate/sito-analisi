Take $0.5\,\mathrm{kg}$ of ice from $-10\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}$ to liquid water at $18\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}$. Use $c_g=2100$, $c_a=4200\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$ and $L_f=334000\,\mathrm{J/kg}$ at normal atmospheric pressure. How much heat is needed? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $215300\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $167000\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $48300\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $204800\,\mathrm{J}$.

## Explanation

There are three stages. Heat the ice to zero, melt the entire mass, then warm the water. The two warming stages use different specific heat capacities, while melting uses latent heat.

$$
\begin{aligned}
Q &= \Delta T_g c_g m + L m + T_a c_a m \\[6pt]
&= \left(10\right) \left(2100\right) \left(0.5\right) + \left(334000\right) \left(0.5\right) + \left(18\right) \left(4200\right) \left(0.5\right) \\[6pt]
&\simeq 215300\,\mathrm{J}
\end{aligned}
$$
