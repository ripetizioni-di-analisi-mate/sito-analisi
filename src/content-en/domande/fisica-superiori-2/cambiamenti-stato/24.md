In separate compartments of the same substance at equal pressure, $0.22\,\mathrm{kg}$ freezes and $0.06\,\mathrm{kg}$ melts. With $L_f=334\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$, what is the magnitude of the net heat released by the two samples?

- $93.52\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $73.48\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $20.04\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $53.44\,\mathrm{kJ}$

## Explanation

The freezing portion releases $0.22\cdot334\,\mathrm{kJ}=73.48\,\mathrm{kJ}$. The melting portion absorbs $0.06\cdot334\,\mathrm{kJ}=20.04\,\mathrm{kJ}$. Adding the signed heat transfers gives $-53.44\,\mathrm{kJ}$ for the samples together. The magnitude released to the surroundings is therefore $53.44\,\mathrm{kJ}$. The system contains both samples. Counting only the first would omit the heat absorbed by the second.

$$
|Q_\mathrm{net}|=(0.22-0.06)\cdot334\,\mathrm{kJ}=53.44\,\mathrm{kJ}.
$$
