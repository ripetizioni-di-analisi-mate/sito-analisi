In separate compartments of the same substance at equal pressure, $0.22\,\mathrm{kg}$ freezes and $0.06\,\mathrm{kg}$ melts. With $L_f=334\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$, what is the magnitude of the combined release?

- $93.52\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $73.48\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $20.04\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $53.44\,\mathrm{kJ}$

## Explanation

The phase change takes place at its transition temperature. The contribution is $m_{\mathrm{tr}}L$ in magnitude, with a sign set by the transfer direction.

$$
|Q_\mathrm{net}|=(0.22-0.06)\cdot334\,\mathrm{kJ}=53.44\,\mathrm{kJ}.
$$
