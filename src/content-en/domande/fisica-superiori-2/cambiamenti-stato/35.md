After condensing at $100\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$, a mass of $0.040\,\mathrm{kg}$ cools to $85\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. With $L_v=2260\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$ and $c_a=4200\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$, find the magnitude released in both stages.

- $90.4\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $2.52\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $92.92\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $87.88\,\mathrm{kJ}$

## Explanation

Separate sections with changing temperature from constant-temperature sections with a phase change. The sum covers the entire path.

$$
|Q|=0.040\cdot2260\,\mathrm{kJ}+0.040\cdot4200\cdot15\,\mathrm J=92.92\,\mathrm{kJ}.
$$
