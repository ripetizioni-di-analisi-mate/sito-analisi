After condensing at $100\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$, a mass of $0.040\,\mathrm{kg}$ cools to $10\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. With $L_v=2260\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$ and $c_a=4200\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$, find the magnitude released in both stages.

- $90.4\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $15.12\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $105.52\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $75.28\,\mathrm{kJ}$

## Explanation

Condensing $0.040\,\mathrm{kg}$ releases $90.4\,\mathrm{kJ}$. After the transition the same mass is liquid, cools by $90\,\mathrm K$, and releases another $15.12\,\mathrm{kJ}$. Both heat transfers have the same sign in the sample balance. Their combined magnitude is $105.52\,\mathrm{kJ}$.

$$
|Q|=0.040\cdot2260\,\mathrm{kJ}+0.040\cdot4200\cdot90\,\mathrm J=105.52\,\mathrm{kJ}.
$$
