Two vessels at atmospheric pressure contain water and ice at zero. The mass of ice increases in the first and decreases in the second. What does this tell us about the net heat transfer $Q$ for each sample?

- It is positive for both
- It is negative for the first and positive for the second
- It is positive for the first and negative for the second
- It is zero for both because temperature stays constant

## Explanation

In the first vessel the solid mass increases, so the transition is freezing and the sample releases heat. Melting takes place in the second vessel and the sample absorbs heat. Equal, constant temperatures show that the phases are in equilibrium, while the direction of the transition determines the sign of the transfer.
