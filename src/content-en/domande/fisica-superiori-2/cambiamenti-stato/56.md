During ideal melting the thermometer reading stays fixed. What follows from a positive measurement of the energy supplied?

- The measurement must be false because temperature is fixed.
- Energy changes part of the solid phase to liquid.
- Energy only lowered the temperature.
- Total mass increases.

## Explanation

The amount of solid can change at constant temperature. For that stage the applicable equation uses latent heat.
