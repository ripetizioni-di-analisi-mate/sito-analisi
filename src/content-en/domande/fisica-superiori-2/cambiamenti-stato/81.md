Ice below zero is mixed with lukewarm water. Which comparison comes before the latent-heat comparison if the mixture can reach zero?

- How much heat is needed to bring the ice to zero.
- How much steam forms immediately.
- The weight of one litre of ice by definition.
- How to convert a joule into kelvins without data.

## Explanation

The energy used to warm the solid before melting is subtracted from the available contribution. Only the remainder can melt ice.
