Ice below zero is mixed with lukewarm water. Which comparison comes before the latent-heat comparison if the mixture can reach zero?

- Compare the heat the water can release with the heat needed to bring the ice to zero.
- How much steam forms immediately.
- The weight of one litre of ice by definition.
- How to convert a joule into kelvins without data.

## Explanation

First find the energy needed to warm the initial ice to zero. Compare it with the heat the water can release by cooling to zero. Only after this amount has been provided can the remaining energy be assigned to melting the ice.
