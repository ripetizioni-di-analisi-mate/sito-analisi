A cool surface receives $180.8\,\mathrm{kJ}$ solely from condensation at normal pressure. With $L_v=2260\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$, what mass is collected?

- $-0.08\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.08\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.8\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $80\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The phase change takes place at its transition temperature. The contribution is $m_{\mathrm{tr}}L$ in magnitude, with a sign set by the transfer direction.

$$
m_{\mathrm{cond}}=\frac{180.8}{2260}\,\mathrm{kg}=0.08\,\mathrm{kg}.
$$
