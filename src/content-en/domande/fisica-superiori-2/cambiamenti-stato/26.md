A cool surface receives $180.8\,\mathrm{kJ}$ solely from condensation at normal pressure. With $L_v=2260\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$, what mass is collected?

- $-0.08\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.08\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.8\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $80\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The surface receives energy only from vapour changing to liquid. Divide $180.8\,\mathrm{kJ}$ by $2260\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$ to get $0.080\,\mathrm{kg}$. The reverse product gives the stated energy. If the surface also absorbed heat from cooling the water, that contribution would need to be subtracted before finding the mass.

$$
m_{\mathrm{cond}}=\frac{180.8}{2260}\,\mathrm{kg}=0.08\,\mathrm{kg}.
$$
