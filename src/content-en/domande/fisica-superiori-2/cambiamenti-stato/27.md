A heating curve against $Q$ enters its plateau at $6\,\mathrm{kJ}$ and leaves it at $46\,\mathrm{kJ}$. How much energy corresponds to the phase change?

- $46\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $52\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $40\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kJ}$

## Explanation

Read energy contributions as differences of horizontal coordinates. Use specific heat capacity on sloped sections and latent heat on the plateau.

$$
Q_{\mathrm{lat}}=(46-6)\,\mathrm{kJ}=40\,\mathrm{kJ}.
$$
