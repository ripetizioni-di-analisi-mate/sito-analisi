Constant useful power doubles and the mass to melt is unchanged. How long does melting now take?

- Half the time.
- Twice the time.
- Four times the time.
- The same time at constant pressure.

## Explanation

For the same mass at the melting point, the energy threshold is still $mL_f$. Doubling useful power supplies twice the joules each second. The threshold is reached in half the time. This result applies to the phase change at constant useful power.
