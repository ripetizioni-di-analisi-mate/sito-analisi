Constant useful power doubles and the mass to melt is unchanged. How long does melting now take?

- Half the time.
- Twice the time.
- Four times the time.
- The same time at constant pressure.

## Explanation

The energy threshold is unchanged. Time is energy divided by useful power, so it halves.
