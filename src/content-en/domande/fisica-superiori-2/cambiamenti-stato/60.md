The same mass of a substance is melted and then frozen at the same temperature and pressure. How do the two signed heat transfers compare?

- They are equal and positive.
- They have equal magnitude and opposite signs.
- Heat of melting is zero.
- Heat of freezing is measured in kelvins.

## Explanation

Each part of the mass goes through the reverse transition under the same condition. Energy first enters and is then released.
