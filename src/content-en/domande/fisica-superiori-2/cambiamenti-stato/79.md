A partial-melting calculation gives more ice melted than was initially present. What check calls for changing the calculation?

- Melting is complete and any excess can warm the water.
- New solid mass formed and then melted.
- Changing the mass unit is enough without changing the balance.
- The sample must have cooled back below zero.

## Explanation

Converted mass is limited by the available amount. The balance must switch from coexistence to the liquid-only regime after melting.
