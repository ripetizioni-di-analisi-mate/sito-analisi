In a mixture at zero, water has released $16.7\,\mathrm{kJ}$ to $0.20\,\mathrm{kg}$ of ice. With $L_f=334\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$, how much ice remains?

- $0.05\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.15\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.2\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

First compare the heat water can release down to zero with that needed to melt the ice. The comparison determines which phases remain.

$$
m_{\mathrm{resto}}=0.20\,\mathrm{kg}-\frac{16.7}{334}\,\mathrm{kg}=0.15\,\mathrm{kg}.
$$
