Water vapour forms frost crystals directly on a cold pane. Which transition is involved?

- Condensation to liquid.
- Vaporisation.
- Melting.
- Deposition.

## Explanation

The frost is solid and the stated initial phase is vapour. The direct transition from gas to solid is deposition and releases energy to the cold surface. A liquid droplet formed from vapour would be a case of condensation. Here the deposit is already crystalline.
