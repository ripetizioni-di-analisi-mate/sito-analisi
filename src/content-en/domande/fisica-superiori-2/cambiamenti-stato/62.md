Two heating curves for equal masses have different slopes in their liquid sections. What does the smaller slope represent?

- A smaller specific heat capacity.
- A larger mass even though both masses are equal.
- A greater specific heat capacity.
- Necessarily a greater latent heat.

## Explanation

At equal mass, greater specific heat capacity requires more energy per kelvin. Each joule therefore gives a smaller rise.
