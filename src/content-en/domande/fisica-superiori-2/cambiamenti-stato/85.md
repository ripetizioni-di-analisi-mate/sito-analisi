The graph shows the saturation vapour pressure of water. External pressure is held at $85\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Within what interval does boiling begin in the equilibrium model?

::grafico{linee="80,47 90,70 100,101" punti="90,70,pieno;100,101,pieno" segmenti="80,85,101,85" etichette="81,87,85 kPa" xmin=80 xmax=102 ymin=43 ymax=109 nomi="T (°C),p (kPa)" titolo="The dashed line shows the external pressure on the vertical axis."}

- Between $90$ and $100\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- Between $80$ and $90\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- Below $80\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- Above $100\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$

## Explanation

Boiling begins when saturation vapour pressure equals external pressure. At $90\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ the curve is still below $85\,\mathrm{kPa}$. At $100\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ it is above that value. Since the curve increases, the intersection lies between those temperatures.
