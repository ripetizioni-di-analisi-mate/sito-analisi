Two pure samples of equal mass boil at different pressures. Why is it insufficient to assign them the same plateau at the same temperature?

- The transition temperature depends on pressure.
- The transition is always at zero.
- Their masses must differ.
- Temperature is independent of phase.

## Explanation

The liquid and vapour phases coexist at a temperature that also depends on pressure. The graph needs to state the conditions.
