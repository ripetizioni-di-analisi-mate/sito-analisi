The sample starts with $0.20\,\mathrm{kg}$ of liquid water at zero. Freezing releases $16.7\,\mathrm{kJ}$. With $L_f=334\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$, what fraction of the mass has frozen?

- $0.75$
- $0.05$
- $0.25$
- $4$

## Explanation

Divide the magnitude of the heat released by the latent heat to find the mass that froze. This gives $\dfrac{16.7}{334}\,\mathrm{kg}=0.050\,\mathrm{kg}$. The question asks for the fraction of the total mass, so divide again by $0.20\,\mathrm{kg}$. The result $0.25$ has no units.

$$
f=\frac{16.7}{334\cdot0.20}=0.25.
$$
