A total mass of $0.20\,\mathrm{kg}$ is water at zero. Freezing releases $16.7\,\mathrm{kJ}$. With $L_f=334\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$, what fraction of the mass has frozen?

- $0.75$
- $0.05$
- $0.25$
- $4$

## Explanation

The phase change takes place at its transition temperature. The contribution is $m_{\mathrm{tr}}L$ in magnitude, with a sign set by the transfer direction.

$$
f=\frac{16.7}{334\cdot0.20}=0.25.
$$
