For $0.50\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water with $c_a=4200\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$, find the minimum initial temperature that melts $0.10\,\mathrm{kg}$ of ice at zero with $L_f=334000\,\mathrm{J/kg}$ in an insulated system of negligible vessel capacity. Round to a tenth.

- $7.95\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $31.8\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $1.59\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $15.9\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$

## Explanation

Melting requires $33400\,\mathrm J$. Each degree of water cooling provides $0.50\cdot4200\,\mathrm J=2100\,\mathrm J$. Divide $33400$ by $2100$ to reach the threshold, about $15.9\,\mathrm K$ above zero. A lower temperature would leave some of the ice solid. Round only at the end. The exact balance describes the state in which the last ice has just melted and all the water is at zero.

$$
T_{a,\min}=\frac{0.10\cdot334000}{0.50\cdot4200}\,{}^\circ\mathrm C\simeq15.9\,{}^\circ\mathrm C.
$$
