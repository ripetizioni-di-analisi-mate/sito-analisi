Two heaters melt equal masses of ice that start at zero. Complete melting takes the same time in both trials. The second heater draws twice the electrical power of the first. How do the efficiencies compare, where efficiency is the fraction of electrical energy that reaches the ice?

- They are equal
- The second is twice as efficient
- The second is half as efficient
- The second is a quarter as efficient

## Explanation

The same mass at the same melting temperature takes the same useful energy. The times are equal, so the useful powers are equal too. In the second heater this power is divided by twice the electrical power, so the ratio of useful to electrical power is half as large.
