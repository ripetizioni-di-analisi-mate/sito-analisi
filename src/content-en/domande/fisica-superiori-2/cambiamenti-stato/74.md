Why can two sources of different power melt the same mass at the same pressure and deliver equal energy?

- Power is equal by definition.
- The weaker source always has zero efficiency.
- The threshold mL is identical and the times differ.
- Mass depends only on power.

## Explanation

The stated transition determines the required energy. A lower-power source can supply it over a longer duration.
