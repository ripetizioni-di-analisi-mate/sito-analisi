A vessel holds liquid water at a pressure above normal atmospheric pressure. In which direction does the boiling point tend to shift?

- Towards higher temperatures.
- Towards lower temperatures.
- Towards absolute zero.
- It stays at exactly one hundred degrees under all conditions.

## Explanation

A higher temperature is needed for vapour to support bubbles at the higher external pressure.
