A mass of water of $0.05\,\mathrm{kg}$, already at $100\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}$, vaporises completely at normal atmospheric pressure. Use $L_v=2.26\cdot10^{6}\,\mathrm{J/kg}$. How much heat is needed? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $226000\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $56500\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $113000\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $452000\,\mathrm{J}$.

## Explanation

The water starts at its boiling point. Multiply $L_v$ by the mass fully converted to steam. The positive product represents incoming energy.

$$
\begin{aligned}
Q &= L m \\[6pt]
&= \left(2.26\cdot10^{6}\right) \left(0.05\right) \\[6pt]
&\simeq 113000\,\mathrm{J}
\end{aligned}
$$
