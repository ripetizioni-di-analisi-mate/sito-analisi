A pure solid becomes liquid while absorbing energy at the transition temperature. What is the reverse transition?

- Boiling.
- Deposition from vapour.
- Sublimation.
- Freezing.

## Explanation

Melting starts with a solid and ends with a liquid. Reversing the process means starting with the liquid at the transition temperature and ending with the solid. This is freezing. Under the same conditions and for the same mass, the reverse path releases the energy previously absorbed.
