A pure solid becomes liquid while absorbing energy at the transition temperature. What is the reverse transition?

- Boiling.
- Deposition from vapour.
- Sublimation.
- Freezing.

## Explanation

Melting goes from solid to liquid. Freezing returns from the liquid phase to the solid, releasing energy.
