Water releases $40\,\mathrm{kJ}$. The ice at $-8\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ first needs $4\,\mathrm{kJ}$ to reach zero. If some solid remains, what energy went into melting?

- $36\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $44\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $40\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kJ}$

## Explanation

First compare the heat water can release down to zero with that needed to melt the ice. The comparison determines which phases remain.

$$
Q_{\mathrm{fus}}=(40-4)\,\mathrm{kJ}=36\,\mathrm{kJ}.
$$
