A tray initially holds $0.80\,\mathrm{kg}$ of liquid water at zero. After cooling, a mixture of water and ice remains at zero, with $0.20\,\mathrm{kg}$ still liquid. Use $L_f=334000\,\mathrm{J/kg}$. What is the signed heat transfer $Q$ for the sample?

- $200400\,\mathrm J$
- $-66800\,\mathrm J$
- $-267200\,\mathrm J$
- $-200400\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Subtract the mass still liquid from the initial mass to get $0.60\,\mathrm{kg}$ of ice. The temperature is zero in both states and both phases coexist at the end, so this is only a latent heat transfer. Heat leaves the sample, giving $-0.60\cdot334000\,\mathrm J=-200400\,\mathrm J$.
