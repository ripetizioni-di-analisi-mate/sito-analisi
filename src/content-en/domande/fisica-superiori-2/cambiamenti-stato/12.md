A mass of water of $0.6\,\mathrm{kg}$ freezes completely at $0\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}$ at normal atmospheric pressure. With $L_f=334000\,\mathrm{J/kg}$, what is the heat for the sample, taken as positive if absorbed? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $200400\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $-100200\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $-400800\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $-200400\,\mathrm{J}$.

## Explanation

Freezing reverses melting. Its magnitude is $mL_f$. The heat is negative for the sample because energy is released to its surroundings.

$$
\begin{aligned}
Q &= - L m \\[6pt]
&= - \left(334000\right) \left(0.6\right) \\[6pt]
&\simeq -200400\,\mathrm{J}
\end{aligned}
$$
