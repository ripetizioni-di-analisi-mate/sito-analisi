At normal atmospheric pressure, $0.10\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water at $95\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ absorbs $6.62\,\mathrm{kJ}$ from a heater. Use $c_a=4200\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$ and $L_v=2260\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$. How many grams are converted to vapour?

- $2.93\,\mathrm g$
- $0.93\,\mathrm g$
- $20\,\mathrm g$
- $2.00\,\mathrm g$

## Explanation

The water must absorb $0.10\cdot4200\cdot5\,\mathrm J=2.10\,\mathrm{kJ}$ before boiling. That leaves $4.52\,\mathrm{kJ}$ for the phase change. Dividing by $2260\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$ gives $0.00200\,\mathrm{kg}$, or $2.00\,\mathrm g$. Using the full energy in the latent heat term would count the initial warming too. Some liquid water therefore remains at its boiling temperature.
