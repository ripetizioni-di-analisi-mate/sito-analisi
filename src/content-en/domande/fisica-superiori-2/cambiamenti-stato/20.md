How many grams of boiling water become steam after absorbing $90.4\,\mathrm{kJ}$? Use $L_v=2260\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$.

- $4\,\mathrm g$
- $0.04\,\mathrm g$
- $400\,\mathrm g$
- $40\,\mathrm g$

## Explanation

The phase change takes place at its transition temperature. The contribution is $m_{\mathrm{tr}}L$ in magnitude, with a sign set by the transfer direction.

$$
m=\frac{90.4}{2260}\,\mathrm{kg}=0.040\,\mathrm{kg}=40\,\mathrm g.
$$
