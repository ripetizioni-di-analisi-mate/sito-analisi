A mass of ice of $0.5\,\mathrm{kg}$, already at $0\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}$, melts completely at normal atmospheric pressure. Use $L_f=334000\,\mathrm{J/kg}$. How much heat does it absorb? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $167000\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $334000\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $83500\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $668000\,\mathrm{J}$.

## Explanation

The ice is already at its melting point, so it needs no warming before the transition. Each kilogram absorbs $334000\,\mathrm J$. Half a kilogram needs half that energy, or $167000\,\mathrm J$. The heat transfer is positive in the ice balance because it enters the sample.

$$
\begin{aligned}
Q &= L m \\[6pt]
&= \left(334000\right) \left(0.5\right) \\[6pt]
&\simeq 167000\,\mathrm{J}
\end{aligned}
$$
