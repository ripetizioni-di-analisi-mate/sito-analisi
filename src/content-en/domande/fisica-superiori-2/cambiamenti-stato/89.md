In the graph the ice absorbs heat without losses. How much heat has been supplied at the start of melting?

::grafico{linee="0,-10 5.25,0 88.75,0 99.25,10" punti="5.25,0,pieno,A;88.75,0,pieno,B" segmenti="5.25,-9,5.25,0" etichette="8,-7,5.25 kJ" xmin=0 xmax=105 ymin=-12 ymax=15 nomi="Q (kJ),T (°C)" titolo="The graph shows the entire process from ice to water above zero."}

- $5.25\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $88.75\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $99.25\,\mathrm{kJ}$

## Explanation

Warming the ice ends when the curve reaches zero and the plateau begins. This is point A. To find the energy already supplied, read its horizontal coordinate, $5.25\,\mathrm{kJ}$. Point B marks the end of the phase change.

$$
Q_{\mathrm{inizio}}=5.25\,\mathrm{kJ}.
$$
