A mixture of ice and pure water is in equilibrium at normal atmospheric pressure. A small amount of energy is added without melting all the ice. What changes?

- The temperature of all phases necessarily rises.
- The amount of liquid increases.
- Total mass increases.
- It becomes only vapour.

## Explanation

Transferred heat changes the amount of substance in each phase. During equilibrium coexistence, temperature stays at the melting point.
