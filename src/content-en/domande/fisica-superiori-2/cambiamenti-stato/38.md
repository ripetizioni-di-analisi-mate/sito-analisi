Ice at zero receives $334\,\mathrm W$ for $90\,\mathrm s$. How much ice melts with $L_f=334000\,\mathrm{J/kg}$ and enough initial ice?

- $0.9\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.09\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.009\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $90\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Useful power is joules delivered each second. On completion, the energy supplied reaches the threshold for the stages included.

$$
m=\frac{334\cdot90}{334000}\,\mathrm{kg}=0.09\,\mathrm{kg}.
$$
