The balance uses one value of $c$ to take water from below boiling to vapour at constant pressure. What term is missing?

- A second Celsius to kelvin conversion for each interval.
- The vaporisation energy.
- A term for the vessel weight.
- Thermal resistance by definition.

## Explanation

In the liquid stage, specific heat capacity describes the warming up to boiling. At the boiling point, water changes phase and the balance needs the term $mL_v$. If the vapour is heated after that, its warming is another stage with the specific heat capacity of vapour.
