The balance uses one value of $c$ to take water from below boiling to vapour at constant pressure. What term is missing?

- A second Celsius to kelvin conversion for each interval.
- The vaporisation energy.
- A term for the vessel weight.
- Thermal resistance by definition.

## Explanation

While liquid water and vapour coexist at boiling, energy changes the phase without raising temperature. The contribution is mass times latent heat.
