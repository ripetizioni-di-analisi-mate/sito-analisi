A $0.15\,\mathrm{kg}$ ice block at zero receives $62.7\,\mathrm{kJ}$ and melts entirely. With $L_f=334\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$ and $c_a=4200\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$, find its final temperature.

- $20\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $0\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $12\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $80\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$

## Explanation

The melting threshold is $0.15\cdot334\,\mathrm{kJ}=50.1\,\mathrm{kJ}$. Of the initial $62.7\,\mathrm{kJ}$, $12.6\,\mathrm{kJ}$ remains for the newly formed water. Its heat capacity is $630\,\mathrm{J/K}$, so it warms by $20\,\mathrm K$. Since the liquid starts at zero, its final temperature is $20\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. The positive remainder is the check that justifies calculating the temperature of the entirely liquid water.

$$
T_f=0\,{}^\circ\mathrm C+\frac{62700-0.15\cdot334000}{0.15\cdot4200}\,\mathrm K=20\,{}^\circ\mathrm C.
$$
