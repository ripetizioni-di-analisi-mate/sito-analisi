A $0.15\,\mathrm{kg}$ ice block at zero receives $62.7\,\mathrm{kJ}$ and melts entirely. With $L_f=334\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$ and $c_a=4200\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$, find its final temperature.

- $20\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $0\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $12\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $80\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$

## Explanation

Separate sections with changing temperature from constant-temperature sections with a phase change. The sum covers the entire path.

$$
T_f=0\,{}^\circ\mathrm C+\frac{62700-0.15\cdot334000}{0.15\cdot4200}\,\mathrm K=20\,{}^\circ\mathrm C.
$$
