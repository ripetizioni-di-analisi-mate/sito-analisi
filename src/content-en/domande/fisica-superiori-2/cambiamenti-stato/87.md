Two liquids of heat capacities $420$ and $840\,\mathrm{J/K}$ are heated within a single phase. What is the ratio of the slope of the first $T(Q)$ curve to the second?

- $2$
- $1$
- $0.5$
- $4$

## Explanation

Read energy contributions as differences of horizontal coordinates. Use specific heat capacity on sloped sections and latent heat on the plateau.

$$
\frac{s_1}{s_2}=\frac{840}{420}=2.
$$
