Two liquids of heat capacities $420$ and $840\,\mathrm{J/K}$ are heated within a single phase. What is the ratio of the slope of the first $T(Q)$ curve to the second?

- $2$
- $1$
- $0.5$
- $4$

## Explanation

On both sections the slope is the reciprocal of heat capacity. The first liquid has a heat capacity of $420\,\mathrm{J/K}$ and the second $840\,\mathrm{J/K}$. The slope ratio is $\dfrac{840}{420}=2$. For equal heat absorbed, the first liquid therefore warms by twice as much.

$$
\frac{s_1}{s_2}=\frac{840}{420}=2.
$$
