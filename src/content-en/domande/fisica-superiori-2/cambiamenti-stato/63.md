Where on a heating graph can latent heat be found from the energy width and the mass?

- The section in which any phase warms uniformly.
- The plateau of complete phase change.
- Only at the starting point without an energy change.
- The whole curve regardless of the process.

## Explanation

The plateau attributed to the transition is where energy changes phase. Its width divided by mass gives L.
