Where on a heating graph can latent heat be found from the energy width and the mass?

- The section in which any phase warms uniformly.
- The plateau of complete phase change.
- Only at the starting point without an energy change.
- The whole curve regardless of the process.

## Explanation

Along the plateau, supplied energy changes the phase while holding the equilibrium temperature. The difference between the energy readings at the two ends of the plateau equals $mL$ if the entire mass changes phase. Dividing that energy width by $m$ gives the specific latent heat.
