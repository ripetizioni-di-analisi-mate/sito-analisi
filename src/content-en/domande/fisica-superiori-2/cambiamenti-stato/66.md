A cooling curve crosses a plateau as the sample becomes solid. Against a graph of cumulative energy removed, how does the temperature change on that section?

- It rises linearly.
- It falls to absolute zero.
- It remains approximately constant.
- It doubles at the end.

## Explanation

Energy continues to be extracted along the plateau, but temperature stays near the freezing point while the sample contains both phases. As the horizontal coordinate increases, the solid fraction grows. Once the remaining liquid has frozen, extracting more energy can cool the solid.
