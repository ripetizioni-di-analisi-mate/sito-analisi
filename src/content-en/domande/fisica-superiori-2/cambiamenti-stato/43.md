All $0.15\,\mathrm{kg}$ of ice must melt at zero. If water can give up $25.05\,\mathrm{kJ}$ per kilogram, with $L_f=334\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$ what minimum water mass is needed?

- $0.5\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.75\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $2\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The ice requires $0.15\cdot334\,\mathrm{kJ}=50.1\,\mathrm{kJ}$ to melt. Each kilogram of water can release $25.05\,\mathrm{kJ}$. Therefore $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water is needed. At the minimum mass, melting has just finished and the heat available by cooling to zero equals the required threshold.

$$
m_a=\frac{0.15\cdot334}{25.05}\,\mathrm{kg}=2\,\mathrm{kg}.
$$
