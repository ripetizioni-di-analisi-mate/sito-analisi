A plateau runs from 75 s to 225 s with constant useful power $200\,\mathrm W$. What energy corresponds to that section?

- $45000\,\mathrm J$
- $30000\,\mathrm J$
- $15000\,\mathrm J$
- $60000\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Use the difference between the end and start times of the plateau, $225-75=150$ seconds. The sample receives $200\,\mathrm J$ each second. The plateau therefore corresponds to $30000\,\mathrm J$. Using the final time alone would include energy transferred before the transition.

$$
Q=200(225-75)\,\mathrm J=30000\,\mathrm J.
$$
