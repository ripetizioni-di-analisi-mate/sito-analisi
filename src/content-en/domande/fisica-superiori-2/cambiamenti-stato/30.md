A plateau runs from 75 s to 225 s with constant useful power $200\,\mathrm W$. What energy corresponds to that section?

- $45000\,\mathrm J$
- $30000\,\mathrm J$
- $15000\,\mathrm J$
- $60000\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Read energy contributions as differences of horizontal coordinates. Use specific heat capacity on sloped sections and latent heat on the plateau.

$$
Q=200(225-75)\,\mathrm J=30000\,\mathrm J.
$$
