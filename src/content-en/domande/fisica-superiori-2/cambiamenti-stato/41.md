In an insulated vessel $0.50\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water at $12\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ cools to zero. With $c_a=4200\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$, what maximum energy is available to melt ice?

- $25200\,\mathrm J$
- $50400\,\mathrm J$
- $2100\,\mathrm J$
- $4200\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

To find the heat available before the mixture reaches zero, consider only the cooling of the water. Its temperature falls by $12\,\mathrm K$. The magnitude of the transfer is $0.50\cdot4200\cdot12\,\mathrm J=25200\,\mathrm J$. This value can then be compared with the heat required to melt the ice.

$$
Q_{\mathrm{disp}}=0.50\cdot4200\cdot12\,\mathrm J=25200\,\mathrm J.
$$
