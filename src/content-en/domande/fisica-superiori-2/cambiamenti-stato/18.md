Supply $100.2\,\mathrm{kJ}$ to ice at zero. With $L_f=334\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$ and enough ice available, how many grams melt?

- $30\,\mathrm g$
- $300\,\mathrm g$
- $0.3\,\mathrm g$
- $3000\,\mathrm g$

## Explanation

The phase change takes place at its transition temperature. The contribution is $m_{\mathrm{tr}}L$ in magnitude, with a sign set by the transfer direction.

$$
m=\frac{100.2}{334}\,\mathrm{kg}=0.30\,\mathrm{kg}=300\,\mathrm g.
$$
