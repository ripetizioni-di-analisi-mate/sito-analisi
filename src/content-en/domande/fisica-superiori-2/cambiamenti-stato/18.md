Two samples at zero contain water and ice. The first contains $0.020\,\mathrm{kg}$ of ice and the second contains $0.080\,\mathrm{kg}$. Each sample receives $10.02\,\mathrm{kJ}$. With $L_f=334\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$ and no heat losses, which sample reaches a temperature above zero?

- Both
- The first sample only
- The second sample only
- Neither sample

## Explanation

The energy supplied can melt $0.030\,\mathrm{kg}$ of ice. In the first sample all $0.020\,\mathrm{kg}$ melts and energy is left to warm the water. In the second, $0.050\,\mathrm{kg}$ of ice remains, so the temperature stays at zero with both phases in equilibrium.
