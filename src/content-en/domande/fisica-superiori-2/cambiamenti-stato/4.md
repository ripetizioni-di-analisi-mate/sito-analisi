A mass of water of $0.5\,\mathrm{kg}$ freezes completely at $0\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}$ at normal atmospheric pressure. With $L_f=334000\,\mathrm{J/kg}$, what is the heat for the sample, taken as positive if absorbed? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $167000\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $-83500\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $-334000\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $-167000\,\mathrm{J}$.

## Explanation

Half a kilogram of freezing water releases half the latent heat for a kilogram. The magnitude is $0.50\cdot334000\,\mathrm J=167000\,\mathrm J$. Because heat leaves the sample, its balance contains $-167000\,\mathrm J$. If the surroundings receive all that heat, the same number appears in their balance with positive sign.

$$
\begin{aligned}
Q &= - L m \\[6pt]
&= - \left(334000\right) \left(0.5\right) \\[6pt]
&\simeq -167000\,\mathrm{J}
\end{aligned}
$$
