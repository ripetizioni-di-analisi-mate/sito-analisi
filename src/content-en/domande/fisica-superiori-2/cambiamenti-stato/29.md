On a heating curve, complete melting has width $8.35\,\mathrm{kJ}$. With $L_f=334\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$, what is the sample mass?

- $0.025\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $25\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.0025\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Read energy contributions as differences of horizontal coordinates. Use specific heat capacity on sloped sections and latent heat on the plateau.

$$
m=\frac{8.35}{334}\,\mathrm{kg}=0.025\,\mathrm{kg}.
$$
