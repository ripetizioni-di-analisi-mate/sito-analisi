On a heating curve, complete melting has width $8.35\,\mathrm{kJ}$. With $L_f=334\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$, what is the sample mass?

- $0.025\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $25\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.0025\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The plateau covers complete melting, so its energy width is $mL_f$. Divide $8.35\,\mathrm{kJ}$ by $334\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$ to get $0.025\,\mathrm{kg}$. To check the value, express the $25\,\mathrm g$ in kilograms and multiply by the latent heat. The vertical coordinate gives the transition temperature. The melting temperature alone does not determine how much ice is present.

$$
m=\frac{8.35}{334}\,\mathrm{kg}=0.025\,\mathrm{kg}.
$$
