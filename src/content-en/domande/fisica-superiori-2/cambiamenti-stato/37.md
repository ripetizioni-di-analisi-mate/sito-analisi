An $835\,\mathrm W$ heater delivers $40\%$ of its power to $0.20\,\mathrm{kg}$ of ice already at zero. With $L_f=334000\,\mathrm{J/kg}$, how long does complete melting take?

- $200\,\mathrm s$
- $80\,\mathrm s$
- $500\,\mathrm s$
- $100\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

The sample receives $40\%$ of $835\,\mathrm W$, or $334\,\mathrm W$. Melting $0.20\,\mathrm{kg}$ at zero requires $66800\,\mathrm J$. Dividing by the useful joules per second gives $200\,\mathrm s$. Efficiency changes the time while the mass sets the energy threshold.

$$
t=\frac{0.20\cdot334000}{0.40\cdot835}\,\mathrm s=200\,\mathrm s.
$$
