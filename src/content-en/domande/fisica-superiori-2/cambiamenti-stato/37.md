A heater delivers the same net power to two pure samples already at their melting points. Sample A has twice the mass of B, yet both take the same time to melt completely. What is the ratio of their specific latent heats $\dfrac{L_A}{L_B}$?

- $\dfrac12$
- $2$
- $1$
- $4$

## Explanation

Equal net powers and times give equal energies. Thus $m_A L_A=m_B L_B$. Since $m_A=2m_B$, each kilogram of A requires half as much energy as a kilogram of B.
