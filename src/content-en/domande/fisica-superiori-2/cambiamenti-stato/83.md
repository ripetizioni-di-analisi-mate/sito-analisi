Why can water boil below one hundred degrees in the mountains?

- Water changes its molar mass.
- The property depends only on the vessel’s heat capacity.
- Kelvins change size with altitude.
- The external pressure is lower.

## Explanation

Atmospheric pressure falls with altitude. Boiling begins when the saturation vapour pressure reaches the external pressure. Since the threshold is lower at altitude, the water reaches it at a lower temperature. The familiar hundred degrees applies at normal atmospheric pressure.
