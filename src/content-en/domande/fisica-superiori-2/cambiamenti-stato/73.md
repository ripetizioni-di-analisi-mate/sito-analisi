A timing measurement includes warming cold ice to zero. Only $mL_f$ is divided by the total duration to estimate power. How does that estimate compare?

- It is too high.
- It always equals the exact power.
- It is too low.
- It is negative.

## Explanation

The measured time also includes warming the initial ice. The corresponding useful energy is the sum of the energy to reach zero and $mL_f$. Dividing a numerator that contains only the latent part by the same time underestimates the average useful power. The simpler ratio would become valid in a trial where the ice starts exactly at the melting temperature.
