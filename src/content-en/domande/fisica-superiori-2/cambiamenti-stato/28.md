On a sloped section, $0.40\,\mathrm{kg}$ warms by $5\,\mathrm K$ while absorbing $4200\,\mathrm J$. Find the specific heat capacity of the phase.

- $840\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$
- $4200\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$
- $1680\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$
- $2100\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$

## Explanation

Read energy contributions as differences of horizontal coordinates. Use specific heat capacity on sloped sections and latent heat on the plateau.

$$
c=\frac{4200}{0.40\cdot5}\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}=2100\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}.
$$
