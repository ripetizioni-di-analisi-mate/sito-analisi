On a sloped section, $0.40\,\mathrm{kg}$ warms by $5\,\mathrm K$ while absorbing $4200\,\mathrm J$. Find the specific heat capacity of the phase.

- $840\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$
- $4200\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$
- $1680\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$
- $2100\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$

## Explanation

The sloped section corresponds to a single phase. The heat capacity of the sample is $\dfrac{4200}{5}\,\mathrm{J/K}=840\,\mathrm{J/K}$. Divide again by $0.40\,\mathrm{kg}$ to find specific heat capacity. The result is $2100\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$ and gives the energy needed per kilogram for a one-kelvin rise.

$$
c=\frac{4200}{0.40\cdot5}\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}=2100\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}.
$$
