How much energy melts $0.075\,\mathrm{kg}$ of ice already at zero with $L_f=334\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$?

- $2.505\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $250.5\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $25.05\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $334\,\mathrm{kJ}$

## Explanation

The phase change takes place at its transition temperature. The contribution is $m_{\mathrm{tr}}L$ in magnitude, with a sign set by the transfer direction.

$$
Q=0.075\cdot334\,\mathrm{kJ}=25.05\,\mathrm{kJ}.
$$
