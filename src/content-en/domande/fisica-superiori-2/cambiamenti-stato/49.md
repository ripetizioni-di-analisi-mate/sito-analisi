A connection to an empty chamber is opened. Which phase of a pure substance tends to occupy the entire accessible volume?

- Only the solid phase.
- The gas phase.
- The liquid phase in every orientation.
- No phase.

## Explanation

Gas particles are far apart and move through the available volume. A liquid occupies a volume set by its mass and density.
