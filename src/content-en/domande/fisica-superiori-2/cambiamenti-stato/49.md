A connection to an empty chamber is opened. Which phase of a pure substance tends to occupy the entire accessible volume?

- Only the solid phase.
- The gas phase.
- The liquid phase in every orientation.
- No phase.

## Explanation

In the gas phase the particles are far apart and can move throughout the connected chamber. After the connection opens, the gas expands to fill the entire accessible volume. With the same mass spread over more volume, its average density is lower than before expansion.
