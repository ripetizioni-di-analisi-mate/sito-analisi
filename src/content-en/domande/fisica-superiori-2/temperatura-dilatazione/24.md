A graph places $t_C$ on the horizontal axis and $T$ on the vertical axis. What is the vertical coordinate when the horizontal coordinate is zero?

- $0\,\mathrm K$
- $-273\,\mathrm K$
- $1\,\mathrm K$
- $273.15\,\mathrm K$

## Explanation

The vertical intercept is the value of $T$ where $t_C=0$. Substitute zero into $T=t_C+273.15$ to obtain $T=273.15\,\mathrm K$. The graph therefore crosses the vertical axis above the origin. The origin of the Celsius scale on the horizontal axis represents a different physical temperature from absolute zero.
