A graph places $t_C$ on the horizontal axis and $T$ on the vertical axis. What is the vertical coordinate when the horizontal coordinate is zero?

- $0\,\mathrm K$
- $-273\,\mathrm K$
- $1\,\mathrm K$
- $273.15\,\mathrm K$

## Explanation

Add $273.15$ to turn a Celsius value into an absolute value or subtract it to obtain the Celsius value.

$$
T=(0+273.15)\,\mathrm K=273.15\,\mathrm K.
$$
