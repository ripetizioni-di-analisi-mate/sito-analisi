A crystalline plate expands by a fraction $\varepsilon_x$ along one edge and $\varepsilon_y$ along the other. What is the first-order fractional area change?

- $2\varepsilon_x$ in all cases.
- $\varepsilon_x+\varepsilon_y$.
- Only $\varepsilon_x\varepsilon_y$.
- $\varepsilon_x-\varepsilon_y$ in all cases.

## Explanation

The area factor is $(1+\varepsilon_x)(1+\varepsilon_y)$. The sum is first order and the product is second order.
