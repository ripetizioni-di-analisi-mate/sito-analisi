Pure liquid water is warmed from $1\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ to $3\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ at atmospheric pressure, without loss of mass. How does its density change?

- It decreases because the volume increases.
- It remains fixed because mass is the same.
- It increases because mass enters.
- It increases because the volume decreases.

## Explanation

In this interval the volume of water decreases. Since $\rho=m/V$ with constant mass, its density increases.
