Which is the correct notation for a temperature of three hundred kelvins?

- $300\,{}^\circ\mathrm K$
- $300\,\mathrm K$
- $300\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $300\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$

## Explanation

The SI symbol for the kelvin is $\mathrm K$. The degree sign is part of the symbol $\mathrm{{}^\circ C}$.

In $300\,\mathrm K$, the space separates the number from the unit, and the letter is uppercase. A change on the absolute scale also uses kelvins, for example $\Delta T=5\,\mathrm K$.
