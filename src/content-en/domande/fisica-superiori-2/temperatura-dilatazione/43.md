The expected length increase of the rod over the temperature interval is $0.2\,\mathrm{mm}$. The ruler rounds to the nearest millimetre. Which change makes the estimate of $\alpha$ more informative?

- Round to the nearest centimetre.
- Express the temperature in Celsius instead of kelvins.
- Use a device that resolves hundredths of a millimetre.
- Reduce the temperature range again without changing the instrument.

## Explanation

The change is smaller than one ruler division. A finer measurement makes the two lengths distinguishable.

With $1\,\mathrm{mm}$ divisions, both measurements could give the same reading despite the different lengths. With $0.01\,\mathrm{mm}$ divisions, the change covers about twenty divisions, making the extension visible on the instrument.
