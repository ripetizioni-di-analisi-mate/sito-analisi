Two length readings on a rod differ by $0.2\,\mathrm{mm}$. The ruler rounds to the nearest millimetre. Which change makes the estimate of $\alpha$ more informative?

- Round to the nearest centimetre.
- Express the temperature in Celsius instead of kelvins.
- Use a device that resolves hundredths of a millimetre.
- Reduce the temperature range again without changing the instrument.

## Explanation

The change is smaller than one ruler division. A finer measurement makes the two lengths distinguishable.
