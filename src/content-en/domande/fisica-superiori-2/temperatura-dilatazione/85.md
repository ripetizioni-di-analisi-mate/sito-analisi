Strip A touches strip B, of the same initial length. Over one interval their free changes would be $0.6\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $1.1\,\mathrm{mm}$. What is the difference between their free expansions?

- $1.7\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $1.1\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.6\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The question compares the two free trends. When the strips are joined, that difference can cause bending.

$$
|\Delta L_B-\Delta L_A|=(1.1-0.6)\,\mathrm{mm}=0.5\,\mathrm{mm}.
$$
