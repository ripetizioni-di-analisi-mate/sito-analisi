A pot of lukewarm water touches a small piece of hotter metal. In which direction does net heat first flow?

- From the water to the metal, because there is more water.
- From the metal to the water.
- In both directions with a permanently zero net transfer.
- The direction depends only on the masses.

## Explanation

Spontaneous transfer is from the body at higher temperature to the one at lower temperature. The masses help determine the final temperature.

The pan may contain much more material than the metal does. To determine the initial direction, compare the two temperatures. Finding the final temperature also requires an energy balance.
