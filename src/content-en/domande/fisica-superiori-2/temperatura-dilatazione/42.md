A $2.0\,\mathrm m$ rod with $\alpha=2.0\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$ warms by $100\,\mathrm K$. What is the fractional extension as a percentage?

- $0.4\%$
- $0.2\%$
- $0.002\%$
- $2\%$

## Explanation

Divide the absolute change by the initial length. Multiply the dimensionless result by one hundred to express it as a percentage.

$$
\frac{\Delta L}{L_0}\simeq(2.0\cdot10^{-5})\cdot100=0.002=0.2\%.
$$

The length of $2.0\,\mathrm m$ would be needed to compute the absolute extension, which would be $4.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ here. Dividing by the initial length removes that factor. Rods of different lengths can therefore have the same percentage change.
