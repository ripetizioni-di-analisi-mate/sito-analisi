A liquid and its container have the same volume expansion coefficient over the small interval considered. What is the apparent change?

- Approximately zero.
- Twice the physical change.
- Always negative.
- Mainly determined by the container colour.

## Explanation

In this model the two changes start from the same volume and are equal. Their difference is approximately zero.

The liquid and the capacity up to the mark change in the same proportion. The liquid’s physical volume can therefore change even while the level still coincides with the reference mark, which has moved.
