A $2\,\mathrm m$ rod has two identical $1\,\mathrm m$ sections. The first warms by $50\,\mathrm K$ and the second stays at its original temperature. With $\alpha=2\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$, how much does the total length increase?

- $1\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $2\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

Temperature differs between the sections. Apply the model to each separately and only the warmed half contributes.

$$
\Delta L\simeq(2\cdot10^{-5})\cdot1\cdot50\,\mathrm m=1\,\mathrm{mm}.
$$
