After increasing by $60\,\mathrm K$, a fluid is at $323.15\,\mathrm K$. Find its initial temperature in degrees Celsius.

- $50\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $110\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $-10\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $263.15\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$

## Explanation

Work backwards through the heating to find the initial temperature. Subtracting $60\,\mathrm K$ from $323.15\,\mathrm K$ gives $263.15\,\mathrm K$. Converting this initial value gives $(263.15-273.15)\,{}^\circ\mathrm C=-10\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. As a check, the final value is $50\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$, exactly $60$ degrees above the initial one.
