After increasing by $60\,\mathrm K$, a fluid is at $323.15\,\mathrm K$. Find its initial temperature in degrees Celsius.

- $50\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $110\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $-10\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $263.15\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$

## Explanation

Add $273.15$ to turn a Celsius value into an absolute value or subtract it to obtain the Celsius value.

$$
t_{C,i}=(323.15-60-273.15)\,{}^\circ\mathrm C=-10\,{}^\circ\mathrm C.
$$
