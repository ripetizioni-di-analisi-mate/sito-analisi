A rod of length $7\,\mathrm m$ expands by $0.005775\,\mathrm m$ when warmed by $55\,\mathrm K$. What is its linear expansion coefficient? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $1.5\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$.
- $3\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$.
- $7.5\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$.
- $0.000825\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$.

## Explanation

Both the temperature and length changes are positive. In the unconstrained expansion model, divide $\Delta L$ by $L_0\Delta T$ to isolate $\alpha$. The ratio of the lengths is dimensionless, so the unit of the coefficient is $\mathrm{K^{-1}}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\alpha &= \frac{\Delta L}{\Delta T L} \\[6pt]
&= \frac{\left(0.005775\right)}{\left(55\right) \left(7\right)} \\[6pt]
&\simeq 1.5\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}
\end{aligned}
$$
