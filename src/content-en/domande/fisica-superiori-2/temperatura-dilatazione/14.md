A rectangular plate with an area of $6\,\mathrm{m^2}$ is heated. One side increases by a fraction of $0.0018$ and the other by $0.0006$. What is the first-order increase in area?

- $0.0108\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $0.0144\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $0.0216\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $0.0036\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

The sides have different relative increases. To first order their contributions add, giving a fractional increase of $0.0018+0.0006=0.0024$. Thus $\Delta A\simeq(6\,\mathrm{m^2})(0.0024)=0.0144\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The product of the two relative increases is $1.08\cdot10^{-6}$, much smaller than their sum, so the first-order estimate leaves it out.
