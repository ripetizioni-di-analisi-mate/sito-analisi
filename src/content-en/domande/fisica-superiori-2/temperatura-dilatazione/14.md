An isotropic plate has area $6\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and linear expansion coefficient $2\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$. Find the increase in area when heated by $60\,\mathrm K$, using the small-deformation approximation. Choose the value closest to the result.

- $0.0072\,\mathrm{m^2}$.
- $0.0144\,\mathrm{m^2}$.
- $0.0216\,\mathrm{m^2}$.
- $0.0288\,\mathrm{m^2}$.

## Explanation

The plate is isotropic and two dimensions contribute to its area. In the stated approximation the fractional change is $2\alpha\Delta T$. Multiply by the initial area to obtain the absolute increase in square metres.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta A &= 2 A \Delta T \alpha \\[6pt]
&= 2 \left(6\right) \left(60\right) \left(2\cdot10^{-5}\right) \\[6pt]
&\simeq 0.0144\,\mathrm{m^2}
\end{aligned}
$$
