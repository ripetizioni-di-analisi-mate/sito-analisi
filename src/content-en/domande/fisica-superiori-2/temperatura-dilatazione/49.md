A hole has diameter $40\,\mathrm{mm}$ before warming the unconstrained plate by $50\,\mathrm K$. With $\alpha=1.5\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$, how much does the diameter increase?

- $-0.03\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.06\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.03\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.003\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The diameter is the distance between opposite points on the rim and expands in the same fraction as any length of the plate.

$$
\Delta d\simeq(1.5\cdot10^{-5})\cdot40\cdot50\,\mathrm{mm}=0.03\,\mathrm{mm}.
$$
