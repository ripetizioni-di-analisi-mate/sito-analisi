An aluminium rod of length $1.8\,\mathrm m$ has $\alpha=2.4\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$. It elongates by $1.296\,\mathrm{mm}$. If it started at $12\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$, what temperature does it reach?

- $30\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $-18\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $54\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $42\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$

## Explanation

Find the interval from the expansion first. Then add it to the initial position on the Celsius scale.

$$
\begin{aligned}\Delta T&=\dfrac{1.296\cdot10^{-3}}{1.8\cdot2.4\cdot10^{-5}}\,\mathrm K=30\,\mathrm K\\[6pt]t_{C,f}&=(12+30)\,{}^\circ\mathrm C=42\,{}^\circ\mathrm C.\end{aligned}
$$
