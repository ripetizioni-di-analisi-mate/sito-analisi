The measured fractional area change is $\Delta A/A_0=8\cdot10^{-4}$ for a rise of $20\,\mathrm K$. What is the linear expansion coefficient of the isotropic plate?

- $4\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$
- $0.0004\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$
- $2\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$
- $8\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$

## Explanation

Two dimensions expand. The first-order fractional area change is $2\alpha\Delta T$.

$$
\alpha\simeq\frac{8\cdot10^{-4}}{2\cdot20}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}=2\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}.
$$

The relative change of one side is half the observed area fraction, or $4\cdot10^{-4}$. That fraction accumulated over $20\,\mathrm K$. Dividing by this interval gives the relative change in length per kelvin.
