In a geometrical expansion of a cube, each side grows by a factor of $1.01$. By how many percentage points does the exact increase in volume exceed the first-order estimate?

- $0.01$
- $0.000301$
- $3.0301$
- $0.0301$

## Explanation

A relative increase of $0.01$ along each side gives a first-order volume increase of $3\%$. The exact factor is $1.01^3=1.030301$, so the exact increase is $3.0301\%$. The difference is $0.0301$ percentage points and comes from the quadratic and cubic terms in the geometrical calculation.
