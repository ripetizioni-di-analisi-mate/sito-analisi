A geometric expansion multiplies each edge of a cube by $1.01$. What is the exact volume factor?

- $1.01$
- $1.0201$
- $1.03$
- $1.030301$

## Explanation

Volume is the product of the three sides. The factor $1.03$ is the linear estimate, while cubing gives the exact factor.

$$
\frac{V_f}{V_i}=(1.01)^3=1.030301.
$$
