A thermometer reads $30\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. What is this temperature in kelvin?

- $303.15\,\mathrm K$
- $-243.15\,\mathrm K$
- $403.15\,\mathrm K$
- $454.725\,\mathrm K$

## Explanation

The thermometer reading is a temperature, so the conversion accounts for the difference between the two origins. On the absolute scale, the point at zero Celsius is $273.15\,\mathrm K$. Starting there and adding another $30\,\mathrm K$ gives $T=(30+273.15)\,\mathrm K=303.15\,\mathrm K$.
