A thermometer reads $30\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}$. What is the temperature in kelvins? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $303.15\,\mathrm{K}$.
- $-243.15\,\mathrm{K}$.
- $403.15\,\mathrm{K}$.
- $454.725\,\mathrm{K}$.

## Explanation

The kelvin has the same size as the degree Celsius, so only the origin changes. The Celsius reading is a temperature value, so add $273.15$ to obtain its value in kelvins.

$$
\begin{aligned}
T &= t_C + \frac{5463}{20} \\[6pt]
&= \left(30\right) + \frac{5463}{20} \\[6pt]
&\simeq 303.15\,\mathrm{K}
\end{aligned}
$$
