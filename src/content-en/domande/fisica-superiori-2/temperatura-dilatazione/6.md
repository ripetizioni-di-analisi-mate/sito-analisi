A free isotropic plate has an area of $5\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and a linear expansion coefficient of $2\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$. Find the first-order increase in area after uniform heating by $50\,\mathrm K$.

- $0.005\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $0.01\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $0.015\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $0.001\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

Both sides have the same relative change $\alpha\Delta T=10^{-3}$. The first-order area calculation adds the contributions of the two sides, giving $2\cdot10^{-3}$. The increase is therefore $\Delta A\simeq(5\,\mathrm{m^2})(2\cdot10^{-3})=0.01\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The small relative change makes the product of the two changes negligible.
