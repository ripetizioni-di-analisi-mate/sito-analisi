A hot object and a cold object are separated by an ideal layer that prevents heat exchange. Their temperatures remain different. What does the absence of exchange imply?

- The temperatures must actually be equal.
- The insulation prevents transfer despite the temperature difference.
- The hot object necessarily has zero internal energy.
- The zeroth law shows that the objects have equal mass.

## Explanation

The experimental definition requires contact that allows heat transfer. Ideal insulation removes that pathway without making the temperatures equal.
