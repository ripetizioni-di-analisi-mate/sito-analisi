An ideal gas at constant volume goes from $300\,\mathrm K$ to $330\,\mathrm K$. What is the percentage increase in its absolute temperature?

- $10\%$
- $30\%$
- $110\%$
- $9.09\%$

## Explanation

Divide the increase by the initial absolute temperature.

$$
\frac{T_f-T_i}{T_i}=\frac{30}{300}=0.10=10\%.
$$
