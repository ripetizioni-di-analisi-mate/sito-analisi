A gas at constant volume goes from $300\,\mathrm K$ to $360\,\mathrm K$. By what percentage does its absolute temperature increase?

- $20\%$
- $60\%$
- $120\%$
- $\dfrac{50}{3}\%$

## Explanation

The increase is $360-300=60\,\mathrm K$. A percentage increase uses the initial temperature as its reference. The calculation is $\dfrac{60}{300}=0.20$, or $20\%$. Dividing the same rise by the final temperature would find the fraction of the final value accounted for by the increase, which answers a different question.
