The graph of $T$ in kelvins as a function of $t_C$ in degrees Celsius passes through $(0\,;\,273.15)$ and $(100\,;\,373.15)$. After swapping the axes, what formula gives the new vertical coordinate?

- $t_C=T-273.15$.
- $t_C=273.15-T$.
- $t_C=T+273.15$.
- $t_C=T$ for every value.

## Explanation

Find the inverse relation by isolating $t_C$ in $T=t_C+273.15$. The slope is still one.
