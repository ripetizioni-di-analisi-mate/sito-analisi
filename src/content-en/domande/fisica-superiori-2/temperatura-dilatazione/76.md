The liquid column in a thermometer expands more than its capillary tube. What happens to the reading when the thermometer warms?

- It falls for every temperature rise.
- It stays fixed because the capillary expands.
- It rises because the apparent expansion is positive.
- It changes only if the liquid mass changes.

## Explanation

Part of the expansion is taken up by the growth of the container. The excess pushes liquid into the column.
