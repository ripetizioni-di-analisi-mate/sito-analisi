Two identical rods are fixed only at their outer ends. Each advances $1.2\,\mathrm{mm}$ towards the central gap when warmed. By how much does the gap narrow?

- $1.2\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.6\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $2.4\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

Both inner ends move towards the gap, so their displacement magnitudes add.

$$
\Delta s_{\mathrm{chiusura}}=(1.2+1.2)\,\mathrm{mm}=2.4\,\mathrm{mm}.
$$

The two motions have opposite directions along the axis, but both reduce the distance between the ends. The gap loses $1.2\,\mathrm{mm}$ on the left and the same amount on the right. Subtracting the magnitudes would use the wrong relative direction.
