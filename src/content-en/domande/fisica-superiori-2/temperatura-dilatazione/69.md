Two initially identical containers hold equal volumes of the same liquid up to their respective reference marks. The first container has the larger volume coefficient. Which shows the smaller apparent increase for the same warming?

- The second.
- The two show the same apparent increase.
- The first.
- The container coefficient affects only the colour of the reading.

## Explanation

The physical change in the liquid is the same. A larger container change is subtracted in the first case.

Both containers start with the same capacity up to the mark. The first therefore has the larger increase in capacity, which accommodates a greater share of the expansion. The comparison is between the apparent readings.
