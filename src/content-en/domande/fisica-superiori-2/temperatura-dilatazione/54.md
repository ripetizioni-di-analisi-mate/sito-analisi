Two solids of the same material occupy $100$ and $350\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. They warm by $40\,\mathrm K$ with $\beta=6\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$. What is the total increase in volume?

- $1.08\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $0.84\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $0.24\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $0.36\,\mathrm{cm^3}$

## Explanation

The two solids have the same fractional change. Multiply that fraction by the total initial volume.

$$
\Delta V_{\mathrm{tot}}\simeq6\cdot10^{-5}\cdot(100+350)\cdot40\,\mathrm{cm^3}=1.08\,\mathrm{cm^3}.
$$
