A plate with an elliptical hole expands equally in all directions. What happens to the ratio of the semimajor to semiminor axis?

- It increases when the plate warms.
- It always becomes 1.
- It decreases to zero.
- It stays the same.

## Explanation

Both semiaxes are multiplied by the same factor. That factor cancels in the ratio.
