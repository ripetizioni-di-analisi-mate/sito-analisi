A plate undergoes a fractional area increase of $0.4\%$. What corresponding fractional volume increase would the isotropic material have in the linear model?

- $0.6\%$
- $0.4\%$
- $1.2\%$
- $0.2\%$

## Explanation

Compare the two first-order terms, which are respectively three and two times the fractional length change.

$$
\frac{\Delta V}{V_0}\simeq\frac32\frac{\Delta A}{A_0}=\frac32\cdot0.4\%=0.6\%.
$$

First recover the relative extension along one side from the area increase, which gives $0.2\%$. Volume depends on three equivalent directions and includes three contributions. The reasoning requires the same relative expansion in every direction.
