The liquid in a container shows an apparent increase of $2.5\,\mathrm{mL}$ from an initial $250\,\mathrm{mL}$. What is the relative apparent change as a percentage?

- $10\%$
- $0.01\%$
- $1\%$
- $2.5\%$

## Explanation

Compare the indicated change with the initial reference volume. Millilitres cancel and the relative change is dimensionless.

$$
\frac{\Delta V_{\mathrm{app}}}{V_0}=\frac{2.5}{250}=0.01=1\%.
$$
