Two samples are each in equilibrium with the same sensor, which stays at the same temperature in both comparisons. What can be inferred about the samples?

- The masses are equal.
- The internal energies are equal.
- The temperatures are equal.
- The materials are the same.

## Explanation

Each sample is at the temperature of the sensor. Because the sensor stays at the same temperature in both comparisons, the zeroth law gives equal temperatures for the samples. Bringing them into contact through a conducting wall would result in zero net heat flow between them.
