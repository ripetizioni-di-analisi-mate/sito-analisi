Two samples are each in equilibrium with the same sensor. What follows when they are placed in contact?

- The masses are equal.
- The internal energies are equal.
- The temperatures are equal.
- The materials are the same.

## Explanation

The zeroth law transfers the equilibrium relation through the sensor. It concerns the temperatures of the bodies.
