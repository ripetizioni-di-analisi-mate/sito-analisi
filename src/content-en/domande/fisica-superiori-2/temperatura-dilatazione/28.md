A physical equation contains the ratio $T_2/T_1$ of absolute temperatures. Which values are suitable for the calculation?

- Two unconverted Celsius values.
- Only the difference in degrees Celsius.
- One kelvin value and one Celsius value.
- Two kelvin values.

## Explanation

Shifting both readings changes their ratio. The kelvin scale gives temperatures from absolute zero.
