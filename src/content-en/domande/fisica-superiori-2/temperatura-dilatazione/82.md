The small-expansion model gives $\alpha\Delta T=0.0004$. What does that number represent?

- The absolute change in millimetres for every rod.
- The final temperature in kelvins.
- The fractional change in length.
- The volume coefficient.

## Explanation

Multiplying $\mathrm{K^{-1}}$ by $\mathrm K$ yields a dimensionless number. In the model it is the ratio $\Delta L/L_0$.
