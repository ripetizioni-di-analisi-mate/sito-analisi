For a free rod of length $2\,\mathrm m$, a displacement sensor reads $0.30\,\mathrm{mm}$ at $\Delta T=0$ and $1.10\,\mathrm{mm}$ at $\Delta T=40\,\mathrm K$. If the zero offset is constant, what linear expansion coefficient follows?

- $1.375\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$
- $5\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$
- $10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$
- $10^{-4}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$

## Explanation

Displacement starts at zero when the temperature remains at its initial value. Subtracting the two readings removes the constant offset and gives an extension of $0.80\,\mathrm{mm}$. Divide $0.00080\,\mathrm m$ by $2\,\mathrm m$ and by $40\,\mathrm K$ to get $10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$. Using the final reading would also include the zero offset in the calculation.
