The end of a rod fixed at just one side moves $2\,\mathrm{mm}$ during a $40\,\mathrm K$ increase. What is the change per kelvin?

- $20\,\mathrm{mm/K}$
- $0.05\,\mathrm{mm/K}$
- $80\,\mathrm{mm/K}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{mm/K}$

## Explanation

Find a slope by dividing the vertical change by the horizontal change.

$$
\dfrac{\Delta L}{\Delta T}=\dfrac{2}{40}\,\mathrm{mm/K}=0.05\,\mathrm{mm/K}.
$$

The opposite end is fixed, so the measured displacement equals the change in length. In this interval the result gives the number of millimetres added per kelvin. The rod’s initial length would also be needed to determine the material coefficient.
