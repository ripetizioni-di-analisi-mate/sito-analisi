When may we replace $(1+\varepsilon)^3-1$ with $3\varepsilon$?

- For any value of $\varepsilon$.
- When the magnitude of $\varepsilon$ is small compared with 1.
- When $\varepsilon$ is exactly 1.
- Only when temperature is measured in Celsius.

## Explanation

The approximation discards $3\varepsilon^2+\varepsilon^3$. Those terms are small in magnitude compared with $3\varepsilon$ when $|\varepsilon|$ is small.
