A pin barely fits through a hole in an unconstrained plate. The plate is heated while the pin stays cool. What happens to the available clearance?

- It increases because the hole diameter grows.
- It decreases because the material closes the hole.
- It is unchanged because the space is empty.
- It depends only on the mass of the pin.

## Explanation

The hole is defined by points on the rim. In the unconstrained material the distance between opposite points grows, while the pin keeps the same diameter.

Diametral clearance is the hole diameter minus the pin diameter. Heating increases only the first term. This conclusion relies on the plate being free to expand and the pin staying at its original temperature.
