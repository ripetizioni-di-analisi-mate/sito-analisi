A sample is heated only at one tip. Why is the tip temperature alone insufficient for using $\Delta L=\alpha L_0\Delta T$ over the whole rod?

- The model with a single $\Delta T$ assumes uniform temperature.
- Only a negative Celsius value is required.
- The coefficient has units of length.
- Each tip must be fixed.

## Explanation

Different sections may undergo different changes. Estimating the total expansion requires the temperature distribution or a model of it.
