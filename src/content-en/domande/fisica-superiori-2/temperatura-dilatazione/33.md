The mean of two Celsius readings is $18\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. How do you get their mean in kelvins?

- Add 273.15 to the mean.
- Add 273.15 twice to the mean.
- The mean remains 18.
- Double the mean.

## Explanation

The sum of the two converted readings contains two copies of the constant. Dividing by two leaves one, so the mean becomes $291.15\,\mathrm K$.

The conversion preserves interval sizes, so the mean remains equally far from the converted values. Converting each reading separately gives the same result as shifting the already computed mean.
