A sample goes from $9\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ to $52\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. What is its temperature rise in kelvin?

- $61\,\mathrm K$
- $-43\,\mathrm K$
- $43\,\mathrm K$
- $316.15\,\mathrm K$

## Explanation

To find the rise, subtract the initial temperature from the final one. Both values are on the same scale, and a difference of one degree is one kelvin. Thus $\Delta T=(52-9)\,\mathrm K=43\,\mathrm K$. Converting both values to kelvin would add the same constant to each and leave the difference unchanged.
