A sample goes from $9\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}$ to $52\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}$. What is the temperature increase in kelvins? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $316.15\,\mathrm{K}$.
- $61\,\mathrm{K}$.
- $43\,\mathrm{K}$.
- $-43\,\mathrm{K}$.

## Explanation

The final value is greater than the initial value, so the temperature interval is positive. The scales differ by a constant that cancels when we subtract. Calculate the difference directly from the Celsius values.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta T &= Tf - Ti \\[6pt]
&= \left(52\right) - \left(9\right) \\[6pt]
&\simeq 43\,\mathrm{K}
\end{aligned}
$$
