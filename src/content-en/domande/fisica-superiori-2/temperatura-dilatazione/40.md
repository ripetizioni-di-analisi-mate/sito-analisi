The line of $\Delta L$ versus $\Delta T$ for a rod has slope $s$. How do you find $\alpha$?

- $sL_0$.
- $L_0/s$.
- The vertical intercept.
- $s/L_0$ in consistent units.

## Explanation

In the linear model the slope equals $\alpha L_0$. Dividing by the initial length gives the coefficient.

If $s$ is measured in millimetres per kelvin, express $L_0$ in millimetres as well. Division cancels the length unit and leaves $\mathrm{K^{-1}}$. The result describes the material over the interval in which the graph is approximately straight.
