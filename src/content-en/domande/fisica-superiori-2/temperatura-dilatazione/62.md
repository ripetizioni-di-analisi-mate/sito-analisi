In an experiment the liquid volume grows by $8\,\mathrm{mL}$ and the capacity up to the mark grows by $2\,\mathrm{mL}$. Find the apparent increase.

- $10\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $8\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $4\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $6\,\mathrm{mL}$

## Explanation

The original mark moves with the container. Subtract the change in volume up to that mark from the change in the liquid volume.

$$
\Delta V_{\mathrm{app}}\simeq(8-2)\,\mathrm{mL}=6\,\mathrm{mL}.
$$

Of the liquid’s extra $8\,\mathrm{mL}$, the first $2\,\mathrm{mL}$ fill the new space below the reference mark. The remaining $6\,\mathrm{mL}$ exceed the mark after warming. If the capacity up to the mark stayed the same, the entire increase would be visible.
