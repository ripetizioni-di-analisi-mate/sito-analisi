Heating raises the reading by $4\,\mathrm{mL}$ relative to the mark. The volume up to that mark also rises by $0.3\,\mathrm{mL}$. How much does the liquid physically expand?

- $3.7\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $4.3\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $4\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $0.3\,\mathrm{mL}$

## Explanation

To recover the physical change, add the change in reference volume to the apparent change.

$$
\Delta V_{\mathrm{liq}}\simeq(4+0.3)\,\mathrm{mL}=4.3\,\mathrm{mL}.
$$
