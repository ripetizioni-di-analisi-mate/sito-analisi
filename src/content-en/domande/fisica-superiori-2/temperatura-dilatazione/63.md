After heating, the liquid extends above its initial mark by an apparent volume of $4\,\mathrm{mL}$. The capacity up to the mark has increased by $0.8\,\mathrm{mL}$. How much has the liquid volume increased?

- $3.2\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $4.8\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $4\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $0.8\,\mathrm{mL}$

## Explanation

Initially the liquid reaches the mark, so its initial volume equals the initial capacity up to that mark. As it warms, the liquid also fills the new space below the mark. The physical increase therefore includes $0.8\,\mathrm{mL}$ below the reference and $4\,\mathrm{mL}$ above it, totalling $4.8\,\mathrm{mL}$.
