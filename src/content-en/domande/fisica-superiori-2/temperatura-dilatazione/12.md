A free isotropic plate has an initial edge length of $4.2\,\mathrm m$. When it is heated uniformly, its area increases by a fraction of $0.0024$. Estimate the first-order increase in the length of the edge in millimetres.

- $10.08\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $2.52\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.00504\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $5.04\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

Both sides of the isotropic plate increase by the same fraction. To first order, the relative increase in area is twice the relative increase along one side, which is therefore $0.0024\div2=0.0012$. Multiply this fraction by $4.2\,\mathrm m$ to obtain $0.00504\,\mathrm m$, or $5.04\,\mathrm{mm}$.
