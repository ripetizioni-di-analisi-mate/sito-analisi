A rod of length $6\,\mathrm m$ has linear expansion coefficient $\alpha=1.2\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$ and is warmed by $70\,\mathrm K$. Find the increase in length in millimetres. Choose the value closest to the result.

- $0.00504\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $10.08\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $0.84\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $5.04\,\mathrm{mm}$.

## Explanation

For an unconstrained rod use $\Delta L\simeq\alpha L_0\Delta T$. The product $\alpha\Delta T$ is the fractional change and has no unit. Multiplying by $L_0$ in metres gives the change in metres. Multiply its numerical value by $1000$ to express it in millimetres.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta L &= 1000 \Delta T L \alpha \\[6pt]
&= 1000 \left(70\right) \left(6\right) \left(1.2\cdot10^{-5}\right) \\[6pt]
&\simeq 5.04\,\mathrm{mm}
\end{aligned}
$$
