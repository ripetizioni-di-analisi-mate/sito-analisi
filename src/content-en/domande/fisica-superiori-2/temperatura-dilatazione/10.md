The temperature of an object is $323.15\,\mathrm K$. Express it in degrees Celsius. Choose the value closest to the result.

- $596.3\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}$.
- $50\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}$.
- $323.15\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}$.
- $60\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}$.

## Explanation

Isolate $t_C$ in $T=t_C+273.15$ by subtracting $273.15$. Use the stated absolute temperature, in $\mathrm K$, and express the result in degrees Celsius.

$$
\begin{aligned}
t_C &= T - \frac{5463}{20} \\[6pt]
&= \left(323.15\right) - \frac{5463}{20} \\[6pt]
&\simeq 50\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}
\end{aligned}
$$
