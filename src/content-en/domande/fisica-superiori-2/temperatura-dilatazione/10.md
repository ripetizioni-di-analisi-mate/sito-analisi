A sensor reads $313.15\,\mathrm K$ and underestimates all temperatures by $10\,\mathrm K$. What is the corrected sample temperature in degrees Celsius?

- $40\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $50\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $30\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $323.15\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$

## Explanation

The reading is below the true value, so add $10\,\mathrm K$ to obtain an absolute temperature of $323.15\,\mathrm K$. Subtract $273.15$ to get the Celsius value, $50\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. A value of $40\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ would correspond to the reading before the correction.
