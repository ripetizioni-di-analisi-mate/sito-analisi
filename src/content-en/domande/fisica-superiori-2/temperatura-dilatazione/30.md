A thermometer reads $2\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ above every true value. Between readings of $14$ and $23\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$, what is the true increase?

- $7\,\mathrm K$
- $9\,\mathrm K$
- $11\,\mathrm K$
- $37\,\mathrm K$

## Explanation

The offset is additive and identical for both readings, so it cancels in the difference.

$$
\Delta T=[(23-2)-(14-2)]\,\mathrm K=9\,\mathrm K.
$$
