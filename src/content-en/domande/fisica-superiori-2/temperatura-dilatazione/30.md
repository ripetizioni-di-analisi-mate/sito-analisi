A thermometer reads $2\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ above every true value. Between readings of $14$ and $23\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$, what is the true increase?

- $7\,\mathrm K$
- $9\,\mathrm K$
- $11\,\mathrm K$
- $37\,\mathrm K$

## Explanation

The offset is additive and identical for both readings, so it cancels in the difference.

$$
\Delta T=[(23-2)-(14-2)]\,\mathrm K=9\,\mathrm K.
$$

The corrected readings are therefore $12$ and $21\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. Their difference equals the difference between the uncorrected readings. If the instrument also distorted the slope of the scale, subtraction alone would leave the scale error.
