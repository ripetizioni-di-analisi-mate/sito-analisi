An object is at $313.15\,\mathrm K$. Express its temperature in degrees Celsius.

- $586.3\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $40\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $-40\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $313.15\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$

## Explanation

Zero Celsius corresponds to $273.15\,\mathrm K$, so find the difference between the given temperature and that point. The difference is $313.15-273.15=40$, giving $40\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ on the Celsius scale. The sign is positive because the object is warmer than the zero Celsius reference.
