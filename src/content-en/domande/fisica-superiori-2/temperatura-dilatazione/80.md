A block is constrained along two axes, and only one dimension increases by the fraction $\varepsilon$. What is the fractional volume change if the other dimensions stay fixed?

- $3\varepsilon$.
- $\varepsilon^3$.
- $0$.
- $\varepsilon$.

## Explanation

Only one factor changes in the product of the three dimensions. The volume is therefore multiplied by $1+\varepsilon$.

In the ratio $V_f/V_0$, the constrained sides each have a factor of $1$, and the free side has a factor of $1+\varepsilon$. The ratio is exactly $1+\varepsilon$. Subtracting $1$ leaves $\varepsilon$, with no higher-order terms to omit.
