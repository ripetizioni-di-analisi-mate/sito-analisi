For $500\,\mathrm{mL}$ of liquid and $\Delta T=20\,\mathrm K$ an apparent increase of $5\,\mathrm{mL}$ is read. If $\beta_{\mathrm{rec}}=4\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$, what is $\beta_{\mathrm{liq}}$?

- $0.00054\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$
- $0.0005\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$
- $0.00046\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$
- $4\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$

## Explanation

The ratio of the apparent change to $V_0\Delta T$ is the difference between the coefficients. Recover the liquid coefficient by adding the container coefficient.

$$
\beta_{\mathrm{liq}}\simeq\frac{5}{500\cdot20}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}+4\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}=5.4\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}.
$$
