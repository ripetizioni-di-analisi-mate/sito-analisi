A metal rod is fixed rigidly at both ends. What does $\alpha L_0\Delta T$ represent when it is warmed?

- The measured expansion between the rigid supports.
- The horizontal force on the walls.
- The expansion it would have if free.
- The heat absorbed.

## Explanation

The supports fix the distance between the ends. The free expansion equation gives the change after releasing the supports, in units of length.

If the ends stay fixed, the actual length remains unchanged. Heating can produce internal compression. Quantifying that force would also require the material’s elastic properties and the cross-sectional geometry of the rod.
