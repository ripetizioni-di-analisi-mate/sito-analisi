A container has $\beta_{\mathrm{rec}}$ greater than $\beta_{\mathrm{liq}}$ and is warmed with its liquid. What change is observed relative to the mark?

- Positive, because the liquid receives heat.
- Zero in all cases.
- Twice the liquid’s change.
- Negative, because the container expands more than the liquid does.

## Explanation

The difference between the coefficients is negative. Multiplying it by $V_0>0$ and $\Delta T>0$ gives an apparent decrease.
