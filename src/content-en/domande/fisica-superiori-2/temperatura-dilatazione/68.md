A glass has $2\,\mathrm{mL}$ of free capacity above the liquid. After warming the liquid would expand by $3.6\,\mathrm{mL}$ and the total glass capacity expands by $0.4\,\mathrm{mL}$. What volume overflows?

- $1.6\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $3.2\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $2.4\,\mathrm{mL}$

## Explanation

The space available when warm is the original headspace plus the increase in container capacity. The liquid expansion would exceed it and the difference overflows.

$$
V_{\mathrm{fuori}}\simeq(3.6-2-0.4)\,\mathrm{mL}=1.2\,\mathrm{mL}.
$$
