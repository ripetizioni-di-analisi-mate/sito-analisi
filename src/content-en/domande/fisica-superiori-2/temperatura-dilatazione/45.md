The measured curve of $\Delta L$ against $\Delta T$ shows clear curvature. What does this suggest for a model with constant $\alpha$?

- A single coefficient may work only over narrower ranges.
- A single coefficient reproduces the entire curve exactly.
- The slope is necessarily zero.
- The initial length must be expressed in kelvins.

## Explanation

A varying slope means $\alpha L_0$ cannot account for the whole curve as a constant. A small region can be approximated by a straight line.
