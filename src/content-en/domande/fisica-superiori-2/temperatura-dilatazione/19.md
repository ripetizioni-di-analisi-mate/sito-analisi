Two samples in thermal equilibrium have very different internal energies. Which statement is compatible with those readings?

- They must have different temperatures.
- The net heat transfer between them is necessarily positive.
- The samples may have different masses and compositions.
- Internal energy is a reading in kelvins.

## Explanation

Equilibrium means equal temperatures. Internal energy is a different quantity and also depends on mass and composition.
