What is the slope in a graph of the kelvin value against the Celsius value?

- $1$
- $273.15$
- $0$
- $-1$

## Explanation

The equation is $T=t_C+273.15$. The coefficient of $t_C$ is one, while the constant is the intercept.

To check, choose two horizontal coordinates, such as $0$ and $20\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. Their vertical coordinates are $273.15$ and $293.15\,\mathrm K$. The ratio of the changes is $20\div20=1$. The offset between the origins affects the vertical position of the line.
