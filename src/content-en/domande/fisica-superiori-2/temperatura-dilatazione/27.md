Which of these readings satisfies the lower bound of absolute zero?

- $-320\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $-3\,\mathrm K$
- $-270\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $-400\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$

## Explanation

Absolute zero corresponds to $-273.15\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. Only $-270\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ lies above it.

Converting the admissible reading gives $(-270+273.15)\,\mathrm K=3.15\,\mathrm K$. The result is positive, so it lies above zero on the absolute scale. Celsius readings below the threshold would give values in kelvin below zero.
