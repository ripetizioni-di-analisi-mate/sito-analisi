An isotropic solid has a measured volume coefficient of $9\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$. What area expansion coefficient do you estimate?

- $3\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$.
- $18\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$.
- $9\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$.
- $6\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$.

## Explanation

First obtain $\alpha\simeq\beta/3$. The area coefficient is about twice $\alpha$, so it is two thirds of the volume coefficient.
