An isotropic sample of $250\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ and $\beta=6\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$ shrinks by $0.30\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. What is $\Delta T$?

- $20\,\mathrm K$
- $-60\,\mathrm K$
- $-2\,\mathrm K$
- $-20\,\mathrm K$

## Explanation

The numerator is the signed change in volume. Dividing by the positive product of initial volume and coefficient gives a cooling interval.

$$
\Delta T\simeq\frac{-0.30}{250\cdot6\cdot10^{-5}}\,\mathrm K=-20\,\mathrm K.
$$

The product $\beta V_0$ is $0.015\,\mathrm{cm^3/K}$. With that slope, losing $0.30\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ requires a fall of $20\,\mathrm K$. A positive temperature change would instead increase the volume.
