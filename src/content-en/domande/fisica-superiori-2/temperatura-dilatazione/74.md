For liquid water heated from $5$ to $8\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ at atmospheric pressure, how does the sign of its volume change compare with heating from $1$ to $3\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$?

- It is positive instead of negative.
- It is always negative.
- It is always zero.
- It is negative instead of positive.

## Explanation

Above about $4\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ the volume increases as temperature rises. Below that value liquid water does the opposite.
