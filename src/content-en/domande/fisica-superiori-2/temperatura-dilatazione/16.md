A cold probe gives rising readings while immersed in a bath at constant temperature. When is it appropriate to record the bath temperature?

- When it reads exactly zero.
- When it changes most rapidly.
- At the instant of immersion.
- When the reading stabilises within its resolution.

## Explanation

While the reading is changing, the sensor is approaching equilibrium. The steady value estimates the bath temperature.

Comparing successive readings helps identify that point. If they still differ by more than one scale division, the reported value depends on the moment at which it was read.
