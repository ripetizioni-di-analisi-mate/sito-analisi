A $5\,\mathrm m$ rod is fixed at one end and can expand towards the other, where a gap of $3\,\mathrm{mm}$ remains. With $\alpha=1.0\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$, at what temperature rise does it close?

- $15\,\mathrm K$
- $300\,\mathrm K$
- $60\,\mathrm K$
- $6\,\mathrm K$

## Explanation

At the threshold, the free expansion equals the width of the gap.

$$
\Delta T\simeq\frac{3\cdot10^{-3}}{(1.0\cdot10^{-5})\cdot5}\,\mathrm K=60\,\mathrm K.
$$

The product $\alpha L_0$ gives the slope of the absolute change, $0.050\,\mathrm{mm/K}$. Filling the available $3\,\mathrm{mm}$ therefore requires $60\,\mathrm K$. Beyond that threshold the wall prevents further free motion, so a different model is needed.
