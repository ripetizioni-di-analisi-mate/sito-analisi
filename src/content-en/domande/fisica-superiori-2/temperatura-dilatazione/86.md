A technician extends a single line of positive volume expansion for water from $8\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ down to $1\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. Which observation contradicts that extrapolation?

- Its volume remains positive.
- Its volume has a minimum near 4 °C.
- The sample mass stays fixed.
- Celsius readings below 8 are smaller than 8.

## Explanation

A straight line of positive slope cannot have an interior minimum. The sign reversal near four degrees needs a different model.
