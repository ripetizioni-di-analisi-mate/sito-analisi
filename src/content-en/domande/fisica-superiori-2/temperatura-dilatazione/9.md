A sensor is linear between two references. In baths at $0\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ and $80\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ it reads $10$ and $50$ divisions, respectively. What is the absolute temperature of a sample for which it reads $30$ divisions?

- $313.15\,\mathrm K$
- $40\,\mathrm K$
- $233.15\,\mathrm K$
- $353.15\,\mathrm K$

## Explanation

A reading of $30$ divisions is halfway between $10$ and $50$. Because the relation is affine, the sample temperature is halfway between $0$ and $80\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$, or $40\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. Converting this temperature gives $T=(40+273.15)\,\mathrm K=313.15\,\mathrm K$. Use the calibration to interpret the scale divisions first.
