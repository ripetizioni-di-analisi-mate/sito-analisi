To estimate expansion of a liquid whose pressure also changes, which condition of the simple model $\Delta V\simeq\beta V_0\Delta T$ needs checking?

- That temperature is always expressed in Celsius.
- That the container mass doubles.
- That the coefficient has units of volume.
- That the pressure stays essentially constant.

## Explanation

The thermal volume coefficient describes a change at constant pressure. A significant pressure change also requires a compression contribution.
