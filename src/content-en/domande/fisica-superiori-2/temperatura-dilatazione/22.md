From $290\,\mathrm K$ a substance warms by $18\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ and then cools by $7\,\mathrm K$. What absolute temperature does it reach?

- $315\,\mathrm K$
- $301\,\mathrm K$
- $279\,\mathrm K$
- $574.15\,\mathrm K$

## Explanation

Temperature changes are differences on a common scale and have the same numerical value in kelvins and degrees Celsius.

$$
T_f=(290+18-7)\,\mathrm K=301\,\mathrm K.
$$
