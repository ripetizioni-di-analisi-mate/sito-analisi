Starting at $290\,\mathrm K$, a substance warms by $18$ degrees Celsius and then cools by $7\,\mathrm K$. What absolute temperature does it reach?

- $265\,\mathrm K$
- $301\,\mathrm K$
- $27.85\,\mathrm K$
- $574.15\,\mathrm K$

## Explanation

Warming by $18$ degrees Celsius is a temperature interval equal to $18\,\mathrm K$. Cooling enters with a negative sign, so the changes add to $18-7=11\,\mathrm K$. Add the total change to the initial absolute temperature of $290\,\mathrm K$ to get $301\,\mathrm K$. The conversion offset applies only to temperature values.
