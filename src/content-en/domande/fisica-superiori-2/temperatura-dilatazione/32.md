Compare $28\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ and $296.15\,\mathrm K$. Which temperature is higher?

- The second by 5 K.
- They are equal.
- The second by 268.15 K.
- The first by 5 K.

## Explanation

The second temperature is $23\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. The difference $28-23$ is five kelvins.

Alternatively, convert the first reading to $301.15\,\mathrm K$. Comparing it with $296.15\,\mathrm K$ again gives $5\,\mathrm K$. Subtracting the numbers $28$ and $296.15$ directly would mix the two origins.
