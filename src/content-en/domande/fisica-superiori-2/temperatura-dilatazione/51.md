A cube changes edge length by the fraction $0.001$. What is the first nonlinear term in the fractional area change?

- $0.002$
- $1\cdot10^{-6}$
- $0.003$
- $3\cdot10^{-6}$

## Explanation

The total surface area is proportional to the square of the edge. The quadratic term is the first one omitted.

$$
(1+\varepsilon)^2-1=2\varepsilon+\varepsilon^2,\qquad(0.001)^2=10^{-6}.
$$
