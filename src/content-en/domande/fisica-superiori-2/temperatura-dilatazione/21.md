A sample cools by $32\,\mathrm K$ from $14\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. Express its final temperature in degrees Celsius.

- $-18\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $18\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $46\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $255.15\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$

## Explanation

Temperature changes are differences on a common scale and have the same numerical value in kelvins and degrees Celsius.

$$
t_{C,f}=(14-32)\,{}^\circ\mathrm C=-18\,{}^\circ\mathrm C.
$$
