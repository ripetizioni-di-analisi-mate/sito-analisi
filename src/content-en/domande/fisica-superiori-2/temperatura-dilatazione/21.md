A sample cools by $32\,\mathrm K$ from $14\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. Express its final temperature in degrees Celsius.

- $-18\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $18\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $46\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $255.15\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$

## Explanation

Cooling corresponds to $\Delta T=-32\,\mathrm K$. A change of $32$ kelvins has the same magnitude as a change of $32$ degrees Celsius. Work on the scale of the initial value and subtract $32$ from $14$. The sample reaches $-18\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$, passing through zero Celsius along the way.
