A liquid with a volume expansion coefficient of $7.9\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$ fills a freely expanding container to its $180\,\mathrm{mL}$ mark. The container has a volume expansion coefficient of $1.2\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$. Both warm by $30\,\mathrm K$ at constant pressure. Estimate the apparent expansion.

- $4.266\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $4.914\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $0.648\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $3.618\,\mathrm{mL}$

## Explanation

Initially, the liquid and the capacity up to the mark have the same volume. The liquid expands by about $180\cdot30\cdot7.9\cdot10^{-4}=4.266\,\mathrm{mL}$. The increase in capacity up to the mark absorbs $0.648\,\mathrm{mL}$ of this. The volume above the reference is therefore $4.266-0.648=3.618\,\mathrm{mL}$.
