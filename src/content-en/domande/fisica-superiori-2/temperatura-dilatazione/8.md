A liquid of volume $180\,\mathrm{mL}$ has volume expansion coefficient $0.0007\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$. The container has volume expansion coefficient $3\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$. What is the apparent expansion for an increase of $30\,\mathrm K$? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $3.78\,\mathrm{mL}$.
- $3.942\,\mathrm{mL}$.
- $0.162\,\mathrm{mL}$.
- $3.618\,\mathrm{mL}$.

## Explanation

The volume of the liquid increases, and so does the volume under the original mark. The reading gives the difference between those changes, which introduces $\beta_{\mathrm{liq}}-\beta_{\mathrm{rec}}$. The result is positive because the liquid has the larger coefficient.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta V_{\mathrm{app}} &= \Delta T V \left(b - \beta_r\right) \\[6pt]
&= \left(30\right) \left(180\right) \left(\left(0.0007\right) - \left(3\cdot10^{-5}\right)\right) \\[6pt]
&\simeq 3.618\,\mathrm{mL}
\end{aligned}
$$
