Two rods A and B are free to expand. Rod A is twice as long as B and has one third of B’s linear expansion coefficient. If both warm uniformly by the same amount, what is the first-order value of $\dfrac{\Delta L_A}{\Delta L_B}$?

- $\dfrac32$
- $\dfrac23$
- $6$
- $\dfrac16$

## Explanation

For each rod the increase is proportional to $\alpha L_0\Delta T$. Divide A’s extension by B’s. The common temperature interval cancels. The length ratio is $2$ and the coefficient ratio is $\dfrac13$, so $\dfrac{\Delta L_A}{\Delta L_B}=\dfrac23$, about $0.667$.
