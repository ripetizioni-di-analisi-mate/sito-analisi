Rod A is twice as long as B and has one third of B’s coefficient. If both warm by the same amount, what is the approximate value of $\Delta L_A/\Delta L_B$?

- $1.5$
- $0.66666667$
- $6$
- $0.16666667$

## Explanation

The common temperature interval cancels in the ratio. The ratios of coefficients and lengths remain.

$$
\frac{\Delta L_A}{\Delta L_B}\simeq\frac{\alpha_A}{\alpha_B}\cdot\frac{L_{0,A}}{L_{0,B}}=\frac13\cdot2\simeq0.667.
$$
