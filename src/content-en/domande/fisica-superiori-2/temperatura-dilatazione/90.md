A probe records the difference between temperatures in two rooms. Absolute readings are shifted from Celsius to kelvins. What happens to the slope of the difference-versus-time graph?

- It increases by $273.15$ kelvins per second.
- It becomes zero at every instant.
- It changes sign.
- It stays unchanged.

## Explanation

At each instant the constant added to the two readings cancels. The difference graph therefore retains both its values and slopes.

If the two chambers differ by $5$ degrees Celsius at one instant, they differ by $5\,\mathrm K$ at that same instant. This correspondence is pointwise, so the change in the difference over any time interval is the same.
