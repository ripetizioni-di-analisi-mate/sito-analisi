An initial reading of $280\,\mathrm K$ and a final reading of $12\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ are subtracted as $12-280$. What is the main error?

- Final and initial readings are always subtracted in the wrong order.
- Values referred to different origins are being subtracted.
- Changes can be expressed only in joules.
- A kelvin is twice the size of a degree Celsius.

## Explanation

To subtract the initial reading from the final one, put them on the same scale. The final reading is $285.15\,\mathrm K$ and the change is $5.15\,\mathrm K$.
