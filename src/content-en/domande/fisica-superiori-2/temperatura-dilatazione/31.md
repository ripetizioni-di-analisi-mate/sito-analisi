A sensor reads $20\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ when a reference in contact reads $298.15\,\mathrm K$. Find the sensor error as its reading minus the true value.

- $5\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $-25\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $-5\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $278.15\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$

## Explanation

First put the reference on the Celsius scale, then subtract the true value.

$$
e=[20-(298.15-273.15)]\,{}^\circ\mathrm C=-5\,{}^\circ\mathrm C.
$$
