A free rod $5\,\mathrm m$ long has a linear expansion coefficient of $\alpha=1.2\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$ and warms uniformly by $60\,\mathrm K$. Estimate its extension in millimetres.

- $0.72\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $36\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.0036\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $3.6\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

In the linear model the temperature rise makes the extension positive. The product $\alpha\Delta T$ is $7.2\cdot10^{-4}$ and is a fraction of the initial length. Multiplying it by $5\,\mathrm m$ gives $\Delta L\simeq0.0036\,\mathrm m$. Multiply the number by $1000$ to express it as $3.6\,\mathrm{mm}$.
