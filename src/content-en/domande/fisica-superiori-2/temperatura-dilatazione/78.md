An unconstrained rod is made of a material with a negative linear expansion coefficient. It is warmed over a range in which that coefficient is constant. What is the sign of its length change?

- Positive.
- Zero for any coefficient.
- Negative.
- Determined by the choice of temperature scale.

## Explanation

The product contains a positive initial length and a positive temperature change, while $\alpha$ is negative. The rod therefore shortens.
