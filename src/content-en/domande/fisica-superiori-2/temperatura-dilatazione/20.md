The first measurement of a test tube is $300.15\,\mathrm K$, the second $32\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. Find the second temperature minus the first.

- $-5\,\mathrm K$
- $268.15\,\mathrm K$
- $332.15\,\mathrm K$
- $5\,\mathrm K$

## Explanation

To subtract the temperatures, express them on the same scale. The second reading, $32\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$, equals $(32+273.15)\,\mathrm K=305.15\,\mathrm K$. Subtracting gives $\Delta T=(305.15-300.15)\,\mathrm K=5\,\mathrm K$. The positive sign shows that the second temperature is higher. Converting the first reading to degrees Celsius would give the same difference.
