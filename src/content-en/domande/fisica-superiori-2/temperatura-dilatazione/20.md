The first measurement of a test tube is $300.15\,\mathrm K$, the second $32\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. Find the second temperature minus the first.

- $-5\,\mathrm K$
- $268.15\,\mathrm K$
- $332.15\,\mathrm K$
- $5\,\mathrm K$

## Explanation

Temperature changes are differences on a common scale and have the same numerical value in kelvins and degrees Celsius.

$$
\Delta T=(32+273.15-300.15)\,\mathrm K=5\,\mathrm K.
$$
