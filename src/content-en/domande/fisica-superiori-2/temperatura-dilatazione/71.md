The apparent coefficient of a liquid is $5\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$ and its initial volume is $200\,\mathrm{mL}$. A decrease of $2\,\mathrm{mL}$ is observed. Find $\Delta T$.

- $20\,\mathrm K$
- $-10\,\mathrm K$
- $-200\,\mathrm K$
- $-20\,\mathrm K$

## Explanation

The apparent decrease is negative. The positive apparent coefficient implies that the temperature change is negative as well.

$$
\Delta T\simeq\frac{-2}{200\cdot5\cdot10^{-4}}\,\mathrm K=-20\,\mathrm K.
$$
