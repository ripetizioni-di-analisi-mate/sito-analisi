A container is made of an isotropic solid with $\alpha=2\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$. A $100\,\mathrm{mL}$ liquid has $\beta_{\mathrm{liq}}=6\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$. Find the apparent expansion over $50\,\mathrm K$.

- $2.7\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $3\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $2.9\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $3.3\,\mathrm{mL}$

## Explanation

The supplied material coefficient is linear. For the container capacity we need the volume coefficient $3\alpha$.

$$
\Delta V_{\mathrm{app}}\simeq(6\cdot10^{-4}-3\cdot2\cdot10^{-5})\cdot100\cdot50\,\mathrm{mL}=2.7\,\mathrm{mL}.
$$
