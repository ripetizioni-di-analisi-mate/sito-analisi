For the same warming an isotropic solid expands each dimension by $0.25\%$. What is the difference between its first-order percentage increases in volume and area?

- $0.25\%$
- $0.5\%$
- $0.75\%$
- $1.25\%$

## Explanation

There are three copies of the linear term for volume and two for area. Their difference leaves one copy of the fractional length change.

$$
(3-2)\cdot0.25\%=0.25\%.
$$
