For the same warming an isotropic solid expands each dimension by $0.25\%$. By how many percentage points does the first-order volume increase exceed the area increase?

- $0.25$
- $0.5$
- $0.75$
- $1.25$

## Explanation

There are three copies of the linear term for volume and two for area. Their difference leaves one copy of the fractional length change.

$$
(3-2)\cdot0.25\%=0.25\%.
$$

To first order, volume increases by $0.75\%$ and area by $0.50\%$. Subtracting the percentages gives $0.25$ percentage points. These are relative changes measured against the initial volume and area, respectively.
