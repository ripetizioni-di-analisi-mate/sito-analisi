Near which temperature is the volume of a fixed mass of liquid water smallest at atmospheric pressure?

- $0\,\mathrm K$.
- $100\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$.
- $-4\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$.
- $4\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$.

## Explanation

The volume decreases from around zero to four degrees Celsius and then increases. At fixed mass the minimum volume coincides with maximum density.

With a constant mass divided by volume, a smaller denominator gives a higher density. The density maximum therefore occurs where contraction gives way to expansion.
