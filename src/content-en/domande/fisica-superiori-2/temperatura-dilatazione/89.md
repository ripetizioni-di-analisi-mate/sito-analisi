Only the apparent expansion of a liquid and the product $V_0\Delta T$ are known. What additional quantity is needed to separate the liquid and container coefficients?

- The name of the temperature scale.
- An independent measurement of the container coefficient.
- The pressure in units of length.
- A Celsius degree sign written before the kelvin.

## Explanation

The reading determines the difference between the coefficients. To recover both, one of them must be known or determined independently.

Two different pairs of coefficients would give the same reading if their differences matched. An independent measurement of the container fixes one term. Adding it to the apparent coefficient gives the coefficient of the liquid.
