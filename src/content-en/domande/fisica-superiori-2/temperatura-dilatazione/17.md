A probe that starts colder is inserted into a small droplet. What property of the instrument reduces the error due to contact?

- A heat capacity small compared with the droplet’s.
- A mass always greater than the droplet’s.
- A scale printed only in kelvins.
- A large volume of metal.

## Explanation

A probe that needs little energy to warm affects the droplet temperature only slightly. The reading scale is independent of that transfer.

For the same initial temperature difference, a lower heat capacity requires less energy per degree of warming. The drop therefore transfers less energy to the probe.
