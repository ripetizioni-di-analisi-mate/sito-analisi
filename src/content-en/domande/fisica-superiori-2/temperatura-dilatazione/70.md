A liquid has slope $0.12\,\mathrm{mL/K}$ in a graph of actual volume. For the container the slope is $0.02\,\mathrm{mL/K}$. What is the apparent slope?

- $0.14\,\mathrm{mL/K}$
- $0.12\,\mathrm{mL/K}$
- $0.1\,\mathrm{mL/K}$
- $0.02\,\mathrm{mL/K}$

## Explanation

The apparent change is a difference of two straight lines. Its slope is the difference of their slopes.

$$
s_{\mathrm{app}}=(0.12-0.02)\,\mathrm{mL/K}=0.10\,\mathrm{mL/K}.
$$
