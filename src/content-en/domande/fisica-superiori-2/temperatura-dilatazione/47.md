Two rods are fixed at the same end, with their free ends initially aligned. After the same heating, A extends $0.4\,\mathrm{mm}$ beyond B. A has lengthened by $0.7\,\mathrm{mm}$. How much has B lengthened?

- $1.1\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.4\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.3\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.7\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

Since the free ends originally coincided, their final separation is the difference between the extensions. Hence $\Delta L_B=0.7-0.4=0.3\,\mathrm{mm}$.
