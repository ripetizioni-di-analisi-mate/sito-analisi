An unconstrained isotropic plate of area $4\,\mathrm{m^2}$ has $\alpha=10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$. It cools by $30\,\mathrm K$. Estimate the final area to first order in the expansion.

- $4.0024\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $3.9988\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $3.9976\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $0.0024\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

Cooling makes the area change negative. To find the final area, add the signed change.

$$
A\simeq4[1+2\cdot10^{-5}\cdot(-30)]\,\mathrm{m^2}=3.9976\,\mathrm{m^2}.
$$
