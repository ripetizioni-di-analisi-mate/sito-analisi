Two rods $1.50\,\mathrm m$ long have one end fixed to the same support and their free ends aligned. Rod A is made of aluminium, with $\alpha_A=2.4\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$. After a uniform heating of $40\,\mathrm K$ the free end of A extends $0.48\,\mathrm{mm}$ beyond that of B. What is the coefficient of linear expansion of B?

- $8.0\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$
- $3.2\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$
- $1.6\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$
- $1.6\cdot10^{-2}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$

## Explanation

The elongation of rod A is $\Delta L_A=\alpha_AL_0\Delta T=2.4\cdot10^{-5}\cdot1.50\cdot40\,\mathrm m=1.44\,\mathrm{mm}$. Since the free ends started aligned, their final separation is the difference of the elongations, so $\Delta L_B=1.44\,\mathrm{mm}-0.48\,\mathrm{mm}=0.96\,\mathrm{mm}$. The coefficient of B is found by dividing its elongation by $L_0\Delta T$:
$$
\alpha_B=\frac{0.96\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm m}{1.50\,\mathrm m\cdot40\,\mathrm K}=1.6\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}.
$$
The value $8.0\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$ is obtained by taking the $0.48\,\mathrm{mm}$ separation as the elongation of B. The value $1.6\cdot10^{-2}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$ comes from leaving the elongation in millimetres.
