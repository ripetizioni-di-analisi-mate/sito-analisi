A free isotropic plate of area $4\,\mathrm{m^2}$ has $\alpha=10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$. It cools uniformly by $30\,\mathrm K$. Estimate the signed change in area in square centimetres.

- $24\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $-24\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $-2.4\,\mathrm{cm^2}$

## Explanation

Cooling gives $\Delta T=-30\,\mathrm K$. Both dimensions of the free plate contract, so $\Delta A\simeq2\alpha A_0\Delta T=-0.0024\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Multiply by $100^2$ to convert to square centimetres, giving $-24\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. The sign belongs to the change, and the plate’s area remains positive.
