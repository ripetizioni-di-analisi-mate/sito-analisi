An isotropic block of initial volume $80\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ increases in volume by $0.45\%$. What is the final volume?

- $80.45\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $80.36\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $79.64\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $0.36\,\mathrm{cm^3}$

## Explanation

The stated percentage already refers to volume. Multiply the initial volume by one plus the fractional change.

$$
V_f=80(1+0.0045)\,\mathrm{cm^3}=80.36\,\mathrm{cm^3}.
$$
