A block with an initial volume of $80\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ increases in volume by $0.45\%$. What is the increase in cubic millimetres?

- $450\,\mathrm{mm^3}$
- $360\,\mathrm{mm^3}$
- $36\,\mathrm{mm^3}$
- $0.36\,\mathrm{mm^3}$

## Explanation

The percentage already refers to volume. Divide by one hundred to get the fraction $0.0045$, then multiply by $80\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. The increase is $0.36\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. Each of the three dimensions of a cubic centimetre contains $10$ millimetres, so the conversion uses $10^3$ and gives $360\,\mathrm{mm^3}$.
