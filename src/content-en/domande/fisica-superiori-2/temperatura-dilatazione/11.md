A sample goes from $10\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}$ to $55\,\mathrm{^{\circ}C}$. What is the temperature increase in kelvins? Choose the value closest to the result.

- $318.15\,\mathrm{K}$.
- $65\,\mathrm{K}$.
- $45\,\mathrm{K}$.
- $-45\,\mathrm{K}$.

## Explanation

The final value is greater than the initial value, so the temperature interval is positive. The scales differ by a constant that cancels when we subtract. Calculate the difference directly from the Celsius values.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta T &= Tf - Ti \\[6pt]
&= \left(55\right) - \left(10\right) \\[6pt]
&\simeq 45\,\mathrm{K}
\end{aligned}
$$
