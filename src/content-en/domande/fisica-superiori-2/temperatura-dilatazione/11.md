On the linear part of a sensor calibration, a rise of $15\,\mathrm K$ increases the reading by $6$ divisions. By how much does the temperature rise when the reading goes from $18$ to $36$ divisions?

- $18\,\mathrm K$
- $7.2\,\mathrm K$
- $45\,\mathrm K$
- $90\,\mathrm K$

## Explanation

The relevant change is $36-18=18$ divisions, three times the $6$ divisions of the known interval. With a constant slope, the temperature rise is three times the reference rise, giving $45\,\mathrm K$. The constant calibration offset cancels in the difference.
