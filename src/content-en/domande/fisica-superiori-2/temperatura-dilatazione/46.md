The side of a square increases by $0.15\%$. Estimate its percentage area change to first order.

- $0.15\%$
- $0.45\%$
- $0.3\%$
- $0.003\%$

## Explanation

Two dimensions expand. The first-order fractional area change is $2\alpha\Delta T$.

$$
\frac{\Delta A}{A_0}\simeq2\cdot0.0015=0.003=0.3\%.
$$
