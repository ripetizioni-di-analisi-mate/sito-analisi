A free rod $6\,\mathrm m$ long extends by $0.0042\,\mathrm m$ when its temperature rises uniformly by $50\,\mathrm K$. Estimate its linear expansion coefficient.

- $1.4\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$
- $7\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$
- $8.4\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$
- $1.4\cdot10^{-8}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$

## Explanation

The $0.0042\,\mathrm m$ is the extension of the whole rod. Dividing by $6\,\mathrm m$ gives the relative change $7\cdot10^{-4}$. That fraction corresponds to an interval of $50\,\mathrm K$, so divide again by $50\,\mathrm K$. This gives $\alpha\simeq1.4\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$, the relative change per kelvin.
