A cylinder has base radius $5$ cm and height $4$ cm. What is its volume?

- $40\pi\ \text{cm}^3$
- $25\pi\ \text{cm}^3$
- $100\pi\ \text{cm}^3$
- $20\pi\ \text{cm}^3$

## Explanation

The volume is the base area times the height, so $V = \pi r^2 h = \pi \cdot 25 \cdot 4 = 100\pi\ \text{cm}^3$. The value $25\pi$ is the base area, and $40\pi$ is the circumference of the base, $10\pi$, multiplied by the height. The volume is measured in cubic centimeters because $r^2 h$ is a product of three lengths.
