Two angles are supplementary and one is three times the other. What is the measure of the smaller angle?

- $60^\circ$
- $90^\circ$
- $45^\circ$
- $135^\circ$

## Explanation

If the smaller angle measures $x$, the other measures $3x$. Their sum is a straight angle, so $x + 3x = 180^\circ$, or $4x = 180^\circ$ and $x = 45^\circ$. The value $135^\circ$ is the larger angle, whereas $60^\circ$ comes from dividing $180^\circ$ by $3$ instead of $4$.
