A box has inside dimensions of $10$ cm, $6$ cm, and $4$ cm. How many cubes with edge length $2$ cm fill it exactly?

- $240$
- $60$
- $15$
- $30$

## Explanation

The numbers of cubes that fit along the three dimensions are $\dfrac{10}{2} = 5$, $\dfrac{6}{2} = 3$, and $\dfrac{4}{2} = 2$, so the box holds $5 \cdot 3 \cdot 2 = 30$ cubes in all. The value $240$ is the box's volume in $\text{cm}^3$ before dividing by the volume of a small cube, which is $8\ \text{cm}^3$.
