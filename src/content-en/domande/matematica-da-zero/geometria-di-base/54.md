Which of the following is a Pythagorean triple?

- $(6, 8, 12)$
- $(9, 12, 16)$
- $(7, 24, 25)$
- $(5, 10, 13)$

## Explanation

A triple $(a, b, c)$ is Pythagorean if $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$. For $(7, 24, 25)$, we have $49 + 576 = 625 = 25^2$. In the other triples, the sum of the squares of the two smaller numbers differs from the square of the largest. For example, $6^2 + 8^2 = 100$ does not equal $12^2 = 144$.
