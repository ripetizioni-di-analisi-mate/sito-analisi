A frame shaped like a rhombus has sides of $18$ cm. What is its perimeter?

- $72\ \text{cm}$
- $36\ \text{cm}$
- $324\ \text{cm}$
- $54\ \text{cm}$

## Explanation

A rhombus has four equal sides, so its perimeter is $4 \cdot 18 = 72$ cm. The value $36$ cm comes from counting just two sides, and $54$ cm from counting three. The value $324$ is $18^2$, the area of a square with the same side length, which is not a perimeter.
