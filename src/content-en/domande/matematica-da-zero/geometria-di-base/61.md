What is the area of a right triangle with a hypotenuse of $10$ cm and a leg of $6$ cm?

- $24\ \text{cm}^2$
- $30\ \text{cm}^2$
- $48\ \text{cm}^2$
- $40\ \text{cm}^2$

## Explanation

The other leg measures $\sqrt{10^2 - 6^2} = \sqrt{64} = 8$ cm. The two legs are perpendicular, so one is the altitude to the other, and the area is $\dfrac{6 \cdot 8}{2} = 24\ \text{cm}^2$. The value $30$ comes from using the hypotenuse in place of a leg, and $48$ comes from omitting the factor of $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
