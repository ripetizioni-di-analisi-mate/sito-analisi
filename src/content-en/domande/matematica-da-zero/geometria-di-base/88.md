A cylindrical glass has a diameter of $6$ cm and a height of $10$ cm. A bottle holds $1$ liter. How many glasses can it fill completely? Use $\pi \approx 3.14$.

- $3$
- $4$
- $35$
- $0$

## Explanation

The radius is $3$ cm and the volume of the glass is $3.14 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 10 = 282.6\ \text{cm}^3$. One liter is $1000\ \text{cm}^3$ and $\dfrac{1000}{282.6} \approx 3.5$, so the number of full glasses is $3$. The value $4$ comes from rounding up, and $35$ comes from omitting the multiplication by the height.
