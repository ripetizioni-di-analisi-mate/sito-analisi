A rectangular lawn is $25$ m long and $12$ m wide. Each bag of seed covers $60\ \text{m}^2$. How many bags are needed?

- $2$
- $12$
- $5$
- $25$

## Explanation

The area of the lawn is $25 \cdot 12 = 300\ \text{m}^2$, so $\dfrac{300}{60} = 5$ bags are needed. The perimeter, $2 \cdot (25 + 12) = 74$ m, does not enter the calculation because the seeds cover a surface. Using the perimeter would give a value close to $\dfrac{74}{60}$, or $2$ bags.
