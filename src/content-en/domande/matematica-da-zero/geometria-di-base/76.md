A rectangular mat has an area of $4500\ \text{cm}^2$. After washing, each of its two sides becomes $10\%$ shorter. What is the new area in square meters?

- $0.405\ \text{m}^2$
- $0.36\ \text{m}^2$
- $0.3645\ \text{m}^2$
- $3.645\ \text{m}^2$

## Explanation

If the original sides measure $a$ and $b$, after washing they measure $0.9\,a$ and $0.9\,b$, so the new area is $0.9 \cdot 0.9 \cdot ab = 0.81\,ab$. The area therefore becomes $0.81 \cdot 4500 = 3645\ \text{cm}^2$. Since $1\ \text{m}^2 = 10\,000\ \text{cm}^2$, dividing by $10\,000$ gives $0.3645\ \text{m}^2$. The value $0.405\ \text{m}^2$ comes from shortening only one side by $10\%$, while $0.36\ \text{m}^2$ comes from removing $20\%$ of the area.
