A small mat has area $4500\ \text{cm}^2$. How many square meters is that?

- $4.5\ \text{m}^2$
- $45\ \text{m}^2$
- $0.45\ \text{m}^2$
- $0.045\ \text{m}^2$

## Explanation

From square centimeters to square meters there are two steps in the sequence of area units, and each step involves dividing by $100$. Therefore, divide by $100 \cdot 100 = 10\,000$ to obtain $\dfrac{4500}{10\,000} = 0.45\ \text{m}^2$. The value $45$ comes from dividing only by $100$, the factor for lengths in centimeters and meters.
