A rectangular mat of area $4500\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ shrinks in the wash. Each of its two dimensions retains $90\%$ of its original length. What is the new area in square metres?

- $0.405$
- $0.36$
- $0.3645$
- $3.645$

## Explanation

Area is a product of two lengths, each multiplied by $0.9$. The washed mat has area $4500\cdot0.9^2=3645\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. A square metre contains $10000$ square centimetres, so the area is $0.3645\,\mathrm{m^2}$.
