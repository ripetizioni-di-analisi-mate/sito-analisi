The angle opposite the base in an isosceles triangle measures $50^\circ$. What is the measure of each base angle?

- $130^\circ$
- $50^\circ$
- $80^\circ$
- $65^\circ$

## Explanation

The base angles are equal, and together with the angle opposite the base they add up to $180^\circ$. The two base angles therefore add up to $180^\circ - 50^\circ = 130^\circ$, and each measures $\dfrac{130^\circ}{2} = 65^\circ$. The value $130^\circ$ is the sum of the base angles rather than the measure of either one.
