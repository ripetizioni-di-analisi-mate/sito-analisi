An aquarium shaped like a rectangular prism is $80$ cm long, $40$ cm wide, and $50$ cm high. How many liters of water does it hold when full?

- $160$ liters
- $160\,000$ liters
- $16$ liters
- $1600$ liters

## Explanation

The volume is $80 \cdot 40 \cdot 50 = 160\,000\ \text{cm}^3$. A liter is $1\ \text{dm}^3 = 1000\ \text{cm}^3$, so the capacity is $\dfrac{160\,000}{1000} = 160$ liters. The value $160\,000$ is the volume in cubic centimeters rather than liters. A cubic decimeter equals a liter, so divide by $1000$ when converting cubic centimeters to liters.
