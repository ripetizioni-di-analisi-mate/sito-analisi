A rectangular room is $6$ m long and $4$ m wide. Baseboard runs along its entire perimeter except for a doorway $2$ m wide. How many meters of baseboard are needed?

- $24\ \text{m}$
- $18\ \text{m}$
- $20\ \text{m}$
- $22\ \text{m}$

## Explanation

The baseboard follows the perimeter of the room, which is $2 \cdot (6 + 4) = 20$ m. The doorway, which is $2$ m wide, needs no baseboard, so the room requires $20 - 2 = 18$ m of baseboard. The value $20$ is the entire perimeter before subtracting the doorway, and $22$ comes from adding the doorway width instead of subtracting it.
