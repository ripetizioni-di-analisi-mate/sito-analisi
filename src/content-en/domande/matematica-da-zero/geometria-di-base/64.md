A disk has diameter $10$ cm. What is its area?

- $100\pi\ \text{cm}^2$
- $10\pi\ \text{cm}^2$
- $5\pi\ \text{cm}^2$
- $25\pi\ \text{cm}^2$

## Explanation

The radius is half the diameter, so $r = 5$ cm and $A = \pi r^2 = 25\pi\ \text{cm}^2$. The value $100\pi$ comes from squaring the diameter instead of the radius, and $10\pi$ is the circumference because $\pi d = 10\pi$. Area is measured in square centimeters, whereas circumference is measured in centimeters.
