From the right-angle vertex of a drawing set square, whose perpendicular sides measure $15$ cm and $20$ cm, a segment is drawn perpendicular to the longest side. How long is this segment, that is, the altitude to the hypotenuse?

- $12\ \text{cm}$
- $20\ \text{cm}$
- $6\ \text{cm}$
- $25\ \text{cm}$

## Explanation

The set square is a right triangle with legs of $15$ cm and $20$ cm, so by the **Pythagorean theorem** the hypotenuse measures $\sqrt{15^2 + 20^2} = \sqrt{225 + 400} = \sqrt{625} = 25$ cm. The two legs are perpendicular, so one is the altitude to the other, and the area of the triangle is $\dfrac{15 \cdot 20}{2} = 150\ \text{cm}^2$. The same area is obtained by taking the hypotenuse as the base and the altitude $h$ to it as the height, so $\dfrac{25 \cdot h}{2} = 150$ and $h = \dfrac{300}{25} = 12$ cm. The value $6$ cm comes from dividing the area by the hypotenuse without first multiplying it by $2$, while $20$ cm is the longer leg and $25$ cm is the hypotenuse.
