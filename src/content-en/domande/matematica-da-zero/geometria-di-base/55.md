Three rods of lengths $5$, $6$ and $8$ cm form a triangle. By comparing the squares of the sides, how would you classify its largest angle?

- Obtuse.
- Right.
- Acute.
- Straight.

## Explanation

The largest angle is opposite the side measuring $8$ cm. For legs of lengths $5$ and $6$, the squared hypotenuse would be $25+36=61$. Here the opposite side has square $64>61$, so the angle between the $5$ and $6$ cm rods is wider than a right angle.
