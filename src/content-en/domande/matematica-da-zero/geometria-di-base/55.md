A right triangle has legs of $1.5$ cm and $2$ cm. What is the length of its hypotenuse?

- $2.5\ \text{cm}$
- $3.5\ \text{cm}$
- $6.25\ \text{cm}$
- $0.5\ \text{cm}$

## Explanation

We have $c^2 = 1.5^2 + 2^2 = 2.25 + 4 = 6.25$, so $c = \sqrt{6.25} = 2.5$ cm. The lengths are half the numbers in the triple $(3, 4, 5)$, and indeed $c = \dfrac{5}{2} = 2.5$. The value $6.25$ is $c^2$ and $3.5$ is the sum of the legs. Take the positive root because the hypotenuse is a length.
