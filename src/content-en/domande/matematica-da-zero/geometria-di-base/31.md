In a rectangle, the diagonal makes an angle of $35^\circ$ with one side. What angle does it make with the other side at the same vertex?

- $55^\circ$
- $145^\circ$
- $35^\circ$
- $65^\circ$

## Explanation

The sides at a vertex of a rectangle form a right angle, and the diagonal divides it into two angles. These angles therefore add up to $90^\circ$, and the required angle measures $90^\circ - 35^\circ = 55^\circ$. The value $145^\circ$ comes from subtracting from $180^\circ$ instead of $90^\circ$.
