A trapezoid has a longer base of $15$ cm, a shorter base of $9$ cm, and a height of $6$ cm. What is its area?

- $144\ \text{cm}^2$
- $72\ \text{cm}^2$
- $90\ \text{cm}^2$
- $54\ \text{cm}^2$

## Explanation

The area of the trapezoid is $A = \dfrac{(B + b) \cdot h}{2} = \dfrac{(15 + 9) \cdot 6}{2} = \dfrac{144}{2} = 72\ \text{cm}^2$. The value $144\ \text{cm}^2$ is the area of the parallelogram formed by joining two copies of the trapezoid, so it is twice as large. The values $90$ and $54$ are the products of just one base and the height. The factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$ appears because two congruent trapezoids form a parallelogram with base $B + b$.
