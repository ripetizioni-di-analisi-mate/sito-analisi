What is the perimeter of a right triangle with legs of $20$ cm and $21$ cm?

- $41\ \text{cm}$
- $29\ \text{cm}$
- $210\ \text{cm}$
- $70\ \text{cm}$

## Explanation

The hypotenuse is $c = \sqrt{20^2 + 21^2} = \sqrt{400 + 441} = \sqrt{841} = 29$ cm. The perimeter is the sum of the three side lengths, or $20 + 21 + 29 = 70$ cm. The value $41$ cm omits the hypotenuse, while $29$ cm is the hypotenuse alone. All three sides enter the perimeter, so calculate the hypotenuse before adding.
