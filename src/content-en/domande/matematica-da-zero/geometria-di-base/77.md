A field measures $2.5$ hectares. How many square meters is that?

- $2500\ \text{m}^2$
- $250\,000\ \text{m}^2$
- $25\,000\ \text{m}^2$
- $250\ \text{m}^2$

## Explanation

A hectare is the area of a square with side length $100$ m, or $100 \cdot 100 = 10\,000\ \text{m}^2$. Therefore, $2.5\ \text{ha} = 2.5 \cdot 10\,000 = 25\,000\ \text{m}^2$. The value $2500$ comes from using a factor of $1000$, and $250$ from using a factor of $100$. A hectare is therefore $10\,000$ times as large as a square meter.
