A ladder leans against a vertical wall and makes an angle of $68^\circ$ with the level floor. What is the angle between the ladder and the wall?

- $112^\circ$
- $68^\circ$
- $22^\circ$
- $158^\circ$

## Explanation

The wall and floor are perpendicular, so the ladder forms a right triangle with them. Its two acute angles are complementary, so the angle with the wall measures $90^\circ - 68^\circ = 22^\circ$. The value $112^\circ$ comes from subtracting from $180^\circ$ instead of $90^\circ$.
