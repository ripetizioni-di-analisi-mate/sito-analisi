A parallelogram has adjacent sides of $9$ cm and $5$ cm. What is its perimeter?

- $14\ \text{cm}$
- $45\ \text{cm}$
- $23\ \text{cm}$
- $28\ \text{cm}$

## Explanation

Opposite sides of a parallelogram are equal, so there are two sides of $9$ cm and two of $5$ cm. The perimeter is $2 \cdot (9 + 5) = 28$ cm. The value $14$ cm is the sum of two adjacent sides, or the semiperimeter, and $23$ cm results from counting the $5$ cm side only once.
