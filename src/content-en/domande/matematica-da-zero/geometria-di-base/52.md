A ladder $5$ m long leans against a vertical wall, with its foot $1.4$ m from the wall. At what height above the ground does the ladder touch the wall?

- $3.6\ \text{m}$
- $4.8\ \text{m}$
- $6.4\ \text{m}$
- $23.04\ \text{m}$

## Explanation

The ladder, wall, and ground form a right triangle with hypotenuse $5$ m and one leg $1.4$ m. The height is $h = \sqrt{5^2 - 1.4^2} = \sqrt{25 - 1.96} = \sqrt{23.04} = 4.8$ m. The value $3.6$ m is the difference $5 - 1.4$ and $23.04$ is $h^2$.
