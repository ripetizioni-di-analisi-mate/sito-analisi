An angle measures $135^\circ$. Which of the following statements is correct?

- It is an acute angle
- It is an obtuse angle
- It is a right angle
- It is a straight angle

## Explanation

An angle is acute if it measures less than $90^\circ$ and obtuse if it measures more than $90^\circ$ and less than $180^\circ$. Since $90^\circ < 135^\circ < 180^\circ$, the angle is obtuse. A right angle measures exactly $90^\circ$ and a straight angle measures $180^\circ$, so neither equals $135^\circ$.
