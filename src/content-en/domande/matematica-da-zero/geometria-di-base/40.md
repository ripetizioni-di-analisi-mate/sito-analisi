A triangle has area $30\ \text{cm}^2$ and base $12$ cm. How long is the altitude to the base?

- $2.5\ \text{cm}$
- $360\ \text{cm}$
- $5\ \text{cm}$
- $18\ \text{cm}$

## Explanation

The formula $A = \dfrac{b \cdot h}{2}$ gives $h = \dfrac{2A}{b} = \dfrac{2 \cdot 30}{12} = 5$ cm. The value $2.5$ cm comes from dividing the area by the base without the factor of $2$, and $360$ is the product of the two given quantities. Indeed, a height of $h = 5$ cm gives an area of $\dfrac{12 \cdot 5}{2} = 30\ \text{cm}^2$, as stated in the problem.
