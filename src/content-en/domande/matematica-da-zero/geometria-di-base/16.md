An equilateral triangle has perimeter $2.4$ m. How long is each side?

- $0.8\ \text{m}$
- $7.2\ \text{m}$
- $1.2\ \text{m}$
- $0.6\ \text{m}$

## Explanation

An equilateral triangle has three equal sides, and the perimeter is their sum, so $3l = 2.4$ m. Thus, $l = \dfrac{2.4}{3} = 0.8$ m. The value $1.2$ m comes from dividing by $2$, while $7.2$ m comes from multiplying by $3$ instead of dividing.
