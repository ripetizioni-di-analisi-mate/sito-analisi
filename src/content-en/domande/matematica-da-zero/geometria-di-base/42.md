A rhombus has diagonals of $10$ cm and $24$ cm. What is its area?

- $240\ \text{cm}^2$
- $34\ \text{cm}^2$
- $120\ \text{cm}^2$
- $60\ \text{cm}^2$

## Explanation

The area of a rhombus is half the product of its diagonals, so $A = \dfrac{10 \cdot 24}{2} = 120\ \text{cm}^2$. The value $240\ \text{cm}^2$ is the area of the rectangle whose side lengths are the lengths of the diagonals, and $34$ is the sum of the diagonals. The formula holds because the diagonals divide the enclosing rectangle into four rectangles and the rhombus occupies half of it.
