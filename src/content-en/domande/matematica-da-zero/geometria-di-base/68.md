A semicircular track has diameter $20$ m. What is the length of the arc of the track?

- $20\pi\ \text{m}$
- $100\pi\ \text{m}$
- $5\pi\ \text{m}$
- $10\pi\ \text{m}$

## Explanation

The full circle of diameter $20$ m has circumference $\pi \cdot 20 = 20\pi$ m, and the arc of the track is half that, or $10\pi$ m. The value $20\pi$ m is the full circumference, and $100\pi$ comes from using the area formula, $\pi \cdot 10^2$.
