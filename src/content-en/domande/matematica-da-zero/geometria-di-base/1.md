At exactly $5$ o'clock, the hands of a clock form two angles. How many degrees are in the smaller angle?

- $120^\circ$
- $180^\circ$
- $50^\circ$
- $150^\circ$

## Explanation

One full turn around the clock face measures $360^\circ$ and is divided into $12$ hours, so consecutive numbers are $\dfrac{360^\circ}{12} = 30^\circ$ apart. At $5$ o'clock the minute hand points to $12$ and the hour hand to $5$, a distance of $5$ intervals. The angle is $5 \cdot 30^\circ = 150^\circ$. The value $120^\circ$ comes from counting $4$ intervals.
