A round baking pan has diameter $30$ cm and a rectangular pan measures $30$ cm by $25$ cm. Which has the larger area? Use $\pi \approx 3.14$.

- The round pan, with $2826\ \text{cm}^2$ compared with $750\ \text{cm}^2$
- They have the same area
- The rectangular pan, with $750\ \text{cm}^2$ compared with $706.5\ \text{cm}^2$
- The round pan, with $94.2\ \text{cm}^2$ compared with $750\ \text{cm}^2$

## Explanation

The radius of the round pan is $15$ cm, so its area is $3.14 \cdot 15^2 = 3.14 \cdot 225 = 706.5\ \text{cm}^2$. The area of the rectangular pan is $30 \cdot 25 = 750\ \text{cm}^2$, so it is larger. The value $2826\ \text{cm}^2$ comes from using the diameter in place of the radius.
