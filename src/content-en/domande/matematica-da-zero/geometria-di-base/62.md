The diagonal of a rectangle measures $25$ cm and one of its sides measures $15$ cm. What is its area?

- $375\ \text{cm}^2$
- $150\ \text{cm}^2$
- $20\ \text{cm}^2$
- $300\ \text{cm}^2$

## Explanation

The diagonal is the hypotenuse of a right triangle with one leg of $15$ cm, so the other side measures $\sqrt{25^2 - 15^2} = \sqrt{400} = 20$ cm. The area is $15 \cdot 20 = 300\ \text{cm}^2$. The value $20$ is the missing side length rather than the area, and $375$ comes from multiplying the side by the diagonal.
