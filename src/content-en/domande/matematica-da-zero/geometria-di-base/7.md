In the figure, lines $r$ and $s$ are parallel and line $t$ crosses them. Angle $\alpha$ measures $60^\circ$. What is the measure of angle $\beta$, which forms a pair of alternate interior angles with $\alpha$?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 segmenti="-3.5,2,3.5,2;-3.5,0,3.5,0;-0.575,-1,1.725,3" linee="0.65,2.00 0.65,1.94 0.67,1.87 0.69,1.81 0.72,1.75 0.75,1.70 0.80,1.65 0.84,1.60 0.90,1.57;0.50,0.00 0.50,0.07 0.48,0.13 0.46,0.19 0.43,0.25 0.40,0.30 0.35,0.35 0.30,0.40 0.25,0.43" punti="1.15,2,pieno;0,0,pieno" etichette="-3.3,2.25,r;-3.3,0.25,s;1.85,2.9,t;0.45,1.72,α;0.65,0.2,β" xmin=-3.8 xmax=3.8 ymin=-1.3 ymax=3.4 legenda=0}

- $60^\circ$
- $120^\circ$
- $30^\circ$
- $300^\circ$

## Explanation

Angles $\alpha$ and $\beta$ lie between the parallel lines and on opposite sides of the transversal $t$, so they are alternate interior angles and are equal. Therefore, $\beta = 60^\circ$. The value $120^\circ$ is the supplement of $\alpha$, that is, the angle adjacent to $\beta$, and $30^\circ$ is the complement.
