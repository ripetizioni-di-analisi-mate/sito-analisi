A wheel has diameter $50$ cm. How many centimeters does it travel in $10$ full turns? Use $\pi \approx 3.14$.

- $785\ \text{cm}$
- $157\ \text{cm}$
- $1570\ \text{cm}$
- $7850\ \text{cm}$

## Explanation

In one turn, the wheel travels a distance equal to its circumference, $\pi d \approx 3.14 \cdot 50 = 157$ cm. In $10$ turns it travels $10 \cdot 157 = 1570$ cm. The value $157$ cm corresponds to just one turn, and $785$ cm comes from using the radius in place of the diameter.
