A disk of radius $5$ cm has a circular hole of radius $3$ cm at its center. What is the area of the remaining material?

- $4\pi\ \text{cm}^2$
- $16\pi\ \text{cm}^2$
- $34\pi\ \text{cm}^2$
- $8\pi\ \text{cm}^2$

## Explanation

The area of the remaining material is the area of the disk minus the area of the hole, so it is $\pi \cdot 5^2 - \pi \cdot 3^2 = 25\pi - 9\pi = 16\pi\ \text{cm}^2$. The value $4\pi$ comes from squaring the difference of the radii, and $34\pi$ comes from adding the two areas.
