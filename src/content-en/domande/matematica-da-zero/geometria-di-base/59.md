Starting from home, Luca walks $800$ m north and then $600$ m east. What is his straight-line distance from home?

- $1400\ \text{m}$
- $200\ \text{m}$
- $1000\ \text{m}$
- $700\ \text{m}$

## Explanation

His path and the segment joining his home to his destination form a right triangle with legs of $800$ m and $600$ m. The distance is the hypotenuse, $\sqrt{800^2 + 600^2} = \sqrt{640\,000 + 360\,000} = \sqrt{1\,000\,000} = 1000$ m. The value $1400$ m is the length of the route he walked rather than his straight-line distance from home.
