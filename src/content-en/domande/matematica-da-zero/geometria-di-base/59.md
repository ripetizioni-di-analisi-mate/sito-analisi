A straight road is shown horizontally in the diagram. Two houses are on opposite sides, respectively $300$ and $600$ m from the road. The feet of their perpendiculars are $900$ m apart along the road. How far from the first house's perpendicular foot does the straight line between the houses cross the road?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 linee="-0.8,0 6.8,0;0,2 0,0;6,-4 6,0;0,2 6,-4;0,0.45 6,0.45;0,0.35 0,0.55;6,0.35 6,0.55" punti="0,2,pieno;6,-4,pieno;2,0,pieno" etichette="-0.6,2.15,A;6.15,-4.05,B;2.05,0.3,P;0.2,0.8,300 m;5.2,-2,600 m;3.1,0.75,900 m" xmin=-1 xmax=7 ymin=-4.8 ymax=2.8 legenda=0 titolo="The houses and their perpendicular feet on the road"}

- $450\,\mathrm m$
- $600\,\mathrm m$
- $300\,\mathrm m$
- $750\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The line between the houses gives two right triangles sharing the same acute angle with the road. Their bases are therefore in the ratio of their heights, $300:600=1:2$. The base on the first house's side is one third of the $900$ m, or $300$ m.
