The interior angles of a triangle are in the ratio $2 : 3 : 4$. What is the measure of the largest angle?

- $80^\circ$
- $60^\circ$
- $20^\circ$
- $90^\circ$

## Explanation

The angles measure $2x$, $3x$, and $4x$ for some value $x$, and their sum is $180^\circ$. Therefore, $9x = 180^\circ$ and $x = 20^\circ$. The largest angle measures $4x = 80^\circ$. The value $20^\circ$ is the measure $x$ of one part, while $60^\circ$ is the middle angle $3x$.
