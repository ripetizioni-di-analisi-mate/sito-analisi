A parallelogram has base $11$ cm, a slanted side of $9$ cm, and an altitude to the base of $7$ cm. What is its area?

- $99\ \text{cm}^2$
- $77\ \text{cm}^2$
- $40\ \text{cm}^2$
- $38.5\ \text{cm}^2$

## Explanation

The area of a parallelogram is the product of its base and height, so $A = 11 \cdot 7 = 77\ \text{cm}^2$. The slanted side is used for the perimeter and does not enter the area formula. The value $99\ \text{cm}^2$ comes from using the slanted side in place of the height, and $38.5$ from dividing $77$ by $2$.
