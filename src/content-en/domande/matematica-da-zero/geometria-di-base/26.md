Which quadrilateral has perpendicular diagonals that bisect each other?

- A rhombus
- A rectangle
- A trapezoid
- A general parallelogram

## Explanation

A rhombus is a parallelogram with four equal sides, and its diagonals are perpendicular and bisect each other. In a rectangle with sides of $4$ cm and $3$ cm, the diagonals are not perpendicular. A trapezoid and a general parallelogram do not have perpendicular diagonals in general.
