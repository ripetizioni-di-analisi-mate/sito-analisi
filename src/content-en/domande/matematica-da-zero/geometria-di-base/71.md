A wire $62.8$ cm long is bent into a circle. What is the area of the enclosed disk? Use $\pi \approx 3.14$.

- $3140\ \text{cm}^2$
- $98.6\ \text{cm}^2$
- $31.4\ \text{cm}^2$
- $314\ \text{cm}^2$

## Explanation

The wire is as long as the circumference, so $2\pi r = 62.8$ and $r = \dfrac{62.8}{2 \cdot 3.14} = 10$ cm. The area is $3.14 \cdot 10^2 = 314\ \text{cm}^2$. The value $98.6$ is $\pi$ multiplied by $31.4$, half the length of the wire, without squaring the radius. The wire length determines the radius, which is needed to find the area.
