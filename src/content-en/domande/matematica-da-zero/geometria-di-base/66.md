A circle has circumference $31.4$ cm. What is its radius? Use $\pi \approx 3.14$.

- $10\ \text{cm}$
- $5\ \text{cm}$
- $2.5\ \text{cm}$
- $15.7\ \text{cm}$

## Explanation

From $C = 2\pi r$, we obtain $r = \dfrac{C}{2\pi} = \dfrac{31.4}{2 \cdot 3.14} = 5$ cm. The value $10$ cm is the diameter $\dfrac{C}{\pi}$, and $15.7$ cm is half the circumference. Checking with the diameter gives $2 \cdot 5 = 10$ cm, consistent with $C = \pi d$.
