Which of the following statements about angles is true?

- Two supplementary angles are always both obtuse
- Two vertical angles are always supplementary
- A right angle has an obtuse complement
- Two complementary angles are always both acute

## Explanation

Two complementary angles add up to $90^\circ$ and each has positive measure, so each measures less than $90^\circ$ and is acute. Two supplementary angles, by contrast, might measure $90^\circ$ and $90^\circ$, or $30^\circ$ and $150^\circ$, so they are not always obtuse. Vertical angles are equal and are supplementary only when each measures $90^\circ$.
