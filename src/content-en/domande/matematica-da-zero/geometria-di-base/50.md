A right triangle has a hypotenuse of $17$ cm and a leg of $8$ cm. How long is the other leg?

- $15\ \text{cm}$
- $9\ \text{cm}$
- $25\ \text{cm}$
- $225\ \text{cm}$

## Explanation

By the Pythagorean theorem, $a^2 = c^2 - b^2 = 17^2 - 8^2 = 289 - 64 = 225$, so $a = 15$ cm. The value $225$ is $a^2$ before taking the square root. The value $9$ cm is the difference $17 - 8$ and $25$ cm is the sum. Again, take the positive root because a length cannot be negative.
