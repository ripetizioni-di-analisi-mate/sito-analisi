If a cube has volume $27\ \text{cm}^3$, how long is one edge?

- $3\ \text{cm}$
- $9\ \text{cm}$
- $13.5\ \text{cm}$
- $81\ \text{cm}$

## Explanation

The volume of a cube is $l^3 = 27$, so $l$ is the number that gives $27$ when used as a factor three times. Since $3 \cdot 3 \cdot 3 = 27$, the edge length is $3$ cm. The value $9$ comes from dividing $27$ by $3$, and $13.5$ from dividing it by $2$.
