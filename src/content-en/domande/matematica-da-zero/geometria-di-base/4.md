An angle of $117^\circ$ and its supplement form a straight angle. What is the measure of the supplement?

- $63^\circ$
- $27^\circ$
- $243^\circ$
- $207^\circ$

## Explanation

Two angles are supplementary if their sum is a straight angle, or $180^\circ$. The supplement of $117^\circ$ therefore measures $180^\circ - 117^\circ = 63^\circ$. The value $27^\circ$ is the difference $117^\circ - 90^\circ$, and $243^\circ$ is $360^\circ - 117^\circ$, calculated using a full turn.
