A right triangle has legs of $9$ cm and $12$ cm. How long is the hypotenuse?

- $21\ \text{cm}$
- $225\ \text{cm}$
- $7.5\ \text{cm}$
- $15\ \text{cm}$

## Explanation

By the Pythagorean theorem, $c^2 = 9^2 + 12^2 = 81 + 144 = 225$, so $c = \sqrt{225} = 15$ cm. The value $21$ cm is the sum of the legs, and $225$ is $c^2$, the square of the hypotenuse. Take the positive root because the hypotenuse is a length.
