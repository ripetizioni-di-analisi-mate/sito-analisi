The diagonals of a rhombus have lengths $12$ cm and $16$ cm and meet at $M$. If $A$ is an endpoint of the $16$ cm diagonal, how long is $AM$?

- $16\ \text{cm}$
- $6\ \text{cm}$
- $4\ \text{cm}$
- $8\ \text{cm}$

## Explanation

The diagonals of a rhombus bisect each other, so $M$ is the midpoint of the $16$ cm diagonal. Thus, $AM = \dfrac{16}{2} = 8$ cm. The value $16$ cm is the length of the entire diagonal, and $6$ cm is half of the other diagonal.
