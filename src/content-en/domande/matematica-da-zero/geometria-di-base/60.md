A square has a diagonal of $\sqrt{50}$ cm. What is its side length?

- $\dfrac{\sqrt{50}}{2}\ \text{cm}$
- $25\ \text{cm}$
- $\sqrt{50}\ \text{cm}$
- $5\ \text{cm}$

## Explanation

The diagonal is the hypotenuse of a right triangle whose legs both equal the side length $l$. Thus, $l^2 + l^2 = 50$, or $2l^2 = 50$ and $l^2 = 25$, which gives $l = 5$ cm. The value $25$ is $l^2$, whereas $\dfrac{\sqrt{50}}{2}$ comes from halving the diagonal.
