A box with a square base of side length $5$ cm has volume $150\ \text{cm}^3$. How high is it?

- $6\ \text{cm}$
- $30\ \text{cm}$
- $25\ \text{cm}$
- $3\ \text{cm}$

## Explanation

The base area is $5 \cdot 5 = 25\ \text{cm}^2$ and $V = 25 \cdot h$, so $h = \dfrac{150}{25} = 6$ cm. The value $30$ cm comes from dividing the volume by the side length instead of the base area, and $25$ is the base area. Dividing the volume by the base area gives the height because division reverses the multiplication.
