A cylindrical can is $12$ cm high and has a base diameter of $6$ cm. What is its volume?

- $432\pi\ \text{cm}^3$
- $108\pi\ \text{cm}^3$
- $36\pi\ \text{cm}^3$
- $72\pi\ \text{cm}^3$

## Explanation

The radius is half the diameter, or $3$ cm, so $V = \pi \cdot 3^2 \cdot 12 = 108\pi\ \text{cm}^3$. The value $432\pi$ comes from using the diameter in place of the radius, and $36\pi$ is the base area calculated using the diameter, with the height omitted.
