In a square, what angle does the diagonal make with each side it meets?

- $90^\circ$
- $60^\circ$
- $30^\circ$
- $45^\circ$

## Explanation

The diagonal divides the square into two right triangles whose legs are equal. In each, the two acute angles are equal and add up to $90^\circ$, so each measures $\dfrac{90^\circ}{2} = 45^\circ$. The $90^\circ$ angle is the angle of the square at its vertex rather than the angle formed with the diagonal.
