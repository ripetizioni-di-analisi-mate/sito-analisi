What is the greatest number of obtuse angles a triangle can have?

- Two
- One
- Three
- None

## Explanation

An obtuse angle measures more than $90^\circ$. Two obtuse angles would have a sum greater than $180^\circ$, but the three angles of a triangle add up to exactly $180^\circ$, so the third angle would have to be negative. Thus, a triangle has at most one obtuse angle, and an obtuse triangle has exactly one.
