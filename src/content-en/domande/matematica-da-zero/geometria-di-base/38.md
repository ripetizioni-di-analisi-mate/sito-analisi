A square has area $49\ \text{cm}^2$. What is its perimeter?

- $28\ \text{cm}$
- $196\ \text{cm}$
- $14\ \text{cm}$
- $12.25\ \text{cm}$

## Explanation

The area of a square is $l^2$, so $l^2 = 49$ and $l = \sqrt{49} = 7$ cm. The perimeter is $4 \cdot 7 = 28$ cm. The value $14$ cm is $2 \cdot 7$, the semiperimeter. The value $196$ comes from multiplying the area by $4$, while $12.25$ comes from dividing it by $4$.
