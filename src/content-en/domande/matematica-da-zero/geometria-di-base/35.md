A rectangle has perimeter $30$ cm and base $9$ cm. What is its area?

- $135\ \text{cm}^2$
- $21\ \text{cm}^2$
- $54\ \text{cm}^2$
- $27\ \text{cm}^2$

## Explanation

The semiperimeter is $\dfrac{30}{2} = 15$ cm and equals the sum of the base and height. The height is therefore $15 - 9 = 6$ cm and the area is $9 \cdot 6 = 54\ \text{cm}^2$. The value $135\ \text{cm}^2$ comes from multiplying the base by the semiperimeter without first subtracting the base.
