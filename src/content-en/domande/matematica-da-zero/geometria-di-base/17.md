A triangle has two angles of $35^\circ$ and a third angle of $110^\circ$. How is it classified?

- Isosceles and obtuse
- Equilateral and acute
- Scalene and right
- Isosceles and right

## Explanation

The two equal angles are the base angles of an isosceles triangle, so the triangle is isosceles. The $110^\circ$ angle is greater than $90^\circ$, so it is obtuse and the triangle is obtuse. An equilateral triangle has three angles of $60^\circ$, and a right triangle has an angle of $90^\circ$.
