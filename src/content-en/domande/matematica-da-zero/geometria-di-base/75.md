A room has area $18\ \text{m}^2$. How many square decimeters is that?

- $180\ \text{dm}^2$
- $18\,000\ \text{dm}^2$
- $180\,000\ \text{dm}^2$
- $1800\ \text{dm}^2$

## Explanation

In the sequence of area units, each unit is $100$ times as large as the next, so to convert square meters to square decimeters, multiply by $100$ to obtain $18 \cdot 100 = 1800\ \text{dm}^2$. The value $180$ comes from multiplying by $10$, the factor for lengths, and $180\,000$ is the same area expressed in $\text{cm}^2$.
