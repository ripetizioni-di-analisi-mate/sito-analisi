An isosceles trapezoid has bases of $20$ cm and $8$ cm and legs of $10$ cm each. What is the distance between its bases?

- $6\ \text{cm}$
- $12\ \text{cm}$
- $8\ \text{cm}$
- $64\ \text{cm}$

## Explanation

The altitudes from the endpoints of the shorter base cut off two congruent right triangles. Each has one leg of $\dfrac{20 - 8}{2} = 6$ cm and a hypotenuse of $10$ cm. The height is $h = \sqrt{10^2 - 6^2} = \sqrt{64} = 8$ cm. The value $6$ cm is a leg of the triangle cut off, and $12$ cm is the difference between the bases.
