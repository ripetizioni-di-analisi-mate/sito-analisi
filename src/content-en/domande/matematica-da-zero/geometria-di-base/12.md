The points $A$, $B$, and $C$ lie on the same line in that order. Segment $AC$ measures $12$ cm and segment $AB$ measures $7.5$ cm. How long is $BC$?

- $19.5\ \text{cm}$
- $4.5\ \text{cm}$
- $5.5\ \text{cm}$
- $6\ \text{cm}$

## Explanation

Since $B$ lies between $A$ and $C$, segment $AC$ consists of $AB$ and $BC$, so $AC = AB + BC$. Thus, $BC = AC - AB = 12 - 7.5 = 4.5$ cm. The value $19.5$ cm is the sum of the two known lengths, and $6$ cm would be the length of $BC$ only if $B$ were the midpoint of $AC$.
