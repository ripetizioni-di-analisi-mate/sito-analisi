In the quadrilateral in the figure, the two horizontal sides are parallel and the two slanted sides are not. What is it called?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="0,0 7,0 5.5,3 1.5,3" xmin=-0.6 xmax=7.6 ymin=-0.6 ymax=3.6 legenda=0}

- Parallelogram
- Trapezoid
- Rhombus
- Rectangle

## Explanation

The quadrilateral has just one pair of parallel sides, the horizontal ones. The slanted sides are not parallel. A quadrilateral with two parallel sides and two nonparallel sides is a trapezoid. A parallelogram also has parallel slanted sides, and rectangles and rhombuses are special cases of parallelograms.
