The figure shows a $10$ cm by $6$ cm rectangle with a $4$ cm by $3$ cm rectangle removed from one corner. What is the area of the remaining figure?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="0,0 10,0 10,3 6,3 6,6 0,6" etichette="4.6,-0.7,10 cm;-1.6,2.8,6 cm;7.6,3.5,4 cm;6.55,4.35,3 cm" xmin=-2.5 xmax=11.5 ymin=-1.3 ymax=7 legenda=0}

- $60\ \text{cm}^2$
- $72\ \text{cm}^2$
- $48\ \text{cm}^2$
- $32\ \text{cm}^2$

## Explanation

Subtract the area removed from the area of the whole rectangle, so the remaining area is $10 \cdot 6 - 4 \cdot 3 = 60 - 12 = 48\ \text{cm}^2$. The value $60\ \text{cm}^2$ is the area of the whole rectangle, and $72\ \text{cm}^2$ comes from adding the area removed instead of subtracting it. The value $32$ is the perimeter of the figure.
