A cube has edge length $6$ cm. What is its volume?

- $36\ \text{cm}^3$
- $216\ \text{cm}^3$
- $18\ \text{cm}^3$
- $24\ \text{cm}^3$

## Explanation

The volume of the cube is $V = l^3 = 6 \cdot 6 \cdot 6 = 216\ \text{cm}^3$. The value $36$ is $l^2$, the area of one face. The value $18$ is the product $3 \cdot 6$, and $24$ is the product $4 \cdot 6$. The volume is measured in cubic centimeters because it is the product of three lengths in centimeters.
