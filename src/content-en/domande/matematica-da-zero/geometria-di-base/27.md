Which of these statements about quadrilaterals is true?

- Every rectangle is also a square
- Every square is also a rhombus
- Every parallelogram has four right angles
- Every trapezoid has both pairs of opposite sides parallel

## Explanation

A square has four equal sides and four right angles. A rhombus is a parallelogram with four equal sides, so every square is a rhombus. A rectangle with sides of $5$ cm and $3$ cm has four right angles but unequal sides, so it is not a square.
