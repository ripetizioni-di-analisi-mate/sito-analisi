The isosceles triangle in the figure has a base of $16$ cm and equal sides of $17$ cm. How long is the altitude $h$ to the base?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="0,0 16,0 8,15" segmenti="8,0,8,15" linee="8,0.8 8.8,0.8 8.8,0" etichette="7.2,-1.3,16 cm;2.4,8,17 cm;8.4,7.2,h" xmin=-1.5 xmax=17.5 ymin=-2.2 ymax=16.2 legenda=0}

- $8\ \text{cm}$
- $25\ \text{cm}$
- $9\ \text{cm}$
- $15\ \text{cm}$

## Explanation

The altitude to the base divides the triangle into two right triangles, each with hypotenuse $17$ cm and one leg equal to half the base, or $8$ cm. Thus, $h = \sqrt{17^2 - 8^2} = \sqrt{289 - 64} = \sqrt{225} = 15$ cm. The value $8$ cm is half the base and $25$ cm is the sum $17 + 8$.
