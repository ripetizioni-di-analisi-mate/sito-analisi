A rectangular floor measuring $4.5$ m by $3$ m is covered with square tiles of side length $30$ cm. How many tiles are needed?

- $150$
- $45$
- $15$
- $1500$

## Explanation

The side measuring $4.5$ m, or $450$ cm, fits $\dfrac{450}{30} = 15$ tiles, and the side measuring $3$ m, or $300$ cm, fits $\dfrac{300}{30} = 10$ tiles. The total number of tiles is therefore $15 \cdot 10 = 150$. The areas give the same number, $\dfrac{13.5\ \text{m}^2}{0.09\ \text{m}^2} = 150$. The value $45$ comes from dividing the floor area by the tile's side length of $0.3$ m instead of its area.
