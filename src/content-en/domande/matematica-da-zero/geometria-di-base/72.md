A disk has area $49\pi\ \text{cm}^2$. What is its circumference?

- $7\pi\ \text{cm}$
- $14\pi\ \text{cm}$
- $98\pi\ \text{cm}$
- $49\pi\ \text{cm}$

## Explanation

From $\pi r^2 = 49\pi$, we obtain $r^2 = 49$, so $r = 7$ cm. The circumference is $2\pi r = 14\pi$ cm. The value $7\pi$ comes from omitting the factor of $2$, and $98\pi$ from doubling the area. The circumference is found from the radius because the area gives $r^2$ rather than $r$.
