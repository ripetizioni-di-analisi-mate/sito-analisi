Every side of a regular pentagon is $6.5$ cm long. What is its perimeter?

- $32.5\ \text{cm}$
- $26\ \text{cm}$
- $39\ \text{cm}$
- $42.25\ \text{cm}$

## Explanation

A pentagon has five sides, and its perimeter is the sum of their lengths. Since the sides are equal, $2p = 5 \cdot 6.5 = 32.5$ cm. The value $39$ cm is the perimeter of a hexagon, which has six sides, and $42.25$ is the square of $6.5$, which does not measure a perimeter.
