Three angles of a quadrilateral measure $85^\circ$, $100^\circ$, and $95^\circ$. What is the measure of the fourth angle?

- $280^\circ$
- $175^\circ$
- $80^\circ$
- $640^\circ$

## Explanation

A diagonal divides the quadrilateral into two triangles, so the sum of its interior angles is $2 \cdot 180^\circ = 360^\circ$. The three known angles add up to $85^\circ + 100^\circ + 95^\circ = 280^\circ$ and the fourth measures $360^\circ - 280^\circ = 80^\circ$. The value $280^\circ$ is the sum of the three angles before subtraction.
