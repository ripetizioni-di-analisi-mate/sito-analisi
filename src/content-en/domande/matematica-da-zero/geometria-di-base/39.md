A triangle has base $14$ cm and corresponding altitude $9$ cm. What is its area?

- $126\ \text{cm}^2$
- $63\ \text{cm}^2$
- $23\ \text{cm}^2$
- $31.5\ \text{cm}^2$

## Explanation

The area of a triangle is half the product of its base and height, so $A = \dfrac{14 \cdot 9}{2} = \dfrac{126}{2} = 63\ \text{cm}^2$. The value $126\ \text{cm}^2$ is the area of a parallelogram with the same base and height, and $23$ is the sum of the base and height.
