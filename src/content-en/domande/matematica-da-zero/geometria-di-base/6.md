Three consecutive angles with a common vertex on a line fill a half-plane. Two of them measure $35^\circ$ and $70^\circ$. What is the measure of the third?

- $105^\circ$
- $75^\circ$
- $145^\circ$
- $110^\circ$

## Explanation

Together the three angles form a straight angle, which measures $180^\circ$. The sum of the two known angles is $35^\circ + 70^\circ = 105^\circ$, so the third measures $180^\circ - 105^\circ = 75^\circ$. The value $105^\circ$ is the sum of the two known angles before subtracting it from $180^\circ$.
