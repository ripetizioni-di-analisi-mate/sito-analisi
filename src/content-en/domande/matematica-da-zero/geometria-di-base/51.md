A rectangular door is $2.4$ m high and $1$ m wide. How long is its diagonal?

- $3.4\ \text{m}$
- $2.6\ \text{m}$
- $6.76\ \text{m}$
- $1.4\ \text{m}$

## Explanation

The diagonal is the hypotenuse of a right triangle with legs of $2.4$ m and $1$ m. Therefore, $d^2 = 2.4^2 + 1^2 = 5.76 + 1 = 6.76$ and $d = \sqrt{6.76} = 2.6$ m. The value $3.4$ m is the sum of the two sides and $6.76$ is $d^2$.
