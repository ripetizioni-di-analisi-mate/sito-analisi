A trapezoid has bases of $14$ cm and $8$ cm and legs of $5$ cm and $6$ cm. What is its perimeter?

- $22\ \text{cm}$
- $33\ \text{cm}$
- $44\ \text{cm}$
- $11\ \text{cm}$

## Explanation

The perimeter is the sum of the lengths of its four sides, the two bases and the two legs. This gives $2p = 14 + 8 + 5 + 6 = 33$ cm. The value $22$ cm is the sum of the bases alone, $11$ cm is the sum of the legs alone, and $44$ cm is twice $22$ cm.
