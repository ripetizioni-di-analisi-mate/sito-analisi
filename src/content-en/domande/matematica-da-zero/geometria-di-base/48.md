A rectangle has base $12$ cm and height $5$ cm. The height increases by $2$ cm and the base stays the same. By how much does the area increase?

- $10\ \text{cm}^2$
- $24\ \text{cm}^2$
- $84\ \text{cm}^2$
- $14\ \text{cm}^2$

## Explanation

The initial area is $12 \cdot 5 = 60\ \text{cm}^2$ and the final area is $12 \cdot 7 = 84\ \text{cm}^2$. The increase is $84 - 60 = 24\ \text{cm}^2$. The value $84\ \text{cm}^2$ is the final area, whereas $10$ comes from multiplying the increase in height by the initial height instead of the base. Only the $2$ cm added to the height contribute to the increase, and they are multiplied by the base of $12$ cm.
