A flower bed is shaped like a hexagon whose consecutive side lengths are $1.5$ m, $2$ m, $2.5$ m, $1.5$ m, $2$ m, and $1.5$ m. What is its perimeter?

- $9.5\ \text{m}$
- $6\ \text{m}$
- $11\ \text{m}$
- $13\ \text{m}$

## Explanation

The perimeter is the sum of all the side lengths, so $2p = 1.5 + 2 + 2.5 + 1.5 + 2 + 1.5 = 11$ m. The value $6$ m comes from adding only the three distinct lengths, and $9.5$ m comes from leaving out one of the $1.5$ m sides.
