A footpath is $0.75$ km long. How many meters is that?

- $7.5\ \text{m}$
- $75\ \text{m}$
- $750\ \text{m}$
- $7500\ \text{m}$

## Explanation

A kilometer is $1000$ m, so multiply by $1000$ to obtain $0.75 \cdot 1000 = 750$ m. From kilometers to meters there are three steps in the sequence of length units, each with a factor of $10$. The value $75$ m comes from multiplying by only $100$, and $7500$ m from multiplying by $10\,000$.
