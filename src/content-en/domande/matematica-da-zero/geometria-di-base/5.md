Two lines intersect and form four angles. One measures $64^\circ$. What is the measure of each angle that shares a side with the given angle?

- $116^\circ$
- $64^\circ$
- $26^\circ$
- $296^\circ$

## Explanation

The $64^\circ$ angle and each angle sharing one of its sides have their other sides on the same line, so together they form a straight angle. Each adjacent angle therefore measures $180^\circ - 64^\circ = 116^\circ$. The value $64^\circ$ is the measure of the vertical angle, which is equal to the given one.
