Four rectangular enclosures each have perimeter $24$ m. Their dimensions are $6$ m by $6$ m, $10$ m by $2$ m, $8$ m by $4$ m, and $9$ m by $3$ m. Which encloses the largest area?

- The $10$ m by $2$ m enclosure
- The $6$ m by $6$ m enclosure
- The $8$ m by $4$ m enclosure
- The $9$ m by $3$ m enclosure

## Explanation

The areas are $6 \cdot 6 = 36\ \text{m}^2$, $10 \cdot 2 = 20\ \text{m}^2$, $8 \cdot 4 = 32\ \text{m}^2$, and $9 \cdot 3 = 27\ \text{m}^2$. The largest is $36\ \text{m}^2$, the area of the square. Figures with the same perimeter can therefore have different areas. The long, narrow enclosure measuring $10$ m by $2$ m encloses the smallest area.
