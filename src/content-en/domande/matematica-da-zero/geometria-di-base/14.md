In triangle $ABC$, the angles at $A$ and $B$ are $48^\circ$ and $67^\circ$, respectively. The bisector of the angle at $A$ meets $BC$ at $D$. How large is angle $ADB$?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="0,0 6,0 4.08,4.53" linee="0,0 5.05,2.25" etichette="-0.45,-0.35,A;6.14,-0.35,B;4.08,4.78,C;5.24,2.28,D" xmin=-0.7 xmax=6.7 ymin=-0.7 ymax=5.5 legenda=0 titolo="The angle bisector at A in triangle ABC"}

- $65^\circ$
- $89^\circ$
- $91^\circ$
- $115^\circ$

## Explanation

The bisector gives $\angle BAD=24^\circ$. Since $D$ lies on $BC$, angle $ABD$ is still $67^\circ$. In triangle $ABD$ the third angle is $180^\circ-24^\circ-67^\circ=89^\circ$.
