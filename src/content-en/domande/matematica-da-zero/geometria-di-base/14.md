Two angles of a triangle measure $48^\circ$ and $67^\circ$. What is the measure of the third angle?

- $115^\circ$
- $65^\circ$
- $132^\circ$
- $113^\circ$

## Explanation

The interior angles of a triangle add up to $180^\circ$. The two known angles add up to $48^\circ + 67^\circ = 115^\circ$, so the third measures $180^\circ - 115^\circ = 65^\circ$. The value $115^\circ$ is the sum of the known angles before subtracting it from $180^\circ$.
