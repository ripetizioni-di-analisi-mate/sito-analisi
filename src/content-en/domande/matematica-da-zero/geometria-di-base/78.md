A rectangle has a base of $80$ cm and a height of $0.5$ m. What is its area in square centimeters?

- $40\ \text{cm}^2$
- $4000\ \text{cm}^2$
- $400\ \text{cm}^2$
- $40\,000\ \text{cm}^2$

## Explanation

Express both lengths in the same unit. We have $0.5$ m $= 50$ cm. The area is $80 \cdot 50 = 4000\ \text{cm}^2$. The value $40$ is the product $80 \cdot 0.5$ calculated using different units, and $40\,000$ comes from converting $0.5$ m to $500$ cm.
