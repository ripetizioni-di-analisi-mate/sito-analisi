An isosceles triangle has perimeter $34$ cm and base $12$ cm. What is the length of each of the equal sides?

- $22\ \text{cm}$
- $17\ \text{cm}$
- $5.5\ \text{cm}$
- $11\ \text{cm}$

## Explanation

The perimeter is the sum of the base and the two equal sides. The two equal sides therefore have a combined length of $34 - 12 = 22$ cm, and each measures $\dfrac{22}{2} = 11$ cm. The value $22$ cm is the sum of the equal sides, and $17$ cm is half the perimeter.
