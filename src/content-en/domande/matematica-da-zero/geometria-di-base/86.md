A tank shaped like a rectangular box has a base of $60$ cm by $40$ cm. A stone is fully submerged and the water level rises from $18$ cm to $20$ cm, without any water spilling. What is the volume of the stone?

- $0.48$ liters
- $4.8$ liters
- $48$ liters
- $43.2$ liters

## Explanation

A fully submerged stone displaces a volume of water equal to its own volume. The rise in level therefore corresponds to a box with the same base as the tank and height $20 - 18 = 2$ cm. The volume of the stone is therefore $60 \cdot 40 \cdot 2 = 4800\ \text{cm}^3$. Since $1$ liter is $1000\ \text{cm}^3$, the stone has a volume of $\dfrac{4800}{1000} = 4.8$ liters. The value $43.2$ liters is the volume of the water in the tank before the stone is submerged.
