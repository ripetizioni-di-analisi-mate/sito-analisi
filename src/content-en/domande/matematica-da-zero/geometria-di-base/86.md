A tank shaped like a rectangular prism is $2$ m long, $1$ m wide, and $0.5$ m high. It is filled with water to a depth of $0.4$ m. How many liters of water does it contain?

- $1000$ liters
- $800$ liters
- $80$ liters
- $8000$ liters

## Explanation

The volume of the water is $2 \cdot 1 \cdot 0.4 = 0.8\ \text{m}^3$. Since $1\ \text{m}^3 = 1000$ liters, the number of liters is $0.8 \cdot 1000 = 800$. The value $1000$ is the capacity of the entire tank, with height $0.5$ m, and $80$ comes from using the factor $100$. The water reaches only $0.4$ m, so use its depth in the calculation rather than the height of the tank.
