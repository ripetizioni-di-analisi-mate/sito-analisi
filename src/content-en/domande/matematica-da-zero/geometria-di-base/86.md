A rectangular tank has a base measuring $60$ cm by $40$ cm. When a stone is fully submerged, the level rises from $18$ to $20$ cm without any water spilling. What is the stone's volume in litres?

- $0.48$
- $4.8$
- $48$
- $43.2$

## Explanation

The stone displaces its own volume of water. The relevant height is the $2$ cm rise. The volume is $60\cdot40\cdot2=4800\,\mathrm{cm^3}$, which is $4.8$ litres.
