The diagonals of a rhombus measure $12$ cm and $16$ cm. How long is one side?

- $14\ \text{cm}$
- $100\ \text{cm}$
- $7\ \text{cm}$
- $10\ \text{cm}$

## Explanation

The diagonals are perpendicular and bisect each other, so a side is the hypotenuse of a right triangle with legs of $6$ cm and $8$ cm. Thus, $l = \sqrt{36 + 64} = \sqrt{100} = 10$ cm. The value $14$ cm is the sum of the two legs and $100$ is $l^2$.
