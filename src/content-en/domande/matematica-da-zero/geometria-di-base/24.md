In the triangle in the figure, the angles at $A$ and $B$ measure $52^\circ$ and $71^\circ$. What is the measure of the angle at $C$?

- $57^\circ$
- $123^\circ$
- $109^\circ$
- $128^\circ$

## Explanation

The interior angles of a triangle add up to $180^\circ$. The angle at $C$ therefore measures $180^\circ - (52^\circ + 71^\circ) = 180^\circ - 123^\circ = 57^\circ$. The value $123^\circ$ is the sum of the two known angles, and $128^\circ$ is $180^\circ - 52^\circ$, which does not account for the angle at $B$.
