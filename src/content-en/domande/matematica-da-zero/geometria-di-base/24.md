In the triangle shown, the angles at $A$ and $B$ measure $52^\circ$ and $71^\circ$. Side $AC$ is extended beyond $C$. What is the measure of the exterior angle between this extension and side $CB$?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="0,0 6,0 4.16,5.33" linee="4.16,5.33 5.0,6.4;0.90,0.00 0.89,0.10 0.88,0.20 0.85,0.30 0.81,0.39 0.76,0.48 0.70,0.57 0.63,0.64 0.55,0.71;5.71,0.85 5.58,0.80 5.46,0.72 5.36,0.63 5.27,0.52 5.20,0.40 5.14,0.27 5.11,0.14 5.10,0.00" etichette="-0.45,-0.45,A;6.1,-0.45,B;4.16,5.65,C;1.1,0.3,52°;4.55,0.45,71°" xmin=-1 xmax=7 ymin=-1 ymax=7.0 legenda=0}

- $123^\circ$
- $57^\circ$
- $109^\circ$
- $128^\circ$

## Explanation

The exterior angle at $C$ and the interior angle at $C$ are adjacent and form a straight angle, so they are supplementary. Since the interior angles of the triangle add up to $180^\circ$, the interior angle at $C$ measures $180^\circ - (52^\circ + 71^\circ) = 57^\circ$. The exterior angle therefore measures $180^\circ - 57^\circ = 123^\circ$. This value equals the sum $52^\circ + 71^\circ$ of the two nonadjacent interior angles, while $57^\circ$ is the interior angle at $C$.
