A door opens by rotating on its hinges one-eighth of a turn from its closed position. Through how many degrees does it rotate?

- $90^\circ$
- $22.5^\circ$
- $45^\circ$
- $135^\circ$

## Explanation

A full turn measures $360^\circ$. One-eighth of a turn measures $\dfrac{360^\circ}{8} = 45^\circ$. The value $90^\circ$ corresponds to a quarter turn. The value $22.5^\circ$ comes from applying the divisor $8$ to a straight angle, or half a turn, instead of a full turn.
