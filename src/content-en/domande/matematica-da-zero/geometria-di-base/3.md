What is the measure of the complement of an angle of $38^\circ$?

- $142^\circ$
- $128^\circ$
- $62^\circ$
- $52^\circ$

## Explanation

Two angles are complementary if their sum is a right angle, or $90^\circ$. The complement of $38^\circ$ therefore measures $90^\circ - 38^\circ = 52^\circ$. The value $142^\circ$ is the supplement, obtained by subtracting from $180^\circ$ instead of $90^\circ$, while $128^\circ$ comes from adding $38^\circ$ to $90^\circ$.
