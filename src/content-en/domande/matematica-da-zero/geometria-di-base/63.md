A circle has radius $9$ cm. What is its circumference?

- $9\pi\ \text{cm}$
- $81\pi\ \text{cm}$
- $18\pi\ \text{cm}$
- $36\pi\ \text{cm}$

## Explanation

The circumference is $C = 2\pi r = 2 \cdot \pi \cdot 9 = 18\pi$ cm. The value $9\pi$ cm comes from omitting the factor of $2$, and $81\pi$ comes from using $\pi r^2$, the area formula, which does not give a length. For a radius of $r = 9$ cm, the diameter is $18$ cm and the circumference is $\pi$ times the diameter.
