Two wooden boards of lengths $1.2$ m and $85$ cm are placed end to end. What is their total length in meters?

- $86.2\ \text{m}$
- $20.5\ \text{m}$
- $1.285\ \text{m}$
- $2.05\ \text{m}$

## Explanation

The lengths must be expressed in the same unit, and $85$ cm $= 0.85$ m. The total length is $1.2 + 0.85 = 2.05$ m. The value $86.2$ comes from adding $1.2$ and $85$ without converting, and $1.285$ from converting $85$ cm to $0.085$ m.
