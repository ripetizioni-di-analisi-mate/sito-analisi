A tank holds $3500$ liters. How many cubic meters is that?

- $3.5\ \text{m}^3$
- $35\ \text{m}^3$
- $0.35\ \text{m}^3$
- $3500\ \text{m}^3$

## Explanation

A liter is $1\ \text{dm}^3$ and a cubic meter is $1000\ \text{dm}^3$, so $1\ \text{m}^3 = 1000$ liters. Therefore, $3500$ liters is $\dfrac{3500}{1000} = 3.5\ \text{m}^3$. The value $35$ comes from dividing by $100$, and $0.35$ from dividing by $10\,000$. Converting liters to cubic meters requires a single factor, $1000$.
