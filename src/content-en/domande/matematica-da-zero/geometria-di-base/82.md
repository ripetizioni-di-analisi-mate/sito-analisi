A rectangular prism has dimensions $12$ cm, $5$ cm, and $3$ cm. What is its volume?

- $180\ \text{cm}^3$
- $20\ \text{cm}^3$
- $60\ \text{cm}^3$
- $360\ \text{cm}^3$

## Explanation

The volume is the product of the three dimensions, so $V = 12 \cdot 5 \cdot 3 = 180\ \text{cm}^3$. The value $60$ is the base area $12 \cdot 5$, which omits the height, and $20$ is the sum of the three dimensions. The base holds $60$ cubes with edge length $1$ cm, and the solid consists of $3$ identical layers.
