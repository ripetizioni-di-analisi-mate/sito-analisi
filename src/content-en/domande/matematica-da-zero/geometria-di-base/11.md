In rectangle $ABCD$, sides $AB$ and $DC$ are both perpendicular to side $BC$. How are lines $AB$ and $DC$ related?

- Perpendicular
- They intersect at an angle of $45^\circ$
- Parallel
- Coincident

## Explanation

A rectangle is a parallelogram, so its opposite sides lie on parallel lines. Sides $AB$ and $DC$ are opposite, so lines $AB$ and $DC$ are parallel. The perpendicular lines are those containing consecutive sides, such as $AB$ and $BC$.
