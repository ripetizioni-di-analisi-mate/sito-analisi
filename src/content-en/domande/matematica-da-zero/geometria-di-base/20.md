A right triangle has legs of $6$ cm and $8$ cm. How long is the altitude to the $6$ cm leg?

- $8\ \text{cm}$
- $6\ \text{cm}$
- $10\ \text{cm}$
- $4.8\ \text{cm}$

## Explanation

The altitude to a side is the segment from the opposite vertex to the line containing that side, meeting it at a right angle. The legs are perpendicular, so the altitude to one leg is the other leg and measures $8$ cm. The value $4.8$ cm is the altitude to the hypotenuse, and $10$ cm is the length of the hypotenuse.
