In the figure, a disk is inscribed in a square of side length $12$ cm, meaning that it touches all four sides. What is the area of the disk?

- $144\pi\ \text{cm}^2$
- $12\pi\ \text{cm}^2$
- $6\pi\ \text{cm}^2$
- $36\pi\ \text{cm}^2$

## Explanation

The disk touches two opposite sides of the square, so its diameter is $12$ cm and its radius is $6$ cm. Its area is $\pi \cdot 6^2 = 36\pi\ \text{cm}^2$. The value $144\pi$ comes from using the side as the radius, and $12\pi$ is the circumference.
