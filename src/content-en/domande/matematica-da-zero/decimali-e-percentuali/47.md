On a map with scale $1 : 25\,000$, two cities are $8$ cm apart. How many kilometers apart are they on the ground?

- $20$ km
- $2$ km
- $0.2$ km
- $200$ km

## Explanation

One centimeter on the map represents $25\,000$ cm on the ground. Therefore $8$ cm represents $8 \cdot 25\,000 = 200\,000$ cm. Since $1$ km $= 100\,000$ cm, the distance is $2$ km. The other values result from errors in converting between centimeters, meters and kilometers.
