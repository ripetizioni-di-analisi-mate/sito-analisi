The price of a television changes from $400$ to $340$ euros. What is the percentage change in its price?

- $-17.6\%$
- $-60\%$
- $-15\%$
- $-85\%$

## Explanation

The percentage change is $\dfrac{340 - 400}{400} \cdot 100 = \dfrac{-60}{400} \cdot 100 = -15$. The difference is divided by the original value. Dividing by the final value gives $\dfrac{60}{340} \cdot 100 \approx 17.6$, a different result.
