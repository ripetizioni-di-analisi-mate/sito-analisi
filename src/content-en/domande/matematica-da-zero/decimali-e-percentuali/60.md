Which of the following tables of pairs $(x\,;\,y)$ represents two directly proportional quantities?

- $(1\,;\,3)$, $(2\,;\,5)$, $(4\,;\,9)$
- $(1\,;\,12)$, $(2\,;\,6)$, $(3\,;\,4)$
- $(2\,;\,3)$, $(4\,;\,5)$, $(6\,;\,7)$
- $(1\,;\,6)$, $(2\,;\,12)$, $(5\,;\,30)$

## Explanation

For two directly proportional quantities, the ratio $\dfrac{y}{x}$ is constant. The pairs $(1\,;\,6)$, $(2\,;\,12)$ and $(5\,;\,30)$ all satisfy $6$. In the other tables, either the ratio changes or the product $x \cdot y = 12$ is constant, which characterizes inverse proportionality.
