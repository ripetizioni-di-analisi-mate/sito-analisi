What is the value of $14.7 + 5.86$?

- $20.56$
- $7.33$
- $73.3$
- $15.286$

## Explanation

Write the numbers with their decimal points aligned and add a zero to $14.7$ to obtain $14.70$. The sum is $14.70 + 5.86 = 20.56$. The value $7.33$ comes from placing the decimal point after the first digit of $147 + 586 = 733$, and $15.286$ comes from reading $5.86$ as $0.586$.
