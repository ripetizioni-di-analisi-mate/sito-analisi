By what number must $0.035$ be multiplied to obtain $3.5$?

- $100$
- $10$
- $1000$
- $35$

## Explanation

Going from $0.035$ to $3.5$ moves the decimal point two places to the right because $0.035 \to 0.35 \to 3.5$. Moving it two places to the right is equivalent to multiplying by $100$. Multiplying by $10$ would give $0.35$, and multiplying by $1000$ would give $35$.
