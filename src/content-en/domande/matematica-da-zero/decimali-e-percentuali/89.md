At a school with $500$ students, $60\%$ are girls. Another $100$ students arrive, all boys. What percentage of the students are girls after they arrive?

- $50\%$
- $60\%$
- $40\%$
- $20\%$

## Explanation

The number of girls is $60\%$ of $500$, or $300$. After the new students arrive, the total is $500 + 100 = 600$, while the number of girls remains $300$. The percentage is $\dfrac{300}{600} \cdot 100 = 50\%$. The value $60\%$ is the percentage before the new students arrived, and $20\%$ is the ratio of the new students to the original $500$ students.
