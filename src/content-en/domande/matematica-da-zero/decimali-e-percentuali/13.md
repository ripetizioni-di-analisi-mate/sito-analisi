What is the value of $0.0378 \cdot 1000$?

- $3.78$
- $378$
- $0.378$
- $37.8$

## Explanation

Multiplying by $1000$ moves the decimal point three places to the right. Starting with $0.0378$ gives $037.8 = 37.8$. Moving it one place would give $0.378$, two places would give $3.78$ and four places would give $378$.
