If $35\%$ of a cake has been eaten, what fraction of the cake is that, in lowest terms?

- $\dfrac{7}{20}$
- $\dfrac{7}{10}$
- $\dfrac{7}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$

## Explanation

The percentage $35\%$ is the fraction $\dfrac{35}{100}$. Dividing numerator and denominator by $5$ gives $\dfrac{7}{20}$. The fraction $\dfrac{7}{10}$ corresponds to $70\%$, and the fraction $\dfrac{7}{2}$ corresponds to $350\%$. The fraction $\dfrac{7}{20}$ is in lowest terms because $7$ does not divide $20$.
