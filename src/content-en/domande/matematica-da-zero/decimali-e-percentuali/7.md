In a long jump competition, the athletes jump $2.08$ m, $2.8$ m, $2.18$ m and $2.79$ m. Which jump is the longest?

- $2.18$ m
- $2.79$ m
- $2.08$ m
- $2.8$ m

## Explanation

The whole-number parts are equal, so compare the tenths digits, which are $0$, $8$, $1$ and $7$. The number $2.8$ has the largest tenths digit, so it is the greatest. Writing $2.8 = 2.80$ shows that $80$ hundredths is greater than $79$, $18$ and $8$ hundredths.
