Which of the following operations gives a number greater than $9$?

- $9 \cdot 0.9$
- $9 \cdot 0.5$
- $9 \cdot 0.99$
- $9 \div 0.9$

## Explanation

Dividing by a number between $0$ and $1$ gives a result greater than the dividend, as in $9 \div 0.9 = \dfrac{90}{9} = 10$. Multiplying $9$ by a number between $0$ and $1$ gives a result less than $9$. The products $9 \cdot 0.9$, $9 \cdot 0.5$ and $9 \cdot 0.99$ equal $8.1$, $4.5$ and $8.91$, respectively.
