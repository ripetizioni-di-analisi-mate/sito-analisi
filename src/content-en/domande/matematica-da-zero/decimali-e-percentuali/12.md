A kilogram of bread costs $2.40$ euros. How much does $1.5$ kg cost?

- $3.60$ euros
- $36$ euros
- $0.36$ euros
- $1.60$ euros

## Explanation

The cost is the product $2.40 \cdot 1.5$. Calculate $240 \cdot 15 = 3600$ and use three decimal places because the factors have $2 + 1 = 3$ in all. This gives $3.600 = 3.60$ euros. The value $1.60$ would come from dividing $2.40$ by $1.5$ instead of multiplying.
