In $3.057$, which digit represents hundredths?

- $0$
- $7$
- $3$
- $5$

## Explanation

After the decimal point, the first digit counts tenths, the second counts hundredths and the third counts thousandths. In $3.057$, the tenths digit is $0$, the hundredths digit is $5$ and the thousandths digit is $7$. The digit $3$ is the whole-number part. Therefore the hundredths digit is $5$.
