What is the value of $4.5 \div 0.15$?

- $30$
- $3$
- $0.3$
- $300$

## Explanation

Multiply the dividend and divisor by $100$ to make the divisor a natural number, giving $\dfrac{4.5}{0.15} = \dfrac{450}{15} = 30$. The quotient stays the same by the invariance property of division. The result is greater than $4.5$ because the divisor is less than $1$.
