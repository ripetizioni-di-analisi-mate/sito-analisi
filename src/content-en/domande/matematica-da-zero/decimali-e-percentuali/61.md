Five workers complete a job in $12$ days. How many days would $8$ workers need at the same rate?

- $19.2$ days
- $7.5$ days
- $9$ days
- $20$ days

## Explanation

More workers need fewer days, so the quantities are inversely proportional and the product of the number of workers and the number of days is constant, giving $5 \cdot 12 = 60$. With $8$ workers the number of days is $\dfrac{60}{8} = 7.5$. The value $19.2$ would result from direct proportionality, giving $\dfrac{12 \cdot 8}{5}$.
