What percentage corresponds to the decimal $0.07$?

- $70\%$
- $0.7\%$
- $7\%$
- $0.07\%$

## Explanation

A percentage is a fraction with denominator $100$. Since $0.07 = \dfrac{7}{100}$, the number corresponds to $7\%$. The value $70\%$ is $0.7$, while $0.7\%$ is $0.007$ and $0.07\%$ is $0.0007$.
