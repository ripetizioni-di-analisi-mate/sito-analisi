Which fraction in lowest terms equals the repeating decimal $0.\overline{27}$?

- $\dfrac{27}{100}$
- $\dfrac{3}{10}$
- $\dfrac{3}{11}$
- $\dfrac{3}{110}$

## Explanation

The repeating block $27$ has two digits and there is no nonrepeating part, so the numerator is $27$ and the denominator is $99$. This gives $\dfrac{27}{99} = \dfrac{3}{11}$. The denominator $100$ corresponds to a terminating decimal, while $990$ would be used with a one-digit nonrepeating part.
