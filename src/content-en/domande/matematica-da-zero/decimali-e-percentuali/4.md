The segment from $2$ to $3$ is divided into ten equal parts. Which number does the shaded point represent?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 linee="-0.6,0 10.8,0;10.4,0.2 10.8,0 10.4,-0.2;0,-0.3 0,0.3;1,-0.15 1,0.15;2,-0.15 2,0.15;3,-0.15 3,0.15;4,-0.15 4,0.15;5,-0.2 5,0.2;6,-0.15 6,0.15;7,-0.15 7,0.15;8,-0.15 8,0.15;9,-0.15 9,0.15;10,-0.3 10,0.3" punti="7,0,pieno" etichette="-0.15,-0.9,2;9.85,-0.9,3" xmin=-1 xmax=11 ymin=-1.4 ymax=1.2 titolo="The segment from $2$ to $3$ is divided into ten equal parts"}

- $2.7$
- $2.07$
- $2.3$
- $3.7$

## Explanation

Each part of the segment represents one tenth. The point is at the seventh tick after $2$, so it represents $2$ whole units and $7$ tenths, or $2.7$. The number $2.07$ would have $7$ hundredths and would lie very close to $2$, while $2.3$ results from counting the remaining ticks up to $3$ as if they started at $2$.
