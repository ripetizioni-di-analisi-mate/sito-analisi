A team has $9$ girls and $6$ boys. What is the ratio of girls to boys in simplest form?

- $2 : 3$
- $3 : 5$
- $3 : 2$
- $2 : 5$

## Explanation

The ratio is $\dfrac{9}{6}$. Dividing numerator and denominator by $3$ gives $\dfrac{3}{2}$, or $3 : 2$. The ratio $2 : 3$ is the ratio of boys to girls, while $3 : 5$ and $2 : 5$ compare each group with the total of $15$ players.
