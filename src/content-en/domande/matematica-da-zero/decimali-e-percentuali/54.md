Which of the following expressions is a proportion?

- $6 : 9 = 10 : 15$
- $6 : 9 = 10 : 14$
- $4 : 5 = 8 : 9$
- $2 : 3 = 4 : 7$

## Explanation

An expression $a : b = c : d$ is a proportion if $a \cdot d = b \cdot c$. For $6 : 9 = 10 : 15$ we have $6 \cdot 15 = 90$ and $9 \cdot 10 = 90$. In the other expressions, the product of the extremes differs from the product of the means. The pairs of products are $84$ and $90$, $36$ and $40$, and $14$ and $12$.
