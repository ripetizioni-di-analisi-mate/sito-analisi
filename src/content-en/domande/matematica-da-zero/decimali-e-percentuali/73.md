If $50\%$ of a number is $36$, what is $10\%$ of the same number?

- $3.6$
- $18$
- $7.2$
- $72$

## Explanation

Taking $50\%$ gives half the number, so the number is $2 \cdot 36 = 72$. To find $10\%$ of $72$, move the decimal point one place to the left, giving $7.2$. The value $3.6$ is $10\%$ of $36$, which is half the original number rather than the whole number.
