Which of the following fractions in lowest terms has a repeating decimal representation?

- $\dfrac{7}{20}$
- $\dfrac{5}{18}$
- $\dfrac{3}{16}$
- $\dfrac{9}{25}$

## Explanation

A fraction in lowest terms gives a repeating decimal when the denominator has a prime factor other than $2$ or $5$. Since $18 = 2 \cdot 3^2$ contains the factor $3$, the decimal form of $\dfrac{5}{18} = 0.2\overline{7}$ repeats. The other denominators are $20 = 2^2 \cdot 5$, $16 = 2^4$ and $25 = 5^2$.
