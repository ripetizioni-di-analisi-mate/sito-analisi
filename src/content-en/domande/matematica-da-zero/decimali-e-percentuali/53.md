Which number replaces the question mark in $2.5 : ? = 10 : 12$?

- $48$
- $\dfrac{25}{12}$
- $3$
- $0.3$

## Explanation

The extremes are $2.5$ and $12$, and the means are the unknown number and $10$. Equating the product of the means and the product of the extremes gives $10 \cdot ? = 2.5 \cdot 12 = 30$, so the number is $3$. The value $48$ is $\dfrac{10 \cdot 12}{2.5}$, obtained by interchanging the terms.
