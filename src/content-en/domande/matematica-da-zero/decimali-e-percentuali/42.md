A machine accepts only $0.05$-euro and $0.20$-euro coins and gives no change. Which amount can be paid exactly using at least one of each coin?

- $0.23$ euros
- $0.35$ euros
- $0.34$ euros
- $0.15$ euros

## Explanation

Possible amounts are multiples of five cents. Using both denominations requires at least $25$ cents. One $20$-cent coin and three $5$-cent coins give $35$ cents.
