A $5$-cent coin is worth $0.05$ euros. What fraction of a euro does it represent, in lowest terms?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{20}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{50}$

## Explanation

The number $0.05$ is $5$ hundredths, or $\dfrac{5}{100}$. Dividing the numerator and denominator by $5$ gives $\dfrac{1}{20}$. The fraction $\dfrac{1}{2}$ equals $0.5$, the fraction $\dfrac{1}{5}$ equals $0.2$ and the fraction $\dfrac{1}{50}$ equals $0.02$.
