The square is made up of $100$ equal small squares. What percentage of its area is shaded?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="0,0 10,0 10,4 5,4 5,5 0,5" linee="0,0 10,0 10,10 0,10 0,0" segmenti="1,0,1,10;2,0,2,10;3,0,3,10;4,0,4,10;5,0,5,10;6,0,6,10;7,0,7,10;8,0,8,10;9,0,9,10;0,1,10,1;0,2,10,2;0,3,10,3;0,4,10,4;0,5,10,5;0,6,10,6;0,7,10,7;0,8,10,8;0,9,10,9" xmin=-0.5 xmax=10.5 ymin=-0.5 ymax=10.5 titolo="A square divided into $100$ equal small squares, with part of the square shaded"}

- $40\%$
- $45\%$
- $50\%$
- $4.5\%$

## Explanation

The shaded squares fill $4$ complete rows of $10$ squares, giving $40$, plus $5$ squares in the fifth row. There are $45$ shaded squares out of $100$ in all. The percentage is therefore $\dfrac{45}{100} = 45\%$.
