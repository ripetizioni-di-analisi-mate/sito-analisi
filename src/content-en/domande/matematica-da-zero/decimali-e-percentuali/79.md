A subscription costing $48$ euros per month increases by $25\%$. What is the new monthly price?

- $12$ euros
- $60$ euros
- $36$ euros
- $73$ euros

## Explanation

The multiplier is $1 + 0.25 = 1.25$, so the new price is $48 \cdot 1.25 = 60$ euros. The value $12$ is just the increase, $36$ is the price discounted by $25\%$, and $73$ is the sum $48 + 25$.
