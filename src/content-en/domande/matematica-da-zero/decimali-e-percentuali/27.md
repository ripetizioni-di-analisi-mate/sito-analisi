A length of $38.5$ cm is rounded to the nearest whole centimeter. What value does this give?

- $38$ cm
- $40$ cm
- $39$ cm
- $39.5$ cm

## Explanation

The tenths digit is $5$, so the ones digit increases by $1$, from $38$ to $39$. The value $38$ would result from dropping the tenths without rounding up. The value $40$ is rounded to the nearest ten, and $39.5$ is not a whole number.
