When $\dfrac{7}{20}$ is written with denominator $100$, what is the numerator?

- $14$
- $140$
- $27$
- $35$

## Explanation

Since $20 \cdot 5 = 100$, multiply the numerator and denominator by $5$ to obtain $\dfrac{7}{20} = \dfrac{7 \cdot 5}{20 \cdot 5} = \dfrac{35}{100}$. The numerator is $35$ and the number is $0.35$. The value $140$ is the product $7 \cdot 20$, and $27$ is the sum $7 + 20$.
