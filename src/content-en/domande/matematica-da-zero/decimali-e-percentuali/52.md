In the proportion $5 : 8 = x : 24$, what is the value of $x$?

- $15$
- $38.4$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $19$

## Explanation

The terms $5$ and $24$ are the extremes, while $8$ and $x$ are the means. Since the product of the means equals the product of the extremes, $8 \cdot x = 5 \cdot 24$, that is, $x = \dfrac{120}{8} = 15$. The value $38.4$ is $\dfrac{8 \cdot 24}{5}$ and $\dfrac{5}{3}$ is $\dfrac{5 \cdot 8}{24}$.
