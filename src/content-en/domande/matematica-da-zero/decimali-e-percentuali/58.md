For the proportion $6 : x = 9 : 15$, a student calculates $x = \dfrac{6 \cdot 9}{15}$. What is the correct value of $x$?

- $10$
- $3.6$
- $22.5$
- $15$

## Explanation

In the proportion, $6$ and $15$ are the extremes, while $x$ and $9$ are the means. We have $x = \dfrac{6 \cdot 15}{9} = 10$. The calculation $\dfrac{6 \cdot 9}{15} = 3.6$ multiplies an extreme by a mean. The value $22.5$ is $\dfrac{9 \cdot 15}{6}$.
