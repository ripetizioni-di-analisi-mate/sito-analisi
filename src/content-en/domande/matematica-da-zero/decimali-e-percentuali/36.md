The number $0.1\overline{6}$ is the quotient of two natural numbers. Which fraction in lowest terms represents it?

- $\dfrac{16}{99}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{8}{45}$
- $\dfrac{5}{33}$

## Explanation

The repeating block is $6$ and the nonrepeating part is $1$. The numerator is $16 - 1 = 15$ and the denominator is $90$, formed by a $9$ for the repeating block and a $0$ for the nonrepeating part. Therefore $\dfrac{15}{90} = \dfrac{1}{6}$. Omitting the subtraction of the nonrepeating part gives $\dfrac{16}{90} = \dfrac{8}{45}$.
