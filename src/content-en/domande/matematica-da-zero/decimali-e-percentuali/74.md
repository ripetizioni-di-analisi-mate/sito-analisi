A bottle with a capacity of $1.5$ liters is $60\%$ full. How many liters does it contain?

- $90$ liters
- $0.9$ liters
- $2.5$ liters
- $0.09$ liters

## Explanation

The volume in liters is $60\%$ of $1.5$, or $0.6 \cdot 1.5 = 0.9$. The value $2.5$ is the quotient $\dfrac{1.5}{0.6}$ obtained by dividing instead of multiplying, while $90$ and $0.09$ come from moving the decimal point incorrectly.
