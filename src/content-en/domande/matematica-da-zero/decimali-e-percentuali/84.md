Two successive discounts of $20\%$ and $30\%$ are equivalent to what single discount?

- $50\%$
- $56\%$
- $44\%$
- $10\%$

## Explanation

The multipliers are $0.8$ and $0.7$, so the overall multiplier is $0.8 \cdot 0.7 = 0.56$. The price falls to $56\%$ of its original value, so the overall discount is $100\% - 56\% = 44\%$. The value $50\%$ is the sum of the two percentages, and $10\%$ is their difference.
