At the same time of day, a tree $6$ m tall casts a shadow $4$ m long and a pole casts a shadow $10$ m long. How tall is the pole if heights and shadow lengths are proportional?

- $\dfrac{20}{3}$ m
- $12$ m
- $15$ m
- $40$ m

## Explanation

Heights and shadow lengths are directly proportional, so $6 : 4 = x : 10$. The terms $4$ and $x$ are the means and we obtain $x = \dfrac{6 \cdot 10}{4} = 15$ m. The value $\dfrac{20}{3}$ is $\dfrac{4 \cdot 10}{6}$, obtained by inverting the ratio.
