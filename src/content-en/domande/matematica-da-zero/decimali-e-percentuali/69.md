At a school with $250$ students, $12\%$ take part in swimming. How many students swim?

- $3$
- $300$
- $238$
- $30$

## Explanation

Multiply $250$ by the fraction $\dfrac{12}{100}$ to obtain $\dfrac{12}{100} \cdot 250 = \dfrac{3000}{100} = 30$. The value $238$ is the difference $250 - 12$, while $300$ and $3$ come from moving the decimal point incorrectly.
