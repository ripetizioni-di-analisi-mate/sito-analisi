A rectangle has side lengths $3.5$ m and $2.4$ m. What is its area?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="0,0 3.5,0 3.5,2.4 0,2.4" etichette="1.2,-0.8,3.5 m;3.8,1.1,2.4 m" xmin=-1 xmax=5.5 ymin=-1.6 ymax=3.4 titolo="The rectangle with sides of length $3.5$ m and $2.4$ m"}

- $84$ m$^2$
- $8.4$ m$^2$
- $0.84$ m$^2$
- $11.8$ m$^2$

## Explanation

The area is the product of the side lengths, $3.5 \cdot 2.4$. Since $35 \cdot 24 = 840$ and the factors have two decimal places in all, the result is $8.40 = 8.4$ m$^2$. The value $11.8$ gives the perimeter $2 \cdot (3.5 + 2.4)$ rather than the area.
