If $y$ is directly proportional to $x$ and for $x = 8$ we have $y = 20$, what is the constant of proportionality $k$ in the relation $y = k \cdot x$?

- $0.4$
- $160$
- $2.5$
- $12$

## Explanation

The constant is the ratio $k = \dfrac{y}{x} = \dfrac{20}{8} = 2.5$. The value $0.4$ is the inverted ratio $\dfrac{x}{y}$, while $160$ is the product $x \cdot y$, which is constant for inversely proportional quantities. The value $12$ is the difference $20 - 8$.
