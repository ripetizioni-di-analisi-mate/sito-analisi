A train travels $270$ km in $3$ hours. What is its average speed in kilometers per hour?

- $90$
- $810$
- $267$
- $273$

## Explanation

Average speed is the ratio of distance to time, giving $\dfrac{270}{3} = 90$ kilometers per hour. The value $810$ is the product $270 \cdot 3$, the value $267$ is the difference $270 - 3$ and the value $273$ is the sum $270 + 3$.
