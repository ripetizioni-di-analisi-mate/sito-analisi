A price of $183$ euros includes value-added tax (VAT) at $22\%$. How many euros of VAT are included?

- $40.26$ euros
- $22$ euros
- $33$ euros
- $150$ euros

## Explanation

The price including VAT is the price before VAT, $V$, multiplied by $1.22$, so $1.22 \cdot V = 183$ and $V = \dfrac{183}{1.22} = 150$ euros. The VAT is $183 - 150 = 33$ euros, or $22\%$ of $150$. The value $40.26$ is $22\%$ of $183$, calculated on the price that already includes VAT.
