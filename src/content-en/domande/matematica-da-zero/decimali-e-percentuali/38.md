What is the product $0.\overline{3} \cdot 0.\overline{6}$?

- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$
- $\dfrac{2}{9}$
- $1$

## Explanation

The two repeating decimals equal $\dfrac{3}{9} = \dfrac{1}{3}$ and $\dfrac{6}{9} = \dfrac{2}{3}$. Their product is $\dfrac{1}{3} \cdot \dfrac{2}{3} = \dfrac{2}{9}$. The value $1$ is the sum of the two repeating decimals.
