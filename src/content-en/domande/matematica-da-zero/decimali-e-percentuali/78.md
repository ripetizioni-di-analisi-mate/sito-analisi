For a discount of $35\%$, what factor multiplies the price?

- $0.65$
- $0.35$
- $1.35$
- $0.065$

## Explanation

With a discount of $p\%$, the value is multiplied by $1 - \dfrac{p}{100}$. For $p = 35$ this gives $1 - 0.35 = 0.65$. The factor $1.35$ corresponds to an increase of $35\%$, while $0.35$ is the fraction taken off the price rather than the multiplier.
