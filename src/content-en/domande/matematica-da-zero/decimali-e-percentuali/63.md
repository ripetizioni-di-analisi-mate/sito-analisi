A car completes a trip in $3$ hours at $60$ kilometers per hour. How many hours does the same trip take at $90$ kilometers per hour?

- $4.5$ hours
- $2$ hours
- $1.5$ hours
- $2.5$ hours

## Explanation

At a higher speed the trip takes less time, so speed and time are inversely proportional. Their product is $60 \cdot 3 = 180$ km, the length of the trip. At $90$ kilometers per hour the trip takes $\dfrac{180}{90} = 2$ hours. The value $4.5$ is $\dfrac{90 \cdot 3}{60}$, obtained by assuming direct proportionality.
