A price of $80$ euros increases by $50\%$ and then decreases by $50\%$. What is the final price?

- $80$ euros
- $60$ euros
- $120$ euros
- $40$ euros

## Explanation

The multipliers are $1.5$ and $0.5$. The final price is $80 \cdot 1.5 \cdot 0.5 = 120 \cdot 0.5 = 60$ euros. The second $50\%$ is calculated on $120$ euros and amounts to $60$ euros, so the two changes do not cancel each other out. The value $120$ is the price after the increase alone.
