What is $5.347$ rounded to the nearest hundredth?

- $5.34$
- $5.35$
- $5.3$
- $5.4$

## Explanation

The hundredths digit is $4$ and the thousandths digit is $7$. Since $7$ is greater than $5$, the digit $4$ increases by $1$, giving $5.35$. Simply dropping the remaining digits would give $5.34$, while $5.3$ and $5.4$ are rounded to tenths.
