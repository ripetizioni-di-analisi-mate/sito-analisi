A price is discounted by $20\%$. What percentage increase in the discounted price will restore the original price?

- $20\%$
- $10\%$
- $30\%$
- $25\%$

## Explanation

Let $V$ be the original price. After the discount the price is $0.8V$. We seek $a$ such that $0.8V \cdot (1 + a) = V$, that is, $1 + a = \dfrac{1}{0.8} = 1.25$. The required increase is therefore $25\%$. An increase of $20\%$ would give $0.8V \cdot 1.2 = 0.96V$.
