A class has $25$ students, and $7$ of them are absent today. What percentage of the students are present?

- $72\%$
- $28\%$
- $18\%$
- $7\%$

## Explanation

The number present is $25 - 7 = 18$. The percentage is $\dfrac{18}{25} \cdot 100 = 72$, or $72\%$. The value $28\%$ is the percentage of students absent, $\dfrac{7}{25} \cdot 100$, and $18\%$ is the number present written as a percentage.
