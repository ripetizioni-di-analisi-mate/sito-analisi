What is the ratio of a length of $45$ cm to a length of $3$ m?

- $15$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{20}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{20}$

## Explanation

Express the two lengths in the same unit. A length of $3$ m is $300$ cm. The ratio is $\dfrac{45}{300} = \dfrac{3}{20}$. The value $15$ is the quotient $45 \div 3$ obtained without converting the units.
