Two mountain huts are $12$ km apart in a straight line. How many centimeters apart are they on a map with scale $1 : 200\,000$?

- $6$ cm
- $60$ cm
- $0.6$ cm
- $24$ cm

## Explanation

Twelve kilometers is $1\,200\,000$ cm. At a scale of $1 : 200\,000$, divide each actual length by $200\,000$, giving $\dfrac{1\,200\,000}{200\,000} = 6$ cm. The values $60$ and $0.6$ result from moving the decimal point incorrectly when converting kilometers to centimeters.
