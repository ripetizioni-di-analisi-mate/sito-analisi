How many thousandths are in $0.45$?

- $45$
- $4$
- $4500$
- $450$

## Explanation

The number $0.45$ can also be written as $0.450$, or $\dfrac{450}{1000}$. It therefore contains $450$ thousandths. The value $45$ is the number of hundredths because $0.45 = \dfrac{45}{100}$, and $4$ is the number of tenths.
