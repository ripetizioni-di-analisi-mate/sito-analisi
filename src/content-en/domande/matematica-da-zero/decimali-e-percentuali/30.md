What is the decimal form of the fraction $\dfrac{3}{8}$?

- $0.38$
- $2.67$
- $0.83$
- $0.375$

## Explanation

The fraction is the quotient of its numerator and denominator, so calculate $3 \div 8$. The division ends after three digits and gives $0.375$ because $8 \cdot 0.375 = 3$. The value $2.67$ is the rounded quotient $8 \div 3$ obtained by dividing with the terms reversed.
