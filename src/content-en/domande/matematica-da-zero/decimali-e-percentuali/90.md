A company has $200$ employees. Of these, $40\%$ are women, and $25\%$ of the women work part time. How many women work part time?

- $50$
- $80$
- $20$
- $65$

## Explanation

Women make up $40\%$ of $200$ employees, or $0.4 \cdot 200 = 80$. The number of women who work part time is $25\%$ of $80$, or $0.25 \cdot 80 = 20$. The value $50$ is $25\%$ of $200$, the value $80$ is the total number of women and the value $65$ is the sum $40 + 25$.
