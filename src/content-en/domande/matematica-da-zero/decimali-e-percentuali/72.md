In a group of $10$ people, $50\%$ arrive by bicycle. In another group of $30$ people, $10\%$ do so. What percentage of all $40$ people arrive by bicycle?

- $30\%$
- $40\%$
- $60\%$
- $20\%$

## Explanation

There are $5$ cyclists in the first group and $3$ in the second. That is $8$ people out of $40$, or $20\%$. The different group sizes determine the weight of each percentage.
