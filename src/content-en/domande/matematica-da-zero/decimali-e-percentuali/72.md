In a group of $10$ people, $50\%$ cycle to work. In a second group of $30$ people, the percentage is $10\%$. What percentage of the $40$ people in the two groups cycle to work?

- $30\%$
- $40\%$
- $60\%$
- $20\%$

## Explanation

In the first group the cyclists are $50\%$ of $10$, that is $5$, and in the second they are $10\%$ of $30$, that is $3$. So there are $5 + 3 = 8$ cyclists out of $40$ people in all. Since $\dfrac{8}{40} = \dfrac{20}{100}$, the overall percentage is $20\%$. The value $30\%$ is the average of the two percentages, which would give the right result only for two groups of equal size, while $60\%$ is their sum.
