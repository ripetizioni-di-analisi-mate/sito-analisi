Which of $\dfrac{3}{5}$, $55\%$, $0.58$ and $\dfrac{4}{7}$ is greatest?

- $55\%$
- $0.58$
- $\dfrac{4}{7}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$

## Explanation

Write them all in decimal form, giving $\dfrac{3}{5} = 0.6$, $55\% = 0.55$, $0.58$ and $\dfrac{4}{7} \approx 0.571$. Comparing tenths and then hundredths, the greatest is $0.6$, or $\dfrac{3}{5}$.
