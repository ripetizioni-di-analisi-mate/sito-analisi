Marco pays with a $20$-euro bill for a purchase costing $13.85$ euros. How much change does he receive?

- $7.15$ euros
- $6.85$ euros
- $33.85$ euros
- $6.15$ euros

## Explanation

The change is the difference $20 - 13.85$. Writing $20 = 20.00$ and aligning the decimal points gives $20.00 - 13.85 = 6.15$ euros. The value $33.85$ is the sum of the two numbers, while $6.85$ and $7.15$ result from an error when borrowing between the hundredths and tenths places.
