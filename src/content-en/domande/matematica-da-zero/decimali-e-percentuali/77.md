A number equals $150\%$ of $40$. By how much does it exceed $40$?

- $60$
- $20$
- $110$
- $10$

## Explanation

Taking $150\%$ of $40$ gives $1.5 \cdot 40 = 60$. The number exceeds $40$ by $60 - 40 = 20$. The value $60$ is the number itself rather than the excess, while $110$ is the difference between $150$ and $40$ calculated without first converting the percentage to the factor $1.5$.
