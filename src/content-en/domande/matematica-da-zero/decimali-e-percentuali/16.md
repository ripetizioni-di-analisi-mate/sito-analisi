There are $6$ liters of juice and each glass holds $0.25$ liters. How many glasses can be filled?

- $1.5$
- $24$
- $6.25$
- $2.4$

## Explanation

The number of glasses is the quotient $6 \div 0.25$. Multiplying both terms by $100$ gives $\dfrac{600}{25} = 24$. The same result follows from $0.25 = \dfrac{1}{4}$ because each liter fills $4$ glasses and $6 \cdot 4 = 24$. The value $1.5$ is the product $6 \cdot 0.25$.
