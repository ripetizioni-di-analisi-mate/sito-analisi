Given that $25 \cdot 34 = 850$, what is the value of $2.5 \cdot 0.34$?

- $8.5$
- $0.085$
- $0.85$
- $85$

## Explanation

The numbers $2.5$ and $0.34$ have the same digits as $25$ and $34$, so the product without the decimal point is $850$. The factors have $1 + 2 = 3$ decimal places in all. Therefore the product is $0.850 = 0.85$.
