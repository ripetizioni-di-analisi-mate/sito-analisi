In the number $0.41\overline{6}$, what are the repeating block and the nonrepeating part?

- Repeating block $16$ and nonrepeating part $4$
- Repeating block $6$ and nonrepeating part $41$
- Repeating block $416$ and no nonrepeating part
- Repeating block $41$ and nonrepeating part $6$

## Explanation

The number is $0.41666\ldots$ and the digit $6$ repeats indefinitely, so the repeating block is $6$. The digits between the decimal point and the block are $4$ and $1$, so the nonrepeating part is $41$. The number is a mixed repeating decimal.
