A classroom $8$ m long is represented by a segment $4$ cm long. What is the scale of the drawing?

- $1 : 2$
- $1 : 20$
- $1 : 2000$
- $1 : 200$

## Explanation

The scale is the ratio of the length on the drawing to the actual length, expressed in the same unit. Since $8$ m $= 800$ cm, we have $\dfrac{4}{800} = \dfrac{1}{200}$, which gives a scale of $1 : 200$. The scale $1 : 2$ would come from comparing $4$ with $8$ without converting the units.
