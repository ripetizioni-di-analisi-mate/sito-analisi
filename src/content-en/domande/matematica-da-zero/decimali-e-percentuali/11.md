What is the product $0.6 \cdot 0.07$?

- $0.42$
- $0.0042$
- $4.2$
- $0.042$

## Explanation

Multiplying without the decimal points gives $6 \cdot 7 = 42$. The factors have $1 + 2 = 3$ decimal places in all, so the result is $0.042$. Using two decimal places would give $0.42$, and using four would give $0.0042$.
