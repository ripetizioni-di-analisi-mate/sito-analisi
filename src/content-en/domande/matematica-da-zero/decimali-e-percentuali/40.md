Which is the greatest of $\dfrac{7}{20}$, $0.4$, $\dfrac{3}{8}$ and $0.\overline{3}$?

- $\dfrac{7}{20}$
- $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $0.\overline{3}$
- $0.4$

## Explanation

Write them all in decimal form, giving $\dfrac{7}{20} = 0.35$, $\dfrac{3}{8} = 0.375$ and $0.\overline{3} = 0.333\ldots$. Comparing tenths, the number $0.4$ has $4$ tenths and the others have $3$. Therefore the greatest is $0.4$.
