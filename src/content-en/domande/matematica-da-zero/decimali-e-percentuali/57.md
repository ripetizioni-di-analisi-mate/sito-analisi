A dough recipe calls for flour and water in the ratio $5 : 2$. How many grams of water are needed for $300$ g of flour?

- $750$ g
- $298$ g
- $600$ g
- $120$ g

## Explanation

Set up the proportion $5 : 2 = 300 : x$. The term $x$ is a mean, so $x = \dfrac{2 \cdot 300}{5} = 120$ g. The value $750$ is $\dfrac{5 \cdot 300}{2}$, obtained by inverting the ratio, while $600$ is the product $2 \cdot 300$ without dividing by $5$.
