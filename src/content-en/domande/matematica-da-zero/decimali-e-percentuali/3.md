Which sum of fractions equals $6.204$?

- $6 + \dfrac{2}{10} + \dfrac{4}{100}$
- $6 + \dfrac{2}{100} + \dfrac{4}{1000}$
- $6 + \dfrac{2}{10} + \dfrac{4}{1000}$
- $6 + \dfrac{2}{10} + \dfrac{4}{10}$

## Explanation

The digits after the decimal point in $6.204$ are $2$, $0$ and $4$, representing $2$ tenths, $0$ hundredths and $4$ thousandths. Therefore $6.204 = 6 + \dfrac{2}{10} + \dfrac{4}{1000}$. The hundredths term is zero and is omitted, while $4$ occupies the thousandths place.
