If $12$ is $40\%$ of a number $N$, what is $N$?

- $30$
- $4.8$
- $480$
- $52$

## Explanation

The part $12$ is to the total $N$ as $40$ is to $100$, that is, $12 : N = 40 : 100$. Therefore $N = \dfrac{12 \cdot 100}{40} = 30$. The value $4.8$ is $40\%$ of $12$, the value $480$ is the product $12 \cdot 40$ and the value $52$ is the sum $12 + 40$.
