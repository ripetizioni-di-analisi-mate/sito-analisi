The ratio of two numbers is $\dfrac{5}{4}$ and the second number is $24$. What is the first?

- $19.2$
- $120$
- $30$
- $20$

## Explanation

If the first number is $a$, we have $a : 24 = 5 : 4$, so $a$ is an extreme and $a = \dfrac{24 \cdot 5}{4} = 30$. The value $19.2$ comes from inverting the ratio, giving $\dfrac{24 \cdot 4}{5}$. The value $120$ comes from multiplying by $5$ without dividing by $4$, and the value $20$ comes from subtracting $4$ from $24$.
