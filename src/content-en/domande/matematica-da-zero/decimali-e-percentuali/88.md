A receipt shows a price of $51$ euros after a discount of $15\%$. How many euros were taken off the price?

- $7.65$ euros
- $15$ euros
- $9$ euros
- $60$ euros

## Explanation

The original price $V$ satisfies $0.85 \cdot V = 51$, so it was $V = \dfrac{51}{0.85} = 60$ euros. The discount is $60 - 51 = 9$ euros, which is also $15\%$ of $60$. The value $7.65$ is $15\%$ of $51$, calculated on the already discounted price.
