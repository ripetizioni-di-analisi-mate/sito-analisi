Round $2.46$ and $3.38$ to the nearest tenth and then add the rounded numbers. What is their sum?

- $5.8$
- $5.9$
- $5.84$
- $5.7$

## Explanation

We have $2.46 \approx 2.5$ and $3.38 \approx 3.4$, so the sum is $2.5 + 3.4 = 5.9$. The sum $5.84$ uses the unrounded numbers, and $5.8$ is that sum rounded to the nearest tenth. The value $5.7$ comes from dropping the hundredths digits, giving $2.4 + 3.3$.
