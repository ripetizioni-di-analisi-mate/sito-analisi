A number has $12$ ones, $3$ tenths, no hundredths and $5$ thousandths. What is the number?

- $12.35$
- $12.3005$
- $12.305$
- $12.053$

## Explanation

The ones go before the decimal point. After the point come the tenths, hundredths and thousandths digits, in that order, namely $3$, $0$ and $5$. Thus the number is $12.305$. The number $12.35$ has $5$ hundredths instead of $5$ thousandths because the zero in the hundredths place was omitted.
