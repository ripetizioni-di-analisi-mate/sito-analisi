A salary increases first by $5\%$ and then by $10\%$. What is the overall percentage increase?

- $15\%$
- $50\%$
- $5.5\%$
- $15.5\%$

## Explanation

The multipliers are $1.05$ and $1.1$, so the overall multiplier is $1.05 \cdot 1.1 = 1.155$. The overall increase is $15.5\%$. The sum $5\% + 10\% = 15\%$ does not account for the fact that the second increase is calculated on the already increased salary.
