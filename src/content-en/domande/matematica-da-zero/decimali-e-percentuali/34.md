A display shows a number of the form $2.1\square7$. Rounded to the nearest tenth, it gives $2.2$. How many different digits can go in the box?

- $4$
- $6$
- $1$
- $5$

## Explanation

The hidden digit is in the hundredths place. To raise the tenths digit from $1$ to $2$, it must be at least $5$. The possibilities are $5$, $6$, $7$, $8$ and $9$.
