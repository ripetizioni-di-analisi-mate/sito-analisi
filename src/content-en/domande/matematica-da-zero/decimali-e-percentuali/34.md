How is $2.125$ written as a fraction in lowest terms?

- $\dfrac{85}{4}$
- $\dfrac{21}{8}$
- $\dfrac{17}{80}$
- $\dfrac{17}{8}$

## Explanation

The number has three decimal places, so $2.125 = \dfrac{2125}{1000}$. Dividing numerator and denominator by $125$ gives $\dfrac{17}{8}$. The fraction $\dfrac{85}{4}$ equals $21.25$, while $\dfrac{17}{80}$ equals $0.2125$. The fraction $\dfrac{17}{8}$ is in lowest terms because $17$ is a prime number that does not divide $8$.
