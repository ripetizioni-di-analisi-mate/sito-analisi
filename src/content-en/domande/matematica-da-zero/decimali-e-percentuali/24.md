The number $2.098$ rounded to the nearest hundredth is

- $2.09$
- $2.00$
- $2.01$
- $2.10$

## Explanation

The thousandths digit is $8$, so the hundredths digit, $9$, increases by $1$ to give $10$ hundredths. Carry one to the tenths column, whose digit changes from $0$ to $1$. The result is $2.10$. The final zero shows that the number was rounded to the nearest hundredth.
