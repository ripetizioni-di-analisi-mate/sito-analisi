A jacket discounted by $40\%$ costs $60$ euros. What was the original price?

- $84$ euros
- $100$ euros
- $150$ euros
- $20$ euros

## Explanation

The discounted price is the original price $V$ multiplied by $0.6$, so $0.6 \cdot V = 60$. Dividing by the multiplier gives $V = \dfrac{60}{0.6} = 100$ euros. The value $84$ results from increasing $60$ by $40\%$, which does not reverse the discount. The value $150$ comes from dividing by $0.4$, and $20$ comes from subtracting $40$ from $60$.
