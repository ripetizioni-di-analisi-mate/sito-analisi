A drink contains $120$ ml of syrup and $480$ ml of water. What fraction of the total volume is syrup?

- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{4}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$

## Explanation

The total volume is $120 + 480 = 600$ ml. The fraction that is syrup is $\dfrac{120}{600} = \dfrac{1}{5}$. The fraction $\dfrac{1}{4}$ is the ratio of syrup to water, $\dfrac{120}{480}$, and $\dfrac{4}{5}$ is the fraction of the total that is water.
