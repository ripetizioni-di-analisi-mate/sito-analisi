What is the value of $\dfrac{1}{4} + 0.35$?

- $1.75$
- $0.85$
- $0.0875$
- $0.6$

## Explanation

Write $\dfrac{1}{4}$ as a decimal, giving $1 \div 4 = 0.25$. Then $0.25 + 0.35 = 0.60 = 0.6$. The value $0.85$ comes from treating $\dfrac{1}{4}$ as $0.5$, and $1.75$ from treating $\dfrac{1}{4}$ as $1.4$. The product $\dfrac{1}{4} \cdot 0.35$ equals $0.0875$.
