An item costing $200$ euros is discounted first by $50\%$ and then by $20\%$. What is its final price?

- $80$ euros
- $60$ euros
- $100$ euros
- $160$ euros

## Explanation

After the first discount, the price is $200 \cdot 0.5 = 100$ euros. The second discount applies to $100$ euros, giving $100 \cdot 0.8 = 80$ euros. The value $60$ would result from adding the discounts into a single discount of $70\%$, while $160$ is the price after only the $20\%$ discount.
