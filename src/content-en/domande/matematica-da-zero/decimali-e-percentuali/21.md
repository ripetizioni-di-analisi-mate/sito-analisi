Three packages weigh $1.25$ kg, $0.8$ kg and $2.075$ kg. What is their total weight?

- $4.125$ kg
- $3.405$ kg
- $41.25$ kg
- $5.125$ kg

## Explanation

Write the weights with three decimal places, $1.250$, $0.800$ and $2.075$, and align the decimal points. The sum is $4.125$ kg. The result $3.405$ comes from reading $0.8$ as $0.08$.
