A taxi charges $4$ euros at the start of a trip and $2$ euros per kilometer. A ride of $3$ km costs $10$ euros. How much does a ride of $6$ km cost?

- $20$ euros
- $16$ euros
- $12$ euros
- $8$ euros

## Explanation

The fare is $4 + 2 \cdot 6 = 16$ euros. The fare and the distance are not directly proportional because the distance doubles from $3$ to $6$ while the fare goes from $10$ to $16$ euros without doubling. The value $20$ would result from assuming direct proportionality.
