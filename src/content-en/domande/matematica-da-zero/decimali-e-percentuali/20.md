Three friends split a bill of $47.40$ euros equally. How much does each pay?

- $1.58$ euros
- $15.80$ euros
- $23.70$ euros
- $142.20$ euros

## Explanation

Each share is the quotient $47.40 \div 3$. We have $47.40 \div 3 = 15.80$ because $3 \cdot 15.80 = 47.40$. The value $23.70$ comes from dividing by $2$ instead of $3$, and $142.20$ comes from multiplying by $3$.
