Which of the following numbers rounds to $3.4$ to the nearest tenth?

- $3.38$
- $3.46$
- $3.29$
- $3.51$

## Explanation

For $3.38$, the hundredths digit is $8$, so the tenths digit increases from $3$ to $4$, giving $3.4$. The number $3.46$ rounds to $3.5$ because its hundredths digit is $6$. The number $3.29$ rounds to $3.3$, and $3.51$ rounds to $3.5$.
