Which fraction in lowest terms equals $0.36$?

- $\dfrac{18}{5}$
- $\dfrac{9}{250}$
- $\dfrac{9}{25}$
- $\dfrac{9}{50}$

## Explanation

The number $0.36$ has two decimal places, so it equals $\dfrac{36}{100}$. Dividing numerator and denominator by $4$ gives $\dfrac{9}{25}$, which is in lowest terms because $9$ and $25$ have no common factor other than one. The fraction $\dfrac{18}{5}$ equals $3.6$, and $\dfrac{9}{50}$ equals $0.18$.
