Interchange the two means in the proportion $9 : 12 = 6 : 8$. Which expression is still a proportion?

- $9 : 6 = 8 : 12$
- $9 : 6 = 12 : 8$
- $12 : 9 = 6 : 8$
- $9 : 8 = 12 : 6$

## Explanation

Apply the cross-product property. In $9 : 6 = 12 : 8$ the product of the extremes is $9 \cdot 8 = 72$ and the product of the means is $6 \cdot 12 = 72$. In the other expressions, the products differ. The pairs of products are $108$ and $48$, $96$ and $54$, and $54$ and $96$.
