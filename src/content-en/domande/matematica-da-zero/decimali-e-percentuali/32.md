The denominator of the fraction $\dfrac{6}{15}$ is divisible by $3$. Which statement about its decimal form is true?

- It is a pure repeating decimal equal to $0.\overline{4}$
- It is a mixed repeating decimal equal to $0.4\overline{6}$
- It is a terminating decimal equal to $0.4$
- It is a terminating decimal equal to $2.5$

## Explanation

The criterion applies to the fraction in lowest terms. Since $\dfrac{6}{15} = \dfrac{2}{5}$ and $5$ has no prime factors other than $2$ and $5$, the decimal terminates. We have $\dfrac{2}{5} = \dfrac{4}{10} = 0.4$. The value $2.5$ is the quotient $\dfrac{15}{6}$.
