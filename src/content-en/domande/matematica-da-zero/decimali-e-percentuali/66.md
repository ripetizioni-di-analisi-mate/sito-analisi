Two inversely proportional quantities have values $x = 8$ and $y = 15$. What is $y$ when $x = 20$?

- $6$
- $37.5$
- $27$
- $12$

## Explanation

The product $x \cdot y$ is constant and equals $8 \cdot 15 = 120$. For $x = 20$ we have $y = \dfrac{120}{20} = 6$. The value $37.5$ would result from direct proportionality, giving $\dfrac{15 \cdot 20}{8}$, and the value $12$ is the difference $20 - 8$.
