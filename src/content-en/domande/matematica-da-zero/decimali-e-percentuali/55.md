In the proportion $8 : 12 = 14 : 21$, what is the sum of the two extremes?

- $26$
- $29$
- $168$
- $55$

## Explanation

The extremes are the first and last terms, $8$ and $21$, and their sum is $29$. The sum of the means $12$ and $14$ is $26$, while $55$ is the sum of all four terms. The value $168$ is the product of the extremes.
