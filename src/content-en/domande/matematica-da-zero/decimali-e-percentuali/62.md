Three kilograms of apples cost $7.20$ euros. How much does $5$ kg cost at the same price per kilogram?

- $12$ euros
- $4.32$ euros
- $9.20$ euros
- $21.60$ euros

## Explanation

Cost and weight are directly proportional. One kilogram costs $\dfrac{7.20}{3} = 2.40$ euros, so $5$ kg costs $5 \cdot 2.40 = 12$ euros. The value $4.32$ is $\dfrac{7.20 \cdot 3}{5}$, obtained by setting up the proportion in reverse, and $21.60$ is the product $7.20 \cdot 3$.
