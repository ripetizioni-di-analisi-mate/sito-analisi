A chocolate bar weighs $35$ g. How many kilograms is that?

- $0.35$ kg
- $3.5$ kg
- $0.035$ kg
- $0.0035$ kg

## Explanation

A kilogram equals $1000$ g, so divide by $1000$. The decimal point moves three places to the left, giving $35 \div 1000 = 0.035$ kg. Moving it one or two places would give $3.5$ kg and $0.35$ kg, corresponding to $3500$ g and $350$ g.
