A gas pump displays a price of $1.985$ euros per liter. What is the price rounded to the nearest hundredth?

- $1.98$ euros
- $1.99$ euros
- $2.00$ euros
- $1.9$ euros

## Explanation

The hundredths digit is $8$, and the thousandths digit is $5$. With $5$, the preceding digit increases by $1$, so $8$ becomes $9$ and the price is $1.99$ euros. The value $2.00$ equals $2.0$, the price rounded to the nearest tenth, while $1.98$ comes from dropping the last digit without rounding up.
