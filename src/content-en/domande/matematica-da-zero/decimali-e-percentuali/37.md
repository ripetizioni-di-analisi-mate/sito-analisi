The decimal $2.\overline{3}$ equals exactly one of the following fractions. Which one?

- $\dfrac{23}{9}$
- $\dfrac{23}{10}$
- $\dfrac{7}{3}$
- $\dfrac{21}{10}$

## Explanation

The repeating block is $3$ and there is no nonrepeating part. The numerator is $23$ minus the part that precedes the block, namely $2$, and the denominator is $9$, giving $\dfrac{23 - 2}{9} = \dfrac{21}{9} = \dfrac{7}{3}$. Omitting the subtraction of $2$ would give $\dfrac{23}{9}$, which is approximately $2.56$.
