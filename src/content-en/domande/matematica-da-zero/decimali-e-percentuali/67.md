The points $(1\,;\,5)$, $(2\,;\,7)$ and $(3\,;\,9)$ lie on the line shown. Which statement is true?

::grafico{f="2*x+3" punti="1,5,pieno;2,7,pieno;3,9,pieno" xmin=-1 xmax=5 ymin=-1 ymax=12 nomi="x,y" legenda=0 titolo="The three points lie on a line that crosses the $y$-axis at $3$"}

- $x$ and $y$ are directly proportional because the points lie on a line
- $x$ and $y$ are inversely proportional because $y$ increases as $x$ increases
- $x$ and $y$ are directly proportional because $y$ is always greater than $x$
- $x$ and $y$ are not directly proportional because the line does not pass through the origin

## Explanation

Two directly proportional quantities have a constant ratio $\dfrac{y}{x}$, and their graph is a line through the origin. Here the ratios are $\dfrac{5}{1}$, $\dfrac{7}{2}$ and $\dfrac{9}{3}$, or $5$, $3.5$ and $3$, and the line crosses the $y$-axis at $3$. In inverse proportionality, $y$ decreases when $x$ increases.
