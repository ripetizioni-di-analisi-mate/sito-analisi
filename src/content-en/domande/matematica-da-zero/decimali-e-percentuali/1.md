Which of the following equalities is true?

- $3.70 = 3.7$
- $3.07 = 3.7$
- $0.5 = 0.05$
- $1.4 = 14$

## Explanation

Adding zeros at the end of the fractional part does not change the number, so $3.70 = 3.7$ because $70$ hundredths is $7$ tenths. In the other equalities, a digit changes place. In $3.07$ the digit $7$ counts hundredths and in $3.7$ it counts tenths, while $0.5$ is $5$ tenths and $0.05$ is $5$ hundredths.
