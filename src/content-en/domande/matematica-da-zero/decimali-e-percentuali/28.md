The result of $10 \div 3$ rounded to the nearest hundredth is

- $3.34$
- $3.3$
- $3.4$
- $3.33$

## Explanation

The division gives $10 \div 3 = 3.333\ldots$, with the digit $3$ repeating. The thousandths digit is $3$, so the hundredths digit does not increase and the result is $3.33$. The value $3.34$ would result if the next digit were greater than or equal to $5$.
