What number do you obtain by evaluating $4 + 6 \cdot 5$ using the order of operations?

- $30$
- $120$
- $34$
- $50$

## Explanation

First multiply, $6 \cdot 5 = 30$, and then add, $4 + 30 = 34$. The value $50$ comes from adding $4$ and $6$ first, whereas $120$ is the product of all three numbers.
