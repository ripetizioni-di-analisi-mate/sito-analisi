A bakery makes $230$ cookies and puts them in bags of $12$. How many cookies are left over once as many bags as possible have been filled?

- $2$
- $12$
- $19$
- $11$

## Explanation

Calculate $230 : 12$. Since $12 \cdot 19 = 228$ and $12 \cdot 20 = 240$, the quotient is $19$ and the remainder is $230 - 228 = 2$. The value $19$ is the number of full bags, whereas $12$ cannot be the remainder because it equals the divisor.
