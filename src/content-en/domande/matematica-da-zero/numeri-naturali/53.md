Which expression equals $6 \cdot 47$?

- $6 \cdot 40 + 7$
- $6 \cdot 40 + 6 \cdot 7$
- $40 + 6 \cdot 7$
- $6 \cdot 4 + 6 \cdot 7$

## Explanation

Write $47 = 40 + 7$ and apply the distributive property: $6 \cdot 47 = 6 \cdot 40 + 6 \cdot 7 = 240 + 42 = 282$. In the other expressions the factor $6$ multiplies only one of the addends, or it multiplies $4$ instead of $40$.
