A cube has edges of length $5$ cm. How many small cubes with edges of length $1$ cm are needed to fill it?

- $25$
- $625$
- $15$
- $125$

## Explanation

The cube consists of $5$ layers, each containing $5 \cdot 5 = 25$ small cubes. The total number is $5 \cdot 25 = 125$, that is, $5^3$. The value $15$ is the product $5 \cdot 3$, whereas $25$ is the number of small cubes in one layer.
