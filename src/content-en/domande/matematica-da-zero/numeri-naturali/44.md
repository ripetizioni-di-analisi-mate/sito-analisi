What is the product of the four factors $13$, $27$, $0$, and $8$?

- $8$
- $2\,808$
- $48$
- $0$

## Explanation

A product with a factor equal to $0$ is $0$, so no other calculations are needed. The value $2\,808$ is the product $13 \cdot 27 \cdot 8$, obtained by ignoring the zero factor, whereas $48$ is the sum of the factors.
