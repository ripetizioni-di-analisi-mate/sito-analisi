Which number can be written as $5 \cdot 1\,000 + 3 \cdot 10 + 8$?

- $538$
- $5\,380$
- $5\,038$
- $53\,008$

## Explanation

The term $5 \cdot 1\,000$ gives $5$ thousands, the term $3 \cdot 10$ gives $3$ tens, and $8$ is the number of ones. There are no hundreds, so $0$ goes in that place. The number is $5\,000 + 30 + 8 = 5\,038$.
