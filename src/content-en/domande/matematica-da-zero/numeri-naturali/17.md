How many digits does the successor of $99\,999$ have?

- $10$
- $5$
- $6$
- $7$

## Explanation

When we add $1$ to $99\,999$, the ones form a ten, the tens form a hundred, and so on until we reach $100\,000$. This number has the digit $1$ followed by five zeros, so it has $6$ digits. The number $99\,999$, in contrast, has $5$ digits, all nines.
