The power $2^5$ represents a product of equal factors. What number does it give?

- $16$
- $32$
- $10$
- $25$

## Explanation

The exponent $5$ indicates five factors equal to the base, so $2^5 = 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 = 32$. The value $10$ is the product $2 \cdot 5$, which treats the exponent as a factor, whereas $25$ is the power $5^2$ and $16$ is the power $2^4$.
