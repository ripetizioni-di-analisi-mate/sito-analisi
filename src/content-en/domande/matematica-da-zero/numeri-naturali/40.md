A natural number is divided by $8$. Which of these values cannot be the remainder?

- $7$
- $8$
- $0$
- $3$

## Explanation

The remainder after division by $8$ is less than the divisor, so it can only be $0$, $1$, $2$, $3$, $4$, $5$, $6$, or $7$. The value $8$ equals the divisor. In that case, $8$ would fit into the dividend one more time.
