A book has $312$ pages and Anna has read $178$ of them. How many pages does she have left to read?

- $490$
- $134$
- $266$
- $234$

## Explanation

The number of pages left is given by the subtraction $312 - 178$. In the ones place, regroup one ten and calculate $12 - 8 = 4$. In the tens place, $0$ tens remain, so regroup one hundred and calculate $10 - 7 = 3$. In the hundreds place, $2 - 1 = 1$ remains. The result is $134$, whereas $490$ is the sum of the two numbers.
