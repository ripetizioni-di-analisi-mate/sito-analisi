A theater has $14$ rows with $22$ seats in each row. How many seats does it have in all?

- $88$
- $36$
- $308$
- $3\,080$

## Explanation

The total is the product $14 \cdot 22$. Write $14 = 10 + 4$, so $22 \cdot 10 = 220$ and $22 \cdot 4 = 88$, and their sum is $220 + 88 = 308$. The value $36$ comes from adding $14$ and $22$, whereas $88$ counts just four rows.
