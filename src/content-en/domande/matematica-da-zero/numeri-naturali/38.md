Dividing $100$ by $7$ gives a quotient of $14$. What is the remainder?

- $7$
- $2$
- $0$
- $98$

## Explanation

We have $100 = 7 \cdot 14 + r$, with $r$ less than $7$. Since $7 \cdot 14 = 98$, the remainder is $100 - 98 = 2$. The value $98$ is the product of the divisor and quotient, whereas the remainder would be $0$ only if the division were exact.
