Evaluating the expression $\{5 + [12 - (4 + 3)] \cdot 2\} : 3$ with parentheses, square brackets, and braces gives

- $20$
- $5$
- $15$
- $9$

## Explanation

Evaluate the expressions inside the parentheses, square brackets, and braces in that order. The parentheses give $4 + 3 = 7$ and the square brackets give $12 - 7 = 5$. The braces give $5 + 5 \cdot 2 = 5 + 10 = 15$, and finally $15 : 3 = 5$. The value $15$ leaves out the final division.
