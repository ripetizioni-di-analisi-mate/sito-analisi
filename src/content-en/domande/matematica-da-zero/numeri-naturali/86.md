A class of $24$ students forms teams of $4$ students each. Then $3$ teams withdraw. Which expression gives the number of students still competing, and what is its value?

- $24 : 4 - 3 = 3$
- $24 : (4 - 3) \cdot 4 = 96$
- $24 : 4 \cdot 3 = 18$
- $(24 : 4 - 3) \cdot 4 = 12$

## Explanation

There are $24 : 4 = 6$ teams, and after $3$ teams withdraw, $6 - 3 = 3$ teams remain. Each team has $4$ students, so there are $3 \cdot 4 = 12$ students still competing. Parentheses are needed because the subtraction $6 - 3$ is performed before multiplication by $4$.
