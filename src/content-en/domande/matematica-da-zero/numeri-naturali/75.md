Admission to an amusement park costs $15$ euros and each ride costs $3$ euros. Which calculation gives the cost of admission and $4$ rides, and what is its value?

- $15 \cdot 3 + 4 = 49$
- $15 + 3 \cdot 4 = 27$
- $15 + 3 + 4 = 22$
- $(15 + 3) \cdot 4 = 72$

## Explanation

Admission is paid only once, and the rides cost $3 \cdot 4 = 12$ euros, so the total cost is $15 + 12 = 27$ euros. In the expression $15 + 3 \cdot 4$, multiplication is performed before addition, as required for this calculation. The expression $(15 + 3) \cdot 4$ would charge admission four times.
