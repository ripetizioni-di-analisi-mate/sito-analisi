Which of the following statements about natural numbers is true?

- The successor of a natural number is always a natural number
- Zero is not a natural number
- Every natural number has a natural number as its predecessor
- There is a natural number greater than all the others

## Explanation

Adding $1$ to a natural number gives another natural number, so each number has a successor. For the same reason, no number is greater than all the others. Zero is a natural number and has no natural number as its predecessor.
