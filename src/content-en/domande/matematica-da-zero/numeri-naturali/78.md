Performing all the operations in $5 \cdot 2^3 - 4^2$ gives

- $24$
- $32$
- $40$
- $14$

## Explanation

First evaluate the powers: $2^3 = 8$ and $4^2 = 16$. Then multiply, $5 \cdot 8 = 40$, and finally subtract, $40 - 16 = 24$. The value $32$ comes from treating $4^2 = 8$ as true, whereas $14$ comes from treating $2^3 = 6$ as true.
