Which of these statements is true?

- $12 < 7$
- $9 \geq 9$
- $9 > 9$
- $15 \leq 14$

## Explanation

The symbol $\geq$ is read as “greater than or equal to,” and $9$ is equal to $9$, so the statement $9 \geq 9$ is true. The others are false: no number is greater than itself, $12$ is greater than $7$, and $15$ is greater than $14$.
