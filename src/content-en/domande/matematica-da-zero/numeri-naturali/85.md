Which expression gives $27$?

- $(4 + 5) \cdot 3$
- $4 + 5 \cdot 3$
- $4 \cdot 5 + 3$
- $4 \cdot (5 + 3)$

## Explanation

The parentheses indicate that $4$ and $5$ are added first, and then the sum is multiplied: $(4 + 5) \cdot 3 = 9 \cdot 3 = 27$. The other expressions give $4 + 15 = 19$, $20 + 3 = 23$, and $4 \cdot 8 = 32$.
