A subtraction gives $503 - 287 = 216$. Which calculation checks this result?

- $216 - 287$ must equal $503$
- $503 + 216$ must equal $287$
- $503 - 216$ must equal $216$
- $287 + 216$ must equal $503$

## Explanation

The difference $216$ is the number that, when added to the subtrahend $287$, gives the minuend $503$. We therefore calculate $287 + 216 = 503$. The result equals the minuend, so the subtraction is correct. The other proposed calculations do not express this relationship.
