A warehouse ships bottles in cases of $100$ and boxes of $10$. To ship $1\,350$ bottles, it uses $13$ cases. How many boxes are needed for the remaining bottles?

- $50$
- $5$
- $13$
- $35$

## Explanation

The $13$ cases hold $13 \cdot 100 = 1\,300$ bottles. That leaves $1\,350 - 1\,300 = 50$ bottles to be put in boxes of $10$. Therefore $50 : 10 = 5$ boxes are needed. The value $50$ counts the remaining bottles rather than the boxes.
