A set of $84$ trading cards is shared equally among $6$ friends. How many cards does each friend receive?

- $16$
- $14$
- $504$
- $78$

## Explanation

Sharing equally calls for the division $84 : 6$. Since $6 \cdot 14 = 84$, the quotient is $14$. The value $16$ is incorrect because $6 \cdot 16 = 96$, whereas $504$ is the product of the two numbers.
