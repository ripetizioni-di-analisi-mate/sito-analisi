The product $250 \cdot 40$ equals

- $100\,000$
- $8\,000$
- $10\,000$
- $1\,000$

## Explanation

Calculate $25 \cdot 4 = 100$ and then append the zeros from the two factors, one from $250$ and one from $40$. The product is therefore $100 \cdot 100 = 10\,000$. The value $1\,000$ accounts for only one of the two zeros.
