In $(\square + 4) \cdot 3 = 27$ the box stands for a natural number. What is it?

- $7$
- $9$
- $5$
- $23$

## Explanation

The product is $27$ and one factor is $3$, so the other factor, the expression inside the parentheses, is $27 : 3 = 9$. It follows from $\square + 4 = 9$ that $\square = 9 - 4 = 5$. The value $9$ is the result inside the parentheses rather than the missing number, while $23$ is $27 - 4$.
