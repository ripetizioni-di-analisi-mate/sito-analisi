The result of $2 + 3^2 \cdot 2$ is

- $20$
- $50$
- $38$
- $22$

## Explanation

First evaluate the power, $3^2 = 9$, then multiply, $9 \cdot 2 = 18$, and finally add, $2 + 18 = 20$. The value $50$ comes from first adding $2 + 3$, whereas $22$ comes from adding $2 + 9$ before multiplying.
