An exact division has quotient $13$ and divisor $15$. What is the dividend?

- $28$
- $145$
- $165$
- $195$

## Explanation

In an exact division, the dividend is the product of the divisor and the quotient, so it equals $15 \cdot 13$. Calculate $15 \cdot 10 = 150$ and $15 \cdot 3 = 45$ to obtain $150 + 45 = 195$. The value $28$ is the sum of the two numbers.
