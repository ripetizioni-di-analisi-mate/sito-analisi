A box contains an odd number of buttons. Three bags are added, each containing an odd number of buttons. Without knowing the exact counts, what can you conclude?

- The total is odd.
- The total is always a multiple of four.
- The total is always greater than ten.
- The total is even.

## Explanation

Pair up the four odd counts. Each pair has an even sum, and two even numbers add to an even number. For example, $1+1+1+3=6$, so a multiple of four is not guaranteed.
