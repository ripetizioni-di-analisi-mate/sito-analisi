The expression $\{3 \cdot [10 - (2 + 4)] + 5\} \cdot 2$ uses all three types of grouping symbols. What number does it equal?

- $24$
- $17$
- $82$
- $34$

## Explanation

The parentheses give $2 + 4 = 6$ and the square brackets give $10 - 6 = 4$. The braces give $3 \cdot 4 + 5 = 12 + 5 = 17$, and finally $17 \cdot 2 = 34$. The value $17$ leaves out the final multiplication, whereas $24$ leaves out the addition of $5$ inside the braces.
