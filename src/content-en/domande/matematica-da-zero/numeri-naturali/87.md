In which of the following expressions can the parentheses be removed without changing the value?

- $12 - (4 + 3)$
- $(12 - 4) \cdot 3$
- $12 : (4 \cdot 3)$
- $(12 : 4) \cdot 3$

## Explanation

In an expression without parentheses, multiplications and divisions are performed first and then additions and subtractions, each time from left to right. In $12 : 4 \cdot 3$ the division is the first operation from the left, so we compute $12 : 4 = 3$ and then $3 \cdot 3 = 9$, as in $(12 : 4) \cdot 3$. In the other expressions the value changes, because $12 : (4 \cdot 3) = 1$ becomes $12 : 4 \cdot 3 = 9$, $(12 - 4) \cdot 3 = 24$ becomes $12 - 4 \cdot 3 = 0$, and $12 - (4 + 3) = 5$ becomes $12 - 4 + 3 = 11$. Therefore the parentheses can be removed only in $(12 : 4) \cdot 3$.
