What is $360 : 12 : 3$?

- $10$
- $120$
- $30$
- $90$

## Explanation

The divisions are performed in the order in which they appear: $360 : 12 = 30$ and then $30 : 3 = 10$. The value $90$ comes from first calculating $12 : 3 = 4$ and then $360 : 4$, whereas $30$ is the result of the first division alone.
