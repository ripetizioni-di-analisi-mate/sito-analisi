Evaluating $[40 - (3 + 5)] : 4$ gives

- $32$
- $16$
- $8$
- $38$

## Explanation

Start inside the parentheses: $3 + 5 = 8$. The square brackets now give $40 - 8 = 32$, and finally $32 : 4 = 8$. The value $38$ comes from dividing by $4$ only the term $8$, whereas $32$ comes from stopping before the division.
