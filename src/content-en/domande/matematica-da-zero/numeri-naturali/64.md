The figure shows a plan of a square floor, with one tile for each small square. How many tiles are needed to cover it?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="0,0 6,0 6,6 0,6" linee="0,0 6,0 6,6 0,6 0,0;1,0 1,6;2,0 2,6;3,0 3,6;4,0 4,6;5,0 5,6;0,1 6,1;0,2 6,2;0,3 6,3;0,4 6,4;0,5 6,5" xmin=-0.5 xmax=6.5 ymin=-0.5 ymax=6.5}

- $18$
- $24$
- $36$
- $12$

## Explanation

There are $6$ small squares along each side and $6$ rows, so the number of small squares is $6 \cdot 6 = 6^2 = 36$. The value $12$ is the sum $6 + 6$, whereas $24$ is the product $4 \cdot 6$ and $18$ is the product $3 \cdot 6$.
