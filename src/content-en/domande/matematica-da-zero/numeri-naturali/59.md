Two natural numbers $a$ and $b$ have product $a \cdot b = 0$, and we know that $a = 7$. What is $b$?

- $7$
- $0$
- $1$
- It cannot be determined

## Explanation

A product whose factors are all different from $0$ is different from $0$. Since $a = 7$ is different from $0$ and the product equals $0$, the factor $b$ must equal $0$. Indeed, $7 \cdot 0 = 0$, whereas $7 \cdot 1 = 7$.
