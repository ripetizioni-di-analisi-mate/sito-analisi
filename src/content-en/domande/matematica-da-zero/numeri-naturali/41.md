A group of $75$ students goes on a trip in buses with $40$ seats each. What is the smallest number of buses needed?

- $3$
- $35$
- $1$
- $2$

## Explanation

Dividing $75 : 40$ gives a quotient of $1$ and a remainder of $35$. One bus fills up and $35$ students remain, who need another bus. Therefore $2$ buses are needed. The value $1$ is just the quotient, while $35$ is the remainder.
