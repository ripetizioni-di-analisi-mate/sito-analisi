What number should replace the box to make $13 \cdot 6 + 13 \cdot \square = 13 \cdot 10$ true?

- $4$
- $6$
- $16$
- $10$

## Explanation

The factor $13$ is common to both terms, so the left-hand side equals $13 \cdot (6 + \square)$. For this to equal $13 \cdot 10$, we must have $6 + \square = 10$, that is, $\square = 4$. The value $10$ is the second factor on the right-hand side rather than the missing number.
