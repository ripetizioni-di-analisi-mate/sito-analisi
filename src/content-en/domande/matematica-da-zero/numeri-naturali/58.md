For $a = 3$, $b = 4$, and $c = 5$, only one of these equalities is false. Which one?

- $3 + 4 \cdot 5 = (3 + 4) \cdot (3 + 5)$
- $3 \cdot (4 + 5) = 3 \cdot 4 + 3 \cdot 5$
- $3 \cdot (5 - 4) = 3 \cdot 5 - 3 \cdot 4$
- $(4 + 5) \cdot 3 = 4 \cdot 3 + 5 \cdot 3$

## Explanation

The left-hand side $3 + 4 \cdot 5$ is not a number times a sum, so the distributive property does not apply. We have $3 + 4 \cdot 5 = 3 + 20 = 23$, whereas $(3 + 4) \cdot (3 + 5) = 7 \cdot 8 = 56$. The other three equalities apply the distributive property and give $27 = 12 + 15$, $3 = 15 - 12$, and $27 = 12 + 15$, respectively.
