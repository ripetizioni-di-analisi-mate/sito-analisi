Station A is $128$ km from station B, and station B is $97$ km from station C, along the same line in the same direction. How many kilometers are there between A and C?

- $225$
- $315$
- $325$
- $31$

## Explanation

The distances are consecutive along the line, so they are added: $128 + 97 = 225$. In the ones place, $8 + 7 = 15$, and one ten is carried. In the tens place, $2 + 9 + 1 = 12$, and one hundred is carried. The value $31$ is the difference between the two distances.
