A ticket costs $12$ euros. How many tickets can you buy with $100$ euros and how many euros will be left?

- $9$ tickets and $4$ euros
- $8$ tickets and $96$ euros
- $8$ tickets and $12$ euros
- $8$ tickets and $4$ euros

## Explanation

Calculate $100 : 12$. Since $12 \cdot 8 = 96$ and $12 \cdot 9 = 108$, the quotient is $8$ and the remainder is $100 - 96 = 4$. Buying $9$ tickets would cost $108$ euros, more than the $100$ available, whereas $96$ euros is the cost of the tickets rather than the amount left over.
