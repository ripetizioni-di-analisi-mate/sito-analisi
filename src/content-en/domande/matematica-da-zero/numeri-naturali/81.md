What is the value of the expression $(7 + 5) \cdot 3 - 2$?

- $36$
- $12$
- $20$
- $34$

## Explanation

First evaluate the expression inside the parentheses, $7 + 5 = 12$. Then multiply, $12 \cdot 3 = 36$, and finally subtract, $36 - 2 = 34$. The value $20$ comes from ignoring the parentheses, whereas $12$ comes from multiplying $12$ by $3 - 2$.
