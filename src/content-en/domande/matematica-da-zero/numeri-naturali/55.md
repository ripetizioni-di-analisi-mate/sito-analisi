Which values of $a$ and $b$ make $31 + a = 31$ and $31 \cdot b = 31$ true?

- $a = 0$ and $b = 1$
- $a = 1$ and $b = 1$
- $a = 0$ and $b = 0$
- $a = 1$ and $b = 0$

## Explanation

The number $0$ is the additive identity because adding $0$ leaves a number unchanged. The number $1$ is the multiplicative identity because $31 \cdot 1 = 31$. If $b = 0$, the result would be $31 \cdot 0 = 0$ rather than $31$.
