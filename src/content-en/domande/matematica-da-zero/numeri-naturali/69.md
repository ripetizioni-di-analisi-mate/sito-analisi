The product $5 \cdot 5 \cdot 5 \cdot 5 \cdot 5 \cdot 5$ can be written as the power

- $5^5$
- $5 \cdot 6$
- $5^6$
- $6^5$

## Explanation

The product contains six factors equal to $5$, so the base is $5$ and the exponent is $6$, giving $5^6$. The power $6^5$ has base $6$ and five factors, whereas $5 \cdot 6$ equals $30$ and does not express the given product as a power.
