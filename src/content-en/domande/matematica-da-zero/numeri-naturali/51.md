Which equality expresses the associative property of addition?

- $2 + 5 = 5 + 2$
- $2 + 0 = 2$
- $(2 + 5) + 8 = 2 + (5 + 8)$
- $2 \cdot (5 + 8) = 2 \cdot 5 + 2 \cdot 8$

## Explanation

The associative property states that, in a sum of three addends, any two adjacent addends can be added first without changing the result. In the correct equality only the grouping changes, and both sides equal $15$. The other equalities express the commutative, distributive, and identity properties.
