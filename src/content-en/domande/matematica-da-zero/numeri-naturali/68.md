In the number $70\,000$, the digit $7$ multiplies a power of $10$. Which power?

- $10^7$
- $10^4$
- $10^3$
- $10^5$

## Explanation

The number $70\,000$ equals $7 \cdot 10\,000$, and $10\,000$ has four zeros, so $10\,000 = 10^4$. Therefore $70\,000 = 7 \cdot 10^4$. The exponent is the number of zeros following the digit $7$, whereas $10^5 = 100\,000$ would give a number ten times as large.
