Evaluate $9 + 12 : 4 - 2 \cdot 3$ using the order of operations. What value do you obtain?

- $12$
- $6$
- $27$
- $30$

## Explanation

First divide, $12 : 4 = 3$, and multiply, $2 \cdot 3 = 6$. This leaves $9 + 3 - 6$, which gives $12 - 6 = 6$ when evaluated from left to right. The value $12$ leaves out the final subtraction.
