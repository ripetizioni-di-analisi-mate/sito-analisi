A zero is written to the right of a nonzero natural number, for example changing $45$ to $450$. How does the number change?

- It becomes one hundred times as large
- It stays the same
- It becomes ten times as large
- It increases by $10$ units

## Explanation

Every digit moves one place to the left and becomes worth ten times as much. For example, $45 = 4 \cdot 10 + 5$, whereas $450 = 4 \cdot 100 + 5 \cdot 10$, that is, $450 = 45 \cdot 10$. The number increases by $405$ units, not by $10$.
