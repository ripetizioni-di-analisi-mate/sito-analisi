The successor of a three-digit natural number has four digits. What is that three-digit number?

- $100$
- $999$
- $1\,000$
- $990$

## Explanation

The successor is obtained by adding $1$. The smallest four-digit number is $1\,000$, so the number we seek is its predecessor, $1\,000 - 1 = 999$. The number $1\,000$ already has four digits, whereas $100$ is the smallest three-digit number.
