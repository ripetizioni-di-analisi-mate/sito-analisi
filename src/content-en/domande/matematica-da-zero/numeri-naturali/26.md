In a subtraction, the minuend is $140$ and the difference is $65$. What is the subtrahend?

- $205$
- $75$
- $65$
- $85$

## Explanation

The difference is the number that gives the minuend when added to the subtrahend, so the subtrahend $s$ satisfies $s + 65 = 140$. Therefore $s = 140 - 65 = 75$. The value $205$ comes from adding the minuend and the difference.
