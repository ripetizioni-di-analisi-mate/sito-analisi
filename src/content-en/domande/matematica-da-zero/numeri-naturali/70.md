A sheet of paper is folded in half repeatedly, and the number of layers doubles with each fold. How many layers are there after $6$ folds?

- $12$
- $36$
- $32$
- $64$

## Explanation

The number of layers doubles with each fold, so after $6$ folds it is $2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 = 2^6 = 64$. The value $12$ is the product $2 \cdot 6$, whereas $32$ is the number of layers after five folds.
