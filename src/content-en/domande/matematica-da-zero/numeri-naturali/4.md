The natural number $a$ satisfies the condition $a \leq 4$. How many different values can $a$ take?

- $6$
- $3$
- $4$
- $5$

## Explanation

The condition allows numbers less than $4$ and also $4$ itself because the symbol $\leq$ includes equality. The values are $0$, $1$, $2$, $3$, and $4$, so there are $5$ of them. A count of $4$ leaves out zero, which is a natural number.
