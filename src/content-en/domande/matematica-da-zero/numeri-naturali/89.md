A student compares $(2 + 3)^2$ with $2 + 3^2$. What is the difference between the first and second values?

- $14$
- $2$
- $25$
- $0$

## Explanation

In the first expression, add inside the parentheses and then square: $(2 + 3)^2 = 5^2 = 25$. In the second expression, the exponent $2$ applies only to $3$: $2 + 3^2 = 2 + 9 = 11$. The difference is $25 - 11 = 14$. The value $2$ comes from treating $(2 + 3)^2 = 2^2 + 3^2 = 13$ as true.
