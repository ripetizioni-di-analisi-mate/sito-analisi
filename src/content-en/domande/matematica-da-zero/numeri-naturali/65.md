How many zeros does $10^6$ have when written in digits?

- $5$
- $7$
- $6$
- $60$

## Explanation

A power of $10$ is written as the digit $1$ followed by as many zeros as the exponent indicates. Therefore $10^6 = 1\,000\,000$, with six zeros after the digit $1$. The value $60$ is the product $10 \cdot 6$, whereas $5$ and $7$ each differ from the exponent by one.
