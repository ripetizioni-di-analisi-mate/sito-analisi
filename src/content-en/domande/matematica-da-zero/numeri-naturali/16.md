Which of these numbers is the largest?

- $3\,974$
- $3\,947$
- $3\,749$
- $3\,794$

## Explanation

All the numbers have four digits, so compare the digits starting from the left. The thousands digits are the same, and in the hundreds place $9$ is greater than $7$, so $3\,947$ and $3\,974$ remain. In the tens place $7 > 4$, so the largest number is $3\,974$.
