By how much do the values of $20 - (6 + 4)$ and $20 - 6 + 4$ differ?

- $10$
- $0$
- $8$
- $4$

## Explanation

The first expression equals $20 - 10 = 10$ because the sum inside the parentheses is evaluated first. Evaluate the second from left to right: $20 - 6 = 14$ and then $14 + 4 = 18$. The difference is $18 - 10 = 8$.
