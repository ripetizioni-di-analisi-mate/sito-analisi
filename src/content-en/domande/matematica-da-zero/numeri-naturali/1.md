A natural number $n$ has $100$ as its successor. What is the predecessor of $n$?

- $101$
- $99$
- $98$
- $97$

## Explanation

The successor of $n$ is obtained by adding $1$, so $n + 1 = 100$ and therefore $n = 99$. The predecessor of $n$ is obtained by subtracting $1$, so it is $99 - 1 = 98$. The value $99$ is the number $n$ itself, not its predecessor.
