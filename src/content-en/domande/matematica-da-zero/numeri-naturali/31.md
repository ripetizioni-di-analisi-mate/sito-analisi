Starting at $15$ on the number line, you take $9$ steps to the left. Which operation describes the move?

- $15 + 9$
- $15 : 9$
- $9 - 15$
- $15 - 9$

## Explanation

Steps to the left decrease the number, so subtract the number of steps from the starting number. The operation is $15 - 9$, and it leads to $6$. The sum $15 + 9$ describes steps to the right, whereas $9 - 15$ is not a natural number.
