In the number $2\,684$, what is the value of the digit $6$?

- $60$
- $6$
- $6\,000$
- $600$

## Explanation

The number $2\,684$ consists of $2$ thousands, $6$ hundreds, $8$ tens, and $4$ ones, that is, $2\,000 + 600 + 80 + 4$. The digit $6$ is in the hundreds place, so its value is $6 \cdot 100 = 600$.
