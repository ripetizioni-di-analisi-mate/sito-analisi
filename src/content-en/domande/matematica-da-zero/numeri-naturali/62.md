Of the powers $3^4$, $4^3$, $2^6$, and $6^2$, which has the smallest value?

- $3^4$
- $4^3$
- $2^6$
- $6^2$

## Explanation

Calculate the values: $3^4 = 81$, $4^3 = 64$, $2^6 = 64$, and $6^2 = 36$. The smallest value is $36$, that is, $6^2$. The largest exponent alone does not determine the answer because the base also matters.
