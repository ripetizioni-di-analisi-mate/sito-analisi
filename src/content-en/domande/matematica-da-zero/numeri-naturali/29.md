A purchase of $37$ euros is paid for with a $50$-euro bill. How many euros are returned as change?

- $17$
- $87$
- $13$
- $23$

## Explanation

The change is the number that, when added to $37$, gives $50$, so we calculate $50 - 37 = 13$. We can also count up: from $37$ to $40$ is $3$ euros, and from $40$ to $50$ is $10$ euros, for a total of $13$. The value $87$ is the sum of the value of the bill and the cost of the purchase.
