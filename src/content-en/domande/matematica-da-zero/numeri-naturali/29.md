You need exactly 18 cents using 10-cent, 5-cent and 1-cent coins, with plenty of each. What is the smallest number of coins?

- $3$
- $4$
- $5$
- $6$

## Explanation

A total of 18 requires three 1-cent coins, or five more than that, since the other denominations are multiples of five. The remaining 15 cents require at least two coins. One 10-cent coin, one 5-cent coin and three 1-cent coins achieve the minimum of five.
