Which expression represents $3\,070$?

- $3 \cdot 10^3 + 7$
- $3 \cdot 10^2 + 7 \cdot 10$
- $3 \cdot 10^3 + 7 \cdot 10$
- $3 \cdot 10^3 + 7 \cdot 10^2$

## Explanation

The number $3\,070$ has $3$ thousands, $0$ hundreds, $7$ tens, and $0$ ones. One thousand is $10^3$ and one ten is $10$, so the number is $3 \cdot 10^3 + 7 \cdot 10 = 3\,000 + 70$. Using $7 \cdot 10^2$ instead would give $3\,700$.
