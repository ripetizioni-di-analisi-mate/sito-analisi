A number has $3$ in the tens place and $8$ in the units place. These two digits are swapped and all the other digits stay where they are. By how much does the number change?

- It increases by $5$.
- It decreases by $45$.
- It increases by $45$.
- It increases by $50$.

## Explanation

The swap affects only the tens and the units, so the hundreds and the higher places contribute the same amount before and after. Before the swap the last two digits are worth $3 \cdot 10 + 8 = 38$, and after the swap they are worth $8 \cdot 10 + 3 = 83$. Since $83 - 38 = 45$, the number increases by $45$. The value $5$ is the difference $8 - 3$ between the two digits and does not take into account the places they occupy.
