A number has $3$ in its tens place and $8$ in its units place. Only these two digits are swapped. By how much does the number change, regardless of the digits before them?

- It increases by $5$.
- It decreases by $45$.
- It increases by $45$.
- It increases by $50$.

## Explanation

The last two digits change from $38$ to $83$, while hundreds and all higher places stay the same. The increase is $83-38=45$.
