An account holds $1\,200$ euros. After a withdrawal of $350$ euros and a deposit of $180$ euros, how many euros are in the account?

- $1\,030$
- $1\,730$
- $1\,370$
- $670$

## Explanation

The withdrawal decreases the balance and the deposit increases it, so calculate $1\,200 - 350 + 180$. From left to right, $1\,200 - 350 = 850$ and then $850 + 180 = 1\,030$. The value $670$ results from subtracting the deposit as well.
