Which of these calculations gives a natural number?

- $24 - 24$
- $24 - 25$
- $0 - 1$
- $7 - 19$

## Explanation

Subtraction within the natural numbers is possible only when the minuend is greater than or equal to the subtrahend. In $24 - 24$, the two numbers are equal and their difference is $0$, which is a natural number. In the other cases, the subtrahend exceeds the minuend.
