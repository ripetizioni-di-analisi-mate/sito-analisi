Which of these expressions has the value $11$?

- $2 \cdot 3 + 3$
- $2 + 3 \cdot 3$
- $2 \cdot (3 + 3)$
- $(2 + 3) \cdot 3$

## Explanation

In the expression $2 + 3 \cdot 3$, first multiply, $3 \cdot 3 = 9$, and then add, $2 + 9 = 11$. The others equal $9$, $12$, and $15$, respectively, because $2 \cdot 3 + 3 = 6 + 3 = 9$, $2 \cdot (3 + 3) = 2 \cdot 6 = 12$, and $(2 + 3) \cdot 3 = 5 \cdot 3 = 15$.
