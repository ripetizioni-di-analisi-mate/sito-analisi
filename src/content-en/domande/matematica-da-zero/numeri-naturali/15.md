A number consists of $9$ thousands, $4$ hundreds, and $2$ ones. How is it written in digits?

- $9\,420$
- $942$
- $94\,002$
- $9\,402$

## Explanation

Write the digits from thousands to ones: $9$ thousands, $4$ hundreds, no tens, and $2$ ones. A zero goes in the tens place, so the number is $9\,402$. Writing $942$ omits the zero in the tens place, leaving the number with no thousands place.
