Starting from $50$ and subtracting first $20$ and then $10$ gives the expression $50 - 20 - 10$. What is its value?

- $20$
- $30$
- $40$
- $80$

## Explanation

Perform the subtractions in the order in which they appear: $50 - 20 = 30$ and then $30 - 10 = 20$. The value $40$ comes from first calculating $20 - 10 = 10$ and then $50 - 10$, which does not follow the left-to-right order.
