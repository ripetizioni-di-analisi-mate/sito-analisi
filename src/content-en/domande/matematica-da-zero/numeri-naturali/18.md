Which expanded form corresponds to the digits of $6\,053$?

- $6\,000 + 50 + 3$
- $6\,000 + 500 + 3$
- $6\,000 + 5 + 3$
- $600 + 50 + 3$

## Explanation

The number $6\,053$ has $6$ thousands, $0$ hundreds, $5$ tens, and $3$ ones. The value of the digit $5$ is therefore $5 \cdot 10 = 50$, and the hundreds contribute nothing because the hundreds digit is $0$. The expanded form is $6\,000 + 50 + 3$.
