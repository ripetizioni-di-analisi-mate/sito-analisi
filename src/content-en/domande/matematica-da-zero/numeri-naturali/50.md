Which property justifies the equality $48 \cdot 25 = 25 \cdot 48$?

- Associative property
- Distributive property
- Commutative property
- Identity element

## Explanation

The two factors are the same on both sides of the equality. Only their positions change. This is the commutative property of multiplication, $a \cdot b = b \cdot a$. The associative property concerns the grouping of three terms, and the distributive property involves a sum.
