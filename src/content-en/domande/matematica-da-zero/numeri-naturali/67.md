The square of the natural number $x$ equals $49$. What is $x^3$?

- $21$
- $14$
- $147$
- $343$

## Explanation

Since $7 \cdot 7 = 49$, the number is $x = 7$. Its cube is therefore $7^3 = 7 \cdot 7 \cdot 7 = 49 \cdot 7 = 343$. The values $147$ and $21$ result from treating the exponent $3$ as a factor and multiplying it by $49$ or $7$, respectively.
