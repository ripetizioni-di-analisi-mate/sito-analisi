The expression $15 \cdot 8 - 15 \cdot 3$ can be written more briefly by factoring out a common factor. Which form is correct?

- $15 \cdot (8 + 3)$
- $15 \cdot (8 - 3)$
- $15 - 8 \cdot 3$
- $(15 - 15) \cdot 5$

## Explanation

The factor $15$ is common to both products, so we factor it out using the distributive property over subtraction: $15 \cdot 8 - 15 \cdot 3 = 15 \cdot (8 - 3) = 15 \cdot 5 = 75$. The expression $15 \cdot (8 + 3)$, on the other hand, equals $165$.
