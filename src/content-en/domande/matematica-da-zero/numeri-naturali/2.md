The numbers $405$, $54$, $450$, and $504$ are arranged in increasing order. Which comes third?

- $450$
- $405$
- $504$
- $54$

## Explanation

The number $54$ has fewer digits than the others, so it is the smallest. For the three-digit numbers, compare the hundreds: $405$ and $450$ each have $4$ hundreds, while $504$ has $5$. In the tens place, $0 < 5$, so $405 < 450$. The order is $54 < 405 < 450 < 504$, and $450$ comes third.
