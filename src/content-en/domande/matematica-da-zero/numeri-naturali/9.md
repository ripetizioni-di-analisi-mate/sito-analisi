How many natural numbers lie between $19$ and $27$, excluding the endpoints?

- $7$
- $6$
- $8$
- $9$

## Explanation

Excluding the endpoints gives $20$, $21$, $22$, $23$, $24$, $25$, and $26$, so there are $7$ numbers. The value $8$ is the difference $27 - 19$, the number of steps between the endpoints, whereas $9$ is obtained by including both endpoints.
