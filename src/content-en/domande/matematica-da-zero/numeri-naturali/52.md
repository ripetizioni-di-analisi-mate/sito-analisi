Using the distributive property to calculate $12 \cdot 98$ as $12 \cdot (100 - 2)$ gives

- $1\,224$
- $1\,076$
- $1\,198$
- $1\,176$

## Explanation

The distributive property gives $12 \cdot (100 - 2) = 12 \cdot 100 - 12 \cdot 2 = 1\,200 - 24 = 1\,176$. The value $1\,198$ comes from subtracting $2$ instead of $12 \cdot 2$, whereas $1\,224$ is the product $12 \cdot 102$.
