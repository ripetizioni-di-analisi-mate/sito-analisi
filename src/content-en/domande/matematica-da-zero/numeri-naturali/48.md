What number, when multiplied by $9$, gives $162$?

- $1\,458$
- $153$
- $14$
- $18$

## Explanation

The number we seek is the quotient $162 : 9$. Since $9 \cdot 18 = 162$, the number is $18$. The value $153$ comes from subtracting $9$ from $162$, whereas $1\,458$ is the product $162 \cdot 9$.
