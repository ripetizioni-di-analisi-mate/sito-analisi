How many squares does it contain?

- $35$
- $11$
- $28$
- $24$

## Explanation

The rectangle has $4$ rows of $7$ squares, so the number of squares is the sum of four addends each equal to $7$, that is, $4 \cdot 7 = 28$. The value $11$ comes from adding the two numbers instead of multiplying them, whereas $24$ and $35$ are the products $4 \cdot 6$ and $5 \cdot 7$.
