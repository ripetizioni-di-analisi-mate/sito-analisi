A student evaluates $18 - 6 : 3 \cdot 2$ and obtains $8$. What is the correct value?

- $17$
- $4$
- $14$
- $8$

## Explanation

First divide and multiply from left to right: $6 : 3 = 2$ and $2 \cdot 2 = 4$. Then subtract: $18 - 4 = 14$. The student instead performed the subtraction $18 - 6 = 12$ first, obtaining $12 : 3 \cdot 2 = 8$.
