The value of the product $5 \cdot 37 \cdot 2$ is

- $3\,700$
- $185$
- $44$
- $370$

## Explanation

By the commutative and associative properties, we can multiply $5$ and $2$ first: $5 \cdot 37 \cdot 2 = 37 \cdot (5 \cdot 2) = 37 \cdot 10 = 370$. The value $185$ is the product $5 \cdot 37$ without the factor $2$, whereas $44$ is the sum of the three numbers.
