Team A has $87$ points and team B has $12$ fewer. How many points do the two teams have in all?

- $99$
- $162$
- $186$
- $75$

## Explanation

Team B has $87 - 12 = 75$ points because it has twelve fewer. The total is $87 + 75 = 162$. The value $186$ comes from adding the twelve points instead of subtracting them, while $75$ is the score of team B alone.
