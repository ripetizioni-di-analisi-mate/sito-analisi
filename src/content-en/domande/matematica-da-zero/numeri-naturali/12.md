Which digit is in the tens place of $7\,305$?

- $5$
- $3$
- $0$
- $7$

## Explanation

The number $7\,305$ has $7$ thousands, $3$ hundreds, $0$ tens, and $5$ ones because $7\,305 = 7\,000 + 300 + 0 + 5$. The tens digit is therefore zero. The digit $3$ is in the hundreds place, and $5$ is in the ones place.
