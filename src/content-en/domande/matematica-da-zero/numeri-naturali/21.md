What place does the digit $4$ occupy in $3\,200\,450$?

- Tens
- Hundred-thousands
- Hundreds
- Thousands

## Explanation

Reading from the right, $3\,200\,450$ has $0$ ones, $5$ tens, $4$ hundreds, $0$ thousands, $0$ ten-thousands, $2$ hundred-thousands, and $3$ millions. The digit $4$ therefore occupies the hundreds place and is worth $400$.
