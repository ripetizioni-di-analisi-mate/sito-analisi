What number must be added to $268$ to obtain $1\,000$?

- $732$
- $632$
- $1\,268$
- $832$

## Explanation

The number we seek is the difference $1\,000 - 268$. Counting up from $268$ to $300$ gives $32$, and from $300$ to $1\,000$ gives $700$, so the total is $32 + 700 = 732$. The value $1\,268$ comes from adding $268$ to $1\,000$.
