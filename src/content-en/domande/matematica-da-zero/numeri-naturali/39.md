In a division problem, the divisor is $9$, the quotient is $12$, and the remainder is $5$. What is the dividend?

- $108$
- $113$
- $69$
- $26$

## Explanation

The dividend $a$ is given by $a = b \cdot q + r$, so $a = 9 \cdot 12 + 5$. Calculate $9 \cdot 12 = 108$ and then $108 + 5 = 113$. The value $108$ leaves out the remainder, while $26$ is the sum of the three numbers.
