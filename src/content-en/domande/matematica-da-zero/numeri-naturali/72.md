Marco evaluates $48 : 6 \cdot 2$. What result should he obtain?

- $14$
- $96$
- $4$
- $16$

## Explanation

Division and multiplication have the same precedence, so they are performed in the order in which they appear: $48 : 6 = 8$ and then $8 \cdot 2 = 16$. The value $4$ comes from first calculating $6 \cdot 2 = 12$ and then $48 : 12$, which does not follow the left-to-right order.
