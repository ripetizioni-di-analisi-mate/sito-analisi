A spider starts at $6$ on the number line, takes $9$ steps to the right, then $4$ steps to the left. At which point does it stop?

- $1$
- $19$
- $15$
- $11$

## Explanation

Each step to the right is added and each step to the left is subtracted, so the final position is $6 + 9 - 4$. Evaluate from left to right: $6 + 9 = 15$ and then $15 - 4 = 11$. The value $15$ leaves out the return trip, whereas $19$ counts even the backward steps as additions.
