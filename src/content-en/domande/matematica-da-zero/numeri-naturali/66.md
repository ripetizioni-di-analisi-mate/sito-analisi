Which of the powers $1^{50}$, $0^{50}$, $2^3$, and $3^1$ has the greatest value?

- $2^3$
- $0^{50}$
- $3^1$
- $1^{50}$

## Explanation

Every power with base $1$ equals $1$, and every power with base $0$ and a positive exponent equals $0$. Also, $3^1 = 3$ and $2^3 = 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 = 8$. The greatest value is therefore $8$, that is, $2^3$.
