In the expression $\{4 + [2 \cdot (9 - 6)]\} : 5$, which operation is performed second?

- $4 + 6$
- $2 \cdot 3$
- $4 + 2$
- $6 : 5$

## Explanation

Start with the expression inside the parentheses, $9 - 6 = 3$. Next evaluate the one inside the square brackets, $2 \cdot 3 = 6$, which is the second operation. This is followed by the sum $4 + 6 = 10$ inside the braces and the division $10 : 5 = 2$.
