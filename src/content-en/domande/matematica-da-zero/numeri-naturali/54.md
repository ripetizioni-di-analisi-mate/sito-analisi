The expression $23 \cdot 7 + 23 \cdot 3$ equals

- $69$
- $164$
- $230$
- $2\,300$

## Explanation

The factor $23$ is common to both terms, so we factor it out using the distributive property: $23 \cdot 7 + 23 \cdot 3 = 23 \cdot (7 + 3) = 23 \cdot 10 = 230$. The value $164$ comes from multiplying $23$ only by $7$ and adding $3$.
