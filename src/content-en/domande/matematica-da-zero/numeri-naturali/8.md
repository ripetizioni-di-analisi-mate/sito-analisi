The scores in a competition are $4\,960$, $4\,609$, $4\,096$, and $4\,906$. Which score is second in decreasing order?

- $4\,096$
- $4\,609$
- $4\,960$
- $4\,906$

## Explanation

The thousands digits are the same, so compare the hundreds: $4\,960$ and $4\,906$ have $9$ hundreds, whereas $4\,609$ has $6$ and $4\,096$ has $0$. Of the first two numbers, the tens digits satisfy $6 > 0$. The numbers in decreasing order are $4\,960$, $4\,906$, $4\,609$, $4\,096$, and $4\,906$ is second.
