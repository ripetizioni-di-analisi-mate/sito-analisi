Using the rule for the product of a sum and a difference, which polynomial equals $(5y + 3)(5y - 3)$?

- $25y^2 + 9$
- $10y^2 - 9$
- $25y^2 - 9$
- $25y^2 - 6$

## Explanation

Multiplying the sum of two terms by their difference gives the difference of their squares. Therefore $(5y)^2 - 3^2 = 25y^2 - 9$. The cross terms $-15y$ and $+15y$ are opposites and cancel. The value $10y^2$ comes from multiplying the coefficient $5$ by $2$ instead of squaring it.
