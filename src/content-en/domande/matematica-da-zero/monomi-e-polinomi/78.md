Calculate $9^3$ by writing $9 = 10 - 1$ and expanding the cube of a binomial. What is its value?

- $999$
- $729$
- $1331$
- $671$

## Explanation

We have $(10 - 1)^3 = 10^3 - 3 \cdot 10^2 \cdot 1 + 3 \cdot 10 \cdot 1^2 - 1^3 = 1000 - 300 + 30 - 1 = 729$. The value $999$ is the difference of the cubes $10^3 - 1^3$ and omits the two products multiplied by three.
