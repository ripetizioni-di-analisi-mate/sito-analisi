Only one of the following divisions has a quotient that is not a monomial. Which one?

- $\dfrac{9ab}{3ab}$
- $\dfrac{4a}{2a^3}$
- $\dfrac{6a^2b}{3a}$
- $\dfrac{8a^3}{2a}$

## Explanation

The quotient of two monomials is a monomial when every variable in the divisor appears in the dividend with an equal or greater exponent. In $\dfrac{4a}{2a^3}$ the exponent in the divisor is $3$, greater than $1$, so the result is $\dfrac{2}{a^2}$, which has a variable in the denominator.
