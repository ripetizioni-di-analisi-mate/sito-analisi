Cube the monomial $-3x^2y$. Which monomial results?

- $-27x^5y^3$
- $-27x^6y^3$
- $27x^6y^3$
- $-9x^6y^3$

## Explanation

Cube each factor. The number $(-3)^3$ equals $-27$ because the exponent is odd. Also, $(x^2)^3 = x^6$ and $y^3$ remains $y^3$. The result is $-27x^6y^3$. The value $-9$ comes from multiplying the coefficient by $3$ instead of raising it to that power.
