For what value of $x$ is the expression $\dfrac{7}{2x - 6}$ undefined?

- $x = 3$
- $x = 0$
- $x = 6$
- $x = -3$

## Explanation

A fraction is undefined when its denominator is zero. The denominator $2x - 6$ equals $0$ if $2x = 6$, that is, for $x = 3$. With $x = 0$ the denominator is $-6$ and the expression equals $-\dfrac{7}{6}$, so it is defined.
