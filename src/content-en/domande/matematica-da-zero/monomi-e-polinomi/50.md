Subtract $2x^2 - 4x - 1$ from $5x^2 - x + 3$ and simplify. Which polynomial results?

- $3x^2 + 3x + 4$
- $3x^2 - 5x + 2$
- $3x^2 + 3x + 2$
- $7x^2 - 5x + 2$

## Explanation

The minus sign reverses the sign of each term of the polynomial being subtracted, giving $5x^2 - x + 3 - 2x^2 + 4x + 1$. Combining like terms gives $3x^2 + 3x + 4$. Reversing only the sign of the first term gives the incorrect result $3x^2 - 5x + 2$.
