What is the degree of the monomial $-9$?

- $0$
- $9$
- $-9$
- $1$

## Explanation

A nonzero number is a monomial without variables. The sum of the exponents is an empty sum, so the degree is $0$. This is equivalent to writing $-9x^0$, where $x^0 = 1$. The value $9$ is the number itself rather than its degree.
