With $x \neq 0$, dividing $15x^3y^2$ by $5x^3$ makes the variable $x$ disappear from the result. Which monomial do you get?

- $10y^2$
- $3y^2$
- $3xy^2$
- $3y$

## Explanation

The coefficient of the quotient is $15 : 5 = 3$. The variable $x$ has the same exponent in both monomials, so $\dfrac{x^3}{x^3} = x^0 = 1$ and the variable disappears from the result. The variable $y$ keeps the exponent $2$. The quotient is $3y^2$.
