After like terms are combined, what is the degree of the polynomial $x^4 + 2x^2 - x^4 + 5x$?

- $5$
- $4$
- $2$
- $1$

## Explanation

Determine the degree after combining like terms. The terms $x^4$ and $-x^4$ are opposites and have sum $0$, so the simplified polynomial is $2x^2 + 5x$. The term of highest degree is $2x^2$, so the degree is $2$.
