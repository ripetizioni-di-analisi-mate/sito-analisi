The square of a four-term polynomial is expanded. How many doubled products appear in the expansion?

- $8$
- $12$
- $6$
- $4$

## Explanation

Each doubled product corresponds to a pair of distinct terms. Four terms form $\dfrac{4 \cdot 3}{2} = 6$ pairs. With three terms there are $3$ pairs, and the square of a binomial has only one doubled product. The value $12$ counts each pair twice.
