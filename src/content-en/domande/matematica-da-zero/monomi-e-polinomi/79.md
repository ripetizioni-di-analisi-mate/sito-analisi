In the expansion of $(a - b)^3$, what are the signs of the four terms $a^3$, $a^2b$, $ab^2$, $b^3$, in that order?

- $+, -, +, -$
- $+, -, +, +$
- $+, -, -, -$
- $+, +, +, -$

## Explanation

Substitute $-b$ for $b$ in $(a + b)^3$. The terms with $b$ raised to an odd power change sign, namely $a^2b$ and $b^3$. The term $ab^2$ contains $(-b)^2 = b^2$ and stays positive. The signs are therefore $+, -, +, -$.
