What is the coefficient of the term $x^2y$ in the expansion of $(x + y)^3$?

- $3$
- $6$
- $2$
- $1$

## Explanation

The cube of a binomial has the form $x^3 + 3x^2y + 3xy^2 + y^3$. The term involving $x^2y$ is three times the square of the first term times the second, so its coefficient is $3$. The value $1$ is the coefficient of both $x^3$ and $y^3$.
