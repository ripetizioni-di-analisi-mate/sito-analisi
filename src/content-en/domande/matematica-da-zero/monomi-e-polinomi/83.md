Dividing each term of $10a^3 - 15a^2 + 5a$ by $5a$, with $a \neq 0$, gives a polynomial. Which one?

- $2a^2 - 3a + 5$
- $2a^3 - 3a^2 + a$
- $2a^2 - 3a + 1$
- $2a^2 - 3a$

## Explanation

Dividing each term by $5a$ gives $\dfrac{10a^3}{5a} = 2a^2$, $\dfrac{-15a^2}{5a} = -3a$, and $\dfrac{5a}{5a} = 1$. The quotient is $2a^2 - 3a + 1$. The final term does not disappear because dividing a monomial by itself gives $1$. The value $2a^2 - 3a$ comes from omitting precisely this quotient.
