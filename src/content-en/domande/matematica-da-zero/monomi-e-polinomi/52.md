Multiply the monomial $-2a$ by the trinomial $3a^2 - ab + 4b$. Which polynomial results?

- $-6a^2 + 2a^2b - 8ab$
- $-6a^3 + 2a^2b - 8ab$
- $-6a^3 + 2a^2b - 8b$
- $-6a^3 - 2a^2b - 8ab$

## Explanation

Multiply the monomial by each term. This gives $-2a \cdot 3a^2 = -6a^3$, then $-2a \cdot (-ab) = +2a^2b$, and finally $-2a \cdot 4b = -8ab$. The result is $-6a^3 + 2a^2b - 8ab$. The second term is positive because it is the product of two negative factors.
