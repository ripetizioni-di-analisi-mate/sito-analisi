Which of the following monomials divides the polynomial $6a^2b + 9ab^2 - 3ab$ with a polynomial quotient?

- $a^2b$
- $3ab$
- $ab^2$
- $6ab^2$

## Explanation

A monomial divides a polynomial if it divides every term. The monomial $3ab$ divides $6a^2b$, $9ab^2$, and $3ab$, giving the quotient $2a + 3b - 1$. The monomial $ab^2$ does not divide $6a^2b$ because $b$ has exponent $1$, less than $2$.
