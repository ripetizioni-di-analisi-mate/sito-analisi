Which of the following monomials forms a pair of like terms with $-2x^2y$?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}x^2y$
- $2x^2$
- $5xy^2$
- $x^2y^2$

## Explanation

Two monomials are like terms if they have the same variable part. The variable part of $-2x^2y$ is $x^2y$, which is also the variable part of $\dfrac{1}{2}x^2y$. The monomial $5xy^2$ has the same exponents assigned to the opposite variables, so it is not a like term.
