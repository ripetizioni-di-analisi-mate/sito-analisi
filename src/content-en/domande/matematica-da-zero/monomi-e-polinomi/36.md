Which monomial $M$ satisfies the equality $M \cdot 4xy^2 = 12x^3y^3$?

- $3x^2y$
- $8x^2y$
- $3x^2y^2$
- $3x^3y$

## Explanation

The monomial $M$ is the quotient $\dfrac{12x^3y^3}{4xy^2}$. The coefficients give $12 : 4 = 3$, and subtracting the exponents gives $x^{3-1} = x^2$ and $y^{3-2} = y$. Therefore $M = 3x^2y$. Multiplying to check gives $3x^2y \cdot 4xy^2 = 12x^3y^3$.
