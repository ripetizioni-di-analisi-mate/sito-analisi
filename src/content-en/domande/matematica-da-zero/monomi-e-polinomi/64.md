Which polynomial is the expansion of the square $(3a + 2b)^2$?

- $3a^2 + 6ab + 2b^2$
- $9a^2 + 6ab + 4b^2$
- $9a^2 + 4b^2$
- $9a^2 + 12ab + 4b^2$

## Explanation

The squares of the two terms are $(3a)^2 = 9a^2$ and $(2b)^2 = 4b^2$. Twice the product is $2 \cdot 3a \cdot 2b = 12ab$. The expansion is $9a^2 + 12ab + 4b^2$. The value $9a^2 + 4b^2$ is the sum of the squares and omits twice the product.
