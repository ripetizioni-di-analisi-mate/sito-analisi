For $n$ people, a recipe calls for $120n + 80$ grams of flour. How many grams are needed for $6$ people?

- $720$
- $800$
- $200$
- $1200$

## Explanation

Substituting $n = 6$ gives $120 \cdot 6 + 80 = 720 + 80 = 800$ grams. The value $720$ comes from omitting the fixed $80$ grams. The value $1200$ comes from $(120 + 80) \cdot 6$, which multiplies the fixed quantity by $6$ as well.
