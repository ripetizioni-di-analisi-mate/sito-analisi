A recipe uses $120n+80$ grams of flour for $n$ people. To go from 6 to 12 people, Ada doubles the entire amount for 6. How many grams must she then remove to follow the recipe?

- $40$
- $80$
- $120$
- $160$

## Explanation

Doubling the batch for six gives $2(120\cdot6+80)=120\cdot12+160$. In the batch for twelve the fixed term is still $80$. The difference is therefore $160-80=80$ grams.
