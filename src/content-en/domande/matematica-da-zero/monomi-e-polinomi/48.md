A program computes $P(a,b)=a^2-3ab+2b^2$. Its inputs $a$ and $b$ turn out to have been swapped. For which real pairs does the result still equal the correct value?

- Only when $a=b$.
- When $a=b$ or $a=-b$.
- Whenever $ab=0$, regardless of the other input.
- For every real pair.

## Explanation

Swapping the inputs gives $P(b,a)=b^2-3ab+2a^2$. The mixed terms are identical. Equating the expressions leaves $a^2=b^2$, which for real numbers is equivalent to $a=b$ or $a=-b$.
