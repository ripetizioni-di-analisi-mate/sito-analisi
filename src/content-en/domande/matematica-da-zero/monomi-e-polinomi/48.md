What is the value of the polynomial $a^2 - 3ab + 2b^2$ for $a = 4$ and $b = -1$?

- $26$
- $30$
- $6$
- $12$

## Explanation

Substituting the numbers gives $4^2 - 3 \cdot 4 \cdot (-1) + 2 \cdot (-1)^2 = 16 + 12 + 2 = 30$. The term $-3ab$ equals $+12$ because $b$ is negative and the coefficient $-3$ is negative. The value $6$ comes from using $-12$, which overlooks the sign of $b$.
