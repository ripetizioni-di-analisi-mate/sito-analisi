The polynomial $x^2 - 7x + 10$ is evaluated for some values of $x$. For which of the following values is the result $0$?

- $-5$
- $5$
- $7$
- $-2$

## Explanation

The value of the polynomial is obtained by writing the number in place of $x$ and carrying out the calculations. For $x = 5$ we have $5^2 - 7 \cdot 5 + 10 = 25 - 35 + 10 = 0$, so $5$ is the value sought. The value $-5$ is chosen if $-7 \cdot (-5)$ is computed as $-35$, but the product of two negative numbers is positive, so for $x = -5$ the polynomial equals $25 + 35 + 10 = 70$. For $x = 7$ we get $49 - 49 + 10 = 10$ and for $x = -2$ we get $4 + 14 + 10 = 28$.
