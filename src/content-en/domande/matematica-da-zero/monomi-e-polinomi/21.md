A container with a square base has volume $a^2h$. The side $a$ of the base is doubled. How must the height change to keep the volume the same?

- Divide it by $2$.
- Multiply it by $2$.
- Multiply it by $4$.
- Divide it by $4$.

## Explanation

The new base area is $(2a)^2=4a^2$. For the product of base area and height to remain $a^2h$, the new height must be $\dfrac{h}{4}$.
