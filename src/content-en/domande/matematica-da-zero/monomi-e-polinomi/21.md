A container with a square base of side length $a$ and height $h$ has volume $a^2h$. What is the total degree of this monomial?

- $2$
- $6$
- $1$
- $3$

## Explanation

The total degree is the sum of the exponents. The variable $a$ has exponent $2$ and the variable $h$ has exponent $1$, so the degree is $2 + 1 = 3$. The value $2$ is just the exponent of $a$ and leaves out the variable $h$.
