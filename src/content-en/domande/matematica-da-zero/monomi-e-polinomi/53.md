Expanding the product $(x - 4)(x + 7)$ gives a trinomial. Which one?

- $x^2 - 3x - 28$
- $x^2 + 3x + 28$
- $x^2 - 28$
- $x^2 + 3x - 28$

## Explanation

Multiply each term of the first binomial by each term of the second, giving $x^2 + 7x - 4x - 28$. The terms $7x$ and $-4x$ are like terms and give $3x$. The result is $x^2 + 3x - 28$. The term $-28$ is the product $(-4) \cdot 7$.
