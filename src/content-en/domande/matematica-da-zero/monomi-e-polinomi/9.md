What is $ab - a^2$ for $a = 2$ and $b = -3$?

- $2$
- $-2$
- $-10$
- $10$

## Explanation

Substituting the numbers gives $2 \cdot (-3) - 2^2 = -6 - 4 = -10$. The product $ab$ equals $-6$ and the square $a^2$ equals $4$, which is subtracted. The value $-2$ comes from adding $4$ instead of subtracting it.
