A cube-shaped box has an edge length of $2a$ centimeters. Which monomial gives its volume in cubic centimeters?

- $2a^3$
- $8a$
- $6a^3$
- $8a^3$

## Explanation

The volume is the cube of the edge length, so it is $(2a)^3$. Cube both the coefficient, $2^3 = 8$, and the variable, $a^3$. The volume is $8a^3$ cubic centimeters. The value $2a^3$ comes from forgetting to cube the coefficient $2$.
