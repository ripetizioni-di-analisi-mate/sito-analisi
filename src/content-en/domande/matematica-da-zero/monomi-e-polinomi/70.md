For $a = 3$ and $b = -5$, what is $(a + b)^2 - a^2 - b^2$?

- $30$
- $-15$
- $-30$
- $34$

## Explanation

By the formula for the square of a binomial, $(a + b)^2 - a^2 - b^2 = 2ab$, which for $a = 3$ and $b = -5$ equals $2 \cdot 3 \cdot (-5) = -30$. Substituting the numbers to check gives $(-2)^2 - 9 - 25 = 4 - 34 = -30$. The value $34$ is the sum $9 + 25$.
