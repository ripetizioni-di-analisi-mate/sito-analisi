Combine the like terms in $5ab^2 - 8ab^2 + 2ab^2$. Which monomial results?

- $-ab^2$
- $15ab^2$
- $-3ab^2$
- $ab^2$

## Explanation

The three monomials are like terms, so add the coefficients and keep the variable part $ab^2$. We have $5 - 8 + 2 = -1$, so the result is $-ab^2$. The value $-3ab^2$ comes from stopping after the first two terms.
