The expression $(a + 3)^2 - (a - 3)(a + 3)$ is equivalent to a linear polynomial. Which one?

- $6a$
- $2a^2 + 6a$
- $6a + 18$
- $6a + 9$

## Explanation

The first term equals $a^2 + 6a + 9$ and the product $(a - 3)(a + 3)$ equals $a^2 - 9$. The minus sign reverses the signs of both terms of the product, so we have $a^2 + 6a + 9 - a^2 + 9$. This leaves $6a + 18$. The value $6a$ comes from leaving $-9$ unchanged instead of reversing its sign.
