Simplify the polynomial $5x^2 - 3x + 2 - 2x^2 + 4x - 7$. What is the simplified polynomial?

- $3x^2 + x - 5$
- $7x^2 + x - 5$
- $3x^2 - 7x - 5$
- $3x^2 + x + 5$

## Explanation

Add like terms. For the terms in $x^2$ we have $5 - 2 = 3$, for the terms in $x$ we have $-3 + 4 = 1$, and for the constants we have $2 - 7 = -5$. The simplified polynomial is $3x^2 + x - 5$ and has three terms.
