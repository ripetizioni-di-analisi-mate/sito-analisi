In calculating $(2a - b + c)(a + 3b - c + d)$, how many products of terms are written before like terms are combined?

- $7$
- $16$
- $9$
- $12$

## Explanation

Each term of the first polynomial is multiplied by each term of the second. The first has $3$ terms and the second has $4$ terms, so there are $3 \cdot 4 = 12$ products. The value $7$ comes from adding the numbers of terms, which does not count the products.
