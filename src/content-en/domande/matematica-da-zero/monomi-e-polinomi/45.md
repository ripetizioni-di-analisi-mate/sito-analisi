What is the constant term of the simplified polynomial obtained from $2x^2 - 5x + 3x - 8 + 5$?

- $-13$
- $-8$
- $-3$
- $5$

## Explanation

The terms without variables are $-8$ and $+5$, which add up to the constant term $-8 + 5 = -3$. The simplified polynomial is $2x^2 - 2x - 3$. The value $-13$ comes from also subtracting $5$, which actually has a plus sign.
