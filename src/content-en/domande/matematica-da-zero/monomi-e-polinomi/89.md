A sum of $18x + 24$ euros is shared equally among $6$ people. Which polynomial gives the amount each person receives?

- $3x + 24$
- $12x + 18$
- $18x + 4$
- $3x + 4$

## Explanation

Each person receives $\dfrac{18x + 24}{6}$. Dividing each term by $6$ gives $\dfrac{18x}{6} = 3x$ and $\dfrac{24}{6} = 4$. Each share is $3x + 4$ euros. The value $3x + 24$ comes from dividing only the first term.
