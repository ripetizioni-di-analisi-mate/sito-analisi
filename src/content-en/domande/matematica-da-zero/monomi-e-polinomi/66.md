Which monomial should replace the dots to make $x^2 + \dots + 49$ the square of a binomial?

- $49x$
- $14x$
- $28x$
- $7x$

## Explanation

The term $49$ is the square of $7$, so the binomial is $x + 7$. The middle term is twice the product, $2 \cdot x \cdot 7 = 14x$. Indeed, $(x + 7)^2 = x^2 + 14x + 49$. The value $7x$ is the product of the two terms before doubling.
