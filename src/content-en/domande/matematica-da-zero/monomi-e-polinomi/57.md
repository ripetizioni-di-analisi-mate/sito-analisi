A house has a rectangular garden with base $x + 5$ meters and height $x - 2$ meters. Which polynomial gives its area in square meters?

- $x^2 - 10$
- $x^2 - 3x - 10$
- $x^2 + 3x + 10$
- $x^2 + 3x - 10$

## Explanation

The area is the product $(x + 5)(x - 2) = x^2 - 2x + 5x - 10$. Adding the terms in $x$ gives $-2x + 5x = 3x$. The area is $x^2 + 3x - 10$ square meters. The constant term is $5 \cdot (-2) = -10$.
