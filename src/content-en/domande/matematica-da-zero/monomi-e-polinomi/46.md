On Monday Luca spends $5a + 2b$ euros, and on Tuesday he spends $3a - 2b + 4$ euros. Which simplified polynomial gives his total spending over the two days?

- $8a + 4b + 4$
- $8a - 4b + 4$
- $8a + 4b$
- $8a + 4$

## Explanation

Add the two polynomials and combine like terms. For the terms in $a$ we have $5 + 3 = 8$. The terms $2b$ and $-2b$ are opposites and have sum $0$, so $b$ disappears. The constant term $4$ remains, and the total cost is $8a + 4$.
