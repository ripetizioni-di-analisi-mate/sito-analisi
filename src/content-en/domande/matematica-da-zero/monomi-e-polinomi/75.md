Which polynomial is the expansion of the square of the trinomial $a - 2b + 3$?

- $a^2 + 2b^2 + 9 - 2ab + 3a - 6b$
- $a^2 + 4b^2 + 9 + 4ab + 6a + 12b$
- $a^2 + 4b^2 + 9$
- $a^2 + 4b^2 + 9 - 4ab + 6a - 12b$

## Explanation

Add the squares of the three terms, $a^2$, $4b^2$, and $9$, and twice the product of each pair. These doubled products are $2 \cdot a \cdot (-2b) = -4ab$, $2 \cdot a \cdot 3 = 6a$, and $2 \cdot (-2b) \cdot 3 = -12b$. The sign of each follows the sign rule.
