A ticket costs $8$ euros, and a one-time fee of $3$ euros is added to the booking. Which expression gives the cost of $n$ tickets?

- $8(n + 3)$
- $8 + 3n$
- $8n + 3$
- $11n$

## Explanation

Tickets cost $8$ euros each, so $n$ tickets cost $8n$ euros. The booking fee is paid only once, so we add $3$ and the cost is $8n + 3$. For $n = 2$, the cost is $19$ euros, whereas $11n$ would give $22$ euros.
