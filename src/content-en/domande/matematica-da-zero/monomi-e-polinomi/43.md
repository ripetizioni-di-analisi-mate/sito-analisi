How do you write $3 - x^3 + 5x$ in descending powers of $x$?

- $5x - x^3 + 3$
- $-x^3 + 5x + 3$
- $3 + 5x - x^3$
- $-x^3 + 3 + 5x$

## Explanation

Write the terms from highest to lowest degree. The term of degree $3$ is $-x^3$, the term of degree $1$ is $5x$, and the constant term, of degree $0$, is $3$. The resulting order is therefore $-x^3 + 5x + 3$. Each term keeps its sign.
