Simplify the polynomial $a^2 - 3ab + b^2 + ab - 2b^2$. Which description of the result is correct?

- A trinomial of degree $4$
- A quadrinomial of degree $2$
- A trinomial of degree $2$
- A binomial of degree $2$

## Explanation

The like terms $-3ab$ and $ab$ add up to $-2ab$, and $b^2 - 2b^2$ gives $-b^2$. The simplified polynomial is $a^2 - 2ab - b^2$, which has three terms. Each term has total degree $2$, so it is a trinomial of degree $2$.
