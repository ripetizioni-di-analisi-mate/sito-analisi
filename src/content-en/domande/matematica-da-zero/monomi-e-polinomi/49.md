Add the two polynomials $4a^2 + a - 6$ and $-a^2 + 2a + 9$. What is their sum in simplified form?

- $3a^2 + 3a - 15$
- $5a^2 + 3a + 3$
- $3a^2 - a + 3$
- $3a^2 + 3a + 3$

## Explanation

Write the terms of both polynomials and combine like terms. For $a^2$ we have $4 - 1 = 3$, for $a$ we have $1 + 2 = 3$, and for the constants we have $-6 + 9 = 3$. The result is $3a^2 + 3a + 3$. The value $5a^2$ comes from adding $4$ and $1$ and overlooking the minus sign.
