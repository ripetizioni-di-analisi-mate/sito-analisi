Which of the following polynomials is complete?

- $x^2 + 3$
- $2x^3 - x^2 + 4x - 1$
- $x^3 + x + 1$
- $x^4 + x^2$

## Explanation

A polynomial in one variable is complete if it contains all powers from the degree of the polynomial down to the constant term. The polynomial $2x^3 - x^2 + 4x - 1$ has terms of degrees $3$, $2$, $1$, and $0$. In $x^3 + x + 1$, the term of degree $2$ is missing.
