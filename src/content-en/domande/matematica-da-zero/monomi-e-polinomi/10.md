A square has a side length of $2.5$ centimeters. What is its area $l^2$ in square centimeters?

- $4.25$
- $5$
- $6.25$
- $10$

## Explanation

The area of the square is $l^2 = 2.5 \cdot 2.5 = 6.25$. The value $5$ is twice the side length, $2 \cdot 2.5$, whereas $10$ is the perimeter, $4 \cdot 2.5$. Neither operation multiplies the side length by itself.
