The square in the figure is divided into four parts and has side length $x + 3$. Which expression gives its total area?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 linee="0,0 7,0 7,7 0,7 0,0;4,0 4,7;0,4 7,4" etichette="1.7,1.8,x²;5.2,1.8,3x;1.7,5.3,3x;5.2,5.3,9;1.9,-0.6,x;5.4,-0.6,3;-0.6,1.8,x;-0.6,5.3,3" xmin=-1 xmax=8 ymin=-1 ymax=8}

- $x^2 + 3x + 9$
- $x^2 + 6x + 9$
- $x^2 + 6x + 3$
- $x^2 + 9$

## Explanation

The area is the sum of the four parts, $x^2 + 3x + 3x + 9$. The two rectangles have the same area $3x$, so together they have area $6x$. The total area is $x^2 + 6x + 9$. This is the expansion of $(x + 3)^2$, with $2 \cdot x \cdot 3$ as twice the product.
