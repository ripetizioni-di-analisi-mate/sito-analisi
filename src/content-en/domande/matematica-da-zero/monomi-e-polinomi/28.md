A monomial is given by $\dfrac{1}{2}a^3b$. Which monomial results from squaring it?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}a^6b^2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}a^6b^2$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}a^5b^2$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}a^6b$

## Explanation

Square each factor. The coefficient becomes $\left( \dfrac{1}{2} \right)^2 = \dfrac{1}{4}$, the exponent of $a$ becomes $3 \cdot 2 = 6$, and the exponent of $b$ becomes $1 \cdot 2 = 2$. The result is $\dfrac{1}{4}a^6b^2$.
