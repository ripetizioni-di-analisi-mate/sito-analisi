Calculate $103^2$ by writing $103 = 100 + 3$. What is its value?

- $10309$
- $10009$
- $10600$
- $10609$

## Explanation

Applying the formula for the square of a binomial gives $100^2 + 2 \cdot 100 \cdot 3 + 3^2 = 10000 + 600 + 9 = 10609$. The value $10009$ omits twice the product, $600$. The value $10600$ omits the square $9$, while $10309$ uses $300$ in place of twice the product.
