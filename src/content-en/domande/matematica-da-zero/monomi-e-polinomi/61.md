Which of the following equalities holds for every value of $x$?

- $2(x - 3) = 2x - 6$
- $x(x + 1) = x^2 + 1$
- $2(x - 3) = 2x - 3$
- $-(x + 4) = -x + 4$

## Explanation

By the distributive property, multiply the monomial $2$ by each term to obtain $2(x - 3) = 2x - 6$. The other equalities do not hold for every $x$. For $x = 2$, for example, $x(x + 1)$ equals $6$ whereas $x^2 + 1$ equals $5$.
