Evaluate the polynomial $x^2 - 5x + 1$ for $x = -3$. What is its value?

- $7$
- $25$
- $-5$
- $24$

## Explanation

In place of $x$, write $(-3)$. The power $(-3)^2$ equals $9$ and $-5 \cdot (-3)$ equals $+15$, so the expression becomes $9 + 15 + 1 = 25$. The value $7$ comes from using $-3^2$, which equals $-9$, a different number.
