The product $2a \cdot 3ab \cdot b^2$ is a monomial. What is its standard form?

- $6a^2b^2$
- $6a^2b^3$
- $5a^2b^3$
- $6ab^3$

## Explanation

Multiply the coefficients to obtain $2 \cdot 3 = 6$. Combine powers of the same variable by adding their exponents, so $a \cdot a = a^2$ and $b \cdot b^2 = b^3$. The standard form is $6a^2b^3$.
