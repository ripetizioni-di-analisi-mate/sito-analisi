What is the product of $x + 1$ and $x^2 - x + 1$ in simplified form?

- $x^3 - 1$
- $x^3 + x^2 + 1$
- $x^3 + x + 1$
- $x^3 + 1$

## Explanation

Multiply the terms to obtain $x^3 - x^2 + x + x^2 - x + 1$. The terms $-x^2$ and $x^2$ are opposites, as are $x$ and $-x$. This leaves $x^3 + 1$. The degree of the product is $1 + 2 = 3$, as expected.
