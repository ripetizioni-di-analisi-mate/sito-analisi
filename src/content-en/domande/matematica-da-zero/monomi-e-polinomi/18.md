What is the opposite of the monomial $-\dfrac{3}{4}ab^2$?

- $-\dfrac{4}{3}ab^2$
- $-\dfrac{3}{4}a^2b^2$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}ab^2$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}ab^2$

## Explanation

Two monomials are opposites when their variable parts are the same and their coefficients are opposites. The opposite of $-\dfrac{3}{4}$ is $\dfrac{3}{4}$, and the variable part $ab^2$ stays the same. The number $\dfrac{4}{3}$ is the reciprocal of the coefficient, which is different.
