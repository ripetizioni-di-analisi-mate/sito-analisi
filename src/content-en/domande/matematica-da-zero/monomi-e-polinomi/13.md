What is the coefficient of the monomial $-x^3y$?

- $-1$
- $-3$
- $1$
- $3$

## Explanation

The monomial $-x^3y$ can be read as $(-1) \cdot x^3y$ because a minus sign before a variable is equivalent to multiplication by $-1$. The coefficient is therefore $-1$. The exponent $3$ belongs to the variable part, not to the coefficient.
