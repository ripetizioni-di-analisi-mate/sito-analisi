Two polynomials in one variable have degrees $3$ and $4$. What is the degree of their product?

- $7$
- $8$
- $4$
- $12$

## Explanation

The highest-degree term of the product is the product of the two highest-degree terms. Their exponents add, so the degree is $3 + 4 = 7$. For example, $x^3 \cdot x^4 = x^7$. The value $12$ is the product of the degrees rather than their sum.
