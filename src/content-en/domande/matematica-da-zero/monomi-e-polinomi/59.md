In the figure, which polynomial represents the total area of the rectangle with side lengths $x$ and $x + 4$?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 linee="0,0 7,0 7,3 0,3 0,0;3,0 3,3" etichette="1.2,1.3,x²;4.8,1.3,4x;1.4,-0.6,x;5,-0.6,4;-0.6,1.3,x" xmin=-1 xmax=8 ymin=-1 ymax=4}

- $x^2 + 4$
- $2x + 4$
- $x^2 + 4x$
- $x^2 + 4x + 4$

## Explanation

The total area is the sum of the areas of the two parts. The square with side length $x$ has area $x^2$ and the rectangle with side lengths $x$ and $4$ has area $4x$. Therefore, the area is $x^2 + 4x$. Applying the distributive property to $x(x + 4)$ gives the same result.
