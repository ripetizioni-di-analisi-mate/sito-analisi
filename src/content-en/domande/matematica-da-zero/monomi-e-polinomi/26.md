What is the product $(-4a^2b) \cdot (5ab^3)$?

- $-20a^3b^4$
- $20a^3b^4$
- $a^3b^4$
- $-20a^3b^3$

## Explanation

The coefficient is $-4 \cdot 5 = -20$. For the variables, add the exponents, giving $a^2 \cdot a = a^3$ and $b \cdot b^3 = b^4$. The result is $-20a^3b^4$. The monomial $a^3b^4$ comes from adding the coefficients, $-4 + 5 = 1$, whereas the product requires multiplying them.
