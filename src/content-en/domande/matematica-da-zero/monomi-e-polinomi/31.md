Simplify the expression $2x \cdot 3x^2 - x^3$. What is the result?

- $5x^3$
- $6x^3$
- $5x^2$
- $5x^6$

## Explanation

The product $2x \cdot 3x^2$ equals $6x^3$. The monomials $6x^3$ and $x^3$ are like terms, so subtract the coefficient $1$ from the coefficient $6$ to obtain $5x^3$. The value $5x^6$ comes from adding the exponents in a subtraction as well.
