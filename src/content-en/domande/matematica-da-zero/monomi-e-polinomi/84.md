We divide $8x^3y^2+6x^2y^k$ by $2x^2y^2$, with $xy\ne0$. What is the smallest nonnegative integer $k$ for which the quotient is a polynomial in $x,y$?

- $0$
- $2$
- $1$
- $3$

## Explanation

The first term gives $4x$ and the second gives $3y^{k-2}$. The second exponent must be at least zero. The smallest possible $k$ is $2$, giving the quotient $4x+3$.
