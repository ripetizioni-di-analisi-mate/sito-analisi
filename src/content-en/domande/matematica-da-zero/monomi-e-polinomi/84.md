The quotient $\dfrac{12x^4y^3 - 8x^3y^2}{4x^2y^2}$, with $x$ and $y$ both nonzero, equals which polynomial?

- $8x^2y - 4x$
- $3x^2y - 2x$
- $3x^2y - 2x^2$
- $3x^2y - 8x$

## Explanation

Divide each term by $4x^2y^2$. The first gives $\dfrac{12}{4}x^{4-2}y^{3-2} = 3x^2y$ and the second gives $-\dfrac{8}{4}x^{3-2}y^{2-2} = -2x$. The quotient is $3x^2y - 2x$ because $y^0 = 1$ in the second term. The value $-2x^2$ comes from incorrectly subtracting the exponent of $x$, while $-8x$ omits the division of the coefficient $8$ by $4$.
