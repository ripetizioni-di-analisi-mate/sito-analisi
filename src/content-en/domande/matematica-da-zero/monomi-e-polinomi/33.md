A square flower bed has side length $3x$ meters and a square pond with side length $x$ meters at its center, as shown in the figure. How many square meters are covered with grass?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 linee="0,0 6,0 6,6 0,6 0,0;2,2 4,2 4,4 2,4 2,2" etichette="2.7,-0.6,3x;-0.8,2.7,3x;2.7,1.3,x;4.4,2.8,x" xmin=-1.5 xmax=7 ymin=-1 ymax=7}

- $2x^2$
- $8x$
- $4x^2$
- $8x^2$

## Explanation

The area of the bed is $(3x)^2 = 9x^2$ and the area of the pond is $x^2$. The grass covers the difference, $9x^2 - x^2 = 8x^2$ square meters. The value $4x^2$ is the area of a square with side length $3x - x = 2x$, which is different from the region in question.
