A rectangular field has sides $a$ and $b$ meters long, and its perimeter is $2a + 2b$. What is its perimeter in meters when $a = 12$ and $b = 8$?

- $96$
- $20$
- $32$
- $40$

## Explanation

Substituting the values for the variables gives $2 \cdot 12 + 2 \cdot 8 = 24 + 16 = 40$. The perimeter is therefore $40$ meters. The value $96$ is the product $12 \cdot 8$, the area of the field, which is a different quantity.
