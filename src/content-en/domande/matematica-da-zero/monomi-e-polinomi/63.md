What is the expansion of $(x - 5)^2$?

- $x^2 + 10x + 25$
- $x^2 - 10x - 25$
- $x^2 - 10x + 25$
- $x^2 - 25$

## Explanation

The square of a binomial is the sum of the squares of the two terms and twice their product. Twice the product is $2 \cdot x \cdot (-5) = -10x$, and the square of $-5$ is $+25$. The expansion is $x^2 - 10x + 25$.
