With respect to the variable $y$, what is the degree of the monomial $4x^3y^2z$?

- $6$
- $3$
- $1$
- $2$

## Explanation

The degree in a variable is its exponent. The variable $y$ has exponent $2$, so the degree in $y$ is $2$. The value $6$ is the total degree, namely $3 + 2 + 1$.
