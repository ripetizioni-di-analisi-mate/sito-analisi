Evaluate $(2x)^2 \cdot x$ for $x = 3$. What is its value?

- $36$
- $54$
- $108$
- $18$

## Explanation

The square $(2x)^2$ equals $4x^2$, so the monomial is $4x^3$. For $x = 3$, we have $4 \cdot 27 = 108$. The value $54$ comes from using $2x^3$, which squares only the variable and leaves the coefficient $2$ unchanged.
