In the figure, four parts form a rectangle with side lengths $x + 5$ and $x + 1$. Which polynomial gives its area?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 linee="0,0 9,0 9,5 0,5 0,0;4,0 4,5;0,4 9,4" etichette="1.7,1.8,x²;6.3,1.8,5x;1.8,4.35,x;6.3,4.35,5;1.9,-0.6,x;6.3,-0.6,5;-0.6,1.8,x;-0.6,4.35,1" xmin=-1 xmax=10 ymin=-1 ymax=6}

- $x^2 + 4x + 5$
- $2x + 6$
- $x^2 + 5$
- $x^2 + 6x + 5$

## Explanation

The area is the sum of the four parts, $x^2 + 5x + x + 5$. The terms $5x$ and $x$ are like terms and give $6x$. The area is $x^2 + 6x + 5$. Expanding $(x + 5)(x + 1)$ gives the same polynomial.
