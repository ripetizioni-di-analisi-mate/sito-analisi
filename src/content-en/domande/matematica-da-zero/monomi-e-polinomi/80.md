For $a = 1$, $b = -2$, and $c = 3$, what is $(a + b + c)^2$?

- $4$
- $14$
- $8$
- $2$

## Explanation

The sum of the three numbers is $1 - 2 + 3 = 2$, so its square equals $4$. Checking with the formula gives $1 + 4 + 9 - 4 + 6 - 12 = 4$. The value $14$ is the sum of the squares alone and omits twice the product of each pair.
