A square sign has side length $2x - 1$ decimeters. Which polynomial gives its area in square decimeters?

- $4x^2 + 4x + 1$
- $2x^2 - 2x + 1$
- $4x^2 - 1$
- $4x^2 - 4x + 1$

## Explanation

The area is the square of the side length, so it is $(2x - 1)^2$. Add the squares of the two terms, $(2x)^2 = 4x^2$ and $(-1)^2 = 1$, and twice their product, $2 \cdot 2x \cdot (-1) = -4x$. The area is $4x^2 - 4x + 1$ square decimeters. The value $4x^2 - 1$ instead comes from multiplying a sum by a difference.
