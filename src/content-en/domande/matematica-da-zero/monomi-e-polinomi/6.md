What is $-a^2$ when $a = -4$?

- $-16$
- $8$
- $-8$
- $16$

## Explanation

Evaluate the power before applying the minus sign. The number $a^2 = (-4)^2$ equals $16$ because the product of two negative numbers is positive. The minus sign in front reverses the sign of the result, so $-a^2 = -16$.
