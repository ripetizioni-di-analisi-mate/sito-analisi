A taxi charges a base fare of $3$ euros plus $2$ euros per kilometer, so a trip of $x$ kilometers costs $3 + 2x$ euros. How much does a trip of $9$ kilometers cost?

- $12$
- $45$
- $27$
- $21$

## Explanation

Substituting $x = 9$ gives $3 + 2 \cdot 9 = 3 + 18 = 21$ euros. The value $45$ comes from first adding $3 + 2 = 5$ and then multiplying by $9$, but the base fare is charged only once for the entire trip.
