A rectangle has area $6x^2 + 9x$ square meters and base $3x$ meters. Which polynomial gives its height in meters?

- $2x + 3$
- $2x + 9$
- $2x^2 + 3$
- $18x^3 + 27x^2$

## Explanation

The height is the area divided by the base, so it is $\dfrac{6x^2 + 9x}{3x}$. Divide each term by $3x$ to obtain $\dfrac{6x^2}{3x} = 2x$ and $\dfrac{9x}{3x} = 3$. The height is $2x + 3$ meters. Checking gives $3x(2x + 3) = 6x^2 + 9x$.
