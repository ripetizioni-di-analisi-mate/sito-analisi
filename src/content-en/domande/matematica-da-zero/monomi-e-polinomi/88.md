Which monomial, when multiplied by $2a - 3b$, gives the product $8a^3b^2 - 12a^2b^3$?

- $4a^2b^2$
- $4a^2b$
- $4a^3b^2$
- $6a^2b^2$

## Explanation

The desired monomial is the quotient $\dfrac{8a^3b^2 - 12a^2b^3}{2a - 3b}$. Factoring out $4a^2b^2$ gives $8a^3b^2 - 12a^2b^3 = 4a^2b^2(2a - 3b)$. Checking the product gives $4a^2b^2 \cdot 2a = 8a^3b^2$ and $4a^2b^2 \cdot (-3b) = -12a^2b^3$.
