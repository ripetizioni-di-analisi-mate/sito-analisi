When evaluating $\dfrac{x + 1}{x(x - 5)}$, which values of $x$ must be excluded?

- $x = 0$ and $x = 5$
- $x = -1$ and $x = 5$
- Only $x = 5$
- $x = 0$ and $x = -5$

## Explanation

The denominator $x(x - 5)$ is a product, so it is zero when either factor is zero. The factor $x$ is zero for $x = 0$, and the factor $x - 5$ is zero for $x = 5$. The value $x = -1$ makes the numerator zero, which is allowed.
