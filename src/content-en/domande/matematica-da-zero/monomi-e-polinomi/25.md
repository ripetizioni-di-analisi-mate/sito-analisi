Which expression is equivalent to $4x^2 + 3x^2 + 2x$?

- $7x^3$
- $9x^2$
- $7x^2 + 2x$
- $12x^2$

## Explanation

The monomials $4x^2$ and $3x^2$ are like terms, and their sum is $7x^2$. The monomial $2x$ is unlike the others because its exponent is different, so it remains separate. The result is $7x^2 + 2x$. For $x = 1$ the values on the two sides are $4 + 3 + 2 = 9$ and $7 + 2 = 9$.
