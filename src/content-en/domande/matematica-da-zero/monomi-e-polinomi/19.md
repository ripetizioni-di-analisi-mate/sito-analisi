The monomials $4x^2y$ and $4xy^2$ have the same coefficient. Which statement about them is true?

- They are like terms because they have the same degree
- They have the same degree but are not like terms
- They have different degrees
- They are opposites

## Explanation

Both monomials have total degree $2 + 1 = 3$. They are not like terms because the variable part of the first is $x^2y$ and the second has a different variable part, $xy^2$. To be opposites, they would have to be like terms with opposite coefficients.
