What is $x^3 - 2x^2 + x - 5$ for $x = 2$?

- $-5$
- $-3$
- $-8$
- $2$

## Explanation

Substituting $x = 2$ gives $2^3 - 2 \cdot 2^2 + 2 - 5 = 8 - 8 + 2 - 5 = -3$. The value $2$ comes from omitting the constant term $-5$. The value $-5$ is the constant term alone, with the other terms left out.
