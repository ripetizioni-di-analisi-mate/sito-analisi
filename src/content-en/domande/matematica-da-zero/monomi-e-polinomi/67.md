Calculate $48 \cdot 52$ mentally by writing it as the product of a sum and a difference. What is its value?

- $2504$
- $2596$
- $2500$
- $2496$

## Explanation

Write $48 \cdot 52 = (50 - 2)(50 + 2)$. The product is the difference of the squares, $50^2 - 2^2 = 2500 - 4 = 2496$. The value $2504$ comes from adding the squares instead of subtracting them.
