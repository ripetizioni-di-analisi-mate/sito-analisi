In the polynomial $2x^2y - xy^2 + 7$, what is the highest exponent of the variable $x$?

- $3$
- $7$
- $2$
- $1$

## Explanation

The degree in a variable is its highest exponent. The variable $x$ has exponent $2$ in the first term and exponent $1$ in the second. The degree in $x$ is therefore $2$. The value $3$ is the total degree of the polynomial.
