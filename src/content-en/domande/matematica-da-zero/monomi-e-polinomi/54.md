Multiplying the binomial $a + 2$ by the trinomial $a^2 - 2a + 4$ gives a polynomial of degree $3$. Which one?

- $a^3 + 4a^2 + 8$
- $a^3 - 8$
- $a^3 + 8$
- $a^3 - 4a^2 + 8$

## Explanation

Multiply each term of $a + 2$ by the trinomial to get $a^3 - 2a^2 + 4a + 2a^2 - 4a + 8$. The terms $-2a^2$ and $2a^2$ are opposites, as are $4a$ and $-4a$. This leaves $a^3 + 8$.
