Given that the product of a sum and a difference gives the difference of the squares, what is $\left( 2x^2 - 3 \right)\left( 2x^2 + 3 \right)$?

- $4x^4 + 9$
- $4x^4 - 9$
- $2x^4 - 9$
- $4x^2 - 9$

## Explanation

The product is the difference of the squares of the two terms. The square of $2x^2$ is $4x^4$ because $(x^2)^2 = x^4$ and $2^2 = 4$, while the square of $3$ is $9$. The result is $4x^4 - 9$.
