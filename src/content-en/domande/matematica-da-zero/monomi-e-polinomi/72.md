A square card of side $x$ is reshaped into a rectangle by lengthening one side by $3$ cm and shortening the other by $3$ cm, where $x>3$ cm. How does the area change?

- It stays the same.
- It always decreases by $9\,\mathrm{cm^2}$.
- It decreases by $6x$ square centimetres, with $x$ in centimetres.
- It always increases by $9\,\mathrm{cm^2}$.

## Explanation

Measuring sides in centimetres, the new area is $(x+3)(x-3)=x^2-9$. The linear terms cancel, but the product of the two changes is negative. The area decreases by nine square centimetres.
