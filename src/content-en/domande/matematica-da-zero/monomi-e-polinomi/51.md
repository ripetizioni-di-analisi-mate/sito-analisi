Subtracting $3x - 2$ from $7$ and then adding $x$ gives $7 - (3x - 2) + x$. Which simplified polynomial is equivalent to this expression?

- $5 - 2x$
- $9 - 2x$
- $9 + 4x$
- $9 - 4x$

## Explanation

The minus sign reverses the signs of both enclosed terms, so $-(3x - 2) = -3x + 2$. The expression becomes $7 - 3x + 2 + x$, or $9 - 2x$. The value $5 - 2x$ comes from leaving $-2$ unchanged instead of reversing its sign.
