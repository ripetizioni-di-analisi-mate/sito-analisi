Which monomial has coefficient $-1$, total degree $4$, and variable part $a^2b^2$?

- $-4a^2b^2$
- $-a^2b^3$
- $-a^2b^2$
- $a^2b^2$

## Explanation

The coefficient $-1$ and the variable part $a^2b^2$ give the monomial $-a^2b^2$. Its degree is $2 + 2 = 4$, as required. The monomial $a^2b^2$ has coefficient $1$, whereas $-a^2b^3$ has degree $5$.
