Only one of the following equalities fails to hold for all values of $a$ and $b$. Which one?

- $(a + b)(a - b) = a^2 - b^2$
- $(a - b)^2 = a^2 - 2ab + b^2$
- $(b - a)^2 = (a - b)^2$
- $(a + b)^2 = a^2 + b^2$

## Explanation

The square of $a + b$ also contains twice the product, $2ab$, so $(a + b)^2$ differs from $a^2 + b^2$. With $a = b = 1$ we have $(1 + 1)^2 = 4$, whereas $1 + 1 = 2$. The other three equalities hold for every $a$ and $b$.
