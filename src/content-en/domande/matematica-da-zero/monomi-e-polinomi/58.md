A trip costs $12n + 30$ euros for $n$ students, and the group receives a discount of $2n + 5$ euros. Which polynomial gives the amount paid?

- $14n + 35$
- $10n + 30$
- $10n + 25$
- $10n + 35$

## Explanation

Subtract the discount from the cost to get $12n + 30 - (2n + 5)$. The minus sign reverses the signs of both terms of the discount, so $12n + 30 - 2n - 5$. Combining like terms gives $10n + 25$ euros.
