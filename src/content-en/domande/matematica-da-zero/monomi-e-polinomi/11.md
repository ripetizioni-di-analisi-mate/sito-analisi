Only one of the following expressions is not a monomial. Which one?

- $7$
- $-4a^2b$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}xy^3$
- $\dfrac{5}{x}$

## Explanation

A monomial contains only products of numbers and variables with natural number exponents. The expression $\dfrac{5}{x}$ has a variable in the denominator, so it is not a monomial. The number $7$ is a monomial without a variable part.
