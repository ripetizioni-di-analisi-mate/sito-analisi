Counting the variable factors in each term, what is the degree of the polynomial $x^3y - 4x^2y^3 + 2xy$?

- $3$
- $5$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

The degree of a polynomial is the highest of the degrees of its terms. The three terms have degrees $3 + 1 = 4$, $2 + 3 = 5$, and $1 + 1 = 2$. The highest degree is $5$. The value $3$ is the degree in the variable $x$ alone.
