What is the total degree of the monomial $5a^2b^3c$?

- $3$
- $6$
- $5$
- $11$

## Explanation

The total degree is the sum of the exponents of all the variables. The variable $c$ has no written exponent, so its exponent is $1$ and the sum is $2 + 3 + 1 = 6$. The coefficient $5$ does not contribute to the degree. Adding it by mistake gives $11$.
