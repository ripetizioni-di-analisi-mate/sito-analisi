A nonzero monomial in $x$ and $y$ changes sign when $x$ is replaced by $-x$, but keeps its sign when $y$ is replaced by $-y$. Which monomial could it be?

- $-3x^3y^2$
- $-3x^2y^3$
- $3x^2y^2$
- $3xy^3$

## Explanation

An odd exponent makes its factor change sign when the base changes sign. The exponent of $x$ must therefore be odd and the exponent of $y$ even. A negative coefficient does not affect this test.
