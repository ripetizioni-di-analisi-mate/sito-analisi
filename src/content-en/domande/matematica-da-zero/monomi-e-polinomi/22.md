The product $(-3x) \cdot 2xy \cdot (-y^2)$ can be written as a monomial in standard form. Which one?

- $6x^2y^3$
- $5x^2y^3$
- $6x^2y^2$
- $-6x^2y^3$

## Explanation

The coefficient is $(-3) \cdot 2 \cdot (-1) = 6$ because the two negative factors give a positive product. For the variables, $x \cdot x = x^2$ and $y \cdot y^2 = y^3$. The monomial is therefore $6x^2y^3$. A minus sign occurs only when there is an odd number of negative factors.
