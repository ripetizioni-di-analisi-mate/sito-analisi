For $x = 3$, what is $\dfrac{x^3 - 2x^2 + x}{x}$?

- $4$
- $6$
- $-6$
- $12$

## Explanation

Dividing each term by $x$ gives $x^2 - 2x + 1$. For $x = 3$ this equals $9 - 6 + 1 = 4$. Substituting the numbers provides a check: the numerator equals $27 - 18 + 3 = 12$ and $\dfrac{12}{3} = 4$. The value $-6$ comes from dividing only the first term.
