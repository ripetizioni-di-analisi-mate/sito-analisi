For $a \neq 0$ and $b \neq 0$, what is the quotient $\dfrac{-18a^4b^3}{6ab}$?

- $3a^3b^2$
- $-12a^3b^2$
- $-3a^5b^4$
- $-3a^3b^2$

## Explanation

Divide the coefficients, $-18 : 6 = -3$, and subtract the exponents of like variables, giving $a^{4-1} = a^3$ and $b^{3-1} = b^2$. The result is $-3a^3b^2$. Exponents are subtracted in division and added in multiplication.
