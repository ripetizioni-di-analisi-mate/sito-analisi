A rectangular pane of glass is cut to a length of $4a$ meters and a height of $3a^2$ meters for a window. Which monomial gives its area in square meters?

- $7a^2$
- $12a^3$
- $7a^3$
- $12a^2$

## Explanation

The area is the product of the base and the height, so it is $4a \cdot 3a^2$. The coefficients give $4 \cdot 3 = 12$ and the powers of $a$ give $a^{1+2} = a^3$. The area is $12a^3$ square meters.
