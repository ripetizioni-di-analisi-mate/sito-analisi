Which polynomial is equivalent to $(x + 1)^3 - \left( x^3 + 1 \right)$?

- $3x^2$
- $3x^2 + 3x$
- $3x^2 + 3x + 2$
- $0$

## Explanation

The cube of the binomial is $x^3 + 3x^2 + 3x + 1$. Subtracting $x^3 + 1$ reverses the signs of both terms, leaving $3x^2 + 3x$. The cube of a sum differs from the sum of the cubes, so the difference is not $0$.
