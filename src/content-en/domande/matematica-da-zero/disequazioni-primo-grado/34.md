With decimal coefficients, $0.5x-1.2>0.3x+0.4$ is equivalent to

- $x>0.8$
- $x<8$
- $x>-8$
- $x>8$

## Explanation

Moving $0.3x$ to the left-hand side and $-1.2$ to the right gives $0.5x-0.3x>0.4+1.2$. This yields $0.2x>1.6$. Dividing by $0.2$, which is positive, preserves the direction, and $\dfrac{1.6}{0.2}=8$. The solutions are $x>8$. As a check, when $x=10$ we have $3.8>3.4$, which is true.
