If we divide by $-4$ on both sides of the inequality $-12\leq20$, which statement do we obtain?

- $3\leq-5$
- $3\geq-5$
- $-3\leq5$
- $-3\geq5$

## Explanation

The calculations give $\dfrac{-12}{-4}=3$ and $\dfrac{20}{-4}=-5$. The divisor $-4$ is negative, so the inequality reverses and $\leq$ becomes $\geq$. This gives $3\geq-5$, which is true because $3$ lies to the right of $-5$ on the number line. Failing to reverse the inequality would give $3\leq-5$, which is false.
