One inequality in a system is always true. For example,

$$
\begin{cases}
x+2>x \\
3x<9
\end{cases}
$$

What is the solution set?

- $\emptyset$
- $(-\infty,3)$
- $\mathbb{R}$
- $(-\infty,3]$

## Explanation

The first inequality becomes $0\cdot x>-2$, that is, the comparison $0>-2$, which is true, so every number satisfies it. The second gives $x<3$. The solutions of the system are those of the second inequality, so $(-\infty,3)$. For example, $x=2$ satisfies $4>2$ and $6<9$.
