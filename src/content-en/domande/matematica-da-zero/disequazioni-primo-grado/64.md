For what values of $x$ is the expression $4-2x$ positive?

- $x>2$
- $x<-2$
- $x<2$
- $x>-2$

## Explanation

The expression is positive when $4-2x>0$. Moving the terms gives $-2x>-4$, and dividing by $-2$ reverses the inequality and gives $x<2$. The coefficient of $x$ is negative, so the expression is positive to the left of its zero. When $x=0$, its value is $4$.
