What is the solution set of the system

$$
\begin{cases}
3x>9 \\
x-1\leq6
\end{cases}
$$

- $[3,7]$
- $(3,7)$
- $(3,7]$
- $(7,+\infty)$

## Explanation

The first inequality gives $x>3$ and the second gives $x\leq7$. The solutions of the system are the numbers satisfying both, that is, $3<x\leq7$. The endpoint $3$ is excluded and $7$ is included, so the interval is $(3,7]$.
