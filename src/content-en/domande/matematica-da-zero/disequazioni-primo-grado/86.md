Which inequality represents the statement “twice a number, increased by $3$, does not exceed $11$”?

- $2x+3\geq11$
- $2(x+3)\leq11$
- $2x+3<11$
- $2x+3\leq11$

## Explanation

Twice a number is $2x$, and increasing it by $3$ gives $2x+3$. The expression “does not exceed” means “is less than or equal to,” so we use $\leq$. When $x=4$, we have $11$, which does not exceed $11$ because equality is allowed.
