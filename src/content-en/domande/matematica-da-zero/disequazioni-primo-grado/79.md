For which numbers is the fraction $\dfrac{3}{x+1}$ negative?

- $x>-1$
- $x<-1$
- $x<1$
- $x<-3$

## Explanation

The numerator $3$ is positive, so the quotient is negative only if the denominator is negative. The condition $x+1<0$ gives $x<-1$. When $x=-2$, the fraction equals $\dfrac{3}{-1}=-3$, which is negative. The number $-1$ makes the denominator zero and is excluded.
