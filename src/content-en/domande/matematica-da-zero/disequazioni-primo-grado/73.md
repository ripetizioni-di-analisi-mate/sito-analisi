Which set of values of $x$ makes the fraction $\dfrac{x-1}{x+3}$ positive?

- $-3<x<1$
- $x>1$
- $x<-3$ or $x>1$
- $x<-1$ or $x>3$

## Explanation

The numerator is zero at $x=1$, and the denominator is zero at $x=-3$. The quotient is positive when the numerator and denominator have the same sign, that is, for $x<-3$, where both are negative, and for $x>1$, where both are positive. The endpoints are excluded.
