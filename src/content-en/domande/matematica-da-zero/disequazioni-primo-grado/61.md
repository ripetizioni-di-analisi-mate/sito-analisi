In one inequality of the following system, the coefficient of $x$ is negative.

$$
\begin{cases}
-x>-3 \\
3x\geq-6
\end{cases}
$$

Which interval contains exactly the solutions of the system?

- $(-2,3]$
- $[-2,3)$
- $(3,+\infty)$
- $[-3,2)$

## Explanation

Multiplying the first inequality by $-1$ reverses its direction and gives $x<3$. Dividing the second by $3$ gives $x\geq-2$. The solutions are $-2\leq x<3$. The endpoint $-2$ is included and $3$ is excluded, so the interval is $[-2,3)$.
