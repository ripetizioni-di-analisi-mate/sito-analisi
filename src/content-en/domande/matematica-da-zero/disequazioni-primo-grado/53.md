The following two conditions form a system.

$$
\begin{cases}
2x-1\geq5 \\
x-4<0
\end{cases}
$$

Which values of $x$ satisfy both?

- $[3,4)$
- $(3,4]$
- $[3,4]$
- $(-\infty,4)$

## Explanation

The first inequality gives $2x\geq6$, that is, $x\geq3$. The second gives $x<4$. The numbers they have in common satisfy $3\leq x<4$. The endpoint $3$ is included and $4$ is excluded, so the interval is $[3,4)$. For example, $3$ and $3.5$ are solutions, whereas $4$ is not.
