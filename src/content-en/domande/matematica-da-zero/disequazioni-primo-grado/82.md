Admission to a pool costs $8$ euros. A membership costs $30$ euros, after which each visit costs $3$ euros. What is the minimum number of visits for which the membership costs less?

- $6$
- $8$
- $10$
- $7$

## Explanation

For $n$ visits the cost is $8n$ euros without a membership and $30+3n$ with one. We require $30+3n<8n$, that is, $5n>30$ and $n>6$. For $6$ visits both options cost the same, $48$ euros, so the membership costs less for $7$ visits or more.
