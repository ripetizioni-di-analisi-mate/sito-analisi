Which of these inequalities is satisfied by every real number?

- $2x+3<2x$
- $2x+3>x$
- $2x+3<2x+1$
- $2x+3>2x$

## Explanation

Subtracting $2x$ from both sides of $2x+3>2x$ leaves $3>0$, which is true for every $x$. The inequality $2x+3<2x$ becomes $3<0$, and $2x+3<2x+1$ becomes $3<1$, both false. The inequality $2x+3>x$ is equivalent to $x>-3$, which does not hold for every $x$.
