Solving $3x+2>2x-5$ gives the solutions

- $x>7$
- $x>-7$
- $x>-3$
- $x<-7$

## Explanation

Moving $2x$ to the left-hand side and $2$ to the right, with their signs changed, gives $3x-2x>-5-2$. Combining like terms gives $x>-7$. The direction does not change because we have not multiplied by a negative number. Dropping the sign of $2$ would give $x>-3$.
