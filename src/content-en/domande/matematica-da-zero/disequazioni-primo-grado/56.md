The system

$$
\begin{cases}
x\geq4 \\
x+2\leq3
\end{cases}
$$

is satisfied by

- all numbers greater than or equal to $4$
- only the number $1$
- no numbers
- all numbers less than or equal to $1$

## Explanation

The first inequality gives $x\geq4$ and the second gives $x\leq1$. A number greater than or equal to $4$ cannot be less than or equal to $1$. The two conditions have no numbers in common, so the system has no solutions.
