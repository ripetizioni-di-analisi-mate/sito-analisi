Solving $\dfrac{x-1}{2}\geq\dfrac{2x+1}{3}$ gives

- $x\geq-5$
- $x\leq-3$
- $x\geq5$
- $x\leq-5$

## Explanation

The least common multiple is $6$. Multiplying by $6$ gives $3(x-1)\geq2(2x+1)$, that is, $3x-3\geq4x+2$. Moving the terms gives $-x\geq5$. Multiplying by $-1$ reverses the inequality and gives $x\leq-5$. As a check, when $x=-6$ the two sides equal $-\dfrac{7}{2}$ and $-\dfrac{11}{3}$, and $-\dfrac{7}{2}\geq-\dfrac{11}{3}$ is true.
