An elevator can carry at most $600$ kg. It carries a cart weighing $90$ kg and several people weighing $75$ kg each. What is the maximum number of people who can ride?

- $7$
- $8$
- $6$
- $5$

## Explanation

With $n$ people, the weight is $90+75n$ kilograms, which must not exceed $600$. This gives $75n\leq510$, that is, $n\leq6.8$. The number of people is an integer, so the maximum is $6$. With $7$ people, the weight would be $615$ kg.
