Three of the following four inequalities have the same solutions as $2x-6<4$. Which one has different solutions?

- $x<5$
- $2x<10$
- $x>5$
- $x-3<2$

## Explanation

Adding $6$ to both sides gives $2x<10$. Dividing by $2$, which is positive, gives $x<5$. Dividing by $2$ on both sides of the original inequality gives $x-3<2$. All three have the same solutions. The inequality $x>5$ points in the opposite direction, so it has different solutions.
