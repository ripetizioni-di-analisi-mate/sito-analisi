How many integers belong to the interval $[-2,3)$?

- $4$
- $6$
- $3$
- $5$

## Explanation

The endpoint $-2$ is included and the endpoint $3$ is excluded. The integers in the interval are $-2$, $-1$, $0$, $1$, and $2$, giving $5$ numbers. Counting $3$ as well would give $6$, but $3$ does not belong to the interval. The number $-2$ is counted because the square bracket includes it.
