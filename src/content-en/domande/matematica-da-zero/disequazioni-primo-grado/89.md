Plan A costs $F$ euros per month plus $0.10$ euros per minute. Plan B costs $0.25$ euros per minute. What is the largest fee $F$ for which A costs at most as much as B at every usage level of at least $40$ minutes?

- $4$ euros
- $10$ euros
- $6$ euros
- $15$ euros

## Explanation

For $m$ minutes we need $F\le(0.25-0.10)m=0.15m$. The tightest restriction occurs at the minimum usage, $m=40$. Thus $F\le6$ euros, with equality allowed.
