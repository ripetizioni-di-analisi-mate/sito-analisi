One phone plan charges $5$ euros per month plus $0.10$ euros per minute. Another charges $0.25$ euros per minute with no fixed fee. What is the minimum number of minutes per month for which the first plan costs less?

- $33$
- $35$
- $34$
- $50$

## Explanation

For $m$ minutes the first plan costs $5+0.1\,m$ and the second costs $0.25\,m$. We require $5+0.1\,m<0.25\,m$, so $0.15\,m>5$ and $m>\dfrac{100}{3}\approx33.3$. Minutes are counted in whole numbers, so the first plan costs less for $34$ minutes or more. For $34$ minutes the first plan costs $8.40$ euros and the second costs $8.50$ euros.
