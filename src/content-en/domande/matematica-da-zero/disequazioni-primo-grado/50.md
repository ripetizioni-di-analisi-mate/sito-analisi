Two fractions have the same denominator in the comparison $\dfrac{x}{2}-\dfrac{x-1}{2}>0$. For what values of $x$ is the inequality true?

- For no number
- For every real number
- Only for $x>1$
- Only for $x>0$

## Explanation

The two terms have the same denominator, so the left-hand side is $\dfrac{x-(x-1)}{2}=\dfrac{1}{2}$. The minus sign changes the sign of the entire numerator. The comparison $\dfrac{1}{2}>0$ is true, so every real number satisfies the inequality.
