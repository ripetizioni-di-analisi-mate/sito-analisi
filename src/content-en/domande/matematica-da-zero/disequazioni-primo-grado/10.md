Which statement about the interval $[-3,0)$ is true?

- It contains $0$ and does not contain $-3$
- It contains both $-3$ and $0$
- It contains $-3$ and does not contain $0$
- It contains neither $-3$ nor $0$

## Explanation

The square bracket on the left indicates that the endpoint $-3$ belongs to the interval. The parenthesis on the right indicates that the endpoint $0$ is excluded. The interval $[-3,0)$ therefore corresponds to the double inequality $-3\leq x<0$. For example, $-3$ and $-1$ belong to the interval, whereas $0$ and $1$ lie outside it.
