After multiplying by the denominator, which values of $x$ satisfy $\dfrac{x}{4}\geq-2$?

- $x\geq-\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $x\leq-8$
- $x\geq8$
- $x\geq-8$

## Explanation

We multiply both sides by $4$, which is positive, so the direction stays the same. This gives $x\geq-2\cdot4$, that is, $x\geq-8$. Dividing $-2$ by $4$ would give $x\geq-\dfrac{1}{2}$, but clearing the denominator requires multiplication.
