Which of these numbers is a solution of $\dfrac{x+5}{x-1}<0$?

- $-7$
- $1$
- $4$
- $-3$

## Explanation

The quotient is negative for $-5<x<1$. When $x=-3$, it equals $\dfrac{2}{-4}$, which is negative. When $x=-7$, it equals $\dfrac{-2}{-8}$, and when $x=4$, it equals $\dfrac{9}{3}$, both positive. The number $1$ makes the denominator zero, so the fraction is undefined. The numerator is zero at $x=-5$.
