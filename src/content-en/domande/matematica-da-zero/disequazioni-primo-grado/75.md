For which values of $x$ does $\dfrac{x-6}{2-x}\geq0$ hold?

- $2\leq x\leq6$
- $2<x\leq6$
- $x<2$ or $x\geq6$
- $x\geq6$

## Explanation

The numerator is positive for $x>6$, and the denominator is positive for $x<2$. The quotient is positive when they have the same sign, that is, for $2<x<6$, where both are negative. At $x=6$, the quotient is zero and this value is included. At $x=2$, the denominator is zero, so $2$ is excluded.
