A student solves $3x+5>8x-10$ by writing $3x-8x>-10-5$, then $-5x>-15$, then $x>3$. What error does her work contain?

- She divided by $-5$ without reversing the inequality
- She moved $5$ without changing its sign
- She calculated the difference $3x-8x$ incorrectly
- It contains no errors

## Explanation

The first two steps are correct: $3x-8x=-5x$ and $-10-5=-15$. The last step divides by $-5$, which is negative, so the inequality reverses and the solution is $x<3$. As a check, when $x=4$ the original inequality gives $17>22$, which is false.
