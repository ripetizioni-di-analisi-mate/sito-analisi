The sign chart for $\dfrac{x-5}{x+2}$ is

$$
\begin{array}{c|ccc}
 & x<-2 & -2<x<5 & x>5 \\ \hline
x-5 & - & - & + \\
x+2 & - & + & + \\ \hline
\dfrac{x-5}{x+2} & + & - & +
\end{array}
$$

What are the solutions of $\dfrac{x-5}{x+2}<0$?

- $-2<x<5$
- $x<-2$ or $x>5$
- $-2<x\leq5$
- $x<5$

## Explanation

The quotient is negative in the middle interval, where the numerator is negative and the denominator is positive. The symbol is $<$, so the number $5$, where the quotient is zero, is excluded. The number $-2$ is excluded because it makes the denominator zero.
