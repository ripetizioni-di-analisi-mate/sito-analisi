Which numbers simultaneously satisfy

$$
\begin{cases}
x+1\geq4 \\
2x\leq6
\end{cases}
$$

- No number
- All numbers greater than or equal to $3$
- Only the number $3$
- All numbers less than or equal to $3$

## Explanation

The first inequality gives $x\geq3$ and the second gives $x\leq3$. The common solutions are the numbers that are both greater than or equal to $3$ and less than or equal to $3$. The only such number is $3$ because both inequalities include the endpoint.
