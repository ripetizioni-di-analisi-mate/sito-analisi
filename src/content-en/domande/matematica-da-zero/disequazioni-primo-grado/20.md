Moving the term $7$ to the right-hand side of $x+7<3$ gives which solution?

- $x<10$
- $x>-4$
- $x<4$
- $x<-4$

## Explanation

The first principle lets us subtract $7$ from both sides, and the direction does not change. This gives $x<3-7$, that is, $x<-4$. The term $7$ moves to the right-hand side with its sign changed. Leaving its sign as $+$ would give $x<10$, which is incorrect.
