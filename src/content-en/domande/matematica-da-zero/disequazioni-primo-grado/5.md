Let $a$ and $b$ be two numbers with $a<b$. Which of these inequalities is true for every choice of $a$ and $b$?

- $-3a<-3b$
- $a-3>b-3$
- $\dfrac{a}{-3}<\dfrac{b}{-3}$
- $-3a>-3b$

## Explanation

Multiplying by $-3$, which is negative, reverses the inequality, so $-3a>-3b$. With $a=1$ and $b=4$, we have $-3>-12$. Subtracting $3$ preserves the direction and gives $a-3<b-3$. Dividing by $-3$ also reverses the inequality and gives $\dfrac{a}{-3}>\dfrac{b}{-3}$.
