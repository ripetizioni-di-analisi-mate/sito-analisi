In the system

$$
\begin{cases}
2x>6 \\
x-1\leq2
\end{cases}
$$

what is the solution set?

- $\{3\}$
- $(-\infty,3]$
- $(3,+\infty)$
- $\emptyset$

## Explanation

The first inequality gives $x>3$ and the second gives $x\leq3$. The endpoint $3$ is excluded by the first, so it is not a solution. A number greater than $3$ is not less than or equal to $3$. The two conditions have no numbers in common, and the set is empty.
