Which list puts $-2$, $-0.5$, $-1.5$, and $1$ in order from least to greatest?

- $-2,\ -1.5,\ -0.5,\ 1$
- $-0.5,\ -1.5,\ -2,\ 1$
- $1,\ -0.5,\ -1.5,\ -2$
- $-2,\ -0.5,\ -1.5,\ 1$

## Explanation

Negative numbers are less than $1$. The smallest negative number in the list is the one farthest to the left on the number line, namely $-2$, followed by $-1.5$ and finally $-0.5$, the one closest to zero. The increasing order is therefore $-2<-1.5<-0.5<1$.
