Which values of $x$ make the product $(2x-4)(x+1)$ nonnegative?

- $x\leq-1$ or $x\geq2$
- $-1\leq x\leq2$
- $x<-1$ or $x>2$
- $x\leq-2$ or $x\geq1$

## Explanation

The factor $2x-4$ is zero at $x=2$, and the factor $x+1$ is zero at $x=-1$. The product is positive outside the interval between the two zeros and equals zero at the zeros. The symbol $\geq$ includes the zeros, so the solutions are $x\leq-1$ or $x\geq2$.
