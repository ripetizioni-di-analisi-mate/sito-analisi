The product of $x$ and $x-6$ is nonpositive. For what values of $x$ does this happen?

- $0\leq x\leq6$
- $x\leq0$ or $x\geq6$
- $0<x<6$
- $x\leq6$

## Explanation

The two factors $x$ and $x-6$ are zero at $x=0$ and $x=6$. The product is negative when the factors have opposite signs, that is, between $0$ and $6$, and it equals zero at the endpoints. The symbol $\leq$ includes the endpoints, so $0\leq x\leq6$.
