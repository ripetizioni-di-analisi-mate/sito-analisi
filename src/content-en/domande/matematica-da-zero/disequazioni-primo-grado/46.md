The solutions of $4(x-2)\geq4x-8$ are

- no numbers
- the numbers $x\geq2$
- all real numbers
- the numbers $x>2$

## Explanation

The left-hand side is $4x-8$, equal to the right-hand side. Moving the terms gives $0\cdot x\geq0$, that is, the comparison $0\geq0$. Since the symbol $\geq$ allows equality, the comparison is true and every real number satisfies the inequality.
