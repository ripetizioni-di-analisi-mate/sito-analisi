We want to replace $-3x\geq12$ with an equivalent inequality in which $x$ is isolated. What do we obtain?

- $x\geq-4$
- $x\leq-4$
- $x\leq4$
- $x\geq4$

## Explanation

We divide both sides by $-3$. The divisor is negative, so the inequality reverses and $\geq$ becomes $\leq$. Since $\dfrac{12}{-3}=-4$, we obtain $x\leq-4$. As a check, when $x=-5$ we have $-3\cdot(-5)=15$, and $15\geq12$ is true.
