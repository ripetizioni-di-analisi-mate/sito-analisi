Solve $\dfrac{5}{x-2}>1$ by moving everything to the left-hand side.

- $2<x<7$
- $x<7$
- $x>7$
- $x<2$ or $x>7$

## Explanation

Subtracting $1$ gives $\dfrac{5-(x-2)}{x-2}>0$, that is, $\dfrac{7-x}{x-2}>0$. The numerator is positive for $x<7$ and the denominator is positive for $x>2$. The quotient is positive when both are positive, so for $2<x<7$. As a check, when $x=3$ the left-hand side equals $5$, which is greater than $1$.
