On a map, $1$ cm represents $5$ km. How long on the map are roads whose actual length exceeds $35$ km?

- More than $175$ cm
- Less than $7$ cm
- More than $30$ cm
- More than $7$ cm

## Explanation

A road measuring $L$ cm on the map is $5L$ km long. We require $5L>35$, and dividing by $5$ gives $L>7$. Roads longer than $35$ km therefore measure more than $7$ cm on the map. Multiplying $35$ by $5$ would give $175$, an incorrect value.
