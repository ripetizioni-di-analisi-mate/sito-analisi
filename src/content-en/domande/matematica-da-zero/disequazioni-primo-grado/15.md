## Explanation

The shaded portion lies to the left of $-1$ and continues indefinitely, so it starts at $-\infty$, with a parenthesis. The dot at $-1$ is solid, so $-1$ is included and takes a square bracket. The interval is $(-\infty,-1]$, that is, $x\leq-1$.
