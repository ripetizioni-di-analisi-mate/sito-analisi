The system

$$
\begin{cases}
3x<-3 \\
x<3
\end{cases}
$$

has solutions

- $(-\infty,3)$
- $(-1,3)$
- $\emptyset$
- $(-\infty,-1)$

## Explanation

Dividing the first inequality by $3$ gives $x<-1$. The second is $x<3$. Numbers less than $-1$ are also less than $3$, so they satisfy both. The solutions are $(-\infty,-1)$, the more restrictive condition. For example, $-2$ satisfies $-6<-3$ and $-2<3$.
