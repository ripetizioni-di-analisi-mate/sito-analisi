For the system $3x<-3$ and $x<3$, a student accepts a number if it satisfies at least one condition. Which value exposes the mistake, being accepted by the student but excluded by the system?

- $-2$
- $3$
- $4$
- $0$

## Explanation

The conditions are $x<-1$ and $x<3$. The value $0$ satisfies only the second. The student accepts it, while membership in the intersection requires both conditions.
