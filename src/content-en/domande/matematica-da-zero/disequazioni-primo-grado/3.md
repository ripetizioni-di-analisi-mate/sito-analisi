Given that $3<8$, which inequality results from multiplying both numbers by $-2$?

- $-6>-16$
- $-6<-16$
- $-16>-6$
- $6<16$

## Explanation

Multiplying by $-2$ gives $3\cdot(-2)=-6$ and $8\cdot(-2)=-16$. The factor $-2$ is negative, so the inequality reverses. On the number line $-6$ lies to the right of $-16$, so $-6>-16$. Keeping the original direction would give $-6<-16$, which is false.
