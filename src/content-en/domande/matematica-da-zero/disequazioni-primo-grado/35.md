The inequality $4x\geq7x-9$ is satisfied for

- $x\leq3$
- $x\geq3$
- $x\leq-3$
- $x\geq-3$

## Explanation

Moving $7x$ to the left-hand side gives $4x-7x\geq-9$, that is, $-3x\geq-9$. We divide by $-3$, which is negative, so the inequality reverses. The quotient $\dfrac{-9}{-3}$ equals $3$, so $x\leq3$. As a check, when $x=0$ we have $0\geq-9$, which is true.
