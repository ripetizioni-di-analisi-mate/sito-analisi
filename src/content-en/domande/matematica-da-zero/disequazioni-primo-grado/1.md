Two cities record low temperatures of $-7$ °C and $-3$ °C. Which of these statements is true?

- $-7>-3$
- $-3<-7$
- $0<-7$
- $-7<-3$

## Explanation

On the number line the greater number lies farther to the right, and $-7$ lies to the left of $-3$. Therefore $-7<-3$. The difference $-3-(-7)=-3+7=4$ is positive, confirming the comparison. The number $-7$ has the greater absolute value, but of two negative numbers the smaller is the one farther from zero.
