The inequality $2(x+3)\leq4x-2$ contains a product to expand. Which numbers satisfy it?

- $x\leq4$
- $x\geq-2$
- $x\geq4$
- $x\geq2$

## Explanation

Expanding the product gives $2x+6\leq4x-2$. Moving the terms gives $2x-4x\leq-2-6$, that is, $-2x\leq-8$. Dividing by $-2$, which is negative, reverses the inequality and gives $x\geq4$. Without the reversal we would obtain $x\leq4$. As a check, when $x=5$ we have $16\leq18$, which is true.
