Which numbers satisfy $9-4x<x-1$?

- $x<2$
- $x>-2$
- $x<-2$
- $x>2$

## Explanation

Moving the terms gives $-4x-x<-1-9$, that is, $-5x<-10$. Dividing by $-5$ reverses the inequality, so $x>2$. As a check, when $x=3$ the left-hand side equals $-3$ and the right-hand side equals $2$, and $-3<2$ is true.
