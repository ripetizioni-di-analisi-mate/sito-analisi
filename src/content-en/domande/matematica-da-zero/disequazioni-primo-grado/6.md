Only one of these statements is false. Which one?

- $4\geq4$
- $-5<-2$
- $-1\leq-3$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}>\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

The number $a$ is less than or equal to $b$ when $b-a\geq0$. For $-1\leq-3$, the difference is $-3-(-1)=-2$, which is negative, so the statement is false. The others are true: $4=4$, $-5$ lies to the left of $-2$, and $\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{3}{6}$ exceeds $\dfrac{1}{3}=\dfrac{2}{6}$.
