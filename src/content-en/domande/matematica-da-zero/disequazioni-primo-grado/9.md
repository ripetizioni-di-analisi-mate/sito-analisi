An amusement ride requires a height of at least $120$ cm. Which interval describes the permitted heights in centimeters?

- $[120,+\infty)$
- $(120,+\infty)$
- $(-\infty,120]$
- $(-\infty,120)$

## Explanation

“At least $120$” means $x\geq120$. The value $120$ is allowed, so the endpoint is included and takes a square bracket. Heights can increase without bound, so the interval extends to the right toward $+\infty$, with a parenthesis. We write $[120,+\infty)$.
