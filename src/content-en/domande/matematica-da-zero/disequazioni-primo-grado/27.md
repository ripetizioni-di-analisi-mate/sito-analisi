The inequality $-\dfrac{x}{2}>3$ has solutions

- $x<-6$
- $x>-6$
- $x<6$
- $x>6$

## Explanation

We multiply both sides by $-2$. The factor is negative, so the inequality reverses and $>$ becomes $<$. The left-hand side becomes $x$ and the right-hand side becomes $3\cdot(-2)=-6$. This gives $x<-6$. When $x=-8$, we have $-\dfrac{-8}{2}=4$, and $4>3$ is true.
