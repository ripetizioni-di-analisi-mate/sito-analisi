What are the solutions of

$$
\begin{cases}
x-1>1 \\
2x>10
\end{cases}
$$

- $(5,+\infty)$
- $(2,+\infty)$
- $(2,5)$
- $\emptyset$

## Explanation

The first inequality gives $x>2$ and the second gives $x>5$. A number satisfies both if it is greater than $5$ because every number greater than $5$ is also greater than $2$. The solutions of the system are therefore $(5,+\infty)$.
