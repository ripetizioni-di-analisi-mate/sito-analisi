A rectangle has height $4$ cm and base $x$ cm. For which values of $x$ is the area at least $36$ cm²?

- $x\leq9$
- $x\geq9$
- $x>9$
- $x\geq32$

## Explanation

The area is $4x$ and must be at least $36$, so $4x\geq36$. Dividing by $4$, which is positive, preserves the direction and gives $x\geq9$. The symbol includes $9$ because with a base of $9$ cm the area is exactly $36$ cm².
