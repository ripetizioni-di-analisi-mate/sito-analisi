After expanding the square and moving the terms, the inequality $(x+3)^2\leq x^2+2x+13$ has solutions

- $x\geq1$
- $x\leq1$
- $x\geq-2$
- $x\leq-1$

## Explanation

The square of the binomial is $(x+3)^2=x^2+6x+9$. The inequality becomes $x^2+6x+9\leq x^2+2x+13$. The terms in $x^2$ cancel, leaving $4x\leq4$. Dividing by $4$ gives $x\leq1$. Omitting twice the product, $6x$, would give a different result.
