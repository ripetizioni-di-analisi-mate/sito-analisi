Which of these inequalities is linear after simplification and transposition of terms?

- $x^2>4$
- $x(x-3)<2$
- $x(x+2)>x^2+6$
- $x^2-x>x$

## Explanation

Expanding the product gives $x^2+2x>x^2+6$. The terms in $x^2$ cancel, leaving $2x>6$, which is linear. The other three remain quadratic because $x^2>4$, $x^2-3x-2<0$, and $x^2-2x>0$ still contain the term $x^2$.
