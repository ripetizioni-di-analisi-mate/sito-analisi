What is the solution set of $5x-3(x+1)>2x-3$?

- $\mathbb{R}$
- $\emptyset$
- $x>0$
- $x\geq0$

## Explanation

The left-hand side becomes $5x-3x-3=2x-3$, equal to the right-hand side. The inequality reduces to $0\cdot x>0$, that is, the comparison $0>0$. The symbol $>$ excludes equality, so the comparison is false and the inequality is never true.
