## Explanation

The shaded segment extends from $-2$ to $3$. Both dots are solid, so both endpoints belong to the set and take square brackets. The interval is $[-2,3]$, that is, $-2\leq x\leq3$. For example, $-2$, $0$, and $3$ belong to the set, whereas $3.5$ lies outside it.
