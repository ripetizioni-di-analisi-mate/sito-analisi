Simplifying an inequality gives $0\cdot x\leq-7$. What can we conclude?

- It is always true because $0\cdot x$ equals zero
- The solutions are $x\leq-7$
- The solutions are $x\leq0$
- It is never true because $0\leq-7$ is false

## Explanation

The left-hand side $0\cdot x$ equals $0$ for every $x$, leaving a comparison between $0$ and $-7$. The statement $0\leq-7$ is false, so no number is a solution. The value $0$ on the left-hand side does not make the inequality true.
