The product of $x-2$ and $x+4$ must be positive. For which values of $x$ is this the case?

- $-4<x<2$
- $x>2$
- $x<-2$ or $x>4$
- $x<-4$ or $x>2$

## Explanation

The factors are zero at $x=2$ and $x=-4$. The product is positive when the two factors have the same sign. For $x>2$ both are positive, and for $x<-4$ both are negative. Between $-4$ and $2$ the signs are opposite. The symbol is $>$, so the endpoints are excluded.
