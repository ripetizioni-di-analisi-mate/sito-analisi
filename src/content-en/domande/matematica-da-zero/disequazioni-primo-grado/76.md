What is the domain restriction for the inequality $\dfrac{x+1}{2x-6}\geq0$?

- $x\neq-1$
- $x\neq3$
- $x\neq6$
- $x\neq-3$

## Explanation

A fraction is defined only if its denominator is nonzero. The denominator $2x-6$ is zero when $2x=6$, that is, when $x=3$. The domain restriction is therefore $x\neq3$. The value $x=-1$ instead makes the numerator zero, giving a quotient of zero.
