Which of the numbers $-7$, $-5$, $0$, and $4$ makes the expression $x+5$ negative?

- $-5$
- $0$
- $-7$
- $4$

## Explanation

The expression $x+5$ is zero when $x=-5$ and negative when $x<-5$. When $x=-7$, it equals $-2$, which is negative. When $x=-5$, it equals $0$, which is not negative. When $x=0$ and when $x=4$, its values are $5$ and $9$, both positive. Indeed, the inequality $x+5<0$ is equivalent to $x<-5$.
