For which numbers is the product $(x+1)(x-5)$ negative?

- $x<-1$ or $x>5$
- $-1<x<5$
- $1<x<5$
- $-5<x<1$

## Explanation

The factors are zero at $x=-1$ and $x=5$. The product is negative when the factors have opposite signs. This happens between the two zeros: when $x=0$, the factors equal $1$ and $-5$, and the product $-5$ is negative. The solutions are $-1<x<5$.
