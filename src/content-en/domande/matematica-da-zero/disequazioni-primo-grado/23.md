A student writes $4-x>1$, then $-x>-3$, then $x<3$. What operation did she perform in the last step?

- She multiplied both sides by $-1$ and reversed the inequality
- She multiplied both sides by $-1$ without reversing the inequality
- She added $3$ to both sides
- She divided both sides by $3$

## Explanation

We go from $-x>-3$ to $x<3$ by changing the sign of both sides, that is, by multiplying by $-1$. The factor is negative, so the inequality reverses and $>$ becomes $<$. The step is correct. Without the reversal, we would write $x>3$, which is incorrect.
