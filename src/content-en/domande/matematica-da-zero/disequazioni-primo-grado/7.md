Why does multiplication by $-1$ in the inequality $2<5$ give $-2>-5$ rather than $-2<-5$?

- Because $-2$ lies to the right of $-5$ on the number line
- Because $-5$ lies to the right of $-2$ on the number line
- Because the minus sign changes $2$ into $5$
- Because $-2$ is farther from zero than $-5$

## Explanation

Multiplying by $-1$ changes the sign of each number and reflects the number line about zero. The number $2$, which was to the left of $5$, becomes $-2$, which is to the right of $-5$. Therefore $-2>-5$. This is why the inequality reverses.
