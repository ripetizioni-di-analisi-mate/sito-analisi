For certain integers, subtracting $2$ from three times the integer gives a result greater than $4$ and less than $20$. How many such integers are there?

- $5$
- $4$
- $6$
- $7$

## Explanation

We write $4<3x-2<20$. Adding $2$ to all three expressions gives $6<3x<22$, and dividing by $3$ gives $2<x<\dfrac{22}{3}\approx7.3$. The integers are $3$, $4$, $5$, $6$, and $7$, giving $5$ numbers. The number $2$ is excluded.
