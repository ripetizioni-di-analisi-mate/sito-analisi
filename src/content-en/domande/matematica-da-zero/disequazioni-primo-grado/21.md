Which of these inequalities is equivalent to $5x>15$?

- $x>10$
- $x>3$
- $x<3$
- $x>75$

## Explanation

Two inequalities are equivalent if they have the same solutions. Dividing both sides of $5x>15$ by $5$, which is positive, preserves the direction and gives $x>3$. The inequalities $x>10$ and $x>75$ each have a smaller solution set, and $x<3$ has solutions in the opposite direction.
