Of the integers that make $2x+1>6-x$ true, which is the smallest?

- $1$
- $3$
- $2$
- $5$

## Explanation

Moving $-x$ to the left-hand side and $1$ to the right gives $3x>5$, that is, $x>\dfrac{5}{3}\approx1.67$. The first integer greater than $1.67$ is $2$. When $x=1$, the original inequality becomes $3>5$, which is false. When $x=2$, the inequality becomes $5>4$, which is true.
