Which of these numbers is a solution of the inequality $3x-2>10$?

- $4$
- $3$
- $5$
- $-4$

## Explanation

Substitute each number. When $x=5$, we obtain $3\cdot5-2=13$, and $13>10$ is true. When $x=4$, we obtain $10$, and $10>10$ is false because the symbol $>$ excludes equality. When $x=3$, we obtain $7$, and when $x=-4$, we obtain $-14$, both less than $10$.
