Write the solution set of $2x-1\leq5x+8$ in interval notation.

- $(-\infty,-3]$
- $[-3,+\infty)$
- $(-3,+\infty)$
- $[3,+\infty)$

## Explanation

Moving the terms gives $2x-5x\leq8+1$, that is, $-3x\leq9$. Dividing by $-3$ reverses the inequality, so $x\geq-3$. The symbol $\geq$ includes the endpoint, so the interval is $[-3,+\infty)$. As a check, when $x=0$ the left-hand side equals $-1$ and the right-hand side equals $8$, and $-1\leq8$ is true.
