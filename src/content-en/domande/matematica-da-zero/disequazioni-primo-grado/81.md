With $20$ euros, you want to buy a pen costing $2$ euros and several notebooks at $1.50$ euros each. What is the maximum number of notebooks you can buy?

- $13$
- $11$
- $12$
- $10$

## Explanation

With $n$ notebooks the total cost is $2+1.5\,n$ euros, which must not exceed $20$. We write $2+1.5\,n\leq20$, so $1.5\,n\leq18$ and $n\leq12$. With $12$ notebooks the total is exactly $20$ euros. With $13$ notebooks it would be $21.50$.
