After the square is expanded, what is the solution set of $(x-2)^2<x^2-4x$?

- $\mathbb{R}$
- $x<0$
- $x<1$
- $\emptyset$

## Explanation

The square is $(x-2)^2=x^2-4x+4$. The inequality becomes $x^2-4x+4<x^2-4x$. The terms in $x$ cancel, leaving the comparison $4<0$, which is false. The inequality is never true, and its solution set is empty. For example, when $x=0$ the two sides equal $4$ and $0$, and $4<0$ is false.
