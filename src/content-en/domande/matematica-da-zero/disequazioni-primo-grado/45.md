For which values of $x$ is the inequality $x+4\leq x-1$ true?

- For every real number
- For $x\leq-5$
- For no number, that is, $\emptyset$
- For $x\leq-1$

## Explanation

Moving $x$ to the left-hand side and $4$ to the right cancels the term in $x$, leaving $0\cdot x\leq-5$. The comparison $0\leq-5$ is false, so the inequality is never true. The solution set is empty.
