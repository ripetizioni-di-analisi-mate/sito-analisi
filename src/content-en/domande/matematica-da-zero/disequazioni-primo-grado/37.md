What is the solution of $3x-4\geq x-1$?

- $x\geq\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $x\geq\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $x\leq\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $x\geq3$

## Explanation

Moving $x$ to the left-hand side and $-4$ to the right gives $3x-x\geq-1+4$, that is, $2x\geq3$. We divide by $2$, which is positive, so the direction stays the same. The solutions are $x\geq\dfrac{3}{2}$. As a check, when $x=2$ we have $2\geq1$, which is true.
