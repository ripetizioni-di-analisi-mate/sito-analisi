The temperature of a liquid after $t$ minutes is $T(t)=20-2t$ degrees for $0\le t\le6$. What is the largest threshold $s$ for which $T(t)\ge s$ throughout this interval?

- $12$
- $8$
- $14$
- $20$

## Explanation

The coefficient of $t$ is negative, so temperature decreases. Its minimum is the final value, $T(6)=8$. Any higher threshold would fail at minute six, whereas $8$ works over the full interval.
