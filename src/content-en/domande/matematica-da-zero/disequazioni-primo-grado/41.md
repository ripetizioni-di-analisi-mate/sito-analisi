What is the largest integer that satisfies $4x-3<10$?

- $3$
- $4$
- $13$
- $2$

## Explanation

We obtain $4x<13$, that is, $x<\dfrac{13}{4}=3.25$. The integers less than $3.25$ are $3$, $2$, $1$, and so on, so the largest is $3$. The number $4$ is greater than $3.25$, and when $x=4$ we have $13<10$, which is false.
