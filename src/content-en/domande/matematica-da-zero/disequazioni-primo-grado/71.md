How many negative integers satisfy $(x+4)(x-1)<0$?

- $4$
- $2$
- $5$
- $3$

## Explanation

The product is negative between the zeros $-4$ and $1$, excluding the endpoints: $-4<x<1$. The negative integers in the interval are $-3$, $-2$, and $-1$, giving $3$ numbers. The number $-4$ is excluded, and $0$ is not negative.
