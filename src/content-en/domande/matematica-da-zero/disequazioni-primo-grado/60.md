How many integers satisfy the system

$$
\begin{cases}
2x+1>-3 \\
x-1<3
\end{cases}
$$

- $5$
- $6$
- $4$
- $7$

## Explanation

The first inequality gives $2x>-4$, that is, $x>-2$. The second gives $x<4$. The solutions of the system are the numbers satisfying $-2<x<4$. The integers are $-1$, $0$, $1$, $2$, and $3$, giving $5$ numbers. The endpoints $-2$ and $4$ are excluded.
