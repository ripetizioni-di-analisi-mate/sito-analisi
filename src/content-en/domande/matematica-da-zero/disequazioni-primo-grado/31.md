In $(x-3)(x+3)\leq x^2-4x+3$, the terms in $x^2$ cancel. What is the solution set?

- $x\leq3$
- $x\geq3$
- $x\leq-\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $x\leq-3$

## Explanation

The special product identity gives $(x-3)(x+3)=x^2-9$. The inequality becomes $x^2-9\leq x^2-4x+3$, so $4x\leq12$. Dividing by $4$ gives $x\leq3$. The direction stays the same because $4$ is positive. As a check, when $x=0$ we have $-9\leq3$, which is true.
