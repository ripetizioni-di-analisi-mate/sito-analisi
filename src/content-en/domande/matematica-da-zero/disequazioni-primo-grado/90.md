Pencils are sold in boxes of $12$. At least $100$ and at most $150$ pencils are needed. How many boxes can be bought?

- From $8$ to $12$ boxes
- From $9$ to $12$ boxes
- From $9$ to $13$ boxes
- From $8$ to $13$ boxes

## Explanation

With $n$ boxes there are $12n$ pencils, and $100\leq12n\leq150$ must hold. Dividing by $12$ gives $8.3\leq n\leq12.5$. The number of boxes is an integer, so $n$ ranges from $9$ to $12$. With $8$ boxes there are $96$ pencils, fewer than $100$.
