After both sides are multiplied by the denominator, which numbers satisfy $\dfrac{2x+1}{5}<3$?

- $x<\dfrac{15}{2}$
- $x>7$
- $x<8$
- $x<7$

## Explanation

Multiplying both sides by $5$, which is positive, gives $2x+1<15$. Subtracting $1$ gives $2x<14$. Dividing by $2$ gives $x<7$. Omitting the term $+1$ would give $x<\dfrac{15}{2}$. As a check, when $x=0$ the fraction equals $\dfrac{1}{5}$, which is less than $3$.
