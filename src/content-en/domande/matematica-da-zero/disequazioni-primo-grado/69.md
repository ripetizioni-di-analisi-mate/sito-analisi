Using a sign chart, for which values of $x$ is the product $(3-x)(x+2)$ positive?

- $x<-2$ or $x>3$
- $-2<x<3$
- $-3<x<2$
- $x>3$

## Explanation

The factor $3-x$ is positive for $x<3$ and the factor $x+2$ is positive for $x>-2$. Both are positive for $-2<x<3$. Outside the interval, one is negative and the other positive, so the product is negative.
