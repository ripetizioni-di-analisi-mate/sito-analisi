For what values of $x$ is the quotient $\dfrac{x+2}{x-4}$ negative or zero?

- $-2\leq x<4$
- $-2\leq x\leq4$
- $x\leq-2$ or $x>4$
- $-2<x<4$

## Explanation

The quotient is negative when the numerator and denominator have opposite signs, that is, between $-2$ and $4$. It equals zero at $x=-2$, which is included because the symbol is $\leq$. The number $4$ makes the denominator zero, so it is excluded. The solutions are $-2\leq x<4$.
