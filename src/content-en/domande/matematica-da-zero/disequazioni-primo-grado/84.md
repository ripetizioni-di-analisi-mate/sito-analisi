A student earned grades of $6$, $7$, and $5$ on her first three tests. What is the minimum grade she needs on the fourth test for an average of at least $6.5$?

- $7$
- $8$
- $9$
- $6.5$

## Explanation

The mean of the four grades is $\dfrac{18+x}{4}$ and must be at least $6.5$. Multiplying by $4$ gives $18+x\geq26$, so $x\geq8$. With $7$ the mean would be $\dfrac{25}{4}=6.25$, less than $6.5$. With $8$ the mean is $\dfrac{26}{4}=6.5$, which satisfies the requirement.
