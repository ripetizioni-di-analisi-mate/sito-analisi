After the two fractions are added, what are the solutions of $\dfrac{x}{2}+\dfrac{x}{3}<5$?

- $x<6$
- $x<\dfrac{25}{2}$
- $x>6$
- $x<30$

## Explanation

The least common multiple of $2$ and $3$ is $6$. Multiplying both sides by $6$ gives $3x+2x<30$, that is, $5x<30$. Dividing by $5$ gives $x<6$. The number $6$ is positive, so the direction does not change. Adding the denominators would give an incorrect result.
