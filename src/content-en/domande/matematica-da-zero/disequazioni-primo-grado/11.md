Which interval corresponds to the double inequality $-1\leq x<4$?

- $[-1,4)$
- $(-1,4]$
- $(-1,4)$
- $[-1,4]$

## Explanation

The symbol $\leq$ before $x$ includes the endpoint $-1$, so we use a square bracket on the left. The symbol $<$ after $x$ excludes the endpoint $4$, so we use a parenthesis on the right. The interval is $[-1,4)$. For example, $-1$ and $3$ belong to the interval, whereas $4$ lies outside it.
