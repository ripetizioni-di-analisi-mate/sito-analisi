After the product is expanded, $-2(3-x)\geq4x$ has solutions

- $x\geq-3$
- $x\leq3$
- $x\leq-1$
- $x\leq-3$

## Explanation

The product is $-2(3-x)=-6+2x$, so the inequality becomes $-6+2x\geq4x$. Moving the terms gives $-2x\geq6$. Dividing by $-2$ reverses the inequality and gives $x\leq-3$. Writing $-6-2x$ instead of $-6+2x$ would give $x\leq-1$. As a check, when $x=-4$ the two sides equal $-14$ and $-16$, and $-14\geq-16$ is true.
