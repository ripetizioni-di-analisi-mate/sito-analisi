What is the smallest integer that satisfies the inequality $x>-2.5$?

- $-2$
- $-3$
- $-2.5$
- $-1$

## Explanation

The numbers satisfying the inequality are those to the right of $-2.5$, excluding $-2.5$ itself. Among the integers, the first to the right of $-2.5$ is $-2$. The number $-3$ lies to the left of $-2.5$, so it is not a solution.
