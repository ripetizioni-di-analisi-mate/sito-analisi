The double inequality $-3<2x+1\leq9$ is equivalent to the system consisting of $-3<2x+1$ and $2x+1\leq9$. What is the solution interval?

- $(-4,8]$
- $[-2,4)$
- $(-2,4]$
- $(-2,4)$

## Explanation

Subtracting $1$ from all three expressions gives $-4<2x\leq8$. We then divide by $2$ without changing the direction, giving $-2<x\leq4$. The endpoint $-2$ is excluded and $4$ is included. The interval is $(-2,4]$. The result $(-4,8]$ comes from omitting the division by $2$.
