After the product is expanded, what set contains the solutions of $3(x+1)>3x+2$?

- $\emptyset$
- $\mathbb{R}$
- $x>0$
- $x>-1$

## Explanation

Expanding the product gives $3x+3>3x+2$. Moving the terms cancels the terms in $x$, leaving $0\cdot x>-1$, that is, the comparison $0>-1$. The comparison is true, so the inequality is always true and its solutions are all real numbers.
