Which inequality describes the numbers in the interval $(-3,2]$?

- $-3\leq x<2$
- $-3<x<2$
- $-3<x\leq2$
- $-3\leq x\leq2$

## Explanation

The parenthesis at $-3$ indicates that the endpoint is excluded, which we express using $<$. The square bracket at $2$ indicates that the endpoint is included, which we express using $\leq$. The double inequality is $-3<x\leq2$.
