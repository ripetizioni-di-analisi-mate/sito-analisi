Which list gives the numbers in increasing order?

- $-3,\ -8,\ 0,\ +4$
- $+4,\ 0,\ -3,\ -8$
- $-8,\ -3,\ 0,\ +4$
- $0,\ -3,\ -8,\ +4$

## Explanation

A list in increasing order goes from least to greatest, or from left to right on the number line. Negative numbers come before zero, and of two negative numbers the one with the greater absolute value comes first, so $-8<-3<0<+4$.
