What is the value of the product $(-6)\cdot(+7)$?

- $-42$
- $+42$
- $-13$
- $+1$

## Explanation

The two factors have opposite signs, so by the rule of signs the product is negative. Its absolute value is the product of the absolute values, $6\cdot 7=42$. Therefore $(-6)\cdot(+7)=-42$. The answer $+42$ would ignore the fact that the factors have opposite signs.
