Of the numbers $-15$, $-2$, $-9$, and $-30$, which is greatest?

- $-30$
- $-15$
- $-9$
- $-2$

## Explanation

Among negative numbers, the greater number has the smaller absolute value. The absolute values of the four numbers are $15$, $2$, $9$, and $30$, and the smallest is $2$. Therefore the greatest number is $-2$, which lies farther to the right than the others on the number line.
