At $22$\:00 the temperature is $-6$ °C. Overnight it drops another $5$ degrees and at dawn it rises $3$ degrees. What is the temperature at dawn, in degrees Celsius?

- $-14$
- $-2$
- $-8$
- $+2$

## Explanation

A drop of $5$ degrees corresponds to adding $-5$, and a rise of $3$ degrees corresponds to adding $+3$. We therefore calculate $-6-5=-11$ and then $-11+3=-8$. The numbers $-11$ and $+3$ have opposite signs, and the result has the sign of $-11$. At dawn the temperature is $-8$ °C.
