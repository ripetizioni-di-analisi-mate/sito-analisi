What is the opposite of the opposite of $-8$?

- $+8$
- $+0$
- $-16$
- $-8$

## Explanation

The opposite of $-8$ is $+8$ because it has the same absolute value and a different sign. The opposite of $+8$ is again $-8$. Taking the opposite twice therefore brings us back to the original number, so the result is $-8$.
