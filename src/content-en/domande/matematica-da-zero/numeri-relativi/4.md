Which of the following quantities is described by a negative number?

- A debt of $40$ euros
- A gain of $40$ euros
- The height of a tower $40$ m tall
- A duration of $40$ minutes

## Explanation

A negative number indicates a quantity on the opposite side of the reference level from a positive number. A gain is positive, and the height of a tower and the duration of a time interval are positive measurements. The debt is written as $-40$ euros.
