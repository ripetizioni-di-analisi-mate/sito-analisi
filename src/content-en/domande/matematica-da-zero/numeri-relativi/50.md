A diver rises from an elevation of $-20$ m to an elevation of $-6$ m. How many meters has the diver risen?

- $-14$
- $26$
- $14$
- $-26$

## Explanation

The change in elevation is the final elevation minus the initial elevation, so $(-6)-(-20)$. Subtracting $-20$ is equivalent to adding $+20$, so we get $-6+20=+14$. The result is positive, so the diver has risen $14$ m.
