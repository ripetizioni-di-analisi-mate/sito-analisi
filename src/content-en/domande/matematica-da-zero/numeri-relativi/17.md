What is the absolute value of $-9$?

- $9$
- $-9$
- $0$
- $18$

## Explanation

The absolute value of an integer is the number written without its sign, or its distance from zero on the number line. The point $-9$ is $9$ units from zero, so $|-9|=9$. A distance is never negative, so $-9$ cannot be an absolute value.
