A cave is at an elevation of $-40$ m. Which statement describes it?

- It is $40$ m above sea level
- It is $40$ m below sea level
- It is at sea level
- It is at a height of $-40$ m above sea level

## Explanation

Elevation is height relative to sea level, which corresponds to $0$. Since the $-$ sign indicates the region below zero, an elevation of $-40$ m is $40$ m below sea level.
