An account balance changes from $-40$ euros to $+25$ euros. By how many euros has it increased?

- $-65$
- $15$
- $65$
- $25$

## Explanation

The increase is the difference between the final and initial balances, that is, $(+25)-(-40)$. Subtracting $-40$ is equivalent to adding $+40$, so we get $25+40=65$. The result is positive, so the balance has increased by $65$ euros.
