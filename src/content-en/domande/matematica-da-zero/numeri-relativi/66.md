A product consists of $7$ factors, all negative. What is the sign of the product?

- Positive
- The product is zero
- It depends on the absolute values of the factors
- Negative

## Explanation

The sign of a product depends on the number of negative factors. If there is an odd number of negative factors, the product is negative. Since $7$ is odd, the product is negative whatever the absolute values of the factors.
