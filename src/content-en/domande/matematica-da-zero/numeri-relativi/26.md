Which pair consists of two numbers with unlike signs?

- $-6$ and $-2$
- $+6$ and $+2$
- $+6$ and $-2$
- $-5$ and $-9$

## Explanation

Two numbers have unlike signs if their signs differ. In the pair $+6$ and $-2$, the first has the sign $+$ and the second has the sign $-$. In the other pairs the two numbers have the same sign, so they have like signs.
