Which pair of numbers corresponds to two points on the number line that are $4$ units apart?

- $-3$ and $+2$
- $+2$ and $+8$
- $-5$ and $-2$
- $-1$ and $+3$

## Explanation

Between $-1$ and $+3$ there are $1$ units to zero and another $3$ units to $+3$, for a total of $4$ units. The other pairs are $5$ units, $6$ units, and $3$ units apart, respectively. Therefore only the pair $-1$ and $+3$ satisfies the condition.
