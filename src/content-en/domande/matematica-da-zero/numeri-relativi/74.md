A student writes $-5^2$ without parentheses. What number does this expression represent?

- $+25$
- $-10$
- $-25$
- $+10$

## Explanation

In $-5^2$ the base is $5$, not $-5$, and the $-$ sign in front denotes the opposite of the power. We have $5^2=25$, so $-5^2=-25$. With a negative base, the expression $(-5)^2$ would instead equal $+25$ because the exponent is even.
