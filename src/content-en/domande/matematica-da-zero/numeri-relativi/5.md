Which of the following numbers belongs to the set $\mathbb{Z}$ of integers but does not belong to the set $\mathbb{N}$ of natural numbers?

- $+7$
- $+0$
- $+12$
- $-7$

## Explanation

The natural numbers are $0, 1, 2, 3, \ldots$ and include no negative numbers. Therefore $7$, $0$, and $12$ are natural numbers, while $-7$ is negative, so it belongs to $\mathbb{Z}$ but not to $\mathbb{N}$.
