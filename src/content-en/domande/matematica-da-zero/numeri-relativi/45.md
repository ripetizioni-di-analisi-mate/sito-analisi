Which sum of three integers equals $0$?

- $(+5)+(-8)+(-3)$
- $(-5)+(+8)+(+3)$
- $(+5)+(-8)+(+3)$
- $(+5)+(+8)+(-3)$

## Explanation

The positive numbers $+5$ and $+3$ give $+8$, which is the opposite of $-8$. Therefore $(+5)+(-8)+(+3)=(+8)+(-8)=0$. The other sums equal $-6$, $+6$, and $+10$.
