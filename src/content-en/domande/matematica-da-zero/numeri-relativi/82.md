What is the value of the expression $7-(-2)\cdot(+3)$?

- $+27$
- $+1$
- $+13$
- $-13$

## Explanation

Multiplication comes before subtraction. The product $(-2)\cdot(+3)$ has factors with opposite signs and equals $-6$. Then $7-(-6)=7+6$ because subtracting a number is equivalent to adding its opposite. Therefore the expression equals $13$. The product must be evaluated first, since doing otherwise would give a different value.
