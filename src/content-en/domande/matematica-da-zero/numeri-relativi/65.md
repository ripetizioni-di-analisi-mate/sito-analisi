What integer do we get by dividing $-72$ by $-9$?

- $-8$
- $+63$
- $-81$
- $+8$

## Explanation

The dividend and the divisor have the same sign because both are negative, so the quotient is positive by the rule of signs. Its absolute value is $72:9=8$. Therefore $(-72):(-9)=+8$. We can check this using $(+8)\cdot(-9)=-72$. Division is the inverse operation of multiplication.
