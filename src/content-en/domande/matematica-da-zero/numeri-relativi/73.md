What is the fourth power of $-3$?

- $+81$
- $-81$
- $-12$
- $+12$

## Explanation

The base is $-3$ and the exponent $4$ is even, so there is an even number of negative factors and the power is positive. Its absolute value is $3^4=81$. Therefore $(-3)^4=+81$. With an odd exponent the result would have been $-81$.
