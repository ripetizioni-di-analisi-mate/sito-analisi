A cold storage room is kept at a temperature of $18$ degrees below zero. Which integer describes this temperature?

- $+18$
- $-18$
- $+0$
- $-8$

## Explanation

A temperature below zero is represented by a negative number because it lies below the reference level. Since it is $18$ degrees from zero, the temperature is $-18$ °C.
