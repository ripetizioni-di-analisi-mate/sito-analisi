Three accounts have balances of $-120$ euros, $-45$ euros, and $-80$ euros. Which balance corresponds to the smallest debt?

- $-120$
- $-80$
- $-45$
- $-125$

## Explanation

The debt is the absolute value of the negative balance. The smallest debt is the one with the smallest absolute value, namely $45$ euros. This corresponds to the balance $-45$, which is also the greatest of the three numbers.
