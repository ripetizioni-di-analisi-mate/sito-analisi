What is $(-2)\cdot[3-(5-8)]+(-1)^7$?

- $+19$
- $-13$
- $-11$
- $+11$

## Explanation

First we calculate $5-8=-3$, then $3-(-3)=3+3=6$ and $(-2)\cdot 6=-12$. Also, $(-1)^7=-1$ because the exponent is odd. The value is therefore $-12+(-1)=-13$, where the two addends have the same sign and their absolute values are added. We work from the innermost grouping symbols outward.
