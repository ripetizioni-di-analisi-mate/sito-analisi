Two numbers with opposite signs are added. Which condition guarantees that the sum is negative?

- The negative number is written first
- The positive number has the greater absolute value
- The two numbers have the same absolute value
- The negative number has the greater absolute value

## Explanation

The sum of two numbers with opposite signs has the sign of the number with the greater absolute value. It is therefore negative when the negative number has the greater absolute value. If the absolute values are equal, the sum is $0$.
