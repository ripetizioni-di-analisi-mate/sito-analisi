A family has $85$ euros in its account. It pays a bill of $120$ euros and then deposits $50$ euros. Which integer represents the final balance in euros?

- $-15$
- $+155$
- $+35$
- $+15$

## Explanation

Paying the bill corresponds to adding $-120$ and the deposit corresponds to adding $+50$. We get $85-120=-35$ because the numbers have opposite signs and $120-85=35$. Then $-35+50=+15$ because $50-35=15$. The final balance is therefore $+15$ euros.
