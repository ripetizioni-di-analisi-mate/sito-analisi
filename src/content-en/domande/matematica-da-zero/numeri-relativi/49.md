What number do we get by evaluating the difference $(-4)-(-9)$?

- $-13$
- $+5$
- $+13$
- $-5$

## Explanation

Subtracting a number is equivalent to adding its opposite, so $(-4)-(-9)=(-4)+(+9)$. The two numbers have opposite signs and $9-4=5$. The sign is the sign of the number with the greater absolute value, namely $+$, so the result is $+5$.
