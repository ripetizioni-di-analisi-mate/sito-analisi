Exactly one integer lies strictly between $-1$ and $+1$. What is it?

- $0$
- $-1$
- $1$
- No number

## Explanation

The integers between $-1$ and $+1$ are the ones between these two tick marks, excluding the endpoints. The numbers $-1$ and $+1$ are the endpoints, so they are not counted. Zero lies between the two tick marks, so the number we want is $0$.
