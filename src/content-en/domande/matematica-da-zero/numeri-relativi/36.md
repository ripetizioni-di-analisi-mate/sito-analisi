On the number line the point $P$ is $5$ units away from the point $+2$ and lies to the left of zero. What is the coordinate of $P$?

- $-3$
- $7$
- $3$
- $-7$

## Explanation

The points at a distance of $5$ units from $+2$ are reached by moving $5$ units to the left or to the right. Moving left we reach $2 - 5 = -3$, and moving right we reach $2 + 5 = 7$. The point $7$ lies to the right of zero, so the coordinate of $P$ is $-3$. The values $3$ and $-7$ come from counting the $5$ units starting from $-2$ instead of $+2$.
