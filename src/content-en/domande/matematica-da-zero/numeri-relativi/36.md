On a number line point $P$ is three units from $-2$ and lies to the left of zero. What is its coordinate?

- $-5$
- $1$
- $-1$
- $5$

## Explanation

There are two points three units from $-2$, namely $-2-3=-5$ and $-2+3=1$. The condition about position relative to zero selects $-5$.
