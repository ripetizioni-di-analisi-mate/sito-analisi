What number do we get by taking the opposite of the power $(-4)^3$?

- $-64$
- $+64$
- $+12$
- $-12$

## Explanation

The power $(-4)^3$ has a negative base and an odd exponent, so it equals $-64$. The sign $-$ before the expression denotes the opposite of $-64$, which is $+64$. The value is therefore positive because we remove the minus sign from the value of the power.
