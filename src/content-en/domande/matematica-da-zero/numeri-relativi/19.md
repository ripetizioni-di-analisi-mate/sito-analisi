How many integers have absolute value $5$?

- $2$
- $1$
- $0$
- $10$

## Explanation

An absolute value is a distance from zero. At a distance of $5$ there are two points on the number line, one to the right and one to the left of zero. The numbers are $+5$ and $-5$, so there are two.
