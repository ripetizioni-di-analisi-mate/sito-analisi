Which of the following powers has a negative value?

- $(-2)^5$
- $(-2)^6$
- $(-4)^2$
- $(+2)^5$

## Explanation

A power with a negative base is negative if its exponent is odd. In $(-2)^5$ the base is negative and the exponent $5$ is odd, so the power equals $-32$. The powers $(-2)^6$, $(-4)^2$, and $(+2)^5$ are positive because they have an even exponent or a positive base.
