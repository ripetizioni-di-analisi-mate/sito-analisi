Using the distributive property, what is the value of $(-4)\cdot(3-7)$?

- $-16$
- $-40$
- $+40$
- $+16$

## Explanation

By the distributive property, we multiply $-4$ by each term, giving $(-4)\cdot 3=-12$ and $(-4)\cdot(-7)=+28$, since the factors in the latter product have the same sign. The sum is $-12+28=+16$ because the numbers have opposite signs and $28-12=16$. We get the same value from $(-4)\cdot(-4)=16$.
