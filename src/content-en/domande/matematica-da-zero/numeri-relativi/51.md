What expression do we get by removing the parentheses from $8-(5-12)$?

- $8-5-12$
- $8+5-12$
- $8+5+12$
- $8-5+12$

## Explanation

The $-$ sign before the parentheses reverses the sign of every term inside. The term $+5$ becomes $-5$ and the term $-12$ becomes $+12$, so the expression is $8-5+12$. This expression equals $15$, as does $8-(-7)=15$.
