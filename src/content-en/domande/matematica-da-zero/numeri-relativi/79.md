For which values of the exponent $n$ is the power $(-7)^n$ positive?

- For every odd $n$
- For every $n$
- For every even $n$
- For no value of $n$

## Explanation

For a power with a negative base, the sign depends on whether the exponent is even or odd. When $n$ is even, there is an even number of negative factors and the product is positive. When $n$ is odd, there is an odd number of negative factors and the product is negative.
