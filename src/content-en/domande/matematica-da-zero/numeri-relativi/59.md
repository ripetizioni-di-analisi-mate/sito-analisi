Which sum has the same value as the difference $(-5)-(+3)$?

- $(-5)+(+3)$
- $(+5)+(-3)$
- $(+5)+(+3)$
- $(-5)+(-3)$

## Explanation

Subtracting a number is equivalent to adding its opposite. The opposite of $+3$ is $-3$, so $(-5)-(+3)=(-5)+(-3)$. The two addends have the same sign, so the sum equals $-8$. None of the other sums gives $-8$. The same rule applies to every subtraction of integers.
