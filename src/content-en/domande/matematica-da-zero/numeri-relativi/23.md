A deep-sea diver is at an elevation of $-25$ m and a seagull is flying at an elevation of $+25$ m. Which statement is true?

- The diver is farther from sea level
- They are the same distance from sea level
- The seagull is farther from sea level
- They are at the same elevation

## Explanation

The elevations $-25$ and $+25$ have absolute value $25$, so both are $25$ m from sea level, one above and one below. Their distances are the same, but their elevations differ because they have opposite signs.
