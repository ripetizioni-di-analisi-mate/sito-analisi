What is the value of $-12:(-3)+5\cdot(-2)$?

- $-6$
- $-18$
- $+14$
- $+6$

## Explanation

First we divide and multiply. We have $-12:(-3)=+4$ because the numbers have the same sign, and $5\cdot(-2)=-10$ because the factors have opposite signs. We then evaluate the sum $4-10$, whose addends have opposite signs and which equals $-6$.
