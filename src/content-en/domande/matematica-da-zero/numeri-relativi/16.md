On a number line, the unit of measurement is $2$ cm long. How many centimeters to the left of zero is the point corresponding to $-5$?

- $5$
- $-10$
- $10$
- $7$

## Explanation

The $-$ sign indicates that the point is to the left of zero. Its distance from zero is $5$ units and each unit measures $2$ cm, so the point is $5 \cdot 2 = 10$ cm from zero, to the left.
