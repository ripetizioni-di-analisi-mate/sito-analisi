What is $\{[5-(-3)^2]\cdot 2+4\}:(-4)$?

- $+1$
- $-1$
- $-8$
- $+4$

## Explanation

First we evaluate the power, obtaining $(-3)^2=+9$. Then we get $5-9=-4$, followed by $-4\cdot 2=-8$, and then $-8+4=-4$. Finally, the quotient $(-4):(-4)$ involves numbers with the same sign, so it equals $+1$. We work from the parentheses outward to the braces, evaluating powers first.
