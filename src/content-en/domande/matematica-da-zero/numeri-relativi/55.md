Five signed numbers are added and the result is $-8$. Then it turns out that one of the terms was written as $-6$ instead of $+6$. What is the correct sum?

- $-2$
- $-14$
- $-20$
- $4$

## Explanation

The other four terms do not change, so the correct sum is obtained from $-8$ by removing the term $-6$ and adding the term $+6$. Removing $-6$ is the same as adding its opposite $+6$, so we compute $-8 + 6 + 6$. From left to right, $-8 + 6 = -2$ and then $-2 + 6 = 4$, so the correct sum is $4$. The value $-2$ comes from removing the wrong term without adding the right one.
