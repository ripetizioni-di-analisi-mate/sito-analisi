What is the value of the algebraic sum $-15+22-8-6+11$?

- $-4$
- $+14$
- $+62$
- $+4$

## Explanation

The positive terms are $+22$ and $+11$, and their sum is $33$. The negative terms are $-15$, $-8$, and $-6$, and their sum is $-29$. Since $+33$ and $-29$ have opposite signs, the sum equals $33-29=+4$, with the sign of the number with the greater absolute value.
