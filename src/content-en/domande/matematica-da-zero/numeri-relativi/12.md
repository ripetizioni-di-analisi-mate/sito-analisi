What does the arrow at one end of a number line indicate?

- The direction in which negative numbers increase in absolute value
- The direction in which the numbers increase
- The distance between two consecutive tick marks
- The position of zero

## Explanation

A number line has an origin, a unit of measurement, and a direction indicated by the arrow. Moving in the direction of the arrow gives successively greater numbers because the positive numbers are to the right of zero. The origin and the unit of measurement are distinct from the direction.
