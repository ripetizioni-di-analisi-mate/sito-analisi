A freight elevator starts at the surface, at elevation $0$, goes down $4$ m each minute for $5$ minutes, and then rises $7$ m. What is its elevation, in meters?

- $+13$
- $-27$
- $-7$
- $-13$

## Explanation

In $5$ minutes the change in elevation is the product $5\cdot(-4)=-20$. After the descent the elevator is at elevation $-20$. It then rises $7$ m, so we add $+7$ and get $-20+7=-13$ because the numbers have opposite signs and $20-7=13$.
