Subtracting a certain number from $-3$ gives $+8$. What is the number?

- $+11$
- $+5$
- $-5$
- $-11$

## Explanation

If $-3-x=+8$, then $x$ is the difference between $-3$ and $+8$, that is, $-3-8=-11$. To check, $(-3)-(-11)=-3+11$, which equals $+8$ because $11-3=8$. The number to subtract is therefore $-11$. The number $-11$ is the opposite of $+11$ and would result from reversing the order of the two numbers.
