What number must be added to $-7$ to obtain $+4$?

- $-3$
- $+11$
- $+3$
- $-11$

## Explanation

The number we want is the signed distance from $-7$ to $+4$ on the number line. It takes $7$ units to reach $0$ and another $4$ to reach $+4$, for a total of $11$ units to the right. We therefore add $+11$.
