Over three days the water level in a pool changes by $+4$ cm, $-7$ cm, and $-2$ cm. What is the overall change in water level, in centimeters?

- $+5$
- $-13$
- $-5$
- $+13$

## Explanation

The overall change is the sum of the three changes. The positive terms give $+4$ and the negative terms give $-7-2=-9$. Since the two results have opposite signs, $4-9=-5$, so the water level has fallen $5$ cm.
