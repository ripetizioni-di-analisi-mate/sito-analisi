What number do we get by adding the third power of $-2$ and the second power of $-3$?

- $+1$
- $-17$
- $+17$
- $-1$

## Explanation

The power $(-2)^3$ has an odd exponent and equals $-8$. The power $(-3)^2$ has an even exponent and equals $+9$. The two numbers have opposite signs, so we subtract their absolute values, $9-8=1$, and keep the sign of $+9$. The sum equals $+1$.
