The number $b$ is negative and $a$ is any integer. Which statement about the difference $a-b$ is true?

- $a-b$ is greater than $a$
- $a-b$ is less than $a$
- $a-b$ equals $a$
- $a-b$ is always negative

## Explanation

Subtracting the negative number $b$ is equivalent to adding its opposite $-b$, which is positive. Adding a positive number moves us to the right on the number line, so $a-b>a$. For example, $3-(-4)=3+4=7$, which is greater than $3$.
