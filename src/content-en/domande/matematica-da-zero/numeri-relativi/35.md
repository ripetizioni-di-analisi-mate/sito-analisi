Two negative numbers $a$ and $b$ have absolute values $|a|=8$ and $|b|=3$. Which statement is true?

- $a > b$
- $a < b$
- $a = b$
- $a > 0$

## Explanation

Since the numbers are negative, $a=-8$ and $b=-3$. On the number line $-8$ lies to the left of $-3$, so $a < b$. Of two negative numbers, the one with the greater absolute value is smaller. The relation $a > 0$ is false because $a$ is negative.
