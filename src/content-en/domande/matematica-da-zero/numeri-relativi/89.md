A student evaluates $-2^2+(-3)^2$ and gets $13$. What error did the student make?

- The student evaluated $-2^2$ as $+4$
- The student evaluated $(-3)^2$ as $-9$
- The student added the bases $-2$ and $-3$ before squaring
- The student made no error

## Explanation

In $-2^2$, the base is $2$ and the sign $-$ denotes the opposite of the power, so $-2^2=-4$. The correct value of the expression is $-4+9=5$. The result $13$ comes from evaluating $4+9$, which means confusing $-2^2$ with $(-2)^2$. The error is therefore in the power preceded by a minus sign.
