A product has factors $-2$, $+5$, $-3$, and $-1$. What is its value?

- $+30$
- $-10$
- $-30$
- $+10$

## Explanation

Three factors are negative. Since three is odd, the product is negative. Its absolute value is the product of the absolute values, $2\cdot 5\cdot 3\cdot 1=30$. The product therefore equals $-30$. With an even number of negative factors, it would be positive.
