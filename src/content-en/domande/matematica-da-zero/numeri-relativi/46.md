Which sum gives $-2$?

- $(-9)+(-7)$
- $(-9)+(+7)$
- $(+9)+(-7)$
- $(+9)+(+7)$

## Explanation

In the sum $(-9)+(+7)$ the numbers have opposite signs, so we subtract their absolute values, giving $9-7=2$. The number with the greater absolute value is $-9$, so the result is $-2$. The other sums equal $-16$, $+2$, and $+16$, all different from $-2$.
