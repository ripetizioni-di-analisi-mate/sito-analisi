What is the value of the algebraic sum $-6-(+4)+(-1)-(-8)$?

- $+3$
- $-3$
- $-19$
- $-11$

## Explanation

Removing the parentheses gives $-(+4)=-4$, $+(-1)=-1$, and $-(-8)=+8$. The expression becomes $-6-4-1+8$. The negative terms give $-11$ and the positive term gives $+8$. Since the two results have opposite signs, the sum equals $-11+8=-3$. The $-$ sign before the parentheses reverses the sign of the number inside.
