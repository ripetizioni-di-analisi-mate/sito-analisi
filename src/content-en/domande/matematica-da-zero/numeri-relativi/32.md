Which integer is greater than $-4$ and less than $-2$?

- $-5$
- $-3$
- $-1$
- $+0$

## Explanation

The number we want lies to the right of $-4$ and to the left of $-2$ on the number line. There is only one integer between the two tick marks, namely $-3$, because $-4 < -3 < -2$. The other numbers listed lie outside the interval.
