What is the value of $(-1)^{2025}$?

- $+1$
- $+0$
- $-1$
- $-2025$

## Explanation

The base is negative and the exponent $2025$ is odd, so there is an odd number of negative factors and the power is negative. Its absolute value is $1^{2025}=1$. Therefore $(-1)^{2025}=-1$. The value $+1$ would result from an even exponent.
