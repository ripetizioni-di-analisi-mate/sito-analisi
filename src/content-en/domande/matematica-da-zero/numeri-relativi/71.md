What number, when multiplied by $-6$, gives $+54$?

- $+9$
- $-324$
- $+324$
- $-9$

## Explanation

The product $+54$ is positive, so the two factors must have the same sign. Since one is $-6$, the number we want is negative. Its absolute value is $54:6=9$. The number is therefore $-9$, as confirmed by $(-9)\cdot(-6)=+54$. The sign follows from the rule of signs.
