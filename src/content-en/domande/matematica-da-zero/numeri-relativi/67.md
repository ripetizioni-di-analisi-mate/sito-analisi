Each hour the temperature in a cold storage room drops $3$ degrees. Which integer represents the change in temperature after $5$ hours, in degrees?

- $+15$
- $-15$
- $-8$
- $+2$

## Explanation

The change in one hour is $-3$ degrees. After $5$ hours the change is the product $(+5)\cdot(-3)$, whose factors have opposite signs, so the product is negative. Its absolute value is $5\cdot 3=15$. The change is therefore $-15$ degrees.
