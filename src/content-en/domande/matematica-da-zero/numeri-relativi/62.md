What number do we get by multiplying $-8$ and $-5$?

- $+40$
- $-40$
- $+13$
- $-3$

## Explanation

The two factors have the same sign, so by the rule of signs the product is positive. Its absolute value is the product of the absolute values, $8\cdot 5=40$. Therefore $(-8)\cdot(-5)=+40$. The value $-40$ would come from ignoring the sign of one of the factors.
