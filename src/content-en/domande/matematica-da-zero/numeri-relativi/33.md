List all the integers between $-3$ and $+2$, excluding the two endpoints. How many numbers are in the list?

- $4$
- $3$
- $5$
- $6$

## Explanation

The integers between $-3$ and $+2$, excluding the endpoints, are $-2$, $-1$, $0$, and $+1$. Zero is an integer and must be counted. There are therefore $4$ numbers. The numbers $-3$ and $+2$ are not counted because the question excludes the endpoints.
