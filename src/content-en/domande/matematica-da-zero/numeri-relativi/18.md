An integer has absolute value $6$ and lies to the left of zero on the number line. What is the number?

- $+6$
- $-1$
- $-6$
- $-12$

## Explanation

The two numbers with absolute value $6$ are $+6$ and $-6$ because both are $6$ units from zero. The one to the left of zero is negative, so the number we want is $-6$. The number $+6$, on the other hand, lies to the right of zero.
