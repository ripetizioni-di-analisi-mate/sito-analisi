Which inequality is true?

- $-7 > -2$
- $-7 > 0$
- $-7 < -2$
- $-2 < -7$

## Explanation

The number $-7$ lies to the left of $-2$ on the number line, so $-7 < -2$. Of two negative numbers, the smaller one has the greater absolute value, and $|-7|=7$ is greater than $|-2|=2$. Also, every negative number is less than $0$.
