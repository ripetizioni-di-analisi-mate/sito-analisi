Which of the following expressions equals $-9$?

- $(-3)\cdot(+6)+(-9)$
- $(-3)\cdot(-6)-(-9)$
- $(-3)\cdot(+6)-(-9)$
- $(+3)\cdot(+6)-(+9)$

## Explanation

In the expression $(-3)\cdot(+6)-(-9)$, we calculate $(-3)\cdot(+6)=-18$ and then $-(-9)=+9$, so its value is $-18+9=-9$. The other expressions equal $-27$, $+27$, and $+9$, respectively, so none equals $-9$.
