What is the sum $(+3)+(-8)+(+6)+(-4)$?

- $+3$
- $-3$
- $-21$
- $+9$

## Explanation

By the commutative and associative properties, we first add the positive numbers and then the negative numbers. The positive numbers give $3+6=9$ and the negative numbers give $-8-4=-12$. Since $+9$ and $-12$ have opposite signs, we subtract their absolute values, $12-9=3$, and keep the minus sign. The sum equals $-3$.
