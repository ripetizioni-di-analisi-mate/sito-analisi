What is the result of the algebraic sum $+(-7)-(-7)$?

- $-14$
- $0$
- $-7$
- $14$

## Explanation

Removing the parentheses gives $+(-7)=-7$ and $-(-7)=+7$. The expression becomes $-7+7$. Since $-7$ and $+7$ are opposites, their sum equals $0$. The result is therefore different from $-14$, which would come from adding two terms with the same sign.
