Consider the sum of absolute values $|-3|+|+4|$. What is its value?

- $1$
- $7$
- $-7$
- $-1$

## Explanation

Taking the absolute value removes the sign from each number, so $|-3|=3$ and $|+4|=4$. Since both values are positive, the sum is $3+4=7$. The sum $-3+4=1$ is a different expression because the signs have not been removed.
