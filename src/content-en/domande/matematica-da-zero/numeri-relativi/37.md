What is the result of $(-5)+(-9)$?

- $-14$
- $+14$
- $-4$
- $+4$

## Explanation

The two numbers have the same sign, so the sum has that sign and its absolute value is the sum of their absolute values. We have $5+9=14$. Since the two numbers are negative, $(-5)+(-9)=-14$. The answer $+14$ would ignore their common sign.
