A test awards $+3$ points for each correct answer and $-2$ points for each incorrect answer. Marco gave $7$ correct answers and $4$ incorrect answers. What is his score?

- $+29$
- $+13$
- $+3$
- $-13$

## Explanation

The correct answers give $7\cdot(+3)=+21$ points and the incorrect answers give $4\cdot(-2)=-8$ points. The two results have opposite signs, so we subtract their absolute values, giving $21-8=13$. The sign is that of $+21$, so Marco's score is $+13$.
