Which pair consists of numbers with the same absolute value?

- $-14$ and $+41$
- $+14$ and $-4$
- $-14$ and $+14$
- $-14$ and $-13$

## Explanation

The absolute value is the number without its sign. The numbers $-14$ and $+14$ are both written as $14$ without their signs, so their absolute value is $14$. In the other pairs, the digits of the two numbers do not match.
