Which property of addition explains the equality $(-7)+(+2)=(+2)+(-7)$?

- The associative property
- The distributive property
- The rule of signs
- The commutative property

## Explanation

In the equality, the two addends have switched places and the sum has stayed the same. This is the commutative property, which also holds for integers. The associative property, on the other hand, concerns the grouping of three addends.
