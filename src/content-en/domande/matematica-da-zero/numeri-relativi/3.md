An elevator starts on the ground floor, goes down $2$ floors, and then goes up $5$ floors. Which floor does it reach?

- $-3$
- $+3$
- $+7$
- $-7$

## Explanation

The ground floor is floor $0$. Going down $2$ floors takes the elevator to floor $-2$, and going up $5$ floors takes it to floor $-2+5$. The two numbers have opposite signs, and $5-2=3$, with the sign of the number with the greater absolute value. The elevator therefore reaches floor $+3$.
