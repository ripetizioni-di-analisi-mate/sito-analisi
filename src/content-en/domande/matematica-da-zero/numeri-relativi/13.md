Which number lies halfway between $-6$ and $+2$ on the number line?

- $-2$
- $-4$
- $+4$
- $-8$

## Explanation

The distance between $-6$ and $+2$ is $6+2=8$ units because from $-6$ to $0$ there are $6$ units and from $0$ to $+2$ there are another $2$. The midpoint is $8:2=4$ units from each endpoint. Starting at $-6$ and moving $4$ units to the right takes us to $-6+4=-2$. Adding the endpoints without dividing by two would give $-4$.
