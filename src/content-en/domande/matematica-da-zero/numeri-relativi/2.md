Anna has $120$ euros in her account and pays a bill of $150$ euros. Which integer represents the new balance in euros?

- $-30$
- $+30$
- $-270$
- $-120$

## Explanation

The payment exceeds the amount available, so a debt remains on the account. The debt is $150-120=30$ euros, and a debt is represented by a minus sign. The new balance is therefore $120-150=-30$ euros. The answer $+30$ would ignore the sign of the debt.
