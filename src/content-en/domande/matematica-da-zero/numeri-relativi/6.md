Which expression represents the same number as $+17$?

- $-17$
- $+(-17)$
- $17$
- $-(+17)$

## Explanation

The $+$ sign on positive numbers is usually omitted, so $+17$ and $17$ represent the same number. The expressions $-17$, $+(-17)$, and $-(+17)$, on the other hand, all equal $-17$ because each has a single minus sign that makes the result negative.
