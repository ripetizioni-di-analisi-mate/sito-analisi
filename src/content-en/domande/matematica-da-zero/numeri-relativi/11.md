On a number line, point $Q$ lies $3$ units to the left of the point corresponding to $-2$. Which number corresponds to $Q$?

- $-1$
- $+1$
- $-5$
- $+5$

## Explanation

On the number line the arrow points to the right, so moving to the left means going toward smaller numbers. Starting at $-2$ and moving $3$ units to the left takes us to $-5$.
