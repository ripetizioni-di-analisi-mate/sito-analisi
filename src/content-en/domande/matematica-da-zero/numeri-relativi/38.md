A card is charged and credited, in order, by $-7$, $+4$, $-6$ and $+9$ euros. What is the smallest starting balance that keeps the balance at or above zero after every transaction?

- $0$ euros
- $9$ euros
- $7$ euros
- $13$ euros

## Explanation

The cumulative changes are $-7$, $-3$, $-9$ and $0$. The lowest point is nine euros below the start. With nine euros initially, the balance reaches zero after the third transaction and never goes below it.
