In a point-based competition, a team has $-2$ points because of an earlier penalty and receives a new penalty of $5$ points. Which integer represents its score?

- $+3$
- $-7$
- $+7$
- $-3$

## Explanation

A penalty amounts to adding a negative number, so we calculate $(-2)+(-5)$. The two numbers have the same sign, so we add their absolute values, giving $2+5=7$. The result has the common sign of the two terms, that is, the minus sign. Therefore the score is $-7$. The value $-3$ comes from subtracting the absolute values as if the two numbers had opposite signs.
