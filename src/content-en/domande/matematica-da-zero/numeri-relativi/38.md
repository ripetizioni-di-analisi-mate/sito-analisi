In a point-based competition, a team earns $12$ points and then receives a penalty of $15$ points. Which integer represents its score?

- $+3$
- $-3$
- $+27$
- $-27$

## Explanation

A penalty amounts to adding a negative number, so we calculate $(+12)+(-15)$. The two numbers have opposite signs, so we subtract their absolute values, giving $15-12=3$. The result has the sign of the number with the greater absolute value, which is $-15$. Therefore the score is $-3$.
