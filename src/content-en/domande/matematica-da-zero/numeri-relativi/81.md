Only one of the following equalities between powers is correct. Which one?

- $(-3)^3=3^3$
- $-3^2=(-3)^2$
- $(-3)^2=-9$
- $(-3)^2=3^2$

## Explanation

With an even exponent, a number and its opposite give the same power, so $(-3)^2=+9=3^2$. With an odd exponent, the powers are opposites, so $(-3)^3=-27$. Also, $-3^2=-9$ differs from $(-3)^2=+9$, and $(-3)^2$ does not equal $-9$.
