What is $(-2)^3\cdot(-1)^4-(-5)$?

- $-13$
- $+3$
- $+13$
- $-3$

## Explanation

First we evaluate the powers, obtaining $(-2)^3=-8$ because the exponent is odd and $(-1)^4=+1$ because it is even. The product is $-8\cdot 1=-8$. Then subtracting $-5$ is equivalent to adding $+5$, so $-8+5=-3$. A power with a negative base and an even exponent is always positive.
