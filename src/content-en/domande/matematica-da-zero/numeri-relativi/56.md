What is the value of $-(5-8)-(2-9)$?

- $-10$
- $+10$
- $-4$
- $+24$

## Explanation

First we evaluate the expressions in parentheses, giving $5-8=-3$ and $2-9=-7$. Then $-(-3)=+3$ and $-(-7)=+7$ because a minus sign before a negative number makes it positive. Therefore the value is $3+7=10$.
