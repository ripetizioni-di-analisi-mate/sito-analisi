What number do we get by multiplying the two expressions $[(-4)+7]$ and $[(-2)-(-6)]$?

- $+12$
- $-12$
- $+7$
- $+0$

## Explanation

First we evaluate the expressions in brackets. The first equals $-4+7=+3$. The second equals $-2-(-6)=-2+6=+4$. The product $(+3)\cdot(+4)$ has factors with the same sign, so it equals $+12$.
