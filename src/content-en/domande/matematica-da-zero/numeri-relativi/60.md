A diver starts at an elevation of $-18$ m, rises to $-7$ m, and then descends to $-11$ m. What is the overall change in elevation, in meters?

- $-7$
- $+7$
- $+11$
- $+29$

## Explanation

The overall change is the final elevation minus the initial elevation, so $(-11)-(-18)$. Subtracting $-18$ is equivalent to adding $+18$, so we get $-11+18=+7$. The diver is therefore $7$ m above the starting point, and the intermediate elevation does not affect the result.
