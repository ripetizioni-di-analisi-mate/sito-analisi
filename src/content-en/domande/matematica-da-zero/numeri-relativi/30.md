The low temperatures on four days were $-3$ °C, $-9$ °C, $0$ °C, and $-6$ °C. What was the temperature on the coldest day, in degrees Celsius?

- $-3$
- $+0$
- $-6$
- $-9$

## Explanation

The coldest day is the one with the lowest temperature, which lies farthest to the left on the number line. Of two negative numbers, the smaller one has the greater absolute value, so the lowest temperature is $-9$ °C.
