A debt of $48$ euros is divided into $6$ equal installments. Which integer represents each installment, in euros?

- $+8$
- $-42$
- $-288$
- $-8$

## Explanation

The debt is the number $-48$ and each installment is the quotient $(-48):(+6)$. The two numbers have opposite signs, so the quotient is negative. Its absolute value is $48:6=8$, so each installment is $-8$ euros. Six installments of $-8$ euros give $-48$ euros again.
