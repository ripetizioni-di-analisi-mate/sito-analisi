What do we get by multiplying an integer by $-1$?

- The opposite of the number
- The number itself
- The absolute value of the number
- Zero

## Explanation

By the rule of signs, multiplying by $-1$ reverses the sign of the number and keeps the same absolute value, so the result is the opposite of the number. For example, $(-1)\cdot(+3)=-3$ and $(-1)\cdot(-3)=+3$. The absolute value of the number stays the same and the sign is reversed.
