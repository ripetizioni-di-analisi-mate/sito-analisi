Which of the following operations is undefined?

- $0:(-5)$
- $(-5)\cdot 0$
- $(-5):0$
- $0\cdot 0$

## Explanation

The quotient $(-5):0$ would have to be a number that, when multiplied by $0$, gives $-5$, but every product with $0$ equals $0$. Therefore no number satisfies the condition and the division is undefined. The other operations are defined and give $0$.
