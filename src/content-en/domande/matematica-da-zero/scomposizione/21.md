To factor $25x^2-16$ as a difference of squares, we need the bases of the two squares. What are they?

- $25x$ and $16$
- $5x$ and $4$
- $5x^2$ and $4$
- $5x$ and $8$

## Explanation

The base of a square is the number or monomial that gives the square when squared. Since $(5x)^2=25x^2$ and $4^2=16$, the bases are $5x$ and $4$, and $25x^2-16=(5x+4)(5x-4)$. The number $8$ is twice $4$ rather than its base, while $25x$ and $16$ represent the squares themselves.
