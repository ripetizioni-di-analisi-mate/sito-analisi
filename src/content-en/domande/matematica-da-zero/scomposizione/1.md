Which product equals $x^2-x-12$ when expanded?

- $(x+4)(x-3)$
- $(x-6)(x+2)$
- $(x-4)(x+3)$
- $(x-4)(x-3)$

## Explanation

Multiply the two factors in the correct factorization to get $(x-4)(x+3)=x^2+3x-4x-12=x^2-x-12$. The linear term is $3x-4x=-x$ and the constant term is $-4\cdot 3=-12$. The other products give $x^2+x-12$, $x^2-4x-12$, and $x^2-7x+12$, so none equals the polynomial.
