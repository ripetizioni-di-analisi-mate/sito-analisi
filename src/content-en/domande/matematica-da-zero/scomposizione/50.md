In the binomial $64a^3-b^3$ each term is a cube. Which monomials, when cubed, give $64a^3$ and $b^3$?

- $8a$ and $b$
- $4a$ and $b$
- $4a^3$ and $b$
- $64a$ and $b$

## Explanation

The base of a cube is the number or monomial that gives the term when cubed. Since $(4a)^3=64a^3$, the bases are $4a$ and $b$. Therefore $64a^3-b^3=(4a-b)(16a^2+4ab+b^2)$. The number $8$ is the square root of $64$ rather than its cube root.
