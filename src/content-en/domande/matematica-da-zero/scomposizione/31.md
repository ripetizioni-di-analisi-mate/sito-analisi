After its common factor is taken out, $2x^2-50$ gives a difference of squares. What is its complete factorization?

- $(2x+5)(2x-5)$
- $2\,(x+5)(x-5)$
- $2\,(x-5)^2$
- $(x+5)(x-5)$

## Explanation

Factor out $2$ to obtain $2x^2-50=2\,(x^2-25)$. The binomial $x^2-25$ is the difference of the squares of $x$ and $5$, so it equals $(x+5)(x-5)$. The complete factorization is $2\,(x+5)(x-5)$. The product $(2x+5)(2x-5)$ equals $4x^2-25$, and $(x+5)(x-5)$ equals $x^2-25$, missing the factor $2$.
