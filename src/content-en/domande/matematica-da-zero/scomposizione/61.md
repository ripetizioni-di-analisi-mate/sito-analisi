The LCM of two polynomials is $(x-1)^2(x+4)$. Which of the following could be one of the polynomials?

- $x^2-1$
- $x^2-2x+1$
- $(x-1)^3$
- $x^2+8x+16$

## Explanation

A polynomial can be one of the two only if its factors appear in the LCM with at least the same exponents. The trinomial $x^2-2x+1=(x-1)^2$ satisfies this condition. In contrast, $x^2-1=(x-1)(x+1)$ contains $x+1$, the polynomial $(x-1)^3$ has exponent $3$, and $x^2+8x+16=(x+4)^2$ has exponent $2$ on $x+4$.
