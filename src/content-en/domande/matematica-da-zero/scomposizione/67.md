For the fraction $\dfrac{x+1}{x^2-5x+6}$, which values of $x$ are excluded?

- $2$ and $3$
- $-2$ and $-3$
- $6$
- $-1$

## Explanation

Factor the denominator using the sum $-5$ and product $6$ to obtain $x^2-5x+6=(x-2)(x-3)$. It is zero for $x=2$ and for $x=3$, so both values are excluded. The value $-1$ makes the numerator zero, which causes no problem because for $x=-1$ the fraction equals $0$.
