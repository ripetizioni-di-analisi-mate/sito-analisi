For which value of $a$ does the fraction $\dfrac{5}{a-2}$ equal $1$?

- $3$
- $5$
- $7$
- $-3$

## Explanation

A fraction with numerator $5$ equals $1$ when the denominator equals the numerator, so $a-2=5$ and $a=7$. For $a=7$ the fraction equals $\dfrac{5}{5}=1$. For $a=3$ it equals $\dfrac{5}{1}=5$, and for $a=5$ it equals $\dfrac{5}{3}$.
