Using the sum of cubes formula, which expression equals the binomial $8x^3+1$?

- $(2x+1)(4x^2+2x+1)$
- $(2x+1)^3$
- $(2x+1)(4x^2-2x+1)$
- $(8x+1)(x^2-x+1)$

## Explanation

Since $8x^3=(2x)^3$ and $1=1^3$, apply $A^3+B^3=(A+B)(A^2-AB+B^2)$ with $A=2x$ and $B=1$. This gives $(2x+1)(4x^2-2x+1)$. In a sum of cubes, the second factor has a minus sign before $AB$, which here equals $2x$.
