In the polynomial $14x^2+7x$, the second term equals the common factor $7x$. Which product do you get by factoring out $7x$?

- $7x\cdot 2x$
- $7x\,(2x-1)$
- $7x\,(2x+1)$
- $7x\,(2x+7)$

## Explanation

Divide each term by $7x$, giving $\dfrac{14x^2}{7x}=2x$ and $\dfrac{7x}{7x}=1$. The second term equals the common factor, so its place in the second factor is taken by $1$ rather than $0$. Therefore $14x^2+7x=7x\,(2x+1)$. The expression $7x\cdot 2x$ equals $14x^2$ and loses the term $7x$.
