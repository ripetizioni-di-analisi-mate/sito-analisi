Group the first two terms and the last two terms of $x^3+4x^2+5x+20$. What factorization results?

- $(x+4)(x^2-5)$
- $(x-4)(x^2+5)$
- $(x+4)(x^2+5)$
- $(x+5)(x^2+4)$

## Explanation

Factoring $x^2$ out of the first two terms and $5$ out of the last two gives $x^2\,(x+4)+5\,(x+4)$. The binomial $x+4$ is common to both groups, so the result is $(x+4)(x^2+5)$. The factorization is complete because $x^2+5\geq 5$ shows that the quadratic factor is never zero.
