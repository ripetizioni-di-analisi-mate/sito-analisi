A coefficient is blurred in the polynomial $x^3+4x^2+kx+20$. Grouping the terms in pairs is known to give the factor $x+4$. What is $k$?

- $4$
- $16$
- $5$
- $80$

## Explanation

The first two terms give $x^2(x+4)$. The last two must be $k(x+4)=kx+4k$. The constant term requires $4k=20$, so $k=5$.
