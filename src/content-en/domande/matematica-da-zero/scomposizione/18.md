A store sells $x$ notebooks and $3$ pens, all at the same price of $x+2$ euros. The revenue is $x\,(x+2)+3\,(x+2)$ euros. Which equivalent expression results from factoring out $x+2$?

- $(x+2)\cdot 3x$
- $(x+2)(x+3)$
- $(x+2)(x-3)$
- $3x\,(x+2)\cdot(x+2)$

## Explanation

The price $x+2$ appears in both terms, so it can be factored out. The first term leaves $x$ and the second leaves $3$, so the revenue is $(x+2)(x+3)$ euros. The factor $x+3$ is the number of items sold because $x$ notebooks and $3$ pens make $x+3$ items, each costing $x+2$ euros.
