What is the factorization of the trinomial $x^2-14x+49$?

- $(x+7)^2$
- $(x-7)(x+7)$
- $(x-14)^2$
- $(x-7)^2$

## Explanation

The bases of the squares are $x$ and $7$ because $7^2=49$. Twice their product is $2\cdot x\cdot 7=14x$ and appears in the trinomial with a minus sign, so $x^2-14x+49=(x-7)^2$. The product $(x-7)(x+7)$ equals $x^2-49$ and has no linear term.
