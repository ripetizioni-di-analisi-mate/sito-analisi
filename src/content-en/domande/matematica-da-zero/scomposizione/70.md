A bus travels $60$ kilometers at a speed of $x-10$ kilometers per hour. The time taken, in hours, is $\dfrac{60}{x-10}$. For which value of $x$ is the formula undefined?

- $-10$
- $60$
- $0$
- $10$

## Explanation

The formula is undefined when its denominator is zero. Set $x-10=0$, which gives $x=10$. In that case the speed would be zero and the travel time could not be calculated. The value $x=0$ gives the denominator $-10$, which is nonzero.
