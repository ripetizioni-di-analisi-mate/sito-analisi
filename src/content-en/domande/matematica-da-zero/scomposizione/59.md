The simplest common denominator of two fractions with denominators $2x^2-8$ and $3x+6$ is their LCM. What is it?

- $6\,(x+2)^2(x-2)$
- $6\,(x+2)(x-2)$
- $(x+2)(x-2)$
- $6\,(x+2)$

## Explanation

We have $2x^2-8=2\,(x+2)(x-2)$ and $3x+6=3\,(x+2)$. The LCM contains all the factors, common or otherwise, with their largest exponents: $2$, $3$, $x+2$, and $x-2$. The factor $x+2$ occurs only once, so $\operatorname{mcm}=6\,(x+2)(x-2)$.
