The difference $x^4-81$ can be factored in several successive steps. Which product contains only irreducible factors?

- $(x^2+9)(x^2-9)$
- $(x+3)^2(x-3)^2$
- $(x^2-9)^2$
- $(x^2+9)(x+3)(x-3)$

## Explanation

Since $x^4=(x^2)^2$ and $81=9^2$, we have $x^4-81=(x^2+9)(x^2-9)$. The factor $x^2-9$ is again a difference of squares and equals $(x+3)(x-3)$, while $x^2+9\geq 9$ is irreducible. The complete factorization is therefore $(x^2+9)(x+3)(x-3)$. The product $(x^2+9)(x^2-9)$ is correct but incomplete.
