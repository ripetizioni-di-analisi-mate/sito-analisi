Which values of $x$ are excluded from the fraction $\dfrac{3}{x^2-9}$?

- $3$
- $3$ and $-3$
- $9$ and $-9$
- $0$

## Explanation

Exclude the values that make the denominator zero. Since $x^2-9=(x+3)(x-3)$, the denominator is zero for $x=-3$ and for $x=3$, while for $x=9$ it would equal $81-9=72$. Therefore, $3$ and $-3$ are excluded. A single value is insufficient because the denominator has two factors.
