The polynomial $2ax+2ay+3x+3y$ has four terms with no factor common to all of them. What factorization results from grouping?

- $(x+y)(2a-3)$
- $(x-y)(2a+3)$
- $(x+y)(2a+3)$
- $(x+y)(a+3)$

## Explanation

Factor $2a$ out of the first two terms and $3$ out of the last two to obtain $2a\,(x+y)+3\,(x+y)$. Since both groups contain the same polynomial $x+y$, factor it out in turn to obtain $(x+y)(2a+3)$. For $a=1$, $x=1$, and $y=1$ the polynomial equals $2+2+3+3=10$ and the product equals $2\cdot 5=10$.
