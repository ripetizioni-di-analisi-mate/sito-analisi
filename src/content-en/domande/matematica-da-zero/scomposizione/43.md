A rectangular plot has area $36$ square meters and half its perimeter is $13$ meters. What are the two side lengths in meters?

- $4$ and $9$
- $6$ and $7$
- $3$ and $12$
- $2$ and $11$

## Explanation

The side lengths have sum $13$ and product $36$, like the two numbers used to factor $x^2-13x+36=(x-4)(x-9)$. The positive integer pairs with product $36$ are $(1,36)$, $(2,18)$, $(3,12)$, $(4,9)$, and $(6,6)$, and only $4$ and $9$ have sum $13$. The pair $6$ and $7$ has sum $13$ but product $42$.
