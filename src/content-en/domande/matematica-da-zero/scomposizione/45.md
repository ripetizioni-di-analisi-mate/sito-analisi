Which values of $x$ make the trinomial $x^2-x-6$ zero?

- $2$ and $-3$
- $2$ and $3$
- $-2$ and $-3$
- $-2$ and $3$

## Explanation

Factor the trinomial. The numbers with sum $-1$ and product $-6$ are $2$ and $-3$, so $x^2-x-6=(x+2)(x-3)$. By the zero product property, the trinomial is zero when $x+2=0$ or $x-3=0$, that is, for $x=-2$ and $x=3$.
