Open circles in the figure mark the values excluded from a rational expression. Which of the following fractions excludes exactly these values?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 linee="-3.5,0 5.5,0;5.3,0.1 5.5,0 5.3,-0.1;-3,-0.08 -3,0.08;-2,-0.08 -2,0.08;-1,-0.08 -1,0.08;0,-0.08 0,0.08;1,-0.08 1,0.08;2,-0.08 2,0.08;3,-0.08 3,0.08;4,-0.08 4,0.08;5,-0.08 5,0.08" punti="-1,0,vuoto;3,0,vuoto" etichette="-3.12,-0.4,−3;-2.12,-0.4,−2;-1.12,-0.4,−1;-0.05,-0.4,0;0.95,-0.4,1;1.95,-0.4,2;2.95,-0.4,3;3.95,-0.4,4;4.95,-0.4,5;5.45,0.25,x" xmin=-3.8 xmax=5.9 ymin=-0.7 ymax=0.5 titolo="A rational expression excludes the values $-1$ and $3$, marked with open circles"}

- $\dfrac{5}{x^2-2x-3}$
- $\dfrac{5}{x^2+2x-3}$
- $\dfrac{5}{x^2-9}$
- $\dfrac{5}{x^2-1}$

## Explanation

The excluded values are $-1$ and $3$, so the denominator must be zero at both numbers. The denominator that satisfies this condition is $(x+1)(x-3)=x^2-2x-3$. The other denominators are zero for $1$ and $-3$, for $3$ and $-3$, and for $1$ and $-1$, respectively.
