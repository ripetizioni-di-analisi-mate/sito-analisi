For which value of $x$ is the fraction $\dfrac{x-4}{x}$ undefined?

- $4$
- $0$
- $0$ and $4$
- $-4$

## Explanation

A fraction is undefined when its denominator is zero. The denominator is $x$, which equals zero only for $x=0$. The value $x=4$ makes the numerator zero and gives $\dfrac{0}{4}=0$, a well-defined number.
