How do you factor $9a^2-b^2$?

- $(3a+b)(3a-b)$
- $(9a+b)(9a-b)$
- $(3a-b)^2$
- $(3a+b)^2$

## Explanation

The polynomial is the difference of the squares of $3a$ and $b$ because $(3a)^2=9a^2$. The formula $A^2-B^2=(A+B)(A-B)$ with $A=3a$ and $B=b$ gives $(3a+b)(3a-b)$. The squares $(3a\pm b)^2$ contain the term $\pm 6ab$, which does not appear in the polynomial.
