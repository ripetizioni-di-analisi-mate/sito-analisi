Which of the following divides all three polynomials $x^2-1$, $x^2+2x+1$, and $x^2+x$ and has the highest possible degree among their common divisors?

- $x\,(x+1)$
- $x+1$
- $(x+1)^2$
- $x^2-1$

## Explanation

We have $x^2-1=(x-1)(x+1)$, $x^2+2x+1=(x+1)^2$, and $x^2+x=x\,(x+1)$. The only factor common to all three is $x+1$, with smallest exponent $1$. Therefore $\operatorname{MCD}=x+1$. The factors $x$ and $x-1$ do not appear in every polynomial.
