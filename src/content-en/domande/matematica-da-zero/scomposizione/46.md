The trinomial $-x^2+5x-6$ has coefficient $-1$ on $x^2$. Which factorization is correct?

- $-(x+2)(x+3)$
- $(x-2)(x-3)$
- $-(x-1)(x-6)$
- $-(x-2)(x-3)$

## Explanation

Factoring out $-1$ gives $-(x^2-5x+6)$. In the trinomial $x^2-5x+6$ the numbers with sum $-5$ and product $6$ are $-2$ and $-3$, so $x^2-5x+6=(x-2)(x-3)$. Therefore $-x^2+5x-6=-(x-2)(x-3)$. Without the minus sign the product would equal $x^2-5x+6$.
