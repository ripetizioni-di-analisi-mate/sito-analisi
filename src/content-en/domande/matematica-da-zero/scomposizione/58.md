The polynomials $x^2-25$ and $x^2-10x+25$ are factored. Which polynomial is their greatest common divisor?

- $(x-5)^2$
- $x+5$
- $x-5$
- $(x+5)(x-5)^2$

## Explanation

Factoring the polynomials gives $x^2-25=(x+5)(x-5)$ and $x^2-10x+25=(x-5)^2$. The GCD is the product of the shared factors with their smallest exponents. The only common factor is $x-5$, with exponent $1$ in the first polynomial and $2$ in the second, so $\operatorname{MCD}=x-5$. The product $(x+5)(x-5)^2$ is the LCM.
