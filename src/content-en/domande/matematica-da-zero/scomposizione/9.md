How do you write $8a^2b-12ab^2$ after factoring out its greatest common factor?

- $4ab\,(2a+3b)$
- $4a^2b^2\,(2a-3b)$
- $4ab\,(2a^2-3b^2)$
- $4ab\,(2a-3b)$

## Explanation

The greatest common divisor of $8$ and $12$ is $4$, and the common variables are $a$ and $b$ with smallest exponent $1$. The common factor is $4ab$. Dividing gives $\dfrac{8a^2b}{4ab}=2a$ and $\dfrac{-12ab^2}{4ab}=-3b$, so $8a^2b-12ab^2=4ab\,(2a-3b)$.
