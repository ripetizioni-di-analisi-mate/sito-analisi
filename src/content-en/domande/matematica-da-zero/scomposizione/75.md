Simplify $\dfrac{x^2-4}{x^2+2x}$ by dividing by the common factor. Which fraction remains?

- $\dfrac{x+2}{x}$
- $-\dfrac{2}{x}$
- $\dfrac{x-2}{x+2}$
- $\dfrac{x-2}{x}$

## Explanation

Factoring the polynomials gives $x^2-4=(x+2)(x-2)$ and $x^2+2x=x\,(x+2)$. Dividing the numerator and denominator by their common factor $x+2$ leaves $\dfrac{x-2}{x}$, with $x\neq 0$ and $x\neq -2$. The result $-\dfrac{2}{x}$ comes from canceling the terms $x^2$, which are not factors.
