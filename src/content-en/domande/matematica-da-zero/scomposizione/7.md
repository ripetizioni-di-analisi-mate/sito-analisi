Elena writes $x^2-6x+9=(x-3)(x+3)$. What polynomial do you actually get by multiplying the two factors?

- $x^2-6x+9$
- $x^2-9$
- $x^2+9$
- $x^2-6x-9$

## Explanation

The product $(x-3)(x+3)$ equals $x^2+3x-3x-9$. The terms $3x$ and $-3x$ cancel, so $x^2-9$ remains. Since $x^2-9$ differs from $x^2-6x+9$, Elena's equality is incorrect. The trinomial $x^2-6x+9$ actually equals $(x-3)^2$.
