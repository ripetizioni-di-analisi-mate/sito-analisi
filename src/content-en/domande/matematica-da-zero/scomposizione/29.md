A square floor has area $x^2-12x+36$ square meters, with $x>6$. What is its side length in meters?

- $x+6$
- $x-12$
- $x-36$
- $x-6$

## Explanation

The area of a square is the square of its side length, so factor $x^2-12x+36$. The bases are $x$ and $6$, and twice their product, $2\cdot x\cdot 6=12x$, has a minus sign, so $x^2-12x+36=(x-6)^2$ and the side measures $x-6$ meters. The condition $x>6$ makes this length positive.
