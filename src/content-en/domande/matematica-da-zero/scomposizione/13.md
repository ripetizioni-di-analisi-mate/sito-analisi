In the expression $5x\,(x+4)-3\,(x+4)$ the two terms share the binomial $x+4$. What expression results when it is factored out?

- $(x+4)(5x+3)$
- $(x+4)(5x-3)$
- $(x+4)^2(5x-3)$
- $(x-4)(5x-3)$

## Explanation

The binomial $x+4$ is a factor of both terms, so it can be taken out just like any common factor. The first term leaves $5x$ and the second leaves $-3$ because the minus sign belongs to the second term. Therefore, the result is $(x+4)(5x-3)$, with a single factor $x+4$.
