A rectangle has area $x^2+9x+14$ and one side of length $x+2$. How long is the other side?

- $x+7$
- $x+12$
- $x+14$
- $x+5$

## Explanation

Factor the area using a sum and a product. Two numbers with sum $9$ and product $14$ are $2$ and $7$, so $x^2+9x+14=(x+2)(x+7)$. One side is $x+2$, so the other is $x+7$. The number $14$ is the product of the two numbers rather than their sum.
