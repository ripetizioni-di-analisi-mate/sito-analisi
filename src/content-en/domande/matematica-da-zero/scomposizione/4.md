A rectangular flower bed has area $x^2+3x$ square meters and one side $x$ meters long. How long is the other side, in meters?

- $x+3$
- $3$
- $x^2+2x$
- $x^2+3$

## Explanation

The area is the product of the two side lengths, so the other side is found by dividing $x^2+3x$ by $x$. Factoring out $x$ gives $x^2+3x=x\,(x+3)$, so the side measures $x+3$. Dividing only the second term by $x$ would give $x^2+3$, while subtracting $x$ would give $x^2+2x$, and neither is a side length.
