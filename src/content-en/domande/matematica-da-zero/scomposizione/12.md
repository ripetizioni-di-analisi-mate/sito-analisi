The polynomial $ab-3a-2b+6$ can be written as $(a-2)(b-3)$. We fix $a$ and can then freely choose $b$. Which value of $a$ guarantees that the polynomial is zero for every $b$?

- $3$
- $-2$
- $2$
- $-3$

## Explanation

The factor $b-3$ need not vanish, for example when $b=4$. To make the product zero for every $b$, the factor $a-2$ must vanish. This also works when $b=3$.
