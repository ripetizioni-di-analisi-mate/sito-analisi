What factorization results from grouping the terms of $ab-3a-2b+6$ in pairs?

- $(a+2)(b-3)$
- $(a-2)(b+3)$
- $(a-2)(b-3)$
- $(a-3)(b-2)$

## Explanation

Factoring $a$ out of the first two terms and $-2$ out of the last two gives $a\,(b-3)-2\,(b-3)$. Factoring out $-2$ leaves the binomial $b-3$ in the second group as well. Since this binomial is common to both groups, the result is $(b-3)(a-2)$, or $(a-2)(b-3)$.
