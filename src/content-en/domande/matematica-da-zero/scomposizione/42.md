Which of the following trinomials cannot be factored by finding two integers with a given sum and product?

- $x^2+4x+5$
- $x^2+6x+5$
- $x^2-4x-5$
- $x^2+4x-5$

## Explanation

For $x^2+4x+5$ the integers with product $5$ are $1$ and $5$ or $-1$ and $-5$, with sums $6$ and $-6$, neither of which is $4$. The other trinomials factor as $x^2+6x+5=(x+1)(x+5)$, $x^2-4x-5=(x+1)(x-5)$, and $x^2+4x-5=(x+5)(x-1)$.
