The trinomial $9x^2-30x+25$ is the square of a binomial. Which one?

- $(3x+5)^2$
- $(9x-5)^2$
- $(3x-5)^2$
- $(3x-25)^2$

## Explanation

The two squares are $9x^2=(3x)^2$ and $25=5^2$, with bases $3x$ and $5$. Twice the product is $2\cdot 3x\cdot 5=30x$ and appears with a minus sign, so the trinomial is $(3x-5)^2$. The bases are not $9x$ and $25$, which represent the squares instead. The base $9x$ would give the term $81x^2$.
