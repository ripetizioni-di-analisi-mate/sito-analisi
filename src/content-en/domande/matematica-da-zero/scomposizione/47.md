In $x^2+8x+15$ the positive integer pairs with product $15$ are $(1,15)$ and $(3,5)$. Which pair is used, and why?

- $1$ and $15$, because their product is $15$
- $3$ and $5$, because their product is $8$
- $1$ and $15$, because their sum is $8$
- $3$ and $5$, because their sum is $8$

## Explanation

Both pairs have the required product $15$, so the choice depends on their sum. Since $3+5=8$ and $1+15=16$, use $3$ and $5$, and $x^2+8x+15=(x+3)(x+5)$. The number $8$ is the required sum rather than the product.
