Which of the following polynomials can be factored by grouping?

- $9x^2-4$
- $xy+2x+3y+6$
- $x^3+8$
- $x^2+7x$

## Explanation

Factoring by grouping applies to four terms with no factor common to all of them. In the polynomial $xy+2x+3y+6$, factor $x$ out of the first two terms and $3$ out of the last two to obtain $x\,(y+2)+3\,(y+2)=(y+2)(x+3)$. The other polynomials have two terms and are factored using other methods.
