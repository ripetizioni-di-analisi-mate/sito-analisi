The linear polynomial $2x+1$ equals zero for only one value of $x$. Which one?

- $-\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $-2$
- $2$

## Explanation

Set $2x+1=0$. Subtracting $1$ from both sides gives $2x=-1$, and dividing by $2$ yields $x=-\dfrac{1}{2}$. Indeed, $2\cdot\left(-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)+1=-1+1=0$. The values $2$ and $-2$ come from interchanging $2$ and $1$, while $\dfrac{1}{2}$ leaves the sign of $1$ unchanged.
