What is $51^2-49^2$ using the difference of squares factorization?

- $4$
- $100$
- $2$
- $200$

## Explanation

With $A=51$ and $B=49$, we have $A^2-B^2=(A+B)(A-B)=100\cdot 2=200$. The result agrees with $2601-2401=200$. The value $4$ comes from calculating $(51-49)^2$, while $100$ and $2$ are the individual factors rather than their product.
