The factorization $x^3+125=(x+5)(x^2-5x+25)$ is complete. Why is the factor $x^2-5x+25$ irreducible?

- Because it is the quadratic factor in a cube identity and is never zero
- Because it is the square of $x-5$
- Because its degree is greater than $1$
- Because $125$ is a cube

## Explanation

The trinomial $x^2-5x+25$ is the quadratic factor in a cube identity because its middle term is $-5x$ rather than twice the product, $-10x$. It can be written as $\left(x-\dfrac{5}{2}\right)^2+\dfrac{75}{4}$, which is positive for every $x$. Therefore, it has no linear factors.
