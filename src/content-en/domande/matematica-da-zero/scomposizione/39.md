All the coefficients of $3x^2+15x+18$ are multiples of $3$. Which factorization contains only irreducible factors?

- $3\,(x+2)(x+3)$
- $(x+2)(x+3)$
- $3\,(x+1)(x+6)$
- $3\,(x-2)(x-3)$

## Explanation

All the coefficients are divisible by $3$, so taking out the common factor gives $3\,(x^2+5x+6)$. In the trinomial $x^2+5x+6$, we look for two numbers with sum $5$ and product $6$. They are $2$ and $3$. Therefore $3x^2+15x+18=3\,(x+2)(x+3)$. Without the factor $3$ the product would equal $x^2+5x+6$.
