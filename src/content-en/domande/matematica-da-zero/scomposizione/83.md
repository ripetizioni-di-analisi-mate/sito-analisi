Write the sum $\dfrac{3}{x}+\dfrac{2}{x+1}$, with $x\neq 0$ and $x\neq -1$, as a single fraction. What is it?

- $\dfrac{5}{2x+1}$
- $\dfrac{5x}{x\,(x+1)}$
- $\dfrac{5x+3}{x+1}$
- $\dfrac{5x+3}{x\,(x+1)}$

## Explanation

The common denominator is $x\,(x+1)$. Multiply the first numerator by $x+1$ and the second by $x$ to obtain $\dfrac{3\,(x+1)+2x}{x\,(x+1)}=\dfrac{5x+3}{x\,(x+1)}$. The result $\dfrac{5}{2x+1}$ comes from adding numerators and denominators separately, which is not a valid rule.
