Which trinomial is the square of a binomial?

- $4x^2+12x+9$
- $4x^2+6x+9$
- $4x^2+12x+3$
- $4x^2+9x+9$

## Explanation

In the trinomial $4x^2+12x+9$ the squares are $4x^2=(2x)^2$ and $9=3^2$, and the middle term is twice the product, $2\cdot 2x\cdot 3=12x$. Therefore, the trinomial equals $(2x+3)^2$. In the other trinomials, either the middle term differs from $12x$ or the constant term is not a square.
