The trinomial $x^2+4x+16$ has two terms that are squares. Why is it not the square of a binomial?

- Twice the product of the bases $x$ and $4$ equals $8x$ rather than $4x$
- The number $16$ is not the square of any number
- The term $x^2$ is not the square of any monomial
- The term $4x$ has a positive sign

## Explanation

The bases of the two squares $x^2$ and $16$ are $x$ and $4$. The middle term of the square $(x+4)^2$ is twice the product, $2\cdot x\cdot 4=8x$, but the middle term of the trinomial is $4x$. Therefore, the trinomial is not the square of a binomial. It is the quadratic factor in a difference of cubes and is irreducible.
