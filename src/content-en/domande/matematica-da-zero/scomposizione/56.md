For every integer $n$, which quantity must be divisible by $7$, as can be verified by factoring?

- $(n+7)^3-n^3$
- $(n+7)^3+n^3$
- $(7n+1)^3$
- $n^3+7$

## Explanation

The difference of cubes contains the difference of the bases, $(n+7)-n=7$, as a factor. The other factor is an integer. Setting $n=1$ rules out all the alternatives, since none of the other three values is a multiple of $7$.
