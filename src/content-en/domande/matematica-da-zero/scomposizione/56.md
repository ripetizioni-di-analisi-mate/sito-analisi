From a cube with edge length $a$ centimeters, with $a>5$, a cube of edge length $5$ centimeters is cut out. The volume that remains is $a^3-125$. Which factorization expresses it as a product?

- $(a-5)(a^2+5a+25)$
- $(a-5)(a^2-5a+25)$
- $(a-5)^3$
- $(a-5)(a^2+25)$

## Explanation

Since $125=5^3$, the remaining volume is a difference of cubes with bases $a$ and $5$. The identity $A^3-B^3=(A-B)(A^2+AB+B^2)$ gives $(a-5)(a^2+5a+25)$. The factor $a^2+5a+25$ is the quadratic factor in this difference of cubes and is irreducible, so the factorization is complete.
