For $x=5$, evaluate the product $\dfrac{x^2-9}{x-1}\cdot\dfrac{x-1}{x+3}$ after simplifying. What is its value?

- $2$
- $8$
- $4$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

Factor $x^2-9=(x+3)(x-3)$ and cancel the factors $x-1$ and $x+3$. The product reduces to $x-3$. For $x=5$ this gives $5-3=2$. The value $8$ is $x+3$, while $4$ and $\dfrac{1}{4}$ come from quotients with the factors $x+3$ and $x-3$ interchanged.
