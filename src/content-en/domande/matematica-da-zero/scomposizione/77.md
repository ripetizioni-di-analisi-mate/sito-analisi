Before simplifying $\dfrac{x^2-25}{x^2-5x}$, determine its domain restrictions. Which values of $x$ are excluded?

- $0$
- $5$ and $-5$
- $-5$ and $0$
- $0$ and $5$

## Explanation

The restrictions come from the original fraction. The denominator $x^2-5x=x\,(x-5)$ is zero for $x=0$ and $x=5$. Simplifying gives $\dfrac{x+5}{x}$, which by itself excludes only $0$, but $5$ remains excluded because it makes the original denominator zero. The value $-5$ makes only the numerator zero.
