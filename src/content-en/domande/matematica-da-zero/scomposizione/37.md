The trinomial $x^2-x-20$ has a negative constant term. What is its factorization?

- $(x+5)(x-4)$
- $(x-5)(x-4)$
- $(x-5)(x+4)$
- $(x-10)(x+2)$

## Explanation

The product $-20$ is negative, so the two numbers have opposite signs. The sum $-1$ is negative, so the negative number has the larger absolute value. The pair $-5$ and $4$ has product $-20$ and sum $-1$, so $x^2-x-20=(x-5)(x+4)$.
