When the numerator and denominator are divided by their GCD, to which fraction does $\dfrac{2x^2+6x}{4x^2-36}$ reduce?

- $\dfrac{x}{x-3}$
- $\dfrac{x}{2\,(x-3)}$
- $\dfrac{x}{2\,(x+3)}$
- $\dfrac{2x}{x-3}$

## Explanation

Factor the numerator by taking out the common factor to obtain $2x^2+6x=2x\,(x+3)$, and factor the denominator by taking out $4$ and applying the difference of squares to obtain $4x^2-36=4\,(x+3)(x-3)$. Dividing by $2\,(x+3)$ leaves $\dfrac{x}{2\,(x-3)}$. The numerical ratio $\dfrac{2}{4}$ leaves $2$ in the denominator.
