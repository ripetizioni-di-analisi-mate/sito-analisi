The polynomial $-3x^2+6x-9$ can be written as a product by factoring out the negative number $-3$. Which product results?

- $-3\,(x^2+2x-3)$
- $-3\,(x^2-2x+3)$
- $-3\,(x^2-2x-3)$
- $3\,(x^2-2x+3)$

## Explanation

Dividing each term by $-3$ gives $\dfrac{-3x^2}{-3}=x^2$, $\dfrac{6x}{-3}=-2x$, and $\dfrac{-9}{-3}=3$. The polynomial can therefore be written as $-3\,(x^2-2x+3)$. When a negative number is factored out, all the signs in the second factor are the opposites of the signs of the original terms.
