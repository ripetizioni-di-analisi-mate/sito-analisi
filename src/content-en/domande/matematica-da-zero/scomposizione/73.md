For $x=3$, what is the value of the fraction $\dfrac{2x+1}{x^2-4x}$?

- $\dfrac{7}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $-\dfrac{7}{3}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

For $x=3$ the numerator equals $2\cdot 3+1=7$ and the denominator equals $9-12=-3$. Their quotient is $\dfrac{7}{-3}=-\dfrac{7}{3}$. The value $\dfrac{1}{3}$ comes from $9+12=21$ and $\dfrac{7}{21}=\dfrac{1}{3}$, a sign error in the denominator.
