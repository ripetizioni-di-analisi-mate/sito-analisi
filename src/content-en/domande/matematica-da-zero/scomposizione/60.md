What is the least common multiple of the monomials $12x^2y$ and $18xy^3$?

- $6xy$
- $36xy$
- $216x^3y^4$
- $36x^2y^3$

## Explanation

The LCM of the coefficients $12=2^2\cdot 3$ and $18=2\cdot 3^2$ is $2^2\cdot 3^2=36$. For each variable take the largest exponent, giving $x^2$ and $y^3$. Therefore $\operatorname{mcm}=36x^2y^3$. The monomial $6xy$ is the GCD, while $216x^3y^4$ is the product of the two monomials.
