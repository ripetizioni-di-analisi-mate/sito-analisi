What is $5^3-2^3$ using the formula $(a-b)(a^2+ab+b^2)$ with $a=5$ and $b=2$?

- $57$
- $87$
- $117$
- $27$

## Explanation

With $a=5$ and $b=2$ the factors are $a-b=3$ and $a^2+ab+b^2=25+10+4=39$. Their product equals $3\cdot 39=117$, which agrees with $125-8$. The value $57$ comes from reversing the sign of the term $ab$, and $87$ comes from omitting it. The value $27$ is the cube of $a-b$.
