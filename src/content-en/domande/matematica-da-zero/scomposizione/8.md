Which monomial is the greatest common factor of the terms in $12x^3+18x^2$?

- $6x^2$
- $6x^3$
- $12x^2$
- $18x^3$

## Explanation

The greatest common divisor of $12$ and $18$ is $6$. The variable $x$ appears in both terms, with smallest exponent $2$. The common factor is therefore $6x^2$ and $12x^3+18x^2=6x^2\,(2x+3)$. The monomial $6x^3$ does not divide $18x^2$, and $12x^2$ does not divide $18x^2$ because $12$ does not divide $18$.
