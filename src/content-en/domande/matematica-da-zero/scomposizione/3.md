For $x=3$, the polynomial $x^2-2x-8$ equals $-5$. Which of the following factorizations gives $-5$ for $x=3$ and could therefore be correct?

- $(x+4)(x-2)$
- $(x-8)(x+1)$
- $(x-4)(x+2)$
- $(x+8)(x-1)$

## Explanation

For $x=3$ the polynomial equals $9-6-8=-5$. The product $(x-4)(x+2)$ equals $(-1)\cdot 5=-5$, so it passes the check. The other products equal $7$, $-20$, and $22$, so they cannot equal the polynomial. A numerical check can rule out incorrect factorizations, but on its own it cannot prove that a factorization is correct.
