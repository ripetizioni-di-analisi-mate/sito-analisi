In the trinomial $x^2-11x+24$ the product $24$ is positive and the sum $-11$ is negative. Which two numbers are needed?

- $-3$ and $-8$
- $3$ and $8$
- $-4$ and $-6$
- $-2$ and $-12$

## Explanation

The product is positive, so the two numbers have the same sign, and the sum is negative, so both are negative. The pairs $(-1,-24)$, $(-2,-12)$, $(-3,-8)$, and $(-4,-6)$ have sums $-25$, $-14$, $-11$, and $-10$. Only $-3$ and $-8$ give $-11$, so $x^2-11x+24=(x-3)(x-8)$.
