One faucet fills a tank in $x$ hours and a second fills it in $x+2$ hours. Together, the two faucets fill $\dfrac{1}{x}+\dfrac{1}{x+2}$ of the tank in one hour. Which expression represents this fraction?

- $\dfrac{2}{2x+2}$
- $\dfrac{2}{x\,(x+2)}$
- $\dfrac{2x+2}{x+2}$
- $\dfrac{2x+2}{x\,(x+2)}$

## Explanation

The common denominator is $x\,(x+2)$. We have $\dfrac{1}{x}+\dfrac{1}{x+2}=\dfrac{(x+2)+x}{x\,(x+2)}=\dfrac{2x+2}{x\,(x+2)}$. For $x=2$ the fraction equals $\dfrac{6}{8}=\dfrac{3}{4}$, and indeed $\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{4}=\dfrac{3}{4}$. The numerator $2x+2$ is the sum of the two times, and the denominator $x\,(x+2)$ is their product.
