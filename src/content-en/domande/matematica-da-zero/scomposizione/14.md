The rectangle in the figure consists of a square of area $x^2$ and a rectangle of area $5x$ sharing a side. What are the side lengths of the large rectangle?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="0,0 4,0 4,4 0,4;4,0 9,0 9,4 4,4" linee="0,0 9,0 9,4 0,4 0,0;4,0 4,4" etichette="1.55,1.9,x²;6.3,1.9,5x" xmin=-1.3 xmax=10 ymin=-1.3 ymax=5.3 titolo="A rectangle made up of a square of area $x^2$ and a rectangle of area $5x$"}

- $x$ and $x+5$
- $x$ and $5x$
- $x^2$ and $5x$
- $x+5$ and $x+5$

## Explanation

The square has area $x^2$, so its side length is $x$. The adjacent rectangle has area $5x$ and the same side length $x$, so its other side measures $5$. The large rectangle therefore has side lengths $x$ and $x+5$, and its area is $x^2+5x=x\,(x+5)$.
