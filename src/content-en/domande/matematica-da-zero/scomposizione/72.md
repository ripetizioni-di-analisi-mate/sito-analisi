The denominator of $\dfrac{1}{x^3-4x}$ factors into three linear factors. For which values of $x$ is the fraction undefined?

- $2$ and $-2$
- $0$ and $4$
- $0$, $2$, and $-2$
- $0$ and $2$

## Explanation

Factor the denominator by taking out $x$ and then using the difference of squares, giving $x^3-4x=x\,(x+2)(x-2)$. It is zero for $x=0$, $x=-2$, and $x=2$, so all three values are excluded. Considering only $x^2-4$ would miss the value $0$.
