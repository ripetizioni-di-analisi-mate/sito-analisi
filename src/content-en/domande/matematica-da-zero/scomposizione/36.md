To factor $x^2+7x+12$, we look for two numbers with sum $7$ and product $12$. What are they?

- $2$ and $6$
- $-3$ and $-4$
- $3$ and $4$
- $1$ and $12$

## Explanation

The pairs of positive integers with product $12$ are $(1,12)$, $(2,6)$, and $(3,4)$, with sums $13$, $8$, and $7$. Only $3$ and $4$ have sum $7$, so $x^2+7x+12=(x+3)(x+4)$. The pair $-3$ and $-4$ has product $12$ but sum $-7$.
