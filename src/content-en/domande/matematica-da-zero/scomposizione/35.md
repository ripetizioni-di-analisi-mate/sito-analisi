How do you write $25-10a+a^2$ as the square of a binomial?

- $(5-a)^2$
- $(5+a)^2$
- $(a+5)(a-5)$
- $(5-a)(5+a)$

## Explanation

The squares are $25=5^2$ and $a^2$, and twice the product of the bases is $2\cdot 5\cdot a=10a$ with a minus sign. Therefore $25-10a+a^2=(5-a)^2$, which equals $(a-5)^2$. The sign inside the binomial is the sign of twice the product.
