For $a = 2$ the polynomial $P(a) = a^2 - 10a + 25$ equals $9$. For which other value of $a$ does it equal $9$ again?

- $8$
- $-2$
- $5$
- $10$

## Explanation

The terms $a^2$ and $25 = 5^2$ are squares and $10a = 2 \cdot a \cdot 5$ is twice the product of the bases $a$ and $5$, so the trinomial is the square of a binomial. The term $10a$ has a minus sign, therefore $P(a) = (a - 5)^2$. Then $P(a) = 9$ when $a - 5 = 3$ or $a - 5 = -3$, because $3^2 = 9$ and $(-3)^2 = 9$. The case $a - 5 = -3$ gives the value already known, $a = 2$, and the case $a - 5 = 3$ gives $a = 8$. The value $-2$ is the opposite of $2$, but $P(-2) = 4 + 20 + 25 = 49$.
