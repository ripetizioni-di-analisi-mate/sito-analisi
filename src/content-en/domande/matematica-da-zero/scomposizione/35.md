The value of $P(a)=a^2-10a+25$ is known at $a=2$. At which other value of $a$ do we get the same result?

- $8$
- $-2$
- $5$
- $10$

## Explanation

Factoring gives $P(a)=(a-5)^2$, so only the distance from $a$ to $5$ matters. The number $2$ is three units to the left of $5$ and $8$ is three units to the right. Both give $9$.
