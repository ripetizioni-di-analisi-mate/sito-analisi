In the binomial $16-x^2$, the square of $x$ is the second term in the difference. What is its factorization?

- $(4-x)(4+x)$
- $(x-4)(x+4)$
- $(4-x)^2$
- $(x+4)^2$

## Explanation

The polynomial $16-x^2$ is the difference of the squares of $4$ and $x$. With $A=4$ and $B=x$ the identity $A^2-B^2=(A+B)(A-B)$ gives $(4+x)(4-x)$. The product $(x-4)(x+4)$ equals $x^2-16$, which is the opposite of the given polynomial.
