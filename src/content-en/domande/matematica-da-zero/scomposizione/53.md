Factor $3x^3-12x$ into irreducible factors. What product results?

- $3x\,(x^2-4)$
- $3x\,(x+2)(x-2)$
- $3x\,(x-2)^2$
- $3\,(x^3-4x)$

## Explanation

Factoring out the common factor $3x$ gives $3x^3-12x=3x\,(x^2-4)$. The binomial $x^2-4$ is a difference of squares and equals $(x+2)(x-2)$. The complete factorization is therefore $3x\,(x+2)(x-2)$. The expressions $3x\,(x^2-4)$ and $3\,(x^3-4x)$ are correct but stop too early because they still contain factors that can be factored further.
