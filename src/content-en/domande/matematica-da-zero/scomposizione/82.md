The numerator of $\dfrac{x^2+10x+25}{x^2+5x}$ is the square of a binomial. Which fraction results from simplifying?

- $\dfrac{2x+5}{x}$
- $\dfrac{x-5}{x}$
- $\dfrac{x+5}{x^2}$
- $\dfrac{x+5}{x}$

## Explanation

The numerator is the square of a binomial, $x^2+10x+25=(x+5)^2$, and taking a common factor out of the denominator gives $x^2+5x=x\,(x+5)$. Dividing by $x+5$ leaves $\dfrac{x+5}{x}$. The result $\dfrac{2x+5}{x}$ comes from canceling the terms $x^2$, which are not factors.
