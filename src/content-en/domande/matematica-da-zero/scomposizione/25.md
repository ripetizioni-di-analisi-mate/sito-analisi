For which value of $k$ does the trinomial $x^2+kx+36$ equal $(x+6)^2$?

- $6$
- $18$
- $12$
- $36$

## Explanation

Expanding gives $(x+6)^2=x^2+12x+36$ because twice the product of $x$ and $6$ equals $2\cdot x\cdot 6=12x$. The coefficient of $x$ must therefore be $k=12$. The values $6$ and $36$ are the base and its square rather than twice the product.
