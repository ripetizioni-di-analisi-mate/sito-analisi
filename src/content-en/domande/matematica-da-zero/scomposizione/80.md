A car travels $x^2-16$ kilometers in $x+4$ hours, with $x>4$. Its average speed in kilometers per hour is $\dfrac{x^2-16}{x+4}$. What is the simplified expression?

- $x+4$
- $x^2-4$
- $\dfrac{x-4}{x+4}$
- $x-4$

## Explanation

The numerator is a difference of squares, $x^2-16=(x+4)(x-4)$. Dividing by the common factor $x+4$, which is nonzero because $x>4$, leaves $x-4$. The average speed is therefore $x-4$ kilometers per hour. The result $x^2-4$ comes from dividing by $4$ only the term $-16$.
