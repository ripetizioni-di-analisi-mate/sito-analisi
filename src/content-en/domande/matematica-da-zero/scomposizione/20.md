The terms of $x^2+3y+xy+3x$ are not in a convenient order for grouping. What factorization results from rearranging them?

- $(x+3)(x-y)$
- $(x-3)(x+y)$
- $(x+3y)(x+1)$
- $(x+3)(x+y)$

## Explanation

Rewrite the polynomial as $x^2+3x+xy+3y$. Factoring $x$ out of the first two terms and $y$ out of the last two gives $x\,(x+3)+y\,(x+3)$. The binomial $x+3$ is shared, so the polynomial is $(x+3)(x+y)$.
