From the square of side length $a$ in the figure, a square of side length $4$ is removed, with $a>4$. The remaining region can be cut up and rearranged to form a rectangle. What are its side lengths?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="0,0 8,0 8,4 4,4 4,8 0,8" linee="0,0 8,0 8,4 4,4 4,8 0,8 0,0" segmenti="4,8,8,8;8,4,8,8" etichette="3.8,-0.7,a;-0.7,3.8,a;5.8,8.4,4;8.4,5.8,4" xmin=-1.3 xmax=9.5 ymin=-1.3 ymax=9.3 titolo="A square of side length $a$ with a square of side length $4$ removed from one corner"}

- $a$ and $a-4$
- $a-4$ and $a-4$
- $a+4$ and $a-4$
- $a^2$ and $16$

## Explanation

The remaining region has area $a^2-16$. By the difference of squares, $a^2-16=(a+4)(a-4)$, so a rectangle with the same area has side lengths $a+4$ and $a-4$. The product $a\,(a-4)$ equals $a^2-4a$, and $(a-4)^2$ equals $a^2-8a+16$, so neither equals $a^2-16$.
