What product results from factoring $3ab$ out of $6a^3b-9a^2b^2+3ab$?

- $3ab\,(2a^2-3ab)$
- $3ab\,(2a^2-3ab+1)$
- $3ab\,(2a^2-3b+1)$
- $3ab\,(2a^2-3ab-1)$

## Explanation

Dividing each term by $3ab$ gives $\dfrac{6a^3b}{3ab}=2a^2$, $\dfrac{-9a^2b^2}{3ab}=-3ab$, and $\dfrac{3ab}{3ab}=1$. The last term equals the common factor, so it leaves $1$. Therefore, the polynomial is $3ab\,(2a^2-3ab+1)$. Without the term $1$ the product would equal $6a^3b-9a^2b^2$, which has one fewer term.
