For $x=1$, compare the fractions $\dfrac{x+2}{x+6}$ and $\dfrac{2}{6}$. What values do they give?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$ and $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{7}$ and $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{7}$ and $\dfrac{3}{7}$
- $\dfrac{7}{3}$ and $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

With $x=1$ the first fraction equals $\dfrac{1+2}{1+6}=\dfrac{3}{7}$ and the second equals $\dfrac{2}{6}=\dfrac{1}{3}$. The two values differ, so $\dfrac{x+2}{x+6}$ and $\dfrac{2}{6}$ are not equal. Simplification cancels common factors and never individual terms of a sum.
