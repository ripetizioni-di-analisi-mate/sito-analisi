For $x^2+3x-10$ we need two numbers with product $-10$ and sum $3$. Which pair satisfies both conditions?

- $-5$ and $2$
- $5$ and $-2$
- $5$ and $2$
- $-5$ and $-2$

## Explanation

A negative product requires numbers of opposite signs, and a positive sum requires the positive number to have the larger absolute value. The pair $5$ and $-2$ has product $-10$ and sum $3$. The pair $-5$ and $2$ has sum $-3$, whereas $5$ and $2$ have product $10$.
