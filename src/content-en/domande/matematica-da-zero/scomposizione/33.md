To show that $x^2+25$ does not equal $(x+5)^2$, substitute $x=1$. What values do the two expressions give?

- $26$ and $26$
- $36$ and $36$
- $26$ and $30$
- $26$ and $36$

## Explanation

For $x=1$ we have $x^2+25=1+25=26$ and $(x+5)^2=6^2=36$. The values are different, so the expressions are not equal. Indeed, $(x+5)^2=x^2+10x+25$ contains twice the product, $10x$, which is absent from $x^2+25$. For $x=1$ this term equals $10$, precisely the difference $36-26$ between the two values.
