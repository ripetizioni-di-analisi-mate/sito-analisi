The side of the square in the figure is divided into two parts of lengths $x$ and $5$. Which expression gives the area of the entire square?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="0,0 4,0 4,4 0,4;4,0 9,0 9,4 4,4;0,4 4,4 4,9 0,9;4,4 9,4 9,9 4,9" linee="0,0 9,0 9,9 0,9 0,0;4,0 4,9;0,4 9,4" etichette="1.85,-0.65,x;6.35,-0.65,5;-0.75,1.9,x;-0.75,6.3,5" xmin=-1.3 xmax=10 ymin=-1.3 ymax=10 titolo="A square whose side is divided into two parts of lengths $x$ and $5$"}

- $x^2+25$
- $x^2+10x+25$
- $x^2+5x+25$
- $2x+10$

## Explanation

The side of the square measures $x+5$, so its area is $(x+5)^2=x^2+10x+25$. In the figure, the square is divided into four parts: a square of area $x^2$, two rectangles of area $5x$, and a square of area $25$. The sum $x^2+25$ omits both rectangles, whereas $2x+10$ is half the perimeter.
