Which of the following polynomials cannot be written as a product of linear factors?

- $x^2-25$
- $x^2+10x+25$
- $x^2+25$
- $x^2+5x$

## Explanation

Since $x^2\geq 0$, we have $x^2+25\geq 25$, so the polynomial is never zero and has no linear factors. The other three can be factored: $x^2-25=(x+5)(x-5)$, $x^2+10x+25=(x+5)^2$, and $x^2+5x=x\,(x+5)$. Therefore, the irreducible polynomial is $x^2+25$.
