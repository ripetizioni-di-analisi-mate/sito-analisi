Consider the quotient $\dfrac{x}{x+3}:\dfrac{x-2}{x}$. Which values of $x$ are excluded?

- $-3$ and $0$
- $-3$, $0$, and $2$
- $-3$ and $2$
- $0$ and $2$

## Explanation

The denominator $x+3$ of the first fraction excludes $-3$, and the denominator $x$ of the second excludes $0$. In division, the second fraction is the divisor, so it must be nonzero. Its numerator $x-2$ excludes $2$. Therefore, the excluded values are $-3$, $0$, and $2$.
