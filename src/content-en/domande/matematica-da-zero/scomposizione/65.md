Find the GCD of $x^2+1$ and $x^2-1$. What is the result?

- $x+1$
- $x-1$
- $x^2+1$
- $1$

## Explanation

The polynomial $x^2+1$ is irreducible because $x^2+1\geq 1$ is never zero, so its only divisors are numbers and the polynomial itself. The polynomial $x^2-1=(x-1)(x+1)$ does not contain the factor $x^2+1$. The polynomials share no factors of positive degree, so their GCD is $1$.
