Two ribbons, one $x^2+3x$ meters long and the other $x^2+4x+3$ meters long, are cut into equal pieces of the greatest possible length. How long is each piece, in meters?

- $x\,(x+1)(x+3)$
- $x$
- $x+3$
- $x+1$

## Explanation

The length of a piece is the GCD of the two lengths. We have $x^2+3x=x\,(x+3)$ and $x^2+4x+3=(x+1)(x+3)$. The only common factor is $x+3$, so each piece measures $x+3$ meters. The product $x\,(x+1)(x+3)$ is the LCM and would make a piece longer than either ribbon.
