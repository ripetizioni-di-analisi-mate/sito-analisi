For which values of $x$ does the product $(x-2)(2x+6)$ equal zero?

- $2$ and $3$
- $-2$ and $-3$
- $2$ and $6$
- $2$ and $-3$

## Explanation

By the zero product property, the product is zero when at least one factor is zero. From $x-2=0$ we obtain $x=2$. From $2x+6=0$ we obtain $2x=-6$ and therefore $x=-3$. The number $6$ is the constant term of the second factor rather than the solution.
