Write $1001=10^3+1^3$. What product of two integers results from applying the sum of cubes formula?

- $11\cdot 111$
- $11\cdot 109$
- $11\cdot 90$
- $11\cdot 91$

## Explanation

With $a=10$ and $b=1$ the formula $a^3+b^3=(a+b)(a^2-ab+b^2)$ gives the first factor $a+b=11$ and the second $a^2-ab+b^2=100-10+1=91$. The product $11\cdot 91$ equals $1001$. The value $111$ comes from using a plus sign before $ab$. The value $109$ comes from using a minus sign before $b^2$ and a plus sign before $ab$, and $90$ comes from omitting $b^2$.
