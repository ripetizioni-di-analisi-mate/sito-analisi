The trinomial $x^2+sx+18$ factors as $(x+3)(x+6)$. What is $s$?

- $18$
- $9$
- $3$
- $6$

## Explanation

Expanding the product gives $(x+3)(x+6)=x^2+6x+3x+18=x^2+9x+18$. The coefficient of $x$ is the sum of the two numbers, $3+6=9$, so $s=9$. The constant term $18$ is the product $3\cdot 6$, so the two numbers have sum $9$ and product $18$.
