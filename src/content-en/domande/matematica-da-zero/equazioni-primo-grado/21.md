For what value of $x$ are the two sides of $9x-4=5x+12$ equal?

- $2$
- $\dfrac{4}{7}$
- $-4$
- $4$

## Explanation

Moving the terms containing $x$ to the left and the numbers to the right gives $9x-5x=12+4$. Therefore $4x=16$ and $x=4$. The value $2$ results from writing $12-4$ on the right-hand side, whereas $\dfrac{4}{7}$ results from adding $9x$ and $5x$.
