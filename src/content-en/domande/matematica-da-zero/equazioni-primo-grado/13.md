Using the transposition rule, which equation results from $6x-5=2x+7$ when the terms containing $x$ are moved to the left and the numbers to the right?

- $6x+2x=7+5$
- $6x-2x=7+5$
- $6x-2x=7-5$
- $6x+2x=7-5$

## Explanation

The term $2x$ moves to the left-hand side and changes sign, so it becomes $-2x$. The term $-5$ moves to the right-hand side and becomes $+5$. The equation is therefore $6x-2x=7+5$, that is, $4x=12$. The other answers give the wrong sign to at least one of the two terms that move.
