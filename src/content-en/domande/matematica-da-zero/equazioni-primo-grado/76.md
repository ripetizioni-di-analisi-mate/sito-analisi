The circumference of a circle of radius $r$ is $C=2\pi r$. Which formula gives the radius?

- $r=\dfrac{2\pi}{C}$
- $r=C-2\pi$
- $r=\dfrac{C}{2\pi}$
- $r=\dfrac{C}{\pi}$

## Explanation

The radius is multiplied by $2\pi$, which is nonzero. Dividing both sides by $2\pi$ gives $r=\dfrac{C}{2\pi}$. With $C=31.4$ and $\pi=3.14$, we have $r=\dfrac{31.4}{6.28}=5$. The formula with $\pi$ in the denominator gives the diameter.
