After simplification, the equation $3(x-2)+6=3x$ becomes an equality involving numbers alone. What are its solutions?

- No solution
- Only $x=0$
- Only $x=2$
- Every number is a solution

## Explanation

The left-hand side equals $3x-6+6=3x$, so the equation is $3x=3x$, that is, $0\cdot x=0$. The equality is true for every $x$, so the equation is an identity and every number is a solution. The term $3x$ appears on both sides and cancels, leaving an equality between numbers that is always true.
