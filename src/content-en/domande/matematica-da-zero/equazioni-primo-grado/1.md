Which of the following numbers is a solution of the equation $4x-7=5$?

- $12$
- $-2$
- $48$
- $3$

## Explanation

Adding $7$ to both sides gives $4x=12$. We then divide both sides by $4$, so $x=3$. Checking gives $4\cdot 3-7=5$. The value $12$ results from omitting the division by $4$, whereas $48$ results from multiplying by $4$ instead of dividing.
