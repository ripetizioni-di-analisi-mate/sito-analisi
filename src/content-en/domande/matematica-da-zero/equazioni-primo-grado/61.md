We seek the number $x$ for which the sum of $\dfrac{1}{x}$ and $\dfrac{1}{2}$ equals $\dfrac{3}{4}$. What is $x$?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $-4$
- $4$
- $2$

## Explanation

The domain restrictions are $x\neq 0$. Multiplying by $4x$ gives $4+2x=3x$, so $x=4$. Checking gives $\dfrac{1}{4}+\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{3}{4}$. The value $\dfrac{1}{4}$ is the right-hand side minus $\dfrac{1}{2}$, that is, the value of $\dfrac{1}{x}$, not of $x$.
