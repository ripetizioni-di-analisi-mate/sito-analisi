The equation $6x+1=10x-5$ has solution $x=\dfrac{3}{2}$. We replace $6x$ by $8x$ on the left and leave the right side unchanged. What number must replace $+1$ to keep the same solution?

- $-1$
- $+3$
- $-2$
- $+4$

## Explanation

At the given solution the new term in $x$ exceeds the old one by $2x=3$. The constant must decrease by $3$ and become $1-3=-2$. Substitution gives $10$ on each side.
