Which is the solution of the equation $6x+1=10x-5$?

- $-\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

Moving the terms gives $6x-10x=-5-1$, that is, $-4x=-6$. Dividing by $-4$ yields $x=\dfrac{-6}{-4}=\dfrac{3}{2}$. The quotient of two negative numbers is positive. The value $-\dfrac{3}{2}$ results from an incorrect final sign, whereas $\dfrac{2}{3}$ results from inverting the quotient.
