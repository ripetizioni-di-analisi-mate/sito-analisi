When you solve $2-\dfrac{x-1}{3}=\dfrac{x}{2}$, what value of $x$ do you obtain?

- $\dfrac{14}{5}$
- $2$
- $\dfrac{4}{5}$
- $14$

## Explanation

Multiplying by $6$ gives $12-2(x-1)=3x$. The minus sign changes the sign of both terms, so $12-2x+2=3x$, that is, $14=5x$ and $x=\dfrac{14}{5}$. The value $2$ results from writing $-2$ instead of $+2$, whereas $14$ results from omitting the division by $5$.
