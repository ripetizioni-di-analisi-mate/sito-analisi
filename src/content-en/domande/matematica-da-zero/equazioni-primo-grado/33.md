In solving $5x-3=2x+9$, a student writes $5x+2x=9+3$. What error was made?

- The term $-3$ should not have become $+3$
- The terms containing $x$ should be moved to the right-hand side
- The term $2x$ moved to the left-hand side without changing sign
- The term $9$ should have become $-9$

## Explanation

By the transposition rule, the term $-3$ moves to the right as $+3$, which is correct. The term $2x$ should have moved to the left as $-2x$. The correct line is therefore $5x-2x=9+3$, that is, $3x=12$, from which $x=4$ follows.
