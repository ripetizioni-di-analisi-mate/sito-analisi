The rational equation $\dfrac{2}{x}=\dfrac{3}{x+1}$ has one admissible solution. What is it?

- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $2$
- $-2$

## Explanation

The domain restrictions are $x\neq 0$ and $x\neq -1$. Multiplying by $x(x+1)$ gives $2(x+1)=3x$, that is, $2x+2=3x$ and $x=2$. The value $2$ satisfies the restrictions. The value $1$ results from omitting the factor $2$ in the term $1$.
