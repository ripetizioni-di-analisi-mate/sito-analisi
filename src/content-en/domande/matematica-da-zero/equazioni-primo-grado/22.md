Solve the equation $3(x-4)=2(x+1)$. Which solution do you find?

- $6$
- $\dfrac{14}{5}$
- $14$
- $10$

## Explanation

The distributive property gives $3x-12=2x+2$. Moving the terms gives $3x-2x=2+12$, so $x=14$. The value $6$ results from multiplying $3$ only by $x$, whereas $\dfrac{14}{5}$ results from moving $2x$ without changing its sign.
