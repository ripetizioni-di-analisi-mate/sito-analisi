After a $20\%$ discount, a sweater costs $48$ euros. What was its price in euros before the discount?

- $60$
- $68$
- $240$
- $38.4$

## Explanation

If $x$ is the original price, the discounted price is $x-\dfrac{20}{100}x=\dfrac{80}{100}x$. The equation is $0.8x=48$, so $x=\dfrac{48}{0.8}=60$. The value $68$ results from adding $20$ to $48$, whereas $38.4$ results from taking $80\%$ of $48$. Checking gives $0.8\cdot 60=48$.
