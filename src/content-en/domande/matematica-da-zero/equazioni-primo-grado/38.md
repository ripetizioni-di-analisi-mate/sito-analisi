Solve $\dfrac{x-1}{2}-\dfrac{x+2}{3}=-1$. What is the value of $x$?

- $1$
- $-7$
- $6$
- $-13$

## Explanation

Multiplying by $6$ gives $3(x-1)-2(x+2)=-6$. The minus sign changes the sign of both terms of $x+2$, so $3x-3-2x-4=-6$ and $x-7=-6$. Therefore $x=1$. The value $6$ results from neglecting to multiply the right-hand side by $6$.
