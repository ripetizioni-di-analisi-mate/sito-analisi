Two cyclists leave at the same time from two cities $90$ km apart and ride toward each other at $14$ km/h and $16$ km/h. After how many hours do they meet?

- $45$
- $\dfrac{45}{7}$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{45}{8}$

## Explanation

In $x$ hours the cyclists travel $14x$ km and $16x$ km. When they meet, the sum of the distances they have traveled equals the distance between the cities, so $14x+16x=90$, that is, $30x=90$ and $x=3$. The distances are $42$ km and $48$ km, and $42+48=90$.
