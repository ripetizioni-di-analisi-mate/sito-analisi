A stationery store sells pens for $2$ euros and notebooks for $3$ euros. Luca buys $10$ items and pays $24$ euros. How many notebooks does he buy?

- $6$
- $8$
- $12$
- $4$

## Explanation

If he buys $q$ notebooks, he buys $10-q$ pens. The cost gives $3q+2(10-q)=24$, that is, $q+20=24$ and $q=4$. There are $6$ pens, and $12+12=24$. The value $6$ counts the pens, not the notebooks. With $q=4$, the notebooks cost $12$ euros and the pens cost $12$ euros.
