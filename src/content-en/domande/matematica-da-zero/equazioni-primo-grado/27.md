A calculator rounds each side to the nearest integer and displays $3=3$ when $x=1$ is entered in the equation $0.4x+3=0.6x+2$. Which conclusion is correct?

- The displayed equality proves that $1$ is a solution.
- Every integer from $1$ to $5$ is a solution.
- The exact sides differ and the solution is $5$.
- The equation has no solution because the decimal coefficients differ.

## Explanation

At $x=1$ the exact sides are $3.4$ and $2.6$, which round to the same value. Exact equality requires $1=0.2x$, giving $x=5$.
