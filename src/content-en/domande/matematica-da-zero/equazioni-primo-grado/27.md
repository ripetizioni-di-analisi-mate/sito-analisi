Solving $0.5x+1.5=2x-3$ gives

- $x=-3$
- $x=1.8$
- $x=3$
- $x=9$

## Explanation

Moving the terms gives $0.5x-2x=-3-1.5$, that is, $-1.5x=-4.5$. Dividing by $-1.5$ yields $x=3$. The value $9$ results from dividing by $0.5$ instead of $1.5$, whereas $1.8$ results from adding the coefficients of $x$ instead of subtracting them.
