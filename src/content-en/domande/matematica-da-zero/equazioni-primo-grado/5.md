Only one of the following is an equation in the unknown $x$. Which one?

- $2x+1=7$
- $2x+1$
- $2\cdot 3+1=7$
- $2+1=7$

## Explanation

An equation is an equality containing an unknown. The statement $2x+1=7$ contains the $=$ sign and the letter $x$. The expression $2x+1$ has no $=$ sign, while $2\cdot 3+1=7$ and $2+1=7$ are equalities involving numbers alone, one true and one false.
