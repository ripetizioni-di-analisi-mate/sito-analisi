For an equation written as “left side $=$ right side”, left minus right is $-6$ at $x=0$ and $-2$ at $x=2$. Both sides are linear expressions. At which $x$ are the sides equal?

- $3$
- $1$
- $2$
- $6$

## Explanation

The difference of two linear expressions changes at a constant rate. Here it rises by $4$ in two steps, or $2$ per unit of $x$. Starting from $-2$ at $x=2$, one more step brings it to zero.
