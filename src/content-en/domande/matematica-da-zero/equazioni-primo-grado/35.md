The coefficient of $x$ on the left-hand side of the equation $-x+9=2x+18$ is negative. Which number solves the equation?

- $-3$
- $3$
- $-9$
- $9$

## Explanation

Moving the terms gives $-x-2x=18-9$, that is, $-3x=9$. Dividing by $-3$ yields $x=-3$. Checking gives $3+9=12$ on the left-hand side and $-6+18=12$ on the right. The value $9$ results from omitting the division by $-3$, whereas $3$ results from an incorrect sign in the quotient.
