The mean of two grades $a$ and $b$ is $m=\dfrac{a+b}{2}$. A student earned $5.5$ on the first test. What grade $b$ does the student need on the second test to obtain a mean of $7$?

- $1.5$
- $12.5$
- $8.5$
- $6.25$

## Explanation

Multiplying both sides by $2$ gives $14=5.5+b$. Therefore $b=14-5.5=8.5$. Checking gives $\dfrac{5.5+8.5}{2}=7$. The value $12.5$ results from adding $5.5$ to $7$, whereas $1.5$ results from subtracting $5.5$ from $7$. The grade $6.25$ is the mean of $5.5$ and $7$.
