Which values are excluded from the solutions of $\dfrac{3}{x^2-9}=\dfrac{1}{x}$?

- $x\neq 0$ and $x\neq 9$
- $x\neq 0$ and $x\neq 3$
- $x\neq 9$ and $x\neq -9$
- $x\neq 0$, $x\neq 3$, and $x\neq -3$

## Explanation

By the difference of squares, $x^2-9=(x-3)(x+3)$, and the product is zero when $x=3$ or $x=-3$. The denominator $x$ is zero when $x=0$. The domain restrictions therefore exclude $0$, $3$, and $-3$. When $x=9$, the denominator $x^2-9$ equals $72$, so the value $9$ should not be excluded.
