The lines $y=2x+1$ and $y=-x+4$ intersect at a point whose x-coordinate solves the equation $2x+1=-x+4$. What is this coordinate?

::grafico{f="2*x+1;-x+4" xmin=-2 xmax=4 ymin=-3 ymax=9 legenda="y = 2x + 1;y = -x + 4" titolo="The lines $y = 2x + 1$ and $y = -x + 4$"}

- $3$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $-1$

## Explanation

The desired value is the x-coordinate of the intersection. The equation gives $2x+x=4-1$, that is, $3x=3$ and $x=1$. The intersection is $(1,3)$, and $3$ is its y-coordinate, not the solution.
