What is the solution of the equation $(x-2)^2=x^2+8$?

- $-2$
- $-3$
- $1$
- $-1$

## Explanation

The square of the binomial is $(x-2)^2=x^2-4x+4$. Therefore $x^2-4x+4=x^2+8$. Subtracting $x^2$ from both sides gives $-4x=4$, so $x=-1$. The value $-2$ results from using $-2x$ instead of $-4x$ for twice the product.
