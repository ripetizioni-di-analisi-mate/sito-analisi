In the equation $7-2t=1$, the unknown is the letter $t$. Which value of $t$ solves the equation?

- $-3$
- $3$
- $4$
- $-4$

## Explanation

Subtracting $7$ from both sides gives $-2t=-6$. We divide both sides by $-2$, so $t=\dfrac{-6}{-2}=3$. The value $-3$ results from an incorrect sign in the quotient, whereas $4$ results from adding $1$ to $7$ and dividing by $2$.
