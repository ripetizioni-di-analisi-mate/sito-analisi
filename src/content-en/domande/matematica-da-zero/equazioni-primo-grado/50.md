The equation $5x+4=2x+4$ has the same constant term on both sides. How is it classified?

- Inconsistent because the constant terms are equal
- An identity because the constant terms are equal
- Conditional, with solution $x=0$
- Conditional, with solution $x=4$

## Explanation

Moving the terms gives $5x-2x=4-4$, that is, $3x=0$. Dividing by $3$ yields $x=0$. The equation therefore has a single solution and is conditional. Equal constant terms alone do not make an equation inconsistent or an identity.
