When $x=4$, what are the values of the left- and right-hand sides of the equation $5x-4=3x+2$?

- $20$ and $14$
- $16$ and $12$
- $16$ and $14$
- $14$ and $16$

## Explanation

The left-hand side equals $5\cdot 4-4=20-4=16$. The right-hand side equals $3\cdot 4+2=12+2=14$. The two values differ, so $x=4$ is not a solution. The value $20$ results from omitting the subtraction of $4$, whereas $12$ results from omitting the addition of $2$.
