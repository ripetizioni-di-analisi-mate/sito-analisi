Marco is $12$ years old and his mother is $40$. In how many years will his mother be twice as old as Marco?

- $16$
- $28$
- $8$
- $52$

## Explanation

In $x$ years his mother will be $40+x$ years old and Marco will be $12+x$. The equation is $40+x=2(12+x)$, that is, $40+x=24+2x$. Therefore $x=16$. In $16$ years his mother will be $56$ years old and Marco will be $28$, and $56=2\cdot 28$. The value $28$ is the difference between their ages, which does not change over time.
