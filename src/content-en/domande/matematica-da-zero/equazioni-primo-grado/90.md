A piggy bank contains $25$ coins in denominations of $1$ euro and $2$ euros, totaling $41$ euros. How many $2$-euro coins are there?

- $16$
- $9$
- $20$
- $12$

## Explanation

If the number of $2$-euro coins is $y$, the number of $1$-euro coins is $25-y$. The total gives $2y+(25-y)=41$, that is, $y+25=41$ and $y=16$. The number of $1$-euro coins is $25-16=9$, and $2\cdot 16+9=41$. The value $9$ counts the $1$-euro coins, not the $2$-euro coins.
