A notebook costs $x$ euros. Three notebooks and a pen costing $2$ euros cost $11$ euros altogether. What is the price of one notebook in euros?

- $3$
- $\dfrac{13}{3}$
- $9$
- $\dfrac{11}{5}$

## Explanation

The total cost is $3x+2=11$. Subtracting $2$ from both sides gives $3x=9$, so $x=3$. Checking gives $3\cdot 3+2=11$. The value $\dfrac{13}{3}$ results from adding $2$ instead of subtracting it, whereas $9$ results from omitting the division by $3$.
