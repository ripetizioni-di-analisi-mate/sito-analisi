Two equations are equivalent if they have the same solutions. Which of the following pairs consists of equivalent equations?

- $x+2=6$ and $2x=6$
- $2x=8$ and $x+1=5$
- $3x=9$ and $x-3=3$
- $x-1=4$ and $x+1=4$

## Explanation

The equation $2x=8$ has solution $x=4$, and $x+1=5$ also has solution $x=4$. In the other pairs the solutions differ: $4$ and $3$, then $3$ and $6$, and finally $5$ and $3$. Therefore only the first pair consists of equivalent equations.
