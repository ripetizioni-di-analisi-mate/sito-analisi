The base of a rectangle exceeds its height by $4$ cm, and its perimeter is $28$ cm. What is its height in cm?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="0,0 9,0 9,5 0,5" linee="0,0 9,0 9,5 0,5 0,0" etichette="3.6,-0.8,h + 4;9.3,2.3,h;3.2,2.3,P = 28 cm" xmin=-1 xmax=11.5 ymin=-1.6 ymax=6 titolo="Rectangle with height $h$, base $h + 4$, and perimeter $28$ cm"}

- $9$
- $5$
- $7$
- $12$

## Explanation

The base is $h+4$, so the perimeter is $2(h+h+4)=28$. This gives $4h+8=28$, that is, $4h=20$ and $h=5$. The base is $9$ cm long, and the perimeter is $2(5+9)=28$ cm. The value $9$ is the base, whereas $12$ results from omitting the factor $2$ in the perimeter formula.
