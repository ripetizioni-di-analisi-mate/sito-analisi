Which of the following equalities is an identity, that is, true for every value of $x$?

- $3(x+2)=3x+2$
- $3(x+2)=x+6$
- $3(x+2)=3x+9$
- $3(x+2)=3x+6$

## Explanation

By the distributive property, the left-hand side is $3(x+2)=3x+6$, so it equals the right-hand side for every $x$. In the other equalities, $3$ multiplies only $x$ or the constant term is incorrect, and for $x=1$ the two sides have different values, for example $9$ and $7$ in the second equality.
