The mean of three numbers is $12$. One is replaced by twice its value and the new mean is $14$. What was the original value of the replaced number?

- $2$
- $6$
- $7$
- $12$

## Explanation

The original sum is $36$ and the final sum is $42$. Replacing $x$ by $2x$ raises the sum by $x$. Thus $36+x=42$ and $x=6$.
