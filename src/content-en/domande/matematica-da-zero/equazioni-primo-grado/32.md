After simplification, the equation $12-3x=2x-18$ has solution

- $x=-\dfrac{6}{5}$
- $x=6$
- $x=-6$
- $x=30$

## Explanation

Moving the terms gives $12+18=2x+3x$, that is, $30=5x$. Dividing by $5$ yields $x=6$. The value $30$ results from omitting the division, whereas $-\dfrac{6}{5}$ results from moving $18$ without changing its sign.
