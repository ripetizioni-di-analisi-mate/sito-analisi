Which number solves $\dfrac{x+3}{4}=-2$?

- $-5$
- $-11$
- $11$
- $-\dfrac{7}{2}$

## Explanation

Multiplying both sides by $4$ gives $x+3=-8$. Subtracting $3$ yields $x=-11$. Checking gives $\dfrac{-11+3}{4}=\dfrac{-8}{4}=-2$. The value $-5$ results from adding $3$ to $-8$ instead of subtracting it. We multiply both sides by $4$ because it is the denominator on the left-hand side.
