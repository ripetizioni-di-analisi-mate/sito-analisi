For what value of $k$ is $x=2$ a solution of the equation $3x+k=10$?

- $16$
- $4$
- $7$
- $5$

## Explanation

Substituting $x=2$ gives $3\cdot 2+k=10$, that is, $6+k=10$. Subtracting $6$ from both sides yields $k=4$. The value $16$ results from adding $6$ instead of subtracting it, whereas $7$ results from subtracting $3$ instead of $6$.
