On the left pan of a balanced scale are $4$ boxes, each weighing $x$ kg, and one weight of $3$ kg. On the right pan are $2$ identical boxes and one weight of $9$ kg. Which equation describes the balance?

- $4x+3=2x\cdot 9$
- $4x+3=2x+9$
- $4+3x=2+9x$
- $4x+9=2x+3$

## Explanation

The weight on the left pan is $4x+3$, and the weight on the right pan is $2x+9$. The scale is balanced, so the two weights are equal and $4x+3=2x+9$. The equation gives $2x=6$, that is, $x=3$, and indeed $4\cdot 3+3=15$ and $2\cdot 3+9=15$.
