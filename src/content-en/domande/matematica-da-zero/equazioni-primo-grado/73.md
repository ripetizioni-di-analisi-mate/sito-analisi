Two receipts for the same pass show $39$ euros for $6$ visits and $55$ euros for $10$. The price is a fixed fee plus a constant charge per visit. What is the fixed fee?

- $4$ euros
- $16$ euros
- $24$ euros
- $15$ euros

## Explanation

The receipts differ by $16$ euros for four visits, so each visit costs $4$ euros. Subtracting $6\cdot4$ from the $39$ euros on the first receipt leaves a fixed fee of $15$ euros.
