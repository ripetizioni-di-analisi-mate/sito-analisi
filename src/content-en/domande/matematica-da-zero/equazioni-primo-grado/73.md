A membership costs $C=15+4n$ euros, where $n$ is the number of visits. How many visits can you make for $63$ euros?

- $\dfrac{39}{2}$
- $48$
- $\dfrac{63}{4}$
- $12$

## Explanation

Set $15+4n=63$. Subtracting $15$ gives $4n=48$, so $n=12$. Checking gives $15+4\cdot 12=63$. The value $\dfrac{39}{2}$ results from adding $15$ instead of subtracting it, whereas $48$ results from omitting the division by $4$.
