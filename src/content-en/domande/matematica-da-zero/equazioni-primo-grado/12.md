Which operation on both sides transforms the equation $\dfrac{x}{4}=3$ into the equation $x=12$?

- Divide both sides by $4$
- Add $4$ to both sides
- Multiply only the left-hand side by $4$
- Multiply both sides by $4$

## Explanation

The left-hand side $\dfrac{x}{4}$ reduces to $x$ when multiplied by $4$. By the second equivalence principle, the same operation must also be applied to the right-hand side, which becomes $3\cdot 4=12$. Multiplying just one side would not preserve the equality.
