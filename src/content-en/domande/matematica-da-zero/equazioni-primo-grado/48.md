The equation $2(x+3)=2x+5$ simplifies to $a\,x=b$. What solutions does it have?

- A single solution, $x=0$
- No solution
- Every number is a solution
- A single solution, $x=-1$

## Explanation

Expanding the product gives $2x+6=2x+5$. Subtracting $2x$ from both sides gives $6=5$, which is false for every $x$. Therefore the equation is inconsistent and has no solutions. The term $2x$ appears with the same coefficient on both sides and cancels, leaving an equality involving numbers alone.
