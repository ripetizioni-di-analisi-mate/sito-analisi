Which value of $x$ satisfies $\dfrac{2}{5}x+1=\dfrac{3}{10}x+\dfrac{3}{2}$?

- $14$
- $5$
- $\dfrac{5}{7}$
- $-5$

## Explanation

The least common multiple is $10$. This gives $4x+10=3x+15$, so $4x-3x=15-10$ and $x=5$. The value $14$ results from omitting the factor $10$ in the term $1$. The value $-5$ results from writing $10-15$ instead of $15-10$.
