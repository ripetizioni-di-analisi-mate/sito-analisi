Subtracting $4$ from three times a number gives the number increased by $10$. Which equation represents this statement?

- $3(x-4)=x+10$
- $3x-4=x-10$
- $3x-4=x+10$
- $3x+4=x+10$

## Explanation

Three times a number is $3x$, and decreasing it by $4$ gives $3x-4$. The number increased by $10$ is $x+10$. The equation is $3x-4=x+10$, from which $2x=14$ and $x=7$ follow. The equation $3(x-4)=x+10$ instead subtracts $4$ from the number before tripling it.
