A car increases its speed from $8$ m/s to $26$ m/s in $6$ s with constant acceleration $a$. The formula is $v=v_0+a\,t$. What is $a$ in m/s²?

- $\dfrac{17}{3}$
- $3$
- $108$
- $\dfrac{13}{3}$

## Explanation

Substituting the data gives $26=8+6a$. Subtracting $8$ gives $18=6a$, so $a=3$. The value $\dfrac{17}{3}$ results from adding the speeds instead of subtracting them, whereas $108$ results from multiplying by $6$ instead of dividing.
