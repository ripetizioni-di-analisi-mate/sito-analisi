Which equation without fractions is equivalent to $\dfrac{x}{5}+2=\dfrac{x}{2}$?

- $2x+20=5x$
- $2x+2=5x$
- $5x+20=2x$
- $2x+20=5$

## Explanation

The least common multiple of $5$ and $2$ is $10$. Multiplying by $10$ gives $10\cdot\dfrac{x}{5}=2x$, then $10\cdot 2=20$ and $10\cdot\dfrac{x}{2}=5x$. The equation is $2x+20=5x$. In the other answers a term is not multiplied by $10$ or the factors are interchanged.
