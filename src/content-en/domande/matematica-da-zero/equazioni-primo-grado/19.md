Which equation is equivalent to $\dfrac{2x}{3}=4$?

- $x=\dfrac{8}{3}$
- $x=12$
- $x=\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $x=6$

## Explanation

Multiplying both sides by $3$ gives $2x=12$. Then dividing them by $2$ gives $x=6$. The value $12$ results from omitting the division by $2$, whereas $\dfrac{8}{3}$ results from multiplying the right-hand side by $\dfrac{2}{3}$ instead of using its reciprocal.
