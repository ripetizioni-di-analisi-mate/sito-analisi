Which number must be excluded from the solutions of the equation $\dfrac{5}{2x-6}=\dfrac{x}{4}$?

- $3$
- $-3$
- $6$
- $0$

## Explanation

The denominator $2x-6$ is zero when $2x=6$, that is, when $x=3$. The value $3$ is excluded. The value $6$ results from omitting the division by $2$, whereas the denominator $4$ is a number and imposes no restriction on $x$.
