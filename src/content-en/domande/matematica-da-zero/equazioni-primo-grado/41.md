Solve $\dfrac{3x}{4}=\dfrac{x}{2}+3$ by multiplying by the least common multiple. What value of $x$ do you obtain?

- $3$
- $6$
- $24$
- $12$

## Explanation

The least common multiple is $4$. Multiplying by $4$ gives $3x=2x+12$, so $x=12$. Checking gives $\dfrac{36}{4}=9$ and $\dfrac{12}{2}+3=9$. The value $3$ results from omitting the factor $4$ in the term $3$, whereas $6$ results from writing $x$ instead of $2x$.
