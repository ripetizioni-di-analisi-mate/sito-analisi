A linear equation simplifies to $0\cdot x=-3$. Which values of $x$ satisfy it?

- Every value
- Only $x=0$
- No value
- Only $x=-3$

## Explanation

The left-hand side $0\cdot x$ equals $0$ for every $x$, whereas the right-hand side equals $-3$. The two sides are never equal, so no number is a solution. When $x=0$, we have $0=-3$, and when $x=-3$, we again have $0=-3$.
