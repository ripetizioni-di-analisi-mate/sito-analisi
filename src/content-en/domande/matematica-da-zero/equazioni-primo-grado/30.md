Only one of the following equations is linear after simplification. Which one?

- $x^2+x=x+9$
- $(x+1)^2=x^2+5$
- $\dfrac{1}{x}+1=2$
- $x^2-3=x$

## Explanation

In the equation $(x+1)^2=x^2+5$, we have $(x+1)^2=x^2+2x+1$, so we subtract $x^2$ from both sides, leaving $2x+1=5$, which is linear. The other equations do not reduce to linear equations: $x^2+x=x+9$ becomes $x^2=9$ and $x^2-3=x$ becomes $x^2-x-3=0$, both of which contain $x^2$, while $\dfrac{1}{x}+1=2$ has the unknown in a denominator.
