Half a number, increased by $7$, equals $19$. What is the number?

- $12$
- $38$
- $52$
- $24$

## Explanation

The equation is $\dfrac{x}{2}+7=19$. Subtracting $7$ gives $\dfrac{x}{2}=12$. Multiplying by $2$ yields $x=24$. Checking gives $12+7=19$. The value $12$ is half the number rather than the number itself, whereas $38$ results from doubling $19$.
