What is the solution of $2(y+5)-(y-4)=3y-2$ in the unknown $y$?

- $y=8$
- $y=4$
- $y=16$
- $y=-8$

## Explanation

Expanding the products gives $2y+10-y+4=3y-2$, that is, $y+14=3y-2$. Moving the terms gives $16=2y$, so $y=8$. The value $4$ results from writing $-4$ instead of $+4$ in the expansion of $-(y-4)$. Checking gives $2(8+5)-(8-4)=26-4=22$ on the left-hand side and $3\cdot 8-2=22$ on the right.
