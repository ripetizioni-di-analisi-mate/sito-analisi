Solve $(x+3)(x-3)=x(x+1)-6$. Which number is the solution?

- $-3$
- $3$
- $-15$
- $0$

## Explanation

The left-hand side is a difference of squares, so it equals $x^2-9$. The right-hand side equals $x^2+x-6$. Subtracting $x^2$ gives $-9=x-6$, so $x=-9+6=-3$. The value $-15$ results from adding $6$ to the right-hand side instead of subtracting it.
