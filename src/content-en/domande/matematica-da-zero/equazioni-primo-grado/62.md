Solve $\dfrac{x+1}{x-2}=3$ after imposing $x\neq 2$. What value do you find for $x$?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{7}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{7}{2}$
- $7$

## Explanation

The domain restriction is $x\neq 2$. Multiplying by $x-2$ gives $x+1=3(x-2)$, that is, $x+1=3x-6$. Therefore $7=2x$ and $x=\dfrac{7}{2}$, a value different from $2$. The value $7$ results from omitting the division by $2$.
