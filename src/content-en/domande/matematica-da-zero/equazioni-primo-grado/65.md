Which values of $x$ satisfy the equation $\dfrac{x^2-4}{x-2}=x+2$?

- Every real number
- Only $x=2$
- No value
- Every number other than $2$

## Explanation

The domain restriction is $x\neq 2$. By the difference of squares, $x^2-4=(x-2)(x+2)$, so for $x\neq 2$ the left-hand side equals $x+2$. The equality is true for every $x$ other than $2$, whereas $x=2$ makes the denominator zero.
