Which value of $x$ solves $5-2(x-3)=x+2$?

- $-1$
- $2$
- $\dfrac{13}{3}$
- $3$

## Explanation

The term $-2(x-3)$ equals $-2x+6$ because the minus sign changes the signs of both terms of $x-3$. The equation becomes $11-2x=x+2$, so $9=3x$ and $x=3$. The value $-1$ results from writing $-6$ in place of $+6$.
