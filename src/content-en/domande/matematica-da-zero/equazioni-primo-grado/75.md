The density is $d=\dfrac{m}{V}$. An object of mass $540$ g and density $2.7$ g/cm³ has a volume $V$ of

- $200$ cm³
- $1458$ cm³
- $0.005$ cm³
- $20$ cm³

## Explanation

From the formula $d=\dfrac{m}{V}$ we obtain $V=\dfrac{m}{d}$ because we multiply both sides by $V$ and then divide by $d$. Therefore the volume is $V=\dfrac{540}{2.7}=200$ cm³. The value $1458$ results from multiplying the mass by the density, whereas $0.005$ results from dividing $d$ by $m$.
