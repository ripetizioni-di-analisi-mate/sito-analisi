The sum of five consecutive even numbers is $110$. What is the smallest of the five?

- $20$
- $22$
- $18$
- $26$

## Explanation

The consecutive even numbers are $x$, $x+2$, $x+4$, $x+6$, and $x+8$. Their sum is $5x+20=110$, so $5x=90$ and $x=18$. The numbers are $18, 20, 22, 24, 26$. The value $22$ is the middle number and $26$ is the largest.
