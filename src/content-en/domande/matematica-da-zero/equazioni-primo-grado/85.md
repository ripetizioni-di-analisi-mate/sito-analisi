In $5$ years Elena will be three times as old as she was $7$ years ago. How old is Elena today?

- $11$
- $13$
- $26$
- $21$

## Explanation

If Elena is $x$ years old, in $5$ years she will be $x+5$ and $7$ years ago she was $x-7$. The equation is $x+5=3(x-7)$, that is, $x+5=3x-21$. Therefore $26=2x$ and $x=13$. The value $26$ results from omitting the division by $2$.
