The temperature in degrees Fahrenheit is $F=\dfrac{9}{5}C+32$, where $C$ is the temperature in degrees Celsius. Which formula gives $C$?

- $C=\dfrac{5}{9}(F-32)$
- $C=\dfrac{5}{9}F-32$
- $C=\dfrac{5}{9}(F+32)$
- $C=\dfrac{9}{5}(F-32)$

## Explanation

Subtracting $32$ from both sides gives $F-32=\dfrac{9}{5}C$. Multiplying both sides by $\dfrac{5}{9}$ yields $C=\dfrac{5}{9}(F-32)$. When $F=50$, we obtain $C=10$. In the second answer the term $32$ is not multiplied by $\dfrac{5}{9}$.
