Solve $2[x-(3-x)]=x+5$. Which solution do you obtain?

- $-11$
- $8$
- $\dfrac{11}{3}$
- $\dfrac{8}{3}$

## Explanation

First expand the inner expression to obtain $x-(3-x)=x-3+x=2x-3$. The product is $2(2x-3)=4x-6$. The equation becomes $4x-6=x+5$, so $3x=11$ and $x=\dfrac{11}{3}$. The value $-11$ results from dropping the sign of $x$ inside the inner parentheses. Checking gives $4\cdot\dfrac{11}{3}-6=\dfrac{26}{3}$ and $\dfrac{11}{3}+5=\dfrac{26}{3}$.
