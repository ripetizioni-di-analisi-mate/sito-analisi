Lucia adds $3$ to both sides of $\dfrac{x}{2}-3=1$. Which equation does she write?

- $\dfrac{x}{2}=-2$
- $\dfrac{x}{2}=1$
- $\dfrac{x}{2}=4$
- $x=4$

## Explanation

Adding $3$ to both sides gives $\dfrac{x}{2}-3+3=1+3$, that is, $\dfrac{x}{2}=4$. The solution then follows from the second principle. The equation $x=4$ is not equivalent to $\dfrac{x}{2}=4$, which has solution $x=8$, and $\dfrac{x}{2}=1$ results from adding $3$ only on the left-hand side.
