For what value of $k$ is the equation $kx=6$ inconsistent?

- $k=6$
- $k=1$
- $k=0$
- $k=-6$

## Explanation

When $k=0$, the equation becomes $0\cdot x=6$, and the left-hand side equals $0$ for every $x$, so it never equals $6$. Every other value of $k$ gives the solution $x=\dfrac{6}{k}$. The value $k=6$ gives $6x=6$, with solution $x=1$, and the value $k=1$ gives $x=6$.
