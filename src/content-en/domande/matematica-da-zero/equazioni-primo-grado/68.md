The value of $x$ such that $\dfrac{x+2}{x}=\dfrac{5}{3}$ is

- $3$
- $1$
- $-\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $6$

## Explanation

The domain restriction is $x\neq 0$. Multiplying by $3x$ gives $3(x+2)=5x$, that is, $3x+6=5x$. Therefore $6=2x$ and $x=3$. Checking gives $\dfrac{5}{3}=\dfrac{5}{3}$. The value $6$ results from omitting the division by $2$. The value $1$ results from writing $3x+2=5x$.
