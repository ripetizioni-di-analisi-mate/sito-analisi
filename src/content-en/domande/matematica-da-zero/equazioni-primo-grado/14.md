Which one of the following equations is not equivalent to $2x+6=10$?

- $2x=4$
- $x+3=5$
- $2x=16$
- $x=2$

## Explanation

The only solution of the equation $2x+6=10$ is $x=2$. Subtracting $6$ from both sides gives $2x=4$, and dividing by $2$ gives $x+3=5$ or $x=2$. The equation $2x=16$ comes from adding $6$ instead of subtracting it and has solution $x=8$.
