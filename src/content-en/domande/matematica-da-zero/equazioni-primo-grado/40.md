To clear the denominators in the equation $\dfrac{x}{2}+\dfrac{x}{3}-\dfrac{x}{8}=7$, by which number should we multiply all terms?

- $24$
- $6$
- $48$
- $12$

## Explanation

The least common multiple of $2$, $3$, and $8$ is the smallest positive number divisible by all three, namely $24$. The number $6$ is a multiple of $2$ and $3$ but not of $8$, whereas $48$ is the product of the three denominators and $12$ is not a multiple of $8$.
