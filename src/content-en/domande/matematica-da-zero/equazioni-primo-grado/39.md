Which value of $x$ solves $x-\dfrac{x}{3}=8$?

- $4$
- $12$
- $24$
- $6$

## Explanation

Multiplying all terms by $3$ gives $3x-x=24$, that is, $2x=24$. Dividing by $2$ yields $x=12$. The value $4$ results from multiplying by $3$ only on the left-hand side, whereas $24$ results from omitting the division by $2$.
