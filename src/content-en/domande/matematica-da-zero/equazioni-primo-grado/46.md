In solving $\dfrac{x}{3}+1=4$, a student multiplies only the fractional term by $3$ and obtains $x=3$. What is the correct solution?

- $3$
- $12$
- $9$
- $15$

## Explanation

The least common multiple multiplies every term, so $x+3=12$ and $x=9$. Checking gives $\dfrac{9}{3}+1=4$. The value $3$ comes from leaving $1$ and $4$ without the factor $3$, and when $x=3$, the left-hand side equals $2$.
