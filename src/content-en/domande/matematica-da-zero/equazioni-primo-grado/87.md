To rent a scooter, shop A charges $5$ euros plus $2$ euros per hour, and shop B charges $2$ euros plus $3$ euros per hour. After how many hours do the two rentals cost the same?

- $7$
- $3$
- $9$
- $15$

## Explanation

After $x$ hours the cost is $5+2x$ at shop A and $2+3x$ at shop B. The equality $5+2x=2+3x$ gives $5-2=3x-2x$, so $x=3$. For $3$ hours, both rentals cost $11$ euros. The value $7$ results from adding the fixed charges.
