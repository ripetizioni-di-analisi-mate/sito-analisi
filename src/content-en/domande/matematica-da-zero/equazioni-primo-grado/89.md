A car sets out at $60$ km/h. After $1$ hour, a second car sets out in the same direction at $90$ km/h. How many hours after it leaves does the second car catch up with the first?

- $1$
- $2$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$

## Explanation

After $x$ hours from the second car's departure, it has traveled $90x$ km and the first has traveled $60(x+1)$ km. Setting $60(x+1)=90x$ gives $60=30x$ and $x=2$. Both cars have traveled $180$ km. The value $3$ is the first car's travel time in hours.
