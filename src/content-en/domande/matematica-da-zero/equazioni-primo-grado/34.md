In the equation $7m-2(m+3)=m+10$, the unknown is $m$. For which number are the two sides equal?

- $1$
- $\dfrac{13}{4}$
- $-4$
- $4$

## Explanation

Expanding the product gives $7m-2m-6=m+10$, that is, $5m-6=m+10$. Moving the terms gives $4m=16$, so $m=4$. The value $1$ results from writing $+6$ instead of $-6$, whereas $\dfrac{13}{4}$ results from multiplying $2$ only by $m$.
