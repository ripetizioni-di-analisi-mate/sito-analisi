Which one of the following equations is an identity?

- $2(x+1)-x=x+3$
- $2(x+1)-x=2x+2$
- $2(x+1)-x=x$
- $2(x+1)-x=x+2$

## Explanation

In all of them the left-hand side equals $2x+2-x=x+2$. With $x+2$ on the right, we obtain $x+2=x+2$, which is true for every $x$. With $x+3$ we obtain $2=3$, and with $x$ we obtain $2=0$, both false. With $2x+2$ we obtain $x+2=2x+2$, that is, $x=0$, which is a single solution.
