Multiplication by $x-4$ on both sides of $\dfrac{x}{x-4}=\dfrac{4}{x-4}$ gives $x=4$. What can we conclude?

- The solution is $x=4$
- The equation is inconsistent because $x=4$ makes the denominator zero
- The equation is an identity
- The solution is $x=0$

## Explanation

The domain restriction is $x\neq 4$. The number $4$ is precisely the excluded value because substituting it into the equation gives $\dfrac{4}{0}$, which is undefined. We discard the candidate solution, and the rational equation is inconsistent. When $x=4$, the numerator equals $4$ and the denominator equals $0$.
