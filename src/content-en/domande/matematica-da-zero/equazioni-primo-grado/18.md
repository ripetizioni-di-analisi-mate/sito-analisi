When solving $x-8=3x$, we move the term $-8$ from the left-hand side to the right. Which equation results?

- $x=3x-8$
- $x=3x+8$
- $x+8=3x$
- $x=-3x+8$

## Explanation

By the transposition rule, the term $-8$ moves to the right-hand side with the opposite sign, that is, as $+8$. The equation becomes $x=3x+8$. The other answers leave the sign of $-8$ unchanged or also change the sign of $3x$, which has not moved.
