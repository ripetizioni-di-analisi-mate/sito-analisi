Which statement corresponds to the equation $2(x+5)=3x$?

- Twice the sum of a number and $5$ equals three times the number
- The sum of twice a number and $5$ equals three times the number
- Twice a number equals three times the number increased by $5$
- Twice the difference between a number and $5$ equals three times the number

## Explanation

The left-hand side $2(x+5)$ is twice $x+5$, that is, twice the sum of the number and $5$. The right-hand side $3x$ is three times the number. The sum of twice the number and $5$ would instead be written $2x+5$.
