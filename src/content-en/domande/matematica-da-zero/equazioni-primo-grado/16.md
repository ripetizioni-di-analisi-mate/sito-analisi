Divide by $-4$ on both sides of the equation $-4x=20$. What is $x$?

- $5$
- $-80$
- $-5$
- $-\dfrac{1}{5}$

## Explanation

By the second principle, dividing both sides by $-4$ gives $x=\dfrac{20}{-4}=-5$. The quotient of a positive number and a negative number is negative. Checking gives $-4\cdot(-5)=20$. The value $-80$ results from multiplying by $-4$ instead of dividing.
