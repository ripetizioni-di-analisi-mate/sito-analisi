Solve $-(x-2)-3x=14$. What number do you obtain for $x$?

- $-4$
- $3$
- $-6$
- $-3$

## Explanation

The minus sign before $x-2$ changes the sign of both terms, so $-(x-2)=-x+2$. The equation becomes $-4x+2=14$, that is, $-4x=12$ and $x=-3$. The value $-4$ results from writing $-x-2$ in place of $-x+2$. Checking gives $-(-3-2)-3\cdot(-3)=5+9=14$.
