Two identical jugs each hold $C$ millilitres. The first is half full and the second one-third full. After $40$ mL is poured from the first into the second, the levels match. What is $C$?

- $240\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $480\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $120\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $720\,\mathrm{mL}$

## Explanation

The transfer reduces the difference by $80$ mL because one quantity drops by $40$ and the other rises by $40$. The initial difference was $\dfrac{C}{2}-\dfrac{C}{3}=\dfrac{C}{6}$. Thus $\dfrac{C}{6}=80$ and the capacity is $480$ mL.
