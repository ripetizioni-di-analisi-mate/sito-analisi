The number $x$ satisfies $\dfrac{x+2}{5}=\dfrac{x-4}{3}$. What is $x$?

- $5$
- $13$
- $11$
- $-7$

## Explanation

The least common multiple is $15$, so $3(x+2)=5(x-4)$. We have $3x+6=5x-20$, that is, $26=2x$ and $x=13$. Checking gives $\dfrac{15}{5}=3$ and $\dfrac{9}{3}=3$. The value $5$ results from omitting the factor $5$ in the term $-4$. The value $11$ results from omitting the factor $3$ in the term $2$.
