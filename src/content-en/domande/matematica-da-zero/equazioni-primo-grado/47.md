Two identical jugs each hold $C$ milliliters. The first is half full and the second one-third full. After $40$ mL is poured from the first into the second, the levels are equal. What is $C$?

- $240\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $480\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $120\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $720\,\mathrm{mL}$

## Explanation

Before the transfer the first jug contains $\dfrac{C}{2}$ milliliters and the second $\dfrac{C}{3}$. After the transfer the first contains $\dfrac{C}{2} - 40$ milliliters and the second $\dfrac{C}{3} + 40$, and since the jugs are identical the levels are equal when the amounts are equal. This gives the equation $\dfrac{C}{2} - 40 = \dfrac{C}{3} + 40$. Multiplying both sides by $6$ gives $3C - 240 = 2C + 240$, so $C = 480\,\mathrm{mL}$. The value $240\,\mathrm{mL}$ comes from taking into account the $40$ mL removed from the first jug but not those added to the second.
