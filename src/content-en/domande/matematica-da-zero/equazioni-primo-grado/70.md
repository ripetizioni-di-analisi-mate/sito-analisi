The perimeter of a rectangle with base $b$ and height $h$ is $P=2(b+h)$. Which formula gives $h$?

- $h=\dfrac{P}{2}+b$
- $h=\dfrac{P-b}{2}$
- $h=\dfrac{P}{2}-b$
- $h=P-2b$

## Explanation

Dividing both sides by $2$ gives $\dfrac{P}{2}=b+h$. Subtracting $b$ from both sides yields $h=\dfrac{P}{2}-b$. With $P=38$ and $b=12$, we have $h=19-12=7$. The formula $h=P-2b$ gives $14$, which is incorrect.
