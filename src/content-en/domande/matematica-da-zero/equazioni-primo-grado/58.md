What are the domain restrictions for the equation $\dfrac{4}{x-3}=\dfrac{1}{x+2}$?

- $x\neq 3$ and $x\neq -2$
- $x\neq -3$ and $x\neq 2$
- $x\neq 3$ only
- $x\neq 4$ and $x\neq 1$

## Explanation

The domain restrictions ensure that all denominators are nonzero. From $x-3\neq 0$ we obtain $x\neq 3$, and from $x+2\neq 0$ we obtain $x\neq -2$. The numerators $4$ and $1$ impose no restrictions.
