The area of a triangle with base $b$ and height $h$ is $A=\dfrac{b\,h}{2}$. Which formula gives the height $h$?

- $h=\dfrac{2A}{b}$
- $h=\dfrac{A}{2b}$
- $h=\dfrac{b}{2A}$
- $h=2Ab$

## Explanation

Multiplying both sides by $2$ gives $2A=b\,h$. Since $b$ is nonzero, we divide both sides by $b$ to obtain $h=\dfrac{2A}{b}$. With $A=15$ and $b=5$, we obtain $h=6$, and indeed $\dfrac{5\cdot 6}{2}=15$.
