Anna has $x$ flowers. Giulia has $3$ fewer than twice the number of flowers Anna has. They have $33$ flowers altogether. How many flowers does Giulia have?

- $12$
- $24$
- $17$
- $21$

## Explanation

Giulia has $2x-3$ flowers, so $x+(2x-3)=33$. This gives $3x=36$ and $x=12$. Giulia has $2\cdot 12-3=21$ flowers, and $12+21=33$. The value $12$ is Anna's number of flowers, whereas $24$ results from omitting the subtraction of $3$.
