The GCD of two numbers $a$ and $b$ is $6$, and their LCM is $180$. If $a = 36$, what is $b$?

- $5$
- $90$
- $216$
- $30$

## Explanation

The product of the GCD and LCM equals the product of the two numbers, so $6 \cdot 180 = 1\,080 = 36 \cdot b$. Therefore $b = 1\,080 : 36 = 30$. We can check that $\operatorname{MCD}(36, 30) = 6$ and $\operatorname{mcm}(36, 30) = 180$. The value $5$ is $180 : 36$, and $216$ is $6 \cdot 36$.
