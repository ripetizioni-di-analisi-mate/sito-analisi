What is the smallest positive number divisible by $6$, $8$, and $15$?

- $30$
- $240$
- $120$
- $720$

## Explanation

We have $6 = 2 \cdot 3$, $8 = 2^3$, and $15 = 3 \cdot 5$. The factors are $2$, $3$, and $5$, with largest exponents $3$, $1$, and $1$, so $\operatorname{mcm}(6, 8, 15) = 2^3 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 = 120$. The number $30$ is a multiple of $6$ and $15$ but not of $8$, whereas $240$ and $720$ are larger common multiples.
