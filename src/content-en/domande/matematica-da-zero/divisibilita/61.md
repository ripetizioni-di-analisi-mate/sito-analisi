What is $\operatorname{MCD}(84, 126)$?

- $6$
- $42$
- $21$
- $252$

## Explanation

We have $84 = 2^2 \cdot 3 \cdot 7$ and $126 = 2 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 7$. The common factors are $2$, $3$, and $7$, each taken with its smaller exponent, $1$. Therefore $\operatorname{MCD}(84, 126) = 2 \cdot 3 \cdot 7 = 42$. The value $252$ is the least common multiple of the two numbers.
