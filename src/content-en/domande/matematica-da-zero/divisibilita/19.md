Which digit in place of $\square$ makes the number $41\square6$ divisible by $9$?

- $1$
- $7$
- $4$
- $9$

## Explanation

The number is divisible by $9$ if its digit sum is a multiple of $9$. The known digits give $4 + 1 + 6 = 11$, and the multiple of $9$ that can be reached by adding a single digit is $18$. Therefore $\square = 18 - 11 = 7$. With $4$ and $1$ the sums are $15$ and $12$, respectively, multiples of $3$ but not of $9$, and with $9$ the sum is $20$.
