For which of the following numbers does division by $25$ leave remainder $0$?

- $6\,355$
- $6\,365$
- $6\,375$
- $6\,385$

## Explanation

A number is divisible by $25$ if it ends in $00$, $25$, $50$, or $75$. The number $6\,375$ ends in $75$, so it is divisible by $25$. Indeed, $6\,375 = 25 \cdot 255$. The others end in $55$, $65$, and $85$, so they are multiples of $5$ but not of $25$.
