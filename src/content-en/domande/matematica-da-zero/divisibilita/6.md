What is the smallest multiple of $9$ greater than $200$?

- $198$
- $209$
- $216$
- $207$

## Explanation

Divide $200$ by $9$ to obtain $200 = 9 \cdot 22 + 2$. The multiple $9 \cdot 22 = 198$ does not exceed $200$, so the smallest multiple greater than $200$ is the next one, $9 \cdot 23 = 207$. The number $216 = 9 \cdot 24$ is a multiple of $9$ but is not the smallest one above the given bound, whereas $209$ is not a multiple of $9$.
