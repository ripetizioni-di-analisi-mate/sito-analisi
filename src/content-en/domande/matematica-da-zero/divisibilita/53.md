What is the value of $2^3 \cdot 3 \cdot 5^2$?

- $120$
- $180$
- $240$
- $600$

## Explanation

Calculate $2^3 = 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 = 8$ and $5^2 = 25$, so $2^3 \cdot 3 \cdot 5^2 = 8 \cdot 3 \cdot 25 = 600$. The value $240$ comes from using $5^2 = 10$, the value $180$ comes from using $2^3 = 6$ and $5^2 = 10$, and the value $120$ comes from omitting the exponent of $5$.
