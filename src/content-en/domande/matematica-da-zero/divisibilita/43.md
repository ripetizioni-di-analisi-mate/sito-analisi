When applying the sieve of Eratosthenes to the numbers from $2$ to $100$, what is the last prime whose multiples must be crossed out?

- $5$
- $7$
- $11$
- $97$

## Explanation

The first multiple of a prime $p$ that has not already been crossed out is $p \cdot p$ because smaller multiples have a smaller prime factor and have already been crossed out. We have $7 \cdot 7 = 49$ and $11 \cdot 11 = 121$, which exceeds $100$. Therefore after crossing out the multiples of $7$, there is nothing left to cross out.
