What is the smallest odd composite number?

- $9$
- $1$
- $3$
- $15$

## Explanation

A composite number has a divisor other than $1$ and itself. The odd numbers less than $9$ are $1, 3, 5, 7$. The number $1$ is not composite and $3, 5, 7$ are prime. The number $9 = 3 \cdot 3$ is composite, so it is the smallest. The number $15$ is composite but larger.
