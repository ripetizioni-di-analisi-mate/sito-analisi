Which of the following numbers is not coprime to $15$?

- $28$
- $32$
- $44$
- $48$

## Explanation

The number $15 = 3 \cdot 5$ is not coprime to a number having a factor of $3$ or $5$. We have $48 = 2^4 \cdot 3$, so $\operatorname{MCD}(15, 48) = 3$. In contrast, $28 = 2^2 \cdot 7$, $32 = 2^5$, and $44 = 2^2 \cdot 11$ contain neither $3$ nor $5$ as a factor, so they are coprime to $15$.
