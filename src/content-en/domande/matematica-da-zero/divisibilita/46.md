The number $35 = 5 \cdot 7$ is the product of two different primes. How many divisors does it have?

- $2$
- $3$
- $4$
- $6$

## Explanation

The divisors of $35$ come from the pairs $35 = 1 \cdot 35$ and $35 = 5 \cdot 7$. Therefore the divisors are $1, 5, 7, 35$, giving $4$ in all. The value $2$ counts only the prime factors $5$ and $7$, and $3$ leaves out either $1$ or $35$.
