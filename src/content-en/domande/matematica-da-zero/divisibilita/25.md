A school has $1\,458$ students and wants to form groups of $9$ with no students left over. Is this possible?

- Yes, because the digits add up to $18$, a multiple of $9$
- No, because $1\,458$ does not end in $0$ or $5$
- No, because $1\,458$ is even
- Yes, because $1\,458$ is even

## Explanation

The groups can be formed if $1\,458$ is divisible by $9$, that is, if its digit sum is a multiple of $9$. We have $1 + 4 + 5 + 8 = 18 = 9 \cdot 2$, so the groups can be formed and there will be $162$ of them. Whether the number is even or ends in $8$ is irrelevant to divisibility by $9$.
