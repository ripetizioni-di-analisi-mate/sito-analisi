What is the largest prime factor of $1\,092$?

- $13$
- $7$
- $91$
- $273$

## Explanation

We have $1\,092 = 2 \cdot 546 = 2^2 \cdot 273 = 2^2 \cdot 3 \cdot 91 = 2^2 \cdot 3 \cdot 7 \cdot 13$. The prime factors are $2, 3, 7, 13$ and the largest is $13$. The numbers $91 = 7 \cdot 13$ and $273 = 3 \cdot 7 \cdot 13$ are larger but composite.
