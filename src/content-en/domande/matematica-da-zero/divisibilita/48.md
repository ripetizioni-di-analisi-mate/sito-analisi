Which of the following numbers has exactly three divisors?

- $14$
- $27$
- $35$
- $49$

## Explanation

A number with three divisors has $1$, itself, and just one other divisor. The number $49 = 7 \cdot 7$ has the divisors $1, 7, 49$. The others have four divisors: $14$ has $1, 2, 7, 14$, $27$ has $1, 3, 9, 27$, and $35$ has $1, 5, 7, 35$.
