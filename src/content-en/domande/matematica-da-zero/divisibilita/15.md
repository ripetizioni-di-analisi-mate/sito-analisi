Today is Monday. What day of the week will it be in $100$ days?

- Monday
- Tuesday
- Thursday
- Wednesday

## Explanation

The days of the week repeat every $7$ days, so a multiple of $7$ days takes us back to the same day of the week. We have $100 = 7 \cdot 14 + 2$, so in $98$ days it will be Monday again. Two more days bring us to Wednesday.
