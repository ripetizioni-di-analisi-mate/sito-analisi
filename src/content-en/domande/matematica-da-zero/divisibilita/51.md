In the prime factorization of $504$, what is the exponent of $3$?

- $1$
- $2$
- $3$
- $9$

## Explanation

We have $504 : 2 = 252$, $252 : 2 = 126$, $126 : 2 = 63$, $63 : 3 = 21$, $21 : 3 = 7$, $7 : 7 = 1$. Therefore $504 = 2^3 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 7$, and the factor $3$ has exponent $2$. The exponent $3$ belongs to the factor $2$.
