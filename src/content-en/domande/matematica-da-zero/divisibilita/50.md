How many distinct prime factors occur in the factorization of $210$?

- $3$
- $4$
- $5$
- $6$

## Explanation

We have $210 = 2 \cdot 105 = 2 \cdot 3 \cdot 35 = 2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 \cdot 7$. The prime factors are $2, 3, 5, 7$, all distinct, so there are $4$. Stopping at $35$ would give $3$ factors, but $35$ is not prime.
