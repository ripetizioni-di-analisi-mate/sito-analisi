According to the digit-sum test, one of the following numbers is divisible by $3$. Which one?

- $4\,725$
- $4\,723$
- $4\,727$
- $4\,729$

## Explanation

A number is divisible by $3$ if its digit sum is. For $4\,725$, we have $4 + 7 + 2 + 5 = 18$, a multiple of $3$. The digit sums of the other numbers are $16$, $20$, and $22$, none of which is a multiple of $3$. Therefore only $4\,725$ is divisible by $3$.
