Only one of the following numbers does not divide $60$ evenly. Which one?

- $8$
- $12$
- $15$
- $20$

## Explanation

A number is a divisor of $60$ if the division $60 : b$ has remainder $0$. We have $60 : 12 = 5$, $60 : 15 = 4$, and $60 : 20 = 3$, all with remainder $0$. In contrast, $60 : 8$ gives quotient $7$ and remainder $4$ because $8 \cdot 7 + 4 = 60$. Therefore $8$ is not a divisor of $60$.
