What is the first prime number greater than $50$?

- $51$
- $55$
- $53$
- $57$

## Explanation

Test successive numbers after $50$. The number $51 = 3 \cdot 17$ is composite and $52$ is even. The number $53$ is not divisible by any of $2, 3, 5, 7$, and $11 \cdot 11 = 121$ exceeds $53$, so $53$ is prime. Both $55 = 5 \cdot 11$ and $57 = 3 \cdot 19$ are also composite.
