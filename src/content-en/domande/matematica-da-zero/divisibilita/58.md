The prime factorization of the product $6 \cdot 10 \cdot 15$ is:

- $2^2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5^2$
- $2 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5^2$
- $2^3 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5^2$
- $2^2 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5^2$

## Explanation

Factor each number: $6 = 2 \cdot 3$, $10 = 2 \cdot 5$, and $15 = 3 \cdot 5$. The product contains two factors of $2$, two of $3$, and two of $5$, giving $2^2 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5^2 = 900$. The other expressions each have an incorrect exponent and equal $300$, $450$, and $1\,800$.
