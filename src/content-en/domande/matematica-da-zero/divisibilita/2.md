How many divisors does $28$ have?

- $4$
- $5$
- $6$
- $8$

## Explanation

The divisors of $28$ come from the factor pairs $28 = 1 \cdot 28$, $28 = 2 \cdot 14$, and $28 = 4 \cdot 7$. Thus the divisors are $1, 2, 4, 7, 14, 28$, giving $6$ in all. A count of $4$ leaves out $1$ and $28$, which are divisors of $28$.
