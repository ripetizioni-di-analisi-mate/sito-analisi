What is the smallest natural number by which $12$ must be multiplied to obtain a square, that is, the product of a natural number and itself?

- $2$
- $6$
- $3$
- $12$

## Explanation

We have $12 = 2^2 \cdot 3$. Multiplying by $2$ gives $24$, which is not a square. Multiplying by $3$ gives $2^2 \cdot 3^2 = (2 \cdot 3)^2 = 36$, which is a square. The number $12 \cdot 12 = 144$ is a square but is not the smallest one.
