If $12$ is a divisor of a number $n$, which of the following numbers must be a divisor of $n$?

- $5$
- $4$
- $24$
- $36$

## Explanation

If $12$ divides $n$, then $n = 12 \cdot k$ for some natural number $k$. Since $12 = 4 \cdot 3$, we have $n = 4 \cdot (3k)$, so $4$ divides $n$. However, $n = 12$ is divisible by none of $5$, $24$, or $36$, so these numbers do not always divide $n$.
