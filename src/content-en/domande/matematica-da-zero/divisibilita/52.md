Which of the following expressions is the prime factorization of $72$?

- $2^3 \cdot 3^2$
- $8 \cdot 9$
- $2 \cdot 4 \cdot 9$
- $2^2 \cdot 3^3$

## Explanation

A prime factorization uses only prime numbers. We have $72 = 2 \cdot 36 = 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 18 = 2^3 \cdot 9 = 2^3 \cdot 3^2$. The expressions $8 \cdot 9$ and $2 \cdot 4 \cdot 9$ equal $72$ but contain composite numbers, whereas $2^2 \cdot 3^3$ equals $108$.
