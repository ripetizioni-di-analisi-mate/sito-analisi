Only one of the following statements about multiples of $6$ is correct. Which one?

- If the sum of two numbers is a multiple of $6$, then each number is a multiple of $6$
- The sum of two multiples of $6$ is always a multiple of $12$
- The difference of two multiples of $6$ is never a multiple of $6$
- The sum of two multiples of $6$ is always a multiple of $6$

## Explanation

If $a = 6h$ and $b = 6k$, then $a + b = 6(h + k)$ is a multiple of $6$. The converse is false: $4 + 8 = 12$ is a multiple of $6$, but $4$ and $8$ are not. Also, $6 + 12 = 18$ is not a multiple of $12$, and $18 - 12 = 6$ is a multiple of $6$.
