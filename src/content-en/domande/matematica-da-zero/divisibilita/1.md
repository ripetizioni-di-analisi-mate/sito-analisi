Which of the following numbers is a multiple of $13$?

- $133$
- $143$
- $153$
- $149$

## Explanation

A number is a multiple of $13$ if it equals $13 \cdot k$ for some natural number $k$. Since $13 \cdot 11 = 143$, the number $143$ is a multiple of $13$. The others have remainders different from $0$: $133 = 13 \cdot 10 + 3$, $153 = 13 \cdot 11 + 10$, and $149 = 13 \cdot 11 + 6$.
