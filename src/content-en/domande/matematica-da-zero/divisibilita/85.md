How many natural numbers from $1$ to $12$ are coprime to $12$?

- $4$
- $3$
- $5$
- $6$

## Explanation

We have $12 = 2^2 \cdot 3$, so a number is coprime to $12$ if it is divisible by neither $2$ nor $3$. From $1$ to $12$, the numbers are $1, 5, 7, 11$, giving $4$ in all. The number $1$ counts because $\operatorname{MCD}(1, 12) = 1$, whereas $12$ does not because $\operatorname{MCD}(12, 12) = 12$.
