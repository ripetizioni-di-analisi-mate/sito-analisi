The number $4\square39$ has an unknown digit $\square$. Which digit makes it divisible by $11$?

- $0$
- $2$
- $9$
- $7$

## Explanation

Counting from the right, the digits in odd positions are $9$ and $\square$, with sum $9 + \square$, and those in even positions are $3$ and $4$, with sum $7$. The difference $9 + \square - 7 = 2 + \square$ must be $0$ or a multiple of $11$. Among the digits from $0$ to $9$, only $\square = 9$ works, giving $11$.
