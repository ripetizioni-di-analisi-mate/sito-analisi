How many primes are there between $20$ and $30$?

- $1$
- $3$
- $4$
- $2$

## Explanation

The numbers between $20$ and $30$ are $21, 22, \dots, 29$. The even numbers are divisible by $2$, while $21$ and $27$ are divisible by $3$ and $25$ by $5$. This leaves $23$ and $29$, which are prime, so there are $2$ primes.
