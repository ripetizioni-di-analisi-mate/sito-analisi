What is the smallest positive number that is a multiple of both $12$ and $36$?

- $12$
- $72$
- $432$
- $36$

## Explanation

Since $36 = 12 \cdot 3$, the number $36$ is a multiple of $12$ and is also a multiple of itself. Therefore it is the smallest positive common multiple, and $\operatorname{mcm}(12, 36) = 36$. The factorizations $12 = 2^2 \cdot 3$ and $36 = 2^2 \cdot 3^2$ give $2^2 \cdot 3^2 = 36$. The number $432$ is the product of the two numbers.
