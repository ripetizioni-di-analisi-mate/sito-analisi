Two gears with $15$ and $20$ teeth mesh. They start in a position where two particular teeth are in contact. After how many revolutions of the smaller gear will those two teeth first be in contact again?

- $3$
- $5$
- $60$
- $4$

## Explanation

The teeth come into contact again when the number of teeth that have passed is a multiple of both $15$ and $20$. The first such count is $\operatorname{mcm}(15, 20) = 60$ teeth. The gear with $15$ teeth makes $60 : 15 = 4$ revolutions, and the gear with $20$ teeth makes $3$. The value $60$ counts teeth rather than revolutions.
