Counting the number itself, how many divisors does $1$ have?

- $0$
- $1$
- $2$
- Infinitely many

## Explanation

A divisor of $1$ is a number $b$ such that $1 = b \cdot k$ for some natural number $k$. This is possible only for $b = 1$ and $k = 1$. Therefore $1$ has just one divisor, itself. For this reason, $1$ is not prime, since a prime number has exactly two divisors.
