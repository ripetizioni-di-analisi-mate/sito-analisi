Given $a = 2^2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5$ and $b = 2 \cdot 3^3$, what is $\operatorname{mcm}(a, b)$?

- $540$
- $6$
- $3\,240$
- $270$

## Explanation

The LCM contains every prime factor with its largest exponent. The factor $2$ has exponents $2$ and $1$, $3$ has exponents $1$ and $3$, and $5$ appears only in $a$. Therefore $\operatorname{mcm}(a, b) = 2^2 \cdot 3^3 \cdot 5 = 540$. The value $6$ is the GCD, $3\,240$ is the product $60 \cdot 54$, and $270$ comes from using $2^1$ instead of $2^2$.
