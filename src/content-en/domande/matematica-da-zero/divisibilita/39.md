To determine whether $131$ is prime, we divide it by the primes in order. What is the last prime we need to test?

- $7$
- $11$
- $13$
- $65$

## Explanation

Stop when you reach the first prime whose square exceeds $131$, without testing that prime. We have $11 \cdot 11 = 121$, which does not exceed $131$, and $13 \cdot 13 = 169$, which does. Therefore the last prime to test is $11$. None of the divisions has remainder $0$, so $131$ is prime.
