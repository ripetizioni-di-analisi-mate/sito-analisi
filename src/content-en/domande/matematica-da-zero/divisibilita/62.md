What is the greatest number that divides $48$, $72$, and $120$?

- $8$
- $12$
- $48$
- $24$

## Explanation

We have $48 = 2^4 \cdot 3$, $72 = 2^3 \cdot 3^2$, and $120 = 2^3 \cdot 3 \cdot 5$. The factor $5$ is not common to all three. The smallest exponent of $2$ is $3$, and the smallest exponent of $3$ is $1$, so the GCD is $2^3 \cdot 3 = 24$. The number $48$ does not divide $72$, so it is not a common divisor.
