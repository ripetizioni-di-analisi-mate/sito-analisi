Two ropes of lengths $150$ m and $210$ m are cut into pieces of equal length, as long as possible, with nothing left over. How many pieces are there altogether?

- $7$
- $12$
- $30$
- $35$

## Explanation

The length of each piece is $\operatorname{MCD}(150, 210)$. Since $150 = 2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5^2$ and $210 = 2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 \cdot 7$, the GCD is $2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 = 30$ m. The first rope gives $150 : 30 = 5$ pieces and the second $210 : 30 = 7$. This makes $5 + 7 = 12$ pieces in all.
