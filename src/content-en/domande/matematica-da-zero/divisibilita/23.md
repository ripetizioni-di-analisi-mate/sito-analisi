The number $23\square4$ is divisible by $4$ for certain choices of the digit $\square$. What is the sum of all those digits?

- $10$
- $25$
- $45$
- $20$

## Explanation

The number is divisible by $4$ if the number formed by its last two digits, $\square4$, is divisible by it. Among $04, 14, 24, \dots, 94$, the multiples of $4$ are $04, 24, 44, 64, 84$, so the possible values of $\square$ are $0, 2, 4, 6, 8$. Their sum is $0 + 2 + 4 + 6 + 8 = 20$. The odd digits would give $25$.
