Rectangular tiles measuring $6$ cm by $4$ cm are to form the smallest possible square. All tiles have the same orientation and none may be cut. How many tiles are needed?

- $6$
- $3$
- $12$
- $24$

## Explanation

The side length of the square must be a multiple of both $6$ and $4$, and the smallest is $\operatorname{mcm}(6, 4) = 12$ cm. The square holds $12 : 6 = 2$ tiles in one direction and $12 : 4 = 3$ in the other, so $2 \cdot 3 = 6$ tiles are needed. The value $12$ is the side length in centimeters.
