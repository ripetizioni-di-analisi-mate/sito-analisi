Four-digit numbers are formed using each of the digits $2$, $3$, $5$, and $7$ once. Which is the smallest divisible by $5$?

- $2\,357$
- $2\,375$
- $2\,735$
- $5\,237$

## Explanation

A number is divisible by $5$ if it ends in $0$ or $5$, so the last digit must be $5$. Arrange the remaining digits in the order that makes the number smallest, $2$, $3$, $7$, giving $2\,375$. The number $2\,357$ is smaller but does not end in $5$, whereas $2\,735$ is divisible by $5$ and is larger.
