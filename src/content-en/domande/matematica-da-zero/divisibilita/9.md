Rectangles are made using $18$ identical squares, with no squares left over and no overlaps. How many different rectangles are possible if interchanging the two side lengths does not count as a new rectangle?

- $2$
- $3$
- $4$
- $6$

## Explanation

A rectangle has sides of $a$ and $b$ squares with $a \cdot b = 18$, so the side lengths are divisors of $18$. The pairs are $1 \cdot 18$, $2 \cdot 9$, and $3 \cdot 6$. The number $18$ has $6$ divisors altogether, but each rectangle uses two, so there are $3$ rectangles.
