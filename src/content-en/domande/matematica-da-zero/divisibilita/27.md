Which of the following numbers passes the divisibility test for $11$?

- $3\,526$
- $3\,562$
- $3\,625$
- $3\,652$

## Explanation

Counting from the right in $3\,652$, the digits in odd positions are $2$ and $6$, with sum $8$, and those in even positions are $5$ and $3$, with sum $8$. The difference is $0$, so the number is divisible by $11$. Indeed, $3\,652 = 11 \cdot 332$. The differences for the other numbers are $6$, $2$, and $6$, none of which is a multiple of $11$.
