A rectangular field measuring $300$ m by $195$ m is fenced with equally spaced posts and a post at each corner. How many posts are needed if the distance between consecutive posts is as large as possible?

- $33$
- $66$
- $68$
- $990$

## Explanation

The distance must divide $300$ and $195$, and the greatest possible distance is $\operatorname{MCD}(300, 195)$. Since $300 = 2^2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5^2$ and $195 = 3 \cdot 5 \cdot 13$, the GCD is $15$ m. The two side lengths span $300 : 15 = 20$ and $195 : 15 = 13$ intervals, so the boundary has $2 \cdot (20 + 13) = 66$ intervals and $66$ posts. The value $33$ counts only half the boundary.
