How many numbers from $1$ to $60$ are divisible by both $4$ and $6$?

- $10$
- $15$
- $5$
- $25$

## Explanation

A number divisible by both $4$ and $6$ is a multiple of $\operatorname{mcm}(4, 6) = 12$. The multiples of $12$ from $1$ through $60$ are $12, 24, 36, 48, 60$, giving $5$ in all. The value $15$ counts the multiples of $4$, the value $10$ counts the multiples of $6$, and $25$ is their sum.
