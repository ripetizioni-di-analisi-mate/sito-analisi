Given that $63$ and $91$ are multiples of $7$, which of the following numbers must be a multiple of $7$?

- $152$
- $156$
- $154$
- $160$

## Explanation

If two numbers are multiples of $7$, their sum is also a multiple. Indeed, $63 = 7 \cdot 9$ and $91 = 7 \cdot 13$, so $63 + 91 = 7 \cdot (9 + 13) = 7 \cdot 22 = 154$. The other numbers are not multiples of $7$: $152$, $156$, and $160$ have remainders $5$, $2$, and $6$, respectively.
