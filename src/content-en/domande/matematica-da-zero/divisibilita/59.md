Given $2^3 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 = 6 \cdot m$, what is $m$?

- $10$
- $40$
- $20$
- $60$

## Explanation

Since $6 = 2 \cdot 3$, start with the factorization of $2^3 \cdot 3 \cdot 5$ and remove one factor of $2$ and one factor of $3$. This leaves $2^2 \cdot 5 = 20$. As a check, $6 \cdot 20 = 120 = 2^3 \cdot 3 \cdot 5$. The value $40$ comes from removing only $3$, and the value $60$ from removing only $2$.
