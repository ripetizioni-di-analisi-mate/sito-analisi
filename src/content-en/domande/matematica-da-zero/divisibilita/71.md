The numbers $90$ and $126$ are each divided by their GCD. What are the quotients?

- $5$ and $7$
- $10$ and $14$
- $15$ and $21$
- $45$ and $63$

## Explanation

We have $90 = 2 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5$ and $126 = 2 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 7$, so $\operatorname{MCD}(90, 126) = 2 \cdot 3^2 = 18$. The quotients are $90 : 18 = 5$ and $126 : 18 = 7$. If we divide by a smaller common divisor such as $9$, $6$, or $2$, the quotients still have common factors.
