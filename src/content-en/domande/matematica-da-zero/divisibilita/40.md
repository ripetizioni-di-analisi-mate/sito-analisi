The number $221$ is not divisible by $2$, $3$, $5$, or $7$. Is $221$ prime?

- Yes, because it is not divisible by $2$, $3$, $5$, or $7$
- No, because $221 = 13 \cdot 17$
- Yes, because it ends in $1$
- No, because it is divisible by $11$

## Explanation

For $221$, test the primes up to $13$ because $13 \cdot 13 = 169$ does not exceed $221$ and $17 \cdot 17 = 289$ does. Division by $13$ leaves remainder $0$ because $13 \cdot 17 = 221$, so $221$ is composite. Stopping at $7$ is not enough. Division by $11$ leaves remainder $1$ rather than $0$.
