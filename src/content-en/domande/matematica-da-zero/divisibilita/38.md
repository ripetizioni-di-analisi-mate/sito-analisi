Which statement about prime numbers is correct?

- Every prime number is odd
- Every odd number greater than $1$ is prime
- $9$ is prime because it is odd
- $2$ is the only even prime number

## Explanation

Every even number greater than $2$ is divisible by $2$, so it has a divisor other than $1$ and itself and is not prime. The number $2$ has only the divisors $1$ and $2$, so it is prime and even. Not every odd number greater than $1$ is prime: $9 = 3 \cdot 3$.
