What is the prime factorization of $180$?

- $2 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5$
- $2^2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5$
- $2^2 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5$
- $2^3 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5$

## Explanation

Divide by successive prime factors: $180 : 2 = 90$, $90 : 2 = 45$, $45 : 3 = 15$, $15 : 3 = 5$, $5 : 5 = 1$. The factors are $2, 2, 3, 3, 5$, so $180 = 2^2 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5$. The other expressions equal $90$, $60$, and $360$.
