A florist has $54$ roses and $90$ tulips and wants to make as many identical bouquets as possible, using all the flowers. How many tulips go in each bouquet?

- $3$
- $15$
- $18$
- $5$

## Explanation

The number of bouquets must divide $54$ and $90$, and the largest possible number is $\operatorname{MCD}(54, 90)$. Since $54 = 2 \cdot 3^3$ and $90 = 2 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5$, the GCD gives $2 \cdot 3^2 = 18$ bouquets. Each bouquet has $90 : 18 = 5$ tulips and $54 : 18 = 3$ roses. The number $18$ is the number of bouquets.
