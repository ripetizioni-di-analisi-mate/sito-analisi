What is the sum of the prime numbers between $10$ and $20$?

- $41$
- $49$
- $60$
- $75$

## Explanation

The numbers between $10$ and $20$ are $11, \dots, 19$. The even numbers are divisible by $2$, and $15$ is divisible by $3$ and by $5$, so $11, 13, 17, 19$ remain. The sum is $11 + 13 + 17 + 19 = 60$. The value $75$ comes from including $15$, which is composite.
