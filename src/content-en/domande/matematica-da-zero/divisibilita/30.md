What is the largest three-digit number divisible by $11$?

- $969$
- $990$
- $979$
- $999$

## Explanation

In a number $99x$ with ones digit $x$, the digits in odd positions are $x$ and $9$, and the digit in the even position is $9$. The difference is $x + 9 - 9 = x$, which is a multiple of $11$ only for $x = 0$. Therefore the largest is $990 = 11 \cdot 90$. The number $979$ is divisible by $11$, but it is smaller than $990$.
