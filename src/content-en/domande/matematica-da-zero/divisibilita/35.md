Which of the following numbers is prime?

- $87$
- $97$
- $91$
- $93$

## Explanation

To test a number for primality, divide it by the primes $2, 3, 5, 7, \dots$ in order, stopping when the square of the next prime exceeds the number. For $97$, no division has remainder $0$ and $11 \cdot 11 = 121$ exceeds $97$, so $97$ is prime. The others are composite: $87 = 3 \cdot 29$, $91 = 7 \cdot 13$, and $93 = 3 \cdot 31$.
