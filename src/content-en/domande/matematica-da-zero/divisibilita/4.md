Which of the following statements about $0$ and $9$ is true?

- $0$ is a divisor of $9$ because $9 \cdot 0 = 0$
- $9$ is a multiple of $0$
- $0$ is not a multiple of $9$
- $0$ is a multiple of $9$ but is not a divisor of $9$

## Explanation

The number $0$ is a multiple of $9$ because $0 = 9 \cdot 0$. However, it is not a divisor of $9$ because division by $0$ is undefined. Thus $9$ cannot be obtained by multiplying $0$ by a natural number, and $9$ is not a multiple of $0$.
