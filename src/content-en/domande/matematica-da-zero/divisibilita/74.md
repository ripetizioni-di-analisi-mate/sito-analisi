The numbers $9$ and $14$ have no common prime factors. What is their least common multiple?

- $23$
- $63$
- $252$
- $126$

## Explanation

Since $9 = 3^2$ and $14 = 2 \cdot 7$ have no common prime factors, the GCD is $1$ and the LCM is the product $9 \cdot 14 = 126$. The number $23$ is the sum of the two numbers, $63$ is a multiple of $9$ but not of $14$, and $252$ is a larger common multiple.
