Which of the following problems calls for a least common multiple?

- With $24$ boys and $18$ girls, what is the largest number of identical teams that can be formed?
- Two doctors are on duty every $5$ days and every $8$ days, respectively. After how many days are they on duty together?
- A floor measuring $300$ cm by $400$ cm is covered with square tiles. What is the largest possible side length?
- Two ribbons are cut into equal pieces. How long can each piece be?

## Explanation

The LCM answers questions about when events that repeat at regular intervals will occur together again. For the doctors, the answer is $\operatorname{mcm}(5, 8) = 40$ days. The problems about teams, tiles, and pieces of ribbon divide different quantities into equal parts that are as large as possible, so they use the GCD.
