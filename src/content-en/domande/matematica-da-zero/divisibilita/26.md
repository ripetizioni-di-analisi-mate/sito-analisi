A crate contains $2\,340$ bottles to be packed in completely full cartons of the same size with no bottles left over. Which carton sizes are possible: $5$, $9$, or $11$ bottles?

- Cartons of $5$, $9$, and $11$
- Only cartons of $5$
- Cartons of $5$ and $9$
- Cartons of $9$ and $11$

## Explanation

The number $2\,340$ ends in $0$, so it is divisible by $5$. The sum of its digits is $2 + 3 + 4 + 0 = 9$, so it is divisible by $9$. For $11$, the digits in odd positions, $0$ and $3$, add up to $3$, and those in even positions, $4$ and $2$, add up to $6$. The difference is $3$, so the number is not divisible by $11$.
