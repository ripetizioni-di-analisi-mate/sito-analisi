Which pair consists of two primes that differ by $2$?

- $47$ and $49$
- $51$ and $53$
- $55$ and $57$
- $41$ and $43$

## Explanation

The number $41$ is not divisible by any of $2, 3, 5$, and $7 \cdot 7 = 49$ exceeds $41$, so it is prime. The same holds for $43$. Each of the other pairs contains a composite number: $49 = 7 \cdot 7$, $51 = 3 \cdot 17$, and $55 = 5 \cdot 11$. The number $57 = 3 \cdot 19$ is also composite.
