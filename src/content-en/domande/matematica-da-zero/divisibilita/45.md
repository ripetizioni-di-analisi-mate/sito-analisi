A room contains $29$ chairs. Can they be arranged in equal rows with at least two rows and at least two chairs per row?

- Yes, in $5$ rows of $6$ chairs
- No, because $29$ is a prime number
- Yes, because $29$ is odd
- No, because $29$ is odd

## Explanation

With $b$ rows of $a$ chairs, we would have $a \cdot b = 29$ with $a$ and $b$ greater than $1$. Then $a$ would be a divisor of $29$ other than $1$ and $29$. The number $29$ is prime because it is not divisible by any of $2, 3, 5$ and $7 \cdot 7 = 49$ exceeds $29$. Therefore no such arrangement exists: $5$ rows of $6$ chairs would use $30$ chairs.
