Which of the following numbers is divisible by $4$?

- $5\,314$
- $5\,318$
- $5\,316$
- $5\,322$

## Explanation

A number is divisible by $4$ if the number formed by its last two digits is divisible by it. In $5\,316$ the last two digits form $16 = 4 \cdot 4$, so $5\,316$ is divisible by $4$. The others end in $14$, $18$, and $22$, which leave remainder $2$ when divided by $4$. They are even but are not divisible by $4$.
