Two lights flash every $8$ seconds and every $14$ seconds, respectively. At time $0$ they flash together. After how many seconds will they flash together for the third time, excluding time $0$?

- $168$
- $56$
- $112$
- $224$

## Explanation

They flash together at times that are multiples of $\operatorname{mcm}(8, 14)$. Since $8 = 2^3$ and $14 = 2 \cdot 7$, the LCM is $2^3 \cdot 7 = 56$. The times when both flash are $56$, $112$, and $168$, so the third time is at $168$ seconds. The value $56$ is the first time and $112$ is the second.
