A store sells pens in packs of $6$ and notebooks in packs of $8$. Marta wants to buy the same number of pens and notebooks in whole packs, buying as few items as possible. How many packs of pens does she buy?

- $3$
- $4$
- $12$
- $24$

## Explanation

The number of pens and the number of notebooks must be a multiple of both $6$ and $8$, and the smallest is the LCM. Since $6 = 2 \cdot 3$ and $8 = 2^3$, we have $\operatorname{mcm}(6, 8) = 24$. She needs $24$ pens, or $24 : 6 = 4$ packs, and $24$ notebooks, or $3$ packs. The number $24$ counts the individual items of each type.
