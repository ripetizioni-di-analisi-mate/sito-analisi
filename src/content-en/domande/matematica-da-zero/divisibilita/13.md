A number divided by $9$ gives quotient $14$ and remainder $4$. What is the number?

- $130$
- $126$
- $135$
- $56$

## Explanation

The dividend equals the divisor times the quotient, plus the remainder. Therefore the number is $9 \cdot 14 + 4 = 126 + 4 = 130$. The value $126$ leaves out the remainder, whereas $56$ is the product $14 \cdot 4$ of the quotient and remainder.
