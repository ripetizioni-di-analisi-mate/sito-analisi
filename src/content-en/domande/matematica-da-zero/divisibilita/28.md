To apply the divisibility test for $11$ to $90\,827$, find the difference between the sum of the digits in odd positions and the sum of those in even positions. What is this difference?

- $2$
- $24$
- $22$
- $26$

## Explanation

From the right, the digits of $90\,827$ are $7, 2, 8, 0, 9$. Those in odd positions are $7$, $8$, and $9$, with sum $24$, and those in even positions are $2$ and $0$, with sum $2$. The difference is $24 - 2 = 22$. Since $22$ is a multiple of $11$, the number is divisible by $11$. Indeed, $90\,827 = 11 \cdot 8\,257$.
