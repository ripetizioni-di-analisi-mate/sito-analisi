The rectangle in the figure measures $24$ cm by $15$ cm. It is to be covered with identical square tiles without cutting or overlapping them. What is the largest possible side length of one tile?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="0,0 24,0 24,15 0,15" linee="0,0 24,0 24,15 0,15 0,0" etichette="9.5,-2.2,24 cm;-5.5,7,15 cm" xmin=-7 xmax=26 ymin=-4 ymax=17 titolo="Rectangle measuring $24$ cm by $15$ cm"}

- $1$ cm
- $5$ cm
- $3$ cm
- $8$ cm

## Explanation

The side length must divide $24$ and $15$, so the greatest possible length is $\operatorname{MCD}(24, 15)$. Since $24 = 2^3 \cdot 3$ and $15 = 3 \cdot 5$, the GCD is $3$. The side length is $3$ cm, with $8$ tiles along the $24$ cm side and $5$ along the $15$ cm side. The value $8$ divides $24$ but not $15$.
