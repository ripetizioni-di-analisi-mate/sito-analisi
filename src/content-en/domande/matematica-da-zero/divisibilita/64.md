The number $90$ is a multiple of $15$. What is the greatest common divisor of $15$ and $90$?

- $5$
- $15$
- $45$
- $90$

## Explanation

Since $90 = 15 \cdot 6$, the number $90$ is a multiple of $15$. Thus $15$ divides both numbers and is the largest possible divisor of $15$, so $\operatorname{MCD}(15, 90) = 15$. The factorizations $15 = 3 \cdot 5$ and $90 = 2 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5$ give the same result, $3 \cdot 5 = 15$.
