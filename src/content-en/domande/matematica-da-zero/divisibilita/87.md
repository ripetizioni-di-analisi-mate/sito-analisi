Two volunteers are on duty every $9$ days and every $12$ days, respectively, and both are on duty today. How many times in the next $100$ days, excluding today, will they be on duty on the same day?

- $2$
- $1$
- $3$
- $8$

## Explanation

They are on duty together on days that are multiples of $\operatorname{mcm}(9, 12)$. Since $9 = 3^2$ and $12 = 2^2 \cdot 3$, the LCM is $2^2 \cdot 3^2 = 36$. The multiples of $36$ no greater than $100$ are $36$ and $72$ because $36 \cdot 3 = 108$ exceeds $100$. Therefore it happens $2$ times.
