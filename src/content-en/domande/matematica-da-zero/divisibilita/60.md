Paolo claims that every number divisible by both $4$ and $6$ is also divisible by $24$. Which number shows that his claim is false?

- $8$
- $24$
- $48$
- $12$

## Explanation

The number $12$ gives whole-number quotients with both $4$ and $6$, yet it is smaller than $24$ and is not a multiple of it. The two divisors share a factor of $2$, so requiring divisibility by their product is a stronger condition.
