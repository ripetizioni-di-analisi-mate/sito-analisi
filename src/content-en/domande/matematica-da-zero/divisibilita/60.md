How many divisors of $60$ are prime?

- $2$
- $4$
- $12$
- $3$

## Explanation

We have $60 = 2^2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5$, so the prime factors are $2$, $3$, and $5$, and every prime divisor is one of these. Therefore there are $3$ prime divisors. The value $12$ is the total number of divisors of $60$, while $4$ counts $1$ as well, although it is not prime.
