A box in the shape of a rectangular prism has edges of lengths $60$ cm, $36$ cm, and $24$ cm. It is filled, without gaps, with identical cubes as large as possible. How many cubes are needed?

- $10$
- $30$
- $12$
- $4\,320$

## Explanation

The edge length of a cube must divide $60$, $36$, and $24$, and the greatest possible length is $\operatorname{MCD}(60, 36, 24)$. Since $60 = 2^2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5$, $36 = 2^2 \cdot 3^2$, and $24 = 2^3 \cdot 3$, the GCD is $2^2 \cdot 3 = 12$ cm. Along the three edges of the box there is room for $5$, $3$, and $2$ cubes, respectively, for a total of $5 \cdot 3 \cdot 2 = 30$.
