Two buses leave the same stop together at 8\:00. The first leaves every $10$ minutes and the second every $16$ minutes. At what time will they next leave together?

- 9\:20
- 8\:02
- 10\:40
- 8\:40

## Explanation

The buses leave together after a number of minutes that is a multiple of both $10$ and $16$, and the first such interval is the LCM. Since $10 = 2 \cdot 5$ and $16 = 2^4$, we have $\operatorname{mcm}(10, 16) = 2^4 \cdot 5 = 80$ minutes, giving 9\:20. The value $2$ minutes is the GCD, and $160$ minutes, which gives 10\:40, is the product.
