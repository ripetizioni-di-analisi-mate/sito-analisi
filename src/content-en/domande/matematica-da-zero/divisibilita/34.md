Without carrying out the additions, which of the following sums must be a multiple of $9$?

- $5\,390 + 2\,142$
- $5\,391 + 2\,140$
- $5\,391 + 2\,142$
- $5\,392 + 2\,142$

## Explanation

The sum of two multiples of $9$ is a multiple of $9$. We have $5 + 3 + 9 + 1 = 18$ and $2 + 1 + 4 + 2 = 9$, so $5\,391$ and $2\,142$ are multiples of $9$, as is their sum $7\,533$. In each of the other sums, one addend has digit sum $17$, $7$, or $19$, none of which is a multiple of $9$.
