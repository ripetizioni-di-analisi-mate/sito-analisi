A natural number ends in the digit $0$. Which statement must be true?

- It is divisible by $2$, $5$, and $10$
- It is divisible by $3$
- It is divisible by $4$
- It is divisible by $100$

## Explanation

A number ending in $0$ is obtained by multiplying by $10$ the number formed by the remaining digits, so it is a multiple of $10$. Since $10 = 2 \cdot 5$, it is also divisible by $2$ and $5$. It need not be divisible by $4$ or $100$, since $30$ is divisible by neither, or by $3$, since $20$ is not.
