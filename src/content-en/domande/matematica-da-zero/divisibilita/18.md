Only one of the following numbers is a multiple of $3$ without being a multiple of $9$. Which one?

- $2\,436$
- $2\,439$
- $2\,430$
- $2\,435$

## Explanation

The digit sum must be a multiple of $3$ but not of $9$. For $2\,436$, we have $2 + 4 + 3 + 6 = 15$, which is a multiple of $3$ but not of $9$. For $2\,439$ and $2\,430$, the sums are $18$ and $9$, multiples of $9$, whereas for $2\,435$ the sum is $14$, which is not a multiple of $3$.
