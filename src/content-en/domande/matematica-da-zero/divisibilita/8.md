How many multiples of $8$ are there from $1$ through $100$, including the endpoints?

- $11$
- $13$
- $8$
- $12$

## Explanation

The positive multiples of $8$ are $8 \cdot 1, 8 \cdot 2, 8 \cdot 3, \dots$. We have $100 = 8 \cdot 12 + 4$, so $8 \cdot 12 = 96$ does not exceed $100$, whereas $8 \cdot 13 = 104$ does. The multiples we seek are therefore $8 \cdot 1, \dots, 8 \cdot 12$, giving $12$ in all.
