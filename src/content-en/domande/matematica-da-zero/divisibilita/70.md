The numbers $2\,024$ and $2\,025$ are consecutive. What is their greatest common divisor?

- $2$
- $3$
- $1$
- $5$

## Explanation

A common divisor of $2\,024$ and $2\,025$ also divides their difference, $2\,025 - 2\,024 = 1$. The only divisor of $1$ is $1$, so $\operatorname{MCD}(2\,024, 2\,025) = 1$. Indeed, $2\,025 = 3^4 \cdot 5^2$ and $2\,024 = 2^3 \cdot 11 \cdot 23$ have no common prime factors.
