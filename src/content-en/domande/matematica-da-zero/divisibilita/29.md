How many two-digit numbers are divisible by $25$?

- $2$
- $3$
- $4$
- $5$

## Explanation

A number is divisible by $25$ if it ends in $00$, $25$, $50$, or $75$. Among the numbers from $10$ to $99$, this leaves $25$, $50$, and $75$, since the ending $00$ would give $100$, which has three digits. Therefore there are $3$ such numbers.
