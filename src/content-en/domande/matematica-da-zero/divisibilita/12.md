The first multiples of a number are marked with dots on the number line, starting at $0$. Which number should be marked immediately after $28$?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 linee="0,0 36.5,0;36,0.3 36.5,0 36,-0.3;0,-0.15 0,0.15;7,-0.15 7,0.15;14,-0.15 14,0.15;21,-0.15 21,0.15;28,-0.15 28,0.15" punti="0,0,pieno;7,0,pieno;14,0,pieno;21,0,pieno;28,0,pieno" etichette="-0.3,-0.9,0;6.7,-0.9,7;13.4,-0.9,14;20.4,-0.9,21;27.4,-0.9,28" xmin=-1.5 xmax=38 ymin=-1.6 ymax=1 titolo="The first points marked on the number line"}

- $35$
- $29$
- $32$
- $56$

## Explanation

The marked points are $0, 7, 14, 21, 28$ and each is $7$ units from the previous one, so they are multiples of $7$. The next is $28 + 7 = 35 = 7 \cdot 5$. The number $56$ is a multiple of $7$, but $35$, $42$, and $49$ come before it, whereas $29$ and $32$ are not multiples of $7$.
