Which of the following pairs consists of coprime numbers?

- $16$ and $45$
- $12$ and $45$
- $14$ and $35$
- $22$ and $55$

## Explanation

Two numbers are coprime if their GCD is $1$. We have $16 = 2^4$ and $45 = 3^2 \cdot 5$, which have no common prime factors. In each of the other pairs there is a common divisor: $3$ for $12$ and $45$, $7$ for $14$ and $35$, and $11$ for $22$ and $55$. Therefore only $16$ and $45$ are coprime, even though both are composite.
