Which statement about numbers divisible by $3$ and by $9$ is true?

- Every number divisible by $3$ is divisible by $9$
- A number divisible by $9$ is never divisible by $3$
- A number divisible by $3$ always has a digit sum equal to $3$
- Every number divisible by $9$ is divisible by $3$, but the converse does not hold

## Explanation

A number divisible by $9$ can be written as $9k = 3 \cdot (3k)$, so it is divisible by $3$. The converse is false: $12$ is divisible by $3$ but not by $9$. The last statement is also false because $15$ is divisible by $3$ and its digits add up to $6$.
