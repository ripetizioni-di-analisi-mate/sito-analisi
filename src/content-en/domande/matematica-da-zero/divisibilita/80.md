What is the smallest number greater than $0$ divisible by both $4$ and $25$?

- $100$
- $29$
- $50$
- $200$

## Explanation

The number we seek is the smallest common multiple of $4$ and $25$, that is, their LCM. Since $4 = 2^2$ and $25 = 5^2$ have no common prime factors, the LCM is $4 \cdot 25 = 100$. The number $50$ is a multiple of $25$ but not of $4$, whereas $200$ is a common multiple but not the smallest.
