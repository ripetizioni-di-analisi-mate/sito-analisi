A number of items must be divisible by $5$, $7$ and $15$ so that packing leaves no remainder. Which requirement can be dropped without changing the allowed quantities, keeping the other two?

- Divisibility by $5$.
- Divisibility by $7$.
- Divisibility by $15$.
- All three together.

## Explanation

A multiple of $15$ is already divisible by $5$ because $15=3\cdot5$. Keeping the requirements for $7$ and $15$ therefore gives exactly the original set. For example, $15$ by itself does not satisfy the requirement for $7$.
