The first bus leaves at 7\:00, and a bus leaves every $15$ minutes after that. Which of these is a departure time?

- 8\:50
- 9\:10
- 8\:45
- 8\:20

## Explanation

Departures are $0, 15, 30, 45, \dots$ minutes after 7\:00, that is, at multiples of $15$ minutes. The time 8\:45 is $105$ minutes after 7\:00, and $105 = 15 \cdot 7$. The times 8\:50, 9\:10, and 8\:20 are $110$, $130$, and $80$ minutes after 7\:00, respectively, and none of those intervals is a multiple of $15$.
