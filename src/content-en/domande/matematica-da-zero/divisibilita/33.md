Replacing $\square$ with a digit makes $54\square$ a three-digit number. Which digit makes it divisible by both $2$ and $9$?

- $2$
- $0$
- $6$
- $9$

## Explanation

For divisibility by $2$, the ones digit must be even. For divisibility by $9$, the sum $5 + 4 + \square = 9 + \square$ must be a multiple of $9$, so $\square = 0$ or $\square = 9$. Only $0$ is even, so the number is $540$. With $9$ the number would be odd.
