- $11$
- $19$
- $25$

## Explanation

A number that is a multiple of neither $2$ nor $3$ can still be divisible by $5$ or by $7$. Among the shaded numbers, $25 = 5 \cdot 5$ is divisible by $5$, so it is composite. The other three, $11$, $19$, and $23$, have no divisors other than $1$ and themselves, so they are prime.
