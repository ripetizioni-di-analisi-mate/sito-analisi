A box contains $360$ bolts to be divided among bags, all with the same number of bolts and none left over. Which of the following primes could be the number of bolts per bag?

- $7$
- $11$
- $5$
- $13$

## Explanation

The possible numbers are the divisors of $360$. We have $360 = 2^3 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5$, so the only prime divisors are $2$, $3$, and $5$. Of the choices, only $5$ is one of them, and indeed $360 : 5 = 72$. Division by $7$, $11$, or $13$ leaves a remainder.
