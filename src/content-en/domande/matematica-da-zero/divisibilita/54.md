Which of the following numbers is a divisor of $n = 2^2 \cdot 3 \cdot 7$?

- $21$
- $8$
- $18$
- $35$

## Explanation

A divisor of $n$ can be formed from some of the factors of $n$, using each no more times than it occurs. The number $21 = 3 \cdot 7$ uses $3$ and $7$ once each, so it divides $n$. The number $8 = 2^3$ has the factor $2$ with exponent $3$, $18 = 2 \cdot 3^2$ has the factor $3$ with exponent $2$, and $35$ contains the factor $5$.
