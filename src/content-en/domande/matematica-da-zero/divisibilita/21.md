Which of the following numbers is divisible by $2$, $3$, and $5$ at the same time?

- $3\,545$
- $3\,546$
- $3\,550$
- $3\,540$

## Explanation

Three conditions must hold. The last digit must be $0$ for divisibility by both $2$ and $5$, and the digit sum must be a multiple of $3$. For $3\,540$ the sum is $3 + 5 + 4 + 0 = 12$, so all three conditions hold. The digits of $3\,550$ add up to $13$, $3\,546$ ends in $6$, and $3\,545$ is odd.
