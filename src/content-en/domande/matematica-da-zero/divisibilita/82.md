Among the common multiples of $6$ and $14$, what is the first that exceeds $100$?

- $120$
- $140$
- $126$
- $168$

## Explanation

The common multiples of $6$ and $14$ are the multiples of their LCM. Since $6 = 2 \cdot 3$ and $14 = 2 \cdot 7$, we have $\operatorname{mcm}(6, 14) = 42$. The multiples of $42$ are $42, 84, 126, \dots$, and the first greater than $100$ is $126$. The number $120$ is not a multiple of $14$, $140$ is not a multiple of $6$, and $168 = 42 \cdot 4$ is not the first.
