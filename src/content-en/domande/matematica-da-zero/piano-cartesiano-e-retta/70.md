What is the equation of the line parallel to $y=-x+4$ through $(3,2)$?

- $y=-x+4$
- $y=-x-1$
- $y=-x+5$
- $y=x-1$

## Explanation

A parallel line has the same slope $-1$. The point-slope formula gives $y-2=-(x-3)$, or $y=-x+3+2=-x+5$. Checking at $(3,2)$ gives $-3+5=2$. The line $y=-x+4$ is the original line and does not pass through $(3,2)$.
