For what value of $k$ does the system $\begin{cases} x+2y=3 \\ 2x+4y=k \end{cases}$ have infinitely many solutions?

- $k=6$
- $k=3$
- $k=8$
- $k=12$

## Explanation

The coefficients in the second equation are twice those in the first. The equations represent the same line when the constant term is also doubled, that is, when $k=2\cdot 3=6$. Then the second equation is the first multiplied by $2$ and every point on the line is a solution.
