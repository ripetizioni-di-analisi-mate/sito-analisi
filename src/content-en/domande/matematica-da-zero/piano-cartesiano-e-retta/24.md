Which of the following points lies on the graph of the function $f(x)=x^2+1$?

- $(3,10)$
- $(3,9)$
- $(3,7)$
- $(3,4)$

## Explanation

A point lies on the graph when its ordinate is the value of the function at its abscissa. For $x=3$, we have $f(3)=3^2+1=10$, so the point is $(3,10)$. The point $(3,9)$ lies on the graph of $x^2$, without the term $+1$.
