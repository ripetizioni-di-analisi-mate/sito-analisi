A budget of $20$ euros is spent entirely on notebooks at $2$ euros each and pens at $1$ euro each, according to the equation $2x+y=20$, where $x$ is the number of notebooks and $y$ the number of pens. How many pens can be bought with $6$ notebooks?

- $14$
- $12$
- $10$
- $8$

## Explanation

Substituting $x=6$ into the equation gives $12+y=20$, so $y=8$. The $6$ notebooks cost $12$ euros, leaving $8$ euros for $8$ pens. The value $14$ comes from subtracting $6$ from $20$ as though each notebook cost $1$ euro.
