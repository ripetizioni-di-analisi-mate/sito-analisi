At a movie theater, $3$ adults and $2$ children pay $30$ euros, while $1$ adult and $4$ children pay $20$ euros. How much is an adult ticket?

- $3$ euros
- $10$ euros
- $6$ euros
- $8$ euros

## Explanation

Let $a$ and $r$ be the ticket prices. From the system $3a+2r=30$ and $a+4r=20$, we obtain $a=20-4r$. Substituting gives $60-12r+2r=30$, so $r=3$ and $a=20-12=8$. The value $3$ is the price of a child's ticket.
