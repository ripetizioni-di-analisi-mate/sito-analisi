What is the slope of the line with equation $4x+2y-10=0$?

- $2$
- $-\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $-2$
- $4$

## Explanation

Solve for $y$. Starting from $2y=-4x+10$ and dividing by $2$ gives $y=-2x+5$. The coefficient of $x$ is $-2$. Using the formula $m=-\dfrac{a}{b}$ gives $m=-\dfrac{4}{2}=-2$. The value $2$ results from dropping the sign.
