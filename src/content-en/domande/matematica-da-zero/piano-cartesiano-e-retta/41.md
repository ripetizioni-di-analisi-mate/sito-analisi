At what point does the line $y=3x-12$ cross the $x$-axis?

- $(-4,0)$
- $(12,0)$
- $(0,-12)$
- $(4,0)$

## Explanation

On the $x$-axis the ordinate is $0$, so set $3x-12=0$. This gives $3x=12$ and $x=4$. The point is $(4,0)$. The point $(0,-12)$ is the intersection with the $y$-axis.
