What are the coordinates of the point where the horizontal and vertical lines in the figure meet?

::grafico{f="3" linee="-2,-4 -2,5" xmin=-5 xmax=4 ymin=-4 ymax=5 uguale=1 punti="-2,3,pieno" legenda=0}

- $(3,-2)$
- $(-2,-3)$
- $(2,3)$
- $(-2,3)$

## Explanation

The vertical line passes through $x=-2$, so all its points have abscissa $-2$. The horizontal line passes through $y=3$, so all its points have ordinate $3$. Their common point is $(-2,3)$. Interchanging the coordinates gives a different point.
