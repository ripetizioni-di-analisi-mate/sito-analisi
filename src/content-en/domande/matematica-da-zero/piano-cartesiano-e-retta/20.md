Two friends live at points $A(1,1)$ and $B(9,7)$ on a map where one unit represents $100$ m. They meet halfway along segment $AB$. How many meters does each travel?

- $1000$ m
- $250$ m
- $700$ m
- $500$ m

## Explanation

The coordinate differences are $9-1=8$ and $7-1=6$, so $\overline{AB}=\sqrt{8^2+6^2}=\sqrt{100}=10$ units, or $1000$ m. They meet at the midpoint, so each travels half the length of the segment, or $500$ m.
