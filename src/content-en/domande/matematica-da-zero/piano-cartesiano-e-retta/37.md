Which line contains all the points whose abscissa and ordinate are opposites, such as $(-3,3)$, $(2,-2)$, and $(5,-5)$?

- $y=-x$
- $y=x$
- $y=-3x$
- $y=0$

## Explanation

At the given points, the ordinate is the opposite of the abscissa, so $y=-x$. This is the line through the origin with $m=-1$, the bisector of quadrants II and IV. The point $(-3,3)$ satisfies the equation because $-(-3)=3$. By contrast, the equation $y=x$ is satisfied by points with equal abscissa and ordinate, such as $(3,3)$.
