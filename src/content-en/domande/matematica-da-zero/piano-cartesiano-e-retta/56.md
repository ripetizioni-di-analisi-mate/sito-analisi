Which of the following lines passes through $(4,-1)$?

- $3x+2y+10=0$
- $3x+2y-10=0$
- $3x-2y-10=0$
- $2x+3y-10=0$

## Explanation

Substitute $x=4$ and $y=-1$ into each equation. The equation $3x+2y-10=0$ gives $12-2-10=0$, so it holds. The equations $3x+2y+10=0$, $3x-2y-10=0$, and $2x+3y-10=0$ give $12-2+10=20$, $12+2-10=4$, and $8-3-10=-5$, respectively, all different from $0$.
