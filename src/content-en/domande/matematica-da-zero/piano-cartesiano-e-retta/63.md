Only one of the following equations describes the line through $A(4,-3)$ and $B(4,5)$. Which one?

- $y=4$
- $x=1$
- $x=4$
- $y=1$

## Explanation

The two points have the same abscissa $4$, so the line is vertical and the ratio of the changes is undefined because the difference between the abscissas is $0$. The equation is $x=4$. The average ordinate $1$ plays no part in the calculation.
