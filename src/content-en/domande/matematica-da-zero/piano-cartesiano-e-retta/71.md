A line has slope $-\dfrac{4}{3}$. What is the slope of a line perpendicular to it?

- $-\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $-\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

Two lines are perpendicular when the product of their slopes is $-1$, that is, $m'=-\dfrac{1}{m}$. This gives $m'=-\dfrac{1}{-\frac{4}{3}}=\dfrac{3}{4}$. Indeed, $-\dfrac{4}{3}\cdot\dfrac{3}{4}=-1$. The value $-\dfrac{3}{4}$ is the reciprocal without the necessary sign change.
