For what value of $x$ does the function $f(x)=2x-7$ equal $9$?

- $1$
- $11$
- $8$
- $16$

## Explanation

Set $2x-7=9$. Adding $7$ to both sides gives $2x=16$, and dividing by $2$ gives $x=8$. The value $16$ comes from omitting the final division by $2$, whereas $1$ comes from dividing $9-7=2$ by $2$ instead of adding $7$.
