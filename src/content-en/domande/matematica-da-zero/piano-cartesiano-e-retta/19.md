Point $M$ is the midpoint of the segment with endpoints $A(0,0)$ and $B(8,0)$, and $C$ has coordinates $(4,6)$. How long is segment $MC$?

- $6$
- $4$
- $10$
- $8$

## Explanation

The midpoint has coordinates $\left(\dfrac{0+8}{2},\dfrac{0+0}{2}\right)=(4,0)$. The points $M(4,0)$ and $C(4,6)$ have the same abscissa, so segment $MC$ is vertical and has length $6-0=6$. The value $4$ is the common abscissa of the points rather than the distance.
