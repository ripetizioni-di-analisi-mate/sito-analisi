For what value of $k$ does the point $(k,6)$ lie on the line $y=\dfrac{2}{3}x$?

- $4$
- $12$
- $18$
- $9$

## Explanation

Substitute the ordinate $6$ into the equation to obtain $6=\dfrac{2}{3}k$. Multiplying both sides by $\dfrac{3}{2}$ gives $k=6\cdot\dfrac{3}{2}=9$. The value $4$ comes from multiplying $6$ by $\dfrac{2}{3}$ instead of dividing.
