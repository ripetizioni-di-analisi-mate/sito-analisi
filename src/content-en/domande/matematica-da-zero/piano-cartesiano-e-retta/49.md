The point $(a,\ 2a)$ lies on the line $y=x+3$. What is $a$?

- $-3$
- $3$
- $1$
- $6$

## Explanation

Substituting the coordinates into the equation gives $2a=a+3$. Subtracting $a$ from both sides gives $a=3$. The point is $(3,6)$, and indeed $3+3=6$. The value $6$ is the ordinate of the point rather than the value of $a$.
