The lines $y=x$ and $y=-x+4$ and the $x$-axis bound a triangle. What is its area?

- $8$
- $2$
- $4$
- $6$

## Explanation

The line $y=x$ meets the $x$-axis at $(0,0)$, and the line $y=-x+4$ meets it at $(4,0)$. The two lines meet at $(2,2)$ because $x=-x+4$ gives $x=2$. The base is $4$, the height is $2$, and the area is $\dfrac{4\cdot 2}{2}=4$. The value $8$ is the base times the height without the factor of one-half.
