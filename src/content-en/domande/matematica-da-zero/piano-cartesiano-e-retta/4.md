Starting at the origin, move $4$ units to the left and then $3$ units down. What point do you reach?

- $(-4,-3)$
- $(4,3)$
- $(-3,-4)$
- $(-4,3)$

## Explanation

Moving left decreases the abscissa by $4$, giving the abscissa $0-4=-4$. Moving down decreases the ordinate by $3$, so the ordinate is $0-3=-3$. The resulting point is therefore $(-4,-3)$, which lies in quadrant III.
