What is the equation of the line through $A(0,1)$ and $B(6,5)$?

- $y=\dfrac{2}{3}x+1$
- $y=\dfrac{3}{2}x+1$
- $y=\dfrac{2}{3}x+5$
- $y=\dfrac{2}{3}x-1$

## Explanation

The slope is the vertical displacement divided by the horizontal displacement, $m=\dfrac{5-1}{6-0}=\dfrac{4}{6}=\dfrac{2}{3}$. Point $A$ lies on the $y$-axis, so $q=1$. The line is $y=\dfrac{2}{3}x+1$. The ratio $\dfrac{3}{2}$ comes from interchanging the numerator and denominator.
