Which equation in implicit form $ax+by+c=0$ is equivalent to $y=\dfrac{2}{3}x+4$?

- $2x+3y-12=0$
- $2x-3y+12=0$
- $3x-2y+12=0$
- $2x-3y-12=0$

## Explanation

Multiplying both sides by $3$ gives $3y=2x+12$. Moving all terms to the left-hand side gives $2x-3y+12=0$. Checking at the point $(0,4)$ gives $0-12+12=0$. With $2x-3y-12=0$, the same point would give $-24$.
