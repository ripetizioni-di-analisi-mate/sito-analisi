What is the equation of the line in the figure through the two marked points?

::grafico{f="-x/2+2" xmin=-3 xmax=7 ymin=-2 ymax=5 uguale=1 punti="0,2,pieno;4,0,pieno" legenda=0}

- $y=-2x+2$
- $y=-\dfrac{1}{2}x+2$
- $y=\dfrac{1}{2}x+2$
- $y=-\dfrac{1}{2}x+4$

## Explanation

The line crosses the $y$-axis at $(0,2)$, so $q=2$. Going from $(0,2)$ to $(4,0)$ takes us $4$ units to the right and $2$ units down, so $m=\dfrac{-2}{4}=-\dfrac{1}{2}$. The equation is $y=-\dfrac{1}{2}x+2$. The number $4$ is the abscissa of the point on the $x$-axis, rather than the y-intercept.
