In the triangle with vertices $A(0,0)$, $B(6,0)$, and $C(3,4)$, side $AB$ is horizontal. What is the perimeter of the triangle?

- $16$
- $11$
- $15$
- $13$

## Explanation

Side $AB$ has length $6$. For the other two sides, $\overline{AC}=\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=5$ and $\overline{BC}=\sqrt{(6-3)^2+(0-4)^2}=\sqrt{9+16}=5$. The perimeter is $6+5+5=16$. The value $11$ comes from omitting one of the two equal sides, while $15$ results from using the altitude $4$ in place of a slanted side.
