What are the slope $m$ and the y-intercept $q$ of the line with equation $2y=4x-6$?

- $m=4$ and $q=-6$
- $m=2$ and $q=-6$
- $m=2$ and $q=-3$
- $m=4$ and $q=-3$

## Explanation

To read off $m$ and $q$, write the equation in slope-intercept form with $y$ isolated. Dividing both sides by $2$ gives $y=2x-3$. The coefficient of $x$ is $m=2$ and the constant term is $q=-3$. The values $4$ and $-6$ come from the equation before division.
