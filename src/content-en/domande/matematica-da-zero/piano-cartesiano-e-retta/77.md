The triangle with vertices $A(0,0)$, $B(4,2)$, and $C(-1,2)$ has a right angle. At which vertex?

- At $A$
- At $B$
- At $C$
- At none of the vertices

## Explanation

Side $AB$ has slope $\dfrac{2}{4}=\dfrac{1}{2}$ and side $AC$ has slope $\dfrac{2}{-1}=-2$. The product is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot(-2)=-1$, so these two sides are perpendicular and the angle at $A$ is a right angle. Side $BC$ is horizontal and $AB$ is not vertical, so neither the angle at $B$ nor the angle at $C$ is a right angle.
