A candle is $20$ cm tall and burns down by $2$ cm each hour. After how many hours does it go out, that is, reach a height of $0$?

- $40$ hours
- $22$ hours
- $18$ hours
- $10$ hours

## Explanation

Its height after $x$ hours is $y=20-2x$. The candle goes out when $y=0$, so $20-2x=0$, which gives $2x=20$ and $x=10$. The value $40$ comes from multiplying $20$ by $2$ instead of dividing.
