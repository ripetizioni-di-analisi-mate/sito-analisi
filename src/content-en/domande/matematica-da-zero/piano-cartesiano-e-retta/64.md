The points $A(0,1)$, $B(2,5)$, and $C(3,k)$ are collinear. What is $k$?

- $6$
- $9$
- $8$
- $7$

## Explanation

The line through $A$ and $B$ has slope $m=\dfrac{5-1}{2-0}=2$ and y-intercept $1$, so its equation is $y=2x+1$. Point $C$ lies on it if $k=2\cdot 3+1=7$. The value $6$ comes from increasing the ordinate by $1$ for each horizontal step.
