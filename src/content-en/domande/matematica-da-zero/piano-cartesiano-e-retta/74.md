For what value of $k$ are the lines $y=(k+1)x$ and $y=3x+5$ parallel?

- $k=3$
- $k=4$
- $k=2$
- $k=-2$

## Explanation

Two lines are parallel when they have the same slope. Set $k+1=3$ to obtain $k=2$. For $k=3$, the slope would be $4$ and the lines would not be parallel. The y-intercept $5$ does not affect parallelism.
