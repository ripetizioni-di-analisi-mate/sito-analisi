On a map, one unit represents $1$ km. The museum is at $(2,-3)$ and the station at $(2,5)$. How many kilometers apart are they?

- $2$ km
- $8$ km
- $5$ km
- $15$ km

## Explanation

The two points have the same abscissa, so the segment is vertical and the distance is the difference between the ordinates. We obtain $5-(-3)=5+3=8$. The value $2$ comes from adding $-3$ and $5$ without changing the sign of the second number.
