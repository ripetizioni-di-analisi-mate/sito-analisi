The two points marked in the figure determine a line. What is its equation?

::grafico{f="-3*x/2+1" xmin=-4 xmax=5 ymin=-4 ymax=6 uguale=1 punti="-2,4,pieno;2,-2,pieno" legenda=0}

- $y=-\dfrac{2}{3}x+1$
- $y=\dfrac{3}{2}x+1$
- $y=-\dfrac{3}{2}x-1$
- $y=-\dfrac{3}{2}x+1$

## Explanation

The figure gives $(-2,4)$ and $(2,-2)$. The slope is $m=\dfrac{-2-4}{2-(-2)}=\dfrac{-6}{4}=-\dfrac{3}{2}$. Using the point $(2,-2)$ gives $y+2=-\dfrac{3}{2}(x-2)$, or $y=-\dfrac{3}{2}x+3-2=-\dfrac{3}{2}x+1$. The line falls, so $m<0$, and it crosses the $y$-axis above the origin, so $q>0$.
