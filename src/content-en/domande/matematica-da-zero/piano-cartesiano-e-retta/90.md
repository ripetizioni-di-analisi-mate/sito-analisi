The line $y=-2x+8$ meets the $x$-axis at $A$ and the line $y=x+2$ at $B$. How long is segment $AB$?

- $4$
- $2\sqrt{5}$
- $2\sqrt{2}$
- $\sqrt{20}+2$

## Explanation

To find $A$, set $y=0$ in $y=-2x+8$ to obtain $A(4,0)$. To find $B$, solve $-2x+8=x+2$, so $x=2$, $y=4$, and $B(2,4)$. The distance is $\overline{AB}=\sqrt{(4-2)^2+(0-4)^2}=\sqrt{20}=2\sqrt{5}$.
