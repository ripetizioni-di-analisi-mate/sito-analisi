What is the equation of the line parallel to the $y$-axis through the midpoint of the segment with endpoints $A(2,1)$ and $B(6,9)$?

- $y=5$
- $x=4$
- $x=5$
- $y=4$

## Explanation

The midpoint is $M\left(\dfrac{2+6}{2},\dfrac{1+9}{2}\right)=(4,5)$. A line parallel to the $y$-axis is vertical and has equation $x=h$, where $h$ is the abscissa of its points. Thus, the line is $x=4$. The ordinate $5$ of the midpoint would instead give the horizontal line $y=5$.
