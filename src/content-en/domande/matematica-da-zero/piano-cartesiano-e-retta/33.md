A line passes through the origin and through the point $P(-2,5)$. What is the $x$-coordinate of the point on the line whose $y$-coordinate is $-10$?

- $4$
- $-4$
- $25$
- $-25$

## Explanation

A line through the origin has equation $y = mx$, and its slope is found from one of its points as $m = \dfrac{y}{x}$. For $P(-2,5)$ we get $m = \dfrac{5}{-2} = -\dfrac{5}{2}$, so the line is $y = -\dfrac{5}{2}x$. The point with $y$-coordinate $-10$ satisfies $-10 = -\dfrac{5}{2}x$, therefore $x = -10 \cdot \left(-\dfrac{2}{5}\right) = 4$. The value $25$ comes from multiplying $-10$ by $m$ instead of dividing it by $m$.
