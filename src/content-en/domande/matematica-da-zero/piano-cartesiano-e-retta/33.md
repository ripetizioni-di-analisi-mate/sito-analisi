A line passes through the origin and $P(-2,5)$. If a point on this line has $y$-coordinate $-10$, what is its $x$-coordinate?

- $4$
- $-4$
- $25$
- $-25$

## Explanation

Along a line through the origin both coordinates scale by the same factor. Going from $5$ to $-10$ requires the factor $-2$. Multiplying the $x$-coordinate by $-2$ as well gives $4$.
