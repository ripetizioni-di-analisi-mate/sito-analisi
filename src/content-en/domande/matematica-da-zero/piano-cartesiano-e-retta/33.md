A line passes through the origin and the point $P(-2,5)$. What is its slope?

- $-\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $3$

## Explanation

For a line $y=mx$, the slope is the ratio of the ordinate to the abscissa of a point on the line. For $P(-2,5)$, this gives $m=\dfrac{5}{-2}=-\dfrac{5}{2}$. The sign is negative because the abscissa and ordinate have opposite signs, so the line falls.
