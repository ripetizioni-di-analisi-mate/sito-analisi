Which of the following lines is parallel to the line with equation $4x-2y+1=0$?

- $y=-2x+1$
- $y=4x+1$
- $y=-\dfrac{1}{2}x+1$
- $y=2x-7$

## Explanation

Solving for $y$ gives $y=2x+\dfrac{1}{2}$, so the slope is $2$. Two lines are parallel when they have the same slope, so the required line is $y=2x-7$. The line with slope $-\dfrac{1}{2}$ is perpendicular instead.
