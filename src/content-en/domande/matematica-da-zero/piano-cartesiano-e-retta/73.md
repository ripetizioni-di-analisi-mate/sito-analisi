Which pair consists of perpendicular lines?

- $y=3x+2$ and $x+3y=6$
- $y=3x+2$ and $y=-3x+2$
- $y=3x+2$ and $y=\dfrac{1}{3}x+2$
- $y=3x+2$ and $3x-y=1$

## Explanation

The line $x+3y=6$ can be written as $y=-\dfrac{1}{3}x+2$, and the product $3\cdot\left(-\dfrac{1}{3}\right)=-1$ shows that the lines are perpendicular. In two of the other pairs, the products are $-9$ and $1$. In the remaining pair, $3x-y=1$ is equivalent to $y=3x-1$, giving parallel lines.
