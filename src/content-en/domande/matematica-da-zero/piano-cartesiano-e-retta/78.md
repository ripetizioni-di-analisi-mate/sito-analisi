What is the equation of the line perpendicular to the segment with endpoints $A(1,2)$ and $B(5,4)$ and passing through its midpoint?

- $y=-2x+4$
- $y=-2x+9$
- $y=\dfrac{1}{2}x+\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $y=2x-3$

## Explanation

The midpoint is $M(3,3)$ and the line $AB$ has slope $\dfrac{4-2}{5-1}=\dfrac{1}{2}$. The perpendicular line has slope $-2$, and $y-3=-2(x-3)$ gives $y=-2x+9$. The line $y=-2x+4$ instead passes through $A$.
