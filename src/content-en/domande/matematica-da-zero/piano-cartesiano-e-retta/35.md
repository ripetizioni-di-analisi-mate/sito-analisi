Which of the following lines through the origin is steepest, that is, has the greatest inclination relative to the $x$-axis?

- $y=3x$
- $y=-4x$
- $y=\dfrac{1}{2}x$
- $y=-x$

## Explanation

Steepness depends on the absolute value of the slope. The absolute values of $-4$, $3$, $\dfrac{1}{2}$, and $-1$ are $4$, $3$, $\dfrac{1}{2}$, and $1$. The greatest is $4$, so the steepest line is $y=-4x$, even though it falls.
