How many of the points $(0,4)$, $(2,0)$, $(-3,5)$, $(0,0)$, and $(-1,0)$ lie on the $x$-axis?

- $2$
- $3$
- $1$
- $4$

## Explanation

A point lies on the $x$-axis when its ordinate is $0$. The points with ordinate $0$ are $(2,0)$, $(0,0)$, and $(-1,0)$. The origin $(0,0)$ lies on both axes, so it counts. Point $(0,4)$ lies on the $y$-axis and $(-3,5)$ lies in quadrant II, so the number of points is $3$.
