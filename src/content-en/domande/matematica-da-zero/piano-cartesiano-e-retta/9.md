Which point in the figure has a positive abscissa and a negative ordinate?

::grafico{xmin=-5 xmax=5 ymin=-4 ymax=4 uguale=1 punti="2,3,pieno,A;-3,2,pieno,B;4,-2,pieno,C;-2,-3,pieno,D" legenda=0}

- $C$
- $A$
- $B$
- $D$

## Explanation

A positive abscissa and a negative ordinate identify quadrant IV, at the lower right. Point $C(4,-2)$ lies there. Point $A(2,3)$ lies in quadrant I, $B(-3,2)$ in quadrant II, and $D(-2,-3)$ in quadrant III.
