The solution of the system $\begin{cases} x+2y=7 \\ 3x-2y=5 \end{cases}$ is a pair $(x,y)$. What is the product $x\cdot y$?

- $6$
- $5$
- $1$
- $12$

## Explanation

Adding corresponding sides of the equations eliminates the term in $y$ and gives $4x=12$, so $x=3$. From the first equation, $3+2y=7$ gives $y=2$. The product is $3\cdot 2=6$. The value $5$ is the sum $x+y$.
