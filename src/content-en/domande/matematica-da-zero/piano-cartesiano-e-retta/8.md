On a grid map, city hall is at the origin $(0,0)$, and each square represents one city block. The library is $4$ blocks west and $1$ block south of city hall. What are its coordinates?

- $(4,1)$
- $(-4,-1)$
- $(-1,-4)$
- $(4,-1)$

## Explanation

West is the negative direction of the $x$-axis, so $4$ blocks west gives abscissa $-4$. South is the negative direction of the $y$-axis, so $1$ block south gives ordinate $-1$. Coordinates are written with the abscissa first and the ordinate second, so the library is at $(-4,-1)$.
