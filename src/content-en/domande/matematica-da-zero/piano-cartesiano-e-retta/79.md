Which pair is a solution of the system $\begin{cases} y=2x-1 \\ 3x+y=9 \end{cases}$?

- $(3,5)$
- $(1,6)$
- $(2,3)$
- $(3,0)$

## Explanation

Substituting $y=2x-1$ into the second equation gives $3x+2x-1=9$, so $5x=10$ and $x=2$. Then $y=2\cdot 2-1=3$. The pair $(3,5)$ satisfies only the first equation, while $(1,6)$ and $(3,0)$ satisfy only the second.
