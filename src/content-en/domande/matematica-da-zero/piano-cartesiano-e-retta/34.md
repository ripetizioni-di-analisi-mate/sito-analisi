The price of pasta is proportional to its weight. If $3$ kg costs $7.50$ euros, how much does $8$ kg cost?

- $60$ euros
- $12.50$ euros
- $10.50$ euros
- $20$ euros

## Explanation

The graph is a line through the origin, $y=mx$, with $m$ equal to the price of $1$ kg. The unit price is $m=\dfrac{7.50}{3}=2.50$ euros per kilogram. Thus, $8$ kg costs $2.50\cdot 8=20$ euros. The value $12.50$ comes from assuming an additive relationship: going from $3$ to $8$ kg adds $5$ kg, so the same number, $5$ euros, is added to the price.
