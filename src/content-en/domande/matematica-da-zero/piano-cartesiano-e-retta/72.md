A line is perpendicular to $y=2x+1$ and passes through $(4,-1)$. What is its equation?

- $y=-2x+7$
- $y=-\dfrac{1}{2}x+1$
- $y=\dfrac{1}{2}x-3$
- $y=-\dfrac{1}{2}x-1$

## Explanation

The slope of the perpendicular line is $-\dfrac{1}{2}$. The point-slope formula gives $y+1=-\dfrac{1}{2}(x-4)=-\dfrac{1}{2}x+2$, so $y=-\dfrac{1}{2}x+1$. The line $y=-2x+7$ passes through the point, but its slope is just the negative of the original slope.
