The point with coordinates $(a+1,\ 2a-6)$ lies on the $x$-axis. What is $a$?

- $-1$
- $6$
- $3$
- $-3$

## Explanation

A point lies on the $x$-axis when its ordinate is $0$. Thus, set $2a-6=0$, which gives $2a=6$ and $a=3$. The point is $(4,0)$. The value $a=-1$ would make the abscissa zero and place the point on the $y$-axis.
