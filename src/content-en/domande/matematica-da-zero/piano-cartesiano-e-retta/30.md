Given $f(x)=x^2-2x$, what is the difference $f(3)-f(1)$?

- $0$
- $2$
- $-4$
- $4$

## Explanation

Evaluate the two values separately: $f(3)=9-6=3$ and $f(1)=1-2=-1$. The difference is $3-(-1)=3+1=4$. The value $0$ comes from evaluating $f(3-1)=f(2)$, but the function evaluated at a difference need not equal the difference of its values.
