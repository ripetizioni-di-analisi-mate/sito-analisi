Are the two lines in the figure parallel, perpendicular, or neither?

::grafico{f="2*x-1;-x/2+3" xmin=-3 xmax=5 ymin=-3 ymax=5 uguale=1 punti="0,-1,pieno;1,1,pieno;0,3,pieno;2,2,pieno" legenda=0}

- Perpendicular
- Parallel
- Coincident
- Neither parallel nor perpendicular

## Explanation

The first line passes through $(0,-1)$ and $(1,1)$, so its slope is $2$. The second passes through $(0,3)$ and $(2,2)$, so its slope is $-\dfrac{1}{2}$. The product is $2\cdot\left(-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)=-1$, so the lines are perpendicular.
