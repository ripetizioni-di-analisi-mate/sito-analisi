A line passes through $A(-2,5)$ and $B(4,-1)$. What is its equation?

- $y=-x+3$
- $y=-x+7$
- $y=x+7$
- $y=x+3$

## Explanation

The slope is $m=\dfrac{-1-5}{4-(-2)}=\dfrac{-6}{6}=-1$. Using the point $A$ gives $y-5=-(x+2)$, or $y=-x-2+5=-x+3$. Point $B$ satisfies the equation because $-4+3=-1$. The slope $1$ results from reversing the order in just one of the two subtractions. This would give a rising line, whereas the line falls from $A$ to $B$.
