For which values of $k$ is point $(3,k)$ at distance $5$ from the origin?

- Only $k=4$
- $k=16$ or $k=-16$
- $k=4$ or $k=-4$
- $k=2$ or $k=-2$

## Explanation

The distance from the origin is $\sqrt{3^2+k^2}$, so set $\sqrt{9+k^2}=5$. Squaring gives $9+k^2=25$, or $k^2=16$. This equation has two solutions, $k=4$ and $k=-4$, because the points $(3,4)$ and $(3,-4)$ are both at distance $5$ from $O$.
