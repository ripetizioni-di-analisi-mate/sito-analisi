Given the function $f(x)=3x-5$, what is $f(-2)$?

- $-1$
- $1$
- $-11$
- $-7$

## Explanation

Substitute $x=-2$ into the rule for the function. This gives $f(-2)=3\cdot(-2)-5=-6-5=-11$. The value $-1$ comes from adding $5$ instead of subtracting it, and $1$ comes from evaluating $3\cdot 2-5$ after dropping the sign of the abscissa.
