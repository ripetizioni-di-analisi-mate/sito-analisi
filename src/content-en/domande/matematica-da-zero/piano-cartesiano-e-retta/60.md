A line with slope $4$ passes through $P(1,-2)$. What is its equation?

- $y=4x-2$
- $y=4x-6$
- $y=4x+2$
- $y=4x+6$

## Explanation

Use the formula $y-y_0=m(x-x_0)$ with $x_0=1$ and $y_0=-2$. This gives $y+2=4(x-1)$, or $y+2=4x-4$, and therefore $y=4x-6$. Checking at $P$ gives $4-6=-2$. The other equations result from a sign error when applying the formula, for example from $y-2=4(x-1)$.
