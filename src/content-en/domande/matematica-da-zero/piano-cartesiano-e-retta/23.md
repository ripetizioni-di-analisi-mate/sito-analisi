A function is defined by $f(x)=x^2-3$. What is its value at $x=-2$?

- $-7$
- $1$
- $7$
- $-5$

## Explanation

Substitute $x=-2$ and evaluate the power first. The square of $-2$ is $(-2)^2=4$ because a negative number raised to an even power is positive. Therefore, $f(-2)=4-3=1$. The value $-7$ comes from writing $(-2)^2=-4$.
