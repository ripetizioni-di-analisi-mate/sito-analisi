Which line passes through $A(1,2)$ and crosses the $y$-axis at $(0,-1)$?

- $y=2x-1$
- $y=3x-1$
- $y=x-1$
- $y=3x+1$

## Explanation

The y-intercept is $q=-1$. The slope is $m=\dfrac{2-(-1)}{1-0}=3$. The line is $y=3x-1$, and at $x=1$ it gives $y=2$. The line $y=2x-1$ does not pass through $A$ because at $x=1$ it gives $y=1$.
