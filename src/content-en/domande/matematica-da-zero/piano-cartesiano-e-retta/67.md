A line passes through $(0,-1)$ and $(1,2)$. The vertical axis is moved to the old line $x=2$, without rotating the axes or changing heights. What is the height of the intersection with the new vertical axis?

- $-1$
- $5$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

Each one-unit increase in the horizontal coordinate raises the height by $2-(-1)=3$. At the old horizontal coordinate $2$, the height is therefore $2+3=5$. Moving the vertical axis leaves this height unchanged.
