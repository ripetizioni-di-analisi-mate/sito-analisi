What is the slope of the line through $A(-5,2)$ and $B(1,-7)$?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{9}{4}$

## Explanation

For two points with different $x$-coordinates, the slope of the line joining them is $m = \dfrac{y_B - y_A}{x_B - x_A}$. The difference of the $y$-coordinates is $-7 - 2 = -9$ and the difference of the $x$-coordinates is $1 - (-5) = 6$. Therefore $m = \dfrac{-9}{6} = -\dfrac{3}{2}$. The value $-\dfrac{2}{3}$ comes from swapping the two differences in the fraction, and the value $\dfrac{9}{4}$ comes from computing $1 - 5 = -4$ instead of $1 - (-5)$.
