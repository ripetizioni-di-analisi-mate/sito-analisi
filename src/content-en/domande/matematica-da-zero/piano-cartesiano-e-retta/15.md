A segment has endpoints $A(-3,6)$ and $B(5,-2)$. What are the coordinates of its midpoint?

- $(2,4)$
- $(1,2)$
- $(4,-4)$
- $(-1,2)$

## Explanation

The coordinates of the midpoint are the averages of the corresponding endpoint coordinates. The abscissa is $\dfrac{-3+5}{2}=1$ and the ordinate is $\dfrac{6+(-2)}{2}=2$. The point $(2,4)$ results from adding the coordinates without dividing by $2$.
