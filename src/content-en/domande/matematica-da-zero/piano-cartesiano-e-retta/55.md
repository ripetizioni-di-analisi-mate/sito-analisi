For what value of $b$ does the equation $2x+by-6=0$ represent a vertical line?

- $b=2$
- $b=6$
- $b=-6$
- $b=0$

## Explanation

For $b=0$, the equation becomes $2x-6=0$, or $x=3$, which is a vertical line. If $b\neq 0$, we can solve for $y$ and the line has a defined slope, so it is not vertical. For example, $b=2$ gives $y=-x+3$, a line with slope $-1$.
