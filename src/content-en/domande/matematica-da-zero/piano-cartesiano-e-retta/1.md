Which of the following points lies in quadrant III?

- $(4,-2)$
- $(-4,-2)$
- $(-4,2)$
- $(0,-2)$

## Explanation

In quadrant III, both the abscissa and the ordinate are negative. The point $(-4,-2)$ satisfies $x<0$ and $y<0$. The point $(4,-2)$ has a positive abscissa and lies in quadrant IV. The point $(-4,2)$ has a positive ordinate and lies in quadrant II, while $(0,-2)$ lies on the $y$-axis and belongs to no quadrant.
