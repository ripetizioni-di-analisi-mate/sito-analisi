A function takes the values $y=1,\ 3,\ 5,\ 7$ for $x=0,\ 1,\ 2,\ 3$. Which rule describes it?

- $y=2x$
- $y=x+1$
- $y=2x+1$
- $y=3x+1$

## Explanation

For $x=0$, the rule must give $y=1$, and whenever $1$ increases by $x$, the value of $y$ increases by $2$. Therefore, $y=2x+1$. To check, take $x=3$, for which $2\cdot 3+1=7$. The rule $y=2x$ gives $0$ for $x=0$, and the rule $y=x+1$ gives $4$ for $x=3$.
