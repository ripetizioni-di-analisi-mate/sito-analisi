On which axis does the point $(-6,0)$ lie?

- On the $x$-axis
- On the $y$-axis
- At the origin
- In quadrant II

## Explanation

To reach a point on the $x$-axis from $O$, move horizontally only, so its ordinate is $0$. The point $(-6,0)$ has ordinate $0$ and abscissa $-6$, so it lies on the $x$-axis, $6$ units to the left of $O$. At the origin, the abscissa would also equal $0$.
