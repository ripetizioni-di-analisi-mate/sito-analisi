What are the coordinates of point $P$ in the figure?

::grafico{xmin=-5 xmax=5 ymin=-4 ymax=4 uguale=1 punti="3,-2,pieno,P;-4,1,pieno,Q" legenda=0}

- $(-2,3)$
- $(3,-2)$
- $(3,2)$
- $(-3,-2)$

## Explanation

From $P$, move vertically down to the $x$-axis to read the abscissa $3$. Then move horizontally to the $y$-axis to read the ordinate $-2$. The abscissa is written first and the ordinate second, so $P(3,-2)$. The pair $(-2,3)$ represents a different point.
