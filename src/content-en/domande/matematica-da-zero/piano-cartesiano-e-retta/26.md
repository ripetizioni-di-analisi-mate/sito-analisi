A taxi charges a fixed fare of $3$ euros plus $2$ euros per kilometer. How much does a $7$ km ride cost?

- $14$ euros
- $21$ euros
- $17$ euros
- $35$ euros

## Explanation

The cost $y$ for $x$ kilometers is given by $y=2x+3$. For $x=7$, we have $y=2\cdot 7+3=14+3=17$. The value $14$ comes from omitting the fixed fare, and $35$ comes from multiplying $7$ by $2+3$.
