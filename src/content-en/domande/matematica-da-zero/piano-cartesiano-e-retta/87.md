If $\begin{cases} \dfrac{x}{2}+y=5 \\ x-2y=2 \end{cases}$, what is $x+y$?

- $6$
- $12$
- $10$
- $8$

## Explanation

Multiplying the first equation by $2$ gives $x+2y=10$. Adding it to the second gives $2x=12$, and therefore $x=6$. From $6-2y=2$ we obtain $y=2$. The sum is $6+2=8$. The value $6$ is just $x$, and $12$ is the product $x\cdot y$.
