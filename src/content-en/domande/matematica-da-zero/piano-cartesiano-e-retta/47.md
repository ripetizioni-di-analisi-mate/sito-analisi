Which of the following lines falls from left to right and crosses the $y$-axis below the origin?

- $y=2x-1$
- $y=-2x+1$
- $y=-2x-1$
- $y=2x+1$

## Explanation

A line falls when $m<0$ and crosses the $y$-axis below the origin when $q<0$. We therefore require $m<0$ and $q<0$, as in $y=-2x-1$ with $m=-2$ and $q=-1$. The line $y=2x-1$ has $q<0$ but rises, whereas $y=-2x+1$ falls but crosses the $y$-axis above the origin.
