At what points does the line $3x+4y-12=0$ cross the axes?

- $(4,0)$ and $(0,3)$
- $(3,0)$ and $(0,4)$
- $(-4,0)$ and $(0,-3)$
- $(12,0)$ and $(0,12)$

## Explanation

On the $x$-axis, set $y=0$ to obtain $3x=12$, so $x=4$. On the $y$-axis, set $x=0$ to obtain $4y=12$, so $y=3$. The points are $(4,0)$ and $(0,3)$. Solving for the wrong unknown interchanges the coefficients $3$ and $4$.
