A line rises by $2$ for each step of $1$ to the right and passes through $A(3,4)$. What is its y-intercept?

- $2$
- $10$
- $-2$
- $-6$

## Explanation

The slope is $m=2$, so the line is $y=2x+q$. Since it passes through $A$, we have $4=2\cdot 3+q$, or $q=4-6=-2$. The line is $y=2x-2$. The value $10$ comes from adding $4$ and $6$ instead of subtracting them.
