On the line $y=-4x+1$, how does $y$ change when $x$ increases by $3$?

- It increases by $12$
- It decreases by $4$
- It decreases by $11$
- It decreases by $12$

## Explanation

If $x$ changes from $x_0$ to $x_0+3$, the change in $y$ is $m\cdot 3=-4\cdot 3=-12$. The slope is negative, so $y$ decreases by $12$. The term $+1$ does not affect the change because it appears in both values and cancels in their difference.
