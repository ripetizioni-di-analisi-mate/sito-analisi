A gym charges a sign-up fee of $15$ euros and $20$ euros per month. How many months can you attend for $95$ euros?

- $5$
- $4$
- $6$
- $3$

## Explanation

The cost for $x$ months is $y=20x+15$. Set $20x+15=95$, or $20x=80$, so $x=4$. A membership lasting $4$ months costs $80+15=95$ euros. Dividing $95$ by $20$ without subtracting the sign-up fee would give the incorrect value $4.75$.
