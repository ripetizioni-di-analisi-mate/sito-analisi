What is the equation of the line through the origin and the point $(4,-8)$?

- $y=2x$
- $y=-\dfrac{1}{2}x$
- $y=-2x$
- $y=-8x$

## Explanation

A line through the origin has equation $y=mx$, and at every point on it other than $O$, we have $m=\dfrac{y}{x}$. For the point $(4,-8)$, this gives $m=\dfrac{-8}{4}=-2$. The equation is $y=-2x$, which for $x=4$ gives exactly $y=-8$.
