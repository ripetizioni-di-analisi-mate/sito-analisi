A car travels along a highway at constant speed. At time $t=0$ h, the odometer reads $20$ kilometers, and after $2$ h it reads $140$ kilometers. What does it read after $3$ h?

- $180$ kilometers
- $160$ kilometers
- $260$ kilometers
- $200$ kilometers

## Explanation

The two points $(0,20)$ and $(2,140)$ determine a line with slope $\dfrac{140-20}{2-0}=60$, that is, $60$ kilometers per hour. The line is $y=60t+20$, so at $t=3$ we have $y=180+20=200$. The value $180$ omits the initial odometer reading.
