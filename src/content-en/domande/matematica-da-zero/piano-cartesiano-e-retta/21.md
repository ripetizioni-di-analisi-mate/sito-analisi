In the figure, the dashed segments are parallel to the axes. What is the length of segment $AB$?

::grafico{xmin=-5 xmax=3 ymin=-1 ymax=6 uguale=1 linee="-3,1 1,4" punti="-3,1,pieno,A;1,4,pieno,B" segmenti="-3,1,1,1;1,1,1,4" legenda=0}

- $7$
- $25$
- $3$
- $5$

## Explanation

The legs of the right triangle have lengths $1-(-3)=4$ and $4-1=3$. By the Pythagorean theorem, $\overline{AB}=\sqrt{4^2+3^2}=\sqrt{25}=5$. The value $7$ is the sum of the leg lengths, and $25$ is the square of the hypotenuse length.
