Point $M(2,1)$ is the midpoint of segment $AB$, with $A(-1,4)$. What are the coordinates of $B$?

- $(3,-3)$
- $(1,5)$
- $(5,-2)$
- $(-4,7)$

## Explanation

Going from $A$ to $M$ involves a horizontal displacement of $2-(-1)=3$ and a vertical displacement of $1-4=-3$. The same displacement takes us from $M$ to $B$, so $B=(2+3,\ 1-3)=(5,-2)$. The midpoint of $A$ and $B$ is indeed $\left(\dfrac{-1+5}{2},\dfrac{4-2}{2}\right)=(2,1)$.
