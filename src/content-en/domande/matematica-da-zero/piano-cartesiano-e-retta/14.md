From $P(-1,2)$ to $Q(4,-3)$ one may follow the direct segment or move only along horizontal and vertical lines of a square grid. What is the ratio of the shortest grid distance to the direct distance?

- $2$
- $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$
- $5$
- $\sqrt{2}$

## Explanation

At least five horizontal and five vertical units are needed, so the shortest grid route has length $10$. The direct segment is the hypotenuse with legs $5$ and $5$, of length $5\sqrt 2$. The ratio is $\dfrac{10}{5\sqrt 2}=\sqrt 2$.
