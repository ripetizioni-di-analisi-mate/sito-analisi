Which of the following points is closest to the origin $O(0,0)$?

- $(0,-5)$
- $(3,3)$
- $(-2,-4)$
- $(4,1)$

## Explanation

By the Pythagorean theorem, the distance of a point $P(x,y)$ from the origin is $\overline{OP} = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$. Of two distances, the smaller one has the smaller square, so it is enough to compare the values of $x^2 + y^2$. For the four points we find $0 + 25 = 25$, $9 + 9 = 18$, $4 + 16 = 20$ and $16 + 1 = 17$. The smallest value is $17$, so the point closest to the origin is $(4,1)$, at a distance of $\sqrt{17}$. Adding the absolute values of the coordinates would give $5$ for both $(4,1)$ and $(0,-5)$, and the two points would seem to be at the same distance.
