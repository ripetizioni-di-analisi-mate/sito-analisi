What is the distance between $P(-1,2)$ and $Q(4,-3)$?

- $10$
- $50$
- $5$
- $5\sqrt{2}$

## Explanation

The differences are $4-(-1)=5$ and $-3-2=-5$. Therefore, $\overline{PQ}=\sqrt{5^2+(-5)^2}=\sqrt{50}=5\sqrt{2}$. The value $50$ is the sum of the squares, or the squared distance, and $10$ is the sum of the absolute values of the differences.
