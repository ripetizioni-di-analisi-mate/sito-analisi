Does the system $\begin{cases} y=3x+1 \\ 6x-2y=-2 \end{cases}$ have a unique solution, no solution, or infinitely many solutions?

- Infinitely many solutions
- No solution
- A unique solution
- Its only solution is $(0,0)$

## Explanation

Solving the second equation for $y$ gives $-2y=-6x-2$ and therefore $y=3x+1$. The two equations represent the same line, so every point on it is a solution and the system has infinitely many solutions. The origin is not a solution because $0\neq 1$.
