For what value of $k$ does the line $y=kx+3$ pass through $(2,11)$?

- $7$
- $5$
- $4$
- $8$

## Explanation

Substitute the coordinates of the point to obtain $11=2k+3$. This gives $2k=8$ and $k=4$. The line is $y=4x+3$, and at $x=2$ it gives $y=8+3=11$. The value $7$ comes from adding $3$ instead of subtracting it.
