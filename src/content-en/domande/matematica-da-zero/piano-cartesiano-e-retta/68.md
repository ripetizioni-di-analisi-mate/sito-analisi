What point on the $x$-axis lies on the line through $A(1,5)$ and $B(3,1)$?

- $\left(\dfrac{7}{2},0\right)$
- $(7,0)$
- $\left(-\dfrac{7}{2},0\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac{2}{7},0\right)$

## Explanation

The slope is $m=\dfrac{1-5}{3-1}=-2$. From $y-5=-2(x-1)$ we obtain $y=-2x+7$. On the $x$-axis, set $y=0$, so $-2x+7=0$ and $x=\dfrac{7}{2}$. The number $7$ is the y-intercept rather than the abscissa of the point.
