At what point do the lines $y=3x-2$ and $y=-x+6$ meet?

- $(4,10)$
- $(4,2)$
- $(1,1)$
- $(2,4)$

## Explanation

At the intersection, the two ordinates agree, so $3x-2=-x+6$. This gives $4x=8$ and $x=2$. Calculating the ordinate gives $y=-2+6=4$, and indeed $3\cdot 2-2=4$. The value $x=4$ would result from adding $x$ instead of subtracting it.
