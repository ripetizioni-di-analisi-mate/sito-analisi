The figure shows the graph of a function. At which points does the curve cross the $x$-axis?

::grafico{f="x^2-4" xmin=-4 xmax=4 ymin=-5 ymax=6 punti="-2,0,pieno;2,0,pieno;0,-4,pieno" legenda=0}

- Only at $x=2$
- At $x=-4$
- At $x=-2$ and $x=2$
- At $x=0$ and $x=4$

## Explanation

The points where the curve crosses the $x$-axis have ordinate $0$. In the figure, the curve passes through $(-2,0)$ and $(2,0)$ and has no other points with $y=0$. The point $(0,-4)$ is the intersection with the $y$-axis rather than the $x$-axis.
