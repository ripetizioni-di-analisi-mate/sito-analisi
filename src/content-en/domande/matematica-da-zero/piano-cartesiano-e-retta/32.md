Which of the following points lies on the line with equation $y=-3x$?

- $(2,6)$
- $(2,-6)$
- $(-2,-6)$
- $(-6,2)$

## Explanation

Substitute the abscissa into the equation. For $x=2$, this gives $y=-3\cdot 2=-6$, so the point $(2,-6)$ lies on the line. For $x=-2$, the result is $y=6$ rather than $-6$, while for $x=-6$, the result is $y=18$ rather than $2$.
