How long is the segment with endpoints $A(1,2)$ and $B(7,10)$?

- $10$
- $14$
- $100$
- $8$

## Explanation

The coordinate differences are $7-1=6$ and $10-2=8$. By the distance formula, $\overline{AB}=\sqrt{6^2+8^2}=\sqrt{36+64}=\sqrt{100}=10$. The value $14$ comes from adding the two differences without squaring them, and $100$ is the sum of the squares without the final square root.
