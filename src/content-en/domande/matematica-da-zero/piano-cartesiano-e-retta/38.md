The line in the figure passes through the origin and point $P$. What is its equation?

::grafico{f="-x/3" xmin=-6 xmax=6 ymin=-3 ymax=3 uguale=1 punti="3,-1,pieno,P" legenda=0}

- $y=-3x$
- $y=-\dfrac{1}{3}x$
- $y=\dfrac{1}{3}x$
- $y=3x$

## Explanation

The figure gives $P(3,-1)$. The slope is the ratio $\dfrac{y}{x}=\dfrac{-1}{3}$. The line falls, and at $x=3$ it reaches only $y=-1$, so its slope is gentle and its equation is $y=-\dfrac{1}{3}x$.
