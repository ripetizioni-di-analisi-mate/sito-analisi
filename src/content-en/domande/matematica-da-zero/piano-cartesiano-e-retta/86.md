What are the coordinates of the point where the two lines in the figure meet?

::grafico{f="2*x+1;-x+4" xmin=-2 xmax=4 ymin=-1 ymax=6 uguale=1 punti="1,3,pieno" legenda=0}

- $(1,3)$
- $(3,1)$
- $(0,1)$
- $(0,4)$

## Explanation

The first line passes through $(0,1)$ and $(1,3)$, and the second through $(0,4)$ and $(1,3)$. The point $(1,3)$ lies on both lines and is the point marked in the figure. The points $(0,1)$ and $(0,4)$ are the intersections with the $y$-axis, each on only one of the lines.
