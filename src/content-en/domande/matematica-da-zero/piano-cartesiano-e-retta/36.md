The line with equation $y=mx$ passes through quadrants I and III. What can we say about $m$?

- $m>0$
- $m<0$
- $m=0$
- $m=1$

## Explanation

The line passes through $(1,m)$. This point lies in quadrant I only if $m>0$. When $m>0$, the line rises from left to right, so for $x<0$ we have $y<0$ and the line also passes through quadrant III. The value $m=1$ is one possible case among others.
