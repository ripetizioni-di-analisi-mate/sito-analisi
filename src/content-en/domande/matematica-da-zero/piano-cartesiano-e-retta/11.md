The points $A(-4,2)$ and $B(3,2)$ have the same ordinate. How long is segment $AB$?

- $1$
- $5$
- $7$
- $14$

## Explanation

The segment is horizontal, and its length is the difference between the larger and smaller abscissas. We obtain $3-(-4)=3+4=7$. The result $1$ comes from adding $-4$ and $3$ instead of subtracting them.
