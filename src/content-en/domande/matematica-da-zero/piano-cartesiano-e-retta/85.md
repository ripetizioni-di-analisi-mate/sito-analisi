The sum of two numbers is $19$ and their difference is $5$. What is the larger number?

- $12$
- $7$
- $14$
- $5$

## Explanation

Write the system $x+y=19$ and $x-y=5$. Adding the equations gives $2x=24$, so $x=12$. The other number is $y=19-12=7$, and the difference is indeed $12-7=5$. The value $7$ is the smaller number.
