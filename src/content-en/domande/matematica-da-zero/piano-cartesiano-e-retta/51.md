The points $(-7,-5)$, $(1,-5)$, and $(4,-5)$ lie on the same line. What is its equation?

- $x=4$
- $x=-5$
- $y=4$
- $y=-5$

## Explanation

The three points have the same ordinate $-5$, so they lie on a horizontal line parallel to the $x$-axis, with equation $y=-5$. The equation $x=-5$ instead represents the vertical line consisting of points with abscissa $-5$, such as $(-5,0)$.
