What is the equation of the line with slope $-2$ that crosses the $y$-axis at $(0,5)$?

- $y=5x-2$
- $y=-2x+5$
- $y=2x+5$
- $y=-2x-5$

## Explanation

In the form $y=mx+q$, the coefficient of $x$ is $m=-2$ and the constant term is the ordinate of the point on the $y$-axis, namely $q=5$. The equation is $y=-2x+5$. The equation $y=5x-2$ has the two numbers interchanged.
