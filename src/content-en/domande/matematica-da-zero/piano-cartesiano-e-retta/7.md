Three vertices of a rectangle with sides parallel to the axes are $(1,1)$, $(5,1)$, and $(5,4)$. What are the coordinates of the fourth vertex?

- $(4,1)$
- $(1,4)$
- $(1,5)$
- $(4,5)$

## Explanation

The side from $(1,1)$ to $(5,1)$ is horizontal and the side from $(5,1)$ to $(5,4)$ is vertical. The fourth vertex is opposite $(5,1)$, so it has the abscissa of $(1,1)$, namely $1$, and the ordinate of $(5,4)$, namely $4$. The vertex is $(1,4)$, and the rectangle has base $4$ and height $3$.
