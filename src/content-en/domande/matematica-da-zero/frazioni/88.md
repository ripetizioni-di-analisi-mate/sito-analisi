A recipe for $4$ people calls for $\dfrac{3}{4}$ of a kilogram of flour. How many kilograms are needed for $6$ people, using the same proportions?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}$ kg
- $\dfrac{3}{16}$ kg
- $\dfrac{9}{8}$ kg
- $\dfrac{5}{4}$ kg

## Explanation

One person needs $\dfrac{3}{4} : 4 = \dfrac{3}{16}$ of a kilogram. For $6$ people, we need $6 \cdot \dfrac{3}{16} = \dfrac{18}{16} = \dfrac{9}{8}$ kg. The value $\dfrac{3}{2}$ comes from doubling the amount, $\dfrac{3}{16}$ kg is enough for just one person, and $\dfrac{5}{4}$ comes from adding $\dfrac{2}{4}$ of a kilogram for the two extra people.
