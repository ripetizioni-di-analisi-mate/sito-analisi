To complete the equality $\dfrac{3}{7} = \dfrac{n}{35}$ between equivalent fractions, what number should replace $n$?

- $15$
- $8$
- $5$
- $21$

## Explanation

The denominator changes from $7$ to $35$, so it has been multiplied by $35 : 7 = 5$. By the invariance property, the numerator must also be multiplied by $5$, so $n = 3 \cdot 5 = 15$. The value $n = 8$ would come from adding $5$ instead of multiplying, and $\dfrac{8}{35}$ does not equal $\dfrac{3}{7}$. The number $21$ is the numerator that would give $\dfrac{21}{35}$, which is equivalent to $\dfrac{3}{5}$.
