What fraction of $2$ kg is $500$ g?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $4$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

To compare the two measurements, we use the same unit: $2$ kg is $2000$ g. The fraction is $\dfrac{500}{2000}$, and dividing numerator and denominator by $500$ gives $\dfrac{1}{4}$. The value $\dfrac{1}{2}$ is the fraction of $1$ kg, $\dfrac{1}{5}$ comes from adding $500$ to $2000$ in the denominator, and $4$ is the ratio in reverse order, that is, $2000 : 500$.
