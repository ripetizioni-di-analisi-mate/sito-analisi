Of $\dfrac{4}{9}$, $\dfrac{4}{5}$, $\dfrac{4}{7}$, and $\dfrac{4}{11}$, which is the smallest fraction?

- $\dfrac{4}{11}$
- $\dfrac{4}{9}$
- $\dfrac{4}{5}$
- $\dfrac{4}{7}$

## Explanation

The fractions have the same numerator, so we take the same number of parts. The parts are smaller when the whole is divided into more parts, that is, when the denominator is greater. The greatest denominator is $11$, so the smallest fraction is $\dfrac{4}{11}$. The fraction $\dfrac{4}{5}$, on the other hand, is the largest because its parts are fifths.
