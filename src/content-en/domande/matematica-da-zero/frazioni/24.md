Which of these fractions is equivalent to $\dfrac{5}{9}$?

- $\dfrac{9}{13}$
- $\dfrac{10}{14}$
- $\dfrac{20}{36}$
- $\dfrac{15}{36}$

## Explanation

By the invariance property, we multiply the numerator and denominator by the same number. Multiplying by $4$ gives $\dfrac{5 \cdot 4}{9 \cdot 4} = \dfrac{20}{36}$. In the fraction $\dfrac{9}{13}$, $4$ has been added to both terms. In $\dfrac{10}{14}$, $5$ has been added, and in $\dfrac{15}{36}$, different factors, $3$ and $4$, have been used. All three operations change the value.
