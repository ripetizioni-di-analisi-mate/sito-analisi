A pitcher holds $\dfrac{3}{4}$ of a liter of water. How many glasses holding $\dfrac{1}{8}$ of a liter each can be filled with all the water in the pitcher?

- $\dfrac{3}{32}$
- $6$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{7}{8}$

## Explanation

The number of glasses is the quotient $\dfrac{3}{4} : \dfrac{1}{8}$. Dividing by a fraction is equivalent to multiplying by its reciprocal, so we get $\dfrac{3}{4} \cdot 8 = \dfrac{24}{4} = 6$ glasses. The value $\dfrac{3}{32}$ is the product of the two fractions, $\dfrac{1}{6}$ is the reciprocal of the result, and $\dfrac{7}{8}$ is the sum $\dfrac{6}{8} + \dfrac{1}{8}$.
