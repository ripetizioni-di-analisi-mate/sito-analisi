A jacket priced at $120$ euros is discounted by $\dfrac{1}{6}$ of its price. How many euros does the customer pay?

- $20$ euros
- $140$ euros
- $100$ euros
- $119$ euros

## Explanation

The discount is $\dfrac{1}{6}$ of $120$, or $120 : 6 = 20$ euros. The customer pays the price minus the discount, so the amount paid is $120 - 20 = 100$ euros. The amount $20$ euros is just the discount, $140$ euros would result from an increase rather than a discount, and $119$ euros comes from subtracting the numerator $\dfrac{1}{6}$ from the price, that is, $120 - 1$.
