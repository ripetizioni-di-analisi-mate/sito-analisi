After an increase of $\dfrac{1}{2}$ of its original price, an item costs $90$ euros. What was its price before the increase?

- $45$ euros
- $135$ euros
- $30$ euros
- $60$ euros

## Explanation

The new price is $1 + \dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{3}{2}$ of the original price. The original price is therefore $90 : \dfrac{3}{2} = 90 \cdot \dfrac{2}{3} = 60$ euros, as confirmed by $60 + 60 : 2 = 90$. The amount $45$ euros comes from subtracting $\dfrac{1}{2}$ from $90$, the amount $135$ euros from multiplying $90$ by $\dfrac{3}{2}$, and $30$ euros is the increase, or $\dfrac{1}{2}$ of $60$.
