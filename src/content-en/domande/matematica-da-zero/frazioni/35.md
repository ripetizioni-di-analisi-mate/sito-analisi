Which list places the fractions $\dfrac{1}{2}$, $\dfrac{2}{3}$, $\dfrac{3}{5}$, and $\dfrac{5}{8}$ in increasing order, from least to greatest?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$, $\dfrac{2}{3}$, $\dfrac{3}{5}$, $\dfrac{5}{8}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$, $\dfrac{5}{8}$, $\dfrac{3}{5}$, $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$, $\dfrac{5}{8}$, $\dfrac{3}{5}$, $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$, $\dfrac{3}{5}$, $\dfrac{5}{8}$, $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

The least common multiple of the denominators is $120$. The fractions become $\dfrac{60}{120}$, $\dfrac{80}{120}$, $\dfrac{72}{120}$, and $\dfrac{75}{120}$. Ordering their numerators, $60 < 72 < 75 < 80$, gives the sequence $\dfrac{1}{2}$, $\dfrac{3}{5}$, $\dfrac{5}{8}$, $\dfrac{2}{3}$. Reversing this list gives the order from greatest to least.
