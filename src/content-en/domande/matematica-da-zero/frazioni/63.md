Which of the following equalities is true?

- $\left( \dfrac{3}{5} \right)^2 = \dfrac{9}{5}$
- $\left( \dfrac{3}{5} \right)^2 = \dfrac{6}{10}$
- $\left( \dfrac{3}{5} \right)^2 = \dfrac{9}{25}$
- $\left( \dfrac{3}{5} \right)^2 = \dfrac{3}{25}$

## Explanation

The exponent $2$ applies to the entire fraction, so we square both the numerator and the denominator, obtaining $3^2 = 9$ and $5^2 = 25$. The result is $\dfrac{9}{25}$. In the equality with $\dfrac{9}{5}$, only the numerator was squared, in the one with $\dfrac{3}{25}$ only the denominator was squared, and in the one with $\dfrac{6}{10}$ the fraction was multiplied by $2$ instead of being squared.
