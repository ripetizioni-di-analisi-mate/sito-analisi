From a spool of wire $3$ meters long, $\dfrac{2}{5}$ of a meter is cut off. How many meters remain?

- $\dfrac{1}{5}$ m
- $\dfrac{17}{5}$ m
- $\dfrac{6}{5}$ m
- $\dfrac{13}{5}$ m

## Explanation

We write $3 = \dfrac{15}{5}$ to obtain the same denominator and subtract, giving $\dfrac{15}{5} - \dfrac{2}{5} = \dfrac{13}{5}$ m, or $2$ meters and $\dfrac{3}{5}$ of a meter. The value $\dfrac{1}{5}$ comes from subtracting $2$ from $3$ and keeping the denominator $5$, the value $\dfrac{17}{5}$ from adding instead of subtracting, and the value $\dfrac{6}{5}$ from multiplying $3$ by $2$ in the numerator.
