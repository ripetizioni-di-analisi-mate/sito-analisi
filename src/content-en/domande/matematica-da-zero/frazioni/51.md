Three of these four expressions represent the same number, and the fourth represents a different one. Which expression is different?

- $\dfrac{-3}{-5}$
- $\dfrac{-3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{-5}$
- $-\left(\dfrac{3}{5}\right)$

## Explanation

A fraction is negative if its numerator and denominator have different signs and positive if they have the same sign. In the fractions $\dfrac{-3}{5}$ and $\dfrac{3}{-5}$, the numerator and denominator have different signs, giving $-\dfrac{3}{5}$, and $-\left(\dfrac{3}{5}\right)$ also equals $-\dfrac{3}{5}$. The fraction $\dfrac{-3}{-5}$ has two like signs, so it equals $\dfrac{3}{5}$.
