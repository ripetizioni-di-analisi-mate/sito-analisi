Which of the following quantities is greatest?

- $\dfrac{2}{3}$ of $90$
- $\dfrac{5}{6}$ of $72$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$ of $84$
- $\dfrac{7}{8}$ of $64$

## Explanation

We calculate all four quantities. Taking $\dfrac{3}{4}$ of $84$ gives $84 : 4 \cdot 3 = 63$, $\dfrac{2}{3}$ of $90$ gives $60$, $\dfrac{5}{6}$ of $72$ gives $60$, and $\dfrac{7}{8}$ of $64$ gives $56$. The greatest quantity is $63$. The greatest fraction, $\dfrac{7}{8}$, gives the smallest quantity because it is applied to the smallest starting quantity.
