A fraction has denominator $12$ and numerator $5$. How many more twelfths are needed to make one whole?

- $5$
- $7$
- $12$
- $17$

## Explanation

One whole consists of $12$ parts of size $\dfrac{1}{12}$, and the fraction $\dfrac{5}{12}$ contains $5$ of them. Therefore $12 - 5 = 7$ more twelfths are needed. The number $5$ counts the twelfths already present, $12$ counts all the twelfths in the whole, and $17$ comes from adding instead of subtracting.
