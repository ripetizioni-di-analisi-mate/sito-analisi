A fraction equals $\dfrac{5}{6}$ in lowest terms, and its original numerator is $35$. What is its original denominator?

- $30$
- $42$
- $49$
- $11$

## Explanation

The original fraction is equivalent to $\dfrac{5}{6}$, so its numerator $35$ is obtained by multiplying $5$ by $35 : 5 = 7$. By the invariance property, we must also multiply the denominator by $7$, so $6 \cdot 7 = 42$, as confirmed by $\dfrac{35}{42} = \dfrac{5}{6}$. The value $30$ comes from multiplying $6$ by $5$, the value $49$ from multiplying $7$ by $7$, and the value $11$ from adding $5$ and $6$.
