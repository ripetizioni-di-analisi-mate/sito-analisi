Between which two consecutive integers does the fraction $\dfrac{17}{5}$ lie?

- $2$ and $3$
- $4$ and $5$
- $3$ and $4$
- $1$ and $2$

## Explanation

The division $17 : 5$ has quotient $3$ and remainder $2$ because $5 \cdot 3 = 15$ and $17 - 15 = 2$. Therefore $\dfrac{17}{5} = 3 + \dfrac{2}{5}$, and since $\dfrac{2}{5}$ is less than $1$, the number is greater than $3$ and less than $4$. With $4 \cdot 5 = 20$ we would have $\dfrac{20}{5} = 4$, which is greater than $\dfrac{17}{5}$.
