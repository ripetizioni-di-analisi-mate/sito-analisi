Marco has covered $\dfrac{3}{5}$ of a trail and still has $6$ km to go. How many kilometers long is the trail?

- $15$ km
- $9$ km
- $10$ km
- $30$ km

## Explanation

The remaining distance is $1 - \dfrac{3}{5} = \dfrac{2}{5}$ of the trail. If $\dfrac{2}{5}$ is $6$ km, one fifth is $6 : 2 = 3$ km and the entire trail is $3 \cdot 5 = 15$ km. The value $9$ km is the distance already covered, $10$ km comes from dividing $6$ by $\dfrac{3}{5}$, the fraction already covered, and $30$ km comes from multiplying $6$ by $5$.
