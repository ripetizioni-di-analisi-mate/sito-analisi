What do we get by multiplying $\dfrac{9}{14}$ by $\dfrac{7}{12}$?

- $\dfrac{54}{49}$
- $\dfrac{103}{84}$
- $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

We cross-cancel. Dividing $9$ and $12$ by $3$ gives $3$ and $4$, while dividing $7$ and $14$ by $7$ gives $1$ and $2$. The product of the numerators is $3 \cdot 1 = 3$ and the product of the denominators is $2 \cdot 4 = 8$, so the result is $\dfrac{3}{8}$. The value $\dfrac{54}{49}$ is the quotient, $\dfrac{103}{84}$ is the sum, and $\dfrac{3}{4}$ is the value of $\dfrac{9}{12}$ in lowest terms, which leaves out the factor $\dfrac{7}{14}$.
