The fraction $\dfrac{7}{9}$ of a tank’s capacity corresponds to $63$ liters. How many liters does the full tank hold?

- $49$
- $567$
- $70$
- $81$

## Explanation

We divide the given amount by the numerator, giving $63 : 7 = 9$ liters per ninth. The whole consists of $9$ parts of $9$ liters each, so the capacity is $9 \cdot 9 = 81$ liters. The value $49$ is $\dfrac{7}{9}$ of $63$, the value $567$ comes from multiplying $63$ by $9$ without dividing by $7$, and $70$ comes from adding $7$ to $63$.
