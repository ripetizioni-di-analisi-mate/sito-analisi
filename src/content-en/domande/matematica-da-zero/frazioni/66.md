A rectangle has a base of $\dfrac{5}{6}$ m and a height of $\dfrac{9}{10}$ m. What is its area in square meters?

- $\dfrac{26}{15}$ m$^2$
- $\dfrac{25}{27}$ m$^2$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$ m$^2$
- $\dfrac{7}{8}$ m$^2$

## Explanation

The area is the product of the base and the height, $\dfrac{5}{6} \cdot \dfrac{9}{10}$. We simplify $5$ with $10$ by dividing by $5$, and $9$ with $6$ by dividing by $3$, giving $\dfrac{1 \cdot 3}{2 \cdot 2} = \dfrac{3}{4}$ m$^2$. The value $\dfrac{26}{15}$ is the sum of the lengths, $\dfrac{25}{27}$ is their quotient, and $\dfrac{7}{8}$ comes from adding numerators and denominators separately.
