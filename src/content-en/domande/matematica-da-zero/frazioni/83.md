## Explanation

Point $P$ lies to the left of $0$, so the number is negative. Its distance from $0$ is $3$ tick intervals and each interval measures $\dfrac{1}{4}$, so $P$ represents $-\dfrac{3}{4}$. The number $\dfrac{3}{4}$ lies to the right of $0$, $-\dfrac{1}{4}$ is the first tick mark to the left, and $-\dfrac{4}{3}$ is less than $-1$.
