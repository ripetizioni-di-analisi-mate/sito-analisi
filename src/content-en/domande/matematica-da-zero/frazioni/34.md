Only one of the following inequalities is true. Which one?

- $\dfrac{3}{8} > \dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{5}{6} > \dfrac{7}{9}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3} > \dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{4}{7} > \dfrac{3}{5}$

## Explanation

We compare the fractions using a common denominator. With $18$ we have $\dfrac{5}{6} = \dfrac{15}{18}$ and $\dfrac{7}{9} = \dfrac{14}{18}$, and $15 > 14$. The others are false because $\dfrac{3}{8}$ is less than $\dfrac{4}{8}$, $\dfrac{2}{3} = \dfrac{8}{12}$ is less than $\dfrac{3}{4} = \dfrac{9}{12}$, and $\dfrac{4}{7} = \dfrac{20}{35}$ is less than $\dfrac{3}{5} = \dfrac{21}{35}$.
