A tank is filled to $\dfrac{3}{5}$ of its capacity, and an amount equal to $\dfrac{1}{4}$ of its capacity is used. What fraction of its capacity remains?

- $\dfrac{7}{20}$
- $\dfrac{17}{20}$
- $\dfrac{2}{9}$
- $2$

## Explanation

The least common denominator is $20$, so $\dfrac{3}{5} = \dfrac{12}{20}$ and $\dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{5}{20}$. There is $\dfrac{12}{20} - \dfrac{5}{20} = \dfrac{7}{20}$ of the capacity left. The value $\dfrac{17}{20}$ comes from adding, $\dfrac{2}{9}$ from subtracting the numerators and adding the denominators, and $2$ from subtracting both the numerators and the denominators, that is, $\dfrac{2}{1}$.
