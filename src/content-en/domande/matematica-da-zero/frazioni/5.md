A plot of land is divided into equal parts. A son is given $5$ of them, which make up $\dfrac{5}{8}$ of the entire plot. Into how many equal parts is the plot divided?

- $5$
- $8$
- $3$
- $13$

## Explanation

The denominator of a fraction tells us how many equal parts the whole is divided into. In the fraction $\dfrac{5}{8}$ the denominator is $8$, so the plot is divided into $8$ parts. The number $5$ is the numerator, which counts the parts assigned to the son, while $3 = 8 - 5$ counts the remaining parts and $13$ is obtained by adding.
