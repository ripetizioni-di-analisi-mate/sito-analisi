Giulia buys $2$ kg of apples and then another $\dfrac{3}{4}$ of a kilogram. How many kilograms has she bought in all?

- $\dfrac{11}{4}$ kg
- $\dfrac{5}{4}$ kg
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$ kg
- $\dfrac{23}{4}$ kg

## Explanation

We write $2 = \dfrac{8}{4}$, so the total is $\dfrac{8}{4} + \dfrac{3}{4} = \dfrac{11}{4}$ kg, or $2$ kg and $\dfrac{3}{4}$ of a kilogram. The value $\dfrac{5}{4}$ comes from adding $2 + 3$ to the numerator, $\dfrac{3}{2}$ from multiplying $2 \cdot \dfrac{3}{4}$, and $\dfrac{23}{4}$ from writing $2$ and $3$ next to each other as though they formed the number $23$.
