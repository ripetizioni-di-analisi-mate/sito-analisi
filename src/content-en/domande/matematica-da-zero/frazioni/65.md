What is the product of the three negative numbers $-\dfrac{3}{4}$, $-\dfrac{8}{9}$, and $-\dfrac{1}{2}$?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

The product of three negative numbers is negative because there is an odd number of negative factors. The product of their absolute values is $\dfrac{3 \cdot 8 \cdot 1}{4 \cdot 9 \cdot 2} = \dfrac{24}{72} = \dfrac{1}{3}$, so the result is $-\dfrac{1}{3}$. The value $\dfrac{1}{3}$ has the wrong sign, $\dfrac{2}{3}$ is the product of the first two factors, and $-\dfrac{1}{6}$ uses the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$ twice.
