What is the least common denominator of the fractions $\dfrac{5}{12}$ and $\dfrac{7}{18}$?

- $6$
- $36$
- $216$
- $30$

## Explanation

The least common denominator is the least common multiple of the denominators. We have $12 = 2^2 \cdot 3$ and $18 = 2 \cdot 3^2$, so we take the factors with their greatest exponents and get $2^2 \cdot 3^2 = 36$. The number $6$ is the greatest common divisor, $216 = 12 \cdot 18$ is a common multiple larger than the least one, and $30$ is the sum of the denominators.
