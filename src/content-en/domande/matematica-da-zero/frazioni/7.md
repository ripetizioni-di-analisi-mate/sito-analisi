A break lasts $20$ minutes. What fraction of an hour does it last?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $20$

## Explanation

An hour lasts $60$ minutes, and $20$ minutes is $20$ parts out of $60$, that is, $\dfrac{20}{60}$. Dividing numerator and denominator by $20$ gives $\dfrac{1}{3}$. Indeed, $3$ breaks of $20$ minutes each fill an hour. The fraction $\dfrac{1}{2}$ would be half an hour, or $30$ minutes, and $\dfrac{2}{3}$ would be $40$ minutes.
