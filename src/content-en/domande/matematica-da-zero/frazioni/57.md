Marta buys $3$ packages of rice, each weighing $\dfrac{4}{9}$ of a kilogram. How many kilograms of rice does she buy in all?

- $\dfrac{4}{27}$ kg
- $\dfrac{4}{9}$ kg
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$ kg
- $\dfrac{7}{9}$ kg

## Explanation

We multiply $3$ by $\dfrac{4}{9}$, writing $3 = \dfrac{3}{1}$. We have $\dfrac{3}{1} \cdot \dfrac{4}{9} = \dfrac{12}{9}$, and simplifying by $3$ gives $\dfrac{4}{3}$ kg. The value $\dfrac{4}{27}$ comes from also multiplying the denominator by $3$, $\dfrac{4}{9}$ is the weight of a single package, and $\dfrac{7}{9}$ comes from adding $3$ to the numerator.
