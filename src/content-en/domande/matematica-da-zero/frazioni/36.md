Which fraction lies between $\dfrac{1}{3}$ and $\dfrac{1}{2}$?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{10}$

## Explanation

With the common denominator $60$ we have $\dfrac{1}{3} = \dfrac{20}{60}$ and $\dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{30}{60}$. Fractions between them have numerators between $20$ and $30$. The fraction $\dfrac{2}{5}$ equals $\dfrac{24}{60}$, so it lies between the two. The fractions $\dfrac{1}{4}$ and $\dfrac{3}{10}$ equal $\dfrac{15}{60}$ and $\dfrac{18}{60}$, both less than $\dfrac{20}{60}$, while $\dfrac{3}{5}$ equals $\dfrac{36}{60}$, which is greater than $\dfrac{30}{60}$.
