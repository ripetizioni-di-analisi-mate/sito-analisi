A package contains $24$ cookies, and $9$ of them are eaten. What fraction of the package remains?

- $\dfrac{15}{24}$
- $\dfrac{9}{24}$
- $\dfrac{15}{9}$
- $\dfrac{24}{15}$

## Explanation

There are $24 - 9 = 15$ cookies left out of $24$. The numerator counts the remaining parts, while the denominator counts all the parts. The fraction is therefore $\dfrac{15}{24}$. The fraction $\dfrac{9}{24}$ is the part eaten, while $\dfrac{15}{9}$ and $\dfrac{24}{15}$ compare numbers of cookies that do not make up a whole package.
