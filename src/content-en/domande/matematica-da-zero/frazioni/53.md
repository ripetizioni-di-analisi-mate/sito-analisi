Which number is halfway between $\dfrac{1}{4}$ and $\dfrac{1}{2}$ on the number line?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{16}$

## Explanation

The point halfway between two numbers is half their sum. With denominator $4$, we have $\dfrac{1}{4} + \dfrac{2}{4} = \dfrac{3}{4}$, and half of $\dfrac{3}{4}$ is $\dfrac{3}{4} \cdot \dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{3}{8}$. Indeed, $\dfrac{3}{8} - \dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{1}{8}$ and $\dfrac{1}{2} - \dfrac{3}{8} = \dfrac{1}{8}$. The value $\dfrac{3}{4}$ is the sum without halving it, $\dfrac{3}{16}$ comes from dividing the sum by $4$ instead of $2$, and $\dfrac{1}{3}$ comes from adding numerators and denominators separately.
