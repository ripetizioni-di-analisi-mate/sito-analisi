The strip is $40$ cm long and is divided into $8$ equal parts. How long is the shaded section?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="0,0 3,0 3,1 0,1" linee="0,0 8,0 8,1 0,1 0,0;1,0 1,1;2,0 2,1;3,0 3,1;4,0 4,1;5,0 5,1;6,0 6,1;7,0 7,1" etichette="4,-0.5,40 cm long in all" xmin=-0.3 xmax=8.3 ymin=-0.9 ymax=1.3}

- $5$ cm
- $25$ cm
- $24$ cm
- $15$ cm

## Explanation

Each part is $40 : 8 = 5$ cm long. There are $3$ shaded parts, so the shaded section is $5 \cdot 3 = 15$ cm long, which is $\dfrac{3}{8}$ of $40$ cm. The value $5$ cm is the length of a single part, $25$ cm is the length of the $5$ white parts, and $24$ comes from multiplying $3$ by $8$.
