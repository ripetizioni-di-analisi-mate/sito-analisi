Which fraction in lowest terms is equal to $-\dfrac{12}{18}$?

- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $-\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $-\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{4}{9}$

## Explanation

The greatest common divisor of $12$ and $18$ is $6$. Dividing the numerator $-12$ by $6$ gives $-2$, and dividing the denominator $18$ by $6$ gives $3$, so the fraction is $-\dfrac{2}{3}$. The negative sign remains because dividing by a positive number does not change it. The value $-\dfrac{3}{2}$ would come from interchanging the terms.
