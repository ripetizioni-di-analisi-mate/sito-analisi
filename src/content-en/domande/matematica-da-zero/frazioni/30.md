A fraction equivalent to $\dfrac35$ is to be written with a whole-number denominator between $16$ and $34$, inclusive. How many different denominators are possible?

- $2$
- $3$
- $4$
- $6$

## Explanation

Since $3$ and $5$ are coprime, every possible denominator is a multiple of $5$. The interval contains $20$, $25$ and $30$. The corresponding numerators are $12$, $15$ and $18$.
