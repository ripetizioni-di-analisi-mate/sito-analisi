Marco ate $\dfrac{3}{8}$ of a cake and Sara ate $\dfrac{2}{5}$. Who ate more, and by how much?

- Marco, by $\dfrac{1}{40}$
- Sara, by $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- Marco, by $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- Sara, by $\dfrac{1}{40}$

## Explanation

The common denominator is $40$, so $\dfrac{3}{8} = \dfrac{15}{40}$ and $\dfrac{2}{5} = \dfrac{16}{40}$. Since $16 > 15$, Sara ate more, and the difference is $\dfrac{16}{40} - \dfrac{15}{40} = \dfrac{1}{40}$ of the cake. The value $\dfrac{1}{3}$ would come from subtracting the denominators, $8 - 5 = 3$, and Marco ate a smaller portion.
