The rectangle is divided into equal parts, some of which are shaded. What fraction of the rectangle is shaded?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="0,0 2,0 2,1.5 0,1.5;5,0 6,0 6,1.5 5,1.5" linee="0,0 8,0 8,1.5 0,1.5 0,0;1,0 1,1.5;2,0 2,1.5;3,0 3,1.5;4,0 4,1.5;5,0 5,1.5;6,0 6,1.5;7,0 7,1.5" xmin=-0.3 xmax=8.3 ymin=-0.3 ymax=1.8}

- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{5}{8}$
- $\dfrac{8}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{8}$

## Explanation

The rectangle is divided into $8$ equal parts, so the denominator is $8$. There are $3$ shaded parts, even though they are not next to each other, so the numerator is $3$ and the shaded area is $\dfrac{3}{8}$ of the rectangle. The fraction $\dfrac{3}{5}$ compares the shaded parts with the white parts, and $\dfrac{5}{8}$ is the white portion.
