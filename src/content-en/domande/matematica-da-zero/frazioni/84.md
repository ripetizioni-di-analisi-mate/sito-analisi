What is the value of the expression $\left( \dfrac{1}{2} - \dfrac{1}{3} \right) : \dfrac{1}{4} + \dfrac{1}{3}$?

- $1$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2}{7}$
- $\dfrac{3}{8}$

## Explanation

First we evaluate the difference in parentheses, giving $\dfrac{3}{6} - \dfrac{2}{6} = \dfrac{1}{6}$. Division then comes before addition, so $\dfrac{1}{6} : \dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{1}{6} \cdot 4 = \dfrac{2}{3}$, and finally $\dfrac{2}{3} + \dfrac{1}{3} = 1$. The value $\dfrac{2}{3}$ comes from omitting the last addition, while $\dfrac{2}{7}$ divides by the sum $\dfrac{1}{4} + \dfrac{1}{3}$ and $\dfrac{3}{8}$ multiplies instead of dividing.
