A board is $\dfrac{11}{15}$ of a meter long, and $\dfrac{4}{15}$ of a meter is cut off. How long is it after the cut?

- $\dfrac{7}{15}$ m
- $\dfrac{7}{30}$ m
- $\dfrac{4}{11}$ m
- $\dfrac{15}{7}$ m

## Explanation

The two values have the same denominator, so we subtract the numerators and keep the denominator $15$, giving $11 - 4 = 7$. The board measures $\dfrac{7}{15}$ m. The value $\dfrac{7}{30}$ comes from also adding the denominators, which instead indicate the size of the parts and stay the same, while $\dfrac{4}{11}$ and $\dfrac{15}{7}$ interchange the terms.
