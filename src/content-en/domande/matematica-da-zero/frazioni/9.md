How many copies of the unit fraction $\dfrac{1}{7}$ make up the fraction $\dfrac{5}{7}$?

- $7$
- $5$
- $2$
- $12$

## Explanation

A unit fraction has numerator $1$. The numerator of $\dfrac{5}{7}$ tells us how many parts are taken, and each part is $\dfrac{1}{7}$, so the fraction consists of $5$ unit fractions. The number $7$ tells us how many parts make up the whole, $2 = 7 - 5$ counts the missing parts, and $12$ is the sum $5 + 7$.
