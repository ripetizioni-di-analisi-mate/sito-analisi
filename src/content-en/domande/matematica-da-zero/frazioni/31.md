## Explanation

The top strip is divided into $4$ parts, of which $3$ are shaded, so the shaded fraction is $\dfrac{3}{4}$. In the bottom strip each part has been cut in half, so there are $8$ parts, of which $6$ are shaded, giving $\dfrac{6}{8}$. The value of the fraction stays the same.
