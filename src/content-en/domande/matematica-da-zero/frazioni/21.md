Which of the following fractions lies between $2$ and $3$ on the number line?

- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $\dfrac{7}{3}$

## Explanation

A number lies between $2$ and $3$ if it is greater than $\dfrac{6}{3} = 2$ and less than $\dfrac{9}{3} = 3$. With denominator $3$, the numerator must therefore lie between $6$ and $9$, and $7$ lies between them. The fraction $\dfrac{7}{3}$ equals $2 + \dfrac{1}{3}$. The fractions $\dfrac{5}{3}$ and $\dfrac{4}{3}$ lie between $1$ and $2$, while $\dfrac{9}{3}$ equals $3$.
