Which of the following decimals is greater than $\dfrac{3}{5}$?

- $0.6$
- $0.7$
- $0.55$
- $0.5$

## Explanation

We write the fraction with denominator $10$, giving $\dfrac{3}{5} = \dfrac{6}{10} = 0.6$. The number $0.7$ is greater than $0.6$ because it has $7$ tenths compared with $6$. The number $0.6$ equals the fraction, so it is not greater, while $0.55$ and $0.5$ are less than $0.6$.
