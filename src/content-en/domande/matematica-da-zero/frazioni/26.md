The numerator and denominator of $\dfrac{60}{84}$ are divided by their greatest common divisor. What fraction does this give?

- $\dfrac{5}{7}$
- $\dfrac{5}{14}$
- $\dfrac{10}{7}$
- $\dfrac{15}{7}$

## Explanation

We have $60 = 2^2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5$ and $84 = 2^2 \cdot 3 \cdot 7$, so the greatest common divisor is $2^2 \cdot 3 = 12$. Dividing gives $60 : 12 = 5$ and $84 : 12 = 7$, so the fraction is $\dfrac{5}{7}$. The other fractions come from dividing numerator and denominator by different numbers, for example $60 : 6 = 10$ and $84 : 12 = 7$, and their values differ from the value of the original fraction.
