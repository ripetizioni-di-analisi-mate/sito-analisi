A strip half as long as a sheet of paper is cut into three equal pieces. What fraction of the full sheet's length do two pieces cover?

- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac16$
- $\dfrac34$

## Explanation

Each piece is one third of half the sheet's length, so its length is one sixth of the full length. Two pieces measure two sixths, equivalent to one third.
