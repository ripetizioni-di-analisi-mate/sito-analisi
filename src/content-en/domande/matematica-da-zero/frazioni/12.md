How many proper fractions are in the list $\dfrac{7}{9}$, $\dfrac{12}{6}$, $\dfrac{11}{5}$, $\dfrac{4}{13}$?

- $1$
- $3$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

A fraction is proper if its numerator is less than its denominator. For $\dfrac{7}{9}$ we have $7 < 9$, and for $\dfrac{4}{13}$ we have $4 < 13$, so both are proper. The fractions $\dfrac{12}{6}$ and $\dfrac{11}{5}$ have numerators greater than their denominators, so they are improper, and $\dfrac{12}{6}$ also represents an integer because it equals $2$.
