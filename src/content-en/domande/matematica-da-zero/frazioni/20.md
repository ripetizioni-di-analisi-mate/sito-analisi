The numerator of a fraction is twice its denominator, which is nonzero. What is the value of the fraction?

- $2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $1$
- $4$

## Explanation

If the denominator is $b$, the numerator is $2b$ and the fraction is $\dfrac{2b}{b}$. For $b \neq 0$ the division $2b : b$ gives $2$, whatever the value of $b$. For example, $\dfrac{10}{5} = 2$ and $\dfrac{18}{9} = 2$. The value $\dfrac{1}{2}$ would come from interchanging numerator and denominator.
