What is the quotient of $-\dfrac{5}{6}$ and $-\dfrac{10}{9}$?

- $-\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $\dfrac{25}{27}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

The quotient of two negative numbers is positive. Dividing by $-\dfrac{10}{9}$ is equivalent to multiplying by the reciprocal $-\dfrac{9}{10}$, so $\left( -\dfrac{5}{6} \right) \cdot \left( -\dfrac{9}{10} \right) = \dfrac{45}{60} = \dfrac{3}{4}$. The value $-\dfrac{3}{4}$ has the wrong sign, $\dfrac{4}{3}$ is the reciprocal of the result, and $\dfrac{25}{27}$ is the product of the two given fractions.
