Of the $50$ euros in a cash box, $\dfrac{3}{5}$ is taken out, and $\dfrac{2}{3}$ of that amount is given away. How many euros are given away?

- $20$ euros
- $30$ euros
- $\dfrac{100}{3}$ euros
- $25$ euros

## Explanation

Taking $\dfrac{3}{5}$ of $50$ euros gives $50 : 5 \cdot 3 = 30$ euros. Taking $\dfrac{2}{3}$ of $30$ euros gives $30 : 3 \cdot 2 = 20$ euros. Using fractions, we get $\dfrac{2}{3} \cdot \dfrac{3}{5} = \dfrac{2}{5}$, and $\dfrac{2}{5}$ of $50$ is $20$. The value $30$ euros is the amount taken out, $\dfrac{100}{3}$ euros is $\dfrac{2}{3}$ of $50$, and $25$ euros is half the money in the cash box.
