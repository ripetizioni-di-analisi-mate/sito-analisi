A bakery has $52$ rolls and packs them in bags of $8$ rolls each. How many full bags are there, and how many rolls are left over?

- $7$ bags and $4$ rolls
- $6$ bags and $2$ rolls
- $4$ bags and $6$ rolls
- $6$ bags and $4$ rolls

## Explanation

We perform the division with remainder $52 : 8$. Since $8 \cdot 6 = 48$ and $8 \cdot 7 = 56$ exceeds $52$, the quotient is $6$ and the remainder is $52 - 48 = 4$. Therefore $6$ bags are filled and $4$ rolls are left over, as confirmed by $\dfrac{52}{8} = 6 + \dfrac{4}{8}$. Filling $7$ bags would require $56$ rolls.
