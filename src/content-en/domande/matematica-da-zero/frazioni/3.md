Three identical pizzas are shared equally among five friends. What fraction of a pizza does each friend get?

- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $15$

## Explanation

Sharing $3$ pizzas among $5$ people means calculating $3 : 5$. Writing the division as a fraction shows that each friend gets $\dfrac{3}{5}$ of a pizza. The fraction $\dfrac{5}{3}$ would mean sharing $5$ pizzas among $3$ people. The value $\dfrac{1}{5}$ is a share of just one pizza, while $15$ is the product $3 \cdot 5$.
