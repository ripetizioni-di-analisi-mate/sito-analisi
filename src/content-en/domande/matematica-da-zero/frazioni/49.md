What do we get by adding $\dfrac{3}{4}$ to $-\dfrac{2}{3}$?

- $-\dfrac{1}{12}$
- $\dfrac{17}{12}$
- $-\dfrac{17}{12}$
- $\dfrac{1}{12}$

## Explanation

The least common denominator is $12$, so $-\dfrac{2}{3} = -\dfrac{8}{12}$ and $\dfrac{3}{4} = \dfrac{9}{12}$. We have $-8 + 9 = 1$, so the sum is $\dfrac{1}{12}$, which is positive because the term with the greater absolute value is positive. The value $-\dfrac{1}{12}$ has the wrong sign, while $\dfrac{17}{12}$ and $-\dfrac{17}{12}$ add $8$ and $9$ without accounting for the sign.
