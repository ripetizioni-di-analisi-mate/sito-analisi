What is the result of adding the three fractions $\dfrac{1}{2}$, $\dfrac{1}{3}$, and $\dfrac{1}{6}$?

- $\dfrac{5}{6}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{3}{11}$

## Explanation

The least common denominator is $6$, so $\dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{3}{6}$, $\dfrac{1}{3} = \dfrac{2}{6}$, and $\dfrac{1}{6}$ stays the same. The sum is $\dfrac{6}{6} = 1$. The value $\dfrac{5}{6}$ comes from omitting $\dfrac{1}{6}$, and $\dfrac{2}{3} = \dfrac{4}{6}$ from omitting $\dfrac{1}{3}$, while $\dfrac{3}{11}$ adds the numerators, $1 + 1 + 1$, and the denominators, $2 + 3 + 6$.
