For what value of $x$ is the expression $\dfrac{6}{x-2}$ undefined?

- $x = 2$
- $x = 0$
- $x = 6$
- $x = -2$

## Explanation

A fraction is undefined when its denominator equals $0$. The denominator $x - 2$ equals $0$ when $x = 2$ because $2 - 2 = 0$. When $x = 0$ the denominator is $-2$ and when $x = -2$ it is $-4$, so the fraction is defined in both cases. The numerator $6$ does not affect whether the fraction is defined.
