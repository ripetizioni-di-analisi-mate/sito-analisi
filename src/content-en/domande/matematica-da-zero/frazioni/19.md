Which of the following fractions is greater than $1$?

- $\dfrac{13}{10}$
- $\dfrac{9}{10}$
- $\dfrac{10}{10}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

A fraction is greater than $1$ when its numerator is greater than its denominator. In the fraction $\dfrac{13}{10}$ we take $13$ parts of size one tenth, while one whole contains $10$, so the fraction is greater than $1$. The fraction $\dfrac{10}{10}$ equals $1$, while $\dfrac{9}{10}$ and $\dfrac{3}{4}$ are proper fractions and are less than $1$.
