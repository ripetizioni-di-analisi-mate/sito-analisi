Three ropes measure $\dfrac{5}{12}$ m, $\dfrac{1}{3}$ m, and $\dfrac{3}{8}$ m. Which rope is the longest?

- The rope measuring $\dfrac{1}{3}$ m
- The rope measuring $\dfrac{3}{8}$ m
- The rope measuring $\dfrac{5}{12}$ m
- The three ropes are the same length

## Explanation

The least common denominator is $24$. The lengths become $\dfrac{10}{24}$ m, $\dfrac{8}{24}$ m, and $\dfrac{9}{24}$ m. Since $10 > 9 > 8$, the longest rope is the one measuring $\dfrac{10}{24} = \dfrac{5}{12}$ m. The rope measuring $\dfrac{1}{3}$ m, which has the smallest denominator, is the shortest, and all three lengths are different.
