What is the product $\left( \dfrac{2}{3} \right)^2 \cdot \left( \dfrac{3}{2} \right)^2$?

- $\dfrac{4}{9}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{9}{4}$
- $\dfrac{16}{81}$

## Explanation

We evaluate the powers, which give $\left( \dfrac{2}{3} \right)^2 = \dfrac{4}{9}$ and $\left( \dfrac{3}{2} \right)^2 = \dfrac{9}{4}$. The product is $\dfrac{4}{9} \cdot \dfrac{9}{4} = 1$ because the fractions are reciprocals. The values $\dfrac{4}{9}$ and $\dfrac{9}{4}$ are the values of just one power each, and $\dfrac{16}{81}$ is the square of $\dfrac{4}{9}$.
