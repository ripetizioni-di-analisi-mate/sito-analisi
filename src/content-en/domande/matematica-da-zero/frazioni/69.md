Perform the divisions $\dfrac{3}{4} : \dfrac{1}{2} : 3$ from left to right. What is the result?

- $\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

First we calculate $\dfrac{3}{4} : \dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{3}{4} \cdot 2 = \dfrac{3}{2}$. Then we divide by $3$, giving $\dfrac{3}{2} : 3 = \dfrac{3}{2} \cdot \dfrac{1}{3} = \dfrac{1}{2}$. The value $\dfrac{3}{2}$ is the result of the first division alone, $\dfrac{1}{6}$ is $\dfrac{1}{2} : 3$, and $\dfrac{9}{2}$ comes from performing the division $\dfrac{1}{2} : 3$ first and then dividing $\dfrac{3}{4}$ by the result.
