What is the value of the quotient $4 : \dfrac{2}{5}$?

- $\dfrac{8}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{10}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $10$

## Explanation

We multiply $4$ by the reciprocal of $\dfrac{2}{5}$, which is $\dfrac{5}{2}$. We get $4 \cdot \dfrac{5}{2} = \dfrac{20}{2} = 10$. The result tells us that $4$ units contain $10$ parts of size $\dfrac{2}{5}$. The value $\dfrac{8}{5}$ is the product $4 \cdot \dfrac{2}{5}$, $\dfrac{2}{5}$ comes from dividing only $4$ by $2$ and leaving $5$ in the denominator, and $\dfrac{1}{10}$ is the reciprocal of the result.
