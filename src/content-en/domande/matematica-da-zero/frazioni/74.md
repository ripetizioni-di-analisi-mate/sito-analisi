The girls make up $\dfrac{2}{5}$ of a class, and there are $12$ girls. How many boys are there?

- $12$
- $18$
- $30$
- $24$

## Explanation

The girls make up $2$ parts out of $5$, so one part is $12 : 2 = 6$ students. The class has $5 \cdot 6 = 30$ students, and the boys make up the remaining $3$ parts, or $3 \cdot 6 = 18$ students. The number $12$ counts the girls, $30$ is the total class size, and $24$ comes from doubling $12$.
