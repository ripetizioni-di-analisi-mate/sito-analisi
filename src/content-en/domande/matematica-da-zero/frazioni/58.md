What is the reciprocal of $-\dfrac{5}{8}$?

- $-\dfrac{8}{5}$
- $\dfrac{8}{5}$
- $\dfrac{5}{8}$
- $-\dfrac{5}{8}$

## Explanation

To take the reciprocal, we interchange numerator and denominator, keeping the sign. The reciprocal of $-\dfrac{5}{8}$ is $-\dfrac{8}{5}$, as the product $\left( -\dfrac{5}{8} \right) \cdot \left( -\dfrac{8}{5} \right) = \dfrac{40}{40} = 1$ confirms. The number $\dfrac{8}{5}$ is the reciprocal of $\dfrac{5}{8}$, which in turn is the opposite of the given number, while $-\dfrac{5}{8}$ is the number itself.
