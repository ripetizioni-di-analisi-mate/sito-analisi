What is the value of $\dfrac{1}{2} - \left( -\dfrac{1}{3} \right)$?

- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $-\dfrac{5}{6}$
- $-\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{5}{6}$

## Explanation

Subtracting a negative number is equivalent to adding its opposite, so $\dfrac{1}{2} - \left( -\dfrac{1}{3} \right) = \dfrac{1}{2} + \dfrac{1}{3}$. With denominator $6$ we have $\dfrac{3}{6} + \dfrac{2}{6} = \dfrac{5}{6}$. The value $\dfrac{1}{6}$ comes from subtracting $\dfrac{1}{3}$ instead of adding it, and the negative values have the opposite of the correct sign.
