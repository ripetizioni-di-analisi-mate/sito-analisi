Which fraction with denominator $6$ has an integer value equal to $4$?

- $\dfrac{4}{6}$
- $\dfrac{6}{4}$
- $\dfrac{24}{6}$
- $\dfrac{10}{6}$

## Explanation

A fraction with an integer value represents a whole number of units. To obtain the value $4$ with denominator $6$, the numerator must be $4 \cdot 6 = 24$ because $\dfrac{24}{6} = 24 : 6 = 4$. The fraction $\dfrac{4}{6}$ is proper, $\dfrac{6}{4}$ equals $1.5$, and $\dfrac{10}{6}$ is improper but less than $2$.
