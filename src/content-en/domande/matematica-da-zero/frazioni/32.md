Of the fractions $\dfrac{4}{10}$, $\dfrac{6}{15}$, $\dfrac{10}{25}$, and $\dfrac{8}{18}$, how many are equivalent to $\dfrac{2}{5}$?

- $2$
- $3$
- $4$
- $1$

## Explanation

We reduce each fraction to lowest terms. Dividing by $2$ gives $\dfrac{4}{10} = \dfrac{2}{5}$, dividing by $3$ gives $\dfrac{6}{15} = \dfrac{2}{5}$, and dividing by $5$ gives $\dfrac{10}{25} = \dfrac{2}{5}$. Dividing the terms of $\dfrac{8}{18}$ by $2$ gives $\dfrac{4}{9}$, which differs from $\dfrac{2}{5}$. Therefore the number of equivalent fractions is $3$.
