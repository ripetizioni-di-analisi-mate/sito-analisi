We divide $\dfrac{3}{4}$ of a liter of juice equally among $6$ identical glasses. What fraction of a liter does each glass contain?

- $8$ liters
- $\dfrac{9}{2}$ liters
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$ of a liter
- $\dfrac{3}{10}$ of a liter

## Explanation

Dividing by $6$ is equivalent to multiplying by $\dfrac{1}{6}$, so each glass contains $\dfrac{3}{4} : 6 = \dfrac{3}{4} \cdot \dfrac{1}{6} = \dfrac{3}{24} = \dfrac{1}{8}$ of a liter. The value $8$ comes from dividing $6$ by $\dfrac{3}{4}$, the value $\dfrac{9}{2}$ from multiplying $\dfrac{3}{4}$ by $6$, and the value $\dfrac{3}{10}$ from adding $6$ to the denominator.
