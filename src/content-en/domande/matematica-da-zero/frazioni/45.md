Marta walks for $\dfrac{3}{4}$ of an hour and then for $\dfrac{5}{6}$ of an hour. How long does she walk in all?

- $\dfrac{4}{5}$ of an hour
- $\dfrac{7}{6}$ of an hour
- $\dfrac{5}{8}$ of an hour
- $\dfrac{19}{12}$ of an hour

## Explanation

The least common denominator is $12$, so $\dfrac{3}{4} = \dfrac{9}{12}$ and $\dfrac{5}{6} = \dfrac{10}{12}$. The sum is $\dfrac{9}{12} + \dfrac{10}{12} = \dfrac{19}{12}$ of an hour, which is more than an hour. The value $\dfrac{4}{5}$ comes from adding numerators and denominators separately, $\dfrac{5}{8}$ from multiplying the two fractions, and $\dfrac{7}{6}$ from converting $\dfrac{5}{6}$ into $\dfrac{5}{12}$.
