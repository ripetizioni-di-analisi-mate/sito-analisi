Only one of the following pairs consists of equivalent fractions. Which one?

- $\dfrac{3}{4}$ and $\dfrac{6}{9}$
- $\dfrac{5}{8}$ and $\dfrac{10}{20}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$ and $\dfrac{4}{9}$
- $\dfrac{6}{10}$ and $\dfrac{9}{15}$

## Explanation

We reduce the fractions to lowest terms. We have $\dfrac{6}{10} = \dfrac{3}{5}$ and $\dfrac{9}{15} = \dfrac{3}{5}$, so the two fractions are equivalent. In the other pairs we get $\dfrac{6}{9} = \dfrac{2}{3}$, which differs from $\dfrac{3}{4}$, then $\dfrac{10}{20} = \dfrac{1}{2}$, which differs from $\dfrac{5}{8}$, and finally $\dfrac{4}{9}$, already in lowest terms and different from $\dfrac{2}{5}$.
