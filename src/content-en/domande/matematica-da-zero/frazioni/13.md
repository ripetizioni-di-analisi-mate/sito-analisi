Which expression equals the fraction $\dfrac{31}{6}$?

- $5 + \dfrac{5}{6}$
- $5 + \dfrac{1}{6}$
- $6 + \dfrac{1}{6}$
- $1 + \dfrac{5}{6}$

## Explanation

We perform the division with remainder $31 : 6$. The quotient is $5$ because $6 \cdot 5 = 30$, and the remainder is $31 - 30 = 1$. Therefore $\dfrac{31}{6}$ consists of $5$ wholes and a fractional part $\dfrac{1}{6}$, so it equals $5 + \dfrac{1}{6}$. The other expressions use an incorrect quotient or remainder. For example, $1 + \dfrac{5}{6}$ equals $\dfrac{11}{6}$.
