Which of these fractions is improper but does not equal an integer?

- $\dfrac{8}{4}$
- $\dfrac{9}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{5}{5}$

## Explanation

An improper fraction has a numerator greater than its denominator and equals an integer if the division has no remainder. The fraction $\dfrac{9}{4}$ is improper, and $9 : 4$ has remainder $1$, so it does not equal an integer. The fraction $\dfrac{8}{4}$ equals the integer $2$, and $\dfrac{5}{5}$ equals the integer $1$, while $\dfrac{3}{4}$ is proper.
