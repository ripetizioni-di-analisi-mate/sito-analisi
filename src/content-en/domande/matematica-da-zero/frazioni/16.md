On the number line each unit is divided into fourths. What fraction does point $P$ represent?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 linee="-0.2,0 2.32,0;2.24,0.05 2.32,0 2.24,-0.05;0,-0.08 0,0.08;0.25,-0.045 0.25,0.045;0.5,-0.045 0.5,0.045;0.75,-0.045 0.75,0.045;1,-0.08 1,0.08;1.25,-0.045 1.25,0.045;1.5,-0.045 1.5,0.045;1.75,-0.045 1.75,0.045;2,-0.08 2,0.08" punti="1.25,0,pieno,P" etichette="-0.025,-0.24,0;0.975,-0.24,1;1.975,-0.24,2" xmin=-0.3 xmax=2.4 ymin=-0.35 ymax=0.3}

- $\dfrac{4}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{5}{4}$
- $\dfrac{5}{8}$

## Explanation

Between $0$ and $1$ there are $4$ parts of size $\dfrac{1}{4}$. Point $P$ lies beyond the number $1$, so its distance from $0$ is $4$ fourths plus one fourth, or $5$ fourths. The point represents $\dfrac{5}{4}$. The fraction $\dfrac{4}{5}$ is less than $1$ and would lie before $1$, while $\dfrac{1}{4}$ would be the first tick mark after $0$.
