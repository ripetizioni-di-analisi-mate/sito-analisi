Which of the following fractions is already in lowest terms?

- $\dfrac{14}{21}$
- $\dfrac{18}{45}$
- $\dfrac{15}{28}$
- $\dfrac{12}{30}$

## Explanation

A fraction is in lowest terms when the only common divisor of its numerator and denominator is $1$. We have $15 = 3 \cdot 5$ and $28 = 2^2 \cdot 7$, which have no common prime factors. The others can be simplified by dividing the terms of $\dfrac{14}{21}$ by $7$, of $\dfrac{18}{45}$ by $9$, and of $\dfrac{12}{30}$ by $6$.
