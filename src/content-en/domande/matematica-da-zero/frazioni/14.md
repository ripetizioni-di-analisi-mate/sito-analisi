A ribbon measures $3$ meters plus $\dfrac{2}{7}$ of a meter. Which improper fraction gives its length in meters?

- $\dfrac{5}{7}$
- $\dfrac{6}{7}$
- $\dfrac{23}{7}$
- $\dfrac{32}{7}$

## Explanation

One whole equals $\dfrac{7}{7}$, so $3$ wholes equal $3 \cdot 7 = 21$ sevenths. Adding the remaining $2$ sevenths gives $21 + 2 = 23$ sevenths, or $\dfrac{23}{7}$ of a meter. The value $\dfrac{5}{7}$ comes from adding $3 + 2$ while keeping the denominator, and $\dfrac{6}{7}$ comes from multiplying $3$ by $2$ in the numerator.
