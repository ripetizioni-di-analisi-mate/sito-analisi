What do we get by cubing $-\dfrac{2}{3}$?

- $\dfrac{8}{27}$
- $-\dfrac{8}{3}$
- $-\dfrac{8}{27}$
- $-\dfrac{2}{27}$

## Explanation

The exponent $3$ applies to both numerator and denominator, giving $\left( -\dfrac{2}{3} \right)^3 = \dfrac{(-2)^3}{3^3} = -\dfrac{8}{27}$. The result is negative because the exponent is odd. The value $\dfrac{8}{27}$ ignores the sign, while $-\dfrac{8}{3}$ and $-\dfrac{2}{27}$ apply the exponent only to the numerator or only to the denominator.
