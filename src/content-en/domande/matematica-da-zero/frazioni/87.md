What do we get by simplifying the fraction with numerator $\dfrac{3}{4}$ and denominator $\dfrac{9}{8}$?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{27}{32}$
- $-\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

The fraction bar denotes division, so we calculate $\dfrac{3}{4} : \dfrac{9}{8} = \dfrac{3}{4} \cdot \dfrac{8}{9}$. Simplifying $3$ with $9$ and $8$ with $4$ gives $\dfrac{1 \cdot 2}{1 \cdot 3} = \dfrac{2}{3}$. The value $\dfrac{3}{2}$ is the reciprocal of the result, $\dfrac{27}{32}$ is the product of the two fractions, and $-\dfrac{3}{8}$ is their difference.
