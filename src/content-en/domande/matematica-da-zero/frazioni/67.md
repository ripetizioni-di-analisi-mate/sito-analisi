The rectangle is divided into $5$ equal columns and $2$ equal rows. The first three columns make up $\dfrac{3}{5}$ of the rectangle, and the top half of these columns is shaded. What fraction of the entire rectangle is shaded?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="0,1 3,1 3,2 0,2" linee="0,0 5,0 5,2 0,2 0,0;1,0 1,2;2,0 2,2;3,0 3,2;4,0 4,2;0,1 5,1" xmin=-0.3 xmax=5.3 ymin=-0.3 ymax=2.3}

- $\dfrac{3}{10}$
- $\dfrac{3}{7}$
- $\dfrac{6}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

We take half of $\dfrac{3}{5}$, so we multiply, giving $\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot \dfrac{3}{5} = \dfrac{3}{10}$. Counting the small squares gives the same value. The rectangle has $5 \cdot 2 = 10$ squares and $3$ of them are shaded. The value $\dfrac{6}{5}$ is twice $\dfrac{3}{5}$, $\dfrac{3}{7}$ comes from adding $2$ to the denominator, and $\dfrac{1}{2}$ is the top row alone.
