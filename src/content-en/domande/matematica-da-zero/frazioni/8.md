Which operation gives the fraction $\dfrac{5}{8}$?

- $5 : 8$
- $8 : 5$
- $5 \cdot 8$
- $8 - 5$

## Explanation

The fraction bar has the same meaning as the division sign. In the fraction $\dfrac{5}{8}$, the numerator is the dividend and the denominator is the divisor, so the fraction is the result of $5 : 8$. The division $8 : 5$ would give $\dfrac{8}{5}$, which is greater than $1$, while $5 \cdot 8 = 40$ and $8 - 5 = 3$ are integers.
