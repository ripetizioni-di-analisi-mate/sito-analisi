What is the result of the subtraction $\dfrac{7}{8} - \dfrac{1}{6}$?

- $\dfrac{25}{24}$
- $\dfrac{17}{24}$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{3}{7}$

## Explanation

The least common denominator is $24$, so $\dfrac{7}{8} = \dfrac{21}{24}$ and $\dfrac{1}{6} = \dfrac{4}{24}$. The difference is $\dfrac{21}{24} - \dfrac{4}{24} = \dfrac{17}{24}$. The value $\dfrac{25}{24}$ comes from adding instead of subtracting, the value $3$ from calculating $\dfrac{6}{2}$, and the value $\dfrac{3}{7} = \dfrac{6}{14}$ from subtracting the numerators and adding the denominators.
