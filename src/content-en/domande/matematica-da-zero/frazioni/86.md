What is the result of evaluating $2 - \dfrac{3}{4} \cdot \dfrac{2}{3}$?

- $\dfrac{5}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{5}{4}$

## Explanation

The product comes before the subtraction, so $\dfrac{3}{4} \cdot \dfrac{2}{3} = \dfrac{6}{12} = \dfrac{1}{2}$. Then $2 - \dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{4}{2} - \dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{3}{2}$, that is, $1.5$. The value $\dfrac{5}{6}$ comes from subtracting first, $\left( 2 - \dfrac{3}{4} \right) \cdot \dfrac{2}{3}$, the value $\dfrac{5}{4}$ from subtracting without multiplying, and $\dfrac{1}{2}$ is the product alone.
