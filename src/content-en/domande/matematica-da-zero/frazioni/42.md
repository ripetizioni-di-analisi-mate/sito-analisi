For which positive integers $n$ is the fraction $\dfrac{n}{9}$ less than $\dfrac{2}{3}$?

- $n \leq 6$
- $n < 3$
- $n \leq 9$
- $n \leq 5$

## Explanation

With denominator $9$ we have $\dfrac{2}{3} = \dfrac{6}{9}$. Fractions with the same denominator are compared by their numerators, so the condition is $n < 6$. Since $n$ is an integer, the values are $1$, $2$, $3$, $4$, $5$, that is, $n \leq 5$. For $n = 6$ the fraction equals $\dfrac{2}{3}$, so the inequality does not hold.
