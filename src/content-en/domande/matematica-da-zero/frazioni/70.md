A school has $480$ students, and $\dfrac{3}{8}$ of them take swimming lessons. How many students take the lessons?

- $60$
- $1280$
- $180$
- $300$

## Explanation

We divide $480$ into $8$ equal parts, giving $480 : 8 = 60$ students in each part. The students taking lessons make up $3$ parts, so there are $60 \cdot 3 = 180$ such students. The number $60$ counts the part of size $\dfrac{1}{8}$, $300$ counts the $\dfrac{5}{8}$ who do not take lessons, and $1280$ comes from dividing $480$ by $\dfrac{3}{8}$ instead of multiplying.
