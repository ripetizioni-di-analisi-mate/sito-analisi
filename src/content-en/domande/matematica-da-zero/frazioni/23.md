What fraction do we get when $\dfrac{42}{56}$ is reduced to lowest terms?

- $\dfrac{7}{8}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$

## Explanation

Factoring the numbers gives $42 = 2 \cdot 3 \cdot 7$ and $56 = 2^3 \cdot 7$, so the greatest common divisor is $2 \cdot 7 = 14$. Dividing by $14$ gives $\dfrac{42}{14}$ in the numerator and $\dfrac{56}{14}$ in the denominator, or $\dfrac{3}{4}$. The other fractions come from dividing numerator and denominator by different numbers, for example $42 : 6 = 7$ and $56 : 7 = 8$, which changes the value.
