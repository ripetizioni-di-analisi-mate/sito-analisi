Sara has $90$ euros. She spends $\dfrac{2}{5}$ of it on a book and then $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of what remains on a trip to the movies. How many euros does she have left?

- $36$ euros
- $24$ euros
- $54$ euros
- $18$ euros

## Explanation

The book costs $\dfrac{2}{5}$ of $90$, or $36$ euros, which leaves $90 - 36 = 54$ euros. The trip to the movies costs $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of $54$, or $18$ euros, so $54 - 18 = 36$ euros remain. The value $24$ comes from subtracting from $54$ one third of $90$, that is, $30$, instead of one third of $54$. The value $54$ is the amount left after buying the book, and $18$ is the cost of the trip to the movies.
