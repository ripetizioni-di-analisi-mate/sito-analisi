Adding a fraction to $\dfrac{1}{6}$ gives $\dfrac{2}{3}$. What fraction was added?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $\dfrac{5}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

The fraction we want is $\dfrac{2}{3} - \dfrac{1}{6}$. With denominator $6$ we have $\dfrac{4}{6} - \dfrac{1}{6} = \dfrac{3}{6} = \dfrac{1}{2}$. Indeed, $\dfrac{1}{6} + \dfrac{3}{6} = \dfrac{4}{6} = \dfrac{2}{3}$. The value $\dfrac{5}{6}$ comes from adding instead of subtracting, while adding $\dfrac{1}{3}$ and $\dfrac{1}{4}$ in turn to $\dfrac{1}{6}$ gives $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $\dfrac{5}{12}$ rather than $\dfrac{2}{3}$.
