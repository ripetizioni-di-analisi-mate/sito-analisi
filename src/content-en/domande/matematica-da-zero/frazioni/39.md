Of $\dfrac{4}{5}$, $\dfrac{2}{3}$, $\dfrac{9}{10}$, and $\dfrac{5}{8}$, which fraction is closest to $1$?

- $\dfrac{9}{10}$
- $\dfrac{4}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{5}{8}$

## Explanation

Each fraction is proper, so its distance from $1$ is $1$ minus the fraction. The distances are $\dfrac{1}{5}$, $\dfrac{1}{3}$, $\dfrac{1}{10}$, and $\dfrac{3}{8}$. With the common denominator $120$, the distances are $\dfrac{24}{120}$, $\dfrac{40}{120}$, $\dfrac{12}{120}$, and $\dfrac{45}{120}$. The smallest is $\dfrac{12}{120} = \dfrac{1}{10}$, so the fraction closest to $1$ is $\dfrac{9}{10}$. The fraction $\dfrac{5}{8}$, on the other hand, is farthest away.
