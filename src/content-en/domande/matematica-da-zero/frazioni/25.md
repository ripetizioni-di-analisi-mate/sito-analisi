Davide evaluates $\dfrac{2+6}{2+10}$ by canceling the two $2$ terms and gets $\dfrac{6}{10}$. What is the correct value of the fraction in lowest terms?

- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$

## Explanation

The two $2$ terms are addends rather than factors, so they cannot be canceled. Evaluating the sums, $2 + 6 = 8$ and $2 + 10 = 12$, gives $\dfrac{8}{12}$. The greatest common divisor is $4$, so the reduced fraction is $\dfrac{2}{3}$. Davide's result, $\dfrac{3}{5}$ after reducing $\dfrac{6}{10}$, has a different value.
