Three partners share a profit of $360$ euros. The first receives $\dfrac{1}{2}$, the second $\dfrac{1}{3}$, and the third the rest. How many euros does the third partner receive?

- $120$ euros
- $180$ euros
- $300$ euros
- $60$ euros

## Explanation

The first two partners receive $\dfrac{1}{2} + \dfrac{1}{3} = \dfrac{3}{6} + \dfrac{2}{6} = \dfrac{5}{6}$ of the profit. The third therefore receives $\dfrac{1}{6}$, or $360 : 6 = 60$ euros. The amount $180$ euros is the first partner's share, $120$ euros is the second partner's share, and $300$ euros is the sum of the first two shares.
