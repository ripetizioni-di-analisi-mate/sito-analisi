What is the difference between $-4^2$ and $(-4)^2$?

- $-32$
- $0$
- $32$
- $-16$

## Explanation

In $-4^2$ the exponent applies only to $4$, so $-4^2 = -(4\cdot4) = -16$. In $(-4)^2$ the base is $-4$, so $(-4)^2 = 16$. The difference is $-16 - 16 = -32$. The value $0$ treats the two expressions as equal, $-16$ ignores the second term and $32$ has the wrong sign.
