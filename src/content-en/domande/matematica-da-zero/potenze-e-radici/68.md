A strip $\sqrt{50}$ cm long is shortened by $\sqrt{18}$ cm. How long is the remaining part?

- $4\sqrt{2}$ cm
- $8\sqrt{2}$ cm
- $2\sqrt{2}$ cm
- $2$ cm

## Explanation

Take the square root of the perfect-square factor out of each radical, giving $\sqrt{50} = 5\sqrt{2}$ and $\sqrt{18} = 3\sqrt{2}$. The two radicals are like radicals, so $5\sqrt{2} - 3\sqrt{2} = 2\sqrt{2}$. The value $4\sqrt{2}$ is $\sqrt{32} = \sqrt{50-18}$, obtained by subtracting the radicands, while $8\sqrt{2}$ adds the coefficients and $2$ drops the radical.
