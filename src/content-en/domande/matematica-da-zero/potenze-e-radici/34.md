Only one of these four numbers exceeds $1$. Which one?

- $5^{-2}$
- $\left(\dfrac{1}{5}\right)^{2}$
- $\left(\dfrac{1}{5}\right)^{-2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}\cdot5^{0}$

## Explanation

Calculating the values gives $\left(\dfrac{1}{5}\right)^{-2} = 5^2 = 25$, $5^{-2} = \dfrac{1}{25}$, $\left(\dfrac{1}{5}\right)^2 = \dfrac{1}{25}$ and $\dfrac{1}{5}\cdot5^0 = \dfrac{1}{5}$. Only $25$ is greater than $1$. The minus sign in the exponent calls for taking the reciprocal of the base, which here is $\dfrac{1}{5}$ and therefore gives a large number.
