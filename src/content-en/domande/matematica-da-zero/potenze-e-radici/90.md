Given that $1.7^2 = 2.89$ and $1.8^2 = 3.24$, which interval contains $\sqrt{3}$?

- Between $1.6$ and $1.7$
- Between $1.7$ and $1.8$
- Between $1.8$ and $1.9$
- Between $2.89$ and $3.24$

## Explanation

Since $2.89 < 3 < 3.24$, we have $1.7^2 < 3 < 1.8^2$ and therefore $1.7 < \sqrt{3} < 1.8$. In the intervals starting at $1.6$ or $1.8$, the squares of the endpoints do not bracket $3$. Indeed, $1.7^2 = 2.89$ is already less than $3$. The interval between $2.89$ and $3.24$ contains the squares rather than the root.
