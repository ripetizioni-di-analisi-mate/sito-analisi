A cell divides in two every hour, and each new cell in turn divides in two every hour. Starting with a single cell, how many cells are there after $6$ hours?

- $12$
- $64$
- $36$
- $32$

## Explanation

The number of cells is multiplied by $2$ every hour, so after $6$ hours there are $6$ factors equal to $2$, giving the power $2^6 = 64$. The value $12$ comes from adding instead of multiplying, $36$ is obtained by calculating $6^2$ with the base and exponent interchanged, and $32$ counts only five hours.
