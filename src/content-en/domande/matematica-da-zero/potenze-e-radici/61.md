Does the cube root also exist for a negative radicand? What is $\sqrt[3]{-64}$?

- $4$
- $-4$
- It is undefined
- $-32$

## Explanation

Negative numbers also have cube roots because the cube of a negative number is negative, as in $(-4)^3 = (-4)\cdot(-4)\cdot(-4) = -64$. Therefore $\sqrt[3]{-64} = -4$. The value $4$ changes the sign. The answer that says the root is undefined applies to square roots rather than cube roots, and $-32$ halves the radicand.
