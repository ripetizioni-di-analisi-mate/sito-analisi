The number $\left(\sqrt{13}\right)^2$ is the area, in cm$^2$, of a square with sides $\sqrt{13}$ cm long. What is its value?

- $169$ cm$^2$
- $26$ cm$^2$
- $13$ cm$^2$
- $6.5$ cm$^2$

## Explanation

The area is the square of the side length, giving $\left(\sqrt{13}\right)^2 = 13$ cm$^2$ because squaring a square root returns the radicand. The value $169$ squares the radicand, $26$ doubles it and $6.5$ halves it.
