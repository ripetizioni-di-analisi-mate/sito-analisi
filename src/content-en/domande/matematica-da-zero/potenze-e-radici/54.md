For which values of $x$ is $\sqrt{x-3}$ defined?

- $x \leq 3$
- $x \geq 3$
- $x \geq -3$
- $x \geq 0$

## Explanation

The radicand of a square root must be nonnegative, so $x - 3 \geq 0$, that is, $x \geq 3$. For $x = 3$ the root equals $\sqrt{0} = 0$. The condition $x \leq 3$ reverses the inequality, $x \geq -3$ changes the sign of $3$ and $x \geq 0$ uses the radicand $x$ in place of $x-3$.
