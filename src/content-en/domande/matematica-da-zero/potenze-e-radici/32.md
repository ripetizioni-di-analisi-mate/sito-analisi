The number $\dfrac{1}{2}$ is raised to the power $-3$ and the result is multiplied by $2^{-3}$. What is the product $\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{-3}\cdot2^{-3}$?

- $64$
- $\dfrac{1}{64}$
- $8$
- $1$

## Explanation

The reciprocal of $\dfrac{1}{2}$ is $2$, so $\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{-3} = 2^3 = 8$. Also, $2^{-3} = \dfrac{1}{8}$. The product is $8\cdot\dfrac{1}{8} = 1$. The value $64$ is the product $8\cdot8$, the value $\dfrac{1}{64}$ is the product $\dfrac{1}{8}\cdot\dfrac{1}{8}$ and $8$ ignores the second factor.
