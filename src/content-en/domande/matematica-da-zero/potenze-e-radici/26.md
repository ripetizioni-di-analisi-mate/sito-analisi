A number is written as $2^{-5}$. What is its value?

- $\dfrac{1}{32}$
- $-32$
- $-10$
- $\dfrac{1}{10}$

## Explanation

A negative exponent indicates the reciprocal of the power with the positive exponent, giving $2^{-5} = \dfrac{1}{2^5} = \dfrac{1}{32}$. The minus sign in the exponent does not make the result negative, so $-32$ is incorrect. The value $-10$ is the product $2\cdot(-5)$, and $\dfrac{1}{10}$ is its reciprocal with the minus sign removed.
