Which of the following operations on two irrational numbers gives a rational number?

- $\sqrt{2}+\sqrt{8}$
- $\sqrt{2}\cdot\sqrt{8}$
- $\sqrt{2}\cdot\sqrt{3}$
- $\sqrt{2}+\sqrt{3}$

## Explanation

We have $\sqrt{2}\cdot\sqrt{8} = \sqrt{16} = 4$, which is rational. The other results are irrational, namely $\sqrt{2}+\sqrt{8} = \sqrt{2}+2\sqrt{2} = 3\sqrt{2}$ and $\sqrt{2}\cdot\sqrt{3} = \sqrt{6}$, the latter because $6$ is not a perfect square. The number $\sqrt{2}+\sqrt{3}$ is also irrational.
