What is the square root of $(-9)^2$?

- $-9$
- $81$
- $3$
- $9$

## Explanation

First evaluate the power, giving $(-9)^2 = 81$. Then $\sqrt{81} = 9$ because the square root is the nonnegative number whose square equals $81$. The value $-9$ cancels the root and square without accounting for the sign, $81$ ignores the square root and $3$ is the square root of $9$.
