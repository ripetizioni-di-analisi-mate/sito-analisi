Which number best approximates $\sqrt{10}$?

- $3.5$
- $5$
- $3.16$
- $2.5$

## Explanation

Squaring the proposed values gives $3.16^2 = 9.9856$, which is close to $10$. In contrast, $3.5^2 = 12.25$, $5^2 = 25$ and $2.5^2 = 6.25$ are farther from $10$. The value $5$ is half of $10$ rather than its square root, so the best approximation is $3.16$.
