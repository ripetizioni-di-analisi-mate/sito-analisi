Which of the following expressions does not represent a real number?

- $\sqrt{-4}$
- $\sqrt[3]{-8}$
- $\sqrt{0}$
- $2^{-3}$

## Explanation

The square of a real number is nonnegative, so no real number has a square equal to $-4$ and the expression $\sqrt{-4}$ is undefined. The other expressions represent real numbers, namely $\sqrt[3]{-8} = -2$, $\sqrt{0} = 0$ and $2^{-3} = \dfrac{1}{8}$.
