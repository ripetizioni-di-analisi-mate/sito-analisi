A cable $9\cdot10^{6}$ mm long is cut into pieces $3\cdot10^{2}$ mm long. How many pieces are there?

- $3\cdot10^{8}$
- $3\cdot10^{3}$
- $6\cdot10^{4}$
- $3\cdot10^{4}$

## Explanation

The number of pieces is the quotient $\dfrac{9\cdot10^6}{3\cdot10^2} = \dfrac{9}{3}\cdot10^{6-2} = 3\cdot10^4$, or $30\,000$. The result $3\cdot10^8$ comes from adding the exponents, $3\cdot10^3$ from subtracting the exponents incorrectly and $6\cdot10^4$ from subtracting the coefficients instead of dividing them.
