A virus measures $0.000\,000\,12$ m. How is this measurement written in scientific notation?

- $1.2\cdot10^{-8}$ m
- $1.2\cdot10^{-7}$ m
- $1.2\cdot10^{7}$ m
- $1.2\cdot10^{-6}$ m

## Explanation

Move the decimal point $7$ places to the right to obtain $1.2$, so the exponent is negative and equals $-7$, giving $0.000\,000\,12 = 1.2\cdot10^{-7}$. The exponent $7$ has the wrong sign, while $-8$ and $-6$ count one place too many or too few.
