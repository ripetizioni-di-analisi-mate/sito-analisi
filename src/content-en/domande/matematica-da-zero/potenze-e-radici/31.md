A product of powers of $3$ has exponents of opposite signs and is written as $3^{-2}\cdot3^{5}$. What is its value?

- $2\,187$
- $\dfrac{1}{27}$
- $27$
- $243$

## Explanation

The product of powers with the same base has an exponent equal to the sum of the exponents, giving $3^{-2}\cdot3^5 = 3^{-2+5} = 3^3 = 27$. The value $2\,187 = 3^7$ adds the exponents without their signs, $\dfrac{1}{27}$ changes the sign of the sum and $243 = 3^5$ ignores the power with a negative exponent.
