Which power results from simplifying $\dfrac{7^9}{7^6}$?

- $7^{15}$
- $7^{54}$
- $7^{\dfrac{3}{2}}$
- $7^{3}$

## Explanation

The quotient of two powers with the same base has that base and an exponent equal to the difference of the exponents, so $\dfrac{7^9}{7^6} = 7^{9-6} = 7^3$. The exponent $15$ comes from adding the exponents, $54$ from multiplying them and $\dfrac{3}{2}$ from dividing them.
