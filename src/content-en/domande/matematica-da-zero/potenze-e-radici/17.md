The product $(-2)^3\cdot(-2)^4$ can be written as a single power with base $-2$. What is its value?

- $-128$
- $128$
- $4\,096$
- $-4\,096$

## Explanation

Both powers have the same base $-2$, so their exponents are added, giving $(-2)^3\cdot(-2)^4 = (-2)^7$. The exponent $7$ is odd, so the result is negative and equals $-128$. The value $4\,096$ comes from multiplying the exponents to obtain the even number $3\cdot4 = 12$, and $-4\,096$ also gives this result the wrong sign.
