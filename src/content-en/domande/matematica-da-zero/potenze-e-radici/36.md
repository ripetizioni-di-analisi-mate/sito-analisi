For what value of $n$ is $4^n = \dfrac{1}{64}$ true?

- $3$
- $-16$
- $-3$
- $16$

## Explanation

Since $64 = 4^3$, we have $\dfrac{1}{64} = \dfrac{1}{4^3} = 4^{-3}$ and therefore $n = -3$. The value $3$ corresponds to $4^3 = 64$, the number before taking its reciprocal. The values $16$ and $-16$ come from dividing $64$ by $4$ instead of writing $64$ as a power of $4$.
