Which of the following equalities holds for every choice of $a$ and $b$?

- $(a+b)^2 = a^2+b^2$
- $(a\cdot b)^2 = a^2\cdot b^2$
- $a^3+a^4 = a^7$
- $\left(a^3\right)^2 = a^5$

## Explanation

A power of a product is the product of the powers, so $(a\cdot b)^2 = a^2\cdot b^2$ always holds. The other three do not hold in general. For $a = 1$ and $b = 2$ we have $(1+2)^2 = 9$ while $1^2+2^2 = 5$. For $a = 2$ we have $2^3+2^4 = 24$ while $2^7 = 128$. Finally, $\left(a^3\right)^2 = a^6$.
