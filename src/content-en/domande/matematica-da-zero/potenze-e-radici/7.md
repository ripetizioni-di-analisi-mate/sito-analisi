A cube has edges $12$ cm long. What is its volume?

- $36$ cm$^3$
- $1\,728$ cm$^3$
- $144$ cm$^3$
- $531\,441$ cm$^3$

## Explanation

The volume of a cube is the cube of its edge length, so it is $12^3 = 12\cdot12\cdot12 = 1\,728$ cm$^3$. The value $36$ is the product $12\cdot3$, $144$ is $12^2$, the area of one face, and $531\,441$ is $3^{12}$, obtained by interchanging the base and exponent.
