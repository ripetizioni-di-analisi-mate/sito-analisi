You want to multiply $\sqrt{5}-2$ by an expression and obtain exactly $1$. Which factor should you choose?

- $\sqrt{5}-2$
- $2-\sqrt{5}$
- $\sqrt{5}+2$
- $\sqrt{5}$

## Explanation

The product of two binomials that differ only in the sign between their terms is the difference of the squares. Here $(\sqrt5-2)(\sqrt5+2)=5-4=1$.
