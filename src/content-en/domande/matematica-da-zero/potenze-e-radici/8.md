Which of the powers $2^6$, $3^4$, $4^3$ and $6^2$ has the greatest value?

- $2^6$
- $3^4$
- $4^3$
- $6^2$

## Explanation

Calculating the four powers gives $2^6 = 64$, $3^4 = 81$, $4^3 = 64$ and $6^2 = 36$. The greatest value is $81$, which is the value of $3^4$. Comparing the bases alone would lead to $6^2$, and comparing the exponents alone would lead to $2^6$.
