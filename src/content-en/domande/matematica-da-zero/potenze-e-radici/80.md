A rectangle has area $12$ cm$^2$ and a side of length $\sqrt{6}$ cm. How long is its other side?

- $2\sqrt{6}$ cm
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{6}}{2}$ cm
- $12\sqrt{6}$ cm
- $2$ cm

## Explanation

The other side length is the quotient $\dfrac{12}{\sqrt{6}}$. Rationalizing gives $\dfrac{12\sqrt{6}}{6} = 2\sqrt{6}$ cm. The value $12\sqrt{6}$ omits the division by $6$, $\dfrac{\sqrt{6}}{2}$ divides $6$ by $12$ and $2$ omits the radical.
