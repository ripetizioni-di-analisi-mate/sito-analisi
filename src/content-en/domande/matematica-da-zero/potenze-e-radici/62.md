The number $2^6\cdot3^3$ is a perfect cube. What is its cube root?

- $24$
- $12$
- $6$
- $1\,728$

## Explanation

To find the cube root, divide the exponents by $3$, giving $\sqrt[3]{2^6\cdot3^3} = 2^2\cdot3^1 = 12$. The value $24$ leaves $2^3$ instead of $2^2$, $6$ sets all exponents equal to $1$ and $1\,728 = 2^6\cdot3^3$ is the original number without taking the root.
