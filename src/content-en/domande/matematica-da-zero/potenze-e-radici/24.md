How do $3^{20}$ and $9^9$ compare?

- $3^{20} < 9^9$
- $3^{20} = 9^9$
- They cannot be compared without calculating the powers
- $3^{20} > 9^9$

## Explanation

Since $9 = 3^2$, we have $9^9 = \left(3^2\right)^9 = 3^{18}$. The two powers have the same base $3$, which is greater than $1$, so the one with the larger exponent is greater. We have $20 > 18$, so $3^{20} > 9^9$. Comparing the bases alone, $3 < 9$, would lead to the opposite conclusion.
