What is $\left(\dfrac{3}{5}\right)^{-2}$ written as a fraction?

- $\dfrac{9}{25}$
- $-\dfrac{9}{25}$
- $\dfrac{25}{9}$
- $-\dfrac{6}{5}$

## Explanation

The reciprocal of a fraction is found by interchanging numerator and denominator, so $\left(\dfrac{3}{5}\right)^{-2} = \left(\dfrac{5}{3}\right)^2 = \dfrac{25}{9}$. The value $\dfrac{9}{25}$ raises the fraction to the power $2$ without inverting it, the value $-\dfrac{9}{25}$ adds a minus sign and the value $-\dfrac{6}{5}$ multiplies the fraction by $-2$.
