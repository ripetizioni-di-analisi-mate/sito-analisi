A square has sides of length $0.3$ m. What is its area?

- $0.9$ m$^2$
- $0.6$ m$^2$
- $0.027$ m$^2$
- $0.09$ m$^2$

## Explanation

The area is the square of the side length, so it is $0.3^2 = 0.3\cdot0.3 = 0.09$ m$^2$. The product of two decimals has as many decimal places as the two factors combined, which here is two. The value $0.9$ multiplies the side length by $3$, $0.6$ doubles it and $0.027$ is the cube $0.3^3$.
