A cube has a volume of $8$ cm$^3$. If the volume increases to $27$ cm$^3$, by how many centimeters does the edge length increase?

- $19$ cm
- $5$ cm
- $1$ cm
- $3$ cm

## Explanation

The edge length is the cube root of the volume, giving $\sqrt[3]{8} = 2$ cm in the first case and $\sqrt[3]{27} = 3$ cm in the second. The increase is $3 - 2 = 1$ cm. The value $19$ is the difference between the volumes, $5$ is the sum of the edge lengths and $3$ is the final edge length.
