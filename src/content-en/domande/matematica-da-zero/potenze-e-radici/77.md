What is $\dfrac{6}{\sqrt{3}}$ equal to once the denominator is rationalized?

- $6\sqrt{3}$
- $2\sqrt{3}$
- $3\sqrt{3}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{6}$

## Explanation

Multiplying numerator and denominator by $\sqrt{3}$ gives $\dfrac{6}{\sqrt{3}} = \dfrac{6\sqrt{3}}{\sqrt{3}\cdot\sqrt{3}} = \dfrac{6\sqrt{3}}{3} = 2\sqrt{3}$. The result $6\sqrt{3}$ omits the division by $3$, $3\sqrt{3}$ divides by $2$ instead of $3$ and $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{6}$ interchanges numerator and denominator.
