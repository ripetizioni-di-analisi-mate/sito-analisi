A square field has an area of $225$ m$^2$. What is its perimeter?

- $60$ m
- $15$ m
- $900$ m
- $30$ m

## Explanation

The side length is the square root of the area, giving $\sqrt{225} = 15$ m because $15^2 = 225$. The perimeter is $4\cdot15 = 60$ m. The value $15$ is the side length rather than the perimeter, $30$ is twice the side length and $900$ is $4\cdot225$, the area multiplied by $4$.
