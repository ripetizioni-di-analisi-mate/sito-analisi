For what value of $x$ does $\dfrac{5^{x}}{5^{3}} = 5^{6}$ hold?

- $3$
- $2$
- $9$
- $18$

## Explanation

The quotient rule for powers with the same base gives $\dfrac{5^x}{5^3} = 5^{x-3}$. Equality with $5^6$ requires $x - 3 = 6$, so $x = 9$. The value $2$ comes from dividing $6$ by $3$, $18$ from multiplying it by $3$ and $3$ is the exponent in the denominator.
