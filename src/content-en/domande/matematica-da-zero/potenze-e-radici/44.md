What is the order of magnitude of $6\,200\,000$?

- $10^{6}$
- $10^{7}$
- $6\cdot10^{6}$
- $10^{5}$

## Explanation

In scientific notation, $6\,200\,000 = 6.2\cdot10^6$. The coefficient is $6.2 \geq 5$, so the order of magnitude is the next power, $10^{7}$. The value $10^6$ ignores the threshold $5$, $6\cdot10^6$ is not a power of $10$ and $10^5$ counts one digit too few.
