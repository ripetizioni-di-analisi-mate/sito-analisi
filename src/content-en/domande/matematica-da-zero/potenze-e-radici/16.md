For a natural number $n$, which expression equals $2^n + 2^n$?

- $2^{2n}$
- $2^{n^2}$
- $2n$
- $2^{n+1}$

## Explanation

The sum of two equal terms is twice either term, so $2^n + 2^n = 2\cdot2^n$. Since $2 = 2^1$, the product rule for powers with the same base gives $2^1\cdot2^n = 2^{n+1}$. For $n = 3$ the sum is $8 + 8 = 16 = 2^4$, while $2^{2n}$ equals $64$ and $2n$ equals $6$.
