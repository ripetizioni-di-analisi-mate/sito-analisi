A signal travels at a speed of $3\cdot10^{8}$ m/s for $5\cdot10^{2}$ s. How many meters does it travel?

- $1.5\cdot10^{11}$ m
- $1.5\cdot10^{10}$ m
- $8\cdot10^{10}$ m
- $1.5\cdot10^{16}$ m

## Explanation

Distance is the product of speed and time, giving $\left(3\cdot10^8\right)\cdot\left(5\cdot10^2\right) = 15\cdot10^{10}$. Since $15 = 1.5\cdot10$, the result is $1.5\cdot10^{11}$ m. The value $1.5\cdot10^{10}$ omits the factor $10$ in $15$, $8\cdot10^{10}$ adds $3$ and $5$, and $1.5\cdot10^{16}$ multiplies the exponents.
