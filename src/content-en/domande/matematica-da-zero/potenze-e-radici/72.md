Given $x \geq 0$, simplify $\sqrt{12x^2}$.

- $4x\sqrt{3}$
- $2\sqrt{3x}$
- $12x$
- $2x\sqrt{3}$

## Explanation

Factor the radicand as $12x^2 = 4\cdot3\cdot x^2$. Since $\sqrt{4} = 2$ and $\sqrt{x^2} = x$ for $x \geq 0$, we obtain $\sqrt{12x^2} = 2x\sqrt{3}$. The coefficient $4$ comes from failing to take the square root of $4$, the result $2\sqrt{3x}$ leaves $x$ under the radical and $12x$ does not apply the square root to the factor $12$.
