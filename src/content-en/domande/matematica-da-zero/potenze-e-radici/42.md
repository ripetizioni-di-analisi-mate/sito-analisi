Two loads weigh $2.5\cdot10^{3}$ kg and $7.5\cdot10^{3}$ kg. What is their combined weight in scientific notation?

- $1\cdot10^{7}$ kg
- $1\cdot10^{3}$ kg
- $5\cdot10^{3}$ kg
- $1\cdot10^{4}$ kg

## Explanation

Both terms have the same power of $10$, so factor it out, giving $(2.5 + 7.5)\cdot10^3 = 10\cdot10^3$. Since $10 = 1\cdot10$, the result is $1\cdot10^4$. The result $1\cdot10^7$ adds the exponents as in a product, $1\cdot10^3$ fails to carry the factor $10$ and $5\cdot10^3$ subtracts the coefficients.
