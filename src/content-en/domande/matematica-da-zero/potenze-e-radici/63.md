Which of the following expressions equals $3$?

- $\sqrt[3]{9}$
- $\sqrt{27}$
- $\sqrt{6}$
- $\sqrt[3]{27}$

## Explanation

Since $3^3 = 27$, we have $\sqrt[3]{27} = 3$. The other expressions do not equal $3$. The value $\sqrt[3]{9}$ is less than $3$ because $3^3 = 27 > 9$, $\sqrt{27}$ is greater than $3$ because $3^2 = 9 < 27$, and $\sqrt{6}$ is less than $3$ because $6 < 9$.
