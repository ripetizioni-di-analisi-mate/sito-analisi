For what value of $k$ does $\left(2^k\right)^3 = 2^{12}$ hold?

- $9$
- $4$
- $15$
- $36$

## Explanation

By the power-of-a-power rule, multiply the exponents, so $\left(2^k\right)^3 = 2^{3k}$. Equality with $2^{12}$ requires $3k = 12$, so $k = 4$. The value $9$ comes from the sum $k + 3 = 12$, while $15$ and $36$ come from applying addition and multiplication, respectively, to the exponent $12$.
