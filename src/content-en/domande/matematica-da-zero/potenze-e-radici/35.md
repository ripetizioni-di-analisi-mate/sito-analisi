Given $x \neq 0$, simplify $\dfrac{x^{-2}}{x^{3}}$.

- $\dfrac{1}{x}$
- $\dfrac{1}{x^5}$
- $x^5$
- $\dfrac{1}{x^6}$

## Explanation

For the quotient of powers with the same base, subtract the exponents, giving $\dfrac{x^{-2}}{x^3} = x^{-2-3} = x^{-5} = \dfrac{1}{x^5}$. The result $\dfrac{1}{x}$ comes from computing $-2+3 = 1$ with the sign reversed, $x^5$ ignores the sign of the exponent and $\dfrac{1}{x^6}$ multiplies the exponents.
