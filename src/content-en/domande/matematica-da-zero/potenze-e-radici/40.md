Calculate $\left(3\cdot10^{5}\right)\cdot\left(4\cdot10^{-2}\right)$ and write the result in scientific notation.

- $1.2\cdot10^{4}$
- $1.2\cdot10^{3}$
- $7\cdot10^{3}$
- $1.2\cdot10^{7}$

## Explanation

Multiply the coefficients and add the exponents, giving $(3\cdot4)\cdot10^{5+(-2)} = 12\cdot10^3$. Since $12 = 1.2\cdot10$, the result is $1.2\cdot10^4$. The result $1.2\cdot10^3$ keeps $12$ without carrying the factor $10$, $7\cdot10^3$ adds the coefficients and $1.2\cdot10^7$ adds the exponents without their signs.
