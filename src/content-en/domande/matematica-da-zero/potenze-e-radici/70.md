A rectangle has side lengths $3\sqrt{2}$ cm and $4\sqrt{2}$ cm. What is its area?

- $12\sqrt{2}$ cm$^2$
- $7\sqrt{2}$ cm$^2$
- $14$ cm$^2$
- $24$ cm$^2$

## Explanation

Multiply the coefficients together and the radicals together, giving $3\cdot4\cdot\sqrt{2}\cdot\sqrt{2} = 12\cdot2 = 24$ because $\sqrt{2}\cdot\sqrt{2} = 2$. The value $12\sqrt{2}$ keeps only one radical, $7\sqrt{2}$ adds the coefficients and $14$ adds the coefficients and then multiplies by $\sqrt{2}\cdot\sqrt{2} = 2$.
