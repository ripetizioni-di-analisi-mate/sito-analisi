A bar $4\cdot10^{5}$ mm long is lengthened by $3\cdot10^{4}$ mm. What is its new length in scientific notation?

- $7\cdot10^{9}$ mm
- $4.3\cdot10^{5}$ mm
- $7\cdot10^{5}$ mm
- $4.3\cdot10^{4}$ mm

## Explanation

The two powers of $10$ differ, so evaluate the two numbers to obtain $400\,000 + 30\,000 = 430\,000 = 4.3\cdot10^5$. The result $7\cdot10^9$ adds both the coefficients and the exponents, $7\cdot10^5$ adds only the coefficients and $4.3\cdot10^4$ has the wrong exponent.
