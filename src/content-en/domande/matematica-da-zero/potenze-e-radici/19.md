How is $27^2$ written as a power of $3$?

- $3^5$
- $3^6$
- $3^9$
- $3^{54}$

## Explanation

Since $27 = 3^3$, the power-of-a-power rule gives $27^2 = \left(3^3\right)^2 = 3^{3\cdot2} = 3^6$. The exponent $5$ comes from adding $3$ and $2$, the exponent $9$ comes from calculating $3^2$, and the exponent $54$ from multiplying $27$ by $2$ without rewriting the base.
