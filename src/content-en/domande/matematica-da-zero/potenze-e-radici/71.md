What is the quotient of $\sqrt{98}$ and $\sqrt{2}$, that is, $\dfrac{\sqrt{98}}{\sqrt{2}}$?

- $49$
- $\sqrt{96}$
- $7$
- $\dfrac{7}{2}$

## Explanation

The root of a quotient is the quotient of the roots, so $\dfrac{\sqrt{98}}{\sqrt{2}} = \sqrt{\dfrac{98}{2}} = \sqrt{49} = 7$. The value $49$ omits the root, $\sqrt{96}$ subtracts the radicands and $\dfrac{7}{2}$ divides the result by $2$ again.
