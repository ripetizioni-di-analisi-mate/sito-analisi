Which of the following fractions has a rational denominator?

- $\dfrac{\sqrt{5}}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{\sqrt{5}}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2\sqrt{7}}$
- $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{3}}$

## Explanation

A fraction has a rational denominator if there are no roots below the fraction bar. In $\dfrac{\sqrt{5}}{3}$, the denominator is $3$. Each of the other fractions has a root in the denominator, namely $\sqrt{5}$, $2\sqrt{7}$ and $\sqrt{3}$, respectively. Each can be rationalized by multiplying numerator and denominator by the root below the bar.
