A rectangular vegetable garden has sides $\sqrt{27}$ m and $\sqrt{12}$ m long. The length of fencing needed equals its perimeter. How many meters of fencing are required?

- $5\sqrt{3}$ m
- $10\sqrt{3}$ m
- $2\sqrt{39}$ m
- $20\sqrt{3}$ m

## Explanation

We have $\sqrt{27} = 3\sqrt{3}$ and $\sqrt{12} = 2\sqrt{3}$, so the side lengths are like radicals. The perimeter is $2\left(3\sqrt{3} + 2\sqrt{3}\right) = 2\cdot5\sqrt{3} = 10\sqrt{3}$ m. The value $5\sqrt{3}$ is half the perimeter, $2\sqrt{39}$ adds the radicands and $20\sqrt{3}$ multiplies the sum of the side lengths by $4$.
