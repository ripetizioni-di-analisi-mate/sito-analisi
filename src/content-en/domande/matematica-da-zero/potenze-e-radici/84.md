Only one of these roots is irrational. Which one?

- $\sqrt{49}$
- $\sqrt{20}$
- $\sqrt{2.25}$
- $\sqrt{\dfrac{9}{16}}$

## Explanation

The number $20 = 2^2\cdot5$ is not a perfect square because the factor $5$ has an odd exponent, so $\sqrt{20} = 2\sqrt{5}$ is irrational. The other numbers are rational, as shown by $\sqrt{49} = 7$, $\sqrt{2.25} = 1.5$ and $\sqrt{\dfrac{9}{16}} = \dfrac{3}{4}$.
