Only one of the following equalities is true. Which one?

- $\sqrt{9}+\sqrt{16} = \sqrt{25}$
- $\sqrt{16-9} = \sqrt{16}-\sqrt{9}$
- $\sqrt{9\cdot16} = \sqrt{9}\cdot\sqrt{16}$
- $\sqrt{9}\cdot\sqrt{16} = \sqrt{25}$

## Explanation

The root of a product is the product of the roots, giving $\sqrt{9\cdot16} = \sqrt{144} = 12 = 3\cdot4$. No such rule holds for a sum or a difference. We have $\sqrt{9}+\sqrt{16} = 7$ while $\sqrt{25} = 5$, and $\sqrt{16-9} = \sqrt{7}$ while $4-3 = 1$. The equality $\sqrt{9}\cdot\sqrt{16} = \sqrt{25}$ is also false because $3\cdot4 = 12$ and $\sqrt{25} = 5$.
