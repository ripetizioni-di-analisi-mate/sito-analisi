Which of the following numbers is a perfect square?

- $200$
- $450$
- $324$
- $600$

## Explanation

A number is a perfect square if every exponent in its prime factorization is even. We have $324 = 2^2\cdot3^4$, with even exponents, and $\sqrt{324} = 2\cdot3^2 = 18$. The other numbers each have an odd exponent, as shown by $200 = 2^3\cdot5^2$, $450 = 2\cdot3^2\cdot5^2$ and $600 = 2^3\cdot3\cdot5^2$.
