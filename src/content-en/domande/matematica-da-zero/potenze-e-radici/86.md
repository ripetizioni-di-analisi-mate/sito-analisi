To which number sets does $-\dfrac{12}{4}$ belong?

- Only $\mathbb{Q}$ and $\mathbb{R}$
- Only $\mathbb{R}$
- $\mathbb{N}$, $\mathbb{Z}$, $\mathbb{Q}$ and $\mathbb{R}$
- $\mathbb{Z}$, $\mathbb{Q}$ and $\mathbb{R}$, but not $\mathbb{N}$

## Explanation

The fraction simplifies to $-\dfrac{12}{4} = -3$. The number $-3$ is a negative integer, so it belongs to $\mathbb{Z}$ and, since the sets are nested, also to $\mathbb{Q}$ and $\mathbb{R}$, but it does not belong to $\mathbb{N}$. The set to which a number belongs depends on its value, regardless of how it is written.
