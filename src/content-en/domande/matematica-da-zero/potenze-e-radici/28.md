Which power of $3$ with an integer exponent equals $\dfrac{1}{81}$?

- $3^{4}$
- $3^{-27}$
- $-3^{4}$
- $3^{-4}$

## Explanation

Since $81 = 3^4$, we have $\dfrac{1}{81} = \dfrac{1}{3^4} = 3^{-4}$. The positive exponent $4$ gives $81$ rather than its reciprocal, and the expression $-3^4$ represents $-81$. The exponent $-27$ comes from starting with the quotient $81 : 3 = 27$ and failing to rewrite $81$ as a power of $3$.
