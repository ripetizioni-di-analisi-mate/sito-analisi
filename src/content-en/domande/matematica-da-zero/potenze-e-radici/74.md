What expression results from moving the factor $4$ inside the radical in $4\sqrt{3}$?

- $\sqrt{12}$
- $\sqrt{19}$
- $\sqrt{7}$
- $\sqrt{48}$

## Explanation

The factor $4$ goes inside the radical as $4^2 = 16$ because $4 = \sqrt{16}$. Therefore $4\sqrt{3} = \sqrt{16}\cdot\sqrt{3} = \sqrt{48}$. The radicand $12$ multiplies $4$ by $3$, the radicand $19$ adds $16$ and $3$, and the radicand $7$ adds $4$ and $3$.
