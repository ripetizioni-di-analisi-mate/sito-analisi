Which of these decimal representations gives an irrational number?

- $0.\overline{3}$
- $0.1010010001\ldots$ with one more zero between each $1$ and the next
- $0.25$
- $2.\overline{45}$

## Explanation

A number is irrational if its decimal expansion continues indefinitely without repeating. In $0.1010010001\ldots$ the number of zeros between successive occurrences of the digit $1$ increases by one each time, so no block of digits repeats indefinitely. The other numbers have terminating or repeating expansions and can be written as fractions, giving $0.25 = \dfrac{1}{4}$, $0.\overline{3} = \dfrac{1}{3}$ and $2.\overline{45} = \dfrac{27}{11}$.
