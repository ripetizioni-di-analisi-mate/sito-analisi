Squaring $2\sqrt{3}$ gives an integer. Which integer?

- $6$
- $36$
- $4\sqrt{3}$
- $12$

## Explanation

A power of a product is the product of the powers, so $\left(2\sqrt{3}\right)^2 = 2^2\cdot\left(\sqrt{3}\right)^2 = 4\cdot3 = 12$. The value $6$ does not square the factor $2$, $36$ squares the product $2\cdot3$ instead of $2\sqrt{3}$ and $4\sqrt{3}$ squares only the factor $2$.
