A square has a side length of $\sqrt{2}$ m. Which statement about its area and perimeter is true?

- The area and perimeter are both rational
- The area is irrational and the perimeter is rational
- The area is rational and the perimeter is irrational
- The area and perimeter are both irrational

## Explanation

The area is the square of the side length, giving $\left(\sqrt{2}\right)^2 = 2$ m$^2$, which is rational. The perimeter is $4\sqrt{2}$ m, the product of a nonzero rational number and an irrational number, so it is irrational. Squaring eliminates the radical, while multiplying by $4$ does not.
