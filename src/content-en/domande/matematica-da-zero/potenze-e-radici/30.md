Two powers of $2$ with negative exponents appear in the sum $2^{-1} + 2^{-2}$. What is its value?

- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$
- $-6$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

The two terms are added, so the exponents are not added and each term is evaluated separately. We have $2^{-1} = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and $2^{-2} = \dfrac{1}{4}$, so the sum is $\dfrac{2}{4} + \dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{3}{4}$. The value $\dfrac{1}{8}$ is $2^{-3}$, obtained by adding the exponents, and $-6$ is $-2-4$.
