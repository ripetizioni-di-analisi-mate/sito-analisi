Which expression equals $x^3\cdot x^5$?

- $x^8$
- $x^{15}$
- $2x^8$
- $x^2$

## Explanation

The product of two powers with the same base is a power with that base and an exponent equal to the sum of the exponents, so $x^3\cdot x^5 = x^{3+5} = x^8$. Multiplying the exponents would give $x^{15}$, and subtracting them would give $x^2$. The coefficient $2$ does not appear because the base $x$ is not being added to itself.
