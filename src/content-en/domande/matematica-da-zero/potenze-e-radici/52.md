Between which two consecutive integers does $\sqrt{75}$ lie?

- Between $8$ and $9$
- Between $7$ and $8$
- Between $9$ and $10$
- Between $37$ and $38$

## Explanation

Since $8^2 = 64$ and $9^2 = 81$, we have $64 < 75 < 81$, so $8 < \sqrt{75} < 9$. The numbers $7$ and $8$ have squares $49$ and $64$, both less than $75$, while $9$ and $10$ have squares $81$ and $100$, both greater. The values $37$ and $38$ come from $75 : 2 = 37.5$.
