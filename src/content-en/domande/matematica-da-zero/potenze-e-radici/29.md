Using the quotient rule, what number is obtained from $\dfrac{5^2}{5^5}$?

- $\dfrac{1}{125}$
- $125$
- $-125$
- $\dfrac{1}{15}$

## Explanation

The quotient rule gives $\dfrac{5^2}{5^5} = 5^{2-5} = 5^{-3} = \dfrac{1}{5^3} = \dfrac{1}{125}$. Canceling two factors of $5$ gives the same result because three factors remain in the denominator. The value $125$ interchanges the numerator and denominator, $-125$ adds a minus sign and $\dfrac{1}{15}$ multiplies the base by the exponent.
