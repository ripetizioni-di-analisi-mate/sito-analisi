Which of the following numbers is negative?

- $(-3)^5$
- $(-3)^4$
- $(-1)^{10}$
- $(-5)^2$

## Explanation

A power with a negative base is negative when its exponent is odd and positive when its exponent is even. The exponent $5$ is odd, so $(-3)^5 = -243$. The exponents $4$, $10$ and $2$ are even, so the other three numbers equal $81$, $1$ and $25$.
