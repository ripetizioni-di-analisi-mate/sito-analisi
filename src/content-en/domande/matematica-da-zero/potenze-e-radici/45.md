A hair is $0.00004$ m thick. What is its order of magnitude?

- $10^{-5}$ m
- $10^{-4}$ m
- $10^{-6}$ m
- $10^{5}$ m

## Explanation

In scientific notation, $0.00004 = 4\cdot10^{-5}$. The coefficient is $4 < 5$, so the order of magnitude is $10^{-5}$. The value $10^{-4}$ rounds up even though the threshold $5$ has not been reached, $10^{-6}$ moves the decimal point incorrectly and $10^5$ has the wrong sign for the exponent.
