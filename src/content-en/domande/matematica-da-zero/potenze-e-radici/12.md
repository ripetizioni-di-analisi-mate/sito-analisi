What number is obtained by evaluating $2^4\cdot5^4$?

- $100\,000\,000$
- $40$
- $10$
- $10\,000$

## Explanation

To multiply two powers with the same exponent, multiply the bases, so $2^4\cdot5^4 = (2\cdot5)^4 = 10^4 = 10\,000$. The value $100\,000\,000 = 10^8$ comes from adding the exponents, $40$ is the product $10\cdot4$ and $10$ ignores the exponent.
