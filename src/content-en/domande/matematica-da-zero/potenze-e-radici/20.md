Given $a \neq 0$ and $b \neq 0$, which expression is equal to $\dfrac{a^7 b^3}{a^4 b}$?

- $a^3b^3$
- $a^{11}b^4$
- $a^3b^2$
- $a^3b^4$

## Explanation

The quotient rule is applied to each base. For base $a$ we have $a^{7-4} = a^3$. For base $b$, since $b = b^1$, we have $b^{3-1} = b^2$. The result is $a^3b^2$. The result $a^3b^3$ ignores the $b$ in the denominator, while $a^{11}b^4$ adds all the exponents and $a^3b^4$ adds the exponents of $b$.
