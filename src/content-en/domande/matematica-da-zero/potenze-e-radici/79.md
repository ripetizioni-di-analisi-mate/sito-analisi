Using $\sqrt{3} \approx 1.73$ and rationalizing the denominator, what is the approximate value of $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$?

- $0.29$
- $5.19$
- $0.58$
- $1.73$

## Explanation

Rationalizing gives $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}} = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3} \approx \dfrac{1.73}{3} = 0.577$, approximately $0.58$. The value $0.29$ comes from dividing by $6$, $5.19$ from multiplying by $3$ and $1.73$ from leaving the root without dividing by $3$.
