Given that $2^{10} = 1\,024$, what is the value of $2^{12}$?

- $2\,048$
- $4\,096$
- $1\,026$
- $12\,288$

## Explanation

By the product rule for powers with the same base, $2^{12} = 2^{10}\cdot2^2$, so $2^{12} = 1\,024\cdot4 = 4\,096$. The value $2\,048$ is $2^{11}$ and multiplies by $2$ only once. The value $1\,026$ comes from adding $2$ instead of multiplying by $2^2$, and $12\,288$ from multiplying $1\,024$ by $12$.
