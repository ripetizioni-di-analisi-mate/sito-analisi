The fraction $\dfrac{49}{121}$ is the radicand of a square root. What is the root as a fraction?

- $\dfrac{7}{121}$
- $\dfrac{49}{11}$
- $\dfrac{49}{121}$
- $\dfrac{7}{11}$

## Explanation

The root of a quotient is the quotient of the roots, giving $\sqrt{\dfrac{49}{121}} = \dfrac{\sqrt{49}}{\sqrt{121}} = \dfrac{7}{11}$. The other answers apply the root to only one part or to neither. The value $\dfrac{7}{121}$ takes only the root of the numerator, $\dfrac{49}{11}$ only that of the denominator and $\dfrac{49}{121}$ neither.
