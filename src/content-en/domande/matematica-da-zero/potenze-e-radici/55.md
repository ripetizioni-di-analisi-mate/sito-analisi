What number is obtained by taking the square root of $2^4\cdot3^2\cdot5^2$?

- $30$
- $60$
- $3\,600$
- $120$

## Explanation

The root of a product is the product of the roots, and taking the square root of a power with an even exponent halves the exponent, giving $\sqrt{2^4\cdot3^2\cdot5^2} = 2^2\cdot3\cdot5 = 60$. The value $3\,600$ is the radicand without taking the root. The value $30$ omits squaring $2$, and $120$ leaves $2^3$.
