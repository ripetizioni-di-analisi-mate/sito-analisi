Calculate the quotient $\dfrac{24^3}{8^3}$.

- $3$
- $1\,728$
- $9$
- $27$

## Explanation

To divide two powers with the same exponent, divide the bases, giving $\dfrac{24^3}{8^3} = \left(\dfrac{24}{8}\right)^3 = 3^3 = 27$. The value $3$ ignores the exponent, $9$ replaces it with $2$ and $1\,728$ is the result of $\dfrac{24^3}{8}$, where the exponent applies only to the numerator.
