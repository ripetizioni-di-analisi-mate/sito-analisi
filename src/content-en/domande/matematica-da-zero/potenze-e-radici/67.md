Simplify $\sqrt{72}$ by taking a factor out of the radical.

- $6\sqrt{2}$
- $36\sqrt{2}$
- $12\sqrt{6}$
- $6\sqrt{3}$

## Explanation

Factor the radicand as $72 = 36\cdot2$, where $36$ is the largest perfect-square factor of $72$. Then $\sqrt{72} = \sqrt{36}\cdot\sqrt{2} = 6\sqrt{2}$. The result $36\sqrt{2}$ takes the square out without first finding its root, $12\sqrt{6}$ uses the factor $12$, which is not a square, and $6\sqrt{3}$ gives the wrong value for the quotient $72 : 36 = 2$.
