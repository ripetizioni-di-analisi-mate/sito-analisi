What is $\dfrac{4^3\cdot2^5}{16^2}$ equal to?

- $16$
- $32$
- $8$
- $2$

## Explanation

The bases are powers of $2$ because $4 = 2^2$ and $16 = 2^4$. Therefore $4^3 = 2^6$ and $16^2 = 2^8$, and the quotient becomes $\dfrac{2^6\cdot2^5}{2^8} = \dfrac{2^{11}}{2^8} = 2^3 = 8$. The other values correspond to a final exponent different from $3$.
