What is the square root of the decimal $0.0081$?

- $0.09$
- $0.9$
- $0.009$
- $0.0009$

## Explanation

Write $0.0081 = \dfrac{81}{10\,000}$, so the root is $\dfrac{9}{100} = 0.09$ because $0.09^2 = 0.0081$. The value $0.9$ has one decimal place too few, $0.009$ has one too many and $0.0009$ is $\dfrac{9}{10\,000}$, the square root of $81$ divided by $10\,000$ without halving the number of zeros.
