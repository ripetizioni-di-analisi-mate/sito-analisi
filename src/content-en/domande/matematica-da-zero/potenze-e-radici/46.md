Which of these numbers is greatest?

- $9.9\cdot10^{3}$
- $1\cdot10^{4}$
- $12\cdot10^{2}$
- $0.5\cdot10^{5}$

## Explanation

Written out in full, the numbers are $0.5\cdot10^5 = 50\,000$, $9.9\cdot10^3 = 9\,900$, $1\cdot10^4 = 10\,000$ and $12\cdot10^2 = 1\,200$. The greatest is $50\,000$. Its coefficient $0.5$ is the smallest, but its exponent $5$ makes it the greatest number.
