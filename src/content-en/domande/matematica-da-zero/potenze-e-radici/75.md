Which of the two numbers $3\sqrt{5}$ and $5\sqrt{2}$ is greater?

- $5\sqrt{2}$
- $3\sqrt{5}$
- They are equal
- It cannot be determined without a calculator

## Explanation

Bringing the factors inside the radicals gives $3\sqrt{5} = \sqrt{9\cdot5} = \sqrt{45}$ and $5\sqrt{2} = \sqrt{25\cdot2} = \sqrt{50}$. Since $45 < 50$, the greater number is $\sqrt{50} = 5\sqrt{2}$. Comparing only the coefficients and comparing only the radicands suggest opposite conclusions, so compare the radicands after bringing the factors inside.
