A cube-shaped tank holds $343$ dm$^3$ of water when full. What is its edge length?

- $7$ dm
- $49$ dm
- $114$ dm
- $1\,029$ dm

## Explanation

The volume of a cube is the cube of its edge length, so the edge length is the cube root of the volume, giving $\sqrt[3]{343} = 7$ dm because $7^3 = 343$. The value $49$ is $7^2$, the area of one face. The value $114$ divides $343$ by $3$, and $1\,029$ multiplies it by $3$.
