A length of $1$ m is halved $4$ times in succession, giving $2^{-4}$ m. What is that length in centimeters?

- $6.25$ cm
- $25$ cm
- $0.0625$ cm
- $16$ cm

## Explanation

The length is $2^{-4} = \dfrac{1}{2^4} = \dfrac{1}{16}$ m. Since $1$ m $= 100$ cm, the length is $\dfrac{100}{16} = 6.25$ cm. The value $25$ comes from dividing $100$ by $4$, $0.0625$ is the length in meters instead of centimeters and $16$ is the denominator.
