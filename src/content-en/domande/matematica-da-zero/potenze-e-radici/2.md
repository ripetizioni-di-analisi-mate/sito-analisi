In $3^5$, how many factors equal $3$, and what is the value of the power?

- $3$ factors, value $125$
- $5$ factors, value $15$
- $3$ factors, value $15$
- $5$ factors, value $243$

## Explanation

The exponent tells how many factors equal to the base appear, so $3^5$ contains $5$ factors equal to $3$ and equals $3\cdot3\cdot3\cdot3\cdot3 = 243$. The value $125$ corresponds to the power $5^3$, with the base and exponent interchanged. The value $15$ is the product $3\cdot5$ rather than a product of five factors.
