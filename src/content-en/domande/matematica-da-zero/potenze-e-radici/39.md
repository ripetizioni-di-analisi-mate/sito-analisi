Which decimal equals $6.03\cdot10^{-3}$?

- $0.00603$
- $0.0603$
- $6\,030$
- $0.000603$

## Explanation

Multiplying by $10^{-3}$ moves the decimal point $3$ places to the left, so $6.03$ becomes $0.00603$. Moving it $2$ places would give $0.0603$, and moving it $4$ places would give $0.000603$. The value $6\,030$ comes from using the exponent $+3$.
