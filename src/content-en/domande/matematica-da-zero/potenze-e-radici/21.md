When $2^{10}\cdot5^{9}$ is written as the digit $2$ followed by zeros, how many zeros are there?

- $19$
- $90$
- $9$
- $10$

## Explanation

Write $2^{10} = 2\cdot2^9$, so $2^{10}\cdot5^9 = 2\cdot2^9\cdot5^9$. Reading the power-of-a-product rule from right to left gives $2^9\cdot5^9 = (2\cdot5)^9 = 10^9$, and the number is $2\cdot10^9$. Since $10^9$ is the digit $1$ followed by $9$ zeros, the number is the digit $2$ followed by $9$ zeros. The value $10$ uses only the exponent of $2$, $19$ adds the exponents and $90$ multiplies them.
