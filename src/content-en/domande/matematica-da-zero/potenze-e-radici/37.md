How is $48\,000$ written in scientific notation?

- $4.8\cdot10^{4}$
- $4.8\cdot10^{3}$
- $4.8\cdot10^{5}$
- $4.8\cdot10^{-4}$

## Explanation

Move the decimal point to the left until a single nonzero digit remains before it. Starting from $48\,000$ gives $4.8$ after a shift of $4$ places. The exponent is therefore $4$ and $48\,000 = 4.8\cdot10^4$. The exponents $3$ and $5$ count one place too few or too many, and the exponent $-4$ has the wrong sign.
