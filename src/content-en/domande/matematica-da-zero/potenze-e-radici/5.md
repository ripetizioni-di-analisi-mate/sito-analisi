A fraction is cubed in the expression $\left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^3$. What is the result?

- $\dfrac{9}{4}$
- $\dfrac{27}{4}$
- $\dfrac{27}{64}$
- $\dfrac{3}{64}$

## Explanation

To raise a fraction to a power, raise both the numerator and denominator to that power, so $\left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^3 = \dfrac{3^3}{4^3} = \dfrac{27}{64}$. The result $\dfrac{9}{4}$ comes from multiplying only the numerator by $3$, $\dfrac{27}{4}$ raises only the numerator to the power and $\dfrac{3}{64}$ raises only the denominator to the power.
