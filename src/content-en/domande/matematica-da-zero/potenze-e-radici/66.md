The product of the roots $\sqrt{8}$ and $\sqrt{18}$ is a rational number. What is its value?

- $144$
- $\sqrt{26}$
- $6$
- $12$

## Explanation

The product of two roots is the root of the product, giving $\sqrt{8}\cdot\sqrt{18} = \sqrt{8\cdot18} = \sqrt{144} = 12$. The value $144$ ignores the root, $\sqrt{26}$ adds the radicands instead of multiplying them and $6$ is half the result.
