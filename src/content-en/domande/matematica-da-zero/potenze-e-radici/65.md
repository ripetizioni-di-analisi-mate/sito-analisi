Which number cubed equals $0.008$?

- $0.02$
- $0.08$
- $0.002$
- $0.2$

## Explanation

Write $0.008 = \dfrac{8}{1\,000}$, so the required number is the cube root $\dfrac{2}{10} = 0.2$ because $0.2^3 = 0.008$. The cube of $0.02$ is $0.000008$ and the cube of $0.08$ is $0.000512$, while $0.002$ has an even smaller cube.
