Calculate $\left(2^2\right)^3 : \left(2^3\right)^2$.

- $1$
- $0$
- $2$
- $64$

## Explanation

By the power-of-a-power rule, $\left(2^2\right)^3 = 2^6$ and $\left(2^3\right)^2 = 2^6$. The quotient is $2^6 : 2^6 = 2^{6-6} = 2^0 = 1$. The value $0$ overlooks the fact that a zero exponent gives $1$, the value $64$ is $2^6$ without the division, and $2$ is $2^1$.
