What is the smallest nonzero natural number $n$ for which $\sqrt[3]{12n}$ is an integer?

- $2$
- $18$
- $3$
- $6$

## Explanation

The cube root of a natural number is an integer when the number is a perfect cube, that is, when every exponent in its prime factorization is a multiple of $3$. Since $12 = 2^2 \cdot 3$, reaching $2^3$ requires one more factor of $2$, and reaching $3^3$ requires two more factors of $3$. Therefore the smallest value is $n = 2 \cdot 3^2 = 18$. Indeed $12 \cdot 18 = 216 = 2^3 \cdot 3^3$, so $\sqrt[3]{216} = 6$. The value $6$ comes from adding only one factor of $3$, but $12 \cdot 6 = 72 = 2^3 \cdot 3^2$ is not a perfect cube.
