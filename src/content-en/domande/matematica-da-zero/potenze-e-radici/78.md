What is the smallest positive integer $n$ for which $\sqrt[3]{12n}$ is an integer?

- $2$
- $18$
- $3$
- $6$

## Explanation

In $12=2^2\cdot3$ the prime exponents are not multiples of three. To complete a cube we still need one factor of $2$ and two factors of $3$. The minimum is $n=2\cdot3^2=18$, giving $12n=216=6^3$.
