Which numbers $x$ satisfy the equation $x^2 = 81$?

- Only $9$
- Only $-9$
- $9$ and $-9$
- No number

## Explanation

The square of $9$ is $81$ and the square of $-9$ is also $81$ because the product of two negative numbers is positive. The solutions are therefore $9$ and $-9$. The symbol $\sqrt{81}$ denotes only the positive number $9$, while the equation asks for all numbers whose square is $81$.
