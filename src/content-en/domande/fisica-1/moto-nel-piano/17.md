Between $t = 1.0\,\mathrm{s}$ and $t = 3.0\,\mathrm{s}$, what is the magnitude of the displacement of a point moving in the plane with position function $\vec{r}(t) = 4.0\,t\;\hat{\imath} + t^2\,\hat{\jmath}$, with $\vec{r}$ in meters and $t$ in seconds?

- $11.3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $16.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5.66\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The positions at the two instants are $\vec{r}(1.0\,\mathrm{s}) = (4.0;\ 1.0)\,\mathrm{m}$ and $\vec{r}(3.0\,\mathrm{s}) = (12;\ 9.0)\,\mathrm{m}$. The displacement is the final position minus the initial position, so $\Delta\vec{r} = (8.0;\ 8.0)\,\mathrm{m}$. Its magnitude is
$$
|\Delta\vec{r}| = \sqrt{8.0^2 + 8.0^2}\,\mathrm{m} = 11.3\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $16.0\,\mathrm{m}$ comes from adding the components of the displacement, which does not give the magnitude of the vector.
