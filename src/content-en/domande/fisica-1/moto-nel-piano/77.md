Two points on a merry-go-round take the same time to complete a full turn. The point under study is at distance $R$ from the center. Given the distance from the center $R = 6\,\mathrm{m}$ and the tangential speed $v = 3 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the period $T$?

- $\dfrac{2}{\pi}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

During one period the point travels a distance equal to the circumference, $2\pi R$. In the stated units, the relation is $v = \dfrac{2 \cdot \pi \cdot R}{T}$. Solving for the unknown gives $T = \dfrac{2 \cdot \pi \cdot R}{v}$. Substituting the given values yields $4\,\mathrm{s}$.
