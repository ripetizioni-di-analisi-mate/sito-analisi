A stone is thrown with initial velocity components $v_{0x} = 15\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_{0y} = 20\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Neglecting air resistance, what is the magnitude of the velocity at time $t = 1{,}0\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $18{,}1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $21{,}3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $15{,}2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10{,}2\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The horizontal component of the velocity remains constant, $v_x = 15\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The vertical component decreases by $9{,}81\,\mathrm{m/s}$ every second, therefore $v_y = v_{0y} - g\,t = (20 - 9{,}81)\,\mathrm{m/s} = 10{,}2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The magnitude is thus
$$
v = \sqrt{15^2 + 10{,}2^2}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 18{,}1\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
