A point starts from the origin at $t = 0$ and its velocity is $\vec{v}(t) = 3.0\,\hat{\imath} + (4.0 - 2.0\,t)\,\hat{\jmath}$, with $\vec{v}$ in $\mathrm{m/s}$ and $t$ in seconds. What is its position at $t = 2.0\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $(6.0;\ 0)\,\mathrm{m}$
- $(6.0;\ 4.0)\,\mathrm{m}$
- $(6.0;\ 8.0)\,\mathrm{m}$
- $(3.0;\ 0)\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The position is obtained by integrating the velocity component by component, starting from $\vec{r}(0) = \vec{0}$. For the $x$ component, $x(t) = 3.0\,t$, so $x(2.0\,\mathrm{s}) = 6.0\,\mathrm{m}$. For the $y$ component,
$$
y(t) = \int_0^t (4.0 - 2.0\,t')\,dt' = 4.0\,t - t^2 ,
$$
therefore $y(2.0\,\mathrm{s}) = 8.0\,\mathrm{m} - 4.0\,\mathrm{m} = 4.0\,\mathrm{m}$. The product $\vec{v}(2.0\,\mathrm{s})\cdot 2.0\,\mathrm{s} = (6.0;\ 0)\,\mathrm{m}$ would give the position only if the velocity were constant.
