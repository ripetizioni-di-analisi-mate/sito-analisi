An airplane flies at $200\,\mathrm{km/h}$ relative to the air and must reach a city located due north of the starting point. A uniform wind of $40\,\mathrm{km/h}$ blows toward the east. In which direction must the airplane head?

- $11.5^\circ$ east of north
- $78.5^\circ$ west of north
- $11.5^\circ$ west of north
- Due north

## Explanation

By **velocity addition** the velocity relative to the ground is $\vec{v} = \vec{v}\,' + \vec{V}$, where $\vec{v}\,'$ is the velocity relative to the air and $\vec{V}$ that of the wind. For $\vec{v}$ to point north, the westward component of $\vec{v}\,'$ must be equal in magnitude to the wind speed. Calling $\varphi$ the angle between $\vec{v}\,'$ and north, one therefore has $200\,\mathrm{km/h} \cdot \sin\varphi = 40\,\mathrm{km/h}$, that is, $\sin\varphi = 0.20$ and $\varphi = 11.5^\circ$. Hence the airplane heads $11.5^\circ$ west of north, toward the side from which the wind comes.
