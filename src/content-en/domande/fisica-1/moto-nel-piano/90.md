In planar motion, the initial $x$ velocity and the constant $y$ acceleration are positive. The remaining initial components are zero. Given the constant vertical acceleration $a = 2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, the elapsed time $t = 3\,\mathrm{s}$ and the tangent of the angle between the velocity and the positive $x$ axis, $q = \dfrac{3}{4}$, what is the horizontal speed $v$?

- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The tangent is the ratio $v_y/v_x$. In this motion $v_y=at$ and $v_x=v$. In the stated units, the relation is $q = \dfrac{a \cdot t}{v}$. Solving for the unknown gives $v = \dfrac{a \cdot t}{q}$. Substituting the given values yields $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
