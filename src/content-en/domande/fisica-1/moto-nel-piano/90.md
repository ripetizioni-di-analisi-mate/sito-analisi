A long flatcar moves on a straight track at $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$. A boy on the flatcar throws a ball at $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ relative to the flatcar, directed $60^\circ$ above the horizontal and backward, and the ball lands on the flatcar's deck. Neglecting air resistance, where does it land relative to the launch point on the flatcar?

- $8.83\,\mathrm{m}$ in front of the launch point
- $8.83\,\mathrm{m}$ behind the launch point
- $17.7\,\mathrm{m}$ behind the launch point
- At the launch point

## Explanation

The flatcar translates at constant velocity, so in its frame the ball has acceleration $\vec{g}$ and moves like a projectile launched at $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at $60^\circ$ backward. The time of flight is
$$
T = \frac{2 \cdot 10\sin 60^\circ}{9.81}\,\mathrm{s} = 1.77\,\mathrm{s} ,
$$
and in this time the ball moves relative to the flatcar by $10\cos 60^\circ \cdot T = 5.0 \cdot 1.77\,\mathrm{m} = 8.83\,\mathrm{m}$ backward. Relative to the track the ball advances at $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ while the flatcar advances at $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and the result is the same. The value $17.7\,\mathrm{m}$ is the displacement of the flatcar during the flight.
