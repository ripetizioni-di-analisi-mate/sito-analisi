A point moves in uniform circular motion on a circle centered at the origin with radius $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$, with angular velocity $3.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. At a certain instant its position vector makes an angle of $30^\circ$ with the $x$ axis. What is the acceleration vector at that instant?

- $(15.6;\ 9.0)\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $(-5.20;\ -3.0)\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $(-15.6;\ -9.0)\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $(4.5;\ -7.8)\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

In uniform circular motion with the origin at the center, the acceleration is $\vec{a} = -\omega^2\,\vec{r}$. The position vector is $\vec{r} = (2.0\cos 30^\circ;\ 2.0\sin 30^\circ)\,\mathrm{m} = (1.73;\ 1.0)\,\mathrm{m}$. Hence
$$
\vec{a} = -9.0 \cdot (1.73;\ 1.0)\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = (-15.6;\ -9.0)\,\mathrm{m/s^2} ,
$$
which has magnitude $\omega^2 R = 18\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and points toward the center. The vector with opposite signs has the same magnitude but points outward.
