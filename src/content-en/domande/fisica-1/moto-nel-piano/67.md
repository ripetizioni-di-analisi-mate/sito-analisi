A boat keeps its bow perpendicular to the parallel banks of a river. A uniform current runs parallel to the banks. The initial data are the river width $L = 48\,\mathrm{m}$, the current speed $u = 2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the boat's speed relative to the water $v = 6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Change the current speed to $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the ratio of the final value of $d$ to its initial value?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

The velocity across the river is $v$, so the crossing takes $L/v$. During that time the current carries the boat downstream at speed $u$. The relation $d = \dfrac{L \cdot u}{v}$ gives $16\,\mathrm{m}$ before the change and $24\,\mathrm{m}$ after it. The ratio is $\dfrac{3}{2}$.
