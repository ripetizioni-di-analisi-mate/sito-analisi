An object launched from the ground must land $50\,\mathrm{m}$ from the launch point, over level ground. Neglecting air resistance, what is the minimum launch speed that makes this possible?

- $22.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $15.7\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $31.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $26.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

For a given $v_0$ the range $R = \dfrac{v_0^2\sin 2\theta}{g}$ is maximum for $\theta = 45^\circ$, where $\sin 2\theta = 1$. Therefore the minimum speed is the one for which the **maximum range** $\dfrac{v_0^2}{g}$ equals $50\,\mathrm{m}$. It follows that $v_0 = \sqrt{g R} = \sqrt{9.81 \cdot 50}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 22.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$. With any other angle $\sin 2\theta < 1$, so reaching $50\,\mathrm{m}$ requires a greater speed.
