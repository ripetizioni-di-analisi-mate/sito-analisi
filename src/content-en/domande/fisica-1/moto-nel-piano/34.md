From the edge of a cliff $20\,\mathrm{m}$ high a stone is thrown with initial velocity of components $v_{0x} = 8.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_{0y} = 15\,\mathrm{m/s}$ upward. Neglecting air resistance and taking $g = 10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, after how much time does the stone hit the ground at the foot of the cliff?

- $3.0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

With the origin at the foot of the cliff the height of the stone is $y(t) = 20 + 15\,t - 5.0\,t^2$, in meters. The stone hits the ground when $y = 0$, that is, when $t^2 - 3t - 4 = 0$, which has the solutions $t = 4.0\,\mathrm{s}$ and $t = -1.0\,\mathrm{s}$. The time sought is the positive solution, therefore $t = 4.0\,\mathrm{s}$. The value $\dfrac{2\,v_{0y}}{g} = 3.0\,\mathrm{s}$ is the time at which the stone passes back through the launch height, and the formula for the time of flight does not apply because the landing point is lower.
