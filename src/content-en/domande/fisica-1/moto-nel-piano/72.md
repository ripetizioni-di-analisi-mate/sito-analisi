A point moves along a circle with constant angular acceleration, starting from rest. Given the trajectory radius $R = 4\,\mathrm{m}$ and the angular acceleration $\alpha = 3\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$, what is the tangential acceleration magnitude $a_{t}$?

- $6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $24\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Tangential speed is $R\omega$. Because $R$ is fixed, its time derivative is $R\alpha$. In the stated units, the relation is $a_{t} = R \cdot \alpha$. Substituting the given values yields $12\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
