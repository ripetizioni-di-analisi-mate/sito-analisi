A passenger on a train moving in uniform straight-line motion drops a coin. Neglecting air resistance, how does an observer standing on the platform describe the motion of the coin?

- A vertical segment, traveled with acceleration $\vec{g}$
- An arc of a parabola, traveled with acceleration $\vec{g}$
- An arc of a parabola, traveled with an acceleration that also has a horizontal component
- An oblique segment, traveled with acceleration $\vec{g}$

## Explanation

In the frame of the train car the coin starts from rest and falls along a vertical segment with acceleration $\vec{g}$. The train car translates with constant velocity $\vec{V}$ relative to the platform, therefore by **velocity addition** the coin starts with horizontal velocity $\vec{V}$ and for the observer on the platform it describes an arc of a parabola, like a body launched horizontally. Since $\vec{V}$ is constant, the accelerations measured in the two frames are equal, $\vec{a} = \vec{a}\,'$, so on the platform too the acceleration is $\vec{g}$.
