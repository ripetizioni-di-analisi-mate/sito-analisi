A cyclist follows a circular bend to the left and is braking at that instant. Which directions do the tangential and normal acceleration components point?

- The tangential component points along velocity and the normal component points toward the center of the bend.
- The tangential component opposes velocity and the normal component points toward the center of the bend.
- The tangential component opposes velocity and the normal component points outward from the bend.
- The tangential component is zero and the normal component points toward the center of the bend.

## Explanation

Braking decreases the speed, so the tangential component points against the motion. The normal component has magnitude $v^2/R$ and points toward the center because the velocity changes direction along the bend.
