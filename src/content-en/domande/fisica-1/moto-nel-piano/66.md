A non-slipping belt connects two pulleys of radii $5.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $15\,\mathrm{cm}$. The small pulley turns at $300$ revolutions per minute. How many revolutions per minute does the large pulley make?

- $900$ revolutions per minute
- $100$ revolutions per minute
- $300$ revolutions per minute
- $33.3$ revolutions per minute

## Explanation

The belt does not slip, so the points on the rims of the two pulleys have the same speed as the belt. Therefore $\omega_1 R_1 = \omega_2 R_2$, and the same holds for the frequencies, $f_1 R_1 = f_2 R_2$. It follows that
$$
f_2 = f_1\,\frac{R_1}{R_2} = 300 \cdot \frac{5.0}{15}\ \text{revolutions per minute} = 100\ \text{revolutions per minute} .
$$
The larger pulley therefore turns more slowly; the value $900$ comes from inverting the ratio of the radii.
