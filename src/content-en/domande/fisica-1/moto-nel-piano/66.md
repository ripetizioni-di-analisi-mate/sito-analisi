A boat keeps its bow perpendicular to the parallel banks of a river. A uniform current runs parallel to the banks. Given the river width $L = 48\,\mathrm{m}$, the boat's speed relative to the water $v = 6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the downstream drift $d = 16\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the current speed $u$?

- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The velocity across the river is $v$, so the crossing takes $L/v$. During that time the current carries the boat downstream at speed $u$. In the stated units, the relation is $d = \dfrac{L \cdot u}{v}$. Solving for the unknown gives $u = \dfrac{d \cdot v}{L}$. Substituting the given values yields $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
