A package is released by a drone flying at constant horizontal speed. Take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and neglect air resistance. Given the drone's speed $v = 8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the release height $h = 20\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the horizontal distance covered by the package before impact $d$?

- $16\,\mathrm{m}$
- $32\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8 \cdot \sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Vertically the package falls from rest, so $h=gt^2/2$. Horizontally it retains its velocity $v$. In the stated units, the relation is $d = \dfrac{\sqrt{5} \cdot \sqrt{h} \cdot v}{5}$. Substituting the given values yields $16\,\mathrm{m}$.
