A stone is thrown horizontally with velocity $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ from a wall $5.0\,\mathrm{m}$ high. Neglecting air resistance, what angle does the velocity of the stone make with the horizontal when it hits the ground?

- $63.2^\circ$
- $26.8^\circ$
- $44.7^\circ$
- $54.5^\circ$

## Explanation

The vertical motion starts with zero velocity and the stone descends by $h = 5.0\,\mathrm{m}$, therefore at impact $|v_y| = \sqrt{2gh} = \sqrt{98.1}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 9.90\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The horizontal component remains $v_x = 5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The angle $\beta$ of the velocity below the horizontal thus satisfies
$$
\tan\beta = \frac{|v_y|}{v_x} = \frac{9.90}{5.0} = 1.98 ,
$$
hence $\beta = 63.2^\circ$. The displacement from the launch point to the landing point makes a different angle with the horizontal, about $45^\circ$, because the trajectory is curved.
