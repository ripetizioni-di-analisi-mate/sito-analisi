## Explanation

At the highest point the velocity is horizontal and equals $v_{0x} = v_0\cos\theta = 15 \cdot \cos 60^\circ\,\mathrm{m/s} = 7.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The acceleration $g$ is vertical, therefore it is perpendicular to the velocity and entirely centripetal, $a_n = g$. From the relation $a_n = \dfrac{v^2}{\rho}$ one thus obtains the **radius of curvature**
$$
\rho = \frac{v_{0x}^2}{g} = \frac{7.5^2}{9.81}\,\mathrm{m} = 5.73\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
