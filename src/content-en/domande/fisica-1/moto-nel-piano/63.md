The platter of a hard disk spins at $7200$ revolutions per minute with constant angular velocity. What is the period of rotation?

- $0.139\,\mathrm{ms}$
- $0.873\,\mathrm{ms}$
- $8.33\,\mathrm{ms}$
- $52.4\,\mathrm{ms}$

## Explanation

The frequency is the number of revolutions per second, so $f = \dfrac{7200}{60\,\mathrm{s}} = 120\,\mathrm{Hz}$. The period is the reciprocal of the frequency, therefore
$$
T = \frac{1}{f} = \frac{1}{120}\,\mathrm{s} = 8.33 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{s} = 8.33\,\mathrm{ms} .
$$
The value $0.139\,\mathrm{ms}$ comes from dividing $1\,\mathrm{s}$ by $7200$, that is, from treating revolutions per minute as revolutions per second.
