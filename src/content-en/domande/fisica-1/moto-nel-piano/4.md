From a balcony $10\,\mathrm{m}$ high a stone is thrown with velocity $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ directed $45^\circ$ above the horizontal. Neglecting air resistance, with what speed does the stone hit the ground?

- $24{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $14{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $17{,}2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

By the relation for **speed as a function of height**, the magnitude of the velocity depends only on the drop in height and not on the direction of the launch. The stone descends by $h = 10\,\mathrm{m}$, therefore $y - y_0 = -h$ and
$$
v = \sqrt{v_0^2 + 2g\,h} = \sqrt{100 + 2 \cdot 9{,}81 \cdot 10}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 17{,}2\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The speed is again $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ when the stone passes back through the height of the balcony, and from there on it increases.
