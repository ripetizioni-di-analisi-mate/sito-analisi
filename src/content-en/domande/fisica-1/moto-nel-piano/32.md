From a terrace three stones are thrown with velocities of the same magnitude, one upward at $40^\circ$, one horizontally and one downward at $40^\circ$. Neglecting air resistance, which statement is true when the stones hit the ground?

- The stone thrown downward has the velocity of greatest magnitude
- Their velocities have the same magnitude
- The stone thrown upward has the velocity of greatest magnitude
- They hit the ground at the same instant

## Explanation

By the relation for **speed as a function of height**, at height $y$ the magnitude of the velocity is $v = \sqrt{v_0^2 - 2g\,(y - y_0)}$, whatever the direction of the launch. The three stones start with the same $v_0$ and at the ground have undergone the same drop in height, therefore their velocities at impact have the same magnitude. What differs is the direction of the velocity at impact and the duration of the flight, which is greatest for the stone thrown upward.
