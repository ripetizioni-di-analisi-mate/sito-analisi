A point on a uniformly rotating disk turns through less than one revolution. Time is measured in seconds and angle in radians. Given the angular speed $\omega = 1\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ and the angle swept out $\theta = 2\,\mathrm{rad}$, what is the elapsed time $t$?

- $2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

In uniform circular motion the angle increases linearly with time, with slope $\omega$. In the stated units, the relation is $\theta = \omega \cdot t$. Solving for the unknown gives $t = \dfrac{\theta}{\omega}$. Substituting the given values yields $2\,\mathrm{s}$.
