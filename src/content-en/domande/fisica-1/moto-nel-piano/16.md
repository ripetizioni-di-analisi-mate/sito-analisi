A point travels with constant speed along half of a circle of radius $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$, from one end of a diameter to the other, in $4.0\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the magnitude of the average velocity over this interval?

- $1.6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.50\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The average velocity is the ratio of the displacement to the time interval. The point starts from one end of the diameter and arrives at the other, therefore the displacement has magnitude $2R = 4.0\,\mathrm{m}$. Hence $|\vec{v}_m| = \dfrac{4.0}{4.0}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 1.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The speed, computed with the arc traveled $\pi R = 6.28\,\mathrm{m}$, is $1.57\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and is greater, because the displacement is shorter than the arc.
