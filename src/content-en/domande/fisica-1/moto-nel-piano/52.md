A projectile lands at its launch height. Its initial vertical velocity component is $u$, and take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Air resistance is negligible. The initial data are the initial horizontal velocity component $v = 12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the initial vertical velocity component $u = 15\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Change the initial vertical velocity component to $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the ratio of the final value of $R$ to its initial value?

- $1$
- $\dfrac{8}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$

## Explanation

The flight time is twice the time $u/g$ required to reach the apex. The horizontal velocity stays equal to $v$. The relation $R = \dfrac{u \cdot v}{5}$ gives $36\,\mathrm{m}$ before the change and $48\,\mathrm{m}$ after it. The ratio is $\dfrac{4}{3}$.
