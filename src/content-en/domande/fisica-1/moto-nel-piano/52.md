What is the maximum height above the ground reached by a ball thrown at $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$ inclined $40^\circ$ upward from the edge of a $12\,\mathrm{m}$ high roof? Neglect air resistance.

- $4.74\,\mathrm{m}$
- $23.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $18.7\,\mathrm{m}$
- $16.7\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

At the highest point the vertical component of the velocity is zero. By the time-independent relation applied to the vertical motion, the ball rises above the roof by
$$
\frac{v_{0y}^2}{2g} = \frac{(15\sin 40^\circ)^2}{2 \cdot 9.81}\,\mathrm{m} = \frac{9.64^2}{19.62}\,\mathrm{m} = 4.74\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The maximum height above the ground is therefore $12\,\mathrm{m} + 4.74\,\mathrm{m} = 16.7\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $23.5\,\mathrm{m}$ comes from using the whole speed $v_0$ instead of its vertical component.
