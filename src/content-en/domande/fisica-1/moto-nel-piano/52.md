A ball crosses the same horizontal observation level at $t=1\,\mathrm{s}$ and $t=5\,\mathrm{s}$ after launch. How far above that level is its highest point? Ignore air resistance and take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

- $5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $40\,\mathrm{m}$
- $80\,\mathrm{m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Height is symmetric about the top of the arc, putting the highest point at $t=3\,\mathrm{s}$. In the following two seconds the initially zero vertical velocity produces a fall of $\dfrac12 g(2\,\mathrm{s})^2=20\,\mathrm m$.
