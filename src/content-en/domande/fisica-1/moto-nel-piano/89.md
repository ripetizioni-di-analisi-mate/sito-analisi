In planar motion, the initial $x$ velocity and the constant $y$ acceleration are positive. The remaining initial components are zero. Given the horizontal speed $v = 8\,\mathrm{m/s}$, the elapsed time $t = 3\,\mathrm{s}$ and the tangent of the angle between the velocity and the positive $x$ axis, $q = \dfrac{3}{4}$, what is the constant vertical acceleration $a$?

- $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $\dfrac{32}{9}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The tangent is the ratio $v_y/v_x$. In this motion $v_y=at$ and $v_x=v$. In the stated units, the relation is $q = \dfrac{a \cdot t}{v}$. Solving for the unknown gives $a = \dfrac{q \cdot v}{t}$. Substituting the given values yields $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
