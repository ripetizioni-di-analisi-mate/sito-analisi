A ball is kicked with velocity $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$ inclined at $30^\circ$ above the horizontal. Neglecting air resistance, what are the velocity and the acceleration of the ball at the highest point of the trajectory?

- $v = 0$, $a = g$ downward
- $v = 17.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ horizontal, $a = 0$
- $v = 17.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ horizontal, $a = g$ downward
- $v = 0$, $a = 0$

## Explanation

The horizontal component of the velocity remains constant throughout the flight and equals $v_0\cos 30^\circ = 20 \cdot 0.866\,\mathrm{m/s} = 17.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. At the highest point only the vertical component vanishes, therefore the velocity is horizontal and has magnitude $17.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The acceleration is $\vec{a} = -g\,\hat{\jmath}$ at every instant of the flight, so at the highest point too it has magnitude $g$ and points downward.
