A ball leaves the edge of a table horizontally. Air resistance is negligible and the ground is horizontal. Given the initial horizontal speed $v = 6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the fall time $t = \dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{s}$, what is the horizontal distance from the edge to impact $d$?

- $\dfrac{27}{5}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{18}{5}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{9}{5}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{36}{5}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Without air resistance the acceleration is vertical, so the horizontal velocity remains constant. In the stated units, the relation is $d = t \cdot v$. Substituting the given values yields $\dfrac{18}{5}\,\mathrm{m}$.
