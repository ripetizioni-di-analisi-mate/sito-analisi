A dart is thrown horizontally at $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ toward the center of a dartboard located $2.4\,\mathrm{m}$ away horizontally. Neglecting air resistance, how far below the center of the dartboard does it hit?

- $39.2\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $19.6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $4.91\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $98.1\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The horizontal component of the velocity stays $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$, so the dart reaches the board after $t = \dfrac{2.4\,\mathrm{m}}{12\,\mathrm{m/s}} = 0.20\,\mathrm{s}$. Vertically it starts with zero velocity and falls with uniformly accelerated motion, therefore in that time it drops by
$$
\Delta y = \frac{1}{2}\,g\,t^2 = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 9.81 \cdot 0.20^2\,\mathrm{m} = 0.196\,\mathrm{m} = 19.6\,\mathrm{cm} .
$$
The value $39.2\,\mathrm{cm}$ comes from forgetting the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
