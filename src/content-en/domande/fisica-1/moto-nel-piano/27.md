Two stones are thrown from the same point of level ground with velocities of the same magnitude, one at $25^\circ$ and the other at $65^\circ$ above the horizontal. Neglecting air resistance, which statement is true?

- They have the same range and the same time of flight
- They have the same range, and the launch at $65^\circ$ lasts longer
- The launch at $65^\circ$ has the greater range
- They have the same range and the same maximum height

## Explanation

The range $R = \dfrac{v_0^2\sin 2\theta}{g}$ depends on the angle through $\sin 2\theta$, and $\sin 50^\circ = \sin 130^\circ$. Therefore the two angles, which are complementary, give the **same range**. The time of flight $T = \dfrac{2\,v_0\sin\theta}{g}$ is proportional to $\sin\theta$, which is greater for $65^\circ$, so the launch at $65^\circ$ stays in flight longer. For the same reason the launch at $65^\circ$ also reaches a greater maximum height.
