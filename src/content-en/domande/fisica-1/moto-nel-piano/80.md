An escalator $30\,\mathrm{m}$ long moves at $0.75\,\mathrm{m/s}$. A person walks on the steps in the direction of motion of the escalator, at $1.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ relative to the steps. How long does it take to cover the escalator?

- $30.0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $40.0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $120\,\mathrm{s}$
- $17.1\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

By the composition of velocities, the person's velocity relative to the building is the sum of the velocity relative to the steps and the escalator's transport velocity. The two vectors have the same direction, so $v = 1.0\,\mathrm{m/s} + 0.75\,\mathrm{m/s} = 1.75\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Therefore the time is
$$
t = \frac{30\,\mathrm{m}}{1.75\,\mathrm{m/s}} = 17.1\,\mathrm{s} .
$$
The value $120\,\mathrm{s}$ comes from subtracting the velocities, as if the person walked against the motion of the escalator.
