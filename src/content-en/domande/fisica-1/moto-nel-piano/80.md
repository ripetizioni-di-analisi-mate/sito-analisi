A target moves in a plane with initial velocity along the $x$ axis and constant acceleration along the $y$ axis. The initial velocity and acceleration point along the positive axes. Given the vertical acceleration $a = 6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and the elapsed time $t = 3\,\mathrm{s}$, what is the vertical displacement $y$?

- $\dfrac{81}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $54\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{27}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $27\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The initial vertical velocity is zero, so only the quadratic term of the $y$ position equation contributes. In the stated units, the relation is $y = \dfrac{a \cdot t^{2}}{2}$. Substituting the given values yields $27\,\mathrm{m}$.
