A wheel spinning at $30\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ comes to rest in $10\,\mathrm{s}$ with constant angular acceleration. How many revolutions does it complete while braking?

- $23.9$ revolutions
- $47.7$ revolutions
- $150$ revolutions
- $942$ revolutions

## Explanation

The angular acceleration is $\alpha = \dfrac{0 - 30}{10}\,\mathrm{rad/s^2} = -3.0\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$. By the **uniformly accelerated circular motion** the angle swept is therefore
$$
\Delta\theta = \omega_0\,t + \frac{1}{2}\,\alpha\,t^2 = (300 - 150)\,\mathrm{rad} = 150\,\mathrm{rad} .
$$
One revolution corresponds to $2\pi\,\mathrm{rad}$, so the number of revolutions is $\dfrac{150}{2\pi} = 23.9$.
