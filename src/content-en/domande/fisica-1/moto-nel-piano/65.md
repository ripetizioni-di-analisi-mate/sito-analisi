A boat points perpendicular to the parallel banks of a river. A uniform current runs parallel to the banks. Given the current speed $u = 2\,\mathrm{m/s}$, the boat's speed relative to the water $v = 6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the downstream drift $d = 16\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the river width $L$?

- $64\,\mathrm{m}$
- $96\,\mathrm{m}$
- $48\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{16}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The velocity across the river is $v$, so the crossing takes $L/v$. During that time the current carries the boat downstream at speed $u$. In the stated units, the relation is $d = \dfrac{L \cdot u}{v}$. Solving for the unknown gives $L = \dfrac{d \cdot v}{u}$. Substituting the given values yields $48\,\mathrm{m}$.
