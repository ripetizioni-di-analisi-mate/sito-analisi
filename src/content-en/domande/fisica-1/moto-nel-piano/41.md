A car travels around a circular roundabout and increases the magnitude of its velocity. Which statement about its acceleration is true?

- It points toward the center of the roundabout
- It is tangent to the trajectory, in the direction of motion
- It has one component in the direction of motion and one toward the center
- It has one component in the direction of motion and one toward the outside

## Explanation

The acceleration splits into a tangential component $a_t = \dfrac{dv}{dt}$ and a centripetal component $a_n = \dfrac{v^2}{R}$. The magnitude of the velocity increases, therefore $a_t > 0$ and the tangential component points in the direction of motion. The centripetal component is positive because $v \neq 0$, and it points toward the center of the roundabout. Hence the acceleration makes an acute angle with the velocity.
