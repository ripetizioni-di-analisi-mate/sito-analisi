## Explanation

The vertical motion starts with zero vertical velocity, therefore the fall time from $h = 1.25\,\mathrm{m}$ is
$$
t_c = \sqrt{\frac{2h}{g}} = \sqrt{\frac{2 \cdot 1.25}{9.81}}\,\mathrm{s} = 0.505\,\mathrm{s} .
$$
Horizontally the motion is uniform straight-line motion, so the distance traveled is $d = v_0\,t_c$. It follows that $v_0 = \dfrac{d}{t_c} = \dfrac{1.50}{0.505}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 2.97\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
