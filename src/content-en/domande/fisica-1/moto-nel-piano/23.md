A projectile is launched from the ground with velocity $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$ inclined at $45^\circ$ above the horizontal. Neglecting air resistance, at what height does it pass over a vertical wall $20\,\mathrm{m}$ from the launch point?

- $15.1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.380\,\mathrm{m}$
- $29.8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10.2\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The equation of the **parabolic trajectory** gives the height as a function of the horizontal distance, $y = \tan\theta\; x - \dfrac{g}{2\,v_0^2\cos^2\theta}\; x^2$. With $\tan 45^\circ = 1$ and $\cos^2 45^\circ = \dfrac{1}{2}$ one has $2\,v_0^2\cos^2\theta = 400\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}$. Therefore
$$
y = 20\,\mathrm{m} - \frac{9.81 \cdot 20^2}{400}\,\mathrm{m} = (20 - 9.81)\,\mathrm{m} = 10.2\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
