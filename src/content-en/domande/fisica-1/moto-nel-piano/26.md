A stone is thrown from the ground with velocity $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$ inclined at $30^\circ$ above the horizontal. Neglecting air resistance, what is the maximum height reached?

- $5{,}10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10{,}2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $15{,}3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $20{,}4\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The maximum height depends only on the vertical component of the initial velocity, which is $v_{0y} = v_0\sin 30^\circ = 10\,\mathrm{m/s}$. By the formula for the **maximum height** one therefore has
$$
H = \frac{v_{0y}^2}{2g} = \frac{10^2}{2 \cdot 9{,}81}\,\mathrm{m} = 5{,}10\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $\dfrac{v_0^2}{2g} = 20{,}4\,\mathrm{m}$ is the height that would be reached with the same $v_0$ in a vertical launch.
