A point moves in the plane with equation of motion $\vec{r}(t) = t^3\,\hat{\imath} + 4{,}0\,t\,\hat{\jmath}$, with $\vec{r}$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. What is the magnitude of the velocity at time $t = 1{,}0\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $7{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4{,}1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The velocity is the derivative of the position vector, computed component by component, therefore $\vec{v}(t) = 3t^2\,\hat{\imath} + 4{,}0\,\hat{\jmath}$. At time $t = 1{,}0\,\mathrm{s}$ one has $v_x = 3{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_y = 4{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The magnitude is thus $v = \sqrt{3{,}0^2 + 4{,}0^2}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 5{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
