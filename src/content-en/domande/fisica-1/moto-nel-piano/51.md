A projectile lands at its launch height. Its initial vertical velocity component is $u$, and take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Air resistance is negligible. Given the initial horizontal velocity component $v = 12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the range $R = 36\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the initial vertical velocity component $u$?

- $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac{15}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac{45}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The flight time is twice the time $u/g$ required to reach the apex. The horizontal velocity stays equal to $v$. In the stated units, the relation is $R = \dfrac{u \cdot v}{5}$. Solving for the unknown gives $u = \dfrac{5 \cdot R}{v}$. Substituting the given values yields $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
