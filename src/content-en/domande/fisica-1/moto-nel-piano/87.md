A car travels at $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$ on a straight road, and its wheels roll without slipping, so that the point of the tire in contact with the road is at rest relative to the road. What is the speed, relative to the road, of the highest point of the tire?

- $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0$
- $40\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $28.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

In the frame of the car, which translates at $V = 20\,\mathrm{m/s}$, the wheel spins about its axle and the points of the rim have speed $\omega R$. The point in contact with the road has zero velocity relative to the road, so by the composition of velocities $\omega R - V = 0$, that is, in the frame of the car that point moves backward at $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The highest point then moves forward at $\omega R = 20\,\mathrm{m/s}$ relative to the car, and relative to the road its speed is
$$
v = \omega R + V = 20\,\mathrm{m/s} + 20\,\mathrm{m/s} = 40\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
