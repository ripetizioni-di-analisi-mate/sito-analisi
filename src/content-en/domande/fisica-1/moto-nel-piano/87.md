A package is released by a drone flying at constant horizontal speed. Take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and neglect air resistance. The initial data are the drone's speed $v = 8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the release height $h = 20\,\mathrm{m}$. Change the drone's speed to $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the release height to $5\,\mathrm{m}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the final value of $d$ minus its initial value?

- $-4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $- 2 \cdot \sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Vertically the package falls from rest, so $h=gt^2/2$. Horizontally it retains its velocity $v$. The relation $d = \dfrac{\sqrt{5} \cdot \sqrt{h} \cdot v}{5}$ gives $16\,\mathrm{m}$ before the change and $12\,\mathrm{m}$ after it. Subtracting the initial value from the final value gives $-4\,\mathrm{m}$.
