Two points on a merry-go-round take the same time to complete a full turn. The point under study is at distance $R$ from the center. Given the period $T = 4\,\mathrm{s}$ and the tangential speed $v = 3 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the distance from the center $R$?

- $12 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

During one period the point travels a distance equal to the circumference, $2\pi R$. In the stated units, the relation is $v = \dfrac{2 \cdot \pi \cdot R}{T}$. Solving for the unknown gives $R = \dfrac{T \cdot v}{2 \cdot \pi}$. Substituting the given values yields $6\,\mathrm{m}$.
