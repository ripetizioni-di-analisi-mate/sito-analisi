At a certain instant a point moving along a curved path has a velocity of magnitude $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and an acceleration of magnitude $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, and the two vectors make an angle of $30^\circ$. What is the radius of curvature of the trajectory at that point?

- $20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $23.1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $40\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The component of the acceleration perpendicular to the velocity is the normal component, which is
$$
a_n = a\sin 30^\circ = 5.0 \cdot 0.50\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 2.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
Since $a_n = \dfrac{v^2}{\rho}$, the radius of curvature is $\rho = \dfrac{v^2}{a_n} = \dfrac{100}{2.5}\,\mathrm{m} = 40\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $20\,\mathrm{m}$ comes from using the whole acceleration instead of its normal component, and $23.1\,\mathrm{m}$ from using the tangential component $a\cos 30^\circ$.
