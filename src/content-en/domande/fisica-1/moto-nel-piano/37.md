A point moves in uniform circular motion on a circle of radius $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ with centripetal acceleration $8.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is its speed?

- $16\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5.7\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

In uniform circular motion the centripetal acceleration is $a_n = \dfrac{v^2}{R}$. Therefore $v^2 = a_n R = 8.0 \cdot 2.0\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2} = 16\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}$, and so $v = 4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The corresponding angular velocity is $\omega = \dfrac{v}{R} = 2.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
