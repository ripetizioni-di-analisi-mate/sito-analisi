A stone is thrown horizontally at $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ from the edge of a cliff, with the $x$ axis horizontal in the direction of the throw and the $y$ axis vertical and pointing up. Neglecting air resistance, what is its velocity $2.0\,\mathrm{s}$ after the throw, before it hits the water?

- $(5.0;\ -9.81)\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $(0;\ -19.6)\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $(5.0;\ 19.6)\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $(5.0;\ -19.6)\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

In a horizontal launch the acceleration is $\vec{a} = -g\,\hat{\jmath}$, so the horizontal component of the velocity stays equal to its initial value, $v_x = 5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The vertical component starts from zero and decreases by $9.81\,\mathrm{m/s}$ every second, therefore after $2.0\,\mathrm{s}$ it is
$$
v_y = -g\,t = -9.81 \cdot 2.0\,\mathrm{m/s} = -19.6\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The negative sign indicates that the vertical component points downward.
