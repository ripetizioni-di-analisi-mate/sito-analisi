A ball leaves the edge of a table horizontally. Air resistance is negligible and the ground is horizontal. Given the initial horizontal speed $v = 6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the horizontal distance from the edge to impact $d = \dfrac{18}{5}\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the fall time $t$?

- $\dfrac{6}{5}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $\dfrac{3}{10}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $\dfrac{9}{10}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Without air resistance the acceleration is vertical, so the horizontal velocity remains constant. In the stated units, the relation is $d = t \cdot v$. Solving for the unknown gives $t = \dfrac{d}{v}$. Substituting the given values yields $\dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{s}$.
