A ball kicked from the ground reaches a maximum height of $6.0\,\mathrm{m}$ and lands $16\,\mathrm{m}$ from the launch point, on level ground. Neglecting air resistance, at what angle above the horizontal was it kicked?

- $20.6^\circ$
- $56.3^\circ$
- $36.9^\circ$
- $71.6^\circ$

## Explanation

Dividing the maximum height by the range gives
$$
\frac{H}{R} = \frac{v_0^2\sin^2\theta}{2g} \cdot \frac{g}{2\,v_0^2\sin\theta\cos\theta} = \frac{\tan\theta}{4} .
$$
Hence $\tan\theta = \dfrac{4H}{R} = \dfrac{4 \cdot 6.0}{16} = 1.5$, therefore $\theta = 56.3^\circ$. The ratio does not depend on $v_0$, so the angle is found without knowing the launch speed. The value $20.6^\circ$ corresponds to $\tan\theta = \dfrac{H}{R}$, which omits the factor $4$.
