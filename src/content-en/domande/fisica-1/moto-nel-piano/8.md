## Explanation

The speed is constant, therefore the tangential acceleration is zero and the acceleration is entirely **centripetal**. In SI units the speed is $v = \dfrac{72}{3{,}6}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 20\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Hence
$$
a_n = \frac{v^2}{R} = \frac{20^2}{60}\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 6{,}67\,\mathrm{m/s^2} ,
$$
and the acceleration points toward the center of the curve.
