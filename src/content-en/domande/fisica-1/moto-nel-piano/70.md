A point on a uniformly rotating disk turns through less than one revolution. Time is measured in seconds and angle in radians. Given the elapsed time $t = 2\,\mathrm{s}$ and the angle swept out $\theta = 2\,\mathrm{rad}$, what is the angular speed $\omega$?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

In uniform circular motion the angle increases linearly with time, with slope $\omega$. In the stated units, the relation is $\theta = \omega \cdot t$. Solving for the unknown gives $\omega = \dfrac{\theta}{t}$. Substituting the given values yields $1\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
