In which direction, and with what period, does a point travel around its circle if its position is $\vec{r}(t) = 2.0\sin(\pi t)\;\hat{\imath} + 2.0\cos(\pi t)\;\hat{\jmath}$, with $\vec{r}$ in meters and $t$ in seconds?

- Clockwise, with period $2.0\,\mathrm{s}$
- Counterclockwise, with period $2.0\,\mathrm{s}$
- Clockwise, with period $1.0\,\mathrm{s}$
- Counterclockwise, with period $6.28\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Since $x^2 + y^2 = 4.0\,\mathrm{m^2}$ at every instant, the point moves on the circle centered at the origin with radius $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$. At $t = 0$ it is at $(0;\ 2.0)$ and its velocity is $\vec{v}(0) = 2.0\pi\,\hat{\imath}\ \mathrm{m/s}$; hence from the top of the circle it moves toward positive $x$, that is, **clockwise**. The argument of the trigonometric functions increases by $2\pi$ when $\pi t$ increases by $2\pi$, therefore the period is
$$
T = \frac{2\pi}{\pi\ \mathrm{rad/s}} = 2.0\,\mathrm{s} .
$$
