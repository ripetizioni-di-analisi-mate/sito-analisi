A point on a uniformly rotating disk turns through less than one revolution. Time is measured in seconds and angle in radians. Given the angular speed $\omega = 1\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ and the elapsed time $t = 2\,\mathrm{s}$, what is the angle swept out $\theta$?

- $3\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $1\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $2\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $4\,\mathrm{rad}$

## Explanation

In uniform circular motion the angle increases linearly with time, with slope $\omega$. In the stated units, the relation is $\theta = \omega \cdot t$. Substituting the given values yields $2\,\mathrm{rad}$.
