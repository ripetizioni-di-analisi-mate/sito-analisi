After how much time does the velocity of a projectile become perpendicular to the launch velocity, if the projectile leaves the ground at $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$ inclined $60^\circ$ above the horizontal? Neglect air resistance.

- $2.35\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3.53\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.77\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4.08\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

By the projectile equations of motion, $\vec{v}(t) = \vec{v}_0 - g\,t\,\hat{\jmath}$. The velocity is perpendicular to $\vec{v}_0$ when $\vec{v}\cdot\vec{v}_0 = 0$, that is, when $v_0^2 - g\,t\,v_0\sin\theta = 0$. Hence
$$
t = \frac{v_0}{g\sin\theta} = \frac{20}{9.81 \cdot 0.866}\,\mathrm{s} = 2.35\,\mathrm{s} .
$$
The time of flight is $\dfrac{2 \cdot 20 \cdot 0.866}{9.81}\,\mathrm{s} = 3.53\,\mathrm{s}$, therefore the instant found falls during the descent, while the projectile is still in flight.
