A cyclist rides along a circular bend at constant speed. Given the speed $v = 12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the magnitude of the centripetal acceleration $a = 8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the radius of curvature $R$?

- $9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m}$
- $36\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Acceleration points toward the center. Its magnitude is the square of speed divided by the radius. In the stated units, the relation is $a = \dfrac{v^{2}}{R}$. Solving for the unknown gives $R = \dfrac{v^{2}}{a}$. Substituting the given values yields $18\,\mathrm{m}$.
