The International Space Station travels in an orbit that can be considered circular, with radius $6{,}78 \cdot 10^6\,\mathrm{m}$ measured from the center of the Earth and period $92{,}7$ minutes. What is its speed?

- $7{,}66 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4{,}60 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1{,}22 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3{,}83 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

In one period the station travels the whole circle, of length $2\pi r$, therefore $v = \dfrac{2\pi r}{T}$. The period in seconds is $T = 92{,}7 \cdot 60\,\mathrm{s} = 5562\,\mathrm{s}$. Hence
$$
v = \frac{2\pi \cdot 6{,}78 \cdot 10^6}{5562}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 7{,}66 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
