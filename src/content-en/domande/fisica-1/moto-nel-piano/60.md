A stone is launched from the ground with initial velocity components $v_{0x} = 10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_{0y} = 20\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Neglecting air resistance, at what instant does its velocity make an angle of $45^\circ$ *above* the horizontal?

- $3.06\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2.04\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.510\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.02\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The velocity makes $45^\circ$ above the horizontal when $v_y = v_x$ with $v_y > 0$. The horizontal component stays $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$, while $v_y(t) = 20\,\mathrm{m/s} - g\,t$. Setting $v_y = 10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ gives
$$
t = \frac{20 - 10}{9.81}\,\mathrm{s} = 1.02\,\mathrm{s} .
$$
At $3.06\,\mathrm{s}$ we have $v_y = -10\,\mathrm{m/s}$, so the velocity makes $45^\circ$ *below* the horizontal, during the descent.
