A projectile lands at its launch height. Its initial vertical velocity component is $u$, and take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Air resistance is negligible. Given the initial horizontal velocity component $v = 12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the initial vertical velocity component $u = 15\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the range $R$?

- $18\,\mathrm{m}$
- $72\,\mathrm{m}$
- $36\,\mathrm{m}$
- $54\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The flight time is twice the time $u/g$ required to reach the apex. The horizontal velocity stays equal to $v$. In the stated units, the relation is $R = \dfrac{u \cdot v}{5}$. Substituting the given values yields $36\,\mathrm{m}$.
