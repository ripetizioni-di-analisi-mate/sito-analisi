From the edge of a very high cliff a stone is thrown horizontally, with velocity $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Neglecting air resistance, after how long has the stone dropped by a distance equal to the horizontal distance it has covered?

- $1.22\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.612\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2.45\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.782\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

With the origin at the launch point, after a time $t$ the stone has covered $v_0\,t$ horizontally and has dropped by $\dfrac{1}{2}\,g\,t^2$. The two distances are equal when $\dfrac{1}{2}\,g\,t^2 = v_0\,t$, and for $t > 0$ we can divide by $t$, hence
$$
t = \frac{2v_0}{g} = \frac{2 \cdot 6.0}{9.81}\,\mathrm{s} = 1.22\,\mathrm{s} .
$$
The value $0.612\,\mathrm{s}$ is the instant at which $|v_y| = v_x$, that is, the instant at which the *velocity* makes an angle of $45^\circ$ with the horizontal.
