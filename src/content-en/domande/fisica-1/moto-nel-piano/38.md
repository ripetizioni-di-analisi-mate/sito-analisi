A radio-controlled toy car moves on a horizontal floor and, in a frame with the origin at a post, its position for $t \ge 0$ is $\vec{r}(t) = 2.0\,t\;\hat{\imath} + (4.0 - t^2)\,\hat{\jmath}$, with $\vec{r}$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. What is the minimum distance of the toy car from the post?

- $4.00\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.83\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3.46\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.41\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The square of the distance from the origin is
$$
d^2(t) = 4.0\,t^2 + (4.0 - t^2)^2 = t^4 - 4.0\,t^2 + 16 .
$$
Its derivative $4t^3 - 8.0\,t = 4t\,(t^2 - 2.0)$ is negative for $0 < t < \sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{s}$ and positive afterwards, so the distance is minimum at $t = \sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{s} = 1.41\,\mathrm{s}$. At that instant $d^2 = 4.0 - 8.0 + 16 = 12\,\mathrm{m^2}$, therefore $d_{\min} = \sqrt{12}\,\mathrm{m} = 3.46\,\mathrm{m}$. The condition $d'(t) = 0$ is equivalent to $\vec{r}\cdot\vec{v} = 0$, that is, at the point closest to the origin the velocity is perpendicular to the position vector.
