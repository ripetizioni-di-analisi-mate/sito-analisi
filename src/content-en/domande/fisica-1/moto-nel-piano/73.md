A point moves along a circle with constant angular acceleration, starting from rest. Given the angular acceleration $\alpha = 3\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$ and the tangential acceleration magnitude $a_{t} = 12\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the trajectory radius $R$?

- $8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Tangential speed is $R\omega$. Because $R$ is fixed, its time derivative is $R\alpha$. In the stated units, the relation is $a_{t} = R \cdot \alpha$. Solving for the unknown gives $R = \dfrac{a_{t}}{\alpha}$. Substituting the given values yields $4\,\mathrm{m}$.
