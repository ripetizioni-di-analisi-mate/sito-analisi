Two balls leave the edge of the same table horizontally at the same instant; the first has twice the speed of the second. Neglecting air resistance, which statement is true?

- The first hits the floor before the second
- They hit the floor together, the first at four times the distance from the table
- They hit the floor together, the first at twice the distance from the table
- The first stays in flight twice as long as the second

## Explanation

In a horizontal launch the vertical motion starts with zero vertical velocity, therefore the **fall time** $t_c = \sqrt{\dfrac{2h}{g}}$ depends only on the height of the table. The two balls start from the same height at the same instant, so they hit the floor together. The horizontal distance is $d = v_0\,t_c$, proportional to $v_0$, therefore the first ball lands at twice the distance.
