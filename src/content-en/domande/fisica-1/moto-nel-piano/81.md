An elevator moves up at a constant velocity of $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. A passenger drops the keys, at rest relative to the car, from a height of $1.2\,\mathrm{m}$ above the floor of the car. Neglecting air resistance, after how long do the keys hit the floor?

- $0.495\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.331\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.739\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.600\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The car translates at constant velocity relative to the building, so by the **invariance of acceleration** the keys have acceleration $\vec{g}$ in the frame of the car as well. In the frame of the car the keys start from rest, therefore they fall as in an elevator at rest and cover $1.2\,\mathrm{m}$ in the time
$$
t = \sqrt{\frac{2 \cdot 1.2}{9.81}}\,\mathrm{s} = 0.495\,\mathrm{s} .
$$
The value $0.331\,\mathrm{s}$ is obtained by assuming that the keys start from rest relative to the building, whereas at the instant they are released they have the velocity of the car, $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ upward.
