A target moves in a plane with initial velocity along the $x$ axis and constant acceleration along the $y$ axis. The initial velocity and acceleration point along the positive axes. Given the elapsed time $t = 3\,\mathrm{s}$ and the vertical displacement $y = 27\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the vertical acceleration $a$?

- $9\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The initial vertical velocity is zero, so only the quadratic term of the $y$ position equation contributes. In the stated units, the relation is $y = \dfrac{a \cdot t^{2}}{2}$. Solving for the unknown gives $a = \dfrac{2 \cdot y}{t^{2}}$. Substituting the given values yields $6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
