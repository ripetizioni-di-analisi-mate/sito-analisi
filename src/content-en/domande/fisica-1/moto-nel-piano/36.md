On a turntable rotating with constant angular velocity, point $B$ is twice as far from the axis as point $A$. Which statement is true?

- $B$ has twice the angular velocity of $A$
- $B$ has the same angular velocity as $A$ and four times the centripetal acceleration
- $B$ has the same angular velocity as $A$ and half the centripetal acceleration
- $B$ has the same angular velocity as $A$ and twice the centripetal acceleration

## Explanation

All points of a rotating rigid disk have the same angular velocity $\omega$. The centripetal acceleration $a_n = \omega^2 R$ is therefore proportional to the distance from the axis, so for $B$ it is twice as large. The same result follows from $a_n = \dfrac{v^2}{R}$, because for $B$ the speed $v = \omega R$ is twice as large, its square is four times as large and the radius is also twice as large.
