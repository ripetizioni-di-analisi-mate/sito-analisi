A bicycle travels at $18\,\mathrm{km/h}$ and its wheels have radius $0{,}35\,\mathrm{m}$. The wheels roll without slipping, so that the speed of the center of the wheel is $\omega R$. What is the angular velocity of the wheels?

- $51{,}4\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $7{,}14\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $1{,}75\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $14{,}3\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

In SI units the speed is $18\,\mathrm{km/h} = \dfrac{18}{3{,}6}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 5{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Since $v = \omega R$, one has $\omega = \dfrac{v}{R} = \dfrac{5{,}0}{0{,}35}\,\mathrm{rad/s} = 14{,}3\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. In one minute the wheels therefore complete $\dfrac{14{,}3 \cdot 60}{2\pi} = 136$ revolutions.
