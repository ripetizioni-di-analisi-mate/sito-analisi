A point moves in the plane with equations of motion $x(t) = 3{,}0\,t$ and $y(t) = 6{,}0\,t^2$, with $x$ and $y$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. What is the Cartesian equation of the trajectory?

- $y = \dfrac{2}{3}\,x^2$
- $y = 2\,x^2$
- $y = 54\,x^2$
- $y = 6\,x^2$

## Explanation

The equation of the trajectory is obtained by eliminating time between the two equations of motion. From the first one obtains $t = \dfrac{x}{3}$, and substituting into the second gives
$$
y = 6\left(\frac{x}{3}\right)^2 = \frac{6\,x^2}{9} = \frac{2}{3}\,x^2 .
$$
Therefore the trajectory is an arc of the **parabola** $y = \dfrac{2}{3}\,x^2$, traveled with $x \ge 0$ for $t \ge 0$.
