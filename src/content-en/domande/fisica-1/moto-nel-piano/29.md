What kind of motion does a particle perform if its position is given by $\vec{r}(t) = (1.0 + 2.0\,t)\,\hat{\imath} + (3.0 - 4.0\,t)\,\hat{\jmath}$, with $\vec{r}$ in meters and $t$ in seconds?

- Uniform rectilinear, with speed $6.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- Uniformly accelerated rectilinear, with acceleration $2.00\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- Uniform rectilinear, with speed $4.47\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- Along a parabolic arc, with variable speed

## Explanation

Differentiating component by component gives $\vec{v} = 2.0\,\hat{\imath} - 4.0\,\hat{\jmath}$, which does not depend on time. Since the velocity is constant in magnitude and direction, the acceleration is zero and the point moves along a straight line at constant velocity. The motion is therefore **uniform rectilinear**, with speed
$$
v = \sqrt{2.0^2 + 4.0^2}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 4.47\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The value $6.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$ comes from adding the magnitudes of the components, which does not give the magnitude of the vector.
