A ball is kicked from the ground with velocity $24.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ inclined at $30^\circ$ above the horizontal and lands on level ground. Neglecting air resistance, how long does the flight last?

- $1.25\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4.33\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4.99\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2.50\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The landing point is at the same height as the launch point, therefore the formula for the **time of flight** applies. With $\sin 30^\circ = \dfrac{1}{2}$ one obtains
$$
T = \frac{2\,v_0\sin\theta}{g} = \frac{24.5}{9.81}\,\mathrm{s} = 2.50\,\mathrm{s} .
$$
The rise time $t_H = \dfrac{v_0\sin\theta}{g} = 1.25\,\mathrm{s}$ is half the time of flight, because the descent lasts as long as the ascent.
