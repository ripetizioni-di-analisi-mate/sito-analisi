In planar motion, the initial $x$ velocity and the constant $y$ acceleration are positive. The remaining initial components are zero. Given the constant vertical acceleration $a = 2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, the horizontal speed $v = 8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the elapsed time $t = 3\,\mathrm{s}$, what is the tangent of the angle between the velocity and the positive $x$ axis, denoted by $q$?

- $\dfrac{3}{8}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

The tangent is the ratio $v_y/v_x$. In this motion $v_y=at$ and $v_x=v$. In the stated units, the relation is $q = \dfrac{a \cdot t}{v}$. Substituting the given values yields $\dfrac{3}{4}$.
