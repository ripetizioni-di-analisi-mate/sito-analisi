A ball kicked from the ground passes through the same height $h$ twice, $1.0\,\mathrm{s}$ and $3.0\,\mathrm{s}$ after the kick. Neglecting air resistance, what is $h$?

- $19.6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $14.7\,\mathrm{m}$
- $29.4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4.91\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The instants $t_1$ and $t_2$ are the two solutions of the equation $\dfrac{1}{2}\,g\,t^2 - v_{0y}\,t + h = 0$. By the relations between the coefficients and the solutions of a quadratic equation, their product is $t_1 t_2 = \dfrac{2h}{g}$. Hence
$$
h = \frac{g\,t_1 t_2}{2} = \frac{9.81 \cdot 1.0 \cdot 3.0}{2}\,\mathrm{m} = 14.7\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $19.6\,\mathrm{m}$ is the maximum height, reached at $2.0\,\mathrm{s}$, halfway between the two passages.
