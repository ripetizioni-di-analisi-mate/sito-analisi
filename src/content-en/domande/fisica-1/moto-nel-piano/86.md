A package is released by a drone flying at constant horizontal speed. Take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and neglect air resistance. The initial data are the drone's speed $v = 8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the release height $h = 20\,\mathrm{m}$. Change the release height to $45\,\mathrm{m}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the ratio of the final value of $d$ to its initial value?

- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $1$
- $3$

## Explanation

Vertically the package falls from rest, so $h=gt^2/2$. Horizontally it retains its velocity $v$. The relation $d = \dfrac{\sqrt{5} \cdot \sqrt{h} \cdot v}{5}$ gives $16\,\mathrm{m}$ before the change and $24\,\mathrm{m}$ after it. The ratio is $\dfrac{3}{2}$.
