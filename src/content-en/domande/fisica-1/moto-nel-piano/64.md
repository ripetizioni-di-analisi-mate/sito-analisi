A merry-go-round of radius $5.0\,\mathrm{m}$ turns through an angle of $120^\circ$. How long is the arc traveled by a horse fixed on the rim?

- $5.24\,\mathrm{m}$
- $600\,\mathrm{m}$
- $31.4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10.5\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The arc length is $s = R\,\theta$ with the angle measured in radians. The angle of $120^\circ$ corresponds to $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\,\mathrm{rad}$, so
$$
s = 5.0\,\mathrm{m} \cdot \frac{2\pi}{3} = 10.5\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $600\,\mathrm{m}$ comes from multiplying the radius by the angle in degrees, which is not a measure of the ratio of arc to radius.
