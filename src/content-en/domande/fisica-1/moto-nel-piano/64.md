A boat points perpendicular to the parallel banks of a river. A uniform current runs parallel to the banks. Given the river width $L = 48\,\mathrm{m}$, the current speed $u = 2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the boat's speed relative to the water $v = 6\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the downstream drift $d$?

- $12\,\mathrm{m}$
- $32\,\mathrm{m}$
- $144\,\mathrm{m}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The velocity across the river is $v$, so the crossing takes $L/v$. During that time the current carries the boat downstream at speed $u$. In the stated units, the relation is $d = \dfrac{L \cdot u}{v}$. Substituting the given values yields $16\,\mathrm{m}$.
