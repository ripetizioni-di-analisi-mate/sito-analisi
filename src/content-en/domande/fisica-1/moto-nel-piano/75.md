A point moves on a circle of radius $6.0\,\mathrm{m}$, and the arc length traveled varies with time according to $s(t) = 0.50\,t^3$, with $s$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. What is the magnitude of the acceleration at $t = 2.0\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $6.00\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $12.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $8.49\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $36.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The speed is $v = \dfrac{ds}{dt} = 1.5\,t^2$, which at $t = 2.0\,\mathrm{s}$ is $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The tangential component is $a_t = \dfrac{dv}{dt} = 3.0\,t = 6.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, and the centripetal component is $a_n = \dfrac{v^2}{R} = \dfrac{36}{6.0}\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 6.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The two components are perpendicular, so
$$
|\vec{a}| = \sqrt{6.0^2 + 6.0^2}\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 8.49\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
The value $12.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ comes from adding the magnitudes of the components, which are not parallel.
