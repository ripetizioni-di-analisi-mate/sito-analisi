Two points on a merry-go-round take the same time to complete a full turn. The point under study is at distance $R$ from the center. Given the distance from the center $R = 6\,\mathrm{m}$ and the period $T = 4\,\mathrm{s}$, what is the tangential speed $v$?

- $3 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac{3 \cdot \pi}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

During one period the point travels a distance equal to the circumference, $2\pi R$. In the stated units, the relation is $v = \dfrac{2 \cdot \pi \cdot R}{T}$. Substituting the given values yields $3 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
