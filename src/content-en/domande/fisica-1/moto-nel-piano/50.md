Two balls leave the same table horizontally at different initial speeds. The second is twice as fast as the first and air resistance is negligible. How do their fall times and horizontal distances compare?

- Twice the time and twice the distance for the second ball
- Equal times, twice the distance for the second ball
- Equal times and equal distances
- Half the time and the same distance for the second ball

## Explanation

Both vertical motions start from rest at the same height, so the times are equal. Horizontal velocity is constant and horizontal distance equals speed times time.
