From a bridge $20\,\mathrm{m}$ above a river a stone is thrown horizontally with velocity $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Neglecting air resistance, what is the magnitude of the average velocity of the stone between the launch and the instant it hits the water?

- $16.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $14.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $22.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The stone hits the water after $t = \sqrt{\dfrac{2h}{g}} = \sqrt{\dfrac{2 \cdot 20}{9.81}}\,\mathrm{s} = 2.02\,\mathrm{s}$, and in this time it covers $v_0\,t = 20.2\,\mathrm{m}$ horizontally. The displacement therefore has components $(20.2;\ -20)\,\mathrm{m}$ and magnitude $|\Delta\vec{r}| = \sqrt{20.2^2 + 20^2}\,\mathrm{m} = 28.4\,\mathrm{m}$. The average velocity is the ratio of the displacement to the time interval, therefore
$$
|\vec{v}_m| = \frac{28.4\,\mathrm{m}}{2.02\,\mathrm{s}} = 14.1\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The value $16.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is the mean of the magnitudes of the initial velocity, $10.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and of the final velocity, $22.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$, which differs from the magnitude of the average velocity.
