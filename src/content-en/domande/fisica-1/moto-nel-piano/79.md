Two points on a merry-go-round take the same time to complete a full turn. The point under study is at distance $R$ from the center. The initial data are the distance from the center $R = 6\,\mathrm{m}$ and the period $T = 4\,\mathrm{s}$. Change the distance from the center to $8\,\mathrm{m}$ and the period to $2\,\mathrm{s}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the final value of $v$ minus its initial value?

- $5 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $- 5 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac{5 \cdot \pi}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

During one period the point travels a distance equal to the circumference, $2\pi R$. The relation $v = \dfrac{2 \cdot \pi \cdot R}{T}$ gives $3 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{m/s}$ before the change and $8 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{m/s}$ after it. Subtracting the initial value from the final value gives $5 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
