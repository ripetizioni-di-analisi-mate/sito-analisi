What angle does the acceleration make with the velocity of a point that, starting from rest and turning with constant angular acceleration, completes its first turn around a circle?

- $76.0^\circ$
- $4.55^\circ$
- $85.5^\circ$
- $45.0^\circ$

## Explanation

By the time-independent relation of uniformly accelerated circular motion, after an angle $\Delta\theta$ we have $\omega^2 = 2\alpha\,\Delta\theta$. After one turn $\Delta\theta = 2\pi$, so $a_n = \omega^2 R = 4\pi\,\alpha R$, while $a_t = \alpha R$. The angle $\varphi$ between the acceleration and the velocity, which is tangent, satisfies
$$
\tan\varphi = \frac{a_n}{a_t} = 4\pi = 12.6 ,
$$
therefore $\varphi = 85.5^\circ$, whatever the radius and the value of $\alpha$.
