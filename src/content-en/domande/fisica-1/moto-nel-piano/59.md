A stone launched from the ground lands on level ground $40\,\mathrm{m}$ from the launch point after $2.5\,\mathrm{s}$ of flight. Neglecting air resistance, at what angle above the horizontal was it launched?

- $56.9^\circ$
- $21.0^\circ$
- $37.5^\circ$
- $45.0^\circ$

## Explanation

The horizontal component of the velocity is constant, so $v_{0x} = \dfrac{R}{T} = \dfrac{40}{2.5}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 16\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The time of flight is $T = \dfrac{2\,v_{0y}}{g}$, therefore $v_{0y} = \dfrac{g\,T}{2} = \dfrac{9.81 \cdot 2.5}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 12.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The launch angle satisfies
$$
\tan\theta = \frac{v_{0y}}{v_{0x}} = \frac{12.3}{16} = 0.766 ,
$$
so $\theta = 37.5^\circ$. The value $56.9^\circ$ comes from $v_{0y} = g\,T$, that is, from using the time of flight instead of the rise time.
