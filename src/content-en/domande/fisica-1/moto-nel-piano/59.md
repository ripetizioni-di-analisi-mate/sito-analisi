A cyclist rides along a circular bend at constant speed. Given the speed $v = 12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the radius of curvature $R = 18\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the magnitude of the centripetal acceleration $a$?

- $8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Acceleration points toward the center. Its magnitude is the square of speed divided by the radius. In the stated units, the relation is $a = \dfrac{v^{2}}{R}$. Substituting the given values yields $8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
