A car travels on a circular track of radius $50\,\mathrm{m}$ at constant speed and takes $31.4\,\mathrm{s}$ to complete one lap. What is its centripetal acceleration?

- $10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0.32\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The angular velocity is $\omega = \dfrac{2\pi}{T} = \dfrac{6.28}{31.4}\,\mathrm{rad/s} = 0.20\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. For the **centripetal acceleration** one therefore has
$$
a_n = \omega^2 R = 0.20^2 \cdot 50\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
The speed is $v = \omega R = 10\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and indeed $\dfrac{v^2}{R} = \dfrac{100}{50}\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
