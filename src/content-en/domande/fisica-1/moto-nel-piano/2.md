A projectile is launched from the ground with velocity $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$ inclined at $60^\circ$ above the horizontal, over level ground. Neglecting air resistance, what is the range?

- $39.7\,\mathrm{m}$
- $91.7\,\mathrm{m}$
- $138\,\mathrm{m}$
- $79.5\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The landing point is at the same height as the launch point, therefore the formula for the **range** $R = \dfrac{v_0^2 \sin 2\theta}{g}$ applies. With $2\theta = 120^\circ$ one has $\sin 120^\circ = 0.866$, so
$$
R = \frac{30^2 \cdot 0.866}{9.81}\,\mathrm{m} = 79.5\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The same value is obtained by multiplying the horizontal component $v_0\cos\theta = 15\,\mathrm{m/s}$ by the time of flight $T = \dfrac{2\,v_0\sin\theta}{g} = 5.30\,\mathrm{s}$.
