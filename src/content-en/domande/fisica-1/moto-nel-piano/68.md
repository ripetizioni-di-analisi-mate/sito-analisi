At noon the hour hand and the minute hand of a clock overlap. After how much time do they overlap again for the first time?

- $54.5\,\mathrm{min}$
- $720\,\mathrm{min}$
- $12.0\,\mathrm{min}$
- $65.5\,\mathrm{min}$

## Explanation

The minute hand makes one turn in $60\,\mathrm{min}$ and the hour hand in $720\,\mathrm{min}$, so the angular velocities are $\omega_m = \dfrac{2\pi}{60\,\mathrm{min}}$ and $\omega_h = \dfrac{2\pi}{720\,\mathrm{min}}$. The angle between the two hands grows with the relative angular velocity $\omega_m - \omega_h$, and the hands overlap again when that angle reaches $2\pi$. Therefore
$$
t = \frac{2\pi}{\omega_m - \omega_h} = \frac{1}{\dfrac{1}{60} - \dfrac{1}{720}}\,\mathrm{min} = \frac{720}{11}\,\mathrm{min} = 65.5\,\mathrm{min} .
$$
After $60\,\mathrm{min}$ the hour hand has moved by one twelfth of a turn, so the minute hand has not yet caught up with it.
