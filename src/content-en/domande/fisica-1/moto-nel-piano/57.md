A stone is launched from the ground with speed $v_0$ directed at an angle $\theta$ upward, and lands on level ground. Neglecting air resistance, what is the velocity of the stone when it hits the ground?

- Magnitude $v_0$, directed $\theta$ below the horizontal
- Magnitude $v_0\cos\theta$, horizontal
- Magnitude $v_0$, vertically downward
- Magnitude $2v_0\sin\theta$, directed $\theta$ below the horizontal

## Explanation

The horizontal component stays $v_x = v_0\cos\theta$ during the whole flight. The stone lands at $T = \dfrac{2\,v_0\sin\theta}{g}$, so the vertical component is $v_y = v_0\sin\theta - g\,T = -v_0\sin\theta$. Therefore the landing velocity has the same magnitude $v_0$ as the launch velocity, and its direction makes the angle $\theta$ below the horizontal. The result expresses the symmetry of the trajectory about the vertical line through the highest point.
