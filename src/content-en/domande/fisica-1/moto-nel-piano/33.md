During the flight of a small drone, observed for $0 < t < 6.0\,\mathrm{s}$, the speed first decreases and then increases. Its position is $\vec{r}(t) = 2.0\,t\;\hat{\imath} + (6.0\,t - t^2)\,\hat{\jmath}$, with $\vec{r}$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. On which interval does the speed decrease?

- $3.0\,\mathrm{s} < t < 6.0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0 < t < 6.0\,\mathrm{s}$
- On no interval, because the acceleration is constant
- $0 < t < 3.0\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Differentiating gives $\vec{v} = 2.0\,\hat{\imath} + (6.0 - 2.0\,t)\,\hat{\jmath}$ and $\vec{a} = -2.0\,\hat{\jmath}$. The derivative of $v^2 = \vec{v}\cdot\vec{v}$ is $2\,\vec{a}\cdot\vec{v}$, so the speed decreases where $\vec{a}\cdot\vec{v} < 0$. We have
$$
\vec{a}\cdot\vec{v} = -2.0\,(6.0 - 2.0\,t) = 4.0\,t - 12 ,
$$
which is negative for $t < 3.0\,\mathrm{s}$. Therefore the speed decreases for $0 < t < 3.0\,\mathrm{s}$, while the point rises, and increases afterwards, when the component $v_y$ becomes negative.
