A package is released by a drone flying at constant horizontal speed. Take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and neglect air resistance. Given the drone's speed $v = 8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the horizontal distance covered by the package before impact $d = 16\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the release height $h$?

- $40\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $30\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Vertically the package falls from rest, so $h=gt^2/2$. Horizontally it retains its velocity $v$. In the stated units, the relation is $d = \dfrac{\sqrt{5} \cdot \sqrt{h} \cdot v}{5}$. Solving for the unknown gives $h = \dfrac{5 \cdot d^{2}}{v^{2}}$. Substituting the given values yields $20\,\mathrm{m}$.
