A ball leaves the edge of a table horizontally. Air resistance is negligible and the ground is horizontal. Given the fall time $t = \dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{s}$ and the horizontal distance from the edge to impact $d = \dfrac{18}{5}\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the initial horizontal speed $v$?

- $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Without air resistance the acceleration is vertical, so the horizontal velocity remains constant. In the stated units, the relation is $d = t \cdot v$. Solving for the unknown gives $v = \dfrac{d}{t}$. Substituting the given values yields $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
