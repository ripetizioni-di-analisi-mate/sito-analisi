The blades of a fan rotate at $1200$ revolutions per minute and have radius $0{,}25\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the speed of the tip of a blade?

- $5{,}00\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $62{,}8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $31{,}4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1{,}88 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The frequency is $f = \dfrac{1200}{60}\,\mathrm{Hz} = 20\,\mathrm{Hz}$, therefore the angular velocity is $\omega = 2\pi f = 40\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. The tip of the blade is $R = 0{,}25\,\mathrm{m}$ from the axis, so
$$
v = \omega R = 40\pi \cdot 0{,}25\,\mathrm{m/s} = 10\pi\,\mathrm{m/s} = 31{,}4\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The same value is obtained by multiplying the circumference $2\pi R = 1{,}57\,\mathrm{m}$ by the $20$ revolutions completed in one second.
