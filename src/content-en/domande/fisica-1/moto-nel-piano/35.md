At $t = 0$ a point has velocity $\vec{v}_0 = 4.0\,\hat{\imath} + 3.0\,\hat{\jmath}$, in $\mathrm{m/s}$, and moves with constant acceleration $\vec{a} = -2.0\,\hat{\imath}$, in $\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is the minimum value of its speed?

- $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0$
- $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

By the constant-acceleration equations of motion the velocity is $\vec{v}(t) = (4.0 - 2.0\,t)\,\hat{\imath} + 3.0\,\hat{\jmath}$. The component $v_y$ stays $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at every instant, while $v_x$ vanishes at $t = 2.0\,\mathrm{s}$. Since $v^2 = v_x^2 + v_y^2$ and $v_y^2$ is constant, the speed is minimum when $v_x = 0$, that is, when the velocity is perpendicular to the acceleration. Hence
$$
v_{\min} = |v_y| = 3.0\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The point never stops, because the velocity component perpendicular to $\vec{a}$ does not change.
