A point starts from position $(1{,}0;\ 0)\,\mathrm{m}$ with velocity $\vec{v}_0 = -3{,}0\,\hat{\jmath}\ \mathrm{m/s}$ and moves with constant acceleration $\vec{a} = 2{,}0\,\hat{\jmath}\ \mathrm{m/s^2}$. What is its trajectory?

- An arc of a parabola with axis parallel to the $y$ axis
- A segment of the line $x = 1{,}0\,\mathrm{m}$
- An arc of a parabola with axis parallel to the $x$ axis
- An arc of a circle

## Explanation

The initial velocity and the acceleration are both along the $y$ axis, therefore they are parallel, even though they point in opposite directions. Then $a_x = 0$ and $v_{0x} = 0$, so $x(t) = 1{,}0\,\mathrm{m}$ at every instant. The point moves along the line $x = 1{,}0\,\mathrm{m}$, first downward and then upward, after the time $t = 1{,}5\,\mathrm{s}$ at which $v_y = -3{,}0 + 2{,}0\,t$ vanishes. The trajectory is a parabola only when $\vec{v}_0$ and $\vec{a}$ are not parallel.
