A projectile is launched from the ground at an angle $\theta$ between $0^\circ$ and $90^\circ$ and lands on level ground. Neglecting air resistance, where is the projectile when half of the time of flight has elapsed?

- At half the maximum height, on the way up
- At three quarters of the maximum height, on the way down
- At a point that depends on the angle $\theta$
- At the highest point of the trajectory

## Explanation

The time of flight is $T = \dfrac{2\,v_0\sin\theta}{g}$, while the projectile reaches the highest point at $t_H = \dfrac{v_0\sin\theta}{g}$. Since $t_H = \dfrac{T}{2}$ for every angle, the ascent and the descent last the same time. Hence at half the time of flight the projectile is **at the highest point of the trajectory**, directly above the midpoint of the range.
