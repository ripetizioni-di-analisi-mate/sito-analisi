A uniform solid sample is weighed in grams and its volume is measured in cubic centimeters. The initial data are the mass $m = 180\,\mathrm{g}$ and the volume $V = 60\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. Change the volume to $90\,\mathrm{cm^3}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the ratio of the final value of $\rho$ to its initial value?

- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

The ratio of mass to volume is initially in grams per cubic centimeter. One gram per cubic centimeter is a thousand kilograms per cubic meter. The relation $\rho = \dfrac{1000 \cdot m}{V}$ gives $3000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ before the change and $2000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ after it. The ratio is $\dfrac{2}{3}$.
