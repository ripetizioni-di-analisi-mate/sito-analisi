The diameter of a copper wire is $0.000\,360\,\mathrm{m}$, written with three significant figures. How is it written in scientific notation?

- $3.60 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3.60 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3.60 \cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3.60 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

In scientific notation the coefficient $a$ satisfies $1 \leq a < 10$, so the significant figures $3$, $6$, $0$ are written $3.60$. To go from $0.000\,360$ to $3.60$ the decimal point moves four places to the right, so the exponent is $-4$. The final zero stays, because it states the third significant figure.
