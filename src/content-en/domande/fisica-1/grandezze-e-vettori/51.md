A constant force has a component along the displacement equal to half its magnitude. The work is positive. Given the magnitude of the force $F = 18\,\mathrm{N}$ and the magnitude of the displacement $d = 4\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the work $W$?

- $36\,\mathrm{J}$
- $72\,\mathrm{J}$
- $18\,\mathrm{J}$
- $54\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Work is the dot product of force and displacement. Only the parallel component contributes. In the stated units, the relation is $W = \dfrac{F \cdot d}{2}$. Substituting the given values yields $36\,\mathrm{J}$.
