The diameter of a hair is about $7.0 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{m}$. What is its order of magnitude, that is, the power of ten closest to the number on the logarithmic scale?

- $10^{-5}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The number is written in the form $a \cdot 10^{n}$ with $a = 7.0$ and $n = -5$. Since $7.0 > \sqrt{10} \approx 3.16$, the order of magnitude is $10^{n + 1} = 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m}$. Indeed, on the logarithmic scale $7.0 \cdot 10^{-5}$ is closer to $10^{-4}$ than to $10^{-5}$, because $\dfrac{10^{-4}}{7.0 \cdot 10^{-5}} \approx 1.4$, while $\dfrac{7.0 \cdot 10^{-5}}{10^{-5}} = 7.0$. The value $10^{-5}\,\mathrm{m}$ is obtained by reading only the exponent of the scientific notation.
