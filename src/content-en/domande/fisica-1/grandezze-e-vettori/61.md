A body of mass $m$ oscillates hanging from a spring of spring constant $k$, measured in $\mathrm{N/m}$. The period of the oscillations depends only on $m$ and $k$. Which of the following expressions has the dimensions of a time?

- $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m}}$
- $2\pi\,\dfrac{m}{k}$
- $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k}}$
- $2\pi\sqrt{m k}$

## Explanation

Since $1\,\mathrm{N} = 1\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s^2}$, the spring constant is measured in $\mathrm{kg/s^2}$ and has dimensions $[k] = [M\,T^{-2}]$. Hence
$$
\left[\frac{m}{k}\right] = \frac{[M]}{[M\,T^{-2}]} = [T^{2}] ,
$$
and the square root of $\dfrac{m}{k}$ has dimensions $[T]$. The factor $2\pi$ is dimensionless, therefore $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k}}$ has the dimensions of a time. The expression $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m}}$ has dimensions $[T^{-1}]$, and $2\pi\,\dfrac{m}{k}$ has dimensions $[T^{2}]$. Finally $[m k] = [M^{2}\,T^{-2}]$, so $2\pi\sqrt{m k}$ has dimensions $[M\,T^{-1}]$.
