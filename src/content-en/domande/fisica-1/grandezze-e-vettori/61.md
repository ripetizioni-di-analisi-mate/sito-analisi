An open rectangular tank has a square base and is filled with liquid to height $h$. Given the base side length $l = 2\,\mathrm{m}$ and the liquid depth $h = 3\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the liquid volume $V$?

- $6000\,\mathrm{L}$
- $1200\,\mathrm{L}$
- $12000\,\mathrm{L}$
- $24000\,\mathrm{L}$

## Explanation

The volume in cubic meters is the product of area $l^2$ and height. Each cubic meter contains one thousand liters. In the stated units, the relation is $V = 1000 \cdot h \cdot l^{2}$. Substituting the given values yields $12000\,\mathrm{L}$.
