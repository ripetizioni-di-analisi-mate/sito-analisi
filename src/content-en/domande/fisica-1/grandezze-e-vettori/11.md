A body undergoes the displacement $\vec{s} = (3.0\,\hat{\imath} + 4.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m}$ under the action of the constant force $\vec{F} = (5.0\,\hat{\imath} - 2.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{N}$. What is the work of the force?

- $7.0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $27\,\mathrm{J}$
- $23\,\mathrm{J}$
- $15\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The force is constant and the displacement is a straight line, so the work is the dot product $L = \vec{F} \cdot \vec{s}$. In components we have
$$
L = F_x s_x + F_y s_y = 5.0 \cdot 3.0\,\mathrm{J} + (-2.0) \cdot 4.0\,\mathrm{J} = 7.0\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
The value $23\,\mathrm{J}$ is obtained by neglecting the negative sign of $F_y$, the value $15\,\mathrm{J}$ by considering only the components along $x$. The value $27\,\mathrm{J}$ is the product of the magnitudes $F s = \sqrt{29} \cdot 5.0\,\mathrm{J}$, which coincides with the work only for a force and a displacement that are parallel and in the same orientation.
