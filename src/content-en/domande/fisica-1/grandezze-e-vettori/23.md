The power of a constant force $\vec{F}$ acting on a body whose velocity $\vec{v}$ is parallel to the force and in the same direction is $P = F v$. What are the dimensions of power?

- $[M\,L^{2}\,T^{-2}]$
- $[M\,L\,T^{-3}]$
- $[M\,L^{2}\,T^{-1}]$
- $[M\,L^{2}\,T^{-3}]$

## Explanation

Force has dimensions $[F] = [M\,L\,T^{-2}]$ and velocity $[v] = [L\,T^{-1}]$. The product therefore has dimensions
$$
[P] = [M\,L\,T^{-2}]\,[L\,T^{-1}] = [M\,L^{2}\,T^{-3}] .
$$
The dimensions $[M\,L^{2}\,T^{-2}]$ are those of work; since power is work divided by a time, it has an extra factor $[T^{-1}]$.
