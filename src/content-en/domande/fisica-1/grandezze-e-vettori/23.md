Which of the following quantities has the dimensions $[M\,L^{2}\,T^{-2}]$?

- Momentum
- Force
- Power
- Work

## Explanation

The work of a constant force is the product $F s\cos\theta$ of a force and a length, and the cosine is dimensionless. Hence the dimensions of work are $[F]\,[s] = [M\,L\,T^{-2}]\,[L] = [M\,L^{2}\,T^{-2}]$. Force has dimensions $[M\,L\,T^{-2}]$, and power, which is work divided by a time, has dimensions $[M\,L^{2}\,T^{-3}]$. Momentum $m\vec{v}$ has dimensions $[M\,L\,T^{-1}]$.
