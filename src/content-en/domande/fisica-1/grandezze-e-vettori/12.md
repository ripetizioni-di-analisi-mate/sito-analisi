The force $\vec{F} = (10\,\hat{\imath} - 5.0\,\hat{\jmath} + 20\,\hat{k})\,\mathrm{N}$ is applied at the point with position $\vec{r} = (0.30\,\hat{\imath} + 0.40\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m}$ relative to the pole $O$. What is the torque of the force about $O$?

- $(-8.0\,\hat{\imath} + 6.0\,\hat{\jmath} + 5.5\,\hat{k})\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $(8.0\,\hat{\imath} + 6.0\,\hat{\jmath} - 5.5\,\hat{k})\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $(8.0\,\hat{\imath} - 6.0\,\hat{\jmath} + 2.5\,\hat{k})\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $(8.0\,\hat{\imath} - 6.0\,\hat{\jmath} - 5.5\,\hat{k})\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$

## Explanation

The torque is $\vec{\tau} = \vec{r} \times \vec{F}$, with $\vec{r}$ as the first factor. Since $r_z = 0$, the components of the cross product are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\tau_x &= r_y F_z - r_z F_y = 0.40 \cdot 20\,\mathrm{N\cdot m} = 8.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m} , \\[6pt]
\tau_y &= r_z F_x - r_x F_z = -0.30 \cdot 20\,\mathrm{N\cdot m} = -6.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m} , \\[6pt]
\tau_z &= r_x F_y - r_y F_x = \big(0.30 \cdot (-5.0) - 0.40 \cdot 10\big)\,\mathrm{N\cdot m} = -5.5\,\mathrm{N\cdot m} .
\end{aligned}
$$
The vector with all signs changed is $\vec{F} \times \vec{r}$, that is, the product with the factors swapped. The vector with $\tau_z = 2.5\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$ is obtained by getting the sign of the term $r_y F_x$ wrong.
