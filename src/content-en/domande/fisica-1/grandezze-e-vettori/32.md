A force $\vec{F}$ of magnitude $50\,\mathrm{N}$ makes an angle of $30^\circ$ with the positive $y$-semiaxis and has a positive $x$ component, as in the figure. What are its Cartesian components?

::grafico{vettori="0,0,2.5,4.33,F" etichette="0.45,1.75,30°" xmin=-0.8 xmax=3.6 ymin=-0.6 ymax=5 uguale=1 titolo="The force $\vec{F}$ makes an angle of $30^\circ$ with the positive $y$-semiaxis"}

- $(43.3\,\hat{\imath} + 25.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{N}$
- $(25.0\,\hat{\imath} + 43.3\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{N}$
- $(25.0\,\hat{\imath} - 43.3\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{N}$
- $(-25.0\,\hat{\imath} + 43.3\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The angle of $\vec{F}$ with the positive $x$-semiaxis, measured counterclockwise, is $90^\circ - 30^\circ = 60^\circ$. Therefore
$$
F_x = 50 \cos 60^\circ\,\mathrm{N} = 25.0\,\mathrm{N},
\qquad
F_y = 50 \sin 60^\circ\,\mathrm{N} = 43.3\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The swapped components, $(43.3;\ 25.0)\,\mathrm{N}$, are obtained by using $30^\circ$ as the angle with the $x$-axis.
