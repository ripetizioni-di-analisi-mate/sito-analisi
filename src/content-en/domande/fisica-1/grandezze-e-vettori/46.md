A red blood cell has a diameter of $7.5\,\mu\mathrm{m}$. What is the diameter in nanometers?

- $7.5 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $7.5 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $7.5 \cdot 10^{2}\,\mathrm{nm}$
- $7.5 \cdot 10^{6}\,\mathrm{nm}$

## Explanation

The prefix micro is $10^{-6}$ and the prefix nano is $10^{-9}$. Therefore $1\,\mu\mathrm{m} = 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m} = 10^{3} \cdot 10^{-9}\,\mathrm{m} = 10^{3}\,\mathrm{nm}$. Hence the diameter is $7.5 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{nm}$. The value $7.5 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{nm}$ is obtained by dividing by $10^{3}$ instead of multiplying; since the nanometer is a smaller unit than the micrometer, the number must increase.
