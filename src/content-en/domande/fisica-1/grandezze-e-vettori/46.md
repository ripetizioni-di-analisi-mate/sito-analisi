Two displacements are perpendicular. The first is horizontal and the second vertical. Given the magnitude of the horizontal displacement $x = 9\,\mathrm{m}$ and the magnitude of the vertical displacement $y = 12\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the magnitude of the resultant displacement $R$?

- $30\,\mathrm{m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m}$
- $21\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The components of the resultant are $x$ and $y$. Its magnitude follows from the Pythagorean theorem. In the stated units, the relation is $R = \sqrt{x^{2} + y^{2}}$. Substituting the given values yields $15\,\mathrm{m}$.
