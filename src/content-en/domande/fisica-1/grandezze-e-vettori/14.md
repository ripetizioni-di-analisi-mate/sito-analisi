A swimming pool contains $3.6 \cdot 10^{5}\,\mathrm{L}$ of water. What is the volume of the water in $\mathrm{m^3}$?

- $3.6 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $3.6 \cdot 10^{2}\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $3.6 \cdot 10^{-1}\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $3.6 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{m^3}$

## Explanation

A liter is a cubic decimeter, and since $1\,\mathrm{dm} = 10^{-1}\,\mathrm{m}$ we have $1\,\mathrm{L} = (10^{-1}\,\mathrm{m})^3 = 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^3}$. Therefore
$$
V = 3.6 \cdot 10^{5} \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^3} = 3.6 \cdot 10^{2}\,\mathrm{m^3} .
$$
The value $3.6 \cdot 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m^3}$ is obtained by multiplying by $10^{3}$ instead of dividing, the value $3.6 \cdot 10^{-1}\,\mathrm{m^3}$ by treating the liter as a cubic centimeter.
