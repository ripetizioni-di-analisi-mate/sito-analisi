A force's line of action is perpendicular to a lever's axis and passes at distance $d$ from it. The initial data are the magnitude of the force $F = 24\,\mathrm{N}$ and the perpendicular moment arm $d = 3\,\mathrm{m}$. Change the magnitude of the force to $36\,\mathrm{N}$ and the perpendicular moment arm to $2\,\mathrm{m}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the final value of $M$ minus its initial value?

- $10\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $72\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $1\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N\,m}$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the cross product of position and force equals the force times the perpendicular distance to its line of action. The relation $M = F \cdot d$ gives $72\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ before the change and $72\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ after it. Subtracting the initial value from the final value gives $0\,\mathrm{N\,m}$.
