The vector $\vec{A}$ in the figure has its tail and its tip at points with integer coordinates. What are its Cartesian components?

::grafico{vettori="1,4,5,1,A" punti="1,4,pieno;5,1,pieno" segmenti="1,4,1,0;5,1,5,0;1,4,0,4;5,1,0,1" xmin=-0.6 xmax=5.8 ymin=-0.6 ymax=4.8 uguale=1 titolo="The vector $\vec{A}$; the tick marks on the axes are $1$ apart"}

- $(-4;\ 3)$
- $(5;\ 1)$
- $(3;\ -4)$
- $(4;\ -3)$

## Explanation

From the figure the tail of $\vec{A}$ is at the point $(1;\ 4)$ and the tip at the point $(5;\ 1)$. The components are the differences between the coordinates of the tip and those of the tail, so
$$
A_x = 5 - 1 = 4,
\qquad
A_y = 1 - 4 = -3 .
$$
The vector $(-4;\ 3)$ is obtained by subtracting in the opposite order, and $(5;\ 1)$ are the coordinates of the tip alone, which coincide with the components only for a vector applied at the origin.
