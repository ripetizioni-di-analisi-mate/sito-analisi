A force's line of action is perpendicular to a lever's axis and passes at distance $d$ from it. Given the magnitude of the force $F = 24\,\mathrm{N}$ and the perpendicular moment arm $d = 3\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the magnitude of the torque $M$?

- $144\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $36\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $72\,\mathrm{N\,m}$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the cross product of position and force equals the force times the perpendicular distance to its line of action. In the stated units, the relation is $M = F \cdot d$. Substituting the given values yields $72\,\mathrm{N\,m}$.
