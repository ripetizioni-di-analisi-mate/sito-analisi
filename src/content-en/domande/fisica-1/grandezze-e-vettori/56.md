A body of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ moves in a straight line from the point $(0;\ 1.5)\,\mathrm{m}$ to the point $(3.0;\ 0)\,\mathrm{m}$, with the $y$-axis vertical and pointing upward. What is the work done by the weight, with $g = 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$?

- $-29.4\,\mathrm{J}$
- $65.8\,\mathrm{J}$
- $29.4\,\mathrm{J}$
- $58.9\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The weight is $\vec{P} = (0;\ -mg) = (0;\ -19.6)\,\mathrm{N}$ and the displacement is $\vec{s} = (3.0;\ -1.5)\,\mathrm{m}$. The work is the dot product
$$
L = \vec{P} \cdot \vec{s} = 0 \cdot 3.0 + (-19.6) \cdot (-1.5)\,\mathrm{J} \approx 29.4\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
Only the vertical component of the displacement contributes, because the weight is perpendicular to the $x$-axis. The work is positive because the body moves down. The value $65.8\,\mathrm{J}$ is obtained by multiplying the weight by the whole length of the displacement.
