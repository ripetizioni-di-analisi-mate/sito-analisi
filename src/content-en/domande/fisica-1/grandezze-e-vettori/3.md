What is the area $A = 2{,}4\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ expressed in $\mathrm{m^2}$?

- $2{,}4 \cdot 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $2{,}4 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $2{,}4 \cdot 10^{-9}\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $2{,}4 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

The prefix milli forms a single unit with the symbol of the meter, so the exponent $2$ also applies to the prefix. Since $1\,\mathrm{mm} = 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m}$, we have $1\,\mathrm{mm^2} = (10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m})^2 = 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^2}$, therefore $A = 2{,}4 \cdot 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The value $2{,}4 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^2}$ is obtained by applying the factor of the prefix without squaring it, the value $2{,}4 \cdot 10^{-9}\,\mathrm{m^2}$ by cubing it as for a volume. The value $2{,}4 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}$ corresponds to square centimeters, since $1\,\mathrm{cm^2} = 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}$.
