For which value of $\lambda$ are the vectors $\vec{A} = 2\,\hat{\imath} + \lambda\,\hat{\jmath} + \hat{k}$ and $\vec{B} = 3\,\hat{\imath} - 2\,\hat{\jmath} + 4\,\hat{k}$ perpendicular?

- $\lambda = -5$
- $\lambda = 3$
- $\lambda = -3$
- $\lambda = 5$

## Explanation

Two nonzero vectors are perpendicular if and only if their dot product is zero. In components we have $\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B} = 2 \cdot 3 + \lambda \cdot (-2) + 1 \cdot 4 = 10 - 2\lambda$. The equation $10 - 2\lambda = 0$ has the solution $\lambda = 5$. The value $\lambda = 3$ is obtained by neglecting the product of the components along $z$, and the value $\lambda = -5$ by getting the sign of the component $B_y$ wrong.
