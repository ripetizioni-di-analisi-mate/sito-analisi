A boat moves on the surface of a lake, and in a reference frame with the origin at the pier its position in meters is $\vec{r}(t) = (2.0 + 3.0\,t)\,\hat{\imath} + (1.0 - 4.0\,t)\,\hat{\jmath}$, with $t$ in seconds. With what speed does the boat move?

- $7.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Differentiating the components gives $\vec{v} = 3.0\,\hat{\imath} - 4.0\,\hat{\jmath}$ in $\mathrm{m/s}$, which does not depend on time, so the motion is uniform rectilinear motion. The magnitude is
$$
v = \sqrt{3.0^2 + (-4.0)^2}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 5.0\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The value $7.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ adds the absolute values of the components, and the number $2.2$ is the magnitude of the initial position in meters.
