A mass moves in a straight line. Mass is measured in kilograms and speed in kilometers per hour. Given the mass $m = 5\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the speed $v = 54\,\mathrm{km/h}$, what is the magnitude of the momentum $p$?

- $972\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$
- $75\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$
- $150\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$
- $270\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$

## Explanation

To express $mv$ in SI units, divide the speed in kilometers per hour by $3{,}6$. In the stated units, the relation is $p = \dfrac{5 \cdot m \cdot v}{18}$. Substituting the given values yields $75\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$.
