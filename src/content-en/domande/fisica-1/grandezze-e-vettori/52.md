A constant force has a component along the displacement equal to half its magnitude. The work is positive. Given the magnitude of the displacement $d = 4\,\mathrm{m}$ and the work $W = 36\,\mathrm{J}$, what is the magnitude of the force $F$?

- $36\,\mathrm{N}$
- $27\,\mathrm{N}$
- $9\,\mathrm{N}$
- $18\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Work is the dot product of force and displacement. Only the parallel component contributes. In the stated units, the relation is $W = \dfrac{F \cdot d}{2}$. Solving for the unknown gives $F = \dfrac{2 \cdot W}{d}$. Substituting the given values yields $18\,\mathrm{N}$.
