A displacement has components $\vec{s} = (2.0;\ -3.0;\ 6.0)\,\mathrm{m}$. What is its magnitude?

- $5.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $11\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3.6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $7.0\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The magnitude of a vector in space is the square root of the sum of the squares of the components, so
$$
s = \sqrt{2.0^2 + (-3.0)^2 + 6.0^2}\,\mathrm{m} = \sqrt{49}\,\mathrm{m} = 7.0\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $5.0\,\mathrm{m}$ is the sum of the components, $11\,\mathrm{m}$ the sum of their absolute values, and $3.6\,\mathrm{m}$ is obtained by neglecting the $z$ component.
