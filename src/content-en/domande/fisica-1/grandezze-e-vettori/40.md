What is the cross product $(2\,\hat{\imath}) \times (3\,\hat{\jmath} + \hat{k})$?

- $-2\,\hat{\jmath} + 6\,\hat{k}$
- $2\,\hat{\jmath} - 6\,\hat{k}$
- $6\,\hat{k}$
- $2\,\hat{\jmath} + 6\,\hat{k}$

## Explanation

For the unit vectors, the identities $\hat{\imath} \times \hat{\jmath} = \hat{k}$ and $\hat{\imath} \times \hat{k} = -\,\hat{k} \times \hat{\imath} = -\hat{\jmath}$ hold. Hence
$$
(2\,\hat{\imath}) \times (3\,\hat{\jmath} + \hat{k}) = 6\,\hat{\imath} \times \hat{\jmath} + 2\,\hat{\imath} \times \hat{k} = 6\,\hat{k} - 2\,\hat{\jmath} .
$$
The same result follows from the component formula with $\vec{A} = (2, 0, 0)$ and $\vec{B} = (0, 3, 1)$. The vector $2\,\hat{\jmath} + 6\,\hat{k}$ is obtained by writing $\hat{\imath} \times \hat{k} = \hat{\jmath}$, and the vector $2\,\hat{\jmath} - 6\,\hat{k}$ is the product with the factors swapped.
