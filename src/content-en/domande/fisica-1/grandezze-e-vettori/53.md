A point travels an arc of length $s$ on a circle of radius $R$ in the time $t$, with speed $v$. Which of the following quantities is dimensionless?

- $\dfrac{v}{R}$
- $\dfrac{s}{t}$
- $\dfrac{s}{R}$
- $\dfrac{v\,t}{R^2}$

## Explanation

The ratio $\dfrac{s}{R}$ is a length divided by a length, so it has dimensions $[1]$, and it is the central angle measured in radians. The ratio $\dfrac{v}{R}$ has dimensions $[T^{-1}]$, those of an angular velocity, and $\dfrac{s}{t}$ has the dimensions of a speed. Finally $v\,t$ is a length, so $\dfrac{v\,t}{R^2}$ has dimensions $[L^{-1}]$.
