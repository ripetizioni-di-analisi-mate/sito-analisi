A constant force has a component along the displacement equal to half its magnitude. The work is positive. Given the magnitude of the force $F = 18\,\mathrm{N}$ and the work $W = 36\,\mathrm{J}$, what is the magnitude of the displacement $d$?

- $8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Work is the dot product of force and displacement. Only the parallel component contributes. In the stated units, the relation is $W = \dfrac{F \cdot d}{2}$. Solving for the unknown gives $d = \dfrac{2 \cdot W}{F}$. Substituting the given values yields $4\,\mathrm{m}$.
