A body of mass $4{,}0\,\mathrm{kg}$ rests on a plane inclined at $30^\circ$ to the horizontal. What is the magnitude of the component of the weight parallel to the plane?

- $20\,\mathrm{N}$
- $34\,\mathrm{N}$
- $39\,\mathrm{N}$
- $2{,}0\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The weight has magnitude $mg = 4{,}0 \cdot 9{,}81\,\mathrm{N} \approx 39\,\mathrm{N}$. With one axis parallel to the plane and one perpendicular, the angle between the weight and the perpendicular to the plane equals the inclination $\alpha$, so the component of the weight parallel to the plane has magnitude $mg\sin\alpha$. We obtain $mg\sin 30^\circ = 39{,}24 \cdot \dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{N} \approx 20\,\mathrm{N}$, and this component points down along the plane. The value $34\,\mathrm{N}$ is the perpendicular component $mg\cos\alpha$, and the value $2{,}0\,\mathrm{N}$ is obtained by using the mass instead of the weight.
