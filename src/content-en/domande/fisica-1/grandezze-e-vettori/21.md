The period $T$ of oscillation of a body of mass $m$ hanging from a spring with spring constant $k$, with $[k] = [M\,T^{-2}]$, is assumed to have the form $T = C\,m^{\alpha} k^{\beta}$ with $C$ dimensionless. What are the values of $\alpha$ and $\beta$?

- $\alpha = -\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $\beta = \dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\alpha = 1$ and $\beta = -1$
- $\alpha = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and $\beta = -\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\alpha = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and $\beta = \dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The dimensions of the right-hand side are $[M]^{\alpha}\,[M\,T^{-2}]^{\beta} = [M^{\alpha + \beta}\,T^{-2\beta}]$, and by the principle of homogeneity they coincide with $[T]$. Equating the exponents of $[M]$ and of $[T]$ gives the system
$$
\begin{cases}
\alpha + \beta = 0 \\[4pt]
-2\beta = 1
\end{cases}
$$
so $\beta = -\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $\alpha = \dfrac{1}{2}$. Therefore $T = C\sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k}}$, and the period increases with the mass and decreases as the spring constant increases. The pair $\alpha = -\dfrac{1}{2}$, $\beta = \dfrac{1}{2}$ gives the dimensions $[T^{-1}]$, that is, those of a frequency.
