The vertical component of position follows $y(t)=ct^2$, with constant $c$. The horizontal component increases at constant speed. Given the quadratic coefficient $c = 4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and the measurement time $t = 3\,\mathrm{s}$, what is the vertical velocity component $v_{y}$?

- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $24\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $36\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $48\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Each position component is differentiated with respect to time. The vertical component is the derivative of $ct^2$. In the stated units, the relation is $v_{y} = 2 \cdot c \cdot t$. Substituting the given values yields $24\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
