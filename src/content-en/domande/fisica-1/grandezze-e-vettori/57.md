A force's line of action is perpendicular to a lever's axis and passes at distance $d$ from it. Given the perpendicular moment arm $d = 3\,\mathrm{m}$ and the magnitude of the torque $M = 72\,\mathrm{N\,m}$, what is the magnitude of the force $F$?

- $12\,\mathrm{N}$
- $48\,\mathrm{N}$
- $24\,\mathrm{N}$
- $216\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the cross product of position and force equals the force times the perpendicular distance to its line of action. In the stated units, the relation is $M = F \cdot d$. Solving for the unknown gives $F = \dfrac{M}{d}$. Substituting the given values yields $24\,\mathrm{N}$.
