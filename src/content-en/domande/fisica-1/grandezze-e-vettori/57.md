A triangle has its vertices at the points $O = (0;\ 0)$, $P = (2;\ 1)$ and $Q = (1;\ 3)$, with coordinates in meters. What is its area?

- $5.0\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $3.5\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $7.0\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

The magnitude of $\overrightarrow{OP} \times \overrightarrow{OQ}$ is the area of the parallelogram with sides $\overrightarrow{OP}$ and $\overrightarrow{OQ}$, and the triangle is half of it. The two vectors lie in the $xy$-plane, so the product has only the $z$ component:
$$
\big(\overrightarrow{OP} \times \overrightarrow{OQ}\big)_z = 2 \cdot 3 - 1 \cdot 1 = 5\,\mathrm{m^2} .
$$
Therefore the area of the triangle is $\dfrac{5}{2}\,\mathrm{m^2} = 2.5\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The value $5.0\,\mathrm{m^2}$ is the area of the parallelogram, the value $3.5\,\mathrm{m^2}$ is obtained by adding the two products instead of subtracting them.
