How many significant figures does the measurement $0.04060\,\mathrm{kg}$ have?

- $5$
- $4$
- $6$
- $3$

## Explanation

Significant figures are counted starting from the first nonzero digit from the left, which here is the $4$. The zeros preceding the $4$ only indicate the position of the decimal point, so they are not significant. The zero between $4$ and $6$ and the trailing zero are significant, and the trailing zero declares a precision to the hundred-thousandth of a kilogram. Therefore there are four significant figures, namely $4$, $0$, $6$ and $0$, as can also be read from the scientific notation $4.060 \cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{kg}$.
