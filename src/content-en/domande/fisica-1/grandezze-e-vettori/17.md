An aluminum block has a mass of $540\,\mathrm{g}$ and a volume of $200\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. What is its density in $\mathrm{kg/m^3}$?

- $2{,}7 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $2{,}7 \cdot 10^{-1}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $2{,}7\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $2{,}7 \cdot 10^{6}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

In SI units the mass is $m = 0{,}54\,\mathrm{kg}$. Since $1\,\mathrm{cm^3} = 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^3}$, the volume is $V = 200 \cdot 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^3} = 2{,}0 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^3}$. The density is therefore
$$
\rho = \frac{m}{V} = \frac{0{,}54\,\mathrm{kg}}{2{,}0 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^3}} = 2{,}7 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3} .
$$
The number $2{,}7$ is the density in $\mathrm{g/cm^3}$, that is, the ratio of the given values without unit conversions. The value $2{,}7 \cdot 10^{6}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ is obtained by converting the volume and leaving the mass in grams.
