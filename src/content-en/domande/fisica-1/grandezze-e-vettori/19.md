The elastic force of a spring has magnitude $F = k\,x$, where $x$ is the extension of the spring. What are the dimensions of the spring constant $k$?

- $[M\,L^{2}\,T^{-2}]$
- $[M\,T^{-2}]$
- $[M\,L^{-1}\,T^{-2}]$
- $[M\,L\,T^{-2}]$

## Explanation

From the relation we get $k = \dfrac{F}{x}$. Since $[F] = [M\,L\,T^{-2}]$ and $[x] = [L]$, we have
$$
[k] = \frac{[M\,L\,T^{-2}]}{[L]} = [M\,T^{-2}] .
$$
Therefore $k$ is measured in $\mathrm{N/m}$, that is, in $\mathrm{kg/s^2}$. The dimensions $[M\,L\,T^{-2}]$ are those of force, and the dimensions $[M\,L^{2}\,T^{-2}]$ are obtained by multiplying the force by the extension instead of dividing it.
