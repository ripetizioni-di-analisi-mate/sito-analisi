The air resistance on a fast-moving body is written $F = \beta v^2$, where $F$ is a force and $v$ a velocity. What is the SI unit of the coefficient $\beta$?

- $\mathrm{kg/m}$
- $\mathrm{kg/s}$
- $\mathrm{kg\cdot m^3/s^4}$
- $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s^2}$

## Explanation

From the relation we get $\beta = \dfrac{F}{v^2}$. Since $[F] = [M\,L\,T^{-2}]$ and $[v^2] = [L^2\,T^{-2}]$, the dimensions of $\beta$ are
$$
[\beta] = \frac{[M\,L\,T^{-2}]}{[L^2\,T^{-2}]} = [M\,L^{-1}] .
$$
Therefore $\beta$ is measured in $\mathrm{kg/m}$. The unit $\mathrm{kg/s}$ is obtained by dividing the force by $v$ instead of $v^2$, the unit $\mathrm{kg\cdot m^3/s^4}$ by multiplying the force by $v^2$, and $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s^2}$ is the newton, the unit of the force itself.
