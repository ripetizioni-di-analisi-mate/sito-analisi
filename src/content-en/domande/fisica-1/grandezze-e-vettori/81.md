A cart travels in a straight line, in the same direction, first $48.27\,\mathrm{m}$ and then $7.3\,\mathrm{m}$, taking $4.000\,\mathrm{s}$ in all. With how many significant figures is the average speed written?

- With two
- With four
- With three
- With five

## Explanation

The total distance is a sum, so it has as many decimal places as the datum with the fewest, and therefore $48.27\,\mathrm{m} + 7.3\,\mathrm{m} = 55.57\,\mathrm{m}$ is written $55.6\,\mathrm{m}$, with three significant figures. The average speed is a quotient, so it has as many significant figures as the datum with the fewest between $55.6\,\mathrm{m}$ (three) and $4.000\,\mathrm{s}$ (four). Therefore it is written with **three** significant figures, $13.9\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The answer with two figures applies the product rule to $7.3\,\mathrm{m}$, which instead enters a sum.
