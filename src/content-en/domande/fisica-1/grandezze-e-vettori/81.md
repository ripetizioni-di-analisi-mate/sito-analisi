A uniform solid sample is weighed in grams and its volume is measured in cubic centimeters. The initial data are the mass $m = 180\,\mathrm{g}$ and the volume $V = 60\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. Change the mass to $240\,\mathrm{g}$ and the volume to $80\,\mathrm{cm^3}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the final value of $\rho$ minus its initial value?

- $2\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $3000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $1\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

The ratio of mass to volume is initially in grams per cubic centimeter. One gram per cubic centimeter is a thousand kilograms per cubic meter. The relation $\rho = \dfrac{1000 \cdot m}{V}$ gives $3000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ before the change and $3000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ after it. Subtracting the initial value from the final value gives $0\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.
