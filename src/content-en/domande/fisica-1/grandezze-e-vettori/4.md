- $6.24 \cdot 10^{2}\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $6.24 \cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $6.24\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

The product of the two measurements is $21.0\,\mathrm{cm} \cdot 29.7\,\mathrm{cm} = 623.7\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. Since $1\,\mathrm{cm^2} = (10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m})^2 = 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}$, the area equals $623.7 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2} = 6.237 \cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Both data have three significant figures, so the product also has three, and the area is written $A = 6.24 \cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The value $6.24\,\mathrm{m^2}$ corresponds to $1\,\mathrm{cm^2} = 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m^2}$, that is, to the factor of the prefix not squared, and the value $6.24 \cdot 10^{2}\,\mathrm{m^2}$ is obtained by multiplying by $10^{4}$ instead of dividing.
