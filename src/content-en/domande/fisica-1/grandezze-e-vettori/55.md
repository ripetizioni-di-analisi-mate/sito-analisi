Two forces of magnitude $5.0\,\mathrm{N}$ and $8.0\,\mathrm{N}$ are applied at the same point and make an angle of $60^\circ$ with each other. What is the magnitude of the resultant?

- $7.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $11\,\mathrm{N}$
- $9.4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $13\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The square of the magnitude of the resultant is the dot product of $\vec{R} = \vec{F}_1 + \vec{F}_2$ with itself, so
$$
R^2 = F_1^2 + F_2^2 + 2 F_1 F_2 \cos 60^\circ = (25 + 64 + 40)\,\mathrm{N^2} = 129\,\mathrm{N^2} .
$$
Therefore $R = \sqrt{129}\,\mathrm{N} \approx 11\,\mathrm{N}$. The value $7.0\,\mathrm{N}$ is the magnitude of the difference $\vec{F}_1 - \vec{F}_2$, the value $9.4\,\mathrm{N}$ holds for perpendicular forces and $13\,\mathrm{N}$ for forces with the same direction and sense.
