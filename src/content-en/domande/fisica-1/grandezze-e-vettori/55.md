A constant force has a component along the displacement equal to half its magnitude. The work is positive. The initial data are the magnitude of the force $F = 18\,\mathrm{N}$ and the magnitude of the displacement $d = 4\,\mathrm{m}$. Change the magnitude of the force to $12\,\mathrm{N}$ and the magnitude of the displacement to $8\,\mathrm{m}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the final value of $W$ minus its initial value?

- $12\,\mathrm{J}$
- $6\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{J}$
- $24\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Work is the dot product of force and displacement. Only the parallel component contributes. The relation $W = \dfrac{F \cdot d}{2}$ gives $36\,\mathrm{J}$ before the change and $48\,\mathrm{J}$ after it. Subtracting the initial value from the final value gives $12\,\mathrm{J}$.
