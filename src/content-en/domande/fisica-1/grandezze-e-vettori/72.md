The force $\vec F$ has components $F_x$ and $F_y=2F_x$ in the plane. The displacement is in the positive $y$ direction. Given the magnitude of the displacement $d = 3\,\mathrm{m}$ and the work done by the force $W = 48\,\mathrm{J}$, what is the horizontal component of the force $F_{x}$?

- $16\,\mathrm{N}$
- $\dfrac{16}{3}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $8\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The contribution of $F_x$ to the dot product is zero because the displacement is perpendicular to the $x$ axis. In the stated units, the relation is $W = 2 \cdot F_{x} \cdot d$. Solving for the unknown gives $F_{x} = \dfrac{W}{2 \cdot d}$. Substituting the given values yields $8\,\mathrm{N}$.
