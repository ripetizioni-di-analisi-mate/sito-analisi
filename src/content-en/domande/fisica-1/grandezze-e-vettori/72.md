What is the dot product of the vectors $\vec{A} = (2;\ -1;\ 3)$ and $\vec{B} = (1;\ 4;\ 2)$?

- $12$
- $4$
- $-8$
- $8$

## Explanation

In components the dot product is the sum of the products of corresponding components, so
$$
\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B} = 2 \cdot 1 + (-1) \cdot 4 + 3 \cdot 2 = 2 - 4 + 6 = 4 .
$$
The value $12$ is obtained by neglecting the sign of $A_y$, the value $8$ by forgetting the term $A_y B_y$.
