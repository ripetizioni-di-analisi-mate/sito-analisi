A pipe delivers water at a constant rate. Volume is measured in liters and time in minutes. Given the delivery time $t = 2\,\mathrm{min}$ and the flow rate $Q = 3\,\mathrm{L/s}$, what is the volume delivered $V$?

- $\dfrac{1}{10}\,\mathrm{L}$
- $720\,\mathrm{L}$
- $360\,\mathrm{L}$
- $6\,\mathrm{L}$

## Explanation

Multiply the time in minutes by sixty to obtain seconds. The flow rate is the volume divided by that time. In the stated units, the relation is $Q = \dfrac{V}{60 \cdot t}$. Solving for the unknown gives $V = 60 \cdot Q \cdot t$. Substituting the given values yields $360\,\mathrm{L}$.
