The fuel consumption of a car is $5.0$ liters of fuel every $100\,\mathrm{km}$. The consumption is a volume divided by a length. What is its value in SI units?

- $5.0 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $5.0 \cdot 10^{-11}\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $5.0 \cdot 10^{-8}\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $5.0 \cdot 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

Since $1\,\mathrm{L} = 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^3}$ and $100\,\mathrm{km} = 10^{5}\,\mathrm{m}$, we have
$$
\frac{5.0 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^3}}{10^{5}\,\mathrm{m}} = 5.0 \cdot 10^{-8}\,\mathrm{m^2} .
$$
A volume divided by a length has the dimensions of an area, and it represents the cross section of a thread of fuel as long as the route. The value $5.0 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{m^2}$ is obtained by leaving the $100\,\mathrm{km}$ as $100\,\mathrm{m}$, the value $5.0 \cdot 10^{-11}\,\mathrm{m^2}$ by treating the liter as a cubic centimeter.
