A mass moves in a straight line. Mass is measured in kilograms and speed in kilometers per hour. The initial data are the mass $m = 5\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the speed $v = 54\,\mathrm{km/h}$. Change the mass to $8\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the speed to $36\,\mathrm{km/h}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the final value of $p$ minus its initial value?

- $-5\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$
- $18\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$
- $\dfrac{324}{5}\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$

## Explanation

To express $mv$ in SI units, divide the speed in kilometers per hour by $3.6$. The relation $p = \dfrac{5 \cdot m \cdot v}{18}$ gives $75\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$ before the change and $80\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$ after it. Subtracting the initial value from the final value gives $5\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$.
