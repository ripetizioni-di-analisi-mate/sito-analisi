One estimates the number of heartbeats in a lifetime of $80$ years, with an average rate of $60$ beats per minute and a year of $365$ days. What is the order of magnitude of the number of beats?

- $10^{8}$
- $10^{9}$
- $10^{10}$
- $10^{7}$

## Explanation

The number of minutes in $80$ years is $80 \cdot 365 \cdot 24 \cdot 60 \approx 4.20 \cdot 10^{7}$. Multiplying by $60$ beats per minute gives about $2.52 \cdot 10^{9}$ beats. Since $2.52 < \sqrt{10} \approx 3.16$, the order of magnitude is $10^{9}$. The value $10^{8}$ corresponds to the minutes, that is, to one beat per minute.
