A force's line of action is perpendicular to a lever's axis and passes at distance $d$ from it. The initial data are the magnitude of the force $F = 24\,\mathrm{N}$ and the perpendicular moment arm $d = 3\,\mathrm{m}$. Change the perpendicular moment arm to $2\,\mathrm{m}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the ratio of the final value of $M$ to its initial value?

- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $1$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the cross product of position and force equals the force times the perpendicular distance to its line of action. The relation $M = F \cdot d$ gives $72\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ before the change and $48\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ after it. The ratio is $\dfrac{2}{3}$.
