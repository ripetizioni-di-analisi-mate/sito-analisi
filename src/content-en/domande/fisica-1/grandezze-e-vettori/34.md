What is the unit vector of the vector $\vec{F} = (6{,}0\,\hat{\imath} - 8{,}0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{N}$?

- $0{,}60\,\hat{\imath} + 0{,}80\,\hat{\jmath}$
- $0{,}43\,\hat{\imath} - 0{,}57\,\hat{\jmath}$
- $0{,}80\,\hat{\imath} - 0{,}60\,\hat{\jmath}$
- $0{,}60\,\hat{\imath} - 0{,}80\,\hat{\jmath}$

## Explanation

The unit vector of a nonzero vector is $\hat{u}_F = \dfrac{\vec{F}}{F}$. The magnitude is $F = \sqrt{6{,}0^2 + 8{,}0^2}\,\mathrm{N} = 10\,\mathrm{N}$, so
$$
\hat{u}_F = \frac{(6{,}0\,\hat{\imath} - 8{,}0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{N}}{10\,\mathrm{N}} = 0{,}60\,\hat{\imath} - 0{,}80\,\hat{\jmath} .
$$
The unit vector is dimensionless and has magnitude $1$, since $0{,}60^2 + 0{,}80^2 = 1$. The vector $0{,}43\,\hat{\imath} - 0{,}57\,\hat{\jmath}$ is obtained by dividing by the sum of the absolute values of the components, $14\,\mathrm{N}$, and its magnitude is different from $1$.
