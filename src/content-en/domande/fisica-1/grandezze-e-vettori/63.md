An open rectangular tank has a square base and is filled with liquid to height $h$. Given the base side length $l = 2\,\mathrm{m}$ and the liquid volume $V = 12000\,\mathrm{L}$, what is the liquid depth $h$?

- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $30\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The volume in cubic meters is the product of area $l^2$ and height. Each cubic meter contains one thousand liters. In the stated units, the relation is $V = 1000 \cdot h \cdot l^{2}$. Solving for the unknown gives $h = \dfrac{V}{1000 \cdot l^{2}}$. Substituting the given values yields $3\,\mathrm{m}$.
