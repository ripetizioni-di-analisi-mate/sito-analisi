A force $\vec{F}$ has magnitude $2.0\,\mathrm{N}$ and points east. What are the magnitude and the direction of the force $-3\vec{F}$?

- $6.0\,\mathrm{N}$, pointing east
- $2.0\,\mathrm{N}$, pointing west
- $0.67\,\mathrm{N}$, pointing west
- $6.0\,\mathrm{N}$, pointing west

## Explanation

The product of a number $k$ and a vector has magnitude equal to $|k|$ times the magnitude of the vector, so $-3\vec{F}$ has magnitude $|-3| \cdot 2.0\,\mathrm{N} = 6.0\,\mathrm{N}$. The number $-3$ is negative, therefore $-3\vec{F}$ has the same line of action as $\vec{F}$ and the opposite sense, that is, it points west. The force of $6.0\,\mathrm{N}$ pointing east is $3\vec{F}$, the force of $2.0\,\mathrm{N}$ pointing west is $-\vec{F}$, and the magnitude $0.67\,\mathrm{N}$ is obtained by dividing by $3$ instead of multiplying.
