A runner is running at $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the runner's speed in $\mathrm{km/h}$?

- $0{,}90\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $54\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $5{,}4 \cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $4{,}2\,\mathrm{km/h}$

## Explanation

There are $3600\,\mathrm{s}$ in one hour, so in one hour the runner covers $15 \cdot 3600\,\mathrm{m} = 54\,000\,\mathrm{m}$. Since $1\,\mathrm{km} = 1000\,\mathrm{m}$, this distance equals $54\,\mathrm{km}$, therefore the speed is $54\,\mathrm{km/h}$. In general, one goes from $\mathrm{m/s}$ to $\mathrm{km/h}$ by multiplying by $3{,}6$. The value $4{,}2\,\mathrm{km/h}$ is obtained by dividing by $3{,}6$, that is, by applying the conversion in the opposite direction, and the value $0{,}90\,\mathrm{km/h}$ by using $60$ seconds in one hour instead of $3600$.
