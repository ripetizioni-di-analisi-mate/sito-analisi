In a planar problem, the first force has components $(a,0)$ and the second $(0,b)$, in newtons. The resultant is in the first quadrant. Given the vertical component $b = 9\,\mathrm{N}$ and the tangent of the angle from the positive horizontal axis $q = \dfrac{3}{4}$, what is the horizontal component $a$?

- $12\,\mathrm{N}$
- $6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $\dfrac{27}{4}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $24\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The slope of the resultant is its vertical component divided by its horizontal component. In the stated units, the relation is $q = \dfrac{b}{a}$. Solving for the unknown gives $a = \dfrac{b}{q}$. Substituting the given values yields $12\,\mathrm{N}$.
