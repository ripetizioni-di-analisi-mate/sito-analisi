A uniform solid sample is weighed in grams and its volume is measured in cubic centimeters. Given the volume $V = 60\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ and the density $\rho = 3000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$, what is the mass $m$?

- $1800\,\mathrm{g}$
- $180\,\mathrm{g}$
- $360\,\mathrm{g}$
- $180000\,\mathrm{g}$

## Explanation

The ratio of mass to volume is initially in grams per cubic centimeter. One gram per cubic centimeter is a thousand kilograms per cubic meter. In the stated units, the relation is $\rho = \dfrac{1000 \cdot m}{V}$. Solving for the unknown gives $m = \dfrac{V \cdot \rho}{1000}$. Substituting the given values yields $180\,\mathrm{g}$.
