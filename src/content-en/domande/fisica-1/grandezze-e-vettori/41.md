A bolt is tightened with a wrench by applying a force of $40\,\mathrm{N}$ at a point of the wrench $30\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the axis of the bolt. The force forms an angle of $30^\circ$ with the wrench. What is the magnitude of the torque of the force about the axis of the bolt?

- $6{,}0 \cdot 10^{2}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $6{,}0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the torque is $\tau = r F\sin\theta$, where $\theta$ is the angle between the vector $\vec{r}$, directed along the wrench, and the force. With $r = 0{,}30\,\mathrm{m}$ we obtain $\tau = 0{,}30 \cdot 40 \cdot \sin 30^\circ\,\mathrm{N\cdot m} = 6{,}0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$. The lever arm is $b = r\sin\theta = 0{,}15\,\mathrm{m}$, that is, the distance of the axis from the line of action of the force. The value $10\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$ is obtained by using the cosine instead of the sine, the value $12\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$ by taking the whole distance $r$ as the lever arm, and the value $6{,}0 \cdot 10^{2}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$ by leaving $r$ in centimeters.
