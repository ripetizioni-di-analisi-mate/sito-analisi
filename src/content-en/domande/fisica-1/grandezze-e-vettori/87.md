A vector in the $xy$-plane has magnitude $10\,\mathrm{N}$ and component $A_x = 6.0\,\mathrm{N}$. What values can the component $A_y$ take?

- Only $8.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{N}$ or $-4.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- Only $4.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $8.0\,\mathrm{N}$ or $-8.0\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

From the formula for the magnitude, $A^2 = A_x^2 + A_y^2$, we get $A_y^2 = (100 - 36)\,\mathrm{N^2} = 64\,\mathrm{N^2}$. The equation has two solutions, $A_y = 8.0\,\mathrm{N}$ and $A_y = -8.0\,\mathrm{N}$, which correspond to two vectors symmetric with respect to the $x$-axis, one in the first and one in the fourth quadrant. Both have magnitude $10\,\mathrm{N}$ and $x$ component equal to $6.0\,\mathrm{N}$. The value $4.0\,\mathrm{N}$ is obtained by subtracting the magnitudes instead of their squares.
