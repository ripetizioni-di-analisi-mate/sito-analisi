A crate is dragged $8.0\,\mathrm{m}$ across a horizontal floor by a constant force of $50\,\mathrm{N}$ inclined at $37^\circ$ above the horizontal, with $\cos 37^\circ = 0.80$ and $\sin 37^\circ = 0.60$. What is the work done by the force?

- $4.0 \cdot 10^{2}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $3.2 \cdot 10^{2}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $5.0 \cdot 10^{2}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $2.4 \cdot 10^{2}\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The displacement is horizontal and the force forms the angle $\theta = 37^\circ$ with it. The work of a constant force is $L = F s\cos\theta$, so $L = 50 \cdot 8.0 \cdot 0.80\,\mathrm{J} = 3.2 \cdot 10^{2}\,\mathrm{J}$. Only the horizontal component of the force, $F\cos\theta = 40\,\mathrm{N}$, does work. The value $2.4 \cdot 10^{2}\,\mathrm{J}$ is obtained by using the sine instead of the cosine, the value $4.0 \cdot 10^{2}\,\mathrm{J}$ by neglecting the inclination of the force, and the value $5.0 \cdot 10^{2}\,\mathrm{J}$ by dividing by $\cos\theta$ instead of multiplying.
