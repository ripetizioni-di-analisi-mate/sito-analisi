In a planar problem, the first force has components $(a,0)$ and the second $(0,b)$, in newtons. The resultant is in the first quadrant. Given the horizontal component $a = 12\,\mathrm{N}$ and the vertical component $b = 9\,\mathrm{N}$, what is the tangent of the angle from the positive horizontal axis $q$?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $21$

## Explanation

The slope of the resultant is its vertical component divided by its horizontal component. In the stated units, the relation is $q = \dfrac{b}{a}$. Substituting the given values yields $\dfrac{3}{4}$.
