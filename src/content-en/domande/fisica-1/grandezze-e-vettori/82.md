For which values of $a$ and $b$ are the vectors $\vec{A} = (a + 1;\ 3)$ and $\vec{B} = (4;\ b - 2)$ equal?

- $a = 5$ and $b = 1$
- $a = 3$ and $b = 5$
- $a = 3$ and $b = 1$
- $a = 4$ and $b = 3$

## Explanation

Two vectors are equal if and only if they have the same components, so the equality $\vec{A} = \vec{B}$ is equivalent to the system
$$
\begin{cases}
a + 1 = 4 \\[4pt]
3 = b - 2
\end{cases}
$$
Therefore $a = 3$ and $b = 5$. The pair $a = 5$, $b = 1$ is obtained by adding $1$ and subtracting $2$ instead of the opposite, the pair $a = 3$, $b = 1$ by getting only the second equation wrong, and the pair $a = 4$, $b = 3$ by setting each letter equal to the number appearing in the other vector.
