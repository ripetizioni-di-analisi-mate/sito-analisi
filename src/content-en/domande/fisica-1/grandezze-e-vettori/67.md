Which of the following mass measurements is written to the nearest gram?

- $2.50\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $2.503\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $2.5 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{g}$
- $2.5030\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

A gram is one thousandth of a kilogram, so a measurement in kilograms precise to the gram has its last significant figure in the third decimal place, like $2.503\,\mathrm{kg}$. The measurement $2.50\,\mathrm{kg}$ is precise to the hundredth of a kilogram, that is, to $10\,\mathrm{g}$. The measurement $2.5 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{g}$ has two significant figures, so it is precise to $100\,\mathrm{g}$, and $2.5030\,\mathrm{kg}$ is precise to a tenth of a gram.
