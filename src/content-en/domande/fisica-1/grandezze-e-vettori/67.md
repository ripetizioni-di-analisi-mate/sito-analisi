A mass moves in a straight line. Mass is measured in kilograms and speed in kilometers per hour. Given the speed $v = 54\,\mathrm{km/h}$ and the magnitude of the momentum $p = 75\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$, what is the mass $m$?

- $5\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $\dfrac{125}{324}\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $10\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $\dfrac{25}{18}\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

To express $mv$ in SI units, divide the speed in kilometers per hour by $3{,}6$. In the stated units, the relation is $p = \dfrac{5 \cdot m \cdot v}{18}$. Solving for the unknown gives $m = \dfrac{18 \cdot p}{5 \cdot v}$. Substituting the given values yields $5\,\mathrm{kg}$.
