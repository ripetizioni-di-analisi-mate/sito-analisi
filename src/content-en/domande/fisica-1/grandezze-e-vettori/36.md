What is the angle between the vectors $\vec{A} = (1{,}0\,\hat{\imath} + 1{,}0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{m}$ and $\vec{B} = (1{,}0\,\hat{\jmath} + 1{,}0\,\hat{k})\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $30^\circ$
- $45^\circ$
- $60^\circ$
- $120^\circ$

## Explanation

In components the dot product is $\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B} = 1{,}0 \cdot 0 + 1{,}0 \cdot 1{,}0 + 0 \cdot 1{,}0 = 1{,}0\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The magnitudes are $A = B = \sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{m}$, so
$$
\cos\theta = \frac{\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B}}{A B} = \frac{1{,}0\,\mathrm{m^2}}{2{,}0\,\mathrm{m^2}} = \frac{1}{2} .
$$
Since $\theta \in [0^\circ, 180^\circ]$, we obtain $\theta = 60^\circ$. The angle $30^\circ$ is the one whose sine equals $\dfrac{1}{2}$, and the angle $120^\circ$ corresponds to $\cos\theta = -\dfrac{1}{2}$.
