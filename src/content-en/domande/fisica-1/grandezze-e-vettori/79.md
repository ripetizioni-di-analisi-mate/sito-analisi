A uniform solid sample is weighed in grams and its volume is measured in cubic centimeters. Given the mass $m = 180\,\mathrm{g}$ and the density $\rho = 3000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$, what is the volume $V$?

- $60\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $6\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $120\,\mathrm{cm^3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{50}\,\mathrm{cm^3}$

## Explanation

The ratio of mass to volume is initially in grams per cubic centimeter. One gram per cubic centimeter is a thousand kilograms per cubic meter. In the stated units, the relation is $\rho = \dfrac{1000 \cdot m}{V}$. Solving for the unknown gives $V = \dfrac{1000 \cdot m}{\rho}$. Substituting the given values yields $60\,\mathrm{cm^3}$.
