Three forces in the $xy$-plane act on a point particle: $40\,\mathrm{N}$ at $0^\circ$, $30\,\mathrm{N}$ at $90^\circ$ and $20\,\mathrm{N}$ at $225^\circ$, with the angles measured counterclockwise from the positive $x$-semiaxis. What is the magnitude of the resultant?

- $69.9\,\mathrm{N}$
- $90.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $30.3\,\mathrm{N}$
- $50.0\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The angle $225^\circ$ lies in the third quadrant, so the third force has components $F_{3x} = F_{3y} = 20\cos 225^\circ\,\mathrm{N} \approx -14.1\,\mathrm{N}$. Adding the components gives $R_x = (40 - 14.1)\,\mathrm{N} = 25.9\,\mathrm{N}$ and $R_y = (30 - 14.1)\,\mathrm{N} = 15.9\,\mathrm{N}$, therefore
$$
R = \sqrt{25.9^2 + 15.9^2}\,\mathrm{N} \approx 30.3\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The value $90.0\,\mathrm{N}$ is the sum of the magnitudes, which coincides with the magnitude of the resultant only for forces with the same direction and the same orientation. The value $69.9\,\mathrm{N}$ is obtained by adding the components of the third force with a positive sign, and the value $50.0\,\mathrm{N}$ by neglecting the third force.
