What is $\dfrac{(5{,}0 \cdot 10^{-3}) \cdot (6{,}0 \cdot 10^{5})}{1{,}5 \cdot 10^{-2}}$ in scientific notation?

- $2{,}0 \cdot 10^{5}$
- $2{,}0 \cdot 10^{4}$
- $2{,}0 \cdot 10^{1}$
- $2{,}0 \cdot 10^{3}$

## Explanation

The coefficients are separated from the powers of ten. The coefficients give $\dfrac{5{,}0 \cdot 6{,}0}{1{,}5} = 20$, and the powers give
$$
\frac{10^{-3} \cdot 10^{5}}{10^{-2}} = 10^{-3 + 5 + 2} = 10^{4} .
$$
The product is $20 \cdot 10^{4}$, and since $20 = 2{,}0 \cdot 10^{1}$, in scientific notation it is written $2{,}0 \cdot 10^{5}$. The value $2{,}0 \cdot 10^{4}$ is obtained by replacing $20$ with $2{,}0$ without adding the factor $10$, the value $2{,}0 \cdot 10^{1}$ by subtracting $2$ instead of $-2$ in the exponent, and the value $2{,}0 \cdot 10^{3}$ by neglecting the power of ten in the denominator.
