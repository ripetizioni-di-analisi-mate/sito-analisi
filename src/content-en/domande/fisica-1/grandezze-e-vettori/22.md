The velocity of a body slowed down by a fluid varies in time according to the law $v(t) = v_0\,e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}}$. What are the dimensions of $\tau$?

- $[1]$
- $[T]$
- $[L\,T^{-1}]$
- $[T^{-1}]$

## Explanation

By the principle of dimensional homogeneity the argument of the exponential function is dimensionless, so $\left[\dfrac{t}{\tau}\right] = [1]$. Since $[t] = [T]$, it follows that $[\tau] = [T]$, and $\tau$ is measured in seconds. The dimensions $[T^{-1}]$ belong to a constant $\lambda$ written in the form $e^{-\lambda t}$. The dimensions $[L\,T^{-1}]$ are those of $v_0$, the factor in front of the exponential that gives $v$ the dimensions of a velocity.
