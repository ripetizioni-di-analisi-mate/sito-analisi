A crate moves up an inclined plane, pushed by a force parallel to the plane, and there is friction between the crate and the plane. Which of the forces acting on the crate does zero work?

- The friction force
- The weight of the crate
- The normal force of the plane
- The force pushing the crate

## Explanation

The work of a constant force along a straight-line displacement is $L = F s\cos\theta$, and it is zero when the force is perpendicular to the displacement. The crate moves along the plane and the normal force is perpendicular to the plane, so its work is zero. The pushing force has the same orientation as the displacement and does positive work. The weight forms an obtuse angle with the displacement, because the crate moves up, and friction has the orientation opposite to the displacement; therefore both do negative work.
