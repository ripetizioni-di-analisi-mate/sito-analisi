An open rectangular tank has a square base and is filled with liquid to height $h$. The initial data are the base side length $l = 2\,\mathrm{m}$ and the liquid depth $h = 3\,\mathrm{m}$. Change the base side length to $3\,\mathrm{m}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the ratio of the final value of $V$ to its initial value?

- $\dfrac{4}{9}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{9}{4}$

## Explanation

The volume in cubic meters is the product of area $l^2$ and height. Each cubic meter contains one thousand liters. The relation $V = 1000 \cdot h \cdot l^{2}$ gives $12000\,\mathrm{L}$ before the change and $27000\,\mathrm{L}$ after it. The ratio is $\dfrac{9}{4}$.
