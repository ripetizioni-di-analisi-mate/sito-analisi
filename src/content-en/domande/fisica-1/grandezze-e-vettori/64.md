A cart is constrained to move on a straight rail directed along the unit vector $\hat{u} = \dfrac{\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath}}{\sqrt{2}}$. The force $\vec{F} = (1.0\,\hat{\imath} + 5.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{N}$ acts on the cart. What is the component of the force along the rail, in the direction of $\hat{u}$?

- $6.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $5.1\,\mathrm{N}$
- $3.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4.2\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The component of $\vec{F}$ along the direction and sense of a unit vector $\hat{u}$ is the dot product $\vec{F} \cdot \hat{u}$. Therefore
$$
F_u = \frac{1.0 \cdot 1 + 5.0 \cdot 1}{\sqrt{2}}\,\mathrm{N} = \frac{6.0}{\sqrt{2}}\,\mathrm{N} \approx 4.2\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The value $6.0\,\mathrm{N}$ is obtained by using the vector $\hat{\imath} + \hat{\jmath}$, which has magnitude $\sqrt{2}$ and is therefore not a unit vector. The value $5.1\,\mathrm{N}$ is the magnitude of $\vec{F}$, which coincides with the component only for a force parallel to the rail.
