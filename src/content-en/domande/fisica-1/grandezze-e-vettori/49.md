Given $\vec{A} = (1;\ 2;\ 0)$ and $\vec{B} = (2;\ 4;\ 0)$, what result is obtained for $\vec{A} \times \vec{B}$?

- $10$
- $\vec{0}$
- $8\,\hat{k}$
- $(2;\ 8;\ 0)$

## Explanation

We have $\vec{B} = 2\vec{A}$, so the two vectors are parallel and the angle between them is $\theta = 0^\circ$. Since the magnitude of the cross product is $AB\sin\theta$, the product is the **zero vector**. The component formula also gives zero, because the only component that can be nonzero is $A_x B_y - A_y B_x = 1 \cdot 4 - 2 \cdot 2 = 0$. The number $10$ is the dot product, the vector $8\,\hat{k}$ is obtained by adding the two products instead of subtracting them, and $(2;\ 8;\ 0)$ by multiplying corresponding components.
