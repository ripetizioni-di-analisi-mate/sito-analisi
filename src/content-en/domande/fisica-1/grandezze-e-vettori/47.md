The vectors $\vec{A}$ and $\vec{B}$ in the figure are applied at the origin and have their tips at points with integer coordinates. What is $\vec{A} - \vec{B}$?

::grafico{vettori="0,0,3,2,A;0,0,-1,3,B" punti="3,2,pieno;-1,3,pieno" xmin=-1.8 xmax=3.8 ymin=-0.6 ymax=3.8 uguale=1 titolo="The vectors $\vec{A}$ and $\vec{B}$ applied at the origin; the tick marks on the axes are $1$ apart"}

- $(-4;\ 1)$
- $(4;\ -1)$
- $(2;\ 5)$
- $(2;\ -1)$

## Explanation

From the figure one reads $\vec{A} = (3;\ 2)$ and $\vec{B} = (-1;\ 3)$. The difference is computed component by component, so
$$
\vec{A} - \vec{B} = \big(3 - (-1);\ 2 - 3\big) = (4;\ -1) .
$$
The vector $(-4;\ 1)$ is $\vec{B} - \vec{A}$, the vector $(2;\ 5)$ is the sum $\vec{A} + \vec{B}$, and $(2;\ -1)$ is obtained by neglecting the sign of $B_x$.
