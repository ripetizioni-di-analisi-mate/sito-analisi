The force $\vec F$ has components $F_x$ and $F_y=2F_x$ in the plane. The displacement is in the positive $y$ direction. Given the horizontal component of the force $F_{x} = 8\,\mathrm{N}$ and the work done by the force $W = 48\,\mathrm{J}$, what is the magnitude of the displacement $d$?

- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The contribution of $F_x$ to the dot product is zero because the displacement is perpendicular to the $x$ axis. In the stated units, the relation is $W = 2 \cdot F_{x} \cdot d$. Solving for the unknown gives $d = \dfrac{W}{2 \cdot F_{x}}$. Substituting the given values yields $3\,\mathrm{m}$.
