A bar can rotate about the point $O$, and the four forces in the figure act on it. Which force has zero torque about $O$?

::grafico{assi=0 linee="0,0 4,0" vettori="4,0,5.4,0,F₁;4,0,4,1.4,F₂;2,0,3,1,F₃;3,0,3,-1.3,F₄" punti="0,0,pieno,O" xmin=-0.6 xmax=6 ymin=-1.7 ymax=1.8 uguale=1 titolo="The bar can rotate about the point $O$"}

- $\vec{F}_2$
- $\vec{F}_3$
- $\vec{F}_4$
- $\vec{F}_1$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the torque is $\tau = F\,b$, where $b$ is the distance of $O$ from the line of action of the force. The force $\vec{F}_1$ is directed along the bar, so its line of action passes through $O$ and the lever arm is zero. Therefore $\vec{F}_1$ has **zero torque**. The lines of action of $\vec{F}_2$, $\vec{F}_3$ and $\vec{F}_4$ do not pass through $O$, so their torques are nonzero.
