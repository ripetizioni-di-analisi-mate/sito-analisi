Two forces of magnitude $6.0\,\mathrm{N}$ and $8.0\,\mathrm{N}$ act on the same point particle, with arbitrary directions. Which of the following values can be the magnitude of their resultant?

- $15\,\mathrm{N}$
- $48\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $11\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the resultant is maximum when the forces have the same direction and the same orientation, and it then equals $6.0\,\mathrm{N} + 8.0\,\mathrm{N} = 14\,\mathrm{N}$. It is minimum when they have the same direction and opposite orientations, and it then equals $8.0\,\mathrm{N} - 6.0\,\mathrm{N} = 2.0\,\mathrm{N}$. As the angle between the forces varies, the magnitude takes all values between $2.0\,\mathrm{N}$ and $14\,\mathrm{N}$, so $11\,\mathrm{N}$ is a possible value. The values $1.0\,\mathrm{N}$ and $15\,\mathrm{N}$ lie outside this interval, and $48\,\mathrm{N}$ is the product of the magnitudes.
