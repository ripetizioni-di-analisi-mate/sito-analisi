The vertical component of position follows $y(t)=ct^2$, with constant $c$. The horizontal component increases at constant speed. Given the measurement time $t = 3\,\mathrm{s}$ and the vertical velocity component $v_{y} = 24\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the quadratic coefficient $c$?

- $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $\dfrac{8}{3}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Each position component is differentiated with respect to time. The vertical velocity component is the derivative of $ct^2$. In the stated units, the relation is $v_{y} = 2 \cdot c \cdot t$. Solving for the unknown gives $c = \dfrac{v_{y}}{2 \cdot t}$. Substituting the given values yields $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
