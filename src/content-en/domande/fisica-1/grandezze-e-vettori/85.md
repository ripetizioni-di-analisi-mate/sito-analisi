A flat wall of uniform thermal conductivity conducts heat in steady state. Its thickness is $0.20\,\mathrm{m}$ and the temperature difference across it is $10\,\mathrm{K}$. Given the thermal conductivity $k = 2\,\mathrm{W/(m\,K)}$ and the wall area $A = 3\,\mathrm{m^2}$, what is the rate of heat transfer $P$?

- $30\,\mathrm{W}$
- $3000\,\mathrm{W}$
- $300\,\mathrm{W}$
- $600\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

The temperature difference divided by thickness is $50\,\mathrm{K/m}$. The conduction law multiplies this by area and conductivity, giving watts. In the stated units, the relation is $P = 50 \cdot A \cdot k$. Substituting the given values yields $300\,\mathrm{W}$.
