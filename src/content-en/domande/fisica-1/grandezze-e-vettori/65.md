An open rectangular tank has a square base and is filled with liquid to height $h$. The initial data are the base side length $l = 2\,\mathrm{m}$ and the liquid depth $h = 3\,\mathrm{m}$. Change the base side length to $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and the liquid depth to $2\,\mathrm{m}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the final value of $V$ minus its initial value?

- $600\,\mathrm{L}$
- $-6000\,\mathrm{L}$
- $6000\,\mathrm{L}$
- $0\,\mathrm{L}$

## Explanation

The volume in cubic meters is the product of area $l^2$ and height. Each cubic meter contains one thousand liters. The relation $V = 1000 \cdot h \cdot l^{2}$ gives $12000\,\mathrm{L}$ before the change and $18000\,\mathrm{L}$ after it. Subtracting the initial value from the final value gives $6000\,\mathrm{L}$.
