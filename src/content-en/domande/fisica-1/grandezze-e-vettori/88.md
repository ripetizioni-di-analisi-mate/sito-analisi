A pipe delivers water at a constant rate. Volume is measured in liters and time in minutes. Given the volume delivered $V = 360\,\mathrm{L}$ and the delivery time $t = 2\,\mathrm{min}$, what is the flow rate $Q$?

- $10800\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $180\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{L/s}$

## Explanation

The time in minutes is multiplied by sixty to obtain seconds. The flow rate is the volume divided by that time. In the stated units, the relation is $Q = \dfrac{V}{60 \cdot t}$. Substituting the given values yields $3\,\mathrm{L/s}$.
