A mass moves in a straight line. Mass is measured in kilograms and speed in kilometers per hour. The initial data are the mass $m = 5\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the speed $v = 54\,\mathrm{km/h}$. Change the speed to $72\,\mathrm{km/h}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the ratio of the final value of $p$ to its initial value?

- $\dfrac{8}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $1$

## Explanation

To express $mv$ in SI units, divide the speed in kilometers per hour by $3{,}6$. The relation $p = \dfrac{5 \cdot m \cdot v}{18}$ gives $75\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$ before the change and $100\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$ after it. The ratio is $\dfrac{4}{3}$.
