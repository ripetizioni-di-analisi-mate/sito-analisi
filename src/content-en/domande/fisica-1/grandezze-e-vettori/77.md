A uniform solid sample is weighed in grams and its volume is measured in cubic centimeters. Given the mass $m = 180\,\mathrm{g}$ and the volume $V = 60\,\mathrm{cm^3}$, what is the density $\rho$?

- $300\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $6000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $3000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $3\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

The ratio of mass to volume is initially in grams per cubic centimeter. One gram per cubic centimeter is a thousand kilograms per cubic meter. In the stated units, the relation is $\rho = \dfrac{1000 \cdot m}{V}$. Substituting the given values yields $3000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.
