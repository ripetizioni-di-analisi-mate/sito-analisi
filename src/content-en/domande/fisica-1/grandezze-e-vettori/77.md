Consider the six vectors that go from the center of a regular hexagon with side $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ to its six vertices. What is the magnitude of their sum?

- $0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

In a regular hexagon every vertex has an opposite vertex symmetric to it with respect to the center. Therefore the six vectors split into three pairs of opposite vectors, and each pair has zero sum. Hence the sum of the six vectors is the zero vector, and its magnitude is $0\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $12\,\mathrm{m}$ is the sum of the magnitudes, which coincides with the magnitude of the sum only for vectors with the same direction and sense.
