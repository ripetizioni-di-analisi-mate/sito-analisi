The momentum of a body is $\vec{p} = m\vec{v}$ and is measured in $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$. In what unit is its derivative $\dfrac{d\vec{p}}{dt}$ measured?

- $\mathrm{N}$
- $\mathrm{J}$
- $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

The time derivative of a vector has the unit of the vector divided by the second. Therefore $\dfrac{d\vec{p}}{dt}$ is measured in $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s^2}$. Since $1\,\mathrm{N} = 1\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s^2}$, the unit is the **newton**, consistently with Newton's second law in the form $\vec{F} = \dfrac{d\vec{p}}{dt}$.
