A force has components $(3,4)\,\mathrm N$ and displacement is $(4,-3)\,\mathrm m$. What follows from their dot product?

- The work is $24\,\mathrm J$
- The work is zero
- The work is $25\,\mathrm J$
- The work is $-25\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The components contribute $3\cdot4$ and $4\cdot(-3)$. The sum is zero because the vectors are perpendicular.
