The shaft of an engine turns at $3000$ revolutions per minute. What is its angular velocity in $\mathrm{rad/s}$?

- $50.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $1.88 \cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $7.96\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $314\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

One revolution corresponds to an angle of $2\pi\,\mathrm{rad}$ and one minute to $60\,\mathrm{s}$. Therefore
$$
\omega = \frac{3000 \cdot 2\pi\,\mathrm{rad}}{60\,\mathrm{s}} = 100\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s} \approx 314\,\mathrm{rad/s} .
$$
The value $50.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ counts the revolutions per second without multiplying by $2\pi$, the value $1.88 \cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ does not convert minutes into seconds.
