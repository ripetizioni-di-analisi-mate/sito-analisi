A constant force has a component along the displacement equal to half its magnitude. The work is positive. The initial data are the magnitude of the force $F = 18\,\mathrm{N}$ and the magnitude of the displacement $d = 4\,\mathrm{m}$. Change the magnitude of the force to $27\,\mathrm{N}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the ratio of the final value of $W$ to its initial value?

- $1$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

Work is the dot product of force and displacement. Only the parallel component contributes. The relation $W = \dfrac{F \cdot d}{2}$ gives $36\,\mathrm{J}$ before the change and $54\,\mathrm{J}$ after it. The ratio is $\dfrac{3}{2}$.
