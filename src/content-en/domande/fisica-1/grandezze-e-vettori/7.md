- $a = 2$ and $b = -1$
- $a = 1$ and $b = 1$
- $a = -\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $b = \dfrac{3}{2}$
- $a = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and $b = \dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The dimensions of the left-hand side of $v = k\,g^{a} h^{b}$ are $[L\,T^{-1}]$, those of the right-hand side are $[L\,T^{-2}]^{a}\,[L]^{b} = [L^{a + b}\,T^{-2a}]$. Equating the exponents of $[L]$ and of $[T]$ gives the system
$$
\begin{cases}
a + b = 1 \\[4pt]
-2a = -1
\end{cases}
$$
so $a = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and $b = 1 - a = \dfrac{1}{2}$. Therefore $v = k\sqrt{g h}$. The pair $a = -\dfrac{1}{2}$, $b = \dfrac{3}{2}$ comes from a sign error in the exponent of $[T]$, and the pair $a = b = 1$ gives $g h$ the dimensions $[L^2\,T^{-2}]$, which are those of a squared velocity.
