The force $\vec F$ has components $F_x$ and $F_y=2F_x$ in the plane. The displacement is in the positive $y$ direction. Given the horizontal component of the force $F_{x} = 8\,\mathrm{N}$ and the magnitude of the displacement $d = 3\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the work done by the force $W$?

- $48\,\mathrm{J}$
- $96\,\mathrm{J}$
- $24\,\mathrm{J}$
- $72\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The contribution of $F_x$ to the dot product is zero because the displacement is perpendicular to the $x$ axis. In the stated units, the relation is $W = 2 \cdot F_{x} \cdot d$. Substituting the given values yields $48\,\mathrm{J}$.
