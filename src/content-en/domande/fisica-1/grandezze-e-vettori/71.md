A point travels half a turn of a circle of radius $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the magnitude of its displacement?

- $6.3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The displacement is the vector $\Delta\vec{r} = \vec{r}_2 - \vec{r}_1$ from the initial position to the final one, and it does not depend on the path followed. After half a turn the point is at the opposite end of a diameter, so the magnitude of the displacement is the diameter, $2R = 4.0\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $6.3\,\mathrm{m}$ is the length of the arc traveled, $\pi R$, and $0\,\mathrm{m}$ is the displacement after a full turn.
