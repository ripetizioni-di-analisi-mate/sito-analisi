The vertical component of position follows $y(t)=ct^2$, with constant $c$. The horizontal component increases at constant speed. Given the quadratic coefficient $c = 4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and the vertical velocity component $v_{y} = 24\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the measurement time $t$?

- $\sqrt{6}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Each position component is differentiated with respect to time. The vertical component is the derivative of $ct^2$. In the stated units, the relation is $v_{y} = 2 \cdot c \cdot t$. Solving for the unknown gives $t = \dfrac{v_{y}}{2 \cdot c}$. Substituting the given values yields $3\,\mathrm{s}$.
