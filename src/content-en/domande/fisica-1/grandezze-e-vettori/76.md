A force has magnitude $15\,\mathrm{N}$ and the direction and sense of the vector $\vec{d} = \hat{\imath} + 2\,\hat{\jmath} + 2\,\hat{k}$. What are its components?

- $(5.0;\ 10;\ 10)\,\mathrm{N}$
- $(15;\ 30;\ 30)\,\mathrm{N}$
- $(3.0;\ 6.0;\ 6.0)\,\mathrm{N}$
- $(1.7;\ 3.3;\ 3.3)\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The magnitude of $\vec{d}$ is $\sqrt{1 + 4 + 4} = 3$, so the unit vector of $\vec{d}$ is $\hat{u} = \dfrac{\vec{d}}{3} = \left(\dfrac{1}{3};\ \dfrac{2}{3};\ \dfrac{2}{3}\right)$. The force is $\vec{F} = F\,\hat{u}$, therefore
$$
\vec{F} = 15 \cdot \left(\frac{1}{3};\ \frac{2}{3};\ \frac{2}{3}\right)\mathrm{N} = (5.0;\ 10;\ 10)\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The vector $(15;\ 30;\ 30)\,\mathrm{N}$ has magnitude $45\,\mathrm{N}$, because it multiplies $\vec{d}$ by $15$ without dividing it by its magnitude.
