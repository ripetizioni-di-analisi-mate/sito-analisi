Which of the following units coincides with the joule?

- $\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s^2}$
- $\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s^3}$
- $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The joule is the unit of work, and $1\,\mathrm{J} = 1\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$, because work is the product of a force and a displacement. Since $1\,\mathrm{N} = 1\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s^2}$, we obtain $1\,\mathrm{J} = 1\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s^2}$. The unit $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s^2}$ is the newton, and $\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s^3}$ is the watt, that is, one joule per second. The unit $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$ is that of momentum.
