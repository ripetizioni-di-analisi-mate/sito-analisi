A force has components $\vec{F} = (4.0;\ 4.0;\ -2.0)\,\mathrm{N}$. What angle does it make with the positive $z$-semiaxis?

- $70.5^\circ$
- $19.5^\circ$
- $109.5^\circ$
- $160.5^\circ$

## Explanation

The magnitude is $F = \sqrt{16 + 16 + 4}\,\mathrm{N} = 6.0\,\mathrm{N}$. The component $F_z$ is the projection of $\vec{F}$ onto the $z$-axis, so $F_z = F\cos\gamma$, where $\gamma$ is the angle with the positive $z$-semiaxis. Therefore
$$
\cos\gamma = \frac{-2.0}{6.0} = -\frac{1}{3},
\qquad
\gamma \approx 109.5^\circ .
$$
The angle is obtuse because the component $F_z$ is negative. The value $70.5^\circ$ is obtained by neglecting the sign of $F_z$.
