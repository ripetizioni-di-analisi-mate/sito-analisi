A mass moves in a straight line. Mass is measured in kilograms and speed in kilometers per hour. Given the mass $m = 5\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the magnitude of the momentum $p = 75\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$, what is the speed $v$?

- $108\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $15\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $\dfrac{25}{6}\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $54\,\mathrm{km/h}$

## Explanation

To express $mv$ in SI units, divide the speed in kilometers per hour by $3{,}6$. In the stated units, the relation is $p = \dfrac{5 \cdot m \cdot v}{18}$. Solving for the unknown gives $v = \dfrac{18 \cdot p}{5 \cdot m}$. Substituting the given values yields $54\,\mathrm{km/h}$.
