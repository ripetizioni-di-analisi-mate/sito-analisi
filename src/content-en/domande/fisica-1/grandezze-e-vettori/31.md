A tracker reports $\vec r+\vec b$ for every true position $\vec r$, with an unknown constant vector offset $\vec b$. Which expression formed from two readings exactly reproduces the corresponding true quantity?

- The difference between the final and initial positions.
- The mean of the two position vectors.
- The magnitude of the final position.
- The sum of the magnitudes of the two positions.

## Explanation

Subtracting the readings gives $(\vec r_f+\vec b)-(\vec r_i+\vec b)=\vec r_f-\vec r_i$. Their mean retains the offset, and distances from the origin also generally depend on it.
