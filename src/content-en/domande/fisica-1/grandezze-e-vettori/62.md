Which of the following speeds has the largest magnitude?

- $100\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{km/min}$
- $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.0 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{cm/s}$

## Explanation

All speeds are expressed in $\mathrm{m/s}$. We have $100\,\mathrm{km/h} = \dfrac{100}{3.6}\,\mathrm{m/s} \approx 27.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $1.5\,\mathrm{km/min} = \dfrac{1500\,\mathrm{m}}{60\,\mathrm{s}} = 25\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Moreover $2.0 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{cm/s} = 2.0 \cdot 10^{3} \cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 20\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Therefore the largest speed is $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
