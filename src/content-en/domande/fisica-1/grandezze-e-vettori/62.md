An open rectangular tank has a square base and is filled with liquid to height $h$. Given the liquid depth $h = 3\,\mathrm{m}$ and the liquid volume $V = 12000\,\mathrm{L}$, what is the base side length $l$?

- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2 \cdot \sqrt{10}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The volume in cubic meters is the product of area $l^2$ and height. Each cubic meter contains one thousand liters. In the stated units, the relation is $V = 1000 \cdot h \cdot l^{2}$. Solving for the unknown gives $l = \dfrac{\sqrt{10} \cdot \sqrt{V}}{100 \cdot \sqrt{h}}$. Substituting the given values yields $2\,\mathrm{m}$.
