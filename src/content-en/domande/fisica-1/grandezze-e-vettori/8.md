- $(21{,}7\,\hat{\imath} + 12{,}5\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{N}$
- $(-12{,}5\,\hat{\imath} - 21{,}7\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The components are $F_x = F\cos\theta$ and $F_y = F\sin\theta$, and these formulas hold for every angle. Since $210^\circ = 180^\circ + 30^\circ$, we have $\cos 210^\circ = -\cos 30^\circ \approx -0{,}866$ and $\sin 210^\circ = -\sin 30^\circ = -\dfrac{1}{2}$. Hence $F_x = 25 \cdot (-0{,}866)\,\mathrm{N} \approx -21{,}7\,\mathrm{N}$ and $F_y = -\dfrac{25}{2}\,\mathrm{N} = -12{,}5\,\mathrm{N}$. Both components are negative, as for every vector in the third quadrant. The vector $(21{,}7\,\hat{\imath} + 12{,}5\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{N}$ is obtained by using $30^\circ$ instead of $210^\circ$, and it points opposite to the force.
