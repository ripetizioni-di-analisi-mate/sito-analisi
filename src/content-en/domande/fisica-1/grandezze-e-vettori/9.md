A velocity has components $v_x = -6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_y = 8.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What angle does it form with the positive $x$-semiaxis, measured counterclockwise?

- $-53.1^\circ$
- $126.9^\circ$
- $53.1^\circ$
- $143.1^\circ$

## Explanation

The ratio of the components is $\tan\theta = \dfrac{v_y}{v_x} = \dfrac{8.0}{-6.0} = -\dfrac{4}{3}$, and the arctangent gives $\arctan\left(-\dfrac{4}{3}\right) \approx -53.1^\circ$. This angle belongs to the fourth quadrant, while the vector, with $v_x < 0$ and $v_y > 0$, lies in the second quadrant. The required angle is therefore obtained by adding $180^\circ$, that is, $\theta \approx 126.9^\circ$. The value $-53.1^\circ$ is the angle of the opposite vector $(6.0;\ -8.0)\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and the value $143.1^\circ$ is obtained by swapping the two components in the ratio.
