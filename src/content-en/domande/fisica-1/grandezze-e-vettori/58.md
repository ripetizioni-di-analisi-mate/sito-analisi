A force's line of action is perpendicular to a lever's axis and passes at distance $d$ from it. Given the magnitude of the force $F = 24\,\mathrm{N}$ and the magnitude of the torque $M = 72\,\mathrm{N\,m}$, what is the perpendicular moment arm $d$?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The magnitude of the cross product of position and force equals the force times the perpendicular distance to its line of action. In the stated units, the relation is $M = F \cdot d$. Solving for the unknown gives $d = \dfrac{M}{F}$. Substituting the given values yields $3\,\mathrm{m}$.
