A pipe delivers water at a constant rate. Volume is measured in liters and time in minutes. Given the volume delivered $V = 360\,\mathrm{L}$ and the flow rate $Q = 3\,\mathrm{L/s}$, what is the delivery time $t$?

- $7200\,\mathrm{min}$
- $2\,\mathrm{min}$
- $4\,\mathrm{min}$
- $120\,\mathrm{min}$

## Explanation

Multiply the time in minutes by sixty to obtain seconds. The flow rate is the volume divided by that time. In the stated units, the relation is $Q = \dfrac{V}{60 \cdot t}$. Solving for the unknown gives $t = \dfrac{V}{60 \cdot Q}$. Substituting the given values yields $2\,\mathrm{min}$.
