Two vectors $\vec{A}$ and $\vec{B}$ have magnitudes $A = 4.0$ and $B = 5.0$, and their dot product is $\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B} = 12$. What is the magnitude of the cross product $\vec{A} \times \vec{B}$?

- $20$
- $16$
- $8.0$
- $12$

## Explanation

From the dot product we get $\cos\theta = \dfrac{\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B}}{A B} = \dfrac{12}{20} = \dfrac{3}{5}$. Since $\theta \in [0^\circ, 180^\circ]$, the sine is nonnegative and equals $\sin\theta = \sqrt{1 - \dfrac{9}{25}} = \dfrac{4}{5}$. Hence $|\vec{A} \times \vec{B}| = A B\sin\theta = 20 \cdot \dfrac{4}{5} = 16$. The value $12$ is the dot product, which coincides with the magnitude of the cross product only for $\theta = 45^\circ$, and the value $20$ is the product of the magnitudes, which is obtained for perpendicular vectors.
