In a freshwater lake, with atmospheric pressure $p_0 = 1{,}013 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$, what is the absolute pressure at a depth of $12{,}0\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $1{,}18 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $1{,}13 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $2{,}19 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $1{,}60 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

By **Stevin's law** the absolute pressure at depth $h$ is $p = p_0 + \rho g h$. With $\rho = 1{,}00 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ the gauge pressure is
$$
\rho g h = 1000 \cdot 9{,}81 \cdot 12{,}0\,\mathrm{Pa} = 1{,}18 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa} .
$$
Therefore the absolute pressure is $1{,}013 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa} + 1{,}18 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa} = 2{,}19 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$. The value $1{,}18 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$ is the gauge pressure alone, which does not include the pressure of the air on the surface of the lake.
