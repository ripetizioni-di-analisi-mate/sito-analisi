A wide open tank has in its wall a hole of area $2.0\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$ below the free surface of the water. What is the flow rate through the hole?

- $5.6 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $7.9 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $3.1 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $7.9 \cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$

## Explanation

By **Torricelli's theorem** the water flows out with speed
$$
v = \sqrt{2 g h} = \sqrt{2 \cdot 9.81 \cdot 0.80}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 3.96\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The flow rate is the product of the area of the hole and the speed, with the area in square meters, $a = 2.0 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}$. So $Q = a v = 2.0 \cdot 10^{-4} \cdot 3.96\,\mathrm{m^3/s} = 7.9 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. The value $3.1 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ is obtained by using $2gh$ instead of its square root.
