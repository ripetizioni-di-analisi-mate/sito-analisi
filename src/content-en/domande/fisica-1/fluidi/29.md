A rectangular vertical gate, $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ wide, separates two basins. On the left the water is $H_1 = 3.0\,\mathrm{m}$ deep, on the right $H_2 = 1.0\,\mathrm{m}$; both free surfaces are in contact with air. What is the magnitude of the resultant force exerted by the water on the gate?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 linee="-0.2,0 6.5,0;3,0 3,3.6" poligoni="0,0 3,0 3,3 0,3;3,0 6.5,0 6.5,1 3,1" segmenti="-0.5,0,-0.5,3;6.9,0,6.9,1" etichette="-0.95,1.5,H₁;7.15,0.5,H₂" xmin=-1.3 xmax=7.6 ymin=-0.3 ymax=3.8 titolo="Vertical gate with water of depth $H_1$ on one side and $H_2$ on the other"}

- $3.9 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $7.8 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $8.8 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1.2 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Atmospheric pressure acts on both free surfaces, so its contribution cancels and on each side only the gauge pressure counts.
By the theorem on the **force on a vertical wall** the water on the left pushes with $F_1 = \dfrac{1}{2}\rho g w H_1^2$ and the water on the right, in the opposite direction, with $F_2 = \dfrac{1}{2}\rho g w H_2^2$.
The resultant force is therefore
$$
F = \frac{1}{2}\,\rho g w \left( H_1^2 - H_2^2 \right) = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 1000 \cdot 9.81 \cdot 2.0 \cdot (9.0 - 1.0)\,\mathrm{N} = 7.8 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{N} ,
$$
directed from left to right.
The value $3.9 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{N}$ is obtained by using the square of the level difference $(H_1 - H_2)^2$ instead of the difference of the squares, and $8.8 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{N}$ by neglecting the water on the right.
