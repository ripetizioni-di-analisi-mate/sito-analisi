An aluminum block ($\rho = 2.70 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$) of volume $1.0\,\mathrm{dm^3}$ hangs from a spring scale and is completely immersed in water. What does the spring scale read?

- $26\,\mathrm{N}$
- $17\,\mathrm{N}$
- $36\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1.7\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The block is in equilibrium under its weight, the buoyant force and the force of the spring scale, so the spring scale reads the apparent weight $P_{\mathrm{app}} = (\rho_c - \rho_f)\,V g$. With $V = 1.0 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^3}$ we obtain
$$
P_{\mathrm{app}} = (2700 - 1000) \cdot 1.0 \cdot 10^{-3} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{N} = 17\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The value $26\,\mathrm{N}$ is the weight of the block in air, and $36\,\mathrm{N}$ is obtained by adding the buoyant force to the weight instead of subtracting it.
