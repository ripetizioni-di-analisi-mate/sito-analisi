A flat plate of area $0.20\,\mathrm{m^2}$ slides with speed $0.50\,\mathrm{m/s}$ on a layer of oil $1.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ thick, spread on a surface at rest. The oil is a Newtonian fluid of viscosity $0.40\,\mathrm{Pa \cdot s}$. What force is needed to keep the plate moving at constant speed?

- $0.040\,\mathrm{N}$
- $40\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4.0 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1.6 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

For a Newtonian fluid between a plate at rest and a moving plate the required force is $F = \eta A\, \dfrac{v}{d}$, with the thickness in meters, $d = 1.0 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m}$. Therefore
$$
F = 0.40 \cdot 0.20 \cdot \frac{0.50}{1.0 \cdot 10^{-3}}\,\mathrm{N} = 40\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The value $0.040\,\mathrm{N}$ is obtained by leaving the thickness in millimeters.
