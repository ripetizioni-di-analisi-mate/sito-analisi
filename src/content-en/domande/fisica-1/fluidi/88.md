Liquid pressure on a flat horizontal base is uniform. The outside face is at atmospheric pressure. Given the liquid gauge pressure $p = 12\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and the area of the base $A = \dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{m^2}$, what is the net pressure force magnitude $F$?

- $3000\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12000\,\mathrm{N}$
- $6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $6000\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The pressure difference acts across the whole area. Convert kilopascals to pascals before multiplying. In the stated units, the relation is $F = 1000 \cdot A \cdot p$. Substituting the given values yields $6000\,\mathrm{N}$.
