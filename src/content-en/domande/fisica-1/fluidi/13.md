A horizontal pipe of length $2{,}0\,\mathrm{m}$ and radius $1{,}0\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries water ($\eta = 1{,}0 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{Pa \cdot s}$) in laminar flow, with a pressure difference of $4{,}0 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{Pa}$ between its ends. What is the flow rate according to Poiseuille's law?

- $1{,}3 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $7{,}9 \cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $6{,}3 \cdot 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$
- $2{,}5 \cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$

## Explanation

By **Poiseuille's law** $Q = \dfrac{\pi R^4\, \Delta p}{8 \eta L}$, with the radius in meters, $R = 1{,}0 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m}$, so $R^4 = 1{,}0 \cdot 10^{-12}\,\mathrm{m^4}$. Then
$$
Q = \frac{\pi \cdot 10^{-12} \cdot 4{,}0 \cdot 10^3}{8 \cdot 1{,}0 \cdot 10^{-3} \cdot 2{,}0}\,\mathrm{m^3/s} = \frac{\pi}{4} \cdot 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^3/s} = 7{,}9 \cdot 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m^3/s} .
$$
Using the diameter instead of the radius multiplies the result by $2^4 = 16$ and gives $1{,}3 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$.
