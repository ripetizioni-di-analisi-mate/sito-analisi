An object is stationary and fully submerged in a uniform liquid. Take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Given the liquid density $\rho = 900\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and the displaced volume $V = \dfrac{1}{50}\,\mathrm{m^3}$, what is the buoyant force magnitude $F$?

- $270\,\mathrm{N}$
- $90\,\mathrm{N}$
- $360\,\mathrm{N}$
- $180\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The buoyant force equals the weight of displaced liquid. Its mass is the liquid density times the displaced volume. In the stated units, the relation is $F = 10 \cdot V \cdot \rho$. Substituting the given values yields $180\,\mathrm{N}$.
