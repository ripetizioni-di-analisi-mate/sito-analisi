A tire pressure gauge reads $2{,}0\,\mathrm{bar}$. What is the absolute pressure of the air in the tire, with $p_0 = 1{,}013 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$?

- $3{,}0 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $2{,}0 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $9{,}9 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $1{,}2 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The gauge reads zero in open air, so it measures the gauge pressure $p - p_0$. Since $1\,\mathrm{bar} = 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$, the gauge pressure is $2{,}0 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$. The absolute pressure is therefore
$$
p = p_0 + 2{,}0 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa} = 1{,}013 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa} + 2{,}0 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa} = 3{,}0 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa} .
$$
The value $9{,}9 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{Pa}$ is obtained by subtracting $p_0$ instead of adding it.
