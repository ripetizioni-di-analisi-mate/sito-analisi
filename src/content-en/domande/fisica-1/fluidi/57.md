An object is stationary and fully submerged in a uniform liquid. Take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Given the displaced volume $V = \dfrac{1}{50}\,\mathrm{m^3}$ and the buoyant force magnitude $F = 180\,\mathrm{N}$, what is the liquid density $\rho$?

- $1350\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $1800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $900\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $450\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

Buoyancy equals the weight of displaced liquid. Its mass is the liquid density times the displaced volume. In the stated units, the relation is $F = 10 \cdot V \cdot \rho$. Solving for the unknown gives $\rho = \dfrac{F}{10 \cdot V}$. Substituting the given values yields $900\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.
