A large tank is open to the atmosphere. A horizontal jet leaves through a hole at depth $h$ below the free surface. The fluid is ideal. Given gravitational acceleration $g = 10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and the outflow speed $v = 5\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the depth of the hole $h$?

- $\dfrac{5}{8}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{5}{4}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{15}{8}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The surface and the hole are both at atmospheric pressure. In Bernoulli's equation the height difference supplies the jet's kinetic term because the free surface falls very slowly. In the stated units, the relation is $v = \sqrt{2} \cdot \sqrt{g} \cdot \sqrt{h}$. Solving for the unknown gives $h = \dfrac{v^{2}}{2 \cdot g}$. Substituting the given values yields $\dfrac{5}{4}\,\mathrm{m}$.
