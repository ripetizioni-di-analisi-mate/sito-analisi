## Explanation

Above the plug the pressure is $p_0 + \rho g h$ and below the plug the air pressure is $p_0$, so the resultant force is computed with the gauge pressure $\rho g h$ alone. The radius of the plug is $r = 2{,}0\,\mathrm{cm} = 0{,}020\,\mathrm{m}$, therefore its area is $\pi r^2 = 1{,}26 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Then
$$
F = \rho g h\, \pi r^2 = 1000 \cdot 9{,}81 \cdot 0{,}40 \cdot 1{,}26 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{N} = 4{,}9\,\mathrm{N} ,
$$
and the force points downward. Using the diameter instead of the radius makes the area $4$ times larger and gives $20\,\mathrm{N}$.
