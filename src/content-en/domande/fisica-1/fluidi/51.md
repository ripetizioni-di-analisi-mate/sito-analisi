An ideal hydraulic press has pistons at the same height. The large piston has area $80\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. Neglect the weights of the pistons. Given the force on the small piston $f = 100\,\mathrm{N}$ and the area of the small piston $A = 10\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, what is the force on the large piston $F$?

- $800\,\mathrm{N}$
- $\dfrac{25}{2}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $400\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1600\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The pressure increase is the same below both pistons. The force ratio therefore equals the area ratio. In the stated units, the relation is $F = \dfrac{80 \cdot f}{A}$. Substituting the given values yields $800\,\mathrm{N}$.
