Honey warmed in a water bath flows from a spoon much more easily than cold honey. Which statement explains this fact?

- The viscosity of honey increases as the temperature increases
- The density of honey decreases, while the viscosity stays the same
- The viscosity of honey decreases as the temperature increases
- Warm honey becomes an ideal fluid, with zero viscosity

## Explanation

How fast a liquid flows under its own weight depends on the tangential forces between the layers, which are proportional to the **viscosity** $\eta$.
In liquids the viscosity decreases as the temperature increases.
Warmed honey therefore has a lower viscosity, and for the same weight the layers slide faster over one another.
The viscosity is still different from zero, so warm honey is not an ideal fluid.
