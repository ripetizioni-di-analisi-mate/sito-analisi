A sample hanging from a spring scale is fully submerged. Its weight in air is $70\,\mathrm{N}$ and air buoyancy is negligible. Take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Given the sample volume $V = \dfrac{3}{1000}\,\mathrm{m^3}$ and the spring scale reading $F = 40\,\mathrm{N}$, what is the liquid density $\rho$?

- $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $500\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $\dfrac{4000}{3}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $2000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

The string tension and the buoyant force both point upward. Their sum balances the weight. In the stated units, the relation is $F = - 10 \cdot V \cdot \rho + 70$. Solving for the unknown gives $\rho = \dfrac{70 - F}{10 \cdot V}$. Substituting the given values yields $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.
