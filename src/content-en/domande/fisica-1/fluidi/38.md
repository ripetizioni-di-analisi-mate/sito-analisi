An ideal fluid flows in a horizontal duct that has a constriction. In which section is the pressure lower?

- In the wide section, where the speed is lower
- In the constriction, where the speed is higher
- In neither, because in a horizontal duct the pressure is the same everywhere
- In the wide section, because the flow rate is greater there

## Explanation

By the **continuity equation** the flow rate is the same in every cross section, so the speed is higher in the constriction. The duct is horizontal, therefore by **Bernoulli's theorem** the sum $p + \dfrac{1}{2}\rho v^2$ is the same in every cross section. Where the speed is higher the pressure is therefore lower, and the minimum pressure occurs **in the constriction**.
