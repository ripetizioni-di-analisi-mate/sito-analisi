Viscosity is measured in $\mathrm{Pa \cdot s}$. Which combination of base units of the International System is equivalent?

- $\mathrm{kg/(m \cdot s^2)}$
- $\mathrm{kg \cdot m/s}$
- $\mathrm{m^2/s}$
- $\mathrm{kg/(m \cdot s)}$

## Explanation

The pascal is one newton per square meter, and the newton is $\mathrm{kg \cdot m/s^2}$, so $1\,\mathrm{Pa} = 1\,\mathrm{kg/(m \cdot s^2)}$.
Multiplying by the second gives
$$
1\,\mathrm{Pa \cdot s} = 1\,\mathrm{kg/(m \cdot s)} .
$$
The same result follows from the relation $F = \eta A\, \dfrac{v}{d}$, because $\eta = \dfrac{F d}{A v}$ is measured in $\dfrac{\mathrm{N \cdot m}}{\mathrm{m^2 \cdot m/s}}$.
The unit $\mathrm{kg/(m \cdot s^2)}$ is that of pressure, not that of viscosity.
