An incompressible liquid flows steadily through a horizontal pipe. The narrow section has area $4\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. Given the speed in the wider section $u = 2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the speed in the narrower section $v = 6\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the area of the wider section $A$?

- $12\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $24\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $6\,\mathrm{cm^2}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{cm^2}$

## Explanation

Volumetric flow rate is conserved. The product of area and speed is therefore the same in both sections. In the stated units, the relation is $v = \dfrac{A \cdot u}{4}$. Solving for the unknown gives $A = \dfrac{4 \cdot v}{u}$. Substituting the given values yields $12\,\mathrm{cm^2}$.
