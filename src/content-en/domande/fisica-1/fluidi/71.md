A river has a rectangular bed, $20\,\mathrm{m}$ wide and $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ deep, and the water flows at $0.50\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Further downstream the river passes through a gorge, where it is $8.0\,\mathrm{m}$ wide and $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$ deep. Taking the speed as uniform over each cross-section, what is the speed in the gorge?

- $1.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.40\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The flow is steady and water is incompressible, so by the **continuity equation** the flow rate is the same at the two cross-sections, that is, $A_1 v_1 = A_2 v_2$.
The areas are $A_1 = 20 \cdot 2.0\,\mathrm{m^2} = 40\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and $A_2 = 8.0 \cdot 2.5\,\mathrm{m^2} = 20\,\mathrm{m^2}$.
Therefore
$$
v_2 = \frac{A_1}{A_2}\, v_1 = \frac{40}{20} \cdot 0.50\,\mathrm{m/s} = 1.0\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The value $1.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ takes into account only the ratio of the widths and neglects the change in depth.
