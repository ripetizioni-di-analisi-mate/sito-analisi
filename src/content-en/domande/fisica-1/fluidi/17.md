At what depth below the surface of the sea ($\rho = 1{,}03 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$) is the absolute pressure three times the atmospheric pressure $p_0 = 1{,}013 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$?

- $20{,}1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $30{,}1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10{,}0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $197\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

By **Stevin's law** $p = p_0 + \rho g h$, so the condition $p = 3 p_0$ is equivalent to $\rho g h = 2 p_0$. Therefore
$$
h = \frac{2 p_0}{\rho g} = \frac{2{,}026 \cdot 10^5}{1{,}03 \cdot 10^3 \cdot 9{,}81}\,\mathrm{m} = 20{,}1\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $30{,}1\,\mathrm{m}$ is obtained with $\rho g h = 3 p_0$, that is, by neglecting the pressure $p_0$ that already acts on the surface.
