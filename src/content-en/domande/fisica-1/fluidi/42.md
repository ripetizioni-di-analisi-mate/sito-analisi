In a horizontal Venturi tube carrying water, two open vertical tubes, connected to the wide section and to the constriction, show a difference in level of $5{,}0\,\mathrm{cm}$ between the water columns. What is the pressure difference between the wide section and the constriction?

- $4{,}9 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $50\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $2{,}5 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $4{,}9 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

In the vertical tubes the water is at rest, so by **Stevin's law** the pressure at the base of each tube is $p_0 + \rho g h$, where $h$ is the height of the column. The pressure difference is therefore due only to the difference in level, and it is
$$
\Delta p = \rho g\,\Delta h = 1000 \cdot 9{,}81 \cdot 0{,}050\,\mathrm{Pa} = 4{,}9 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{Pa} .
$$
The taller column is the one connected to the wide section, where the pressure is greater. The value $4{,}9 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{Pa}$ is obtained by leaving the difference in level in centimeters.
