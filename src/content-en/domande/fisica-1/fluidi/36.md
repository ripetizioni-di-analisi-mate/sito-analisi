A sealed-reference sensor and a vented sensor are side by side on a lake bed. Atmospheric pressure falls by $1.5\,\mathrm{kPa}$ as a weather front passes. At fixed depth and density, how do absolute pressure and gauge pressure change?

- Both fall by $1.5\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
- Gauge pressure rises by $1.5\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and absolute pressure stays constant.
- Absolute pressure falls by $1.5\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and gauge pressure stays constant.
- Both stay constant.

## Explanation

At the bed $p_{\mathrm{abs}}=p_{\mathrm{atm}}+\rho gh$. As depth and density are unchanged, the vented sensor's reading $\rho gh$ remains the same.
