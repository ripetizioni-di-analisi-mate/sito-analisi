- $8.54 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

In Torricelli's barometer the pressure above the mercury is negligible, so by **Stevin's law** the atmospheric pressure equals the pressure of the column, $p_0 = \rho g h$. The height must be expressed in meters, $h = 640\,\mathrm{mm} = 0.640\,\mathrm{m}$. Therefore
$$
p_0 = 13.6 \cdot 10^3 \cdot 9.81 \cdot 0.640\,\mathrm{Pa} = 8.54 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{Pa} .
$$
The value is less than $1.013 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$, as expected in the mountains.
