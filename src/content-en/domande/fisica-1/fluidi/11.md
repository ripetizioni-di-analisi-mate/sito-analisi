A wide open tank has a small hole $5.0\,\mathrm{m}$ below the free surface of the water. With what speed does the water flow out of the hole?

- $7.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $98\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $9.9\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The hole is small compared with the free surface, so the speed at which the surface descends is negligible. The free surface and the jet are both at pressure $p_0$, therefore **Bernoulli's theorem** between the two points gives $\rho g h = \dfrac{1}{2}\rho v^2$, that is, **Torricelli's theorem** $v = \sqrt{2gh}$. Then
$$
v = \sqrt{2 \cdot 9.81 \cdot 5.0}\,\mathrm{m/s} = \sqrt{98.1}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 9.9\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The value $7.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ corresponds to $\sqrt{gh}$, that is, to forgetting the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$ of the kinetic energy.
