Liquid pressure on a flat horizontal base is uniform. The outside face is at atmospheric pressure. Given the liquid gauge pressure $p = 12\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and the net pressure force magnitude $F = 6000\,\mathrm{N}$, what is the area of the base $A$?

- $1\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $500\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

The pressure difference acts across the whole area. Convert kilopascals to pascals before multiplying. In the stated units, the relation is $F = 1000 \cdot A \cdot p$. Solving for the unknown gives $A = \dfrac{F}{1000 \cdot p}$. Substituting the given values yields $\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{m^2}$.
