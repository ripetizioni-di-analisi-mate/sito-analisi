A large tank is open to the atmosphere. A horizontal jet leaves through a hole at depth $h$ below the free surface. The fluid is ideal. Given gravitational acceleration $g = 10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and the depth of the hole $h = \dfrac{5}{4}\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the outflow speed $v$?

- $5 \cdot \sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac{5 \cdot \sqrt{2}}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The surface and the hole are both at atmospheric pressure. In Bernoulli's equation the height difference supplies the jet's kinetic term because the free surface falls very slowly. In the stated units, the relation is $v = \sqrt{2} \cdot \sqrt{g} \cdot \sqrt{h}$. Substituting the given values yields $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
