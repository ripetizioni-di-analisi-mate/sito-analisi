In a hydraulic press the area of the large piston is $50$ times that of the small piston. How far must the small piston move down to raise the large piston by $2{,}0\,\mathrm{cm}$?

- $4{,}0 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0{,}020\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1{,}0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0{,}14\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The liquid is incompressible, so the volume that leaves the small cylinder equals the volume that enters the large cylinder, $A_1 s_1 = A_2 s_2$. Therefore
$$
s_1 = \frac{A_2}{A_1}\, s_2 = 50 \cdot 0{,}020\,\mathrm{m} = 1{,}0\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The press multiplies the force by $50$ and divides the displacement by the same factor, so the work is the same on the two pistons. The value $0{,}14\,\mathrm{m}$ is obtained by using the ratio of the radii, $\sqrt{50}$, instead of the ratio of the areas.
