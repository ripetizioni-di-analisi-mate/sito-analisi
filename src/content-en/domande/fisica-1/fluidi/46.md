A probe is submerged in a uniform liquid open to the atmosphere. Take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Given the density $\rho = 800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and the depth of the probe $h = 3\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the gauge pressure $p$?

- $36000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $12000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $24000\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $48000\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

The hydrostatic pressure increase from the free surface is $\rho gh$. In the stated units, the relation is $p = 10 \cdot h \cdot \rho$. Substituting the given values yields $24000\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
