A block floats in water. A mass is placed on it, leaving part of the block above the surface. What increases by an amount equal to the added weight?

- The weight of the block alone
- The weight of displaced water
- Atmospheric pressure
- The weight of water above the block's top face

## Explanation

The new equilibrium requires the buoyant force to increase by the weight of the added mass. The buoyant force equals the weight of displaced water.
