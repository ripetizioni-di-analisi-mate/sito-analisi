Liquid pressure on a flat horizontal base is uniform. The outside face is at atmospheric pressure. Given the area of the base $A = \dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and the net pressure force magnitude $F = 6000\,\mathrm{N}$, what is the liquid gauge pressure $p$?

- $24\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $12000\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

The pressure difference acts across the whole area. Convert kilopascals to pascals before multiplying. In the stated units, the relation is $F = 1000 \cdot A \cdot p$. Solving for the unknown gives $p = \dfrac{F}{1000 \cdot A}$. Substituting the given values yields $12\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
