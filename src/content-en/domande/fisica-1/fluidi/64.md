A uniform block floats in water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. Its density is lower than that of water. The initial data are the block density $\rho = 600\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and the block's total volume $V = 20\,\mathrm{L}$. Change the block density to $750\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the ratio of the final value of $V_{i}$ to its initial value?

- $\dfrac{4}{5}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{5}{8}$
- $\dfrac{5}{4}$

## Explanation

The block and the displaced water have equal weights. This gives $\rho V=1000 V_i$ with both volumes in the same unit. The relation $V_{i} = \dfrac{V \cdot \rho}{1000}$ gives $12\,\mathrm{L}$ before the change and $15\,\mathrm{L}$ after it. The ratio is $\dfrac{5}{4}$.
