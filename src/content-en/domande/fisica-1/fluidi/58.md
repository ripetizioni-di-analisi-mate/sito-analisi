An object is stationary and fully submerged in a uniform liquid. Take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Given the liquid density $\rho = 900\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and the buoyant force magnitude $F = 180\,\mathrm{N}$, what is the displaced volume $V$?

- $\dfrac{3}{100}\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{50}\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{25}\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{100}\,\mathrm{m^3}$

## Explanation

The buoyant force equals the weight of displaced liquid. Its mass is the liquid density times the displaced volume. In the stated units, the relation is $F = 10 \cdot V \cdot \rho$. Solving for the unknown gives $V = \dfrac{F}{10 \cdot \rho}$. Substituting the given values yields $\dfrac{1}{50}\,\mathrm{m^3}$.
