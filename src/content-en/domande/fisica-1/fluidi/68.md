A boat with a large stone on board floats in a swimming pool. The stone is thrown into the water and settles on the bottom. How does the water level of the pool change?

- It goes up
- It stays the same
- It goes up or down depending on the mass of the boat
- It goes down

## Explanation

While the stone is on board, the boat floats and displaces a volume of water whose weight equals the weight of the boat plus that of the stone; for the stone the displaced volume is $\dfrac{m}{\rho_w}$.
On the bottom, the stone displaces only its own volume $\dfrac{m}{\rho_s}$.
The stone is denser than water, so $\dfrac{m}{\rho_s} < \dfrac{m}{\rho_w}$, while the volume displaced by the boat alone stays the same.
Therefore the total volume of displaced water decreases and the level of the pool goes down.
