- $1.25 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $1.10 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $200\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

In each liquid the buoyant force is the difference between the weight in air and the apparent weight, so in water $S_a = 1.00\,\mathrm{N}$ and in the liquid $S_L = 0.80\,\mathrm{N}$. The body is completely immersed in both cases, therefore the immersed volume is the same and the buoyant forces are in the ratio of the densities of the liquids. Then
$$
\rho_L = \frac{S_L}{S_a}\,\rho_a = \frac{0.80}{1.00} \cdot 1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3} = 800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3} .
$$
The value $1.25 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ is obtained with the ratio of the buoyant forces inverted.
