A uniform block floats in water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. Its density is lower than that of water. Given the block density $\rho = 600\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and the submerged volume $V_{i} = 12\,\mathrm{L}$, what is the block's total volume $V$?

- $20\,\mathrm{L}$
- $30\,\mathrm{L}$
- $40\,\mathrm{L}$
- $10\,\mathrm{L}$

## Explanation

The block and the displaced water have equal weights. This gives $\rho V=1000 V_i$ with both volumes in the same unit. In the stated units, the relation is $V_{i} = \dfrac{V \cdot \rho}{1000}$. Solving for the unknown gives $V = \dfrac{1000 \cdot V_{i}}{\rho}$. Substituting the given values yields $20\,\mathrm{L}$.
