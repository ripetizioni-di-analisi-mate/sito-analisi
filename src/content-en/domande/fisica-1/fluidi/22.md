A rectangular vertical gate, $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ wide, holds back water $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ deep on one side; on the other side there is air at atmospheric pressure. What is the resultant force on the gate?

- $3.9 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $7.8 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $2.0 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4.4 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Atmospheric pressure acts on both faces, so only the gauge pressure counts, which grows linearly from zero at the surface to $\rho g H$ at the bottom. By the theorem on the **force on a vertical wall**
$$
F = \frac{1}{2}\,\rho g w H^2 = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 1000 \cdot 9.81 \cdot 2.0 \cdot (2.0)^2\,\mathrm{N} = 3.9 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The value $7.8 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{N}$ is obtained by using the pressure at the bottom over the whole gate instead of the average pressure $\rho g\,\dfrac{H}{2}$. The value $4.4 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{N}$ also includes atmospheric pressure, which acts on the other face as well.
