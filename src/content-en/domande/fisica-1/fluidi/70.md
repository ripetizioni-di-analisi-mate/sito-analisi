An incompressible liquid flows steadily through a horizontal pipe. The narrow section has area $4\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. The initial data are the area of the wider section $A = 12\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and the speed in the wider section $u = 2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. In the second configuration, change the area of the wider section to $8\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and the speed in the wider section to $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the final value of $v$ minus its initial value?

- $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Volumetric flow rate is conserved. The product of area and speed is therefore the same in both sections. The relation $v = \dfrac{A \cdot u}{4}$ gives $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ before the change and $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ after it. Subtracting the initial value from the final value gives $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
