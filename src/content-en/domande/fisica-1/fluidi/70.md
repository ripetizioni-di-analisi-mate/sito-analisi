In the aorta, which has a circular cross-section of radius $1.0\,\mathrm{cm}$, blood flows with average speed $0.30\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the blood flow rate in liters per minute?

- $23\,\mathrm{L/min}$
- $5.7\,\mathrm{L/min}$
- $0.094\,\mathrm{L/min}$
- $1.1 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{L/min}$

## Explanation

The area of the cross-section is $A = \pi r^2 = \pi \cdot (1.0 \cdot 10^{-2})^2\,\mathrm{m^2} = 3.14 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}$.
The **flow rate** is therefore $Q = A v = 3.14 \cdot 10^{-4} \cdot 0.30\,\mathrm{m^3/s} = 9.4 \cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$.
One cubic meter is $10^3\,\mathrm{L}$ and one minute is $60\,\mathrm{s}$, so
$$
Q = 9.4 \cdot 10^{-5} \cdot 10^3 \cdot 60\,\mathrm{L/min} = 5.7\,\mathrm{L/min} .
$$
The value $23\,\mathrm{L/min}$ is obtained by using in the area the diameter $2.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ instead of the radius, and $0.094\,\mathrm{L/min}$ is the flow rate in liters per second.
