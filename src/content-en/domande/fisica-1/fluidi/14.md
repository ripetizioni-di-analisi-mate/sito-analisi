A solid homogeneous cylinder has radius $2{,}0\,\mathrm{cm}$, height $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ and mass $1{,}0\,\mathrm{kg}$. What is its density?

- $3{,}2 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $2{,}5 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $8{,}0\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $8{,}0 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

The volume of the cylinder is $V = \pi r^2 h = \pi \cdot (2{,}0)^2 \cdot 10\,\mathrm{cm^3} = 126\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. Since $1\,\mathrm{cm^3} = 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^3}$, the volume is $1{,}26 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^3}$. Therefore
$$
\rho = \frac{m}{V} = \frac{1{,}0}{1{,}26 \cdot 10^{-4}}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3} = 8{,}0 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{kg/m^3} .
$$
The value $8{,}0\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ is obtained by converting the cubic centimeters as if they were liters, with the factor $10^{-3}$.
