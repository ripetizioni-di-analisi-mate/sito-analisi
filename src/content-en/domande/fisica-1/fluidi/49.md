A probe is submerged in a uniform liquid open to the atmosphere. Take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The initial data are the density $\rho = 800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and the depth of the probe $h = 3\,\mathrm{m}$. In the second configuration, change the depth of the probe to $6\,\mathrm{m}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the ratio of the final value of $p$ to its initial value?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $1$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

Stevin's law gives a pressure increase of $\rho gh$ relative to the free surface. The relation $p = 10 \cdot h \cdot \rho$ gives $24000\,\mathrm{Pa}$ before the change and $48000\,\mathrm{Pa}$ after it. The ratio is $2$.
