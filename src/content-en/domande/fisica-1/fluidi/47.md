A probe is submerged in a uniform liquid open to the atmosphere. Take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Given the depth of the probe $h = 3\,\mathrm{m}$ and the gauge pressure $p = 24000\,\mathrm{Pa}$, what is the density $\rho$?

- $800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $1200\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $1600\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $400\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

The hydrostatic pressure increase from the free surface is $\rho gh$. In the stated units, the relation is $p = 10 \cdot h \cdot \rho$. Solving for the unknown gives $\rho = \dfrac{p}{10 \cdot h}$. Substituting the given values yields $800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.
