An incompressible liquid flows steadily through a horizontal pipe. The narrow section has area $4\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. Given the area of the wider section $A = 12\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and the speed in the wider section $u = 2\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the speed in the narrower section $v$?

- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Volumetric flow rate is conserved. The product of area and speed is therefore the same in both sections. In the stated units, the relation is $v = \dfrac{A \cdot u}{4}$. Substituting the given values yields $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
