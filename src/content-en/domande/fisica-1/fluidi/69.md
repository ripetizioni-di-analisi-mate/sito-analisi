An incompressible liquid flows steadily through a horizontal pipe. The narrow section has area $4\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. The initial data are the area of the wider section $A = 12\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and the speed in the wider section $u = 2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. In the second configuration, change the area of the wider section to $16\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the ratio of the final value of $v$ to its initial value?

- $\dfrac{8}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $1$

## Explanation

Volumetric flow rate is conserved. The product of area and speed is therefore the same in both sections. The relation $v = \dfrac{A \cdot u}{4}$ gives $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ before the change and $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ after it. The ratio is $\dfrac{4}{3}$.
