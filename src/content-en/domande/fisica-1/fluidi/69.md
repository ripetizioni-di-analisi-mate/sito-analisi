A pump has a flow rate of $18\,\mathrm{m^3/h}$. What is the flow rate in liters per second?

- $5.0\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $0.30\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $3.0 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{L/s}$
- $5.0 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{L/s}$

## Explanation

One cubic meter is $10^3\,\mathrm{L}$ and one hour is $3600\,\mathrm{s}$, so
$$
Q = \frac{18 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{L}}{3600\,\mathrm{s}} = 5.0\,\mathrm{L/s} .
$$
The value $3.0 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{L/s}$ divides by the $60$ minutes of an hour instead of its $3600$ seconds. The value $5.0 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{L/s}$ is the flow rate in cubic meters per second, written with the wrong unit.
