## Explanation

Below the height of the interface the two arms contain the same connected water, so the two points at that height have the same pressure. Above them there are $15.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ of liquid in one arm and $12.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ of water in the other, therefore by **Stevin's law** $\rho\, g \cdot 0.150\,\mathrm{m} = \rho_a\, g \cdot 0.120\,\mathrm{m}$. It follows that
$$
\rho = \frac{12.0}{15.0} \cdot 1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3} = 800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3} .
$$
The liquid forms the taller column, so it is less dense than water.
