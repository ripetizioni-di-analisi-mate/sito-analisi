A sphere of diameter $20\,\mathrm{cm}$ is held completely immersed in water. What is the buoyant force?

- $3.3 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{N}$
- $31\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4.2\,\mathrm{N}$
- $41\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The radius of the sphere is $r = 0.10\,\mathrm{m}$, so its volume is $V = \dfrac{4}{3}\pi r^3 = 4.19 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^3}$. By **Archimedes' principle** the buoyant force equals the weight of the displaced water, therefore
$$
S = \rho V g = 1000 \cdot 4.19 \cdot 10^{-3} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{N} = 41\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
Using the diameter instead of the radius makes the volume $8$ times larger and the buoyant force $3.3 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{N}$.
