An object is stationary and fully submerged in a uniform liquid. Take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The initial data are the liquid density $\rho = 900\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and the displaced volume $V = \dfrac{1}{50}\,\mathrm{m^3}$. Change the liquid density to $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and the displaced volume to $\dfrac{3}{100}\,\mathrm{m^3}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the final value of $F$ minus its initial value?

- $60\,\mathrm{N}$
- $240\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-120\,\mathrm{N}$
- $120\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Buoyancy equals the weight of displaced liquid. Its mass is the liquid density times the displaced volume. The relation $F = 10 \cdot V \cdot \rho$ gives $180\,\mathrm{N}$ before the change and $300\,\mathrm{N}$ after it. Subtracting the initial value from the final value gives $120\,\mathrm{N}$.
