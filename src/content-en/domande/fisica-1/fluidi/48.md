A probe is submerged in a uniform liquid open to the atmosphere. Take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Given the density $\rho = 800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and the gauge pressure $p = 24000\,\mathrm{Pa}$, what is the depth of the probe $h$?

- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Stevin's law gives a pressure increase of $\rho gh$ relative to the free surface. In the stated units, the relation is $p = 10 \cdot h \cdot \rho$. Solving for the unknown gives $h = \dfrac{p}{10 \cdot \rho}$. Substituting the given values yields $3\,\mathrm{m}$.
