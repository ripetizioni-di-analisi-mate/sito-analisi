A ship moves from the sea ($\rho = 1{,}03 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$) to the mouth of a freshwater river, without changing its load. How does the volume of the immersed part of the hull change?

- It increases
- It decreases
- It stays the same
- It depends on the depth of the river

## Explanation

The ship floats in both cases, so the buoyant force equals its weight, which does not change. Since the buoyant force is $\rho_f V_{\mathrm{imm}}\, g$, the product $\rho_f V_{\mathrm{imm}}$ stays constant. Fresh water has a lower density than seawater, therefore the immersed volume **increases** by a factor of $1{,}03$ and the ship sits lower in the water.
