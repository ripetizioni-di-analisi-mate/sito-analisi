A uniform block floats in water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. Its density is lower than that of water. The initial data are the block density $\rho = 600\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and the block's total volume $V = 20\,\mathrm{L}$. In the second configuration, change the block density to $500\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and the block's total volume to $30\,\mathrm{L}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the final value of $V_{i}$ minus its initial value?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{L}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{L}$
- $3\,\mathrm{L}$
- $7\,\mathrm{L}$

## Explanation

The block and the displaced water have equal weights. This gives $\rho V=1000 V_i$ with both volumes in the same unit. The relation $V_{i} = \dfrac{V \cdot \rho}{1000}$ gives $12\,\mathrm{L}$ before the change and $15\,\mathrm{L}$ after it. Subtracting the initial value from the final value gives $3\,\mathrm{L}$.
