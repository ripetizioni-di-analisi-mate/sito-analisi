The stream from a faucet fills a $12\,\mathrm{L}$ bucket in $40\,\mathrm{s}$. At the point where the stream has a cross section of $1.5\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, what is the speed of the water?

- $0.020\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.0 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.50\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The flow rate is the volume divided by the time, $Q = \dfrac{12 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^3}}{40\,\mathrm{s}} = 3.0 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. Since $1\,\mathrm{cm^2} = 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}$, the cross section is $A = 1.5 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}$. From the relation $Q = A v$ it follows that
$$
v = \frac{Q}{A} = \frac{3.0 \cdot 10^{-4}}{1.5 \cdot 10^{-4}}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 2.0\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The value $0.020\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is obtained by converting the square centimeters with the factor $10^{-2}$.
