The rotor of a fan has three blades; each can be modeled as a thin uniform rod of mass $0.30\,\mathrm{kg}$ and length $0.40\,\mathrm{m}$, attached by one end to the axis of rotation and perpendicular to it. The mass of the hub is negligible. What is the moment of inertia of the rotor about the axis?

- $0.012\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.016\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.048\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.144\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$

## Explanation

Each blade rotates about a perpendicular axis through one of its ends, so its moment of inertia is $\dfrac{1}{3} m L^2 = \dfrac{1}{3} \cdot 0.30 \cdot 0.40^2\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2} = 0.016\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$. The moment of inertia is additive, therefore the rotor has
$$
I = 3 \cdot 0.016\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2} = 0.048\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2} .
$$
The value $0.012\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ is obtained by using for each blade the moment of inertia about its center, $\dfrac{1}{12} m L^2$.
