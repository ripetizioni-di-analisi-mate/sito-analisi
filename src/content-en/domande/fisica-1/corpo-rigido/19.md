A door rotates about the vertical axis of its hinges, and the handle is $0{,}80\,\mathrm{m}$ from this axis. A horizontal force of magnitude $F$ is applied to the door in four different ways. In which case is the torque of the force about the axis of the hinges greatest?

- At $0{,}40\,\mathrm{m}$ from the hinges, perpendicular to the door
- On the handle, at $30^\circ$ to the plane of the door
- On the handle, perpendicular to the door
- On the handle, in the plane of the door and directed toward the hinges

## Explanation

The axial torque of a horizontal force has magnitude $F b$, where $b$ is the distance of the axis from the line of action. For the force on the handle perpendicular to the door $b = 0{,}80\,\mathrm{m}$, and for the force at $0{,}40\,\mathrm{m}$ from the hinges perpendicular to the door $b = 0{,}40\,\mathrm{m}$. For the force on the handle at $30^\circ$ to the plane of the door the lever arm is $b = 0{,}80\sin 30^\circ\,\mathrm{m} = 0{,}40\,\mathrm{m}$. The line of action of the force directed toward the hinges passes through the axis, so its torque is zero. Therefore the greatest torque, $F \cdot 0{,}80\,\mathrm{m}$, is that of the force **on the handle, perpendicular to the door**.
