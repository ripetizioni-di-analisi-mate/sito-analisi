- $3.96\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.80\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5.60\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.23\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

In pure rolling static friction does no work, so mechanical energy is conserved. For a solid cylinder $I_C = \dfrac{1}{2} M R^2$ and $\omega = \dfrac{v_C}{R}$, therefore the kinetic energy is $K = \dfrac{1}{2} M v_C^2 + \dfrac{1}{4} M v_C^2 = \dfrac{3}{4} M v_C^2$. Setting $K$ equal to the decrease $M g h$ of the potential energy gives
$$
v_C = \sqrt{\frac{4 g h}{3}} = \sqrt{\frac{4 \cdot 9.81 \cdot 0.80}{3}}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 3.23\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The value $3.96\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is the speed of a body that slides without friction, and $5.60\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is obtained by taking into account only the rotational kinetic energy.
