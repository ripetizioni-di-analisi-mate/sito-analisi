A particle of mass $0{,}20\,\mathrm{kg}$ moves in uniform circular motion on a circle of diameter $1{,}0\,\mathrm{m}$, with speed $3{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the magnitude of its angular momentum about the center of the circle?

- $0{,}60\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $0{,}90\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $0{,}30\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$
- $0{,}15\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$

## Explanation

About the center the position vector has magnitude equal to the radius, $r = 0{,}50\,\mathrm{m}$, and is perpendicular to the velocity. Therefore
$$
L_O = m v r = 0{,}20 \cdot 3{,}0 \cdot 0{,}50\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s} = 0{,}30\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s} .
$$
The same value is obtained from $L_O = m r^2\omega$ with $\omega = \dfrac{v}{r} = 6{,}0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. The value $0{,}60\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$ is obtained by using the diameter in place of the radius.
