A uniform solid sphere of mass $5{,}0\,\mathrm{kg}$ and radius $0{,}20\,\mathrm{m}$ rotates at $10\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ about a fixed axis through its center. What is its kinetic energy?

- $4{,}0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $8{,}0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $5{,}0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $10\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The moment of inertia of the solid sphere about an axis through the center is $I = \dfrac{2}{5} M R^2 = \dfrac{2}{5} \cdot 5{,}0 \cdot 0{,}20^2\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2} = 0{,}080\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$. The rotational kinetic energy is therefore
$$
K = \frac{1}{2} I\omega^2 = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 0{,}080 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{J} = 4{,}0\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
The value $8{,}0\,\mathrm{J}$ is obtained by forgetting the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$, and $5{,}0\,\mathrm{J}$ by using the moment of inertia of the solid cylinder, $\dfrac{1}{2} M R^2$.
