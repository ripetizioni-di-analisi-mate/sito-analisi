A solid uniform disk rolls without slipping on a surface. Consider its total kinetic energy. Given the mass $m = 8\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the speed $v = 4\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the total kinetic energy $K$?

- $192\,\mathrm{J}$
- $64\,\mathrm{J}$
- $32\,\mathrm{J}$
- $96\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Rolling gives $\omega=v/R$. Add $I\omega^2/2=mv^2/4$ to the translational energy $mv^2/2$. In the stated units, the relation is $K = \dfrac{3 \cdot m \cdot v^{2}}{4}$. Substituting the given values yields $96\,\mathrm{J}$.
