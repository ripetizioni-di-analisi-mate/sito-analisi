A uniform disk rotates about its central axis. A tangential force acts at the rim and friction is negligible. Given the force magnitude $F = 12\,\mathrm{N}$, the mass $m = 4\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the radius $R = 2\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the angular acceleration magnitude $\alpha$?

- $6\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $3\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$

## Explanation

The torque is $FR$ and the disk's moment of inertia is $mR^2/2$. Their ratio gives the angular acceleration. In the stated units, the relation is $\alpha = \dfrac{2 \cdot F}{R \cdot m}$. Substituting the given values yields $3\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$.
