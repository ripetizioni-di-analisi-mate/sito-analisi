What is the angular velocity of the minute hand of a clock?

- $1.75 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $1.05 \cdot 10^{-1}\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $2.78 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $1.45 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

The minute hand makes one complete turn, that is, it sweeps the angle $2\pi\,\mathrm{rad}$, in one hour, that is, in $3600\,\mathrm{s}$. The rotation is uniform, so
$$
\omega = \frac{2\pi}{3600}\,\mathrm{rad/s} = 1.75 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{rad/s} .
$$
The value $1.05 \cdot 10^{-1}\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ is the angular velocity of the second hand, and $1.45 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ that of the hour hand, which makes one turn in $12$ hours.
