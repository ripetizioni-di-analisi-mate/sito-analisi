- $4{,}90\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

During the collision the hinge exerts an impulsive reaction, which has zero torque about its axis, so the angular momentum about the axis of the hinge is conserved. Before the collision the angular momentum is that of the bullet, $m v_0 L = 0{,}010 \cdot 300 \cdot 1{,}2\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s} = 3{,}6\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$. After the collision the rod, with $\dfrac{1}{3} M L^2 = 0{,}72\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$, and the bullet at the end, with $m L^2 = 0{,}0144\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$, rotate together, so $I_O = 0{,}734\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$. Therefore
$$
\omega = \frac{m v_0 L}{I_O} = \frac{3{,}6}{0{,}734}\,\mathrm{rad/s} = 4{,}90\,\mathrm{rad/s} .
$$
The value $1{,}66\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ is obtained by imposing conservation of momentum, which does not hold during the collision because of the impulsive reaction of the hinge, and $18{,}5\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ by using the moment of inertia of the rod about its center.
