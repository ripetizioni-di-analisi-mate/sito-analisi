Two identical uniform disks can rotate freely. One rolls without slipping at speed $v$ and the other translates at speed $v$ without spinning. What fraction of the rolling disk's kinetic energy is rotational?

- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac23$

## Explanation

With $I=mR^2/2$ and $\omega=v/R$, rotational energy is $mv^2/4$. The total is $3mv^2/4$ because it also includes translation.
