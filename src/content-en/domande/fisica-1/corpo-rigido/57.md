A solid uniform disk rolls without slipping on a surface. Consider its total kinetic energy. Given the speed $v = 4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the total kinetic energy $K = 96\,\mathrm{J}$, what is the mass $m$?

- $16\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $24\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Rolling gives $\omega=v/R$. Add $I\omega^2/2=mv^2/4$ to the translational energy $mv^2/2$. In the stated units, the relation is $K = \dfrac{3 \cdot m \cdot v^{2}}{4}$. Solving for the unknown gives $m = \dfrac{4 \cdot K}{3 \cdot v^{2}}$. Substituting the given values yields $8\,\mathrm{kg}$.
