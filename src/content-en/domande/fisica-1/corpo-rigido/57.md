A thin hoop, a solid disk and a solid sphere, all uniform, have the same mass and the same radius. What is the increasing order of their moments of inertia about a symmetry axis through the center?

- Hoop, disk, sphere
- Disk, sphere, hoop
- Sphere, disk, hoop
- Sphere, hoop, disk

## Explanation

About a symmetry axis through the center the moments of inertia are $\dfrac{2}{5} M R^2$ for the solid sphere, $\dfrac{1}{2} M R^2$ for the solid disk and $M R^2$ for the hoop. Since $\dfrac{2}{5} < \dfrac{1}{2} < 1$, the increasing order is sphere, disk, hoop. In the hoop all the mass is at distance $R$ from the axis, whereas in the disk and in the sphere part of the mass is closer to the axis, and in the sphere this part is larger.
