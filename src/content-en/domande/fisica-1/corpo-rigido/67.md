A horizontal beam of negligible weight rests on two supports $6\,\mathrm{m}$ apart. A vertical load acts between them. Given the distance of the load from the left support $d = 2\,\mathrm{m}$ and the vertical reaction at the right support $N = 40\,\mathrm{N}$, what is the load's weight $F$?

- $120\,\mathrm{N}$
- $80\,\mathrm{N}$
- $240\,\mathrm{N}$
- $60\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

About the left support, the load has moment arm $d$ and the right reaction has moment arm $6\,\mathrm{m}$. The torques balance. In the stated units, the relation is $N = \dfrac{F \cdot d}{6}$. Solving for the unknown gives $F = \dfrac{6 \cdot N}{d}$. Substituting the given values yields $120\,\mathrm{N}$.
