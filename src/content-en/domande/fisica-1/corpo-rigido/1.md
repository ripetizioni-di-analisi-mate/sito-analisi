Four point masses of $0.50\,\mathrm{kg}$ each are fixed at the vertices of a square frame of side $0.40\,\mathrm{m}$ and negligible mass. What is the moment of inertia of the system about the axis perpendicular to the plane of the square through a vertex?

- $0.16\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.32\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.48\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.24\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$

## Explanation

About the axis through a vertex, the mass at that vertex is at zero distance, the two masses at the adjacent vertices are at the distance of the side $\ell = 0.40\,\mathrm{m}$, and the mass at the opposite vertex is at the distance of the diagonal $\ell\sqrt{2}$. Therefore
$$
I = m\left( 0 + \ell^2 + \ell^2 + 2\ell^2 \right) = 4 m \ell^2 = 4 \cdot 0.50 \cdot 0.40^2\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2} = 0.32\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2} .
$$
The same value follows from the **parallel-axis theorem**. About the center each mass is at distance $\dfrac{\ell}{\sqrt{2}}$, so $I_C = 2 m \ell^2 = 0.16\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$, and the axis through the vertex is at distance $d = \dfrac{\ell}{\sqrt{2}}$ from the center, therefore $I = I_C + 4 m d^2 = 0.32\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$. The value $0.48\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ is obtained by taking the side instead of the half-diagonal as $d$.
