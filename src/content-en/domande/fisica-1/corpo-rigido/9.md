## Explanation

For a body with $I_C = k M R^2$ that rolls without slipping down a plane inclined at an angle $\beta$, static friction equals $f = \dfrac{k}{1 + k}\,M g\sin\beta$ and the normal reaction equals $N = M g\cos\beta$. The condition $f \leq \mu_s N$ is therefore equivalent to $\mu_s \geq \dfrac{k}{1 + k}\tan\beta$. For the solid sphere $k = \dfrac{2}{5}$, so $\dfrac{k}{1 + k} = \dfrac{2}{7}$ and
$$
\mu_s \geq \frac{2}{7}\tan 40^\circ = \frac{2}{7} \cdot 0.839 = 0.24 .
$$
With $k$ in place of $\dfrac{k}{1 + k}$ one would obtain $0.34$, and with the sine in place of the tangent $0.18$.
