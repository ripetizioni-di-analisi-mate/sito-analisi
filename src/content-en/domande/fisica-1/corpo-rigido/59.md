A solid uniform disk rolls without slipping on a surface. Consider its total kinetic energy. The initial data are the mass $m = 8\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the speed $v = 4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Change the speed to $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the ratio of the final value of $K$ to its initial value?

- $\dfrac{9}{4}$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $\dfrac{4}{9}$
- $1$

## Explanation

Rolling gives $\omega=v/R$. The translational kinetic energy $mv^2/2$ is added to the rotational kinetic energy $I\omega^2/2=mv^2/4$. The relation $K = \dfrac{3 \cdot m \cdot v^{2}}{4}$ gives $96\,\mathrm{J}$ before the change and $216\,\mathrm{J}$ after it. The ratio is $\dfrac{9}{4}$.
