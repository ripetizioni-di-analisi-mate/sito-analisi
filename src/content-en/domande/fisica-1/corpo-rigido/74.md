A flywheel rotates about a fixed axis. A constant net torque acts in the direction of rotation. The initial angular speed is $3\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. The initial data are the moment of inertia $I = 4\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$, the magnitude of the net torque $\tau = 8\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ and the angle swept out $\theta = 5\,\mathrm{rad}$. Change the angle swept out to $10\,\mathrm{rad}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the ratio of the final value of $K$ to its initial value?

- $2$
- $\dfrac{49}{29}$
- $\dfrac{29}{19}$
- $\dfrac{29}{49}$

## Explanation

The constant torque does work $\tau\theta$. The initial kinetic energy $I\omega_0^2/2$ is added to the work. The relation $K = \dfrac{9 \cdot I}{2} + \tau \cdot \theta$ gives $58\,\mathrm{J}$ before the change and $98\,\mathrm{J}$ after it. The ratio is $\dfrac{49}{29}$.
