A bicycle moves in a straight line toward the east on a horizontal road and its wheels roll without slipping. In which direction does the angular velocity vector of a wheel point?

- South
- North
- East
- Vertical, upward

## Explanation

Take the $x$ axis toward the east, the $y$ axis toward the north and the $z$ axis upward. The top point of the wheel, whose position relative to the center is $\vec{r}\,' = R\,\hat{k}$, moves toward the east relative to the center, so $\vec{\omega} \times R\,\hat{k}$ must point along $\hat{\imath}$. Since $\hat{\jmath} \times \hat{k} = \hat{\imath}$, the vector $\vec{\omega}$ points along $\hat{\jmath}$, that is, toward the north. The same direction follows from the right-hand rule, with the fingers curled in the direction in which the wheel turns.
