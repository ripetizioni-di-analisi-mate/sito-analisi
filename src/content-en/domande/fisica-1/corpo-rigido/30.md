A motor delivers a power of $3.0\,\mathrm{kW}$ to a shaft that turns at a constant $1500$ revolutions per minute. What is the torque applied by the motor to the shaft?

- $2.00\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $38.2\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $19.1\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $120\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$

## Explanation

The angular velocity of the shaft is $\omega = \dfrac{1500 \cdot 2\pi}{60}\,\mathrm{rad/s} = 50\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s} \approx 157\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. The power of a torque is $P = \tau_z\,\omega$, so
$$
\tau_z = \frac{P}{\omega} = \frac{3000}{157}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m} = 19.1\,\mathrm{N\cdot m} .
$$
The value $2.00\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$ is obtained by dividing by the revolutions per minute, and $120\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$ by dividing by the revolutions per second, without converting to radians per second.
