A horizontal beam of negligible weight rests on two supports $6\,\mathrm{m}$ apart. A vertical load acts between them. Given the load's weight $F = 120\,\mathrm{N}$ and the vertical reaction at the right support $N = 40\,\mathrm{N}$, what is the distance of the load from the left support $d$?

- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

About the left support, the load has moment arm $d$ and the right reaction has moment arm $6\,\mathrm{m}$. The torques balance. In the stated units, the relation is $N = \dfrac{F \cdot d}{6}$. Solving for the unknown gives $d = \dfrac{6 \cdot N}{F}$. Substituting the given values yields $2\,\mathrm{m}$.
