The wheel of an upside-down bicycle, of diameter $0.70\,\mathrm{m}$, turns about its own fixed axle at $300$ revolutions per minute. What is the speed of a point on the rim of the wheel?

- $22.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $105\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $11.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5.50\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The angular velocity is $\omega = \dfrac{300 \cdot 2\pi}{60}\,\mathrm{rad/s} = 10\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s} \approx 31.4\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. The rim is at the distance of the radius $r = 0.35\,\mathrm{m}$ from the axis, so
$$
v = \omega r = 31.4 \cdot 0.35\,\mathrm{m/s} = 11.0\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The value $22.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is obtained by using the diameter in place of the radius, and $105\,\mathrm{m/s}$ by using the revolutions per minute without converting them to radians per second.
