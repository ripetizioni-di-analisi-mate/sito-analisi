A wheel with moment of inertia $I$ starts from rest about a fixed axis. A constant net torque acts on it. The initial data are the magnitude of the net torque $\tau = 12\,\mathrm{N\,m}$, the elapsed time $t = 3\,\mathrm{s}$ and the moment of inertia $I = 6\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$. Change the magnitude of the net torque to $18\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ and the moment of inertia to $9\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the final value of $\theta$ minus its initial value?

- $0\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $1\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $2\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $9\,\mathrm{rad}$

## Explanation

Angular acceleration is constant and equals $\tau/I$. Starting from rest gives $\theta=\alpha t^2/2$. The relation $\theta = \dfrac{t^{2} \cdot \tau}{2 \cdot I}$ gives $9\,\mathrm{rad}$ before the change and $9\,\mathrm{rad}$ after it. Subtracting the initial value from the final value gives $0\,\mathrm{rad}$.
