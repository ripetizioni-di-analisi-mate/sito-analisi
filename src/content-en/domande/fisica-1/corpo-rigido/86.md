A uniform box with base $b = 0.40\,\mathrm{m}$ and height $h = 1.0\,\mathrm{m}$ rests on a board that is slowly tilted, with the base parallel to the line of steepest slope. Friction is enough to prevent sliding. At what angle of tilt does the box start to tip over?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 poligoni="0,0 6,0 6,1.608;2.415,0.647 3.188,0.854 2.67,2.786 1.897,2.579" vettori="2.542,1.717,2.542,0.417,mg" punti="2.542,1.717,pieno,C" etichette="2.9,0.45,b;1.75,1.7,h;1.2,0.12,β" xmin=-0.3 xmax=6.3 ymin=-0.4 ymax=3.2 titolo="Box of base $b$ and height $h$ at rest on a plane inclined at an angle $\beta$"}

- $21.8^\circ$
- $68.2^\circ$
- $38.7^\circ$
- $11.3^\circ$

## Explanation

The box stays in equilibrium as long as the total reaction of the plane, which balances the weight, can pass through a point of the base. The reaction is opposite to the weight and has the same line of action, that is, the vertical through the center of mass; so tipping begins when this vertical passes through the lower edge of the base. The center of mass is at $\dfrac{h}{2}$ from the base and at $\dfrac{b}{2}$ from the edge, measured perpendicular and parallel to the plane respectively, therefore the condition is $\tan\beta = \dfrac{\;\dfrac{b}{2}\;}{\dfrac{h}{2}}$, that is
$$
\beta = \arctan\frac{b}{h} = \arctan 0.40 = 21.8^\circ .
$$
The value $68.2^\circ$ is obtained by swapping base and height.
