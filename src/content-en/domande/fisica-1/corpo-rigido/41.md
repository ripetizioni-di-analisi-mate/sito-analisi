A uniform solid cylinder and a uniform solid sphere have the same mass and roll without slipping on a horizontal plane, with their centers moving at the same speed. Which statement about their kinetic energies is true?

- The cylinder has greater kinetic energy
- The sphere has greater kinetic energy
- They have the same kinetic energy
- The comparison depends on the radii of the two bodies

## Explanation

In pure rolling the kinetic energy is $K = \dfrac{1}{2}\left( M + \dfrac{I_C}{R^2} \right) v_C^2$, and with $I_C = k M R^2$ it becomes $K = \dfrac{1}{2}(1 + k)\,M v_C^2$, which does not depend on the radius. For the solid cylinder $k = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and $K = \dfrac{3}{4} M v_C^2$, for the solid sphere $k = \dfrac{2}{5}$ and $K = \dfrac{7}{10} M v_C^2$. Since $\dfrac{3}{4} > \dfrac{7}{10}$, **the cylinder has greater kinetic energy**. The translational energies are equal, and the difference lies in the rotational energy.
