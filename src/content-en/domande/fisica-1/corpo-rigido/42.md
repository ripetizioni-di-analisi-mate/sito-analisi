A uniform solid sphere moves down an inclined plane rolling without slipping. Which statement about the friction force exerted by the plane on the sphere is true?

- It is static friction and does no work
- It is kinetic friction and dissipates mechanical energy
- It is zero, because the sphere does not slip
- It is directed down the plane

## Explanation

In pure rolling the contact point has zero velocity, so the friction is static. Its power $\vec{f} \cdot \vec{v}_P$ is zero, because $\vec{v}_P = \vec{0}$, therefore friction does no work and mechanical energy is conserved. For a plane inclined at an angle $\beta$ the friction nevertheless has magnitude $f = \dfrac{2}{7}\,M g\sin\beta$ and is directed up the plane, because its torque about the center makes the angular velocity increase.
