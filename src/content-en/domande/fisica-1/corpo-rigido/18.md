A force of $50\,\mathrm{N}$ is applied at the end of the handle of a wrench $30\,\mathrm{cm}$ long and forms an angle of $30^\circ$ with the handle. What is the magnitude of the torque of the force about the bolt, which is at the other end of the handle?

- $7{,}5\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $13\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $750\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$

## Explanation

The vector $\vec{r}$ goes from the bolt to the point of application and has magnitude $r = 0{,}30\,\mathrm{m}$, and the angle between $\vec{r}$ and $\vec{F}$ is $\varphi = 30^\circ$. Therefore
$$
\tau = r F\sin\varphi = 0{,}30 \cdot 50 \cdot 0{,}50\,\mathrm{N\cdot m} = 7{,}5\,\mathrm{N\cdot m} ,
$$
and the lever arm of the force is $b = r\sin\varphi = 0{,}15\,\mathrm{m}$. With the cosine in place of the sine one would obtain $13\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$, and with the force perpendicular to the handle $15\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$. The value $750\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$ is obtained by leaving the length in centimeters.
