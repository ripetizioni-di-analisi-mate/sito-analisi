## Explanation

The weight and the reaction of the axle have zero torque about the vertical axis, and the axle is frictionless, so the component $L_z$ of the angular momentum of the system formed by the platform and the person is conserved. Before the jump the person has zero angular momentum, therefore $L_z = I_1\omega_1$ with $I_1 = \dfrac{1}{2} M R^2 = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 150 \cdot 1{,}5^2\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2} = 168{,}75\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$. After the jump the person on the rim adds $m R^2 = 75 \cdot 1{,}5^2\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2} = 168{,}75\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$, so $I_2 = 337{,}5\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$. Therefore
$$
\omega_2 = \frac{I_1}{I_2}\,\omega_1 = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 0{,}80\,\mathrm{rad/s} = 0{,}40\,\mathrm{rad/s} .
$$
With the moment of inertia $M R^2$ for the platform one would obtain $0{,}53\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
