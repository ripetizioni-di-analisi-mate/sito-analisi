A uniform solid cylinder of mass $4.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ rests on a rough horizontal plane. A horizontal force of $12\,\mathrm{N}$, perpendicular to the axis of the cylinder, is applied to the axis itself, and the cylinder rolls without slipping. What is the acceleration of the center?

- $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $1.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Besides the weight and the normal force, the cylinder is acted on by the force $F$ applied at the center and by static friction $f$ at the contact point, opposite to $F$. The first cardinal equation gives $F - f = M a$; about the center only friction has a torque, so $f R = \dfrac{1}{2} M R^2\,\alpha$, and with $a = \alpha R$ one gets $f = \dfrac{1}{2} M a$. Therefore
$$
a = \frac{2F}{3M} = \frac{2 \cdot 12}{3 \cdot 4.0}\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
The value $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ is obtained by neglecting friction, as if the cylinder slid without rotating.
