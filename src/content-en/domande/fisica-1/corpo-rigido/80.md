A wheel rotates about a fixed axis with constant moment of inertia. A braking torque of constant magnitude brings it to rest. Given the magnitude of the net torque $\tau = 6\,\mathrm{N\,m}$, the moment of inertia $I = 4\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$ and the stopping time $t = 10\,\mathrm{s}$, what is the initial angular speed $\omega$?

- $\dfrac{45}{2}\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $\dfrac{15}{2}\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $30\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $15\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

The angular impulse $\tau t$ has magnitude equal to the initial angular momentum $I\omega$. In the stated units, the relation is $t = \dfrac{I \cdot \omega}{\tau}$. Solving for the unknown gives $\omega = \dfrac{t \cdot \tau}{I}$. Substituting the given values yields $15\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
