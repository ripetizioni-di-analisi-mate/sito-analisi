A skater pulls her arms inward with negligible external torque about her rotation axis. Her initial moment of inertia is $6\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$. Given the initial angular speed $u = 4\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ and the final moment of inertia $I = 2\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$, what is the final angular speed $\omega$?

- $\dfrac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $24\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

Angular momentum is conserved, so the product of moment of inertia and angular speed stays the same. In the stated units, the relation is $\omega = \dfrac{6 \cdot u}{I}$. Substituting the given values yields $12\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
