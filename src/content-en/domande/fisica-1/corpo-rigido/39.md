A uniform solid sphere rolls without slipping down a plane inclined at $30^\circ$ to the horizontal. What is the acceleration of its center?

- $4.91\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3.50\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2.45\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $6.07\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

For a body with $I_C = k M R^2$ that rolls without slipping down a plane inclined at an angle $\beta$, the acceleration of the center is $a_C = \dfrac{g\sin\beta}{1 + k}$. For the solid sphere $k = \dfrac{2}{5}$, so
$$
a_C = \frac{5}{7}\,g\sin 30^\circ = \frac{5}{7} \cdot 9.81 \cdot 0.50\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 3.50\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
The value $4.91\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ is the acceleration of a body that slides without friction, $2.45\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ that of a ring, and $6.07\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ is obtained with the cosine in place of the sine.
