In which of these cases is the angular momentum of the body about the indicated axis conserved? Air resistance is negligible.

- A simple pendulum swinging, about the horizontal axis of the pivot
- A yo-yo descending as the string unwinds, about its own axis
- A wheel braked by a brake shoe, about its own axis
- A diver in flight who tucks in, about a horizontal axis through the diver's center of mass

## Explanation

The angular momentum is conserved when the torque of the external forces about the axis is zero. The only force on the diver in flight is the weight, applied at the center of mass, so its torque about an axis through the center of mass is zero, and the second cardinal equation also holds with the pole at the moving center of mass. In the pendulum the weight has a torque about the pivot, in the yo-yo the tension of the string has a torque about the axis, and in the braked wheel the shoe exerts a friction torque; in these three cases the angular momentum changes.
