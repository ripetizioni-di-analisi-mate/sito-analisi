A flywheel rotates about a fixed axis. A constant net torque acts in the direction of rotation. The initial angular speed is $3\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. Given the magnitude of the net torque $\tau = 8\,\mathrm{N\,m}$, the angle swept out $\theta = 5\,\mathrm{rad}$ and the final kinetic energy $K = 58\,\mathrm{J}$, what is the moment of inertia $I$?

- $2\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$

## Explanation

The constant torque does work $\tau\theta$. Add the initial rotational kinetic energy $I\omega_0^2/2$. In the stated units, the relation is $K = \dfrac{9 \cdot I}{2} + \tau \cdot \theta$. Solving for the unknown gives $I = \dfrac{2 \cdot K}{9} - \dfrac{2 \cdot \tau \cdot \theta}{9}$. Substituting the given values yields $4\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$.
