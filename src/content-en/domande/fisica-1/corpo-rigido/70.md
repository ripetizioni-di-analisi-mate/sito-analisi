A horizontal beam of negligible weight rests on two supports $6\,\mathrm{m}$ apart. A vertical load acts between them. The initial data are the load's weight $F = 120\,\mathrm{N}$ and the distance of the load from the left support $d = 2\,\mathrm{m}$. Change the load's weight to $90\,\mathrm{N}$ and the distance of the load from the left support to $3\,\mathrm{m}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the final value of $N$ minus its initial value?

- $-5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-35\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-15\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

About the left support, the load has moment arm $d$ and the right reaction has moment arm $6\,\mathrm{m}$. The torques balance. The relation $N = \dfrac{F \cdot d}{6}$ gives $40\,\mathrm{N}$ before the change and $45\,\mathrm{N}$ after it. Subtracting the initial value from the final value gives $5\,\mathrm{N}$.
