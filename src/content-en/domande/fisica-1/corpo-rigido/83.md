Two identical point masses are fixed to the ends of a rod of negligible mass. The axis is perpendicular to the rod and passes through its center. Given the total rod length $L = 4\,\mathrm{m}$ and the moment of inertia $I = 24\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$, what is the mass of each body $m$?

- $3\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Each mass is at distance $L/2$ from the axis. The moment of inertia is the sum of the two contributions $m(L/2)^2$. In the stated units, the relation is $I = \dfrac{L^{2} \cdot m}{2}$. Solving for the unknown gives $m = \dfrac{2 \cdot I}{L^{2}}$. Substituting the given values yields $3\,\mathrm{kg}$.
