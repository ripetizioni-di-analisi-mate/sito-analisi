A uniform solid sphere of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ rolls without slipping on a horizontal plane, and its center moves at $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is its kinetic energy?

- $9.00\,\mathrm{J}$
- $12.6\,\mathrm{J}$
- $3.60\,\mathrm{J}$
- $18.0\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

For the kinetic energy of a rigid body $K = \dfrac{1}{2} M v_C^2 + \dfrac{1}{2} I_C\,\omega^2$, and in pure rolling $\omega = \dfrac{v_C}{R}$. With $I_C = \dfrac{2}{5} M R^2$ the rotational energy is $\dfrac{1}{5} M v_C^2 = 3.60\,\mathrm{J}$, and the translational energy is $\dfrac{1}{2} M v_C^2 = 9.00\,\mathrm{J}$. Therefore
$$
K = \frac{7}{10} M v_C^2 = \frac{7}{10} \cdot 2.0 \cdot 3.0^2\,\mathrm{J} = 12.6\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
The radius of the sphere cancels out and is not needed. The value $18.0\,\mathrm{J}$ is the kinetic energy of a ring with the same mass and the same velocity.
