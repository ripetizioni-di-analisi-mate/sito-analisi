A wheel with moment of inertia $I$ starts from rest about a fixed axis. A constant net torque acts on it. Given the elapsed time $t = 3\,\mathrm{s}$, the moment of inertia $I = 6\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$ and the angle swept out $\theta = 9\,\mathrm{rad}$, what is the magnitude of the net torque $\tau$?

- $36\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $24\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N\,m}$

## Explanation

Angular acceleration is constant and equals $\tau/I$. Starting from rest gives $\theta=\alpha t^2/2$. In the stated units, the relation is $\theta = \dfrac{t^{2} \cdot \tau}{2 \cdot I}$. Solving for the unknown gives $\tau = \dfrac{2 \cdot I \cdot \theta}{t^{2}}$. Substituting the given values yields $12\,\mathrm{N\,m}$.
