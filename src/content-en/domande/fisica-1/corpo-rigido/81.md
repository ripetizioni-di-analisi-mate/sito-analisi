A wheel rotates about a fixed axis with constant moment of inertia. A braking torque of constant magnitude brings it to rest. Given the initial angular speed $\omega = 15\,\mathrm{rad/s}$, the moment of inertia $I = 4\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$ and the stopping time $t = 10\,\mathrm{s}$, what is the magnitude of the net torque $\tau$?

- $9\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{N\,m}$

## Explanation

The angular impulse $\tau t$ has magnitude equal to the initial angular momentum $I\omega$. In the stated units, the relation is $t = \dfrac{I \cdot \omega}{\tau}$. Solving for the unknown gives $\tau = \dfrac{I \cdot \omega}{t}$. Substituting the given values yields $6\,\mathrm{N\,m}$.
