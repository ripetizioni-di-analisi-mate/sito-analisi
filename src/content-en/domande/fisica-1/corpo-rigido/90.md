A rod is hinged on the left and held horizontal by a vertical cable at its right end. The uniform rod weighs $40\,\mathrm{N}$ and is $4\,\mathrm{m}$ long. A load hangs between the hinge and cable. Given the hanging load's weight $W = 60\,\mathrm{N}$ and the cable tension $F = 50\,\mathrm{N}$, what is the distance of the load from the hinge $d$?

- $\dfrac{2}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{10}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The torque from the cable balances the torques from the load and from the rod's weight, which acts at its center. In the stated units, the relation is $F = \dfrac{W \cdot d}{4} + 20$. Solving for the unknown gives $d = \dfrac{4 \cdot \left(F - 20\right)}{W}$. Substituting the given values yields $2\,\mathrm{m}$.
