A rod is at rest on a smooth horizontal plane. Besides the weight and the reaction of the plane, the only forces acting on the rod are two horizontal forces of equal magnitude $F$ and opposite directions, applied at two different points, with parallel lines of action at distance $d$ from each other. Which statement is true?

- The resultant is zero and the rod starts to rotate
- The resultant is zero and the rod stays at rest
- The resultant has magnitude $2F$ and the rod starts to translate
- The resultant torque depends on the chosen pole

## Explanation

The two forces have zero sum, so the resultant is zero and the center of mass stays at rest; the weight and the reaction of the plane balance each other. The two forces form a **couple**. About a point on the line of action of one of the two forces, the torque of the other has magnitude $F d$, and since the resultant is zero the same torque is obtained about every pole. The resultant torque is not zero, therefore the second equilibrium condition is not satisfied and the rod **starts to rotate**.
