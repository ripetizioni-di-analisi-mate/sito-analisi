- $0.068\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.090\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.23\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$

## Explanation

About the symmetry axis, perpendicular to the plane and through the center, the ring has moment of inertia $I_C = M R^2$. The axis through a point of the ring is parallel to it and at distance $d = R$ from the center, so by the **parallel-axis theorem**
$$
I = M R^2 + M R^2 = 2 M R^2 = 2 \cdot 0.50 \cdot 0.30^2\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2} = 0.090\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2} .
$$
The value $0.045\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ is $I_C$ alone, and $0.068\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ is obtained by using for the ring the moment of inertia of the disk, $\dfrac{1}{2} M R^2$.
