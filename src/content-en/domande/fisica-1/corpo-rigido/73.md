A flywheel rotates about a fixed axis. A constant net torque acts in the direction of rotation. The initial angular speed is $3\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. Given the moment of inertia $I = 4\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$, the angle swept out $\theta = 5\,\mathrm{rad}$ and the final kinetic energy $K = 58\,\mathrm{J}$, what is the magnitude of the net torque $\tau$?

- $\dfrac{58}{5}\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $\dfrac{22}{5}\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $16\,\mathrm{N\,m}$

## Explanation

The constant torque does work $\tau\theta$. The initial kinetic energy $I\omega_0^2/2$ is added to the work. In the stated units, the relation is $K = \dfrac{9 \cdot I}{2} + \tau \cdot \theta$. Solving for the unknown gives $\tau = \dfrac{- \dfrac{9 \cdot I}{2} + K}{\theta}$. Substituting the given values yields $8\,\mathrm{N\,m}$.
