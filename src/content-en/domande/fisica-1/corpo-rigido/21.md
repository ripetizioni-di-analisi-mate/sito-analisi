A uniform rod of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ and length $1.2\,\mathrm{m}$ is hinged at one end and forms an angle of $60^\circ$ with the horizontal. What is the magnitude of the torque of the weight of the rod about the hinge?

- $10.2\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $11.8\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $0.600\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $5.89\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$

## Explanation

By the theorem on the torque of the weight, the weight of the rod is treated as a single force $M\vec{g}$ applied at the center of mass, that is at the midpoint, $\dfrac{L}{2} = 0.60\,\mathrm{m}$ from the hinge. The weight is vertical, so its lever arm is the horizontal distance between the hinge and the midpoint, $b = \dfrac{L}{2}\cos 60^\circ = 0.30\,\mathrm{m}$. Therefore
$$
\tau = M g b = 2.0 \cdot 9.81 \cdot 0.30\,\mathrm{N\cdot m} = 5.89\,\mathrm{N\cdot m} .
$$
With the sine in place of the cosine one would obtain $10.2\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$, and with the lever arm $\dfrac{L}{2}$ one would obtain $11.8\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$, which is the torque for the horizontal rod.
