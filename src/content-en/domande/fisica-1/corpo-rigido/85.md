A uniform shelf of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ and length $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$ sticks out horizontally from a wall, in which one of its ends is built in. A vase of mass $3.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ rests on the free end. What is the magnitude of the torque that the wall exerts on the shelf about the built-in end?

- $14.7\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $19.6\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $24.5\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $12.3\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$

## Explanation

The shelf is in equilibrium, so the net torque about the built-in end is zero. The weight of the shelf, $19.6\,\mathrm{N}$, is applied at the midpoint, $0.25\,\mathrm{m}$ from the wall, and the weight of the vase, $29.4\,\mathrm{N}$, is applied at the end, $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$ from the wall. Therefore the wall exerts a torque whose magnitude equals the sum of the torques of the two weights:
$$
\tau = (19.6 \cdot 0.25 + 29.4 \cdot 0.50)\,\mathrm{N\cdot m} = 19.6\,\mathrm{N\cdot m} .
$$
The value $14.7\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$ is obtained by neglecting the weight of the shelf, and $24.5\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$ by applying the weight of the shelf at the free end as well.
