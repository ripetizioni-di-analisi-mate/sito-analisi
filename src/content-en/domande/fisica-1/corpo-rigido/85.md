A load hangs from a rope wrapped around a drum with a fixed axis. The rope does not slip. The drum has radius $0{,}5\,\mathrm{m}$ and the load descends at constant speed. Given the magnitude of the braking torque $\tau = 15\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ and the speed of descent $v = 2\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the power absorbed by the brake $P$?

- $15\,\mathrm{W}$
- $30\,\mathrm{W}$
- $60\,\mathrm{W}$
- $120\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

Without slipping, $\omega=v/R=2v$. The power magnitude is braking torque times $\omega$. In the stated units, the relation is $P = 2 \cdot \tau \cdot v$. Substituting the given values yields $60\,\mathrm{W}$.
