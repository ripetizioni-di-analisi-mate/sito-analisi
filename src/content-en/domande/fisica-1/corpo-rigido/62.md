The angular momentum of a rotor about its own fixed axis increases uniformly from $2.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$ to $8.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2/s}$ in $3.0\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the axial torque of the external forces acting on the rotor?

- $2.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $6.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $18\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $3.3\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$

## Explanation

Projecting the **second cardinal equation** onto the axis gives $\tau_z = \dfrac{dL_z}{dt}$. The angular momentum increases uniformly, so its derivative is constant and equal to the ratio of the change to the time:
$$
\tau_z = \frac{8.0 - 2.0}{3.0}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m} = 2.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m} .
$$
The value $6.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$ is the change in angular momentum, obtained by forgetting to divide by the time.
