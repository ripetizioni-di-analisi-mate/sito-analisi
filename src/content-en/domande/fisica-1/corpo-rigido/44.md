A uniform rod of mass $4{,}0\,\mathrm{kg}$ is horizontal, hinged to a vertical wall at one end and supported at the other end by a string that forms an angle of $30^\circ$ with the rod. What is the tension of the string?

- $39{,}2\,\mathrm{N}$
- $19{,}6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $22{,}7\,\mathrm{N}$
- $78{,}5\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Torques are computed about the hinge, so that the reaction of the hinge does not appear. The weight $M g = 39{,}2\,\mathrm{N}$ is applied at the midpoint and has lever arm $\dfrac{L}{2}$, while the tension is applied at the end and has lever arm $L\sin 30^\circ = \dfrac{L}{2}$. The balance of torques gives $T\,\dfrac{L}{2} = M g\,\dfrac{L}{2}$, so $T = M g = 39{,}2\,\mathrm{N}$. With the cosine in place of the sine one would obtain $22{,}7\,\mathrm{N}$, and taking the whole length $L$ as the lever arm of the tension one would obtain $19{,}6\,\mathrm{N}$.
