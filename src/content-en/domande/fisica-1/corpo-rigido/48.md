A uniform disk rotates about its central axis. A tangential force acts at the rim and friction is negligible. Given the force magnitude $F = 12\,\mathrm{N}$, the radius $R = 2\,\mathrm{m}$ and the angular acceleration magnitude $\alpha = 3\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$, what is the mass $m$?

- $2\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $1\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The torque is $FR$ and the disk's moment of inertia is $mR^2/2$. Their ratio gives the angular acceleration. In the stated units, the relation is $\alpha = \dfrac{2 \cdot F}{R \cdot m}$. Solving for the unknown gives $m = \dfrac{2 \cdot F}{R \cdot \alpha}$. Substituting the given values yields $4\,\mathrm{kg}$.
