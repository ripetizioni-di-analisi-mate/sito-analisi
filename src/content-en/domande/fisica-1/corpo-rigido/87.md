A uniform door of mass $20\,\mathrm{kg}$ and width $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$ is supported by two hinges on its vertical edge, $1.6\,\mathrm{m}$ apart. The door is at rest. What is the magnitude of the horizontal component of the force each hinge exerts on the door?

- $49.1\,\mathrm{N}$
- $98.1\,\mathrm{N}$
- $196\,\mathrm{N}$
- $24.5\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The horizontal components of the hinge forces are the only horizontal forces, so they are equal and opposite and form a couple with arm $1.6\,\mathrm{m}$. The lower hinge is chosen as the pole. About it the weight, applied at the center of the door, has lever arm $0.40\,\mathrm{m}$, and the horizontal force $H$ of the upper hinge has lever arm $1.6\,\mathrm{m}$. The balance of torques gives $H \cdot 1.6 = 20 \cdot 9.81 \cdot 0.40$, therefore
$$
H = \frac{20 \cdot 9.81 \cdot 0.40}{1.6}\,\mathrm{N} = 49.1\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The value $98.1\,\mathrm{N}$ is obtained by using the whole width of the door as the lever arm of the weight instead of half of it.
