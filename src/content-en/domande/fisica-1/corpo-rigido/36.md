A rod lies at rest on a smooth horizontal table and is not constrained to any axis. A small ball sliding on the table, perpendicular to the rod, hits one of its ends and sticks to it. Which statement about the system formed by the rod and the ball, during the collision, is true?

- Only the angular momentum is conserved, because the rod rotates after the collision
- Only the momentum is conserved, because the rod has no fixed axis
- Both the momentum and the kinetic energy are conserved
- Both the momentum and the angular momentum about a fixed pole are conserved

## Explanation

The external forces on the system are the weights and the reactions of the table, which are vertical and balance each other, so their resultant and their torque are zero. The table is smooth and there is no axis exerting impulsive reactions. Therefore, by the two cardinal equations, both the momentum and the angular momentum about a fixed pole are conserved. The ball sticks to the rod, so the collision is inelastic and the kinetic energy is **not** conserved.
