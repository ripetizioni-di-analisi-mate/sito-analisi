A ball of clay, moving horizontally, strikes the lower end of a rod hanging from a frictionless hinge at its upper end, and sticks to the rod. Which quantity of the system formed by the rod and the ball is conserved during the collision?

- The angular momentum about the axis of the hinge
- The momentum
- The kinetic energy
- The momentum and the kinetic energy

## Explanation

During the collision the hinge exerts an impulsive reaction, which changes the momentum of the system, therefore momentum is not conserved. The reaction is applied on the axis, so it has zero axial torque, while the weight produces a negligible change in the short time of the collision. It follows that **the angular momentum about the axis of the hinge** is conserved. The ball sticks to the rod, so the collision is inelastic and the kinetic energy decreases.
