A rotor on bearings initially spins at $\omega_0$. A brake exerts a constant torque of magnitude $M$ for a time $t$ satisfying $Mt=I\omega_0$. What is the rotor's angular momentum immediately afterwards? Its inertia $I$ is constant and the braking torque opposes rotation.

- $I\omega_0$ in the initial direction
- $2I\omega_0$ in the initial direction
- Zero
- $I\omega_0$ in the opposite direction

## Explanation

The external angular impulse is $-Mt$. Adding it to the initial angular momentum gives $I\omega_0-Mt=0$.
