A horizontal beam of negligible weight rests on two supports $6\,\mathrm{m}$ apart. A vertical load acts between them. The initial data are the load's weight $F = 120\,\mathrm{N}$ and the distance of the load from the left support $d = 2\,\mathrm{m}$. Change the distance of the load from the left support to $4\,\mathrm{m}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the ratio of the final value of $N$ to its initial value?

- $4$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $2$
- $1$

## Explanation

About the left support, the load has moment arm $d$ and the right reaction has moment arm $6\,\mathrm{m}$. The torques balance. The relation $N = \dfrac{F \cdot d}{6}$ gives $40\,\mathrm{N}$ before the change and $80\,\mathrm{N}$ after it. The ratio is $2$.
