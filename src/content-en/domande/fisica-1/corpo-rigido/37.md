A car travels at $72\,\mathrm{km/h}$ and its wheels roll without slipping on the road. What is the velocity, relative to the road, of the highest point of a wheel?

- $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $40\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $144\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $28\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The velocity of the center of the wheel is that of the car, $v_C = \dfrac{72}{3{,}6}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 20\,\mathrm{m/s}$. In pure rolling the contact point is at rest, and at every instant the wheel rotates about it, with the speed of each point proportional to its distance from the contact point. The highest point is at distance $2R$ from the contact point, while the center is at distance $R$, so its speed is $2 v_C = 40\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The value $144\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is obtained by doubling the speed without converting the kilometers per hour.
