A rod is hinged on the left and held horizontal by a vertical cable at its right end. The uniform rod weighs $40\,\mathrm{N}$ and is $4\,\mathrm{m}$ long. A load hangs between the hinge and cable. Given the hanging load's weight $W = 60\,\mathrm{N}$ and the distance of the load from the hinge $d = 2\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the cable tension $F$?

- $70\,\mathrm{N}$
- $100\,\mathrm{N}$
- $30\,\mathrm{N}$
- $50\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The torque from the cable balances the torques from the load and from the rod's weight, which acts at its center. In the stated units, the relation is $F = \dfrac{W \cdot d}{4} + 20$. Substituting the given values yields $50\,\mathrm{N}$.
