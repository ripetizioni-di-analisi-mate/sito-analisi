A uniform rod of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ and length $1.0\,\mathrm{m}$ has a mass of $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ hanging from one end. The rod must stay horizontal while hanging from a single vertical string. At what distance from the end with the hanging mass must the string be attached?

- $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.33\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.67\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The forces are the tension of the string and two weights, all vertical. The net torque about the attachment point of the string must be zero, so the string must be attached at the center of mass of the system. Measuring distances from the end with the hanging mass, the $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass is at $0$ and the center of the rod at $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$, therefore
$$
x = \frac{1.0 \cdot 0 + 2.0 \cdot 0.50}{1.0 + 2.0}\,\mathrm{m} = 0.33\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $0.67\,\mathrm{m}$ is the distance of the same point from the other end.
