A rod is hinged on the left and held horizontal by a vertical cable at its right end. The uniform $4\,\mathrm{m}$ rod weighs $40\,\mathrm{N}$. A load hangs between the hinge and cable. Given the distance of the load from the hinge $d = 2\,\mathrm{m}$ and the cable tension $F = 50\,\mathrm{N}$, what is the hanging load's weight $W$?

- $20\,\mathrm{N}$
- $120\,\mathrm{N}$
- $60\,\mathrm{N}$
- $100\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The torque from the cable balances the torques from the load and from the rod's weight, which acts at its center. In the stated units, the relation is $F = \dfrac{W \cdot d}{4} + 20$. Solving for the unknown gives $W = \dfrac{4 \cdot \left(F - 20\right)}{d}$. Substituting the given values yields $60\,\mathrm{N}$.
