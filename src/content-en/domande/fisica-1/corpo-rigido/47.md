A uniform disk rotates about its central axis. A tangential force acts at the rim and friction is negligible. Given the mass $m = 4\,\mathrm{kg}$, the radius $R = 2\,\mathrm{m}$ and the angular acceleration magnitude $\alpha = 3\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$, what is the force magnitude $F$?

- $12\,\mathrm{N}$
- $6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $24\,\mathrm{N}$
- $48\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The torque is $FR$ and the disk's moment of inertia is $mR^2/2$. Their ratio gives the angular acceleration. In the stated units, the relation is $\alpha = \dfrac{2 \cdot F}{R \cdot m}$. Solving for the unknown gives $F = \dfrac{R \cdot \alpha \cdot m}{2}$. Substituting the given values yields $12\,\mathrm{N}$.
