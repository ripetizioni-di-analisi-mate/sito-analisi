A horizontal board is pulled along the floor at a constant speed of $1.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. On the board a cylinder of radius $0.15\,\mathrm{m}$ rolls without slipping relative to the board, while its center stays at rest relative to the floor. What is the angular velocity of the cylinder?

- $8.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $0.18\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- It is zero, because the center of the cylinder is at rest

## Explanation

The cylinder does not slip on the board, so its contact point has the same velocity as the board, $1.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The center is at rest, therefore the cylinder rotates about its own axis, which is fixed, and the contact point, at distance $R$ from the axis, has speed $\omega R$. Then
$$
\omega = \frac{v}{R} = \frac{1.2}{0.15}\,\mathrm{rad/s} = 8.0\,\mathrm{rad/s} .
$$
The rolling condition concerns the relative velocity between the cylinder and the board at the contact point, and the center at rest only means that the cylinder does not translate. The value $4.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ is obtained by using the diameter instead of the radius.
