## Explanation

The ladder is acted on by the weight at the midpoint, by the normal reaction $N$ and the friction $f$ of the floor at the foot, and by the horizontal reaction $N_p$ of the smooth wall. The balance of forces gives $N = m g$ and $f = N_p$, and the balance of torques about the foot gives $m g\,\dfrac{\ell}{2}\cos\theta = N_p\,\ell\sin\theta$, so $f = \dfrac{m g}{2\tan\theta}$. The condition $f \leq \mu_s N$ is equivalent to $\tan\theta \geq \dfrac{1}{2\mu_s} = \dfrac{1}{0.70} = 1.43$, therefore the minimum angle is $\theta_{\min} = 55.0^\circ$. The value $70.7^\circ$ is obtained by forgetting the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$, which comes from the weight applied at the midpoint.
