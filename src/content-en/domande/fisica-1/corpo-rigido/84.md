Two identical point masses are fixed to the ends of a rod of negligible mass. The axis is perpendicular to the rod and passes through its center. Given the mass of each body $m = 3\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the moment of inertia $I = 24\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$, what is the total rod length $L$?

- $4 \cdot \sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Each mass is at distance $L/2$ from the axis. The moment of inertia is the sum of the two contributions $m(L/2)^2$. In the stated units, the relation is $I = \dfrac{L^{2} \cdot m}{2}$. Solving for the unknown gives $L = \dfrac{\sqrt{2} \cdot \sqrt{I}}{\sqrt{m}}$. Substituting the given values yields $4\,\mathrm{m}$.
