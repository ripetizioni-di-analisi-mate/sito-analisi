A crate of mass $m$ is at rest on a rough plane inclined at an angle $\beta$. Which statement about the total reaction of the plane, that is, the sum of the normal reaction and the friction force, is true?

- It is perpendicular to the plane and has magnitude $m g\cos\beta$
- It is perpendicular to the plane and has magnitude $m g$
- It is vertical, points upward, and has magnitude $m g$
- It is parallel to the plane, points up the slope, and has magnitude $m g\sin\beta$

## Explanation

The only forces on the crate are the weight and the total reaction of the plane. The crate is in equilibrium, so the resultant is zero and the total reaction is opposite to the weight, that is vertical, upward and with magnitude $m g$. The net torque is also zero, therefore the two opposite forces must have the same line of action, that is, the vertical through the center of mass. The magnitude $m g\cos\beta$ is that of the normal reaction alone, and $m g\sin\beta$ that of the friction force alone.
