Three point masses are held in the $xy$ plane by a frame of negligible mass: $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ at the point $(0.30,\ 0)\,\mathrm{m}$, $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ at the point $(0,\ 0.40)\,\mathrm{m}$ and $4.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ at the point $(0.20,\ 0.10)\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the moment of inertia of the system about the $x$ axis?

- $0.34\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.20\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.54\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$
- $0.80\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$

## Explanation

A point $(x,\ y)$ of the plane is at distance $|y|$ from the $x$ axis, so the moment of inertia about the $x$ axis is $I_x = \sum_i m_i y_i^2$. The first mass lies on the axis and does not contribute, therefore
$$
I_x = 1.0 \cdot 0.40^2 + 4.0 \cdot 0.10^2\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2} = 0.20\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2} .
$$
The value $0.34\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ is the moment of inertia about the $y$ axis, and $0.54\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ the one about the $z$ axis, perpendicular to the plane.
