A solid uniform disk rolls without slipping on a surface. Consider its total kinetic energy. Given the mass $m = 8\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the total kinetic energy $K = 96\,\mathrm{J}$, what is the speed $v$?

- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2 \cdot \sqrt{6}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4 \cdot \sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Rolling gives $\omega=v/R$. Add $I\omega^2/2=mv^2/4$ to the translational energy $mv^2/2$. In the stated units, the relation is $K = \dfrac{3 \cdot m \cdot v^{2}}{4}$. Solving for the unknown gives $v = \dfrac{2 \cdot \sqrt{3} \cdot \sqrt{K}}{3 \cdot \sqrt{m}}$. Substituting the given values yields $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
