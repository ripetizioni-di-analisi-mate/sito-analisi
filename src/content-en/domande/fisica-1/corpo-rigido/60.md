A solid uniform disk rolls without slipping on a surface. Consider its total kinetic energy. The initial data are the mass $m = 8\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the speed $v = 4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Change the mass to $4\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the speed to $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the final value of $K$ minus its initial value?

- $64\,\mathrm{J}$
- $32\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-96\,\mathrm{J}$
- $96\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Rolling gives $\omega=v/R$. The translational kinetic energy $mv^2/2$ is added to the rotational kinetic energy $I\omega^2/2=mv^2/4$. The relation $K = \dfrac{3 \cdot m \cdot v^{2}}{4}$ gives $96\,\mathrm{J}$ before the change and $192\,\mathrm{J}$ after it. Subtracting the initial value from the final value gives $96\,\mathrm{J}$.
