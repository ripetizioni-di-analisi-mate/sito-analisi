The propeller of a model airplane has a moment of inertia of $0.020\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ about its own axis. The motor applies a torque of $0.50\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$ to it and friction is negligible. What is the angular acceleration of the propeller?

- $0.040\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $0.010\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $12.5\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$
- $25\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$

## Explanation

The propeller rotates about a fixed axis and the only axial torque is the one of the motor, so the equation of rotational motion $\tau_z = I\alpha$ holds. Therefore
$$
\alpha = \frac{\tau_z}{I} = \frac{0.50}{0.020}\,\mathrm{rad/s^2} = 25\,\mathrm{rad/s^2} .
$$
The value $0.040\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$ is obtained by dividing the moment of inertia by the torque, and $0.010\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$ by multiplying them.
