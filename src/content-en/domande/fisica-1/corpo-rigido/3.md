A wheel with moment of inertia $2.5\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ about its own axis turns at $90$ revolutions per minute. A brake applies a constant torque of $3.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$ that opposes the rotation; the friction of the axle is negligible. After how long does the wheel stop?

- $75.0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3.93\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.26\,\mathrm{s}$
- $7.85\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The initial angular velocity is $\omega_0 = \dfrac{90 \cdot 2\pi}{60}\,\mathrm{rad/s} = 3\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s} \approx 9.42\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. By the equation of rotational motion the magnitude of the angular acceleration is $|\alpha| = \dfrac{\tau}{I} = \dfrac{3.0}{2.5}\,\mathrm{rad/s^2} = 1.2\,\mathrm{rad/s^2}$. The torque is constant, so $\omega = \omega_0 - |\alpha|\,t$ and the wheel stops at the instant
$$
t = \frac{\omega_0}{|\alpha|} = \frac{9.42}{1.2}\,\mathrm{s} = 7.85\,\mathrm{s} .
$$
The value $75.0\,\mathrm{s}$ is obtained by using the revolutions per minute without converting them to radians per second.
