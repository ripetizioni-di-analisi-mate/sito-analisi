A flywheel rotates about a fixed axis. A constant net torque acts in the direction of rotation. The initial angular speed is $3\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. Given the moment of inertia $I = 4\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$, the magnitude of the net torque $\tau = 8\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ and the angle swept out $\theta = 5\,\mathrm{rad}$, what is the final kinetic energy $K$?

- $58\,\mathrm{J}$
- $116\,\mathrm{J}$
- $40\,\mathrm{J}$
- $76\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The constant torque does work $\tau\theta$. The initial kinetic energy $I\omega_0^2/2$ is added to the work. In the stated units, the relation is $K = \dfrac{9 \cdot I}{2} + \tau \cdot \theta$. Substituting the given values yields $58\,\mathrm{J}$.
