A uniform rod $0.60\,\mathrm{m}$ long oscillates in a vertical plane about a frictionless horizontal axis through one of its ends. What is the period of small oscillations?

- $1.55\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.10\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.27\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.634\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The rod is a physical pendulum with the center of mass at distance $d = \dfrac{L}{2}$ from the axis and moment of inertia $I_O = \dfrac{1}{3} M L^2$. The angular frequency of small oscillations is $\omega_0 = \sqrt{\dfrac{M g d}{I_O}} = \sqrt{\dfrac{3g}{2L}}$, so the period is
$$
T_0 = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{2L}{3g}} = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{1.2}{29.43}}\,\mathrm{s} = 1.27\,\mathrm{s} .
$$
The value $1.55\,\mathrm{s}$ is the period of a simple pendulum of length $L$, and $0.634\,\mathrm{s}$ is obtained by using the moment of inertia about the center, $\dfrac{1}{12} M L^2$, in place of $I_O$.
