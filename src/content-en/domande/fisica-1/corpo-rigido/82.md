Two identical point masses are fixed to the ends of a rod of negligible mass. The axis is perpendicular to the rod and passes through its center. Given the mass of each body $m = 3\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the total rod length $L = 4\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the moment of inertia $I$?

- $48\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$
- $24\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$
- $96\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$

## Explanation

Each mass is at distance $L/2$ from the axis. The moment of inertia is the sum of the two contributions $m(L/2)^2$. In the stated units, the relation is $I = \dfrac{L^{2} \cdot m}{2}$. Substituting the given values yields $24\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$.
