A wheel of radius $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$ rolls without slipping along a straight road and makes $100$ revolutions. How far does its center advance?

- $94.2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $30.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $188\,\mathrm{m}$
- $377\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

In pure rolling $v_C = \omega R$, so the displacement of the center equals the arc swept by a point of the rim, and in one revolution the center advances by $2\pi R$. Therefore in $100$ revolutions
$$
s = 100 \cdot 2\pi \cdot 0.30\,\mathrm{m} = 188\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $94.2\,\mathrm{m}$ is obtained by using $\pi R$ per revolution, and $377\,\mathrm{m}$ by using the diameter instead of the radius in the formula $2\pi R$.
