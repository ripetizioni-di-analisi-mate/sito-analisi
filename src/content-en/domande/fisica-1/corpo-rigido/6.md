A uniform rod $0{,}90\,\mathrm{m}$ long, hinged without friction at one end, is released from rest in a horizontal position. What is the angular velocity of the rod when it passes through the vertical?

- $5{,}72\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $4{,}67\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $8{,}09\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $11{,}4\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

The hinge is frictionless and the weight is a conservative force, so mechanical energy is conserved. From the horizontal to the vertical position the center of mass descends by $\dfrac{L}{2}$, and the moment of inertia about the end is $I = \dfrac{1}{3} M L^2$. Therefore
$$
M g\,\frac{L}{2} = \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{3} M L^2\,\omega^2
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\omega = \sqrt{\frac{3g}{L}} = \sqrt{\frac{3 \cdot 9{,}81}{0{,}90}}\,\mathrm{rad/s} = 5{,}72\,\mathrm{rad/s} .
$$
With the moment of inertia about the center, $\dfrac{1}{12} M L^2$, one would obtain $11{,}4\,\mathrm{rad/s}$, and with a descent of the center of mass equal to $L$ one would obtain $8{,}09\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
