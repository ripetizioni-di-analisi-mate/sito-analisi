A wheel with moment of inertia $I$ starts from rest about a fixed axis. A constant net torque acts on it. Given the magnitude of the net torque $\tau = 12\,\mathrm{N\,m}$, the elapsed time $t = 3\,\mathrm{s}$ and the moment of inertia $I = 6\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$, what is the angle swept out $\theta$?

- $9\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $18\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $3\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}\,\mathrm{rad}$

## Explanation

Angular acceleration is constant and equals $\tau/I$. Starting from rest gives $\theta=\alpha t^2/2$. In the stated units, the relation is $\theta = \dfrac{t^{2} \cdot \tau}{2 \cdot I}$. Substituting the given values yields $9\,\mathrm{rad}$.
