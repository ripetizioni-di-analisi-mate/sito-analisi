In the $xy$ plane the force $\vec{F} = (3.0\,\hat{\imath} + 4.0\,\hat{\jmath})\,\mathrm{N}$ is applied at the point $P = (2.0,\ 1.0)\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the component $\tau_z$ of the torque of the force about the origin?

::grafico{uguale=1 vettori="0,0,2,1,r;2,1,3.5,3,F" punti="2,1,pieno,P" xmin=-0.5 xmax=4.2 ymin=-0.5 ymax=3.5 titolo="The force $\vec{F}$ is applied at the point $P$; lengths are in meters"}

- $5.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $-5.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $11\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$

## Explanation

The torque about the origin is $\vec{\tau} = \vec{r} \times \vec{F}$ with $\vec{r} = (2.0,\ 1.0,\ 0)\,\mathrm{m}$. Both vectors lie in the $xy$ plane, so the torque is directed along $z$ and its component is
$$
\tau_z = x F_y - y F_x = (2.0 \cdot 4.0 - 1.0 \cdot 3.0)\,\mathrm{N\cdot m} = 5.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m} .
$$
The positive sign means that the force tends to turn the body counterclockwise about the origin. The value $-5.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$ is obtained by reversing the order of the factors in the cross product.
