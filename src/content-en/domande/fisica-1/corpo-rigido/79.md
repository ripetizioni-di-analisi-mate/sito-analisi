A wheel rotates about a fixed axis with constant moment of inertia. A braking torque of constant magnitude brings it to rest. Given the initial angular speed $\omega = 15\,\mathrm{rad/s}$, the magnitude of the net torque $\tau = 6\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ and the moment of inertia $I = 4\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$, what is the stopping time $t$?

- $10\,\mathrm{s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $15\,\mathrm{s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The angular impulse $\tau t$ has magnitude equal to the initial angular momentum $I\omega$. In the stated units, the relation is $t = \dfrac{I \cdot \omega}{\tau}$. Substituting the given values yields $10\,\mathrm{s}$.
