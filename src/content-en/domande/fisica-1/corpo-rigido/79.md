A wheel rolls without slipping on a horizontal road and its center $C$ moves at $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Relative to the road, what is the speed of the point $Q$ of the rim that is at the same height as the center, ahead of it?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 curve="cos(t),1+sin(t),0,6.29" linee="-1.8,0 2.6,0" vettori="0,1,0.7,1,v" punti="0,1,pieno,C;1,1,pieno,Q;0,0,pieno,P" xmin=-2 xmax=2.8 ymin=-0.4 ymax=2.3 titolo="The wheel rolls to the right; $Q$ is the point of the rim at the height of the center $C$, $P$ is the contact point"}

- $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $7.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

In pure rolling the body rotates at every instant about the contact point $P$, and each point has a velocity perpendicular to the segment joining it to $P$, with magnitude proportional to the distance from $P$. The center is at distance $R$ from $P$ and has speed $v_C = \omega R$; the point $Q$ is at distance $R\sqrt{2}$ from $P$. So
$$
v_Q = \omega R\sqrt{2} = \sqrt{2}\,v_C = 7.1\,\mathrm{m/s} ,
$$
directed $45^\circ$ below the horizontal. The value $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is the speed of the top point of the wheel.
