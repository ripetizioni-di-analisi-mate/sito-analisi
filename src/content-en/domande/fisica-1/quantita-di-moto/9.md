A cart of mass $3{,}0\,\mathrm{kg}$ with velocity $4{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ collides elastically, along a frictionless horizontal straight track, with a cart at rest of mass $1{,}0\,\mathrm{kg}$. What is the velocity of the $3{,}0\,\mathrm{kg}$ cart just after the collision?

- $2{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ opposite to the initial direction
- $6{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the initial direction
- $2{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the initial direction
- $3{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the initial direction

## Explanation

The second cart is at rest, so for the **elastic collision** in one dimension
$$
v_1' = \frac{m_1 - m_2}{m_1 + m_2}\,v_1 = \frac{3{,}0 - 1{,}0}{4{,}0} \cdot 4{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s} = 2{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The result is positive, therefore the $3{,}0\,\mathrm{kg}$ cart keeps moving in the initial direction. The light cart starts with $v_2' = \dfrac{2 m_1}{m_1 + m_2}\,v_1 = 6{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$, so the relative velocity after the collision, $4{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$, is the opposite of the one before the collision, $-4{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$, as in every elastic collision.
