A body at rest on a smooth horizontal plane explodes into two fragments, which slide on the plane. Which statement about the velocities of the two fragments right after the explosion is true?

- They are perpendicular
- They have the same magnitude
- They can have any directions in the plane
- They have the same direction and opposite senses

## Explanation

The forces of the explosion are internal and the external forces are vertical, so the horizontal momentum is conserved and stays zero: $m_1 \vec{v}_1 + m_2 \vec{v}_2 = \vec{0}$. It follows that $\vec{v}_2 = -\dfrac{m_1}{m_2}\,\vec{v}_1$, that is, the two velocities lie along the same line and point in opposite senses. The magnitudes are equal only if the masses are equal, because they are inversely proportional to the masses.
