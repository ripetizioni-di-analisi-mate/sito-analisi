A ball sticks in a stationary block on a frictionless track. The block's mass is $3\,\mathrm{kg}$. The initial data are the initial speed $u = 12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the ball's mass $m = 1\,\mathrm{kg}$. Change the initial speed to $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the ball's mass to $3\,\mathrm{kg}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the final value of $K$ minus its initial value?

- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $9\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-9\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-18\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Momentum is conserved and the shared speed is $mu/(m+3)$. The final kinetic energy uses the combined mass $m+3$. The relation $K = \dfrac{m^{2} \cdot u^{2}}{2 \cdot \left(m + 3\right)}$ gives $18\,\mathrm{J}$ before the change and $27\,\mathrm{J}$ after it. Subtracting the initial value from the final value gives $9\,\mathrm{J}$.
