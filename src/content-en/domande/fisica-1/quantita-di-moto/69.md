A cart collides elastically in one dimension with a stationary cart of three times its mass. The first cart reverses direction. Given the first cart's mass $m = 4\,\mathrm{kg}$ and its initial speed $u = 8\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the magnitude of its final momentum $p$?

- $24\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$
- $32\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$
- $16\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$

## Explanation

In a one-dimensional elastic collision with a stationary target, the first cart leaves with velocity $(m-3m)u/(m+3m)=-u/2$. In the stated units, the relation is $p = \dfrac{m \cdot u}{2}$. Substituting the given values yields $16\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$.
