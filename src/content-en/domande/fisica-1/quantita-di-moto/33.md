Two trolleys couple on a horizontal track. Their momenta just before impact are $+6$ and $-6\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$. If their masses differ and the external impulse is negligible, what follows?

- They come to rest, and all their initial translational kinetic energy has been converted.
- Their common velocity is positive.
- Their initial kinetic energy was zero.
- The heavier trolley retains its initial velocity.

## Explanation

Total momentum is zero. Once coupled, both masses have the same velocity, which must be zero. Before impact both were moving, so their kinetic energy was positive.
