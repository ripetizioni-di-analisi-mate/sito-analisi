A cart moving on a smooth horizontal track hits an identical cart at rest, and the two stay coupled. What fraction of the initial kinetic energy is dissipated?

- All of it
- One quarter
- One half
- None, because momentum is conserved

## Explanation

Momentum is conserved, so $m v = 2m\,V$ and the common velocity is $V = \dfrac{v}{2}$. The final kinetic energy is $\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 2m \cdot \dfrac{v^2}{4} = \dfrac{1}{4}\,m v^2$, that is, half of the initial one $\dfrac{1}{2}\,m v^2$. Therefore **one half** of the initial kinetic energy is dissipated, in agreement with the fraction $\dfrac{m_2}{m_1 + m_2}$ that holds when the second body is at rest.
