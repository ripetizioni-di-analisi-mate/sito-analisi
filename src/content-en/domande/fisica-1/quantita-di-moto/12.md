Two bodies of masses $4{,}0\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $1{,}0\,\mathrm{kg}$ move along the same line with velocities $2{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $-3{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the kinetic energy $K^{*}$ of the system in the center-of-mass frame?

- $12{,}5\,\mathrm{J}$
- $10{,}0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $2{,}50\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0{,}400\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The velocity of the center of mass is $v_{cm} = \dfrac{4{,}0 \cdot 2{,}0 + 1{,}0 \cdot (-3{,}0)}{5{,}0}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 1{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The total kinetic energy is $K = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 4{,}0 \cdot 2{,}0^2\,\mathrm{J} + \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 1{,}0 \cdot 3{,}0^2\,\mathrm{J} = 12{,}5\,\mathrm{J}$. By **König's theorem** $K = \dfrac{1}{2}\,M v_{cm}^2 + K^{*}$, with $\dfrac{1}{2}\,M v_{cm}^2 = 2{,}50\,\mathrm{J}$, so $K^{*} = 12{,}5\,\mathrm{J} - 2{,}50\,\mathrm{J} = 10{,}0\,\mathrm{J}$. The same value is obtained from $K^{*} = \dfrac{1}{2}\,\mu\,(v_1 - v_2)^2$, with $\mu = 0{,}80\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $v_1 - v_2 = 5{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
