A small rocket exhausts gas in one direction at constant relative speed. Neglect weight and drag in calculating thrust. Given the expelled mass per second $b = \dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{kg/s}$ and the thrust magnitude $F = 60\,\mathrm{N}$, what is the exhaust speed relative to the rocket $u$?

- $600\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $300\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $150\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $450\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Thrust is the magnitude of momentum transferred to the gas per unit time in the instantaneous rocket frame. In the stated units, the relation is $F = b \cdot u$. Solving for the unknown gives $u = \dfrac{F}{b}$. Substituting the given values yields $300\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
