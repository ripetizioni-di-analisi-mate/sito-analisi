Two carts move in the same direction. The faster first cart catches the second and sticks to it. The second cart's initial speed is $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. External impulse is negligible. Given the initial speed of the first cart $u = 8\,\mathrm{m/s}$, the mass of the second body $m_{2} = 4\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the shared speed after impact $v = 4\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the mass of the first body $m_{1}$?

- $4\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $2\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $1\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The initial momenta point in the same direction. After the collision the combined mass moves at speed $v$. In the stated units, the relation is $v = \dfrac{m_{1} \cdot u + 2 \cdot m_{2}}{m_{1} + m_{2}}$. Solving for the unknown gives $m_{1} = \dfrac{m_{2} \cdot \left(v - 2\right)}{u - v}$. Substituting the given values yields $2\,\mathrm{kg}$.
