On a horizontal table a billiard ball with velocity $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ collides elastically with a ball of equal mass at rest. After the collision the first ball moves in a direction that makes $60^\circ$ with the initial one. Treating the balls as particles and neglecting friction, what is the speed of the second ball?

- $4.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The masses are equal and the collision is elastic, so after the collision the velocities $\vec{v}_1'$ and $\vec{v}_2'$ are perpendicular and are the legs of a right triangle with hypotenuse $\vec{v}_1$. The leg $\vec{v}_1'$ makes the angle $\theta = 60^\circ$ with the hypotenuse, therefore $v_1' = v_1 \cos 60^\circ = 2.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and
$$
v_2' = v_1 \sin 60^\circ = 5.0 \cdot \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s} \approx 4.3\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The second ball leaves at $30^\circ$ from the initial direction, on the other side with respect to the first.
