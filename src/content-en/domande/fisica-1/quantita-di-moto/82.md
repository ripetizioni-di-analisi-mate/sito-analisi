Two bodies lie along a track. The first mass is at the origin and the second at positive coordinate $d$. The first mass is $2\,\mathrm{kg}$. Given the second mass $m = 6\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the distance $d = 8\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the center-of-mass coordinate $x$?

- $12\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The center-of-mass coordinate is a mass-weighted average. The first body contributes zero to the numerator. In the stated units, the relation is $x = \dfrac{d \cdot m}{m + 2}$. Substituting the given values yields $6\,\mathrm{m}$.
