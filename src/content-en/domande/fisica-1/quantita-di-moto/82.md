On a smooth horizontal plane a disk moving east hits a disk at rest. Right after the collision the first disk moves northeast. Which statement about the motion of the second disk after the collision is true?

- It moves east, because it was hit by a disk moving east
- It moves northeast, like the first disk
- Its velocity has a component directed south
- It moves north, perpendicular to the initial velocity of the first disk

## Explanation

Choose the $x$ axis pointing east and the $y$ axis pointing north. The external forces are vertical, so momentum in the plane is conserved, and in particular its $y$ component, which is zero before the collision. After the collision the first disk has a positive $y$ component of velocity, therefore the second disk must have a negative $y$ component that compensates it, with $m_1 v_{1y}' + m_2 v_{2y}' = 0$. So the velocity of the second disk has a component directed south, whatever the type of collision.
