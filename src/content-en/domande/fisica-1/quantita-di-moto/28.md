A cannon of mass $500\,\mathrm{kg}$, free to slide on frictionless horizontal rails, fires a shell of mass $5.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ with a velocity of $200\,\mathrm{m/s}$ relative to the ground, inclined at $60^\circ$ above the horizontal. What is the magnitude of the recoil velocity of the cannon?

- $1.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.7\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.50\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

During the shot the rails exert an impulsive vertical reaction on the cannon, so the vertical component of the momentum changes. The external forces, however, have no horizontal component, therefore the horizontal component is conserved, and before the shot it is zero. So $m v \cos 60^\circ + M V = 0$, and it follows that
$$
|V| = \frac{m v \cos 60^\circ}{M} = \frac{5.0 \cdot 200 \cdot 0.50}{500}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 1.0\,\mathrm{m/s} ,
$$
opposite to the horizontal component of the velocity of the shell.
