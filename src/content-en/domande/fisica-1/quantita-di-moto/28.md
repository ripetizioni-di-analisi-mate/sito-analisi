Only trolley A is tracked on a horizontal track. Its momentum changes from $+5$ to $+1\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$ while it interacts with B. In the same interval a track sensor measures an external impulse of $+3\,\mathrm{N\,s}$ on A and B together. What is B's momentum change?

- $+7\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$
- $+4\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$
- $-1\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$
- $-7\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$

## Explanation

For the whole system $\Delta p_A+\Delta p_B=+3\,\mathrm{N\,s}$. Since $\Delta p_A=1-5=-4$ in SI units, $\Delta p_B=3-(-4)=+7\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$.
