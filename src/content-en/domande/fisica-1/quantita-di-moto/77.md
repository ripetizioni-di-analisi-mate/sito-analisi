A ball sticks in a stationary block on a frictionless track. The block's mass is $3\,\mathrm{kg}$. The initial data are the initial speed $u = 12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the ball's mass $m = 1\,\mathrm{kg}$. Change the initial speed to $18\,\mathrm{m/s}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the ratio of the final value of $K$ to its initial value?

- $1$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $\dfrac{9}{4}$
- $\dfrac{4}{9}$

## Explanation

Momentum is conserved and the shared speed is $mu/(m+3)$. The final kinetic energy uses the combined mass $m+3$. The relation $K = \dfrac{m^{2} \cdot u^{2}}{2 \cdot \left(m + 3\right)}$ gives $18\,\mathrm{J}$ before the change and $\dfrac{81}{2}\,\mathrm{J}$ after it. The ratio is $\dfrac{9}{4}$.
