Two carts have combined mass $M$. A constant force parallel to the track is the only external horizontal force. Given the force magnitude $F = 18\,\mathrm{N}$ and the center-of-mass acceleration magnitude $a = 3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the total mass $M$?

- $9\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $3\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Internal forces cancel in the system balance. The center of mass accelerates according to $M a=F$. In the stated units, the relation is $a = \dfrac{F}{M}$. Solving for the unknown gives $M = \dfrac{F}{a}$. Substituting the given values yields $6\,\mathrm{kg}$.
