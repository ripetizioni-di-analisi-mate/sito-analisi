A steady water jet strikes a plate normally. The water leaves parallel to the plate, so its normal velocity component is lost. Given the mass flow rate $b = 3\,\mathrm{kg/s}$ and the normal force magnitude on the plate $F = 24\,\mathrm{N}$, what is the incident jet speed $v$?

- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The normal momentum flux falls from $bv$ to zero. The magnitude of the force on the plate equals the flux lost by the water. In the stated units, the relation is $F = b \cdot v$. Solving for the unknown gives $v = \dfrac{F}{b}$. Substituting the given values yields $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
