Three bodies move along the $x$ axis. The first has mass $2{,}0\,\mathrm{kg}$ and velocity $4{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$, the second mass $3{,}0\,\mathrm{kg}$ and velocity $-2{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$, the third mass $5{,}0\,\mathrm{kg}$ and velocity $1{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the velocity of the center of mass?

- $1{,}9\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0{,}70\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2{,}3\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The total momentum is the sum of the momenta, each with the sign of its velocity, so $P = (8{,}0 - 6{,}0 + 5{,}0)\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s} = 7{,}0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$. Since $P = M v_{cm}$ with $M = 10\,\mathrm{kg}$, one has $v_{cm} = \dfrac{7{,}0}{10}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 0{,}70\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The positive sign indicates that the center of mass moves in the positive direction of the $x$ axis.
