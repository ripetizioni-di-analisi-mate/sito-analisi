A cart hits a bumper and rebounds along the same line. Before impact its speed is $u$, and afterward its speed is $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The initial data are the mass $m = 2\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the initial speed $u = 7\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Change the initial speed to $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the ratio of the final value of $J$ to its initial value?

- $\dfrac{12}{7}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{9}{4}$

## Explanation

The rebound reverses the velocity. The momentum change has magnitude equal to the sum of the initial and final momentum magnitudes. The relation $J = m \cdot \left(u + 3\right)$ gives $20\,\mathrm{N\,s}$ before the change and $30\,\mathrm{N\,s}$ after it. The ratio is $\dfrac{3}{2}$.
