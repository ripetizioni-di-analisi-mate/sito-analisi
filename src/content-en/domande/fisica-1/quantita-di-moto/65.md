A stationary cart receives an impulse. The force-time graph is triangular, with the force always pointing in the same direction and returning to zero at the end. The initial data are the peak force $F = 60\,\mathrm{N}$, the duration of the impulse $t = \dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm{s}$ and the mass $m = 3\,\mathrm{kg}$. Change the peak force to $80\,\mathrm{N}$ and the mass to $4\,\mathrm{kg}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the final value of $v$ minus its initial value?

- $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The triangular area is $Ft/2$. The impulse equals the final momentum because the cart starts at rest. The relation $v = \dfrac{F \cdot t}{2 \cdot m}$ gives $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ before the change and $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ after it. Subtracting the initial value from the final value gives $0\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
