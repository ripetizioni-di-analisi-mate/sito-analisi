Which of the following collisions is perfectly inelastic?

- A billiard ball hits an identical ball at rest and stops
- A bullet passes through a wooden block and exits on the opposite side
- An arrow sticks into a straw target hanging from a rope
- A ball bounces on the floor and rises back to half its initial height

## Explanation

A collision is **perfectly inelastic** if after the collision the two bodies stay joined and move with the same velocity. The arrow stays stuck in the target, so after the collision the two bodies move together, and the collision is perfectly inelastic. The bullet and the block separate, therefore their collision is inelastic but not perfectly inelastic; the ball separates from the floor and rises again, with coefficient of restitution $e = \sqrt{\dfrac{1}{2}} \approx 0.71$. The exchange of velocities between two equal balls is the case of the elastic collision.
