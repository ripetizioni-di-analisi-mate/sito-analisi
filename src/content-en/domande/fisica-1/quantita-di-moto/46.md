Two carts move in the same direction. The faster first cart catches the second and sticks to it. The second cart's initial speed is $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. External impulse is negligible. Given the initial speed of the first cart $u = 8\,\mathrm{m/s}$, the mass of the first body $m_{1} = 2\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the mass of the second body $m_{2} = 4\,\mathrm{kg}$, what is the shared speed after impact $v$?

- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac{8}{3}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The initial momenta point in the same direction. After the collision the combined mass moves at speed $v$. In the stated units, the relation is $v = \dfrac{m_{1} \cdot u + 2 \cdot m_{2}}{m_{1} + m_{2}}$. Substituting the given values yields $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
