## Explanation

Positions along the rod are measured from the free end. The rod is uniform, so its center of mass is at the midpoint, at $0{,}60\,\mathrm{m}$, while the sphere has negligible size and is at $1{,}2\,\mathrm{m}$. By the rule for the **center of mass of a composite body** each part is treated as a particle placed at its center of mass, therefore
$$
d = \frac{2{,}0 \cdot 0{,}60 + 1{,}0 \cdot 1{,}2}{3{,}0}\,\mathrm{m} = 0{,}80\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The center of mass is therefore $0{,}40\,\mathrm{m}$ from the sphere.
