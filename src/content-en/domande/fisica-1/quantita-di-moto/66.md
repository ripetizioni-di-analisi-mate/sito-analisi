Two carts have combined mass $M$. A constant force parallel to the track is the only external horizontal force. Given the force magnitude $F = 18\,\mathrm{N}$ and the total mass $M = 6\,\mathrm{kg}$, what is the center-of-mass acceleration magnitude $a$?

- $6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Internal forces cancel in the system balance. The center of mass accelerates according to $M a=F$. In the stated units, the relation is $a = \dfrac{F}{M}$. Substituting the given values yields $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
