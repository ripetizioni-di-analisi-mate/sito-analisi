A clay ball is thrown horizontally against a cart at rest on smooth horizontal rails, sticks to it and the cart starts moving. Which quantity has the same value just before and just after the collision?

- The total momentum of the ball and the cart
- The total kinetic energy of the ball and the cart
- The velocity of the ball
- The kinetic energy of the ball

## Explanation

During the collision the forces between ball and cart are internal and the external forces are vertical, so the horizontal component of the **total momentum** is conserved. The ball sticks to the cart, therefore the collision is perfectly inelastic and the total kinetic energy decreases: part of it goes into the deformation of the clay and into heat. The ball, on the other hand, slows down, so its velocity and its kinetic energy change.
