On a smooth horizontal track a cart of $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ at $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ hits an identical cart at rest. The coefficient of restitution of the collision is $e = 0.50$. What is the velocity of the second cart after the collision?

- $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Conservation of momentum, divided by the common mass, and the definition of the coefficient of restitution give the conditions to be satisfied together
$$
\begin{cases}
v_1' + v_2' = 4.0\,\mathrm{m/s} \\[4pt]
v_2' - v_1' = e \cdot 4.0\,\mathrm{m/s} = 2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}
\end{cases}
$$
Adding the two equations one obtains $2 v_2' = 6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$, so $v_2' = 3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_1' = 1.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. With $e = 1$ the carts would exchange their velocities, and with $e = 0$ they would move on together at $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
