A cart hits a bumper and rebounds along the same line. Before impact its speed is $u$, and afterward its speed is $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Given the mass $m = 2\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the initial speed $u = 7\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the magnitude of the impulse $J$?

- $20\,\mathrm{N\,s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{N\,s}$
- $14\,\mathrm{N\,s}$
- $40\,\mathrm{N\,s}$

## Explanation

The rebound reverses the velocity. The momentum change has magnitude equal to the sum of the initial and final momentum magnitudes. In the stated units, the relation is $J = m \cdot \left(u + 3\right)$. Substituting the given values yields $20\,\mathrm{N\,s}$.
