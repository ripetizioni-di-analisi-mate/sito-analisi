A steady water jet strikes a plate normally. The water leaves parallel to the plate, so its normal velocity component is lost. Given the mass flow rate $b = 3\,\mathrm{kg/s}$ and the incident jet speed $v = 8\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the normal force magnitude on the plate $F$?

- $48\,\mathrm{N}$
- $36\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$
- $24\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The normal momentum flux falls from $bv$ to zero. The magnitude of the force on the plate equals the flux lost by the water. In the stated units, the relation is $F = b \cdot v$. Substituting the given values yields $24\,\mathrm{N}$.
