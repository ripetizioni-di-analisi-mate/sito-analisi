Along the $x$ axis a body of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ moves with velocity $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and a body of mass $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ is at rest. What is the velocity $v_2^{*}$ of the $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ body in the center-of-mass frame?

- $v_2^{*} = 2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $v_2^{*} = -2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $v_2^{*} = -1.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $v_2^{*} = 0$

## Explanation

The velocity of the center of mass is $v_{cm} = \dfrac{2.0 \cdot 3.0 + 1.0 \cdot 0}{3.0}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. By the Galilean transformations the velocity in the center-of-mass frame is $v_i^{*} = v_i - v_{cm}$, so $v_2^{*} = (0 - 2.0)\,\mathrm{m/s} = -2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. In the same way $v_1^{*} = 1.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and the total momentum in the center-of-mass frame is zero, since $2.0 \cdot 1.0 + 1.0 \cdot (-2.0) = 0$.
