On a smooth straight horizontal track a cart $A$ of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ with velocity $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ hits a cart $B$ at rest, of equal mass, located in front of it. Which pair of velocities $(v_A',\ v_B')$ right after the collision is physically possible, with velocities positive in the initial direction of $A$?

- $(3.0,\ 1.0)\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $(1.0,\ 3.0)\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $(-1.0,\ 5.0)\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $(1.0,\ 2.0)\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The external forces are vertical, so momentum is conserved and, since the masses are equal, the sum of the velocities remains $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The pair $(1.0,\ 2.0)\,\mathrm{m/s}$ has sum $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$, therefore it is not possible. The other three pairs conserve momentum, and for each one we compute the **coefficient of restitution** $e = -\dfrac{v_B' - v_A'}{0 - 4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}}$, which must lie between $0$ and $1$. For $(3.0,\ 1.0)\,\mathrm{m/s}$ we have $e = -0.50$, so $A$ would pass through $B$, which is in front of it. For $(-1.0,\ 5.0)\,\mathrm{m/s}$ we have $e = 1.5$, so the kinetic energy would increase from $16\,\mathrm{J}$ to $26\,\mathrm{J}$. For $(1.0,\ 3.0)\,\mathrm{m/s}$ we have $e = 0.50$, therefore this is the only possible pair. It corresponds to an inelastic collision in which the kinetic energy goes from $16\,\mathrm{J}$ to $10\,\mathrm{J}$.
