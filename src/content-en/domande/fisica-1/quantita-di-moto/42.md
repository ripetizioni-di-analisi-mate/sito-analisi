On a smooth horizontal track a cart of $3{,}0\,\mathrm{kg}$ at $4{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ hits a cart at rest of $1{,}0\,\mathrm{kg}$. What is the minimum possible value of the total kinetic energy just after the collision, whatever the type of collision?

- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $6{,}0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $24\,\mathrm{J}$
- $18\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The external forces are not impulsive, so the collision leaves the velocity of the center of mass unchanged, $v_{cm} = \dfrac{3{,}0 \cdot 4{,}0}{4{,}0}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 3{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. By **König's theorem** $K = \dfrac{1}{2}\,M v_{cm}^2 + K^{*}$, and the first term is $\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 4{,}0 \cdot 3{,}0^2\,\mathrm{J} = 18\,\mathrm{J}$ both before and after the collision. The collision can only reduce $K^{*}$, at most down to zero, and this happens in the perfectly inelastic collision. Therefore the kinetic energy after the collision is at least $18\,\mathrm{J}$, out of the initial $24\,\mathrm{J}$.
