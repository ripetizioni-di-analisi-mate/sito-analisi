A steady water jet strikes a plate normally. The water leaves parallel to the plate, so its normal velocity component is lost. Given the incident jet speed $v = 8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the normal force magnitude on the plate $F = 24\,\mathrm{N}$, what is the mass flow rate $b$?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{kg/s}$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}\,\mathrm{kg/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{kg/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kg/s}$

## Explanation

The normal momentum flux falls from $bv$ to zero. The magnitude of the force on the plate equals the flux lost by the water. In the stated units, the relation is $F = b \cdot v$. Solving for the unknown gives $b = \dfrac{F}{v}$. Substituting the given values yields $3\,\mathrm{kg/s}$.
