Two small spheres of masses $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $6.0\,\mathrm{kg}$, of negligible size, are fixed at the ends of a rigid rod $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$ long and of negligible mass. At what distance from the $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ sphere is the center of mass?

- $0.20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.40\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.60\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.27\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The origin of the $x$ axis is placed at the $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ sphere, so the other sphere is at $x = 0.80\,\mathrm{m}$. The mass of the rod is negligible, therefore
$$
x_{cm} = \frac{2.0 \cdot 0 + 6.0 \cdot 0.80}{8.0}\,\mathrm{m} = 0.60\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The center of mass divides the rod into two parts, of $0.60\,\mathrm{m}$ and $0.20\,\mathrm{m}$, inversely proportional to the masses, and it is closer to the heavier sphere.
