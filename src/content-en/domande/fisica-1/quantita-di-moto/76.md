A ball sticks in a stationary block on a frictionless track. The block's mass is $3\,\mathrm{kg}$. Given the initial speed $u = 12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the kinetic energy of the system after impact $K = 18\,\mathrm{J}$, what is the ball's mass $m$?

- $3\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $2\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $1\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Momentum is conserved and the shared speed is $mu/(m+3)$. The final kinetic energy uses the combined mass $m+3$. In the stated units, the relation is $K = \dfrac{m^{2} \cdot u^{2}}{2 \cdot \left(m + 3\right)}$. Solving for the unknown gives $m = \dfrac{\sqrt{K} \cdot \sqrt{K + 6 \cdot u^{2}} + K}{u^{2}}$. Substituting the given values yields $1\,\mathrm{kg}$.
