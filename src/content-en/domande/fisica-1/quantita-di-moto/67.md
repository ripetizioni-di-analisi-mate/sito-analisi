On a smooth horizontal track two carts move in the same direction; cart 1 catches up with cart 2, which has mass $3.0\,\mathrm{kg}$, and the two remain coupled. The graph shows their velocities: before the collision $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$, after the collision $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the mass of cart 1?

::grafico{linee="0,6 1,6;0,1.5 1,1.5;1,3 3,3" segmenti="1,0,1,6" etichette="0.1,6.5,cart 1;0.1,2.0,cart 2;1.6,3.5,joined carts" xmin=-0.3 xmax=3.4 ymin=-0.6 ymax=7.2 nomi="t,v" titolo="Velocities of the two carts (in $\mathrm{m/s}$) as functions of time (in seconds); the collision occurs at time $t = 1\,\mathrm{s}$"}

- $3.0\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.75\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $6.0\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

On the smooth track momentum is conserved, so the momentum lost by cart 1 equals the momentum gained by cart 2:
$$
m_1\,(6.0 - 3.0) = 3.0 \cdot (3.0 - 1.5)
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
m_1 = \frac{4.5}{3.0}\,\mathrm{kg} = 1.5\,\mathrm{kg} .
$$
The value $3.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ would be obtained by neglecting the initial velocity of cart 2, which instead contributes to the momentum before the collision.
