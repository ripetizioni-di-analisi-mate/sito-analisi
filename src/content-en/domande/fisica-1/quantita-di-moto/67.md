Two carts have combined mass $M$. A constant force parallel to the track is the only external horizontal force. Given the total mass $M = 6\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the center-of-mass acceleration magnitude $a = 3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the force magnitude $F$?

- $18\,\mathrm{N}$
- $9\,\mathrm{N}$
- $27\,\mathrm{N}$
- $36\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Internal forces cancel in the system balance. The center of mass accelerates according to $M a=F$. In the stated units, the relation is $a = \dfrac{F}{M}$. Solving for the unknown gives $F = M \cdot a$. Substituting the given values yields $18\,\mathrm{N}$.
