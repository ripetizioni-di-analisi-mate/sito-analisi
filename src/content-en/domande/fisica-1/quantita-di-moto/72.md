A cart collides elastically in one dimension with a stationary cart of three times its mass. The first cart reverses direction. The initial data are the first cart's mass $m = 4\,\mathrm{kg}$ and its initial speed $u = 8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Change its initial speed to $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the ratio of the final value of $p$ to its initial value?

- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $1$
- $3$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$

## Explanation

In a one-dimensional elastic collision with a stationary target, the first cart leaves with velocity $(m-3m)u/(m+3m)=-u/2$. The relation $p = \dfrac{m \cdot u}{2}$ gives $16\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$ before the change and $24\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$ after it. The ratio is $\dfrac{3}{2}$.
