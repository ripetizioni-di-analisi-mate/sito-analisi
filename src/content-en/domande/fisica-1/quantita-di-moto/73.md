A cart collides elastically in one dimension with a stationary cart of three times its mass. The first cart reverses direction. The initial data are the first cart's mass $m = 4\,\mathrm{kg}$ and its initial speed $u = 8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Change the first cart's mass to $6\,\mathrm{kg}$ and its initial speed to $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the final value of $p$ minus its initial value?

- $-8\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$

## Explanation

In a one-dimensional elastic collision with a stationary target, the first cart leaves with velocity $(m-3m)u/(m+3m)=-u/2$. The relation $p = \dfrac{m \cdot u}{2}$ gives $16\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$ before the change and $12\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$ after it. Subtracting the initial value from the final value gives $-4\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$.
