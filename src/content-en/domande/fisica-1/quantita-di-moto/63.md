A package of mass $4.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ slides at $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ on a smooth floor and moves onto a cart at rest of mass $8.0\,\mathrm{kg}$, free to move on the floor without friction. Because of friction between the package and the bed of the cart, after a short distance the package is at rest relative to the cart. What is the magnitude of the impulse received by the cart?

- $12\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$
- $8.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$
- $24\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$

## Explanation

The friction between the package and the cart is an internal force of the system of the two bodies, and the external forces are vertical, so the horizontal component of momentum is conserved. At the end the two bodies move together, as after a **perfectly inelastic collision**, therefore
$$
V = \frac{4.0 \cdot 3.0}{4.0 + 8.0}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 1.0\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The cart starts from rest, so by the **impulse-momentum theorem** the impulse it receives equals its final momentum, $8.0 \cdot 1.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot s} = 8.0\,\mathrm{N\cdot s}$, in the direction of motion of the package. The package receives the opposite impulse; indeed its momentum goes from $12\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$ to $4.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$.
