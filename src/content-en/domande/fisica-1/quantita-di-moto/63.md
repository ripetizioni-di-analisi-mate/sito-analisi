A stationary cart receives an impulse. The force-time graph is triangular, with the force always pointing in the same direction and returning to zero at the end. Given the peak force $F = 60\,\mathrm{N}$, the mass $m = 3\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the final speed $v = 4\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the duration of the impulse $t$?

- $\dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $\dfrac{4}{5}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The triangular area is $Ft/2$. The impulse equals the final momentum because the cart starts at rest. In the stated units, the relation is $v = \dfrac{F \cdot t}{2 \cdot m}$. Solving for the unknown gives $t = \dfrac{2 \cdot m \cdot v}{F}$. Substituting the given values yields $\dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm{s}$.
