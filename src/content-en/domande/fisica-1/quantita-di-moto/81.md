Two skates approach from perpendicular directions and stick together. Each has mass $2\,\mathrm{kg}$. Neglect horizontal external impulse. Given the first initial speed $a_{1} = 6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the final speed $v = 5\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the second initial speed $a_{2}$?

- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The initial momenta add as vectors. Divide the magnitude of their resultant by the total mass. In the stated units, the relation is $v = \dfrac{\sqrt{a_{1}^{2} + a_{2}^{2}}}{2}$. Solving for the unknown gives $a_{2} = \sqrt{- a_{1}^{2} + 4 \cdot v^{2}}$. Substituting the given values yields $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
