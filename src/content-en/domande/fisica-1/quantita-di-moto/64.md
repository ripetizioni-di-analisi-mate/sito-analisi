A stationary cart receives an impulse. The force-time graph is triangular, with the force always pointing in the same direction and returning to zero at the end. The initial data are the peak force $F = 60\,\mathrm{N}$, the duration of the impulse $t = \dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm{s}$ and the mass $m = 3\,\mathrm{kg}$. Change the duration of the impulse to $\dfrac{4}{5}\,\mathrm{s}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the ratio of the final value of $v$ to its initial value?

- $1$
- $4$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $2$

## Explanation

The triangular area is $Ft/2$. The impulse equals the final momentum because the cart starts at rest. The relation $v = \dfrac{F \cdot t}{2 \cdot m}$ gives $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ before the change and $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ after it. The ratio is $2$.
