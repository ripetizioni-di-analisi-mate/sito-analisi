A stationary cart receives an impulse. The force-time graph is triangular, with the force always pointing in the same direction and returning to zero at the end. Given the peak force $F = 60\,\mathrm{N}$, the duration of the impulse $t = \dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm{s}$ and the mass $m = 3\,\mathrm{kg}$, what is the final speed $v$?

- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The triangular area is $Ft/2$. The impulse equals the final momentum because the cart starts at rest. In the stated units, the relation is $v = \dfrac{F \cdot t}{2 \cdot m}$. Substituting the given values yields $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
