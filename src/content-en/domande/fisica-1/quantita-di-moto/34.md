Along a smooth horizontal straight track, oriented as the $x$ axis, a body of mass $2{,}0\,\mathrm{kg}$ with velocity $3{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ collides elastically with a body of mass $4{,}0\,\mathrm{kg}$ with velocity $-1{,}5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the velocity $v_2'$ of the $4{,}0\,\mathrm{kg}$ body after the collision?

- $v_2' = -1{,}5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $v_2' = 3{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $v_2' = 0$
- $v_2' = 1{,}5\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The total momentum is $(2{,}0 \cdot 3{,}0 - 4{,}0 \cdot 1{,}5)\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s} = 0$, so the center of mass is at rest. By the formula of the **elastic collision**
$$
v_2' = \frac{(m_2 - m_1)\,v_2 + 2 m_1 v_1}{m_1 + m_2} = \frac{2{,}0 \cdot (-1{,}5) + 2 \cdot 2{,}0 \cdot 3{,}0}{6{,}0}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 1{,}5\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The center of mass is at rest, therefore each body reverses its velocity without changing its magnitude, and also $v_1' = -3{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
