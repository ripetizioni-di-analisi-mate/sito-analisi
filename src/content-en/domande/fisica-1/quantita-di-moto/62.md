A stationary cart receives an impulse. The force-time graph is triangular, with the force always pointing in the same direction and returning to zero at the end. Given the duration of the impulse $t = \dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm{s}$, the mass $m = 3\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the final speed $v = 4\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the peak force $F$?

- $30\,\mathrm{N}$
- $60\,\mathrm{N}$
- $120\,\mathrm{N}$
- $90\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The triangular area is $Ft/2$. The impulse equals the final momentum because the cart starts at rest. In the stated units, the relation is $v = \dfrac{F \cdot t}{2 \cdot m}$. Solving for the unknown gives $F = \dfrac{2 \cdot m \cdot v}{t}$. Substituting the given values yields $60\,\mathrm{N}$.
