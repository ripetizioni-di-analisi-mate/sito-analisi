Two carts on a smooth horizontal track, oriented like the $x$ axis, move toward each other: the components of their momenta are $p_1 = 6.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$ and $p_2 = -4.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$. After the collision the first cart has $p_1' = 1.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$. What is $p_2'$?

- $1.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $-1.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $9.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $-9.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$

## Explanation

On the smooth track the external forces are vertical, so the $x$ component of the total momentum is conserved. Before the collision it equals $6.0 - 4.0 = 2.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$, therefore
$$
p_2' = 2.0 - p_1' = 2.0 - 1.0 = 1.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s} .
$$
The value is positive, so after the collision the second cart also moves in the positive $x$ direction.
