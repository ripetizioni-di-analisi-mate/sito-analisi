A bullet of mass $5{,}0\,\mathrm{g}$, with horizontal velocity $400\,\mathrm{m/s}$, embeds itself in a block of mass $1{,}0\,\mathrm{kg}$ at rest, hanging from inextensible strings of negligible mass. Neglecting air resistance, up to what maximum height does the block with the bullet rise?

- $41\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0{,}40\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0{,}10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0{,}20\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

During the collision the strings are vertical, so the horizontal component of the momentum is conserved and the velocity just after the collision is
$$
V = \frac{m v}{m + M} = \frac{0{,}0050 \cdot 400}{1{,}005}\,\mathrm{m/s} \approx 1{,}99\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The collision is perfectly inelastic, therefore in this phase mechanical energy is not conserved. After the collision the only force that does work is the weight, so mechanical energy is conserved and $\dfrac{1}{2}\,(m + M)\,V^2 = (m + M)\,g h$. It follows that $h = \dfrac{V^2}{2g} = \dfrac{1{,}99^2}{2 \cdot 9{,}81}\,\mathrm{m} \approx 0{,}20\,\mathrm{m}$.
