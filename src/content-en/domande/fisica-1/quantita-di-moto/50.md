A projectile sticks in a block that then rises along a frictionless ramp. External impulse during impact is negligible. Which quantity is conserved during impact and which during the climb?

- Mechanical energy in both stages
- Momentum during impact, mechanical energy during the climb
- Momentum in both stages
- Mechanical energy during impact, momentum during the climb

## Explanation

Momentum is conserved in the inelastic collision because external impulse is negligible. During the climb gravity is conservative and the track's normal force does no work.
