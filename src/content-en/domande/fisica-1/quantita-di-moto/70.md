Two balls of equal mass move along the same line, oriented like the $x$ axis, with velocities $v_1 = 2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_2 = -3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and collide elastically. What is the velocity $v_1'$ of the first ball after the collision?

- $v_1' = -2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $v_1' = 3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $v_1' = -3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $v_1' = -0.50\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

For the **one-dimensional elastic collision** with $m_1 = m_2$ the formulas for the final velocities reduce to
$$
v_1' = \frac{(m_1 - m_2)\,v_1 + 2 m_2 v_2}{m_1 + m_2} = v_2,
\qquad
v_2' = v_1 .
$$
So the two balls exchange their velocities, and $v_1' = -3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The exchange also holds when both balls are moving, and not only when the target is at rest.
