A cart collides elastically in one dimension with a stationary cart of three times its mass. The first cart reverses direction. Given its initial speed $u = 8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the magnitude of its final momentum $p = 16\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$, what is the first cart's mass $m$?

- $6\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $2\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

In a one-dimensional elastic collision with a stationary target, the first cart leaves with velocity $(m-3m)u/(m+3m)=-u/2$. In the stated units, the relation is $p = \dfrac{m \cdot u}{2}$. Solving for the unknown gives $m = \dfrac{2 \cdot p}{u}$. Substituting the given values yields $4\,\mathrm{kg}$.
