The momentum of a body moving along a straight line is $p(t) = 3{,}0\,t^2 + 2{,}0\,t$, with $p$ in $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$ and $t$ in seconds. What is the net force on the body at the instant $t = 2{,}0\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $14\,\mathrm{N}$
- $8{,}0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$
- $16\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

By **Newton's second law in terms of momentum**, the net force is the derivative of $p$ with respect to time, $F(t) = \dfrac{dp}{dt} = 6{,}0\,t + 2{,}0$. At the instant $t = 2{,}0\,\mathrm{s}$ one therefore obtains $F = (12 + 2{,}0)\,\mathrm{N} = 14\,\mathrm{N}$. The ratio $\dfrac{p(2{,}0\,\mathrm{s}) - p(0)}{2{,}0\,\mathrm{s}} = 8{,}0\,\mathrm{N}$ is instead the average force between $0$ and $2{,}0\,\mathrm{s}$.
