## Explanation

The total mass is $M = 10\,\mathrm{kg}$. Each coordinate of the center of mass is the mean of the coordinates of the points weighted by the masses, so
$$
x_{cm} = \frac{2{,}0 \cdot 1{,}0 + 3{,}0 \cdot 0 + 5{,}0 \cdot 4{,}0}{10}\,\mathrm{m} = 2{,}2\,\mathrm{m},
\qquad
y_{cm} = \frac{2{,}0 \cdot 0 + 3{,}0 \cdot 2{,}0 + 5{,}0 \cdot 4{,}0}{10}\,\mathrm{m} = 2{,}6\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The center of mass is closer to the point $(4{,}0;\ 4{,}0)$, which has the largest mass.
