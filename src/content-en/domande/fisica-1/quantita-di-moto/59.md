Two skaters initially at rest push apart on frictionless ice. The first skater has mass $50\,\mathrm{kg}$. The initial data are the first skater's speed $u = 3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the second skater's mass $m = 75\,\mathrm{kg}$. Change the second skater's mass to $60\,\mathrm{kg}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the ratio of the final value of $v$ to its initial value?

- $\dfrac{5}{4}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{25}{22}$
- $\dfrac{4}{5}$

## Explanation

Total momentum remains zero. The final momenta are therefore equal in magnitude and opposite in direction. The relation $v = \dfrac{50 \cdot u}{m}$ gives $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ before the change and $\dfrac{5}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s}$ after it. The ratio is $\dfrac{5}{4}$.
