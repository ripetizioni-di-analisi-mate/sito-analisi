A cart hits a bumper and rebounds along the same line. Before impact its speed is $u$, and afterward its speed is $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Given the initial speed $u = 7\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the magnitude of the impulse $J = 20\,\mathrm{N\,s}$, what is the mass $m$?

- $\dfrac{20}{7}\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $5\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $2\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The rebound reverses the velocity. The momentum change has magnitude equal to the sum of the initial and final momentum magnitudes. In the stated units, the relation is $J = m \cdot \left(u + 3\right)$. Solving for the unknown gives $m = \dfrac{J}{u + 3}$. Substituting the given values yields $2\,\mathrm{kg}$.
