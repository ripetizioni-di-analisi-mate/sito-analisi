A boy stands on a cart holding a $10\,\mathrm{kg}$ sack; the mass of the boy and the cart together is $50\,\mathrm{kg}$. The system slides on a smooth horizontal plane at $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The boy throws the sack forward horizontally, and right after the throw the sack has velocity $8.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ relative to the ground. What is the velocity of the cart with the boy right after the throw?

- $1.7\,\mathrm{m/s}$ forward
- $3.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ forward
- $5.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ forward
- $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ forward

## Explanation

The forces of the throw are internal and the external forces are vertical, so the horizontal component of the momentum of the system formed by the boy, the cart and the sack is conserved. Before the throw the momentum is $60 \cdot 3.0\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s} = 180\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$, therefore
$$
180 = 10 \cdot 8.0 + 50\,V
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
V = \frac{180 - 80}{50}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 2.0\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The cart slows down but keeps moving forward, because the sack carries away part of the momentum while the total does not change.
