Two skaters initially at rest push apart on frictionless ice. The first skater has mass $50\,\mathrm{kg}$. Given the first skater's speed $u = 3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the second skater's mass $m = 75\,\mathrm{kg}$, what is the second skater's speed $v$?

- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac{6}{5}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Total momentum remains zero. The final momenta are therefore equal in magnitude and opposite in direction. In the stated units, the relation is $v = \dfrac{50 \cdot u}{m}$. Substituting the given values yields $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
