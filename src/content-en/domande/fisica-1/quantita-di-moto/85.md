A small rocket exhausts gas in one direction at constant relative speed. Neglect weight and drag in calculating thrust. Given the exhaust speed relative to the rocket $u = 300\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the expelled mass per second $b = \dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{kg/s}$, what is the thrust magnitude $F$?

- $30\,\mathrm{N}$
- $120\,\mathrm{N}$
- $60\,\mathrm{N}$
- $90\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Thrust is the magnitude of momentum transferred to the gas per unit time in the instantaneous rocket frame. In the stated units, the relation is $F = b \cdot u$. Substituting the given values yields $60\,\mathrm{N}$.
