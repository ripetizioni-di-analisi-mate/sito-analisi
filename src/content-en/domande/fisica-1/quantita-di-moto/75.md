A ball sticks in a stationary block on a frictionless track. The block's mass is $3\,\mathrm{kg}$. Given the ball's mass $m = 1\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the system kinetic energy after impact $K = 18\,\mathrm{J}$, what is the initial speed $u$?

- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $24\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Momentum is conserved and the shared speed is $mu/(m+3)$. The final kinetic energy uses the combined mass $m+3$. In the stated units, the relation is $K = \dfrac{m^{2} \cdot u^{2}}{2 \cdot \left(m + 3\right)}$. Solving for the unknown gives $u = \dfrac{\sqrt{2} \cdot \sqrt{K} \cdot \sqrt{m + 3}}{m}$. Substituting the given values yields $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
