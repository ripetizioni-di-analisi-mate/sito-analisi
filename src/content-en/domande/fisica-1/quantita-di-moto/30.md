A block slides down the inclined face of a wedge, which rests on a horizontal floor and is free to slide. All surfaces are smooth. For the system formed by the block and the wedge, which statement is true during the descent?

- The total momentum is conserved
- Only the horizontal component of the total momentum is conserved
- The momentum of the block is conserved
- No component of the total momentum is conserved

## Explanation

The external forces on the system are the weights of the block and of the wedge and the normal force of the floor, all vertical, while the forces between block and wedge are internal. The resultant of the external forces therefore has zero horizontal component, and by the equation of motion for total momentum the horizontal component of the total momentum is conserved. The vertical component changes, because the block acquires a downward velocity while the wedge stays on the floor. The momentum of the block alone changes also horizontally, because the wedge exerts on it a normal force with a horizontal component.
