On a smooth horizontal plane a disk collides elastically with a disk of equal mass at rest; the disks are treated as particles. After the collision both move, and the first disk is deflected by $30^\circ$ with respect to the initial direction. In which direction does the second disk leave?

- At $30^\circ$ from the initial direction, on the other side with respect to the first disk
- At $60^\circ$ from the initial direction, on the same side as the first disk
- At $60^\circ$ from the initial direction, on the other side with respect to the first disk
- Along the initial direction

## Explanation

By the theorem on the **oblique elastic collision between equal masses**, with the target at rest and with both bodies moving after the collision, the final velocities are perpendicular. So the two final directions make an angle of $90^\circ$, and the second disk makes the angle $90^\circ - 30^\circ = 60^\circ$ with the initial direction. The component of the momentum perpendicular to the initial direction is zero before the collision and is conserved, therefore the second disk leaves on the other side with respect to the first.
