Two skaters initially at rest push apart on frictionless ice. The first skater has mass $50\,\mathrm{kg}$. Given the second skater's mass $m = 75\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the second skater's speed $v = 2\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the first skater's speed $u$?

- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Total momentum remains zero. The final momenta are therefore equal in magnitude and opposite in direction. In the stated units, the relation is $v = \dfrac{50 \cdot u}{m}$. Solving for the unknown gives $u = \dfrac{m \cdot v}{50}$. Substituting the given values yields $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
