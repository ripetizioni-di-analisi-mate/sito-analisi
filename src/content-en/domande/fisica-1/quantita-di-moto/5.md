- $22\,\mathrm{m/s}$ opposite to the bullet

## Explanation

Before the shot the rifle and the bullet are at rest, and the forces of the gases are internal to the system; the external forces are vertical. Therefore the horizontal component of the momentum is conserved and stays zero, that is, $m v + M V = 0$. It follows that
$$
V = -\frac{m}{M}\,v = -\frac{0{,}0070}{3{,}5} \cdot 500\,\mathrm{m/s} = -1{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The minus sign indicates that the rifle **recoils** in the direction opposite to the bullet.
