An open railcar of mass $4{,}0 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg}$ slides without friction on a horizontal track at $3{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. It collects $1{,}0 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water from rain falling vertically. What is the final speed of the railcar?

- $3{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2{,}7\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2{,}4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3{,}8\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The system formed by the railcar and the collected water is subject to vertical external forces, namely the weights and the reaction of the track. So the horizontal component of the momentum is conserved. The water falls vertically, therefore before entering the railcar the horizontal component of its velocity is zero, and
$$
4{,}0 \cdot 10^{3} \cdot 3{,}0 = 5{,}0 \cdot 10^{3}\,V,
\qquad
V = 2{,}4\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The speed decreases because the same horizontal momentum belongs to a larger mass.
