Two skates approach from perpendicular directions and stick together. Each has mass $2\,\mathrm{kg}$. Neglect horizontal external impulse. Given the second initial speed $a_{2} = 8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the final speed $v = 5\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the first initial speed $a_{1}$?

- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The initial momenta add as vectors. Divide the magnitude of their resultant by the total mass. In the stated units, the relation is $v = \dfrac{\sqrt{a_{1}^{2} + a_{2}^{2}}}{2}$. Solving for the unknown gives $a_{1} = \sqrt{- a_{2}^{2} + 4 \cdot v^{2}}$. Substituting the given values yields $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
