A car of mass $1{,}0 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg}$ travels at $72\,\mathrm{km/h}$. What is the magnitude of its momentum?

- $7{,}2 \cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $2{,}0 \cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $2{,}6 \cdot 10^{5}\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$
- $1{,}0 \cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$

## Explanation

The velocity must be expressed in meters per second. Since $1\,\mathrm{km/h} = \dfrac{1000\,\mathrm{m}}{3600\,\mathrm{s}}$, one divides by $3{,}6$ and obtains $v = \dfrac{72}{3{,}6}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 20\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The magnitude of the momentum is the product of the mass and the speed, so $p = 1{,}0 \cdot 10^{3} \cdot 20\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s} = 2{,}0 \cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$.
