A cart hits a bumper and rebounds along the same line. Before impact its speed is $u$, and afterward its speed is $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Given the mass $m = 2\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the magnitude of the impulse $J = 20\,\mathrm{N\,s}$, what is the initial speed $u$?

- $14\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac{7}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The rebound reverses the velocity. The momentum change has magnitude equal to the sum of the initial and final momentum magnitudes. In the stated units, the relation is $J = m \cdot \left(u + 3\right)$. Solving for the unknown gives $u = \dfrac{J}{m} - 3$. Substituting the given values yields $7\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
