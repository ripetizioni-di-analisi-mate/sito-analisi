A uranium-238 nucleus at rest decays into a thorium-234 nucleus and an alpha particle, the helium-4 nucleus. Approximating the masses by the mass numbers, $234\,u$ and $4\,u$, what fraction of the kinetic energy released in the decay goes to the alpha particle?

- $0.017$
- $0.50$
- $0.98$
- $0.13$

## Explanation

The forces of the decay are internal and the nucleus is at rest, so after the decay the total momentum is zero and the two products have momenta of equal magnitude $p$ and opposite directions. The kinetic energy can be written as $K = \dfrac{p^2}{2m}$, therefore for the same $p$ it is inversely proportional to the mass:
$$
\frac{K_\alpha}{K_\alpha + K_{Th}} = \frac{\dfrac{1}{4}}{\dfrac{1}{4} + \dfrac{1}{234}} = \frac{234}{238} \approx 0.98 .
$$
Almost all the energy goes to the lighter particle, while the thorium nucleus recoils slowly.
