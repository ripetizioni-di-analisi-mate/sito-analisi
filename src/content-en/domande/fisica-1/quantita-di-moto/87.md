A small rocket exhausts gas in one direction at constant relative speed. Neglect weight and drag in calculating thrust. Given the exhaust speed relative to the rocket $u = 300\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the thrust magnitude $F = 60\,\mathrm{N}$, what is the expelled mass per second $b$?

- $\dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{kg/s}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm{kg/s}$
- $\dfrac{1}{10}\,\mathrm{kg/s}$
- $\dfrac{3}{10}\,\mathrm{kg/s}$

## Explanation

Thrust is the magnitude of momentum transferred to the gas per unit time in the instantaneous rocket frame. In the stated units, the relation is $F = b \cdot u$. Solving for the unknown gives $b = \dfrac{F}{u}$. Substituting the given values yields $\dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{kg/s}$.
