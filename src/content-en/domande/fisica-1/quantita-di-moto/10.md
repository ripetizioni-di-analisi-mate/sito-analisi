A small ball is dropped from rest from a height of $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ above a rigid floor and, after the bounce, rises up to $1.28\,\mathrm{m}$. Neglecting air resistance, what is the coefficient of restitution of the collision with the floor?

- $0.80$
- $0.64$
- $0.36$
- $1.3$

## Explanation

During the descent and the rise the only force is the weight, so mechanical energy is conserved. Therefore the ball reaches the floor with speed $v = \sqrt{2 g h}$ and leaves it with speed $v' = \sqrt{2 g h'}$. The floor is at rest, so the coefficient of restitution is the ratio of the speeds:
$$
e = \frac{v'}{v} = \sqrt{\frac{h'}{h}} = \sqrt{\frac{1.28}{2.0}} = \sqrt{0.64} = 0.80 .
$$
The value $0.64 = e^2$ is the ratio of the kinetic energy after the collision to the kinetic energy before it.
