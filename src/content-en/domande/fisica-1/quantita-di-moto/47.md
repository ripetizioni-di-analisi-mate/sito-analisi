A machine gun held still fires $10$ bullets per second; each bullet has mass $20\,\mathrm{g}$ and leaves with speed $300\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the magnitude of the average force exerted by the bullets on the gun?

- $6.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $6.0 \cdot 10^{2}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $60\,\mathrm{N}$
- $30\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

In an interval $\Delta t = 1.0\,\mathrm{s}$ the gun fires $10$ bullets, and each goes from rest to speed $v$; so the momentum given to the bullets in one second is
$$
\Delta p = 10 \cdot 0.020 \cdot 300\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s} = 60\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s} .
$$
By the **impulse-momentum theorem** the average force of the gun on the bullets is $F_m = \dfrac{\Delta p}{\Delta t} = 60\,\mathrm{N}$. By the third law the bullets exert on the gun an average force of the same magnitude, pointing backward, which the person holding it must balance.
