Two carts move in the same direction. The faster first cart catches the second and sticks to it. The second cart's initial speed is $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. External impulse is negligible. The initial data are the initial speed of the first cart $u = 8\,\mathrm{m/s}$, the mass of the first body $m_{1} = 2\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the mass of the second body $m_{2} = 4\,\mathrm{kg}$. Change the initial speed of the first cart to $14\,\mathrm{m/s}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the ratio of the final value of $v$ to its initial value?

- $\dfrac{7}{4}$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

The initial momenta point in the same direction. After the collision the combined mass moves at speed $v$. The relation $v = \dfrac{m_{1} \cdot u + 2 \cdot m_{2}}{m_{1} + m_{2}}$ gives $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ before the change and $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ after it. The ratio is $\dfrac{3}{2}$.
