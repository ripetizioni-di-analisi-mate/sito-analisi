A ball sticks in a stationary block on a frictionless track. The block's mass is $3\,\mathrm{kg}$. Given the initial speed $u = 12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the ball's mass $m = 1\,\mathrm{kg}$, what is the kinetic energy of the system after impact $K$?

- $72\,\mathrm{J}$
- $18\,\mathrm{J}$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $36\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Momentum is conserved and the shared speed is $mu/(m+3)$. The final kinetic energy uses the combined mass $m+3$. In the stated units, the relation is $K = \dfrac{m^{2} \cdot u^{2}}{2 \cdot \left(m + 3\right)}$. Substituting the given values yields $18\,\mathrm{J}$.
