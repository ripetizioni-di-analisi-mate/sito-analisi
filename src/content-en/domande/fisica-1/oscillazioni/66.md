A physical pendulum is a rigid body with moment of inertia $I$ about its pivot. It has mass $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ and swings through small angles. Assume $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Given the moment of inertia $I = 5\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$ and the distance from pivot to center of mass $d = 1\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the period $T$?

- $2 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{s}$
- $\pi\,\mathrm{s}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $\sqrt{2} \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Linearizing the torque from gravity gives $I\ddot\theta=-mgd\theta$. It follows that $\omega=\sqrt{mgd/I}$. In the stated units, the relation is $T = \dfrac{\sqrt{5} \cdot \pi \cdot \sqrt{I}}{5 \cdot \sqrt{d}}$. Substituting the given values yields $\pi\,\mathrm{s}$.
