A particle moves with equation of motion $x(t) = 0{,}30\cos(2{,}0\,t)$, with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. At which instant $t > 0$ does the particle pass through the equilibrium position for the first time?

- $1{,}57\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3{,}14\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0{,}393\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0{,}785\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The equilibrium position is $x = 0$, so we look for the first positive instant at which $\cos(2{,}0\,t) = 0$. The cosine vanishes for the first time when its argument equals $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, therefore
$$
t = \frac{\pi}{2 \cdot 2{,}0}\,\mathrm{s} = \frac{\pi}{4}\,\mathrm{s} = 0{,}785\,\mathrm{s} .
$$
The period is $T = \dfrac{2\pi}{2{,}0}\,\mathrm{s} = 3{,}14\,\mathrm{s}$, and the instant found is $\dfrac{T}{4}$, the time the particle takes to go from the endpoint $x = A$ to equilibrium. At the instant $1{,}57\,\mathrm{s}$, that is, $\dfrac{T}{2}$, the particle is at the opposite endpoint $x = -A$, and at the instant $0{,}393\,\mathrm{s}$ it is at $x = 0{,}30\cos\dfrac{\pi}{4}\,\mathrm{m} = 0{,}21\,\mathrm{m}$.
