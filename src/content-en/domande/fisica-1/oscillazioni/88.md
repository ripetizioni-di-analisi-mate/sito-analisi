A mass on a horizontal spring is released from rest at maximum displacement. The motion is harmonic and the time to advance through a positive phase angle of less than one full cycle is measured. Given the angular frequency $\omega = 4\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ and the phase advance $\phi = \dfrac{\pi}{2}\,\mathrm{rad}$, what is the elapsed time $t$?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{16}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $\dfrac{3 \cdot \pi}{16}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{8}\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Phase increases linearly with time. Angular frequency is its rate of change, so the phase advances by $\omega t$. In the stated units, the relation is $t = \dfrac{\phi}{\omega}$. Substituting the given values yields $\dfrac{\pi}{8}\,\mathrm{s}$.
