## Explanation

At equilibrium the elastic force balances the weight, so $k\,\Delta\ell = mg$ and $\dfrac{k}{m} = \dfrac{g}{\Delta\ell}$. The period of the vertical spring is therefore
$$
T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{\Delta\ell}{g}} = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{0{,}025}{9{,}81}}\,\mathrm{s} = 0{,}317\,\mathrm{s} .
$$
The value $3{,}17\,\mathrm{s}$ is obtained by leaving the extension in centimeters and the value $0{,}0505\,\mathrm{s}$ by forgetting the factor $2\pi$. The value $0{,}159\,\mathrm{s}$ is the time the body takes to go from one endpoint to the other, that is, half a period.
