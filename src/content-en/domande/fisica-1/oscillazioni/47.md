A mass oscillates without friction on an ideal vertical spring. The period is measured about equilibrium. Given the spring constant $k = 100\,\mathrm{N/m}$ and the period $T = \dfrac{2 \cdot \pi}{5}\,\mathrm{s}$, what is the mass $m$?

- $4\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $2\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Weight shifts the equilibrium, while the change in spring force is $-kx$. Thus $\omega=\sqrt{k/m}$ and the period is $2\pi/\omega$. In the stated units, the relation is $T = \dfrac{2 \cdot \pi \cdot \sqrt{m}}{\sqrt{k}}$. Solving for the unknown gives $m = \dfrac{T^{2} \cdot k}{4 \cdot \pi^{2}}$. Substituting the given values yields $4\,\mathrm{kg}$.
