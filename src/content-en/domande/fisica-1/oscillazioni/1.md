A particle moves along the $x$-axis with equation of motion $x(t) = 0{,}080\cos(10\pi t)$, with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. What is the maximum magnitude of the acceleration?

- $158\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2{,}51\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $79{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $8{,}00\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

By comparison with $x(t) = A\cos(\omega t + \varphi)$ we obtain $A = 0{,}080\,\mathrm{m}$ and $\omega = 10\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. The acceleration is $a(t) = -\omega^2 x(t)$, so its magnitude is maximum at the endpoints $x = \pm A$ and equals
$$
a_{\max} = A\omega^2 = 0{,}080 \cdot 100\pi^2\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 8{,}0\pi^2\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 79{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
The value $2{,}51$ is the product $A\omega$, that is, the maximum speed in $\mathrm{m/s}$, and the value $8{,}00$ is obtained by taking $\omega = 10\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ without the factor $\pi$. The value $158$ uses as amplitude the distance $2A$ between the two endpoints.
