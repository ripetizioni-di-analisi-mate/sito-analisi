An undamped harmonic oscillator has amplitude $A$. Its speed is measured as it passes equilibrium. Given the amplitude $A = \dfrac{1}{4}\,\mathrm{m}$ and the maximum speed $v = 2\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the angular frequency $\omega$?

- $4\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $16\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

The sinusoidal velocity has amplitude $\omega A$, attained when displacement is zero. In the stated units, the relation is $v = A \cdot \omega$. Solving for the unknown gives $\omega = \dfrac{v}{A}$. Substituting the given values yields $8\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
