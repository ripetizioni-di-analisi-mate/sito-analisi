A torsion pendulum oscillates at small amplitudes. Its restoring torque is $-k\theta$. Given the torsional stiffness $k = 8\,\mathrm{N\,m/rad}$ and the period $T = \pi\,\mathrm{s}$, what is the moment of inertia $I$?

- $2\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$
- $3\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$
- $1\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$

## Explanation

The rotational equation gives $I\ddot\theta=-k\theta$. The angular frequency is $\sqrt{k/I}$. In the stated units, the relation is $T = \dfrac{2 \cdot \pi \cdot \sqrt{I}}{\sqrt{k}}$. Solving for the unknown gives $I = \dfrac{T^{2} \cdot k}{4 \cdot \pi^{2}}$. Substituting the given values yields $2\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$.
