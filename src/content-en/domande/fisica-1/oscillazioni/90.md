A mass on a horizontal spring is released from rest at maximum displacement. The motion is harmonic and the time to advance through a positive phase less than one cycle is measured. Given the angular frequency $\omega = 4\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ and the elapsed time $t = \dfrac{\pi}{8}\,\mathrm{s}$, what is the phase advance $\phi$?

- $\pi\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $\dfrac{3 \cdot \pi}{4}\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}\,\mathrm{rad}$

## Explanation

Phase increases linearly with time. Angular frequency is its rate of change, so the phase advances by $\omega t$. In the stated units, the relation is $t = \dfrac{\phi}{\omega}$. Solving for the unknown gives $\phi = \omega \cdot t$. Substituting the given values yields $\dfrac{\pi}{2}\,\mathrm{rad}$.
