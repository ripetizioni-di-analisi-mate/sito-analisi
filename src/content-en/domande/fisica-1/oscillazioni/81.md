An undamped spring scale is displaced from equilibrium. The hanging mass oscillates at frequency $f$. Given the frequency $f = 2\,\mathrm{Hz}$ and the spring constant $k = 48 \cdot \pi^{2}\,\mathrm{N/m}$, what is the mass $m$?

- $6\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $3\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $6 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Angular frequency equals $2\pi f$. From $\omega^2=k/m$, it follows that $k=m(2\pi f)^2$. In the stated units, the relation is $k = 4 \cdot \pi^{2} \cdot f^{2} \cdot m$. Solving for the unknown gives $m = \dfrac{k}{4 \cdot \pi^{2} \cdot f^{2}}$. Substituting the given values yields $3\,\mathrm{kg}$.
