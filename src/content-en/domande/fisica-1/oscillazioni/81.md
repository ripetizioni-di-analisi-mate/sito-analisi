A weakly damped oscillator has quality factor $Q = 50$. What fraction of its mechanical energy is dissipated, to a good approximation, in one complete oscillation?

- $2.0\%$
- $6.3\%$
- $25\%$
- $12.6\%$

## Explanation

With weak damping the energy decreases as $e^{-2\gamma t}$, so in one period $T = \dfrac{2\pi}{\omega_0}$ the dissipated fraction is $1 - e^{-2\gamma T} \approx 2\gamma T$, because $2\gamma T$ is small. Substituting $\gamma = \dfrac{\omega_0}{2Q}$ gives
$$
\frac{\Delta E}{E} \approx 2 \cdot \frac{\omega_0}{2Q} \cdot \frac{2\pi}{\omega_0} = \frac{2\pi}{Q} = \frac{2\pi}{50} = 0.126 .
$$
The value $2.0\%$ is $\dfrac{1}{Q}$, the value $6.3\%$ is $\dfrac{\pi}{Q}$, which corresponds to the relative decrease of the amplitude instead of the energy, and $25\%$ is $\dfrac{4\pi}{Q}$.
