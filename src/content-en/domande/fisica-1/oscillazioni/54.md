An undamped harmonic oscillator has amplitude $A$. Its speed is measured as it passes equilibrium. The initial data are the angular frequency $\omega = 8\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ and the amplitude $A = \dfrac{1}{4}\,\mathrm{m}$. In the second configuration, change the amplitude to $\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the ratio of the final value of $v$ to its initial value?

- $1$
- $2$
- $4$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The sinusoidal velocity has amplitude $\omega A$, attained when displacement is zero. The relation $v = A \cdot \omega$ gives $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ before the change and $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ after it. The ratio is $2$.
