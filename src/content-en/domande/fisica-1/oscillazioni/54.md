The graph shows the magnitude $F$ of the force needed to stretch an ideal spring as a function of the extension $x$, with $F$ in newtons and $x$ in centimeters. A body of mass $0.20\,\mathrm{kg}$ is attached to the spring and oscillates on a smooth horizontal surface. What is the period of the oscillations?

::grafico{f="0.8*x" punti="5,4,pieno" segmenti="5,0,5,4;0,4,5,4" xmin=-0.5 xmax=6.5 ymin=-0.5 ymax=5.5 nomi="x,F" legenda=0 titolo="Force $F$, in newtons, as a function of the extension $x$, in centimeters"}

- $0.31\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.050\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3.1\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.16\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The graph is a straight line through the origin passing through the point $x = 5\,\mathrm{cm}$, $F = 4\,\mathrm{N}$, so the spring constant is
$$
k = \frac{4.0\,\mathrm{N}}{0.050\,\mathrm{m}} = 80\,\mathrm{N/m} .
$$
Therefore the period is $T = 2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k}} = 2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{0.20}{80}}\,\mathrm{s} = 0.31\,\mathrm{s}$. The value $3.1\,\mathrm{s}$ is obtained by using the slope $0.8\,\mathrm{N/cm}$ as if it were in $\mathrm{N/m}$, the value $0.050\,\mathrm{s}$ is $\sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k}}$ without the factor $2\pi$, and $0.16\,\mathrm{s}$ is half a period.
