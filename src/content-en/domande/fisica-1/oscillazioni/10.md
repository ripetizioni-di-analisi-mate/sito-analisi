A physical-pendulum clock uses a hoop suspended at its rim. A small point mass is fixed exactly on the pivot axis without shifting the hoop. How does the small-oscillation period change for an ideal pivot?

- It stays the same.
- It always increases.
- It always decreases.
- It becomes infinite.

## Explanation

A mass on the axis has zero lever arm for both inertia and gravitational torque. In $I\ddot\theta=-mgd\sin\theta$ both $I$ and the product $mgd$ therefore remain unchanged.
