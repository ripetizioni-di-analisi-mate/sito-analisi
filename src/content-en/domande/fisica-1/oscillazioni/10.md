- $1{,}42\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1{,}00\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2{,}24\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1{,}23\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The nail passes through a point on the rim, so the center of mass, which is the center of the ring, is at distance $d = R$ from the axis. By the parallel-axis theorem $I = mR^2 + mR^2 = 2mR^2$, therefore
$$
T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{I}{mgd}} = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{2R}{g}} = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{0{,}50}{9{,}81}}\,\mathrm{s} = 1{,}42\,\mathrm{s} .
$$
The value $1{,}00\,\mathrm{s}$ is obtained with $I = mR^2$, that is, without the parallel-axis term, and the value $2{,}24\,\mathrm{s}$ with the distance $2R$ in the parallel-axis term, that is, with $I = 5mR^2$. The value $1{,}23\,\mathrm{s}$ uses the moment of inertia $\dfrac{1}{2}mR^2$ of the disk instead of that of the ring.
