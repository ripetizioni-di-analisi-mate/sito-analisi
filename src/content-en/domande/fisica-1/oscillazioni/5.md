- $2{,}01\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3{,}26\,\mathrm{s}$
- $12{,}1\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0{,}519\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

For small oscillations the period of the simple pendulum is $T_0 = 2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{\ell}{g}}$, where $g$ is the gravitational acceleration at the place where the pendulum oscillates. On Mars, therefore,
$$
T_0 = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{1{,}00}{3{,}71}}\,\mathrm{s} = 3{,}26\,\mathrm{s} .
$$
The value $2{,}01\,\mathrm{s}$ is the period of the same pendulum on Earth, with $g = 9{,}81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The value $12{,}1\,\mathrm{s}$ is obtained by swapping $\ell$ and $g$ under the square root, and the value $0{,}519\,\mathrm{s}$ by forgetting the factor $2\pi$.
