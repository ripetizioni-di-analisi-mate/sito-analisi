- $49.3\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $79.0\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $31.4\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $197\,\mathrm{N/m}$

## Explanation

For the mass-spring oscillator $T = 2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k}}$. Squaring gives $T^2 = \dfrac{4\pi^2 m}{k}$, therefore
$$
k = \frac{4\pi^2 m}{T^2} = \frac{4\pi^2 \cdot 0.80}{0.16}\,\mathrm{N/m} = 20\pi^2\,\mathrm{N/m} = 197\,\mathrm{N/m} .
$$
The value $79.0\,\mathrm{N/m}$ is obtained by dividing by $T$ instead of $T^2$, the value $49.3\,\mathrm{N/m}$ by using $\pi^2$ instead of $4\pi^2$, and the value $31.4\,\mathrm{N/m}$ by using $2\pi$ instead of $4\pi^2$.
