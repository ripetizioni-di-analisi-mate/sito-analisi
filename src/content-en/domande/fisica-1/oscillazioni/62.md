A particle of mass $0.50\,\mathrm{kg}$ moves along the half-axis $x > 0$ with potential energy $U(x) = \dfrac{a}{x^2} - \dfrac{b}{x}$, where $a = 1.0\,\mathrm{J\cdot m^2}$ and $b = 2.0\,\mathrm{J\cdot m}$; it is a model of the interaction energy between the two atoms of a molecule. What is the angular frequency of small oscillations about the stable equilibrium position?

- $3.5\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $1.4\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

The derivative is $U'(x) = -\dfrac{2a}{x^3} + \dfrac{b}{x^2}$, which vanishes at $x_e = \dfrac{2a}{b} = 1.0\,\mathrm{m}$. The second derivative is $U''(x) = \dfrac{6a}{x^4} - \dfrac{2b}{x^3}$, so $U''(x_e) = 6 - 4 = 2.0\,\mathrm{N/m} > 0$ and the position is a minimum. By the theorem on small oscillations
$$
\omega = \sqrt{\frac{U''(x_e)}{m}} = \sqrt{\frac{2.0}{0.50}}\,\mathrm{rad/s} = 2.0\,\mathrm{rad/s} .
$$
The value $3.5\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ is obtained with only the term $\dfrac{6a}{x^4}$ of the second derivative, $1.4\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ is $\sqrt{U''(x_e)}$ without dividing by the mass, and $4.0$ is $\omega^2$.
