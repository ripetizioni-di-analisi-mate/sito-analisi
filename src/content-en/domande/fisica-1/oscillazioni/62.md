Near an equilibrium an oscillator has potential energy $U(x)=C+ax^4$, with $a>0$. What does linearising the force about $x=0$ predict?

- The linear stiffness equals $4a$.
- The linear stiffness vanishes, although a cubic restoring force exists.
- The force is repulsive on both sides.
- The equilibrium is a maximum of the potential.

## Explanation

The force is $-4ax^3$ and points towards the origin. Its derivative at zero vanishes, so linearisation misses the first nonzero restoring term and yields no finite harmonic period.
