A mass oscillates without friction on an ideal vertical spring. The period is measured about equilibrium. Given the mass $m = 4\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the spring constant $k = 100\,\mathrm{N/m}$, what is the period $T$?

- $\dfrac{4 \cdot \pi}{5}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $\dfrac{2 \cdot \pi}{5}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{5}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $10 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Weight shifts the equilibrium, while the change in spring force is $-kx$. Thus $\omega=\sqrt{k/m}$ and the period is $2\pi/\omega$. In the stated units, the relation is $T = \dfrac{2 \cdot \pi \cdot \sqrt{m}}{\sqrt{k}}$. Substituting the given values yields $\dfrac{2 \cdot \pi}{5}\,\mathrm{s}$.
