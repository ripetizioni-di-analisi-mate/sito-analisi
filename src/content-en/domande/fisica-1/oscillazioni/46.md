The graph shows the position $x(t)$ of a point in simple harmonic motion, with $x$ in centimeters and $t$ in seconds. What is the angular frequency of the motion?

::grafico{f="3*cos(pi*x/2)" punti="0,3,pieno;4,3,pieno;8,3,pieno" asintoti="y=3;y=-3" xmin=-0.6 xmax=9 ymin=-4 ymax=4.5 nomi="t,x" legenda=0 titolo="Position $x$, in centimeters, as a function of time $t$, in seconds"}

- $0.25\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $0.785\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $25.1\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $1.57\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

The graph reaches its maximum $x = 3\,\mathrm{cm}$ at the instants $t = 0$, $t = 4\,\mathrm{s}$ and $t = 8\,\mathrm{s}$, so the period is $T = 4.0\,\mathrm{s}$. The angular frequency is related to the period by $\omega = \dfrac{2\pi}{T}$, therefore
$$
\omega = \frac{2\pi}{4.0\,\mathrm{s}} = 1.57\,\mathrm{rad/s} .
$$
The value $0.25$ is the frequency $f = \dfrac{1}{T}$ in hertz, the value $0.785\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ is obtained by taking as the period the $8\,\mathrm{s}$ interval between the first and the third maximum, and $25.1\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ is obtained by multiplying $2\pi$ by $T$ instead of dividing.
