A simple pendulum of length $0.90\,\mathrm{m}$ oscillates with angular amplitude $\theta_0 = 0.10\,\mathrm{rad}$. What is the speed of the bob at the lowest point?

- $0.42\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.33\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.88\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.30\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

For small oscillations the angle varies as $\theta(t) = \theta_0\cos(\omega t)$, with $\omega = \sqrt{\dfrac{g}{\ell}}$, so the maximum angular velocity is $\theta_0\,\omega$. The bob moves along an arc of radius $\ell$, therefore its speed at the lowest point is
$$
v = \ell\,\theta_0\,\omega = \theta_0\sqrt{g\ell} = 0.10\sqrt{9.81 \cdot 0.90}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 0.30\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
Conservation of energy, $v^2 = 2g\ell(1 - \cos\theta_0)$, gives the same value, because $1 - \cos\theta_0 \approx \dfrac{\theta_0^2}{2}$. The value $0.42\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is obtained with $1 - \cos\theta_0 \approx \theta_0^2$, $0.33$ is the angular velocity $\theta_0\,\omega$ in $\mathrm{rad/s}$, and $0.88$ is $\theta_0\,g\ell$.
