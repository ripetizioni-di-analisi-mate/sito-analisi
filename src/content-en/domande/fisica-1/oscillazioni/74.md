A torsion balance consists of a light rod $20\,\mathrm{cm}$ long, hanging from its center on a vertical fiber, with two small spheres of mass $50\,\mathrm{g}$ fixed at its ends. The torsion constant of the fiber is $4.0 \cdot 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m/rad}$. What is the period of the torsional oscillations?

- $20\,\mathrm{s}$
- $7.0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $9.9\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.6\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The spheres are small and the rod is light, so the moment of inertia about the fiber is that of two particles at distance $r = 0.10\,\mathrm{m}$ from the axis
$$
I = 2mr^2 = 2 \cdot 0.050 \cdot 0.10^2\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2} = 1.0 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2} .
$$
The torsion pendulum has period $T = 2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{I}{\kappa}}$, therefore $T = 2\pi\sqrt{2.5}\,\mathrm{s} = 9.9\,\mathrm{s}$. The value $20\,\mathrm{s}$ is obtained by using the whole length of the rod as the distance from the axis, $7.0\,\mathrm{s}$ with only one sphere, and $1.6\,\mathrm{s}$ is $\sqrt{\dfrac{I}{\kappa}}$ without the factor $2\pi$.
