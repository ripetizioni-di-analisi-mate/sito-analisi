A body undergoes harmonic motion about the origin with angular frequency $\omega$ and zero initial velocity. At the instant considered its coordinate is positive. Given the angular frequency $\omega = 5\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ and the instantaneous coordinate $x = \dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the acceleration magnitude $a$?

- $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Harmonic motion satisfies $a_x=-\omega^2 x$. A positive coordinate gives acceleration toward the origin. In the stated units, the relation is $a = \omega^{2} \cdot x$. Substituting the given values yields $10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
