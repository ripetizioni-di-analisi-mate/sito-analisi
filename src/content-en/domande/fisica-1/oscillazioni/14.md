A body oscillating in harmonic motion completes $24$ full oscillations in $6{,}00\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the angular frequency of the motion?

- $25{,}1\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $4{,}00\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $1{,}57\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $12{,}6\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

The frequency is the number of full oscillations per unit time, so $f = \dfrac{24}{6{,}00\,\mathrm{s}} = 4{,}00\,\mathrm{Hz}$. The angular frequency is related to the frequency by $\omega = 2\pi f$, therefore
$$
\omega = 2\pi \cdot 4{,}00\,\mathrm{rad/s} = 8\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s} = 25{,}1\,\mathrm{rad/s} .
$$
The value $4{,}00\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ confuses the angular frequency with the frequency, the value $1{,}57\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ is obtained by computing $\dfrac{2\pi}{f}$, and the value $12{,}6\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ by forgetting the factor $2$.
