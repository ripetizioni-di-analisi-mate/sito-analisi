- $x(t) = 0.10\cos(4.0\,t + 0.927)\,\mathrm{m}$
- $x(t) = 0.10\cos(4.0\,t - 0.927)\,\mathrm{m}$
- $x(t) = 0.10\cos(4.0\,t + 4.07)\,\mathrm{m}$
- $x(t) = 0.14\cos(4.0\,t + 0.927)\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The amplitude is
$$
A = \sqrt{x_0^2 + \frac{v_0^2}{\omega^2}} = \sqrt{0.0036 + 0.0064}\,\mathrm{m} = 0.10\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The initial phase satisfies both $\cos\varphi = \dfrac{x_0}{A} = 0.60$ and $\sin\varphi = -\dfrac{v_0}{A\omega} = 0.80$. Since sine and cosine are both positive, $\varphi$ lies in the first quadrant and equals $0.927\,\mathrm{rad}$. The phase $-0.927$ gives a positive initial velocity, and the phase $4.07 = 0.927 + \pi$, which has the same tangent, gives a negative initial position. The amplitude $0.14\,\mathrm{m}$ is obtained by adding $x_0$ and $\dfrac{|v_0|}{\omega}$ instead of their squares.
