An underdamped oscillator has amplitude envelope $A(t)=A_0 e^{-\beta t}$. Measure the time taken to reduce the amplitude to a fraction of its initial value. Given the decay rate $\beta = \dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$ and the elapsed time $t = 2 \cdot \log{\left(2 \right)}\,\mathrm{s}$, what is the final-to-initial amplitude ratio $a$?

- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

The final-to-initial amplitude ratio is $a=e^{-\beta t}$. Taking logarithms gives $\beta t=-\ln a$. In the stated units, the relation is $t = - \dfrac{\log{\left(a \right)}}{\beta}$. Solving for the unknown gives $a = e^{- \beta \cdot t}$. Substituting the given values yields $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
