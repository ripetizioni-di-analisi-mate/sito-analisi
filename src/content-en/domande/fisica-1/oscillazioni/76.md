A body undergoes harmonic motion about the origin with angular frequency $\omega$ and zero initial speed. At the instant considered it is at a positive coordinate. Given the angular frequency $\omega = 5\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ and the acceleration magnitude $a = 10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the instantaneous coordinate $x$?

- $\dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{4}{5}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Harmonic motion satisfies $a_x=-\omega^2 x$. A positive coordinate gives acceleration toward the origin. In the stated units, the relation is $a = \omega^{2} \cdot x$. Solving for the unknown gives $x = \dfrac{a}{\omega^{2}}$. Substituting the given values yields $\dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm{m}$.
