A small sphere, which can be treated as a particle, of mass $m$ is fixed at one end of a uniform rod of mass $m$ and length $L = 0.60\,\mathrm{m}$; the rod hangs from its other end on a frictionless horizontal pivot. The moment of inertia of the rod about the pivot is $\dfrac{1}{3}mL^2$. What is the period of small oscillations?

- $1.47\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2.07\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.27\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.79\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The moment of inertia of the system about the pivot is the sum of those of the rod and of the sphere, $I = \dfrac{1}{3}mL^2 + mL^2 = \dfrac{4}{3}mL^2$. The total mass is $2m$ and the center of mass lies halfway between the center of the rod, at $\dfrac{L}{2}$, and the sphere, at $L$, that is, at $d = \dfrac{3}{4}L$ from the pivot. Therefore
$$
T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{I}{2m\,g\,d}} = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{8L}{9g}} = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{8 \cdot 0.60}{9 \cdot 9.81}}\,\mathrm{s} = 1.47\,\mathrm{s} .
$$
The value $2.07\,\mathrm{s}$ is obtained with only the mass $m$ in the torque of the weight, the value $1.27\,\mathrm{s}$ by taking $d = L$, and $1.79\,\mathrm{s}$ by taking $d = \dfrac{L}{2}$.
