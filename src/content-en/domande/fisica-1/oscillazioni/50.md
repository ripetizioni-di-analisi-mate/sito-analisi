A damped mass-spring oscillator is driven by a sinusoidal force. After the transient has died out, what frequency does the response have?

- The undamped natural frequency in all cases
- The driving frequency
- The damped free oscillation frequency in all cases
- The difference between the natural and driving frequencies

## Explanation

The free response decays due to damping. The steady response to the periodic force has the same frequency as the driving force.
