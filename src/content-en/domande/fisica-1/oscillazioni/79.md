An undamped spring scale is displaced from equilibrium. The hanging mass oscillates at frequency $f$. Given the frequency $f = 2\,\mathrm{Hz}$ and the mass $m = 3\,\mathrm{kg}$, what is the spring constant $k$?

- $48 \cdot \pi^{2}\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $24 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $96 \cdot \pi^{2}\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $12 \cdot \pi^{2}\,\mathrm{N/m}$

## Explanation

Angular frequency equals $2\pi f$. From $\omega^2=k/m$, it follows that $k=m(2\pi f)^2$. In the stated units, the relation is $k = 4 \cdot \pi^{2} \cdot f^{2} \cdot m$. Substituting the given values yields $48 \cdot \pi^{2}\,\mathrm{N/m}$.
