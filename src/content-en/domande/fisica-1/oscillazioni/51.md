An undamped harmonic oscillator has amplitude $A$. Its speed is measured as it passes equilibrium. Given the angular frequency $\omega = 8\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ and the amplitude $A = \dfrac{1}{4}\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the maximum speed $v$?

- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The sinusoidal velocity has amplitude $\omega A$, attained when displacement is zero. In the stated units, the relation is $v = A \cdot \omega$. Substituting the given values yields $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
