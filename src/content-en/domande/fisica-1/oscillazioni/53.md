The suspension of a car of mass $1000\,\mathrm{kg}$ is equivalent to an ideal vertical spring with constant $4.0 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{N/m}$, and the shock absorbers are negligible. At what frequency does the car body oscillate vertically?

- $6.3\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $32\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $1.0\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

The car body is a vertical mass-spring oscillator, so its angular frequency is $\omega = \sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m}} = \sqrt{40}\,\mathrm{rad/s} = 6.3\,\mathrm{rad/s}$; the weight only shifts the equilibrium position. The frequency is $f = \dfrac{\omega}{2\pi}$, therefore
$$
f = \frac{1}{2\pi}\sqrt{\frac{4.0 \cdot 10^4}{1000}}\,\mathrm{Hz} = 1.0\,\mathrm{Hz} .
$$
The value $6.3\,\mathrm{Hz}$ is the angular frequency written as a frequency, the value $32\,\mathrm{Hz}$ is obtained by expressing the mass in tonnes, and $2.0\,\mathrm{Hz}$ is obtained by dividing the angular frequency by $\pi$ instead of $2\pi$.
