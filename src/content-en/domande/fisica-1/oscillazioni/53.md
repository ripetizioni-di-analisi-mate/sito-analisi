An undamped harmonic oscillator has amplitude $A$. Its speed is measured as it passes equilibrium. Given the angular frequency $\omega = 8\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ and the maximum speed $v = 2\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the amplitude $A$?

- $\dfrac{1}{8}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{3}{8}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The sinusoidal velocity has amplitude $\omega A$, attained when displacement is zero. In the stated units, the relation is $v = A \cdot \omega$. Solving for the unknown gives $A = \dfrac{v}{\omega}$. Substituting the given values yields $\dfrac{1}{4}\,\mathrm{m}$.
