A disk with moment of inertia $2{,}0 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^2}$ about its own axis hangs from a vertical wire with torsional stiffness $0{,}080\,\mathrm{N\cdot m/rad}$ and rotates about the axis of the wire. What is the period of the torsional oscillations?

- $0{,}16\,\mathrm{s}$
- $40\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0{,}50\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0{,}99\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The wire exerts a restoring torque $M = -\kappa\theta$, so the rotational equation of motion gives $I\ddot{\theta} = -\kappa\theta$ and the motion is harmonic with $\omega = \sqrt{\dfrac{\kappa}{I}}$. The period is therefore
$$
T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{I}{\kappa}} = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{2{,}0 \cdot 10^{-3}}{0{,}080}}\,\mathrm{s} = 2\pi\sqrt{0{,}025}\,\mathrm{s} = 0{,}99\,\mathrm{s} .
$$
The value $0{,}16\,\mathrm{s}$ is obtained by forgetting the factor $2\pi$, the value $0{,}50\,\mathrm{s}$ by using $\pi$ instead of $2\pi$, and the value $40\,\mathrm{s}$ by inverting the ratio under the square root.
