A small ball slides without friction and without rolling inside a bowl shaped like a spherical cap of radius $40\,\mathrm{cm}$, performing small oscillations about the lowest point. What is the period?

- $1.27\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.79\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.897\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.202\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The ball moves along an arc of the circle of radius $R = 0.40\,\mathrm{m}$ centered at the center of the sphere, and the reaction of the bowl is perpendicular to the surface, so it is directed toward that center like the tension of a string. The motion is therefore the same as that of a simple pendulum of length $\ell = R$, and for small oscillations
$$
T_0 = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{R}{g}} = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{0.40}{9.81}}\,\mathrm{s} = 1.27\,\mathrm{s} .
$$
The value $1.79\,\mathrm{s}$ is obtained by using the diameter instead of the radius, $0.897\,\mathrm{s}$ by using half the radius, and $0.202\,\mathrm{s}$ is $\sqrt{\dfrac{R}{g}}$ without the factor $2\pi$.
