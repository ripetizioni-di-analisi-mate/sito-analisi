A body of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$, attached to an ideal spring with constant $50\,\mathrm{N/m}$, is subject to a viscous force with coefficient $b = 0.50\,\mathrm{kg/s}$. What is the quality factor $Q$ of the oscillator?

- $40$
- $10$
- $20$
- $0.050$

## Explanation

The natural angular frequency is $\omega_0 = \sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m}} = \sqrt{25}\,\mathrm{rad/s} = 5.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ and the damping constant is $\gamma = \dfrac{b}{2m} = \dfrac{0.50}{4.0}\,\mathrm{s^{-1}} = 0.125\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$. The quality factor is therefore
$$
Q = \frac{\omega_0}{2\gamma} = \frac{5.0}{0.25} = 20 .
$$
The value $40$ is obtained with the ratio $\dfrac{\omega_0}{\gamma}$, without the factor $2$ in the definition of $Q$, and the value $10$ with $\gamma = \dfrac{b}{m}$. The value $0.050$ is the inverse ratio $\dfrac{2\gamma}{\omega_0}$.
