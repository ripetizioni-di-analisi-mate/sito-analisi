A torsion pendulum oscillates at small amplitudes. Its restoring torque is $-k\theta$. Given the moment of inertia $I = 2\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$ and the torsional stiffness $k = 8\,\mathrm{N\,m/rad}$, what is the period $T$?

- $2 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{s}$
- $\pi\,\mathrm{s}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The rotational equation gives $I\ddot\theta=-k\theta$. The angular frequency is $\sqrt{k/I}$. In the stated units, the relation is $T = \dfrac{2 \cdot \pi \cdot \sqrt{I}}{\sqrt{k}}$. Substituting the given values yields $\pi\,\mathrm{s}$.
