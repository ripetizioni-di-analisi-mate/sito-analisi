Two ideal springs with constants $300\,\mathrm{N/m}$ and $600\,\mathrm{N/m}$ are connected in series, one after the other, and a body of mass $0.50\,\mathrm{kg}$ attached to the free end oscillates on a smooth horizontal plane. What is the period of the oscillations?

- $0.148\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.209\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.257\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.314\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

In series the equivalent constant satisfies $\dfrac{1}{k_s} = \dfrac{1}{k_1} + \dfrac{1}{k_2}$, therefore
$$
k_s = \frac{k_1 k_2}{k_1 + k_2} = \frac{300 \cdot 600}{900}\,\mathrm{N/m} = 200\,\mathrm{N/m} .
$$
The period is therefore $T = 2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k_s}} = 2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{0.50}{200}}\,\mathrm{s} = 2\pi \cdot 0.050\,\mathrm{s} = 0.314\,\mathrm{s}$. The value $0.148\,\mathrm{s}$ is obtained with the constant $k_1 + k_2$ of springs in parallel, the value $0.209\,\mathrm{s}$ with the average of the two constants, and the value $0.257\,\mathrm{s}$ with the $300\,\mathrm{N/m}$ spring alone. The series constant is smaller than both constants, so the period is longer than the one the body would have with either spring alone.
