A mass on a horizontal spring is released from rest at maximum displacement. The motion is harmonic and the time to advance through a positive phase angle of less than one full cycle is measured. Given the elapsed time $t = \dfrac{\pi}{8}\,\mathrm{s}$ and the phase advance $\phi = \dfrac{\pi}{2}\,\mathrm{rad}$, what is the angular frequency $\omega$?

- $2\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

Phase increases linearly with time. Angular frequency is its rate of change, so the phase advances by $\omega t$. In the stated units, the relation is $t = \dfrac{\phi}{\omega}$. Solving for the unknown gives $\omega = \dfrac{\phi}{t}$. Substituting the given values yields $4\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
