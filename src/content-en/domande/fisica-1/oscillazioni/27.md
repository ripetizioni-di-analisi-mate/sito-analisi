A body attached to an ideal spring oscillates on a smooth horizontal plane with period $0.60\,\mathrm{s}$. The spring is cut in half and the same body is attached to one of the two halves. What is the new period?

- $0.42\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.85\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.30\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.60\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The two halves, connected in series, make up the whole spring with constant $k$. If $k'$ is the constant of each half, the rule for springs in series gives $\dfrac{1}{k} = \dfrac{1}{k'} + \dfrac{1}{k'} = \dfrac{2}{k'}$, so $k' = 2k$. The period is proportional to $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{k}}$, therefore it is divided by $\sqrt{2}$ and equals $\dfrac{0.60}{\sqrt{2}}\,\mathrm{s} = 0.42\,\mathrm{s}$. The value $0.85\,\mathrm{s}$ is obtained by assigning the constant $\dfrac{k}{2}$ to each half, and the value $0.30\,\mathrm{s}$ by dividing the period by $2$ instead of $\sqrt{2}$.
