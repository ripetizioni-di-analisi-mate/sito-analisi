A particle moves with equation of motion $x(t) = A\cos(\omega t)$, with period $T$. Which statement is true at the instant $t = \dfrac{T}{4}$?

- The particle is at rest and the acceleration has maximum magnitude
- Velocity and acceleration both have maximum magnitude
- Velocity and acceleration are both zero
- The velocity has maximum magnitude and the acceleration is zero

## Explanation

At the instant $t = \dfrac{T}{4}$ the phase is $\omega t = \dfrac{2\pi}{T} \cdot \dfrac{T}{4} = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$, so $x = A\cos\dfrac{\pi}{2} = 0$ and the particle passes through the equilibrium position. The velocity is $v = -A\omega\sin\dfrac{\pi}{2} = -A\omega$, therefore its magnitude is maximum. The acceleration is $a = -\omega^2 x = 0$. The particle is at rest, with acceleration of maximum magnitude, at the endpoints, which it reaches at the instants $0$ and $\dfrac{T}{2}$.
