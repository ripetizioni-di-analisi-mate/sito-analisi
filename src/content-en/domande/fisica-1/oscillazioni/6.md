- $17.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2.44\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $9.75\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $1.55\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

From the formula $T_0 = 2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{\ell}{g}}$, squaring, we obtain $g = \dfrac{4\pi^2\ell}{T_0^2}$. With the data
$$
g = \frac{4\pi^2 \cdot 0.800}{1.80^2}\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = \frac{31.58}{3.24}\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 9.75\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
The value $17.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ is obtained by dividing by $T_0$ instead of $T_0^2$, the value $2.44\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ by using $\pi^2$ instead of $4\pi^2$, and the value $1.55\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ by using $2\pi$ instead of $4\pi^2$.
