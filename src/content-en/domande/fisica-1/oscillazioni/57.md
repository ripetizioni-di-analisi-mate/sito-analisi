A mass attached to an ideal spring oscillates on a frictionless horizontal surface. Given the amplitude $A = \dfrac{3}{10}\,\mathrm{m}$ and the maximum kinetic energy $K = 9\,\mathrm{J}$, what is the spring constant $k$?

- $300\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $100\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $200\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $400\,\mathrm{N/m}$

## Explanation

At maximum displacement from equilibrium, the oscillator energy is potential. On passing through equilibrium it is entirely kinetic. In the stated units, the relation is $K = \dfrac{A^{2} \cdot k}{2}$. Solving for the unknown gives $k = \dfrac{2 \cdot K}{A^{2}}$. Substituting the given values yields $200\,\mathrm{N/m}$.
