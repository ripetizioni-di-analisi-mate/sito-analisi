A quantity $x(t)$, with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds, satisfies the equation $4\ddot{x} + 36x = 0$. What is the period of the motion?

- $1{,}05\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2{,}09\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0{,}698\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0{,}333\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

After division by $4$ the equation becomes $\ddot{x} = -9x$, which has the form $\ddot{x} = -\omega^2 x$ with $\omega^2 = 9\,\mathrm{s^{-2}}$. By the theorem on the equation of harmonic motion the motion is harmonic with $\omega = 3{,}0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$, therefore
$$
T = \frac{2\pi}{\omega} = \frac{2\pi}{3{,}0}\,\mathrm{s} = 2{,}09\,\mathrm{s} .
$$
The value $1{,}05\,\mathrm{s}$ is obtained by taking $\omega = \sqrt{36}$ without dividing by $4$, the value $0{,}698\,\mathrm{s}$ by using $\omega = 9$ without taking the square root, and the value $0{,}333\,\mathrm{s}$ by writing $T = \dfrac{1}{\omega}$.
