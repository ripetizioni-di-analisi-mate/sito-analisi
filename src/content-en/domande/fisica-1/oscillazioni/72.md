An underdamped oscillator has amplitude envelope $A(t)=A_0 e^{-\beta t}$. The time taken to reduce the amplitude to a fraction of its initial value is measured. Given the elapsed time $t = 2 \cdot \log{\left(2 \right)}\,\mathrm{s}$ and the final-to-initial amplitude ratio $a = \dfrac{1}{2}$, what is the decay rate $\beta$?

- $1\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$

## Explanation

The final-to-initial amplitude ratio is $a=e^{-\beta t}$. Taking logarithms gives $\beta t=-\ln a$. In the stated units, the relation is $t = - \dfrac{\log{\left(a \right)}}{\beta}$. Solving for the unknown gives $\beta = - \dfrac{\log{\left(a \right)}}{t}$. Substituting the given values yields $\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$.
