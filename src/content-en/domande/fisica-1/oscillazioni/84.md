A torsion pendulum oscillates at small amplitudes. Its restoring torque is $-k\theta$. Given the moment of inertia $I = 2\,\mathrm{kg\,m^2}$ and the period $T = \pi\,\mathrm{s}$, what is the torsional stiffness $k$?

- $16\,\mathrm{N\,m/rad}$
- $4\,\mathrm{N\,m/rad}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N\,m/rad}$
- $8\,\mathrm{N\,m/rad}$

## Explanation

The rotational equation gives $I\ddot\theta=-k\theta$. The angular frequency is $\sqrt{k/I}$. In the stated units, the relation is $T = \dfrac{2 \cdot \pi \cdot \sqrt{I}}{\sqrt{k}}$. Solving for the unknown gives $k = \dfrac{4 \cdot \pi^{2} \cdot I}{T^{2}}$. Substituting the given values yields $8\,\mathrm{N\,m/rad}$.
