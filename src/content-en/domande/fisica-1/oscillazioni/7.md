A body of mass $0.10\,\mathrm{kg}$ oscillates on a smooth horizontal plane attached to an ideal spring with constant $250\,\mathrm{N/m}$, with amplitude $4.0\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the speed at the position $x = 2.0\,\mathrm{cm}$?

- $1.73\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.24\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The angular frequency is $\omega = \sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m}} = \sqrt{2500}\,\mathrm{rad/s} = 50\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. The speed at the position $x$ is $|v| = \omega\sqrt{A^2 - x^2}$, therefore
$$
|v| = 50\sqrt{0.040^2 - 0.020^2}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 50 \cdot 0.0346\,\mathrm{m/s} = 1.73\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The same value follows from conservation of energy, because $\dfrac{1}{2}mv^2 = \dfrac{1}{2}k\,(A^2 - x^2) = 0.15\,\mathrm{J}$. The value $2.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is the maximum speed $\omega A$, which the body has only when it passes through equilibrium. The values $1.00\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $2.24\,\mathrm{m/s}$ are obtained with $\omega\,(A - x)$ and with $\omega\sqrt{A^2 + x^2}$.
