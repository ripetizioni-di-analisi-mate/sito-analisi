A mass attached to an ideal spring oscillates on a frictionless horizontal surface. Given the spring constant $k = 200\,\mathrm{N/m}$ and the amplitude $A = \dfrac{3}{10}\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the maximum kinetic energy $K$?

- $\dfrac{27}{2}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $18\,\mathrm{J}$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $9\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

At maximum displacement from equilibrium, the oscillator energy is potential. On passing through equilibrium it is entirely kinetic. In the stated units, the relation is $K = \dfrac{A^{2} \cdot k}{2}$. Substituting the given values yields $9\,\mathrm{J}$.
