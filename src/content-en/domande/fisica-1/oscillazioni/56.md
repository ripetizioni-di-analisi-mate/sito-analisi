In a frictionless mass-spring oscillator the spring constant is $100\,\mathrm{N/m}$ and the amplitude is $0.20\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the average value of the kinetic energy of the body taken over one period?

- $2.0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0.50\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1.4\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1.0\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The mechanical energy is $E = \dfrac{1}{2}kA^2 = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 100 \cdot 0.040\,\mathrm{J} = 2.0\,\mathrm{J}$. The kinetic energy is $K = E\sin^2(\omega t + \varphi)$, and the average of $\sin^2$ over one period is $\dfrac{1}{2}$, so
$$
\langle K \rangle = \frac{E}{2} = 1.0\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
The potential energy has the same average, because $K$ and $U$ have the same behavior shifted by a quarter of a period. The value $2.0\,\mathrm{J}$ is the maximum kinetic energy, and the values $0.50\,\mathrm{J}$ and $1.4\,\mathrm{J}$ are obtained by dividing $E$ by $4$ and by $\sqrt{2}$.
