- $16\,\mathrm{kg/s}$
- $8{,}0\,\mathrm{kg/s}$
- $45\,\mathrm{kg/s}$
- $32\,\mathrm{kg/s}$

## Explanation

The damping is critical when $\gamma = \omega_0$, with $\gamma = \dfrac{b}{2m}$ and $\omega_0 = \sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m}} = \sqrt{64}\,\mathrm{rad/s} = 8{,}0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. Therefore
$$
b_c = 2m\omega_0 = 2\sqrt{km} = 2 \cdot 2{,}0 \cdot 8{,}0\,\mathrm{kg/s} = 32\,\mathrm{kg/s} .
$$
The value $16\,\mathrm{kg/s}$ is obtained by forgetting the factor $2$ in the definition of $\gamma$, and the value $8{,}0\,\mathrm{kg/s}$ by confusing the coefficient $b$ with the constant $\gamma$. The value $45\,\mathrm{kg/s}$ is obtained by writing $2m\sqrt{k}$, that is, with the mass outside the square root.
