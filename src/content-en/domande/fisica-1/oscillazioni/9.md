- $1.74\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The pivot is at distance $d = \dfrac{L}{2}$ from the center, so by the parallel-axis theorem the moment of inertia about the pivot is $I = \dfrac{1}{12}mL^2 + m\left(\dfrac{L}{2}\right)^2 = \dfrac{1}{3}mL^2$. The period of the physical pendulum is therefore
$$
T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{I}{mgd}} = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{2L}{3g}} = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{3.00}{29.43}}\,\mathrm{s} = 2.01\,\mathrm{s} .
$$
The value $1.00\,\mathrm{s}$ is obtained by using the moment of inertia about the center, without the parallel-axis term. The values $2.46\,\mathrm{s}$ and $1.74\,\mathrm{s}$ are the periods of simple pendulums of length $L$ and $\dfrac{L}{2}$, and they are obtained by treating the rod as a mass concentrated at a point.
