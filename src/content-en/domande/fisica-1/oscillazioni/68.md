A simple pendulum has a string of length $1.00\,\mathrm{m}$. On the vertical through the point of suspension, $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$ lower, a horizontal nail is fixed: when the bob passes to the side of the nail, the string rests on the nail and the bob swings about it. What is the period of small oscillations?

- $1.71\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2.01\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.42\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3.42\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

On one side of the vertical the bob swings like a pendulum of length $\ell_1 = 1.00\,\mathrm{m}$, on the other side like a pendulum of length $\ell_2 = 0.50\,\mathrm{m}$. In each half of the motion the bob performs half an oscillation of the corresponding pendulum, so the period is the mean of the two periods
$$
T = \frac{1}{2}\left(2\pi\sqrt{\frac{\ell_1}{g}} + 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{\ell_2}{g}}\right) = \frac{2.01 + 1.42}{2}\,\mathrm{s} = 1.71\,\mathrm{s} .
$$
The value $2.01\,\mathrm{s}$ is the period without the nail, $1.42\,\mathrm{s}$ is the period of the $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$ pendulum, and $3.42\,\mathrm{s}$ is the sum of the two full periods.
