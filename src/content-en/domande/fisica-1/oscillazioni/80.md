An undamped spring scale is displaced from equilibrium. The hanging mass oscillates at frequency $f$. Given the spring constant $k = 48 \cdot \pi^{2}\,\mathrm{N/m}$ and the mass $m = 3\,\mathrm{kg}$, what is the frequency $f$?

- $1\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $4\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $4 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $2\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

Angular frequency equals $2\pi f$. From $\omega^2=k/m$, it follows that $k=m(2\pi f)^2$. In the stated units, the relation is $k = 4 \cdot \pi^{2} \cdot f^{2} \cdot m$. Solving for the unknown gives $f = \dfrac{\sqrt{k}}{2 \cdot \pi \cdot \sqrt{m}}$. Substituting the given values yields $2\,\mathrm{Hz}$.
