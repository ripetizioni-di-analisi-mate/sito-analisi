In a weakly damped oscillator the amplitude decreases by a factor $e$ in $20$ complete oscillations. What is, to a good approximation, the quality factor $Q$?

- $20$
- $31$
- $126$
- $63$

## Explanation

The amplitude is proportional to $e^{-\gamma t}$, so it decreases by a factor $e$ in the time $\dfrac{1}{\gamma}$; by hypothesis this time is $20T$, therefore $\gamma = \dfrac{1}{20T}$. With weak damping the angular frequency is practically $\omega_0 = \dfrac{2\pi}{T}$, and the quality factor is
$$
Q = \frac{\omega_0}{2\gamma} = \frac{2\pi}{T} \cdot \frac{20T}{2} = 20\pi \approx 63 .
$$
The value $20$ is the number of oscillations, the value $31$ is obtained with $\dfrac{\omega_0}{4\gamma}$, and $126$ with $\dfrac{\omega_0}{\gamma}$, without the factor $2$ of the definition.
