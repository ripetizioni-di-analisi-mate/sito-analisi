A particle of mass $1{,}5\,\mathrm{kg}$ moves along the $x$-axis with potential energy $U(x) = x^3 - 3x$, with $x$ in meters and $U$ in joules. What is the angular frequency of small oscillations about the position of stable equilibrium?

- $2{,}0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $1{,}4\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $4{,}0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $3{,}0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

The equilibrium positions are the zeros of $U'(x) = 3x^2 - 3$, so they are $x = 1\,\mathrm{m}$ and $x = -1\,\mathrm{m}$. Since $U''(x) = 6x$, we have $U''(1) = 6 > 0$ and $U''(-1) = -6 < 0$, therefore the stable equilibrium is at $x_e = 1\,\mathrm{m}$. The theorem on small oscillations gives
$$
\omega = \sqrt{\frac{U''(x_e)}{m}} = \sqrt{\frac{6}{1{,}5}}\,\mathrm{rad/s} = 2{,}0\,\mathrm{rad/s} .
$$
The value $4{,}0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ is obtained by forgetting the square root, the value $3{,}0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ by multiplying by the mass instead of dividing, and the value $1{,}4\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ by using the coefficient $3$ of $U'$ instead of $U''(x_e)$.
