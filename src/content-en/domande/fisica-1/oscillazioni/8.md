A block of mass $1.2\,\mathrm{kg}$ slides on a smooth horizontal plane with speed $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and hits an ideal spring with constant $300\,\mathrm{N/m}$ fixed to a wall. How long does the contact between the block and the spring last?

- $0.397\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.0993\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.0632\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.199\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

During the contact the only horizontal force on the block is the elastic force, so the block performs a portion of harmonic motion with $\omega = \sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m}} = \sqrt{250}\,\mathrm{rad/s} = 15.8\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. The block touches the spring at the equilibrium position, reaches the maximum compression and returns to the equilibrium position, where it leaves with the opposite velocity. This portion is half an oscillation, therefore the contact lasts
$$
\frac{T}{2} = \pi\sqrt{\frac{m}{k}} = \pi\sqrt{0.0040}\,\mathrm{s} = 0.199\,\mathrm{s} .
$$
The value $0.397\,\mathrm{s}$ is the whole period, the value $0.0993\,\mathrm{s}$ is the time to reach the maximum compression, and the value $0.0632\,\mathrm{s}$ is $\sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k}} = \dfrac{1}{\omega}$.
