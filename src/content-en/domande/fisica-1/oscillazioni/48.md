A mass oscillates without friction on an ideal vertical spring. The period is measured about equilibrium. Given the mass $m = 4\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the period $T = \dfrac{2 \cdot \pi}{5}\,\mathrm{s}$, what is the spring constant $k$?

- $200\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $50\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $150\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $100\,\mathrm{N/m}$

## Explanation

Weight shifts the equilibrium, while the change in spring force is $-kx$. Thus $\omega=\sqrt{k/m}$ and the period is $2\pi/\omega$. In the stated units, the relation is $T = \dfrac{2 \cdot \pi \cdot \sqrt{m}}{\sqrt{k}}$. Solving for the unknown gives $k = \dfrac{4 \cdot \pi^{2} \cdot m}{T^{2}}$. Substituting the given values yields $100\,\mathrm{N/m}$.
