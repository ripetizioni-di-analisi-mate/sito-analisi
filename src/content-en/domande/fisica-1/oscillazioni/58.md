A mass attached to an ideal spring oscillates on a frictionless horizontal surface. Given the spring constant $k = 200\,\mathrm{N/m}$ and the maximum kinetic energy $K = 9\,\mathrm{J}$, what is the amplitude $A$?

- $\dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{3}{10}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{3 \cdot \sqrt{2}}{20}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{3 \cdot \sqrt{2}}{10}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

At maximum displacement from equilibrium, the oscillator energy is potential. On passing through equilibrium it is entirely kinetic. In the stated units, the relation is $K = \dfrac{A^{2} \cdot k}{2}$. Solving for the unknown gives $A = \dfrac{\sqrt{2} \cdot \sqrt{K}}{\sqrt{k}}$. Substituting the given values yields $\dfrac{3}{10}\,\mathrm{m}$.
