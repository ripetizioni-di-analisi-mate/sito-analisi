An accelerometer on a stationary trolley has a constant reading of $0.20\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. A program treats it as real acceleration and integrates for $10\,\mathrm{s}$ from zero position and velocity. What position error accumulates?

- $10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The velocity error starts at zero and grows to $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Its mean over the interval is $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$, so the program reports a spurious displacement of $10\,\mathrm{m}$.
