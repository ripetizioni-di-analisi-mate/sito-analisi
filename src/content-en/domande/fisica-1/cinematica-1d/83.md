The initial conditions of a rectilinear motion are given at the instant $t = 2\,\mathrm{s}$, when the body is at rest at the origin; the acceleration is $a(t) = 2t$, with $a$ in $\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and $t$ in seconds. Where is the body at the instant $t = 3\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $2.3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $9.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-3.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6.3\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The initial conditions are given at the instant $t_0 = 2\,\mathrm{s}$, so the integrals start from $2$. The velocity is
$$
v(t) = \int_2^t 2s\,ds = t^2 - 4 ,
$$
and the position at the instant $3\,\mathrm{s}$ is
$$
x(3) = \int_2^3 \left(s^2 - 4\right) ds = \frac{27 - 8}{3} - 4 = \frac{7}{3}\,\mathrm{m} \approx 2.3\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $9.0\,\mathrm{m}$ is obtained by imposing the conditions at the instant $0$ instead of at the instant $2\,\mathrm{s}$.
