A robot travels forward and then covers a shorter distance backward on the same track. The direction of its first leg is positive. Given the return distance $b = 12\,\mathrm{m}$, the total time $t = 8\,\mathrm{s}$ and the average velocity $v = 2\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the distance of the first leg $a$?

- $28\,\mathrm{m}$
- $14\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $56\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The displacement is the difference between the two distances. Average velocity is displacement divided by total time. In the stated units, the relation is $v = \dfrac{a - b}{t}$. Solving for the unknown gives $a = b + t \cdot v$. Substituting the given values yields $28\,\mathrm{m}$.
