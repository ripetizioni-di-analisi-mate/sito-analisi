Two carts move in the same direction at constant speeds. The faster cart starts behind the other. The initial data are the initial separation $d = 36\,\mathrm{m}$, the speed of the first cart $v = 9\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the speed of the second cart $u = 3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Change the speed of the second cart to $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the ratio of the final value of $t$ to its initial value?

- $\dfrac{6}{7}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

The separation decreases at the relative speed $v-u$. The relation $t = - \dfrac{d}{u - v}$ gives $6\,\mathrm{s}$ before the change and $9\,\mathrm{s}$ after it. The ratio is $\dfrac{3}{2}$.
