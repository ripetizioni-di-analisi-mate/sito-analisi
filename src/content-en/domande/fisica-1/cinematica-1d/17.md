At a certain instant a point moving along the $x$ axis has velocity $v = -4{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and acceleration $a = 2{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Which statement is true?

- The point is slowing down, because $v$ and $a$ have opposite signs
- The point is speeding up, because $a$ is positive
- The point is speeding up, because $v$ and $a$ have opposite signs
- The point moves in the positive direction along the axis, because $a$ is positive

## Explanation

Since $\dfrac{d}{dt}\left(v^2\right) = 2\,v\,a$, the magnitude of the velocity increases where $v$ and $a$ have the same sign and decreases where they have opposite signs. In this case $2\,v\,a = -16\,\mathrm{m^2/s^3}$, so $|v|$ decreases and the motion is **decelerated**. The sign of $a$ alone depends on the orientation chosen for the axis and does not determine whether the point goes faster or slower. The direction of motion follows from the sign of $v$, therefore the point moves in the negative direction along the axis.
