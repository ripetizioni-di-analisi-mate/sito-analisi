A ball is thrown vertically upward from the ground with velocity $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Neglecting air resistance, what maximum height does it reach?

- $14{,}7\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3{,}67\,\mathrm{m}$
- $7{,}34\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0{,}612\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

At the highest point the velocity is zero, so the relation $v^2 = v_0^2 - 2g\,(y - y_0)$ with $v = 0$ and $y_0 = 0$ gives the maximum height. One obtains
$$
h_{\max} = \frac{v_0^2}{2g} = \frac{(12\,\mathrm{m/s})^2}{2 \cdot 9{,}81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}} = \frac{144}{19{,}62}\,\mathrm{m} = 7{,}34\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $14{,}7\,\mathrm{m}$ is obtained by forgetting the factor $2$ in the denominator.
