The graph shows the velocity $v(t)$ of a point in rectilinear motion between the instants $0$ and $5\,\mathrm{s}$, with $v$ in $\mathrm{m/s}$ and $t$ in seconds. What is the distance traveled by the point in this interval?

::grafico{f="6-2*x+0*sqrt(x*(5-x))" area="6-2*x;0;0;3|0;6-2*x;3;5" punti="0,6,pieno;5,-4,pieno" segmenti="0,-4,5,-4" xmin=-1 xmax=6 ymin=-6 ymax=8 nomi="t,v" titolo="Velocity $v(t)$ in $\mathrm{m/s}$, with $t$ in seconds"}

- $5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $13\,\mathrm{m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The velocity is $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at $t = 0$ and vanishes at $t = 3\,\mathrm{s}$, then becomes negative down to $-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at $t = 5\,\mathrm{s}$. The triangle above the axis has area $\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 3 \cdot 6 = 9$ and the one below has area $\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 2 \cdot 4 = 4$. The distance traveled is the sum of the areas taken as positive, so it equals $9\,\mathrm{m} + 4\,\mathrm{m} = 13\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $5\,\mathrm{m}$ is the displacement, that is, the difference of the two areas.
