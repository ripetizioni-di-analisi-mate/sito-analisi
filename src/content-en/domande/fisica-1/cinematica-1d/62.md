A cart starts from rest. Its acceleration along the track increases according to $a(t)=kt$. Given the elapsed time $t = 4\,\mathrm{s}$ and the displacement $d = 32\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the growth coefficient $k$?

- $1\,\mathrm{m/s^3}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s^3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s^3}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s^3}$

## Explanation

Integrating $a$ gives $v(t)=kt^2/2$. The displacement is the integral of this velocity from zero. In the stated units, the relation is $d = \dfrac{k \cdot t^{3}}{6}$. Solving for the unknown gives $k = \dfrac{6 \cdot d}{t^{3}}$. Substituting the given values yields $3\,\mathrm{m/s^3}$.
