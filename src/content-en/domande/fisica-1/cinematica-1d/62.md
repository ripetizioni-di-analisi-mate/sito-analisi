A stone is thrown vertically upward and caught again at the launch point. With the axis pointing upward and neglecting air resistance, which statement about the graph of the velocity $v(t)$, from the throw to the catch, is true?

- It is a segment of slope $-g$, and the area above the time axis is greater than the one below
- It consists of two segments, one decreasing down to zero and one increasing
- It is a parabolic arc with vertex at the instant the stone is at the highest point
- It is a segment of slope $-g$, and the areas above and below the time axis are equal

## Explanation

The velocity is $v(t) = v_0 - g\,t$, so the graph is a segment of slope $-g$, positive during the ascent and negative during the descent. The displacement between the throw and the catch is zero, because the stone returns to its starting point. The displacement is the signed area under the velocity graph, therefore the area of the triangle above the axis equals that of the triangle below. The two-segment graph describes the magnitude of the velocity, and the parabolic arc describes the height $y(t)$.
