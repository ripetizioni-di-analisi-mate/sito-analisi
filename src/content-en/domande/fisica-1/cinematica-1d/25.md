A car enters a straight tunnel $600\,\mathrm{m}$ long at a speed of $25\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and leaves it at a speed of $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$, with constant deceleration. How long does it stay in the tunnel?

- $24\,\mathrm{s}$
- $40\,\mathrm{s}$
- $60\,\mathrm{s}$
- $30\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

In uniformly accelerated motion the average velocity is the arithmetic mean of the initial and the final velocity, so
$$
v_m = \frac{25\,\mathrm{m/s} + 15\,\mathrm{m/s}}{2} = 20\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
Therefore the time spent in the tunnel is $\Delta t = \dfrac{600\,\mathrm{m}}{20\,\mathrm{m/s}} = 30\,\mathrm{s}$. The values $24\,\mathrm{s}$ and $40\,\mathrm{s}$ are obtained by using only one of the two velocities as the average velocity.
