From the roof of a building $20\,\mathrm{m}$ high a stone is thrown vertically downward with velocity $5{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Neglecting air resistance, after how much time does the stone hit the ground?

- $2{,}02\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2{,}59\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1{,}57\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4{,}00\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The $y$ axis is oriented upward with the origin at the ground, so $y_0 = 20\,\mathrm{m}$ and $v_0 = -5{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$, negative because the stone is thrown downward. The stone hits the ground when $y(t) = 0$, that is, when
$$
20 - 5{,}0\,t - 4{,}905\,t^2 = 0 .
$$
The positive solution is $t = \dfrac{-5{,}0 + \sqrt{25 + 392{,}4}}{9{,}81}\,\mathrm{s} = \dfrac{-5{,}0 + 20{,}4}{9{,}81}\,\mathrm{s} = 1{,}57\,\mathrm{s}$. The time is shorter than that of the fall from rest, $\sqrt{\dfrac{40}{9{,}81}}\,\mathrm{s} = 2{,}02\,\mathrm{s}$, because the stone starts with a velocity already directed downward.
