An object moves in a straight line with acceleration $a=-kv$ and positive velocity. The initial data are the initial speed $v = 14\,\mathrm{m/s}$, the final speed $u = 6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the damping coefficient $k = 2\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$. Change the damping coefficient to $1\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the ratio of the final value of $d$ to its initial value?

- $1$
- $4$
- $2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

Since $a=v\,dv/dx$, it follows that $dv/dx=-k$. The change in velocity is therefore $-kd$. The relation $d = \dfrac{- u + v}{k}$ gives $4\,\mathrm{m}$ before the change and $8\,\mathrm{m}$ after it. The ratio is $2$.
