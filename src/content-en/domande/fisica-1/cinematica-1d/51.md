A motorcyclist covers two equal distances along a straight road without stopping. Given the speed over the first stretch $a = 30\,\mathrm{km/h}$ and the speed over the second stretch $b = 60\,\mathrm{km/h}$, what is the average speed $v$?

- $40\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $45\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $20\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- $80\,\mathrm{km/h}$

## Explanation

The average speed is the total distance divided by the sum of the two travel times. In the stated units, the relation is $v = \dfrac{2 \cdot a \cdot b}{a + b}$. Substituting the given values yields $40\,\mathrm{km/h}$.
