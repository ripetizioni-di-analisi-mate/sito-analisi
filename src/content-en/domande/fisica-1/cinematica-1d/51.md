A point moves in uniform rectilinear motion along the $x$-axis. At the instant $t = 2\,\mathrm{s}$ it is at $x = -12\,\mathrm{m}$ and at the instant $t = 8\,\mathrm{s}$ it is at $x = 18\,\mathrm{m}$. What is its position function, with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds?

- $x(t) = 5t - 12$
- $x(t) = 5t - 22$
- $x(t) = 3t - 18$
- $x(t) = 6t - 24$

## Explanation

In uniform motion the velocity is constant and equals the average velocity over any interval, so
$$
v = \frac{18\,\mathrm{m} - (-12\,\mathrm{m})}{8\,\mathrm{s} - 2\,\mathrm{s}} = 5\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The position function passes through $(2, -12)$, therefore $x(t) = -12 + 5\,(t - 2) = 5t - 22$. Indeed $x(8) = 40 - 22 = 18$. The function $x(t) = 5t - 12$ has the right velocity but assigns the position $-12\,\mathrm{m}$ to the instant $t = 0$.
