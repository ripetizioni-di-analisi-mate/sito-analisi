A body starts from rest with constant acceleration and covers a stretch of length $d$ in the time $t_1$. In what time does it cover the same stretch, again starting from rest, if the acceleration is four times larger?

- $\dfrac{t_1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{t_1}{2}$
- $2\,t_1$
- $\dfrac{t_1}{16}$

## Explanation

Starting from rest one has $d = \dfrac{1}{2}\,a\,t^2$, so the time to cover the stretch is $t = \sqrt{\dfrac{2d}{a}}$. The time is inversely proportional to $\sqrt{a}$, therefore when the acceleration is quadrupled the time is divided by $\sqrt{4} = 2$. The new time is thus $\dfrac{t_1}{2}$.
