A point moves along the $x$ axis with equation of motion $x(t) = 2t^3 - t$, with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. What is the average velocity between the instants $t = 1\,\mathrm{s}$ and $t = 3\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $29\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $17\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $50\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $25\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The positions at the two instants are $x(1) = 2 - 1 = 1\,\mathrm{m}$ and $x(3) = 54 - 3 = 51\,\mathrm{m}$. The average velocity is the ratio of the displacement to the time interval, so
$$
v_m = \frac{51\,\mathrm{m} - 1\,\mathrm{m}}{3\,\mathrm{s} - 1\,\mathrm{s}} = 25\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The arithmetic mean of the instantaneous velocities $v(1) = 5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v(3) = 53\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is $29\,\mathrm{m/s}$, a different value, because the acceleration $a(t) = 12t$ is not constant.
