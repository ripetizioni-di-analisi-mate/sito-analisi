An elevator moving upward brakes uniformly until it stops. Given the initial speed $v = 12\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the braking distance $d = 24\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the magnitude of the acceleration $a$?

- $\dfrac{9}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The velocity-displacement equation gives $0=v^2-2ad$. In the stated units, the relation is $d = \dfrac{v^{2}}{2 \cdot a}$. Solving for the unknown gives $a = \dfrac{v^{2}}{2 \cdot d}$. Substituting the given values yields $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
