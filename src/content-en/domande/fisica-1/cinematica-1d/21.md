A car travels at the constant speed of $90\,\mathrm{km/h}$. How long does it take to cover $500\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $5{,}6\,\mathrm{s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1{,}5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $72\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The speed in SI units is $\dfrac{90}{3{,}6}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 25\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The motion is uniform, so the time needed for a displacement $\Delta x$ is $\Delta t = \dfrac{\Delta x}{v}$. Therefore $\Delta t = \dfrac{500\,\mathrm{m}}{25\,\mathrm{m/s}} = 20\,\mathrm{s}$.
