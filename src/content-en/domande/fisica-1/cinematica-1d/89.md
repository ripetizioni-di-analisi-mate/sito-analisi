A sensor measures $x(t)=x_0+bt^2+ct^3$ in SI units. At the stated time the object moves in the positive direction. Given the cubic coefficient $c = 2\,\mathrm{m/s^3}$, the measurement time $t = 2\,\mathrm{s}$ and the instantaneous velocity $v = 36\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the quadratic coefficient $b$?

- $5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $14\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Instantaneous velocity is the time derivative of $x$. In the stated units, the relation is $v = t \cdot \left(2 \cdot b + 3 \cdot c \cdot t\right)$. Solving for the unknown gives $b = \dfrac{- 3 \cdot c \cdot t^{2} + v}{2 \cdot t}$. Substituting the given values yields $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
