A point moves along the $x$ axis with equation of motion $x(t) = t^2 - 4t$, with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. What is the distance traveled between the instants $t = 0$ and $t = 6\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $12\,\mathrm{m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The velocity is $v(t) = 2t - 4$, which vanishes at $t = 2\,\mathrm{s}$; so at that instant the point reverses its motion. Between $0$ and $2\,\mathrm{s}$ the point goes from $x(0) = 0$ to $x(2) = -4\,\mathrm{m}$ and travels $4\,\mathrm{m}$. Between $2\,\mathrm{s}$ and $6\,\mathrm{s}$ it goes from $-4\,\mathrm{m}$ to $x(6) = 12\,\mathrm{m}$ and travels $16\,\mathrm{m}$. Therefore the distance traveled is $4\,\mathrm{m} + 16\,\mathrm{m} = 20\,\mathrm{m}$, whereas the displacement is $12\,\mathrm{m}$.
