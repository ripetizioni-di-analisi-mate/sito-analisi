The position of a cart on a straight track is $x(t) = t^3$, with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. What is the average acceleration between the instants $0$ and $2\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $12\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The velocity is the derivative of the position function, $v(t) = 3t^2$, so $v(0) = 0$ and $v(2) = 12\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The average acceleration is the ratio of the change in velocity to the time interval, therefore
$$
a_m = \frac{12\,\mathrm{m/s} - 0}{2\,\mathrm{s}} = 6\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
The value $12\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ is the instantaneous acceleration $a(t) = 6t$ at the instant $2\,\mathrm{s}$, and $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is the average velocity, which is not an acceleration.
