A cart speeds up and its velocity-time graph is a straight line segment entirely above the time axis. Given the final speed $v = 10\,\mathrm{m/s}$, the time interval $t = 3\,\mathrm{s}$ and the distance traveled $d = 21\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the initial speed $u$?

- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The distance is the area of the trapezoid under the velocity-time graph. In the stated units, the relation is $d = \dfrac{t \cdot \left(u + v\right)}{2}$. Solving for the unknown gives $u = \dfrac{2 \cdot d}{t} - v$. Substituting the given values yields $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
