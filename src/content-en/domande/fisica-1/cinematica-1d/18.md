A car goes from $36\,\mathrm{km/h}$ to $108\,\mathrm{km/h}$ in $8{,}0\,\mathrm{s}$. What is its average acceleration?

- $9{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2{,}5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3{,}8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $1{,}3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The velocities in SI units are obtained by dividing by $3{,}6$, so $v_1 = 10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_2 = 30\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The average acceleration is the ratio of the change in velocity to the time interval, therefore
$$
a_m = \frac{30\,\mathrm{m/s} - 10\,\mathrm{m/s}}{8{,}0\,\mathrm{s}} = 2{,}5\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
The number $9{,}0$ is obtained by dividing $72\,\mathrm{km/h}$ by $8{,}0\,\mathrm{s}$ without converting the units.
