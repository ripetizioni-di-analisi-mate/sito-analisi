A point moves along the $x$ axis with equation of motion $x(t) = t^3 - 3t^2 - 9t + 4$, with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds, for $t \geq 0$. At what position does the point reverse direction?

- $-23\,\mathrm{m}$
- $23\,\mathrm{m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The velocity is the derivative of the equation of motion, $v(t) = 3t^2 - 6t - 9 = 3\,(t - 3)(t + 1)$. For $t \geq 0$ the velocity vanishes only at $t = 3\,\mathrm{s}$, it is negative for $0 \leq t < 3\,\mathrm{s}$ and positive for $t > 3\,\mathrm{s}$; so at $t = 3\,\mathrm{s}$ the point reverses its motion. The position at that instant is $x(3) = 27 - 27 - 27 + 4 = -23\,\mathrm{m}$. The root $t = -1$ is not acceptable, because the motion is described for $t \geq 0$.
