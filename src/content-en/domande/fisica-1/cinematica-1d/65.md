The graph shows the position function $x(t)$ of a point in rectilinear motion, made of three straight pieces, with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. In which time interval does the velocity of the point have the largest magnitude?

::grafico{f="min(3*x,6,26-4*x)+0*sqrt(x*(7-x))" punti="0,0,pieno;2,6,pieno;5,6,pieno;7,-2,pieno" segmenti="2,0,2,6;5,0,5,6;7,0,7,-2" xmin=-1 xmax=8 ymin=-4 ymax=8 nomi="t,x" titolo="Position function $x(t)$ in meters, with $t$ in seconds"}

- Between $5\,\mathrm{s}$ and $7\,\mathrm{s}$
- Between $0$ and $2\,\mathrm{s}$
- Between $2\,\mathrm{s}$ and $5\,\mathrm{s}$
- The magnitude of the velocity is the same in the three intervals

## Explanation

The velocity in each piece is the slope of the corresponding segment. Between $0$ and $2\,\mathrm{s}$ the position goes from $0$ to $6\,\mathrm{m}$, so $v = 3\,\mathrm{m/s}$; between $2$ and $5\,\mathrm{s}$ the position stays at $6\,\mathrm{m}$, so the point is at rest. Between $5$ and $7\,\mathrm{s}$ the position goes from $6\,\mathrm{m}$ to $-2\,\mathrm{m}$, therefore $v = \dfrac{-8\,\mathrm{m}}{2\,\mathrm{s}} = -4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The largest magnitude, $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$, therefore occurs in the last interval, even though the velocity is negative.
