A cart starts from rest. Its acceleration along the track increases according to $a(t)=kt$. The initial data are the growth coefficient $k = 3\,\mathrm{m/s^3}$ and the elapsed time $t = 4\,\mathrm{s}$. Change the growth coefficient to $6\,\mathrm{m/s^3}$ and the elapsed time to $2\,\mathrm{s}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the final value of $d$ minus its initial value?

- $-48\,\mathrm{m}$
- $24\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-24\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-72\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Integrating $a$ gives $v(t)=kt^2/2$. The displacement is the integral of this velocity from zero. The relation $d = \dfrac{k \cdot t^{3}}{6}$ gives $32\,\mathrm{m}$ before the change and $8\,\mathrm{m}$ after it. Subtracting the initial value from the final value gives $-24\,\mathrm{m}$.
