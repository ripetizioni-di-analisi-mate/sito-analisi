An object is thrown upward and reaches its highest point before falling. Air resistance is negligible. Given the initial speed $v = 18\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the ascent time $t = \dfrac{9}{5}\,\mathrm{s}$, what is the rise to the highest point $h$?

- $\dfrac{81}{10}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{81}{5}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{243}{10}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{162}{5}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

On the way up the velocity decreases linearly to zero, so its average is half the initial value. In the stated units, the relation is $h = \dfrac{t \cdot v}{2}$. Substituting the given values yields $\dfrac{81}{5}\,\mathrm{m}$.
