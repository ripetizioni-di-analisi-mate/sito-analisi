At the instant $t = 0$ a cart is at the position $x_0 = -3\,\mathrm{m}$ of a straight track, and the graph shows its velocity $v(t)$ up to the instant $6\,\mathrm{s}$, with $v$ in $\mathrm{m/s}$ and $t$ in seconds. Where is the cart at the instant $t = 6\,\mathrm{s}$?

::grafico{f="min(4,6-x)+0*sqrt(x*(6-x))" punti="0,4,pieno;2,4,pieno;6,0,pieno" segmenti="2,0,2,4" xmin=-1 xmax=7 ymin=-1 ymax=6 nomi="t,v" titolo="Velocity $v(t)$ of the cart in $\mathrm{m/s}$, with $t$ in seconds"}

- $16\,\mathrm{m}$
- $13\,\mathrm{m}$
- $19\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The displacement between $0$ and $6\,\mathrm{s}$ is the area under the velocity graph, which is never negative. Between $0$ and $2\,\mathrm{s}$ the region is a rectangle of area $4 \cdot 2 = 8$, between $2\,\mathrm{s}$ and $6\,\mathrm{s}$ it is a triangle of area $\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 4 \cdot 4 = 8$, so the displacement is $\Delta x = 16\,\mathrm{m}$. The final position is the initial position plus the displacement, therefore $x(6) = -3\,\mathrm{m} + 16\,\mathrm{m} = 13\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $16\,\mathrm{m}$ is the displacement alone, $19\,\mathrm{m}$ is obtained by adding $3\,\mathrm{m}$ instead of subtracting them, and $5\,\mathrm{m}$ accounts for the first piece only.
