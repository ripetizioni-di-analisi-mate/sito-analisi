A ball is thrown vertically from the ground. It passes the same height at times $t_1$ and $t_2$ before returning to the ground. Take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and neglect air resistance. Given the upward crossing time $t_{1} = 2\,\mathrm{s}$ and the downward crossing time $t_{2} = 6\,\mathrm{s}$, what is the height of the crossings $h$?

- $30\,\mathrm{m}$
- $120\,\mathrm{m}$
- $60\,\mathrm{m}$
- $80\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The two times are the roots of $\dfrac{g}{2}t^2-v_0t+h=0$. Their product is $2h/g$. In the stated units, the relation is $h = 5 \cdot t_{1} \cdot t_{2}$. Substituting the given values yields $60\,\mathrm{m}$.
