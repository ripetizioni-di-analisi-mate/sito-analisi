A car $A$ passes the origin of a straight road at $25\,\mathrm{m/s}$; at the same instant a car $B$ is $200\,\mathrm{m}$ ahead and travels in the same direction at $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Both keep their velocity constant. After how much time does $A$ catch up with $B$?

- $20\,\mathrm{s}$
- $5{,}0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $8{,}0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $13\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

With the origin at the point where $A$ passes and the positive direction along the motion, the equations of motion are $x_A(t) = 25\,t$ and $x_B(t) = 200 + 15\,t$, with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. Car $A$ catches up with $B$ when $x_A(t) = x_B(t)$, that is, when $25\,t = 200 + 15\,t$. So $10\,t = 200$ and $t = 20\,\mathrm{s}$, and the meeting takes place at $x = 500\,\mathrm{m}$.
