An object is thrown upward and reaches its highest point before falling. Air resistance is negligible. Given the initial speed $v = 18\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the rise to the highest point $h = \dfrac{81}{5}\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the ascent time $t$?

- $\dfrac{27}{10}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $\dfrac{9}{10}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $\dfrac{18}{5}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $\dfrac{9}{5}\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

On the way up the velocity decreases linearly to zero, so its average is half the initial value. In the stated units, the relation is $h = \dfrac{t \cdot v}{2}$. Solving for the unknown gives $t = \dfrac{2 \cdot h}{v}$. Substituting the given values yields $\dfrac{9}{5}\,\mathrm{s}$.
