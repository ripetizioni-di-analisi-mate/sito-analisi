Two cars $A$ and $B$ start from the same position at the instant $t = 0$ and move in the same direction; the graph shows their velocities, with $v$ in $\mathrm{m/s}$ and $t$ in seconds. Which statement is true at the instant $t = 10\,\mathrm{s}$?

::grafico{f="6+0*sqrt(x*(10-x));1.2*x+0*sqrt(x*(10-x))" legenda="v_A;v_B" segmenti="10,0,10,12" punti="10,12,pieno;10,6,pieno" xmin=-2 xmax=12 ymin=-2 ymax=14 nomi="t,v" titolo="Velocities of the cars $A$ and $B$ in $\mathrm{m/s}$, with $t$ in seconds"}

- $B$ is ahead of $A$, because at that instant it is faster
- $A$ is $60\,\mathrm{m}$ ahead of $B$
- $A$ and $B$ have the same velocity
- $A$ and $B$ are at the same position

## Explanation

The distance covered by each car is the area under its velocity graph. For $A$ the area is the rectangle $10 \cdot 6 = 60$, for $B$ it is the triangle $\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 10 \cdot 12 = 60$, so both have covered $60\,\mathrm{m}$. Since they start from the same position, at the instant $10\,\mathrm{s}$ they are at the same point. The velocities are equal at the instant $5\,\mathrm{s}$, when $A$ has its largest lead, while at $10\,\mathrm{s}$ the velocity of $B$ is twice that of $A$.
