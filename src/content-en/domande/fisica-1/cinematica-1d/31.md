A ball is thrown vertically upward from the ground with velocity $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Neglecting air resistance, with what velocity, in magnitude, does it pass the height of $10\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $20.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $11.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5.37\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $28.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

With the $y$ axis upward and the origin at the ground the acceleration is $-g$, so the relation between velocity and displacement gives $v^2 = v_0^2 - 2g\,y$. At the height $y = 10\,\mathrm{m}$ one has
$$
v^2 = 225 - 2 \cdot 9.81 \cdot 10 = 28.8\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2} ,
$$
therefore $|v| = 5.37\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The magnitude is the same on the way up and on the way down, because the motion is symmetric with respect to the instant at which the ball reaches the maximum height.
