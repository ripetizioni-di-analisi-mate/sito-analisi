A moped starts from rest and reaches the speed of $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in $12\,\mathrm{s}$ with constant acceleration. What distance does it travel in this time?

- $360\,\mathrm{m}$
- $90\,\mathrm{m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m}$
- $180\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The acceleration is $a = \dfrac{\Delta v}{\Delta t} = \dfrac{30\,\mathrm{m/s}}{12\,\mathrm{s}} = 2{,}5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Since the moped starts from rest, the equation of motion is $x(t) = \dfrac{1}{2}\,a\,t^2$, so
$$
x = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 2{,}5\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot (12\,\mathrm{s})^2 = 180\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The same result is obtained from the average velocity, which in uniformly accelerated motion is $\dfrac{0 + 30}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 15\,\mathrm{m/s}$, multiplied by $12\,\mathrm{s}$.
