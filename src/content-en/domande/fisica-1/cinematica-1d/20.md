The position–time graph $x(t)$ of a point moving along a straight line is a parabola concave downward with vertex at the instant $t = 3\,\mathrm{s}$. Which statement is true?

- At the instant $t = 3\,\mathrm{s}$ the velocity and the acceleration are both zero
- At the instant $t = 3\,\mathrm{s}$ the velocity is maximum and the acceleration is zero
- The velocity is constant and the acceleration is negative
- At the instant $t = 3\,\mathrm{s}$ the velocity is zero and the acceleration is negative

## Explanation

A parabolic position–time graph corresponds to uniformly accelerated motion, so the acceleration is constant. The parabola is concave downward, therefore the acceleration is negative at every instant, including $t = 3\,\mathrm{s}$. At the vertex the tangent to the graph is horizontal, so the velocity is zero and the point reverses the orientation of its motion.
