A flowerpot falls from rest from a windowsill and hits the sidewalk with speed $14\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Neglecting air resistance, from what height did it fall?

- $20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5.0\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

In a fall from rest the relation between velocity and displacement gives $v^2 = 2\,g\,h$. So
$$
h = \frac{v^2}{2g} = \frac{(14\,\mathrm{m/s})^2}{2 \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}} = 10\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $20\,\mathrm{m}$ is obtained by forgetting the factor $2$ in the denominator.
