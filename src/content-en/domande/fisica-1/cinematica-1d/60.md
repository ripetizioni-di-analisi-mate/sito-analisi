In a stroboscopic photograph of a freely falling drop, three consecutive downward positions are $0$, $12$ and $34\,\mathrm{cm}$. The time between flashes is constant. Which flash frequency is consistent with $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$?

- $5\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $20\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $10\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $100\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

Over the two equal intervals the distances increase from $12$ to $22\,\mathrm{cm}$. Under constant acceleration the difference is $g(\Delta t)^2$. With a difference of $0.10\,\mathrm m$ the interval is $0.10\,\mathrm s$ and the frequency is $10\,\mathrm{Hz}$.
