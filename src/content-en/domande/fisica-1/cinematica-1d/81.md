A cart speeds up and its velocity-time graph is a straight line entirely above the time axis. Given the initial speed $u = 4\,\mathrm{m/s}$, the time interval $t = 3\,\mathrm{s}$ and the distance traveled $d = 21\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the final speed $v$?

- $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The distance is the area of the trapezoid under the velocity-time graph. In the stated units, the relation is $d = \dfrac{t \cdot \left(u + v\right)}{2}$. Solving for the unknown gives $v = \dfrac{2 \cdot d}{t} - u$. Substituting the given values yields $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
