A conveyor starts from rest. Its acceleration is $+2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ for one second and then $-2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ for one second. How would reversing the order of the intervals change its final displacement?

- It would change from $+2\,\mathrm m$ to $-2\,\mathrm m$.
- It would remain zero.
- It would double in the same direction.
- It would remain $+2\,\mathrm m$.

## Explanation

In the original order the velocity rises from zero to $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and returns to zero. The velocity–time triangle has area $2\,\mathrm m$. Reversing the intervals puts the triangle below the time axis.
