A point moves along the $x$ axis with velocity $v(t) = 3t^2 - 2t$, with $v$ in $\mathrm{m/s}$ and $t$ in seconds, and at the instant $t = 0$ it is at $x_0 = 5\,\mathrm{m}$. At what position is it at the instant $t = 2\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $13\,\mathrm{m}$
- $17\,\mathrm{m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The equation of motion is obtained by integrating the velocity with the initial condition $x(0) = 5\,\mathrm{m}$, so
$$
x(t) = 5 + \int_0^t \left(3s^2 - 2s\right)ds = 5 + t^3 - t^2 .
$$
At the instant $t = 2\,\mathrm{s}$ one has $x(2) = 5 + 8 - 4 = 9\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $4\,\mathrm{m}$ is the displacement between $0$ and $2\,\mathrm{s}$, which is added to the initial position.
