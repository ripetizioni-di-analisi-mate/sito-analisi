A sensor measures $x(t)=x_0+bt^2+ct^3$ in SI units. At the stated time the object moves in the positive direction. Given the quadratic coefficient $b = 3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, the measurement time $t = 2\,\mathrm{s}$ and the instantaneous velocity $v = 36\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the cubic coefficient $c$?

- $3\,\mathrm{m/s^3}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s^3}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s^3}$
- $\dfrac{15}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s^3}$

## Explanation

Instantaneous velocity is the time derivative of $x$. In the stated units, the relation is $v = t \cdot \left(2 \cdot b + 3 \cdot c \cdot t\right)$. Solving for the unknown gives $c = \dfrac{- 2 \cdot b \cdot t + v}{3 \cdot t^{2}}$. Substituting the given values yields $2\,\mathrm{m/s^3}$.
