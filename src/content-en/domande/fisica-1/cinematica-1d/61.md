A cart starts from rest. Its acceleration along the track increases according to $a(t)=kt$. Given the growth coefficient $k = 3\,\mathrm{m/s^3}$ and the elapsed time $t = 4\,\mathrm{s}$, what is the displacement $d$?

- $96\,\mathrm{m}$
- $64\,\mathrm{m}$
- $32\,\mathrm{m}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Integrating $a$ gives $v(t)=kt^2/2$. The displacement is the integral of this velocity from zero. In the stated units, the relation is $d = \dfrac{k \cdot t^{3}}{6}$. Substituting the given values yields $32\,\mathrm{m}$.
