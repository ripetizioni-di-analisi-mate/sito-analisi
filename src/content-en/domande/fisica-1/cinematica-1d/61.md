From the edge of a terrace two stones are thrown with velocities of the same magnitude, the first vertically upward and the second vertically downward. Neglecting air resistance, which statement is true?

- The two stones hit the ground with velocities of the same magnitude
- The first stone hits the ground with a velocity of greater magnitude
- The second stone hits the ground with a velocity of greater magnitude
- The two stones hit the ground at the same instant

## Explanation

For a vertical throw from a height $y_0$ the velocity on reaching the ground satisfies $v^2 = v_0^2 + 2\,g\,y_0$. The formula contains only the square of the initial velocity, so the direction of the throw does not matter and the two magnitudes are equal. Indeed the first stone passes the terrace again with velocity $-v_0$, and from then on it moves exactly like the second. The first stone arrives later, however, because it takes the time $\dfrac{2\,v_0}{g}$ to rise and return to the terrace.
