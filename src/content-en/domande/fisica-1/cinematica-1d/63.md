A cart starts from rest. Its acceleration along the track increases according to $a(t)=kt$. Given the growth coefficient $k = 3\,\mathrm{m/s^3}$ and the displacement $d = 32\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the elapsed time $t$?

- $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Integrating $a$ gives $v(t)=kt^2/2$. The displacement is the integral of this velocity from zero. In the stated units, the relation is $d = \dfrac{k \cdot t^{3}}{6}$. Solving for the unknown gives $t = \dfrac{\sqrt[3]{6} \cdot \sqrt[3]{d}}{\sqrt[3]{k}}$. Substituting the given values yields $4\,\mathrm{s}$.
