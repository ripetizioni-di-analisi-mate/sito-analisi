A point starts from rest at the origin of the $x$ axis with acceleration $a(t) = 4 - 2t$, with $a$ in $\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and $t$ in seconds. What is its velocity at the instant $t = 3\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The velocity is obtained by integrating the acceleration with the initial condition $v(0) = 0$, so
$$
v(t) = \int_0^t (4 - 2s)\,ds = 4t - t^2 .
$$
At the instant $t = 3\,\mathrm{s}$ one has $v(3) = 12 - 9 = 3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. At that instant the acceleration is $a(3) = -2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, with sign opposite to the velocity, therefore the point is slowing down.
