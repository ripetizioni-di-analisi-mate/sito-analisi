On a test bench, a piston starts from rest at the position $x = 0$ and is given the acceleration $a(t) = 2 \cos t$, with $a$ in $\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and $t$ in seconds. Where is it at the instant $t = \pi\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Integrating the acceleration with the condition $v(0) = 0$ gives $v(t) = \displaystyle\int_0^t 2 \cos s\,ds = 2 \sin t$. Integrating the velocity with the condition $x(0) = 0$ gives
$$
x(t) = \int_0^t 2 \sin s\,ds = 2\,(1 - \cos t) .
$$
So $x(\pi) = 2\,(1 - \cos \pi) = 4\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $2\,\mathrm{m}$ is obtained by writing the antiderivative $-2\cos t$ without imposing the initial condition on the position.
