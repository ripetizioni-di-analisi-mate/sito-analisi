Two carts move in the same direction at constant speeds. The faster cart starts behind the other. Given the speed of the first cart $v = 9\,\mathrm{m/s}$, the speed of the second cart $u = 3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the catch-up time $t = 6\,\mathrm{s}$, what is the initial separation $d$?

- $36\,\mathrm{m}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m}$
- $72\,\mathrm{m}$
- $54\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The separation decreases at the relative speed $v-u$. In the stated units, the relation is $t = - \dfrac{d}{u - v}$. Solving for the unknown gives $d = t \cdot \left(- u + v\right)$. Substituting the given values yields $36\,\mathrm{m}$.
