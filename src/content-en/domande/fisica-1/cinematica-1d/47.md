The graph shows the position function $x(t)$ of a point in rectilinear motion, with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds; the points $A$ and $B$ correspond to the instants $t = 1\,\mathrm{s}$ and $t = 5\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the average velocity between these two instants?

::grafico{f="x^2-2*x+0*sqrt(x*(6-x))" punti="1,-1,pieno,A;5,15,pieno,B" segmenti="1,0,1,-1;5,0,5,15;0,15,5,15;0,-1,1,-1" xmin=-1 xmax=7 ymin=-3 ymax=20 nomi="t,x" titolo="Position function $x(t)$, with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds; $A = (1, -1)$ and $B = (5, 15)$"}

- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

From the graph one reads the positions $x(1) = -1\,\mathrm{m}$ and $x(5) = 15\,\mathrm{m}$. The average velocity is the slope of the secant line through $A$ and $B$, so
$$
v_m = \frac{x(5) - x(1)}{5\,\mathrm{s} - 1\,\mathrm{s}} = \frac{15\,\mathrm{m} - (-1\,\mathrm{m})}{4\,\mathrm{s}} = 4\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The value $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is the slope of the tangent line at $B$, that is, the instantaneous velocity at the instant $t = 5\,\mathrm{s}$.
