A highway route is $120\,\mathrm{km}$ long. How many minutes are saved by covering it at the constant speed of $120\,\mathrm{km/h}$ instead of $100\,\mathrm{km/h}$?

- $20\,\mathrm{min}$
- $12\,\mathrm{min}$
- $6.0\,\mathrm{min}$
- $24\,\mathrm{min}$

## Explanation

In uniform motion the time taken is $\Delta t = \dfrac{d}{v}$. At $100\,\mathrm{km/h}$ it takes $\dfrac{120\,\mathrm{km}}{100\,\mathrm{km/h}} = 1.2\,\mathrm{h} = 72\,\mathrm{min}$, while at $120\,\mathrm{km/h}$ it takes $1.0\,\mathrm{h} = 60\,\mathrm{min}$. Therefore the time saved is $72\,\mathrm{min} - 60\,\mathrm{min} = 12\,\mathrm{min}$.
