A robot travels forward and then covers a shorter distance backward on the same track. The direction of its first leg is positive. Given the distance of the first leg $a = 28\,\mathrm{m}$, the return distance $b = 12\,\mathrm{m}$ and the total time $t = 8\,\mathrm{s}$, what is the average velocity $v$?

- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The displacement is the difference between the two distances. Average velocity is displacement divided by total time. In the stated units, the relation is $v = \dfrac{a - b}{t}$. Substituting the given values yields $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
