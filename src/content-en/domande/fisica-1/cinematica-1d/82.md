A small ball is pushed along a straight tube and leaves the inlet, located at $x = 0$, with velocity $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at the instant $t = 0$. In the tube its acceleration is $a(t) = -6t$, with $a$ in $\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and $t$ in seconds. At what instant does the ball return to the inlet?

- $1.7\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3.0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.4\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Integrating the acceleration with the condition $v(0) = 3$ gives $v(t) = 3 - 3t^2$, and integrating the velocity with the condition $x(0) = 0$ gives $x(t) = 3t - t^3$. The ball is at the inlet when
$$
3t - t^3 = t\,(3 - t^2) = 0 ,
$$
so, besides the initial instant, at the instant $t = \sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{s} = 1.7\,\mathrm{s}$. At the instant $1.0\,\mathrm{s}$ the velocity vanishes and the ball reverses its motion, at the position $x = 2\,\mathrm{m}$.
