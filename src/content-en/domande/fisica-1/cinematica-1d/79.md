A cart speeds up and its velocity-time graph is a straight line entirely above the time axis. Given the initial speed $u = 4\,\mathrm{m/s}$, the final speed $v = 10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the time interval $t = 3\,\mathrm{s}$, what is the distance traveled $d$?

- $21\,\mathrm{m}$
- $42\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{21}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The distance is the area of the trapezoid under the velocity-time graph. In the stated units, the relation is $d = \dfrac{t \cdot \left(u + v\right)}{2}$. Substituting the given values yields $21\,\mathrm{m}$.
