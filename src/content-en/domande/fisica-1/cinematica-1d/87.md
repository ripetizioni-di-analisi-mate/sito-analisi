A projectile moves in a straight line in a medium that slows it down with acceleration $a = -c\,v^2$, with $c = 0.020\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$. It enters the medium with speed $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is its speed after traveling $50\,\mathrm{m}$ in the medium?

- $19\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $7.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.7\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Since the velocity stays positive, $v$ can be regarded as a function of $x$, and the chain rule gives $a = v\,\dfrac{dv}{dx}$. So $v\,\dfrac{dv}{dx} = -c\,v^2$, that is $\dfrac{dv}{dx} = -c\,v$, and therefore the velocity decreases exponentially with distance,
$$
v = v_0\,e^{-c\,x} = 20\,\mathrm{m/s} \cdot e^{-0.020 \cdot 50} = 20\,e^{-1}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 7.4\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The value $19\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is obtained by wrongly applying the relation $v = v_0 - c\,x$, which holds for the law $a = -k\,v$ with $k$ in place of $c$.
