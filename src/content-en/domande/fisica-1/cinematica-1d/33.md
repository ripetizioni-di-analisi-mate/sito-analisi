Two stones are dropped from rest; the second starts from a height four times that of the first. Neglecting air resistance, what is the ratio of the fall time of the second to that of the first?

- $4$
- $2$
- $16$
- $\sqrt{2}$

## Explanation

The fall time from rest from a height $h$ is $t = \sqrt{\dfrac{2h}{g}}$, so it is proportional to $\sqrt{h}$. If the height becomes $4h$, the time becomes $\sqrt{4} = 2$ times the previous one. The mass of the stones plays no role, because the acceleration in free fall is the same for every body.
