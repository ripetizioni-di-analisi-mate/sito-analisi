An object moves in a straight line with acceleration $a=-kv$ and positive velocity. Given the initial speed $v = 14\,\mathrm{m/s}$, the final speed $u = 6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the damping coefficient $k = 2\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$, what is the distance traveled $d$?

- $10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Since $a=v\,dv/dx$, it follows that $dv/dx=-k$. The change in velocity is therefore $-kd$. In the stated units, the relation is $d = \dfrac{- u + v}{k}$. Substituting the given values yields $4\,\mathrm{m}$.
