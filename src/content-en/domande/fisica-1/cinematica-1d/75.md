The graph shows the position function $x(t)$ of a point in rectilinear motion, with four highlighted points $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$. At which of these points is the velocity negative and the acceleration positive?

::grafico{f="x^3-6*x^2+9*x+1+0*sqrt(x*(4.3-x))" punti="0.5,4.125,pieno,A;1.5,4.375,pieno,B;2.5,1.625,pieno,C;3.7,2.813,pieno,D" xmin=-0.5 xmax=5 ymin=-1 ymax=7 nomi="t,x" titolo="Position function $x(t)$ with the points $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$"}

- At $A$
- At $B$
- At $D$
- At $C$

## Explanation

The velocity is the slope of the position graph and the sign of the acceleration gives the concavity. The velocity is negative where the graph decreases, that is at $B$ and at $C$. At $B$ the graph is concave down, so $a < 0$; at $C$ it is concave up, so $a > 0$. Therefore the required point is $C$. At $A$ the graph increases and is concave down, and at $D$ it increases and is concave up.
