An object moves in a straight line with acceleration $a=-kv$ and positive velocity. Given the final speed $u = 6\,\mathrm{m/s}$, the damping coefficient $k = 2\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$ and the distance traveled $d = 4\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the initial speed $v$?

- $14\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $22\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $28\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Since $a=v\,dv/dx$, it follows that $dv/dx=-k$. The change in velocity is therefore $-kd$. In the stated units, the relation is $d = \dfrac{- u + v}{k}$. Solving for the unknown gives $v = d \cdot k + u$. Substituting the given values yields $14\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
