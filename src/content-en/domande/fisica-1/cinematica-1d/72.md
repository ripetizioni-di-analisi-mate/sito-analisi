A ball is thrown vertically from the ground. It passes the same height at times $t_1$ and $t_2$ before coming back down. Take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and neglect air resistance. The initial data are the upward crossing time $t_{1} = 2\,\mathrm{s}$ and the downward crossing time $t_{2} = 6\,\mathrm{s}$. Change the upward crossing time to $3\,\mathrm{s}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the ratio of the final value of $h$ to its initial value?

- $\dfrac{9}{16}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$

## Explanation

The two times are the roots of $\dfrac{g}{2}t^2-v_0t+h=0$. Their product is $2h/g$. The relation $h = 5 \cdot t_{1} \cdot t_{2}$ gives $60\,\mathrm{m}$ before the change and $90\,\mathrm{m}$ after it. The ratio is $\dfrac{3}{2}$.
