For $t \geq 0$ the position of a slider along a track is $x(t) = 2t^3 - 15t^2 + 24t$, with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. In which time intervals is the motion decelerated, that is, does the magnitude of the velocity decrease?

- For $0 < t < 1\,\mathrm{s}$ and for $2.5\,\mathrm{s} < t < 4\,\mathrm{s}$
- For $1\,\mathrm{s} < t < 4\,\mathrm{s}$
- For $0 < t < 2.5\,\mathrm{s}$
- For $1\,\mathrm{s} < t < 2.5\,\mathrm{s}$ and for $t > 4\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The velocity is $v(t) = 6t^2 - 30t + 24 = 6\,(t - 1)(t - 4)$, positive for $0 < t < 1$ and for $t > 4$, negative for $1 < t < 4$. The acceleration is $a(t) = 12t - 30$, negative for $t < 2.5$ and positive for $t > 2.5$. The motion is decelerated where $v$ and $a$ have opposite signs, so for $0 < t < 1\,\mathrm{s}$, where $v > 0$ and $a < 0$, and for $2.5\,\mathrm{s} < t < 4\,\mathrm{s}$, where $v < 0$ and $a > 0$. In the intervals $1 < t < 2.5$ and $t > 4$ the signs agree, therefore there the motion is accelerated.
