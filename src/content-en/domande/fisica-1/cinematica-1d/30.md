A stone is dropped from rest and falls for $3.0\,\mathrm{s}$ without hitting the ground. Neglecting air resistance, what distance does it travel?

- $44.1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $88.3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $29.4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $14.7\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

By the law of falling bodies the motion is uniformly accelerated with acceleration of magnitude $g$, so starting from rest the distance traveled in the time $t$ is $\dfrac{1}{2}\,g\,t^2$. One obtains
$$
\frac{1}{2} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot (3.0\,\mathrm{s})^2 = 4.905 \cdot 9\,\mathrm{m} = 44.1\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The number $29.4$ is the magnitude of the velocity at that instant, $g\,t$, which is measured in $\mathrm{m/s}$.
