A stone is dropped from rest into a well, and the sound of its impact with the water is heard $5.0\,\mathrm{s}$ after the instant the stone was released. Sound travels at $340\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and air resistance is negligible. How deep is the well, down to the water surface?

- $123\,\mathrm{m}$
- $61\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.7\,\mathrm{km}$
- $108\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The time of $5.0\,\mathrm{s}$ is the sum of the fall time $t_c$ and the time $t_s$ taken by the sound to travel back up. The depth $h$ satisfies $h = \dfrac{1}{2}\,g\,t_c^2$ and $h = 340\,\mathrm{m/s} \cdot t_s$, so, with $t_s = 5.0\,\mathrm{s} - t_c$,
$$
4.905\,t_c^2 + 340\,t_c - 1700 = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
t_c = 4.68\,\mathrm{s} .
$$
Therefore $h = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot (4.68\,\mathrm{s})^2 = 108\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $123\,\mathrm{m}$ is obtained by neglecting the travel time of the sound, which is about $0.32\,\mathrm{s}$.
