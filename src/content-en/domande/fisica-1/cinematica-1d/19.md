A point moves along the $x$ axis with equation of motion $x(t) = t^3 - 6t^2 + 2$, with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds, for $t \geq 0$. At what instant is the acceleration zero?

- $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The velocity is $v(t) = 3t^2 - 12t$ and the acceleration, its derivative, is $a(t) = 6t - 12$. The acceleration vanishes for $6t - 12 = 0$, that is, at the instant $t = 2\,\mathrm{s}$. At that instant the position–time graph changes concavity, from downward to upward. The instant $t = 4\,\mathrm{s}$ is the one at which the velocity vanishes and the point reverses its motion.
