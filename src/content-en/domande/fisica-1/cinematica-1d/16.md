A point moves along the $x$ axis with equation of motion $x(t) = 5 + 3t - 2t^2$, with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. What is the instantaneous velocity at the instant $t = 2\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $11\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The instantaneous velocity is the derivative of the equation of motion, so $v(t) = 3 - 4t$. At the instant $t = 2\,\mathrm{s}$ one has $v(2) = 3 - 8 = -5\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and the negative sign indicates that at that instant the point moves in the negative orientation of the axis. The value $-1\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is the average velocity between $0$ and $2\,\mathrm{s}$, that is, $\dfrac{x(2) - x(0)}{2\,\mathrm{s}} = \dfrac{3\,\mathrm{m} - 5\,\mathrm{m}}{2\,\mathrm{s}}$.
