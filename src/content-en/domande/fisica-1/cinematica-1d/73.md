The position function of a point is $x(t) = \alpha\,t^3 + \beta\,t$, with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. In which unit is the coefficient $\alpha$ measured?

- $\mathrm{m/s^3}$
- $\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $\mathrm{m \cdot s^3}$
- $\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The terms of a sum must have the same unit, so $\alpha\,t^3$ is measured in meters like $x$. Since $t^3$ is measured in $\mathrm{s^3}$, the coefficient $\alpha$ is measured in $\mathrm{m/s^3}$. In the same way $\beta$ is measured in $\mathrm{m/s}$, and it is the velocity at the instant $t = 0$.
