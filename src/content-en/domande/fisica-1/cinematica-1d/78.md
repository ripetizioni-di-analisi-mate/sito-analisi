An object moves in a straight line with acceleration $a=-kv$ and positive velocity. The initial data are the initial speed $v = 14\,\mathrm{m/s}$, the final speed $u = 6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the damping coefficient $k = 2\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$. Change the initial speed to $18\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the final speed to $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the final value of $d$ minus its initial value?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Since $a=v\,dv/dx$, it follows that $dv/dx=-k$. The change in velocity is therefore $-kd$. The relation $d = \dfrac{- u + v}{k}$ gives $4\,\mathrm{m}$ before the change and $5\,\mathrm{m}$ after it. Subtracting the initial value from the final value gives $1\,\mathrm{m}$.
