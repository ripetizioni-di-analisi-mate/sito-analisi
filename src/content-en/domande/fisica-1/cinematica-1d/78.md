The acceleration of an electric car starting from rest grows in the first seconds according to the law $a(t) = 3\sqrt{t}$, with $a$ in $\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and $t$ in seconds. What is the velocity of the car at the instant $t = 4\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $24\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $36\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The velocity is obtained by integrating the acceleration with the condition $v(0) = 0$, so
$$
v(4) = \int_0^4 3\,t^{\frac{1}{2}}\,dt = \left[2\,t^{\frac{3}{2}}\right]_0^4 = 2 \cdot 8 = 16\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The value $24\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is obtained by multiplying the final acceleration $a(4) = 6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ by the time, that is, by treating the acceleration as constant.
