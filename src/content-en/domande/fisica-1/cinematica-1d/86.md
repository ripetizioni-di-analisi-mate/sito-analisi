A small steel sphere is released from rest at the surface of the glycerin contained in a vertical tube, and it sinks with acceleration $a = A - k\,v$, where $A$ is a positive constant and $k = 2.0\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$. After how much time does the velocity of the sphere reach $90\%$ of the terminal velocity?

- $0.45\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.053\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4.6\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Starting from rest the velocity is $v(t) = \dfrac{A}{k}\left(1 - e^{-kt}\right)$, and the terminal velocity is $v_L = \dfrac{A}{k}$. The condition $v = 0.90\,v_L$ is equivalent to $e^{-kt} = 0.10$, so
$$
t = \frac{\ln 10}{k} = \frac{2.30}{2.0\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}} = 1.2\,\mathrm{s} .
$$
The result does not depend on $A$, which only fixes the value of the terminal velocity.
