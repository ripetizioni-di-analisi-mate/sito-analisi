A point moves along the $x$ axis with velocity $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for $3.0\,\mathrm{s}$ and then with velocity $-2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for the next $5.0\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the overall displacement?

- $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $22\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-2.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The displacement is the signed area between the velocity graph and the time axis. The first rectangle lies above the axis and has area $4.0 \cdot 3.0\,\mathrm{m} = 12\,\mathrm{m}$, the second lies below the axis and has area $2.0 \cdot 5.0\,\mathrm{m} = 10\,\mathrm{m}$. So the displacement is $\Delta x = 12\,\mathrm{m} - 10\,\mathrm{m} = 2.0\,\mathrm{m}$. The sum of the two areas, $22\,\mathrm{m}$, is the distance traveled.
