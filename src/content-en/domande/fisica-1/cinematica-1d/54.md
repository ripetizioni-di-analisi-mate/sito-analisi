A motorcycle travels at $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and accelerates with constant acceleration $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ for $4.0\,\mathrm{s}$. What speed does it reach?

- $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $22\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $13\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

In uniformly accelerated motion the velocity is $v(t) = v_0 + a\,t$. Therefore after $4.0\,\mathrm{s}$
$$
v = 10\,\mathrm{m/s} + 1.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot 4.0\,\mathrm{s} = 16\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The value $6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is the increase in speed alone, without the initial speed.
