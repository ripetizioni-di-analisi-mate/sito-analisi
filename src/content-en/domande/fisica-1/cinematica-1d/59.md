A juggler throws a club vertically upward at $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Neglecting air resistance, what is the velocity of the club $1.0\,\mathrm{s}$ after the throw?

- $29.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ upward
- $15.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$ upward
- $5.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ upward
- $10.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ upward

## Explanation

With the $y$-axis pointing upward the velocity is $v(t) = v_0 - g\,t$. So after $1.0\,\mathrm{s}$
$$
v = 20\,\mathrm{m/s} - 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot 1.0\,\mathrm{s} = 10.2\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The value is positive, therefore the club is still rising, because the highest point is reached at the instant $\dfrac{v_0}{g} = 2.04\,\mathrm{s}$.
