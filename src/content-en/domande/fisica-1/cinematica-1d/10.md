A cart starts from rest and its velocity increases uniformly up to $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in $5{,}0\,\mathrm{s}$; it stays constant for $10\,\mathrm{s}$ and then decreases uniformly to zero in $5{,}0\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the average velocity over the whole motion?

- $5{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $7{,}5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The distance traveled is the area under the velocity graph, a trapezoid made of two triangles and a rectangle. The two triangles have area $\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 5{,}0 \cdot 10\,\mathrm{m} = 25\,\mathrm{m}$ each and the rectangle has area $10 \cdot 10\,\mathrm{m} = 100\,\mathrm{m}$, so the distance traveled is $150\,\mathrm{m}$. The cart never reverses its motion, therefore the displacement coincides with the distance traveled. The motion lasts $20\,\mathrm{s}$, and the average velocity is $\dfrac{150\,\mathrm{m}}{20\,\mathrm{s}} = 7{,}5\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
