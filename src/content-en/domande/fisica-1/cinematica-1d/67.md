The graph of the velocity $v(t)$ of an elevator, which starts from rest and stops, consists of an increasing segment, a horizontal segment and a decreasing segment down to $v = 0$, all above the time axis. Which description of the graph of the acceleration $a(t)$ is correct?

- Three horizontal pieces: the first above the time axis, the second on the axis, the third below the axis
- An increasing segment, a horizontal one and a decreasing one, all above the time axis
- Three horizontal pieces, all above the time axis
- A single horizontal piece on the time axis, because the final velocity equals the initial one

## Explanation

The acceleration is the slope of the velocity graph. In the first piece the slope is constant and positive, in the second it is zero and in the third it is constant and negative. Therefore the graph of $a(t)$ consists of three horizontal pieces, at a positive level, on the axis and at a negative level. The fact that the final velocity equals the initial one only says that the signed area under the graph of $a(t)$ is zero.
