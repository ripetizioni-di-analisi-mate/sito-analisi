An object is thrown upward and reaches its highest point before falling. Air resistance is negligible. Given the ascent time $t = \dfrac{9}{5}\,\mathrm{s}$ and the rise to the highest point $h = \dfrac{81}{5}\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the initial speed $v$?

- $18\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $36\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $27\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

On the way up the velocity decreases linearly to zero, so its average is half the initial value. In the stated units, the relation is $h = \dfrac{t \cdot v}{2}$. Solving for the unknown gives $v = \dfrac{2 \cdot h}{t}$. Substituting the given values yields $18\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
