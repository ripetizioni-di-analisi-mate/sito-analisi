A cart starts from rest and moves with constant acceleration $3{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What velocity does it have after traveling $24\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $8{,}5\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $17\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The time is neither given nor asked for, so one uses the relation between velocity and displacement $v^2 = v_0^2 + 2\,a\,(x - x_0)$. With $v_0 = 0$ one obtains $v^2 = 2 \cdot 3{,}0 \cdot 24\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2} = 144\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}$. Therefore $v = 12\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
