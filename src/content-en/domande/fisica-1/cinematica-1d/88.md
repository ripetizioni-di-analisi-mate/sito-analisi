A sensor measures $x(t)=x_0+bt^2+ct^3$ in SI units. At the stated time the object moves in the positive direction. Given the quadratic coefficient $b = 3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, the cubic coefficient $c = 2\,\mathrm{m/s^3}$ and the measurement time $t = 2\,\mathrm{s}$, what is the instantaneous velocity $v$?

- $28\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $72\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $14\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $36\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Instantaneous velocity is the time derivative of $x$. In the stated units, the relation is $v = t \cdot \left(2 \cdot b + 3 \cdot c \cdot t\right)$. Substituting the given values yields $36\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
