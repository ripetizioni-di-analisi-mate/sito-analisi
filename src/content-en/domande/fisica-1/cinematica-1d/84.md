A robot travels forward and then covers a shorter distance backward on the same track. The direction of its first leg is positive. Given the distance of the first leg $a = 28\,\mathrm{m}$, the total time $t = 8\,\mathrm{s}$ and the average velocity $v = 2\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the return distance $b$?

- $16\,\mathrm{m}$
- $24\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The displacement is the difference between the two distances. Average velocity is displacement divided by total time. In the stated units, the relation is $v = \dfrac{a - b}{t}$. Solving for the unknown gives $b = a - t \cdot v$. Substituting the given values yields $12\,\mathrm{m}$.
