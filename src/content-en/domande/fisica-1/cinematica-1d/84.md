A small sphere enters an oil bath with velocity $0.40\,\mathrm{m/s}$ directed horizontally, and in its horizontal motion it is slowed down with acceleration $a = -k\,v$, with $k = 5.0\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$. What is its acceleration at the instant it enters the oil?

- $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-0.080\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-12.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The acceleration depends on the instantaneous velocity, so at the entry it equals
$$
a = -k\,v_0 = -5.0\,\mathrm{s^{-1}} \cdot 0.40\,\mathrm{m/s} = -2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
The negative sign indicates that the acceleration is opposite to the velocity, therefore the sphere slows down. The value $-0.080\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ is obtained by dividing the velocity by $k$ instead of multiplying it.
