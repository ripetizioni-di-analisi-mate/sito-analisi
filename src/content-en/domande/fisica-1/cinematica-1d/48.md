On a straight stretch of highway two gantries of an average speed control system are $18\,\mathrm{km}$ apart. A car passes under the second gantry $10$ minutes after the first. The speed limit is $130\,\mathrm{km/h}$. Which statement is correct on the basis of these data?

- The data guarantee that the speed never exceeded $108\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- The data do not rule out that at some instant the speed exceeded $130\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- The average speed of the car is $180\,\mathrm{km/h}$
- The data guarantee that the speed was below $130\,\mathrm{km/h}$ at every instant

## Explanation

The time taken is $10\,\mathrm{min} = \dfrac{1}{6}\,\mathrm{h}$, so the average speed is
$$
v_m = \frac{18\,\mathrm{km}}{\frac{1}{6}\,\mathrm{h}} = 108\,\mathrm{km/h} .
$$
The average speed depends only on the total distance and the total time, therefore it is compatible with stretches covered faster balanced by slower stretches. Hence the data do not rule out that the car exceeded $130\,\mathrm{km/h}$ at some instant, and they guarantee no upper bound for the instantaneous speed.
