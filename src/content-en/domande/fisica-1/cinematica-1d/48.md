Two carts move in the same direction at constant speeds. The faster cart starts behind the other. Given the initial separation $d = 36\,\mathrm{m}$, the speed of the second cart $u = 3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the catch-up time $t = 6\,\mathrm{s}$, what is the speed of the first cart $v$?

- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $18\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The separation decreases at the relative speed $v-u$. In the stated units, the relation is $t = - \dfrac{d}{u - v}$. Solving for the unknown gives $v = \dfrac{d}{t} + u$. Substituting the given values yields $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
