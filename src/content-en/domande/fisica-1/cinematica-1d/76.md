An object moves in a straight line with acceleration $a=-kv$ and positive velocity. Given the initial speed $v = 14\,\mathrm{m/s}$, the damping coefficient $k = 2\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$ and the distance traveled $d = 4\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the final speed $u$?

- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Since $a=v\,dv/dx$, it follows that $dv/dx=-k$. The change in velocity is therefore $-kd$. In the stated units, the relation is $d = \dfrac{- u + v}{k}$. Solving for the unknown gives $u = - d \cdot k + v$. Substituting the given values yields $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
