An object is dropped from rest from the top of a tower $80\,\mathrm{m}$ high. Neglecting air resistance, after how much time does the object hit the ground?

- $2.86\,\mathrm{s}$
- $8.15\,\mathrm{s}$
- $16.3\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4.04\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The axis is oriented downward with the origin at the starting point; by the law of falling bodies the object therefore travels $\dfrac{1}{2}\,g\,t^2$ in the time $t$. The fall time is obtained from $\dfrac{1}{2}\,g\,t^2 = h$, therefore
$$
t = \sqrt{\frac{2h}{g}} = \sqrt{\frac{160\,\mathrm{m}}{9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}}} = 4.04\,\mathrm{s} .
$$
An estimate confirms the value, because $\dfrac{160}{9.81}$ is a little more than $16$ and its square root is a little more than $4$.
