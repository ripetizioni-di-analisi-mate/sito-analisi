A point moves along the $x$ axis with acceleration $a(t) = 6t$, with $a$ in $\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and $t$ in seconds. At the instant $t = 0$ it is at $x_0 = 1\,\mathrm{m}$ with velocity $v_0 = -3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. At what position is it at the instant $t = 2\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Integrating the acceleration with the condition $v(0) = -3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ one obtains $v(t) = -3 + \displaystyle\int_0^t 6s\,ds = 3t^2 - 3$. Integrating the velocity with the condition $x(0) = 1\,\mathrm{m}$ one obtains
$$
x(t) = 1 + \int_0^t \left(3s^2 - 3\right)ds = t^3 - 3t + 1 .
$$
Therefore at the instant $t = 2\,\mathrm{s}$ the point is at $x(2) = 8 - 6 + 1 = 3\,\mathrm{m}$.
