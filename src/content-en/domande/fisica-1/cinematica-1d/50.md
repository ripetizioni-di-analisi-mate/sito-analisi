A train moves at $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. It brakes at $-2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ for $4\,\mathrm s$ and then stays still for $2\,\mathrm s$. Which calculation gives the distance traveled in $6\,\mathrm s$?

- $8\cdot6/2$
- $8\cdot4/2$
- $8\cdot4$
- $8\cdot4/4$

## Explanation

The velocity-time graph has a triangle of base $4\,\mathrm s$ and height $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The train is at rest during the last interval, so that interval adds no area.
