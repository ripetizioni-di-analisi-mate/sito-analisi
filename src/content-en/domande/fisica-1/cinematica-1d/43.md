A motorboat with the engine off has initial speed $8.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and is slowed down by the water with acceleration $a = -k\,v$, with $k = 0.50\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$. What is its speed after $2.0\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $0$
- $2.9\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

In motion with acceleration proportional to velocity the velocity decreases exponentially, $v(t) = v_0\,e^{-kt}$. So
$$
v(2.0\,\mathrm{s}) = 8.0\,\mathrm{m/s} \cdot e^{-0.50 \cdot 2.0} = 8.0\,e^{-1}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 2.9\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The value $0$ is obtained by treating the initial acceleration $-k\,v_0 = -4.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ as constant, whereas its magnitude decreases together with the velocity.
