Two carts move in the same direction at constant speeds. The faster cart starts behind the other. Given the initial separation $d = 36\,\mathrm{m}$, the speed of the first cart $v = 9\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the speed of the second cart $u = 3\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the catch-up time $t$?

- $12\,\mathrm{s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The separation decreases at the relative speed $v-u$. In the stated units, the relation is $t = - \dfrac{d}{u - v}$. Substituting the given values yields $6\,\mathrm{s}$.
