A Carnot engine operates between $450\,\mathrm{K}$ and $300\,\mathrm{K}$ and absorbs $900\,\mathrm{J}$ per cycle. How much work does it produce in one cycle?

- $300\,\mathrm{J}$
- $600\,\mathrm{J}$
- $450\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1350\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The efficiency of the Carnot cycle is $\eta_C = 1 - \dfrac{T_2}{T_1} = 1 - \dfrac{300}{450} = \dfrac{1}{3}$. The work produced in one cycle is therefore $L = \eta_C\, Q_1 = \dfrac{1}{3} \cdot 900\,\mathrm{J} = 300\,\mathrm{J}$. The value $600\,\mathrm{J}$ is the heat released to the cold reservoir, $Q_1 \cdot \dfrac{T_2}{T_1}$.
