A reversible cyclic engine exchanges heat with just two reservoirs. The hot reservoir is at $480\,\mathrm{K}$. Given the heat absorbed from the hot reservoir $Q = 960\,\mathrm{J}$ and the cold reservoir temperature $T = 300\,\mathrm{K}$, what is the heat rejected to the cold reservoir $Q_{f}$?

- $1536\,\mathrm{J}$
- $360\,\mathrm{J}$
- $600\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1200\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The reversible entropy balance of the reservoirs is $-Q/480+Q_f/T=0$. In the stated units, the relation is $Q_{f} = \dfrac{Q \cdot T}{480}$. Substituting the given values yields $600\,\mathrm{J}$.
