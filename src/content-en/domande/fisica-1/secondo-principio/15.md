An engine produces $400\,\mathrm{J}$ of work and releases $1600\,\mathrm{J}$ to the cold reservoir in each cycle. What is its efficiency?

- $0{,}25$
- $0{,}80$
- $0{,}75$
- $0{,}20$

## Explanation

The heat absorbed in one cycle is the sum of the work produced and the heat released, $Q_{\text{ass}} = L + Q_{\text{ced}} = 2000\,\mathrm{J}$. The efficiency is the ratio of the work to the heat *absorbed*, therefore $\eta = \dfrac{400}{2000} = 0{,}20$. Dividing the work by the heat released would give $0{,}25$.
