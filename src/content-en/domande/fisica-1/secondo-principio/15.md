In a thermal power plant the steam goes through a cycle: it absorbs the heat $Q_1$ from the boiler, releases the heat $Q_2$ to the condenser and drives the turbine. With the sign convention of the first law ($Q$ heat absorbed by the fluid, $L$ work done by the fluid), what are the signs of the three quantities in one cycle?

- $Q_1 > 0$, $Q_2 > 0$, $L > 0$
- $Q_1 < 0$, $Q_2 > 0$, $L < 0$
- $Q_1 > 0$, $Q_2 < 0$, $L > 0$
- $Q_1 > 0$, $Q_2 < 0$, $L < 0$

## Explanation

The steam receives heat from the boiler, therefore $Q_1 > 0$, and releases heat to the condenser, therefore $Q_2 < 0$. The turbine receives work from the steam, hence the work done by the fluid is positive, $L > 0$. These signs agree with the balance of the cycle $L = Q_1 + Q_2$, which is positive because $Q_1 > |Q_2|$. The set $Q_1 < 0$, $Q_2 > 0$, $L < 0$ describes a refrigerator instead.
