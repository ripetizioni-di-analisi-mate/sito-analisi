- $3.55 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $2.77 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $-3.13 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

The temperatures in kelvins are $T_A = 283\,\mathrm{K}$ and $T_B = 363\,\mathrm{K}$. During the heating the temperature of the water changes continuously, therefore the change in entropy is
$$
\Delta S = m c \ln\frac{T_B}{T_A} = 3.0 \cdot 4186 \cdot \ln\frac{363}{283}\,\mathrm{J/K} \approx 3.13 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{J/K} .
$$
The result is positive because the water absorbs heat. Dividing the total heat $Q = m c\,\Delta T \approx 1.00 \cdot 10^6\,\mathrm{J}$ by the initial temperature alone or by the final temperature alone would give $3.55 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{J/K}$ or $2.77 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{J/K}$.
