A block melts completely at its melting temperature. Its specific latent heat is $120\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$. Given the mass $m = \dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the block's entropy change $S = 200\,\mathrm{J/K}$, what is the absolute melting temperature $T$?

- $150\,\mathrm{K}$
- $300\,\mathrm{K}$
- $600\,\mathrm{K}$
- $\dfrac{3}{10}\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

Isothermal melting requires $Q=mL$. The entropy change is that heat in joules divided by absolute temperature. In the stated units, the relation is $S = \dfrac{120000 \cdot m}{T}$. Solving for the unknown gives $T = \dfrac{120000 \cdot m}{S}$. Substituting the given values yields $300\,\mathrm{K}$.
