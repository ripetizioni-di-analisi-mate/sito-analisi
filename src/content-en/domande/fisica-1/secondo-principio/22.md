A Carnot engine operates between a reservoir at $327\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ and a reservoir at $27\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. What is its efficiency?

- $0{,}917$
- $0{,}955$
- $0{,}0826$
- $0{,}500$

## Explanation

In the formula for the Carnot efficiency the temperatures are expressed in kelvins, therefore $T_1 = 600\,\mathrm{K}$ and $T_2 = 300\,\mathrm{K}$. Hence $\eta_C = 1 - \dfrac{300}{600} = 0{,}500$. With the temperatures in degrees Celsius one would obtain $1 - \dfrac{27}{327} \approx 0{,}917$, a value that depends on the zero of the Celsius scale and has no physical meaning.
