A reversible engine $R$ and an irreversible engine $M$ work between two reservoirs at $500\,\mathrm{K}$ and $300\,\mathrm{K}$. In each cycle both absorb $1000\,\mathrm{J}$ from the hot reservoir. Which statement about the heat released to the cold reservoir in one cycle is true?

- Both engines release $600\,\mathrm{J}$
- $R$ releases $600\,\mathrm{J}$ and $M$ releases less than $600\,\mathrm{J}$
- $R$ releases $600\,\mathrm{J}$ and $M$ releases more than $600\,\mathrm{J}$
- $R$ releases $400\,\mathrm{J}$ and $M$ releases more than $400\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The reversible engine has efficiency $\eta_C = 1 - \dfrac{300}{500} = 0.40$, therefore it produces $400\,\mathrm{J}$ and releases $1000\,\mathrm{J} - 400\,\mathrm{J} = 600\,\mathrm{J}$. By **Carnot's theorem** the irreversible engine has efficiency less than $0.40$, hence with the same heat absorbed it produces less than $400\,\mathrm{J}$. Since in a cycle the heat released is the difference between the heat absorbed and the work, $M$ releases more than $600\,\mathrm{J}$. The value $400\,\mathrm{J}$ is the work of $R$.
