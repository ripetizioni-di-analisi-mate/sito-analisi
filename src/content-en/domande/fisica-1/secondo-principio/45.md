A Carnot refrigerator operates between its interior at $250\,\mathrm{K}$ and the kitchen at $300\,\mathrm{K}$ and extracts $1000\,\mathrm{J}$ from the interior. How much work does it receive?

- $200\,\mathrm{J}$
- $167\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1200\,\mathrm{J}$
- $5000\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The coefficient of performance of the Carnot refrigerator is $\varepsilon_C = \dfrac{T_2}{T_1 - T_2} = \dfrac{250}{50} = 5.0$. Since $\varepsilon = \dfrac{Q_2}{|L|}$, the work received is $|L| = \dfrac{1000\,\mathrm{J}}{5.0} = 200\,\mathrm{J}$. The kitchen receives $1200\,\mathrm{J}$, that is, the heat extracted from the interior plus the work. The value $167\,\mathrm{J}$ is obtained by using the coefficient of performance of the heat pump, $\dfrac{T_1}{T_1 - T_2} = 6.0$.
