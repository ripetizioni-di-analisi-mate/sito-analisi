A Carnot engine runs between a hot reservoir at $600\,\mathrm{K}$ and a cold reservoir. Heat quantities are given as magnitudes. Given the cold reservoir temperature $T = 300\,\mathrm{K}$ and the useful work per cycle $W = 450\,\mathrm{J}$, what is the heat absorbed per cycle $Q$?

- $450\,\mathrm{J}$
- $600\,\mathrm{J}$
- $900\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1800\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Carnot efficiency is $1-T_f/T_c$, with temperatures in kelvin. Work is efficiency times absorbed heat. In the stated units, the relation is $W = \dfrac{Q \cdot \left(600 - T\right)}{600}$. Solving for the unknown gives $Q = - \dfrac{600 \cdot W}{T - 600}$. Substituting the given values yields $900\,\mathrm{J}$.
