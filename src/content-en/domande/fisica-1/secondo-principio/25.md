In the $(S, T)$ plane a Carnot cycle between $800\,\mathrm{K}$ and $300\,\mathrm{K}$ is a rectangle with base $\Delta S = 2{,}0\,\mathrm{J/K}$. How much work does it produce in one cycle?

- $1{,}6\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $0{,}60\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $2{,}2\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $1{,}0\,\mathrm{kJ}$

## Explanation

In the $(S, T)$ plane the heat exchanged along a reversible process is the area under the curve. The heat absorbed on the isotherm at $800\,\mathrm{K}$ is therefore $Q_1 = 800 \cdot 2{,}0\,\mathrm{J} = 1{,}6\,\mathrm{kJ}$, and the heat released on the isotherm at $300\,\mathrm{K}$ is $|Q_2| = 300 \cdot 2{,}0\,\mathrm{J} = 0{,}60\,\mathrm{kJ}$. The work is the difference between the two amounts of heat, that is, the area of the rectangle, $L = (800 - 300) \cdot 2{,}0\,\mathrm{J} = 1{,}0\,\mathrm{kJ}$.
