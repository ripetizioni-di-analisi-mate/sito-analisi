In the entropy-temperature plane the reversible cycle of an engine is the triangle with vertices $A = (1.0\,\mathrm{J/K};\ 300\,\mathrm{K})$, $B = (1.0\,\mathrm{J/K};\ 600\,\mathrm{K})$ and $C = (3.0\,\mathrm{J/K};\ 300\,\mathrm{K})$, traversed in the order $A \to B \to C \to A$. What is the efficiency of the engine?

::grafico{poligoni="1,300 1,600 3,300" vettori="1,300,1,600;1,600,3,300;3,300,1,300" punti="1,300,pieno,A;1,600,pieno,B;3,300,pieno,C" xmin=-0.3 xmax=4 ymin=0 ymax=700 nomi="S,T" titolo="Cycle in the $(S, T)$ plane, with $S$ in $\mathrm{J/K}$ and $T$ in kelvins"}

- $0.50$
- $0.67$
- $0.33$
- $0.25$

## Explanation

In a reversible process the heat absorbed is the area under the curve in the $(S, T)$ plane, positive when the entropy increases. On the part $A \to B$ the entropy is constant and the heat is zero. On the part $B \to C$ the entropy increases and the fluid absorbs the area of the trapezoid, $Q_{\text{ass}} = \dfrac{600 + 300}{2} \cdot 2.0\,\mathrm{J} = 900\,\mathrm{J}$. On the part $C \to A$ the fluid releases $300 \cdot 2.0\,\mathrm{J} = 600\,\mathrm{J}$, therefore $L = 300\,\mathrm{J}$ and
$$
\eta = \frac{300}{900} \approx 0.33 .
$$
The value $0.50$ is the Carnot efficiency between $600\,\mathrm{K}$ and $300\,\mathrm{K}$, which the cycle does not reach because it also absorbs heat at temperatures below $600\,\mathrm{K}$.
