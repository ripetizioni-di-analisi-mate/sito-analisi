A block melts completely at its melting temperature. Its specific latent heat is $120\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$. Given the mass $m = \dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the absolute melting temperature $T = 300\,\mathrm{K}$, what is the block's entropy change $S$?

- $100\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $400\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $200\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

Isothermal melting requires $Q=mL$. The entropy change is that heat in joules divided by absolute temperature. In the stated units, the relation is $S = \dfrac{120000 \cdot m}{T}$. Substituting the given values yields $200\,\mathrm{J/K}$.
