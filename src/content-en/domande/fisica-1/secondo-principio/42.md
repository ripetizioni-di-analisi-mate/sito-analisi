A reversible engine receives all its heat at $600\,\mathrm K$ and rejects $120\,\mathrm J$ at $300\,\mathrm K$ and $180\,\mathrm J$ at $450\,\mathrm K$ in each cycle. How much heat enters per cycle?

- $480\,\mathrm J$
- $300\,\mathrm J$
- $360\,\mathrm J$
- $600\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Over a reversible cycle the entropy exchanges with the reservoirs balance. In joules per kelvin, $\dfrac{Q_{\rm in}}{600}=\dfrac{120}{300}+\dfrac{180}{450}=0.8$, giving a heat input of $480\,\mathrm J$.
