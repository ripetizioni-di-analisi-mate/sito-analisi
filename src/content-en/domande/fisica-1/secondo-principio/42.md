A reversible engine operates between a reservoir at the temperature of the triple point of water, $273{,}16\,\mathrm{K}$, and a hotter reservoir. In each cycle it absorbs $500\,\mathrm{J}$ from the hot reservoir and releases $200\,\mathrm{J}$ to the cold reservoir. What is the thermodynamic temperature of the hot reservoir?

- $683\,\mathrm{K}$
- $109\,\mathrm{K}$
- $455\,\mathrm{K}$
- $956\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

For a reversible engine the ratio of the amounts of heat exchanged equals the ratio of the thermodynamic temperatures of the reservoirs, $\dfrac{T_2}{T_1} = \dfrac{|Q_2|}{Q_1}$. Therefore
$$
T_1 = T_2 \cdot \frac{Q_1}{|Q_2|} = 273{,}16\,\mathrm{K} \cdot \frac{500}{200} \approx 683\,\mathrm{K} .
$$
The value $109\,\mathrm{K}$, lower than the temperature of the cold reservoir, is obtained by inverting the heat ratio.
