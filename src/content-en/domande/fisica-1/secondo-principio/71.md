Two reservoirs at constant temperatures exchange a positive amount of heat from hot to cold. The cold reservoir is at $300\,\mathrm{K}$. Given the heat transferred $Q = 1200\,\mathrm{J}$ and the hot reservoir temperature $T = 600\,\mathrm{K}$, what is the total entropy change $S$?

- $2\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $1\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $6\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $4\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

The cold reservoir gains entropy $Q/300$ and the hot reservoir loses $Q/T$. The sum is positive. In the stated units, the relation is $S = \dfrac{Q}{300} - \dfrac{Q}{T}$. Substituting the given values yields $2\,\mathrm{J/K}$.
