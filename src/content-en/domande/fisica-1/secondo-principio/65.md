A heat pump warms a room by delivering heat to it. In each cycle it consumes work and draws heat from outdoors. The initial data are the heat drawn from outdoors $Q = 1200\,\mathrm{J}$ and the work input $W = 400\,\mathrm{J}$. Change the heat drawn from outdoors to $900\,\mathrm{J}$ and the work input to $300\,\mathrm{J}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the final value of $b$ minus its initial value?

- $1$
- $2$
- $0$
- $4$

## Explanation

The room receives the extracted heat plus the work input. The heating coefficient of performance is delivered heat divided by work input. The relation $b = \dfrac{Q + W}{W}$ gives $4$ before the change and $4$ after it. Subtracting the initial value from the final value gives $0$.
