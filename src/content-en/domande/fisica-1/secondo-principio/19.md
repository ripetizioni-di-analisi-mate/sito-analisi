A refrigerator has coefficient of performance $\varepsilon = 3.0$. What is the coefficient of performance of the same machine used as a heat pump, with the same cycle and between the same reservoirs?

- $4.0$
- $2.0$
- $3.0$
- $0.75$

## Explanation

For each unit of work spent the machine extracts $3.0$ units of heat from the cold reservoir, because $\varepsilon = \dfrac{Q_2}{|L|} = 3.0$. The hot reservoir receives $|Q_1| = Q_2 + |L|$, that is, the heat extracted plus the work, therefore $4.0$ units. Hence the coefficient of performance as a heat pump is $\varepsilon_{\text{hp}} = \dfrac{|Q_1|}{|L|} = \varepsilon + 1 = 4.0$.
