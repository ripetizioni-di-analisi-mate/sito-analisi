A heat engine has efficiency $0.35$ and produces $700\,\mathrm{J}$ of work in each cycle. How much heat does it release in one cycle?

- $2.0\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $1.3\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $1.1\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $0.46\,\mathrm{kJ}$

## Explanation

The efficiency is $\eta = \dfrac{L}{Q_{\text{in}}}$, therefore the heat absorbed in one cycle is
$$
Q_{\text{in}} = \frac{L}{\eta} = \frac{700\,\mathrm{J}}{0.35} = 2000\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
In a cycle $L = Q_{\text{in}} - Q_{\text{out}}$, hence the heat released is $Q_{\text{out}} = 2000\,\mathrm{J} - 700\,\mathrm{J} = 1300\,\mathrm{J} = 1.3\,\mathrm{kJ}$. The value $2.0\,\mathrm{kJ}$ is the heat absorbed.
