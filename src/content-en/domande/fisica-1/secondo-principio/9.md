- $244\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

During melting the temperature remains constant at $273\,\mathrm{K}$ and the ice absorbs the heat $Q = m \lambda_f = 0.20 \cdot 3.33 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{J} = 6.66 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{J}$. Therefore
$$
\Delta S = \frac{m\lambda_f}{T} = \frac{6.66 \cdot 10^4}{273}\,\mathrm{J/K} \approx 244\,\mathrm{J/K} .
$$
The sign is positive because in melting the heat is absorbed. The value $1.22 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{J/K}$ is obtained by forgetting the mass, and $2.44 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{J/K}$ by expressing the mass in grams.
