A monatomic ideal gas is heated at constant volume. Use $R\simeq8\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$. Given the amount of gas $n = 3\,\mathrm{mol}$ and the entropy change $S = 36 \cdot \log{\left(2 \right)}\,\mathrm{J/K}$, what is the final-to-initial absolute temperature ratio $b$?

- $1$
- $\log{\left(2 \right)} + 1$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

At constant volume, reversible heat is $nC_V\,dT$. Integrate $nC_V\,dT/T$ with $C_V=3R/2$. In the stated units, the relation is $S = 12 \cdot n \cdot \log{\left(b \right)}$. Solving for the unknown gives $b = e^{\dfrac{S}{12 \cdot n}}$. Substituting the given values yields $2$.
