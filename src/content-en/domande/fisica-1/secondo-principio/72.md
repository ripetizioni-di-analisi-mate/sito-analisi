Two reservoirs at constant temperatures exchange a positive amount of heat from hot to cold. The cold reservoir is at $300\,\mathrm{K}$. Given the hot reservoir temperature $T = 600\,\mathrm{K}$ and the total entropy change $S = 2\,\mathrm{J/K}$, what is the heat transferred $Q$?

- $400\,\mathrm{J}$
- $2400\,\mathrm{J}$
- $600\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1200\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The cold reservoir gains entropy $Q/300$ and the hot reservoir loses $Q/T$. The sum is positive. In the stated units, the relation is $S = \dfrac{Q}{300} - \dfrac{Q}{T}$. Solving for the unknown gives $Q = \dfrac{300 \cdot S \cdot T}{T - 300}$. Substituting the given values yields $1200\,\mathrm{J}$.
