A Carnot engine runs between a hot reservoir at $600\,\mathrm{K}$ and a cold reservoir. Heat quantities are given as magnitudes. The initial data are the heat absorbed per cycle $Q = 900\,\mathrm{J}$ and the cold reservoir temperature $T = 300\,\mathrm{K}$. In the second configuration, change the cold reservoir temperature to $400\,\mathrm{K}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the ratio of the final value of $W$ to its initial value?

- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $\dfrac{8}{9}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$

## Explanation

Carnot efficiency is $1-T_f/T_c$, with temperatures in kelvin. Work is efficiency times absorbed heat. The relation $W = \dfrac{Q \cdot \left(600 - T\right)}{600}$ gives $450\,\mathrm{J}$ before the change and $300\,\mathrm{J}$ after it. The ratio is $\dfrac{2}{3}$.
