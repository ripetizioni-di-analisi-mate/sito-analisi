In the $(S, T)$ plane a reversible process is represented by the segment going from state $A$, with $S_A = 2{,}0\,\mathrm{J/K}$ and $T_A = 300\,\mathrm{K}$, to state $B$, with $S_B = 4{,}0\,\mathrm{J/K}$ and $T_B = 500\,\mathrm{K}$. How much heat does the system absorb?

- $800\,\mathrm{J}$
- $600\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{J}$
- $200\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

In a reversible process $\delta Q = T\, dS$, therefore the heat absorbed is the area under the curve in the $(S, T)$ plane. The segment from $A$ to $B$ bounds, together with the $S$ axis, a trapezoid with bases $300\,\mathrm{K}$ and $500\,\mathrm{K}$ and height $\Delta S = 2{,}0\,\mathrm{J/K}$. Hence
$$
Q = \frac{300 + 500}{2} \cdot 2{,}0\,\mathrm{J} = 800\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
The heat is positive because the entropy increases. Using only the initial temperature or only the final temperature would give $600\,\mathrm{J}$ or $1000\,\mathrm{J}$.
