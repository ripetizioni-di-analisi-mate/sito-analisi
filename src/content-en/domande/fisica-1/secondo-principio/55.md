An irreversible heat engine takes heat from a reservoir at $500\,\mathrm{K}$ and releases heat to one at $300\,\mathrm{K}$. The actual efficiency is known. The initial data are the heat absorbed per cycle $Q = 1000\,\mathrm{J}$ and the actual efficiency $\eta = \dfrac{3}{10}$. In the second configuration, change the heat absorbed per cycle to $1200\,\mathrm{J}$ and the actual efficiency to $\dfrac{1}{4}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the final value of $Q_{f}$ minus its initial value?

- $200\,\mathrm{J}$
- $120\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-200\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Over a cycle the internal energy change is zero. Useful work is $\eta Q$, so the cold reservoir receives $Q-\eta Q$. The relation $Q_{f} = Q \cdot \left(1 - \eta\right)$ gives $700\,\mathrm{J}$ before the change and $900\,\mathrm{J}$ after it. Subtracting the initial value from the final value gives $200\,\mathrm{J}$.
