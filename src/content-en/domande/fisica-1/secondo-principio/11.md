## Explanation

The container is rigid and insulated, therefore the gas exchanges neither heat nor work, $\Delta U = 0$ and the final temperature equals the initial one. The surroundings undergo no change, hence $\Delta S_{\text{u}}$ coincides with the change in entropy of the gas. Since entropy is a state function, it is computed along a reversible isothermal process between the same states, and one obtains
$$
\Delta S_{\text{u}} = nR\ln\frac{V_B}{V_A} = 0{,}50 \cdot 8{,}314 \cdot \ln 2\,\mathrm{J/K} \approx 2{,}88\,\mathrm{J/K} .
$$
The value $0\,\mathrm{J/K}$ is obtained by using $\dfrac{Q}{T}$ along the actual process, which is irreversible.
