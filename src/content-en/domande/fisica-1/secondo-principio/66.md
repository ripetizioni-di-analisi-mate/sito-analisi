An ideal gas expands at constant temperature. Use $R\simeq8\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$. Given the amount of gas $n = 2\,\mathrm{mol}$ and the final-to-initial volume ratio $b = 2$, what is the entropy change $S$?

- $8 \cdot \log{\left(2 \right)}\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $16 \cdot \log{\left(2 \right)}\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $32 \cdot \log{\left(2 \right)}\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $16\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

Entropy is a state function. Integrating $\delta Q_{\mathrm{rev}}/T=nR\,dV/V$ along a reversible isotherm between the same states gives the gas's entropy change. In the stated units, the relation is $S = 8 \cdot n \cdot \log{\left(b \right)}$. Substituting the given values yields $16 \cdot \log{\left(2 \right)}\,\mathrm{J/K}$.
