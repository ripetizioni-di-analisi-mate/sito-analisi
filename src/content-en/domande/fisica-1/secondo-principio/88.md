A body with constant heat capacity is heated by a reservoir. Its initial temperature is $300\,\mathrm{K}$. Consider the entropy change of the body alone. Given the heat capacity of the body $C = 400\,\mathrm{J/K}$ and the final body temperature $T = 360\,\mathrm{K}$, what is the entropy change $S$?

- $80\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $- 800 \cdot \log{\left(5 \right)} + 800 \cdot \log{\left(6 \right)}\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $\dfrac{200}{3}\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $- 400 \cdot \log{\left(5 \right)} + 400 \cdot \log{\left(6 \right)}\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

Calculate the entropy change by integrating $C\,dT/T$ between the initial and final temperatures along an equivalent reversible heating path. In the stated units, the relation is $S = \log{\left(\left(\dfrac{T}{300}\right)^{C} \right)}$. Substituting the given values yields $- 400 \cdot \log{\left(5 \right)} + 400 \cdot \log{\left(6 \right)}\,\mathrm{J/K}$.
