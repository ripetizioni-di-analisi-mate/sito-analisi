A Carnot engine runs between a hot reservoir at $600\,\mathrm{K}$ and a cold reservoir. Heat quantities are given as magnitudes. Given the heat absorbed per cycle $Q = 900\,\mathrm{J}$ and the useful work per cycle $W = 450\,\mathrm{J}$, what is the cold reservoir temperature $T$?

- $600\,\mathrm{K}$
- $150\,\mathrm{K}$
- $450\,\mathrm{K}$
- $300\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

Carnot efficiency is $1-T_f/T_c$, with temperatures in kelvin. Work is efficiency times absorbed heat. In the stated units, the relation is $W = \dfrac{Q \cdot \left(600 - T\right)}{600}$. Solving for the unknown gives $T = 600 - \dfrac{600 \cdot W}{Q}$. Substituting the given values yields $300\,\mathrm{K}$.
