Two engines are connected in cascade: engine $A$, with efficiency $0.30$, absorbs heat from a hot reservoir, and all the heat it releases is absorbed by engine $B$, with efficiency $0.20$, which in turn releases heat to a cold reservoir. What is the overall efficiency of the two engines?

- $0.50$
- $0.25$
- $0.44$
- $0.060$

## Explanation

Let $Q$ be the heat absorbed by $A$ in one cycle. Engine $A$ produces $0.30\,Q$ and releases $0.70\,Q$, which is the heat absorbed by $B$. Engine $B$ therefore produces $0.20 \cdot 0.70\,Q = 0.14\,Q$. The total work is $0.44\,Q$, for the single heat $Q$ absorbed from the hot reservoir, hence the overall efficiency is $0.44$. The sum $0.50$ is obtained by referring the efficiency of $B$ to the heat $Q$, and the product $0.060$ by multiplying the two efficiencies.
