A heat pump warms a room by delivering heat to it. In each cycle it consumes work and draws heat from outdoors. Given the work input $W = 400\,\mathrm{J}$ and the heating coefficient of performance $b = 4$, what is the heat drawn from outdoors $Q$?

- $1600\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1200\,\mathrm{J}$
- $\dfrac{400}{3}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $2400\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The room receives the extracted heat plus the work input. The heating coefficient of performance is delivered heat divided by work input. In the stated units, the relation is $b = \dfrac{Q + W}{W}$. Solving for the unknown gives $Q = W \cdot \left(b - 1\right)$. Substituting the given values yields $1200\,\mathrm{J}$.
