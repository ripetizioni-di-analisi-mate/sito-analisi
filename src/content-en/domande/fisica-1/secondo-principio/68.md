An ideal gas expands at constant temperature. Use $R\simeq8\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$. Given the amount of gas $n = 2\,\mathrm{mol}$ and the entropy change $S = 16 \cdot \log{\left(2 \right)}\,\mathrm{J/K}$, what is the final-to-initial volume ratio $b$?

- $1$
- $\log{\left(2 \right)} + 1$
- $4$
- $2$

## Explanation

Entropy is a state function. Integrating $\delta Q_{\mathrm{rev}}/T=nR\,dV/V$ along a reversible isotherm between the same states gives the gas's entropy change. In the stated units, the relation is $S = 8 \cdot n \cdot \log{\left(b \right)}$. Solving for the unknown gives $b = e^{\dfrac{S}{8 \cdot n}}$. Substituting the given values yields $2$.
