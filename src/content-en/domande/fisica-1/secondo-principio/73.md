Two reservoirs at constant temperatures exchange a positive amount of heat from hot to cold. The cold reservoir is at $300\,\mathrm{K}$. Given the heat transferred $Q = 1200\,\mathrm{J}$ and the total entropy change $S = 2\,\mathrm{J/K}$, what is the hot reservoir temperature $T$?

- $1200\,\mathrm{K}$
- $300\,\mathrm{K}$
- $600\,\mathrm{K}$
- $900\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

The cold reservoir gains entropy $Q/300$ and the hot reservoir loses $Q/T$. The sum is positive. In the stated units, the relation is $S = \dfrac{Q}{300} - \dfrac{Q}{T}$. Solving for the unknown gives $T = \dfrac{300 \cdot Q}{Q - 300 \cdot S}$. Substituting the given values yields $600\,\mathrm{K}$.
