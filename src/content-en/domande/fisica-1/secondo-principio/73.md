A system goes from the equilibrium state $A$ to the equilibrium state $B$ absorbing $900\,\mathrm{J}$ from a reservoir at $450\,\mathrm{K}$, and its entropy increases by $2.5\,\mathrm{J/K}$. How much entropy was produced by the irreversibilities of the process?

- $2.0\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $0.50\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $-0.50\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

By the theorem on **entropy in irreversible processes** one has $\Delta S \geq \displaystyle\int_A^B \frac{\delta Q}{T}$, and the difference between the two sides is the entropy produced by the irreversibility. The reservoir has constant temperature, therefore the integral is $\dfrac{900\,\mathrm{J}}{450\,\mathrm{K}} = 2.0\,\mathrm{J/K}$. Hence the entropy produced is $2.5\,\mathrm{J/K} - 2.0\,\mathrm{J/K} = 0.50\,\mathrm{J/K}$. The value $2.0\,\mathrm{J/K}$ is the entropy exchanged with the reservoir.
