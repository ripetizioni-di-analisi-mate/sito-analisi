A heat pump with coefficient of performance $\varepsilon_{\text{pc}} = 3{,}5$ must deliver a thermal power of $7{,}0\,\mathrm{kW}$ to an apartment. What mechanical power does it require?

- $2{,}8\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $25\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $1{,}6\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $2{,}0\,\mathrm{kW}$

## Explanation

For a heat pump the useful effect is the heat released to the warm space, therefore $\varepsilon_{\text{pc}} = \dfrac{|Q_1|}{|L|}$. In one second $|Q_1| = 7{,}0\,\mathrm{kJ}$, hence $|L| = \dfrac{7{,}0\,\mathrm{kJ}}{3{,}5} = 2{,}0\,\mathrm{kJ}$ and the power required is $2{,}0\,\mathrm{kW}$. The value $2{,}8\,\mathrm{kW}$ is obtained by using the refrigeration coefficient of performance, $\varepsilon = \varepsilon_{\text{pc}} - 1 = 2{,}5$, instead of that of the heat pump.
