In an apartment a $2.4\,\mathrm{kW}$ electric heater, which turns all the electrical energy into heat, is replaced by a heat pump with coefficient of performance $\varepsilon_{\text{pc}} = 4.0$ that delivers the same thermal power to the apartment. How much electrical power is saved?

- $0.60\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $1.6\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $9.6\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $1.8\,\mathrm{kW}$

## Explanation

The heat pump must deliver $2.4\,\mathrm{kW}$ to the apartment, and its coefficient of performance is the ratio of the thermal power delivered to the mechanical power spent. Therefore the pump draws $\dfrac{2.4\,\mathrm{kW}}{4.0} = 0.60\,\mathrm{kW}$. The heater drew $2.4\,\mathrm{kW}$, hence the saving is $2.4\,\mathrm{kW} - 0.60\,\mathrm{kW} = 1.8\,\mathrm{kW}$. The value $1.6\,\mathrm{kW}$ is obtained by using the coefficient of performance as a refrigerator, $\varepsilon_{\text{pc}} - 1 = 3.0$.
