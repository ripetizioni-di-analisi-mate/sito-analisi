A heat pump with coefficient of performance $\varepsilon_{\text{hp}} = 3.5$ must deliver a thermal power of $7.0\,\mathrm{kW}$ to an apartment. What mechanical power does it require?

- $2.8\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $25\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $1.6\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{kW}$

## Explanation

For a heat pump the useful effect is the heat released to the warm space, therefore $\varepsilon_{\text{hp}} = \dfrac{|Q_1|}{|L|}$. In one second $|Q_1| = 7.0\,\mathrm{kJ}$, hence $|L| = \dfrac{7.0\,\mathrm{kJ}}{3.5} = 2.0\,\mathrm{kJ}$ and the power required is $2.0\,\mathrm{kW}$. The value $2.8\,\mathrm{kW}$ is obtained by using the coefficient of performance as a refrigerator, $\varepsilon = \varepsilon_{\text{hp}} - 1 = 2.5$, instead of that of the heat pump.
