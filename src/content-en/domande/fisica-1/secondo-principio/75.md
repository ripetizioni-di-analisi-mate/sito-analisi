Two reservoirs at constant temperatures exchange a positive amount of heat from hot to cold. The cold reservoir is at $300\,\mathrm{K}$. The initial data are the heat transferred $Q = 1200\,\mathrm{J}$ and the hot reservoir temperature $T = 600\,\mathrm{K}$. In the second configuration, change the heat transferred to $1800\,\mathrm{J}$ and the hot reservoir temperature to $450\,\mathrm{K}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the final value of $S$ minus its initial value?

- $0\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $4\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $2\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $1\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

The cold reservoir gains entropy $Q/300$ and the hot reservoir loses $Q/T$. The sum is positive. The relation $S = \dfrac{Q}{300} - \dfrac{Q}{T}$ gives $2\,\mathrm{J/K}$ before the change and $2\,\mathrm{J/K}$ after it. Subtracting the initial value from the final value gives $0\,\mathrm{J/K}$.
