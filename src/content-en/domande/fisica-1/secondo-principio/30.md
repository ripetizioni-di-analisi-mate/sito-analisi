A system goes through a cycle exchanging heat with three reservoirs: it absorbs $1200\,\mathrm{J}$ from a reservoir at $600\,\mathrm{K}$, absorbs $400\,\mathrm{J}$ from a reservoir at $400\,\mathrm{K}$ and releases $1000\,\mathrm{J}$ to a reservoir at $300\,\mathrm{K}$. Denoting by $Q_i$ the heat absorbed by the system from the reservoir at temperature $T_i$, what is the value of the sum $\displaystyle\sum_i \frac{Q_i}{T_i}$ computed over the cycle?

- $0{,}333\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $6{,}33\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $-1{,}33\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $-0{,}333\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

In the Clausius inequality $Q_i$ is the heat absorbed by the system, therefore the heat released to the reservoir at $300\,\mathrm{K}$ enters with the minus sign. Hence
$$
\sum_i \frac{Q_i}{T_i} = \left(\frac{1200}{600} + \frac{400}{400} - \frac{1000}{300}\right)\mathrm{J/K} = (2{,}00 + 1{,}00 - 3{,}33)\,\mathrm{J/K} \approx -0{,}333\,\mathrm{J/K} .
$$
The sum is negative, therefore the **Clausius inequality** is satisfied with the strict less-than sign and the cycle is possible but irreversible.
