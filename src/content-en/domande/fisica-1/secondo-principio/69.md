One mole of ideal gas fills the whole volume of a rigid, insulated container. It is hypothesized that the gas spontaneously gathers in the left half of the container, at the same temperature, leaving the right half empty. Which statement is true?

- The process is ruled out by the first law, because the internal energy of the gas would change
- The process is possible, because the gas exchanges no heat and therefore its entropy stays constant
- The process is ruled out, because the entropy of the gas would decrease by about $5.76\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- The process is ruled out, because the entropy of the gas would increase by about $5.76\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

The initial and final temperatures are equal, therefore the internal energy of the ideal gas does not change and the first law is satisfied. The change in entropy is computed along a reversible isotherm between the same states, and it is
$$
\Delta S = R \ln\frac{1}{2} = -8.314 \cdot \ln 2\,\mathrm{J/K} \approx -5.76\,\mathrm{J/K} .
$$
The container is insulated, hence the **principle of entropy increase** rules out every process with $\Delta S < 0$. The reverse process, the free expansion, has $\Delta S \approx +5.76\,\mathrm{J/K}$ and is the one that occurs spontaneously.
