An ideal gas expands at constant temperature. Use $R\simeq8\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$. The initial data are the amount of gas $n = 2\,\mathrm{mol}$ and the final-to-initial volume ratio $b = 2$. Change the final-to-initial volume ratio to $4$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the ratio of the final value of $S$ to its initial value?

- $1$
- $3$
- $2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

Entropy is a state function. Integrating $\delta Q_{\mathrm{rev}}/T=nR\,dV/V$ along a reversible isotherm between the same states gives the gas's entropy change. The relation $S = 8 \cdot n \cdot \log{\left(b \right)}$ gives $16 \cdot \log{\left(2 \right)}\,\mathrm{J/K}$ before the change and $32 \cdot \log{\left(2 \right)}\,\mathrm{J/K}$ after it. The ratio is $2$.
