The fluid of a refrigerator goes through a quasi-static cycle that encloses, in the $(V, p)$ plane, an area corresponding to $150\,\mathrm{J}$. In each cycle the fluid extracts $600\,\mathrm{J}$ from the refrigerator compartment. What is the coefficient of performance of the refrigerator?

- $5.0$
- $0.25$
- $3.0$
- $4.0$

## Explanation

The cycle of a refrigerator is traversed counterclockwise, and the area enclosed in the $(V, p)$ plane is the work received by the fluid, therefore $|L| = 150\,\mathrm{J}$. The coefficient of performance is the ratio of the heat extracted from the cold reservoir to the work spent, hence
$$
\varepsilon = \frac{Q_2}{|L|} = \frac{600}{150} = 4.0 .
$$
The value $5.0$ is the coefficient of performance of the same machine used as a heat pump, $\dfrac{600 + 150}{150}$.
