An irreversible heat engine takes heat from a reservoir at $500\,\mathrm{K}$ and releases heat to one at $300\,\mathrm{K}$. The actual efficiency is known. Given the heat absorbed per cycle $Q = 1000\,\mathrm{J}$ and the heat rejected per cycle $Q_{f} = 700\,\mathrm{J}$, what is the actual efficiency $\eta$?

- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{20}$
- $\dfrac{3}{10}$
- $\dfrac{7}{10}$

## Explanation

Over a cycle the internal energy change is zero. Useful work is $\eta Q$, so the cold reservoir receives $Q-\eta Q$. In the stated units, the relation is $Q_{f} = Q \cdot \left(1 - \eta\right)$. Solving for the unknown gives $\eta = \dfrac{Q - Q_{f}}{Q}$. Substituting the given values yields $\dfrac{3}{10}$.
