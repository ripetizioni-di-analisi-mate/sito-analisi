A cyclic engine absorbs $500\,\mathrm J$ from a single reservoir and claims to turn all of it into work with no other effect. Which statement is correct?

- The engine violates the first law because the heat received is positive
- The energy balance holds, but the engine violates the second law
- The engine is possible if the cycle is reversible
- The engine is possible if its work equals the absorbed heat

## Explanation

The first law is satisfied with $\Delta U=0$ and $Q=W$. The Kelvin-Planck statement rules out complete conversion to work as the only effect of a cycle using one reservoir.
