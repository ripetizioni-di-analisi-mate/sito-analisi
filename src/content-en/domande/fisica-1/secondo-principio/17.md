An air conditioner extracts a thermal power of $3.5\,\mathrm{kW}$ from a room and has coefficient of performance $\varepsilon = 3.5$. How much electrical energy does it use in $8$ hours of operation?

- $8.0\,\mathrm{kWh}$
- $28\,\mathrm{kWh}$
- $36\,\mathrm{kWh}$
- $98\,\mathrm{kWh}$

## Explanation

The coefficient of performance is $\varepsilon = \dfrac{Q_2}{|L|}$, therefore the electrical power drawn is
$$
P = \frac{3.5\,\mathrm{kW}}{3.5} = 1.0\,\mathrm{kW} .
$$
In $8$ hours the energy used is therefore $1.0\,\mathrm{kW} \cdot 8\,\mathrm{h} = 8.0\,\mathrm{kWh}$. The value $28\,\mathrm{kWh}$ is the heat extracted from the room, while $36\,\mathrm{kWh}$ is the heat released outdoors, the sum of the heat extracted and the electrical energy.
