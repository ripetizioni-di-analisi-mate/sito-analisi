A refrigerator with coefficient of performance $\varepsilon = 4{,}0$ receives $150\,\mathrm{J}$ of work from its electric motor every second. What heating power does it deliver to the kitchen?

- $600\,\mathrm{W}$
- $750\,\mathrm{W}$
- $450\,\mathrm{W}$
- $150\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

By the definition of coefficient of performance, $\varepsilon = \dfrac{Q_2}{|L|}$, the heat extracted from the interior in one second is $Q_2 = 4{,}0 \cdot 150\,\mathrm{J} = 600\,\mathrm{J}$. The kitchen receives both this heat and the equivalent of the work, $|Q_1| = Q_2 + |L| = 750\,\mathrm{J}$. Therefore the thermal power delivered to the kitchen is $750\,\mathrm{W}$. The value $600\,\mathrm{W}$ is the thermal power extracted from the interior.
