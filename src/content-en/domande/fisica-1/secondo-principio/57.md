A Carnot engine works between two reservoirs and has efficiency $0.20$. The same machine is run in reverse, between the same reservoirs, as a heat pump. What is its coefficient of performance $\varepsilon_{\text{pc}}$?

- $4.0$
- $0.80$
- $1.25$
- $5.0$

## Explanation

The Carnot cycle traversed in reverse exchanges the same heats and the same work with the signs changed. As an engine the machine has $\eta_C = \dfrac{L}{Q_1}$, where $Q_1$ is the heat exchanged with the hot reservoir. As a heat pump it delivers the same heat $|Q_1|$ to the hot reservoir while receiving the same work $|L|$, therefore
$$
\varepsilon_{\text{pc}} = \frac{|Q_1|}{|L|} = \frac{1}{\eta_C} = \frac{1}{0.20} = 5.0 .
$$
The value $4.0$ is the coefficient of performance of the same machine used as a refrigerator, $\varepsilon_{\text{pc}} - 1$, and $1.25 = \dfrac{1}{1 - \eta_C}$ is obtained by dividing the heat delivered to the hot reservoir by the heat extracted from the cold reservoir instead of by the work.
