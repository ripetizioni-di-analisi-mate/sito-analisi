A reversible refrigerator exchanges heat with a cold reservoir at $270\,\mathrm{K}$ and a warmer reservoir at temperature $T$. Given the hot reservoir temperature $T = 300\,\mathrm{K}$ and the work input per cycle $W = 90\,\mathrm{J}$, what is the heat removed from the cold reservoir per cycle $Q$?

- $1620\,\mathrm{J}$
- $81\,\mathrm{J}$
- $810\,\mathrm{J}$
- $900\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The reversible coefficient of performance is $270/(T-270)$. Work input is the extracted heat divided by that coefficient. In the stated units, the relation is $W = \dfrac{Q \cdot \left(T - 270\right)}{270}$. Solving for the unknown gives $Q = \dfrac{270 \cdot W}{T - 270}$. Substituting the given values yields $810\,\mathrm{J}$.
