Consider a machine that goes through one cycle after another and in each cycle produces $50\,\mathrm{J}$ of work without absorbing heat from any body. Which statement is true?

- The machine is ruled out only by the Kelvin-Planck statement
- The machine is ruled out by the first law
- The machine is possible, if the cycle is reversible
- The machine is ruled out only by the Clausius statement

## Explanation

In a cycle the final state coincides with the initial one, therefore $\Delta U = 0$ and the first law gives $L = Q$. The machine absorbs no heat, hence $Q \le 0$ and the work produced cannot be positive. A work of $50\,\mathrm{J}$ per cycle is therefore ruled out by the **first law**, whatever the way the cycle is traversed. The statements of the second law concern instead machines that satisfy the energy balance.
