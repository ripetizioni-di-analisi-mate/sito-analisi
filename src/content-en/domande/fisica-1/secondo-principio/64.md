A heat pump keeps a room warm by delivering heat to it. In each cycle it takes in work and draws heat from outdoors. The initial data are the heat drawn from outdoors $Q = 1200\,\mathrm{J}$ and the work input $W = 400\,\mathrm{J}$. In the second configuration, change the work input to $600\,\mathrm{J}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the ratio of the final value of $b$ to its initial value?

- $\dfrac{9}{8}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## Explanation

The room receives the extracted heat plus the work input. The heating coefficient of performance is delivered heat divided by work input. The relation $b = \dfrac{Q + W}{W}$ gives $4$ before the change and $3$ after it. The ratio is $\dfrac{3}{4}$.
