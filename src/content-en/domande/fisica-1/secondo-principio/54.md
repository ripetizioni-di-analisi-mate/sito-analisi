An irreversible heat engine takes heat from a reservoir at $500\,\mathrm{K}$ and releases heat to one at $300\,\mathrm{K}$. The actual efficiency is known. The initial data are the heat absorbed per cycle $Q = 1000\,\mathrm{J}$ and the actual efficiency $\eta = \dfrac{3}{10}$. Change the actual efficiency to $\dfrac{1}{5}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the ratio of the final value of $Q_{f}$ to its initial value?

- $1$
- $\dfrac{8}{7}$
- $\dfrac{7}{8}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

Over a cycle the internal energy change is zero. Useful work is $\eta Q$, so the cold reservoir receives $Q-\eta Q$. The relation $Q_{f} = Q \cdot \left(1 - \eta\right)$ gives $700\,\mathrm{J}$ before the change and $800\,\mathrm{J}$ after it. The ratio is $\dfrac{8}{7}$.
