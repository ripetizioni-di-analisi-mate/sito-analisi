A reversible refrigerator exchanges heat with a cold reservoir at $270\,\mathrm{K}$ and a warmer reservoir at temperature $T$. The initial data are the heat removed from the cold reservoir per cycle $Q = 810\,\mathrm{J}$ and the hot reservoir temperature $T = 300\,\mathrm{K}$. In the second configuration, change the heat removed from the cold reservoir per cycle to $540\,\mathrm{J}$ and the hot reservoir temperature to $315\,\mathrm{K}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the final value of $W$ minus its initial value?

- $-270\,\mathrm{J}$
- $- \dfrac{27}{7}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $90\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The reversible coefficient of performance is $270/(T-270)$. Work input is the extracted heat divided by that coefficient. The relation $W = \dfrac{Q \cdot \left(T - 270\right)}{270}$ gives $90\,\mathrm{J}$ before the change and $90\,\mathrm{J}$ after it. Subtracting the initial value from the final value gives $0\,\mathrm{J}$.
