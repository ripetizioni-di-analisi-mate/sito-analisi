An ideal gas occupies part of an insulated vessel. An internal partition is removed and the gas expands freely into vacuum until equilibrium. Use $R\simeq8\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$. Given the final-to-initial volume ratio $b = 3$ and the entropy increase of the gas $S = 16 \cdot \log{\left(3 \right)}\,\mathrm{J/K}$, what is the amount of gas $n$?

- $2\,\mathrm{mol}$
- $1\,\mathrm{mol}$
- $4\,\mathrm{mol}$
- $\log{\left(3 \right)}\,\mathrm{mol}$

## Explanation

Free expansion has $Q=W=0$, so internal energy and temperature are unchanged between equilibrium states. Entropy is calculated along a reversible isotherm joining those states. In the stated units, the relation is $S = 8 \cdot n \cdot \log{\left(b \right)}$. Solving for the unknown gives $n = \dfrac{S}{8 \cdot \log{\left(b \right)}}$. Substituting the given values yields $2\,\mathrm{mol}$.
