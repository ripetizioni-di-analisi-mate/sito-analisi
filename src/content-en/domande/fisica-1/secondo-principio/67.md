An ideal gas expands at constant temperature. Use $R\simeq8\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$. Given the final-to-initial volume ratio $b = 2$ and the entropy change $S = 16 \cdot \log{\left(2 \right)}\,\mathrm{J/K}$, what is the amount of gas $n$?

- $2\,\mathrm{mol}$
- $4\,\mathrm{mol}$
- $1\,\mathrm{mol}$
- $2 \cdot \log{\left(2 \right)}\,\mathrm{mol}$

## Explanation

Entropy is a state function. Integrating $\delta Q_{\mathrm{rev}}/T=nR\,dV/V$ along a reversible isotherm between the same states gives the gas's entropy change. In the stated units, the relation is $S = 8 \cdot n \cdot \log{\left(b \right)}$. Solving for the unknown gives $n = \dfrac{S}{8 \cdot \log{\left(b \right)}}$. Substituting the given values yields $2\,\mathrm{mol}$.
