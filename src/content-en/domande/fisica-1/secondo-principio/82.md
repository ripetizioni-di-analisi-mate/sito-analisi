An ideal gas occupies part of an insulated vessel. An internal partition is removed and the gas expands freely into vacuum until equilibrium. Use $R\simeq8\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$. Given the amount of gas $n = 2\,\mathrm{mol}$ and the final-to-initial volume ratio $b = 3$, what is the entropy increase of the gas $S$?

- $32 \cdot \log{\left(3 \right)}\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $16 \cdot \log{\left(3 \right)}\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $8 \cdot \log{\left(3 \right)}\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $32\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

Free expansion has $Q=W=0$, so internal energy and temperature are unchanged between equilibrium states. Entropy is calculated along a reversible isotherm joining those states. In the stated units, the relation is $S = 8 \cdot n \cdot \log{\left(b \right)}$. Substituting the given values yields $16 \cdot \log{\left(3 \right)}\,\mathrm{J/K}$.
