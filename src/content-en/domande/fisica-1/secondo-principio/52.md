One mole of monatomic ideal gas goes reversibly through a cycle made of three processes: isochoric heating $A \to B$ from $300\,\mathrm{K}$ to $600\,\mathrm{K}$, isothermal expansion $B \to C$ at $600\,\mathrm{K}$ down to the initial pressure, isobaric compression $C \to A$. What is the efficiency of the cycle?

- $0.500$
- $0.257$
- $0.134$
- $0.866$

## Explanation

On the isochore the gas absorbs $Q_{AB} = \dfrac{3}{2} R \cdot 300\,\mathrm{K} \approx 3741\,\mathrm{J}$. On the isotherm the pressure halves, therefore the volume doubles and the gas absorbs $Q_{BC} = R \cdot 600\,\mathrm{K} \cdot \ln 2 \approx 3458\,\mathrm{J}$. On the isobar the temperature goes back from $600\,\mathrm{K}$ to $300\,\mathrm{K}$, hence the gas releases $|Q_{CA}| = \dfrac{5}{2} R \cdot 300\,\mathrm{K} \approx 6236\,\mathrm{J}$. The work is $L = 3741\,\mathrm{J} + 3458\,\mathrm{J} - 6236\,\mathrm{J} \approx 963\,\mathrm{J}$, therefore
$$
\eta = \frac{963}{3741 + 3458} \approx 0.134 .
$$
The value $0.500$ is the Carnot efficiency between $600\,\mathrm{K}$ and $300\,\mathrm{K}$, and $0.257$ is obtained by neglecting the heat absorbed on the isotherm.
