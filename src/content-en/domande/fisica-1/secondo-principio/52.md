An irreversible heat engine takes heat from a reservoir at $500\,\mathrm{K}$ and releases heat to one at $300\,\mathrm{K}$. The actual efficiency is known. Given the actual efficiency $\eta = \dfrac{3}{10}$ and the heat rejected per cycle $Q_{f} = 700\,\mathrm{J}$, what is the heat absorbed per cycle $Q$?

- $\dfrac{3500}{3}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $2000\,\mathrm{J}$
- $\dfrac{7000}{3}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Over a cycle the internal energy change is zero. Useful work is $\eta Q$, so the cold reservoir receives $Q-\eta Q$. In the stated units, the relation is $Q_{f} = Q \cdot \left(1 - \eta\right)$. Solving for the unknown gives $Q = - \dfrac{Q_{f}}{\eta - 1}$. Substituting the given values yields $1000\,\mathrm{J}$.
