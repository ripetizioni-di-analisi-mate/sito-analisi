A Carnot engine operates between $600\,\mathrm{K}$ and $400\,\mathrm{K}$ and releases $800\,\mathrm{J}$ per cycle to the cold reservoir. How much work does it produce in one cycle?

- $267\,\mathrm{J}$
- $400\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1200\,\mathrm{J}$
- $2000\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

For the Carnot cycle $\dfrac{|Q_2|}{Q_1} = \dfrac{T_2}{T_1}$, therefore the heat absorbed is $Q_1 = 800\,\mathrm{J} \cdot \dfrac{600}{400} = 1200\,\mathrm{J}$. The work is the difference between the heat absorbed and the heat released, hence $L = 1200\,\mathrm{J} - 800\,\mathrm{J} = 400\,\mathrm{J}$. The value $267\,\mathrm{J}$ is obtained by multiplying the efficiency $\dfrac{1}{3}$ by the heat released instead of the heat absorbed.
