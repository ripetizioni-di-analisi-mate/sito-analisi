A monatomic ideal gas is heated at constant volume. Use $R\simeq8\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$. Given the final-to-initial absolute temperature ratio $b = 2$ and the entropy change $S = 36 \cdot \log{\left(2 \right)}\,\mathrm{J/K}$, what is the amount of gas $n$?

- $6\,\mathrm{mol}$
- $\dfrac{9}{5}\,\mathrm{mol}$
- $3 \cdot \log{\left(2 \right)}\,\mathrm{mol}$
- $3\,\mathrm{mol}$

## Explanation

At constant volume, reversible heat is $nC_V\,dT$. Integrate $nC_V\,dT/T$ with $C_V=3R/2$. In the stated units, the relation is $S = 12 \cdot n \cdot \log{\left(b \right)}$. Solving for the unknown gives $n = \dfrac{S}{12 \cdot \log{\left(b \right)}}$. Substituting the given values yields $3\,\mathrm{mol}$.
