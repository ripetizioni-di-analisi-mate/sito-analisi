A reversible refrigerator exchanges heat with a cold reservoir at $270\,\mathrm{K}$ and a warmer reservoir at temperature $T$. Given the heat removed from the cold reservoir per cycle $Q = 810\,\mathrm{J}$ and the work input per cycle $W = 90\,\mathrm{J}$, what is the hot reservoir temperature $T$?

- $600\,\mathrm{K}$
- $300\,\mathrm{K}$
- $30\,\mathrm{K}$
- $\dfrac{1215}{4}\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

The reversible coefficient of performance is $270/(T-270)$. Work input is the extracted heat divided by that coefficient. In the stated units, the relation is $W = \dfrac{Q \cdot \left(T - 270\right)}{270}$. Solving for the unknown gives $T = 270 + \dfrac{270 \cdot W}{Q}$. Substituting the given values yields $300\,\mathrm{K}$.
