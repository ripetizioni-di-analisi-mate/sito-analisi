An ideal gas occupies part of an insulated vessel. An internal partition is removed and the gas expands freely into vacuum until it reaches a new equilibrium. Use $R\simeq8\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$. Given the amount of gas $n = 2\,\mathrm{mol}$ and the entropy increase of the gas $S = 16 \cdot \log{\left(3 \right)}\,\mathrm{J/K}$, what is the final-to-initial volume ratio $b$?

- $1 + \log{\left(3 \right)}$
- $6$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $3$

## Explanation

Free expansion has $Q=W=0$, so internal energy and temperature are unchanged between equilibrium states. Entropy is calculated along a reversible isotherm joining those states. In the stated units, the relation is $S = 8 \cdot n \cdot \log{\left(b \right)}$. Solving for the unknown gives $b = e^{\dfrac{S}{8 \cdot n}}$. Substituting the given values yields $3$.
