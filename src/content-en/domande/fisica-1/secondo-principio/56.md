A reversible refrigerator exchanges heat with a cold reservoir at $270\,\mathrm{K}$ and a warmer reservoir at temperature $T$. Given the heat removed from the cold reservoir per cycle $Q = 810\,\mathrm{J}$ and the hot reservoir temperature $T = 300\,\mathrm{K}$, what is the work input per cycle $W$?

- $180\,\mathrm{J}$
- $900\,\mathrm{J}$
- $81\,\mathrm{J}$
- $90\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The reversible coefficient of performance is $270/(T-270)$. Work input is the extracted heat divided by that coefficient. In the stated units, the relation is $W = \dfrac{Q \cdot \left(T - 270\right)}{270}$. Substituting the given values yields $90\,\mathrm{J}$.
