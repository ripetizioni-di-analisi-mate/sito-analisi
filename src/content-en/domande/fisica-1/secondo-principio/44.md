Two bodies, with heat capacities $C_1 = 50\,\mathrm{J/K}$ and $C_2 = 150\,\mathrm{J/K}$, are at the temperatures $500\,\mathrm{K}$ and $300\,\mathrm{K}$ in an insulated container. The hot body gives a heat $Q$ to the cold body. For which value of $Q$ is the entropy of the system maximum?

- $5.0\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $10\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $15\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{kJ}$

## Explanation

The entropy of the system increases as long as heat flows from the hotter body to the colder one, therefore it is maximum when the two temperatures are equal. The common temperature $T_e$ follows from energy conservation, $C_1 (500\,\mathrm{K} - T_e) = C_2 (T_e - 300\,\mathrm{K})$, that is
$$
T_e = \frac{50 \cdot 500 + 150 \cdot 300}{50 + 150}\,\mathrm{K} = 350\,\mathrm{K} .
$$
Hence the heat transferred is $Q = C_1 \cdot (500 - 350)\,\mathrm{K} = 7.5\,\mathrm{kJ}$. The value $5.0\,\mathrm{kJ}$ is obtained by taking the arithmetic mean $400\,\mathrm{K}$ as final temperature, which holds only for equal heat capacities.
