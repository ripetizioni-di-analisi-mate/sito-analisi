A heat pump keeps a room warm by delivering heat to it. In each cycle it takes in work and draws heat from outdoors. Given the heat drawn from outdoors $Q = 1200\,\mathrm{J}$ and the heating coefficient of performance $b = 4$, what is the work input $W$?

- $400\,\mathrm{J}$
- $300\,\mathrm{J}$
- $3600\,\mathrm{J}$
- $800\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The room receives the extracted heat plus the work input. The heating coefficient of performance is delivered heat divided by work input. In the stated units, the relation is $b = \dfrac{Q + W}{W}$. Solving for the unknown gives $W = \dfrac{Q}{b - 1}$. Substituting the given values yields $400\,\mathrm{J}$.
