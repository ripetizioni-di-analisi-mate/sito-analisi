A system goes through a reversible cycle exchanging heat with three reservoirs: it absorbs $900\,\mathrm{J}$ from a reservoir at $450\,\mathrm{K}$, absorbs a heat $Q$ from a reservoir at $300\,\mathrm{K}$ and releases $1500\,\mathrm{J}$ to a reservoir at $250\,\mathrm{K}$. What is the value of $Q$?

- $600\,\mathrm{J}$
- $2400\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1200\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1800\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The cycle is reversible, therefore the **Clausius inequality** holds with the equality sign and
$$
\frac{900\,\mathrm{J}}{450\,\mathrm{K}} + \frac{Q}{300\,\mathrm{K}} - \frac{1500\,\mathrm{J}}{250\,\mathrm{K}} = 0 .
$$
Since $\dfrac{900}{450} = 2.0$ and $\dfrac{1500}{250} = 6.0$, one obtains $\dfrac{Q}{300\,\mathrm{K}} = 4.0\,\mathrm{J/K}$, that is, $Q = 1200\,\mathrm{J}$. The value $600\,\mathrm{J}$ is obtained by requiring the heat absorbed to equal the heat released, that is, by forgetting the work produced in the cycle.
