A heat pump warms a room by delivering heat to it. In each cycle it consumes work and draws heat from outdoors. Given the heat drawn from outdoors $Q = 1200\,\mathrm{J}$ and the work input $W = 400\,\mathrm{J}$, what is the heating coefficient of performance $b$?

- $3$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $4$
- $8$

## Explanation

The room receives the extracted heat plus the work input. The heating coefficient of performance is delivered heat divided by work input. In the stated units, the relation is $b = \dfrac{Q + W}{W}$. Substituting the given values yields $4$.
