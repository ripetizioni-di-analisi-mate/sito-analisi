A body with constant heat capacity is heated by a reservoir. Its initial temperature is $300\,\mathrm{K}$. Consider the entropy change of the body alone. Given the heat capacity of the body $C = 400\,\mathrm{J/K}$ and the entropy change $S = - 400 \cdot \log{\left(5 \right)} + 400 \cdot \log{\left(6 \right)}\,\mathrm{J/K}$, what is the final body temperature $T$?

- $- 300 \cdot \log{\left(5 \right)} + 300 + 300 \cdot \log{\left(6 \right)}\,\mathrm{K}$
- $360\,\mathrm{K}$
- $720\,\mathrm{K}$
- $\dfrac{300}{- \log{\left(6 \right)} + 1 + \log{\left(5 \right)}}\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

Calculate the entropy change by integrating $C\,dT/T$ between the initial and final temperatures along an equivalent reversible heating path. In the stated units, the relation is $S = \log{\left(\left(\dfrac{T}{300}\right)^{C} \right)}$. Solving for the unknown gives $T = 300 \cdot e^{\dfrac{S}{C}}$. Substituting the given values yields $360\,\mathrm{K}$.
