Which of the following processes is reversible, in the ideal model of thermodynamics?

- The cooling of a cup of coffee resting on the table of a colder room
- The quasi-static, frictionless compression of a gas in contact with a reservoir at the same temperature as the gas
- The free expansion of a gas into vacuum
- The slowing down to rest of a block sliding on a plane with friction

## Explanation

A process is reversible if it is quasi-static, free of dissipative effects, and if heat is exchanged only between bodies whose temperatures differ by an infinitesimal amount. The quasi-static compression without friction, with the gas at the same temperature as the reservoir, satisfies all these conditions. The coffee cools by exchanging heat with surroundings at a different temperature, the free expansion is not quasi-static and the block stops because of friction, therefore these three processes are **irreversible**.
