A system absorbs $6000\,\mathrm{J}$ reversibly at the constant temperature of $127\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. What is its change in entropy?

- $47{,}2\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $15{,}0\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $22{,}0\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $-15{,}0\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

In a reversible process at constant temperature $\Delta S = \dfrac{Q}{T}$, with $T$ in kelvins. The temperature is $T = (127 + 273)\,\mathrm{K} = 400\,\mathrm{K}$, hence $\Delta S = \dfrac{6000}{400}\,\mathrm{J/K} = 15{,}0\,\mathrm{J/K}$. The sign is positive because the system absorbs heat. Dividing by the temperature in degrees Celsius would give $47{,}2\,\mathrm{J/K}$.
