In a Carnot cycle $0{,}50\,\mathrm{mol}$ of ideal gas operates between $500\,\mathrm{K}$ and $300\,\mathrm{K}$, and in the isothermal expansion the volume of the gas doubles. How much work does the cycle produce?

- $864\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1{,}44 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{J}$
- $576\,\mathrm{J}$
- $831\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Along the isotherm at $T_1 = 500\,\mathrm{K}$ the internal energy does not change, therefore the heat absorbed equals the work of the isothermal process,
$$
Q_1 = nRT_1 \ln 2 = 0{,}50 \cdot 8{,}314 \cdot 500 \cdot \ln 2\,\mathrm{J} \approx 1{,}44 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
The Carnot efficiency is $\eta_C = 1 - \dfrac{300}{500} = 0{,}40$, hence the work of the cycle is $L = 0{,}40 \cdot 1{,}44 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{J} \approx 576\,\mathrm{J}$. The value $864\,\mathrm{J}$ is the heat released to the cold reservoir.
