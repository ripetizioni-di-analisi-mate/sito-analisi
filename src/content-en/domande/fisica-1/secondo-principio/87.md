A reversible cyclic engine exchanges heat with just two reservoirs. The hot reservoir is at $480\,\mathrm{K}$. Given the heat absorbed from the hot reservoir $Q = 960\,\mathrm{J}$ and the heat rejected to the cold reservoir $Q_{f} = 600\,\mathrm{J}$, what is the cold reservoir temperature $T$?

- $300\,\mathrm{K}$
- $768\,\mathrm{K}$
- $180\,\mathrm{K}$
- $600\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

The reversible entropy balance of the reservoirs is $-Q/480+Q_f/T=0$. In the stated units, the relation is $Q_{f} = \dfrac{Q \cdot T}{480}$. Solving for the unknown gives $T = \dfrac{480 \cdot Q_{f}}{Q}$. Substituting the given values yields $300\,\mathrm{K}$.
