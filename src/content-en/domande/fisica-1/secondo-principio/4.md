- $412\,\mathrm{K}$
- $500\,\mathrm{K}$
- $168\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

By **Carnot's theorem** a reversible engine between $T_1$ and $T_2$ has efficiency $\eta = 1 - \dfrac{T_2}{T_1}$. Therefore $\dfrac{T_2}{T_1} = 1 - 0{,}42 = 0{,}58$, and the temperature of the hot reservoir is
$$
T_1 = \frac{290\,\mathrm{K}}{0{,}58} = 500\,\mathrm{K} .
$$
The value $690\,\mathrm{K}$ is obtained by dividing by $0{,}42$, that is, by confusing the efficiency with the ratio of the temperatures.
