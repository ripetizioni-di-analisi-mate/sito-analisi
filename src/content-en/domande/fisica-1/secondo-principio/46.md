A Carnot engine runs between a hot reservoir at $600\,\mathrm{K}$ and a cold reservoir. Heat quantities are given as magnitudes. Given the heat absorbed per cycle $Q = 900\,\mathrm{J}$ and the cold reservoir temperature $T = 300\,\mathrm{K}$, what is the useful work per cycle $W$?

- $225\,\mathrm{J}$
- $450\,\mathrm{J}$
- $675\,\mathrm{J}$
- $900\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Carnot efficiency is $1-T_{\mathrm{cold}}/T_{\mathrm{hot}}$, with temperatures in kelvin. Work is efficiency times absorbed heat. In the stated units, the relation is $W = \dfrac{Q \cdot \left(600 - T\right)}{600}$. Substituting the given values yields $450\,\mathrm{J}$.
