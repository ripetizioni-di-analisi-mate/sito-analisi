A block melts completely at its melting temperature. Its specific latent heat is $120\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$. Given the absolute melting temperature $T = 300\,\mathrm{K}$ and the block's entropy change $S = 200\,\mathrm{J/K}$, what is the mass $m$?

- $1\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $500\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Isothermal melting requires $Q=mL$. The entropy change is that heat in joules divided by absolute temperature. In the stated units, the relation is $S = \dfrac{120000 \cdot m}{T}$. Solving for the unknown gives $m = \dfrac{S \cdot T}{120000}$. Substituting the given values yields $\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{kg}$.
