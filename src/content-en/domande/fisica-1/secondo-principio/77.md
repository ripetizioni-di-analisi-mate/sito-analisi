An ideal gas is enclosed in a rigid, insulated container, and a paddle wheel driven from outside stirs it until its temperature rises. Which statement about the change in entropy of the gas is true?

- It is positive, and it is computed along a reversible isochoric heating between the same states
- It is zero, because the gas exchanges no heat
- It is negative, because the gas receives work from the outside
- It is positive only if the gas releases heat to the outside

## Explanation

The gas exchanges no heat, therefore the Clausius integral along the actual process is zero. The process is irreversible, however, hence $\Delta S = 0$ does not follow from $Q = 0$. Entropy is a state function, therefore its change is computed along a reversible process between the same states, an isochoric heating, and it is $\Delta S = n c_V \ln\dfrac{T_B}{T_A} > 0$ because $T_B > T_A$. The result agrees with the theorem on entropy in irreversible processes, which for $\delta Q = 0$ gives $\Delta S \geq 0$.
