Two moles of diatomic ideal gas, with $c_p = \dfrac{7}{2}R$, are cooled at constant pressure from $400\,\mathrm{K}$ to $200\,\mathrm{K}$. What is the change in entropy of the gas?

- $40.3\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $-40.3\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $-28.8\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $-17.5\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

At constant pressure the term $nR\ln\dfrac{p_B}{p_A}$ is zero, therefore $\Delta S = n c_p \ln\dfrac{T_B}{T_A}$. One has $\ln\dfrac{200}{400} = -\ln 2$, hence
$$
\Delta S = -2 \cdot \frac{7}{2} \cdot 8.314 \cdot \ln 2\,\mathrm{J/K} \approx -40.3\,\mathrm{J/K} .
$$
The entropy of the gas decreases because the gas releases heat. The value $-28.8\,\mathrm{J/K}$ corresponds to cooling at constant volume, with $c_V = \dfrac{5}{2}R$.
