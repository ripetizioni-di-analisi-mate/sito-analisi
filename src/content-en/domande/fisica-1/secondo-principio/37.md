One mole of monatomic ideal gas, with $c_V = \dfrac{3}{2}R$, goes through an irreversible process from the state with $T_A = 300\,\mathrm{K}$ and $V_A = 10\,\mathrm{L}$ to the state with $T_B = 600\,\mathrm{K}$ and $V_B = 20\,\mathrm{L}$. What is the change in entropy of the gas?

- $8.64\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $5.76\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $2.88\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $14.4\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

Entropy is a state function, therefore the formula for the ideal gas also holds for an irreversible process. The ratios $\dfrac{T_B}{T_A}$ and $\dfrac{V_B}{V_A}$ are both equal to $2$, hence
$$
\Delta S = n c_V \ln 2 + nR \ln 2 = \left(\frac{3}{2} + 1\right) \cdot 8.314 \cdot \ln 2\,\mathrm{J/K} \approx 14.4\,\mathrm{J/K} .
$$
The value $8.64\,\mathrm{J/K}$ accounts only for the change in temperature, and $5.76\,\mathrm{J/K}$ only for the change in volume.
