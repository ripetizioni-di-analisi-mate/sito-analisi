A monatomic ideal gas is heated at constant volume. Use $R\simeq8\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$. Given the amount of gas $n = 3\,\mathrm{mol}$ and the final-to-initial absolute temperature ratio $b = 2$, what is the entropy change $S$?

- $36 \cdot \log{\left(2 \right)}\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $36\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $72 \cdot \log{\left(2 \right)}\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $60 \cdot \log{\left(2 \right)}\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

At constant volume, reversible heat is $nC_V\,dT$. Integrate $nC_V\,dT/T$ with $C_V=3R/2$. In the stated units, the relation is $S = 12 \cdot n \cdot \log{\left(b \right)}$. Substituting the given values yields $36 \cdot \log{\left(2 \right)}\,\mathrm{J/K}$.
