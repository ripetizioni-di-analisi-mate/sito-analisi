One mole of ideal gas, in contact with a reservoir at $300\,\mathrm{K}$, doubles its volume in a reversible isothermal expansion. What is the change in entropy of the universe?

- $5.76\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $-5.76\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $11.5\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

The entropy of the gas increases by $\Delta S_{\text{gas}} = R \ln 2 \approx 5.76\,\mathrm{J/K}$, and the gas absorbs the heat $Q = T\,\Delta S_{\text{gas}}$. The reservoir gives up the same heat at the same temperature, therefore its entropy decreases by $\dfrac{Q}{T} = 5.76\,\mathrm{J/K}$. Hence $\Delta S_{\text{u}} = 5.76\,\mathrm{J/K} - 5.76\,\mathrm{J/K} = 0$, as it must be for a reversible process. The value $5.76\,\mathrm{J/K}$ is the change in entropy of the gas alone.
