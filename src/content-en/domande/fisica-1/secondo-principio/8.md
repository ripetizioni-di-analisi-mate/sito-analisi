## Explanation

The reservoirs exchange heat at constant temperature, therefore for each of them $\Delta S = \dfrac{Q}{T}$, with $Q$ the heat absorbed. The hot reservoir absorbs $-5.0\,\mathrm{kJ}$ and the cold one $+5.0\,\mathrm{kJ}$, hence
$$
\Delta S_{\text{u}} = -\frac{5000}{600}\,\mathrm{J/K} + \frac{5000}{300}\,\mathrm{J/K} = \frac{5000}{600}\,\mathrm{J/K} \approx 8.33\,\mathrm{J/K} .
$$
The change is positive, in agreement with the **principle of entropy increase** for a spontaneous transfer of heat from the hot body to the cold body.
