In each cycle a Carnot engine absorbs $800\,\mathrm{J}$ from a reservoir at $400\,\mathrm{K}$ and releases heat to a reservoir at $300\,\mathrm{K}$. By how much does the entropy of the cold reservoir change in one cycle?

- $0\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $-2.0\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $2.7\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

For the Carnot cycle $\dfrac{|Q_2|}{Q_1} = \dfrac{T_2}{T_1}$, therefore the heat released to the cold reservoir is $|Q_2| = 800\,\mathrm{J} \cdot \dfrac{300}{400} = 600\,\mathrm{J}$. The cold reservoir absorbs this heat at the constant temperature of $300\,\mathrm{K}$, hence its entropy increases by $\dfrac{600\,\mathrm{J}}{300\,\mathrm{K}} = 2.0\,\mathrm{J/K}$. The hot reservoir loses the same entropy, $\dfrac{800\,\mathrm{J}}{400\,\mathrm{K}} = 2.0\,\mathrm{J/K}$, therefore the entropy of the universe does not change, as required for a reversible engine. The zero value is the change in entropy of the universe, and $2.7\,\mathrm{J/K}$ is obtained by dividing by $300\,\mathrm{K}$ the heat absorbed from the hot reservoir.
