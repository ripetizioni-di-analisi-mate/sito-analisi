A reversible cyclic engine exchanges heat with just two reservoirs. The hot reservoir is at $480\,\mathrm{K}$. Given the cold reservoir temperature $T = 300\,\mathrm{K}$ and the heat rejected to the cold reservoir $Q_{f} = 600\,\mathrm{J}$, what is the heat absorbed from the hot reservoir $Q$?

- $375\,\mathrm{J}$
- $960\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1600\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1920\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The reversible entropy balance of the reservoirs is $-Q/480+Q_f/T=0$. In the stated units, the relation is $Q_{f} = \dfrac{Q \cdot T}{480}$. Solving for the unknown gives $Q = \dfrac{480 \cdot Q_{f}}{T}$. Substituting the given values yields $960\,\mathrm{J}$.
