In the ideal Otto cycle of a gasoline engine the air, treated as a diatomic ideal gas with $\gamma = 1.4$, goes through four reversible processes: adiabatic compression from volume $V_1$ to volume $V_2 = \dfrac{V_1}{8}$, isochoric heating, adiabatic expansion back to $V_1$, isochoric cooling. What is the efficiency of the cycle?

- $0.875$
- $0.565$
- $0.435$
- $0.750$

## Explanation

The gas absorbs heat only on the isochore at volume $V_2$ and releases it only on the isochore at volume $V_1$, therefore, calling $T_A, T_B, T_C, T_D$ the temperatures at the start of each process,
$$
\eta = 1 - \frac{n c_V (T_D - T_A)}{n c_V (T_C - T_B)} = 1 - \frac{T_D - T_A}{T_C - T_B} .
$$
Along the two adiabats $T V^{\gamma - 1} = \text{constant}$ holds with the same volume ratio, hence $T_A = T_B \cdot 8^{1 - \gamma}$ and $T_D = T_C \cdot 8^{1 - \gamma}$. Substituting gives $\eta = 1 - 8^{-0.4} \approx 1 - 0.435 = 0.565$. The value $0.875$ is obtained from $1 - \dfrac{1}{8}$, without the exponent $\gamma - 1$, the value $0.750$ by using $\gamma = \dfrac{5}{3}$ of a monatomic gas, and $0.435$ is the temperature ratio $8^{-0.4}$, not the efficiency.
