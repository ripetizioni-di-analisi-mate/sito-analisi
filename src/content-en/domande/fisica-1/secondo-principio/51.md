An irreversible heat engine takes heat from a reservoir at $500\,\mathrm{K}$ and releases heat to one at $300\,\mathrm{K}$. The actual efficiency is known. Given the heat absorbed per cycle $Q = 1000\,\mathrm{J}$ and the actual efficiency $\eta = \dfrac{3}{10}$, what is the heat rejected per cycle $Q_{f}$?

- $700\,\mathrm{J}$
- $300\,\mathrm{J}$
- $600\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1400\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Over a cycle the internal energy change is zero. Useful work is $\eta Q$, so the cold reservoir receives $Q-\eta Q$. In the stated units, the relation is $Q_{f} = Q \cdot \left(1 - \eta\right)$. Substituting the given values yields $700\,\mathrm{J}$.
