An engine absorbs $1500\,\mathrm{J}$ and releases $1050\,\mathrm{J}$ in each cycle, and it completes $20$ cycles per second. What is the mechanical power of the engine?

- $9.0\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $30\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $21\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $0.45\,\mathrm{kW}$

## Explanation

In a cycle $\Delta U = 0$, therefore the work produced is $L = Q_{\text{in}} - Q_{\text{out}} = 1500\,\mathrm{J} - 1050\,\mathrm{J} = 450\,\mathrm{J}$. The engine completes $20$ cycles per second, hence in one second it produces $20 \cdot 450\,\mathrm{J} = 9000\,\mathrm{J}$. The power is therefore $9.0\,\mathrm{kW}$. The value $0.45\,\mathrm{kW}$ corresponds to the work of a single cycle.
