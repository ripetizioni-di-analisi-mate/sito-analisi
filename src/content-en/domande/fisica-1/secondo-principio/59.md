A reversible refrigerator exchanges heat with a cold reservoir at $270\,\mathrm{K}$ and a warmer reservoir at temperature $T$. The initial data are the heat removed from the cold reservoir per cycle $Q = 810\,\mathrm{J}$ and the hot reservoir temperature $T = 300\,\mathrm{K}$. Change the hot reservoir temperature to $330\,\mathrm{K}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the ratio of the final value of $W$ to its initial value?

- $2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{11}{10}$
- $\dfrac{20}{11}$

## Explanation

The reversible coefficient of performance is $270/(T-270)$. Work input is the extracted heat divided by that coefficient. The relation $W = \dfrac{Q \cdot \left(T - 270\right)}{270}$ gives $90\,\mathrm{J}$ before the change and $180\,\mathrm{J}$ after it. The ratio is $2$.
