A mass hangs at equilibrium from an ideal vertical spring. Take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The initial data are the mass $m = 3\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the spring constant $k = 150\,\mathrm{N/m}$. Change the spring constant to $300\,\mathrm{N/m}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the ratio of the final value of $x$ to its initial value?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $2$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

At equilibrium the upward spring force balances weight, so $kx=mg$. The relation $x = \dfrac{10 \cdot m}{k}$ gives $\dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{m}$ before the change and $\dfrac{1}{10}\,\mathrm{m}$ after it. The ratio is $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
