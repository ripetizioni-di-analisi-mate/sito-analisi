A crate rests on the horizontal bed of a truck, with coefficient of static friction $\mu_s = 0{,}40$ between crate and bed. What is the maximum acceleration of the truck for which the crate does not slide?

- $3{,}92\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $9{,}81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $24{,}5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $5{,}89\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

If the crate does not slide, it has the acceleration $a$ of the truck, and the only horizontal force that accelerates it is static friction; so $f_s = m a$, while along the vertical $N = m g$. The law of static friction requires $f_s \leq \mu_s N$, that is, $m a \leq \mu_s m g$, therefore $a \leq \mu_s g$. The maximum acceleration is therefore $\mu_s g = 0{,}40 \cdot 9{,}81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 3{,}92\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, and it does not depend on the mass of the crate.
