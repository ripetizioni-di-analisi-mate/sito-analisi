Two carts on a frictionless surface are linked by an ideal string. An external force parallel to the string is applied to the second cart and pulls both carts. The initial data are the force magnitude $F = 30\,\mathrm{N}$, the mass of the first body $m_{1} = 2\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the mass of the second body $m_{2} = 4\,\mathrm{kg}$. Change the mass of the second body to $8\,\mathrm{kg}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the ratio of the final value of $T_{0}$ to its initial value?

- $\dfrac{3}{5}$
- $\dfrac{5}{3}$
- $\dfrac{6}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The system accelerates at $a=F/(m_1+m_2)$. Tension is the only horizontal force on the first cart, so $T_0=m_1a$. The relation $T_{0} = \dfrac{F \cdot m_{1}}{m_{1} + m_{2}}$ gives $10\,\mathrm{N}$ before the change and $6\,\mathrm{N}$ after it. The ratio is $\dfrac{3}{5}$.
