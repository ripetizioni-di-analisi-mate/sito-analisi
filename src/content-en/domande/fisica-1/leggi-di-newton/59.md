A sphere of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ hangs from an ideal string and is held at rest by a horizontal force, in a position where the string forms an angle of $30^\circ$ with the vertical. What is the tension of the string?

- $19.6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $22.7\,\mathrm{N}$
- $17.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $39.2\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The sphere is acted on by its weight, vertical, by the horizontal force $F$ and by the tension $T$, directed along the string. The horizontal force has no vertical component, so along the vertical the component $T\cos 30^\circ$ of the tension alone balances the weight.

Equilibrium along the vertical therefore gives
$$
T\cos 30^\circ = m g
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
T = \frac{m g}{\cos 30^\circ} = \frac{2.0 \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{N}}{0.866} = 22.7\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The tension exceeds the weight, because its horizontal component must also balance the force $F = m g \tan 30^\circ = 11.3\,\mathrm{N}$.
