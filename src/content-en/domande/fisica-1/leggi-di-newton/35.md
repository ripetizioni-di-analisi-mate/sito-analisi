A body rests on a plane whose inclination is slowly increased, and it starts to slide when the inclination reaches $25^\circ$. The test is repeated on the same plane with a body of the same material and twice the mass. At what inclination does the second body start to slide?

- $50^\circ$
- $25^\circ$
- $13^\circ$
- $43^\circ$

## Explanation

The component of the weight along the plane, $mg\sin\alpha$, and the maximum static friction, $\mu_s\,mg\cos\alpha$, are both proportional to the mass. Therefore the equilibrium condition $mg\sin\alpha \leq \mu_s\,mg\cos\alpha$ is equivalent to $\tan\alpha \leq \mu_s$, which does not contain the mass. The **angle of repose** $\alpha_{\lim} = \arctan\mu_s$ therefore depends only on the surfaces in contact. The surfaces are the same, therefore $\mu_s = \tan 25^\circ$ is unchanged and the second body starts to slide again at $25^\circ$.
