## Explanation

The table is smooth, so the only horizontal force on the block resting on it is the tension. The second law along the string for the block, of mass $m_1 = 4.0\,\mathrm{kg}$, and for the hanging body, of mass $m_2 = 1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$, gives
$$
\begin{cases}
T = m_1 a \\[4pt]
m_2 g - T = m_2 a
\end{cases}
$$
Adding the two equations gives $a = \dfrac{m_2}{m_1 + m_2}\,g = \dfrac{1.0}{5.0} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 1.96\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Therefore $T = m_1 a = 4.0\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 1.96\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 7.85\,\mathrm{N}$, a value smaller than the hanging weight, $9.81\,\mathrm{N}$, because the hanging body accelerates downward.
