A car travels at constant speed around a circular curve of radius $45\,\mathrm{m}$ on a level road. The coefficient of static friction between tires and asphalt is $\mu_s = 0{,}60$. What is the maximum speed at which the curve can be taken without skidding?

- $16{,}3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $21{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $23{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $27{,}1\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The only horizontal force on the car is static friction, which provides the centripetal force, so $f_s = m\dfrac{v^2}{r}$, while along the vertical $N = m g$. The law of static friction requires $f_s \leq \mu_s N$, that is, $v^2 \leq \mu_s g r$. Therefore
$$
v_{\max} = \sqrt{\mu_s\,g\,r} = \sqrt{0{,}60 \cdot 9{,}81 \cdot 45}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 16{,}3\,\mathrm{m/s} ,
$$
about $59\,\mathrm{km/h}$.
