## Explanation

The crate is moving, so the friction is kinetic. Along the vertical the acceleration is zero, therefore $N = m g = 490.5\,\mathrm{N}$ and the friction force is $f_d = \mu_d N = 0.40 \cdot 490.5\,\mathrm{N} = 196\,\mathrm{N}$, directed opposite to the motion. The second law along the horizontal gives $F - f_d = m a$, so
$$
a = \frac{300\,\mathrm{N} - 196\,\mathrm{N}}{50\,\mathrm{kg}} = 2.08\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
