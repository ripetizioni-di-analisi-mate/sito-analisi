Two equal ideal springs, connected in series, support a body and at equilibrium stretch by $8.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ in total. The same springs, connected in parallel, support the same body. What is the extension at equilibrium?

- $4.0\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $8.0\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $16\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Two equal springs of constant $k$ are equivalent to a spring of constant $\dfrac{k}{2}$ if they are in series and to a spring of constant $2k$ if they are in parallel. At equilibrium the extension is $x = \dfrac{m g}{k_{\text{eq}}}$, so it is inversely proportional to the equivalent constant. The constant goes from $\dfrac{k}{2}$ to $2k$, that is, it becomes four times greater, therefore the extension becomes four times smaller, $x = \dfrac{8.0\,\mathrm{cm}}{4} = 2.0\,\mathrm{cm}$.
