## Explanation

The body is at rest, so the net force is zero. By symmetry the two strings have the same tension $T$ and the horizontal components of the two tensions cancel. Each string makes an angle of $45^\circ$ with the horizontal, therefore the vertical component of each tension is $T\sin 45^\circ$, and equilibrium along the vertical gives
$$
2\,T \sin 45^\circ - m g = 0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
T = \frac{m g}{2 \sin 45^\circ} = \frac{117{,}7\,\mathrm{N}}{1{,}414} = 83{,}2\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
