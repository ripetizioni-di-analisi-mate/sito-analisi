A sphere of $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ and a sphere of $5.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ are dropped from rest from the same height. Air resistance is negligible. Which statement is true?

- The $5.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ sphere has an acceleration five times greater
- The two spheres have the same acceleration
- The two spheres are subject to the same weight force
- The $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ sphere has a greater acceleration, because it has a smaller mass

## Explanation

Only the weight $m\,\vec{g}$, which is proportional to the mass, acts on each sphere. By the second law $m\,\vec{a} = m\,\vec{g}$, so $\vec{a} = \vec{g}$ for both spheres. The $5.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ sphere has a weight five times greater, $49.1\,\mathrm{N}$ against $9.81\,\mathrm{N}$, and also a mass five times greater, therefore the two spheres fall with the **same acceleration** and reach the ground together.
