## Explanation

The body starts from rest and the net force is constant, so the motion is uniformly accelerated with acceleration
$$
a = \frac{\Delta v}{\Delta t} = \frac{6.0\,\mathrm{m/s}}{3.0\,\mathrm{s}} = 2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
By **Newton's second law** the net force has magnitude $R = m\,a = 8.0\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 16\,\mathrm{N}$. The weight does not enter the calculation, because on the horizontal surface it is balanced by the normal force.
