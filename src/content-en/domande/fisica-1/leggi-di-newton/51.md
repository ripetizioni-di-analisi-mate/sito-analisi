A magnet of mass $200\,\mathrm{g}$ attracts a nail of mass $5.0\,\mathrm{g}$ with a force of $0.50\,\mathrm{N}$. What is the magnitude of the force exerted by the nail on the magnet?

- $0.013\,\mathrm{N}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.049\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.50\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The magnet exerts a force of $0.50\,\mathrm{N}$ on the nail. By **Newton's third law** the nail exerts on the magnet an opposite force, with the same line of action and the same magnitude, hence $0.50\,\mathrm{N}$.

The magnitude of the two forces does not depend on the masses of the bodies. The masses only determine the accelerations produced by the two forces, which by the second law are inversely proportional to the masses. The value $0.049\,\mathrm{N}$ is the weight of the nail, which has no relation to the magnetic force.
