An elevator accelerates upward and a passenger stands still relative to its cabin. Take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Given the mass $m = 60\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the acceleration magnitude $a = 2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the normal force magnitude $N$?

- $720\,\mathrm{N}$
- $600\,\mathrm{N}$
- $480\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1440\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The normal force points up and weight points down. The vertical equation is $N-mg=ma$. In the stated units, the relation is $N = m \cdot \left(a + 10\right)$. Substituting the given values yields $720\,\mathrm{N}$.
