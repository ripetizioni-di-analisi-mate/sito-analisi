A lamp hangs from the ceiling on a string and is at rest. Which forces appear in the free-body diagram of the lamp?

- The weight of the lamp, the tension in the string and the force with which the lamp pulls on the string
- The weight of the lamp and the tension in the string
- The tension in the string and the reaction of the ceiling
- The weight of the lamp, the tension in the string and the force with which the lamp attracts the Earth

## Explanation

The **free-body diagram** contains exactly the forces that act on the lamp. The lamp is attracted by the Earth and touches only the string, so the forces acting on it are its weight and the tension in the string.

The force with which the lamp pulls on the string acts on the string, and the force with which the lamp attracts the Earth acts on the Earth, therefore neither of them appears in the diagram. The reaction of the ceiling acts on the string, because the ceiling touches the string and not the lamp. The lamp is at rest, so the weight and the tension have the same magnitude.
