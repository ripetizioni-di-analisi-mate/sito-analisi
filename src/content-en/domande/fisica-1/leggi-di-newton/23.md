A person stands on a scale in an elevator that is moving up and slowing down to stop at a floor. What does the scale read, compared with the weight of the person?

- A value greater than the weight
- A value smaller than the weight
- A value equal to the weight
- Zero

## Explanation

The elevator moves up and slows down, so its velocity is directed upward and decreases in magnitude, therefore the acceleration is directed downward. With the vertical axis pointing upward we therefore have $a < 0$. The reading of the scale is the normal force $N$, and the second law gives $N - m g = m a$, that is, $N = m\,(g + a)$. Since $a < 0$, the scale reads **a value smaller than the weight**; the reading would be zero only for $a = -g$, that is, in free fall.
