## Explanation

Along the perpendicular to the cover the normal force is $N = mg\cos\alpha$, while along the cover static friction must balance the component of the weight $mg\sin\alpha$. By the law of static friction equilibrium is possible if and only if $mg\sin\alpha \leq \mu_s\,mg\cos\alpha$, that is, $\tan\alpha \leq \mu_s$. The coin starts to slide when equality is reached, so $27^\circ$ is the **angle of repose** and $\mu_s = \tan 27^\circ = 0{,}51$. The result does not depend on the mass of the coin.
