A block is launched up an inclined plane at $30^\circ$ with initial speed $8.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The coefficient of kinetic friction is $\mu_d = 0.20$. What distance does it cover along the plane before stopping?

- $6.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4.8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The block moves up, so kinetic friction is directed down the plane, like the component of the weight. By the result for the **inclined plane with friction** the deceleration has magnitude
$$
a = g\,(\sin 30^\circ + \mu_d \cos 30^\circ) = 9.81 \cdot (0.500 + 0.173)\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 6.60\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
The motion is uniformly decelerated, therefore the distance covered up to the stop is $d = \dfrac{v_0^2}{2a} = \dfrac{64\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}}{13.2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}} = 4.8\,\mathrm{m}$.
