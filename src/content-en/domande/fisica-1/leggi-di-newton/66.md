A block slides on a horizontal board. A horizontal pull of $9\,\mathrm N$ keeps its speed constant. The test is repeated with two identical blocks fastened one on top of the other, with the same lower contact conditions. What pull maintains constant speed in the kinetic-friction model?

- $9\,\mathrm N$
- $4.5\,\mathrm N$
- $18\,\mathrm N$
- $36\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

In the first trial the pull directly measures $\mu_k mg$. Adding the second block doubles the normal force at the lower contact. For constant speed the pull must equal the new friction force of $18\,\mathrm N$.
