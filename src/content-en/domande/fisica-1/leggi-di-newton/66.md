A block is pressed against a vertical wall by a horizontal force and is on the verge of slipping downward. Take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Given the mass $m = 3\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the coefficient of static friction $\mu = \dfrac{1}{2}$, what is the horizontal pressing force $F$?

- $120\,\mathrm{N}$
- $60\,\mathrm{N}$
- $30\,\mathrm{N}$
- $15\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The normal force balances the horizontal push. At impending slip, upward static friction equals $\mu F$ and balances the weight. In the stated units, the relation is $F = \dfrac{10 \cdot m}{\mu}$. Substituting the given values yields $60\,\mathrm{N}$.
