A vertical pendulum is at rest in a cabin accelerating downward with $a<g$. Take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Given the acceleration magnitude $a = 4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and the string tension $T_{0} = 18\,\mathrm{N}$, what is the mass $m$?

- $3\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $\dfrac{9}{5}\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $\dfrac{9}{7}\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

With upward positive, the acceleration is $-a$ and the net force is $T_0-mg$. In the stated units, the relation is $T_{0} = m \cdot \left(10 - a\right)$. Solving for the unknown gives $m = - \dfrac{T_{0}}{a - 10}$. Substituting the given values yields $3\,\mathrm{kg}$.
