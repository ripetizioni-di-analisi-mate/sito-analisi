An ideal spring of spring constant $300\,\mathrm{N/m}$ is cut into two equal parts. What is the spring constant of each of the two halves?

- $150\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $300\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $600\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $1200\,\mathrm{N/m}$

## Explanation

The whole spring can be regarded as two equal halves, of constant $k'$, connected in series. For **springs in series** the equivalent constant satisfies
$$
\frac{1}{k} = \frac{1}{k'} + \frac{1}{k'} = \frac{2}{k'} ,
$$
so $k' = 2k = 600\,\mathrm{N/m}$.

The same result is obtained by noting that, stretched by a force $F$, each half extends by half of the extension of the whole spring. For the same force, half the extension corresponds to twice the constant.
