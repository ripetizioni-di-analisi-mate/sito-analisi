A body rests on a horizontal surface. Two opposing horizontal forces act on it, and it remains at rest. The first force is larger than the second. Given the second force magnitude $F_{2} = 9\,\mathrm{N}$ and the static friction magnitude $f = 8\,\mathrm{N}$, what is the first force magnitude $F_{1}$?

- $8\,\mathrm{N}$
- $17\,\mathrm{N}$
- $34\,\mathrm{N}$
- $\dfrac{17}{2}\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Since the body stays at rest, static friction balances the resultant of the two applied forces. In the stated units, the relation is $f = F_{1} - F_{2}$. Solving for the unknown gives $F_{1} = F_{2} + f$. Substituting the given values yields $17\,\mathrm{N}$.
