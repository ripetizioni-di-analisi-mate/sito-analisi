A stationary block on a horizontal floor is pulled by a horizontal force of $12\,\mathrm N$. The maximum static friction is $20\,\mathrm N$. What is the actual friction magnitude?

- $20\,\mathrm N$
- $12\,\mathrm N$
- $8\,\mathrm N$
- $0\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The applied force is below the threshold, so the block stays at rest. Static friction adjusts to balance the pull and has magnitude $12\,\mathrm N$.
