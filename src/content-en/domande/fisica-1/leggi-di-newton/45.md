A coin stays at rest relative to a turntable whose angular speed is increasing. Away from the centre, once its speed is nonzero, in which direction does static friction act on the coin?

- It points only towards the centre.
- It is purely tangential and opposes the motion.
- It has an inward component and a component along the motion.
- It has an outward component.

## Explanation

Friction is the only horizontal force. It supplies both the inward acceleration $r\omega^2$ and the tangential acceleration $r\alpha$ that increases the speed.
