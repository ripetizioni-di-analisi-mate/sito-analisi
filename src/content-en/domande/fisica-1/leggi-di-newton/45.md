An object rests on a horizontal platform that rotates about a vertical axis, at $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$ from the axis, with coefficient of static friction $\mu_s = 0.40$ between object and platform. What is the maximum angular velocity of the platform for which the object stays at rest relative to it?

- $7.85\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $1.40\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $2.80\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $4.43\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

In the frame of the platform the object is at rest, so static friction balances the **centrifugal force** $m\,\omega^2 r$, while along the vertical $N = m g$. The law of static friction requires $m\,\omega^2 r \leq \mu_s m g$, that is, $\omega^2 \leq \dfrac{\mu_s g}{r}$. Therefore
$$
\omega_{\max} = \sqrt{\frac{\mu_s\,g}{r}} = \sqrt{\frac{0.40 \cdot 9.81}{0.50}}\,\mathrm{rad/s} = 2.80\,\mathrm{rad/s} .
$$
