A block of mass $5.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ slides down a smooth inclined plane at $60^\circ$ to the horizontal. What is the normal force from the plane?

- $24.5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $42.5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $49.1\,\mathrm{N}$
- $98.1\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

We choose the $x$ axis along the plane and the $y$ axis perpendicular to the plane. The block stays on the plane, so the acceleration along $y$ is zero and the normal force balances the component of the weight perpendicular to the plane. The angle between the weight and the perpendicular to the plane equals the inclination, therefore
$$
N = m g \cos 60^\circ = 5.0\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} \cdot \frac{1}{2} = 24.5\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
