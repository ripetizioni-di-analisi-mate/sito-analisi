A book is at rest on a horizontal table. According to Newton's third law, what is the reaction to the weight of the book?

- The normal force that the table exerts on the book
- The force with which the book attracts the Earth
- The force with which the book presses on the table
- The force with which the table presses on the floor

## Explanation

The weight of the book is the force with which the Earth attracts the book. By **Newton's third law** its reaction is the force that the book exerts on the Earth, with the same magnitude and opposite direction, that is, the force with which the book attracts the Earth. The normal force from the table acts on the book, the same body on which the weight acts, therefore the two forces do not form an action-reaction pair. The weight and the normal force have the same magnitude because the book is in equilibrium, and this equality follows from the second law.
