A car takes a flat circular bend. Static friction is the only available horizontal force. Take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Given the radius $R = 40\,\mathrm{m}$ and the coefficient of static friction $\mu = \dfrac{2}{5}$, what is the maximum speed without skidding $v$?

- $4 \cdot \sqrt{5}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10 \cdot \sqrt{10}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4 \cdot \sqrt{10}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8 \cdot \sqrt{10}\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

At the threshold of skidding, maximum static friction $\mu mg$ supplies the entire centripetal force $mv^2/R$. In the stated units, the relation is $v = \sqrt{10} \cdot \sqrt{R} \cdot \sqrt{\mu}$. Substituting the given values yields $4 \cdot \sqrt{10}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
