A crate of mass $10\,\mathrm{kg}$ rests on a horizontal floor and is pushed with a horizontal force $F$ that increases slowly from zero. The graph shows the friction force $f$ on the crate as a function of $F$, both in newtons.

::grafico{f="x<=30?x:(x>30.3?24:NaN)" punti="30,30,pieno;30,24,vuoto" segmenti="30,0,30,24" nomi="F,f" xmin=0 xmax=45 ymin=0 ymax=36 titolo="Friction force $f$ on the crate as a function of the push $F$: maximum of $30\,\mathrm{N}$, then a constant value of $24\,\mathrm{N}$"}

What is the coefficient of kinetic friction between crate and floor?

- $0.31$
- $0.80$
- $0.24$
- $2.4$

## Explanation

As long as the crate is at rest the friction is static and balances the push, so in the graph $f = F$ up to the maximum value $\mu_s N = 30\,\mathrm{N}$. When the push exceeds this value the crate starts to move and the friction becomes kinetic, with the constant value $f_d = \mu_d N = 24\,\mathrm{N}$.

Along the vertical $N = m g = 98.1\,\mathrm{N}$, therefore
$$
\mu_d = \frac{f_d}{N} = \frac{24\,\mathrm{N}}{98.1\,\mathrm{N}} = 0.24 .
$$
The value $0.31$ is the coefficient of static friction, obtained from the maximum of $30\,\mathrm{N}$.
