A car takes a flat circular bend. Static friction is the only available horizontal force. Take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Given the coefficient of static friction $\mu = \dfrac{2}{5}$ and the maximum speed without skidding $v = 4 \cdot \sqrt{10}\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the radius $R$?

- $80\,\mathrm{m}$
- $40\,\mathrm{m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $60\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

At the threshold of skidding, maximum static friction $\mu mg$ supplies the entire centripetal force $mv^2/R$. In the stated units, the relation is $v = \sqrt{10} \cdot \sqrt{R} \cdot \sqrt{\mu}$. Solving for the unknown gives $R = \dfrac{v^{2}}{10 \cdot \mu}$. Substituting the given values yields $40\,\mathrm{m}$.
