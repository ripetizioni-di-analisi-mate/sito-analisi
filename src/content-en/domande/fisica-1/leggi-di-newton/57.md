A block of mass $4.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ rests on a smooth incline of $30^\circ$. An ideal string, parallel to the incline, connects it to a body of mass $3.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ hanging vertically beyond an ideal pulley fixed at the top of the incline. What is the magnitude of the acceleration of the two bodies?

- $4.20\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $7.01\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2.45\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $1.40\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Along the incline the block is acted on by the component of its weight $m_1 g \sin 30^\circ = 19.6\,\mathrm{N}$, pointing down the incline, and by the tension, pointing up. The weight of the hanging body is $m_2 g = 29.4\,\mathrm{N}$, so it is larger and the hanging body descends, pulling the block toward the top of the incline.

With the positive direction along the motion, the second law for the two bodies gives
$$
\begin{cases}
T - m_1 g \sin 30^\circ = m_1 a \\[4pt]
m_2 g - T = m_2 a
\end{cases}
$$
Adding the two equations eliminates the tension, therefore
$$
a = \frac{m_2 - m_1 \sin 30^\circ}{m_1 + m_2}\,g = \frac{3.0 - 2.0}{7.0} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 1.40\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
