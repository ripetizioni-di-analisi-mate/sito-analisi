Two carts on a frictionless surface are linked by an ideal string. An external force parallel to the string is applied to the second cart and pulls both carts. Given the mass of the first body $m_{1} = 2\,\mathrm{kg}$, the mass of the second body $m_{2} = 4\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the string tension $T_{0} = 10\,\mathrm{N}$, what is the force magnitude $F$?

- $60\,\mathrm{N}$
- $15\,\mathrm{N}$
- $30\,\mathrm{N}$
- $20\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The system accelerates at $a=F/(m_1+m_2)$. Tension is the only horizontal force on the first cart, so $T_0=m_1a$. In the stated units, the relation is $T_{0} = \dfrac{F \cdot m_{1}}{m_{1} + m_{2}}$. Solving for the unknown gives $F = \dfrac{T_{0} \cdot \left(m_{1} + m_{2}\right)}{m_{1}}$. Substituting the given values yields $30\,\mathrm{N}$.
