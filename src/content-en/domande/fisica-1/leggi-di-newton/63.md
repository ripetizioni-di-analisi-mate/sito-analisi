A brick is dragged at constant velocity on a horizontal floor by a horizontal force, once resting on its largest face and once on its smallest face. The faces are made of the same material and are equally smooth. Which statement about the required force is true?

- It is larger when the brick rests on its largest face
- It is larger when the brick rests on its smallest face
- It is the same in both cases
- It is proportional to the area of the face in contact

## Explanation

The brick moves at constant velocity, so the applied force balances the kinetic friction, $F = \mu_d N$. Along the vertical $N = m g$ in both cases, because the weight of the brick does not change.

By the **laws of dry friction** the coefficient $\mu_d$ depends on the materials and on the condition of the surfaces, and to good accuracy it does not depend on the contact area. The faces are made of the same material and are equally smooth, therefore the required force is **the same** in both cases.
