A car takes a flat circular bend. Static friction is the only available horizontal force. Take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Given the radius $R = 40\,\mathrm{m}$ and the maximum speed without skidding $v = 4 \cdot \sqrt{10}\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the coefficient of static friction $\mu$?

- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $\dfrac{4}{5}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}$
- $\dfrac{3}{5}$

## Explanation

At the threshold of skidding, maximum static friction $\mu mg$ supplies the entire centripetal force $mv^2/R$. In the stated units, the relation is $v = \sqrt{10} \cdot \sqrt{R} \cdot \sqrt{\mu}$. Solving for the unknown gives $\mu = \dfrac{v^{2}}{10 \cdot R}$. Substituting the given values yields $\dfrac{2}{5}$.
