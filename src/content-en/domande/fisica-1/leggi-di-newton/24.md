A ring is at rest on a smooth horizontal plane under the action of three horizontal forces. Two of them have magnitude $10\,\mathrm{N}$ and form an angle of $120^\circ$ with each other. What is the magnitude of the third force?

- $20\,\mathrm{N}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N}$
- $17.3\,\mathrm{N}$
- $5.0\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The ring is at rest, so by the **equilibrium of a point particle** the net force of the three forces is zero. Therefore the third force is opposite to the resultant of the first two and has the same magnitude.

The first two forces have the same magnitude $F = 10\,\mathrm{N}$, so their resultant lies along the bisector of the angle between them. Each of them forms an angle of $60^\circ$ with the bisector, therefore the components perpendicular to the bisector cancel and those along the bisector add up, therefore
$$
R_{12} = 2F\cos 60^\circ = 2 \cdot 10\,\mathrm{N} \cdot \frac{1}{2} = 10\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
Hence the third force has magnitude $10\,\mathrm{N}$, and the three forces form angles of $120^\circ$ with one another.
