A mass hangs at equilibrium from an ideal vertical spring. Take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Given the mass $m = 3\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the spring constant $k = 150\,\mathrm{N/m}$, what is the extension from the natural length $x$?

- $\dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{1}{10}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{1}{50}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

At equilibrium the upward spring force balances weight, so $kx=mg$. In the stated units, the relation is $x = \dfrac{10 \cdot m}{k}$. Substituting the given values yields $\dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{m}$.
