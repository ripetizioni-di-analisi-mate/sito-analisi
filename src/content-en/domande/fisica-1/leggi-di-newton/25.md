A body of mass $30\,\mathrm{kg}$ hangs from an ideal movable pulley. The ideal string that passes under the pulley has one end fixed to the ceiling and the other pulled vertically upward by a person. What force must the person exert to keep the body in equilibrium?

- $294\,\mathrm{N}$
- $589\,\mathrm{N}$
- $147\,\mathrm{N}$
- $73.6\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The string and the pulley are ideal, so the tension $T$ is the same along the whole string and equals the force exerted by the person. The movable pulley is supported by two vertical segments of the same string, each of which pulls upward with force $T$. The pulley has negligible mass, therefore equilibrium of the system made of the pulley and the body gives $2T = m g$. It follows that $T = \dfrac{m g}{2} = \dfrac{294\,\mathrm{N}}{2} = 147\,\mathrm{N}$.
