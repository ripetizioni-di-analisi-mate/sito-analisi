A block of mass $4.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ is at rest on a smooth inclined plane at $25^\circ$, held by an ideal string parallel to the plane and fixed at the top of the plane. What is the tension in the string?

- $35.6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $39.2\,\mathrm{N}$
- $18.3\,\mathrm{N}$
- $16.6\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The block is at rest, so the net force is zero. The $x$ axis is chosen along the plane. The normal force is perpendicular to the plane and therefore has no component along $x$, so along the plane the tension alone balances the component of the weight $m g\sin 25^\circ$.

It follows that
$$
T = m g \sin 25^\circ = 39.2\,\mathrm{N} \cdot 0.423 = 16.6\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The component of the weight perpendicular to the plane, $m g\cos 25^\circ = 35.6\,\mathrm{N}$, is balanced by the normal force and not by the string.
