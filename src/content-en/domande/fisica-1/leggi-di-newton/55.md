A spring balance of negligible mass is connected to two horizontal ideal strings. Each string passes over a fixed ideal pulley and supports at its other end a hanging body of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$. The system is at rest. What does the spring balance read?

- $39.2\,\mathrm{N}$
- $19.6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $9.81\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Each hanging body is at rest, so the tension of its string balances the weight, $T = m g = 19.6\,\mathrm{N}$. The string and the pulley are ideal, therefore the same tension acts at the horizontal end of the string, connected to the spring balance.

The spring balance is thus pulled at its two ends by two opposite forces of $19.6\,\mathrm{N}$ and reads $19.6\,\mathrm{N}$. The situation is the same as that of a spring balance hanging from the ceiling and supporting a single body of $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$. In that case the second force of $19.6\,\mathrm{N}$ is exerted by the ceiling.
