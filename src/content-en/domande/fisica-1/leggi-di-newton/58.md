Two carts on a frictionless surface are linked by an ideal string. An external force parallel to the string is applied to the second cart and pulls both carts. Given the force magnitude $F = 30\,\mathrm{N}$, the mass of the second body $m_{2} = 4\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the string tension $T_{0} = 10\,\mathrm{N}$, what is the mass of the first body $m_{1}$?

- $4\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $2\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The system accelerates at $a=F/(m_1+m_2)$. Tension is the only horizontal force on the first cart, so $T_0=m_1a$. In the stated units, the relation is $T_{0} = \dfrac{F \cdot m_{1}}{m_{1} + m_{2}}$. Solving for the unknown gives $m_{1} = \dfrac{T_{0} \cdot m_{2}}{F - T_{0}}$. Substituting the given values yields $2\,\mathrm{kg}$.
