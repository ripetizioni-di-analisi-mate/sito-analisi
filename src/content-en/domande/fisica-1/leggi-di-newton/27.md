A crate of mass $20\,\mathrm{kg}$ is at rest on a horizontal floor, with friction coefficients $\mu_s = 0{,}50$ and $\mu_d = 0{,}30$. A person pushes it with a horizontal force of $80{,}0\,\mathrm{N}$. What is the friction force on the crate?

- $98{,}1\,\mathrm{N}$
- $80{,}0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $58{,}9\,\mathrm{N}$
- $196\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The normal force is $N = m g = 196\,\mathrm{N}$, so static friction can reach the maximum value $\mu_s N = 98{,}1\,\mathrm{N}$. The push of $80{,}0\,\mathrm{N}$ is smaller than this value, therefore the crate stays at rest. At equilibrium static friction balances the push, so it equals $80{,}0\,\mathrm{N}$ and is directed opposite to the push. The value $\mu_s N$ is reached only when the crate is about to start moving.
