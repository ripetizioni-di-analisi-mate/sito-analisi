A block rests on the floor of a vehicle accelerating horizontally. Friction is the only horizontal force on the block and the block does not slip. Given the mass $m = 7\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the magnitude of static friction on the block $F = 21\,\mathrm{N}$, what is the acceleration magnitude $a$?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

In the ground frame the block has the same acceleration as the vehicle. The horizontal net force is the static friction force. In the stated units, the relation is $F = a \cdot m$. Solving for the unknown gives $a = \dfrac{F}{m}$. Substituting the given values yields $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
