A crate of mass $10\,\mathrm{kg}$ stays at rest on a horizontal floor while it is pulled by a force of $50\,\mathrm{N}$ inclined $30^\circ$ upward with respect to the horizontal. What is the normal force from the floor?

- $73{,}1\,\mathrm{N}$
- $98{,}1\,\mathrm{N}$
- $123\,\mathrm{N}$
- $54{,}8\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The crate does not move along the vertical, so the sum of the vertical components of the forces is zero. The vertical forces are the weight $mg = 98{,}1\,\mathrm{N}$ downward, the normal force $N$ and the component $F\sin 30^\circ = 25{,}0\,\mathrm{N}$ of the applied force, the last two upward. Therefore $N + F\sin 30^\circ - mg = 0$, which gives $N = 98{,}1\,\mathrm{N} - 25{,}0\,\mathrm{N} = 73{,}1\,\mathrm{N}$. The normal force is smaller than the weight, because the applied force partly supports the crate.
