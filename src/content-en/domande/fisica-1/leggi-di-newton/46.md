A block slides across a horizontal surface. A horizontal pull accelerates it in the direction of motion. Take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Given the mass $m = 5\,\mathrm{kg}$, the acceleration magnitude $a = 2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and the coefficient of kinetic friction $\mu = \dfrac{3}{10}$, what is the magnitude of the pull $F$?

- $50\,\mathrm{N}$
- $25\,\mathrm{N}$
- $10\,\mathrm{N}$
- $15\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Kinetic friction has magnitude $\mu mg$ and opposes the pull. The net force along the motion is $ma$. In the stated units, the relation is $F = m \cdot \left(a + 10 \cdot \mu\right)$. Substituting the given values yields $25\,\mathrm{N}$.
