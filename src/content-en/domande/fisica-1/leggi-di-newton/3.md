## Explanation

Only the weight and the normal force act on the block, so, as for any body on a frictionless inclined plane, the acceleration along the plane is $a = g \sin 20^\circ = 9{,}81 \cdot 0{,}342\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 3{,}36\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The block starts from rest with constant acceleration, therefore after a time $t$ it has covered the distance $d = \dfrac{1}{2}\,a t^2$. It follows that
$$
t = \sqrt{\frac{2d}{a}} = \sqrt{\frac{8{,}0\,\mathrm{m}}{3{,}36\,\mathrm{m/s^2}}} = 1{,}54\,\mathrm{s} .
$$
