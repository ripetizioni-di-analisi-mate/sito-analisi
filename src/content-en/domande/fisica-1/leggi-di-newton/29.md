A crate rests on the bed of a truck that accelerates forward on a horizontal road, and the crate does not slide relative to the bed. Which statement about the friction force acting on the crate is true?

- It is static friction, directed backward
- It is kinetic friction, directed backward
- It is static friction, directed forward
- It is zero, because the crate is at rest relative to the bed

## Explanation

The crate does not slide, so it has the same acceleration as the truck, directed forward. By the second law the net force on the crate is directed forward, and the only horizontal force is the friction from the bed. Therefore the friction is directed **forward**. It is static friction because the surfaces do not slide over each other, and it opposes the backward sliding that would occur relative to the bed without friction.
