A body of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ moves along the $x$ axis with position function $x(t) = 3t^2 - 2t$, with $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. What is the component $R_x$ of the net force on the body?

- $6.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $8.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $3.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The velocity is the derivative of the position and the acceleration is the derivative of the velocity, so
$$
v_x(t) = \frac{dx}{dt} = 6t - 2 ,
\qquad
a_x = \frac{dv_x}{dt} = 6\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
The acceleration is constant, therefore by **Newton's second law** the net force is constant too and equals $R_x = m\,a_x = 2.0\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 6\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 12\,\mathrm{N}$.

The value $6.0\,\mathrm{N}$ would be obtained by taking as acceleration the coefficient $3$ of $t^2$, which is instead half of the acceleration.
