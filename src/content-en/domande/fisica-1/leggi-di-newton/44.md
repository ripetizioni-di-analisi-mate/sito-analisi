A block of mass $6.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ on a horizontal table, with coefficient of kinetic friction $\mu_d = 0.25$, is connected by an ideal string, which passes over an ideal pulley at the edge of the table, to a hanging body of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$. The system is moving and the hanging body is going down. What is the acceleration of the two bodies?

- $0.613\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2.45\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $4.29\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $1.84\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

On the block resting on the table, of mass $m_1 = 6.0\,\mathrm{kg}$, the normal force is $N_1 = m_1 g$, so kinetic friction equals $\mu_d m_1 g$ and is directed opposite to the motion. The second law along the string for the block and for the hanging body, of mass $m_2 = 2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$, gives
$$
\begin{cases}
T - \mu_d\,m_1 g = m_1 a \\[4pt]
m_2 g - T = m_2 a
\end{cases}
$$
Adding the two equations eliminates the tension, therefore
$$
a = \frac{m_2 - \mu_d\,m_1}{m_1 + m_2}\,g = \frac{2.0 - 1.5}{8.0} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 0.613\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
