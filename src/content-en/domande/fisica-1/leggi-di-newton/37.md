A body of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ hangs from a vertical ideal spring, which at equilibrium is stretched by $5.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ with respect to its rest length. What is the spring constant?

- $40.0\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $3.92\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $785\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $392\,\mathrm{N/m}$

## Explanation

At equilibrium the elastic force, of magnitude $k x$, balances the weight, so by **Hooke's law** $k x = m g$. The extension in meters is $x = 0.050\,\mathrm{m}$, therefore
$$
k = \frac{m g}{x} = \frac{19.6\,\mathrm{N}}{0.050\,\mathrm{m}} = 392\,\mathrm{N/m} .
$$
