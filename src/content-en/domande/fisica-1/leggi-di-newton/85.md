A body rests on a horizontal surface. Two opposing horizontal forces act on it, and it remains at rest. The first force is larger than the second. Given the first force magnitude $F_{1} = 17\,\mathrm{N}$ and the second force magnitude $F_{2} = 9\,\mathrm{N}$, what is the static friction magnitude $f$?

- $26\,\mathrm{N}$
- $17\,\mathrm{N}$
- $8\,\mathrm{N}$
- $16\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Since the body stays at rest, static friction balances the resultant of the two applied forces. In the stated units, the relation is $f = F_{1} - F_{2}$. Substituting the given values yields $8\,\mathrm{N}$.
