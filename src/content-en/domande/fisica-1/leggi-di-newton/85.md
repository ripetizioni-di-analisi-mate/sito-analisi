In a rotor ride people stand with their backs against the inner wall of a vertical cylinder of radius $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$, which rotates about its axis. When the cylinder rotates fast enough, the floor is lowered and the people stay at rest against the wall. The coefficient of static friction between people and wall is $\mu_s = 0.40$. What is the minimum angular velocity for which the people do not slide down?

- $3.13\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $1.25\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $1.98\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $6.26\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

The wall exerts on a person the normal force, horizontal and pointing toward the axis, and static friction, vertical. The person moves on a horizontal circle, so the normal force provides the centripetal force and friction balances the weight, so
$$
\begin{cases}
N = m\,\omega^2 r \\[4pt]
f_s = m g
\end{cases}
$$
The law of static friction requires $f_s \leq \mu_s N$, that is, $m g \leq \mu_s m\,\omega^2 r$. Therefore
$$
\omega_{\min} = \sqrt{\frac{g}{\mu_s\,r}} = \sqrt{\frac{9.81}{0.40 \cdot 2.5}}\,\mathrm{rad/s} = 3.13\,\mathrm{rad/s} ,
$$
a value that does not depend on the mass of the person.
