A block rests on the floor of a vehicle accelerating horizontally. Friction is the only horizontal force on the block and the block does not slip. Given the mass $m = 7\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the acceleration magnitude $a = 3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the magnitude of static friction on the block $F$?

- $42\,\mathrm{N}$
- $\dfrac{63}{2}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $\dfrac{21}{2}\,\mathrm{N}$
- $21\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

In the ground frame the block has the same acceleration as the vehicle. The horizontal net force is the static friction force. In the stated units, the relation is $F = a \cdot m$. Substituting the given values yields $21\,\mathrm{N}$.
