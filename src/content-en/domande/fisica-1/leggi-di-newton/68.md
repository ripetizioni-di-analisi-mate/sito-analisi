A block is pressed against a vertical wall by a horizontal force and is on the verge of slipping downward. Take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Given the mass $m = 3\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the horizontal pressing force $F = 60\,\mathrm{N}$, what is the coefficient of static friction $\mu$?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $2$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The normal force balances the horizontal push. At impending slip, upward static friction equals $\mu F$ and balances the weight. In the stated units, the relation is $F = \dfrac{10 \cdot m}{\mu}$. Solving for the unknown gives $\mu = \dfrac{10 \cdot m}{F}$. Substituting the given values yields $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
