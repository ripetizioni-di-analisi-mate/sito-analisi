A block is at rest on a rough incline and only its weight and the forces of the incline act on it. How is the friction force on the block directed?

- Along the incline, downward
- Along the incline, upward
- Perpendicular to the incline, outward
- Vertical, upward

## Explanation

The block is at rest, so the net force is zero along the incline as well. Along the incline the component of the weight $m g\sin\alpha$ acts, pointing downward, therefore static friction, which is parallel to the contact surface, must point **up along the incline**.

The force perpendicular to the incline is the normal force. The resultant of friction and normal force is vertical and points upward, because it balances the weight.
