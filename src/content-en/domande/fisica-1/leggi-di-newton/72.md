A block moves up a $30^\circ$ incline at constant speed under a pull parallel to the incline. Kinetic friction has constant magnitude $f$, and take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Given the friction magnitude $f = 6\,\mathrm{N}$ and the pull magnitude $F = 26\,\mathrm{N}$, what is the mass $m$?

- $2\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $\dfrac{32}{5}\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Along the incline, the pull balances both the weight component $mg\sin 30^\circ$ and friction. In the stated units, the relation is $F = f + 5 \cdot m$. Solving for the unknown gives $m = \dfrac{F}{5} - \dfrac{f}{5}$. Substituting the given values yields $4\,\mathrm{kg}$.
