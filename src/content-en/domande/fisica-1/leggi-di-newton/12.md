- $0{,}81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ downward
- $9{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ downward
- $19\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ upward

## Explanation

The forces on the person are the weight and the normal force $N$ from the scale, whose magnitude is the reading. With the vertical axis pointing upward the second law gives $N - m g = m a$, so
$$
a = \frac{N}{m} - g = \frac{540\,\mathrm{N}}{60\,\mathrm{kg}} - 9{,}81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = -0{,}81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
The negative sign means that the acceleration is directed **downward**, and indeed the reading is smaller than the weight $m g = 589\,\mathrm{N}$.
