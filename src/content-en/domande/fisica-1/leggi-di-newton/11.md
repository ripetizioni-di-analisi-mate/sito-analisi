## Explanation

At the highest point the water is subject to its weight and to the normal force from the bottom of the bucket, both directed downward, that is, toward the center. Their sum provides the centripetal force, so $N + m g = m\dfrac{v^2}{r}$. The bottom can only push, therefore the water stays in the bucket if and only if $N \geq 0$, that is, $v^2 \geq g r$. The minimum speed is therefore $v_{\min} = \sqrt{g r} = \sqrt{9{,}81 \cdot 0{,}90}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 2{,}97\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
