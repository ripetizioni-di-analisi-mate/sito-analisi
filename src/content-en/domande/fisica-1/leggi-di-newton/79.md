A mass hangs at equilibrium from an ideal vertical spring. Take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Given the mass $m = 3\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the extension from the natural length $x = \dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the spring constant $k$?

- $150\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $300\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $75\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{N/m}$

## Explanation

At equilibrium the upward spring force balances weight, so $kx=mg$. In the stated units, the relation is $x = \dfrac{10 \cdot m}{k}$. Solving for the unknown gives $k = \dfrac{10 \cdot m}{x}$. Substituting the given values yields $150\,\mathrm{N/m}$.
