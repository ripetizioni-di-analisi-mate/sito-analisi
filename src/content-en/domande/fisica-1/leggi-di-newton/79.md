An ideal spring of constant $k = 200\,\mathrm{N/m}$ lies along the $x$ axis, with the origin at the position of the free end at rest. A body attached to the free end is at $x = -3.0\,\mathrm{cm}$, so the spring is compressed. What is the component $F_x$ of the force exerted by the spring on the body?

- $-6.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $+600\,\mathrm{N}$
- $+6.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-600\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

By **Hooke's law** the component of the elastic force is $F_x = -k x$, with $x$ in meters. With $x = -0.030\,\mathrm{m}$ one obtains
$$
F_x = -200\,\mathrm{N/m} \cdot (-0.030\,\mathrm{m}) = +6.0\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The component is positive, so the compressed spring pushes the body in the positive direction of the axis, that is, toward the rest position. The minus sign in the law expresses precisely the fact that the force is always opposite to the displacement from the rest position.
