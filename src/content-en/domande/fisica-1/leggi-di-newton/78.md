A mass hangs at equilibrium from an ideal vertical spring. Take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Given the spring constant $k = 150\,\mathrm{N/m}$ and the extension from the natural length $x = \dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the mass $m$?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $3\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $30\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

At equilibrium the upward spring force balances weight, so $kx=mg$. In the stated units, the relation is $x = \dfrac{10 \cdot m}{k}$. Solving for the unknown gives $m = \dfrac{k \cdot x}{10}$. Substituting the given values yields $3\,\mathrm{kg}$.
