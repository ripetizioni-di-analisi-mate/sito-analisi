A block of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ rests on a rough horizontal table, with coefficient of static friction $\mu_s = 0.40$, and is attached to a horizontal spring of constant $k = 160\,\mathrm{N/m}$, fixed to the wall at its other end. What is the maximum extension of the spring for which the block, released at rest, stays at rest?

- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $4.9\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $0.049\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Along the horizontal the elastic force, of magnitude $k x$, must be balanced by static friction, so $f_s = k x$. Along the vertical $N = m g$.

The law of static friction requires $f_s \leq \mu_s N$, that is, $k x \leq \mu_s m g$. The maximum extension is therefore
$$
x_{\max} = \frac{\mu_s\,m g}{k} = \frac{0.40 \cdot 19.6\,\mathrm{N}}{160\,\mathrm{N/m}} = 0.049\,\mathrm{m} = 4.9\,\mathrm{cm} .
$$
The same value holds for the compression, because static friction can point either way.
