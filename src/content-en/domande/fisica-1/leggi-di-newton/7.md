## Explanation

The two springs in parallel have the same extension $x$ and are equivalent to a single spring of constant $k_{\text{par}} = 2k = 800\,\mathrm{N/m}$. At equilibrium the elastic force balances the weight, so $2k\,x = m g$. Therefore
$$
x = \frac{m g}{2k} = \frac{49.05\,\mathrm{N}}{800\,\mathrm{N/m}} = 0.0613\,\mathrm{m} = 6.13\,\mathrm{cm} .
$$
