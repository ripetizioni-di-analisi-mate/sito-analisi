A block starts from rest at a height of $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ and slides down a smooth incline of $25^\circ$ to the bottom of the incline. What is the speed of the block at the bottom?

- $4.07\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $9.64\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.65\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6.26\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

On the **smooth incline** the acceleration is constant and equals $a = g\sin 25^\circ$. The block descends from a height $h$, so it covers along the incline the distance $d = \dfrac{h}{\sin 25^\circ}$.

In uniformly accelerated motion from rest $v^2 = 2 a d$ holds, therefore
$$
v^2 = 2\,g\sin 25^\circ \cdot \frac{h}{\sin 25^\circ} = 2 g h
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
v = \sqrt{2 \cdot 9.81 \cdot 2.0}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 6.26\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The sine of the angle cancels, so the speed at the bottom depends only on the height and is the same as that of a body falling freely from the same height.
