A ball is thrown vertically upward and air resistance is negligible. Which statement about the net force at the highest point of the path is true?

- It is the weight of the ball, pointing downward
- It is zero, because the velocity is zero at that point
- It points upward, because the ball has just been rising
- It is zero, because the push of the throw balances the weight

## Explanation

After the throw the hand is no longer in contact with the ball, so the only force acting on the ball is its weight $m\vec{g}$. The weight does not depend on the velocity, therefore at the highest point too the net force is the weight, pointing downward.

By the second law the acceleration at that point is $\vec{g}$. The velocity is zero at a single instant and changes immediately from upward to downward, and this change requires precisely a nonzero acceleration.
