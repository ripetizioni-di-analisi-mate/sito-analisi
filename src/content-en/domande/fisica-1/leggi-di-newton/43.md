A body of mass $30\,\mathrm{kg}$ moves along the inside of a vertical circular track of radius $8{,}0\,\mathrm{m}$ and at the highest point has speed $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the normal force from the track at that point?

- $834\,\mathrm{N}$
- $540\,\mathrm{N}$
- $294\,\mathrm{N}$
- $246\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

At the highest point the weight and the normal force from the track are both directed downward, that is, toward the center. Their sum provides the centripetal force, so $N + m g = m\dfrac{v^2}{r}$, with $\dfrac{v^2}{r} = \dfrac{144}{8{,}0}\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 18\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Therefore
$$
N = m\left(\frac{v^2}{r} - g\right) = 30\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 8{,}19\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 246\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The value is positive, so the body stays in contact with the track, in agreement with $v \geq \sqrt{g r} = 8{,}86\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
