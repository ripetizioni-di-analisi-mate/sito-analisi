In an elevator accelerating downward with $a<g$, a vertical pendulum is at rest relative to the elevator car. Take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Given the mass $m = 3\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the acceleration magnitude $a = 4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the string tension $T_{0}$?

- $30\,\mathrm{N}$
- $18\,\mathrm{N}$
- $42\,\mathrm{N}$
- $36\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

With upward positive, the acceleration is $-a$ and the net force is $T_0-mg$. In the stated units, the relation is $T_{0} = m \cdot \left(10 - a\right)$. Substituting the given values yields $18\,\mathrm{N}$.
