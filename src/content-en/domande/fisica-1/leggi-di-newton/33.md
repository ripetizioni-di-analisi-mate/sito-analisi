A block slides down an inclined plane at $45^\circ$, with coefficient of kinetic friction $\mu_d = 0.25$. What is the acceleration of the block?

- $8.67\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $6.94\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $5.20\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2.45\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The block moves down, so the kinetic friction $\mu_d\,m g \cos 45^\circ$ is directed up the plane, opposite to the component of the weight $m g \sin 45^\circ$. The second law along the plane, with the axis pointing down the plane, gives $m g \sin 45^\circ - \mu_d\,m g \cos 45^\circ = m a$. Since $\sin 45^\circ = \cos 45^\circ = 0.707$, we obtain
$$
a = g \cdot 0.707 \cdot (1 - 0.25) = 9.81 \cdot 0.530\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 5.20\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
