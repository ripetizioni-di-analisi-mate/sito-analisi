A block slides on a horizontal floor with initial speed $6{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$, with coefficient of kinetic friction $\mu_d = 0{,}30$. What distance does it cover before stopping?

- $6{,}1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m}$
- $60\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1{,}8\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Along the vertical $N = mg$, so kinetic friction equals $\mu_d m g$ and is the only horizontal force. By the second law the deceleration has magnitude $a = \mu_d g = 0{,}30 \cdot 9{,}81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 2{,}94\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, independent of the mass. In uniformly decelerated motion up to the stop $v_0^2 = 2 a d$ holds, therefore
$$
d = \frac{v_0^2}{2a} = \frac{36\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}}{5{,}89\,\mathrm{m/s^2}} = 6{,}1\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
