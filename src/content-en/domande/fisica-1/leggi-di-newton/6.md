## Explanation

The body moves up at constant velocity, so the acceleration is zero and the net force is zero. The motion is upward, therefore the kinetic friction $\mu_d\,mg\cos 30^\circ$ is directed down the plane, like the component of the weight $mg\sin 30^\circ$. The applied force balances the sum of these two forces, so
$$
F = m g\,(\sin 30^\circ + \mu_d \cos 30^\circ) = 98.1 \cdot (0.500 + 0.173)\,\mathrm{N} = 66.0\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
