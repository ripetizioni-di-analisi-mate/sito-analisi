A horizontal conveyor belt moves at a constant speed of $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. A box is placed at rest on the belt and slides on it until it reaches the speed of the belt. The coefficient of kinetic friction between box and belt is $\mu_d = 0.40$. How long does it take the box to reach the speed of the belt?

- $0.51\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $5.0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.20\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

While the box slides, its velocity relative to the belt points backward, so the kinetic friction points **forward**, in the direction of motion of the belt. It is the only horizontal force, and along the vertical $N = m g$, therefore the second law gives
$$
a = \frac{\mu_d\,m g}{m} = \mu_d\,g = 0.40 \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 3.92\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
The box starts from rest with constant acceleration, so it reaches the speed $v = 2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the time
$$
t = \frac{v}{a} = \frac{2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}}{3.92\,\mathrm{m/s^2}} = 0.51\,\mathrm{s} .
$$
From that instant there is no more sliding and, since the belt moves at constant speed, the friction vanishes.
