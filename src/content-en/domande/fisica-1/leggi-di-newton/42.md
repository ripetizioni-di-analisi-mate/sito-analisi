A stone of mass $0.40\,\mathrm{kg}$, tied to an ideal string $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$ long, rotates in a vertical plane. At the lowest point of the path it has speed $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the tension in the string at that point?

- $11.9\,\mathrm{N}$
- $4.08\,\mathrm{N}$
- $8.00\,\mathrm{N}$
- $5.92\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

At the lowest point the center of the path is above, so the tension is directed toward the center and the weight in the opposite sense. The component of the net force toward the center provides the centripetal force, therefore $T - m g = m\dfrac{v^2}{r}$, with $\dfrac{v^2}{r} = \dfrac{16}{0.80}\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 20.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. It follows that
$$
T = m \left( g + \frac{v^2}{r} \right) = 0.40\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 29.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 11.9\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
