A block, on a smooth horizontal plane, lies between two walls and is connected to each of them by a horizontal ideal spring: on the left a spring of constant $k_1 = 100\,\mathrm{N/m}$, on the right a spring of constant $k_2 = 150\,\mathrm{N/m}$. In the equilibrium position both springs are at their rest length. What is the magnitude of the total force of the springs on the block when it is displaced $2.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ to the right?

- $5.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{N}$
- $3.0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1.0\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

With the displacement $x = 0.020\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right, the left spring is stretched by $x$ and pulls the block to the left with force $k_1 x$. The right spring is compressed by $x$ and also pushes the block to the left, with force $k_2 x$.

The two springs have the same deformation $x$ and their forces have the same direction, so the springs behave as **springs in parallel** and the total force is
$$
(k_1 + k_2)\,x = 250\,\mathrm{N/m} \cdot 0.020\,\mathrm{m} = 5.0\,\mathrm{N} ,
$$
pointing to the left. The value $1.2\,\mathrm{N}$ would be obtained by treating the two springs as connected in series, but springs in series are stretched by the same force, while here they have the same deformation.
