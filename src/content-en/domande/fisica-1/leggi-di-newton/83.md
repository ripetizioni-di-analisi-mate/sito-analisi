Two hanging masses on opposite sides of an ideal pulley are joined by an inextensible string. The second mass is $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the first is heavier. Given gravitational acceleration $g = 10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and the acceleration magnitude $a = 5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the first mass $m$?

- $9\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $18\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Adding the equations along the directions of motion eliminates the tension. The difference in weights accelerates the total mass. In the stated units, the relation is $a = \dfrac{g \cdot \left(m - 3\right)}{m + 3}$. Solving for the unknown gives $m = \dfrac{3 \cdot \left(- a - g\right)}{a - g}$. Substituting the given values yields $9\,\mathrm{kg}$.
