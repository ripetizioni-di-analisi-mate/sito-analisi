## Explanation

In the frame of the railway car the pendulum is at rest, so the tension balances the weight $mg$, vertical and downward, and the fictitious force, horizontal, opposite to the acceleration of the car and of magnitude $mA$, with $A = 3.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The components of the tension therefore satisfy $T\cos\theta = m g$ along the vertical and $T\sin\theta = m A$ along the horizontal. Dividing the second relation by the first gives
$$
\tan\theta = \frac{A}{g} = \frac{3.0}{9.81} = 0.306 ,
$$
so $\theta = 17^\circ$, with the string tilted opposite to the acceleration.
