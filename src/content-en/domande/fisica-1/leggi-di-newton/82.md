Two hanging masses on opposite sides of an ideal pulley are joined by an inextensible string. The second mass is $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the first is heavier. Given the first mass $m = 9\,\mathrm{kg}$ and gravitational acceleration $g = 10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the acceleration magnitude $a$?

- $10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $\dfrac{20}{3}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Adding the equations along the directions of motion eliminates the tension. The difference in weights accelerates the total mass. In the stated units, the relation is $a = \dfrac{g \cdot \left(m - 3\right)}{m + 3}$. Substituting the given values yields $5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
