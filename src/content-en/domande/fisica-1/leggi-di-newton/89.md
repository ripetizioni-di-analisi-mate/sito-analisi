A block rests on the floor of a vehicle accelerating horizontally. Friction is the only horizontal force on the block and the block does not slip. Given the acceleration magnitude $a = 3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and the magnitude of static friction on the block $F = 21\,\mathrm{N}$, what is the mass $m$?

- $\dfrac{7}{2}\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $\dfrac{21}{2}\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $7\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $14\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

In the ground frame the block has the same acceleration as the vehicle. The horizontal net force is the static friction force. In the stated units, the relation is $F = a \cdot m$. Solving for the unknown gives $m = \dfrac{F}{a}$. Substituting the given values yields $7\,\mathrm{kg}$.
