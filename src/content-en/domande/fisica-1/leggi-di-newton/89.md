A particle moves in uniform circular motion. Which statement about the net force acting on it is true?

- It points toward the center and has constant magnitude
- It is zero, because the speed is constant
- It is tangent to the path, in the direction of the velocity
- It points away from the center of the circle

## Explanation

In uniform circular motion the speed is constant, but the direction of the velocity changes continuously, so the acceleration is not zero. By the kinematics of uniform circular motion the acceleration points toward the center and has constant magnitude $\dfrac{v^2}{r}$.

By the second law the net force has the direction of the acceleration, therefore it **points toward the center** and has constant magnitude $m\dfrac{v^2}{r}$. A tangential net force would change the speed.
