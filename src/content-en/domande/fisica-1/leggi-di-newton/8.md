- $9.81\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

In the **Atwood machine** the body of mass $m_1 = 2.5\,\mathrm{kg}$ goes down and the body of mass $m_2 = 1.5\,\mathrm{kg}$ goes up, both with acceleration
$$
a = \frac{m_1 - m_2}{m_1 + m_2}\,g = \frac{1.0}{4.0} \cdot 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 2.45\,\mathrm{m/s^2} .
$$
The second law for the rising body gives $T - m_2 g = m_2 a$, so $T = m_2\,(g + a) = 1.5\,\mathrm{kg} \cdot 12.26\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 18.4\,\mathrm{N}$. The tension lies between the two weights, $14.7\,\mathrm{N}$ and $24.5\,\mathrm{N}$, because one body accelerates upward and the other downward.
