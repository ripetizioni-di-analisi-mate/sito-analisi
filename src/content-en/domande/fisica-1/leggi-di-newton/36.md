A body of mass $4{,}0\,\mathrm{kg}$ is at rest on an inclined plane at $30^\circ$, with coefficient of static friction $\mu_s = 0{,}70$. What is the static friction force on the body?

- $23{,}8\,\mathrm{N}$
- $34{,}0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $19{,}6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $27{,}5\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Since $\tan 30^\circ = 0{,}577$ is smaller than $\mu_s = 0{,}70$, the body stays in equilibrium. Along the plane static friction balances the component of the weight, so it is directed up the plane and equals
$$
f_s = m g \sin 30^\circ = 39{,}2\,\mathrm{N} \cdot \frac{1}{2} = 19{,}6\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The value $\mu_s N = \mu_s\,m g\cos 30^\circ = 23{,}8\,\mathrm{N}$ is the maximum that static friction can reach, and friction reaches it only when the body is about to slide.
