A book of mass $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ lies on a horizontal table, and a second book of mass $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ lies on top of it. Both books are at rest. What is the normal force exerted by the table on the lower book?

- $19.6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $29.4\,\mathrm{N}$
- $9.81\,\mathrm{N}$
- $49.1\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The upper book is acted on by its weight $m_1 g = 9.81\,\mathrm{N}$ and by the reaction of the lower book, so in equilibrium the lower book pushes it upward with $9.81\,\mathrm{N}$. By **Newton's third law** the upper book presses on the lower book with a force of $9.81\,\mathrm{N}$ pointing downward.

The lower book is therefore acted on by its weight $m_2 g = 19.6\,\mathrm{N}$, by the push of $9.81\,\mathrm{N}$ from the upper book and by the normal force $N$ of the table. Equilibrium along the vertical gives $N = 19.6\,\mathrm{N} + 9.81\,\mathrm{N} = 29.4\,\mathrm{N}$, equal to the total weight of the two books.
