## Explanation

Without friction only the weight and the normal force, perpendicular to the roadway, act on the car. Their resultant is horizontal, directed toward the center and of magnitude $m\dfrac{v^2}{r}$, so
$$
\begin{cases}
N\cos 15^\circ = m g \\[4pt]
N \sin 15^\circ = m\,\dfrac{v^2}{r}
\end{cases}
$$
Dividing the second equation by the first gives $\tan 15^\circ = \dfrac{v^2}{g r}$. Therefore $v = \sqrt{g r \tan 15^\circ} = \sqrt{9.81 \cdot 120 \cdot 0.268}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 17.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$, about $64\,\mathrm{km/h}$.
