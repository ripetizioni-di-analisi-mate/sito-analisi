A vertical pendulum is at rest in a cabin accelerating downward with $a<g$. Take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Given the mass $m = 3\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the string tension $T_{0} = 18\,\mathrm{N}$, what is the acceleration magnitude $a$?

- $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

With upward positive, the acceleration is $-a$ and the net force is $T_0-mg$. In the stated units, the relation is $T_{0} = m \cdot \left(10 - a\right)$. Solving for the unknown gives $a = - \dfrac{T_{0}}{m} + 10$. Substituting the given values yields $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
