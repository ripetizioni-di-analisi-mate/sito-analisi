A block moves up a $30^\circ$ incline at constant speed under a pull parallel to the incline. Kinetic friction has constant magnitude $f$, and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ is assumed. Given the mass $m = 4\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the friction magnitude $f = 6\,\mathrm{N}$, what is the pull magnitude $F$?

- $26\,\mathrm{N}$
- $52\,\mathrm{N}$
- $46\,\mathrm{N}$
- $14\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Along the incline, the pull balances both the weight component $mg\sin 30^\circ$ and friction. In the stated units, the relation is $F = f + 5 \cdot m$. Substituting the given values yields $26\,\mathrm{N}$.
