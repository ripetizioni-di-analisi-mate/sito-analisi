To keep a block of mass $5.0\,\mathrm{kg}$ at rest on a rough ramp inclined at $40^\circ$, a force parallel to the ramp and pointing up the ramp is applied. The coefficient of static friction between block and ramp is $\mu_s = 0.30$. What is the minimum magnitude of this force?

- $42.8\,\mathrm{N}$
- $31.5\,\mathrm{N}$
- $16.8\,\mathrm{N}$
- $20.3\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Since $\tan 40^\circ = 0.839$ is larger than $\mu_s = 0.30$, without the force the block would slide down. The force is minimum when the block is about to slide down, so the static friction has its maximum value $\mu_s N$ and points up the incline, like the force.

Perpendicular to the incline $N = m g\cos 40^\circ$, therefore equilibrium along the incline gives $F + \mu_s m g \cos 40^\circ = m g \sin 40^\circ$. It follows that
$$
F_{\min} = m g\,(\sin 40^\circ - \mu_s \cos 40^\circ) = 49.1\,\mathrm{N} \cdot (0.643 - 0.230) = 20.3\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The value $42.8\,\mathrm{N}$ is instead the maximum force, beyond which the block starts moving up.
