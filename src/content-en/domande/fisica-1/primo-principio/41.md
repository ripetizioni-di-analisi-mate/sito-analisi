$2{,}0\,\mathrm{mol}$ of ideal gas at $300\,\mathrm{K}$ expand quasi-statically and isothermally until the pressure goes from $4{,}0 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$ to $1{,}0 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$. How much work does the gas do? ($\ln 2 \approx 0{,}693$)

- $-6{,}9\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $3{,}5\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $6{,}9\,\mathrm{kJ}$

## Explanation

In the isotherm $p_i V_i = p_f V_f$, therefore $\dfrac{V_f}{V_i} = \dfrac{p_i}{p_f} = 4$. The work of the **isothermal process** is
$$
L = nRT \ln\frac{V_f}{V_i} = 2{,}0 \cdot 8{,}314 \cdot 300 \cdot \ln 4\,\mathrm{J} ,
$$
and since $\ln 4 = 2 \ln 2 \approx 1{,}39$, one obtains $L \approx 4{,}99 \cdot 10^3 \cdot 1{,}39\,\mathrm{J} \approx 6{,}9 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{J}$. The value $-6{,}9\,\mathrm{kJ}$ is obtained with the volume ratio equal to $\dfrac{p_f}{p_i}$, that is, inverted, and $3{,}5\,\mathrm{kJ}$ with $\ln 2$ instead of $\ln 4$.
