A car engine is modeled by a thermodynamic cycle that does $400\,\mathrm{J}$ of work per cycle, and the engine runs $3000$ cycles per minute. What power does it deliver?

- $20\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $1.2 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $0.40\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{kW}$

## Explanation

Power is the work done in one second. In one second the engine runs $\dfrac{3000}{60} = 50$ cycles, so it does the work $50 \cdot 400\,\mathrm{J} = 2.0 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{J}$. Therefore the power is $P = 2.0 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{W} = 20\,\mathrm{kW}$. The value $1.2 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{kW}$ is obtained by using the number of cycles per minute as if it were per second.
