A vessel contains a diatomic ideal gas in a regime where vibrational modes are negligible. It is heated at constant pressure. Use $R\simeq8\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$. Given the amount of gas $n = 2\,\mathrm{mol}$ and the temperature rise $T = 40\,\mathrm{K}$, what is the heat absorbed $Q$?

- $1600\,\mathrm{J}$
- $640\,\mathrm{J}$
- $2240\,\mathrm{J}$
- $4480\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

For heating at constant pressure, use $C_P=C_V+R$. With five active degrees of freedom, $C_V=5R/2$ and $C_P=7R/2$. In the stated units, the relation is $Q = 28 \cdot T \cdot n$. Substituting the given values yields $2240\,\mathrm{J}$.
