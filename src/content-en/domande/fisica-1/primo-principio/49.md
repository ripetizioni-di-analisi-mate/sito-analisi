An ideal gas expands slowly at constant pressure. Work done by the gas is positive. The initial data are the pressure $p = 150\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and the volume increase $V = 4\,\mathrm{L}$. Change the volume increase to $8\,\mathrm{L}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the ratio of the final value of $W$ to its initial value?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $1$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

On the pressure-volume diagram, work is the area of a rectangle of height $p$. One kilopascal-liter equals one joule. The relation $W = V \cdot p$ gives $600\,\mathrm{J}$ before the change and $1200\,\mathrm{J}$ after it. The ratio is $2$.
