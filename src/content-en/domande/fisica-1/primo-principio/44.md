In a cycle a gas absorbs a total of $800\,\mathrm{J}$ of heat and releases $500\,\mathrm{J}$. What is the total work done by the gas in the cycle?

- $1300\,\mathrm{J}$
- $300\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-300\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

At the end of the cycle the gas returns to its initial state, and internal energy is a state function, therefore $\Delta U = 0$. By the first law the total work is then equal to the total heat exchanged, $L_{\text{tot}} = Q_{\text{abs}} - Q_{\text{rel}}$. Hence $L_{\text{tot}} = 800\,\mathrm{J} - 500\,\mathrm{J} = 300\,\mathrm{J}$. The value $0\,\mathrm{J}$ is obtained by confusing the work with the change in internal energy, which is zero in the cycle.
