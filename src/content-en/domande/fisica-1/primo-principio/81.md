An ideal gas expands reversibly at constant temperature to three times its initial volume. Use $R\simeq8\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$. Given the amount of gas $n = 2\,\mathrm{mol}$ and the work done by the gas $W = 4800 \cdot \log{\left(3 \right)}\,\mathrm{J}$, what is the temperature $T$?

- $600\,\mathrm{K}$
- $150 \cdot \log{\left(3 \right)}\,\mathrm{K}$
- $300\,\mathrm{K}$
- $100 \cdot \log{\left(3 \right)}\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

For an isothermal process $p=nRT/V$. Integrating the pressure gives $nRT\ln(V_f/V_i)$. In the stated units, the relation is $W = 8 \cdot T \cdot n \cdot \log{\left(3 \right)}$. Solving for the unknown gives $T = \dfrac{W}{8 \cdot n \cdot \log{\left(3 \right)}}$. Substituting the given values yields $300\,\mathrm{K}$.
