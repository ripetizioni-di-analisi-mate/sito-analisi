## Explanation

By **Fourier's law** the power through the glass is $P = k\,A\,\dfrac{T_1 - T_2}{d}$, with the thickness in meters, $d = 4{,}0\,\mathrm{mm} = 4{,}0 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m}$. Therefore
$$
P = 0{,}80 \cdot 1{,}5 \cdot \frac{2{,}0}{4{,}0 \cdot 10^{-3}}\,\mathrm{W} = 600\,\mathrm{W} .
$$
The value $0{,}60\,\mathrm{W}$ is obtained by leaving the thickness in millimeters, and $60\,\mathrm{W}$ by converting it as if it were expressed in centimeters.
