The mixer of a shower combines hot water at $64\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ and cold water at $10\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ and delivers $9.0\,\mathrm{L}$ per minute of water at $34\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. Neglecting heat exchanges with the pipes, how many liters per minute of hot water are needed?

- $5.0\,\mathrm{L/min}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{L/min}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{L/min}$
- $4.8\,\mathrm{L/min}$

## Explanation

In one minute $V_h$ liters of hot water and $9.0 - V_h$ liters of cold water are mixed, and one liter has the same mass in both cases. The heat given off by the hot water equals the heat absorbed by the cold water, so $V_h\,(64 - 34) = (9.0 - V_h)(34 - 10)$. It follows that
$$
V_h = \frac{9.0 \cdot 24}{30 + 24}\,\mathrm{L} = 4.0\,\mathrm{L} ,
$$
hence $4.0\,\mathrm{L}$ of hot water per minute are needed. The value $5.0\,\mathrm{L/min}$ is obtained by swapping the two temperature differences.
