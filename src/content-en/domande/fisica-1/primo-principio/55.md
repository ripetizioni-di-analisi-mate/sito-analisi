A monatomic ideal gas in a rigid vessel is heated. Use $R\simeq 8\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$. The initial data are the amount of gas $n = 3\,\mathrm{mol}$ and the temperature increase $T = 25\,\mathrm{K}$. In the second configuration, change the amount of gas to $5\,\mathrm{mol}$ and the temperature increase to $30\,\mathrm{K}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the final value of $Q$ minus its initial value?

- $900\,\mathrm{J}$
- $600\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-900\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1500\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

At constant volume the gas does no work. The heat received is the change $nC_V\Delta T$, with $C_V=3R/2$. The relation $Q = 12 \cdot T \cdot n$ gives $900\,\mathrm{J}$ before the change and $1800\,\mathrm{J}$ after it. Subtracting the initial value from the final value gives $900\,\mathrm{J}$.
