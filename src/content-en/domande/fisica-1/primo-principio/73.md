An unconstrained bar of uniform material is heated by $50\,\mathrm{K}$. The linear approximation applies. Given the linear expansion coefficient $a = 12\,\mathrm{\mu m/(m\,K)}$ and the extension $d = \dfrac{12}{5}\,\mathrm{mm}$, what is the initial length $L$?

- $\dfrac{1}{250}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The extension is $\alpha L\Delta T$. With the coefficient in micrometers per meter per kelvin, divide the result by a thousand to obtain millimeters. In the stated units, the relation is $d = \dfrac{L \cdot a}{20}$. Solving for the unknown gives $L = \dfrac{20 \cdot d}{a}$. Substituting the given values yields $4\,\mathrm{m}$.
