- $0{,}95\,\mathrm{L}$
- $2{,}9\,\mathrm{L}$
- $1{,}9\,\mathrm{L}$
- $0{,}32\,\mathrm{L}$

## Explanation

For a liquid one uses **volumetric expansion** with the coefficient $\beta$ of the liquid, which is already a volumetric coefficient. The temperature increases by $\Delta T = 20\,\mathrm{K}$, therefore
$$
\Delta V = \beta\,V_0\,\Delta T = 9{,}5 \cdot 10^{-4} \cdot 50{,}0 \cdot 20\,\mathrm{L} = 0{,}95\,\mathrm{L} .
$$
The value $2{,}9\,\mathrm{L}$ is obtained by multiplying $\beta$ by $3$, as if it were a linear expansion coefficient, and $1{,}9\,\mathrm{L}$ by adding the two temperatures instead of subtracting them.
