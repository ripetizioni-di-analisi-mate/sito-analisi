An ideal gas expands slowly at constant pressure. Work done by the gas is positive. Given the pressure $p = 150\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and the volume increase $V = 4\,\mathrm{L}$, what is the work done by the gas $W$?

- $900\,\mathrm{J}$
- $600\,\mathrm{J}$
- $300\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1200\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

On the pressure-volume diagram, work is the area of a rectangle of height $p$. One kilopascal-liter equals one joule. In the stated units, the relation is $W = V \cdot p$. Substituting the given values yields $600\,\mathrm{J}$.
