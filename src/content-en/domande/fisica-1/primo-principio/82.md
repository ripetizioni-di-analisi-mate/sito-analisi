A system follows a clockwise rectangular cycle on a pressure-volume diagram. Volume is on the horizontal axis. Given the pressure difference between the isobars $p = 60\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and the volume difference between the isochores $V = 5\,\mathrm{L}$, what is the net work per cycle $W$?

- $450\,\mathrm{J}$
- $300\,\mathrm{J}$
- $150\,\mathrm{J}$
- $600\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The constant-volume legs do no work. The difference between the isobaric work terms equals the enclosed area, positive for a clockwise cycle. In the stated units, the relation is $W = V \cdot p$. Substituting the given values yields $300\,\mathrm{J}$.
