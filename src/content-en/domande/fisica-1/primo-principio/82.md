A monatomic ideal gas at a pressure of $2.0 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$ occupies $1.0\,\mathrm{L}$ and expands adiabatically and quasi-statically to $8.0\,\mathrm{L}$. How much work does it do?

- $416\,\mathrm{J}$
- $225\,\mathrm{J}$
- $282\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-225\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

In the adiabatic process $pV^{\gamma}$ is constant with $\gamma = \dfrac{5}{3}$, so $p_f = 2.0 \cdot 10^5 \cdot 8^{-\frac{5}{3}}\,\mathrm{Pa} = \dfrac{2.0 \cdot 10^5}{32}\,\mathrm{Pa} = 6.25 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{Pa}$. It follows that $p_i V_i = 200\,\mathrm{J}$ and $p_f V_f = 6.25 \cdot 10^3 \cdot 8.0 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{J} = 50\,\mathrm{J}$. The work of the adiabat is
$$
L = \frac{p_i V_i - p_f V_f}{\gamma - 1} = \frac{200 - 50}{\frac{2}{3}}\,\mathrm{J} = 225\,\mathrm{J} ,
$$
positive because the gas expands. The value $416\,\mathrm{J}$ is the work of an isotherm between the same volumes, $p_i V_i \ln 8$, and $282\,\mathrm{J}$ is obtained with $\gamma = 1.4$.
