An unconstrained bar of uniform material is heated by $50\,\mathrm{K}$. The linear approximation applies. Given the initial length $L = 4\,\mathrm{m}$ and the extension $d = \dfrac{12}{5}\,\mathrm{mm}$, what is the linear expansion coefficient $a$?

- $\dfrac{3}{250}\,\mathrm{\mu m/(m\,K)}$
- $6\,\mathrm{\mu m/(m\,K)}$
- $24\,\mathrm{\mu m/(m\,K)}$
- $12\,\mathrm{\mu m/(m\,K)}$

## Explanation

The extension is $\alpha L\Delta T$. With the coefficient in micrometers per meter per kelvin, divide the result by a thousand to obtain millimeters. In the stated units, the relation is $d = \dfrac{L \cdot a}{20}$. Solving for the unknown gives $a = \dfrac{20 \cdot d}{L}$. Substituting the given values yields $12\,\mathrm{\mu m/(m\,K)}$.
