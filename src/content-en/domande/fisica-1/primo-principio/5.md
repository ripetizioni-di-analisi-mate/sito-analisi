- $146\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

The container is insulated and has negligible heat capacity, therefore the **equilibrium temperature** is the average of the initial temperatures weighted by the heat capacities. The heat capacities are $m_{\mathrm{Fe}}\,c_{\mathrm{Fe}} = 0.25 \cdot 450\,\mathrm{J/K} = 112.5\,\mathrm{J/K}$ and $m_a\,c_a = 4186\,\mathrm{J/K}$, hence
$$
T_e = \frac{112.5 \cdot 200 + 4186 \cdot 20}{112.5 + 4186}\,^\circ\mathrm{C} = \frac{106\,220}{4298.5}\,^\circ\mathrm{C} \approx 24.7\,^\circ\mathrm{C} .
$$
The heat capacity of the water is about $37$ times that of the iron, therefore the final temperature is close to the initial temperature of the water. The value $56.0\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ is obtained by weighting the temperatures by the masses alone, and $146\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ by swapping the two specific heats.
