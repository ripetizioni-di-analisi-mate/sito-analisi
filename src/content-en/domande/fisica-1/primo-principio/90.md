A monatomic ideal gas undergoes adiabatic compression. Take work done on the gas as positive and assume $R\simeq8\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$. Given the work done on the gas $W = 720\,\mathrm{J}$ and the temperature rise $T = 30\,\mathrm{K}$, what is the amount of gas $n$?

- $3\,\mathrm{mol}$
- $2\,\mathrm{mol}$
- $4\,\mathrm{mol}$
- $\dfrac{6}{5}\,\mathrm{mol}$

## Explanation

There is no heat transfer. Work done on the gas increases its internal energy by $nC_V\Delta T$, with $C_V=3R/2$. In the stated units, the relation is $T = \dfrac{W}{12 \cdot n}$. Solving for the unknown gives $n = \dfrac{W}{12 \cdot T}$. Substituting the given values yields $2\,\mathrm{mol}$.
