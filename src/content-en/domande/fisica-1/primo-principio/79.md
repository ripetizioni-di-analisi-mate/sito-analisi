A monatomic ideal gas at a pressure of $1.0 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$ is compressed adiabatically and quasi-statically to one eighth of its initial volume. What pressure does it reach?

- $8.0 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $1.8 \cdot 10^6\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $3.2 \cdot 10^6\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $4.0 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

In a quasi-static adiabatic process $pV^{\gamma}$ is constant, and for a monatomic gas $\gamma = \dfrac{5}{3}$. So $p_f = p_i \left(\dfrac{V_i}{V_f}\right)^{\gamma} = 1.0 \cdot 10^5 \cdot 8^{\frac{5}{3}}\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Since $8^{\frac{1}{3}} = 2$, we have $8^{\frac{5}{3}} = 2^5 = 32$, therefore $p_f = 3.2 \cdot 10^6\,\mathrm{Pa}$. The value $8.0 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$ is that of an isothermal compression, and $1.8 \cdot 10^6\,\mathrm{Pa}$ is obtained with $\gamma = 1.4$, which is the value for a diatomic gas.
