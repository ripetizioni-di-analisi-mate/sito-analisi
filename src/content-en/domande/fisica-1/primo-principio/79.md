An ideal gas expands reversibly at constant temperature to three times its initial volume. Use $R\simeq8\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$. Given the amount of gas $n = 2\,\mathrm{mol}$ and the temperature $T = 300\,\mathrm{K}$, what is the work done by the gas $W$?

- $4800 \cdot \log{\left(3 \right)}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $14400\,\mathrm{J}$
- $9600 \cdot \log{\left(3 \right)}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $9600\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

For an isothermal process $p=nRT/V$. Integrating the pressure gives $nRT\ln(V_f/V_i)$. In the stated units, the relation is $W = 8 \cdot T \cdot n \cdot \log{\left(3 \right)}$. Substituting the given values yields $4800 \cdot \log{\left(3 \right)}\,\mathrm{J}$.
