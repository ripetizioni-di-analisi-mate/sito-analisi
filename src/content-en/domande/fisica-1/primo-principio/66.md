A solid sample is already at its melting temperature. Heating melts it completely, without losses. Given the mass $m = \dfrac{2}{5}\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the specific latent heat $l = 300\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$, what is the heat required to melt the sample $Q$?

- $60000\,\mathrm{J}$
- $120000\,\mathrm{J}$
- $240000\,\mathrm{J}$
- $120\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Temperature remains constant during melting. Heat equals mass times latent heat, converted to joules per kilogram. In the stated units, the relation is $Q = 1000 \cdot l \cdot m$. Substituting the given values yields $120000\,\mathrm{J}$.
