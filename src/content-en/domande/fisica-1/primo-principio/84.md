A system follows a clockwise rectangular cycle on a pressure-volume diagram. Volume is on the horizontal axis. Given the pressure difference between the isobars $p = 60\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and the net work per cycle $W = 300\,\mathrm{J}$, what is the volume difference between the isochores $V$?

- $10\,\mathrm{L}$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}\,\mathrm{L}$
- $\dfrac{15}{2}\,\mathrm{L}$
- $5\,\mathrm{L}$

## Explanation

The constant-volume legs do no work. The difference between the isobaric work terms equals the enclosed area, positive for a clockwise cycle. In the stated units, the relation is $W = V \cdot p$. Solving for the unknown gives $V = \dfrac{W}{p}$. Substituting the given values yields $5\,\mathrm{L}$.
