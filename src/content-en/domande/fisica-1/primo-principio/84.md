A gas goes around a cycle made of the processes $A \to B$, $B \to C$ and $C \to A$. In the process $A \to B$ its internal energy increases by $300\,\mathrm{J}$, in the process $B \to C$ it decreases by $500\,\mathrm{J}$. What is the change in internal energy in the process $C \to A$?

- $-200\,\mathrm{J}$
- $+800\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $+200\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Internal energy is a state function and at the end of the cycle the gas is back in state $A$, so the sum of the three changes is zero, $\Delta U_{AB} + \Delta U_{BC} + \Delta U_{CA} = 0$. Therefore $\Delta U_{CA} = -(300 - 500)\,\mathrm{J} = +200\,\mathrm{J}$. The value $0\,\mathrm{J}$ is the change in internal energy of the whole cycle.
