## Explanation

The temperature change is $\Delta T = 40\,^\circ\mathrm{C} - (-10\,^\circ\mathrm{C}) = 50\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$, which is equivalent to $50\,\mathrm{K}$ because the kelvin and the degree Celsius have the same magnitude. By the law of **linear expansion**
$$
\Delta L = \alpha\,L_0\,\Delta T = 1{,}2 \cdot 10^{-5} \cdot 200 \cdot 50\,\mathrm{m} = 0{,}12\,\mathrm{m} ,
$$
therefore the structure lengthens by $12\,\mathrm{cm}$. The value $0{,}072\,\mathrm{m}$ is obtained with $\Delta T = 30\,\mathrm{K}$, that is, ignoring the sign of the initial temperature, and $0{,}36\,\mathrm{m}$ with the coefficient $3\alpha$ of volume expansion.
