A gas absorbs heat as it expands and does work. Heat entering the gas and work done by it are positive. The initial data are the heat absorbed $Q = 750\,\mathrm{J}$ and the work done by the gas $W = 450\,\mathrm{J}$. Change the heat absorbed to $500\,\mathrm{J}$ and the work done by the gas to $700\,\mathrm{J}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the final value of $U$ minus its initial value?

- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $500\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-200\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-500\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Heat adds energy to the gas. Work done on the surroundings removes it, so $\Delta U=Q-W$. The relation $U = Q - W$ gives $300\,\mathrm{J}$ before the change and $-200\,\mathrm{J}$ after it. Subtracting the initial value from the final value gives $-500\,\mathrm{J}$.
