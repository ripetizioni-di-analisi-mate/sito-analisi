An ideal gas in an insulated cylinder is compressed as the piston does $100\,\mathrm J$ of work on it. Which pair gives the heat received and the internal energy change?

- $Q=0$, $\Delta U=-100\,\mathrm J$
- $Q=0$, $\Delta U=+100\,\mathrm J$
- $Q=+100\,\mathrm J$, $\Delta U=0$
- $Q=-100\,\mathrm J$, $\Delta U=0$

## Explanation

Insulation prevents heat transfer. Work supplied through the piston is the only energy transfer, so internal energy increases by $100\,\mathrm J$.
