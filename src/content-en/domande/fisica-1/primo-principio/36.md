An ideal gas occupies half of a rigid container with adiabatic walls, and the other half is empty. The wall separating the two halves is broken and the gas fills the whole container. Which statement is true for the final equilibrium state?

- The temperature decreases, because the gas does work while expanding
- The temperature and the pressure stay the same
- The temperature is halved and the pressure stays the same
- The temperature stays the same and the pressure is halved

## Explanation

In the **free expansion** the gas exchanges no heat, because the walls are adiabatic, and does no work, because nothing opposes the expansion into vacuum. By the first law, therefore, $\Delta U = Q - L = 0$. The internal energy of the ideal gas depends only on the temperature, hence the temperature stays the same. The volume doubles at constant temperature, therefore by the equation of state the pressure **is halved**.
