## Explanation

The drink cools from $25\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ to $5.0\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ and releases the heat $0.30 \cdot 4186 \cdot 20\,\mathrm{J} \approx 2.51 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{J}$. The ice of mass $m_g$ absorbs the heat $m_g\,\lambda_f$ to melt and then $m_g\,c_a \cdot 5.0\,\mathrm{K}$ to bring the meltwater to $5.0\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. There are no exchanges with the surroundings, therefore the heat released equals the heat absorbed and
$$
m_g = \frac{2.51 \cdot 10^4}{3.33 \cdot 10^5 + 4186 \cdot 5.0}\,\mathrm{kg} = \frac{2.51 \cdot 10^4}{3.54 \cdot 10^5}\,\mathrm{kg} \approx 0.071\,\mathrm{kg} .
$$
The value $94\,\mathrm{g}$ is obtained by cooling the drink down to $0\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ and ignoring the warming of the meltwater, and $1.2 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{g}$ by ignoring the latent heat of fusion.
