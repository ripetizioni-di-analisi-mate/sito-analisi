An unconstrained bar of uniform material is heated by $50\,\mathrm{K}$. The linear approximation applies. Given the linear expansion coefficient $a = 12\,\mathrm{\mu m/(m\,K)}$ and the initial length $L = 4\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the extension $d$?

- $\dfrac{12}{5}\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $\dfrac{6}{5}\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $2400\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $\dfrac{24}{5}\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The extension is $\alpha L\Delta T$. With the coefficient in micrometers per meter per kelvin, divide the result by a thousand to obtain millimeters. In the stated units, the relation is $d = \dfrac{L \cdot a}{20}$. Substituting the given values yields $\dfrac{12}{5}\,\mathrm{mm}$.
