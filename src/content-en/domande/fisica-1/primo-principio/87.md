An ideal gas goes around the cycle $ABCA$ in the figure: an isothermal expansion from $A$ to $B$, an isobaric compression from $B$ to $C$ and an isochoric process from $C$ to $A$. In which processes does the gas absorb heat?

::grafico{curve="t,4/t,1,4" vettori="1.9,2.105,2.1,1.905;4,1,1,1;1,1,1,4" punti="1,4,pieno,A;4,1,pieno,B;1,1,pieno,C" xmin=0 xmax=5 ymin=0 ymax=5 nomi="V,p" legenda=0 titolo="Cycle $ABCA$ in the $(V, p)$ plane, in arbitrary units"}

- Only in the process $A \to B$
- In the processes $A \to B$ and $B \to C$
- In the processes $A \to B$ and $C \to A$
- Only in the process $C \to A$

## Explanation

In the isothermal expansion $\Delta U = 0$ and the gas does positive work, so $Q_{AB} = L_{AB} > 0$ and the gas absorbs heat. In the isobaric compression the volume decreases at constant pressure, hence by the equation of state the temperature decreases and $Q_{BC} = n\,c_p\,\Delta T < 0$. On the isochore the pressure increases at constant volume, so the temperature increases and $Q_{CA} = n\,c_V\,\Delta T > 0$. Therefore the gas absorbs heat in the processes $A \to B$ and $C \to A$.
