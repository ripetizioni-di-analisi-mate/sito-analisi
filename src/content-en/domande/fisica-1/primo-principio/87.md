A vessel contains a diatomic ideal gas in a regime where vibrational modes are negligible. It is heated at constant pressure. Use $R\simeq8\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$. Given the amount of gas $n = 2\,\mathrm{mol}$ and the heat absorbed $Q = 2240\,\mathrm{J}$, what is the temperature rise $T$?

- $40\,\mathrm{K}$
- $56\,\mathrm{K}$
- $140\,\mathrm{K}$
- $80\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

For heating at constant pressure, use $C_P=C_V+R$. With five active degrees of freedom, $C_V=5R/2$ and $C_P=7R/2$. In the stated units, the relation is $Q = 28 \cdot T \cdot n$. Solving for the unknown gives $T = \dfrac{Q}{28 \cdot n}$. Substituting the given values yields $40\,\mathrm{K}$.
