A monatomic ideal gas in a rigid vessel is heated. Use $R\simeq 8\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$. Given the amount of gas $n = 3\,\mathrm{mol}$ and the heat absorbed $Q = 900\,\mathrm{J}$, what is the temperature increase $T$?

- $\dfrac{75}{2}\,\mathrm{K}$
- $50\,\mathrm{K}$
- $25\,\mathrm{K}$
- $15\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

At constant volume the gas does no work. The heat received is the change $nC_V\Delta T$, with $C_V=3R/2$. In the stated units, the relation is $Q = 12 \cdot T \cdot n$. Solving for the unknown gives $T = \dfrac{Q}{12 \cdot n}$. Substituting the given values yields $25\,\mathrm{K}$.
