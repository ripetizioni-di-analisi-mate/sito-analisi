For an ideal gas the molar heat capacity at constant pressure is measured to be $c_p = 29{,}1\,\mathrm{J/(mol\cdot K)}$. What is the ratio $\gamma = \dfrac{c_p}{c_V}$? ($R = 8{,}314\,\mathrm{J/(mol\cdot K)}$)

- $1{,}67$
- $3{,}50$
- $0{,}714$
- $1{,}40$

## Explanation

By **Mayer's relation** $c_V = c_p - R = (29{,}1 - 8{,}31)\,\mathrm{J/(mol\cdot K)} \approx 20{,}8\,\mathrm{J/(mol\cdot K)}$. Therefore $\gamma = \dfrac{29{,}1}{20{,}8} \approx 1{,}40$, which is the value for a diatomic gas. The value $0{,}714$ is the inverse ratio $\dfrac{c_V}{c_p}$, $3{,}50$ is the ratio $\dfrac{c_p}{R}$, and $1{,}67$ is the value for a monatomic gas.
