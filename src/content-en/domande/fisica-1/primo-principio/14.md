What is the temperature $T = 323.15\,\mathrm{K}$ in degrees Fahrenheit?

- $90.0\,^\circ\mathrm{F}$
- $59.8\,^\circ\mathrm{F}$
- $122\,^\circ\mathrm{F}$
- $614\,^\circ\mathrm{F}$

## Explanation

First one converts to degrees Celsius with $t_C = T - 273.15$, therefore $t_C = 50.00\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. Then one applies $t_F = \dfrac{9}{5}\,t_C + 32$, which gives $t_F = 90 + 32 = 122\,^\circ\mathrm{F}$. The value $90.0\,^\circ\mathrm{F}$ is obtained by forgetting the $32$-degree shift of the zero, and $614\,^\circ\mathrm{F}$ by applying the formula directly to the kelvins.
