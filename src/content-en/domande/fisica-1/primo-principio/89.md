A monatomic ideal gas undergoes adiabatic compression. Work received by the gas is positive, and use $R\simeq8\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$. Given the amount of gas $n = 2\,\mathrm{mol}$ and the temperature rise $T = 30\,\mathrm{K}$, what is the work received $W$?

- $480\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1440\,\mathrm{J}$
- $720\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1200\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

There is no heat transfer. Work received increases internal energy by $nC_V\Delta T$, with $C_V=3R/2$. In the stated units, the relation is $T = \dfrac{W}{12 \cdot n}$. Solving for the unknown gives $W = 12 \cdot T \cdot n$. Substituting the given values yields $720\,\mathrm{J}$.
