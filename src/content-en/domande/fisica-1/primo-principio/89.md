A gas returns to its initial state after three processes. Over the first two, its net heat input is $+80\,\mathrm J$ and it does $+110\,\mathrm J$ of work. During the third process its work is $-20\,\mathrm J$. How much heat must it absorb during the third process?

- $10\,\mathrm J$
- $30\,\mathrm J$
- $50\,\mathrm J$
- $-10\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Internal energy returns to its initial value in a cycle, so net heat equals net work. In joules, $80+Q_3=110-20=90$, hence $Q_3=10\,\mathrm J$.
