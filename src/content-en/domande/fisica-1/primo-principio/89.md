A gas goes quasi-statically, clockwise, around the cycle in the figure, which in the $(V, p)$ plane is an ellipse centered at the state ($3.0\,\mathrm{L}$; $2.0 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$), with horizontal semi-axis $1.0\,\mathrm{L}$ and vertical semi-axis $1.0 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$. What is the total work done by the gas?

::grafico{curve="3+cos(t),2+sin(t),0,6.29" vettori="2.9,3,3.1,3;3.1,1,2.9,1" punti="3,2,pieno" segmenti="2,0,2,2;4,0,4,2;0,1,3,1;0,3,3,3" xmin=0 xmax=5 ymin=0 ymax=4 nomi="V,p" legenda=0 titolo="Elliptical cycle with $V$ in liters and $p$ in $10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$"}

- $314\,\mathrm{J}$
- $400\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-314\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1.26 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The work of a quasi-static cycle is, in absolute value, the area enclosed by the curve of the cycle. The area of an ellipse with semi-axes $a$ and $b$ is $\pi a b$, so
$$
|L| = \pi \cdot 1.0 \cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^3} \cdot 1.0 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa} \approx 314\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
The cycle is traversed clockwise, hence the work is positive and equals $314\,\mathrm{J}$. The value $400\,\mathrm{J}$ is the area of the rectangle that contains the ellipse, and $1.26 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{J}$ is obtained by using the full axes instead of the semi-axes.
