A gas absorbs heat as it expands and does work. Heat entering the gas and work done by it are positive. Given the heat absorbed $Q = 750\,\mathrm{J}$ and the work done by the gas $W = 450\,\mathrm{J}$, what is the change in internal energy $U$?

- $600\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1200\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-300\,\mathrm{J}$
- $300\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Heat adds energy to the gas. Work done on the surroundings removes it, so $\Delta U=Q-W$. In the stated units, the relation is $U = Q - W$. Substituting the given values yields $300\,\mathrm{J}$.
