An ideal gas expands slowly at constant pressure. Work done by the gas is positive. Given the volume increase $V = 4\,\mathrm{L}$ and the work done by the gas $W = 600\,\mathrm{J}$, what is the pressure $p$?

- $150\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $225\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $300\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $75\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

On the pressure-volume diagram, work is the area of a rectangle of height $p$. One kilopascal-liter equals one joule. In the stated units, the relation is $W = V \cdot p$. Solving for the unknown gives $p = \dfrac{W}{V}$. Substituting the given values yields $150\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
