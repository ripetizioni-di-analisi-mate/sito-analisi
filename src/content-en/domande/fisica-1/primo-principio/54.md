A monatomic ideal gas in a rigid vessel is heated. Use $R\simeq 8\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$. The initial data are the amount of gas $n = 3\,\mathrm{mol}$ and the temperature increase $T = 25\,\mathrm{K}$. Change the temperature increase to $50\,\mathrm{K}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the ratio of the final value of $Q$ to its initial value?

- $1$
- $2$
- $4$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

At constant volume the gas does no work. The heat received is the change $nC_V\Delta T$, with $C_V=3R/2$. The relation $Q = 12 \cdot T \cdot n$ gives $900\,\mathrm{J}$ before the change and $1800\,\mathrm{J}$ after it. The ratio is $2$.
