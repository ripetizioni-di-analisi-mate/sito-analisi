An ideal gas is in equilibrium in a vessel. The amount of gas is $2\,\mathrm{mol}$ and use $R\simeq8\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$. Given the volume $V = 24\,\mathrm{L}$ and the gas pressure $p = 200\,\mathrm{kPa}$, what is the absolute temperature $T$?

- $150\,\mathrm{K}$
- $450\,\mathrm{K}$
- $300\,\mathrm{K}$
- $600\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

The equation of state gives $pV=nRT$. The pressure-volume product in kilopascal-liters has the same numerical value in joules. In the stated units, the relation is $p = \dfrac{16 \cdot T}{V}$. Solving for the unknown gives $T = \dfrac{V \cdot p}{16}$. Substituting the given values yields $300\,\mathrm{K}$.
