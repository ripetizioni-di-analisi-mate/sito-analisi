The figure shows four curves of the $(V, p)$ plane, in arbitrary units, passing through the same state $A$ of a diatomic ideal gas: an isobar, an isochore, an isotherm and a quasi-static adiabat. Which curve is the adiabat?

- Curve 1
- Curve 3
- Curve 4
- Curve 2

## Explanation

The isobar is the horizontal line, because $p$ is constant, and the isochore is the vertical line, because $V$ is constant; they are curves 1 and 4. The isotherm and the adiabat are both decreasing, with $pV$ constant on the first and $pV^{\gamma}$ constant on the second. Since $\gamma = 1.40 > 1$, along the adiabat the pressure decreases faster as the volume increases, so at state $A$ the adiabat is steeper than the isotherm. Therefore the adiabat is curve 2, which lies below the isotherm to the right of $A$.
