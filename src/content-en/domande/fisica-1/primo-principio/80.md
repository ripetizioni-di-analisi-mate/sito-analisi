An ideal gas expands reversibly at constant temperature to three times its initial volume. Use $R\simeq8\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$. Given the temperature $T = 300\,\mathrm{K}$ and the work done by the gas $W = 4800 \cdot \log{\left(3 \right)}\,\mathrm{J}$, what is the amount of gas $n$?

- $4\,\mathrm{mol}$
- $\log{\left(3 \right)}\,\mathrm{mol}$
- $\dfrac{2 \cdot \log{\left(3 \right)}}{3}\,\mathrm{mol}$
- $2\,\mathrm{mol}$

## Explanation

For an isothermal process $p=nRT/V$. Integrating the pressure gives $nRT\ln(V_f/V_i)$. In the stated units, the relation is $W = 8 \cdot T \cdot n \cdot \log{\left(3 \right)}$. Solving for the unknown gives $n = \dfrac{W}{8 \cdot T \cdot \log{\left(3 \right)}}$. Substituting the given values yields $2\,\mathrm{mol}$.
