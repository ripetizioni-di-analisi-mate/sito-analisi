A monatomic ideal gas undergoes adiabatic compression. Take work done on the gas as positive and assume $R\simeq8\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$. Given the work done on the gas $W = 720\,\mathrm{J}$ and the amount of gas $n = 2\,\mathrm{mol}$, what is the temperature rise $T$?

- $45\,\mathrm{K}$
- $60\,\mathrm{K}$
- $18\,\mathrm{K}$
- $30\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

There is no heat transfer. Work done on the gas increases its internal energy by $nC_V\Delta T$, with $C_V=3R/2$. In the stated units, the relation is $T = \dfrac{W}{12 \cdot n}$. Substituting the given values yields $30\,\mathrm{K}$.
