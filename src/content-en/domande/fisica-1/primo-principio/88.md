$1.0\,\mathrm{mol}$ of ideal gas goes quasi-statically around a Stirling cycle: an isothermal expansion at $600\,\mathrm{K}$ from volume $V$ to volume $2V$, an isochoric cooling down to $300\,\mathrm{K}$, an isothermal compression at $300\,\mathrm{K}$ from $2V$ to $V$ and an isochoric heating up to $600\,\mathrm{K}$. What is the total work of the cycle? ($R = 8.314\,\mathrm{J/(mol\cdot K)}$)

- $3.46\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $2.49\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $5.19\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $1.73\,\mathrm{kJ}$

## Explanation

The two isochores have zero work, so the work of the cycle is the sum of the works of the two isotherms. The expansion at $600\,\mathrm{K}$ does $L_1 = nR \cdot 600\,\mathrm{K} \cdot \ln 2$, and the compression at $300\,\mathrm{K}$ does $L_2 = nR \cdot 300\,\mathrm{K} \cdot \ln\dfrac{1}{2} = -nR \cdot 300\,\mathrm{K} \cdot \ln 2$. Therefore
$$
L = nR\,(600 - 300)\,\mathrm{K} \cdot \ln 2 = 1.0 \cdot 8.314 \cdot 300 \cdot 0.693\,\mathrm{J} \approx 1.73\,\mathrm{kJ} .
$$
The value $3.46\,\mathrm{kJ}$ is the work of the expansion alone, and $5.19\,\mathrm{kJ}$ is obtained by adding the absolute values of the two works.
