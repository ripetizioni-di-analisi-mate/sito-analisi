A system follows a clockwise rectangular cycle on a pressure-volume diagram. Volume is on the horizontal axis. Given the volume difference between the isochores $V = 5\,\mathrm{L}$ and the net work per cycle $W = 300\,\mathrm{J}$, what is the pressure difference between the isobars $p$?

- $60\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $90\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $120\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $30\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

The constant-volume legs do no work. The difference between the isobaric work terms equals the enclosed area, positive for a clockwise cycle. In the stated units, the relation is $W = V \cdot p$. Solving for the unknown gives $p = \dfrac{W}{V}$. Substituting the given values yields $60\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
