What volume do $2{,}00\,\mathrm{mol}$ of ideal gas occupy at a temperature of $27{,}0\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ and a pressure of $1{,}00 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$?

- $4{,}49\,\mathrm{L}$
- $49{,}9\,\mathrm{L}$
- $25{,}0\,\mathrm{L}$
- $0{,}0499\,\mathrm{L}$

## Explanation

From the **equation of state** $pV = nRT$, with the temperature in kelvins $T = 300{,}15\,\mathrm{K}$, one obtains
$$
V = \frac{nRT}{p} = \frac{2{,}00 \cdot 8{,}314 \cdot 300{,}15}{1{,}00 \cdot 10^5}\,\mathrm{m^3} \approx 4{,}99 \cdot 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m^3} .
$$
Since $1\,\mathrm{m^3} = 1000\,\mathrm{L}$, the volume is $49{,}9\,\mathrm{L}$. The value $4{,}49\,\mathrm{L}$ is obtained with the temperature in degrees Celsius, and $0{,}0499\,\mathrm{L}$ by reading in liters the result expressed in cubic meters.
