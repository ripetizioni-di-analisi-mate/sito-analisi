A monatomic ideal gas in a rigid vessel is heated. Use $R\simeq 8\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$. Given the temperature increase $T = 25\,\mathrm{K}$ and the heat absorbed $Q = 900\,\mathrm{J}$, what is the amount of gas $n$?

- $\dfrac{9}{2}\,\mathrm{mol}$
- $6\,\mathrm{mol}$
- $\dfrac{9}{5}\,\mathrm{mol}$
- $3\,\mathrm{mol}$

## Explanation

At constant volume the gas does no work. The heat received is the change $nC_V\Delta T$, with $C_V=3R/2$. In the stated units, the relation is $Q = 12 \cdot T \cdot n$. Solving for the unknown gives $n = \dfrac{Q}{12 \cdot T}$. Substituting the given values yields $3\,\mathrm{mol}$.
