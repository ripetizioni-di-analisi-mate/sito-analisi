A gas absorbs heat as it expands and does work. Heat entering the gas and work done by it are positive. The initial data are the heat absorbed $Q = 750\,\mathrm{J}$ and the work done by the gas $W = 450\,\mathrm{J}$. Change the work done by the gas to $900\,\mathrm{J}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the ratio of the final value of $U$ to its initial value?

- $- \dfrac{1}{2}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{11}{8}$
- $-2$

## Explanation

Heat adds energy to the gas. Work done on the surroundings removes it, so $\Delta U=Q-W$. The relation $U = Q - W$ gives $300\,\mathrm{J}$ before the change and $-150\,\mathrm{J}$ after it. The ratio is $- \dfrac{1}{2}$.
