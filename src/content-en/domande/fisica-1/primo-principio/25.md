A body of constant emissivity is heated from $27\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ to $327\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. By what factor, to a good approximation, is the power it emits by radiation multiplied?

- $2$
- $4$
- $2.2 \cdot 10^4$
- $16$

## Explanation

By the **Stefan-Boltzmann law** $P = e\,\sigma\,A\,T^4$, with $T$ the absolute temperature. In kelvins the body goes from about $300\,\mathrm{K}$ to about $600\,\mathrm{K}$, therefore the absolute temperature doubles. Hence the emitted power is multiplied by $2^4 = 16$. The value $2.2 \cdot 10^4$ is obtained by using the ratio of the temperatures in degrees Celsius, $\left( \dfrac{327}{27} \right)^4$.
