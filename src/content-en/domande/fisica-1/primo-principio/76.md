An ideal gas is in equilibrium in a vessel. The amount of gas is $2\,\mathrm{mol}$ and use $R\simeq8\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$. Given the absolute temperature $T = 300\,\mathrm{K}$ and the volume $V = 24\,\mathrm{L}$, what is the gas pressure $p$?

- $400\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $300\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $100\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $200\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

The equation of state gives $pV=nRT$. The pressure-volume product in kilopascal-liters has the same numerical value in joules. In the stated units, the relation is $p = \dfrac{16 \cdot T}{V}$. Substituting the given values yields $200\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
