An ideal gas is in equilibrium in a vessel. The amount of gas is $2\,\mathrm{mol}$ and use $R\simeq8\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$. Given the absolute temperature $T = 300\,\mathrm{K}$ and the gas pressure $p = 200\,\mathrm{kPa}$, what is the volume $V$?

- $48\,\mathrm{L}$
- $24\,\mathrm{L}$
- $36\,\mathrm{L}$
- $12\,\mathrm{L}$

## Explanation

The equation of state gives $pV=nRT$. The pressure-volume product in kilopascal-liters has the same numerical value in joules. In the stated units, the relation is $p = \dfrac{16 \cdot T}{V}$. Solving for the unknown gives $V = \dfrac{16 \cdot T}{p}$. Substituting the given values yields $24\,\mathrm{L}$.
