A vessel contains a diatomic ideal gas in a regime where vibrational modes are negligible. It is heated at constant pressure. Use $R\simeq8\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$. Given the temperature rise $T = 40\,\mathrm{K}$ and the heat absorbed $Q = 2240\,\mathrm{J}$, what is the amount of gas $n$?

- $\dfrac{14}{5}\,\mathrm{mol}$
- $2\,\mathrm{mol}$
- $7\,\mathrm{mol}$
- $4\,\mathrm{mol}$

## Explanation

For heating at constant pressure, use $C_P=C_V+R$. With five active degrees of freedom, $C_V=5R/2$ and $C_P=7R/2$. In the stated units, the relation is $Q = 28 \cdot T \cdot n$. Solving for the unknown gives $n = \dfrac{Q}{28 \cdot T}$. Substituting the given values yields $2\,\mathrm{mol}$.
