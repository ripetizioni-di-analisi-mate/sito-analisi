A monatomic ideal gas in a rigid vessel is heated. Use $R\simeq 8\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$. Given the amount of gas $n = 3\,\mathrm{mol}$ and the temperature increase $T = 25\,\mathrm{K}$, what is the heat absorbed $Q$?

- $900\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1500\,\mathrm{J}$
- $600\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1800\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

At constant volume the gas does no work. The heat received is the change $nC_V\Delta T$, with $C_V=3R/2$. In the stated units, the relation is $Q = 12 \cdot T \cdot n$. Substituting the given values yields $900\,\mathrm{J}$.
