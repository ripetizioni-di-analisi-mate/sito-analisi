A gas absorbs heat as it expands and does work. Heat entering the gas and work done by it are positive. Given the heat absorbed $Q = 750\,\mathrm{J}$ and the change in internal energy $U = 300\,\mathrm{J}$, what is the work done by the gas $W$?

- $225\,\mathrm{J}$
- $450\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1050\,\mathrm{J}$
- $900\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Heat adds energy to the gas. Work done on the surroundings removes it, so $\Delta U=Q-W$. In the stated units, the relation is $U = Q - W$. Solving for the unknown gives $W = Q - U$. Substituting the given values yields $450\,\mathrm{J}$.
