## Explanation

The extensions in meters are $x_A = 0{,}040\,\mathrm{m}$ and $x_B = 0{,}12\,\mathrm{m}$. The **work done by the spring force** is
$$
L = \frac{1}{2} k\,(x_A^2 - x_B^2) = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 250 \cdot (0{,}0016 - 0{,}0144)\,\mathrm{J} = -1{,}6\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
The work is negative, because during the stretching the elastic force is opposite to the displacement. The result depends on the difference between the squares of the extensions, which is different from the square of the difference $(x_B - x_A)^2$.
