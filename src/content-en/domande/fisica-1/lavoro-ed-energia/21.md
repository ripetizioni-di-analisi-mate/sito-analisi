A crane lifts a load of $500\,\mathrm{kg}$ at constant velocity through $12\,\mathrm{m}$ in $20\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the average power delivered by the crane to the load?

- $3{,}0 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{W}$
- $1{,}2 \cdot 10^6\,\mathrm{W}$
- $2{,}9 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{W}$
- $5{,}9 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

The velocity is constant, so the force of the crane balances the weight of the load and equals $mg$. The work of the crane is $L = mgh = 500 \cdot 9{,}81 \cdot 12\,\mathrm{J} = 58\,860\,\mathrm{J}$. The average power is the ratio of the work to the time taken, therefore
$$
P_m = \frac{L}{\Delta t} = \frac{58\,860}{20}\,\mathrm{W} \approx 2{,}9 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{W} .
$$
