Constant power is delivered to a cart whose initial kinetic energy is $18\,\mathrm{J}$. No other forces do work. Given the elapsed time $t = 4\,\mathrm{s}$ and the final kinetic energy $K = 66\,\mathrm{J}$, what is the power $P$?

- $12\,\mathrm{W}$
- $21\,\mathrm{W}$
- $24\,\mathrm{W}$
- $\dfrac{33}{2}\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

The driving force does work $Pt$. The cart has an initial kinetic energy of $18\,\mathrm{J}$. In the stated units, the relation is $K = P \cdot t + 18$. Solving for the unknown gives $P = \dfrac{K - 18}{t}$. Substituting the given values yields $12\,\mathrm{W}$.
