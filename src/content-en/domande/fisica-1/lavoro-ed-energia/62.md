The force along a track is $F(x)=kx$. A cart moves from the origin to $d>0$. Given the distance $d = 4\,\mathrm{m}$ and the work $W = 48\,\mathrm{J}$, what is the force coefficient $k$?

- $3\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N/m}$

## Explanation

Work is the integral of $kx$ from zero to $d$, equal to the area of the triangle under the graph. In the stated units, the relation is $W = \dfrac{d^{2} \cdot k}{2}$. Solving for the unknown gives $k = \dfrac{2 \cdot W}{d^{2}}$. Substituting the given values yields $6\,\mathrm{N/m}$.
