## Explanation

The drop in height is $h = d\sin\alpha = 1{,}0\,\mathrm{m}$ and the normal force is $mg\cos\alpha$, so friction does the work $-\mu_d\,mg\cos\alpha\,d$. By the theorem on the **work of nonconservative forces**, with zero height at the base,
$$
-\mu_d\,mg\cos\alpha\,d = \frac{1}{2} m v^2 - mg\,d\sin\alpha ,
$$
therefore
$$
v = \sqrt{2gd\,(\sin\alpha - \mu_d\cos\alpha)} = \sqrt{2 \cdot 9{,}81 \cdot 2{,}0 \cdot (0{,}50 - 0{,}26)}\,\mathrm{m/s} \approx 3{,}1\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The result does not depend on the mass, because the mass cancels out.
