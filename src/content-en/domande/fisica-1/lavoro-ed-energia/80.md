A crate slides along a rough horizontal track until it stops. Kinetic friction of constant magnitude is the only force that does work. Given the force magnitude $F = 9\,\mathrm{N}$, the mass $m = 5\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the stopping distance $d = 10\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the initial speed $u$?

- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3 \cdot \sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6 \cdot \sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The work of friction is $-Fd$ and the change in kinetic energy is $-\frac12 mu^2$. In the stated units, the relation is $d = \dfrac{m \cdot u^{2}}{2 \cdot F}$. Solving for the unknown gives $u = \dfrac{\sqrt{2} \cdot \sqrt{F} \cdot \sqrt{d}}{\sqrt{m}}$. Substituting the given values yields $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
