The motor of a freight elevator has efficiency $0.75$ and lifts a cab of total mass $600\,\mathrm{kg}$ at the constant speed of $1.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What power does the motor take in?

- $8.8\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $6.6\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kW}$

## Explanation

The speed is constant, so the force lifting the cab balances the weight and equals $mg$. By the relation between **power and velocity** the useful power is $P_{\text{u}} = mg\,v = 600 \cdot 9.81 \cdot 1.5\,\mathrm{W} \approx 8.8\,\mathrm{kW}$. The efficiency is the ratio of the useful power to the input power, therefore
$$
P_{\text{in}} = \frac{P_{\text{u}}}{\eta} \approx \frac{8.8}{0.75}\,\mathrm{kW} \approx 12\,\mathrm{kW} .
$$
The input power is greater than the useful power, because $\eta < 1$.
