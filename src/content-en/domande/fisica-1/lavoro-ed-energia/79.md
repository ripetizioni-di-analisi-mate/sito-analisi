A drone of mass $1.2\,\mathrm{kg}$ flies at a height of $15\,\mathrm{m}$ above the ground with a speed of $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$. With zero height at ground level, what is its mechanical energy?

- $1.8 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1.0 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{J}$
- $3.5 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{J}$
- $2.6 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The **mechanical energy** is the sum of the kinetic energy and the potential energy of the weight.

The potential energy is $U = m g h = 1.2 \cdot 9.81 \cdot 15\,\mathrm{J} \approx 177\,\mathrm{J}$ and the kinetic energy is $K = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 1.2 \cdot 12^2\,\mathrm{J} \approx 86\,\mathrm{J}$. Therefore
$$
E = K + U \approx (86 + 177)\,\mathrm{J} \approx 2.6 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
The value $1.8 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{J}$ is the potential energy alone.
