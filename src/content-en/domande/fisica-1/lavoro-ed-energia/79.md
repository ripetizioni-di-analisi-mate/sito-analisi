A crate slides along a rough horizontal track until it stops. Kinetic friction of constant magnitude is the only force that does work. Given the initial speed $u = 6\,\mathrm{m/s}$, the force magnitude $F = 9\,\mathrm{N}$ and the mass $m = 5\,\mathrm{kg}$, what is the stopping distance $d$?

- $10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The work of friction is $-Fd$ and the change in kinetic energy is $-\frac12 mu^2$. In the stated units, the relation is $d = \dfrac{m \cdot u^{2}}{2 \cdot F}$. Substituting the given values yields $10\,\mathrm{m}$.
