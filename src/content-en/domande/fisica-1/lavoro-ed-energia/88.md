A force along $x$ rises linearly with positive displacement from $4\,\mathrm{N}$ to a larger value $F$. Given the final force $F = 12\,\mathrm{N}$ and the distance $d = 5\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the work $W$?

- $20\,\mathrm{J}$
- $80\,\mathrm{J}$
- $60\,\mathrm{J}$
- $40\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Work is the area of the trapezoid under the force-position graph. Its average height is the mean of the endpoint values. In the stated units, the relation is $W = \dfrac{d \cdot \left(F + 4\right)}{2}$. Substituting the given values yields $40\,\mathrm{J}$.
