A winch lifts a load at constant speed. Its mechanical efficiency is $0.8$, and take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The initial data are the mass $m = 80\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the speed $v = \dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Change the speed to $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the ratio of the final value of $P$ to its initial value?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $1$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

The useful power is $mgv$. Divide by efficiency to find the input power. The relation $P = \dfrac{25 \cdot m \cdot v}{2}$ gives $500\,\mathrm{W}$ before the change and $1000\,\mathrm{W}$ after it. The ratio is $2$.
