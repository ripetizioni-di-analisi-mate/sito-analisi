A winch lifts a load at constant speed. Its mechanical efficiency is $0.8$, and take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Given the mass $m = 80\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the speed $v = \dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the input power $P$?

- $1000\,\mathrm{W}$
- $500\,\mathrm{W}$
- $400\,\mathrm{W}$
- $320\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

The useful power is $mgv$. Divide by efficiency to find the input power. In the stated units, the relation is $P = \dfrac{25 \cdot m \cdot v}{2}$. Substituting the given values yields $500\,\mathrm{W}$.
