A particle in one dimension has potential energy $U(x)=cx^2$. Consider a point with $x>0$. Given the potential energy coefficient $c = 3\,\mathrm{J/m^2}$ and the coordinate of the point $x = 4\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the conservative force magnitude $F$?

- $36\,\mathrm{N}$
- $24\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$
- $48\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The force is $F_x=-dU/dx=-2cx$. The negative sign indicates a force toward the origin. In the stated units, the relation is $F = 2 \cdot c \cdot x$. Substituting the given values yields $24\,\mathrm{N}$.
