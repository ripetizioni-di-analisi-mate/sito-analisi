A small ball is released from rest at point $A$ of a smooth track, whose profile is drawn in the figure, and travels along it to point $D$. Air resistance is negligible. At which point does the ball have the greatest speed?

::grafico{assi=0 f="2.9+1.7*cos(x)-0.2*x" linee="-0.05,0 6.6,0" punti="0,4.6,pieno,A;1.6,2.53,pieno,B;3.26,0.56,pieno,C;5.5,3.0,pieno,D" xmin=-0.05 xmax=6.7 ymin=-0.4 ymax=5.2 uguale=1 legenda=0 titolo="Profile of the smooth track, above the horizontal ground"}

- At point $C$
- At point $B$
- At point $D$
- The speed has the same magnitude at all points

## Explanation

The track is smooth, so the normal force does no work and mechanical energy is conserved: $\dfrac{1}{2} m v^2 + m g y = m g y_A$.

Hence $v^2 = 2g\,(y_A - y)$, and the speed is greater the lower the ball is. The lowest of the marked points is $C$, so at $C$ the ball has the **greatest speed**. Point $D$ is lower than $A$, so the ball reaches it with nonzero speed.
