A simple pendulum has an inextensible string of negligible mass and length $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$. The ball is released from rest with the string forming with the vertical an angle $\theta_0$ such that $\cos\theta_0 = 0.80$. With what speed does it pass through the lowest point?

- $6.3\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $7.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3.1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.2\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The tension in the string is perpendicular to the velocity and does no work, so mechanical energy is conserved. In the initial position the ball is higher than the lowest point by $h = \ell\,(1 - \cos\theta_0) = 2.5 \cdot 0.20\,\mathrm{m} = 0.50\,\mathrm{m}$. Therefore $mgh = \dfrac{1}{2} m v^2$ and
$$
v = \sqrt{2gh} = \sqrt{2 \cdot 9.81 \cdot 0.50}\,\mathrm{m/s} \approx 3.1\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The length $\ell\cos\theta_0 = 2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ is the vertical distance between the suspension point and the ball in the initial position.
