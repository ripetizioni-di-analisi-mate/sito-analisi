A crate slides down a rough inclined plane at constant velocity. Which statement about the work done by the forces acting on the crate is true?

- The total work is positive, because the weight does positive work
- The work of the weight is positive and the total work is zero
- The work of friction is zero, because the velocity is constant
- The normal force does negative work, because it opposes the weight

## Explanation

The forces acting on the crate are the weight, the normal force and kinetic friction. The velocity is constant, so the kinetic energy does not change and by the **work-energy theorem** the total work is zero. The weight has a component in the same direction as the downward displacement, therefore its work is positive. Friction is opposite to the displacement and does negative work, equal in absolute value to the work of the weight. The normal force is perpendicular to the plane, hence to the displacement, and its work is zero.
