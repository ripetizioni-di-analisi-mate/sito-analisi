A particle moves along the $x$-axis subject only to a conservative force with potential energy $U(x) = x^2 - 4x + 7$, where $x$ is in meters and $U$ in joules, and has mechanical energy $E = 10\,\mathrm{J}$. What is the maximum kinetic energy of the particle?

- $7\,\mathrm{J}$
- $10\,\mathrm{J}$
- $3\,\mathrm{J}$
- $13\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

By the conservation of mechanical energy $K = E - U(x)$, so the kinetic energy is maximum where $U$ is minimum. The derivative $U'(x) = 2x - 4$ vanishes at $x = 2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $U''(x) = 2 > 0$, therefore the minimum of $U$ is $U(2) = (4 - 8 + 7)\,\mathrm{J} = 3\,\mathrm{J}$. It follows that $K_{\max} = E - U(2) = 10\,\mathrm{J} - 3\,\mathrm{J} = 7\,\mathrm{J}$.
