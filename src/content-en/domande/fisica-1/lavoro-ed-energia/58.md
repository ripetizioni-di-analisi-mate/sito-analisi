A block compresses an ideal spring on a frictionless surface and momentarily stops at maximum compression. Given the speed $v = 3\,\mathrm{m/s}$, the mass $m = 4\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the maximum compression $x = \dfrac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the spring constant $k$?

- $150\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $100\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $200\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $50\,\mathrm{N/m}$

## Explanation

By energy conservation, the initial kinetic energy $\frac12 mv^2$ becomes elastic energy $\frac12 kx^2$. In the stated units, the relation is $x = \dfrac{\sqrt{m} \cdot v}{\sqrt{k}}$. Solving for the unknown gives $k = \dfrac{m \cdot v^{2}}{x^{2}}$. Substituting the given values yields $100\,\mathrm{N/m}$.
