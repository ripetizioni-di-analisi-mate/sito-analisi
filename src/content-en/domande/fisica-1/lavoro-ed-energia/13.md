A particle moves along the $x$-axis subject only to a conservative force with potential energy $U(x) = 3x^2 - x^3$, where $x$ is in meters and $U$ in joules. What are the equilibrium positions, and what type of equilibrium is each one?

- $x = 0$ stable and $x = 2\,\mathrm{m}$ unstable
- $x = 0$ unstable and $x = 2\,\mathrm{m}$ stable
- $x = 0$ stable and $x = 3\,\mathrm{m}$ unstable
- $x = 0$ and $x = 2\,\mathrm{m}$, both unstable

## Explanation

The equilibrium positions are the points where the force $F_x = -U'(x)$ is zero. Since $U'(x) = 6x - 3x^2 = 3x\,(2 - x)$, equilibrium occurs at $x = 0$ and at $x = 2\,\mathrm{m}$. The second derivative is $U''(x) = 6 - 6x$. Since $U''(0) = 6 > 0$, the position $x = 0$ is a minimum of $U$ and the equilibrium is **stable**; since $U''(2) = -6 < 0$, the position $x = 2\,\mathrm{m}$ is a maximum of $U$ and the equilibrium is **unstable**. The point $x = 3\,\mathrm{m}$ is a zero of $U$, where the force is $F_x = -U'(3) = 9\,\mathrm{N}$.
