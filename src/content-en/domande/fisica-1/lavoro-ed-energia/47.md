A winch lifts a load at constant speed. Its mechanical efficiency is $0{,}8$. Take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Given the speed $v = \dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the input power $P = 500\,\mathrm{W}$, what is the mass $m$?

- $160\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $100\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $80\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $125\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The useful power is $mgv$. The input power is obtained by dividing by the efficiency. In the stated units, the relation is $P = \dfrac{25 \cdot m \cdot v}{2}$. Solving for the unknown gives $m = \dfrac{2 \cdot P}{25 \cdot v}$. Substituting the given values yields $80\,\mathrm{kg}$.
