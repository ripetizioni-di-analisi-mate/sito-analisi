## Explanation

The initial speed in SI units is $v_0 = \dfrac{90}{3{,}6}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 25\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The weight and the normal force are perpendicular to the displacement, so the only force that does work is kinetic friction, of magnitude $\mu_d\,mg$ and opposite to the displacement. Since the final speed is zero, the **work-energy theorem** gives $-\mu_d\,mg\,d = -\dfrac{1}{2} m v_0^2$. Therefore
$$
d = \frac{v_0^2}{2\mu_d\,g} = \frac{625}{2 \cdot 0{,}70 \cdot 9{,}81}\,\mathrm{m} \approx 46\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
