A constant power is delivered to a moving cart whose initial kinetic energy is $18\,\mathrm{J}$. No other forces do work. The initial data are the power $P = 12\,\mathrm{W}$ and the elapsed time $t = 4\,\mathrm{s}$. Change the elapsed time to $7\,\mathrm{s}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the ratio of the final value of $K$ to its initial value?

- $\dfrac{11}{17}$
- $\dfrac{7}{4}$
- $\dfrac{17}{11}$
- $\dfrac{11}{5}$

## Explanation

The driving force does work $Pt$. The cart has an initial kinetic energy of $18\,\mathrm{J}$. The relation $K = P \cdot t + 18$ gives $66\,\mathrm{J}$ before the change and $102\,\mathrm{J}$ after it. The ratio is $\dfrac{17}{11}$.
