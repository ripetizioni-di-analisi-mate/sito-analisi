The force along a track is $F(x)=kx$. A cart moves from the origin to $d>0$. Given the force coefficient $k = 6\,\mathrm{N/m}$ and the work $W = 48\,\mathrm{J}$, what is the distance $d$?

- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2 \cdot \sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2 \cdot \sqrt{6}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Work is the integral of $kx$ from zero to $d$, equal to the area of the triangle under the graph. In the stated units, the relation is $W = \dfrac{d^{2} \cdot k}{2}$. Solving for the unknown gives $d = \dfrac{\sqrt{2} \cdot \sqrt{W}}{\sqrt{k}}$. Substituting the given values yields $4\,\mathrm{m}$.
