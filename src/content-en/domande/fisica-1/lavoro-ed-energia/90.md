A force along $x$ rises linearly with positive displacement from $4\,\mathrm{N}$ to a larger value $F$. Given the final force $F = 12\,\mathrm{N}$ and the work $W = 40\,\mathrm{J}$, what is the distance $d$?

- $10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{5}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{10}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Work is the area of the trapezoid under the force-position graph. Its average height is the mean of the endpoint values. In the stated units, the relation is $W = \dfrac{d \cdot \left(F + 4\right)}{2}$. Solving for the unknown gives $d = \dfrac{2 \cdot W}{F + 4}$. Substituting the given values yields $5\,\mathrm{m}$.
