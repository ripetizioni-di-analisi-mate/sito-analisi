Two surfaces slide past each other against a constant kinetic friction force of $6\,\mathrm N$. A marker on the upper surface advances $5\,\mathrm m$ in the laboratory while the lower surface advances $2\,\mathrm m$ in the same direction. How much mechanical energy is converted into internal energy at the contact?

- $18\,\mathrm J$
- $30\,\mathrm J$
- $12\,\mathrm J$
- $42\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The relative sliding distance is $5-2=3\,\mathrm m$. The net work by the two opposite friction forces is $-6\cdot3=-18\,\mathrm J$, so $18\,\mathrm J$ is dissipated.
