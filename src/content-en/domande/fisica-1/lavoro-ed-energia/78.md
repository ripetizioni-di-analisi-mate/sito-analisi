A force pulls a sled at constant speed on a horizontal surface. Its horizontal component is $4/5$ of its magnitude and points along the motion. Given the force magnitude $F = 30\,\mathrm{N}$ and the power delivered by the applied force $P = 48\,\mathrm{W}$, what is the speed $v$?

- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac{8}{5}\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Power is $\vec F\cdot\vec v$. Since the velocity is horizontal, only the horizontal component of force contributes. In the stated units, the relation is $P = \dfrac{4 \cdot F \cdot v}{5}$. Solving for the unknown gives $v = \dfrac{5 \cdot P}{4 \cdot F}$. Substituting the given values yields $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
