What is the maximum elongation of an ideal vertical spring with spring constant $k = 98\,\mathrm{N/m}$, fixed at the top, if a body of mass $0.50\,\mathrm{kg}$ is hung from its lower end and released from rest when the spring has its natural length? Air resistance is negligible.

- $10\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $5.0\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $20\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The body is acted on by the weight and the elastic force, which are conservative, so the mechanical energy $E = K + mgy + \dfrac{1}{2} k x^2$ is conserved.

Take zero height at the initial position, where the spring is unstretched and the body is at rest, so $E = 0$. At the maximum elongation $x$ the body is again at rest and is at height $y = -x$, therefore
$$
0 = -mg\,x + \frac{1}{2} k x^2
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
x = \frac{2mg}{k} = \frac{2 \cdot 0.50 \cdot 9.81}{98}\,\mathrm{m} \approx 0.10\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $5.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ is the elongation at the equilibrium position, $\dfrac{mg}{k}$, where the body passes with maximum speed.
