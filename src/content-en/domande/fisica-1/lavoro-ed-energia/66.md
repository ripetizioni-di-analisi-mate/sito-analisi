A constant power is delivered to a moving cart whose initial kinetic energy is $18\,\mathrm{J}$. No other forces do work. Given the power $P = 12\,\mathrm{W}$ and the elapsed time $t = 4\,\mathrm{s}$, what is the final kinetic energy $K$?

- $30\,\mathrm{J}$
- $66\,\mathrm{J}$
- $132\,\mathrm{J}$
- $48\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The driving force does work $Pt$. The cart has an initial kinetic energy of $18\,\mathrm{J}$. In the stated units, the relation is $K = P \cdot t + 18$. Substituting the given values yields $66\,\mathrm{J}$.
