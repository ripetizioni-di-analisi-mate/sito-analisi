## Explanation

The speed is constant and friction is negligible, so the force of the cyclist along the road balances the component of the weight parallel to the road, $mg\sin\alpha$. Since $\sin 6.0^\circ \approx 0.105$, this component is $mg\sin\alpha \approx 80 \cdot 9.81 \cdot 0.105\,\mathrm{N} \approx 82\,\mathrm{N}$. By the relation between **power and velocity**, with force and velocity parallel,
$$
P = mg\sin\alpha\,v \approx 82 \cdot 4.0\,\mathrm{W} \approx 3.3 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{W} .
$$
