## Explanation

Air resistance is negligible and only the weight acts on the ball, so mechanical energy is conserved. With zero height at the launch point, at the highest point the speed is zero and $\dfrac{1}{2} m v_0^2 = mg\,\Delta h$, that is,
$$
\Delta h = \frac{v_0^2}{2g} = \frac{144}{2 \cdot 9{,}81}\,\mathrm{m} \approx 7{,}3\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
To find the height above the ground, add the launch height. This gives $h_{\max} \approx 7{,}3\,\mathrm{m} + 1{,}5\,\mathrm{m} = 8{,}8\,\mathrm{m}$.
