A force pulls a sled at constant speed on a horizontal surface. Its horizontal component is $4/5$ of its magnitude and points along the motion. Given the force magnitude $F = 30\,\mathrm{N}$ and the speed $v = 2\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the power delivered by the applied force $P$?

- $12\,\mathrm{W}$
- $96\,\mathrm{W}$
- $60\,\mathrm{W}$
- $48\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

Power is $\vec F\cdot\vec v$. Since the velocity is horizontal, only the horizontal component of force contributes. In the stated units, the relation is $P = \dfrac{4 \cdot F \cdot v}{5}$. Substituting the given values yields $48\,\mathrm{W}$.
