A car of mass $1000\,\mathrm{kg}$ accelerates on a horizontal road. At the instant its speed is $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$ the driving force points along the motion and delivers a power of $50\,\mathrm{kW}$, while the resistive forces have a resultant of magnitude $600\,\mathrm{N}$. What is the acceleration of the car at that instant?

- $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3.1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0.60\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $1.9\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The driving force is parallel to the velocity, so by the relation between power and velocity $P = F v$, and at that instant
$$
F = \frac{P}{v} = \frac{5.0 \cdot 10^4}{20}\,\mathrm{N} = 2.5 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
The resistive forces oppose the motion, so the horizontal net force is $(2500 - 600)\,\mathrm{N} = 1900\,\mathrm{N}$. By Newton's second law the acceleration is $a = \dfrac{1900}{1000}\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 1.9\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

The value $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ is obtained by neglecting the resistive forces.
