A block slides from rest to the bottom of a ramp. Friction of constant magnitude $12\,\mathrm{N}$ opposes motion over $3\,\mathrm{m}$. Take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Given the mass $m = 6\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the height difference $h = 2\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the final kinetic energy $K$?

- $84\,\mathrm{J}$
- $120\,\mathrm{J}$
- $156\,\mathrm{J}$
- $168\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Gravity does work $mgh$ and friction does $-36\,\mathrm{J}$ of work. The initial kinetic energy is zero. In the stated units, the relation is $K = 10 \cdot h \cdot m - 36$. Substituting the given values yields $84\,\mathrm{J}$.
