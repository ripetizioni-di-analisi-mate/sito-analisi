The force along a track is $F(x)=kx$. A cart moves from the origin to $d>0$. The initial data are the force coefficient $k = 6\,\mathrm{N/m}$ and the distance $d = 4\,\mathrm{m}$. Change the force coefficient to $9\,\mathrm{N/m}$ and the distance to $2\,\mathrm{m}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the final value of $W$ minus its initial value?

- $-20\,\mathrm{J}$
- $30\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-30\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-60\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Work is the integral of $kx$ from zero to $d$, equal to the area of the triangle under the graph. The relation $W = \dfrac{d^{2} \cdot k}{2}$ gives $48\,\mathrm{J}$ before the change and $18\,\mathrm{J}$ after it. Subtracting the initial value from the final value gives $-30\,\mathrm{J}$.
