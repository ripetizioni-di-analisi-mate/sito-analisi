A block is launched with speed $v_0$ up a rough inclined plane; it stops, slides back down and passes again through the launch point with speed $v$. Which statement is true?

- $v < v_0$, because friction does negative work both on the way up and on the way down
- $v = v_0$, because the work of the weight over the round trip is zero
- $v > v_0$, because on the way down the weight does positive work
- $v = v_0$, because the works of friction on the way up and on the way down have opposite signs

## Explanation

The block returns to its starting point, so the work of the weight over the whole path is zero, and the normal force does no work. Kinetic friction is opposite to the velocity, therefore it points down the incline on the way up and up the incline on the way down, and on both legs it does negative work. By the **work-energy theorem** $\dfrac{1}{2} m v^2 - \dfrac{1}{2} m v_0^2 = L_{\text{fr}} < 0$, so $v < v_0$.
