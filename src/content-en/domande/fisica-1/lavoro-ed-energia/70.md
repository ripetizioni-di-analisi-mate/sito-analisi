A constant power is delivered to a moving cart whose initial kinetic energy is $18\,\mathrm{J}$. No other forces do work. The initial data are the power $P = 12\,\mathrm{W}$ and the elapsed time $t = 4\,\mathrm{s}$. Change the power to $18\,\mathrm{W}$ and the elapsed time to $3\,\mathrm{s}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the final value of $K$ minus its initial value?

- $66\,\mathrm{J}$
- $6\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{J}$
- $72\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The driving force does work $Pt$. The cart has an initial kinetic energy of $18\,\mathrm{J}$. The relation $K = P \cdot t + 18$ gives $66\,\mathrm{J}$ before the change and $72\,\mathrm{J}$ after it. Subtracting the initial value from the final value gives $6\,\mathrm{J}$.
