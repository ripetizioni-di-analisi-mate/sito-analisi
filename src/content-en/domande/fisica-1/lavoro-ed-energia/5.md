## Explanation

The mass in kilograms is $m = 0.800\,\mathrm{kg}$. By the **work-energy theorem** the total work is the change in kinetic energy,
$$
L_{\text{tot}} = \frac{1}{2} m\,(v_B^2 - v_A^2) = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 0.800 \cdot (36.0 - 4.0)\,\mathrm{J} = 12.8\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
Kinetic energy depends on the square of the speed, so the change contains the difference of the squares $v_B^2 - v_A^2 = 32\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}$, which is different from $(v_B - v_A)^2 = 16\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}$.
