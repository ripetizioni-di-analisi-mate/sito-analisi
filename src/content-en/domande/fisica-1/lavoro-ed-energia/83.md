A particle in one dimension has potential energy $U(x)=cx^2$. Consider a point with $x>0$. Given the coordinate of the point $x = 4\,\mathrm{m}$ and the conservative force magnitude $F = 24\,\mathrm{N}$, what is the potential energy coefficient $c$?

- $3\,\mathrm{J/m^2}$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}\,\mathrm{J/m^2}$
- $6\,\mathrm{J/m^2}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{J/m^2}$

## Explanation

The force is $F_x=-dU/dx=-2cx$. The negative sign indicates a force toward the origin. In the stated units, the relation is $F = 2 \cdot c \cdot x$. Solving for the unknown gives $c = \dfrac{F}{2 \cdot x}$. Substituting the given values yields $3\,\mathrm{J/m^2}$.
