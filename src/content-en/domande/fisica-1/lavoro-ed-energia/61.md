The force along a track is $F(x)=kx$. A cart moves from the origin to $d>0$. Given the force coefficient $k = 6\,\mathrm{N/m}$ and the distance $d = 4\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the work $W$?

- $96\,\mathrm{J}$
- $32\,\mathrm{J}$
- $48\,\mathrm{J}$
- $24\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Work is the integral of $kx$ from zero to $d$, equal to the area of the triangle under the graph. In the stated units, the relation is $W = \dfrac{d^{2} \cdot k}{2}$. Substituting the given values yields $48\,\mathrm{J}$.
