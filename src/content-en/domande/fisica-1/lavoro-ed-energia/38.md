Three balls are thrown from a balcony with velocities of the same magnitude, one upward, one horizontally and one downward. Neglecting air resistance, which ball reaches the ground with the velocity of greatest magnitude?

- They all arrive with velocities of the same magnitude
- The one thrown downward
- The one thrown upward
- The one thrown horizontally

## Explanation

Only the weight acts on the balls, so the mechanical energy of each one is conserved. The three balls start from the same height with kinetic energy $\dfrac{1}{2} m v_0^2$, which depends on the magnitude of the velocity and not on its direction. At the ground $\dfrac{1}{2} m v^2 = \dfrac{1}{2} m v_0^2 + mgh$, therefore $v = \sqrt{v_0^2 + 2gh}$ for all three. The directions of arrival and the times of flight are different.
