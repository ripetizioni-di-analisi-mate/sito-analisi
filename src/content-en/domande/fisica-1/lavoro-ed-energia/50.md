On a potential energy graph $U(x)$, a particle is to the right of a minimum where the local slope is positive. Only the force associated with $U$ acts. In which direction does it accelerate?

- Toward increasing $x$
- Toward decreasing $x$
- Always in the direction of velocity
- The acceleration is zero

## Explanation

The force is $F_x=-dU/dx$. Since the slope is positive, both force and acceleration have a negative component.
