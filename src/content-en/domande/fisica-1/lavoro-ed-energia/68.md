A constant power is delivered to a moving cart whose initial kinetic energy is $18\,\mathrm{J}$. No other forces do work. Given the power $P = 12\,\mathrm{W}$ and the final kinetic energy $K = 66\,\mathrm{J}$, what is the elapsed time $t$?

- $8\,\mathrm{s}$
- $\dfrac{11}{2}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $7\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The driving force does work $Pt$. The cart has an initial kinetic energy of $18\,\mathrm{J}$. In the stated units, the relation is $K = P \cdot t + 18$. Solving for the unknown gives $t = \dfrac{K - 18}{P}$. Substituting the given values yields $4\,\mathrm{s}$.
