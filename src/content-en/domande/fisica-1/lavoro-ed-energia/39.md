A stone is dropped from rest from a height of $20\,\mathrm{m}$, with negligible air resistance. With zero height at the ground, at what height is the kinetic energy of the stone three times its potential energy?

- $15\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5{,}0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6{,}7\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Mechanical energy is conserved and equals $E = mgh_0$, with $h_0 = 20\,\mathrm{m}$. At the required height $h$, we have $K = 3U$, so $E = K + U = 4U = 4mgh$. Equating the two expressions of $E$ gives $mgh_0 = 4mgh$, therefore $h = \dfrac{h_0}{4} = 5{,}0\,\mathrm{m}$. At the height of $15\,\mathrm{m}$ the inverse relation $U = 3K$ holds.
