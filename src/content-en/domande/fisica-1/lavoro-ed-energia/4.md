## Explanation

The force depends on the position, so the work is the integral of its component between the initial and final positions,
$$
L = \int_0^{4} (2{,}0 + 1{,}5\,x)\,dx\,\mathrm{J} = \left[ 2{,}0\,x + 0{,}75\,x^2 \right]_0^{4}\,\mathrm{J} = (8{,}0 + 12)\,\mathrm{J} = 20\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
The same value is the area of the trapezoid under the graph of $F_x$, with bases $F_x(0) = 2{,}0\,\mathrm{N}$ and $F_x(4) = 8{,}0\,\mathrm{N}$ and height $4{,}0\,\mathrm{m}$. The product $F_x(4) \cdot 4{,}0\,\mathrm{m} = 32\,\mathrm{J}$ is the work of a constant force equal to the final value.
