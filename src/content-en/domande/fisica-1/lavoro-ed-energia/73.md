The function $U(x) = x^2 + a\,x$, with $x$ in meters, $U$ in joules and $a$ constant, is required to be the potential energy of a particle constrained to the $x$ axis, with an equilibrium position at $x = 3.0\,\mathrm{m}$. What value must $a$ have?

- $a = 6\,\mathrm{J/m}$
- $a = -3\,\mathrm{J/m}$
- $a = -6\,\mathrm{J/m}$
- $a = -9\,\mathrm{J/m}$

## Explanation

A position $x_0$ is an equilibrium position if the force $F_x = -U'(x)$ vanishes at $x_0$, that is, if $U'(x_0) = 0$.

The derivative is $U'(x) = 2x + a$, so the condition at $x_0 = 3\,\mathrm{m}$ is $6 + a = 0$, hence $a = -6\,\mathrm{J/m}$. The value $a = -9\,\mathrm{J/m}$ is obtained by imposing $U(3) = 0$ instead of $U'(3) = 0$.
