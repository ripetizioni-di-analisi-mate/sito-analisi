## Explanation

The tension is constant and the displacement is along a straight line, so the work is $L = F\,s\cos\theta$, where $\theta = 40^\circ$ is the angle between the rope and the sidewalk. This gives
$$
L = 60 \cdot 5.0 \cdot \cos 40^\circ\,\mathrm{J} \approx 300 \cdot 0.766\,\mathrm{J} \approx 2.3 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
The work is positive, because the angle between the tension and the displacement is acute. The vertical component of the tension, $F\sin\theta$, is perpendicular to the displacement and does no work.
