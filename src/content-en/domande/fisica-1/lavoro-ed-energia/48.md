A winch lifts a load at constant speed. Its mechanical efficiency is $0.8$, and take $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Given the mass $m = 80\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the input power $P = 500\,\mathrm{W}$, what is the speed $v$?

- $\dfrac{5}{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac{25}{32}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The useful power is $mgv$. Divide by efficiency to find the input power. In the stated units, the relation is $P = \dfrac{25 \cdot m \cdot v}{2}$. Solving for the unknown gives $v = \dfrac{2 \cdot P}{25 \cdot m}$. Substituting the given values yields $\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
