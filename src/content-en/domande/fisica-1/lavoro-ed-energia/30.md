A skier of mass $70\,\mathrm{kg}$ goes down from a point $A$ to a point $B$ located $50\,\mathrm{m}$ lower, along a winding slope $800\,\mathrm{m}$ long. How much work does the weight do?

- $5{,}5 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{J}$
- $3{,}4 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-3{,}4 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{J}$
- $3{,}5 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The weight is a **conservative force**, and its work along any path depends only on the difference in height, $L_P = mg\,(y_A - y_B)$. The skier goes down by $50\,\mathrm{m}$, so
$$
L_P = 70 \cdot 9{,}81 \cdot 50\,\mathrm{J} \approx 3{,}4 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
The work is positive because the skier goes down, and the length of the slope does not enter the calculation.
