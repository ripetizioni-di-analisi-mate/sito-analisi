A force along $x$ rises linearly with positive displacement from $4\,\mathrm{N}$ to $F$. The final value $F$ is greater than the initial value. Given the distance $d = 5\,\mathrm{m}$ and the work $W = 40\,\mathrm{J}$, what is the final force $F$?

- $20\,\mathrm{N}$
- $24\,\mathrm{N}$
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$
- $8\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Work is the area of the trapezoid under the force-position graph. Its average height is the mean of the endpoint values. In the stated units, the relation is $W = \dfrac{d \cdot \left(F + 4\right)}{2}$. Solving for the unknown gives $F = \dfrac{2 \cdot W}{d} - 4$. Substituting the given values yields $12\,\mathrm{N}$.
