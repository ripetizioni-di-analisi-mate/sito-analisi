Two bodies $A$ and $B$ have the same kinetic energy, and the mass of $A$ is four times the mass of $B$. What is the ratio $\dfrac{v_A}{v_B}$ of the magnitudes of their velocities?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $4$

## Explanation

The equality of the kinetic energies gives $\dfrac{1}{2} m_A v_A^2 = \dfrac{1}{2} m_B v_B^2$, so $\dfrac{v_A^2}{v_B^2} = \dfrac{m_B}{m_A} = \dfrac{1}{4}$. The magnitudes of the velocities are positive, therefore $\dfrac{v_A}{v_B} = \dfrac{1}{2}$. Kinetic energy depends on the square of the speed, and for this reason the ratio of the speeds is the square root of the inverse ratio of the masses.
