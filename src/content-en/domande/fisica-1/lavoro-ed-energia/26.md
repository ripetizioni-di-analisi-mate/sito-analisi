A cyclist, whose total mass together with the bicycle is $75\,\mathrm{kg}$, rides at $36\,\mathrm{km/h}$. What is the cyclist's kinetic energy?

- $4.9 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{J}$
- $7.5 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{J}$
- $7.5 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{J}$
- $3.8 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The speed must be expressed in meters per second, and since $1\,\mathrm{km/h} = \dfrac{1}{3.6}\,\mathrm{m/s}$ we have $v = \dfrac{36}{3.6}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 10\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

By the definition of **kinetic energy**
$$
K = \frac{1}{2} m v^2 = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 75 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{J} = 3750\,\mathrm{J} \approx 3.8 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
The value $4.9 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{J}$ is obtained by leaving the speed in kilometers per hour.
