A particle moves along the $x$-axis subject to a conservative force with potential energy $U(x) = 4x^2 - 2x$, where $x$ is in meters and $U$ in joules. What is the component $F_x$ of the force at $x = 1\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $6\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-8\,\mathrm{N}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The force is related to the potential energy by $F_x(x) = -\dfrac{dU}{dx}$. Since $\dfrac{dU}{dx} = 8x - 2$, at $x = 1\,\mathrm{m}$ the derivative is $6\,\mathrm{J/m} = 6\,\mathrm{N}$, so $F_x(1) = -6\,\mathrm{N}$. The negative sign indicates that the force points toward decreasing $x$, where the potential energy decreases.
