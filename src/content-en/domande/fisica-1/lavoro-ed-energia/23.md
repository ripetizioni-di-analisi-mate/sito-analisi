A car travels at the constant speed of $72\,\mathrm{km/h}$ on a horizontal road, and the resistive forces have a resultant of magnitude $500\,\mathrm{N}$. What power does the driving force deliver?

- $10\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $36\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $2.8\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $5.0\,\mathrm{kW}$

## Explanation

The speed in SI units is $v = \dfrac{72}{3.6}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 20\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The velocity is constant, so the driving force balances the resistive forces and has magnitude $500\,\mathrm{N}$, in the direction of the velocity. By the relation between **power and velocity**
$$
P = F\,v = 500 \cdot 20\,\mathrm{W} = 1.0 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{W} = 10\,\mathrm{kW} .
$$
