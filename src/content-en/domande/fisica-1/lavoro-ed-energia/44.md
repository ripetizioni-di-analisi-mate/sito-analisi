A particle moves along the $x$-axis subject only to a conservative force with potential energy $U(x) = 50\,x^2$, where $x$ is in meters and $U$ in joules. Its mechanical energy is $2.0\,\mathrm{J}$. Between which turning points does the particle oscillate?

- $x = \pm 0.28\,\mathrm{m}$
- $x = \pm 0.040\,\mathrm{m}$
- $x = \pm 0.20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $x = \pm 0.14\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Motion can take place only where $U(x) \leq E$, and the turning points are those where $U(x) = E$, where the speed is zero. The equation $50\,x^2 = 2.0$ gives $x^2 = 0.040\,\mathrm{m^2}$, so $x = \pm 0.20\,\mathrm{m}$. The particle therefore oscillates between $-0.20\,\mathrm{m}$ and $0.20\,\mathrm{m}$, with maximum speed at $x = 0$, where $U$ is minimum. The coefficient $50\,\mathrm{J/m^2}$ equals $\dfrac{1}{2} k$ with $k = 100\,\mathrm{N/m}$.
