The force along a track is $F(x)=kx$. A cart moves from the origin to $d>0$. The initial data are the force coefficient $k = 6\,\mathrm{N/m}$ and the distance $d = 4\,\mathrm{m}$. Change the distance to $6\,\mathrm{m}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the ratio of the final value of $W$ to its initial value?

- $1$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}$
- $\dfrac{4}{9}$
- $\dfrac{9}{4}$

## Explanation

Work is the integral of $kx$ from zero to $d$, equal to the area of the triangle under the graph. The relation $W = \dfrac{d^{2} \cdot k}{2}$ gives $48\,\mathrm{J}$ before the change and $108\,\mathrm{J}$ after it. The ratio is $\dfrac{9}{4}$.
