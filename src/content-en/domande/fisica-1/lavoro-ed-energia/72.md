A scuba diver with a total mass of $80\,\mathrm{kg}$ is $12\,\mathrm{m}$ below sea level. With the $y$ axis pointing vertically upward and zero height at sea level, what is the potential energy associated with the diver's weight?

- $9.4 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-9.6 \cdot 10^2\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-4.7 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-9.4 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The potential energy of the weight is $U = m g y$, with $y$ the height relative to the chosen reference. The diver is below the reference level, so the height is negative, $y = -12\,\mathrm{m}$.

Therefore
$$
U = 80 \cdot 9.81 \cdot (-12)\,\mathrm{J} \approx -9.4 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
The negative sign means that, while the diver rises to sea level, the weight would do on the diver the negative work $U - 0 \approx -9.4 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{J}$.
