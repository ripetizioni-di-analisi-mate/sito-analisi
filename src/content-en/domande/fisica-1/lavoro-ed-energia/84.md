A particle in one dimension has potential energy $U(x)=cx^2$. Consider a point with $x>0$. Given the potential energy coefficient $c = 3\,\mathrm{J/m^2}$ and the conservative force magnitude $F = 24\,\mathrm{N}$, what is the coordinate of the point $x$?

- $8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2 \cdot \sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The force is $F_x=-dU/dx=-2cx$. The negative sign indicates a force toward the origin. In the stated units, the relation is $F = 2 \cdot c \cdot x$. Solving for the unknown gives $x = \dfrac{F}{2 \cdot c}$. Substituting the given values yields $4\,\mathrm{m}$.
