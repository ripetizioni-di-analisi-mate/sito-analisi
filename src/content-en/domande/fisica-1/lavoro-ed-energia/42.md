Two identical packages start from rest at the same height and slide down two straight slides with the same coefficient of kinetic friction; the first slide is steeper than the second, and both packages reach the bottom. Which statement about the final speeds is true?

- They arrive with the same speed, because the difference in height is the same
- The package on the steeper slide arrives faster
- The package on the less steep slide arrives faster, because the normal force is greater
- The package on the less steep slide arrives faster, because the path is longer

## Explanation

On a slide inclined at $\alpha$ and of length $d$ the normal force is $mg\cos\alpha$, so friction does the work $-\mu_d\,mg\cos\alpha\,d$. The product $d\cos\alpha$ is the length $b$ of the horizontal projection of the slide, therefore $L_{\text{fr}} = -\mu_d\,mg\,b$. By the theorem on the **work of nonconservative forces** $\dfrac{1}{2} m v^2 = mgh - \mu_d\,mg\,b$. For the same height $h$ the steeper slide has the shorter horizontal projection, so friction dissipates less energy and the package arrives **faster**.
