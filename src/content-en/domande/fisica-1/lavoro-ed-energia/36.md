A cart moves along a smooth track. At point $A$, at a height of $8{,}0\,\mathrm{m}$ above the ground, its speed is $6{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. With what speed does it pass through point $B$, at a height of $3{,}0\,\mathrm{m}$ above the ground? Air resistance is negligible.

- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $9{,}9\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $14\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The track is smooth and air resistance is negligible, so the normal force from the track does no work and mechanical energy is conserved. With zero height at the ground, $\dfrac{1}{2} m v_A^2 + mg\,y_A = \dfrac{1}{2} m v_B^2 + mg\,y_B$, therefore
$$
v_B = \sqrt{v_A^2 + 2g\,(y_A - y_B)} = \sqrt{36 + 2 \cdot 9{,}81 \cdot 5{,}0}\,\mathrm{m/s} \approx \sqrt{134}\,\mathrm{m/s} \approx 12\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The result depends only on the difference in height $y_A - y_B = 5{,}0\,\mathrm{m}$, whatever the profile of the track between $A$ and $B$.
