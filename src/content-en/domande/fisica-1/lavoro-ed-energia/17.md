A body moves along the $x$-axis from $x = 0$ to $x = 6.0\,\mathrm{m}$. The component $F_x$ of the force acting on it is $10\,\mathrm{N}$ for $0 \leq x \leq 2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ and then decreases linearly to zero at $x = 6.0\,\mathrm{m}$. How much work does the force do?

- $60\,\mathrm{J}$
- $40\,\mathrm{J}$
- $20\,\mathrm{J}$
- $30\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The work of a variable force is the area under the graph of $F_x$ as a function of $x$. Between $0$ and $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ the graph is a horizontal segment, and the area is that of the rectangle, $10 \cdot 2.0\,\mathrm{J} = 20\,\mathrm{J}$. Between $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ and $6.0\,\mathrm{m}$ the graph decreases from $10\,\mathrm{N}$ to zero, and the area is that of the triangle, $\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 4.0 \cdot 10\,\mathrm{J} = 20\,\mathrm{J}$. Therefore the total work is $L = 40\,\mathrm{J}$.
