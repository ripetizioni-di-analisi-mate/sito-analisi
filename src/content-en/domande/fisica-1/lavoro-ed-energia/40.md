A block is launched with speed $8.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$ up a plane inclined at $30^\circ$, with coefficient of kinetic friction $0.20$. What distance does it travel along the plane before stopping?

- $6.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4.8\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

During the ascent the normal force is $mg\cos\alpha$, and friction, of magnitude $\mu_d\,mg\cos\alpha$, points down the incline like the component of the weight along the plane. Traveling the distance $d$, the block rises by $d\sin\alpha$, so by the theorem on the **work of nonconservative forces**
$$
-\mu_d\,mg\cos\alpha\,d = mg\,d\sin\alpha - \frac{1}{2} m v_0^2 .
$$
Therefore
$$
d = \frac{v_0^2}{2g\,(\sin\alpha + \mu_d\cos\alpha)} = \frac{64}{2 \cdot 9.81 \cdot (0.500 + 0.173)}\,\mathrm{m} \approx 4.8\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $2.4\,\mathrm{m}$ is the rise in height $d\sin\alpha$ of the block.
