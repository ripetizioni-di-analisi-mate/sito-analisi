## Explanation

With zero height at the bottom of the slide, the initial mechanical energy is $mgh = 2.0 \cdot 9.81 \cdot 3.0\,\mathrm{J} \approx 58.9\,\mathrm{J}$ and the final one is $\dfrac{1}{2} m v^2 = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 2.0 \cdot 36\,\mathrm{J} = 36\,\mathrm{J}$. By the theorem on the **work of nonconservative forces** the work of friction equals the change in mechanical energy, and the dissipated energy is its absolute value, so
$$
E_{\text{diss}} = mgh - \frac{1}{2} m v^2 \approx 58.9\,\mathrm{J} - 36\,\mathrm{J} \approx 23\,\mathrm{J} .
$$
