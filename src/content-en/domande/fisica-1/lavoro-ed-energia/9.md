A block of mass $0.25\,\mathrm{kg}$ rests against a spring of constant $k = 400\,\mathrm{N/m}$ compressed by $5.0\,\mathrm{cm}$, on a smooth horizontal plane. Once the spring is released, with what speed does the block leave it when the spring returns to its rest length?

- $8.9\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The weight and the normal force are perpendicular to the displacement, so the sum of the kinetic energy and the elastic potential energy is conserved. At the start the block is at rest and when it leaves the spring the spring is at rest length, therefore $\dfrac{1}{2} k x^2 = \dfrac{1}{2} m v^2$. With $x = 0.050\,\mathrm{m}$ this gives
$$
v = x\sqrt{\frac{k}{m}} = 0.050 \cdot \sqrt{\frac{400}{0.25}}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 0.050 \cdot 40\,\mathrm{m/s} = 2.0\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
The factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$ appears in both energies and cancels out.
