A force pulls a sled at constant speed on a horizontal surface. Its horizontal component is $4/5$ of its magnitude and points along the motion. Given the speed $v = 2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and the power delivered by the applied force $P = 48\,\mathrm{W}$, what is the force magnitude $F$?

- $120\,\mathrm{N}$
- $60\,\mathrm{N}$
- $30\,\mathrm{N}$
- $24\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Power is $\vec F\cdot\vec v$. Since the velocity is horizontal, only the horizontal component of force contributes. In the stated units, the relation is $P = \dfrac{4 \cdot F \cdot v}{5}$. Solving for the unknown gives $F = \dfrac{5 \cdot P}{4 \cdot v}$. Substituting the given values yields $30\,\mathrm{N}$.
