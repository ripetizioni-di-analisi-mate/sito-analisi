A body travels along a closed path and returns to its starting point. Which of the following forces does zero work on the body along every closed path?

- Kinetic friction from the floor
- Air resistance
- The weight
- A force of constant magnitude, always tangent to the path and in the same direction as the velocity

## Explanation

A force does zero work along every closed path if and only if it is conservative. The weight is conservative, because its work $mg\,(y_A - y_B)$ depends only on the initial and final heights, which coincide on a closed path. Kinetic friction and air resistance are opposite to the velocity, so they do negative work on every stretch. A force of magnitude $F$ tangent to the path and in the same direction as the velocity does the positive work $F\,\ell$, where $\ell$ is the length of the path.
