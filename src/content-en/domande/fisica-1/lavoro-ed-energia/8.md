## Explanation

The tension in the string is always perpendicular to the velocity and does no work, so mechanical energy is conserved. When the string is horizontal the ball has risen by $\ell$ with respect to the lowest point. The minimum speed is the one that brings the ball to that position with zero speed, therefore $\dfrac{1}{2} m v^2 = mg\ell$ and
$$
v = \sqrt{2 g \ell} = \sqrt{2 \cdot 9{,}81 \cdot 0{,}80}\,\mathrm{m/s} \approx 4{,}0\,\mathrm{m/s} .
$$
