A particle moves along the $x$-axis subject only to a conservative force with potential energy $U(x) = x^4 - 2x^2$, where $x$ is in meters and $U$ in joules. What are the positions of stable equilibrium?

- $x = -1\,\mathrm{m}$ and $x = 1\,\mathrm{m}$
- Only $x = 0$
- $x = -\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{m}$ and $x = \sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $x = -1\,\mathrm{m}$, $x = 0$ and $x = 1\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The equilibrium positions are the zeros of $U'(x) = 4x^3 - 4x = 4x\,(x - 1)(x + 1)$, that is, $x = -1\,\mathrm{m}$, $x = 0$ and $x = 1\,\mathrm{m}$. The second derivative is $U''(x) = 12x^2 - 4$. Since $U''(\pm 1) = 8 > 0$, the positions $x = \pm 1\,\mathrm{m}$ are minima of $U$ and the equilibrium is **stable**; since $U''(0) = -4 < 0$, the position $x = 0$ is a maximum of $U$ and the equilibrium is unstable. The points $x = \pm\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{m}$ are the zeros of $U$, where the force is $F_x = \mp 4\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{N}$.
