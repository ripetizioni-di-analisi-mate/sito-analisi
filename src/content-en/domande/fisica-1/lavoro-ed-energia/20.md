An ideal spring is stretched from an existing extension of $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ to $6\,\mathrm{cm}$. Is the quasistatic external work during the last centimetre greater or less than during the first centimetre of this operation?

- It is three times the work during the first centimetre.
- It is equal to the work during the first centimetre.
- It is $\dfrac{5}{11}$ of the work during the first centimetre.
- It is $\dfrac{11}{5}$ times the work during the first centimetre.

## Explanation

For an ideal spring the external work equals the change in $\dfrac12 kx^2$. Over the two intervals the differences of squares are proportional to $6^2-5^2=11$ and $3^2-2^2=5$, giving a ratio of $\dfrac{11}{5}$.
