The mass of the Earth is about $81$ times that of the Moon. The Earth attracts the Moon with a force of magnitude $F$. Which statement is true?

- The Moon attracts the Earth with a force of magnitude $\dfrac{F}{81}$
- The Moon attracts the Earth with a force of magnitude $81F$
- The Moon attracts the Earth with a force of magnitude $\dfrac{F}{9}$
- The Moon attracts the Earth with a force of magnitude $F$

## Explanation

In the **law of universal gravitation** the magnitude $G\,\dfrac{M_T M_L}{d^2}$ contains the product of the two masses, so it is the same for the force of the Earth on the Moon and for the force of the Moon on the Earth. The two forces have opposite directions, $\vec{F}_{21} = -\vec{F}_{12}$, in agreement with Newton's third law. The accelerations, instead, are different, because for the same force the acceleration is inversely proportional to the mass. Therefore the acceleration of the Earth is $81$ times smaller than that of the Moon.
