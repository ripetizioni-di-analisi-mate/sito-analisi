## Explanation

At perihelion and at aphelion the velocity is perpendicular to the position vector, so the magnitude of the angular momentum is $m r v$. By **Kepler's second law** the angular momentum is constant, therefore $r_p v_p = r_a v_a$. Then
$$
v_a = \frac{r_p}{r_a}\,v_p = \frac{1{,}0}{9{,}0} \cdot 40\,\mathrm{km/s} = 4{,}4\,\mathrm{km/s} .
$$
At the two points the speed is inversely proportional to the distance from the Sun, and the comet is slower where it is farther away.
