A probe follows an elliptical orbit. At apocenter and pericenter its velocity is perpendicular to the radius vector. The pericenter radius is $8\,\mathrm{Mm}$. Given the pericenter speed $u = 6\,\mathrm{km/s}$ and the apocenter speed $v = 2\,\mathrm{km/s}$, what is the apocenter radius $r$?

- $\dfrac{8}{3}\,\mathrm{Mm}$
- $48\,\mathrm{Mm}$
- $12\,\mathrm{Mm}$
- $24\,\mathrm{Mm}$

## Explanation

Gravity is a central force. Angular momentum is conserved and has magnitude $mrv$ at both apsides. In the stated units, the relation is $v = \dfrac{8 \cdot u}{r}$. Solving for the unknown gives $r = \dfrac{8 \cdot u}{v}$. Substituting the given values yields $24\,\mathrm{Mm}$.
