According to Kepler's first law, where is the Sun located relative to the elliptical orbit of a planet?

- At the center of the ellipse
- At an end of the minor axis of the ellipse
- At one of the two foci of the ellipse
- At the midpoint between the center of the ellipse and the aphelion

## Explanation

Kepler's first law states that each planet moves on an ellipse and that the Sun occupies one of the two foci.

Since the focus does not coincide with the center, the distance from the Sun varies along the orbit: the perihelion and the aphelion are the ends of the major axis, respectively the nearest to and the farthest from the focus occupied by the Sun.

The other focus is an empty point with no physical meaning.
