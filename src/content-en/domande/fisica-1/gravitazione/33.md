In the model of a spherical Earth with uniform density, two points near the surface are compared, one at altitude $h$ and one at depth $d = h$, with $h$ small compared with $R_T$. Which statement is true?

- At both points $g < g_0$, and the decrease at depth is twice the decrease at altitude
- At both points $g < g_0$, and the two decreases are equal
- At both points $g < g_0$, and the decrease at depth is half the decrease at altitude
- At altitude $g$ decreases, while at depth $g$ increases

## Explanation

At altitude $g \approx g_0 \left( 1 - \dfrac{2h}{R_T} \right)$, so the decrease is $g_0\,\dfrac{2h}{R_T}$. At depth, by the **field of a homogeneous sphere** inside the sphere, $g = g_0 \left( 1 - \dfrac{d}{R_T} \right)$, and with $d = h$ the decrease is $g_0\,\dfrac{h}{R_T}$. Therefore at depth $g$ decreases by half as much as it decreases at altitude.
