A satellite follows a circular orbit. Gravitational potential energy is zero at infinity. The central body's gravitational parameter is $GM=24000\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$. Given the mass $m = 4\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the distance from the center $r = 12\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the orbital mechanical energy $U$?

- $-2000\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-4000\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-6000\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-8000\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

In a circular orbit the kinetic energy is $GMm/(2r)$ and the potential energy is $-GMm/r$. Their sum is negative. In the stated units, the relation is $U = - \dfrac{12000 \cdot m}{r}$. Substituting the given values yields $-4000\,\mathrm{J}$.
