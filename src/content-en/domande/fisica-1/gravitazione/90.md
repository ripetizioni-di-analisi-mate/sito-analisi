A circular orbit has period $T$. Over a short interval the satellite sweeps out a circular sector of angle $\theta$, measured in radians. Given the orbital period $T = 8\,\mathrm{h}$ and the duration of the interval $t = 4\,\mathrm{h}$, what is the swept angle $\theta$?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $\pi\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $2 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}\,\mathrm{rad}$

## Explanation

The swept angle is the same fraction of $2\pi$ as the elapsed time is of the period. In the stated units, the relation is $t = \dfrac{T \cdot \theta}{2 \cdot \pi}$. Solving for the unknown gives $\theta = \dfrac{2 \cdot \pi \cdot t}{T}$. Substituting the given values yields $\pi\,\mathrm{rad}$.
