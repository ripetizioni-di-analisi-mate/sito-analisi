An object is at altitude $h$ above a spherical body of radius $R$. Surface gravity is $10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Given the altitude $h = 8\,\mathrm{km}$ and gravitational acceleration at altitude $g = \dfrac{18}{5}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the body's radius $R$?

- $6\,\mathrm{km}$
- $\dfrac{9}{2}\,\mathrm{km}$
- $12\,\mathrm{km}$
- $24\,\mathrm{km}$

## Explanation

Distance from the center increases from $R$ to $R+h$. Gravitational acceleration is inversely proportional to the square of that distance. In the stated units, the relation is $g = \dfrac{10 \cdot R^{2}}{\left(R + h\right)^{2}}$. Solving for the unknown gives $R = \dfrac{h \cdot \left(- \sqrt{10} \cdot \sqrt{g} - g\right)}{g - 10}$. Substituting the given values yields $12\,\mathrm{km}$.
