- $6.02 \cdot 10^{24}\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

By **Kepler's third law** for circular orbits, $\dfrac{T^2}{r^3} = \dfrac{4\pi^2}{G M_T}$, so $M_T = \dfrac{4\pi^2 r^3}{G T^2}$. The period must be expressed in seconds, $T = 27.3 \cdot 86\,400\,\mathrm{s} = 2.36 \cdot 10^6\,\mathrm{s}$, and the cube of the radius is $r^3 = 5.66 \cdot 10^{25}\,\mathrm{m^3}$. Substituting gives
$$
M_T = \frac{4\pi^2 \cdot 5.66 \cdot 10^{25}}{6.67 \cdot 10^{-11} \cdot \left( 2.36 \cdot 10^6 \right)^2}\,\mathrm{kg} = 6.02 \cdot 10^{24}\,\mathrm{kg} ,
$$
close to the value $5.97 \cdot 10^{24}\,\mathrm{kg}$ obtained from $g_0$ and $R_T$.
