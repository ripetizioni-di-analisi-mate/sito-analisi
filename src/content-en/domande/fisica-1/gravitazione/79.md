A probe of negligible mass moves from an inner circular orbit of radius $r$ to a circular orbit of radius $2r$. The gravitational parameter is $b=GM$. Given the gravitational parameter $b = 9600\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$ and the inner orbital radius $r = 20\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the increase in mechanical energy per unit mass $e$?

- $120\,\mathrm{J/kg}$
- $480\,\mathrm{J/kg}$
- $60\,\mathrm{J/kg}$
- $240\,\mathrm{J/kg}$

## Explanation

The mechanical energy per unit mass is $-GM/(2r)$. The final value is $-GM/(4r)$, giving a positive difference. In the stated units, the relation is $e = \dfrac{b}{4 \cdot r}$. Substituting the given values yields $120\,\mathrm{J/kg}$.
