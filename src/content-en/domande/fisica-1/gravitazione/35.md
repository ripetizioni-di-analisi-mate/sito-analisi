A satellite moves in a circular orbit of radius $r = 4R_T$ around the Earth. With $g_0 = 9{,}81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and $R_T = 6{,}37 \cdot 10^6\,\mathrm{m}$, what is its speed?

- $3{,}95\,\mathrm{km/s}$
- $1{,}98\,\mathrm{km/s}$
- $5{,}59\,\mathrm{km/s}$
- $15{,}8\,\mathrm{km/s}$

## Explanation

The relation between **speed and period in a circular orbit** gives $v = \sqrt{\dfrac{G M_T}{r}}$, and with $G M_T = g_0 R_T^2$ we obtain $v = \sqrt{\dfrac{g_0 R_T^2}{4R_T}} = \dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{g_0 R_T}$. Since $\sqrt{g_0 R_T} = \sqrt{9{,}81 \cdot 6{,}37 \cdot 10^6}\,\mathrm{m/s} = 7{,}91\,\mathrm{km/s}$, the speed is $v = 3{,}95\,\mathrm{km/s}$. The speed is inversely proportional to the square root of the radius, so when the radius is quadrupled the speed is halved.
