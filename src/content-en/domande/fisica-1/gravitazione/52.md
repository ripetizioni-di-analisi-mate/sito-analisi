A probe launches radially from an isolated spherical body's surface. Neglect atmosphere and rotation. The gravitational parameter is $b=GM$. Given the gravitational parameter $b = 400\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$ and the escape speed $v = 10\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the radius $R$?

- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

At the escape threshold, total energy is zero. At the surface, energy per unit mass is $v^2/2-GM/R$. In the stated units, the relation is $v = \dfrac{\sqrt{2} \cdot \sqrt{b}}{\sqrt{R}}$. Solving for the unknown gives $R = \dfrac{2 \cdot b}{v^{2}}$. Substituting the given values yields $8\,\mathrm{m}$.
