A probe launches radially from an isolated spherical body's surface. Neglect atmosphere and rotation. The gravitational parameter is $b=GM$. Given the radius $R = 8\,\mathrm{m}$ and the escape speed $v = 10\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the gravitational parameter $b$?

- $200\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$
- $600\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$
- $400\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$
- $800\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$

## Explanation

At the escape threshold, total energy is zero. At the surface, energy per unit mass is $v^2/2-GM/R$. In the stated units, the relation is $v = \dfrac{\sqrt{2} \cdot \sqrt{b}}{\sqrt{R}}$. Solving for the unknown gives $b = \dfrac{R \cdot v^{2}}{2}$. Substituting the given values yields $400\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$.
