An object is at altitude $h$ above a spherical body of radius $R$. Surface gravity is $10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Given the body's radius $R = 12\,\mathrm{km}$ and the altitude $h = 8\,\mathrm{km}$, what is gravitational acceleration at altitude $g$?

- $\dfrac{36}{5}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $\dfrac{250}{9}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $\dfrac{18}{5}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Distance from the center increases from $R$ to $R+h$. Gravitational acceleration is inversely proportional to the square of that distance. In the stated units, the relation is $g = \dfrac{10 \cdot R^{2}}{\left(R + h\right)^{2}}$. Substituting the given values yields $\dfrac{18}{5}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
