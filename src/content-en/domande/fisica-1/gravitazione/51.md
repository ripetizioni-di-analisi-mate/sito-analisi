A probe launches radially from an isolated spherical body's surface. Neglect atmosphere and rotation. The gravitational parameter is $b=GM$. Given the radius $R = 8\,\mathrm{m}$ and the gravitational parameter $b = 400\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$, what is the escape speed $v$?

- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5 \cdot \sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10 \cdot \sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

At the escape threshold, total energy is zero. At the surface, energy per unit mass is $v^2/2-GM/R$. In the stated units, the relation is $v = \dfrac{\sqrt{2} \cdot \sqrt{b}}{\sqrt{R}}$. Substituting the given values yields $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
