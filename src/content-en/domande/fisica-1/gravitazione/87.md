A mass is outside a spherical body. Gravitational potential energy is zero at infinity. Given the mass $m = 3\,\mathrm{kg}$, the distance from the center $r = 20\,\mathrm{m}$ and the potential energy $U = -120\,\mathrm{J}$, what is the gravitational parameter $GM$ $b$?

- $800\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$
- $16000\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$
- $1600\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$
- $400\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$

## Explanation

Integrating the gravitational field with infinity as reference gives potential $-GM/r$. Multiply by the test mass. In the stated units, the relation is $U = - \dfrac{b \cdot m}{r}$. Solving for the unknown gives $b = - \dfrac{U \cdot r}{m}$. Substituting the given values yields $800\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$.
