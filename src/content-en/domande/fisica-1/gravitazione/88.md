A circular orbit has period $T$. Over a short interval the satellite sweeps out a circular sector of angle $\theta$, measured in radians. Given the orbital period $T = 8\,\mathrm{h}$ and the swept angle $\theta = \pi\,\mathrm{rad}$, what is the duration of the interval $t$?

- $8 \cdot \pi\,\mathrm{h}$
- $2\,\mathrm{h}$
- $8\,\mathrm{h}$
- $4\,\mathrm{h}$

## Explanation

The swept angle is the same fraction of $2\pi$ as the elapsed time is of the period. In the stated units, the relation is $t = \dfrac{T \cdot \theta}{2 \cdot \pi}$. Substituting the given values yields $4\,\mathrm{h}$.
