- $5.73 \cdot 10^{5}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6.65 \cdot 10^{6}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.40 \cdot 10^{5}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.80 \cdot 10^{5}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

By the **variation of g with altitude**, $g = g_0\,\dfrac{R_T^2}{(R_T + h)^2}$, so $\dfrac{R_T + h}{R_T} = \sqrt{\dfrac{g_0}{g}}$. With the data, $\sqrt{\dfrac{9.81}{9.00}} = \sqrt{1.09} = 1.0440$, therefore
$$
h = R_T \left( \sqrt{\frac{g_0}{g}} - 1 \right) = 6.37 \cdot 10^6 \cdot 0.0440\,\mathrm{m} = 2.80 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{m} .
$$
The value $6.65 \cdot 10^6\,\mathrm{m}$ is the distance of the point from the center of the Earth, that is, $R_T + h$.
