A probe follows an elliptical orbit. At apocenter and pericenter its velocity is perpendicular to the radius vector. The pericenter radius is $8\,\mathrm{Mm}$. Given the pericenter speed $u = 6\,\mathrm{km/s}$ and the apocenter radius $r = 24\,\mathrm{Mm}$, what is the apocenter speed $v$?

- $4\,\mathrm{km/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{km/s}$
- $18\,\mathrm{km/s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{km/s}$

## Explanation

Gravity is a central force. Angular momentum is conserved and has magnitude $mrv$ at both apsides. In the stated units, the relation is $v = \dfrac{8 \cdot u}{r}$. Substituting the given values yields $2\,\mathrm{km/s}$.
