A satellite moves from a circular orbit of radius $r$ around the Earth to a circular orbit of radius $2r$. Which statement is true?

- The kinetic energy increases and the mechanical energy increases
- The kinetic energy decreases and the mechanical energy decreases
- The kinetic energy decreases and the mechanical energy increases
- The kinetic energy and the mechanical energy stay the same

## Explanation

By the **energy of a satellite in a circular orbit**, $K = \dfrac{GMm}{2r}$ and $E = -\dfrac{GMm}{2r}$. Going from $r$ to $2r$ the kinetic energy is halved, so it decreases. The mechanical energy goes from $-\dfrac{GMm}{2r}$ to $-\dfrac{GMm}{4r}$, which is a larger value because it is closer to zero. Therefore moving up to the higher orbit requires supplying the work $\dfrac{GMm}{4r}$, even though in the new orbit the satellite is slower.
