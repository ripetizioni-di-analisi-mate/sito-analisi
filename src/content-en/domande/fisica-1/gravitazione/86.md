A mass is outside a spherical body. Gravitational potential energy is zero at infinity. Given the gravitational parameter $GM$ $b = 800\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$, the distance from the center $r = 20\,\mathrm{m}$ and the potential energy $U = -120\,\mathrm{J}$, what is the mass $m$?

- $60\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $3\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Integrating the gravitational field with infinity as reference gives potential $-GM/r$. The potential is multiplied by the test mass. In the stated units, the relation is $U = - \dfrac{b \cdot m}{r}$. Solving for the unknown gives $m = - \dfrac{U \cdot r}{b}$. Substituting the given values yields $3\,\mathrm{kg}$.
