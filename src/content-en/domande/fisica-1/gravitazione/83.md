A probe follows an elliptical orbit. At apocenter and pericenter its velocity is perpendicular to the radius vector. The pericenter radius is $8\,\mathrm{Mm}$. Given the apocenter radius $r = 24\,\mathrm{Mm}$ and the apocenter speed $v = 2\,\mathrm{km/s}$, what is the pericenter speed $u$?

- $6\,\mathrm{km/s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{km/s}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}\,\mathrm{km/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{km/s}$

## Explanation

Gravity is a central force. Angular momentum is conserved and has magnitude $mrv$ at both apsides. In the stated units, the relation is $v = \dfrac{8 \cdot u}{r}$. Solving for the unknown gives $u = \dfrac{r \cdot v}{8}$. Substituting the given values yields $6\,\mathrm{km/s}$.
