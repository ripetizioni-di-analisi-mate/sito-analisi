A probe is launched radially from an isolated body's surface at half escape speed. Potential energy is zero at infinity. What is its initial kinetic energy divided by the magnitude of its initial potential energy?

- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac34$
- $1$

## Explanation

At escape speed the two energies have equal magnitudes. At half that speed, kinetic energy is one quarter as large because it depends on $v^2$.
