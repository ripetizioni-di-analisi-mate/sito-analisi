Two equal point masses $M$ are placed at a distance $2a$. At the midpoint of the segment joining them, what are the magnitude of the gravitational field $g$ and the potential $V$, zero at infinite distance?

- $g = 0$ and $V = 0$
- $g = \dfrac{2GM}{a^2}$ and $V = -\dfrac{2GM}{a}$
- $g = 0$ and $V = -\dfrac{2GM}{a}$
- $g = 0$ and $V = -\dfrac{GM}{a}$

## Explanation

Each mass produces at the midpoint a field of magnitude $\dfrac{GM}{a^2}$ directed toward itself, so the two fields have opposite senses. By the **superposition principle** their vector sum is zero, and $g = 0$. The potential is a scalar quantity, therefore the two contributions $-\dfrac{GM}{a}$ add up and give $V = -\dfrac{2GM}{a}$.
