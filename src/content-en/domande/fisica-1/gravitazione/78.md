A test mass is outside a spherical body. The central body's gravitational parameter is $GM=12000\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$. Given the mass $m = 3\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the gravitational force magnitude $F = 90\,\mathrm{N}$, what is the distance from the center $r$?

- $10 \cdot \sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $40\,\mathrm{m}$
- $400\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Outside the spherical body the force is the same as that of a point mass at its center. The center-to-center distance is squared. In the stated units, the relation is $F = \dfrac{12000 \cdot m}{r^{2}}$. Solving for the unknown gives $r = \dfrac{20 \cdot \sqrt{30} \cdot \sqrt{m}}{\sqrt{F}}$. Substituting the given values yields $20\,\mathrm{m}$.
