In the model of a spherical Earth with uniform density, with $g_0 = 9{,}81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ at the surface, what is the gravitational acceleration at a depth equal to $\dfrac{3}{4}$ of the Earth's radius?

- $7{,}36\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2{,}45\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $157\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0{,}613\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

At depth $\dfrac{3}{4} R_T$ the distance from the center is $r = R_T - \dfrac{3}{4} R_T = \dfrac{1}{4} R_T$. By the **field of a homogeneous sphere**, inside the sphere the field is proportional to the distance from the center, $g(r) = \dfrac{G M_T}{R_T^3}\,r = g_0\,\dfrac{r}{R_T}$. Hence $g = \dfrac{g_0}{4} = 2{,}45\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The $\dfrac{1}{r^2}$ law holds only outside the sphere, and applied here it would give $16\,g_0 = 157\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
