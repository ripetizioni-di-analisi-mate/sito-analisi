## Explanation

The gravitational acceleration at the surface is the magnitude of the field at the surface, $g_L = \dfrac{G M_L}{R_L^2}$, with the radius squared. The numerator is $G M_L = 6.67 \cdot 10^{-11} \cdot 7.35 \cdot 10^{22}\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2} = 4.90 \cdot 10^{12}\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$ and the denominator is $R_L^2 = 3.03 \cdot 10^{12}\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Hence $g_L = 1.62\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, about one sixth of the value on the Earth.
