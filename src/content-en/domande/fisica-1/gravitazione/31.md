A satellite is at the altitude $h = 2R_T$ above the Earth's surface. What is the gravitational acceleration at that altitude, if at the surface it is $g_0 = 9{,}81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$?

- $1{,}09\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2{,}45\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3{,}27\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $4{,}91\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

At the altitude $2R_T$ the distance from the center of the Earth is $R_T + 2R_T = 3R_T$. The relation for the **variation of g with altitude** gives $g = g_0\,\dfrac{R_T^2}{(3R_T)^2} = \dfrac{g_0}{9}$. Hence $g = \dfrac{9{,}81}{9}\,\mathrm{m/s^2} = 1{,}09\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The value $\dfrac{g_0}{4} = 2{,}45\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ is obtained by using the altitude instead of the distance from the center.
