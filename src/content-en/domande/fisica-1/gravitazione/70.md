In the model of a spherical Earth with uniform density, with $R_T = 6.37 \cdot 10^6\,\mathrm{m}$ and $g_0 = 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, imagine a straight tunnel that crosses the Earth through its center, with no air and no friction. A body is dropped from rest at the entrance. After how long does it reach the other end of the tunnel? The Earth's rotation is neglected.

- $84\,\mathrm{min}$
- $21\,\mathrm{min}$
- $42\,\mathrm{min}$
- $13\,\mathrm{min}$

## Explanation

Inside the homogeneous sphere the field points toward the center and has magnitude $g_0\,\dfrac{r}{R_T}$. Calling $x$ the position along the tunnel, measured from the center, the force on the body is $F_x = -\dfrac{m g_0}{R_T}\,x$, proportional to the displacement and opposite to it.

The motion is therefore harmonic with angular frequency $\omega = \sqrt{\dfrac{g_0}{R_T}}$ and period
$$
T = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{R_T}{g_0}} = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{6.37 \cdot 10^6}{9.81}}\,\mathrm{s} = 5.06 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{s} .
$$
The body goes from one end to the other in half a period, that is, in $2.53 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{s} = 42\,\mathrm{min}$. The full period, $84\,\mathrm{min}$, is the round-trip time.
