A satellite follows a circular orbit. Gravitational potential energy is zero at infinity. The central body's gravitational parameter is $GM=24000\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$. The initial data are the mass $m = 4\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the distance from the center $r = 12\,\mathrm{m}$. Change the mass to $6\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the distance from the center to $18\,\mathrm{m}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the final value of $U$ minus its initial value?

- $2\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-4000\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

In a circular orbit the kinetic energy is $GMm/(2r)$ and the potential energy is $-GMm/r$. Their sum is negative. The relation $U = - \dfrac{12000 \cdot m}{r}$ gives $-4000\,\mathrm{J}$ before the change and $-4000\,\mathrm{J}$ after it. Subtracting the initial value from the final value gives $0\,\mathrm{J}$.
