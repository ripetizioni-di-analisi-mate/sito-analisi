A satellite follows a circular orbit. Gravitational potential energy is zero at infinity. The central body's gravitational parameter is $GM=24000\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$. Given the distance from the center $r = 12\,\mathrm{m}$ and the orbital mechanical energy $U = -4000\,\mathrm{J}$, what is the mass $m$?

- $4\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $2\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

In a circular orbit the kinetic energy is $GMm/(2r)$ and the potential energy is $-GMm/r$. Their sum is negative. In the stated units, the relation is $U = - \dfrac{12000 \cdot m}{r}$. Solving for the unknown gives $m = - \dfrac{U \cdot r}{12000}$. Substituting the given values yields $4\,\mathrm{kg}$.
