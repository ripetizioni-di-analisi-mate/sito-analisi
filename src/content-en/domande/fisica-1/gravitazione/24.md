A body of mass $m$ approaches a planet of mass $M$, going from the distance $3R$ to the distance $R$ from the center. What is the work done by the gravitational force of the planet?

- $-\dfrac{2GMm}{3R}$
- $\dfrac{2GMm}{3R}$
- $\dfrac{GMm}{R}$
- $\dfrac{4GMm}{3R}$

## Explanation

The gravitational force is conservative, so its work is the difference between the initial and the final potential energy, $L = U(3R) - U(R)$. With $U(r) = -\dfrac{GMm}{r}$ we obtain
$$
L = -\,\frac{GMm}{3R} + \frac{GMm}{R} = \frac{2GMm}{3R} .
$$
The work is **positive**, because the force is directed toward the planet and the body moves in the same sense.
