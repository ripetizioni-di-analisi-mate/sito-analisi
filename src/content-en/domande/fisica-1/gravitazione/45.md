A planet moves in an elliptical orbit around the Sun. Which statement is true?

- The speed of the planet is greatest at perihelion
- The speed of the planet is constant, because the position vector sweeps out equal areas in equal times
- The speed of the planet is greatest at aphelion
- The angular momentum of the planet about the Sun is greatest at perihelion

## Explanation

By **Kepler's second law** the angular momentum about the Sun is constant along the whole orbit, because the gravitational force is directed along the position vector. At perihelion and at aphelion the velocity is perpendicular to the position vector, so $r_p v_p = r_a v_a$, and since $r_p < r_a$ we have $v_p > v_a$. More generally, by the conservation of energy $\dfrac{1}{2} m v^2 = E + \dfrac{GMm}{r}$, which is greatest where $r$ is smallest, that is, at perihelion. Equal areas in equal times correspond to constant speed only in a circular orbit.
