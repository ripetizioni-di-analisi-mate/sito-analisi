A satellite follows a circular orbit. Gravitational potential energy is zero at infinity. The central body's gravitational parameter is $GM=24000\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$. The initial data are the mass $m = 4\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the distance from the center $r = 12\,\mathrm{m}$. Change the distance from the center to $24\,\mathrm{m}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the ratio of the final value of $U$ to its initial value?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $1$

## Explanation

In a circular orbit the kinetic energy is $GMm/(2r)$ and the potential energy is $-GMm/r$. Their sum is negative. The relation $U = - \dfrac{12000 \cdot m}{r}$ gives $-4000\,\mathrm{J}$ before the change and $-2000\,\mathrm{J}$ after it. The ratio is $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
