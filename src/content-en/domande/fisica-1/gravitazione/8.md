## Explanation

The satellite stays above the same point if its period equals the rotation period of Mars, $T = 88\,642\,\mathrm{s}$. From the formula for the period in a circular orbit, $T^2 = \dfrac{4\pi^2 r^3}{GM}$, we get $r = \sqrt[3]{\dfrac{G M T^2}{4\pi^2}}$. With the data, $GM = 4{,}28 \cdot 10^{13}\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$ and $T^2 = 7{,}86 \cdot 10^9\,\mathrm{s^2}$, so $\dfrac{G M T^2}{4\pi^2} = 8{,}52 \cdot 10^{21}\,\mathrm{m^3}$. The cube root gives $r = 2{,}04 \cdot 10^7\,\mathrm{m}$, about six times the radius of Mars.
