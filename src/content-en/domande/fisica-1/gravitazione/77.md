A test mass is outside a spherical body. The central body's gravitational parameter is $GM=12000\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$. Given the distance from the center $r = 20\,\mathrm{m}$ and the gravitational force magnitude $F = 90\,\mathrm{N}$, what is the mass $m$?

- $6\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $3\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $\dfrac{3}{20}\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Outside the spherical body the force is the same as that of a point mass at its center. The center-to-center distance is squared. In the stated units, the relation is $F = \dfrac{12000 \cdot m}{r^{2}}$. Solving for the unknown gives $m = \dfrac{F \cdot r^{2}}{12000}$. Substituting the given values yields $3\,\mathrm{kg}$.
