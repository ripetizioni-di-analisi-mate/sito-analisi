- $2.8\,\mathrm{AU}$

## Explanation

By **Kepler's third law** the ratio $\dfrac{T^2}{a^3}$ is the same for all bodies orbiting the Sun. For the Earth $T = 1$ year and $a = 1\,\mathrm{AU}$, so with $T$ in years and $a$ in astronomical units, $T^2 = a^3$. For the asteroid $a^3 = 8.0^2 = 64$, therefore $a = \sqrt[3]{64}\,\mathrm{AU} = 4.0\,\mathrm{AU}$.
