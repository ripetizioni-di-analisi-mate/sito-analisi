A satellite moves in a circular orbit around a spherical body with gravitational parameter $b=GM$. Given the distance from the center $r = 16\,\mathrm{m}$ and the orbital speed $v = 10\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the gravitational parameter $b$?

- $1600\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$
- $2400\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$
- $3200\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$
- $800\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$

## Explanation

The gravitational acceleration $GM/r^2$ is the centripetal acceleration $v^2/r$. In the stated units, the relation is $v = \dfrac{\sqrt{b}}{\sqrt{r}}$. Solving for the unknown gives $b = r \cdot v^{2}$. Substituting the given values yields $1600\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$.
