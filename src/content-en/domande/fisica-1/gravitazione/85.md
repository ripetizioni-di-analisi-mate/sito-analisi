A mass is outside a spherical body. Gravitational potential energy is zero at infinity. Given the mass $m = 3\,\mathrm{kg}$, the gravitational parameter $GM$ $b = 800\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$ and the distance from the center $r = 20\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the potential energy $U$?

- $-6\,\mathrm{J}$
- $120\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-120\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-240\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Integrating the gravitational field with infinity as reference gives potential $-GM/r$. The potential is multiplied by the test mass. In the stated units, the relation is $U = - \dfrac{b \cdot m}{r}$. Substituting the given values yields $-120\,\mathrm{J}$.
