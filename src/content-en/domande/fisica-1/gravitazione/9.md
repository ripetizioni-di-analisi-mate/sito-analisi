## Explanation

By the theorem on the **escape velocity**, $v_f = \sqrt{\dfrac{2GM}{R}}$, with the mass and the radius of Mars. We have $\dfrac{2GM}{R} = \dfrac{2 \cdot 4.28 \cdot 10^{13}}{3.39 \cdot 10^6}\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2} = 2.53 \cdot 10^7\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}$, so $v_f = 5.03 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Without the factor $2$ one would obtain the speed of the grazing circular orbit, $3.56 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{m/s}$, which is $\sqrt{2}$ times smaller.
