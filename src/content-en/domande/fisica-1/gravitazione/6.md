A satellite moves in a circular orbit at altitude $h = 1000\,\mathrm{km}$ above the Earth's surface, with $G M_T = 3.98 \cdot 10^{14}\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$ and $R_T = 6.37 \cdot 10^6\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the period of the orbit?

- $6.30 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $5.06 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.00 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3.15 \cdot 10^{2}\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The radius of the orbit is the distance from the center of the Earth, $r = R_T + h = 7.37 \cdot 10^6\,\mathrm{m}$. By the **speed and period in a circular orbit**, $T = 2\pi \sqrt{\dfrac{r^3}{G M_T}}$. We have $\dfrac{r^3}{G M_T} = \dfrac{4.00 \cdot 10^{20}}{3.98 \cdot 10^{14}}\,\mathrm{s^2} = 1.01 \cdot 10^6\,\mathrm{s^2}$, whose square root is about $1.00 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{s}$. Multiplying by $2\pi$ gives $T = 6.30 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{s}$, about $105$ minutes.
