A circular orbit has period $T$. Over a short interval the satellite sweeps out a circular sector of angle $\theta$, measured in radians. Given the swept angle $\theta = \pi\,\mathrm{rad}$ and the duration of the interval $t = 4\,\mathrm{h}$, what is the orbital period $T$?

- $\dfrac{4}{\pi}\,\mathrm{h}$
- $16\,\mathrm{h}$
- $8\,\mathrm{h}$
- $4\,\mathrm{h}$

## Explanation

The swept angle is the same fraction of $2\pi$ as the elapsed time is of the period. In the stated units, the relation is $t = \dfrac{T \cdot \theta}{2 \cdot \pi}$. Solving for the unknown gives $T = \dfrac{2 \cdot \pi \cdot t}{\theta}$. Substituting the given values yields $8\,\mathrm{h}$.
