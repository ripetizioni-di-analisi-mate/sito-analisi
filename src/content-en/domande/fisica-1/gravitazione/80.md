A probe of negligible mass moves from an inner circular orbit of radius $r$ to a circular orbit of radius $2r$. The gravitational parameter is $b=GM$. Given the inner orbital radius $r = 20\,\mathrm{m}$ and the increase in mechanical energy per unit mass $e = 120\,\mathrm{J/kg}$, what is the gravitational parameter $b$?

- $19200\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$
- $4800\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$
- $2400\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$
- $9600\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$

## Explanation

The mechanical energy per unit mass is $-GM/(2r)$. The final value is $-GM/(4r)$, giving a positive difference. In the stated units, the relation is $e = \dfrac{b}{4 \cdot r}$. Solving for the unknown gives $b = 4 \cdot e \cdot r$. Substituting the given values yields $9600\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$.
