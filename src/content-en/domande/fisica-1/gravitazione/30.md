With $g_0 = 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, $R_T = 6.37 \cdot 10^6\,\mathrm{m}$ and $G = 6.67 \cdot 10^{-11}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m^2/kg^2}$, what is the mean density of the Earth, taken as a sphere?

- $5.51 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $2.31 \cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $1.38 \cdot 10^{3}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
- $1.10 \cdot 10^{4}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

## Explanation

The mass of a sphere of mean density $\rho$ is $M = \rho\,\dfrac{4}{3}\pi R_T^3$. Substituting into $g_0 = \dfrac{G M}{R_T^2}$ gives
$$
g_0 = \frac{4}{3}\pi G \rho R_T ,
$$
hence
$$
\rho = \frac{3 g_0}{4\pi G R_T} = \frac{3 \cdot 9.81}{4\pi \cdot 6.67 \cdot 10^{-11} \cdot 6.37 \cdot 10^6}\,\mathrm{kg/m^3} = 5.51 \cdot 10^3\,\mathrm{kg/m^3} .
$$
The value $2.31 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ is the ratio of the mass to $R_T^3$, which is not the volume of the sphere.
