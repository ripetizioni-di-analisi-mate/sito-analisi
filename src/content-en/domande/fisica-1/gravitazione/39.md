A satellite in a circular orbit around a planet has kinetic energy $K = 5{,}0 \cdot 10^9\,\mathrm{J}$. What is its mechanical energy, with potential energy zero at infinite distance?

- $-1{,}0 \cdot 10^{10}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-5{,}0 \cdot 10^{9}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $5{,}0 \cdot 10^{9}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-2{,}5 \cdot 10^{9}\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The expressions for the **energy of a satellite in a circular orbit** give $K = \dfrac{GMm}{2r}$ and $U = -\dfrac{GMm}{r}$, so $U = -2K = -1{,}0 \cdot 10^{10}\,\mathrm{J}$. The mechanical energy is $E = K + U = -K$, therefore $E = -5{,}0 \cdot 10^9\,\mathrm{J}$. The value $-1{,}0 \cdot 10^{10}\,\mathrm{J}$ is the potential energy alone, without the kinetic energy.
