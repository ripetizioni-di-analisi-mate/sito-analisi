In base units of the International System, what is the unit of the universal gravitational constant $G$?

- $\mathrm{m^2/(kg\cdot s^2)}$
- $\mathrm{m^3/(kg\cdot s^2)}$
- $\mathrm{kg\cdot m^3/s^2}$
- $\mathrm{m^3/(kg^2\cdot s^2)}$

## Explanation

From the law of gravitation $F = G\,\dfrac{m_1 m_2}{r^2}$ we get $G = \dfrac{F r^2}{m_1 m_2}$, so $G$ is measured in $\mathrm{N\cdot m^2/kg^2}$.

In base units the newton is $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s^2}$. Substituting gives
$$
\mathrm{\frac{kg\cdot m}{s^2} \cdot \frac{m^2}{kg^2}} = \mathrm{\frac{m^3}{kg\cdot s^2}} .
$$
One kilogram in the numerator cancels one of the two in the denominator, therefore in the result the mass appears to the first power in the denominator.
