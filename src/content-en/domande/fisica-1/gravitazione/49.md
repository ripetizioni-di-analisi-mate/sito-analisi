A satellite moves in a circular orbit around a spherical body with gravitational parameter $b=GM$. The initial data are the gravitational parameter $b = 1600\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$ and the distance from the center $r = 16\,\mathrm{m}$. Change the distance from the center to $64\,\mathrm{m}$, keeping the other data fixed. What is the ratio of the final value of $v$ to its initial value?

- $2$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

The gravitational acceleration $GM/r^2$ is the centripetal acceleration $v^2/r$. The relation $v = \dfrac{\sqrt{b}}{\sqrt{r}}$ gives $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ before the change and $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ after it. The ratio is $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
