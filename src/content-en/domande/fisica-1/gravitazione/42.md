A probe is receding from a small planet. With potential taken as zero at infinity, instruments report specific kinetic energy $12\,\mathrm{MJ/kg}$ and specific potential energy $-8\,\mathrm{MJ/kg}$. With engines off and only the planet's gravity acting, what speed will remain at a very large distance?

- $\sqrt{8}\,\mathrm{km/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{km/s}$
- $\sqrt{24}\,\mathrm{km/s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{km/s}$

## Explanation

Specific mechanical energy is $12-8=4\,\mathrm{MJ/kg}$. At infinity it is entirely kinetic, so $v_\infty^2=8\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{m^2/s^2}$.
