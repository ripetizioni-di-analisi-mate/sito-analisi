A satellite moves in a circular orbit around a spherical body with gravitational parameter $b=GM$. Given the gravitational parameter $b = 1600\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$ and the distance from the center $r = 16\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the orbital speed $v$?

- $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10 \cdot \sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $5 \cdot \sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The gravitational acceleration $GM/r^2$ is the centripetal acceleration $v^2/r$. In the stated units, the relation is $v = \dfrac{\sqrt{b}}{\sqrt{r}}$. Substituting the given values yields $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
