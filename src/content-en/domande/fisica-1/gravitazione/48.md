A satellite moves in a circular orbit around a spherical body with gravitational parameter $b=GM$. Given the gravitational parameter $b = 1600\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$ and the orbital speed $v = 10\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the distance from the center $r$?

- $32\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $24\,\mathrm{m}$
- $16\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The gravitational acceleration $GM/r^2$ is the centripetal acceleration $v^2/r$. In the stated units, the relation is $v = \dfrac{\sqrt{b}}{\sqrt{r}}$. Solving for the unknown gives $r = \dfrac{b}{v^{2}}$. Substituting the given values yields $16\,\mathrm{m}$.
