A test mass is outside a spherical body. The central body's gravitational parameter is $GM=12000\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$. Given the mass $m = 3\,\mathrm{kg}$ and the distance from the center $r = 20\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the gravitational force magnitude $F$?

- $45\,\mathrm{N}$
- $180\,\mathrm{N}$
- $1800\,\mathrm{N}$
- $90\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Outside the spherical body the force is the same as that of a point mass at its center. The center-to-center distance is squared. In the stated units, the relation is $F = \dfrac{12000 \cdot m}{r^{2}}$. Substituting the given values yields $90\,\mathrm{N}$.
