A probe of negligible mass moves from an inner circular orbit of radius $r$ to a circular orbit of radius $2r$. The gravitational parameter is $b=GM$. Given the gravitational parameter $b = 9600\,\mathrm{m^3/s^2}$ and the increase in mechanical energy per unit mass $e = 120\,\mathrm{J/kg}$, what is the inner orbital radius $r$?

- $10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $40\,\mathrm{m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $80\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The mechanical energy per unit mass is $-GM/(2r)$. The final value is $-GM/(4r)$, giving a positive difference. In the stated units, the relation is $e = \dfrac{b}{4 \cdot r}$. Solving for the unknown gives $r = \dfrac{b}{4 \cdot e}$. Substituting the given values yields $20\,\mathrm{m}$.
