In the model of a spherical Earth with uniform density, what is the gravitational force of the Earth on a body of mass $m$ placed at the center of the Earth?

- It is zero
- It is equal to the weight $m g_0$ that the body has at the surface
- It is infinite, because the distance from the center is zero
- It is half of the weight $m g_0$ that the body has at the surface

## Explanation

By the **field of a homogeneous sphere**, inside the sphere the field is $g(r) = \dfrac{G M_T}{R_T^3}\,r$ and is proportional to the distance from the center. At the center $r = 0$, so the field is zero and the force $m\,g(0)$ is **zero**. The same result follows from symmetry, because the parts of the Earth attract the body in all directions with forces that balance one another. The formula $\dfrac{G M_T m}{r^2}$ holds only for external points, therefore it does not apply at the center.
