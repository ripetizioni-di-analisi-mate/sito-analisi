- $4.2\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The two ships are treated as equal point masses, $m = 5.0 \cdot 10^7\,\mathrm{kg}$, so in the **law of universal gravitation** the product of the masses is $m^2 = 2.5 \cdot 10^{15}\,\mathrm{kg^2}$. The distance appears squared, and $d^2 = 1.0 \cdot 10^4\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Therefore
$$
F = G\,\frac{m^2}{d^2} = 6.67 \cdot 10^{-11} \cdot \frac{2.5 \cdot 10^{15}}{1.0 \cdot 10^{4}}\,\mathrm{N} = 17\,\mathrm{N} .
$$
This is about the weight of a body of $1.7\,\mathrm{kg}$, negligible compared with the other forces acting on the ships.
