- $5.73 \cdot 10^{6}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.20 \cdot 10^{5}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6.37 \cdot 10^{5}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

At depth $d$ the distance from the center is $R_T - d$, and by the **field of a homogeneous sphere** inside the sphere the field is proportional to the distance from the center. Hence $g(d) = g_0 \left( 1 - \dfrac{d}{R_T} \right)$. The condition $g(d) = 0.90\,g_0$ gives $\dfrac{d}{R_T} = 0.10$, therefore $d = 0.10 \cdot 6.37 \cdot 10^6\,\mathrm{m} = 6.37 \cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{m}$. The value $5.73 \cdot 10^6\,\mathrm{m}$ is the distance of the point from the center, $0.90\,R_T$.
