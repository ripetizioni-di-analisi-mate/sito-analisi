Two point masses attract each other with a gravitational force of magnitude $F$. If both masses are doubled and the distance between the bodies is also doubled, what is the new magnitude of the force?

- $2F$
- $\dfrac{F}{2}$
- $F$
- $4F$

## Explanation

By the **law of universal gravitation**, $F = G\,\dfrac{m_1 m_2}{r^2}$. Doubling both masses makes the numerator $2m_1 \cdot 2m_2 = 4 m_1 m_2$, four times as large. Doubling the distance makes the denominator $(2r)^2 = 4r^2$, also four times as large. The two factors $4$ cancel, so the magnitude of the force **remains unchanged**.
