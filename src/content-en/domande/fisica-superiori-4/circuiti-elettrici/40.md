## Explanation

The entire potential difference between the nodes is across the battery in the middle branch. This leaves zero volts across the resistor, so no current flows in that branch.

$$
I_2=\frac{V_A-V_B-6\,\mathrm V}{3\,\Omega}=0\,\mathrm A.
$$
