An ideal $20\,\mathrm V$ source powers a $2\,\Omega$ resistor in series with a parallel combination of $4\,\Omega$ and $12\,\Omega$. Find the voltage across the parallel combination.

- $12\,\mathrm V$.
- $20\,\mathrm V$.
- $15\,\mathrm V$.
- $8\,\mathrm V$.

## Explanation

The total current first passes through the series resistor and then splits. The equivalent parallel resistance gives the voltage common to both branches.

$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{\mathrm p}&=\frac{4\cdot12}{4+12}\,\Omega=3\,\Omega,\\[4pt]
I&=\frac{20\,\mathrm V}{(2+3)\,\Omega}=4\,\mathrm A,\\[4pt]
V_{\mathrm p}&=(4\,\mathrm A)(3\,\Omega)=12\,\mathrm V.
\end{aligned}
$$
