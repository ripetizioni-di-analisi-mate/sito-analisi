A source with electromotive force $9\,\mathrm V$ and internal resistance $2\,\Omega$ supplies $1.5\,\mathrm A$. What is the terminal voltage?

- $6\,\mathrm V$.
- $12\,\mathrm V$.
- $3\,\mathrm V$.
- $9\,\mathrm V$.

## Explanation

The supply current also passes through the internal resistance. Subtract its three-volt drop from the electromotive force to obtain the load voltage.

$$
V=9\,\mathrm V-(2\,\Omega)(1.5\,\mathrm A)=6\,\mathrm V.
$$
