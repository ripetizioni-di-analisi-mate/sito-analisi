Two light bulbs are in series in one loop with a battery. One filament breaks. What current flows through the other bulb after the break?

- It retains its initial value.
- It is zero.
- It doubles.
- It halves.

## Explanation

The broken filament opens the only loop. The current through every point in the series circuit is therefore zero.
