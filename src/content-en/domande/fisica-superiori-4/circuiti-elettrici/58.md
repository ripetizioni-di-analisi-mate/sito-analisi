A source with electromotive force $12\,\mathrm V$ and internal resistance $2\,\Omega$ powers a $2\,\Omega$ load. What power reaches the load?

- $18\,\mathrm W$.
- $36\,\mathrm W$.
- $9\,\mathrm W$.
- $12\,\mathrm W$.

## Explanation

Half the electromotive force drops across the load and half across the internal resistance. Since both carry the same current, each dissipates half the produced power.

$$
I=\frac{12\,\mathrm V}{(2+2)\,\Omega}=3\,\mathrm A,
\qquad P_R=(3\,\mathrm A)^2(2\,\Omega)=18\,\mathrm W.
$$
