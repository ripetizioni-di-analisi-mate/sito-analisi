In the real-source model, $I_R$ is the current with load $R=r$, and $I_{\mathrm{cc}}$ is the theoretical value at zero load resistance. What is $\dfrac{I_{\mathrm{cc}}}{I_R}$?

- $1$.
- $4$.
- $0.5$.
- $2$.

## Explanation

In the theoretical zero-resistance case, only the internal resistance remains. Halving the total resistance doubles the current.

$$
I_R=\frac{\mathcal E}{2r},\qquad
I_{\mathrm{cc}}=\frac{\mathcal E}{r},\qquad
\frac{I_{\mathrm{cc}}}{I_R}=2.
$$
