A battery with internal resistance $2\,\Omega$ reads $12\,\mathrm V$ with an open circuit. With a resistor attached it reads $8\,\mathrm V$. Find the attached resistance.

- $4\,\Omega$.
- $2\,\Omega$.
- $6\,\Omega$.
- $8\,\Omega$.

## Explanation

The four-volt internal drop determines the current through the internal resistance. The same current flows through the load, across which eight volts is measured.

$$
I=\frac{(12-8)\,\mathrm V}{2\,\Omega}=2\,\mathrm A,
\qquad R=\frac{8\,\mathrm V}{2\,\mathrm A}=4\,\Omega.
$$
