Two parallel branches of $9\,\Omega$ and $18\,\Omega$ receive a total of $3\,\mathrm A$. How much current flows through the $9\,\Omega$ branch?

- $1.5\,\mathrm A$.
- $2\,\mathrm A$.
- $1\,\mathrm A$.
- $3\,\mathrm A$.

## Explanation

At a common voltage the branch with half the resistance carries twice the current. The current splits into three equal shares, two of which flow through the less resistive branch.

$$
I_9=2I_{18},\qquad I_9+I_{18}=3\,\mathrm A
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad I_9=2\,\mathrm A.
$$
