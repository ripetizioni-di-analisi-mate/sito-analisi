Two resistors in parallel across $12\,\mathrm V$ draw a total of $4\,\mathrm A$. One branch has resistance $4\,\Omega$. Find the resistance of the other branch.

- $16\,\Omega$.
- $12\,\Omega$.
- $4\,\Omega$.
- $3\,\Omega$.

## Explanation

The common voltage gives the current in the known branch. Kirchhoff's current law gives the other current by subtraction from the total. The unknown branch also has twelve volts across it.

$$
\begin{aligned}
I_1&=\frac{12\,\mathrm V}{4\,\Omega}=3\,\mathrm A,\\[4pt]
I_2&=4\,\mathrm A-3\,\mathrm A=1\,\mathrm A,\\[4pt]
R_2&=\frac{12\,\mathrm V}{1\,\mathrm A}=12\,\Omega.
\end{aligned}
$$
