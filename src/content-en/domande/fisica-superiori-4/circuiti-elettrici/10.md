A current of $2\,\mathrm{mA}$ enters a node and $5\,\mathrm{mA}$ leaves in a second branch. For the remaining branch, take the outward direction as positive. Find the signed current.

- $-7\,\mathrm{mA}$.
- $-3\,\mathrm{mA}$.
- $3\,\mathrm{mA}$.
- $7\,\mathrm{mA}$.

## Explanation

Since the unknown current is referenced outward, it adds to the five milliamperes already leaving. The negative sign shows that the third branch supplies current to the node.

$$
I=(2-5)\,\mathrm{mA}=-3\,\mathrm{mA}.
$$
