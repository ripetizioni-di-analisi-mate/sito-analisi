A real source supplies $0.50\,\mathrm A$ for $30\,\mathrm s$. Its internal resistance is $4\,\Omega$. How much energy is dissipated internally?

- $15\,\mathrm J$.
- $30\,\mathrm J$.
- $60\,\mathrm J$.
- $120\,\mathrm J$.

## Explanation

First calculate resistive heating power in the internal resistance alone. Since current is constant, multiply the resulting watt by elapsed time to obtain energy.

$$
E_r=I^2r\Delta t=(0.50\,\mathrm A)^2(4\,\Omega)(30\,\mathrm s)=30\,\mathrm J.
$$
