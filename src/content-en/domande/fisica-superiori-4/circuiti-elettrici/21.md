A $6\,\Omega$ resistor and a $12\,\Omega$ resistor are connected in parallel across an ideal $12\,\mathrm V$ battery. What power is dissipated in the $6\,\Omega$ branch?

- $48\,\mathrm W$.
- $24\,\mathrm W$.
- $72\,\mathrm W$.
- $12\,\mathrm W$.

## Explanation

The requested resistor has the full battery voltage across it. Its power depends on its own branch current, which Ohm's law can eliminate.

$$
P_6=\frac{(12\,\mathrm V)^2}{6\,\Omega}=24\,\mathrm W.
$$
