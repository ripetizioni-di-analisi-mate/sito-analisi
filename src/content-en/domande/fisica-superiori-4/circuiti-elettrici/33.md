A resistor is traversed from terminal $A$ to $B$. Positive conventional current flows from $B$ to $A$. What is the potential change $V_B-V_A$?

- $-RI$.
- $\dfrac{I}{R}$.
- $+RI$.
- $0$.

## Explanation

Current flows from higher to lower potential in a passive resistor. Traversing it opposite to $I$ therefore raises the potential by $RI$.
