A $500\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ capacitor is charged to $4\,\mathrm V$ and then fully discharged through a single resistor. How much energy does the resistor receive in total?

- $4\,\mathrm{mJ}$.
- $8\,\mathrm{mJ}$.
- $40\,\mathrm{mJ}$.
- $2\,\mathrm{mJ}$.

## Explanation

The battery is absent from the discharge circuit. The dissipated energy therefore equals the energy stored before the resistor was connected.

$$
E_R=U_0=\frac{1}{2}(500\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm F)(4\,\mathrm V)^2
=4\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm J.
$$
