A capacitor discharges with $\tau=4\,\mathrm s$. How long does it take for the stored energy to fall to one-half?

- $(2\ln 2)\,\mathrm s$.
- $2\,\mathrm s$.
- $(8\ln 2)\,\mathrm s$.
- $(4\ln 2)\,\mathrm s$.

## Explanation

Energy varies with the square of voltage, so the exponent contains twice the elapsed time divided by the time constant. Energy halves in half the voltage half-life.

$$
e^{-\frac{2t}{\tau}}=\frac{1}{2}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
t=\frac{\tau}{2}\ln 2=(2\ln 2)\,\mathrm s.
$$
