A $12\,\mathrm V$ source with internal resistance $2\,\Omega$ supplies two $4\,\Omega$ loads in parallel. What potential difference is present between the common load nodes?

- $12\,\mathrm V$.
- $8\,\mathrm V$.
- $4\,\mathrm V$.
- $6\,\mathrm V$.

## Explanation

The parallel combination is two ohms. The model loop therefore contains two equal equivalent resistances, one internal and one external, that split the ideal voltage in half.

$$
R_{\mathrm p}=2\,\Omega,\qquad
V_{\mathrm p}=(12\,\mathrm V)\frac{2}{2+2}=6\,\mathrm V.
$$
