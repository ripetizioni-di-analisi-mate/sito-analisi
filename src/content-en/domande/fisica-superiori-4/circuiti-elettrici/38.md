A current of $7\,\mathrm{mA}$ enters $A$. From $A$, $2\,\mathrm{mA}$ goes to a load and the rest goes to $B$. Two branches lead out of $B$, one carrying $2\,\mathrm{mA}$ outward. What current leaves through the other branch of $B$?

- $11\,\mathrm{mA}$.
- $5\,\mathrm{mA}$.
- $7\,\mathrm{mA}$.
- $3\,\mathrm{mA}$.

## Explanation

Start at the node with a known input and first output. The A–B connection carries the difference to B. Then apply the current balance at the second node.

$$
I_{AB}=(7-2)\,\mathrm{mA}=5\,\mathrm{mA},
\qquad I=(5-2)\,\mathrm{mA}=3\,\mathrm{mA}.
$$
