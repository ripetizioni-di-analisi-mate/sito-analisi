The graph shows the voltage of a real source as its supply current changes. What electromotive force does it indicate?

::grafico{f="12-2*x" xmin=0 xmax=5 ymin=0 ymax=14 nomi="I,V" punti="0,12,pieno;2,8,pieno;4,4,pieno" titolo="Terminal voltage in volts versus current in amperes."}

- $2\,\mathrm V$.
- $12\,\mathrm V$.
- $8\,\mathrm V$.
- $4\,\mathrm V$.

## Explanation

At zero current, $rI$ vanishes. The intercept at the left of the graph therefore gives the electromotive force.

$$
\mathcal E=V(0)=12\,\mathrm V.
$$
