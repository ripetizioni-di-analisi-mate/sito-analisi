A $16\,\mathrm V$ divider has two $6\,\mathrm{k\Omega}$ resistors in series. A $3\,\mathrm{k\Omega}$ load is connected across the output resistor. Find the load voltage.

- $12\,\mathrm V$.
- $16\,\mathrm V$.
- $8\,\mathrm V$.
- $4\,\mathrm V$.

## Explanation

The output resistor and load share both terminals, so their parallel equivalent replaces the lower resistance in the divider. The unloaded reading of eight volts does not apply to this connection.

$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{\mathrm p}&=\frac{6\cdot3}{6+3}\,\mathrm{k\Omega}=2\,\mathrm{k\Omega},\\[4pt]
V&=(16\,\mathrm V)\frac{2}{6+2}=4\,\mathrm V.
\end{aligned}
$$
