A capacitor at $15\,\mathrm V$ discharges with $\tau=2\,\mathrm s$. Find $V_C$ after $(2\ln 3)\,\mathrm s$.

- $10\,\mathrm V$.
- $\dfrac{5}{3}\,\mathrm V$.
- $5\,\mathrm V$.
- $15\,\mathrm V$.

## Explanation

Divide the elapsed time by the time constant. The exponent is therefore minus the logarithm of three, and the voltage is one-third of its initial value.

$$
V_C=(15\,\mathrm V)e^{-\ln 3}=\frac{15}{3}\,\mathrm V=5\,\mathrm V.
$$
