The graph shows the voltage across a capacitor while it charges. The marked point is at about $0.632$ of the final voltage. What is the time constant?

::grafico{f="10*(1-exp(-x/2))" xmin=0 xmax=8 ymin=0 ymax=11 nomi="t,V_C" punti="2,6.3212,pieno" asintoti="y=10" titolo="Capacitor voltage in volts versus time in seconds."}

- $6\,\mathrm s$.
- $8\,\mathrm s$.
- $4\,\mathrm s$.
- $2\,\mathrm s$.

## Explanation

This fraction is reached when the exponent in the charging law is minus one. Read the time coordinate of the marked point.

$$
\frac{V_C(\tau)}{\mathcal E}=1-e^{-1}\simeq0.632
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \tau=2\,\mathrm s.
$$
