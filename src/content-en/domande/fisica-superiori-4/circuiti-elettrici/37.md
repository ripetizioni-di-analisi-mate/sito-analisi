An ideal voltmeter is connected across a divider's output resistor. What allows the unloaded-divider formula to remain valid?

- The current in the voltmeter equals the current in the series combination.
- The voltmeter imposes a voltage equal to the battery voltage.
- An ideal voltmeter shorts the output.
- An ideal voltmeter draws zero current.

## Explanation

An ideal voltmeter has infinite resistance. The current in its branch is zero, so the two divider resistors carry the same current.
