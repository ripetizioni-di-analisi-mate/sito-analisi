A real battery powers $R=9\,\Omega$ and has an internal resistance of $r=3\,\Omega$. What percentage of the power produced by the ideal part reaches the load?

- $75\,\%$.
- $25\,\%$.
- $50\,\%$.
- $100\,\%$.

## Explanation

The two resistances carry the same current, so the power fraction is the ratio of load resistance to total resistance.

$$
\frac{P_R}{P_{\mathrm{gen}}}=\frac{I^2R}{I^2(R+r)}
=\frac{9}{9+3}=75\%.
$$
