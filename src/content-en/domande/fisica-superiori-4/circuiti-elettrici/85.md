A capacitor at $10\,\mathrm V$ is connected across $2\,\mathrm{k\Omega}$. What is the initial power in the resistor?

- $5\,\mathrm{mW}$.
- $25\,\mathrm{mW}$.
- $50\,\mathrm{mW}$.
- $100\,\mathrm{mW}$.

## Explanation

At closure the capacitor's ten volts are applied across the resistor. Their square divided by the resistance gives the instantaneous power.

$$
P(0^+)=\frac{(10\,\mathrm V)^2}{2000\,\Omega}=0.050\,\mathrm W=50\,\mathrm{mW}.
$$
