An unknown node is connected to a point held at $12\,\mathrm V$ through $2\,\Omega$ and to a point at $0\,\mathrm V$ through $4\,\Omega$. What is the node potential?

- $8\,\mathrm V$.
- $12\,\mathrm V$.
- $6\,\mathrm V$.
- $4\,\mathrm V$.

## Explanation

The node has only two branches. The current entering from the twelve-volt point must equal the current leaving toward the reference.

$$
\frac{12\,\mathrm V-V}{2\,\Omega}=\frac{V}{4\,\Omega}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad V=8\,\mathrm V.
$$
