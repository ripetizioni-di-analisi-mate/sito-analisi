In an RC discharge, what fraction of the capacitor's energy remains after one interval $\tau$?

- $1-e^{-1}$.
- $1-e^{-2}$.
- $e^{-2}$.
- $e^{-1}$.

## Explanation

After $\tau$, the voltage is $e^{-1}V_0$. Since $U=\dfrac{1}{2}CV_C^2$, the energy ratio is the square of the voltage ratio.
