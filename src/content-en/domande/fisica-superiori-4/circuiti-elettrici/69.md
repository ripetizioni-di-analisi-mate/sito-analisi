An initially uncharged $200\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ capacitor charges from $10\,\mathrm V$. Which value is nearest to the charge at $t=\tau$? Use $e^{-1}\simeq0.37$.

- $0.74\,\mathrm{mC}$.
- $2\,\mathrm{mC}$.
- $0.63\,\mathrm{mC}$.
- $1.26\,\mathrm{mC}$.

## Explanation

After one time constant, the capacitor has reached about sixty-three percent of its final voltage. Multiplying that voltage by the capacitance gives the charge on one plate.

$$
\begin{aligned}
V_C &\simeq (10\,\mathrm V)(1-0.37)=6.3\,\mathrm V,\\[4pt]
q &\simeq (200\,\mathrm{\mu F})(6.3\,\mathrm V)
=1.26\,\mathrm{mC}.
\end{aligned}
$$
