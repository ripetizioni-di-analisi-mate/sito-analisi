A $6\,\Omega$ resistor and a $12\,\Omega$ resistor are connected between the same two nodes. Find the equivalent resistance.

- $8\,\Omega$.
- $4\,\Omega$.
- $18\,\Omega$.
- $9\,\Omega$.

## Explanation

The branches are in parallel, so their currents add at the common voltage. The resulting resistance must be below both branch resistances.

$$
R_{\mathrm{eq}}=\frac{6\cdot12}{6+12}\,\Omega=4\,\Omega.
$$
