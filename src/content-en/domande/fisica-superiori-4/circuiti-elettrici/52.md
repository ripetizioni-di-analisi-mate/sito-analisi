While a battery with $\mathcal E=12\,\mathrm V$ and $r=0.5\,\Omega$ is charged, $4\,\mathrm A$ enters its positive terminal. What is its terminal voltage?

- $14\,\mathrm V$.
- $12\,\mathrm V$.
- $10\,\mathrm V$.
- $2\,\mathrm V$.

## Explanation

Traversing the battery from positive to negative gives both the ideal-source drop and the internal-resistance drop. The charger has to provide their sum.

$$
V=\mathcal E+rI=12\,\mathrm V+(0.5\,\Omega)(4\,\mathrm A)=14\,\mathrm V.
$$
