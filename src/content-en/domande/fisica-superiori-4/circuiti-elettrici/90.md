The same capacitor first charges from zero toward $\mathcal E$ through $R$. In a second trial it discharges from $\mathcal E$ through the same $R$. Compare the times to reach $\dfrac{\mathcal E}{2}$.

- The discharging time is twice as long.
- The two times are equal.
- Both times equal τ.
- The charging time is twice as long.

## Explanation

For charging, set $1-e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}}=\dfrac{1}{2}$. For discharging, set $e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}}=\dfrac{1}{2}$. Both equations give $\tau\ln 2$ because $R$ and $C$ are the same.
