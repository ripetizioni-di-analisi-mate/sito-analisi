Current enters the positive terminal of a rechargeable battery modeled by an ideal source and a series internal resistance. How is incoming power accounted for?

- The ideal part delivers power to the external circuit.
- All power is dissipated in the internal resistance.
- The internal resistance returns energy to the ideal source.
- Some is absorbed by the ideal source and some is dissipated internally.

## Explanation

The ideal part of the battery absorbs $\mathcal E I$. The term $I^2r$ is positive and gives the energy dissipated as heat per second.
