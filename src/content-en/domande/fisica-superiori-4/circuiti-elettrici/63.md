In one loop, a battery supplies $30\,\mathrm W$ while a second battery absorbs $12\,\mathrm W$. The only other component is a resistor. What power does it dissipate?

- $30\,\mathrm W$.
- $18\,\mathrm W$.
- $42\,\mathrm W$.
- $12\,\mathrm W$.

## Explanation

The second battery receives part of the power supplied by the first. By energy balance, the resistor dissipates what remains.

$$
P_R=(30-12)\,\mathrm W=18\,\mathrm W.
$$
