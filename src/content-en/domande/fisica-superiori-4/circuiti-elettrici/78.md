At the start of discharge a capacitor stores $4\,\mathrm{mJ}$. How much energy has been dissipated in the resistor when the voltage has halved?

- $3\,\mathrm{mJ}$.
- $1\,\mathrm{mJ}$.
- $2\,\mathrm{mJ}$.
- $4\,\mathrm{mJ}$.

## Explanation

Halving the voltage leaves one-fourth of the stored energy. In the ideal model the resistor receives the energy that the capacitor has lost.

$$
U=(4\,\mathrm{mJ})\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^2=1\,\mathrm{mJ},
\qquad E_{\mathrm{diss}}=4\,\mathrm{mJ}-1\,\mathrm{mJ}=3\,\mathrm{mJ}.
$$
