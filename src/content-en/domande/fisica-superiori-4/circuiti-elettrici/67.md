An RC circuit has resistance $30\,\mathrm{k\Omega}$ and a measured time constant of $6\,\mathrm s$. What is its capacitance?

- $500\,\mathrm{\mu F}$.
- $180\,\mathrm{\mu F}$.
- $50\,\mathrm{\mu F}$.
- $200\,\mathrm{\mu F}$.

## Explanation

The measured time gives the product of the two quantities, so divide by the resistance. One farad is one million microfarads.

$$
C=\frac{\tau}{R}=\frac{6\,\mathrm s}{30000\,\Omega}
=2\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm F=200\,\mathrm{\mu F}.
$$
