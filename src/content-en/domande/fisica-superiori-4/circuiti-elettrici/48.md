The terminal voltage is $12\,\mathrm V$ at $1\,\mathrm A$ and $8\,\mathrm V$ at $3\,\mathrm A$. In the constant-internal-resistance model, what voltage would be measured with an open circuit?

- $16\,\mathrm V$.
- $12\,\mathrm V$.
- $14\,\mathrm V$.
- $10\,\mathrm V$.

## Explanation

Find the drop per ampere from the difference between the measurements. Going back from one ampere to zero then restores two volts.

$$
r=\frac{(12-8)\,\mathrm V}{(3-1)\,\mathrm A}=2\,\Omega,
\qquad \mathcal E=12\,\mathrm V+(2\,\Omega)(1\,\mathrm A)=14\,\mathrm V.
$$
