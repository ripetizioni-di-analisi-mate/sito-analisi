An RC circuit has $R=2\,\mathrm{k\Omega}$ and $C=150\,\mathrm{\mu F}$. What is the time constant?

- $0.15\,\mathrm s$.
- $3\,\mathrm s$.
- $0.3\,\mathrm s$.
- $0.075\,\mathrm s$.

## Explanation

Multiply resistance and capacitance in SI units. The kilo and micro prefixes leave a factor of one thousand in the denominator.

$$
\tau=RC=(2\cdot10^3\,\Omega)(150\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm F)
=0.30\,\mathrm s.
$$
