The mesh currents are $3\,\mathrm A$ and $1\,\mathrm A$. They contribute in opposite directions through a shared $5\,\Omega$ resistor. What is the magnitude of the voltage drop across it?

- $5\,\mathrm V$.
- $25\,\mathrm V$.
- $10\,\mathrm V$.
- $20\,\mathrm V$.

## Explanation

In the shared branch, the physical current is the algebraic sum of the two contributions. Because their directions are opposite, its magnitude is their difference.

$$
\lvert I\rvert=(3-1)\,\mathrm A=2\,\mathrm A,
\qquad \lvert\Delta V\rvert=(5\,\Omega)(2\,\mathrm A)=10\,\mathrm V.
$$
