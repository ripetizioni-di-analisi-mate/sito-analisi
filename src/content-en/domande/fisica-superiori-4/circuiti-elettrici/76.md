During discharge, the measured voltage is $9\,\mathrm V$ at $2\,\mathrm s$ and $3\,\mathrm V$ at $6\,\mathrm s$. What is the time constant?

- $\dfrac{4}{\ln 3}\,\mathrm s$.
- $(4\ln 3)\,\mathrm s$.
- $\dfrac{6}{\ln 3}\,\mathrm s$.
- $\dfrac{2}{\ln 3}\,\mathrm s$.

## Explanation

The ratio of the readings eliminates the voltage at the instant of switching. The exponent contains the interval between the readings, which is four seconds.

$$
\frac{3}{9}
=e^{-\frac{(6-2)\,\mathrm s}{\tau}}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \tau=\frac{4}{\ln 3}\,\mathrm s.
$$
