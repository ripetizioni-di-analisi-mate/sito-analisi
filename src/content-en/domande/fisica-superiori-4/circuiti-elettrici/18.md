Along the current direction through two resistors in series, the drops are $5\,\mathrm V$ across $R_1$ and $10\,\mathrm V$ across $R_2$. What is $\dfrac{R_2}{R_1}$?

- $2$.
- $0.5$.
- $3$.
- $1.5$.

## Explanation

The same current appears in both drops. Dividing one by the other eliminates this unknown.

$$
\frac{R_2}{R_1}=\frac{IR_2}{IR_1}=\frac{10}{5}=2.
$$
