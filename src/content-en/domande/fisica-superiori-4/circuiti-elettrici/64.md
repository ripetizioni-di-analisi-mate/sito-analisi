A battery modeled as an ideal source in series with an internal resistance is disconnected from its load. What are the terminal voltage and the power dissipated in the internal resistance?

- $V=0$ and $P_r=0$.
- $V=\mathcal E$ and $P_r=0$.
- $V=\mathcal E$ and $P_r=\dfrac{\mathcal E^2}{r}$.
- $V=0$ and $P_r=\dfrac{\mathcal E^2}{r}$.

## Explanation

With an open circuit, current is zero. In $V=\mathcal E-rI$ and $P_r=I^2r$, the terms that depend on $I$ vanish.
