While charging, a capacitor has $V_C=8\,\mathrm V$ and $1\,\mathrm{mA}$ flows in the charging direction through the $4\,\mathrm{k\Omega}$ resistor. What is the ideal source voltage?

- $12\,\mathrm V$.
- $4\,\mathrm V$.
- $16\,\mathrm V$.
- $8\,\mathrm V$.

## Explanation

The battery maintains the sum of the capacitor voltage and the resistor drop. At that instant, the resistor has four volts across it.

$$
\mathcal E=V_C+RI=8\,\mathrm V+
(4\,\mathrm{k\Omega})(1\,\mathrm{mA})=12\,\mathrm V.
$$
