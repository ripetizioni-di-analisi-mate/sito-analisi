A loop has opposing ideal sources of $14\,\mathrm V$ and $6\,\mathrm V$ and a total resistance of $4\,\Omega$. The direction driven by the $6\,\mathrm V$ source is taken as positive. What is the signed current?

- $5\,\mathrm A$.
- $2\,\mathrm A$.
- $-2\,\mathrm A$.
- $-5\,\mathrm A$.

## Explanation

In the chosen direction, the six-volt source raises the potential and the fourteen-volt source lowers it. The actual current is driven by the larger electromotive force.

$$
6\,\mathrm V-14\,\mathrm V-(4\,\Omega)I=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad I=-2\,\mathrm A.
$$
