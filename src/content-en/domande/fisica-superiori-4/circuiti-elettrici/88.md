Two identical capacitors at the same initial voltage discharge through different resistors. In the second case, $R$ is doubled. Compare the initial current and total dissipated energy.

- In the second, current and energy are both doubled.
- The second has the same initial current and twice the energy.
- The second has half the initial current and the same total energy.
- In the second, current and energy are both halved.

## Explanation

The initial current has magnitude $\dfrac{V_0}{R}$, so it halves in the second case. Both capacitors store the same initial energy, all of which is eventually dissipated in the resistor.
