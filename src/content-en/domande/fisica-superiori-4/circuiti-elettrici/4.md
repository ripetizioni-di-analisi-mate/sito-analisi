An unloaded divider supplied with $15\,\mathrm V$ uses $R_1=2\,\mathrm{k\Omega}$ and $R_2=3\,\mathrm{k\Omega}$. Find the voltage across $R_1$.

- $6\,\mathrm V$.
- $9\,\mathrm V$.
- $10\,\mathrm V$.
- $15\,\mathrm V$.

## Explanation

The current is common, so the fraction of voltage across R₁ matches the fraction of total resistance represented by R₁. Kilohms cancel in the ratio.

$$
V_1=(15\,\mathrm V)\frac{2}{2+3}=6\,\mathrm V.
$$
