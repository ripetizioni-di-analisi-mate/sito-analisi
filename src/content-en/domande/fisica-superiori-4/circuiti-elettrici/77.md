A capacitor discharges from $16\,\mathrm V$ through $4\,\mathrm{k\Omega}$. What is the current magnitude at $t=\tau\ln 2$?

- $8\,\mathrm{mA}$.
- $1\,\mathrm{mA}$.
- $2\,\mathrm{mA}$.
- $4\,\mathrm{mA}$.

## Explanation

The voltage has halved after the stated time. The resistor connects across the capacitor plates, so it has the same eight volts across it at that instant.

$$
V_C=(16\,\mathrm V)e^{-\ln 2}=8\,\mathrm V,
\qquad I_{\mathrm{mod}}=\frac{8\,\mathrm V}{4\,\mathrm{k\Omega}}=2\,\mathrm{mA}.
$$
