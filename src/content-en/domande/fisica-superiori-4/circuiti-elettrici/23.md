Adding a resistor in parallel with a load on an ideal $9\,\mathrm V$ source increases the total current by $1.5\,\mathrm A$. What resistance was added?

- $13.5\,\Omega$.
- $1.5\,\Omega$.
- $9\,\Omega$.
- $6\,\Omega$.

## Explanation

The original load still has the same voltage. The increase in current therefore equals the current through the new branch.

$$
R=\frac{9\,\mathrm V}{1.5\,\mathrm A}=6\,\Omega.
$$
