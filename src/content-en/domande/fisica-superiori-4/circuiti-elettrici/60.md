A resistor bridge has two parallel branches. The first has $2\,\Omega$ above its midpoint and $3\,\Omega$ below. The second has $4\,\Omega$ above its midpoint and an unknown resistance below. The midpoint potentials are equal at nonzero supply voltage. What is the unknown resistance?

- $6\,\Omega$.
- $9\,\Omega$.
- $3\,\Omega$.
- $12\,\Omega$.

## Explanation

The midpoints have the same fraction of the total voltage. Since the upper resistance doubles in the second branch, the lower resistance must also double.

$$
\frac{3}{2+3}=\frac{R_x}{4\,\Omega+R_x}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad R_x=6\,\Omega.
$$
