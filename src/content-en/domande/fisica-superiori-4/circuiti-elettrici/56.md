A real battery supplies a resistive load. As the load resistance increases, what changes are observed at the terminals?

- The current falls and the voltage rises.
- Both current and voltage fall.
- The current rises and the voltage falls.
- Both current and voltage rise.

## Explanation

The current $I=\dfrac{\mathcal E}{R+r}$ falls. The internal drop $rI$ shrinks, so the terminal voltage rises toward the open-circuit voltage.
