An ideal $24\,\mathrm V$ source powers $6\,\Omega$ in series with a group of parallel resistors. An ideal wire is added between the two nodes of the group. Find the new current through the $6\,\Omega$ resistor.

- $3\,\mathrm A$.
- $4\,\mathrm A$.
- $0\,\mathrm A$.
- $2\,\mathrm A$.

## Explanation

The wire reduces the potential difference across the entire group to zero. The full source voltage is therefore applied across the six-ohm series resistor.

$$
I=\frac{24\,\mathrm V}{6\,\Omega}=4\,\mathrm A.
$$
