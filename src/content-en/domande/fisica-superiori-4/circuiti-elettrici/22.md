A $3\,\Omega$ resistor and a $6\,\Omega$ resistor are in parallel across an ideal $12\,\mathrm V$ source. The $3\,\Omega$ branch opens. What current does the source now supply?

- $4\,\mathrm A$.
- $1\,\mathrm A$.
- $6\,\mathrm A$.
- $2\,\mathrm A$.

## Explanation

The circuit still has a closed path through the six-ohm branch. Since the source is ideal, this branch still receives twelve volts and alone determines total current.

$$
I_{\mathrm{tot}}=\frac{12\,\mathrm V}{6\,\Omega}=2\,\mathrm A.
$$
