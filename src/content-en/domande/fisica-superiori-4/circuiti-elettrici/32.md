An ideal $12\,\mathrm V$ source powers a $6\,\Omega$ series resistor and two parallel branches of $6\,\Omega$ each. One branch opens. What is the new voltage across the surviving branch?

- $12\,\mathrm V$.
- $2\,\mathrm V$.
- $6\,\mathrm V$.
- $4\,\mathrm V$.

## Explanation

Opening the branch leaves two equal resistors in series. The voltage now divides in half. The four volts across the parallel branches applied to the initial circuit.

$$
V_{\mathrm{ramo}}=(12\,\mathrm V)\frac{6}{6+6}=6\,\mathrm V.
$$
