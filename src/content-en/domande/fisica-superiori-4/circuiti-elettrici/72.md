An RC circuit has $\tau=3\,\mathrm s$. The capacitor is initially uncharged. How long does it take for its voltage to reach three-quarters of the final value?

- $(3\ln 3)\,\mathrm s$.
- $(3\ln 4)\,\mathrm s$.
- $3\,\mathrm s$.
- $(3\ln 2)\,\mathrm s$.

## Explanation

Three-quarters is the fraction already acquired by the capacitor. To find the time, use the remaining fraction in the exponential.

$$
1-e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}}=\frac{3}{4},\qquad
e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}}=\frac{1}{4}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad t=(3\ln 4)\,\mathrm s.
$$
