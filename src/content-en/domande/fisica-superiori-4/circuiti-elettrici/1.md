An ideal $12\,\mathrm V$ battery powers $4\,\Omega$ and $8\,\Omega$ resistors in series. Find the current.

- $1.5\,\mathrm A$.
- $3\,\mathrm A$.
- $1\,\mathrm A$.
- $2.4\,\mathrm A$.

## Explanation

The same current flows through both resistors, so divide the battery voltage by the sum of the resistances.

$$
I=\frac{12\,\mathrm V}{(4+8)\,\Omega}=1\,\mathrm A.
$$
