Nodes $A$ and $B$ are both at $6\,\mathrm V$ relative to the reference. A $2\,\Omega$ resistor is inserted between them. What current passes through the resistor if their potentials are held fixed?

- $6\,\mathrm A$.
- $3\,\mathrm A$.
- $0\,\mathrm A$.
- $1\,\mathrm A$.

## Explanation

Ohm's law uses the potential difference between the two terminals. Since the potentials agree, the voltage drop is zero.

$$
I=\frac{V_A-V_B}{R}=\frac{6\,\mathrm V-6\,\mathrm V}{2\,\Omega}=0\,\mathrm A.
$$
