Start at $A$ at $0\,\mathrm V$ and cross a $12\,\mathrm V$ battery from negative to positive. Then cross a $5\,\Omega$ resistor in the direction of its $1\,\mathrm A$ current. What potential is reached?

- $17\,\mathrm V$.
- $5\,\mathrm V$.
- $12\,\mathrm V$.
- $7\,\mathrm V$.

## Explanation

Add the changes in the order in which the branch is traversed. The battery raises the potential, and the resistor lowers it when traversed with the current.

$$
V=0\,\mathrm V+12\,\mathrm V-(1\,\mathrm A)(5\,\Omega)=7\,\mathrm V.
$$
