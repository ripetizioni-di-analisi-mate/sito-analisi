A capacitor at $6\,\mathrm V$ is discharging through $500\,\Omega$. That resistor is switched out for $2\,\mathrm{k\Omega}$ instantaneously. What is the current magnitude just after the change?

- $0\,\mathrm{mA}$.
- $12\,\mathrm{mA}$.
- $3\,\mathrm{mA}$.
- $0.75\,\mathrm{mA}$.

## Explanation

The capacitor voltage remains six volts at switching. The current uses the new branch resistance.

$$
I(0^+)=\frac{6\,\mathrm V}{2\,\mathrm{k\Omega}}=3\,\mathrm{mA}.
$$
