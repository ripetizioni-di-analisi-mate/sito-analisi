An ideal $12\,\mathrm V$ source is part of a real battery model with $r=3\,\Omega$. A load $R=1\,\Omega$ connects to the terminals. What does an ideal voltmeter read across the load?

- $4\,\mathrm V$.
- $3\,\mathrm V$.
- $12\,\mathrm V$.
- $9\,\mathrm V$.

## Explanation

The internal resistance is three times the load resistance. The voltage across the load is one-fourth of the ideal source voltage.

$$
I=\frac{12\,\mathrm V}{(3+1)\,\Omega}=3\,\mathrm A,
\qquad V=(3\,\mathrm A)(1\,\Omega)=3\,\mathrm V.
$$
