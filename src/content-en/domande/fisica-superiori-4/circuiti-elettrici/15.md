In a series circuit, resistor $R_1$ dissipates $4\,\mathrm W$ and $R_2=3R_1$. What power does $R_2$ dissipate?

- $36\,\mathrm W$.
- $4\,\mathrm W$.
- $\dfrac{4}{3}\,\mathrm W$.
- $12\,\mathrm W$.

## Explanation

The same current passes through both resistors, so the powers are proportional to their resistances.

$$
P_2=I^2R_2=3I^2R_1=3P_1=12\,\mathrm W.
$$
