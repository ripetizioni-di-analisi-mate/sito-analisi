Two RC circuits use the same ideal battery and the same initially uncharged capacitor. In the second circuit, resistance is doubled. Compare the final voltages and the times to reach half the final voltage.

- Same final voltage and half the time in the second.
- Half the final voltage and twice the time.
- Twice the final voltage and the same time.
- Same final voltage and twice the time in the second.

## Explanation

At steady state the current is zero, so the capacitor voltage equals the battery voltage in both circuits. At half voltage, $t=\tau\ln 2$. Doubling $R$ doubles $\tau$ and the required time.
