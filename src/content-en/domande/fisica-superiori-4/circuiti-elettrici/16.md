Two resistors in series draw $2\,\mathrm A$ from an ideal $20\,\mathrm V$ source. One resistor is $3\,\Omega$. What is the other resistance?

- $13\,\Omega$.
- $7\,\Omega$.
- $10\,\Omega$.
- $3\,\Omega$.

## Explanation

The voltage-to-current ratio applies to the whole series circuit. Subtract the known three ohms to obtain the unknown component.

$$
R_2=\frac{20\,\mathrm V}{2\,\mathrm A}-3\,\Omega=7\,\Omega.
$$
