A battery reads $12\,\mathrm V$ with an open circuit. When it supplies $2\,\mathrm A$, its terminal voltage falls to $8\,\mathrm V$. What is its internal resistance?

- $0.5\,\Omega$.
- $6\,\Omega$.
- $4\,\Omega$.
- $2\,\Omega$.

## Explanation

The open-circuit measurement gives the electromotive force. The difference between the measurements is the internal drop at the stated current.

$$
r=\frac{12\,\mathrm V-8\,\mathrm V}{2\,\mathrm A}=2\,\Omega.
$$
