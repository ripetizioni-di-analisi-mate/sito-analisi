A capacitor has been connected to $12\,\mathrm V$ for a long time. At $t=10\,\mathrm s$ it is disconnected and connected across a resistor giving $\tau=2\,\mathrm s$. What is $V_C$ at $t=(10+2\ln 2)\,\mathrm s$?

- $3\,\mathrm V$.
- $12\,\mathrm V$.
- $6\,\mathrm V$.
- $9\,\mathrm V$.

## Explanation

Discharge begins at ten seconds. Use the time since switching and the twelve volts the capacitor had at that instant.

$$
V_C=(12\,\mathrm V)e^{-\frac{(2\ln 2)\,\mathrm s}{2\,\mathrm s}}
=6\,\mathrm V.
$$
