An uncharged capacitor is connected to an ideal $9\,\mathrm V$ battery through $3\,\mathrm{k\Omega}$. What is the current just after connection?

- $3\,\mathrm{mA}$.
- $0\,\mathrm{mA}$.
- $1.5\,\mathrm{mA}$.
- $6\,\mathrm{mA}$.

## Explanation

The capacitor voltage starts at zero. The full battery voltage is therefore across the resistor, which sets the initial current.

$$
I(0^+)=\frac{9\,\mathrm V-0\,\mathrm V}{3\,\mathrm{k\Omega}}=3\,\mathrm{mA}.
$$
