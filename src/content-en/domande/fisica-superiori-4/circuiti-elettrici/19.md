An ideal battery powers two resistors in series. The battery voltage doubles and the resistances are constant. By what factor does the power dissipated in one resistor change?

- It multiplies by two.
- It multiplies by four.
- It multiplies by eight.
- It is divided by two.

## Explanation

By Ohm's law, doubling the voltage at fixed equivalent resistance doubles $I$. Since each resistor dissipates $I^2R$, the power becomes four times its initial value.
