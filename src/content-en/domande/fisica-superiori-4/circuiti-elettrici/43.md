Opposing ideal sources of $12\,\mathrm V$ and $6\,\mathrm V$ are in series with $3\,\Omega$. What is the power absorbed by the $6\,\mathrm V$ source?

- $6\,\mathrm W$.
- $12\,\mathrm W$.
- $24\,\mathrm W$.
- $36\,\mathrm W$.

## Explanation

The larger source drives the current. Current enters the smaller source at its positive terminal, so voltage times current is absorbed power.

$$
I=\frac{(12-6)\,\mathrm V}{3\,\Omega}=2\,\mathrm A,
\qquad P=(6\,\mathrm V)(2\,\mathrm A)=12\,\mathrm W.
$$
