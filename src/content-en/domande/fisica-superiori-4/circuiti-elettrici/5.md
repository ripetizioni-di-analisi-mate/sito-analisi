Currents of $2\,\mathrm A$ and $3\,\mathrm A$ enter a node. One outgoing current is $1\,\mathrm A$. Find the current in the other outgoing branch.

::schema{riferimento=0 punti="0,0,A,pieno" linee="-2.5,0 2.5,0;0,0 0,2;0,0 0,-2" frecce="-2.45,0.45,-0.9,0.45,2 A;0.55,1.8,0.55,0.45,3 A;0.9,0.45,2.45,0.45,1 A;0.55,-0.4,0.55,-1.8,I" titolo="Two currents entering and two leaving node A"}

- $2\,\mathrm A$.
- $5\,\mathrm A$.
- $6\,\mathrm A$.
- $4\,\mathrm A$.

## Explanation

Five amperes enter in total. Since charge does not accumulate at the node, five amperes must leave through the two outgoing branches together.

$$
I=2\,\mathrm A+3\,\mathrm A-1\,\mathrm A=4\,\mathrm A.
$$
