During charging from zero, what relation always holds between $\dfrac{V_C(t)}{\mathcal E}$ and $\dfrac{I(t)}{I(0^+)}$?

- Their sum is one.
- Their difference is constant.
- The first always equals the second.
- Their product is one.

## Explanation

The loop equation gives $V_C+RI=\mathcal E$. Since $RI(0^+)=\mathcal E$, dividing by $\mathcal E$ shows that the two fractions add to one.
