Two identical $6\,\mathrm V$ batteries, each with $1\,\Omega$ internal resistance, are connected in series in the same direction. They supply a $4\,\Omega$ load. What is the current?

- $2.4\,\mathrm A$.
- $3\,\mathrm A$.
- $2\,\mathrm A$.
- $1\,\mathrm A$.

## Explanation

The electromotive forces add because the batteries drive current in the same direction. The current also passes through both internal resistances.

$$
I=\frac{(6+6)\,\mathrm V}{(1+1+4)\,\Omega}=2\,\mathrm A.
$$
