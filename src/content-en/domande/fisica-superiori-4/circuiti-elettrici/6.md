A battery has an electromotive force of $15\,\mathrm V$ and internal resistance of $1\,\Omega$. It powers a $4\,\Omega$ load. Find the supply current.

- $5\,\mathrm A$.
- $3\,\mathrm A$.
- $3.75\,\mathrm A$.
- $15\,\mathrm A$.

## Explanation

The same current passes through the load and internal resistance, so the electromotive force drops across the full five ohms.

$$
I=\frac{15\,\mathrm V}{(4+1)\,\Omega}=3\,\mathrm A.
$$
