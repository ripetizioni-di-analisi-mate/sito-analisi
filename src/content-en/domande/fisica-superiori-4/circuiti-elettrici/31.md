An ideal $24\,\mathrm V$ source supplies $3\,\mathrm A$ through an unknown resistance in series with a parallel combination of $12\,\Omega$ and $6\,\Omega$. What is the unknown resistance?

- $2\,\Omega$.
- $8\,\Omega$.
- $12\,\Omega$.
- $4\,\Omega$.

## Explanation

The supply current gives the equivalent resistance of the whole circuit. Subtract the equivalent of the two resistors sharing both nodes.

$$
R_{\mathrm s}=\frac{24\,\mathrm V}{3\,\mathrm A}-\frac{12\cdot6}{12+6}\,\Omega=4\,\Omega.
$$
