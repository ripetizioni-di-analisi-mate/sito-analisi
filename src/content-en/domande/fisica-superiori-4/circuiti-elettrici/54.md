The ideal part of a battery produces $48\,\mathrm W$ while supplying $4\,\mathrm A$. The load receives $36\,\mathrm W$. What is the internal resistance?

- $1.5\,\Omega$.
- $2.25\,\Omega$.
- $0.75\,\Omega$.
- $3\,\Omega$.

## Explanation

The difference between produced power and useful power is dissipated by the internal resistance. Divide that difference by the square of the current.

$$
P_r=(48-36)\,\mathrm W=12\,\mathrm W,\qquad
r=\frac{12\,\mathrm W}{(4\,\mathrm A)^2}=0.75\,\Omega.
$$
