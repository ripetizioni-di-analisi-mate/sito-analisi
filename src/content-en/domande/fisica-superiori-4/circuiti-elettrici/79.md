In a second trial, $R$ is tripled and $C$ is halved. What is the ratio of the new time constant to the initial one?

- $0.5$.
- $2$.
- $6$.
- $1.5$.

## Explanation

The time constant depends on the product of the two quantities. Multiply the scale factors.

$$
\frac{\tau_{\mathrm{nuova}}}{\tau_{\mathrm{iniziale}}}
=\frac{(3R)\left(\frac{C}{2}\right)}{RC}=\frac{3}{2}.
$$
