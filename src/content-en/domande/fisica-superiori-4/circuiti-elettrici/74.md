At steady state, a capacitor connected to $5\,\mathrm V$ has a charge of $1\,\mathrm{mC}$ on one plate. What is its capacitance?

- $200\,\mathrm{\mu F}$.
- $20\,\mathrm{\mu F}$.
- $100\,\mathrm{\mu F}$.
- $400\,\mathrm{\mu F}$.

## Explanation

At steady state the current is zero and the battery's full voltage appears across the capacitor. Divide the measured charge by those five volts.

$$
C=\frac{q}{V_C}=\frac{10^{-3}\,\mathrm C}{5\,\mathrm V}
=2\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm F=200\,\mathrm{\mu F}.
$$
