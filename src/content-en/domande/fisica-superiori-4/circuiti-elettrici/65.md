Four branches meet at an ideal node. In the first three, $2\,\mathrm{mA}$ and $6\,\mathrm{mA}$ enter and $3\,\mathrm{mA}$ leaves. Which measurement in the fourth branch is consistent with steady state?

- $11\,\mathrm{mA}$ leaves.
- $5\,\mathrm{mA}$ enters.
- $8\,\mathrm{mA}$ leaves.
- $5\,\mathrm{mA}$ leaves.

## Explanation

The first three branches supply a net $(2+6-3)\,\mathrm{mA}=5\,\mathrm{mA}$ to the node. The fourth must take this current away to balance the input.
