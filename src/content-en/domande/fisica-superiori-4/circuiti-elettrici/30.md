An ideal $30\,\mathrm V$ source powers $5\,\Omega$ in series with two $10\,\Omega$ resistors in parallel. What power is dissipated in one of the parallel resistors?

- $22.5\,\mathrm W$.
- $15\,\mathrm W$.
- $45\,\mathrm W$.
- $90\,\mathrm W$.

## Explanation

The parallel combination is equivalent to five ohms, so it receives half the battery voltage. Each branch has that same voltage, and its power uses the resistance of that individual branch.

$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{\mathrm p}&=5\,\Omega,\\[4pt]
V_{\mathrm p}&=\frac{30\,\mathrm V}{2}=15\,\mathrm V,\\[4pt]
P&=\frac{(15\,\mathrm V)^2}{10\,\Omega}=22.5\,\mathrm W.
\end{aligned}
$$
