A box has two terminals and contains only ideal ohmic resistors. Two proposed circuits are a single $6\,\Omega$ resistor or two $12\,\Omega$ resistors in parallel. Terminal voltage and current can be measured at any DC voltage. Which test distinguishes the circuits?

- Measure current at $12$ V.
- No measurement limited to terminal voltages and currents.
- Reverse the polarity of a $6$ V supply.
- Double the voltage and compare the two total currents.

## Explanation

At voltage $V$, the single element draws $\dfrac{V}{6}$ amperes, with $V$ in volts. The two branches each draw $\dfrac{V}{12}$ amperes, giving the same total. Their terminal voltage–current relations agree for every $V$, including when its sign is reversed.

Theory — [The voltage is common and the currents add](/en/fisica-superiori-4/circuiti-elettrici/teoria#parallelo).
