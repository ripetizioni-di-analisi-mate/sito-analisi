Three resistors of $3\,\Omega$, $6\,\Omega$, and $6\,\Omega$ share the same two nodes. Find the equivalent resistance.

- $3\,\Omega$.
- $1.5\,\Omega$.
- $15\,\Omega$.
- $2\,\Omega$.

## Explanation

Together the two six-ohm branches draw the same current as the three-ohm branch. The overall resistance is therefore half of three ohms.

$$
\frac{1}{R_{\mathrm{eq}}}=
\left(\frac{1}{3}+\frac{1}{6}+\frac{1}{6}\right)\,\Omega^{-1}
=\frac{2}{3}\,\Omega^{-1}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad R_{\mathrm{eq}}=1.5\,\Omega.
$$
