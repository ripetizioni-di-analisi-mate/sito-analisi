Two identical resistors are first connected in series and then in parallel across the same ideal battery. What is the ratio $\dfrac{I_{\mathrm{parallelo}}}{I_{\mathrm{serie}}}$ of the supply currents?

- $2$.
- $0.25$.
- $4$.
- $1$.

## Explanation

In series the two values add. In parallel the equivalent resistance is half of one resistance. At the same voltage, the current ratio is the reciprocal of the resistance ratio.

$$
\frac{I_{\mathrm{parallelo}}}{I_{\mathrm{serie}}}=
\frac{R_{\mathrm{eq,serie}}}{R_{\mathrm{eq,parallelo}}}
=\frac{2R}{\frac{R}{2}}=4.
$$
