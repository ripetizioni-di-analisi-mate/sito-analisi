The traversal direction in a loop equation is reversed while the current reference arrows remain fixed. How does the equation change?

- Only the resistor terms change sign.
- Every term changes sign.
- Only the battery terms change sign.
- The sum of the terms becomes positive.

## Explanation

Each potential difference is traversed in reverse, so every term changes sign. Multiplying the equation by minus one gives the same solutions.
