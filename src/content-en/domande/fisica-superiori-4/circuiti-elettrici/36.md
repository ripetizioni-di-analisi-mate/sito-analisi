In an unloaded divider, both resistances are tripled at the same supply voltage. How do the output voltage and supply current change?

- The output becomes one-third and the current is the same.
- Both output and current become one-third.
- The output is the same and the current becomes one-third.
- Both output and current triple.

## Explanation

The output voltage depends on the ratio of the output resistance to total resistance, which is identical in both arrangements. The current is divided by three because total resistance triples.
