An ideal battery at fixed voltage $\mathcal E$ charges an initially uncharged capacitor through a resistor. At steady state the capacitor holds charge $Q$. What fraction of the energy supplied by the battery remains stored in it?

- $\dfrac{1}{4}$.
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
- $1$.
- $0$.

## Explanation

The battery does work $\mathcal E Q$ because it supplies $\mathcal E$ joules per coulomb throughout charging. The final capacitor has $U=\dfrac{1}{2}C\mathcal E^2=\dfrac{1}{2}\mathcal E Q$. Thus half the supplied energy remains stored.
