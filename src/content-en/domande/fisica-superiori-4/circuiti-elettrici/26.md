The terminals of two resistors are connected this way. The first is between A and B, and the second between A and C. An ideal wire joins B and C. Which relation between the voltages measured from A to the other terminals always holds?

- $V_A-V_B=2(V_A-V_C)$.
- $V_A-V_B=-(V_A-V_C)$ at any voltage.
- The relation depends on the sum of the resistances.
- $V_A-V_B=V_A-V_C$.

## Explanation

The ideal wire makes potentials $V_B$ and $V_C$ equal, so subtracting either from $V_A$ gives the same voltage. The two resistors are in parallel.
