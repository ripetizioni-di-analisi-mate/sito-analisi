Two parallel resistors have $R_2=3R_1$ and a nonzero common voltage. What is $\dfrac{P_1}{P_2}$?

- $9$.
- $1$.
- $3$.
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$.

## Explanation

Writing power in terms of voltage cancels the common factor. The branch with lower resistance dissipates more power.

$$
\frac{P_1}{P_2}=\frac{\frac{V^2}{R_1}}{\frac{V^2}{R_2}}=\frac{R_2}{R_1}=3.
$$
