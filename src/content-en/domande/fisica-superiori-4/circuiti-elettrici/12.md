An initially uncharged capacitor is connected to an ideal $12\,\mathrm V$ source through a resistor. The time constant is $\tau=1\,\mathrm s$. What is $V_C$ at $(\ln 2)\,\mathrm s$?

- $9\,\mathrm V$.
- $3\,\mathrm V$.
- $12\,\mathrm V$.
- $6\,\mathrm V$.

## Explanation

The exponential gives the fraction of voltage still across the resistor. The capacitor voltage is the remainder of the supply voltage.

$$
V_C=(12\,\mathrm V)(1-e^{-\ln 2})=(12\,\mathrm V)\left(1-\frac{1}{2}\right)=6\,\mathrm V.
$$
