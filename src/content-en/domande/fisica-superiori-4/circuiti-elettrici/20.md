An ideal $12\,\mathrm V$ source powers $R_1=4\,\Omega$ and $R_2=8\,\Omega$ in series. An ideal wire directly connects the two terminals of $R_2$. What is the new supply current?

- $3\,\mathrm A$.
- $2\,\mathrm A$.
- $1\,\mathrm A$.
- $1.5\,\mathrm A$.

## Explanation

The wire makes the potentials at both terminals of $R_2$ equal. Its drop is zero, so all the supply current passes through $R_1$ and the wire that closes the loop.

$$
I=\frac{12\,\mathrm V}{4\,\Omega}=3\,\mathrm A.
$$
