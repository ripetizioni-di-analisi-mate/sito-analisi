A capacitor is already at $12\,\mathrm V$. It is connected with the same polarity to an ideal $8\,\mathrm V$ battery through $2\,\mathrm{k\Omega}$. Take the direction from the battery to the capacitor through the resistor as positive. What is the signed current just after connection?

- $12\,\mathrm{mA}$.
- $5\,\mathrm{mA}$.
- $-2\,\mathrm{mA}$.
- $2\,\mathrm{mA}$.

## Explanation

The capacitor keeps its twelve-volt value at the switching instant. Current crosses the resistor from the higher-potential side, which is the capacitor side.

$$
I(0^+)=\frac{8\,\mathrm V-12\,\mathrm V}{2\,\mathrm{k\Omega}}
=-2\,\mathrm{mA}.
$$
