An unloaded divider is powered at $12\,\mathrm V$. The output is taken across a $6\,\mathrm{k\Omega}$ resistor. What series resistance is needed for a $3\,\mathrm V$ output?

- $18\,\mathrm{k\Omega}$.
- $6\,\mathrm{k\Omega}$.
- $24\,\mathrm{k\Omega}$.
- $12\,\mathrm{k\Omega}$.

## Explanation

The output resistor receives one-fourth of the voltage. It must therefore represent one-fourth of the total resistance, which is twenty-four kilohms.

$$
\frac{3}{12}=\frac{6}{\dfrac{R_{\mathrm s}}{\mathrm{k\Omega}}+6}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad R_{\mathrm s}=18\,\mathrm{k\Omega}.
$$
