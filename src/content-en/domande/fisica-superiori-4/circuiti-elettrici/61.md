A source maintains $8\,\mathrm V$ at its terminals and supplies $2\,\mathrm A$ for $10\,\mathrm s$. How much energy is transferred to the external components?

- $200\,\mathrm J$.
- $160\,\mathrm J$.
- $16\,\mathrm J$.
- $80\,\mathrm J$.

## Explanation

The terminal voltage applies to the external circuit, so multiplying it by current gives useful power. Energy is that power multiplied by duration.

$$
E=(8\,\mathrm V)(2\,\mathrm A)(10\,\mathrm s)=160\,\mathrm J.
$$
