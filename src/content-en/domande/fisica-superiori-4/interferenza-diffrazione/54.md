A grating illuminated normally has $d=3.6\lambda$. How many principal maxima reach an ideally extended front-facing plane, counting the center and both sides?

- $3$.
- $7$.
- $6$.
- $9$.

## Explanation

The condition for the plane is $|m|<3.6$, giving orders $0,\pm1,\pm2,\pm3$. Three on each side plus the central order give seven maxima.
