At normal incidence, a grating of spacing $1.10\,\mathrm{\mu m}$ receives light with $\lambda=500\,\mathrm{nm}$. A report lists a maximum of order $+3$. Which check is correct?

- The order is impossible because the required sine exceeds one.
- The maximum is grazing.
- The maximum is along the central direction.
- The maximum exists because the spacing exceeds the wavelength.

## Explanation

Third order requires $\sin\theta_3=\dfrac{3\cdot0.500}{1.10}\simeq1.36$. A real sine cannot take that value.
