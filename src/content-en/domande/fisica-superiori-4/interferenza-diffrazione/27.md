In Young's small-angle pattern, $\Delta y=2.5\,\mathrm{mm}$. What is the coordinate of maximum $m=-3$?

- $y_{-3}=7.5\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $y_{-3}=-5\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $y_{-3}=-1.25\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $y_{-3}=-7.5\,\mathrm{mm}$.

## Explanation

With the origin at the central maximum, the signed coordinate is $y_m=m\Delta y$. Thus $y_{-3}=-3\cdot2.5\,\mathrm{mm}=-7.5\,\mathrm{mm}$.
