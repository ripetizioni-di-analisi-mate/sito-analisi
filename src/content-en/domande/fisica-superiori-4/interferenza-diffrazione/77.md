Two coherent beams of the same polarization with different intensities produce a minimum in antiphase. What change makes the minimum zero without changing the phase difference?

- Bring the two intensities to the same value.
- Double both intensities.
- Change the wavelength while keeping the same fields at the point.
- Make one intensity zero and keep the other positive.

## Explanation

In antiphase the intensity is $\left(\sqrt{I_1}-\sqrt{I_2}\right)^2$. The difference vanishes for equal field magnitudes, which means equal intensities.
