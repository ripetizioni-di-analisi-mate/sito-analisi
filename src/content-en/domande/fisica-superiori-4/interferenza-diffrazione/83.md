Two slits are examined using the same light. The first is several hundred wavelengths wide and the second only a few wavelengths wide. In which case is wave spreading more apparent?

- In the first slit.
- In the second slit.
- It is equal because the light is the same.
- It depends only on the screen color.

## Explanation

The first minimum direction satisfies $\sin\theta_1=\dfrac{\lambda}{a}$. The narrower slit gives the larger ratio and spreads its central maximum over a wider angular range.
