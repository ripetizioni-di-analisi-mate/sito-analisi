Two in-phase beams of the same polarization have total intensity $36\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$. One beam has intensity $4\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$. What is the other intensity?

- $I_2=8\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.
- $I_2=16\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.
- $I_2=32\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.
- $I_2=4\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.

## Explanation

In phase the amplitudes add. In consistent units, $\sqrt{36}=\sqrt4+\sqrt{I_2}$, so $\sqrt{I_2}=6-2=4$ and $I_2=16\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.
