A double slit has separation $d$ between centers and width $a$ for each opening. What conditions give the first interference minimum and the first envelope minimum, respectively?

- $a\sin\theta=\dfrac{\lambda}{2}$ and $d\sin\theta=\lambda$.
- $d\sin\theta=\lambda$ and $a\sin\theta=\dfrac{\lambda}{2}$.
- $d\sin\theta=a\sin\theta=\lambda$.
- $d\sin\theta=\dfrac{\lambda}{2}$ and $a\sin\theta=\lambda$.

## Explanation

Between the two slits the difference is half a cycle, so use $d\sin\theta=\dfrac{\lambda}{2}$. Within a slit the matched points are $\dfrac a2$ apart, so $\dfrac a2\sin\theta=\dfrac{\lambda}{2}$ is equivalent to $a\sin\theta=\lambda$.
