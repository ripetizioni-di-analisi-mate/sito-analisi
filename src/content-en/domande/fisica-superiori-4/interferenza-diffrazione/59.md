The beam illuminates more slits of the same grating, keeping the spacing, wavelength, and normal incidence fixed. How do the principal maxima change?

- They broaden without changing angular position.
- They narrow without changing angular position.
- They all move toward zeroth order.
- They all move away from zeroth order.

## Explanation

The directions follow $d\sin\theta_m=m\lambda$, which does not involve the slit count. More slits contribute to cancellation just off the maximum direction, so the maxima narrow.
