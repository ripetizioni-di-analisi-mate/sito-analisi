Two waves of equal amplitude start in antiphase. What path difference in the same medium produces a maximum?

- $0$.
- $\lambda$.
- $\dfrac{\lambda}{2}$.
- $\dfrac{\lambda}{4}$.

## Explanation

The initial phase difference is $\pi$. An extra path of $\dfrac{\lambda}{2}$ adds another $\pi$, so the total phase difference is $2\pi$ and the fields arrive in phase.
