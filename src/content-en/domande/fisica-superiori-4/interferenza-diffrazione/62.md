A diffraction-limited telescope is replaced by one with triple the diameter and the same wavelength. How does the Rayleigh angular limit change?

- It triples.
- It becomes one third.
- It becomes one ninth.
- It stays the same.

## Explanation

For a circular aperture, $\theta_{\min}\simeq1.22\dfrac{\lambda}{D}$. Tripling $D$ gives one third of the limiting angle. That makes it possible to distinguish sources closer in direction.
