A slit has width $a=2.8\lambda$. In the far field, what is the highest positive order of a minimum at a real angle?

- $m=1$.
- $m=3$.
- $m=2$.
- $m=4$.

## Explanation

We need $\sin\theta_m=\dfrac{m}{2.8}\leq1$. The largest positive integer satisfying the condition is $2$. Third order would require a sine greater than one.
