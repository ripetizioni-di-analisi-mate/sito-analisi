Two coherent beams of the same polarization have intensities $16\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$ and $4\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$. What is the intensity in antiphase?

- $I=0\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.
- $I=4\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.
- $I=12\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.
- $I=20\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.

## Explanation

In antiphase subtract the amplitudes, which are proportional to the square roots of the intensities. Squaring their difference gives $I=(4-2)^2\,\mathrm{W/m^2}=4\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.
