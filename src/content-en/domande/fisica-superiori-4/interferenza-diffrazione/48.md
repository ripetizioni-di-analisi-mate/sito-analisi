In the far field, the second minimum of a slit $2.4\,\mathrm{\mu m}$ wide is observed at $30^\circ$. What is $\lambda$?

- $\lambda=300\,\mathrm{nm}$.
- $\lambda=600\,\mathrm{nm}$.
- $\lambda=1200\,\mathrm{nm}$.
- $\lambda=2400\,\mathrm{nm}$.

## Explanation

Order $m=2$ gives $\lambda=\dfrac{a\sin30^\circ}{2}=\dfrac{2.4\cdot0.5}{2}\,\mathrm{\mu m}=600\,\mathrm{nm}$.
