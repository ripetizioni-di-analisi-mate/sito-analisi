For a single slit, the first positive minimum is $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ from the center. How far apart are the two minima of order $m=\pm2$ in the small-angle model?

- $16\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $4\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $8\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $12\,\mathrm{mm}$.

## Explanation

At small angles $y_m\simeq m y_1$, so the minima are at $+8\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $-8\,\mathrm{mm}$. The difference between their coordinates is $8-(-8)=16\,\mathrm{mm}$.
