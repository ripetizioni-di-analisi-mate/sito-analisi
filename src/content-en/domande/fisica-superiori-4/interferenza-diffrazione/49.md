For a grating at normal incidence, what positive order of $450\,\mathrm{nm}$ light overlaps the second order of $675\,\mathrm{nm}$ light? Its spacing exceeds $1350\,\mathrm{nm}$.

- $m=1$.
- $m=2$.
- $m=3$.
- $m=4$.

## Explanation

The product $m\lambda$ is equal because the two directions have the same sine. From $m\cdot450=2\cdot675$ we get $m=3$. The common product is below the grating spacing, so the angle is allowed.
