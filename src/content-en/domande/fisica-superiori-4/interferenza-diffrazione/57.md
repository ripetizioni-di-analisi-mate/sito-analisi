A grating is illuminated normally. The measurement gives $\theta_{+2}=40^\circ$. In which direction is the order-$-2$ maximum in the symmetric model?

- $+40^\circ$.
- $-20^\circ$.
- $-40^\circ$.
- $-80^\circ$.

## Explanation

Changing $+2$ to $-2$ changes the sign of $m\lambda$. Sine is odd in the observation interval, so the angle changes sign with the same magnitude.
