Two waves of the same amplitude and polarization arrive with a phase difference of $3\pi$. What intensity occurs at the overlap point if each wave alone has intensity $I_s$?

- Zero.
- $I_s$.
- $2I_s$.
- $4I_s$.

## Explanation

The phase difference $3\pi$ is equivalent to $\pi$ after removing one cycle of $2\pi$. The amplitudes are equal and opposite, so the resultant field and its intensity are zero.
