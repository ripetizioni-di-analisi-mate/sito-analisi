Two waves initially in phase travel paths in the same medium that differ by $\dfrac54\lambda$. What equivalent phase belongs to $[0,2\pi)$?

- $\pi$.
- $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$.
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$.
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.

## Explanation

The total phase is $2\pi\cdot\dfrac54=\dfrac{5\pi}{2}$. Removing one cycle of $2\pi$ leaves $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.
