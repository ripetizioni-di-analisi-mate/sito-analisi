For an ideal circular aperture of diameter $61\,\mathrm{mm}$ at $\lambda=500\,\mathrm{nm}$, estimate the Rayleigh angular separation in radians.

- $\theta_{\min}=1\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{rad}$.
- $\theta_{\min}=0.5\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{rad}$.
- $\theta_{\min}=1.22\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{rad}$.
- $\theta_{\min}=2\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{rad}$.

## Explanation

The formula uses the diameter rather than the radius. Converting to meters gives $1.22\dfrac{500\cdot10^{-9}}{61\cdot10^{-3}}\,\mathrm{rad}=1.0\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{rad}$.
