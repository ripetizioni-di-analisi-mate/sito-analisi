Two rays from one source travel $2\,\mathrm{mm}$ at $n=1.50$ and $3\,\mathrm{mm}$ at $n=1.00$, respectively. How do their optical paths compare?

- The first optical path is longer.
- The second optical path is longer.
- Their optical paths match only for red light.
- Their optical paths are equal.

## Explanation

The two products are $1.50\cdot2\,\mathrm{mm}=3\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $1.00\cdot3\,\mathrm{mm}=3\,\mathrm{mm}$. The indices compensate for the difference in geometrical lengths.
