In the far field the third-order maximum has $\sin\theta_3=0.60$. The screen is at $L=1.0\,\mathrm m$. What is the positive coordinate of the maximum without the small-angle approximation?

- $y_3=0.6\,\mathrm m$.
- $y_3=0.75\,\mathrm m$.
- $y_3=0.9\,\mathrm m$.
- $y_3=1.67\,\mathrm m$.

## Explanation

Since $\cos\theta_3=\sqrt{1-0.60^2}=0.80$, the slope is $\tan\theta_3=\dfrac{0.60}{0.80}=0.75$. Screen geometry gives $y_3=L\tan\theta_3=0.75\,\mathrm m$.
