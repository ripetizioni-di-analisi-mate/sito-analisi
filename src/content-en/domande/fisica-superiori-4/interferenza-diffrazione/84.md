For a single slit with $a=0.80\lambda$, a calculation places the first minimum at $\sin\theta=1.25$. How is this interpreted in the model?

- The minimum exists in the grazing direction.
- The minimum is at $1.25^\circ$.
- The first minimum is absent because that sine is impossible.
- The central direction is a minimum.

## Explanation

The condition would require $\sin\theta_1=\dfrac{1}{0.80}=1.25$. The sine of a real angle has magnitude at most one, so the model gives no first minimum.
