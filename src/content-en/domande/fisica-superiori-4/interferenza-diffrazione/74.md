In a double slit, one opening is covered completely while the width of the other stays the same. What pattern remains?

- The diffraction pattern of the open slit.
- The same Young fringes at half the spacing.
- The same double-slit pattern, only brighter.
- A pattern determined only by the separation of the two opening centers.

## Explanation

The second contribution is blocked and can no longer interfere. Different parts of the remaining opening still interfere with one another, producing the single-slit pattern.
