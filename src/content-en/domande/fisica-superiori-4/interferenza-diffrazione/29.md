The first minima on either side of Young's central maximum are $3\,\mathrm{mm}$ apart. How far from the center is the first positive maximum in the small-angle model?

- $y_1=1.5\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $y_1=4.5\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $y_1=6\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $y_1=3\,\mathrm{mm}$.

## Explanation

The first minima are at $\pm\dfrac{\Delta y}{2}$. The distance between them is one fringe spacing, which is also the distance from the center to the first positive maximum.
