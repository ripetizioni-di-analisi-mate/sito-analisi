Two gratings are normally illuminated by the same light. Grating B has more lines per millimeter than A and first order exists for both. Which gives a larger first-order angle?

- Grating B.
- Grating A.
- The angles are equal.
- The order depends only on the total number of illuminated lines.

## Explanation

More lines per millimeter give a smaller spacing $d$. Since $\sin\theta_1=\dfrac{\lambda}{d}$, the first angle increases.
