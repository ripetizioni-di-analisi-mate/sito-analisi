The graph shows two consecutive maxima A and B in Young's pattern, as well as the central maximum. What is the fringe spacing?

::grafico{f="cos(pi*x/4)^2" xmin=-1 xmax=9 ymin=0 ymax=1.29 nomi="y,I/Imax" punti="4,1,pieno,A;8,1,pieno,B" segmenti="4,0,4,1;8,0,8,1" legenda="I/I_{\max}" titolo="Coordinate $y$ is in millimeters. A is at $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ and B is at $8\,\mathrm{mm}$."}

- $2\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $8\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $4\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $12\,\mathrm{mm}$.

## Explanation

Fringe spacing is the difference between the coordinates of consecutive maxima, so $8\,\mathrm{mm}-4\,\mathrm{mm}=4\,\mathrm{mm}$. The distance from the center to B contains two intervals.
