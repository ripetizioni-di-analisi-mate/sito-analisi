With $\lambda=500\,\mathrm{nm}$, a slit has its first minimum at $30^\circ$ from the normal. What is $a$ in the far-field model?

- $a=0.5\,\mathrm{\mu m}$.
- $a=1\,\mathrm{\mu m}$.
- $a=2\,\mathrm{\mu m}$.
- $a=1.5\,\mathrm{\mu m}$.

## Explanation

The condition $a\sin30^\circ=\lambda$ gives $a=2\lambda=1000\,\mathrm{nm}=1\,\mathrm{\mu m}$.
