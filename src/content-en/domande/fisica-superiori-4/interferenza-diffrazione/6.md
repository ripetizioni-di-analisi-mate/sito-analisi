For $\lambda=500\,\mathrm{nm}$ and $L=1.5\,\mathrm m$, a single slit has a central maximum $14.4\,\mathrm{mm}$ wide. Estimate the slit width. Choose the closest value.

- $a=52\,\mathrm{\mu m}$.
- $a=208\,\mathrm{\mu m}$.
- $a=104\,\mathrm{\mu m}$.
- $a=144\,\mathrm{\mu m}$.

## Explanation

The measurement is the full distance between the first minima. Thus $a\simeq\dfrac{2(500\cdot10^{-9})\cdot1.5}{14.4\cdot10^{-3}}\,\mathrm m\simeq104\,\mathrm{\mu m}$.
