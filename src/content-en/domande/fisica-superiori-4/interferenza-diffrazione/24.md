A plate of thickness $900\,\mathrm{nm}$ and index $1.50$ is crossed normally by light with $\lambda_0=600\,\mathrm{nm}$. What phase difference is added relative to the same length in air of index one?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.
- $3\pi$.
- $\pi$.
- $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$.

## Explanation

The extra optical path is $(1.50-1)\cdot900\,\mathrm{nm}=450\,\mathrm{nm}$. Dividing by $\lambda_0$ gives three quarters of a cycle, or $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$.
