Two coherent waves have a certain phase difference. One path is lengthened by exactly one wavelength in the same medium, with amplitudes fixed. How does the resultant intensity at the observation point change?

- It doubles.
- It becomes zero.
- Every maximum becomes a minimum.
- It stays the same.

## Explanation

An extra path of $\lambda$ adds $2\pi$ to the phase. The cosine of the phase difference stays the same, so the intensity is unchanged.
