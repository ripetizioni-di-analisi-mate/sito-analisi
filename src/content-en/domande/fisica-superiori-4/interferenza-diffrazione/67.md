An off-center maximum predicted by the two-source model coincides with a zero of the diffraction envelope. What intensity is observed at that point in the ideal model?

- The central maximum intensity.
- Zero.
- Half of the central maximum intensity.
- The sum of the intensities of the two maxima.

## Explanation

The interference factor is at a maximum, but it multiplies a zero envelope. The product remains zero and the order is missing.
