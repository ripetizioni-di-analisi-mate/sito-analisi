Two waves of equal amplitude start in phase with $\lambda=520\,\mathrm{nm}$. What is the smallest extra path traveled by one wave that produces a zero minimum?

- $\delta=130\,\mathrm{nm}$.
- $\delta=520\,\mathrm{nm}$.
- $\delta=780\,\mathrm{nm}$.
- $\delta=260\,\mathrm{nm}$.

## Explanation

The first minimum requires antiphase, or half a cycle. The extra path is therefore $\dfrac{\lambda}{2}=260\,\mathrm{nm}$.
