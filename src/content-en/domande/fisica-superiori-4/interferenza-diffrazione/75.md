Two beams with intensities $6\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$ and $4\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$ have a rapidly and randomly varying relative phase. What reading does a detector give when it averages over times long compared with the variations?

- $I=2\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.
- $I=24\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.
- $I=20\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.
- $I=10\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.

## Explanation

The average of $\cos\Delta\varphi$ vanishes because the phase randomly spans the cycle. The two beam intensities remain and sum to $10\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.
