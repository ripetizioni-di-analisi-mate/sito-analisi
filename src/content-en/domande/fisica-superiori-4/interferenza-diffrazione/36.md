In Young's small-angle pattern, one quarter of a fringe spacing from the center, what is the magnitude of the path difference?

- $\dfrac{\lambda}{2}$.
- $\lambda$.
- $2\lambda$.
- $\dfrac{\lambda}{4}$.

## Explanation

At small angles $|\delta|\simeq\dfrac{d|y|}{L}$. At distance $\Delta y$ it is $\lambda$, so at a quarter of that distance the path difference is $\dfrac{\lambda}{4}$.
