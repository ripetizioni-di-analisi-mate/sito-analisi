In the Rayleigh criterion for two incoherent sources viewed through a circular aperture, which features of their patterns coincide at the limiting separation?

- The two central maxima.
- The first minimum of one and the second minimum of the other.
- Both centers and both first minima simultaneously.
- The central maximum of one pattern and the first minimum of the other.

## Explanation

The second source's pattern is shifted relative to the first. Rayleigh sets the separation to the angular radius from the central maximum to the first minimum, rather than the diameter of the central pattern.
