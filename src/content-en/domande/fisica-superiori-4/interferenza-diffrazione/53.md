The first orders of violet and red light are observed on the same grating. Both orders exist and red light has the longer wavelength. Which angle has the greater magnitude?

- The violet angle.
- The angles have equal magnitudes.
- The red angle.
- The comparison depends on the number of illuminated lines.

## Explanation

At fixed spacing and order, the sine magnitude grows with $\lambda$. Between $0^\circ$ and $90^\circ$ the angle increases with its sine. The red angle is therefore larger.
