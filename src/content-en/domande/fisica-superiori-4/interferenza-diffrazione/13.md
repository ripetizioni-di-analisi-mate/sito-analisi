A grating at normal incidence has $d=3\lambda$. What is the highest positive order that reaches a plane screen in front of the grating?

- $m=1$.
- $m=3$.
- $m=2$.
- $m=6$.

## Explanation

The condition gives $\sin\theta_m=\dfrac{m}{3}$. At $m=3$ the ray would be grazing at $90^\circ$ and would not reach the screen in front. The highest order visible on the plane is therefore $m=2$.
