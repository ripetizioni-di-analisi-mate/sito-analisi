Two slits separated by $d=0.36\,\mathrm{mm}$ are illuminated in phase with $\lambda=600\,\mathrm{nm}$. The screen is $2.0\,\mathrm m$ away. Estimate the fringe spacing. Choose the closest value.

- $\Delta y=1.67\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $\Delta y=6.67\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $\Delta y=12.0\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $\Delta y=3.33\,\mathrm{mm}$.

## Explanation

At small angles $\Delta y=\dfrac{\lambda L}{d}$. In meters the ratio is $\dfrac{(600\cdot10^{-9})\cdot2.0}{0.36\cdot10^{-3}}\simeq3.33\cdot10^{-3}$, or about $3.33\,\mathrm{mm}$.
