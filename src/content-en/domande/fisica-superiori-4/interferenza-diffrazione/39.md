In the far-field model, where is the first positive minimum for a slit exactly $a=\lambda$ wide?

- In the grazing direction, outside a front-facing plane screen.
- Halfway between the normal and the surface.
- Along the slit normal.
- At an angle of one radian.

## Explanation

The first minimum requires $\sin\theta_1=\dfrac{\lambda}{a}=1$. This gives $90^\circ$ from the normal. That direction is parallel to the front-facing screen.
