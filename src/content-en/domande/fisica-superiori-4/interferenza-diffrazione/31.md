Young's experiment is repeated with $L$ doubled and $d$ halved. The light is unchanged and the angles are small. How does the fringe spacing change?

- It quadruples.
- It doubles.
- It stays the same.
- It halves.

## Explanation

In the ratio $\dfrac{\lambda L}{d}$, doubling the numerator and halving the denominator introduces two factors of two. The fringe spacing becomes four times its original value.
