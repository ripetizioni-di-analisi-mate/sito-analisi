At normal incidence with $\lambda=500\,\mathrm{nm}$ a grating's first maximum is at $30^\circ$. Find the number of lines per millimeter.

- $1000\,\mathrm{mm^{-1}}$.
- $500\,\mathrm{mm^{-1}}$.
- $2000\,\mathrm{mm^{-1}}$.
- $250\,\mathrm{mm^{-1}}$.

## Explanation

The spacing is $d=\dfrac{\lambda}{\sin30^\circ}=1000\,\mathrm{nm}=0.001\,\mathrm{mm}$. Its reciprocal in millimeters gives $1000$ lines per millimeter.
