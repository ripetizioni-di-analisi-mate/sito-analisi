A single slit has its first positive far-field minimum at $30^\circ$. Which statement applies to the second positive minimum?

- It is at $60^\circ$ and reaches the screen.
- It is at $45^\circ$ and reaches the screen.
- It is grazing and does not reach a screen in front of the slit.
- Its location requires the central maximum intensity.

## Explanation

The first minimum gives $\dfrac{\lambda}{a}=\sin30^\circ=\dfrac12$. The second requires $\sin\theta_2=2\dfrac{\lambda}{a}=1$, so $\theta_2=90^\circ$.
