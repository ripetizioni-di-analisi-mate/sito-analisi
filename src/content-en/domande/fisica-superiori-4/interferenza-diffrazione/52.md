A grating of spacing $600\,\mathrm{nm}$ is illuminated normally. Which wavelength can produce a first positive maximum that reaches a plane screen in front of the grating?

- $600\,\mathrm{nm}$.
- $650\,\mathrm{nm}$.
- $550\,\mathrm{nm}$.
- $750\,\mathrm{nm}$.

## Explanation

To reach the screen we need $0<\sin\theta_1=\dfrac{\lambda}{d}<1$, hence $\lambda<600\,\mathrm{nm}$. Equality would give the grazing direction.
