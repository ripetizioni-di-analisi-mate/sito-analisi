With $L=2.0\,\mathrm m$ and $\lambda=600\,\mathrm{nm}$, Young's second positive maximum is $6.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ from the center. Estimate the slit separation.

- $d=0.2\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $d=0.4\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $d=0.3\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $d=0.8\,\mathrm{mm}$.

## Explanation

The second maximum is two fringe spacings away, so $\Delta y=3.0\,\mathrm{mm}$. Inverting $\Delta y\simeq\dfrac{\lambda L}{d}$ gives $d\simeq\dfrac{(600\cdot10^{-9})\cdot2.0}{3.0\cdot10^{-3}}\,\mathrm m=0.40\,\mathrm{mm}$.
