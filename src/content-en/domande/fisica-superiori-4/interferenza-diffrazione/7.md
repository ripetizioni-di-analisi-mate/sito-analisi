A grating of spacing $2.0\,\mathrm{\mu m}$ receives light of wavelength $600\,\mathrm{nm}$ at normal incidence. Which value is closest to the angle of its first positive maximum?

- $\theta_1=8.63^\circ$.
- $\theta_1=30^\circ$.
- $\theta_1=36.9^\circ$.
- $\theta_1=17.5^\circ$.

## Explanation

For $m=1$ the condition is $\sin\theta_1=\dfrac{0.600}{2.0}=0.30$, with both lengths in micrometers. The inverse sine in degrees gives about $17.5^\circ$.
