The goal is to halve the fringe spacing of a double slit while keeping the central envelope width unchanged. Wavelength and screen distance are fixed. What change achieves this in the small-angle model?

- Double $a$ and keep $d$ fixed.
- Halve $d$ and keep $a$ fixed.
- Double both $d$ and $a$.
- Double $d$ and keep $a$ fixed.

## Explanation

Fringe spacing is about $\dfrac{\lambda L}{d}$ and halves when $d$ doubles. The envelope width is about $\dfrac{2\lambda L}{a}$ and stays the same if $a$ is fixed.
