The first and second positive minima of a slit are $5\,\mathrm{mm}$ apart in the small-angle model. What is the central maximum width?

- $w=5\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $w=15\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $w=20\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $w=10\,\mathrm{mm}$.

## Explanation

The positive minima are at $y_1$ and $2y_1$, so the measured distance is $y_1=5\,\mathrm{mm}$. The central maximum runs from $-y_1$ to $+y_1$, giving $w=10\,\mathrm{mm}$.
