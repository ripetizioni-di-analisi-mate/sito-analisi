A double slit with $d=0.50\,\mathrm{mm}$ gives fringes spaced $2.2\,\mathrm{mm}$ apart on a screen $2.0\,\mathrm m$ away. Estimate the wavelength.

- $\lambda=275\,\mathrm{nm}$.
- $\lambda=440\,\mathrm{nm}$.
- $\lambda=550\,\mathrm{nm}$.
- $\lambda=1100\,\mathrm{nm}$.

## Explanation

Solving for $\lambda$ gives $\lambda\simeq\dfrac{\Delta y\,d}{L}$. Substituting $\dfrac{(2.2\cdot10^{-3})(0.50\cdot10^{-3})}{2.0}\,\mathrm m$ gives $550\,\mathrm{nm}$.
