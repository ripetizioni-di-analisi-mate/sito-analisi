In Young's geometry, two slits at heights $\pm\dfrac d2$ have $d=0.10\,\mathrm m$. A screen point is at height $y=0.04\,\mathrm m$ from the center. The paths from the lower and upper slits are $r_-$ and $r_+$. What is $r_-^2-r_+^2$ without a small-angle approximation?

- $r_-^2-r_+^2=0.004\,\mathrm{m^2}$.
- $r_-^2-r_+^2=0.014\,\mathrm{m^2}$.
- $r_-^2-r_+^2=0.008\,\mathrm{m^2}$.
- $r_-^2-r_+^2=0.028\,\mathrm{m^2}$.

## Explanation

The $L^2$ terms cancel. The difference of the squares is $\left(y+\dfrac d2\right)^2-\left(y-\dfrac d2\right)^2=2dy=0.008\,\mathrm{m^2}$.
