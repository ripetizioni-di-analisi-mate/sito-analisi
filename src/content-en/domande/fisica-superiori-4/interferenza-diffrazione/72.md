Two coherent beams of the same polarization have individual intensities $16\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$ and $9\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$. What is the ratio of maximum to minimum intensity?

- $\dfrac{I_{\max}}{I_{\min}}=7$.
- $\dfrac{I_{\max}}{I_{\min}}=25$.
- $\dfrac{I_{\max}}{I_{\min}}=16$.
- $\dfrac{I_{\max}}{I_{\min}}=49$.

## Explanation

In consistent units the amplitudes are $4$ and $3$. The maximum and minimum are proportional to $(4+3)^2=49$ and $(4-3)^2=1$. Their ratio is $49$.
