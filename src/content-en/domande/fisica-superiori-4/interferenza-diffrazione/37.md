Two equal slits are illuminated with equal amplitudes and an initial phase difference of $\pi$. The center of the screen has equal geometrical paths. How does it appear?

- As a zero minimum.
- As a principal maximum.
- As a point with twice the intensity of one slit.
- As a point with the same intensity as one slit.

## Explanation

The path difference is zero, so propagation adds no relative phase. The phase difference remains $\pi$ and the equal amplitudes cancel.
