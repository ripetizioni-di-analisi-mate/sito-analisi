A grating is illuminated normally by light with $\lambda>d$. Which principal orders are possible?

- $m=0,\pm1$.
- $m=\pm1$.
- Only the central order.
- $m=0,\pm1,\pm2$.

## Explanation

For every nonzero order, $\dfrac{|m|\lambda}{d}$ is greater than one. The only allowed order is $m=0$, along the grating normal.
