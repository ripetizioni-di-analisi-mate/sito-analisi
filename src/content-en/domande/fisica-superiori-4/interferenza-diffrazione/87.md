An ideal experiment shows completely dark fringes and fringes brighter than with only one slit open. What description is consistent with energy conservation?

- The light absorbs all its energy at dark points.
- Superposition redistributes intensity in space.
- Every bright fringe creates energy independently.
- The contributions at extrema must always be added as local intensities.

## Explanation

The interference term can increase or decrease local intensity because the fields add. Dark and brighter regions belong to a different distribution of the energy carried by the light.
