The second-order maximum of a $630\,\mathrm{nm}$ line overlaps third order of another line on the same grating. What is the wavelength of the second line?

- $\lambda=420\,\mathrm{nm}$.
- $\lambda=315\,\mathrm{nm}$.
- $\lambda=630\,\mathrm{nm}$.
- $\lambda=945\,\mathrm{nm}$.

## Explanation

At the same angle the products $m\lambda$ are equal. Thus $3\lambda=2\cdot630\,\mathrm{nm}$ and $\lambda=420\,\mathrm{nm}$.
