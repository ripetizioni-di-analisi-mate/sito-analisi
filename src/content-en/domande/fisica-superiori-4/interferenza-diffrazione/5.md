A slit $0.24\,\mathrm{mm}$ wide receives $600\,\mathrm{nm}$ light. The screen is $2.0\,\mathrm m$ away. What is the central maximum width at small angles?

- $w=10\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $w=5\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $w=15\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $w=20\,\mathrm{mm}$.

## Explanation

The first positive minimum is at $y_1\simeq\dfrac{\lambda L}{a}=5\,\mathrm{mm}$. The negative side also contributes to the width, so $w=2y_1=10\,\mathrm{mm}$.
