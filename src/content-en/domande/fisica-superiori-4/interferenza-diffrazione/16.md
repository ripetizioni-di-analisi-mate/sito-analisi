Two beams of the same polarization with intensities $5\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$ and $2\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$ differ in phase by $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. What is the resultant intensity?

- $I=3\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.
- $I=10\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.
- $I=13\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.
- $I=7\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.

## Explanation

At phase $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ the cosine is zero. The cross term vanishes, leaving $(5+2)\,\mathrm{W/m^2}=7\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.
