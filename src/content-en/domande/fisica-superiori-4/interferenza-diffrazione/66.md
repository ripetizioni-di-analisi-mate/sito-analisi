In a double slit $d=2.5a$ and $\lambda\ll a$. How many nominal Young maxima lie strictly between the first envelope minima, including the center?

- $2$.
- $5$.
- $3$.
- $7$.

## Explanation

Nominal positions have $|\sin\theta|=\dfrac{|m|\lambda}{d}$. Inside the central lobe, $|\sin\theta|<\dfrac{\lambda}{a}$. Thus $|m|<2.5$ and the orders are $0,\pm1,\pm2$.
