Two beams interfere after equal optical paths. A plate in the first increases its optical path by $250\,\mathrm{nm}$. How much path in air of index one must be added to the second beam to restore equal optical paths?

- $125\,\mathrm{nm}$.
- $500\,\mathrm{nm}$.
- $250\,\mathrm{nm}$.
- $0\,\mathrm{nm}$.

## Explanation

For air of index one, the optical path increase equals the geometrical path increase. The two paths become equal again when $250\,\mathrm{nm}$ is added to the second branch.
