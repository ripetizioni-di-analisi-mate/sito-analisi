Two equal coherent waves start in phase and travel in the same medium. At one point the intensity is exactly twice that from one wave. With $\lambda=640\,\mathrm{nm}$, what is the smallest compatible positive path difference?

- $\delta=160\,\mathrm{nm}$.
- $\delta=80\,\mathrm{nm}$.
- $\delta=320\,\mathrm{nm}$.
- $\delta=640\,\mathrm{nm}$.

## Explanation

The intensity is $2I_s(1+\cos\Delta\varphi)$. To obtain $2I_s$ we need $\cos\Delta\varphi=0$. The least positive phase is $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, one quarter of a cycle, so $\delta=160\,\mathrm{nm}$.
