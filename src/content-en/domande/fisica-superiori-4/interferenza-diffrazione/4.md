In Young's experiment $\lambda=550\,\mathrm{nm}$, $L=2.16\,\mathrm m$, and $d=0.25\,\mathrm{mm}$. In the small-angle model, which value is closest to the coordinate of maximum $m=+2$?

- $y_2=4.75\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $y_2=9.50\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $y_2=-9.5\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $y_2=14.3\,\mathrm{mm}$.

## Explanation

The $+2$ maximum is two fringe spacings from the center on the positive side. Substitution in $y_2\simeq\dfrac{2(550\cdot10^{-9})\cdot2.16}{0.25\cdot10^{-3}}\,\mathrm m$ gives $9.50\,\mathrm{mm}$.
