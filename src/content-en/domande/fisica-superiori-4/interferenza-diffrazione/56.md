The first maximum of a grating at normal incidence is at $30^\circ$. What happens to the second positive order?

- It is at $60^\circ$.
- It is at $45^\circ$.
- It is grazing and does not reach a front-facing plane.
- It coincides with the first maximum.

## Explanation

First order has $\sin\theta_1=\dfrac12$. Second order doubles the sine to $1$. Its direction is therefore at $90^\circ$ from the normal.
