Two coherent waves start in phase and have $\lambda=600\,\mathrm{nm}$. Their paths through the same medium differ by $480\,\mathrm{nm}$. Calculate the phase difference before reducing it modulo $2\pi$. Choose the closest value.

- $\Delta\varphi=0.8\,\mathrm{rad}$.
- $\Delta\varphi=2.51\,\mathrm{rad}$.
- $\Delta\varphi=5.03\,\mathrm{rad}$.
- $\Delta\varphi=6.28\,\mathrm{rad}$.

## Explanation

The path difference is $\dfrac{480}{600}$ of a cycle. Since one cycle is $2\pi$ radians, the phase difference is $2\pi\cdot\dfrac{480}{600}\simeq5.03\,\mathrm{rad}$.
