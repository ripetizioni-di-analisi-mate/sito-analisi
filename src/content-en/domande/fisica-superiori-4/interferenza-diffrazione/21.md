Light crosses a stationary interface from $n_1$ to $n_2=2n_1$. How do frequency and wavelength change?

- Both halve.
- Frequency doubles and wavelength stays the same.
- Frequency stays the same and wavelength halves.
- Both double.

## Explanation

The interface preserves $f$. Since $u=\dfrac cn$, the speed halves, so $\lambda=\dfrac uf$ halves by the same factor.
