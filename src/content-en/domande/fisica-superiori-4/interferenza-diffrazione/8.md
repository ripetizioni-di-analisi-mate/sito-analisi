A grating has $480$ lines per millimeter. Which value is closest to the spacing in micrometers?

- $d=0.48\,\mathrm{\mu m}$.
- $d=4.8\,\mathrm{\mu m}$.
- $d=20.8\,\mathrm{\mu m}$.
- $d=2.08\,\mathrm{\mu m}$.

## Explanation

The spacing is the reciprocal of the line density. Thus $d=\dfrac{1}{480}\,\mathrm{mm}=\dfrac{1000}{480}\,\mathrm{\mu m}\simeq2.08\,\mathrm{\mu m}$.
