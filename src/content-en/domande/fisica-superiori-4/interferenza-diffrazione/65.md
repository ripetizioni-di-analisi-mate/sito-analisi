In a double-slit pattern, $d=0.45\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $a=0.15\,\mathrm{mm}$. At small angles, how many fringe spacings fit across the full width of the central envelope maximum?

- $\dfrac{w}{\Delta y}=1.5$.
- $\dfrac{w}{\Delta y}=3$.
- $\dfrac{w}{\Delta y}=9$.
- $\dfrac{w}{\Delta y}=6$.

## Explanation

Dividing $w\simeq\dfrac{2\lambda L}{a}$ by $\Delta y\simeq\dfrac{\lambda L}{d}$ gives $\dfrac{2d}{a}$. Substituting gives $2\cdot\dfrac{0.45}{0.15}=6$.
