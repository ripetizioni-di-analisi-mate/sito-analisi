Young's fringe spacing is $2\,\mathrm{mm}$. How far apart are maxima of orders $-2$ and $+3$?

- $10\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $2\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $5\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $12\,\mathrm{mm}$.

## Explanation

The maxima lie at $-4\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $+6\,\mathrm{mm}$. Five intervals of $2\,\mathrm{mm}$ lie between them, so the distance is $10\,\mathrm{mm}$.
