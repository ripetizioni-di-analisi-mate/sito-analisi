At an angle of $45^\circ$, a student uses $y=L\sin\theta$ in place of $y=L\tan\theta$. What effect does this have on the result?

- It overestimates the positive coordinate.
- It underestimates the positive coordinate.
- The results agree exactly.
- The coordinate changes sign.

## Explanation

At $45^\circ$, $\tan\theta=1$ and $\sin\theta=\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}<1$. The exact distance is $L$, whereas the approximation gives a smaller value.
