In one of two initially equal optical paths, air of index one is replaced by a plate of index $1.60$ crossed at normal incidence. For $\lambda_0=540\,\mathrm{nm}$, what is the least positive thickness that produces a phase shift of $\pi$?

- $t=450\,\mathrm{nm}$.
- $t=270\,\mathrm{nm}$.
- $t=900\,\mathrm{nm}$.
- $t=337.5\,\mathrm{nm}$.

## Explanation

Half a cycle requires an extra optical path of $\dfrac{\lambda_0}{2}=270\,\mathrm{nm}$. This equals $(1.60-1)t$, so $t=\dfrac{270}{0.60}\,\mathrm{nm}=450\,\mathrm{nm}$.
