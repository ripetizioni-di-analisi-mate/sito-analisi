A single-slit diffraction experiment gives $w=9.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ in vacuum. It is repeated with the whole setup immersed in a uniform medium of index $1.50$ and the same source frequency. What is $w$ in the small-angle approximation?

- $w=9\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $w=6\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $w=13.5\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $w=4\,\mathrm{mm}$.

## Explanation

In the medium $\lambda=\dfrac{\lambda_0}{1.50}$. With width and distance fixed, the pattern width is proportional to $\lambda$. The new maximum is $\dfrac{9.0}{1.50}\,\mathrm{mm}=6.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ wide.
