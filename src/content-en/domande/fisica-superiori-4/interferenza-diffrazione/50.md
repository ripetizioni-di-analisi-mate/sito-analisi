A grating has spacing $d=1.6\,\mathrm{\mu m}$. At $30^\circ$ from the normal, what is the far-field path difference between contributions from adjacent openings?

- $\delta=0.8\,\mathrm{\mu m}$.
- $\delta=0.4\,\mathrm{\mu m}$.
- $\delta=1.6\,\mathrm{\mu m}$.
- $\delta=3.2\,\mathrm{\mu m}$.

## Explanation

Project the spacing onto the observation direction. The path difference is $d\sin30^\circ=1.6\cdot0.5\,\mathrm{\mu m}=0.8\,\mathrm{\mu m}$.
