At $a=0.10\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $L=1.0\,\mathrm m$, the second positive slit minimum is $10\,\mathrm{mm}$ from the center. Estimate $\lambda$.

- $\lambda=250\,\mathrm{nm}$.
- $\lambda=500\,\mathrm{nm}$.
- $\lambda=1000\,\mathrm{nm}$.
- $\lambda=2000\,\mathrm{nm}$.

## Explanation

The second minimum satisfies $y_2\simeq\dfrac{2\lambda L}{a}$. Thus $\lambda\simeq\dfrac{(10\cdot10^{-3})(0.10\cdot10^{-3})}{2\cdot1.0}\,\mathrm m=500\,\mathrm{nm}$.
