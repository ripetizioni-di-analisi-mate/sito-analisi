In vacuum $\lambda_0=660\,\mathrm{nm}$. Use $c=3.00\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to calculate the frequency. Choose the closest value.

- $f=4.55\cdot10^{14}\,\mathrm{Hz}$.
- $f=1.98\cdot10^{14}\,\mathrm{Hz}$.
- $f=6.6\cdot10^{14}\,\mathrm{Hz}$.
- $f=22.0\cdot10^{14}\,\mathrm{Hz}$.

## Explanation

Convert $660\,\mathrm{nm}=6.60\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm m$. Since $c=\lambda_0 f$, the quotient $\dfrac{3.00\cdot10^8}{6.60\cdot10^{-7}}$ gives about $4.55\cdot10^{14}\,\mathrm{Hz}$.
