A plate of index $1.50$ and thickness $800\,\mathrm{nm}$ replaces the same length of air of index one. A ray crosses it at normal incidence. By how much does the optical path increase?

- $\Delta s=800\,\mathrm{nm}$.
- $\Delta s=400\,\mathrm{nm}$.
- $\Delta s=1200\,\mathrm{nm}$.
- $\Delta s=200\,\mathrm{nm}$.

## Explanation

Subtract the optical path through the air already present. The difference is $\Delta s=(1.50-1)\cdot800\,\mathrm{nm}=400\,\mathrm{nm}$.
