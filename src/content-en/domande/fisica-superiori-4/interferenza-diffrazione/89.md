A grating has $d=2.0\,\mathrm{\mu m}$ and is illuminated normally at $\lambda=400\,\mathrm{nm}$. A maximum is observed with $\sin\theta=0.80$. What is its order?

- $m=1$.
- $m=2$.
- $m=5$.
- $m=4$.

## Explanation

Converting to $\lambda=0.400\,\mathrm{\mu m}$ gives $m=\dfrac{d\sin\theta}{\lambda}=\dfrac{2.0\cdot0.80}{0.400}=4$. Fifth order would require the grazing direction.
