For a single slit, $\dfrac{\lambda}{a}=\dfrac{3}{5}$ in the far field. The front-facing screen is $2\,\mathrm m$ away. Find the central width without a small-angle approximation.

- $w=1.5\,\mathrm m$.
- $w=3\,\mathrm m$.
- $w=2.4\,\mathrm m$.
- $w=4\,\mathrm m$.

## Explanation

At the first minimum $\sin\theta_1=\dfrac35$ and hence $\tan\theta_1=\dfrac34$. The width is twice the distance from the center to that minimum, $w=2L\tan\theta_1=3\,\mathrm m$.
