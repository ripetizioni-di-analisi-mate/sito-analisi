For a grating with $\dfrac{\lambda}{d}=0.30$, how does the difference $\theta_2-\theta_1$ compare with $\theta_1-\theta_0$?

- The first difference is smaller.
- The first difference is greater.
- The two differences are equal.
- The second maximum is grazing.

## Explanation

We have $\theta_0=0^\circ$, $\theta_1\simeq17.5^\circ$, and $\theta_2\simeq36.9^\circ$. The gap between second and first order is about $19.4^\circ$, greater than the first-to-center gap. The sines are equally spaced, but the angles are not.
