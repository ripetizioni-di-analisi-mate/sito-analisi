A system must distinguish two sources separated by $2.44\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{rad}$ using $500\,\mathrm{nm}$ light. What is the minimum ideal diameter according to Rayleigh?

- $D=12.5\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $D=25\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $D=50\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $D=61\,\mathrm{mm}$.

## Explanation

The minimum diameter occurs when the Rayleigh limit equals the given angle. Thus $D=\dfrac{1.22\cdot500\cdot10^{-9}}{2.44\cdot10^{-5}}\,\mathrm m=25\,\mathrm{mm}$.
