A circular aperture has a Rayleigh limit of $3.0\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{rad}$ at $\lambda=600\,\mathrm{nm}$. What is the limit at $\lambda=400\,\mathrm{nm}$?

- $\theta_{\min}=3\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{rad}$.
- $\theta_{\min}=4.5\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{rad}$.
- $\theta_{\min}=2\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{rad}$.
- $\theta_{\min}=1.33\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{rad}$.

## Explanation

At fixed diameter the angular limit is proportional to $\lambda$. Multiply the initial value by $\dfrac{400}{600}$ to obtain $2.0\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{rad}$.
