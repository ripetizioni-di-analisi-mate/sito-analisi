In the diffraction pattern the first minima are marked A and B. What is the central maximum width?

::grafico{f="(sin(pi*x/6)/(pi*x/6))^2" xmin=-14 xmax=14 ymin=0 ymax=1.18 nomi="y,I/Imax" punti="-6,0,pieno,A;0,1,pieno;6,0,pieno,B" legenda="I/I_{\max}" titolo="Coordinate $y$ is in millimeters. A is at $-6\,\mathrm{mm}$ and B is at $+6\,\mathrm{mm}$."}

- $6\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $18\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $12\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $24\,\mathrm{mm}$.

## Explanation

The width is measured from A to B. Subtracting coordinates gives $6-(-6)=12\,\mathrm{mm}$. The coordinate of B gives only half of the width.
