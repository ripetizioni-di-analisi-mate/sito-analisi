Two independent lamps emit light of the same color. What property is required to obtain stable fringes?

- The same distance to every point on the screen.
- A stable phase difference.
- Equal electrical power consumption.
- Identical lamp masses.

## Explanation

The term $2\sqrt{I_1I_2}\cos\Delta\varphi$ depends on relative phase. If it varies randomly during observation, the term averages to zero and the fringes disappear.
