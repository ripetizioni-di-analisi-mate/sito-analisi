Two single slits are illuminated at the same wavelength and screen distance. The second is three times as wide and the angles are small. How does the central maximum width change?

- It triples.
- It becomes one third.
- It doubles.
- It stays the same.

## Explanation

From $w\simeq\dfrac{2\lambda L}{a}$ at fixed $\lambda$ and $L$, $w$ varies as the reciprocal of $a$. Substituting $3a$ gives $\dfrac{w}{3}$.
