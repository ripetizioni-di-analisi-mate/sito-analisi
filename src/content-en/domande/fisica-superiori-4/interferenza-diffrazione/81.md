In the same Young apparatus the fringe spacing is $4.8\,\mathrm{mm}$ in vacuum and $3.2\,\mathrm{mm}$ with the entire apparatus in a liquid. The frequency is the same. Estimate the liquid index using the small-angle model.

- $n=0.667$.
- $n=1.8$.
- $n=2.4$.
- $n=1.5$.

## Explanation

Since $\Delta y\simeq\dfrac{\lambda L}{d}$, the ratio of vacuum and liquid fringe spacings is $\dfrac{\lambda_0}{\lambda}=n$. This gives $n=\dfrac{4.8}{3.2}=1.50$.
