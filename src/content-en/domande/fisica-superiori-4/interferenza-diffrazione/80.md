In the far field a fringe has $\sin\theta=0.80$. What is the ratio of the coordinate estimated by $L\sin\theta$ to the geometrical coordinate $L\tan\theta$?

- $\dfrac{y_{\mathrm{approx}}}{y}=0.6$.
- $\dfrac{y_{\mathrm{approx}}}{y}=0.8$.
- $\dfrac{y_{\mathrm{approx}}}{y}=0.75$.
- $\dfrac{y_{\mathrm{approx}}}{y}=1.25$.

## Explanation

The ratio reduces to $\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\tan\theta}=\cos\theta$. In the observation interval $\cos\theta=\sqrt{1-0.80^2}=0.60$.
