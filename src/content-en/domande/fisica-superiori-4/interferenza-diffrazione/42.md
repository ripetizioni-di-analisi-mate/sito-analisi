A slit produces a central maximum $8\,\mathrm{mm}$ wide. The screen distance is halved and the slit width doubled, with unchanged light. Estimate the new width of the maximum using the small-angle model and assuming the far field in both cases.

- $w=4\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $w=8\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $w=16\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $w=2\,\mathrm{mm}$.

## Explanation

$w$ is proportional to $L$ and inversely proportional to $a$. The two changes reduce $w$ by a combined factor of four, giving $w=2\,\mathrm{mm}$.
