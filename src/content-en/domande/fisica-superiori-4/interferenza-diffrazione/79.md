Two spectral lines of wavelengths $450$ and $600\,\mathrm{nm}$ fall normally on the same grating. The grating spacing allows all orders listed. Which orders occur at the same angle on the same side of the central maximum?

- Third for $450\,\mathrm{nm}$ and fourth for $600\,\mathrm{nm}$.
- Second for both lines.
- Fourth for $450\,\mathrm{nm}$ and third for $600\,\mathrm{nm}$.
- First for $450\,\mathrm{nm}$ and second for $600\,\mathrm{nm}$.

## Explanation

At the same angle and spacing, order times wavelength must be the same. Here $4\cdot450=3\cdot600=1800\,\mathrm{nm}$.
