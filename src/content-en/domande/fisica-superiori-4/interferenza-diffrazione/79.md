A grating has $300$ lines per centimeter. What is the spacing in micrometers? Choose the closest value.

- $d=3.33\,\mathrm{\mu m}$.
- $d=66.7\,\mathrm{\mu m}$.
- $d=33.3\,\mathrm{\mu m}$.
- $d=333\,\mathrm{\mu m}$.

## Explanation

One centimeter contains $10^4\,\mathrm{\mu m}$. Divide that distance by $300$ to obtain about $33.3\,\mathrm{\mu m}$. Confusing centimeters with millimeters changes the result by a factor of ten.
