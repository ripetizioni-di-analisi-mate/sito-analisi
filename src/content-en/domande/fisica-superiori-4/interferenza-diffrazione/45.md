To explain the single-slit minimum of order $m=3$, into how many equal sections can the slit be divided and what is the separation between paired points in adjacent sections?

- Six sections, separation $\dfrac a6$.
- Three sections, separation $\dfrac a3$.
- Six sections, separation $\dfrac a2$.
- Two sections, separation $\dfrac a2$.

## Explanation

In each of the three pairs of adjacent sections, corresponding points are $\dfrac a6$ apart. The path difference of each pair is $\dfrac a6\cdot\dfrac{3\lambda}{a}=\dfrac{\lambda}{2}$. Each pair cancels.
