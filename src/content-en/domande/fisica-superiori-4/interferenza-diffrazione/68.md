Two equal coherent beams of the same polarization each give $3\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$ with the other turned off. What is the maximum when both are on?

- $I_{\max}=12\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.
- $I_{\max}=3\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.
- $I_{\max}=6\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.
- $I_{\max}=9\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.

## Explanation

At the maximum the fields are in phase and the amplitude doubles. Since intensity is proportional to amplitude squared, the result is $4\cdot3\,\mathrm{W/m^2}=12\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.
