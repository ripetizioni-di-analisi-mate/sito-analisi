Two layers in series have thermal resistances $0.10$ and $0.30\,\mathrm{K/W}$. The outer faces differ by $20\,\mathrm K$. With perfect contact and negligible lateral losses, what steady heat transfer rate passes through the layers?

- $100\,\mathrm W$
- $200\,\mathrm W$
- $50\,\mathrm W$
- $8\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

In steady state the same heat transfer rate passes through each layer. The temperature drops add to $P(0.10+0.30)\,\mathrm K$ when $P$ is expressed in watts. Thus $P=\dfrac{20}{0.40}\,\mathrm W=50\,\mathrm W$.
