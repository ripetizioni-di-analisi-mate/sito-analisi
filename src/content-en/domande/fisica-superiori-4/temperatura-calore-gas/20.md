The edge length of an isotropic cube increases by $0.20\%$. What is the approximate percentage increase in volume in the linear model?

- $0.20\%$
- $0.0080\%$
- $0.40\%$
- $0.60\%$

## Explanation

All three dimensions are multiplied by $1.002$, so the volume is multiplied by $(1.002)^3$. To first order, the fractional increase is $3\cdot0.002=0.006$, or $0.60\%$.
