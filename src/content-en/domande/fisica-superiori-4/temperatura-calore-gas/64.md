An ideal gas occupies $4.0\,\mathrm L$ at an absolute pressure of $120\,\mathrm{kPa}$. The gas stays sealed and at constant temperature. What volume does it occupy when the absolute pressure falls to $80\,\mathrm{kPa}$?

- $2.67\,\mathrm L$
- $4.0\,\mathrm L$
- $6.0\,\mathrm L$
- $8.0\,\mathrm L$

## Explanation

At fixed $n$ and $T$, $p_1V_1=p_2V_2$. Thus $V_2=\dfrac{120\cdot4.0}{80}\,\mathrm L=6.0\,\mathrm L$. The pressure drop corresponds to an increase in volume.
