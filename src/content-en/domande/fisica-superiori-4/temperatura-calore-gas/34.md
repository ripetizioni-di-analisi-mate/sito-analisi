A metal initially at $80\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ is immersed in water at $20\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. The final temperature is $25\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. The water's heat capacity is $440\,\mathrm{J/K}$ and the insulated vessel has negligible heat capacity. What is the metal's heat capacity?

- $440\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $2200\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $40\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $55\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

The water absorbs $440(25-20)\,\mathrm J=2200\,\mathrm J$. The metal releases the same energy while cooling by $55\,\mathrm K$, so $C_m=\dfrac{2200}{55}\,\mathrm{J/K}=40\,\mathrm{J/K}$.
