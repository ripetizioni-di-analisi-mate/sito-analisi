A rod is heated uniformly while both ends are held fixed. What does $\alpha L_0\Delta T$ represent?

- The heat capacity of the rod.
- The actual increase in length between the supports.
- The force on each support.
- The increase in length it would have if unconstrained.

## Explanation

The linear expansion law gives the change in length without constraints. The fixed ends keep the actual length equal to $L_0$. Stress can therefore build up in the material.
