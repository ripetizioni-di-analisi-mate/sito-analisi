Two bodies of different materials separately receive heat at the same constant rate. On a temperature-versus-time graph, the slope of $A$ is twice the slope of $B$, with no phase changes or heat loss. What is $\dfrac{C_A}{C_B}$?

- $2$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac14$
- $1$

## Explanation

Since $P\Delta t=C\Delta T$, the slope $\dfrac{\Delta T}{\Delta t}$ is $\dfrac PC$. At equal power, twice the slope means half the heat capacity.
