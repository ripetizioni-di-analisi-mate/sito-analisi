One quarter of the moles of an ideal gas is released from a rigid container. Pressure is measured after the remaining gas returns to its initial temperature. What fraction of the initial absolute pressure is measured?

- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac43$
- $1$
- $\dfrac34$

## Explanation

At fixed $T$ and $V$, $p=\dfrac{nRT}{V}$ is proportional to $n$. Since three quarters of the moles remain, the absolute pressure is three quarters of its initial value.
