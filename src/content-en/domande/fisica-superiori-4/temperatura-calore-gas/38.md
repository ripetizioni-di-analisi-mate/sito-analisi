An insulated calorimeter contains two solids initially at $30$ and $50\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$, with no phase changes. A student obtains a final temperature of $55\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. Which check reveals the error without repeating the calculation?

- At $55\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$, both solids would have to absorb heat.
- At equilibrium both heat transfers must have the same sign.
- At equilibrium both solids must have the same mass.
- The initial temperatures must be expressed as negative numbers.

## Explanation

Above both initial temperatures, the terms $C_i(t_f-t_i)$ would both be positive. With no energy supplied from outside, those terms must sum to zero, so that result cannot satisfy the balance.
