Two walls have the same area, thickness, and temperature difference across them. The conductivity of $A$ is half that of $B$. How do the steady-state heat transfer rates compare?

- $P_A=2P_B$
- $P_A=\dfrac {P_B}4$
- $P_A=\dfrac {P_B}2$
- $P_A=P_B$

## Explanation

In $P=\kappa A\dfrac{\Delta T}{d}$ the quantities $A$, $\Delta T$, and $d$ are the same. Halving $\kappa$ therefore halves the heat transfer rate.
