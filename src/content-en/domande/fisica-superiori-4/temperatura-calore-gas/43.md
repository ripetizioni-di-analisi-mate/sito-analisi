At atmospheric pressure, $0.10\,\mathrm{kg}$ of liquid water at $0\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ freezes completely at $0\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. With $\ell_f=334\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$, what is $Q$ for the water if absorbed heat is positive?

- $-33.4\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $33.4\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $-334\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kJ}$

## Explanation

Freezing releases the heat that the same mass would absorb in the reverse melting process. Thus $Q=-m\ell_f=-0.10\cdot334\,\mathrm{kJ}=-33.4\,\mathrm{kJ}$. Temperature remains constant while energy is transferred.
