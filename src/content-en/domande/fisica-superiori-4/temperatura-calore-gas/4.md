A mass of ice $m=0.144\,\mathrm{kg}$ is already at $0\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. How much energy melts it completely at atmospheric pressure, given $\ell_f=3.34\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{J/kg}$?

- $4.81\cdot10^3\,\mathrm J$
- $4.81\cdot10^4\,\mathrm J$
- $2.32\cdot10^6\,\mathrm J$
- $3.34\cdot10^5\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The ice is already at its melting temperature. All the energy goes into the phase change, so $Q=m\ell_f=0.144\cdot334\,000\,\mathrm J=48\,096\,\mathrm J\simeq4.81\cdot10^4\,\mathrm J$.
