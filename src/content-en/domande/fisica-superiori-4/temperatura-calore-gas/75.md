An ideal gas goes to twice its initial volume and one third of its initial pressure. The amount of gas stays fixed. How does its absolute temperature change?

- It becomes $\dfrac16$ of its initial value.
- It becomes $\dfrac23$ of its initial value.
- It stays the same.
- It becomes $\dfrac32$ of its initial value.

## Explanation

For fixed $n$, $\dfrac{T_2}{T_1}$ is the ratio of the products $pV$. Multiplying $\dfrac13$ by $2$ gives $\dfrac23$, so the temperature decreases.
