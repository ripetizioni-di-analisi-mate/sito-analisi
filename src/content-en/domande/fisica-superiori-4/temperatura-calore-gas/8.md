A uniform flat wall of area $A=9.6\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and thickness $d=0.12\,\mathrm m$ separates two environments with a temperature difference $\Delta T=24\,\mathrm K$. Its thermal conductivity is $\kappa=0.050\,\mathrm{W/(m\,K)}$. What is the steady rate of heat transfer?

- $11.52\,\mathrm W$
- $9.6\,\mathrm W$
- $4\,\mathrm W$
- $96\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

Multiply the temperature gradient $\dfrac{\Delta T}{d}=200\,\mathrm{K/m}$ by conductivity and area to obtain $P=0.050\cdot9.6\cdot200\,\mathrm W=96\,\mathrm W$. A rate of $96\,\mathrm W$ means $96\,\mathrm J$ is transferred each second.
