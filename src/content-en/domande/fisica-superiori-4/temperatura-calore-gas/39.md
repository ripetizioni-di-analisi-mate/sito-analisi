A pure liquid absorbs heat while boiling at fixed pressure. What happens while liquid and vapor coexist?

- More vapor forms at constant temperature.
- The temperature falls as vapor forms.
- The temperature rises while the amount of vapor stays constant.
- The amount of vapor decreases at constant temperature.

## Explanation

At fixed pressure the boiling temperature stays constant as long as both phases are present. The energy absorbed converts additional liquid into vapor according to $Q=m_{\mathrm{vap}}\ell_v$.
