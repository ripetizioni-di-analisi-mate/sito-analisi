A sealed ideal gas passes through $A=(2\,\mathrm L,150\,\mathrm{kPa})$, $B=(3\,\mathrm L,150\,\mathrm{kPa})$, and $C=(3\,\mathrm L,100\,\mathrm{kPa})$, in that order. How do their temperatures compare?

::grafico{linee="2,150 3,150 3,100" punti="2,150,pieno,A;3,150,pieno,B;3,100,pieno,C" xmin=1 xmax=4 ymin=40 ymax=175 nomi="V,p (kPa)" titolo="States A, B, and C on the diagram with V in liters"}

- $T_A<T_C<T_B$
- $T_A=T_C<T_B$
- $T_B<T_A=T_C$
- $T_A=T_B=T_C$

## Explanation

The products $pV$ are $300$, $450$, and $300\,\mathrm J$, respectively. Since $nR$ is the same in all three states, their temperatures have the same ordering.
