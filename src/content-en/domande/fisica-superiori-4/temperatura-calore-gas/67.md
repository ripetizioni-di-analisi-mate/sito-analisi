The number of moles of an ideal gas in a rigid container doubles. The final absolute temperature is $75\%$ of its initial value. What is the ratio of final to initial pressure?

- $0.75$
- $1.5$
- $2$
- $2.75$

## Explanation

Volume stays fixed, so $\dfrac{p_2}{p_1}=\dfrac{n_2}{n_1}\dfrac{T_2}{T_1}=2\cdot0.75=1.5$. The increase in moles and the decrease in temperature have opposite effects on pressure.
