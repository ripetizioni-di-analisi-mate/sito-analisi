A mixture of water would reach $45\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ if the container's heat capacity were ignored. The container has appreciable heat capacity and starts at $30\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. Which interval contains the new final temperature?

- Strictly below $30\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$.
- Strictly between $30$ and $45\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$.
- Strictly above $45\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$.
- Exactly $45\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$.

## Explanation

The heat balance adds the container's heat capacity as a positive weight at $30\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. The weighted mean of this temperature and $45\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ lies strictly between the two.
