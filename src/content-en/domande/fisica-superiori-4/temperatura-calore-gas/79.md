Two ideal gases are at equilibrium at the same temperature, but particles of $A$ are twice as massive as particles of $B$. What holds for the mean translational kinetic energy per particle?

- It is half as high in $A$.
- It is the same in both gases.
- It is twice as high in $A$.
- It is four times as high in $A$.

## Explanation

In the classical model $\langle K_{\mathrm{trasl}}\rangle=\dfrac32 k_BT$. The expression depends only on temperature, so the different particle masses do not change that mean energy.
