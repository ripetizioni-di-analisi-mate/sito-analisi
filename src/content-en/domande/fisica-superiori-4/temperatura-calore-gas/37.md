An insulated calorimeter receives $6.0\,\mathrm{kJ}$, and it and its contents warm by $5\,\mathrm K$. What quantity does $\dfrac{6.0\,\mathrm{kJ}}{5\,\mathrm K}$ represent?

- The specific heat capacity of the contents.
- The combined heat capacity.
- The specific latent heat.
- The heater's power.

## Explanation

The energy supplied heats the whole system. The ratio $\dfrac Q{\Delta T}$ is $1200\,\mathrm{J/K}$ and measures the energy required to raise the temperature of the calorimeter and its contents by one kelvin.
