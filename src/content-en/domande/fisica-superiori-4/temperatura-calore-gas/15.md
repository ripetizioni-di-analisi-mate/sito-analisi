Two vessels $A$ and $B$ separately come to thermal equilibrium with the same bath. The temperatures of the bath and vessels stay constant. What happens when $A$ and $B$ are brought into contact and insulated from their surroundings?

- The less massive vessel gives off heat.
- Both vessels get warmer.
- The more massive vessel gives off heat.
- There is no net heat transfer.

## Explanation

By the zeroth law, being in equilibrium with the same bath means that $A$ and $B$ have the same temperature. With no temperature difference, there is no net heat transfer.
