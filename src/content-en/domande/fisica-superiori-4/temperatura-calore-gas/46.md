At atmospheric pressure, a mass of $0.25\,\mathrm{kg}$ of ice initially at $-20\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ receives $100\,\mathrm{kJ}$ without heat loss. With $c_s=2100\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$ and $\ell_f=334\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$, which description of the final state is correct?

- Ice and water remain at $0\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$.
- The entire mass is liquid at $0\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$.
- The entire mass stays solid.
- The entire mass is liquid at a temperature above $0\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$.

## Explanation

Heating the ice to $0\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ requires $10.5\,\mathrm{kJ}$. Complete melting takes another $83.5\,\mathrm{kJ}$. Together they require $94\,\mathrm{kJ}$, so $6\,\mathrm{kJ}$ remains to warm the water.
