An isotropic plate with a circular hole is free to expand and is heated uniformly. The plate's linear expansion coefficient is positive. How does the hole's diameter change?

- It stays constant.
- It increases in one direction and decreases in the perpendicular direction.
- It decreases.
- It increases.

## Explanation

Isotropic expansion multiplies every distance between two points on the same solid by $1+\alpha\Delta T$. Two opposite points on the rim move apart by that factor, so the diameter increases.
