On a diagram with $V$ on the horizontal axis and $p$ on the vertical axis, a process for a sealed ideal gas follows a vertical segment upward. Which changes are consistent with this path?

- $T$ constant, with $V$ and $p$ increasing.
- $p$ constant, with $V$ and $T$ increasing.
- $V$ constant, with $p$ increasing and $T$ decreasing.
- $V$ constant, with $p$ and $T$ increasing.

## Explanation

Along the vertical segment volume stays fixed and moving upward increases $p$. With the number of moles constant, $T=\dfrac{pV}{nR}$ increases with $p$.
