Two samples of equal mass absorb the same amount of heat with no phase change. Sample $A$ warms by $12\,\mathrm K$ and sample $B$ by $4\,\mathrm K$. What is the ratio $\dfrac{c_B}{c_A}$?

- $3$
- $\dfrac13$
- $1$
- $9$

## Explanation

For the same $Q$ and $m$, $c=\dfrac{Q}{m\Delta T}$ shows that $c$ is inversely proportional to the temperature rise. Thus $\dfrac{c_B}{c_A}=\dfrac{12}{4}=3$.
