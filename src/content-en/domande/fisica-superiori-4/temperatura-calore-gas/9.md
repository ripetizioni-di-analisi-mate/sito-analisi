A mass of $0.50\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water receives $20.9\,\mathrm{kJ}$. Its initial temperature is $18\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ and its specific heat capacity is $4180\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$. With no phase change, what is its final temperature?

- $18\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $28\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $38\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $10\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$

## Explanation

After converting the energy to joules, solve for $\Delta T=\dfrac{20\,900}{0.50\cdot4180}\,\mathrm K=10\,\mathrm K$. This is a temperature rise, so the final reading is $18+10=28$ degrees Celsius.
