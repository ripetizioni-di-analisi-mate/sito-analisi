Two moles of a monatomic ideal gas warm by $100\,\mathrm K$. With $R=8.314\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$, what is the change in internal energy?

- $0\,\mathrm{kJ}$.
- About $1.66\,\mathrm{kJ}$.
- About $2.49\,\mathrm{kJ}$.
- About $4.16\,\mathrm{kJ}$.

## Explanation

For a monatomic ideal gas, $\Delta U=\dfrac32 nR\Delta T$. Substituting $n=2\,\mathrm{mol}$ and $\Delta T=100\,\mathrm K$ gives $2494.2\,\mathrm J\simeq2.49\,\mathrm{kJ}$.
