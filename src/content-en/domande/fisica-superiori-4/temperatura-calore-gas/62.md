Part of a room warms because hot air above a radiator rises and is replaced by cooler air. Which mechanism describes this transfer of energy?

- Convection.
- Radiation through a vacuum.
- Melting.
- Conduction in a solid.

## Explanation

Thermal energy moves with parcels of air that rise or fall. Bulk fluid motion characterizes convection.
