A mass of $0.10\,\mathrm{kg}$ of ice at $0\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ receives $41.76\,\mathrm{kJ}$. With $\ell_f=334\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$, $c_{\mathrm{acqua}}=4180\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$, and negligible heat loss, what is the final temperature?

- $0\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $100\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $20\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $41.76\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$

## Explanation

Melting requires $33.4\,\mathrm{kJ}$, so all the ice melts. That leaves $8.36\,\mathrm{kJ}$ to warm the water. The temperature rises by $\dfrac{8360}{0.10\cdot4180}\,\mathrm K=20\,\mathrm K$ from $0\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$.
