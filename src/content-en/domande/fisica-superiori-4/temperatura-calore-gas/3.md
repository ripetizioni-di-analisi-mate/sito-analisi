In an insulated container of negligible heat capacity, $m_h=0.30\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water at $t_h=70\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ is mixed with $m_c=0.40\,\mathrm{kg}$ at $t_c=20\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. What is the final temperature if the specific heat capacity is constant?

- $29.0\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $58.6\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $50.0\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $41.4\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$

## Explanation

The two samples have the same specific heat capacity, which cancels from the heat balance. Collecting the terms in the final temperature gives $t_f=\dfrac{0.30\cdot70+0.40\cdot20}{0.30+0.40}\,{}^\circ\mathrm C\simeq41.4\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. There is more cold water, so the result is below the midpoint $45\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$.
