At constant pressure, $0.020\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water vapor condenses at $100\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. The specific latent heat of vaporization is $2260\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$. What is the magnitude of the heat released during condensation alone?

- $2260\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $4.52\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $113\,000\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $45.2\,\mathrm{kJ}$

## Explanation

The heat released has the same magnitude as the heat needed to vaporize the mass at that temperature. The product $0.020\cdot2260\,\mathrm{kJ}$ gives $45.2\,\mathrm{kJ}$.
