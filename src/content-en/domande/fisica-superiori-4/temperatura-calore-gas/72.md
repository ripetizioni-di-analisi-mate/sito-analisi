On a graph of $p$ versus $T$ in kelvin, two rigid containers of equal volume hold different amounts of the same ideal gas. The slope for $A$ is twice that for $B$. How do the amounts of gas compare?

- $n_A=4n_B$
- $n_A=2n_B$
- $n_A=n_B$
- $n_A=\dfrac{n_B}2$

## Explanation

The graph has slope $\dfrac{nR}{V}$. With equal $R$ and $V$, twice the slope corresponds to twice the number of moles.
