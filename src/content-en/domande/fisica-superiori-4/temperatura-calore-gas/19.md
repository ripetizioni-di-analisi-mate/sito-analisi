An aluminum rod and a steel rod have the same initial length. Both are unconstrained and warm by the same amount. If $\alpha_{\mathrm{Al}}=2\alpha_{\mathrm{acc}}$, what is the ratio of their increases in length?

- $\dfrac{\Delta L_{\mathrm{Al}}}{\Delta L_{\mathrm{acc}}}=4$
- $\dfrac{\Delta L_{\mathrm{Al}}}{\Delta L_{\mathrm{acc}}}=2$
- $\dfrac{\Delta L_{\mathrm{Al}}}{\Delta L_{\mathrm{acc}}}=1$
- $\dfrac{\Delta L_{\mathrm{Al}}}{\Delta L_{\mathrm{acc}}}=\dfrac12$

## Explanation

In the ratio of $\alpha L_0\Delta T$ for the two rods, $L_0$ and $\Delta T$ cancel. The remaining ratio is that of the expansion coefficients, which equals $2$.
