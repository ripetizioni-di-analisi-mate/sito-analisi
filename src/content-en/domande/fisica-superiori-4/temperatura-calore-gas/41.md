A $0.40\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass of ice at $-10\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ receives $8.4\,\mathrm{kJ}$. With $c_s=2100\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$ and no heat loss, what is the final state at atmospheric pressure?

- Ice at $-5\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$.
- A mixture at $10\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$.
- Ice at $0\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$.
- Water at $0\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$.

## Explanation

Reaching $0\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ without melting takes $0.40\cdot2100\cdot10\,\mathrm J=8400\,\mathrm J$. That is all the energy supplied. The ice therefore reaches its melting temperature without an appreciable mass melting.
