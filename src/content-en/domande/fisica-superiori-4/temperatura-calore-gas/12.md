In an insulated container, $0.20\,\mathrm{kg}$ of ice at $0\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ absorbs $33.4\,\mathrm{kJ}$. Take $\ell_f=334\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$. What mass of ice remains at equilibrium?

- $0.050\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.20\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.10\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Melting all the ice takes $0.20\cdot334\,\mathrm{kJ}=66.8\,\mathrm{kJ}$. Less energy is supplied, so $\dfrac{33.4}{334}\,\mathrm{kg}=0.10\,\mathrm{kg}$ melts. Subtracting the melted mass from the initial mass leaves $0.10\,\mathrm{kg}$ of ice.
