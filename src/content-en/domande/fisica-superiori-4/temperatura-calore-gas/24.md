An ideal gas in a rigid sealed container goes from $27$ to $327\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. Which value approximates the ratio of final to initial pressure?

- $2$
- $12$
- $1.5$
- $0.50$

## Explanation

Absolute temperature rises from about $300$ to about $600\,\mathrm K$. With $n$ and $V$ fixed, $p$ is proportional to $T$, so $\dfrac{p_2}{p_1}\simeq\dfrac{600}{300}=2$.
