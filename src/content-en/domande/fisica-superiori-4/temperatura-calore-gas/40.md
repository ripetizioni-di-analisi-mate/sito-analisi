The graph represents the heating of $0.50\,\mathrm{kg}$ of a pure substance. What specific latent heat of fusion follows from the horizontal segment?

::grafico{linee="0,0 20,20 100,20 120,30" punti="20,20,pieno,A 20 kJ;100,20,pieno,B 100 kJ" xmin=-10 xmax=130 ymin=-5 ymax=35 nomi="Q,t (°C)" titolo="Heating curve. A and B mark 20 kJ and 100 kJ"}

- $160\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$
- $80\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$
- $40\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$
- $200\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$

## Explanation

Melting starts after $20\,\mathrm{kJ}$ has been supplied and ends at $100\,\mathrm{kJ}$. Melting alone uses the difference of $80\,\mathrm{kJ}$. Dividing by $0.50\,\mathrm{kg}$ gives $\ell_f=160\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$.
