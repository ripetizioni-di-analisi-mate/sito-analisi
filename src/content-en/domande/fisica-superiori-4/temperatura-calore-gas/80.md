Two ideal gases at the same temperature have particle masses $m_A=4m_B$. What is the ratio of their root-mean-square speeds $\dfrac{v_{\mathrm{rms},A}}{v_{\mathrm{rms},B}}$?

- $2$
- $\dfrac12$
- $1$
- $\dfrac14$

## Explanation

Root-mean-square speed is $\sqrt{\dfrac{3k_BT}{m}}$. At the same temperature the ratio is $\sqrt{\dfrac{m_B}{m_A}}=\sqrt{\dfrac14}=\dfrac12$.
