An unconstrained rod has length $L_0=3\,\mathrm m$ and coefficient $\alpha=1.2\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$. Its temperature increases by $\Delta T=40\,\mathrm K$. How much longer is it in the linear model?

- $14.4\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.48\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $1.44\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.036\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The increase in length is $\Delta L=\alpha L_0\Delta T=1.44\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm m$. Since $10^{-3}\,\mathrm m$ equals one millimeter, the rod lengthens by $1.44\,\mathrm{mm}$.
