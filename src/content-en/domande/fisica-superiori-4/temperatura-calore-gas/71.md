Two equal-volume containers hold $4\,\mathrm g$ of helium and $4\,\mathrm g$ of neon at the same temperature. Their molar masses are $4$ and $20\,\mathrm{g/mol}$ and the gases are ideal. What is $\dfrac{p_{\rm He}}{p_{\rm Ne}}$?

- $5$
- $1$
- $\dfrac15$
- $25$

## Explanation

At equal temperature and volume, the pressure ratio is the mole ratio. The helium has $1\,\mathrm{mol}$ and the neon has $0.20\,\mathrm{mol}$, so the ratio is $5$.
