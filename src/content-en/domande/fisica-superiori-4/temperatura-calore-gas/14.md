The gauge pressure of an ideal gas is $50\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Atmospheric pressure is $100\,\mathrm{kPa}$, the volume is $16.628\,\mathrm L$, and the temperature is $300\,\mathrm K$. With $R=8.314\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$, how many moles are present?

- $0.33\,\mathrm{mol}$
- $0.67\,\mathrm{mol}$
- $1.00\,\mathrm{mol}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{mol}$

## Explanation

The absolute pressure is $150\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Since $\mathrm{kPa\,L}$ equals $\mathrm J$, the equation of state gives $n=\dfrac{150\cdot16.628}{8.314\cdot300}\,\mathrm{mol}=1.00\,\mathrm{mol}$. Using the gauge reading alone would give a third as many moles.
