In the Stefan–Boltzmann model, measure the surface's emitted power separately from the power it absorbs. The absolute temperature doubles while area and emissivity remain the same. By what factor does emitted power increase?

- $8$
- $16$
- $4$
- $2$

## Explanation

Emitted power alone is $\varepsilon\sigma AT^4$. Doubling $T$ multiplies its fourth power by $2^4=16$.
