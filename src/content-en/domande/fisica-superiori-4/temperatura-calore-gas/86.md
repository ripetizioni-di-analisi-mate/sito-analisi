On the $p$-$V$ diagram, the two curves represent isotherms of the same sealed gas. Which curve corresponds to the higher absolute temperature?

::grafico{f="300/x;600/x" legenda="A;B" punti="4,75,pieno,A;4,150,pieno,B" xmin=2 xmax=8 ymin=0 ymax=310 nomi="V,p (kPa)" titolo="Isotherms of a fixed amount of ideal gas with V in liters"}

- It depends on the volume at which they are compared.
- The lower curve $A$.
- Both curves have the same temperature.
- The upper curve $B$.

## Explanation

At the same volume the upper curve has higher pressure. For the same amount of gas, $T=\dfrac{pV}{nR}$ is therefore higher on $B$.
