A wall transfers heat at $80\,\mathrm W$ in steady state. Between the same environments, its area is doubled and its thickness is tripled, using the same material. What is the new rate?

- $160\,\mathrm W$.
- About $27\,\mathrm W$.
- $120\,\mathrm W$.
- About $53\,\mathrm W$.

## Explanation

At fixed conductivity and temperature difference, $P$ is proportional to $\dfrac Ad$. The two changes produce a factor of $\dfrac23$, giving $P'=80\cdot\dfrac23\,\mathrm W\simeq53\,\mathrm W$.
