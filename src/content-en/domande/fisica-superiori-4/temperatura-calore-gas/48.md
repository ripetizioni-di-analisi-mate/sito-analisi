Two pure solids of equal mass, already at their respective melting points, melt completely using heaters that deliver the same power. $A$ takes three minutes and $B$ takes two. What is $\dfrac{\ell_A}{\ell_B}$?

- $3$
- $\dfrac32$
- $1$
- $\dfrac23$

## Explanation

During melting $P\Delta t=m\ell_f$. Both samples have the same $P$ and $m$, so the ratio of their specific latent heats is the ratio of their times, $\dfrac32$.
