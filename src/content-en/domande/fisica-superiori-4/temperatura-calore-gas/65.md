At constant pressure, a sealed ideal gas goes from $300$ to $450\,\mathrm K$. Its initial volume is $8\,\mathrm L$. What is its final volume?

- $16\,\mathrm L$
- $5.33\,\mathrm L$
- $12\,\mathrm L$
- $8\,\mathrm L$

## Explanation

At fixed pressure and amount of gas, $\dfrac VT$ stays constant. The ratio $\dfrac{450}{300}=\dfrac32$ multiplies the initial volume, yielding $V_2=12\,\mathrm L$.
