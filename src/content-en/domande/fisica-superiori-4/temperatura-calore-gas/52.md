In an insulated calorimeter, $0.10\,\mathrm{kg}$ of ice at $0\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ receives $33.4\,\mathrm{kJ}$. With $\ell_f=334\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$, which state corresponds exactly to this energy?

- Equal masses of ice and water at $0\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$.
- All liquid water above $0\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$.
- All ice at $0\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$.
- All liquid water at $0\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$.

## Explanation

The threshold for complete melting is $m\ell_f=0.10\cdot334\,\mathrm{kJ}=33.4\,\mathrm{kJ}$. All the heat supplied goes into completing the melting, so the resulting liquid water is still at $0\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$.
