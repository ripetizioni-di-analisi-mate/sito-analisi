A temperature drops from $17$ to $-3\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. What value should be used for $\Delta T=T_f-T_i$ in a formula whose coefficient is in $\mathrm{K^{-1}}$?

- $-20\,\mathrm K$
- $20\,\mathrm K$
- $253.15\,\mathrm K$
- $-14\,\mathrm K$

## Explanation

The offset between the Celsius and kelvin scales cancels in the difference, so $\Delta T=(-3-17)\,\mathrm K=-20\,\mathrm K$. The sign indicates that the final temperature is lower than the initial temperature.
