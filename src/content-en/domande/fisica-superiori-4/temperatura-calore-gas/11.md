An insulated calorimeter receives $0.25\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water at $60\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. The vessel is initially at $20\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ and has heat capacity $1045\,\mathrm{J/K}$. With $c_{\mathrm{acqua}}=4180\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$, at what temperature do they reach equilibrium?

- $60\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $50\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $30\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $40\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$

## Explanation

The water has heat capacity $0.25\cdot4180\,\mathrm{J/K}=1045\,\mathrm{J/K}$, equal to that of the vessel. The balance gives $1045(t_f-60)+1045(t_f-20)=0$, so $t_f=40\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. The equal weights in the average follow from the equal heat capacities.
