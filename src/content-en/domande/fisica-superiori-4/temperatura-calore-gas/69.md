In the product $pV$, pressure is $120\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and volume is $3\,\mathrm L$. What is their product in joules?

- $360\,000\,\mathrm J$
- $360\,\mathrm J$
- $40\,\mathrm J$
- $0.360\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

One kilopascal equals $10^3\,\mathrm{Pa}$ and one liter equals $10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^3}$. The conversion factors cancel, so $120\cdot3\,\mathrm{kPa\,L}=360\,\mathrm J$.
