An ideal gas goes from $200$ to $800\,\mathrm K$. The particle mass stays fixed. By what factor does the root-mean-square speed change?

- $2$
- $\dfrac12$
- $16$
- $4$

## Explanation

For fixed mass, $\langle v^2\rangle$ is proportional to $T$. Temperature quadruples, so the square root of $\langle v^2\rangle$ doubles.
