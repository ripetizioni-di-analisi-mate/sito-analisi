A mass of water $m=0.42\,\mathrm{kg}$ is heated by $\Delta T=24\,\mathrm K$. Assume a constant $c=4180\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$ and no phase change. How much heat does it absorb?

- $1.00\cdot10^5\,\mathrm J$
- $1.76\cdot10^3\,\mathrm J$
- $4.21\cdot10^4\,\mathrm J$
- $4.21\cdot10^3\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Since the water remains liquid while being heated, $Q=mc\Delta T$. Substitution gives $Q=0.42\cdot4180\cdot24\,\mathrm J=42\,134.4\,\mathrm J$, which rounds to $4.21\cdot10^4\,\mathrm J$ to three significant figures.
