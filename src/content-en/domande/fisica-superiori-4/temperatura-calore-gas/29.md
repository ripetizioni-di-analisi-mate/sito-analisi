A $500\,\mathrm W$ electric heater delivers $60\%$ of its input power to water. How much energy does the water receive in $40\,\mathrm s$?

- $8.0\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $30\,\mathrm{kJ}$

## Explanation

The useful power is $0.60\cdot500\,\mathrm W=300\,\mathrm W$. Over $40\,\mathrm s$ it transfers $300\cdot40\,\mathrm J=12\,000\,\mathrm J$. The $20\,\mathrm{kJ}$ is the electrical energy supplied.
