A linear thermometer column measures $20\,\mathrm{mm}$ at $0\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ and $70\,\mathrm{mm}$ at $100\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. What temperature corresponds to a length of $35\,\mathrm{mm}$?

- $15\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $50\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $70\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $30\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$

## Explanation

The calibration interval spans $50\,\mathrm{mm}$ and corresponds to $100$ degrees. At $35\,\mathrm{mm}$ the column extends $15\,\mathrm{mm}$ past the zero reference, so $t=100\dfrac{15}{50}\,{}^\circ\mathrm C=30\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$.
