Two states are to be compared using $\dfrac{p_1V_1}{T_1}=\dfrac{p_2V_2}{T_2}$. Which circumstance invalidates this equality for an otherwise ideal gas?

- The number of moles changes between the states.
- Absolute pressure changes.
- Temperature in kelvin changes.
- Volume changes between the states.

## Explanation

The value of $\dfrac{pV}{T}$ is $nR$ in each state. Equating the two sides therefore requires $n_1=n_2$. Pressure, volume, and temperature may change even when this condition holds.
