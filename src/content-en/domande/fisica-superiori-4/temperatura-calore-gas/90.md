A sealed monatomic ideal gas goes from a state with $pV=600\,\mathrm J$ to one with $pV=400\,\mathrm J$. What is $\Delta U$?

- $-600\,\mathrm J$
- $-200\,\mathrm J$
- $-300\,\mathrm J$
- $300\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

For a monatomic gas $U=\dfrac32pV$. The change in $pV$ is $(400-600)\,\mathrm J=-200\,\mathrm J$, so $\Delta U=\dfrac32(-200)\,\mathrm J=-300\,\mathrm J$. The sign indicates a decrease in internal energy.
