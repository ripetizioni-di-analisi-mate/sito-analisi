One liter of water is kept in an open container at $25\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ and atmospheric pressure. After a few hours the liquid mass has decreased. Which explanation fits the measured temperature?

- The water boils throughout its volume.
- Some of the water freezes at the bottom.
- Molecules evaporate from the surface.
- The mass falls because specific heat capacity is negative.

## Explanation

Evaporation can take place at the surface below the boiling point. A temperature of $25\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ is consistent with that loss of liquid mass.
