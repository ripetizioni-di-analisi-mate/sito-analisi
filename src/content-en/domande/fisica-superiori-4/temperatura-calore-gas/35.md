A $0.20\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass of water and its container of heat capacity $164\,\mathrm{J/K}$ are heated together. They start at the same temperature. The water remains liquid. Given $c_{\mathrm{acqua}}=4180\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$, how much energy warms both by $15\,\mathrm K$ without losses?

- $15\,000\,\mathrm J$
- $2460\,\mathrm J$
- $12\,540\,\mathrm J$
- $1000\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The heat capacities add because the temperature rise is the same for both. Their total heat capacity is $0.20\cdot4180+164=1000$ in $\mathrm{J/K}$, so the required energy is $1000\cdot15\,\mathrm J=15\,000\,\mathrm J$.
