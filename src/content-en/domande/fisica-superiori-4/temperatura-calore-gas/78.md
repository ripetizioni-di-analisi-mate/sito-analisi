Which situation is best suited to the ideal gas model?

- A very dense gas in which the molecules occupy an appreciable fraction of the available volume.
- A dilute gas far from condensation.
- A liquid in equilibrium with its vapor at condensation.
- A solid warmed by a few kelvin.

## Explanation

The model assumes negligible molecular volume and negligible interactions between collisions. A dilute gas far from a phase change is most consistent with those assumptions.
