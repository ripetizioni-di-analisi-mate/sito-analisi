A pure sample still contains solid and liquid at equilibrium at the melting point. The heater transfers more thermal power to the sample at constant pressure. How do the temperature and mass melted per second change while both phases remain?

- Same temperature and less mass melted per second.
- Lower temperature and more mass melted per second.
- Higher temperature and the same mass melted per second.
- Same temperature and more mass melted per second.

## Explanation

The fixed pressure determines the melting point. When the heater delivers more power, $P=\ell_f\dfrac{\Delta m}{\Delta t}$ predicts that more mass melts in the same time.
