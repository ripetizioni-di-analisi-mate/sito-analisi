A body with heat capacity $240\,\mathrm{J/K}$ cools from $50$ to $35\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. What is its heat transfer $Q$ if absorbed energy counts as $Q>0$?

- $3600\,\mathrm J$
- $-12\,000\,\mathrm J$
- $-16\,\mathrm J$
- $-3600\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

The change $35-50=-15$ has the same value in kelvin. The product $Q=C\Delta T=240(-15)\,\mathrm J=-3600\,\mathrm J$ is negative because the body releases heat.
