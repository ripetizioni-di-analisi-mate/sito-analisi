A $400\,\mathrm g$ sample is divided into two equal parts. At the same temperature each part has the same specific heat capacity as the original substance. How does the heat capacity of each part compare with that of the original sample?

- It is twice as large.
- It is the same.
- It is half.
- It is one quarter.

## Explanation

Heat capacity is $C=mc$. Each part has half the mass and the same $c$, so it absorbs half as much heat as the full sample for the same temperature change.
