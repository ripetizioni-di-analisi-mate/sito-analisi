An ideal gas in equilibrium contains $n=0.72\,\mathrm{mol}$ in volume $V=0.018\,\mathrm{m^3}$ at $T=310\,\mathrm K$. What is its absolute pressure with $R=8.314\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$?

- $1.03\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $1.43\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $1.86\cdot10^3\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $1.03\cdot10^2\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

Solve the equation of state for $p$ to get $p=\dfrac{0.72\cdot8.314\cdot310}{0.018}\,\mathrm{Pa}\simeq1.03\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$. The numerator has units of energy, so dividing by volume gives pascals.
