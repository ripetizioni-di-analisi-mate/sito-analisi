A container holds $8\,\mathrm g$ of ideal helium with molar mass $4\,\mathrm{g/mol}$. What amount of substance goes into $pV=nRT$?

- $2\,\mathrm{mol}$
- $8\,\mathrm{mol}$
- $32\,\mathrm{mol}$
- $0.50\,\mathrm{mol}$

## Explanation

Each mole has mass $4\,\mathrm g$. The ratio $n=\dfrac mM=\dfrac84\,\mathrm{mol}=2\,\mathrm{mol}$ converts mass to amount of substance.
