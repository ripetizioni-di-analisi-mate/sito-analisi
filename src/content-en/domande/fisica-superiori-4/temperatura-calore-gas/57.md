A uniform wall of area $4\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and thickness $0.10\,\mathrm m$ transfers heat at $48\,\mathrm W$ for a temperature difference of $12\,\mathrm K$. What is its thermal conductivity?

- $1.0\,\mathrm{W/(m\,K)}$
- $0.40\,\mathrm{W/(m\,K)}$
- $0.10\,\mathrm{W/(m\,K)}$
- $10\,\mathrm{W/(m\,K)}$

## Explanation

Solve the conduction equation for $\kappa=\dfrac{Pd}{A\Delta T}$. This gives $\dfrac{48\cdot0.10}{4\cdot12}\,\mathrm{W/(m\,K)}=0.10\,\mathrm{W/(m\,K)}$.
