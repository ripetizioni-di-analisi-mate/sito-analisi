A rigid vessel contains an ideal gas at $300\,\mathrm K$. A valve lets some gas escape during heating. At $375\,\mathrm K$ the pressure has returned to its initial value. What fraction of the original moles has escaped?

- $\dfrac15$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac45$
- $\dfrac34$

## Explanation

The final pressure and volume equal their initial values, so $n_fT_f=n_iT_i$. The remaining fraction is $\dfrac{300}{375}=\dfrac45$. The fraction that escaped is $1-\dfrac45=\dfrac15$.
