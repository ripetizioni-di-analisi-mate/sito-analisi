Two bodies with heat capacities $200\,\mathrm{J/K}$ and $600\,\mathrm{J/K}$ start at $80$ and $20\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$, respectively. They come to equilibrium while insulated and without changing phase. What is the common temperature?

- $35\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $65\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $50\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$
- $25\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$

## Explanation

Equate the heat released by the warm body with that absorbed by the cool body. Dividing the balance by $200$ gives $80-t_f=3(t_f-20)$, so $4t_f=140$ and $t_f=35\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$.
