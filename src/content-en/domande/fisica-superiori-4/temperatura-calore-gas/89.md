For a monatomic ideal gas, a diagram gives a pressure of $200\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and volume of $5\,\mathrm L$. What is its internal energy in the classical model?

- $667\,\mathrm J$
- $1500\,\mathrm J$
- $3000\,\mathrm J$
- $1000\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Since $pV=nRT$, the internal energy is $U=\dfrac32pV$. The product $200\,\mathrm{kPa}\cdot5\,\mathrm L$ is $1000\,\mathrm J$, so $U=1500\,\mathrm J$.
