A rigid vessel contains a gas at gauge pressure $-25\,\mathrm{kPa}$ where atmospheric pressure is $100\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Which pressure goes into the equation of state?

- $-25\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $125\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $75\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $25\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

Gauge pressure is the difference $p-p_{\mathrm{atm}}$. Adding atmospheric pressure gives $p=100+(-25)=75$ in $\mathrm{kPa}$, the absolute pressure required.
