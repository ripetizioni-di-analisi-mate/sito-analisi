An initially cooler thermometer measures the temperature of a drop in an insulated system. The thermometer's heat capacity is comparable to the drop's. How does the equilibrium reading compare with the drop's initial temperature?

- It is higher.
- It is lower.
- It equals the thermometer's initial temperature.
- It is equal.

## Explanation

The drop gives the thermometer the heat needed to raise the instrument's temperature. Because their heat capacities are comparable, the drop cools significantly before the reading is taken.
