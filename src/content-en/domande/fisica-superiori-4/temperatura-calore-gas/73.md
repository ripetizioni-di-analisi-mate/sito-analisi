Two samples of the same ideal gas contain the same number of moles. At the same pressure, sample $A$ occupies three times the volume of $B$. How do their absolute temperatures compare?

- $T_A=3T_B$
- $T_A=T_B$
- $T_A=\dfrac{T_B}3$
- $T_A=9T_B$

## Explanation

The equation $T=\dfrac{pV}{nR}$ shows that at fixed $p$ and $n$, $T$ is proportional to $V$. Tripling $V$ triples the absolute temperature.
