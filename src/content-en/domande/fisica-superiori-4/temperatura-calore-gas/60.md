A gray surface with positive emissivity at absolute temperature $300\,\mathrm K$ is in a large environment at $320\,\mathrm K$. Under the convention $P_{\mathrm{net}}>0$ for net outgoing energy, which statement holds?

- $P_{\mathrm{net}}<0$ because net radiation carries energy to the surface.
- The sign changes when kelvin replaces degrees Celsius.
- $P_{\mathrm{net}}=0$ because the environment also emits radiation.
- $P_{\mathrm{net}}>0$ because the surface emits radiation.

## Explanation

The surface both emits and absorbs. In $P_{\mathrm{net}}=\varepsilon\sigma A(T^4-T_a^4)$, $300^4<320^4$. The net term is negative because more energy is absorbed.
