Two adjacent sections of a facade, one glass and one insulated, separate the same environments. Their steady heat transfer rates are $120$ and $30\,\mathrm W$, respectively. What is the total rate through the facade?

- $24\,\mathrm W$
- $75\,\mathrm W$
- $90\,\mathrm W$
- $150\,\mathrm W$

## Explanation

Energy can pass through both sections simultaneously. Each second $120\,\mathrm J$ passes through the glass and $30\,\mathrm J$ through the insulated section, so the total rate is $150\,\mathrm W$.
