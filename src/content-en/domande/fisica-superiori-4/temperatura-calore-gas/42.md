A pure substance absorbs $30\,\mathrm{kJ}$ while melting. A heater supplies it with a constant $200\,\mathrm W$. How long does the constant-temperature interval last?

- $150\,\mathrm s$
- $15\,\mathrm s$
- $60\,\mathrm s$
- $300\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

During melting the heater continues to supply energy even though the temperature does not rise. The time is $\Delta t=\dfrac{30\,000}{200}\,\mathrm s=150\,\mathrm s$.
