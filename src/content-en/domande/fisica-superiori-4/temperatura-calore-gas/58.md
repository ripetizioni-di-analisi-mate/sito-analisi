A window transfers heat outside at a constant rate of $75\,\mathrm W$. How much energy is transferred in $20\,\mathrm{min}$?

- $1.5\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $270\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $15\,\mathrm{kJ}$
- $90\,\mathrm{kJ}$

## Explanation

One watt is one joule per second, and $20\,\mathrm{min}=1200\,\mathrm s$. Thus $Q=P\Delta t=75\cdot1200\,\mathrm J=90\,\mathrm{kJ}$.
