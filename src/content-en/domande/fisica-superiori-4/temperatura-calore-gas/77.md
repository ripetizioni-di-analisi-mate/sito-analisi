A sealed syringe contains an ideal gas at $150\,\mathrm{kPa}$ absolute pressure. At constant temperature, the volume decreases to $60\%$ of its initial value. If atmospheric pressure is $100\,\mathrm{kPa}$, what is the final gauge pressure?

- $150\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $250\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $90\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $50\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

The constant product $pV$ gives $p_2=\dfrac{150}{0.60}\,\mathrm{kPa}=250\,\mathrm{kPa}$. The gauge reads the excess over atmospheric pressure, so it shows $250-100=150$ in $\mathrm{kPa}$.
