Water at $20\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ is mixed with water at $80\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. The target is $40\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ in an insulated vessel of negligible heat capacity. What ratio of hot to cold mass is required?

- $2$
- $1$
- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

Each kilogram of hot water releases heat as it cools by $40\,\mathrm K$, and each kilogram of cold water absorbs heat as it warms by $20\,\mathrm K$. So $40m_hc=20m_cc$ and $\dfrac{m_h}{m_c}=\dfrac12$.
