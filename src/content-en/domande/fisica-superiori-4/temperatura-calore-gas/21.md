An initially full vessel holds $1.00\,\mathrm L$ of liquid. Per kelvin, the fractional volume increases of the liquid and the vessel's capacity are $\beta_\ell=8.0\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$ and $\beta_r=2.0\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$. How much liquid overflows for a temperature rise of $25\,\mathrm K$, to first order?

- $20\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $5\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $15\,\mathrm{mL}$
- $25\,\mathrm{mL}$

## Explanation

Free to expand, the liquid would gain $1.00\cdot8.0\cdot10^{-4}\cdot25\,\mathrm L=20\,\mathrm{mL}$ of volume. The vessel gains $5\,\mathrm{mL}$ of capacity. The excess $15\,\mathrm{mL}$ overflows.
