A sealed ideal gas reaches the same initial and final states along two different paths on a $p$-$V$ diagram. Which conclusion follows from the endpoints alone?

- Pressure is constant over each entire path.
- The work must be the same.
- The temperature change is the same in both cases.
- The heat transferred must be the same.

## Explanation

The equation of state determines the temperature at each endpoint. Their difference is therefore the same. Work and heat require information about the paths.
