Compare temperature-versus-energy curves for two samples of the same material, one with twice the mass of the other. At the same pressure, how do the horizontal segments for complete melting compare?

- The larger sample has twice the melting temperature.
- The segment for the larger sample spans twice as much energy.
- The segment for the larger sample spans half as much energy.
- The segments span equal amounts of energy.

## Explanation

The melting temperature is the same because the material and pressure match. The horizontal span measures $Q_{\mathrm{fus}}=m\ell_f$, so it doubles with the mass.
