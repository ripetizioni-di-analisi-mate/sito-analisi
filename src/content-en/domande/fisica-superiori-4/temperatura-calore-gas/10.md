An unconstrained rod $2.0\,\mathrm m$ long shortens by $1.2\,\mathrm{mm}$ when its temperature falls by $50\,\mathrm K$. What is $\alpha$ in the linear model?

- $-0.000012\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$
- $0.000012\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$
- $0.012\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$
- $0.000024\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$

## Explanation

The change in length is negative and is expressed in meters. Solving for the coefficient gives $\alpha=\dfrac{-1.2\cdot10^{-3}}{2.0(-50)}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}=1.2\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$. The positive sign is consistent with a shorter rod when the temperature decreases.
