The graph shows the change in length of a $1.0\,\mathrm m$ rod as its temperature changes. What coefficient $\alpha$ follows from the marked point?

::grafico{f="0.02*x" punti="50,1,pieno,A 50 K" xmin=-5 xmax=65 ymin=-0.1 ymax=1.3 nomi="ΔT,ΔL (mm)" titolo="Change in length versus temperature change. Point A marks 1 mm at 50 K"}

- $2.0\cdot10^{-2}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$
- $5.0\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$
- $1.0\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$
- $2.0\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$

## Explanation

The marked point gives $\Delta T=50\,\mathrm K$ and $\Delta L=1.0\,\mathrm{mm}$. The slope is therefore $0.020\,\mathrm{mm/K}$. Converting to meters and dividing by $L_0=1.0\,\mathrm m$ gives $\alpha=2.0\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$.
