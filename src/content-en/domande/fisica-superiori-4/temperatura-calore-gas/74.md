For one mole of gas at $T=300\,\mathrm K$ and $V=25\,\mathrm L$, a student obtains about $100\,\mathrm{Pa}$ using $R=8.314\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$. Which step must be corrected to obtain pressure in pascals?

- Multiply the volume by $1000$ when converting to cubic meters.
- Divide the amount of substance by the molar mass.
- Subtract $273.15$ from the temperature.
- Express the volume as $0.025\,\mathrm{m^3}$.

## Explanation

With $R$ in the given units, $nRT$ is in joules. The volume must be in cubic meters because $\mathrm{J/m^3}$ equals pascals. Dividing by $0.025$ gives about $100\,000\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
