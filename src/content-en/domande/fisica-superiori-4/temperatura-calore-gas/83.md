A sealed monatomic ideal gas doubles its volume at constant temperature. How does its internal energy change between the equilibrium states?

- It doubles.
- It stays the same.
- It halves.
- It becomes zero.

## Explanation

Internal energy in this model is $U=\dfrac32 nRT$. Since the amount of substance and temperature stay fixed, the volume change leaves $U$ unchanged.
