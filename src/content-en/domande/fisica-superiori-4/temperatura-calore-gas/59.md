Two surfaces at different temperatures face each other across a vacuum. Which mechanism can transfer energy between them?

- Transfer by bulk movement of matter.
- Conduction through air.
- Radiation.
- Natural convection of gas.

## Explanation

Electromagnetic radiation travels through a vacuum. Since the gap between the surfaces contains no matter, it cannot act as a conducting or convecting medium.
