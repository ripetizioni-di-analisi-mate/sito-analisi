A heat pump heats a room at $300\,\mathrm{K}$ using outdoor air at $275\,\mathrm{K}$. It must deliver $12\,\mathrm{kW}$ of heating power to the room. What is the minimum input power?

- $P_{\min}=11\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $P_{\min}=1\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $P_{\min}=2\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $P_{\min}=12\,\mathrm{kW}$

## Explanation

The best performance is reversible. The useful effect is heat delivered to the hot side, so $300\,\mathrm{K}$ is in the numerator of the limiting coefficient. The minimum power is the delivered heating power divided by this coefficient.

$$
\begin{gathered}
\mathrm{COP}_{p,C}=\frac{300}{300-275}=12,\\[6pt]
P_{\min}=\frac{12\,\mathrm{kW}}{12}=1\,\mathrm{kW}.
\end{gathered}
$$
