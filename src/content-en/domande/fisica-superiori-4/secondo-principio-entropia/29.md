An engine operates between $600$ and $300\,\mathrm{K}$. To increase the Carnot limit, the hot reservoir can be raised to $660\,\mathrm{K}$ or the cold reservoir lowered to $240\,\mathrm{K}$. Which change gives the greater limit?

- Raise the hot temperature.
- The two changes give the same limit.
- Both leave the limit unchanged.
- Lower the cold temperature.

## Explanation

The temperature difference increases by the same amount in both cases. However, the denominator in the efficiency ratio is the hot temperature. The two fractions give a higher limit when the cold reservoir is lowered.

$$
\begin{gathered}
1-\frac{300}{660}=\frac{6}{11}\simeq0.545,\\[6pt]
1-\frac{240}{600}=0.60.
\end{gathered}
$$
