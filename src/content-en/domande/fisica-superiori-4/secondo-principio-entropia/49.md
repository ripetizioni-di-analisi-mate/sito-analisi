A refrigerator runs with the door open. The compartment and the condenser exchange heat with the same thermally insulated room. Electrical work enters from outside. How does one cycle affect the room's energy?

- The energy increases by the work received by the refrigerator.
- The energy decreases by the heat removed by the compartment.
- Since both sides exchange with the same room, the net effect is zero.
- The energy increases by the sum of heat removed and heat rejected.

## Explanation

The room loses $Q_c$ and receives $Q_h$. Since $Q_h=Q_c+W_{\mathrm{in}}$, the difference left in the room is exactly the work input.
