Two reservoirs, one at $300\,\mathrm{K}$ and the other at $450\,\mathrm{K}$, each receive $90\,\mathrm{J}$. Which has the larger entropy increase?

- The one at $450\,\mathrm{K}$.
- The one at $300\,\mathrm{K}$.
- The increase is the same for both reservoirs.
- Both entropies decrease because the reservoirs receive heat.

## Explanation

Each reservoir receives positive heat and stays at constant temperature. For the same heat, the lower temperature gives the larger entropy increase.

$$
\frac{90}{300}\,\mathrm{J/K}=0.30\,\mathrm{J/K},\qquad\frac{90}{450}\,\mathrm{J/K}=0.20\,\mathrm{J/K}.
$$
