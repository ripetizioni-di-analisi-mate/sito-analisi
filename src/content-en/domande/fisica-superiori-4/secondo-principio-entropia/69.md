Two bodies at different temperatures and with different heat capacities reach equilibrium by exchanging heat only with each other. What relation holds between their entropy changes?

- The colder body's increase exceeds the magnitude of the hotter body's decrease.
- The hotter body's decrease exceeds the magnitude of the colder body's increase.
- Their magnitudes are always equal because the heats cancel.
- Both are positive because the combined system is isolated.

## Explanation

The transfer warms the colder body and cools the hotter one, so the contributions have opposite signs. Since the transfer is irreversible in the isolated whole, their sum is positive. The positive contribution must therefore exceed the magnitude of the negative one.
