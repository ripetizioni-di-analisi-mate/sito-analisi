A reversible engine operates between $600$ and $400\,\mathrm{K}$ and delivers $300\,\mathrm{J}$ of work per cycle. How much heat does it reject to the cold reservoir?

- $200\,\mathrm{J}$
- $600\,\mathrm{J}$
- $300\,\mathrm{J}$
- $900\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Work is one-third of the absorbed heat, as follows from the temperature ratio. The absorbed heat is therefore nine hundred joules. Subtract work in the energy balance to find the rejected heat.

$$
\begin{gathered}
\eta_C=1-\frac{400}{600}=\frac13,\\[6pt]
Q_c=\frac{300}{\eta_C}\,\mathrm{J}-300\,\mathrm{J}=600\,\mathrm{J}.
\end{gathered}
$$
