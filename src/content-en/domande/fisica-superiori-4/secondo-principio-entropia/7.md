A body with constant heat capacity $192\,\mathrm{J/K}$ goes from $300$ to $450\,\mathrm{K}$ without a phase change. Use $\ln(1.5)\simeq0.405465$. Calculate its entropy change.Choose the closest value.

- $\Delta S=96\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $\Delta S\simeq 77.8\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $\Delta S=64\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $\Delta S=-77.8\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

The temperature varies during heating. Adding the reversible contributions at the different temperatures gives the heat capacity times the logarithm of the ratio of final to initial temperature.

$$
\Delta S=192\ln\frac{450}{300}\,\mathrm{J/K}\simeq77.8\,\mathrm{J/K}.
$$
