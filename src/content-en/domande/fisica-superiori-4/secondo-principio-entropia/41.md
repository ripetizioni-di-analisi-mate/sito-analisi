A heat pump removes $4.5\,\mathrm{kW}$ from outdoor air and draws $1.5\,\mathrm{kW}$ of electricity, all used as work. What is the heating coefficient of performance?

- $3$
- $1$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

The heat delivered indoors includes the power extracted outside and the electric power. Divide their sum by the electric power alone.

$$
\mathrm{COP}_p=\frac{4.5+1.5}{1.5}=4.
$$
