An engine between $800$ and $320\,\mathrm{K}$ has an efficiency equal to three-quarters of the Carnot limit. If it absorbs $600\,\mathrm{kW}$ as heat, what is its mechanical power?

- $270\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $360\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $450\,\mathrm{kW}$
- $90\,\mathrm{kW}$

## Explanation

First compute the limit from the temperatures. Three-quarters refers to that limit and gives the actual efficiency. Useful power is that efficiency times the incoming thermal power.

$$
\begin{gathered}
\eta_C=1-\frac{320}{800}=0.60,\\[6pt]
\eta=0.45,\\[6pt]
P=0.45\cdot600\,\mathrm{kW}=270\,\mathrm{kW}.
\end{gathered}
$$
