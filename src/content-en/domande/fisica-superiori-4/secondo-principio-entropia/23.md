A rectangular cycle spans volumes from $2\,\mathrm{L}$ to $5\,\mathrm{L}$ and pressures of $100$ and $300\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Expansion is at the higher pressure and compression at the lower one. What is the net work done by the gas?

::grafico{linee="2,300 5,300 5,100 2,100 2,300" vettori="3,300,4,300;4,100,3,100;5,240,5,165;2,165,2,240" xmin=0 xmax=6 ymin=0 ymax=400 nomi="V,p (kPa)" titolo="Volume V is in liters. Expansion occurs along the upper edge."}

- $-600\,\mathrm{J}$
- $600\,\mathrm{J}$
- $900\,\mathrm{J}$
- $300\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The vertical segments contribute no work because the volume is constant. The expansion work exceeds the work input on compression. The net work is the area of the rectangle, and $1\,\mathrm{kPa}\cdot1\,\mathrm{L}=1\,\mathrm{J}$.

$$
W=(300-100)(5-2)\,\mathrm{kPa}\cdot\mathrm{L}=600\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
