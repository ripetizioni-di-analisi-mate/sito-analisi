One mole of ideal gas with $C_V=12\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$ doubles its absolute temperature at constant volume. Use $\ln2\simeq0.693$. Calculate the entropy change.Choose the closest value.

- $0\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $12\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $8.32\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $-8.32\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

Volume remains constant and its logarithmic contribution is zero. In the temperature term the amount of gas is one mole and the temperature ratio is two.

$$
\Delta S=nC_V\ln2\simeq12\cdot0.693\,\mathrm{J/K}=8.32\,\mathrm{J/K}.
$$
