Two independent subsystems have $\Omega_A$ and $\Omega_B$ equally probable microstates, respectively. What is the entropy of the combined system in Boltzmann's model?

- $S_A-S_B$
- $k_B\ln(\Omega_A+\Omega_B)$
- $\dfrac{S_A+S_B}{2}$
- $S_A+S_B$

## Explanation

Every microstate of the first subsystem can be paired with every microstate of the second, so the counts multiply. The logarithm of the product gives the sum of the two entropies.

$$
S=k_B\ln(\Omega_A\Omega_B)=k_B\ln\Omega_A+k_B\ln\Omega_B=S_A+S_B.
$$
