Which information identifies a microstate in a classical gas model?

- The positions and velocities of all particles.
- The pressure and temperature of the gas.
- The total available volume and the amount in moles.
- The internal energy of the gas alone.

## Explanation

A microstate specifies the configuration of the individual particles. The same pressure or internal energy can arise from many different microscopic configurations, so those data describe aspects of the macrostate.
