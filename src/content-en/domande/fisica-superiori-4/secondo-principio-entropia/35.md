In a Carnot cycle, along the hot isotherm at $500\,\mathrm{K}$ entropy goes from $2$ to $5\,\mathrm{J/K}$. How much heat does the fluid absorb on that segment?

::grafico{linee="2,500 5,500" punti="2,500,pieno;5,500,pieno" vettori="3,500,4,500" segmenti="2,0,2,500;5,0,5,500" xmin=0 xmax=6 ymin=0 ymax=640 nomi="S,T (K)" titolo="The arrow shows the entropy increase along the isotherm."}

- $1000\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1500\,\mathrm{J}$
- $2500\,\mathrm{J}$
- $500\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The transfer is reversible and the temperature is constant. Multiply the entropy change by temperature. On the graph this is the product of the horizontal width and the height of the isotherm.

$$
Q_h=T_h\Delta S=(500\cdot(5-2))\,\mathrm{J}=1500\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
