Per cycle, engine $A$ absorbs $800\,\mathrm{J}$ and delivers $320\,\mathrm{J}$ of work. Engine $B$ absorbs $1400\,\mathrm{J}$ and delivers $420\,\mathrm{J}$ of work. Which comparison is correct?

- B has higher efficiency and produces more work.
- A and B have the same efficiency.
- A has higher efficiency and B produces more work.
- A produces more work and B has higher efficiency.

## Explanation

B delivers more work because four hundred twenty exceeds three hundred twenty. Efficiency instead compares the work with the heat input of each engine, which differs here.

$$
\begin{gathered}
\eta_A=\frac{320}{800}=0.40,\\[6pt]
\eta_B=\frac{420}{1400}=0.30.
\end{gathered}
$$
