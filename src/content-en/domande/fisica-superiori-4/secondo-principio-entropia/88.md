An engine cycling between $600$ and $300\,\mathrm{K}$ reports $Q_h=1000\,\mathrm{J}$, $Q_c=650\,\mathrm{J}$, and $W=450\,\mathrm{J}$. These are all reported energy exchanges for one cycle. Which check rules out the values?

- The reported efficiency exceeds the Carnot limit.
- The two reservoirs would have a negative total entropy change.
- The data satisfy both laws and are possible.
- The energy balance over the cycle.

## Explanation

The reported work divided by the incoming heat is below the Carnot limit, but the energies do not balance. Incoming heat minus outgoing heat must equal the work over the cycle.

$$
Q_h-Q_c=(1000-650)\,\mathrm{J}=350\,\mathrm{J}\neq450\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
