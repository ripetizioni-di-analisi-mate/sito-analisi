In one stage of a cycle the gas absorbs $200\,\mathrm{J}$ and does $300\,\mathrm{J}$ of work. How does its internal energy change during that stage?

- $100\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $500\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-100\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The return to the initial state occurs at the end of the cycle. For the individual stage apply the first law with incoming heat positive and work done by the gas positive.

$$
\Delta U=Q-W=(200-300)\,\mathrm{J}=-100\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
