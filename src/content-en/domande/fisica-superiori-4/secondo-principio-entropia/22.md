An engine absorbs $1000\,\mathrm{J}$ per cycle and rejects $600\,\mathrm{J}$ as heat. A generator converts $80\%$ of the resulting work to electrical energy. What is the efficiency from heat input to electrical energy?

- $0.32$
- $0.4$
- $0.48$
- $0.8$

## Explanation

First find the work from the engine balance. Only four-fifths of that work becomes electrical energy. The combined efficiency is the latter energy divided by the initial heat input.

$$
\eta_{\mathrm{el}}=\frac{0.80(1000-600)}{1000}=0.32.
$$
