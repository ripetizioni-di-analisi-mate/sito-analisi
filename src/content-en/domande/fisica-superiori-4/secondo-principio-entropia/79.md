One macrostate has $64$ equally probable microstates and another has $16$. For a transition from the second to the first, what is $\Delta S$ according to Boltzmann's formula?

- $48k_B$
- $4k_B$
- $k_B\ln4$
- $-k_B\ln4$

## Explanation

The entropies of the two macrostates are proportional to the logarithms of their respective counts. The difference of logarithms is the logarithm of the ratio, so use the ratio of sixty-four to sixteen.

$$
\Delta S=k_B(\ln64-\ln16)=k_B\ln4.
$$
