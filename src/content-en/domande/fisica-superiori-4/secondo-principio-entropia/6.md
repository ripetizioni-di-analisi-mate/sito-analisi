An ideal thermal reservoir at $540\,\mathrm{K}$ gives up $900\,\mathrm{J}$. Calculate its entropy change.Choose the closest value.

- $\Delta S\simeq -1.67\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $\Delta S=1.67\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $\Delta S=-3.33\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $\Delta S=0\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

The reservoir stays at constant temperature. It gives up heat, so its entropy change uses the negative signed heat.

$$
\Delta S=-\frac{900}{540}\,\mathrm{J/K}\simeq-1.67\,\mathrm{J/K}.
$$
