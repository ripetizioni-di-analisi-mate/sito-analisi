A reversible refrigerator extracts $600\,\mathrm{J}$ from a compartment at $250\,\mathrm{K}$ and rejects heat at $375\,\mathrm{K}$. How much heat is rejected?

- $400\,\mathrm{J}$
- $600\,\mathrm{J}$
- $900\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1200\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The working fluid returns to its initial state and reversibility requires the reservoir entropy exchanges to have equal magnitudes. The heat rejected is therefore the heat removed times the ratio of hot to cold temperature.

$$
Q_h=Q_c\frac{T_h}{T_c}=600\frac{375}{250}\,\mathrm{J}=900\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
