An engine operates only between two reservoirs at $780\,\mathrm{K}$ and $300\,\mathrm{K}$. Which value is closest to the maximum possible efficiency?

- $\eta_C=0.385$
- $\eta_C=1$
- $\eta_C=1.6$
- $\eta_C=0.615$

## Explanation

The maximum efficiency belongs to the reversible cycle. In its balance the ratio of the heat rejected to the heat absorbed equals the ratio of the absolute temperatures of the cold and hot reservoirs.

$$
\eta_C=1-\frac{300}{780}\simeq0.615.
$$
