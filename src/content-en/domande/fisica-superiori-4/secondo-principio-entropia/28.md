A reversible engine operates between $327\,{}^\circ\mathrm{C}$ and $27\,{}^\circ\mathrm{C}$. The conversion to kelvin adds $273$. What is its efficiency?

- $0.917$
- $0.083$
- $0.5$
- $1$

## Explanation

The temperature difference alone is the same on both scales, while the ratio changes. Both temperatures must be absolute before calculating efficiency.

$$
\eta_C=1-\frac{27+273}{327+273}=1-\frac{300}{600}=0.50.
$$
