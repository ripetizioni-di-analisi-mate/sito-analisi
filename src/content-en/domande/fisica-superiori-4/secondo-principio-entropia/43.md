A room needs $18\,\mathrm{kWh}$ of heat. An ideal resistance heater converts all electrical energy to heat. How much electrical energy is saved with a heat pump with $\mathrm{COP}_p=3$?

- $6\,\mathrm{kWh}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kWh}$
- $9\,\mathrm{kWh}$
- $18\,\mathrm{kWh}$

## Explanation

The resistance heater needs $18\,\mathrm{kWh}$ of electricity. The heat pump needs one-third as much to deliver the same heat. The saving is the difference between the two demands.

$$
E=\left(18-\frac{18}{3}\right)\,\mathrm{kWh}=12\,\mathrm{kWh}.
$$
