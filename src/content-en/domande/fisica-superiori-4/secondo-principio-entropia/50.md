A thermal reservoir at $500\,\mathrm{K}$ loses $1.8\,\mathrm{J/K}$ of entropy. What is the magnitude of the heat it rejected?

- $-900\,\mathrm{J}$
- $900\,\mathrm{J}$
- $500\,\mathrm{J}$
- $278\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The reservoir is at constant temperature, so signed heat is temperature times entropy change. Since the question asks for the magnitude, take the positive value of the result.

$$
\begin{gathered}
Q=T\Delta S=500\cdot(-1.8)\,\mathrm{J}=-900\,\mathrm{J},\\[6pt]
|Q|=900\,\mathrm{J}.
\end{gathered}
$$
