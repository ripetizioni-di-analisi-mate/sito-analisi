Two reversible refrigerators operate between $240$ and $270\,\mathrm{K}$ and between $270$ and $300\,\mathrm{K}$, respectively. Which has the higher refrigeration coefficient?

- The first refrigerator.
- Their coefficients are equal.
- It depends on the amount of heat removed in each cycle.
- The second refrigerator.

## Explanation

The temperature difference is the same in both cases. The numerator of the coefficient is the absolute temperature of the cold compartment. The second coefficient is therefore higher.

$$
\frac{240}{270-240}=8,\qquad\frac{270}{300-270}=9.
$$
