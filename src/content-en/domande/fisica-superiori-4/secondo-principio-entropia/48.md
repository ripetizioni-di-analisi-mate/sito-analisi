An engine with efficiency $0.30$ absorbs $1000\,\mathrm{J}$ per cycle. All the work it produces drives a refrigerator with $\mathrm{COP}_f=4$. How much heat does the refrigerator remove using one engine cycle's work?

- $300\,\mathrm{J}$
- $75\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1500\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1200\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The engine supplies thirty percent of its heat input as work. The refrigerator removes four joules from the compartment for each joule of work it receives.

$$
\begin{gathered}
W_{\mathrm{in}}=0.30\cdot1000\,\mathrm{J}=300\,\mathrm{J},\\[6pt]
Q_c=4\cdot300\,\mathrm{J}=1200\,\mathrm{J}.
\end{gathered}
$$
