Two bodies have the same constant heat capacity. One is heated from $200$ to $400\,\mathrm{K}$, the other from $300$ to $600\,\mathrm{K}$. Both stay in one phase. How do their entropy changes compare?

- The second is larger because its temperature increase is larger.
- The first is larger because it starts colder.
- The changes are equal.
- The changes have opposite signs.

## Explanation

For each body the same heat capacity is multiplied by $\ln\dfrac{T_f}{T_i}$. Each final temperature is twice its initial value, so both changes equal $C\ln2$.
