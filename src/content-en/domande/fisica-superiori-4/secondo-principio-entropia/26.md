One engine operates between $800$ and $400\,\mathrm{K}$, another between $600$ and $300\,\mathrm{K}$. How do their maximum efficiencies compare?

- Their maximum efficiencies are equal.
- The first engine's is higher because its temperature difference is larger.
- The second engine's is higher because its cold reservoir is colder.
- The amounts of rejected heat are needed to compare their maximum efficiencies.

## Explanation

The limit depends on the ratio of absolute temperatures. In both pairs the cold temperature is half the hot temperature, so the limit is fifty percent.

$$
1-\frac{400}{800}=1-\frac{300}{600}=\frac12.
$$
