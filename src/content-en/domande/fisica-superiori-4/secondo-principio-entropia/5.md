A refrigerator removes $864\,\mathrm{J}$ from the cold reservoir and receives $240\,\mathrm{J}$ of work per cycle. Calculate its refrigeration coefficient of performance.

- $\mathrm{COP}_f=3.6$
- $\mathrm{COP}_f=4.6$
- $\mathrm{COP}_f=0.278$
- $\mathrm{COP}_f=2.6$

## Explanation

In refrigeration the useful effect is the energy removed from the cold side. Divide $864$ by the work input of $240\,\mathrm{J}$. The ratio is dimensionless.

$$
\mathrm{COP}_f=\frac{864\,\mathrm{J}}{240\,\mathrm{J}}=3.6.
$$
