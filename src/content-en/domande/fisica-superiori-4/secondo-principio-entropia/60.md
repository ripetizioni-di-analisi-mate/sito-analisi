During boiling at $400\,\mathrm{K}$, $0.2\,\mathrm{kg}$ of a liquid becomes vapor in equilibrium. The latent heat of vaporization is $900\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}$. How much does the entropy of the vaporizing mass change?

- $-450\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $180\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $2250\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $450\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

For the mass in question multiply the mass by the specific latent heat to find the heat absorbed. The process occurs at constant equilibrium temperature, so divide the absorbed energy by that temperature.

$$
\Delta S=\frac{m\ell}{T}=\frac{0.2\cdot900000}{400}\,\mathrm{J/K}=450\,\mathrm{J/K}.
$$
