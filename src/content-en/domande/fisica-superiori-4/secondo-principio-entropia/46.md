A refrigerator's compartment stays at the same temperature as the kitchen gets warmer. For the same heat to be removed from the compartment, what happens to the minimum work?

- It decreases because the surroundings receive heat at a higher temperature.
- It increases because the temperature difference grows.
- It stays constant because the compartment temperature is unchanged.
- It drops to zero when the external temperature rises.

## Explanation

For a reversible device $W_{\min}=Q_c\dfrac{T_h-T_c}{T_c}$. With $Q_c$ and $T_c$ fixed, increasing $T_h$ increases the numerator and thus the minimum work.
