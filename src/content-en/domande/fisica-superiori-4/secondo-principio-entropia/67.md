A body of constant heat capacity $100\,\mathrm{J/K}$ goes from $300$ to $600\,\mathrm{K}$ by exchanging heat only with a reservoir at $600\,\mathrm{K}$, with no work. Use $\ln2\simeq0.693$. Calculate the total entropy change.

- $69.3\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $50\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $19.3\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $-19.3\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

The body's temperature varies and its contribution uses the logarithm, so it is about $69.3$ joules per kelvin. The reservoir gives up thirty thousand joules at constant temperature and its entropy decreases by fifty joules per kelvin. The sum is positive.

$$
\Delta S_{\mathrm{tot}}=\left(100\ln2-\frac{100(600-300)}{600}\right)\,\mathrm{J/K}\simeq19.3\,\mathrm{J/K}.
$$
