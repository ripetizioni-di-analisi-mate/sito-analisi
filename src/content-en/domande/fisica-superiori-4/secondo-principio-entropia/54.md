A gas expands adiabatically in two different ways. In the first, it pushes a frictionless piston very slowly, maintaining mechanical equilibrium in the limit. In the second, it expands into a vacuum. Which expansion is reversible?

- Only the expansion into a vacuum.
- Only the expansion against the piston.
- Both, because no heat is transferred.
- Neither, because the volume increases.

## Explanation

In the first case an infinitesimal change in external pressure reverses the piston motion, with no dissipation. Free expansion has a finite pressure difference, and restoring the initial state would require an intervention from outside.
