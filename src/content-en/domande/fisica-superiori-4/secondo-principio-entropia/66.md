A body of constant heat capacity is heated from $200$ to $800\,\mathrm{K}$. At what temperature has it accumulated exactly half of its final entropy change?

- $500\,\mathrm{K}$
- $400\,\mathrm{K}$
- $600\,\mathrm{K}$
- $300\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

Half the entropy change is half the logarithm of the final temperature ratio. Since that ratio is four, the ratio at the requested temperature is its square root, which is two.

$$
\ln\frac{T}{200\,\mathrm{K}}=\frac12\ln4=\ln2\quad\Longrightarrow\quad T=400\,\mathrm{K}.
$$
