In a reversible engine cycle, the hot reservoir loses entropy. How does the cold reservoir's entropy change?

- It decreases by the same magnitude.
- It remains unchanged because its temperature is constant.
- It necessarily increases by a larger amount.
- It increases by the same magnitude.

## Explanation

The working fluid returns to its initial state, so its net contribution is zero. In a reversible cycle the sum of the reservoir contributions is also zero. Their individual changes therefore have equal magnitudes and opposite signs.
