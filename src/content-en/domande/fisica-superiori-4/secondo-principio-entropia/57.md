An actual engine completes a cycle and returns to its initial state. What is the entropy change of its working fluid?

- Zero.
- Positive for every actual cycle.
- Negative if the fluid rejects heat to the cold reservoir.
- Equal to the net heat absorbed.

## Explanation

Since the final state equals the initial state, the fluid entropy returns to the same value. In an actual cycle total entropy can increase through changes in the surroundings.
