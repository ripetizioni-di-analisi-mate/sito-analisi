A cyclic engine absorbs $Q_h=2880\,\mathrm{J}$ from the hot reservoir and rejects $Q_c=1500\,\mathrm{J}$ to the cold one. Which value is closest to its efficiency?

- $\eta=0.28$
- $\eta=0.92$
- $\eta=1$
- $\eta=0.479$

## Explanation

The internal energy of the working fluid returns to its initial value every cycle. The difference between the heats therefore equals the work. For efficiency, divide the work by all the energy absorbed from the hot reservoir.

$$
\eta=\frac{Q_h-Q_c}{Q_h}=\frac{2880-1500}{2880}\simeq0.479.
$$
