A body of constant heat capacity is first heated from $300$ to $400\,\mathrm{K}$ and then from $400$ to $500\,\mathrm{K}$. On which segment does the body's entropy increase more?

- On the first segment.
- On the second segment.
- The entropy changes are equal because the intervals are equal.
- The body's entropy decreases on both segments.

## Explanation

The temperature intervals are equal, so the body absorbs equal amounts of heat. Each joule on the first segment is transferred at a lower temperature, however. In ratio terms, $\dfrac{400}{300}=\dfrac43$ exceeds $\dfrac{500}{400}=\dfrac54$ and gives a larger logarithm.
