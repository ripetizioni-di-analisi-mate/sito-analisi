Between reservoirs at $800$ and $400\,\mathrm{K}$ a cyclic engine absorbs $1000\,\mathrm{J}$ per cycle. Which amount of rejected heat describes the reversible case?

- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $500\,\mathrm{J}$
- $300\,\mathrm{J}$
- $700\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

In the reversible case the ratio of heat magnitudes equals the ratio of absolute temperatures. With the same heat input, rejecting more is consistent with an irreversible cycle that produces less work. Rejecting less would give an efficiency above the limit.

$$
Q_c=Q_h\frac{T_c}{T_h}=1000\frac{400}{800}\,\mathrm{J}=500\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
