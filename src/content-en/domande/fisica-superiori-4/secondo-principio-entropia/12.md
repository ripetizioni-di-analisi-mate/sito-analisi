Half a mole of ideal gas expands into a vacuum inside an insulated vessel until it occupies twice its initial volume. Use $R=8.31\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$ and $\ln2\simeq0.693$. Calculate the gas entropy change.Choose the closest value.

- $\Delta S=0\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $\Delta S=-2.88\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $\Delta S\simeq 2.88\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $\Delta S=5.76\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

In the actual process heat and work are both zero, so the ideal gas temperature stays constant. A reversible isotherm can be used between the same states. The entropy contribution is $nR\ln2$.

$$
\Delta S=0.5\cdot8.31\ln2\,\mathrm{J/K}\simeq2.88\,\mathrm{J/K}.
$$
