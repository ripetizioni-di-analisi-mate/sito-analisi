A cyclic engine exchanges heat only between $T_h=600\,\mathrm{K}$ and $T_c=300\,\mathrm{K}$. It absorbs $1200\,\mathrm{J}$ and the total entropy change per cycle is $1\,\mathrm{J/K}$. How much work does it produce?

- $300\,\mathrm{J}$
- $600\,\mathrm{J}$
- $900\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The hot reservoir loses two joules per kelvin and the sum must equal one. The cold reservoir therefore gains three joules per kelvin, which corresponds to nine hundred joules of rejected heat. Subtract this from the absorbed heat to find the work.

$$
\begin{gathered}
Q_c=300\left(1+\frac{1200}{600}\right)\,\mathrm{J}=900\,\mathrm{J},\\[6pt]
W=(1200-900)\,\mathrm{J}=300\,\mathrm{J}.
\end{gathered}
$$
