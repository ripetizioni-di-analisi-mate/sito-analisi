A refrigerator exchanges heat only with a compartment at $240\,\mathrm{K}$ and surroundings at $300\,\mathrm{K}$. The manufacturer reports a refrigeration coefficient of performance of $5$. What follows?

- The value is incompatible with the Carnot refrigeration limit.
- The value is possible and implies reversible operation.
- The value is possible for an irreversible refrigerator.
- The value can only be evaluated if the cycle duration is known.

## Explanation

The cold temperature is in the numerator of the refrigeration coefficient. The reported ratio exceeds this result, although it matches the limit for the device used as a heat pump.

$$
\mathrm{COP}_{f,C}=\frac{240}{300-240}=4<5.
$$
