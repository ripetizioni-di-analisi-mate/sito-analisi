An engine must achieve an efficiency of $40\%$ with a hot reservoir at $700\,\mathrm{K}$. What is the greatest allowable cold reservoir temperature under the reversible limit?

- $280\,\mathrm{K}$
- $420\,\mathrm{K}$
- $490\,\mathrm{K}$
- $700\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

The required efficiency must be at most the Carnot limit. The inequality $0.40\leq1-\dfrac{T_c}{700\,\mathrm{K}}$ gives an upper bound on $T_c$. At the highest temperature only a reversible cycle reaches the specified efficiency.

$$
T_{c,\max}=(1-0.40)\cdot700\,\mathrm{K}=420\,\mathrm{K}.
$$
