A gas goes between the same initial and final states along two different paths, one reversible and one irreversible. Which quantity necessarily has the same value in both processes?

- The heat entering the gas.
- The work done by the gas.
- The total entropy generated by the system and surroundings.
- The entropy change of the gas.

## Explanation

The gas entropy is a state function. The difference between the final and initial values is the same along both paths. Heat and work depend on the processes followed, and entropy generation depends on the presence of irreversibility.
