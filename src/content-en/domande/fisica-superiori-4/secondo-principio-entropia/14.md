A refrigerator operates between a compartment at $260\,\mathrm{K}$ and surroundings at $300\,\mathrm{K}$. Calculate the minimum work required to remove $520\,\mathrm{J}$ from the compartment.

- $W_{\min}=520\,\mathrm{J}$
- $W_{\min}=80\,\mathrm{J}$
- $W_{\min}=40\,\mathrm{J}$
- $W_{\min}=600\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The two reservoirs set the maximum refrigeration coefficient of performance. Dividing the heat to be removed by that coefficient gives the minimum work, attained in the reversible case.

$$
\begin{gathered}
\mathrm{COP}_{f,C}=\frac{260}{300-260}=6.5,\\[6pt]
W_{\min}=\frac{520\,\mathrm{J}}{6.5}=80\,\mathrm{J}.
\end{gathered}
$$
