Four independent particles occupy a volume notionally divided into two equal halves. Each particle is equally likely to be in either half. What is the probability that all are in the left half?

- $\dfrac1{16}$
- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac18$

## Explanation

The condition must hold for all four particles. By independence, multiply the four individual probabilities, each equal to one-half.

$$
P=\left(\frac12\right)^4=\frac1{16}.
$$
