A reversible engine operates between two reservoirs. The hot temperature is $750\,\mathrm{K}$ and its efficiency is $0.40$. What is the temperature of the cold reservoir?

- $T_c=450\,\mathrm{K}$
- $T_c=300\,\mathrm{K}$
- $T_c=1250\,\mathrm{K}$
- $T_c=750\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

Reversibility permits the use of the Carnot equality. The efficiency gives the temperature ratio $\dfrac{T_c}{T_h}=1-\eta$, and multiplying it by the hot temperature gives the cold temperature.

$$
T_c=T_h(1-\eta)=750(1-0.40)\,\mathrm{K}=450\,\mathrm{K}.
$$
