As a reversible engine, a device absorbs $700\,\mathrm{J}$ from the hot reservoir and produces $280\,\mathrm{J}$ of work. The same device runs the cycle in reverse between the same reservoirs. How much heat does it remove from the cold reservoir?

- $280\,\mathrm{J}$
- $420\,\mathrm{J}$
- $700\,\mathrm{J}$
- $980\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

In forward operation the device rejects four hundred twenty joules to the cold reservoir. Exactly reversing the reversible cycle reverses the directions of the exchanges and preserves their magnitudes. The device now removes from the cold reservoir the heat it previously rejected there.

$$
Q_c=Q_h-W=(700-280)\,\mathrm{J}=420\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
