In a cyclic process a gas absorbs $420\,\mathrm{J}$ of heat and releases $500\,\mathrm{J}$. What does the first law establish about work?

- The gas does $80\,\mathrm{J}$ of work.
- The gas receives $920\,\mathrm{J}$ of work.
- The net work is zero.
- The gas receives $80\,\mathrm{J}$ of work.

## Explanation

At the end of the cycle the gas returns to its initial state, so the change in internal energy is zero. By the first law, the work done by the gas therefore equals the net heat exchanged. The net heat is negative, so the work done by the gas is negative and the gas receives $80\,\mathrm{J}$ of work.

$$
W=Q=(420-500)\,\mathrm{J}=-80\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
