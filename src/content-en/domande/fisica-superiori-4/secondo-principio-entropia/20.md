A cyclic device absorbs $420\,\mathrm{J}$ of heat and releases $500\,\mathrm{J}$. What does the first law imply about work?

- It delivers $80\,\mathrm{J}$ of work.
- It receives $920\,\mathrm{J}$ of work.
- The net work is zero.
- It receives $80\,\mathrm{J}$ of work.

## Explanation

More energy leaves as heat than enters. The working fluid completes a cycle, so the difference must enter as work.

$$
W=Q=(420-500)\,\mathrm{J}=-80\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
