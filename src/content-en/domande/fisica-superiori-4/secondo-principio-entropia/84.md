A gas held at constant temperature and volume receives work from a stirrer. After the motion dissipates, the heat produced is rejected to a colder reservoir and the gas returns exactly to its initial state. Which combination is correct?

- $\Delta S_{\mathrm{gas}}>0$ and $\Delta S_{\mathrm{tot}}=0$.
- $\Delta S_{\mathrm{gas}}<0$ and $\Delta S_{\mathrm{tot}}>0$.
- $\Delta S_{\mathrm{gas}}=0$ and $\Delta S_{\mathrm{tot}}=0$.
- $\Delta S_{\mathrm{gas}}=0$ and $\Delta S_{\mathrm{tot}}>0$.

## Explanation

The gas returns to the same state, so its entropy change is zero. Stirring dissipates the work and the reservoir receives heat, increasing the total entropy.
