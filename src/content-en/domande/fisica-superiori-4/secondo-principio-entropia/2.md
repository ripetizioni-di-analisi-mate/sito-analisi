A heat engine absorbs $2160\,\mathrm{J}$ per cycle and has an efficiency of $0.28$. Calculate the work produced in one cycle.Choose the closest value.

- $W\simeq 605\,\mathrm{J}$
- $W=2160\,\mathrm{J}$
- $W=1560\,\mathrm{J}$
- $W=7710\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Efficiency is the share of the absorbed heat converted into work. To find the output energy, multiply the heat input by that share.

$$
W=\eta Q_h=0.28\cdot2160\,\mathrm{J}=604.8\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
