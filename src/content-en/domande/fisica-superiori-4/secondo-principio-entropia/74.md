An ideal gas in a rigid insulated vessel expands into a vacuum, doubling the volume it occupies. Which pair of statements is correct at final equilibrium?

- The pressure stays constant and entropy increases.
- The pressure halves and entropy increases.
- The pressure halves and entropy stays constant.
- The pressure doubles and entropy decreases.

## Explanation

In free expansion no work is done on the surroundings and no heat is transferred, so the temperature of the ideal gas stays constant. For fixed temperature and amount, doubling the volume halves the pressure. The entropy change is $nR\ln2>0$.
