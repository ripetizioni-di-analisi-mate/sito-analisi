A reversible refrigerator maintains a compartment at $270\,\mathrm{K}$ and has $\mathrm{COP}_f=6$. What is the temperature of the reservoir to which it rejects heat?

- $225\,\mathrm{K}$
- $270\,\mathrm{K}$
- $315\,\mathrm{K}$
- $360\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

The coefficient is the ratio of the cold temperature to the temperature difference. First find that difference and then add it to the cold temperature.

$$
T_h=T_c+\frac{T_c}{\mathrm{COP}_f}=\left(270+\frac{270}{6}\right)\,\mathrm{K}=315\,\mathrm{K}.
$$
