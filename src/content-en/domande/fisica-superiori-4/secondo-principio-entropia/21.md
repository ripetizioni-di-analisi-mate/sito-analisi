A first reversible engine absorbs $900\,\mathrm{J}$ at $900\,\mathrm{K}$ and rejects heat at $600\,\mathrm{K}$. A second reversible engine absorbs all of that rejected heat and rejects part of it at $300\,\mathrm{K}$. How much total work is produced?

- $300\,\mathrm{J}$
- $450\,\mathrm{J}$
- $750\,\mathrm{J}$
- $600\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The first cycle rejects six hundred joules because reversibility makes the heat ratio equal the temperature ratio. The second cycle rejects half of six hundred joules. The energy remaining after both engines is the heat rejected by the final stage.

$$
\begin{gathered}
Q_{\mathrm{int}}=900\frac{600}{900}\,\mathrm{J}=600\,\mathrm{J},\\[6pt]
Q_c=600\frac{300}{600}\,\mathrm{J}=300\,\mathrm{J},\\[6pt]
W_{\mathrm{tot}}=600\,\mathrm{J}.
\end{gathered}
$$
