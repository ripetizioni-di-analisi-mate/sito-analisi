Between $600$ and $300\,\mathrm{K}$ an actual engine absorbs $900\,\mathrm{J}$ and rejects $540\,\mathrm{J}$ per cycle. How much more work could a reversible cycle deliver with the same heat input?

- $180\,\mathrm{J}$
- $360\,\mathrm{J}$
- $450\,\mathrm{J}$
- $90\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The first law gives the actual work. The temperatures determine the reversible work for the same input. Subtract the actual value from the reversible value to compare the two outputs per cycle.

$$
\begin{gathered}
W_{\mathrm{rev}}=\left(1-\frac{300}{600}\right)900\,\mathrm{J}=450\,\mathrm{J},\\[6pt]
W_{\mathrm{rev}}-W_{\mathrm{eff}}=(450-360)\,\mathrm{J}=90\,\mathrm{J}.
\end{gathered}
$$
