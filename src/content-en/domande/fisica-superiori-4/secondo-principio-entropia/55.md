What is the SI unit of $\Delta S$ in a reversible constant-temperature calculation?

- $\mathrm{J}\cdot\mathrm{K}$
- $\mathrm{W/K}$
- $\mathrm{J/K}$
- $\mathrm{K/J}$

## Explanation

In the ratio $\dfrac{Q_{\mathrm{rev}}}{T}$ the numerator is energy and the denominator is absolute temperature. The unit is therefore joule per kelvin.
