A heat engine absorbs $2400\,\mathrm{J}$ and delivers $620\,\mathrm{J}$ of work per cycle. What is the magnitude of the heat rejected to the cold reservoir?

- $Q_c=3020\,\mathrm{J}$
- $Q_c=1780\,\mathrm{J}$
- $Q_c=620\,\mathrm{J}$
- $Q_c=-1780\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Of the energy entering, $620$ joules leave as work. Since the working fluid completes a cycle, the rest leaves as heat. The term $Q_c$ is the magnitude of the heat leaving.

$$
Q_c=Q_h-W=(2400-620)\,\mathrm{J}=1780\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
