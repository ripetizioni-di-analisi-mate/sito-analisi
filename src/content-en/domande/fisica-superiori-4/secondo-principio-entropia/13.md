Two moles of a monatomic ideal gas, with $C_V=\dfrac32R$, expand reversibly at constant pressure until the volume doubles. With $R=8.31\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$ and $\ln2\simeq0.693$, what is the entropy change of the gas?

- $\Delta S\simeq11.5\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $\Delta S\simeq17.3\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $\Delta S\simeq83.1\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $\Delta S\simeq28.8\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

At constant pressure the equation of state $pV=nRT$ gives $\dfrac{T_f}{T_i}=\dfrac{V_f}{V_i}$, so the absolute temperature doubles as well. For an ideal gas $\Delta S=nC_V\ln\dfrac{T_f}{T_i}+nR\ln\dfrac{V_f}{V_i}$, and here both ratios equal $2$. Therefore
$$
\Delta S=n\left(\frac32R+R\right)\ln2=2\cdot\frac52\cdot8.31\cdot0.693\,\mathrm{J/K}\simeq28.8\,\mathrm{J/K}.
$$
The value $11.5\,\mathrm{J/K}$ accounts only for the change in volume and $17.3\,\mathrm{J/K}$ only for the change in temperature.
