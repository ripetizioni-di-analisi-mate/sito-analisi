A body of heat capacity $250\,\mathrm{J/K}$ cools from $400$ to $200\,\mathrm K$ and loses $173\,\mathrm{J/K}$ of entropy. At which constant temperature would an ideal reservoir lose the same entropy if it released the same heat as the body?

- About $145\,\mathrm K$.
- About $433\,\mathrm K$.
- About $500\,\mathrm K$.
- About $289\,\mathrm K$.

## Explanation

The body releases $250\cdot200=50000\,\mathrm J$ of heat. For a reservoir at constant temperature the magnitude of the entropy loss is $\dfrac{Q}{T}$. The required temperature is therefore $\dfrac{50000}{173}\,\mathrm K\simeq289\,\mathrm K$.
