A body with constant heat capacity $250\,\mathrm{J/K}$ cools from $400$ to $200\,\mathrm{K}$ without a phase change. Use $\ln2\simeq0.693$. Calculate its entropy change.Choose the closest value.

- $\Delta S=173\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $\Delta S=-125\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $\Delta S=-250\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $\Delta S\simeq -173\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

The formula for constant heat capacity uses the ratio of final to initial temperature. In this cooling process the ratio is one-half, so its logarithm is the negative of the logarithm of two.

$$
\Delta S=250\ln\frac{200}{400}\,\mathrm{J/K}=-250\ln2\,\mathrm{J/K}\simeq-173\,\mathrm{J/K}.
$$
