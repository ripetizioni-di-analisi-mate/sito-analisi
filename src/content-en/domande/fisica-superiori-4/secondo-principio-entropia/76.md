Two samples of the same ideal gas expand isothermally with the same ratio of final to initial volume. The second contains three times the amount of the first. How do their entropy changes compare?

- The changes are equal.
- The second is one-third of the first.
- The second is nine times the first.
- The second is three times the first.

## Explanation

At constant temperature, $\Delta S=nR\ln\dfrac{V_f}{V_i}$. The logarithm and the constant $R$ are the same in both cases. Only the amount of gas changes, so $\Delta S$ changes in the same proportion.
