In an independent-particle model, the volume accessible to each of three particles is tripled. By what factor does the number of configurations of their positions increase?

- $3$
- $27$
- $9$
- $81$

## Explanation

Each particle has three times as many position choices. The choices are independent, so the factors for the three positions are multiplied.

$$
\frac{\Omega_f}{\Omega_i}=3\cdot3\cdot3=27.
$$
