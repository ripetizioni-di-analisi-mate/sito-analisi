How much heat must a heat engine with efficiency $0.30$ absorb to produce $270\,\mathrm{J}$ of work?

- $81\,\mathrm{J}$
- $270\,\mathrm{J}$
- $630\,\mathrm{J}$
- $900\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Work is thirty percent of the heat absorbed. To recover the initial energy, divide the work by the fraction converted.

$$
Q_h=\frac{W}{\eta}=\frac{270\,\mathrm{J}}{0.30}=900\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
