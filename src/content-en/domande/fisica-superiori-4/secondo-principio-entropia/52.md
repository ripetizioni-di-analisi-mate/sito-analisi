Direct heat transfer between reservoirs at $600$ and $300\,\mathrm{K}$ generates $3\,\mathrm{J/K}$ of total entropy. How much heat passes from hot to cold?

- $600\,\mathrm{J}$
- $900\,\mathrm{J}$
- $2700\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1800\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The entropy contributions have opposite signs. For each joule transferred, their net contribution is the difference of the reciprocal temperatures. Divide the given change by this net contribution per joule.

$$
\begin{gathered}
\Delta S_{\mathrm{tot}}=Q\left(\frac{1}{300\,\mathrm{K}}-\frac{1}{600\,\mathrm{K}}\right),\\[6pt]
Q=3\cdot600\,\mathrm{J}=1800\,\mathrm{J}.
\end{gathered}
$$
