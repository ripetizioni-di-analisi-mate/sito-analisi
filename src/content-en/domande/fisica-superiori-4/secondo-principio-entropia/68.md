Two identical bodies with heat capacity $200\,\mathrm{J/K}$ are initially at $300$ and $500\,\mathrm{K}$. They reach equilibrium in isolation with no work. With $\ln\dfrac{16}{15}\simeq0.06454$, which value is closest to the total entropy change?

- $-12.9\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $66.7\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $12.9\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

The heat capacities are equal in the energy balance, so equilibrium is at the arithmetic mean temperature, $400\,\mathrm{K}$. For entropy, add the logarithms of the two ratios. The sum of the logarithms is the logarithm of their product.

$$
\Delta S_{\mathrm{tot}}=200\ln\frac{400^2}{300\cdot500}\,\mathrm{J/K}=200\ln\frac{16}{15}\,\mathrm{J/K}\simeq12.9\,\mathrm{J/K}.
$$
