An engine with efficiency $0.40$ rejects $840\,\mathrm{J}$ to the cold reservoir per cycle. How much heat does it absorb?

- $1400\,\mathrm{J}$
- $504\,\mathrm{J}$
- $2100\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1180\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The rejected heat is the complement to one of the useful fraction. Eight hundred forty joules is therefore sixty percent of the amount sought.

$$
Q_h=\frac{Q_c}{1-\eta}=\frac{840\,\mathrm{J}}{0.60}=1400\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
