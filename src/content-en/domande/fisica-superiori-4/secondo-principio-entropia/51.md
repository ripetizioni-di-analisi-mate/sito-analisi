Two reservoirs at $600$ and $300\,\mathrm{K}$ transfer heat directly from hot to cold. The cold reservoir's entropy increases by $4\,\mathrm{J/K}$. How much does the total entropy increase?

- $4\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $6\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $2\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

The cold reservoir's contribution gives the heat it received. The hot reservoir gives up the same energy, but its contribution is negative and is divided by the higher temperature.

$$
Q=300\cdot4\,\mathrm{J}=1200\,\mathrm{J},\qquad\Delta S_{\mathrm{tot}}=\left(4-\frac{1200}{600}\right)\,\mathrm{J/K}=2\,\mathrm{J/K}.
$$
