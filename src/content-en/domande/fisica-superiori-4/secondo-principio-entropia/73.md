Two moles of ideal gas are compressed at constant temperature to half their initial volume. With $R=8.31\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$ and $\ln2\simeq0.693$, which value is closest to the entropy change of the gas?

- $-11.5\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $11.5\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $-5.76\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

The initial and final temperatures are the same, so only the volume term remains. The final-to-initial volume ratio is one-half, so the logarithm is negative.

$$
\Delta S=2R\ln\frac12=-2R\ln2\simeq-11.5\,\mathrm{J/K}.
$$
