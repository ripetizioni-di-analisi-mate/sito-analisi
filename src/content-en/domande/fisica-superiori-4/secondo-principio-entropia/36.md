A Carnot cycle operates between $T_h=650\,\mathrm{K}$ and $T_c=350\,\mathrm{K}$. Along the hot isotherm, entropy increases by $4\,\mathrm{J/K}$. How much work does one cycle deliver?

- $2600\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1400\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1200\,\mathrm{J}$
- $4000\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Cycle closure and the reversible adiabatic stages require the same entropy span for both isotherms. The work is the difference between the heats transferred on the isotherms.

$$
W=Q_h-Q_c=(650-350)\,\mathrm{K}\cdot4\,\mathrm{J/K}=1200\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
