A monatomic ideal gas with $C_V=\dfrac32R$ expands reversibly and adiabatically from $2\,\mathrm{L}$ while its temperature falls to one-quarter of its initial value. What is its final volume?

- $8\,\mathrm{L}$
- $4\,\mathrm{L}$
- $16\,\mathrm{L}$
- $32\,\mathrm{L}$

## Explanation

Reversibility and no heat transfer imply constant entropy. In the gas formula the decrease from temperature is offset by the increase from volume. Solving for the volume ratio gives a factor of eight.

$$
\begin{gathered}
\ln\frac{V_f}{V_i}=-\frac32\ln\frac14=\ln8,\\[6pt]
V_f=8V_i=16\,\mathrm{L}.
\end{gathered}
$$
