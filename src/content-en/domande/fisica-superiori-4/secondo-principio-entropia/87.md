A cyclic refrigerator removes $600\,\mathrm{J}$ at $250\,\mathrm{K}$, receives $300\,\mathrm{J}$ of work, and rejects all its outgoing heat to a reservoir at $300\,\mathrm{K}$. What total entropy change does the cycle produce?

- $-0.6\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $3\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $0.6\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

The cold reservoir loses $600\,\mathrm{J}$. The hot reservoir receives the sum of the removed heat and the work input, which is $900\,\mathrm{J}$. Add the changes with these two signs, while the working fluid has no net change over a cycle.

$$
\Delta S_{\mathrm{tot}}=\left(-\frac{600}{250}+\frac{900}{300}\right)\,\mathrm{J/K}=0.6\,\mathrm{J/K}.
$$
