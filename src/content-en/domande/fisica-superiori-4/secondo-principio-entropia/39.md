Apply the first law to the working fluid in a refrigerator. What are the signs of the heat exchanged with the cold side and the work done by the fluid?

- Negative heat, negative work.
- Positive heat, negative work.
- Positive heat, positive work.
- Negative heat, positive work.

## Explanation

The fluid receives heat from the cold reservoir, so that contribution is $+Q_c$. It also receives work from the compressor. The work done by the fluid is therefore $-W_{\mathrm{in}}$.
