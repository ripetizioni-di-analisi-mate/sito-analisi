A body is heated without changing phase from $300$ to $600\,\mathrm{K}$, and its entropy rises by $138.6\,\mathrm{J/K}$. Using $\ln2\simeq0.693$, what is its constant heat capacity?

- $100\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $96\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $300\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $200\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

The entropy change is proportional to the heat capacity with a factor equal to the logarithm of the temperature ratio. Divide the given change by that factor.

$$
C=\frac{\Delta S}{\ln 2}\simeq\frac{138.6}{0.693}\,\mathrm{J/K}=200\,\mathrm{J/K}.
$$
