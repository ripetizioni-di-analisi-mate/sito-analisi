A hot body, mechanically isolated but in thermal contact with a colder reservoir, loses heat. How can its entropy decrease while satisfying the second law?

- The entropy of the combined system decreases whenever the system gives up heat.
- The second law forbids the entropy of any individual body from decreasing.
- The colder reservoir's entropy increases by a greater magnitude.
- The lost heat is converted entirely to work.

## Explanation

The isolated whole for the balance contains the body and the reservoir. Contact across a finite temperature difference is irreversible, so the sum is positive even though the body's contribution is negative.
