Compare two direct transfers with equal amounts of heat from a hot reservoir to a cold reservoir. The hot temperature is brought closer to the cold temperature, which stays fixed. How does total entropy production change?

- It decreases and approaches zero as the temperatures approach equality.
- It increases because the hot body is cooler.
- It stays the same because the transferred heat is unchanged.
- It turns negative before the temperatures become equal.

## Explanation

In the product $Q\left(\dfrac1{T_c}-\dfrac1{T_h}\right)$ the difference is positive for $T_h>T_c$. As $T_h$ comes closer to $T_c$, the reciprocals come closer and their difference approaches zero.
