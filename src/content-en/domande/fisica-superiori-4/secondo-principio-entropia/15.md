In $20\,\mathrm{s}$ a cyclic engine absorbs $150\,\mathrm{kJ}$ and delivers an average mechanical power of $3\,\mathrm{kW}$. What is its efficiency?

- $0.02$
- $0.6$
- $0.4$
- $2.5$

## Explanation

First express the work over the same interval as the heat input. One kilowatt is one kilojoule per second, so twenty seconds yields sixty kilojoules.

$$
\begin{gathered}
W=3\cdot20\,\mathrm{kJ}=60\,\mathrm{kJ},\\[6pt]
\eta=\frac{60}{150}=0.40.
\end{gathered}
$$
