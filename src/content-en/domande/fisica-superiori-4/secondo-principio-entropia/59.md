On a temperature–entropy diagram, what are the units of the rectangular area $T\,\Delta S$ under a horizontal segment?

- $\mathrm{K}$
- $\mathrm{J/K}$
- $\mathrm{J}$
- $\mathrm{J/K^2}$

## Explanation

The height is measured in kelvins and the horizontal width in joules per kelvin. The kelvins cancel in the product. On a reversible isotherm the area represents an amount of heat.
