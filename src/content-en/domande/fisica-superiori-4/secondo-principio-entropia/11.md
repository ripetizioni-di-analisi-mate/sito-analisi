A cyclic engine absorbs $600\,\mathrm{J}$ at $600\,\mathrm{K}$ and is reported to reject only $240\,\mathrm{J}$ at $300\,\mathrm{K}$. The two reservoirs are its only thermal contacts. Are the data consistent with the second law?

- Yes, because the work comes out positive.
- No, because the total entropy would decrease.
- Yes, because the working fluid returns to its initial state.
- No, because energy absorbed must equal energy rejected as heat.

## Explanation

The energy balance would allow $360\,\mathrm{J}$ of work. The working fluid, however, has zero entropy change over a cycle. The calculation for both reservoirs gives a negative total, which is incompatible with the second law.

$$
\Delta S_{\mathrm{tot}}=\left(-\frac{600}{600}+\frac{240}{300}\right)\,\mathrm{J/K}=-0.2\,\mathrm{J/K}.
$$
