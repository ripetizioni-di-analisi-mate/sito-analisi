In the independent-particle model, the probability that all $N$ particles are in the left half is $2^{-N}$. How does the probability change from $N$ to $N+3$?

- It is divided by three.
- It is divided by eight.
- It is halved.
- It is multiplied by eight.

## Explanation

Each additional particle must be in the same half. Independence adds a factor of one-half for each of the three particles.

$$
2^{-(N+3)}=2^{-N}2^{-3}=\frac{2^{-N}}8.
$$
