The graph shows the Carnot limit with $T_c=300\,\mathrm{K}$. The marked point represents a reported engine at $T_h=500\,\mathrm{K}$ with $\eta=0.47$. What does its position indicate?

::grafico{f="1-300/x" punti="500,0.47,pieno,P" segmenti="500,0.4,500,0.47" xmin=300 xmax=700 ymin=0 ymax=0.65 nomi="Tₕ,η" titolo="The curve gives the upper bound and P represents the reported value."}

- The efficiency is consistent with an irreversible engine.
- The engine is reversible because the point is on the curve.
- The reported efficiency exceeds the theoretical maximum.
- The curve is the theoretical minimum and the engine meets it.

## Explanation

At the given hot temperature, the curve bounds the attainable efficiencies. The point is above that value, so a cyclic engine between the stated reservoirs alone cannot achieve it.

$$
\eta_C=1-\frac{300}{500}=0.40<0.47.
$$
