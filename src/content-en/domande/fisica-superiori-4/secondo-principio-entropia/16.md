An engine absorbs heat at $8\,\mathrm{kW}$ and is $25\%$ efficient. How long does it take to produce $48\,\mathrm{kJ}$ of work?

- $6\,\mathrm{s}$
- $8\,\mathrm{s}$
- $24\,\mathrm{s}$
- $192\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Efficiency applies to the heat input rate, so useful power is one-quarter of eight kilowatts. Time is the specified work divided by the useful power.

$$
t=\frac{48\,\mathrm{kJ}}{0.25\cdot8\,\mathrm{kW}}=24\,\mathrm{s}.
$$
