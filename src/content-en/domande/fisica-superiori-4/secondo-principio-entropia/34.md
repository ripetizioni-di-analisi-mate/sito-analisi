In which stage of a Carnot engine cycle does heat enter from the hot reservoir?

- During isothermal expansion at the hot temperature.
- During adiabatic expansion.
- During isothermal compression at the cold temperature.
- During adiabatic compression.

## Explanation

The hot reservoir is in thermal contact during expansion at its own temperature. In the cold isotherm the fluid rejects heat. The two adiabatic stages involve no heat transfer.
