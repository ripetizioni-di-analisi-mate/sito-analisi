A refrigerator rejects $1050\,\mathrm{J}$ to the surroundings and receives $350\,\mathrm{J}$ of work per cycle. What is its refrigeration coefficient of performance?

- $3$
- $4$
- $2$
- $0.5$

## Explanation

The heat removed from the compartment is the heat rejected to the surroundings minus the work input. The refrigerator coefficient uses the heat removed from the compartment, whereas the ratio with the rejected heat would describe heating.

$$
Q_c=(1050-350)\,\mathrm{J}=700\,\mathrm{J},\qquad\mathrm{COP}_f=\frac{700}{350}=2.
$$
