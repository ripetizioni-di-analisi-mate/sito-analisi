A mass of liquid freezes in equilibrium at $320\,\mathrm{K}$ and rejects $1600\,\mathrm{J}$. How much does the material's entropy change?

- $5\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $-5\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

In an equilibrium phase change the temperature stays constant. The material rejects heat, so the numerator and therefore the entropy change are negative.

$$
\Delta S=\frac{-1600\,\mathrm{J}}{320\,\mathrm{K}}=-5\,\mathrm{J/K}.
$$
