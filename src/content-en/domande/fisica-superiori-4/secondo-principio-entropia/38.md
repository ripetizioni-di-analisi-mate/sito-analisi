During the cold isothermal compression in a Carnot cycle, how do the entropies of the working fluid and cold reservoir change?

- The fluid entropy decreases and the reservoir entropy increases.
- Both increase.
- Both remain constant.
- The fluid entropy increases and the reservoir entropy decreases.

## Explanation

The fluid gives up heat to the reservoir. At constant temperature the signed heat divided by temperature is negative for the fluid and positive for the reservoir. Since the transfer is reversible, the two ratios have equal magnitudes.
