A reservoir at $600\,\mathrm{K}$ transfers $1440\,\mathrm{J}$ directly to a reservoir at $300\,\mathrm{K}$. What is the total entropy change?

- $\Delta S_{\mathrm{tot}}=-2.4\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $\Delta S_{\mathrm{tot}}=4.8\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $\Delta S_{\mathrm{tot}}=2.4\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $\Delta S_{\mathrm{tot}}=0\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

The hot reservoir loses the same energy that the cold one receives. The entropy changes therefore have opposite signs. Divide each signed heat by its reservoir temperature and add the results.

$$
\Delta S_{\mathrm{tot}}=\left(-\frac{1440}{600}+\frac{1440}{300}\right)\,\mathrm{J/K}=2.4\,\mathrm{J/K}.
$$
