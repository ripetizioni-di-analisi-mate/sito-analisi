A heat engine operates alongside a hypothetical device that returns all heat rejected to the cold reservoir back to the hot one, without requiring work. What would be the net effect of the pair?

- Extract heat from just one reservoir and convert it all to work.
- Leave both reservoirs unchanged and produce no work.
- Remove heat from the cold reservoir and use it to heat the hot one.
- Transfer all the heat absorbed from the hot reservoir to the cold one.

## Explanation

The cold reservoir returns exactly the heat it received and therefore remains unchanged. The hot reservoir loses a net amount $Q_h-Q_c$. All of this difference is delivered as work, violating Kelvin–Planck.
