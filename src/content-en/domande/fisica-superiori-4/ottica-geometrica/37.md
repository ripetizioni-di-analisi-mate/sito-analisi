A lens of power $+4\,\mathrm D$ is placed in contact with a second lens. The equivalent focal length is $+0.50\,\mathrm m$. What is the second lens's optical power?

- $D_2=2\,\mathrm D$.
- $D_2=6\,\mathrm D$.
- $D_2=-2\,\mathrm D$.
- $D_2=-4\,\mathrm D$.

## Explanation

The equivalent focal length gives $D_{\mathrm{eq}}=\dfrac{1}{0.50}\,\mathrm D=+2\,\mathrm D$. Since the lenses are in contact, $D_2=D_{\mathrm{eq}}-D_1=(2-4)\,\mathrm D=-2\,\mathrm D$.
