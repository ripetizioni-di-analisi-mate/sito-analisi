Light enters a slab of index $1.50$ normally from air. Which pair describes its direction and speed in glass?

- The direction changes and the speed decreases.
- The direction is unchanged and the speed decreases.
- The direction is unchanged and the speed increases.
- The direction and speed are unchanged.

## Explanation

With $\theta_1=0^\circ$, Snell's law also gives $\theta_2=0^\circ$. The direction stays the same. The speed in glass is $u=\dfrac{c}{1.50}$, below the speed in air.
