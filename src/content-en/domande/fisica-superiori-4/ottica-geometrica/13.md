An object $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ high is $8\,\mathrm{cm}$ from a lens. Its image is upright and $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ high. Find the signed value of $q$.

- $q=-24\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $q=24\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $q=-8\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $q=-4\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

Magnification is positive because the image is upright, so $M=\dfrac{6}{2}=3$. The relation $M=-\dfrac{q}{p}$ gives $q=-3\cdot8\,\mathrm{cm}=-24\,\mathrm{cm}$. The sign agrees with a virtual image of a real object.
