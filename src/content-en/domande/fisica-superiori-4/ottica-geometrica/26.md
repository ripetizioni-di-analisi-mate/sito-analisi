A lens of focal length $f>0$ is replaced by a lens of focal length $2f$, and the object distance is doubled. The object remains outside the focal length. How do $q$ and $M$ change?

- $q$ doubles and $M$ doubles.
- $q$ stays the same and $M$ halves.
- $q$ halves and $M$ changes sign.
- $q$ doubles and $M$ stays the same.

## Explanation

The new distance is $\dfrac{(2f)(2p)}{2p-2f}=2\dfrac{fp}{p-f}=2q$. The new magnification is $-\dfrac{2q}{2p}=M$. The geometry scales up with the same proportions.
