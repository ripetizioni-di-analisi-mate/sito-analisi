Two thin lenses in contact have powers $+2.5\,\mathrm D$ and $-1\,\mathrm D$. What is the power of the combination?

- $D_{\mathrm{eq}}=1.5\,\mathrm D$.
- $D_{\mathrm{eq}}=3.5\,\mathrm D$.
- $D_{\mathrm{eq}}=-1.5\,\mathrm D$.
- $D_{\mathrm{eq}}=-2.5\,\mathrm D$.

## Explanation

For lenses in contact, add the powers with their signs. Thus $D_{\mathrm{eq}}=(2.5-1)\,\mathrm D=+1.5\,\mathrm D$. The second lens reduces the converging power of the first.
