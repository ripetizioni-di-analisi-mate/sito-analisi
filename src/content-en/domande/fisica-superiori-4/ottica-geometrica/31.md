The focus of a convex spherical mirror lies $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ behind its surface. Find the magnitude of its radius of curvature.

- $R=24\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $R=6\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $R=12\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $R=36\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

The focus is virtual and $f=-12\,\mathrm{cm}$. The radius of curvature is a magnitude and equals $R=2|f|=24\,\mathrm{cm}$ in the paraxial approximation.
