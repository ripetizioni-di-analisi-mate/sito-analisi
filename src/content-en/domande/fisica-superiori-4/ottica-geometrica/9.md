A candle is $0.85\,\mathrm m$ from a plane mirror. How far is the candle from its image?

- $0.85\,\mathrm m$.
- $1.7\,\mathrm m$.
- $0.425\,\mathrm m$.
- $2.55\,\mathrm m$.

## Explanation

By symmetry the image is $0.85\,\mathrm m$ behind the mirror. The segments from the candle and from the image to the mirror plane add. The distance is therefore $2\cdot0.85\,\mathrm m=1.70\,\mathrm m$.
