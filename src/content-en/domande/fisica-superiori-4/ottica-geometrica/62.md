Two plane mirrors are perpendicular. A ray in the plane of the two normals reflects once from each. How is the final direction oriented relative to the initial direction?

- Parallel and pointing in the same direction.
- Perpendicular at every incidence angle.
- Offset by the first angle of incidence.
- Parallel and pointing in the opposite direction.

## Explanation

The first reflection reverses the component normal to the first mirror and preserves the tangential component. The second reverses the other component because the normals are perpendicular. The final direction vector is therefore the opposite of the initial one.
