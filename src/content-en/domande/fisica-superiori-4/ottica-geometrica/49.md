A converging lens of focal length $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ forms the image of an object $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ high at distance $p=3f$. What is the signed height of the image?

- $h'=-1\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $h'=1\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $h'=-2\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $h'=-4\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

Since $p=30\,\mathrm{cm}$, the image distance is $q=\dfrac{10\cdot30}{30-10}\,\mathrm{cm}=15\,\mathrm{cm}$. It follows that $h'=-\dfrac{15}{30}\cdot2\,\mathrm{cm}=-1\,\mathrm{cm}$.
