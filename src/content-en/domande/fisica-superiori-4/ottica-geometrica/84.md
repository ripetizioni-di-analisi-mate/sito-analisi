In the same glass, the refractive index for violet light is greater than for red light. The two colors enter from air at the same oblique angle. Which refracted ray is closer to the normal?

- The red ray.
- The two rays have the same angle.
- The violet ray.
- The comparison depends only on the glass thickness.

## Explanation

At equal incidence and approximating the index of air by $1$, $\sin\theta_2=\dfrac{\sin\theta_1}{n_{\mathrm{glass}}}$. For violet light the denominator is larger, so the angle from the normal is smaller.
