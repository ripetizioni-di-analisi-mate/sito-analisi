Two thin lenses in contact have powers $+3\,\mathrm D$ and $-3\,\mathrm D$. What happens to an incident beam parallel to the axis?

- It converges $33\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the combination.
- It diverges as if from a focus $33\,\mathrm{cm}$ away.
- It remains parallel in the thin lens model.
- It converges $17\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the combination.

## Explanation

The total power is $3\,\mathrm D-3\,\mathrm D=0$. Since $D=\dfrac{1}{f}$, zero power describes an afocal system. In the thin lens model a parallel beam stays parallel.
