A ray travels from a medium with $n_1=1.86$ toward air with $n_2=1.00$. Which value is closest to the critical angle from the normal?

- $\theta_c=21.7^\circ$.
- $\theta_c=57.5^\circ$.
- $\theta_c=32.5^\circ$.
- $\theta_c=65.0^\circ$.

## Explanation

At the critical angle the transmitted ray has $\theta_2=90^\circ$. Thus $\sin\theta_c=\dfrac{1.00}{1.86}$ and $\theta_c\simeq32.5^\circ$. An angle of $57.5^\circ$ is the complementary angle measured from the surface.
