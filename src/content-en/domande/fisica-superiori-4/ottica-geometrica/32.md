A concave mirror has radius of curvature $R=48\,\mathrm{cm}$. An object on the axis forms a real image of equal absolute height. How far is the object from the vertex?

- $p=24\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $p=48\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $p=12\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $p=96\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

Since $|M|=\dfrac{q}{p}=1$, we have $q=p$. Substituting $\dfrac{1}{f}=\dfrac{2}{p}$ gives $p=2f=R=48\,\mathrm{cm}$. The object is at the center of curvature.
