A converging lens and a diverging lens are placed in contact. The diverging lens has the smaller focal length magnitude. What kind of combination results?

- Diverging.
- Converging.
- Afocal.
- The type depends on the direction in which light passes through the lenses.

## Explanation

The optical power has a larger magnitude when the focal length magnitude is smaller. The negative contribution therefore has larger magnitude than the positive one. Their sum is negative.
