A coin in a liquid of index $1.50$ appears $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ below the surface when viewed from air ($n=1$) nearly along the normal. Estimate its actual depth.

- $d=4\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $d=6\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $d=9\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $d=12\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

For small angles, $d'\simeq d\dfrac{n_{\mathrm{air}}}{n_{\mathrm{liq}}}$. The given depth is the apparent one $d'$, so $d\simeq6\cdot1.50\,\mathrm{cm}=9\,\mathrm{cm}$. The coin is deeper than its image.
