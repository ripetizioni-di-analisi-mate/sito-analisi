A calculation predicts that a diverging lens of focal length $-12\,\mathrm{cm}$ produces $q=-18\,\mathrm{cm}$ for a real object. What does comparing this with the focal length show?

- The value is possible for any real object because $q<0$.
- The calculated distance is incompatible with the model because $|q|>|f|$.
- The result must describe a real image because $|q|>|f|$.
- The value corresponds to a ray incident along the normal.

## Explanation

For a diverging lens, $|q|=|f|\dfrac{p}{p+|f|}$ with $p>0$. The fraction is always less than one. The virtual image therefore forms between the lens and the focus, less than $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the lens.
