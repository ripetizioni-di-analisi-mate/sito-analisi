A corrective lens is marked $-5\,\mathrm D$. What is its signed focal length in centimeters?

- $f=20\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $f=-5\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $f=-20\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $f=-0.2\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

The reciprocal of a power in diopters gives the focal length in meters. We obtain $f=\dfrac{1}{-5}\,\mathrm m=-0.20\,\mathrm m=-20\,\mathrm{cm}$. The negative sign corresponds to a diverging lens.
