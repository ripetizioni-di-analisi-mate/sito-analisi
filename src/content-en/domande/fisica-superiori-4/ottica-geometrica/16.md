A converging lens with $f=12\,\mathrm{cm}$ forms a real image whose absolute height is twice that of the object. What is $p$?

- $p=8\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $p=24\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $p=18\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $p=36\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

For a real image, $|M|=2$ implies $q=2p$. Thus $\dfrac{1}{f}=\dfrac{1}{p}+\dfrac{1}{2p}=\dfrac{3}{2p}$ and $p=\dfrac{3f}{2}=18\,\mathrm{cm}$. The value lies between $f$ and $2f$, where the real image is enlarged.
