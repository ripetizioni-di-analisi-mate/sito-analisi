An oblique ray travels from air into a parallel-sided glass slab and back into air. How does the emerging ray lie relative to the incident ray?

- On the same line as the incident ray.
- Closer to the normal than the incident ray.
- Farther from the normal than the incident ray.
- Parallel and shifted sideways.

## Explanation

The surface normals are parallel and the outside index is the same. Applying Snell's law twice gives $n_1\sin\theta_1=n_1\sin\theta_3$, so $\theta_3=\theta_1$. The path in the slab has a different angle and shifts the emerging ray.
