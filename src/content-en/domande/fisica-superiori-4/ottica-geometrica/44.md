A beam parallel to the axis crosses a lens and diverges as if from a point $9\,\mathrm{cm}$ away on the entrance side. What is the signed focal length of the lens?

- $+9\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $-9\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $-18\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $+18\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

For parallel incident rays, the point the emerging rays appear to come from is the virtual focus. The lens diverges, so $f<0$. The magnitude is the distance of $9\,\mathrm{cm}$.
