Two rays emerge from a lens and diverge. Extending their lines backward reveals a common point. What does that point represent?

- A virtual image point.
- A real image point.
- The rear focus for any lens.
- The optical center for any lens.

## Explanation

The outgoing light travels along rays pointing away from one another. The common point belongs to the backward extensions, so the rays appear to come from it. This is the definition of a virtual image.
