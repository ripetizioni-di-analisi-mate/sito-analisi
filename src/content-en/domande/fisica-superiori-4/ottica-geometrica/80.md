A plane mirror rotates by $10^\circ$ in the plane of incidence, with the incident ray and the point of incidence fixed. By how much does the reflected direction rotate?

- $5^\circ$.
- $10^\circ$.
- $20^\circ$.
- $30^\circ$.

## Explanation

The reflected direction is symmetric to the direction pointing back from the incidence point along the incident ray. The normal bisects the two segments. Since one segment is fixed, shifting the bisector by $10^\circ$ requires the other segment to rotate by $20^\circ$.
