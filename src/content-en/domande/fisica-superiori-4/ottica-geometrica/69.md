A convex mirror has $f=-20\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the image of a real object appears $15\,\mathrm{cm}$ behind the mirror. Find $p$.

- $p=12\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $p=20\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $p=35\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $p=60\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

Because the image is behind the mirror, $q=-15\,\mathrm{cm}$. Therefore $\dfrac{1}{p}=\dfrac{1}{f}-\dfrac{1}{q}=\left(-\dfrac{1}{20}+\dfrac{1}{15}\right)\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}=\dfrac{1}{60}\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$. Taking the reciprocal gives $p=60\,\mathrm{cm}$.
