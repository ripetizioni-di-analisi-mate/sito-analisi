A ray passes from air with $n_1=1$ into a transparent medium. The incidence angle is $45^\circ$ and the refraction angle is $30^\circ$, both from the normal. What is the index of the medium?

- $n_2=\sqrt{3}$.
- $n_2=1$.
- $n_2=\sqrt{2}$.
- $n_2=2$.

## Explanation

Solving Snell's law for $n_2$ gives $n_2=\dfrac{\sin45^\circ}{\sin30^\circ}=\dfrac{\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}}{\frac{1}{2}}=\sqrt{2}$. The index exceeds that of air because the angle from the normal decreased.
