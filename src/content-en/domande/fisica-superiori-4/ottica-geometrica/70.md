A lens of power $+4\,\mathrm D$ is placed in contact with a diverging lens of power $D_2$. Which interval of $D_2$ leaves the combination converging with a longer focal length than before?

- $-4\,\mathrm D<D_2<0\,\mathrm D$.
- $D_2<-4\,\mathrm D$.
- $0\,\mathrm D<D_2<4\,\mathrm D$.
- $D_2=-4\,\mathrm D$.

## Explanation

The combined power must be positive but less than $4\,\mathrm D$ for the focal length to increase. So $0\,\mathrm D<4\,\mathrm D+D_2<4\,\mathrm D$, equivalent to $-4\,\mathrm D<D_2<0\,\mathrm D$.
