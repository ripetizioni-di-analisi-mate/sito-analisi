A light wave crosses a stationary interface between two materials with different indices. Which quantity is the same for the incident and transmitted waves?

- Frequency.
- Phase speed.
- Wavelength.
- The ratio of index to wavelength.

## Explanation

The oscillation frequency stays the same on both sides of the stationary interface. Because $u=\dfrac{c}{n}$ changes, so does $\lambda=\dfrac{u}{\nu}$. The ratio of index to wavelength therefore varies with the index.
