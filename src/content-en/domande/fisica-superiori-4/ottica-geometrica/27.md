In a paraxial ray diagram of a converging lens, two rays start at the tip of the object. Which ray continues along the same line after the lens?

- The ray entering parallel to and above the axis.
- The ray through the front focus that meets the lens away from the axis.
- The ray that passes through the optical center.
- Any ray from a real object.

## Explanation

In the thin lens model the central ray continues undeviated. The axis-parallel ray is deflected toward the rear focus. These two paths are enough to locate the image point.
