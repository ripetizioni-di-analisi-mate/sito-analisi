Two coplanar rays strike the same point on a mirror from opposite sides of the normal. Their incidence angles are $25^\circ$ and $35^\circ$. What is the angle between the reflected rays?

- $10^\circ$.
- $25^\circ$.
- $120^\circ$.
- $60^\circ$.

## Explanation

The reflected rays remain on opposite sides of the normal and keep their respective angles. The angle between them spans the normal, so its size is the sum $25^\circ+35^\circ=60^\circ$.
