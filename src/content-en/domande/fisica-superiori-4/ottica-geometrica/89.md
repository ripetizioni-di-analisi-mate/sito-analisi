A person $1.80\,\mathrm m$ tall stands in front of a vertical plane mirror. What is the minimum height of the mirror that allows the person to see their whole body, from the feet to the top of the head?

- $1.80\,\mathrm m$
- $3.60\,\mathrm m$
- $0.45\,\mathrm m$
- $0.90\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The image formed by a plane mirror is symmetric to the person with respect to the plane of the mirror, so it is $1.80\,\mathrm m$ tall and lies at the same distance $d$ behind the mirror. The eye sees a point of the image along the segment joining the eye to that point, and the reflected light comes from the point where the segment crosses the mirror. Since the eye is at distance $d$ from the mirror and $2d$ from the image, by similar triangles the part of the mirror between the segments aimed at the feet and at the top of the head is half the height of the image. The minimum height is therefore $\dfrac{1.80\,\mathrm m}{2}=0.90\,\mathrm m$, whatever the distance from the mirror.
