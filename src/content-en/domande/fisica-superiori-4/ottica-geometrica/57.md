Light travels from a material of index $1.50$ toward one of index $1.20$. The data sheet reports the critical angle from the surface. Which value belongs on the sheet? Choose the closest one.

- $\alpha_c=36.9^\circ$.
- $\alpha_c=53.1^\circ$.
- $\alpha_c=30^\circ$.
- $\alpha_c=63.1^\circ$.

## Explanation

First find the critical angle from the normal, $\theta_c=\arcsin\left(\dfrac{1.20}{1.50}\right)\simeq53.1^\circ$. The requested angle is its complement, $90^\circ-53.1^\circ=36.9^\circ$.
