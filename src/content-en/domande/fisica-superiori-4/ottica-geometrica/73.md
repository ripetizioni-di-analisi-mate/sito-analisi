In a medium of index $1.50$, light has wavelength $400\,\mathrm{nm}$. With $c=3.00\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is its frequency?

- $\nu=7.50\cdot10^{14}\,\mathrm{Hz}$.
- $\nu=5.00\cdot10^{14}\,\mathrm{Hz}$.
- $\nu=3.33\cdot10^{14}\,\mathrm{Hz}$.
- $\nu=5.00\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{Hz}$.

## Explanation

The speed in the medium is $u=\dfrac{c}{1.50}=2.00\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Converting $400\,\mathrm{nm}=4.00\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm m$ gives $\nu=\dfrac{u}{\lambda}=5.00\cdot10^{14}\,\mathrm{Hz}$.
