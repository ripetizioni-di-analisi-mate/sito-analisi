Two slabs have equal thickness and indices $n_A=1.20$ and $n_B=1.80$. At normal incidence, what is the ratio of phase transit times $\dfrac{t_B}{t_A}$?

- $\dfrac{t_B}{t_A}=0.667$.
- $\dfrac{t_B}{t_A}=1$.
- $\dfrac{t_B}{t_A}=2.25$.
- $\dfrac{t_B}{t_A}=1.5$.

## Explanation

For thickness $L$, the travel time of a fixed phase is $t=\dfrac{L}{u}=\dfrac{Ln}{c}$. Since $L$ is the same, $\dfrac{t_B}{t_A}=\dfrac{1.80}{1.20}=\dfrac{3}{2}$. The higher index makes the transit time longer.
