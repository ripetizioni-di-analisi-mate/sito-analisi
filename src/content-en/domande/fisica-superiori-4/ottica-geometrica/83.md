A beam crosses a slit whose width is comparable to its wavelength. What model is needed to describe the spreading of the light beyond the opening?

- Only the model of straight rays in a homogeneous medium.
- Only the law of reflection at a plane mirror.
- The thin lens model with zero focal length.
- A wave model that accounts for diffraction.

## Explanation

Geometrical optics neglects wave effects and requires openings much larger than $\lambda$. When the width is comparable to $\lambda$, diffraction redistributes light beyond the region predicted by straight rays.
