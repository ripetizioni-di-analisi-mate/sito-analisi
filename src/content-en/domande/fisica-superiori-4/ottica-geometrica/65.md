Two parallel-sided slabs of the same glass are surrounded by air. The second is twice as thick. The same oblique ray crosses both. How does the lateral displacement of the outgoing ray change?

- It halves.
- It stays the same.
- It becomes zero.
- It doubles.

## Explanation

The refraction angles depend on the indices and incidence, so they are the same in both slabs. The triangles formed by the internal path and the extended incident path are similar. The lateral displacement scales with the thickness, so it doubles.
