An object and screen are fixed $45\,\mathrm{cm}$ apart. A lens of focal length $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ is moved between them. At which object distances from the lens is the image sharp?

-  $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $35\,\mathrm{cm}$.
-  $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $40\,\mathrm{cm}$.
-  Only $22.5\,\mathrm{cm}$.
-  $15\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $30\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

We have $p+q=45\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the lens equation requires $pq=f(p+q)=450\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. The distances are $15\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $30\,\mathrm{cm}$ because they have that sum and product. The object can be at either of the two distances.
