A lens receives a parallel beam and its focus lies $40\,\mathrm{cm}$ on the exit side. What is the optical power of the lens?

- $D=-2.5\,\mathrm D$.
- $D=2.5\,\mathrm D$.
- $D=0.4\,\mathrm D$.
- $D=25\,\mathrm D$.

## Explanation

The focus is real, so $f=+40\,\mathrm{cm}=+0.40\,\mathrm m$. The reciprocal in meters is $D=\dfrac{1}{0.40}\,\mathrm D=+2.5\,\mathrm D$.
