A converging thin lens has focal length $f=0.12\,\mathrm m$. The real object is at $p=0.432\,\mathrm m$. Find the signed image distance. Choose the closest value.

- $q=-0.166\,\mathrm m$.
- $q=0.166\,\mathrm m$.
- $q=0.12\,\mathrm m$.
- $q=0.312\,\mathrm m$.

## Explanation

Since $p>f$, the image is real and $q$ must be positive. Isolating $q$ gives $\dfrac{0.12\cdot0.432}{0.432-0.12}\,\mathrm m\simeq0.166\,\mathrm m$. The value $0.312\,\mathrm m$ is just the difference $p-f$.
