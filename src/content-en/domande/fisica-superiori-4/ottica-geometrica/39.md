A ray strikes a plane mirror at $25^\circ$ from the surface. What is the angle of reflection measured from the normal?

- $\theta_r=25^\circ$.
- $\theta_r=50^\circ$.
- $\theta_r=65^\circ$.
- $\theta_r=130^\circ$.

## Explanation

The line perpendicular to the mirror makes $90^\circ$ with the surface. The incidence angle from the normal is therefore $90^\circ-25^\circ=65^\circ$. Reflection preserves that angle from the normal.
