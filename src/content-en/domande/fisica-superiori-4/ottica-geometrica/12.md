An object $20\,\mathrm{cm}$ from a lens forms an image on a screen $60\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the lens on the exit side. What is the focal length?

- $f=30\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $f=40\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $f=80\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $f=15\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

The screen captures a real image, so $q=+60\,\mathrm{cm}$. Add the reciprocals $\dfrac{1}{20}+\dfrac{1}{60}=\dfrac{1}{15}$ in $\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$. Taking the reciprocal gives $f=15\,\mathrm{cm}$.
