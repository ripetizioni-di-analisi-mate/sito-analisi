A lens projects a real image with $M=-3$. The object and screen trade places while the lens remains fixed. Which value is closest to the new signed magnification?

- $M'=-0.333$.
- $M'=0.333$.
- $M'=-3$.
- $M'=3$.

## Explanation

Swapping $p$ and $q$ preserves $\dfrac{1}{p}+\dfrac{1}{q}$, so the image remains in focus on the screen. The new magnification is $M'=-\dfrac{p}{q}=\dfrac{1}{M}=-\dfrac{1}{3}$. The image remains inverted.
