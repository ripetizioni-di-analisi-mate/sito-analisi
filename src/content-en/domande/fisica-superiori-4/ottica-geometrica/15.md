A real object is at $p=2f$ from a converging lens. What image forms?

- Real, upright, and reduced.
- Real, inverted, and equal in absolute height.
- Virtual, upright, and enlarged.
- Virtual, inverted, and equal in absolute height.

## Explanation

Substituting $p=2f$ gives $q=\dfrac{f\cdot2f}{2f-f}=2f$. The sign of $q$ gives a real image. The magnification $M=-\dfrac{q}{p}=-1$ indicates an inverted image of equal absolute height.
