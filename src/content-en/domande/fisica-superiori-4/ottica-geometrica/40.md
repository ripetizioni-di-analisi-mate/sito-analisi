An oblique ray enters a liquid from a transparent solid and bends away from the normal. How do the indices compare?

- $n_{\mathrm{liq}}<n_{\mathrm{sol}}$.
- $n_{\mathrm{liq}}>n_{\mathrm{sol}}$.
- $n_{\mathrm{liq}}=n_{\mathrm{sol}}$.
- The comparison also requires the frequency of the refracted wave.

## Explanation

The refracted angle is greater than the incident angle. To keep the products $n\sin\theta$ equal, the larger angle must have the lower index. Thus the liquid has the smaller index.
