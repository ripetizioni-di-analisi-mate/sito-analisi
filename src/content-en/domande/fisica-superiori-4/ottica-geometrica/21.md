A magnifier has focal length $15\,\mathrm{cm}$ and must produce a virtual image three times the object height. At what distance $p$ from the lens should the object be placed?

- $p=5\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $p=15\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $p=22.5\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $p=10\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

The virtual image is upright, so $M=+3$ and $q=-3p$. Substitution into the thin lens equation gives $\dfrac{1}{15}=\dfrac{1}{p}-\dfrac{1}{3p}=\dfrac{2}{3p}$, with distances in centimeters. This gives $p=10\,\mathrm{cm}$, inside the focal length.
