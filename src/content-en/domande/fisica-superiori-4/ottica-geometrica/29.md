Two thin lenses in contact have focal lengths $+0.50\,\mathrm m$ and $-0.25\,\mathrm m$. Find the equivalent focal length.

- $f_{\mathrm{eq}}=0.25\,\mathrm m$.
- $f_{\mathrm{eq}}=0.5\,\mathrm m$.
- $f_{\mathrm{eq}}=-0.25\,\mathrm m$.
- $f_{\mathrm{eq}}=-0.5\,\mathrm m$.

## Explanation

The powers are $\dfrac{1}{0.50}\,\mathrm D=+2\,\mathrm D$ and $\dfrac{1}{-0.25}\,\mathrm D=-4\,\mathrm D$. They add to $-2\,\mathrm D$. The reciprocal gives $f_{\mathrm{eq}}=-0.50\,\mathrm m$, so the combination is diverging.
