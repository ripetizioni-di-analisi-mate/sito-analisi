The same light has wavelength $540\,\mathrm{nm}$ in vacuum and $360\,\mathrm{nm}$ in a material. What is the refractive index of the material?

- $n=0.667$.
- $n=1.5$.
- $n=1.0$.
- $n=1.8$.

## Explanation

Frequency stays the same, so $n=\dfrac{c}{u}=\dfrac{\lambda_0}{\lambda}=\dfrac{540}{360}=1.50$. The ratio is dimensionless because both wavelengths are in nanometers.
