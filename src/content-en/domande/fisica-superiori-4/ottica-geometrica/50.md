An object $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ from a lens has a virtual image $24\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the lens. What is the signed focal length?

- $f=-24\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $f=8\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $f=-8\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $f=24\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

Substitute $p=+12\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $q=-24\,\mathrm{cm}$. The sum of reciprocals is $\dfrac{1}{12}-\dfrac{1}{24}=\dfrac{1}{24}$ in $\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$. Thus $f=+24\,\mathrm{cm}$. The positive focal length indicates a converging lens used inside its focal length.
