For a lens, the magnification is $M=-0.75$. Which description of the image follows from this value?

- Upright and $75\%$ of the object's height.
- Inverted and $75\%$ of the object's absolute height.
- Inverted and $25\%$ of the object's absolute height.
- Upright and $125\%$ of the object's height.

## Explanation

By definition $h'=Mh$. The minus sign puts the image tip on the other side of the axis. The magnitude $0.75$ makes the absolute height $\dfrac{3}{4}$ of the original.
