A real object in front of a concave mirror remains beyond the focus as it moves from $p=3f$ to $p=2f$. How does the image move?

- It moves toward the vertex and stays in front of the mirror.
- It moves behind the mirror.
- It stays at the focus of the mirror.
- It moves away from the vertex and stays in front of the mirror.

## Explanation

At $p=3f$ the image distance is $\dfrac{3}{2}f$. At $p=2f$ it is $q=2f$. Both values are positive and the second is greater, so the real image moves farther away in front of the mirror.
