In a thin-lens eye model, the image distance at the retina is fixed. The eye switches from focusing on an object $1.00\,\mathrm m$ away to an object $0.25\,\mathrm m$ away. By how much does the lens's optical power increase?

- $\Delta D=1\,\mathrm D$.
- $\Delta D=4\,\mathrm D$.
- $\Delta D=3\,\mathrm D$.
- $\Delta D=5\,\mathrm D$.

## Explanation

Since $D=\dfrac{1}{p}+\dfrac{1}{q}$ and $q$ is fixed, the image term cancels in the difference. This gives $\Delta D=\left(\dfrac{1}{0.25}-\dfrac{1}{1.00}\right)\,\mathrm D=+3\,\mathrm D$.
