A lens of focal length $50\,\mathrm{mm}$ is focused on a very distant subject, with the sensor at $q\simeq f$. The next subject is at $p=250\,\mathrm{mm}$. By how much must the lens-to-sensor distance increase?

- $\Delta q=5\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $\Delta q=50\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $\Delta q=62.5\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $\Delta q=12.5\,\mathrm{mm}$.

## Explanation

For the nearer subject, $q=\dfrac{50\cdot250}{250-50}\,\mathrm{mm}=62.5\,\mathrm{mm}$. The distant subject needs about $50\,\mathrm{mm}$. The difference is $62.5-50=12.5\,\mathrm{mm}$.
