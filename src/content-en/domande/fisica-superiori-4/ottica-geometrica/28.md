In a diagram with distances in centimeters, the lens is at $x=0$, the object at $x=-4$, and the backward extensions of the outgoing rays meet at $x=-6$. What is $M$?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 linee="-7,0 5,0;0,-2 0,3" unicolore=1 segmenti="0,1.6,-6,2.4;0,0,-6,2.4" vettori="-4,0,-4,1.6;-4,1.6,0,1.6;0,1.6,4,1.0667;-4,1.6,0,0;0,0,4,-1.6" punti="-4,0,pieno,O;-6,2.4,pieno,I" xmin=-7.4 xmax=5.4 ymin=-2.4 ymax=3.2 titolo="The dashed lines extend the rays on the object side."}

- $M=-1.5$.
- $M=0.667$.
- $M=1.5$.
- $M=-0.667$.

## Explanation

The object distance is $p=4\,\mathrm{cm}$. The backward extensions meet on the entrance side, so $q=-6\,\mathrm{cm}$. The ratio $M=-\dfrac{-6}{4}=+1.5$ is positive and exceeds $1$.
