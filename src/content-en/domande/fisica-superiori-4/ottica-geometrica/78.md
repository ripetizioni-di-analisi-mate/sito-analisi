A converging lens and a concave mirror both give $q=+20\,\mathrm{cm}$ for their respective real objects. On which sides relative to the objects are the two images?

- On the object side for both.
- Beyond the lens and behind the mirror.
- On the object side for the lens and behind the mirror.
- Beyond the lens and in front of the mirror.

## Explanation

In both cases, $q>0$ means the rays physically meet after the interaction. The lens transmits the rays and the mirror reflects them. The outgoing side is therefore different in the two setups.
