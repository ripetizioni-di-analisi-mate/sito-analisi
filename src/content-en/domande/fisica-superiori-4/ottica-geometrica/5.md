A converging lens has $f=0.20\,\mathrm m$ and a real object at $p=0.096\,\mathrm m$. Find the signed image distance. Choose the closest value.

- $q=-0.185\,\mathrm m$.
- $q=0.185\,\mathrm m$.
- $q=-0.096\,\mathrm m$.
- $q=-0.065\,\mathrm m$.

## Explanation

The object distance is less than the focal length, so $p-f<0$. The calculation gives $q=\dfrac{0.20\cdot0.096}{0.096-0.20}\,\mathrm m\simeq-0.185\,\mathrm m$. The sign places the image on the object side.
