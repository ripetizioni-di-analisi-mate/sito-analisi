A converging lens of focal length $20$ cm cannot form a sharp image on a screen of a slide placed $9.6$ cm from the lens. Which change makes a real image possible at a finite distance, after repositioning the screen?

- Place the slide $30$ cm from the lens.
- Move the slide to $5$ cm from the lens.
- Place the slide exactly at the focus.
- Keep the slide fixed and cover half the lens.

## Explanation

For a real object, a converging lens gives a real image at a finite distance when $p>f$. Of the proposed changes, only $p=30$ cm exceeds the focal length. At $p=f$ the emerging rays are parallel, and an aperture does not change the focal length in the thin-lens model.
