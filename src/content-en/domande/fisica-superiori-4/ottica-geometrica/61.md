An object $9\,\mathrm{cm}$ high is placed $50\,\mathrm{cm}$ from a convex mirror with $f=-25\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the signed image height?

- $h'=3\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $h'=-3\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $h'=6\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $h'=13.5\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

For a convex mirror $M=\dfrac{|f|}{p+|f|}=\dfrac{25}{75}=\dfrac{1}{3}$. Thus $h'=Mh=+3\,\mathrm{cm}$. The positive sign denotes an upright image.
