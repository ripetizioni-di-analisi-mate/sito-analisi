An object is moved away from a converging lens from $p=\dfrac{f}{2}$ to a position just short of $p=f$. How do $q$ and the magnitude of $M$ change?

- $q$ becomes positive and the magnitude of $M$ increases.
- $q$ stays negative and the magnitude of $M$ increases.
- $q$ stays negative and the magnitude of $M$ decreases.
- $q$ becomes positive and the magnitude of $M$ stays constant.

## Explanation

Throughout the displacement, $p<f$, so $q=\dfrac{fp}{p-f}<0$. In addition, $|M|=\dfrac{f}{f-p}$ and $f-p$ decreases. The virtual image grows.
