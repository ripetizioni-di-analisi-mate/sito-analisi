A makeup mirror is concave with focal length $20\,\mathrm{cm}$. An object is $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ in front of it. Find the signed transverse magnification.

- $M=-2.5$.
- $M=1.5$.
- $M=2.5$.
- $M=0.4$.

## Explanation

The object distance is shorter than the focal length. This gives $q=\dfrac{20\cdot12}{12-20}\,\mathrm{cm}=-30\,\mathrm{cm}$, so $M=-\dfrac{-30}{12}=+2.5$. The virtual image is upright and enlarged.
