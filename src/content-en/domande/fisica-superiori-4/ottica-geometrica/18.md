A lens of focal length $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ initially projects an object $30\,\mathrm{cm}$ away. The object is moved closer, to $20\,\mathrm{cm}$. How much farther from the lens must the screen move for the image to be in focus?

- $\Delta q=10\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $\Delta q=15\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $\Delta q=20\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $\Delta q=5\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

At $p=30\,\mathrm{cm}$, $q=\dfrac{10\cdot30}{30-10}\,\mathrm{cm}=15\,\mathrm{cm}$. At $p=20\,\mathrm{cm}$, $q=20\,\mathrm{cm}$ because $p=2f$. The screen moves $20-15=5\,\mathrm{cm}$ farther away.
