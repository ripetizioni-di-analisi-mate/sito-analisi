For a diverging lens an object $8\,\mathrm{cm}$ high is at $p=30\,\mathrm{cm}$ and its image has $q=-7.5\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the signed image height?

- $h'=2\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $h'=-2\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $h'=4\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $h'=32\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

The signed ratio is $M=-\dfrac{-7.5}{30}=+\dfrac{1}{4}$. Thus $h'=Mh=2\,\mathrm{cm}$. The positive sign puts the tip of the image on the same side of the axis as the tip of the object.
