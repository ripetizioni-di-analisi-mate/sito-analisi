Two thin lenses of known optical powers share the same axis but are separated. What assumption underlies the equation $D_{\mathrm{eq}}=D_1+D_2$ used in this chapter?

- Both lenses have positive power.
- The lenses are in contact and in the same medium.
- The focal lengths have equal magnitudes.
- The incident beam consists of a single ray.

## Explanation

With separated lenses, a ray can reach the second at a different height from where it left the first. The sum equation applies to two thin lenses in contact in the same medium.
