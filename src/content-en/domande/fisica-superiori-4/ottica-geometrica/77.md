A lens produces diverging outgoing rays that an eye sees as a virtual image. What happens if a screen is moved along the exit side to try to capture it?

- The image is sharp at the focal distance.
- The image is sharp at twice the focal distance.
- The screen intercepts light without forming a sharp image.
- Any screen position gives the same sharp image.

## Explanation

Virtual image points are intersections of the backward extensions, on the side opposite to where the outgoing rays travel. An exit-side screen intercepts rays that are still separate and lacks a point-by-point correspondence with the object.
