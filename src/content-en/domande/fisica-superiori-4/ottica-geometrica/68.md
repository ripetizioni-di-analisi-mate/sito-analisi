An object is $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ from a converging lens. Its virtual image appears $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ farther from the lens than the object. What is the focal length?

- $f=4.5\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $f=9\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $f=6\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $f=18\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

The distance from the lens is $|q|=6\,\mathrm{cm}+12\,\mathrm{cm}=18\,\mathrm{cm}$. For a virtual image, $q=-18\,\mathrm{cm}$. Then $\dfrac{1}{f}=\left(\dfrac{1}{6}-\dfrac{1}{18}\right)\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}=\dfrac{1}{9}\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$, so $f=9\,\mathrm{cm}$.
