A plane mirror is initially to the right of an object. Along the horizontal axis pointing right, the mirror moves by $\Delta x_S=+8\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the object by $\Delta x_O=+3\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find the signed displacement of the image.

- $\Delta x_I=13\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $\Delta x_I=5\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $\Delta x_I=11\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $\Delta x_I=19\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

By symmetry $x_I=2x_S-x_O$. Therefore $\Delta x_I=2\cdot8\,\mathrm{cm}-3\,\mathrm{cm}=+13\,\mathrm{cm}$. The object's displacement enters with the opposite sign because the image is symmetric across the mirror.
