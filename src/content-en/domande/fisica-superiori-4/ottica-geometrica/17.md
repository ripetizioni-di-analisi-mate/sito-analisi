A point source is at the front focus of a converging lens. What are the rays like after the lens?

- Converging at the rear focus.
- Parallel to the optical axis.
- Diverging as if from the optical center.
- Reflected back toward the starting point.

## Explanation

An incoming ray parallel to the axis passes through the rear focus on exit. Reversing the path, rays from the entrance focus emerge parallel. Indeed, with $p=f$, $\dfrac{1}{q}=\dfrac{1}{f}-\dfrac{1}{p}=0$.
