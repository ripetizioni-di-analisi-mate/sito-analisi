Light has a wavelength of $600\,\mathrm{nm}$ in vacuum and enters a medium of index $1.50$. What is its wavelength in the medium?

- $\lambda=300\,\mathrm{nm}$.
- $\lambda=400\,\mathrm{nm}$.
- $\lambda=600\,\mathrm{nm}$.
- $\lambda=900\,\mathrm{nm}$.

## Explanation

Frequency is conserved and the speed becomes $\dfrac{c}{n}$. Thus $\lambda=\dfrac{\lambda_0}{n}=\dfrac{600}{1.50}\,\mathrm{nm}=400\,\mathrm{nm}$.
