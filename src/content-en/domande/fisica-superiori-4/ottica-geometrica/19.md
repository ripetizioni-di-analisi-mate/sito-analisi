Moving a screen along the exit side of a lens reveals a position where a luminous arrow is sharp. What does this experiment establish?

- Rays from each point of the arrow meet on the screen.
- The backward extensions of the rays meet on the screen.
- The image necessarily has the same dimensions as the arrow.
- The screen necessarily lies at the lens focus.

## Explanation

A sharp figure on the screen requires light from each object point to reach its image point. The image is therefore real. The screen distance can exceed the focal length because it also depends on the object distance.
