Light travels from air ($n_1=1.00$) into glass ($n_2=1.50$) at $\theta_1=42^\circ$ from the normal. Which value is closest to the refraction angle from the normal?

- $\theta_2=42^\circ$.
- $\theta_2=63.5^\circ$.
- $\theta_2=18.2^\circ$.
- $\theta_2=26.5^\circ$.

## Explanation

Snell's law gives $\sin\theta_2=\dfrac{1.00}{1.50}\sin42^\circ\simeq0.4461$. The inverse sine in degrees gives about $26.5^\circ$. The ray bends toward the normal because it enters the medium with higher index.
