A slide forms a sharp image on a screen with equal absolute height. The distance between the slide and the screen is $80\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the thin lens's focal length?

- $f=20\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $f=40\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $f=80\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $f=10\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

Because the image is real and $|M|=1$, we have $p=q$. Their sum is $80\,\mathrm{cm}$, so both are $40\,\mathrm{cm}$. The optical equation gives $\dfrac{1}{f}=\dfrac{2}{p}$, so $f=20\,\mathrm{cm}$.
