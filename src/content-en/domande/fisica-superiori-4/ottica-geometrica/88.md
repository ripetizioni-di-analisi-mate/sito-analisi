Two identical diverging lenses, each with focal length $-0.50\,\mathrm m$, are in contact. Find their equivalent focal length.

- $f_{\mathrm{eq}}=0.25\,\mathrm m$.
- $f_{\mathrm{eq}}=-0.25\,\mathrm m$.
- $f_{\mathrm{eq}}=-0.5\,\mathrm m$.
- $f_{\mathrm{eq}}=-1\,\mathrm m$.

## Explanation

Each lens contributes $D=-2\,\mathrm D$, so $D_{\mathrm{eq}}=-4\,\mathrm D$. The focal length is $f_{\mathrm{eq}}=\dfrac{1}{-4}\,\mathrm m=-0.25\,\mathrm m$. Add the powers and calculate the focal length after that sum.
