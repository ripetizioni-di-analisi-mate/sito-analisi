An object and a screen are fixed $90\,\mathrm{cm}$ apart on an optical bench. A positive thin lens forms a sharp image at two lens positions separated by $30\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its focal length?

- $30\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $20\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $45\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $15\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Object and image distances swap between the two positions. Their sum is $90\,\mathrm{cm}$ and their difference is $30\,\mathrm{cm}$, so they are $60$ and $30\,\mathrm{cm}$. The focal length is $\dfrac{60\cdot30}{60+30}=20\,\mathrm{cm}$.
