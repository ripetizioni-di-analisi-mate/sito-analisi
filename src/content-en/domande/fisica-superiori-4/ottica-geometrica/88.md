Two identical diverging lenses, each with focal length $-0.50\,\mathrm m$, are in contact. What is their equivalent focal length?

- $f_{\mathrm{eq}}=0.25\,\mathrm m$.
- $f_{\mathrm{eq}}=-0.25\,\mathrm m$.
- $f_{\mathrm{eq}}=-0.5\,\mathrm m$.
- $f_{\mathrm{eq}}=-1\,\mathrm m$.

## Explanation

The power of a lens is the reciprocal of its focal length in metres, so each lens has $D=\dfrac{1}{-0.50\,\mathrm m}=-2.0\,\mathrm D$. For two thin lenses in contact the powers add, so $D_{\mathrm{eq}}=-4.0\,\mathrm D$. The equivalent focal length is the reciprocal of this power, hence $f_{\mathrm{eq}}=\dfrac{1}{-4.0}\,\mathrm m=-0.25\,\mathrm m$. The value $-1\,\mathrm m$ comes from adding the focal lengths instead of the powers.
