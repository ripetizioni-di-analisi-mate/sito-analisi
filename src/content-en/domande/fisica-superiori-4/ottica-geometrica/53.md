Two coplanar rays strike the same point of a mirror from the same side of the normal. Their angles of incidence are $15^\circ$ and $36^\circ$. What angle separates the reflected rays?

- $51^\circ$.
- $15^\circ$.
- $36^\circ$.
- $21^\circ$.

## Explanation

Reflection sends both rays to the other side of the normal, preserving their angles. Since the reflected rays are on the same side, subtract their angles to obtain $36^\circ-15^\circ=21^\circ$.
