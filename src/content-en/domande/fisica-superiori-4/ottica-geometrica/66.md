Light in a liquid reaches the boundary with air of index one. The critical angle is $30^\circ$. What is the liquid's refractive index?

- $n=0.5$.
- $n=1$.
- $n=2$.
- $n=1.5$.

## Explanation

For the ray along the boundary, $n\sin\theta_c=1$. Since $\sin30^\circ=\dfrac{1}{2}$, we obtain $n=2$. The index of the medium the light starts in is the denominator of $\sin\theta_c$.
