A thin lens has focal length $f=0.30\,\mathrm m$. Calculate its optical power. Choose the closest value.

- $D=0.3\,\mathrm D$.
- $D=-3.33\,\mathrm D$.
- $D=33.3\,\mathrm D$.
- $D=3.33\,\mathrm D$.

## Explanation

One diopter equals $1\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$. Since the focal length is already given in meters, $D=\dfrac{1}{0.30}\,\mathrm D\simeq+3.33\,\mathrm D$. The power is positive because the lens is converging.
