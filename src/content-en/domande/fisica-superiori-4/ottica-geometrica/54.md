An object lies $8\,\mathrm{cm}$ below the surface of a liquid of index $1.50$. Estimate its apparent depth, viewed from air ($n=1$) nearly normal to the surface. Choose the closest value.

- $d'=4.0\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $d'=8.0\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $d'=5.33\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $d'=12.0\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

For nearly normal viewing, $d'\simeq d\dfrac{n_2}{n_1}$. Air is the viewing medium, so $d'\simeq\dfrac{8}{1.50}\,\mathrm{cm}=5.33\,\mathrm{cm}$. The image appears closer to the surface.
