A ray leaves a medium of index $1.50$. The incident and transmitted angles from the normal are $35^\circ$ and $50^\circ$. What is the index of the second medium? Choose the closest value.

- $n_2=1.5$.
- $n_2=2.0$.
- $n_2=0.75$.
- $n_2=1.12$.

## Explanation

Snell's law gives $n_2=\dfrac{1.50\sin35^\circ}{\sin50^\circ}\simeq1.12$. The angle increased and the index decreased, so the result agrees with the direction of the refracted ray.
