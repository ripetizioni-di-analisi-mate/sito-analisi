The incident and reflected ray segments, drawn from the same point on the surface toward the medium, make an angle of $84^\circ$. What is the angle of incidence from the normal?

- $\theta_i=48^\circ$.
- $\theta_i=42^\circ$.
- $\theta_i=84^\circ$.
- $\theta_i=6^\circ$.

## Explanation

The normal divides the angle between the segments into two equal angles because $\theta_r=\theta_i$. Therefore $\theta_i=\dfrac{84^\circ}{2}=42^\circ$. The angle from the surface is its complement.
