A concave mirror has a radius of curvature of $30\,\mathrm{cm}$ and a real object is $45\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the vertex. What is the magnitude of paraxial magnification?

- $|M|=1.0$.
- $|M|=2.0$.
- $|M|=0.5$.
- $|M|=0.333$.

## Explanation

The focal length is $f=\dfrac{30}{2}\,\mathrm{cm}=15\,\mathrm{cm}$. This gives $q=\dfrac{15\cdot45}{45-15}\,\mathrm{cm}=22.5\,\mathrm{cm}$. The magnitude is $\dfrac{22.5}{45}=\dfrac{1}{2}$.
