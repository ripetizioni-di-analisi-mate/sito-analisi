A ray exits a transparent medium into air at $58^\circ$ from the normal. In the transparent medium the incidence angle was $35^\circ$. A second ray in air is sent exactly along the reverse path. What is its refraction angle in the medium?

- $\theta_2=35^\circ$.
- $\theta_2=58^\circ$.
- $\theta_2=23^\circ$.
- $\theta_2=55^\circ$.

## Explanation

Reversing the path swaps the two sides of the equality in Snell's law. The new incidence angle is $58^\circ$ in air and the new refraction angle is that of the original ray in the medium, $35^\circ$.
