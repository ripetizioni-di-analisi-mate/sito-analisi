A ray goes from a medium with $n_1=\sqrt{2}$ toward air with $n_2=1$. The incidence angle is $45^\circ$ from the normal. What is the limiting direction of the transmitted ray in the geometrical model?

- Along the normal.
- Along the surface.
- At $45^\circ$ from the normal.
- At $60^\circ$ from the normal.

## Explanation

The product $n_1\sin45^\circ=\sqrt{2}\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ is $1$, equal to $n_2$. Thus $\sin\theta_2=1$ and the limiting direction is $90^\circ$ from the normal. The incidence is exactly critical.
