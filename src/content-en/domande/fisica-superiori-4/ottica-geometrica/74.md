Two blocks A and B receive rays from air at the same oblique incidence. The refracted ray makes $20^\circ$ with the normal in A and $30^\circ$ in B. In which material does light travel more slowly?

- In B.
- It travels at the same speed in A and B.
- In A.
- The speed depends on the thickness of the blocks.

## Explanation

The products $n_A\sin20^\circ$ and $n_B\sin30^\circ$ are equal because incidence in air is the same. Since $\sin20^\circ<\sin30^\circ$, it follows that $n_A>n_B$. The speed $u=\dfrac{c}{n}$ is therefore lower in A.
