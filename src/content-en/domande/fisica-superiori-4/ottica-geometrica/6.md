A diverging lens has a focal length of $-15$ cm. A real object, initially $36$ cm away, is moved farther and farther away. Which point does the image approach in the thin-lens model?

- The focus $15$ cm from the lens on the object side.
- The focus $15$ cm from the lens on the opposite side.
- The centre of the lens.
- A point ever farther away on the object side.

## Explanation

In $\dfrac{1}{q}=\dfrac{1}{f}-\dfrac{1}{p}$ the term $\dfrac{1}{p}$ tends to zero. The image distance therefore tends to $q=f=-15$ cm. Its negative sign identifies the virtual focus on the side from which the light arrives.
