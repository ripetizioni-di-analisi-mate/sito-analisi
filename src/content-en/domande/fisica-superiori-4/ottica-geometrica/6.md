A diverging lens has focal length $f=-0.15\,\mathrm m$. A real object is at $p=0.36\,\mathrm m$. Find the signed image distance. Choose the closest value.

- $q=-0.106\,\mathrm m$.
- $q=0.106\,\mathrm m$.
- $q=-0.257\,\mathrm m$.
- $q=-0.36\,\mathrm m$.

## Explanation

The focal length enters with a negative sign in both places in the formula. Thus $q=\dfrac{(-0.15)\cdot0.36}{0.36+0.15}\,\mathrm m\simeq-0.106\,\mathrm m$. The image is between the lens and its front focus because $|q|<|f|$.
