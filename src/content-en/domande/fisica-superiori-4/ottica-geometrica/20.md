A person stays still while a plane mirror moves $30\,\mathrm{cm}$ toward them along the normal. How far does the image move in the laboratory frame?

- $60\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $15\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $30\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $90\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

The position of the symmetric image satisfies $x_I=2x_S-x_O$. Since $x_O$ is constant, the change is $\Delta x_I=2\Delta x_S$. The image therefore moves $2\cdot30\,\mathrm{cm}=60\,\mathrm{cm}$ toward the person.
