A lens brings rays parallel to its axis to a point $18\,\mathrm{cm}$ beyond the lens. An object is placed $36\,\mathrm{cm}$ away on the entrance side. How far away should a screen be placed on the exit side?

- $q=36\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $q=18\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $q=9\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $q=54\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

The convergence point of parallel rays is the focus, so $f=18\,\mathrm{cm}$. The new object is at $p=2f$. Then $q=\dfrac{f(2f)}{2f-f}=2f=36\,\mathrm{cm}$.
