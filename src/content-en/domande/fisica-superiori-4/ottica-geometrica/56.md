A ray in glass of index $1.50$ meets a surface at $50^\circ$ from the normal. In one case the outside medium is air ($n=1$), and in the other it is a liquid of index $1.33$. In which case is there total internal reflection?

- Only with the liquid.
- Only with air.
- In both cases.
- In neither case.

## Explanation

The product $n_1\sin50^\circ$ is about $1.15$. It exceeds the index of air but is below the liquid's index. The transmitted sine would be greater than one only with air.
