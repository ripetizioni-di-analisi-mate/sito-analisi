A ray in glass ($n_1=1.50$) strikes an air boundary ($n_2=1.00$) at $30^\circ$ from the normal. Find the transmitted angle by choosing the closest value.

- $\theta_2=19.5^\circ$.
- $\theta_2=30^\circ$.
- $\theta_2=41.4^\circ$.
- $\theta_2=48.6^\circ$.

## Explanation

This gives $\sin\theta_2=\dfrac{1.50}{1.00}\sin30^\circ=0.75$. The sine is less than one, so transmission is possible and $\theta_2=\arcsin(0.75)\simeq48.6^\circ$.
