A concave mirror has $f=0.18\,\mathrm m$. A real object is at $p=0.648\,\mathrm m$. Find $q$ in the paraxial model, with $q>0$ for a real image. Choose the closest value.

- $q=0.249\,\mathrm m$.
- $q=-0.249\,\mathrm m$.
- $q=0.141\,\mathrm m$.
- $q=0.468\,\mathrm m$.

## Explanation

The mirror equation solved for $q$ gives $\dfrac{0.18\cdot0.648}{0.648-0.18}\,\mathrm m\simeq0.249\,\mathrm m$. Since $q>0$, the reflected light forms the image in front of the mirror.
