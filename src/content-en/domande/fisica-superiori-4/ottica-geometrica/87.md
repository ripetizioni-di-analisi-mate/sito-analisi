A convex mirror has focal length $-16\,\mathrm{cm}$. For a very distant object, which value approximates $q$?

- $q\simeq16\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $q\simeq-16\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $q\simeq-32\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $q\simeq0\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

In the mirror equation, $\dfrac{1}{p}$ is negligible for a very distant object. This leaves $\dfrac{1}{q}\simeq\dfrac{1}{f}$, so $q\simeq-16\,\mathrm{cm}$. The reflected rays appear to come from the focus behind the mirror.
