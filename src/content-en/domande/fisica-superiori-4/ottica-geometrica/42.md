A beam travels from water ($n=1.33$) toward glass ($n=1.50$). At what incidence angle would total internal reflection occur?

- At any angle greater than $60^\circ$.
- Only at $45^\circ$.
- At no angle.
- Already at normal incidence.

## Explanation

Snell's law requires $\sin\theta_2=\dfrac{1.33}{1.50}\sin\theta_1$. The factor is less than one, so the right side stays below one at every incidence angle. Transmission is possible and there is no critical angle.
