A transparent medium has index $n=1.8$. Use $c=3.00\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to calculate the phase speed. Choose the closest value.

- $u=5.4\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $u=1.67\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $u=1.2\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $u=2.2\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

The index is the ratio $\dfrac{c}{u}$, so $u=\dfrac{3.00}{1.8}\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}\simeq1.67\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Dividing by an index larger than one gives a speed below $c$.
