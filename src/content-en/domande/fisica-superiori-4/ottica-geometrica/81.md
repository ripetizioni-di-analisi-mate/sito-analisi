A point is $8\,\mathrm{cm}$ below a flat surface. A ray from the point reaches the surface with $\tan\theta_1=\dfrac{1}{4}$ and is transmitted with $\tan\theta_2=\dfrac{1}{2}$, with both angles measured from the normal. At what depth does the backward extension of the transmitted ray intersect the vertical through the point?

- $d'=2\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $d'=4\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $d'=8\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $d'=16\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

The incidence point is at horizontal distance $x=d\tan\theta_1=2\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the vertical. The backward extension covers the same $x$ with tangent $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Thus $d'=\dfrac{x}{\tan\theta_2}=4\,\mathrm{cm}$.
