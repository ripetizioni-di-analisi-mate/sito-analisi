A convex mirror has radius of curvature $30\,\mathrm{cm}$. A real object is $30\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the vertex. Find $q$ with the paraxial convention.

- $q=10\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $q=-15\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $q=-10\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $q=30\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

For a convex mirror use $f=-15\,\mathrm{cm}$. Thus $q=\dfrac{(-15)\cdot30}{30+15}\,\mathrm{cm}=-10\,\mathrm{cm}$. The image is behind the mirror.
