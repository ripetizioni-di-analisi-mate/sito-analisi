Compare the field just outside a conductor with the field of an infinite insulating plane at the same nonzero magnitude of surface charge density $\sigma_q$. Which magnitude is larger?

- The conductor's is twice as large.
- The insulating plane's is twice as large.
- The magnitudes are the same.
- The conductor's field is zero even just outside.

## Explanation

For the conductor only the outer cap of the Gaussian cylinder contributes. For the insulating plane there are two contributing caps, each carrying half the flux from the same charge, so the conductor's exterior field is twice as large.
