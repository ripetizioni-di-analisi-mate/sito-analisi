A field of magnitude $160\,\mathrm{N/C}$ makes a $30^\circ$ angle with a flat surface of area $0.20\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The component along the chosen normal is negative. What is the flux?

- $\Phi_E=-16\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $\Phi_E=16\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $\Phi_E=-32\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $\Phi_E=-27.7\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$

## Explanation

The acute angle to the nearer normal is $60^\circ$. The assigned normal points the other way, so the angle to use is $120^\circ$ and the cosine is negative.

$$
\Phi_E=160\cdot0.20\cos120^\circ\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}=-16\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}.
$$
