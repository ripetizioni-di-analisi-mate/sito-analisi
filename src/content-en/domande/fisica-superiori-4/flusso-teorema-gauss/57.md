A Gaussian cylinder is coaxial with a uniformly charged infinite wire. Why is the flux through each cap zero?

- The field is tangent to each cap.
- The field is zero over the entire cylinder.
- The two caps have different distances from the wire.
- Charge is outside the cylinder only.

## Explanation

An end cap's normal is parallel to the wire axis. The field is radial in the plane perpendicular to that axis. The dot product is therefore zero everywhere on the cap.
