A closed cavity in a conductor at equilibrium contains a point charge of $+6\,\mathrm{nC}$. Calculate the total charge induced on the inner wall.

- $Q_{\mathrm{parete}}=6\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $Q_{\mathrm{parete}}=-6\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $Q_{\mathrm{parete}}=0\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $Q_{\mathrm{parete}}=-12\,\mathrm{nC}$

## Explanation

A Gaussian surface in the metal surrounding the cavity has zero field and therefore zero flux. The point charge and induced charge must add to zero.

$$
\begin{gathered}
Q_{\mathrm{parete}}+6\,\mathrm{nC}=0,\\[6pt]
Q_{\mathrm{parete}}=-6\,\mathrm{nC}.
\end{gathered}
$$
