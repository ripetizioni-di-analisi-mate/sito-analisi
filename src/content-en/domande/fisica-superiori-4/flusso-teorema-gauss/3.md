A closed surface contains $+3.6\,\mathrm{nC}$ and $-1.0\,\mathrm{nC}$. Another charge of $+5\,\mathrm{nC}$ is outside. Calculate the total flux. Use $\varepsilon_0=8.85\cdot10^{-12}\,\mathrm{C^2/(N\,m^2)}$. Choose the closest value.

- $\Phi_E=407\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $\Phi_E=859\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $\Phi_E=-294\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $\Phi_E\simeq 294\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$

## Explanation

The external charge produces a field but no net closed flux. Add only the two enclosed charges with their signs, then divide by the constant.

$$
\Phi_E=\frac{(3.6-1.0)\cdot10^{-9}}{8.85\cdot10^{-12}}\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}\simeq294\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}.
$$
