A Gaussian surface passes exactly through an ideal point charge. Which choice removes the ambiguity in computing flux?

- Always count the charge as outside without moving the surface.
- Assign half the charge to every surface, regardless of its shape.
- Shift the surface so the charge is clearly inside or outside.
- Point the normals inward to remove the problem.

## Explanation

The point charge's field is singular where the charge lies. Shifting the boundary slightly makes it clear whether the charge is inside or outside.
