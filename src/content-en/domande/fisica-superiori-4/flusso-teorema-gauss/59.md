An outward field of $100\,\mathrm{N/C}$ is measured at radius $0.10\,\mathrm{m}$ around an infinite wire. What is the total flux for a coaxial cylinder of that radius and length $0.40\,\mathrm{m}$? Choose the closest value.

- $12.6\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $50.3\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $25.1\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$

## Explanation

The field crosses only the curved side and has the measured magnitude there. Side area is circumference times length, so it contains the factor two pi.

$$
\Phi_E=100\cdot2\pi\cdot0.10\cdot0.40\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}=8\pi\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}\simeq25.1\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}.
$$
