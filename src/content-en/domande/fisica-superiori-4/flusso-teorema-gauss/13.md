Outside an isolated spherically symmetric sphere, $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$ from the center, the field points inward with magnitude $200\,\mathrm{N/C}$. With $k_e=9\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$, what is the total charge in nanocoulombs?

- $Q=2\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $Q=-2\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $Q=-6\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $Q=-0.67\,\mathrm{nC}$

## Explanation

Spherical symmetry makes the exterior field equivalent to the total charge at the center. The radial component is negative, so the charge is negative too.

$$
Q=\frac{E_r r^2}{k_e}=\frac{(-200)(0.30)^2}{9\cdot10^9}\,\mathrm{C}=-2\,\mathrm{nC}.
$$
