A closed surface encloses net charge $+4\,\mathrm{nC}$. A charge of $-9\,\mathrm{nC}$ moves from outside to inside. Calculate the new net outward flux. Use $\varepsilon_0=8.85\cdot10^{-12}\,\mathrm{C^2/(N\,m^2)}$. Choose the closest value.

- $\Phi_E=565\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $\Phi_E\simeq -565\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $\Phi_E=-1020\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $\Phi_E=452\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$

## Explanation

Add the charges already enclosed and the one just moved inside with their signs. The new total is negative, so the net flux is inward.

$$
\Phi_E=\frac{(4-9)\cdot10^{-9}}{8.85\cdot10^{-12}}\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}\simeq-565\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}.
$$
