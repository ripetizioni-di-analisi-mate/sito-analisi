A closed surface initially encloses $+4\,\mathrm{nC}$. Between two readings its outward flux decreases by $\dfrac{9\,\mathrm{nC}}{\varepsilon_0}$. Which history is consistent with the same result as a charge of $-9\,\mathrm{nC}$ entering?

- A charge of $+4\,\mathrm{nC}$ enters and $-5\,\mathrm{nC}$ leaves.
- A charge of $-4\,\mathrm{nC}$ enters and $+5\,\mathrm{nC}$ leaves.
- Charges of $-4\,\mathrm{nC}$ and $+5\,\mathrm{nC}$ both leave.
- Charges of $-4\,\mathrm{nC}$ and $+5\,\mathrm{nC}$ both enter.

## Explanation

Gauss's law constrains the net change of enclosed charge. Four negative nanocoulombs entering contribute $-4\,\mathrm{nC}$, and five positive nanocoulombs leaving contribute $-5\,\mathrm{nC}$. The total change is therefore $-9\,\mathrm{nC}$.
