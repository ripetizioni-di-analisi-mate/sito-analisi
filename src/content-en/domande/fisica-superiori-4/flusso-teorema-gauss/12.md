Inside an insulating sphere of uniform density the field measures $180\,\mathrm{N/C}$ a distance $r$ from the center. What is it at distance $\dfrac r2$?

- $E_2=90\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $E_2=45\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $E_2=180\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $E_2=360\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

Inside, enclosed charge is proportional to the cube of the radius and area to its square. Their ratio, which determines field magnitude, is proportional to the radius.

$$
E_2=180\cdot\frac12\,\mathrm{N/C}=90\,\mathrm{N/C}.
$$
