An infinite insulating plane produces a field of magnitude $400\,\mathrm{N/C}$ per side, pointing away from the plane. Find its surface charge density in nanocoulombs per square meter. Use $\varepsilon_0=8.85\cdot10^{-12}\,\mathrm{C^2/(N\,m^2)}$.

- $3.54\,\mathrm{nC/m^2}$
- $-7.08\,\mathrm{nC/m^2}$
- $14.2\,\mathrm{nC/m^2}$
- $7.08\,\mathrm{nC/m^2}$

## Explanation

The outward direction on both sides indicates positive density. Recover the density from field magnitude by multiplying by twice the permittivity.

$$
\sigma_q=2\varepsilon_0 E=7.08\cdot10^{-9}\,\mathrm{C/m^2}=7.08\,\mathrm{nC/m^2}.
$$
