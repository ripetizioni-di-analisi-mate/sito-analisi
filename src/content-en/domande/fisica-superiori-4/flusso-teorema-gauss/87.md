Two insulating spheres of radii $R$ and $2R$ have the same uniform positive volume density and are studied separately. What is the ratio of their surface field magnitudes, second sphere to first?

- $4$
- $8$
- $2$
- $\dfrac12$

## Explanation

The larger sphere contains eight times the charge because it has eight times the volume. At its surface the flux crosses four times the area, so the field magnitude doubles.
