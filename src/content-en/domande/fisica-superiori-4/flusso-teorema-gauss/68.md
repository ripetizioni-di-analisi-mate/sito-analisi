Two ideal infinite sheets have densities $+5\,\mathrm{nC/m^2}$ and $-3\,\mathrm{nC/m^2}$. What is the ratio of the field magnitude between the sheets to that outside?

- $0.25$
- $8$
- $2$
- $4$

## Explanation

Between the sheets the contributions point the same way and add. Outside they oppose and their magnitudes subtract. The same constant occurs in both magnitudes.

$$
\frac{E_{\mathrm{int}}}{E_{\mathrm{est}}}=\frac{5+3}{5-3}=4.
$$
