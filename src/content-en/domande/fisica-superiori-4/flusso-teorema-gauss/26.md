A closed cylinder is immersed in a uniform field along its axis. With outward normals, which surfaces contribute to the flux?

- Only the cap the arrows leave through.
- Only the curved side.
- Both caps, with opposite contributions.
- All surfaces, with positive contributions.

## Explanation

The field is normal to the caps and tangent to the curved side. The caps have equal area and opposite normals, so each cap has nonzero flux and they cancel.
