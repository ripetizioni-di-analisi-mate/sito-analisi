A Gaussian surface lies entirely within the metal of an equilibrium conductor. There may be a cavity inside the surface. What conclusion is certain?

- The net enclosed charge is zero.
- Every individual enclosed charge is zero.
- The conductor's total charge must be zero.
- The field inside any cavity is always zero.

## Explanation

The field on the surface is zero because the surface lies in the metal. Flux vanishes and Gauss's law requires all enclosed charges added with signs to give zero. There can be individual charges that cancel.
