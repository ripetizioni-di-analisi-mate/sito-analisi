A pillbox with caps parallel to an infinite charged plane is moved entirely to one side of the plane. What happens to its net flux?

- It doubles.
- It stays the same as for the cylinder crossing the plane.
- It becomes zero.
- It becomes the flux through a single cap.

## Explanation

In the new position the cylinder encloses no charge. The field is uniform on the two caps on the same side, so their nonzero fluxes have opposite signs and cancel.
