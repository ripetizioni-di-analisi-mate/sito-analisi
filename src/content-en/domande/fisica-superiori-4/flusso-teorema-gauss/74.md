At a smooth point just outside the surface of a metal in equilibrium, which component of its electric field is necessarily zero?

- The normal component.
- The tangential component.
- The magnitude at every point.
- The vertical component, whatever the shape.

## Explanation

A tangential component would exert a force along the surface on the mobile charges. They would flow and the conductor would not be in equilibrium.
