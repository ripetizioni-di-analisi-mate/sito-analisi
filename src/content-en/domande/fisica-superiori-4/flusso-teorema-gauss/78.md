A neutral conductor contains two closed cavities, one with a charge of $+7\,\mathrm{nC}$ and the other with $-3\,\mathrm{nC}$. Calculate the total charge on the conductor's outer surface.

- $-4\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $4\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $10\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $0\,\mathrm{nC}$

## Explanation

A Gaussian surface in the metal around each cavity requires its own wall to carry the opposite charge. The sum on the two walls is negative. Since the conductor is neutral, the outer surface carries the opposite positive value.

$$
\begin{gathered}
Q_{\mathrm{pareti}}=(-7+3)\,\mathrm{nC}=-4\,\mathrm{nC},\\[6pt]
Q_{\mathrm{est}}=+4\,\mathrm{nC}.
\end{gathered}
$$
