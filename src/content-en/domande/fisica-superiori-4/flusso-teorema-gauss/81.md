An isolated spherical conducting shell has nonzero net charge and an empty central cavity. In which regions is the field zero?

- In the metal and in the cavity.
- In the cavity only.
- Outside only.
- In all regions.

## Explanation

The field in the metal is zero by equilibrium. The cavity is empty and has an equipotential boundary, so it is shielded. An exterior surface encloses the shell's net charge and the exterior field is nonzero.
