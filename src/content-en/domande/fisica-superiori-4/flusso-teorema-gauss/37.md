Outside a spherically symmetric distribution the field measures $360\,\mathrm{N/C}$ at $0.20\,\mathrm{m}$ from the center. At what exterior distance does the magnitude fall to $40\,\mathrm{N/C}$?

- $0.0667\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.8\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The field falls by a factor of nine. Since the exterior field is inversely proportional to the square of the distance from the center, the distance grows by a factor of three.

$$
r_2=0.20\sqrt{\frac{360}{40}}\,\mathrm{m}=0.60\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
