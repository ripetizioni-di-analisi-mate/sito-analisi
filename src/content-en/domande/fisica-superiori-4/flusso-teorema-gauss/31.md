A spherical surface is deformed into an ellipsoid without crossing any charge. What can be said about total flux?

- It increases if the area increases.
- It decreases for every deformation.
- It becomes zero because spherical symmetry is lost.
- It stays the same.

## Explanation

The law depends on net enclosed charge. The deformation changes area and local normals while leaving the enclosed charge and thus the net flux unchanged.
