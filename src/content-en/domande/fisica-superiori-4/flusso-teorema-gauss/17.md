On a flat area in a uniform field of magnitude $E>0$, net flux is $-\dfrac{EA}{2}$. What is the angle between the field and normal?

- $60^\circ$
- $120^\circ$
- $30^\circ$
- $150^\circ$

## Explanation

Dividing the formula by $EA$ gives a cosine of $-\dfrac12$. Between zero and $180^\circ$, this cosine corresponds to an obtuse angle of $120^\circ$.
