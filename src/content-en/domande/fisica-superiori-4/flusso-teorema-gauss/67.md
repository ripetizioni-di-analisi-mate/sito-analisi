Two infinite parallel planes have the same uniform positive density. Where is the total field zero?

- In both outer regions.
- Only on the plane equidistant from both sheets.
- Throughout the region between the planes.
- The field is zero in no region.

## Explanation

Both fields point away from their own plane. Between the planes they oppose each other with equal magnitude. In the outside regions they point the same way.
