Two points lie on opposite sides of the same positive infinite wire, along a line perpendicular to it and at equal distances. How do the field vectors compare?

- Equal magnitudes and the same direction.
- Different magnitudes and opposite directions.
- Both parallel to the wire.
- Equal magnitudes and opposite directions.

## Explanation

Equal distances give equal magnitudes. At each point the field points away from the wire, so the vectors are antiparallel.
