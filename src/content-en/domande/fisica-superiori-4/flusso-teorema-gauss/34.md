A positive charge at the center of a cube is moved toward one face while staying inside. Which statement is correct?

- Every face keeps its initial flux.
- The sum of the fluxes grows because one face gets closer to the charge.
- Total flux stays constant, while individual face fluxes can change.
- Total flux is zero when the charge is off center.

## Explanation

All faces were equivalent when the charge was centered. Moving it breaks that symmetry, so the face fluxes generally differ. Their sum remains fixed by the same enclosed charge.
