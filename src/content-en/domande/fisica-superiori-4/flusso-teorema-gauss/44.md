A graph shows zero field inside a charged sphere's material, a jump just outside, and then an inverse-square decrease. What model does it describe?

- An isolated conducting sphere at equilibrium.
- An insulating sphere with charge uniform throughout its volume.
- An infinite plane distribution.
- A uniformly charged infinite wire.

## Explanation

In a metal at equilibrium excess charge lies on the surface. The field is zero before the boundary and just outside it is the field of the total charge at the center, by spherical symmetry.
