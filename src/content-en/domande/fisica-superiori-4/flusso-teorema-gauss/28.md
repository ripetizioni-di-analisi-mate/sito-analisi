The flux through a single open face of a box is known. Can the charge enclosed by the box be found by applying Gauss's law directly to that value?

- The net flux through the entire closed surface is required.
- Yes, because each face encloses the same charge as the box.
- Yes, multiply the face flux by two.
- Yes, whenever the flux through that face is positive.

## Explanation

The law uses the sum of all contributions over the closed surface. Knowing one face alone does not determine the sum without further information about the others.
