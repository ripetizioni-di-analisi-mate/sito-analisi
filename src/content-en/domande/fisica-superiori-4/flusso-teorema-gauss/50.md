Which surface lets us derive the field of an infinite straight wire with just one unknown normal component over the curved side?

- A cylinder whose axis is perpendicular to the wire.
- A cylinder with the same axis as the wire.
- A sphere centered on a point of the wire.
- A cube with the wire passing through its center.

## Explanation

On a coaxial cylinder every point on the curved side is the same distance from the axis and its normal coincides with the radial direction. The field does not cross the caps.
