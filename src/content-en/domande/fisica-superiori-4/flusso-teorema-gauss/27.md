A hemisphere of radius $0.20\,\mathrm{m}$ lies above its horizontal disk, which closes it. A uniform field of magnitude $100\,\mathrm{N/C}$ points upward, and there is no charge inside. What is the outward flux through the curved surface alone? Choose the closest value.

- $-12.6\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $25.1\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $12.6\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$

## Explanation

The outward normal on the base points down, giving negative flux across the base. The closed flux is zero, so flux across the curved part exactly cancels the base contribution.

$$
\Phi_{\mathrm{curva}}=-\Phi_{\mathrm{base}}=100\pi(0.20)^2\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}\simeq12.6\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}.
$$
