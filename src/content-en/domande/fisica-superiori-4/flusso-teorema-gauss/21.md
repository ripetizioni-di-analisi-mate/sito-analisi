An open surface consists of two flat portions with negligible connecting areas, placed in the same uniform field of magnitude $60\,\mathrm{N/C}$. Their areas are $0.20$ and $0.10\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Their normals make angles of $0^\circ$ and $180^\circ$ with the field, respectively. What is the total flux?

- $18\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $6\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$

## Explanation

Compute each panel's contribution relative to its own normal. The cosines have opposite signs and the areas differ, so the contributions subtract and do not cancel.

$$
\Phi_E=60(0.20-0.10)\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}=6\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}.
$$
