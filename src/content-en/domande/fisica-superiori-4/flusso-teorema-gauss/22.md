The graph shows $\Phi_E$ as the angle to the normal varies. At what angles is the flux negative?

::grafico{f="cos(x*PI/180)" segmenti="90,-1,90,1" punti="90,0,pieno" xmin=0 xmax=180 ymin=-1.1 ymax=1.1 nomi="θ,Φ_E/(EA)" legenda="\cos\theta" titolo="The horizontal axis gives the angle in degrees. The plotted value is flux divided by EA."}

- For $0^\circ\leq\theta<90^\circ$.
- Only for $\theta=90^\circ$.
- For $90^\circ<\theta\leq180^\circ$.
- Only for $\theta=0^\circ$.

## Explanation

For fixed field and area the flux has the sign of the cosine. Beyond $90^\circ$ the graph lies below the horizontal axis and the field has a component opposite the normal.
