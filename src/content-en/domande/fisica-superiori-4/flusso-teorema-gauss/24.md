A cube has edge $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and faces parallel to the coordinate planes. The uniform field components are $(3,-1,2)\,\mathrm{N/C}$. What is the sum of the fluxes through the three faces with normals along the positive axes?

- $0\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $8\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $24\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $16\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$

## Explanation

Each face has area $4\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and receives the contribution of one field component. Add the three signed components before multiplying by the area.

$$
\Phi_+=(3-1+2)\cdot4\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}=16\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}.
$$
