Two flat surfaces lie in the same uniform field. The first has area $A$ and normal along the field. The second has area $2A$ and its normal is at $60^\circ$ to the field. Compare their fluxes.

- The second flux is twice as large.
- The second flux is half the first.
- The fluxes have opposite signs.
- The fluxes are equal and positive.

## Explanation

For the second surface the field component along the normal is halved and the area is doubled. For the first the full field is normal. The product is the same in both cases.

$$
\begin{gathered}
\Phi_1=EA\cos0^\circ=EA,\\[6pt]
\Phi_2=E(2A)\cos60^\circ=EA.
\end{gathered}
$$
