Around the same infinite wire, a Gaussian cylinder is made twice as long without changing its radius. How do total flux and the field magnitude at the curved side change?

- Flux doubles and the field stays the same.
- Flux and field both double.
- Flux stays the same and the field is halved.
- Flux and field both stay the same.

## Explanation

Enclosed charge doubles with length, so flux doubles. The curved area also doubles. The field at the side, which is their ratio, stays unchanged.
