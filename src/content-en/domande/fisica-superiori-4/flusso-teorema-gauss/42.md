Two concentric, uniformly charged spherical shells carry $+Q$ and $-Q$. What is the field outside both?

- It equals the field of the outer shell alone.
- It equals the field of the inner shell alone.
- It is zero.
- It equals the field of twice the charge at the center.

## Explanation

Outside both shells, a Gaussian sphere encloses zero net charge. Symmetry forces the field to be radial and constant in magnitude on the sphere, so it is zero.
