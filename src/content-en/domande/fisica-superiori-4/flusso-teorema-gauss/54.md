Two infinite parallel wires have the same positive linear density. What occurs at the midpoint of the perpendicular segment joining them?

- The total field has twice the magnitude produced by one wire.
- The total field is zero.
- The total field points along the wires.
- The total field equals that of the wire on the left.

## Explanation

The wires are at equal distances, so they produce equal magnitudes. Since each field points away from its own wire, their directions at the midpoint are opposite and the sum is zero.
