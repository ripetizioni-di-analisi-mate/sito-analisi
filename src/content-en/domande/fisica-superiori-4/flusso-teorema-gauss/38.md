An insulating sphere of radius $R$ has uniform volume charge. Compare the field magnitudes at $\dfrac R4$ and $2R$.

- The inner magnitude is four times the outer one.
- The outer magnitude is twice the inner one.
- The two magnitudes are equal.
- The inner magnitude is zero.

## Explanation

The inner field at one-quarter of the radius is one-quarter of the surface value because it rises linearly. The outer field at twice the radius is also one-quarter of the surface value because it falls with the square of the distance.
