A closed cylinder with equal caps parallel to two infinite sheets crosses both sheets, whose densities are equal and opposite. Both caps lie outside the gap. Why is the total flux zero?

- All surface normals point in the same direction.
- Both charges lie outside the cylinder.
- Each sheet by itself produces zero field.
- The enclosed charge is zero.

## Explanation

The cylinder encloses equal areas of the two sheets. Those portions have equal charge magnitudes and opposite signs, so net enclosed charge is zero. The field between the sheets can still be nonzero.
