A positive infinite wire is at the center of an infinite coaxial conducting tube. The tube has total linear charge density equal and opposite to the wire's. What is the field outside the tube?

- It equals the field of the wire alone.
- It is zero.
- It equals the field of the tube alone.
- It is constant and nonzero.

## Explanation

An outer cylindrical surface encloses the two opposite charges for each meter, so flux is zero. By cylindrical symmetry the normal component is constant on the curved side and zero on the caps. The outer field is therefore zero.
