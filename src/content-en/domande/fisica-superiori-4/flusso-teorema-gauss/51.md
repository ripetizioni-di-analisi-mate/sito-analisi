Moving away from an axis from $r$ to $3r$, the measured field falls to one-third of its previous value. Which model has this dependence?

- An infinite plane.
- A point charge.
- An infinite straight wire.
- The region inside a uniformly charged sphere.

## Explanation

For an infinite line source the field is proportional to $\dfrac1r$. An ideal plane gives a constant field, and the exterior of a sphere follows $\dfrac1{r^2}$.
