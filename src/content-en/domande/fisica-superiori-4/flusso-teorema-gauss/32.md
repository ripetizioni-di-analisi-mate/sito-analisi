A charge is brought closer to a closed surface while always staying outside. Which quantity can change?

- The local field at the surface.
- The total enclosed charge.
- The net flux attributable to the external charge.
- The total outward flux.

## Explanation

The external charge gets closer to some surface points and its field there can change. In the Gauss balance the enclosed charge does not change.
