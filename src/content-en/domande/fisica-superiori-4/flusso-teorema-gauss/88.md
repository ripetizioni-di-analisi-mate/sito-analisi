Inside an insulating sphere of radius $R$ with uniform positive density, which concentric Gaussian radius captures one-eighth of the total exterior flux?

- $\dfrac R8$
- $\dfrac R4$
- $\dfrac{3R}{4}$
- $\dfrac R2$

## Explanation

Flux is proportional to the enclosed charge, which grows as the cube of the radius. Take the cube root of one-eighth, which is one-half.
