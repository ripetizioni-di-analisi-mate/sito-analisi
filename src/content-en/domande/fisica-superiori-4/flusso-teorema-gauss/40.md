An insulating sphere has uniform volume density. An inner concentric Gaussian sphere has one-third of the outer radius. What fraction of the total charge does it enclose?

- $\dfrac1{27}$
- $\dfrac13$
- $\dfrac19$
- $\dfrac1{81}$

## Explanation

Charge is distributed throughout the volume. The volume ratio is the cube of the radius ratio, so the charge fraction is one twenty-seventh.
