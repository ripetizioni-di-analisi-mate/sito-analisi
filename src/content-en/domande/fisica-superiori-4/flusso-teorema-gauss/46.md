An infinite wire's field is $300\,\mathrm{N/C}$ at $4\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the axis. At what distance is it $75\,\mathrm{N/C}$?

- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $16\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $1\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $64\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

At fixed cylinder length the enclosed wire charge stays the same. The curved side grows in proportion to distance, so a field one-quarter as large needs four times the initial distance.

$$
r_2=r_1\frac{E_1}{E_2}=4\frac{300}{75}\,\mathrm{cm}=16\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
