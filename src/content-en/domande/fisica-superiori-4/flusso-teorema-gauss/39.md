For a uniformly charged insulating sphere of radius $12\,\mathrm{cm}$, where inside is the field $75\%$ of its surface value?

- $3\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $6\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $16\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $9\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

Inside, the ratio of field magnitudes equals the ratio of radii. Take seventy-five percent of the surface radius.

$$
r=0.75R=0.75\cdot12\,\mathrm{cm}=9\,\mathrm{cm}.
$$
