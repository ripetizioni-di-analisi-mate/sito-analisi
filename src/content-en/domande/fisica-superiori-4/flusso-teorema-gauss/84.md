A spherical Gaussian surface grows in radius around its center. Flux is zero for $r<R$ and constant and positive for $r>R$. Which distribution matches this behavior?

::grafico{f="x<1?0:NaN;x>1?1:NaN" punti="1,0,vuoto;1,1,vuoto" xmin=0 xmax=3 ymin=-0.1 ymax=1.25 nomi="r/R,Φ_E/Φ₀" legenda=0 titolo="The graph shows flux normalized to the positive exterior value."}

- A sphere with positive charge uniform throughout its volume.
- A single negative charge at the center.
- A single positive charge at the center.
- A positively charged thin spherical shell at radius R.

## Explanation

Flux follows enclosed charge. Passing the radius of a thin shell encloses all of its charge at once, producing the jump.
