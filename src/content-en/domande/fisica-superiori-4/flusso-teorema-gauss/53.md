Two infinite parallel wires have equal and opposite densities. At the midpoint of the perpendicular segment joining them, each wire alone would produce a field of magnitude $75\,\mathrm{N/C}$. What is the total field magnitude?

- $150\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $75\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $225\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

The positive wire's field points from that wire toward the midpoint. The negative wire's field points from the midpoint toward the negative wire. Both point in the same direction, so their magnitudes add.

$$
E=(75+75)\,\mathrm{N/C}=150\,\mathrm{N/C}.
$$
