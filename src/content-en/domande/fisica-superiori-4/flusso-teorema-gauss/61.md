An infinite charged plane produces a field of magnitude $E_0$ a distance $d$ on one side. What is the magnitude at distance $3d$ on that same side?

- $E_0$
- $\dfrac{E_0}{3}$
- $\dfrac{E_0}{9}$
- $3E_0$

## Explanation

For a pillbox surface, cap areas and enclosed charge depend on the cross-section. Moving the caps away from the plane leaves those quantities unchanged, so the field stays constant.
