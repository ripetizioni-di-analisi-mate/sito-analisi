Between two infinite insulating sheets of equal and opposite uniform charge density the field measures $180\,\mathrm{N/C}$. One sheet is removed and the other's density stays the same. What is the field magnitude at the same position?

- $0\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $90\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $45\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $180\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

Initially the two contributions pointed the same way and were equal. After removal only one contribution remains, so the magnitude is half the original value.

$$
E_{\mathrm{una}}=\frac{180}{2}\,\mathrm{N/C}=90\,\mathrm{N/C}.
$$
