The normal to an open surface is reversed and its initial flux is $\Phi\ne0$. What happens to the flux?

- It becomes $-\Phi$.
- It stays $\Phi$.
- It becomes $2\Phi$.
- It becomes zero.

## Explanation

The area vector is multiplied by $-1$. By linearity of the dot product, the flux keeps its magnitude and changes sign.
