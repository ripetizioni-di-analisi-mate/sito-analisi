A short cylindrical Gaussian surface crosses a plane with uniform negative charge density. What are the signs of the fluxes on its two caps?

- Both positive.
- Both negative.
- One positive and one negative.
- Both zero.

## Explanation

The two outward normals point away from the plane, one on either side. The negative plane's field points toward the plane on both sides. The flux is therefore negative on each cap.
