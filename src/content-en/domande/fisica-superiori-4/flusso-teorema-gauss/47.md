Two infinite charged wires are studied separately. The second has three times the linear charge density in magnitude. At what distance from the second is the field magnitude the same as at distance $r$ from the first?

- $\dfrac r3$
- $9r$
- $3r$
- $r$

## Explanation

The field is proportional to $\lvert\lambda_q\rvert$ and inversely proportional to $r$. To keep the same ratio, multiply distance by the same factor as density.
