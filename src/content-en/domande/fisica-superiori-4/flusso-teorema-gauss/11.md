Two concentric Gaussian spheres of radii $r$ and $2r$ enclose the same positive point charge at their center. What is the ratio of the field magnitude on the larger sphere to that on the smaller sphere?

- $\dfrac{1}{16}$
- $4$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

The surfaces enclose the same charge and have areas in a one-to-four ratio. Dividing their equal flux by the two areas gives the comparison of field magnitudes.

$$
\begin{gathered}
\frac{\Phi_{2r}}{\Phi_r}=1,\\[6pt]
\frac{E_{2r}}{E_r}=\frac{4\pi r^2}{4\pi(2r)^2}=\frac14.
\end{gathered}
$$
