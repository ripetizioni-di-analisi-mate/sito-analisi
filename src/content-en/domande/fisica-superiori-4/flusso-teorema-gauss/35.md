A positive charge at the center of a cube produces total flux $720\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$. How much flux crosses a single face?

- $60\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $240\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $360\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $120\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$

## Explanation

The six faces are equivalent under rotations about the center, so they share total flux equally. Field magnitude need not be constant over an individual face.

$$
\Phi_{\mathrm{faccia}}=\frac{720}{6}\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}=120\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}.
$$
