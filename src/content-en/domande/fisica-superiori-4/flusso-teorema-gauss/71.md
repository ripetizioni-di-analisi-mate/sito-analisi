A pillbox with caps of area $0.30\,\mathrm{m^2}$ crosses an infinite plane with density $8.85\,\mathrm{nC/m^2}$. With $\varepsilon_0=8.85\cdot10^{-12}\,\mathrm{C^2/(N\,m^2)}$, what is the total outward flux?

- $150\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $600\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $300\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$

## Explanation

The cylinder encloses the part of the plane whose area equals one cap. Multiply density by that area and divide by permittivity. This gives the total flux through both caps.

$$
\Phi_E=\frac{\sigma_q A}{\varepsilon_0}=\frac{8.85\cdot10^{-9}\cdot0.30}{8.85\cdot10^{-12}}\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}=300\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}.
$$
