Two ideal plates produce an electric field from the positive plate to the negative one. A small negative test charge is placed between the plates, well away from the edges. In which direction is the electric force?

- Toward the negative plate.
- Parallel to the plates.
- The force is zero because the plates are oppositely charged.
- Toward the positive plate.

## Explanation

The force is $\vec F=q\vec E$. Since $q<0$, the force points opposite the field, toward the positive plate. The field produced by the plates keeps its direction.
