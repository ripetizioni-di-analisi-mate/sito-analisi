A nonsymmetric closed surface has total area $A$ and flux $\Phi_E$. What exactly does the ratio $\dfrac{\Phi_E}{A}$ represent?

- The surface average of the signed normal component.
- The field magnitude at every point.
- The average of the tangential component alone.
- The net enclosed charge.

## Explanation

Flux sums the normal component times each corresponding area element. Dividing by total area gives the area-weighted mean of that component.
