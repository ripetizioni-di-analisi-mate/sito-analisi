An inner spherical shell has uniform charge $-2\,\mathrm{nC}$ and a concentric outer shell has uniform charge $+5\,\mathrm{nC}$. Which way does the field point between the shells?

- Away from the center.
- The field is zero throughout the gap.
- Tangentially to the shells.
- Toward the center.

## Explanation

A Gaussian surface between the shells encloses only the inner shell. The radial component has the sign of its charge, so the field points toward the center.
