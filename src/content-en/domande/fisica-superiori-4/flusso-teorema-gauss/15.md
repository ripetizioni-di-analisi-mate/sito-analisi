A uniform field is perpendicular to the front face of a box and enters the box through that face. What is the angle with its outward normal?

- $0^\circ$
- $90^\circ$
- $180^\circ$
- $45^\circ$

## Explanation

The outward normal points from the box to the exterior. The entering field points along the same line in the opposite direction, so the angle is straight.
