An infinite wire lies along the $z$-axis and has negative linear density. Which way does the field point at $(a,0,0)$ with $a>0$?

- Along $+x$.
- Along $+z$.
- Along $-z$.
- Along $-x$.

## Explanation

The point lies on the positive horizontal axis. The field of negative density is radially inward, from the point toward the nearest part of the wire's axis.
