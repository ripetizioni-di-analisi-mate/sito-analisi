An insulating sphere of radius $R$ has uniform negative volume density. How does the signed radial component $E_r$ behave from $r=0$ to $r=R$?

- It increases linearly from zero.
- It decreases linearly from zero.
- It stays negative and constant.
- It follows an inverse-square law.

## Explanation

For uniform charge, $E_r=\dfrac{Qr}{4\pi\varepsilon_0R^3}$. The coefficient of $r$ is negative. The component starts at zero and becomes increasingly negative as the radius increases.
