A point is to the left of two infinite parallel planes with equal and opposite uniform charge densities. The left plane is positive. Which directions do the two field contributions have?

- Both contribute rightward.
- The positive contributes leftward and the negative rightward.
- Both contribute leftward.
- The positive contributes rightward and the negative leftward.

## Explanation

To the left of the positive plane its field points away from the plane, hence left. In the same region the negative plane's contribution points toward the negative plane, hence right.
