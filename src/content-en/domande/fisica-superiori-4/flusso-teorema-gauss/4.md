A spherically symmetric distribution of radius $0.050\,\mathrm{m}$ has total charge $2.4\,\mathrm{nC}$. With $\varepsilon_0=8.85\cdot10^{-12}\,\mathrm{C^2/(N\,m^2)}$, which value is closest to the field magnitude $0.20\,\mathrm{m}$ from the center?

- $E=8630\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $E=135\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $E=1080\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $E=540\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

The point is outside, so its Gaussian sphere encloses all the charge. The area that carries flux has radius $0.20\,\mathrm{m}$.

$$
E=\frac{2.4\cdot10^{-9}}{4\pi(8.85\cdot10^{-12})(0.20)^2}\,\mathrm{N/C}\simeq540\,\mathrm{N/C}.
$$
