A closed surface has total outward flux $288\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$. With $\varepsilon_0=8.85\cdot10^{-12}\,\mathrm{C^2/(N\,m^2)}$, which value is closest to the net enclosed charge in nanocoulombs?

- $Q_{\mathrm{int}}=-2.55\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $Q_{\mathrm{int}}=5.1\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $Q_{\mathrm{int}}=2.55\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $Q_{\mathrm{int}}=0.392\,\mathrm{nC}$

## Explanation

Multiply the net flux by the constant because the surface is closed. Converting coulombs to nanocoulombs multiplies the numerical value by $10^9$.

$$
Q_{\mathrm{int}}=\varepsilon_0\Phi_E=2.5488\cdot10^{-9}\,\mathrm{C}\simeq2.55\,\mathrm{nC}.
$$
