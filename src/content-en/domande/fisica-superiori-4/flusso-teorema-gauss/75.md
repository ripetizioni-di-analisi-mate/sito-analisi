Just outside an equilibrium conductor, the field measures $200\,\mathrm{N/C}$ and points into the metal. What is the local surface density in nanocoulombs per square meter? Use $\varepsilon_0=8.85\cdot10^{-12}\,\mathrm{C^2/(N\,m^2)}$.

- $1.77\,\mathrm{nC/m^2}$
- $-3.54\,\mathrm{nC/m^2}$
- $-1.77\,\mathrm{nC/m^2}$
- $0\,\mathrm{nC/m^2}$

## Explanation

The normal points outward while the field points inward. Only the exterior cap of the Gaussian cylinder contributes, with a negative component. Multiply that signed component by the permittivity to get the density.

$$
\sigma_q=\varepsilon_0 E_{n,\mathrm{est}}=-8.85\cdot10^{-12}\cdot200\,\mathrm{C/m^2}=-1.77\,\mathrm{nC/m^2}.
$$
