A uniform field of magnitude $300\,\mathrm{N/C}$ makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with the normal to a flat surface of area $0.040\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Calculate the flux.

- $\Phi_E=12\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $\Phi_E=6\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $\Phi_E=-6\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $\Phi_E=10.4\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$

## Explanation

Flux uses the component along the normal. Here the cosine is positive and equal to one-half. Multiply it by the field and the area to get the signed flux.

$$
\Phi_E=EA\cos60^\circ=300\cdot0.040\cdot\frac12\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}=6\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}.
$$
