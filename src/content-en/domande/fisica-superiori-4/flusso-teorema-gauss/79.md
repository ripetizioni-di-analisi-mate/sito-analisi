An exterior charge is brought close to a conductor in equilibrium with an empty closed cavity. Does the field inside the cavity change?

- It becomes the field of the exterior charge alone.
- It always points toward the geometric center of the cavity.
- It remains zero.
- It has nonzero net flux through every interior surface.

## Explanation

The whole inner wall is at the same potential. In a cavity with no sources an equipotential boundary gives a constant-potential solution, so the field in the cavity remains zero.
