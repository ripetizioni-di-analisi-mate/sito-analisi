A thin spherical shell is uniformly charged and far from other sources. What is the field in the region entirely inside the shell?

- It is zero only at the center.
- It is zero throughout the interior region.
- It increases linearly from the center.
- It is inversely proportional to the square of the radius.

## Explanation

An inner concentric Gaussian sphere contains no charge. By symmetry the normal component is constant on each sphere, so zero flux implies zero field at every interior radius.
