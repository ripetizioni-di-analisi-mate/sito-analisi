An infinite wire has uniform linear density $1.44\,\mathrm{nC/m}$. With $\varepsilon_0=8.85\cdot10^{-12}\,\mathrm{C^2/(N\,m^2)}$, which value is closest to the field magnitude at a distance of $0.080\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $E=162\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $E=648\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $E=324\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $E=25.9\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

The field is constant in magnitude and normal over the curved side of a coaxial cylinder. Flux through the caps is zero. The cylinder length cancels between the side area and the enclosed charge.

$$
E=\frac{1.44\cdot10^{-9}}{2\pi(8.85\cdot10^{-12})(0.080)}\,\mathrm{N/C}\simeq324\,\mathrm{N/C}.
$$
