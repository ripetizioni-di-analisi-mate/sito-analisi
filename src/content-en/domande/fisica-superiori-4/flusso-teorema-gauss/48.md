A Gaussian cylinder coaxial with an infinite wire encloses $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$ of wire and has net outward flux $200\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$. Find the linear density in nanocoulombs per meter. Use $\varepsilon_0=8.85\cdot10^{-12}\,\mathrm{C^2/(N\,m^2)}$.

- $1.77\,\mathrm{nC/m}$
- $-3.54\,\mathrm{nC/m}$
- $7.08\,\mathrm{nC/m}$
- $3.54\,\mathrm{nC/m}$

## Explanation

Flux gives the enclosed charge on the half-meter segment. Dividing by the length gives the wire's linear density.

$$
\lambda_q=\frac{\varepsilon_0\Phi_E}{L}=\frac{8.85\cdot10^{-12}\cdot200}{0.50}\,\mathrm{C/m}=3.54\,\mathrm{nC/m}.
$$
