Which SI unit corresponds to electric flux?

- $\mathrm{N/C}$
- $\mathrm{C/m^2}$
- $\mathrm{N/(C\,m^2)}$
- $\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$

## Explanation

The normal component is measured in newtons per coulomb and area in square meters. Their product has units of newton square meters per coulomb.
