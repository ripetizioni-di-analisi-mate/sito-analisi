In a uniform field $\vec E=(4,3,0)\,\mathrm{N/C}$, a flat face of area $2\,\mathrm{m^2}$ has normal $\hat n=(-1,0,0)$. What is its flux?

- $-8\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $8\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $-10\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$
- $6\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$

## Explanation

The component along the normal is the negative of the horizontal-axis component. The transverse component does not cross the face.

$$
\Phi_E=(\vec E\cdot\hat n)A=(-4)\cdot2\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}=-8\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}.
$$
