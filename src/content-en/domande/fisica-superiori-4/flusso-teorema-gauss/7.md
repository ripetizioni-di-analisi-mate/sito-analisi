An infinite insulating plane has uniform surface density $1.8\cdot10^{-8}\,\mathrm{C/m^2}$. Calculate the field magnitude on one side. Use $\varepsilon_0=8.85\cdot10^{-12}\,\mathrm{C^2/(N\,m^2)}$. Choose the closest value.

- $E=2030\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $E=508\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $E=0\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $E\simeq 1020\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

For a pillbox surface, the field crosses both caps in the same direction relative to their respective normals. Both contribute to the flux, giving a factor of two.

$$
E=\frac{1.8\cdot10^{-8}}{2\cdot8.85\cdot10^{-12}}\,\mathrm{N/C}\simeq1.02\cdot10^3\,\mathrm{N/C}.
$$
