A flat surface of area $0.25\,\mathrm{m^2}$ has a $60^\circ$ angle between its normal and a uniform field. The flux is $15\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$. What is the field magnitude?

- $60\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $240\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $120\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $30\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

Divide the flux by the projected area, which is the area times the angle's cosine. Dividing by the area alone would give the normal field component.

$$
E=\frac{15}{0.25\cos60^\circ}\,\mathrm{N/C}=120\,\mathrm{N/C}.
$$
