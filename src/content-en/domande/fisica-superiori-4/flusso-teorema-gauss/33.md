A closed surface contains only two charges, $+q$ and $-q$, at different points. What does Gauss's law show?

- The field is zero everywhere on the surface.
- Total flux is zero, while the field can be nonzero.
- The flux equals that of the positive charge alone.
- Total flux is negative because one charge is negative.

## Explanation

The charge sum is zero, so the total flux is zero. Vector field contributions do not generally cancel point by point.
