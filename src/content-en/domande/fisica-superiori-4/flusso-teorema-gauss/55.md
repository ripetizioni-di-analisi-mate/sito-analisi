Wire A has linear density of magnitude $2\,\mathrm{nC/m}$, and B has magnitude $3\,\mathrm{nC/m}$. A is observed alone at $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ and B alone at $9\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is the ratio of B's field magnitude to A's?

- $1.5$
- $2$
- $0.5$
- $0.333$

## Explanation

The constants cancel in the ratio. Include both linear density and distance for each wire because both change.

$$
\frac{E_B}{E_A}=\frac{\lvert\lambda_B\rvert r_A}{\lvert\lambda_A\rvert r_B}=\frac{3\cdot3}{2\cdot9}=\frac12.
$$
