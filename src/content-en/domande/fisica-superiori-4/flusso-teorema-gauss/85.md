Charge on a sphere has greater surface density in its northern hemisphere than in its southern hemisphere. A concentric Gaussian sphere still gives the total flux. Can its area be used to find the field at every point by division?

- No, the ratio gives only the mean normal component.
- Yes, a spherical Gaussian surface is sufficient.
- Yes, provided the flux is positive.
- No, because the flux in this case must be zero.

## Explanation

The shape is spherical, while the distribution is not invariant under all rotations. The normal component can vary from point to point. Dividing by area gives its average.
