Two oppositely charged plates have surface densities of magnitude $9.6\cdot10^{-9}\,\mathrm{C/m^2}$. Neglecting edges, calculate the field magnitude between them. Use $\varepsilon_0=8.85\cdot10^{-12}\,\mathrm{C^2/(N\,m^2)}$. Choose the closest value.

- $E\simeq 1080\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $E=542\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $E=2170\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $E=0\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

The contributions from the positive and negative plates point in the same direction within the gap. Each has the magnitude from a single plane. Adding them cancels the factor of two.

$$
E=\frac{9.6\cdot10^{-9}}{8.85\cdot10^{-12}}\,\mathrm{N/C}\simeq1.08\cdot10^3\,\mathrm{N/C}.
$$
