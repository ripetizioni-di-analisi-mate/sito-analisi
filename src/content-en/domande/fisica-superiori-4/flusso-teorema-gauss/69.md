Two ideal parallel plates have fixed, opposite surface charge densities. Their separation is doubled without changing the densities. What happens to the field magnitude between the plates?

- It stays the same.
- It doubles.
- It is halved.
- It becomes one-quarter as large.

## Explanation

The field between two infinite planes depends on the density, $E=\dfrac{\sigma_q}{\varepsilon_0}$. The formula has no separation term, so doubling it leaves the magnitude unchanged.
