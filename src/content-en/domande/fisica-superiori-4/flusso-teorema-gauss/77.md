A conductor has net charge $-2\,\mathrm{nC}$ and a closed cavity containing a point charge $+5\,\mathrm{nC}$. What is the total charge on the conductor's outer surface?

- $3\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $-7\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $5\,\mathrm{nC}$

## Explanation

The field is zero in the metal. A Gaussian surface enclosing the cavity gives zero total charge, so the inner wall carries the opposite of the point charge. The outer surface carries the remainder needed to give the conductor's net charge.

$$
\begin{gathered}
Q_{\mathrm{parete}}=-5\,\mathrm{nC},\\[6pt]
Q_{\mathrm{est}}=(-2-(-5))\,\mathrm{nC}=+3\,\mathrm{nC}.
\end{gathered}
$$
