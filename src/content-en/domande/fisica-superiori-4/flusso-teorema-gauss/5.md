An insulating sphere of radius $0.10\,\mathrm{m}$ has uniformly distributed total charge $1.2\,\mathrm{nC}$. Calculate the field magnitude $0.040\,\mathrm{m}$ from the center. Use $\varepsilon_0=8.85\cdot10^{-12}\,\mathrm{C^2/(N\,m^2)}$. Choose the closest value.

- $E\simeq 432\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $E=0\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $E=1080\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $E=6740\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

The Gaussian surface is inside. The enclosed charge is the volume fraction, $(0.4)^3$, of the total charge. Divide its flux contribution by the inner sphere's area.

$$
E=\frac{1.2\cdot10^{-9}(0.040)}{4\pi(8.85\cdot10^{-12})(0.10)^3}\,\mathrm{N/C}\simeq432\,\mathrm{N/C}.
$$
