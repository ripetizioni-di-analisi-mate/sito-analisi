A nonzero uniform field lies exactly within a flat surface. The surface doubles in area without changing orientation. What is the new flux?

- $EA$
- $2EA$
- $0$
- $\dfrac{EA}{2}$

## Explanation

The field does not cross the plane. The normal component stays zero because the angle with the normal remains $90^\circ$. Multiplying by the new area therefore still gives zero flux.
