The net outward flux through a closed surface is negative. What is guaranteed?

- The field points inward at every point of the surface.
- The net enclosed charge is negative.
- Every individual charge inside is negative.
- The net enclosed charge is positive.

## Explanation

The constant $\varepsilon_0$ is positive, so net enclosed charge has the flux's sign. This does not determine the sign of every individual charge or local contribution.
