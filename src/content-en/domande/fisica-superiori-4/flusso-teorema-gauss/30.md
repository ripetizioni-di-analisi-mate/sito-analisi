Two nested closed surfaces have net fluxes of $100$ and $-200\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}$, respectively, from inner to outer. Find the net charge between the surfaces in nanocoulombs. Use $\varepsilon_0=8.85\cdot10^{-12}\,\mathrm{C^2/(N\,m^2)}$. Choose the closest value.

- $2.66\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $-0.885\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $-2.66\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $0.885\,\mathrm{nC}$

## Explanation

The outer surface encloses the inner region plus the layer between the surfaces. Subtracting the two Gauss equations leaves only the charge in that layer.

$$
Q_{\mathrm{strato}}=\varepsilon_0(\Phi_{\mathrm{est}}-\Phi_{\mathrm{int}})\simeq-2.66\,\mathrm{nC}.
$$
