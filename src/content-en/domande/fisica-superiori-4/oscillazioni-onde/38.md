A simple pendulum is released from rest at $60^\circ$ from vertical. Which assumption of $T=2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{\ell}{g}}$ needs checking?

- The constancy of $g$ over time
- Cancellation of mass
- The approximation $\sin\theta\simeq\theta$
- The direction of tension toward the pivot

## Explanation

The two values differ appreciably at release, so a linear restoring law does not accurately describe the entire oscillation.

$$
\sin 60^\circ=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2},\qquad \theta=\frac{\pi}{3}\,\mathrm{rad}.
$$
