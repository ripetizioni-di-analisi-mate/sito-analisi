A $1.44\,\mathrm{m}$ string fixed at both ends has a wave speed of $120\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the frequency of the third harmonic?

- $62.5\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $250\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $125\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $375\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

In the third harmonic, three half wavelengths fill the string. The wavelength is therefore two thirds of $L$, and the ratio of wave speed to wavelength gives the frequency.

$$
f_3=\frac{3u}{2L}=\frac{3\cdot120}{2\cdot1.44}\,\mathrm{Hz}=125\,\mathrm{Hz}.
$$
