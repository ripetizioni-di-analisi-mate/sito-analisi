The signed angle of an ideal pendulum from the downward vertical is $+30^\circ$. What is its signed tangential acceleration?

- $+\dfrac{g}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{\sqrt{3}g}{2}$
- $-\dfrac{g}{2}$
- $0$

## Explanation

The tension acts along the string, so only the weight has a tangential component. It points back toward the vertical and has the opposite sign from the positive angle.

$$
a_{\mathrm{tan}}=-g\sin 30^\circ=-\frac{g}{2}.
$$
