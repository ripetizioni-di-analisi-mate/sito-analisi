Two ideal oscillators use identical springs and have the same amplitude. The second mass is four times the first. How are their maximum speeds related?

- $v_{\max,2}=2v_{\max,1}$
- $v_{\max,2}=\dfrac{v_{\max,1}}{2}$
- $v_{\max,2}=v_{\max,1}$
- $v_{\max,2}=\dfrac{v_{\max,1}}{4}$

## Explanation

The spring constant and amplitude set the same energy. At equilibrium all of it is kinetic, so quadrupling the mass gives one quarter of the squared speed.

$$
\frac{1}{2}m_1v_{\max,1}^2=\frac{1}{2}(4m_1)v_{\max,2}^2.
$$
