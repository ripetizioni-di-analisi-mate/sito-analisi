The vibrating length of a string fixed at both ends is halved without changing its tension or linear density. How does its fundamental frequency change?

- $f_{1,2}=\dfrac{f_{1,1}}{2}$
- $f_{1,2}=f_{1,1}$
- $f_{1,2}=4f_{1,1}$
- $f_{1,2}=2f_{1,1}$

## Explanation

Wave speed stays the same because tension and linear density stay the same. The fundamental wavelength is twice the vibrating length, so its frequency doubles.

$$
f_{1,2}=\frac{u}{2(L/2)}=2f_{1,1}.
$$
