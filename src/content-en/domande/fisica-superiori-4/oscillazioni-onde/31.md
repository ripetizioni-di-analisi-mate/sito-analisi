At which positive value of $x$ are the kinetic and spring potential energies of a spring oscillator with amplitude $A$ equal?

- $\dfrac{A}{\sqrt{2}}$
- $\dfrac{A}{2}$
- $A$
- $\dfrac{A}{4}$

## Explanation

When the two energies are equal, each is half the total. Spring energy depends on $x^2$, so half the energy is reached beyond half the amplitude.

$$
\frac{kx^2}{2}=\frac{1}{2}\frac{kA^2}{2}\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad x=\frac{A}{\sqrt{2}}.
$$
