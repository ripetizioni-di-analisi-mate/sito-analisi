A positive pulse reaches the fixed right end of a string. With what sign and direction does the reflected pulse return?

- Positive and leftward
- Negative and leftward
- Negative and rightward
- Positive and rightward

## Explanation

The boundary must stay at zero displacement. During reflection the reflected contribution cancels the incident one at the boundary, so it is inverted and travels away from the end.
