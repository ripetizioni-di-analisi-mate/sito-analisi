The mass of an ideal spring oscillator quadruples while $k$ stays constant. What is the new period relative to the old one?

- $2$
- $4$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $1$

## Explanation

The same spring produces the same force at a given displacement. The larger mass reduces acceleration and the period increases with the square root of mass.

$$
\frac{T_2}{T_1}=\sqrt{\frac{4m}{m}}=2.
$$
