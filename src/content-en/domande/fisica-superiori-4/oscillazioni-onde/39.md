A clock pendulum is shortened, while the clock still counts each cycle as before. How does its reading change relative to real time?

- The clock runs fast
- The clock stays on time
- The clock runs slow
- The clock stops

## Explanation

Shortening the pendulum reduces its period. In a fixed interval of real time, the clock counts more cycles, so it reports more elapsed time.
