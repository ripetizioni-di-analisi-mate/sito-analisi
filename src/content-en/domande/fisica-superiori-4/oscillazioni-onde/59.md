A transverse wave has amplitude $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ and frequency $4\,\mathrm{Hz}$. What is the maximum speed of a material point on the string?

- $16\pi\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.08\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.08\pi\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.16\pi\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

At a fixed position the motion is harmonic. The greatest local speed is angular frequency times amplitude, converted to meters.

$$
v_{\max}=\omega A=2\pi\cdot4\cdot0.02\,\mathrm{m/s}=0.16\pi\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
