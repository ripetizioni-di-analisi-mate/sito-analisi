A body oscillates as $x(t)=0.20\cos(4t)$ in SI units. What is its acceleration magnitude at the positive turning point?

- $0.80\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $6.4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $3.2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

The coefficient of $t$ is the angular frequency. At the turning point velocity is zero and displacement is greatest, so the acceleration toward equilibrium has its largest magnitude.

$$
|a|=\omega^2A=4^2\cdot0.20\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=3.2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.
$$
