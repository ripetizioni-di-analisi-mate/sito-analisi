A $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$ pendulum has period $\pi\,\mathrm{s}$ for small oscillations. What value of $g$ follows?

- $10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $40\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Square the period formula to isolate $g$. The powers of $\pi$ cancel after substitution.

$$
g=\frac{4\pi^2\ell}{T^2}=\frac{4\pi^2\cdot2.5}{\pi^2}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.
$$
