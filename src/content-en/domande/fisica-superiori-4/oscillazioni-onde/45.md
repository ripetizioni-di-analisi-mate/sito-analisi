The body crosses equilibrium toward increasing $x$ at $t=1\,\mathrm{s}$ and crosses in the opposite direction at $t=4\,\mathrm{s}$, with no crossings in between. What is the period?

- $3\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $6\,\mathrm{s}$
- $12\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The first state recurs when position and direction match. These two crossings have opposite directions and are half a period apart.

$$
T=2(4-1)\,\mathrm{s}=6\,\mathrm{s}.
$$
