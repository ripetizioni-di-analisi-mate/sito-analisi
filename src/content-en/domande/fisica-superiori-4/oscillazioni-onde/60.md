The profiles show the same pulse at two times separated by $0.50\,\mathrm{s}$. What is its signed velocity?

::grafico{f="exp(-4*(x-2)^2);exp(-4*(x-4)^2)" xmin=0 xmax=6 ymin=-0.15 ymax=1.4 nomi="x,y/A" punti="2,1,pieno;4,1,pieno" legenda="\text{first time};\text{second time}" titolo="Two snapshots of the same pulse. Horizontal coordinates in meters."}

- $-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $+2\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $+1\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $+4\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The peak of the pulse moves from two to four meters. Its displacement is positive, so divide the difference in horizontal coordinates by the time elapsed.

$$
u_x=\frac{4-2}{0.50}\,\mathrm{m/s}=+4\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
