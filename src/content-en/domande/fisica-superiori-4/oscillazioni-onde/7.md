A uniform string has tension $108\,\mathrm{N}$ and linear density $0.012\,\mathrm{kg/m}$. Which value is closest to the speed of small transverse waves?

- $47.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $94.9\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $10.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $9000\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Tension divided by linear density has units of speed squared. The square root is therefore required and gives the rate at which the profile advances.

$$
u=\sqrt{\frac{F_T}{\mu}}=\sqrt{\frac{108}{0.012}}\,\mathrm{m/s}\simeq94.9\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
