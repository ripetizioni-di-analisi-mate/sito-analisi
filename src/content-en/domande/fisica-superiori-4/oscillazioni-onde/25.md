A $81\,\mathrm{N/m}$ spring produces oscillations with angular frequency $9\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. What is the oscillating mass?

- $9\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $1\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.50\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $2\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

Spring constant divided by mass must equal the angular frequency squared. Divide by $9^2$ to find the mass.

$$
m=\frac{k}{\omega^2}=\frac{81}{9^2}\,\mathrm{kg}=1\,\mathrm{kg}.
$$
