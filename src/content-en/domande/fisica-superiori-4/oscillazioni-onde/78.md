A $2.4\,\mathrm{m}$ string is fixed at both ends and vibrates in its fourth harmonic. How far is the first antinode from the left end?

- $0.60\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.15\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.20\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The first interval between nodes extends from the left end to one quarter of $L$. The first antinode lies halfway along this interval.

$$
x_{\mathrm{v},1}=\frac{L}{2n}=\frac{2.4}{2\cdot4}\,\mathrm{m}=0.30\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
