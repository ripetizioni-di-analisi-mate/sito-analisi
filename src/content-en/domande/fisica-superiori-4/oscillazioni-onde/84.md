The graph is the profile of a transverse wave moving to the right without changing shape. At the marked point $P$, how does the segment of string move as the profile advances?

::grafico{f="sin(pi*x/2)" xmin=-2 xmax=2.3 ymin=-1.4 ymax=1.4 nomi="x,y/A" punti="0,0,pieno,P" titolo="Profile at a fixed instant. Horizontal coordinates in meters."}

- Upward
- Downward
- Horizontally to the right
- It stays at rest

## Explanation

At $P$ the curve rises to the right. After a small rightward shift of the profile, the fixed location receives a $y$ value that was just to its left and was lower.
