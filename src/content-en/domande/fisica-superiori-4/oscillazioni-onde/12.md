An ideal spring oscillator has mechanical energy $E=0.80\,\mathrm{J}$. What is the kinetic energy when $x=\dfrac{A}{2}$?

- $0.20\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0.60\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0.40\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0.80\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Spring potential energy grows with the square of displacement, so halfway to the turning point it is one quarter of its value at the turning point. The rest of the energy is kinetic.

$$
K=E-\frac{E}{4}=\frac{3}{4}\cdot0.80\,\mathrm{J}=0.60\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
