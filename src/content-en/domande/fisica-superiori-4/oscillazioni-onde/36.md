Two pendulums at the same location oscillate at small angles. The first is $0.40\,\mathrm{m}$ long and has half the period of the second. How long is the second?

- $1.60\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.10\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

At fixed gravity the period grows with the square root of length. A period ratio of two requires a length ratio of four.

$$
\ell_2=\ell_1\left(\frac{T_2}{T_1}\right)^2=0.40\cdot2^2\,\mathrm{m}=1.60\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
