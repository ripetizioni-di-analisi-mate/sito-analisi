A weakly damped oscillator has a natural frequency of $2.0\,\mathrm{Hz}$. With a periodic force of fixed amplitude, at which driving frequency is the largest steady-state response expected?

- $0.20\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $1.0\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $8.0\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

Damping is weak, so the response peaks near the natural frequency. Among the listed values, driving at $2.0\,\mathrm{Hz}$ puts the system closest to resonance.
