A simple pendulum of length $0.96\,\mathrm{m}$ oscillates with small amplitude and negligible friction. With $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, which value is closest to the period?

- $0.313\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.97\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.509\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3.93\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The small angle lets us approximate the tangential component of the weight by a force proportional to displacement along the arc. The angular frequency is set by gravity and length, so the mass cancels.

$$
T=2\pi\sqrt{\frac{0.96}{9.81}}\,\mathrm{s}\simeq1.97\,\mathrm{s}.
$$
