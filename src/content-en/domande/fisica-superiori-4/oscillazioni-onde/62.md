A negative pulse reaches an ideal free end. What property does the reflected pulse have?

- It becomes positive and reverses direction
- It keeps both sign and direction
- It disappears at the end
- It stays negative and reverses direction

## Explanation

At the free boundary the slope of the string must vanish. Reflection without inversion meets this condition because the two profiles are mirror images at the boundary.
