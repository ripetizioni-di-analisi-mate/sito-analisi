In the single standing mode shown, antinode $A$ reaches its positive maximum. What happens at antinode $B$ at the same instant?

::grafico{f="sin(pi*x)" xmin=0 xmax=2 ymin=-1.4 ymax=1.4 nomi="x,y/A" punti="0.5,1,pieno,A;1.5,-1,pieno,B" titolo="Second-mode profile at a time of maximum displacement. Horizontal coordinates in meters."}

- It is at its positive maximum
- It crosses equilibrium with positive velocity
- It crosses equilibrium with negative velocity
- It is at its negative minimum

## Explanation

Adjacent antinodes lie in intervals where the spatial factor has opposite signs. When the time factor produces a maximum at $A$, it therefore produces a minimum at $B$.
