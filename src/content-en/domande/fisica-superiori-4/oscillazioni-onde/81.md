In a single standing mode, a vibrating string reaches the instant when all points are on the equilibrium line. At which points is transverse speed then greatest?

- At the nodes
- Equally at every point on the string
- Nowhere, because displacement is zero everywhere
- At the antinodes

## Explanation

This instant is a zero of the time factor. Points with nonzero amplitude are crossing equilibrium, each at its own maximum speed. The largest amplitude and hence speed occur at the antinodes.
