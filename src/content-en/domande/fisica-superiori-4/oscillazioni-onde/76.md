The fundamental frequency of a string fixed at both ends is $13\,\mathrm{Hz}$. Which harmonic corresponds to a $65\,\mathrm{Hz}$ mode?

- $n=5$
- $n=4$
- $n=9$
- $n=6$

## Explanation

Individual mode frequencies are integer multiples of the fundamental. The quotient also gives the number of antinodes in the profile.

$$
n=\frac{f_n}{f_1}=\frac{65}{13}=5.
$$
