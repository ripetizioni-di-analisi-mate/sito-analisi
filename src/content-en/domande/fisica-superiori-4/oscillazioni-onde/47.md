The graph records the displacement of one point on a string. How much time elapses between two successive maxima?

::grafico{f="cos(pi*(x-1))" xmin=0 xmax=4.5 ymin=-1.4 ymax=1.4 nomi="t,y/A" punti="1,1,pieno;3,1,pieno" titolo="Displacement of the same point over time. Time in seconds."}

- $2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.50\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Position is fixed, so the difference between the horizontal coordinates of the maxima is a time interval. The first maximum after $t=1\,\mathrm{s}$ is at $t=3\,\mathrm{s}$.

$$
T=(3-1)\,\mathrm{s}=2\,\mathrm{s}.
$$
