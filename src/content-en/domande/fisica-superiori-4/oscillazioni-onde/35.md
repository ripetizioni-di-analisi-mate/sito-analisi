Between two maxima of the same damped spring oscillator, its mechanical energy falls to one quarter. What is the ratio of the amplitudes measured at the second and first maxima?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{1}{16}$
- $2$

## Explanation

At both maxima the velocity is zero and the spring constant is the same. The ratio of the energies is the square of the amplitude ratio.

$$
\frac{A_2}{A_1}=\sqrt{\frac{E_2}{E_1}}=\frac{1}{2}.
$$
