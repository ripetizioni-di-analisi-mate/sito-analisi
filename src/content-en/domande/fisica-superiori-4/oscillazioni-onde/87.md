The same simple pendulum oscillates first with $3^\circ$ amplitude and then with $6^\circ$ amplitude. In the small-angle approximation, how do the periods compare?

- The second is twice as long
- The second is half as long
- They are equal
- The second is four times as long

## Explanation

For both angles the linear model gives the same period because length and gravity are identical. The pendulum with greater amplitude travels through a longer arc in the same time.

$$
T=2\pi\sqrt{\frac{\ell}{g}}.
$$
