At one point, two oscillations have the same frequency and opposite phases. The larger has amplitude $7\,\mathrm{mm}$ and the resultant has amplitude $4\,\mathrm{mm}$. What is the smaller amplitude?

- $4\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $3\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $7\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $11\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

When the larger oscillation is at its maximum, the other has opposite displacement. The amplitudes subtract, and the smaller is the difference between the larger and the resultant.

$$
A_{\mathrm{ris}}=7\,\mathrm{mm}-A_{\mathrm{min}}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad A_{\mathrm{min}}=3\,\mathrm{mm}.
$$
