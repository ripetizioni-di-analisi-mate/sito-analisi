The mass and spring constant become $9m$ and $4k$. By what factor does the ideal oscillator's period change?

- $\dfrac{9}{4}$
- $6$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

The larger mass tends to increase the period and the stiffer spring tends to reduce it. Both changes enter the same ratio under the square root.

$$
\frac{T_2}{T_1}=\sqrt{\frac{9m}{4k}\cdot\frac{k}{m}}=\frac{3}{2}.
$$
