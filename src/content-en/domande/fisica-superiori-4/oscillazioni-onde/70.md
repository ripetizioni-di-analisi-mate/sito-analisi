Two ideal strings have the same tension. The second has four times the first's linear density. How do the wave speeds compare?

- $u_2=2u_1$
- $u_2=\dfrac{u_1}{4}$
- $u_2=4u_1$
- $u_2=\dfrac{u_1}{2}$

## Explanation

A greater mass per unit length lowers speed at the same tension. Here the square root of one quarter gives half the speed.

$$
\frac{u_2}{u_1}=\sqrt{\frac{\mu_1}{4\mu_1}}=\frac{1}{2}.
$$
