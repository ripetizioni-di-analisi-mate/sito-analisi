A $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ string fixed at both ends vibrates with three antinodes at $60\,\mathrm{Hz}$. What is the wave speed?

- $60\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $90\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $180\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Three antinodes require three half wavelengths along the string. After finding $\lambda$, multiply that distance by the observed mode frequency.

$$
\lambda=\frac{2L}{3}=1\,\mathrm{m},\qquad u=\lambda f=60\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
