In the linear regime two pulses would separately produce displacements of $+5\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $-8\,\mathrm{mm}$ at the same point and time. What is the total displacement?

- $-3\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $+3\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $-13\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $+13\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

At that point add the displacements with their signs. The negative contribution is larger in magnitude, so the result is negative.

$$
y=y_1+y_2=(5-8)\,\mathrm{mm}=-3\,\mathrm{mm}.
$$
