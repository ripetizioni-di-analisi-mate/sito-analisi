Two points on the same traveling sinusoidal wave are $\dfrac{\lambda}{2}$ apart along the direction of propagation. How do their displacements compare at every instant?

- $y_2=y_1$
- $y_2=-y_1$
- $y_2=2y_1$
- $y_2=0$

## Explanation

A phase shift of $\pi$ changes the cosine into its negative. When one displacement is positive, the other is negative at that same instant.

$$
\Delta\varphi=\frac{2\pi}{\lambda}\frac{\lambda}{2}=\pi.
$$
