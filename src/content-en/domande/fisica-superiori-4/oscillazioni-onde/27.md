The same mass oscillates on the same ideal spring at three times the amplitude. What is $\dfrac{T_2}{T_1}$?

- $3$
- $9$
- $1$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}$

## Explanation

Both oscillations have the same angular frequency because the force remains proportional to displacement with the same constant. The amplitude changes the turning points.

$$
T_1=T_2=2\pi\sqrt{\frac{m}{k}}.
$$
