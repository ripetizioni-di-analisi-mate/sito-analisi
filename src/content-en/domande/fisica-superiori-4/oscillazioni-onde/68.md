On a string under $16\,\mathrm{N}$ of tension, transverse disturbances travel at $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the linear density?

- $0.040\,\mathrm{kg/m}$
- $0.80\,\mathrm{kg/m}$
- $0.40\,\mathrm{kg/m}$
- $25\,\mathrm{kg/m}$

## Explanation

Square the wave speed equation. After solving for density, the denominator therefore contains the square of $20$.

$$
\mu=\frac{F_T}{u^2}=\frac{16}{20^2}\,\mathrm{kg/m}=0.040\,\mathrm{kg/m}.
$$
