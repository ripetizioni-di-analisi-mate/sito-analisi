On a string fixed at both ends, one standing mode shows four antinodes. How many internal nodes are there?

- $4$
- $5$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

Each antinode lies between consecutive nodes. Four intervals require five nodes, and the two at the ends are excluded.

$$
n_{\mathrm{int}}=4+1-2=3.
$$
