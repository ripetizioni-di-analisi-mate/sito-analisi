An ideal string fixed at both ends has fundamental frequency $20\,\mathrm{Hz}$. Which frequency does not correspond to one of its standing modes?

- $40\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $50\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $60\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $80\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

To satisfy both fixed ends, an integer number of half wavelengths must fit. The ratio to the fundamental must therefore be an integer.

$$
\frac{50}{20}=2.5.
$$
