The wave $y(x,t)=A\cos(\pi x+4\pi t)$, with $A>0$, uses $x$ in meters and $t$ in seconds. At $t=0.25\,\mathrm{s}$, where is the crest that was initially at $x=4\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The initial crest has phase $4\pi$. The time term increases by $\pi$ and the spatial term must decrease by the same amount, so the crest moves one meter to the left.

$$
\pi x+4\pi\cdot0.25=4\pi\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad x=3\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
