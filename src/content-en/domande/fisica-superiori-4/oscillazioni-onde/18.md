Two harmonic oscillations have period $0.80\,\mathrm{s}$. A maximum of one occurs $0.20\,\mathrm{s}$ after the corresponding maximum of the other. What is the smaller phase difference in magnitude?

- $\pi\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $2\pi\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}\,\mathrm{rad}$

## Explanation

One period corresponds to $2\pi$ radians. A delay of one quarter period covers one quarter of that angle.

$$
|\Delta\varphi|=2\pi\frac{0.20}{0.80}\,\mathrm{rad}=\frac{\pi}{2}\,\mathrm{rad}.
$$
