The first and fourth consecutive nodes of a standing wave are $0.90\,\mathrm{m}$ apart. What is the wavelength?

- $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.90\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.45\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.60\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Four nodes enclose three intervals. The spacing between nodes is therefore $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$ and equals half a wavelength.

$$
\lambda=2\frac{0.90}{3}\,\mathrm{m}=0.60\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
