A $0.54\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass oscillates on a frictionless surface with an ideal $180\,\mathrm{N/m}$ spring. Which value is closest to the period?

- $0.0548\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.688\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2.91\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.344\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The spring force is proportional to displacement with the opposite direction, so the motion is harmonic with angular frequency equal to the square root of $\dfrac{k}{m}$. A cycle requires a phase change of $2\pi$ radians.

$$
T=2\pi\sqrt{\frac{m}{k}}=2\pi\sqrt{\frac{0.54}{180}}\,\mathrm{s}\simeq0.344\,\mathrm{s}.
$$
