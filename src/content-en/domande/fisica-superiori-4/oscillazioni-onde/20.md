For an ideal spring oscillator, what are the restoring force and the speed during an equilibrium crossing?

- Maximum force magnitude and zero speed
- Both zero
- Both maximum in magnitude
- Zero force and maximum speed

## Explanation

Displacement from equilibrium is zero, so the restoring force is zero. At that point all the energy is kinetic and the speed is greatest.

$$
F=-kx=0,\qquad |v|=\omega A.
$$
