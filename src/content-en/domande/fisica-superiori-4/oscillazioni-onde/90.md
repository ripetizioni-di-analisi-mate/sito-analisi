For a string fixed at both ends, the vibrating length doubles and tension quadruples at unchanged linear density. What is the ratio of the new fundamental frequency to the old one?

- $1$
- $2$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $4$

## Explanation

Quadrupling the tension doubles wave speed through the square root. The fundamental wavelength also doubles because the string is twice as long. Their ratio stays the same.

$$
\frac{f'_1}{f_1}=\frac{2u}{u}\frac{L}{2L}=1.
$$
