An internal point on a string in a single standing mode has zero displacement and nonzero transverse velocity. What can be concluded?

- It is a node because its displacement is zero
- It must be an antinode
- It is crossing equilibrium and is not a node
- Its amplitude is zero

## Explanation

At a node the spatial factor is zero, so displacement and velocity both vanish at all times. Another point can have zero displacement at one instant simply by passing through equilibrium.
