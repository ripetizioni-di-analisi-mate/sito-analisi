On the same string fixed at both ends, what is the wavelength ratio $\dfrac{\lambda_2}{\lambda_5}$ of the second and fifth harmonics?

- $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- $\dfrac{2}{5}$
- $5$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$

## Explanation

The fifth harmonic fits more half wavelengths into the same space. Its wavelength is therefore shorter, and the requested ratio is greater than one.

$$
\frac{\lambda_2}{\lambda_5}=\frac{2L/2}{2L/5}=\frac{5}{2}.
$$
