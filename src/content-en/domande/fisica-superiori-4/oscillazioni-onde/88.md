Which expression results from adding $A\sin(kx-\omega t)$ and $A\sin(kx+\omega t)$?

- $2A\sin(kx)\cos(\omega t)$
- $2A\cos(kx)\sin(\omega t)$
- $2A\sin(kx+\omega t)$
- $2A\cos(kx-\omega t)$

## Explanation

Expanding the two sines cancels the terms with the sine of $\omega t$. The terms with the cosine of $\omega t$ add, so the result separates the spatial and time dependence.

$$
\sin(a-b)+\sin(a+b)=2\sin a\cos b.
$$
