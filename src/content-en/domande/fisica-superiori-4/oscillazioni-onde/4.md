An ideal spring oscillator with $k=192\,\mathrm{N/m}$ has amplitude $0.045\,\mathrm{m}$. Which value is closest to the total mechanical energy?

- $0.389\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0.194\,\mathrm{J}$
- $4.32\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0.0972\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

At a turning point the mass is momentarily at rest, so its total energy equals the spring potential energy. The amplitude is squared, giving the final unit $\mathrm{N\,m}=\mathrm{J}$.

$$
E=\frac{kA^2}{2}=\frac{192}{2}\cdot0.045^2\,\mathrm{J}\simeq0.194\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
