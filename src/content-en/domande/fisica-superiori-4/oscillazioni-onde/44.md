An oscillator completes $90$ cycles per minute. What is its angular frequency in radians per second?

- $1.5\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $90\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $3\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

First convert the count to a one-second interval by dividing by sixty. Each cycle then contributes $2\pi$ radians.

$$
f=\frac{90}{60}\,\mathrm{Hz}=1.5\,\mathrm{Hz},\qquad \omega=2\pi f=3\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}.
$$
