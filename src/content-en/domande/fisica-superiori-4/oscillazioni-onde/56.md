A wave is described by $y(x,t)=A\cos(2\pi x-4\pi t)$ in SI units. When does the crest starting at $x=0$ reach $x=3\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $0.50\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3.0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{s}$
- $6.0\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Track the requested crest by keeping its initial phase at zero. At $x=3$ the spatial contribution is $6\pi$, so the time term must subtract $6\pi$.

$$
2\pi\cdot3-4\pi t=0\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad t=1.5\,\mathrm{s}.
$$
