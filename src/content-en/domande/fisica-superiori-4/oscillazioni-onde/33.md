Two spring oscillators have the same energy. The second spring's spring constant is four times the first's. How are the amplitudes related?

- $A_2=\dfrac{A_1}{4}$
- $A_2=2A_1$
- $A_2=\dfrac{A_1}{2}$
- $A_2=A_1$

## Explanation

At the turning points both energies are entirely spring potential energy. If the spring constant quadruples, the squared amplitude must fall to one quarter to keep the same energy.

$$
k_1A_1^2=4k_1A_2^2\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad A_2=\frac{A_1}{2}.
$$
