At some instant, the kinetic energy of an ideal spring oscillator is $36\%$ of its total energy. What is the magnitude of displacement in terms of $A$?

- $0.64A$
- $0.80A$
- $0.60A$
- $0.36A$

## Explanation

Subtract the kinetic fraction from the total. The remaining $64\%$ is the spring-energy fraction, which equals the ratio of the squared displacements.

$$
\frac{U}{E}=1-0.36=0.64=\frac{x^2}{A^2}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad |x|=0.80A.
$$
