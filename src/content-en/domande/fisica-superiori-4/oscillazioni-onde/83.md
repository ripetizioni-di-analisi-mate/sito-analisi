On the same string in a single standing mode, one point is at an antinode and a second point between it and a nearby node oscillates with smaller amplitude. Which quantity is the same at both points?

- The greatest displacement magnitude
- The maximum speed
- The period
- The maximum acceleration magnitude

## Explanation

The spatial factor changes amplitude with position. Both points have the same time factor, which repeats the motion after the same period.

$$
y(x,t)=2A\sin(kx)\cos(\omega t).
$$
