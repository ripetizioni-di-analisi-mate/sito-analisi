An oscillator with $m=2\,\mathrm{kg}$, $k=20\,\mathrm{N/m}$, and total energy $1.6\,\mathrm{J}$ passes through $x=+0.20\,\mathrm{m}$ moving toward equilibrium. What is its velocity?

- $-\sqrt{1.2}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $+\sqrt{1.2}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-1.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-\sqrt{1.6}\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Subtract the spring energy at that position from the total. The sign is negative because the coordinate decreases toward equilibrium from the positive side.

$$
K=1.6\,\mathrm{J}-\frac{20}{2}\cdot0.20^2\,\mathrm{J}=1.2\,\mathrm{J},\qquad v=-\sqrt{\frac{2K}{m}}=-\sqrt{1.2}\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
