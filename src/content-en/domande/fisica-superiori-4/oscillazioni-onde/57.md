A $5\,\mathrm{Hz}$ source generates a wave traveling at $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is its wavenumber?

- $10\pi\,\mathrm{rad/m}$
- $0.80\pi\,\mathrm{rad/m}$
- $2.5\pi\,\mathrm{rad/m}$
- $5\pi\,\mathrm{rad/m}$

## Explanation

A crest keeps the same phase, so each second the spatial contribution $ku$ must offset the angular frequency. Divide angular frequency by wave speed.

$$
k=\frac{\omega}{u}=\frac{2\pi\cdot5}{4.0}\,\mathrm{rad/m}=2.5\pi\,\mathrm{rad/m}.
$$
