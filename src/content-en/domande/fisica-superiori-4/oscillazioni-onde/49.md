A pulse passes two sensors $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$ apart along a string. It travels at $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the delay of the second signal?

- $0.20\,\mathrm{s}$
- $5.0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3.2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.40\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The sensors detect the same pulse at different times. Divide the distance traveled by the pulse by its wave speed.

$$
\Delta t=\frac{\Delta x}{u}=\frac{0.80}{4.0}\,\mathrm{s}=0.20\,\mathrm{s}.
$$
