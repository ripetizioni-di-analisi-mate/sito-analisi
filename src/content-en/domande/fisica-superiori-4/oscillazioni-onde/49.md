A pulse travels along a uniform taut string at $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. How long does it take to go from a point $A$ to a point $B$ of the string $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$ away from $A$?

- $0.20\,\mathrm{s}$
- $5.0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3.2\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.40\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

On a uniform taut string the pulse travels at constant speed, so it covers equal distances in equal times. The time taken is therefore obtained by dividing the distance between $A$ and $B$ by the wave speed.

$$
\Delta t=\frac{\Delta x}{u}=\frac{0.80}{4.0}\,\mathrm{s}=0.20\,\mathrm{s}.
$$
