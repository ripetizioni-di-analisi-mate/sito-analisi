A harmonic oscillator with angular frequency $4\,\mathrm{rad/s}$ is at $x=-0.050\,\mathrm{m}$. Find its signed acceleration.

- $-0.80\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $+0.20\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $-0.20\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$
- $+0.80\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$

## Explanation

Acceleration points toward equilibrium. From a point with a negative coordinate, this is the positive direction along the axis.

$$
a=-\omega^2x=-4^2(-0.050)\,\mathrm{m/s^2}=+0.80\,\mathrm{m/s^2}.
$$
