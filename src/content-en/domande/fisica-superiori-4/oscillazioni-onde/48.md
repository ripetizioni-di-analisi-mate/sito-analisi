A harmonic wave travels along a string. At a given instant two successive crests are $1.2\,\mathrm{m}$ apart, and a crest passes a fixed point of the string every $0.30\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the wave speed?

- $0.25\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.36\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The distance between two successive crests at a given instant is the wavelength, so $\lambda=1.2\,\mathrm{m}$. The interval between two crests passing the same point is the period, so $T=0.30\,\mathrm{s}$. In one period a crest advances by one wavelength, so the wave speed is the ratio of the two quantities.

$$
u=\frac{\lambda}{T}=\frac{1.2}{0.30}\,\mathrm{m/s}=4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
