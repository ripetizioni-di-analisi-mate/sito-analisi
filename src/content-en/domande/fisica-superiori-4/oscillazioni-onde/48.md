A wave snapshot shows successive crests $1.2\,\mathrm{m}$ apart. A fixed sensor records maxima every $0.30\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the wave speed?

- $0.25\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.36\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $8.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The snapshot gives the wavelength, and the sensor gives the period. A crest travels that distance in that time.

$$
u=\frac{\lambda}{T}=\frac{1.2}{0.30}\,\mathrm{m/s}=4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
