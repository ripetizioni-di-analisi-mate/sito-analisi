In the position–time graph, at which point does the body pass through equilibrium with negative velocity?

::grafico{f="cos(pi*x/2)" xmin=-0.2 xmax=4.3 ymin=-1.4 ymax=1.4 nomi="t,x/A" punti="0,1,pieno,A;1,0,pieno,B;2,-1,pieno,C;3,0,pieno,D" titolo="Normalized position versus time. Time in seconds."}

- $A$
- $C$
- $D$
- $B$

## Explanation

Velocity has the sign of the slope. At $B$ the vertical coordinate is zero and the curve descends. At $D$ the coordinate is again zero, but the curve rises.
