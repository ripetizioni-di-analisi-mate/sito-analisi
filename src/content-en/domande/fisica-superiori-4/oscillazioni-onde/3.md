A harmonic oscillator has amplitude $0.042\,\mathrm{m}$ and frequency $2.5\,\mathrm{Hz}$. Find its maximum speed. Choose the closest value.

- $0.105\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1.32\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.66\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.330\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

When the mass crosses equilibrium, the sine of the phase has magnitude one, so the speed is greatest. The factor $2\pi$ converts cycles per second to radians per second.

$$
v_{\max}=2\pi fA=2\pi\cdot2.5\cdot0.042\,\mathrm{m/s}\simeq0.66\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
