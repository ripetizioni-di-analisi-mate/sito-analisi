A harmonic oscillator passes through $x<0$ with velocity $v<0$. How is its speed changing at that instant?

- It increases
- It is constant
- It is already zero
- It decreases

## Explanation

Velocity points toward more negative coordinates, while acceleration points toward equilibrium. Because the directions are opposite, speed decreases.

$$
x<0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad a=-\omega^2x>0.
$$
