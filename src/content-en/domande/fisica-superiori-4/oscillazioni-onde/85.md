In harmonic motion, $a=+0.80\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ is measured at $x=-0.20\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the angular frequency?

- $4\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $0.50\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $2\,\mathrm{rad/s}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

## Explanation

Acceleration and displacement have opposite signs. Their ratio with the sign reversed is therefore positive and gives the square of the angular frequency.

$$
\omega=\sqrt{-\frac{a}{x}}=\sqrt{-\frac{0.80}{-0.20}}\,\mathrm{rad/s}=2\,\mathrm{rad/s}.
$$
