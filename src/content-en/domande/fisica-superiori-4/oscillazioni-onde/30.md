An ideal oscillator with $k=100\,\mathrm{N/m}$ has $0.50\,\mathrm{J}$ of total energy. What is its amplitude?

- $0.010\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.050\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.20\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

At a turning point all the energy is spring potential energy. Isolate amplitude squared and take the positive square root because amplitude is a distance.

$$
A=\sqrt{\frac{2E}{k}}=\sqrt{\frac{2\cdot0.50}{100}}\,\mathrm{m}=0.10\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
