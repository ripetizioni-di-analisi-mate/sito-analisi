Two sinusoidal waves of the same frequency produce oscillations of equal amplitude $A>0$ at one point. Which phase difference gives zero total displacement there at all times?

- $\pi\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $0\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}\,\mathrm{rad}$

## Explanation

Every displacement from one wave must be matched by an opposite displacement from the other. For cosines, a half-turn phase change reverses the sign.

$$
A\cos(\omega t)+A\cos(\omega t+\pi)=0.
$$
