The wave $y(x,t)=A\cos(5\pi x-20\pi t)$ travels along a string. What is the velocity of the profile if $x$ is in meters and $t$ is in seconds?

- $-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $+0.25\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $+4\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-0.25\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Angular frequency divided by wavenumber gives the magnitude. The sign is positive because the phase changes cancel only with a positive displacement as time advances.

$$
5\pi\Delta x=20\pi\Delta t\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad \frac{\Delta x}{\Delta t}=+4\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
