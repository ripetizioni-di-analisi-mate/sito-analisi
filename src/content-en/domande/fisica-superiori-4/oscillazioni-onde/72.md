On a string, successive nodes of a standing wave are $0.60\,\mathrm{m}$ apart. How far is a node from its nearest antinode?

- $0.60\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.15\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The antinode lies halfway between successive nodes. The node–antinode spacing is one quarter of a wavelength, half the distance between nodes.

$$
d=\frac{0.60}{2}\,\mathrm{m}=0.30\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
