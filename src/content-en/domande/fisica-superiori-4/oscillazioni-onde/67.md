Tension changes from $F_T$ to $9F_T$ and linear density from $\mu$ to $9\mu$. How does the wave speed on an ideal string change?

- $u_2=3u_1$
- $u_2=9u_1$
- $u_2=u_1$
- $u_2=\dfrac{u_1}{3}$

## Explanation

Tension and linear density enter the same ratio. Multiplying both by nine leaves the ratio and its square root unchanged.

$$
\frac{u_2}{u_1}=\sqrt{\frac{9F_T}{9\mu}\frac{\mu}{F_T}}=1.
$$
