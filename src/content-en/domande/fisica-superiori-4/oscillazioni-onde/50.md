A $10\,\mathrm{Hz}$ sinusoidal wave arrives from a string where it travels at $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and is transmitted to a string where it travels at $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the transmitted wavelength?

- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The points of the two segments at the junction oscillate at the source frequency. Use the new speed while keeping the frequency at $10\,\mathrm{Hz}$.

$$
\lambda_2=\frac{u_2}{f}=\frac{30}{10}\,\mathrm{m}=3\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
