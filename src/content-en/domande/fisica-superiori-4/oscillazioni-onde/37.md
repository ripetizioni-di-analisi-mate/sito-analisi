Two ideal pendulums of the same length oscillate at the same location with small angles. One mass is three times the other. How do their frequencies compare?

- The heavier mass has three times the frequency
- The heavier mass has one third of the frequency
- The heavier mass has nine times the frequency
- They are equal

## Explanation

Tangential force increases in proportion to mass, but we divide by that same mass to find acceleration. The frequencies agree because length and gravity agree.

$$
f=\frac{1}{2\pi}\sqrt{\frac{g}{\ell}}.
$$
