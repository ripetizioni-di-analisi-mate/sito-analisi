When a crest of a transverse wave passes a point on a string, what is the point's instantaneous transverse velocity?

- It is zero
- Its magnitude is greatest
- It equals the wave velocity
- It equals the point's velocity at equilibrium

## Explanation

At that instant the material point is at its maximum transverse displacement. In that part of its oscillation it reverses direction, and its local velocity is zero.
