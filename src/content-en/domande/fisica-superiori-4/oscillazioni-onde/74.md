A string fixed at both ends is driven by a small transverse force applied exactly at its midpoint. Which normal mode has a node there and receives no work from this force?

- The second harmonic.
- The first harmonic.
- The third harmonic.
- The fifth harmonic.

## Explanation

The second harmonic has two antinodes separated by a central node. The point where the force acts does not move in that mode. Each listed odd harmonic has an antinode at the centre.
