A $2\,\mathrm{m}$ string is fixed at both ends. Waves travel along the string at $16\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is its fundamental frequency?

- $4\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $8\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $16\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $2\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

In the fundamental, a single loop joins the fixed ends. The string holds half a wavelength, so divide wave speed by twice the string length.

$$
f_1=\frac{u}{2L}=\frac{16}{4}\,\mathrm{Hz}=4\,\mathrm{Hz}.
$$
