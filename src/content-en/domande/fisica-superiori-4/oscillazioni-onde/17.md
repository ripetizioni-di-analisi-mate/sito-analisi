In harmonic motion, the time from a positive maximum to the next minimum is $0.75\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the frequency?

- $\dfrac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $\dfrac{1}{3}\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

The two turning points are half a cycle apart. A complete period also includes the return to the maximum.

$$
T=2\cdot0.75\,\mathrm{s}=1.50\,\mathrm{s},\qquad f=\frac{1}{T}=\frac{2}{3}\,\mathrm{Hz}.
$$
