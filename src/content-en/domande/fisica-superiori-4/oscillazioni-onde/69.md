A uniform string with mass $24\,\mathrm{g}$ and length $2\,\mathrm{m}$ must carry waves at $50\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What tension is required?

- $60\,\mathrm{N}$
- $30\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0.60\,\mathrm{N}$
- $30000\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

First divide the total mass in kilograms by two meters. Multiply the resulting linear density by the wave speed squared.

$$
\mu=\frac{0.024}{2}\,\mathrm{kg/m}=0.012\,\mathrm{kg/m},\qquad F_T=\mu u^2=30\,\mathrm{N}.
$$
