For a wave with wavelength $6\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the least positive distance between two points $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ out of phase?

- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.75\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

A phase change of a quarter turn corresponds to one quarter of the distance between successive crests. This is the first positive distance that gives that phase difference in magnitude.

$$
\Delta x=\frac{\pi/2}{2\pi}\lambda=\frac{6}{4}\,\mathrm{m}=1.5\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
