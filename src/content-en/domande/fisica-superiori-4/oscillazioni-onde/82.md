In $0\leq x\leq L$ the string oscillates as $y=2A\sin\!\left(\dfrac{3\pi x}{L}\right)\cos(\omega t)$, with $A>0$. Where are the internal nodes?

- $\dfrac{L}{6},\ \dfrac{5L}{6}$
- $\dfrac{L}{3},\ \dfrac{2L}{3}$
- $\dfrac{L}{2},\ \dfrac{2L}{3}$
- $0,\ L$

## Explanation

A node requires zero displacement at every time. Find the zeros of the spatial sine and exclude those at the two ends.

$$
\frac{3\pi x}{L}=\pi,\ 2\pi\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad x=\frac{L}{3},\ \frac{2L}{3}.
$$
