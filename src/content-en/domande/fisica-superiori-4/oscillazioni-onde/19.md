For $x(t)=A\cos(\omega t+\pi)$ with $A>0$ and $\omega>0$, what is the state at $t=0$?

- $x=A,\ v=0$
- $x=0,\ v>0$
- $x=-A,\ v=0$
- $x=0,\ v<0$

## Explanation

The cosine of the initial phase equals negative one, so the body starts at the negative turning point. The sine is zero, so the body is momentarily at rest.

$$
x(0)=A\cos\pi=-A,\qquad v(0)=-\omega A\sin\pi=0.
$$
