In a snapshot of the wave, the first marked crest is at $x=1\,\mathrm{m}$ and the fourth at $x=7\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the wavelength?

::grafico{f="cos(pi*(x-1))" xmin=0 xmax=7.6 ymin=-1.4 ymax=1.4 nomi="x,y/A" punti="1,1,pieno;3,1,pieno;5,1,pieno;7,1,pieno" titolo="Snapshot of the wave with four marked crests. Horizontal coordinates in meters."}

- $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The horizontal axis measures distance in space. Four successive crests enclose three intervals, each one wavelength long.

$$
\lambda=\frac{7-1}{4-1}\,\mathrm{m}=2\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
