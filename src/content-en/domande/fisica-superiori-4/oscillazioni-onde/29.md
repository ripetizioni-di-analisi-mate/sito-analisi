An ideal $40\,\mathrm{N/m}$ spring is displaced by $x=-0.30\,\mathrm{m}$ from equilibrium. What is the spring energy, taking it as zero at equilibrium?

- $-1.8\,\mathrm{J}$
- $3.6\,\mathrm{J}$
- $6.0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1.8\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The displacement is negative, but spring energy depends on its square. Compression and extension of the same magnitude store the same energy.

$$
U=\frac{1}{2}\cdot40\cdot(-0.30)^2\,\mathrm{J}=1.8\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
