An oscillator completes $43$ oscillations in $12\,\mathrm{s}$. Find its frequency. Choose the closest value.

- $0.279\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $7.17\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $3.58\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $5.42\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

Frequency is the number of cycles per second, so time goes in the denominator. Dividing the time by the cycle count would give the period in seconds.

$$
f=\frac{43}{12}\,\mathrm{Hz}\simeq3.58\,\mathrm{Hz}.
$$
