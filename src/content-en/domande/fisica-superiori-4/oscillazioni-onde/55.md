For $y(x,t)=0.04\cos(\pi x-2\pi t)$ in SI units, what is the displacement of the point $x=1\,\mathrm{m}$ at $t=0.25\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $+0.04\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-0.04\,\mathrm{m}$
- $+0.02\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Fix both the requested point and time. The spatial contribution is $\pi$ and the temporal contribution is $-\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, so the cosine of the phase is zero.

$$
y(1,0.25)=0.04\cos\left(\pi-\frac{\pi}{2}\right)\,\mathrm{m}=0\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
