At one point the total displacement in millimeters is $y(t)=3\cos(\omega t)+4\sin(\omega t)$. What is the amplitude of the sum?

- $7\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $5\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $1\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $12\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

Write the resultant as $A\cos(\omega t-\varphi)$. Sine and cosine peak at different times. Squaring and adding the coefficients eliminates the phase and gives the amplitude.

$$
A\cos\varphi=3\,\mathrm{mm},\qquad A\sin\varphi=4\,\mathrm{mm},\qquad A=\sqrt{3^2+4^2}\,\mathrm{mm}=5\,\mathrm{mm}.
$$
