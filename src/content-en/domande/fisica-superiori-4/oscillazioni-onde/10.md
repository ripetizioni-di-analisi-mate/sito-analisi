An oscillator with mass $0.45\,\mathrm{kg}$ has angular frequency $10\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. What is the spring constant of the ideal spring?

- $4.5\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $90\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $45\,\mathrm{N/m}$
- $22.5\,\mathrm{N/m}$

## Explanation

The angular frequency squared is the ratio of spring constant to mass. Multiply the mass by the square of $10$.

$$
k=m\omega^2=0.45\cdot10^2\,\mathrm{N/m}=45\,\mathrm{N/m}.
$$
