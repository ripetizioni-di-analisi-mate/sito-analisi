The first maximum occurs at $t=2\,\mathrm{s}$ and the sixth at $t=5\,\mathrm{s}$. Find the period.

- $0.50\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.75\,\mathrm{s}$
- $1.20\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.60\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The elapsed time from the first maximum to the sixth is three seconds. The maxima mark the ends of the intervals, so there are five complete intervals.

$$
T=\frac{5-2}{6-1}\,\mathrm{s}=\frac{3}{5}\,\mathrm{s}=0.60\,\mathrm{s}.
$$
