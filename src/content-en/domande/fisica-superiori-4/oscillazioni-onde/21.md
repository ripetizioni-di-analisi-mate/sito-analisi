A body follows $x(t)=0.12\cos(5\pi t)$ in SI units. When does it first cross equilibrium after $t=0$?

- $0.20\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.30\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.10\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.40\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

From the positive turning point, the first zero displacement corresponds to the first positive zero of cosine. This is one quarter of a period.

$$
5\pi t=\frac{\pi}{2}\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad t=0.10\,\mathrm{s}.
$$
