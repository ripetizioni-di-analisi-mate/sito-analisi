A mass oscillates on the same ideal spring first horizontally without friction and then vertically. Measuring displacement from the respective equilibrium position, what does gravity change?

- The static extension of the spring
- The angular frequency
- The spring constant
- The period

## Explanation

In the vertical spring, the static extension balances the weight. Measuring from that equilibrium leaves a restoring force proportional to $x$ with the same $k$.

$$
k\Delta\ell=mg,\qquad \omega=\sqrt{\frac{k}{m}}.
$$
