For $y(x,t)=A\cos(3x+2t+\varphi)$, with $A>0$ and SI units, which description of propagation is correct?

- Toward increasing $x$
- Perpendicular to $x$
- Toward decreasing $x$
- The profile is stationary

## Explanation

When following a crest, its phase remains constant. A positive $\Delta t$ gives a decrease in $x$, even if the initial phase is nonzero.

$$
3\Delta x+2\Delta t=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\Delta x=-\frac{2}{3}\Delta t.
$$
