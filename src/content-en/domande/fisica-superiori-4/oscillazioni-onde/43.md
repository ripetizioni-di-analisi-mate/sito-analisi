A small-angle pendulum is taken to a place with gravity $g_2=2g_1$. What new length keeps its period unchanged?

- $2\ell_1$
- $\ell_1$
- $\dfrac{\ell_1}{2}$
- $4\ell_1$

## Explanation

To keep the same period, hold the length-to-gravity ratio under the square root constant. Doubling the denominator requires doubling the numerator.

$$
\frac{\ell_2}{g_2}=\frac{\ell_1}{g_1}\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad \ell_2=2\ell_1.
$$
