A periodic wave travels at $28.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ with frequency $6\,\mathrm{Hz}$. What is its wavelength?

- $4.8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $9.6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $172.8\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The distance a crest travels between two successive passes through a point is the wave speed times the period. Period is the reciprocal of frequency.

$$
\lambda=\frac{u}{f}=\frac{28.8}{6}\,\mathrm{m}=4.8\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
