A simple pendulum has a small-angle period of $2.0\,\mathrm{s}$ where the gravitational acceleration is $g_1$. At the same length, find the period if $g_2=\dfrac{g_1}{4}$.

- $1.0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $8.0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.50\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Length cancels in the ratio of periods. Gravitational acceleration is in the denominator under the square root, so dividing it by four doubles the period.

$$
T_2=T_1\sqrt{\frac{g_1}{g_2}}=2.0\sqrt{4}\,\mathrm{s}=4.0\,\mathrm{s}.
$$
