A $2\,\mathrm{kW}$ appliance runs for $45\,\mathrm{min}$. Energy alone costs $0.30$ euros per kilowatt-hour. What does the energy used cost?

- 0.60 euros
- 0.30 euros
- 0.45 euros
- 1.50 euros

## Explanation

The time is three quarters of an hour. First find energy in kilowatt-hours, then apply the unit price to that energy.

$$
E=2\cdot0.75\,\mathrm{kWh}=1.50\,\mathrm{kWh},\qquad 1.50\cdot0.30=0.45.
$$
