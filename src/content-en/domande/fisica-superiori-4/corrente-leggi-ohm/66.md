A $16\,\Omega$ resistor can dissipate at most $4\,\mathrm{W}$. What is the maximum current magnitude?

- $0.25\,\mathrm{A}$
- $2\,\mathrm{A}$
- $0.50\,\mathrm{A}$
- $4\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

At constant resistance, power grows with the square of current magnitude. Use equality at maximum power to find the limiting current.

$$
I_{\max}=\sqrt{\frac{P_{\max}}{R}}=\sqrt{\frac{4}{16}}\,\mathrm{A}=0.50\,\mathrm{A}.
$$
