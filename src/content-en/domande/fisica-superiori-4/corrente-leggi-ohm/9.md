A constant current carries $30\,\mathrm{C}$ in $2.5\,\mathrm{min}$. Find the current.

- $0.20\,\mathrm{A}$
- $12\,\mathrm{A}$
- $5\,\mathrm{A}$
- $0.50\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

An ampere counts coulombs per second. The time in the denominator must therefore be in seconds.

$$
I=\frac{30}{2.5\cdot60}\,\mathrm{A}=0.20\,\mathrm{A}.
$$
