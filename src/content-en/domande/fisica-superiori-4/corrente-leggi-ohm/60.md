For a passive metal wire, a linear model extrapolated far beyond the measured range gives $R=-2\,\Omega$. Which interpretation is correct?

- The wire becomes an ideal source
- The current is necessarily zero
- Temperature differences must use Celsius instead of kelvin
- The extrapolation beyond the valid range does not describe the wire

## Explanation

Negative resistance from this extrapolation contradicts the physical model of a passive wire. It shows that the linear approximation cannot be extended that far.
