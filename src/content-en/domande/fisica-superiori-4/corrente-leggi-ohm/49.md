For a wire with $\rho=2\cdot10^{-8}\,\Omega\,\mathrm{m}$ and $A=10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^2}$, what length gives $R=0.40\,\Omega$?

- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $80\,\mathrm{m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.050\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Material and cross section fix resistance per unit length. Multiply $R$ by area and divide by resistivity to find length.

$$
L=\frac{RA}{\rho}=\frac{0.40\cdot10^{-6}}{2\cdot10^{-8}}\,\mathrm{m}=20\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
