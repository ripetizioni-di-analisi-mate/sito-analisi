Two resistors of $6\,\Omega$ and $18\,\Omega$ each carry the same current, separately. What is the ratio of the second's voltage to the first's?

- $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- $1$
- $3$
- $9$

## Explanation

At the same current, voltage drop grows in proportion to resistance. The current factor cancels in the ratio.

$$
\frac{V_2}{V_1}=\frac{18I}{6I}=3.
$$
