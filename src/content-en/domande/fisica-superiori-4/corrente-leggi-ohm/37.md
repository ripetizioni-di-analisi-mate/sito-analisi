For a $6\,\Omega$ ohmic resistor, the signed voltage changes from $+12\,\mathrm{V}$ to $-12\,\mathrm{V}$ with the same references. What is the final current?

- $+2\,\mathrm{A}$
- $-72\,\mathrm{A}$
- $0\,\mathrm{A}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

The resistor is described by the same positive constant $R$. Reversing voltage reverses current without changing its magnitude.

$$
I=\frac{-12}{6}\,\mathrm{A}=-2\,\mathrm{A}.
$$
