An ideal source powers a resistor at steady state. Which statement distinguishes the role of charge from the role of energy?

- Net charge entering and leaving the resistor is equal, while energy is transferred
- Charge is consumed and all energy stays in the source
- The resistor creates charge and gives energy back to the source
- Charge accumulates indefinitely in the resistor

## Explanation

Incoming and outgoing current agree at steady state. The voltage drop tells how much energy is transferred to the resistor per coulomb.
