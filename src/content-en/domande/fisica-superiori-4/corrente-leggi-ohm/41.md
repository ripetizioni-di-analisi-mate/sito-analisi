For the two data points $(I,V)=(1,2)$ and $(1.5,6)$ of a non-ohmic characteristic, what is the secant slope in volts per ampere?

- $8\,\mathrm{V/A}$
- $4\,\mathrm{V/A}$
- $2\,\mathrm{V/A}$
- $0.125\,\mathrm{V/A}$

## Explanation

The secant joins the two points, so subtract the vertical and horizontal coordinates before dividing. The ratio of the coordinates at the second point alone would give four.

$$
\frac{\Delta V}{\Delta I}=\frac{6-2}{1.5-1}\,\mathrm{V/A}=8\,\mathrm{V/A}.
$$
