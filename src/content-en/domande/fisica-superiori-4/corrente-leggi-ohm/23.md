A metal wire with uniform carrier density has two sections in series. The second has twice the area at the same steady current. How do their drift speeds compare?

- $v_{d,2}=2v_{d,1}$
- $v_{d,2}=\dfrac{v_{d,1}}{2}$
- $v_{d,2}=v_{d,1}$
- $v_{d,2}=\dfrac{v_{d,1}}{4}$

## Explanation

Since density, carrier charge, and current are the same, area times drift speed must stay constant. Twice the cross section carries the same charge per second with half the drift speed.

$$
A_1v_{d,1}=2A_1v_{d,2}.
$$
