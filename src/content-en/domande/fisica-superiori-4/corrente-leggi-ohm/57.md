At $50\,{}^\circ\mathrm{C}$ a wire has $R_0=20\,\Omega$ and $\alpha=0.005\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$. What is $R$ at $10\,{}^\circ\mathrm{C}$ in the linear model?

- $16\,\Omega$
- $24\,\Omega$
- $4\,\Omega$
- $20\,\Omega$

## Explanation

The temperature difference is negative because the wire cools. With a positive coefficient, the term added to one is therefore negative.

$$
R=20[1+0.005(10-50)]\,\Omega=16\,\Omega.
$$
