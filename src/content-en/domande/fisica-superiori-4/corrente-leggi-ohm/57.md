A wire of unchanged material keeps exactly the same length and cross-section. An ohmmeter reads $8\,\Omega$ before heating and $10\,\Omega$ afterwards. What change in resistivity can be inferred without knowing its temperature coefficient?

- It increases by $25\%$.
- It increases by $20\%$.
- It decreases by $20\%$.
- It stays constant.

## Explanation

The length-to-area ratio is unchanged. From $R=\rho\dfrac{L}{A}$ it follows that resistivity and resistance change in the same proportion. The fractional increase relative to the initial value is $\dfrac{10-8}{8}=0.25$.
