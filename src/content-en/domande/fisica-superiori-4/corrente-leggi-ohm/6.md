A $30\,\Omega$ ohmic resistor carries a direct current of $0.40\,\mathrm{A}$. What is the power dissipated?

- $12\,\mathrm{W}$
- $2.4\,\mathrm{W}$
- $4.8\,\mathrm{W}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

The voltage drop is $RI$. Multiplying the drop by current therefore gives current squared.

$$
P=RI^2=30\cdot0.40^2\,\mathrm{W}=4.8\,\mathrm{W}.
$$
