A $4\,\Omega$ resistor carries $1\,\mathrm{A}$ for $3\,\mathrm{s}$ and then $2\,\mathrm{A}$ for $2\,\mathrm{s}$. How much energy does it dissipate?

- $36\,\mathrm{J}$
- $44\,\mathrm{J}$
- $20\,\mathrm{J}$
- $80\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Power is four watts in the first interval and sixteen in the second. Weight each by its duration before adding.

$$
E=4(1^2\cdot3+2^2\cdot2)\,\mathrm{J}=44\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
