A metal wire carries $0.32\,\mathrm{A}$. How many net electrons cross a section in one second, using $e=1.60\cdot10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}$?

- $2\cdot10^{19}$
- $5\cdot10^{18}$
- $2\cdot10^{18}$
- $1\cdot10^{18}$

## Explanation

Current already gives transfer per second. Convert that many coulombs into a carrier count by dividing by elementary charge.

$$
N=\frac{0.32}{1.60\cdot10^{-19}}=2\cdot10^{18}.
$$
