A uniform wire is stretched to three times its length at constant volume and resistivity. How does resistance change?

- $R_2=3R_1$
- $R_2=R_1$
- $R_2=\dfrac{R_1}{3}$
- $R_2=9R_1$

## Explanation

At constant volume, tripling the length reduces area to one third. Both changes raise resistance.

$$
A_2=\frac{A_1}{3},\qquad \frac{R_2}{R_1}=\frac{3L_1}{L_1}\frac{A_1}{\frac{A_1}{3}}=9.
$$
