Current is $+2\,\mathrm{A}$ relative to the left-to-right direction. Only the reference direction is reversed. What is the new reading?

- $+2\,\mathrm{A}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{A}$
- $0\,\mathrm{A}$
- $-1\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

Changing reference direction changes the sign assigned to the transfer across the section. Physical current has the same magnitude and keeps its actual direction.
