A device draws $1.44\,\mathrm{kW}$ for $2.5\,\mathrm{h}$. Find the energy in kilowatt-hours.

- $0.576\,\mathrm{kWh}$
- $1.44\,\mathrm{kWh}$
- $3.6\,\mathrm{kWh}$
- $3600\,\mathrm{kWh}$

## Explanation

Kilowatts and hours are already the required units for the two factors. Their product is the energy the device draws over the whole interval.

$$
E=P\Delta t=1.44\cdot2.5\,\mathrm{kWh}=3.6\,\mathrm{kWh}.
$$
