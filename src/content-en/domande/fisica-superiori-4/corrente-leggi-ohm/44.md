A filament has $V=3\,\mathrm{V}$ and $I=1\,\mathrm{A}$ when cold, and $V=8\,\mathrm{V}$ and $I=2\,\mathrm{A}$ after warming up. Which conclusion is justified?

- Resistance is 3 Ω in both conditions
- Resistance is 5 Ω in both conditions
- The measured ratio rises from 3 Ω to 4 Ω
- Resistance halves

## Explanation

The measurements are at different temperatures. They have different ratios, so a single constant resistance cannot describe both.

$$
\frac{3}{1}\,\Omega=3\,\Omega,\qquad\frac{8}{2}\,\Omega=4\,\Omega.
$$
