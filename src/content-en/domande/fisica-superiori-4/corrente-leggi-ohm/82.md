A device delivers $840\,\mathrm{W}$ of useful power to water for $50\,\mathrm{s}$. With $c=4200\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$, how much water can it heat by $20\,\mathrm{K}$?

- $2.0\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.50\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $1.0\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The stated power already reaches the water. Divide the energy delivered by the heat needed to raise one kilogram by twenty kelvins.

$$
m=\frac{P_{\mathrm{useful}}\Delta t}{c\Delta T}=\frac{840\cdot50}{4200\cdot20}\,\mathrm{kg}=0.50\,\mathrm{kg}.
$$
