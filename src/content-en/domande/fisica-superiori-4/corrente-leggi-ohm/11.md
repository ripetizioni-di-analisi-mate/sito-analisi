An ohmic characteristic passes through $(V,I)=(2\,\mathrm{V},0.20\,\mathrm{A})$ and $(6\,\mathrm{V},0.60\,\mathrm{A})$. Use the slope of $I$ versus $V$ to find resistance.

- $0.10\,\Omega$
- $4\,\Omega$
- $10\,\Omega$
- $20\,\Omega$

## Explanation

Slope measures how much current rises per volt. It is the reciprocal of resistance because current and voltage are on the vertical and horizontal axes, respectively.

$$
p=\frac{0.60-0.20}{6-2}\,\mathrm{A/V}=0.10\,\mathrm{A/V},\qquad R=\frac{1}{p}=10\,\Omega.
$$
