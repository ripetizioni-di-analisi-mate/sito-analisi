A model gives electrons a mean drift speed of $4\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m/s}$. How long would their drift take to cover $2\,\mathrm{m}$?

- $0.0008\,\mathrm{s}$
- $5000\,\mathrm{s}$
- $2500\,\mathrm{s}$
- $500\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Divide the distance by the small drift speed. This long time concerns the average motion of electrons, not propagation of the change in the field.

$$
\Delta t=\frac{2}{4\cdot10^{-4}}\,\mathrm{s}=5000\,\mathrm{s}.
$$
