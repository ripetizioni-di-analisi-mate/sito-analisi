A heater draws $900\,\mathrm{W}$ and transfers $80\%$ of its energy to the water. What is the average power transferred elsewhere?

- $720\,\mathrm{W}$
- $180\,\mathrm{W}$
- $90\,\mathrm{W}$
- $1125\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

The fraction that does not reach the water is one minus the efficiency. It is one fifth of the electrical input power.

$$
P_{\mathrm{other}}=(1-0.80)900\,\mathrm{W}=180\,\mathrm{W}.
$$
