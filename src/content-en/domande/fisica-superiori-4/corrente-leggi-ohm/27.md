A bulb responds nearly immediately to closing a switch, even though electron drift is slow. Which explanation agrees with both observations?

- Electrons travel from the source to the bulb at the signal speed
- The wire carries only positive charge when the circuit closes
- Drift becomes independent of the conductor's properties
- A field change propagates and acts on carriers already present

## Explanation

Electrons near the bulb begin to drift when the field change reaches them. Propagation time for the change differs from an individual electron's drift time.
