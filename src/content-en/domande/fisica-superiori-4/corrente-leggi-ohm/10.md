A wire with $n=8\cdot10^{28}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}}$ and $A=1\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ carries $1.28\,\mathrm{A}$. With $e=1.60\cdot10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}$, find the drift speed.

- $0.0000001\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.001\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.0002\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $0.0001\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Density is per cubic meter, so the area in square millimeters must be converted to square meters. Then divide the current by carrier charge per unit length.

$$
v_d=\frac{1.28}{8\cdot10^{28}\cdot1.60\cdot10^{-19}\cdot10^{-6}}\,\mathrm{m/s}=10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
