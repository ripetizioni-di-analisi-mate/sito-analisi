An ohmic resistor draws $0.40\,\mathrm{A}$ at $2\,\mathrm{V}$. What current flows at $6\,\mathrm{V}$ at the same temperature?

- $0.40\,\mathrm{A}$
- $0.133\,\mathrm{A}$
- $1.20\,\mathrm{A}$
- $3.60\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

Resistance stays constant under the stated conditions, so current grows by the same factor as voltage.

$$
I_2=I_1\frac{V_2}{V_1}=0.40\frac{6}{2}\,\mathrm{A}=1.20\,\mathrm{A}.
$$
