A charge of magnitude $0.576\,\mathrm{C}$ crosses a section. What is the net number of electrons, using $e=1.60\cdot10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}$?

- $3.6\cdot10^{18}$
- $9.216\cdot10^{18}$
- $3.6\cdot10^{19}$
- $1.8\cdot10^{18}$

## Explanation

Each electron contributes the same charge magnitude. The net count is total charge divided by the charge magnitude of one carrier.

$$
N=\frac{|\Delta q|}{e}=\frac{0.576}{1.60\cdot10^{-19}}=3.6\cdot10^{18}.
$$
