A wire measures $10\,\Omega$ at $20\,{}^\circ\mathrm{C}$ with $\alpha=0.004\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$. At what temperature does it reach $11\,\Omega$ in the linear model?

- $25^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $30^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $75^\circ\mathrm{C}$
- $45^\circ\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

The rise from ten to eleven ohms is a relative increase of one tenth. First find the temperature increase and then add it to the reference temperature.

$$
\Delta T=\frac{11/10-1}{0.004}\,\mathrm{K}=25\,\mathrm{K},\qquad T=45\,{}^\circ\mathrm{C}.
$$
