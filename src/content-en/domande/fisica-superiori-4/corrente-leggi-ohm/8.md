A $1200\,\mathrm{W}$ heater transfers a fraction $0.80$ of its electric energy to water. Find the time to heat $1.08\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water by $25\,\mathrm{K}$ with $c=4180\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$. Choose the closest value.

- $118\,\mathrm{s}$
- $94.1\,\mathrm{s}$
- $147\,\mathrm{s}$
- $59\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Divide the required heat by the power that reaches the water. Energy that the device dissipates elsewhere does not heat the stated mass.

$$
\Delta t=\frac{mc\Delta T}{\eta P}=\frac{1.08\cdot4180\cdot25}{0.80\cdot1200}\,\mathrm{s}\simeq118\,\mathrm{s}.
$$
