At fixed voltage, a hot wire's resistance is $1.25$ times its cold resistance. What is $I_{\mathrm{hot}}$ relative to $I_{\mathrm{cold}}$?

- $0.80$
- $1.25$
- $0.75$
- $1.00$

## Explanation

Voltage cancels in the ratio. Rising resistance by twenty-five percent reduces current by twenty percent.

$$
\frac{I_2}{I_1}=\frac{R_1}{1.25R_1}=0.80.
$$
