Which unit substitution confirms that $\Omega\,\mathrm{A^2}$ measures power?

- $(\mathrm{V/A})\mathrm{A^2}=\mathrm{V\,A}$
- $(\mathrm{A/V})\mathrm{A^2}=\mathrm{V\,A}$
- $(\mathrm{V/A})\mathrm{A^2}=\mathrm{V\,s}$
- $(\mathrm{V/A})\mathrm{A^2}=\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Replace an ohm with a volt per ampere. Cancel one ampere, leaving the product of voltage and current.

$$
\Omega\,\mathrm{A^2}=\frac{\mathrm{V}}{\mathrm{A}}\mathrm{A^2}=\mathrm{V\,A}=\mathrm{W}.
$$
