In one second the same section of an electrolyte is crossed by $2\,\mathrm{C}$ of positive charge rightward and $3\,\mathrm{C}$ of negative charge leftward in magnitude. Taking right as positive, what is the average current?

- $-1\,\mathrm{A}$
- $+1\,\mathrm{A}$
- $-5\,\mathrm{A}$
- $+5\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

The negative carriers contribute positive current relative to the reference because they travel in the opposite direction. The two contributions therefore add.

$$
I=\frac{2+3}{1}\,\mathrm{A}=+5\,\mathrm{A}.
$$
