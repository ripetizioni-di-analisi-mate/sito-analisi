For $1\,\mathrm{mm^2}$, someone enters $10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^2}$ instead of the correct value. How does the calculated resistance compare to the correct one?

- A thousand times larger
- Ten times smaller
- A thousand times smaller
- Unchanged

## Explanation

The area entered is a thousand times too large. Dividing by cross-sectional area makes calculated resistance a thousand times too small.

$$
A_{\mathrm{correct}}=(10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m})^2=10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^2}.
$$
