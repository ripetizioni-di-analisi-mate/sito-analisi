A resistor's resistance rises from $10\,\Omega$ to $12\,\Omega$ as it warms. A current source maintains $0.70\,\mathrm{A}$. What is the new drop across the resistor?

- $7.0\,\mathrm{V}$
- $5.83\,\mathrm{V}$
- $12\,\mathrm{V}$
- $8.4\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

The source maintains current, so voltage rises by the same factor as resistance. Use the hot value of twelve ohms.

$$
V_2=R_2I=12\cdot0.70\,\mathrm{V}=8.4\,\mathrm{V}.
$$
