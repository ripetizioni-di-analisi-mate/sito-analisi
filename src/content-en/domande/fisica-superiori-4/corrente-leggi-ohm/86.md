Two heaters deliver the same useful heat to water. The first is $0.60$ efficient and the second is $0.90$ efficient. What is the second heater's electrical input divided by the first's?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $0.30$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- $1$

## Explanation

At the same useful heat, input energy is inversely proportional to efficiency. The more efficient device therefore needs less energy.

$$
\frac{E_2}{E_1}=\frac{Q/0.90}{Q/0.60}=\frac{0.60}{0.90}=\frac{2}{3}.
$$
