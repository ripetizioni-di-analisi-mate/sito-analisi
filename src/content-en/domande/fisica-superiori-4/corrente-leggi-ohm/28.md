A current is $+1\,\mathrm{A}$ for $4\,\mathrm{s}$ and $-2\,\mathrm{A}$ for $2\,\mathrm{s}$. What is its average over the six seconds?

- $-0.50\,\mathrm{A}$
- $+1\,\mathrm{A}$
- $-1\,\mathrm{A}$
- $0\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

Both intervals carry equal charge magnitudes in opposite directions. The net transfer is zero even though current flows within each interval.

$$
\Delta q=(1\cdot4-2\cdot2)\,\mathrm{C}=0\,\mathrm{C},\qquad I_{\mathrm{avg}}=0\,\mathrm{A}.
$$
