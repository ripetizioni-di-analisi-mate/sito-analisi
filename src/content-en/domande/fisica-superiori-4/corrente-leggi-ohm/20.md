Current is $+0.50\,\mathrm{A}$ for $8\,\mathrm{s}$ and $-0.20\,\mathrm{A}$ for the next $5\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the net charge?

- $+5\,\mathrm{C}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{C}$
- $+1\,\mathrm{C}$
- $+3\,\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

The second interval transfers one coulomb against the reference direction, so subtract it from the four coulombs carried in the first.

$$
\Delta q=(0.50\cdot8-0.20\cdot5)\,\mathrm{C}=+3\,\mathrm{C}.
$$
