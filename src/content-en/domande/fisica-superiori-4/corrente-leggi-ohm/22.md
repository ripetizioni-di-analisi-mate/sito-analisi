On the cumulative net charge graph, what is the current between $t=2\,\mathrm{s}$ and $t=6\,\mathrm{s}$?

::grafico{f="2*x" xmin=0 xmax=7 ymin=0 ymax=15 nomi="t,q" punti="2,4,pieno;6,12,pieno" titolo="Time in seconds and cumulative charge in coulombs."}

- $8\,\mathrm{A}$
- $2\,\mathrm{A}$
- $0.50\,\mathrm{A}$
- $3\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

Charge rises from four to twelve coulombs over four seconds. The slope is this increase divided by elapsed time.

$$
I=\frac{12-4}{6-2}\,\mathrm{A}=2\,\mathrm{A}.
$$
