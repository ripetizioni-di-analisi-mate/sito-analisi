In a second test the resistor voltage is four times the first and the resistance is twice as large. What is the ratio of the second power to the first?

- $2$
- $16$
- $8$
- $32$

## Explanation

The voltage squared rises by a factor of sixteen. Twice the resistance in the denominator reduces that factor by half.

$$
\frac{P_2}{P_1}=\frac{(4V)^2}{2R}\frac{R}{V^2}=8.
$$
