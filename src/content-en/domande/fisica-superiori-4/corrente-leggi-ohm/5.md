A wire has $R_0=18\,\Omega$ at $20\,{}^\circ\mathrm{C}$ and $\alpha=4.0\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$. Find $R$ at $70\,{}^\circ\mathrm{C}$ in the linear model.

- $14.4\,\Omega$
- $3.6\,\Omega$
- $25.2\,\Omega$
- $21.6\,\Omega$

## Explanation

Temperature rises by $50\,\mathrm{K}$. The coefficient times this difference gives a $20\%$ increase over the initial resistance.

$$
R=18[1+0.004(70-20)]\,\Omega=21.6\,\Omega.
$$
