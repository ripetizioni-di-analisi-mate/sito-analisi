At what voltage magnitude does a $9\,\Omega$ resistor dissipate $4\,\mathrm{W}$?

- $36\,\mathrm{V}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{V}$
- $6\,\mathrm{V}$
- $3\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

First solve the power relation for voltage squared. Take the positive square root for the size of the voltage drop.

$$
V=\sqrt{PR}=\sqrt{4\cdot9}\,\mathrm{V}=6\,\mathrm{V}.
$$
