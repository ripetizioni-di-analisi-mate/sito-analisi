The second of two wires has twice the resistivity and three times the length of the first. What area gives equal resistance?

- $6A_1$
- $3A_1$
- $2A_1$
- $\dfrac{A_1}{6}$

## Explanation

The numerator becomes six times as large. To keep the same ratio, area must also increase by a factor of six.

$$
\frac{(2\rho_1)(3L_1)}{A_2}=\frac{\rho_1L_1}{A_1}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad A_2=6A_1.
$$
