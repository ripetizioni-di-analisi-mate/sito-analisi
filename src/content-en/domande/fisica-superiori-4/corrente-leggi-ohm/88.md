An ideal $12\,\mathrm{V}$ source must power a resistor that dissipates $24\,\mathrm{W}$. What resistance is needed?

- $2\,\Omega$
- $12\,\Omega$
- $24\,\Omega$
- $6\,\Omega$

## Explanation

The source fixes voltage and resistance is unknown, so solve for $R$ in the expression using voltage squared. Two amperes through this resistance give the desired power.

$$
R=\frac{V^2}{P}=\frac{12^2}{24}\,\Omega=6\,\Omega.
$$
