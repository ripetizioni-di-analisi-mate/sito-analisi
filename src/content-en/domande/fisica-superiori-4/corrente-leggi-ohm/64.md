A passive component draws $0.40\,\mathrm{A}$ with a $15\,\mathrm{V}$ drop. What electric power does it absorb?

- $6.0\,\mathrm{W}$
- $37.5\,\mathrm{W}$
- $15.4\,\mathrm{W}$
- $2.4\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

Fifteen volts means fifteen joules transferred per coulomb. With four tenths of a coulomb per second, power is six joules per second.

$$
P=VI=15\cdot0.40\,\mathrm{W}=6.0\,\mathrm{W}.
$$
