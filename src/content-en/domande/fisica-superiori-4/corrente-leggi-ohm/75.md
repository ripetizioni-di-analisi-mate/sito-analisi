For a resistor of constant resistance, how does $P$ depend on $I$?

- Linearly increasing with I
- Quadratically and symmetrically about I = 0
- Inversely proportional to I
- It is constant as I changes

## Explanation

Opposite currents have the same square and produce the same heat. The curve is a parabola touching the horizontal axis at the origin.

$$
P(I)=RI^2.
$$
