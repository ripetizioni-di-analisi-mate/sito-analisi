A $450\,\mathrm{W}$ electric heater stays on for $40\,\mathrm{min}$. How long must a $900\,\mathrm{W}$ electric heater stay on to draw the same electrical energy?

- $80\,\mathrm{min}$
- $40\,\mathrm{min}$
- $20\,\mathrm{min}$
- $10\,\mathrm{min}$

## Explanation

The energy drawn by an appliance of constant power is the product $P\,\Delta t$. The two energies must be equal, so $P_2\Delta t_2=P_1\Delta t_1$. Since the second power is twice the first, the duration is halved.

$$
\Delta t_2=\frac{P_1}{P_2}\,\Delta t_1=\frac{450}{900}\cdot40\,\mathrm{min}=20\,\mathrm{min}.
$$
