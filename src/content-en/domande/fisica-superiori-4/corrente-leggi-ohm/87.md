A $450\,\mathrm{W}$ device runs for $40\,\mathrm{min}$. How long must a $900\,\mathrm{W}$ device run to draw the same energy?

- $80\,\mathrm{min}$
- $40\,\mathrm{min}$
- $20\,\mathrm{min}$
- $10\,\mathrm{min}$

## Explanation

Keeping power times duration constant when power doubles requires halving the duration.

$$
P_2\Delta t_2=P_1\Delta t_1\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad\Delta t_2=\frac{450}{900}\cdot40\,\mathrm{min}=20\,\mathrm{min}.
$$
