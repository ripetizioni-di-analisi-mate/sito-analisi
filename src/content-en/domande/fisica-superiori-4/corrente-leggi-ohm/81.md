A heater draws $60\,\mathrm{kJ}$ and the water gains $45\,\mathrm{kJ}$. What is its efficiency for heating the water?

- $0.25$
- $0.75$
- $1.33$
- $0.45$

## Explanation

Efficiency is the useful share of the input energy. Here fifteen kilojoules go elsewhere, so the remaining seventy-five percent reaches the water.

$$
\eta=\frac{Q_{\mathrm{water}}}{E_{\mathrm{el}}}=\frac{45}{60}=0.75.
$$
