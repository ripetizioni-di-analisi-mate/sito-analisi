A measurement gives $V=2+4I$ with $V$ in volts and $I$ in amperes. Which condition for describing it by $V=RI$ is missing?

- Constant slope
- Zero absolute temperature
- The line passing through the origin
- A positive slope

## Explanation

Slope is constant, but at zero current the voltage is still two volts. Direct proportionality requires zero voltage at that point.

$$
V(0)=2\,\mathrm{V}.
$$
