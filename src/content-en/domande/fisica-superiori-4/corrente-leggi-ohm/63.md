A wire's $R(T)$ slope is $0.040\,\Omega/\mathrm{K}$. If a point with $R_0=10\,\Omega$ is chosen as reference, what relative coefficient should be used?

- $0.040\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$
- $0.40\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$
- $250\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$
- $0.0040\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$

## Explanation

In the line $R=R_0+R_0\alpha(T-T_0)$, the coefficient of $T$ is $R_0\alpha$. The slope of the same line is unchanged, so divide it by the new $R_0$.

$$
\alpha=\frac{0.040}{10}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}=0.0040\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}.
$$
