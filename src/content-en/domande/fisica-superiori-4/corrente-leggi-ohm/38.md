Which unit is equivalent to one ohm?

- $\mathrm{V/A}$
- $\mathrm{A/V}$
- $\mathrm{V\,A}$
- $\mathrm{C/s}$

## Explanation

In the experimental law, the numerator is measured in volts and the denominator in amperes. Volts per ampere is therefore the ohm.

$$
R=\frac{V}{I}.
$$
