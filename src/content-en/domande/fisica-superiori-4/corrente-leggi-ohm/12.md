A $10\,\mathrm{m}$ wire with cross-sectional area $2\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ has $R=0.50\,\Omega$. What is its resistivity?

- $0.0000001\,\Omega\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.0001\,\Omega\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.00001\,\Omega\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.0000002\,\Omega\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Solve for resistivity to separate the material property from geometry. Square meters from the area divided by meters from length leave a factor of meters.

$$
\rho=\frac{RA}{L}=\frac{0.50\cdot2\cdot10^{-6}}{10}\,\Omega\,\mathrm{m}=10^{-7}\,\Omega\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
