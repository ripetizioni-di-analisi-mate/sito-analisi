A current of $250\,\mathrm{mA}$ transfers $15\,\mathrm{C}$. How long does the transfer take?

- $0.060\,\mathrm{s}$
- $3.75\,\mathrm{s}$
- $60\,\mathrm{s}$
- $120\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

Two hundred fifty milliamperes is a quarter coulomb per second. One coulomb takes four seconds, so fifteen coulombs take sixty seconds.

$$
\Delta t=\frac{15}{0.250}\,\mathrm{s}=60\,\mathrm{s}.
$$
