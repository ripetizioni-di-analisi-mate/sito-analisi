A uniform wire has $\rho=1.7\cdot10^{-8}\,\Omega\,\mathrm{m}$, length $14.4\,\mathrm{m}$, and cross-sectional area $1.5\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Find its resistance. Choose the closest value.

- $0.326\,\Omega$
- $0.0163\,\Omega$
- $0.163\,\Omega$
- $0.0816\,\Omega$

## Explanation

The cross section is already in square meters, so divide resistivity times length directly by area. The units of length cancel.

$$
R=\rho\frac{L}{A}=\frac{1.7\cdot10^{-8}\cdot14.4}{1.5\cdot10^{-6}}\,\Omega\simeq0.163\,\Omega.
$$
