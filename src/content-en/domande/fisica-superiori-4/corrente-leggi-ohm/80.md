A $1000\,\mathrm{W}$ heater runs for $60\,\mathrm{s}$ with $0.70$ efficiency for heating the water. With $1\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water and $c=4200\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$, what temperature rise results?

- $14.3\,\mathrm{K}$
- $7\,\mathrm{K}$
- $10\,\mathrm{K}$
- $20\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

The water receives 42000 joules of useful energy. Dividing by the heat capacity of that mass gives its temperature rise.

$$
\Delta T=\frac{\eta P\Delta t}{mc}=\frac{0.70\cdot1000\cdot60}{1\cdot4200}\,\mathrm{K}=10\,\mathrm{K}.
$$
