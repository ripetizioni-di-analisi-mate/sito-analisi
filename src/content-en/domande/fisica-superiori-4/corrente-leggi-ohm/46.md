Which connection lets an ideal voltmeter measure the drop across a single resistor?

- Both terminals at the same end
- One terminal at each end of the resistor
- The meter inserted in series in the branch
- One terminal on the resistor and the other disconnected

## Explanation

Voltage is the difference between potentials at two nodes. Connecting the meter to those nodes places it in parallel with the resistor.
