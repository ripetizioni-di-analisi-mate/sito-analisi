On a graph of $I$ versus $V$, line $A$ passes through $(2\,\mathrm{V},2\,\mathrm{A})$ and line $B$ through $(2\,\mathrm{V},1\,\mathrm{A})$. Which resistor has lower resistance?

::grafico{f="x;x/2" xmin=0 xmax=3 ymin=0 ymax=3.5 nomi="V,I" punti="2,2,pieno,A;2,1,pieno,B" titolo="Voltage in volts and current in amperes."}

- B
- Their values are equal
- Time measurements are needed
- A

## Explanation

At the same voltage, more current flows through the less resistive component. On these axes that is the steeper line.

$$
R_A=\frac{2}{2}\,\Omega=1\,\Omega,\qquad R_B=\frac{2}{1}\,\Omega=2\,\Omega.
$$
