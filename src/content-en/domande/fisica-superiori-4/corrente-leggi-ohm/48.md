A voltmeter measures the voltage of a $1\,\mathrm{k\Omega}$ branch. Which meter resistance minimizes the current drawn by the meter?

- $10\,\mathrm{k\Omega}$
- $1\,\mathrm{k\Omega}$
- $10\,\mathrm{M\Omega}$
- $100\,\Omega$

## Explanation

The voltmeter is across the same voltage as the branch. A large input resistance reduces current through the meter and therefore its effect on the measurement.

$$
I_{\mathrm{V}}=\frac{V}{R_{\mathrm{V}}}.
$$
