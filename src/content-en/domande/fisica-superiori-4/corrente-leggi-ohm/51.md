Two wires of the same material and length have circular cross sections. The second has twice the diameter. What is $R_2$ in terms of $R_1$?

- $\dfrac{R_1}{4}$
- $\dfrac{R_1}{2}$
- $2R_1$
- $4R_1$

## Explanation

Area increases with the square of diameter. Since area is in the denominator, resistance falls by a factor of four.

$$
A_2=\frac{\pi(2d_1)^2}{4}=4A_1,\qquad R_2=\frac{R_1}{4}.
$$
