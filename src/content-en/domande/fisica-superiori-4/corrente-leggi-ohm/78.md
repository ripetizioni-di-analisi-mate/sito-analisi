How many joules equal $50\,\mathrm{Wh}$?

- $5.0\cdot10^4\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1.8\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1.8\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{J}$
- $3.0\cdot10^3\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Power is already in watts, so convert only hours to seconds. An extra factor of one thousand would be needed for kilowatt-hours.

$$
E=50\cdot3600\,\mathrm{J}=1.8\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
