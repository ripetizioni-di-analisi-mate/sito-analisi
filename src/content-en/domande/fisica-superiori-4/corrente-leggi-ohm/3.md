A $21.6\,\Omega$ ohmic resistor is held at $9.0\,\mathrm{V}$ and constant temperature. Find the current. Choose the closest value.

- $2.40\,\mathrm{A}$
- $0.417\,\mathrm{A}$
- $0.833\,\mathrm{A}$
- $0.208\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

Ohmic proportionality applies under the stated conditions. Divide voltage by resistance, giving a quotient in amperes.

$$
I=\frac{V}{R}=\frac{9.0}{21.6}\,\mathrm{A}\simeq0.417\,\mathrm{A}.
$$
