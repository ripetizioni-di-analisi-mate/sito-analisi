The voltage–current graph marks $(I,V)=(1\,\mathrm{A},4\,\mathrm{V})$ and $(2\,\mathrm{A},8\,\mathrm{V})$. What resistance does the characteristic represent?

::grafico{f="4*x" xmin=0 xmax=3 ymin=0 ymax=13 nomi="I,V" punti="1,4,pieno;2,8,pieno" titolo="Current in amperes and voltage in volts."}

- $4\,\Omega$
- $0.25\,\Omega$
- $8\,\Omega$
- $2\,\Omega$

## Explanation

The vertical coordinate is voltage. The slope in volts per ampere gives resistance directly, without taking its reciprocal.

$$
R=\frac{8-4}{2-1}\,\Omega=4\,\Omega.
$$
