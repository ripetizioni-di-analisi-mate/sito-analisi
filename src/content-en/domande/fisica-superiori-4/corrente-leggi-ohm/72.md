An $8\,\Omega$ resistor carrying $2\,\mathrm{A}$ is replaced by a $32\,\Omega$ resistor. What current gives the same power?

- $0.50\,\mathrm{A}$
- $1\,\mathrm{A}$
- $4\,\mathrm{A}$
- $2\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

Quadrupling resistance requires current squared to fall to one quarter to keep the same power. Current magnitude halves.

$$
I_2=I_1\sqrt{\frac{R_1}{R_2}}=2\sqrt{\frac{8}{32}}\,\mathrm{A}=1\,\mathrm{A}.
$$
