In a metal wire electrons drift from right to left. Which direction does conventional current point?

- From right to left
- From left to right
- Perpendicular to the wire
- No direction because the wire is neutral

## Explanation

Electrons are negatively charged, so their passage to the left produces the same sign of current as positive charge flowing to the right.
