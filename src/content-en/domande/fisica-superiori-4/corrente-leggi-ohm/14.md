On a $4\,\mathrm{V}$ source, a $10\,\Omega$ resistor is replaced by a $20\,\Omega$ resistor. What is the new power?

- $0.80\,\mathrm{W}$
- $1.60\,\mathrm{W}$
- $3.20\,\mathrm{W}$
- $0.20\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

Voltage stays fixed and the larger resistance halves the current. Power, the product of voltage and current, also halves.

$$
P=\frac{V^2}{R}=\frac{4^2}{20}\,\mathrm{W}=0.80\,\mathrm{W}.
$$
