During one hour a device draws $1\,\mathrm{kW}$ for $15\,\mathrm{min}$ and $0.04\,\mathrm{kW}$ on standby for the rest. What energy does it use in the hour?

- $0.28\,\mathrm{kWh}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{kWh}$
- $0.50\,\mathrm{kWh}$
- $1.04\,\mathrm{kWh}$

## Explanation

Standby lasts three quarters of an hour and draws nonzero power. Add its energy use to the quarter hour of active operation.

$$
E=1\cdot0.25\,\mathrm{kWh}+0.04\cdot0.75\,\mathrm{kWh}=0.28\,\mathrm{kWh}.
$$
