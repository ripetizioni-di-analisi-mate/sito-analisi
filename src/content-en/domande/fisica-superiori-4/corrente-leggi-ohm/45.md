Where should an ideal ammeter be placed to measure only the current through one resistor?

- In series with the resistor in the same branch
- In parallel with the resistor
- In parallel at the source terminals
- In a separate branch

## Explanation

In a series branch the same current goes through the resistor and the meter. The ammeter therefore measures the charge flow in the branch of interest.
