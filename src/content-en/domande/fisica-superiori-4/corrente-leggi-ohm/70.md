A resistor dissipates $720\,\mathrm{J}$ in $2\,\mathrm{min}$. What is its average power?

- $360\,\mathrm{W}$
- $6\,\mathrm{W}$
- $12\,\mathrm{W}$
- $0.167\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

Average power is energy divided by the entire elapsed time in seconds. A watt is one joule per second.

$$
P_{\mathrm{avg}}=\frac{720}{2\cdot60}\,\mathrm{W}=6\,\mathrm{W}.
$$
