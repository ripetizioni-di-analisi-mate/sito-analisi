An unknown component draws $I=0.50\,\mathrm{A}$ at $V=2\,\mathrm{V}$. Which additional point supports a $4\,\Omega$ ohmic law over the measured range?

- $V=6\,\mathrm{V},\ I=0.50\,\mathrm{A}$
- $V=6\,\mathrm{V},\ I=1.5\,\mathrm{A}$
- $V=4\,\mathrm{V},\ I=0.50\,\mathrm{A}$
- $V=8\,\mathrm{V},\ I=1\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

A second point supports the model if it has the same voltage-to-current ratio. Only six volts at one and a half amperes meets that condition.

$$
V=(4\,\Omega)I,\qquad I(6)=\frac{6}{4}\,\mathrm{A}=1.5\,\mathrm{A}.
$$
