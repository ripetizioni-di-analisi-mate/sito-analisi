Two wires carry the same current with the same carrier charge. The second wire has three times the carrier density and half the cross-sectional area. What is the ratio of the second wire's drift speed to the first wire's?

- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{1}{6}$
- $6$
- $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Explanation

At fixed current, drift speed is inverse to density times area. The new product is one and a half times the old one, so speed falls to its reciprocal.

$$
\frac{v_{d,2}}{v_{d,1}}=\frac{n_1A_1}{(3n_1)(\frac{A_1}{2})}=\frac{2}{3}.
$$
