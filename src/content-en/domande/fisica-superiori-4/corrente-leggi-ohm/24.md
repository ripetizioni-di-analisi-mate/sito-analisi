A wire carries $0.32\,\mathrm{A}$ with $A=2\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and $v_d=2\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Using $e=1.60\cdot10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}$, what electron density follows?

- $5\cdot10^{27}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}}$
- $10^{29}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}}$
- $2.5\cdot10^{28}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}}$
- $5\cdot10^{28}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}}$

## Explanation

Area times speed gives the volume crossing the section per second. Divide the electron count per second by that volume.

$$
n=\frac{I}{eAv_d}=\frac{0.32}{1.60\cdot10^{-19}\cdot2\cdot10^{-6}\cdot2\cdot10^{-5}}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}}=5\cdot10^{28}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}}.
$$
