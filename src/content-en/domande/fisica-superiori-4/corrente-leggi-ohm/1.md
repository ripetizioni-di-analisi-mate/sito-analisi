A direct current of $0.42\,\mathrm{A}$ crosses a section for $150\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the net charge transported?

- $63\,\mathrm{C}$
- $21\,\mathrm{C}$
- $126\,\mathrm{C}$
- $0.063\,\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

A net 0.42 coulombs crosses the section each second. Since current is constant, multiply this per-second transfer by the elapsed time.

$$
\Delta q=I\Delta t=0.42\cdot150\,\mathrm{C}=63\,\mathrm{C}.
$$
