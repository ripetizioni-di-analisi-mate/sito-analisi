A piece one quarter as long is cut from a uniform $12\,\Omega$ wire. At the same area and temperature, what is the piece's resistance?

- $48\,\Omega$
- $3\,\Omega$
- $6\,\Omega$
- $12\,\Omega$

## Explanation

Resistance per meter is unchanged by cutting the wire. One quarter of the length gives one quarter of the resistance.

$$
R_{\mathrm{piece}}=12\,\Omega\frac{1}{4}=3\,\Omega.
$$
