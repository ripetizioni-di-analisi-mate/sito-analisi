The same ohmic resistor carries $0.20\,\mathrm{A}$ first and then $0.60\,\mathrm{A}$, with unchanged resistance. What is the second power divided by the first?

- $3$
- $9$
- $6$
- $\dfrac{1}{9}$

## Explanation

Resistance cancels. Tripling the current also triples voltage drop across the same resistor, giving nine times the power.

$$
\frac{P_2}{P_1}=\left(\frac{0.60}{0.20}\right)^2=9.
$$
