At the same temperature, a component draws $1\,\mathrm{A}$ at $2\,\mathrm{V}$ and $1.5\,\mathrm{A}$ at $6\,\mathrm{V}$. What is the ratio of $V$ to $I$ at the second point?

- $2\,\Omega$
- $8\,\Omega$
- $4\,\Omega$
- $0.25\,\Omega$

## Explanation

Divide the coordinates of the same point. The first point would give two ohms, so the ratio changes with operating point.

$$
\frac{V_2}{I_2}=\frac{6}{1.5}\,\Omega=4\,\Omega.
$$
