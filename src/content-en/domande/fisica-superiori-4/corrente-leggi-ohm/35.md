Measurements give $(I,V)=(1,3)$, $(2,6)$, $(3,9)$, $(4,16)$ in amperes and volts. Which is the first point incompatible with $R$ from the first two?

- $(3,9)$
- $(2,6)$
- $(1,3)$
- $(4,16)$

## Explanation

Resistance from the first two data points predicts nine volts at three amperes and twelve at four. The observed value at four amperes is therefore the first point that departs.

$$
R=\frac{3}{1}\,\Omega=\frac{6}{2}\,\Omega=3\,\Omega,\qquad V(4)=12\,\mathrm{V}.
$$
