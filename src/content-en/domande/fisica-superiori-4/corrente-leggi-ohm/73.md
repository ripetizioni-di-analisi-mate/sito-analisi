A net charge of $25\,\mathrm{C}$ crosses a potential drop of $12\,\mathrm{V}$. How much energy is transferred to the component?

- $2.08\,\mathrm{J}$
- $300\,\mathrm{J}$
- $37\,\mathrm{J}$
- $13\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The potential drop gives the joules transferred per coulomb. Multiply by the total amount of charge.

$$
E=V\Delta q=12\cdot25\,\mathrm{J}=300\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
