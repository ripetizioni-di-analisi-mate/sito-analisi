An ohmic resistor carries first $+0.50\,\mathrm{A}$ and then $-0.50\,\mathrm{A}$ in the same reference direction. How does dissipated power change?

- It stays equal and positive
- It changes sign
- It doubles
- It becomes zero

## Explanation

Joule heating depends on current squared. Reversing charge flow leaves the rate of energy transfer to the resistor unchanged.

$$
P_1=R(0.50)^2,\qquad P_2=R(-0.50)^2=P_1.
$$
