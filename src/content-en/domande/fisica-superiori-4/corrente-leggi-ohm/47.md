An ideal $6\,\mathrm{V}$ source supplies a $5\,\Omega$ resistor. After adding a real ammeter of resistance $1\,\Omega$ in series, what current does it measure?

- $1\,\mathrm{A}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{A}$
- $6\,\mathrm{A}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

The meter's resistance adds to the resistance in series. The measured current is therefore lower than the current that would flow without the real ammeter.

$$
I=\frac{6}{5+1}\,\mathrm{A}=1\,\mathrm{A}.
$$
