Power is constant at $600\,\mathrm{W}$ for the first $10\,\mathrm{s}$ and zero for the next $20\,\mathrm{s}$. What is average power over the entire interval?

- $300\,\mathrm{W}$
- $600\,\mathrm{W}$
- $20\,\mathrm{W}$
- $200\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

The active time is one third of the total. Divide the transferred energy by all thirty seconds to obtain the equivalent constant power.

$$
P_{\mathrm{avg}}=\frac{600\cdot10+0\cdot20}{30}\,\mathrm{W}=200\,\mathrm{W}.
$$
