A current is $2\,\mathrm{A}$ for $3\,\mathrm{s}$ and $0.50\,\mathrm{A}$ in the same direction for another $9\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the average current?

- $1.25\,\mathrm{A}$
- $1.75\,\mathrm{A}$
- $0.50\,\mathrm{A}$
- $0.875\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

The lower current acts for three times as long as the higher one. First add the charges by weighting each current by its duration.

$$
I_{\mathrm{avg}}=\frac{2\cdot3+0.50\cdot9}{3+9}\,\mathrm{A}=0.875\,\mathrm{A}.
$$
