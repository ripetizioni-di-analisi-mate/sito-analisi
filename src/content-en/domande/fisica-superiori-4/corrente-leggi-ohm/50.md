A $5\,\mathrm{m}$ wire with resistivity $2\cdot10^{-8}\,\Omega\,\mathrm{m}$ must have $R\leq0.10\,\Omega$. What is the minimum cross-sectional area?

- $1\,\mathrm{mm^2}$
- $0.1\,\mathrm{mm^2}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{mm^2}$
- $10\,\mathrm{mm^2}$

## Explanation

A larger area lowers resistance, so the minimum area occurs when resistance exactly reaches its allowed maximum.

$$
A_{\min}=\frac{\rho L}{R_{\max}}=\frac{2\cdot10^{-8}\cdot5}{0.10}\,\mathrm{m^2}=1\,\mathrm{mm^2}.
$$
