A device uses $7.2\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{J}$. How much energy is that in kilowatt-hours?

- $0.50\,\mathrm{kWh}$
- $2.0\,\mathrm{kWh}$
- $7200\,\mathrm{kWh}$
- $7.2\,\mathrm{kWh}$

## Explanation

One kilowatt-hour contains 3.6 million joules. Divide the energy in joules by the number of joules in that unit.

$$
E=\frac{7.2\cdot10^6}{3.6\cdot10^6}\,\mathrm{kWh}=2.0\,\mathrm{kWh}.
$$
