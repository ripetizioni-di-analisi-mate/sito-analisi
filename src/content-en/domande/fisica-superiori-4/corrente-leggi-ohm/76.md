A $1.2\,\mathrm{kW}$ device runs for $30\,\mathrm{min}$ and a $0.60\,\mathrm{kW}$ device runs for $1.5\,\mathrm{h}$. How much total energy do they draw?

- $2.70\,\mathrm{kWh}$
- $0.90\,\mathrm{kWh}$
- $3.60\,\mathrm{kWh}$
- $1.50\,\mathrm{kWh}$

## Explanation

Convert the first time to half an hour. Since the durations differ, add the two energies calculated separately.

$$
E=1.2\cdot0.50\,\mathrm{kWh}+0.60\cdot1.5\,\mathrm{kWh}=1.50\,\mathrm{kWh}.
$$
