A $2\,\Omega$ resistor carries $+3\,\mathrm{A}$ for $2\,\mathrm{s}$ and $-3\,\mathrm{A}$ for another $2\,\mathrm{s}$. How much energy does it dissipate over the four seconds?

- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $72\,\mathrm{J}$
- $36\,\mathrm{J}$
- $24\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Charge transfers in the two directions cancel, but power depends on current squared. Both intervals contribute thirty-six joules.

$$
E=2(3^2\cdot2+(-3)^2\cdot2)\,\mathrm{J}=72\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
