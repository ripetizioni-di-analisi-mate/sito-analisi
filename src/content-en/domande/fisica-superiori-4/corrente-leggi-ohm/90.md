In a $3\,\Omega$ resistor the current is first $+2\,\mathrm A$ and then $-1\,\mathrm A$. By the end of the two intervals the net charge transferred is zero and $54\,\mathrm J$ have been dissipated. What is the total duration of the current?

- $3\,\mathrm s$
- $9\,\mathrm s$
- $6\,\mathrm s$
- $18\,\mathrm s$

## Explanation

If the first interval lasts $t$, the second must last $2t$ for the charges to cancel. The dissipated energies add over both intervals, giving $3(2^2t+1^2\cdot2t)=18t$ joules for $t$ in seconds. Thus $t=3\,\mathrm s$ and the total duration is $3t=9\,\mathrm s$.
