Current increases linearly from $0$ to $3\,\mathrm{A}$ in the first $4\,\mathrm{s}$. How much charge crosses the section in this interval?

::grafico{f="0.75*x" xmin=0 xmax=4 ymin=0 ymax=4 nomi="t,I" area="0.75*x;0;0;4" punti="4,3,pieno" titolo="Time in seconds and current in amperes."}

- $6\,\mathrm{C}$
- $12\,\mathrm{C}$
- $3\,\mathrm{C}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

Charge transfer is the area under the current–time curve. The region is a triangle. Omitting the factor of one half would assume three amperes throughout.

$$
\Delta q=\frac{1}{2}\cdot4\cdot3\,\mathrm{C}=6\,\mathrm{C}.
$$
