In one measurement, $5\,\mathrm{C}$ passes in one direction and $2\,\mathrm{C}$ in the opposite direction. What is the sum of the two charge magnitudes?

- $3\,\mathrm{C}$
- $5\,\mathrm{C}$
- $2\,\mathrm{C}$
- $7\,\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

Adding magnitudes counts all the transfer in both directions. Subtracting the contributions instead gives net transfer relative to a reference direction.

$$
|\Delta q_1|+|\Delta q_2|=(5+2)\,\mathrm{C}=7\,\mathrm{C}.
$$
