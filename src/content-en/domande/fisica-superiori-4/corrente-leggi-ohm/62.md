In a certain range a material has $\alpha<0$. The component is heated at fixed voltage across it. How do resistance and current change?

- Both decrease
- Resistance increases and current decreases
- Both increase
- Resistance decreases and current increases

## Explanation

As temperature rises, $\alpha\Delta T$ is negative and lowers resistance. At fixed voltage the smaller denominator gives a larger current.
