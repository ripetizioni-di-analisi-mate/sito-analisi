An ohmic line on a graph of $I$ versus $V$ passes through $(4\,\mathrm{V},0.50\,\mathrm{A})$. What current is read at $2\,\mathrm{V}$?

- $0.50\,\mathrm{A}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{A}$
- $1.00\,\mathrm{A}$
- $0.125\,\mathrm{A}$

## Explanation

The line passes through the origin, so the point at half the voltage also has half the vertical coordinate. There is no need to find $R$ separately.

$$
I(2)=\frac{2}{4}I(4)=0.25\,\mathrm{A}.
$$
