An ohmic resistor draws $0.60\,\mathrm{A}$ at $3\,\mathrm{V}$. What is its conductance, the reciprocal of resistance?

- $5\,\mathrm{A/V}$
- $0.20\,\mathrm{A/V}$
- $1.8\,\mathrm{A/V}$
- $0.60\,\mathrm{A/V}$

## Explanation

Resistance is voltage divided by current. Swap numerator and denominator to get conductance.

$$
G=\frac{I}{V}=\frac{0.60}{3}\,\mathrm{A/V}=0.20\,\mathrm{A/V}.
$$
