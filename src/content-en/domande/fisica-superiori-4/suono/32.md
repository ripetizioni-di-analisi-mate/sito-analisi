A string and a $440\,\mathrm{Hz}$ tuning fork produce beats. The string's frequency is lowered slightly without crossing that of the fork, and the beats slow down. What was the initial relation?

- The string's frequency was above $440\,\mathrm{Hz}$.
- The string's frequency was below $440\,\mathrm{Hz}$.
- The frequencies were equal.
- The frequencies were an octave apart.

## Explanation

Slower beats mean closer frequencies. Lowering the initially higher frequency reduces the difference, while lowering the lower frequency increases it. The string therefore began on the higher side.
