An ideal pipe open at both ends is $0.90\,\mathrm m$ long. Its second harmonic is $380\,\mathrm{Hz}$. What is the speed of sound in the pipe?

- $684\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $171\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $422\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $342\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The second mode fits two half wavelengths, or one full wavelength. Multiplying that wavelength by the frequency gives the sound speed.

$$
\lambda_2=\frac{2L}{2}=0.90\,\mathrm m,\qquad u=f_2\lambda_2=380\cdot0.90\,\mathrm{m/s}=342\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
