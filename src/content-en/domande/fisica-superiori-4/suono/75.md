The microphone remains fixed. An isotropic point source of $0.20\,\mathrm W$ moves from $2\,\mathrm m$ to $5\,\mathrm m$ from the microphone, then stops. What power must it emit to restore the initial intensity in a free field?

- $0.50\,\mathrm{W}$
- $0.03\,\mathrm{W}$
- $1.25\,\mathrm{W}$
- $1.80\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

To achieve the same intensity, keep the ratio of power to area fixed. The new sphere is larger by the squared radius ratio. Power must increase by the same factor.

$$
P_2=P_1\left(\frac{r_2}{r_1}\right)^2=0.20\left(\frac52\right)^2\,\mathrm W=1.25\,\mathrm W.
$$
