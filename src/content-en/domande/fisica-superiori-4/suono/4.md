Sound intensity is $2.4\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$ and the reference is $I_0=10^{-12}\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$. What is the level in decibels?

- $33.8\,\mathrm{dB}$
- $127.6\,\mathrm{dB}$
- $3.8\,\mathrm{dB}$
- $63.8\,\mathrm{dB}$

## Explanation

The intensity ratio is $2.4\cdot10^6$. The factor $10^6$ contributes $60\,\mathrm{dB}$ and the factor $2.4$ raises the level further.

$$
\beta=10\log_{10}(2.4\cdot10^6)\,\mathrm{dB}\simeq63.8\,\mathrm{dB}.
$$
