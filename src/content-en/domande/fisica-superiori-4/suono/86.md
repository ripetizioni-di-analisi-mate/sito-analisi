In an ideal pipe open at both ends and $0.80\,\mathrm m$ long, sound travels at $320\,\mathrm{m/s}$. In a second gas it travels at $400\,\mathrm{m/s}$. How long must the pipe be adjusted to keep the fundamental frequency fixed?

- $0.64\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.00\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.60\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The type of mode stays the same, so the factor of two cancels. To keep the frequency fixed, the ratio of speed to length must remain unchanged.

$$
\frac{u_1}{2L_1}=\frac{u_2}{2L_2}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad L_2=0.80\frac{400}{320}\,\mathrm m=1.00\,\mathrm m.
$$
