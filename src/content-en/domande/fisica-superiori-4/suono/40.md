The graph shows the longitudinal displacement of air in a pipe at the peak of oscillation. The ends are at $x=0$ and $x=1\,\mathrm m$. Which setup is compatible with this profile?

::grafico{curve="t,sin(pi*t/2),0,1" xmin=-0.04 xmax=1.12 ymin=-0.28 ymax=1.25 nomi="x,s/sₘₐₓ" punti="0,0,pieno;1,1,pieno" legenda="s(x)/s_{\max}" titolo="Normalized air displacement."}

- Open on the left and closed on the right.
- Open at both ends.
- Closed on the left and open on the right.
- Closed at both ends.

## Explanation

At the left end displacement is zero, so that boundary is a displacement node. At the right end displacement reaches its maximum, an antinode. The node is at the closed wall and the antinode is at the opening.
