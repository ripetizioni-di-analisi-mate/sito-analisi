Background noise alone gives $50\,\mathrm{dB}$. With an independent source switched on, the total is $53.01\,\mathrm{dB}$. What level would the source produce on its own?

- $53.0\,\mathrm{dB}$
- $50.0\,\mathrm{dB}$
- $3.0\,\mathrm{dB}$
- $26.5\,\mathrm{dB}$

## Explanation

A rise of about three decibels doubles the total intensity. Subtract the background intensity to find a source intensity nearly equal to the initial one. The two levels are therefore nearly equal.

$$
I_{\mathrm{tot}}\simeq2I_{\mathrm{f}},\qquad I_{\mathrm{s}}=I_{\mathrm{tot}}-I_{\mathrm{f}}\simeq I_{\mathrm{f}},\qquad \beta_{\mathrm{s}}\simeq50.0\,\mathrm{dB}.
$$
