In air a sound front travels along a horizontal axis. How does an air particle move about equilibrium?

- It oscillates along the same axis.
- It oscillates along a vertical axis.
- It follows the front to the receiver.
- It always remains stationary.

## Explanation

For longitudinal sound, small displacements along the direction of travel produce alternating compressions and rarefactions. After one cycle the particle returns to its initial position.
