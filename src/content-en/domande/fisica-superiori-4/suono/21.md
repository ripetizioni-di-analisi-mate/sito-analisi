Two independent sources separately produce $50\,\mathrm{dB}$ and $70\,\mathrm{dB}$ at the same microphone. Which value is closest to the total level?

- $120.00\,\mathrm{dB}$
- $60.00\,\mathrm{dB}$
- $90.00\,\mathrm{dB}$
- $70.04\,\mathrm{dB}$

## Explanation

The $50\,\mathrm{dB}$ source provides one hundredth of the other source's intensity. Their sum is $1.01$ times the larger intensity, so only a few hundredths of a decibel are added to the higher level.

$$
\beta_{\mathrm{tot}}=70\,\mathrm{dB}+10\log_{10}(1.01)\,\mathrm{dB}\simeq70.04\,\mathrm{dB}.
$$
