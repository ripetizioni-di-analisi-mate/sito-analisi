An instrument measures $0.44\,\mathrm s$ from an electronic trigger until an echo returns to the source. The pulse is emitted $0.04\,\mathrm s$ after the trigger. With $u=340\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the distance to the wall?

- $74.8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $136.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $81.6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $68.0\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Acoustic travel starts at emission. Subtract the internal delay before using the round-trip relation.

$$
d=\frac{340(0.44-0.04)}{2}\,\mathrm m=68.0\,\mathrm m.
$$
