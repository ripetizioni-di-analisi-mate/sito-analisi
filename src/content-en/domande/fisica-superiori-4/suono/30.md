A person stands between two parallel walls, closer to one of them. They clap and hear the first echo after $0.16\,\mathrm s$ and the second after $0.24\,\mathrm s$. With $u=340\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the distance between the two walls?

- $13.6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $136.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $40.8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $68.0\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Each echo travels from the person to a wall and back, so the distance to that wall is half the path travelled. The first echo comes from the nearer wall and the second from the farther one. Since the person is between the walls, their separation is the sum of the two distances.

$$
d_1=\frac{340\cdot0.16}{2}\,\mathrm m=27.2\,\mathrm m,\qquad d_2=\frac{340\cdot0.24}{2}\,\mathrm m=40.8\,\mathrm m,\qquad d=d_1+d_2=68.0\,\mathrm m.
$$
