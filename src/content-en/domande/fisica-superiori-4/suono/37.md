An ideal uniform pipe open at both ends has consecutive resonances at $480\,\mathrm{Hz}$ and $640\,\mathrm{Hz}$. What is its fundamental frequency?

- $80\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $160\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $480\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $640\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

An open pipe allows all integer multiples of its fundamental. Two consecutive modes are therefore separated by exactly $f_1$.

$$
f_1=f_{n+1}-f_n=(640-480)\,\mathrm{Hz}=160\,\mathrm{Hz}.
$$
