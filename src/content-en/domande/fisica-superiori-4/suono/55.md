A source emits at $500\,\mathrm{Hz}$ and moves at $40\,\mathrm{m/s}$ toward an observer. The observer approaches it at $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$. In still air with $u=340\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what frequency is received?

- $421\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $600\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $500\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $474\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

The two motions enter at different steps. The source reduces the wavefront spacing, and the observer increases the encounter speed. Both components are positive.

$$
f'=500\frac{340+20}{340-40}\,\mathrm{Hz}=600\,\mathrm{Hz}.
$$
