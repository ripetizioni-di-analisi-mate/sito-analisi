In a room a microphone measures nearly the same intensity at $2\,\mathrm m$ and $4\,\mathrm m$ from a stationary loudspeaker. Which assumption of the prediction $I\propto\dfrac{1}{r^2}$ may fail?

- The local speed is finite.
- Intensity is measured per unit area.
- The tone frequency can be measured.
- Reflected sound contributes negligibly.

## Explanation

In a free field, outgoing power spreads over spheres. In a room reflected waves reach the microphone from different directions and alter the field. The four-to-one ratio no longer follows from distance alone.
