A stationary source emits at $450\,\mathrm{Hz}$. A moving observer receives $495\,\mathrm{Hz}$ and $u=340\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Taking the component toward the source as positive, what is $v_o$?

- $-34\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $34\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $45\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $68\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The emitted spacing is fixed by the stationary source. Receiving ten percent more fronts per second requires an encounter speed ten percent above $u$.

$$
v_o=u\left(\frac{f'}{f}-1\right)=340\left(\frac{495}{450}-1\right)\,\mathrm{m/s}=34\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
