Two stationary microphones lie along the path of a moving source, one ahead and one behind. They receive $600\,\mathrm{Hz}$ ahead and $400\,\mathrm{Hz}$ behind. The air is still and the motion is subsonic. What is the emitted frequency?

- $500\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $600\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $400\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $480\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

The speed term has opposite signs in the two measurements. Add the reciprocals to cancel it. The arithmetic mean of the frequencies does not give the same cancellation.

$$
\frac{1}{f'_\mathrm{A}}+\frac{1}{f'_\mathrm{B}}=\frac{u-v_s+u+v_s}{fu}=\frac{2}{f},\qquad f=\frac{2}{\frac1{600}+\frac1{400}}\,\mathrm{Hz}=480\,\mathrm{Hz}.
$$
