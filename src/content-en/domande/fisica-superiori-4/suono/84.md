Two sound waves in different media have the same wavelength. Their periods measured at fixed points are $T_A=2\,\mathrm{ms}$ and $T_B=5\,\mathrm{ms}$. What is $\dfrac{u_A}{u_B}$?

- $0.40$
- $1.00$
- $2.50$
- $6.25$

## Explanation

A front travels the same distance in each cycle. A shorter period means less time for that distance and therefore greater speed. The ratio is the inverse of the period ratio.

$$
\frac{u_A}{u_B}=\frac{\lambda/T_A}{\lambda/T_B}=\frac{T_B}{T_A}=\frac52=2.50.
$$
