An ideal pipe open at both ends has fundamental frequency $180\,\mathrm{Hz}$. How many of its resonances, including the fundamental, are strictly below $900\,\mathrm{Hz}$?

- $5$
- $3$
- $4$
- $2$

## Explanation

All positive integer multiples are allowed, but the fifth lies on the cutoff. The first four modes are therefore included.

$$
n(180\,\mathrm{Hz})<900\,\mathrm{Hz}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad n<5.
$$
