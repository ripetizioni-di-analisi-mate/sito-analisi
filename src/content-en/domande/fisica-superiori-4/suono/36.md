Two closely spaced tones have pressure amplitudes $3\,\mathrm{Pa}$ and $2\,\mathrm{Pa}$ at the microphone. What is the minimum envelope amplitude?

- $0\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $1\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $5\,\mathrm{Pa}$
- $6\,\mathrm{Pa}$

## Explanation

At the minimum the two oscillations are in opposite phase. Subtract the amplitudes, giving $(3-2)\,\mathrm{Pa}=1\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Complete cancellation would require equal amplitudes.
