An ideal uniform pipe of length $0.78\,\mathrm m$ is closed at one end and open at the other. With $u=340\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is its fundamental frequency?

- $218\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $327\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $54\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $109\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

The displacement node at the wall and the antinode at the opening are adjacent in the fundamental mode, so $L=\dfrac{\lambda_1}{4}$.

$$
f_1=\frac{u}{4L}=\frac{340}{4\cdot0.78}\,\mathrm{Hz}\simeq109\,\mathrm{Hz}.
$$
