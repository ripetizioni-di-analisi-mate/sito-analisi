A microphone next to the source receives an echo after $0.42\,\mathrm s$. Sound travels at $340\,\mathrm{m/s}$. How far away is the reflecting wall?

- $142.8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $71.4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $35.7\,\mathrm{m}$
- $285.6\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The delay includes the path to the wall and the path back to the microphone. The product of speed and delay therefore gives $2d$.

$$
d=\frac{u\Delta t}{2}=\frac{340\cdot0.42}{2}\,\mathrm m=71.4\,\mathrm m.
$$
