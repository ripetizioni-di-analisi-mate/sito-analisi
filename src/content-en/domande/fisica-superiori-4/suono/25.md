Two sound waves of the same frequency and amplitude arrive at a microphone in opposite phase. What is the amplitude of the resulting pressure variation at that point?

- Twice that of one wave.
- The same as that of one wave.
- Zero.
- Four times that of one wave.

## Explanation

At every instant a positive pressure variation is accompanied by an equal negative one. Superposition applies to the pressure variations, so their sum is zero at the point.
