A stationary source emits at $750\,\mathrm{Hz}$. One observer approaches at $34\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and another recedes at the same speed, both along their line to the source. The air is still and $u=340\,\mathrm{m/s}$. By how much do they differ in received frequency?

- $75\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $167\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $0\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $150\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

Both observers receive the same emitted spacing. Their encounter speeds are $u+v$ and $u-v$. Subtracting gives $2v$, so the difference is twice the shift from the emitted tone.

$$
f'_+-f'_- = f\frac{(u+v)-(u-v)}{u}=750\frac{2\cdot34}{340}\,\mathrm{Hz}=150\,\mathrm{Hz}.
$$
