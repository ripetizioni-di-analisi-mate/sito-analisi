A source moves toward an observer at the speed of sound in the medium. Why does the subsonic Doppler formula from the chapter not give a finite frequency?

- The stationary observer makes the numerator zero.
- The subsonic calculation of wavefront spacing gives zero in the denominator.
- Wavefront propagation speed falls to zero.
- The period of the source becomes zero.

## Explanation

When $v_s=u$, in one period the source reaches the position of the preceding front along the direction of motion. The subsonic formula gives $\lambda'=(u-v_s)T=0$, so its requirement of separated fronts fails.
