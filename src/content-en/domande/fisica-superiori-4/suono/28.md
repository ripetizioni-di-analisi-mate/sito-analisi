The distance to a wall is fixed. An initial echo takes $0.50\,\mathrm s$ at a sound speed of $320\,\mathrm{m/s}$. With a different gas between the source and wall, the sound speed is $400\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What delay is now measured?

- $0.625\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.500\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.400\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.200\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

The path length is $2d$ in both measurements. Because the length is fixed, the time decreases in the inverse ratio of the speeds.

$$
\Delta t_2=\frac{u_1}{u_2}\Delta t_1=\frac{320}{400}\cdot0.50\,\mathrm s=0.400\,\mathrm s.
$$
