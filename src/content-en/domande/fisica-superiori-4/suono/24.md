Each machine operating alone produces the same intensity at the microphone. The machines are independent. How many operating machines raise the level about $9.03\,\mathrm{dB}$ above the level from one?

- $8$
- $2$
- $4$
- $16$

## Explanation

With $N$ units, the total intensity is $NI_1$. Its ratio to one machine is therefore $N$. The result corresponds to three doublings of about $3.01\,\mathrm{dB}$ each.

$$
\Delta\beta=10\log_{10}N\,\mathrm{dB},\qquad N=10^{\frac{9.03}{10}}\simeq8.
$$
