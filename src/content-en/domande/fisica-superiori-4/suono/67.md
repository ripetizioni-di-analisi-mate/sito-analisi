A light, steady uniform wind blows along the line between a fixed loudspeaker and a fixed microphone. The readings have settled. What occurs for a steady periodic emission?

- Frequency increases for either wind direction.
- Frequency decreases for either wind direction.
- The speed relative to the ground is the same in both directions.
- Travel time can change, while received frequency stays equal to emitted frequency.

## Explanation

With steady wind, every wavefront takes the same time to travel from source to microphone. Wind can change that common time, but successive fronts arrive at the same interval at which they were emitted.
