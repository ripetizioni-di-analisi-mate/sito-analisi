A snapshot of the wave gives the positions of four consecutive maxima. The first is at $0.30\,\mathrm m$ and the fourth at $2.10\,\mathrm m$. The frequency is $550\,\mathrm{Hz}$. What is the wave speed?

- $248\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $990\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $385\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $330\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Four maxima mark three intervals. Divide by three and then multiply the wavelength by the frequency.

$$
\lambda=\frac{2.10-0.30}{3}\,\mathrm m=0.60\,\mathrm m,\qquad u=\lambda f=330\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
