A stationary source emits at $600\,\mathrm{Hz}$. An observer approaches at $34\,\mathrm{m/s}$, with $u=340\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What frequency does the observer receive?

- $540\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $660\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $667\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $600\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

With a stationary source, wavefront spacing stays fixed. The observer moves toward the fronts, so they are crossed at speed $u+v_o$.

$$
f'=f\frac{u+v_o}{u}=600\frac{340+34}{340}\,\mathrm{Hz}=660\,\mathrm{Hz}.
$$
