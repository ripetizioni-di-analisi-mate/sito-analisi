One source alone gives $50\,\mathrm{dB}$. Adding an independent source raises the level to $60\,\mathrm{dB}$. What is $\dfrac{I_2}{I_1}$, where $I_2$ is due to the second source alone?

- $9$
- $10$
- $1$
- $60$

## Explanation

The final reading includes both sources. A ten-decibel rise gives a total intensity ten times that of the first. Subtract the first contribution to isolate the second.

$$
1+\frac{I_2}{I_1}=10^{\frac{60-50}{10}}=10\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \frac{I_2}{I_1}=9.
$$
