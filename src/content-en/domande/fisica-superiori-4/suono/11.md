An isotropic point source produces intensity $I_1$ at $2\,\mathrm m$. In a free field with no absorption, at what distance does intensity equal $\dfrac{I_1}{9}$?

- $18.00\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6.00\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.67\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3.46\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Power stays constant. Intensity varies inversely with the square of distance, so the distance ratio is the square root of the inverse intensity ratio.

$$
\frac{I_1}{I_2}=\left(\frac{r_2}{r_1}\right)^2=9\quad\Longrightarrow\quad r_2=2\sqrt9\,\mathrm m=6.00\,\mathrm m.
$$
