Eight independent machines contribute the same intensity at a point. Six are switched off. How much does the level change?

- $-6.02\,\mathrm{dB}$
- $6.02\,\mathrm{dB}$
- $-1.25\,\mathrm{dB}$
- $0.00\,\mathrm{dB}$

## Explanation

Count the two machines that remain on. The intensity ratio is two to eight, corresponding to two halvings. Each reduces the level by about three decibels.

$$
\Delta\beta=10\log_{10}\!\left(\frac{2}{8}\right)\,\mathrm{dB}\simeq-6.02\,\mathrm{dB}.
$$
