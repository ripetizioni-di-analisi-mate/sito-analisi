A microphone in front of an instrument measures sound components at $180$, $360$ and $540\,\mathrm{Hz}$. All three lines are significant. Which description is compatible with this spectrum?

- A pure tone at $360\,\mathrm{Hz}$.
- A $540\,\mathrm{Hz}$ fundamental and two higher harmonics.
- A complex sound with a $180\,\mathrm{Hz}$ fundamental.
- Three frequencies with no harmonic relationship.

## Explanation

The frequencies are the first, second and third multiples of $180\,\mathrm{Hz}$. Multiple components distinguish this signal from a pure tone.
