An ideal pipe open at both ends is $1.50\,\mathrm m$ long. In its fifth mode, what is the wavelength of the displacement pattern?

- $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.75\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.60\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The fifth mode fits five half wavelengths. One half-wave is one fifth of $L$, so the full wavelength is twice that.

$$
L=5\frac{\lambda_5}{2}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\lambda_5=\frac{2\cdot1.50}{5}\,\mathrm m=0.60\,\mathrm m.
$$
