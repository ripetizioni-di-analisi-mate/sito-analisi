A sound intensity level of $0\,\mathrm{dB}$ is reported. What does it mean relative to the reference $I_0$?

- $I=0$.
- $I=I_0$.
- $I=10I_0$.
- $I$ has a negative value.

## Explanation

The base-ten logarithm is zero when its argument is one. Thus $\beta=0$ means $\dfrac{I}{I_0}=1$.
