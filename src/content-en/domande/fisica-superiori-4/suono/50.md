A complete scan from the bottom of the spectrum finds the first three peaks of an ideal pipe at $120$, $360$ and $600\,\mathrm{Hz}$. Which end conditions explain this sequence?

- Both ends open.
- Both ends closed.
- One open end and one closed end.
- The peaks cannot come from one ideal pipe.

## Explanation

The first three ratios to the fundamental are $1$, $3$ and $5$. Matching boundary conditions would also allow the first even multiple. The observed sequence is that of a pipe with a displacement node at one end and an antinode at the other.
