Intensity is $10^{-14}\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$ and the reference is $10^{-12}\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$. What is the level?

- $20\,\mathrm{dB}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{dB}$
- $0\,\mathrm{dB}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{dB}$

## Explanation

The intensity is positive. Its ratio to the reference is below one, so its logarithm and the level are negative.

$$
\beta=10\log_{10}(10^{-2})\,\mathrm{dB}=-20\,\mathrm{dB}.
$$
