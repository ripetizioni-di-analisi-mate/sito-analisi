A meter records intensity $5I_0$ with a source on and $I_0$ with it off. The source and constant background are independent. What is the level of the source alone relative to $I_0$? Use $\log_{10}2\simeq0.30$.

- $4\,\mathrm{dB}$
- $12\,\mathrm{dB}$
- $0\,\mathrm{dB}$
- $6\,\mathrm{dB}$

## Explanation

Independent intensities add. Subtracting the background leaves $4I_0$. The requested level is $10\log_{10}4=20\log_{10}2\simeq6\,\mathrm{dB}$.
