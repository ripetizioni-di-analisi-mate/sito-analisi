The microphone receives a local $440\,\mathrm{Hz}$ tone and the tone from a receding source that also emits at $440\,\mathrm{Hz}$. The spacing of fronts from the receding source is $0.80\,\mathrm m$ and $u=344\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the beat frequency?

- $10\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $0\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $430\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $20\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

Beats depend on the frequencies reaching the same microphone. Use the incoming front spacing to find the received frequency from the moving source, then take the difference.

$$
f'_2=\frac{344}{0.80}\,\mathrm{Hz}=430\,\mathrm{Hz},\qquad f_b=|440-430|\,\mathrm{Hz}=10\,\mathrm{Hz}.
$$
