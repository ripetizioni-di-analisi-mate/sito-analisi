An ideal pipe open at both ends has a $150\,\mathrm{Hz}$ fundamental. A second ideal pipe closed at one end has a $100\,\mathrm{Hz}$ fundamental. What is the lowest resonance frequency they share?

- $300\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $150\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $200\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $600\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

The first pipe allows $150$, $300$, $450\,\mathrm{Hz}$ and higher multiples. The second allows $100$, $300$, $500\,\mathrm{Hz}$ and only odd multiples. The first value in both sequences is $300\,\mathrm{Hz}$.

$$
2\cdot150\,\mathrm{Hz}=3\cdot100\,\mathrm{Hz}=300\,\mathrm{Hz}.
$$
