A $440\,\mathrm{Hz}$ tone and a higher tone produce intensity maxima every $0.125\,\mathrm s$. What is the frequency of the second tone?

- $448.00\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $432.00\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $440.12\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $456.00\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

The interval between maxima is the beat period. Its reciprocal gives the frequency difference. The second tone is higher, so add that difference.

$$
f_b=\frac{1}{0.125\,\mathrm s}=8\,\mathrm{Hz},\qquad f_2=(440+8)\,\mathrm{Hz}=448.00\,\mathrm{Hz}.
$$
