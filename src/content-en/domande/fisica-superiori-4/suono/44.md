In an ideal pipe open at both ends, the third harmonic has a displacement maximum at $x=0$. Which set lists all the interior displacement nodes?

- $0,\ \dfrac{L}{3},\ \dfrac{2L}{3},\ L$
- $\dfrac{L}{3},\ \dfrac{2L}{3}$
- $\dfrac{L}{6},\ \dfrac{L}{2},\ \dfrac{5L}{6}$
- $\dfrac{L}{4},\ \dfrac{3L}{4}$

## Explanation

In an open pipe, the third harmonic has $\lambda=\dfrac{2L}{3}$. The first node lies one quarter wavelength from the antinode at $x=0$. Nodes then recur every $\dfrac{\lambda}{2}=\dfrac L3$.
