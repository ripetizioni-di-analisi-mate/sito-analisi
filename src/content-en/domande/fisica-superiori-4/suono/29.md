A source and microphone are on the same line perpendicular to a wall. The microphone is between the source and the wall, $20\,\mathrm m$ from the source. Its echo arrives $0.20\,\mathrm s$ after the direct signal, with $u=340\,\mathrm{m/s}$. How far is the microphone from the wall?

::grafico{xmin=-0.7 xmax=6.4 ymin=-1 ymax=1.4 assi=0 linee="5.8,-0.65 5.8,1" punti="0,0,pieno;2,0,pieno" segmenti="0,0,5.8,0" etichette="0,0.43,S;2,0.43,M;5.8,1.17,P;1,-0.49,20 m;3.9,-0.49,d" titolo="S is the source, M the microphone, and P the wall."}

- $68\,\mathrm{m}$
- $54\,\mathrm{m}$
- $34\,\mathrm{m}$
- $14\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The direct path is $20\,\mathrm m$. The reflected path contains that segment and twice the distance from the microphone to the wall, so the delay between arrivals corresponds to $2d$.

$$
d=\frac{u\Delta t}{2}=\frac{340\cdot0.20}{2}\,\mathrm m=34\,\mathrm m.
$$
