An ideal pipe open at both ends is $1.20\,\mathrm m$ long. A pipe closed at one end must have the same fundamental in the same air. How long must the second pipe be?

- $1.20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.40\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.60\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

At the same frequency and speed the wavelengths are equal. Half a wavelength fits in the open pipe and a quarter fits in the closed pipe.

$$
\frac{u}{2L_A}=\frac{u}{4L_B}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad L_B=\frac{1.20}{2}\,\mathrm m=0.60\,\mathrm m.
$$
