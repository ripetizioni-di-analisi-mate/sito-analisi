The level is $72\,\mathrm{dB}$, with a reference of $10^{-12}\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$. Which expression gives the intensity?

- $10^{-12}\cdot10^{72}\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $72\cdot10^{-12}\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $10^{-12}\cdot10^{7.2}\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $10^{-12}\cdot10^{3.6}\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$

## Explanation

Dividing $72$ by $10$ gives $\log_{10}\dfrac{I}{I_0}=7.2$. Raising $10$ to this power yields the ratio $\dfrac{I}{I_0}$. Then multiply by $I_0$.
