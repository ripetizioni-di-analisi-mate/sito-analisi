A stationary point source emits a steady tone in a free field. A stationary microphone moves toward it and then stops closer to it. How do the initial and final readings compare after the signal has settled in each case?

- Final frequency is higher and intensity stays the same.
- The frequency stays the same and final intensity is higher.
- Both increase.
- Both stay the same.

## Explanation

Both readings are taken with the microphone stationary, so there is no Doppler shift. The final position is at a smaller radius, and at fixed power $I$ increases as $\dfrac{1}{r^2}$.
