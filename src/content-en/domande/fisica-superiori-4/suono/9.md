A source emits sound in two media separated by a stationary interface. In the first, $u_1=340\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $\lambda_1=0.40\,\mathrm m$. In the second, $\lambda_2=1.60\,\mathrm m$. What is $u_2$?

- $85\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1360\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $340\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $680\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The source sets a common frequency for the two media. Since $u=\lambda f$, the speeds are in the same ratio as the wavelengths.

$$
u_2=u_1\frac{\lambda_2}{\lambda_1}=340\frac{1.60}{0.40}\,\mathrm{m/s}=1360\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
