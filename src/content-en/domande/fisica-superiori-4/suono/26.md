Two traveling harmonic waves are compared in the same medium. The second wave's pressure amplitude is three times that of the first. What is $\dfrac{I_2}{I_1}$?

- $9.00$
- $3.00$
- $1.73$
- $0.11$

## Explanation

For a fixed medium, the intensity of a traveling wave is proportional to the square of its pressure amplitude. The ratio is therefore the square of three.

$$
\frac{I_2}{I_1}=\left(\frac{\Delta p_{2,\max}}{\Delta p_{1,\max}}\right)^2=3^2=9.00.
$$
