An ideal uniform pipe open at both ends has a fundamental frequency of $320\,\mathrm{Hz}$. One end is closed, with the length and sound speed unchanged. What is the new fundamental?

- $320\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $640\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $160\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $80\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

Before closing the end, the pipe fits half a wavelength. In the new fundamental it fits a quarter wavelength. The wavelength doubles, so the frequency halves.

$$
f_{1,\mathrm{B}}=\frac{u}{4L}=\frac12\cdot320\,\mathrm{Hz}=160\,\mathrm{Hz}.
$$
