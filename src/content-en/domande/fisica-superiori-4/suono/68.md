The diagram shows the fronts emitted by a moving source S. Points A and B are stationary in the medium. What frequencies do they receive before the source reaches either point?

::grafico{xmin=-5.4 xmax=5.4 ymin=-1.25 ymax=1.25 assi=0 punti="-4.9,0,pieno;0,0,pieno;4.9,0,pieno" vettori="0.45,0.67,2.3,0.67" linee="-4,-0.45 -4,0.45;-2.45,-0.45 -2.45,0.45;-0.9,-0.45 -0.9,0.45;0.75,-0.45 0.75,0.45;1.47,-0.45 1.47,0.45;2.19,-0.45 2.19,0.45;2.91,-0.45 2.91,0.45;3.63,-0.45 3.63,0.45;4.35,-0.45 4.35,0.45" etichette="-4.9,0.63,A;0,-0.68,S;4.9,0.63,B" titolo="Wavefront section along the direction of motion."}

- At A it is higher and at B lower than the emitted frequency.
- At both points it is higher than the emitted frequency.
- At both points it equals the emitted frequency.
- At B it is higher and at A lower than the emitted frequency.

## Explanation

The motion is toward B. Fronts on the right are closer and those on the left farther apart than for a stationary source. Since the fronts travel at the same speed in the medium, the microphone on the right counts them more often, and the one on the left less often.
