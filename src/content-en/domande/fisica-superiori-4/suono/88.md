An ideal pipe is closed at $x=0$ and open at $x=L$. In the second allowed mode, where are the pressure nodes?

- $x=\dfrac{L}{3}$ and $x=L$.
- $x=0$ and $x=\dfrac{2L}{3}$.
- Only $x=L$.
- $x=0$ and $x=L$.

## Explanation

The second mode fits three quarter wavelengths, so $\lambda=\dfrac{4L}{3}$. The closed wall is a pressure antinode. The first node is $\dfrac{\lambda}{4}=\dfrac L3$ away and the next is a further $\dfrac{\lambda}{2}=\dfrac{2L}{3}$ away.
