A source and microphone are stationary at the same point. The source emits at $600\,\mathrm{Hz}$ along the normal to a wall moving toward them at $40\,\mathrm{m/s}$, in still air with $u=340\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Treat the wall first as a receiver and then as a reflecting source. What is the echo frequency?

- $760\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $474\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $600\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $1200\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

The approaching wall meets the fronts faster than a stationary receiver would. It reflects them at the same rate in its own frame, and its approach sends closely spaced fronts to the microphone. Multiply the two factors.

$$
f_{mathrm{parete}}=600\frac{340+40}{340}\,\mathrm{Hz},\qquad f'=f_{mathrm{parete}}\frac{340}{340-40}=760\,\mathrm{Hz}.
$$
