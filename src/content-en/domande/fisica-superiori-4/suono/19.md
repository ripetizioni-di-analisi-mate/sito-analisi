A periodic sound has its second harmonic at $320\,\mathrm{Hz}$. Its fundamental is raised by one octave. What is the new third-harmonic frequency?

- $960\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $1920\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $480\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $640\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

First find the fundamental by dividing by the order of the known harmonic. An octave doubles it, and the new third harmonic is three times the new fundamental.

$$
f_{1,A}=\frac{320}{2}\,\mathrm{Hz},\qquad f_{3,B}=3\cdot2\cdot\frac{320}{2}\,\mathrm{Hz}=960\,\mathrm{Hz}.
$$
