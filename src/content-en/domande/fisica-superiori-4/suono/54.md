In still air, a source and observer move to the right along the same line at the same subsonic speed. The observer is ahead of the source. What frequency does the observer receive relative to the emitted frequency?

- The same frequency.
- A higher frequency.
- A lower frequency.
- No front reaches the observer.

## Explanation

The source moves toward the observer, so $v_s=v$. The observer moves away from the source, so $v_o=-v$. Both numerator and denominator in the Doppler relation are $u-v$.
