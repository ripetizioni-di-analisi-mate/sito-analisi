A stationary microphone records the pressure variation shown in the graph. What frequency does it measure?

::grafico{f="cos(pi*(x-2)/2)" xmin=0 xmax=8 ymin=-1.3 ymax=1.5 nomi="t,Δp (Pa)" punti="2,1,pieno;6,1,pieno" segmenti="2,0,2,1;6,0,6,1" legenda="\Delta p(t)" titolo="Recording at a fixed point. Time is in milliseconds."}

- $167\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $250\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $500\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $125\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

Read consecutive maxima at $2$ and $6\,\mathrm{ms}$. The horizontal axis is time and the measurement is at one point, so their separation is a period.

$$
T=(6-2)\,\mathrm{ms}=4\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm s,\qquad f=\frac{1}{T}=250\,\mathrm{Hz}.
$$
