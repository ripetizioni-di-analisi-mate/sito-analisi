A very small microphone is placed at the open end of an ideal sounding pipe, where there is a displacement antinode. What feature of the pressure variation is expected at that point?

- A pressure node.
- A pressure antinode.
- The same pressure amplitude as at a closed wall.
- Zero absolute pressure.

## Explanation

At the opening, pressure stays near the outside pressure. Its variation about the mean is small, so there is a pressure node.
