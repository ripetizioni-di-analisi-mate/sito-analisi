The second of two sounds has a level $20\,\mathrm{dB}$ above the first. Both use the same reference. What is the ratio $\dfrac{I_2}{I_1}$?

- $20$
- $2$
- $100$
- $10$

## Explanation

The levels can be subtracted because they share a reference. Divide the difference by $10\,\mathrm{dB}$ and apply the inverse of the base-ten logarithm.

$$
\frac{I_2}{I_1}=10^{\frac{20}{10}}=100.
$$
