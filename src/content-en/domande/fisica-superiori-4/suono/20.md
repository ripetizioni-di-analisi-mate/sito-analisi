The power of an isotropic point source triples and the microphone's distance doubles. In a free field with no absorption, what is $\dfrac{I_2}{I_1}$?

- $1.50$
- $0.25$
- $12.00$
- $0.75$

## Explanation

Both power and area change between the two spheres. The new area is four times the original, so tripling the power still leaves a lower intensity.

$$
\frac{I_2}{I_1}=\frac{P_2}{P_1}\left(\frac{r_1}{r_2}\right)^2=3\cdot\frac14=0.75.
$$
