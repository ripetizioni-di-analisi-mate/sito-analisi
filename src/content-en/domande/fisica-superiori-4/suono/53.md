A source emits at $500\,\mathrm{Hz}$ and moves through still air at $40\,\mathrm{m/s}$. With $u=340\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the spacing between successive fronts ahead of the source?

- $0.76\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.68\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.08\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.60\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Between emissions the first front advances by $uT$ and the new front starts $v_sT$ farther forward. Their spacing is the difference between those distances.

$$
\lambda'=\frac{u-v_s}{f}=\frac{340-40}{500}\,\mathrm m=0.60\,\mathrm m.
$$
