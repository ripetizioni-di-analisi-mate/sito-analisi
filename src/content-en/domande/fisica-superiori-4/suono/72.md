An isotropic point source in a free field gives a certain level at $5\,\mathrm m$. At what distance is the level $20\,\mathrm{dB}$ lower, with no absorption?

- $500.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $50.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $10.0\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

A twenty-decibel decrease corresponds to an intensity one hundred times smaller. Because intensity varies as the inverse square of distance, the radius increases by a factor of ten.

$$
\frac{I_2}{I_1}=10^{\frac{-20}{10}}=\frac{1}{100},\qquad r_2=5\sqrt{100}\,\mathrm m=50.0\,\mathrm m.
$$
