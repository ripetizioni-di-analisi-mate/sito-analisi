A source emits at $660\,\mathrm{Hz}$ and a stationary observer receives $600\,\mathrm{Hz}$. Motion is along the joining line, in air with $u=330\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the component $v_s$, positive toward the observer?

- $33.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $30.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-33.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $-66.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

With the observer fixed, the numerator is $u$. Solve for $v_s$ in the denominator. The received frequency is lower, so spacing is greater and the sign must indicate recession.

$$
v_s=u\left(1-\frac{f}{f'}\right)=330\left(1-\frac{660}{600}\right)\,\mathrm{m/s}=-33.0\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
