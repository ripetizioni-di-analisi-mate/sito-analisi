A sound source moving at subsonic speed changes the spacing of the wavefronts ahead of it. In still uniform air, at what speed do the fronts travel once emitted?

- At $u+v_s$ relative to the air.
- At $u-v_s$ relative to the air.
- At $u$ relative to the air.
- At the source speed.

## Explanation

The emission position changes between wavefronts. After emission each front travels through the medium at a speed set by the properties of the air.
