Two instruments have fundamental frequency $250\,\mathrm{Hz}$. One has a much stronger $750\,\mathrm{Hz}$ component than the other. Which observation is compatible with these data?

- The second harmonic is at $750\,\mathrm{Hz}$.
- The fundamental is shared and the timbre can differ.
- Both emit a pure tone.
- The sound with more harmonics travels faster.

## Explanation

The $750\,\mathrm{Hz}$ component is the third harmonic because $750=3\cdot250$. A different relative amplitude for this component changes the spectrum and can change the timbre.
