For two tones of very close frequencies, only one amplitude is doubled while both frequencies stay fixed. How does the number of beats per second change?

- It doubles.
- It stays the same.
- It halves.
- It becomes zero.

## Explanation

Amplitude affects the depth of the envelope minimum. The number of maxima per second depends on how often the phases align again, so it is set by the frequency difference.
