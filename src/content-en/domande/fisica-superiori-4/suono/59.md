Two trials use the same subsonic approach speed and the same tone. In the first only the source moves. In the second only the observer moves. Which relation holds between the received frequencies?

- It is higher in the second trial.
- It is the same in both trials.
- It is higher in the first trial.
- It is below the emitted frequency in both trials.

## Explanation

For $a=\dfrac{v}{u}$, with $0<a<1$, the Doppler factors are $\dfrac{1}{1-a}$ and $1+a$. Since $(1-a)(1+a)=1-a^2<1$, the first factor is greater.
