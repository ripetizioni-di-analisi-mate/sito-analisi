An ideal uniform pipe is $0.90\,\mathrm m$ long and open at both ends. With $u=340\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is its fundamental frequency?

- $94\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $378\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $126\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $189\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

In the first mode the displacement antinodes at the ends are half a wavelength apart. It follows that $\lambda_1=2L$.

$$
f_1=\frac{u}{2L}=\frac{340}{2\cdot0.90}\,\mathrm{Hz}\simeq189\,\mathrm{Hz}.
$$
