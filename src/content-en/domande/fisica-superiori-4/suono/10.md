Two reflections of the same pulse return to the emission point after $0.20\,\mathrm s$ and $0.50\,\mathrm s$. With $u=340\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the difference in distance to the two reflecting surfaces?

- $102\,\mathrm{m}$
- $51\,\mathrm{m}$
- $119\,\mathrm{m}$
- $85\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The difference in delays comes from the difference between two round trips. Subtract the delays and divide by two to obtain the difference in distances.

$$
d_2-d_1=\frac{340(0.50-0.20)}{2}\,\mathrm m=51\,\mathrm m.
$$
