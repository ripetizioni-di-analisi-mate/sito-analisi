Two tones of equal amplitude have frequencies $500\,\mathrm{Hz}$ and $504\,\mathrm{Hz}$. An envelope maximum occurs at $t=0$. When does the first minimum occur?

- $0.250\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.001\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.500\,\mathrm{s}$
- $0.125\,\mathrm{s}$

## Explanation

At the maximum the oscillations are in phase. For the first minimum their phase difference must grow by half a cycle, which takes half a beat period.

$$
T_b=\frac{1}{4}\,\mathrm s,\qquad t_{\min}=\frac{T_b}{2}=0.125\,\mathrm s.
$$
