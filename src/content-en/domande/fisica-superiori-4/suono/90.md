In the same medium, a direct measurement between two microphones $34\,\mathrm m$ apart on the same ray of propagation gives a delay of $0.10\,\mathrm s$. Another sensor, with its emitter and receiver at the same point, receives an echo after $0.24\,\mathrm s$. How far is that sensor from the wall?

- $81.6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $163.2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $40.8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $23.8\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The first delay comes from one-way travel, so it gives the speed without a factor of two. In the second experiment the echo includes the outward trip and return. Take half the new path length.

$$
u=\frac{34}{0.10}\,\mathrm{m/s}=340\,\mathrm{m/s},\qquad d=\frac{340\cdot0.24}{2}\,\mathrm m=40.8\,\mathrm m.
$$
