An isotropic point source multiplies its power by $100$. At the same time the microphone is placed ten times farther away. How much does the level change in a free field?

- $20\,\mathrm{dB}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{dB}$
- $0\,\mathrm{dB}$
- $10\,\mathrm{dB}$

## Explanation

The spherical area grows by the same factor as the power. The intensity ratio is one before converting to the logarithmic scale.

$$
\frac{I_2}{I_1}=\frac{100}{10^2}=1,\qquad\Delta\beta=10\log_{10}(1)\,\mathrm{dB}=0\,\mathrm{dB}.
$$
