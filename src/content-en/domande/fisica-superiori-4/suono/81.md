Two identical independent isotropic point sources are $2\,\mathrm m$ and $4\,\mathrm m$ from a microphone in a free field. What is $\dfrac{I_{\mathrm{tot}}}{I_1}$, where $I_1$ is the intensity of the nearer source alone?

- $2.00$
- $1.50$
- $1.25$
- $0.25$

## Explanation

Because the sources emit the same power, their intensity ratio follows from the inverse squared distance ratio. Independence then allows the two contributions to be added.

$$
\frac{I_2}{I_1}=\left(\frac24\right)^2=\frac14,\qquad \frac{I_{\mathrm{tot}}}{I_1}=1+\frac14=1.25.
$$
