The graph shows the envelope amplitude of two tones. The horizontal axis is in milliseconds. What is the beat frequency?

::grafico{f="2*abs(cos(pi*(x-125)/250))" xmin=0 xmax=500 ymin=0 ymax=2.6 nomi="t,A (Pa)" punti="125,2,pieno;375,2,pieno" segmenti="125,0,125,2;375,0,375,2" etichette="128,2.13,125 ms;378,2.13,375 ms" legenda="A(t)" titolo="Envelope amplitude over time."}

- $2.0\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $4.0\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $8.0\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $2.7\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

The envelope has successive marked maxima at $0.125$ and $0.375\,\mathrm s$. Their time separation is the beat period.

$$
T_b=(0.375-0.125)\,\mathrm s=0.250\,\mathrm s,\qquad f_b=\frac1{T_b}=4.0\,\mathrm{Hz}.
$$
