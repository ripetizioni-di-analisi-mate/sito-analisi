Two tones produce an $8\,\mathrm{Hz}$ beat and their mean frequency is $440\,\mathrm{Hz}$. What is the lower frequency?

- $436\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $432\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $444\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $440\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

The mean lies halfway between the two frequencies. The lower one is therefore half the beat frequency below the mean.

$$
f_{\min}=\frac{f_1+f_2}{2}-\frac{f_b}{2}=(440-4)\,\mathrm{Hz}=436\,\mathrm{Hz}.
$$
