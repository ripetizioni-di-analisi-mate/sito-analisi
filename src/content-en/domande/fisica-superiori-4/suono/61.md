A source chases an observer on the same line at speeds $60\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$, respectively, in the same direction. The source emits at $420\,\mathrm{Hz}$ and $u=340\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What frequency reaches the observer ahead?

- $540\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $476\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $336\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $480\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

The source moves toward the receiver, so $v_s$ is positive. The observer moves in the same direction but its own motion carries it away from the source, so $v_o$ is negative. Both are measured separately relative to the air.

$$
f'=420\frac{340-20}{340-60}\,\mathrm{Hz}=480\,\mathrm{Hz}.
$$
