The first and seventh envelope maxima of two tones are $1.5\,\mathrm s$ apart. What is the beat frequency?

- $4.00\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $4.67\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $2.00\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

There are six complete intervals between the first and seventh maxima. Divide the number of intervals by the elapsed time.

$$
T_b=\frac{1.5}{6}\,\mathrm s,\qquad f_b=\frac{6}{1.5}\,\mathrm{Hz}=4.00\,\mathrm{Hz}.
$$
