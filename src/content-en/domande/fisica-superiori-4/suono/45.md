The fourth harmonic of an ideal pipe open at both ends is $800\,\mathrm{Hz}$. The pipe is shortened to three quarters of its initial length, with both ends still open. What is the new fourth harmonic?

- $600\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $800\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $1067\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $3200\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

Compare the same mode order before and after. Only the length changes in $f_4=\dfrac{4u}{2L}$, so frequency increases in the inverse ratio.

$$
f_4'=f_4\frac{L}{L'}=800\cdot\frac43\,\mathrm{Hz}\simeq1067\,\mathrm{Hz}.
$$
