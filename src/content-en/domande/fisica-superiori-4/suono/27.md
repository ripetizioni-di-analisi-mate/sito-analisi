Two microphones are on the same side of a source and in line with it. A direct pulse reaches the second $0.15\,\mathrm s$ after the first. Sound travels at $340\,\mathrm{m/s}$. How far apart are the microphones?

- $25.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $51.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $102.0\,\mathrm{m}$
- $17.0\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The pulse travels the extra segment only once, from the first microphone to the second. The whole delay is spent traveling that segment.

$$
r_2-r_1=u(t_2-t_1)=340\cdot0.15\,\mathrm m=51.0\,\mathrm m.
$$
