An ideal pipe closed at one end has fundamental frequency $150\,\mathrm{Hz}$. How many resonances are at or below $1050\,\mathrm{Hz}$?

- $7$
- $3$
- $5$
- $4$

## Explanation

The cutoff divided by the fundamental is seven. Count only the odd multiples $1$, $3$, $5$ and $7$, including the one at the cutoff.

$$
(2n-1)\cdot150\,\mathrm{Hz}\leq1050\,\mathrm{Hz}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad n\leq4.
$$
