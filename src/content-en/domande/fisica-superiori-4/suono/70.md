A person standing $51\,\mathrm m$ from a wall claps and hears the echo after $0.30\,\mathrm s$. In the same air a tuning fork emits a $425\,\mathrm{Hz}$ sound. What is the wavelength of this sound?

- $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.40\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.60\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.25\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The sound of the clap travels there and back, that is $2\cdot51=102\,\mathrm m$ in $0.30\,\mathrm s$, so the speed of sound in the air is $340\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The sound of the tuning fork travels through the same air, so it has the same speed. The wavelength is therefore the ratio of the speed to the frequency.

$$
u=\frac{2\cdot51}{0.30}\,\mathrm{m/s}=340\,\mathrm{m/s},\qquad \lambda=\frac{340}{425}\,\mathrm m=0.80\,\mathrm m.
$$
