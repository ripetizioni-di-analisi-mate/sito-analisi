A sensor $51\,\mathrm m$ from a wall receives the echo of its own pulse after $0.30\,\mathrm s$. In the same medium a stationary source emits a $425\,\mathrm{Hz}$ tone. What is the wavelength?

- $0.80\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.40\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.60\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.25\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The pulse gives the speed in the medium because its known path is twice the distance. For the periodic wave, divide that speed by the number of cycles per second.

$$
u=\frac{2\cdot51}{0.30}\,\mathrm{m/s}=340\,\mathrm{m/s},\qquad \lambda=\frac{340}{425}\,\mathrm m=0.80\,\mathrm m.
$$
