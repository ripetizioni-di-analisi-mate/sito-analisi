The same harmonic sound is measured on either side of a stationary boundary between two media. Sound travels more slowly in the second medium. What do time traces from two stationary microphones show?

- Twice the interval in the second medium.
- A shorter interval in the second medium.
- A longer interval in the second medium.
- The same interval between maxima on both sides.

## Explanation

The source sets the frequency in both media and the boundary is stationary. The lower speed changes the spatial spacing of the fronts, while maxima pass each fixed point with the same period.
