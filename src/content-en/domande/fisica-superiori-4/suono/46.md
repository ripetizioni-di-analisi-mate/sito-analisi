In a vertical pipe, water acts as a closed end and the top is open. The initial air column is $0.80\,\mathrm m$ long. The water is raised by $0.30\,\mathrm m$, with air conditions unchanged. By what factor does the fundamental change?

- $1.600$
- $0.625$
- $2.667$
- $1.375$

## Explanation

Only the air column participates in this resonance in the model. After the water is raised, the column is half a meter long, so the quarter-wave fundamental shortens and the frequency rises.

$$
\frac{f_{\mathrm{dopo}}}{f_{\mathrm{prima}}}=\frac{L_{\mathrm{prima}}}{L_{\mathrm{dopo}}}=\frac{0.80}{0.50}=1.600.
$$
