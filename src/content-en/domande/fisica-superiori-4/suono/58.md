A source passes a stationary observer along the line joining them. Far from the crossing, the approach frequency is $\dfrac32$ times the recession frequency. With $u=340\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is the source speed?

- $68\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $170\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $113\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $57\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

Divide the two received frequencies. The common factor $fu$ cancels. The denominators are inverted, placing $u+v$ in the numerator of the ratio.

$$
\frac{u+v}{u-v}=\frac32\quad\Longrightarrow\quad2u+2v=3u-3v\quad\Longrightarrow\quad v=\frac{u}{5}=68\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
