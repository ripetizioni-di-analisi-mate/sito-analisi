A small speaker emits a steady tone along a straight route past a stationary microphone. Far from the point of closest approach, its motion is nearly along the line to the microphone. Which sequence describes the recorded frequencies before and after it passes?

- Lower than the emitted frequency before, higher after.
- Higher than the emitted frequency before, lower after.
- Always equal to the emitted frequency.
- Always higher than the emitted frequency.

## Explanation

Before passage the microphone receives the more closely spaced fronts ahead of the source. After passage it receives the more widely spaced fronts behind it. At fixed propagation speed the first frequency is above the emitted frequency and the second below it.
