An ideal pipe closed at one end has two consecutive resonances at $250\,\mathrm{Hz}$ and $350\,\mathrm{Hz}$. With $u=340\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is its length?

- $1.70\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.85\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.34\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.24\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Only odd harmonics occur in a pipe closed at one end. Two adjacent modes are separated by twice the fundamental. The measured frequencies are the fifth and seventh multiples of $f_1$.

$$
f_1=\frac{350-250}{2}\,\mathrm{Hz}=50\,\mathrm{Hz},\qquad L=\frac{340}{4\cdot50}\,\mathrm m=1.70\,\mathrm m.
$$
