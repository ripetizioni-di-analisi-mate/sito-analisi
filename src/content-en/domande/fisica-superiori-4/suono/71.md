A stationary pipe has a fundamental frequency of $400\,\mathrm{Hz}$. A $360\,\mathrm{Hz}$ source approaches along its axis. In still air with $u=340\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what source speed makes the received frequency match the fundamental?

- $34.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $37.8\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $40.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $68.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The pipe responds to the frequency that reaches it. Set it equal to the mode frequency with the receiver fixed. The result is positive because the received frequency must rise.

$$
400=360\frac{340}{340-v_s}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad v_s=340\left(1-\frac{360}{400}\right)\,\mathrm{m/s}=34.0\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$
