An ideal uniform pipe closed at one end has fundamental frequency $125\,\mathrm{Hz}$. Which frequency cannot be that of one of its longitudinal modes?

- $125\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $375\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $250\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $625\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

$250\,\mathrm{Hz}$ is twice $125\,\mathrm{Hz}$. A pipe closed at one end must fit an odd number of quarter wavelengths to have a node at one end and an antinode at the other. Even multiples are therefore excluded.
