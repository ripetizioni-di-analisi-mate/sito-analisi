Two pure tones of comparable amplitudes have frequencies $440\,\mathrm{Hz}$ and $447\,\mathrm{Hz}$. What is the beat frequency?

- $443.5\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $7.0\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $14.0\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $887.0\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

In one second the higher tone gains seven cycles on the other one. The oscillations come back into phase seven times per second, producing seven maxima.

$$
f_b=|447-440|\,\mathrm{Hz}=7.0\,\mathrm{Hz}.
$$
