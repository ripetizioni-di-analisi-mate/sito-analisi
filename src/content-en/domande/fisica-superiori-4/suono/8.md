A $744\,\mathrm{Hz}$ source approaches a stationary observer at $18\,\mathrm{m/s}$ along the line between them. Sound travels at $340\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in still air. What frequency is received?

- $786\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $707\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $783\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $705\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

The source advances between emissions, bringing the fronts ahead of it closer together. Their spacing is $\dfrac{u-v_s}{f}$, and their speed relative to the stationary observer is $u$.

$$
f'=744\frac{340}{340-18}\,\mathrm{Hz}\simeq786\,\mathrm{Hz}.
$$
