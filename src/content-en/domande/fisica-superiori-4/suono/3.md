An isotropic point source emits $0.72\,\mathrm W$ of sound power. In a free field with no absorption, what is the intensity at $3\,\mathrm m$?

- $0.01910\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $0.00637\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $0.02546\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$
- $0.00159\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$

## Explanation

The same power passes through the sphere of radius $3\,\mathrm m$. Because the source is isotropic, divide it by the area of the entire sphere.

$$
I=\frac{P}{4\pi r^2}=\frac{0.72}{4\pi\cdot3^2}\,\mathrm{W/m^2}\simeq0.00637\,\mathrm{W/m^2}.
$$
