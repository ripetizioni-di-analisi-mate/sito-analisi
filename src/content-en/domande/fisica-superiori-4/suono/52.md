A $550\,\mathrm{Hz}$ source recedes at $34\,\mathrm{m/s}$ from a stationary observer. In still air $u=340\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What frequency is received?

- $611\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $500\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $550\,\mathrm{Hz}$
- $495\,\mathrm{Hz}$

## Explanation

The fronts reaching the observer are emitted backward and are farther apart because the source recedes between emissions. With the stated sign convention $v_s=-34\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

$$
f'=550\frac{340}{340+34}\,\mathrm{Hz}=500\,\mathrm{Hz}.
$$
