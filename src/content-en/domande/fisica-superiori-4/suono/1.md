A sound of frequency $600\,\mathrm{Hz}$ travels at $340\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is its wavelength?

- $1.765\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.283\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.133\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.567\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

In one period the front advances by one wavelength. The period is the reciprocal of the frequency, so the distance is $uT$.

$$
\lambda=\frac{u}{f}=\frac{340}{600}\,\mathrm m\simeq 0.567\,\mathrm m.
$$
