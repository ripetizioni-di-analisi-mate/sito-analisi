Two isotropic point sources A and B are $2\,\mathrm m$ and $6\,\mathrm m$ from a microphone, respectively. In a free field, with each source tested on its own, the farther one produces a quarter of the intensity of the other one. What is $\dfrac{P_B}{P_A}$?

- $2.25$
- $0.25$
- $0.75$
- $9.00$

## Explanation

The microphone measures local intensities. To recover power, multiply each reading by its spherical area. The second source's sphere is nine times larger, so its power is greater even though its intensity at the microphone is lower.

$$
\frac{P_B}{P_A}=\frac{I_B}{I_A}\left(\frac{r_B}{r_A}\right)^2=\frac14\cdot3^2=2.25.
$$
