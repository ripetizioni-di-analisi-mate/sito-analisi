In one reference $V_A=-18\,\mathrm V$ and $V_B=12\,\mathrm V$. The reference is changed so that $V_A'=0$. What is $V_B'$?

- $30\,\mathrm{V}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{V}$
- $12\,\mathrm{V}$
- $-30\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

Making the potential zero at A adds $18$ volts to every value. The difference between B and A remains the same as in the initial reference.

$$
V_B'=V_B-V_A=[12-(-18)]\,\mathrm V=30\,\mathrm V.
$$
