The same point charge, with zero at infinity, gives $V=-60\,\mathrm V$ at distance $r$. What is the potential at distance $\dfrac32r$?

- $-90\,\mathrm{V}$
- $-40\,\mathrm{V}$
- $40\,\mathrm{V}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

As distance increases, the magnitude decreases in proportion to inverse distance. The sign stays negative because the source is unchanged.

$$
V'=V\frac{r}{\dfrac32r}=\frac23(-60\,\mathrm V)=-40\,\mathrm V.
$$
