Two capacitors of $2$ and $5\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ are in parallel at $10\,\mathrm V$. What is the total charge on the plates connected to the positive node?

- $Q_{\mathrm{tot}}=50\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $Q_{\mathrm{tot}}=20\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $Q_{\mathrm{tot}}=70\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $Q_{\mathrm{tot}}=0\,\mathrm{\mu C}$

## Explanation

Each positive plate has the same voltage relative to the opposite node. To get the charge on that node, add the charges on the two connected plates.

$$
Q_{\mathrm{tot}}=(C_1+C_2)\Delta V=(2+5)10\,\mathrm{\mu C}=70\,\mathrm{\mu C}.
$$
