In an isolated parallel-plate capacitor the plate separation doubles, with area and material fixed. How does the field far from the edges change?

- It halves.
- It stays the same.
- It doubles.
- It quadruples.

## Explanation

The field depends on surface charge density and permittivity. With charge and area fixed, the density stays the same. The potential difference, proportional to the separation, doubles.
