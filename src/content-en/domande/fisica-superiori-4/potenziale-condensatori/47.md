An ideal capacitor with fixed geometry and dielectric goes from $4$ to $8\,\mathrm V$. What happens to its capacitance and one plate's charge?

- Both double.
- Capacitance doubles and charge stays the same.
- Capacitance halves and charge stays the same.
- Capacitance stays the same and charge magnitude doubles.

## Explanation

Capacitance is set by the geometry and material between the plates. At the same capacitance, the product $C\Delta V$ increases by the same factor as voltage.
