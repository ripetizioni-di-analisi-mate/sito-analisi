A $6\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ capacitor charged to $10\,\mathrm V$ is disconnected and placed in parallel with an initially uncharged $4\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ capacitor. The system remains isolated. What is the common final voltage?

- $4\,\mathrm{V}$
- $6\,\mathrm{V}$
- $10\,\mathrm{V}$
- $15\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

When the plates of the two capacitors are joined, charge can redistribute but stays the same on the combined, isolated node. In equilibrium both capacitors have the same voltage.

$$
\begin{aligned}
Q_{\mathrm{tot}}&=6\cdot10\,\mathrm{\mu C}=60\,\mathrm{\mu C}, \\[6pt]
\Delta V_f&=\frac{60}{6+4}\,\mathrm V=6\,\mathrm V.
\end{aligned}
$$
