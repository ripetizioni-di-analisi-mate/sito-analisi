An $8\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ capacitor is at $10\,\mathrm V$. Its voltage then falls to $5\,\mathrm V$ without a change in geometry. By how much does its energy decrease?

- $300\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $200\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $100\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $400\,\mathrm{\mu J}$

## Explanation

The final energy is one quarter of the initial energy because voltage halves at constant capacitance. The loss is therefore three quarters of the initial value.

$$
U_i-U_f=\frac12\cdot8(10^2-5^2)\,\mathrm{\mu J}=300\,\mathrm{\mu J}.
$$
