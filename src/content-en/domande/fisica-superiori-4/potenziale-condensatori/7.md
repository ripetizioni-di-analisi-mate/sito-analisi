Two initially uncharged capacitors of $3.6$ and $6\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ are connected in series. What is the equivalent capacitance?

- $C_{\mathrm{eq}}=2.25\,\mathrm{\mu F}$
- $C_{\mathrm{eq}}=3.6\,\mathrm{\mu F}$
- $C_{\mathrm{eq}}=9.6\,\mathrm{\mu F}$
- $C_{\mathrm{eq}}=1.5\,\mathrm{\mu F}$

## Explanation

In series the charge magnitude is common while the voltages add. The equivalent capacitance is therefore smaller than either individual capacitance.

$$
C_{\mathrm{eq}}=\frac{C_1C_2}{C_1+C_2}=\frac{3.6\cdot6}{9.6}\,\mathrm{\mu F}=2.25\,\mathrm{\mu F}.
$$
