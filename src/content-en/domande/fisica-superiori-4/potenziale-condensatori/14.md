A capacitor with an initially evacuated gap, at $18\,\mathrm V$ is disconnected from the source. The entire gap is filled with a dielectric of $\varepsilon_r=3$. What is the new voltage?

- $\Delta V'=6\,\mathrm{V}$
- $\Delta V'=18\,\mathrm{V}$
- $\Delta V'=54\,\mathrm{V}$
- $\Delta V'=2\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

Once disconnected, the capacitor retains its charge. The dielectric triples capacitance, so the voltage required to hold that same charge falls to one third.

$$
\Delta V'=\frac{Q}{3C}=\frac{18}{3}\,\mathrm V=6\,\mathrm V.
$$
