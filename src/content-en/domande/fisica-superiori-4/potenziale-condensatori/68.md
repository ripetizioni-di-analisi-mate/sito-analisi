Starting with three identical capacitors of capacitance $C$, two are placed in parallel and the third is put in series with the group. Which value is closest to the equivalent capacitance in units of $C$?

- $0.333$
- $1.5$
- $3$
- $0.667$

## Explanation

The parallel group has capacitance $2C$. The series combination of $2C$ and $C$ is their product divided by their sum.

$$
\frac{C_{\mathrm{eq}}}{C}=\frac{2C\cdot C}{(2C+C)C}=\frac23\simeq0.667.
$$
