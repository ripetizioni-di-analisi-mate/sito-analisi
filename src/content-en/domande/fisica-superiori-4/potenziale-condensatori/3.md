A charge $q=+3\,\mathrm{\mu C}$ goes from $120$ to $40\,\mathrm V$. How much work does the electric force do?

- $W=-240\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $W=240\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $W=120\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $W=360\,\mathrm{\mu J}$

## Explanation

For a positive charge, moving toward lower potential means losing potential energy. The work done by the force is the negative of that change, so it is positive.

$$
W=q(V_i-V_f)=3(120-40)\,\mathrm{\mu J}=240\,\mathrm{\mu J}.
$$
