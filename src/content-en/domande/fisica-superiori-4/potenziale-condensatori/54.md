Inserting a material that completely fills the gap between two plates changes the capacitance from $60$ to $240\,\mathrm{pF}$. If the gap was initially in vacuum, what is $\varepsilon_r$?

- $0.25$
- $3$
- $5$
- $4$

## Explanation

With area and separation fixed, capacitance is proportional to permittivity. The capacitance ratio therefore gives relative permittivity.

$$
\varepsilon_r=\frac{C'}{C_0}=\frac{240}{60}=4.
$$
