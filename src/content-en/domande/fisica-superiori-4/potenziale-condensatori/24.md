A charge $-8\,\mathrm{\mu C}$ is moved slowly from $20$ to $50\,\mathrm V$. What is the external work if its kinetic energy change is negligible?

- $240\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $-160\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $-240\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $-400\,\mathrm{\mu J}$

## Explanation

As potential increases, the negative charge loses potential energy. The electric force does positive work, and the external agent does negative work to limit the speed.

$$
W_{\mathrm{est}}=\Delta U=q(V_B-V_A)=(-8)(50-20)\,\mathrm{\mu J}=-240\,\mathrm{\mu J}.
$$
