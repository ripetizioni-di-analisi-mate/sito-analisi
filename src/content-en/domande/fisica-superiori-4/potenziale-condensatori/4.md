An electron at rest gains energy under the electric force alone while going from $0$ to $96\,\mathrm V$. What is its final kinetic energy in electronvolts?

- $K_f=-96\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $K_f=48\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $K_f=192\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $K_f=96\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

The electron has negative charge, so the electric force does positive work when potential increases. Its final kinetic energy equals that work because it starts at rest.

$$
K_f=(-e)(0-96\,\mathrm V)=96\,\mathrm{eV}.
$$
