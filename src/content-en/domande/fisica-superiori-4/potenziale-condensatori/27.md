At the midpoint of a dipole with equal opposite charges, what happens to $V$ and $\vec E$ when zero is at infinity?

- Both are zero.
- Potential is positive and the field is zero.
- $V=0$ and the field points toward the negative charge.
- The field points toward the positive charge and potential is zero.

## Explanation

Equal distances make the scalar potential contributions opposite. The positive source's field points away from it and the negative source's field points toward it. At the midpoint those directions coincide.
