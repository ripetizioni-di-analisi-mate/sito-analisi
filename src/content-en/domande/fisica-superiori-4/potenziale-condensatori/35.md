An electron starts at rest in a nonzero uniform electrostatic field. Which description gives its initial motion?

- Along the field, toward lower potential.
- Opposite to the field, toward higher potential.
- Along an equipotential.
- Toward higher potential, in the direction of the field.

## Explanation

The electron is negatively charged, so the force is opposite to the field. Potential increases in the direction opposite to the field.
