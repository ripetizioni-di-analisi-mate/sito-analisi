The graph of the potential of a uniform field along $x$ is an increasing straight line. What is the direction of the component $E_x$?

::grafico{f="10*x-20" xmin=0 xmax=5 ymin=-25 ymax=35 nomi="x,V" legenda="V(x)" titolo="The coordinate x is in centimeters and V in volts."}

- Toward decreasing x.
- Toward increasing x.
- It is zero.
- It changes direction where the potential is zero.

## Explanation

Potential decreases in the direction of the field. A positive slope of the graph along x therefore indicates a negative field component.

$$
E_x=-\frac{\Delta V}{\Delta x}.
$$
