Two capacitors of capacitances $C$ and $4C$ are in parallel at a nonzero voltage. Which comparison of their energies is correct?

- $U_C=4U_{4C}$.
- $U_{4C}=16U_C$.
- $U_{4C}=4U_C$.
- $U_C=U_{4C}$.

## Explanation

The branches share the voltage. At equal voltage, $U=\dfrac12C(\Delta V)^2$ is proportional to capacitance and the factor is four.
