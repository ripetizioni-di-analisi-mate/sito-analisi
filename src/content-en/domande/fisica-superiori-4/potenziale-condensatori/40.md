A particle of charge $+e$ enters a region of increasing potential with initial kinetic energy $60\,\mathrm{eV}$. Only the electric force acts. How much must the potential rise to stop it?

- $-60\,\mathrm{V}$
- $30\,\mathrm{V}$
- $60\,\mathrm{V}$
- $120\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

The field does negative work on a positive charge moving to higher potential. The particle stops when the kinetic energy loss reaches $60\,\mathrm{eV}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
0-K_i&=-e\Delta V, \\[6pt]
\Delta V&=\frac{60\,\mathrm{eV}}e=60\,\mathrm V.
\end{aligned}
$$
