At the midpoint between two equal sources $+Q$, with zero potential at infinity, what are the potential and the field?

- $V=0$ and $\vec E=\vec 0$.
- $V<0$ and $\vec E=\vec 0$.
- $V=0$ and $\vec E\ne\vec 0$.
- $V>0$ and $\vec E=\vec 0$.

## Explanation

The potential contributions have the same sign and add. The fields are vectors of equal magnitude and opposite directions, so their sum is zero.
