A parallel-plate capacitor supplied at $12\,\mathrm V$ has capacitance $5\,\mathrm{\mu F}$. If separation doubles and permittivity quadruples, what energy does it store while remaining connected to the source?

- $360\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $720\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $180\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $1440\,\mathrm{\mu J}$

## Explanation

Capacitance is proportional to permittivity and inversely proportional to separation. The combined factor is two. The source holds voltage fixed, so the energy doubles as well.

$$
\begin{aligned}
C'&=\frac42 C=10\,\mathrm{\mu F}, \\[6pt]
U_C'&=\frac12\cdot10\cdot12^2\,\mathrm{\mu J}=720\,\mathrm{\mu J}.
\end{aligned}
$$
