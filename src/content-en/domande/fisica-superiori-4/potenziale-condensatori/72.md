Two initially uncharged capacitors are in series with capacitances of $2$ and $6\,\mathrm{\mu F}$. If the voltage across the first must not exceed $9\,\mathrm V$, what total voltage corresponds exactly to that limit?

- $9\,\mathrm{V}$
- $18\,\mathrm{V}$
- $12\,\mathrm{V}$
- $36\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

At the limit the first capacitor has nine volts. The same charge magnitude applies to the series pair and gives three volts across the second. The series voltage is the sum of the two drops.

$$
\begin{aligned}
Q&=2\cdot9\,\mathrm{\mu C}=18\,\mathrm{\mu C}, \\[6pt]
\Delta V_2&=\frac{18}{6}\,\mathrm V=3\,\mathrm V, \\[6pt]
\Delta V_{\mathrm{tot}}&=12\,\mathrm V.
\end{aligned}
$$
