In an electrostatic field, a charge is moved along a closed path crossing several regions. What is the total electric work?

- Positive, because the charge actually moves.
- It depends on where the field is zero.
- Zero, because final potential energy equals initial potential energy.
- It depends on path length.

## Explanation

The initial and final points coincide. For a conservative force the total work is the negative of the potential energy change, which is zero here.
