An ion of charge $+2e$ starts at rest and moves under the electric force alone from $45$ to $10\,\mathrm V$. What is its final kinetic energy in electronvolts?

- $K_f=70\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $K_f=35\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $K_f=110\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $K_f=-70\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

The potential drops by $35\,\mathrm V$. A doubly charged ion gains two electronvolts for every volt of drop in its accelerating direction.

$$
K_f-K_i=q(V_i-V_f)=2e\cdot35\,\mathrm V=70\,\mathrm{eV}.
$$
