Two ions start from rest at $45$ V and reach $10$ V under the electric force alone. The first, of charge $+2e$, gains $70$ eV. The second gains $35$ eV. Which conclusion about the second ion follows from the data?

- Its charge is $+e$, while its mass is undetermined.
- Its mass is half that of the first, whatever its charge.
- Its charge is $-e$ and its mass equals that of the first.
- Its final speed is certainly half that of the first.

## Explanation

The energy gain is $q(V_i-V_f)$. For the same potential drop, half the energy means half the charge, or $+e$. Mass does not enter the electric work, and without it the final speeds cannot be compared.
