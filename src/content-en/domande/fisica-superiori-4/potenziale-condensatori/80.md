In vacuum the uniform field of a capacitor is doubled while the volume between the plates stays the same. How does the field energy density change?

- It doubles.
- It quadruples.
- It halves.
- It stays the same.

## Explanation

The density is $u_E=\dfrac12\varepsilon_0E^2$. Permittivity remains that of vacuum, so doubling the field multiplies the energy density by four.
