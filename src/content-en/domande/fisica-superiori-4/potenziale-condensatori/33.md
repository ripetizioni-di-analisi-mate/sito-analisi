Between two ideal plates at $0$ and $90\,\mathrm V$, $6\,\mathrm{mm}$ apart, a point is $2\,\mathrm{mm}$ from the zero-potential plate and $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ from the other. What is its potential?

- $30\,\mathrm{V}$
- $60\,\mathrm{V}$
- $45\,\mathrm{V}$
- $15\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

In a uniform field the change in potential is proportional to distance normal to the plates. The point is one third of the way from the zero-potential plate, so it has one third of the total change.

$$
V=0+\frac26(90-0)\,\mathrm V=30\,\mathrm V.
$$
