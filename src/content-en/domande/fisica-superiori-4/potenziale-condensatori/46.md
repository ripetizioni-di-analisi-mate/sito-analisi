A particle gains $90\,\mathrm{eV}$ when going from $20$ to $50\,\mathrm V$ under the electric force alone. What is its charge in units of $e$?

- $-3$
- $3$
- $-1$
- $-9$

## Explanation

Potential rises and kinetic energy also increases. This requires a negative charge. The ratio of electronvolts gained to volts crossed gives the magnitude in units of elementary charge.

$$
\frac qe=-\frac{\Delta K}{e\Delta V}=-\frac{90}{30}=-3.
$$
