Two identical capacitors are connected first in series and then in parallel. By what factor does equivalent capacitance change from the first connection to the second?

- It doubles.
- It is multiplied by four.
- It halves.
- It stays the same.

## Explanation

The series combination of two equal capacitances is half the individual value. The parallel combination is twice the individual value, so the ratio of parallel to series is four.
