An isolated capacitor with a vacuum-filled gap stores $240\,\mathrm{\mu J}$ of energy. Completely inserting a dielectric of $\varepsilon_r=4$ leaves its charge unchanged. How much energy does the capacitor lose?

- $60\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $240\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $180\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $120\,\mathrm{\mu J}$

## Explanation

At fixed charge, the energy is inversely proportional to capacitance. Quadrupling capacitance reduces energy to one quarter, so the remaining three quarters are lost.

$$
\begin{aligned}
U_f&=\frac{U_i}{4}=60\,\mathrm{\mu J}, \\[6pt]
U_i-U_f&=180\,\mathrm{\mu J}.
\end{aligned}
$$
