A $96\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ capacitor is charged to $18\,\mathrm V$. What is its stored energy in millijoules?

- $U_C=31.104\,\mathrm{mJ}$
- $U_C=7.776\,\mathrm{mJ}$
- $U_C=15.552\,\mathrm{mJ}$
- $U_C=1.5552\,\mathrm{mJ}$

## Explanation

The voltage grows during charging, so the energy is half the product of the final charge and final voltage. The calculation first gives microjoules, which are divided by one thousand.

$$
U_C=\frac12 C(\Delta V)^2=48\cdot324\,\mathrm{\mu J}=15.552\,\mathrm{mJ}.
$$
