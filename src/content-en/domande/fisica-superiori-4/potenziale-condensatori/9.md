Point $P$ is $20\,\mathrm{cm}$ from each of two sources, $+4\,\mathrm{nC}$ and $-2\,\mathrm{nC}$. What is $V_P$ using $k_e\simeq9\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$ and zero at infinity?

- $V_P=180\,\mathrm{V}$
- $V_P=270\,\mathrm{V}$
- $V_P=-90\,\mathrm{V}$
- $V_P=90\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

Potential is a scalar. At equal distances, add the two effects directly using the signed charges.

$$
V_P\simeq9\left(\frac4{0.20}+\frac{-2}{0.20}\right)\mathrm V=90\,\mathrm V.
$$
