Three capacitors of $2$, $4$, and $6\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ are in parallel across an ideal $10\,\mathrm V$ source. The $4\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ branch is disconnected. What is the total charge on the plates still connected to the positive node?

- $40\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $120\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $60\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $80\,\mathrm{\mu C}$

## Explanation

The source maintains the voltage across the remaining branches, so each keeps the charge determined by its capacitance. The disconnected branch no longer contributes to the sum.

$$
Q_{\mathrm{rim}}=(2+6)10\,\mathrm{\mu C}=80\,\mathrm{\mu C}.
$$
