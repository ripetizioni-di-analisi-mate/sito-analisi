A parallel-plate capacitor stays at $20\,\mathrm V$ while the separation increases from $2$ to $4\,\mathrm{mm}$. What is the final field in $\mathrm{kV/m}$?

- $10\,\mathrm{kV/m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kV/m}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{kV/m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{kV/m}$

## Explanation

The source holds the potential difference fixed while the distance over which it drops increases. The final field is voltage divided by the new separation.

$$
E'=\frac{20\,\mathrm V}{0.004\,\mathrm m}=5000\,\mathrm{V/m}=5\,\mathrm{kV/m}.
$$
