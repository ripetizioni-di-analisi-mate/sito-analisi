Three positive finite capacitances are connected in series. Which inequality always holds for the equivalent capacitance?

- It is greater than the largest capacitance.
- It always equals the mean capacitance.
- It is smaller than the smallest capacitance.
- It is between the smallest and largest capacitance.

## Explanation

The sum of the reciprocals is greater than each individual term. Taking reciprocals reverses the inequality, so the equivalent capacitance is smaller than each capacitance.
