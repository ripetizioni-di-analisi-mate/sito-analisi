A conductor in electrostatic equilibrium is at $50\,\mathrm V$ relative to ground. What is the potential difference between two points inside the metal?

- $50\,\mathrm V$.
- $100\,\mathrm V$.
- It depends on the distance.
- $0\,\mathrm V$.

## Explanation

In equilibrium the field in the metal is zero and the potential is constant. The value of that constant relative to ground does not change differences between interior points.
