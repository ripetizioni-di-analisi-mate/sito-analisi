Two capacitors share a node that also connects to a third branch. Which rule can be used for the pair based on this information alone?

- The shared node alone does not establish a simple series connection.
- They are certainly in series.
- They are certainly in parallel.
- They certainly have equal charge magnitudes.

## Explanation

The series formula requires that only the two plates of the pair connect to the intermediate node and that it be initially neutral. A third branch allows different charge distributions.
