In a graph of voltage during charging, the line goes from $(0,0)$ to $(20\,\mathrm{\mu C},12\,\mathrm V)$. How much work has been stored as field energy?

::grafico{linee="0,0 20,12" punti="20,12,pieno,A" xmin=0 xmax=24 ymin=0 ymax=16 nomi="Q,ΔV" titolo="Charge is in microcoulombs and voltage in volts."}

- $240\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $60\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $12\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $120\,\mathrm{\mu J}$

## Explanation

The area under the line represents the work of slowly charging the capacitor. The base is the final charge and the height is the final voltage.

$$
U_C=\frac12\cdot20\,\mathrm{\mu C}\cdot12\,\mathrm V=120\,\mathrm{\mu J}.
$$
