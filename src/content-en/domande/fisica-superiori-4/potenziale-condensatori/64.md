Two parallel capacitors have capacitances $C$ and $3C$ and carry a total of $64\,\mathrm{\mu C}$ on the positive node. How much charge is on the positive plate of the smaller capacitor?

- $32\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $16\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $48\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $64\,\mathrm{\mu C}$

## Explanation

Because the common voltage is the same, charge divides in the same ratio as the capacitances. The smaller capacitor receives one quarter of the total charge.

$$
Q_{\mathrm{min}}=\frac{C}{C+3C}\cdot64\,\mathrm{\mu C}=16\,\mathrm{\mu C}.
$$
