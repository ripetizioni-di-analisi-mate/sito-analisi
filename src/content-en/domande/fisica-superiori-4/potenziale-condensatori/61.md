Two capacitors of $4$ and $12\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ are in series. A $2\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ capacitor is connected in parallel with the entire branch. What is the equivalent capacitance?

- $3\,\mathrm{\mu F}$
- $18\,\mathrm{\mu F}$
- $5\,\mathrm{\mu F}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{\mu F}$

## Explanation

Reduce the series combination first because it has an isolated intermediate node. The equivalent branch and the $2\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ capacitor are then in parallel between the outer nodes.

$$
\begin{aligned}
C_{\mathrm{ser}}&=\frac{4\cdot12}{4+12}\,\mathrm{\mu F}=3\,\mathrm{\mu F}, \\[6pt]
C_{\mathrm{eq}}&=3\,\mathrm{\mu F}+2\,\mathrm{\mu F}=5\,\mathrm{\mu F}.
\end{aligned}
$$
