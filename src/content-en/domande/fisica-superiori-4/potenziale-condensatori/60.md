Four identical $6\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ capacitors form two parallel branches. Each branch contains two capacitors in series. What is the equivalent capacitance?

- $6\,\mathrm{\mu F}$
- $3\,\mathrm{\mu F}$
- $12\,\mathrm{\mu F}$
- $24\,\mathrm{\mu F}$

## Explanation

First reduce each series pair, which has half the individual capacitance. The resulting branches share the external nodes, and their capacitances add.

$$
\begin{aligned}
C_{\mathrm{ramo}}&=\frac{6}{2}\,\mathrm{\mu F}=3\,\mathrm{\mu F}, \\[6pt]
C_{\mathrm{eq}}&=3\,\mathrm{\mu F}+3\,\mathrm{\mu F}=6\,\mathrm{\mu F}.
\end{aligned}
$$
