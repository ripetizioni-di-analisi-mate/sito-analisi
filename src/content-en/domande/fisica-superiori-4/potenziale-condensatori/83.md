With a $20\,\mathrm V$ source connected, a dielectric increases capacitance by $3\,\mathrm{\mu F}$. What magnitude of additional charge does the source supply?

- $20\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $60\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $30\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $120\,\mathrm{\mu C}$

## Explanation

Both initial and final charge are capacitance multiplied by the same voltage. Subtracting leaves the increase in capacitance times twenty volts.

$$
\Delta Q=\Delta C\Delta V=3\cdot20\,\mathrm{\mu C}=60\,\mathrm{\mu C}.
$$
