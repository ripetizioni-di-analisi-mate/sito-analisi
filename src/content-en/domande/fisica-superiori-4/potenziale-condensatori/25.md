The potential of a uniform field falls from $80$ to $20\,\mathrm V$ between two points $3\,\mathrm{cm}$ apart along the field. What is $E$?

- $20\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $6000\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $200\,\mathrm{V/m}$
- $2000\,\mathrm{V/m}$

## Explanation

The field magnitude is the magnitude of potential change per meter along the field. The distance must therefore be expressed in meters.

$$
E=\frac{|\Delta V|}{d}=\frac{80-20}{0.03}\,\mathrm{V/m}=2000\,\mathrm{V/m}.
$$
