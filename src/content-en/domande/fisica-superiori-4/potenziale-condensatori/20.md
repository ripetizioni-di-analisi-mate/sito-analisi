A test charge $q=-5\,\mathrm{nC}$ has energy $U=-0.40\,\mathrm{\mu J}$ at a point. What is the potential at that point in the same reference?

- $-80\,\mathrm{V}$
- $8\,\mathrm{V}$
- $0.08\,\mathrm{V}$
- $80\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

Divide energy by signed charge. The ratio is positive because both are negative. A microjoule is one thousand nanojoules.

$$
V=\frac Uq=\frac{-0.40\cdot10^{-6}}{-5\cdot10^{-9}}\,\mathrm V=80\,\mathrm V.
$$
