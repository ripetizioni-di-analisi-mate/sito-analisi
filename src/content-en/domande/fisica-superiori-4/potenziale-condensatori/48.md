The charge magnitude on a plate of a capacitor is $60\,\mathrm{\mu C}$ when the voltage is $15\,\mathrm V$. What is its capacitance?

- $8\,\mathrm{\mu F}$
- $4\,\mathrm{\mu F}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{\mu F}$
- $15\,\mathrm{\mu F}$

## Explanation

Capacitance measures how much charge is needed per volt of voltage. Use the magnitude on one plate.

$$
C=\frac{Q}{\Delta V}=\frac{60\,\mathrm{\mu C}}{15\,\mathrm V}=4\,\mathrm{\mu F}.
$$
