The relation $K=\dfrac12mv^2$ is to be used to find the speed of a particle accelerated by an electric field. What physical condition justifies its use?

- The particle has negative charge.
- The speed is much less than the speed of light.
- The initial potential is zero.
- The field is necessarily uniform.

## Explanation

The kinetic energy formula is classical. It is a good approximation when speed is small compared with the speed of light, regardless of the charge sign.
