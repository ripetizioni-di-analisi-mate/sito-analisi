In a region with a uniform field pointing left, the displacement from $A$ to $B$ is $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ to the right. With $E=250\,\mathrm{V/m}$, what is $V_B-V_A$?

- $15\,\mathrm{V}$
- $-15\,\mathrm{V}$
- $0\,\mathrm{V}$
- $150\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

The motion is opposite to the field, so the signed projection is negative. Potential increases along a displacement opposite to the field direction.

$$
\Delta V=-E(0.06\,\mathrm m)\cos 180^\circ =15\,\mathrm V.
$$
