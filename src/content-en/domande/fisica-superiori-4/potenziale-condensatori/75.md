A $4\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ capacitor has energy $72\,\mathrm{\mu J}$. What voltage maintains it in this state?

- $18\,\mathrm{V}$
- $12\,\mathrm{V}$
- $6\,\mathrm{V}$
- $36\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

Energy is proportional to the square of voltage. After isolating the square, take the positive root because the requested voltage is from the negative to the positive plate.

$$
\Delta V=\sqrt{\frac{2U_C}{C}}=\sqrt{\frac{144}{4}}\,\mathrm V=6\,\mathrm V.
$$
