An electron initially at rest moves against a uniform field of $1000\,\mathrm{V/m}$ for $5\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its kinetic energy gain?

- $5\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $-50\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $50\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $500\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

Moving opposite to the field, the electron reaches higher potential. The force on a negative charge points that way, so kinetic energy increases.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta V&=1000\cdot0.05\,\mathrm V=50\,\mathrm V, \\[6pt]
\Delta K&=e\Delta V=50\,\mathrm{eV}.
\end{aligned}
$$
