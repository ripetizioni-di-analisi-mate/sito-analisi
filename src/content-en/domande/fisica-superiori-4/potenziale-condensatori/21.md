Two equal charges move apart from a separation $r$ to $4r$. The initial energy was $120\,\mathrm{nJ}$ with zero at infinity. How much work does the electric force do?

- $-90\,\mathrm{nJ}$
- $90\,\mathrm{nJ}$
- $30\,\mathrm{nJ}$
- $120\,\mathrm{nJ}$

## Explanation

Repulsion does positive work during separation. To calculate it, subtract the final energy from the initial energy, because electric work measures the loss of potential energy.

$$
W=U_i-U_f=120\,\mathrm{nJ}-\frac{120}{4}\,\mathrm{nJ}=90\,\mathrm{nJ}.
$$
