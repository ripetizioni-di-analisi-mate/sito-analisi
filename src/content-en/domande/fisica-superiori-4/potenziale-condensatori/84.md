A dielectric is added to a parallel-plate capacitor and fills the gap. Which quantity stays fixed if the plates remain connected to an ideal voltage source?

- The charge magnitude on each plate.
- The stored energy.
- The capacitance.
- The potential difference between the plates.

## Explanation

The source imposes the voltage. When the material raises capacitance, the source supplies additional charge and the capacitor stores more energy.
