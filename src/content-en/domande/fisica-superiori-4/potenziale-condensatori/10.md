A pair of opposite charges has $U_i=-360\,\mathrm{nJ}$ at a separation of $10\,\mathrm{cm}$. How much external work is needed to slowly increase the separation to $30\,\mathrm{cm}$?

- $W_{\mathrm{est}}=-240\,\mathrm{nJ}$
- $W_{\mathrm{est}}=240\,\mathrm{nJ}$
- $W_{\mathrm{est}}=120\,\mathrm{nJ}$
- $W_{\mathrm{est}}=360\,\mathrm{nJ}$

## Explanation

Energy is inversely proportional to separation. Tripling that separation reduces the magnitude to one third. The external work is positive because the pair's energy increases with negligible kinetic energy change.

$$
\begin{aligned}
U_f&=\frac{U_i}{3}=-120\,\mathrm{nJ}, \\[6pt]
W_{\mathrm{est}}&=U_f-U_i=240\,\mathrm{nJ}.
\end{aligned}
$$
