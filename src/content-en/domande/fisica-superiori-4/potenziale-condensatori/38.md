The same electron starts at rest in two nonrelativistic trials. In the second it crosses a voltage of four times the magnitude. How does its final speed change?

- It quadruples.
- It becomes sixteen times larger.
- It stays the same.
- It doubles.

## Explanation

The kinetic energy gained is proportional to voltage, so it quadruples. With the same mass, speed is proportional to the square root of that energy.
