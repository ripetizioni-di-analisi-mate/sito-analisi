Two different capacitors are charged in series with a neutral, isolated intermediate node. Which voltage comparison is correct?

- The greater voltage is across the larger capacitance.
- The voltages are necessarily equal.
- The voltage across the smaller capacitance is always zero.
- The greater voltage is across the smaller capacitance.

## Explanation

The isolated node imposes the same charge magnitude on the two capacitors. Dividing the same charge by a smaller capacitance gives a greater voltage.
