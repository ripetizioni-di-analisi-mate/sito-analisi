Two capacitors of $2$ and $6\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ in series are initially uncharged. They are connected to $16\,\mathrm V$. How much energy does the smaller capacitor store?

- $48\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $192\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $288\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $144\,\mathrm{\mu J}$

## Explanation

The series pair shares a common charge magnitude. It follows from equivalent capacitance and total voltage. Once charge is known, divide its square by twice the capacitance of the individual capacitor.

$$
\begin{aligned}
C_{\mathrm{eq}}&=1.5\,\mathrm{\mu F}, \\[6pt]
Q&=24\,\mathrm{\mu C}, \\[6pt]
U_1&=\frac{24^2}{2\cdot2}\,\mathrm{\mu J}=144\,\mathrm{\mu J}.
\end{aligned}
$$
