An ion of charge $+3e$ gains $120\,\mathrm{eV}$ of kinetic energy under the electric force alone. What is $V_i-V_f$?

- $120\,\mathrm{V}$
- $360\,\mathrm{V}$
- $-40\,\mathrm{V}$
- $40\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

Every volt of drop transfers three electronvolts to an ion of charge $+3e$. Dividing the gain by three gives the number of volts.

$$
V_i-V_f=\frac{\Delta K}{3e}=40\,\mathrm V.
$$
