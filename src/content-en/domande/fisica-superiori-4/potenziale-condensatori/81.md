A $2\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ capacitor remains connected to an ideal $10\,\mathrm V$ source. A dielectric raises it to $8\,\mathrm{\mu F}$. How much work does the source supply during insertion?

- $600\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $300\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $200\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $800\,\mathrm{\mu J}$

## Explanation

The source maintains the voltage. All charge it transfers crosses ten volts. The factor of one half appears in the stored energy change, which here is $300\,\mathrm{\mu J}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta Q&=(8-2)10\,\mathrm{\mu C}=60\,\mathrm{\mu C}, \\[6pt]
W_{\mathrm{gen}}&=\Delta Q\Delta V=600\,\mathrm{\mu J}.
\end{aligned}
$$
