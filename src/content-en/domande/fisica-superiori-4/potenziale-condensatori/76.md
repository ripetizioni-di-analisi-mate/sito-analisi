Two capacitors of capacitances $C$ and $3C$ are charged in series with a neutral intermediate node isolated from other branches. Which ratio holds for their stored energies?

- $U_C=3U_{3C}$.
- $U_{3C}=3U_C$.
- $U_C=U_{3C}$.
- $U_C=9U_{3C}$.

## Explanation

The charge magnitudes are equal, so the useful form is $U=\dfrac{Q^2}{2C}$. The smaller capacitance stores more energy at equal charge.
