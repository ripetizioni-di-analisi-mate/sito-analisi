A $6\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ capacitor is in series with a parallel combination of $2$ and $4\,\mathrm{\mu F}$. The total voltage is $10\,\mathrm V$. Starting with uncharged capacitors, what is the charge magnitude on the $2\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ capacitor?

- $20\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $30\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $10\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $60\,\mathrm{\mu C}$

## Explanation

The parallel combination has the same capacitance as the series capacitor, so the two blocks split the voltage equally. In the parallel branches the voltage is common, so the $2\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ capacitor also has five volts.

$$
\begin{aligned}
C_{\mathrm{par}}&=6\,\mathrm{\mu F}, \\[6pt]
\Delta V_{\mathrm{par}}&=5\,\mathrm V, \\[6pt]
Q_2&=2\cdot5\,\mathrm{\mu C}=10\,\mathrm{\mu C}.
\end{aligned}
$$
