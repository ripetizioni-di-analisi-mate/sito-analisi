Which unit is equivalent to one farad?

- $\mathrm{C/V}$.
- $\mathrm{V/C}$.
- $\mathrm{C\,V}$.
- $\mathrm{J/C}$.

## Explanation

The ratio defining capacitance has charge in the numerator and potential difference in the denominator. Coulombs divided by volts therefore give farads.
