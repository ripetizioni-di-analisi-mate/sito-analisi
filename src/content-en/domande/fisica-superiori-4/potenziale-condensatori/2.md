Two charges $+4.8\,\mathrm{nC}$ and $-2\,\mathrm{nC}$ are $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ apart. With $k_e\simeq9\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$ and zero energy at infinite separation, what is their potential energy in nanojoules?

- $U=864\,\mathrm{nJ}$
- $U=-432\,\mathrm{nJ}$
- $U=-864\,\mathrm{nJ}$
- $U=-86.4\,\mathrm{nJ}$

## Explanation

The two charges in nanocoulombs give a factor of $10^{-18}$. Multiplication by the Coulomb constant leaves the $10^{-9}$ factor of the nanojoule, and the opposite signs make the result negative.

$$
U=k_e\frac{Qq}{r}\simeq\frac{9\cdot4.8\cdot(-2)}{0.10}\,\mathrm{nJ}=-864\,\mathrm{nJ}.
$$
