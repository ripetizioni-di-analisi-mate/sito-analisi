Two capacitors in parallel at $15\,\mathrm V$ have a total of $90\,\mathrm{\mu C}$ on the positive node. If one is $2\,\mathrm{\mu F}$, what is the capacitance of the other?

- $4\,\mathrm{\mu F}$
- $2\,\mathrm{\mu F}$
- $6\,\mathrm{\mu F}$
- $8\,\mathrm{\mu F}$

## Explanation

Total charge divided by the common voltage gives the equivalent capacitance. Since capacitances add in parallel, subtract the known capacitance to find the unknown one.

$$
C_2=\frac{Q_{\mathrm{tot}}}{\Delta V}-C_1=\left(\frac{90}{15}-2\right)\mathrm{\mu F}=4\,\mathrm{\mu F}.
$$
