Two nonrelativistic particles of the same charge and masses $m$ and $4m$ start at rest and gain energy across the same potential difference. Which comparison is correct?

- The particle of mass m is twice as fast.
- The particle of mass m is four times as fast.
- The particle of mass 4m is twice as fast.
- Their speeds are equal.

## Explanation

The particles receive the same electric work and therefore the same kinetic energy. Quadrupling mass at the same energy halves speed.
