Three identical $9\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ capacitors are in series across $18\,\mathrm V$, starting uncharged. What is the charge magnitude of each capacitor?

- $54\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $162\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $27\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $18\,\mathrm{\mu C}$

## Explanation

Charge has the same magnitude and capacitances are equal, so the three voltages are equal. Each capacitor has one third of the total voltage.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta V_{\mathrm{sing}}&=\frac{18}{3}\,\mathrm V=6\,\mathrm V, \\[6pt]
Q&=9\cdot6\,\mathrm{\mu C}=54\,\mathrm{\mu C}.
\end{aligned}
$$
