The charges $+3Q$ and $-Q$ are $40\,\mathrm{cm}$ apart. On the segment joining them, at what distance from $-Q$ is the point where the potential is zero relative to infinity?

- $20\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $10\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $30\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $5\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The magnitudes of the contributions must match. Since the first charge has three times the magnitude, the point must be three times as far from it as from the smaller charge.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{3Q}{40-d}&=\frac Qd, \\[6pt]
3d&=40-d, \\[6pt]
d&=10\,\mathrm{cm}.
\end{aligned}
$$
