Along the segment joining $+3Q$ and $-Q$, which are $40\,\mathrm{cm}$ apart, where is the potential zero relative to infinity? Give the distance from $-Q$.

- $20\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $10\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $30\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $5\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The magnitudes of the contributions must match. Since the first charge has three times the magnitude, the point must be three times as far from it as from the smaller charge.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{3Q}{40-d}&=\frac Qd, \\[6pt]
3d&=40-d, \\[6pt]
d&=10\,\mathrm{cm}.
\end{aligned}
$$
