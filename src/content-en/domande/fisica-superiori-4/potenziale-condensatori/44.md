A particle of charge $+e$ moves away from a positive source under the electric force alone. The potential falls from $90$ to $30\,\mathrm V$. How much does its kinetic energy change?

- $60\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $-60\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $30\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $90\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

Repulsion does positive work as the particle moves away. Even though the source field varies, its work follows from the endpoint potential difference.

$$
\Delta K=e(90-30)\,\mathrm V=60\,\mathrm{eV}.
$$
