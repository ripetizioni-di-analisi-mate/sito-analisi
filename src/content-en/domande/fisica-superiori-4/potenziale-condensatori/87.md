Two charged, isolated parallel plates are moved apart, keeping area and permittivity fixed. Why does the energy of the ideal capacitor increase?

- Mechanical work is required to separate the attracting plates.
- The voltage source must supply more charge.
- The capacitance increases and therefore the energy rises.
- The field doubles at fixed charge.

## Explanation

Separating the plates decreases capacitance. Charge is fixed, and the energy $Q^2$ divided by $2C$ increases. The added energy comes from work against attraction.
