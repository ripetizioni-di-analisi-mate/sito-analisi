A $4.2\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ capacitor is held at $12\,\mathrm V$. What is the charge magnitude on one plate?

- $Q=25.2\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $Q=100.8\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $Q=0\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $Q=50.4\,\mathrm{\mu C}$

## Explanation

The definition uses the charge magnitude on one plate. The total charge of both plates is zero because their signs are opposite.

$$
Q=C\Delta V=4.2\cdot12\,\mathrm{\mu C}=50.4\,\mathrm{\mu C}.
$$
