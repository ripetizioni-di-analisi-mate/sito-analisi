A test charge $+q$ is replaced by $-2q$ at the same point of nonzero potential, with the sources kept fixed. How do $V$ and $U$ change?

- $V$ stays the same and $U$ becomes $-2U$.
- Both change sign.
- $V$ becomes $-2V$ and $U$ stays the same.
- Both double.

## Explanation

Potential describes the sources at the chosen position. The test charge's energy is its product with potential, so it changes by the same signed factor as the charge.
