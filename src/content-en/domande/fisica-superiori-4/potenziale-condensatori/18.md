A point source produces $-120\,\mathrm V$ at $30\,\mathrm{cm}$ in vacuum. What is its charge, using $k_e\simeq9\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$ and zero at infinity?

- $4\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $-36\,\mathrm{nC}$

## Explanation

With that reference, potential has the same sign as its source. Multiplying by the distance and dividing by the constant gives its magnitude.

$$
Q=\frac{Vr}{k_e}\simeq\frac{(-120)0.30}{9}\,\mathrm{nC}=-4\,\mathrm{nC}.
$$
