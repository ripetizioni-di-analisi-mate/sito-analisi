Two initially uncharged capacitors of $3$ and $9\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ are in series across $16\,\mathrm V$. What voltage is across the $3\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ capacitor?

- $4\,\mathrm{V}$
- $8\,\mathrm{V}$
- $12\,\mathrm{V}$
- $16\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

The plates have equal charge magnitudes. The smaller capacitor therefore has three times the voltage, and the total of sixteen volts splits into four equal parts, three across the first capacitor.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{\Delta V_1}{\Delta V_2}&=\frac{9}{3}=3, \\[6pt]
\Delta V_1&=\frac34(16\,\mathrm V)=12\,\mathrm V.
\end{aligned}
$$
