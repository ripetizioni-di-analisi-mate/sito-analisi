Capacitors A and B are in series with an isolated, initially neutral intermediate node. The plate of A connected there is measured at $-24$ microcoulombs. What is the charge on the plate of B connected to that node?

- $-24\,\mathrm{\mu C}$.
- $0\,\mathrm{\mu C}$.
- $+48\,\mathrm{\mu C}$.
- $+24\,\mathrm{\mu C}$.

## Explanation

The intermediate node is not connected to a charge source, so its total charge stays zero. The second plate exactly balances the first.
