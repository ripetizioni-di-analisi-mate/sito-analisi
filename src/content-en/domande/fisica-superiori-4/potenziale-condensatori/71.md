Three capacitors are connected between nodes A and B as shown in the diagram. Which pair is in parallel?

::grafico{linee="0.5,2 1.2,2;1.2,1.4 1.2,2.6;1.6,1.4 1.6,2.6;1.6,2 2.4,2;2.4,1 2.4,3;2.4,3 3.3,3;2.4,1 3.3,1;3.3,2.55 3.3,3.45;3.7,2.55 3.7,3.45;3.3,0.55 3.3,1.45;3.7,0.55 3.7,1.45;3.7,3 4.6,3;3.7,1 4.6,1;4.6,1 4.6,3;4.6,2 5.5,2" etichette="1.4,3,C1;3.5,3.9,C2;3.5,0.18,C3" punti="0.5,2,pieno,A;5.5,2,pieno,B;2.4,2;4.6,2" xmin=0 xmax=6 ymin=0 ymax=4.5 assi=0 titolo="Each pair of plates represents a capacitor."}

- $C_2$ and $C_3$.
- $C_1$ and $C_2$.
- $C_1$ and $C_3$.
- No pair.

## Explanation

The two branches on the right start at the same junction and return to node B. The two capacitors therefore have the same potential difference.
