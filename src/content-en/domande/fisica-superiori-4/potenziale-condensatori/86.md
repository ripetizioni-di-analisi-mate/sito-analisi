A $4\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ capacitor charged to $12\,\mathrm V$ is disconnected and connected in parallel to an identical initially uncharged capacitor. The system stays isolated. How much energy is stored at the new equilibrium?

- $288\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $72\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $576\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $144\,\mathrm{\mu J}$

## Explanation

The total charge on each node is conserved. At the new equilibrium the equivalent capacitance doubles, so stored energy at fixed charge halves. The difference can be dissipated during the transient.

$$
\begin{aligned}
Q&=48\,\mathrm{\mu C}, \\[6pt]
C_{\mathrm{eq}}&=8\,\mathrm{\mu F}, \\[6pt]
U_f&=\frac{48^2}{2\cdot8}\,\mathrm{\mu J}=144\,\mathrm{\mu J}.
\end{aligned}
$$
