An electrostatic field does $-36\,\mathrm{\mu J}$ of work on a charge of $+3\,\mathrm{\mu C}$ moved from $A$ to $B$. What is $V_B-V_A$?

- $-12\,\mathrm{V}$
- $12\,\mathrm{V}$
- $9\,\mathrm{V}$
- $36\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

Electric work is negative on a positive charge, so potential energy increases. Dividing that increase in energy by the charge gives a positive potential increase.

$$
V_B-V_A=-\frac Wq=-\frac{-36}{3}\,\mathrm V=12\,\mathrm V.
$$
