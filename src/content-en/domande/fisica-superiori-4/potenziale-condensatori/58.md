Two capacitors have an equivalent capacitance of $3\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ in series. One is $4\,\mathrm{\mu F}$. What is the other?

- $1\,\mathrm{\mu F}$
- $12\,\mathrm{\mu F}$
- $7\,\mathrm{\mu F}$
- $6\,\mathrm{\mu F}$

## Explanation

In series subtract reciprocals to isolate the unknown reciprocal. The value of $12\,\mathrm{\mu F}$ is greater than $C_{\mathrm{eq}}$, as required.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac1{C_2}&=\frac1{3\,\mathrm{\mu F}}-\frac1{4\,\mathrm{\mu F}} \\[6pt]
&=\frac1{12\,\mathrm{\mu F}}, \\[6pt]
C_2&=12\,\mathrm{\mu F}.
\end{aligned}
$$
