An air parallel-plate capacitor is modeled with $\varepsilon_0=9\,\mathrm{pF/m}$. The plates have area $0.020\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and capacitance $60\,\mathrm{pF}$. What is their separation?

- $1.5\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $6\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $3\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $30\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

Solve the ideal formula for separation. Using picofarads in both capacitance and permittivity leaves meters directly from square meters divided by meters.

$$
d=\frac{\varepsilon_0A}{C}=\frac{9\cdot0.020}{60}\,\mathrm m=3\,\mathrm{mm}.
$$
