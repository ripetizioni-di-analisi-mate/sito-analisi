A charged capacitor has $Q=40\,\mathrm{\mu C}$ and voltage $15\,\mathrm V$. How much energy has it stored?

- $600\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $150\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $40\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $300\,\mathrm{\mu J}$

## Explanation

During charging the voltage rises from zero to its final value. The average voltage over the charge transfer is therefore half the final voltage.

$$
U_C=\frac12Q\Delta V=\frac12\cdot40\cdot15\,\mathrm{\mu J}=300\,\mathrm{\mu J}.
$$
