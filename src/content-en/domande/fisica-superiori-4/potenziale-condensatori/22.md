A charge travels from $A$ to $B$ along two different paths. At the endpoints $V_A=V_B$, and the second path crosses regions at different potentials. What is the total electric work?

- Zero only along the first path.
- It depends on the lengths of the two paths.
- Positive along both paths.
- Zero along both paths.

## Explanation

The electrostatic force is conservative. Individual contributions along a path may be nonzero, but their sum depends only on the endpoint potentials.
