An electrostatic field line crosses an equipotential surface where $E\ne0$. How is the tangent to the field line oriented?

- Tangent to the surface.
- Perpendicular to the surface.
- At 45 degrees to the surface.
- It depends on the sign of the test charge.

## Explanation

Electric work is zero for every small tangent displacement. That requires the tangent component of the field to vanish, so the field direction is the normal direction.
