A point source of $+3.6\,\mathrm{nC}$ is in vacuum. What is the potential $20\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the source, with $k_e\simeq9\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$ and zero at infinity?

- $V=162\,\mathrm{V}$
- $V=81\,\mathrm{V}$
- $V=324\,\mathrm{V}$
- $V=-162\,\mathrm{V}$

## Explanation

The source is positive and the reference is at infinity, so the potential is positive. The nanocoulomb conversion gives a factor that cancels the power of ten in $k_e$.

$$
V=k_e\frac Qr\simeq\frac{9\cdot3.6}{0.20}\,\mathrm V=162\,\mathrm V.
$$
