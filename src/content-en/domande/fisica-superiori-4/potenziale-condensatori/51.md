Both the area and separation of an ideal parallel-plate capacitor are doubled, with the same material. What happens to capacitance?

- It doubles.
- It stays the same.
- It halves.
- It quadruples.

## Explanation

In the capacitance formula area is in the numerator and separation in the denominator. The two factors of two cancel.
