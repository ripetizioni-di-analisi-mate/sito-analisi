A capacitor at $10\,\mathrm V$ remains connected to the ideal source during dielectric insertion. The source supplies $400\,\mathrm{\mu J}$. By how much does stored energy increase?

- $0\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $400\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $200\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $800\,\mathrm{\mu J}$

## Explanation

At fixed voltage, the capacitor energy increase is half the voltage times the added charge. For the source, work is the full product because it moves all the charge through the fixed voltage.

$$
\Delta U_C=\frac12\Delta Q\Delta V=\frac12 W_{\mathrm{gen}}=200\,\mathrm{\mu J}.
$$
