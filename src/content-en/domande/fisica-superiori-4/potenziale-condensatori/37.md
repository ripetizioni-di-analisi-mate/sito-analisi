An electron has kinetic energy $250\,\mathrm{eV}$. What is its value in joules using $e=1.60\cdot10^{-19}\,\mathrm C$? Give the value in attojoules, with $1\,\mathrm{aJ}=10^{-18}\,\mathrm J$.

- $4\,\mathrm{aJ}$
- $40\,\mathrm{aJ}$
- $400\,\mathrm{aJ}$
- $250\,\mathrm{aJ}$

## Explanation

An electronvolt is the magnitude of the elementary charge times one volt. Multiply by the number of electronvolts and use the requested unit.

$$
K=250\cdot1.60\cdot10^{-19}\,\mathrm J=40\,\mathrm{aJ}.
$$
