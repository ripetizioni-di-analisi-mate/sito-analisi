A capacitor at $12\,\mathrm V$ stores $180\,\mathrm{\mu J}$. What is the charge magnitude on one plate?

- $15\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $30\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $60\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $180\,\mathrm{\mu C}$

## Explanation

From the known energy, multiply by two to get the charge-voltage product. The charge magnitude on one plate then follows by dividing by the voltage across the plates.

$$
Q=\frac{2U_C}{\Delta V}=\frac{2\cdot180}{12}\,\mathrm{\mu C}=30\,\mathrm{\mu C}.
$$
