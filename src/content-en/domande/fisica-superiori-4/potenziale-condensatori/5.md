A vacuum parallel-plate capacitor has plate area $180\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and separation $1\,\mathrm{mm}$. What is its capacitance in the ideal model with $\varepsilon_0=8.85\,\mathrm{pF/m}$?

- $C=15.93\,\mathrm{pF}$
- $C=159.3\,\mathrm{pF}$
- $C=88.5\,\mathrm{pF}$
- $C=177\,\mathrm{pF}$

## Explanation

Convert the area using the square of the centimeter conversion. The area-to-distance ratio is in meters and multiplying by permittivity gives picofarads.

$$
C=\varepsilon_0\frac Ad=8.85\frac{0.018}{0.001}\,\mathrm{pF}=159.3\,\mathrm{pF}.
$$
