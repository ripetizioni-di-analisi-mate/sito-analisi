Two regions have equipotential surfaces differing by $10\,\mathrm V$ from one to the next. They are $1\,\mathrm{cm}$ apart in A and $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ apart in B, in each case with a uniform field. How do the field magnitudes compare?

- $E_A=E_B$.
- $E_B=2E_A$.
- $E_A=2E_B$.
- $E_A=4E_B$.

## Explanation

Between consecutive surfaces the field is the same potential drop divided by the normal distance. Halving that distance in region A doubles the field.
