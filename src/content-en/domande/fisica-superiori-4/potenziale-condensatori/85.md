Two capacitors isolated from any source and connected in parallel are at $10\,\mathrm V$, with capacitances $2$ and $3\,\mathrm{\mu F}$. How much energy do they store together?

- $100\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $250\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $150\,\mathrm{\mu J}$
- $500\,\mathrm{\mu J}$

## Explanation

Energy adds and the two capacitors share the voltage. Thus the squared voltage can be factored out and the capacitances added.

$$
U_{\mathrm{tot}}=\frac12(C_1+C_2)(\Delta V)^2=\frac12(2+3)10^2\,\mathrm{\mu J}=250\,\mathrm{\mu J}.
$$
