An electron with kinetic energy $80\,\mathrm{eV}$ moves under the electric force alone from $30$ to $10\,\mathrm V$. What is its final kinetic energy?

- $100\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $20\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $80\,\mathrm{eV}$
- $60\,\mathrm{eV}$

## Explanation

A decrease in potential increases the potential energy of a negative charge. Its kinetic energy therefore drops by $20\,\mathrm{eV}$.

$$
K_f=K_i+q(V_i-V_f)=80\,\mathrm{eV}-e(30-10)\,\mathrm V=60\,\mathrm{eV}.
$$
