Two ions of charges $+e$ and $-e$ cross the same region from A to B with equal initial kinetic energies. Only the electric force acts. At B their energies are $43$ and $17\,\mathrm{eV}$, respectively. What is $V_A-V_B$?

- $+26\,\mathrm V$
- $-13\,\mathrm V$
- $+13\,\mathrm V$
- $+30\,\mathrm V$

## Explanation

If the potential drop is $D$ volts, the positive ion gains $D$ electronvolts and the negative ion loses the same amount. Their final energies therefore differ by $2D=43-17=26$ in electronvolts. The potential drop is $13\,\mathrm V$.
