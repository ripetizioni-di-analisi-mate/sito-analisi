Which graph of $F$ versus separation $r>0$ represents two point charges of fixed magnitude?

- A decreasing curve with $F$ proportional to $\dfrac{1}{r^2}$.
- A decreasing straight line.
- A horizontal line.
- An increasing curve with $F$ proportional to $r^2$.

## Explanation

In Coulomb's law for the magnitude, the charges and $k_e$ are constant. All of the distance dependence is in the factor $\dfrac{1}{r^2}$.
