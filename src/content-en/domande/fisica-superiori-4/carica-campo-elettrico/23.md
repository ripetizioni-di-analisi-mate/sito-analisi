A charged rod attracts initially neutral scraps of paper without touching them. Which process explains the attraction?

- The formation of protons in the paper.
- The loss of all positive charge in the paper.
- Polarization of the paper.
- Grounding the paper through air without any transfer.

## Explanation

Small shifts in the paper's charges leave the region near the rod polarized with the opposite sign. Attraction on the near side outweighs repulsion on the far side.
