At one point the contributions $(3,4)\,\mathrm{N/C}$ and $(-3,4)\,\mathrm{N/C}$ are measured. What is the total field vector?

- $(6,8)\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $(0,8)\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $(8,0)\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $(0,0)\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

Along $x$, $3-3=0$, and along $y$, $4+4=8$. The result is therefore vertical and positive.
