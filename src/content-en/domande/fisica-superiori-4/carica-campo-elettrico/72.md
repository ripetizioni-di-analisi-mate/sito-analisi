Two identical dipoles in the same uniform field make angles $30^\circ$ and $150^\circ$ with $\vec E$. How do their torque magnitudes compare?

- The one at $30^\circ$ is five times smaller.
- The one at $150^\circ$ is twice as large.
- They are equal.
- The one at $150^\circ$ is zero.

## Explanation

The angles are supplementary and have the same sine. In $M=pE\sin\theta$, $p$ and $E$ also match, so the magnitudes are equal.
