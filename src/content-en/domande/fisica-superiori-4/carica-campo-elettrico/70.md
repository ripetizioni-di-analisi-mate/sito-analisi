A dipole has charges of magnitude $5\,\mathrm{nC}$ separated by $4\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its dipole moment magnitude?

- $2.0\cdot10^{-8}\,\mathrm{C\,m}$
- $1.25\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{C\,m}$
- $2.0\cdot10^{-10}\,\mathrm{C\,m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{C\,m}$

## Explanation

Multiply one charge magnitude by the separation. In SI units this gives $p=(5\cdot10^{-9})\cdot0.040\,\mathrm{C\,m}=2.0\cdot10^{-10}\,\mathrm{C\,m}$.
