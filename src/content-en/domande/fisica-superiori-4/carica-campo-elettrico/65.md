Three equal positive charges are at the vertices of an equilateral triangle. What is the total field at its center?

- Its magnitude is three times one contribution.
- It equals the contribution from the top vertex.
- It is zero.
- It always points downward.

## Explanation

All three distances are equal, so the fields have equal magnitudes. The vectors are $120^\circ$ apart and their vector sum vanishes by symmetry.
