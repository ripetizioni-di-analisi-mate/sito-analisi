In the model using only the force $\vec F=q\vec E$, an object has zero net charge. What follows if the local field is nonzero?

- The object makes the field zero.
- The force is parallel to the field and nonzero.
- The force predicted by that model is zero.
- The force is perpendicular to the field and nonzero.

## Explanation

The product of $q=0$ and the field vector is the zero vector. This conclusion applies to the stated model, which describes the object using only its net charge.
