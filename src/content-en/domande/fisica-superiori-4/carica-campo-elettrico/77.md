An initially stationary negative particle is free in a uniform field pointing upward. Neglecting every other force, how does it begin to move?

- Its velocity remains zero.
- It moves upward at constant velocity.
- It accelerates upward.
- It accelerates downward.

## Explanation

The factor $\dfrac qm$ is negative, so $\vec a=\dfrac qm\vec E$ points down. Starting from rest, the particle gains velocity in that direction.
