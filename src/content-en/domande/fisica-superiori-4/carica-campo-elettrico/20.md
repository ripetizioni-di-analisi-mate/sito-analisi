A positive rod near a grounded sphere is moved away while the ground connection remains in place. Ground is disconnected only after equilibrium returns. With no other fields, what is the sphere's final charge?

- Negative.
- Positive.
- It switches sign at regular intervals.
- Zero.

## Explanation

After the rod is removed, excess electrons can return to ground through the connection. Disconnecting only after equilibrium leaves the sphere neutral.
