A proton and an electron are released from rest in the same uniform electric field. Their charges have equal magnitudes and opposite signs. How do their accelerations compare?

- They point opposite ways and the electron's is larger in magnitude.
- They have the same direction and magnitude.
- They point opposite ways and the proton's is larger in magnitude.
- They point opposite ways with equal magnitudes.

## Explanation

The opposite charge signs make the forces point oppositely. Since the force magnitudes match, the electron's smaller mass gives it a greater acceleration magnitude.
