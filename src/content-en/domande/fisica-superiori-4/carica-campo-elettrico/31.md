In vacuum two charges exert a force of $12\,\mathrm{\mu N}$. At the same positions the force is measured in a uniform medium whose Coulomb constant is one third of its vacuum value. What magnitude is measured?

- $0\,\mathrm{\mu N}$
- $12\,\mathrm{\mu N}$
- $36\,\mathrm{\mu N}$
- $4\,\mathrm{\mu N}$

## Explanation

For fixed charges and distance, force is proportional to the Coulomb constant. The new magnitude is therefore $\dfrac{12}{3}\,\mathrm{\mu N}=4\,\mathrm{\mu N}$.
