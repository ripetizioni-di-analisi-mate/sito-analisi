A source $+Q$ is at $(-a,0)$ and a source $-Q$ at $(a,0)$, with $a>0$. At a point $P$ on the vertical axis above the origin, where does the total field point?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 punti="-2,0,pieno,+Q;2,0,pieno,−Q;0,2.6,pieno,P" segmenti="-2,0,0,2.6;2,0,0,2.6" xmin=-3.3 xmax=3.3 ymin=-0.8 ymax=3.6 titolo="P is the same distance from both sources"}

- To the left.
- To the right.
- The total field is zero.
- Upward.

## Explanation

The positive charge's field points up and right, whereas the negative charge's field points down and right. Vertical components cancel by symmetry and horizontal components add.
