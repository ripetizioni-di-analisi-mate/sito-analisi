A point charge $Q=+4.8\,\mathrm{nC}$ is in vacuum. What is the field magnitude at $15\,\mathrm{cm}$ with $k_e=8.99\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$?

- $288\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
- $1.92\cdot10^3\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
- $19.2\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
- $3.84\cdot10^3\,\mathrm{N/C}$.

## Explanation

Use only the source charge and the square of the distance. This gives $E=\dfrac{8.99\cdot10^9\cdot4.8\cdot10^{-9}}{0.15^2}\,\mathrm{N/C}\simeq1.92\cdot10^3\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
