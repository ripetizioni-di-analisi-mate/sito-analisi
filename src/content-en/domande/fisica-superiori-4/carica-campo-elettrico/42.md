The field points right and the force on a particle points left. What is the sign of the particle's charge?

- It depends on mass.
- Negative.
- Zero.
- Positive.

## Explanation

The factor $q$ in $\vec F=q\vec E$ must reverse the vector direction. This occurs only if $q<0$.
