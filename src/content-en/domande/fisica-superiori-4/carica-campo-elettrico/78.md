Two particles have the same charge in the same uniform field. The mass of $A$ is three times that of $B$. How do their acceleration magnitudes compare?

- $a_A=3a_B$
- $a_A=a_B$
- $a_A=\dfrac{a_B}{3}$
- $a_A=9a_B$

## Explanation

The electric forces are equal because $q$ and $E$ are equal. The magnitude $a=\dfrac Fm$ is inversely proportional to mass, so $A$ has one third the acceleration.
