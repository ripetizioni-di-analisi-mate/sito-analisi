A positive rod approaches a neutral isolated metal sphere without making contact. How does charge on the sphere change?

- The near side becomes positive and the far side negative.
- Each region of the sphere keeps its initial distribution.
- The near side becomes negative and total charge stays zero.
- Total charge becomes negative.

## Explanation

Mobile electrons move toward the positive rod. With no contact or electrical connection, no charge crosses the sphere's boundary, so its net charge remains zero.
