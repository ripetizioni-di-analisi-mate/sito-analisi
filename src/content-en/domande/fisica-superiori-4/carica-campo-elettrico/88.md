A particle crosses a region of length $L$ horizontally while the uniform field is vertical. Initially $v_y=0$. With twice the horizontal speed in a second trial, what happens to the magnitude of vertical deflection at the exit?

- It becomes one quarter.
- It stays the same.
- It halves.
- It doubles.

## Explanation

The field does not change horizontal speed, so the time $t=\dfrac{L}{v_x}$ halves. The deflection magnitude is $\dfrac12|a_y|t^2$ and falls to one quarter.
