Point $P$ is to the right of a negative source $Q$. The sign of $Q$ is reversed and a new point is chosen at the same distance on the left. How does the field vector compare with the original one at $P$?

- It vanishes.
- Its magnitude halves without changing direction.
- It stays the same.
- It reverses without changing magnitude.

## Explanation

To the right of the negative source the field points left. To the left of the positive source it still points left. The same separation and $|Q|$ give the same magnitude.
