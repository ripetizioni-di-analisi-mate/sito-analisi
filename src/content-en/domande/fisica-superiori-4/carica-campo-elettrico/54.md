Two positive sources have charges $Q$ and $9Q$. At the interior cancellation point, how does the distance from the stronger source compare with that from the weaker one?

- It is one third.
- It is three times as large.
- It is nine times as large.
- It is equal.

## Explanation

Equal field magnitudes require canceling the factor $9$ in charge with the same factor in squared distance. The distances are positive, so their ratio is $\sqrt9=3$.
