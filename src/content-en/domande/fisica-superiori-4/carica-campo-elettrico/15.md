In an isolated system two objects start at charges $+9\,\mathrm{nC}$ and $-3\,\mathrm{nC}$. The first ends at $+4\,\mathrm{nC}$. What is the final charge of the second?

- $+2\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $+6\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $-8\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{nC}$

## Explanation

The initial sum is $+6\,\mathrm{nC}$ and is conserved. Therefore the second final charge is $(6-4)\,\mathrm{nC}=+2\,\mathrm{nC}$.
