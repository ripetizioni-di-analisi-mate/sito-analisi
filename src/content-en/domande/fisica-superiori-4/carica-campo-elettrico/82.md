A uniform field has $E_x=+200\,\mathrm{N/C}$. A particle accelerates at $a_x=-4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ under the electric force alone. What is $\dfrac qm$?

- $-800\,\mathrm{C/kg}$
- $-0.020\,\mathrm{C/kg}$
- $-50\,\mathrm{C/kg}$
- $+0.020\,\mathrm{C/kg}$

## Explanation

From $a_x=\dfrac qm E_x$, it follows that $\dfrac qm=\dfrac{-4}{200}\,\mathrm{C/kg}$. The negative ratio agrees with acceleration opposite the field.
