A droplet of mass $2.0\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{kg}$ is stationary in an upward field of $2000\,\mathrm{N/C}$. Only gravity and the electric force act, with $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. What charge is on the droplet?

- $-10\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $+100\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $+1\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $+10\,\mathrm{nC}$

## Explanation

The electric force must point upward to balance weight, so $q>0$. With $qE=mg$ we obtain $q=\dfrac{2.0\cdot10^{-6}\cdot10}{2000}\,\mathrm C=10\,\mathrm{nC}$.
