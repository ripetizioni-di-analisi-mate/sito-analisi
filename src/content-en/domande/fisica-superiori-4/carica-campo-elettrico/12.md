A charge $q=-2.0\,\mathrm{nC}$ is held at rest by a string in an electric field with components $(300,400)\,\mathrm{N/C}$. All other forces are negligible. Which pair is proportional, with a positive factor, to the vector from the charge to the string's anchor?

::grafico{assi=0 uguale=1 punti="0,0,pieno,P" vettori="0,0,1.5,2,E" segmenti="0,2,1.5,2;1.5,0,1.5,2" xmin=-2.5 xmax=3.3 ymin=-2.4 ymax=3.2 titolo="The field components at P are 300 and 400 N/C"}

- $(-3,-4)$
- $(3,4)$
- $(4,3)$
- $(-4,3)$

## Explanation

The force on a negative charge is opposite to the field. To balance it the tension must point along the field, with component ratio $300:400=3:4$. The string pulls toward its anchor.
