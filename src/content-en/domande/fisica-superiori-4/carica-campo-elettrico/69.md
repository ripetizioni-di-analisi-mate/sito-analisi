In a dipole, the negative charge is above the positive charge. In which direction does the dipole moment vector $\vec p$ point?

- Downward.
- Upward.
- Rightward.
- It is zero because the net charge is zero.

## Explanation

By definition $\vec p$ points from the negative charge to the positive charge. Here the two charges are vertically separated and the positive one is below.
