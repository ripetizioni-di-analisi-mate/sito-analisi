A charge $q=-2.4\,\mathrm{nC}$ lies in a uniform field of $450\,\mathrm{N/C}$ pointing right. What is the horizontal force component if right is positive?

- $+1.08\,\mathrm{\mu N}$
- $-0.54\,\mathrm{\mu N}$
- $-10.8\,\mathrm{\mu N}$
- $-1.08\,\mathrm{\mu N}$

## Explanation

Multiply the positive field component by the negative charge. The product $(-2.4\cdot10^{-9})\cdot450\,\mathrm N=-1.08\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm N$ means the force points left.
