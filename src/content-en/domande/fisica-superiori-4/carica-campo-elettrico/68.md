A curved field line has a leftward horizontal tangent at $P$. Which way does the force on a small positive charge point at $P$?

- Perpendicular to the tangent.
- Horizontally right.
- Toward the line's center of curvature.
- Horizontally left.

## Explanation

The field at $P$ follows the directed tangent. The force on a positive charge points along the field, so it points left.
