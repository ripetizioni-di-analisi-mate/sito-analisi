At the midpoint two positive sources give opposite contributions of equal magnitude. Only the right source is moved to twice its distance from the observation point, on the same side. What fraction of the left contribution is now the total field?

- $\dfrac14$
- $\dfrac34$
- $0$
- $\dfrac54$

## Explanation

The moved source gives one quarter of its original magnitude and still points left. The total is therefore $1-\dfrac14=\dfrac34$ of the rightward contribution from the left source.
