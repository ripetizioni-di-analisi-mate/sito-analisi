In a pair of point charges, the sign of one charge is reversed without changing its magnitude or position. What happens to the force on the second charge?

- It vanishes.
- Its magnitude doubles and its direction stays the same.
- It keeps its magnitude and reverses direction.
- It keeps both its magnitude and its direction.

## Explanation

The absolute value of the charge product stays the same. The signed product reverses sign, so the force reverses along the line joining the charges.
