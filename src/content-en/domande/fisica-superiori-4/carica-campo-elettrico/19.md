With a positive rod nearby, a metal sphere is grounded. The ground connection is removed first and the rod is then moved away. Which net charge remains?

- The sign depends only on the sphere's radius.
- Zero.
- Negative.
- Positive.

## Explanation

The positive rod attracts electrons from ground onto the sphere. Disconnecting the ground while the rod is present leaves that excess of electrons on the sphere.
