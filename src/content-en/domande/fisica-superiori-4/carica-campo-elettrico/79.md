A particle starts from rest with constant electric acceleration $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ along $+x$. How much does its position change in the first $3\,\mathrm s$?

- $+6\,\mathrm m$
- $+18\,\mathrm m$
- $+3\,\mathrm m$
- $+9\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Since the particle starts from rest, $\Delta x=\dfrac12 at^2$. The given values yield $\dfrac12\cdot2\cdot3^2\,\mathrm m=+9\,\mathrm m$.
