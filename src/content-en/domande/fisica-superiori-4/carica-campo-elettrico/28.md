The magnitude of one charge is doubled and its separation from a second unchanged charge is tripled. By what factor does the force magnitude change?

- $\dfrac19$
- $\dfrac23$
- $\dfrac29$
- $\dfrac92$

## Explanation

The numerator gains a factor of $2$. The squared distance in the denominator gives $3^2=9$, so the force is multiplied by $\dfrac29$.
