A dipole has charges of magnitude $1.8\,\mathrm{nC}$ separated by $2.0\,\mathrm{cm}$. It is perpendicular to a uniform field of $800\,\mathrm{N/C}$. What is the torque magnitude?

- $0\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $2.88\cdot10^{-8}\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $1.44\cdot10^{-8}\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $1.44\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{N\,m}$

## Explanation

At $90^\circ$ the moment arm is the full separation $d$. Thus $M=qEd=(1.8\cdot10^{-9})\cdot800\cdot0.020\,\mathrm{N\,m}=2.88\cdot10^{-8}\,\mathrm{N\,m}$.
