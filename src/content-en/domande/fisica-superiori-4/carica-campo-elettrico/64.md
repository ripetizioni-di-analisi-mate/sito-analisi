Two contributions to a field are $500\,\mathrm{N/C}$ rightward and $200\,\mathrm{N/C}$ leftward. What is the horizontal force component on $q=-4\,\mathrm{nC}$, taking right as positive?

- $-2.8\,\mathrm{\mu N}$
- $+1.2\,\mathrm{\mu N}$
- $-1.2\,\mathrm{\mu N}$
- $-0.60\,\mathrm{\mu N}$

## Explanation

The field component is $500-200=300$ in $\mathrm{N/C}$. Multiply by $-4\cdot10^{-9}\,\mathrm C$ to get $-1.2\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm N$.
