At a point $30\,\mathrm{cm}$ to the left of an isolated point charge, the measured field is $200\,\mathrm{N/C}$ to the right. With $k_e=9.0\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$, what is the source charge?

- $-2.0\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $-6.67\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $+2.0\,\mathrm{nC}$

## Explanation

From the observation point the source lies to the right. The field points toward the source, so $Q$ is negative. Its magnitude is $\dfrac{200\cdot0.30^2}{9.0\cdot10^9}\,\mathrm C=2.0\,\mathrm{nC}$.
