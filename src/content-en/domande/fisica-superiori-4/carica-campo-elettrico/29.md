Two charges with fixed magnitudes exert a force of $18\,\mathrm{\mu N}$ at $6\,\mathrm{cm}$. At what separation does it decrease to $2\,\mathrm{\mu N}$?

- $2\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $18\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $12\,\mathrm{cm}$
- $54\,\mathrm{cm}$

## Explanation

The magnitude decreases by a factor of $9$. Since it is inversely proportional to $r^2$, the separation increases by $\sqrt9=3$ and becomes $18\,\mathrm{cm}$.
