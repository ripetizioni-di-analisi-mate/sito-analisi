Two point charges in vacuum have magnitudes $2.4\,\mathrm{nC}$ and $3.0\,\mathrm{nC}$. They are $12\,\mathrm{cm}$ apart. With $k_e=8.99\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$, which value approximates the force magnitude?

- $4.50\,\mathrm{\mu N}$
- $0.0450\,\mathrm{\mu N}$
- $44.95\,\mathrm{\mu N}$
- $0.54\,\mathrm{\mu N}$

## Explanation

For point charges, $F=k_e\dfrac{|q_1q_2|}{r^2}$. Using coulombs and meters gives $F=\dfrac{8.99\cdot10^9\cdot(2.4\cdot10^{-9})(3.0\cdot10^{-9})}{0.12^2}\,\mathrm N\simeq4.50\,\mathrm{\mu N}$.
