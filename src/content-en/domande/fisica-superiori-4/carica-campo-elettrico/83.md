A particle with charge $+6\,\mathrm{nC}$ accelerates at $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ in a field of $500\,\mathrm{N/C}$. Other forces are negligible. What is its mass?

- $3.0\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $9.0\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $1.0\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{kg}$
- $1.0\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{kg}$

## Explanation

The force magnitude is $(6\cdot10^{-9})\cdot500\,\mathrm N=3.0\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm N$. Dividing by $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ gives $m=1.0\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{kg}$.
