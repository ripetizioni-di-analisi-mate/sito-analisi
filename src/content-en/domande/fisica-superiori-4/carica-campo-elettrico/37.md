What unit results from $k_e\dfrac{q_1q_2}{r^2}$ if $k_e$ is in $\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$?

- $\mathrm N$
- $\mathrm{N/C}$
- $\mathrm{J}$
- $\mathrm{C/N}$

## Explanation

The charge product contributes $\mathrm{C^2}$ and the squared distance in the denominator contributes $\mathrm{m^2}$. These cancel their counterparts in $k_e$, leaving newtons.
