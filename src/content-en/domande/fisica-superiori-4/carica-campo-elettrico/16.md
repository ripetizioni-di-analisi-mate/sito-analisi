A neutral object is charged solely by transferring electrons. Which final charge is compatible with charge quantization?

- $+\dfrac e3$
- $-3e$
- $+\dfrac e2$
- $-\dfrac{3e}{2}$

## Explanation

Each transfer adds or removes an integer multiple of $e$. Of the options, only $-3e$ corresponds to a whole number of electrons.
