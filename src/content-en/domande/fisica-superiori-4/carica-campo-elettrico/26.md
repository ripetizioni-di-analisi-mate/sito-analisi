Charge $q_1$ is to the left of $q_2$. Both are negative. In which direction does the force of $q_1$ on $q_2$ point?

- Leftward.
- Upward.
- The force is zero.
- Rightward.

## Explanation

The product $q_1q_2$ is positive, so the force on $q_2$ points along the unit vector from $q_1$ to $q_2$, to the right.
