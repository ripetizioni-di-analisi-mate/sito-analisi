A charge enters a uniform-field region with $v_x(0)=+12\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Its electric acceleration is $a_x=-3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and the field extends over the entire motion considered. When does it first stop?

- Its velocity never reaches zero.
- After $6\,\mathrm s$.
- After $2\,\mathrm s$.
- After $4\,\mathrm s$.

## Explanation

The velocity decreases by $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ each second. The condition $12-3t=0$ gives $t=4\,\mathrm s$.
