A negative rod approaches a neutral isolated metal sphere. Where does a deficit of electrons form?

- On the side nearest the rod.
- Uniformly throughout the metal.
- The rod transfers electrons to the near side.
- On the side farthest from the rod.

## Explanation

The negative rod repels mobile electrons to the far side. The near side is left with a deficit of electrons and therefore a local positive charge.
