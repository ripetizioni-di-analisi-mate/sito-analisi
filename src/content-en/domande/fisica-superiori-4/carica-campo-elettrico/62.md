Two identical positive charges lie on opposite sides of the origin, equally far along $x$. A third positive charge is on the $y$ axis above the origin. Which way does the total field point at the origin?

- It is zero.
- Upward.
- Downward.
- Rightward.

## Explanation

The horizontal fields from the two sides cancel at the origin. The third positive charge gives an outward contribution which, below the source, points down.
