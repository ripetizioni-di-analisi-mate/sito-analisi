A test charge of $+3\,\mathrm{nC}$ experiences a downward force of $1.2\,\mathrm{\mu N}$. What is the field?

- $0.40\,\mathrm{N/C}$ downward.
- $400\,\mathrm{N/C}$ downward.
- $400\,\mathrm{N/C}$ upward.
- $3600\,\mathrm{N/C}$ downward.

## Explanation

For a positive test charge, the field points along the force. Dividing $1.2\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm N$ by $3\cdot10^{-9}\,\mathrm C$ gives $400\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
