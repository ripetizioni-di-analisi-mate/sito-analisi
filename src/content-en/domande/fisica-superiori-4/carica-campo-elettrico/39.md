A positive test charge is replaced with twice that charge at the same point, without altering the sources. What happens to field and force?

- Both double.
- The field stays the same and the force doubles.
- The field halves and the force stays the same.
- The force stays the same and the field doubles.

## Explanation

The field depends on its sources. From $\vec F=q_0\vec E$, doubling $q_0$ doubles only the force.
