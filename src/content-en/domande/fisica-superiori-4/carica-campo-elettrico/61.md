Point $P$ is off the line through two nonzero point charges, the only sources. Why can their total field not vanish at $P$?

- The radial directions are not collinear.
- Both fields are always vertical.
- Their magnitudes can never be equal.
- The field is always perpendicular to the line through the sources.

## Explanation

The two vectors at $P$ lie along the lines to the individual sources. Off the source line those directions differ, while two nonzero vectors can cancel only if they are opposite.
