Charges $+3.6\,\mathrm{nC}$ on the left and $+1.0\,\mathrm{nC}$ on the right are $20\,\mathrm{cm}$ apart. With $k_e=8.99\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$, what is the approximate field component at the midpoint along the positive rightward direction?

- $+2.34\cdot10^3\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $+4.14\cdot10^3\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $-2.34\cdot10^3\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $+0.585\cdot10^3\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

The contributions point in opposite directions. At $0.10\,\mathrm m$ from both sources, the component is $E_x=\dfrac{k_e(3.6-1.0)\cdot10^{-9}}{0.10^2}\,\mathrm{N/C}\simeq+2.34\cdot10^3\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
