In a drawing, two electric field lines cross at a point where the field is defined and nonzero. What is wrong with the drawing?

- It uses lines that can exist only in vacuum.
- The drawing is correct if the field is strong enough.
- It predicts that every positive charge disappears there.
- It gives the field two directions at the same point.

## Explanation

The tangent to a line gives the field direction. A crossing with different tangents gives two directions to a single vector, so it is inconsistent with the field.
