The resultant field has components $E_x=-6\,\mathrm{N/C}$ and $E_y=+8\,\mathrm{N/C}$. Which description is correct for axes pointing right and up?

- Magnitude $2\,\mathrm{N/C}$, pointing left and down.
- Magnitude $14\,\mathrm{N/C}$, pointing left and up.
- Magnitude $10\,\mathrm{N/C}$, pointing left and up.
- Magnitude $10\,\mathrm{N/C}$, pointing right and up.

## Explanation

The magnitude is $\sqrt{(-6)^2+8^2}\,\mathrm{N/C}=10\,\mathrm{N/C}$. Negative $x$ and positive $y$ place the vector in the second quadrant.
