Two positive charges produce zero field at a point $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the first and $30\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the second. What is the ratio of the second charge to the first?

- $9$
- $3$
- $1$
- $\dfrac19$

## Explanation

The magnitudes satisfy $\dfrac{Q_1}{10^2}=\dfrac{Q_2}{30^2}$ using the same distance units. Therefore $\dfrac{Q_2}{Q_1}=\left(\dfrac{30}{10}\right)^2=9$.
