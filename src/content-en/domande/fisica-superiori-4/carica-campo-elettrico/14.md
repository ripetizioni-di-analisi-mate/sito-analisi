Charges $+Q$ and $-4Q$ occupy $x=0$ and $x=0.60\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the coordinate where the field is zero?

- $-0.60\,\mathrm{m}$
- $+1.20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $+0.20\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-0.20\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Between sources both fields point the same way. To the right the stronger source is also closer. The point must be left of $+Q$, where the distances $s$ and $s+0.60$ must satisfy $s+0.60=2s$. Thus $x=-0.60\,\mathrm{m}$.
