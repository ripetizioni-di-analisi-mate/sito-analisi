A field measurement is repeated along the same ray at twice the distance from a point source whose charge has quadrupled. What happens to the field?

- The magnitude quadruples.
- The vector reverses direction.
- The magnitude doubles.
- The vector stays the same.

## Explanation

The factor $4$ in charge is canceled by $2^2=4$ in the denominator. The source sign and observation ray also preserve direction.
