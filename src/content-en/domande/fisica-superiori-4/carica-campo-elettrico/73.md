A dipole in a uniform field has $\vec p$ pointing along $\vec E$. What are its net force and torque?

- Zero force and maximum torque.
- Both zero.
- Both nonzero.
- Nonzero force and zero torque.

## Explanation

In a uniform field the forces cancel. With $\theta=0^\circ$ their lines of action coincide, so the torque is zero as well.
