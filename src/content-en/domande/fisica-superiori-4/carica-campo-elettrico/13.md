The charge $+3\,\mathrm{nC}$ is $60\,\mathrm{cm}$ to the left of $-2\,\mathrm{nC}$. With $k_e=9.0\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$, what is the field component at the midpoint, with right positive?

::grafico{assi=0 punti="-3,0,pieno,+3 nC;3,0,pieno,−2 nC;0,0,pieno,P" xmin=-4.3 xmax=4.3 ymin=-1.5 ymax=1.5 titolo="Midpoint P is 30 cm from each source"}

- $+500\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $+125\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $+100\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $-500\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

At the midpoint the field points away from the positive charge and toward the negative charge. Both point right, so add the magnitudes $300\,\mathrm{N/C}$ and $200\,\mathrm{N/C}$ calculated at $0.30\,\mathrm m$ from the sources.
