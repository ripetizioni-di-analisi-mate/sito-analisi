Two charged spheres have radii comparable to their separation and unknown charge distributions. Which assumption of the point-charge calculation needs attention?

- That the charges always have the same sign.
- That dimensions are negligible compared with separation, unless there is suitable symmetry.
- That the two bodies have equal mass.
- That the force acts only when the bodies touch.

## Explanation

The point-charge formula assigns each charge a single position. If dimensions are not small, a symmetry allowing this representation is needed, or the distribution must be taken into account.
