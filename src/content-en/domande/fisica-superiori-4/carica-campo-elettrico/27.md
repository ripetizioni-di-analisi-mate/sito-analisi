Positive charge $q_1$ has three times the magnitude of negative charge $q_2$. How do the magnitudes of their mutual forces compare?

- The force on $q_2$ is three times as large.
- They are equal.
- One force is zero.
- The force on $q_1$ is three times as large.

## Explanation

The two forces have the same product $|q_1q_2|$ and the same squared distance. They have equal magnitudes and opposite directions, acting on different bodies.
