Two points on the same circle centered at a positive point charge are $90^\circ$ apart as seen from the center. How do their fields compare?

- They have equal magnitudes and are perpendicular.
- They are equal vectors.
- One is zero.
- They have equal magnitudes and point in opposite directions.

## Explanation

The common radius gives equal magnitudes. The directions are radially outward and make the same angle as the two radii, namely $90^\circ$.
