A uniform field of $900\,\mathrm{N/C}$ points right. A $+9\,\mathrm{nC}$ source is at the origin. With $k_e=9.0\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$, at what coordinate does the net field vanish?

- $+0.30\,\mathrm m$
- $-0.090\,\mathrm m$
- $-0.90\,\mathrm m$
- $-0.30\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The charge's field opposes the uniform field on the negative half-axis. Equal magnitudes give $r^2=\dfrac{9.0\cdot10^9\cdot9\cdot10^{-9}}{900}\,\mathrm{m^2}=0.090\,\mathrm{m^2}$, so $x=-0.30\,\mathrm m$.
