A uniform field of $200\,\mathrm{N/C}$ pointing up exerts a downward force of $1.0\,\mathrm{\mu N}$. What is the charge?

- $-5.0\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $+5.0\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $-0.50\,\mathrm{nC}$
- $-200\,\mathrm{nC}$

## Explanation

With up positive, $q=\dfrac{F_y}{E_y}=\dfrac{-1.0\cdot10^{-6}}{200}\,\mathrm C=-5.0\,\mathrm{nC}$. The sign reflects the force pointing opposite to the field.
