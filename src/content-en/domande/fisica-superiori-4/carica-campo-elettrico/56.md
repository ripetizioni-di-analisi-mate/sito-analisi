For two positive charges, consider a point on their line to the right of both. Can the fields cancel there?

- Yes, if the stronger source is closer.
- No, because both point left.
- Yes, if the charges have equal magnitude.
- No, both point right.

## Explanation

The field from a positive source points outward. To the right of both sources, outward is rightward for both, so the sum is nonzero.
