A neutral conducting sphere and an identical sphere carrying $-8\,\mathrm{nC}$ are touched in isolation and separated symmetrically. What are their final charges?

- Both $-4\,\mathrm{nC}$.
- Both $+4\,\mathrm{nC}$.
- One $+4\,\mathrm{nC}$ and the other $-4\,\mathrm{nC}$.
- One $-8\,\mathrm{nC}$ and the other zero.

## Explanation

Symmetry makes the final charges equal and their sum remains $-8\,\mathrm{nC}$. Each is therefore $-4\,\mathrm{nC}$.
