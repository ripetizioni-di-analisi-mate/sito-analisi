Two charges of equal magnitude repel. Both are multiplied by $-2$ without moving them. How do the magnitude and character of the force change?

- The magnitude quadruples and the force becomes attractive.
- The magnitude doubles and the force becomes attractive.
- The magnitude quadruples and the force remains repulsive.
- The magnitude stays the same and the force remains repulsive.

## Explanation

The charge product is multiplied by $(-2)(-2)=4$. It stays positive, so the magnitude quadruples and the interaction stays repulsive.
