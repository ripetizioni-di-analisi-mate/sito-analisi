An object of charge $+0.32\,\mathrm{\mu C}$ receives $3.0\cdot10^{12}$ electrons. With $e=1.60\cdot10^{-19}\,\mathrm C$, what is its final charge?

- $-0.16\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $+0.80\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $+0.16\,\mathrm{\mu C}$
- $-0.48\,\mathrm{\mu C}$

## Explanation

The electrons carry $-0.48\,\mathrm{\mu C}$. Adding this to the initial charge gives $(0.32-0.48)\,\mathrm{\mu C}=-0.16\,\mathrm{\mu C}$.
