Two neutral objects are rubbed together in isolation. $A$ becomes negatively charged. Which way did the excess electrons move, and what is the sign of $B$?

- From $A$ to $B$, and $B$ is positive.
- From $B$ to $A$, and $B$ is positive.
- From $B$ to $A$, and $B$ is negative.
- From $A$ to $B$, and $B$ stays neutral.

## Explanation

The negative charge of $A$ means it gained electrons. Since the system is isolated and starts neutral, $B$ lost them and becomes positive.
