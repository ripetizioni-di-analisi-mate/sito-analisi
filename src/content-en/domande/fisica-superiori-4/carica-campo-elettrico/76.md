The ends of a dipole lie where both fields point right but have different magnitudes. What can be said about the dipole's total force?

- It is zero because the total charge is zero.
- It is zero in every field.
- It is always perpendicular to the fields.
- It is nonzero.

## Explanation

The forces point oppositely because the charges have different signs. Their magnitudes differ because the field is different at the two ends, so they do not cancel.
