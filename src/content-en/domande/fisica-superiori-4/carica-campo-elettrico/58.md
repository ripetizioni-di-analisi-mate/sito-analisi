Two point sources $+Q$ and $-Q$ are separated. Is there any point at finite distance from both sources, other than their positions, where the total field is zero?

- Yes, everywhere on the perpendicular bisector.
- Yes, to the right of the negative source.
- Yes, at the midpoint only.
- No.

## Explanation

To cancel, the vectors must be opposite and have equal magnitudes. Equal magnitudes require equal distances, and the only such point on the line through the sources is the midpoint. There both fields point the same way.
