An initially neutral object reaches charge $+0.48\,\mathrm{\mu C}$ by electron transfer. With $e=1.60\cdot10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}$, what is $\Delta N=N_f-N_i$, the signed change in the number of electrons?

- $-4.8\cdot10^{12}$
- $+3\cdot10^{12}$
- $-3\cdot10^6$
- $-3\cdot10^{12}$

## Explanation

The change in charge is $\Delta q=-e\Delta N$. Since $\Delta q$ is positive, the electron count decreases. The calculation gives $\Delta N=-\dfrac{0.48\cdot10^{-6}}{1.60\cdot10^{-19}}=-3\cdot10^{12}$.
