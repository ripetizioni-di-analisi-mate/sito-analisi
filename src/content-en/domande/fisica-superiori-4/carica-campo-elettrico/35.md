The product of the charge magnitudes stays the same, but separation goes from $9$ to $3\,\mathrm{cm}$. How does the force magnitude change?

- It becomes nine times as large.
- It falls to one ninth.
- It remains the same.
- It becomes three times as large.

## Explanation

The new denominator is the square of one third of the initial distance, so it is one ninth of the previous denominator. The force magnitude is nine times as large.
