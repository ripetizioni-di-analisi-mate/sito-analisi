At distance $r$ from a point charge the electric field has magnitude $800\,\mathrm{N/C}$. At what distance from the same charge is the field magnitude $200\,\mathrm{N/C}$?

- $2r$
- $\dfrac r2$
- $8r$
- $4r$

## Explanation

The field of a point charge has magnitude $E=k_e\dfrac{|q|}{r^2}$, so it is inversely proportional to the square of the distance. The magnitude goes from $800$ to $200\,\mathrm{N/C}$, that is, it falls to one quarter, so the square of the distance becomes four times as large. Consequently the distance doubles and equals $2r$.
