A sensor measures $800\,\mathrm{N/C}$ at distance $r$ from a point charge. At what distance from the same charge would it measure $200\,\mathrm{N/C}$?

- $2r$
- $\dfrac r2$
- $8r$
- $4r$

## Explanation

The field magnitude must fall to one quarter. The square of the distance must therefore become four times as large, so the distance doubles.
