Two opposite sources of equal magnitude lie at the endpoints of a segment. A positive test charge is at its midpoint. Which way does the net force point?

- The force is zero.
- Toward the negative source.
- Toward the positive source.
- Perpendicular to the segment.

## Explanation

The field points away from the positive source and toward the negative one, so the two contributions point the same way. The positive test charge preserves that direction in the force.
