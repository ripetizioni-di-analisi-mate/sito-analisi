A dipole starts perpendicular to a uniform field. It turns to make $30^\circ$ with that field, with charge and separation unchanged. How does the torque magnitude change?

- It doubles.
- It falls to one quarter.
- It halves.
- It stays the same.

## Explanation

The factors $p$ and $E$ are unchanged. The sine of the angle falls from $1$ to $\dfrac12$, so the torque halves.
