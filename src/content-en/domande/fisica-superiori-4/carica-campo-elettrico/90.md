A particle moves above a horizontal plane and is free to accelerate in any direction. The electric force and weight are vertical, with the electric force pointing up and half the weight in magnitude. What is its acceleration?

- Zero.
- $\dfrac g2$ upward.
- $\dfrac g2$ downward.
- $\dfrac{3g}{2}$ downward.

## Explanation

Taking up as positive, the net force is $\dfrac{mg}{2}-mg=-\dfrac{mg}{2}$. Dividing by mass gives downward acceleration of magnitude $\dfrac g2$.
