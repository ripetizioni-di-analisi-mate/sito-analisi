A vertical rod carries $+q$ at the top and $-q$ at the bottom. The uniform field points right. Looking straight at the diagram, which way does the dipole tend to turn?

::grafico{assi=0 punti="0,1,pieno,+q;0,-1,pieno,−q" linee="0,-1 0,1" vettori="1,0,2.6,0,E" xmin=-2 xmax=3.4 ymin=-2 ymax=2.3 titolo="Vertical dipole in a uniform field pointing right"}

- It does not turn because the net force is zero.
- It rotates in alternating directions from the outset.
- Counterclockwise.
- Clockwise.

## Explanation

The force on the upper charge points right and that on the lower charge points left. The force couple moves the upper end toward the right and downward, tending to produce clockwise rotation.
