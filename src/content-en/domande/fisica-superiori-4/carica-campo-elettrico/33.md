A force result uses $r=5$ with $k_e$ in SI units, but the given distance was $5\,\mathrm{cm}$. By what factor should the calculated magnitude be multiplied?

- $10^4$
- $10^{-4}$
- $10^2$
- $10^{-2}$

## Explanation

The value of $r$ in meters is a hundred times smaller than the inserted value. Squaring makes the denominator $10^4$ times smaller and the force increases by that factor.
