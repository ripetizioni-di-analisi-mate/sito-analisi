Two sources, $-9Q$ on the left and $+Q$ on the right, with $Q>0$, are on a line. In which region can their field vanish?

- To the left of $-9Q$.
- To the right of $+Q$.
- Between the sources.
- At points off the line through the sources.

## Explanation

Between the sources both fields point the same way. Outside, they point oppositely, but the stronger charge must be farther away to produce the same magnitude. That happens on the outer side of the weaker charge, to the right.
