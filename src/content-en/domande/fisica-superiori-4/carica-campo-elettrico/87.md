A classical calculation in a uniform field predicts an electron's final speed comparable to the speed of light. Which assumption must be reconsidered before using the result?

- That the electron has negative charge.
- That classical laws of motion are adequate.
- That speed is measured in $\mathrm{m/s}$.
- That a uniform field points the same way everywhere.

## Explanation

The classical formulas used here require speeds small relative to the speed of light. If the speed is comparable, the motion requires a relativistic description.
