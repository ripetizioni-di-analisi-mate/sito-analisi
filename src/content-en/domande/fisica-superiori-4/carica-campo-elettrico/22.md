Two conducting spheres of different radii are connected by a wire and reach equilibrium. Which property must be equal?

- Charge density at every point on their surfaces.
- Net charge.
- Electric potential.
- Radius.

## Explanation

The connection lets charge move until the two conductors have equal potential. Equal division of charge would require symmetry conditions that do not hold here.
