A uniform vertical field accelerates a particle initially moving horizontally. After $2\,\mathrm s$ its vertical velocity component is $-6\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the vertical displacement over that interval?

- $-6\,\mathrm m$
- $-12\,\mathrm m$
- $-3\,\mathrm m$
- $+6\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Acceleration is constant and the initial vertical velocity is zero. The average component over the interval is $\dfrac{0-6}{2}\,\mathrm{m/s}=-3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Multiplying it by $2\,\mathrm s$ gives $-6\,\mathrm m$.
