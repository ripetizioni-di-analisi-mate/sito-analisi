A source $Q=-3\,\mathrm{nC}$ is at the origin. With $k_e=9.0\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$, what is $E_x$ at $x=-0.30\,\mathrm m$, with positive $x$ to the right?

- $-300\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $+30\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $+300\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $+90\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

From a point on the left the field points toward the negative source, so it points right. Its magnitude is $\dfrac{9.0\cdot10^9\cdot3\cdot10^{-9}}{0.30^2}\,\mathrm{N/C}=300\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
