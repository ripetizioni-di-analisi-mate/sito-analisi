Which unit fits the ratio of the electric force magnitude to the test charge magnitude?

- $\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $\mathrm{N/C}$
- $\mathrm{N\,C}$
- $\mathrm{C/N}$

## Explanation

Divide a force in newtons by a charge in coulombs. The ratio is measured in newtons per coulomb.
