In an electrostatic diagram, lines connect two opposite charges directly. From which charge do the arrows start?

- The lines have no direction.
- From both toward the center.
- From the negative charge.
- From the positive charge.

## Explanation

Near a positive source the field points outward, while near a negative one it points inward. The directed line therefore goes from the positive charge to the negative charge.
