One point charge is at the origin and a second is directly above it. The charges attract. Which components of the force on the second can be nonzero if the $y$ axis points up?

- Only $F_x$, with positive sign.
- Only $F_y$, with positive sign.
- Only $F_y$, with negative sign.
- Both $F_x$ and $F_y$.

## Explanation

The line joining the charges is vertical. Attraction points the force on the second toward the origin, downward, so $F_x=0$ and $F_y<0$.
