Positive charges $Q$ and $4Q$ are separated by $0.54\,\mathrm{m}$. At what distance from $Q$ does the total field vanish?

- $0.36\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.108\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.27\,\mathrm{m}$
- $0.18\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The fields point in opposite directions between the sources. The charge $4Q$ must be twice as far away to produce the same field, so $0.54-x=2x$ and $x=0.18\,\mathrm{m}$.
