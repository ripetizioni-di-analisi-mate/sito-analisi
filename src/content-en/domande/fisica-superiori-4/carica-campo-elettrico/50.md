One field contribution is $300\,\mathrm{N/C}$ rightward and another is $400\,\mathrm{N/C}$ upward. What is the resultant magnitude?

- $100\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $700\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $500\,\mathrm{N/C}$
- $350\,\mathrm{N/C}$

## Explanation

The components lie along perpendicular axes. The magnitude is $\sqrt{300^2+400^2}\,\mathrm{N/C}=500\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
