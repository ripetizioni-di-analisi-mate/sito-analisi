An object has $4.8\cdot10^{12}$ excess electrons. With $e=1.60\cdot10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}$, what is its net charge?

- $-3.0\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{C}$
- $-7.68\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{C}$
- $-7.68\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{C}$
- $+7.68\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{C}$

## Explanation

Each excess electron carries charge $-e$. Thus $q=-(4.8\cdot10^{12})(1.60\cdot10^{-19})\,\mathrm{C}=-7.68\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm{C}$.
