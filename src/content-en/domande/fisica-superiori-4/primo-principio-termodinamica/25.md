During rapid expansion in a cylinder with an ideal piston of negligible mass and friction, the gas has a different internal pressure at different points. Which information gives the boundary work in the piston model?

- Only the initial internal pressure.
- The mean of only the endpoint internal pressures.
- Only the final temperature.
- The external pressure along the displacement.

## Explanation

The resisting force on the piston is found from the external pressure times the area. The equilibrium endpoints determine changes in state functions, but work also requires the displacement under that force.
