A gas does $250\,\mathrm{J}$ of net work over a cycle and releases a total of $400\,\mathrm{J}$ as heat during its heat-loss stages. How much heat does it absorb during the other stages?

- $250\,\mathrm{J}$
- $650\,\mathrm{J}$
- $400\,\mathrm{J}$
- $150\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Internal energy returns to its initial value. The incoming heat must therefore supply both the heat released and the net work transferred to the surroundings.

$$
Q_{\mathrm{ass}}=W_{\mathrm{tot}}+|Q_{\mathrm{ced}}|=(250+400)\,\mathrm{J}=650\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
