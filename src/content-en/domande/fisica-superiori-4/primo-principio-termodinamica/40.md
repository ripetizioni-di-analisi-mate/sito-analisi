An ideal gas with heat capacity $nC_V=18\,\mathrm{J/K}$ releases $270\,\mathrm{J}$ in an isochoric process. Only volume work is possible. What is the temperature change?

- $15\,\mathrm{K}$
- $0\,\mathrm{K}$
- $-15\,\mathrm{K}$
- $-30\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

Heat is negative and the rigid container prevents volume work, so internal energy falls by $270$ joules. Each kelvin corresponds to $18$ joules, so the temperature drops by $15$ kelvins.

$$
\Delta T=\frac{Q}{nC_V}=\frac{-270}{18}\,\mathrm{K}=-15\,\mathrm{K}.
$$
