In a cycle the gas receives a total of $900\,\mathrm{J}$ as heat in the absorption stages and releases $520\,\mathrm{J}$ in the other stages. What is the net work?

- $1420\,\mathrm{J}$
- $520\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-380\,\mathrm{J}$
- $380\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The change in internal energy in a cycle is zero. To calculate net heat, give a positive sign to what enters and a negative sign to what leaves.

$$
W_{\mathrm{tot}}=Q_{\mathrm{tot}}=(900-520)\,\mathrm{J}=380\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
