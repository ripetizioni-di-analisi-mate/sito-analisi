An ideal gas expands into a vacuum inside a rigid, insulated vessel, with no other work transfers. Which pair describes transfers across the system boundary?

- $Q>0$ and $W>0$.
- $Q=0$ and $W>0$.
- $Q<0$ and $W=0$.
- $Q=0$ and $W=0$.

## Explanation

Insulation makes the heat transfer zero, and expansion is against zero external pressure. The gas occupies a larger space without transferring energy to the surroundings.
