Two quasistatic expansions occur between the same volumes. The pressure along A exceeds that along B at every intermediate volume. How do the amounts of work compare?

- $W_A<W_B$.
- $W_A=W_B$.
- The initial temperature is required.
- $W_A>W_B$.

## Explanation

The contributions $p\Delta V$ are positive along both paths. On A, every strip of the diagram is higher, so the total area is larger.
