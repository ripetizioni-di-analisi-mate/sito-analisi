An ideal gas at $300\,\mathrm{K}$, maintained quasistatically at constant pressure, goes from $3$ to $5\,\mathrm{L}$. What is the final temperature?

- $500\,\mathrm{K}$
- $180\,\mathrm{K}$
- $300\,\mathrm{K}$
- $800\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

The isobaric process of a fixed amount of an ideal gas has constant $\dfrac{V}{T}$. Increasing the volume from three to five liters multiplies the absolute temperature by five thirds.

$$
T_f=T_i\frac{V_f}{V_i}=300\cdot\frac53\,\mathrm{K}=500\,\mathrm{K}.
$$
