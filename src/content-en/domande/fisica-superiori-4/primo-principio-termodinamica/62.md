An ideal gas has $nC_V=25\,\mathrm{J/K}$ and an initial temperature of $310\,\mathrm{K}$. It does $1500\,\mathrm{J}$ of work in an adiabatic process. What is the final temperature?

- $250\,\mathrm{K}$
- $370\,\mathrm{K}$
- $310\,\mathrm{K}$
- $125\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

The gas gives up $1500$ joules as work without receiving any as heat. The decrease in internal energy corresponds to $60$ kelvins, which are subtracted from the initial temperature.

$$
T_f=T_i-\frac{W}{nC_V}=(310-60)\,\mathrm{K}=250\,\mathrm{K}.
$$
