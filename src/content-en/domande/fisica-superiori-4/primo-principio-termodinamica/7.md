An ideal gas containing $0.78\,\mathrm{mol}$ expands reversibly at $320\,\mathrm{K}$ from $10$ to $25\,\mathrm{L}$. With $R=8.314\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$ and $\ln(2.5)\simeq0.916$, which value is closest to the work done by the gas?

- $W\simeq 1900\,\mathrm{J}$
- $W\simeq 3110\,\mathrm{J}$
- $W\simeq 825\,\mathrm{J}$
- $W\simeq -1900\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

At constant temperature, the gas pressure falls as $\dfrac{1}{V}$ during expansion. The area under this curve gives $nRT$ multiplied by the natural logarithm of the volume ratio.

$$
W=0.78\cdot8.314\cdot320\ln\frac{25}{10}\,\mathrm{J}\simeq1900\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
