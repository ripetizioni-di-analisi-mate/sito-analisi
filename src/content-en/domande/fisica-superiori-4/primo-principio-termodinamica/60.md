From the same initial state A, a monatomic ideal gas expands reversibly along curves B and C. One is isothermal and the other adiabatic. Which curve is adiabatic?

::grafico{f="320/x;320/x^(5/3)" punti="1,320,pieno,A" xmin=0 xmax=3.3 ymin=0 ymax=440 nomi="V,p (kPa)" legenda="\mathrm{B};\mathrm{C}" titolo="Both paths start at A and continue toward larger volumes. Volume is in liters."}

- Curve C, with lower pressure at the same volume.
- Curve B, with higher pressure at the same volume.
- Both curves.
- Neither curve.

## Explanation

In the adiabatic process the energy needed for work comes from the gas's internal energy. The temperature falls, and at the same volume the pressure is lower than on the isothermal curve through A.
