The work done on the gas is $480\,\mathrm{J}$ and its internal energy decreases by $120\,\mathrm{J}$. What is the signed heat?

- $-360\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-600\,\mathrm{J}$
- $600\,\mathrm{J}$
- $360\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The gas receives energy as work and ends with less internal energy. Thus the amount of heat released must exceed both the internal energy decrease and the work received.

$$
Q=\Delta U+W=(-120-480)\,\mathrm{J}=-600\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
