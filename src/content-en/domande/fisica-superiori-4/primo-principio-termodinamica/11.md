On the $p$-$V$ diagram, the gas quasistatically follows the segment from $A=(1\,\mathrm{L},300\,\mathrm{kPa})$ to $B=(4\,\mathrm{L},100\,\mathrm{kPa})$. How much work does it do?

::grafico{linee="1,300 4,100" punti="1,300,pieno,A;4,100,pieno,B" vettori="2,233.3333,3,166.6667" xmin=0 xmax=5 ymin=0 ymax=380 nomi="V,p (kPa)" titolo="During expansion the pressure falls from A to B. Volume is in liters."}

- $W=300\,\mathrm{J}$
- $W=900\,\mathrm{J}$
- $W=600\,\mathrm{J}$
- $W=-600\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The pressure changes linearly along the segment. The average pressure is half the sum of the endpoint pressures. Multiplied by the volume increase, it gives the area of the trapezoid.

$$
W=\frac{300+100}{2}(4-1)\,\mathrm{J}=600\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
