A monatomic ideal gas absorbs $1000\,\mathrm{J}$ during quasistatic isobaric heating, with only volume work. How much of the energy that enters is released as work?

- $600\,\mathrm{J}$
- $400\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Both work and heat are proportional to the same $n\Delta T$. Their ratio is $\dfrac{R}{C_p}$. For a monatomic gas, $C_p$ is five halves of $R$.

$$
W=\frac{nR\Delta T}{nC_p\Delta T}Q=\frac25Q=400\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
