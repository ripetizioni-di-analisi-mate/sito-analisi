A diatomic ideal gas has active rotations and negligible vibrations. During a process the quantity $nR\Delta T$ equals $-80\,\mathrm{J}$. What is $\Delta U$?

- $-200\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-120\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-280\,\mathrm{J}$
- $200\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Under the stated assumptions, the molar heat capacity at constant volume is five halves of $R$. Thus internal energy falls regardless of how cooling took place.

$$
\Delta U=\frac52 nR\Delta T=\frac52(-80\,\mathrm{J})=-200\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
