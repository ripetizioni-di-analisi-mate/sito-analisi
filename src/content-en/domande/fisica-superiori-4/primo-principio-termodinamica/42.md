For two identical ideal gas samples, the graph plots heat absorbed $Q$ against $\Delta T$. Which line describes the quasistatic isobaric process?

::grafico{f="10*x;15*x" xmin=0 xmax=20 ymin=0 ymax=330 nomi="ΔT,Q (J)" legenda="\mathrm{A};\mathrm{B}" titolo="The increase in temperature is in kelvins and the heat absorbed is in joules."}

- Line A, with the smaller slope.
- Line B, with the greater slope.
- Both lines.
- The temperature cannot change in an isobaric process.

## Explanation

The slopes are $nC_V$ and $nC_p$. In the isobaric process, for the same temperature increase, heat must also supply the work of expansion. Therefore $C_p$ is greater and line B represents the isobaric process.
