To find the work of a reversible isothermal process, which expression has a dimensionally correct logarithm argument?

- $nRT\ln\left(\dfrac{V_f}{V_i}\right)$.
- $nRT\ln(V_f-V_i)$.
- $nRT\ln(p_fV_f)$.
- $nRT\ln(T_fV_i)$.

## Explanation

A logarithm requires a pure number. In a ratio of two volumes the units cancel. The other expressions retain units inside the logarithm.
