The temperature of $0.96\,\mathrm{mol}$ of a monatomic ideal gas increases by $45\,\mathrm{K}$. Using $R=8.314\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$, what is $\Delta U$ rounded to the nearest joule?

- $\Delta U\simeq 359\,\mathrm{J}$
- $\Delta U\simeq 898\,\mathrm{J}$
- $\Delta U\simeq -539\,\mathrm{J}$
- $\Delta U\simeq 539\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The gas is monatomic, so internal energy uses $C_V=\dfrac32R$. The temperature increases, so the internal energy rises along any path.

$$
\Delta U=nC_V\Delta T=0.96\cdot\frac32\cdot8.314\cdot45\,\mathrm{J}\simeq539\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
