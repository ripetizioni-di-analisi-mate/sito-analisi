A monatomic ideal gas is cooled quasistatically at $100\,\mathrm{kPa}$ until its volume falls from $6$ to $4\,\mathrm{L}$. What is the signed heat?

- $-300\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-200\,\mathrm{J}$
- $500\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-500\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The equation of state along the isobaric path relates the decrease in volume to the decrease in temperature. The heat can therefore be calculated without knowing $n$ or the endpoint temperatures.

$$
\begin{aligned}
nR\Delta T&=p\Delta V=-200\,\mathrm{J}, \\[6pt]
Q&=\frac52nR\Delta T=-500\,\mathrm{J}.
\end{aligned}
$$
