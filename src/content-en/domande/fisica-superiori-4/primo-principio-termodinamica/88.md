For a clockwise rectangular cycle with $p_l=100\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and $V_h-V_l=2\,\mathrm{L}$, the net heat is $300\,\mathrm{J}$. What is the maximum pressure?

- $150\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $200\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $250\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $400\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

By the first law over a cycle, net heat equals net work, which is the enclosed area. Dividing by the width gives the height of the rectangle, to which the minimum pressure is added.

$$
p_h=p_l+\frac{Q_{\mathrm{tot}}}{V_h-V_l}=\left(100+\frac{300}{2}\right)\,\mathrm{kPa}=250\,\mathrm{kPa}.
$$
