During a first stage, a gas absorbs $410\,\mathrm{J}$ and does $100\,\mathrm{J}$ of work. During a second stage it releases $90\,\mathrm{J}$ and the piston does $80\,\mathrm{J}$ of work on the gas. What is the total change in internal energy?

- $\Delta U=140\,\mathrm{J}$
- $\Delta U=300\,\mathrm{J}$
- $\Delta U=340\,\mathrm{J}$
- $\Delta U=480\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The net heat is $410-90=320$ joules. The net work done by the gas is $100-80=20$ joules because the piston returns energy in the second stage. Their difference gives the overall balance.

$$
\Delta U=(410-90)\,\mathrm{J}-(100-80)\,\mathrm{J}=300\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
