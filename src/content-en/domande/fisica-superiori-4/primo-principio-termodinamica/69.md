An ideal gas at $360\,\mathrm{kPa}$ expands into a vacuum from $2$ to $6\,\mathrm{L}$ in an insulated vessel. The external work is zero. What is its final equilibrium pressure?

- $60\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $120\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $360\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $1080\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

The first law gives zero change in internal energy. For equilibrium states of an ideal gas this implies the same temperature, so tripling the volume reduces the pressure to one third.

$$
\begin{aligned}
Q&=W=0, \\[6pt]
T_f&=T_i, \\[6pt]
p_f&=p_i\frac{V_i}{V_f}=120\,\mathrm{kPa}.
\end{aligned}
$$
