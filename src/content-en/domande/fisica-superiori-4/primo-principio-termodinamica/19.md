A piston of area $0.010\,\mathrm{m^2}$ moves outward by $5\,\mathrm{cm}$ against $120\,\mathrm{kPa}$. How much work does the gas do?

- $6\,\mathrm{J}$
- $60\,\mathrm{J}$
- $120\,\mathrm{J}$
- $6000\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The external pressure times the area is the force to overcome. The work is that force times the piston stroke, which is five hundredths of a meter.

$$
W=p_{\mathrm{est}}A\Delta x=120000\cdot0.010\cdot0.05\,\mathrm{J}=60\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
