A gas quasistatically follows a simple counterclockwise cycle on the $p$-$V$ plane. Which conclusion follows for each complete cycle if only volume work occurs?

- The gas receives net work and releases net heat.
- The gas delivers net work and absorbs net heat.
- Internal energy decreases with each cycle.
- The net work is zero.

## Explanation

The signed area is negative for a counterclockwise cycle. Thus the work done by the gas is negative, and because internal energy returns to its initial value, net heat has the same sign.
