A graph has $p$ in pascals on the vertical axis and $V$ in liters on the horizontal axis. What is an area of $1\,\mathrm{Pa}\cdot\mathrm{L}$ worth in joules?

- $10^{-3}\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $1\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $10^3\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $10^{-6}\,\mathrm{J}$.

## Explanation

A liter is one thousandth of a cubic meter. Therefore on these axes each unit of area is one thousandth of a joule.
