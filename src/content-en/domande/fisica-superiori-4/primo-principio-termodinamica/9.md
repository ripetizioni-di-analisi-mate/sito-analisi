During compression, a gas releases $250\,\mathrm{J}$ as heat and receives $730\,\mathrm{J}$ as work from the piston. How much does its internal energy change?

- $\Delta U=-480\,\mathrm{J}$
- $\Delta U=980\,\mathrm{J}$
- $\Delta U=-980\,\mathrm{J}$
- $\Delta U=480\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The gas releases heat, so $Q=-250\,\mathrm{J}$. Work is done on the gas and the value of $W$ is $-730\,\mathrm{J}$. The energy received as work exceeds the heat loss.

$$
\Delta U=Q-W=[-250-(-730)]\,\mathrm{J}=480\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
