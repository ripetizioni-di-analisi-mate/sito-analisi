A monatomic ideal gas, first at constant volume and then quasistatically at constant pressure, warms by $20\,\mathrm{K}$ in each stage. The value of $nR$ is $10\,\mathrm{J/K}$ and the only work is volume work. How much total heat does it receive?

- $600\,\mathrm{J}$
- $500\,\mathrm{J}$
- $800\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The two stages produce the same increase in internal energy. In the second stage the gas also does work, so use $C_p$ for that stage and $C_V$ for the first.

$$
Q_{\mathrm{tot}}=\frac32\cdot10\cdot20\,\mathrm{J}+\frac52\cdot10\cdot20\,\mathrm{J}=800\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
