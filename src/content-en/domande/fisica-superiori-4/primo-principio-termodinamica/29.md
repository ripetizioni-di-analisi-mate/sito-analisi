A constant absolute pressure of $210\,\mathrm{kPa}$ is maintained during an expansion from $1.5$ to $3.5\,\mathrm{L}$ lasting $4\,\mathrm{s}$. What is the average power delivered by the gas?

- $52.5\,\mathrm{W}$
- $210\,\mathrm{W}$
- $105\,\mathrm{W}$
- $420\,\mathrm{W}$

## Explanation

First find the energy released as work, which is $420\,\mathrm{J}$. Divide by the elapsed time to get the average power.

$$
\overline P=\frac{p(V_f-V_i)}{\Delta t}=\frac{210(3.5-1.5)}{4}\,\mathrm{W}=105\,\mathrm{W}.
$$
