An ideal gas initially at $280\,\mathrm{K}$ is heated in a rigid vessel until the pressure increases by $25\%$. What is its final temperature?

- $305\,\mathrm{K}$
- $210\,\mathrm{K}$
- $280\,\mathrm{K}$
- $350\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

With volume and amount of gas fixed, pressure is proportional to absolute temperature. The factor multiplying pressure is $1.25$, so the same factor multiplies temperature in kelvins.

$$
T_f=T_i\frac{p_f}{p_i}=280\cdot1.25\,\mathrm{K}=350\,\mathrm{K}.
$$
