A quasistatic isochoric process goes from $A=(2\,\mathrm{L},80\,\mathrm{kPa})$ to $B=(2\,\mathrm{L},240\,\mathrm{kPa})$. Which conclusion follows from the diagram for an ideal gas with only volume work?

::grafico{linee="2,80 2,240" punti="2,80,pieno,A;2,240,pieno,B" vettori="2,125,2,200" xmin=0 xmax=4 ymin=0 ymax=305 nomi="V,p (kPa)" titolo="The process goes from A to B. Volume is in liters."}

- $W=0$ and $Q<0$.
- $W=0$ and $Q>0$.
- $W>0$ and $Q=0$.
- $W<0$ and $Q<0$.

## Explanation

The vertical segment has constant volume, so the work is zero. Pressure rises at fixed volume, so temperature also rises. Internal energy increases because heat enters.
