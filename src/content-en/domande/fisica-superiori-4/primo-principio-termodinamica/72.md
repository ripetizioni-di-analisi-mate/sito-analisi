During a reversible isothermal expansion with $nRT=500\,\mathrm{J}$, the gas absorbs $500\ln 4\,\mathrm{J}$ of heat. What is the ratio $\dfrac{p_i}{p_f}$?

- $0.25$
- $2$
- $4$
- $8$

## Explanation

For an ideal gas in an isothermal process $Q=W$, so the stated heat gives the logarithm of the volume ratio. At the same temperature pressure is inversely proportional to volume.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\ln\frac{V_f}{V_i}&=\frac{Q}{nRT}=\ln4, \\[6pt]
\frac{p_i}{p_f}&=\frac{V_f}{V_i}=4.
\end{aligned}
$$
