In a first adiabatic stage, the gas does $240\,\mathrm{J}$ of work. In the second stage it receives heat at constant volume, with no other work, and recovers half the internal energy lost. How much total heat does it receive?

- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $240\,\mathrm{J}$
- $360\,\mathrm{J}$
- $120\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The first stage has no heat transfer, and internal energy decreases by the work delivered. In the second stage there is no work, so the heat equals the internal energy recovered.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta U_1&=-240\,\mathrm{J}, \\[6pt]
Q_2&=\Delta U_2=\frac{240}{2}\,\mathrm{J}=120\,\mathrm{J}.
\end{aligned}
$$
