A gas expands quasistatically at a constant pressure of $156\,\mathrm{kPa}$ from $2$ to $5\,\mathrm{L}$. How much work does it do?

::grafico{linee="2,156 5,156" punti="2,156,pieno,A;5,156,pieno,B" vettori="2.8,156,4.2,156" xmin=0 xmax=6 ymin=0 ymax=205 nomi="V,p (kPa)" titolo="The path goes from A to B at constant pressure. Volume is in liters."}

- $W=780\,\mathrm{J}$
- $W=468\,\mathrm{J}$
- $W=312\,\mathrm{J}$
- $W=-468\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The work is the area under the horizontal segment. The rectangle's width is the volume difference. The product of kilopascals and liters directly gives joules.

$$
W=p(V_f-V_i)=156(5-2)\,\mathrm{J}=468\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
