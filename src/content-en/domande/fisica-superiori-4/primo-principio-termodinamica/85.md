Two simple cycles are traversed quasistatically in opposite directions and enclose the same area on the $p$-$V$ plane. How do their net amounts of work compare if there is only volume work?

- They are equal and positive.
- They have the same magnitude and opposite signs.
- They are both zero.
- The magnitude depends only on the amount in moles.

## Explanation

The enclosed area determines the magnitude of the cycle's volume work. Direction decides whether the expansion path lies above or below the compression path, and hence determines the sign.
