The internal energy of a gas increases by $504\,\mathrm{J}$ while the gas does $180\,\mathrm{J}$ of work. How much heat does it absorb?

- $Q=324\,\mathrm{J}$
- $Q=504\,\mathrm{J}$
- $Q=684\,\mathrm{J}$
- $Q=-684\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The heat input must supply both the increase in internal energy and the energy transferred to the surroundings as work.

$$
Q=\Delta U+W=(504+180)\,\mathrm{J}=684\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
