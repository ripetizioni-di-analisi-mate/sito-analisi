Which data are always sufficient to determine the change in internal energy between two states of a closed monatomic ideal gas?

- The $pV$ products for the two states.
- Only the pressures of the two states.
- Only the volumes of the two states.
- Only the masses of the pistons.

## Explanation

For a monatomic ideal gas the change in internal energy is three halves of the change in $nRT$. The equation of state gives $pV=nRT$, so the two $pV$ products determine the required difference.
