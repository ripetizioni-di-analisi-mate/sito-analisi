In one process a gas releases $210\,\mathrm{J}$ and receives $450\,\mathrm{J}$ of work. In a second process it returns exactly to the starting state and does $390\,\mathrm{J}$ of work. What is the signed heat exchanged in the second process?

- $630\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-150\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-240\,\mathrm{J}$
- $150\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Returning to the initial state requires the internal energy changes of the two stages to cancel. The work in the second stage is greater than the internal energy the gas loses, so heat input is also required.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta U_1&=(-210+450)\,\mathrm{J}=240\,\mathrm{J}, \\[6pt]
Q_2&=\Delta U_2+W_2=(-240+390)\,\mathrm{J}=150\,\mathrm{J}.
\end{aligned}
$$
