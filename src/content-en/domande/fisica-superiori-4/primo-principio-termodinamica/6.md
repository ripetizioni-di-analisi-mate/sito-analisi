A monatomic ideal gas containing $0.90\,\mathrm{mol}$ is heated quasistatically by $60\,\mathrm{K}$ at constant pressure. Which value is closest to the heat absorbed using $R=8.314\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$?

- $Q\simeq 673\,\mathrm{J}$
- $Q\simeq 449\,\mathrm{J}$
- $Q\simeq 1870\,\mathrm{J}$
- $Q\simeq 1120\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

At constant pressure, some heat increases internal energy and some enables expansion. Therefore $C_p=C_V+R=\dfrac52R$ is used.

$$
Q=n(C_V+R)\Delta T=0.90\cdot\frac52\cdot8.314\cdot60\,\mathrm{J}\simeq1120\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
