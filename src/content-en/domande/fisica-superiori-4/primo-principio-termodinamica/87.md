A monatomic ideal gas starts at $p_AV_A=400\,\mathrm{J}$. It undergoes an isochoric process to $p_BV_B=600\,\mathrm{J}$ and then a reversible isothermal process in which it does $160\,\mathrm{J}$ of work. How much total heat does it absorb, assuming only volume work?

- $460\,\mathrm{J}$
- $300\,\mathrm{J}$
- $160\,\mathrm{J}$
- $760\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The isochoric stage converts heat into internal energy without work. In the ideal gas's isothermal stage, temperature and internal energy stay fixed, so heat balances work. The two contributions add.

$$
\begin{aligned}
Q_{AB}&=\frac32(600-400)\,\mathrm{J}=300\,\mathrm{J}, \\[6pt]
Q_{BC}&=W_{BC}=160\,\mathrm{J}, \\[6pt]
Q_{\mathrm{tot}}&=460\,\mathrm{J}.
\end{aligned}
$$
