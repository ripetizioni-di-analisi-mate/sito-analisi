For an isochoric process of an ideal gas, the graph of $Q$ against $\Delta T$ is a line through $(30\,\mathrm{K},600\,\mathrm{J})$. What is $nC_V$, if volume work is the only possible form of work?

- $20\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $10\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $30\,\mathrm{J/K}$
- $60\,\mathrm{J/K}$

## Explanation

In an isochoric process the heat equals the internal energy change. The heat per kelvin for the whole sample is therefore the slope of the line from the origin to the given point.

$$
nC_V=\frac{600\,\mathrm{J}}{30\,\mathrm{K}}=20\,\mathrm{J/K}.
$$
