During adiabatic compression an ideal gas with total heat capacity at constant volume $nC_V=30\,\mathrm{J/K}$ receives $600\,\mathrm{J}$ of work. By how many kelvins does its temperature change?

- $\Delta T=-20\,\mathrm{K}$
- $\Delta T=0\,\mathrm{K}$
- $\Delta T=20\,\mathrm{K}$
- $\Delta T=40\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

The gas exchanges no heat, so all the work received increases internal energy. Dividing the increase by the heat capacity of the whole sample gives the temperature change.

$$
\Delta T=\frac{\Delta U}{nC_V}=\frac{600\,\mathrm{J}}{30\,\mathrm{J/K}}=20\,\mathrm{K}.
$$
