A paddle stirs a gas in a rigid insulated container. Why does the formula $Q=\Delta U$ not apply in this case?

- The gas must change volume.
- Insulation makes internal energy constant.
- The temperature must be constant.
- The paddle work crosses the boundary.

## Explanation

Fixed volume rules out pressure work, but mechanical work by the paddle remains. Because the wall is insulated, $Q=0$ and the increase in internal energy is the work received.
