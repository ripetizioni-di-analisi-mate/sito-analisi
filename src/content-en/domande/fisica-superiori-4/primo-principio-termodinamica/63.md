In which case may $pV^\gamma=\text{constant}$ be applied?

- Any compression of an ideal gas.
- Any process of a gas in an insulated vessel.
- Reversible isothermal process at constant temperature.
- Reversible adiabatic process of an ideal gas with constant molar heat capacities.

## Explanation

The law uses a succession of equilibrium states, the absence of heat transfer, and the same value of $\gamma$ along the process. The condition $Q=0$ alone gives $\Delta U=-W$.
