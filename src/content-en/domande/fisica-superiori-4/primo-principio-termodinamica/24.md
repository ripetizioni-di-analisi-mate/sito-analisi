A wall supplies $400\,\mathrm{J}$ as heat and a paddle does $160\,\mathrm{J}$ of work on the gas. The gas also does $90\,\mathrm{J}$ of expansion work. What is $\Delta U$?

- $310\,\mathrm{J}$
- $150\,\mathrm{J}$
- $470\,\mathrm{J}$
- $650\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The positive volume work is added to the paddle work, which is negative in the gas convention. Net work is negative because the paddle supplies more energy than leaves in expansion.

$$
\Delta U=400\,\mathrm{J}-(90-160)\,\mathrm{J}=470\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
