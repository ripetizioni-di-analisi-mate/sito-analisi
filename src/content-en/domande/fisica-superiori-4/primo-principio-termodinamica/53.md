A monatomic ideal gas in a rigid $4\,\mathrm{L}$ container absorbs $360\,\mathrm{J}$. Assuming only volume work, by how many kilopascals does the pressure increase?

- $90\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $60\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $135\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $30\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

With volume fixed, the increase in $pV$ is $V\Delta p$. Since the heat goes entirely into increasing internal energy, divide the heat by three halves of the volume in liters.

$$
\begin{aligned}
Q&=\frac32 V\Delta p, \\[6pt]
\Delta p&=\frac{360}{\dfrac32\cdot4}\,\mathrm{kPa}=60\,\mathrm{kPa}.
\end{aligned}
$$
