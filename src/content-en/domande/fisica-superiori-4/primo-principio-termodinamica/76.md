In the pressure–volume plot of a closed cycle, the sensor added $20$ kPa to every pressure. The volume is correct, and the external pressure matches the recorded pressure apart from this constant error. How should the estimate of the gas's net work over the full cycle be corrected?

- No correction to the net work is needed.
- Subtract $20$ kPa times the maximum volume.
- Halve the calculated net work.
- The calculated work necessarily has the opposite sign to the true work.

## Explanation

On each part the work error is the added pressure times the signed change in volume. In a cycle the final and initial volumes are equal and all changes in volume sum to zero. A vertical shift of the plot therefore preserves the enclosed area.
