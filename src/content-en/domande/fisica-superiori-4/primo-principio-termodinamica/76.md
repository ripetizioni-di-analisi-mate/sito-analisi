A gas expands from $1$ to $4\,\mathrm{L}$ at $180\,\mathrm{kPa}$. It is then compressed back to its initial volume at $120\,\mathrm{kPa}$, closing a cycle with two isochoric segments. What is the net work?

- $180\,\mathrm{J}$
- $360\,\mathrm{J}$
- $540\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-180\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

During compression the volume traverses the same interval with the opposite sign. Since the pressure is higher during expansion, the net work is positive.

$$
W_{\mathrm{tot}}=180(4-1)\,\mathrm{J}+120(1-4)\,\mathrm{J}=180\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
