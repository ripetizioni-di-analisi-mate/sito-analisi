A rigid container holds $1.44\,\mathrm{mol}$ of a monatomic ideal gas. Its temperature increases by $35\,\mathrm{K}$, and volume work is the only possible type of work. Find the heat absorbed to the nearest joule using $R=8.314\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$.

- $Q\simeq 419\,\mathrm{J}$
- $Q\simeq 629\,\mathrm{J}$
- $Q\simeq 1050\,\mathrm{J}$
- $Q\simeq 0\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The container's volume is fixed, so $W=0$. The energy received as heat equals the increase in internal energy $nC_V\Delta T$.

$$
Q=\Delta U=1.44\cdot\frac32\cdot8.314\cdot35\,\mathrm{J}\simeq629\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
