The net heat in a process equals the net work, and the gas returns to the same volume. For an ideal gas in a closed system, which quantities necessarily return to their initial values?

- Only internal energy.
- Only pressure.
- Only temperature and internal energy.
- Temperature, pressure, and internal energy.

## Explanation

The balance gives $\Delta U=0$. For an ideal gas this requires the same temperature. Since the volume and the amount in moles also match, the equation of state requires the same pressure.
