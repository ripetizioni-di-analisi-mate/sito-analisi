Two states of an ideal gas have the same temperature and different volumes. The process connecting them is unknown. Which conclusion always holds for a closed system under the first-law assumptions?

- $Q=W=0$.
- $W=nRT\ln\dfrac{V_f}{V_i}$ certainly.
- The process must be reversible.
- $Q=W$.

## Explanation

For an ideal gas the internal energy depends only on temperature. Its change is therefore zero between the specified states. Finding the specific value of $W$ requires more information about the path.
