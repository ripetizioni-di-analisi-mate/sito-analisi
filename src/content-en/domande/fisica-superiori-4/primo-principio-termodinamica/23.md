The two endpoints of a process are known on the $p$-$V$ plane. In which case is it justified to use the arithmetic mean of the endpoint pressures to find the work?

- When the quasistatic path is straight.
- For every monotonic expansion.
- For every cycle.
- When the gas returns to the initial temperature.

## Explanation

On a straight segment the pressure changes linearly with volume. The area is a trapezoid and the two heights determine it. A general curve requires the relationship along the entire path.
