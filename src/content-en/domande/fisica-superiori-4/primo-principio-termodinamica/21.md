A gas moves quasistatically from $(2\,\mathrm{L},100\,\mathrm{kPa})$ to $(4\,\mathrm{L},100\,\mathrm{kPa})$ and then to $(4\,\mathrm{L},250\,\mathrm{kPa})$. How much work does it do?

- $500\,\mathrm{J}$
- $200\,\mathrm{J}$
- $700\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

In the second stage the pressure increases at fixed volume. This vertical segment adds zero work. Only the first segment includes an increase in volume.

$$
W=100(4-2)\,\mathrm{J}+0=200\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
