A monatomic ideal gas goes from $p_i=150\,\mathrm{kPa}$, $V_i=2\,\mathrm{L}$ to $p_f=200\,\mathrm{kPa}$, $V_f=3\,\mathrm{L}$. What is $\Delta U$?

- $\Delta U=450\,\mathrm{J}$
- $\Delta U=300\,\mathrm{J}$
- $\Delta U=150\,\mathrm{J}$
- $\Delta U=900\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The equation of state allows $nR\Delta T$ to be replaced by the difference of the $pV$ products. For the monatomic gas, multiply that difference by $\dfrac32$.

$$
\Delta U=\frac32(p_fV_f-p_iV_i)=\frac32(200\cdot3-150\cdot2)\,\mathrm{J}=450\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
