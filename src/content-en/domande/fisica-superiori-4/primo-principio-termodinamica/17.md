Two paths connect the same initial state to the same final state. Along the first, the gas does $80\,\mathrm{J}$ more work. How does the heat absorbed compare?

- The first is greater by $80\,\mathrm{J}$.
- The second is greater by $80\,\mathrm{J}$.
- They are equal.
- The absolute value of $U_i$ is required.

## Explanation

Both paths have the same change in internal energy. Subtracting one energy balance from the other shows that the heat difference equals the work difference.

$$
Q_1-Q_2=(\Delta U+W_1)-(\Delta U+W_2)=W_1-W_2.
$$
