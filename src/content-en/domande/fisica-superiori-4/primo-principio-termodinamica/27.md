A wall is impermeable to matter and allows energy transfer by conduction. Which description applies to the system bounded by the wall?

- The system is open because it exchanges energy.
- The system is isolated because it retains matter.
- The system is closed and can absorb heat.
- Heat can cross it only if matter does.

## Explanation

Classification as a closed system concerns transfer of matter. Conduction transfers energy through the wall when there is a temperature difference.
