Along the same reversible isothermal curve, an ideal gas expands first from $V_0$ to $2V_0$ and then from $2V_0$ to $4V_0$. The work on the first segment is $500\,\mathrm{J}$. What is the work on the second?

- $1000\,\mathrm{J}$
- $500\,\mathrm{J}$
- $250\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1500\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Isothermal work depends on the volume ratio, which is two in both segments. In the second segment the pressure is lower, while the volume interval is wider. The two effects give the same area.

$$
\frac{W_2}{W_1}=\frac{\ln\left(\dfrac{4V_0}{2V_0}\right)}{\ln\left(\dfrac{2V_0}{V_0}\right)}=1.
$$
