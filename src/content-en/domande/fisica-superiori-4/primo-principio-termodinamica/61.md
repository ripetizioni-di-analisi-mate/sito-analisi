During an adiabatic expansion of an ideal gas, the internal energy decreases by $300\,\mathrm{J}$. How much work does the gas do?

- $-300\,\mathrm{J}$
- $600\,\mathrm{J}$
- $300\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The heat transfer is zero. The gas thus transfers to the surroundings as work an amount of energy equal to its decrease in internal energy.

$$
\begin{aligned}
Q&=0, \\[6pt]
W&=-\Delta U=300\,\mathrm{J}.
\end{aligned}
$$
