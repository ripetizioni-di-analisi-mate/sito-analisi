A monatomic ideal gas is enclosed in a rigid $5\,\mathrm{L}$ vessel. During heating the pressure rises from $100$ to $160\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Only volume work is possible. How much heat does it absorb?

- $300\,\mathrm{J}$
- $450\,\mathrm{J}$
- $750\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1200\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

At fixed volume the work is zero. Only the increase in $pV$, proportional to the temperature increase, is needed. The factor three halves accounts for the molar heat capacity of a monatomic gas.

$$
Q=\Delta U=\frac32 V(p_f-p_i)=\frac32\cdot5(160-100)\,\mathrm{J}=450\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
