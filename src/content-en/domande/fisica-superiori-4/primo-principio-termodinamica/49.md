A monatomic ideal gas has an initial temperature of $400\,\mathrm{K}$. At the end of a process the pressure has doubled and the volume is one quarter of its initial value. What is $\Delta T$?

- $-200\,\mathrm{K}$
- $200\,\mathrm{K}$
- $-100\,\mathrm{K}$
- $-300\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

With the amount of gas fixed, temperature follows the product $pV$. Doubling the pressure does not offset reducing volume to one quarter, so temperature is halved.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{T_f}{T_i}&=2\cdot\frac14=\frac12, \\[6pt]
\Delta T&=(200-400)\,\mathrm{K}=-200\,\mathrm{K}.
\end{aligned}
$$
