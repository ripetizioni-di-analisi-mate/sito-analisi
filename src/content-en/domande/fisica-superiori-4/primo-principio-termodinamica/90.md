A monatomic ideal gas with $nC_V=12\,\mathrm{J/K}$ starts at $330\,\mathrm{K}$. It is compressed adiabatically with $240\,\mathrm{J}$ of work done on it, then releases $360\,\mathrm{J}$ of heat at constant volume without other work. What is the total temperature change?

- $10\,\mathrm{K}$
- $-10\,\mathrm{K}$
- $-30\,\mathrm{K}$
- $20\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

Adiabatic compression increases internal energy by the work received. Isochoric cooling decreases it by the heat released. The second contribution has greater magnitude, so the overall temperature change is negative.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta U&=(240-360)\,\mathrm{J}=-120\,\mathrm{J}, \\[6pt]
\Delta T&=\frac{-120}{12}\,\mathrm{K}=-10\,\mathrm{K}.
\end{aligned}
$$
