A rectangular cycle has net work of $-480\,\mathrm{J}$ and extreme pressures of $80$ and $240\,\mathrm{kPa}$. What is the difference between its maximum and minimum volumes?

- $-3\,\mathrm{L}$
- $2\,\mathrm{L}$
- $3\,\mathrm{L}$
- $6\,\mathrm{L}$

## Explanation

The negative sign identifies the counterclockwise direction. The width of the rectangle is positive and is found by dividing the magnitude of the work by the pressure difference.

$$
V_{\max}-V_{\min}=\frac{|W_{\mathrm{tot}}|}{p_h-p_l}=\frac{480}{240-80}\,\mathrm{L}=3\,\mathrm{L}.
$$
