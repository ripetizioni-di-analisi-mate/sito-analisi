An ideal gas is compressed adiabatically. Its final internal energy is $1300\,\mathrm{J}$ relative to the same reference used for the initial $900\,\mathrm{J}$. How much work did the surroundings do on the gas?

- $-400\,\mathrm{J}$
- $900\,\mathrm{J}$
- $2200\,\mathrm{J}$
- $400\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The change in internal energy is independent of the common reference. Since heat transfer is zero, it equals the work received. The work done by the gas would have the opposite sign.

$$
W_{\mathrm{ricevuto}}=\Delta U=(1300-900)\,\mathrm{J}=400\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
