An ideal gas with constant molar heat capacities has $C_p=28\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$ and $C_V=20\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$. What is the exponent in the reversible adiabatic law?

- $0.4$
- $0.714$
- $1.4$
- $2.4$

## Explanation

The exponent is the ratio of the molar heat capacities. Their difference, which is $R$ for an ideal gas, retains units and cannot be the exponent.

$$
\gamma=\frac{C_p}{C_V}=\frac{28}{20}=1.4.
$$
