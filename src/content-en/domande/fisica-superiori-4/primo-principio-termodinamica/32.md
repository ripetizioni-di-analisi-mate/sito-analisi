Two states of a monatomic ideal gas have $p_iV_i=800\,\mathrm{J}$ and $p_fV_f=440\,\mathrm{J}$. The gas does $70\,\mathrm{J}$ of work. What is the signed heat?

- $-610\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-470\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-290\,\mathrm{J}$
- $470\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The final product $pV$ is smaller, so the temperature falls and the internal energy decreases. To find the heat, add the work leaving the gas to the change in internal energy.

$$
Q=\frac32(p_fV_f-p_iV_i)+W=\left[\frac32(440-800)+70\right]\mathrm{J}=-470\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
