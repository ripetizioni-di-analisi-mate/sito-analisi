A rigid thermally insulated vessel contains a gas and a heating element. How is the energy supplied through the wires classified when the system includes both?

- Heat through the wall
- Expansion work
- Transfer of matter
- Electrical work

## Explanation

The wires cross the system boundary and transfer electrical energy. The element dissipates it inside the boundary. The wall is insulated and its volume is fixed.
