An ideal gas does $720\,\mathrm{J}$ of work in a reversible isothermal expansion. The pressure goes from $p_i=2p_f$ to $p_f$. Which value is closest to $nRT$? Use $\ln2\simeq0.69$.

- $500\,\mathrm{J}$
- $720\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1040\,\mathrm{J}$
- $2080\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Along the isothermal curve the product $pV$ is constant. Halving the pressure doubles the volume, so the logarithm of the volume ratio is $\ln2$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{V_f}{V_i}&=\frac{p_i}{p_f}=2, \\[6pt]
nRT&=\frac{720\,\mathrm{J}}{\ln2}\simeq1040\,\mathrm{J}.
\end{aligned}
$$
