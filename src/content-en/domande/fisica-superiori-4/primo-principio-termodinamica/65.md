A reversible adiabatic compression of an ideal gas with $\gamma=\dfrac32$ reduces the volume to one quarter. If the initial pressure is $70\,\mathrm{kPa}$, what is the final pressure?

- $560\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $280\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $140\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $1120\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

The pressure rise is steeper than in an isothermal compression because work increases the temperature. The factor is $4^{\frac32}=8$.

$$
p_f=p_i\left(\frac{V_i}{V_f}\right)^{\frac32}=70\cdot8\,\mathrm{kPa}=560\,\mathrm{kPa}.
$$
