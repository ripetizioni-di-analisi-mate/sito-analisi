An ideal gas with $nRT=1000\,\mathrm{J}$ reversibly doubles its volume at constant temperature. With $\ln 2\simeq0.693$, which value is closest to $Q$?

- $693\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-693\,\mathrm{J}$
- $1000\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The ideal gas stays at constant temperature, so its internal energy does not change. Work is the area under the reversible isothermal curve and the incoming heat balances it.

$$
Q=W=nRT\ln2\simeq693\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
