A gas receives $120\,\mathrm{J}$ as heat and its internal energy decreases by $80\,\mathrm{J}$. How much work does it do?

- $40\,\mathrm{J}$
- $200\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-200\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-40\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The work exceeds the heat input because the gas also gives up some of its initial internal energy.

$$
W=Q-\Delta U=[120-(-80)]\,\mathrm{J}=200\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
