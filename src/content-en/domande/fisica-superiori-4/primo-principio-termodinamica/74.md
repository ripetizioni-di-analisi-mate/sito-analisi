Two identical samples of ideal gas start in the same state and double their volume in adiabatic processes. The first expands into a vacuum without work and the second reversibly against a piston. How do their final temperatures compare?

- The first cools more.
- The first stays at its initial temperature and the second cools.
- Both stay at their initial temperature.
- Both warm up.

## Explanation

In the first case $Q$ and $W$ are both zero. In the second the work is positive and the heat transfer is still zero. Thus only in the second case does the internal energy of the ideal gas change.
