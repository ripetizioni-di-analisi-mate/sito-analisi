An ideal gas has $C_V=20\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$. For this exercise use $R=8\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$. How much heat does $2\,\mathrm{mol}$ absorb to warm quasistatically by $10\,\mathrm{K}$ at constant pressure?

- $400\,\mathrm{J}$
- $240\,\mathrm{J}$
- $560\,\mathrm{J}$
- $160\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The quantity $nC_V\Delta T$ describes the internal energy change. In an isobaric process, add expansion work, which equals $R$ per mole per kelvin.

$$
Q=n(C_V+R)\Delta T=2(20+8)10\,\mathrm{J}=560\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
