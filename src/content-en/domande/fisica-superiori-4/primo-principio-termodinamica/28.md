A quasistatic isobaric process produces work of $-540\,\mathrm{J}$ and takes the volume from $7$ to $4\,\mathrm{L}$. What is the gas pressure?

- $180\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $-180\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $135\,\mathrm{kPa}$
- $270\,\mathrm{kPa}$

## Explanation

Work done by the gas and the volume change are both negative during compression. Their ratio is the pressure, a positive quantity.

$$
p=\frac{W}{V_f-V_i}=\frac{-540}{4-7}\,\mathrm{kPa}=180\,\mathrm{kPa}.
$$
