A gas in a closed system absorbs $1080\,\mathrm{J}$ and does $250\,\mathrm{J}$ of work on the surroundings. What is $\Delta U$ if macroscopic changes in kinetic and potential energy are negligible?

- $\Delta U=830\,\mathrm{J}$
- $\Delta U=1330\,\mathrm{J}$
- $\Delta U=1080\,\mathrm{J}$
- $\Delta U=-830\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Heat brings energy into the gas, while work transfers some of it to the surroundings. The two contributions therefore have opposite effects on the internal energy.

$$
\Delta U=Q-W=(1080-250)\,\mathrm{J}=830\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
