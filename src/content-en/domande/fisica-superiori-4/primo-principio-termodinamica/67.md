An ideal gas with $\gamma=\dfrac53$ has a temperature of $240\,\mathrm{K}$. In a reversible adiabatic compression it reaches $960\,\mathrm{K}$. What is the ratio $\dfrac{V_i}{V_f}$?

- $4$
- $8$
- $16$
- $2$

## Explanation

With $\gamma-1=\dfrac23$, temperature grows as the compression ratio to the two-thirds power. To get the ratio, raise the temperature factor to the reciprocal power.

$$
\frac{V_i}{V_f}=\left(\frac{T_f}{T_i}\right)^{\frac32}=4^{\frac32}=8.
$$
