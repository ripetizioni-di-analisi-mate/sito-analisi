An ideal gas with $\gamma=\dfrac32$ has $T_i=450\,\mathrm{K}$ and quadruples its volume during reversible adiabatic expansion. What is the final temperature?

- $112.5\,\mathrm{K}$
- $225\,\mathrm{K}$
- $450\,\mathrm{K}$
- $900\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

With constant molar heat capacities use constant $TV^{\gamma-1}$. The exponent is one half, so the factor multiplying the temperature is the square root of one quarter.

$$
T_f=T_i\left(\frac{V_i}{V_f}\right)^{\frac12}=450\sqrt{\frac14}\,\mathrm{K}=225\,\mathrm{K}.
$$
