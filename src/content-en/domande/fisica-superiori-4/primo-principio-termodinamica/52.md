An ideal gas sample with only volume work separately undergoes two heating processes with the same $\Delta T$. Constant volume requires $380\,\mathrm{J}$ and quasistatic constant pressure requires $540\,\mathrm{J}$. What is the work done during the second process?

- $380\,\mathrm{J}$
- $540\,\mathrm{J}$
- $920\,\mathrm{J}$
- $160\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

For the ideal gas, equal increases in temperature give the same change in internal energy. The heat in the isochoric process measures that change, so the extra heat in the isobaric process is the work.

$$
W_p=Q_p-\Delta U=Q_p-Q_V=(540-380)\,\mathrm{J}=160\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
