An ideal gas follows two quasistatic expansions with the same endpoints. It expands by $3\,\mathrm{L}$ on the horizontal segment of each path; the isobaric segments are at $100$ and $180\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and the vertical connections do zero work. What is the difference between the amounts of heat on the two paths?

- $300\,\mathrm{J}$
- $240\,\mathrm{J}$
- $540\,\mathrm{J}$
- $840\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The change in internal energy is the same. Therefore the heat difference is the difference between the two areas, a rectangle three liters wide and eighty kilopascals high.

$$
Q_{\mathrm{alto}}-Q_{\mathrm{basso}}=(180-100)\cdot3\,\mathrm{J}=240\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
