A monatomic ideal gas is taken from $300$ to $330\,\mathrm{K}$ by two different paths. Which statement about $nC_V\Delta T$ is correct?

- It is the heat along both paths.
- It applies only when the pressure is constant.
- It applies only when the final volume is larger.
- It gives the change in internal energy along both paths.

## Explanation

The subscript $V$ indicates how the molar heat capacity in the law is measured. The internal energy of an ideal gas depends on temperature, so the law for its change also applies when volume changes.
