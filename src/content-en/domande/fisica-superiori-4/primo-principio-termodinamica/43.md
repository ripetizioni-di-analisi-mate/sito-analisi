Quasistatically heating $2\,\mathrm{mol}$ of an ideal gas by $20\,\mathrm{K}$ at constant pressure requires $1120\,\mathrm{J}$. Using $R=8\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$ in this exercise, what is $C_V$?

- $28\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$
- $8\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$
- $20\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$
- $36\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$

## Explanation

The experimental data first give the molar heat capacity at constant pressure. To find the constant-volume value, subtract the work per mole per kelvin, which is $R$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
C_p&=\frac{1120}{2\cdot20}\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}=28\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}, \\[6pt]
C_V&=C_p-R=20\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}.
\end{aligned}
$$
