An ideal gas at constant temperature is reversibly compressed to one third of its initial volume. If $nRT=600\,\mathrm{J}$ and $\ln3\simeq1.10$, which value is closest to the heat of the gas?

- $660\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-220\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-660\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-1800\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Compression reduces the ratio $\dfrac{V_f}{V_i}$ to one third. Its logarithm is therefore the negative of the logarithm of three. The energy that enters as work leaves as heat because temperature is constant.

$$
Q=W=nRT\ln\frac13\simeq600(-1.10)\,\mathrm{J}=-660\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
