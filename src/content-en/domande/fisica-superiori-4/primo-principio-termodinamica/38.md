Two identical samples of ideal gas receive the same positive heat, one at constant volume and the other quasistatically at constant pressure. If only volume work is possible, which sample warms more?

- The constant-pressure sample.
- The temperature increases are equal.
- The final temperature of the second sample is required.
- The constant-volume sample.

## Explanation

In the rigid container all the heat increases internal energy. In the isobaric process a fraction goes to expansion work. With the same heat input, the smaller amount remaining in the gas produces a smaller temperature increase.
