A gas expands against a constant external pressure of $90\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and does $270\,\mathrm{J}$ of volume work. By how much does the volume increase?

- $1.5\,\mathrm{L}$
- $6\,\mathrm{L}$
- $3\,\mathrm{L}$
- $30\,\mathrm{L}$

## Explanation

At constant external pressure, work is proportional to volume displacement. Dividing joules by kilopascals gives liters.

$$
\Delta V=\frac{W}{p_{\mathrm{est}}}=\frac{270}{90}\,\mathrm{L}=3\,\mathrm{L}.
$$
