Two processes take an ideal gas between the same states at the same temperature. On the first the gas does $440\,\mathrm{J}$ of work and on the second it receives $110\,\mathrm{J}$ of work. What is $Q_1-Q_2$?

- $330\,\mathrm{J}$
- $440\,\mathrm{J}$
- $550\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-550\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The change in internal energy is zero on each process because the initial and final states have equal temperature. In the second, the work done by the gas is negative, so the heat exchanged is also negative.

$$
Q_1-Q_2=W_1-W_2=[440-(-110)]\,\mathrm{J}=550\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
