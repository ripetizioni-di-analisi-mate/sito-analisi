Two samples with the same number of moles of monatomic and diatomic ideal gases are warmed by one kelvin. The diatomic gas has active rotations and negligible vibrations. What is the ratio of the increases in internal energy, diatomic divided by monatomic?

- $\dfrac35$.
- $1$.
- $\dfrac53$.
- $2$.

## Explanation

The number of moles and the temperature change are equal. The ratio is that of molar heat capacities at constant volume, which also accounts for rotations in the diatomic sample.

$$
\frac{\Delta U_{\mathrm{bi}}}{\Delta U_{\mathrm{mono}}}=\frac{\dfrac52R}{\dfrac32R}=\frac53.
$$
