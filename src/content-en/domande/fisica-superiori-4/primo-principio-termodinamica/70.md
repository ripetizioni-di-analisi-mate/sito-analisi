Along a reversible adiabatic process of an ideal gas with $\gamma>1$, the volume increases. What are the signs of work and the change in internal energy?

- $W>0$ and $\Delta U>0$.
- $W<0$ and $\Delta U>0$.
- $W=0$ and $\Delta U=0$.
- $W>0$ and $\Delta U<0$.

## Explanation

The gas expands against positive pressure and does work. In the absence of heat transfer that energy comes from internal energy.
