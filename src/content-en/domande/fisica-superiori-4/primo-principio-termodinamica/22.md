Over a full thermodynamic cycle a gas receives $80\,\mathrm J$ of net heat. The work done by the gas in two of its three processes is $+55\,\mathrm J$ and $-10\,\mathrm J$. How much work does the gas do in the third?

- $+15\,\mathrm J$
- $-35\,\mathrm J$
- $+125\,\mathrm J$
- $+35\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

After a cycle the internal energy returns to its initial value. Total work done by the gas must therefore equal its net heat input. From $55-10+W_3=80$ it follows that $W_3=35\,\mathrm J$.
