A quasistatic compression follows a straight segment from $(5\,\mathrm{L},80\,\mathrm{kPa})$ to $(2\,\mathrm{L},160\,\mathrm{kPa})$. What is the work done by the gas?

- $360\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-240\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-480\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-360\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

For a straight segment the average pressure is $120\,\mathrm{kPa}$. The final volume is smaller than the initial volume, so the signed width is $-3\,\mathrm{L}$.

$$
W=\frac{80+160}{2}(2-5)\,\mathrm{J}=-360\,\mathrm{J}.
$$
