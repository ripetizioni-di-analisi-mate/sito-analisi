In a group of $35$ people, $13$ know neither Excel nor LibreOffice. Seventeen know Excel and $7$ know both. How many know LibreOffice, including those who also know Excel?

- $15$
- $5$
- $12$
- $22$

## Explanation

There are $35 - 13 = 22$ people who know at least one program. Of these, $22 - 17 = 5$ know only LibreOffice. The LibreOffice total also includes the $7$ people in the **intersection**, so it is $5 + 7 = 12$.
