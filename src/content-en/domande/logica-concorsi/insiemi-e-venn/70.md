In a group of $40$ employees, $12$ know French and $27$ know English. What is the largest possible number who know both languages?

- $12$
- $15$
- $27$
- $1$

## Explanation

The **intersection** cannot have more members than the smaller group, which has $12$ here. This number is attainable if everyone who knows French also knows English.
