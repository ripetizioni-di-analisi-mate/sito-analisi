In an office of $64$ employees, $25$ took only the spreadsheet course, $13$ took only the English course, and $15$ took both courses. How many employees took neither course?

- $26$
- $11$
- $53$
- $41$

## Explanation

The counts $25$ and $13$ describe people who took *only* one course, so they exclude the $15$ employees who took both. Therefore, $25 + 13 + 15 = 53$ employees took at least one course, and $64 - 53 = 11$ took neither. The number $26$ overlooks the $15$ employees who took both courses. The number $41$ comes from applying the union formula and subtracting the $15$ employees in the overlap from counts that did not include them.
