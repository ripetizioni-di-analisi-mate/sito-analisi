Of the $80$ members of a club, $52$ play chess, $43$ play checkers, and $7$ play neither. How many members play just one of the two games?

- $22$
- $51$
- $73$
- $58$

## Explanation

The number of members who play at least one game is $80 - 7 = 73$. Let $S$ and $D$ be the sets of chess and checkers players. The formula for the size of a union gives $|S \cap D| = 52 + 43 - 73 = 22$. Those who play just one game belong to the union but not the intersection, so the required number of members is $73 - 22 = 51$. The number $22$ counts those who play both, and $58$ comes from calculating $80 - 22$, which also counts the $7$ who play neither.
