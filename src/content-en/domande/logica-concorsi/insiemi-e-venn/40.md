An office handles $120$ cases. Each may involve taxes, fines, refunds, several of these topics, or none. Taxes are involved in $50$ cases, fines in $40$, and refunds in $35$. Taxes and fines are involved in $15$ cases, taxes and refunds in $10$, fines and refunds in $12$, and all three topics in $6$. The pairwise counts include cases involving all three topics. How many cases involve taxes but not fines?

- $35$
- $31$
- $41$
- $29$

## Explanation

The cases in question form the difference between the set of tax cases and the set of cases involving fines. The $15$ cases involving taxes and fines already include the $6$ that also involve refunds, so the difference contains $50 - 15 = 35$ cases. The number $31$ counts cases involving only taxes. It leaves out the $4$ cases involving taxes and refunds but not fines. The number $41$ comes from subtracting only the $15 - 6 = 9$ cases involving taxes and fines without refunds. It retains the $6$ cases in the center, which also involve fines.
