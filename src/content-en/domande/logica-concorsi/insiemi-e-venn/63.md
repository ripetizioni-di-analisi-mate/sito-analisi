At a meeting of $52$ people, $28$ voted for proposal A and $26$ for proposal B. People could vote for both, and $11$ did so. How many voted for neither proposal?

- $9$
- $20$
- $11$
- $7$

## Explanation

By **inclusion–exclusion**, $28 + 26 - 11 = 43$ people voted for at least one proposal. Subtracting this from the total gives $52 - 43 = 9$.
