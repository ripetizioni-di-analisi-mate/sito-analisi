Two sets satisfy $A \cup B = B$ and $A \cap B = A$. Which statement must be correct?

- $A = \varnothing$
- $A = B$
- $B \subseteq A$
- $A \subseteq B$

## Explanation

The **union** equals $B$ only if $A$ adds no elements outside $B$. The fact that the intersection equals $A$ confirms that all the elements of $A$ also belong to $B$. Thus the inclusion $A \subseteq B$ is guaranteed, and it allows the two sets to be different.
