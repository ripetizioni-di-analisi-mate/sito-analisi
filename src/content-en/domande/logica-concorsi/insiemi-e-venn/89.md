What is the intersection of the positive integers less than $5$ with the integers greater than $7$?

- $\{5,7\}$
- $\{4,8\}$
- $\varnothing$
- $\{5,6,7\}$

## Explanation

An element in the **intersection** would have to be both less than $5$ and greater than $7$. No integer satisfies both conditions, so the intersection is empty.
