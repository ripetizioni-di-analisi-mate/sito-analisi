Which expression represents the set of elements belonging to exactly one of $A$ and $B$?

- $A \cap B$
- $(A \setminus B) \cup (B \setminus A)$
- $A \cup B$
- $(A \cap B) \setminus (A \cup B)$

## Explanation

The **difference** $A \setminus B$ contains the elements of $A$ only, and $B \setminus A$ contains those of $B$ only. Their union collects exactly the elements that occur in just one set.
