Which set corresponds to the shaded region of the Venn diagram?

::venn{tipo="tre" nomi="A,B,C" zone="ab,ac,abc"}

- $A \cap (B \cup C)$
- $(A \cap B) \cup C$
- $(A \cap B) \cup (A \cap C) \cup (B \cap C)$
- $A \cup (B \cap C)$

## Explanation

The shaded region lies entirely inside circle $A$ and consists of the parts of $A$ that also belong to $B$ or to $C$. Its elements therefore belong to both $A$ and $B \cup C$, so the set is $A \cap (B \cup C)$. The set $(A \cap B) \cup (A \cap C) \cup (B \cap C)$ also includes the region common to $B$ and $C$ but outside $A$, which is not shaded. The sets $(A \cap B) \cup C$ and $A \cup (B \cap C)$ each include an entire circle, $C$ and $A$, respectively.
