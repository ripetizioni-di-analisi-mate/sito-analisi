Two finite sets $A$ and $B$ satisfy $A \setminus B = \varnothing$. Which relationship follows?

- $B \subseteq A$
- $A = \varnothing$
- $A \cap B = \varnothing$
- $A \subseteq B$

## Explanation

If $A \setminus B$ is empty, every element of $A$ also belongs to $B$. This is the definition of a **subset**. For example, the condition holds for $A = \{1\}$ and $B = \{1, 2\}$, showing that the reverse inclusion need not hold.
