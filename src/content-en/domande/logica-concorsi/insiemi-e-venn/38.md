The diagram shows the numbers of club members enrolled in swimming ($N$), running ($C$), and cycling ($B$) classes. How many members are enrolled in at least two classes?

::venn{tipo="tre" nomi="N,C,B" elementi="a:12;b:9;c:7;ab:4;ac:3;bc:5;abc:2;u:6" titolo="Club members enrolled in swimming ($N$), running ($C$), and cycling ($B$) classes"}

- $14$
- $2$
- $12$
- $28$

## Explanation

The members enrolled in at least two classes lie in the three regions shared by exactly two circles and in the central region. In the diagram these regions contain $4$, $3$, $5$, and $2$ members, so $4 + 3 + 5 + 2 = 14$ are enrolled in at least two classes. The number $12$ leaves out the center and counts only those in exactly two classes. The number $28$ counts those in just one class.
