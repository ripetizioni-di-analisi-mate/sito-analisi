In an apartment building, $14$ households have a dog, $9$ have a cat, and $4$ have both. How many households have just one of the two pets?

- $23$
- $19$
- $10$
- $15$

## Explanation

The $14$ households with a dog include the $4$ that also have a cat, so $14 - 4 = 10$ households have only a dog. Similarly, $9 - 4 = 5$ households have only a cat. Therefore, $10 + 5 = 15$ households have just one of the two pets. The number $19$ counts the households with at least one of the pets, including the $4$ that have both. The number $10$ counts only the households with a dog alone.
