Of the $60$ employees having lunch in a cafeteria, $30$ order a first course, $25$ a main course, and $20$ dessert. There are $12$ who order a first course and a main course, $9$ a first course and dessert, $8$ a main course and dessert, and $5$ all three courses. The pairwise counts include those who order all three courses. How many employees order exactly two courses?

- $19$
- $24$
- $14$
- $29$

## Explanation

Fill in the diagram **from the center outward**. The center contains $5$ employees, so the regions for exactly two courses contain $12 - 5 = 7$, $9 - 5 = 4$, and $8 - 5 = 3$ employees. Therefore, $7 + 4 + 3 = 14$ employees order exactly two courses. The number $29$ adds the pairwise counts without removing the center, which is counted three times. The number $19$ also includes the $5$ employees who order all three courses.
