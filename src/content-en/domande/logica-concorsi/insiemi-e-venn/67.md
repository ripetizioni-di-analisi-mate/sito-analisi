At a seminar, $9$ people who registered attend both in person and online, while $18$ attend in only one of these ways. Three who registered do not attend in either way. How many people registered for the seminar?

- $27$
- $30$
- $21$
- $39$

## Explanation

The three categories are **disjoint** because each person who registered attends in zero, one, or two ways. Adding the counts gives $3 + 18 + 9 = 30$ people, counting each person who attends in both ways once.
