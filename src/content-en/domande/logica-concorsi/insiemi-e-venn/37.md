Each of the $45$ students at a school chose at least one of three workshops: theater, music, and painting. Theater was chosen by $23$ students, music by $24$, and painting by $22$. Theater and music were chosen by $10$ students, theater and painting by $9$, and music and painting by $11$. The pairwise counts include those who chose all three workshops. How many students chose only theater?

- $16$
- $10$
- $4$
- $6$

## Explanation

Let $x$ be the number of students who chose all three workshops. The formula for the number of elements in the union of three sets gives
$$
23 + 24 + 22 - 10 - 9 - 11 + x = 45 \iff 39 + x = 45 \iff x = 6 .
$$
The regions for theater and just one other workshop contain $10 - 6 = 4$ and $9 - 6 = 3$ students, so $23 - 4 - 3 - 6 = 10$ students chose theater only. The number $4$ comes from calculating $23 - 10 - 9$, which subtracts the $6$ students in the center twice. The number $6$ counts those who chose all three workshops.
