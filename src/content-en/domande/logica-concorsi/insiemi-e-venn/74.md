Of $35$ applications, $17$ are complete and $16$ arrived by the deadline. What is the smallest possible number that are both incomplete and late?

- $18$
- $0$
- $16$
- $2$

## Explanation

To minimize the number outside both sets, maximize the **union**. If the groups are disjoint, the union contains $17 + 16 = 33$ applications. That leaves $35 - 33 = 2$ outside it.
