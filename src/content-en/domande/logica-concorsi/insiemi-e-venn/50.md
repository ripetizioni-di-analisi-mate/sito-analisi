Among the integers from $10$ to $30$ inclusive, how many are divisible by $2$ or by $5$, counting numbers divisible by both only once?

- $3$
- $12$
- $13$
- $16$

## Explanation

The interval contains $11$ even numbers and $5$ multiples of $5$. The integers $10$, $20$, and $30$ belong to both sets. By **inclusion–exclusion**, the total is $11 + 5 - 3 = 13$.
