In the universal set $U = \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6\}$, take $A = \{2, 4, 6\}$ and $B = \{1, 2, 3\}$. Which elements belong to just one of the sets $U \setminus A$ and $B$?

- $\{2,5\}$
- $\{2,4,6\}$
- $\{1,3\}$
- $\{1,2,3,5\}$

## Explanation

The **complement** $U \setminus A$ is $\{1, 3, 5\}$, and $B$ is $\{1, 2, 3\}$. The element $5$ belongs to the first set only, and $2$ to the second only. The elements $1$ and $3$ are excluded because they occur in both.
