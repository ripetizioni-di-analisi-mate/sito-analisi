An archive holds $31$ files. Eighteen show the date of receipt, eleven show the name of the person in charge, and six show both. How many show just one of these details?

- $29$
- $17$
- $6$
- $23$

## Explanation

There are $18 - 6 = 12$ files showing only the date and $11 - 6 = 5$ showing only the person in charge. The two regions outside the **intersection** are disjoint, so the required total is $12 + 5 = 17$.
