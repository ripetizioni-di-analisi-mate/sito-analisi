Nineteen users requested notification A, seven requested both A and B, and six requested both A and C. The last two counts include the two users who requested all three notifications. How many requested only A?

- $10$
- $8$
- $13$
- $6$

## Explanation

Among the users who requested A, $7 + 6 - 2 = 11$ also requested B or C, by **inclusion–exclusion**. Subtracting them from the $19$ who requested A leaves $19 - 11 = 8$ who requested A only.
