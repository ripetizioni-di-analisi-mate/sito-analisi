In a class of $25$ students, we count those who play soccer, those who swim, those who do both, and those who do neither. Which of the following reports must be wrong?

- $16$ play soccer, $11$ swim, $3$ do both, $1$ do neither
- $10$ play soccer, $8$ swim, $5$ do both, $12$ do neither
- $15$ play soccer, $12$ swim, $4$ do both, $2$ do neither
- $18$ play soccer, $12$ swim, $3$ do both, $0$ do neither

## Explanation

The four regions of the diagram together contain the $25$ students. Therefore, in a correct report, the number who play soccer plus the number who swim, minus the number who do both, plus the number who do neither, must equal $25$. The report with $18$ soccer players gives $18 + 12 - 3 + 0 = 27$, more than the number of students in the class, so it contains an error. The other three give $25$ because $15 + 12 - 4 + 2 = 25$, $16 + 11 - 3 + 1 = 25$, and $10 + 8 - 5 + 12 = 25$. In each of them the number who do both does not exceed either the number who play soccer or the number who swim, so all three reports are possible.
