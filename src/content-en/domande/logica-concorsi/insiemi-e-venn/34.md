At a company, $60\%$ of the employees are women. Of all employees, $35\%$ have a university degree, and women with degrees make up $15\%$ of the entire workforce. What percentage of the men at the company have a degree?

- $25\%$
- $35\%$
- $50\%$
- $20\%$

## Explanation

Men make up $40\%$ of all employees, and men with degrees make up $35\% - 15\% = 20\%$ of all employees. The requested percentage is relative to the men, so it is
$$
\frac{20}{40} = \frac{1}{2} = 50\% .
$$
The value $20\%$ is the percentage of men with degrees relative to all employees. The value $25\%$ is the percentage of women with degrees among all women because $\dfrac{15}{60} = \dfrac{1}{4}$.
