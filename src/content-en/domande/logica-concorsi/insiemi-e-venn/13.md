At a library, $R$ is the set of members who borrowed at least one novel last month and $S$ is the set of members who borrowed at least one nonfiction book. Which set consists of members who borrowed at least one novel and no nonfiction books?

- $R \setminus S$
- $\overline{R \cup S}$
- $R \cap S$
- $S \setminus R$

## Explanation

The members in question belong to $R$ and do not belong to $S$, so they form the **difference** $R \setminus S$. The order of the sets matters because $S \setminus R$ is the set of those who borrowed at least one nonfiction book and no novels. The set $R \cap S$ contains those who borrowed both novels and nonfiction books, and $\overline{R \cup S}$ contains those who borrowed neither.
