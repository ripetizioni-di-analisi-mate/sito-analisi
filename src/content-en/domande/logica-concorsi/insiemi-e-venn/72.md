Lists A and B contain 13 and 11 people from a group of 20. At least 3 people appear on neither list. What is the smallest possible overlap?

- $4$
- $3$
- $11$
- $7$

## Explanation

The union can contain at most 17 people. The overlap is at least $13+11-17=7$. This is attainable with 6 people only in A and 4 only in B.
