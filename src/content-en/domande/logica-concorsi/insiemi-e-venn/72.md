Of $70$ users, $42$ have enabled text alerts and $39$ have enabled email alerts. Which number of users could have enabled both?

- $40$
- $43$
- $10$
- $22$

## Explanation

Let $x$ be the number of users with both alerts. By the **inclusion–exclusion principle** the users with at least one alert number $42 + 39 - x = 81 - x$, and since they cannot exceed $70$ we have $x \geq 11$. Moreover, the users with both alerts are among those with email alerts, so $x \leq 39$. The value $22$ lies between $11$ and $39$ and leaves $81 - 22 = 59$ users with at least one alert and $11$ users with no alerts, so it is possible. The values $40$ and $43$ exceed $39$, while $10$ is less than $11$.
