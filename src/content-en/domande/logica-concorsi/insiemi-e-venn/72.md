Of $70$ users, $42$ have enabled text alerts and $39$ have enabled email alerts. Which number of users could have enabled both?

- $40$
- $43$
- $10$
- $22$

## Explanation

By **inclusion–exclusion**, the intersection has at least $42 + 39 - 70 = 11$ and at most $39$ users. Twenty-two is in that range and gives $42 + 39 - 22 = 59$ users with at least one alert. The other $11$ have enabled neither alert.
