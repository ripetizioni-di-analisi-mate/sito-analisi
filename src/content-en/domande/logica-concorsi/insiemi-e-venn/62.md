For a library shift, $22$ volunteers are available on Monday and $17$ on Tuesday. Eight are available both days. How many are available only on Tuesday?

- $8$
- $31$
- $14$
- $9$

## Explanation

Of the volunteers on Tuesday’s list, $8$ are also on Monday’s. The **set difference** leaves $17 - 8 = 9$ volunteers available only on Tuesday.
