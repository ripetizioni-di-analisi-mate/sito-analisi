In an archive, $A$ is the set of open cases, $U$ is the set of all cases, and $F$ is the set of signed cases. Which expression represents the signed cases that are not open?

- $F \cup (U \setminus A)$
- $A \cap F$
- $A \setminus F$
- $F \cap (U \setminus A)$

## Explanation

The **complement** $U \setminus A$ contains all cases that are not open. Taking its **intersection** with $F$ selects just the signed cases among them.
