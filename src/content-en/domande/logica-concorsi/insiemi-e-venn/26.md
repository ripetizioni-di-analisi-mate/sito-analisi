At a conference, every attendee went to at least one of the two sessions, one in the morning and one in the afternoon. The morning session had $45$ attendees, the afternoon session had $38$, and $21$ attended both. How many people attended the conference?

- $83$
- $62$
- $41$
- $104$

## Explanation

Every attendee went to at least one session, so the attendees form the union of the two sets. By the formula for the **number of elements in a union**, the conference had $45 + 38 - 21 = 62$ attendees. The number $83$ counts the $21$ people who attended both sessions twice: it involves **double counting**. The number $41$ comes from subtracting the $21$ people in the overlap twice.
