Of $36$ registration records, $20$ list a phone number and $27$ list an email address. What is the largest possible number of records with exactly one of these two contact details?

- $25$
- $27$
- $36$
- $11$

## Explanation

The **intersection** has at least $20 + 27 - 36 = 11$ records, attained if every record has at least one contact detail. In this case the number with just one contact detail reaches its maximum of $20 + 27 - 2 \cdot 11 = 25$.
