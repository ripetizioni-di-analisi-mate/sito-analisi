In a class of $30$ students, $12$ play soccer and $9$ play volleyball. What is the largest possible number who play neither sport?

- $21$
- $9$
- $18$
- $12$

## Explanation

The number who play neither sport is $30$ minus the number in the union, so it is largest when the union is smallest. The union contains at least the $12$ soccer players. It contains exactly $12$ students when all $9$ volleyball players also play soccer. The maximum is therefore $30 - 12 = 18$. The number $9$ is the minimum, attained when nobody plays both sports and the union has $12 + 9 = 21$ elements.
