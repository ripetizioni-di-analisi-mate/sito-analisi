Each of the $40$ members of a club is enrolled in at least one of three classes: bridge, burraco, and chess. There are $25$ members in only one class and $10$ in exactly two. How many class enrollments are there in total, counting one enrollment per member for each class they attend?

- $40$
- $50$
- $60$
- $45$

## Explanation

The number of members in all three classes is $40 - 25 - 10 = 5$. Each member contributes one enrollment for each class they take, so the number of enrollments is
$$
25 \cdot 1 + 10 \cdot 2 + 5 \cdot 3 = 25 + 20 + 15 = 60 .
$$
This number is the sum of the enrollment counts for the three classes, in which a member who takes two classes is counted twice and a member who takes three is counted three times. The number $40$ counts members rather than enrollments, and $45$ overlooks the $5$ members in all three classes.
