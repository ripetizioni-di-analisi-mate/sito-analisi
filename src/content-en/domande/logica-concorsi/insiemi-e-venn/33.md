At a company with $50$ employees, $8$ speak neither English nor French. Three times as many speak only English as speak both languages, and twice as many speak only French as speak both. How many employees speak French?

- $14$
- $7$
- $28$
- $21$

## Explanation

Let $x$ be the number of employees who speak both languages. Then $3x$ speak only English and $2x$ speak only French. There are $50 - 8 = 42$ employees who speak at least one language, so
$$
3x + x + 2x = 42 \iff 6x = 42 \iff x = 7 .
$$
The number who speak French is the number who speak only French plus the number who speak both languages, namely $14 + 7 = 21$. The number $14$ counts only those who speak French alone, and $28$ counts those who speak English.
