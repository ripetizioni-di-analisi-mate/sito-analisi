Which description corresponds to the Venn diagram for “books,” “novels,” and “books written in the nineteenth century”?

- The three circles are nested
- The three circles overlap pairwise, with a region common to all three, and each extends outside the other two
- The circles for novels and for books written in the nineteenth century are separate and both lie inside the circle for books
- The circles for novels and for books written in the nineteenth century overlap in part and both lie inside the circle for books

## Explanation

Novels and books written in the nineteenth century are books, so their circles are **included** in the circle for books. *The Betrothed* is a novel written in the nineteenth century, so the two inner circles overlap. There are also twentieth-century novels and nineteenth-century books that are not novels, such as poetry collections, so neither inner circle is contained in the other. A diagram in which each of the three circles extends outside the other two would imply that some novels are not books, which is impossible.
