Of $48$ customers, $19$ have a membership card and $24$ have the app. What is the largest possible number with neither the card nor the app?

- $19$
- $24$
- $29$
- $5$

## Explanation

To leave as many customers as possible outside the **union**, the union must be as small as possible. This happens if all $19$ cardholders also have the app. The union then contains $24$ customers, leaving $48 - 24 = 24$ outside it.
