A report on $27$ files says that $18$ are digital, $14$ are signed, and $4$ are both digital and signed. What can be concluded?

- The counts are consistent, and all files have at least one of the two characteristics.
- The three counts are inconsistent with the total.
- The counts are consistent, and four files have neither characteristic.
- The counts are consistent, and one file has neither characteristic.

## Explanation

By **inclusion–exclusion**, the number of files that are digital or signed would be $18 + 14 - 4 = 28$. A group of $27$ files cannot contain a subset of $28$ files, so the report is inconsistent.
