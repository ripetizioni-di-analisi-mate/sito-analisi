We want to apply the filter $A \cap (B \cup C)$. Which expression gives the same result for every choice of sets?

- $(A \cap B) \cup (A \cap C)$
- $(A \cap B) \cap (A \cap C)$
- $(A \cup B) \cap (A \cup C)$
- $A \cup (B \cap C)$

## Explanation

An element must belong to $A$ and also to $B$ or $C$. By the **distributive property**, we can take the elements common to $A$ and $B$ and form their union with the elements common to $A$ and $C$.
