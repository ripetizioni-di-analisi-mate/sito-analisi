Thirty-five people use at least one of rooms A and B. Eleven use room A without using B or C. Eight use B without A or C, seven use only A and B, four use only A and C, and three use only B and C. How many use all three rooms?

- $7$
- $5$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

The five given regions are **disjoint** and contain $11 + 8 + 7 + 4 + 3 = 33$ people. The only region of the union of A and B still unaccounted for is the one common to all three rooms. It therefore contains $35 - 33 = 2$ people.
