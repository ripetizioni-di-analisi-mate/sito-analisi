A survey of three services records $29$ users for A, $25$ for B, and $20$ for C. The pairs A–B, A–C, and B–C have $11$, $9$, and $7$ users in common, respectively, including users of all three services. Given that $50$ people use at least one service, how many use all three?

- $5$
- $3$
- $2$
- $7$

## Explanation

Without the term for the triple intersection, the **inclusion–exclusion** formula gives $29 + 25 + 20 - 11 - 9 - 7 = 47$. To reach the union of $50$, we must add $50 - 47 = 3$, the number who use all three services.
