In a group of $80$ people, $32$ speak English, $28$ French, and $25$ German. Of these, $6$ speak only English and French, $5$ only English and German, $4$ only French and German, and $3$ speak all three languages. How many people speak none of the three?

- $16$
- $10$
- $19$
- $7$

## Explanation

The word “only” indicates the regions shared by exactly two languages, excluding the center, so the counts $6$, $5$, and $4$ are entered directly in the diagram. The regions for one language contain $32 - 6 - 5 - 3 = 18$, $28 - 6 - 4 - 3 = 15$, and $25 - 5 - 4 - 3 = 13$ people. The seven regions inside the circles contain $18 + 15 + 13 + 6 + 5 + 4 + 3 = 64$ people in total, so $80 - 64 = 16$ speak none of the three languages. The number $7$ comes from treating $6$, $5$, and $4$ as intersections that include the center, that is, from calculating $80 - (32 + 28 + 25 - 6 - 5 - 4 + 3)$. The number $19$ comes from leaving the center out of the sum of the seven regions.
