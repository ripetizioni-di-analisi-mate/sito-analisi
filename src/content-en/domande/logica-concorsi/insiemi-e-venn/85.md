In a group of $38$ documents, $16$ have already been signed and $13$ are still awaiting signature. The two categories are disjoint. How many documents fall outside both?

- $9$
- $29$
- $3$
- $13$

## Explanation

Since the categories are **disjoint**, we can add $16 + 13 = 29$ without counting any document twice. The complement of the union contains $38 - 29 = 9$ documents.
