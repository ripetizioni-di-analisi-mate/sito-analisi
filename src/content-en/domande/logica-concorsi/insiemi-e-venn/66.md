Of $32$ workstations, $12$ have a webcam. Fourteen have a printer but no webcam, and five have both devices. How many have neither device?

- $6$
- $7$
- $11$
- $5$

## Explanation

The $12$ workstations with webcams already include the $5$ with both devices. The $14$ with only a printer are **disjoint** from those with webcams, so the union contains $12 + 14 = 26$ workstations. This leaves $32 - 26 = 6$ with neither device.
