Of $45$ applications, $25$ include a certificate and $21$ include a letter of reference. Six include neither document. How many include both?

- $7$
- $13$
- $18$
- $4$

## Explanation

The number of applications with at least one document is $45 - 6 = 39$. The sum $25 + 21 = 46$ counts the applications with both twice. By **inclusion–exclusion**, the intersection has $46 - 39 = 7$ elements.
