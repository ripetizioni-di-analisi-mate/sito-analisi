Let $P$ be the set of prime numbers and $Q$ the set of multiples of $4$, both taken among the positive integers. Which of the following statements is true?

- $P \cap Q = \{2\}$
- $Q \subseteq P$
- $P \cap Q = \{4\}$
- $P \cap Q = \emptyset$

## Explanation

Every multiple of $4$ can be written as $4k = 2 \cdot 2k$ with $k$ a positive integer, so it is divisible by $2$ and greater than $2$ and is therefore not prime. Thus $P$ and $Q$ have no elements in common: they are **disjoint**, and $P \cap Q = \emptyset$. The number $2$ is prime but not a multiple of $4$. The number $4$ is a multiple of $4$ but is not prime because $4 = 2 \cdot 2$.
