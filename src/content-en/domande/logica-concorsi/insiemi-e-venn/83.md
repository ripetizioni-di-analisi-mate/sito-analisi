In a group, $23$ people use service A, $18$ use B, and $21$ use C. The A–B, A–C, and B–C intersections contain $7$, $8$, and $6$ people, respectively, counting the $2$ users of all three services. How many use exactly one service?

- $26$
- $30$
- $24$
- $43$

## Explanation

To find the regions for just one service, subtract from each group its two pairwise intersections and then add back the **triple intersection**. The counts are $23 - 7 - 8 + 2 = 10$, $18 - 7 - 6 + 2 = 7$, and $21 - 8 - 6 + 2 = 9$ people. Their total is $26$.
