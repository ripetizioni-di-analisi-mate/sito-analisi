All $9$ people authorized to enter the storage room are also authorized to enter the archive. In total, $23$ people have permission to enter the archive. How many have exactly one of the two permissions?

- $23$
- $9$
- $14$
- $32$

## Explanation

The people authorized to enter the storage room form a **subset** of those authorized to enter the archive, so all $9$ have both permissions. Subtracting them from the $23$ people with access to the archive leaves $23 - 9 = 14$ people with only one permission.
