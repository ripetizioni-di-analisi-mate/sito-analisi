On a platform, $12$ users follow both channels A and B, $9$ follow A and C, and $8$ follow B and C. Each count includes the $5$ users who follow all three channels. How many follow exactly two?

- $24$
- $14$
- $9$
- $19$

## Explanation

Each **pairwise intersection** also contains the $5$ users who follow all three channels. Subtracting them from each pair leaves $7$, $4$, and $3$ users. The resulting three regions are disjoint, so the total is $14$.
