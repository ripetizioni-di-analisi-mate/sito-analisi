Among $60$ students, $37$ choose the writing course and $34$ the arithmetic course. Enrollment in both is allowed. What is the smallest possible number enrolled in both?

- $0$
- $23$
- $11$
- $34$

## Explanation

The two enrollment counts add up to $37 + 34 = 71$. Since there are only $60$ students, at least $71 - 60 = 11$ must appear twice. The minimum is attained when everyone chooses at least one course.
