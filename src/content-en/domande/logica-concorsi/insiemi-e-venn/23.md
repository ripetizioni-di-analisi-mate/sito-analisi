Which of the following sets of three categories is represented by two separate circles inside a third circle?

- Days of the year, Saturdays, Sundays
- Integers, even numbers, multiples of $4$
- Integers, even numbers, multiples of $3$
- Even numbers, odd numbers, multiples of $5$

## Explanation

Saturdays and Sundays are days of the year, and no day is both a Saturday and a Sunday. Their circles are therefore separate and contained in the circle for days of the year. The number $6$ is even and is a multiple of $3$, so the circles for even numbers and for multiples of $3$ overlap. Every multiple of $4$ is even, so the circle for multiples of $4$ lies inside the one for even numbers. The multiples of $5$ include even numbers such as $10$ and odd numbers such as $5$, so their circle overlaps the separate circles for even and odd numbers without containing either.
