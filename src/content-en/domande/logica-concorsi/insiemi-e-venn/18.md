Which description corresponds to the Venn diagram for “mammals,” “cats,” and “fish”?

- The circle for cats is inside the circle for mammals, and the circle for fish is separate from both
- The circles for cats and fish are separate and both lie inside the circle for mammals
- The three circles are separate
- The circles for cats and mammals overlap in part, and the circle for fish is separate from both

## Explanation

Every cat is a mammal, so the circle for cats is **included** in the circle for mammals. Fish are not mammals, so the circle for fish is **disjoint** from the circle for mammals and therefore also from the circle for cats inside it. Two separate circles inside the circle for mammals would classify fish as mammals. Two partially overlapping circles for cats and mammals would imply that some cats are not mammals, which is impossible.
