Every approved application is registered, and every registered application has a case number. At least one approved application was submitted by email. Which conclusion is guaranteed?

- All applications submitted by email were approved.
- All applications with a case number were approved.
- At least one application submitted by email has a case number.
- At least one approved application has no case number.

## Explanation

The approved application submitted by email belongs to the set of registered applications. By the second **inclusion**, all registered applications have a case number. This application is therefore an example of one submitted by email that has a case number.
