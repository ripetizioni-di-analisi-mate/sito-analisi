Forty participants could choose workshops A, B, and C. The numbers who chose A, B, and C were $18$, $18$, and $16$, respectively. The intersections of A with B, A with C, and B with C contain $6$, $7$, and $5$ people, respectively, each including those who chose all three. Two people chose all three. How many chose no workshop?

- $10$
- $2$
- $6$
- $4$

## Explanation

By **inclusion–exclusion**, the union contains $18 + 18 + 16 - 6 - 7 - 5 + 2 = 36$ people. The $2$ people who chose all three workshops must be added back because they belong to all three pairwise intersections. This leaves $40 - 36 = 4$ people.
