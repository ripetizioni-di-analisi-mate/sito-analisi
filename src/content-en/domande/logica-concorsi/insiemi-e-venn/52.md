Every reviewed report is archived, and no archived report is still a draft. There is at least one reviewed report and at least one draft. What is the relationship between the sets of reviewed reports and draft reports?

- They are equal
- They are disjoint
- They have exactly one element in common
- Every draft has been reviewed

## Explanation

A reviewed report also belongs to the set of archived reports. Since no archived report is a draft, a reviewed report cannot be a draft. The two sets are therefore **disjoint**.
