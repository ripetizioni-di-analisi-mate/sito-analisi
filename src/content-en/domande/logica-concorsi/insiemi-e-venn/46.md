Among the integers from $1$ to $25$, $A$ is the set of multiples of $4$ and $B$ is the set of multiples of $6$. What is $A \setminus B$?

- $\{4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24\}$
- $\{4, 8, 16, 20\}$
- $\{6, 18\}$
- $\{12, 24\}$

## Explanation

The **set difference** keeps the elements of $A$ that are not in $B$. The multiples of $4$ are $4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24$. Among them, $12$ and $24$ are also multiples of $6$. Removing them leaves $4, 8, 16, 20$.
