A train carries $200$ passengers, of whom $120$ travel in second class and the rest in first class. There are $70$ passengers with a rail pass, and $25$ of them travel in first class. How many second-class passengers do not have a pass?

- $50$
- $75$
- $55$
- $95$

## Explanation

Arrange the data in a **two-way table** and find the missing entries by subtracting from the totals.

| | Rail pass | No rail pass | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First class | $25$ | $55$ | $80$ |
| Second class | $45$ | $75$ | $120$ |
| Total | $70$ | $130$ | $200$ |

There are $70 - 25 = 45$ second-class passengers with passes, so $120 - 45 = 75$ second-class passengers have no pass. The number $50$ comes from calculating $120 - 70$, which subtracts even the first-class pass holders from the second-class total. The number $55$ counts first-class passengers without passes.
