At a company, $U$ is the set of employees, $A$ the set of employees with a university degree, and $B$ the set of employees with a law degree. Which of the following relationships holds regardless of who works at the company?

- $A \cup B = U$
- $A \cap B = \emptyset$
- $B \subseteq A$
- $A \subseteq B$

## Explanation

Every employee with a law degree has a university degree, so each element of $B$ belongs to $A$, and $B \subseteq A$ holds. The inclusion $A \subseteq B$ says that everyone with a degree has a law degree. An employee with a degree in economics and no law degree would provide a counterexample. Since the elements of $B$ also belong to $A$, we have $A \cap B = B$, which is empty only if the company has no employees with law degrees. Finally, $A \cup B = A$, which coincides with $U$ only if all the employees have degrees.
