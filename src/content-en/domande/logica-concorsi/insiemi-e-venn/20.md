Which description corresponds to the Venn diagram for “teachers,” “women,” and “people from Naples”?

- Three circles that overlap pairwise, with a region common to all three
- Two separate circles and a third circle that overlaps both
- One circle inside another and a third circle that overlaps both
- Three circles that overlap pairwise, with no region common to all three

## Explanation

Examine the three pairs one at a time. There are women who teach, teachers from Naples, and women from Naples, and each category contains people who belong to neither of the other two. Thus, each pair of circles **overlaps** in part. A woman from Naples who teaches belongs to all three categories, so the diagram also has a region common to all three circles. A diagram without a common region would exclude precisely those women from Naples who teach.
