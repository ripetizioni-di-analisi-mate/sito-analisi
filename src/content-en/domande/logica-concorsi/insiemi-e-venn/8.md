How many subsets with exactly two elements does the set $\{\text{red}, \text{green}, \text{blue}, \text{yellow}\}$ have?

- $4$
- $16$
- $6$
- $12$

## Explanation

A two-element subset is determined by its two elements. Order does not matter because $\{\text{red}, \text{green}\}$ and $\{\text{green}, \text{red}\}$ are the same set. Red pairs with green, blue, or yellow, green pairs with blue or yellow, and blue pairs with yellow. This gives $3 + 2 + 1 = 6$ subsets. The number $12$ counts each pair twice, once in each order. The number $16 = 2^4$ counts all subsets, including the empty set and the whole set.
