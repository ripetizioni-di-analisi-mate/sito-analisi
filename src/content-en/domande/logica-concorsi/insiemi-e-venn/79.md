At a gathering, $20$ people drink tea, $17$ coffee, and $17$ juice. The tea–coffee, tea–juice, and coffee–juice intersections contain $7$, $6$, and $5$ people, respectively, including the $2$ who drink all three beverages. How many drink at least two?

- $16$
- $12$
- $18$
- $14$

## Explanation

Adding the **pairwise intersections** gives $7 + 6 + 5 = 18$ occurrences. The $2$ people who drink all three beverages are counted three times each, so we subtract two occurrences per person. The result is $18 - 4 = 14$.
