All temporary permits are registered. No registered permit is anonymous. There is at least one temporary permit. Which conclusion follows?

- Some permits are both anonymous and temporary.
- Every permit that is not anonymous is temporary.
- All registered permits are temporary.
- There is at least one registered permit that is not anonymous.

## Explanation

The temporary permit whose existence is given in the problem is **registered** by the first premise. The second premise rules out a registered permit being anonymous. That one permit establishes the required existence.
