Which of the following properties defines the set $\{2, 3, 5, 7\}$ among the natural numbers?

- Being a prime number less than $7$
- Being an odd number less than $8$
- Being an odd prime number less than $10$
- Being a prime number less than $10$

## Explanation

The primes less than $10$ are $2$, $3$, $5$, and $7$, so the property “being a prime number less than $10$” defines exactly the given set. The odd numbers less than $8$ form the set $\{1, 3, 5, 7\}$, which contains $1$ and does not contain $2$. The odd primes less than $10$ form $\{3, 5, 7\}$ because $2$ is prime and even. The primes less than $7$ form $\{2, 3, 5\}$, since the strict inequality excludes $7$.
