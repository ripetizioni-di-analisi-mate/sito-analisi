A recruitment notice excludes from the oral test applicants who have no university degree and no experience in the field. All other applicants are admitted. Let $L$ be the set of applicants with degrees and $E$ the set of applicants with experience in the field. Which set consists of the applicants who are admitted?

- $L \setminus E$
- $L \cap E$
- $\overline{L} \cup \overline{E}$
- $L \cup E$

## Explanation

The excluded applicants belong to neither $L$ nor $E$, so they form the set $\overline{L} \cap \overline{E}$, which equals $\overline{L \cup E}$ by **De Morgan’s law**. The admitted applicants form its complement, so they form the set $L \cup E$ of applicants who meet at least one requirement. The set $L \cap E$ would also leave out those who meet just one of the requirements. The set $\overline{L} \cup \overline{E}$ is the complement of $L \cap E$, that is, the set of those who lack at least one requirement, and it includes the excluded applicants.
