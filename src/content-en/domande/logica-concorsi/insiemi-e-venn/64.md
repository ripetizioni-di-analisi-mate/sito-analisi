Two lists have 16 and 19 distinct names respectively, with 28 people in their recorded union. One matching name was a mistaken merger of two different people, one on each list. How many names still match after it is corrected?

- $6$
- $7$
- $8$
- $9$

## Explanation

The intersection originally contained $16+19-28=7$ names. Splitting one mistaken match reduces that count by one.
