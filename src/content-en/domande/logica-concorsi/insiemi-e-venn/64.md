Of $30$ files, $20$ are digital and $18$ are confidential. Four are neither digital nor confidential. How many are either digital or confidential, but not both?

- $14$
- $12$
- $16$
- $26$

## Explanation

The union contains $30 - 4 = 26$ files. The **intersection** contains $20 + 18 - 26 = 12$. Removing these from the union leaves $26 - 12 = 14$ files with exactly one of the characteristics.
