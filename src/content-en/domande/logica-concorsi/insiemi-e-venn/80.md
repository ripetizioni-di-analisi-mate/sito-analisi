In a warehouse, $19$ boxes have label A, $17$ have label B, and $17$ have label C. The pairs A and B, A and C, and B and C appear on $9$, $6$, and $5$ boxes, respectively. Each count includes the $2$ boxes with all three labels. How many boxes have at least one label?

- $37$
- $33$
- $35$
- $39$

## Explanation

By **inclusion–exclusion**, add the three groups and subtract the pairwise intersections. Add back the boxes in all three groups once. This gives $19 + 17 + 17 - 9 - 6 - 5 + 2 = 35$ boxes.
