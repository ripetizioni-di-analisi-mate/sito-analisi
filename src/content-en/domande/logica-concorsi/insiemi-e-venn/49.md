Given $A = \{1, 3, 5\}$, $B = \{2, 3, 5, 8\}$, and $C = \{2, 3, 8\}$, what is the set $(A \cup B) \setminus C$?

- $\{3, 5\}$
- $\{1, 5\}$
- $\{1, 3, 5\}$
- $\{2, 3, 8\}$

## Explanation

The **union** of $A$ and $B$ contains $1, 2, 3, 5, 8$. Subtracting $C$ removes $2$, $3$, and $8$, even though $3$ belonged to $A$. This leaves $1$ and $5$.
