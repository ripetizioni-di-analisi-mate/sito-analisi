Each student in a class of $28$ studies at least one of two languages, English and Spanish. There are $20$ students who study English and $15$ who study Spanish. How many study only Spanish?

- $15$
- $13$
- $8$
- $7$

## Explanation

Every student studies at least one language, so the union of the two sets has $28$ elements. By the formula for the size of a union, $20 + 15 - 28 = 7$ students study both languages. Therefore, $15 - 7 = 8$ study only Spanish. The number $15$ also includes the $7$ students who study both languages, and $13$ counts those who study only English.
