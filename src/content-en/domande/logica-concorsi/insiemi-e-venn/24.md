In the Venn diagram, circle $A$ represents the first category in a pair, and circle $B$ represents the second. Which pair of categories is represented correctly?

::venn{nomi="A,B"}

- Even numbers, multiples of $4$
- Days in March, days in April
- Dogs, mammals
- Nurses, men

## Explanation

The diagram has two circles that overlap in part, so the categories have elements in common and each also has elements that the other does not. There are men who are nurses, women who are nurses, and men who are not nurses, so the pair “nurses, men” has this relationship. Every dog is a mammal and every multiple of $4$ is even, so each of those two pairs needs one circle inside the other. No day in March is a day in April, so that pair needs two separate circles.
