In the following Venn diagram the rectangle represents the universal set. Which set does the shaded region correspond to?

::venn{tipo="tre" nomi="A,B,C" zone="c,u"}

- $C \setminus (A \cup B)$
- $\overline{A \cup B}$
- $\overline{A} \cup \overline{B}$
- $\overline{A \cup B \cup C}$

## Explanation

The shaded region includes the part of circle $C$ outside $A$ and $B$, as well as the part of the rectangle outside the three circles. Its elements are precisely those that belong to neither $A$ nor $B$. Thus the region is $\overline{A} \cap \overline{B}$, which equals $\overline{A \cup B}$ by **De Morgan’s law**. The set $C \setminus (A \cup B)$ omits the part of the rectangle outside the circles, and the set $\overline{A \cup B \cup C}$ omits the shaded part of circle $C$. The set $\overline{A} \cup \overline{B}$ is the complement of $A \cap B$, so it also includes the parts of $A$ and $B$ outside $A \cap B$.
