How many subsets of $A = \{2, 4, 6, 8\}$ contain $2$ but do not contain $8$?

- $4$
- $6$
- $2$
- $8$

## Explanation

Including $2$ and excluding $8$ are fixed requirements. Each of the two remaining elements, $4$ and $6$, can be included or excluded independently. Therefore, $2 \cdot 2 = 4$ **subsets** meet the requirements.
