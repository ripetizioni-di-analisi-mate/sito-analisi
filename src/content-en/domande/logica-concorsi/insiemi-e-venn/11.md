Which set corresponds to the shaded region of the Venn diagram, where the rectangle represents the universal set?

::venn{nomi="A,B" zone="a,b" universo="1"}

- $(A \cup B) \setminus (A \cap B)$
- $A \cup B$
- $A \setminus B$
- $\overline{A \cap B}$

## Explanation

The shaded region includes the part of $A$ outside $B$ and the part of $B$ outside $A$. It includes neither the overlap of the circles nor the region outside both. Its elements therefore belong to the union $A \cup B$ and not to the intersection $A \cap B$, so the set is $(A \cup B) \setminus (A \cap B)$. The union $A \cup B$ also includes the overlap of the circles. The complement $\overline{A \cap B}$ also includes the area of the rectangle outside both circles.
