A travel agency has $100$ customers. Last year, $45$ took a cruise, $40$ vacationed in the mountains, and $35$ visited a city known for its art. Of these customers, $15$ took a cruise and vacationed in the mountains, $12$ took a cruise and visited a city known for its art, $5$ took all three kinds of trips, and $10$ took none of them. The pairwise counts include customers who took all three kinds of trips. How many vacationed in the mountains and visited a city known for its art?

- $3$
- $5$
- $8$
- $13$

## Explanation

There are $100 - 10 = 90$ customers who took at least one of these trips. Let $x$ be the number we seek. The formula for the **number of elements in the union of three sets** gives
$$
45 + 40 + 35 - 15 - 12 - x + 5 = 90 \iff 98 - x = 90 \iff x = 8 .
$$
The number $x$ includes the $5$ customers who took all three kinds of trips. The number $3$ comes from omitting the term $+5$ in the formula and counts the customers who vacationed in the mountains and visited a city known for its art but did not take a cruise.
