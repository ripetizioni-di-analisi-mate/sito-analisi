Five visitors, A, B, C, D, and E, are called one at a time. C is third. A and B must be called in consecutive slots, in either order. In how many different orders can the visitors be called?

- $12$
- $6$
- $8$
- $4$

## Explanation

A and B can take first and second place or fourth and fifth place, since C is third. For each choice, there are two orders for A and B and two ways to arrange D and E, giving $2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 = 8$ in total.
