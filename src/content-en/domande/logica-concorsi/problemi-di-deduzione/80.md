Ada, Bruno, and Cleo each either always tell the truth or always lie. Ada says, “Bruno is a truth-teller.” Bruno says, “Cleo is lying.” Cleo says, “Ada is lying.” If exactly one person is a truth-teller, who is it?

- Cleo
- It is impossible to tell
- Bruno
- Ada

## Explanation

Ada and Bruno are the same type: if Ada tells the truth, Bruno is a truth-teller, and if she is lying, Bruno is lying too. Since there is only one truth-teller, they must both be lying. The truth-teller is Cleo, who correctly says that Ada is lying.
