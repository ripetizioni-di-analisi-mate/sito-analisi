Ada, Bruno, and Cleo each either always tell the truth or always lie. Ada says, “Bruno is a truth-teller.” Bruno says, “Cleo is lying.” Cleo says, “Ada is lying.” If exactly one person is a truth-teller, who is it?

- Cleo
- It is impossible to tell
- Bruno
- Ada

## Explanation

If Ada tells the truth, her statement is true and Bruno is a truth-teller; if Ada lies, her statement is false and Bruno lies too. Hence Ada and Bruno are of the same type. Since there is only one truth-teller, Ada and Bruno cannot both be truth-tellers, so they both lie and the truth-teller is Cleo. This assignment is consistent, because Bruno lies when he says that Cleo is lying and Cleo tells the truth when she says that Ada is lying.
