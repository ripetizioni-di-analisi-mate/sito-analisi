Ada and Bruno each always tell the truth or always lie. Asked “Are you both truthful?”, Ada answers “No”. Whose types can be determined?

- Both: Ada is truthful and Bruno lies
- Only Bruno, who is truthful
- Both: both lie
- Only Ada, who lies

## Explanation

If Ada were lying, her “no” would be false, so both would have to be truthful, contradicting the assumption. Ada is truthful, and her “no” requires Bruno to lie.
