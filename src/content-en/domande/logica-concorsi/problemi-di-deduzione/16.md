Five employees, Sara, Teo, Ugo, Vito, and Zaira, work at five service counters in a row, numbered $1$ through $5$. Vito works at counter $4$. Zaira’s counter is next to Vito’s. Ugo works at a counter at one end. Sara’s counter number is one greater than Teo’s. Teo does not work at counter $1$. At which counter does Sara work?

- At counter $2$
- At counter $3$
- At counter $5$
- At counter $1$

## Explanation

Zaira is next to Vito, so she is at counter $3$ or $5$. If Zaira were at counter $3$, counters $1$, $2$, and $5$ would remain. Teo and Sara, whose numbers are consecutive, would have to be at counters $1$ and $2$, with Teo at $1$. That contradicts the last clue. So Zaira is at counter $5$, and Ugo, who is at one end, is at counter $1$. Teo and Sara occupy counters $2$ and $3$, so Sara works at **counter $3$**. The answer “At counter $2$” confuses Sara with Teo.
