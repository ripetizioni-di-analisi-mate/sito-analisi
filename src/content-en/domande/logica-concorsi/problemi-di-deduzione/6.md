Fabio, Gemma, Lia, and Ivo each participate in a different sport (tennis, swimming, volleyball, or basketball) and live in a different city (Genoa, Lecce, Como, or Siena), one person per city. The tennis player lives in Genoa. Fabio lives in Lecce. The person who lives in Como swims. Lia plays basketball. Ivo does not live in Como. Who plays volleyball, and in which city does that person live?

- Ivo, in Genoa
- Fabio, in Lecce
- Gemma, in Siena
- Ivo, in Siena

## Explanation

Each sport and each city belongs to just one person, so the tennis player is the person who lives in Genoa, and the swimmer is the person who lives in Como. Fabio lives in Lecce, so he neither plays tennis nor swims. Lia plays basketball. That leaves **volleyball** for Fabio, who lives in Lecce. The other clues complete the table: Ivo plays tennis in Genoa, Gemma swims in Como, and Lia lives in Siena. The answer “Ivo, in Genoa” confuses tennis with volleyball.
