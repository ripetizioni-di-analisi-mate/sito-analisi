Four library employees, Aurelio, Bruna, Cinzia, and Duccio, take turns staffing the front desk, one person per shift: 9\:00–11\:00, 11\:00–13\:00, 14\:00–16\:00, and 16\:00–18\:00. Bruna works one of the last two shifts. Duccio works the shift immediately after Aurelio’s. Cinzia does not work the last shift. Duccio does not work from 14\:00 to 16\:00. Who works from 16\:00 to 18\:00?

- Cinzia
- Duccio
- Aurelio
- Bruna

## Explanation

Aurelio and Duccio work consecutive shifts, and Duccio does not work from 14\:00 to 16\:00, so the two cover either the first two shifts or the last two. In the latter case, Aurelio and Duccio would cover the last two shifts, leaving neither of them for Bruna. Therefore Aurelio works from 9\:00 to 11\:00 and Duccio from 11\:00 to 13\:00. Cinzia does not work the last shift, so she works from 14\:00 to 16\:00, and **Bruna** works from 16\:00 to 18\:00.
