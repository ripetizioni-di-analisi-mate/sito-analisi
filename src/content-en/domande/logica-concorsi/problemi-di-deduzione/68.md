Three employees, Lina, Nico, and Olga, each review a different document: a financial statement, an inventory, or the minutes of a meeting. One employee attends each day, on Monday, Tuesday, and Wednesday. The minutes are reviewed on Monday. Nico attends on Wednesday. Lina reviews neither the minutes nor the inventory. Which document does Nico review?

- meeting minutes
- Cannot be determined
- inventory
- financial statement

## Explanation

Lina reviews the financial statement. Nico cannot review the minutes because he attends on Wednesday and the minutes are reviewed on Monday. That leaves the inventory for Nico and the minutes for Olga.
