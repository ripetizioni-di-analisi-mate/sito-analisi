Ari, Bea, and Ciro each either always tell the truth or always lie. Ari says, “Bea is lying.” Bea says, “Ciro is lying.” Ciro says, “Ari is a truth-teller.” We also know that two people are truth-tellers. Who is lying?

- Only Bea
- Only Ciro
- Only Ari
- Ari and Ciro

## Explanation

Ari and Ciro are the same type because Ciro says Ari is a truth-teller. If they were both liars, there would be at most one truth-teller. Since there are two truth-tellers, Ari and Ciro tell the truth and Bea is the liar.
