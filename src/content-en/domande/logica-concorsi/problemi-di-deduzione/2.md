Ugo, Vera, Teo, and Zoe each order a different dessert at a pastry shop: tiramisu, a cannolo, a babà, or a sfogliatella. Vera orders the babà. Ugo does not order the sfogliatella. Zoe orders either the tiramisu or the cannolo. Who orders the sfogliatella?

- Ugo
- Zoe
- There is not enough information to tell
- Teo

## Explanation

Vera orders the babà, so the sfogliatella goes to Ugo, Teo, or Zoe. Ugo does not order it, and Zoe orders the tiramisu or the cannolo, so **Teo** orders the sfogliatella. The clues do not tell us which of Ugo and Zoe orders the tiramisu and which orders the cannolo, so there are two possible solutions. Teo orders the sfogliatella in both. The answer “There is not enough information to tell” confuses having more than one complete solution with being unable to answer the question.
