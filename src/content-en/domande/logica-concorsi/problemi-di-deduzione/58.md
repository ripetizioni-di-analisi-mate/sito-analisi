Five candidates are lined up with Mina first. Exactly two people stand between Nello and Piero. Sara is immediately after Rita, and Rita is immediately after Nello. Who is fifth?

- Sara
- Nello
- Piero
- Rita

## Explanation

With Mina first, only the second and fifth positions have two people between them. Nello must be second because Rita and Sara come after him. That leaves fifth place for Piero.
