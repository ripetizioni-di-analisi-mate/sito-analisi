Five people each make one site visit, one person per day from Monday through Friday. Ines goes on Wednesday. Leo goes the day immediately after Mara. Nora goes after Leo and before Piero. Who makes the site visit on Thursday?

- Piero
- Leo
- Mara
- Nora

## Explanation

Ines takes Wednesday. Mara and Leo need consecutive days with two later days left for Nora and Piero. They can only take Monday and Tuesday. Nora goes before Piero on the two remaining days, so she goes on Thursday.
