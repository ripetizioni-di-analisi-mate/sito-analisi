Adele, Beppe, and Clara each present a different project (park, school, or market) on a different day (Monday, Tuesday, or Wednesday). Adele presents the park project. The school project is presented on Tuesday. Beppe presents before Clara and does not present the market project. Who presents on Wednesday?

- Beppe
- Clara
- Cannot be determined
- Adele

## Explanation

Adele presents the park project and Beppe does not present the market project, so Beppe presents the school project on Tuesday. Clara goes after him, so she can only present on Wednesday. Adele presents on Monday.
