Exactly one of Nico, Omar, and Pia sent a notice. Nico says, “Omar didn’t send it.” Omar says, “Pia sent it.” Pia says, “Nico sent it.” Two statements are true, and we know it was not Nico. Who sent the notice?

- Omar
- Pia
- Cannot be determined
- Nico

## Explanation

Since it was not Nico, Pia’s statement is false. The other two must be true. Omar says it was Pia, consistent with Nico’s statement ruling out Omar.
