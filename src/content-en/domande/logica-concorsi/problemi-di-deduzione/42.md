Luana, Mattia, Nerea, and Oscar take the top four places in a photography contest, with no ties. Mattia finishes ahead of Nerea, though not necessarily immediately ahead of her. Oscar finishes immediately behind Luana. Which of the following statements cannot be true?

- Mattia finishes first
- Oscar finishes third
- Luana finishes third
- Nerea finishes third

## Explanation

Luana and Oscar occupy two consecutive places, so their positions are $1$ and $2$, $2$ and $3$, or $3$ and $4$. Mattia and Nerea take the two remaining places, with Mattia ahead. The possible rankings are (Luana, Oscar, Mattia, Nerea), (Mattia, Luana, Oscar, Nerea), and (Mattia, Nerea, Luana, Oscar). Nerea is third in none of them, so “Nerea finishes third” **cannot be true**. Mattia is first and Oscar is third in the second ranking, and Luana is third in the third ranking.
