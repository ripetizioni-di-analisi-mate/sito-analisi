Containers A, B, C, and D are each a different color: white, gray, black, or purple. A is purple. C is neither gray nor black. B is not black. What color is D?

- white
- black
- gray
- purple

## Explanation

Purple is already assigned to A. Of the remaining colors, C can only be white. That leaves gray and black for B and D, but B is not black. Therefore D is black.
