Three employees, Ivo, Mara, and Pia, each work a shift on a different day from Monday through Wednesday. Ivo cannot work on Monday, and Mara must work before Pia. How many complete weekly schedules are possible?

- $2$
- $3$
- $4$
- $1$

## Explanation

Ivo can work on Tuesday or Wednesday. In either case there is just one way to assign the two remaining days to Mara and Pia, with Mara on the earlier day. That gives two complete schedules.
