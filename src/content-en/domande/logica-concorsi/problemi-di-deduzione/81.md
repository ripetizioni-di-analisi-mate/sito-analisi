Dino, Emma, and Fabio each either always tell the truth or always lie. Dino says, “Exactly one of Emma and Fabio is a truth-teller.” Emma says, “Dino is a truth-teller.” Fabio says, “Emma is lying.” Who is lying?

- Dino and Emma
- Only Fabio
- Only Emma
- Only Dino

## Explanation

Fabio’s statement makes him the opposite type from Emma. So exactly one of Emma and Fabio really is a truth-teller, and Dino is telling the truth. Emma, who calls him a truth-teller, is also telling the truth. Fabio is the only liar.
