Six people sit at workstations in a row, one person per station. Eva sits at the far left and Luca at the far right. From left to right, Nino, Ida, and Tea occupy three consecutive stations. Rosa does not sit next to Eva. Who sits immediately to Luca’s left?

- Nino
- Tea
- Eva
- Rosa

## Explanation

With both ends taken, stations two through five remain. The Nino–Ida–Tea group must start at station two. Starting at station three would leave Rosa next to Eva. So Rosa sits fifth, immediately to Luca’s left.
