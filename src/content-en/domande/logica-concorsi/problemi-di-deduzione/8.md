At dinner, Ada, Bea, Ciro, and Dino order four different dishes (pasta, savory pie, salad, and pizza) and four different drinks (water, juice, wine, and beer), one dish and one drink per person. The person who eats the savory pie drinks juice. Ada drinks wine. Bea eats neither the pasta nor the savory pie. The person who eats pizza drinks beer. Dino eats pasta. Who drinks beer?

- Ciro
- Ada
- Bea
- Dino

## Explanation

Ada drinks wine, so she drinks neither juice nor beer. Taking the **contrapositive** of each of the two clues linking a dish to a drink, Ada eats neither the savory pie nor the pizza. Dino has the pasta, so Ada eats the salad. Bea eats neither the pasta nor the savory pie, and Ada has the salad, so Bea eats the pizza and drinks beer. The answer is **Bea**. That leaves the savory pie and juice for Ciro, and water for Dino.
