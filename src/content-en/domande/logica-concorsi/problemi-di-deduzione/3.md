Five offices occupy a five-story town hall, one on each floor, numbered $1$ through $5$ from bottom to top: the civil registry, the tax office, the document registration office, the public information office, and the archives. There are five clues. The tax office is one floor directly above the civil registry. The archives are on the first floor. The public information office is higher than the tax office. The document registration office is not on the fifth floor. The civil registry is not on the second floor. Which clue could you remove and still have exactly one possible arrangement?

- The document registration office is not on the fifth floor
- The archives are on the first floor
- The public information office is higher than the tax office
- The civil registry is not on the second floor

## Explanation

The public information office is above the tax office, and the tax office is directly above the civil registry, so the civil registry can be no higher than the third floor. The archives are on the first floor and the civil registry is not on the second, so the civil registry is on the third floor, the tax office on the fourth, and the public information office on the fifth. That leaves the second floor for the document registration office. This deduction never uses the clue about the document registration office, so that clue follows from the others and can be removed. Without the clue about the civil registry, another arrangement is possible: the civil registry on the second floor, the tax office on the third, the document registration office on the fourth, and the public information office on the fifth.
