An office accepts applications through an online portal, by mail, and at a service counter. Exactly two channels are active today. If the counter is open, the portal is active too. If mail is accepted, the portal is offline. Which channels are active?

- The portal and mail
- Only the portal
- The portal and the counter
- Mail and the counter

## Explanation

If mail were accepted, the portal would be offline. The counter would also have to be closed, because for it to open, the portal must be active. So mail is not accepted, and the other two channels are active.
