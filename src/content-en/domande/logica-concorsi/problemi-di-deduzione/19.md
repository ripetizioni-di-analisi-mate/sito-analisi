Everyone on an island is either a truth-teller who always tells the truth or a liar who always lies. Pietro says, “Rita told me she was a liar.” Rita says, “Pietro is a liar.” Which of the following statements is true?

- Pietro is a truth-teller and Rita is a liar
- Pietro is a liar and Rita is a truth-teller
- Pietro and Rita are both truth-tellers
- Pietro and Rita are both liars

## Explanation

One of the **recurring statements** is “I am a liar.” No islander can say it: a truth-teller would be saying something false, and a liar would be saying something true. So Rita never said she was a liar, and Pietro’s statement is false. Pietro is therefore a liar, and Rita’s statement “Pietro is a liar” is true. This makes Rita a truth-teller. The answer “Pietro is a truth-teller and Rita is a liar” takes Pietro’s word for what Rita said without checking whether she could ever have said it.
