Everyone on an island is either a truth-teller who always tells the truth or a liar who always lies. Iris says, “Leone is a liar.” Leone says, “At least two of the three of us are truth-tellers.” Mirko says, “Iris and Leone are different types.” Who tells the truth?

- Only Mirko
- Iris and Mirko
- Leone and Mirko
- All three

## Explanation

Iris says Leone is a liar. By one of the **recurring statements**, Iris and Leone are different types. Mirko’s statement is therefore true, making Mirko a truth-teller. Exactly one of Iris and Leone is a truth-teller, so there are exactly two truth-tellers in total and Leone’s statement is true. Thus Leone is a truth-teller and Iris is a liar. The truth-tellers are **Leone and Mirko**. The answer “Iris and Mirko” accepts Iris’s accusation without checking Leone’s statement.
