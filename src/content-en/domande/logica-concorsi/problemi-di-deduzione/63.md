Three candidates, Aldo, Bianca, and Cecilia, each choose a different time from 09\:00, 10\:00, and 11\:00. Aldo does not choose 09\:00. Bianca chooses an earlier time than Aldo. Cecilia does not choose 11\:00. Which time does Aldo choose?

- 11\:00
- 09\:00
- Cannot be determined
- 10\:00

## Explanation

If Aldo chose 10\:00, Bianca would have to choose 09\:00. That would leave 11\:00 for Cecilia, which is ruled out. So Aldo chooses 11\:00, leaving the two earlier times for Bianca and Cecilia.
