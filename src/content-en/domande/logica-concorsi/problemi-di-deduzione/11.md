Aurora, Bianca, Cesare, and Dante work for a company with two offices, one in Milan and one in Rome. Exactly two of them work in each office. Each holds a different position: director, secretary, technician, or accountant. The director works in Rome. Bianca works in Milan. Aurora and Cesare work in the same office. The technician works in Milan. Aurora is the accountant. Dante is not the technician. Which of the following statements is true?

- Cesare is the director
- Dante is the director
- Dante works in Rome
- Bianca is the secretary

## Explanation

Two people work in Milan, and Bianca is one of them. Aurora and Cesare work in the same office, so they must both work in Rome. Dante therefore works in Milan with Bianca. The director works in Rome, and Aurora is the accountant, so **Cesare** is the director. The technician works in Milan and is not Dante, so Bianca is the technician and Dante is the secretary. The answer “Dante is the director” overlooks the limit of two people per office.
