Six people, A, B, C, D, E, and F, sit in a circle. B sits immediately after A clockwise. D is across from A, and E is across from B. C does not sit next to A. Who sits immediately before D clockwise?

- C
- E
- F
- B

## Explanation

A, B, D, and E occupy the first, second, fourth, and fifth seats, respectively. The sixth seat is next to A and cannot go to C, so C takes the third seat. The third seat is immediately before D clockwise.
