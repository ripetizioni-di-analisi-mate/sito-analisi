Alba, Bruno, Cesare, Diana, Enea, and Flora run a race. They all finish, with no ties. Diana finishes immediately after Bruno. Cesare finishes four places behind Alba. Flora finishes after Diana. Alba is not first. Who finishes fifth?

- Diana
- Cesare
- Enea
- Flora

## Explanation

Alba’s and Cesare’s places differ by $4$, so they are $1$ and $5$ or $2$ and $6$. Since Alba is not first, she is second and Cesare is sixth. The remaining places are $1$, $3$, $4$, and $5$. Bruno and Diana take either $3$ and $4$ or $4$ and $5$. If Diana finished fifth, Flora could not finish after her, because Cesare is sixth. Therefore Bruno is third, Diana fourth, **Flora** fifth, and Enea first. The answer “Diana” comes from placing Bruno and Diana in positions $4$ and $5$ without checking the clue about Flora.
