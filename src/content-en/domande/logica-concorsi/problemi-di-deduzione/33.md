Achille, Bruna, Cecilia, and Davide each make a statement. Exactly one of them says something false, and the other three tell the truth. Achille says, “Bruna is telling the truth.” Bruna says, “Cecilia is telling the truth.” Cecilia says, “Davide is lying.” Davide says, “Achille is lying.” Who is lying?

- Achille
- Cecilia
- Bruna
- Davide

## Explanation

If Achille were lying, his statement “Bruna is telling the truth” would be false, so Bruna would be lying as well. That would give more than one false statement. Similarly, if Bruna were lying, Cecilia would also be lying. If Cecilia were lying, Bruna’s statement “Cecilia is telling the truth” would be false, making Bruna a second person who is lying. Therefore **Davide** is lying. This makes the statements by Achille, Bruna, and Cecilia true, and Davide’s statement “Achille is lying” false, as required. The answer “Achille” takes Davide’s accusation as true.
