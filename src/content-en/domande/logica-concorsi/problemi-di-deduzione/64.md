In a relay race, three teams, Yellow, Blue, and Green, finish with different times of $5$, $6$, and $7$ minutes. Blue is faster than Yellow, Green is slower than Blue, and Yellow does not have the longest time. What is Green’s time?

- Cannot be determined
- $6$
- $7$
- $5$

## Explanation

Blue is faster than both other teams, so its time is $5$ minutes. Yellow does not have the longest time, so its time is $6$ minutes. That leaves $7$ minutes for Green.
