Arturo, Bice, Carmen, Dora, and Elia are in line to enter a museum. Bice is ahead of Arturo, though not necessarily immediately ahead of him. Carmen is immediately behind Dora. Elia is first or last in line. Arturo is not last. Elia is not next to Carmen. Who cannot be third in line?

- Dora
- Arturo
- Bice
- Carmen

## Explanation

Try placing the person named in each choice third, following the method of **testing the choices one at a time**. The orders (Elia, Bice, Arturo, Dora, Carmen), (Bice, Dora, Carmen, Arturo, Elia), and (Dora, Carmen, Bice, Arturo, Elia) satisfy all the clues, with Arturo, Carmen, and Bice in third place, respectively. If Dora were third, Carmen would be fourth. Since Elia is not next to Carmen, Elia would have to be first. That would leave the second and fifth positions for Bice and Arturo, with Arturo fifth because he comes after Bice. This contradicts the clue “Arturo is not last.” So **Dora** cannot be third.
