Pia, Remo, Sole, and Tito have appointments in four different departments of an agency (human resources, legal, purchasing, and archives) at 9\:00, 9\:15, 9\:30, and 9\:45, one appointment per time slot. The archives appointment is half an hour after Pia’s. Remo is going to purchasing. Sole’s appointment is after that of the person going to legal. The human resources appointment is at 9\:00. Tito’s appointment is not at 9\:00. What time is Sole’s appointment, and in which department?

- At 9\:45, in archives
- At 9\:30, in archives
- At 9\:30, in legal
- At 9\:45, in purchasing

## Explanation

The archives appointment is half an hour after Pia’s, so Pia’s appointment is at 9\:00 or 9\:15. Suppose Pia’s appointment is at 9\:15. Then Sole would have to take the 9\:00 appointment in human resources, because Tito is not scheduled at 9\:00 and Remo is going to purchasing. That contradicts the clue that Sole comes after the person going to legal. Therefore Pia is scheduled for 9\:00 in human resources, and the archives appointment is at 9\:30. Sole cannot be going to legal because her appointment is later than the legal appointment. That puts Tito in legal and **Sole in archives at 9\:30**. Tito comes before Sole, so Tito is scheduled for 9\:15 and Remo for 9\:45.
