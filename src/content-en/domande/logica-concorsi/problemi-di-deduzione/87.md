A work trip can involve travel by bus, car, and train. Exactly two modes of transportation are used. If the car is used, the train is not used. If the train is used, the bus is used as well. What can you conclude with certainty?

- The train is used.
- The bus is used.
- The bus is the only mode of transportation used.
- The car is used.

## Explanation

The car and the train cannot be used together. To use exactly two modes of transportation, the bus must therefore be paired with either the car or the train. Both combinations include the bus.
