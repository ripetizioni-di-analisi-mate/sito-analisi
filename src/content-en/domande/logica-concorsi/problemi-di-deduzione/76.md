Six people sit around a round table. Bea is on Ada's left. Ada swaps seats with the person directly opposite Bea. How many people, including Ada and Bea, now have at least one different neighbour, ignoring left and right?

- $4$
- $3$
- $5$
- $6$

## Explanation

Number the seats from 0 to 5 with Bea in 0 and Ada in 1. The occupants of 1 and 3 swap. Seat 2 keeps the same two neighbours in reverse order, and seat 5 keeps both neighbours. The occupants of seats 0, 1, 3 and 4 have different neighbours.
