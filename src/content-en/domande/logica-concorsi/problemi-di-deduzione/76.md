Five people, A, B, C, D, and E, sit in a circle. B and C occupy the two seats next to A. D sits immediately after C clockwise. Who sits immediately before B clockwise?

- E
- A
- D
- C

## Explanation

If C occupied the seat immediately before A clockwise, the seat after C would be A's, whereas it must be D's. Hence C sits immediately after A and B occupies the seat immediately before A. Going clockwise from A, the order is therefore A, C, D, E, B, because E takes the only remaining seat. The seat before B is thus occupied by E.
