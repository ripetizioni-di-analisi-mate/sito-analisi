Five people, A, B, C, D, and E, sit in a circle. B and C occupy the two seats next to A. D sits immediately after C clockwise. Who sits immediately before B clockwise?

- E
- A
- D
- C

## Explanation

C cannot occupy the seat immediately before A clockwise because the seat after C must go to D. Starting with A, the clockwise order is therefore A, C, D, E, B. E occupies the seat before B.
