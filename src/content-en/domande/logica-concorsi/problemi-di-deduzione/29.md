Anna, Bruno, and Carla staff an office information desk on Monday, Tuesday, and Wednesday. Each day has a morning shift and an afternoon shift, with one person per shift. Each person works two shifts, always on different days. Anna works only mornings. Bruno works Wednesday afternoon. Carla does not work on Tuesday. Which of the following statements is true?

- Bruno works Monday afternoon
- Anna works Wednesday morning
- Carla works Monday afternoon
- Bruno works Tuesday morning

## Explanation

Carla does not work on Tuesday, so Anna and Bruno take Tuesday’s two shifts. Anna only works mornings, so Anna takes Tuesday morning and Bruno takes Tuesday afternoon. Bruno now has both of his shifts, Tuesday afternoon and Wednesday afternoon. Carla cannot work twice on the same day, so she works one shift on Monday and one on Wednesday. Only the morning is open on Wednesday, so Carla works Wednesday morning, Anna works Monday morning, and **Carla works Monday afternoon**.
