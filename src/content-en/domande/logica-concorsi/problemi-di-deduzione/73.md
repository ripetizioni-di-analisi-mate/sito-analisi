An office must carry out four checks, one per day from Monday through Thursday. X must come after Z, Y before Z, and W after X. What is the latest day on which Z can take place?

- Monday
- Tuesday
- Thursday
- Wednesday

## Explanation

The constraints require the order Y, Z, X, W. The four checks fill all four available days, so Z takes place on the second day, Tuesday.
