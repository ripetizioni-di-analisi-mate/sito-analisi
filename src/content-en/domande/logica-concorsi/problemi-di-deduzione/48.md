Five folders, A, B, C, D, and E, are arranged from left to right. C is at the far right. A and D are on opposite sides of B, each immediately next to it. A is to the left of D. If E is at the far left, which folder is in the middle?

- A
- E
- B
- D

## Explanation

E and C occupy the two ends. A, B, and D occupy the middle three places, with B between the other two. The order is E, A, B, D, C, with B in the middle.
