Everyone on an island is either a truth-teller who always tells the truth or a liar who always lies. Lia says, “Marta is a truth-teller.” Marta says, “Lia and I are different types.” Which of the following statements is true?

- Lia and Marta are both liars
- Lia and Marta are both truth-tellers
- Lia is a truth-teller and Marta is a liar
- Lia is a liar and Marta is a truth-teller

## Explanation

Lia says Marta is a truth-teller. By one of the **recurring statements**, Lia and Marta must be the same type. Marta’s statement is therefore false, so Marta is a liar. Since Lia is the same type as Marta, Lia is a liar too. They are **both liars**. The answer “Lia and Marta are both truth-tellers” takes Lia’s statement as true without checking Marta’s.
