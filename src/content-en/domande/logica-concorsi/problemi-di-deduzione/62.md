Four packages, A, B, C, and D, have different weights: $1$, $2$, $3$, and $4$ kg. A weighs twice as much as B. C is heavier than A and D is lighter than C. How much does D weigh?

- $2$
- $1$
- $4$
- $3$

## Explanation

A cannot weigh $4$ kg because C is heavier than A. The only remaining way for A to weigh twice as much as B gives $2$ kg for A and $1$ kg for B. Of the two remaining weights, $3$ and $4$ kg, D has the lower one, so it weighs $3$ kg.
