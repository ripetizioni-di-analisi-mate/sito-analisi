Three nurses, Anna, Bruno, and Carla, cover the morning, afternoon, and night shifts over three days (Monday, Tuesday, and Wednesday). Each day they work different shifts. Over the three days each nurse works one morning, one afternoon, and one night. Anna works Monday night. Bruno works Tuesday morning. Which of the following statements is true?

- Carla works Wednesday night
- Carla works Monday afternoon
- Carla works Tuesday afternoon
- Bruno works Wednesday night

## Explanation

Bruno works Tuesday morning, and Anna cannot work the night shift again because she worked it on Monday. So on Tuesday, Anna works the afternoon and Carla works the night. On Monday, Bruno cannot work the morning, which he works on Tuesday, or the night, which Anna works. He therefore works Monday afternoon, and Carla works Monday morning. On Wednesday each nurse takes the shift they still need: Anna works the morning, Carla the afternoon, and **Bruno the night**. The answer “Carla works Wednesday night” overlooks the fact that Carla already worked Tuesday night.
