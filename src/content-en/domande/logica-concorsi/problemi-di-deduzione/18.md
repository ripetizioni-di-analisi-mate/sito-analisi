Rocco, Sabina, Tea, and Ugo are waiting at a clinic. They are called in one at a time. Ugo is called first. Sabina is called after Rocco, though not necessarily immediately after him. Tea is called immediately after Sabina. Who is called third?

- Rocco
- Tea
- Sabina
- There is not enough information to tell

## Explanation

Ugo is first, so Rocco, Sabina, and Tea occupy positions $2$, $3$, and $4$. Tea comes immediately after Sabina, so Sabina and Tea occupy positions $2$ and $3$ or $3$ and $4$. In the first case, Rocco would be fourth, and Sabina would not be called after him. So Rocco is second, **Sabina** third, and Tea fourth. The clue about Sabina and Rocco does not require consecutive positions, yet only one order is possible. This rules out “There is not enough information to tell.”
