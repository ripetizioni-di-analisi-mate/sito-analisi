Anna, Bruno, Carla, Dario, and Elena sit in five theater seats in a row, numbered $1$ through $5$ from the left. Carla is immediately to Anna’s right. Dario is to Elena’s left, though not necessarily next to her. Bruno is not at either end of the row. Elena is not next to Carla. Which seating order, from left to right, is consistent with the clues?

- Dario, Carla, Anna, Bruno, Elena
- Anna, Carla, Dario, Elena, Bruno
- Dario, Anna, Carla, Bruno, Elena
- Dario, Bruno, Anna, Carla, Elena

## Explanation

Check the clues against each arrangement, following the method of **testing the choices one at a time**. In the order Dario, Carla, Anna, Bruno, Elena, Carla is on Anna’s left. In the order Anna, Carla, Dario, Elena, Bruno, Bruno is at the right end of the row. In the order Dario, Bruno, Anna, Carla, Elena, Elena is next to Carla. The arrangement **Dario, Anna, Carla, Bruno, Elena** satisfies all four clues.
