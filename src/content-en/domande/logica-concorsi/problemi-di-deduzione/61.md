Ada, Bruno, Cora, and Diego work on four different floors, from the first to the fourth. Cora works on the fourth floor. Diego works on a higher floor than Bruno. Ada works on neither the first nor the third floor. On which floor does Bruno work?

- second
- fourth
- first
- third

## Explanation

Cora works on the fourth floor, leaving only the second floor for Ada. The first and third floors go to Bruno and Diego. Since Diego works on the higher floor, Bruno works on the first.
