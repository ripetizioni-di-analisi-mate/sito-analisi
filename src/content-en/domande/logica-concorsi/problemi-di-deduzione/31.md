Everyone in a village is either a truth-teller who always tells the truth or a liar who always lies. Carlo says, “If I am a truth-teller, then Dora is a truth-teller.” Dora says nothing. Which of the following statements is true?

- Carlo is a truth-teller and Dora is a liar
- Carlo is a liar and Dora is a truth-teller
- Carlo and Dora are both liars
- Carlo and Dora are both truth-tellers

## Explanation

A conditional is false only when its antecedent is true and its consequent is false. If Carlo were a liar, the antecedent “I am a truth-teller” would be false. The conditional would be true, so a liar would be telling the truth, a contradiction. By the **method of testing assumptions for contradictions**, Carlo is therefore a truth-teller and his statement is true. Since both the conditional and its antecedent are true, its consequent must also be true. Dora is a truth-teller, so they are **both truth-tellers**. The answer “Carlo is a truth-teller and Dora is a liar” assumes that Dora’s silence leaves her type undetermined.
