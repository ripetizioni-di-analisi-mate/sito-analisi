Exactly one of four people, Ettore, Fausta, Giorgio, and Ines, left the meeting room computer on. Ettore says, “It was Ines.” Fausta says, “It was Ettore or Ines.” Giorgio says, “It was Ettore.” Ines says, “It wasn’t Ettore.” Exactly one of the four statements is true. Who told the truth?

- Fausta
- Giorgio
- Ettore
- Ines

## Explanation

Try each of the four possible culprits and count the true statements. If it was Ettore, Fausta’s and Giorgio’s statements are true. If it was Ines, Ettore’s, Fausta’s, and Ines’s statements are true. Both cases are ruled out. If it was Fausta or Giorgio, only Ines’s statement is true. The clues leave two possible culprits, and in both cases the person who told the truth is **Ines**.
