Ari, Bea, Ciro, and Dora take an oral exam one at a time. Ari goes before Dora, Bea immediately after Ciro, and Dora is not last. Which order fits the clues?

- Ari → Ciro → Dora → Bea
- Ari → Dora → Ciro → Bea
- Dora → Ari → Ciro → Bea
- Ciro → Bea → Ari → Dora

## Explanation

Ciro and Bea take two consecutive slots. If they took the first two, Dora would be last. If they took the second and third, Dora would still be last because Ari goes before her. So they must take the last two slots, after Ari and Dora.
