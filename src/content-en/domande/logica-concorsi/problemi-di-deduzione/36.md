Exactly one of four people, Elio, Fabia, Gemma, and Ivo, left the office copier jammed. Elio says, “It wasn’t Fabia.” Fabia says, “It wasn’t Gemma.” Gemma says, “It was Elio.” Ivo says, “It was Fabia.” Exactly one of the four statements is false. Who left the copier jammed?

- Elio
- Fabia
- Gemma
- Ivo

## Explanation

Elio’s and Ivo’s statements are **contradictory**: one says it was Fabia, and the other says it was not. One of them must therefore be the only false statement. So Fabia’s and Gemma’s statements are true, and Gemma’s statement tells us it was **Elio**. As a check, if it was Elio, only Ivo’s statement is false. The answer “Fabia” accepts Ivo’s accusation.
