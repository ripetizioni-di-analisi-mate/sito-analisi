Four employees pick up a key in four separate time slots. Omar goes immediately after Lia. Rita is neither first nor last. If Fabio goes before Lia, who picks up the key second?

- Omar
- Fabio
- Rita
- Lia

## Explanation

Lia and Omar cannot take the first two slots because Fabio has to go before them. If they took the second and third slots, Fabio would be first and Rita last. That leaves the third and fourth slots for Lia and Omar, so Rita is second.
