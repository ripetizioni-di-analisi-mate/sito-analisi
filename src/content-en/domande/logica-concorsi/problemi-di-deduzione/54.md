Five runners, A, B, C, D, and E, finish a race with no ties. A finishes before B, B before C, and D before E. D finishes second. How many finishing orders fit these conditions?

- $3$
- $6$
- $4$
- $2$

## Explanation

With D second, A must be first. E must finish after D and can take third, fourth, or fifth place. For each choice, B and C fill the two open places in that order, giving three possible finishing orders.
