Alda, Bina, Ciro, and Dino sit around a round table with four seats. Alda sits across from Ciro. Bina is in the next seat clockwise from Alda. Who sits across from Bina?

- Ciro
- Bina
- Alda
- Dino

## Explanation

Fix Alda’s seat. Moving clockwise, the other three people are Bina, Ciro, and Dino. At a four-seat table, opposite seats are two places apart. Dino is two places from Bina.
