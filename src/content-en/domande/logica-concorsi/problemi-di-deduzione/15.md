Aldo, Bea, Carlo, Dina, and Ezio sit in a row of five adjacent movie theater seats, numbered $1$ through $5$ from the left. Carlo sits in seat $1$. Bea sits between Aldo and Carlo. Bea is not next to Carlo. Dina and Ezio sit next to each other. Dina is not next to Carlo. Who sits in seat $3$?

- Ezio
- Bea
- Aldo
- Dina

## Explanation

Bea sits between Carlo, in seat $1$, and Aldo, so Aldo sits farther to the right than Bea. Bea is not next to Carlo, so she is in seat $3$ or $4$. If Bea were in seat $3$, Aldo would be in $4$ or $5$, leaving Dina and Ezio seat $2$ and another seat that is not next to it. Therefore Bea is in seat $4$, Aldo in seat $5$, and Dina and Ezio occupy seats $2$ and $3$. Dina is not next to Carlo, so seat $3$ belongs to **Dina**. The answer “Ezio” overlooks the last clue.
