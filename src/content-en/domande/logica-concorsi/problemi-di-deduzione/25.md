Five neighborhood pharmacies, Centrale, Porto, Duomo, Stazione, and Moderna, take turns covering the night shift, one per night from Monday through Friday of the same week. Porto is on duty the night after Duomo. Centrale is on duty on Tuesday. Stazione is on duty on neither Monday nor Friday. Which pharmacy is on duty on Monday?

- Stazione
- Moderna
- Duomo
- Porto

## Explanation

Centrale takes Tuesday, so Duomo and Porto, which cover two consecutive nights, must take Wednesday and Thursday or Thursday and Friday. Stazione cannot be on duty Monday, Tuesday, or Friday, so it must take Wednesday or Thursday. If Duomo and Porto took Wednesday and Thursday, no night would be available for Stazione. Therefore Duomo is on duty Thursday, Porto on Friday, and Stazione on Wednesday, leaving Monday for **Moderna**.
