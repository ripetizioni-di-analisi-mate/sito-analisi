Alessia, Bruno, Ciro, Dalia, and Ennio sit facing the center of a round table in five equally spaced seats. Left and right are from the seated person’s perspective. Bruno is on Alessia’s right. Ciro is not next to Bruno. Dalia is on Ciro’s left. Ennio is not next to Bruno. Who sits on Ciro’s right?

- Dalia
- Ennio
- Alessia
- Bruno

## Explanation

Put Alessia in seat $0$ and number the seats counterclockwise, so the seat to the right of seat $k$ is $k + 1$. After seat $4$ the count returns to seat $0$. Bruno is in seat $1$. The seats next to him are $0$ and $2$. Neither Ciro nor Ennio sits next to Bruno, so they occupy seats $3$ and $4$, and Dalia is in seat $2$. Dalia is on Ciro’s left, so Ciro is in seat $3$ and Ennio in seat $4$. Thus **Ennio** sits on Ciro’s right. The answer “Dalia” identifies the person on his left.
