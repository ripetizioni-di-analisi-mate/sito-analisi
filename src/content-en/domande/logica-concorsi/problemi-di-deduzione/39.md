Exactly one of four people, Aurelio, Bianca, Cesira, and Dante, took a coworker’s umbrella by mistake. Aurelio says, “It was Cesira or Dante.” Bianca says, “It was Cesira.” Cesira says, “It was Dante.” Dante says, “Bianca is lying.” Exactly two of the four statements are true. Who took the umbrella?

- Dante
- Cesira
- Aurelio
- Bianca

## Explanation

Dante says Bianca’s statement is false, so Bianca’s and Dante’s statements are **contradictory**, and exactly one of them is true. Since there are two true statements in all, exactly one of Aurelio’s and Cesira’s statements must be true. If Cesira’s statement were true, Dante would be responsible, making Aurelio’s statement true too. So Cesira’s statement is false and Aurelio’s is true. It was therefore Cesira or Dante, and it was not Dante, so it was **Cesira**. The answer “Dante” takes Cesira’s statement as true.
