Aldo, Bice, Ciro, and Dora are standing one behind the other in line at a counter. All we know is that Aldo is first and Bice is not second. How many different orders are consistent with this information?

- $6$
- $4$
- $2$
- $24$

## Explanation

Since Aldo is first, Bice, Ciro, and Dora occupy positions $2$, $3$, and $4$. Bice is not second, so she is either third or fourth. For each of Bice’s two possible positions, Ciro and Dora can fill the remaining two positions in $2$ ways. This gives $2 \cdot 2 = 4$ possible orders. The number $6$ counts every way to arrange Bice, Ciro, and Dora behind Aldo, ignoring the clue about Bice.
