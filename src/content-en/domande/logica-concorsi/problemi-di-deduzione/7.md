Aldo, Beppe, and Cosimo live in houses of three different colors (red, yellow, and green) and each have a different pet (a dog, a cat, or a hamster). The dog’s owner lives in the green house. Aldo lives in the yellow house. Cosimo does not live in the green house. Aldo does not have the hamster. Who has the hamster, and in which house does he live?

- Beppe, in the green house
- Aldo, in the yellow house
- Beppe, in the red house
- Cosimo, in the red house

## Explanation

Aldo lives in the yellow house and Cosimo does not live in the green one, so Cosimo lives in the red house and Beppe lives in the green one. The dog’s owner lives in the green house, so Beppe has the dog. Aldo has neither the hamster nor the dog, so he has the cat. The hamster therefore belongs to **Cosimo**, who lives in the red house. The answer “Aldo, in the yellow house” overlooks the last clue.
