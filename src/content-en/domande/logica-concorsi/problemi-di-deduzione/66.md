Celia, Dario, and Elisa each take a different course (language, math, or writing) at a different campus (North, South, or East). Dario attends the North campus. Writing is offered at the East campus, and Celia takes language. Which pairing is certain?

- Elisa takes language
- Dario takes math
- Celia attends the North campus
- Celia attends the East campus

## Explanation

Dario cannot take writing because that course is at the East campus and he is at the North campus. Celia takes language. Elisa must therefore take writing, leaving math for Dario.
