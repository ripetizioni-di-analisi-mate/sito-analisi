Sara, Tito, Ugo, Vera, Walter, and Zeno sit facing the center of a round table in six equally spaced seats. Left and right are from the seated person’s perspective. Sara sits between Tito and Ugo, next to both. Vera sits across from Sara. Walter is on Vera’s right. Zeno is not next to Ugo. Who sits across from Zeno?

- Ugo
- Walter
- Tito
- Sara

## Explanation

Put Sara in seat $0$ and number the seats counterclockwise, so the seat to the right of seat $k$ is $k + 1$. Vera is across from Sara in seat $3$, and Walter is on Vera’s right in seat $4$. Tito and Ugo are next to Sara, so they occupy seats $1$ and $5$, leaving seat $2$ for Zeno. Seat $1$ is next to seat $2$, so Ugo must be in seat $5$ and Tito in seat $1$. Across from seat $2$ is seat $2 + 3 = 5$, occupied by **Ugo**.
