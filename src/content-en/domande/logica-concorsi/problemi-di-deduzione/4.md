Luca, Mauro, Nino, and Omar work for the same company and each take a different training course: English, computing, accounting, or safety. Luca takes neither English nor safety. Omar does not take English, safety, or accounting. Mauro does not take English. Who takes English?

- Mauro
- Luca
- Nino
- Omar

## Explanation

Three cells in Omar’s row have been ruled out, so by the **row and column rule**, Omar takes computing. Luca takes neither English nor safety, and Omar already takes computing, so Luca takes accounting. In the English column we can now rule out Luca, Omar, and Mauro, so **Nino** takes English. That leaves safety for Mauro.
