Five boxes, P, Q, R, S, and T, are in a row. R is at one end and P is immediately to its right. T is immediately to the left of S, and neither of those two boxes is at the far right. Which box is at the far right?

- T
- Q
- P
- S

## Explanation

R must be at the left end, since otherwise P could not be to its right. P is therefore second. Neither T nor S can go last, and R is already first, so Q must be at the right end.
