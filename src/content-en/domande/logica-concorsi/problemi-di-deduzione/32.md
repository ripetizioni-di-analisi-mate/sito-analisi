In a role-playing game, Elisa, Franco, and Giulio are each secretly assigned the role of either a truth-teller who always tells the truth or a liar who always lies. Elisa says, “All three of us are liars.” Franco says, “Elisa and I are different types.” Giulio says, “Franco and I are different types.” Who is a liar?

- Only Elisa
- Elisa and Franco
- All three
- Only Franco

## Explanation

If Elisa were a truth-teller, her statement would be true and she would be a liar, a contradiction. So Elisa is a liar, and at least one of the three is a truth-teller. Giulio says he and Franco are different types. If Giulio is a truth-teller, Franco is the other type, a liar. If Giulio is a liar, Franco is the same type as Giulio, again a liar. Since Elisa and Franco are liars and at least one person is a truth-teller, Giulio is a truth-teller. The liars are **Elisa and Franco**. The answer “Only Elisa” takes Franco’s statement as true.
