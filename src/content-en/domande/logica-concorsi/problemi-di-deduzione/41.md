Lorenzo, Marisa, Nora, and Ottone each take one week of vacation during the first four weeks of August, one person per week. Nora takes vacation at least two weeks after Marisa. Ottone does not take the first week. Lorenzo takes vacation before Ottone, though not necessarily the week immediately before. Which of the following statements must be true?

- Nora takes vacation in the fourth week
- Ottone takes vacation after Marisa
- Lorenzo takes vacation in the second week
- Marisa takes vacation in the first week

## Explanation

Nora takes vacation at least two weeks after Marisa, so Marisa takes the first or second week. If Ottone took the second week, Lorenzo, who goes before him, would take the first, leaving neither of the first two weeks for Marisa. So Ottone takes the third or fourth week. Marisa therefore goes before Ottone in every solution, and “Ottone takes vacation after Marisa” **must be true**. In order by week, the possible schedules are (Lorenzo, Marisa, Ottone, Nora), (Marisa, Lorenzo, Nora, Ottone), and (Marisa, Lorenzo, Ottone, Nora). Each of the other three statements is false in at least one of these schedules.
