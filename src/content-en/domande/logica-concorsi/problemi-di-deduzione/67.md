Marco, Nives, and Omar each prepare a different report (purchasing, transportation, or personnel) in a different format (paper, spreadsheet, or online form). The purchasing report is on paper. Nives uses the online form. Marco does not prepare the purchasing report. Omar does not prepare the personnel report. Which pairing is certain?

- Marco uses the spreadsheet
- Omar uses the online form
- Nives prepares the purchasing report
- Marco uses paper

## Explanation

The purchasing report is on paper and does not belong to Marco. Nives uses the online form, so Omar must prepare the purchasing report on paper. That leaves the spreadsheet for Marco, since the other two formats are already taken.
