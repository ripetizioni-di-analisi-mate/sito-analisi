Four reports, A, B, C, and D, are reviewed one at a time. A comes before B and C before D, with no other restrictions. How many orders are possible?

- $8$
- $12$
- $6$
- $4$

## Explanation

Once the two places for A and B are chosen, their order is fixed. C and D can also fill the two remaining places in just one order. The number of ways to choose two distinct places out of four is $4 \cdot 3 : 2 = 6$.
