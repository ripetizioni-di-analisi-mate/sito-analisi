Everyone on an island is either a truth-teller who always tells the truth or a liar who always lies. Alba says, “Exactly two of the four of us are liars.” Bruno says, “Carla is a liar.” Carla says, “Dario is a truth-teller.” Dario says, “Alba and I are the same type.” Who are the liars?

- Only Bruno
- Carla and Dario
- Bruno and Dario
- Alba and Carla

## Explanation

Dario says he and Alba are the same type. By one of the **recurring statements**, Alba must be a truth-teller, so there are exactly two liars. Bruno says Carla is a liar, so Bruno and Carla are different types. Carla says Dario is a truth-teller, so Carla and Dario are the same type. If Carla and Dario were truth-tellers, Bruno would be the only liar and Alba’s statement would be false. So **Carla and Dario** are the liars, and Alba and Bruno are truth-tellers. The answer “Only Bruno” corresponds to the rejected case, which contradicts Alba’s statement.
