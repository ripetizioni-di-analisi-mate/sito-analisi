Anna, Bruno, Carla, Dario, and Elena are in line at a service counter. Bruno is immediately ahead of Carla. Dario is ahead of Anna, who is immediately ahead of Elena. Dario is third. Who is first?

- Carla
- Anna
- Dario
- Bruno

## Explanation

Dario is third and comes before Anna and Elena, who stand together in that order. The two places after Dario must therefore go to Anna and Elena. Bruno and Carla occupy the first two places, with Bruno ahead of Carla.
