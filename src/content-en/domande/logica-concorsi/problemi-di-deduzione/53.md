Five cases, A, B, C, D, and E, are processed one at a time. C comes before B, D is processed immediately after A, and exactly one case separates E and C. Which order is possible?

- C → A → E → D → B
- C → B → E → A → D
- E → C → A → D → B
- A → D → B → C → E

## Explanation

In the order C, B, E, A, D, C comes before B, D comes immediately after A, and only B separates C and E. In the other orders, C and E are adjacent, B comes before C, or D does not come immediately after A.
