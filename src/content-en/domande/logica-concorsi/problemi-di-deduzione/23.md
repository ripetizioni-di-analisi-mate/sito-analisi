Aldo, Bruno, Carlo, Dario, Ezio, Fabio, Gino, and Ivo sit facing the center of a round table in eight equally spaced seats. Left and right are from the seated person’s perspective. Bruno is across from Aldo. Ivo is on Aldo’s right. Carlo is on Ivo’s right. Dario is across from Carlo. Ezio is on Bruno’s right. Fabio is next to Dario. Who sits on Aldo’s left?

- Ivo
- Gino
- Fabio
- Dario

## Explanation

Put Aldo in seat $0$ and number the seats counterclockwise, so the seat to the right of seat $k$ is $k + 1$. With eight seats, moving $4$ seats around the table takes you to the person directly across. Bruno is in seat $4$, Ivo in seat $1$, Carlo in seat $2$, Dario in seat $2 + 4 = 6$, and Ezio in seat $5$. Seats $3$ and $7$ remain. Fabio is next to Dario, so Fabio is in seat $7$ and Gino in seat $3$. The seat on Aldo’s left is $7$, so **Fabio** sits on Aldo’s left. The answer “Ivo” identifies the person on Aldo’s right.
