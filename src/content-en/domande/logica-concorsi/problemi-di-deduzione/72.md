Four tests, A, B, C, and D, take place one per day from Monday through Thursday. A takes place before C. B takes place the day after C. If D is on Thursday, which test takes place on Tuesday?

- D
- A
- B
- C

## Explanation

With D on Thursday, the first three days are available for A, B, and C. A comes before C and B immediately follows C, so the order for the first three days is A, C, B. C takes place on Tuesday.
