Gino, Ida, Lino, Mara, Nico, and Olga are in line at a supermarket checkout. Mara is somewhere behind Nico, though not necessarily immediately behind him. Olga is immediately behind Mara. Ida is third. Exactly two people stand between Lino and Gino, with Lino ahead of Gino. Who is fourth in line?

- Gino
- Mara
- Nico
- Olga

## Explanation

There are two people between Lino and Gino, so their positions differ by $3$. With Ida in third place, their positions must be $1$ and $4$ or $2$ and $5$. In the latter case, positions $1$, $4$, and $6$ would remain, with no two consecutive positions for Mara and Olga. So Lino is first and **Gino** fourth. Positions $2$, $5$, and $6$ go to Nico, Mara, and Olga in that order, because Olga is immediately behind Mara and Mara is behind Nico.
