Alba and Berto each either always tell the truth or always lie. Alba says, “Berto is lying.” Berto says, “We are both truth-tellers.” Which of them tells the truth?

- Both
- Neither of them
- Only Berto
- Only Alba

## Explanation

If Berto were telling the truth, both would be truth-tellers. But Alba’s statement that Berto is lying would contradict that assumption. So Berto is lying, and Alba is telling the truth when she says he is lying.
