Gioia, Luca, Mina, Neri, and Omar present a project in five consecutive time slots. Exactly two people present before Mina. Luca presents after Neri and before Omar. Gioia presents last. Who presents fourth?

- Omar
- Luca
- Neri
- Gioia

## Explanation

Mina takes the third slot and Gioia the fifth. Neri, Luca, and Omar take the three open slots in that order. The open slots are first, second, and fourth, so Omar presents fourth.
