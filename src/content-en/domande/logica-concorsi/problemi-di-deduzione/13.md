Four potted plants sit on a windowsill in positions numbered $1$ through $4$ from the left: geranium, basil, rosemary, and mint. The basil is immediately to the right of the geranium. The mint is at one end of the row. The rosemary is not at either end. The geranium is not in position $1$. Which plant is in position $4$?

- The geranium
- The mint
- The basil
- The rosemary

## Explanation

The geranium is not in position $1$ and the basil is immediately to its right, so the two occupy positions $2$ and $3$ or $3$ and $4$. In the first case, positions $1$ and $4$ would go to the mint and rosemary, but the rosemary cannot be at either end. Therefore the geranium is in position $3$ and the **basil** in position $4$. The mint, which is at one end, is in position $1$, and the rosemary is in position $2$. The answer “The geranium” comes from putting the basil to the left of the geranium.
