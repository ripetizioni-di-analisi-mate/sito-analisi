Andrea, Bianca, Carlo, Dario, and Elena take an oral exam one after another. Dario goes immediately after Bianca. Elena is third. Carlo goes before Andrea. Andrea is not last. Who goes second?

- Carlo
- Bianca
- Dario
- Andrea

## Explanation

Bianca and Dario occupy two consecutive positions. With Elena in third place, their positions must be $1$ and $2$ or $4$ and $5$. In the first case, Carlo and Andrea would be fourth and fifth, with Andrea fifth because he comes after Carlo. That contradicts the last clue. Therefore Bianca is fourth and Dario fifth. Carlo comes before Andrea, so Carlo is first and **Andrea** second.
