Elio, Fede, and Gaia work partly from home and take turns covering the office Monday through Friday. Exactly one of them goes into the office each day. Elio goes in on just one day. Fede and Gaia each go in on two days, and no one goes in two days in a row. Elio goes in on Tuesday. Fede does not go in on Wednesday. Which days does Gaia go in?

- Wednesday and Friday
- Monday and Thursday
- Monday and Wednesday
- Thursday and Friday

## Explanation

Elio goes into the office on Tuesday, so Fede and Gaia split Monday, Wednesday, Thursday, and Friday. Fede does not go in on Wednesday, so Gaia does. No one goes in two days in a row, so Fede goes in on Thursday and Gaia again on Friday. That leaves Monday for Fede. Gaia goes in on **Wednesday and Friday**. The answer “Monday and Thursday” fits one of the schedules possible without the clue about Fede, in which Fede goes in on Wednesday.
