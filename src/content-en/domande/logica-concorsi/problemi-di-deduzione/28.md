A technician schedules visits to five clients, Ottavio, Paolo, Quirino, Rino, and Sandro, one per day from Monday through Friday of the same week. Paolo’s visit is two days after Sandro’s. Rino’s visit is the day after Ottavio’s. Paolo’s visit is after Ottavio’s. On which day is Quirino’s visit?

- Wednesday
- Tuesday
- Thursday
- Friday

## Explanation

Sandro’s and Paolo’s visits are two days apart, so they take Monday and Wednesday, Tuesday and Thursday, or Wednesday and Friday. Ottavio and Rino need two consecutive days. In the second case only Monday, Wednesday, and Friday would remain, with no two consecutive days. In the first case Ottavio and Rino would take Thursday and Friday, and Paolo’s visit on Wednesday would not be after Ottavio’s. Therefore Sandro’s visit is on Wednesday, Paolo’s on Friday, Ottavio’s on Monday, and Rino’s on Tuesday. That leaves **Thursday** for Quirino.
