Luisa, Marco, Nadia, Oscar, Paola, and Renzo sit facing the center of a round table in six equally spaced seats. Left and right are from the seated person’s perspective. Marco sits across from Luisa. Nadia is on Marco’s left. Oscar is across from Nadia. Paola is not next to Luisa. Who sits on Luisa’s right?

- Renzo
- Oscar
- Paola
- Nadia

## Explanation

Put Luisa in seat $0$ and number the seats counterclockwise. The seat to the right of seat $k$ is then $k + 1$, and the one to the left is $k - 1$. Marco is across from Luisa in seat $3$, Nadia is on his left in seat $2$, and Oscar is across from Nadia in seat $2 + 3 = 5$. Seats $1$ and $4$ remain. Seat $1$ is next to Luisa, so Paola is in seat $4$ and Renzo in seat $1$. The seat on Luisa’s right is $1$, so **Renzo** sits on her right. The answer “Oscar” identifies the person on Luisa’s left.
