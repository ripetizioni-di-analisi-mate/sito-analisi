A car ran a red light. Its color was red, blue, green, or white. Three pedestrians describe it to the local police. Anna says, “The car was red.” Bruno says, “The car was neither red nor blue.” Carlo says, “The car was blue or green.” Exactly two of the three statements are true. What color was the car?

- Green
- Blue
- White
- Red

## Explanation

No two statements are contradictory, so, as in other **problems with a fixed number of true statements**, try each color and count the true statements. If the car was red, only Anna’s statement is true. If it was blue, only Carlo’s is true, and if it was white, only Bruno’s is true. If the car was green, Bruno’s and Carlo’s statements are true, giving exactly two. Therefore the car was **green**. The answers “Red,” “Blue,” and “White” are the colors for which just one statement is true.
