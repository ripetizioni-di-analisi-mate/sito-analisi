Lidia, Milo, Nora, and Remo each staff one service counter. The counters handle civil registration, taxes, education, and transportation. Milo handles taxes. Nora handles either civil registration or education. Remo handles neither civil registration nor education. Lidia does not handle education. Which counter does Nora staff?

- taxes
- education
- transportation
- civil registration

## Explanation

Milo handles taxes. Remo can take neither civil registration nor education, so he takes transportation. That leaves civil registration and education for Lidia and Nora. Since Lidia does not handle education, Nora takes the education counter.
