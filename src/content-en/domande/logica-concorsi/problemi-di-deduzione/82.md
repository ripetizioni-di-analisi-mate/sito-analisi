Exactly one of A, B, and C took a folder. A says, “B didn’t take it.” B says, “I took it.” C says, “A didn’t take it.” Only one statement is true, and we know that B did not take the folder. Who took it?

- Cannot be determined
- C
- A
- B

## Explanation

Since B did not take it, A’s statement is true and B’s is false. To have exactly one true statement, C’s statement must also be false. So A took the folder.
