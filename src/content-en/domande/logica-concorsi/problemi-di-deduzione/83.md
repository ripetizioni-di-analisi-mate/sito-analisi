A missing card is in exactly one of three drawers, A, B, and C. Drawer A reads “The card is in B.” Drawer B reads “The card is not in B.” Drawer C reads “The card is in A.” Exactly two statements are true. Which drawer contains the card?

- Cannot be determined
- C
- B
- A

## Explanation

The statements on A and B are negations of each other, so exactly one of them is true. For there to be two true statements, the label on drawer C must be true. Therefore the card is in A.
