Five books, one blue, one green, one red, one yellow, and one black, sit on a shelf, one book per position. The green and red books are adjacent. The yellow book is first and the black one last. The blue book is to the right of the red book and to the left of the black book. Which of the following colors can occupy the second position?

- black
- blue
- yellow
- green

## Explanation

Positions two, three, and four belong to the blue, green, and red books. If green is second, red is third and blue fourth, so that arrangement is possible. Blue cannot be second because red must come before it, and yellow and black already occupy the ends.
