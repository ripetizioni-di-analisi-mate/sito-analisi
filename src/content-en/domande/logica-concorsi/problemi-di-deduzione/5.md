Nadia, Oreste, and Pina have three different jobs (doctor, engineer, and cook) and live in three different cities (Turin, Bari, and Pisa), one person per city. The doctor lives in Bari. Oreste lives in Pisa. Nadia does not live in Bari. The cook does not live in Turin. Which of the following statements is true?

- Oreste is the cook
- Nadia is the doctor
- Pina is the engineer
- Oreste is the engineer

## Explanation

Oreste lives in Pisa and Nadia does not live in Bari, so Nadia lives in Turin and Pina lives in Bari. The doctor lives in Bari, so Pina is the doctor. The cook does not live in Turin. By the **contrapositive** of this clue, the person who lives in Turin cannot be the cook, so Nadia is not the cook. Therefore **Oreste** is the cook and Nadia is the engineer. The answer “Nadia is the doctor” is tempting if you use the clue about the doctor before working out where each person lives.
