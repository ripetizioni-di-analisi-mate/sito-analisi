Alba, Bruna, Cora, and Dea live on different floors of a four-story building, numbered $1$ through $4$ from bottom to top. They each have a different pet: a dog, a cat, goldfish, or a rabbit. The cat lives two floors above the dog. Alba lives one floor directly above Bruna. Cora has the goldfish. Neither Dea nor Cora lives on the first floor. Bruna does not have the dog. Who has the dog, and on which floor does she live?

- Dea, on the second floor
- Bruna, on the first floor
- Alba, on the second floor
- Dea, on the fourth floor

## Explanation

Dea and Cora do not live on the first floor, and Alba lives above Bruna, so Bruna lives on the first floor and Alba on the second. The cat is two floors above the dog, so the dog must be on the first or second floor. Bruna lives on the first floor and does not have the dog, so the dog belongs to **Alba, on the second floor**, and the cat is on the fourth. Cora has the fish, so she lives on the third floor, leaving Dea on the fourth with the cat. The answer “Dea, on the fourth floor” reverses the clue, placing the dog above the cat.
