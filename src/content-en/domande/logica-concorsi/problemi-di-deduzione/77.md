A, B, C, and D sit in four seats in a circle. A and C must sit across from each other. How many arrangements are possible if arrangements that differ only by rotation count as one?

- $1$
- $2$
- $4$
- $6$

## Explanation

Fix A’s seat to count rotations only once. C must sit in the opposite seat. B and D can trade places in the two remaining seats, giving two different arrangements.
