The statement "If Ivo wins or ties the game, he celebrates with the team," where "or" is inclusive, is false. Which of the following statements must be true?

- Ivo celebrates with the team
- Ivo wins the game and does not celebrate with the team
- Ivo does not celebrate with the team
- Ivo neither wins nor ties the game

## Explanation

An implication is false only when its antecedent is true and its consequent is false. Therefore its antecedent, "Ivo wins or ties the game," is true, and its consequent, "Ivo celebrates with the team," is false, so Ivo **does not celebrate** with the team. "Ivo wins the game and does not celebrate with the team" need not be true because the antecedent is also true when Ivo ties. "Ivo neither wins nor ties the game" makes the antecedent false, contradicting the fact that the given statement is false.
