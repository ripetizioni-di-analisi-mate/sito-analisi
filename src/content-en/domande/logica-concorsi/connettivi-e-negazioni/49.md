Let $A$ mean "the name is registered," $B$ "the payment has been received," and $C$ "the contact information has been verified." In which of the following cases is $A \Leftrightarrow \left(B \land C\right)$ false?

- $A$ is true, $B$ is false, and $C$ is false
- $A$ is true, $B$ is true, and $C$ is true
- $A$ is false, $B$ is true, and $C$ is false
- $A$ is false, $B$ is false, and $C$ is true

## Explanation

A **biconditional** is false when its two parts have different truth values. The conjunction of $B$ and $C$ is true only when both are true.
