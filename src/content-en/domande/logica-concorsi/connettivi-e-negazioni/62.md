Let $A$ mean "the badge is valid," $B$ "the service counter is open," and $C$ "the clerk is present." In which of the following cases is $A \land \left(B \land \lnot C\right)$ true?

- $A$ is true, $B$ is true, and $C$ is false
- $A$ is false, $B$ is true, and $C$ is false
- $A$ is false, $B$ is false, and $C$ is true
- $A$ is false, $B$ is false, and $C$ is false

## Explanation

A conjunction is true if all its parts are true. Here $A$ must be true, $B$ must be true, and $C$ must be false.
