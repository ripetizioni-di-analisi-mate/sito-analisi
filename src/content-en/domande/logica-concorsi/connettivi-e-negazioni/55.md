Which expression has the same truth values as $\operatorname{XOR}\!\left(A, B\right) \land \left(A \Leftrightarrow B\right)$? XOR denotes exclusive "or."

- $A \lor \lnot A$
- $A \land \lnot A$
- $A \Leftrightarrow B$
- $\operatorname{XOR}\!\left(A, B\right)$

## Explanation

The exclusive disjunction requires different truth values and the biconditional requires equal ones. Taken together, the two conditions are a **contradiction**.
