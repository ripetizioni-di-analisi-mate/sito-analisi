Which of the following statements is true in every case, whatever happens? In the answers, "or" is inclusive.

- The train is on time and the train is not on time
- The train is on time or Marco is at the station
- If the train is on time, the train is not on time
- If the train is on time and Marco is at the station, the train is on time

## Explanation

An implication is false only when its antecedent is true and its consequent is false. If the antecedent "the train is on time and Marco is at the station" is true, then the train is on time and the consequent is true. Therefore the implication is never false and is a **tautology**. "The train is on time and the train is not on time" is a contradiction, and "If the train is on time, the train is not on time" is false when the train is on time. "The train is on time or Marco is at the station" is false when the train is not on time and Marco is not at the station.
