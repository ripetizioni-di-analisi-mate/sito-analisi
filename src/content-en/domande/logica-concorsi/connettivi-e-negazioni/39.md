A music school has the rule "You may enter the rehearsal room only if you have booked a time slot." Giada has booked a time slot. Which of the following statements must be true?

- Giada enters the rehearsal room
- Giada does not enter the rehearsal room
- Giada enters the rehearsal room if and only if she has booked a time slot
- It is impossible to determine whether Giada enters the rehearsal room

## Explanation

The expression **only if** introduces the consequent, so the rule means "If you enter the rehearsal room, you have booked a time slot." For Giada the consequent is true, so the implication is true both when she enters and when she does not. Consequently, the information given does not determine whether she enters. The answer "Giada enters the rehearsal room" reads "only if" as "if," and the answer with "if and only if" adds an implication that is absent from the rule.
