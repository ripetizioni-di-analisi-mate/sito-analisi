What is obtained by expanding $A \land \left(B \lor C\right)$ with the distributive law?

- $\left(A \land B\right) \land C$
- $\left(A \land B\right) \lor \left(A \land C\right)$
- $\left(A \lor B\right) \land \left(A \lor C\right)$
- $\left(A \land B\right) \lor C$

## Explanation

The **distributive law** separates the two cases of the disjunction. It requires $A$ to be true along with at least one of $B$ and $C$.
