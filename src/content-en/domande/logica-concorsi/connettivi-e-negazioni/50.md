Which expression represents "The lab opens either in the morning or in the afternoon, with just one opening period"? Let $A$ = "the lab opens in the morning" and $B$ = "the lab opens in the afternoon." XOR denotes exclusive "or."

- $\operatorname{XOR}\!\left(A, B\right)$
- $A \land B$
- $A \Leftrightarrow B$
- $A \lor B$

## Explanation

Only one of the two opening periods is scheduled. The **exclusive disjunction** is true in exactly the two cases in which one proposition is true and the other is false.
