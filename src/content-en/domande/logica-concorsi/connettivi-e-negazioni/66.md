Let $A$ mean "the test has begun," $B$ "time is up," and $C$ "the paper has been turned in." In which of the following cases is $\lnot \left(A \Rightarrow B\right) \land C$ true?

- $A$ is true, $B$ is false, and $C$ is true
- $A$ is true, $B$ is true, and $C$ is true
- $A$ is true, $B$ is true, and $C$ is false
- $A$ is true, $B$ is false, and $C$ is false

## Explanation

For the implication to be false, $A$ must be true and $B$ false. The conjunction also requires $C$ to be true.
