The statement "The flight departed on time, but the luggage did not arrive" is true. Which of the following statements is false? In the answers, "or" is inclusive.

- The luggage did not arrive
- The flight departed on time or the luggage arrived
- If the flight departed on time, the luggage arrived
- If the luggage arrived, the flight did not depart on time

## Explanation

The word "but" has the same truth conditions as "and," so both "The flight departed on time" and "The luggage did not arrive" are true. The implication "If the flight departed on time, the luggage arrived" has a true antecedent and a false consequent, so it is **false**. The disjunction is true because the flight departed on time. "If the luggage arrived, the flight did not depart on time" has a false antecedent and is therefore true.
