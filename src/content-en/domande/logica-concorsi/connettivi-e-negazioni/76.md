Let $A$ mean "the lesson is booked," $B$ "the instructor is present," and $C$ "the classroom is fit for use." In which of the following cases is $\left(A \land B\right) \Leftrightarrow \lnot C$ true?

- $A$ is true, $B$ is false, and $C$ is false
- $A$ is true, $B$ is false, and $C$ is true
- $A$ is false, $B$ is false, and $C$ is false
- $A$ is true, $B$ is true, and $C$ is true

## Explanation

The two parts of the **biconditional** must have the same truth value. If $C$ is false, both $A$ and $B$ must be true. If $C$ is true, at least one of $A$ and $B$ must be false.
