Which expression represents "The form is filled out, but the signature is missing"? Let $A$ = "the form is filled out" and $B$ = "the signature is present."

- $A \lor \lnot B$
- $\lnot A \land B$
- $A \land \lnot B$
- $A \Rightarrow \lnot B$

## Explanation

"But" joins two statements that are both asserted. The first says that the form is filled out and the second that the signature is missing.
