Let $A$ mean "the book is available," $B$ "the reader is registered," and $C$ "the loan is active." In which of the following cases is $\left(A \lor \lnot B\right) \Rightarrow C$ false?

- $A$ is true, $B$ is true, and $C$ is true
- $A$ is false, $B$ is true, and $C$ is false
- $A$ is false, $B$ is false, and $C$ is false
- $A$ is false, $B$ is false, and $C$ is true

## Explanation

$C$ must be false, and the antecedent must hold. The antecedent is true when $A$ is true or $B$ is false.
