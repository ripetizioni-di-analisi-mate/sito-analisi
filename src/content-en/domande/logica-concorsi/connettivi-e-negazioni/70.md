Which expression represents "Neither the administrative office nor the library is open"? Let $A$ = "the administrative office is open" and $B$ = "the library is open." XOR denotes exclusive "or."

- $\operatorname{XOR}\!\left(A, B\right)$
- $A \land B$
- $\lnot \left(A \land B\right)$
- $\lnot \left(A \lor B\right)$

## Explanation

The statement rules out either location being open. By **De Morgan's law**, it requires the disjunction of the two statements about being open to be false.
