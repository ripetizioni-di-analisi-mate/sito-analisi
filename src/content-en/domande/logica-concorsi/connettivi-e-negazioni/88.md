Which formula has the same truth value as $\lnot \left(A \lor \left(B \land C\right)\right)$ for every assignment?

- $\lnot A \lor \left(\lnot B \lor \lnot C\right)$
- $\lnot A \lor \left(\lnot B \land \lnot C\right)$
- $\lnot A \land \left(\lnot B \land \lnot C\right)$
- $\lnot A \land \left(\lnot B \lor \lnot C\right)$

## Explanation

By **De Morgan's law**, neither part of the disjunction may be true. For the conjunction to be false, it is enough for at least one of $B$ and $C$ to be false.
