In which cases does $\lnot \left(\left(A \lor B\right) \Rightarrow C\right)$ hold? Choose the equivalent expression.

- $\lnot C \Rightarrow \lnot \left(A \lor B\right)$
- $\left(A \lor B\right) \lor \lnot C$
- $\left(A \land B\right) \land \lnot C$
- $\left(A \lor B\right) \land \lnot C$

## Explanation

To negate the implication, require its antecedent to be true and its consequent to be false. The antecedent is true when at least one of $A$ and $B$ is true.
