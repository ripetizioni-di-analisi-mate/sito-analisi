Which condition describes the cases in which $\lnot \left(A \Leftrightarrow B\right)$ holds?

- $A \lor B$
- $\left(A \land \lnot B\right) \lor \left(\lnot A \land B\right)$
- $\lnot A \land \lnot B$
- $A \land B$

## Explanation

A biconditional is false if its two truth values differ. Thus $A$ can be true and $B$ false, or $A$ false and $B$ true.
