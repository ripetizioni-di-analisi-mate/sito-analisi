Let $A$ mean "the room is available," $B$ "the microphone works," and $C$ "the projector is on." In which of the following cases is $\left(A \lor B\right) \lor C$ false?

- $A$ is false, $B$ is true, and $C$ is true
- $A$ is true, $B$ is false, and $C$ is true
- $A$ is false, $B$ is false, and $C$ is false
- $A$ is true, $B$ is false, and $C$ is false

## Explanation

The disjunction is false only when all its parts are false. Just one true proposition among the three would make it true.
