Let $A$ mean "the record is complete," $B$ "the record has been verified," and $C$ "the record is up to date." In which of the following cases is $A \lor \left(B \land \lnot C\right)$ false?

- $A$ is false, $B$ is false, and $C$ is false
- $A$ is true, $B$ is true, and $C$ is true
- $A$ is false, $B$ is true, and $C$ is false
- $A$ is true, $B$ is true, and $C$ is false

## Explanation

Both $A$ and the conjunction of $B$ with the negation of $C$ must be false. This conjunction is false when $B$ is false or $C$ is true.
