Which formula is the contrapositive of $A \Rightarrow \lnot B$?

- $B \Rightarrow \lnot A$
- $\lnot B \Rightarrow A$
- $A \Rightarrow B$
- $A \Leftrightarrow \lnot B$

## Explanation

The **contrapositive** exchanges the antecedent and the consequent and negates them both. The negation of "$B$ is false" is "$B$ is true."
