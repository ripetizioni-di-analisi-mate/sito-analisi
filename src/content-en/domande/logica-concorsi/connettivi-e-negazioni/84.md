The contrapositive of $A\Rightarrow\lnot B$ has antecedent $B$. Which truth-table row makes both implications false?

- $A$ true and $B$ true
- $A$ true and $B$ false
- $A$ false and $B$ true
- $A$ false and $B$ false

## Explanation

The contrapositive is $B\Rightarrow\lnot A$. Both implications have a true antecedent and false consequent precisely when A and B are both true.
