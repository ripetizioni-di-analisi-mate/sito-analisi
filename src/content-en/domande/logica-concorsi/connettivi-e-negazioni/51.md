Which expression represents "The room is booked exactly when the calendar is free"? Let $A$ = "the room is booked" and $B$ = "the calendar is free." XOR denotes exclusive "or."

- $\operatorname{XOR}\!\left(A, B\right)$
- $B \Rightarrow A$
- $A \Rightarrow B$
- $A \Leftrightarrow B$

## Explanation

"Exactly when" requires each event to occur whenever the other does. This is the meaning of a **biconditional**.
