Which expression is logically equivalent to $\left(A \Rightarrow B\right) \lor \left(B \Rightarrow A\right)$?

- $A \lor \lnot A$
- $A \Rightarrow B$
- $A \Leftrightarrow B$
- $A \land \lnot A$

## Explanation

At most one of the two implications can be false in any one case. Their disjunction is therefore a **tautology**, as is the disjunction of $A$ and its negation.
