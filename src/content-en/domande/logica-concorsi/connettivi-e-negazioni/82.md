Let $A$ mean "the file is numbered," $B$ "the file is officially stamped," and $C$ "the file is accessible." XOR denotes exclusive "or." In which of the following cases is $\operatorname{XOR}\!\left(A, B \land C\right)$ true?

- $A$ is true, $B$ is true, and $C$ is true
- $A$ is false, $B$ is false, and $C$ is false
- $A$ is true, $B$ is false, and $C$ is true
- $A$ is false, $B$ is true, and $C$ is false

## Explanation

The exclusive disjunction requires exactly one of the two parts to be true. The second part is the conjunction of $B$ and $C$.
