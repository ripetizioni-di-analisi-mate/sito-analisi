Consider the statements "Gino speaks English or German," where "or" is inclusive, and "Gino speaks either English or German, but not both." In which of the following cases is the first statement true and the second false?

- Gino speaks only English
- Gino speaks neither English nor German
- Gino speaks both English and German
- Gino speaks only German

## Explanation

Inclusive "or" and exclusive "or" have the same truth value in every case except one, when both parts are true. If Gino speaks both languages, the **inclusive disjunction** is true, whereas the **exclusive disjunction** is false because it rules out precisely this case. If Gino speaks just one of the two languages, both statements are true. If he speaks neither, both are false.
