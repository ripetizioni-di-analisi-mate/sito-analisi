The statements "If Rita goes to Milan, she takes the train," "If Rita takes the train, she buys her ticket online," and "Rita does not buy her ticket online" are true. Which of the following statements must be true?

- Rita goes to Milan and does not take the train
- Rita does not go to Milan
- Rita takes the train
- Rita takes the train and does not go to Milan

## Explanation

The statement "If Rita takes the train, she buys her ticket online" is equivalent to its **contrapositive**, "If Rita does not buy her ticket online, she does not take the train." Rita does not buy her ticket online, so she does not take the train. "If Rita goes to Milan, she takes the train" is equivalent to "If Rita does not take the train, she does not go to Milan," so Rita does not go to Milan. "Rita takes the train" and "Rita takes the train and does not go to Milan" are both false because Rita does not take the train. "Rita goes to Milan and does not take the train" is also false because Rita does not go to Milan.
