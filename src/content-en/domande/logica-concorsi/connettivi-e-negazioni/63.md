Replace the implication in $\left(A \land B\right) \Rightarrow C$ with an equivalent expression.

- $\left(\lnot A \lor \lnot B\right) \land C$
- $\left(\lnot A \land \lnot B\right) \lor C$
- $\left(\lnot A \lor \lnot B\right) \lor C$
- $\left(A \land B\right) \land C$

## Explanation

An **implication** is equivalent to the disjunction of the negation of its antecedent and its consequent. By De Morgan's law, negating the conjunction of $A$ and $B$ means that $A$ is false or $B$ is false.
