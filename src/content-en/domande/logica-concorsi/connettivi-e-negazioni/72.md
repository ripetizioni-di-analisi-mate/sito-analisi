Which formula expresses the biconditional $A \Leftrightarrow B$ in terms of simple implications?

- $A \land B$
- $\left(A \Rightarrow B\right) \land \left(B \Rightarrow A\right)$
- $A \lor B$
- $\left(A \Rightarrow B\right) \lor \left(B \Rightarrow A\right)$

## Explanation

The **biconditional** requires the two implications in opposite directions. This makes the truth values of $A$ and $B$ agree.
