What is the converse of "If Bruno is tired, he goes to bed early"?

- If Bruno is tired, he does not go to bed early
- If Bruno does not go to bed early, he is not tired
- If Bruno goes to bed early, he is tired
- If Bruno goes to bed early, he is not tired

## Explanation

The **converse** of $A \Rightarrow B$ is $B \Rightarrow A$, which exchanges the antecedent and the consequent without negating them. With $A$ = "Bruno is tired" and $B$ = "Bruno goes to bed early," the converse is "If Bruno goes to bed early, he is tired." The statement "If Bruno does not go to bed early, he is not tired" exchanges and negates both parts, so it is the contrapositive. In "If Bruno goes to bed early, he is not tired," the parts are exchanged and the consequent is negated. In "If Bruno is tired, he does not go to bed early," the parts stay in place and only the consequent is negated.
