Which expression results from applying the distributive law to $A \lor \left(B \land C\right)$?

- $\left(A \land B\right) \lor \left(A \land C\right)$
- $\left(A \lor B\right) \land C$
- $\left(A \lor B\right) \lor C$
- $\left(A \lor B\right) \land \left(A \lor C\right)$

## Explanation

If $A$ is true, the two disjunctions containing $A$ are both true. If $A$ is false, both are true only when $B$ and $C$ are also true.
