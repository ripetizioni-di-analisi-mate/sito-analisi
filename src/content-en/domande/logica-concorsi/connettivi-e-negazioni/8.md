An office has the rule "Either Laura or Nadia, but not both, closes the office in the evening." The rule was followed last night. Which of the following statements must be true? In the answers, "or" is inclusive.

- Laura or Nadia closed the office last night
- Laura closed the office last night
- If Laura closed the office last night, Nadia did too
- Neither Laura nor Nadia closed the office last night

## Explanation

The **exclusive disjunction** is true when exactly one of the two closed the office. In either such case at least one of them closed the office, so the inclusive disjunction "Laura or Nadia closed the office" is also true. The statement "Laura closed the office" is false when Nadia alone closed it. The implication "If Laura closed the office, Nadia did too" is false when Laura alone closed it, and the statement with "neither … nor" is false in both cases.
