Let $A$ mean "the available credit is sufficient," $B$ "the card is active," and $C$ "the code is correct." In which of the following cases is $\left(A \Rightarrow B\right) \lor C$ false?

- $A$ is true, $B$ is true, and $C$ is true
- $A$ is false, $B$ is false, and $C$ is true
- $A$ is true, $B$ is false, and $C$ is false
- $A$ is false, $B$ is false, and $C$ is false

## Explanation

Both parts of the disjunction must be false. Therefore $A$ is true, $B$ is false, and $C$ is false.
