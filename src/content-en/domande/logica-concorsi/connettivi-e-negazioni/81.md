Apply contraposition to $\lnot A \Rightarrow B$. Which rule results?

- $A \Rightarrow B$
- $\lnot A \Leftrightarrow B$
- $\lnot B \Rightarrow A$
- $B \Rightarrow \lnot A$

## Explanation

In the **contrapositive**, the new antecedent is the negation of $B$. The new consequent is the negation of "$A$ is false," that is, $A$.
