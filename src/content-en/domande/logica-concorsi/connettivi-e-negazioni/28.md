What is the contrapositive of "If the temperature exceeds $30$ degrees, the air conditioner turns on"?

- If the air conditioner does not turn on, the temperature does not exceed $30$ degrees
- If the air conditioner turns on, the temperature exceeds $30$ degrees
- If the temperature does not exceed $30$ degrees, the air conditioner does not turn on
- If the air conditioner does not turn on, the temperature exceeds $30$ degrees

## Explanation

The **contrapositive** of $A \Rightarrow B$ is $\lnot B \Rightarrow \lnot A$, which exchanges the antecedent and the consequent and negates both. With $A$ = "The temperature exceeds $30$ degrees" and $B$ = "The air conditioner turns on," this gives "If the air conditioner does not turn on, the temperature does not exceed $30$ degrees." The statement that exchanges the two parts without negating them is the converse, and the one that negates them without exchanging them is the inverse. In the remaining statement the parts are exchanged but only one is negated.
