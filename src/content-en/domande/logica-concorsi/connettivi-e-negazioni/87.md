Let $A$ mean "the inventory is complete," $B$ "the totals agree," and $C$ "the managers have signed." In which of the following cases is $\left(A \lor B\right) \land \left(\lnot A \lor C\right)$ true?

- $A$ is true, $B$ is true, and $C$ is false
- $A$ is false, $B$ is false, and $C$ is false
- $A$ is true, $B$ is false, and $C$ is true
- $A$ is true, $B$ is false, and $C$ is false

## Explanation

If $A$ is true, $C$ must be true for the second disjunction to be true. If $A$ is false, $B$ must be true for the first one to be true.
