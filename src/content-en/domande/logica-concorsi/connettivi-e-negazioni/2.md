Proposition $P$ is true, whereas propositions $Q$ and $R$ are false. Which of the following propositions is true?

- $(P \land Q) \lor R$
- $(Q \Rightarrow R) \land P$
- $P \Rightarrow (Q \lor R)$
- $\lnot P \lor Q$

## Explanation

The implication $Q \Rightarrow R$ has a false antecedent $Q$, so it is true. Since $P$ is also true, the conjunction $(Q \Rightarrow R) \land P$ is **true**. Propositions $P \land Q$ and $R$ are both false, so $(P \land Q) \lor R$ is false. In $P \Rightarrow (Q \lor R)$ the antecedent is true and the consequent $Q \lor R$ is false, so the implication is false. Finally, $\lnot P \lor Q$ is false because $\lnot P$ and $Q$ are both false.
