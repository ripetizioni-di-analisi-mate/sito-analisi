Let $A$ mean "the file is open," $B$ "the file is saved," and $C$ "the copy has been sent." In which of the following cases is $\lnot A \Rightarrow \left(B \land C\right)$ false?

- $A$ is false, $B$ is true, and $C$ is true
- $A$ is false, $B$ is true, and $C$ is false
- $A$ is true, $B$ is true, and $C$ is false
- $A$ is true, $B$ is true, and $C$ is true

## Explanation

The negation of $A$ must be true, so $A$ is false. The consequent is false if at least one of $B$ and $C$ is false.
