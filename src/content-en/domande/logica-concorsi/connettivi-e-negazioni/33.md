What is the inverse of "If Lia does not take time off, she finishes the project"?

- If Lia takes time off, she finishes the project
- If Lia takes time off, she does not finish the project
- If Lia does not finish the project, she takes time off
- If Lia does not take time off, she does not finish the project

## Explanation

The **inverse** of an implication negates its antecedent and consequent without exchanging them. With $F$ = "Lia takes time off" and $P$ = "Lia finishes the project," the given statement is $\lnot F \Rightarrow P$. The negation of $\lnot F$ is $F$, so the inverse is $F \Rightarrow \lnot P$, that is, "If Lia takes time off, she does not finish the project." "If Lia does not finish the project, she takes time off" is the contrapositive. "If Lia does not take time off, she does not finish the project" negates only the consequent, and "If Lia takes time off, she finishes the project" negates only the antecedent, because the negation of "Lia does not take time off" is "Lia takes time off."
