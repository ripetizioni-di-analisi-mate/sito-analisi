Let $A$ mean "the door is closed," $B$ "the sensor is on," and $C$ "the window is closed." In which of the following cases is $\lnot \left(\lnot A \lor \left(B \lor C\right)\right)$ true?

- $A$ is true, $B$ is false, and $C$ is false
- $A$ is true, $B$ is true, and $C$ is false
- $A$ is true, $B$ is true, and $C$ is true
- $A$ is true, $B$ is false, and $C$ is true

## Explanation

For the negated statement to be true, the disjunction inside it must be false. Thus the negation of $A$ is false, and $B$ and $C$ are also false.
