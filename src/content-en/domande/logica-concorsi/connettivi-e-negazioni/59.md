A filter rejects a form when the signature is missing or both attachments are missing. Which combination gives the minimum requirements for acceptance?

- A signature and no attachments
- At least one attachment even without a signature
- A signature and at least one attachment
- Exactly two attachments even without a signature

## Explanation

Avoiding the first reason for rejection requires a signature. Avoiding the second requires at least one attachment. Both conditions must hold.
