Which expression shows explicitly what follows from the negation in $\lnot \left(A \land \left(B \lor C\right)\right)$?

- $\lnot A \land \left(\lnot B \land \lnot C\right)$
- $\lnot A \land \left(\lnot B \lor \lnot C\right)$
- $\lnot A \lor \left(\lnot B \land \lnot C\right)$
- $\lnot A \lor \left(\lnot B \lor \lnot C\right)$

## Explanation

**De Morgan's law** turns the negation of the conjunction into a disjunction. Negating "$B$ or $C$" in turn requires $B$ and $C$ both to be false.
