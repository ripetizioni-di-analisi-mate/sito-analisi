A store displays the notice "If a customer pays cash, they receive a $5\%$ discount." Each customer pays either by cash or by card, but not both. In which of the following cases was the notice not honored?

- The customer pays by card and receives the discount
- The customer pays cash and does not receive the discount
- The customer pays by card and does not receive the discount
- The customer pays cash and receives the discount

## Explanation

The notice is an **implication** with antecedent "the customer pays cash" and consequent "the customer receives the discount." An implication is false in just one case, when its antecedent is true and its consequent is false. Thus the notice is not honored when the customer pays cash and does not receive the discount. A customer who pays by card and receives the discount does not contradict the notice because the notice says nothing about card payments.
