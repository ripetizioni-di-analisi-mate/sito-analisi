The compound proposition $(A \land B) \lor C$ is false. Which of the following statements must be true?

- $C$ is false, and $A$ and $B$ are both false
- $C$ is false, and $A$ and $B$ are both true
- $C$ is true, and at least one of $A$ and $B$ is false
- $C$ is false, and at least one of $A$ and $B$ is false

## Explanation

A disjunction is false only when all its parts are false, so $A \land B$ and $C$ are both false. The conjunction $A \land B$ is false when at least one of $A$ and $B$ is false. Therefore $C$ is false and **at least one** of $A$ and $B$ is false. With $A$ true, $B$ false, and $C$ false, the given proposition is false even though $A$ and $B$ are not both false. Consequently, the statement "$C$ is false, and $A$ and $B$ are both false" need not be true.
