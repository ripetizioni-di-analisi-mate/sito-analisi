Which expression describes exactly the cases in which the negation of the implication in $\lnot \left(A \Rightarrow \left(B \land C\right)\right)$ is true?

- $A \lor \left(\lnot B \land \lnot C\right)$
- $A \land \left(\lnot B \lor \lnot C\right)$
- $\lnot A \land \left(B \lor C\right)$
- $A \land \left(\lnot B \land \lnot C\right)$

## Explanation

An implication is false when its antecedent is true and its consequent is false. Thus $A$ must be true and at least one of $B$ and $C$ must be false.
