Let $A$ mean "the package has been weighed," $B$ "the package is labeled," and $C$ "the package is in the warehouse." In which of the following cases is $\left(A \Leftrightarrow B\right) \Rightarrow C$ false?

- $A$ is false, $B$ is true, and $C$ is false
- $A$ is false, $B$ is false, and $C$ is true
- $A$ is false, $B$ is true, and $C$ is true
- $A$ is false, $B$ is false, and $C$ is false

## Explanation

The antecedent must be true, so $A$ and $B$ must have the same value. In addition, $C$ must be false.
