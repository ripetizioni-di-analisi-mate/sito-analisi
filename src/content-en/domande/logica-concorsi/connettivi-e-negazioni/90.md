Let $A$ mean "the receipt is attached," $B$ "the form is signed," and $C$ "the copy is legible." In which of the following cases is $\left(A \land B\right) \Rightarrow C$ false?

- $A$ is false, $B$ is true, and $C$ is true
- $A$ is true, $B$ is true, and $C$ is false
- $A$ is false, $B$ is true, and $C$ is false
- $A$ is false, $B$ is false, and $C$ is true

## Explanation

The **implication** is false when its antecedent is true and its consequent is false. The antecedent requires both $A$ and $B$.
