The statement "The store is open if and only if the owner is present" is false. The owner is absent. Which of the following statements must be true?

- The store is not open
- The store is open
- The store is open and the owner is present
- If the store is open, the owner is present

## Explanation

A **biconditional** is true when its two parts have the same truth value. Since the given statement is false, its two parts have different values. The part "the owner is present" is false, so "the store is open" is true. The answer "The store is not open" treats the given statement as true. The conjunction is false because the owner is absent. The implication "If the store is open, the owner is present" has a true antecedent and a false consequent, so it is false.
