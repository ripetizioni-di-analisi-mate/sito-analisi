A store refunds a purchase if and only if the customer has the receipt and also meets at least one of two conditions: having a loyalty card or having made the purchase online. Here "or" is inclusive. Which of these customers qualifies for a refund?

- Aldo, who has the receipt, has no loyalty card, and made the purchase in the store
- Bruna, who has no receipt, has a loyalty card, and made the purchase online
- Carlo, who has no receipt or loyalty card and made the purchase online
- Dora, who has the receipt and a loyalty card and made the purchase online

## Explanation

Let $S$ = "the customer has the receipt," $T$ = "the customer has a loyalty card," and $O$ = "the customer made the purchase online," so the rule is $S \land (T \lor O)$. Dora has a loyalty card and made the purchase online, so $T \lor O$ is true because "or" is inclusive. Dora also has the receipt, so she **qualifies** for a refund. Bruna and Carlo have no receipt, so the conjunction is false for both. Aldo has the receipt but has no loyalty card and did not buy online, so $T \lor O$ is false for him. Reading "or" as exclusive would rule Dora out precisely because she meets both conditions.
