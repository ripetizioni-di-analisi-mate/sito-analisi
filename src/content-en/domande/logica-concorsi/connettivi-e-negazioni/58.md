Which expression is true in exactly the same cases as $\lnot \operatorname{XOR}\!\left(A, B\right)$? XOR denotes exclusive "or."

- $A \Leftrightarrow B$
- $A \land B$
- $A \lor B$
- $\lnot \left(A \lor B\right)$

## Explanation

The exclusive disjunction is true when exactly one proposition is true. Its negation requires two equal truth values, as does the **biconditional**.
