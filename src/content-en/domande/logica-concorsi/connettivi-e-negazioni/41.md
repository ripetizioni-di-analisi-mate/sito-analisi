What is the negation of "The construction site opens on Monday unless it rains"?

- The construction site does not open on Monday unless it rains
- The construction site does not open on Monday and it does not rain
- The construction site does not open on Monday and it rains
- If it rains, the construction site opens on Monday

## Explanation

"$A$ unless $B$" means "if not $B$, then $A$," so the given statement is "If it does not rain, the construction site opens on Monday." To **negate the implication**, assert the antecedent and negate the consequent. This gives "It does not rain and the construction site does not open on Monday." "The construction site does not open on Monday and it rains" describes a case in which the given statement is true because the statement says nothing about opening when it rains. "The construction site does not open on Monday unless it rains" and "If it rains, the construction site opens on Monday" are implications. Both are true if it rains and the site opens, but the required conjunction is false in that case.
