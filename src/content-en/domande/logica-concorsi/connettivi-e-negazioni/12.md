Which of the following statements is equivalent to "It is not true that Livio does not study and does not work"? In the answers, "or" is inclusive.

- Livio studies or works
- Livio studies and works
- Livio does not study or does not work
- Livio does not study and does not work

## Explanation

Let $S$ = "Livio studies" and $L$ = "Livio works," so the given statement is $\lnot (\lnot S \land \lnot L)$. By **De Morgan's laws**, it is equivalent to $\lnot \lnot S \lor \lnot \lnot L$. Double negation restores the original statement, so the given statement is equivalent to $S \lor L$, that is, "Livio studies or works." The statement "Livio studies and works" is false when Livio studies and does not work, whereas the given statement is true in that case.
