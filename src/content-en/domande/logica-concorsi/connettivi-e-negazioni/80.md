How is $\left(A \land B\right) \Rightarrow A$ classified as $A$ and $B$ vary in truth value?

- Always true, whatever the truth values of the propositions.
- True for some assignments and false for others.
- Always false, whatever the truth values of the propositions.
- True exactly when both propositions are true.

## Explanation

If the conjunction is true, $A$ is also true. If the conjunction is false, the implication is true anyway.
