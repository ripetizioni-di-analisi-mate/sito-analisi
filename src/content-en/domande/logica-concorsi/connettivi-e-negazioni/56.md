Let $A$ mean "the van is available," $B$ "the driver is present," and $C$ "the delivery is scheduled." In which of the following cases is $\left(A \Rightarrow B\right) \land \lnot C$ true?

- $A$ is true, $B$ is false, and $C$ is true
- $A$ is true, $B$ is false, and $C$ is false
- $A$ is false, $B$ is true, and $C$ is false
- $A$ is true, $B$ is true, and $C$ is true

## Explanation

The conjunction requires $C$ to be false and the implication to be true. The implication is true if $A$ is false or $B$ is true.
