What is the negation of the statement "Carlo is not on vacation or Dina is not on vacation"?

- Carlo and Dina are both on vacation
- Carlo is on vacation or Dina is on vacation
- Neither Carlo nor Dina is on vacation
- Carlo is on vacation and Dina is not on vacation

## Explanation

Let $C$ = "Carlo is on vacation" and $D$ = "Dina is on vacation," so the given statement is $\lnot C \lor \lnot D$. By **De Morgan's laws**, the negation of a disjunction is the conjunction of the negations, namely $\lnot \lnot C \land \lnot \lnot D$. By double negation, this is equivalent to $C \land D$, that is, "Carlo and Dina are both on vacation." The statement "Carlo is on vacation or Dina is on vacation" drops both occurrences of "not" but retains "or," so both it and the given statement are true when Carlo is on vacation and Dina is not.
