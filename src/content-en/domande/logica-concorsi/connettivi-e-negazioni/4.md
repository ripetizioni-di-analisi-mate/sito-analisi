What is the negation of the statement "The warehouse is full"?

- The warehouse is not full
- The warehouse is empty
- The warehouse is half full
- It is not true that the warehouse is not full

## Explanation

The **negation** of a statement is true exactly when the statement is false, so the negation of "The warehouse is full" is "The warehouse is not full." A warehouse that is a quarter full is not full, not empty, and not half full. In that case the given statement, "The warehouse is empty," and "The warehouse is half full" are all false, so neither of the latter two is its negation. "It is not true that the warehouse is not full" contains two negations, so it is equivalent to the given statement.
