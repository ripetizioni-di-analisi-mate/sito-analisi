Which of the following is a proposition, that is, a statement that is either true or false?

- Fill out the form by Friday
- What time does the service counter open?
- Milan is the capital of Italy
- If only tomorrow were Saturday!

## Explanation

The statement "Milan is the capital of Italy" is false because the capital of Italy is Rome. A false statement still has a truth value, so "Milan is the capital of Italy" is a **proposition**. "Fill out the form by Friday" is a command, and "What time does the service counter open?" is a question, so neither is true or false. Similarly, "If only tomorrow were Saturday!" expresses a wish and does not assert a fact, so it is not a proposition.
