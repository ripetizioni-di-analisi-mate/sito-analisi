The statement "Payment is made by bank transfer or by card," where "or" is inclusive, is true. Payment is not made by bank transfer. Which of the following statements must be true?

- Payment is made both by bank transfer and by card
- Payment is not made by card
- Payment is made by card
- It is impossible to determine whether payment is made by card

## Explanation

An **inclusive disjunction** is true when at least one part is true. The part "payment is made by bank transfer" is false, so the other part is true and payment is made by card. The answer "It is impossible to determine" would be correct without the information about the transfer, because the given statement alone does not say which part is true.
