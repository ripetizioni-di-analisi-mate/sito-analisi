Which formula with two implications expresses exactly the rule $\left(A \lor B\right) \Rightarrow C$?

- $\left(A \Rightarrow C\right) \lor \left(B \Rightarrow C\right)$
- $\left(A \Rightarrow C\right) \land \left(B \Rightarrow C\right)$
- $\left(C \Rightarrow A\right) \land \left(C \Rightarrow B\right)$
- $\left(A \land B\right) \land C$

## Explanation

If at least one of the two conditions holds, $C$ must hold. Thus each condition, even on its own, must imply $C$.
