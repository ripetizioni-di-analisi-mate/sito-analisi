Which expression represents "The team sets off unless the vehicle has broken down"? Let $A$ = "the team sets off" and $B$ = "the vehicle has broken down."

- $A \land B$
- $A \Leftrightarrow \lnot B$
- $A \Rightarrow \lnot B$
- $\lnot B \Rightarrow A$

## Explanation

"Unless" introduces the circumstance in which the rule no longer guarantees the outcome. Here departure is guaranteed when the vehicle has not broken down.
