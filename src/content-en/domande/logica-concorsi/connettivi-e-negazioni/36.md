Which of the following statements means the same as "A visitor gets in free only if they are under $18$ years old"?

- If a visitor gets in free, they are under $18$ years old
- If a visitor is under $18$ years old, they get in free
- A visitor gets in free if and only if they are under $18$ years old
- If a visitor does not get in free, they are at least $18$ years old

## Explanation

The expression **only if** introduces the consequent, so "$A$ only if $B$" means $A \Rightarrow B$. With $A$ = "the visitor gets in free" and $B$ = "the visitor is under $18$ years old," the statement means "If a visitor gets in free, they are under $18$ years old." "If a visitor is under $18$ years old, they get in free" reads "only if" as "if" and reverses the implication. "If a visitor does not get in free, they are at least $18$ years old" is $\lnot A \Rightarrow \lnot B$, the inverse, which is not equivalent to the given statement.
