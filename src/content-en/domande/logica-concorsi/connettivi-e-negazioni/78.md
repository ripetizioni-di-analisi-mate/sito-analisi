Which expression simplifies $A \land \left(\lnot A \lor B\right)$ without changing its truth value?

- $A \land B$
- $A \Rightarrow B$
- $A \lor B$
- $B \Rightarrow A$

## Explanation

The conjunction requires $A$ to be true. In that case the part "$A$ is false or $B$ is true" is true only if $B$ is true.
