Which disjunction has the same truth table as $A \Rightarrow \left(B \lor C\right)$?

- $\lnot A \lor \left(B \land C\right)$
- $A \lor \left(B \land C\right)$
- $\lnot A \land \left(B \lor C\right)$
- $\lnot A \lor \left(B \lor C\right)$

## Explanation

The implication is true when $A$ is false or its consequent is true. The consequent is the disjunction of $B$ and $C$.
