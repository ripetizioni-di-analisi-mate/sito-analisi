Which of the five figures does *not* follow the rule shared by the other four?

::figura{tipo="opzioni" celle="freccia o; freccia r90 e | freccia r45 o; freccia r135 e | freccia r180 o; freccia r-90 e | freccia o; freccia r45 e | freccia r-45 o; freccia r45 e" etichette="numeri"}

- Figure 4
- Figure 1
- Figure 2
- Figure 5

## Explanation

Each figure contains two arrows, and the rule concerns the angle between their directions. In figures 1, 2, 3, and 5 the arrows are perpendicular, so their directions form an angle of $90°$. In figure 4 the left arrow points up and the right arrow points to the top right, so the angle is $45°$. Thus figure 4 alone does not follow the rule. Both 1 and 4 have the left arrow pointing up, so the direction of one arrow alone does not single out a figure.
