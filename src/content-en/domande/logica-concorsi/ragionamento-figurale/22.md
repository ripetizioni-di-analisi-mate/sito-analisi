Which figure completes the matrix?

- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="triangolo piccolo pieno; punto o"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="quadrato piccolo pieno; punto no"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="triangolo piccolo grigio; punto no"}

## Explanation

The shape depends on the column and the fill on the row, so the missing cell contains a solid triangle. The dot starts at the top left corner. Reading the cells row by row from left to right, it moves one position clockwise along the edge at each cell. In the eighth cell it is in the middle of the left side and therefore returns to the top left corner in the ninth. The required figure is the solid triangle with the dot at the top left. The solid triangle with the dot on the left leaves the dot in place. The solid square has the right dot position but repeats the shape of the second column.
