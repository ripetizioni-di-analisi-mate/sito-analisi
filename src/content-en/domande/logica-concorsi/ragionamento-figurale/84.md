In the rectangular grid the vertical segment separating the two middle unit squares has been erased. How many squares of any size have all four sides drawn?

::figura{tipo="linee" segmenti="1,1,1,3;1,1,3,1;1,3,1,5;1,3,3,3;1,5,1,7;1,5,3,5;1,7,3,7;3,1,3,3;3,1,5,1;3,3,3,5;3,3,5,3;3,5,3,7;3,5,5,5;3,7,5,7;5,1,5,3;5,1,7,1;5,3,7,3;5,5,5,7;5,5,7,5;5,7,7,7;7,1,7,3;7,1,9,1;7,3,7,5;7,3,9,3;7,5,7,7;7,5,9,5;7,7,9,7;9,1,9,3;9,3,9,5;9,5,9,7" titolo="Grid showing the segments to count"}

- $14$
- $16$
- $10$
- $12$

## Explanation

Of the twelve unit squares, ten have complete outlines. For side length two, the gap leaves only the two squares starting in the second column intact. Both squares of side length three contain the missing segment in their interior, so both remain intact. The total is $10 + 2 + 2 = 14$ squares.
