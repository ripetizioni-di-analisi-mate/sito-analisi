To complete a row, use the direction of the arrow in the first cell and the fill of the arrow in the second. Which arrow completes the third row?

::figura{tipo="matrice" celle="freccia grigio r0 | freccia pieno r90 | freccia pieno r0 | freccia vuoto r180 | freccia grigio r0 | freccia grigio r180 | freccia pieno r90 | freccia grigio r270 | ?" titolo="Matrix to complete"}

- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="freccia grigio r270"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="freccia pieno r90"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="freccia grigio r90"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="freccia vuoto r0"}

## Explanation

The first arrow in the third row points right. The second arrow supplies the gray fill, so the missing arrow is gray and points right.
