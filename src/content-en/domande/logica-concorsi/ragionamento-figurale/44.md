A structure consists of identical unit cubes stacked on a base with $3 \times 3$ positions. The figure shows the top view, with each number giving the number of cubes stacked at that position. How many cubes make up the structure?

::figura{tipo="matrice" celle="lettera:3 | lettera:1 | lettera:2 | lettera:2 | lettera:4 | lettera:1 | lettera:0 | lettera:2 | lettera:3" titolo="Top view of the structure"}

- $8$
- $27$
- $18$
- $36$

## Explanation

Each number in the **top view** gives the height of a stack, so the number of cubes is the sum of the nine numbers. Adding them by rows gives $6 + 7 + 5 = 18$. The answer $8$ counts one cube per occupied position, $27$ gives the number in a solid $3 \times 3 \times 3$ block, and $36$ multiplies the $9$ positions by the height of the tallest stack.
