Which figure is the mirror image of the figure shown across the horizontal axis of the cell?

::figura{tipo="serie" celle="squadra grigio; punto ne" titolo="The original figure"}

- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="squadra grigio specchio; punto no"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="squadra grigio r180; punto so"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="squadra grigio specchio-o; punto ne"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="squadra grigio specchio-o; punto se"}

## Explanation

**Reflection across the horizontal axis** swaps top and bottom and leaves left and right unchanged. The right angle of the triangle is at the bottom left, so its image has the right angle at the top left. The dot is in the top right corner and moves to the bottom right corner. Therefore, the image is the gray triangle with its right angle at the top left and its dot at the bottom right. The triangle with the right angle at the bottom right and the dot at the top left is the reflection across the vertical axis. The triangle with its right angle at the top left and the dot still at the top right reflects only the triangle.
