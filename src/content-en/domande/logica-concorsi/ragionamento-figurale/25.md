Four of the five figures are rotations of the same figure. Which figure is the odd one out?

::figura{tipo="opzioni" celle="semicerchio pieno; punto ne | semicerchio pieno r90; punto se | semicerchio pieno r180; punto so | semicerchio pieno; punto no | semicerchio pieno r-90; punto no" etichette="numeri"}

- Figure 2
- Figure 3
- Figure 5
- Figure 4

## Explanation

In figure 1 the arc of the semicircle faces up and the dot is at the top right. Figures 2, 3, and 5 are obtained by rotating figure 1 through $90°$ clockwise, $180°$, and $90°$ counterclockwise, respectively, since the dot rotates together with the semicircle. In figure 4 the arc faces up and the dot is at the top left, so figure 4 is the **mirror image** of figure 1 and no rotation makes them coincide. Figure 5 has the dot in the same position as figure 4, but it is figure 1 rotated $90°$ counterclockwise, with the arc facing left.
