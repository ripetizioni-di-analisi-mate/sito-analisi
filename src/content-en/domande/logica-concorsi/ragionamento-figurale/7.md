An illuminated sign displays one figure at a time according to a fixed rule. Which figure appears in place of the question mark?

::figura{tipo="serie" celle="squadra righe | squadra righe r45 | squadra righe r90 | ?" titolo="The first three figures on the sign"}

- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="squadra righe r135"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="squadra righe r180"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="squadra righe r90"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="squadra pieno r135"}

## Explanation

The shape and striped fill stay the same while the right triangle rotates $45°$ clockwise at each step. The third figure is rotated $90°$ from the first, so the fourth is rotated $135°$. After a clockwise **rotation** through $135°$, the right angle, originally at the bottom left, points up and the long side is horizontal at the bottom. The triangle with the right angle at the top right is rotated through $180°$, one step too far. The solid triangle has the correct orientation but a different fill.
