The cardboard is folded along the shared edges. If the circle is placed against the table, which symbol appears on the top face?

::figura{tipo="sviluppo" schema=".xx.|.x..|xx..|x..." celle="cerchio | quadrato pieno | croce grigio | anello | punto | x pieno" titolo="Net of a cube"}

- Gray cross
- Dot
- Ring
- Solid square

## Explanation

The circle, gray cross, and dot are three consecutive faces in the same column. The two end faces become **opposite faces**, so the dot is on top when the circle is on the bottom.
