The cube in the figure rests on a table and is rolled twice through a quarter turn to the right, about its bottom right edge. Which symbol ends up on the face resting on the table?

::figura{tipo="cubi" celle="anello / x / quadrato grigio" titolo="The cube before rolling"}

- The gray square
- The ring
- The diagonal cross
- The symbol that was on the face resting on the table at the start

## Explanation

In a quarter turn to the right the right face goes onto the table, the top face moves to the right and the face resting on the table moves to the left. The face toward the viewer stays where it is, because the cube turns about an edge perpendicular to that face. So after the first quarter turn the gray square is on the table and the ring is on the right face. With the second quarter turn the right face again goes onto the table, therefore at the end the ring is on the table. The gray square is the face on the table after only one quarter turn, the diagonal cross always stays at the front, and the face that was on the table at the start ends up on top.
