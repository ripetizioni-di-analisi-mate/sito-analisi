The cube is folded and placed on a table. The open circle and dot are on opposite faces. A plane cuts the cube halfway between these faces and parallel to both. How many symbols belong to faces crossed by the cut?

::figura{tipo="sviluppo" schema=".xx.|.x..|xx..|x..." celle="cerchio | quadrato pieno | croce grigio | anello | punto | x pieno" titolo="The six marked faces"}

- $2$
- $4$
- $3$
- $6$

## Explanation

The plane misses the two parallel faces on either side of it. Its square cross-section has one side on each of the four lateral faces, each marked with one symbol.
