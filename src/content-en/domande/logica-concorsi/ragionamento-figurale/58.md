The cardboard is folded along the shared edges. If the cube is placed with the circle on the table, which symbol appears on the top face?

::figura{tipo="sviluppo" schema=".xx.|.x..|xx..|x..." celle="cerchio | quadrato pieno | croce grigio | anello | punto | x pieno" titolo="Net of a cube"}

- Gray cross
- Dot
- Ring
- Solid square

## Explanation

In the net the circle, the gray cross and the dot occupy three consecutive squares of the same column. By the **opposite faces** rule, in three consecutive faces in a straight line the first and the third are opposite, so the circle is opposite the dot. Therefore, when the circle rests on the table, the dot appears on the top face. The other four faces, namely the solid square, the gray cross, the ring and the solid diagonal cross, form the sides of the cube. The gray cross, for example, shares an edge with the circle in the net, so in the cube it is adjacent to the face resting on the table and cannot be on top.
