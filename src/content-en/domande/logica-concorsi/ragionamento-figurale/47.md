The numbers give the heights of solid stacks of unit cubes. Looking horizontally from the side at the bottom of the drawing, how many small squares make up the front view?

::figura{tipo="matrice" celle="lettera:1 | lettera:4 | lettera:0 | lettera:3 | lettera:2 | lettera:3 | lettera:2 | lettera:1 | lettera:1" titolo="Top view with the front at the bottom"}

- $4$
- $8$
- $10$
- $12$

## Explanation

In the front view, stacks aligned in the direction of sight overlap. The greatest height in each grid column determines its visible height. The three columns in the front view are therefore $3$, $4$, and $3$ units high and show $10$ small squares.
