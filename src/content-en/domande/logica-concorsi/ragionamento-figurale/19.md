Which figure completes the matrix?

::figura{tipo="matrice" celle="croce piccolo x4 | croce piccolo x1 | croce piccolo x3 | cerchio piccolo x5 | cerchio piccolo x3 | cerchio piccolo x2 | rombo piccolo x6 | rombo piccolo x2 | ?"}

- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="rombo piccolo x8"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="rombo piccolo x2"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="cerchio piccolo x4"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="rombo piccolo x4"}

## Explanation

The shape is constant in each row, so the missing cell contains diamonds. The first row has $4$, $1$, and $3$ crosses, and the second has $5$, $3$, and $2$ circles. In both rows the count in the third cell is the **difference** between the counts in the first two, since $4 - 1 = 3$ and $5 - 3 = 2$. Therefore, the number of diamonds in the third row is $6 - 2 = 4$. The cell with $8$ diamonds comes from the sum $6 + 2$, which in the first row would give $5$ crosses instead of $3$.
