A program rotates the entire cell $90°$ counterclockwise and then through $180°$. Which drawing appears after both operations?

::figura{tipo="serie" celle="triangolo grigio r0; punto se" titolo="Drawing to be transformed"}

- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="triangolo grigio r180; punto no"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="triangolo grigio r270; punto ne"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="triangolo grigio r90; punto so"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="triangolo grigio r90; punto ne"}

## Explanation

The two **rotations** combine to give $90°$ clockwise. The upward-pointing vertex moves to the right, and the dot at the bottom right moves to the bottom left.
