The entire cell is rotated $90°$ counterclockwise and then through $180°$. Which drawing is obtained after both rotations?

::figura{tipo="serie" celle="triangolo grigio r0; punto se" titolo="Drawing to be transformed"}

- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="triangolo grigio r180; punto no"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="triangolo grigio r270; punto ne"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="triangolo grigio r90; punto so"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="triangolo grigio r90; punto ne"}

## Explanation

A $90°$ counterclockwise rotation followed by a $180°$ rotation is equivalent to a single **rotation** of $180° - 90° = 90°$ clockwise. Under this rotation the upward-pointing vertex moves to the right, and the dot at the bottom right moves to the bottom left. Therefore the resulting drawing has the vertex of the triangle pointing right and the dot at the bottom left.
