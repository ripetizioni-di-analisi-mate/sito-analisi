Which figure is obtained by rotating the figure shown $90°$ clockwise about the center of the cell?

::figura{tipo="serie" celle="freccia pieno; punto no" titolo="The figure to be rotated"}

- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="freccia pieno r-90; punto so"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="freccia pieno r90; punto no"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="freccia pieno r90; punto ne"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="freccia pieno r180; punto se"}

## Explanation

The clockwise **rotation** through $90°$ turns the entire cell, so the arrow's direction and the dot's position change together. The arrow points up before the rotation and points right after a quarter turn clockwise. The dot starts in the top left corner and moves to the top right corner under the same rotation. The required figure is therefore the solid right-pointing arrow with the dot at the top right. The right-pointing arrow with the dot still at the top left rotates the arrow alone, while the left-pointing arrow with the dot at the bottom left is the result of a counterclockwise rotation.
