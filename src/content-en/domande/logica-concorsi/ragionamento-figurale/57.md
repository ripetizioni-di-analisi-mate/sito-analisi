The triangle contains a segment parallel to the base and two segments connecting the vertices at the base to the opposite ends of the interior segment. How many triangles do the segments form?

::figura{tipo="linee" segmenti="5,1,1,9;1,9,9,9;9,9,5,1;3,5,7,5;1,9,7,5;9,9,3,5" titolo="Diagram for counting triangles"}

- $8$
- $10$
- $14$
- $12$

## Explanation

Group the triangles by their horizontal side. Each horizontal segment has two sloping segments starting at each end, so it forms $2 \cdot 2 = 4$ triangles. For triangles with no horizontal side, each choice of three of the four sloping segments forms a triangle, giving four more. The total is $4 + 4 + 4 = 12$.
