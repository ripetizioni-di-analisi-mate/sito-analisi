Which figure *cannot* be obtained by rotating the figure shown?

::figura{tipo="serie" celle="triangolo grigio; punto ne" titolo="The original figure"}

- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="triangolo grigio r90; punto se"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="triangolo grigio r180; punto so"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="triangolo grigio r90; punto ne"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="triangolo grigio r-90; punto no"}

## Explanation

In the original figure one vertex of the triangle points up and the dot is at the top right, on the side toward that vertex and to its right when looking in the direction it points. Rotating the figure turns the triangle and the dot together, so the dot always stays on the side toward the vertex and to its right. This arrangement holds when the vertex points right and the dot is at the bottom right, when the vertex points down and the dot is at the bottom left, and when the vertex points left and the dot is at the top left. In the figure with the vertex pointing right and the dot at the top right, the dot is to the left when looking in the direction of the vertex. The figure is therefore a **mirror image** and cannot be produced by rotation.
