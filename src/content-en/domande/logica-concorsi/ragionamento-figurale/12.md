Which figure continues the sequence?

::figura{tipo="serie" celle="quadrato grande; cerchio piccolo pieno | cerchio grande; quadrato piccolo grigio | quadrato grande; cerchio piccolo | cerchio grande; quadrato piccolo pieno | ?"}

- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="quadrato grande; cerchio piccolo grigio"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="quadrato grande; cerchio piccolo pieno"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="cerchio grande; quadrato piccolo grigio"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="quadrato grande; cerchio piccolo"}

## Explanation

The large square and the large circle alternate as the outer shape. The inner figure always has the other shape, so the fifth cell contains a large square with a small circle inside it. The inner figure cycles through solid, gray, and unfilled, a pattern with **period** $3$. The fifth cell therefore has the same fill as the second, gray. The required figure is the large square with the small gray circle. The square with a solid circle repeats the first cell, as if the fill had period $4$. The large circle with a gray square has the correct fill but breaks the pattern of alternating shapes.
