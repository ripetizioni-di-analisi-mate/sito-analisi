From one cell to the next a dot disappears and the shape advances a quarter turn in the same direction. Which figure comes fourth?

- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="pentagono pieno r270; punto se"}

- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="pentagono pieno r270; punto ne"}

## Explanation

The vertex of the pentagon points down, then right, then up, and finally left. The last dot in the group disappears at each step, leaving the dot at the top right in the fourth cell.
