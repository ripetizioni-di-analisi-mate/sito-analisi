Which figure is in the fourth position of the sequence?

::figura{tipo="serie" celle="esagono | pentagono grigio | quadrato | ?" titolo="The first three figures in the sequence"}

- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="triangolo"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="rombo grigio"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="quadrato grigio"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="triangolo grigio"}

## Explanation

From one cell to the next the **number of sides** of the figure decreases by one, because the hexagon has $6$ sides, the pentagon $5$ and the square $4$. So the fourth cell contains a figure with $3$ sides, that is, a triangle. The fill alternates between empty and gray, and the cells in even positions are gray, therefore the triangle in the fourth cell is gray. The empty triangle has the right number of sides but breaks the alternation of the fill. The gray rhombus and the gray square have $4$ sides, like the figure in the third cell, so they do not follow the decrease in the number of sides.
