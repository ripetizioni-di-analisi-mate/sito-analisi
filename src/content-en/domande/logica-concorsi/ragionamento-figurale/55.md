The interior lines of the triangle start at the top vertex, except for one segment parallel to the base that crosses them all. How many triangles are bounded by the segments shown?

::figura{tipo="linee" segmenti="1,9,9,9;5,1,1,9;5,1,3,9;5,1,5,9;5,1,7,9;5,1,9,9;3,5,7,5" titolo="Diagram for counting triangles"}

- $21$
- $10$
- $15$
- $20$

## Explanation

All the triangles have the top vertex because the two horizontal segments are parallel. On the base, choose two of the five intersections with the sloping segments, giving $4 + 3 + 2 + 1 = 10$ triangles. There are another five intersection points on the shorter horizontal segment, so the total number of triangles is $10 + 10 = 20$.
