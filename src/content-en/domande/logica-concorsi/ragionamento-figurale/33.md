Which figure is the reflection of the letter J across the horizontal axis?

::figura{tipo="serie" celle="lettera:J" titolo="The letter J"}

- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="lettera:J specchio"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="lettera:J r180"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="lettera:J specchio-o"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="lettera:J r-90"}

## Explanation

**Reflection across the horizontal axis** swaps top and bottom and leaves left and right unchanged. The J has its stem on the right and its hook at the bottom, curving to the left. The reflected letter therefore keeps the stem on the right, and the hook moves to the top, still curving left. The J with the hook at the top and the stem on the left is the J rotated through $180°$. The J with the stem on the left and the hook at the bottom curving right is the reflection across the vertical axis.
