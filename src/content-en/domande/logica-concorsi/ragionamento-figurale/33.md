One arrow points up and one points right, as shown. Each is reflected in the vertical axis of its own cell. How many arrows change direction?

::figura{tipo="serie" celle="freccia pieno | freccia pieno r90" titolo="The two initial directions"}

- $0$
- $2$
- $1$
- It depends on the lengths of the arrows

## Explanation

Reflection in a vertical axis preserves the upward component and reverses the horizontal component. Only the right-pointing arrow changes direction.
