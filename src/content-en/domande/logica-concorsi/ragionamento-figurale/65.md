The two diagonals of the smaller rectangles meet on the bottom edge of the large rectangle. How many triangles are bounded by the outline and the interior segments?

::figura{tipo="linee" segmenti="1,2,9,2;9,2,9,8;9,8,1,8;1,8,1,2;5,2,5,8;1,2,5,8;5,8,9,2" titolo="Diagram for counting triangles"}

- $8$
- $4$
- $5$
- $6$

## Explanation

Each diagonal divides one of the two small rectangles into two triangles. Together the two diagonals also form a larger triangle whose third side is the top edge of the large rectangle. There are $2 + 2 + 1 = 5$ triangles in all.
