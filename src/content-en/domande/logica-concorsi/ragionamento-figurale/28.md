The small shapes occupy two corners in each panel. Which panel stands out because of the relative positions of those shapes?

::figura{tipo="opzioni" celle="stella no; cerchio pieno se | quadrato ne; triangolo pieno so | cerchio so; rombo pieno ne | croce no; stella pieno ne | triangolo se; quadrato pieno no" etichette="numeri"}

- Figure 1
- Figure 4
- Figure 2
- Figure 5

## Explanation

Each figure has two small shapes, one unfilled and one solid, in two corners of the cell. In figures 1, 2, 3, and 5 the shapes occupy opposite corners, the ends of the same diagonal. In figure 4 the cross is at the top left and the solid star at the top right, so both shapes lie along the same side. Thus figure 4 is the only one that does not follow the rule. The shapes themselves do not single out one figure: each figure contains a solid shape and an unfilled shape, always different from each other.
