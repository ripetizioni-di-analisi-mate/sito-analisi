The first four figures of a sequence are shown, and the sequence continues according to the same rule. Which figure is fifteenth in the sequence?

::figura{tipo="serie" celle="semicerchio grigio | semicerchio grigio r-45 | semicerchio grigio r-90 | semicerchio grigio r-135 | ?" titolo="The first four figures, with a question mark for the fifteenth"}

- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="semicerchio grigio r45"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="semicerchio grigio r-90"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="semicerchio grigio r90"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="semicerchio grigio"}

## Explanation

The semicircle rotates $45°$ counterclockwise at each step, so after $8$ steps it makes a full turn and the rule has **period** $8$. There are $14$ steps from the first figure to the fifteenth. Since $14 = 1 \cdot 8 + 6$, the semicircle makes a full turn and $6$ further steps, corresponding to a further rotation through $6 \cdot 45° = 270°$ counterclockwise. Rotating $270°$ counterclockwise is equivalent to rotating $90°$ clockwise, so in the fifteenth figure the arc faces right. The semicircle with the arc facing left is the result of the same rotation clockwise. The semicircle with the arc facing the top right comes from counting $15$ steps instead of $14$.
