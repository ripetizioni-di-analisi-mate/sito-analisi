Which figure continues the sequence?

::figura{tipo="serie" celle="cerchio grigio n | cerchio pieno no | cerchio o | cerchio grigio so | ?"}

- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="cerchio pieno se"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="cerchio s"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="cerchio pieno s"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="cerchio pieno so"}

## Explanation

The circle moves one position at a time counterclockwise around the edge, from the top middle to the top left corner, then to the middle of the left side and the bottom left corner. Therefore, the circle is at the bottom middle in the fifth cell. The fill cycles through gray, solid, and unfilled, so the gray circle in the fourth cell is followed by a solid circle. The required figure is the solid circle at the bottom middle. The solid circle at the bottom right moves two positions instead of one, and the unfilled circle at the bottom middle has the correct position but the wrong fill.
