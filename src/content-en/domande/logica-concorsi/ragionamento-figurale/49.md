Four arrows point toward the dot or directly away from it. In one figure the arrow is perpendicular to the direction from the center to the dot. Which figure is it?

::figura{tipo="opzioni" celle="freccia piccolo r45; punto ne | freccia piccolo r90; punto o | freccia piccolo r180; punto e | freccia piccolo r315; punto se | freccia piccolo r270; punto o" titolo="Five numbered figures" etichette="numeri"}

- Figure 5
- Figure 3
- Figure 2
- Figure 4

## Explanation

In the required figure the arrow is vertical and points down, while the dot is to the right. The direction to the dot is horizontal, so it makes a **right angle** with the arrow.
