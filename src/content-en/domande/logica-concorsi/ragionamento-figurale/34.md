Which figure is obtained by rotating the figure shown $90°$ counterclockwise about the center of the cell?

::figura{tipo="serie" celle="freccia pieno r45; lettera:R so" titolo="The figure to be rotated"}

- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="freccia pieno r135; lettera:R r90 no"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="freccia pieno r-45; lettera:R r-90 se"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="freccia pieno r-45; lettera:R se"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="freccia pieno r-45; lettera:R specchio se"}

## Explanation

A counterclockwise rotation through $90°$ turns the whole figure, changing the arrow's direction, the position of the letter, and the orientation of the letter together. The arrow points to the top right before the rotation and to the top left afterward. The R moves from the bottom left to the bottom right corner and also turns a quarter turn counterclockwise, with its stem horizontal at the bottom. The figure with the upright R at the bottom right moves the letter without rotating it. The figure with the reflected R at the bottom right is the reflection across the vertical axis, which gives the same arrow direction but is not a **rotation**.
