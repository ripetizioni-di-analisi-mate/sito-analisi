A square cell has a dot at its centre and one at the top. After a quarter-turn the top dot is on the right. Which property has remained unchanged for both dots?

::figura{tipo="serie" celle="punto; punto n" titolo="A central dot and an outer dot"}

- Distance from the top side
- Distance from the centre
- Distance from the right side
- Position relative to the vertical diameter

## Explanation

The centre is the fixed point of the rotation and each dot follows an arc of constant radius. The outer dot changes its position relative to the sides and to the vertical diameter.
