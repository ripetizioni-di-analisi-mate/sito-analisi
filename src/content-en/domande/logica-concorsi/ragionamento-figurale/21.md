In each row of the matrix, the third figure is obtained from the first two by the same rule. Which figure completes the matrix?

::figura{tipo="matrice" celle="linea medio o | linea medio n r90 | linea medio o; linea medio n r90 | linea medio s r90 | linea medio e | linea medio s r90; linea medio e | linea r45 | linea r-45 | ?"}

- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="linea r45"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="linea r45; linea r-45"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="linea r45; linea r-45; linea"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="linea; linea r90"}

## Explanation

In each row the third cell contains the segment from the first cell and the segment from the second, so it is the **superposition** of the first two. In the third row the first cell contains the diagonal sloping up to the right, and the second contains the diagonal sloping down to the right. The missing cell therefore contains both diagonals, forming a diagonal cross. A cross made of a vertical and a horizontal segment uses segments absent from the third row, and the cell with three segments adds a middle vertical segment.
