In each column of the matrix, the bottom figure is obtained from the two figures above it, always by the same rule. Which figure completes the matrix?

::figura{tipo="matrice" celle="linea medio o | linea r45 | linea | linea medio n r90 | linea r-45 | linea medio s r90 | linea medio o; linea medio n r90 | linea r45; linea r-45 | ?"}

- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="linea medio s r90"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="linea; linea medio s r90"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="linea medio o; linea medio n r90; linea r45; linea r-45"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="linea; linea medio n r90"}

## Explanation

In the first column the bottom cell contains the left side from the top cell and the top side from the middle cell, and in the second column it contains the two diagonals from the cells above it. So the third cell of each column is the **superposition** of the two cells above it. In the third column the top cell contains the middle vertical segment and the middle cell contains the bottom side, therefore the missing cell contains these two segments. The cell with four segments superimposes the first two cells of the third row, but the rule does not hold along the rows, because in the first row the middle vertical segment is not the superposition of the left side and the diagonal. The cell with only the bottom side leaves out the segment from the first row, and the cell with the top side replaces the bottom side with the opposite one.
