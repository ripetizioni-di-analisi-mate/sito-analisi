The figure shows a rectangle with one diagonal and two segments joining the midpoints of opposite sides. How many triangles have all their sides drawn?

::figura{tipo="linee" segmenti="1,2,9,2;9,2,9,8;9,8,1,8;1,8,1,2;1,2,9,8;5,2,5,8;1,5,9,5" titolo="Diagram for counting triangles"}

- $8$
- $6$
- $10$
- $4$

## Explanation

Every triangle must use the diagonal to have a sloping side. The portions of the diagonal to the left and right of the center each serve as a side of two triangles, while the entire diagonal serves as a side of two large triangles. The total is $2 + 2 + 2 = 6$.
