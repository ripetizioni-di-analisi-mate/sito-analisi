A dot follows the edge of the square in steps of changing length. Which tile matches both the next step and the next fill of the square?

::figura{tipo="serie" celle="quadrato piccolo; punto no | quadrato piccolo grigio; punto n | quadrato piccolo pieno; punto e | quadrato piccolo; punto so | ?"}

- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="quadrato piccolo grigio; punto ne"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="quadrato piccolo grigio; punto n"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="quadrato piccolo grigio; punto o"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="quadrato piccolo pieno; punto ne"}

## Explanation

The square cycles through unfilled, gray, and solid, so in the fifth cell it is gray as in the second. The dot moves clockwise around the eight positions on the edge. At each step it advances one more position than on the preceding step: $1$, then $2$, then $3$ positions. From the fourth to the fifth cell it therefore advances $4$ positions from the bottom left to the top right corner. The required figure is the gray square with the dot at the top right. The gray square with the dot at the top middle repeats the advance of $3$ positions, and the solid square with the dot at the top right has the correct position but the wrong fill.
