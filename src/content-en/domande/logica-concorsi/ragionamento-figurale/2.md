The arrow shown is rotated $135°$ counterclockwise. Which figure results?

::figura{tipo="serie" celle="freccia grigio r90" titolo="The arrow before rotation"}

- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="freccia grigio r-45"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="freccia grigio r-135"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="freccia grigio r45"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="freccia grigio r-90"}

## Explanation

The arrow points right. Rotating it $90°$ counterclockwise makes it point up, and a further $45°$ in the same direction makes it tilt left. So after the $135° = 90° + 45°$ rotation the arrow points to the top left. The arrow pointing to the bottom left results from the same **rotation** clockwise, while the arrow pointing to the top right corresponds to a rotation of only $45°$. The arrow pointing left has rotated through $180°$.
