- Arrangement 4
- Arrangement 2
- Arrangement 3
- Arrangement 1

## Explanation

The four faces in a row form a band around the cube. The two squares attached to opposite sides of the row close the two openings of the band. Four squares arranged in a square block would make four faces meet at one point, while five squares in a line would overlap.
