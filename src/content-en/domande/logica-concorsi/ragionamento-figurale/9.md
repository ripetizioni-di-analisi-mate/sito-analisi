The arrow changes direction while dots disappear from the corners of some cells. Which panel continues both changes correctly?

- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="freccia pieno; punto no"}

- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="freccia pieno r180"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="freccia pieno r180; punto no"}

## Explanation

The sequence has two **independent rules**. The arrow turns $90°$ counterclockwise at every step, pointing first right, then up, then left. Thus it points down in the fourth cell. One dot disappears at each step in the order bottom left, bottom right, and top right, so only the dot at the top left remains in the fourth cell. The required figure is the downward-pointing arrow with only the top left dot. An upward-pointing arrow results from rotating clockwise, and the downward-pointing arrow with the dot at the top right removes the wrong dot.
