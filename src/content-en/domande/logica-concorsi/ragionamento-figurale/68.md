The letter R in the first figure is compared with the five numbered figures. How many of them can be obtained from the R by a rotation?

::figura{tipo="serie" celle="lettera:R" titolo="The starting letter"}

::figura{tipo="opzioni" celle="lettera:R r90 | lettera:R specchio | lettera:R specchio r90 | lettera:R r180 | lettera:R specchio r-90" etichette="numeri"}

- $2$
- $3$
- $4$
- $5$

## Explanation

By the theorem on **rotated figures and mirror images**, no rotation turns the R into its mirror image. So each figure is turned mentally until the stem is vertical again with the slanted leg at the bottom, and one checks on which side of the stem the bowl lies. In figures 1 and 4 the bowl returns to the right of the stem, as in the original letter, therefore they are the R rotated $90°$ clockwise and $180°$. In figures 2, 3 and 5 the bowl lies to the left of the stem, so they are mirror images of the R, and in figures 3 and 5 they are also rotated. The figures obtained by a rotation are therefore $2$, and the answer $4$ also counts the rotated mirror images, which have a horizontal stem like figure 1.
