The net shown is folded into a cube. Which pair consists of two opposite faces?

::figura{tipo="sviluppo" schema="xx..|.xxx|...x" celle="anello | quadrato | cerchio grigio | croce | punto | x pieno" titolo="The net to be folded"}

- The ring and the cross
- The ring and the dot
- The square and the gray circle
- The cross and the dot

## Explanation

The ring and square are side by side in the first row, the gray circle is below the square, and the cross is next to the gray circle. Thus the ring, square, gray circle, and cross form a Z. By the rule for **opposite faces**, the first and last faces of a Z are opposite, so the ring is opposite the cross. The square shares a side with the gray circle, as does the cross with the dot, so the faces in each of these pairs are adjacent. The gray circle, cross, and dot are three consecutive faces in a straight line, so the dot is opposite the gray circle rather than the ring.
