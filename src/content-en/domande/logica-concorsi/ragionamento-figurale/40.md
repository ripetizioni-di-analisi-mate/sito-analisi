The net has been cut out in one piece. Its faces are folded along shared sides and the cube is closed with tape. How many cube edges need tape because the two faces were not connected by a fold in the card?

::figura{tipo="sviluppo" schema="xx..|.xxx|...x" celle="anello | quadrato | cerchio grigio | croce | punto | x pieno" titolo="Folds and edges to be joined"}

- $7$
- $5$
- $12$
- $14$

## Explanation

The six squares have five shared sides in the net, giving five folds. A cube has twelve edges. The remaining $12-5=7$ are formed by bringing two free edges together, so they need tape.
