In each row of the matrix, the third figure is obtained from the first two by the same rule. Which figure completes the matrix?

::figura{tipo="matrice" celle="cerchio no; triangolo ne | triangolo ne; quadrato so | cerchio no; triangolo ne; quadrato so | rombo n; cerchio s | cerchio s; stella e | rombo n; cerchio s; stella e | quadrato no; croce e | croce e; cerchio so; rombo n | ?"}

- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="quadrato no; cerchio so; rombo n"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="croce e"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="quadrato no; croce e; cerchio so; rombo n"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="quadrato no; croce e; cerchio so"}

## Explanation

In the first row the top right triangle appears in the first two cells and is also present in the third. The same happens with the circle at the bottom in the second row. Therefore, the third cell of each row is the **union** of the first two and contains every shape present in at least one of them. In the third row the first two cells contain the square at the top left, cross on the right, circle at the bottom left, and diamond at the top. The cross appears in both. Therefore, the missing cell contains all four figures, with the cross appearing only once. The cell without the cross shows the symmetric difference, which removes shared figures. The cell containing only the cross shows just the shared figure.
