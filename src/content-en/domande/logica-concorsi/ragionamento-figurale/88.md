The sign shows an L above an F. Which figure results from reflecting the sign across its central horizontal axis?

::figura{tipo="serie" celle="lettera:L n; lettera:F s" titolo="Sign with two letters, one above the other"}

- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="lettera:F specchio-o n; lettera:L specchio-o s"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="lettera:L specchio-o n; lettera:F specchio-o s"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="lettera:F n; lettera:L s"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="lettera:F r180 n; lettera:L r180 s"}

## Explanation

**Reflection across the horizontal axis** swaps top and bottom and preserves left and right. The F moves up, the L moves down, and the strokes of each letter are flipped from top to bottom.
