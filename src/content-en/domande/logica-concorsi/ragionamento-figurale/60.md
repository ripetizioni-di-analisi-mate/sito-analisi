The image is first turned upside down by a rotation through $180°$ and then reflected across the central horizontal axis. Which cell results?

- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="semicerchio grigio r180; punto so"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="semicerchio grigio r180; punto no"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="semicerchio grigio r0; punto so"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="semicerchio grigio r0; punto no"}

## Explanation

The half turn makes the arc face down and moves the dot to the bottom left. **Reflection across the horizontal axis** brings both back to the top while keeping the dot on the left.
