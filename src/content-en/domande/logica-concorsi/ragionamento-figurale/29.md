The letter P is reflected first across the vertical axis and then across the horizontal axis. Which figure results?

::figura{tipo="serie" celle="lettera:P" titolo="The letter P"}

- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="lettera:P r180"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="lettera:P specchio"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="lettera:P specchio-o"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="lettera:P"}

## Explanation

The P has a stem on the left and a loop attached on the right to the upper part of the stem. **Reflection across the vertical axis** swaps left and right, moving the stem to the right and the loop to the top left. Reflection across the horizontal axis then swaps top and bottom, so the stem stays on the right and the loop moves to the bottom left. The result coincides with the P rotated through $180°$, as stated by the result on **rotated figures and mirror images**. The P with its stem on the right and its loop at the top results from the first reflection alone. The P with its stem on the left and its loop at the bottom results from the second alone. Choosing the original P assumes the reflections cancel. Two consecutive reflections cancel when they are across the same line.
