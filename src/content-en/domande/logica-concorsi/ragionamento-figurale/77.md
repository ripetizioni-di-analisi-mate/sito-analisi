In the matrix each step to the right turns the right triangle a quarter turn clockwise. Moving down a row also changes the fill. Which figure is missing?

::figura{tipo="matrice" celle="squadra pieno r0 | squadra pieno r90 | squadra pieno r180 | squadra grigio r180 | squadra grigio r270 | squadra grigio r0 | squadra vuoto r0 | squadra vuoto r90 | ?" titolo="Matrix to complete"}

- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="squadra vuoto r270"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="squadra pieno r180"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="squadra vuoto r180"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="squadra vuoto r0"}

## Explanation

In the third row the triangle goes from its initial orientation to a quarter turn and then to a half turn. Since the shapes in the third row are unfilled, the missing triangle also has only its outline.
