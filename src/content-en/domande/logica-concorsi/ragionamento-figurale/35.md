The net shown is folded into a cube. Which face is opposite the face with the circle?

::figura{tipo="sviluppo" schema="x...|xxxx|x..." celle="cerchio | croce pieno | quadrato grigio | anello | punto | x" titolo="The net to be folded"}

- The diagonal cross
- The solid cross
- The gray square
- The ring

## Explanation

The circle, solid cross, and diagonal cross are three consecutive faces in a straight line in the first column of the net. By the rule for **opposite faces**, the first and third faces in such a line are opposite, so the circle is opposite the diagonal cross. The solid cross shares a side with the circle, so their faces are adjacent on the cube. In the middle row the solid cross, gray square, ring, and dot are in a straight line, so the ring is opposite the solid cross and the gray square is opposite the dot. Each face has only one opposite, so neither the ring nor the gray square is opposite the circle.
