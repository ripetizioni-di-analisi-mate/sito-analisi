In the top view each number gives the height of a stack of unit cubes. The first layer rests on the table. How many cubes are in the third layer?

::figura{tipo="matrice" celle="lettera:3 | lettera:0 | lettera:2 | lettera:1 | lettera:3 | lettera:2 | lettera:2 | lettera:1 | lettera:4" titolo="Stack heights viewed from above"}

- $3$
- $6$
- $4$
- $2$

## Explanation

Every stack at least three cubes high contributes exactly one cube to the third layer. Two stacks are three cubes high and one is four cubes high, so the third layer contains $3$ cubes.
