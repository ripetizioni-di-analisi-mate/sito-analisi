A rotation takes the dot from the middle of the left side to the middle of the top side. Which drawing is obtained by applying the same rotation to the whole cell?

::figura{tipo="serie" celle="freccia pieno piccolo r180; punto o" titolo="Drawing to be transformed"}

- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="freccia pieno piccolo r270; punto o"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="freccia pieno piccolo r270; punto n"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="freccia pieno piccolo r180; punto n"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="freccia pieno piccolo r90; punto n"}

## Explanation

Moving the dot from the left side to the top requires a clockwise **rotation** through $90°$. The downward-pointing arrow rotates through the same angle and ends up pointing left.
