Which figure completes the matrix?

::figura{tipo="matrice" celle="rombo | pentagono | croce | rombo grigio | pentagono grigio | croce grigio | rombo pieno | pentagono pieno | ?"}

- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="croce grigio"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="croce pieno"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="pentagono pieno"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="croce"}

## Explanation

The shape is constant down each column: diamond in the first, pentagon in the second, and cross in the third. The fill is constant across each row: unfilled in the first, gray in the second, and solid in the third. Thus the missing cell in the third row and third column contains a solid cross. The gray cross has the fill of the second row, and the solid pentagon repeats the preceding cell.
