A sign contains the letters F and R as shown. Which figure shows the reflection of the sign across a vertical axis?

::figura{tipo="serie" celle="lettera:F medio o; lettera:R medio e" titolo="The sign"}

- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="lettera:F specchio medio o; lettera:R specchio medio e"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="lettera:R medio o; lettera:F medio e"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="lettera:R r180 medio o; lettera:F r180 medio e"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="lettera:R specchio medio o; lettera:F specchio medio e"}

## Explanation

**Reflection across the vertical axis** swaps left and right throughout the figure. Thus the R on the right moves to the left, the F moves to the right, and each letter is itself reflected, with its strokes extending in the direction opposite to those of the original. The image therefore has a reflected R on the left and a reflected F on the right. The figure with the reflected F on the left and the reflected R on the right reflects the letters without exchanging their places. The figure with the letters upside down is a rotation of the sign through $180°$.
