Which transformation takes the first cell shown to the second?

::figura{tipo="serie" celle="pentagono grigio; punto ne | pentagono grigio r90; punto se" titolo="The cell before and after the transformation"}

- A $90°$ counterclockwise rotation
- A $90°$ clockwise rotation
- A reflection across the horizontal axis
- A rotation through $180°$

## Explanation

In the first cell a vertex of the pentagon points up and the dot is at the top right. In the second, the vertex points right and the dot is at the bottom right. A clockwise **rotation** through $90°$ turns the vertex from up to right and moves the top right corner of the cell to the bottom right, so it takes the first cell to the second. A counterclockwise rotation would turn the vertex to the left. Reflection across the horizontal axis would move the dot to the bottom right but turn the vertex downward. A rotation through $180°$ would move the dot to the bottom left.
