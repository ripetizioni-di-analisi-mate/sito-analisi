The large symbols in a sequence alternate between two shapes, while the small symbol repeats its own cycle of three fills. Which cell comes immediately after the five shown?

::figura{tipo="serie" celle="rombo grande; anello piccolo vuoto | quadrato grande; anello piccolo grigio | rombo grande; anello piccolo pieno | quadrato grande; anello piccolo vuoto | rombo grande; anello piccolo grigio | ?" titolo="Figure sequence to complete"}

- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="quadrato grande; anello piccolo pieno"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="rombo grande; anello piccolo pieno"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="quadrato grande; anello piccolo vuoto"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="quadrato grande; anello piccolo grigio"}

## Explanation

The outer shapes alternate between a diamond and a square, so the sixth cell has a square. The ring has **period** $3$ and repeats the fill of the third cell, which is solid.
