The display shows only groups of dots. Which group continues the numerical sequence encoded by the five cells?

::figura{tipo="serie" celle="punto piccolo x1 | punto piccolo x1 | punto piccolo x2 | punto piccolo x3 | punto piccolo x5 | ?"}

- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="punto piccolo x7"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="punto piccolo x8"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="punto piccolo x9"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="punto piccolo x6"}

## Explanation

The number of dots increases by $0$, $1$, $1$, and $2$, so the increase is not constant. Each count is the sum of the preceding two, since $1 + 1 = 2$, $1 + 2 = 3$, and $2 + 3 = 5$. Therefore, the sixth cell contains $3 + 5 = 8$ dots. The cell with $7$ dots repeats the last increase of $2$, and the cell with $6$ dots adds just one dot.
