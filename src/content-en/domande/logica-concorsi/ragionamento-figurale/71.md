A completed cube is cut along the four edges of its ring face. Look at the original net. How many of these four cuts reopen an original fold in the card?

::figura{tipo="sviluppo" schema="..x.|xxxx|.x.." celle="cerchio | quadrato pieno | croce grigio | anello | punto | x pieno" titolo="The face to remove and its neighbours"}

- $1$
- $2$
- $3$
- $4$

## Explanation

In the net the ring has a square directly above it, one on its left and one on its right. These three sides are original folds. Its lower side is free in the net and meets another edge only when the cube is closed.
