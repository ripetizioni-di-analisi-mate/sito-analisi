Which pair of symbols must lie on two faces that do not touch after folding?

::figura{tipo="sviluppo" schema="..x.|xxxx|.x.." celle="cerchio | quadrato pieno | croce grigio | anello | punto | x pieno" titolo="Net of a cube"}

- Solid square and gray cross
- Circle and ring
- Circle and solid diagonal cross
- Ring and dot

## Explanation

The circle is above the ring, the ring is next to the gray cross, and the gray cross is above the solid diagonal cross, so these four faces form a **Z** in the net. By the **opposite faces** rule, the first and last faces of a Z are opposite, therefore the circle and the solid diagonal cross do not touch. The circle and the ring share an edge in the net, and so do the solid square and the gray cross and the ring and the dot, so these pairs consist of adjacent faces. In the row of four faces the solid square is opposite the ring and the gray cross is opposite the dot.
