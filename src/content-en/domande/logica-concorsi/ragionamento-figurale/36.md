The net shown is folded into a cube. Which face is opposite the face with the solid square?

::figura{tipo="sviluppo" schema="xx..|.xxx|..x." celle="anello | quadrato pieno | punto | croce grigio | x | cerchio pieno" titolo="The net to be folded"}

- The gray cross
- The dot
- The diagonal cross
- The solid circle

## Explanation

The solid square and the dot lie in a column, the dot and gray cross are side by side, and the gray cross and solid circle again lie in a column, so the four faces form a Z. By the rule for **opposite faces**, the first and last faces of a Z are opposite. Thus the solid square is opposite the solid circle. The dot shares a side with the solid square, so their faces are adjacent on the cube. The ring, solid square, dot, and gray cross also form a Z, so the gray cross is opposite the ring. The dot, gray cross, and diagonal cross are three consecutive faces on a straight line, so the diagonal cross is opposite the dot rather than the solid square.
