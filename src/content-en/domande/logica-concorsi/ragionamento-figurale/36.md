An ant starts at the centre of the filled-square face and wants to reach the centre of the filled-circle face of the cube whose net is shown. At every step it may cross to a face sharing an edge. How many intermediate faces can it choose for a route of exactly two steps?

::figura{tipo="sviluppo" schema="xx..|.xxx|..x." celle="anello | quadrato pieno | punto | croce grigio | x | cerchio pieno" titolo="Net of the cube for the ant’s route"}

- $1$
- $2$
- $3$
- $4$

## Explanation

The filled square, dot, grey cross and filled circle form a zigzag in the net. The first and last faces become opposite. Each of the four side faces shares an edge with both and gives a different two-step route.
