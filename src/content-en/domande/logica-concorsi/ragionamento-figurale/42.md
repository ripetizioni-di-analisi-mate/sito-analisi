How many triangles are in the figure made up of a square, its two diagonals, and the two segments joining the midpoints of opposite sides?

::figura{tipo="linee" segmenti="1,1,9,1;9,1,9,9;9,9,1,9;1,9,1,1;1,1,9,9;9,1,1,9;5,1,5,9;1,5,9,5" titolo="The square with its diagonals and the segments joining the midpoints of opposite sides"}

- $8$
- $16$
- $12$
- $14$

## Explanation

Count the triangles by size. The diagonals and the two segments joining the midpoints of opposite sides divide the square into $8$ small triangles. Two adjacent small triangles form a triangle with a side of the square as its base and the center as its vertex. There are $4$ of these, one per side. Each diagonal divides the square into two large triangles, so there are $2 \cdot 2 = 4$ large triangles. Thus there are $8 + 4 + 4 = 16$ triangles. Counting only the small ones gives $8$, and overlooking the large ones gives $12$. Counting only one large triangle per diagonal gives $8 + 4 + 2 = 14$.
