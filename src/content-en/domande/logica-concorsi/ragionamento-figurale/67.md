Each row shows unfilled circles. The final cell in the row has as many circles as the two cells to its left combined. Which drawing is missing?

::figura{tipo="matrice" celle="cerchio x1 | cerchio x3 | cerchio x4 | cerchio x2 | cerchio x1 | cerchio x3 | cerchio x2 | cerchio x3 | ?" titolo="Matrix to complete"}

- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="cerchio x3"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="cerchio x5"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="cerchio x6"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="cerchio x4"}

## Explanation

The first two cells in the last row show two and three circles. The final cell must contain $2 + 3 = 5$.
