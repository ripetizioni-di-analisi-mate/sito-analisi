Two dots occupy the upper-left and lower-right corners of a square tile as shown. What is the smallest positive rotation that maps the set of dots onto itself?

::figura{tipo="serie" celle="punto no; punto se" titolo="The two identical dots on the tile"}

- $45^\circ$
- $180^\circ$
- $90^\circ$
- $270^\circ$

## Explanation

A quarter-turn moves the dots to the other diagonal. A half-turn exchanges them, preserving the set of occupied positions.
