Which figure is obtained by rotating the figure shown through $180°$ about the center of the cell?

::figura{tipo="serie" celle="lettera:G; punto so" titolo="The figure to be rotated"}

- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="lettera:G specchio-o; punto no"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="lettera:G r180; punto ne"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="lettera:G r180; punto so"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="lettera:G specchio; punto se"}

## Explanation

Rotating through $180°$ turns the letter upside down and moves every point of the cell to the position opposite it across the center. The dot therefore moves from the bottom left to the top right corner. The upside-down G opens to the left, since the half **rotation** swaps both left with right and top with bottom. The figure that leaves the dot at the bottom left while turning the G upside down rotates only the letter. The figure with the G opening to the right and the dot at the top left is the reflection across the horizontal axis.
