A die has its symbols arranged according to the net shown. Which cube is the die?

::figura{tipo="sviluppo" schema="x...|xxxx|...x" celle="punto | croce grigio | anello | quadrato | cerchio pieno | x" titolo="The net of the die"}

- ::figura{tipo="cubi" celle="punto / anello / croce grigio"}
- ::figura{tipo="cubi" celle="punto / croce grigio / quadrato"}
- ::figura{tipo="cubi" celle="anello / punto / cerchio pieno"}
- ::figura{tipo="cubi" celle="punto / croce grigio / anello"}

## Explanation

The gray cross, ring, square, and solid circle form a straight line in the middle row of the net, so the gray cross is opposite the square and the ring is opposite the solid circle. Eliminate the two cubes showing the members of either pair together. In the net the dot, gray cross, and ring meet at the bottom right corner of the dot's face, and their clockwise order around that corner is dot, ring, gray cross. By the rule for **faces around a vertex**, the same order holds on the die from top to right to front, so the dot is on top, the ring on the right, and the gray cross in front. The cube with the dot on top, ring in front, and gray cross on the right has the reverse order.
