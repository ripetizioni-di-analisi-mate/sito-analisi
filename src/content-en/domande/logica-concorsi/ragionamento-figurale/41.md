A structure consists of identical unit cubes stacked on a base with $3 \times 3$ positions. The figure shows the top view, with each number giving the number of cubes stacked at that position. How many cubes are *not* visible from above?

::figura{tipo="matrice" celle="lettera:2 | lettera:3 | lettera:1 | lettera:1 | lettera:4 | lettera:2 | lettera:3 | lettera:1 | lettera:2" titolo="Top view of the structure"}

- $19$
- $9$
- $15$
- $10$

## Explanation

Only the top cube in each stack is visible from above. The number of visible cubes therefore equals the number of occupied positions, $9$. The total number of cubes is the sum of the numbers in the **top view**, $2 + 3 + 1 + 1 + 4 + 2 + 3 + 1 + 2 = 19$. Thus $19 - 9 = 10$ cubes are hidden. The answer $19$ gives the total number of cubes, $9$ gives the number visible, and $15$ subtracts only the height of the tallest stack from the total.
