Both diagonals of a rectangle are drawn, along with the vertical segment joining the midpoints of its top and bottom sides. How many triangles are there in all?

::figura{tipo="linee" segmenti="1,2,9,2;9,2,9,8;9,8,1,8;1,8,1,2;1,2,9,8;9,2,1,8;5,2,5,8" titolo="Diagram for counting triangles"}

- $12$
- $10$
- $14$
- $8$

## Explanation

The diagonals and the boundary of the rectangle form eight triangles: four with a vertex at the center and four that are each half the rectangle. The vertical segment passes through the center, dividing into two both the triangle on the top side and the triangle on the bottom side. These four additional triangles bring the total to $8 + 4 = 12$.
