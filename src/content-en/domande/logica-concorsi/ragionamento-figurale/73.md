## Explanation

The cells in odd positions contain a right triangle and those in even positions contain circles, so the sequence is made of two interleaved sequences, each with its own rule. In the odd cells the right triangle turns $90°$ clockwise at each appearance, because the right angle moves from the bottom left to the top left and then to the top right. The seventh position is odd, therefore it contains the fourth right triangle, rotated $270°$ from the first, with the right angle at the bottom right. The four circles are the figure in the eighth position, which continues the sequence of even cells. The right triangle with the right angle at the top right repeats the third right triangle, and the right triangle with the right angle at the bottom left restarts the rotation after three right triangles.
