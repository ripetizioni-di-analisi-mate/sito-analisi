Track the segments in matching positions in the first two cells of a row. Which drawing preserves the same combining rule in the last row?

::figura{tipo="matrice" celle="linea medio n r90; linea | linea; linea r45 | linea medio n r90; linea r45 | linea medio o; linea r90; linea medio e | linea r90; linea medio s r90 | linea medio o; linea medio e; linea medio s r90 | linea medio n r90; linea medio o; linea r-45 | linea medio o; linea medio s r90; linea r-45; linea | ?"}

- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="linea medio n r90; linea medio o; linea r-45; linea medio s r90; linea"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="linea medio n r90; linea medio s r90; linea"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="linea medio o; linea r-45"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="linea medio n r90; linea medio s r90"}

## Explanation

In the first row the middle vertical segment appears in the first two cells and is absent from the third. In the second row the same holds for the middle horizontal segment. The segments present in only one of the first two cells do appear in the third. The third cell of each row is therefore the **symmetric difference** of the first two. In the third row the left side and the diagonal sloping down to the right appear in both cells and cancel. The top side, bottom side, and middle vertical segment remain. The cell with five segments shows the union of the first two cells, and the cell containing the left side and diagonal shows only the shared segments.
