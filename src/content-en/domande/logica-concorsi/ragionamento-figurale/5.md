The letter R is first rotated $90°$ counterclockwise and then reflected across the vertical axis. Which figure results?

::figura{tipo="serie" celle="lettera:R" titolo="The original letter"}

- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="lettera:R specchio r-90"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="lettera:R specchio r90"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="lettera:R r-90"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="lettera:R r90"}

## Explanation

The R has its vertical stem on the left and its loop attached to the upper part of the stem. A counterclockwise rotation through $90°$ takes left to bottom and top to left, so the stem becomes horizontal at the bottom and the loop sits above its left half. **Reflection across the vertical axis** then swaps left and right, leaving the stem at the bottom, so the loop moves above its right half. The required R has a horizontal stem at the bottom and its loop at the top right. The R with the stem at the bottom and the loop at the top left is the result of the rotation alone. The R with the stem at the top and the loop at the bottom left is obtained by performing the two transformations in reverse order.
