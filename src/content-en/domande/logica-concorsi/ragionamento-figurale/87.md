A stepped grid has left-aligned rows of four, four, three, and two unit squares. How many complete squares are visible, including those with sides longer than one unit?

- $21$
- $13$
- $20$
- $19$

## Explanation

The four rows contain $4 + 4 + 3 + 2 = 13$ unit squares. Successive pairs of rows contain three, two, and one square of side length two, respectively, giving six. Only the first three rows accommodate a square of side length three, and the total is $13 + 6 + 1 = 20$.
