Which figure continues the sequence?

::figura{tipo="serie" celle="lettera:G r-90 | lettera:G | lettera:G r90 | ?"}

- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="lettera:G specchio-o"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="lettera:G r-90"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="lettera:G r180"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="lettera:G specchio"}

## Explanation

The letter G rotates $90°$ clockwise from one cell to the next: it is upright in the second cell and turned a quarter turn to the right in the third. In the fourth cell it has rotated through $180°$, with its opening facing left and its inner stroke at the top. The G that opens to the right with the inner stroke at the top is a reflection across the horizontal axis. By the result on **rotated figures and mirror images**, no rotation of the G produces it. The G rotated $90°$ counterclockwise repeats the first cell.
