The face with the dot is on top. Which view also preserves the order of the other faces printed on the net?

- Cube 3
- Cube 4
- Cube 2

## Explanation

With the dot on top, the gray cross and the solid square can meet at the visible vertex. In the net the square is to the left of the gray cross and the dot is below the cross. Bringing the dot to the top with the gray cross in front places the square on the right.
