In the figure the circle is on top, the square faces the observer and the cross is on the right. A person rolls the cube first to the right and then towards themself, a quarter-turn each time. They repeat from the starting position, reversing the order of the two moves. Which symbols end up against the table in the two trials, respectively?

::figura{tipo="cubi" celle="cerchio / quadrato pieno / croce grigio" titolo="Starting position for both trials"}

- Cross, then square
- Square, then cross
- Circle in both trials
- Square in both trials

## Explanation

Rolling right first leaves the square at the front. Rolling the cube towards the observer then puts the square underneath. In the reverse order the first move leaves the cross on the right, and the second puts it against the table.
