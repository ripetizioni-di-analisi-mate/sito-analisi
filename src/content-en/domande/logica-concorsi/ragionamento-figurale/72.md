The cube in the figure has the circle on the top face, the solid square on the face toward the observer and the gray cross on the right face. The cube is rolled a quarter turn to the right and then a quarter turn toward the observer. In a second trial, from the same starting position, the two moves are made in the reverse order. Which symbol is on the face resting on the table at the end of the first trial and at the end of the second?

::figura{tipo="cubi" celle="cerchio / quadrato pieno / croce grigio" titolo="Starting position for both trials"}

- Gray cross, then solid square
- Solid square, then gray cross
- Circle in both trials
- Solid square in both trials

## Explanation

When the cube rolls to the right, the right face goes onto the table, while the face toward the observer stays in place. When the cube rolls toward the observer, the face toward the observer goes onto the table, while the right and left faces stay in place. In the first trial the first roll leaves the solid square in front, so the second roll brings it onto the table. In the second trial the first roll leaves the gray cross on the right, therefore the second roll brings the gray cross onto the table. The answer "Solid square in both trials" comes from assuming that the order of the moves does not matter, and "Circle in both trials" from thinking that two quarter turns always bring the top face to the bottom.
