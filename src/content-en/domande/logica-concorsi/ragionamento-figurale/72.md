The solid square is on the face toward the viewer. Which symbol is on the back, on the face that shares no edge with the square?

::figura{tipo="sviluppo" schema="xx..|.xxx|..x." celle="cerchio | quadrato pieno | croce grigio | anello | punto | x pieno" titolo="Net of a cube"}

- Ring
- Solid diagonal cross
- Circle
- Gray cross

## Explanation

In the net the faces with the solid square, gray cross, ring, and diagonal cross form a **Z**. The two end faces become opposite, so the solid diagonal cross is behind the square.
