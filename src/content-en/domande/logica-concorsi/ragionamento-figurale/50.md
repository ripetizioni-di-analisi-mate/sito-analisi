In the sequence the right triangle always turns in the same direction, and the dot alternates between two opposite positions. Which figure comes fifth?

::figura{tipo="serie" celle="squadra grigio r90; punto se | squadra grigio r180; punto no | squadra grigio r270; punto se | squadra grigio r0; punto no | ?" titolo="Figure sequence to complete"}

- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="squadra grigio r90; punto se"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="squadra grigio r0; punto no"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="squadra grigio r90; punto no"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="squadra grigio r180; punto se"}

## Explanation

The triangle makes a **quarter turn** clockwise at each step. After four steps it has its original orientation. The dot also returns to the bottom right because it changes position every time.
