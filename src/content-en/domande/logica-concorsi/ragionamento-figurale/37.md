Which cube can be obtained by folding the net shown?

- ::figura{tipo="cubi" celle="anello / cerchio / croce pieno"}
- ::figura{tipo="cubi" celle="anello / croce pieno / cerchio"}
- ::figura{tipo="cubi" celle="anello / x / punto"}

## Explanation

In the net the ring is opposite the diagonal cross, and the solid cross is opposite the dot. Eliminate the cube showing the ring and diagonal cross together and the cube showing the dot and solid cross together. The two remaining cubes have the same three faces, the ring, solid cross, and circle, in two different orders. In the net these faces meet at the top left corner of the circle. Their clockwise order around that corner is ring, circle, solid cross. By the rule for **faces around a vertex**, the cube preserves this order, read in the drawing from top to right to front. The required cube therefore has the ring on top, circle on the right, and solid cross in front. The cube with the circle in front and the solid cross on the right reverses this order.
