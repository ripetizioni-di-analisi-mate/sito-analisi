Which figure continues the sequence?

::figura{tipo="serie" celle="esagono piccolo x1 | esagono piccolo grigio x2 | esagono piccolo x3 | esagono piccolo grigio x4 | ?"}

- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="esagono piccolo grigio x5"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="esagono piccolo x4"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="esagono piccolo x6"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="esagono piccolo x5"}

## Explanation

The number of hexagons increases by one at each step, so the fifth cell contains $5$ hexagons. The fill alternates between unfilled and gray. The odd-numbered cells contain unfilled shapes, so the hexagons in the fifth cell are unfilled. The required figure contains five unfilled hexagons. Five gray hexagons give the correct number but break the alternating fill pattern, and four unfilled hexagons repeat the count from the preceding cell.
