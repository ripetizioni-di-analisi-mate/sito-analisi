Each panel contains four small circles. Which panel has a filled-circle count different from the others?

- Figure 1
- Figure 4
- Figure 3
- Figure 5

## Explanation

Each figure has four circles, one in each corner. In figures 1, 2, 4, and 5 two circles are solid and two are unfilled, whereas figure 3 has three solid circles. Figure 3 is therefore the only one that does not follow the rule. The positions of the solid circles do not single out one figure. For example, in both figures 4 and 5 the two solid circles lie on the same diagonal.
