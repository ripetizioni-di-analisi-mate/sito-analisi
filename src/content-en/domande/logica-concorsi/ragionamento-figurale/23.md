Which of the five figures does *not* follow the rule shared by the other four?

::figura{tipo="opzioni" celle="pentagono grande; pentagono piccolo pieno | quadrato grande; quadrato piccolo pieno | triangolo grande; triangolo piccolo pieno | rombo grande; cerchio piccolo pieno | esagono grande; esagono piccolo pieno" etichette="numeri"}

- Figure 1
- Figure 3
- Figure 5
- Figure 4

## Explanation

Each figure contains a large shape with a small solid shape at its center. In figures 1, 2, 3, and 5 the small shape is the same as the large one, whereas figure 4 has a circle inside a diamond. Figure 4 is therefore the only one that does not follow the rule. The outer shapes are all different, so the outer shape alone does not single out any one figure.
