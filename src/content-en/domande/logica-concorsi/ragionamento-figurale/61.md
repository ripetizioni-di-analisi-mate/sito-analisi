A row of tiles repeats the same pattern of shapes and the same movement of a dot around the corners. Which tile is seventeenth?

- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="rombo grigio; punto no"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="croce grigio; punto ne"}

## Explanation

The seventeenth position is $16$ steps from the first. The dot has completed four circuits and returns to the top right. The number $16$ gives a remainder of $1$ on division by $3$, so the shape is the cross.
