Four of the drawings have a line that divides them into mirror-image halves. Which drawing has no such line?

::figura{tipo="opzioni" celle="semicerchio r180; punto n | semicerchio r90; punto e | triangolo; punto s | freccia r90; punto ne | quadrato; punto ne" etichette="numeri"}

- Figure 2
- Figure 3
- Figure 4
- Figure 5

## Explanation

Figures 1, 2, 3, and 5 have an **axis of symmetry**, a line across which the figure coincides with its own reflection. In figures 1 and 3 the axis is vertical and passes through the dot, and in figure 2 it is horizontal. In figure 5 the axis is the diagonal of the cell from the bottom left corner to the top right: the square is symmetric across its diagonals and the dot lies on this diagonal. In figure 4 the arrow points right and the dot is at the top right. Reflection across the horizontal line, the arrow's only axis of symmetry, would move the dot down. Figure 4 therefore has no axis of symmetry and is the only one that does not follow the rule.
