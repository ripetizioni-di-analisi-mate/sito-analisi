Which figure completes the matrix?

::figura{tipo="matrice" celle="squadra | squadra grigio r90 | squadra righe r180 | squadra righe r90 | squadra r180 | squadra grigio | squadra grigio r180 | squadra righe | ?"}

- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="squadra r90"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="squadra grigio r90"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="squadra r180"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="squadra righe r180"}

## Explanation

Each row contains the right triangle in three orientations, with the right angle at the bottom left, top left, and top right, and with three fills, unfilled, gray, and striped. Both features therefore follow the **three values in each row** rule, and the same rule holds down the columns. The third row already contains the gray triangle with the right angle at the top right and the striped triangle with the right angle at the bottom left. The missing cell must therefore contain the unfilled triangle with the right angle at the top left. The gray triangle has the correct orientation but repeats the fill of the first cell in the row. The unfilled triangle with the right angle at the top right repeats the orientation of the first cell. The striped triangle with the right angle at the top right repeats the top cell in the third column.
