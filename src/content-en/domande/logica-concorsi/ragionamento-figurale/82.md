A figure is reflected across the vertical axis of the cell. Which drawing shows the circle and the arrow in the correct positions and orientations?

::figura{tipo="serie" celle="cerchio pieno piccolo no; freccia grigio piccolo r45 se" titolo="Circle and arrow at the sides of the cell"}

- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="cerchio pieno piccolo ne; freccia grigio piccolo r315 so"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="cerchio pieno piccolo ne; freccia grigio piccolo r45 so"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="cerchio pieno piccolo so; freccia grigio piccolo r315 ne"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="cerchio pieno piccolo no; freccia grigio piccolo r315 se"}

## Explanation

**Reflection across the vertical axis** moves the circle to the top right and the arrow to the bottom left. The arrowhead's direction also reflects, changing from top right to top left.
