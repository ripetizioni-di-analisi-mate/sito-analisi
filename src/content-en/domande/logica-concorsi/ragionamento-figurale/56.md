The original arrow points diagonally. Which cell results from rotating only the arrow $45°$ counterclockwise, leaving the dot in place?

::figura{tipo="serie" celle="freccia grigio piccolo r135; punto so" titolo="Drawing to be transformed"}

- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="freccia grigio piccolo r90; punto so"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="freccia grigio piccolo r90; punto s"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="freccia grigio piccolo r135; punto s"}
- ::figura{tipo="cella" celle="freccia grigio piccolo r180; punto so"}

## Explanation

A counterclockwise rotation through $45°$ turns the arrow from the bottom right to a horizontal direction pointing right. The dot remains at the bottom left because only the arrow is rotated.
