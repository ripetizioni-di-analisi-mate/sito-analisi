The third cell in each row is obtained by removing from the first cell any dots whose positions also appear in the second cell. Which cell completes the matrix?

## Explanation

In the last row the two cells have a dot at the center and one at the middle of the left side in common. Removing them from the first cell leaves the dot at the top right and the one in the middle of the bottom side.
