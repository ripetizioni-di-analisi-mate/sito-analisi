Offices A, B, C, D, and E are on five floors numbered 1 through 5, one office per floor. Office A is on the floor immediately above C and below B. Office D is on the floor immediately below C. Office E is not on the fifth floor and is above D. On which floor is office E?

- 1st
- 4th
- 5th
- 3rd

## Explanation

Offices D, C, and A occupy three consecutive floors in ascending order, and B is above A. If D were on the second floor, C would be on the third, A on the fourth, and B on the fifth. That would leave E on the first, below D, although E must be above D. D cannot be on the third floor or higher because there must be a floor above A for B. Thus D is on the first floor, C on the second, and A on the third. The fourth and fifth floors remain for B and E. Since E is not on the fifth floor, E is on the fourth and B on the fifth. The fifth floor is tempting if the condition on E is overlooked. The first floor is E’s position in the arrangement that was ruled out.
