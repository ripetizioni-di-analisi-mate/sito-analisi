Through how many degrees does the hour hand of a clock rotate in $2$ hours and $20$ minutes?

- $840°$
- $60°$
- $70°$
- $140°$

## Explanation

The hour hand advances $30°$ per hour and $0.5°$ per minute. In $2$ hours it covers $60°$, and in the next $20$ minutes it covers another $20 \cdot 0.5° = 10°$, so its total rotation is $70°$. The value $60°$ ignores the $20$ minutes, and $140°$ comes from moving the hour hand $1°$ per minute. The value $840°$ is the rotation of the minute hand, which covers $140 \cdot 6° = 840°$ in $140$ minutes.
