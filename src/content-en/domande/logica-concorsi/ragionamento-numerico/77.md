Each file has exactly one of $6$ possible labels. What is the smallest number of files needed to guarantee that at least four have the same label?

- $18$
- $7$
- $19$
- $24$

## Explanation

With at most $3$ files per label, it is possible to distribute $18$ files. By the **pigeonhole principle**, with $19$ files at least four files must share a label. Assigning three files to each label shows that $18$ are insufficient.
