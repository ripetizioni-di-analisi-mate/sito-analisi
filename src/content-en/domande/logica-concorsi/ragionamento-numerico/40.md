An office has $40$ employees. What is the largest number $k$ for which we can be certain that at least $k$ employees were born in the same month?

- $4$
- $3$
- $2$
- $13$

## Explanation

The $12$ months serve as the pigeonholes. Since $40 = 3 \cdot 12 + 4$, there are more than $3 \cdot 12$ employees, so by the **pigeonhole principle**, at least one month contains at least $4$ birthdays. The value $4$ is also the largest number guaranteed: there could be $4$ employees born in each of $4$ months and $3$ in each of the other $8$, in which case no month would have $5$ birthdays. The value $3$ is the integer part of $\dfrac{40}{12}$ and overlooks the $4$ extra employees, while $13$ is the number of people needed to guarantee two birthdays in the same month.
