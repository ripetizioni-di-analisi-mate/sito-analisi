In a non-leap year, the days are numbered starting with January 1 as day $1$. What date corresponds to day $100$?

- April 8
- April 11
- April 9
- April 10

## Explanation

January, February, and March have $31 + 28 + 31 = 90$ days in total, so March 31 is day $90$. Day $100$ comes $10$ days later, so it is April 10. April 9 is day $100$ in a leap year, when February has $29$ days. April 8 results from giving February $30$ days. April 11 is the date $100$ days after January 1, whereas the numbering already counts January 1 as day $1$.
