In a cycling race with $40$ riders and no ties, Ugo is twenty-fifth from the front. He passes $3$ riders, then $1$ rider passes him. What is his position now, counting from the back?

- $14$
- $19$
- $18$
- $17$

## Explanation

Each rider Ugo passes moves him up one place, and each rider who passes him moves him down one. Ugo therefore moves from position $25$ to position $25 - 3 + 1 = 23$ from the front. In a race with $40$ competitors, the formula $n = k + h - 1$ gives his position from the back as $h = 40 - 23 + 1 = 18$. The value $17$ omits the addition of $1$, and $19$ ignores the rider who passes Ugo. The value $14$ reverses the passing moves and puts Ugo in position $27$.
