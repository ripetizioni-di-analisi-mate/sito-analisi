A bus leaves on Friday at 19:35 and arrives on Sunday at 6:10. Departure and arrival are in the same time zone. How long is the trip?

- $34$ h $35$ min
- $35$ h $15$ min
- $58$ h $35$ min
- $10$ h $35$ min

## Explanation

From Friday at 19:35 to Saturday at 19:35, $24$ hours pass. From Saturday at 19:35 to midnight is $4$ hours and $25$ minutes, and from midnight to Sunday at 6:10 is another $6$ hours and $10$ minutes, so this second part takes $10$ hours and $35$ minutes. Altogether, the trip takes $34$ hours and $35$ minutes. The value $10$ h $35$ min overlooks Saturday. The value $58$ h $35$ min adds $48$ hours, as though Friday evening to Sunday morning were two full days. The value $35$ h $15$ min comes from using decimal subtraction to find the time to midnight: $24 - 19.35 = 4.65$ is read as $4$ hours and $65$ minutes, that is, $5$ hours and $5$ minutes.
