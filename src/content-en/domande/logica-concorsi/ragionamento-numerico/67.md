A notice will be posted on the first Monday in October. October 1 that year falls on a Wednesday. On what date will the notice be posted?

- $5$
- $6$
- $7$
- $4$

## Explanation

The first day of the month is Wednesday. Thursday, Friday, Saturday, and Sunday take the next four dates. The first Monday therefore falls on October $6$.
