Five athletes, Luca, Marco, Nino, Oscar, and Pino, run a race with no ties. Nino finishes immediately after Luca. Marco finishes before Luca but is not first. Oscar finishes after Pino but is not last. Which of the following must be true?

- Marco finishes second.
- Nino finishes fourth.
- Oscar finishes third.
- Luca finishes fourth.

## Explanation

Marco cannot be first. Neither can Luca or Nino, who finish after Marco, or Oscar, who finishes after Pino. Therefore Pino is first. Oscar cannot be last, and neither can Pino, Marco, or Luca, each of whom finishes before someone else. Therefore Nino is last, and Luca, immediately ahead of him, is fourth. Marco and Oscar occupy second and third in an order that the information does not determine. Thus the two possible orders are Pino, Marco, Oscar, Luca, Nino and Pino, Oscar, Marco, Luca, Nino. The statements “Marco finishes second” and “Oscar finishes third” are true in the first order and false in the second, so neither must be true.
