July 4, 2026, is a Saturday. The year 2028 is a leap year, while 2026, 2027, and 2029 are not. On what day of the week does July 4, 2029, fall?

- Wednesday
- Thursday
- Saturday
- Tuesday

## Explanation

From a date to the same date the following year there are $365$ days, or $366$ if the interval contains February 29. Since $365 = 52 \cdot 7 + 1$, the day of the week advances by one or two places. From July 4, 2026, to July 4, 2027, and from July 4, 2028, to July 4, 2029, the day advances by one place. From July 4, 2027, to July 4, 2028, it advances by two because the interval includes February 29, 2028. In total, the day advances by $1 + 2 + 1 = 4$ places, taking Saturday to Wednesday. Tuesday overlooks February 29, while Thursday also advances by two from 2028 to 2029, a July-to-July interval that does not include February 29.
