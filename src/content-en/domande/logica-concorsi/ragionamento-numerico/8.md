A clock is set to the correct time at 9:00 and loses $4$ minutes per hour at a constant rate. What time does it show at 15:30 the same day?

- 15\:56
- 15\:02
- 15\:04
- 15\:06

## Explanation

From 9:00 to 15:30, $6.5$ actual hours pass, or $6$ and a half hours. The clock loses $4$ minutes per actual hour, so it falls $6.5 \cdot 4 = 26$ minutes behind and shows 15:04. The time 15:56 comes from adding the $26$ minutes instead of subtracting them, as though the clock ran fast. The time 15:06 counts only the $6$ full hours, while 15:02 rounds the interval to $7$ hours.
