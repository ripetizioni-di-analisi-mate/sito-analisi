In $3$ years Noemi will be as old as Leo was $6$ years ago. Leo is $32$ years old today. How old is Noemi?

- $26$
- $23$
- $29$
- $20$

## Explanation

Six years ago Leo was $32 - 6 = 26$. According to the problem, Noemi will reach that **age** in three years. She is therefore $26 - 3 = 23$ today.
