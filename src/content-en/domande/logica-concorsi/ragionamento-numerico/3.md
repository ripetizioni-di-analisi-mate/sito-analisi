A station clock is visible in a photograph. The minute hand is on the 10 and the smaller angle between the hands is exactly $95^\circ$. Which of the following evening times is consistent with the photograph?

- 17\:50
- 19\:50
- 18\:50
- 20\:50

## Explanation

The minute hand is $300^\circ$ from twelve. The hour hand must be at $205^\circ$ or $35^\circ$. At minute $50$ it is $25^\circ$ past the hour mark, so among the choices only $180^\circ+25^\circ=205^\circ$, at 18\:50, fits.
