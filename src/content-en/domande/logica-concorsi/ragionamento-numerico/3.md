An evening train leaves at 18:50. What is the smaller of the two angles between the hands of the station clock at that time?

- $120°$
- $90°$
- $95°$
- $265°$

## Explanation

On the clock face, the hour in 18:50 corresponds to $6$ because $18 - 12 = 6$, so the formula uses $h = 6$. The minute hand is $50 \cdot 6° = 300°$ from $12$, and the hour hand is at $180° + 50 \cdot 0.5° = 205°$, just before $7$. The angle between the hands is therefore $300° - 205° = 95°$. Leaving the hour hand on $6$ gives $120°$, and placing it on $7$ already gives $90°$. The value $265°$ is the larger angle, which also results from using $h = 18$ in the formula.
