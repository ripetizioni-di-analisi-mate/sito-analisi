A drawer contains $12$ right-hand gloves and $12$ left-hand gloves, all the same color and size. What is the minimum number of gloves you must draw in the dark to guarantee a pair, meaning one right-hand and one left-hand glove?

- $13$
- $24$
- $3$
- $2$

## Explanation

In the **worst case**, the gloves drawn are all of the same type, so $12$ draws could produce $12$ right-hand gloves and no left-hand gloves. After those $12$ draws, only left-hand gloves remain, so the thirteenth glove completes the pair and $13$ draws are needed. The value $3$ guarantees two gloves of the *same* type, the opposite of what is required. The value $2$ is the number of gloves in a pair. The value $24$ corresponds to drawing every glove.
