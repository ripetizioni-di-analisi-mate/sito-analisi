Matilde is $5$ years older than Andrea. In $7$ years, their ages will add up to $49$ years. How old is Matilde now?

- $20$
- $15$
- $22$
- $17$

## Explanation

The **sum of their ages** today is $49 - 14 = 35$. Subtracting the $5$-year difference from Matilde’s age would make the two ages equal, with a sum of $30$. Therefore, Andrea is $15$ years old and Matilde is $20$.
