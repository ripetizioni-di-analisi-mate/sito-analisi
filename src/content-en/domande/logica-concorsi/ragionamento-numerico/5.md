How many minutes after 4:00 are the clock hands first in opposite positions, forming an angle of $180°$?

- $60$ minutes
- $50$ minutes
- $38 + \dfrac{2}{11}$ minutes
- $54 + \dfrac{6}{11}$ minutes

## Explanation

At 4:00 the hour hand is at $120°$ and the minute hand at $0°$. The hands are opposite when the minute hand is $180°$ ahead of the hour hand, that is, when it has gained $120° + 180° = 300°$. The minute hand gains $5.5°$ per minute, so the required time is
$$
m = \frac{300}{5.5} = \frac{600}{11} = 54 + \frac{6}{11} \ \text{minutes}.
$$
The value $50$ minutes assumes the hour hand is stationary, and $60$ minutes assumes that it moves $1°$ per minute. The value $38 + \dfrac{2}{11}$ minutes is one of the times when the hands form a right angle: the instant when the minute hand has gained $120° + 90° = 210°$.
