Teo’s maternal grandfather is the only son of Ivo’s paternal grandfather. How is Teo related to Ivo?

- His brother
- His son
- His cousin
- His nephew

## Explanation

Ivo’s father is a son of Ivo’s paternal grandfather. Since that grandfather has only one son, Ivo’s father is Teo’s maternal grandfather. Teo’s mother is therefore a daughter of Ivo’s father, that is, a sister of Ivo. Thus Teo is the son of one of Ivo’s sisters and is his **nephew**. A first cousin would be the child of a sibling of one of Ivo’s parents. The word *only* establishes that Teo is the child of Ivo’s own sister.
