Two operations start together at 9:10. The first repeats every $18$ minutes and the second every $24$ minutes. When do they next start together?

- 10\:46
- 10\:04
- 10\:22
- 09\:52

## Explanation

We need the smallest positive number of minutes that is a multiple of both $18$ and $24$. The **least common multiple** is $72$, so the next simultaneous start is at 10:22.
