At which of the following times do the hands of an analog clock lie along the same line and point in opposite directions?

- 6\:00
- 6\:30
- 6\:20
- 6\:10

## Explanation

At 6:00 the minute hand is on 12 and the hour hand is on 6. Their directions are $180^\circ$ apart. By each of the three later times, the minute hand has already reduced the **separation** because it moves faster than the hour hand.
