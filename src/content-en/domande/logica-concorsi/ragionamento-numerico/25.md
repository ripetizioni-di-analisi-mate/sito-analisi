A training course meets every Tuesday and Thursday from September 1 through October 29, 2026, including both dates, with no breaks. September 1, 2026, is a Tuesday. How many classes are held?

- $19$
- $17$
- $18$
- $16$

## Explanation

From September 1 through October 29 inclusive there are $30 + 29 = 59$ days, or $8$ full weeks plus $3$ days. The $8$ weeks from September 1 through October 26 contain $8$ Tuesdays and $8$ Thursdays, giving $16$ classes. The remaining $3$ days are Tuesday, October 27, Wednesday, October 28, and Thursday, October 29. They add $2$ classes, so there are $18$ classes in total. The value $16$ ignores the days after the full weeks, and $17$ excludes the last day, which is included.
