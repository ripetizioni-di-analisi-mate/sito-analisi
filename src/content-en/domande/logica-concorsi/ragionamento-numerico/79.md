A drawer contains $4$ left-hand gloves and $5$ right-hand gloves, identical apart from the side. What is the minimum number that must be drawn in the dark without replacement to guarantee a usable pair?

- $9$
- $6$
- $2$
- $5$

## Explanation

In the **worst case**, the first five gloves drawn are all right-hand gloves. The sixth is then left-handed and makes a pair with one of the first five. Five draws could still yield gloves for just one hand.
