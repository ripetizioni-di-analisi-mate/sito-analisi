An employee works from 8:40 to 13:10 and, after a break, from 14:30 to 17:15. How much time does the employee work that day?

- $7$ h $15$ min
- $8$ h $35$ min
- $7$ h $55$ min
- $7$ h $45$ min

## Explanation

From 8:40 to 13:10, there are $20$ minutes to 9:00, $4$ hours from there to 13:00, and a further $10$ minutes, making $4$ hours and $30$ minutes. From 14:30 to 17:15, there are $30$ minutes to 15:00 and then $2$ hours and $15$ minutes, making $2$ hours and $45$ minutes. The employee therefore works $7$ hours and $15$ minutes in total. The value $7$ h $55$ min comes from the decimal subtractions $13.10 - 8.40 = 4.70$ and $17.15 - 14.30 = 2.85$, followed by adding the results as decimals and misreading the digits after the decimal point as minutes. The value $8$ h $35$ min is the time from 8:40 to 17:15 without subtracting the break.
