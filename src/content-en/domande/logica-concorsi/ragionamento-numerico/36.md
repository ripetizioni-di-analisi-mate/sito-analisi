Dario is $34$ years old today. When Dario was the age Elsa is today, Elsa was $10$ years old. How old is Elsa today?

- $17$
- $12$
- $22$
- $24$

## Explanation

Let $e$ be Elsa’s age today. The age difference between Dario and Elsa is $34 - e$ and remains constant, so Dario was $e$ years old exactly $34 - e$ years ago. At that time Elsa was $e - (34 - e) = 2e - 34$ years old, and the condition $2e - 34 = 10$ gives $e = 22$. Indeed, $12$ years ago Dario was $22$ and Elsa was $10$. The value $24$ is the difference $34 - 10$, $17$ is half Dario’s age, and $12$ is the age difference between the two.
