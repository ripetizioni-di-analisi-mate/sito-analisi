A service counter opens Monday through Friday, including on holidays. On how many of $24$ consecutive days is it open if the period starts on a Thursday, counted as the first day?

- $15$
- $16$
- $17$
- $18$

## Explanation

In the first $3$ **weeks** there are $15$ days when it is open. The three remaining days are Thursday, Friday, and Saturday, so it is open on two more days. The total is $17$.
