How many leap years are there from 1888 through 1916 inclusive?

- $6$
- $5$
- $8$
- $7$

## Explanation

The years divisible by $4$ from 1888 through 1916 are 1888, 1892, 1896, 1900, 1904, 1908, 1912, and 1916, a total of $8$. The year 1900 is divisible by $100$ but not by $400$, so by the **leap year rule** it is not a leap year. There are therefore $7$ leap years. The value $8$ includes 1900, while $6$ and $5$ exclude one or both endpoints, both of which are included and are leap years.
