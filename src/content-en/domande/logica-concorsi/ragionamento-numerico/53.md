An automated check starts at 22:48 and lasts $4$ hours and $37$ minutes. At what time does it end?

- 02\:25
- 03\:15
- 03\:25
- 03\:35

## Explanation

It takes $72$ minutes to reach midnight. The **remaining time** is $277 - 72 = 205$ minutes, or $3$ hours and $25$ minutes. The check ends at 03:25 the next day.
