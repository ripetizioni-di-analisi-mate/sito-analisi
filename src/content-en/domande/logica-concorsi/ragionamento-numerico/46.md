What is the smaller of the two angles between the hands of an analog clock at 8:24?

- $108^\circ$
- $252^\circ$
- $120^\circ$
- $96^\circ$

## Explanation

The hour hand is $8 \cdot 30^\circ + 24 \cdot 0.5^\circ = 252^\circ$ from 12. The minute hand is at $24 \cdot 6^\circ = 144^\circ$. Their **angular difference** is $108^\circ$, smaller than the other angle of $252^\circ$.
