A list numbered from $1$ to $39$ ranks candidates from highest to lowest score. Emma is 16th. How many candidates are ahead of her and how many are behind her?

- 16 are ahead of her, 23 behind
- 15 are ahead of her, 24 behind
- 23 are ahead of her, 15 behind
- 15 are ahead of her, 23 behind

## Explanation

Ahead of position $16$ are positions $1$ through $15$. After Emma come positions $17$ through $39$, a total of $39 - 16 = 23$. Emma’s own **position** is counted in neither group.
