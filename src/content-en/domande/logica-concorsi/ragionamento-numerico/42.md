A person’s initials are the first letter of their first name and the first letter of their last name, for example M. R. for Maria Rossi. Suppose the alphabet has $21$ letters. What is the smallest number of people needed to guarantee that at least two share the same initials?

- $441$
- $22$
- $43$
- $442$

## Explanation

There are $21 \cdot 21 = 441$ possible pairs of initials because each of the $21$ first-name initials can be combined with $21$ possible last-name initials. With $441$ people, each could have a different pair, so a match is not guaranteed. By the **pigeonhole principle**, with $441$ pigeonholes, $442$ people guarantee that two have the same initials. The value $43$ comes from adding the two alphabet sizes and then adding one person. The value $22$ considers only the first-name initial.
