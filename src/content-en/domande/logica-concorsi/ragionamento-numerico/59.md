A period starts on February $24$, 2028, with the starting date excluded. It expires after $12$ calendar days. On what date does it expire?

- March 8, 2028
- March 6, 2028
- March 9, 2028
- March 7, 2028

## Explanation

The year 2028 is a **leap year**, so February $25$ through February $29$ accounts for $5$ days. The remaining $7$ days take us to March $7$.
