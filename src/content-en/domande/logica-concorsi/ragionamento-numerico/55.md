At office A it is 23:15 on Wednesday. Office B is $5$ hours ahead of A. A message arrives after $1$ hour and $30$ minutes. At what local time and on what day does it arrive at office B?

- 06\:45 on Thursday
- 05\:45 on Wednesday
- 04\:45 on Thursday
- 05\:45 on Thursday

## Explanation

When it is 23:15 on Wednesday at A, it is already 04:15 on Thursday at B because its **time zone** is $5$ hours ahead. Adding the transmission time of $1$ hour and $30$ minutes gives 05:45 on Thursday.
