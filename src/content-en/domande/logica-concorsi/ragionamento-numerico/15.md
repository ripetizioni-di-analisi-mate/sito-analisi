A flight leaves Rome at 13:40 Rome time and lands in Mexico City at 18:15 local time on the same day. Assume Mexico City is $7$ hours behind Rome. How long is the flight?

- $5$ h $15$ min
- $11$ h $35$ min
- $21$ h $35$ min
- $4$ h $35$ min

## Explanation

When it is 18:15 in Mexico City, Rome is $7$ hours ahead, so it is 1:15 the next day in Rome. From 13:40 to midnight is $10$ hours and $20$ minutes, and from midnight to 1:15 is one hour and $15$ minutes. The flight therefore takes $11$ hours and $35$ minutes. The value $4$ h $35$ min is the difference between the two local times without adjusting for the time zones. The value $21$ h $35$ min comes from subtracting the $7$ hours instead of adding them. The value $5$ h $15$ min comes from the decimal subtraction $18.15 - 13.40 = 4.75$, read as $4$ hours and $75$ minutes.
