Of $11$ distinct integers chosen arbitrarily, at least how many must have the same remainder when divided by $4$?

- $2$
- $7$
- $3$
- $4$

## Explanation

The possible **remainders** are $0$, $1$, $2$, and $3$. If each occurred at most twice, there would be only $8$ integers. At least three of the integers must have the same remainder, and the integers from $1$ through $11$ show that a fourth occurrence is not guaranteed.
