April 1 in a certain year falls on a Sunday. On what day of the week does June 1 of the same year fall?

- Wednesday
- Sunday
- Saturday
- Friday

## Explanation

Between the two dates, $30$ days of April and $31$ days of May pass, a total of $61$. Dividing by $7$ leaves $5$ days beyond the full weeks. Five days after Sunday is Friday.
