October 1, 2027, is a Friday. Which days of the week occur five times in October 2027?

- Friday, Saturday, and Sunday
- Thursday, Friday, and Saturday
- Friday and Saturday
- Saturday, Sunday, and Monday

## Explanation

October has $31$ days, or $4$ full weeks plus $3$ days. The $4$ weeks from October 1 to 28 contain each day of the week four times. The $3$ extra days are October 29, 30, and 31. October 29 is $28$ days after October 1, so it is also a Friday. The extra days are therefore Friday, Saturday, and Sunday. Friday and Saturday would apply to a month with $30$ days, while Saturday, Sunday, and Monday starts with the day after October 1.
