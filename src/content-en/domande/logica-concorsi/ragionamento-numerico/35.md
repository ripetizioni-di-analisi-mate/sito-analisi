Marco was born when his father was $28$ years old. Today their ages add up to $70$ years. How old is Marco today?

- $35$
- $49$
- $21$
- $42$

## Explanation

The age difference between father and son remains $28$ years, so if Marco is $x$ years old today, his father is $x + 28$. The sum of their ages gives the equation $x + (x + 28) = 70$, or $2x = 42$, so Marco is $21$ and his father is $49$. The value $35$ halves the sum and ignores the age difference. The value $42$ subtracts $28$ from the sum without dividing by $2$. The value $49$ is the father’s age.
