Today is Saturday. What day of the week was it $30$ days ago?

- Wednesday
- Friday
- Monday
- Thursday

## Explanation

Since $30 = 4 \cdot 7 + 2$, the **remainder modulo 7** tells us that thirty days ago the day of the week was $2$ places behind today. Going back two places from Saturday gives Thursday. Monday comes from going forward two places instead and is the day of the week $30$ days from now.
