The present ages of a mother and daughter add up to $58$ years. In $4$ years, the mother will be twice as old as the daughter. How old is the daughter now?

- $22$
- $16$
- $18$
- $20$

## Explanation

In four years, the **sum of their ages** will be $58 + 8 = 66$. The daughter’s age will be one-third of that sum because the mother will be twice as old. The daughter will then be $22$ years old, so she is $22 - 4 = 18$ now.
