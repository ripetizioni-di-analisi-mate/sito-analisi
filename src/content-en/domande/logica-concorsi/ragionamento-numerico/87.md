A sister is $28$ years old and her brother is $16$. How many whole years back from today must we go for the sister to be twice her brother’s age?

- $6$
- $2$
- $8$
- $4$

## Explanation

The **age difference** is always $12$ years. When the sister is twice her brother’s age, the difference equals the brother’s age, so he must be $12$. That was $16 - 12 = 4$ years ago.
