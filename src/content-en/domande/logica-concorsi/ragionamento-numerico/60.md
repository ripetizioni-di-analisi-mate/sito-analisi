A service counter opens every day. How many days is it open from November $26$ through December $4$, including both dates?

- $11$
- $8$
- $10$
- $9$

## Explanation

November has $30$ days, so November $26$ through November $30$ accounts for $5$ days. With the additional $4$ days in December, the total is $9$ days including both **endpoints**.
