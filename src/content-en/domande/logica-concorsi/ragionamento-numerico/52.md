At 12:00 two clocks show the same time. The first gains $2$ minutes per actual hour and the second loses $3$ minutes per actual hour. How many minutes apart are their readings at 18:00 that day?

- $18$
- $6$
- $12$
- $30$

## Explanation

Each hour, the first clock gains $2$ minutes on the actual time and the second falls $3$ minutes behind. The **difference between the readings** therefore grows by $5$ minutes each hour. After $6$ hours, it reaches $30$ minutes.
