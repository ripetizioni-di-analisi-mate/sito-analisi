A box holds $8$ red tokens, $4$ yellow tokens, and $3$ green tokens. What is the smallest number of tokens that must be drawn without replacement to guarantee at least three red tokens?

- $10$
- $8$
- $11$
- $9$

## Explanation

It is possible to draw all $7$ nonred tokens and just two red ones. This **worst case** gives nine draws without reaching the target. By the tenth draw, at least three tokens must be red.
