A room has a row of $30$ chairs, all occupied. Aldo is in the twelfth chair from the left and Bea in the tenth chair from the right. How many people are seated between them?

- $9$
- $18$
- $8$
- $7$

## Explanation

Bea occupies the tenth chair from the right. By the formula for **positions in a line**, hers is the twenty-first chair from the left because $30 - 10 + 1 = 21$. Between chairs $12$ and $21$ are chairs $13$ through $20$, for a total of $21 - 12 - 1 = 8$ chairs. The value $9$ is the difference $21 - 12$, which includes Bea’s chair, and $7$ puts Bea in chair $30 - 10 = 20$. The value $18$ counts the people seated to Aldo’s right.
