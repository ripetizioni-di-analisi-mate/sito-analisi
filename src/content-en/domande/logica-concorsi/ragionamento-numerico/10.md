An analog clock has stopped and always shows 4:15. How many times does it show the correct time from 00:00 on Monday to 00:00 on Thursday of the same week?

- $0$
- $6$
- $8$
- $3$

## Explanation

The hands of an analog clock repeat their positions every $12$ hours, so hands stopped at 4:15 show the correct time at 4:15 and 16:15 each day. From 00:00 on Monday to 00:00 on Thursday there are three full days, Monday, Tuesday, and Wednesday. Therefore, the clock shows the correct time $3 \cdot 2 = 6$ times. The value $3$ counts just once a day, as for a $24$-hour digital clock. The value $8$ also counts Thursday, although the interval includes only its first instant. The value $0$ assumes that a stopped clock never shows the correct time.
