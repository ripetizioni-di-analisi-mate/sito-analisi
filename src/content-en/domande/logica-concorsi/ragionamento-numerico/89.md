Marta is Luca’s sister. Luca is Sara’s father, and Sara is Tommaso’s mother. How is Marta related to Tommaso?

- She is his aunt.
- She is his great-aunt.
- She is his great-grandmother.
- She is his grandmother.

## Explanation

Luca is the father of Sara, who in turn is Tommaso’s mother. Luca is therefore Tommaso’s grandfather. Marta is **his grandfather’s sister**, making her Tommaso’s great-aunt.
