Assume Bangkok is $6$ hours ahead of Rome. An online meeting starts at 21:30 Bangkok time and lasts $1$ hour and $45$ minutes. At what time in Rome does the meeting end?

- 15\:30
- 23\:15
- 5\:15
- 17\:15

## Explanation

The meeting ends at 21:30 plus $1$ hour and $45$ minutes, which is 23:15 Bangkok time. Rome is $6$ hours behind, so the meeting ends at 17:15 in Rome. The time 23:15 is the ending time in Bangkok, and 5:15 comes from adding the time difference instead of subtracting it. The time 15:30 is the starting time in Rome.
