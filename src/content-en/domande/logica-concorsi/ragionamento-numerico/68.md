In a line, Nadia is 8th from the front and 14th from the back. How many people are in the line?

- $21$
- $20$
- $22$
- $23$

## Explanation

There are $7$ people ahead of Nadia and $13$ behind her. Nadia appears just once in the **total**, so there are $7 + 1 + 13 = 21$ people.
