In a line of $34$ people, Irma is 11th from the front and Karim is 9th from the back. How many people stand strictly between them?

- $15$
- $13$
- $16$
- $14$

## Explanation

Counting from the front, Karim is in position $34 - 9 + 1 = 26$. The people strictly between Irma and Karim occupy positions 12 through 25. There are $14$ of them.
