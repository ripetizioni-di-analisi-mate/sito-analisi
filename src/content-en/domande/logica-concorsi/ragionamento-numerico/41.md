A drawer contains $10$ red socks, $10$ blue socks, and $10$ green socks. A pair consists of two socks of the same color. What is the minimum number you must draw in the dark to guarantee two pairs, which may be of the same color?

- $6$
- $5$
- $4$
- $8$

## Explanation

With $5$ socks, there might be only one pair, for example with $3$ red, $1$ blue, and $1$ green sock, so $5$ draws are insufficient. Pairing socks of each color leaves at most one unmatched sock per color. Thus, among $6$ socks at most $3$ are unmatched and at least $3$ are paired. The number of paired socks must be even, so there are at least $4$, forming at least two pairs. The value $8$ doubles the $4$ draws that guarantee one pair, and $5$ adds just one draw to those $4$. The value $4$ is the number of socks in two pairs, without allowing for the **worst case**.
