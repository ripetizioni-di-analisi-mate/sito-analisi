In the Gregorian calendar, December $31$, 1999, was a Friday. On what day of the week did December $31$, 2000, fall?

- Friday
- Sunday
- Monday
- Saturday

## Explanation

There are $366$ days between the dates because 2000 was a **leap year**: it is divisible by $400$. Removing $52$ full weeks leaves two days. Two days after Friday is Sunday.
