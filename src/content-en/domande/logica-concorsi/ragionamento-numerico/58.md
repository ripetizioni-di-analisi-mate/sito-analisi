If day $14$ of a month falls on a Monday, on what day of the week does day $3$ of the same month fall?

- Saturday
- Thursday
- Wednesday
- Sunday

## Explanation

Going from day $14$ to day $3$ means going back $11$ days, or one **full week** and $4$ days. Counting four days back from Monday gives Thursday.
