January 1 of a leap year falls on a Saturday. Which days of the week occur $53$ times in that year?

- Friday and Saturday
- Saturday and Monday
- Saturday and Sunday
- Only Saturday

## Explanation

A leap year has $366$ days, and since $366 = 52 \cdot 7 + 2$ the year consists of $52$ whole weeks plus two days. In the whole weeks every day of the week occurs $52$ times, so only the weekdays of the two extra days occur $53$ times. The $52$ whole weeks run from January 1 to December 29, therefore the two extra days are December 30 and 31, which fall on Saturday and Sunday. The answer "Only Saturday" holds for a non-leap year starting on a Saturday, and "Friday and Saturday" comes from counting the extra day backward.
