According to the Gregorian calendar, how many leap years are there from 2088 through 2112 inclusive?

- $7$
- $8$
- $6$
- $5$

## Explanation

There are seven multiples of $4$ in the interval. The **Gregorian rule** excludes 2100 because it is divisible by $100$ but not by $400$. This leaves six leap years.
