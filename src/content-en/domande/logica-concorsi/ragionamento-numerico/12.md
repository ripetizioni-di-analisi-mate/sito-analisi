A written test starts at 9:50 and lasts $2.4$ hours. What time does it end?

- 12\:14
- 12\:30
- 11\:54
- 11\:14

## Explanation

The number $2.4$ means $2$ hours plus $0.4$ of an hour, and that fraction is $0.4 \cdot 60 = 24$ minutes, so the test lasts $2$ hours and $24$ minutes. Adding $2$ hours to 9:50 gives 11:50. Adding $24$ minutes brings the minute count to $50 + 24 = 74$, which is one hour and $14$ minutes. Therefore, the test ends at 12:14. The time 12:30 results from reading $2.4$ hours as $2$ hours and $40$ minutes, and 11:54 from reading it as $2$ hours and $4$ minutes. The time 11:14 subtracts $60$ from the $74$ minutes without adding the corresponding hour.
