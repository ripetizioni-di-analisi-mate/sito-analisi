A store opens at 10:15 in the morning. What is the smaller of the two angles between the hands of the clock at the entrance at that time?

- $217.5°$
- $150°$
- $135°$
- $142.5°$

## Explanation

At 10:15 the minute hand is on $3$, which is $90°$ from $12$. The hour hand is a quarter of an hour past $10$, so its position is $300° + 15 \cdot 0.5° = 307.5°$. The difference between the positions is $307.5° - 90° = 217.5°$, which exceeds $180°$, so the smaller angle is $360° - 217.5° = 142.5°$. The value $150°$ comes from leaving the hour hand on $10$, and $217.5°$ is the larger angle. The value $135°$ comes from moving the hour hand $1°$ per minute.
