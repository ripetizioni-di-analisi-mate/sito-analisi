During a meeting the hour hand of a clock advances by $25°$. By how many degrees does the minute hand advance in the same time?

- $150°$
- $50°$
- $12.5°$
- $300°$

## Explanation

The hour hand advances $0.5°$ per minute, so it takes $\dfrac{25}{0.5} = 50$ minutes to cover $25°$. The minute hand advances $6°$ per minute, therefore in $50$ minutes it covers $50 \cdot 6° = 300°$. The same result follows from noting that the minute hand is $\dfrac{6}{0.5} = 12$ times as fast as the hour hand, and $12 \cdot 25° = 300°$. The value $150°$ comes from treating the $25$ degrees as if they were minutes and multiplying them by $6°$, and $50°$ is the number of elapsed minutes written as an angle. The value $12.5°$ is the motion of the hour hand in $25$ minutes.
