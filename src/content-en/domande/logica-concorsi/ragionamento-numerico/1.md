What is the smaller of the two angles between the hands of a clock at 4:40?

- $260°$
- $100°$
- $80°$
- $120°$

## Explanation

At 4:40 the minute hand is $40 \cdot 6° = 240°$ from $12$. The hour hand has moved $40$ minutes past $4$, so its position is $120° + 40 \cdot 0.5° = 140°$. By the formula for the **angle between the hands**, the angle is $|30 \cdot 4 - 5.5 \cdot 40| = |120 - 220| = 100°$. The value $120°$ comes from leaving the hour hand on $4$, and $260°$ is the larger angle, namely $360° - 100°$. The value $80°$ comes from moving the hour hand $1°$ per minute instead of $0.5°$.
