Three bus routes leave a terminal. Buses on the first route leave every $12$ minutes, on the second every $18$ minutes, and on the third every $30$ minutes. All three leave together at 7:00. At what time do they next leave together?

- 8\:30
- 10\:00
- 7\:06
- 7\:36

## Explanation

The three routes have simultaneous departures after a number of minutes that is a multiple of $12$, $18$, and $30$. The smallest positive such number is the least common multiple. Since $12 = 2^2 \cdot 3$, $18 = 2 \cdot 3^2$, and $30 = 2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5$, we have $\operatorname{lcm}(12, 18, 30) = 2^2 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5 = 180$. The buses therefore next leave together after $180$ minutes, or $3$ hours, at 10:00. The time 7:36 corresponds to $\operatorname{lcm}(12, 18) = 36$ and ignores the third route. The time 8:30 corresponds to $\operatorname{lcm}(18, 30) = 90$ and ignores the route with departures every $12$ minutes. The time 7:06 uses the greatest common divisor, $6$, in place of the least common multiple.
