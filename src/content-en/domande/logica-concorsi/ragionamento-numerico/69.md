There are $47$ people in a ranking with no ties. Lucia is 18th from the top. What is her position from the bottom?

- $31$
- $29$
- $18$
- $30$

## Explanation

There are $47 - 18 = 29$ people below Lucia. To find her **position from the bottom**, count those $29$ people and then Lucia. She is therefore 30th.
