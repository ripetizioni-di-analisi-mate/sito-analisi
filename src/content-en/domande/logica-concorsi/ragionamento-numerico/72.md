In a line, Alice is 7th from the front and Matteo is 12th from the back. There are $5$ people between them, and Alice is ahead of Matteo. How many people are in the line?

- $25$
- $24$
- $22$
- $23$

## Explanation

There are $6$ people ahead of Alice and $11$ behind Matteo. To these two groups add the $5$ people between Alice and Matteo and the two people at the ends of that part of the line. The total is $6 + 1 + 5 + 1 + 11 = 24$.
