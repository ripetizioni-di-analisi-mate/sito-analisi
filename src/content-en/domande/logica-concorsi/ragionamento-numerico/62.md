In a non-leap year, what date is the 150th day of the year, counting January 1 as day one?

- May 30
- May 29
- May 31
- June 1

## Explanation

January through April account for $31 + 28 + 31 + 30 = 120$ days in a year that is not a **leap year**. Subtracting gives $150 - 120 = 30$, so the 150th day is May 30.
