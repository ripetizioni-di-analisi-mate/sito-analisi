Bruno is 9th from the back of a line. Three people behind him leave, and two people join ahead of him. What is Bruno’s new position from the back?

- $5$
- $6$
- $8$
- $11$

## Explanation

Before the changes there are $8$ people behind Bruno. After three leave, $5$ remain. People who join ahead of him do not affect the count **from the back**, so Bruno is 6th.
