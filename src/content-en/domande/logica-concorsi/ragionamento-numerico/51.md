At 9:00 a clock shows the correct time. For every actual hour after that, it loses $4$ minutes. When the clock shows 13:40, what time is it really?

- 14\:20
- 13\:20
- 14\:00
- 13\:44

## Explanation

In one actual hour, the clock advances $60 - 4 = 56$ minutes. From 9:00 to 13:40, its **displayed time** advances $280$ minutes, corresponding to $280 : 56 = 5$ actual hours. The correct time is therefore 14:00.
