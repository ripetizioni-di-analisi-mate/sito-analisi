From a ranking with no ties, only the candidates in odd-numbered positions move to a second ranking, in the same order. If Lia was 27th in the first ranking, what is her position in the second?

- $26$
- $13$
- $15$
- $14$

## Explanation

The candidates in positions $1, 3, 5, \ldots, 25$ precede Lia in the second list. Those are $13$ odd-numbered positions, so Lia is 14th in the second **ranking**.
