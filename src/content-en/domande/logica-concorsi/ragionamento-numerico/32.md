Paola is younger than Rino. Sara is younger than Rino. Tina is younger than Sara. No two of the four are the same age. Who is the youngest?

- Tina
- It cannot be determined
- Paola
- Sara

## Explanation

Rino is older than Paola and Sara and, by the **transitive property**, also older than Tina, so Rino is the oldest. Tina is younger than Sara, so the youngest is either Tina or Paola. The statements do not compare Paola with Sara or Tina. Both orders, Rino, Sara, Tina, Paola and Rino, Paola, Sara, Tina, are consistent with the information when listed from oldest to youngest. Paola is the youngest in the first and Tina in the second, so the youngest cannot be determined. Tina is a tempting answer if the statements are followed in order without placing Paola.
