Two clocks are set to the correct time at noon. The first gains $2$ minutes per hour and the second loses $3$ minutes per hour, both at constant rates. After how many actual hours will the times shown first differ by one hour?

- $30$
- $20$
- $12$
- $60$

## Explanation

In each actual hour the first clock gains $2$ minutes and the second loses $3$, so the difference between their readings increases by $2 + 3 = 5$ minutes per hour. A difference of $60$ minutes therefore takes $\dfrac{60}{5} = 12$ hours. The value $60$ comes from subtracting the two errors to obtain $3 - 2 = 1$ minute per hour, as if both clocks were off in the same direction. The values $30$ and $20$ consider only one clock at a time.
