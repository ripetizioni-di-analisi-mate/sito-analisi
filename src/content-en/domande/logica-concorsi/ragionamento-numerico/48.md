A normally running clock is photographed in a mirror. Its reflection appears to read exactly 2:20. What is the actual time on the twelve-hour cycle?

- 10:40
- 9:20
- 9:40
- 10:20

## Explanation

Reflection in the 12–6 diameter reverses direction. The real and reflected readings complete one cycle: 12:00 minus 2 hours 20 minutes gives 9:40.
