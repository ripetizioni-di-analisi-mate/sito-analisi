An analog clock, whose face is marked only with ticks, is seen reflected in a vertical mirror. In the mirror image the hands appear to show 2\:20. What time does the clock actually show?

- 10\:40
- 9\:20
- 9\:40
- 10\:20

## Explanation

A vertical mirror swaps right and left, so it reflects the clock face in the line through $12$ and $6$. A hand that in the image makes an angle of $\alpha$ degrees with the direction of $12$, measured clockwise, makes an angle of $360° - \alpha$ on the real clock. Therefore the minute hand, which is on $4$ in the image, is actually on $8$, and the hour hand, which is at $70°$ in the image, is actually at $290°$, that is two thirds of the way from $9$ to $10$. The actual time is therefore $12$ hours minus $2$ hours and $20$ minutes, that is 9\:40. The value 10\:40 comes from subtracting the hours from $12$ and the minutes from $60$ separately, and the value 9\:20 leaves the minutes unchanged.
