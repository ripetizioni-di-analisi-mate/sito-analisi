What is the larger angle between the hands of a clock at 2:50?

- $240^\circ$
- $145^\circ$
- $215^\circ$
- $120^\circ$

## Explanation

The minute hand is $300^\circ$ from 12, and the hour hand is $60^\circ + 25^\circ = 85^\circ$ from 12. The two **angles** are $300^\circ - 85^\circ = 215^\circ$ and $360^\circ - 215^\circ = 145^\circ$. The larger is $215^\circ$.
