A summer camp is open every day from June 15 through August 15, including both dates. How many days is it open?

- $62$
- $61$
- $63$
- $60$

## Explanation

The dates included in June are the 15th through the 30th, or $30 - 15 + 1 = 16$ days. All $31$ days of July are included, as are August 1 through 15. The camp is therefore open for $16 + 31 + 15 = 62$ days. The value $61$ is the number of days *elapsed* from June 15 to August 15, which excludes one endpoint. The value $63$ gives June $31$ days. The value $60$ counts two months of $30$ days each.
