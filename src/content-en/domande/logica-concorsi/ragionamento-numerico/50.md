A clock shows the correct time at 08:00. Until 12:00 it loses $2$ minutes per actual hour. From 12:00 onward, it gains $3$ minutes per actual hour. What time does it show at 15:00?

- 15\:01
- 14\:59
- 15\:09
- 15\:17

## Explanation

In the four hours before noon, the clock **falls behind** by $4 \cdot 2 = 8$ minutes. In the next three hours it gains $3 \cdot 3 = 9$ minutes. It makes up the loss and ends one minute ahead of the correct time, so it shows 15:01.
