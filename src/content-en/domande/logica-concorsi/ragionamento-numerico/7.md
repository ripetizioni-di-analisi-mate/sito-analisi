An analog clock shows a time with an odd number of minutes past the hour, such as 8:17 or 3:45. Which of the following could be the smaller of the two angles between the hands?

- $72°$
- $78°$
- $72.5°$
- $90°$

## Explanation

At $h$ hours and $m$ minutes, the angle between the hands is $|30h - 5.5m|$ or $360°$ minus this value. If $m$ is odd, the fractional part of $5.5m$ is $0.5$, while $30h$ is an integer. The angle therefore also has fractional part $0.5$. Of the values given, only $72.5°$ is possible. It is the angle at 7:25 because $|210 - 137.5| = 72.5$. The values $72°$, $78°$, and $90°$ are multiples of $6°$, like the angles obtained by leaving the hour hand on the hour mark.
