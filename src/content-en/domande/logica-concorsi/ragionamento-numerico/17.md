Assume Los Angeles is $17$ hours behind Tokyo. A flight leaves Tokyo at 22:30 on Monday, Tokyo time, and lasts $13$ hours and $50$ minutes. On what day and at what local time does it arrive in Los Angeles?

- Tuesday at 19:20
- Tuesday at 12:20
- Wednesday at 5:20
- Monday at 19:20

## Explanation

Monday at 22:30 plus $13$ hours and $50$ minutes gives Tuesday at 12:20 in Tokyo. Los Angeles is $17$ hours behind. Subtracting $17$ hours from Tuesday at 12:20 takes us back to Monday at 19:20. The local arrival time comes before the departure time shown in Tokyo because Los Angeles is $17$ hours behind. Tuesday at 19:20 subtracts the hours without changing the day. Wednesday at 5:20 adds the time difference instead of subtracting it. Tuesday at 12:20 is the arrival time in Tokyo.
