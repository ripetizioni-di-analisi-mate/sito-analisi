How many times do the hands of a clock form a right angle between 12:00 and 18:00 on the same day?

- $22$
- $11$
- $12$
- $6$

## Explanation

At 12:00 the hands coincide. The minute hand gains $5.5°$ on the hour hand per minute, so in the $360$ minutes up to 18:00 it gains $5.5 \cdot 360 = 1980°$. The hands form a right angle each time the minute hand leads by an odd multiple of $90°$, that is, $90°$, $270°$, $450°$, and so on. The odd multiples of $90°$ up to $1980°$ range from $1 \cdot 90°$ to $21 \cdot 90° = 1890°$, so the hands form $11$ right angles. The value $12$ counts two right angles in every hour, but between 14:00 and 16:00 there are only three, at about 14:27, at 15:00, and at about 15:33. The value $22$ is the number of right angles in $12$ hours.
