In a line of $23$ people, Carlo is 6th from the front and Rosa is 7th from the back. They switch places. What is Carlo’s position from the back after the switch?

- $6$
- $16$
- $7$
- $17$

## Explanation

The **switch** puts Carlo in the place Rosa occupied. That place is already given as 7th from the back. The other people stay put, and the length of the line is unchanged.
