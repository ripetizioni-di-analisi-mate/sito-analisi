An urn contains $7$ red balls, $5$ green balls, and $3$ blue balls. What is the minimum number you must draw in the dark to guarantee at least one ball of each color?

- $3$
- $15$
- $13$
- $9$

## Explanation

In the **worst case**, all balls of the two most numerous colors are drawn first: the $7$ red balls and the $5$ green balls. After $12$ draws there is still no blue ball. The next ball must be blue, so $7 + 5 + 1 = 13$ draws are needed. The value $9$ uses up the green and blue balls first, although these are the two least numerous colors. The value $3$ is the minimum number of balls among which all three colors *can* appear, without a guarantee. The value $15$ corresponds to drawing every ball.
