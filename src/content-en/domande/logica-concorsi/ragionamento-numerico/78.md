An office assigns $37$ applications to $8$ staff members. Each application goes to exactly one person. Consider the staff member who receives the most applications. What is the smallest number this person could receive?

- $4$
- $8$
- $6$
- $5$

## Explanation

If each staff member received at most $4$ applications, only $32$ could be assigned. By the **pigeonhole principle**, at least one staff member receives at least $5$ applications. Assigning $5$ applications each to five staff members and $4$ each to three staff members accounts for $37$ without anyone receiving more than $5$.
