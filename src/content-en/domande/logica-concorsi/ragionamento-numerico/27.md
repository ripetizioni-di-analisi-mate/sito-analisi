A non-leap year starts on a Tuesday. On what day of the week does March 1 fall that year?

- Thursday
- Saturday
- Sunday
- Friday

## Explanation

From January 1 to March 1, the $31$ days of January and the $28$ days of February pass, a total of $59$ days. Since $59 = 8 \cdot 7 + 3$, the **remainder modulo 7** gives an advance of $3$ places in the week, taking Tuesday to Friday. Saturday results from giving February $29$ days, as in a leap year, and Sunday from giving February $30$ days. Thursday counts $58$ days and is the day of the week on February 28.
