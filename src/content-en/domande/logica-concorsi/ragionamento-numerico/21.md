March 1, 2100, is a Monday. On what day of the week does February 1, 2100, fall?

- Friday
- Monday
- Tuesday
- Sunday

## Explanation

The interval from February 1 to March 1 equals the number of days in February. The year 2100 is divisible by $100$ but not by $400$, so by the **leap year rule** it is not a leap year, and February 2100 has $28$ days. Since $28 = 4 \cdot 7$, February 1 falls on the same day of the week as March 1, namely Monday. Sunday treats 2100 as a leap year and counts $29$ days backward. Tuesday counts those $29$ days forward. Friday uses the $31$ days in January instead of the number in February.
