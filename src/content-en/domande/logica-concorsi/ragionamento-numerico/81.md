A class has $30$ students. At least how many students are guaranteed to share a birth month?

- $3$
- $4$
- $2$
- $6$

## Explanation

If there were at most $2$ births in each month, that would account for only $24$ students. By the **pigeonhole principle**, at least $3$ students must share a birth month. Six months with three births and six with two show that the guaranteed number cannot be increased to four.
