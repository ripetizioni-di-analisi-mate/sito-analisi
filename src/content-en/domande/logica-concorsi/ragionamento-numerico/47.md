At 11:18 and at 11:28, the smaller angle between the hands of a clock is measured. By how much does it increase between the two measurements?

- $47^\circ$
- $5^\circ$
- $55^\circ$
- $60^\circ$

## Explanation

Measured from 12, the hour hand is at $339^\circ$ and the minute hand is at $108^\circ$ at 11:18. The difference is $231^\circ$ and the **smaller angle** is $360^\circ - 231^\circ = 129^\circ$. At 11:28 the positions are $344^\circ$ and $168^\circ$, so the smaller angle is $176^\circ$. The increase is $176^\circ - 129^\circ = 47^\circ$.
