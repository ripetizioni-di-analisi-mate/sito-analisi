In two consecutive months, the 13th falls on a Friday. How many days does the first month have?

- $29$
- $28$
- $31$
- $30$

## Explanation

The interval from the 13th of one month to the 13th of the next equals the number of days in the first month. Two dates falling on the same day of the week are separated by a multiple of $7$ days. Of $28$, $29$, $30$, and $31$, the only multiple of $7$ is $28 = 4 \cdot 7$. The first month is therefore February in a non-leap year, as in 2026. If it had $29$, $30$, or $31$ days, the 13th of the next month would fall on a Saturday, Sunday, or Monday, respectively.
