Irene and Davide are siblings. Irene is Viola’s mother and Davide is Leo’s father. How are Viola and Leo related?

- Leo is Viola’s uncle.
- They are first cousins.
- They are siblings.
- Viola is Leo’s aunt.

## Explanation

Viola is Irene’s daughter, and Leo is Davide’s son. Since Irene and Davide are siblings, their children share the grandparents on that side of the family. They are **first cousins**.
