A box holds $5$ black pens, $4$ blue pens, and $3$ green pens. What is the smallest number of pens that must be drawn without replacement to guarantee at least one of each color?

- $12$
- $10$
- $8$
- $9$

## Explanation

All $5$ black pens and all $4$ blue pens could be drawn without finding a green one. In the **worst case**, no green pen has appeared after $9$ draws. The tenth draw guarantees all colors because at most nine pens can come from only two colors.
