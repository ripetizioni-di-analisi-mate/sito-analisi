If the day before yesterday was Thursday, what day of the week will it be the day after tomorrow?

- Sunday
- Monday
- Saturday
- Tuesday

## Explanation

The day before yesterday is two days before today, so today is Saturday, two places after Thursday. The day after tomorrow is two days after today, so it is Monday. In all, $4$ days pass between the day before yesterday and the day after tomorrow. Saturday is today, and Sunday is tomorrow.
