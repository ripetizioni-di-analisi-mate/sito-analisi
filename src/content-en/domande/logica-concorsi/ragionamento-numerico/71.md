In a ranking with no ties, Elia is 17th. After some withdrawals, $4$ candidates who were ahead of him and $6$ who were behind him are removed. What is Elia’s new position?

- $13$
- $7$
- $11$
- $15$

## Explanation

Elia’s **position** depends only on the number of people ahead of him. There were $16$ and $4$ are removed, so $12$ remain ahead. Elia moves to 13th place.
