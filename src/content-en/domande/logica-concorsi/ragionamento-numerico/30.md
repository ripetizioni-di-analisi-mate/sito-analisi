In line at the post office, Teresa has $6$ people ahead of her and $9$ behind her. How many people are in line, including Teresa?

- $15$
- $16$
- $14$
- $17$

## Explanation

The line consists of those ahead of Teresa, Teresa herself, and those behind her, so it contains $6 + 1 + 9 = 16$ people. The formula for **positions in a line** gives the same result because Teresa is seventh from the front and tenth from the back, and $7 + 10 - 1 = 16$. The value $15$ leaves out Teresa, and $17$ adds her two positions without subtracting $1$. The value $14$ applies the formula to $6$ and $9$, which count the people ahead of and behind her rather than giving her positions.
