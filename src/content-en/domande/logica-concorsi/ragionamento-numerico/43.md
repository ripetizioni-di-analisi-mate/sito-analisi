How is my father’s father related to my daughter?

- Her uncle
- Her grandfather
- Her great-grandfather
- Her great-great-grandfather

## Explanation

My father’s father is my grandfather. My daughter is one generation below me, so my father is her grandfather and my father’s father is her **great-grandfather**. “Grandfather” describes the relationship to me instead of the relationship to my daughter. “Great-great-grandfather” adds one generation too many.
