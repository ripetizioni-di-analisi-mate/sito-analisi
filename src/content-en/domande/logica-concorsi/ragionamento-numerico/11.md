A clock is set to the correct time at 6:00 and runs fast at a constant rate. At 18:00 that day it shows 18:24. What time did it show at 11:30?

- 11\:41
- 11\:42
- 11\:54
- 11\:40

## Explanation

From 6:00 to 18:00, $12$ hours pass and the clock gains $24$ minutes, so it gains $\dfrac{24}{12} = 2$ minutes per hour. From 6:00 to 11:30, $5.5$ hours pass, so it gains $5.5 \cdot 2 = 11$ minutes and shows 11:41. The time 11:40 counts only the $5$ full hours and 11:42 rounds the interval to $6$ hours. The time 11:54 adds the entire gain for the day.
