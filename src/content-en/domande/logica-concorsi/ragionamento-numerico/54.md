A shift runs from 21:35 to 05:10 the next day. There are two breaks of $20$ and $35$ minutes. How much time is actually spent working?

- 7 hours and 35 minutes
- 6 hours and 40 minutes
- 6 hours and 55 minutes
- 7 hours and 10 minutes

## Explanation

From 21:35 to midnight is $145$ minutes, and from midnight to 05:10 is $310$ minutes. Subtract the $55$ minutes of **breaks** from the total. That leaves $145 + 310 - 55 = 400$ minutes, or $6$ hours and $40$ minutes.
