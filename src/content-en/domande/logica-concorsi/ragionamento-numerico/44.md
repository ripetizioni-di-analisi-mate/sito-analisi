Anna, who is married, says of Bruno, “Bruno’s mother is my husband’s mother-in-law.” How is Bruno’s mother related to Anna?

- Her mother
- Her sister-in-law
- Her grandmother
- Her mother-in-law

## Explanation

A person’s mother-in-law is the mother of their spouse. The mother-in-law of Anna’s husband is therefore his wife’s mother, that is, Anna’s mother. Thus Bruno’s mother is Anna’s **mother**, and Bruno and Anna have the same mother. “Mother-in-law” gives the relationship to Anna’s husband instead of the relationship to Anna.
