A first assessment takes place on a Tuesday. The second takes place $73$ days after the first, and the third takes place another $73$ days after the second. On what day of the week does the third assessment take place?

- Monday
- Wednesday
- Friday
- Sunday

## Explanation

Each interval of $73$ days equals ten **full weeks** plus three days. Across two intervals, the day of the week advances by six days. Six days after Tuesday is Monday.
