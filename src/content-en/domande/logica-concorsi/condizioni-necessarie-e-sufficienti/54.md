For integers, what kind of condition is being a multiple of $6$ for being a multiple of $18$?

- Necessary but not sufficient
- Sufficient but not necessary
- Neither necessary nor sufficient
- Necessary and sufficient

## Explanation

Every multiple of $18$ is a multiple of $6$ because $18=6\cdot 3$. The number $12$ shows that divisibility by $6$, although **necessary**, is not enough on its own.
