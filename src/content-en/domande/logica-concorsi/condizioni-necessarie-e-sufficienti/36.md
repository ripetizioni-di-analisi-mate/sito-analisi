The statements “If Elisa organizes the trip, the group travels by bus” and “If the trip is not on Sunday, the group does not travel by bus” are true. Which statement must be true?

- If Elisa organizes the trip, it takes place on Sunday.
- If the trip takes place on Sunday, Elisa organizes it.
- If Elisa does not organize the trip, it does not take place on Sunday.
- If the group travels by bus, Elisa organizes the trip.

## Explanation

The statement about the trip not taking place on Sunday is equivalent to its **contrapositive**, “if the group travels by bus, the trip takes place on Sunday.” Written this way, the two implications form a chain from Elisa organizing the trip to traveling by bus, and from traveling by bus to Sunday. By the **transitive property**, “if Elisa organizes the trip, it takes place on Sunday.” The statement beginning “if the trip takes place on Sunday” is the converse of this conclusion, the one beginning “if Elisa does not organize the trip” is its inverse, and the one beginning “if the group travels by bus” is the converse of the premise about Elisa.
