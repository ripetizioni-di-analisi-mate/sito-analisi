A company has this rule: “Only employees with at least five years of service can become office managers.” In the purchasing department, Gino has eight years of service and all the other employees have fewer than five. Which statement about the employees in the purchasing department must be true?

- Gino becomes an office manager.
- If an employee in the purchasing department becomes an office manager, that employee is Gino.
- No employee in the purchasing department becomes an office manager.
- An employee in the purchasing department other than Gino becomes an office manager.

## Explanation

The word “only” makes at least five years of service a **necessary condition**, so the rule translates as “if an employee becomes an office manager, then they have at least five years of service.” Gino is the only employee in the purchasing department who meets this requirement. By **modus tollens**, no other employee in the department becomes an office manager. Therefore, if an employee in purchasing becomes an office manager, that employee is Gino. The rule does not say that meeting the requirement is enough, so we cannot conclude that Gino becomes an office manager. For the same reason, we cannot conclude that nobody in the department becomes one, since Gino could.
