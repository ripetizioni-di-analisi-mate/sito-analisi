In a selection process, scoring at least $24$ points guarantees admission. A candidate who scored $22$ points was also admitted. What kind of condition is the $24$-point threshold for admission?

- Necessary but not sufficient
- Neither necessary nor sufficient
- Sufficient but not necessary
- Necessary and sufficient

## Explanation

The rules guarantee admission at or above the threshold. The candidate admitted with $22$ points shows that reaching $24$ points, although **sufficient**, is not required in every case.
