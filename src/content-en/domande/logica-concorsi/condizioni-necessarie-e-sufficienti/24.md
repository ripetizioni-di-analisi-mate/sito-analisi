A department has this rule: “If an employee works both Sunday and Monday, they have Tuesday off.” This week Sara works Sunday and Monday, while Ugo works Sunday but not Monday. Which statement must be true?

- Sara has Tuesday off.
- Ugo does not have Tuesday off.
- Ugo has Tuesday off.
- Sara and Ugo both have Tuesday off.

## Explanation

For Sara the antecedent is true because she works both Sunday and Monday. Therefore, by **modus ponens**, Sara has Tuesday off. For Ugo the antecedent is false, and a false antecedent implies nothing about the consequent. Ugo may or may not have Tuesday off, so the statements about him are not necessarily true. Choosing “Ugo does not have Tuesday off” is the error of **denying the antecedent**.
