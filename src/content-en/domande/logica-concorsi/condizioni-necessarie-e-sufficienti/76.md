To award a grant, an agency requires both the representative's signature and the account details. An application lacks the signature. Which decision follows?

- The application cannot receive the grant.
- The application is accepted even without the account details.
- The account details were not provided.
- The application is accepted if it includes the account details.

## Explanation

Both requirements must be met to receive the grant. The missing signature rules out acceptance by **modus tollens**, regardless of whether the account details were provided.
