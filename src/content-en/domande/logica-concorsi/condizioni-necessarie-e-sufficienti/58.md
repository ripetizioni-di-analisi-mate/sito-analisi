Anyone who has completed the training may collect the keys to the storage facility. We know that Eva has not completed the training. Which statement contradicts the given facts?

- Eva cannot collect the keys.
- Eva may collect the keys under a separate authorization.
- Eva has not completed the training and may collect the keys.
- Eva has completed the training and may collect the keys.

## Explanation

The given fact about Eva rules out her having completed the training. The rule permits anyone who has completed it to collect the keys, while leaving open other ways to obtain them.
