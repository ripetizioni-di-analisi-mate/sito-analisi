The statements “If the client signs, the contract goes to the legal department,” “If the contract goes to the legal department, Bassi reviews it,” and “If Bassi reviews the contract, it is filed by Friday” are true. The contract was not filed by Friday. Which statement must be true?

- Bassi reviewed the contract.
- The client signed and Bassi did not review the contract.
- The contract went to the legal department.
- The client did not sign.

## Explanation

The three premises form a chain of implications from the client's signature to the contract being filed by Friday. The given fact negates the last link, so follow the chain backward by applying **modus tollens** repeatedly. Since the contract was not filed by Friday, Bassi did not review it, and therefore it did not go to the legal department. Consequently the client did not sign, and each of the other three statements contradicts one of these steps.
