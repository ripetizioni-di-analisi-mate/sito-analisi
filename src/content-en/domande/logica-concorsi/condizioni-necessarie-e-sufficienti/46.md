A faulty sensor is enough to stop a production line. Opening the access door is also enough to stop it. If the line has stopped and the door is closed, which situation is still possible?

- The door is closed and the line is running.
- The sensor is faulty and the line is running.
- The door is open and the sensor is working.
- The sensor is working and the line has stopped for another reason.

## Explanation

Each condition on its own guarantees that the line stops. A third cause may keep the line stopped even when the door is closed and the sensor is working.
