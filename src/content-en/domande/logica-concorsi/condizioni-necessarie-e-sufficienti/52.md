Participating in a workshop requires a reservation. Booking online is sufficient to receive an entry code. Which situation is possible under these two rules alone?

- Someone books at the counter, receives a code, and enters.
- Someone books online without having a reservation.
- Someone books online and receives no code.
- Someone enters without having made a reservation.

## Explanation

Entry requires a reservation, and an online reservation guarantees a code. Both rules allow someone to book at the counter, receive a code there, and enter the workshop.
