An announcement says that certification is required to receive an appointment. Which pair fills in the blanks correctly? Certification is a ___ condition for receiving the appointment, and receiving the appointment is a ___ condition for concluding that the person is certified.

- Sufficient, sufficient.
- Necessary, necessary.
- Necessary, sufficient.
- Sufficient, necessary.

## Explanation

The implication goes from appointment to certification. Certification is **necessary**, and knowing that the person received the appointment is **sufficient** to infer that they are certified.
