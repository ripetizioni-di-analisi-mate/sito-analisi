The rules of a competition state, “Only federation members are admitted to the competition” and “People who have been federation members for at least two years are admitted.” Lia has been a member for three years, Max is not a member, and Nadia has been a member for one year. Which statement must be true?

- Nadia is admitted to the competition.
- Nadia is not admitted to the competition.
- Lia is admitted to the competition and Max is not.
- Max is admitted to the competition.

## Explanation

The first rule makes federation membership a **necessary condition** for admission, and the second makes at least two years of membership a **sufficient condition**. Lia has been a member for three years, so by modus ponens applied to the second rule she is admitted. Max is not a member, so by modus tollens applied to the first rule he is not admitted. Nadia meets the necessary condition but does not meet the sufficient one, so the rules do not determine whether she is admitted.
