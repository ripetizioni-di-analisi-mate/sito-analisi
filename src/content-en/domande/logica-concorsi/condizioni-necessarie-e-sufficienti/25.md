A store follows this rule: “If new merchandise arrives by Thursday, the store puts it on display on Saturday.” On Saturday the new merchandise was not on display. Which statement must be true?

- The new merchandise arrived by Thursday.
- The new merchandise arrived on Friday.
- The new merchandise did not arrive by Thursday.
- The new merchandise arrived on Thursday and the store did not display it on Saturday.

## Explanation

The given fact negates the consequent, because on Saturday the new merchandise was not on display. By **modus tollens**, the antecedent is false as well, so the merchandise did not arrive by Thursday. This conclusion does not tell us when it arrived: it might have arrived on Friday or it might not have arrived yet. Therefore, the statement about Friday is not necessarily true. Merchandise that arrived on Thursday but was not displayed on Saturday would make the rule false.
