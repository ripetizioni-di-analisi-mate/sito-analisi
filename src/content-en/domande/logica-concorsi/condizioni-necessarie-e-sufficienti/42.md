A transit company's rules state, “The reduced-fare pass is reserved for students under $26$.” Which situation contradicts the rules?

- Ilaria, a $22$-year-old student, has a regular pass.
- Marco, a $24$-year-old worker, has a regular pass.
- Nina, a $25$-year-old student, has a reduced-fare pass.
- Gianni, a $27$-year-old student, has a reduced-fare pass.

## Explanation

“Reserved for” expresses a **necessary condition**, so the rule translates as “if someone has a reduced-fare pass, then they are a student under $26$.” The only case that makes this implication false is someone who has a reduced-fare pass but is not a student under $26$. Gianni has a reduced-fare pass and is $27$, so his situation contradicts the rule. Ilaria meets the requirements and uses a regular pass. This is consistent with the rule, which makes the requirements necessary without obliging those who meet them to choose the reduced fare.
