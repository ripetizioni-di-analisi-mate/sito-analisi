A system restarts when a fault has been fixed unless the manual lockout is active. The fault was fixed and the system restarted. What can we conclude about the lockout?

- On the given facts alone, the lockout could be active or inactive.
- The lockout is definitely active.
- The lockout can be active only if the fault is still present.
- The lockout is definitely inactive.

## Explanation

When the fault has been fixed and the lockout is inactive, a restart is guaranteed. If the lockout is active, the statement alone does not determine whether the system restarts.
