A company has this rule: “An employee receives the bonus if and only if they meet their annual target.” Which statement does *not* follow from the rule?

- Some employee receives the bonus without having met the annual target.
- If an employee does not meet the annual target, they do not receive the bonus.
- If an employee receives the bonus, they met the annual target.
- Meeting the annual target is sufficient for receiving the bonus.

## Explanation

“If and only if” expresses a **necessary and sufficient condition**, so the rule contains two implications: “if an employee meets the target, they receive the bonus” and “if an employee receives the bonus, they met the target.” The statement about a sufficient condition is the first implication, and the statement beginning “if an employee receives the bonus” is the second. The statement beginning “if an employee does not meet the target” is the contrapositive of the second, so it also follows. An employee who receives the bonus without meeting the target would make the second implication false. Therefore the statement beginning “some employee” contradicts the rule.
