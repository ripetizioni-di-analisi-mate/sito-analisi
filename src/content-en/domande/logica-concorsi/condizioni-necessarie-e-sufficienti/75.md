A maintenance task requires a technical form. The form is completed if the task is scheduled and someone has been assigned responsibility for it. Which information would be enough to establish that the form was completed?

- The task is scheduled.
- The task is scheduled and someone has been assigned responsibility for it.
- Someone has been assigned responsibility for the task.
- The task is scheduled and nobody has been assigned responsibility for it.

## Explanation

The second statement gives a **sufficient condition** consisting of two facts. Both scheduling the task and assigning someone responsibility for it are needed to apply this rule.
