An online store applies this rule: “If an order exceeds $50$ euros, shipping is free.” Which of the following statements follows from the rule?

- Exceeding $50$ euros is a sufficient condition for free shipping.
- Exceeding $50$ euros is a necessary condition for free shipping.
- Exceeding $50$ euros is a necessary and sufficient condition for free shipping.
- Exceeding $50$ euros is neither a necessary nor a sufficient condition for free shipping.

## Explanation

The rule has the form “if $A$, then $B$,” where $A$ = “the order exceeds $50$ euros” and $B$ = “shipping is free.” In an implication, the antecedent is a **sufficient condition** for the consequent, so exceeding $50$ euros is enough to qualify for free shipping. The rule says nothing about smaller orders, which may qualify for free shipping during a promotion. Therefore, it does not follow that the condition is necessary. Treating it as necessary reverses the direction of the arrow.
