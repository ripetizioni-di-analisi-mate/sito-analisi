A badge is activated only after the identification document has been verified. The badge is active. Which step in the reasoning is valid?

- The verification also covers the holder's home address.
- From activation we can infer that the document was verified.
- The document was not verified.
- The badge holder performed the verification.

## Explanation

“Only after” makes verification a **necessary condition** for activation. Since the badge is active, we can infer that the check took place.
