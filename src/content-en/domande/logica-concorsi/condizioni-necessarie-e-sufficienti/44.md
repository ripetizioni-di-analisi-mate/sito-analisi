A university has this rule: “The laboratory stays open after 18\:00 only if a technician is present and the request to keep it open has been approved.” Which situation *cannot* occur?

- The lab stays open after 18\:00, a technician is present, and the request was not approved.
- The lab closes at 18\:00, a technician is present, and the request was approved.
- The lab closes at 18\:00, no technician is present, and the request was approved.
- The lab stays open after 18\:00, a technician is present, and the request was approved.

## Explanation

“Only if” introduces a **necessary condition**, so the rule translates as “if the lab stays open after 18\:00, then a technician is present and the request has been approved.” The only case ruled out has the lab open and the consequent false. The consequent is false if even one requirement is missing. Therefore the lab cannot be open without approval, even if the technician is present. The lab may close when both requirements are met because the rule makes them necessary without saying that they are sufficient.
