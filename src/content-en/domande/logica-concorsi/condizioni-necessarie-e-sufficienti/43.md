A selection process has this rule: “A candidate is admitted to the oral exam if and only if they score at least $60$ on the written exam.” The written scores are integers. Which statement is true?

- Scoring at least $70$ is a necessary condition for admission to the oral exam.
- Scoring at least $50$ is a sufficient condition for admission to the oral exam.
- Scoring at least $70$ is a sufficient condition for admission to the oral exam.
- Scoring at least $50$ is a necessary and sufficient condition for admission to the oral exam.

## Explanation

Under the rule, a candidate is admitted exactly when they score at least $60$. Anyone who scores at least $70$ also scores at least $60$ and is admitted, so scoring at least $70$ is a **sufficient condition**. A candidate with $65$ is admitted without scoring $70$, so the $70$-point condition is not necessary. A candidate with $55$ has at least $50$ points and is not admitted, so scoring at least $50$ is only a **necessary condition**.
