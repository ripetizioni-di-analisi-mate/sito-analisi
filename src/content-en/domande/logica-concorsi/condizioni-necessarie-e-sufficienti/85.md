A late application is rejected unless the system was down. Anita's application was late and was not rejected. What follows?

- The system was down.
- The system was working.
- The system was down and the application included all attachments.
- The application included all attachments.

## Explanation

With the system working, every late application would have been rejected. Anita's application was late and was not rejected, which implies that the system was down.
