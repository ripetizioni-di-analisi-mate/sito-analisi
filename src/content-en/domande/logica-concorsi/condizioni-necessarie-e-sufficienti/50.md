Forwarding a case requires a designated representative. If a representative is appointed, a notification is sent. Which additional rule would guarantee that every notification sent corresponds to a case that has been forwarded?

- Require the case to be forwarded whenever a notification has been sent.
- Allow a case to be forwarded even without a representative.
- Make notification a requirement for forwarding the case.
- Make appointing a representative sufficient to forward the case.

## Explanation

The required direction goes from notification to forwarding. A rule requiring the case to be forwarded whenever a notification about it is sent gives exactly this implication, even if something other than the appointment triggered the notification.
