If the receipt is validated, the case is closed. If the case is closed, the file becomes available for inspection. Anna's case is closed and the file is available for inspection. What still needs to be verified?

- The file is not available for inspection.
- The case was closed.
- The receipt was validated.
- The file is available for inspection.

## Explanation

Closing the case follows from validating the receipt. Closure may have another cause, however, so neither closure nor availability for inspection lets us infer that the receipt was validated.
