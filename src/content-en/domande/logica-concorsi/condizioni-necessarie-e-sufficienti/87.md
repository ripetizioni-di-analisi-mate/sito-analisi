A refund is approved only if the receipt is legible. Which situation disproves the rule?

- Rejection with an illegible receipt.
- Rejection with a legible receipt.
- Approval with an illegible receipt.
- Approval with a legible receipt.

## Explanation

Approval implies a legible receipt. The only case that makes the **implication** false combines approval with the absence of the requirement.
