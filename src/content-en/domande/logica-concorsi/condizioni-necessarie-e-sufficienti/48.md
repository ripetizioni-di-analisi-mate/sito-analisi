A service admits only users with a verified account. Having both a verified account and active user status is sufficient for access. Elena has a verified account, but her user status is inactive. Which statement follows from these rules alone?

- Elena is guaranteed access.
- Elena's account is not verified.
- Elena cannot gain access.
- Elena meets the verified-account requirement, and whether she gains access is undetermined.

## Explanation

A verified account is necessary for access. A verified account together with active user status would be sufficient, so verification alone does not settle Elena's case.
