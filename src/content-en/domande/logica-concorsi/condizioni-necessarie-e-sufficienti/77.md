An operator issues passes to all and only those residents who have paid the fee. Luca is a resident and received a pass. Which deduction is valid?

- Luca paid the fee and did not receive a pass.
- Luca paid the fee.
- Luca is exempt from the fee.
- Luca received a pass without being a resident.

## Explanation

“All and only” requires both directions. From the issue of a pass, we can infer both residency and payment of the fee.
