Four offices use different rules to approve applications. In which office is the designated contact's signature sufficient for approval?

- An application is approved only if the designated contact signed it.
- Every application that is signed by the designated contact and officially registered is approved.
- Every application signed by the designated contact is approved.
- Signed applications that have not been officially registered are rejected.

## Explanation

Approving every signed application means the signature alone guarantees approval. The rule imposing two requirements does not guarantee approval for signed applications that have not been registered.
