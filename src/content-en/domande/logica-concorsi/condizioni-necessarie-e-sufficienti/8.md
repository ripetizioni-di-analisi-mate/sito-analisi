For a positive integer $n$, which of the following is a necessary condition for $n$ to be a multiple of $15$?

- $n$ is a multiple of $30$
- $n$ is a multiple of $10$
- $n$ is odd
- $n$ is a multiple of $5$

## Explanation

A condition is necessary if every multiple of $15$ satisfies it. If $n = 15k$ for an integer $k$, then $n = 5 \cdot 3k$, so every multiple of $15$ is a multiple of $5$. The number $15$ is a multiple of neither $30$ nor $10$, and $30$ is a multiple of $15$ and is even. Therefore, the other three conditions are not necessary. The condition “$n$ is a multiple of $30$” is sufficient. Choosing it confuses a necessary condition with a sufficient one.
