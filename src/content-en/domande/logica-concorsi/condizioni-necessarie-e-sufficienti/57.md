The rules require at least one of two qualifications to drive a vehicle: a category C license or internal authorization. Andrea drives the vehicle and does not have a category C license. Which deduction is valid?

- Andrea has both a category C license and internal authorization.
- Andrea has a category C license.
- Andrea does not have internal authorization.
- Andrea has internal authorization.

## Explanation

Anyone who drives the vehicle must hold at least one of the two qualifications. Since Andrea does not have a category C license, Andrea must have internal authorization.
