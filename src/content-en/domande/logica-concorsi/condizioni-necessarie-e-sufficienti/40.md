An announcement states, “Only candidates who are at least $18$ years old and hold a diploma may take part in the selection process.” Which candidate is definitely excluded?

- Ivo, who is $17$ years old and holds a diploma
- Lea, who is $18$ years old and holds a diploma
- Nico, who is $19$ years old, holds a diploma, and has no driver's license
- Paola, who is $22$ years old, holds a diploma, and submitted her application on the last possible day

## Explanation

The word “only” makes the two requirements **necessary conditions**, so by **modus tollens** any candidate who lacks even one is excluded. Ivo does not meet the age requirement, so he is definitely excluded. Lea is exactly $18$ years old, and “at least $18$” includes $18$, so she meets both requirements. Nico and Paola also meet both requirements, and the announcement mentions neither a license nor the application date. Therefore, we cannot conclude that Lea, Nico, or Paola is excluded.
