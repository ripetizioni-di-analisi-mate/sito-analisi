An integer $n$ is divisible by $12$ exactly when it is divisible by both $3$ and $4$. What kind of condition is divisibility by $12$ for meeting the other two requirements together?

- Neither necessary nor sufficient
- Sufficient but not necessary
- Necessary but not sufficient
- Necessary and sufficient

## Explanation

Since $3$ and $4$ are relatively prime, their least common multiple is $12$. The numbers that meet both requirements are exactly the multiples of $12$, so the condition is **necessary and sufficient**.
