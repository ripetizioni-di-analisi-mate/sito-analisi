For a real number $x$, what kind of condition is “$x > 2$” for “$x^2 > 4$”?

- sufficient but not necessary
- necessary but not sufficient
- necessary and sufficient
- neither necessary nor sufficient

## Explanation

If $x > 2$, then $x$ is positive and $x^2 = x \cdot x > 2 \cdot 2 = 4$, so “$x > 2$” is a **sufficient condition** for “$x^2 > 4$.” The number $x = -3$ satisfies $x^2 = 9 > 4$ but does not satisfy $x > 2$, so the condition is not necessary. The inequality $x^2 > 4$ holds for $x > 2$ and also for $x < -2$. Overlooking the negative values leads to the mistaken conclusion that the two statements are equivalent.
