For a positive integer $n$, which of the following is a sufficient condition for $n$ to be divisible by $6$?

- $n$ is divisible by $3$
- $n$ is even
- the units digit of $n$ is $6$
- $n$ is divisible by $12$

## Explanation

A condition is sufficient if every number satisfying it is divisible by $6$. If $n$ is divisible by $12$, then $n = 12k = 6 \cdot 2k$ for some integer $k$, so $n$ is divisible by $6$. Each of the other three conditions has a counterexample: $9$ is divisible by $3$, $4$ is even, and $16$ has units digit $6$, yet none of the three is divisible by $6$. The conditions “$n$ is divisible by $3$” and “$n$ is even” are *necessary*. Choosing either confuses a necessary condition with a sufficient one.
