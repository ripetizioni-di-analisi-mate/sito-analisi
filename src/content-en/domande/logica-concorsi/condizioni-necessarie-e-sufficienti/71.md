Passing the written exam is sufficient for taking the oral exam. Anyone absent from the written exam does not pass it. A candidate was absent. Which conclusion do the two rules guarantee?

- The candidate is definitely excluded from the oral exam.
- The candidate passed the written exam.
- The candidate did not pass the written exam.
- The candidate definitely may take the oral exam.

## Explanation

Absence implies that the candidate did not pass the written exam. The oral-exam rule provides a sufficient route to admission, so these premises leave admission undetermined.
