Four yes-or-no statuses are recorded for an automated message: sending is authorized, the message is signed, a receipt is generated, and the log is updated. Authorization requires a signature, the receipt is generated exactly when sending is authorized, and the log can be updated only if there is a receipt. How many combinations satisfy these rules?

- $6$
- $2$
- $16$
- $4$

## Explanation

When sending is authorized, the signature and receipt are required and the log can be in either of two states. Without authorization there is no receipt and no log update, while the signature can be in either of two states. This gives $2+2=4$ combinations.
