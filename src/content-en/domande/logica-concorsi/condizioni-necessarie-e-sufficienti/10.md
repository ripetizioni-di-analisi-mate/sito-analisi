A store has this rule: “Merchandise cannot be exchanged without a receipt.” Laura exchanged a shirt, and Paolo had the receipt for a pair of shoes. Which of the following must be true?

- Paolo exchanged the shoes.
- Laura did not have the receipt for the shirt.
- Laura had the receipt for the shirt.
- Paolo did not exchange the shoes.

## Explanation

“Without $B$, there is no $A$” means that $B$ is a **necessary condition** for $A$, so the rule translates as “if a customer exchanges an item, then they have the receipt.” Laura exchanged an item, so by **modus ponens** she had the receipt. All we know about Paolo is that he had the receipt. The rule requires a receipt without saying that having one is enough. Therefore, whether Paolo exchanged the shoes cannot be determined, and neither statement about him must be true.
