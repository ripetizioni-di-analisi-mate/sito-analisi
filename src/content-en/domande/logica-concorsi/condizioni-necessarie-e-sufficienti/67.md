Accessing the archive requires submitting an identification document. Maria submitted the document. What can we determine about her access?

- Access was denied.
- Both granting and denying access are consistent with the facts.
- The document was submitted after she gained access.
- Maria entered.

## Explanation

Submitting the document is a **necessary condition** for access. Someone who submits it could still be turned away for another reason, such as lacking the required authorization.
