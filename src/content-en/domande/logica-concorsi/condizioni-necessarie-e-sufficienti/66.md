An alert is sent if the river level exceeds the threshold or if the sluice gate jams. No alert was sent. Which conclusion must follow?

- The level exceeded the threshold and the gate remained free.
- The level did not exceed the threshold and the gate did not jam.
- At least one of the two events occurred.
- The gate jammed and the level stayed below the threshold.

## Explanation

Each event on its own is sufficient to trigger the alert. The absence of an alert rules out both events by **modus tollens**.
