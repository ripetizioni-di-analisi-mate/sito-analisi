The statements “If there is traffic on the beltway, Dario arrives late” and “If the regional train is canceled, Dario arrives late” are true. Today Dario arrived late. Which statement is correct?

- There is traffic on the beltway today.
- There is traffic on the beltway today or the regional train is canceled.
- Whether there is traffic on the beltway today cannot be determined.
- The regional train is canceled today.

## Explanation

The two implications have the same consequent, “Dario arrives late.” They do not form a chain because neither begins with the consequent of the other. The given fact affirms the consequent, and **affirming the consequent** yields no conclusion about the antecedents. Dario may have arrived late because of traffic, because the train was canceled, or for some other reason. Thus even the statement with “or” need not be true, and whether there is traffic on the beltway cannot be determined.
