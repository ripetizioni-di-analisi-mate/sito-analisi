Which statement has the same meaning as “If a candidate does not submit the documents by the end of March, they are not admitted”?

- If a candidate submits the documents by the end of March, they are admitted.
- If a candidate is admitted, they submitted the documents by the end of March.
- A candidate is admitted if and only if they submit the documents by the end of March.
- If a candidate is not admitted, they did not submit the documents by the end of March.

## Explanation

To form the **contrapositive** of “if not $C$, then not $M$,” interchange the two statements and negate both. Canceling both double negations gives “if $M$, then $C$.” With $C$ = “submits the documents by the end of March” and $M$ = “is admitted,” this is “if a candidate is admitted, they submitted the documents by the end of March.” Thus submitting the documents is a **necessary condition** for admission. “If a candidate submits the documents by the end of March, they are admitted” is the inverse and treats submission as sufficient. The statement beginning “if a candidate is not admitted” is the converse, which is equivalent to the inverse.
