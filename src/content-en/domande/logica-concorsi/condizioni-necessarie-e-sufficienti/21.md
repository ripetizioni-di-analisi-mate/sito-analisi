Two staff members interpret “A badge is required only for visitors.” Ada writes “if a badge is required, the person is a visitor”; Leo reverses the implication. Which case gives different truth values for their translations?

- A visitor required to wear a badge
- A visitor for whom a badge is optional
- A nonvisiting employee for whom a badge is optional
- No one is present

## Explanation

An optional badge for a visitor respects Ada's rule: no badge requirement was imposed on a nonvisitor. Leo's rule would require this visitor to wear a badge.
