The rules $A \Rightarrow B$ and $B \Leftrightarrow C$ are given. Which additional condition would make $A$ and $C$ equivalent?

- $C \Rightarrow B$
- $A \Rightarrow C$
- $C \Rightarrow A$
- $B \Rightarrow C$

## Explanation

$A$ implies $B$, and $B$ implies $C$, so $A \Rightarrow C$ already holds. The implication $C \Rightarrow A$ supplies the other direction required by **if and only if**.
