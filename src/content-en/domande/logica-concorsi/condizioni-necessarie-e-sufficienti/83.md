In an archive, all urgent cases are routed by the end of the morning. All cases routed by the end of the morning receive a number. Which statement is incompatible with these rules taken together?

- A routine case was not routed by the end of the morning.
- An urgent case went without a number.
- A numbered case was routed by the end of the morning.
- A routine case received a number.

## Explanation

Every urgent case is routed by the end of the morning. Because routing by that time guarantees a number, **transitivity** implies that every urgent case has a number.
