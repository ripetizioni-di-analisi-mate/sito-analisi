A store applies this rule: “If a customer spends more than $100$ euros, they receive a discount coupon or a free gift.” Here “or” allows the customer to receive both. Which customer shows that the rule was broken?

- Aldo, who spent $120$ euros and received a free gift but no discount coupon
- Bice, who spent $80$ euros and received neither the coupon nor the gift
- Ciro, who spent $150$ euros and received neither the coupon nor the gift
- Dina, who spent $200$ euros and received both the coupon and the gift

## Explanation

An implication is false only when its antecedent is true and its consequent is false. The consequent “receives a discount coupon or a gift” is false only for a customer who receives neither. Thus a customer who spends more than $100$ euros and receives nothing violates the rule. Ciro spent $150$ euros and received nothing, so his case shows that the rule was broken. Bice spent less than $100$ euros, so the rule places no restriction on her case. Aldo and Dina each received at least one of the two rewards. The inclusive “or” also covers Dina, who received both.
