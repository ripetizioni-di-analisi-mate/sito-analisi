Entering a lab requires an ID card. Anyone with an authorized escort may enter if they have the card. Sara has the card and an authorized escort. What follows?

- Sara must obtain a replacement escort.
- Sara may enter only after paying a fee.
- Sara may enter the lab.
- Sara must request an ID card.

## Explanation

The second rule states that the card and the escort are jointly **sufficient**. Sara meets both requirements and may enter.
