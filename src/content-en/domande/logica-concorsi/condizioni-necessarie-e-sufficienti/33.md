The statements “If the project is approved by the end of May, the office hires two technicians” and “If the office hires two technicians, the move is postponed” are true. The project was approved in April. Which statement must be true?

- The office does not hire any technicians.
- The move is postponed.
- The move is not postponed.
- The office hires two technicians and the move is not postponed.

## Explanation

The project was approved in April, hence by the end of May, so the antecedent of the first statement is true. By modus ponens the office hires two technicians. Applying modus ponens again to the second statement shows that the move is postponed. This argument uses the **transitive property**: from the two premises it derives “if the project is approved by the end of May, the move is postponed.”
