A course is always free for people under $18$. When there is no charge, a receipt showing a zero amount is issued. Which sequence is incompatible with the rule?

- A minor enrolls and receives a zero-amount receipt.
- An adult enrolls and pays.
- An adult enrolls and receives a zero-amount receipt.
- A minor enrolls without receiving a zero-amount receipt.

## Explanation

The age condition guarantees free enrollment, which in turn entails a zero-amount receipt. By **transitivity**, an enrolled minor must receive that receipt.
