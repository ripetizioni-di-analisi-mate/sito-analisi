An office has this rule: “If a case is urgent, Bianchi or Neri handles it.” Neither Bianchi nor Neri handles case $34$, and Neri handles case $35$. Which statement must be true?

- Case $34$ is not urgent.
- Case $35$ is urgent.
- Case $35$ is not urgent.
- Cases $34$ and $35$ are both urgent.

## Explanation

For case $34$, the consequent “Bianchi or Neri handles it” is false because neither handles it. By **modus tollens**, the case is not urgent. For case $35$, the consequent is true, and a true consequent implies nothing about the antecedent. Thus case $35$ may or may not be urgent. Concluding that it is urgent is the error of **affirming the consequent**.
