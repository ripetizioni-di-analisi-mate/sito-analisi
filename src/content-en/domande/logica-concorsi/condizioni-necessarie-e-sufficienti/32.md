A store has this rule: “If a customer has a loyalty card, they receive the discount.” Rosa received the discount. Which statement *could* be true?

- Rosa does not have a loyalty card.
- Rosa did not receive the discount.
- Rosa has a loyalty card and did not receive the discount.
- Rosa does not have a loyalty card and did not receive the discount.

## Explanation

A statement can be true if there is at least one situation consistent with the premises in which it is true. The three statements in which Rosa did not receive the discount contradict the given fact, so they cannot be true. Rosa may have received the discount during a promotion open to everyone. Thus the possibility that Rosa has no loyalty card is consistent with both the rule and the given fact. Ruling it out is the error of **affirming the consequent**: it assumes that receiving the discount implies having the card.
