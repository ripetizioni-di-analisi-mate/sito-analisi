A language center states, “Every course meets in person unless it has fewer than $10$ students enrolled.” Each course meets either in person or online, never both. The English course has $14$ students and the German course meets online. Which statement must be true?

- The English course meets in person and the German course has at least $10$ students.
- The English course meets in person and the German course has fewer than $10$ students.
- The English course meets online and the German course has fewer than $10$ students.
- The English course meets online and the German course has at least $10$ students.

## Explanation

“$A$ unless $B$” translates as “if not $B$, then $A$,” so the rule says “if a course has at least $10$ students, it meets in person.” The English course has $14$ students, so by **modus ponens** it meets in person. The German course meets online, so it does not meet in person. By **modus tollens**, it does not have at least $10$ students, meaning it has fewer than $10$. A German course with at least $10$ students would meet in person, so choices that assign it at least $10$ students contradict the rule.
