The statements “If it is foggy, the plane departs late” and “If it is foggy, the ferry does not depart” are true. Today the plane did not depart late. Which statement must be true?

- The ferry departs today.
- It is not foggy today.
- The ferry does not depart today.
- It is foggy today and the ferry departs.

## Explanation

The given fact negates the consequent of the first statement, so by **modus tollens** it is not foggy today. Without fog, the antecedent of the second statement is false, and a false antecedent implies nothing about the consequent. Thus the ferry may or may not depart. Concluding that it departs is the error of **denying the antecedent**. The statement combining fog with the ferry's departure contradicts the conclusion reached by modus tollens.
