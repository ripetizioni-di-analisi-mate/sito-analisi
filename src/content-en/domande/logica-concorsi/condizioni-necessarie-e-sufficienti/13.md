For a triangle, what kind of condition is “it has at least two equal sides” for “it is equilateral”?

- sufficient but not necessary
- necessary and sufficient
- necessary but not sufficient
- neither necessary nor sufficient

## Explanation

An equilateral triangle has three equal sides, so it has at least two equal sides, making the condition **necessary**. A triangle with side lengths $5$, $5$, and $6$ has two equal sides but is not equilateral, so the condition is not sufficient. Choosing “sufficient but not necessary” reverses the direction of the implication between the two statements.
