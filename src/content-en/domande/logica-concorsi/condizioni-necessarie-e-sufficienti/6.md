Which statement says that earning at least $500$ points is necessary for receiving the loyalty reward, without saying that it is also sufficient?

- “Only customers who earn at least $500$ points receive the loyalty reward.”
- “Customers who earn at least $500$ points receive the loyalty reward.”
- “If a customer does not receive the loyalty reward, they did not earn at least $500$ points.”
- “All and only those customers who earn at least $500$ points receive the loyalty reward.”

## Explanation

The word “only” introduces a **necessary condition**, so the statement containing “only” translates as “if a customer receives the reward, then they earned at least $500$ points.” It does not say that $500$ points are enough. “Customers who earn at least $500$ points receive the loyalty reward” makes the points sufficient. The statement beginning “if a customer does not receive the reward” is its contrapositive, so it says the same thing. The statement containing “all and only” makes the points both necessary and sufficient.
