A session is canceled if the room is unavailable. If the session is canceled, a notice is sent. No notice was sent today. Which pair is consistent with all the information?

- Room unavailable and session canceled.
- Room available and session not canceled.
- Room unavailable and session not canceled.
- Room available and session canceled.

## Explanation

The absence of a notice rules out cancellation. Applying **modus tollens** again rules out the room being unavailable.
