The office also opens on Saturdays unless maintenance is scheduled. Which consequence does this rule guarantee?

- If the office is closed on Saturday, maintenance is scheduled.
- If the office is open on Saturday, maintenance is scheduled.
- If the office is closed on Saturday, no maintenance is scheduled.
- If maintenance is scheduled, the office is closed on Saturday.

## Explanation

In the absence of scheduled maintenance, opening is guaranteed. The **contrapositive** lets us infer from the office being closed that maintenance is scheduled.
