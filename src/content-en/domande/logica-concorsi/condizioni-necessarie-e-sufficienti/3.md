Which description in terms of sets has the same meaning as “Every employee in the sales department has a company car”?

- The set of employees who have a company car is contained in the set of sales employees.
- The set of sales employees is contained in the set of employees who have a company car.
- The set of sales employees equals the set of employees who have a company car.
- The two sets overlap, and neither is contained in the other.

## Explanation

The statement is equivalent to “if an employee works in the sales department, then they have a company car.” An implication between two properties means that the set of elements with the first property is contained in the set of elements with the second. Thus all sales employees belong to the set of employees with a company car. The statement leaves open the possibility that employees in other departments also have company cars, so neither the reverse inclusion nor the equality of the sets follows.
