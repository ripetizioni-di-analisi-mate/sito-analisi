A company has this rule: “The Monday meeting takes place in room A unless room A is occupied.” This Monday the meeting did not take place in room A. Which statement must be true?

- Room A was not occupied this Monday.
- Room A is occupied every Monday.
- Room A was occupied this Monday.
- Room A was free this Monday, and the meeting took place in another room.

## Explanation

“$A$ unless $B$” translates as “if not $B$, then $A$.” In the Italian expression “a meno che non,” the word “non” does not negate the condition. Thus the rule says “if room A is not occupied, the meeting takes place in room A.” This Monday the consequent was false, so by **modus tollens** the antecedent was false: room A was occupied. This conclusion concerns only this Monday, so the statement that room A is occupied every Monday does not follow.
