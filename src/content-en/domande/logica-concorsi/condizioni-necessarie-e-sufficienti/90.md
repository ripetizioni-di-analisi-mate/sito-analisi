A certificate is issued if and only if the instructor signs. If the certificate is issued, the file can be closed. The file cannot be closed. Which deduction is guaranteed?

- The instructor did not sign and the certificate was issued.
- The instructor signed and the certificate was not issued.
- The instructor signed and the certificate was issued.
- The instructor did not sign and the certificate was not issued.

## Explanation

**Modus tollens** rules out the certificate having been issued. Since the instructor's signature would have been sufficient for it to be issued, the signature is ruled out as well.
