Passing the test is sufficient for enrollment in the advanced course. Davide is enrolled. Which additional information would be enough to conclude that he passed the test?

- Davide received the class schedule.
- Davide may also enroll in a second course.
- Some candidates passed the test.
- All enrolled students are required to have passed the test.

## Explanation

The initial rule goes from passing to enrollment. The added requirement gives the direction from enrollment to passing, allowing us to apply **modus ponens** to Davide.
