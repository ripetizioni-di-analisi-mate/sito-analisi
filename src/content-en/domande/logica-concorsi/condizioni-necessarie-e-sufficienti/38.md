The statements “If Ottavio is on vacation, Pia fills in for him,” “If Pia fills in for Ottavio, she works on Saturdays,” and “If Pia takes the training course, she does not work on Saturdays” are true. Which statement must be true?

- If Pia does not take the training course, Ottavio is on vacation.
- If Ottavio is on vacation, Pia does not take the training course.
- If Ottavio is not on vacation, Pia takes the training course.
- If Pia works on Saturdays, Ottavio is on vacation.

## Explanation

The statements about filling in for Ottavio form a chain that gives “if Ottavio is on vacation, Pia works on Saturdays.” The statement about the training course is equivalent to its **contrapositive**, “if Pia works on Saturdays, she does not take the training course,” which adds a final link. By the **transitive property**, “if Ottavio is on vacation, Pia does not take the training course.” The statement beginning “if Pia works on Saturdays” is the converse of part of the chain, and the statements beginning “if Pia does not take the course” and “if Ottavio is not on vacation” are respectively the converse and inverse of the conclusion.
