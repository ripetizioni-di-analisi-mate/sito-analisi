Which statement has the same meaning as “To enroll in the advanced course, you must have passed the basic course”?

- Passing the basic course is enough to enroll in the advanced course.
- Anyone enrolled in the advanced course has passed the basic course.
- All and only those who have passed the basic course enroll in the advanced course.
- Anyone who is not enrolled in the advanced course has not passed the basic course.

## Explanation

“For $A$ to hold, $B$ is necessary” translates as “if $A$, then $B$,” so the statement says that anyone enrolled in the advanced course has passed the basic course. The statement containing “enough” makes passing the basic course a **sufficient condition**, reversing the direction of the arrow. “Anyone who is not enrolled in the advanced course has not passed the basic course” is the contrapositive of the statement containing “enough,” so it is wrong for the same reason. The statement containing “all and only” adds the converse to the given implication.
