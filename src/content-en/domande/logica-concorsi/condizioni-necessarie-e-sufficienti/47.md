A workstation may be assigned only to someone who is registered. Assigning it entails handing over the key. Which fact guarantees that the workstation was not assigned?

- The key was not handed over.
- The person is registered.
- The key was handed over.
- The person is registered and has the key.

## Explanation

If the workstation is assigned, the key is handed over. If the key was not handed over, **modus tollens** rules out the assignment.
