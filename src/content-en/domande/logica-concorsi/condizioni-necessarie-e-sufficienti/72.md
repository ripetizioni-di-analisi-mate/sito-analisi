To receive a credential, a technician must have signed the log. To sign the log, the technician must have attended training. Which condition is necessary for the credential to be issued?

- Not having signed the log yet.
- Having signed the log without attending training.
- Having already received another credential.
- Having attended training.

## Explanation

Issuing the credential implies a signature, and the signature implies attendance at training. By **transitivity**, every technician who receives the credential attended training.
