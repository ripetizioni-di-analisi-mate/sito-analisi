A sample is analyzed if it arrives by $16$\:00 or is marked urgent. A sample that was analyzed arrived at $17$\:00. Which assessment is correct?

- The rule prohibits analyzing any sample after $16$\:00.
- The sample was definitely urgent.
- The sample was not urgent.
- The given facts allow for the sample to have been urgent or not urgent.

## Explanation

The rule guarantees analysis in two cases, one of which is urgency. Analyzing a sample that arrived at $17$\:00 is consistent both with an urgent sample and with an analysis ordered for another reason.
