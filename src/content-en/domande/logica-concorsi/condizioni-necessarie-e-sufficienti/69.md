All and only those candidates who passed both tests are eligible. A candidate is not eligible. What do we know about their results?

- They did not pass at least one of the tests.
- They passed only the second test.
- They passed neither test.
- They passed only the first test.

## Explanation

Passing both tests guarantees eligibility. If the candidate is not eligible, then by **modus tollens** there is at least one test they did not pass.
