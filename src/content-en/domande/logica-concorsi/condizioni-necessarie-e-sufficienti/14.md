For a positive integer $n$, what kind of condition is “$n$ is prime” for “$n$ is odd”?

- sufficient but not necessary
- neither necessary nor sufficient
- necessary but not sufficient
- necessary and sufficient

## Explanation

The number $2$ is prime and even, so the implication “if $n$ is prime, then $n$ is odd” does not hold and the condition is not sufficient. The number $9 = 3 \cdot 3$ is odd and is not prime, so the implication “if $n$ is odd, then $n$ is prime” does not hold and the condition is not necessary. Choosing “sufficient but not necessary” overlooks the fact that $2$ is an even prime.
