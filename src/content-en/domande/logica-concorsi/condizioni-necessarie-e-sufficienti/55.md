An interview is always followed by a written report. A case may be filed away only after the report is ready. Which situation shows that an interview alone does not guarantee that the case will be filed away?

- The interview takes place without any report being produced.
- The interview takes place and the case is filed away without a report.
- The interview takes place, the report is ready, and the case remains open.
- The case is filed away with neither a report nor an interview.

## Explanation

The report is a consequence of the interview and a requirement for filing the case away. Both rules allow the report to be ready while the case is still open.
