A museum displays at most two previously unpublished photographs. The unpublished photographs on display include at least one color photograph and at least one black-and-white photograph. Each photograph belongs to just one type. What follows?

- The museum displays exactly two previously unpublished photographs, one of each type.
- The museum displays just one previously unpublished photograph.
- The museum displays two previously unpublished photographs, both in color.
- The museum displays three previously unpublished photographs, two of them in black and white.

## Explanation

The presence of both types requires two distinct previously unpublished photographs. The upper limit of two prevents there being any more.
