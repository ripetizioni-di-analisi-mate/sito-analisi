“Some city council members are lawyers” and “Some lawyers are university faculty members” are true. Which of the following must be true?

- Some city council members are university faculty members
- Some city council members are not university faculty members
- No city council member is a university faculty member
- Some lawyers are city council members

## Explanation

From **two particular premises**, no statement follows that connects city council members with university faculty members. The lawyer on the council and the lawyer on the faculty could be the same person or two different people. Thus each of the three statements about council and faculty members could be false. “Some lawyers are city council members,” however, simply interchanges the categories in the first premise, and “some … are” has the same meaning in either direction.
