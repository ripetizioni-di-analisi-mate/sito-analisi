The group consists only of Marta and Pietro. A claim is made: “Some auditors are not consultants, so some consultants are not auditors.” Which situation makes the premise true and the conclusion false?

- Marta and Pietro are both auditors and consultants.
- Marta is both an auditor and a consultant. Pietro is a consultant but not an auditor.
- Marta is an auditor but not a consultant. Pietro is a consultant but not an auditor.
- Marta is both an auditor and a consultant. Pietro is an auditor but not a consultant.

## Explanation

Pietro satisfies the premise because he is an auditor without being a consultant. Marta is the only consultant and is also an auditor, so the conclusion is false.
