What is the negation of “Only residents may park on Via Roma”?

- All residents may park on Via Roma
- At least one nonresident may park on Via Roma
- At least one resident may not park on Via Roma
- Only nonresidents may park on Via Roma

## Explanation

The given statement says that anyone who may park on Via Roma is a resident. It has the form “All $A$ are $B$,” where $A$ denotes people who may park on Via Roma and $B$ the residents. By the **rules for negating the four forms**, its negation is “Some $A$ are not $B$,” that is, at least one person may park on Via Roma and is not a resident. “At least one resident may not park on Via Roma” negates the converse, “All residents may park on Via Roma,” which expresses a different rule.
