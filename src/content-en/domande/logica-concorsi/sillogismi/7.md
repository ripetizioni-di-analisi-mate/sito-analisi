Which of the following statements is equivalent to “There is no call center employee who works on Sundays”?

- Anyone who works on Sundays is not a call center employee
- Some call center employees don't work on Sundays
- Everyone who does not work on Sundays is a call center employee
- Some of those working on Sundays are not call center employees

## Explanation

The given statement has the form “No $A$ is $B$,” where $A$ denotes call center employees and $B$ people who work on Sundays. Since the two categories have no members in common, anyone who works on Sundays is outside the group of call center employees. The statement therefore means “Anyone who works on Sundays is not a call center employee.” Both statements containing “some” follow from the given statement when the named categories have at least one member, but they can also be true when a call center employee works on Sundays. They are therefore not equivalent.
