At an agency, “No employee on probation can request to work remotely” is true. Chiara is an employee on probation. Which of the following must be true?

- Chiara can request to work remotely
- Chiara cannot request to work remotely
- Everyone who cannot request to work remotely is on probation
- Some employees on probation can request to work remotely

## Explanation

The premise states that the categories of employees on probation and people who can request to work remotely have no members in common. Chiara belongs to the first category, so she does not belong to the second and therefore cannot request to work remotely. “Everyone who cannot request to work remotely is on probation” does not follow because employees who are not on probation may be barred from remote work for other reasons.
