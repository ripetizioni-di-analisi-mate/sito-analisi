“All electric vehicles are available.” Which statement expresses its logical negation?

- Some electric vehicles are available.
- No electric vehicle is among the available vehicles.
- Some available vehicles are not electric.
- Some electric vehicles are not available.

## Explanation

One counterexample suffices to disprove a universal inclusion. Here the counterexample is a vehicle that is electric and lies outside the category of available vehicles.
