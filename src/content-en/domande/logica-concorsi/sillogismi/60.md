The report says “No fragile parcel was opened.” Which single count, if positive, proves the negation?

- All fragile parcels
- All opened parcels
- Parcels that were not fragile
- Parcels that were both fragile and opened

## Explanation

The negation requires a witness with both attributes. Each separate group can have a positive count even if the groups are disjoint.
