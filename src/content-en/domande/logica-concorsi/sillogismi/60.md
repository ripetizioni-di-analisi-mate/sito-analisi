“All electric vehicles are available.” Which statement expresses its logical negation?

- Some electric vehicles are available.
- No electric vehicle is among the available vehicles.
- Some available vehicles are not electric.
- Some electric vehicles are not available.

## Explanation

The statement "All electric vehicles are available" is false as soon as there is a single electric vehicle that is not available. By the rule for the **negation of the four forms**, therefore, the negation of "All $A$ are $B$" is "Some $A$ are not $B$", that is, "Some electric vehicles are not available". The statement "No electric vehicle is among the available vehicles" is the contrary of the original statement, and the two statements can both be false, for example if half of the electric vehicles are available. The statement "Some electric vehicles are available" can be true together with the original statement, so it does not negate it.
