Which statement is the logical negation of “At least one municipality opened exactly one collection point”?

- Every municipality opened either zero collection points or at least two.
- No municipality opened more than one collection point.
- Every municipality opened at least two collection points.
- At least one municipality opened zero collection points.

## Explanation

The negation rules out a municipality with exactly one collection point. The possible numbers for each municipality are therefore zero and every integer from two upward.
