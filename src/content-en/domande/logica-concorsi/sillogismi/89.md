All numbered files are confidential. None of the confidential files are among the complete files. The categories may be empty. What can we conclude about the categories of files?

- All complete files are numbered.
- All confidential files are numbered.
- Some numbered files are not complete.
- None of the numbered files are among the complete files.

## Explanation

Every member of “numbered” belongs to “confidential.” Since no member of the latter belongs to “complete,” the numbered and complete files are disjoint.
