Both “All tutors in the lab use the electronic register” and “No tutor in the lab uses the electronic register” are true. What follows from the truth of these two statements?

- The lab has at least one tutor who uses the electronic register and one who does not.
- The lab has no tutors.
- The lab has tutors and none uses the electronic register.
- The lab has tutors and all of them use the electronic register.

## Explanation

If a tutor existed, the first statement would require that tutor to use the register and the second would rule that out. With no tutors, both universal statements are true because neither has a counterexample.
