“All laboratory technicians wear lab coats” is true. Which of the following premises, added to it, permits the conclusion “No laboratory technician is a visitor”?

- Some visitors do not wear lab coats
- Everyone who wears a lab coat is a laboratory technician
- Some people who wear lab coats are not visitors
- No visitor wears a lab coat

## Explanation

The given premise puts technicians in the category of people who wear lab coats. If no visitor wears a lab coat, all visitors are outside that category, so they are also outside the category of technicians. This is the valid form “All $A$ are $B$, no $B$ is $C$, so no $A$ is $C$.” With the premise “Some visitors do not wear lab coats,” another visitor could wear a lab coat and be a technician, so the conclusion would not follow.
