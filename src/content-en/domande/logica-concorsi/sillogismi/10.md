In which statement can the two categories be interchanged without changing its meaning?

- Some notaries are university faculty members
- All notaries are law graduates
- Some lawyers are not notaries
- All judges have passed a competitive exam

## Explanation

The statements “Some $A$ are $B$” and “No $A$ is $B$” work in either direction because they concern only the overlap between the categories. “Some notaries are university faculty members” says that there is a notary who is a faculty member, in other words, a faculty member who is a notary. Therefore it is equivalent to “Some university faculty members are notaries.” Statements containing “all” or “some … are not” cannot be reversed. For example, “Some lawyers are not notaries” can be true if every notary is a lawyer, a situation in which “Some notaries are not lawyers” is false.
