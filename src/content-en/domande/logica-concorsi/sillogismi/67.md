All electric vehicles are on the municipal register, and all registered vehicles are insured. There is at least one insured vehicle that is not registered. Which hypothesis must be ruled out?

- At least one insured vehicle is not electric.
- All electric vehicles are insured.
- All insured vehicles are electric.
- No electric vehicle is insured.

## Explanation

The unregistered insured vehicle cannot be electric, because all electric vehicles are registered. The category of electric vehicles could be empty, so it is consistent with the premises that there are no insured electric vehicles.
