Only vehicles with an annual permit or a day pass enter the depot. The rules allow a vehicle to have both. Which fact disproves the rule?

- A vehicle entered with neither an annual permit nor a day pass.
- A vehicle entered with both an annual permit and a day pass.
- A vehicle with an annual permit remained outside.
- A vehicle entered with a day pass and without an annual permit.

## Explanation

Entry requires at least one of the two credentials. A vehicle entering without either is therefore a counterexample, whereas an eligible vehicle remaining outside is consistent with the rule.
