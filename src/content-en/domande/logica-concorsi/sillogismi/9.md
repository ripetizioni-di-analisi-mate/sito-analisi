Three of the following statements have the same meaning. Which means something different?

- Some municipal police officers are not on vacation
- No municipal police officer is on vacation
- No one on vacation is a municipal police officer
- There is no municipal police officer on vacation

## Explanation

“No municipal police officer is on vacation,” “No one on vacation is a municipal police officer,” and “There is no municipal police officer on vacation” all say the categories have no members in common, because the form “No $A$ is $B$” works in either direction. “Some municipal police officers are not on vacation” says only that at least one officer is not on vacation, so it is true even if some officers are on vacation and others are not. In that situation the other three statements are false, so its meaning is different.
