All original copies are kept in the archive. No copy kept in the archive is displayed in the exhibition room. The categories may be empty. Which additional piece of information would be inconsistent with these premises?

- At least one original copy is displayed in the exhibition room.
- At least one copy displayed in the exhibition room is not an original.
- At least one copy is kept in the archive.
- All original copies are displayed in the exhibition room.

## Explanation

An original copy on display would also have to be kept in the archive, which the second premise rules out for a displayed copy. The universal statement that all original copies are on display can, however, hold if the category of originals is empty.
