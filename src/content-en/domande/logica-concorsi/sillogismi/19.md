An office has $25$ employees. Which pair of statements can both be false?

- “Some employees use their badges” and “Some employees do not use their badges”
- “All employees use their badges” and “No employee uses their badge”
- “All employees use their badges” and “Some employees do not use their badges”
- “No employee uses their badge” and “Some employees use their badges”

## Explanation

The statements with “all” and “no” are **contrary**, so they cannot both be true, but both are false when some employees use their badges and others do not. “Some employees use their badges” and “Some employees do not use their badges” are subcontrary. They cannot both be false, because each employee either uses their badge or does not. In each of the other two pairs, the statements are contradictory. One is the negation of the other, so one of them is true in every situation.
