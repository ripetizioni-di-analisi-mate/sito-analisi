In the argument “All lifeguards at the beach facility are certified in lifesaving; no one certified in lifesaving is under $18$; therefore, no lifeguard at the facility is under $18$,” what is the middle term?

- The lifeguards at the beach facility
- People under $18$
- People who are at least $18$
- People certified in lifesaving

## Explanation

The **middle term** is the category that appears in both premises but does not appear in the conclusion. People certified in lifesaving appear in the first and second premises, while the conclusion links only the lifeguards at the facility and people under $18$. These are the two terms that the syllogism relates, so neither is the middle term.
