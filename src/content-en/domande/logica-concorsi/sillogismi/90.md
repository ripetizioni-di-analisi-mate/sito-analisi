Some signed forms are archived, and all approved forms are signed. Which statement could be false even if both premises are true?

- All forms that are both approved and archived are signed.
- There is at least one archived form.
- There is a form that is both approved and archived.
- There is at least one signed form.

## Explanation

A signed and archived form might still be awaiting approval. Its existence guarantees only that there is at least one signed form and at least one archived form. The rule about approved forms still holds.
