An association has $40$ volunteers. “No volunteer in the association receives a salary” and “All the association’s volunteers are on the regional register” are true. Which conclusion necessarily follows?

- Some people on the regional register do not receive a salary
- No one on the regional register receives a salary
- Some people on the regional register receive a salary
- Everyone who does not receive a salary is a volunteer in the association

## Explanation

Every volunteer in the association is on the regional register and does not receive a salary. Since there are $40$ volunteers, there are people on the regional register who do not receive a salary. Thus, “Some people on the regional register do not receive a salary” follows. “No one on the regional register receives a salary” generalizes to everyone on the register what the premises say only about volunteers. The register may also include people who are not association volunteers and who receive a salary.
