What is the negation of “Some patients on the waiting list have not received an appointment notice”?

- Some patients on the waiting list have received an appointment notice
- No patient on the waiting list has received an appointment notice
- Everyone who has received an appointment notice is on the waiting list
- All patients on the waiting list have received an appointment notice

## Explanation

The given statement says that at least one patient on the waiting list has not received an appointment notice. It is false exactly when there is no such patient, that is, when every patient on the waiting list has received a notice. By the **rules for negating the four forms**, the negation of “Some $A$ are not $B$” is therefore “All $A$ are $B$.” “Some patients on the waiting list have received an appointment notice” is not the negation because it can be true along with the given statement when some patients have been notified and others have not.
