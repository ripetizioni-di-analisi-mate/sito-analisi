Each published report underwent either an internal review or an external review, never both. All published reports underwent internal review. The rules also apply if the catalog is empty. What else must be true?

- At least one published report underwent internal review.
- All internally reviewed reports are published.
- No report underwent both types of review.
- No published report underwent external review.

## Explanation

For a published report, internal review rules out external review. The rule applies to published reports and leaves the status of other reports undetermined.
