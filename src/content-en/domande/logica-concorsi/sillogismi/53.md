The active cards are exactly the cards that have been renewed. Renewed cards do not belong to the blue series. What follows from these two facts?

- All active cards belong to the blue series.
- No active card has been renewed.
- No active card belongs to the blue series.
- At least one active card belongs to the blue series.

## Explanation

The sets are equal, so “active” can be substituted for “renewed.” Exclusion from the blue series therefore applies to all active cards.
