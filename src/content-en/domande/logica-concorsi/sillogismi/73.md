Every authorized staff member took the training course. At least one staff member on call did not take it. What do we know about the staff members on call?

- At least one is authorized.
- None are authorized.
- All authorized staff members are on call.
- At least one is not authorized.

## Explanation

An authorized staff member would necessarily have taken the course. The staff member on call who did not take it therefore cannot be authorized.
