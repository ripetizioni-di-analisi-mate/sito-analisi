In a condominium, “Some unit owners did not pay the October installment” is true. Which of the following must be false?

- No unit owner paid the October installment
- Some unit owners paid the October installment
- Not all unit owners paid the October installment
- All unit owners paid the October installment

## Explanation

The statements “Some unit owners did not pay the October installment” and “All unit owners paid the October installment” are **contradictory**, so if the first is true, the second is false. “No unit owner paid the October installment” is possible, because the given statement is true even if no one paid. “Some unit owners paid the October installment” is also possible, since the given statement says nothing about the other owners. “Not all unit owners paid the October installment” is equivalent to the given statement, so it is true.
