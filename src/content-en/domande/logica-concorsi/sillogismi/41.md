At an agency, “All employees in the technical department have company laptops” and “Some second-floor employees do not work in the technical department” are true. Which conclusion necessarily follows?

- None of the other three conclusions necessarily follows
- Some second-floor employees do not have company laptops
- Some second-floor employees have company laptops
- No second-floor employee works in the technical department

## Explanation

The second premise concerns second-floor employees outside the technical department, and the first says nothing about anyone outside that department. Thus “Some second-floor employees do not have company laptops” does not follow, because the premises are true even in an agency where everyone has a laptop. Choosing this conclusion commits the fallacy of **denying the antecedent**. “Some second-floor employees have company laptops” is false if just one employee works on the second floor, outside the technical department and without a laptop. “No second-floor employee works in the technical department” is false if technical staff also work on the second floor. Thus none of the three conclusions follows.
