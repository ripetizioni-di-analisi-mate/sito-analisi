At a boating club, “No one enrolled in the introductory course has a boating license” and “No one with a boating license is under $18$” are true. There are $8$ people under $18$ at the club. Which of the following must be true?

- No one enrolled in the introductory course is under $18$
- Some people enrolled in the introductory course are under $18$
- Everyone under $18$ is enrolled in the introductory course
- Some people under $18$ do not have a boating license

## Explanation

From **two negative premises**, no statement follows that connects people enrolled in the introductory course with people under $18$. The second premise alone says that people under $18$ are outside the category of boating license holders. Since the club has $8$ people under $18$, there is someone under $18$ without a boating license. “No one enrolled in the introductory course is under $18$” is tempting if we treat the premises as a chain, but people in the introductory course can be under $18$.
