Some videos in an archive have subtitles. Each subtitled video is either accessible remotely or available only in the viewing room, and these two options are mutually exclusive. What additional information guarantees that at least one video is accessible remotely?

- All videos available only in the viewing room have subtitles.
- No subtitled video is available only in the viewing room.
- Every video accessible remotely has subtitles.
- Some videos without subtitles are available only in the viewing room.

## Explanation

There are subtitled videos, and each has one of the two modes of access. Ruling out viewing-room-only access for these videos guarantees that at least one is accessible remotely.
