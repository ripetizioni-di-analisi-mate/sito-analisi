The group consists only of Ilaria and Omar. Which situation provides a counterexample to the argument “Some assistants are librarians. No technician is an assistant. Therefore, no technician is a librarian.”?

- Ilaria is an assistant and has no other role. Omar is a technician and a librarian, but not an assistant.
- Ilaria holds all three roles. Omar is a librarian and has no other role.
- Ilaria is an assistant and a librarian, but not a technician. Omar is a technician and has no other role.
- Ilaria is an assistant and a librarian, but not a technician. Omar is a technician and a librarian, but not an assistant.

## Explanation

Ilaria makes the first premise true. Keeping the roles of assistant and technician separate makes the second premise true, while Omar is both a technician and a librarian and makes the conclusion false.
