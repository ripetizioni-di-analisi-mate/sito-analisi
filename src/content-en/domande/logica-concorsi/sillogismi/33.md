On a train line, “Some morning trains do not have Wi-Fi” and “All morning trains stop in Florence” are true. Which conclusion necessarily follows?

- Some trains that stop in Florence have Wi-Fi
- Some trains that stop in Florence do not have Wi-Fi
- No train that stops in Florence has Wi-Fi
- Some trains with Wi-Fi do not stop in Florence

## Explanation

The first premise guarantees the existence of a morning train without Wi-Fi. By the second premise, that train stops in Florence, so it is a train that stops in Florence and has no Wi-Fi. Hence “Some trains that stop in Florence do not have Wi-Fi” follows. “Some trains that stop in Florence have Wi-Fi” is tempting if we read “some” as “some do and others do not,” but the premises are true even if no train on the line has Wi-Fi.
