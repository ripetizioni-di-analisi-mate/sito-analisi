What is the logical negation of “There is a coordinator who has reviewed every financial report”?

- Every coordinator has reviewed at least one financial report.
- For every coordinator there is at least one financial report that this coordinator has not reviewed.
- There is a coordinator who has not reviewed any financial report.
- There is a financial report that no coordinator has reviewed.

## Explanation

To negate the existence of a coordinator who completed all the reviews, every coordinator must have left at least one report unreviewed. The missing report may differ from one coordinator to another.
