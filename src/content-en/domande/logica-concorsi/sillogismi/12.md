In a town, “No school cafeteria volunteer is a municipal employee” and “All school bus drivers are municipal employees” are true. Which of the following people could there be in the town?

- A school cafeteria volunteer who is also a school bus driver
- A municipal employee who is not a school bus driver
- A school bus driver who is not a municipal employee
- A school cafeteria volunteer who is also a municipal employee

## Explanation

In the **diagram of the premises**, the first statement makes the region common to cafeteria volunteers and municipal employees empty, and the second makes the region for school bus drivers outside the municipal employees empty. Thus there can be neither a cafeteria volunteer who is a municipal employee nor a school bus driver who is not a municipal employee. A volunteer who was also a driver would be a municipal employee by the second statement, but the first rules that out for a volunteer. No such person can exist either. Neither statement makes the region for municipal employees who are not school bus drivers empty, so it could contain someone, for example a registry office employee.
