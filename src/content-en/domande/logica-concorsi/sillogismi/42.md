For a trip, “All participants paid the fee” and “Some participants are teachers” are true. Which of the following conclusions does *not* necessarily follow?

- Some teachers paid the fee
- Some trip participants paid the fee
- All teachers taking part in the trip paid the fee
- Some trip participants are not teachers

## Explanation

The teacher taking part in the trip paid the fee, so “Some teachers paid the fee” follows from the premises. For the same reason, “Some trip participants paid the fee” follows. Teachers who participate are a subset of the participants, so they too paid the fee. However, “Some trip participants are not teachers” does not follow, because the premises are true even if only teachers take part. Treating it as a consequence amounts to reading “some” as “some are and others are not.”
