“None of the confirmed reservations can be changed.” Which statement is its negation?

- All confirmed reservations can be changed.
- Some confirmed reservations can be changed.
- Some reservations that can be changed are not confirmed.
- Some confirmed reservations cannot be changed.

## Explanation

The statement rules out any overlap between “confirmed” and “can be changed.” Its negation says that at least one reservation belongs to both categories.
