A competitive exam has $15$ successful applicants. “All successful applicants passed the oral test with at least $24$ points” and “All successful applicants live in Tuscany” are true. Which conclusion necessarily follows?

- Some Tuscany residents passed the oral test with at least $24$ points
- All Tuscany residents passed the oral test with at least $24$ points
- Everyone who passed the oral test with at least $24$ points lives in Tuscany
- Some Tuscany residents did not pass the oral test with at least $24$ points

## Explanation

The two premises concern the same $15$ successful applicants. Each passed the oral test with at least $24$ points and lives in Tuscany, so each is a Tuscany resident who passed with at least $24$ points. Since at least one applicant was successful, “Some Tuscany residents passed the oral test with at least $24$ points” follows. The statement about all Tuscany residents extends the conclusion to people about whom the premises say nothing, namely Tuscany residents who were not successful in the competitive exam.
