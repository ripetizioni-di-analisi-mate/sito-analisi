“No one enrolled in the evening session submitted both forms.” Which statement has exactly the same meaning?

- Everyone enrolled in the evening session submitted exactly one of the two forms.
- Everyone enrolled in the evening session submitted zero forms.
- At least one person enrolled in the evening session submitted at most one of the two forms.
- Everyone enrolled in the evening session submitted at most one of the two forms.

## Explanation

The statement rules out an enrollee submitting two forms. For each enrollee, both zero submissions and one submission remain possible.
