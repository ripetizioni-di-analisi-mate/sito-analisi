A company has $300$ employees. “Some of the company’s employees requested a transfer” is false. Which of the following must be true?

- Some of the company’s employees did not request a transfer
- All of the company’s employees requested a transfer
- Some of the company’s employees requested a transfer and others did not
- Some people who requested a transfer are employees of the company

## Explanation

If a statement is false, its negation is true. The negation of “Some of the company’s employees requested a transfer” is “None of the company’s employees requested a transfer,” so none of its $300$ employees requested one. Since the company has at least one employee, “Some of the company’s employees did not request a transfer” is also true. “Some of the company’s employees requested a transfer and others did not” includes the original statement, which is false, so it too is false. “Some people who requested a transfer are employees of the company” simply interchanges the categories in the original statement, so it is false.
