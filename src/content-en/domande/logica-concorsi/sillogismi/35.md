At a company, “All night-shift employees receive an allowance,” “Everyone who receives the allowance fills out form M,” and “No one who fills out form M works part-time” are true. Which conclusion necessarily follows?

- Some night-shift employees work part-time
- Everyone who does not work part-time works the night shift
- No night-shift employee works part-time
- Everyone who fills out form M works the night shift

## Explanation

The first two premises form a chain, so each night-shift employee receives the allowance and fills out form M. By the third premise, people who fill out form M do not work part-time. Therefore, no night-shift employee works part-time. Neither statement beginning with “Everyone who” follows, because the premises place night-shift employees inside the other categories and say nothing about the reverse direction.
