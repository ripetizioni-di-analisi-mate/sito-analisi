Some operators are both qualified and trained. All trained operators are on call. What other overlap is guaranteed?

- All qualified operators are on call.
- Some qualified operators are on call.
- All operators on call are trained.
- No qualified operator is among those on call.

## Explanation

The first premise gives at least one member common to “qualified” and “trained.” Every member of the second category is on call, so that operator is also on call.
