All financial bids are admissible. Some compliant bids are not admissible. What can we conclude about this group?

- Some compliant bids are not financial bids.
- Some compliant bids are financial bids.
- None of the compliant bids are among the admissible bids.
- None of the compliant bids are among the financial bids.

## Explanation

The second premise gives a compliant bid outside the admissible bids. Since the financial bids are contained in the admissible bids, that bid must also lie outside the financial bids.
