There are $30$ cars in an agency’s parking lot. “No car in the lot is electric” is true. Which of the following must be true?

- Some cars in the parking lot are electric
- All cars that are not in the parking lot are electric
- No electric car is outside the parking lot
- Some cars in the parking lot are not electric

## Explanation

The given statement says that every car in the lot is outside the category of electric cars. Since there are $30$ cars in the lot, at least one car in the lot is not electric, so “Some cars in the parking lot are not electric” is true. This statement is the **subaltern** of “No car in the parking lot is electric” and follows from it because the category of cars in the lot has at least one member. “Some cars in the parking lot are electric” is the contradictory of the given statement and is therefore false.
