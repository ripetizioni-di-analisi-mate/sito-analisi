What is the negation of “At least one tourist in the group visited both the Colosseum and the Vatican Museums”?

- No tourist in the group visited the Colosseum and none visited the Vatican Museums
- Every tourist in the group missed at least one of the two visits
- At least one tourist in the group visited neither the Colosseum nor the Vatican Museums
- At least one tourist in the group visited the Colosseum but not the Vatican Museums

## Explanation

The statement says that a tourist in the group made both visits. Its negation says there was no such tourist, that is, no tourist in the group made both visits. By **De Morgan’s laws**, “not (Colosseum and Vatican Museums)” is equivalent to “not Colosseum or not Vatican Museums,” with inclusive “or.” Thus every tourist missed at least one visit. “No tourist in the group visited the Colosseum and none visited the Vatican Museums” is false in a group where every tourist made exactly one visit. The original statement is false in that group too, so the two statements are not each other’s negations.
