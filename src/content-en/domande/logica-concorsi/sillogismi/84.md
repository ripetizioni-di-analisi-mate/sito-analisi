Some revised texts are not illustrated. All published texts are illustrated. What can we establish with certainty?

- Some revised texts are not published.
- Some revised texts are published.
- All revised texts are published.
- None of the revised texts are among those published.

## Explanation

The first premise guarantees a revised text outside the illustrated texts. All published texts are in the illustrated category, so that text must also be outside the published texts.
