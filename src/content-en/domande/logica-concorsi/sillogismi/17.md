What is the negation of “People with an annual pass do not pay for parking”?

- At least one person with an annual pass pays for parking
- Everyone with an annual pass pays for parking
- Everyone without an annual pass pays for parking
- At least one person without an annual pass does not pay for parking

## Explanation

The given statement says that no one with an annual pass pays for parking, so it has the form “No $A$ is $B$.” By the **rules for negating the four forms**, its negation is “Some $A$ are $B$,” that is, at least one person with an annual pass pays for parking. “Everyone with an annual pass pays for parking” is tempting if we confuse the negation of a universal statement with its contrary. If some pass holders pay and others do not, both statements are false, so they are not each other’s negations.
