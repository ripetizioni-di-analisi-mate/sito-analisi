There are packets that are both sealed and labeled. There are packets that are both labeled and delivered. Which conclusion follows?

- Some sealed packets have been delivered.
- Some labeled packets are sealed.
- All sealed packets have been delivered.
- None of the sealed packets are among those delivered.

## Explanation

The first premise guarantees at least one member in the intersection of “sealed” and “labeled.” The second may refer to a different member, so it does not guarantee an overlap between “sealed” and “delivered.”
