At a depot, some sealed packets are labeled. All sealed packets have been weighed. Some labeled packets, however, have not been weighed. What can we deduce about the labeled packets?

- All weighed packets are labeled.
- At least one is unsealed.
- At least one is sealed and has not been weighed.
- At least one weighed packet is unlabeled.

## Explanation

There is a labeled packet that has not been weighed. Since every sealed packet has been weighed, that labeled packet is unsealed.
