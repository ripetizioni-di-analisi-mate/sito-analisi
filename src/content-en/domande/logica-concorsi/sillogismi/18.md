A club has $40$ members. The statements “No club member voted against the budget” and “Some club members voted against the budget” are

- contradictory
- contrary
- subcontrary
- equivalent

## Explanation

Two statements are **contradictory** if they can neither both be true nor both be false. If no member voted against the budget, the first is true and the second false. If at least one member voted against it, the first is false and the second true. The statements thus have opposite truth values in every situation, and each is the negation of the other. The contrary statements are “All members voted against the budget” and “No member voted against it.” Both are false if some members voted against it and others did not.
