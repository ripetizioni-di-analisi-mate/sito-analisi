A training course has $20$ participants. “Some participants passed the final test” is true. What are all the possible values for the number of participants who passed?

- The integers from $2$ to $19$
- The integers from $1$ to $19$
- The integers from $1$ to $20$
- The integers from $2$ to $20$

## Explanation

In logic, *some* means *at least one, possibly all*. The statement is therefore true if $1$ participant passed the test and also if all $20$ participants passed. It is false only if no participant passed. The range from $2$ to $19$ comes from two everyday readings of “some,” namely “more than one” and “some did and others did not.” The statement conveys neither of those restrictions.
