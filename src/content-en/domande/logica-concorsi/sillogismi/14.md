Today $20$ flights departed from the airport, and “All flights that departed from the airport today were on time” is false. What are all the possible values for the number of flights that departed on time today?

- The integers from $1$ to $19$
- Only $0$
- The integers from $0$ to $19$
- The integers from $0$ to $9$

## Explanation

If a statement is false, its negation is true. By the **rules for negating the four forms**, the negation of “All $A$ are $B$” is “At least one $A$ is not a $B$,” so at least one of the $20$ flights was not on time. At most $19$ flights were on time, and there could even have been $0$, because the statement is also false if no flight was on time. Choosing $0$ alone amounts to negating “all” with “none.” The range from $0$ to $9$ corresponds to “most flights were not on time,” which says more than the negation.
