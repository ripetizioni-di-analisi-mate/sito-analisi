Which of the following statements is equivalent to “All trains that stop in Bologna have first-class seating”?

- All trains with first-class seating stop in Bologna
- Some trains that stop in Bologna do not have first-class seating
- No train without first-class seating stops in Bologna
- Some trains with first-class seating do not stop in Bologna

## Explanation

The given statement puts all trains that stop in Bologna in the category of trains with first-class seating. A train without first-class seating is outside that category, so it does not stop in Bologna. Thus the statement is equivalent to “No train without first-class seating stops in Bologna.” “All trains with first-class seating stop in Bologna” interchanges the categories, and the form “All $A$ are $B$” does not work in either direction. “Some trains that stop in Bologna do not have first-class seating” is the negation of the given statement.
