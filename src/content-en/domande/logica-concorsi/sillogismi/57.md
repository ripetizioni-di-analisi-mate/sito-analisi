Some signed forms lack a case number. Every form without a case number is reviewed manually. Which fact is established?

- At least one signed form is excluded from manual review.
- All signed forms are reviewed manually.
- At least one signed form is reviewed manually.
- At least one form with a case number is reviewed manually.

## Explanation

A missing case number triggers a manual review. Signed forms without a case number therefore undergo this review.
