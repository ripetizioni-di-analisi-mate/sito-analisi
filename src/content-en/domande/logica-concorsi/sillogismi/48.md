For a delivery, reinforced packaging is required for every heavy package and also for every fragile package. The list includes fragile packages. Which conclusion is guaranteed?

- No heavy package requires reinforced packaging.
- Every package that requires reinforced packaging is fragile.
- At least one fragile package requires reinforced packaging.
- At least one heavy package requires reinforced packaging.

## Explanation

The rule applies to all fragile packages, regardless of their weight. Since the list contains at least one, the requirement certainly applies to at least one fragile package.
