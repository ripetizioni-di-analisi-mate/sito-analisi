A course has participants who submitted the project, participants who attended the interview, and participants who returned the materials. No one both submitted the project and attended the interview. What is the minimum number of participants consistent with all this information?

- Four.
- Three.
- Two.
- One.

## Explanation

The project and the interview require two distinct participants. Either of them could have returned the materials, so two participants suffice.
