No funded course is among the accredited courses. All free courses are funded. There are free courses. Which statement is inconsistent with this information?

- Some funded courses are not accredited.
- Some accredited courses are free.
- Some funded courses are free.
- Some free courses are not accredited.

## Explanation

The category of free courses is contained in the category of funded courses. Since “funded” and “accredited” have no members in common, no free course belongs to the accredited courses.
