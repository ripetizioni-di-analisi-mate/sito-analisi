At a company, “All legal department employees are lawyers” and “Some employees at the Milan office are not lawyers” are true. Which conclusion necessarily follows?

- Some employees at the Milan office do not work in the legal department
- No Milan office employee works in the legal department
- Some lawyers do not work in the legal department
- Some legal department employees do not work at the Milan office

## Explanation

The second premise guarantees the existence of a Milan office employee who is not a lawyer. If that employee worked in the legal department, the first premise would make them a lawyer. Since they are not a lawyer, they do not work in the legal department, so “Some employees at the Milan office do not work in the legal department” follows. “No Milan office employee works in the legal department” extends one employee’s property to everyone, whereas the premises allow other employees in Milan to be lawyers in the legal department.
