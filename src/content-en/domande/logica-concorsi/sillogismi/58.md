Two requirements state that every certified container must be sealed and that every certified container must be unsealed. Both requirements are met. What follows?

- No container is certified.
- No container is sealed.
- All containers are certified.
- At least one certified container is sealed.

## Explanation

A certified container would have to satisfy two incompatible properties. For both requirements to be met, the category of certified containers must be empty.
