An office announces, “Each security key is assigned to at most two cabinets.” Which observation suffices to disprove the announcement?

- One security key is assigned to at least three cabinets.
- There are three cabinets, none of which has a security key assigned to it.
- Three security keys are assigned to the same cabinet.
- One security key is assigned to just one cabinet.

## Explanation

The limit applies to the number of cabinets assigned to an individual key. It suffices to find one key assigned to three or more cabinets.
