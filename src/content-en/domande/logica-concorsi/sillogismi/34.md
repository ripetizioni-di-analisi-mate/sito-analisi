At a station, “Some commuters have season passes” and “All season pass holders have electronic cards” are true. Which conclusion necessarily follows?

- All commuters have electronic cards
- Some commuters do not have electronic cards
- Some electronic card holders are commuters
- All electronic card holders have season passes

## Explanation

The first premise guarantees the existence of a commuter with a season pass. By the second premise, that commuter has an electronic card, so there is an electronic card holder who is a commuter. This is the valid form “Some $A$ are $B$, all $B$ are $C$, so some $A$ are $C$,” with the conclusion expressed in the reverse direction, which preserves the meaning of an affirmative statement with “some.” “All commuters have electronic cards” extends to all commuters what the premises say about those with season passes.
