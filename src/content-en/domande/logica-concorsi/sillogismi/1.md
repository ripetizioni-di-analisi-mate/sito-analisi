A warehouse holds $50$ boxes, all of them damaged. Which of the following statements is false?

- Some boxes in the warehouse are damaged
- No box in the warehouse is undamaged
- Some boxes in the warehouse are not damaged
- More than one box in the warehouse is damaged

## Explanation

Since all $50$ boxes are damaged, there is no undamaged box in the warehouse, so “Some boxes in the warehouse are not damaged” is false. “Some boxes in the warehouse are damaged” is true, because in logic *some* means *at least one, possibly all*. Someone who reads “some” as “some are and others are not” would consider it false, but the statement does not convey that extra information. The other two statements are true because no box is undamaged and $50$ boxes are damaged.
