An inspector wants to show that “No regional train on this line has an accessible restroom” is false. Which of the following trains would suffice?

- A regional train on this line without an accessible restroom
- A regional train on this line with an accessible restroom
- A train on this line that is not a regional train and has an accessible restroom
- A train on this line that is not a regional train and does not have an accessible restroom

## Explanation

The statement has the form “No $A$ is $B$,” so it is universal and says that the two categories have no members in common. A single member of both categories therefore makes it false, namely a regional train on the line with an accessible restroom. A regional train without an accessible restroom is consistent with the statement and does not make it false. The nonregional trains fall outside the category the statement concerns, so neither of them makes it false.
