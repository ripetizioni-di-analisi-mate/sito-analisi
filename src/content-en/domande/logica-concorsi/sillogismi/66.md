All funded courses are accredited, and all accredited courses are listed in the catalog. The director also confirms that there is at least one funded course. What is certain to appear in the catalog?

- At least one funded course is listed.
- No funded course is listed.
- All listed courses are accredited.
- At least one unaccredited course is listed.

## Explanation

The director confirms that a funded course exists. The two inclusions place it first among the accredited courses and then among those listed in the catalog.
