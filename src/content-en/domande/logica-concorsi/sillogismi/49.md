All accessible rooms are reserved. All accessible rooms are heated. All reserved rooms are heated. The categories may be empty. Which statement is consistent with the premises?

- None of the accessible rooms are among the heated rooms.
- Some accessible rooms are not heated.
- Some accessible rooms are not reserved.
- Some reserved rooms are not heated.

## Explanation

The universal premises allow the category of accessible rooms to be empty. In that case no room belongs to both “accessible” and “heated.”
