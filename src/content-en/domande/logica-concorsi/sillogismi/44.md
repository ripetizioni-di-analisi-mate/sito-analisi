At a bakery, “No frozen product is on display” and “Some pastries are on display” are true. How many of the following conclusions necessarily follow?

1. Some pastries are not frozen.
2. No pastry is frozen.
3. Some products on display are not frozen.
4. Some frozen products are not pastries.

- One
- Two
- Three
- Four

## Explanation

There is a pastry on display, and by the first premise it is not frozen. Therefore “Some pastries are not frozen” and “Some products on display are not frozen” follow. “No pastry is frozen” does not follow, because pastries that are not on display may be frozen. “Some frozen products are not pastries” does not follow either, because the premises could be true even if all frozen products were pastries kept out of the display. Thus two conclusions follow.
