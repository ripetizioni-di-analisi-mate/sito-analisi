Every office in a ministry has at least one employee. What is the negation of “Every office in the ministry has at least one employee with a degree”?

- No office in the ministry has an employee with a degree
- At least one office in the ministry has no employee with a degree
- Every office in the ministry has at least one employee without a degree
- At least one office in the ministry has at least one employee without a degree

## Explanation

The given statement is universal over the offices, so its negation says that *at least one* office lacks the specified property. The property is “having at least one employee with a degree,” and its negation is “having no employee with a degree.” The negation of the original statement is therefore “At least one office in the ministry has no employee with a degree.” The statement “At least one office in the ministry has at least one employee without a degree” changes “every office” to “at least one office,” but retains “at least one employee” instead of using “no employee.” It is true even in a ministry where every office has employees both with and without degrees, where the given statement is also true.
