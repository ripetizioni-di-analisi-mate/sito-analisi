Which of the following statements is equivalent to “Some researchers in the laboratory are foreign nationals”?

- Some researchers in the laboratory are not foreign nationals
- All foreign nationals are researchers in the laboratory
- Some foreign nationals are researchers in the laboratory
- Some foreign nationals are not researchers in the laboratory

## Explanation

“Some $A$ are $B$” says that the two categories have at least one member in common. A foreign national who is a researcher in the laboratory is also a laboratory researcher who is a foreign national. The statement thus works in either direction and is equivalent to “Some foreign nationals are researchers in the laboratory.” “Some researchers in the laboratory are not foreign nationals” is tempting if we read “some” as “some are and others are not.” It is false at a laboratory where all the researchers are foreign nationals, whereas the given statement is true there.
