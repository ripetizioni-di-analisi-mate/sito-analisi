None of the translated documents are among the authenticated documents. Some authenticated documents have been cataloged. What necessarily follows?

- All cataloged documents have been authenticated.
- None of the translated documents are among the cataloged documents.
- Some translated documents have been cataloged.
- Some cataloged documents have not been translated.

## Explanation

At least one document belongs to both “authenticated” and “cataloged.” Since “translated” and “authenticated” are disjoint categories, that document belongs to “cataloged” and lies outside “translated.”
