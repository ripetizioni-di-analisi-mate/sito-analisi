Every reservation can be changed or refunded, or both. No promotional reservation can be changed. There may be no promotional reservations. What can we say about the group of promotional reservations?

- None are refundable.
- They are all refundable.
- It includes at least one refundable reservation.
- It includes all refundable reservations.

## Explanation

Every reservation must have at least one of the two properties. Promotional reservations cannot be changed, so they must be refundable. Whether any promotional reservations exist remains undetermined.
