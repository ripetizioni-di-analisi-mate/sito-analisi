A rule states, “Only library members can reserve at least one seat in the study room.” A check confirms that no member can reserve a seat in the study room. What follows from these two facts?

- All nonmembers can reserve seats in the study room.
- The library has no members.
- No one can reserve a seat in the study room.
- At least one member can reserve a seat in the study room.

## Explanation

The first rule makes membership a prerequisite and thus rules out nonmembers. The check also rules out members, so no one can make a reservation.
