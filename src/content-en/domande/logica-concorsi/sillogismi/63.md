All complete forms are valid. All valid forms are complete. There are forms that are both complete and legible. What further relationship between the categories must hold?

- All legible forms are complete.
- All complete forms are legible.
- Some valid forms are not legible.
- Some valid forms are legible.

## Explanation

Inclusion in both directions makes “complete” and “valid” the same category. The form that belongs to both “complete” and “legible” therefore also belongs to both “valid” and “legible.”
