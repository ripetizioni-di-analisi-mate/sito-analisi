A list includes at least two urgent notices. At least three notices require a signature, and no urgent notice requires a signature. At least one notice has neither property. What is the minimum number of notices on the list?

- Seven.
- Eight.
- Six.
- Five.

## Explanation

The urgent notices, the notices requiring a signature, and the notices with neither property belong to three disjoint groups. Two urgent notices, three requiring a signature, and one in the third group satisfy all the conditions with six notices.
