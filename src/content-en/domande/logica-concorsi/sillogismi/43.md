At a company, “Only employees on permanent contracts can access the pension fund” and “Some employees in logistics can access the pension fund” are true. Which of the following *cannot* be true?

- Some employees on permanent contracts cannot access the pension fund
- All logistics employees have permanent contracts
- No logistics employee has a permanent contract
- Some logistics employees cannot access the pension fund

## Explanation

The word “only” identifies the broader category, so the first statement is equivalent to “Everyone who can access the pension fund has a permanent contract.” The logistics employee who can access the fund therefore has a permanent contract, and “Some logistics employees have permanent contracts” follows. “No logistics employee has a permanent contract” is the **contradictory** of this conclusion, so it cannot be true. The statement about permanent employees who cannot access the fund is possible, because “only” does not guarantee access to every permanent employee.
