“Some signed reports have not been forwarded.” Which statement is the exact logical negation of the one quoted?

- None of the signed reports are among those that have been forwarded.
- Some signed reports have been forwarded.
- All signed reports have been forwarded.
- All forwarded reports are signed.

## Explanation

The statement guarantees a signed report outside the forwarded reports. The negation rules out every such counterexample and therefore says that the signed reports are included in the forwarded reports.
