No provisional registration has been validated. Some provisional registrations have no receipt. Which combination of properties must at least one registration have?

- Provisional and validated.
- Not provisional and without a receipt.
- Provisional and with a receipt.
- Provisional, unvalidated, and without a receipt.

## Explanation

The second premise identifies at least one provisional registration without a receipt. The first establishes that every provisional registration is also unvalidated.
