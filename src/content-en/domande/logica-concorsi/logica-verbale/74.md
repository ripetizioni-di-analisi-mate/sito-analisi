“The committee should compare the two quotations taking the duration of the service into account, because the price in the first covers six months and the price in the second covers twelve.” Which statement is a premise of the argument?

- The two prices should be compared taking the duration into account.
- The second service is of higher quality.
- The price in the first quotation covers six months of service.
- The committee should choose the first quotation.

## Explanation

The two periods covered by the prices are the facts given in support of the argument. The proposed conclusion is that the comparison should take the duration into account.
