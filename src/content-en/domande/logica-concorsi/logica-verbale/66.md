A memo says that forms received by Tuesday will be reviewed by the end of the month. It specifies that forms received after Tuesday will be reviewed starting the following month. What does this tell us about a form received on Wednesday of the same week?

- It will be reviewed starting the following month.
- It will necessarily be reviewed on the first day of the following month.
- It will be reviewed by the end of the current month.
- It is permanently excluded from review.

## Explanation

Wednesday comes after Tuesday, so the second rule applies. “Starting the following month” establishes when review can begin but leaves the exact day open.
