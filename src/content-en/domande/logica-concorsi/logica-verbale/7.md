Which word completes the analogy “Room is to apartment as apartment is to …”?

- kitchen
- apartment building
- tenant
- rent

## Explanation

The connecting sentence is “A room is part of an apartment,” so the relationship is **part and whole**, with the part first. With apartment, the sentence becomes “An apartment is part of an apartment building.” A kitchen and an apartment form a part–whole pair in the opposite direction, because “An apartment is part of a kitchen” is false. The tenant lives in the apartment, and rent is what is paid to live there.
