“In the first trial, ten participants chose the large model. Therefore, all future users will prefer the large model.” What is the main flaw in the reasoning?

- It mistakes a sequence of events for a causal relationship.
- It introduces a contradiction between two measurements from the same trial.
- It generalizes from a limited group to all future users.
- It repeats the conclusion as a premise without adding evidence.

## Explanation

The argument moves from behavior observed in a small group to a universal prediction. It gives no reason to regard that group as representative of all future users.
