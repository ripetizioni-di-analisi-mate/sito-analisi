Which word completes the analogy “Ignorant is to knowledge as poor is to …”?

- rich
- beggar
- wealth
- hunger

## Explanation

The precise connecting sentence is “An ignorant person lacks knowledge,” so the relationship links a person to what they lack. With poor, the sentence becomes “A poor person lacks wealth.” “Rich” appeals to anyone who reads the complete pair as opposites, although the opposite of “ignorant” is an adjective such as “educated,” and “knowledge” is a noun. A beggar is someone who asks for alms, often because of poverty, and hunger is a possible effect of poverty, so they form different types of relationships with “poor.”
