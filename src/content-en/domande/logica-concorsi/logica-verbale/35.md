Read the following passage.

“The train to Lago Verde leaves Pianello at 8\:10 and 14\:10 on working days and only at 9\:30 on Sundays and public holidays. The journey takes $50$ minutes. Bicycles are allowed only on the Sunday and public holiday trains.”

Based solely on the passage, which of the following statements is true?

- Someone traveling by train from Pianello to Lago Verde with a bicycle arrives in Lago Verde at 10\:20.
- Someone traveling by train from Pianello to Lago Verde with a bicycle can arrive in Lago Verde at 9\:00.
- Two trains leave Pianello for Lago Verde on Sundays and public holidays.
- The train to Lago Verde is more crowded on Sundays and public holidays than on working days.

## Explanation

Bicycles are allowed only on Sunday and public holiday trains, and on those days the only train departs at 9\:30. The journey takes $50$ minutes, so a passenger with a bicycle reaches Lago Verde at 10\:20 and the statement is **true**. The 9\:00 arrival is the working-day train that leaves at 8\:10, which does not accept bicycles, so the statement about arriving at 9\:00 is false. The claim that there are two trains on Sundays and public holidays contradicts the word *only*, and the passage gives no passenger counts, so the comparison of how crowded the trains are **cannot be inferred**.
