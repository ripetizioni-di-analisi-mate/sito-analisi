Consider the following argument.

“In the town offices of Belvedere, employees who use the break room during breaks close more cases each day than colleagues who do not use it. Therefore, using the break room increases the number of cases closed.”

Which of the following statements, if true, weakens the argument?

- The break room was furnished last year.
- Employees who have started using the break room close more cases than they did before.
- More than half the employees use the break room.
- The break room is used primarily by employees who close cases more quickly and therefore have time for a break.

## Explanation

The argument observes that two things go together, using the break room and closing many cases, and concludes that the former is the **cause** of the latter. If the room is used mainly by employees who close cases more quickly, the relationship runs in the opposite direction: working quickly is what makes the break possible. The argument is therefore **weakened**. The statement that employees close more cases after starting to use the room than they used to strengthens the argument. The room’s furnishings and the number of employees who use it do not address the link between taking breaks and completing work.
