An exhibition guide says that a free ticket gives access to the permanent collection and that every guided tour must be booked. Which statement follows from the two rules without adding a new requirement?

- A free ticket automatically includes a place on a guided tour.
- The permanent collection can be visited only with a guide.
- Access to the collection with a free ticket does not replace a guided tour reservation.
- Only those with a paid ticket may book a guided tour.

## Explanation

The ticket gives access to the permanent collection. For guided tours, the text requires a separate reservation.
