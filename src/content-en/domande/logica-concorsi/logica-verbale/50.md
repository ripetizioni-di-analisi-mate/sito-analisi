A report argues that repairing the tables is preferable to replacing them because repair costs less and the repaired tables last as long as new ones. Which new finding would directly contradict one of the premises?

- Both options meet the size requirements.
- Repaired tables last half as long as new ones on average.
- New tables can be delivered before the course starts.
- The supplier picks up irreparable tables at no charge.

## Explanation

The argument says that the lifespans are equal. A finding that repaired tables last half as long directly contradicts this premise.
