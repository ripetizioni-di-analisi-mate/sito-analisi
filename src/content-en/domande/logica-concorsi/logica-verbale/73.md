Which pair shows the same opposition between an excess and a very small amount as “overabundance : scarcity”?

- Attentiveness : punctuality
- Verbosity : terseness
- Prudence : hesitation
- Rigor : precision

## Explanation

Overabundance indicates an excess and scarcity a small amount. Verbosity is the use of more words than necessary, whereas terseness is expression in very few words.
