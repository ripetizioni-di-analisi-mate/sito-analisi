A writer claims that a reminder will reduce missed appointments. Which result most strengthens that prediction?

- Many patients book appointments in the morning.
- In a randomized comparison under otherwise identical conditions, the group receiving the reminder missed fewer appointments.
- The reminder is shorter than the booking form.
- Almost all patients own a cell phone.

## Explanation

Random assignment and comparable conditions help isolate the effect of the reminder. The smaller number of missed appointments in the group that received it supports the prediction.
