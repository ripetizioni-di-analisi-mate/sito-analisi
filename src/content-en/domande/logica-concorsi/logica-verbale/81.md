A manager argues that the library should extend its evening hours because many members work during the day. Which finding directly strengthens the argument?

- A survey shows that many members who work during the day would come after the current closing time.
- Energy costs rose in the last quarter.
- Members can view the online catalog even at night.
- A neighboring library bought new fiction books.

## Explanation

The proposal assumes there is demand for evening access. The survey shows that the extension would serve some of the very readers the argument refers to.
