Which term measures a different quantity from the other three?

- day
- year
- kilometer
- month

## Explanation

A day, a year, and a month are units of time, whereas a kilometer is a unit of length. The **criterion** “measures a duration” therefore applies to three words but not the fourth, so *kilometer* is the odd one out. “Is a unit of measurement” applies to all four words and so does not single out any item.
