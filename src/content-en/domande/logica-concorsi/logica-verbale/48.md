A report explains that the office received fewer phone calls after opening an online service desk. It received more written messages during the same period. Which statement is justified?

- Phone calls decreased and written messages increased.
- All the requests received were less urgent.
- Opening the online service desk was the sole cause of the changes.
- The total number of contacts decreased.

## Explanation

The report describes two changes in opposite directions. Without their respective sizes and further information, neither the net change nor the cause can be determined.
