A manager claims a course is effective because the participants earned high scores on the final test. Which information is most relevant for distinguishing the effect of the course from their initial level of preparation?

- The number of occupied seats in the classroom.
- The time it took to publish the results.
- The participants’ scores on an equivalent test taken before the course.
- The list of materials provided at the end of the course.

## Explanation

The initial scores provide a baseline for comparing the final scores. The comparison can show whether there was an improvement over the starting level.
