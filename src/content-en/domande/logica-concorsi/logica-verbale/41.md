Consider the following argument.

“Since new lights were installed in the park on Via Garibaldi, fewer bicycles have been stolen there. Therefore, the new lights reduced bicycle theft.”

Which of the following statements, if true, weakens the argument?

- The new lights use less energy than the old ones.
- During the same period, a night security guard began patrolling the park.
- More bicycles were stolen in the park before the lights were installed.
- Bicycle thefts did not decline in city parks where new lights were not installed.

## Explanation

The argument concludes that the lights are the **cause** of the decline in thefts. If a night guard also began patrolling the park during the same period, the decline can be explained without the lights, so this statement **weakens** the argument. The statement about the other parks strengthens it, since it shows that thefts did not decline where no lights were installed. The energy consumption of the lights is irrelevant, and the statement about more thefts before installation repeats the premise.
