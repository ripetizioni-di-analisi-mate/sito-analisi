In which pair is the relationship between the part and the whole reversed?

- page – book
- brick – wall
- link – chain
- bunch of grapes – grape

## Explanation

A page is part of a book, a brick part of a wall, and a link part of a chain. In these three pairs the relationship is therefore **part and whole**, with the part first. In *bunch of grapes – grape*, the relationship is of the same type but in the opposite direction, because a grape is part of a bunch. The **criterion** that singles out the odd pair is therefore the direction of the relationship.
