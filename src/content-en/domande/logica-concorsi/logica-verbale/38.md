Consider the following argument.

“Da Gino received the highest score in the city’s restaurant guide, so it serves the best food in the city.”

Which of the following is an unstated assumption of the argument?

- Da Gino received the highest score in the guide.
- All city residents consult the guide.
- Da Gino is the most expensive restaurant in the city.
- The guide’s score measures food quality.

## Explanation

Apply the **negation test**. If the guide’s score does not measure food quality, perhaps because it rates only the decor, the highest score says nothing about where the food is best, so the conclusion no longer follows. The statement about what the score measures is therefore an **assumption**. The statement about Da Gino’s score repeats the premise. Negating the statement about residents consulting the guide leaves the conclusion supportable. The restaurant’s prices have no bearing on the link between the score and the quality of the food.
