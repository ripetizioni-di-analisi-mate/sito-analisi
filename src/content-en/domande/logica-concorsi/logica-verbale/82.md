To justify closing a pickup point, a manager cites the low average number of daily pickups. Which fact most directly weakens this justification?

- The point uses the same booking system as the others.
- Some of the staff also work at other pickup points.
- The average number of pickups is similar to that of the previous month.
- On certain predictable days, the point handles a surge in pickups that the other points cannot accommodate.

## Explanation

A low average can coexist with substantial peaks. If the other points lack the capacity to handle those peaks, the average is insufficient to justify closure.
