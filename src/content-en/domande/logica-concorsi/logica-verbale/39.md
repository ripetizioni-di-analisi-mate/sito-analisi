Consider the following argument.

“Route 7 is the only bus route linking the town of Valle to the new school complex, so students in Valle will take route 7 to school.”

Which of the following is an unstated assumption of the argument?

- Students in Valle will travel to the school complex by bus.
- All residents of Valle prefer the bus to the car.
- Route 7 is the only bus route linking Valle to the school complex.
- Buses on route 7 run every $10$ minutes.

## Explanation

Apply the **negation test**. If the students in Valle do not travel to the school complex by bus, perhaps because their parents drive them, the fact that route 7 is the only bus route tells us nothing about their journey, so the conclusion no longer follows. The statement that they travel by bus is therefore an **assumption**. The statement about *all* residents is too strong because its negation, “At least one resident does not prefer the bus to the car,” leaves the conclusion supportable. The statement about route 7 repeats the premise, and the frequency of service does not affect the link between premise and conclusion.
