Which word completes the analogy “Rapid is to fast as tired is to …”?

- rested
- weary
- sleep
- slow

## Explanation

“Rapid” and “fast” have the same meaning, so the relationship is **synonymy**. The missing word is a synonym of “tired,” namely *weary*. “Rested” means the opposite of “tired” and appeals to anyone who recognizes that the two words are related without checking what kind of relationship they have. “Slow” is the opposite of “rapid,” so it relates to the complete pair and does not form a pair of synonyms with “tired.” Sleep is a state that tiredness can bring on, so “tired – sleep” expresses cause and effect.
