“The shorter route is closed for construction on working days. It is open on Sundays, but the longer route remains accessible then too.” What follows for Sundays?

- Both routes are accessible.
- Only the shorter route is accessible.
- Only the longer route is accessible.
- Both routes are closed.

## Explanation

The passage says that the shorter route is open on Sundays. It adds that the longer route remains accessible on those days as well.
