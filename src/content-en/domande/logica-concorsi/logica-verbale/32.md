Read the following passage.

“The town of Valbruna offers residents a grant toward the purchase of an electric bicycle. The grant covers $30\%$ of the price, up to a maximum of $300$ euros, and is available only once per family. Applications must be submitted by May 31.”

Based solely on the passage, which of the following statements is true?

- The grant for a bicycle costing $1200$ euros is $360$ euros.
- Two brothers in the same family can each receive a grant.
- The grant for a bicycle costing $800$ euros is $240$ euros.
- Most families in Valbruna have already applied for the grant.

## Explanation

The grant covers $30\%$ of the price. For an $800$-euro bicycle, that is $800 \cdot 0.3 = 240$ euros, below the $300$-euro maximum, so the statement is **true**. For a $1200$-euro bicycle, $30\%$ is $360$ euros, but the grant cannot exceed $300$ euros, so the statement about $360$ euros is false. The grant is available only once per family, so two brothers in the same family cannot both receive it. The passage does not say how many applications have been received, so the statement about most families **cannot be inferred**.
