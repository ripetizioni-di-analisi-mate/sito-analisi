Which pair of expressions makes the following sentence coherent? “The regional train is ___ than the intercity bus, but it costs more. ___, many commuters choose the intercity bus.”

- cheaper – therefore
- faster – therefore
- faster – although
- cheaper – although

## Explanation

The connective *but* links two facts in **contrast**, so the first missing expression must identify an advantage of the train that contrasts with its higher price. The first blank therefore takes *faster*. “Cheaper” contradicts “it costs more.” The choice of the intercity bus follows from the higher price of rail travel, so the second relationship is **consequence** and requires *therefore*. The connective *although* introduces a concession and takes the subjunctive in Italian. Placing it before “many commuters choose the intercity bus” would leave the sentence without a main clause.
