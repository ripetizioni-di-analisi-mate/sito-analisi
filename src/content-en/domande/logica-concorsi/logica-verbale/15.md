Which pair of words does not belong to the group formed by the other three?

- painter – painting
- actor – stage
- sculptor – statue
- writer – novel

## Explanation

Write the **connecting sentence** for each pair. A painter creates a painting, a sculptor a statue, and a writer a novel, so in three pairs the second word is the work produced by the person named first. A stage is the place where an actor works, so *actor – stage* expresses a different relationship. All four pairs concern people in artistic occupations, so their common theme does not distinguish any one pair.
