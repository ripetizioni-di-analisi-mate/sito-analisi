Which connective presents the extension as the reason for the postponement? “The deadline was extended. ___, the committee postponed the review.”

- or
- nevertheless
- therefore
- previously

## Explanation

In the sentence the extension of the deadline is the cause and the postponement of the review is its consequence, so the blank needs a connective that introduces a consequence. “Therefore” has this meaning and links the postponement to the extension. “Nevertheless” introduces a contrast, “previously” a time sequence and “or” an alternative, so none of the three presents the postponement as an effect of the extension.
