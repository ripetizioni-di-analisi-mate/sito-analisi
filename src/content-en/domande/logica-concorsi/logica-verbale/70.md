A fictional notice states that interviews take place in person unless individual authorization is granted. Requests for authorization may be submitted through the preceding Friday. Which statement does the text support?

- Individual authorization is required even for in-person interviews.
- All remote interviews take place on the preceding Friday.
- All requests submitted by Friday are granted.
- A remote interview requires individual authorization.

## Explanation

The normal arrangement is an in-person interview, and authorization allows an exception. The deadline given applies to submitting the request.
