“Only registered users can view the full document. The table of contents, however, is visible to everyone.” According to the text, what can someone who is not registered do?

- Always view at least half of the document.
- View the table of contents.
- Register automatically by opening the table of contents.
- View the full document on request.

## Explanation

The text makes the table of contents available to everyone. Access to the full document remains restricted to registered users.
