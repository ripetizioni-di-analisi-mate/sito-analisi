Read the following passage.

“In Monte Rosso Regional Park, rangers count the deer living in the park every year. There were $46$ deer in 2021 and $58$ in 2023. According to the rangers, the increase is due to the hunting ban introduced in 2020.”

Based solely on the passage, which of the following statements is false?

- Between 2021 and 2023, the deer population increased by $12$.
- The hunting ban was introduced before 2021.
- In 2022 there were $52$ deer.
- The rangers count the deer once every two years.

## Explanation

The passage says the rangers count the deer *every year*, so the statement that they count them every two years is **false**. That option appeals to anyone who infers the frequency of the counts from the years cited, 2021 and 2023. The number of deer in 2022 does not appear in the text, so the statement about that number **cannot be inferred**, even though $52$ is halfway between $46$ and $58$. The other two statements are true, because $58 - 46 = 12$ and the ban dates to 2020.
