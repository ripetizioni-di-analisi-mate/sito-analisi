Which connective makes the following sentence coherent? “The flight left on time ___ it had been snowing for hours.”

- because
- though
- so
- in fact

## Explanation

Hours of snowfall lead us to expect a delay, yet the flight left on time. The clause introduced by the connective leads us to expect the opposite of the main clause, which remains true, so the relationship is **concession**. The only concessive connective among the options is *though*, which in Italian is “sebbene” with the subjunctive “nevicasse.” In this sentence the subjunctive would give Italian “perché” the sense of “so that”, but snowfall is not the purpose of departing on time. “So” and “in fact” take the indicative in Italian and would present the snow as a consequence or confirmation of the on-time departure.
