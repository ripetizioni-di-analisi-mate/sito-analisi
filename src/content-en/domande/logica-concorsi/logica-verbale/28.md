Read the following passage.

“Starting September 1, $40$ bicycle spaces are available to students in the inner courtyard of Rodari Middle School in Coste. The courtyard opens at 7\:45 and closes at 14\:30.”

Based solely on the passage, which of the following statements cannot be inferred?

- More than $40$ students cycle to school.
- There are $40$ bicycle spaces in the courtyard.
- The courtyard is open at 15\:00.
- The courtyard stays open for less than seven hours a day.

## Explanation

The passage gives the number of bicycle spaces, but it does not say how many students cycle to school. The statement that there are more than $40$ such students therefore **cannot be inferred**. The courtyard is open from 7\:45 to 14\:30, a total of $6$ hours and $45$ minutes, so “less than seven hours” is true. The statement that the courtyard is open at 15\:00 is false because by then it has closed.
