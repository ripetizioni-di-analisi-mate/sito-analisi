Which pair of connectives makes the following sentence coherent? “___ the underground parking lot was free, many drivers left their cars on the street ___ the parking entrance was hard to find.”

- Since – because
- Though – because
- Though – so
- As – however

## Explanation

A free parking lot leads us to expect drivers to use it, yet many left their cars on the street. The first relationship is therefore **concession** and requires *though* (Italian “sebbene,” followed by the subjunctive “fosse”). The entrance being hard to find is the reason many drivers left their cars on the street, so the second relationship is **cause** and requires *because*. In *Though – so*, the first connective is correct, but *so* presents the hard-to-find entrance as a result of the drivers’ choice. “Since” and “as” introduce a cause and take the indicative in Italian, but free parking is not a reason to leave a car on the street.
