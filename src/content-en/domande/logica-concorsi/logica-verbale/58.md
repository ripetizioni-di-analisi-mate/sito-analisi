“The laboratory will not buy new instruments this year. It will, however, continue to replace faulty components.” Which summary preserves both pieces of information?

- The laboratory will continue replacing faulty components without buying new instruments.
- The laboratory is suspending all spending until next year.
- More instruments and fewer components will be purchased this year.
- The laboratory will replace every instrument that breaks.

## Explanation

The first sentence concerns new instruments. The second specifies that the replacement of faulty components will continue.
