Which word completes the analogy “Running is to sweat as cold is to …”?

- shiver
- winter
- heat
- scarf

## Explanation

Running produces sweat, so the relationship is **cause and effect**, with the cause first. Cold produces shivers, so the missing word is *shiver*. “Winter” appeals to anyone who overlooks the direction of the relationship: winter brings cold weather, but “Cold produces winter” is false. “Heat” is the opposite of “cold,” and a scarf offers protection from the cold.
