Which connective makes the following sentence coherent? “Last winter it often snowed in Pratofiorito. ___, this winter no snow has fallen.”

- so
- because
- in fact
- by contrast

## Explanation

The two parts describe two winters with opposite conditions, so the relationship is **contrast** and the connective is *by contrast*. With “so,” the lack of snow this winter would become a consequence of last year’s snowfalls. With “because,” it would become their cause. “In fact” introduces confirmation of a previous statement, whereas the second part says the opposite.
