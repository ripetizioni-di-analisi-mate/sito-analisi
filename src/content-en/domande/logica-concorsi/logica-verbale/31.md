Read the following passage.

“In 2024, the university library in Corvara stayed open until 23\:00 in January and June, when exams are held. The number of evening visits that year was triple the number in 2023. The library is always closed on Sundays.”

Based solely on the passage, which of the following statements cannot be inferred?

- In 2024, there were more evening visits in January than in June.
- In 2024, the number of evening visits was triple the number in 2023.
- On a Sunday in January 2024, the library was open at 22\:00.
- In 2024, January was the only month in which the library stayed open until 23\:00.

## Explanation

The passage compares evening visits in 2024 with those in 2023 but does not say how the 2024 visits were distributed across the months. The claim that there were more evening visits in January than in June therefore **cannot be inferred**. The statement about the number tripling repeats a fact from the passage and is true. The Sunday statement is false because the library is always closed on Sundays. The statement limiting the 23\:00 closing time to January is false because those hours also applied in June.
