Read the following passage.

“The Lauria covered market is open Tuesday through Sunday. It has $24$ stalls: $10$ selling fruit and vegetables, $8$ selling fish, and $6$ selling cheese. The fish stalls close at 13\:00. All other stalls close at 19\:00.”

Based solely on the passage, which of the following statements is false?

- There are more fruit and vegetable stalls than cheese stalls.
- There are $16$ stalls that do not sell fish.
- The fish stalls are open at 15\:00.
- The market is busier on Sundays than on Tuesdays.

## Explanation

The fish stalls close at 13\:00, so they are closed at 15\:00 and the statement that they are open is **false**. There are $10$ fruit and vegetable stalls and $6$ cheese stalls, so the comparison between these numbers is true. The statement about the stalls that do not sell fish is also true, because $10 + 6 = 16$. The passage gives no visitor numbers, so the comparison between Sundays and Tuesdays **cannot be inferred**.
