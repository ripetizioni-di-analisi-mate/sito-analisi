Choose the connective that specifies how early the team finished. “The team finished ahead of schedule, ___ it met the deadline a day early.”

- despite the fact that
- which is to say that
- with the aim that
- on condition that

## Explanation

The second clause specifies how far ahead of the deadline the team finished. “Which is to say that” introduces the explanation of the original wording.
