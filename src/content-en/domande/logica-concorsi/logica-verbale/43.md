Consider the following argument.

“In October Rita’s store sold many more umbrellas than in September. Therefore, it rained more in the city in October than in September.”

Which of the following statements, if true, does not weaken the argument?

- In October the city’s other stores also sold more umbrellas than in September.
- In October Rita put the umbrellas on sale at half price.
- In October the neighborhood’s other umbrella store closed and its customers switched to Rita’s.
- In October a group of tourists bought many umbrellas displaying the city’s name from Rita as souvenirs.

## Explanation

The argument infers from sales in a single store that it rained more in October. The half-price sale, the closure of the nearby store, and the tourists’ purchase explain the increase in sales without appealing to rainfall. They offer possible **alternative causes** and weaken the argument. An increase in sales at every store in the city, on the other hand, rules out causes specific to Rita’s store as the sole explanation. It therefore does not weaken the argument and, if anything, **strengthens** it.
