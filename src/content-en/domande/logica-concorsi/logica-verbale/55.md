An office proposes seeing visitors by appointment to reduce waiting times, arguing that scheduling meetings at assigned times will prevent simultaneous arrivals. Which assumption does this explanation directly require?

- Every visitor prefers appointments to waiting in line.
- All meetings last exactly the same length of time.
- Visitors keep to their assigned times closely enough to spread out arrivals.
- The office can double the number of counters.

## Explanation

The proposed mechanism depends on arrivals largely following the assigned times. If everyone arrived together, booking appointments would not spread arrivals out as expected.
