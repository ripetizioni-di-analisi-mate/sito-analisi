Which word makes the following sentence coherent? “The new online form was supposed to make it easier to request a certificate. By contrast, many users found it ___.”

- convenient
- useful
- quick
- complicated

## Explanation

The connective *by contrast* indicates a **contrast** between the second part and what the first leads us to expect. The first part leads us to expect a simple form, so the missing word means the opposite: *complicated*. “Convenient,” “useful,” and “quick” describe a form that works well and match the expectation in the first part, so *by contrast* would make the sentence incoherent with them.
