Read the following passage.

“The adult swimming course at the Selva pool goes ahead only if at least $8$ people sign up by September 15. This year, $6$ people had signed up by September 15, and the course did not go ahead. Those who signed up had their fees refunded.”

Based solely on the passage, which of the following statements cannot be inferred?

- As of September 15, $2$ more people had to sign up to reach the minimum.
- This year, the course went ahead with $6$ people enrolled.
- Those who signed up got their fees back.
- If $8$ people had signed up by September 15, the course would have gone ahead.

## Explanation

“The course goes ahead only if at least $8$ people sign up” makes enrollment of at least $8$ people a **necessary condition** for running the course. A necessary condition does not guarantee the result, because there may be other requirements. The claim that the course would have gone ahead with $8$ people therefore **cannot be inferred**. The claim that the course went ahead with $6$ people is false because the passage says it did not go ahead. The other two statements are true because $8 - 6 = 2$ and the passage says the fees were refunded.
