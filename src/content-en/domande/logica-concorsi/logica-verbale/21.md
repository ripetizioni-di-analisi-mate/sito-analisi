Which connective makes the following sentence coherent? “The museum will be closed throughout March; ___, restoration work is underway in the galleries.”

- however
- so
- in fact
- although

## Explanation

The restoration work is the reason for the museum closure, so the second clause explains the first. The relationship is **explanation**, and the connective that expresses it after the semicolon is *in fact*. “So” would present the work as an effect of the closure, reversing the relationship. “However” would introduce a fact contrary to expectations, and “although” a concession with the subjunctive in Italian, whereas the work in progress makes the closure predictable.
