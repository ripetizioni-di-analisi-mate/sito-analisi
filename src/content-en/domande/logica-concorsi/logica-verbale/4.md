Which word completes the analogy “Library is to reading as swimming pool is to …”?

- water
- lifeguard
- swimming
- swim cap

## Explanation

A library is a place where people read, so the relationship is **place and activity**. With swimming pool, the connecting sentence becomes “A swimming pool is a place where people swim.” Water is what the pool contains, a lifeguard is someone who works there, and a swim cap is an object used there, so none of these words denotes an activity.
