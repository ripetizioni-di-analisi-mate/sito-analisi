A memo specifies that the inventory should count objects physically present at the site on the day of the inspection. Items on loan are listed separately. On the inspection day, a projector belonging to the site is on loan. What follows?

- The projector should be counted twice in the total of items present.
- The projector should be counted among the items physically present.
- The projector should be removed from the site’s records.
- The projector belongs on the separate list of items on loan.

## Explanation

The memo distinguishes physical presence from ownership. The projector is on loan on the relevant day, so it belongs on the separate list.
