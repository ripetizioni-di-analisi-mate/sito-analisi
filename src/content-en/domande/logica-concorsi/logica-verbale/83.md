In “sonnet : poem,” the first term denotes a particular type of the second. Which pair follows the same pattern?

- Chapter : novel
- Plot : play
- Series : volume
- Short story : narrative

## Explanation

A sonnet is a type of poem, just as a short story is a type of narrative. A chapter and a plot, by contrast, are parts or aspects of works.
