Read the following passage.

“The Montefalco municipal office for identity cards sees the public only Monday through Friday, from 9\:00 to 12\:00, and only by appointment. Appointments are booked on the town’s website. Anyone who arrives without an appointment will not be seen.”

Based solely on the passage, which of the following statements is true?

- In urgent cases, the office also sees people without an appointment.
- The office also sees the public on Tuesday afternoons.
- The identity card is issued on the day of the appointment.
- The office does not see the public on Saturdays.

## Explanation

The passage says the office sees the public *only* Monday through Friday, so it does not see the public on Saturdays and the statement about Saturday is **true**. The statement about Tuesday afternoons is false, since the office’s public hours end at 12\:00. The statement about urgent cases is also false: people arriving without an appointment will not be seen, and the passage allows no exceptions. The passage says nothing about how long it takes to issue a card, so the statement about same-day issuance **cannot be inferred**.
