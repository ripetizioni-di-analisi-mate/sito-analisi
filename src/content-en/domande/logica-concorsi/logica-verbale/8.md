Which pair of words completes the analogy “… is to lock as button is to …”?

- key – shirt
- door – shirt
- door – buttonhole
- key – buttonhole

## Explanation

With two missing words, each option forms two pairs that must have the same relationship. A key goes into a lock and a button goes into a buttonhole, so with *key – buttonhole* both pairs link an object to the opening intended to receive it. With *door – shirt*, the relationships are part and whole in opposite directions: a lock is part of a door, whereas a button is part of a shirt. In each of the other two options, only one word is correct, so the two pairs express different relationships.
