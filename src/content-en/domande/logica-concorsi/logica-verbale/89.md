A text says that the gym is open from 8:00 to 20:00, except on Thursdays, when it opens at 10:00 and still closes at 20:00. Which visit is definitely within the stated hours?

- Thursday at 11:00
- Thursday at 9:00
- Wednesday at 21:00
- Tuesday at 7:00

## Explanation

On Thursdays the gym opens at 10:00, so 11:00 is within opening hours. The other visits are before opening or after closing.
