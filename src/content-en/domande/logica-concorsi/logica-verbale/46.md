“To reduce errors on forms, the instructions need to be rewritten. In fact, nearly all errors concern fields where it is unclear what information is being requested.” What is the conclusion of the argument?

- The meaning of every field is ambiguous.
- The instructions on the forms need to be rewritten.
- Any revision of the instructions eliminates all errors.
- Nearly all errors concern ambiguous fields.

## Explanation

“In fact” introduces the information used to justify the proposal. The conclusion is the proposal to rewrite the instructions to reduce errors.
