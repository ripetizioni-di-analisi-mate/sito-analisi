Which word completes the analogy “Win is to lose as remember is to …”?

- recall
- memory
- repeat
- forget

## Explanation

“Win” and “lose” have opposite meanings, so the relationship is **opposition** and the missing word is the opposite of “remember,” namely *forget*. The verb “recall” is a synonym of “remember,” so it forms a different kind of pair. Memory is the faculty we use to remember, and repeating something helps us remember it. Neither word is therefore the opposite of “remember.”
