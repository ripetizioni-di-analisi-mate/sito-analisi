Which word makes the following sentence coherent? “Even though the trail was ___, the guide decided to continue to the summit.”

- icy
- well marked
- sunny
- easy

## Explanation

The connective *even though* introduces a **concession**, a fact that leads us to expect the opposite of the main clause. The main clause says that the guide continued, so the missing word must describe a trail on which we would expect the guide to turn back. An icy trail is dangerous, so *icy* makes the sentence coherent. A well-marked, sunny, or easy trail would give a reason to continue, and a reason is introduced by *since*.
