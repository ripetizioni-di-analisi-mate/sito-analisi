Which word means the opposite of what the other three have in common?

- broad
- narrow
- vast
- extensive

## Explanation

Broad, vast, and extensive are **synonyms** here because they all describe something large in width or area, as in “a vast plain” or “an extensive territory.” “Narrow” is the opposite of “broad,” so the criterion of meaning singles it out.
