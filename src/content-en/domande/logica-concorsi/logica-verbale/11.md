Which word does not belong to the group formed by the other three?

- pot
- frying pan
- saucepan
- drinking glass

## Explanation

A pot, a frying pan, and a saucepan are containers placed over heat to cook food. A drinking glass is a container from which people drink, so the **criterion** “used for cooking over heat” singles out the drinking glass. “Is a container” applies to all four words, so it does not identify an odd one out.
