Which connective makes the following sentence coherent? “The store has run out of milk. ___, it cannot sell any today.”

- therefore
- because
- although
- by contrast

## Explanation

A store with no milk in stock cannot sell milk, so the second part follows from the first. The relationship is **consequence**, and the only connective among the options that expresses it is *therefore*. “Because” presents the consequence as the cause and reverses the relationship. “Although” introduces a concession, and its Italian equivalent takes the subjunctive. “By contrast” would introduce something contrary to expectations, whereas being unable to sell milk is exactly what we would expect.
