The manager compares the utility bills before and after new lights were installed and concludes that the lights reduced monthly energy use. Which finding most weakens that conclusion?

- The new lights were installed in the same rooms.
- The new lights are a different color from the old ones.
- After the change, the premises were closed for half the month.
- The bills are in the same account holder’s name.

## Explanation

The closure reduced the time the premises were in use and could explain the decrease. Comparing the two months therefore does not isolate the effect of the lights.
