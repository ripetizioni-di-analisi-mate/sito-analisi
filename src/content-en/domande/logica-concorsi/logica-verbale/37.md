Consider the following argument.

“The school gym is unsuitable for volleyball games because the ceiling is too low. Therefore, the school volleyball tournament should be held at the municipal sports center, which is available on Saturday mornings.”

What is the main conclusion of the argument?

- The school gym is unsuitable for volleyball games.
- The school volleyball tournament should be held at the municipal sports center.
- The gym ceiling is too low.
- The municipal sports center is available on Saturday mornings.

## Explanation

The statement about the low ceiling supports “The gym is unsuitable for volleyball games,” making it an intermediate conclusion. That statement and the sports center’s availability in turn support the proposal to hold the tournament there, which is introduced by *therefore*. The proposal does not support any other statement in the text, so it is the **main conclusion**. The claim about the gym appeals to anyone who stops at the first conclusion they encounter.
