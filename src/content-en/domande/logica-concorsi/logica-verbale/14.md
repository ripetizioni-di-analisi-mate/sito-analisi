Which term denotes the whole object to which the other three refer?

- car
- steering wheel
- hood
- seat

## Explanation

A steering wheel, a hood, and a seat are parts of a car, whereas a car is the whole object. The **criterion** of **part and whole** therefore distinguishes the only word for the whole from the three words for its parts. The odd one out is *car*.
