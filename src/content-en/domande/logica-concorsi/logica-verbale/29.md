Read the following passage.

“In the condominium at 12 Via Mazzini, the rules prohibit hanging laundry on balconies facing the street. The ban does not apply to balconies facing the courtyard. Some residents proposed lifting the ban, but the March meeting rejected the proposal.”

Based solely on the passage, which of the following statements is true?

- All residents support the ban.
- The ban remained in effect after the March meeting.
- Hanging laundry on balconies facing the courtyard is prohibited.
- Most residents voted against the proposal.

## Explanation

The March meeting rejected the proposal to lift the ban, and the passage uses the present tense, “prohibit.” The ban therefore remained in effect after the meeting, and that statement is **true**. The passage does not say how many residents voted against the proposal, so the statement about most residents **cannot be inferred**. The statement that all residents support the ban is false because some proposed lifting it. The claim about courtyard balconies contradicts the sentence excluding them from the ban.
