Which pair of words completes the analogy “Scale is to weight as … is to …”?

- measuring tape – length
- length – measuring tape
- watch – wrist
- ruler – notebook

## Explanation

The connecting sentence is “A scale is the instrument that measures weight,” so the pair links an instrument to the quantity it measures. With *measuring tape – length*, the sentence becomes “A measuring tape is the instrument that measures length.” The option *length – measuring tape* contains the correct words in reverse order. In *watch – wrist*, the second word is the part of the body where a watch is worn, while in *ruler – notebook*, the notebook is the object on which the ruler is used.
