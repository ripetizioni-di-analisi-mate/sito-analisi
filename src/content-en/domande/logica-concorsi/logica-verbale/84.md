Which word completes the sentence so that losing automatic renewal is an avoidable consequence? “The card must be updated by the end of the month. ___, renewal will not be automatic.”

- in any case
- furthermore
- consequently
- otherwise

## Explanation

“Otherwise” makes failure to renew automatically the consequence if the card is not updated. “Consequently” would present it as an effect of updating the card.
