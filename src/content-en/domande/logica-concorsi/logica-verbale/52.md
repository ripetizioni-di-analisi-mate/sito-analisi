A group argues that an information sheet is clear because very few people called to ask for clarification. Which possibility weakens the argument?

- The operators recorded the number of calls.
- Many readers who were unsure chose not to call.
- The sheet was printed in black and white.
- Some readers kept the sheet.

## Explanation

There may have been few calls because readers gave up on asking for clarification. In that case, the number of calls does not directly measure clarity.
