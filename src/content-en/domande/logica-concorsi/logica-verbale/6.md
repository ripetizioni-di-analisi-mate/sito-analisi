Which word completes the analogy “Whisper is to shout as walk is to …”?

- stroll
- stop
- run
- shoes

## Explanation

Whispering and shouting are two ways of speaking, and shouting is the more intense, so the relationship is **degree of intensity**, from weaker to stronger. Walking and running are two ways of moving on foot, and running is the more intense, so the missing word is *run*. The verb “stroll” describes a slower way of walking, so it forms a pair in which the intensity decreases, reversing the direction. To stop is the opposite of walking, and shoes are objects used for walking.
