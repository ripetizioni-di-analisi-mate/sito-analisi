Read the following passage.

“A total of $150$ athletes took part in the Castelnuovo running race. Some did not complete the course because of the heat. Every athlete who completed the course received a medal.”

Based solely on the passage, which of the following statements is false?

- Athletes who did not receive a medal did not complete the course.
- Every athlete completed the course.
- At least one athlete did not complete the course.
- More than $100$ athletes completed the course.

## Explanation

The passage says that some athletes did not complete the course, and “some” means *at least one*, so the statement that everyone finished is **false**. For the same reason, “At least one athlete did not complete the course” is true. The sentence about medals gives the implication “If an athlete completed the course, then the athlete received a medal.” Its **contrapositive** says that anyone who did not receive a medal did not complete the course, so that statement is also true. The passage does not say how many athletes completed the course, so the statement about more than $100$ athletes **cannot be inferred**.
