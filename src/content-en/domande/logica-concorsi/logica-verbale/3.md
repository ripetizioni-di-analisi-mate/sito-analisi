Which word completes the analogy “Hammer is to tool as violin is to …”?

- musical instrument
- musician
- bow
- concert

## Explanation

The connecting sentence is “A hammer is a type of tool,” so the relationship is **member and category**. With violin, this becomes “A violin is a type of musical instrument.” A musician is a person who plays a violin, a bow is the object used to play it, and a concert is an occasion when people hear it. None of these words names the category to which a violin belongs.
