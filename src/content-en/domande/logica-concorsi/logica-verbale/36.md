Consider the following argument.

“The town should install a traffic light at the Via Po intersection. Hundreds of students pass through that intersection every day, and there have been four crashes there in the past year.”

What is the conclusion of the argument?

- Hundreds of students pass through the Via Po intersection every day.
- There were four crashes at the Via Po intersection in the past year.
- The town should install a traffic light at the Via Po intersection.
- Students should avoid the Via Po intersection.

## Explanation

The statements about students and crashes are **premises** because they explain why the intersection is dangerous. Installing a traffic light is the proposal these reasons support, so it is the **conclusion**, even though it appears first. The *therefore* test confirms this: “Hundreds of students pass through that intersection and there have been four crashes there. Therefore, the town should install a traffic light” is coherent. The text does not propose that students avoid the intersection.
