For nonzero vectors of fixed magnitudes, when is their dot product greatest?

- When their angle is $120^\circ$.
- When they are parallel with opposite directions.
- When they are parallel with matching directions.
- When they are perpendicular.

## Explanation

At fixed magnitudes only the angle’s cosine changes. Its maximum is one at zero angle. Perpendicularity gives zero and opposition gives the negative minimum. Sine instead governs cross-product magnitude and would peak at ninety degrees.
