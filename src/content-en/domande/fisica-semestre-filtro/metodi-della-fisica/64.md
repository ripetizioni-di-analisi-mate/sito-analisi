If $\vec{a}=(3;-4)\,\mathrm{m}$, what does $-2\vec{a}$ describe?

- Components $(-6;8)\,\mathrm{m}$, magnitude $-10\,\mathrm{m}$.
- Components $(6;-8)\,\mathrm{m}$, magnitude $10\,\mathrm{m}$.
- Components $(-6;-8)\,\mathrm{m}$, same direction.
- Components $(-6;8)\,\mathrm{m}$, magnitude $10\,\mathrm{m}$, opposite direction.

## Explanation

Minus two multiplies every component, while magnitude multiplies by the factor’s absolute value. Initial magnitude is five metres, so it becomes ten and cannot become negative. The scalar sign reverses direction without choosing only one axis to reverse.
