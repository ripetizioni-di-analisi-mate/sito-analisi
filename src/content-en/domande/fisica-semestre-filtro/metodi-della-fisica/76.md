In torque magnitude $|\vec\tau|=Fb$, what is lever arm $b$?

- Perpendicular distance from the reference point to the force’s line of action.
- Always the distance from reference point to application point.
- The force component along the position vector.
- Distance travelled by the application point over time.

## Explanation

The formula follows from $rF\sin\theta$: factor $r\sin\theta$ is the perpendicular distance to the line of action. It equals the distance to the application point only in the perpendicular case. A force component has different units, while travelled distance concerns motion rather than this instantaneous geometry.
