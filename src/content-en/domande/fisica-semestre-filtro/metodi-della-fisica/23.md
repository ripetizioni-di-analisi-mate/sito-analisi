To convert $72\,\mathrm{km/h}$, which factor product directly cancels kilometre and hour symbols, leaving metres per second?

- $\dfrac{1000\,\mathrm{m}}{1\,\mathrm{km}}\dfrac{3600\,\mathrm{s}}{1\,\mathrm{h}}$.
- $\dfrac{1\,\mathrm{km}}{1000\,\mathrm{m}}\dfrac{3600\,\mathrm{s}}{1\,\mathrm{h}}$.
- $\dfrac{1\,\mathrm{km}}{1000\,\mathrm{m}}\dfrac{1\,\mathrm{h}}{3600\,\mathrm{s}}$.
- $\dfrac{1000\,\mathrm{m}}{1\,\mathrm{km}}\dfrac{1\,\mathrm{h}}{3600\,\mathrm{s}}$.

## Explanation

The initial kilometre is in the numerator and must cancel a kilometre in the factor’s denominator. The initial hour is in the denominator and needs an hour in the numerator. This unit check determines both conversion directions and leaves only metres per second, independently of the initial number.
