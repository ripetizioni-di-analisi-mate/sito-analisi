A positive length is rewritten from centimetres to metres without physical transformation. What changes?

- The numerical value must remain equal.
- The numerical value increases one hundredfold while dimension remains length.
- The quantity increases because the metre is larger.
- Numerical value and unit change, while the quantity stays equal.

## Explanation

Measurement expresses the product of a number and a unit. Changing the unit requires a compensating numerical change so the product represents the same length. Its length dimension is unchanged: it does not depend on scale choice. Converting to metres decreases the number one hundredfold.
