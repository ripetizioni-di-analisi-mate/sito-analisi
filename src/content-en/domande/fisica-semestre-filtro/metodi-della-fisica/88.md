For $y=\dfrac kx$ with $k>0$ and $x>0$, how does doubling $x$ change $y$?

- It halves it.
- It reduces it to one quarter.
- It leaves it unchanged.
- It doubles it.

## Explanation

The product $xy$ is constant: doubling one factor requires halving the other. A reduction to one quarter would belong to an inverse-square law, different from the given one. The graph is not a constant-slope straight line; the relation between changes depends on the point considered.
