An area is $2.0\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. Which value in square metres is correct?

- $2.0\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}$.
- $4.0\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}$.
- $2.0\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^2}$.
- $2.0\cdot10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m^2}$.

## Explanation

Square the centimetre unit factor, not the numerical two already multiplying an area. The factor is $10^{-4}$. Four times ten to the minus four would instead be the area of a square with a two-centimetre side. Confusing given area with given side produces that error.
