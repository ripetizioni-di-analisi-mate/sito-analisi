For nonzero vectors of fixed magnitudes, when is cross-product magnitude greatest?

- When the angle is $180^\circ$.
- When the angle is $0^\circ$.
- When the angle is $90^\circ$.
- When the angle is $45^\circ$.

## Explanation

Magnitude is the product of magnitudes times angle sine, maximal at ninety degrees. Parallel cases, matching or opposite, instead give zero. Geometrically, a fixed-side parallelogram has maximum area when rectangular. At forty-five degrees the factor $\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$ is less than one, so it does not give the maximum.
