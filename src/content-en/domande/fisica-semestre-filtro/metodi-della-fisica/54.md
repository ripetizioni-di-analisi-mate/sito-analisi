A student wants $\sin30^\circ$ but enters 30 in radian mode. What is wrong?

- The calculator interprets a different angle, not the same measurement in another notation.
- Sine cannot use radians.
- The result must always be multiplied by 180 to correct it.
- The sine result must be expressed in degrees.

## Explanation

Thirty radians and thirty degrees are different angles. In radian mode, enter pi over six to represent thirty degrees. Sine is a dimensionless ratio, not an angle to convert after calculation. The correct conversion applies to the argument before evaluating the function.
