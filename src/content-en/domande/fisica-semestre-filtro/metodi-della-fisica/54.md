A calculator is set to radians. Which number must be entered as the argument of the sine to obtain $\sin30^\circ$?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$
- $30$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$
- $30\cdot\dfrac{180}{\pi}$

## Explanation

In radian mode the calculator reads the number entered as the measure of the angle in radians. Since $180^\circ=\pi\,\mathrm{rad}$, degrees are converted to radians by multiplying by $\dfrac{\pi}{180}$. Hence $30^\circ=30\cdot\dfrac{\pi}{180}\,\mathrm{rad}=\dfrac{\pi}{6}\,\mathrm{rad}$, and $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$ must be entered. Entering $30$ would give the sine of an angle of $30$ radians, about $-0.99$, which is different from $\sin30^\circ=\dfrac12$.
