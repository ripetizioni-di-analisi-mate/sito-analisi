Dimensionless vectors of magnitudes $3$ and $4$ have dot product $6$. Using $|\vec{a}\times\vec{b}|^2+(\vec{a}\cdot\vec{b})^2=a^2b^2$, what is cross-product magnitude?

- $6\sqrt3$.
- $\sqrt{138}$.
- $6$.
- $12$.

## Explanation

The squared magnitude product is one hundred and forty-four; subtract squared dot product, thirty-six, obtaining one hundred and eight. Its square root is six square root of three. The relation follows from sine squared plus cosine squared equal to one. Subtracting six without squaring would violate the supplied equation.
