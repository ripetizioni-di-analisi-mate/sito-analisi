What dimensions does a position–time graph’s slope have?

- $LT$.
- $L^2T^{-1}$.
- $LT^{-2}$.
- $LT^{-1}$.

## Explanation

Slope is change in the vertical coordinate divided by change in the horizontal coordinate. Here length divided by time gives velocity. Length times time would concern graph area, not slope. Another time in the denominator gives acceleration, which would require a velocity–time graph.
