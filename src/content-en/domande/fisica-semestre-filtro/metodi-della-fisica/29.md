What is $\dfrac{6\cdot10^{-9}}{2\cdot10^{-3}}$?

- $3\cdot10^{-12}$.
- $3\cdot10^6$.
- $3\cdot10^{-6}$.
- $12\cdot10^{-6}$.

## Explanation

Divide coefficients and subtract the denominator exponent: minus nine minus minus three gives minus six. Adding exponents would give minus twelve and apply the wrong rule. Multiplying the result by the denominator checks that the supplied numerator is recovered.
