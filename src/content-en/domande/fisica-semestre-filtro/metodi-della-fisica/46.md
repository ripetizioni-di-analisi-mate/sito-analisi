Which property of the zero vector is correct?

- It has magnitude one because it is the additive identity.
- It always points along positive $x$.
- All its components equal one.
- It has zero magnitude and undefined direction.

## Explanation

Zero components give zero magnitude in every orthonormal frame. No preferred direction can be assigned. It is the additive identity, but that does not make it a unit vector. Normalizing it by its magnitude would divide by zero and would not define a direction.
