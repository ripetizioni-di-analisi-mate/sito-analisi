A graph has force in newtons vertically and time in seconds horizontally. What units do area and slope have?

- $\mathrm{N}$; $\mathrm{s}$.
- $\mathrm{N\,s}$; $\mathrm{N/s}$.
- $\mathrm{J}$; $\mathrm{W}$.
- $\mathrm{N/s}$; $\mathrm{N\,s}$.

## Explanation

Area multiplies both axis units, whereas slope divides the vertical unit by the horizontal one. Meaning cannot be transferred automatically from position–time or velocity–time graphs. In particular, newton seconds is not joules: work requires force times displacement, not time.
