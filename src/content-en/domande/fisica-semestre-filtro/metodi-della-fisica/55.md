Two arcs subtend the same angle but the second circle has twice the radius. How do arc lengths and arc-to-radius ratios compare?

- Arc length stays equal; the ratio halves.
- Both arc length and ratio double.
- Arc length quadruples; the ratio doubles.
- Arc length doubles; the ratio stays equal.

## Explanation

At fixed radian angle, $s=r\theta$ makes arc length proportional to radius. Dividing arc by radius recovers the same angle. A quadratic factor belongs to areas, not arc lengths. Radian definition is therefore independent of the size of the circle used.
