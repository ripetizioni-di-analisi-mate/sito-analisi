Two systems record mass measurements, each with its own uncertainty, one in grams and the other in kilograms. What is sufficient for comparison at the unit level?

- Round both numbers to the same integer without conversion.
- Compare numbers directly because dimension is the same.
- Convert to a common unit while retaining quantity and uncertainty information.
- Convert only the central value while keeping uncertainty numerically unchanged.

## Explanation

Equal dimension permits comparison without making units equal. Both central value and absolute uncertainty must be converted by the same ratio. Conversion does not remove relative uncertainty. Converting only the central value would assign a precision different from the original one; premature rounding can lose information.
