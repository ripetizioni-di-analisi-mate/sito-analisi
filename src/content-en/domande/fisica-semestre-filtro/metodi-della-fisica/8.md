For a homogeneous system under usual additive conditions, how are internal energy, mass, temperature and density classified?

- All intensive.
- Internal energy and mass extensive; temperature and density intensive.
- Internal energy and temperature extensive; mass and density intensive.
- Temperature and internal energy intensive; mass and density extensive.

## Explanation

Combining identical portions in the same state increases mass and internal energy by addition. Temperature and density instead characterize the state and remain equal under the conditions considered. Temperatures are not added as energy amounts. The criterion concerns system amount at fixed state, not absence of change in any process.
