A position–time graph segment lies below $x=0$ but rises to the right. What are position and velocity signs?

- Negative position, positive velocity.
- Positive position, negative velocity.
- Both negative.
- Both positive.

## Explanation

Position sign comes from height relative to zero; velocity sign comes from slope. Increasing negative position can approach the origin while moving positively. These are independent pieces of information: being left of the origin does not necessarily mean moving away from it.
