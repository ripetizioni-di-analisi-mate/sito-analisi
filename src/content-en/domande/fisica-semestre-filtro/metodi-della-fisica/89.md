From $V=\dfrac43\pi r^3$, with sphere volume $V>0$, which formula gives the physical radius?

- $r=\sqrt[3]{\dfrac{3V}{4\pi}}$.
- $r=\dfrac{3V}{4\pi}$.
- $r=\sqrt[3]{\dfrac{4\pi V}{3}}$.
- $r=\sqrt{\dfrac{3V}{4\pi}}$.

## Explanation

First isolate radius cubed by dividing by the coefficient, then take the cube root. A square root would give wrong dimensions; omitting the root would leave a volume. Positive volume gives positive radius, and substitution into the original law must recover the datum.
