In a velocity–time graph, a segment lies entirely below the horizontal axis. What is the sign of its displacement contribution?

- Zero, because velocity is negative.
- Negative for both displacement and distance travelled.
- Positive, because geometric area cannot be negative.
- Negative; its contribution to distance travelled is instead positive.

## Explanation

Displacement uses signed area, equivalent to velocity times positive duration on constant segments. Distance uses absolute velocity and does not decrease when motion changes direction. Using only positive geometric area would confuse these quantities.
