For dimensionless vectors $\vec{a}=(2;3)$ and $\vec{b}=(4;-1)$, what is $\vec{a}\cdot\vec{b}$?

- $-5$.
- $24$.
- $11$.
- $5$.

## Explanation

The dot product adds products of corresponding components: $2\cdot4+3(-1)=5$. The second contribution is negative. Using its magnitude would give eleven; reversing the whole result’s sign is not a requested operation. The result is a scalar, not a component pair.
