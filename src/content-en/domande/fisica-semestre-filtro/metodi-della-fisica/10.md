Which pair gives angle $\dfrac\pi4\,\mathrm{rad}$ in degrees and its sine?

- $90^\circ$ and $1$.
- $30^\circ$ and $\dfrac12$.
- $45^\circ$ and $\dfrac12$.
- $45^\circ$ and $\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$.

## Explanation

Pi radians corresponds to one hundred and eighty degrees, so one quarter is forty-five degrees. In an isosceles right triangle the two legs are equal and each is hypotenuse divided by square root of two. Sine is therefore square root of two over two; one half would be the sine of thirty degrees.
