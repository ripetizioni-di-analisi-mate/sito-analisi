Position follows $x(t)=(3\,\mathrm{m/s})t-1\,\mathrm{m}$. What are slope and intercept of the position–time graph?

- $3\,\mathrm{m}$; $-1\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $-1\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $3\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $-1\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $-3\,\mathrm{m/s}$; $1\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

The coefficient multiplying time is position change per unit time, hence slope. The independent term is position at zero time, hence intercept. Units identify them as a velocity and a length. A negative intercept does not imply negative velocity.
