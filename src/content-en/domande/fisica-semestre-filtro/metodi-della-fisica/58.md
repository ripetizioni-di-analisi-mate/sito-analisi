A table gives $\sin\theta=0.8$ and $\cos\theta=0.8$ for the same angle. Which check shows inconsistency?

- Sine and cosine can never be equal.
- Sine must always exceed cosine.
- The squared sum is $1.28$, not one.
- The sine–cosine product must always be one.

## Explanation

Unit-circle coordinates have squared sum one. Two values of zero point eight violate that identity. Sine and cosine can be equal, as at forty-five degrees, but their common positive value is square root of two over two, not an arbitrary number.
