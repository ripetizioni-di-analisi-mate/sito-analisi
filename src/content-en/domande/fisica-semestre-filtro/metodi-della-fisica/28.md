What is $3\cdot10^5+2\cdot10^4$ in normalized scientific notation?

- $3.2\cdot10^5$.
- $3.2\cdot10^4$.
- $5\cdot10^5$.
- $5\cdot10^9$.

## Explanation

The second term is $0.2\cdot10^5$, so add three and zero point two while keeping the common power. Adding exponents belongs to multiplication, not addition. The answer should be only slightly above the first term because the second is much smaller, providing an order-of-magnitude check.
