A sample has density $800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and volume $3.0\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^3}$. What is its mass?

- $240\,\mathrm{kg}$.
- $0.024\,\mathrm{kg}$.
- $0.24\,\mathrm{kg}$.
- $2.4\,\mathrm{kg}$.

## Explanation

From $\rho=\dfrac mV$ follows $m=\rho V$. Volume units cancel density’s denominator, leaving kilograms. The numerical product is zero point two four. An intuitive check uses a volume of three tenths of a litre: with density below water’s, mass must be below three tenths of a kilogram.
