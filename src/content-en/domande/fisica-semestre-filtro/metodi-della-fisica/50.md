A displacement has both components negative and neither zero. In which interval is its anticlockwise angle from $+x$ between zero and one turn?

- $0^\circ<\theta<90^\circ$.
- $180^\circ<\theta<270^\circ$.
- $90^\circ<\theta<180^\circ$.
- $270^\circ<\theta<360^\circ$.

## Explanation

Negative horizontal component means left; negative vertical component means down. Together they identify the third quadrant. Endpoints are excluded because an axis would make one component zero. The component ratio is positive in the first quadrant too, so tangent sign alone cannot distinguish them.
