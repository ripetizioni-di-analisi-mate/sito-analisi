What is $1.06\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$ in the coherent SI unit?

- $10600\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.
- $1060\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.
- $106\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.
- $1.06\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.

## Explanation

The numerator contributes $10^{-3}$ and the denominator $10^{-6}$. Their ratio is $10^3$, so the numerical value increases one thousandfold. The cube applies to the whole centimetre, including its prefix. Converting only mass or applying a linear conversion to volume gives an incorrect result.
