A vector has components $(3;4)\,\mathrm{m}$. Axes rotate $90^\circ$ anticlockwise while the vector stays fixed. What are its new components?

- $(-4;3)\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $(4;3)\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $(4;-3)\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $(3;4)\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

The new positive $x$ axis points along old positive vertical; the new positive $y$ axis points along old negative horizontal. Projections therefore become four and minus three. Rotating the vector with axes fixed is a different operation and would use the opposite rotation sign. Magnitude stays five metres.
