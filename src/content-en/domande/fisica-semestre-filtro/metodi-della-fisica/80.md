Which relation describes direct proportionality between $y$ and $x$, with nonzero constant $k$?

- $y=kx$.
- $y=\dfrac kx$.
- $y=kx+b$, $b\ne0$.
- $y=kx^2$.

## Explanation

In direct proportionality the quantity ratio is constant for nonzero values and the line passes through the origin. A nonzero intercept preserves an affine relation but prevents one quantity from doubling whenever the other doubles. Inverse and quadratic relations have different scaling laws.
