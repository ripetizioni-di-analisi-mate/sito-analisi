Which pair correctly expresses $25\,\mathrm{mg}$?

- $2.5\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{kg}=2.5\cdot10^5\,\mu\mathrm{g}$.
- $2.5\cdot10^{-2}\,\mathrm{kg}=2.5\cdot10^7\,\mu\mathrm{g}$.
- $2.5\cdot10^{-8}\,\mathrm{kg}=25\,\mu\mathrm{g}$.
- $2.5\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{kg}=2.5\cdot10^4\,\mu\mathrm{g}$.

## Explanation

A milligram is $10^{-3}$ grams, hence $10^{-6}$ kilograms; it is also one thousand micrograms. The number must decrease for a larger unit and increase for a smaller one. Confusing kilograms with grams loses a factor of one thousand even if the final unit symbols are correctly written.
