In a position–time graph, the vertical unit changes from metres to centimetres while the horizontal unit stays seconds. What happens to numerical slope?

- It divides by one hundred and physical velocity increases.
- Slope becomes acceleration.
- It multiplies by one hundred while physical velocity is unchanged.
- It necessarily stays equal because the line represents the same motion.

## Explanation

Position differences have numerical values one hundred times larger while durations are unchanged. Numerical ratio grows one hundredfold and the unit changes from metres per second to centimetres per second. Number and unit compensate. Physical slope is independent of unit choice or apparent inclination on the page.
