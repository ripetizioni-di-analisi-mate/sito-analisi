What are pressure dimensions, using $M,L,T$ for mass, length and time?

- $ML^{-1}T^{-2}$.
- $MLT^{-2}$.
- $ML^{-2}T^{-2}$.
- $ML^2T^{-2}$.

## Explanation

Pressure is force per unit area. Force dimensions are $MLT^{-2}$; division by $L^2$ leaves length exponent minus one. Positive length exponent one still describes force; exponent two describes energy. Dimensions follow the definition rather than the quantity symbol alone.
