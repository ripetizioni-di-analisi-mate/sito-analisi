For dimensionless vectors, if $\vec{b}=-3\vec{a}$ with $\vec{a}\ne\vec0$, what is $\vec{a}\times\vec{b}$?

- $3\vec{a}$.
- $-3\vec{a}$.
- $-3|\vec{a}|\vec{a}$.
- $\vec0$.

## Explanation

The vectors are parallel and opposite, with a straight angle. Sine is zero and cancels the cross product regardless of factor three. The negative sign creates no perpendicular component. Other answers lie along the original direction and do not satisfy this pair’s cross-product geometry.
