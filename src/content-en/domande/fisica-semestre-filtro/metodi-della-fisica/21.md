An interval lasts $0.20\,\mathrm{ms}$. How many microseconds is that?

- $20\,\mu\mathrm{s}$.
- $200\,\mu\mathrm{s}$.
- $0.00020\,\mu\mathrm{s}$.
- $2000\,\mu\mathrm{s}$.

## Explanation

Three powers of ten separate milli and micro: a millisecond contains one thousand microseconds. The numerical value increases because the new unit is smaller. Converting both to seconds gives the same interval; using ten instead of one thousand confuses the prefix difference.
