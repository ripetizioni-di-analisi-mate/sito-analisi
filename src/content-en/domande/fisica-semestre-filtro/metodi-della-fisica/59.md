Which angle change always preserves both sine and cosine?

- $\theta\mapsto\dfrac\pi2-\theta$.
- $\theta\mapsto\theta+2\pi$.
- $\theta\mapsto-\theta$.
- $\theta\mapsto\theta+\pi$.

## Explanation

A complete turn returns to the same unit-circle point, preserving both coordinates. A half-turn reverses both signs. Negating the angle preserves cosine but generally reverses sine; the complementary angle exchanges sine and cosine. “Always” excludes coincidences at special angles.
