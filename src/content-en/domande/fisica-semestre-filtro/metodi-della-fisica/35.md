Power is energy transferred per unit time. What are its dimensions?

- $ML^2T^{-3}$.
- $ML^2T^{-2}$.
- $MLT^{-2}$.
- $ML^2T^{-1}$.

## Explanation

Energy dimensions are $ML^2T^{-2}$, and division by time adds another inverse-time factor. The result agrees with watts equal to joules per second. Retaining exponent minus two confuses power with energy; multiplying by time rather than dividing gives exponent minus one.
