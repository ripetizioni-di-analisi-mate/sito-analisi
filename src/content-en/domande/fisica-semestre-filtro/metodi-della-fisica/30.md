Two lengths are $1.2\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m}$ and $1.2\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m}$. By how many powers of ten do they differ?

- $9$.
- $3$.
- $-9$.
- $2$.

## Explanation

Equal coefficients cancel in the ratio, which is one thousand. Exponents differ by three: one length is one thousand times the other. Adding exponent magnitudes is inappropriate because comparison requires a quotient. Both measurements must use the same unit to read this difference directly.
