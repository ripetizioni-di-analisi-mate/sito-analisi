A force has magnitude $5\,\mathrm{N}$. Which Cartesian component pair is incompatible with this datum?

- $(0;-5)\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $(3;4)\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $(5;5)\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $(-3;4)\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

Magnitude is the square root of the summed component squares. Two components both equal to five give $5\sqrt2\,\mathrm{N}$, exceeding the datum. Every other pair has squared sum twenty-five. Sign distinguishes directions but disappears from magnitude after squaring.
