An analysis uses the ratio $\dfrac{2\,\mathrm{cm}}{5\,\mathrm{mm}}$. Which result is correct?

- $0.04$.
- $4$.
- $0.4$.
- $40$.

## Explanation

First express lengths in the same unit: two centimetres are twenty millimetres. The ratio is four and units cancel. Dividing two by five alone would ignore the centimetre-to-millimetre ratio. The result is dimensionless because it compares quantities of the same kind.
