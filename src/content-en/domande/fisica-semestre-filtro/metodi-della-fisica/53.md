For $270^\circ<\theta<360^\circ$, what are the signs of cosine and sine?

- Both negative.
- Both positive.
- Positive cosine, negative sine.
- Negative cosine, positive sine.

## Explanation

In the fourth quadrant, the unit-circle point is right of the vertical axis and below the horizontal axis. Its coordinates are cosine and sine respectively, determining the signs. Remembering only an acute reference angle’s absolute value would lose quadrant information.
