Using the usual significant-figure rule, how should the product of measurements $2.4\,\mathrm{m}$ and $1.326\,\mathrm{m}$ be reported?

- $3.2\,\mathrm{m^2}$.
- $3\,\mathrm{m^2}$.
- $3.18\,\mathrm{m^2}$.
- $3.1824\,\mathrm{m^2}$.

## Explanation

The unrounded product is $3.1824\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The measurement with fewer significant figures has two, so retain three point two. This is the stated practical criterion rather than full uncertainty propagation. Rounding inputs first or keeping every calculator digit does not follow the requested rule.
