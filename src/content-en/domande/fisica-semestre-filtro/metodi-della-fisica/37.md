In the magnitude law $F=cv^2$, with force $F$ and speed $v$, what is the SI unit of $c$?

- $\mathrm{N/m}$.
- $\mathrm{kg/s}$.
- $\mathrm{kg/m}$.
- $\mathrm{kg\,m}$.

## Explanation

Isolate $c=\dfrac F{v^2}$. Dividing newtons by square metres per second squared cancels time factors and leaves kilograms per metre. Kilograms per second would be the coefficient unit for force proportional to velocity alone. The velocity power therefore changes the constant’s unit.
