Only $\vec{a}\cdot\vec{b}=0$ is known. Can a right angle between the vectors always be inferred?

- No: zero dot product always requires parallel vectors.
- Yes, but only if their magnitudes are equal.
- Yes, even if one vector is zero.
- No: both must also be nonzero.

## Explanation

A dot product can vanish because cosine is zero or because at least one magnitude is zero. In the latter case the angle is undefined. For nonzero vectors, zero dot product implies perpendicularity regardless of equal magnitudes. The nonzero assumption is essential to the inference.
