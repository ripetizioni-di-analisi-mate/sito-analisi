Two instruments display the same length, one with two decimal places and the other with five. Which conclusion is justified without further data?

- Both have zero uncertainty if their numbers agree.
- The displayed decimal count is insufficient to establish which is more accurate.
- The first is necessarily more accurate because it rounds.
- The second is necessarily one thousand times more accurate.

## Explanation

Display resolution is only one measurement characteristic. Calibration errors, noise and operating conditions can limit accuracy independently of decimal count. Agreement between readings does not prove absence of shared error. Comparing instruments requires metrological characteristics rather than only the displayed number’s format.
