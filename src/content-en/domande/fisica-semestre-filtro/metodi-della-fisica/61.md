Two displacements have magnitudes $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and $4\,\mathrm{m}$, but unknown angle. What range can the sum magnitude take?

- $7\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $0$ – $7\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $1$ – $7\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

The maximum comes from matching directions, adding magnitudes; the minimum from opposite directions, taking absolute difference. Five requires perpendicularity, which is not stated. The zero vector is impossible because magnitudes differ: even complete opposition cannot cancel them.
