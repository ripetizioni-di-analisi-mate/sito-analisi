What type of result and unit does the cross product of two displacements have?

- An always dimensionless number.
- A vector with unit $\mathrm{m^2}$.
- A vector with unit $\mathrm{m}$.
- A scalar with unit $\mathrm{m^2}$.

## Explanation

The cross product is a vector and multiplies factor units. Two lengths therefore give square metres, consistent with the parallelogram area representing its magnitude. The dot product would instead be scalar while sharing the squared unit in this example. Result type and unit are separate information.
