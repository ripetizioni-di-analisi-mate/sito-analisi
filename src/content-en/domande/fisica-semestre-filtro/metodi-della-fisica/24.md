Which distinction is correct between $3\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ and $(3\,\mathrm{cm})^3$?

- The second volume is twenty-seven times the first.
- The second volume is three times the first.
- The second volume is nine times the first.
- They are equal because both contain cubic centimetres.

## Explanation

In the first expression, three multiplies a volume unit. In the second, parentheses cube both number and unit, giving twenty-seven cubic centimetres. Both are volumes but their ratio is nine. Parenthesis scope is essential mathematical information.
