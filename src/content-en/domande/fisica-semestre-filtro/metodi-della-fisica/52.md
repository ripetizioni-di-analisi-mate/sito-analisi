What is the unit vector in the direction of $\vec{a}=(-3;4)$?

- $\left(\dfrac35;\dfrac45\right)$.
- $(-1;1)$.
- $\left(-\dfrac35;\dfrac45\right)$.
- $\left(-\dfrac3{25};\dfrac4{25}\right)$.

## Explanation

The vector’s magnitude is five. Dividing both components by this positive magnitude preserves direction and signs while giving magnitude one. Dividing by twenty-five incorrectly uses squared magnitude. Minus one and one instead has magnitude square root of two and does not preserve the component ratio.
