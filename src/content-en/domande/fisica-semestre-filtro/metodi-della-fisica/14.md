In right-handed axes, for dimensionless $\vec{a}=(2;0;0)$ and $\vec{b}=(0;3;0)$, what is $\vec{a}\times\vec{b}$?

- $(0;0;6)$.
- $(6;0;0)$.
- $(0;0;-6)$.
- $(0;0;0)$.

## Explanation

The factors are perpendicular, so product magnitude is six. The right-hand rule for $+x$ followed by $+y$ gives $+z$. Negative direction would describe reversed order. A first-axis result would not be perpendicular to both factors; the zero vector would apply to parallel factors.
