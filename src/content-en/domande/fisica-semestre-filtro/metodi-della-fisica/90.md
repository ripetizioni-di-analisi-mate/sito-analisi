Straight motion without reversal, in the positive $x$ direction, covers $6\,\mathrm{m}$ in $2\,\mathrm{s}$ and another $8\,\mathrm{m}$ in $4\,\mathrm{s}$. What is overall average velocity?

- $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $\dfrac34\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $\dfrac73\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

Divide total displacement, fourteen metres, by total time, six seconds. The arithmetic mean of the two velocities, three and two metres per second, would give two point five but would treat segments as equally long in time. The slower segment lasts longer and must carry more weight in the time average.
