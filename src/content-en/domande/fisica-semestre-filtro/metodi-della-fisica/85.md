Velocity increases linearly from zero to $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in $4\,\mathrm{s}$. Which pair gives acceleration and displacement?

- $32\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$; $2\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$; $16\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$; $32\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$; $8\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

Acceleration is slope, eight divided by four, while displacement is the triangle area below the line. The one-half factor is needed because velocity does not remain at its final value. A rectangle of height eight would overestimate displacement by a factor two.
