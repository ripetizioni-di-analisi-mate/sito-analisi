A nonzero constant force does negative work during a nonzero displacement. Which interval contains their angle?

- $90^\circ<\theta\le180^\circ$.
- $\theta=0^\circ$.
- $\theta=90^\circ$.
- $0^\circ\le\theta<90^\circ$.

## Explanation

In work $W=Fs\cos\theta$, both magnitudes are positive. Sign therefore depends on cosine, negative for obtuse angles through opposition. A force can have some positive components and still do negative work: what matters is projection onto the complete displacement direction, not one isolated sign.
