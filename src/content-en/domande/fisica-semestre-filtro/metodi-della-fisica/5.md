What is the normalized scientific notation for $(3.0\cdot10^{-6})(4.0\cdot10^3)$?

- $1.2\cdot10^{-3}$.
- $1.2\cdot10^{-9}$.
- $1.2\cdot10^{-2}$.
- $1.2\cdot10^2$.

## Explanation

Multiply coefficients and add exponents to obtain $12\cdot10^{-3}$. Changing the coefficient from twelve to one point two requires increasing the exponent by one. Keeping the exponent unchanged during normalization would reduce the number tenfold; subtracting exponents is a quotient rule.
