Flow rate is $6.0\,\mathrm{L/min}$. What is its value in $\mathrm{m^3/s}$?

- $1.0\cdot10^{-1}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$.
- $1.0\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$.
- $3.6\cdot10^{-1}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$.
- $6.0\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$.

## Explanation

Convert volume to $6.0\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^3}$ and divide by sixty seconds. Forgetting the minute leaves a factor sixty; multiplying by sixty reverses denominator conversion. Over one minute, correct flow rate times duration must recover the initial six litres.
