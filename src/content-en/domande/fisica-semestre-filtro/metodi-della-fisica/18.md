A measurement file confuses $\mathrm{mW}$ with $\mathrm{MW}$. What is the unit ratio $\dfrac{1\,\mathrm{MW}}{1\,\mathrm{mW}}$?

- $10^9$.
- $10^{-9}$.
- $10^6$.
- $10^3$.

## Explanation

Case changes the prefix: mega is $10^6$ and milli $10^{-3}$. Their ratio is nine powers of ten. Considering only the mega prefix is insufficient because the denominator also differs from a watt. Ratio units cancel, leaving a pure number.
