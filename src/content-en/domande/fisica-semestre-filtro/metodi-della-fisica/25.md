Temperature rises from $20\,{}^\circ\mathrm{C}$ to $25\,{}^\circ\mathrm{C}$. What is the increase in kelvins?

- $278.15\,\mathrm{K}$.
- $45\,\mathrm{K}$.
- $298.15\,\mathrm{K}$.
- $5\,\mathrm{K}$.

## Explanation

Intervals of one degree Celsius and one kelvin have equal size. The offset between scale zeros cancels when initial temperature is subtracted from final temperature. Adding two hundred and seventy-three point one five to the increase would confuse a difference with an absolute temperature. Adding both readings does not give the increase.
