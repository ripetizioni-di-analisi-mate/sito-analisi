A person completes a closed route and returns to the starting point. Which comparison of distance and displacement is correct?

- Displacement is the sum of segment magnitudes.
- Zero distance and positive displacement.
- Distance and displacement magnitude are always equal.
- Zero displacement; positive distance for a nontrivial route.

## Explanation

Displacement depends only on endpoints and vanishes when they coincide. Distance counts all travelled segments and stays positive. The vector sum of segments can vanish while their magnitude sum does not. Distance equals displacement magnitude only for a straight route without reversals.
