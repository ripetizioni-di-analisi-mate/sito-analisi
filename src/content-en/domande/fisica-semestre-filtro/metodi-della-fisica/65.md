Which pair of properties holds for every pair of three-dimensional vectors?

- $\vec{a}\cdot\vec{b}=\vec{b}\cdot\vec{a}$; $\vec{a}\times\vec{b}=\vec{b}\times\vec{a}$.
- $\vec{a}\cdot\vec{b}=-\vec{b}\cdot\vec{a}$; $\vec{a}\times\vec{b}=\vec{b}\times\vec{a}$.
- $\vec{a}\cdot\vec{b}=\vec{b}\cdot\vec{a}$; $\vec{a}\times\vec{b}=-\vec{b}\times\vec{a}$.
- $\vec{a}\cdot\vec{b}=-\vec{b}\cdot\vec{a}$; $\vec{a}\times\vec{b}=-\vec{b}\times\vec{a}$.

## Explanation

The dot product adds ordinary component products and is commutative. In a cross product, orientation of the ordered pair matters: swapping factors reverses direction. When the cross product is zero its opposite coincides with itself, but that does not make the operation generally commutative.
