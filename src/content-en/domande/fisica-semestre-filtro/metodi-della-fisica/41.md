A heterogeneous sample is divided into two equal volumes. What can be inferred about both masses from total mass and volume alone?

- Both densities necessarily equal the overall average.
- The masses are necessarily equal.
- Their mass sum is known, but individual masses are undetermined.
- Overall mean density is the sum of both parts’ densities.

## Explanation

Mass conservation fixes the sum, not its distribution. Equal-volume portions have equal mass only if their mean densities match. Heterogeneity does not guarantee this. Overall density is an average over the sample and does not automatically determine each subvolume’s composition or mass.
