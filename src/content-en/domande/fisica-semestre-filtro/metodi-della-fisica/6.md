What is the order of magnitude of $7.0\cdot10^{-8}\,\mathrm{m}$ using the nearest power on a logarithmic scale?

- $10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $10^{-8}\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $10^{-9}\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

The boundary between successive powers is coefficient $\sqrt{10}$, about three point one six. Seven exceeds it, so choose the next power. Merely reading exponent minus eight would ignore the specified criterion. Order of magnitude is a conventional estimate, not an exact rewriting of the datum.
