How is $1\,\mathrm{mm^{-2}}$ expressed in $\mathrm{m^{-2}}$?

- $10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^{-2}}$.
- $10^3\,\mathrm{m^{-2}}$.
- $10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^{-2}}$.
- $10^6\,\mathrm{m^{-2}}$.

## Explanation

The negative exponent inverts the unit: $(10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m})^{-2}=10^6\,\mathrm{m^{-2}}$. Exponent sign is essential: this converts inverse area rather than area. Multiplying by one square millimetre expressed in square metres checks that the result is one.
