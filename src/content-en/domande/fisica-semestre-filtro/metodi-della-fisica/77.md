A nonzero force’s line of action passes through the chosen reference point. What is torque about that point?

- Zero, because the perpendicular lever arm is zero.
- Equal to $rF$ even when position and force are parallel.
- Always maximal because the force is close to the reference point.
- Nonzero provided the application point differs from the reference point.

## Explanation

Torque depends on line-of-action distance from the reference point, not merely application-point distance. If the line passes through the reference point and $r\ne0$, position and force are parallel or opposite and their cross product vanishes. If $r=0$, torque is also zero because the position vector is zero. A nonzero force can therefore have zero torque about a point.
