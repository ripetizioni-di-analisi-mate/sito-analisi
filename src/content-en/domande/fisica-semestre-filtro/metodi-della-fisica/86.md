A point moves at $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for $2\,\mathrm{s}$, then $-1\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for $3\,\mathrm{s}$. What are displacement and distance?

- $7\,\mathrm{m}$; $7\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $1\,\mathrm{m}$; $1\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $1\,\mathrm{m}$; $7\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $7\,\mathrm{m}$; $1\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

The segments give four metres positively and three negatively. Their signed sum is one metre; magnitude sum is seven metres. Direction reversal makes the quantities different. A general check requires distance to be at least absolute displacement.
