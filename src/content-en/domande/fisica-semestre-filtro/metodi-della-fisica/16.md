Which pair matches an SI base quantity with its base unit?

- Mass — kilogram.
- Force — newton.
- Pressure — pascal.
- Volume — cubic metre.

## Explanation

All pairs match quantities with suitable units, but only mass and kilogram are base. Force, volume and pressure are derived quantities. A special name such as newton or pascal does not make a unit one of the seven base units; the quantity’s definition must be examined.
