Which normalized scientific form preserves $0.00720$ and its three indicated significant figures?

- $72.0\cdot10^{-4}$.
- $7.2\cdot10^{-3}$.
- $7.20\cdot10^3$.
- $7.20\cdot10^{-3}$.

## Explanation

The normalized coefficient must be at least one and below ten. The final zero in seven point two zero retains the indicated precision, whereas original leading zeros only locate the decimal place. Seventy-two times ten to the minus four has equal value but is not normalized; dropping the final zero loses significant-figure information.
