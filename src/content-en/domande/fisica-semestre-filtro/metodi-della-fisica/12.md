For displacements $\vec{a}=(3;4)\,\mathrm{m}$ and $\vec{b}=(-1;2)\,\mathrm{m}$, what is $\vec{a}+\vec{b}$?

- $(2;6)\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $(4;2)\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $(-3;8)\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $(2;2)\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

Add only corresponding components: along the first axis three plus minus one gives two; along the second four plus two gives six. Multiplying components is not vector addition. The pair four and two is instead the difference; the operation’s signs must apply on both axes.
