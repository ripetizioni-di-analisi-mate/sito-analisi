For dimensionless vectors $\vec{a}=(2;1;0)$ and $\vec{b}=(-1;3;0)$ in right-handed axes, what is the $z$ component of $\vec{a}\times\vec{b}$?

- $-5$.
- $-7$.
- $5$.
- $7$.

## Explanation

The required component is $a_xb_y-a_yb_x=2\cdot3-1(-1)=7$. The subtraction sign turns the negative second product positive. Five would come from adding rather than subtracting the cross terms. Minus seven would instead correspond to reversed vector order.
