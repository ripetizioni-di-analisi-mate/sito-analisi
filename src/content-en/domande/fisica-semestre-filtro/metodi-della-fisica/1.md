Which conversion of $250\,\mathrm{mL}$ is correct?

- $0.0250\,\mathrm{L}=2.50\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{m^3}$.
- $0.250\,\mathrm{L}=2.50\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^3}$.
- $250\,\mathrm{L}=0.250\,\mathrm{m^3}$.
- $2.50\,\mathrm{L}=2.50\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^3}$.

## Explanation

A millilitre is one thousandth of a litre; a litre is one thousandth of a cubic metre. Two successive factors of one thousand therefore lead to cubic metres. Checking with $1\,\mathrm{mL}=1\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ gives the same volume. The other pairs consistently convert litres to cubic metres but fail to match the initial amount.
