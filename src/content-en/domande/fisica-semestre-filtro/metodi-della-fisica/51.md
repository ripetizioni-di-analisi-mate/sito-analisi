To reconstruct direction from $\arctan\!\left(\dfrac{a_y}{a_x}\right)$, which precaution is needed?

- Check component signs separately to select the quadrant.
- Always use component absolute values in the ratio.
- Always exchange numerator and denominator to obtain the correct quadrant.
- Always add one hundred and eighty degrees when arctangent is negative.

## Explanation

Reversing both component signs gives the opposite vector without changing the ratio. Principal arctangent cannot recover from that ratio alone which of two half-turn-separated directions is intended. Separate component signs or a two-argument function are needed; zero horizontal component must also be handled correctly. Always adding a half-turn to a negative result would correct the second quadrant but misidentify the fourth; original signs are needed.
