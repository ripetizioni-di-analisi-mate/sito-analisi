Two measurements on a nonlinear position–time graph determine $\dfrac{\Delta x}{\Delta t}$. What does this ratio certainly represent?

- Average acceleration over the interval.
- Average velocity over the measurement interval.
- Instantaneous velocity at every time in the interval.
- Total distance travelled without needing duration.

## Explanation

The ratio is the secant slope between points and measures displacement per duration. It does not generally determine local curve slope or motion within the interval. Curves can share a secant while having different local slopes. Acceleration would require a velocity change rather than a position change.
