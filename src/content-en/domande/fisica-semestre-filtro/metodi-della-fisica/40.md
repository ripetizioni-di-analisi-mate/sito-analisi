Assume time $T=C\ell^\alpha g^\beta$, with dimensionless $C$, length $\ell$ and acceleration $g$. Which exponents are required dimensionally?

- $\alpha=\dfrac12$, $\beta=\dfrac12$.
- $\alpha=1$, $\beta=-1$.
- $\alpha=-\dfrac12$, $\beta=\dfrac12$.
- $\alpha=\dfrac12$, $\beta=-\dfrac12$.

## Explanation

The right side has dimensions $L^{\alpha+\beta}T^{-2\beta}$. Obtaining time requires zero length exponent and time exponent one, determining both values. Coefficient $C$ remains unknown. The assumption that length and acceleration alone suffice belongs to the model, not to dimensional analysis.
