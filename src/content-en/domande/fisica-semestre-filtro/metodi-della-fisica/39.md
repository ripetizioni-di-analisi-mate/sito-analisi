With positive times $t$ and $\tau$, which expression is dimensionally appropriate as an exponential factor?

- $\exp\!\left(-\dfrac t\tau\right)$.
- $\exp(-t\tau)$.
- $\exp\!\left(-\dfrac t{\tau^2}\right)$.
- $\exp(-(t+\tau))$.

## Explanation

An exponential argument must be dimensionless. The ratio of two times meets this condition; product, sum and ratio with a squared time retain dimensions. Consistency makes the expression admissible but does not prove that it describes the phenomenon: a model and the characteristic time’s meaning are still needed.
