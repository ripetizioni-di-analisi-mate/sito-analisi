Which expression in SI base units equals a newton?

- $\mathrm{kg\,m^{-1}\,s^{-2}}$.
- $\mathrm{kg\,m\,s^{-2}}$.
- $\mathrm{kg\,m^2\,s^{-2}}$.
- $\mathrm{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$.

## Explanation

Force has mass-times-acceleration dimensions: the time factor is inverse seconds squared and the spatial factor is one metre. An extra metre gives the energy unit; division by area gives pressure. A single inverse-second factor instead describes momentum.
