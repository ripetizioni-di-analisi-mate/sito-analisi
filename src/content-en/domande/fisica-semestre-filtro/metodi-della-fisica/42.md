Combine $20\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ at $1\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$ with $10\,\mathrm{cm^3}$ at $2\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$, assuming additive volumes. What is final density?

- $1.5\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.
- $3\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.
- $\dfrac23\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.
- $\dfrac43\,\mathrm{g/cm^3}$.

## Explanation

Both masses are twenty grams, giving forty grams in thirty cubic centimetres. Density is the ratio of these totals. The arithmetic density mean would apply to equal volumes, which are absent here. Directly adding densities would confuse an intensive property with an additive quantity.
