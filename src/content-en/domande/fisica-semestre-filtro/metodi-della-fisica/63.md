With $\vec{a}=(2;-1)\,\mathrm{m}$ and $\vec{b}=(-3;4)\,\mathrm{m}$, what is $\vec{a}-\vec{b}$?

- $(5;3)\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $(5;-5)\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $(-1;3)\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $(-5;5)\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

Subtracting the second vector means adding its opposite: both components reverse sign. This gives two plus three and minus one minus four. The opposite result corresponds to reversed subtraction order, while minus one and three is the original vectors’ sum.
