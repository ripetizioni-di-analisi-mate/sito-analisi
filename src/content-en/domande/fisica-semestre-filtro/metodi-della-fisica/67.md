Which operation is physically meaningful without introducing other quantities?

- Add two displacements expressed in compatible length units.
- Add a force and a displacement because both are vectors.
- Add mass and temperature because both are scalars.
- Add a vector and its magnitude as though they were the same type of object.

## Explanation

Both being scalars or vectors does not guarantee additive compatibility. Quantities must be of the same kind with compatible units. Two displacements meet this requirement; force and displacement have different dimensions and meanings. Their products can be meaningful, but simple addition is not.
