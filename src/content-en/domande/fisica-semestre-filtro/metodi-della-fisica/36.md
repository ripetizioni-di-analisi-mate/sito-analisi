In $x(t)=At^2+B$, with length $x$ and time $t$, what dimensions must $A$ and $B$ have?

- $[A]=LT^{-1}$, $[B]=L$.
- $[A]=LT^{-2}$, $[B]=LT^{-2}$.
- $[A]=LT^{-2}$, $[B]=L$.
- $[A]=L$, $[B]=T$.

## Explanation

The term $At^2$ must be a length, so $A$ has length-times-inverse-time-squared dimensions. $B$ must also be a length because it is added to that term. Constants in one formula need not share units: their units depend on the quantities they multiply.
