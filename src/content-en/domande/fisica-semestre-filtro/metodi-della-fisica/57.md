Which interpretation of $\tan90^\circ$ is correct?

- It is zero because cosine is zero.
- It is $\dfrac\pi2$ because converting degrees to radians suffices.
- It is one because sine and cosine are equal.
- It is not a finite real number: denominator cosine is zero.

## Explanation

At ninety degrees sine is one and cosine zero. Defining tangent as their ratio requires a nonzero denominator, so no finite value is assigned there. Magnitudes grow on approaching from either side, but this behaviour does not permit ordinary division by zero.
