A passive capillary has reached its equilibrium rise in a closed reservoir without evaporation or other energy inputs. What does the model predict?

- No persistent flow: capillary force and weight balance.
- Identical pressure at every height in the column.
- Indefinite constant flow from surface tension alone.
- Height increasing linearly with time.

## Explanation

The rise law describes static equilibrium, not a pump capable of supplying work without changes. Once the balancing height is reached, liquid does not keep rising. Persistent transport would require different conditions, such as evaporation or a maintained pressure difference; a hydrostatic gradient still remains.
