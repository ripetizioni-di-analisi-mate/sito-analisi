In a circular pipe with a Poiseuille profile, axial speed is $0.40\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and area is $2.0\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. What is the flow rate?

- $40\,\mathrm{mL/s}$.
- $20\,\mathrm{mL/s}$.
- $400\,\mathrm{mL/s}$.
- $80\,\mathrm{mL/s}$.

## Explanation

For the parabolic profile, $\bar v=\dfrac{v_{\max}}{2}=0.20\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Thus $Q=A\bar v=2.0\cdot10^{-4}\cdot0.20=4.0\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}=40\,\mathrm{mL/s}$. Using axial speed directly would double flow rate by assigning the maximum to the entire section.
