An incompressible Newtonian fluid flows steadily and laminarly through a rigid horizontal cylindrical pipe, with no slip at the walls. Its profile is still changing shape along the pipe. Which assumption of the developed Poiseuille formula is missing?

- Steadiness of the flow.
- No slip at the walls.
- Full profile development.
- A constant circular pipe cross section.

## Explanation

In the entrance region the profile adapts to the walls and changes along the axis. Being laminar is insufficient to establish the developed parabola and full relation $\Delta p=\dfrac{8\eta LQ}{\pi r^4}$ over the chosen section. Entrance effects must be included or a sufficiently developed section selected.
