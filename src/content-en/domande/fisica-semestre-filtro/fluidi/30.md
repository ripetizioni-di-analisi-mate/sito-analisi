A stationary object hanging from a spring balance weighs $6.0\,\mathrm N$ in air and $4.0\,\mathrm N$ fully submerged in water. Neglect air buoyancy and contacts. With $\rho=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is its volume?

- $400\,\mathrm{cm^3}$.
- $20\,\mathrm{cm^3}$.
- $200\,\mathrm{cm^3}$.
- $600\,\mathrm{cm^3}$.

## Explanation

The difference between readings measures buoyancy: $F_A=6.0-4.0=2.0\,\mathrm N$. Thus $V=\dfrac{F_A}{\rho g}=\dfrac{2.0}{10000}=2.0\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^3}=200\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. Using either reading directly would confuse buoyancy with weight or supporting tension.
