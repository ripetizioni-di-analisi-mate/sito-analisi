A vertical capillary in a large reservoir has radius $0.50\,\mathrm{mm}$, zero contact angle, $\gamma=0.050\,\mathrm{N/m}$ and $\rho=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, common gas pressure and negligible meniscus volume, what is the rise?

- $0.20\,\mathrm m$.
- $0.020\,\mathrm m$.
- $0.0020\,\mathrm m$.
- $0.010\,\mathrm m$.

## Explanation

The angle cosine is one: $h=\dfrac{2\gamma}{\rho gr}=\dfrac{0.10}{1000\cdot10\cdot0.00050}=0.020\,\mathrm m$. Capillary force around the circumference balances column weight. Height is positive because the liquid wets the wall; using diameter instead of radius would incorrectly halve the result.
