An incompressible liquid flows steadily through a horizontal pipe of constant cross section, without pumps or turbines and with equal velocity profiles at both ends. The pressure drop is $4.0\,\mathrm{kPa}$ at flow rate $5.0\,\mathrm{mL/s}$. What mechanical power is dissipated?

- $20000\,\mathrm W$.
- $20\,\mathrm W$.
- $800\,\mathrm W$.
- $0.020\,\mathrm W$.

## Explanation

Power is pressure work per unit time: $\mathcal P=\Delta p\,Q=4000\cdot5.0\cdot10^{-6}=0.020\,\mathrm W$. Multiply pressure and flow rather than dividing. Both kilopascal and millilitre conversions are needed; $\mathrm{Pa\,m^3/s}=\mathrm W$.
