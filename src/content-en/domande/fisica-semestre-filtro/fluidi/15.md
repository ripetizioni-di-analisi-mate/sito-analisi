A homogeneous sample has mass $280\,\mathrm g$ and volume $200\,\mathrm{mL}$. What is its density in SI units?

- $1.4\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.
- $1400\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.
- $714\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.
- $140\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.

## Explanation

Conversion gives $m=0.280\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $V=2.00\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^3}$. Thus $\rho=\dfrac{m}{V}=1400\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. The value $1.4$ would be correct in grams per millilitre, not the requested units. The inverse ratio would represent specific volume, not density.
