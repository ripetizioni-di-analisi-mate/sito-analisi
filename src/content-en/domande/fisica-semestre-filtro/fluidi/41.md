A vessel of area $0.50\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ feeds $100$ identical channels, each of area $0.010\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and carrying equal flow. In steady incompressible flow, what is the ratio of mean speed in each channel to that in the vessel?

- $50$.
- $0.50$.
- $2$.
- $0.010$.

## Explanation

Total channel area is $100\cdot0.010=1.0\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. The same total flow crosses the vessel and all channels together, so the speed ratio is $\dfrac{0.50}{1.0}=0.50$. Using one channel’s area alone would ignore flow splitting among all paths.
