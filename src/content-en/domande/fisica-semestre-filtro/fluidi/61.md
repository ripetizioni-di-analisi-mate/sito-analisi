A pipe satisfies Poiseuille’s assumptions and carries $30\,\mathrm{mL/s}$. Viscosity is tripled while geometry and pressure difference remain fixed. What is the new flow rate if the regime remains valid?

- $30\,\mathrm{mL/s}$.
- $90\,\mathrm{mL/s}$.
- $10\,\mathrm{mL/s}$.
- $\dfrac{10}{3}\,\mathrm{mL/s}$.

## Explanation

Resistance is proportional to $\eta$ and triples. With $Q=\dfrac{\Delta p}{R_h}$ and fixed pressure, flow rate is divided by three: $\dfrac{30}{3}=10\,\mathrm{mL/s}$. The pressure constraint is essential: if a pump imposed flow, the required pressure would rise instead.
