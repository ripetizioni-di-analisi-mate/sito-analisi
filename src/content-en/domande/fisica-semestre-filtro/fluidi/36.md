In a pipe the layers remain ordered, but velocity at each point oscillates periodically because of a pump. How is the described flow classified?

- Turbulent solely because it changes with time.
- At rest because velocity periodically returns to its initial value.
- Laminar and necessarily steady.
- Laminar and unsteady.

## Explanation

Ordered layers concern laminar character, while time variation at a fixed point excludes steadiness. Pulsation and turbulence are not synonyms. This distinction prevents applying steady Poiseuille flow to every ordered flow without assessing time-dependent effects.
