An incompressible liquid flows steadily through a conduit without branches or leaks. At one section the radius is half its initial value. How does mean speed change?

- It becomes one quarter.
- It halves.
- It quadruples.
- It doubles.

## Explanation

Flow rate is conserved and $A\bar v=Q$. Since $A=\pi r^2$, the new area is one quarter of the original. Speed must increase by the inverse factor. The argument does not require zero viscosity: it follows from mass conservation under the stated conditions.
