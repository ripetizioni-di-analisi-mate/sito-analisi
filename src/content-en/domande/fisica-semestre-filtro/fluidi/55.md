At fixed temperature, which measurement is compatible with a Newtonian fluid over the explored range?

- Stress is constant while the gradient changes widely.
- Stress is proportional to the square of the gradient.
- Apparent viscosity must always decrease as gradient increases.
- The ratio of shear stress to gradient remains constant.

## Explanation

In the Newtonian model, stress is linearly proportional to gradient, with constant coefficient $\eta$ under the conditions considered. A ratio depending on gradient indicates non-Newtonian behaviour over the measured range. A quadratic relationship remains non-Newtonian even when stress increases regularly with gradient.
