An ideal Pitot tube locally stops steady incompressible flow at the same height. Stagnation minus static pressure is $2000\,\mathrm{Pa}$, with $\rho=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. Neglecting losses, what was the fluid speed?

- $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $1.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $\sqrt2\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

Speed is zero at the stagnation point. Bernoulli along the approaching streamline gives $\Delta p=\dfrac{\rho v^2}{2}$, so $v=\sqrt{\dfrac{2\Delta p}{\rho}}=\sqrt4=2.0\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Stagnation pressure exceeds static pressure because kinetic energy is converted into pressure work.
