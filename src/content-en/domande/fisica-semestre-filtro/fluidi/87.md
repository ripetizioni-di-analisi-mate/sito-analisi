Two liquids have equal surface tension and contact angle in the same capillary; the second has half the first density. In the static model, with a wetting angle other than a right angle, what rise ratio follows?

- $\dfrac{h_2}{h_1}=2$.
- $\dfrac{h_2}{h_1}=\dfrac12$.
- $\dfrac{h_2}{h_1}=1$.
- $\dfrac{h_2}{h_1}=4$.

## Explanation

Capillary force is the same, but each unit height of the second column weighs half as much. Twice the height is therefore needed to balance it: $h\propto\dfrac1\rho$. The conclusion requires equal tension and angle, not automatically guaranteed when changing liquids but explicitly specified here.
