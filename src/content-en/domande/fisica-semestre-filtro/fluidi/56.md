A Newtonian fluid at constant temperature shows stress $4\,\mathrm{Pa}$ for gradient $100\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$. What stress is predicted at $200\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$ in the same regime?

- $4\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
- $8\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
- $2\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
- $16\,\mathrm{Pa}$.

## Explanation

The inferred viscosity is $\eta=\dfrac{4}{100}=0.040\,\mathrm{Pa\,s}$. Keeping it constant gives $\tau=0.040\cdot200=8\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Doubling gradient doubles stress rather than its square. The prediction depends on Newtonian linearity and fixed thermal conditions.
