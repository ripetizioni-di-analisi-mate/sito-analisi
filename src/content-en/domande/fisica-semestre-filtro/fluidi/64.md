For a horizontal linear element, the graph of $\Delta p$ against $Q$ passes through the origin and $Q=4.0\,\mathrm{mL/s}$, $\Delta p=200\,\mathrm{Pa}$. What is its resistance?

- $50\,\mathrm{Pa\,s/m^3}$.
- $5.0\cdot10^7\,\mathrm{Pa\,s/m^3}$.
- $8.0\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{Pa\,s/m^3}$.
- $2.0\cdot10^{-8}\,\mathrm{Pa\,s/m^3}$.

## Explanation

The graph slope is $R_h=\dfrac{\Delta p}{Q}$. Converting $Q=4.0\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$ gives $\dfrac{200}{4.0\cdot10^{-6}}=5.0\cdot10^7\,\mathrm{Pa\,s/m^3}$. The inverse ratio would be conductance with inverse units; linearity allows one point to describe the whole relationship.
