An initially uniform pipe is narrowed to half-radius over only $10\%$ of its length. Treat the sections as Poiseuille resistances in series and neglect local losses. What is $\dfrac{R_2}{R_1}$?

- $16$.
- $2.5$.
- $1.6$.
- $0.4$.

## Explanation

The unchanged section contributes $0.90R_1$. The narrowed section, length fraction $0.10$, has resistance $16\cdot0.10R_1=1.60R_1$. Adding gives $2.50R_1$. The factor of sixteen does not apply to the whole pipe because the geometric change affects only part of it.
