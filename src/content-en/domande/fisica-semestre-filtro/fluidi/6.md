A homogeneous body of density $600\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ floats freely in water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. Neglecting air and surface tension, what fraction of its volume is submerged?

- $\dfrac53$.
- $1$.
- $0.40$.
- $0.60$.

## Explanation

Equilibrium requires $\rho_f gV_{\mathrm{sub}}=\rho_c gV$, so $\dfrac{V_{\mathrm{sub}}}{V}=\dfrac{600}{1000}=0.60$. The emerged fraction is instead $0.40$. Reversing the ratio would give a submerged volume larger than the whole body, inconsistent with the stated floating condition.
