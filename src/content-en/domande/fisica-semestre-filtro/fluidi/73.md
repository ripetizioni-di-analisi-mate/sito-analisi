To slowly create $2.0\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ of new single interface at constant tension $0.030\,\mathrm{N/m}$, what reversible surface work is required?

- $6.0\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm J$.
- $6.0\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm J$.
- $1.2\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm J$.
- $6.0\cdot10^{-2}\,\mathrm J$.

## Explanation

Tension is energy per area: $W=\gamma\Delta A=0.030\cdot2.0\cdot10^{-4}=6.0\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm J$. The specified area concerns one interface, so no factor of two is added. Square-centimetre to square-metre conversion requires $10^{-4}$.
