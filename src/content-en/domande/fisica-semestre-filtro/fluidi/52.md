A Pitot probe measures $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at the centre of a pipe of cross-sectional area $4\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. What additional information is needed to obtain the exact volume flow rate if speed varies across the section?

- The total length of the pipe.
- The outside atmospheric pressure alone.
- The maximum speed by itself.
- The mean normal velocity over the whole cross-section.

## Explanation

Volume flow rate sums the contributions over the entire cross-section, so it is the area times the mean normal velocity. The centre may flow at a different speed from the mean, and that one reading does not determine the product.
