A large reservoir contains water whose surface pressure is $20\,\mathrm{kPa}$ above jet pressure. The outlet is at the surface height in the limiting model, and surface speed is negligible. With $\rho=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and zero losses, what exit speed follows?

- $\sqrt{20}\,\mathrm{m/s}\simeq4.47\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $0\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $40\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $\sqrt{40}\,\mathrm{m/s}\simeq6.32\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

Even without a height difference, pressure difference can provide kinetic energy: $\Delta p=\dfrac{\rho v^2}{2}$. This gives $v=\sqrt{\dfrac{2\cdot20000}{1000}}=\sqrt{40}\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Using only the gravitational form of Torricelli would omit work from the imposed liquid pressure.
