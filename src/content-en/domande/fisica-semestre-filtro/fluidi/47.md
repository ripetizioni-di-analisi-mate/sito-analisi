In a real horizontal section without pumps, initial and final areas and velocity profiles are equal, but mechanical energy is dissipated. At steady flow, what pressure comparison is expected?

- Final pressure is equal because areas are equal.
- Final pressure is higher because speed is unchanged.
- Final pressure is lower.
- Final pressure decreases only if speed also decreases.

## Explanation

Endpoint kinetic and potential energies cancel, but dissipation still has an energy cost. Pressure must therefore decrease along the flow. Equal areas ensure continuity, not mechanical-energy conservation as in the ideal Bernoulli equation.
