In the capillary-rise model, contact angle through the liquid is $90^\circ$. What is the height relative to the reservoir at common gas pressure?

- It cannot be determined without knowing the surface-tension value.
- It is zero.
- It is maximal and positive.
- Its magnitude is maximal and it is negative.

## Explanation

The vertical force component is proportional to $\cos\theta$. At $\theta=90^\circ$, cosine is zero and $h=\dfrac{2\gamma\cos\theta}{\rho gr}=0$. Tension itself need not vanish: its vertical component along the contact line is zero in the model.
