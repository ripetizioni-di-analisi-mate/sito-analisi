At equal radius and tension, how does the pressure jump of a single long cylindrical interface compare with that of a spherical interface?

- It is double.
- It is half.
- It is equal.
- It is one quarter.

## Explanation

In the cylinder one principal curvature is $\dfrac1r$ and the other zero; in the sphere both are $\dfrac1r$. Thus $\Delta p_{\mathrm{cyl}}=\dfrac{\gamma}{r}$ and $\Delta p_{\mathrm{sph}}=\dfrac{2\gamma}{r}$. Both cases involve one interface, not different numbers of surfaces.
