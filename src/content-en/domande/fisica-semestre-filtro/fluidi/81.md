Two single-interface spherical models have $r_2=\dfrac{r_1}{2}$ and $\gamma_2=\dfrac{\gamma_1}{2}$. How do excess pressures compare?

- The second is double.
- They are equal.
- The second is half.
- The second is one quarter.

## Explanation

The ratio is $\dfrac{\Delta p_2}{\Delta p_1}=(\dfrac{\gamma_2}{\gamma_1})(\dfrac{r_1}{r_2})=(\dfrac12)\cdot2=1$. Lower tension compensates for smaller radius. This shows why radius alone is insufficient to compare required pressures in models involving surfactant.
