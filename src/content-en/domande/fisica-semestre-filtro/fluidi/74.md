A flat soap film has two surfaces and pulls perpendicularly on a movable rod $5.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ long. With tension $0.030\,\mathrm{N/m}$ on each surface, what force balances the film?

- $1.5\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm N$.
- $0.30\,\mathrm N$.
- $3.0\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm N$.
- $6.0\cdot10^{-2}\,\mathrm N$.

## Explanation

Each surface contributes $\gamma\ell$ along the rod. Total force is $F=2\gamma\ell=2\cdot0.030\cdot0.050=0.0030\,\mathrm N$. The factor of two counts film surfaces; it does not arise from curvature here because the film is flat. Tension can be non-zero even with zero Laplace jump.
