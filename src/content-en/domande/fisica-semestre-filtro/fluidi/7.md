Over $10\,\mathrm s$, a volume of $100\,\mathrm{mL}$ crosses a section. What is the mean volumetric flow rate in SI units?

- $10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$.
- $10^{-5}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$.
- $10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$.
- $10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$.

## Explanation

The volume is $100\cdot10^{-6}=10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^3}$. Flow rate is $Q=\dfrac{\Delta V}{\Delta t}=\dfrac{10^{-4}}{10}=10^{-5}\,\mathrm{m^3/s}$. This is a time average: it does not establish constant flow throughout the interval and is not a speed, which would require cross-sectional area.
