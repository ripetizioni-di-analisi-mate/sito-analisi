An ideal press with pistons at the same height has areas $5\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and $100\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. A force of $10\,\mathrm N$ is applied to the small piston in addition to common external pressure. Neglecting friction and piston weight, what additional force balances the large piston?

- $200\,\mathrm N$.
- $2000\,\mathrm N$.
- $20\,\mathrm N$.
- $0.50\,\mathrm N$.

## Explanation

Pascal equates pressure increments: $F_2=\dfrac{F_1A_2}{A_1}=\dfrac{10\cdot100}{5}=200\,\mathrm N$. Areas have the same unit, which cancels in the ratio. Force increases because it acts over a larger area; additional pressure stays the same.
