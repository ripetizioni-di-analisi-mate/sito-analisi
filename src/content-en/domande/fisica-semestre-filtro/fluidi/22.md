In an open U-tube, two immiscible liquids form columns above the same interface level. If the first density is twice the second, what relation between equilibrium heights holds, neglecting capillarity?

- $h_1=h_2$.
- $h_1=\dfrac{h_2}{4}$.
- $h_1=\dfrac{h_2}{2}$.
- $h_1=2h_2$.

## Explanation

At the common level pressure is equal and external pressures are equal. Thus $\rho_1gh_1=\rho_2gh_2$, giving $\dfrac{h_1}{h_2}=\dfrac{\rho_2}{\rho_1}=\dfrac12$. Heights are inversely proportional to densities: a denser column needs less height to produce the same pressure.
