A floating platform receives an additional load of $0.20\,\mathrm{kg}$ and remains afloat. In water of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$, neglecting air and surface tension, how much does displaced volume increase?

- $0.020\,\mathrm L$.
- $200\,\mathrm L$.
- $2.0\,\mathrm L$.
- $0.20\,\mathrm L$.

## Explanation

Added weight must be balanced by added buoyancy: $\rho g\Delta V=\Delta m\,g$. Hence $\Delta V=\dfrac{0.20}{1000}=2.0\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^3}=0.20\,\mathrm L$. Gravity cancels. Initial mass is unnecessary because two equilibria of the same platform are compared.
