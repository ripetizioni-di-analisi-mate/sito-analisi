A spherical gas bubble is completely surrounded by liquid, without a soap film. With one interfacial tension $\gamma$ and radius $r$, which formula describes the inside-minus-outside jump in the static model?

- $\dfrac{\gamma}{r}$.
- $\dfrac{\gamma r}{2}$.
- $\dfrac{4\gamma}{r}$.
- $\dfrac{2\gamma}{r}$.

## Explanation

The word “bubble” does not imply two surfaces. Here gas and liquid meet at one spherical interface with two equal curvatures: the jump is $\dfrac{2\gamma}{r}$. A factor of four requires a thin liquid film with gas on both sides and therefore two distinct interfaces.
