Between plates $1.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ apart, a Newtonian fluid has a linear profile from zero to $0.20\,\mathrm{m/s}$. With $\eta=3.0\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{Pa\,s}$, what is the shear-stress magnitude?

- $0.015\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
- $0.00060\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
- $0.60\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
- $6.0\,\mathrm{Pa}$.

## Explanation

The gradient is $\dfrac{\Delta v}{\Delta y}=\dfrac{0.20}{0.0010}=200\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$. The Newtonian law gives $\tau=\eta\left|\dfrac{dv}{dy}\right|=0.0030\cdot200=0.60\,\mathrm{Pa}$. The gradient, not plate speed alone, is required, and distance must be in metres before division.
