Two linear branches with resistances $R$ and $3R$ are in parallel. What fraction of total flow crosses the branch with resistance $R$?

- $\dfrac34$.
- $\dfrac12$.
- $\dfrac13$.
- $\dfrac14$.

## Explanation

At common pressure difference, flows are $Q_1=\dfrac{\Delta p}{R}$ and $Q_2=\dfrac{\Delta p}{3R}=\dfrac{Q_1}{3}$. Thus $\dfrac{Q_1}{Q_1+Q_2}=\dfrac{1}{1+\dfrac13}=\dfrac34$. The less resistive branch carries more flow, not less; splitting is inverse to resistance.
