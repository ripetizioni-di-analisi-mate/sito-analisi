A homogeneous rigid body has the same density as the surrounding incompressible liquid. It is fully submerged, stationary and without contacts. Neglecting other effects, which situation is possible?

- Zero buoyancy because densities are equal.
- Equilibrium only on the container bottom.
- Floating with half its volume above the liquid.
- Translational equilibrium at different depths.

## Explanation

When fully submerged, weight and buoyancy both equal $\rho gV$ and balance. Neither is individually zero. With constant density and a rigid body, the balance is independent of depth. This is neutral translational equilibrium in the model, not an automatic restoring action towards a preferred height.
