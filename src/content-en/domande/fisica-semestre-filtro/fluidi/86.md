At fixed radius, density and tension, capillary rise is half that predicted for zero contact angle measured through the liquid. Assuming $0^\circ\leq\theta\leq90^\circ$, what angle follows?

- $90^\circ$.
- $45^\circ$.
- $60^\circ$.
- $30^\circ$.

## Explanation

The height ratio gives $\dfrac{h}{h_0}=\cos\theta=\dfrac12$. In the specified interval the angle is $60^\circ$. Height is not linearly proportional to angle: cosine enters. The interval and measurement through the liquid make the comparison unambiguous.
