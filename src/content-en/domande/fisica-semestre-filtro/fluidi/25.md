A flat vertical wall is wetted by a liquid at rest and exposed on the other side to the same pressure as the free surface. Which mean gauge pressure is used to calculate the net force magnitude?

- The value at the centre-of-pressure depth, selected before calculation.
- The value at the depth of the wetted area centroid.
- The value at the deepest point of the wetted area.
- The value at the free surface.

## Explanation

Gauge pressure increases linearly with depth. Integrating it over area equals multiplying area by its value at the area centroid: $F=\rho gh_cA$. The centre of pressure is instead the resultant force application point and is generally lower; it does not determine mean pressure.
