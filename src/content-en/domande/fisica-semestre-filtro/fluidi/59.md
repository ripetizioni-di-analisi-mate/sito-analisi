The same incompressible-liquid flow rate crosses two circular sections; the second diameter is half the first. With equal density and viscosity, what ratio of local Reynolds numbers follows using mean speed?

- $\dfrac{\mathrm{Re}_2}{\mathrm{Re}_1}=4$.
- $\dfrac{\mathrm{Re}_2}{\mathrm{Re}_1}=\dfrac12$.
- $\dfrac{\mathrm{Re}_2}{\mathrm{Re}_1}=2$.
- $\dfrac{\mathrm{Re}_2}{\mathrm{Re}_1}=1$.

## Explanation

At fixed flow rate, halving diameter quadruples mean speed by continuity. Reynolds is proportional to $\bar vD$, so the ratio is $4\cdot\dfrac12=2$. Considering diameter alone would give the wrong direction. This comparison does not assign a universal transition threshold to the narrowing.
