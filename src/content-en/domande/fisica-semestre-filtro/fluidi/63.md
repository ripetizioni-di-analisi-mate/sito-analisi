In the Poiseuille model, length doubles and viscosity triples while radius and flow rate are maintained. How must pressure difference change?

- It must become $\dfrac32$ of its initial value.
- It must become $\dfrac23$ of the initial value.
- It must become one sixth of the initial value.
- It must become $6$ times its initial value.

## Explanation

Resistance is $R_h=\dfrac{8\eta L}{\pi r^4}$ and rises by $3\cdot2=6$. At fixed flow rate, $\Delta p=R_hQ$ rises by the same factor. Length and viscosity effects must not be divided: both increase resistance under the model assumptions.
