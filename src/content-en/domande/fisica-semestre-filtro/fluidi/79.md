A single convex interface has positive principal radii $R_1=r$ and $R_2=2r$. At uniform tension $\gamma$, what inside-minus-outside jump follows?

- $\dfrac{3\gamma}{r}$.
- $\dfrac{\gamma}{2r}$.
- $\dfrac{3\gamma}{2r}$.
- $\dfrac{2\gamma}{3r}$.

## Explanation

Add curvatures, not radii: $\Delta p=\gamma(\dfrac1r+\dfrac1{2r})=\dfrac{3\gamma}{2r}$. Taking the reciprocal of the sum of radii would confuse different operations. Positive radii are specified because an interface with opposite-signed curvatures would require an algebraic sum.
