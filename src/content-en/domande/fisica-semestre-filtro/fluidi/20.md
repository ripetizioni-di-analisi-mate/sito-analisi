The graph of gauge pressure versus depth in a homogeneous liquid at rest is a straight line with slope $7.84\,\mathrm{kPa/m}$. With $g=9.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what density follows?

- $7840\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.
- $0.800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.
- $800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.
- $76832\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.

## Explanation

The slope is $\dfrac{dp}{dh}=\rho g$, so $\rho=\dfrac{7840}{9.8}=800\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$. Convert kilopascals into pascals first. The graph intercept concerns the pressure reference, whereas density is determined by slope; multiplying by $g$ again would reverse the relation.
