A normal force of $500\,\mathrm N$ acts uniformly over $25\,\mathrm{cm^2}$. What is the pressure?

- $2.0\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
- $2.0\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
- $20\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
- $2.0\cdot10^4\,\mathrm{Pa}$.

## Explanation

The area is $25\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}=0.0025\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Thus $p=\dfrac{500}{0.0025}=2.0\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$. The area conversion factor is the square of the length factor. Dividing by $25$ without conversion would give newtons per square centimetre, not pascals.
