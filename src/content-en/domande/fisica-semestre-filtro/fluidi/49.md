In steady ideal flow, two points belong to different streamlines. Without further assumptions, may the same Bernoulli constant be imposed?

- Yes, equal height is sufficient.
- No: the general form studied here preserves the constant along each line.
- Yes, different speeds are sufficient.
- Yes, equal density is sufficient.

## Explanation

A derivation along a streamline does not automatically assign the same constant to all streamlines. Additional conditions, such as suitable irrotational flow in a connected region, can permit it, but are not given. Comparing speeds or heights alone does not remove this requirement.
