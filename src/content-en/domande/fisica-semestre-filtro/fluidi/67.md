A resistance $3R$ is in series with the parallel combination of $6R$ and $3R$. All elements are linear. What is the equivalent resistance?

- $2R$.
- $12R$.
- $\dfrac65R$.
- $5R$.

## Explanation

First reduce the parallel part: $R_p=\dfrac{6R\cdot3R}{6R+3R}=2R$. Then add the series element: $R_{\mathrm{eq}}=3R+2R=5R$. Adding all resistances immediately ignores topology; the parallel value alone omits the section carrying all the flow.
