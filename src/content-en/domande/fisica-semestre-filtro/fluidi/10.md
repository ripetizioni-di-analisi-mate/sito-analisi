In the Poiseuille model, a pipe radius is halved at fixed length, viscosity and pressure difference. What is $\dfrac{Q_2}{Q_1}$?

- $\dfrac14$.
- $\dfrac12$.
- $16$.
- $\dfrac1{16}$.

## Explanation

Flow rate is proportional to $r^4$, so $\dfrac{Q_2}{Q_1}=\left(\dfrac12\right)^4=\dfrac1{16}$. Area falls only to one quarter, but the velocity profile produced by the same pressure difference also changes. Pressure is held fixed: the original flow rate must not also be imposed.
