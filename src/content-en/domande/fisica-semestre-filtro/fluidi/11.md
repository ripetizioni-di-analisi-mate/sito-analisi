Two linear hydraulic resistances of $2\cdot10^9$ and $3\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{Pa\,s/m^3}$ are in series without accumulation. What is their equivalent resistance?

- $10^9\,\mathrm{Pa\,s/m^3}$.
- $5\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{Pa\,s/m^3}$.
- $1.2\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{Pa\,s/m^3}$.
- $6\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{Pa\,s/m^3}$.

## Explanation

The same flow crosses both elements and pressure drops add: $\Delta p=(R_1+R_2)Q$. Thus $R_{\mathrm{eq}}=5\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{Pa\,s/m^3}$. Equivalent resistance exceeds either element; the reciprocal formula would instead apply to alternative paths between the same nodes.
