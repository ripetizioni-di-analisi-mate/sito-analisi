A gauge referenced to atmosphere reads $-20\,\mathrm{kPa}$ when atmospheric pressure is $101\,\mathrm{kPa}$. What is the absolute pressure?

- $121\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
- $-20\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
- $20\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
- $81\,\mathrm{kPa}$.

## Explanation

From $p_{\mathrm{rel}}=p_{\mathrm{ass}}-p_{\mathrm{atm}}$ follows $p_{\mathrm{ass}}=101-20=81\,\mathrm{kPa}$. The negative sign describes pressure below atmosphere, not below vacuum. Subtracting the negative reading again would incorrectly reverse the reference.
