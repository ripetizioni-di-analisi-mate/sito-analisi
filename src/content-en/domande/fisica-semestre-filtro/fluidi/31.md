A fully submerged body weighs $5\,\mathrm N$ and receives buoyancy $8\,\mathrm N$. What additional force holds it stationary without other contacts?

- $3\,\mathrm N$ upwards.
- $5\,\mathrm N$ upwards.
- $13\,\mathrm N$ downwards.
- $3\,\mathrm N$ downwards.

## Explanation

Without restraint the resultant is $8-5=3\,\mathrm N$ upwards. An equal opposite force is needed, for example from a string anchored to the bottom. A string pulling from above cannot establish this equilibrium: the tension formula must respect its physical direction.
