An incompressible fluid of density $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ goes from $1$ to $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ along the same horizontal streamline. Flow is steady, inviscid and without pumps or turbines. What is $p_1-p_2$?

- $4000\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
- $2000\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
- $-4000\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
- $8000\,\mathrm{Pa}$.

## Explanation

Bernoulli gives $p_1-p_2=\dfrac12\rho(v_2^2-v_1^2)=500(9-1)=4000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Subtract squared speeds rather than squaring their difference. The positive result means that the faster point has lower static pressure under the specified conditions.
