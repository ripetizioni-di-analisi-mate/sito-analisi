Eight identical spherical drops of radius $r$ merge into one without volume loss and at unchanged surface tension. Neglecting gravity, what is final excess pressure divided by that of each initial drop?

- $2$.
- $\dfrac12$.
- $8$.
- $\dfrac18$.

## Explanation

Volume conservation gives $R^3=8r^3$, hence $R=2r$. For a single spherical interface the jump is inversely proportional to radius, so $\dfrac{\Delta p_f}{\Delta p_i}=\dfrac{r}{R}=\dfrac12$. Drop count determines total volume, not a linear increase in radius.
