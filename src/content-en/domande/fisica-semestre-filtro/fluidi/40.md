At a junction without accumulation, incompressible liquid enters at $12\,\mathrm{mL/s}$. One branch carries $5\,\mathrm{mL/s}$ out and the only other branch is also an outlet. What flow crosses it?

- $6\,\mathrm{mL/s}$.
- $2.4\,\mathrm{mL/s}$.
- $17\,\mathrm{mL/s}$.
- $7\,\mathrm{mL/s}$.

## Explanation

The balance is $Q_{\mathrm{in}}=Q_1+Q_2$, so $Q_2=12-5=7\,\mathrm{mL/s}$. The stated directions determine term signs. Do not automatically divide by the number of branches: splitting depends on their conditions, and one branch flow is already known.
