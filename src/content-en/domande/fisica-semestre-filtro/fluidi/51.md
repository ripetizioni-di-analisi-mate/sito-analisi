The quasi-steady Bernoulli model is used for an incompressible liquid leaving an open vessel, without losses or machinery. Free-surface speed is not negligible. At common external pressure, which relation replaces $v_2=\sqrt{2g\Delta h}$ between surface $1$ and outlet $2$?

- $v_2^2-v_1^2=g\Delta h$.
- $v_2^2+v_1^2=2g\Delta h$.
- $v_2-v_1=2g\Delta h$.
- $v_2^2-v_1^2=2g\Delta h$.

## Explanation

Bernoulli retains both kinetic terms: $\rho g\Delta h=\dfrac{\rho(v_2^2-v_1^2)}{2}$. The classical relation is recovered by neglecting $v_1$. Finding individual speeds also requires continuity with vessel and outlet areas; a term known to be significant cannot be omitted. The relation assumes negligible unsteady contributions: it is not an exact formula for every rapidly draining vessel.
