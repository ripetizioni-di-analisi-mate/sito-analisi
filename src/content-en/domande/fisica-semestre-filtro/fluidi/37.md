A flow meter shows constant mean flow rate while rapid velocity measurements reveal irregular fluctuations and eddies. Which conclusion is justified?

- A constant mean is compatible with turbulent flow.
- Flow is instantaneously steady at every point.
- A constant mean proves laminar flow.
- Fluctuations show that mass is not conserved.

## Explanation

A time average can remain constant even when instantaneous quantities fluctuate. Eddies and irregular fluctuations are compatible with turbulence; they do not violate mass conservation. Statistical stationarity is not absence of instantaneous changes as required by the elementary definition of steady flow.
