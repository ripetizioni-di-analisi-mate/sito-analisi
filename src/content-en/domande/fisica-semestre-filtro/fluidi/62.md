A student inserts diameter instead of radius in Poiseuille’s formula, with all other data correct. How is the calculated flow rate affected?

- It is $4$ times the correct value.
- It is $2$ times the correct value.
- It is $16$ times the correct value.
- It is one sixteenth of the correct value.

## Explanation

The incorrectly inserted length is twice as large and enters to the fourth power: $(2r)^4=16r^4$. The result is therefore sixteen times too large. Units remain formally correct, so a dimensional check alone will not detect this geometric error: the datum’s meaning must be checked.
