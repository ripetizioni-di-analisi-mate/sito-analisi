In water at rest, with $\rho=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, how much does pressure increase when moving down by $0.50\,\mathrm m$?

- $50000\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
- $500\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
- $5000\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
- $1000\,\mathrm{Pa}$.

## Explanation

The hydrostatic law gives $\Delta p=\rho gh=1000\cdot10\cdot0.50=5000\,\mathrm{Pa}$. The question asks for the increase, so initial pressure is not added. The sign is positive because the final point is lower and supports a larger liquid column.
