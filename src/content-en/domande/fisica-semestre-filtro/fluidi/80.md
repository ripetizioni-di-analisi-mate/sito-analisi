A single spherical interface keeps its radius while surface tension halves. What is the final-to-initial excess-pressure ratio?

- $2$.
- $\dfrac12$.
- $\dfrac14$.
- $1$.

## Explanation

At fixed radius, $\Delta p=\dfrac{2\gamma}{r}$ is linear in tension, so the jump halves. The result concerns pressure difference, not necessarily internal absolute pressure, which includes external pressure. The tension change is not squared.
