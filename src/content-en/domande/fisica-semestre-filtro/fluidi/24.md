The top of a manometer column lies along a straight tube $1.00\,\mathrm m$ from the reference level. The tube makes $30^\circ$ with the horizontal. Which height enters $\rho gh$?

- $0.866\,\mathrm m$.
- $1.00\,\mathrm m$.
- $2.00\,\mathrm m$.
- $0.500\,\mathrm m$.

## Explanation

Hydrostatics depends on vertical difference, not distance along the tube. With angle measured from horizontal, $h=L\sin30^\circ=1.00\cdot0.5=0.500\,\mathrm m$. Cosine would give the horizontal projection; using the full length would be correct only for a vertical tube.
