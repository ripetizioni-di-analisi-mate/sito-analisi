A finite-resistance linear branch is added in parallel to a passive network between nodes maintained at constant positive pressure difference. What happens to total flow between those nodes?

- It necessarily stays the same.
- It becomes zero because pressure is shared.
- It decreases.
- It increases.

## Explanation

The new branch adds positive conductance and carries additional flow $\dfrac{\Delta p}{R}$. At imposed pressure, existing branches retain their flows, so the total increases. Shared branch pressure means equal drop, not zero difference between the two nodes.
