The same capillary shows height $h_0>0$ under complete wetting. If only the angle through the liquid changes from $0^\circ$ to $120^\circ$, what height is predicted?

- $-\dfrac{h_0}{2}$.
- $h_0$.
- $-2h_0$.
- $\dfrac{h_0}{2}$.

## Explanation

All other factors remain fixed, so $\dfrac{h}{h_0}=\dfrac{\cos120^\circ}{\cos0^\circ}=-\dfrac12$. The negative sign indicates depression below reservoir level. Taking the absolute value of cosine would lose the information about the direction of level displacement.
