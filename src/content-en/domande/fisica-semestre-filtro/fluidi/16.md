Which observation distinguishes an ordinary fluid from an elastic solid under a small sustained shear stress?

- The fluid must necessarily be compressible.
- The fluid keeps deforming while the stress acts.
- The fluid cannot exert normal forces.
- The fluid always reaches a finite deformation and stops.

## Explanation

Sustained shear stress produces continuous flow in an ordinary fluid. An elastic solid can instead balance it after a finite deformation. Compressibility is a separate property: a nearly incompressible liquid remains a fluid and at rest exerts pressures normal to surfaces.
