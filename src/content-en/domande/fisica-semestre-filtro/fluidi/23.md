In a barometer the product $\rho gh$ is $98\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and gas pressure above the column is $2\,\mathrm{kPa}$. What is atmospheric pressure on the reservoir?

- $100\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
- $49\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
- $96\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
- $98\,\mathrm{kPa}$.

## Explanation

The column measures a difference: $p_{\mathrm{atm}}-p_{\mathrm{top}}=\rho gh$. Thus $p_{\mathrm{atm}}=98+2=100\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Ignoring gas above the column would underestimate atmospheric pressure. Top pressure is added because pressure increases when moving down the column.
