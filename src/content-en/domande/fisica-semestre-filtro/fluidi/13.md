A single convex spherical interface has $\gamma=0.020\,\mathrm{N/m}$ and radius $0.10\,\mathrm{mm}$. At equilibrium, neglecting gravity, what is the internal excess pressure?

- $400\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
- $200\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
- $800\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
- $4\,\mathrm{Pa}$.

## Explanation

A sphere has two equal curvatures on the same interface: $\Delta p=\dfrac{2\gamma}{r}$. Converting $r=10^{-4}\,\mathrm m$ gives $\dfrac{0.040}{10^{-4}}=400\,\mathrm{Pa}$. A factor of four would concern two interfaces of a thin soap film; a factor of one a single cylindrical interface.
