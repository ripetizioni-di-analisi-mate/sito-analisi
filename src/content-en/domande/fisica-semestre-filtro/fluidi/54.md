From $\tau=\eta\left|\dfrac{dv}{dy}\right|$, which SI unit follows for dynamic viscosity?

- $\mathrm{Pa/s}$.
- $\mathrm{m^2/s}$.
- $\mathrm{Pa\,s}$.
- $\mathrm{Pa\,s/m^3}$.

## Explanation

Stress has units of pascals and velocity gradient has units $\dfrac{\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}}{\mathrm m}=\mathrm{s^{-1}}$. Dividing gives $\mathrm{Pa\,s}$. Hydraulic resistance also includes tube geometry and has different units; $\mathrm{m^2/s}$ instead concerns kinematic viscosity.
