A gas flows steadily through a conduit without leaks or branches; its density at the second section is half its value at the first. What relation between volumetric flow rates holds?

- $Q_2=4Q_1$.
- $Q_2=2Q_1$.
- $Q_2=Q_1$.
- $Q_2=\dfrac{Q_1}{2}$.

## Explanation

Mass flow is conserved: $\rho_1Q_1=\rho_2Q_2$. With $\rho_2=\dfrac{\rho_1}{2}$, this gives $Q_2=2Q_1$. The same mass occupies more volume when density decreases. Imposing equal volumetric flow would incorrectly add a constant-density assumption.
