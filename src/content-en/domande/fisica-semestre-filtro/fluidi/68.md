A pump maintains total flow through two identical linear parallel branches. One is closed without changing the other. How does pressure difference between nodes change?

- It stays the same.
- It quadruples.
- It halves.
- It doubles.

## Explanation

Equivalent resistance changes from $\dfrac{R}{2}$ to $R$, so it doubles. Since the pump maintains $Q$, $\Delta p=R_{\mathrm{eq}}Q$ requires pressure to double. The remaining branch carries all the flow; the conclusion would differ if pressure rather than flow were fixed.
