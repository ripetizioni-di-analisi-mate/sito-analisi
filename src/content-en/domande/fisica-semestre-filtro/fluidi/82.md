At an equilibrium concave wetting meniscus, how does liquid pressure just below the interface compare with gas pressure just above?

- Liquid absolute pressure must be zero.
- Pressures must be equal because the fluid is at rest.
- Liquid pressure is higher.
- Liquid pressure is lower.

## Explanation

The concave curvature as viewed from the liquid produces a jump opposite to that of a convex drop. This lower pressure relates to the capillary column height: moving down towards the reservoir increases liquid pressure. Equilibrium does not imply equality across a curved interface.
