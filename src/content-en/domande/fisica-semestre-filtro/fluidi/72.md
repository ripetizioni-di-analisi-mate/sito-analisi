Which statement correctly describes using Poiseuille’s law for blood in vessels?

- It is useful under appropriate conditions, with limitations from cells, pulsations and wall deformability.
- It is exact with fixed viscosity regardless of regime.
- It can never provide qualitative resistance comparisons.
- It is exact for every vessel because blood is a liquid.

## Explanation

The law requires a Newtonian fluid, rigid tube and steady developed laminar flow. Circulation can depart from all these assumptions. The model remains useful for isolating dependencies such as radius effects, provided an ideal comparison is not turned into a universal prediction for a real vessel.
