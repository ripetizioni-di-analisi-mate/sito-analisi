Two identical linear branches, each with resistance $4\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{Pa\,s/m^3}$, connect the same nodes in parallel. What is their equivalent resistance?

- $2\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{Pa\,s/m^3}$.
- $4\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{Pa\,s/m^3}$.
- $8\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{Pa\,s/m^3}$.
- $10^9\,\mathrm{Pa\,s/m^3}$.

## Explanation

The branches share pressure difference and each carries $\dfrac{\Delta p}{R}$. Total flow is $\dfrac{2\Delta p}{R}$, so $R_{\mathrm{eq}}=\dfrac{R}{2}$. Adding an identical path facilitates flow: it does not double resistance as connecting pipes in succession would.
