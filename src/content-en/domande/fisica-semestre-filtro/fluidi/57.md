In the developed profile $v(s)=v_{\max}(1-\dfrac{s^2}{r^2})$, at what distance from the axis does local speed equal the cross-sectional mean?

- $\dfrac{r}{2}$.
- $\dfrac{r}{\sqrt2}$.
- $r$.
- $0$.

## Explanation

For Poiseuille flow, $\bar v=\dfrac{v_{\max}}{2}$. Setting $1-\dfrac{s^2}{r^2}=\dfrac12$ gives $\dfrac{s^2}{r^2}=\dfrac12$, hence $s=\dfrac{r}{\sqrt2}$. The area mean is not speed at half-radius: outer rings have greater area and contribute more to the integral.
