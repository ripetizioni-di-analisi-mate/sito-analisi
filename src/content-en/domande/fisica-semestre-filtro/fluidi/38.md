An incompressible viscous fluid flows steadily in a rigid conduit without branches or leaks. May $A_1\bar v_1=A_2\bar v_2$ be used?

- Yes, because it follows from mass conservation.
- Only if pressure is equal at both sections.
- No, because viscosity consumes volume.
- Only if the speeds are axis maxima.

## Explanation

Viscosity dissipates mechanical energy, not mass. The stated assumptions exclude density changes and accumulation, so volumetric flow rate is equal. Speed in the formula is the cross-sectional mean. Different pressures can be necessary precisely to sustain viscous flow.
