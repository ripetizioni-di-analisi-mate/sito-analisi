A pump supplying work to the fluid lies between two points on the same streamline. What conceptual change is needed relative to Bernoulli without machinery?

- Include the pump energy contribution in the balance.
- Impose the same energy sum at both points anyway.
- Always remove the pressure term.
- Consider pressure alone because a pump makes height and speed irrelevant.

## Explanation

The simple form conserves the mechanical sum without external work or losses. A pump transfers energy to the fluid and can increase that sum, so the balance must be extended. Arbitrarily changing a quantity in the formula is insufficient: added work per unit volume must be identified.
