A soap bubble has a thin film, two interfaces of radius approximately $1.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ and tension $0.025\,\mathrm{N/m}$ each. What is internal gas pressure minus external pressure?

- $100\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
- $50\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
- $25\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
- $200\,\mathrm{Pa}$.

## Explanation

The two spherical pressure jumps add: $\Delta p\simeq\dfrac{4\gamma}{r}=\dfrac{4\cdot0.025}{0.0010}=100\,\mathrm{Pa}$. The approximation uses nearly equal radii, meaning a very thin film. The formula for a single gas bubble in liquid would give half the value and describe a different system.
