A pressure difference is $120\,\mathrm{mmHg}$. With $1\,\mathrm{mmHg}=133.3\,\mathrm{Pa}$, what is its value in pascals?

- $120\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
- $0.900\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
- $159960\,\mathrm{Pa}$.
- $15996\,\mathrm{Pa}$.

## Explanation

Multiply by the conversion factor: $120\cdot133.3=15996\,\mathrm{Pa}\simeq16\,\mathrm{kPa}$. One millimetre of mercury corresponds to many pascals, so the numerical value must increase. Since this is a pressure difference, the absolute or atmospheric reference does not change the conversion.
