A body entirely surrounded by water at rest displaces $10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^3}$ of liquid. With $\rho=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what buoyant force acts on it?

- $10\,\mathrm N$ upwards.
- $100\,\mathrm N$ upwards.
- $1\,\mathrm N$ upwards.
- $10\,\mathrm N$ downwards.

## Explanation

Archimedes gives $F_A=\rho gV=1000\cdot10\cdot10^{-3}=10\,\mathrm N$. Use the density of displaced water. Buoyancy acts upwards because pressure on the lower part predominates; this alone does not determine whether the body rises or sinks, since its weight must also be considered.
