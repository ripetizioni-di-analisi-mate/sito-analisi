A fluid has $\rho=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$ and $\eta=10^{-3}\,\mathrm{Pa\,s}$. In a pipe of diameter $4.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ its mean speed is $0.20\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the Reynolds number?

- $1600$.
- $800$.
- $800000$.
- $400$.

## Explanation

With diameter in metres, $\mathrm{Re}=\dfrac{\rho\bar vD}{\eta}=\dfrac{1000\cdot0.20\cdot0.0040}{0.001}=800$. The number is dimensionless. Using radius would halve the result; leaving diameter in millimetres would multiply it by one thousand. This value alone does not verify all Poiseuille assumptions.
