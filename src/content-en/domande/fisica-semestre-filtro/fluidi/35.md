In steady flow, speed increases along a narrowing. Which statement is correct?

- Fluid mass must accumulate in the narrowing.
- Speed must be equal at all sections.
- A particle can accelerate even though velocity at each fixed point is time-independent.
- Every particle must have zero acceleration.

## Explanation

Steady describes what a stationary sensor observes at one point. A particle moves between points with different velocities and can therefore accelerate. This does not contradict continuity: smaller area and higher speed can maintain equal flow rates without accumulation.
