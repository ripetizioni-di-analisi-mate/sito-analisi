A nucleus follows the exponential model and has already survived three half-lives. What is the probability that it also survives the next half-life?

- $0$.
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
- $\dfrac{1}{16}$.
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$.

## Explanation

The conditional probability is $\dfrac{P(\tau>4T_{1/2})}{P(\tau>3T_{1/2})}=\dfrac{2^{-4}}{2^{-3}}=\dfrac{1}{2}$. The value $\dfrac{1}{16}$ would be the probability, assessed initially, of surviving four half-lives. Previous survival does not move the nucleus closer to a deterministic deadline.
