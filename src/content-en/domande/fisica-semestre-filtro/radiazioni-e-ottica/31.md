A plot of $\ln(\dfrac{I}{I_0})$ against $x$ in centimetres is a straight line of slope $-0.400\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$. What is the half-value thickness?

- $5.76\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $2.50\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $1.73\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $0.277\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

The slope is $-\mu$, so $\mu=0.400\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$ and $x_{1/2}=\dfrac{\ln2}{\mu}\simeq1.73\,\mathrm{cm}$. The value $\dfrac1\mu=2.50\,\mathrm{cm}$ gives transmission $\dfrac1e$, not one half. Using $\dfrac{\ln10}{\mu}$ would instead give the thickness transmitting one tenth.
