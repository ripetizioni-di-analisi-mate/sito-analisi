A photon carries $8.01\cdot10^{-19}\,\mathrm J$. With $1\,\mathrm{eV}=1.602\cdot10^{-19}\,\mathrm J$, what is its energy in electronvolts?

- $5.00\cdot10^{-38}\,\mathrm{eV}$.
- $5.00\,\mathrm{eV}$.
- $12.8\,\mathrm{eV}$.
- $0.200\,\mathrm{eV}$.

## Explanation

To count how many one-electronvolt units the energy contains, divide by $1.602\cdot10^{-19}\,\mathrm J$. Powers of ten cancel, leaving $\dfrac{8.01}{1.602}=5.00$. Multiplication would perform the reverse conversion if the starting value were already in electronvolts.
