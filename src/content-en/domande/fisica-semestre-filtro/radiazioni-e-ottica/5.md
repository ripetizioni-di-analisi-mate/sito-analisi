A material has half-value thickness $0.500\,\mathrm{cm}$ for a monochromatic beam. What thickness transmits $12.5\%$ of initial intensity in the exponential model?

- $4.00\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $1.00\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $1.50\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $0.875\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

$12.5\%$ is $\dfrac{1}{8}=(\dfrac{1}{2})^3$, requiring three half-value thicknesses: $x=3\cdot0.500=1.50\,\mathrm{cm}$. Two thicknesses would give one quarter. Thickness cannot be obtained by linearly scaling the fraction of intensity lost: each layer removes the same fraction of what reaches it.
