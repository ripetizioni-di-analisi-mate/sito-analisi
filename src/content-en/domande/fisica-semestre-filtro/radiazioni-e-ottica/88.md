A real object forms a virtual image through a single thin converging lens. What ray property identifies its position?

- Emerging rays really meet on the opposite side, but the image is called virtual because it is enlarged.
- Actual emerging rays necessarily converge on a screen at that position.
- Rays parallel to the axis before the lens remain parallel afterwards, while only the central ray changes.
- Backward extensions of emerging rays intersect on the object side.

## Explanation

Emerging rays diverge, but their geometry is that of rays coming from the virtual position. The intersection belongs to extensions, not actual paths after the lens. An eye can receive those rays; a screen at the virtual position does not receive a real convergence from them.
