Light crosses a stationary interface without absorption into a higher-index medium. A student inserts the wavelength in the medium into $\dfrac{hc}{\lambda}$ and concludes that each photon gained energy. What corrects this reasoning?

- With $c$, use vacuum wavelength; $h\nu$ remains unchanged.
- Individual energy is determined only by transmitted intensity.
- Energy decreases in the same ratio as phase speed.
- Energy really does increase in proportion to index.

## Explanation

Frequency does not change at a stationary interface. Thus $h\nu$ stays unchanged: phase speed and wavelength decrease together. The formula with numerator $hc$ requires $\lambda_0$; transparency excludes absorption in the model, not energy transport.
