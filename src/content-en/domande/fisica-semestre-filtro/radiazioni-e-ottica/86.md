A thin lens forms a sharp image on a screen of a real object $20.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the lens; the screen is $30.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ away on the opposite side. What is the focal length?

- $25.0\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $50.0\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $60.0\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $12.0\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

Object and image are real, so $p$ and $q$ are positive. Use $f=\dfrac{pq}{p+q}=\dfrac{600}{50}=12.0\,\mathrm{cm}$. Adding or averaging distances does not apply the reciprocal law. The result must be smaller than both $p$ and $q$, as required for this real image.
