Which pair represents two isotopes of the same element?

- ${}^{16}_{8}\mathrm O$ and ${}^{19}_{9}\mathrm F$.
- ${}^{12}_{6}\mathrm C$ and ${}^{14}_{6}\mathrm C$.
- ${}^{23}_{11}\mathrm{Na}$ and ${}^{24}_{12}\mathrm{Mg}$.
- ${}^{14}_{6}\mathrm C$ and ${}^{14}_{7}\mathrm N$.

## Explanation

Isotopes have equal proton number, hence equal $Z$, and different neutron number, hence different $A$. The carbon pair satisfies both conditions. Equal mass number, as in carbon-14 and nitrogen-14, is insufficient because the elements differ.
