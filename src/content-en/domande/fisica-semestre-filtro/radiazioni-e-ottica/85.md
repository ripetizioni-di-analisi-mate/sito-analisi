An optical system images two points as a single peak because their separation is below its resolution limit. The sensor has already sampled the image adequately. What happens if only the digital magnification is doubled?

- The actual angular separation doubles.
- The peak looks wider on the screen and the two points remain unresolved.
- The collected wavelength is halved.
- The objective's numerical aperture doubles.

## Explanation

Digital magnification redistributes the recorded signal over more screen pixels. Resolving the points requires acquiring finer optical information, for example by increasing numerical aperture at the same wavelength.
