A thin converging lens has $f=10.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ and a real object at $p=6.00\,\mathrm{cm}$. With $q>0$ for a real image, which result is correct?

- $q=+3.75\,\mathrm{cm}$, $M=-0.625$.
- $q=-15.0\,\mathrm{cm}$, $M=+2.50$.
- $q=+15.0\,\mathrm{cm}$, $M=-2.50$.
- $q=-15.0\,\mathrm{cm}$, $M=-2.50$.

## Explanation

$q=\dfrac{fp}{p-f}=\dfrac{60}{-4}=-15\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $M=-\dfrac{q}{p}=+2.50$. Negative $q$ places the image on the object side; positive $M$ means upright. The lens is converging, but an object inside its focus produces a virtual image.
