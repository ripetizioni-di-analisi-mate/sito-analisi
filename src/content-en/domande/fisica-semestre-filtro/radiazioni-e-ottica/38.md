An absorbance–concentration calibration becomes curved because increasing concentration causes the solute to associate into a species with a different spectrum. Which simple Beer–Lambert assumption has failed?

- The absorbing species’ effective molar coefficient remains constant.
- Absorbance is defined as a base-ten logarithm.
- Optical path length remains constant.
- The light used to measure absorbance is monochromatic.

## Explanation

Proportionality $\mathcal A=\varepsilon c_s\ell$ with one coefficient requires appropriate, constant composition and absorption properties. If species proportions change, the contribution per total mole changes. The logarithmic definition of absorbance remains valid even when the linear concentration model fails.
