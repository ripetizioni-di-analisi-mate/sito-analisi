A sample absorbs microwaves and warms without direct single-photon ionization. Which interpretation is correct?

- Increased intensity necessarily increased each photon’s energy.
- Energy can be deposited through nonionizing interactions and transferred to thermal motion.
- Heating can only be attributed to conduction from an external body, not absorption.
- Heating necessarily proves that photons exceeded the ionization threshold.

## Explanation

Energy transfer to matter does not always require electron removal. Dielectric interactions and subsequent dissipation can contribute to heating. Nonionizing classification concerns the relevant energetic processes and does not mean intensity is irrelevant to every physical effect.
