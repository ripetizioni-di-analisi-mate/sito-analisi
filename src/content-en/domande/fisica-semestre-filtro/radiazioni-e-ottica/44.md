The nucleus ${}^{18}_{9}\mathrm F$ undergoes an energetically allowed beta-plus decay. Which daughter nucleus accompanies the positron and neutrino?

- ${}^{18}_{10}\mathrm{Ne}$.
- ${}^{18}_{8}\mathrm O$.
- ${}^{14}_{7}\mathrm N$.
- ${}^{17}_{8}\mathrm O$.

## Explanation

Within the nucleus a proton becomes a neutron: $Z$ decreases from $9$ to $8$, while $A=18$ remains unchanged. Nucleon number stays fixed because an existing nucleon is transformed. Increasing $Z$ would describe beta-minus decay; reducing $A$ by four would concern alpha emission.
