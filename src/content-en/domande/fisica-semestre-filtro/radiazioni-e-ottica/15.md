At a point in a plane wave in vacuum, $\mathbf E$ points along $+x$ and $\mathbf B$ along $+y$. In which direction does energy propagate?

- $+y$.
- $-z$.
- $+x$.
- $+z$.

## Explanation

The direction is $\mathbf E\times\mathbf B$: for right-handed Cartesian axes, $\hat x\times\hat y=\hat z$. Reversing the cross-product order changes the sign. Propagation is not along either field: the plane wave is transverse.
