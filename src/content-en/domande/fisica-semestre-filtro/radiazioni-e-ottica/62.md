Two samples have equal activity. Radionuclide B has five times the half-life of A. How many B nuclei are required relative to A nuclei?

- The same number.
- Five times as many.
- One fifth as many.
- Twenty-five times as many.

## Explanation

From $N=\dfrac{\mathscr A T_{1/2}}{\ln2}$, at equal activity population grows in proportion to half-life. Each B nucleus has a smaller decay probability per unit time, so more are needed to maintain the same total rate. The inverse ratio would instead describe activities at equal population.
