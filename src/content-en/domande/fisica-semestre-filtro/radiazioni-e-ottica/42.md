A beam follows $\dfrac{dI}{dx}=-\mu I$ with constant $\mu$. Where intensity is half its initial value, how does $|\dfrac{dI}{dx}|$ compare with its entrance value?

- It is half, while fractional loss per thickness is unchanged.
- It is twice as large because the material has already attenuated the beam.
- It is zero because a half-value thickness has been reached.
- It is equal because $\mu$ is constant.

## Explanation

The slope magnitude is $\mu I$, so it halves with $I$. However, $\dfrac{\left|\dfrac{dI}{dx}\right|}{I}=\mu$ stays constant. A constant coefficient does not imply constant absolute loss: this distinction separates an exponential from a decreasing straight line and explains the ordinary graph’s curvature.
