Radiation in vacuum has $\lambda_0=600\,\mathrm{nm}$. Using $c=3.00\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is its frequency?

- $1.80\cdot10^2\,\mathrm{Hz}$.
- $5.00\cdot10^{11}\,\mathrm{Hz}$.
- $2.00\cdot10^{-15}\,\mathrm{Hz}$.
- $5.00\cdot10^{14}\,\mathrm{Hz}$.

## Explanation

Convert $600\,\mathrm{nm}=6.00\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm m$ and use $\nu=\dfrac{c}{\lambda_0}$. The value $2.00\cdot10^{-15}$ would represent the period in seconds, not frequency. Multiplying speed by wavelength or confusing nano with micro produces the other incorrect orders of magnitude.
