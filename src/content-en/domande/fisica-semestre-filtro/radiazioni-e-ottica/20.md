Which sequence lists the indicated electromagnetic regions in increasing frequency?

- Ultraviolet, visible, infrared, microwave.
- Microwave, visible, infrared, ultraviolet.
- Microwave, infrared, visible, ultraviolet.
- Infrared, microwave, ultraviolet, visible.

## Explanation

Microwaves have lower frequency than infrared, which precedes visible light; ultraviolet follows visible light towards higher frequencies. Vacuum wavelengths would be ordered oppositely because $\lambda_0=\dfrac{c}{\nu}$. Source power does not enter this spectral ordering.
