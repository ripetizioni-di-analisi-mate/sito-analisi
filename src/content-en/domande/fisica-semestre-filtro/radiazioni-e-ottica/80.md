Which situation can permit total internal reflection at sufficiently large incidence angles in a model of two transparent isotropic media?

- The ray arrives from the higher-index medium towards the lower-index medium.
- Frequency increases at the interface while indices remain equal.
- The ray always arrives from the lower-index medium towards the higher-index medium.
- Indices are equal and incidence is nonzero.

## Explanation

A real critical angle below $90^\circ$ requires $\dfrac{n_2}{n_1}<1$, hence $n_1>n_2$. This is necessary but insufficient: incidence must also exceed $\theta_c$. Equal indices provide no such limit; a higher final index always allows a transmitted ray under Snell’s law.
