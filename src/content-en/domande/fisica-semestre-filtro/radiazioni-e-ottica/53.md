After two half-lives, what fraction of initially present nuclei has decayed on average for an isolated radionuclide?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$.
- $1$.
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$.

## Explanation

The remaining fraction is $2^{-2}=\dfrac{1}{4}$. However, the question asks for its complement, $1-\dfrac{1}{4}=\dfrac{3}{4}$. Subtracting half the initial population twice would incorrectly give one: the second halving applies only to the half remaining after the first.
