In coordinates $(x,y)$ in centimetres, a thin converging lens is at $x=0$ with rear focus $(10,0)$. A ray initially parallel to the axis reaches the lens at $(0,2)$. Which point does it cross at $x=30$ after the lens in the paraxial model?

- $(30,-4)$.
- $(30,4)$.
- $(30,0)$.
- $(30,2)$.

## Explanation

The ray emerges along the line joining $(0,2)$ to focus $(10,0)$: $y=2-\dfrac{x}{5}$. At $x=30$, $y=-4$. Keeping it horizontal ignores refraction; keeping it on the axis after the focus confuses a crossing with a nonexistent further deflection.
