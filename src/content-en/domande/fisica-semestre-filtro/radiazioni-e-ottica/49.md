In a nuclear transformation $A$ remains unchanged and $Z$ decreases by one. Which listed emission is compatible with this change?

- Beta minus, accompanied by an antineutrino.
- Beta plus, accompanied by a neutrino.
- Gamma only.
- Alpha.

## Explanation

Beta-plus decay converts a nuclear proton into a neutron, reducing $Z$ without changing $A$ when energetically allowed. Beta-minus decay changes $Z$ oppositely; alpha and gamma emissions have other balances. Electron capture can also produce this change but is not among the options.
