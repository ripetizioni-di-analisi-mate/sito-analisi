A sinusoidal plane wave in vacuum keeps the same frequency, but its electric amplitude changes from $E_0$ to $3E_0$. What is $\dfrac{I^{\prime}}{I}$?

- $1$; frequency is unchanged.
- $9$.
- $\sqrt3$.
- $3$.

## Explanation

Average intensity is proportional to $E_0^2$ under the stated conditions, so $\dfrac{I^{\prime}}{I}=3^2=9$. A factor of three incorrectly treats intensity as linear in amplitude. Unchanged frequency fixes photon energy, not the total energy transported per unit time.
