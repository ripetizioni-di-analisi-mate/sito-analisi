A ray goes from higher to lower index. At the exact critical angle, what limiting direction does the refracted ray have in the geometrical model?

- It has an angle above $90^\circ$ found by continuing the arcsine.
- It is perpendicular to the surface, at $0^\circ$ to the normal.
- It has the same angle as the incident ray.
- It grazes the surface, at $90^\circ$ to the normal.

## Explanation

The critical-angle definition imposes $n_1\sin\theta_c=n_2$, hence $\sin\theta_t=1$. The limiting direction runs along the interface. For strictly larger incidences, the required value exceeds one and no propagating transmitted ray exists; the arcsine is not extended beyond its real domain.
