What mean fraction of an isolated radionuclide remains after half of its half-life?

- $0.250$.
- $0.500$.
- $0.750$.
- $\dfrac1{\sqrt2}\simeq0.707$.

## Explanation

The fraction is $2^{-\dfrac{t}{T_{1/2}}}=2^{-\dfrac{1}{2}}$. The value $0.750$ comes from linear interpolation between one and one half, but decay is exponential. As a check, two identical half-half-life intervals must multiply the fraction by itself and give $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
