Many identical samples of one hundred independent nuclei are prepared and one half-life elapses. Which prediction is correct?

- Every sample must have exactly fifty survivors.
- Each nucleus has completed half its predetermined lifetime.
- Mean survivors approach fifty, with differing values in individual samples.
- A sample with forty-eight survivors alone violates the exponential law.

## Explanation

Half-life determines a one-half survival probability, not an exact count imposed on each sample. The sum of one hundred random events has mean fifty and fluctuates. Observing forty-eight alone does not refute the model; the dataset requires statistical assessment.
