Against the same reference, sample and blank give absorbances $0.700$ and $0.100$. Assuming independent additive contributions, what blank-relative transmittance corresponds to the solute alone?

- $10^{-0.700}\simeq0.200$.
- $10^{-0.800}\simeq0.158$.
- $10^{-0.600}\simeq0.251$.
- $0.600$.

## Explanation

Subtract absorbances to obtain $\mathcal A_s=0.600$, then convert back using $10^{-\mathcal A_s}$. Do not subtract transmittances directly: dividing sample and blank transmitted powers corresponds to subtracting their logarithms. Using $0.700$ would ignore the blank contribution.
