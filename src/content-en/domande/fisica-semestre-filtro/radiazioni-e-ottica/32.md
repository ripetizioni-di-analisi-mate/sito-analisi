A linear coefficient is $\mu=2.00\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$ and thickness is $x=5.00\,\mathrm{mm}$. What is the transmittance?

- $1-e^{-1}\simeq0.632$.
- $e^{-10}\simeq0.0000454$.
- $e^{-1}\simeq0.368$.
- $e^{-0.01}\simeq0.990$.

## Explanation

The exponent must be dimensionless: $5.00\,\mathrm{mm}=0.500\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $\mu x=1$. Transmittance is $e^{-1}$; its complement $1-e^{-1}$ is the fraction removed from the direct beam. Using the number five without matching units incorrectly multiplies the exponent by ten.
