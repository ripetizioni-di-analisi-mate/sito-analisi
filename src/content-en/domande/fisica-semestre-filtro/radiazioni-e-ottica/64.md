A detector records $50.0\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$ with a source and $10.0\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$ background. Each decay emits one channel photon and overall efficiency is $0.200$. What is the activity estimate with no dead time?

- $40.0\,\mathrm{Bq}$.
- $8.00\,\mathrm{Bq}$.
- $200\,\mathrm{Bq}$.
- $250\,\mathrm{Bq}$.

## Explanation

Net rate is $50-10=40\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$. Since only $20\%$ of emissions are recorded, estimated activity is $\dfrac{40}{0.200}=200\,\mathrm{Bq}$. Ignoring background gives $250$; multiplying by efficiency further reduces an already incomplete count instead of reconstructing the total.
