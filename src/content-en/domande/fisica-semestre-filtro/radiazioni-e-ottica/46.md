A nucleus with $A=226$ and $Z=88$ emits an alpha particle. How many neutrons does the daughter contain?

- $136$.
- $140$.
- $138$.
- $134$.

## Explanation

The daughter has $A^{\prime}=222$ and $Z^{\prime}=86$, so $N_n^{\prime}=222-86=136$. A direct check starts from $226-88=138$ initial neutrons and subtracts the two carried by the alpha particle. Subtracting four neutrons would confuse all emitted nucleons with neutrons alone.
