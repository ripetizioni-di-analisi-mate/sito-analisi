Two radionuclides initially have equal populations, but $T_B=4T_A$. Which initial activity ratio is correct?

- $\dfrac{\mathscr A_B}{\mathscr A_A}=4$.
- $\dfrac{\mathscr A_B}{\mathscr A_A}=\dfrac{1}{4}$.
- $\dfrac{\mathscr A_B}{\mathscr A_A}=\dfrac{1}{16}$.
- $\dfrac{\mathscr A_B}{\mathscr A_A}=1$.

## Explanation

At equal population, $\mathscr A=\dfrac{N\ln2}{T_{1/2}}$ is inversely proportional to half-life. Radionuclide B decays more slowly and has fourfold smaller initial activity. The ratio is not squared: activity is linear in decay constant, unlike intensity versus field amplitude.
