A source has virtually constant activity, and detector efficiency and background remain constant. When measurement duration doubles, which prediction correctly concerns net counts?

- Every observed count must double exactly.
- Mean count stays equal because activity is constant.
- Mean net count doubles, but an individual measurement need not be exactly doubled.
- Source activity doubles because collection time increases.

## Explanation

A constant rate multiplied by twice the time gives twice the expected count. Activity is a property of the sample at that instant and does not grow because observation lasts longer. Actual counts are random: doubling relates mean values, not every realization.
