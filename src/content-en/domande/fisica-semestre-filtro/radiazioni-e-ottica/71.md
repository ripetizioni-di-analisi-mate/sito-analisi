An ionization threshold is $8.00\,\mathrm{eV}$. Using $hc\simeq1240\,\mathrm{eV\,nm}$, what vacuum-wavelength condition energetically permits a single-photon process?

- $\lambda_0\le9920\,\mathrm{nm}$.
- $\lambda_0\ge155\,\mathrm{nm}$.
- $\lambda_0\ge9920\,\mathrm{nm}$.
- $\lambda_0\le155\,\mathrm{nm}$.

## Explanation

The condition is $\dfrac{hc}{\lambda_0}\ge8.00\,\mathrm{eV}$, hence $\lambda_0\le\dfrac{1240}{8}=155\,\mathrm{nm}$. The inequality reverses relative to energy because energy and wavelength are inversely proportional. Energetic threshold access does not guarantee absorption of every photon; it is a necessary condition for the considered process.
