An ideal neutral filter reduces the power of a red laser without changing transmitted-photon frequency. Which property certainly remains unchanged?

- The total number of transmitted photons each second.
- Intensity, if beam area stays the same.
- Power, because colour is unchanged.
- The spectral region and individual energy of transmitted photons.

## Explanation

Spectral region depends on frequency, as does individual energy $h\nu$. Lower power at fixed frequency implies a smaller photon-number rate. If area is unchanged, intensity also falls: colour and the beam’s energy brightness describe different properties.
