A solution follows Beer–Lambert and transmits $\mathcal T=0.400$ in a cuvette. Optical path is doubled at fixed concentration. What new transmittance is predicted?

- $0.160$.
- $0.800$.
- $0.200$.
- $0.600$.

## Explanation

Absorbance doubles: $\mathcal A^{\prime}=2\mathcal A$. Therefore $\mathcal T^{\prime}=10^{-2\mathcal A}=\mathcal T^2=0.160$. Simply halving transmittance confuses exponential dependence with inverse proportionality. The absorbing species is assumed unchanged and the same molar coefficient remains valid.
