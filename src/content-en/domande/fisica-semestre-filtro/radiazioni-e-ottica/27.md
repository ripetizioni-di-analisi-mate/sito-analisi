A steady emitter produces $2.00\cdot10^{15}$ photons per second. How many does it emit in $2.00\,\mathrm{min}$?

- $4.00\cdot10^{15}$.
- $1.67\cdot10^{13}$.
- $2.40\cdot10^{17}$.
- $1.20\cdot10^{17}$.

## Explanation

Duration is $120\,\mathrm s$, so $N_\gamma=\dot N_\gamma\Delta t=2.40\cdot10^{17}$. Count grows with time: dividing by duration would contradict this dependence. Multiplying only by two incorrectly keeps minutes in a calculation using a per-second rate.
