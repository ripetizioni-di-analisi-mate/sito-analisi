A monochromatic beam must be reduced to $10.0\%$ of initial intensity with $\mu=0.500\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$. What thickness is required?

- $2.00\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $1.39\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $4.61\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $0.211\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

Solve $e^{-\mu x}=0.100$, giving $x=-\dfrac{\ln(0.100)}{\mu}\simeq4.61\,\mathrm{cm}$. Using $0.900$ inside the logarithm would require ninety-percent transmission rather than removal. $\dfrac{\ln2}{\mu}$ instead gives the half-value thickness, insufficient for this attenuation.
