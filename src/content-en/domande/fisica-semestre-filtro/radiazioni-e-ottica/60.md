A model distinguishes nuclear decay with half-life $6.00\,\mathrm h$ and independent physical removal with half-life $3.00\,\mathrm h$. Both remove nuclei exponentially from the observed region. What half-life does the amount present in that region have?

- $9.00\,\mathrm h$.
- $6.00\,\mathrm h$.
- $4.50\,\mathrm h$.
- $2.00\,\mathrm h$.

## Explanation

In the stated model, retention probabilities multiply: $e^{-\lambda_dt}e^{-\lambda_bt}=e^{-(\lambda_d+\lambda_b)t}$. Thus $\dfrac{1}{T_{\mathrm{eff}}}=\dfrac{1}{6}+\dfrac{1}{3}=\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{h^{-1}}$. Nuclear decay itself does not accelerate: the observed amount decreases through an additional independent process.
