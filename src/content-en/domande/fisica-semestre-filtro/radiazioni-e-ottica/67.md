A $500\,\mathrm{Bq}$ source emits one photon of a particular line in $30.0\%$ of decays and none of that line otherwise. How many line photons does it emit on average per second before detection?

- $150\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$.
- $1.67\cdot10^3\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$.
- $350\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$.
- $500\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$.

## Explanation

Multiply decay rate by channel probability: $\dot N_\gamma=b\mathscr A=0.300\cdot500=150\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$. Dividing by $b$ would infer activity from emission, not perform the requested forward calculation. A detector would record fewer still if its efficiency were below one.
