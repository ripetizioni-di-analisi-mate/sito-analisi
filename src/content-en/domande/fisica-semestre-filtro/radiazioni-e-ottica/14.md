A thin converging lens in air has $f=12.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ and a real object at $p=18.0\,\mathrm{cm}$. Taking $q>0$ on the opposite side from the object, which pair $(q,M)$ is correct in the paraxial model?

- $(-36.0\,\mathrm{cm},2.00)$.
- $(36.0\,\mathrm{cm},2.00)$.
- $(7.20\,\mathrm{cm},-0.400)$.
- $(36.0\,\mathrm{cm},-2.00)$.

## Explanation

The conjugate-point equation gives $q=\dfrac{fp}{p-f}=36.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $M=-\dfrac{q}{p}=-2.00$. An object between $f$ and $2f$ produces a real, inverted, enlarged image. Adding reciprocals instead of subtracting gives $7.20\,\mathrm{cm}$; dropping the magnification sign gives an inconsistent orientation.
