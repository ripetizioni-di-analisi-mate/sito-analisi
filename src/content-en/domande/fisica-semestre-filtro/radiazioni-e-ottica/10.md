A sample contains $3.60\cdot10^6$ nuclei with half-life $10.0\,\mathrm{min}$. Using $\ln2\simeq0.693$, what is its activity?

- $2.49\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{Bq}$.
- $6.00\cdot10^3\,\mathrm{Bq}$.
- $4.16\cdot10^3\,\mathrm{Bq}$.
- $8.66\cdot10^3\,\mathrm{Bq}$.

## Explanation

Use $\mathscr A=\dfrac{N\ln2}{T_{1/2}}$ with $T_{1/2}=600\,\mathrm s$: the unrounded result is $4158\,\mathrm{Bq}$. Using ten without converting minutes gives a per-minute rate incorrectly labelled as becquerels. Omitting or inverting $\ln2$ confuses half-life with the exponential time constant.
