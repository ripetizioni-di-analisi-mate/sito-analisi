Light enters stationary transparent glass perpendicularly from air. Which combination describes the transmitted ray?

- Unchanged direction, lower speed and unchanged frequency.
- Direction bent towards the surface, lower speed and unchanged frequency.
- Unchanged direction, unchanged speed and lower frequency.
- Unchanged direction, lower speed and higher frequency.

## Explanation

At normal incidence Snell gives $\theta_t=0$, so no bending occurs. The higher index still reduces phase speed and wavelength. Frequency remains the source frequency: no directional change does not imply that all other wave properties remain unchanged.
