An energetic alpha particle crosses matter and ionizes atoms along its path. Which description avoids misapplying the photon model?

- It is a photon with frequency determined by $E=\dfrac{hc}{\lambda_0}$.
- It cannot ionize because only X rays and gamma rays cause ionization.
- It transfers energy through interactions of a massive charged particle with matter.
- The particle must deposit all its energy in a single ionization.

## Explanation

An alpha particle is a charged helium nucleus, not a photon. It can transfer part of its kinetic energy to material electrons through electromagnetic interactions, producing successive ionizations. This process does not require a nuclear transformation of the alpha particle for every atom ionized.
