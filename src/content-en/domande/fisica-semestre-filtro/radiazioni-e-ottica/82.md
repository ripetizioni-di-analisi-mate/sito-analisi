An ideal fibre has a fixed core index. Its cladding index increases while remaining below the core index. What happens to the critical angle measured from the normal?

- All total internal reflection immediately becomes impossible even though the indices remain different.
- It increases, and some previously totally reflected rays may no longer be so.
- It decreases, making every ray totally reflected.
- It stays equal because it depends only on core index.

## Explanation

$\theta_c=\arcsin(\dfrac{n_2}{n_1})$ grows when $n_2$ increases at fixed $n_1$. A ray with unchanged incidence therefore has more difficulty remaining above threshold. Total internal reflection depends on index contrast, not the core material alone.
