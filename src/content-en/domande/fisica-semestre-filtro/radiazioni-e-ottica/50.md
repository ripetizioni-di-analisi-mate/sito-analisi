A student writes only $n\to p+e^-$ as a complete description of beta-minus decay. Which product must be added?

- A positron in place of the electron.
- A mandatory gamma photon in every decay.
- An electron antineutrino.
- An electron neutrino rather than an antineutrino.

## Explanation

Beta-minus decay emits an electron antineutrino in addition to the electron: $n\to p+e^-+\bar\nu_e$. This product contributes to energy, momentum and other conservation balances. The positron and electron neutrino belong to beta-plus decay; gamma emission can accompany some transformations but is not mandatory in every beta-minus decay.
