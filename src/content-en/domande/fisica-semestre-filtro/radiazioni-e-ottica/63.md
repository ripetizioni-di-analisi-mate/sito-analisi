Two sources have activity $1000\,\mathrm{Bq}$. What additional information is needed to compare their emitted radiative powers?

- None: equal activities always imply equal powers.
- Only the mass of a target that might be irradiated.
- Mean energy emitted per decay in the channels considered.
- Only the nuclei’s atomic numbers.

## Explanation

Activity gives decays per second; multiplying it by mean emitted energy per decay gives power. That mean depends on channel energies and probabilities. Target mass may be needed for dose, not for energy emitted by the source each second.
