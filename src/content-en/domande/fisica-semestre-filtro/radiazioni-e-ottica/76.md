Two exposures deposit equal mean energy per kilogram but have different radiation types and spatial distributions. What follows from these data alone?

- They must produce identical effects in every part of the material.
- Average absorbed dose is equal; an identical biological effect is not determined.
- They must consist of photons with equal energy.
- They must have equal source activity.

## Explanation

The definition $D=\dfrac{E_{\mathrm{ass}}}{m}$ fixes the average energy budget. It does not identify individual energy, decay number, microscopic distribution or deposition timing. These can matter for effects; the supplied data therefore establish equal average dose, not the additional proposed equivalences.
