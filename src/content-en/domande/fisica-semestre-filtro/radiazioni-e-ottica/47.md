A chain contains one alpha and two beta-minus decays. What relates initial and final nuclei, considering only these changes?

- Final $Z$ smaller by four and unchanged $A$.
- Equal $Z$, but final $A$ smaller by four.
- Final $Z$ greater by two and $A$ smaller by four.
- Equal $Z$ and $A$.

## Explanation

Alpha emission changes $(A,Z)$ by $(-4,-2)$; each beta-minus decay changes them by $(0,+1)$. The total is $(-4,0)$. The final nucleus is the same chemical element but a different isotope. Adding only beta changes would ignore the two protons removed by the alpha particle.
