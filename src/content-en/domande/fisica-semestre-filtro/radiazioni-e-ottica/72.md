Photons all below a target’s threshold illuminate a surface. When power increases at fixed frequency, what remains true in the single-photon model with unchanged target state?

- Wavelength automatically decreases until the threshold is exceeded.
- Each photon’s energy grows in proportion to power.
- Absence of ionization necessarily implies absence of heating.
- More photons are available, but each stays below threshold.

## Explanation

At fixed frequency, $h\nu$ stays constant while photon rate grows. The model excludes combining several photons in one event and changes in initial state. Other absorption mechanisms can still deposit energy and cause heating: ionization and thermal transfer are not synonyms.
