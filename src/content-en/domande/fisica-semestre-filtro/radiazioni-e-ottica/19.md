A plane wave in vacuum has $B_0=2.00\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm T$. Using $c=3.00\cdot10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$, which electric amplitude is consistent?

- $6.67\cdot10^{-16}\,\mathrm{V/m}$.
- $60.0\,\mathrm{V/m}$.
- $0.0600\,\mathrm{V/m}$.
- $1.50\cdot10^{15}\,\mathrm{V/m}$.

## Explanation

Use $E_0=cB_0$: the product is $60.0\,\mathrm{V/m}$. Dividing the magnetic field by $c$ reverses the relation; dividing $c$ by magnetic field does not even have electric-field dimensions. The large numerical field ratio reflects their different physical units.
