A converging lens in air has focal length $20.0\,\mathrm{cm}$. What is its optical power?

- $+20.0\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$.
- $+5.00\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$.
- $-5.00\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$.
- $+0.0500\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$.

## Explanation

Convert focal length to metres, $f=0.200\,\mathrm m$, then calculate $\mathcal D=\dfrac1f=+5.00\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$. The sign is positive for a converging lens under the adopted convention. The value $0.0500$ would be the reciprocal in inverse centimetres, not the numerical value in dioptres.
