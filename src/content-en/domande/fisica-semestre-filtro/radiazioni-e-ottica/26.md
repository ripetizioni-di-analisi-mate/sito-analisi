Two monochromatic sources have equal power, with vacuum wavelengths $\lambda_B=2\lambda_A$. What is the photon-rate ratio $\dfrac{\dot N_B}{\dot N_A}$?

- $4$.
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
- $1$.
- $2$.

## Explanation

Photon B carries half the energy of photon A because $E_\gamma\propto\dfrac1{\lambda_0}$. Maintaining equal power therefore requires two B photons for each A photon: $\dot N=\dfrac P{E_\gamma}$. Do not confuse the number-rate ratio with the energy ratio, which is its reciprocal.
