A beam contains $10^{12}$ photons per second at $2.00\,\mathrm{eV}$ and $2\cdot10^{12}$ per second at $4.00\,\mathrm{eV}$. Using $1\,\mathrm{eV}=1.60\cdot10^{-19}\,\mathrm J$, what power does it carry?

- $0.960\,\mathrm{\mu W}$.
- $2.88\,\mathrm{\mu W}$.
- $1.44\,\mathrm{\mu W}$.
- $1.60\,\mathrm{\mu W}$.

## Explanation

Add component powers: $(10^{12}\cdot2+2\cdot10^{12}\cdot4)\,\mathrm{eV/s}=10^{13}\,\mathrm{eV/s}$. Conversion gives $1.60\,\mathrm{\mu W}$. The arithmetic energy mean, $3\,\mathrm{eV}$, is not the mean per photon because the counts are unequal.
