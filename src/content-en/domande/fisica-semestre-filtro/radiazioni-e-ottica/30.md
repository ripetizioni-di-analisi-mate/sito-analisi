Only power $P$ and duration $\Delta t$ are known for a monochromatic source. Which additional quantity allows the emitted photon count to be calculated?

- The radiation frequency.
- Only the index of a medium crossed later.
- Only beam area.
- Only the source-to-screen distance.

## Explanation

$P\Delta t$ determines total energy, but the count requires individual energy: $N_\gamma=\dfrac{P\Delta t}{h\nu}$. Area and distance can change the distribution of received energy without identifying photon energy. The medium’s index also does not determine source frequency.
