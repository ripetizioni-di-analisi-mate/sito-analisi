For a sample, $\mathscr A=120\,\mathrm{Bq}$ and $N=24000$ are known. Which pair $(\lambda_d,T_{1/2})$ is consistent?

- Approximately $(200\,\mathrm{s^{-1}},0.00347\,\mathrm s)$.
- Approximately $(0.00500\,\mathrm{min^{-1}},139\,\mathrm s)$.
- Approximately $(0.00500\,\mathrm{s^{-1}},139\,\mathrm s)$.
- $(0.00500\,\mathrm{s^{-1}},200\,\mathrm s)$.

## Explanation

From $\mathscr A=\lambda_dN$, $\lambda_d=\dfrac{120}{24000}=0.00500\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$. Then $T_{1/2}=\dfrac{\ln2}{\lambda_d}\simeq139\,\mathrm s$. The reciprocal $200\,\mathrm s$ is the exponential time constant, not half-life. Per-minute units are inconsistent with the initial becquerel value without conversion.
