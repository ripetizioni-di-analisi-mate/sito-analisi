An excited nucleus emits only a gamma photon and recoils. Which statement correctly distinguishes mass number and internal energy?

- $A$ and $Z$ remain unchanged; nuclear internal energy and rest mass decrease.
- $Z$ decreases by one because the photon carries negative charge.
- $A$ decreases by one because a particle was emitted.
- Rest mass must remain unchanged because $A$ is unchanged.

## Explanation

A gamma photon carries neither nucleons nor electric charge, so counts $A$ and $Z$ remain unchanged. However, the nucleus reaches lower internal energy and lower rest mass. Mass number is an integer count, not the exact nuclear mass expressed in mass units.
