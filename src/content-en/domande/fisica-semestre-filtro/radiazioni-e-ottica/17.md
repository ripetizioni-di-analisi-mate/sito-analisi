A uniform beam keeps total power and frequency constant but is spread over twice the cross-sectional area. What follows, neglecting losses?

- Intensity stays unchanged; energy per photon halves.
- Intensity halves; energy per photon stays unchanged.
- Intensity doubles; energy per photon stays unchanged.
- Intensity and photon energy both halve.

## Explanation

For a uniform beam, $I=\dfrac{P}{S}$, so doubling $S$ at fixed power halves intensity. Individual energy is $h\nu$, and frequency has not changed. Photon arrivals per unit area and time decrease, not the total number emitted each second.
