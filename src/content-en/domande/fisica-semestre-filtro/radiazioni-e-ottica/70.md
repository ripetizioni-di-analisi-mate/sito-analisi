A $0.200\,\mathrm{kg}$ target receives $1.00\,\mathrm{mJ}$ of radiant energy and deposits $40.0\%$ of it. What is the average dose?

- $2.00\,\mathrm{mGy}$.
- $0.0800\,\mathrm{mGy}$.
- $12.5\,\mathrm{mGy}$.
- $5.00\,\mathrm{mGy}$.

## Explanation

Absorbed energy is $0.400\,\mathrm{mJ}$, so $D=\dfrac{0.400\cdot10^{-3}}{0.200}=0.00200\,\mathrm{Gy}$. Using all incident energy gives $5.00\,\mathrm{mGy}$ and ignores the fraction not retained by the target. Multiply energy by the deposited fraction rather than dividing by it.
