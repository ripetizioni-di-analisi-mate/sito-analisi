An incident ray makes $20.0^\circ$ with a plane mirror. What angle does the reflected ray make with the normal?

- $20.0^\circ$.
- $40.0^\circ$.
- $140^\circ$.
- $70.0^\circ$.

## Explanation

The incidence angle from the normal is the complement of the given angle: $90^\circ-20^\circ=70^\circ$. Reflection requires the same normal-relative angle for the reflected ray. The value $140^\circ$ can arise between the two ray arms but is not the requested angle to the normal.
