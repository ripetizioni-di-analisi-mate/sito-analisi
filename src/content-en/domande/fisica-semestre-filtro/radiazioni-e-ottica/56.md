A daughter radionuclide is initially absent but is produced by parent decay. Which statement about its population is correct?

- It can initially increase: the model must include production and decay.
- It must stay zero because $N_0e^{-\lambda_dt}=0$.
- It must increase without limit because each parent decay adds a daughter.
- Its half-life becomes equal to the parent’s by definition.

## Explanation

The simple exponential describes a population without feeding. Here the balance includes production from the parent and loss of the daughter: starting from zero, production can increase population. The daughter’s intrinsic half-life is not redefined by the parent’s presence.
