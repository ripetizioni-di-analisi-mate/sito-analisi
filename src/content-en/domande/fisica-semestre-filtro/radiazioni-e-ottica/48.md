An atom loses an electron from its cloud through ionization. Which comparison with beta-minus decay is correct?

- Ionization leaves nuclear $Z$ unchanged; beta-minus decay increases $Z$ by one.
- Ionization decreases $A$ by one, whereas beta-minus decay does not.
- Both increase $Z$ by one because an electron leaves.
- Beta-minus decay changes only atomic charge without transforming the nucleus.

## Explanation

The electron cloud does not determine proton number. Removing one of its electrons creates an ion of the same element. Beta-minus decay instead involves a nuclear neutron-to-proton transformation emitting an electron and antineutrino: the element changes while nucleon number remains fixed.
