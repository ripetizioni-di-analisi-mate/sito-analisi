A real object point lies exactly at the front focus of an ideal thin converging lens. How do rays from that point emerge in the paraxial model?

- Parallel: no image forms at a finite distance.
- As if from a virtual image at distance $-f$.
- Converging at distance $2f$.
- Converging at the rear focus a distance $f$ away.

## Explanation

With $p=f$, the conjugate-point equation gives $\dfrac1q=0$. This means an image at infinity, or parallel emerging rays, not $q=0$. The rear focus collects initially parallel rays; here the reverse arrangement places the object point at the front focus.
