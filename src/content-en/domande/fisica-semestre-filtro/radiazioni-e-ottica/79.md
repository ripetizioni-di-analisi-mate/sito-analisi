A ray passes from air approximated as $n_1=1$ into a transparent medium. Measured angles are $\theta_i=60.0^\circ$ and $\theta_t=30.0^\circ$, both from the normal. What is the second medium’s index?

- $\dfrac1{\sqrt3}\simeq0.577$.
- $2.00$.
- $0.500$.
- $\sqrt3\simeq1.73$.

## Explanation

Solve $n_2=\dfrac{n_1\sin\theta_i}{\sin\theta_t}=\dfrac{\sin60^\circ}{\sin30^\circ}=\sqrt3$. Do not divide one angle directly by the other: Snell relates their sines. An index greater than one agrees with bending towards the normal.
