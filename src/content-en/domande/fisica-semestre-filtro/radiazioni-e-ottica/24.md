An apparatus separates alpha, beta-minus and gamma emissions using a magnetic field. Which association of emission with physical nature is correct?

- Alpha: helium nucleus; beta minus: electron; gamma: photon.
- Alpha: helium nucleus; beta minus: photon; gamma: electron.
- Alpha: helium nucleus; beta minus: positron; gamma: photon.
- Alpha: neutral helium atom; beta minus: electron; gamma: photon.

## Explanation

An emitted alpha particle is a positively charged nucleus, not a neutral atom; beta-minus radiation consists of electrons, while positrons belong to beta-plus decay. Gamma radiation consists of uncharged photons. Calling all these emissions “radiation” does not make them all electromagnetic waves.
