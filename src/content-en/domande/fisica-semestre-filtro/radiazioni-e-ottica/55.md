Three independent nuclei have the same half-life. What is the probability that all three remain after one half-life?

- $\dfrac{1}{8}$.
- $\dfrac{7}{8}$.
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$.
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$.

## Explanation

“All” requires three simultaneous independent survivals: multiply probabilities, $(\dfrac{1}{2})^3=\dfrac{1}{8}$. The complement $\dfrac{7}{8}$ is the probability that at least one has decayed. The sum $\dfrac{3}{2}$ would be the expected number of survivors, not a probability.
