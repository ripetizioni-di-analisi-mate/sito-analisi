An isolated radionuclide has initial activity $\mathscr A_0$ and half-life $T$. What is the mean number of decays during the first half-life?

- $\mathscr A_0T$.
- $\dfrac{\mathscr A_0T}{2}$.
- $2\mathscr A_0T$.
- $\dfrac{\mathscr A_0T}{2\ln2}$.

## Explanation

Half the initial population decays: $N_{\mathrm{dec}}=\dfrac{N_0}{2}$. Since $N_0=\dfrac{\mathscr A_0T}{\ln2}$, the correct expression follows. The product $\mathscr A_0T$ assumes constant activity and overestimates decays: activity falls to half its initial value during the interval.
