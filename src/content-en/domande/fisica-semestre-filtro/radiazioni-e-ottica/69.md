Two bodies absorb equal energy from ionizing radiation. Body B has twice the mass of A. How do their average doses compare?

- $D_B=D_A$.
- $D_B=\dfrac{D_A}{4}$.
- $D_B=2D_A$.
- $D_B=\dfrac{D_A}{2}$.

## Explanation

Average dose is absorbed energy divided by mass. Equal numerator and doubled denominator give $D_B=\dfrac{D_A}{2}$. Equal absorbed energy is essential: equal source activity or equal incident energy alone would not guarantee this dose ratio.
