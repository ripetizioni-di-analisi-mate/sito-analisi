Light enters a stationary transparent medium of index $n=2$ from vacuum. Which medium-to-vacuum ratios for $(v,\nu,\lambda)$ are correct?

- $(\dfrac{1}{2},2,\dfrac{1}{4})$.
- $(\dfrac{1}{2},\dfrac{1}{2},1)$.
- $(\dfrac{1}{2},1,\dfrac{1}{2})$.
- $(1,\dfrac{1}{2},2)$.

## Explanation

Phase speed becomes $\dfrac{c}{n}=\dfrac{c}{2}$, while frequency remains the source frequency. The relation $v=\lambda\nu$ therefore gives $\lambda=\dfrac{\lambda_0}{2}$. Changing frequency as well would require a temporal change in oscillations not produced by this stationary interface.
