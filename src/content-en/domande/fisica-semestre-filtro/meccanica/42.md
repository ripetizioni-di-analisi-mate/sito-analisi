Draw the free-body diagram of a book lifted by a hand, neglecting air resistance. Which forces belong in it?

- Both hand-on-book and book-on-hand forces, without weight.
- Weight, the hand force and an additional force equal to $ma$.
- Weight and the book's force on the hand.
- Weight and the hand's force on the book.

## Explanation

A free-body diagram contains forces acting on the selected body. The book's force on the hand acts on another body; $ma$ is the resultant of forces, not an additional interaction to add. Selection depends on the isolated body, not on the direction or magnitude of acceleration.
