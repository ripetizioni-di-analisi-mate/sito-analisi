Masses of $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ are connected by an ideal taut string over a fixed ideal pulley, with both sides vertical. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is acceleration magnitude?

- $5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

## Explanation

The heavier mass descends. Adding $3g-T=3a$ and $T-2g=2a$ eliminates tension and gives $a=\dfrac{(3-2)g}{3+2}=2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The weight difference accelerates both masses, not only the heavier one.
