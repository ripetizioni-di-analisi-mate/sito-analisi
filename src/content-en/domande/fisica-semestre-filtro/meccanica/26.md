For which situation can $R=\dfrac{v_0^2\sin(2\theta)}{g}$ not be applied directly?

- Launch at $60^\circ$ over level ground without air resistance.
- Launch from a balcony to a lower landing height.
- Launch at $30^\circ$ over level ground without air resistance.
- Launch without air resistance to the same height.

## Explanation

The formula eliminates time using equal initial and final heights. If heights differ, first find time from vertical motion, then horizontal distance. The particular launch angle is not the issue. The rule of an optimum forty-five-degree angle also requires those particular height conditions.
