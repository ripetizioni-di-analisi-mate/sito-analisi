At the highest point of a vertical throw without air resistance, with upwards positive, what are velocity and acceleration?

- $v_y>0$, $a_y=-g$.
- $v_y=0$, $a_y=0$.
- $v_y=0$, $a_y=+g$.
- $v_y=0$, $a_y=-g$.

## Explanation

Vertical velocity vanishes momentarily before reversal, while weight continues to act and acceleration remains downward. An instant of zero velocity is not a phase of equilibrium. Acceleration continues to make velocity negative, initiating the subsequent descending phase.
