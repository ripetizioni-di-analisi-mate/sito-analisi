For an ideal spring along $x$, $F_x=-kx$, where $x$ is algebraic extension. If $x<0$, which conclusion is correct?

- The spring is compressed and force has a positive component.
- The spring constant must become negative.
- The spring is stretched and force has a negative component.
- Force is zero because extension is not positive.

## Explanation

The coordinate is measured from natural length. Compression gives $x<0$ and, with $k>0$, $-kx>0$: force tends to restore the body towards $x=0$. The force changes sign, not the spring constant.
