With initial speed $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$, $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and landing at launch height without air resistance, can a target $12\,\mathrm{m}$ horizontally away be reached?

- Yes, by choosing $45^\circ$.
- Yes, by choosing an angle close to $90^\circ$.
- Yes, two complementary angles always exist.
- No, maximum range is $10\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

The condition would be $\sin2\theta=\dfrac{gR}{v_0^2}=1.2$, impossible for a sine. Maximum range is $\dfrac{v_0^2}{g}=10\,\mathrm{m}$. Two complementary angles exist only for ranges below that maximum under the stated assumptions.
