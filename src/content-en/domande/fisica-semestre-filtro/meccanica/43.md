Two bodies of masses $m$ and $3m$ experience the same nonzero net force. What is the ratio of the first acceleration magnitude to the second?

- $1$.
- $9$.
- $\dfrac13$.
- $3$.

## Explanation

For fixed net force, $a=\dfrac{F}{m}$ is inversely proportional to mass. The body of mass $m$ accelerates three times as much as the body of mass $3m$. Greater mass does not produce greater acceleration under the same specified force.
