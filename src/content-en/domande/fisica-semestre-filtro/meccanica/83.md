Where is the centre of mass of a uniform circular ring?

- It cannot be defined because the body is hollow.
- Always at the ring's lowest point.
- At any point on the circumference.
- At the geometric centre, even though no material is there.

## Explanation

Symmetry cancels contributions from opposite elements in the weighted position average. The centre of mass is a mathematical system position and need not coincide with a particle or material point of the object. The same symmetry applies regardless of the ring’s orientation in space, provided its mass distribution is unchanged.
