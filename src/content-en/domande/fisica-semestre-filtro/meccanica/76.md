During a collision, external horizontal impulse on the system is negligible but vertical impulse is not. Which conservation is justified?

- The entire total-momentum vector.
- The horizontal component of total momentum.
- Each body's momentum separately.
- Total kinetic energy in every collision type.

## Explanation

The impulse theorem applies component by component. Negligible external impulse along one axis allows the corresponding total component to be conserved, not the others. Internal collision forces can change each body's momentum. It is therefore necessary to specify both system and axis before applying momentum conservation.
