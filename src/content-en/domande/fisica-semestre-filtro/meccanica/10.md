A body has velocity $+2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for $3\,\mathrm{s}$ and then $-1\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for $2\,\mathrm{s}$. Which operation on its velocity–time graph gives displacement?

- $(2-1)(3+2)=5\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $2\cdot3+(-1)\cdot2=4\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $2\cdot3+1\cdot2=8\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $\dfrac{2-1}{3+2}=0.2\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

Add the signed areas of the two rectangles. Replacing negative velocity by its magnitude would give distance, $8\,\mathrm{m}$, instead of displacement. Dividing a velocity change by time would yield acceleration, not length.
