Potential energy $U(x)$ is replaced by $U(x)+C$, with constant $C$. What changes in the predicted motion?

- Work between two points increases by $C$.
- Speed increases because potential energy is greater.
- All forces increase by $C$.
- Neither forces nor potential-energy differences.

## Explanation

The constant cancels from $\Delta U$ and has zero derivative, so $F_x=-\dfrac{dU}{dx}$ also remains unchanged. Absolute potential energy depends on the chosen zero; energy transfers and forces do not.
