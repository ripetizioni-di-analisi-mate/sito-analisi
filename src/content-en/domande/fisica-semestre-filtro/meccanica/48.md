A block stays on a smooth floor while pulled by a rope inclined at $\theta$ above the horizontal. Which relation gives the normal force if vertical acceleration is zero?

- $N=F\cos\theta$.
- $N=mg-F\sin\theta$.
- $N=mg+F\sin\theta$.
- $N=mg$.

## Explanation

Projecting upwards gives $N+F\sin\theta-mg=0$. The rope supports part of the weight and reduces normal force. The expression applies while contact is possible, meaning the resulting value is non-negative.
