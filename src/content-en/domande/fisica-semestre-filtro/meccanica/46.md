An ideal spherically symmetric Earth has radius $R$. At height $h=R$ above its surface, what is gravitational acceleration divided by its surface value?

- $1$.
- $\dfrac12$.
- $4$.
- $\dfrac14$.

## Explanation

Distance from the centre is $r=R+h=2R$, not $R$. Since $g(r)=\dfrac{GM}{r^2}$, the ratio is $\dfrac{R^2}{(2R)^2}=\dfrac14$. Height above the surface must not be confused with distance from the centre.
