A person walks rightwards on a free cart, with the whole system initially at rest and no horizontal external forces. Which relation between ground-frame displacements must hold?

- $m_p\Delta x_p-m_c\Delta x_c=0$ with both algebraic displacements along the same axis.
- $m_p\Delta x_p+m_c\Delta x_c=0$.
- $\Delta x_p=\Delta x_c$ for any masses.
- $\Delta x_c=0$ because the person exerts only internal forces.

## Explanation

The horizontal centre of mass stays at its initial position, so the mass-weighted displacement sum is zero. Signs are already contained in displacements and must not be reversed a second time. The cart moves backwards while the person advances relative to the ground.
