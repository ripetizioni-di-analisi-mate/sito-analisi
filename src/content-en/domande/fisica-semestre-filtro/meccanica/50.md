A $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ block is on a fixed plane inclined at $60^\circ$. No other forces act perpendicular to the plane. With $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is the normal force?

- $10\sqrt3\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $20\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $40\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $10\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

Perpendicular equilibrium gives $N=mg\cos60^\circ=10\,\mathrm{N}$. Component $mg\sin60^\circ$ lies along the plane instead. Normal force does not equal the full weight because their directions differ.
