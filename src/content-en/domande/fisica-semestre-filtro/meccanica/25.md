Two balls start at the same height: one is dropped from rest and the other launched horizontally. Without air resistance and with the same horizontal landing surface, which comparison is correct?

- They necessarily have the same impact speed.
- The horizontally launched ball always arrives first.
- They have the same fall time.
- The horizontally launched ball always arrives later.

## Explanation

Their vertical motions have equal initial positions, zero initial vertical velocities and equal gravitational acceleration, giving equal fall times. The launched ball also retains a horizontal component and has greater impact speed. Independence of components does not mean different times; both share the same flight time.
