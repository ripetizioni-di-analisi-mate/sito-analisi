A motor delivers average useful mechanical power $80\,\mathrm{W}$ with efficiency $0.80$. What is average input power over the same interval?

- $100\,\mathrm{W}$.
- $64\,\mathrm{W}$.
- $20\,\mathrm{W}$.
- $80\,\mathrm{W}$.

## Explanation

Efficiency is useful/input, so $P_{\mathrm{in}}=\dfrac{80}{0.80}=100\,\mathrm{W}$. Multiplying by efficiency would reverse the balance. The $20\,\mathrm{W}$ difference is average power transferred into non-useful forms in the model.
