Two people pull opposite ends of a stationary ideal string, each with $300\,\mathrm{N}$. What is its tension?

- $150\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $0\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $600\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $300\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

Isolating a portion of string, its external $300\,\mathrm{N}$ force is balanced by tension from the adjacent portion. The whole string's resultant is zero, but tension is neither the sum nor the resultant of the two external pulls.
