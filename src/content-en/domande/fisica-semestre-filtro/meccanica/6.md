Over a time interval the position–time graph is a horizontal straight line. What are velocity and acceleration inside that interval?

- Both zero.
- Constant nonzero velocity and zero acceleration.
- Zero velocity and constant nonzero acceleration.
- Increasing velocity and positive acceleration.

## Explanation

Position is constant over an entire interval, so its derivative is zero throughout the interior; the derivative of velocity is also zero. This differs from a horizontal tangent at just one instant. At the interval boundaries, behaviour depends on adjacent sections, which the question does not specify.
