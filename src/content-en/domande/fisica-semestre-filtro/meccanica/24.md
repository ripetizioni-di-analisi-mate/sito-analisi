A projectile is launched at $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $60^\circ$ above the horizontal, without air resistance. What are its velocity components at the apex?

- $v_x=10\,\mathrm{m/s}$, $v_y=0$.
- $v_x=10\,\mathrm{m/s}$, $v_y=10\sqrt3\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $v_x=0$, $v_y=0$.
- $v_x=10\sqrt3\,\mathrm{m/s}$, $v_y=0$.

## Explanation

Only the vertical component vanishes at the apex. The horizontal component remains $v_0\cos60^\circ=10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ because no horizontal force acts. Speed at the apex is therefore nonzero.
