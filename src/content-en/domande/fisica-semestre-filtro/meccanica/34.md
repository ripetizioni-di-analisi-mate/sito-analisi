A point rotates at frequency $2\,\mathrm{Hz}$ on a circle of radius $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$. What is its speed?

- $1\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $\pi\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $2\pi\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $4\pi\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

Frequency counts revolutions per second; each revolution covers $2\pi r$. Thus $v=2\pi rf=2\pi\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Period is $\dfrac1f=0.50\,\mathrm{s}$, not $2\,\mathrm{s}$.
