Position is $x(t)=5+2t-t^2$ in SI units. What are velocity and acceleration at $t=3\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $v_x=2\,\mathrm{m/s}$, $a_x=-1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $v_x=-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$, $a_x=-2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $v_x=-4\,\mathrm{m/s}$, $a_x=-6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $v_x=+4\,\mathrm{m/s}$, $a_x=-2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

## Explanation

Differentiate position: $v_x=2-2t$, then $a_x=-2$ in SI units. The coefficient of $t^2$ is half the acceleration, not acceleration itself. At $3\,\mathrm{s}$ the velocity component is negative.
