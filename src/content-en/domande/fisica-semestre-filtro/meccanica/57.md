Two identical ideal springs of constant $k$ are connected in series. What is the equivalent constant?

- $\dfrac{k}{4}$.
- $k$.
- $2k$.
- $\dfrac{k}{2}$.

## Explanation

The same force $F$ acts through both springs, producing total extension $\dfrac{F}{k}+\dfrac{F}{k}=\dfrac{2F}{k}$. Since $F=k_{\mathrm{eq}}x$, $k_{\mathrm{eq}}=\dfrac{k}{2}$. The series combination is softer than either spring.
