A horizontal forearm supports $20\,\mathrm{N}$ at $0.30\,\mathrm{m}$ from the elbow and weighs $10\,\mathrm{N}$ acting at $0.15\,\mathrm{m}$. The muscle's perpendicular moment arm is $0.050\,\mathrm{m}$. What muscle force balances torques?

- $90\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $120\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $150\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $30\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

The two weights produce torques in the same direction: $20\cdot0.30+10\cdot0.15=7.5\,\mathrm{N\,m}$. Dividing by $0.050\,\mathrm{m}$ gives $150\,\mathrm{N}$. Neglecting forearm weight would give $120\,\mathrm{N}$.
