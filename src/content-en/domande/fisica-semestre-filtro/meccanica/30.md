A vehicle takes two curves at the same speed; the second has twice the radius of the first. How does centripetal acceleration change?

- It remains equal.
- It quadruples.
- It doubles.
- It halves.

## Explanation

Here linear speed is fixed, so $a_c=\dfrac{v^2}{r}$ is inversely proportional to radius. A wider curve changes velocity direction more slowly. The comparison would differ at fixed angular velocity. Angular velocity decreases on the wider curve because each revolution takes longer.
