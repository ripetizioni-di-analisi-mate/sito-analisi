A braking model is $v_x=12-3t$ in SI units until stopping. Which use of the formula is physically correct?

- End the model at $t=4\,\mathrm{s}$.
- Set velocity to zero from $t=3\,\mathrm{s}$.
- Infer that the brakes launch the body backwards after $4\,\mathrm{s}$.
- Use the absolute value of $12-3t$ to extend the same acceleration law.

## Explanation

The formula describes braking and gives stopping when $12-3t=0$, at $4\,\mathrm{s}$. Its mathematical extension to negative velocity would represent different motion, not continuation of the stated braking process. After stopping, a new description of the forces and motion conditions is required.
