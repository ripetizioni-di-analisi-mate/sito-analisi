An unknown mass $m$ is at $x=0$ and a $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass at $x=4\,\mathrm{m}$. The centre of mass is at $x=3\,\mathrm{m}$. What is $m$?

- $1\,\mathrm{kg}$.
- $4\,\mathrm{kg}$.
- $3\,\mathrm{kg}$.
- $9\,\mathrm{kg}$.

## Explanation

The balance $3=\dfrac{12}{m+3}$ in SI units gives $m+3=4$, hence $m=1\,\mathrm{kg}$. The centre of mass lies closer to the $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass, consistent with a smaller mass at the origin.
