Only the shape of a body's trajectory is known, without timing information. Which quantity cannot be determined?

- Speed at the different points.
- The set of points visited.
- The geometric shape of the path.
- Whether the drawn path is straight or curved.

## Explanation

A trajectory describes where the body passes, but not when. Bodies following the same curve can have completely different speeds; a position function or equivalent timing information is required. An arrow along the curve may indicate direction, but by itself it does not assign a speed.
