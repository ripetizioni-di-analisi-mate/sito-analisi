Two position–time graphs intersect with different slopes. Which conclusion is correct at the intersection time?

- The bodies have the same position and different velocities.
- Both bodies are stationary.
- The bodies have the same velocity and different positions.
- The bodies necessarily have the same acceleration.

## Explanation

The ordinate gives position, while slope gives velocity. The intersection indicates a meeting; different slopes indicate different velocities. Acceleration requires information about how the slope changes. Equal velocities would require parallel tangents, even if the bodies occupied different positions.
