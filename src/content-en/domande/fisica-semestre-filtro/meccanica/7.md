An athlete completes one lap and returns to the starting point in a positive time. Which statement necessarily follows?

- Average velocity and average speed are both zero.
- Final instantaneous velocity is necessarily zero.
- Average velocity is zero, average speed is positive.
- Distance travelled equals displacement magnitude.

## Explanation

Returning makes total displacement zero, hence average velocity zero. Lap length is positive and gives positive average speed. Returning to the starting point alone does not determine final instantaneous velocity. This result does not require uniform circular motion; finishing at the starting point is sufficient.
