A block slides rightwards on a belt moving rightwards faster than the block. In which direction does the belt's kinetic friction act on the block?

- Rightwards.
- Vertically upwards.
- It is zero because both move rightwards.
- Leftwards.

## Explanation

Relative to the belt the block slips backwards; friction on the block opposes that relative motion and points rightwards. It can therefore do positive work in the ground frame. Its direction cannot be determined from ground-frame motion alone.
