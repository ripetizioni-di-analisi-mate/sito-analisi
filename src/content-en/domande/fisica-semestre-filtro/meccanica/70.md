An ideal spring is first compressed by length $d$ and then stretched by the same length $d$. Which comparison is correct?

- Energy and force both have the same sign and direction.
- Elastic energy is equal in both states and forces have opposite directions.
- Stretching energy is twice compression energy.
- Compression energy is negative.

## Explanation

With zero at natural length, $U=\dfrac12kx^2$ depends on the square and is equal for $x=\pm d$. Force $F_x=-kx$ instead depends on the sign of extension and reverses direction. Energy is a scalar quantity; it has no direction like that of the elastic force.
