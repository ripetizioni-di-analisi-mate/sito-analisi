In the graph, force follows $F_x=(4\,\mathrm{N/m})x$. What is the work over the section from $x=2\,\mathrm{m}$ to $x=3\,\mathrm{m}$ alone?

![Force as a function of position.](/figure/fisica-semestre-filtro/meccanica/lavoro-en.svg)

- $4\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $12\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $10\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $18\,\mathrm{J}$.

## Explanation

Over the requested section force rises from $8$ to $12\,\mathrm{N}$. The area is a trapezoid of width $1\,\mathrm{m}$ and average height $10\,\mathrm{N}$, hence $10\,\mathrm{J}$. The $18\,\mathrm{J}$ instead represents the entire section from zero to $3\,\mathrm{m}$.
