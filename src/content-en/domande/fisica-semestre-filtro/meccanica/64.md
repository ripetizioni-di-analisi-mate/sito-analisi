A body's mass is halved and its speed doubled. How does kinetic energy change?

- It quadruples.
- It halves.
- It doubles.
- It stays unchanged.

## Explanation

The ratio is $\dfrac{K_f}{K_i}=(\dfrac12)\cdot2^2=2$, since $K=\dfrac{mv^2}{2}$. Speed appears squared; treating it as linear would confuse energy with momentum magnitude.
