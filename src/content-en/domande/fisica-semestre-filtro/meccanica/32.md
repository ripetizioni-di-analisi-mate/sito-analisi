A ball rotates on a smooth table, held by a string. If the string breaks, what direction does velocity have immediately afterwards?

- Radially inwards.
- Radially outwards.
- Opposite to the previous tangent.
- Tangent to the circle at the breaking point.

## Explanation

Breaking removes tension without providing an additional instantaneous impulse. Velocity therefore retains its previous tangential direction; without other horizontal forces, subsequent motion is uniform and straight. Radially outward motion would require an instantaneous change in the direction of velocity.
