A car takes an unbanked horizontal curve without slipping. With negligible air resistance, which force can provide the centripetal resultant?

- The road's vertical normal force.
- An extra centripetal force in addition to contact forces.
- Static friction from the road on the tyres.
- The car's weight.

## Explanation

The radial resultant must be horizontal. Weight and normal force are vertical and balance; lateral static friction can bend the path without slipping. “Centripetal” describes the resultant's role, not a new interaction.
