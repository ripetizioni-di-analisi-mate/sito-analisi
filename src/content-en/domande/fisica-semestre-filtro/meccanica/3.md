At an instant $v_x<0$ and $a_x<0$. What is happening to speed in straight-line motion?

- It decreases.
- It remains constant.
- It is necessarily zero.
- It increases.

## Explanation

Velocity becomes more negative: its magnitude increases because velocity and acceleration have the same sign. Negative acceleration does not automatically mean slowing down; compare its sign with velocity. Reversing the axis changes both signs but leaves this increase in speed unchanged.
