In an ideal ballistic pendulum, a projectile embeds in a block during a brief collision, then the system rises without dissipation. Which sequence of laws is correct?

- Horizontal momentum during collision; mechanical energy during ascent.
- Mechanical energy during collision; vertical momentum during ascent.
- Unchanged kinetic energy during both collision and ascent.
- Momentum of the projectile alone conserved while embedding.

## Explanation

Embedding is completely inelastic and dissipates kinetic energy; the brief collision allows external horizontal impulse to be neglected. During ascent, gravity changes momentum but exchanges potential and kinetic energy without dissipation. The shared speed immediately after collision connects the two balances without merging them into one conservation law.
