A body moves forward $3\,\mathrm{m}$ on a horizontal plane under a constant $10\,\mathrm{N}$ pull parallel to and along displacement and opposing $4\,\mathrm{N}$ friction. Other forces are perpendicular to motion. What is the kinetic-energy change?

- $+18\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $+30\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $+42\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $-12\,\mathrm{J}$.

## Explanation

The work–kinetic energy theorem uses total work: $\Delta K=(10-4)\cdot3=18\,\mathrm{J}$. Considering only the pull or only friction would not give the body's balance. Normal force and weight add no work during this horizontal displacement.
