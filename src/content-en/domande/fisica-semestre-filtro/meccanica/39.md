A bus accelerates relative to the road. A passenger describes a free object's motion in the bus frame. What caution is necessary?

- The frame is inertial because it belongs to a rigid body.
- The object's mass changes because the bus accelerates.
- Newton's third law always allows zero net force on the object.
- The frame is non-inertial; the usual Newton equation using only real forces does not apply directly.

## Explanation

A frame accelerating relative to an inertial frame is non-inertial. Study motion from the road or correctly introduce apparent forces in the accelerating frame; the observed change is not attributed to a different mass.
