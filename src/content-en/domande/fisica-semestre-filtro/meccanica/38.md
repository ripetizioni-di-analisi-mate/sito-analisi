Which study is suited to a particle model without describing body rotation?

- The force needed to balance a lever with unequal arms.
- The torque of a force about a door hinge.
- The trajectory of a ball's centre when size and spin are irrelevant.
- The distribution of reactions at two beam supports.

## Explanation

A particle model is a choice tied to the question, not an absolute property of a body. It can suffice for trajectories without relevant size or rotation effects; levers, beams and doors require the positions of force lines of action.
