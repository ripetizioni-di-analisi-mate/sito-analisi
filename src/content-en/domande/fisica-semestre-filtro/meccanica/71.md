A system's mechanical energy changes from $50$ to $38\,\mathrm{J}$. If friction is the only non-conservative contribution in the balance, what is its work?

- $+88\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $-38\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $+12\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $-12\,\mathrm{J}$.

## Explanation

The balance $\Delta(K+U)=W_{\mathrm{non\,cons}}$ gives $38-50=-12\,\mathrm{J}$. Lost mechanical energy is $12\,\mathrm{J}$, positive as an amount dissipated, whereas the work reducing it is negative.
