A $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ body experiences only two perpendicular forces of magnitudes $3\,\mathrm{N}$ and $4\,\mathrm{N}$. What is its acceleration magnitude?

- $0.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $3.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

## Explanation

Add forces vectorially: $F_{\mathrm{tot}}=\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=5\,\mathrm{N}$. Dividing by $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ gives $2.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Directly adding or subtracting magnitudes would apply only to parallel forces.
