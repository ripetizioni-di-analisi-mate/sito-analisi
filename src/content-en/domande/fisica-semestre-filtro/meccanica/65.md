A particle has total mechanical energy $12\,\mathrm{J}$ and moves without dissipation. A position would require potential energy $15\,\mathrm{J}$ using the same zero. What follows?

- It arrives there with kinetic energy $-3\,\mathrm{J}$.
- It arrives there with kinetic energy $3\,\mathrm{J}$.
- That position is inaccessible without additional energy.
- Changing the energy zero automatically makes the position accessible.

## Explanation

The relation $K=E-U$ would give $-3\,\mathrm{J}$, incompatible with $K\ge0$. This signals a limit on accessible motion, not physically negative kinetic energy. Shifting the zero of $U$ changes $E$ by the same constant and leaves $E-U$ unchanged.
