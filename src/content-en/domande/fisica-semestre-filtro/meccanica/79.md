Which statement holds for an inelastic collision of a system with negligible external impulse?

- Each body separately preserves its velocity.
- Total momentum is conserved even if kinetic energy decreases.
- A kinetic-energy decrease implies a decrease in total momentum magnitude.
- Total energy disappears by the amount of lost kinetic energy.

## Explanation

Momentum and kinetic energy are different quantities obeying different balances. Dissipation transforms kinetic energy into other forms, such as internal energy, without violating momentum conservation for a system with negligible external impulse. A completely inelastic collision is the particular case in which the bodies remain joined.
