A particle starts at $x_0=3\,\mathrm{m}$ with constant velocity $v_x=-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is its position after $5\,\mathrm{s}$?

- $-7\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $+7\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $-10\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $+13\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

Uniform motion gives $x=x_0+v_xt=3-10=-7\,\mathrm{m}$. Displacement is $-10\,\mathrm{m}$, but position includes the initial value. A negative sign indicates the negative side of the chosen origin.
