Two equal opposite forces act along distinct parallel lines, forming a couple. Which description is correct?

- Nonzero resultant force but zero torque.
- Static equilibrium guaranteed merely by equal magnitudes.
- Zero resultant force but nonzero total torque.
- Both resultant force and torque zero.

## Explanation

The forces cancel in the translational balance, but their separated lines of action produce a couple torque. For a rigid body, zero resultant force is insufficient; the torque sum must also be checked. Perpendicular separation between the lines determines the couple torque, not their resultant force.
