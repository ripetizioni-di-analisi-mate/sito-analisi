When calculating beam torques, the ideal hinge is chosen as the pivot. How does this choice affect hinge reaction?

- The hinge force becomes physically zero.
- Its torque becomes maximal because it acts at the pivot.
- The reaction can no longer be found from force equilibrium.
- Its torque is zero, but the force remains in translational equations.

## Explanation

The reaction's line of action passes through the pivot, so its moment arm is zero. This choice simplifies the torque equation without eliminating the physical interaction; reaction components can still be found from force sums. An ideal hinge transmits forces but provides no additional support couple in this planar model.
