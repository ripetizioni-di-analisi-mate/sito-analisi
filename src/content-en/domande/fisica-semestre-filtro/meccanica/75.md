Net force has the triangular profile shown. A second impulse, also triangular, has the same area but lasts half as long. What must its peak force be?

![Initial triangular impulse with peak 100 N and duration 0.10 s.](/figure/fisica-semestre-filtro/meccanica/impulso-en.svg)

- $100\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $200\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $50\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $400\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

The initial impulse is $J=\dfrac12(100)(0.10)=5\,\mathrm{N\,s}$. Preserving triangular area over a duration of $0.05\,\mathrm{s}$ requires $F_{\max}=\dfrac{2J}{\Delta t}=200\,\mathrm{N}$. Halving the base requires twice the height, not four times. The $50\,\mathrm{N}$ shown in the figure is instead the average force of the first impulse.
