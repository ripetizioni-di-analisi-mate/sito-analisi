A $10\,\mathrm{N}$ force is applied $0.40\,\mathrm{m}$ from a pivot, making $30^\circ$ with the vector from pivot to application point. What is the torque magnitude?

- $2\,\mathrm{N\,m}$.
- $4\,\mathrm{N\,m}$.
- $2\sqrt3\,\mathrm{N\,m}$.
- $8\,\mathrm{N\,m}$.

## Explanation

The perpendicular moment arm is $b=r\sin30^\circ=0.20\,\mathrm{m}$, so $\tau=Fb=2\,\mathrm{N\,m}$. Distance to the application point is not the moment arm when force is not perpendicular.
