A graph $v_x(t)$ crosses the time axis from positive to negative values. What does the crossing indicate?

- A reversal of motion direction.
- Passage through the spatial origin.
- Zero total distance travelled.
- Necessarily zero acceleration.

## Explanation

The sign of velocity indicates motion direction: crossing zero with a sign change marks reversal. Position is not the ordinate of this graph; acceleration depends on its slope. Crossing the spatial origin would instead require the initial position and the accumulated displacement.
