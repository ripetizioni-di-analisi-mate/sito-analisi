A body brakes uniformly from $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to rest over $25\,\mathrm{m}$. With the axis along initial motion, what is acceleration?

- $-0.4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $-4\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $-2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.
- $+2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

## Explanation

From $v_f^2=v_0^2+2a\Delta x$, $a=-\dfrac{100}{50}=-2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Its sign must oppose positive initial velocity. Velocity divided by distance does not have acceleration units.
