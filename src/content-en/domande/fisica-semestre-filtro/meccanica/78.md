A cart collides elastically in one dimension with an identical initially stationary cart. With no external impulse, which outcome satisfies both conservation laws for an actual collision?

- The first stops and the second takes its initial velocity.
- Both continue at the first cart's initial velocity.
- The first reverses at equal speed and the second stays at rest.
- They remain joined at half the initial velocity.

## Explanation

For equal masses, an elastic one-dimensional collision exchanges velocities. Stopping the first and assigning its initial velocity to the second conserves both momentum and kinetic energy. Remaining joined would conserve momentum but dissipate energy.
