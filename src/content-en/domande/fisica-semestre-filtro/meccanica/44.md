A body moves rightwards while net force points leftwards. Which statement is correct before any eventual stop?

- It can continue rightwards while slowing down.
- Net force must be zero while velocity remains rightwards.
- It accelerates rightwards because it moves rightwards.
- It must immediately move leftwards.

## Explanation

Net force determines acceleration direction, not the direction of existing velocity. A force opposing motion initially reduces speed; reversal, if it occurs, first requires passing through zero velocity. In the stated case, resultant instantaneous power is negative and kinetic energy decreases.
