An object is released without a push from a cart in uniform straight-line motion on a horizontal plane. In the ground frame, without air resistance, how does it move horizontally after release?

- It accelerates horizontally with magnitude $g$.
- It reverses horizontal velocity.
- It retains the cart's horizontal velocity component.
- Its horizontal velocity immediately becomes zero.

## Explanation

At release the object shares the cart's velocity. Afterwards only vertical gravity acts, so horizontal velocity stays constant. Being dropped means initially zero vertical velocity here, not necessarily zero total velocity.
