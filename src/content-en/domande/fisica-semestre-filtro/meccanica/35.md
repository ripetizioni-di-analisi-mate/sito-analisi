In an inertial frame the net force on a body is zero over an interval. The body is initially moving. What follows?

- The body gradually stops even without friction.
- The velocity vector remains constant.
- Position remains constant.
- Speed stays constant but direction can change.

## Explanation

Newton's first law preserves the velocity vector when net force is zero. A changing direction would require acceleration and thus nonzero resultant force. Position remains constant only with zero initial velocity. A moving body and a stationary one are both compatible with the same zero resultant force.
