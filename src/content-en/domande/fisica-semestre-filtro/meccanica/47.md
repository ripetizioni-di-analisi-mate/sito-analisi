Both of two point masses are doubled and their separation is doubled. How does their mutual gravitational force change?

- It becomes one quarter.
- It quadruples.
- It stays unchanged.
- It doubles.

## Explanation

In $F=\dfrac{Gm_1m_2}{r^2}$, the mass product grows by a factor of $4$, and the denominator also grows by a factor of $4$. The effects cancel. Considering only one mass or forgetting the squared distance would change the ratio. The force remains attractive; changing these parameters does not reverse its direction.
