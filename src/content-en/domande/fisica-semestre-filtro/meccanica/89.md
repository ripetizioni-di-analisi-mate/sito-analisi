On a horizontal beam supported at both ends, a constant load is slowly moved towards the right support while staying between the supports. How do vertical reactions change?

- Both increase because torque about the left support increases.
- Left reaction increases and right reaction decreases.
- Right reaction increases and left reaction decreases, with unchanged sum.
- Both stay unchanged because total load is unchanged.

## Explanation

Reaction sum balances total weight, which stays constant. In the torque balance about the left support, moving the load rightwards increases its arm and requires greater right reaction; left reaction decreases to preserve the sum.
