A $10\,\mathrm{N}$ force forms $60^\circ$ with instantaneous velocity of magnitude $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What instantaneous power does it deliver?

- $15\sqrt3\,\mathrm{W}$.
- $15\,\mathrm{W}$.
- $\dfrac{10}{3}\,\mathrm{W}$.
- $30\,\mathrm{W}$.

## Explanation

Power is the dot product $P=Fv\cos\theta=10\cdot3\cdot\dfrac12=15\,\mathrm{W}$. The angular factor selects the force component along velocity. Units are $\mathrm{N\,m/s}$, equivalent to watts.
