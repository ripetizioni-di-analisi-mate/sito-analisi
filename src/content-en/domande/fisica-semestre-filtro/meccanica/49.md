A person and the scale under their feet are in ideal free fall. What does the scale read?

- A force equal to half the weight.
- Zero normal force despite nonzero gravitational weight.
- A force equal to gravitational weight.
- Zero because the person's mass has become zero.

## Explanation

With upwards positive, $N-mg=ma_y$ and $a_y=-g$ give $N=0$. The scale measures contact, not gravity directly. A zero reading does not require zero mass or zero gravitational field. Person and scale accelerate together under gravity, without needing support through contact pressure.
