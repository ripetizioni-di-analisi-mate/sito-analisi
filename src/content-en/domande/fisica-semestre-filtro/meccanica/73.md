Two smooth fixed guides of different shapes connect the same heights in the same uniform gravitational field. A particle subject only to gravity and guide reaction starts at rest and reaches the final height on each while maintaining contact. What follows from the energy balance alone?

- Final speed and time must both be equal.
- Final speed is equal, but travel time need not be.
- The longer guide always gives greater final speed.
- The steeper guide always gives smaller final kinetic energy.

## Explanation

Height drop determines $v_f^2=2g\Delta h$ under the given assumptions. Time instead depends on speed throughout the path and guide shape. An endpoint energy comparison alone does not contain that information.
