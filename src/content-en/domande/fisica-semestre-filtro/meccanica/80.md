An initially stationary system separates into two fragments by releasing internal energy, without external impulse. What must be true?

- Final total kinetic energy must be zero.
- The more massive fragment necessarily has greater speed.
- Fragment momenta are equal in magnitude and opposite.
- Fragment velocities necessarily have equal magnitudes.

## Explanation

The balance gives $\vec p_1+\vec p_2=0$, hence $\vec p_1=-\vec p_2$. With different masses, speeds differ inversely with mass. Kinetic energy can increase using the available internal energy.
