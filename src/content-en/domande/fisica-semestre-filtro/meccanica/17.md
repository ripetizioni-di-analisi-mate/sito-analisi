Before braking, a car maintains $15\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for a reaction time of $0.60\,\mathrm{s}$. What distance does it travel during reaction alone?

- $25\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $9\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $4.5\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $15.6\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

During reaction motion is uniform, so $s=v_0t_r=9\,\mathrm{m}$. No factor $\dfrac12$ belongs here: it would apply to velocity increasing linearly from zero, not constant velocity.
