Initial velocity is $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Acceleration is $+2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ for $3\,\mathrm{s}$, then $-1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ for $2\,\mathrm{s}$. What is final velocity?

- $8\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $12\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

The acceleration–time area is $\Delta v=6-2=4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Add it to initial velocity: $v_f=4+4=8\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Treating the area as final velocity forgets the initial condition.
