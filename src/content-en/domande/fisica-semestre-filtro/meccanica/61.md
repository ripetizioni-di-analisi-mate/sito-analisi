A body of weight $20\,\mathrm{N}$ is raised vertically by $3\,\mathrm{m}$. How much work is done by gravity?

- $-\dfrac{20}{3}\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $-60\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$.
- $+60\,\mathrm{J}$.

## Explanation

Gravity opposes displacement, so $W_P=P\Delta y\cos180^\circ=-60\,\mathrm{J}$. Potential-energy change is instead $+60\,\mathrm{J}$. Lifting speed is unnecessary for calculating this work.
