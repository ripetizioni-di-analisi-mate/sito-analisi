A free fall from rest lasts at least $1\,\mathrm{s}$. In the final second the body travels $\dfrac34$ of the total height drop. With uniform gravity and negligible air resistance, how long does the fall last?

- $\dfrac23\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $2\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $3\,\mathrm{s}$.
- $\dfrac43\,\mathrm{s}$.

## Explanation

Writing $T$ for time in seconds, the ratio is $\dfrac{T^2-(T-1)^2}{T^2}=\dfrac34$. This gives $3T^2-8T+4=0$, with roots $2$ and $\dfrac23$. The second is incompatible with $T\ge1$ because the final second must lie entirely within the fall. Duration is therefore $2\,\mathrm{s}$.
