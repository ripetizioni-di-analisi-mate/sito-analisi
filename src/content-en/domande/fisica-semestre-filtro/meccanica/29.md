Two points on a rigid disc rotate at the same angular velocity. The second is twice as far from the axis. How do their centripetal accelerations compare?

- The second is four times as large.
- The second is half as large.
- The second is twice as large.
- They are equal.

## Explanation

At fixed angular velocity, $a_c=\omega^2r$, so acceleration grows linearly with radius. Using $\dfrac{v^2}{r}$ while incorrectly keeping $v$ equal would change the assumption: on the disc $v=\omega r$ also doubles.
