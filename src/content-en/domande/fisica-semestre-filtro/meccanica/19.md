Two spheres of different masses are dropped simultaneously from rest at the same height in vacuum, under uniform gravity. What does the model predict?

- They arrive together with the same speed.
- They arrive together but the heavier one has greater speed.
- The lighter one has greater acceleration because its inertia is smaller.
- The heavier one arrives first because its weight is greater.

## Explanation

Weight $mg$ divided by inertial mass $m$ gives the same acceleration $g$. With equal initial conditions and height drop, the motion equations are identical. This prediction assumes no air resistance.
