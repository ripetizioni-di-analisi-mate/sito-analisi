Two ideal parallel springs have constants $k$ and $2k$ and equal nonzero extension. How do they share total force?

- The $2k$ spring exerts twice the other spring's force.
- Only the softer spring exerts force.
- The $2k$ spring exerts half the other spring's force.
- The two forces are equal.

## Explanation

With shared extension $x$, forces are $kx$ and $2kx$. Forces add in parallel but are not equal when constants differ. Equal force instead characterises an ideal series connection of massless springs.
