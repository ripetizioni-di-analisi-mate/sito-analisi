A $0.20\,\mathrm{kg}$ ball changes velocity from $-5$ to $+5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ along an axis. What net impulse does it receive?

- $0\,\mathrm{N\,s}$.
- $+1\,\mathrm{N\,s}$.
- $+2\,\mathrm{N\,s}$.
- $-2\,\mathrm{N\,s}$.

## Explanation

$J_x=m(v_f-v_i)=0.20[5-(-5)]=2\,\mathrm{N\,s}$. Speeds are equal but velocities are opposite: momentum changes even without a kinetic-energy change.
