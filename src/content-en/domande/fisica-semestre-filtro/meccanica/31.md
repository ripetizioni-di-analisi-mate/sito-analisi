A mass completes one revolution in uniform circular motion. What is the vector change of momentum between the beginning and end?

- It has magnitude $2mv$ and is tangential.
- It is zero.
- It has magnitude $mv$ and is radial.
- It is nonzero because force never vanishes.

## Explanation

After one revolution the velocity vector returns to its initial value, and so does momentum. Total vector impulse is zero even though centripetal force is nonzero at every instant; its direction changes around the circle.
