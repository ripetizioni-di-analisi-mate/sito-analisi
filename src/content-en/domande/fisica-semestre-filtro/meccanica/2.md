A body moves in one direction at $6\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for $2\,\mathrm{s}$ and at $3\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for $4\,\mathrm{s}$. What is its average velocity?

- $9\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $4.5\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $5\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

Displacements are $12\,\mathrm{m}$ and $12\,\mathrm{m}$; total time is $6\,\mathrm{s}$, giving $v_m=\dfrac{24}{6}=4\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The arithmetic mean of the two velocities would require equal times, which are absent here. Each velocity must be weighted by its section duration, not by the number of sections.
