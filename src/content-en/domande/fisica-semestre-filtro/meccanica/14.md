A body starts from rest with constant positive acceleration. What is the ratio of distance travelled during the second second to distance travelled during the first second?

- $4$.
- $2$.
- $3$.
- $1$.

## Explanation

Displacement from the initial position is proportional to $t^2$. Distance in the first second is $\dfrac{a}{2}$ in SI units; in the second it is $\dfrac a2(2^2-1^2)=\dfrac{3a}{2}$. The value $4$ instead compares total distance after two seconds with distance after one.
