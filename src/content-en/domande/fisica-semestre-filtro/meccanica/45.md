On a planet, gravitational acceleration is half Earth's value. How do the same object's mass and weight change?

- Both mass and weight halve.
- Mass halves and weight stays the same.
- Mass stays the same and weight doubles.
- Mass stays the same and weight halves.

## Explanation

Local weight is $P=mg$, while mass is an inertial property of the body. Halving $g$ halves gravitational force without changing mass measured in kilograms. Force measured in newtons and mass measured in kilograms remain distinct physical quantities.
