A $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ cart at $4\,\mathrm{m/s}$ hits a stationary $1\,\mathrm{kg}$ cart and remains joined to it. External impulse is negligible. What fraction of initial kinetic energy is dissipated?

- $1$.
- $\dfrac34$.
- $\dfrac14$.
- $\dfrac12$.

## Explanation

Common velocity is $V=\dfrac{3\cdot4}{4}=3\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Energies are $K_i=24\,\mathrm{J}$ and $K_f=18\,\mathrm{J}$; relative loss is $\dfrac{24-18}{24}=\dfrac14$. The retained fraction, $\dfrac34$, is not the dissipated fraction.
