A ball is thrown upwards at $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Without air resistance and with $g=10\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, what is its velocity after $1.5\,\mathrm{s}$, before returning to the ground?

- $0\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $-5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ with upwards positive.
- $-15\,\mathrm{m/s}$ with upwards positive.
- $+5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ with upwards positive.

## Explanation

$v_y=v_{0y}-gt=10-15=-5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The apex is reached at $1\,\mathrm{s}$ and return to initial height at $2\,\mathrm{s}$; the requested instant lies on descent.
