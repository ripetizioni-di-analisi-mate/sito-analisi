A block at rest on a fixed horizontal plane has maximum static friction $12\,\mathrm{N}$. A horizontal $8\,\mathrm{N}$ force attempts to move it; no other horizontal forces act apart from friction. What is the actual friction magnitude?

- $8\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $20\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $4\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $12\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

Static friction adjusts to the force needed for equilibrium up to its available maximum. Here $8\,\mathrm{N}$ opposite the push suffices. Always setting $f_s=\mu_sN$ confuses an inequality with an equality. Friction direction follows by requiring the horizontal resultant force to vanish.
