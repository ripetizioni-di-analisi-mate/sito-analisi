A block slides on a surface with normal force $80\,\mathrm{N}$ and kinetic coefficient $0.25$. What is kinetic friction magnitude?

- $80\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $20\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $0.25\,\mathrm{N}$.
- $320\,\mathrm{N}$.

## Explanation

In the specified model, $f_d=\mu_dN=0.25\cdot80=20\,\mathrm{N}$. Use the actual normal force already supplied without assuming it equals weight. The coefficient is dimensionless, not itself a force. Direction must then be assigned against relative sliding, separately from calculating magnitude.
