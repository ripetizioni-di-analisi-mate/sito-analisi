Which force is the Newton's-third-law reaction to a book's weight?

- The table's friction on the book.
- The book's gravitational attraction on Earth.
- The table's normal force on the book.
- The book's force on the table.

## Explanation

Weight is Earth's gravitational force on the book. An action–reaction pair swaps agent and receiving body while retaining the same interaction. Contact forces involving the table belong to different pairs. The gravitational pair therefore involves two bodies even though Earth’s acceleration is practically imperceptible.
