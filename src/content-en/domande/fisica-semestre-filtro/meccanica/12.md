A train moves at $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$ relative to the ground. A person walks towards its rear at $2\,\mathrm{m/s}$ relative to the train. What is their ground-frame velocity, positive along the train's motion?

- $+22\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $+18\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $-18\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $-2\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

In the classical model, relative velocities along the axis add with signs: $v=20+(-2)=18\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Walking towards the rear does not imply moving backwards relative to the ground; train motion also matters. The relative velocity must be referred to the same axis before performing the algebraic sum.
