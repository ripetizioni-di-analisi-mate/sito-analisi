A particle travels $10\,\mathrm{m}$ rightwards and then $6\,\mathrm{m}$ leftwards. What are algebraic displacement and total distance, with rightwards positive?

- $+4\,\mathrm{m}$ and $16\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $+4\,\mathrm{m}$ and $4\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $+16\,\mathrm{m}$ and $4\,\mathrm{m}$.
- $-4\,\mathrm{m}$ and $16\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

Displacement adds signed sections: $10-6=4\,\mathrm{m}$. Distance adds lengths, $10+6=16\,\mathrm{m}$. Turning back reduces displacement but does not erase the path already travelled. Distance would be zero only if no path were travelled, not merely because motion partly reverses.
