Which property characterises a conservative force in the domain considered?

- Work around every closed path is zero.
- Force magnitude is always constant.
- Work is zero along every open path.
- Force always opposes velocity.

## Explanation

Path independence of work is equivalent to zero work around every closed path. A spring is conservative despite variable force; a conservative force can do positive or negative work between different positions. A force always opposing sliding instead dissipates energy even over a closed path.
