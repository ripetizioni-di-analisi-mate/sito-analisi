Why is acceleration nonzero in uniform circular motion?

- Velocity direction changes even though its magnitude is constant.
- Velocity points towards the centre.
- Speed increases on every revolution.
- The period continually decreases.

## Explanation

Acceleration measures change of the velocity vector, not just speed. In uniform circular motion velocity is tangent, while acceleration is radially inward. Speed and period remain constant. If speed varied, a tangential acceleration component would appear in addition to the radial component.
