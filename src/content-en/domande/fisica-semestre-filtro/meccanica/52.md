In the ideal static-friction model, a horizontal force on a block at rest on a fixed horizontal plane, with no other horizontal forces apart from friction, reaches exactly $F=\mu_sN$. Which statement is rigorous?

- Static friction must be zero.
- The block must already have acceleration $\mu_sg$.
- The kinetic coefficient must be used even if the block remains at rest.
- Equilibrium is still compatible; exceeding the limit makes it impossible.

## Explanation

The static condition is $|f_s|\le\mu_sN$ and includes equality. Acceleration after sliding starts requires the kinetic regime and the applied force value. Equality at the threshold alone does not impose an already nonzero velocity. A disturbance can initiate motion, but it is not included in the limiting static condition.
