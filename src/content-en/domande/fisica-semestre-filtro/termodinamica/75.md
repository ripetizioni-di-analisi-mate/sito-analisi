A body cools by releasing heat to its surroundings, and its entropy decreases. Which conclusion is correct?

- The surroundings must lose the same entropy as the body.
- The process can be possible if total entropy does not decrease.
- Decreasing body entropy always violates the second law.
- The process must be reversible because body entropy decreases.

## Explanation

The second law constrains system and surroundings together. A body can have a negative change while its surroundings gain an equal or greater amount. Reversibility or irreversibility requires the total balance, not one part’s sign.
