A body with constant heat capacity $C=100\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$ changes from $300$ to $600\,\mathrm K$ without transitions. What is its entropy change? Use $\ln2\simeq0.693$.

- $100\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$.
- $30000\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$.
- $50\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$.
- $69.3\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$.

## Explanation

$\Delta S=C\ln(\dfrac{T_f}{T_i})=100\ln2\simeq69.3\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$. Heat is $30000\,\mathrm J$, but dividing only by $T_f$ gives 50 and by $T_i$ gives 100. The integral accounts for varying temperature. Estimates using a single endpoint temperature provide lower and upper bounds for the correct result.
