$16.7\,\mathrm{kJ}$ is supplied to $0.100\,\mathrm{kg}$ of pure ice at melting temperature and coexistence pressure. With $\ell_f=334\,\mathrm{kJ\,kg^{-1}}$, what is the final state?

- $0.100\,\mathrm{kg}$ of ice above melting temperature.
- $0.025\,\mathrm{kg}$ of ice and $0.075\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water at melting temperature.
- $0.050\,\mathrm{kg}$ of ice and $0.050\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water at melting temperature.
- $0.100\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water at melting temperature.

## Explanation

The melted mass is $m_f=\dfrac{Q}{\ell_f}=\dfrac{16.7}{334}=0.050\,\mathrm{kg}$. This is half the initial mass, so ice remains and equilibrium temperature stays at melting. Complete melting would require $33.4\,\mathrm{kJ}$.
