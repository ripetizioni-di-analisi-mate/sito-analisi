Two surfaces separated by vacuum exchange thermal energy without solid contacts. Which mechanism can transfer it across the space between them?

- Natural convection.
- Forced convection.
- Molecular conduction.
- Electromagnetic radiation.

## Explanation

Electromagnetic radiation propagates through vacuum. Conduction and convection require matter along the transfer path: microscopic interactions or bulk fluid transport, respectively. Solid contacts are explicitly excluded. The vacuum excludes energy transfer through collective motion of an intervening fluid.
