For reservoirs at $400\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ and $25\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$, a student proposes $\eta_C=1-\dfrac{25}{400}$. Which correction makes the expression valid?

- Invert the ratio of Celsius temperatures.
- Convert only the cold temperature to kelvin.
- Replace the denominator with $400-25$.
- Convert both temperatures to kelvin before taking their ratio.

## Explanation

The correct ratio is $\dfrac{T_c}{T_h}=\dfrac{298.15}{673.15}$. The additive conversion does not cancel in ratios as it does in differences. Efficiency remains dimensionless regardless of the consistent energy units used. Converting only one temperature would mix scales with different zero points within the same ratio.
