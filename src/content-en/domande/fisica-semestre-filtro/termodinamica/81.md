The working fluid of an irreversible engine completes a cycle and returns to its initial state. How do its entropy and total entropy change?

- Both changes are zero because the process is cyclic.
- The fluid’s $\Delta S$ is zero; total change is positive.
- The fluid’s $\Delta S$ is positive; total change is zero.
- The fluid’s $\Delta S$ is negative; total change is negative.

## Explanation

State function S returns to its initial fluid value regardless of cycle reversibility. Irreversibility appears in the total balance including reservoirs and other environmental effects. Any entropy generated within the fluid is transferred to the surroundings during the cycle rather than accumulating in the fluid; other irreversibilities can occur in external exchanges.
