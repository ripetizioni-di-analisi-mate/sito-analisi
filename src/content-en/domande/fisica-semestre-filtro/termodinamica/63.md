Which internal-energy statement explicitly requires the ideal-gas model with fixed amount and composition?

- Internal-energy change depends on endpoint states.
- Internal energy returns to its initial value over a complete cycle.
- Total energy is conserved in an isolated system.
- Between states at the same temperature, internal-energy change is zero.

## Explanation

Independence of U from volume at fixed temperature is an ideal-gas property. Real-gas interactions can also introduce density dependence. State-function behaviour and energy conservation have broader validity. The amount of gas must remain fixed: adding moles at the same temperature can increase total internal energy.
