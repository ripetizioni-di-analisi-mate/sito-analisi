Which model justifies using $C_V=\dfrac52R$ for an ideal diatomic gas?

- Active translation and rotation, negligible vibration.
- Any temperature, regardless of active modes.
- Only translation active, negligible rotation and vibration.
- Fully active translation, rotation and vibration.

## Explanation

With five active quadratic degrees of freedom, classical equipartition gives $C_V=\dfrac52R$. Vibrations add contributions when activated. With only three translational degrees active, $C_V=\dfrac32R$. Validity depends on the temperature range, not merely on how many atoms the molecule contains.
