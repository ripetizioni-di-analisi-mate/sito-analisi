Equal water masses at $20$ and $60\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ are mixed in an adiabatic vessel of negligible heat capacity. With constant $c$ and no transitions, what is the final temperature?

- $40\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$.
- $50\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$.
- $80\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$.
- $30\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$.

## Explanation

The heat capacities are equal, so $mc(t_f-20)+mc(t_f-60)=0$ gives $2t_f=80$. The mean is $40\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. Adding temperatures does not conserve energy; a different mean would require unequal heat capacities or additional exchanges.
