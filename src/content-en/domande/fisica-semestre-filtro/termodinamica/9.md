A gas expands mechanically reversibly at constant $2.0\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$ from $1.0$ to $3.0\,\mathrm L$. How much volume work does it do?

- $+400\,\mathrm J$.
- $-400\,\mathrm J$.
- $+600\,\mathrm J$.
- $+4.0\cdot10^5\,\mathrm J$.

## Explanation

$W=p(V_f-V_i)=2.0\cdot10^5\cdot2.0\cdot10^{-3}=400\,\mathrm J$. Expansion gives positive work. Use the volume increase; converting litres to cubic metres introduces $10^{-3}$. Using $V_f$ alone would give $600\,\mathrm J$, counting volume already occupied before expansion.
