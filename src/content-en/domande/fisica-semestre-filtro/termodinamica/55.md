A mechanically reversible cycle with only volume work runs clockwise on a diagram with horizontal volume and vertical pressure. What does its enclosed area indicate?

- Negative net work done by the system.
- Total absorbed heat without subtracting rejected heat.
- Positive internal-energy change over the cycle.
- Positive net work done by the system.

## Explanation

Clockwise traversal expands at a higher average pressure than compression, giving $\oint p\,dV>0$. The area gives net work, equal to net cycle heat. Internal energy instead returns to its initial value.
