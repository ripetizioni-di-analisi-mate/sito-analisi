In a steady state, a heat pump supplies $5\,\mathrm{kW}$ to a room while using $1\,\mathrm{kW}$ of electrical power, entirely converted to work in the model. How much power does it take from the cold source?

- $4\,\mathrm{kW}$.
- $0.20\,\mathrm{kW}$.
- $5\,\mathrm{kW}$.
- $6\,\mathrm{kW}$.

## Explanation

The steady balance is $P_h=P_c+P_{\mathrm{in}}$, so $P_c=5-1=4\,\mathrm{kW}$. The five kilowatts delivered include supplied work and transferred heat. A COP of five does not represent energy creation.
