A closed ideal gas expands isothermally against constant $p_{\mathrm{ext}}=100\,\mathrm{kPa}$ from $10$ to $20\,\mathrm L$. Volume work is the only work. What are work done and absorbed heat?

- $W=0$, $Q=1000\,\mathrm J$.
- $W=Q=2000\,\mathrm J$.
- $W=Q=1000\,\mathrm J$.
- $W=1000\,\mathrm J$, $Q=0$.

## Explanation

Work uses external pressure: $W=p_{\mathrm{ext}}\Delta V=100\cdot10=1000\,\mathrm J$, since $\mathrm{kPa\,L}=\mathrm J$. Isothermal ideal gas has $\Delta U=0$, giving $Q=W$. The logarithmic work formula would instead require a mechanically reversible path.
