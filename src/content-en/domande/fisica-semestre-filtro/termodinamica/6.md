How much heat melts $0.10\,\mathrm{kg}$ of ice already at melting temperature, with $\ell_f=3.34\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{J\,kg^{-1}}$?

- $0\,\mathrm J$.
- $3.34\,\mathrm{kJ}$.
- $334\,\mathrm{kJ}$.
- $33.4\,\mathrm{kJ}$.

## Explanation

Latent heat is required: $Q=m\ell_f=0.10\cdot3.34\cdot10^5=33400\,\mathrm J$. Constant temperature does not imply zero heat: the energy changes the phase. The term $mc\Delta T$ does not describe this transition.
