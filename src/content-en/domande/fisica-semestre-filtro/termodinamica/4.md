A body absorbs $600\,\mathrm J$ and warms by $3\,\mathrm K$, with constant heat capacity, without transitions or appreciable work. Which quantity can be determined without knowing its mass?

- Heat capacity: $200\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$.
- Specific heat capacity: $1800\,\mathrm{J\,kg^{-1}K^{-1}}$.
- Specific heat capacity: $200\,\mathrm{J\,kg^{-1}K^{-1}}$.
- Heat capacity: $1800\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$.

## Explanation

From $Q=C\Delta T$, $C=\dfrac{600}{3}=200\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$. This quantity concerns the whole body. Specific heat capacity also requires $m$, since $c=\dfrac{C}{m}$; the units distinguish the quantities.
