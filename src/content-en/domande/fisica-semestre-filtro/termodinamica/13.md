An ideal reservoir at $300\,\mathrm K$ absorbs $900\,\mathrm J$. What is its entropy change?

- $+3\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$.
- $-3\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$.
- $+270000\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$.
- $0\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$.

## Explanation

The reservoir maintains constant $T$, so $\Delta S=\dfrac{Q}{T}=\dfrac{900}{300}=3\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$. The sign is positive because heat enters. Constant temperature and constant entropy are different conditions. Multiplying heat by temperature would also produce units different from those of entropy.
