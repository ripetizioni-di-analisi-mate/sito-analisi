A body temperature of $37\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ corresponds to which absolute temperature?

- $236.15\,\mathrm K$.
- $310.15\,\mathrm K$.
- $273.15\,\mathrm K$.
- $37\,\mathrm K$.

## Explanation

Apply $T=t+273.15$, giving $T=37+273.15=310.15\,\mathrm K$. The offset is added when converting a Celsius temperature to kelvin. A temperature difference would instead have the same numerical value on both scales.
