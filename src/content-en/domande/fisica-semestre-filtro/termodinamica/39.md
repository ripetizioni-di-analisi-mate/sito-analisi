A positive thermal contact resistance is introduced between two series layers. The two unequal outer temperatures remain fixed. What changes in the steady state?

- Power decreases, but temperature remains continuous even at the contact.
- Power decreases and a temperature jump appears at the contact.
- Power increases because the contact concentrates heat.
- Power is unchanged and only the initial transient changes.

## Explanation

Total resistance becomes $R_1+R_2+R_c$. At fixed outer difference, $P=\dfrac{\Delta T}{R_1+R_2+R_c}$ decreases. The contact has temperature jump $\Delta T_c=PR_c$ while carrying the same power as both layers. A continuous contact temperature instead belongs to the limit where contact resistance is negligible.
