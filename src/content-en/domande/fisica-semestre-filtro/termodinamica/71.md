Two engines have the same efficiency. Is that sufficient to conclude that they produce equal mechanical power?

- Yes, provided both are cyclic.
- No: only fluid mass is needed, without energy data.
- No: absorbed thermal power is also needed.
- Yes: efficiency uniquely determines output watts.

## Explanation

The ratio $\eta=\dfrac{P_{\mathrm{useful}}}{P_{\mathrm{abs}}}$ does not separately fix either power. Engines of different sizes or frequencies can have equal efficiency. Output watts require absorbed power, or heat per cycle together with frequency.
