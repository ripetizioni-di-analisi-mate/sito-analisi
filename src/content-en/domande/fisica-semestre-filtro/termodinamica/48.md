A closed model receives $100\,\mathrm W$ from a heater and loses $30\,\mathrm W$ by radiation and $50\,\mathrm W$ by convection. It does no work. What is its rate of change of $U$?

- $+20\,\mathrm W$.
- $-20\,\mathrm W$.
- $+180\,\mathrm W$.
- $0\,\mathrm W$.

## Explanation

The power balance gives $\dfrac{dU}{dt}=100-30-50=20\,\mathrm W$. Losses leave the system and are subtracted. A steady energy balance would require incoming and outgoing powers to cancel; the data instead show energy accumulation.
