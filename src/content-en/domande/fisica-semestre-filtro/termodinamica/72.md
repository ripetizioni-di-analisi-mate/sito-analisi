A refrigerator removes $600\,\mathrm J$ from its compartment and receives $200\,\mathrm J$ of work per cycle. What are refrigerator COP and heat rejected to the surroundings?

- $3$ and $800\,\mathrm J$.
- $3$ and $400\,\mathrm J$.
- $\dfrac13$ and $600\,\mathrm J$.
- $4$ and $800\,\mathrm J$.

## Explanation

The refrigerator’s useful output is heat removed from cold: $\mathrm{COP}_R=\dfrac{Q_c}{W_{\mathrm{in}}}=\dfrac{600}{200}=3$. The balance gives $Q_h=Q_c+W_{\mathrm{in}}=800\,\mathrm J$. The ratio $\dfrac{800}{200}=4$ would describe heat-pump performance.
