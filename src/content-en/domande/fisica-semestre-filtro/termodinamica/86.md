For a reversible two-reservoir engine, which relation makes total entropy change per cycle zero, with $Q_h,Q_c>0$ denoting magnitudes?

- $\dfrac{Q_h}{T_c}=\dfrac{Q_c}{T_h}$.
- $\dfrac{Q_h}{T_h}+\dfrac{Q_c}{T_c}=0$.
- $\dfrac{Q_h}{T_h}=\dfrac{Q_c}{T_c}$.
- $Q_h=Q_c$ for any $T_h>T_c$.

## Explanation

The hot reservoir loses entropy $\dfrac{Q_h}{T_h}$, the cold gains $\dfrac{Q_c}{T_c}$, and the fluid returns to the same S. Reversibility requires $-\dfrac{Q_h}{T_h}+\dfrac{Q_c}{T_c}=0$. Hence $\dfrac{Q_c}{Q_h}=\dfrac{T_c}{T_h}$, determining Carnot efficiency. Adding two positive magnitudes without the released-heat sign would not represent the reservoirs’ entropy balance.
