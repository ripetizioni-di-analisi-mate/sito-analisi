A piston is moved very slowly, but sliding friction is present. Which assessment of reversibility is correct?

- The process is reversible if the final state is at equilibrium.
- Every slow process is automatically reversible.
- Reversing motion alone also restores the surroundings.
- Slowness does not eliminate frictional irreversibility.

## Explanation

The process can remain near mechanical equilibrium throughout, but friction dissipates energy and generates entropy. Reversibility requires restoring system and surroundings without residual effects. Returning to the starting position does not automatically undo the heating produced.
