In the grey model with unit view factor, a surface hotter than its surroundings changes emissivity from $0.40$ to $0.80$. Area and temperatures remain fixed. How does net radiative loss change?

- It is unchanged.
- It doubles.
- It halves.
- It quadruples.

## Explanation

At fixed temperatures and area, $P_{\mathrm{net}}$ is proportional to $\varepsilon$. The ratio is $\dfrac{0.80}{0.40}=2$. The fourth power concerns temperature, not emissivity; the same factor describes emission and absorption in the stated model.
