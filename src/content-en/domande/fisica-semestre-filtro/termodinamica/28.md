For $2.00\,\mathrm{kg}$ of a solid, $c(T)=1000+10(T-300)$ in $\mathrm{J\,kg^{-1}K^{-1}}$, with $T$ in kelvin. Without transitions, how much heat takes it from $300$ to $320\,\mathrm K$?

- $44\,\mathrm{kJ}$.
- $22\,\mathrm{kJ}$.
- $48\,\mathrm{kJ}$.
- $40\,\mathrm{kJ}$.

## Explanation

Since $c$ increases linearly from $1000$ to $1200$, its interval mean is $1100\,\mathrm{J\,kg^{-1}K^{-1}}$. Thus $Q=2.00\cdot1100\cdot20=44000\,\mathrm J$. Using only the initial or final value underestimates or overestimates the integral.
