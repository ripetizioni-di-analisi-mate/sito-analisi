A sample warms from $20$ to $35\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. What is its temperature change in kelvin?

- $308.15\,\mathrm K$.
- $288.15\,\mathrm K$.
- $15\,\mathrm K$.
- $55\,\mathrm K$.

## Explanation

$\Delta T=(35+273.15)-(20+273.15)=15\,\mathrm K$. The additive offset cancels in a difference. The value $308.15\,\mathrm K$ is the final temperature; adding $273.15$ directly to the rise would be an incorrect conversion.
