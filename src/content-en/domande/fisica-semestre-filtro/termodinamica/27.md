Hot water is poured into cold water in a calorimeter initially at the cold temperature. If the vessel’s positive heat capacity is incorrectly neglected, how does the predicted final temperature compare?

- It is too high compared with the full balance.
- It is unchanged because the vessel is adiabatic.
- It is too low compared with the full balance.
- It is always the arithmetic mean of initial temperatures.

## Explanation

The vessel warms with the cold water and absorbs part of the hot water’s released energy. Excluding it reduces the initially cold heat capacity and raises the calculated final temperature. External insulation does not eliminate energy storage in the vessel.
