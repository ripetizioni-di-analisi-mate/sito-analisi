A closed system absorbs $500\,\mathrm J$ and does $200\,\mathrm J$ of work. Neglecting macroscopic kinetic and potential energy changes, what is $\Delta U$?

- $-700\,\mathrm J$.
- $+700\,\mathrm J$.
- $+300\,\mathrm J$.
- $-300\,\mathrm J$.

## Explanation

With work done positive, $\Delta U=Q-W=500-200=300\,\mathrm J$. Some absorbed energy leaves as work. Adding both positive values would treat work done as work received.
