One mole of ideal gas, with constant molar heat capacities over the interval, in a rigid vessel receives $250\,\mathrm J$ and warms by $10\,\mathrm K$. Heat is the only energy exchange. Which molar quantity can be inferred from these data?

- $R=25\,\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}K^{-1}}$.
- $\gamma=25$.
- $C_V=25\,\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}K^{-1}}$.
- $C_p=25\,\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}K^{-1}}$.

## Explanation

The rigid constraint eliminates volume work, so $Q=\Delta U=nC_V\Delta T$. With one mole, $C_V=\dfrac{250}{1\cdot10}=25\,\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}K^{-1}}$. Measuring $C_p$ would require constant pressure; $\gamma$ is dimensionless. The amount in moles is essential for converting the whole sample’s heat capacity to a molar value.
