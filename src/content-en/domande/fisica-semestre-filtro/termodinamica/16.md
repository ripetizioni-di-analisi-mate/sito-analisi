Two samples are each in thermal equilibrium with the same thermometer. What follows from the zeroth law?

- They have equal pressures even in different vessels.
- They have the same temperature even if masses and pressures differ.
- They have equal heat capacities even if substances differ.
- They have equal total internal energy even if masses differ.

## Explanation

The zeroth law makes thermal equilibrium transitive. The comparison establishes a common temperature, not equal internal energy, heat capacity or pressure. Those quantities also depend on sample amount, composition and constraints.
