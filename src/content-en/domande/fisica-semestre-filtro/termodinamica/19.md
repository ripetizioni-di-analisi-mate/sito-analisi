A gauge reads $80\,\mathrm{kPa}$ above atmospheric pressure of $100\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Which pressure belongs in $pV=nRT$?

- $180\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
- $80\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
- $100\,\mathrm{kPa}$.
- $20\,\mathrm{kPa}$.

## Explanation

The gas law requires absolute pressure: $p=80+100=180\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Gauge pressure measures only the difference from atmosphere. Subtracting the pressures would reverse the meaning of a reading above atmospheric pressure. A zero gauge reading would correspond to atmospheric pressure, not to a vacuum.
