A reversible refrigerator operates between $270\,\mathrm K$ and $300\,\mathrm K$. What is its maximum COP, and how does it change if the hot source rises to $330\,\mathrm K$ with the cold source unchanged?

- It rises from $9$ to $18$.
- It falls from $10$ to $5.5$.
- It falls from $9$ to $4.5$.
- It rises from $0.10$ to about $0.18$.

## Explanation

$\mathrm{COP}_{R,C}=\dfrac{T_c}{T_h-T_c}$ gives $\dfrac{270}{30}=9$ and then $\dfrac{270}{60}=4.5$. A larger temperature lift requires more work for the same heat removed. Using $T_h$ in the numerator would give heat-pump COP.
