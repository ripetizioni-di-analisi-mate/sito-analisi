A homogeneous slab has area $2\,\mathrm{m^2}$, thickness $0.10\,\mathrm m$ and conductivity $0.50\,\mathrm{W\,m^{-1}K^{-1}}$. With a $20\,\mathrm K$ face temperature difference, insulated sides and steady conduction, what is the power?

- $400\,\mathrm W$.
- $200\,\mathrm W$.
- $20\,\mathrm W$.
- $2\,\mathrm W$.

## Explanation

$P=\dfrac{\kappa A\Delta T}{L}=\dfrac{0.50\cdot2\cdot20}{0.10}=200\,\mathrm W$. Thickness is in the denominator because a longer path provides greater thermal resistance. The result is power; energy also requires a duration.
