An isolated container holds two blocks initially at different temperatures, which are brought into contact. Which statement is correct?

- It is already in thermal equilibrium because it is isolated.
- Each block separately conserves its internal energy.
- Temperatures remain different because external heat is zero.
- It can approach equilibrium while conserving total energy.

## Explanation

Isolation prevents external exchanges but allows internal transfers. The hot block supplies energy to the cold block until their temperatures equalise. Total energy is conserved while total entropy increases during the irreversible process.
