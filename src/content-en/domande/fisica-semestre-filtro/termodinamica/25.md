To calculate moles using $R=8.314\,\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}K^{-1}}$, which units make $n=\dfrac{pV}{RT}$ consistent without further conversion?

- Pascals, cubic metres and Celsius.
- Pascals, cubic metres and kelvin.
- Pascals, litres and kelvin.
- Atmospheres, cubic metres and kelvin.

## Explanation

A joule equals $\mathrm{Pa\,m^3}$, so $pV$ must use these units with the specified $R$. Temperature must be absolute. Litres and atmospheres are possible only with a numerically consistent gas constant $R$.
