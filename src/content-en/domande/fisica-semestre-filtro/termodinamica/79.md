During a reversible ideal-gas adiabat, which combination is possible?

- Positive heat and constant entropy.
- Increasing entropy despite reversibility and zero heat.
- Constant entropy and changing temperature.
- Constant temperature and necessarily increasing entropy.

## Explanation

Reversibility permits $dS=\dfrac{\delta Q_{\mathrm{rev}}}{T}$. Adiabaticity gives $\delta Q_{\mathrm{rev}}=0$, so entropy is constant. Internal energy can nevertheless change through work and alter temperature: isentropic and isothermal are different constraints.
