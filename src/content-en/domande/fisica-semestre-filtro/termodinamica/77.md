The heat released by a reservoir to another system and the reservoir’s constant temperature are known. The combined system is isolated. Are these data sufficient to calculate total entropy production?

- No: the entropy change of the system receiving heat is missing.
- Yes: total entropy decreases by the same amount as the reservoir’s entropy.
- Yes: it is zero because released and absorbed heat cancel.
- Yes: it always equals the magnitude of the reservoir’s entropy change.

## Explanation

Signed heat and temperature determine the reservoir’s entropy change, but the total also requires the receiver’s change. Isolation requires a nonnegative sum; it does not specify its value. Reversible transfer can give zero total, whereas a finite temperature difference gives a positive total. Energy balance does not automatically imply entropy cancellation.
