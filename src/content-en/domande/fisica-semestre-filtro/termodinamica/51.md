An ideal gas expands into vacuum after a partition is removed inside an isolated rigid vessel. Which balance holds between the initial and final equilibrium states?

- $Q=W>0$ and $T_f=T_i$.
- $Q=0$, $W>0$ and $T_f<T_i$.
- $W=0$, $Q>0$ and $T_f>T_i$.
- $Q=W=\Delta U=0$ and $T_f=T_i$.

## Explanation

Vacuum exerts no opposing pressure, so free expansion transfers no external work. Insulation gives $Q=0$, hence $\Delta U=0$. For fixed-composition ideal gas, $U$ depends only on $T$, so final and initial temperatures coincide. The process remains irreversible.
