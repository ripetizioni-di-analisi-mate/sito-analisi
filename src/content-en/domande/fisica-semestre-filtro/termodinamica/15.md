A sealed cylinder has adiabatic walls and piston. The piston moves and transfers work. How is the gas classified?

- An isolated system because no heat enters.
- A closed system, but not isolated.
- An isolated system because moles are conserved.
- An open system because volume changes.

## Explanation

The boundary prevents matter transfer, so the system is closed. Work crosses the boundary and transfers energy, so the system is not energetically isolated. Adiabaticity concerns heat only; changing volume does not imply matter exchange.
