On the horizontal temperature–heat segment for a pure substance melting at equilibrium and fixed pressure, what changes as heat enters?

- The solid warms while the liquid stays at melting temperature.
- The liquid fraction stays fixed until all latent heat has been supplied.
- Temperature increases according to the mean specific heat of both phases.
- The liquid fraction increases at constant temperature.

## Explanation

At the specified pressure, the phases coexist at a determined temperature. Absorbed energy progressively changes solid into liquid. Once melting is complete, further input can raise liquid temperature; beforehand, phase fractions change continuously.
