In one cycle, an engine absorbs $1000\,\mathrm J$ and rejects $650\,\mathrm J$. What are its useful work and efficiency?

- $1650\,\mathrm J$ and $165\%$.
- $350\,\mathrm J$ and $35\%$.
- $650\,\mathrm J$ and $65\%$.
- $350\,\mathrm J$ and about $54\%$.

## Explanation

Over the cycle, $\Delta U=0$, so $W=Q_h-Q_c=350\,\mathrm J$. Efficiency divides by absorbed heat: $\eta=\dfrac{350}{1000}=0.35$. Dividing by rejected heat would define a different ratio.
