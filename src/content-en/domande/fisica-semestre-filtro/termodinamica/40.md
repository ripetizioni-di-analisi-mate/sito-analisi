Two parallel conduction paths connect the same reservoirs. Their resistances are $2$ and $3\,\mathrm{K\,W^{-1}}$, with a $30\,\mathrm K$ temperature difference. What is the total power?

- $15\,\mathrm W$.
- $150\,\mathrm W$.
- $6\,\mathrm W$.
- $25\,\mathrm W$.

## Explanation

Each path has the same temperature difference. Their powers are $\dfrac{30}{2}=15\,\mathrm W$ and $\dfrac{30}{3}=10\,\mathrm W$, totalling $25\,\mathrm W$. Adding resistances would describe series paths rather than the stated parallel arrangement.
