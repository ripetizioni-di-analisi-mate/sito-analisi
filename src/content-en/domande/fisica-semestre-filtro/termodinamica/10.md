During adiabatic compression of a closed system, work done by the gas is $W=-150\,\mathrm J$. There are no macroscopic kinetic or potential energy changes. How does $U$ change?

- It increases by $300\,\mathrm J$.
- It decreases by $150\,\mathrm J$.
- It is unchanged because $Q=0$.
- It increases by $150\,\mathrm J$.

## Explanation

Adiabaticity gives $Q=0$, hence $\Delta U=-W=+150\,\mathrm J$. Negative work done indicates energy received by the gas through compression. Zero heat does not imply constant internal energy. This balance does not require reversibility because total work has already been specified.
