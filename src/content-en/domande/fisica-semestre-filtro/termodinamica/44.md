A grey surface retains its area and emissivity while its absolute temperature doubles. How does emitted power alone change?

- It increases by a factor of 2.
- It increases by a factor of 4.
- It increases by a factor of 8.
- It increases by a factor of 16.

## Explanation

Emitted power is $\varepsilon\sigma AT^4$, so $(2T)^4=16T^4$. This uses kelvin and concerns emission. Net power would also subtract radiation absorbed from the surroundings, whose term has not doubled.
