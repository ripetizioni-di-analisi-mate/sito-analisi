In a closed cycle, the working fluid returns exactly to its initial state. Which equality must hold, neglecting macroscopic changes?

- $Q_{\mathrm{net}}=0$ in every cycle.
- $Q_h=Q_c$ for every heat engine.
- $Q_{\mathrm{net}}=W_{\mathrm{net}}$.
- $W_{\mathrm{net}}=0$ in every cycle.

## Explanation

State function $U$ returns to its initial value, so $0=Q_{\mathrm{net}}-W_{\mathrm{net}}$. Net heat and net work can both be nonzero. In an engine, absorbed heat exceeds rejected heat by the amount converted to work.
