Two layers conduct heat in series in a steady state with ideal contact and insulated sides. Which relation must hold at the interface?

- Incoming and outgoing powers are equal.
- Power decreases across the more resistant layer.
- Temperature gradients are always equal in both layers.
- Temperature drops across the two layers are equal even with different resistances.

## Explanation

A steady interface does not accumulate energy: incoming power must leave. Gradients and temperature drops across individual layers can differ because they depend on conductivity and resistance. Temperature is continuous at ideal contact. Power loss along a layer without lateral exchange would imply energy accumulation.
