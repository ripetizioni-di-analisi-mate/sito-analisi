A grey surface and its large surroundings have equal temperatures. In the balanced radiative-exchange model, which statement is correct?

- Emission and absorption continue with zero net power.
- Absorption stops while emission continues.
- Net outward power still equals emitted power alone.
- Surface emission stops completely.

## Explanation

In $P_{\mathrm{net}}=\varepsilon\sigma A(T^4-T_a^4)$, equal temperatures cancel the difference. Both radiative fluxes remain present. Equilibrium concerns the net balance rather than absent microscopic exchanges. If only one flux stopped, the surface would gain or lose energy and would no longer be in equilibrium.
