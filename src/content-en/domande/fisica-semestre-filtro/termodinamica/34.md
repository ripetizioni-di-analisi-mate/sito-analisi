Two models produce the same sweat mass at the same temperature. All evaporates in the first; half evaporates and half drips away in the second. Neglecting other exchanges, how do latent heat losses compare?

- It is equal because produced masses are equal.
- It is zero in the second because some sweat drips away.
- It is half as large in the second.
- It is twice as large in the second because there are two paths.

## Explanation

The latent contribution is $Q=m_{\mathrm{evap}}\ell_v$. At fixed $\ell_v$, halving the actually evaporated mass halves that contribution. Dripping liquid can transfer energy through other mechanisms, excluded here, but does not provide the latent contribution being counted.
