Pure vapour condenses at equilibrium at its condensation temperature and specified pressure. What is the sample’s heat sign and relevant energy contribution?

- $Q=mc\Delta T$: sensible cooling is sufficient.
- $Q=0$: temperature is constant.
- $Q=-m\ell_v$: the sample releases latent heat.
- $Q=+m\ell_v$: the sample absorbs latent heat.

## Explanation

Condensation reverses vaporisation. With $\ell_v>0$ defined for vaporisation and absorbed heat positive, the sample has $Q=-m\ell_v$. Coexistence temperature stays constant while the condensed fraction changes.
