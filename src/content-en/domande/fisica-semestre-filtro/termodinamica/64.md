One mole of ideal gas with constant $C_V=20\,\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}K^{-1}}$ changes adiabatically from $400$ to $350\,\mathrm K$. What total work does the gas do, neglecting macroscopic changes?

- $-1000\,\mathrm J$.
- $+1000\,\mathrm J$.
- $0\,\mathrm J$.
- $+7000\,\mathrm J$.

## Explanation

$\Delta U=nC_V(T_f-T_i)=1\cdot20\cdot(-50)=-1000\,\mathrm J$. Since $Q=0$, $W=-\Delta U=1000\,\mathrm J$. This endpoint balance does not require imposing the reversible law $pV^\gamma=\text{constant}$. The energy used for work comes here from cooling the gas rather than from incoming heat.
