One mole of ideal gas doubles its volume through free expansion in an isolated vessel. What is $\Delta S$ between equilibrium states?

- $0$ because $Q=0$.
- $R\ln2>0$.
- $-R\ln2$.
- $2R$.

## Explanation

The ideal gas has equal endpoint temperatures, so $\Delta S=nR\ln(\dfrac{V_f}{V_i})=R\ln2$. Actual heat is zero, but the process is irreversible: using $\dfrac{Q_{\mathrm{actual}}}{T}$ would miss internally generated entropy.
