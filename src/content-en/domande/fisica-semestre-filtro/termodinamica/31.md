To change ice at $-10\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ into liquid water at $20\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$, which contributions are required at atmospheric pressure without heat loss?

- Heat the ice to $20\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ using its specific heat throughout.
- Heat ice and water without latent heat.
- Melt the ice and heat the water, without initial heating.
- Heat the ice, melt it and heat the water.

## Explanation

The path includes $mc_g\cdot10$, $m\ell_f$ and $mc_a\cdot20$. Each phase uses its own specific heat. Latent heat is required despite constant temperature during melting, and initially cold ice also requires the first contribution.
