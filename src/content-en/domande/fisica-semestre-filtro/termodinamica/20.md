An ideal gas sealed in a rigid vessel warms from $27$ to $54\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. Which prediction agrees with the equation of state?

- Pressure stays constant because volume is fixed.
- Pressure doubles.
- Pressure rises by about $9\%$.
- Pressure halves.

## Explanation

At fixed $n,V$, $\dfrac{p_f}{p_i}=\dfrac{T_f}{T_i}=\dfrac{327.15}{300.15}\simeq1.09$. Doubling Celsius readings does not double absolute temperature. Constant volume makes pressure proportional to $T$, not unchanging. The comparison also requires unchanged amount of substance, ensured by the sealed vessel.
