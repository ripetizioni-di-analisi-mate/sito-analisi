A closed system does $W=120\,\mathrm J$ of work and its internal energy decreases by $50\,\mathrm J$. Neglecting macroscopic changes, what heat does it exchange?

- It absorbs $170\,\mathrm J$.
- It absorbs $70\,\mathrm J$.
- It releases $170\,\mathrm J$.
- It releases $70\,\mathrm J$.

## Explanation

From $\Delta U=Q-W$, $Q=\Delta U+W=-50+120=70\,\mathrm J$. Internal energy can fall while heat enters if outgoing work is larger. The sign of $\Delta U$ alone does not determine the heat sign.
