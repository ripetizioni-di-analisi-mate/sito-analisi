For a homogeneous slab in steady conduction, both area and thickness double while conductivity and temperature difference stay fixed. How does power change?

- It is unchanged.
- It quadruples.
- It doubles.
- It halves.

## Explanation

The law $P=\dfrac{\kappa A\Delta T}{L}$ contains the area-to-thickness ratio. After the change, $\dfrac{2A}{2L}=\dfrac{A}{L}$, so power is unchanged. Considering only one change would give a factor of two or one half.
