$0.100\,\mathrm{kg}$ of ice melts reversibly at $273.15\,\mathrm K$, with $\ell_f=334\,\mathrm{kJ\,kg^{-1}}$. Approximately what is the sample’s entropy change?

- $+122\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$.
- $0\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$.
- $-122\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$.
- $+0.122\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$.

## Explanation

Absorbed reversible heat is $Q=m\ell_f=33400\,\mathrm J$. At coexistence temperature, $\Delta S=\dfrac{Q}{T}=\dfrac{33400}{273.15}\simeq122\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$. The sign is positive for melting; converting kilojoules to joules avoids a factor-of-one-thousand error.
