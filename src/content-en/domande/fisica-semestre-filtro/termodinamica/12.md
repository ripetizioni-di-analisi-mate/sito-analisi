What is the efficiency limit for a cyclic engine exchanging heat only with reservoirs at $600\,\mathrm K$ and $300\,\mathrm K$?

- $50\%$.
- $100\%$.
- $75\%$.
- About $33\%$.

## Explanation

The reversible limit is $\eta_C=1-\dfrac{T_c}{T_h}=1-\dfrac{300}{600}=0.50$. An irreversible engine between the same reservoirs has lower efficiency. A temperature sum in the denominator does not represent Carnot’s theorem.
