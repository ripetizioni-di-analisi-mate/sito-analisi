An ideal monatomic gas with $\gamma=\dfrac53$ is compressed adiabatically and reversibly from $V_i$ to $\dfrac{V_i}{8}$. What is $\dfrac{T_f}{T_i}$?

- $2$.
- $8$.
- $4$.
- $\dfrac14$.

## Explanation

From $TV^{\gamma-1}=\text{constant}$, $\dfrac{T_f}{T_i}=(\dfrac{V_i}{V_f})^{\gamma-1}=8^{\dfrac23}=4$. The exponent is $\gamma-1$, not $\gamma$, and the ratio exceeds one during compression. The cube root of eight is two, which is then squared.
