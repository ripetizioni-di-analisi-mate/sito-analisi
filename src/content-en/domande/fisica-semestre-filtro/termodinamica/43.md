In the convection model $P=hA(T_s-T_f)$, what are the units of $h$?

- $\mathrm{W\,m^{-2}K^{-1}}$.
- $\mathrm{J\,kg^{-1}K^{-1}}$.
- $\mathrm{K\,W^{-1}}$.
- $\mathrm{W\,m^{-1}K^{-1}}$.

## Explanation

Isolating $h=\dfrac{P}{A\Delta T}$ divides watts by square metres and kelvin. Conductivity instead has units $\mathrm{W\,m^{-1}K^{-1}}$ because the conduction law also includes thickness. The convection coefficient depends on fluid motion.
