A vertical segment on a pressure–volume diagram describes heating a closed ideal gas. With only volume work, which statement holds?

- $W>0$, while $\Delta U=0$.
- $W=0$, while $Q$ and $\Delta U$ are positive.
- $W=Q=\Delta U=0$.
- $Q=0$, while $W$ is negative.

## Explanation

The vertical segment has constant volume, so $dV=0$ and volume work is zero. Heating raises ideal-gas temperature and internal energy; the first law gives $Q=\Delta U>0$. Rising pressure produces no volume work without boundary displacement.
