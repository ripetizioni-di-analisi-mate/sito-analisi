A reversible cyclic heat engine exchanges heat with only two reservoirs. It has efficiency $0.40$ and a cold reservoir at $300\,\mathrm K$. What is its hot-reservoir temperature?

- $750\,\mathrm K$.
- $420\,\mathrm K$.
- $180\,\mathrm K$.
- $500\,\mathrm K$.

## Explanation

From $\eta=1-\dfrac{T_c}{T_h}$, $T_h=\dfrac{T_c}{1-\eta}=\dfrac{300}{0.60}=500\,\mathrm K$. Dividing by efficiency instead of its complement would give 750 K. The correct result exceeds 300 K, as required for a heat engine.
