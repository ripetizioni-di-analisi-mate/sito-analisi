In Boltzmann’s model, the number of equally probable microstates changes from $\Omega$ to $4\Omega$. What is the entropy change?

- $3k_B\ln\Omega$.
- $k_B\ln4$.
- $\dfrac{k_B}{4}$.
- $4k_B\ln\Omega$.

## Explanation

$\Delta S=k_B\ln(4\Omega)-k_B\ln\Omega=k_B\ln4$. Multiplying microstate count produces an additive logarithmic increase. It does not multiply initial entropy by four. The change depends on the multiplicative increase in microstates and does not require knowing the initial multiplicity.
