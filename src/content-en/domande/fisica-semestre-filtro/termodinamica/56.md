A mechanically reversible cycle with only volume work follows $A=(1\,\mathrm L,100\,\mathrm{kPa})$, $B=(3\,\mathrm L,100\,\mathrm{kPa})$, $C=(3\,\mathrm L,300\,\mathrm{kPa})$ and returns to A along a straight line. What is the net work done?

- $+200\,\mathrm J$.
- $-200\,\mathrm J$.
- $-400\,\mathrm J$.
- $0\,\mathrm J$.

## Explanation

AB gives $100\cdot2=200\,\mathrm J$, BC gives zero, and CA gives $\dfrac{300+100}{2}(1-3)=-400\,\mathrm J$. The sum is $-200\,\mathrm J$, consistent with counterclockwise traversal and triangular area $\dfrac12\cdot2\cdot200=200\,\mathrm J$.
