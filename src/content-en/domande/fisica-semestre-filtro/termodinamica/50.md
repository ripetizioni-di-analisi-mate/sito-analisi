A gas absorbs $40\,\mathrm J$ of heat. During the same process it expands and does $70\,\mathrm J$ of work on a piston. A stirrer does $50\,\mathrm J$ of work on the gas. What is the change in internal energy?

- $+20\,\mathrm J$
- $-80\,\mathrm J$
- $+160\,\mathrm J$
- $-20\,\mathrm J$

## Explanation

Heat input and work received from the stirrer add energy to the gas, while work on the piston removes it. The balance is $40+50-70=20\,\mathrm J$.
