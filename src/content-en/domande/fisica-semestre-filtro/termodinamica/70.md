Two engines operate between the same two reservoirs and absorb equal heat per cycle from the hot source. One is reversible; the other has friction. How do their obtainable work outputs compare?

- The frictional engine produces more work by converting dissipation.
- The reversible engine produces more work per cycle.
- Work outputs coincide because absorbed heat is equal.
- The reversible engine must reject all absorbed heat.

## Explanation

Between two reservoirs, reversible efficiency is maximal. At equal $Q_h$, $W=\eta Q_h$ is therefore greater in the reversible limit. Friction generates entropy and reduces available useful work without creating additional energy.
