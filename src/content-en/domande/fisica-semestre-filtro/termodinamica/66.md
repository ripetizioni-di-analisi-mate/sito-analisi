Which device directly violates the Kelvin–Planck statement?

- A cyclic engine converting all heat from one reservoir into work as its sole effect.
- A gas expanding isothermally once while doing work.
- A refrigerator transferring heat to hot surroundings while receiving external work.
- A cyclic engine absorbing heat and rejecting part to a colder reservoir.

## Explanation

The statement concerns cyclic operation with no other effects. A single expansion changes the gas state and therefore is not the forbidden cycle by itself. A two-reservoir engine can produce work while rejecting some heat.
