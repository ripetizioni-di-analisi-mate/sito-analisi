An ideal gas has molar heat capacity $C_V=\dfrac32R$. What is $C_p$?

- $\dfrac12R$.
- $3R$.
- $\dfrac52R$.
- $\dfrac32R$.

## Explanation

For an ideal gas, $C_p-C_V=R$, giving $C_p=\dfrac32R+R=\dfrac52R$. At constant pressure, heat also supplies expansion work, explaining why $C_p$ exceeds $C_V$. Their ratio is different from their difference.
