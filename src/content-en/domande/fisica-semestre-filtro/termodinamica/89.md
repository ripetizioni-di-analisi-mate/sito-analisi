To make heat exchange between a body and a sequence of reservoirs reversible, which limit must be approached?

- Very large temperature differences to shorten the process.
- Zero heat transfer in every possible reversible transformation.
- Infinitesimal temperature differences at each exchange.
- A single reservoir at the final body temperature in every case.

## Explanation

A finite temperature difference generates entropy as heat flows. In the reversible limit, the body exchanges heat with reservoirs differing infinitesimally from its temperature. Total heat can be nonzero: the driving differences and dissipative effects must tend to zero.
