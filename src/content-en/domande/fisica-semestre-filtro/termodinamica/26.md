A material’s mass is doubled at unchanged composition, phase and initial temperature. In the constant-specific-heat model, how do heat capacity and specific heat capacity change?

- Heat capacity doubles; specific heat capacity halves.
- Heat capacity is unchanged; specific heat capacity doubles.
- Heat capacity doubles; specific heat capacity is unchanged.
- Both double.

## Explanation

The relation $C=mc$ includes mass in the whole sample’s heat capacity. At fixed material properties, $c$ is unchanged and doubling $m$ doubles $C$. The same temperature rise therefore needs twice the heat.
