A closed monatomic ideal gas starts at $300\,\mathrm K$ and doubles its volume in an adiabatic expansion with internal irreversibility. It does strictly positive volume work, with no other work or macroscopic energy changes. With constant $C_V=\dfrac32R$, which final temperature is compatible with both laws?

- $210\,\mathrm K$.
- $150\,\mathrm K$.
- $\dfrac{300\,\mathrm K}{2^{\dfrac23}}$.
- $300\,\mathrm K$.

## Explanation

From $Q=0$ and $W>0$, $\Delta U=-W<0$, hence $T_f<300\,\mathrm K$. Adiabaticity requires $\Delta S\ge0$; here irreversibility requires $\Delta S=nR\left[\dfrac32\ln\dfrac{T_f}{300\,\mathrm K}+\ln2\right]>0$. Thus $T_f>\dfrac{300\,\mathrm K}{2^{\dfrac23}}\simeq189\,\mathrm K$. Only $210\,\mathrm K$ meets both constraints: $150\,\mathrm K$ would require decreasing entropy, the exact limit of about $189\,\mathrm K$ would give zero entropy production, and $300\,\mathrm K$ would give zero work.
