Two independent subsystems have multiplicities $\Omega_A$ and $\Omega_B$. Which relation explains entropy additivity?

- $\Omega_{AB}=\Omega_A\Omega_B$ and $S_{AB}=S_AS_B$.
- $\Omega_{AB}=\Omega_A\Omega_B$ and $\ln\Omega_{AB}=\ln\Omega_A+\ln\Omega_B$.
- $\Omega_{AB}=\Omega_A+\Omega_B$ and the logarithm distributes over addition.
- $\Omega_{AB}=\dfrac{\Omega_A}{\Omega_B}$ and entropies add.

## Explanation

Every A microstate can combine with every B microstate, so the counts multiply. The logarithm of a product is a sum of logarithms; multiplying by the same Boltzmann constant gives the sum of entropies. Subsystem independence is the assumption used in counting.
