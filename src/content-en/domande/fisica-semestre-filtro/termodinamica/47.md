A surface at $280\,\mathrm K$ is completely surrounded by large surroundings at $300\,\mathrm K$. In the grey model, what does the sign of $P_{\mathrm{net}}=\varepsilon\sigma A(T^4-T_a^4)$ indicate?

- Net power leaves the surface.
- The surface has negative electromagnetic emission.
- Net power enters the surface.
- Net exchange is zero because the surroundings maintain their own temperature.

## Explanation

Since $T<T_a$, $T^4-T_a^4$ is negative. Outward power was defined positive, so this sign indicates net absorption. Emitted power remains positive but is smaller than absorbed power. Constant ambient temperature does not prevent the surroundings from transferring energy to the colder surface.
