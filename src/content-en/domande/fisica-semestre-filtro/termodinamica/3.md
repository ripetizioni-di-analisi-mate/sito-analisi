How much heat does $0.20\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water absorb for a $10\,\mathrm K$ rise, with constant $c=4200\,\mathrm{J\,kg^{-1}K^{-1}}$ and no transitions?

- $42\,\mathrm{kJ}$.
- $0.84\,\mathrm{kJ}$.
- $84\,\mathrm{kJ}$.
- $8.4\,\mathrm{kJ}$.

## Explanation

$Q=mc\Delta T=0.20\cdot4200\cdot10=8400\,\mathrm J=8.4\,\mathrm{kJ}$. Mass must be in kilograms for the given $c$. Using only $c\Delta T$ would omit the sample’s amount of water. The calculated heat concerns the whole sample, whereas specific heat capacity is defined per unit mass.
