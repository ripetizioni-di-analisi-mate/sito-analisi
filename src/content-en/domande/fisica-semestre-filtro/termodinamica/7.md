One mole of ideal gas is at $300\,\mathrm K$ and absolute pressure $10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Using $R=8.314\,\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}K^{-1}}$, what volume does it occupy?

- About $2.49\,\mathrm L$.
- About $24.9\,\mathrm L$.
- About $0.0249\,\mathrm L$.
- About $249\,\mathrm L$.

## Explanation

$V=\dfrac{nRT}{p}=0.024942\,\mathrm{m^3}$. Each cubic metre contains one thousand litres, giving about $24.9\,\mathrm L$. The number $0.0249$ represents cubic metres rather than litres. Pressure is already absolute, so atmospheric pressure must not be added again.
