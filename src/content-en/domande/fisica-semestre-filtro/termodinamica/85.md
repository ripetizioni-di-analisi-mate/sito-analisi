Two bodies have equal constant heat capacity $C$ and temperatures $300$ and $500\,\mathrm K$. They contact each other in an isolated enclosure without transitions or work. Which expression gives total final minus initial entropy?

- $C\ln\dfrac{16}{15}>0$.
- $2C\ln\dfrac53$.
- $0$ because total heat is zero.
- $C\ln\dfrac{15}{16}<0$.

## Explanation

Energy balance gives $T_f=400\,\mathrm K$. The sum is $C\ln(\dfrac{400}{300})+C\ln(\dfrac{400}{500})=C\ln(\dfrac{160000}{150000})=C\ln(\dfrac{16}{15})>0$. The heats cancel, while entropy changes have different weights during transfer. The cold portion’s contribution exceeds the magnitude of the hot portion’s entropy loss.
