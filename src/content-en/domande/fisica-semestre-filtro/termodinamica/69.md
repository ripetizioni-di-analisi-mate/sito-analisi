A cyclic engine exchanges heat only with reservoirs at $600$ and $300\,\mathrm K$. It claims $Q_h=1000\,\mathrm J$ and $W=600\,\mathrm J$ per cycle. Which assessment is correct?

- Only the first law is violated because there is insufficient energy for work.
- The engine is reversible because its efficiency is positive.
- Both laws hold because work is less than absorbed heat.
- The first law can be balanced, but the second is violated.

## Explanation

The first law would give $Q_c=1000-600=400\,\mathrm J$. However, $\eta=0.60$ exceeds $\eta_C=1-\dfrac{300}{600}=0.50$. Energy conservation is necessary but insufficient for thermodynamic feasibility. Rejected heat must also be large enough to satisfy the entropy balance of the two reservoirs.
