Which additional assumption permits $pV^\gamma=\text{constant}$ for an ideal gas of fixed composition and heat capacities, beyond $Q=0$ and volume work only?

- Temperature remains constant throughout.
- External pressure is zero.
- The process is reversible.
- Final volume is twice initial volume.

## Explanation

The derivation uses $dU=-p\,dV$ along equilibrium states, with gas pressure usable in the work integral: a reversible adiabat. Zero heat alone is insufficient. For example, ideal free expansion has equal endpoint temperatures $T$ and is not described by the $\gamma$ relation.
