$1200\,\mathrm J$ passes from a reservoir at $600\,\mathrm K$ to one at $300\,\mathrm K$ with no other effects. How much does total entropy increase?

- $0\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$.
- $-2\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$.
- $6\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$.
- $2\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$.

## Explanation

The hot reservoir loses $\dfrac{1200}{600}=2\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$; the cold one gains $\dfrac{1200}{300}=4\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$. The signed sum is $-2+4=2\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$. Transferred energies are equal, but entropy received at lower temperature is larger.
