A closed ideal gas is compressed isothermally and reversibly, with only volume work. Taking work done as positive, what are the signs of work and heat?

- $W>0$ and $Q>0$.
- $W<0$ and $Q>0$.
- $W<0$ and $Q<0$.
- $W>0$ and $Q<0$.

## Explanation

Compression has $V_f<V_i$, giving $\ln(\dfrac{V_f}{V_i})<0$ and $W<0$. Isothermal ideal gas has $\Delta U=0$, so $Q=W<0$. The gas releases heat to balance received work and maintain temperature.
