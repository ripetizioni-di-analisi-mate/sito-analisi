Two processes take the same closed system from state A to state B. If $Q_1-Q_2=80\,\mathrm J$, what relation holds between work done?

- $\Delta U_1-\Delta U_2=80\,\mathrm J$.
- $W_1-W_2=-80\,\mathrm J$.
- $W_1-W_2=80\,\mathrm J$.
- $W_1=W_2$ because the states coincide.

## Explanation

Internal energy changes coincide, so $Q_1-W_1=Q_2-W_2$. Rearranging gives $W_1-W_2=Q_1-Q_2=80\,\mathrm J$. Individual heat and work values may depend on the path even though their difference is fixed.
