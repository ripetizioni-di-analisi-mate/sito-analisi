External pressure on liquid water is moderately reduced, remaining where liquid is stable. How does boiling temperature change?

- It always remains at 100 °C regardless of pressure.
- It decreases in the same ratio as pressure over any interval.
- It rises because molecules experience lower external pressure.
- It falls because a lower vapour pressure is required.

## Explanation

Boiling occurs when vapour pressure equals external pressure. A lower external pressure is matched at a lower temperature. The dependence is not generally proportional, and the 100 °C reference requires particular pressure conditions.
