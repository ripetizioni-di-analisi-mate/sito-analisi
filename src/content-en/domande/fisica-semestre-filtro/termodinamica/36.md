Two samples without transitions have straight $T(Q)$ curves. Sample A’s slope is twice sample B’s. How do their heat capacities compare?

- $C_A=C_B$.
- $C_A=4C_B$.
- $C_A=2C_B$.
- $C_A=\dfrac12C_B$.

## Explanation

From $Q=C\Delta T$, $\Delta T=\dfrac{Q}{C}$: with $T$ on the vertical axis, slope is $\dfrac{1}{C}$. A sample warming more for the same energy has smaller heat capacity. Confusing the axes reverses the ratio.
