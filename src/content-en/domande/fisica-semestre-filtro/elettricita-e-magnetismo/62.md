An ideal $6\,\mathrm V$ source has its terminals disconnected from any load. Which current-voltage pair describes the open circuit?

- Zero current and zero voltage between terminals.
- Zero current and $6\,\mathrm V$ between terminals.
- $6\,\mathrm A$ current and zero voltage.
- Infinite current and $6\,\mathrm V$ voltage.

## Explanation

Without a closed path there is no sustained current in the external circuit, but the source maintains the potential separation. Zero current does not imply zero potential difference. Current cannot be inferred by reusing a number stated in volts: the quantities and units differ.
