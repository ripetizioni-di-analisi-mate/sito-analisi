Two positive particles A and B enter the same nonzero uniform electric field with no other forces. A has mass $m$ and charge $q$; B has mass $4m$ and charge $2q$. What is $\dfrac{a_B}{a_A}$?

- $2$.
- $8$.
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
- $4$.

## Explanation

Acceleration depends on the charge-to-mass ratio: $\dfrac{a_B}{a_A}=\dfrac{\dfrac{2q}{4m}}{\dfrac{q}{m}}=\dfrac{1}{2}$. Force on B is twice as large, but its mass is four times greater, so acceleration is smaller. Comparing charges alone would confuse force with acceleration.
