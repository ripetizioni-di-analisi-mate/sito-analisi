At a node, $2.4\,\mathrm A$ enters; $0.7\,\mathrm A$ and $0.9\,\mathrm A$ leave through two branches. In steady conditions, what current leaves through the third branch?

- $4.0\,\mathrm A$.
- $2.4\,\mathrm A$.
- $0.8\,\mathrm A$.
- $1.6\,\mathrm A$.

## Explanation

The node does not accumulate charge with time: $2.4=0.7+0.9+I$, hence $I=0.8\,\mathrm A$. The stated directions distinguish inflow from outflow. Adding all magnitudes without tracking direction would violate charge conservation at the node.
