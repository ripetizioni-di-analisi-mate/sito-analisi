For a uniform wire, $\rho_e=\dfrac{RA}{L}$. What SI unit must resistivity have?

- $\Omega\,\mathrm m$.
- $\Omega$.
- $\Omega\,\mathrm{m^2}$.
- $\dfrac{\Omega}{\mathrm m}$.

## Explanation

Cross-sectional area has units $\mathrm{m^2}$ and length has units $\mathrm m$, so $\dfrac{RA}{L}$ has units $\Omega\,\mathrm m$. Resistivity and resistance differ: the former characterizes the material under given conditions, whereas the latter also includes specimen geometry.
