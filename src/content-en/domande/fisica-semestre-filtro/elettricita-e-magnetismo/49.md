In a solution, cations move right and anions move left. How do they contribute to conventional current?

- Their contributions are always opposite and cancel.
- Both contribute leftwards.
- Only cations contribute because anions are negative.
- Both contribute rightwards.

## Explanation

Conventional current follows positive-charge motion and opposes negative-charge motion. The two stated transports therefore contribute in the same direction despite opposite velocities. Equal ion counts, if present, would not imply cancellation of current.
