A constant current transports $0.60\,\mathrm C$ in $3.0\,\mathrm s$. What is its magnitude?

- $0.020\,\mathrm A$.
- $1.8\,\mathrm A$.
- $0.20\,\mathrm A$.
- $5.0\,\mathrm A$.

## Explanation

Current measures charge per time: $I=\dfrac{\Delta q}{\Delta t}=\dfrac{0.60}{3.0}=0.20\,\mathrm A$. The dimensional check is $\mathrm{C/s}=\mathrm A$. Multiplying charge by time or reversing the ratio does not yield the requested quantity; this value is not the carriers’ drift speed.
