Charges $+Q$ and $-Q$ lie respectively to the left and right of their midpoint P. What is the direction of the total field at P?

- To the right, because the two contributions add.
- It is zero because the charges sum to zero.
- To the left, because the negative sign dominates.
- Perpendicular to the joining line because the magnitudes are equal.

## Explanation

The positive charge’s field points away from it, hence rightwards at P. The negative charge’s field points towards its source, also rightwards. The sum of charges alone does not determine the field at a point; positions and vector addition are also required.
