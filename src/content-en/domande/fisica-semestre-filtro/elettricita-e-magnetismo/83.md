A charged particle initially has nonzero velocity components both parallel and perpendicular to a uniform magnetic field. No other forces act. Which description is correct in the nonrelativistic regime?

- It follows a circle because the parallel component vanishes.
- It follows a helix with constant parallel component and constant total speed.
- It exactly follows a straight field line.
- It accelerates along the field with continuously increasing speed.

## Explanation

Magnetic force depends on the perpendicular component and rotates it, while leaving the parallel component unchanged. Combining circular motion and uniform straight-line motion produces a helix. The force does no work, so total speed remains constant.
