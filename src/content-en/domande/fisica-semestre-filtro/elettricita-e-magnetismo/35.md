An ion of charge $+2e$ gains kinetic energy while crossing a potential decrease of $150\,\mathrm V$, with no other work. How much does it gain in electronvolts?

- $-300\,\mathrm{eV}$.
- $150\,\mathrm{eV}$.
- $300\,\mathrm{eV}$.
- $75\,\mathrm{eV}$.

## Explanation

The gain is $\Delta K=q(V_i-V_f)=2e\cdot150\,\mathrm V=300\,\mathrm{eV}$. An electronvolt is an energy unit, not the crossed voltage: the number of elementary charges must be included. The sign is positive because the positive ion loses potential energy.
