An ohmic $30\,\Omega$ resistor is connected to an ideal $12\,\mathrm V$ source. What current flows at constant temperature?

- $0.040\,\mathrm A$.
- $360\,\mathrm A$.
- $0.40\,\mathrm A$.
- $2.5\,\mathrm A$.

## Explanation

Solve Ohm’s law for current: $I=\dfrac{\Delta V}{R}=\dfrac{12}{30}=0.40\,\mathrm A$. The source imposes voltage and the resistor determines current. The inverse ratio and the product have the wrong units; constant temperature makes use of the specified resistance consistent.
