A proton starts with kinetic energy $100\,\mathrm{eV}$ and moves in a straight line towards increasing potential in a sufficiently extensive uniform electric field parallel to its path. With no other work, what maximum potential increase can it reach before stopping?

- $100\,\mathrm V$.
- No finite limit, because the charge is positive.
- $50\,\mathrm V$.
- $200\,\mathrm V$.

## Explanation

At the stopping point, all initial kinetic energy has become potential energy: $e\Delta V=100\,\mathrm{eV}$, hence $\Delta V=100\,\mathrm V$. A larger increase would require negative kinetic energy in the balance, showing that the point cannot be reached under the specified forces alone.
