Two equal positive charges are equidistant from their midpoint P. With zero potential at infinity, which pair describes P?

- Nonzero field and zero potential.
- Zero field and positive potential.
- Zero field and zero potential.
- Nonzero field and negative potential.

## Explanation

The fields are equal and opposite vectors, so they cancel. Potentials are positive scalars and add: $V=\dfrac{2k_eQ}{r}>0$. A zero field at one point does not require zero potential; field and potential require different addition operations.
