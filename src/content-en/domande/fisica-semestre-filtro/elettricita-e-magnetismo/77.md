An electron moves right in a magnetic field directed into the page. What is the magnetic force direction?

- Downwards in the page.
- Upwards in the page.
- Out of the page.
- Rightwards in the page.

## Explanation

For a positive charge, rightward velocity and inward field would give upward force from $\vec v\times\vec B$. The electron is negatively charged, so reverse that direction and the force points downwards. It must be perpendicular to both velocity and field.
