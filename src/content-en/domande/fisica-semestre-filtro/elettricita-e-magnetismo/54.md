An experiment aims to test whether a metal wire follows an ohmic law at fixed temperature. Which procedure addresses the main possible confounder?

- Change both length and voltage at every measurement.
- Control heating while measuring current and voltage.
- Replace current measurement with the wire’s initial total charge.
- Use only one voltage-current measurement.

## Explanation

Current can heat the wire and change resistivity. Testing proportionality under fixed conditions requires several measurement pairs without significant temperature or geometry changes. A single ratio $\dfrac{\Delta V}{I}$ does not establish constant resistance over an interval.
