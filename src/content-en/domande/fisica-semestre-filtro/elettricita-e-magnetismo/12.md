A charge of $2\,\mu\mathrm C$ moves at $1000\,\mathrm{m/s}$ perpendicular to a magnetic field of $0.50\,\mathrm T$. What is the magnetic force magnitude?

- $10^{-3}\,\mathrm N$.
- $10^{-6}\,\mathrm N$.
- $10^{-2}\,\mathrm N$.
- $0\,\mathrm N$.

## Explanation

The sine of the angle between velocity and field is one: $F_B=|q|vB=2\cdot10^{-6}\cdot1000\cdot0.50=10^{-3}\,\mathrm N$. Force would vanish for velocity parallel to the field, not perpendicular. Calculating the magnitude alone does not determine direction, which requires the cross product and charge sign.
