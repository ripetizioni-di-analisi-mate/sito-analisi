A closed resistive loop is viewed from the front. A magnetic field into the page increases in magnitude; self-inductance is negligible. What is the induced current direction?

- Counterclockwise, to produce an inward field.
- Clockwise, to produce an inward field.
- Zero, because the loop is stationary.
- Counterclockwise, to produce an outward field.

## Explanation

Increasing inward flux is opposed by an outward induced field. With the right hand, an outward thumb corresponds to counterclockwise fingers. The loop may remain stationary: time-varying flux is sufficient. A current reinforcing the external increase would have the sign opposite to Lenz’s law.
