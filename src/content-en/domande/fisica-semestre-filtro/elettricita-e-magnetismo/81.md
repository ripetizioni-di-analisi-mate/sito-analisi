Two nonrelativistic ions enter the same uniform magnetic field perpendicularly at the same speed, with no other forces. A has mass $m$ and charge $+e$; B has mass $4m$ and charge $+2e$. What is $\dfrac{r_B}{r_A}$?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
- $8$.
- $2$.
- $4$.

## Explanation

At common speed and field, radius is proportional to $\dfrac{m}{|q|}$. Hence $\dfrac{r_B}{r_A}=\dfrac{\dfrac{4m}{2e}}{\dfrac{m}{e}}=2$. Magnetic force on B is doubled, but its mass is quadrupled: the path curves less, with larger radius. Mass alone is insufficient for the comparison.
