Three equal point charges $+q$ occupy the vertices of an equilateral triangle of side $a$ in vacuum. Excluding self-energies and taking zero at infinite separation, what is the distribution’s energy?

- $\dfrac{k_eq^2}{a}$.
- $-\dfrac{3k_eq^2}{a}$.
- $\dfrac{3k_eq^2}{a}$.
- $\dfrac{6k_eq^2}{a}$.

## Explanation

There are three distinct pairs, each with positive energy $\dfrac{k_eq^2}{a}$. Their sum is therefore $\dfrac{3k_eq^2}{a}$. Separately counting each charge’s interaction with both others counts every pair twice and produces a factor six. The positive sign reflects work needed to bring repelling charges together.
