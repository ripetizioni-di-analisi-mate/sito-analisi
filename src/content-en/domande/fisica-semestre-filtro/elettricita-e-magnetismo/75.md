In an elementary electrical model of a lipid membrane between conducting solutions, what does capacitance represent?

- Ionic charge flow through open channels at constant voltage.
- Storage of separated charges across the insulating layer.
- Membrane resistance to current flow.
- Power dissipated by currents through the membrane.

## Explanation

The solutions act as conducting regions and the bilayer as a dielectric separator, allowing opposite charges on its sides. Capacitance describes storage, whereas currents through channels and transporters require other model elements. A capacitor alone cannot describe every electrical property of the membrane.
