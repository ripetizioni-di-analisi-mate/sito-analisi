A positive rod approaches an isolated neutral conductor without touching it. What happens before any grounding?

- The conductor remains uniform because its total charge is zero.
- The near side becomes positive because like charges accumulate.
- The conductor acquires net negative charge from the rod.
- Electrons accumulate on the near side, but total charge remains zero.

## Explanation

Induction redistributes charges already present: electrons are attracted by the positive rod. The isolated conductor exchanges no charge, so its sum stays zero and the far side develops an electron deficit. Overall neutrality and uniform distribution are different properties.
