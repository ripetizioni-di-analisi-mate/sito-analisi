Capacitors of $3\,\mu\mathrm F$ and $6\,\mu\mathrm F$ are in series across $12\,\mathrm V$, with an initially neutral intermediate node. What voltage is across the $3\,\mu\mathrm F$ capacitor?

- $4\,\mathrm V$.
- $6\,\mathrm V$.
- $8\,\mathrm V$.
- $12\,\mathrm V$.

## Explanation

Equivalent capacitance is $2\,\mu\mathrm F$, so the common charge magnitude is $24\,\mu\mathrm C$. The smaller capacitor has $\dfrac{q}{C}=\dfrac{24}{3}=8\,\mathrm V$. In series, voltage is inversely proportional to capacitance: the resistance-proportional rule for resistors does not apply.
