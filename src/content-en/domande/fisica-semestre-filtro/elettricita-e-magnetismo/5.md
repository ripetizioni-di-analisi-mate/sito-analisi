A positive charge of $3\,\mu\mathrm C$ moves from $100\,\mathrm V$ to $40\,\mathrm V$. What is the work done by the electrostatic force?

- $+4.2\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm J$.
- $+1.8\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm J$.
- $-1.8\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm J$.
- $+1.8\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm J$.

## Explanation

Work is $W_e=q(V_i-V_f)=3\cdot10^{-6}(100-40)=1.8\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm J$. It is positive because the positive charge’s potential energy decreases. The opposite sign would belong to $\Delta U$; adding potentials instead of subtracting them confuses the reference with a physical difference.
