A neutral dielectric polarizes in an external field. Which description distinguishes this phenomenon from conduction in a metal?

- Free electrons steadily cross the entire sample as in an ohmic wire.
- Every molecule must acquire positive net charge.
- The total field must vanish throughout the volume, as in an equilibrium conductor.
- Bound charges shift locally or dipoles orient.

## Explanation

Polarization creates local charge separation or dipole orientation without requiring free transport across the sample. A material can remain globally neutral while carrying bound surface charge. Its internal field changes but is not generally cancelled as in conducting material at equilibrium.
