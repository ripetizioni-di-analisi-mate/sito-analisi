To measure current through a resistor and voltage across it using ideal instruments, how should they be connected?

- Ammeter in parallel and voltmeter in series.
- Ammeter in series and voltmeter in parallel.
- Both in parallel.
- Both in series.

## Explanation

The ammeter must carry the branch current and ideally has zero resistance. The voltmeter compares the resistor’s two nodes and ideally has infinite resistance. Reversing them would alter the circuit: the ideal ammeter would short the component and a series voltmeter would interrupt current.
