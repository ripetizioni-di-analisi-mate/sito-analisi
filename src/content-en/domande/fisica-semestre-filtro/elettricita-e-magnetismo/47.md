A dielectric is described by $\varepsilon=\varepsilon_r\varepsilon_0$ with one constant value of $\varepsilon_r$. Which assumption makes the elementary model consistent?

- A linear, homogeneous, isotropic material within the field regime considered.
- An anisotropic material, assuming the same constant in every direction.
- A material with spatially varying permittivity, using the same value everywhere.
- A material with polarization quadratic in field, using the same constant at every strength.

## Explanation

A constant scalar coefficient assumes a response proportional to field, identical in direction and uniform in space. Strong fields or anisotropic and nonlinear materials require other descriptions. Relative permittivity is dimensionless and describes the medium’s response, not its net charge.
