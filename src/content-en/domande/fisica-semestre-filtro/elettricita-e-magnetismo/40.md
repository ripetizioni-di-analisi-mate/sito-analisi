A charge moves along an equipotential surface in a nonzero electrostatic field. Which statement is correct?

- Electric work between the points is zero and the field is normal to the surface.
- The field is tangent to the surface and does positive work.
- Work is zero because the field must vanish over the entire surface.
- Work depends on the path length along the surface.

## Explanation

Along the surface, $\Delta V=0$, hence $W_e=-q\Delta V=0$. A nonzero field cannot have a tangential component, or potential would change along the surface. Equipotential does not mean field-free: a charged conductor’s surface in equilibrium is an example.
