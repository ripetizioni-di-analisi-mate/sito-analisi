A rigid loop is stationary in a uniform magnetic field constant in time; area and orientation do not change. What can be said about induced emf, even if flux is large?

- It always equals $BA$.
- It increases with flux magnitude even if constant.
- It must sustain a current that completely cancels the external field.
- It is zero because flux does not change.

## Explanation

Faraday’s law contains the time derivative of flux, not flux itself. With constant field, area and orientation, that derivative is zero. Lenz’s law concerns opposition to a change; it does not require cancelling any magnetic field present inside an ordinary loop.
