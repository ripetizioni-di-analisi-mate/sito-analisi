A graph has $I$ on the vertical axis and $\Delta V$ on the horizontal axis. An ohmic resistor’s line passes through the origin with slope $0.25\,\mathrm{A/V}$. What is its resistance?

- $0.25\,\Omega$.
- $4\,\Omega$.
- $2\,\Omega$.
- $8\,\Omega$.

## Explanation

With these axes, slope is $\dfrac{I}{\Delta V}=\dfrac{1}{R}$. Therefore $R=\dfrac{1}{0.25}=4\,\Omega$. If voltage were on the vertical axis, slope would instead equal $R$ directly: the graph’s name is insufficient; read its axes and units.
