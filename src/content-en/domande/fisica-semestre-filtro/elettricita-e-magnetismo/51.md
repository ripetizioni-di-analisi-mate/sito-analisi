At controlled temperature, a component carries $1\,\mathrm A$ at $2\,\mathrm V$ and $1.5\,\mathrm A$ at $4\,\mathrm V$. What follows from these data?

- Current is inversely proportional to voltage.
- It does not have constant ohmic resistance over the interval.
- It has a constant resistance of $4\,\Omega$.
- It has a constant resistance of $2\,\Omega$.

## Explanation

The ratios are $\dfrac{2}{1}=2\,\Omega$ and $\dfrac{4}{1.5}\simeq2.67\,\Omega$, so they differ. A constant ohmic resistor would double current when voltage doubles. Current increasing with voltage alone does not establish direct proportionality.
