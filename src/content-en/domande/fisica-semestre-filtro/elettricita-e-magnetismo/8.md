Two resistors of $6\,\Omega$ and $3\,\Omega$ connect to the same two nodes. What is their equivalent resistance?

- $9\,\Omega$.
- $2\,\Omega$.
- $4.5\,\Omega$.
- $3\,\Omega$.

## Explanation

The same two nodes mean parallel: $R_{\mathrm{eq}}=\dfrac{R_1R_2}{R_1+R_2}=\dfrac{18}{9}=2\,\Omega$. The result must be smaller than either branch because an additional path for charge transport is provided. The sum would apply in series; the arithmetic mean does not describe this connection.
