A nonzero test charge changes from $q$ to $2q$ at the same point, whose potential is nonzero relative to the chosen reference, without altering the sources. How do potential $V$ and the probe’s potential energy $U$ change?

- Both halve.
- Both double.
- $V$ is unchanged and $U$ doubles.
- $V$ doubles and $U$ is unchanged.

## Explanation

Potential at the point is produced by the sources, which remain unchanged by assumption. Probe energy is $U=qV$, so it doubles with charge. The definition $V=\dfrac{U}{q}$ does not mean potential varies inversely with the probe: $U$ changes with it too.
