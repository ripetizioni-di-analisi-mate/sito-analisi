A planar surface of area $0.020\,\mathrm{m^2}$ is in a uniform $0.50\,\mathrm T$ field. The field makes $60^\circ$ with the chosen normal. What is the flux?

- $-5.0\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{Wb}$.
- Approximately $8.7\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{Wb}$.
- $5.0\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{Wb}$.
- $1.0\cdot10^{-2}\,\mathrm{Wb}$.

## Explanation

Use $\Phi_B=BA\cos\theta=0.50\cdot0.020\cdot0.5=5.0\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{Wb}$. The angle is measured from the normal, not the plane. Cosine is positive for the assigned orientation; reversing the normal would change the sign, but that is not the stated orientation.
