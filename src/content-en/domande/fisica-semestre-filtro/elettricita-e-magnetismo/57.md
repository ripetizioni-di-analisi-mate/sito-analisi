Resistors of $2\,\Omega$ and $4\,\Omega$ are in series across $12\,\mathrm V$. What voltage drops across the $4\,\Omega$ resistor?

- $8\,\mathrm V$.
- $12\,\mathrm V$.
- $6\,\mathrm V$.
- $4\,\mathrm V$.

## Explanation

The common current is $I=\dfrac{12}{2+4}=2\,\mathrm A$. The requested drop is $IR=2\cdot4=8\,\mathrm V$, while the other resistor drops $4\,\mathrm V$. Voltage divides in proportion to resistance, not equally; the equal quantity in series is current.
