In the nonrelativistic model, the speed of the same particle moving perpendicular to a uniform magnetic field doubles. How do radius and period change?

- Radius quadruples and period doubles.
- Radius stays unchanged and period halves.
- Both double.
- Radius doubles and period remains unchanged.

## Explanation

From $r=\dfrac{mv}{|q|B}$, radius doubles. But $T=\dfrac{2\pi r}{v}=\dfrac{2\pi m}{|q|B}$: the larger circumference is travelled at proportionally higher speed. Period’s independence from speed requires the stated nonrelativistic regime and uniform field.
