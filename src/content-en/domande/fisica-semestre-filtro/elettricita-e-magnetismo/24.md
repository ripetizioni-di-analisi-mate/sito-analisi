At P, two sources produce fields $\vec E_1=(3,0)\,\mathrm{N/C}$ and $\vec E_2=(0,4)\,\mathrm{N/C}$. What is the total field magnitude?

- $7\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
- $25\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
- $5\,\mathrm{N/C}$.
- $1\,\mathrm{N/C}$.

## Explanation

Add components: $\vec E=(3,4)\,\mathrm{N/C}$. Since the components are orthogonal, the magnitude is $\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=5\,\mathrm{N/C}$. Adding or subtracting magnitudes would apply only to collinear contributions; stopping at $25$ omits the square root and dimensional consistency.
