A device draws a constant power of $100\,\mathrm W$ for $2\,\mathrm h$. What energy does it absorb in joules?

- $7.2\cdot10^5\,\mathrm J$.
- $3.6\cdot10^5\,\mathrm J$.
- $7200\,\mathrm J$.
- $200\,\mathrm J$.

## Explanation

Convert time: $2\,\mathrm h=7200\,\mathrm s$. Then $E=Pt=100\cdot7200=7.2\cdot10^5\,\mathrm J$. The numerical product $100\cdot2=200$ would be in watt-hours, not joules. A watt measures the rate of energy transfer, not accumulated energy.
