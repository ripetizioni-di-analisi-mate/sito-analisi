A $10\,\Omega$ resistor carries a constant $2\,\mathrm A$ for $30\,\mathrm s$. What power and energy are dissipated?

- $400\,\mathrm W$ and $12000\,\mathrm J$.
- $20\,\mathrm W$ and $600\,\mathrm J$.
- $40\,\mathrm W$ and $1200\,\mathrm J$.
- $40\,\mathrm W$ and $120\,\mathrm J$.

## Explanation

Power is $P=RI^2=10\cdot2^2=40\,\mathrm W$; energy is $E=Pt=40\cdot30=1200\,\mathrm J$. Current and resistance remain constant, so power does too. Using $RI$ mistakes voltage for power; power and energy are different quantities.
