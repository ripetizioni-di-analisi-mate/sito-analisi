Two stationary particles carry charges $q$ and $4q$ and have different masses. Which comparison of their mutual electric forces is correct?

- The force on charge $4q$ is four times larger.
- The force on the more massive particle is always larger.
- Equal in magnitude and opposite in direction, although accelerations may differ.
- The forces are equal in magnitude and have the same direction.

## Explanation

Both forces contain the same product of charges and the same separation. Action and reaction therefore have equal magnitudes and opposite directions. Masses affect acceleration through $a=\dfrac{F}{m}$, not the comparison of the two mutual electric forces.
