A positive charge moves along $+x$. The electric field is along $+y$ and the magnetic field along $+z$, all uniform. What speed makes total force zero?

- None, because the two forces point the same way.
- $v=EB$.
- $v=\dfrac{E}{B}$.
- $v=\dfrac{B}{E}$.

## Explanation

The product $\vec v\times\vec B$ points along $-y$, opposite the electric force. Cancellation requires $qE=qvB$, hence $v=\dfrac{E}{B}$. Charge cancels, but directions must be checked before equating magnitudes; crossed fields alone do not guarantee balance at every velocity.
