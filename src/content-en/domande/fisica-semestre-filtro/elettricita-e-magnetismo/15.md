Two identical conducting spheres, far from other sources, carry $+8\,\mathrm{nC}$ and $-2\,\mathrm{nC}$. They are connected by a wire and reach equilibrium in a symmetric arrangement. What is the final charge on each?

- $+6\,\mathrm{nC}$.
- $+3\,\mathrm{nC}$.
- $+5\,\mathrm{nC}$.
- $0\,\mathrm{nC}$.

## Explanation

Total charge is the algebraic sum $8-2=6\,\mathrm{nC}$. Symmetry and equal potential divide it into equal parts of $3\,\mathrm{nC}$. Adding magnitudes gives an incorrect total; opposite signs do not imply complete neutralization when initial magnitudes differ.
