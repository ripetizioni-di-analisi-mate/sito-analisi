A nonzero test charge experiences no electric force at P. What necessarily follows, assuming the probe does not disturb the sources?

- Potential at P is zero relative to infinity.
- There are no nearby charges.
- The total electric field at P is zero.
- Every source has zero charge.

## Explanation

From $\vec F=q\vec E$ with $q\ne0$, it follows that $\vec E=0$. Fields from nonzero sources may cancel by superposition, so the sources need not vanish. Potential is not determined either: it depends on scalar addition and the reference.
