Potential on a segment of the axis is $V(x)=ax$ with $a\ne0$, and does not depend on the other coordinates. What happens at the origin?

- $V=0$ and $E_x=+a$.
- $V=0$ and $E_x=0$.
- $V=a$ and $E_x=0$.
- $V=0$ but $E_x=-a\ne0$.

## Explanation

Potential follows by setting $x=0$, whereas field follows from the slope: $E_x=-\dfrac{dV}{dx}=-a$. A zero on the graph does not make its slope zero. The minus sign means that the field points towards decreasing potential.
