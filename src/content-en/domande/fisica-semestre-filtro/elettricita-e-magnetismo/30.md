Which pair of units can express the same quantity, electric field?

- $\mathrm{V/m}$ and $\mathrm{C/V}$.
- $\mathrm{N/C}$ and $\mathrm{N\,m/C}$.
- $\mathrm{N/C}$ and $\mathrm{J/C}$.
- $\mathrm{N/C}$ and $\mathrm{V/m}$.

## Explanation

Since $\mathrm V=\mathrm{J/C}=\mathrm{N\,m/C}$, dividing a volt by a metre gives $\mathrm{N/C}$. Joules per coulomb and newton metres per coulomb instead measure potential. Coulombs per volt measure capacitance, a different quantity from field.
