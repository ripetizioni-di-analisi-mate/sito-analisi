A nonrelativistic particle has mass $3\cdot10^{-27}\,\mathrm{kg}$, charge $1.5\cdot10^{-19}\,\mathrm C$ and speed $2\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ perpendicular to a uniform magnetic field $B=0.20\,\mathrm T$. With no other forces, what is the orbit radius?

- $0.0020\,\mathrm m$.
- $0.020\,\mathrm m$.
- $0.20\,\mathrm m$.
- $2.0\,\mathrm m$.

## Explanation

Magnetic force provides centripetal acceleration: $|q|vB=\dfrac{mv^2}{r}$. Thus $r=\dfrac{mv}{|q|B}=\dfrac{6\cdot10^{-22}}{3\cdot10^{-20}}=0.020\,\mathrm m$. Speed remains constant while direction changes. The alternatives reflect powers-of-ten errors, not different rotation directions.
