A closed resistive loop is viewed from the front. A magnetic field into the page decreases in magnitude; self-inductance is negligible. What direction does induced current take?

- Clockwise, to sustain the inward flux that is decreasing.
- Counterclockwise, because induced field must always oppose the external field.
- Clockwise, to produce an outward field.
- Zero, because the field does not reverse.

## Explanation

The change being opposed is now a loss of inward flux. The induced field must therefore point inward, produced by clockwise current. Opposing change does not mean always opposing the external field: this distinction separates increasing-field and decreasing-field cases.
