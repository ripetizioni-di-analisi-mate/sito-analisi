A negative point source is at the origin. At a point on the positive $x$ axis, what directions do the field and the force on a negative test charge have?

- Field towards $-x$, force towards $+x$.
- Field and force towards $-x$.
- Field towards $+x$, force towards $-x$.
- Field and force towards $+x$.

## Explanation

The field points towards the negative source, hence towards the origin. Force on the negative test charge opposes the field because $\vec F=q\vec E$ with $q<0$. Do not reverse the field twice: it is defined using a positive test charge regardless of the actual charge placed there.
