A second cylindrical wire of the same material at the same temperature has twice the length and twice the radius of the first. What is $\dfrac{R_2}{R_1}$?

- $1$.
- $2$.
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
- $4$.

## Explanation

From $R=\dfrac{\rho_eL}{\pi r^2}$, $\dfrac{R_2}{R_1}=\dfrac{2}{2^2}=\dfrac{1}{2}$. Doubling length doubles resistance, while doubling radius quadruples area and divides it by four. Treating radius and area as the same variable loses this geometric factor.
