A capacitor remains connected to an ideal source while a linear dielectric doubles its capacitance. Which pair describes charge and stored energy?

- Doubled charge and quadrupled energy.
- Both halve.
- Both double.
- Constant charge and halved energy.

## Explanation

The source maintains voltage: $q=C\Delta V$ and $U_C=\dfrac{C(\Delta V)^2}{2}$ are both proportional to $C$. The source exchanges charge and energy with the capacitor, so plate charge cannot also be held fixed. Quadrupling energy would concern doubling voltage at constant capacitance.
