A charge $q=-2\,\mu\mathrm C$ is in a uniform field of $1000\,\mathrm{N/C}$ along $+x$. What is the electric force component $F_x$?

- $-2.0\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm N$.
- $-2.0\,\mathrm N$.
- $+2.0\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm N$.
- $-2.0\cdot10^{-9}\,\mathrm N$.

## Explanation

Use the signed charge: $F_x=qE_x=-2\cdot10^{-6}\cdot1000=-2\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm N$. The magnitude is positive, but the component is negative because the force opposes the field. Forgetting the micro prefix or dividing by the field produces incorrect orders of magnitude.
