A calculation replaces $2\,\mathrm{nC}$ with $2\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm C$ in Coulomb’s law while all other data are correct. How is the force magnitude affected?

- It is a hundred times too large.
- It is a million times too large.
- It is a thousand times too small.
- It is a thousand times too large.

## Explanation

Nano means $10^{-9}$ whereas micro means $10^{-6}$. The inserted charge is therefore a thousand times the true value. Because only one charge is wrong and force is linear in each charge, force is also multiplied by a thousand, not by its square.
