What is the potential $0.20\,\mathrm m$ from a stationary point charge $Q=2\,\mathrm{nC}$ in vacuum, with zero at infinity and $k_e=9.0\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$?

- $18\,\mathrm V$.
- $9\,\mathrm V$.
- $450\,\mathrm V$.
- $90\,\mathrm V$.

## Explanation

The source potential is $V=\dfrac{k_eQ}{r}=\dfrac{9.0\cdot10^9\cdot2\cdot10^{-9}}{0.20}=90\,\mathrm V$. It depends on inverse distance, not inverse distance squared, which occurs in the field. Its positive sign follows that of $Q$ with the chosen reference; no test charge needs to be specified.
