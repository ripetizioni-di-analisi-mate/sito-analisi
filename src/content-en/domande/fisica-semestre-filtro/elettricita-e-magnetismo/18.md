The distance between two stationary point charges in vacuum triples and only one charge doubles. How does the force magnitude change?

- It becomes $\dfrac{2}{9}$ of its initial value.
- It becomes $\dfrac{4}{9}$ of its initial value.
- It becomes $\dfrac{2}{3}$ of its initial value.
- It becomes $6$ times its initial value.

## Explanation

In the force ratio, Coulomb’s constant cancels: $\dfrac{F'}{F}=\dfrac{2}{3^2}=\dfrac{2}{9}$. Distance is squared, whereas each charge appears to the first power. A factor $\dfrac{4}{9}$ would require doubling both charges, which the question does not specify.
