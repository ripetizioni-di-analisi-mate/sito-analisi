An ideal $4\,\mu\mathrm F$ capacitor is charged to $5\,\mathrm V$. What are the charge magnitude on one plate and the stored energy?

- $0.80\,\mu\mathrm C$ and $2\,\mu\mathrm J$.
- $20\,\mu\mathrm C$ and $50\,\mu\mathrm J$.
- $20\,\mu\mathrm C$ and $100\,\mu\mathrm J$.
- $4\,\mu\mathrm C$ and $10\,\mu\mathrm J$.

## Explanation

Charge is $q=C\Delta V=20\,\mu\mathrm C$ and energy is $U_C=\dfrac{C(\Delta V)^2}{2}=50\,\mu\mathrm J$. The factor one half accounts for the voltage increasing during charging. The other plate carries the opposite charge: the pair’s net charge is not the quantity to insert into the capacitance relation.
