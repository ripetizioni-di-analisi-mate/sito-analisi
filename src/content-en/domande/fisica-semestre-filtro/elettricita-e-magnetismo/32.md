A constant $50\,\mathrm V$ is added to potential at every point of an electrostatic region. What physical effect occurs?

- All charges gain the same kinetic energy.
- Field increases by $50\,\mathrm{V/m}$ everywhere.
- Potential differences increase by $50\,\mathrm V$.
- No change in field or work between two points.

## Explanation

The constant cancels when two potentials are subtracted and has zero spatial derivative. Thus $\Delta V$, field and work $W_e=-q\Delta V$ remain unchanged. Only the reference changes; no physical source or particle kinetic energy is added.
