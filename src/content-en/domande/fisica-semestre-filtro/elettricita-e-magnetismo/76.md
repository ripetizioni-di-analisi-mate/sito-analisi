A long straight wire passes perpendicularly through the page with conventional current coming out towards the viewer. What direction do magnetic field lines take around the wire?

- Counterclockwise, viewed from the front.
- Radially inward towards the wire.
- Clockwise, viewed from the front.
- Radially outward from the wire.

## Explanation

With the right-hand thumb towards the observer, the fingers curl counterclockwise. Lines surround the wire rather than being radial like electric lines from a point charge. Reversing current would reverse the direction of all magnetic circles.
