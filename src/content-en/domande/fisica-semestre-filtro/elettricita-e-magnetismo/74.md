A $2\,\mu\mathrm F$ capacitor charged to $6\,\mathrm V$ is disconnected and connected in parallel to an identical uncharged capacitor, joining corresponding plates. After redistribution, what is their common voltage with no charge leakage to the outside?

- $6\,\mathrm V$.
- $\dfrac{6}{\sqrt2}\,\mathrm V$.
- $12\,\mathrm V$.
- $3\,\mathrm V$.

## Explanation

Initial positive-node charge is $12\,\mu\mathrm C$ and spreads over total capacitance $4\,\mu\mathrm F$: final voltage is $3\,\mathrm V$. Charge is conserved, but capacitor electrostatic energy need not be. Imposing the latter gives the square-root value, ignoring dissipation and other transfers during the transient.
