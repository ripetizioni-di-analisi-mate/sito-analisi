A charged conductor is in electrostatic equilibrium. Which statement holds inside the conducting material?

- Field and potential must both be zero.
- Field is uniform and nonzero because total charge is nonzero.
- Potential varies through the metal even though field is zero.
- Field is zero and potential is constant, not necessarily zero.

## Explanation

A nonzero internal field would move free charges, contradicting equilibrium. Zero field implies uniform potential, but its value depends on reference and charge. This conclusion concerns conducting material in equilibrium, not every cavity or a wire carrying resistive current.
