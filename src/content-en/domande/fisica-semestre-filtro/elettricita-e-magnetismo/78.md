A charged particle moves parallel to a nonzero uniform magnetic field, with no electric field or other forces. What is its motion?

- Uniform circular motion with finite radius.
- Uniform straight-line motion.
- Helical motion with necessarily nonzero radius.
- Straight-line motion with increasing speed.

## Explanation

For velocity parallel to the field, $\sin\theta=0$ and magnetic force is zero. With no other forces, the velocity vector remains constant. Circular motion requires a perpendicular component; a nonzero-radius helix requires both velocity components.
