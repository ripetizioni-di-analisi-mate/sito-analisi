In a one-dimensional region, potential decreases linearly from $12\,\mathrm V$ to $4\,\mathrm V$ between $x=0$ and $x=0.020\,\mathrm m$. What is $E_x$?

- $+0.16\,\mathrm{V/m}$.
- $+400\,\mathrm{V/m}$.
- $+800\,\mathrm{V/m}$.
- $-400\,\mathrm{V/m}$.

## Explanation

The slope is $\dfrac{4-12}{0.020}=-400\,\mathrm{V/m}$. Field is its negative, $E_x=-\dfrac{dV}{dx}=+400\,\mathrm{V/m}$, directed towards lower potential. Adding potentials or multiplying by distance does not represent the graph’s slope.
