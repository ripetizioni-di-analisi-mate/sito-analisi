A particle enters along $+x$ at $2.0\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{m/s}$ into a region $0.040\,\mathrm m$ long with a uniform electric field along $y$. If it does not hit the plates and no other forces act, what is its transit time?

- $2.0\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm s$.
- $8.0\cdot10^3\,\mathrm s$.
- $2.0\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm s$.
- It cannot be determined without the mass.

## Explanation

The field has no $x$ component, so $v_x$ is constant and $t=\dfrac{L}{v_x}=\dfrac{0.040}{2.0\cdot10^5}=2.0\cdot10^{-7}\,\mathrm s$. Mass and charge are needed for transverse deflection, not this time. The no-collision condition ensures that the particle crosses the full stated length.
