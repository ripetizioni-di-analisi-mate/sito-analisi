A straight wire segment $0.20\,\mathrm m$ long carries $3.0\,\mathrm A$ and makes $30^\circ$ with a uniform $0.50\,\mathrm T$ field. What is the force magnitude on the segment?

- $1.5\,\mathrm N$.
- Approximately $0.26\,\mathrm N$.
- $0.15\,\mathrm N$.
- $0.30\,\mathrm N$.

## Explanation

The relation is $F=I\ell B\sin\theta=3.0\cdot0.20\cdot0.50\cdot0.5=0.15\,\mathrm N$. The angle is between the current-oriented wire and the field. Omitting the sine gives the perpendicular-wire value; using cosine confuses force geometry with flux geometry.
