A parallel-plate capacitor retains its material and plate area; separation is halved while fringing remains negligible. How does capacitance change?

- It doubles.
- It quadruples.
- It halves.
- It is unchanged because charge is unspecified.

## Explanation

From $C=\dfrac{\varepsilon A}{d}$, halving $d$ doubles $C$. In the linear model, capacitance is determined by geometry and material, without needing to know charge beforehand. Charge and voltage adjust differently depending on whether the capacitor is isolated or connected to a source.
