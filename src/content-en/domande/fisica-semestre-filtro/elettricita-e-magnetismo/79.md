A particle follows a semicircle in a static uniform magnetic field with no other forces in the classical model. How much work does the magnetic force do?

- $-\dfrac{mv^2}{2}$, because velocity direction reverses.
- $2F_Br$, because that is the endpoint displacement.
- $F_B\pi r$, because that is the path length.
- Zero.

## Explanation

At every instant force is normal to velocity, so $\vec F_B\cdot\vec v$ is zero. Momentum direction changes, not speed or kinetic energy. Multiplying force magnitude by path length would ignore the dot product and local perpendicularity.
