Compare resistors $R$ and $2R$. Which statement about Joule power correctly distinguishes the constraints?

- $2R$ always dissipates twice as much, regardless of constraint.
- At equal current, $2R$ dissipates twice as much; at equal voltage, half as much.
- At equal current, $2R$ dissipates half as much; at equal voltage, twice as much.
- $2R$ always dissipates half as much, regardless of constraint.

## Explanation

At fixed current use $P=RI^2$, whereas at fixed voltage use $P=\dfrac{(\Delta V)^2}{R}$. These are the same law combined with Ohm’s law, but the comparison changes because the fixed quantity changes. A conclusion based on resistance alone without stating the constraint is incomplete.
