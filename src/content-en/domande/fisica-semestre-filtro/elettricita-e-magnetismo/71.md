Capacitors of $2\,\mu\mathrm F$ and $5\,\mu\mathrm F$ are in parallel across $10\,\mathrm V$. What is the sum of charge magnitudes on their two positive plates?

- $20\,\mu\mathrm C$.
- $70\,\mu\mathrm C$.
- Approximately $14.3\,\mu\mathrm C$.
- $35\,\mu\mathrm C$.

## Explanation

Both have $10\,\mathrm V$, so their positive plates accumulate $20\,\mu\mathrm C$ and $50\,\mu\mathrm C$. The sum is $70\,\mu\mathrm C$, equal to $(C_1+C_2)\Delta V$. Equal voltages do not imply equal charges when capacitances differ.
