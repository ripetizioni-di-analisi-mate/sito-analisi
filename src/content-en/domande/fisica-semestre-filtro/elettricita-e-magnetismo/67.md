On a graph of voltage against charge transferred to an initially uncharged ideal capacitor, the line runs from the origin to $(q_f,V_f)$. What area gives the stored energy?

- The rectangle with area $q_fV_f$.
- The triangle under the line, with area $\dfrac{q_fV_f}{2}$.
- The area $\dfrac{q_f}{V_f}$ obtained by dividing the coordinates.
- The line’s slope, $\dfrac{V_f}{q_f}$.

## Explanation

The elementary work to transfer charge is $dW=V\,dq$, and voltage rises during charging. Integration gives the triangle, not the rectangle corresponding to the final voltage being present from the start. The slope is inverse capacitance, not energy.
