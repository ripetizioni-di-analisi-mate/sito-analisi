Two positive point charges of $1\,\mu\mathrm C$ and $2\,\mu\mathrm C$ are $0.30\,\mathrm m$ apart in vacuum. With $k_e=9.0\cdot10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$, what force acts on each?

- $0.020\,\mathrm N$, repulsive.
- $0.20\,\mathrm N$, repulsive.
- $2.0\,\mathrm N$, repulsive.
- $0.20\,\mathrm N$, attractive.

## Explanation

Coulomb’s law gives $F=\dfrac{9.0\cdot10^9\cdot10^{-6}\cdot2\cdot10^{-6}}{0.30^2}=0.20\,\mathrm N$. Like signs make the force repulsive. The two forces have equal magnitudes: the larger charge does not experience twice the force. The other repulsive magnitudes result from incorrect conversions or powers of ten.
