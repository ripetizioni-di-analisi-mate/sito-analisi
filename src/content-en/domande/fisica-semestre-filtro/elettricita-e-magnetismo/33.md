The same charge reaches B from A along two different paths in an electrostatic field. Which comparison of electric work is correct?

- The works have opposite signs because the paths differ.
- The longer path always requires greater electric work.
- The works are equal because they depend only on $V_A-V_B$.
- The straight path always gives zero work.

## Explanation

The electrostatic field is conservative: $W_e=q(V_A-V_B)$. Geometric path length is not decisive; field projections onto displacements matter and, overall, the endpoints. This property does not automatically extend to an electric field induced by varying magnetic flux.
