A $2\,\Omega$ resistor is in series with a parallel pair of $6\,\Omega$ and $3\,\Omega$, across $12\,\mathrm V$. What current flows through the $6\,\Omega$ branch?

- $3\,\mathrm A$.
- $1\,\mathrm A$.
- $6\,\mathrm A$.
- $2\,\mathrm A$.

## Explanation

The parallel pair is $2\,\Omega$, so the network is $4\,\Omega$ and draws $3\,\mathrm A$. The first resistor drops $6\,\mathrm V$, leaving $6\,\mathrm V$ across the parallel pair. Thus the requested branch carries $\dfrac{6}{6}=1\,\mathrm A$, not the source’s entire current.
