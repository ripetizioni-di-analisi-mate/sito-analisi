Two ideal $6\,\mu\mathrm F$ capacitors are in series with an initially neutral intermediate node. What is the equivalent capacitance?

- $1.5\,\mu\mathrm F$.
- $12\,\mu\mathrm F$.
- $6\,\mu\mathrm F$.
- $3\,\mu\mathrm F$.

## Explanation

In series, plates carry equal charge magnitudes and voltages add: $\dfrac{1}{C_{\mathrm{eq}}}=\dfrac{1}{6}+\dfrac{1}{6}$ in reciprocal microfarad units. Therefore $C_{\mathrm{eq}}=3\,\mu\mathrm F$. Adding capacitances directly would be correct for parallel connections, where voltage is equal and charges add.
