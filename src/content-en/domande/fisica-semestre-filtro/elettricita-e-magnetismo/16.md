An initially neutral object transfers electrons to another object in an isolated system. Which statement correctly describes the balance?

- The first remains neutral because its proton count is unchanged.
- The first becomes positive and total system charge is unchanged.
- The first becomes positive and total charge increases.
- The first becomes negative and total charge is unchanged.

## Explanation

Losing negative charge leaves a relative excess of positive charge even though protons do not move. The other object acquires the lost negative charge, leaving the sum unchanged. Initial neutrality means cancellation between charges, not absence of electrons available for transfer.
