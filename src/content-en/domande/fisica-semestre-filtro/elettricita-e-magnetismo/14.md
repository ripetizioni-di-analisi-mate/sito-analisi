In a $100$-turn coil, flux through each turn changes uniformly by $2.0\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{Wb}$ in $0.010\,\mathrm s$. What is the magnitude of the average emf?

- $2.0\,\mathrm V$.
- $200\,\mathrm V$.
- $0.20\,\mathrm V$.
- $0.020\,\mathrm V$.

## Explanation

The given quantity is flux per turn, so $|\mathcal E_{\mathrm m}|=\dfrac{N|\Delta\Phi_B|}{\Delta t}=\dfrac{100\cdot2.0\cdot10^{-4}}{0.010}=2.0\,\mathrm V$. Omitting $N$ would give the emf of one turn. The uniform change makes average and instantaneous values equal during the interval.
