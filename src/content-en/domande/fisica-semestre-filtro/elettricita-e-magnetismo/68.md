A charged capacitor is disconnected, then completely filled with a linear dielectric of $\varepsilon_r=3$, without leakage. How do voltage and stored energy change?

- Voltage stays constant and energy triples.
- Both triple.
- Voltage becomes one third and energy stays constant.
- Both become one third of their initial values.

## Explanation

The isolated capacitor retains $q$, while $C'=3C$. Thus $\Delta V'=\dfrac{q}{C'}=\dfrac{\Delta V}{3}$ and $U'_C=\dfrac{q^2}{2C'}=\dfrac{U_C}{3}$. Assuming constant voltage would describe a capacitor still connected to a source, not the stated constraint.
