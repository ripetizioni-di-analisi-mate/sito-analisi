Flux linked with a coil changes, but a break prevents a closed conducting path. Which statement is correct in the elementary model?

- Emf is measured in newtons and acts only at the open ends.
- An emf may exist without sustained current in the coil.
- Current is set by flux derivative alone, independently of the circuit.
- Emf must vanish because current is zero.

## Explanation

Faraday’s law determines emf from changing flux. Sustained current also requires a closed path and knowledge of circuit properties. Emf is energy per charge and is measured in volts; it is not a mechanical force.
