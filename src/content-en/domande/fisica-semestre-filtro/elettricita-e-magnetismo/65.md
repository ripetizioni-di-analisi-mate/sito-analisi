A capacitor’s plates carry $+8\,\mu\mathrm C$ and $-8\,\mu\mathrm C$ with a voltage of $4\,\mathrm V$. What capacitance follows?

- $0\,\mu\mathrm F$.
- $4\,\mu\mathrm F$.
- $2\,\mu\mathrm F$.
- $32\,\mu\mathrm F$.

## Explanation

In $C=\dfrac{q}{\Delta V}$, use the charge magnitude on one plate: $\dfrac{8}{4}=2\,\mu\mathrm F$. The algebraic sum of the pair’s charges is zero but does not determine capacitance. Adding magnitudes would incorrectly double the charge used.
