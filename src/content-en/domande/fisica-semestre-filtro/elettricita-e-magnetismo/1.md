An object has an excess of $10^6$ electrons. With $e=1.60\cdot10^{-19}\,\mathrm C$, what is its charge?

- $+1.60\cdot10^{-13}\,\mathrm C$.
- $-1.60\cdot10^{-25}\,\mathrm C$.
- $-1.60\cdot10^{-19}\,\mathrm C$.
- $-1.60\cdot10^{-13}\,\mathrm C$.

## Explanation

Excess electrons make the charge negative: $q=-Ne=-10^6\cdot1.60\cdot10^{-19}=-1.60\cdot10^{-13}\,\mathrm C$. Multiply by the particle count rather than divide. The positive value would describe a deficit of the same number of electrons; a single elementary charge does not account for $N$.
