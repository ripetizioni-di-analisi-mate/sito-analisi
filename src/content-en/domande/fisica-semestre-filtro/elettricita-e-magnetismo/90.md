Flux per turn rises linearly from $0$ to $4\,\mathrm{mWb}$ in the first $2\,\mathrm s$, then stays constant for $2\,\mathrm s$. The coil has $50$ turns. What are emf magnitudes in the two intervals?

- $0.10\,\mathrm V$ and $0\,\mathrm V$.
- $0\,\mathrm V$ and $0.10\,\mathrm V$.
- $0.10\,\mathrm V$ and $0.10\,\mathrm V$.
- $100\,\mathrm V$ and $0\,\mathrm V$.

## Explanation

In the first segment, slope is $\dfrac{4\cdot10^{-3}}{2}=2\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{Wb/s}$ and emf magnitude is $50\cdot2\cdot10^{-3}=0.10\,\mathrm V$. In the second, slope is zero even though flux has reached its maximum. The milli prefix must not be omitted during conversion.
