An object carries $+4.8\cdot10^{-19}\,\mathrm C$ solely because of electron transfer. Using $e=1.6\cdot10^{-19}\,\mathrm C$, what happened?

- It gained three protons.
- It gained three electrons.
- It lost one third of an electron.
- It lost three electrons.

## Explanation

The transferred count is $N=\dfrac{|q|}{e}=3$. Positive charge indicates electron loss, not gain. The question specifies electron transfer, so proton transfer cannot be substituted; reversing the ratio would give a count incompatible with the model.
