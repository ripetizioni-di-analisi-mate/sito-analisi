On an ideal string, tension quadruples while linear density and source frequency stay fixed. How does wavelength change?

- It stays unchanged.
- It doubles.
- It quadruples.
- It halves.

## Explanation

Speed varies as $\sqrt{F_T}$ and therefore doubles. At fixed frequency, $\lambda=\dfrac{v}{f}$ also doubles. Dependence is not directly proportional to tension: tension affects propagation through a square root.
