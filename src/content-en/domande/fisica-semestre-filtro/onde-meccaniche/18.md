Mass is quadrupled on the same ideal spring. How do period and natural frequency change?

- Period doubles and frequency halves.
- Period quadruples and frequency falls to one quarter.
- Both remain unchanged.
- Period halves and frequency doubles.

## Explanation

At fixed stiffness, period is proportional to $\sqrt m$: $\dfrac{T_2}{T_1}=\sqrt4=2$. Frequency is reciprocal to period and halves. Greater inertia slows motion; elastic force at the same displacement is unchanged because the spring is unchanged.
