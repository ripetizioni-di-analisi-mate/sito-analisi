In a harmonic-wave snapshot, two points are separated by $\dfrac{\lambda}{4}$. What is the magnitude of their phase difference before reduction modulo $2\pi$?

- $\pi$.
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.
- $2\pi$.
- $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$.

## Explanation

A full wavelength corresponds to $2\pi$ radians. One quarter of that distance gives one quarter of the phase: $k\Delta x=(\dfrac{2\pi}{\lambda})(\dfrac{\lambda}{4})=\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. Using $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$ treats half a cycle as full; $\pi$ would correspond to half a wavelength.
