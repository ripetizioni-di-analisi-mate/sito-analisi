The number of identical incoherent sources increases from one to four, each delivering the same receiver intensity. What is the total level change? Assume travelling contributions in the same medium and direction.

- $+4.00\,\mathrm{dB}$.
- $+3.01\,\mathrm{dB}$.
- $+12.04\,\mathrm{dB}$.
- $+6.02\,\mathrm{dB}$.

## Explanation

Intensities add, making total intensity four times that of one source, so the change is $10\log_{10}4\simeq6.02\,\mathrm{dB}$. Amplitude is not multiplied by four: that would require a suitable coherent phase relationship. Equivalently, there are two successive intensity doublings.
