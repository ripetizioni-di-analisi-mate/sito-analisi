A pulse transfers $2.0\,\mathrm J$ in $0.50\,\mathrm s$. What information can be obtained without knowing beam area?

- Mean power is $4.0\,\mathrm W$; intensity is undetermined.
- Intensity is $4.0\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$; power is undetermined.
- Power and intensity both have numerical value $4.0$ for any area.
- Mean power is $1.0\,\mathrm W$; intensity is undetermined.

## Explanation

Energy divided by duration gives mean power, $\bar P=4.0\,\mathrm W$. Obtaining spatially averaged intensity also requires the perpendicular area crossed. One cannot implicitly assign a one-square-metre beam area. Energy multiplied by time does not have power units.
