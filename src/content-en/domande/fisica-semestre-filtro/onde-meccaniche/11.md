An isotropic source without absorption gives $9\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$ at $1\,\mathrm m$. What intensity does it give at $3\,\mathrm m$?

- $1\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.
- $\dfrac13\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.
- $81\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.
- $3\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.

## Explanation

Spherical area grows by a factor of nine, so intensity falls to one ninth: $I_2=9(\dfrac{1}{3})^2=1\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$. A $\dfrac{1}{r}$ dependence would apply to amplitude under the same conditions, not intensity. Power through the sphere remains constant.
