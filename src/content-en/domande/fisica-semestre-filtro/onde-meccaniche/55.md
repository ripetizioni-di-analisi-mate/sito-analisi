For the same ideal gas, assume $v\propto\sqrt{T_{\mathrm K}}$. Temperature changes from $300$ to $363\,\mathrm K$. What is the speed ratio?

- $\sqrt{63}$.
- $1.21$.
- $0.91$.
- $1.10$.

## Explanation

The absolute-temperature ratio is $\dfrac{363}{300}=1.21$; its square root is $1.10$. The relation uses kelvin temperatures, not temperature differences or Celsius values. Gas identity and regime must remain those of the stated model.
