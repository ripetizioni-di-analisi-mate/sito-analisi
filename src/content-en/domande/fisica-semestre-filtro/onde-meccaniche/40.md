Which positive separation between two points of the same harmonic travelling wave guarantees equal phase at every instant?

- $\dfrac{\lambda}{4}$.
- $2\lambda$.
- $\dfrac{\lambda}{2}$.
- $\dfrac{3\lambda}{2}$.

## Explanation

Spatial phase difference is $k\Delta x$. For $\Delta x=2\lambda$ it is $4\pi$, equivalent to zero. Half and one-and-a-half wavelengths give antiphase, whereas a quarter gives quadrature. Equal displacement at one instant would be weaker than equal phase.
