An observer moves towards a stationary source in a stationary medium. What changes in the Doppler effect?

- The source must oscillate faster.
- Wavefronts in the medium move closer together because the observer compresses them.
- The wavefront encounter rate increases; their spacing in the medium is unchanged.
- Received frequency stays equal because the source is stationary.

## Explanation

Observer motion does not alter emission or the field already present in the medium in this model. It changes the number of fronts crossed each second and hence the frequency measured along the observer’s path. Spatial wavefront compression instead results from a source moving during emission.
