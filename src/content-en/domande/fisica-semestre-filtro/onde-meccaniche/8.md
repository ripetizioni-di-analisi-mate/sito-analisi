Two coherent waves of equal amplitude and frequency arrive in antiphase at one point. What is total displacement there?

- Zero at every instant.
- A sinusoid with double amplitude.
- Zero only when one wave crosses zero.
- A sinusoid with the amplitude of one contribution.

## Explanation

A phase shift of $\pi$ reverses the sinusoid’s sign without changing its magnitude. Both disturbances are therefore equal and opposite at every instant, so their sum vanishes. This is a local conclusion: it does not require the same phase relation everywhere or imply energy destruction.
