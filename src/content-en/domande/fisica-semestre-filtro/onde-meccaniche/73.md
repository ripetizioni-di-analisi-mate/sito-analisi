In steady spherical propagation, intensity at twice the radius is $\dfrac{1}{8}$ its original value. With reflections excluded, what fraction of initial power has been absorbed in the shell?

- $\dfrac{7}{8}$.
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$.
- $\dfrac{1}{8}$.

## Explanation

Relative outgoing power is $(\dfrac{I_2}{I_1})(\dfrac{r_2}{r_1})^2=(\dfrac{1}{8})\cdot4=\dfrac{1}{2}$. The remaining half has been absorbed. Assigning the entire intensity loss to absorption would also count geometrical spreading: some decrease would occur even without loss.
