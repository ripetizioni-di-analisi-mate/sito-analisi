Two strings have the same tension. The second has four times the linear density. What is $\dfrac{v_2}{v_1}$?

- $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$.
- $4$.
- $2$.

## Explanation

From $v=\sqrt{\dfrac{F_T}{\mu_\ell}}$, $\dfrac{v_2}{v_1}=\sqrt{\dfrac{\mu_1}{\mu_2}}=\dfrac{1}{2}$. Greater mass per unit length increases medium inertia and slows propagation. The ratio $\dfrac{1}{4}$ omits the square root; ratios greater than one reverse the density dependence.
