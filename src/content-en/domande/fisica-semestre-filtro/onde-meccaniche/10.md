In the same linear medium at the same frequency, displacement amplitude of a travelling wave halves. What is $\dfrac{I_2}{I_1}$?

- $2$.
- $4$.
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$.

## Explanation

Under the specified conditions the coefficient relating intensity to squared amplitude stays constant. Thus $\dfrac{I_2}{I_1}=(\dfrac{A_2}{A_1})^2=(\dfrac{1}{2})^2=\dfrac{1}{4}$. The ratio $\dfrac{1}{2}$ incorrectly assumes intensity is linear in amplitude; ratios above one reverse the described change.
