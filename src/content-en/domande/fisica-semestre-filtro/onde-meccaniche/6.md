With $k,\omega>0$, which motion does a crest of $y=A\cos(kx+\omega t)$ follow?

- $x(t)=x_0+(\dfrac{\omega}{k})t$.
- $x(t)=x_0-(\dfrac{\omega}{k})t$.
- $x(t)=x_0$.
- $x(t)=x_0\cos(\omega t)$.

## Explanation

A crest corresponds to constant phase, not a medium particle. From $kx+\omega t=C$, coordinate decreases linearly with time. The crest travels towards $-x$ at speed $\dfrac{\omega}{k}$. Sinusoidal time motion instead belongs to local medium displacement.
