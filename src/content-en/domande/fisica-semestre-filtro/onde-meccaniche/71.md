Far from an isotropic point source in a uniform non-absorbing medium, how does pressure amplitude change when distance doubles?

- It becomes $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}$ of its original value.
- It becomes one quarter.
- It stays unchanged.
- It halves.

## Explanation

Intensity falls to one quarter, but is proportional to squared pressure amplitude in the same medium. Taking the square root gives a one-half pressure factor. Applying the inverse-square law directly to amplitude would confuse amplitude with energy.
