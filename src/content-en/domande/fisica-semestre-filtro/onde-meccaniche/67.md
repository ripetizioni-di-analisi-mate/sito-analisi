An ideal string has $\mu_\ell=0.020\,\mathrm{kg/m}$ and $v=10\,\mathrm{m/s}$. For a sinusoidal wave with $A=0.010\,\mathrm m$ and $f=5.0\,\mathrm{Hz}$, what is mean power?

- $9.87\,\mathrm{mW}$.
- $987\,\mathrm{mW}$.
- $19.7\,\mathrm{mW}$.
- $0.250\,\mathrm{mW}$.

## Explanation

Use $\bar P=\dfrac{\mu_\ell v(2\pi f)^2A^2}{2}$, giving $9.87\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm W$. The value $0.250\,\mathrm{mW}$ substitutes $f$ for $\omega$, losing the factor $4\pi^2$; $19.7\,\mathrm{mW}$ omits the one-half time-average factor. Amplitude is squared and must be in metres.
