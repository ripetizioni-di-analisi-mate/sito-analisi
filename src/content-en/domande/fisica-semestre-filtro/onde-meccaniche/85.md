A stationary siren emits at $680\,\mathrm{Hz}$ in still air with $v=340\,\mathrm{m/s}$. An observer moves away at $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$ along propagation. What frequency is received?

- $640\,\mathrm{Hz}$.
- $720\,\mathrm{Hz}$.
- $642.2\,\mathrm{Hz}$.
- $680\,\mathrm{Hz}$.

## Explanation

Wavefront spacing in the medium is $\lambda=\dfrac{v}{f_s}=0.50\,\mathrm m$. Fronts catch the observer at relative speed $v-u_o=320\,\mathrm{m/s}$, so $f_o=\dfrac{v-u_o}{\lambda}=640\,\mathrm{Hz}$. Using $\dfrac{vf_s}{v+u_o}$ would instead treat the observer as a moving source.
