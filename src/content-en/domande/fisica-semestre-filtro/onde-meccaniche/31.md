In a sinusoidal profile, a crest and the immediately following trough are $0.15\,\mathrm m$ apart. What is the wavelength?

- $0.60\,\mathrm m$.
- $0.075\,\mathrm m$.
- $0.30\,\mathrm m$.
- $0.15\,\mathrm m$.

## Explanation

From a crest to the next trough, phase changes by $\pi$, half a spatial cycle. The distance is therefore $\dfrac{\lambda}{2}$, giving $\lambda=0.30\,\mathrm m$. Wavelength joins successive points in the same phase, such as two crests, not a maximum and a minimum.
