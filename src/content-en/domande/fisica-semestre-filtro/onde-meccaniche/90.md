Source and receiver, with the receiver ahead, both move at constant speed $u<v$ in the same direction through a stationary medium. What frequency does the receiver receive?

- $f_s\dfrac{v}{v-u}$.
- $f_s\dfrac{v+u}{v-u}$.
- $f_s\dfrac{v-u}{v}$.
- The emitted frequency $f_s$.

## Explanation

Ahead of the source, fronts are separated by $\lambda=(v-u)T_s$. The receiver moves in the same direction and meets them at relative speed $v-u$. The ratio $\dfrac{v-u}{\lambda}$ gives $\dfrac{1}{T_s}=f_s$. Considering only one participant’s motion would miss this cancellation, even though medium wavelength differs from $vT_s$.
