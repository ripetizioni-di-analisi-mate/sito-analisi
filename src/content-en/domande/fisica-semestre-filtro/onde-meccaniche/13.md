An echo returns in $40\,\mu\mathrm s$ through a homogeneous medium with $v=1500\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the stationary reflector’s distance for a straight path and no instrumental delay?

- $6.0\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $30\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $1.5\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $3.0\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

Time includes outward and return travel: $2d=v\Delta t$. Converting microseconds to seconds gives $d=0.030\,\mathrm m$. Six centimetres is the complete path; dividing by two again gives $1.5\,\mathrm{cm}$. Thirty centimetres comes from an incorrect decimal conversion.
