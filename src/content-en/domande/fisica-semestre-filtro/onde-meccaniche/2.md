Harmonic motion has frequency $5.0\,\mathrm{Hz}$. What is its angular frequency?

- $\dfrac5{2\pi}\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
- $10\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
- $5\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
- $5\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.

## Explanation

Each cycle corresponds to $2\pi$ radians: $\omega=2\pi f=10\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. The numerical value of $f$ counts cycles per second, not radians per second. Dividing by $2\pi$ converts in the opposite direction, from angular frequency to frequency; using only $\pi$ treats a half-cycle as complete.
