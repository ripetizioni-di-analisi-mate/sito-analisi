A source moves away from a stationary receiver at speed $u_s<v$ in a stationary medium. Which spacing–frequency pair is correct behind the source?

- $\lambda=(v+u_s)T_s$, $f_o=\dfrac{v}{v+u_s}f_s$.
- $\lambda=vT_s$, $f_o=\dfrac{v-u_s}{v}f_s$.
- $\lambda=(v+u_s)T_s$, $f_o=\dfrac{v+u_s}{v}f_s$.
- $\lambda=(v-u_s)T_s$, $f_o=\dfrac{v}{v-u_s}f_s$.

## Explanation

Between emissions the source moves away from the receiver, adding $u_sT_s$ to rear wavefront separation. The stationary receiver sees fronts pass at speed $v$, so it divides that speed by the larger wavelength. The result is below emitted frequency; front speed in the medium does not become $v+u_s$.
