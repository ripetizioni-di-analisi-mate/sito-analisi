An ideal oscillator doubles amplitude while retaining mass and spring. How do mechanical energy and period change?

- Energy quadruples; period is unchanged.
- Energy doubles; period is unchanged.
- Energy quadruples; period doubles.
- Energy is unchanged; period halves.

## Explanation

Energy is $\dfrac{KA^2}{2}$ and therefore increases fourfold. Period depends on $\dfrac{m}{K}$, which is unchanged. Amplitude independence is a property of the linear harmonic model; it does not justify extending the result to springs deformed beyond the elastic regime.
