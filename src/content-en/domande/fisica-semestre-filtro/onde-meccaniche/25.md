A crest moves right along an ideal stretched string. What does a small painted mark on the string do in the transverse model?

- It oscillates transversely about its equilibrium position.
- It advances by one wavelength after each period.
- It remains stationary because only energy propagates.
- It travels right at the crest’s speed.

## Explanation

A crest is a configuration passing from one material point to the next. The mark follows a string particle and oscillates perpendicular to propagation. Energy transport does not mean absence of local motion, nor does it require matter to travel with the crest.
