A harmonic oscillator is at $x=A$ with $A>0$. What are its velocity and acceleration at that instant?

- $v_x=0$, $a_x=+\omega^2A$.
- $v_x=0$, $a_x=-\omega^2A$.
- $v_x=A\omega$, $a_x=0$.
- $v_x=0$, $a_x=0$.

## Explanation

$x=A$ is a turning point: instantaneous velocity is zero but the motion is not at equilibrium. Restoring force points towards the centre and gives negative acceleration with maximum magnitude. Zero velocity does not imply zero acceleration; equilibrium is at $x=0$.
