A signal has level $60\,\mathrm{dB}$ for half an interval and $80\,\mathrm{dB}$ for the other half. What level corresponds to its time-averaged intensity?

- $140.00\,\mathrm{dB}$.
- $77.03\,\mathrm{dB}$.
- $80.04\,\mathrm{dB}$.
- $70.00\,\mathrm{dB}$.

## Explanation

The requested average concerns energy per time, so average intensities first: $\dfrac{\bar I}{I_0}=\dfrac{10^6+10^8}{2}=5.05\cdot10^7$. Its logarithm gives about $77.03\,\mathrm{dB}$. The arithmetic level average, seventy, is not the energy average; adding intensities without dividing by two would describe simultaneous sources.
