The same crest is tracked: at $t=0$ it is at $x=0.20\,\mathrm m$, and at $t=0.010\,\mathrm s$ at $x=0.70\,\mathrm m$. Which phase velocity describes the data?

- $-50\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $+50\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $+20\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $+70\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

Use crest displacement, $0.70-0.20=0.50\,\mathrm m$, divided by elapsed time. Coordinate increases, so the sign is positive. Dividing final position by time would ignore initial position. The result describes the tracked phase, not a material string particle’s velocity.
