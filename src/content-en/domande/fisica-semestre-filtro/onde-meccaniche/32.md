A source and a transducer lie on the same line perpendicular to a flat wall. The transducer detects a direct pulse and its echo. Moving both devices along this line by $0.15\,\mathrm m$ toward the wall reduces the delay between the pulses by $0.20\,\mathrm{ms}$. What is the wave speed in the medium?

- $750\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $1500\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $3000\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $75\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

The echo path shortens by $0.15\,\mathrm m$ both outward and back. The direct path stays the same because the source and transducer move together. The speed is $\dfrac{0.30}{0.00020}=1500\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
