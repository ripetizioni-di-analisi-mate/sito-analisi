A harmonic wave travels along a taut string. The same crest is at $x=0.20\,\mathrm m$ at time $t=0$ and at $x=0.70\,\mathrm m$ at time $t=0.010\,\mathrm s$. What is the propagation speed of the wave, with its sign referred to the $x$ axis?

- $-50\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $+50\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $+20\,\mathrm{m/s}$
- $+70\,\mathrm{m/s}$

## Explanation

A crest is a point of constant phase, so the propagation speed is the displacement of the crest divided by the time interval. The crest moves by $0.70-0.20=0.50\,\mathrm m$ in $0.010\,\mathrm s$, therefore the magnitude of the velocity is $\dfrac{0.50}{0.010}=50\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Since the coordinate of the crest increases with time, the wave advances in the positive sense of the $x$ axis and the velocity is $+50\,\mathrm{m/s}$. The values $+20\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $+70\,\mathrm{m/s}$ are obtained by dividing only one of the two positions by the time instead of the displacement. The velocity found is that of the crest along the string, while each point of the string oscillates transversely.
