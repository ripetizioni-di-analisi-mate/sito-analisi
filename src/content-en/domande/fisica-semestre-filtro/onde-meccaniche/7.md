Two coherent in-phase contributions have amplitudes $2$ and $4\,\mathrm{mm}$. What is total amplitude in a linear medium?

- $2\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $6\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $\sqrt{20}\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $3\,\mathrm{mm}$.

## Explanation

In phase, maxima occur together and matching displacements add to $6\,\mathrm{mm}$. The root of summed squares would apply to phase difference $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$; the amplitude difference applies to antiphase. An arithmetic mean does not represent superposition of two disturbances.
