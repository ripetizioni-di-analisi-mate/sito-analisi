Two echoes along the same direction in a homogeneous medium with $v=1500\,\mathrm{m/s}$ arrive $30$ and $70\,\mu\mathrm s$ after emission. What is the separation of the two reflectors?

- $6.0\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $5.25\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $3.0\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $7.5\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

Subtract the round-trip times: $\Delta t=40\,\mu\mathrm s$. The depth difference is $\dfrac{v\Delta t}{2}=3.0\,\mathrm{cm}$. The factor two is still needed for a difference. Using the second echo time alone gives the second reflector’s depth, not the separation between them.
