Uniform power of $2.0\,\mathrm W$ crosses a perpendicular area of $0.50\,\mathrm{m^2}$. What is intensity?

- $1.0\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.
- $0.25\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.
- $4.0\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.
- $2.0\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$.

## Explanation

By definition $I=\dfrac{P}{S}$, so $\dfrac{2.0}{0.50}=4.0\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$. Multiplying power and area does not give intensity; reversing the ratio gives different units. Multiplying the result by $0.50\,\mathrm{m^2}$ checks the two watts crossing the whole section.
