A perpendicular cross-section has two uniform regions: $2\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ at $10\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$ and $3\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ at $20\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$. What is total power?

- $8.0\,\mathrm{mW}$.
- $7.5\,\mathrm{mW}$.
- $15\,\mathrm{mW}$.
- $80\,\mathrm{mW}$.

## Explanation

The powers of both portions add: $P=10(2\cdot10^{-4})+20(3\cdot10^{-4})=0.0080\,\mathrm W$. An arithmetic intensity mean is unsuitable because areas differ; the mean must be weighted. Adding intensities first and multiplying by the whole area would count each contribution over both regions.
