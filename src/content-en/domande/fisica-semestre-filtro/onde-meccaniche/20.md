A mass extends a vertical spring by $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ at equilibrium. It is then lowered another $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ and released from rest. What is amplitude relative to loaded equilibrium?

- $4\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $2\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $6\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $8\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

The oscillation centre is where weight and elastic force balance. Release occurs two centimetres below that centre, so amplitude is two centimetres. Eight centimetres is total extension from natural length, whereas four centimetres is the distance between the two turning points.
