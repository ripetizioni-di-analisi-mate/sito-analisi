Two sources start in antiphase; path difference introduces an additional phase shift of $\pi$. With equal local contributions, what results?

- Quadrature between contributions.
- Constructive interference.
- Complete cancellation.
- Necessary incoherence.

## Explanation

Initial and propagation phase shifts must be combined. Two shifts of $\pi$ give $2\pi$, equivalent to zero; opposite signs would also give zero. Local contributions become in phase. A half-integer path difference would give a minimum only for initially in-phase sources.
