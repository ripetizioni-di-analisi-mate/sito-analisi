A device obtains $d=\dfrac{v\Delta t}{2}$ assuming $1540\,\mathrm{m/s}$, but actual uniform speed is $1400\,\mathrm{m/s}$. With no other errors, how is distance affected?

- Correct, because the factor two removes the speed error.
- Overestimated by $21\%$.
- Overestimated by $10\%$.
- Underestimated by $10\%$.

## Explanation

At fixed measured time, reconstructed distance is proportional to assumed speed. The ratio is $\dfrac{1540}{1400}=1.10$. It is not squared, and the factor two appears in both actual and estimated distances, cancelling in their ratio. The error sign follows the assumed speed error.
