In the model $v=\sqrt{\dfrac{B}{\rho}}$, modulus $B$ quadruples and density doubles. What is $\dfrac{v_2}{v_1}$?

- $\sqrt2$.
- $2$.
- $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}$.
- $4$.

## Explanation

The ratio $\dfrac{B}{\rho}$ grows by $\dfrac{4}{2}=2$, so its square root grows by $\sqrt2$. Stiffness and inertia act in opposite directions in the formula. Considering modulus alone gives doubling; considering density alone gives a decrease.
