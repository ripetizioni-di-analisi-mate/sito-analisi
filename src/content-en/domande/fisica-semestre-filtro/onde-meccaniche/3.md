A $0.25\,\mathrm{kg}$ mass oscillates on an ideal $100\,\mathrm{N/m}$ spring. What is its period?

- $0.050\pi\,\mathrm s$.
- $0.20\pi\,\mathrm s$.
- $0.10\pi\,\mathrm s$.
- $0.0050\pi\,\mathrm s$.

## Explanation

The ratio $\dfrac{m}{K}=0.0025\,\mathrm{s^2}$ has square root $0.050\,\mathrm s$. Multiplying by $2\pi$ gives $T=0.10\pi\,\mathrm s$. Omitting the square root or confusing $\pi$ with $2\pi$ changes the result. The formula assumes linear elastic force and negligible friction.
