A point-like isotropic source emits steadily without absorption. Intensity falls with distance: what happens to power through complete spheres?

- It stays equal: growing area compensates for falling intensity.
- It falls as inverse radius squared.
- It increases as radius squared.
- It halves whenever radius doubles.

## Explanation

Power is $P=4\pi r^2I(r)$. With $I\propto r^{-2}$ the product stays constant, as required by steady energy balance without loss. Falling energy received by a small fixed-area sensor does not mean less energy crosses the whole sphere.
