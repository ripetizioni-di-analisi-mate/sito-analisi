A system measures total delay $50\,\mu\mathrm s$, including $10\,\mu\mathrm s$ instrumental delay. With $v=1500\,\mathrm{m/s}$, what is reflector distance along the echo path?

- $6.0\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $3.75\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $3.0\,\mathrm{cm}$.
- $4.5\,\mathrm{cm}$.

## Explanation

First subtract delay not caused by propagation: round-trip time is $40\,\mu\mathrm s$. Then apply $d=\dfrac{v\Delta t}{2}=3.0\,\mathrm{cm}$. Using fifty microseconds overestimates distance; omitting the factor two confuses total path with reflector distance.
