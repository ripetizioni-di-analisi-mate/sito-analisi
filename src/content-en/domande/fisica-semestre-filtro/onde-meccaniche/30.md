Two sensors are $0.60\,\mathrm m$ apart along a string. The same identifiable pulse reaches the second $3.0\,\mathrm{ms}$ after the first. What is mean propagation speed between the sensors?

- $100\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $200\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $1800\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $0.20\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

The delay is $0.0030\,\mathrm s$, so $v=\dfrac{0.60}{0.0030}=200\,\mathrm{m/s}$. This is one-way travel between sensors, not an echo, so no factor of two applies. Identifying the same pulse removes the ambiguity of matching crests from different sinusoidal cycles.
