A transverse sinusoid has $A=1.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $f=20\,\mathrm{Hz}$; propagation speed is $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the medium’s greatest transverse speed?

- $126\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $0.020\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $0.126\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

Maximum local speed is $A\omega=2\pi fA\simeq0.126\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Propagation speed instead describes phase movement along the medium. Using $Af$ omits $2\pi$; failing to convert amplitude from millimetres creates a factor-of-one-thousand error.
