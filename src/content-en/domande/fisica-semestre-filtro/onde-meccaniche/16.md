For $x=A\cos(\omega t+\varphi)$ with $A,\omega>0$, $x(0)=0$ and $v_x(0)>0$. Which initial phase is compatible?

- $+\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.
- $-\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.
- $0$.
- $\pi$.

## Explanation

Zero position requires $\cos\varphi=0$. Of the two candidate phases, $-\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ gives $v_x(0)=-A\omega\sin\varphi>0$, whereas $+\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ gives negative velocity. Zero and $\pi$ correspond to turning points, not equilibrium. Phases equivalent modulo $2\pi$ also work.
