At $2.0\,\mathrm m$ from an isotropic point source, intensity is $0.010\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$. Without absorption, what power is emitted?

- $0.010\pi\,\mathrm W$.
- $0.080\pi\,\mathrm W$.
- $0.16\pi\,\mathrm W$.
- $0.040\pi\,\mathrm W$.

## Explanation

The local measurement extends over the whole sphere because emission is isotropic. Area is $4\pi(2.0)^2=16\pi\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and $P=IS=0.16\pi\,\mathrm W$. Using a disc area loses a factor four; using circumference would not even give the required units.
