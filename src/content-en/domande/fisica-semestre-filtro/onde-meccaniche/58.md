A source keeps frequency fixed but increases amplitude in a linear medium. Which effect follows directly from the model?

- Frequency increases and period decreases.
- Wavelength decreases in proportion to amplitude.
- Intensity increases while period stays unchanged.
- Sound speed necessarily increases.

## Explanation

At fixed medium and frequency, intensity grows with amplitude squared. Period remains reciprocal to frequency, and speed is set by medium properties. A change related to loudness should not be confused with the frequency change associated with pitch.
