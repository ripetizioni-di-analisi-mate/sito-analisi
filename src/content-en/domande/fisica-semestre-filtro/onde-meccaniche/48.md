Two emitters have the same mean frequency, but their phase difference varies randomly during measurement. Which prediction is appropriate over times long compared with those variations?

- Amplitudes always add before squaring.
- The point necessarily remains a permanent node.
- Equal mean frequency guarantees zero relative phase.
- The cross term averages to zero if phases sample the cycle uniformly.

## Explanation

Coherence concerns stability of the phase relation, not merely equal mean frequency. If the cosine of relative phase averages to zero, the interference term disappears from the average and intensity contributions add under appropriate geometry. No stable maximum or minimum follows.
