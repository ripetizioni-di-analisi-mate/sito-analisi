A $2.0\,\mathrm m$ string has mass $0.040\,\mathrm{kg}$ and tension $8.0\,\mathrm N$. What speed follows from the ideal model?

- $20\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $400\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
- $\sqrt{200}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.

## Explanation

First obtain linear density: $\mu_\ell=\dfrac{0.040}{2.0}=0.020\,\mathrm{kg/m}$. Thus $v=\sqrt{\dfrac{8.0}{0.020}}=20\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Substituting total mass for $\mu_\ell$ is dimensionally inconsistent. Four hundred is speed squared, not speed.
