In an interference field, one sensor is at a minimum and another at a maximum. Which energy interpretation is compatible with the ideal model?

- Displacement is small at a minimum, so velocity and gradients must always vanish.
- Fluxes redistribute in space; a local minimum does not prove energy destruction.
- Every maximum creates additional energy without any flux change elsewhere.
- Every minimum necessarily converts all energy into heat.

## Explanation

Energy conservation must be assessed for the whole system and its fluxes, not from one local amplitude. Reinforcement and cancellation regions can coexist. Linear interference alone introduces no dissipation: automatically assigning heat to a minimum adds an unspecified process.
