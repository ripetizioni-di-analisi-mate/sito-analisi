In a weakly damped oscillator, amplitude changes from $A$ to $0.80A$ while the same spring remains linear. What is the ratio of mechanical energies measured at the two turning points?

- $1.25$.
- $0.64$.
- $0.20$.
- $0.80$.

## Explanation

At a turning point velocity is zero and elastic energy is $\dfrac{KA^2}{2}$. The ratio is therefore $0.80^2=0.64$. Damping has dissipated $36\%$ of initial energy, not $20\%$: the latter is the relative amplitude decrease. This comparison does not assume conserved energy.
