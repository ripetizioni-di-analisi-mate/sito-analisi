An ideally collimated beam crosses two equal areas without loss or spreading. The second is twice as far from the source. Which comparison is correct?

- The same power and mean intensity cross both.
- Power doubles because the path is twice as long.
- Power and intensity both fall to one quarter.
- Power stays equal and intensity falls to one quarter.

## Explanation

Lossless balance conserves power and the specified areas are equal, so $I=\dfrac{P}{S}$ also stays equal. The $\dfrac{1}{r^2}$ law follows from spherical spreading, absent from this model. Distance alone does not determine intensity without beam geometry.
