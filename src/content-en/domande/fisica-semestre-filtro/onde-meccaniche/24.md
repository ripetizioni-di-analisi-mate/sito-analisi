With $x$ a coordinate in metres and $t$ a time in seconds, which cosine argument is dimensionally correct for a wave?

- $(2\,\mathrm{s^{-1}})x-(6\,\mathrm{m^{-1}})t$.
- $(2\,\mathrm{m^{-1}})x-(6\,\mathrm{s^{-1}})t$.
- $(2\,\mathrm{m/s})x-(6\,\mathrm{m/s})t$.
- $(2\,\mathrm m)x-(6\,\mathrm s)t$.

## Explanation

Terms added in the phase must be dimensionless angles in radians. Wave number times position and angular frequency times time satisfy this requirement. The other products have different dimensions or fail to cancel units. Writing bare numbers would instead assume a numerical-value equation with declared units.
