Two contributions with amplitudes $3$ and $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ have stable phase difference $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. What amplitude results?

- $5\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $1\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $7\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $3.5\,\mathrm{mm}$.

## Explanation

The cross term is proportional to $\cos(\dfrac{\pi}{2})=0$. Amplitude is therefore $\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=5\,\mathrm{mm}$. Sum and difference are the extremes reached at phases zero and $\pi$; averaging amplitudes does not describe the requested superposition.
