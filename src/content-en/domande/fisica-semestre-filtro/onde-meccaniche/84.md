An isotropic point source quadruples power while the receiver doubles its distance. In a non-absorbing medium, how does received level change?

- It stays unchanged.
- It increases by $3.01\,\mathrm{dB}$.
- It decreases by $6.02\,\mathrm{dB}$.
- It increases by $6.02\,\mathrm{dB}$.

## Explanation

The intensity ratio is $(\dfrac{P_2}{P_1})(\dfrac{r_1}{r_2})^2=\dfrac{4}{4}=1$. The level difference is therefore zero. Considering only power would give an approximately six-decibel rise, whereas considering only distance would give an equal fall: both effects cancel.
