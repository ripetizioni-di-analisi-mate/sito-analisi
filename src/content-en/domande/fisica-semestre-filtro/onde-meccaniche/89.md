In the low-speed Doppler-echo model, $|\Delta f|\simeq(\dfrac{2f_su}{v})|\cos\theta|$, where $\theta$ is the angle between reflector velocity and beam. With all other data fixed, how does the magnitude change from $0^\circ$ to $60^\circ$?

- It stays equal because only speed matters.
- It becomes zero.
- It doubles.
- It halves.

## Explanation

The velocity component along the beam changes from $u$ to $u\cos60^\circ=\dfrac{u}{2}$. The frequency shift halves in the stated model; it would vanish at ninety degrees, not sixty. The factor two describes emission and reflection, but is common to both cases and cancels from the ratio.
