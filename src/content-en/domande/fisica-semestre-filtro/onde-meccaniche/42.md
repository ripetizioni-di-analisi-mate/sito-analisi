For $x=A\cos(\omega t)$, where is the oscillator after $\dfrac{T}{4}$ from starting at $x=A$?

- At $x=\dfrac{A}{2}$, with zero velocity.
- At equilibrium, with maximum-magnitude negative velocity.
- At equilibrium, with maximum-magnitude positive velocity.
- At $x=-A$, with zero velocity.

## Explanation

After a quarter-period, phase is $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$: cosine is zero and the derivative is $-A\omega$. The point has moved from the positive turning point to the centre. It reaches the negative turning point after half a period, not a quarter; at the centre force is zero but velocity is not.
