Two incoherent sources separately produce $60$ and $50\,\mathrm{dB}$ at the same point. What is total level? Assume travelling contributions in the same medium and direction.

- $110\,\mathrm{dB}$.
- $63.01\,\mathrm{dB}$.
- $60.41\,\mathrm{dB}$.
- $55\,\mathrm{dB}$.

## Explanation

In reference units, intensities are $10^6$ and $10^5$. Their sum is $1.1\cdot10^6$, giving $60+10\log_{10}(1.1)\simeq60.41\,\mathrm{dB}$. Levels are neither added nor averaged. An approximately three-decibel increase would require two equal-intensity contributions.
