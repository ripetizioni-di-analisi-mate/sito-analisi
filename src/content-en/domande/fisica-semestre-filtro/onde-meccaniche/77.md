Intensity level increases by $20\,\mathrm{dB}$ with the same reference. What is the final-to-initial intensity ratio?

- $20$.
- $10$.
- $100$.
- $2$.

## Explanation

The level difference is $10\log_{10}(\dfrac{I_2}{I_1})\,\mathrm{dB}$. Dividing the numerical value twenty by ten gives a logarithm of two, hence a ratio of one hundred. The reference cancels from the difference. Treating twenty decibels as a factor twenty confuses logarithmic and linear scales.
