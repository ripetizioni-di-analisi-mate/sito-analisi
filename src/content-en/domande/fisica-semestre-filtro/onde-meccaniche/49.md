A medium responds nonlinearly: doubling the drive also changes wave shape. Which conclusion about superposition is justified?

- Intensities can always be added directly instead of displacements.
- Preserving initial phase is sufficient to make total response linear.
- Superposition remains exact provided contributions have the same frequency.
- Adding separate solutions is not guaranteed to give the total solution.

## Explanation

Superposition is a property of linear equations. When response depends nonlinearly on the disturbance, adding solutions can generate terms absent from separate solutions. The observation does not determine a new universal intensity or phase law: a model of the nonlinearity is required. Equal frequency, controlled phase or replacing displacements with intensities cannot restore linear equations.
