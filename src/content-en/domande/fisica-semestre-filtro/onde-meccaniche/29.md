One photograph of a harmonic wave shows amplitude $2\,\mathrm{mm}$ and crest spacing $0.40\,\mathrm m$. Which additional datum allows frequency to be found?

- Propagation speed in the medium.
- Converting amplitude from millimetres to metres.
- Total crest count in the image without a time scale.
- Displacement sign at one point.

## Explanation

The photograph supplies a spatial scale, $\lambda$, but no time scale. Knowing $v$ allows $f=\dfrac{v}{\lambda}$. More crest counting in the same photograph, displacement sign, or amplitude units add no time information. Waves of different frequencies can share that profile when travelling in different media.
