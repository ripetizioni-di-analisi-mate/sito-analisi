An oscillator completes $50$ periods in $2.0\,\mathrm s$. Which frequency–period pair is correct?

- $0.040\,\mathrm{Hz}$ / $25\,\mathrm s$.
- $25\,\mathrm{Hz}$ / $0.040\,\mathrm s$.
- $25\,\mathrm{Hz}$ / $0.020\,\mathrm s$.
- $100\,\mathrm{Hz}$ / $0.010\,\mathrm s$.

## Explanation

Frequency is cycle count divided by duration: $f=\dfrac{50}{2.0\,\mathrm s}=25\,\mathrm{Hz}$. Its reciprocal gives $T=0.040\,\mathrm s$. The product of both results must be one. Multiplying count by duration does not give frequency, and halving the period again confuses half a cycle with a complete cycle.
