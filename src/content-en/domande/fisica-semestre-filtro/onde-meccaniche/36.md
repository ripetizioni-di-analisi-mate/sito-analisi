In a new string, both tension and linear density become four times their original values. Source frequency is unchanged. Which prediction is correct?

- Speed and wavelength double.
- Speed halves and wavelength doubles.
- Speed stays unchanged and wavelength quadruples.
- Speed and wavelength stay unchanged.

## Explanation

The ratio $\dfrac{F_T}{\mu_\ell}$ stays unchanged because numerator and denominator grow by the same factor. Speed, its square root, stays equal; at fixed frequency, $\lambda$ does too. Considering only greater tension would neglect the simultaneous inertia increase.
