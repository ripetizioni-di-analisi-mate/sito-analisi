For a travelling wave on a string with unchanged tension and linear density, $f$ doubles and $A$ halves. Which quantities remain unchanged?

- Wavelength and acceleration amplitude.
- Wavelength and mean power.
- Period and maximum transverse speed.
- Maximum transverse speed and mean power.

## Explanation

Maximum speed is $A\omega$: factors one half and two cancel. Mean power $\bar P=\dfrac{\mu_\ell v\omega^2A^2}{2}$ also stays constant. Propagation speed is fixed by string properties, so $\lambda=\dfrac{v}{f}$ and period halve. Maximum acceleration $A\omega^2$ doubles.
