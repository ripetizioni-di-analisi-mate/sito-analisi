For a travelling sound wave, $\xi=A\cos(kx-\omega t)$ and $p^{\prime}=BkA\sin(kx-\omega t)$. Where acoustic pressure has its positive maximum, what is displacement?

- $\dfrac{A}{2}$.
- $-A$.
- $+A$.
- $0$.

## Explanation

Positive maximum pressure requires sine equal to one. At the same phase cosine is zero, so displacement vanishes. Compression depends on displacement differences between neighbouring particles, not their absolute values. Pressure and displacement are in quadrature for the specified wave.
