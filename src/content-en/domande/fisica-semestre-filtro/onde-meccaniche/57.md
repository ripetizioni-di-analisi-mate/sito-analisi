An acoustic signal at $50\,\mathrm{kHz}$ has very low intensity. How is it classified relative to the conventional audible range?

- Infrasound, because intensity is low.
- Ultrasound only if amplitude also increases.
- Ultrasound, because frequency exceeds roughly $20\,\mathrm{kHz}$.
- Audible sound, because low intensity reduces frequency.

## Explanation

Conventional boundaries classify bands by frequency. Fifty kilohertz exceeds the nominal upper boundary regardless of intensity. Actual detectability requires further conditions, but lowering intensity does not shift a signal from ultrasound to infrasound.
