In the same medium, a sinusoidal plane travelling wave’s RMS pressure doubles. By how much does intensity level increase?

- $20\,\mathrm{dB}$.
- $6.02\,\mathrm{dB}$.
- $2.00\,\mathrm{dB}$.
- $3.01\,\mathrm{dB}$.

## Explanation

At unchanged impedance, intensity quadruples because it depends on squared RMS pressure. The difference is $10\log_{10}4\simeq6.02\,\mathrm{dB}$, equivalent to $20\log_{10}2$. Three decibels correspond to doubled intensity, not doubled pressure.
