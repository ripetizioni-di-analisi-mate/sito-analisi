For $y=(0.004\,\mathrm m)\cos[(2\pi\,\mathrm{m^{-1}})x-(20\pi\,\mathrm{s^{-1}})t]$, which description is correct?

- $A=4\,\mathrm{mm}$, $f=10\,\mathrm{Hz}$, $v=10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ towards $+x$.
- $A=4\,\mathrm{mm}$, $f=10\,\mathrm{Hz}$, $v=10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ towards $-x$.
- $A=4\,\mathrm{mm}$, $f=20\,\mathrm{Hz}$, $v=20\,\mathrm{m/s}$ towards $+x$.
- $A=0.004\,\mathrm{mm}$, $f=10\,\mathrm{Hz}$, $v=10\,\mathrm{m/s}$ towards $+x$.

## Explanation

Convert amplitude from metres to millimetres and divide angular frequency by $2\pi$: $f=10\,\mathrm{Hz}$. The ratio $\dfrac{\omega}{k}$ gives $10\,\mathrm{m/s}$. At constant phase, $kx-\omega t=C$ requires $x$ to increase with $t$: propagation is positive regardless of instantaneous displacement sign.
