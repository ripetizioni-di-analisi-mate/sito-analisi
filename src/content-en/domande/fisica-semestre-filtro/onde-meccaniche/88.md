At equal approach speed $u>0$, with $u<v$, compare a moving observer and stationary source with a moving source and stationary observer. Which case gives the larger $\dfrac{f_o}{f_s}$?

- They are exactly equal at every subsonic speed.
- Both ratios are less than one.
- The moving observer: $1+\dfrac{u}{v}>\dfrac{1}{1-\dfrac{u}{v}}$.
- The moving source: $\dfrac{1}{1-\dfrac{u}{v}}>1+\dfrac{u}{v}$.

## Explanation

For the moving observer the ratio is $1+\dfrac{u}{v}$; for the moving source it is $\dfrac{1}{1-\dfrac{u}{v}}$. Writing $q=\dfrac{u}{v}$, their difference is $\dfrac{q^2}{1-q}>0$. Both agree only to first order for small $q$, not exactly. The medium provides a physical reference, preventing an unconditional source–observer interchange.
