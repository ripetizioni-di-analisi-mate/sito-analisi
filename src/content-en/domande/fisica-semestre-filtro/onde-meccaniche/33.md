A periodic wave crosses a stationary interface: speed in the second medium is half that in the first. Which transmitted parameters change in the steady linear model?

- Frequency halves and wavelength stays equal.
- Frequency stays equal and wavelength doubles.
- Frequency stays equal and wavelength halves.
- Frequency and wavelength both halve.

## Explanation

The stationary interface oscillates at the source-imposed frequency. From $\lambda=\dfrac{v}{f}$, halving $v$ at constant $f$ halves wavelength. Halving both $f$ and $\lambda$ would instead reduce speed to one quarter, contradicting the given change.
