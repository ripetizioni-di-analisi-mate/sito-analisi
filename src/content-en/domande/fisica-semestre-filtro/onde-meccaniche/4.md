A $680\,\mathrm{Hz}$ sound wave travels at $340\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What is its wavelength?

- $1.0\,\mathrm m$.
- $0.25\,\mathrm m$.
- $2.0\,\mathrm m$.
- $0.50\,\mathrm m$.

## Explanation

In one period the wave travels one wavelength, so $\lambda=\dfrac{v}{f}=0.50\,\mathrm m$. Reversing the ratio gives inverse-length units. The check $f\lambda=680(0.50)\,\mathrm{m/s}$ recovers the given speed; no factor of two is required.
