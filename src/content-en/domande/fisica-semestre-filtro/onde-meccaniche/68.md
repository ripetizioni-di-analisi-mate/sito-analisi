For a sinusoidal plane travelling sound wave, a sensor gives peak acoustic-pressure amplitude $p_{\max}$. Which formula gives mean intensity?

- $I=\dfrac{p_{\max}^2}{\rho v}$.
- $I=\dfrac{p_{\max}^2}{2\rho v^2}$.
- $I=\dfrac{p_{\max}^2}{2\rho v}$.
- $I=\dfrac{2p_{\max}^2}{\rho v}$.

## Explanation

For a sinusoid, mean squared pressure is half its peak square: $p_{\mathrm{rms}}^2=\dfrac{p_{\max}^2}{2}$. Substitution into $I=\dfrac{p_{\mathrm{rms}}^2}{\rho v}$ gives the answer. The formula without two applies when the supplied value is already RMS. An extra power of $v$ would also alter physical dimensions.
