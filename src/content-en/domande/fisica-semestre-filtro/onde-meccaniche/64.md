A uniform beam has intensity $20\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$. It crosses a flat $0.10\,\mathrm{m^2}$ surface whose normal is at $60^\circ$ to propagation. What power crosses the surface?

- $2.0\,\mathrm W$.
- $4.0\,\mathrm W$.
- $1.0\,\mathrm W$.
- $1.73\,\mathrm W$.

## Explanation

The relevant area is the projection perpendicular to the beam: $S_\perp=S\cos60^\circ=0.050\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Thus $P=IS_\perp=1.0\,\mathrm W$. Sine would apply if the angle were defined relative to the plane rather than its normal. The tilted area intercepts no more energy than its projection.
