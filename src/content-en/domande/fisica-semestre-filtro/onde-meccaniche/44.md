Which expression describes a fixed-node pattern formed by two equal waves travelling in opposite directions?

- $A\cos(2kx+\omega t)$.
- $A\cos(kx-2\omega t)$.
- $2A\cos(kx)\cos(\omega t)$.
- $2A\cos(kx-\omega t)$.

## Explanation

The product separates spatial from temporal dependence. Wherever the spatial factor vanishes, displacement is zero at every instant. In the other expressions, space and time share one phase and constant-phase points move: these are travelling waves, potentially with different parameters.
