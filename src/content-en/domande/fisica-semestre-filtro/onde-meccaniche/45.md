Two coherent contributions with amplitudes $8$ and $3\,\mathrm{mm}$ are in antiphase. What is the resultant amplitude?

- $11\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $\sqrt{73}\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $0\,\mathrm{mm}$.
- $5\,\mathrm{mm}$.

## Explanation

In antiphase, contributions subtract and amplitude magnitude is $|8-3|=5\,\mathrm{mm}$. They do not cancel because amplitudes differ. Their sum gives the in-phase maximum, whereas the root of summed squares corresponds to quadrature.
