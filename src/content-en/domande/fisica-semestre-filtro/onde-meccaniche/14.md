A source approaches a receiver stationary in the medium, at less than sound speed. Which pair of changes ahead of the source is correct?

- Smaller front spacing and higher received frequency.
- Unchanged front spacing and greater sound speed.
- Smaller front spacing and unchanged received frequency.
- Greater front spacing and lower received frequency.

## Explanation

The source emits each new front from a position closer to the previous one along its motion. Forward spacing becomes $(v-u_s)T$, so the receiver encounters more fronts per second. Each front still moves at $v$ in the medium: source speed is not added to it.
