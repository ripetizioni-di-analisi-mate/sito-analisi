Two successive nodes of a standing wave are $0.30\,\mathrm m$ apart. What is the wavelength of its travelling components?

- $1.20\,\mathrm m$.
- $0.15\,\mathrm m$.
- $0.30\,\mathrm m$.
- $0.60\,\mathrm m$.

## Explanation

For $y=2A\cos(kx)\cos(\omega t)$, nodes satisfy $\cos(kx)=0$. Successive solutions differ by $\dfrac{\pi}{k}=\dfrac{\lambda}{2}$. The observed spacing is therefore half the travelling components’ wavelength. A node and adjacent antinode would instead be a quarter-wavelength apart.
