Which expression is equivalent to $-A\cos(\omega t)$ with $A>0$ while retaining positive amplitude?

- $A\cos(-\omega t)$.
- $A\cos(\omega t+\pi)$.
- $A\cos(2\omega t)$.
- $A\cos(\omega t+\dfrac{\pi}{2})$.

## Explanation

A minus sign is incorporated by phase shift $\pi$: cosine reverses sign after half a cycle. Reversing the argument’s sign is insufficient because cosine is even. A $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ shift exchanges maxima and zeros; doubling $\omega$ changes period instead.
