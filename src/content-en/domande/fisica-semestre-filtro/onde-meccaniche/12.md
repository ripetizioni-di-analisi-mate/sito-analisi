What is the level of $10^{-8}\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$ relative to $I_0=10^{-12}\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$?

- $4\,\mathrm{dB}$.
- $80\,\mathrm{dB}$.
- $40\,\mathrm{dB}$.
- $-40\,\mathrm{dB}$.

## Explanation

The dimensionless ratio is $10^4$; its decimal logarithm is four and the definition multiplies by ten. A factor of twenty applies to pressure ratios under suitable conditions, not this intensity ratio. The sign is positive because intensity exceeds the reference.
