An intensity level of $-10\,\mathrm{dB}$ relative to $I_0>0$ indicates which intensity?

- $0$.
- $10I_0$.
- $-\dfrac{I_0}{10}$.
- $\dfrac{I_0}{10}$.

## Explanation

Level is negative when $\dfrac{I}{I_0}$ is below one, not when intensity is negative. From $10\log_{10}(\dfrac{I}{I_0})=-10$, the ratio is $10^{-1}$. Zero intensity does not produce a finite level: in the model the logarithm tends to negative infinity.
