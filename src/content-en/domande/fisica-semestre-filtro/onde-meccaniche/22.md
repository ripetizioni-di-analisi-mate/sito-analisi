For an ideal mass–spring oscillator, what fraction of total energy is kinetic at $x=\dfrac{A}{2}$?

- $0$.
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$.
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
- $\dfrac{3}{4}$.

## Explanation

Potential energy as a fraction of the total is $\dfrac{x^2}{A^2}=\dfrac{1}{4}$. Energy conservation leaves kinetic fraction $1-\dfrac{1}{4}=\dfrac{3}{4}$. Quadratic dependence prevents half displacement from meaning half energy. The result is the same on both passages through that position despite opposite velocity signs.
