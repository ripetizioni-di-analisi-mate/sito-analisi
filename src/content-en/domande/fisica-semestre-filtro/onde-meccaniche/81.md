With a source on, the measured level is $60\,\mathrm{dB}$; with it off, incoherent background is $57\,\mathrm{dB}$. Approximately what is the source-only level? Assume travelling contributions in the same medium and direction.

- $60.0\,\mathrm{dB}$.
- $3.0\,\mathrm{dB}$.
- $57.0\,\mathrm{dB}$.
- $63.0\,\mathrm{dB}$.

## Explanation

Intensities must be subtracted: $\dfrac{I_s}{I_0}=10^6-10^{5.7}\simeq4.988\cdot10^5$. The source level is therefore about $56.98\,\mathrm{dB}$. Directly subtracting fifty-seven from sixty represents an intensity ratio through a level difference, not the residual contribution’s level.
