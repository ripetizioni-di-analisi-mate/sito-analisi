Two pure tones have equal physical intensity but very different frequencies. Is equal perceived loudness guaranteed?

- Yes: equal intensity requires equal frequency.
- No: the higher-pitched tone must always have greater intensity.
- Yes: intensity alone determines every aspect of perception.
- No: perceptual response also depends on frequency.

## Explanation

Intensity is a physical quantity, whereas perceived loudness depends on auditory response, which varies with frequency. Equal intensity does not make signals identical or guarantee equal perception. It also does not establish a perceptual ranking without further conditions.
