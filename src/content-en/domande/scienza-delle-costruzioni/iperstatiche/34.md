To calculate the vertical displacement at point $C$ of a beam, an upward unit force is applied at $C$ and gives $\displaystyle\int\frac{Mm}{EI}\,\mathrm dx=-4\,\mathrm{mm}$. Which statement is true?

- $C$ rises by $4\,\mathrm{mm}$
- The result is incorrect because the calculated displacement must be positive
- $C$ moves horizontally by $4\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $C$ moves downward by $4\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The unit-load method gives the signed displacement component along the unit force in its chosen direction. The unit force points upward, so a negative value indicates downward displacement. Therefore, **$C$ moves downward by $4\,\mathrm{mm}$**. A downward unit force would give $+4\,\mathrm{mm}$, describing the same physical displacement. The horizontal displacement component would require a horizontal unit force.
