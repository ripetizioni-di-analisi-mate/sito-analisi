A bar is restrained axially at both ends. The first segment has length $2\,\mathrm{m}$, with $EA_1=100\,000\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $\alpha_1\Delta T_1=3\cdot10^{-4}$. The second has length $1\,\mathrm{m}$, with $EA_2=50\,000\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $\alpha_2\Delta T_2=4\cdot10^{-4}$. Calculate the axial force, taking tension as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,0;C,3,0" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,incastro,180;C,incastro,0" quote="A,B,2 m;B,C,1 m" etichette="1,0.4,segment 1;2.5,0.4,segment 2" titolo="Two segments with different thermal strains"}

- $25\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-25\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-50\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The free thermal elongation is $0.001\,\mathrm{m}$. The total axial flexibility is $\dfrac2{100\,000}+\dfrac1{50\,000}=4\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{m/kN}$. Canceling the elongation requires $N=-25\,\mathrm{kN}$.
