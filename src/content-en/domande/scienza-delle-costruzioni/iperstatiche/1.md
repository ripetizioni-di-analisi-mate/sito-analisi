A cantilever of length $5\,\mathrm{m}$ has flexural rigidity $EI=12\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. A force of $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward at a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from the fixed end. Neglect shear deformation and find the downward deflection at the free end.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;C,2,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" forze="C,0,-1,P" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,3 m" titolo="Vertical point load within the span of a cantilever"}

- $4\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $9\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $13\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $26\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

Apply a downward unit load at the free end. The product of the two bending moments contributes to the integral only between the fixed end and the point of application of the actual load. This gives $\dfrac{P a^2(3L-a)}{6EI}=0.013\,\mathrm{m}$.
