A beam of length $4\,\mathrm m$ with $EI=12\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ is fixed at $A$ and rests at $B$ on a unilateral support that can only push upward. Before loading, the support settles by $20\,\mathrm{mm}$. A uniform downward load of intensity $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ is then applied. What is the reaction at $B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.6,q" frecce="4.55,0.12,4.55,-0.32,s" quote="A,B,4 m" titolo="Unilateral support lowered by 20 mm"}

- $-2.25\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $2.25\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

If the support were bilateral, compatibility with settlement $s$ would give
$$
R_B=\frac{3qL}{8}-\frac{3EI}{L^3}\,s=9\,\mathrm{kN}-11.25\,\mathrm{kN}=-2.25\,\mathrm{kN},
$$
that is, a downward force. A unilateral support cannot pull on the beam, so analyze the cantilever with the support removed. The free tip deflects downward by $\dfrac{qL^4}{8EI}=\dfrac{6\cdot256}{8\cdot12\,000}\,\mathrm m=16\,\mathrm{mm}$, less than the support settlement $20\,\mathrm{mm}$. The beam therefore does not reach the support and the **reaction is zero**.
