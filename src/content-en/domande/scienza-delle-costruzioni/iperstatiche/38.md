A beam fixed at both ends has span $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and $EI=16\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. With no applied loads, the right fixed support settles by $6\,\mathrm{mm}$ without rotating. The left support remains stationary. If a positive bending moment puts the bottom fibers in tension, what is the internal moment at the left end?

- $-36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

With no distributed load, the deflected shape is cubic. For $t=\dfrac{x}{L}$ and settlement $\Delta$ positive downward, the function $v=-\Delta(3t^2-2t^3)$ satisfies all four kinematic end conditions. At the left end, $M=EI\cdot v''(0)=-\dfrac{6EI\Delta}{L^2}$. This gives $-36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
