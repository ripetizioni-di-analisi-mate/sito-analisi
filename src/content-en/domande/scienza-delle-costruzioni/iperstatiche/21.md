A closed rectangular frame consists of four members connected by rigid joints. It is supported by a pin at $A$ and a roller with a vertical reaction at $B$. Which release makes the structure statically determinate?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,4,3;D,0,3" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-A" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" titolo="Closed frame with statically determinate external supports"}

- An internal hinge at one joint
- A complete cut through a cross section, releasing $N$, $T$, and $M$
- Internal hinges at two joints
- Replacing the pin at $A$ with a roller

## Explanation

The three external reactions restrain all rigid-body modes of the frame and can be determined from overall equilibrium. The internal forces and moments remain indeterminate because a single cut cannot divide a closed loop into two free bodies. A complete cut releases three internal force and moment components and turns the loop into a statically determinate open frame. The closed frame is therefore **internally statically indeterminate to degree $3$**. One hinge releases one internal action and two hinges release two, leaving one degree of indeterminacy in the latter case. Replacing the pin with a roller removes a necessary external restraint and makes the structure unstable.
