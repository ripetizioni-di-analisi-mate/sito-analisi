A cantilever of length $4\,\mathrm m$ with $EI=16\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ carries a uniform downward load of intensity $10\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. A vertical spring of stiffness $k=1\,500\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ supports its tip. The spring is unstressed before loading. What is the force in the spring?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,pendolo,270" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.6,q" etichette="4.45,-0.55,k" quote="A,B,4 m" titolo="Cantilever with a spring support at its tip"}

- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $30\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Remove the spring and choose its tip force $X$ as the redundant, taking upward as positive. On the released cantilever, the load lowers the tip by $\dfrac{qL^4}{8EI}=0.02\,\mathrm m$ and an upward unit force raises it by $\dfrac{L^3}{3EI}=\dfrac{1}{750}\,\mathrm{m/kN}$. Compressed by $X$, the spring shortens by $\dfrac Xk$. The tip lowers by the same amount, giving the compatibility condition
$$
\frac{qL^4}{8EI}-\frac{L^3}{3EI}X=\frac Xk,\qquad X=\frac{0.02}{\frac1{750}+\frac1{1\,500}}\,\mathrm{kN}=10\,\mathrm{kN}.
$$
The spring flexibility adds to the beam flexibility, so the force is less than the rigid-support value of $15\,\mathrm{kN}$.
