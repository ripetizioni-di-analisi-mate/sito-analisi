A propped cantilever $AB$ of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$ is fixed at $A$ and has a vertical roller reaction at $B$. A uniform downward load of intensity $15\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts only over the first $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. $EI$ is constant. What is the vertical reaction at $B$, taking upward as positive?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,6,0" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" carichi="A,C,0.8,0.8,15 kN/m" quote="A,C,3 m;C,B,3 m"}

- $4.92\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $16.88\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $33.75\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $2.46\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

In the released cantilever, a differential force $q\,ds$ at $s$ contributes $\dfrac{qs^2(3L-s)\,ds}{6EI}$ to the downward tip deflection. Integrating from $0$ to $a$ gives $\dfrac{q\left(La^3-\dfrac{a^4}{4}\right)}{6EI}$. Equate this to $\dfrac{R_B L^3}{3EI}$, the upward deflection due to the prop, to obtain $R_B$. This gives $4.92\,\mathrm{kN}$.
