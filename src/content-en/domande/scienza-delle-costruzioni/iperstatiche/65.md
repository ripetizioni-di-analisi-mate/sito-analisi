For two redundants in a beam deforming in bending, the calculated coefficients are $f_{11}=0.24\,\mathrm{mm/kN}$ and $f_{22}=0.30\,\mathrm{mm/kN}$. Which value of the off-diagonal coefficient $f_{12}$ is incompatible with these diagonal terms?

- $0.06\,\mathrm{mm/kN}$
- $-0.05\,\mathrm{mm/kN}$
- $0.20\,\mathrm{mm/kN}$
- $0.30\,\mathrm{mm/kN}$

## Explanation

The flexibility matrix is symmetric and positive definite, so its determinant must be positive, requiring
$$
f_{12}^2<f_{11}f_{22}=0.072\,\mathrm{(mm/kN)^2}.
$$
Therefore, $|f_{12}|<\sqrt{0.072}\approx0.268\,\mathrm{mm/kN}$. For $0.30$ the result is $f_{12}^2=0.09$, which exceeds $0.072$. The quadratic form would be negative for some combination of the two unit loads, implying negative strain energy. Values $0.06$, $-0.05$, and $0.20$ satisfy the inequality. A negative off-diagonal sign depends only on the directions assigned to the redundants.
