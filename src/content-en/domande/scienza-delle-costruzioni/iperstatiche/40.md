A simply supported beam of span $6\,\mathrm m$ with $EI=16\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ carries a downward force of $36\,\mathrm{kN}$ at section $C$, a distance $2\,\mathrm m$ from left support $A$. How far does $C$ deflect downward?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1,P" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,4 m" titolo="Off-center point load"}

- $10.1\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $4\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $16\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $8\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

Apply the unit force at the same location as the actual load. Its moment diagram is therefore the actual moment diagram divided by $P$. The actual moment diagram is triangular, with peak $\dfrac{Pab}{L}=\dfrac{36\cdot2\cdot4}{6}=48\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at $C$. The virtual moment has peak $\dfrac{ab}{L}=\dfrac43\,\mathrm m$. The integral of the product of two triangular diagrams with aligned peaks equals one-third of the span times the product of their peak values, giving
$$
v_C=\frac{1}{EI}\cdot\frac{48\cdot\frac43\cdot6}{3}=\frac{Pa^2b^2}{3EIL}=\frac{128}{16\,000}\,\mathrm m=8\,\mathrm{mm}.
$$
The midspan deflection formula $\dfrac{PL^3}{48EI}$ applies only to a force at midspan.
