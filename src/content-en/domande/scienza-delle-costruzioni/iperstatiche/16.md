A portal frame consists of two columns of height $3\,\mathrm m$ and a crossbeam of length $4\,\mathrm m$ connected by rigid joints. Both bases $A$ and $D$ are fixed to the ground. What is the degree of static indeterminacy of the planar structure?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;C,4,3;D,4,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" vincoli="A,incastro;D,incastro" quote="B,C,4 m,-0.6;D,C,3 m,0.7" titolo="Portal frame with fixed bases"}

- $6$
- $3$
- $4$
- $2$

## Explanation

Each fixed support prevents two translations and one rotation, giving $6$ reaction components in total. Joints $B$ and $C$ are rigid and have no internal hinges. The entire frame can therefore be treated as one body, giving $3$ independent equilibrium equations. The frame is stable, so its degree of static indeterminacy is the difference
$$
6-3=3 .
$$
The force method thus requires three redundant unknowns, for example the three reactions at base $D$. Releasing them leaves the primary frame fixed at $A$, with an L-shaped load path.
