A beam of span $4\,\mathrm{m}$ is fixed at both ends. The downward vertical load increases linearly from zero at the left end to $15\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at the right. $EI$ is constant. If a positive bending moment puts the bottom fibers in tension, what is the internal moment at the right fixed end?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,incastro,0" carichi="A,B,0,1,15 kN/m" quote="A,B,4 m"}

- $-8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The bending moment is $M_A+R_A x-\dfrac{qx^3}{6L}$. Because both displacements and rotations vanish at the fixed ends, $\int M\,dx$ and $\int(L-x)M\,dx$ over the full span are both zero. The equations give $R_A=\dfrac{3qL}{20}$ and $M_A=-\dfrac{qL^2}{30}$. Evaluating $M$ at $x=L$ gives $M_B=-\dfrac{qL^2}{20}$. This gives $-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
