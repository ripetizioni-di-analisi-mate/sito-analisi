A bar of length $4\,\mathrm m$ with $EA=100\,000\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $\alpha=12\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$ is restrained axially at ends $A$ and $B$. At midlength $C$ an axial force of $40\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts toward $B$, and the temperature increases uniformly by $25\,\mathrm K$. What is the axial force in segment $AC$, taking tension as positive?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,incastro,0" forze="C,0.8,0,F" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,2 m" etichette="1,0.45,ΔT;3,0.45,ΔT" titolo="Axial force and uniform heating"}

- $20\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-30\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-10\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-50\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Because the problem is linear, superpose the effects of the force and the temperature change. The two equal segments divide the midpoint force equally between the supports, giving $+20\,\mathrm{kN}$ in segment $AC$, which elongates, and $-20\,\mathrm{kN}$ in segment $CB$. Heating the bar with its ends restrained produces the following uniform axial force.
$$
-EA\alpha\Delta T=-100\,000\cdot12\cdot10^{-6}\cdot25\,\mathrm{kN}=-30\,\mathrm{kN}.
$$
Segment $AC$ therefore carries $N=20-30=-10\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression, and segment $CB$ carries $N=-50\,\mathrm{kN}$.
