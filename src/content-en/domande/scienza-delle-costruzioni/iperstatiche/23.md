A continuous beam $A\text{–}B\text{–}C$ is fixed at $A$ and propped vertically at $B$. $AB$ has length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and $BC$ has length $2\,\mathrm{m}$. A downward force of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $C$. $EI$ is constant. What is the reaction at $B$, taking upward as positive?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,5,0" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1,12 kN" quote="A,B,3 m;B,C,2 m"}

- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $48\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $24\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

With the prop removed, the load at $C$ produces a downward displacement $\dfrac{Pa^2(3L-a)}{6EI}$ at $B$, with $a=AB$ and $L=AC$. The upward reaction produces displacement $\dfrac{R_B a^3}{3EI}$ at $B$. The support condition requires the two magnitudes to be equal. This gives $24\,\mathrm{kN}$.
