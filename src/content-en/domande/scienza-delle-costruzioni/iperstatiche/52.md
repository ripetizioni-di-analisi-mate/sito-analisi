A horizontal rigid beam hangs from three identical vertical ties attached at coordinates $-2\,\mathrm m$, $0$, and $2\,\mathrm m$. A downward force of $60\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at coordinate $1\,\mathrm m$. What is the tensile force in the left tie?

::struttura{nodi="S1,-2,2;S2,0,2;S3,2,2;T1,-2,0;T2,0,0;T3,2,0" nomi=0 aste="T1-T3" catene="S1-T1;S2-T2;S3-T3" vincoli="S1,cerniera,90;S2,cerniera,90;S3,cerniera,90" forze="1 0,0,-1,F" quote="T1,T2,2 m,0.6;T2,T3,2 m,0.6" titolo="Rigid beam suspended from three identical ties"}

- $20\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $35\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $5\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Because the beam is rigid, the tie elongations vary linearly with their horizontal coordinate. This gives the compatibility condition. The ties are identical, so their tensions also vary linearly, $N_i=\alpha+\beta x_i$. Vertical force equilibrium gives $3\alpha=60\,\mathrm{kN}$, so $\alpha=20\,\mathrm{kN}$. Taking moments about the origin gives
$$
\beta\left(2^2+0^2+2^2\right)=60\cdot1,\qquad \beta=7.5\,\mathrm{kN/m}.
$$
The left tie therefore carries $N=20-7.5\cdot2=5\,\mathrm{kN}$, the middle tie $20\,\mathrm{kN}$, and the right tie $35\,\mathrm{kN}$.
