An elastic beam has two interior sections $C$ and $D$. The required displacement is $\theta_D-\theta_C$, with counterclockwise positive for both rotations. Which virtual loads produce precisely this coordinate as their external work on the actual deformation?

- Two counterclockwise unit moments.
- Opposite unit vertical forces.
- A single counterclockwise unit moment at $D$.
- Unit moments, clockwise at $C$ and counterclockwise at $D$.

## Explanation

Virtual moments acting through actual rotations do work $C_C^*\theta_C+C_D^*\theta_D$. To obtain the difference, take $C_C^*=-1$ and $C_D^*=+1$. A pair of vertical forces would select a difference of vertical displacements.
