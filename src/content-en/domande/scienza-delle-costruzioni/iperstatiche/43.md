A simply supported beam of span $6\,\mathrm m$ with $EI=15\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ carries only a counterclockwise couple of $30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at its right end $B$. What is the rotation of the left end $A$, taking counterclockwise as positive?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" coppie="B,1" etichette="5.4,0.6,C" quote="A,B,6 m" titolo="Couple applied at end B"}

- $0.002\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $0.004\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $-0.004\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $-0.002\,\mathrm{rad}$

## Explanation

The reactions balancing the couple have magnitude $\dfrac CL=5\,\mathrm{kN}$ and act upward at $A$ and downward at $B$. The actual moment is $M=\dfrac{Cx}{L}$ and puts the bottom fibers in tension. A counterclockwise unit couple at $A$ gives $m=\dfrac xL-1$. Therefore
$$
\varphi_A=\frac{C}{EI}\int_0^L\frac xL\left(\frac xL-1\right)\mathrm dx=-\frac{CL}{6EI}=-\frac{30\cdot6}{6\cdot15\,000}\,\mathrm{rad}=-0.002\,\mathrm{rad}.
$$
The negative sign denotes clockwise rotation of $A$. Section $B$ rotates counterclockwise by $\dfrac{CL}{3EI}=0.004\,\mathrm{rad}$ because the corresponding virtual moment diagram equals the actual diagram divided by $C$.
