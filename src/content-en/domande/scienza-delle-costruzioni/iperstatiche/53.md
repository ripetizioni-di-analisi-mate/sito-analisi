Two columns with the same $EI$ support a rigid horizontal crossbeam. Both columns have fixed bases and rigid connections to the crossbeam. The left column has height $2\,\mathrm m$ and the right column height $4\,\mathrm m$. The crossbeam can translate horizontally without rotating and carries a horizontal force of $45\,\mathrm{kN}$. What is the shear force in the left column?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,2;C,5,2;D,5,-2" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" vincoli="A,incastro;D,incastro" forze="B,1,0,F" quote="A,B,2 m,-0.7;D,C,4 m,0.7" titolo="Rigid crossbeam on columns of unequal height"}

- $40\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $22.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $30\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The crossbeam translates by $u$ without rotating. Thus each column behaves as a beam fixed at its base with its top translating without rotation. For this structure the force method, using the top couple as the redundant, gives shear $T=\dfrac{12EI}{h^3}\,u$. The lateral stiffness is therefore proportional to $\dfrac1{h^3}$. The columns have the same displacement $u$ and share the force in proportion to their stiffnesses, in the ratio $\dfrac18$ to $\dfrac1{64}$. The left column therefore carries
$$
T=45\cdot\frac{\frac18}{\frac18+\frac1{64}}\,\mathrm{kN}=45\cdot\frac89\,\mathrm{kN}=40\,\mathrm{kN}.
$$
The shorter column is eight times as stiff and carries the larger share of the force.
