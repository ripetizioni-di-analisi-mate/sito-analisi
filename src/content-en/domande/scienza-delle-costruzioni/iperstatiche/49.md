In the propped cantilever shown, with $EI=16\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$, the fixed end $A$ and roller $B$ support a uniform load of intensity $12\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Once the redundant reaction is known, what is the rotation at the supported section $B$? Take counterclockwise rotation as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,B,4 m" titolo="Propped cantilever"}

- $-0.001\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $0.002\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $0.008\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $0.001\,\mathrm{rad}$

## Explanation

Compatibility gives $R_B=\dfrac{3qL}{8}=18\,\mathrm{kN}$. Calculate the rotation on the cantilever carrying the applied load and $R_B$, which together reproduce the actual structure. A counterclockwise unit couple at $B$ produces $m=1$ over the entire cantilever. The actual moment is $M=R_B(L-x)-\dfrac q2(L-x)^2$. Therefore
$$
\varphi_B=\frac1{EI}\int_0^L M\,\mathrm dx=\frac1{EI}\left(\frac{R_BL^2}{2}-\frac{qL^3}{6}\right)=\frac{144-128}{16\,000}\,\mathrm{rad}=10^{-3}\,\mathrm{rad}.
$$
The result is $\dfrac{qL^3}{48EI}$ counterclockwise, consistent with the deflected centerline rising toward support $B$.
