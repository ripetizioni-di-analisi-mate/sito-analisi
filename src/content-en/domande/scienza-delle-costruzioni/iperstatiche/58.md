A continuous beam on three stationary supports has two equal spans and constant $EI$. A uniform load acts on both spans. Which change leaves the reaction at the middle support unchanged?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,8,0" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;C,carrello" carichi="A,C,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,B,L;B,C,L" titolo="Continuous beam on three supports"}

- Move the middle support to make the spans unequal
- Double the load intensity
- Double $EI$ along the entire beam
- Impose a settlement of $2\,\mathrm{mm}$ at the middle support

## Explanation

The middle support reaction is $X=-\dfrac{\delta_0}{f_{11}}$. With constant $EI$, both $\delta_0$ and $f_{11}$ are proportional to $\dfrac1{EI}$. The rigidity cancels, so $X=\dfrac{5qL}{4}$ is independent of $EI$. Doubling $EI$ throughout the beam consequently leaves the reaction unchanged. Moving the middle support changes the shapes of diagrams $M_0$ and $m$, changing their ratio and hence the reaction. The load enters $\delta_0$ linearly, so doubling the load doubles the reaction. A settlement adds the nonzero term $\dfrac{\Delta}{f_{11}}$.
