In the force method, the redundant $X$ is a couple measured in $\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Its conjugate coordinate is a rotation in radians. What are the units of the flexibility coefficient $f_{11}$?

- $\mathrm{m/kN}$
- $\mathrm{kN\,m/rad}$
- $\mathrm{rad/(kN\,m)}$
- $\mathrm{rad}$

## Explanation

Coefficient $f_{11}$ is the rotation produced by a unit couple in the direction of $X$. Thus $f_{11}X$ in the compatibility equation is a rotation, and $f_{11}$ has units of rotation per moment. The same result follows from $f_{11}=\displaystyle\int\frac{m^2}{EI}\,\mathrm dx$ because the moment $m$ due to a unit couple is dimensionless, giving the integral the units of $\dfrac{\mathrm m}{\mathrm{kN\,m^2}}=\dfrac{1}{\mathrm{kN\,m}}$. Unit $\mathrm{kN\,m/rad}$ belongs to rotational stiffness, the reciprocal of flexibility.
