In the force method the redundant moments $X_B,X_C$, in kN m, satisfy $4X_B+X_C=15$ and $X_B+4X_C=-15$. What is $X_B-X_C$?

- $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

Adding the rows gives $5(X_B+X_C)=0$, so the two moments are opposite. Subtracting row two from row one gives $3(X_B-X_C)=30$ and a difference of $10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The antisymmetric right-hand side excites only the antisymmetric combination of redundant moments.
