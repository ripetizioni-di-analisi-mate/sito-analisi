A truss has joints $A$, $B$, $C$, and $D$ at the corners of a square of side $3\,\mathrm m$. Its members are the four sides and two diagonals. The diagonals cross without being connected. Joint $A$ is pinned and joint $B$ has a roller with a vertical reaction. What is the degree of static indeterminacy?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,3,3;D,0,3" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-A;A-C;B-D" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,B,3 m" titolo="Square truss with two diagonals"}

- $1$
- $0$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

Each joint of a plane truss gives two force equilibrium equations, so $n=4$ joints give $2n=8$ equations. The unknowns are the forces in the $b=6$ members and the $r=3$ external support reactions. The degree of static indeterminacy is therefore
$$
b+r-2n=6+3-8=1 .
$$
A square with a single diagonal already forms a stable truss, so the second diagonal is redundant. The external reactions are statically determinate, so the indeterminacy is internal. Cutting either diagonal releases a redundant member force.
