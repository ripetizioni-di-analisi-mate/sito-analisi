A statically determinate portal frame has columns of height $4\,\mathrm m$, a crossbeam of length $6\,\mathrm m$, a pin at its left base $A$, and a roller with a vertical reaction at its right base $D$. Only the crossbeam undergoes a uniform temperature increase of $40\,\mathrm K$, with $\alpha=12\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$. What is the horizontal displacement of the roller support?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,6,4;D,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,carrello" etichette="3,3.55,ΔT" quote="B,C,6 m,-0.7;D,C,4 m,0.7" titolo="Statically determinate portal frame with a heated crossbeam"}

- $0\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $5.76\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right
- $2.88\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the left
- $2.88\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right

## Explanation

Apply a horizontal unit force to the right at $D$. The horizontal reaction at pin $A$ balances it. The two opposing forces produce tensile axial force $n=1$ in the crossbeam and only bending moments in the columns. Thermal strain is axial and occurs only in the crossbeam, so only the term $\int n\,\varepsilon_T\,\mathrm dx$ enters the internal work, giving
$$
u_D=1\cdot\alpha\Delta T\,b=12\cdot10^{-6}\cdot40\cdot6\,\mathrm m=2.88\,\mathrm{mm}.
$$
The frame is statically determinate, so the roller moves to the right without developing internal forces or moments.
