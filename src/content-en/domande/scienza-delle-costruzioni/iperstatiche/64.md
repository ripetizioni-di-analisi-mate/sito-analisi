Continuous beam $A$–$B$–$C$ has three supports, two equal spans of length $L$, constant $EI$, and a uniform downward load $q$ on both spans. Use symmetry about support $B$ and consider only span $AB$. Which structural model is equivalent to span $AB$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,8,0" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;C,carrello" carichi="A,C,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,B,L;B,C,L" titolo="Continuous beam symmetric about B"}

- A beam simply supported at $A$ and fixed at $B$
- A beam simply supported at $A$ and $B$
- A cantilever fixed at $A$ and free at $B$
- A beam fixed at $A$ and $B$

## Explanation

The structure and load are symmetric about the vertical through $B$. The deflected shape is therefore symmetric, and the section over $B$ has zero rotation. The support also prevents vertical displacement at $B$. Vertical displacement and rotation are both zero at $B$, so span $AB$ behaves as a beam simply supported at $A$ and fixed at $B$. The moment over the support is therefore $-\dfrac{qL^2}{8}$, as at the fixed end of a propped cantilever. Each span contributes $\dfrac{5qL}{8}$ to the reaction at $B$, giving a total of $\dfrac{5qL}{4}$.
