For a beam with bending deformation only, two redundants $X_1$ and $X_2$ have linearly independent unit-load diagrams and flexibility coefficients $f_{ij}=\displaystyle\int\frac{m_im_j}{EI}\,\mathrm dx$. Which statement is true?

- $f_{12}=f_{21}>0$ for any choice of positive directions
- $f_{12}=-f_{21}$
- $f_{12}=f_{21}$ and $f_{11}f_{22}-f_{12}^2>0$
- $f_{11}=f_{22}$ for any pair of redundants

## Explanation

Interchanging the indices merely interchanges the two factors in the product $m_im_j$, so $f_{12}=f_{21}$. The quadratic form $\displaystyle\int\frac{(a_1m_1+a_2m_2)^2}{EI}\,\mathrm dx$ is positive for every nonzero pair $(a_1,a_2)$ because the diagrams are independent. A symmetric quadratic form in two variables is positive definite if and only if $f_{11}>0$ and the determinant $f_{11}f_{22}-f_{12}^2$ is positive. The sign of $f_{12}$ depends on the directions chosen for the redundants and changes if one direction is reversed. The diagonal terms generally differ, as for two supports at different distances from the fixed end of a cantilever.
