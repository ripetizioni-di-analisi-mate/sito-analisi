A portal frame has columns of height $3\,\mathrm m$, a crossbeam of length $6\,\mathrm m$, rigid joints, and constant $EI$. Bases $A$ and $D$ are pinned to the ground. The crossbeam carries a uniform downward load of intensity $12\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Neglecting axial deformation, what is the magnitude of the horizontal reaction at each base?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;C,6,3;D,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,cerniera" carichi="B,C,0.6,0.6,q" quote="B,C,6 m,-1.2;D,C,3 m,0.7" titolo="Two-hinged portal frame with a load on the crossbeam"}

- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $18\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Replace the pin at $D$ by a roller with a vertical reaction and take thrust $X$ as the redundant, with both horizontal reactions pointing inward. In the primary structure, only the crossbeam develops a bending moment under the load, $M_0=\dfrac q2x(b-x)$. The two unit forces produce moments of magnitude $y$ in the columns, where $y$ is the elevation, and constant magnitude $h$ in the crossbeam. They put the fibers opposite to those under $M_0$ in tension. Therefore
$$
\delta_0=-\frac{h}{EI}\int_0^b\frac q2x(b-x)\,\mathrm dx=-\frac{qhb^3}{12EI}=-\frac{648}{EI},\qquad
f_{11}=\frac1{EI}\left(\frac{2h^3}{3}+h^2b\right)=\frac{72}{EI}.
$$
The compatibility condition $\delta_0+f_{11}X=0$ gives $X=9\,\mathrm{kN}$. With the roller alone, the bases would spread apart. The thrust restores their original separation.
