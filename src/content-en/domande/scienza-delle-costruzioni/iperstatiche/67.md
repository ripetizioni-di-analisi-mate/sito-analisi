A beam of span $L$ with constant $EI$ is fixed at both ends. It carries a downward force $P$ at a distance $\dfrac L4$ from its left end and an upward force $P$ at a distance $\dfrac L4$ from its right end. Which quantity vanishes at midspan?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,incastro,0" forze="2 0,0,-1,P;6 0,0,1,P" quote="A,2 0,L/4;6 0,B,L/4" titolo="Loading antisymmetric about midspan"}

- The shear force
- The rotation
- The shear force and bending moment
- The bending moment

## Explanation

The structure is symmetric about midspan, while the loading is antisymmetric. Reflecting each force about midspan brings it to the position of the oppositely directed force. The response is therefore antisymmetric. Deflection and bending moment have opposite signs at mirror-image sections. Shear force and rotation have equal values at these sections. At midspan an antisymmetric quantity must equal its own negative and therefore vanishes. Thus the **bending moment** and the deflection vanish at midspan, while the shear force and rotation are generally nonzero.
