A simply supported beam of span $4\,\mathrm m$ with $EI=20\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ carries a downward force of $40\,\mathrm{kN}$ at midspan. Use a unit couple to find the rotation at the left support $A$, taking counterclockwise as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1,P" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,2 m" titolo="Simply supported beam with a midspan force"}

- $0.002\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $-0.004\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $-0.002\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $-0.001\,\mathrm{rad}$

## Explanation

A counterclockwise unit couple at $A$ is balanced by reactions $\dfrac1L$, upward at $A$ and downward at $B$. The virtual moment is therefore $m=\dfrac xL-1$, equal to $-1$ at $A$ and zero at $B$. The actual moment diagram is triangular, with $M=20x$ up to midspan and $M=20(4-x)$ beyond it. Therefore
$$
\varphi_A=\frac1{EI}\int_0^4 M\,m\,\mathrm dx=-\frac{PL^2}{16EI}=-\frac{40\cdot16}{16\cdot20\,000}\,\mathrm{rad}=-0.002\,\mathrm{rad}.
$$
The negative sign indicates a clockwise rotation of $A$, consistent with the deflected centerline sloping downward toward midspan.
