A simply supported beam of span $6\,\mathrm m$ with $EI=45\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ carries a uniform downward load $16\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Use the unit-load method to find the downward deflection at midspan.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,B,6 m" titolo="Simply supported beam under a uniform load"}

- $9.6\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $3\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $6\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The actual moment is $M=\dfrac q2x(L-x)=8x(6-x)$. A downward unit force at midspan produces a triangular bending moment diagram $m=\dfrac x2$ for $0\le x\le3\,\mathrm m$, mirrored in the other half. By symmetry, integrate over half the beam and double the result, giving
$$
v_C=\frac{2}{EI}\int_0^3 8x(6-x)\,\frac x2\,\mathrm dx=\frac{270}{45\,000}\,\mathrm m=6\,\mathrm{mm}.
$$
The result agrees with $\dfrac{5qL^4}{384EI}$. Both moment diagrams put the bottom fibers in tension. Their product is therefore positive and the midpoint deflects downward.
