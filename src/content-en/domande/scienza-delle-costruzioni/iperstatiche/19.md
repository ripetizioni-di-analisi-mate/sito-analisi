A horizontal beam $AB$ is fixed at $A$ and pinned at $B$. Its degree of static indeterminacy is $2$. Release two restraint components to obtain the primary structure. Which choice makes the structure unstable?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,cerniera" quote="A,B,5 m" titolo="Beam fixed at A and pinned at B"}

- Remove the pin at $B$
- Release rotation at $A$ and horizontal translation at $B$
- Release horizontal translation at both $A$ and $B$
- Release vertical translation at $A$ and horizontal translation at $B$

## Explanation

If horizontal translation is released at both $A$ and $B$, no remaining restraint prevents horizontal rigid-body motion of the beam. Three reactions remain, the two vertical forces and the couple at $A$. The structure cannot balance a horizontal force, so it is **unstable**. Removing the pin at $B$ leaves a stable cantilever fixed at $A$. Releasing rotation at $A$ and horizontal translation at $B$ gives a stable beam with a pin at $A$ and a roller at $B$. Releasing vertical translation at $A$ turns the fixed end into a guided support that restrains horizontal translation and rotation. $B$ still restrains vertical translation, so this structure is also stable.
