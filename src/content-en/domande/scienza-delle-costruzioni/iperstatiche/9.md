A prismatic beam of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$ is fixed at $A$ and supported vertically at $B$ and $C$, at distances $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. A uniform load of intensity $14\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts downward. Calculate the vertical reaction at $B$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello;C,carrello" carichi="A,C,0.7,0.7,q" quote="A,B,3 m;B,C,3 m" titolo="Cantilever with intermediate and tip supports"}

- $24\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $48\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $36\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $60\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Impose zero deflection at $3$ and $6\,\mathrm{m}$ on the released cantilever. Multiplying the flexibility matrix by $EI$ gives the row expressions $(9,22.5)$ and $(22.5,72)$. The right-hand terms are $\dfrac{3213}{4}$ and $2268$, giving $R_B=48\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $R_C=16.5\,\mathrm{kN}$.
