A beam fixed at both ends has $EI=14\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. A temperature gradient constant along the beam would produce a free positive curvature of $0.8\cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$. The supports remain stationary. Given $M=EI(v''-\kappa_{\mathrm{thermal}})$, what is the internal moment at midspan?

- $0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-11.2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-5.6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $11.2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The fixed ends restrain the uniform thermal curvature. The solution $v=0$ satisfies the kinematic conditions and gives a constant moment, consistent with equilibrium under zero transverse load. It follows that $M=-EI\kappa_{\mathrm{thermal}}$. This gives $-11.2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
