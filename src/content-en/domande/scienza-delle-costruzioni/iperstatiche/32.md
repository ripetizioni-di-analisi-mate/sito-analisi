Beam $AB$ of span $6\,\mathrm{m}$ is fixed at both ends and has constant $EI$. A downward force of $27\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. If a positive bending moment puts the bottom fibers in tension, what is the internal bending moment at $A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,6,0" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,incastro,0" forze="C,0,-1,27 kN" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,4 m"}

- $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-20.25\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

Set $a=AC$ and $b=CB$. Integrating the curvatures in both segments with zero $v$ and $\theta$ at the ends gives $M_A=-\dfrac{Pab^2}{L^2}$. An equivalent calculation uses a virtual unit rotation at $A$ and zero rotation at $B$. It produces a displacement $\dfrac{ab^2}{L^2}$ at the load. This gives $-24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
