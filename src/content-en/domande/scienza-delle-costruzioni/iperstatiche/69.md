Continuous beam $A$–$B$–$C$ has two spans of length $4\,\mathrm m$, with $EI=12\,800\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ and bilateral supports. No external loads act. Support $A$ settles by $6\,\mathrm{mm}$, while $B$ and $C$ remain stationary. If a positive bending moment puts the bottom fibers in tension, what is the moment at $B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,8,0" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;C,carrello" frecce="-0.5,0.12,-0.5,-0.32,s_A" quote="A,B,4 m;B,C,4 m" titolo="Settlement of end support A"}

- $7.2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-14.4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-7.2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-3.6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

Remove support $B$ and take its reaction $X$ as the redundant, positive upward. Without the middle support, beam $AC$ rotates as a rigid body along the chord from lowered support $A$ to stationary support $C$. Point $B$ at the midpoint of the chord drops by $3\,\mathrm{mm}$. Support $B$ remains stationary, so the reaction must raise the midpoint of the simply supported beam of span $2L=8\,\mathrm m$ by $3\,\mathrm{mm}$. Thus
$$
\frac{X(2L)^3}{48EI}=0.003\,\mathrm m,\qquad X=\frac{48\cdot12\,800\cdot0.003}{512}\,\mathrm{kN}=3.6\,\mathrm{kN}.
$$
An upward force at midspan of a simply supported beam produces moment $-\dfrac{X\cdot2L}{4}$ there and puts the top fibers in tension. Therefore, $M_B=-\dfrac{3.6\cdot8}{4}\,\mathrm{kN\,m}=-7.2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
