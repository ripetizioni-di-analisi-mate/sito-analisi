A cantilever of length $4\,\mathrm m$ with $EI=17\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ carries a uniform downward load of intensity $12\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. What is the downward deflection of section $D$, a distance $2\,\mathrm m$ from the fixed end?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,2,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,D,2 m;D,B,2 m" titolo="Cantilever under a uniform load"}

- $22.6\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $8\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $11.3\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $1.4\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

Apply a downward unit force at $D$. The unit-load moment is $m=-(2-x)$ between the fixed end and $D$ and zero beyond $D$, so the outer segment contributes nothing to the integral. The load beyond $D$ still matters because the actual moment $M=-\dfrac q2(L-x)^2$ near the fixed end depends on the entire load. Therefore
$$
v_D=\frac{q}{2EI}\int_0^2(4-x)^2(2-x)\,\mathrm dx=\frac{6}{17\,000}\cdot\frac{68}{3}\,\mathrm m=8\,\mathrm{mm}.
$$
The result equals $\dfrac{17qL^4}{384EI}$, a little over one-third of the tip deflection $\dfrac{qL^4}{8EI}$.
