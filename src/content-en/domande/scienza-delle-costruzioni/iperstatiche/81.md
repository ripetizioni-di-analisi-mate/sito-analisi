The tip of cantilever $AB$ of length $3\,\mathrm m$, with $EI=27\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$, hangs from a vertical tie of length $2\,\mathrm m$ anchored to a fixed point above, with $EA=12\,000\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $\alpha=12\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$. The system carries no loads, and only the tie undergoes a temperature decrease of $50\,\mathrm K$. What is the tensile force in the tie?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;S,3,2" aste="A-B" catene="S-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;S,cerniera,90" quote="A,B,3 m;B,S,2 m,0.6" titolo="Cantilever suspended from a tie that is cooled"}

- $3.6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $7.2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $2.4\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Cut the tie and take its tensile force $X$ as the redundant. Cooling shortens the free tie by $\alpha\Delta T h=12\cdot10^{-6}\cdot50\cdot2\,\mathrm m=1.2\,\mathrm{mm}$. The cantilever tip and the lower end of the tie must remain at the same point. The tip rises by $\dfrac{XL^3}{3EI}$. The lower end of the tie rises by $1.2\,\mathrm{mm}$ less its elastic elongation $\dfrac{Xh}{EA}$, giving
$$
X\left(\frac{L^3}{3EI}+\frac{h}{EA}\right)=1.2\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm m .
$$
The two flexibilities are $\dfrac{1}{3\,000}$ and $\dfrac{1}{6\,000}\,\mathrm{m/kN}$, with sum $\dfrac{1}{2\,000}\,\mathrm{m/kN}$, so $X=1.2\cdot10^{-3}\cdot2\,000\,\mathrm{kN}=2.4\,\mathrm{kN}$.
