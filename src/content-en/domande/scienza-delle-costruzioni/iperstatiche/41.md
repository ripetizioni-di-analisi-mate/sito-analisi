An unloaded horizontal beam $4\,\mathrm{m}$ long is clamped at both ends. The left support stays at its initial height and the right support rises by $8\,\mathrm{mm}$. Which rotations must be imposed on the clamps so that, to first order, the beam can follow the movement without bending?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,incastro,0" frecce="4.55,0,4.55,1.05,8 mm" quote="A,B,4 m" titolo="Original positions of both clamps"}

- Left counterclockwise and right clockwise by $2\,\mathrm{mrad}$.
- Both counterclockwise by $2\,\mathrm{mrad}$.
- Both clockwise by $2\,\mathrm{mrad}$.
- Both zero.

## Explanation

The chord joining the clamps rotates counterclockwise through $\dfrac{8}{4000}=0.002$ radians. If both end sections rotate by this amount, the deformed shape is the straight line between the ends and curvature remains zero over the full span.
