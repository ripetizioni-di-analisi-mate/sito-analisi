A beam of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ with $EI=20\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ is fixed at both ends. The right fixed end rotates counterclockwise by $2\,\mathrm{mrad}$ without translating, and the left fixed end remains stationary. No other loads act. If a positive bending moment puts the bottom fibers in tension, what is the internal moment at the left end?

- $0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

With no transverse loads, the deflected shape is cubic. Function $v(x)=\dfrac{\theta_B x^2(x-L)}{L^2}$ has zero displacement at both fixed ends and satisfies the prescribed rotations. Differentiating twice gives $M_A=EI\cdot v''(0)=-\dfrac{2EI\theta_B}{L}$. This gives $-20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
