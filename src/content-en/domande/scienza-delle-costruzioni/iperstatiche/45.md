Continuous beam $A$–$B$–$C$ has constant $EI$, two spans of length $4\,\mathrm m$, and stationary bilateral supports. A downward force of $32\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at the midpoint of span $AB$, while span $BC$ is unloaded. What is the reaction at support $C$, taking upward as positive?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,2,0;B,4,0;C,8,0" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;C,carrello" forze="D,0,-1,P" quote="A,D,2 m;D,B,2 m;B,C,4 m" titolo="Force applied only to span AB"}

- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Choose moment $M_B$ over the middle support as the redundant and insert a hinge at $B$. In the primary structure, only the loaded span contributes to the relative rotation $\dfrac{PL^2}{16EI}$ at $B$. The unit couples produce $\dfrac{L}{3EI}$ in each span. Compatibility requires
$$
\frac{PL^2}{16EI}+\frac{2L}{3EI}M_B=0,\qquad M_B=-\frac{3PL}{32}=-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}.
$$
Span $BC$ is unloaded, so taking moments about $B$ gives $R_C=\dfrac{M_B}{L}=-3\,\mathrm{kN}$. The reaction therefore acts **downward**. Support $C$ holds down the end of the beam, which would otherwise lift.
