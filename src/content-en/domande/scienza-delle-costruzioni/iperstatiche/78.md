Bar $A$–$B$–$C$ is restrained axially at $A$ and $C$. Segment $AB$ has length $2\,\mathrm m$ and $EA_1=100\,000\,\mathrm{kN}$. Segment $BC$ has length $2\,\mathrm m$ and $EA_2=50\,000\,\mathrm{kN}$. Only segment $AB$ is heated, with $\alpha\Delta T=6\cdot10^{-4}$. What is the displacement of joint $B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,0;C,4,0" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,incastro,180;C,incastro,0" quote="A,B,2 m;B,C,2 m" etichette="1,0.4,ΔT" titolo="Two-segment bar with heating confined to AB"}

- $0.8\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right
- $1.2\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right
- $0.4\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right
- $0.8\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the left

## Explanation

Both segments carry the same axial force, and their elongations must sum to zero. Thus
$$
N\left(\frac{2}{100\,000}+\frac{2}{50\,000}\right)+6\cdot10^{-4}\cdot2=0,\qquad N=-\frac{1.2\cdot10^{-3}}{6\cdot10^{-5}}\,\mathrm{kN}=-20\,\mathrm{kN}.
$$
Segment $BC$ shortens by $\dfrac{20\cdot2}{50\,000}\,\mathrm m=0.8\,\mathrm{mm}$. End $C$ is fixed, so joint $B$ moves a distance $0.8\,\mathrm{mm}$ toward $C$, to the right. Segment $AB$ elongates by the same amount, the sum of thermal change $1.2\,\mathrm{mm}$ and elastic change $-0.4\,\mathrm{mm}$, satisfying compatibility.
