Continuous beam $A$–$B$–$C$ has two spans of length $4\,\mathrm m$, with $EI=16\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ and a uniform downward load $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on both spans. Inserting a hinge at $B$ gives two simply supported beams. In this structure, what is the relative rotation of the sections immediately to either side of $B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,8,0" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;C,carrello" cerniere="B" carichi="A,C,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,B,4 m;B,C,4 m" titolo="Primary structure with a hinge at B"}

- $0.001\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $0\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $0.002\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $0.004\,\mathrm{rad}$

## Explanation

To calculate relative rotation, apply opposite unit couples to the two sections adjacent to $B$. Each span is simply supported and has a linear virtual moment that equals $1$ at $B$ and $0$ at the other end. For span $AB$, this gives
$$
\frac1{EI}\int_0^L\frac q2x(L-x)\,\frac xL\,\mathrm dx=\frac{qL^3}{24EI}=\frac{6\cdot64}{24\cdot16\,000}\,\mathrm{rad}=10^{-3}\,\mathrm{rad},
$$
and by symmetry span $BC$ makes the same contribution. The sections rotate in opposite directions, so their contributions add and the relative rotation is $\dfrac{qL^3}{12EI}=2\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{rad}$. This is the known term in the compatibility equation for the bending moment over $B$.
