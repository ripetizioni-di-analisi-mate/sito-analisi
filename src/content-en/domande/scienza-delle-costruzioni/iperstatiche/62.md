A simply supported beam of span $8\,\mathrm m$ has two vertical redundants, positive downward, at points $1$ and $2$ respectively $2\,\mathrm m$ and $4\,\mathrm m$ from the left support. What is $EI\,f_{12}$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;1,2,0;2,4,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,1,2 m;1,2,2 m;2,B,4 m" titolo="Points 1 and 2 where the redundant forces act"}

- $\dfrac{22}{3}\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $\dfrac{32}{3}\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m^3}$
- $\dfrac{11}{3}\,\mathrm{m^3}$

## Explanation

A unit force at $1$ produces a triangular moment diagram, $m_1=\dfrac34x$ up to $x=2$ and $m_1=\dfrac14(8-x)$ beyond it. A unit force at $2$ produces moment $m_2=\dfrac x2$ up to $x=4$ and $m_2=\dfrac{8-x}{2}$ beyond it. Integrate the product over the three intervals on which both expressions are linear, giving
$$
EI\,f_{12}=\int_0^2\frac38x^2\,\mathrm dx+\int_2^4\frac{(8-x)\,x}{8}\,\mathrm dx+\int_4^8\frac{(8-x)^2}{8}\,\mathrm dx=1+\frac{11}{3}+\frac83=\frac{22}{3}\,\mathrm{m^3}.
$$
Dividing this value by $EI$ gives the downward displacement of point $1$ due to a unit force at point $2$. The other values $6$ and $\dfrac{32}{3}$ represent $EI\,f_{11}$ and $EI\,f_{22}$, respectively.
