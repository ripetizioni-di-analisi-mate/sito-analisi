A beam of span $6\,\mathrm m$, pinned at $A$ and supported on a roller at $B$, experiences a temperature gradient producing constant free curvature $\kappa_T=v_T''=10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$, with $v$ positive downward. What is the vertical displacement at midspan?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" etichette="3,0.45,ΔT_sup;3,-0.45,ΔT_inf" quote="A,B,6 m,1" titolo="Simply supported beam with a temperature gradient"}

- $4.5\,\mathrm{mm}$ downward
- $18\,\mathrm{mm}$ upward
- $9\,\mathrm{mm}$ upward
- $4.5\,\mathrm{mm}$ upward

## Explanation

The beam is statically determinate, so the gradient produces neither reactions nor moments and $v''=\kappa_T$ in every section. Integrating twice with $v(0)=v(L)=0$ gives $v(x)=\dfrac{\kappa_T}{2}\,x(x-L)$. At midspan
$$
v\Bigl(\frac L2\Bigr)=-\frac{\kappa_TL^2}{8}=-\frac{10^{-3}\cdot36}{8}\,\mathrm m=-4.5\,\mathrm{mm}.
$$
The negative value means the midpoint rises by $4.5\,\mathrm{mm}$.
