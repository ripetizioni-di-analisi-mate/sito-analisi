A beam of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ has a fixed end at $A$, a vertical support at $B$, $EI=16\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$, and a uniform downward load $12\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Support $B$ settles by $2\,\mathrm{mm}$. Calculate its vertical reaction.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.65,0.65,q" quote="A,B,4 m" frecce="4.55,0.12,4.55,-0.28,s" titolo="Support B settles 2 mm"}

- $24\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $19.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $16.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $13.5\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

With a stationary support, the reaction would be $\dfrac{3qL}{8}=18\,\mathrm{kN}$. The settlement reduces the reaction by $\dfrac{3EI}{L^3}\cdot0.002=1.5\,\mathrm{kN}$.
