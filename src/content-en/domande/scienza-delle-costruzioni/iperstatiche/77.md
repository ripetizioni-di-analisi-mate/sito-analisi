A $2\,\mathrm{m}$ strut is fixed at the left and separated from a rigid stop on the right by a $0.6\,\mathrm{mm}$ gap. Its expansion coefficient is $10^{-5}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$, with $EA=40\,000\,\mathrm{kN}$. If the temperature rises by $50\,\mathrm K$, what is the axial force, with tension positive?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" linee="4.5,-0.7 4.5,0.7" quote="A,B,2 m,0.9;B,4.5 0,g,-0.55" titolo="Initial gap between the strut and the stop"}

- $0\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $+8\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The free expansion would be $2000\cdot10^{-5}\cdot50=1\,\mathrm{mm}$. The first $0.6\,\mathrm{mm}$ closes the gap. The imposed mechanical strain is therefore $-\dfrac{0.4}{2000}$, which gives $-8\,\mathrm{kN}$ when multiplied by $EA$.
