A propped cantilever has $EI=14\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ over the first $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from the fixed end and $EI=28\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ over the last $2\,\mathrm{m}$. A clockwise couple of $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ acts at the propped end. The prop does not settle. What is the upward reaction at the prop?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,4,0" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" coppie="B,-1,M" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,2 m"}

- $7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $7\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $14\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $5\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

In the released structure, the end couple and the unit tip force give bending moments of magnitudes $C$ and $L-x$. Compatibility requires $C\int\dfrac{L-x}{EI}\,dx=R_B\int\dfrac{(L-x)^2}{EI}\,dx$. Integrating separately over the two segments gives $500\cdot 10^{-6}$ and $1\,428.57\cdot 10^{-6}$ for the two integrals in consistent units. This gives $7\,\mathrm{kN}$.
