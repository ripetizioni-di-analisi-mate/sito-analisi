A cantilever of length $4\,\mathrm m$ with constant $EI$ carries a uniform downward load of intensity $8\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. A stationary support holds the beam at the midspan section $C$, and the tip $B$ is free. What is the support reaction at $C$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;C,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,2 m" titolo="Cantilever with a support at midspan"}

- $4.25\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $17\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $34\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Remove support $C$ and choose its reaction $X$ as the redundant, positive upward. On the released cantilever the load lowers $C$ by $\dfrac{17qL^4}{384EI}$, while the unit force at $C$ raises it by $\dfrac{1}{3EI}\Bigl(\dfrac L2\Bigr)^3=\dfrac{L^3}{24EI}$. The compatibility condition $v_C=0$ gives
$$
X=\frac{17qL^4}{384EI}\cdot\frac{24EI}{L^3}=\frac{17qL}{16}=34\,\mathrm{kN}.
$$
The reaction exceeds the total load of $32\,\mathrm{kN}$, so the fixed end exerts a downward vertical force of $2\,\mathrm{kN}$. The load on the overhang beyond $C$ tends to lift the portion near the fixed end.
