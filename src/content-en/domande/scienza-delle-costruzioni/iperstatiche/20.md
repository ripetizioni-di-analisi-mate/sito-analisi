A continuous beam $A$–$B$–$C$ rests on three supports. Take the bending moment over support $B$ as the redundant. Insert a hinge at $B$ and apply couples $X$ and $-X$ to the adjacent sections. Let $\varphi_{\mathrm{L}}$ and $\varphi_{\mathrm{R}}$ denote the counterclockwise rotations of the sections to the left and right of $B$. What compatibility condition must be imposed?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,8,0" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;C,carrello" cerniere="B" quote="A,B,L;B,C,L" titolo="Primary structure with a hinge above support B"}

- $\varphi_{\mathrm{L}}=0$ and $\varphi_{\mathrm{R}}=0$
- $\varphi_{\mathrm{L}}-\varphi_{\mathrm{R}}=0$
- $\varphi_{\mathrm{L}}+\varphi_{\mathrm{R}}=0$
- $v_B=0$

## Explanation

The couples $X$ and $-X$ perform work $X\varphi_{\mathrm{L}}-X\varphi_{\mathrm{R}}=X(\varphi_{\mathrm{L}}-\varphi_{\mathrm{R}})$, so the coordinate conjugate to the redundant is the **relative rotation** of the two sections. The original beam is continuous over $B$, so the adjacent sections have equal rotations. The compatibility condition is therefore $\varphi_{\mathrm{L}}-\varphi_{\mathrm{R}}=0$. The individual rotations generally do not vanish because the support at $B$ restrains only vertical displacement. Condition $v_B=0$ already holds in the primary structure since the support remains in place.
