A triangular truss has a pin at $A$ and a roller with a vertical reaction at $B$, a distance $6\,\mathrm m$ from $A$. Apex $C$ lies a distance $2\,\mathrm m$ above the midpoint of $AB$. A downward force of $40\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $C$. All the members have $EA=60\,000\,\mathrm{kN}$. What is the horizontal displacement of roller support $B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,3,2" aste="A-B;A-C;B-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1,P" quote="A,B,6 m" titolo="Triangular truss"}

- $3\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right
- $1.5\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right
- $3\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the left
- $6\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right

## Explanation

Apply a horizontal unit force to the right at $B$. The horizontal reaction at pin $A$ balances it. Only member $AB$ balances this pair of forces, with $n_{AB}=1$. The inclined members carry no force. In the actual system the vertical reaction at $A$ is $20\,\mathrm{kN}$, so member $AC$ is in compression with horizontal component $20\cdot\dfrac32=30\,\mathrm{kN}$. Equilibrium of joint $A$ gives the tensile force $N_{AB}=30\,\mathrm{kN}$. Therefore
$$
u_B=\frac{N_{AB}\,n_{AB}\,\ell_{AB}}{EA}=\frac{30\cdot1\cdot6}{60\,000}\,\mathrm m=3\,\mathrm{mm}.
$$
The value is positive, so $B$ moves to the right in the direction of the unit load as the tension tie elongates.
