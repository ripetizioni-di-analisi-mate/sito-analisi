A beam of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ is fixed at $A$ and has a roller with a vertical reaction at $B$. The downward load increases linearly from zero at $A$ to $15\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $B$. $EI$ is constant. If a positive bending moment puts the bottom fibers in tension, what is the internal bending moment at $A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0,1,15 kN/m" quote="A,B,4 m"}

- $-14\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-7\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $14\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-80\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

Without the roller, integrating the product of the actual and unit-load moments gives a deflection of $\dfrac{11qL^4}{120EI}$ at $B$. Setting $\dfrac{R_B L^3}{3EI}$ equal to this deflection gives $R_B=\dfrac{11qL}{40}$. The bending moment at the left end is $R_B L-\dfrac{qL^2}{3}=-\dfrac{7qL^2}{120}$. This gives $-14\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
