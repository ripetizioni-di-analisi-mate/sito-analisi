A beam of length $4\,\mathrm m$ with $EI=16\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ is supported at $B$. At $A$ a pin has a rotational spring of stiffness $k_\varphi=12\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m/rad}$. The uniform load acts downward with intensity $12\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. If a positive bending moment puts the bottom fibers in tension, what is the bending moment at $A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.6,q" etichette="-0.3,0.55,k_φ" quote="A,B,4 m" titolo="Beam with a rotational spring at A"}

- $-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

Remove the spring to obtain a simply supported beam. Choose as the redundant the magnitude $X$ of the spring couple at $A$, whose action puts the top fibers in tension. The load rotates section $A$ clockwise by $\dfrac{qL^3}{24EI}=0.002\,\mathrm{rad}$, and couple $X$ rotates it in the opposite direction by $\dfrac{XL}{3EI}$. The spring rotates through $\dfrac{X}{k_\varphi}$, equal to the net beam rotation, so compatibility gives
$$
\frac{qL^3}{24EI}-\frac{XL}{3EI}=\frac{X}{k_\varphi},\qquad X=\frac{0.002}{\frac{1}{12\,000}+\frac{1}{12\,000}}\,\mathrm{kN\,m}=12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}.
$$
The moment at $A$ is therefore $-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, between the pinned-end value $0$ and the perfectly fixed-end value $-\dfrac{qL^2}{8}=-24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
