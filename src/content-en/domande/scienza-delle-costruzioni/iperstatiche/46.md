A beam of length $8\,\mathrm m$ is fixed at $A$ and supported at $B$, with constant $EI$ and a uniform downward load of intensity $10\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. At what distance from $B$ is the shear force zero?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.8,0.8,q" quote="A,B,8 m" titolo="Propped cantilever under a uniform load"}

- $4\,\mathrm m$
- $3\,\mathrm m$
- $2\,\mathrm m$
- $5\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

Compatibility of the propped cantilever gives $R_B=\dfrac{3qL}{8}=30\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. At a section a distance $s$ from $B$, the beam portion toward $B$ carries the reaction $R_B$ and load $qs$. The shear magnitude is therefore $|R_B-qs|$. The shear force therefore vanishes where
$$
s=\frac{R_B}{q}=\frac{3L}{8}=3\,\mathrm m .
$$
At that section the positive bending moment reaches its maximum $\dfrac{R_B^2}{2q}=45\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, or $\dfrac{9qL^2}{128}$.
