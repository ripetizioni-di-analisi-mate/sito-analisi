A continuous beam on four supports has three equal spans of length $5\,\mathrm m$ and constant $EI$. A uniform downward load of intensity $8\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts over its full length. What is the reaction at an interior support?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;C,10,0;D,15,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;C,carrello;D,carrello" carichi="A,D,0.8,0.8,q" quote="A,B,5 m;B,C,5 m;C,D,5 m" titolo="Continuous beam with three equal spans"}

- $24\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $50\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $44\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $22\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Insert hinges over internal supports $B$ and $C$ and take moments $M_B$ and $M_C$ as the redundants. By symmetry $M_B=M_C$, so it suffices to impose compatibility at $B$. Multiplying that equation by $EI$ gives
$$
2\cdot\frac{qL^3}{24}+\frac{2L}{3}M_B+\frac L6M_C=0 .
$$
Substituting $M_C=M_B$ gives $M_B=-\dfrac{qL^2}{10}=-20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The reaction at $B$ is the sum of the contributions from the two adjoining spans. Span $AB$ contributes $\dfrac{qL}{2}-\dfrac{M_B}{L}=24\,\mathrm{kN}$. Span $BC$ contributes $\dfrac{qL}{2}=20\,\mathrm{kN}$ because its end moments are equal. Thus $R_B=44\,\mathrm{kN}$, or $1.1\,qL$.
