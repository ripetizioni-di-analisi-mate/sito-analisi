Beam $AB$ is fixed at $A$, supported at $B$, and carries a uniform load $q$. Choose the fixed-end reaction moment $X=M_A$ as the redundant. Which primary structure and compatibility condition form a valid pair?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.7,0.7,q" quote="A,B,L" titolo="Propped cantilever under a uniform load"}

- A cantilever fixed at $A$, with zero downward displacement at $B$
- A beam simply supported at $A$ and $B$, with zero rotation at $B$
- A cantilever fixed at $A$, with zero rotation at $B$
- A beam simply supported at $A$ and $B$, with zero rotation at $A$

## Explanation

The reaction moment $M_A$ prevents rotation at the fixed end $A$. To introduce it as an unknown, release only that rotation and leave both translations at $A$ restrained. The fixed end becomes a pin. The primary structure is then a stable, statically determinate beam supported at $A$ and $B$. Compatibility applies to the coordinate conjugate to $X$, the rotation at $A$, which is zero in the original beam. A cantilever with zero deflection at $B$ would correspond to choosing $X=R_B$. Rotation at $B$ is free in both structures.
