A beam of length $4\,\mathrm m$ with $EI=16\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ is fixed at $A$ and has a bilateral vertical support at $B$. No external loads act. The fixed support rotates counterclockwise through $2\,\mathrm{mrad}$ without translating. What is the reaction at $B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" quote="A,B,4 m" titolo="Prescribed rotation at fixed support A"}

- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward
- $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward

## Explanation

Remove the support at $B$ and take its reaction $X$ as the redundant, positive upward. In the released cantilever, the counterclockwise rigid-body rotation $\theta$ of the fixed end raises the tip by $\theta L=0.002\cdot4\,\mathrm m=8\,\mathrm{mm}$. Support $B$ remains stationary, so the reaction must bring the tip back to its original elevation. Hence
$$
\theta L+\frac{L^3}{3EI}X=0,\qquad X=-\frac{3EI\theta}{L^2}=-\frac{3\cdot16\,000\cdot0.002}{16}\,\mathrm{kN}=-6\,\mathrm{kN}.
$$
The negative sign indicates that the support pulls the beam **downward**. A unilateral support could not exert this force, and the beam would lose contact.
