A straight beam fixed at both ends carries only vertical loads, distributed asymmetrically. Include axial deformation. How many redundant forces remain to be found from bending compatibility conditions?

- $3$
- $1$
- $0$
- $2$

## Explanation

In the plane, the two fixed ends supply six reactions, giving a degree of static indeterminacy of $3$. Horizontal force equilibrium gives $H_A+H_B=0$, so the axial force is constant along the beam. The distance between the fixed ends cannot change. Thus $\dfrac{NL}{EA}=0$, and the axial force and both horizontal reactions are zero. Two vertical reactions and two end couples remain, related by two equilibrium equations. This leaves **two redundants**, for example the two end couples. Because the load is asymmetric, symmetry gives no further reduction in the unknowns.
