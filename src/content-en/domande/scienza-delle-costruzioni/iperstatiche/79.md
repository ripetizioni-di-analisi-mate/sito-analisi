Four straight horizontal beams of the same material and cross section undergo a uniform temperature increase of $\Delta T$ throughout their cross sections. Which one develops an axial force?

- A beam pinned at the left and supported at the right by a roller with a vertical reaction
- A cantilever fixed at the left
- A beam fixed at the left and supported at the right by a roller with a vertical reaction
- A beam with fixed pin supports at both ends

## Explanation

Uniform heating produces free elongation $\alpha\Delta T L$ and no curvature. An axial force develops only if the supports restrain this elongation. A roller with a vertical reaction permits horizontal movement, so the simply supported beam, the cantilever, and the propped cantilever expand without developing internal forces. The two stationary pins keep the ends a fixed distance apart, so their beam develops axial force $N=-EA\alpha\Delta T$. The propped cantilever is statically indeterminate under vertical loads, but its supports permit free axial expansion.
