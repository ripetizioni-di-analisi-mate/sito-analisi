A cantilever of length $4\,\mathrm m$ undergoes a temperature gradient producing constant free curvature $\kappa_T=v_T''=1.5\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$, with $v$ positive downward. What is the tip rotation, taking counterclockwise as positive?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" etichette="2,0.45,ΔT_sup;2,-0.45,ΔT_inf" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9" titolo="Cantilever with a through-depth temperature gradient"}

- $6\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $-6\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $-3\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $-1.2\cdot10^{-2}\,\mathrm{rad}$

## Explanation

The cantilever is statically determinate, so the gradient produces no bending moment and the actual curvature equals the free curvature, $v''=\kappa_T$. Integrating with condition $v'(0)=0$ at the fixed end gives $v'(x)=\kappa_T x$. With this course’s sign convention, counterclockwise rotation is $\varphi=-v'$, giving at the tip
$$
\varphi_B=-\kappa_T L=-1.5\cdot10^{-3}\cdot4\,\mathrm{rad}=-6\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{rad}.
$$
The tip rotates clockwise and moves downward by $\dfrac{\kappa_TL^2}{2}=12\,\mathrm{mm}$.
