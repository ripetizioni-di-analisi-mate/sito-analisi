Beam $AB$ of length $3\,\mathrm m$ and constant $EI$ is fixed at $A$. At $B$, a guided support prevents rotation and horizontal translation while permitting vertical translation. A downward force of $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $B$. If a positive bending moment puts the bottom fibers in tension, what is the bending moment at $B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,doppio-pendolo,0" forze="B,0,-1,P" quote="A,B,3 m" titolo="Beam fixed at one end and guided at B"}

- $30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $60\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The guide provides no vertical reaction, so the fixed end carries the entire force and the shear force is $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ throughout the beam. Choose the counterclockwise guide couple $X$ as the redundant and use a cantilever as the primary structure. The tip force rotates $B$ clockwise by $\dfrac{PL^2}{2EI}$, while $X$ rotates it counterclockwise by $\dfrac{XL}{EI}$. The compatibility condition $\varphi_B=0$ gives
$$
X=\frac{PL}{2}=30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}.
$$
A counterclockwise couple at the right end puts the bottom fibers in tension, so $M_B=30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. At the fixed end, we instead have $M_A=30-20\cdot3=-30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
