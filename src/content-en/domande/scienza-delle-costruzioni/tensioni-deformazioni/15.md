At a point, $\sigma_{x}=70\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_{y}=30\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\tau_{xy}=30\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Let $\theta$ be the counterclockwise angle from $+x$ to the normal $n$. Rotating $n$ a further $90^\circ$ counterclockwise gives the positive tangent $t$. For what $\theta$ is the shear stress $\tau_{nt}$ positive and largest? Choose an angle between $-90^\circ$ and $+90^\circ$.

- $-33.7^\circ$
- $16.8^\circ$
- $-16.8^\circ$
- $73.2^\circ$

## Explanation

The shear stress is $\dfrac{(\sigma_{y}-\sigma_{x})\sin(2\theta)}{2}+\tau_{xy}\cos(2\theta)$. To obtain its positive maximum, align the unit vector $(\cos 2\theta,\sin 2\theta)$ with $(\tau_{xy},\dfrac{\sigma_{y}-\sigma_{x}}{2})$. This direction gives $\theta=-16.8^\circ$.
