A bar in uniaxial tension has a transverse strain gauge that reads $-90\cdot10^{-6}$. The material has $E=210\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\nu=0.3$. Find its axial stress.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 5,0 5,1 0,1;2.35,0.15 2.65,0.15 2.65,0.85 2.35,0.85" frecce="5.1,0.5,6,0.5,N;-0.1,0.5,-1,0.5,N" etichette="2.5,1.35,strain gauge" titolo="Bar in tension with a transverse strain gauge"}

- $18.9\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $-63\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $63\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $5.7\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

In uniaxial tension the transverse strain is $-\nu\varepsilon_x$.

Thus $\varepsilon_x=\dfrac{90\cdot10^{-6}}{0.3}=300\cdot10^{-6}$.

Hooke's law gives $\sigma_x=E\varepsilon_x=210\,000\cdot300\cdot10^{-6}=63\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

Multiplying the transverse reading directly by $E$ would give magnitude $18.9\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and omit the Poisson effect.
