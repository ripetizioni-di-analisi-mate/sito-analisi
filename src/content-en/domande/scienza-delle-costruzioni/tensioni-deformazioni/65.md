The in-plane principal stresses in $xy$ are $50$ and $-10\,\mathrm{MPa}$. A reading along $z$, also a principal direction, gives $80\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Between which principal directions does the normal to a plane of maximum three-dimensional shear lie?

- At $45^\circ$ between the $50$ and $-10\,\mathrm{MPa}$ directions
- Along the $80\,\mathrm{MPa}$ direction alone
- Along the $50\,\mathrm{MPa}$ direction alone
- At $45^\circ$ between the $80$ and $-10\,\mathrm{MPa}$ directions

## Explanation

The largest Mohr circle has endpoints at $-10$ and $80\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Maximum shear occurs at its top or bottom, $90^\circ$ around the circle from a principal point. In the body this is a $45^\circ$ rotation of the normal between those two directions.
