The stress tensor at a point has components $\sigma_x=\sigma_y=20$, $\tau_{xy}=30$, $\sigma_z=80$, and $\tau_{xz}=\tau_{yz}=0$ in $\mathrm{MPa}$. Find the maximum shear stress magnitude over all planes.

- $15\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $40\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $30\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $45\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

Since $\tau_{xz}=\tau_{yz}=0$, direction $z$ is principal with stress $80\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

In the $xy$ plane the components are $p=20$, $d=0$, and $R=30$. The other two principal stresses are therefore $50$ and $-10\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

The ordered principal stresses are $80$, $50$, and $-10\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

Thus maximum shear stress is $\dfrac{80-(-10)}{2}=45\,\mathrm{MPa}$, exceeding the value $30\,\mathrm{MPa}$ for the $xy$ plane alone.
