A plane stress state has $\sigma_x=10\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_y=50\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\tau_{xy}=-20\,\mathrm{MPa}$. In which interval does the angle $\theta_1$ of the normal to the plane of maximum principal stress lie, measured counterclockwise from $x$ and choosing $\theta_1\in(-90^\circ,90^\circ]$?

- $(-90^\circ,-45^\circ)$
- $(-45^\circ,0^\circ)$
- $(45^\circ,90^\circ)$
- $(0^\circ,45^\circ)$

## Explanation

Determine the direction from $\cos2\theta_1=\dfrac dR$ and $\sin2\theta_1=\dfrac{\tau_{xy}}R$, with $R>0$.

Here $d=-20\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\tau_{xy}=-20\,\mathrm{MPa}$, so the cosine and sine of the double angle are both negative.

Thus $2\theta_1$ is in the third quadrant, giving $2\theta_1=-135^\circ$ and $\theta_1=-67.5^\circ$.

The angle therefore belongs to $(-90^\circ,-45^\circ)$, consistent with the larger principal stress acting close to direction $y$, where $\sigma_y>\sigma_x$.
