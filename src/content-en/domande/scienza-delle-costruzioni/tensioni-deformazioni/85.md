In plane stress the in-plane principal stresses are $80\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $-40\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and the third principal stress is zero. Find the Tresca equivalent stress, the difference between the largest and smallest principal stresses.

- $120\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $40\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $60\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $80\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The ordered principal stresses are $80$, $0$, and $-40\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

The largest is $80\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and the smallest is $-40\,\mathrm{MPa}$, giving Tresca equivalent stress $80-(-40)=120\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

The in-plane principal stresses have opposite signs, so the third, zero stress lies between them.

The value $60\,\mathrm{MPa}$ is the maximum shear stress, half the Tresca equivalent stress.
