An element is in pure shear in the $xy$ plane with $\tau_{xy}=45\,\mathrm{MPa}$. The normal to a section makes an angle $-45^\circ$ with $+x$, measured counterclockwise. Find the normal stress on the section.

::struttura{frecce="0,0,5.5,0,x;0,0,0,5.5,y;0,0,3.53553,-3.53553,n" titolo="Direction of the normal to the section" riferimento="0"}

- $-45\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $45\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $-90\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $0\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

With $\sigma_{x}=\sigma_{y}=0$, stress transformation gives $\sigma_{n}=\tau_{xy}\sin(2\theta)$. The sine is $-1$ for the specified orientation, so $\sigma_{n}=-45\,\mathrm{MPa}$. The shear stress on this plane vanishes.
