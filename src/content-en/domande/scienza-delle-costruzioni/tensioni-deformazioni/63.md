The Mohr circle shown, in $\mathrm{MPa}$, has center $C=(30,0)$ and radius $50$. Point $X=(\sigma_x,\tau_{xy})$ represents the face with normal $\mathbf e_x$. It lies to the right of the center and has ordinate $30$. Find $\sigma_x$, $\sigma_y$, and $\tau_{xy}$.

::grafico{curve="30+50*cos(t),50*sin(t),0,6.283185307" xmin=-30 xmax=90 ymin=-60 ymax=60 uguale=1 nomi="σ,τ" punti="30,0,pieno,C;70,30,pieno,X;-10,-30,pieno,Y" segmenti="-10,-30,70,30" titolo="Mohr circle with center 30 MPa and radius 50 MPa"}

- $80$, $-20$, and $30\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $70$, $-10$, and $30\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $70$, $-10$, and $-30\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $-10$, $70$, and $30\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

Point $X$ has ordinate $\tau_{xy}=30\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and distance $50$ from the center, so $(\sigma_x-30)^2+30^2=50^2$.

Since $X$ is to the right of the center, $\sigma_x-30=40$ and $\sigma_x=70\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

The center equals the mean of the normal stresses, so $\sigma_y=2\cdot30-70=-10\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

The values $80$ and $-20\,\mathrm{MPa}$ are the principal stresses at the intersections of the circle with the horizontal axis.
