A rectangular section of width $20\,\mathrm{mm}$ and depth $80\,\mathrm{mm}$ has an upward $y$ axis through its centroid. The normal stress is $\sigma_x=30+\dfrac{y}{2}$, with $\sigma_x$ in $\mathrm{MPa}$ and $y$ in $\mathrm{mm}$. Find the axial force $N$ on the section.

- $80\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $48\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $24\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $16\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The axial force is the area integral of normal stress, $N=\displaystyle\int_A\sigma_x\,dA$.

The term $\dfrac y2$ is odd about the centroid, so it integrates to zero over the section.

Only the constant term remains, giving $N=30\cdot(20\cdot80)=48\,000\,\mathrm N=48\,\mathrm{kN}$.

Multiplying the top-fiber value $50\,\mathrm{MPa}$ by the area would give $80\,\mathrm{kN}$, which differs from the resultant.
