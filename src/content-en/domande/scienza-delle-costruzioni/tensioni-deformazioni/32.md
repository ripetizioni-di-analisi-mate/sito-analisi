In the $xy$ plane the strains are $\varepsilon_{x}=500\,\mu\varepsilon$, $\varepsilon_{y}=200\,\mu\varepsilon$, and engineering shear strain $\gamma_{xy}=300\,\mu\varepsilon$. Find the direction of the larger principal strain. Give the positive counterclockwise angle from $+x$ between $-90^\circ$ and $+90^\circ$ to one decimal place.

- $31.7^\circ$
- $-67.5^\circ$
- $22.5^\circ$
- $-22.5^\circ$

## Explanation

The off-diagonal tensor component is $\dfrac{\gamma_{xy}}{2}$. The transformation contains $\dfrac{(\varepsilon_{x}-\varepsilon_{y})\cos(2\theta)}{2}+\dfrac{\gamma_{xy}\sin(2\theta)}{2}$. Aligning $(\cos 2\theta,\sin 2\theta)$ with $(\varepsilon_{x}-\varepsilon_{y},\gamma_{xy})$ gives the maximum, yielding $\theta=22.5^\circ$.
