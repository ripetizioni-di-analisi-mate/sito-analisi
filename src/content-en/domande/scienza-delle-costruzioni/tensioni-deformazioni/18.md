In a Cauchy continuum without distributed couples, the traction on the face with normal $-x$ has $y$ component $18\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $x$ component $50\,\mathrm{MPa}$. What is the $x$ component of the traction on the face with normal $+y$ at the same point?

- $50\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $18\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $-18\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $0\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

Moment equilibrium makes the off-diagonal shear components symmetric. On the second face take the reciprocal component with the opposite normal sign to that on the first face. This gives $-18\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
