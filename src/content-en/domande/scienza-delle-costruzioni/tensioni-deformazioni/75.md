The concrete lining of a long tunnel warms uniformly by $20\,\mathrm K$. Away from the entrances it is in plane strain with $\varepsilon_z=0$ and can expand freely in the plane of the section, so $\sigma_x=\sigma_y=0$. Given $E=30\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\nu=0.2$, and $\alpha=10\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$, find $\sigma_z$.

- $-7.5\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $-4.8\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $6\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

Including thermal expansion, Hooke's law along $z$ is $\varepsilon_z=\dfrac{\sigma_z-\nu(\sigma_x+\sigma_y)}{E}+\alpha\Delta T$.

Since $\sigma_x=\sigma_y=0$, Poisson's ratio drops out.

Condition $\varepsilon_z=0$ then gives $\sigma_z=-E\alpha\Delta T=-30\,000\cdot10\cdot10^{-6}\cdot20=-6\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

The negative sign indicates compression because neighboring sections prevent elongation along the tunnel axis.
