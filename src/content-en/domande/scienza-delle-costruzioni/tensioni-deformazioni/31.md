At a point in an isotropic sheet under plane stress, $\sigma_{x}=80\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_{y}=40\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\tau_{xy}=20\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $E=200\,\mathrm{GPa}$, and $\nu=0.25$. Find the elastic strain energy stored per unit volume to one decimal place.

- $37.0\,\mathrm{kJ/m^3}$
- $16.0\,\mathrm{kJ/m^3}$
- $18.5\,\mathrm{kJ/m^3}$
- $21.0\,\mathrm{kJ/m^3}$

## Explanation

The strain energy density is $\dfrac{1}{2}(\sigma_{x}\varepsilon_{x}+\sigma_{y}\varepsilon_{y}+\tau_{xy}\gamma_{xy})$. The normal terms contribute $\dfrac{\sigma_{x}^{2}+\sigma_{y}^{2}-2\nu \sigma_{x}\sigma_{y}}{2E}$ and the shear term contributes $\dfrac{\tau_{xy}^{2}}{2G}$, with $G=\dfrac{E}{2(1+\nu)}$. Their sum is $18.5\,\mathrm{kJ/m^3}$.
