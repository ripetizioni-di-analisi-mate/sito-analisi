In a plane stress state, $\sigma_x=80\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\sigma_y=20\,\mathrm{MPa}$ are known, as is the normal stress $70\,\mathrm{MPa}$ on a face whose normal lies $45^\circ$ counterclockwise from $x$. Find $\tau_{xy}$.

- $10\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $40\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $20\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

For $\theta=45^\circ$, the results are $\cos2\theta=0$ and $\sin2\theta=1$.

The transformation formula therefore reduces to $\sigma_n=\dfrac{\sigma_x+\sigma_y}{2}+\tau_{xy}$.

Since $\dfrac{\sigma_x+\sigma_y}{2}=50\,\mathrm{MPa}$, it follows that $\tau_{xy}=70-50=20\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
