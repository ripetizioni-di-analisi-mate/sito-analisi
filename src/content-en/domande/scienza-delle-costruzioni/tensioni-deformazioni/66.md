In a thin spherical vessel wall both tangential principal stresses equal $50\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and radial stress is negligible. A probe measures a normal stress of $25\,\mathrm{MPa}$ on its face. What must the magnitude of the shear traction on that face be?

- $0\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $25\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $50\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $\dfrac{25}{\sqrt2}\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

If $\alpha$ is the angle between the face normal and the radial direction, the normal stress is $50\sin^2\alpha$ in MPa. The reading requires $\sin^2\alpha=\dfrac12$. Shear traction has magnitude $50|\sin\alpha\cos\alpha|=25\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
