At a point in a body, the traction on the face with normal $\mathbf n$ is $\mathbf t(\mathbf n)=(20,\,-10)\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Find the traction $\mathbf t(-\mathbf n)$ on the opposite face at the same point.

- $(20,\,10)\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $(20,\,-10)\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $(-20,\,10)\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $(-20,\,-10)\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

Cauchy's relation gives $\mathbf t(\mathbf n)=\boldsymbol\sigma\mathbf n$, so traction depends linearly on the normal.

Thus $\mathbf t(-\mathbf n)=\boldsymbol\sigma(-\mathbf n)=-\mathbf t(\mathbf n)=(-20,\,10)\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

The two tractions are the forces per unit area that the two portions of the body exert on each other across the same cut, so they are opposite.
