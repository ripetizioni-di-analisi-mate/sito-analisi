An isotropic sheet has $E=200\,\mathrm{GPa}$, $\nu=0.25$, and $\sigma_{z}=0$. A load imposes $\sigma_{x}=160\,\mathrm{MPa}$ while a restraint enforces $\varepsilon_{y}=0$. Find $\sigma_{y}$ and $\varepsilon_{x}$.

- $\sigma_{y}=40.0\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\varepsilon_{x}=750.0\,\mu\varepsilon$
- $\sigma_{y}=-40.0\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\varepsilon_{x}=750.0\,\mu\varepsilon$
- $\sigma_{y}=0.0\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\varepsilon_{x}=800.0\,\mu\varepsilon$
- $\sigma_{y}=40.0\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\varepsilon_{x}=800.0\,\mu\varepsilon$

## Explanation

From $E\varepsilon_{y}=\sigma_{y}-\nu \sigma_{x}=0$ we obtain $\sigma_{y}=\nu \sigma_{x}=40.0\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Then $E\varepsilon_{x}=\sigma_{x}-\nu \sigma_{y}=(1-\nu^{2})\sigma_{x}$ gives $\varepsilon_{x}=750.0\,\mu\varepsilon$.
