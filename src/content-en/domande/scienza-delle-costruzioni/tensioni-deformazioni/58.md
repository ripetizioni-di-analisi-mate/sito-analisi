A plane stress state has $\sigma_x=60\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_y=-20\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\tau_{xy}=0$. For what normal angle $\theta\in[0^\circ,90^\circ]$ measured counterclockwise from $x$ is the normal stress on the face zero?

- $30^\circ$
- $45^\circ$
- $15^\circ$
- $60^\circ$

## Explanation

With $p=20\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $d=40\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\tau_{xy}=0$ the normal stress is $\sigma_n=20+40\cos2\theta$.

It vanishes at $\cos2\theta=-\dfrac12$.

In the specified interval $2\theta\in[0^\circ,180^\circ]$, so $2\theta=120^\circ$ and $\theta=60^\circ$.

At $\theta=45^\circ$ the result would be $\sigma_n=p=20\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
