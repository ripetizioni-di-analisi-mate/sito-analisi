In the $xy$ plane the stresses are $\sigma_{x}=-40\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_{y}=30\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\tau_{xy}=-20\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Find the $t_{x}$ and $t_{y}$ components of the traction on the face with normal $+y$.

- $20\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $-30\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $30\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $-40\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $-20\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $30\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $-20\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

Cauchy's relation gives $t=\sigma n$. The normal $+y$ selects column $y$ of the tensor, multiplied by $1$. In the order $x$ and $y$ the components are $-20\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $30\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
