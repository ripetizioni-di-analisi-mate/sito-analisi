In a plane stress state the shear stress on the face whose normal is at $30^\circ$ from $x$ equals $\tau_{nt}=-7.32\,\mathrm{MPa}$. The positive tangent is obtained by rotating the normal $90^\circ$ counterclockwise. Find $\tau_{nt}$ on the face whose normal is at $120^\circ$ at the same point.

- $7.32\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $-7.32\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $-14.64\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $0\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The transformation formula gives $\tau_{nt}(\theta)=-d\sin2\theta+\tau_{xy}\cos2\theta$.

From $\theta$ to $\theta+90^\circ$ the double angle increases by $180^\circ$, so both its sine and cosine change sign.

Therefore $\tau_{nt}(\theta+90^\circ)=-\tau_{nt}(\theta)$, and on the face at $120^\circ$ the result is $7.32\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

The magnitude is unchanged by stress tensor symmetry. The sign changes because the positive tangent of the second face is also rotated through $90^\circ$.
