Under small-strain assumptions, with $x$ and $y$ in $\mathrm{mm}$, the displacements in $\mathrm{mm}$ are $u=10^{-3}\cdot[4x+7y]$ and $v=10^{-3}\cdot[x-3y]$. Find the engineering shear strain $\gamma_{xy}$ and the counterclockwise rigid-body rotation $\omega$. Express both coefficients in units of $10^{-3}$.

- $\gamma_{xy}=4.0\times 10^{-3}$ and $\omega=-3.0\,\mathrm{mrad}$
- $\gamma_{xy}=-6.0\times 10^{-3}$ and $\omega=4.0\,\mathrm{mrad}$
- $\gamma_{xy}=8.0\times 10^{-3}$ and $\omega=-3.0\,\mathrm{mrad}$
- $\gamma_{xy}=8.0\times 10^{-3}$ and $\omega=3.0\,\mathrm{mrad}$

## Explanation

Engineering shear strain uses the sum $\dfrac{\partial u}{\partial y}+\dfrac{\partial v}{\partial x}$. The antisymmetric displacement gradient gives counterclockwise rotation $\dfrac{\dfrac{\partial v}{\partial x}-\dfrac{\partial u}{\partial y}}{2}$. The coefficients of $10^{-3}$ are therefore $8.0$ and $-3.0$.
