A specimen carries a uniaxial stress of $120\,\mathrm{MPa}$, with tension positive. Its lateral faces are free. The measured longitudinal and transverse strains are $600\,\mu\varepsilon$ and $-150\,\mu\varepsilon$, respectively. Find $E$ and $\nu$.

- $E=200\,\mathrm{GPa}$ and $\nu=-0.25$
- $E=200\,\mathrm{GPa}$ and $\nu=0.25$
- $E=200\,\mathrm{GPa}$ and $\nu=4.00$
- $E=800\,\mathrm{GPa}$ and $\nu=0.25$

## Explanation

The uniaxial constitutive relation gives $E=\dfrac{\sigma}{\varepsilon_{\mathrm{longitudinal}}}=200\,\mathrm{GPa}$. Poisson's ratio is the negative of the transverse-to-longitudinal strain ratio, hence $0.25$.
