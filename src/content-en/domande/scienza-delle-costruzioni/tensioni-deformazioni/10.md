An isotropic linear elastic solid is in plane strain with $\sigma_x=120\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\sigma_y=80\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Its Poisson's ratio is $\nu=0.30$. Find the normal stress $\sigma_z$.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 2.8,0 2.8,1.8 0,1.8" linee="2.8,0 3.6,0.65 3.6,2.45 0.8,2.45 0,1.8;2.8,1.8 3.6,2.45;2.8,1.8 2.8,0" etichette="1.38,0.83,ε_z = 0" titolo="Element with out-of-plane normal strain restrained"}

- $36\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $24\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $60\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $0\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The condition $\varepsilon_z=0$ requires $\sigma_z=\nu(\sigma_x+\sigma_y)$. Substitution gives $0.30\cdot200=60\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
