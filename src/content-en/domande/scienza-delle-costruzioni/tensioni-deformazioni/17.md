The three principal stresses are $120$, $50$, and $-40\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Find the maximum shear stress magnitude and the normal stress acting on the same plane.

- $35.0\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $85.0\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $80.0\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $40.0\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $80.0\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $50.0\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $160.0\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $40.0\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

Use the Mohr circle for the largest and smallest principal stresses. Its radius is $\dfrac{\sigma_{1}-\sigma_{3}}{2}=80.0\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and its center is $\dfrac{\sigma_{1}+\sigma_{3}}{2}=40.0\,\mathrm{MPa}$. At the points of maximum shear stress the horizontal coordinate equals that of the center.
