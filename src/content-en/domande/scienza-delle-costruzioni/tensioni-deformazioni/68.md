The Poisson's ratio of an isotropic linear elastic material approaches $\dfrac12$ at fixed $E$. Which statement holds in the limit?

- In plane stress, $\varepsilon_z=0$
- The shear modulus $G$ tends to infinity
- Young's modulus tends to zero
- The volumetric strain tends to zero for every stress state

## Explanation

Adding the three normal components of Hooke's law gives $\operatorname{tr}\boldsymbol\varepsilon=\dfrac{1-2\nu}{E}\operatorname{tr}\boldsymbol\sigma$.

As $\nu\to\dfrac12$, the coefficient $1-2\nu$ approaches zero, so volume change vanishes for every stress state.

The material is therefore **incompressible** and the bulk modulus $K=\dfrac{E}{3(1-2\nu)}$ approaches infinity.

The shear modulus $G=\dfrac{E}{2(1+\nu)}$ approaches the finite value $\dfrac E3$.
