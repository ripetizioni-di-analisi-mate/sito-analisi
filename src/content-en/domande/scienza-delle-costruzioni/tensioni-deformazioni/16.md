In a plane stress state, $\sigma_{x}$ equals $55\,\mathrm{MPa}$. The in-plane principal stresses are $65$ and $15\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Find the magnitude of $\tau_{xy}$.

- $20\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $50\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $15\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $25\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The mean of the principal stresses is $40\,\mathrm{MPa}$, so $\sigma_{y}=2\times 40-55=25\,\mathrm{MPa}$. The circle radius is $25\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Since $R^{2}=[\dfrac{\sigma_{x}-\sigma_{y}}{2}]^{2}+\tau_{xy}^{2}$, the shear stress magnitude is $\sqrt{25^{2}-15^{2}}=20\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
