On a face with normal $\mathbf n=(\dfrac{3}{5},\dfrac{4}{5})$ the traction vector is $\mathbf t=(50,\,0)\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Find its normal component $\sigma_n$ and the magnitude of its tangential component.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 2.4,0 0,1.8" frecce="1.2,0.9,1.74,1.62,n;1.2,0.9,2.2,0.9,t" titolo="Inclined face with normal $\mathbf n$ and traction $\mathbf t$"}

- $\sigma_n=40\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $30\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $\sigma_n=30\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $50\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $\sigma_n=30\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $40\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $\sigma_n=50\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $0\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The normal component is the projection of traction onto the normal, so $\sigma_n=\mathbf t\cdot\mathbf n=50\cdot\dfrac35=30\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

The tangential part is $\mathbf t-\sigma_n\mathbf n=(50-18,\,0-24)=(32,\,-24)\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

Its magnitude is $\sqrt{32^2+24^2}=40\,\mathrm{MPa}$, consistent with $\sqrt{50^2-30^2}$ because the two components are orthogonal.
