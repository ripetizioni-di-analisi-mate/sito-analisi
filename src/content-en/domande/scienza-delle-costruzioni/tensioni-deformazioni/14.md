An isotropic linear elastic material has $E=180\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\nu=0.25$. The point shown is under hydrostatic pressure $60\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Find its first-order relative volume change.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 2.8,0 2.8,1.8 0,1.8" linee="2.8,0 3.6,0.65 3.6,2.45 0.8,2.45 0,1.8;2.8,1.8 3.6,2.45;2.8,1.8 2.8,0" frecce="3.95,1.225,3.2,1.225,σ_x;1.8,2.85,1.8,2.125,σ_y;0.7,0.45,1.4,1.02,σ_z" titolo="Hydrostatic pressure shown on three faces"}

- $-1000\cdot10^{-6}$
- $-250\cdot10^{-6}$
- $-500\cdot10^{-6}$
- $500\cdot10^{-6}$

## Explanation

The three normal stresses are equal and negative. Each normal strain equals $-\dfrac{(1-2\nu)60}{E}$ and the volumetric strain is their sum.
