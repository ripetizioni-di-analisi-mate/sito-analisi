A sheet is in uniform plane stress with $\sigma_x=32\,\mathrm{MPa}$. The inclined edge shown, with normal $\mathbf n=(\dfrac{3}{5},\dfrac{4}{5})$, is traction-free. Find $\tau_{xy}$.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 2.4,0 0,1.8" frecce="1.2,0.9,1.74,1.62,n" etichette="1.75,1.05,free edge" titolo="Sheet with an unloaded inclined edge"}

- $-19.2\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $-24\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $24\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $-\dfrac{128}{3}\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

Traction vanishes on the free edge, so $\boldsymbol\sigma\mathbf n=\mathbf 0$.

The component along $x$ gives $\dfrac35\sigma_x+\dfrac45\tau_{xy}=0$.

Therefore $\tau_{xy}=-\dfrac34\sigma_x=-24\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

The component along $y$ then gives $\sigma_y=-\dfrac34\tau_{xy}=18\,\mathrm{MPa}$, completing the stress state compatible with the free edge.
