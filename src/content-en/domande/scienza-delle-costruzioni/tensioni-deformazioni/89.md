A point in plane stress has $\sigma_x=120\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\tau_{xy}=0$. What value of $\sigma_y$ minimizes the von Mises equivalent stress?

- $60\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $0\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $120\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $-60\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

With $\tau_{xy}=0$ the result is $\sigma_{\mathrm{eq}}^2=\sigma_x^2-\sigma_x\sigma_y+\sigma_y^2=14\,400-120\,\sigma_y+\sigma_y^2$.

As a function of $\sigma_y$ this is an upward-opening parabola, with a minimum where the derivative $2\sigma_y-120$ vanishes.

The minimum therefore occurs at $\sigma_y=60\,\mathrm{MPa}$ with $\sigma_{\mathrm{eq}}=\sqrt{10\,800}\simeq103.9\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

At $\sigma_y=0$ and $\sigma_y=120\,\mathrm{MPa}$ the equivalent stress is $120\,\mathrm{MPa}$, whereas at $\sigma_y=-60\,\mathrm{MPa}$ it is approximately $158.7\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
