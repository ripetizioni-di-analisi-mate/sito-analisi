The sheet shown is in plane stress. The measured strains are $\varepsilon_x=500\cdot10^{-6}$, $\varepsilon_y=-125\cdot10^{-6}$, and $\gamma_{xy}=-250\cdot10^{-6}$. The isotropic linear elastic material has $E=200\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\nu=0.25$. Find $\tau_{xy}$.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 3,0 3,2 0,2" frecce="0,0,3.8,0,x;0,0,0,2.8,y" punti="1.5,1,P,pieno" riferimento=0 titolo="Measurement point in the midplane of the sheet"}

- $20\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $-40\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $-25\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The shear modulus is $G=\dfrac{E}{2(1+\nu)}=80\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$, so $\tau_{xy}=G\gamma_{xy}=80\,000\cdot(-250\cdot10^{-6})\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
