The stress field shown has components $\sigma_x=12+3x+y$, $\sigma_y=9-x+5y$, and $\tau_{xy}=2x-4y$ in $\mathrm{MPa}$. Here $x$ and $y$ are the numerical values of the coordinates in meters. Under static equilibrium, what is the $b_x$ component of the body force per unit volume?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 3,0 3,2 0,2" frecce="0,0,3.8,0,x;0,0,0,2.8,y" punti="1.5,1,P,pieno" riferimento=0 titolo="Interior point in a planar field"}

- $1\,\mathrm{MN/m^3}$
- $-1\,\mathrm{MN/m^3}$
- $7\,\mathrm{MN/m^3}$
- $-7\,\mathrm{MN/m^3}$

## Explanation

Equilibrium along $x$ gives $b_x=-\partial_x\sigma_x-\partial_y\tau_{xy}=-3-(-4)$. The derivatives of stresses in $\mathrm{MPa}$ with respect to coordinates in meters are in $\mathrm{MN/m^3}$.
