An ideal ductile material has uniaxial yield strength $240\,\mathrm{MPa}$. In pure shear, at what magnitudes of $\tau$ do Tresca and von Mises predict the onset of yield, respectively? Round to one decimal place.

- $120.0\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $138.6\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $240.0\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $169.7\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $120.0\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $80.0\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $138.6\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $120.0\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

In pure shear the principal stresses are $+|\tau |$, $0$, and $-|\tau |$. Tresca sets $2|\tau |$ equal to the uniaxial yield stress. Von Mises sets $\sqrt{3}|\tau |$ equal to that same value. This gives $120.0\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $138.6\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
