A simply connected plane region has prescribed strains $\varepsilon_x=20y^2\cdot10^{-6}$, $\varepsilon_y=10x^2\cdot10^{-6}$, and $\gamma_{xy}=kxy\cdot10^{-6}$, with $x,y$ denoting the numerical coordinate values in meters. What coefficient $k$ satisfies strain compatibility?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 3,0 3,2 0,2" frecce="0,0,3.8,0,x;0,0,0,2.8,y" punti="1.5,1,P,pieno" riferimento=0 titolo="Planar region with Cartesian coordinates"}

- $30$
- $60$
- $20$
- $120$

## Explanation

In-plane strain compatibility requires $\partial^2_y\varepsilon_x+\partial^2_x\varepsilon_y=\partial_{xy}^2\gamma_{xy}$. The two terms on the left are $40$ and $20$ in microstrain.
