Under small-strain assumptions a solid has displacement field $\mathbf u=(2x,\;3y,\;-z)\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm m$, with coordinates in meters. Find the volumetric strain.

- $6\cdot10^{-4}$
- $5\cdot10^{-4}$
- $-4\cdot10^{-4}$
- $4\cdot10^{-4}$

## Explanation

The first-order relative volume change is the trace of the strain tensor.

The three normal strains are $\varepsilon_x=2\cdot10^{-4}$, $\varepsilon_y=3\cdot10^{-4}$, and $\varepsilon_z=-10^{-4}$.

The volumetric strain is therefore $(2+3-1)\cdot10^{-4}=4\cdot10^{-4}$.

The volume increases even though the solid shortens along $z$.
