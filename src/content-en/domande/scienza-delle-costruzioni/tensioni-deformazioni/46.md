A column of height $4\,\mathrm m$ and unit weight $25\,\mathrm{kN/m^3}$ stands on its base $y=0$ and is free at the top. The stress field is $\sigma_y(y)$ with $\sigma_x=\tau_{xy}=0$, and the body force per unit volume is $b_y=-25\,\mathrm{kN/m^3}$. Find $\sigma_y$ at the base.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 1,0 1,4 0,4" linee="-0.6,0 1.6,0" quote="0 0,0 4,4 m,-0.5" frecce="0.5,2.3,0.5,1.5,b_y" titolo="Column under its own weight"}

- $-0.1\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $-1\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $-0.05\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $0.1\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The second local equilibrium equation reduces to $\dfrac{d\sigma_y}{dy}+b_y=0$, giving $\dfrac{d\sigma_y}{dy}=25\,\mathrm{kN/m^3}$.

The top $y=4\,\mathrm m$ is free, so $\sigma_y(4)=0$ and $\sigma_y(y)=25\,(y-4)\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$.

At the base, $\sigma_y(0)=-100\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}=-0.1\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

The negative sign denotes compression from the weight of the material above.
