For the same isotropic linear elastic material, a tensile test gives $E=210\,\mathrm{GPa}$ and a shear test gives $G=84\,\mathrm{GPa}$. Find Poisson's ratio.

- $0.25$
- $-0.25$
- $0.40$
- $0.50$

## Explanation

The isotropic elastic constants satisfy $E=2G(1+\nu)$, so $\nu=\dfrac{210}{2\times 84}-1=0.25$.
