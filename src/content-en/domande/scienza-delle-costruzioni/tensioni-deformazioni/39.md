A rectangular section of width $30\,\mathrm{mm}$ and depth $100\,\mathrm{mm}$ has an upward $y$ axis through its centroid. The normal stress is $\sigma_x=-\dfrac{y}{5}$, with $\sigma_x$ in $\mathrm{MPa}$ and $y$ in $\mathrm{mm}$. Find the resultant bending moment and identify which fibers it puts in tension.

- $2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, putting the bottom fibers in tension
- $0.375\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, putting the bottom fibers in tension
- $0.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, putting the top fibers in tension
- $0.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, putting the bottom fibers in tension

## Explanation

For $y<0$, $\sigma_x>0$. The bottom fibers are therefore in tension and the bending moment is positive under the course convention.

The bending moment magnitude is $\displaystyle\int_A |\sigma_x y|\,dA=\dfrac15\int_A y^2\,dA=\dfrac{I}{5}$.

The centroidal second moment of area is $I=\dfrac{30\cdot100^3}{12}=2.5\cdot10^{6}\,\mathrm{mm^4}$.

Therefore $M=5\cdot10^{5}\,\mathrm{N\,mm}=0.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
