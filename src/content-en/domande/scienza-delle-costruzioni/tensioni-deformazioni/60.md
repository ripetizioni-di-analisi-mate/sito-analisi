A thin-walled cylindrical vessel of mean radius $500\,\mathrm{mm}$ and thickness $10\,\mathrm{mm}$ contains gas at pressure $2\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Its hoop and axial stresses are $\dfrac{pr}{t}$ and $\dfrac{pr}{2t}$, respectively. Find the shear stress magnitude on a helical weld inclined at $45^\circ$ to the axis.

- $50\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $75\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $25\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $12.5\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The stresses are $\sigma_\theta=\dfrac{2\cdot500}{10}=100\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\sigma_a=50\,\mathrm{MPa}$. They are principal because there is no shear stress between these two wall directions.

The weld normal makes an angle $45^\circ$ to each principal direction, so $\sin2\theta=\pm1$ and $\cos2\theta=0$.

Thus the shear stress magnitude is $\dfrac{|\sigma_\theta-\sigma_a|}{2}=25\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

The normal stress on the same weld is $75\,\mathrm{MPa}$, the mean of the two principal stresses.
