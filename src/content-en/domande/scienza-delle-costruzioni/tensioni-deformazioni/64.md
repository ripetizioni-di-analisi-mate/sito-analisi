A plane stress state has $\sigma_x=-20\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_y=40\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\tau_{xy}=0$. Find the larger in-plane principal stress and the normal to its plane.

- $30\,\mathrm{MPa}$, with normal at $45^\circ$
- $40\,\mathrm{MPa}$, with normal along $x$
- $40\,\mathrm{MPa}$, with normal along $y$
- $-20\,\mathrm{MPa}$, with normal along $x$

## Explanation

Since $\tau_{xy}=0$, the faces with normals $\mathbf e_x$ and $\mathbf e_y$ carry zero shear stress and are already principal planes.

The principal stresses are therefore $-20\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $40\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

The larger one, $40\,\mathrm{MPa}$, acts on the face with normal $\mathbf e_y$, corresponding to $\theta_1=90^\circ$.

The value $30\,\mathrm{MPa}$ is the Mohr circle radius, equal to the maximum in-plane shear stress at $45^\circ$.
