Coordinates are in meters and stresses are in $\mathrm{MPa}$. Which plane stress field satisfies local equilibrium in the absence of body forces?

- $\sigma_x=y^2$, $\sigma_y=x^2$, $\tau_{xy}=-2xy$
- $\sigma_x=x^2$, $\sigma_y=-y^2$, $\tau_{xy}=-2xy$
- $\sigma_x=x^2$, $\sigma_y=y^2$, $\tau_{xy}=2xy$
- $\sigma_x=x^2$, $\sigma_y=y^2$, $\tau_{xy}=-2xy$

## Explanation

In the absence of body forces the equations are $\dfrac{\partial\sigma_x}{\partial x}+\dfrac{\partial\tau_{xy}}{\partial y}=0$ and $\dfrac{\partial\tau_{xy}}{\partial x}+\dfrac{\partial\sigma_y}{\partial y}=0$.

For $\sigma_x=x^2$, $\sigma_y=y^2$, and $\tau_{xy}=-2xy$, the first equation gives $2x-2x=0$ and the second gives $-2y+2y=0$, so the field is in equilibrium.

With $\tau_{xy}=2xy$ the first equation gives $4x$, with $\sigma_y=-y^2$ the second gives $-4y$, and with $\sigma_x=y^2$ the first gives $-2x$.

The other three fields therefore require a nonzero body force.
