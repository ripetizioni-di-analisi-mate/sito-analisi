A plane stress state has $\sigma_{x}=100\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_{y}=50\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\tau_{xy}=30\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Find the von Mises equivalent stress to one decimal place.

- $123.3\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $101.0\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $91.7\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $86.6\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

For $\sigma_{z}=\tau_{xz}=\tau_{yz}=0$, the von Mises equivalent stress is $\sqrt{\sigma_{x}^{2}-\sigma_{x}\sigma_{y}+\sigma_{y}^{2}+3\tau_{xy}^{2}}$. The shear stress enters as its square. Substitution gives $101.0\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
