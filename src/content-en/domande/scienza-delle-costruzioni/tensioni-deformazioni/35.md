The principal stresses at a point are $90$, $30$, and $0\,\mathrm{MPa}$, in that order. Find the deviatoric stress in principal direction number $2$.

- $30.00\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $40.00\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $70.00\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $-10.00\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The hydrostatic stress is $\dfrac{\sigma_{1}+\sigma_{2}+\sigma_{3}}{3}=40.00\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Subtracting it from the principal stress in the specified direction gives $-10.00\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
