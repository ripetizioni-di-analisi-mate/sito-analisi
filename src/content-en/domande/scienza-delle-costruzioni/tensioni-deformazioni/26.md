At a point the strains are $\varepsilon_{x}=400\,\mu\varepsilon$, $\varepsilon_{y}=-200\,\mu\varepsilon$, and engineering shear strain $\gamma_{xy}=300\,\mu\varepsilon$. What would a strain gauge oriented at $30^\circ$ from $+x$ read, taking counterclockwise angles as positive? Round to one decimal place.

::struttura{frecce="0,0,5.5,0,x;0,0,0,5.5,y;0,0,4.33013,2.5,n" titolo="Strain gauge orientation" riferimento="0"}

- $250.0\,\mu\varepsilon$
- $379.9\,\mu\varepsilon$
- $120.1\,\mu\varepsilon$
- $509.8\,\mu\varepsilon$

## Explanation

The gauge reads $n^\mathsf{T}\varepsilon n$. The off-diagonal strain tensor entry is $\dfrac{\gamma_{xy}}{2}$, so $\varepsilon(\theta)=\varepsilon_{x}\cos^{2}\theta+\varepsilon_{y}\sin^{2}\theta+\gamma_{xy}\sin \theta \cos \theta=379.9\,\mu\varepsilon$.
