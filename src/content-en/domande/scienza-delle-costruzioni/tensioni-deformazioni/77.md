For an isotropic linear elastic material, which relationship has the same form in plane stress and plane strain?

- $\gamma_{xy}=\dfrac{\tau_{xy}}{G}$
- $\sigma_z=0$
- $\varepsilon_z=-\dfrac{\nu(\sigma_x+\sigma_y)}{E}$
- $\varepsilon_x=\dfrac{\sigma_x-\nu\sigma_y}{E}$

## Explanation

The two assumptions differ in the out-of-plane condition, $\sigma_z=0$ or $\varepsilon_z=0$.

The out-of-plane condition enters only the normal-component equations. In isotropic Hooke's law, $\sigma_z$ is absent from the shear relation.

Thus $\gamma_{xy}=\dfrac{\tau_{xy}}{G}$ has the same form in both models.

The other three relations hold only in plane stress. In plane strain $\sigma_z=\nu(\sigma_x+\sigma_y)$ and $\varepsilon_z=0$.
