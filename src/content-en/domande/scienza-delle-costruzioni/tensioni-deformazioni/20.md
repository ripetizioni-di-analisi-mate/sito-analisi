At a point in an isotropic linear elastic material with $E=200\,\mathrm{GPa}$ and $\nu=0.25$, the normal stresses are $100$, $60$, and $-40\,\mathrm{MPa}$, with tension positive. Find the first-order volumetric strain. Round to the nearest $\mu\varepsilon$.

- $600\,\mu\varepsilon$
- $300\,\mu\varepsilon$
- $100\,\mu\varepsilon$
- $-300\,\mu\varepsilon$

## Explanation

The first-order volumetric strain is the sum of the three normal strains. Adding the three Hooke-law equations gives $\dfrac{\Delta V}{V}=\dfrac{(1-2\nu)(\sigma_{x}+\sigma_{y}+\sigma_{z})}{E}$. The stress trace is $120\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and the fractional volume change is $300\,\mu\varepsilon$.
