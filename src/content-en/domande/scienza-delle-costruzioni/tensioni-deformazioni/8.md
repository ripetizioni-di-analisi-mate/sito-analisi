An isotropic linear elastic sheet is in plane stress with $E=200\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\nu=0.25$, $\sigma_x=120\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_y=60\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\tau_{xy}=30\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Find $\varepsilon_z$.

::struttura{poligoni="-1,-1 1,-1 1,1 -1,1" reazioni="1 0,0.55,0,σ_x;-1 0,-0.55,0;0 1,0,0.55,σ_y;0 -1,0,-0.55" frecce="1.13,-0.65,1.13,0.58,τ_xy;-1.13,0.65,-1.13,-0.58;-0.55,1.13,0.58,1.13;0.55,-1.13,-0.58,-1.13" titolo="Sheet with traction-free faces perpendicular to z"}

- $-225\cdot10^{-6}$
- $225\cdot10^{-6}$
- $-150\cdot10^{-6}$
- $-450\cdot10^{-6}$

## Explanation

In plane stress $\sigma_z=0$. The three-dimensional Hooke law then gives $\varepsilon_z=-\dfrac{\nu(\sigma_x+\sigma_y)}{E}=-\dfrac{0.25\cdot180}{200\,000}$.
