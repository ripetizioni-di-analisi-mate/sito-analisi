An isotropic linearly elastic sheet has traction-free thickness faces, $\nu=0.30$ and constant temperature. A gauge measures $\varepsilon_z=0$ while $\sigma_x=90\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Which $\sigma_y$ is consistent with the measurement?

- $-90\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $0\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $27\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $90\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

Under plane stress $\sigma_z=0$. Hooke's law gives $\varepsilon_z=-\dfrac{\nu}{E}(\sigma_x+\sigma_y)$. For the thickness strain to vanish, the in-plane stresses must cancel, giving $\sigma_y=-90\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
