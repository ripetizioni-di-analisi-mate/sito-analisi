A strain-gauge rosette has grids at $0^\circ$, $90^\circ$, and $-45^\circ$, measured counterclockwise from $x$. They give readings $\varepsilon_0=300\cdot10^{-6}$, $\varepsilon_{90}=100\cdot10^{-6}$, and $\varepsilon_{-45}=50\cdot10^{-6}$, respectively. Find $\gamma_{xy}$.

::struttura{poligoni="-0.2,-2 3.2,-2 3.2,2.8 -0.2,2.8" frecce="0,0,2.5,0,0°;0,0,0,2.3,90°;0,0,1.8,-1.8,−45°" titolo="Strain-gauge rosette with grids at 0°, 90°, and −45°"}

- $400\cdot10^{-6}$
- $150\cdot10^{-6}$
- $300\cdot10^{-6}$
- $-300\cdot10^{-6}$

## Explanation

For $\theta=-45^\circ$ we have $\cos2\theta=0$ and $\sin2\theta=-1$, so $\varepsilon_{-45}=\dfrac{\varepsilon_x+\varepsilon_y-\gamma_{xy}}{2}$.

Therefore $\gamma_{xy}=\varepsilon_0+\varepsilon_{90}-2\varepsilon_{-45}$.

Substitution gives $\gamma_{xy}=(300+100-100)\cdot10^{-6}=300\cdot10^{-6}$.

The relation for the grid at $+45^\circ$ would give the opposite sign.
