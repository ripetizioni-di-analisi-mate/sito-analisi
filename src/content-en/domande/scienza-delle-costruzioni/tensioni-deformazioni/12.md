A rectangular strain-gauge rosette gives readings $\varepsilon_0=420\cdot10^{-6}$, $\varepsilon_{45}=300\cdot10^{-6}$, and $\varepsilon_{90}=-180\cdot10^{-6}$. The isotropic linear elastic material has $G=80\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Find $\tau_{xy}$.

::struttura{poligoni="-0.2,-0.2 3.2,-0.2 3.2,2.8 -0.2,2.8" frecce="0,0,2.5,0,0°;0,0,1.8,1.8,45°;0,0,0,2.3,90°" titolo="Orientations of the three rosette grids"}

- $28.8\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $14.4\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $48.0\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $9.6\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The rosette gives $\gamma_{xy}=2\varepsilon_{45}-\varepsilon_0-\varepsilon_{90}=360\cdot10^{-6}$. The shear stress–strain relation gives $\tau_{xy}=G\gamma_{xy}$.
