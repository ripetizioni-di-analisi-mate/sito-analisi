At a point in a steel beam under plane stress, $\sigma_x=150\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\sigma_y=0$. The yield strength is $240\,\mathrm{MPa}$. What is the largest $|\tau_{xy}|$ before yielding according to von Mises?

- $108.2\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $138.6\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $52.0\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $90.0\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

With $\sigma_y=0$, the equivalent stress is $\sigma_{\mathrm{eq}}=\sqrt{\sigma_x^2+3\tau_{xy}^2}$.

Yielding starts at $\sigma_{\mathrm{eq}}=240\,\mathrm{MPa}$, giving $3\tau_{xy}^2=240^2-150^2=35\,100$.

Therefore $|\tau_{xy}|=\sqrt{11\,700}\simeq108.2\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

The value $\dfrac{240}{\sqrt3}\simeq138.6\,\mathrm{MPa}$ applies only to pure shear, without normal stress.
