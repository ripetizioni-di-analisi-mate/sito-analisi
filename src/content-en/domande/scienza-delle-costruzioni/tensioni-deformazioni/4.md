In the plane shown, the displacements are $u_x=(200x+70y)\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m}$ and $u_y=(-30x-100y)\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m}$. The symbols $x,y$ denote the numerical coordinate values in meters. Find the engineering shear strain $\gamma_{xy}$.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 3,0 3,2 0,2" frecce="0,0,3.8,0,x;0,0,0,2.8,y" punti="1.5,1,P,pieno" riferimento=0 titolo="Coordinates of the point where the field is examined"}

- $70\cdot10^{-6}$
- $40\cdot10^{-6}$
- $20\cdot10^{-6}$
- $100\cdot10^{-6}$

## Explanation

The engineering shear strain is the sum $\partial_yu_x+\partial_xu_y$. Its two coefficients are $70$ and $-30$, giving a sum of $40$ in microstrain.
