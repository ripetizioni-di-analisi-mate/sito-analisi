The top edge of a sheet, with normal $+\mathbf e_y$, carries uniform normal pressure $5\,\mathrm{MPa}$ with no tangential traction. Find $\sigma_y$ and $\tau_{xy}$ on that edge.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 3,0 3,1.2 0,1.2" carichi="0 1.2,3 1.2,0.5,0.5,p" titolo="Sheet under pressure along its top edge"}

- $\sigma_y=-5\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\tau_{xy}=0$
- $\sigma_y=-5\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\tau_{xy}=-5\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $\sigma_y=0$ and $\tau_{xy}=-5\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $\sigma_y=5\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\tau_{xy}=0$

## Explanation

The boundary condition requires $\boldsymbol\sigma\mathbf e_y=\overline{\mathbf t}$, and the components of $\boldsymbol\sigma\mathbf e_y$ are $(\tau_{xy},\,\sigma_y)$.

Pressure acts inward, that is, along $-y$, so $\overline{\mathbf t}=(0,\,-5)\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

Thus $\tau_{xy}=0$ and $\sigma_y=-5\,\mathrm{MPa}$ on the edge.

Component $\sigma_x$ acts on faces normal to $\mathbf e_x$ and is not determined by this boundary condition.
