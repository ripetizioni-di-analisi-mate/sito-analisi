Two sheets of different materials are bonded along interface $y=0$ with normal $\mathbf e_y$ and loaded in their own plane. Which stress components must be equal on the two sides of the interface at the same point?

- $\sigma_x$, $\sigma_y$, and $\tau_{xy}$
- $\sigma_y$ and $\tau_{xy}$
- Only $\sigma_x$
- $\sigma_x$ and $\tau_{xy}$

## Explanation

The lower sheet has outward normal $\mathbf e_y$ at the interface, so its traction is $\boldsymbol\sigma^{-}\mathbf e_y=(\tau_{xy}^{-},\,\sigma_y^{-})$.

The upper sheet has outward normal $-\mathbf e_y$ and traction $-\boldsymbol\sigma^{+}\mathbf e_y=(-\tau_{xy}^{+},\,-\sigma_y^{+})$.

The two tractions are exchanged across the same surface and must be opposite, giving $\tau_{xy}^{+}=\tau_{xy}^{-}$ and $\sigma_y^{+}=\sigma_y^{-}$.

Component $\sigma_x$ acts on faces normal to $\mathbf e_x$ and may jump. For instance, if the two sheets have equal $\varepsilon_x$ but different Young's moduli, their $\sigma_x$ values differ.
