Which pair of elastic constants cannot give a stable isotropic linear elastic response?

- $E=200\,\mathrm{GPa}$ and $\nu=0.3$
- $E=30\,\mathrm{GPa}$ and $\nu=0.6$
- $E=70\,\mathrm{GPa}$ and $\nu=0.33$
- $E=0.005\,\mathrm{GPa}$ and $\nu=0.49$

## Explanation

The elastic response is stable if $E>0$ and $-1<\nu<\dfrac12$.

The pairs with $\nu=0.3$, $\nu=0.33$, and $\nu=0.49$ satisfy both conditions, even though $E$ is very small in the third case, as for rubber.

With $\nu=0.6$ the result is $1-2\nu<0$, so the bulk modulus $K=\dfrac{E}{3(1-2\nu)}$ would be negative.

The pair $E=30\,\mathrm{GPa}$, $\nu=0.6$ is therefore inadmissible.
