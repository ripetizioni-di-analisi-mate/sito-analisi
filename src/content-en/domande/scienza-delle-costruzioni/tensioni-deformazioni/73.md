Two strain gauges oriented at $+45^\circ$ and $-45^\circ$ from $x$ read $-50$ and $+150$ microstrain, respectively, at the same point. For $G=80\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$, what is the signed shear stress $\tau_{xy}$?

- $+16\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $-8\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $+4\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $-16\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The readings are $\dfrac{\varepsilon_x+\varepsilon_y}{2}\pm\dfrac{\gamma_{xy}}2$. Subtracting the second from the first gives engineering shear strain $-200\cdot10^{-6}$. Hence $\tau_{xy}=G\gamma_{xy}=-16\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
