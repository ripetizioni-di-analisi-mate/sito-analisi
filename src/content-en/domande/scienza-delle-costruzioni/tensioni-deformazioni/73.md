A shaft surface is in pure torsional shear relative to the axial direction $x$ and the circumferential direction $y$. A gauge oriented at $45^\circ$ to $x$ reads $250\cdot10^{-6}$. Given $G=80\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$, find the magnitude of $\tau_{xy}$.

- $20\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $80\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $50\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $40\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

In pure shear $\varepsilon_x=\varepsilon_y=0$, so the reading at $45^\circ$ is $\varepsilon_{45}=\dfrac{\gamma_{xy}}{2}$.

Therefore $|\gamma_{xy}|=2\cdot250\cdot10^{-6}=500\cdot10^{-6}$.

The shear stress–strain relation gives $|\tau_{xy}|=G|\gamma_{xy}|=80\,000\cdot500\cdot10^{-6}=40\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

The product $G\varepsilon_{45}$ would give half the correct value because the gauge reading is half the engineering shear strain.
