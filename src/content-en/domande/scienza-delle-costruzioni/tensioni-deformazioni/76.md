Which of the following situations is described by plane strain?

- The lining of a long tunnel at a section far from its entrances
- A thin disk rotating about its axis
- The wall of a thin cylindrical vessel
- A thin sheet loaded in its own plane

## Explanation

Plane strain requires $\varepsilon_z=\gamma_{xz}=\gamma_{yz}=0$, with geometry and loading uniform along $z$.

Away from the entrances, a long tunnel lining meets these conditions because neighboring sections restrain axial elongation.

The sheet, disk, and vessel wall are thin and have free faces, so $\sigma_z\simeq0$.

Plane stress therefore applies in those three cases.
