In a state of plane stress, $\sigma_x=70\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_y=-10\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\tau_{xy}=24\,\mathrm{MPa}$. What is the normal stress component $\sigma_n$ on the plane with unit normal $\mathbf n=(\dfrac{3}{5},\dfrac{4}{5})$?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 2.4,0 0,1.8" frecce="1.2,0.9,1.74,1.62,n;1.2,0.9,0.48,1.44,t" titolo="Normal and tangent to the inclined face"}

- $18.8\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $-4.24\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $45.12\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $41.84\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The normal component is given by $\mathbf n^\mathsf{T}\boldsymbol\sigma\mathbf n$. The three contributions are $70\cdot\dfrac9{25}$, $-10\cdot\dfrac{16}{25}$ and $2\cdot24\cdot\dfrac{12}{25}$.
