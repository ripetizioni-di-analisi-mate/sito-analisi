A plane stress state has $\sigma_x=40\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\sigma_y=10\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Above what threshold value of $|\tau_{xy}|$ do the two in-plane principal stresses have opposite signs?

- $15\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $25\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $50\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $20\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The determinant of the in-plane stress matrix is invariant, so $\sigma_1\sigma_2=\sigma_x\sigma_y-\tau_{xy}^2=400-\tau_{xy}^2$.

The in-plane principal stresses have opposite signs exactly when their product is negative, that is, when $\tau_{xy}^2>400\,\mathrm{MPa^2}$.

The threshold value is therefore $|\tau_{xy}|=20\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

For $|\tau_{xy}|=20\,\mathrm{MPa}$ the smaller principal stress is $\sigma_2=25-\sqrt{15^2+20^2}=0$.
