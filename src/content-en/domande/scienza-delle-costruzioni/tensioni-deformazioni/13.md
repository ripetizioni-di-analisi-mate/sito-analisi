The strain-gauge rosette shown gives readings $\varepsilon_0=150\cdot10^{-6}$, $\varepsilon_{45}=250\cdot10^{-6}$, and $\varepsilon_{90}=-50\cdot10^{-6}$. Find the larger in-plane principal strain.

::struttura{poligoni="-0.2,-0.2 3.2,-0.2 3.2,2.8 -0.2,2.8" frecce="0,0,2.5,0,0°;0,0,1.8,1.8,45°;0,0,0,2.3,90°" titolo="Rosette with grids at 0°, 45°, and 90°"}

- $(50-100\sqrt5)\cdot10^{-6}$
- $(50+100\sqrt5)\cdot10^{-6}$
- $250\cdot10^{-6}$
- $200\cdot10^{-6}$

## Explanation

The center of the strain circle is the mean of the readings at $0^\circ$ and $90^\circ$, namely $50$ in microstrain. The radius is $\sqrt{100^2+200^2}=100\sqrt5$. Adding it to the center gives the larger principal strain.
