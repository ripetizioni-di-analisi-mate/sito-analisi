At a point the stress tensor, in $\mathrm{MPa}$, has components $\sigma_x=45$, $\sigma_z=9$, and $\tau_{xz}=18$, with all other components zero. Find the normal stress on the face with normal $\mathbf n=\dfrac{1}{3}(1,2,2)$.

- $13\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $17\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $51\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $9\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

By Cauchy's relation the normal stress is $\sigma_n=\mathbf n^{\mathsf T}\boldsymbol\sigma\mathbf n$.

Retaining only the nonzero components gives $\sigma_n=\sigma_x n_x^2+\sigma_z n_z^2+2\tau_{xz}n_xn_z$.

Since $n_x=\dfrac13$ and $n_z=\dfrac23$, the result is $\sigma_n=\dfrac{45+36+72}{9}=\dfrac{153}{9}=17\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

The mixed term appears twice because the tensor is symmetric. The component $\tau_{xz}$ occurs in both the first and third rows.
