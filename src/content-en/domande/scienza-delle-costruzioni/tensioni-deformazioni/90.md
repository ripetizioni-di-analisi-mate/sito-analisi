The principal stresses at a point are $100$, $50$, and $-50\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Find the von Mises equivalent stress.

- $86.6\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $132.3\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $111.8\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $150.0\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

In terms of the principal stresses, $\sigma_{\mathrm{eq}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{(\sigma_1-\sigma_2)^2+(\sigma_2-\sigma_3)^2+(\sigma_3-\sigma_1)^2}{2}}$.

The differences are $50$, $100$, and $150\,\mathrm{MPa}$, giving a sum of squares $35\,000$.

Therefore $\sigma_{\mathrm{eq}}=\sqrt{17\,500}\simeq132.3\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

The plane stress formula using only $100$ and $50\,\mathrm{MPa}$ would give $86.6\,\mathrm{MPa}$ because it omits the third principal stress.
