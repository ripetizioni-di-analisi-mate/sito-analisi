All three grids of a rectangular strain-gauge rosette at $0^\circ$, $45^\circ$, and $90^\circ$ read $200\cdot10^{-6}$. Which statement is true?

- $\gamma_{xy}=200\cdot10^{-6}$ and the principal directions are at $\pm45^\circ$
- The only principal directions are $x$ and $y$
- $\gamma_{xy}=0$ and every in-plane direction is principal
- The larger principal strain is $300\cdot10^{-6}$

## Explanation

The rosette relations give $\varepsilon_x=\varepsilon_y=200\cdot10^{-6}$ and $\gamma_{xy}=2\varepsilon_{45}-\varepsilon_0-\varepsilon_{90}=0$.

Mohr's strain circle therefore has zero radius.

The strain is thus $200\cdot10^{-6}$ in every in-plane direction, and every such direction is principal.

The two principal strains are equal, so neither is larger than the other.
