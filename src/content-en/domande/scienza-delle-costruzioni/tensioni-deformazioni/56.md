A wooden board is compressed along $x$ with $\sigma_x=-6\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and all other stress components are zero. Its grain makes a counterclockwise angle $30^\circ$ with the $x$ axis. Find the normal stress perpendicular to the grain, on a face parallel to the grain.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 4,0 4,1.5 0,1.5" tratteggi="0.3,0 2.9,1.5;1.3,0 3.9,1.5;2.3,0 4,0.98;0,0.4 1.9,1.5" frecce="4.8,0.75,4.1,0.75,σ_x;-0.8,0.75,-0.1,0.75" titolo="Compressed board with grain inclined at $30^\circ$"}

- $1.5\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $-4.5\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $-2.6\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $-1.5\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

A face parallel to the grain has its normal rotated by $90^\circ$ from the grain, so $\theta=120^\circ$.

With $p=d=-3\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\tau_{xy}=0$, the transformation formula gives $\sigma_n=-3-3\cos240^\circ$.

Since $\cos240^\circ=-\dfrac12$, this gives $\sigma_n=-1.5\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

The value $-4.5\,\mathrm{MPa}$ belongs to the face perpendicular to the grain, whose normal is at $30^\circ$.
