A plane stress state has $\sigma_x=65\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\sigma_y=35\,\mathrm{MPa}$. In an in-plane coordinate system $x'y'$ rotated from the original axes, $\sigma_{x'}=80\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Find $\sigma_{y'}$.

- $35\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $15\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $20\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $50\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The sum of normal stresses on orthogonal faces is the trace of the in-plane stress matrix, independent of the coordinate system.

Thus $\sigma_{x'}+\sigma_{y'}=\sigma_x+\sigma_y=100\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

Therefore $\sigma_{y'}=100-80=20\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
