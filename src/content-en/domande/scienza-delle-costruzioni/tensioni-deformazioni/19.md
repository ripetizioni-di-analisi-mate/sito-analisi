An isotropic sheet in plane stress has $E=200\,\mathrm{GPa}$ and $\nu=0.250$. Strain gauges measure $\varepsilon_{x}=500\,\mu\varepsilon$ and $\varepsilon_{y}=0\,\mu\varepsilon$. Find $\sigma_{x}$ and $\sigma_{y}$ to one decimal place.

- $26.7\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $106.7\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $100.0\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $0.0\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $106.7\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $-26.7\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $106.7\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $26.7\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

With $\sigma_{z}=0$, the equations are $E\varepsilon_{x}=\sigma_{x}-\nu \sigma_{y}$ and $E\varepsilon_{y}=\sigma_{y}-\nu \sigma_{x}$. Solving gives $\sigma_{x}=\dfrac{E(\varepsilon_{x}+\nu \varepsilon_{y})}{1-\nu^{2}}$ and $\sigma_{y}=\dfrac{E(\varepsilon_{y}+\nu \varepsilon_{x})}{1-\nu^{2}}$. Convert $\mathrm{GPa}$ to $\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\mu\varepsilon$ to dimensionless strain. The results are $106.7\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $26.7\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
