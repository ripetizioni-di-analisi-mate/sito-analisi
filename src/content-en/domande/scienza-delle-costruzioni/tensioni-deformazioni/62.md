At a point in a plane stress state, $\sigma_x=\sigma_y=40\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\tau_{xy}=0$. Which statement is true?

- Every in-plane direction is principal, and on every face perpendicular to the plane $\tau_{nt}=0$
- The normal stress vanishes on the face whose normal is at $45^\circ$
- The only in-plane principal directions are $x$ and $y$
- The maximum in-plane shear stress magnitude is $40\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

With $\sigma_x=\sigma_y$ we have $d=0$, and with $\tau_{xy}=0$ the Mohr circle radius is $R=\sqrt{d^2+\tau_{xy}^2}=0$.

The circle reduces to point $(40,0)$, so $\sigma_n=40\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\tau_{nt}=0$ on every face perpendicular to the plane.

Every in-plane direction is therefore principal.

The maximum in-plane shear stress is $R=0$. Including the third principal stress, which is zero, gives shear stress $20\,\mathrm{MPa}$ on planes inclined at $45^\circ$ to the $z$ axis.
