A square element with sides along $+x$ and $+y$ undergoes an engineering shear strain $\gamma_{xy}=5\cdot10^{-4}$. To first order, what is the angle between its sides after deformation?

- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}+5\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}-2.5\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}-5\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}-10^{-3}\,\mathrm{rad}$

## Explanation

Engineering shear strain $\gamma_{xy}$ measures the decrease in the initially right angle between segments directed along $+x$ and $+y$.

Since $\gamma_{xy}>0$, the angle decreases by $5\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{rad}$.

The deformed angle is therefore $\dfrac{\pi}{2}-5\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{rad}$.

Half this value, $\varepsilon_{xy}=2.5\cdot10^{-4}$, is the tensor shear component. It differs from the change in angle.
