A point is in pure shear with $\tau_{xy}=-36\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Find the angle of the normal to the plane of maximum principal stress in the interval $[-90^\circ,90^\circ)$.

::struttura{poligoni="-1,-1 1,-1 1,1 -1,1" frecce="1.15,0.55,1.15,-0.55,τ_xy;-1.15,-0.55,-1.15,0.55;0.55,1.15,-0.55,1.15;-0.55,-1.15,0.55,-1.15" titolo="Directions of negative shear stresses"}

- $45\,{}^\circ$
- $0\,{}^\circ$
- $-90\,{}^\circ$
- $-45\,{}^\circ$

## Explanation

In negative pure shear the unit vector $(\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2},-\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2})$ is an eigenvector of the positive principal stress. Its direction makes an angle $-45^\circ$ with $x$.
