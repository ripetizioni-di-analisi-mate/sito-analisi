An isotropic linear elastic solid with $\nu=0.25$ is in plane strain with $\sigma_x=100\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_y=0$, and $\tau_{xy}=0$. Find the von Mises equivalent stress.

- $90.1\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $114.6\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $100\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $75\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

Restraint $\varepsilon_z=0$ produces $\sigma_z=\nu(\sigma_x+\sigma_y)=25\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

The principal stresses are therefore $100$, $25$, and $0\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and the plane stress formula with $\sigma_z=0$ does not apply.

The three-dimensional formula gives $\sigma_{\mathrm{eq}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{(100-25)^2+(25-0)^2+(0-100)^2}{2}}=\sqrt{8125}$.

Therefore $\sigma_{\mathrm{eq}}\simeq90.1\,\mathrm{MPa}$, less than the uniaxial value $100\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
