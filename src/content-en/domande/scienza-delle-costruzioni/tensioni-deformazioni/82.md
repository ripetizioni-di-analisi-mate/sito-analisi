At a point $\varepsilon_x+\varepsilon_y=200\cdot10^{-6}$. A gauge whose grid is at $30^\circ$ from $x$ reads $380\cdot10^{-6}$. What does a gauge at $120^\circ$ read at the same point?

- $-380\cdot10^{-6}$
- $-180\cdot10^{-6}$
- $580\cdot10^{-6}$
- $180\cdot10^{-6}$

## Explanation

The grids are perpendicular, so their double angles differ by $180^\circ$ and the terms containing $\cos2\theta$ and $\sin2\theta$ reverse sign.

Adding the two readings leaves only the mean terms, giving $\varepsilon_{30}+\varepsilon_{120}=\varepsilon_x+\varepsilon_y$.

Thus $\varepsilon_{120}=(200-380)\cdot10^{-6}=-180\cdot10^{-6}$.
