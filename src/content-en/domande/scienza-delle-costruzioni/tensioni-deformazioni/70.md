A cube of isotropic linear elastic material with $\nu=0.3$ fits snugly into a rigid, frictionless mold that prevents the strains $\varepsilon_x=\varepsilon_y=0$. The top face is loaded with $\sigma_z=-100\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Find the stress $\sigma_x$ exerted by the walls.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 2,0 2,2 0,2" linee="-0.15,2.6 -0.15,-0.15 2.15,-0.15 2.15,2.6" frecce="1,3.1,1,2.05,σ_z;2.8,0.3,3.6,0.3,x;2.8,0.3,2.8,1.1,z" riferimento=0 titolo="Cube compressed in a rigid mold"}

- $-30\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $-100\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $-42.9\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $-75\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

By symmetry $\sigma_x=\sigma_y$, and condition $\varepsilon_x=0$ gives $\sigma_x-\nu(\sigma_x+\sigma_z)=0$.

Thus $\sigma_x(1-\nu)=\nu\sigma_z$ and $\sigma_x=\dfrac{\nu}{1-\nu}\sigma_z$.

With $\nu=0.3$ this gives $\sigma_x=\dfrac{0.3}{0.7}\cdot(-100)\simeq-42.9\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

The value $-30\,\mathrm{MPa}$ would result from neglecting the contribution of $\sigma_y$ to the strain along $x$.
