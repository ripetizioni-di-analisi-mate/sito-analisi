An isotropic linear elastic solid in plane strain has $E=200\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\nu=0.25$, $\varepsilon_x=450\cdot10^{-6}$, $\varepsilon_y=-100\cdot10^{-6}$, and $\gamma_{xy}=0$. Find $\sigma_z$.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 2.8,0 2.8,1.8 0,1.8" linee="2.8,0 3.6,0.65 3.6,2.45 0.8,2.45 0,1.8;2.8,1.8 3.6,2.45;2.8,1.8 2.8,0" etichette="1.38,0.83,ε_z = 0" titolo="Element with out-of-plane displacement restrained"}

- $0\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $12\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $56\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $28\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

In plane strain the out-of-plane stress is $\sigma_z=\lambda(\varepsilon_x+\varepsilon_y)$. Here $\lambda=80\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and the sum is $350\cdot10^{-6}$.
