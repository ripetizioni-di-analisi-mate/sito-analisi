In a sheet, with coordinates in meters and stresses in $\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_x=6xy$ and $\sigma_y=0$, and the body forces vanish. The edges $y=1$ and $y=-1$ are free. Find $\tau_{xy}$ along the $y=0$ axis.

- $-3\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $\dfrac32\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $6\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $3\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The first local equilibrium equation gives $\dfrac{\partial\tau_{xy}}{\partial y}=-\dfrac{\partial\sigma_x}{\partial x}=-6y$.

Integration with respect to $y$ gives $\tau_{xy}=-3y^2+c$, with constant $c$.

On the edges $y=\pm1$ the traction vanishes, so $\tau_{xy}=0$ and $c=3$.

Thus $\tau_{xy}=3(1-y^2)$, which gives $3\,\mathrm{MPa}$ for $y=0$.
