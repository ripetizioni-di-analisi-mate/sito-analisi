A rosette reads $500\,\mu\varepsilon$ along $+x$, $350\,\mu\varepsilon$ at $+60^\circ$, and $50\,\mu\varepsilon$ at $-60^\circ$. All angles are measured counterclockwise from $+x$. Find $\varepsilon_{y}$ and the engineering shear strain $\gamma_{xy}$ to one decimal place.

::struttura{frecce="0,0,5.5,0,x;0,0,0,5.5,y;0,0,2.5,4.33013,+60°;0,0,2.5,-4.33013,−60°" titolo="Measurement directions of the strain-gauge rosette" riferimento="0"}

- $100.0\,\mu\varepsilon$ and $-346.4\,\mu\varepsilon$
- $200.0\,\mu\varepsilon$ and $346.4\,\mu\varepsilon$
- $100.0\,\mu\varepsilon$ and $173.2\,\mu\varepsilon$
- $100.0\,\mu\varepsilon$ and $346.4\,\mu\varepsilon$

## Explanation

The strain transformation is $\varepsilon(\theta)=\varepsilon_{x}\cos^{2}\theta+\varepsilon_{y}\sin^{2}\theta+\gamma_{xy}\sin \theta \cos \theta$. Subtracting the readings at $\pm\theta$ gives $\gamma_{xy}=346.4\,\mu\varepsilon$. Their mean eliminates the shear term. Solving for $\varepsilon_{y}$ gives $100.0\,\mu\varepsilon$.
