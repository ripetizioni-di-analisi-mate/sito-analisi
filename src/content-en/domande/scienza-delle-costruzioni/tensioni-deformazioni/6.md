An element in plane stress has $\sigma_x=65\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_y=35\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\tau_{xy}=20\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Find the maximum shear stress magnitude over all planes in three dimensions.

::struttura{poligoni="-1,-1 1,-1 1,1 -1,1" reazioni="1 0,0.55,0,σ_x;-1 0,-0.55,0;0 1,0,0.55,σ_y;0 -1,0,-0.55" frecce="1.13,-0.65,1.13,0.58,τ_xy;-1.13,0.65,-1.13,-0.58;-0.55,1.13,0.58,1.13;0.55,-1.13,-0.58,-1.13" titolo="Plane element with positive normal and shear stresses"}

- $25\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $75\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $37.5\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $50\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The in-plane principal stresses are $50\pm25\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and the third is zero. The maximum shear stress in three dimensions is half the difference between the largest and smallest, namely $\dfrac{75-0}{2}$.
