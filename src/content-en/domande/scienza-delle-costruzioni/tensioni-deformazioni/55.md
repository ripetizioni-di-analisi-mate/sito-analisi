A plate has side lengths $100\,\mathrm{mm}$ along $x$ and $50\,\mathrm{mm}$ along $y$. Strain gauges measure uniform strains $\varepsilon_x=500\cdot10^{-6}$ and $\varepsilon_y=-200\cdot10^{-6}$, with $\gamma_{xy}=0$. Find the elongation of a diagonal joining opposite corners.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 2,0 2,1 0,1" tratteggi="0,0 2,1" quote="0 0,2 0,100 mm,0.4;2 0,2 1,50 mm,0.4" titolo="Rectangular sheet and its diagonal"}

- $0.056\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.040\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.034\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.017\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The diagonal makes an angle $\theta$ with $x$, with $\cos^2\theta=\dfrac{100^2}{12\,500}=\dfrac45$ and $\sin^2\theta=\dfrac15$.

Since $\gamma_{xy}=0$, the longitudinal strain along the diagonal is $\varepsilon_d=\varepsilon_x\cos^2\theta+\varepsilon_y\sin^2\theta=(400-40)\cdot10^{-6}=360\cdot10^{-6}$.

The diagonal has length $\sqrt{12\,500}\simeq111.8\,\mathrm{mm}$.

Its elongation is therefore $360\cdot10^{-6}\cdot111.8\simeq0.040\,\mathrm{mm}$.
