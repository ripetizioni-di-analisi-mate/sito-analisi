The two broad faces of a thin sheet have normal $\pm\mathbf e_z$ and carry no load. Which stress tensor components vanish at a point on either face?

- $\sigma_z$, $\tau_{xz}$, and $\tau_{yz}$
- Only $\sigma_z$
- Only $\tau_{xz}$ and $\tau_{yz}$
- $\sigma_x$, $\sigma_y$, and $\tau_{xy}$

## Explanation

By Cauchy's relation the traction on the face with normal $\mathbf e_z$ is $\boldsymbol\sigma\mathbf e_z$, the third column of the stress tensor.

The face is free, so the traction vanishes.

All three components of the third column are therefore zero, namely $\tau_{xz}$, $\tau_{yz}$, and $\sigma_z$.

Components $\sigma_x$, $\sigma_y$, and $\tau_{xy}$ act on faces with in-plane normals and may be nonzero, as in **plane stress**.
