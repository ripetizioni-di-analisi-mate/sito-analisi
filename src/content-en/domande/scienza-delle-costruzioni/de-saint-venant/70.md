In the solid circular section of a shaft under pure torsion, point $P$ is at a distance $\rho$ from center $O$, with $0<\rho<R$. What is the direction of shear stress at $P$?

- Along radius $OP$ toward the center
- Parallel to the beam axis
- Always parallel to axis $y$
- Perpendicular to radius $OP$ in the plane of the section

## Explanation

In pure torsion a circular section rotates rigidly about the shaft axis, so point $P$ moves perpendicular to $OP$.

Shear strain $\gamma=\rho\vartheta'$ has the same direction as the relative displacement between neighboring sections.

By the elastic constitutive law, shear stress acts along $\gamma$, perpendicular to the radius.

Its moment arm about the axis is therefore $\rho$, as used to calculate $M_t$.
