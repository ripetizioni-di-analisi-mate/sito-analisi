A T-section has a top flange of width $80\,\mathrm{mm}$ and thickness $20\,\mathrm{mm}$ and a web of width $20\,\mathrm{mm}$ and height $80\,\mathrm{mm}$. The vertical shear force has magnitude $20\,\mathrm{kN}$. Find the mean shear stress magnitude along the centroidal chord.

::struttura{poligoni="-40,15 40,15 40,35 -40,35;-10,-65 10,-65 10,15 -10,15" frecce="-50,0,50,0,z;0,-75,0,42,y" quote="-40 35,40 35,80 mm,-16;-10 15,-10 -65,80 mm,14;-10 -65,10 -65,20 mm,8" riferimento=0 titolo="T-section with coordinate origin at the centroid"}

- $14.5\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $3.6\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $9.4\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $18.8\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

Flange and web each have area $1\,600\,\mathrm{mm^2}$, with centroid heights $90\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $40\,\mathrm{mm}$ above the bottom fiber. The section centroid is therefore $65\,\mathrm{mm}$ above the bottom fiber, within the web.

The parallel-axis theorem gives $I_z=\dfrac{80\cdot20^3}{12}+1\,600\cdot25^2+\dfrac{20\cdot80^3}{12}+1\,600\cdot25^2\simeq2.907\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$.

The first moment of the portion below the chord is $S_z=20\cdot65\cdot32.5\,\mathrm{mm^3}=42\,250\,\mathrm{mm^3}$, equal in magnitude to that of the portion above.

The chord width equals the web thickness, so $\overline\tau=\dfrac{20\,000\cdot42\,250}{2.907\cdot10^6\cdot20}\,\mathrm{MPa}\simeq14.5\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
