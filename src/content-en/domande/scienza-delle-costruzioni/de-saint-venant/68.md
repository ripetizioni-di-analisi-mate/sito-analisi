In a solid shaft of radius $20\,\mathrm{mm}$ under uniform torsion, the measured relative rotation is $0.02\,\mathrm{rad}$ between sections spaced $500\,\mathrm{mm}$ apart. Given $G=80\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$, find the shear stress magnitude at the outer surface.

- $128\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $32\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $0.8\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $64\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The twist per unit length is $\vartheta'=\dfrac{0.02}{500}\,\mathrm{rad/mm}=4\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{rad/mm}$.

The surface shear strain is $\gamma=R\vartheta'=20\cdot4\cdot10^{-5}=8\cdot10^{-4}$.

The elastic constitutive law gives $\tau=G\gamma=80\,000\cdot8\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{MPa}=64\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

This calculation uses only rigid cross-section rotation and the elastic constitutive law. The torque is not needed.
