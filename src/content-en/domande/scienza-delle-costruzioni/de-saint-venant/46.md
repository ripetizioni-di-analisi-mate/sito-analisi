A rectangular section has width $100\,\mathrm{mm}$ along $z$ and depth $200\,\mathrm{mm}$ along $y$. The bending moment vector has magnitude $10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and makes an angle $30^\circ$ with the $z$ axis, so $|M_z|=10\cos30^\circ\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and $|M_y|=10\sin30^\circ\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Find the maximum normal stress magnitude.

::struttura{poligoni="-50,-100 50,-100 50,100 -50,100" frecce="-65,0,65,0,z;0,-115,0,115,y" quote="-50 -100,50 -100,100 mm,12;-50 100,-50 -100,200 mm,14" riferimento=0 titolo="Rectangular section under biaxial bending"}

- $15.0\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $33.5\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $19.8\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $28.0\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

Resolve the moment into components $|M_z|=8.66\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and $|M_y|=5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

The section moduli are $W_z=\dfrac{100\cdot200^2}{6}\,\mathrm{mm^3}\simeq666\,667\,\mathrm{mm^3}$ and $W_y=\dfrac{200\cdot100^2}{6}\,\mathrm{mm^3}\simeq333\,333\,\mathrm{mm^3}$.

Stress is affine in $y$ and $z$, so the greatest magnitude occurs at a corner where the contributions have the same sign.

Therefore $|\sigma|_{\max}=\dfrac{8.66\cdot10^6}{666\,667}+\dfrac{5\cdot10^6}{333\,333}\,\mathrm{MPa}=13.0+15.0\,\mathrm{MPa}\simeq28.0\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
