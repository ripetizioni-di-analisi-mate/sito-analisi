A rectangular steel beam of width $30\,\mathrm{mm}$ and depth $60\,\mathrm{mm}$ is in pure bending with $M_z=1.08\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Given $E=200\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$, find the radius of curvature of its axis.

- $10\,\mathrm{m}$
- $100\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1\,000\,\mathrm{m}$
- $50\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

In the derivation of Navier's formula, the coefficient $\kappa$ in the linear strain field is the curvature and equals $\kappa=\dfrac{M_z}{EI_z}$.

The second moment of area is $I_z=\dfrac{30\cdot60^3}{12}\,\mathrm{mm^4}=540\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$.

The radius of curvature is therefore $\dfrac{EI_z}{M_z}=\dfrac{200\,000\cdot540\,000}{1.08\cdot10^6}\,\mathrm{mm}=10^5\,\mathrm{mm}$.

This gives a radius of $100\,\mathrm m$.
