At the interface between two parts of a built-up beam, the longitudinal shear flow is $120\,\mathrm{N/mm}$. Two identical connectors share the load at each connection station. Each connector can transmit $3.6\,\mathrm{kN}$. Neglecting local effects, what is the greatest allowable spacing between stations?

- $60\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $30\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $15\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $120\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

Over a length $s$, the flow transfers a force of $120s$ newtons if $s$ is in millimetres. Both connectors together can transmit $7200\,\mathrm{N}$. Thus $s\le\dfrac{7200}{120}\,\mathrm{mm}=60\,\mathrm{mm}$.
