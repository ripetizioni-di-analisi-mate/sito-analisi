A symmetric I-section of overall depth $200\,\mathrm{mm}$ has flanges of width $100\,\mathrm{mm}$ and thickness $10\,\mathrm{mm}$ and a web of thickness $6\,\mathrm{mm}$. Its second moment of area is $I_z\simeq2.098\cdot10^7\,\mathrm{mm^4}$ and the vertical shear force has magnitude $50\,\mathrm{kN}$. Find the mean shear stress magnitude at the neutral axis.

::struttura{poligoni="-50,-100 50,-100 50,-90 3,-90 3,90 50,90 50,100 -50,100 -50,90 -3,90 -3,-90 -50,-90" frecce="-60,0,60,0,z;0,-110,0,108,y" quote="-50 100,50 100,100 mm,-18;-50 100,-50 -100,200 mm,14" riferimento=0 titolo="I-section"}

- $47.4\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $37.7\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $2.8\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $69.4\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The first moment of the upper half has two contributions. The flange contributes $100\cdot10\cdot95\,\mathrm{mm^3}=95\,000\,\mathrm{mm^3}$ and the upper half of the web, of height $90\,\mathrm{mm}$, contributes $6\cdot90\cdot45\,\mathrm{mm^3}=24\,300\,\mathrm{mm^3}$.

Thus $S_z=119\,300\,\mathrm{mm^3}$.

At the neutral axis the width equals the web thickness, so $\overline\tau=\dfrac{50\,000\cdot119\,300}{2.098\cdot10^7\cdot6}\,\mathrm{MPa}\simeq47.4\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

The value $37.7\,\mathrm{MPa}$ results from omitting the web's contribution to the first moment of area.
