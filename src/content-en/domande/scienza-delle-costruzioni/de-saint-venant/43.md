A simply supported beam of length $4\,\mathrm m$ carries a uniform load $q=6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over the whole span. Its rectangular section has width $100\,\mathrm{mm}$ and depth $200\,\mathrm{mm}$. Find the maximum tensile normal stress.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9" titolo="Simply supported beam under uniform loading"}

- $36\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $9\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $12\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $18\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The maximum bending moment occurs at midspan, equals $\dfrac{qL^2}{8}=\dfrac{6\cdot4^2}{8}\,\mathrm{kN\,m}=12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, and puts the bottom fibers in tension.

The section modulus is $W_z=\dfrac{100\cdot200^2}{6}\,\mathrm{mm^3}\simeq666\,667\,\mathrm{mm^3}$.

Navier's formula gives bottom-fiber stress $\sigma_x=\dfrac{12\cdot10^6}{666\,667}\,\mathrm{MPa}=18\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

The value $36\,\mathrm{MPa}$ corresponds to bending moment $\dfrac{qL^2}{4}$, which differs from that for this simply supported beam.
