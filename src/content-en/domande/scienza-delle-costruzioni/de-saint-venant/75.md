In a single-cell closed section under pure torsion, a wall of thickness $3\,\mathrm{mm}$ has mean shear stress $40\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Find the mean shear stress in another wall of the same cell with thickness $5\,\mathrm{mm}$.

- $24\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $40\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $66.7\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $14.4\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

Equilibrium of a longitudinal wall element makes shear flow $q_t=\overline\tau\,t$ constant around the cell.

In the wall of thickness $3\,\mathrm{mm}$ the shear flow is $40\cdot3\,\mathrm{N/mm}=120\,\mathrm{N/mm}$.

In the wall of thickness $5\,\mathrm{mm}$ the mean shear stress is therefore $\dfrac{120}{5}\,\mathrm{MPa}=24\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
