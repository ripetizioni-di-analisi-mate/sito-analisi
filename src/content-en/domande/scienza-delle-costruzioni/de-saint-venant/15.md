A round bar of diameter $36\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries a uniform tensile stress $140\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and has traction-free lateral faces. Given $E = 70\,\mathrm{GPa}$ and $\nu = 0.3$, find the decrease in diameter in micrometers to the nearest hundredth.

- $21.60\,\mu\mathrm{m}$
- $72.00\,\mu\mathrm{m}$
- $43.20\,\mu\mathrm{m}$
- $10.80\,\mu\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The axial strain is $\dfrac{\sigma}{E}$ and the transverse strain is $-\dfrac{\nu\sigma}{E}$. The diameter decreases by $\dfrac{d\nu\sigma}{E}$. Substituting $E$ in $\mathrm{MPa}$ gives $0.021600\,\mathrm{mm}$, or $21.60\,\mu\mathrm{m}$.
