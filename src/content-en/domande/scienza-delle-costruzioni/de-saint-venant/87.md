In a rectangle under bending and vertical shear, the normal stresses at the extreme fibers are $\pm20\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and the Jourawski shear stress at the centroid is $15\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Find the maximum von Mises equivalent stress over the depth.

- $20.0\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $32.8\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $35.0\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $26.0\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

With $\eta=\dfrac{2y}{h}$ we have $\sigma_x=-20\,\eta$ and $\overline\tau=15(1-\eta^2)$, so $\sigma_{\mathrm{eq}}^2=400\,\eta^2+675(1-\eta^2)^2$.

Let $u=\eta^2\in[0,1]$. The function $400u+675(1-u)^2$ is a convex quadratic in $u$ and therefore attains its maximum at an endpoint of the interval.

At $u=0$ it is $675$ and at $u=1$ it is $400$. Its maximum therefore occurs at the centroid.

Thus $\sigma_{\mathrm{eq},\max}=\sqrt{675}\,\mathrm{MPa}\simeq26.0\,\mathrm{MPa}$, exceeding the extreme-fiber value.
