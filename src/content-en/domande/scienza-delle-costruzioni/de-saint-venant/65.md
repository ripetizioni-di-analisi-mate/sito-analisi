Shaft $ABC$ is fixed at $A$ and carries torque $100\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ at free end $C$. Solid segment $AB$ has diameter $40\,\mathrm{mm}$ and length $500\,\mathrm{mm}$, and solid segment $BC$ has diameter $20\,\mathrm{mm}$ and length $250\,\mathrm{mm}$. Given $G=80\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$, find the rotation of $C$ relative to $A$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;C,7.5,0" poligoni="0,-0.8 5,-0.8 5,0.8 0,0.8;5,-0.4 7.5,-0.4 7.5,0.4 5,0.4" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="A,B,500 mm,1.4;B,C,250 mm,1.4" etichette="2.5,1.2,Ø 40 mm;6.25,0.8,Ø 20 mm;8.3,0.5,M_t" titolo="Stepped shaft fixed at A with a torque at C"}

- $0.0224\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $0.0199\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $0.0037\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $0.0448\,\mathrm{rad}$

## Explanation

Both segments carry the same torque and their relative rotations add.

The polar second moments of area are $J_{AB}=\dfrac{\pi\cdot40^4}{32}\,\mathrm{mm^4}\simeq251\,327\,\mathrm{mm^4}$ and $J_{BC}=\dfrac{\pi\cdot20^4}{32}\,\mathrm{mm^4}\simeq15\,708\,\mathrm{mm^4}$.

This gives
$$
\varphi=\frac{M_t}{G}\left(\frac{L_{AB}}{J_{AB}}+\frac{L_{BC}}{J_{BC}}\right)=\frac{10^5}{8\cdot10^4}\left(0.00199+0.01592\right)\simeq0.0224\,\mathrm{rad}.
$$

The thinner segment accounts for almost all the twist because $J$ increases with the fourth power of diameter.
