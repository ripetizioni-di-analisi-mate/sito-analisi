The wall midline of a single-cell closed section forms an equilateral triangle of side $60\,\mathrm{mm}$. The wall thickness is uniformly $2\,\mathrm{mm}$, the torque is $0.18\sqrt3\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, and $G=80\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Find the twist per unit length.

::struttura{poligoni="-31.7,-18.3 31.7,-18.3 0,36.6" fori="-28.3,-16.3 28.3,-16.3 0,32.7" tratteggi="-30,-17.3 30,-17.3 0,34.6 -30,-17.3" frecce="-42,0,42,0,z;0,-28,0,48,y" quote="-30 -17.3,30 -17.3,60 mm,9" riferimento=0 titolo="Triangular cell with dashed wall midline"}

- $0.072\,\mathrm{rad/m}$
- $0.036\,\mathrm{rad/m}$
- $0.0068\,\mathrm{rad/m}$
- $0.0090\,\mathrm{rad/m}$

## Explanation

The area enclosed by the wall midline is $A_m=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}\cdot60^2\,\mathrm{mm^2}=900\sqrt3\,\mathrm{mm^2}$.

The integral around the perimeter is $\displaystyle\oint\frac{\mathrm ds}{t}=\frac{180}{2}=90$, so Bredt's formula gives $J_t=\dfrac{4A_m^2}{90}=\dfrac{4\cdot2\,430\,000}{90}\,\mathrm{mm^4}=108\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$.

The twist per unit length is $\vartheta'=\dfrac{M_t}{GJ_t}=\dfrac{0.18\sqrt3\cdot10^6}{80\,000\cdot108\,000}\,\mathrm{rad/mm}\simeq3.6\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{rad/mm}$.

Therefore $\vartheta'\simeq0.036\,\mathrm{rad/m}$.
