In a solid circular section of radius $20\,\mathrm{mm}$, the circumferential shear stress has magnitude $\tau=0.5\,\rho$, with $\rho$ in $\mathrm{mm}$ measured from the center and $\tau$ in $\mathrm{MPa}$. What torque does this stress field produce?

- $80\pi\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $40\pi\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $20\pi\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $4\pi\,\mathrm{N\,m}$

## Explanation

Each elementary force $\tau\,\mathrm dA$ is tangent to the circle, so its moment arm about the center is $\rho$.

The resulting torque is therefore $M_t=\displaystyle\int_A 0.5\,\rho^2\,\mathrm dA=0.5\,J$.

For a solid circle $J=\dfrac{\pi R^4}{2}=80\,000\pi\,\mathrm{mm^4}$.

The result is $M_t=40\,000\pi\,\mathrm{N\,mm}=40\pi\,\mathrm{N\,m}\simeq126\,\mathrm{N\,m}$.
