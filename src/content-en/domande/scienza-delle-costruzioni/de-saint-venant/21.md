After cooling, the rectangular cross-section of a rolled bar, of height $h$ and constant width $b$, retains residual normal stresses $\sigma_x=k\left(y^2-\dfrac{h^2}{12}\right)$, with $k$ constant and $y$ measured from the centroid along the height. Which internal actions correspond to this distribution?

- Zero axial force and zero bending moments
- Only an axial force, tensile for $k>0$
- Only a bending moment $M_z$
- An axial force and a bending moment $M_z$

## Explanation

The axial force is $N=b\displaystyle\int_{-\frac h2}^{\frac h2}k\left(y^2-\dfrac{h^2}{12}\right)\mathrm dy$. Since $\displaystyle\int_{-\frac h2}^{\frac h2}y^2\,\mathrm dy=\dfrac{h^3}{12}$, the integral equals $k\left(\dfrac{h^3}{12}-\dfrac{h^3}{12}\right)=0$, so $N=0$. The moment $M_z$ contains the integral of $y\,\sigma_x$, which is an odd function of $y$ over a symmetric interval, therefore $M_z=0$. The stress does not depend on $z$ and the section is symmetric about the $y$ axis, so $M_y=0$ as well. The residual stresses therefore form a **self-equilibrated** system, with zero axial force and zero bending moments.
