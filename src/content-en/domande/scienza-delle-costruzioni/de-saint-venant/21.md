Only normal stresses $\sigma_x=k(y^2-\dfrac{h^2}{12})$ act on a rectangular end section of height $h$ and constant width, with $y$ measured from the centroid. What resultants does this distribution produce?

- Zero axial force and zero resultant moments.
- Only a tensile axial force for $k>0$.
- Only a bending moment about the horizontal axis.
- Only a torque about the beam axis.

## Explanation

The average of $y^2$ between $-\dfrac{h}{2}$ and $\dfrac{h}{2}$ is $\dfrac{h^2}{12}$, so the integral of stress over the area is zero. The moment about the horizontal axis contains an odd function of $y$ and vanishes. Symmetry about the other axis also cancels the second bending moment.
