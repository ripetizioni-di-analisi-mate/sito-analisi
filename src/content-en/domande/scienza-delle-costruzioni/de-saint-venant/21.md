A rectangular section of width $60\,\mathrm{mm}$ along $z$ and depth $40\,\mathrm{mm}$ along $y$ carries normal stress $\sigma_x=0.1\,z$, with $z$ in $\mathrm{mm}$ measured from the centroid and $\sigma_x$ in $\mathrm{MPa}$. Which internal resultants correspond to this field, given $M_y=\displaystyle\int_A z\,\sigma_x\,\mathrm dA$?

::struttura{poligoni="-30,-20 30,-20 30,20 -30,20" frecce="-40,0,40,0,z;0,-28,0,28,y" quote="-30 -20,30 -20,60 mm,7;-30 20,-30 -20,40 mm,8" riferimento=0 titolo="Rectangular section with a stress field linear in z"}

- Only $M_y=72\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- Only $M_z=-72\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $N=7.2\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $M_y=72\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- Only $M_y=-72\,\mathrm{N\,m}$

## Explanation

The axial force is $N=0.1\displaystyle\int_A z\,\mathrm dA=0$ because the $y$ axis passes through the centroid.

The moment $M_z=-0.1\displaystyle\int_A yz\,\mathrm dA$ vanishes because the rectangle is symmetric and its axes are principal.

This leaves $M_y=0.1\,I_y$, with $I_y=\dfrac{40\cdot60^3}{12}\,\mathrm{mm^4}=720\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$.

The stress field therefore corresponds to **only** the moment $M_y=72\,000\,\mathrm{N\,mm}=72\,\mathrm{N\,m}$, positive like the coefficient of the field.
