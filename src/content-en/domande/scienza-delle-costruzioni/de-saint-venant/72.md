Four prismatic members carry the same torque in uniform torsion with free warping. In which one do plane cross sections remain plane, with no warping?

- In the solid rectangular beam
- In the open channel
- In the square tube
- In the circular tube

## Explanation

For a solid or annular circular section, the torsion solution is a rigid rotation of each section in its own plane.

It satisfies the free lateral boundary condition because the stress is tangent to the circular contour.

For the other shapes this same field would produce stress oblique to the boundary. Their solutions therefore require longitudinal displacements, or warping.

Of the choices, only the circular tube retains plane cross sections.
