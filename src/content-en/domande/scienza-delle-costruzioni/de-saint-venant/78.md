An open I-section has two flanges of dimensions $100\,\mathrm{mm}\times8\,\mathrm{mm}$ and a web of dimensions $184\,\mathrm{mm}\times6\,\mathrm{mm}$, measured along the wall midlines. Find the torsion constant using the thin-strip model.

::struttura{poligoni="-50,-96 50,-96 50,-88 3,-88 3,88 50,88 50,96 -50,96 -50,88 -3,88 -3,-88 -50,-88" frecce="-62,0,62,0,z;0,-106,0,110,y" tratteggi="-50,92 50,92;0,-92 0,92;-50,-92 50,-92" quote="-50 96,50 96,100 mm,-22;3 -92,3 92,184 mm,-50" riferimento=0 titolo="Open I-section with dashed wall midline"}

- $1.42\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $3.41\cdot10^4\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $4.74\cdot10^4\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $7.11\cdot10^3\,\mathrm{mm^4}$

## Explanation

For a thin-walled open section $J_t\simeq\displaystyle\sum_i\frac{b_it_i^3}{3}$.

The two flanges contribute $2\cdot\dfrac{100\cdot8^3}{3}\,\mathrm{mm^4}\simeq34\,133\,\mathrm{mm^4}$.

The web contributes $\dfrac{184\cdot6^3}{3}\,\mathrm{mm^4}=13\,248\,\mathrm{mm^4}$.

Their sum is $J_t\simeq47\,381\,\mathrm{mm^4}\simeq4.74\cdot10^4\,\mathrm{mm^4}$. Omitting the factor $\dfrac13$ would triple the result.
