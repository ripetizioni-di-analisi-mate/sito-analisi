A rectangular footing of width $1\,000\,\mathrm{mm}$ and length $1\,200\,\mathrm{mm}$ in the eccentricity direction rests on soil with no tensile capacity. It carries a vertical compressive force of $300\,\mathrm{kN}$ with eccentricity $400\,\mathrm{mm}$ from the centroid along the side of length $1\,200\,\mathrm{mm}$. Assuming linear pressure over the contact area alone, find the maximum pressure.

- $0.75\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $1.00\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $1.50\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The rectangular kern extends $\dfrac{1\,200}{6}=200\,\mathrm{mm}$ from the centroid. Since $400\,\mathrm{mm}>200\,\mathrm{mm}$, assuming full contact would give tension, so contact is partial.

The pressure is triangular, vanishing at the edge of the contact region. Its resultant acts one third of the contact length from the edge of maximum compression.

The resultant must follow the load line a distance $600-400=200\,\mathrm{mm}$ from the edge, so the contact length is $3\cdot200=600\,\mathrm{mm}$.

Equilibrium $\dfrac12\,p_{\max}\cdot1\,000\cdot600=300\,000\,\mathrm N$ gives $p_{\max}=1.00\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
