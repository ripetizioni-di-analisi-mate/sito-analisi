A steel prism of length $1\,000\,\mathrm{mm}$ and square side $40\,\mathrm{mm}$ is in concentric tension at $100\,\mathrm{MPa}$, with a free lateral surface. Given $E=200\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\nu=0.3$, find the increase in volume.

- $800\,\mathrm{mm^3}$
- $320\,\mathrm{mm^3}$
- $560\,\mathrm{mm^3}$
- $1\,280\,\mathrm{mm^3}$

## Explanation

The axial strain is $\varepsilon_x=\dfrac{100}{200\,000}=5\cdot10^{-4}$.

The transverse stresses vanish, so Hooke's law gives $\varepsilon_y=\varepsilon_z=-\nu\varepsilon_x$.

The volumetric strain is therefore $\varepsilon_x+\varepsilon_y+\varepsilon_z=(1-2\nu)\,\varepsilon_x=2\cdot10^{-4}$.

The initial volume is $1.6\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^3}$, so $\Delta V=320\,\mathrm{mm^3}$.
