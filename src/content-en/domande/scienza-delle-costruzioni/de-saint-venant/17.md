Two segments in series carry the same tensile force $P$. Segment $A$ has area $540\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ and allowable stress $110\,\mathrm{MPa}$, while segment $B$ has area $360\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ and allowable stress $190\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Assuming uniform axial stress, find the maximum allowable $P$.

- $68.4\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $59.4\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $63.9\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $39.6\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Multiply each area by its allowable stress to find its force limit. The results are $59.4\,\mathrm{kN}$ for $A$ and $68.4\,\mathrm{kN}$ for $B$. The same force passes through both segments, so the smaller limit, $59.4\,\mathrm{kN}$, governs.
