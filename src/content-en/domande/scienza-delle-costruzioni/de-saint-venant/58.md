A thin-walled circular tube of mean radius $50\,\mathrm{mm}$ and thickness $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries a vertical shear force of magnitude $4\pi\,\mathrm{kN}$. Find the mean shear stress magnitude at the elevation of the center.

- $10\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $13.3\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $40\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $20\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

At the center elevation the chord intersects the wall twice, giving total width $b=2t$.

The upper semi-annulus has first moment $S_z=2R^2t$ because its area $\pi Rt$ has its centroid $\dfrac{2R}{\pi}$ from the center.

With $I_z=\pi R^3t$ the Jourawski shear formula gives $\overline\tau=\dfrac{V\cdot2R^2t}{\pi R^3t\cdot2t}=\dfrac{V}{\pi Rt}$.

Thus $\overline\tau=\dfrac{4\pi\cdot10^3}{\pi\cdot50\cdot4}\,\mathrm{MPa}=20\,\mathrm{MPa}$, twice the overall mean shear stress $\dfrac VA$.
