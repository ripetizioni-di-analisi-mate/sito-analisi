A solid circular shaft of diameter $40\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries a bending moment of magnitude $0.2\pi\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Find the maximum normal stress magnitude.

- $50\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $100\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $200\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $25\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

For a circle of diameter $d$ the second moment of area about a diameter is $I=\dfrac{\pi d^4}{64}$.

The greatest distance from the neutral axis is $\dfrac d2$, so the section modulus is $W=\dfrac{\pi d^3}{32}=2\,000\pi\,\mathrm{mm^3}$.

Navier's flexure formula gives $|\sigma|_{\max}=\dfrac{0.2\pi\cdot10^6}{2\,000\pi}\,\mathrm{MPa}=100\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

The value $50\,\mathrm{MPa}$ comes from incorrectly using $\dfrac{\pi d^3}{16}$, the torsional section modulus.
