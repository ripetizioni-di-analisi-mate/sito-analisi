A solid shaft of diameter $40\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries vertical shear force and torque. At both ends of its horizontal diameter, the mean Jourawski shear stress has magnitude $20\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and the torsional shear stress has magnitude $20\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Both are vertical. Find the largest combined shear stress magnitude at these two points.

- $40\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $20\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $28.3\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $0\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

At the two ends of the horizontal diameter the torsional shear stress is perpendicular to the radius. It is vertical and acts in opposite directions at the two points.

The Jourawski shear stress acts in the same direction at both points, that of the shear force.

Add the vertical components algebraically. The stress is $20+20=40\,\mathrm{MPa}$ at one point and $20-20=0$ at the other.

Its maximum magnitude is therefore $40\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
