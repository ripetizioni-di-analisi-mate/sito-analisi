A solid circular section of diameter $60\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries a vertical shear force of magnitude $27\pi\,\mathrm{kN}$. Find the magnitude of the mean shear stress along the horizontal chord through the center.

- $30\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $45\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $20\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $40\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

On the chord through the center the width is $b=2R$ and the first moment of the upper semicircle is $S_z=\dfrac{\pi R^2}{2}\cdot\dfrac{4R}{3\pi}=\dfrac{2R^3}{3}$.

With $I_z=\dfrac{\pi R^4}{4}$ the Jourawski shear formula gives $\overline\tau=\dfrac{V\cdot\frac{2R^3}{3}}{\frac{\pi R^4}{4}\cdot2R}=\dfrac{4V}{3A}$.

Since $A=900\pi\,\mathrm{mm^2}$, it follows that $\overline\tau=\dfrac{4\cdot27\pi\cdot10^3}{3\cdot900\pi}\,\mathrm{MPa}=40\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
