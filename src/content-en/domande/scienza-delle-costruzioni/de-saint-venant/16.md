Straight member $ABC$ is fixed at $A$. Its cross-sectional areas are $600\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ on $AB$ and $480\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ on $BC$. At $B$ a force of $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts toward $A$, and at $C$ a force of $48\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. Find the mean normal stress in $AB$ to the nearest hundredth, taking tension as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,incastro,180" forze="B,-1.1,0;C,1.1,0" etichette="3.55,0.34,24 kN;5.45,0.34,48 kN" titolo="Bar under two opposing axial forces"}

- $50.00\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $40.00\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $80.00\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $120.00\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

To the right of a cut in $AB$ both forces act in opposite directions. Axial equilibrium gives $N = 48 - 24 = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$, with tension positive. Dividing $24\,000\,\mathrm{N}$ by the area of $AB$ gives $40.00\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
