A rectangular section initially has width $60\,\mathrm{mm}$ and depth $120\,\mathrm{mm}$. It is rotated by $90^\circ$ about the beam's longitudinal axis. For the same bending moment about the horizontal axis, find the ratio of the extreme-fiber stress magnitude after rotation to its initial value. Round to the nearest hundredth.

- $2.00$
- $8.00$
- $4.00$
- $0.50$

## Explanation

The section modulus is $\dfrac{bh^2}{6}$ before rotation and $\dfrac{hb^2}{6}$ afterward. The extreme-fiber stress is inversely proportional to section modulus, so the required ratio is $\dfrac{h}{b} = \dfrac{120}{60} = 2.00$.
