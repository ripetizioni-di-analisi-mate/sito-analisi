A T-section with its flange at the top carries vertical shear. Consider the first moment $S_z(y)$ of the area above the chord at elevation $y$. Where is it largest?

- On the chord at mid-depth
- On the chord at the flange–web junction
- At the top fiber
- On the chord through the centroid

## Explanation

Lowering the chord by $\mathrm dy$ adds a strip of area $b(y)\,\mathrm dy$ at elevation $y$, so $\dfrac{\mathrm dS_z}{\mathrm dy}=-b(y)\,y$.

The derivative vanishes and changes sign at $y=0$, on the centroidal axis.

Above the centroid the added strips have $y>0$ and increase the first moment. Below it, the strips have $y<0$ and reduce it.

The maximum is therefore on the centroidal chord, which for this T-section is away from mid-depth.
