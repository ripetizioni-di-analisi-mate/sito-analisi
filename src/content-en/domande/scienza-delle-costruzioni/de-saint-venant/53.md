A tie has a rectangular section of width $40\,\mathrm{mm}$ along $z$ and depth $60\,\mathrm{mm}$ along $y$. A tensile force $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts through corner $P$. Away from the load application region, find the normal stress at corner $Q$ diagonally opposite $P$.

::struttura{poligoni="-20,-30 20,-30 20,30 -20,30" frecce="-30,0,30,0,z;0,-40,0,40,y" punti="20,30,P;-20,-30,Q" quote="-20 -30,20 -30,40 mm,8;-20 30,-20 -30,60 mm,10" riferimento=0 titolo="Tie cross section with load application point P"}

- $70\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $-50\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $10\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The point of application has $e_y=30\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $e_z=20\,\mathrm{mm}$, so transferring the force to the centroid gives $N=24\,\mathrm{kN}$, $M_z=-Ne_y$, and $M_y=Ne_z$.

With $I_z=\dfrac{Ah^2}{12}$ and $I_y=\dfrac{Ab^2}{12}$ the stress becomes $\sigma_x=\dfrac NA\left(1+\dfrac{12e_yy}{h^2}+\dfrac{12e_zz}{b^2}\right)$.

At corner $Q$, with $y=-30\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $z=-20\,\mathrm{mm}$, each bending term equals $-3$, giving $\sigma_x=\dfrac NA(1-3-3)=-5\,\dfrac NA$.

Since $\dfrac NA=\dfrac{24\,000}{2\,400}\,\mathrm{MPa}=10\,\mathrm{MPa}$, the stress at corner $Q$ is $\sigma_x=-50\,\mathrm{MPa}$, so the corner is in compression.
