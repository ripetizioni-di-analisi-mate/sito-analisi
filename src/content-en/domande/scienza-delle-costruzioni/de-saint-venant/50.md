A solid circular column of diameter $100\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries a compressive force of magnitude $100\pi\,\mathrm{kN}$ whose line of action is $10\,\mathrm{mm}$ from the center. Find the normal stress at the most highly compressed point.

::struttura{cerchi="0,0,50" frecce="-62,0,62,0,z;0,-58,0,62,y" punti="10,0,P" quote="-50 -50,50 -50,Ø 100 mm,8" riferimento=0 titolo="Circular section with load application point P"}

- $-40\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $-72\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $-8\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $-104\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The area is $A=2\,500\pi\,\mathrm{mm^2}$, so the uniform stress contribution is $-\dfrac{100\pi\cdot10^3}{2\,500\pi}\,\mathrm{MPa}=-40\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

Transferring the load to the center gives a bending moment of magnitude $100\pi\cdot10^3\cdot10\,\mathrm{N\,mm}=10^6\pi\,\mathrm{N\,mm}$.

The circular section modulus is $W=\dfrac{\pi d^3}{32}=31\,250\pi\,\mathrm{mm^3}$, so the bending stress magnitude is $32\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

On the side nearest the load the stresses add, giving $\sigma_x=-40-32\,\mathrm{MPa}=-72\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
