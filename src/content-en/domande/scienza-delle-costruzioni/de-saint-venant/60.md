A rectangular box section has outside width $100\,\mathrm{mm}$, outside depth $200\,\mathrm{mm}$, and wall thickness $10\,\mathrm{mm}$. It carries a vertical shear force of magnitude $40\,\mathrm{kN}$. Find the mean shear stress magnitude in the webs at the neutral axis.

::struttura{poligoni="-50,-100 50,-100 50,100 -50,100" fori="-40,-90 40,-90 40,90 -40,90" frecce="-62,0,62,0,z;0,-112,0,114,y" quote="-50 -100,50 -100,100 mm,10;-50 100,-50 -100,200 mm,14" riferimento=0 titolo="Box section with 10 mm thick walls"}

- $2.5\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $16.7\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $12.7\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $25.3\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

Subtract the opening to find the second moment of area, $I_z=\dfrac{100\cdot200^3-80\cdot180^3}{12}\,\mathrm{mm^4}\simeq2.779\cdot10^7\,\mathrm{mm^4}$.

The first moment of the upper half is $S_z=100\cdot100\cdot50-80\cdot90\cdot45\,\mathrm{mm^3}=176\,000\,\mathrm{mm^3}$.

At the neutral axis the chord crosses both webs, so $b=2\cdot10=20\,\mathrm{mm}$.

Therefore $\overline\tau=\dfrac{40\,000\cdot176\,000}{2.779\cdot10^7\cdot20}\,\mathrm{MPa}\simeq12.7\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
