In a solid rectangle of width $b$ and height $h$, shear stress is $\tau(y)=\tau_0(1-\dfrac{4y^2}{h^2})$ with the origin at mid-depth. What fraction of the shear force is carried by the central half of the depth, $-\dfrac{h}{4}\le y\le \dfrac{h}{4}$?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 3,0 3,4 0,4" tratteggi="0,1 3,1;0,3 3,3;0,2 3,2" quote="0 0,0 4,h,-0.8;3 1,3 3,h/2,0.75" titolo="Central half of the section depth"}

- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac{11}{16}$
- $\dfrac{5}{16}$

## Explanation

By symmetry one can integrate from $0$ to $\dfrac{h}{4}$ and from $0$ to $\dfrac{h}{2}$. An antiderivative of $1-\dfrac{4y^2}{h^2}$ is $y-\dfrac{4y^3}{3h^2}$. The ratio is $\dfrac{\dfrac{11h}{48}}{\dfrac{h}{3}}=\dfrac{11}{16}$. The central region carries more than half the shear force.
