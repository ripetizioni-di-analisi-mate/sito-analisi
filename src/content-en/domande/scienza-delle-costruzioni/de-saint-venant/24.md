A section of area $3\,000\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ has $I_z = 4.5 \times 10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$. The extreme fibers are at $y = +35\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $y = -70\,\mathrm{mm}$. It carries $N = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $M_z = 2.4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with $\sigma_x = \dfrac{N}{A} - \dfrac{M_z y}{I_z}$. At what elevation $y$ is the normal stress zero?

- $7.5\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $15\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $-15\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

Setting $\sigma_x = 0$ gives $y = \dfrac{N}{A}\dfrac{I_z}{M_z}$. In units of $\mathrm{N}$ and $\mathrm{mm}$, $\dfrac{N}{A}$ equals $8\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\dfrac{M_z}{I_z}$ equals $0.5333\,\mathrm{MPa/mm}$. Dividing with the appropriate sign gives $y = 15\,\mathrm{mm}$, between the extreme fibers.
