A symmetric I-section of overall depth $300\,\mathrm{mm}$ has flange width $150\,\mathrm{mm}$, flange thickness $12\,\mathrm{mm}$, and web thickness $8\,\mathrm{mm}$, giving $I_z\simeq8.87\cdot10^7\,\mathrm{mm^4}$. The section carries $M_z=60\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and a vertical shear force of magnitude $120\,\mathrm{kN}$. Find the von Mises equivalent stress in the web immediately below the top flange–web junction.

::struttura{poligoni="-75,-150 75,-150 75,-138 4,-138 4,138 75,138 75,150 -75,150 -75,138 -4,138 -4,-138 -75,-138" frecce="-88,0,88,0,z;0,-162,0,165,y" tratteggi="-75,138 75,138" quote="-75 150,75 150,150 mm,-30;-75 150,-75 -150,300 mm,16" riferimento=0 titolo="I-section and chord at the flange–web junction"}

- $101.5\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $93.4\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $120.3\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $141.2\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The junction is at elevation $y=138\,\mathrm{mm}$, so Navier's formula gives $|\sigma_x|=\dfrac{60\cdot10^6\cdot138}{8.87\cdot10^7}\,\mathrm{MPa}\simeq93.3\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

The first moment of the top flange is $S_z=150\cdot12\cdot144\,\mathrm{mm^3}=259\,200\,\mathrm{mm^3}$. Immediately below the junction use the web thickness for the width, giving $|\overline\tau|=\dfrac{120\,000\cdot259\,200}{8.87\cdot10^7\cdot8}\,\mathrm{MPa}\simeq43.8\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

The stresses act at the same point, so $\sigma_{\mathrm{eq}}=\sqrt{93.3^2+3\cdot43.8^2}\,\mathrm{MPa}\simeq120.3\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

This exceeds the extreme-fiber stress $101.5\,\mathrm{MPa}$, where shear stress vanishes. Using the flange width in place of the web thickness would give $93.4\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
