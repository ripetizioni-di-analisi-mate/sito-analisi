A rectangular section of width $80\,\mathrm{mm}$ and depth $120\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries an axial compressive force $P$ with eccentricity $12\,\mathrm{mm}$ toward the top fiber. The greatest compressive stress magnitude is limited to $90\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Find the maximum allowable $P$ to the nearest hundredth.

- $864.00\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $2\,160.00\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $540.00\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $1\,440.00\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The top fiber is the most highly compressed. The stress magnitudes $\dfrac{P}{A}$ and $\dfrac{Pe}{W_z}$ add there. With $A = 9\,600\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ and $W_z = 192\,000\,\mathrm{mm^3}$, solving $P\left(\dfrac{1}{A} + \dfrac{e}{W_z}\right) = 90\,\mathrm{MPa}$ gives $P = 540.00\,\mathrm{kN}$.
