A strip of width $60\,\mathrm{mm}$ and thickness $20\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries a tensile force $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ along the axis of the full strip. A notch removes a width $20\,\mathrm{mm}$ from one side, reducing the load-bearing width to $40\,\mathrm{mm}$. Away from the ends of the notch, find the maximum normal stress in the reduced section.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 130,0 130,60 0,60;130,0 170,0 170,40 130,40;170,0 300,0 300,60 170,60" tratteggi="0,30 300,30" forze="0 30,-40,0,N;300 30,40,0,N" quote="300 0,300 60,60 mm,14" titolo="Strip with a notch on one side, viewed in its own plane"}

- $30\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $75\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $20\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $52.5\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The reduced-section centroid is $20\,\mathrm{mm}$ from the uncut edge and the force line is $30\,\mathrm{mm}$ from it, giving eccentricity $10\,\mathrm{mm}$.

Transferring the load to the centroid gives $N=24\,\mathrm{kN}$ and a bending moment of magnitude $24\,000\cdot10\,\mathrm{N\,mm}=240\,000\,\mathrm{N\,mm}$.

The reduced section has $A=800\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ and $I=\dfrac{20\cdot40^3}{12}\,\mathrm{mm^4}\simeq106\,667\,\mathrm{mm^4}$.

On the side toward which the force is offset, $\sigma_x=30+\dfrac{240\,000\cdot20}{106\,667}\,\mathrm{MPa}=30+45\,\mathrm{MPa}=75\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
