On a rectangular section of width $50\,\mathrm{mm}$ along $z$ and depth $100\,\mathrm{mm}$ along $y$, the measured normal stress is $\sigma_x=10-0.2\,y$, with $y$ in $\mathrm{mm}$ measured from the centroid and $\sigma_x$ in $\mathrm{MPa}$. What bending moment $M_z$ does this field produce under the convention $M_z=-\displaystyle\int_A y\,\sigma_x\,\mathrm dA$?

::struttura{poligoni="-25,-50 25,-50 25,50 -25,50" frecce="-34,0,34,0,z;0,-60,0,60,y" quote="-25 -50,25 -50,50 mm,8;-25 50,-25 -50,100 mm,10" riferimento=0 titolo="Section on which the stress field is measured"}

- $0.83\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-0.83\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $1.67\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $0.42\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The constant term $10\,\mathrm{MPa}$ contributes no bending moment because $\displaystyle\int_A y\,\mathrm dA=0$ about the centroidal axis.

The linear term gives $M_z=-\displaystyle\int_A y\,(-0.2\,y)\,\mathrm dA=0.2\,I_z$.

Since $I_z=\dfrac{50\cdot100^3}{12}\,\mathrm{mm^4}=4.17\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$, it follows that $M_z=8.33\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{N\,mm}\simeq0.83\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

The positive sign agrees with stresses $20\,\mathrm{MPa}$ at the bottom and $0$ at the top. The bottom fibers carry the greatest tensile stress.
