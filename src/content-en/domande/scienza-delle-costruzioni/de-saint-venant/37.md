Two end loads have the same axial resultant $P$. One is concentric, and the other has eccentricity $e \ne 0$. Away from the end the member is prismatic. In the beam model, what additional resultant persists for the eccentric load?

- A second uniform axial stress of magnitude $\dfrac{P}{A}$.
- A transverse shear force of magnitude $P$.
- A bending moment $Pe$.
- A torque of magnitude $Pe$ about the beam axis.

## Explanation

Transferring the eccentric load to the centroid leaves the axial force $P$ and adds a couple of magnitude $Pe$. The force acts parallel to the beam axis, so the couple axis is transverse and produces bending. The two loads therefore have different resultant moments.
