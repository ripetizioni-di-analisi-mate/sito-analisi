A compressive force acts on a rectangular section of width $120\,\mathrm{mm}$ along $z$ and depth $180\,\mathrm{mm}$ along $y$. Its line of action passes through $y=15\,\mathrm{mm}$. Find the largest value of $|e_z|$ for which the normal stress is everywhere less than or equal to zero.

::struttura{poligoni="-60,-90 60,-90 60,90 -60,90" frecce="-74.4,0,74.4,0,z;0,-104.4,0,104.4,y" quote="-60 -90,60 -90,120 mm,14.4;60 -90,60 90,180 mm,28.8" tratteggi="-60,15 60,15" riferimento=0 titolo="Rectangle and elevation of the line of action"}

- $20\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $15\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $10\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $30\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The condition for the force to lie within the kern is $\dfrac{|e_y|}{30\,\mathrm{mm}}+\dfrac{|e_z|}{20\,\mathrm{mm}}\leq1$. The first term is $\dfrac12$, leaving $\dfrac{|e_z|}{20}\leq\dfrac12$.
