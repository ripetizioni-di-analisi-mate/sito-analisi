A positive bending moment compresses the top fiber and puts the bottom fiber in tension. The corresponding section moduli are $180 \times 10^3\,\mathrm{mm^3}$ and $90 \times 10^3\,\mathrm{mm^3}$, and the allowable stress magnitudes are $100\,\mathrm{MPa}$ in compression and $140\,\mathrm{MPa}$ in tension. Find the maximum allowable positive bending moment.

- $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $15.3\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $12.6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The top fiber limits the moment to $\sigma_c W_{\mathrm{upper}} = 18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and the bottom fiber to $\sigma_t W_{\mathrm{lower}} = 12.6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Both limits must be satisfied, so $12.6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ governs.
