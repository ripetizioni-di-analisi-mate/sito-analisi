A solid shaft of diameter $20\,\mathrm{mm}$ and length $400\,\mathrm{mm}$ is restrained against rotation at one end. A rigid lever of length $200\,\mathrm{mm}$ is keyed to the other end perpendicular to the shaft. At its tip a force $100\,\mathrm N$ acts perpendicular to both the lever and the shaft. Given $G=80\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$, find the displacement of the lever tip.

- $0.0064\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $2.55\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $1.27\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.64\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The force on the lever produces shaft torque $M_t=100\cdot200\,\mathrm{N\,mm}=20\,000\,\mathrm{N\,mm}$.

With $J=\dfrac{\pi\cdot20^4}{32}\,\mathrm{mm^4}\simeq15\,708\,\mathrm{mm^4}$ the end rotation is $\varphi=\dfrac{M_tL}{GJ}=\dfrac{20\,000\cdot400}{80\,000\cdot15\,708}\,\mathrm{rad}\simeq6.37\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{rad}$.

The lever rotates rigidly with the section, so its tip moves by $\varphi\cdot200\,\mathrm{mm}\simeq1.27\,\mathrm{mm}$.
