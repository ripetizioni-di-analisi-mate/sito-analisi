An open channel has flanges of thickness $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ and a web of thickness $6\,\mathrm{mm}$. Each wall is thin relative to its length. In uniform torsion with free warping, which wall has the highest maximum shear stress magnitude?

- In the flanges, the thinner walls
- It is equal in all walls
- In the web, the thicker wall
- In the longest wall regardless of its thickness

## Explanation

All walls experience the same twist per unit length $\vartheta'$.

In the thin-strip model the maximum shear stress magnitude in a wall is $G|\vartheta'|t_i=\dfrac{|M_t|\,t_i}{J_t}$.

At a fixed $\vartheta'$ the maximum shear stress is therefore proportional to wall thickness.

Thus the highest value occurs in the web of thickness $6\,\mathrm{mm}$.
