A solid circular shaft carries nonzero bending moment $M_z$ and torque $M_t$. At which points of the section is the von Mises equivalent stress largest?

- At the center of the section
- At the two surface points farthest from the neutral axis
- At every point of the outer surface, with the same value
- At the two surface points lying on the neutral axis

## Explanation

Torsional shear stress has the same magnitude all around the surface because it depends only on $\rho$.

The normal bending stress vanishes on the neutral axis and is greatest in magnitude at the farthest fibers.

Since $\sigma_{\mathrm{eq}}=\sqrt{\sigma_x^2+3\tau^2}$ increases with both stress magnitudes, its maximum occurs at the surface where $|\sigma_x|$ is largest.

The check therefore uses the two surface points farthest from the neutral axis.
