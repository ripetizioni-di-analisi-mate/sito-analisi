For the centroidal principal axes $y$ and $z$, $\dfrac{N}{A} = 4\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $I_z = 7.5 \times 10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$, and $I_y = 3 \times 10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$. The applied moments are $M_z = 3\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and $M_y = 1.2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. With $\sigma_x = \dfrac{N}{A} - \dfrac{M_z y}{I_z} + \dfrac{M_y z}{I_y}$, at what coordinate $z$ does the neutral axis cross elevation $y = 25\,\mathrm{mm}$? Round to the nearest hundredth.

- $25.00\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $-15.00\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $15.00\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $-10.00\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

At the specified elevation the known terms give $\dfrac{N}{A} - \dfrac{M_z y}{I_z} = -6\,\mathrm{MPa}$. The coefficient of $z$ is $\dfrac{M_y}{I_y} = 0.4\,\mathrm{MPa/mm}$. The stress vanishes at $z = 15.00\,\mathrm{mm}$.
