A tube of outer radius $25\,\mathrm{mm}$ and inner radius $15\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries a tensile force $N=20\pi\,\mathrm{kN}$. What is the normal stress in the wall away from the ends?

::struttura{cerchi="0,0,25;0,0,15,foro" frecce="-32.5,0,32.5,0,z;0,-26.25,0,32.5,y" quote="-25 -25,25 -25,Ø 50 mm,5" riferimento=0 titolo="Tube with an inner radius of 15 mm"}

- $50\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $40\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $32\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $200\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

Away from the ends the normal stress is uniform over the annulus, so it equals $\sigma_x=\dfrac{N}{A}$. The area of the annulus is $A=\pi(25^2-15^2)=400\pi\,\mathrm{mm^2}$. Since $N=20\pi\,\mathrm{kN}=20\,000\pi\,\mathrm N$, this gives $\sigma_x=\dfrac{20\,000\pi}{400\pi}\,\mathrm{MPa}=50\,\mathrm{MPa}$. The value $32\,\mathrm{MPa}$ results from using the area of the full disc of radius $25\,\mathrm{mm}$.
