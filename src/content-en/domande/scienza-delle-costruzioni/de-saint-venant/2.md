The tube shown, with inner and outer radii of $15$ and $25\,\mathrm{mm}$, is under centred tension. The stress is uniform away from the ends. What fraction of the total tensile force is carried by the wall between radii $20$ and $25\,\mathrm{mm}$?

::struttura{cerchi="0,0,25;0,0,15,foro" frecce="-32.5,0,32.5,0,z;0,-26.25,0,32.5,y" quote="-25 -25,25 -25,Ø 50 mm,5" riferimento=0 titolo="Tube with an inner radius of 15 mm"}

- $\dfrac{9}{16}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}$
- $\dfrac{5}{16}$
- $\dfrac{4}{5}$

## Explanation

At uniform stress, the force carried by part of the section is proportional to its area. The ratio is $\dfrac{\pi(25^2-20^2)}{\pi(25^2-15^2)}=\dfrac{225}{400}=\dfrac9{16}$. Equal radial thickness does not imply equal area.
