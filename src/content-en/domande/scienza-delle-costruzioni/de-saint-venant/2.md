A tube of outer radius $25\,\mathrm{mm}$ and inner radius $15\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries a tensile force $N=20\pi\,\mathrm{kN}$. Find the normal stress in the wall away from the ends.

::struttura{cerchi="0,0,25;0,0,15,foro" frecce="-32.5,0,32.5,0,z;0,-26.25,0,32.5,y" quote="-25 -25,25 -25,Ø 50 mm,5" riferimento=0 titolo="Tube with an inner radius of 15 mm"}

- $50\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $20\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $32\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $100\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The annular area is $\pi(25^2-15^2)=400\pi\,\mathrm{mm^2}$. Therefore $\sigma_x=\dfrac{20\,000\pi}{400\pi}=50\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
