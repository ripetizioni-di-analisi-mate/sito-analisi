A solid shaft of diameter $30\,\mathrm{mm}$ rotates at $1\,500$ revolutions per minute and transmits power $30\,\mathrm{kW}$. Find the maximum shear stress magnitude.

- $36\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $72\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $3.8\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $18\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The angular velocity is $\omega=\dfrac{2\pi\cdot1\,500}{60}\,\mathrm{rad/s}\simeq157.1\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.

Since the power is $M_t\omega$, the torque is $\dfrac{30\,000}{157.1}\,\mathrm{N\,m}\simeq191\,\mathrm{N\,m}$.

For a solid circle $|\tau|_{\max}=\dfrac{16M_t}{\pi d^3}=\dfrac{16\cdot1.91\cdot10^5}{\pi\cdot27\,000}\,\mathrm{MPa}\simeq36\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

The value $3.8\,\mathrm{MPa}$ results from dividing power by revolutions per minute without converting to $\mathrm{rad/s}$.
