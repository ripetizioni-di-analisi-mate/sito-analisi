A uniform shaft of length $900\,\mathrm{mm}$ is restrained against rotation at ends $A$ and $C$. At section $B$, a distance $300\,\mathrm{mm}$ from $A$, a torque of $900\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ is applied. Find the maximum internal torque magnitude.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,9,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,incastro,180;C,incastro,0" etichette="3,0.6,M_t" quote="A,B,300 mm,0.9;B,C,600 mm,0.9" titolo="Shaft fixed against rotation at both ends with a torque at B"}

- $450\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $300\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $900\,\mathrm{N\,m}$
- $600\,\mathrm{N\,m}$

## Explanation

Equilibrium gives $M_A+M_C=900\,\mathrm{N\,m}$, one equation for two unknowns.

Compatibility requires the same rotation of $B$ when calculated from either side, $\dfrac{M_A\cdot300}{GJ}=\dfrac{M_C\cdot600}{GJ}$.

Thus $M_A=2M_C$, giving $M_C=300\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ and $M_A=600\,\mathrm{N\,m}$.

The largest torque occurs on the short segment $AB$ and equals $600\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ because the stiffer segment carries the greater share.
