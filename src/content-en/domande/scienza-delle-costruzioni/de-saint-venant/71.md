A circular tube with radii $40\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $30\,\mathrm{mm}$ and shear modulus $G=80\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$ must satisfy two limits. Shear stress must not exceed $60\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and twist per unit length must not exceed $0.5^\circ/\mathrm m$. Find the maximum allowable torque.

- $1.92\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $4.12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $5.50\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $0.96\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The polar second moment of area is $J=\dfrac\pi2(40^4-30^4)\,\mathrm{mm^4}=875\,000\pi\,\mathrm{mm^4}\simeq2.75\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$.

At the outside radius the stress limit gives $M_t\le\dfrac{60J}{40}\simeq4.12\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{N\,mm}$.

The twist limit, with $0.5^\circ/\mathrm m=8.73\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{rad/mm}$, gives $M_t\le GJ\vartheta'\simeq1.92\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{N\,mm}$.

Both conditions must hold, so the twist limit governs and $M_t\le1.92\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
