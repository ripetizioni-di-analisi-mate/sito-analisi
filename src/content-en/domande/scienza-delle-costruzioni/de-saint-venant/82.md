A thin strip of thickness $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ is in uniform torsion away from its short edges. On each face the shear stress magnitude is $60\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Find its magnitude a distance $1\,\mathrm{mm}$ from the wall midline.

- $30\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $60\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $15\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $0\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

In the thin-strip model $|\tau(n)|\simeq2G|\vartheta'||n|$, so the magnitude increases linearly with distance $|n|$ from the midline.

The faces lie at $|n|=2\,\mathrm{mm}$, where the magnitude is $60\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

At $|n|=1\,\mathrm{mm}$, half that distance, the magnitude is therefore $30\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

The shear stress vanishes at the midline and acts in opposite directions at the two faces.
