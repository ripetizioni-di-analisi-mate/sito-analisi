A thin-walled closed section with rectangular midline of sides $100\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $50\,\mathrm{mm}$ and uniform thickness $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries concentric tensile force $96\,\mathrm{kN}$ and torque $1.2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Find the von Mises equivalent stress in the walls.

- $110\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $95.4\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $85.4\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $132\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The wall area is approximately its midline perimeter times thickness, $300\cdot4\,\mathrm{mm^2}=1\,200\,\mathrm{mm^2}$, so $\sigma_x=\dfrac{96\,000}{1\,200}\,\mathrm{MPa}=80\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

With $A_m=5\,000\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ Bredt's formula gives $\overline\tau=\dfrac{M_t}{2A_mt}=\dfrac{1.2\cdot10^6}{2\cdot5\,000\cdot4}\,\mathrm{MPa}=30\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

Both stresses are uniform in the walls and thus act together at every point.

Therefore $\sigma_{\mathrm{eq}}=\sqrt{80^2+3\cdot30^2}\,\mathrm{MPa}=\sqrt{9\,100}\,\mathrm{MPa}\simeq95.4\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
