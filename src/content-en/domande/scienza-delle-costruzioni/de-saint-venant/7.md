A rectangular section of width $120\,\mathrm{mm}$ and depth $240\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries a vertical shear force of magnitude $36\,\mathrm{kN}$. Find the magnitude of the width-averaged shear stress at elevation $y=60\,\mathrm{mm}$.

::struttura{poligoni="-60,-120 60,-120 60,120 -60,120" frecce="-74.4,0,74.4,0,z;0,-134.4,0,134.4,y" quote="-60 -120,60 -120,120 mm,14.4;60 -120,60 120,240 mm,28.8" tratteggi="-60,60 60,60" riferimento=0 titolo="Strip at an elevation of 60 mm"}

- $1.875\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $0.9375\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $1.40625\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

For a rectangle use $\tau(y)=\dfrac{3T}{2bh}\left(1-\dfrac{4y^2}{h^2}\right)$. The geometric factor at the specified elevation is $\dfrac34$ and the shear stress on the axis is $1.875\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
