An I-section of overall depth $100\,\mathrm{mm}$ has flanges of width $100\,\mathrm{mm}$ and thickness $12\,\mathrm{mm}$. The web has thickness $8\,\mathrm{mm}$. For a nonzero vertical shear force, find the ratio of the magnitude of the width-averaged shear stress immediately below the top flange–web junction to that immediately above it.

::struttura{poligoni="-50,-50 50,-50 50,-38 4,-38 4,38 50,38 50,50 -50,50 -50,38 -4,38 -4,-38 -50,-38" frecce="-59,0,59,0,z;0,-58,0,56.5,y" quote="-50 50,50 50,100 mm,-18;50 -50,50 50,100 mm,25" tratteggi="-50,38 50,38" riferimento=0 titolo="Top flange–web junction"}

- $12.5$
- $8.33$
- $1$
- $0.08$

## Explanation

At the junction, the first moment of the area above the shear force is continuous. In the Jourawski formula only the local width in the denominator changes, from $100$ to $8\,\mathrm{mm}$.
