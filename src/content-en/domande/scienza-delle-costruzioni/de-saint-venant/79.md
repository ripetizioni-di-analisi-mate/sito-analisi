A thin strip of cross section $100\,\mathrm{mm}\times4\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries torque $10\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ with free warping and $G=80\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Find the twist per unit length.

- $0.0059\,\mathrm{rad/m}$
- $0.020\,\mathrm{rad/m}$
- $0.23\,\mathrm{rad/m}$
- $0.059\,\mathrm{rad/m}$

## Explanation

In the thin-strip model the torsion constant is $J_t=\dfrac{bt^3}{3}=\dfrac{100\cdot4^3}{3}\,\mathrm{mm^4}\simeq2\,133\,\mathrm{mm^4}$.

The twist per unit length is $\vartheta'=\dfrac{M_t}{GJ_t}=\dfrac{10\,000}{80\,000\cdot2\,133}\,\mathrm{rad/mm}\simeq5.9\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{rad/mm}$.

Therefore $\vartheta'\simeq0.059\,\mathrm{rad/m}$.
