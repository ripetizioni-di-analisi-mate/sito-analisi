A rectangular section of area $50\,\mathrm{mm}\times80\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries uniform shear stress $\tau_{xy}=3\,\mathrm{MPa}$ directed along $y$ on the face with positive normal $x$. Find the shear force $T$ using the course convention $V_y=-T$.

- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-1.2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The resultant of the shear stresses along $y$ is $V_y=\displaystyle\int_A\tau_{xy}\,\mathrm dA=3\cdot4\,000\,\mathrm N=12\,\mathrm{kN}$.

The course defines the shear diagram value in terms of that resultant by $V_y=-T$, giving $T=-12\,\mathrm{kN}$.

The value $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the resultant $V_y$, whose sign is opposite to that of the plotted shear force.
