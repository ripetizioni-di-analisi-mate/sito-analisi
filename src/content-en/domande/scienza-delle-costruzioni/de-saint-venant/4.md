A rectangular beam of width $100\,\mathrm{mm}$ and depth $160\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries an axial force $N=-32\,\mathrm{kN}$ and a bending moment $M_z=8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Find the normal stress at the bottom fiber.

::struttura{poligoni="-50,-80 50,-80 50,80 -50,80" frecce="-62,0,62,0,z;0,-92,0,92,y" quote="-50 -80,50 -80,100 mm,12;50 -80,50 80,160 mm,24" riferimento=0 titolo="Cross section under compression and bending"}

- $18.75\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $20.75\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $16.75\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $-20.75\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The uniform compressive stress is $-2\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Bending gives a bottom-fiber stress of $+\dfrac{6\cdot8\cdot10^6}{100\cdot160^2}=18.75\,\mathrm{MPa}$, so their sum is $16.75\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
