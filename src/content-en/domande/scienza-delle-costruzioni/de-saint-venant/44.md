A solid circular steel bar of diameter $40\,\mathrm{mm}$ with $E=200\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$ carries axial force and bending moment $M_z$. Longitudinal strain gauges at the top and bottom of the section read $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{upper}}=-1\cdot10^{-4}$ and $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{lower}}=5\cdot10^{-4}$. Find the axial force $N$.

- $125.7\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-25.1\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $75.4\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $50.3\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Plane sections remain plane, so longitudinal strain varies linearly through the depth, $\varepsilon_x=\varepsilon_0-\kappa y$.

The gauge positions are symmetric about the centroid, so centroidal strain equals the mean of the two readings, $\varepsilon_0=\dfrac{-1\cdot10^{-4}+5\cdot10^{-4}}{2}=2\cdot10^{-4}$.

The term linear in $y$ gives zero resultant because axis $z$ is centroidal, so $N=EA\varepsilon_0$.

With $A=400\pi\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ this gives $N=200\,000\cdot400\pi\cdot2\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm N=16\,000\pi\,\mathrm N\simeq50.3\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension.
