A rectangular section has width $40\,\mathrm{mm}$ along $z$ and depth $60\,\mathrm{mm}$ along $y$. The applied moments are $M_z=1.44\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and $M_y=0.64\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Find $\sigma_x$ at point $P$ with coordinates $y=15\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $z=20\,\mathrm{mm}$. Use $\sigma_x=-\dfrac{M_z y}{I_z}+\dfrac{M_y z}{I_y}$.

::struttura{poligoni="-20,-30 20,-30 20,30 -20,30" frecce="-24.8,0,24.8,0,z;0,-34.8,0,34.8,y" quote="-20 -30,20 -30,40 mm,4.8;20 -30,20 30,60 mm,9.6" punti="20,15,P" riferimento=0 titolo="Point P on the side edge"}

- $-10\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $10\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $50\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $30\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

With the convention $\sigma_x=-\dfrac{M_z y}{I_z}+\dfrac{M_y z}{I_y}$, the contribution of the first moment is $-30\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and that of the second is $40\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Their sum is $10\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
