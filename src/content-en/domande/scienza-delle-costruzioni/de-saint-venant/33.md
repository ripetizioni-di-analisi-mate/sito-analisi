A prismatic steel bar of length $50\,\mathrm{m}$ hangs from its top end and carries only its own weight. Its unit weight is $78.5\,\mathrm{kN/m^3}=7.85\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm{N/mm^3}$ and $E=200\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Find its elongation.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,-5" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,90" quote="A,B,50 m,0.8" titolo="Hanging bar under its own weight"}

- $0.98\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $3.93\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.49\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.25\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

At a distance $s$ from the free end, the axial force equals the weight of the segment below, $N(s)=\gamma A s$.

The local strain is $\varepsilon_x=\dfrac{N}{EA}=\dfrac{\gamma s}{E}$ and varies along the bar.

Integrating from $0$ to $L$ gives
$$
\Delta L=\int_0^L\frac{\gamma s}{E}\,\mathrm ds=\frac{\gamma L^2}{2E}.
$$

With $L=50\,000\,\mathrm{mm}$ the result is $\Delta L=\dfrac{7.85\cdot10^{-5}\cdot2.5\cdot10^9}{4\cdot10^5}\,\mathrm{mm}\simeq0.49\,\mathrm{mm}$, half the value obtained by applying the maximum axial force throughout.
