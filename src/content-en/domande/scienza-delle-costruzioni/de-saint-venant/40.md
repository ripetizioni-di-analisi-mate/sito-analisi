A rectangular steel beam ($E=200\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$) of width $50\,\mathrm{mm}$ and depth $120\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries only bending moment $M_z$. A longitudinal strain gauge at the **bottom** fiber reads $\varepsilon_x=4\cdot10^{-4}$. Find $M_z$, taking bending that puts the bottom fibers in tension as positive.

- $-9.6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $9.6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $4.8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $19.2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The bottom-fiber stress is $\sigma_x=E\varepsilon_x=80\,\mathrm{MPa}$, in tension.

The section modulus is $W_z=\dfrac{50\cdot120^2}{6}\,\mathrm{mm^3}=120\,000\,\mathrm{mm^3}$.

Thus $|M_z|=\sigma_xW_z=9.6\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{N\,mm}$.

The bottom fibers are in tension, so the moment is positive and $M_z=9.6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
