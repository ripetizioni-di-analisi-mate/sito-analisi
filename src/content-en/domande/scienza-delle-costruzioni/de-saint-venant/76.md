A thin-walled circular tube has mean radius $40\,\mathrm{mm}$ and wall thickness $2\,\mathrm{mm}$. Find its torsion constant using Bredt's formula.

- $8.04\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $4.02\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $1.61\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $6.70\cdot10^2\,\mathrm{mm^4}$

## Explanation

The midline encloses area $A_m=\pi R^2$ and the perimeter integral is $\displaystyle\oint\frac{\mathrm ds}{t}=\frac{2\pi R}{t}$.

Bredt's formula therefore gives $J_t=\dfrac{4\pi^2R^4}{2\pi R/t}=2\pi R^3t$.

With $R=40\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $t=2\,\mathrm{mm}$ the result is $J_t=2\pi\cdot64\,000\cdot2\,\mathrm{mm^4}\simeq8.04\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{mm^4}$.

The exact value for an annulus of radii $41\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $39\,\mathrm{mm}$ is $\dfrac\pi2(41^4-39^4)\simeq8.05\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{mm^4}$, nearly identical.
