A rectangle of depth $h$ along $y$ and width $b$ along $z$ carries a compressive force through the point $y=\dfrac h6$, $z=0$, a vertex of the kern. At which points of the section is the normal stress zero?

- At the centroid only
- Along the entire top edge
- Along the entire bottom edge
- At the two bottom corners only

## Explanation

With $N=-P$ and $M_z=P\dfrac h6$ the stress is $\sigma_x=-\dfrac PA-\dfrac{P\,h\,y}{6I_z}$, independent of $z$.

Since $I_z=\dfrac{Ah^2}{12}$, it follows that $\sigma_x=-\dfrac PA\left(1+\dfrac{2y}{h}\right)$.

The stress vanishes for $y=-\dfrac h2$ along the entire bottom edge and is compressive everywhere else.

This kern vertex therefore corresponds to a neutral axis coincident with the opposite edge of the section.
