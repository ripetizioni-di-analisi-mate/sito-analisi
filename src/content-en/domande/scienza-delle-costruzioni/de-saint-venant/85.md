A rectangular section of width $50\,\mathrm{mm}$ and depth $100\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries $M_z=2.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and a vertical shear force of magnitude $20\,\mathrm{kN}$. Find the von Mises equivalent stress on the chord at a distance $25\,\mathrm{mm}$ below the centroid.

- $16.9\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $31.7\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $22.8\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $10.4\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

With $I_z=\dfrac{50\cdot100^3}{12}\,\mathrm{mm^4}\simeq4.17\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$, at $y=-25\,\mathrm{mm}$ Navier's formula gives $\sigma_x=\dfrac{2.5\cdot10^6\cdot25}{4.17\cdot10^6}\,\mathrm{MPa}=15\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

The Jourawski shear formula gives $|\overline\tau|=\dfrac{3V}{2A}\left(1-\dfrac{4y^2}{h^2}\right)=6\cdot0.75\,\mathrm{MPa}=4.5\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

Both stresses act at the same point, so $\sigma_{\mathrm{eq}}=\sqrt{15^2+3\cdot4.5^2}\,\mathrm{MPa}\simeq16.9\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

The value $31.7\,\mathrm{MPa}$ incorrectly combines the maximum $\sigma$ at the extreme fiber with the maximum $\tau$ at the centroid, which occur at different points.
