A circular annulus has outer radius $40\,\mathrm{mm}$ and inner radius $20\,\mathrm{mm}$. An axial compressive force has eccentricity $e$ from the center. Find the largest $e$ for which normal stress is nonpositive everywhere, to the nearest hundredth.

- $6.25\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $25.00\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $13.33\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $12.50\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The least compressive stress is $-\dfrac{P}{A} + \dfrac{PeR}{I}$, where $R$ is the outside radius. Setting this stress to zero gives $e = \dfrac{I}{AR}$. With $I = \dfrac{\pi(R^4 - r^4)}{4}$ and $A = \pi(R^2 - r^2)$ this becomes $\dfrac{R^2 + r^2}{4R}$, with $r = 0$ for a solid section. The limit is $12.50\,\mathrm{mm}$.
