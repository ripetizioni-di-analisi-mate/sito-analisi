A rectangular section has centroidal principal axes $y$ pointing upward and $z$ pointing out of the plane. It carries $M_z>0$ and $M_y>0$, with $\sigma_x=-\dfrac{M_zy}{I_z}+\dfrac{M_yz}{I_y}$. At which corner is the tensile stress largest?

::struttura{poligoni="-30,-45 30,-45 30,45 -30,45" frecce="-42,0,42,0,z;0,-55,0,55,y" punti="30,45,A;-30,45,B;-30,-45,C;30,-45,D" riferimento=0 titolo="Corners of the rectangular section"}

- At corner $A$ with $y>0$ and $z>0$
- At corner $C$ with $y<0$ and $z<0$
- At corner $B$ with $y>0$ and $z<0$
- At corner $D$ with $y<0$ and $z>0$

## Explanation

The term $-\dfrac{M_zy}{I_z}$ is positive for $y<0$ because $M_z>0$ puts the bottom fibers in tension.

The term $\dfrac{M_yz}{I_y}$ is positive for $z>0$ because $M_y>0$.

Stress is affine, so its maximum occurs at a corner. Both contributions are positive and greatest in magnitude at the corner with $y=-\dfrac h2$ and $z=\dfrac b2$.

The maximum tensile stress therefore occurs at corner $D$.
