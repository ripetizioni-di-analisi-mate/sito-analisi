A section of depth $160\,\mathrm{mm}$ is symmetric only about its vertical axis and is in pure bending about $z$. Measured stresses are $-30\,\mathrm{MPa}$ at the top fiber and $50\,\mathrm{MPa}$ at the bottom fiber. How far is the centroid from the bottom fiber?

- $100\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $60\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $80\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $120\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

In pure bending the stress is $\sigma_x=-\dfrac{M_zy}{I_z}$. It vanishes on the centroidal axis and varies linearly with distance from that axis.

Thus the ratio of the extreme-fiber stress magnitudes equals the ratio of their distances from the centroid, $\dfrac{d_{\mathrm{upper}}}{d_{\mathrm{lower}}}=\dfrac{30}{50}$.

Since $d_{\mathrm{upper}}+d_{\mathrm{lower}}=160\,\mathrm{mm}$, the results are $d_{\mathrm{upper}}=60\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $d_{\mathrm{lower}}=100\,\mathrm{mm}$.

The centroid therefore lies $100\,\mathrm{mm}$ from the bottom fiber.
