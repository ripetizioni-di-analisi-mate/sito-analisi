Two timber boards of dimensions $100\,\mathrm{mm}\times50\,\mathrm{mm}$ are stacked to form a section $100\,\mathrm{mm}\times100\,\mathrm{mm}$ and fastened by a single row of nails. The shear force is $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ and each nail can transmit up to $0.9\,\mathrm{kN}$. Find the maximum nail spacing along the beam.

::struttura{poligoni="-50,0 50,0 50,50 -50,50;-50,-50 50,-50 50,0 -50,0" tratteggi="-55,0 55,0" frecce="-62,0,62,0,z;0,-60,0,62,y" quote="-50 -50,50 -50,100 mm,8;50 0,50 50,50 mm,10;50 -50,50 0,50 mm,10" riferimento=0 titolo="Two stacked boards and their contact surface"}

- $45\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $60\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $15\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $30\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

At the interface the shear flow is $q_V=\dfrac{VS_z}{I_z}$, where $S_z$ is the first moment of the upper board's area.

This gives $S_z=100\cdot50\cdot25\,\mathrm{mm^3}=125\,000\,\mathrm{mm^3}$ and $I_z=\dfrac{100\cdot100^3}{12}\,\mathrm{mm^4}\simeq8.33\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$.

Thus $q_V=\dfrac{2\,000\cdot125\,000}{8.33\cdot10^6}\,\mathrm{N/mm}=30\,\mathrm{N/mm}$.

Each nail must transfer the longitudinal force over one nail spacing, so $s\le\dfrac{900}{30}\,\mathrm{mm}=30\,\mathrm{mm}$.
