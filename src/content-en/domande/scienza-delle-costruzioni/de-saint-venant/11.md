In a single-cell closed section, the wall midline is a rectangle with sides $80\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $60\,\mathrm{mm}$. The horizontal and vertical walls have thicknesses $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $2\,\mathrm{mm}$, respectively. For a torque of magnitude $480\,\mathrm{N\,m}$, find the magnitude of the average shear stress in the vertical walls.

::struttura{poligoni="-41,-32 41,-32 41,32 -41,32" fori="-39,-28 39,-28 39,28 -39,28" tratteggi="-40,-30 40,-30 40,30 -40,30 -40,-30" frecce="-49.6,0,49.6,0,z;0,-37.2,0,37.2,y" quote="-40 -30,40 -30,80 mm,9;40 -30,40 30,60 mm,22.4" riferimento=0 titolo="Dimensions measured along the wall midline"}

- $12.5\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $50\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $6.25\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $25\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

Bredt's formula gives shear flow $\dfrac{480\,000}{2\cdot80\cdot60}=50\,\mathrm{N/mm}$. The vertical walls have thickness $2\,\mathrm{mm}$, so $\tau=25\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
