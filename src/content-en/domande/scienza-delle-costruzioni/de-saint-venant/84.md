A solid shaft of diameter $40\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries bending moment $0.06\pi\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and torque $0.16\pi\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Find the von Mises equivalent stress at the most highly stressed surface point.

- $99.3\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $50.0\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $45.8\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $75.5\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The maximum normal bending stress is $\dfrac{32M}{\pi d^3}=\dfrac{32\cdot0.06\pi\cdot10^6}{\pi\cdot64\,000}\,\mathrm{MPa}=30\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

The torsional shear stress at the surface is $\dfrac{16M_t}{\pi d^3}=\dfrac{16\cdot0.16\pi\cdot10^6}{\pi\cdot64\,000}\,\mathrm{MPa}=40\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

The two stresses act together at the surface point farthest from the neutral axis.

Therefore $\sigma_{\mathrm{eq}}=\sqrt{30^2+3\cdot40^2}\,\mathrm{MPa}=\sqrt{5\,700}\,\mathrm{MPa}\simeq75.5\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
