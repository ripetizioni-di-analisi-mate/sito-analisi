A section has nonprincipal centroidal axes $y$, $z$, with $I_z=4\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$, $I_y=2\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$, and $I_{yz}=\displaystyle\int_A yz\,\mathrm dA=1\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$. Only $M_z=7\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and $M_y=0$ act. Writing $\sigma_x=ay+bz$, find the stress at point $y=-10\,\mathrm{mm}$, $z=30\,\mathrm{mm}$.

- $17.5\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $-50\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $50\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $20\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The moment conditions give the system
$$
\begin{cases}
aI_z+bI_{yz}=-M_z,\\
aI_{yz}+bI_y=M_y.
\end{cases}
$$

Dividing by $10^6$ gives $4a+b=-7$ and $a+2b=0$, with $a$ and $b$ in $\mathrm{MPa/mm}$.

The second equation gives $a=-2b$, then $-7b=-7$, leading to $b=1$ and $a=-2$.

At the specified point $\sigma_x=(-2)(-10)+1\cdot30\,\mathrm{MPa}=50\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Neglecting $I_{yz}$ would give $17.5\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
