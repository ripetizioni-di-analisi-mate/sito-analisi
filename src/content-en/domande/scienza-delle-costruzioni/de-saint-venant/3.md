A rectangular beam of width $60\,\mathrm{mm}$ and depth $100\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries only a bending moment $M_z=2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, taken as positive when it puts the bottom fibers in tension. Find the normal stress at the top fiber.

::struttura{poligoni="-30,-50 30,-50 30,50 -30,50" frecce="-37.2,0,37.2,0,z;0,-57.2,0,57.2,y" quote="-30 -50,30 -50,60 mm,7.2;30 -50,30 50,100 mm,14.4" riferimento=0 titolo="Rectangular section in bending"}

- $-20\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $20\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $-10\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $-40\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The section modulus for a rectangle is $W_z=\dfrac{60\cdot100^2}{6}=100\,000\,\mathrm{mm^3}$. The top fiber is in compression, so $\sigma_{\rm sup}=-\dfrac{2\cdot10^6}{100\,000}$.
