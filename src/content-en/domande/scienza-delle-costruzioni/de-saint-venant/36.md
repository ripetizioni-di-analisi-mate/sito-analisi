A strip of width $100\,\mathrm{mm}$ and thickness $10\,\mathrm{mm}$ has a central hole of diameter $20\,\mathrm{mm}$ and carries tensile force $60\,\mathrm{kN}$. Find the nominal mean normal stress on the section through the hole.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 300,0 300,100 0,100" cerchi="150,50,10,foro" forze="0 50,-45,0,N;300 50,45,0,N" quote="300 0,300 100,100 mm,14;140 100,160 100,20 mm,-10" titolo="Perforated strip in tension viewed in its own plane"}

- $60\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $75\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $100\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $50\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

At the cross section through the hole the remaining material width is $100-20=80\,\mathrm{mm}$.

The net area is therefore $80\cdot10=800\,\mathrm{mm^2}$.

The nominal stress is thus $\dfrac{60\,000}{800}\,\mathrm{MPa}=75\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

This is the nominal reference stress. The local stress around the hole is higher and requires a model of the hole geometry.
