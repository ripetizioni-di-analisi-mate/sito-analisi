Two segments of a tie in series have lengths $500\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $400\,\mathrm{mm}$. Their axial rigidities $EA$ are $50\,\mathrm{MN}$ and $80\,\mathrm{MN}$, respectively. What tensile force $P$ is permitted if their total elongation is limited to $0.45\,\mathrm{mm}$? Round to the nearest hundredth.

- $30.00\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $45.00\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $60.00\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $90.00\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Both segments carry the same force and their elongations add. From $\Delta = P\left(\dfrac{L_1}{E_1 A_1} + \dfrac{L_2}{E_2 A_2}\right)$ we obtain $P = \dfrac{0.45}{\dfrac{500}{50\,000} + \dfrac{400}{80\,000}}\,\mathrm{kN}$, equal to $30.00\,\mathrm{kN}$.
