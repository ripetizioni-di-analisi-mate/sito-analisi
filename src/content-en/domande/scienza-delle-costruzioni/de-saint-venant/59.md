An isosceles triangular section of height $h=180\,\mathrm{mm}$ has its apex at the top and its base at the bottom and carries vertical shear. At what distance from the apex is the Jourawski mean shear stress largest?

::struttura{poligoni="-60,-60 60,-60 0,120" tratteggi="-60,0 60,0" frecce="-75,0,75,0,z;0,-72,0,135,y" punti="0,0,G,baricentro" quote="60 -60,60 120,180 mm,14;-60 -60,60 -60,120 mm,10" riferimento=0 titolo="Triangular section with centroid G"}

- $120\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $60\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $90\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $180\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

At a distance $s$ from the apex the chord has width $b(s)=\dfrac{bs}{h}$ and the triangle above it has area $\dfrac{bs^2}{2h}$ with centroid $\dfrac{2s}{3}$ from the apex.

The section centroid is $\dfrac{2h}{3}$ from the apex, so $S_z(s)=\dfrac{bs^2}{2h}\cdot\dfrac23(h-s)$.

The Jourawski formula gives $\overline\tau=\dfrac{VS_z}{I_zb(s)}$, proportional to $s(h-s)$.

The product $s(h-s)$ is largest for $s=\dfrac h2$, placing the maximum $90\,\mathrm{mm}$ from the apex, above the centroid at $120\,\mathrm{mm}$.
