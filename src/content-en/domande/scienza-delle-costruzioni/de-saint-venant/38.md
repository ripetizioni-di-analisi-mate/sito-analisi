A T-section has a top flange of width $60\,\mathrm{mm}$ and thickness $20\,\mathrm{mm}$ and a web of width $20\,\mathrm{mm}$ and height $60\,\mathrm{mm}$. A bending moment $M_z=2.72\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ acts, positive when it puts the bottom fibers in tension. Find the normal stress at the bottom fiber.

::struttura{poligoni="-30,10 30,10 30,30 -30,30;-10,-50 10,-50 10,10 -10,10" frecce="-40,0,40,0,z;0,-60,0,38,y" quote="-30 30,30 30,60 mm,-14;-10 10,-10 -50,60 mm,12;30 10,30 30,20 mm,8;-10 -50,10 -50,20 mm,8" riferimento=0 titolo="T-section with the origin at the centroid"}

- $100\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $60\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $-100\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $80\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The flange and web each have area $1\,200\,\mathrm{mm^2}$. Their centroid heights above the bottom fiber are $70\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $30\,\mathrm{mm}$, giving a section centroid $50\,\mathrm{mm}$ above that fiber.

Using the parallel-axis theorem,
$$
I_z=\frac{60\cdot20^3}{12}+1\,200\cdot20^2+\frac{20\cdot60^3}{12}+1\,200\cdot20^2=1.36\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}.
$$

At the bottom fiber $y=-50\,\mathrm{mm}$, so Navier's flexure formula gives $\sigma_x=-\dfrac{2.72\cdot10^6\cdot(-50)}{1.36\cdot10^6}\,\mathrm{MPa}=100\,\mathrm{MPa}$.

The top fiber lies closer to the neutral axis and carries compressive stress $-60\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
