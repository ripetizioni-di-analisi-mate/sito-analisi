A short column consists of concrete of area $40\,000\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ with $E_c=30\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and reinforcing steel bars of total area $2\,000\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ with $E_s=200\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$. The materials are bonded and share the same axial strain. The column carries a concentric compressive force of $1\,000\,\mathrm{kN}$. Find the stress in the steel.

- $-23.8\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $-18.75\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $-125\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $-500\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

Both materials experience the same strain, so the axial force splits in proportion to their axial rigidities.

The combined axial rigidity is $E_cA_c+E_sA_s=1.2\cdot10^9\,\mathrm N+0.4\cdot10^9\,\mathrm N=1.6\cdot10^9\,\mathrm N$.

The common strain is therefore $\varepsilon_x=-\dfrac{10^6}{1.6\cdot10^9}=-6.25\cdot10^{-4}$.

The stresses are $\sigma_s=E_s\varepsilon_x=-125\,\mathrm{MPa}$ in the steel and $\sigma_c=-18.75\,\mathrm{MPa}$ in the concrete.
