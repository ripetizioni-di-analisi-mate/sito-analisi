The three-hinged portal frame shown has base pins at $A=(0,0)$ and $B=(8,0)$ and an internal hinge $E=(4,4)$ at the crossbeam midpoint, with coordinates in meters. A uniform downward load $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts along the entire crossbeam. Find $H_A$, taking rightward as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,0,4;E,4,4;D,8,4;B,8,0" aste="A-C;C-E;E-D;D-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" cerniere="E" carichi="C,D,0.7,0.7,q" quote="A,B,8 m,0.7;D,B,4 m,-0.8" titolo="Three-hinged portal frame under uniform loading"}

- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $16\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The symmetric load has resultant $16\,\mathrm{kN}$, so $V_A=V_B=8\,\mathrm{kN}$.

Isolate the right half $EDB$. About $E$, the load on $ED$ is $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $x=6\,\mathrm{m}$ and produces moment $-16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Reaction $V_B$ has moment arm $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and moment $32\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Reaction $H_B$ acts $4\,\mathrm{m}$ below $E$ and produces moment $4H_B$.

Moment equilibrium gives $32-16+4H_B=0$, hence $H_B=-4\,\mathrm{kN}$. There are no horizontal loads, so $H_A=-H_B=4\,\mathrm{kN}$. The base pins push the column feet inward.
