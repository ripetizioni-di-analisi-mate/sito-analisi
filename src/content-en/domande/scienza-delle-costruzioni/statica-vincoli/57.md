The sliding surface of a roller rises to the right by $3\,\mathrm{m}$ for every horizontal distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$. Which unit vector gives its positive reaction direction, pushing the body upward away from the surface?

::struttura{nodi="B,0,0" linee="-2,-1.5 2,1.5" vincoli="B,carrello,306.8699" titolo="Roller on an inclined plane"}

- $\left(-\dfrac{3}{5},\dfrac{4}{5}\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac{3}{5},\dfrac{4}{5}\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac{4}{5},\dfrac{3}{5}\right)$
- $\left(-\dfrac{4}{5},\dfrac{3}{5}\right)$

## Explanation

An ideal roller reaction is perpendicular to its sliding surface. The surface direction is $(4,3)$, or $\left(\dfrac{4}{5},\dfrac{3}{5}\right)$ after normalization.

Vector $(-3,4)$ is perpendicular to $(4,3)$ because their dot product is $-12+12=0$. Its vertical component is positive, so it points upward.

Dividing by the magnitude $5$ gives unit vector $\left(-\dfrac{3}{5},\dfrac{4}{5}\right)$. Its horizontal component is negative because the surface rises to the right.
