Two plane rigid bodies $AC$ and $CB$ meet at internal hinge $C$. There are ground pins at $A=(0,\,0)$ and $B=(6,\,0)$. In the initial configuration $C=(3,\,0)$, with coordinates in meters. If the internal hinge moves to elevation $y=2\,\mathrm{m}$ at the same horizontal coordinate, which statement compares the two geometries when treated as distinct structures?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,3,0" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" titolo="Initial configuration with three collinear hinges" cerniere="C"}

- The structure is statically determinate in both configurations.
- Both configurations have an infinitesimal mechanism.
- The first structure is statically determinate and the second is unstable.
- The initial configuration admits an infinitesimal mechanism. In the second configuration, the structure is statically determinate.

## Explanation

When $A$, $C$, and $B$ are collinear, both bodies can instantaneously rotate about their respective ground pins with the same vertical velocity at $C$. When $C$ lies off line $AB$, the two admissible velocity directions at $C$ are distinct, so their velocities can coincide only if both are zero. The six reaction components are then independent for the two bodies.
