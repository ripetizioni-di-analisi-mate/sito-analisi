The horizontal beam shown consists of segments $AC$ and $CB$ connected by internal hinge $C$. There is a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. All three points are collinear. How is the structure classified?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,7,0" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" cerniere="C" titolo="Two segments connected by an internal hinge"}

- A mechanism with one degree of freedom
- Statically determinate
- A mechanism with two degrees of freedom
- Statically indeterminate to degree $1$

## Explanation

The two separate bodies have six degrees of freedom. The pin at $A$ imposes two constraints, the internal hinge two, and the roller one, totaling five.

Fewer constraints than degrees of freedom leave at least one rigid-body motion. If $AC$ rotates through $\theta$ about $A$, point $C$ moves vertically by $3\theta$. Segment $CB$ must follow $C$ while its vertical displacement at $B$ is zero, so its rotation is determined by $\theta$.

Every admissible motion therefore depends on one parameter. The structure has one degree of freedom and forms a mechanism as $C$ moves downward.
