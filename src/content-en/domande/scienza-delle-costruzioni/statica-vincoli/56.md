The horizontal beam $AB$ shown has length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and is pinned to a wall at $A=(0,0)$. Its end $B=(4,0)$ is connected by a straight tie to point $K=(0,3)$ on the wall, with coordinates in meters. A downward load of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ hangs at $B$. Find the tension in the tie.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;K,0,3" aste="A-B" catene="B-K" vincoli="A,cerniera,180;K,cerniera,180" forze="B,0,-1.1,P" quote="A,B,4 m,-0.7;A,K,3 m,-0.8" titolo="Beam pinned to a wall and supported by a tie"}

- $16\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The tie runs from $B$ to $K$ and has length $\sqrt{4^2+3^2}=5\,\mathrm{m}$. Its force on the beam acts from $B$ toward $K$ and has components $-\dfrac{4}{5}T$ and $\dfrac{3}{5}T$.

About $A$ the horizontal component acts along the beam axis with zero moment arm. The vertical component has arm $4\,\mathrm{m}$, giving tie moment $4\cdot\dfrac{3}{5}T=\dfrac{12}{5}T$. The load moment is $-4\cdot12=-48\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

Taking moments about $A$ gives $\dfrac{12}{5}T-48=0$, hence $T=20\,\mathrm{kN}$. The horizontal tie component $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ presses the beam against the wall.
