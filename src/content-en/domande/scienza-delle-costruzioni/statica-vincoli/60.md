A rigid bar $AB$ of length $5\,\mathrm{m}$ has its foot $A=(0,0)$ pinned to the ground and end $B=(3,4)$ against a smooth vertical wall that exerts only a horizontal reaction. Its weight $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at the midpoint. What force does the wall exert on the bar?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,4" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,0" forze="1.5 2,0,-1.1,W" quote="0 0,3 0,3 m,0.7;3 0,B,4 m,0.9" titolo="Bar resting against a smooth wall"}

- $3.75\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right
- $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $3.75\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left

## Explanation

Take moments about $A$, where both pin reaction components have zero moment arm.

The vertical weight acts at $(1.5;\,2)$ with horizontal moment arm $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ and moment $-15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The horizontal wall reaction acts at elevation $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and has moment $-4H_B$.

Moment equilibrium gives $-4H_B-15=0$, or $H_B=-3.75\,\mathrm{kN}$. Thus the wall pushes the bar leftward with $3.75\,\mathrm{kN}$, as a compression-only contact must.
