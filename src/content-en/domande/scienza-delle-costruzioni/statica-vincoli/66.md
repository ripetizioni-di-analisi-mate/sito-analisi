A straight roof slope spans a horizontal distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and rises by $3\,\mathrm{m}$. Snow applies a vertical load of $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ per meter of horizontal projection. Find the resultant snow load on the slope.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,3" aste="A-B" carichi="0 3.4,4 3.4,0.5,0.5,q" quote="0 0,4 0,4 m,0.7;4 0,B,3 m,0.7" titolo="Snow load per unit horizontal projection"}

- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $6.4\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The load is specified per meter of horizontal projection. Multiply by the projected length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, measured horizontally.

The resultant is therefore $2\cdot4=8\,\mathrm{kN}$ vertically downward through the midpoint of the slope.

If the same load intensity referred to the inclined length $\sqrt{4^2+3^2}=5\,\mathrm{m}$, the resultant would be $10\,\mathrm{kN}$. The length used to define the intensity changes the load.
