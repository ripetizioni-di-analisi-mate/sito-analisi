Three plane rigid bars are joined in series by internal hinges, the first to the second and the second to the third. There are no external supports. How many in-plane degrees of freedom does the system have?

::struttura{nodi="P,0,0;Q,3,0;R,5,1.5;S,8,1.5" aste="P-Q;Q-R;R-S" cerniere="Q;R" titolo="Three-member chain with no external supports"}

- $5$
- $7$
- $3$
- $9$

## Explanation

Each free plane rigid body has three degrees of freedom, so the three separate bars have $9$.

An internal hinge requires the connected bodies to have the same displacement at the hinge, imposing two scalar conditions. The two hinges therefore impose $4$ independent constraints.

This leaves $9-4=5$ degrees of freedom, comprising the three rigid-body motions of the entire assembly and the two relative hinge rotations.
