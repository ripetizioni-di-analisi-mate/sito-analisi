Horizontal beam $AB$ is fixed at $A$ and rests on a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$, making it statically indeterminate to degree $1$. Which change in supports makes it statically determinate?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" titolo="Propped cantilever"}

- Replace the roller with a pin
- Replace the fixed support with a pin
- Move the roller to midspan
- Replace the fixed support with a roller on a horizontal surface

## Explanation

A pin at $A$ and a roller at $B$ impose three independent constraints because the roller reaction does not pass through $A$. This makes the beam statically determinate.

Replacing the roller with a pin gives five constraints with rank $3$, increasing the degree of indeterminacy to $2$. Moving the roller leaves four constraints of rank $3$, so the degree remains $1$.

Two rollers on horizontal surfaces would both allow horizontal translation and make the beam unstable.
