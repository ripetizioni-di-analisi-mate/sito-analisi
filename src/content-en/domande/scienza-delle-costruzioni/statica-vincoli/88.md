The frame shown is fixed at $A=(0,0)$. It consists of column $AC$ with $C=(0,3)$, crossbeam $CD$ with $D=(4,3)$, and a hanging column $DE$ with $E=(4,1)$, free at $E$. Coordinates are in meters. A rightward force of $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $E$. Find $M_A$, taking counterclockwise as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,0,3;D,4,3;E,4,1" aste="A-C;C-D;D-E" vincoli="A,incastro" forze="E,1.1,0,F" quote="A,C,3 m,0.8;C,D,4 m,-0.7;D,E,2 m,-0.8" titolo="Frame with a hanging column"}

- $15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $25\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The force is horizontal, so its moment arm about $A$ is the vertical distance from its line of action to $A$. The line of action is $y=1\,\mathrm{m}$.

Its moment about $A$ is $-(1-0)\cdot5=-5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ clockwise, independent of the path of the members between $A$ and $E$.

Moment equilibrium gives $M_A-5=0$, hence $M_A=5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise.
