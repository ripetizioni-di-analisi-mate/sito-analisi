Three bilateral rollers restrain a plane rigid body. Reactions at $A=(0,0)$ and $B=(0,2)$, in meters, are horizontal, and the reaction at $C=(3,0)$ is vertical. How is this support arrangement classified?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,2;C,3,0;D,3,2,-" aste="A-C;C-D;D-B;B-A" vincoli="A,carrello,180;B,carrello,180;C,carrello" titolo="Rigid body on three rollers"}

- Statically determinate
- Unstable because two parallel reactions leave vertical translation unrestrained
- Unstable because the structure can rotate about $A$
- Statically indeterminate to degree $1$

## Explanation

Taking the origin as reference, an infinitesimal rigid-body motion has components $\delta u$, $\delta v$, and $\delta\varphi$. The roller at $A$ imposes $\delta u=0$.

Point $B$ has horizontal displacement $\delta u-2\,\delta\varphi$, so the roller at $B$ imposes $\delta\varphi=0$. Point $C$ has vertical displacement $\delta v+3\,\delta\varphi$, so the roller at $C$ imposes $\delta v=0$.

The three constraints have rank $3$ and their number equals the degrees of freedom. The support arrangement is statically determinate. The two horizontal reactions on distinct lines also restrain rotation.
