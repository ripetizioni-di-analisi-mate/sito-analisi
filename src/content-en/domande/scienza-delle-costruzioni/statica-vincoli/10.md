For the beam shown, the roller normal at $B$ is $\mathbf n=(-\dfrac35,\dfrac45)$. A free-body diagram reports components $(-6,6)\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $B$. Which local check rules them out without finding the reaction at $A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,306.8699" quote="0 0,2 0,2 m;2 0,5 0,3 m" forze="2 0,0.5,-1,P_1" titolo="Inclined roller and load position"}

- The horizontal component must vanish at every roller.
- A roller can provide only a moment.
- The component ratio is $-1$, whereas it must be $-\dfrac34$.
- The reaction magnitude must equal the applied load magnitude.

## Explanation

The reaction is a scalar multiple of the normal. Its component ratio $\dfrac{R_x}{R_y}$ must therefore equal $\dfrac{n_x}{n_y}=-\dfrac34$. Correct signs alone do not verify the required direction.
