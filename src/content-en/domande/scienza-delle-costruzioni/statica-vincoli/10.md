On the horizontal beam shown, the distance from $A$ to $B$ is $5\,\mathrm{m}$. There is a pin support at $A$. The unit normal to the roller surface at $B$ is $\left(- \dfrac{3}{5},\dfrac{4}{5}\right)$. A force with components $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward acts at coordinate $2\,\mathrm{m}$ measured from $A$. What is $H_A$, taking rightward as positive?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,306.8699" quote="0 0,2 0,2 m;2 0,5 0,3 m" forze="2 0,0.5,-1,P_1" titolo="Inclined roller and load position"}

- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-10\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-16\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The roller at $B$ provides a reaction $R_B$ directed along the normal to its plane, with components $-\dfrac{3}{5}R_B$ horizontally and $\dfrac{4}{5}R_B$ vertically.

The horizontal components act along the beam axis, so about $A$ only the vertical component of $R_B$, with lever arm $5\,\mathrm{m}$, and the vertical component of the force, with lever arm $2\,\mathrm{m}$, have a moment.

Moment equilibrium about $A$ therefore gives $5\cdot\dfrac{4}{5}R_B-20\cdot2=0$, so $R_B=10\,\mathrm{kN}$. The horizontal component of the reaction at $B$ is then $-\dfrac{3}{5}\cdot10=-6\,\mathrm{kN}$.

Horizontal equilibrium gives $H_A+10-6=0$, so $H_A=-4\,\mathrm{kN}$, that is $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left.
