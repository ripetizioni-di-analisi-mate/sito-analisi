A rectangular frame of width $6\,\mathrm{m}$ and height $4\,\mathrm{m}$ has a pin at $A=(0,0)$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B=(6,0)$. An impact applies a rightward horizontal force of $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ at the foot of the right column, point $B$. Find the roller's vertical reaction, taking upward as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,0,4;D,6,4;B,6,0" aste="A-C;C-D;D-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="B,1.1,0,F" quote="A,B,6 m,0.7;D,B,4 m,-0.8" titolo="Horizontal force at support elevation"}

- $\dfrac{20}{3}\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-\dfrac{20}{3}\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The force acts along horizontal line $y=0$ through $A$, so its moment about $A$ is zero.

Taking moments about $A$ gives $6V_B=0$, hence $V_B=0$. Vertical equilibrium gives $V_A=0$.

The pin alone balances the force, with $H_A=-10\,\mathrm{kN}$. The frame height does not enter because the moment arm depends on the line of action relative to the reference point.
