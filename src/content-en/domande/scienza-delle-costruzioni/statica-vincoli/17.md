A beam rests on two supports spaced $6\,\mathrm{m}$ apart that can exert only upward vertical reactions. An independent horizontal restraint prevents sliding. A downward load $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts between the supports. The right overhang of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$ carries a downward tip load $P$. At what value of $P$ does the first support reaction become zero?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,8,0" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,carrello;A,pendolo,180;B,carrello" titolo="Limiting load for loss of contact at the first support" forze="8 0,0,-1.35,P" carichi="0 0,6 0,0.8,0.8,4 kN/m,270" quote="A,B,6 m,0.85;B,C,2 m,0.85"}

- $36\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $72\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

At loss of contact with the first support, take moments about the second. The uniform load has resultant $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ and moment arm $3\,\mathrm{m}$. Load $P$ has the opposite moment and moment arm $2\,\mathrm{m}$. This gives $P=36\,\mathrm{kN}$.
