The beam shown has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$, a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Beyond $B$ it extends to internal hinge $C$, a distance $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Span $CD$ of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ rests on a horizontal roller at $D$. A downward force of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at the midpoint of $CD$. Find $V_A$, taking upward as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0;D,10,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" cerniere="C" forze="8 0,0,-1.2,P" quote="A,B,4 m;B,C,2 m;C,8 0,2 m;8 0,D,2 m" titolo="Span CD supported by overhang BC"}

- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Span $CD$ is symmetrically loaded, so the roller at $D$ and hinge $C$ each support it with force $6\,\mathrm{kN}$.

On segment $ABC$ the hinge therefore transmits a downward force of $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$, a distance $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Taking moments about $A$ gives $4V_B-6\cdot6=0$, hence $V_B=9\,\mathrm{kN}$.

Vertical equilibrium of segment $ABC$ gives $V_A+9-6=0$. Thus $V_A=-3\,\mathrm{kN}$. The pin at $A$ holds the beam downward because the load transmitted at $C$ acts on the overhang.
