The portal frame shown has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. A horizontal force of magnitude $P$ is moved along its own line of action from joint $C$ at the top of the left column to joint $D$ at the top of the right column. What happens to the support reactions?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,0,4;D,6,4;B,6,0" aste="A-C;C-D;D-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,1.1,0,P" tratteggi="0,4 6,4" titolo="Horizontal force along the crossbeam axis"}

- $V_A$ and $V_B$ reverse sign
- $H_A$ vanishes and the force is transferred to the roller
- They are unchanged
- Both $V_A$ and $V_B$ vanish

## Explanation

Moving a force along its own line of action preserves both its resultant and its moment about any point because its moment arm is unchanged.

Rigid-body equilibrium depends only on the resultant force and moment of the loads and therefore gives the same reactions.

Thus $H_A=-P$ and the equal and opposite vertical reactions remain unchanged. The latter form the couple needed to balance the applied force moment.
