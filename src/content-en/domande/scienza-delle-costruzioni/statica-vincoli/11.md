Frame $ACD$ is fixed at $A=(0,0)$. The coordinates of $C$ and $D$, in meters, are $(0,3)$ and $(5,3)$. A force with components $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward acts at point $(5,3)$. Find $M_A$, taking counterclockwise moments as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,0,3;D,5,3" aste="A-C;C-D" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="A,C,3 m;C,D,5 m,0.8" forze="5 3,0.5,-1,P_1" titolo="Frame geometry and loading"}

- $40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $28\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-52\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $52\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The vertical force produces a clockwise moment of magnitude $8\cdot5$. The horizontal force also produces a clockwise moment of magnitude $4\cdot3$. Thus $M_A=52\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
