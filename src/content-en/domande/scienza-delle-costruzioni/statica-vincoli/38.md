A straight segment of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$ carries a downward vertical load, with $x$ measured from its left end. Its intensity is $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ for $0\leq x<3\,\mathrm{m}$ and $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ for $3\leq x\leq6\,\mathrm{m}$. At what coordinate does the resultant act?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;E,6,0" aste="O-E" carichi="0 0,3 0,1,1,4 kN/m;3 0,6 0,0.5,0.5,2 kN/m" quote="O,3 0,3 m,0.7;3 0,E,3 m,0.7" titolo="Piecewise uniform load"}

- $x_Q=3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $x_Q=3.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $x_Q=2.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $x_Q=2\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Split the load into two rectangles. Their resultants are $4\cdot3=12\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $x=1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ and $2\cdot3=6\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $x=4.5\,\mathrm{m}$.

The total resultant is $18\,\mathrm{kN}$. Its moment about the origin must equal the sum of the two moments, giving
$$
x_Q=\frac{12\cdot1.5+6\cdot4.5}{18}\,\mathrm{m}=\frac{45}{18}\,\mathrm{m}=2.5\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
The point lies left of midspan because the left half carries the higher load intensity.
