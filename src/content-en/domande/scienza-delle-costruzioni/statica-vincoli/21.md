A Gerber beam has a fixed support at $A$, an internal hinge $C$ a distance $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and a roller with vertical reaction $B$ a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $C$. Beyond $B$ the beam extends a further $2\,\mathrm{m}$. Its only load is a downward force of $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ at the tip of the overhang. Find the vertical force exerted by the right segment on the left at $C$, taking upward as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,7,0;C,3,0;D,9,0" aste="A-C;C-B;B-D" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" titolo="Internal hinge C and loaded overhang" forze="9 0,0,-1.4,18 kN" cerniere="C" quote="A,C,3 m,0.992;C,B,4 m,0.992;B,D,2 m,0.992"}

- $27\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-9\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $18\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

For the right-hand body, moments about $C$ give an upward reaction of $27\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $B$. Vertical equilibrium requires the left body to exert $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward on the right body. The force reverses when the bodies are interchanged, so it acts on the left segment with magnitude $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward.
