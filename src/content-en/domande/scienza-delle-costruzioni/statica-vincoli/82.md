A beam has a pin at $B$ and a support at $A$ that can push only upward. An overhang beyond $B$ carries a tip load. Assuming an upward direction for $V_A$ gives $V_A=-8\,\mathrm{kN}$. Which statement is correct?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;D,7,0" aste="A-B;B-D" vincoli="A,carrello;B,cerniera" forze="D,0,-1.1,P" titolo="Loaded overhang and unilateral support at A"}

- The support exerts $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- The support exerts $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward because the assumed reaction direction can simply be reversed
- The support cannot exert the required force, so the beam lifts off at $A$
- The negative sign indicates a calculation error because a support reaction cannot be negative

## Explanation

The negative sign means equilibrium requires a downward force $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $A$, opposite to the assumed direction.

A bilateral support could exert this force. The support at $A$ can push only upward, so it cannot hold the beam down.

The assumed contact is lost. The beam rotates about $B$ and lifts off at $A$. Maintaining equilibrium requires a tension-capable connection or a load that makes $V_A$ positive.
