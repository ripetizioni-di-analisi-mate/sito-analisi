Horizontal beam $AB$ has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. It carries vertical and horizontal forces. In which equilibrium equation is $V_A$ the only support reaction?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" reazioni="A,0,0.9,V_A;A,0.9,0,H_A;B,0,0.9,V_B" titolo="Simply supported beam and its reactions"}

- Moment equilibrium about $A$
- Moment equilibrium about $B$
- Vertical force equilibrium
- Horizontal force equilibrium

## Explanation

About $B$ reaction $V_B$ has zero moment arm because it acts at $B$. Reaction $H_A$ also has zero moment arm because it is horizontal at the elevation of $B$.

Thus $V_A$ is the only reaction in the moment equation about $B$, with moment arm equal to the span.

Taking moments about $A$ would instead leave $V_B$. Vertical equilibrium includes both $V_A$ and $V_B$, while horizontal equilibrium includes $H_A$.
