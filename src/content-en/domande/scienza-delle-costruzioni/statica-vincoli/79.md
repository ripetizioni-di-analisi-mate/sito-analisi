The $L$-shaped frame shown has a column $AC$ fixed at its base $A=(0,0)$ and of height $4\,\mathrm{m}$. Beam $CD$ of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ is connected to the column by an internal hinge at $C$ and rests at $D$ on a roller on a horizontal surface. The beam carries a uniform downward load $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Find $M_A$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,0,4;D,4,4" aste="A-C;C-D" vincoli="A,incastro;D,carrello" cerniere="C" carichi="C,D,0.7,0.7,q" quote="A,C,4 m,-0.8;C,D,4 m,0.8" titolo="Frame with an internal hinge at the joint"}

- $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $32\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-32\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

Beam $CD$ is supported at $C$ and $D$ and has a symmetric load, so each end carries $\dfrac{4\cdot4}{2}=8\,\mathrm{kN}$.

The column therefore receives a downward force of $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$ and no couple, since the hinge transmits no moment. This force acts along the column axis through $A$.

The transmitted force has zero moment about $A$, so column moment equilibrium gives $M_A=0$.
