On the horizontal beam shown, the distance from $A$ to $B$ is $5\,\mathrm{m}$. There is a pin support at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. The overhang beyond $B$ ends at $D$ and has length $2\,\mathrm{m}$. The supports can resist force in either direction. A downward force of $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at coordinate $7\,\mathrm{m}$ measured from $A$. Find $V_A$, taking upward as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;D,7,0" aste="A-B;B-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="0 0,5 0,5 m;5 0,7 0,2 m" forze="7 0,0,-1,P_1" titolo="Span, overhang, and applied loads"}

- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $28\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-8\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Taking moments about $B$ gives $-5V_A-20\cdot2=0$, hence $V_A=-8\,\mathrm{kN}$. The reaction acts downward.
