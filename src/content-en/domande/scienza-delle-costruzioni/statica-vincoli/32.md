A horizontal pair of parallel links at $A$ prevents a rigid body from translating horizontally or rotating. At $B$, a distance $6\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right of $A$ at the same elevation, a roller reacts only vertically. A rightward force of $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at a height $2\,\mathrm{m}$ above $A$, and a counterclockwise couple of $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is applied. Find the reaction moment at $A$, taking clockwise as positive.

- $-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $28\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The applied loads have zero vertical resultant, so the roller reaction is zero. The horizontal force gives clockwise moment $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and the applied couple gives counterclockwise moment $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Their net moment is $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ clockwise. The support exerts the opposite moment, $-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ under the specified convention.
