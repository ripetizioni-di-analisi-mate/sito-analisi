An inclined force has components $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward and acts directly above the right support of a beam of length $5\,\mathrm{m}$. The right support $B$ is a roller on a horizontal surface, and the left support $A$ is a pin. Find the pin's vertical reaction, taking upward as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="B,0.75,-1,F" quote="A,B,5 m,-0.8" titolo="Inclined force applied above the roller"}

- $0\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

About $A$ the horizontal force component acts along the axis with zero moment arm. The vertical component has moment arm $5\,\mathrm{m}$, the same as $V_B$.

Taking moments about $A$ gives $5V_B-5\cdot4=0$, hence $V_B=4\,\mathrm{kN}$. The roller carries the full vertical force component.

Vertical equilibrium then gives $V_A=4-4=0$. The pin balances only the horizontal component, with $H_A=-3\,\mathrm{kN}$.
