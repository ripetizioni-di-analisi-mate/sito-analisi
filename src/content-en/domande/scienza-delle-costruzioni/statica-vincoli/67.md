On a straight segment, with $x$ in meters, a downward load increases linearly from zero at $x=0$ to $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $x=2\,\mathrm{m}$, then decreases linearly to zero at $x=6\,\mathrm{m}$. At what coordinate does the resultant act?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;P,2,0;E,6,0" aste="O-E" carichi="O,P,0,1.2,q;P,E,1.2,0" quote="O,P,2 m,0.7;P,E,4 m,0.7" titolo="Triangular load peaking inside the interval"}

- $x_Q=\dfrac{8}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $x_Q=2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $x_Q=3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $x_Q=4\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The triangular load diagram has vertex coordinates $0$, $2$, and $6\,\mathrm{m}$. Divide it into two right triangles.

The first increases over $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and has resultant $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ two thirds of the way along, at $x=\dfrac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$. The second decreases over $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and has resultant $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ one third of the way along, at $x=2+\dfrac{4}{3}=\dfrac{10}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$.

Their total resultant is $18\,\mathrm{kN}$, so
$$
x_Q=\frac{6\cdot\frac{4}{3}+12\cdot\frac{10}{3}}{18}\,\mathrm{m}=\frac{8}{3}\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
This equals the mean of the three vertex coordinates, $\dfrac{0+2+6}{3}$.
