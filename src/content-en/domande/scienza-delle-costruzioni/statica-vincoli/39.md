Beam $AB$ of length $10\,\mathrm{m}$ is pinned at $A$ and rests on a roller with vertical reaction at $B$. A uniform downward load of $5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ starts a distance $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ and extends over $4\,\mathrm{m}$ of the beam. At $B$ a clockwise couple of $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is also applied. Find $B_{y}$, taking upward as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,10,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" titolo="Partial uniform load and a couple at end B" carichi="3 0,7 0,1.5,1.5,5 kN/m,270" coppie="10 0,-1,C_0" quote="A,B,10 m,1.42"}

- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The distributed load is equivalent to $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ a distance $5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. About $A$ the applied couple contributes $-20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ with counterclockwise positive. Thus $10B_{y}=120\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and $B_{y}=12\,\mathrm{kN}$.
