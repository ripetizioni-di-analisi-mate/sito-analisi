A trapezoidal load acts between $x=2$ and $x=8\,\mathrm{m}$ on the simply supported $10\,\mathrm{m}$ beam shown. The scales read $10.8\,\mathrm{kN}$ at the left and $13.2\,\mathrm{kN}$ at the right. From these readings, what is the horizontal coordinate of the resultant?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,10,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="0 0,2 0,2 m;2 0,8 0,6 m;8 0,10 0,2 m" carichi="2 0,8 0,0.25,0.75,q" titolo="Distributed load and beam supports"}

- $4.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6.6\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The readings give a total force of $10.8+13.2=24\,\mathrm{kN}$. Its moment about the left support is $13.2\cdot10=132\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The line of action therefore passes $\dfrac{132}{24}=5.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from the left.
