On the horizontal beam shown, the distance from $A$ to $B$ is $10\,\mathrm{m}$. There is a pin support at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. Between coordinates $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $8\,\mathrm{m}$, the intensity of a downward vertical load varies linearly from $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ to $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Find $V_B$, taking upward as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,10,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="0 0,2 0,2 m;2 0,8 0,6 m;8 0,10 0,2 m" carichi="2 0,8 0,0.25,0.75,q" titolo="Distributed load and beam supports"}

- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $13.2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $10.8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $15.6\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Split the load into a rectangle of resultant $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $5\,\mathrm{m}$ and a triangle of resultant $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ a distance $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Thus $10V_B=12\cdot5+12\cdot6$.
