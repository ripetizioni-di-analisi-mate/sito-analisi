On the horizontal beam shown, the distance from $A$ to $B$ is $10\,\mathrm{m}$. There is a pin support at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. Between coordinates $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $8\,\mathrm{m}$, the intensity of a downward vertical load varies linearly from $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ to $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. What is $V_B$, taking upward as positive?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,10,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="0 0,2 0,2 m;2 0,8 0,6 m;8 0,10 0,2 m" carichi="2 0,8 0,0.25,0.75,q" titolo="Distributed load and beam supports"}

- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $13.2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $10.8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $15.6\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The trapezoidal load is split into a rectangle of intensity $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and a triangle growing from $0$ to $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, both between $x=2$ and $x=8\,\mathrm{m}$.

The rectangle has resultant $2\cdot6=12\,\mathrm{kN}$, applied at the middle of the segment, at $x=5\,\mathrm{m}$.

The triangle has resultant $\dfrac{4\cdot6}{2}=12\,\mathrm{kN}$, applied at two thirds of the segment from the side of smaller intensity, at $x=6\,\mathrm{m}$.

Moment equilibrium about $A$ gives $10V_B=12\cdot5+12\cdot6=132\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, so $V_B=13.2\,\mathrm{kN}$.
