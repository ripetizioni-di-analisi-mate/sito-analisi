Horizontal beam $AB$ of length $L$ has a pin at $A$ and a support at $B$ that can push only upward. The only applied load is a concentrated couple. For which sense of the couple is contact maintained at $B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" titolo="Beam with a unilateral support at B"}

- Only for a counterclockwise couple
- For either sense
- Only if the couple acts clockwise
- For neither sense

## Explanation

Let $\mathcal{M}$ be the applied couple, positive counterclockwise. Both pin reaction components have zero moment arm about $A$, so moment equilibrium gives $L\,V_B+\mathcal{M}=0$.

Hence $V_B=-\dfrac{\mathcal{M}}{L}$. The support can exert only $V_B\geq0$, requiring $\mathcal{M}\leq0$.

Contact is maintained only for a clockwise couple. The beam then tends to move downward at $B$ and the support resists it.
