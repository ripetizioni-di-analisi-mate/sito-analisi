Only two vertical forces with distinct lines of action act on a rigid body. A downward force of $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts along the vertical line $x=1\,\mathrm{m}$ and an upward force of $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ along the vertical line $x=5\,\mathrm{m}$. At what horizontal coordinate does their resultant act?

- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $-0.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The resultant is $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. With clockwise positive, the total moment about the origin is $18 \times 1-6 \times 5=-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Dividing by the resultant gives $x=-1\,\mathrm{m}$.
