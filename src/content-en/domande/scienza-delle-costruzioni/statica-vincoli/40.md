The portal frame shown has bases at different elevations. Pin $A$ is at $(0,0)$ and roller $B$ on a horizontal surface is at $(6,2)$, with coordinates in meters. Crossbeam $CD$ is horizontal at elevation $5\,\mathrm{m}$. A rightward force of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $C=(0,5)$. Find $V_B$, taking upward as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,0,5;D,6,5;B,6,2" aste="A-C;C-D;D-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,1.2,0,F" quote="A,C,5 m,-0.8;D,B,3 m,-0.8;C,D,6 m,-0.7" titolo="Portal frame with columns of unequal height"}

- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-10\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The force at $C$ is horizontal with its line of action at elevation $5\,\mathrm{m}$. About $A$ its moment arm is $5\,\mathrm{m}$ and its moment is $-60\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

The roller reaction is vertical, so its moment arm about $A$ is horizontal distance $6\,\mathrm{m}$. The elevation of $B$ does not enter.

Taking moments about $A$ gives $6V_B-60=0$. Thus $V_B=10\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward.
