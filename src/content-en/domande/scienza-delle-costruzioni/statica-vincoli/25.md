Beam $AB$ of length $5\,\mathrm{m}$ is pinned at $A$ and rests on a horizontal roller at $B$. A rigid vertical bracket of height $1\,\mathrm{m}$ is welded at midspan. A rightward horizontal force of $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at the top of the bracket. Find $V_B$, taking upward as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;S,2.5,0,-;E,2.5,1" aste="A-B;S-E" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="E,1.1,0,P" quote="A,S,2.5 m,0.7;S,B,2.5 m,0.7;S,E,1 m,0.6" titolo="Horizontal force applied to a bracket"}

- $0\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $5\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The horizontal line of action lies $1\,\mathrm{m}$ above the beam axis, giving a clockwise moment $-(1)(10)=-10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ about $A$.

Transferring the force to the beam axis gives a horizontal force $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ at midspan plus a clockwise couple $10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ balanced by the vertical support reactions.

Taking moments about $A$ gives $5V_B-10=0$, hence $V_B=2\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. Therefore $V_A=-2\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $H_A=-10\,\mathrm{kN}$.
