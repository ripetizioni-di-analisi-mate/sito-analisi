A cantilever initially in equilibrium under a uniform load receives an additional clockwise $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ tip couple. How do the vertical reaction force and the reaction moment at the fixed end change?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" carichi="A,B,0.7,0.7,q" coppie="B,-1" etichette="4.3,-0.75,6 kN m" titolo="Couple added at the tip of the loaded cantilever"}

- $\Delta V_A=6\,\mathrm{kN}$, $\Delta M_A=0$
- $\Delta V_A=0$, additional clockwise moment of $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $\Delta V_A=0$, additional counterclockwise moment of $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- Neither reaction changes.

## Explanation

A couple adds moment without adding a resultant vertical force. Force equilibrium therefore leaves $V_A$ unchanged. Moment equilibrium requires an increment equal and opposite to the applied couple.
