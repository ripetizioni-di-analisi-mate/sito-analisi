At point $P=(2,5)$, with coordinates in meters, a force has components $F_x=3\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $F_y=4\,\mathrm{kN}$. For which point $O=(x_O,0)$ of the $x$ axis is the force moment zero?

::struttura{nodi="P,2,5" forze="P,0.75,1,F" tratteggi="-2,0 5,0" titolo="Inclined force at P"}

- $x_O=-\dfrac{7}{4}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $x_O=\dfrac{23}{4}\,\mathrm{m}$
- $x_O=2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $x_O=-\dfrac{14}{3}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

About reference point $O=(x_O,0)$ the force moment is
$$
M_O=(2-x_O)\cdot4-(5-0)\cdot3=-7-4x_O .
$$
This moment vanishes for $x_O=-\dfrac{7}{4}\,\mathrm{m}$.

Geometrically the moment vanishes when the reference point lies on the force line. Starting at $P$ and descending by $5\,\mathrm{m}$ along direction $(3,4)$ gives a horizontal displacement $-\dfrac{3}{4}\cdot5=-\dfrac{15}{4}\,\mathrm{m}$, reaching $x=2-\dfrac{15}{4}=-\dfrac{7}{4}\,\mathrm{m}$.
