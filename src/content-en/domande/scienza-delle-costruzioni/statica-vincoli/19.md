A plane rigid body has a pin support at $A=(0,\,0)$ and a bilateral roller at $B=(6,\,3)$, with coordinates in meters. The horizontal roller reaction is parallel to the $x$ axis. Which modification makes the body unstable to first order?

- Turn the roller reaction vertical while keeping $B=(6,\,3)$
- Move the roller to $B=(6,\,0)$ without changing its reaction direction
- Move the roller to $B=(0,\,3)$ without changing its reaction direction
- Move the roller to $B=(7,\,3)$

## Explanation

The pin prevents either translation of $A$. The roller prevents rotation if its line of reaction has a nonzero moment arm about $A$. Placing $B$ at the same elevation as $A$ makes the horizontal reaction pass through $A$, allowing infinitesimal rotation.
