Points $A$ and $B$ on the horizontal beam shown are $8\,\mathrm{m}$ apart. The beam has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. A force with components $7\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward acts at coordinate $3\,\mathrm{m}$ measured from $A$. What is $V_B$, taking upward as positive?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="0 0,3 0,3 m;3 0,8 0,5 m" forze="3 0,0.4375,-1,P_1" titolo="Supports and concentrated loads"}

- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $16\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The moment about $A$ is $8V_B-16\cdot 3$. The line of action of the horizontal component passes through $A$, so equilibrium gives $V_B=6\,\mathrm{kN}$.
