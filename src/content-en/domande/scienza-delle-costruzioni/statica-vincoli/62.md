Horizontal beam $AB$ is pinned at $A=(0,0)$ and rests on a roller on a horizontal surface at $B=(6,0)$, with coordinates in meters. Point $C=(3,0)$ is at midspan. Under general loading, which set of three equilibrium equations determines all three reactions?

- $\sum M_A=0$, $\sum M_B=0$, $\sum F_y=0$
- $\sum M_A=0$, $\sum M_B=0$, $\sum F_x=0$
- $\sum M_A=0$, $\sum M_B=0$, $\sum M_C=0$
- $\sum F_y=0$, $\sum M_A=0$, $\sum M_C=0$

## Explanation

Horizontal reaction $H_A$ acts on axis $x$ and therefore has zero moment about every point of axis $x$. It is also absent from vertical force equilibrium.

Thus any set consisting only of moments about points on the axis and the equation $\sum F_y=0$ leaves $H_A$ undetermined.

The set $\sum M_A=0$, $\sum M_B=0$, and $\sum F_x=0$ instead gives $V_B$ from the first equation, $V_A$ from the second, and $H_A$ from the third. The equations are independent.
