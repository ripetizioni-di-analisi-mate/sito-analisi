The three-hinged arch shown has external pins at $A=(0,0)$ and $B=(6,0)$ and an internal hinge at $C=(2,4)$, with coordinates in meters. Loads act only on member $AC$. What angle does the line of action of the reaction at $B$ make with the horizontal?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,4;B,6,0" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" cerniere="C" forze="1 2,0,-1.1,P" titolo="Three-hinged arch loaded on the left only"}

- $45^{\circ}$
- $63.4^{\circ}$
- $90^{\circ}$
- $33.7^{\circ}$

## Explanation

Segment $CB$ is unloaded except for forces at pins $C$ and $B$. A two-force body can be in equilibrium only if its forces are equal, opposite, and collinear.

Their common line must pass through $B$ and $C$. The reaction at $B$ therefore acts along $BC$. The vector $C-B$ is $(-4,4)$.

The magnitude of the slope is $1$, so the line makes an angle $45^{\circ}$ with the horizontal.
