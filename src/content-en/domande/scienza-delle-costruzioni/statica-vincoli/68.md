A reinforced concrete cantilever of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, fixed at $A$, has a rectangular section $0.3\,\mathrm{m}$ by $0.5\,\mathrm{m}$. The unit weight of the reinforced concrete is $25\,\mathrm{kN/m^3}$. Find the magnitude of the reaction moment $M_A$ due to self-weight alone.

- $15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $60\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

Self-weight per unit length is unit weight multiplied by cross-sectional area, giving
$$
q=25\cdot0.3\cdot0.5=3.75\,\mathrm{kN/m}.
$$
The load is uniform, so the resultant is $3.75\cdot4=15\,\mathrm{kN}$ at a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from the fixed support.

Taking moments about $A$ gives $M_A=15\cdot2=30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
