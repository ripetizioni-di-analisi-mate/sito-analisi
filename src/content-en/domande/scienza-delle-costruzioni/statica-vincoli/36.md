A floor carries a load $5\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$ including its own weight. Its parallel supporting beams are spaced $3\,\mathrm{m}$ on center, giving each a tributary strip of width $3\,\mathrm{m}$. One beam is simply supported over a span $6\,\mathrm{m}$. Find the vertical reaction at either support.

- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $90\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $45\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $22.5\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Each meter of beam carries the floor load on a strip of width $3\,\mathrm{m}$, giving a uniform beam load $q=5\cdot3=15\,\mathrm{kN/m}$.

Over span $6\,\mathrm{m}$ the resultant is $15\cdot6=90\,\mathrm{kN}$ and acts at midspan.

The load and supports are symmetric, so each support carries half the resultant and its reaction is $45\,\mathrm{kN}$.
