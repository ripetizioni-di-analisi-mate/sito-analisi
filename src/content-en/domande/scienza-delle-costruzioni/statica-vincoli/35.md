Beam $AB$ of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. A downward force $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts a distance $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ and an upward force $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts a distance $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Find $V_B$, taking upward as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="1 0,0,-1.1,F_1;3 0,0,1.1,F_2" quote="A,1 0,1 m,-0.7;1 0,3 0,2 m,-0.7;3 0,B,1 m,-0.7" titolo="Two vertical forces in opposite directions"}

- $-2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $7\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

About $A$ the downward force has moment $1\cdot(-10)=-10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and the upward force has moment $3\cdot6=18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

Taking moments about $A$ gives $4V_B-10+18=0$, hence $V_B=-2\,\mathrm{kN}$. The roller pulls downward because the upward load is closer to $B$ and produces the greater moment.

Vertical force equilibrium checks the result, giving $V_A=10-6+2=6\,\mathrm{kN}$.
