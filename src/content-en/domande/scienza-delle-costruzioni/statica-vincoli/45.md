Cantilever $AC$ of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$ is fixed to a wall at $A$. A hinge at its free end $C$ supports a span $CB$ of length $5\,\mathrm{m}$, whose other end rests on a roller on a horizontal surface. A downward point load of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts directly on hinge pin $C$. Find the reaction moment at the wall, taking counterclockwise as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,7,0" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" cerniere="C" forze="C,0,-1.2,P" quote="A,C,2 m,0.7;C,B,5 m,0.7" titolo="Load applied to the hinge pin"}

- $0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $84\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

Isolate span $CB$. The pin transmits only a force at $C$. The span therefore carries the force exchanged at $C$ and reaction $V_B$.

Moment equilibrium of $CB$ about $C$ gives $5V_B=0$, hence $V_B=0$. The span is unloaded and the entire load passes to the cantilever.

On the cantilever the load has moment arm $2\,\mathrm{m}$ about $A$. Thus $M_A-2\cdot12=0$ and $M_A=24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise.
