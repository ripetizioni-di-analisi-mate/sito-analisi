The rigid portal frame $ACDB$ has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. The supports can resist force in either direction. The joint coordinates, in meters, are $A=(0,0)$, $C=(0,3)$, $D=(8,3)$, and $B=(8,0)$. A force of $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right at point $(2,3)$. Find $V_A$, taking upward as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,8,0;C,0,3;D,8,3" aste="A-C;C-D;D-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,C,3 m;C,D,8 m,0.8" forze="2 3,1,0,P_1" titolo="Portal frame with an off-axis load"}

- $-6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-16\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The horizontal force at elevation $3\,\mathrm{m}$ gives a clockwise moment of magnitude $48\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Moments about $A$ give $8V_B=48$ and vertical equilibrium then gives $V_A=-6\,\mathrm{kN}$.
