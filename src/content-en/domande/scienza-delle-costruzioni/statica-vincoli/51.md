A plane rigid body undergoes infinitesimal counterclockwise rotation $\delta\varphi=0.002\,\mathrm{rad}$ about the origin $O$, with no translation of point $O$. Find the displacement components $(\delta x_P,\delta y_P)$ of point $P=(3,4)$, with coordinates in meters.

- $(-8\,\mathrm{mm};\ 6\,\mathrm{mm})$
- $(8\,\mathrm{mm};\ -6\,\mathrm{mm})$
- $(-6\,\mathrm{mm};\ 8\,\mathrm{mm})$
- $(6\,\mathrm{mm};\ 8\,\mathrm{mm})$

## Explanation

An infinitesimal rigid-body motion with $\delta u_O=\delta v_O=0$ satisfies
$$
\delta x_P=-(y_P-y_O)\,\delta\varphi,\qquad \delta y_P=(x_P-x_O)\,\delta\varphi .
$$
Substitution gives $\delta x_P=-4\cdot0.002=-0.008\,\mathrm{m}$ and $\delta y_P=3\cdot0.002=0.006\,\mathrm{m}$.

The displacement is perpendicular to radius $OP$ and its magnitude is $5\cdot0.002=0.01\,\mathrm{m}$, as required by rotation about $O$.
