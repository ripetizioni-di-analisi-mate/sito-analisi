A rigid block rests on the ground without anchors. Contact can supply only compression, and friction prevents sliding. The base width is $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and the weight $30\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts vertically through its center. A horizontal force $H$ acts at height $3\,\mathrm{m}$. Find the largest $H$ for which the block does not overturn.

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0,-;E,2,0,-" poligoni="0,0 2,0 2,3.4 0,3.4" linee="-0.6,0 2.6,0" forze="1 1.7,0,-1.1,W;0 3,1.1,0,H" punti="2,0,S" quote="O,E,2 m,0.6;E,2 3,3 m,0.7" titolo="Unanchored block under a horizontal force"}

- $20\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

At incipient overturning the block rests only on edge $S$ on the side toward which $H$ pushes. The full ground reaction passes through $S$ and has zero moment about $S$.

About $S$ the force $H$ has moment arm $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and tends to overturn the block. The weight, with moment arm $1\,\mathrm{m}$, stabilizes it. Moment equilibrium therefore requires $3H\leq30\cdot1$.

The maximum value is therefore $H=10\,\mathrm{kN}$.
