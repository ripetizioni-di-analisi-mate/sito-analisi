A vertical force of $14\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward at a distance $3\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right of $A$. The structure also carries a counterclockwise couple of $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Move the force to $A$. What couple moment must accompany it, taking counterclockwise as positive?

- $-34\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-42\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $50\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The equivalent system must preserve the force resultant and moment about $A$. Initially the force gives moment $-42\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and the couple contributes $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. After transfer the force passes through $A$, so its moment is zero. The required equivalent couple is their sum $-34\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
