Beam $AB$ of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. Its only load is a counterclockwise couple of $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at midspan. Find $V_A$, taking upward as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" coppie="2 0,1,M" quote="A,2 0,2 m,-0.7;2 0,B,2 m,-0.7" titolo="Beam with a concentrated couple"}

- $-3\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The moment of a couple is independent of the reference point, so its position along the beam does not enter the equilibrium equations.

Taking moments about $A$ gives $4V_B+12=0$, hence $V_B=-3\,\mathrm{kN}$.

The couple has zero resultant force, so vertical equilibrium gives $V_A=-V_B=3\,\mathrm{kN}$. The reactions form a clockwise couple $3\cdot4=12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ that balances the applied couple.
