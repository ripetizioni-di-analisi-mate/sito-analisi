A beam has a pin support at $A$ and a roller at $B$ on a surface inclined to the horizontal. How many scalar reaction unknowns appear on its free-body diagram?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,233.1301" titolo="Pin and inclined roller"}

- $4$
- $2$
- $3$
- $5$

## Explanation

The pin restrains point $A$ in both directions, introducing reactions $H_A$ and $V_A$.

The roller restrains motion only along the normal to its sliding surface. Its reaction is $R_B\boldsymbol n$, with known unit vector $\boldsymbol n$, so its horizontal and vertical components are projections of the single unknown $R_B$.

The three unknowns $H_A$, $V_A$, and $R_B$ match the three equilibrium equations.
