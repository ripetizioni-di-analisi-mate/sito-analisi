A suspended span $C$–$D$ of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$ is joined at its ends to two horizontal cantilevers by internal hinges. The left cantilever has length $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and the right cantilever, fixed at $B$ on its right, has length $3\,\mathrm{m}$. The only load is a downward force $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ on the span at a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $C$. Find the reaction moment at $B$, taking counterclockwise as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,11,0;C,2,0;D,8,0" aste="A-C;C-D;D-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,incastro,0" titolo="Central span suspended between two cantilevers" forze="4 0,0,-2.2,18 kN" cerniere="C;D" quote="A,C,2 m,1.56;C,D,6 m,1.56;D,B,3 m,1.56"}

- $-36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-54\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

Moments of the suspended span about $C$ give $D_{y}=\dfrac{18 \times 2}{6}=6\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. The span therefore applies $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward at the left tip of the right cantilever. The load produces a counterclockwise moment about $B$. The fixed support exerts the opposing moment $-18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
