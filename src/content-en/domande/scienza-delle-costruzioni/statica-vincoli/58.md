A plane force system has resultant $\boldsymbol R=(0,-10)\,\mathrm{kN}$ and moment $M_O=-30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ about the origin. Find its moment about point $B=(5,0)$, with coordinates in meters, taking counterclockwise as positive.

- $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-80\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

On moving the reference point from $O$ to $B$, the moment transforms according to
$$
M_B=M_O-(x_B-x_O)R_y+(y_B-y_O)R_x .
$$
Substitution gives $M_B=-30-5\cdot(-10)+0=20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

The same result follows by replacing the system with $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward at $x=3\,\mathrm{m}$, since $-10\cdot3=-30$. This force is $2\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left of $B$ and produces a counterclockwise moment $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ about $B$.
