A rigid body carries a downward force $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $(2,0)$ and a rightward force $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $(0,3)$, with coordinates in meters. Where does the resultant line of action intersect the $x$ axis?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0,-;P,2,0;Q,0,3" forze="P,0,-1.1,F_1;Q,1.1,0,F_2" tratteggi="-1,0 6,0" quote="O,P,2 m,0.7;O,Q,3 m,-0.7" titolo="Two forces to be replaced by a resultant"}

- $x=3.8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $x=2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $x=0.2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $x=-3.8\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The resultant is $\boldsymbol R=(6,-10)\,\mathrm{kN}$. The moment of the system about the origin is
$$
M_O=2\cdot(-10)-3\cdot6=-38\,\mathrm{kN\,m}.
$$
If its line of action passes through $(x,0)$, the resultant moment about the origin is $x\,R_y-0\cdot R_x=-10x$.

Equivalence requires $-10x=-38$, giving $x=3.8\,\mathrm{m}$. Because the horizontal force acts above the axis, the resultant line lies to the right of the vertical force.
