Two vertical forces of magnitude $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ are the only loads on a rigid body. The first acts upward through $(1,0)$ and the second downward through $(4,0)$, with coordinates in meters. Find their resultant moment about point $(10,0)$, taking counterclockwise as positive.

- $15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-25\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

Equal and opposite forces on distinct lines form a couple. Its moment is independent of reference point.

About the origin the upward force gives $1\cdot5=5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and the downward force gives $4\cdot(-5)=-20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, so the couple moment is $-15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

The same value is obtained about $(10,0)$ because $(1-10)\cdot5+(4-10)\cdot(-5)=-45+30=-15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. It acts clockwise.
