On the horizontal beam shown, the distance from $A$ to $B$ is $9\,\mathrm{m}$. The beam is fixed at $A$, with an internal hinge at $C$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. Segment $AC$ has length $5\,\mathrm{m}$. A downward force of $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at coordinate $8\,\mathrm{m}$ measured from $A$. Find $V_B$, taking upward as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,9,0;C,5,0" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" quote="0 0,5 0,5 m;5 0,8 0,3 m;8 0,9 0,1 m" cerniere="C" forze="8 0,0,-1,P_1" titolo="Internal hinge and span dimensions"}

- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $16\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

On segment $CB$ the moment arms about hinge $C$ are $4\,\mathrm{m}$ for $V_B$ and $3\,\mathrm{m}$ for the load. Thus $4V_B=16\cdot3$.
