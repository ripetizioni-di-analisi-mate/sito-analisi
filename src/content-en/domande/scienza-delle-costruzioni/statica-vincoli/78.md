The Gerber beam shown has a fixed support at $A$, an internal hinge at $C$ a distance $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$ a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $C$. A counterclockwise couple of $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is applied on segment $CB$ at the section adjacent to hinge $C$. Find $V_B$, taking upward as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,7,0" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" cerniere="C" coppie="3.4 0,1,M" quote="A,C,3 m,-0.7;C,B,4 m,-0.7" titolo="Couple on segment CB adjacent to the hinge"}

- $-2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-\dfrac{8}{7}\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The couple acts on segment $CB$ and is an external load in its equilibrium equations. The hinge transmits only forces.

The hinge force components have zero moment arm about $C$. Moment equilibrium of segment $CB$ therefore gives $4V_B+8=0$.

Thus $V_B=-2\,\mathrm{kN}$. The roller holds the beam downward.
