A uniform downward load of intensity $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ must be statically equivalent to a downward force $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ along line $x=5\,\mathrm{m}$. Over which interval must the load act?

- From $x=5\,\mathrm{m}$ to $x=8\,\mathrm{m}$
- From $x=2\,\mathrm{m}$ to $x=5\,\mathrm{m}$
- From $x=3.5\,\mathrm{m}$ to $x=6.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- From $x=2\,\mathrm{m}$ to $x=8\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Two load systems are statically equivalent if they have the same resultant force and moment about a point.

The uniform load has resultant $4\ell$. The condition $4\ell=12$ therefore gives length $\ell=3\,\mathrm{m}$.

The resultant passes through the midpoint of the loaded interval. Center that interval on $x=5\,\mathrm{m}$ so it extends from $x=3.5\,\mathrm{m}$ to $x=6.5\,\mathrm{m}$.
