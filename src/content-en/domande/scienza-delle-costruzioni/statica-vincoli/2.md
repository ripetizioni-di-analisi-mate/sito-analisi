On the horizontal beam shown, the distance from $A$ to $B$ is $8\,\mathrm{m}$. There is a pin support at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. A downward force of $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at coordinate $2\,\mathrm{m}$ measured from $A$. Between coordinates $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and $8\,\mathrm{m}$, a downward load has uniform intensity $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Find $V_B$, taking upward as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="0 0,2 0,2 m;2 0,4 0,2 m;4 0,8 0,4 m" forze="2 0,0,-1,P_1" carichi="4 0,8 0,0.75,0.75,q" titolo="Distributed load and beam supports"}

- $9.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $5.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $6.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $8.5\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The uniform load has resultant $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ acting a distance $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Thus $8V_B=10\cdot2+8\cdot6$ and $V_B=8.5\,\mathrm{kN}$.
