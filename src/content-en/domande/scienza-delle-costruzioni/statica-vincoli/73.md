A plane rigid body has two bilateral rollers, one with a vertical reaction at $A=(0,0)$ and one with a horizontal reaction at $B=(4,2)$, with coordinates in meters. About which point can it rotate infinitesimally while satisfying the restraints?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,2" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello;B,carrello,0" titolo="Two rollers with orthogonal reactions"}

- $(4,0)$
- $(0,2)$
- $(2,1)$
- $(0,0)$

## Explanation

During an infinitesimal rotation about $O$, the displacement of each point is perpendicular to its radius from $O$. A roller's constraint is satisfied if its point displacement is perpendicular to the guide normal, so its radius must be parallel to the reaction.

The center therefore lies on the line of the reaction at $A$, the vertical $x=0$, and on the line of the reaction at $B$, the horizontal $y=2\,\mathrm{m}$.

The lines intersect at $(0,2)$, about which the unstable body can rotate.
