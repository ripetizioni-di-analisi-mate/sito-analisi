Vertical gate $AB$ of height $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and width $1\,\mathrm{m}$ is pinned at its foot $A=(0,0)$ and restrained at its top $B=(0,3)$ by a roller with horizontal reaction. Water on its left reaches the top. The pressure produces a rightward load increasing linearly from zero at $B$ to $30\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $A$. Find the magnitude of the roller reaction at $B$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,0" carichi="B,A,0,0.9,q,0" quote="A,B,3 m,0.8" titolo="Gate under water pressure"}

- $22.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $30\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $45\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The triangular load has a horizontal rightward resultant $\dfrac{30\cdot3}{2}=45\,\mathrm{kN}$.

The load triangle is highest at $A$, so the resultant acts one third of the height from $A$, at elevation $1\,\mathrm{m}$. Its moment about $A$ is $-1\cdot45=-45\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

Roller reaction $H_B$ acts at elevation $3\,\mathrm{m}$ with moment $-3H_B$. Taking moments about $A$ gives $-3H_B-45=0$, hence $H_B=-15\,\mathrm{kN}$. The roller pushes the gate top to the left with $15\,\mathrm{kN}$.
