The straight beam $AB$ shown is inclined, with $A=(0,0)$ and $B=(4,3)$ in meters. It has a pin at $A$ and a bilateral roller at $B$ whose sliding surface is parallel to the beam axis, so its reaction is perpendicular to the beam. A downward force of $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at the midpoint. Find the magnitude of the roller reaction.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,3" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,306.8699" forze="2 1.5,0,-1.1,P" quote="0 0,4 0,4 m,0.7;4 0,4 3,3 m,0.6" titolo="Inclined beam with roller surface parallel to its axis"}

- $5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $3.2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The beam length is $\sqrt{4^2+3^2}=5\,\mathrm{m}$. The roller reaction is perpendicular to the beam, so its moment arm about $A$ is the distance $AB=5\,\mathrm{m}$.

The vertical force acts at point $(2;\,1.5)$, so its moment arm about $A$ is the horizontal distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and its moment is $-20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

Taking moments about $A$ gives $5R_B-20=0$. Thus $R_B=4\,\mathrm{kN}$ and its vertical component is $4\cdot\dfrac{4}{5}=3.2\,\mathrm{kN}$.
