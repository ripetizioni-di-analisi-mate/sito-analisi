On the horizontal beam shown, the distance from $A$ to $B$ is $4\,\mathrm{m}$. There is a pin support at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. The overhang beyond $B$ ends at $D$ and has length $3\,\mathrm{m}$. The supports can resist force in either direction. A downward force of $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at coordinate $1\,\mathrm{m}$ measured from $A$. Between coordinates $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and $7\,\mathrm{m}$, a downward load has uniform intensity $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. What is $V_B$, taking upward as positive?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;D,7,0" aste="A-B;B-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="0 0,1 0,1 m;1 0,4 0,3 m;4 0,7 0,3 m" forze="1 0,0,-1,P_1" carichi="4 0,7 0,0.75,0.75,q" titolo="Span, overhang, and applied loads"}

- $18.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $20.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $12.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $9.5\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The uniform load on the overhang has resultant $4\cdot3=12\,\mathrm{kN}$, applied at the middle of the overhang, that is $5.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$.

The concentrated force of $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ has lever arm $1\,\mathrm{m}$ about $A$.

Both loads produce a clockwise moment about $A$ and $V_B$ produces a counterclockwise moment, so moment equilibrium gives $4V_B-8\cdot1-12\cdot5.5=0$.

Therefore $V_B=\dfrac{74}{4}\,\mathrm{kN}=18.5\,\mathrm{kN}$.
