The beam shown carries a downward $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ force one metre from $A$ and a downward uniform load of $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on the overhang from $x=4$ to $x=7\,\mathrm{m}$. At $A$ the pin still prevents horizontal movement, but the vertical contact can only push upward. Does the beam remain in contact at $A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;D,7,0" aste="A-B;B-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="0 0,1 0,1 m;1 0,4 0,3 m;4 0,7 0,3 m" forze="1 0,0,-1,P_1" carichi="4 0,7 0,0.75,0.75,q" titolo="Span, overhang, and applied loads"}

- Yes, it requires $1.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward at $A$.
- No, it requires $1.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward at $A$.
- It is just losing contact, with zero reaction.
- No, any load on the overhang causes lift-off.

## Explanation

About $B$, the point load produces $8\cdot3=24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise. The overhang load totals $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ and produces $12\cdot1.5=18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ clockwise. Support $A$ must supply $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ clockwise, requiring $V_A=\dfrac64=1.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, consistent with contact.
