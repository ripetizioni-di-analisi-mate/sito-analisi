Four forces act on a free rigid plate, with coordinates in meters. They are $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward at $(0,0)$, $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward at $(2,0)$, $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right at $(0,1)$, and $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left at $(0,3)$. Which couple must be added to bring the plate into equilibrium?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0,-;P,2,0,-;Q,0,1,-;R,0,3,-" poligoni="-0.5,-0.5 2.5,-0.5 2.5,3.5 -0.5,3.5" forze="O,0,1.1,6 kN;P,0,-1.1,6 kN;Q,1.1,0,3 kN;R,-1.1,0,3 kN" punti="0,0,,pieno;2,0,,pieno;0,1,,pieno;0,3,,pieno" titolo="Plate subject to two force couples"}

- A clockwise couple of $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- A counterclockwise couple of $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- No couple, because the resultant force is already zero
- A counterclockwise couple of $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The four forces form two couples. Their resultant force is zero and force equilibrium holds.

About the origin the vertical forces give $0\cdot6+2\cdot(-6)=-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and the horizontal forces give $-(1)(3)-(3)(-3)=6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The resultant moment is therefore $-6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ clockwise.

Moment equilibrium requires zero total moment, so a counterclockwise couple $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ must be added.
