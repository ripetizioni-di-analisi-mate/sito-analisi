The distance between $A$ and $B$ on the horizontal beam shown is $6\,\mathrm{m}$. There is a pin at $A$, and the unit normal to the roller surface at $B$ is $\left(\dfrac{3}{5},\dfrac{4}{5}\right)$. A force with components $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left and $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward acts at coordinate $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Find $R_B$ along the indicated unit vector.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,233.1301" quote="0 0,3 0,3 m;3 0,6 0,3 m" forze="3 0,-0.125,-1,P_1" titolo="Inclined roller and load position"}

- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $16\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The vertical force has moment arm $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and the vertical component of $R_B$ has moment arm $6\,\mathrm{m}$. Thus $6\cdot\dfrac{4}{5} R_B=3\cdot16$.
