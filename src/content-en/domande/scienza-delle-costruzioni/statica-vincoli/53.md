The Gerber beam shown has a fixed support at $A$, an internal hinge at $C$, and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. An inclined force $F$ acts on segment $CB$. Isolate segment $CB$ only. Which unknowns appear on its free-body diagram?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,7,0" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" cerniere="C" forze="5 0,0.6,-1,F" quote="A,C,3 m,-0.7;C,B,4 m,-0.7" titolo="Gerber beam with an inclined force on the suspended span"}

- $X_C$, $Y_C$, and $V_B$
- $X_C$, $Y_C$, a moment at $C$, and $V_B$
- $H_A$, $V_A$, $M_A$, and $V_B$
- Only $V_B$, because an internal hinge transmits no forces or moments

## Explanation

Segment $CB$ is joined to the rest of the beam by hinge $C$ and to the ground by roller $B$.

The internal hinge enforces equal displacement at $C$ and permits relative rotation. It transmits two force components $X_C$ and $Y_C$ and zero moment. The roller on a horizontal surface adds only the vertical reaction $V_B$.

The fixed-support reactions act on $AC$ and do not appear on the free-body diagram of $CB$. Its three unknowns match its three equilibrium equations, so segment $CB$ can be solved first.
