A plane rigid body is subject to three scalar support constraints. Matrix $K$ mapping its three infinitesimal rigid-body motions to the restrained motion components has rank $2$. Which statement is true?

- The body is statically determinate because there are three constraint conditions
- There is an infinitesimal rigid-body motion and equilibrium determines all reactions
- There are two independent infinitesimal rigid-body motions
- There is an infinitesimal rigid-body motion and a self-equilibrating combination of reactions

## Explanation

By rank–nullity the compatible motion space has dimension $3-r=3-2=1$. One rigid-body motion is possible and the structure is unstable.

The space of self-equilibrating reactions has dimension $m-r=3-2=1$. A nonzero combination of reactions therefore has zero resultant force and moment, leaving it undetermined by equilibrium.

A beam on three collinear vertical rollers illustrates this. Horizontal translation remains free and one vertical reaction is redundant.
