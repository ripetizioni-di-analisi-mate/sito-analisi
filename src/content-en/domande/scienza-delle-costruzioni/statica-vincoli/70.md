Horizontal beam $AB$ is held at $A$ by a pair of parallel vertical links that restrain vertical translation and rotation. At $B$ it rests on a roller on a horizontal surface. How is this support arrangement classified?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,doppio-pendolo,270;B,carrello" titolo="Pair of vertical links and roller on a horizontal surface"}

- Statically determinate
- Unstable because horizontal translation is unrestrained
- Unstable because rotation about $B$ is unrestrained
- Unstable because vertical translation is unrestrained

## Explanation

The pair of parallel links at $A$ sets both the vertical displacement of $A$ and rotation to zero. Without rotation every beam point has the same vertical displacement, so the displacement at $B$ is already zero.

The roller at $B$ therefore repeats a constraint already satisfied, and the rank of the three constraints is $2$.

No support prevents horizontal translation. The beam is unstable in that direction and cannot balance an applied horizontal force.
