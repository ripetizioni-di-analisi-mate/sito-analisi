The portal frame shown has a pin at $A$ and a support at $B$ that can push only upward. The bases are $6\,\mathrm{m}$ apart and the crossbeam is at height $3\,\mathrm{m}$. A leftward force of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $C$ at the top of the left column. A weight $W$ acts downward at the midpoint of the crossbeam. Find the minimum $W$ that keeps the support at $B$ in compressive contact.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,0,3;D,6,3;B,6,0" aste="A-C;C-D;D-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,-1.1,0,F;3 3,0,-1.1,W" quote="A,B,6 m,0.7;D,B,3 m,-0.8" titolo="Portal frame with a unilateral support at B"}

- $24\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $36\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

About $A$ the leftward horizontal force at $C$ acts at elevation $3\,\mathrm{m}$, giving counterclockwise moment $-(3)(-12)=36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The weight $W$ has moment arm $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and moment $-3W$.

Taking moments about $A$ gives $6V_B+36-3W=0$, so
$$
V_B=\frac{3W-36}{6} .
$$
The unilateral support requires $V_B\geq0$, giving $W\geq12\,\mathrm{kN}$.
