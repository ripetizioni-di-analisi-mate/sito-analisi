The portal frame shown has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. An internal hinge $E$ interrupts the crossbeam at midspan. How is the frame classified?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,0,4;E,3,4;D,6,4;B,6,0" aste="A-C;C-E;E-D;D-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" cerniere="E" titolo="Portal frame with an internal hinge in the crossbeam"}

- Statically determinate
- Unstable
- Statically indeterminate to degree $1$
- Statically determinate, like a three-hinged arch

## Explanation

The internal hinge divides the portal into bodies $ACE$ and $EDB$, with six degrees of freedom before connection.

The pin at $A$ imposes two constraints, the internal hinge two, and the roller at $B$ one. These five constraints leave at least one rigid-body motion free.

The frame is therefore unstable. A three-hinged arch is statically determinate because a pin at $B$ adds the missing horizontal displacement restraint.
