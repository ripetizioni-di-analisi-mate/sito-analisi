A steel bridge bearing has a pin allowing the beam to rotate, mounted on parallel rollers that roll on a horizontal plate. Which ideal support represents this bearing in a plane model?

- A pin support
- A fixed support
- A pair of parallel vertical links
- A roller with a vertical reaction

## Explanation

The pin permits beam rotation about the bearing. The rollers allow horizontal travel along the plate.

Only vertical translation of the support point is restrained. A roller on a horizontal surface imposes precisely this constraint and has a vertical reaction.

A pin would also prevent sliding. A fixed support or a pair of parallel links would also restrain rotation.
