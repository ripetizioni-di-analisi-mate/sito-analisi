An ideal bilateral roller at each of the points $A=(0,\,0)$ and $B=(5,\,0)$, in meters, restrains a plane rigid body. The distinct reaction lines are parallel and inclined at $90^{\circ}$ to the $+x$ axis. What infinitesimal motion remains possible?

- Rotation about $A$
- Rotation about $B$
- Translation parallel to the reactions
- Horizontal translation

## Explanation

The zero-normal-velocity constraints on two distinct parallel lines prevent rotation. A translation perpendicular to the reactions keeps both normal velocities zero and is the only possible infinitesimal motion.
