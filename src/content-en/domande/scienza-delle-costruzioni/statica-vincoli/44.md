Horizontal beam $AB$ of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$ has a pin at $B$ and at $A$ a bilateral roller with horizontal reaction on a vertical sliding surface. Points $A$ and $B$ are at the same elevation. Which load can the structure not balance?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,carrello,180;B,cerniera" titolo="Pin at B and roller with horizontal reaction at A"}

- A horizontal force at midspan
- A vertical force at midspan
- A vertical force at $B$
- A horizontal force at $A$

## Explanation

The horizontal roller reaction passes through $B$. Both pin reaction components also pass through $B$, so all reactions have zero moment about $B$.

A load can be balanced only if its moment about $B$ is also zero. A horizontal force along the axis or any force applied at $B$ satisfies this condition.

A vertical midspan force has moment arm $3\,\mathrm{m}$ about $B$, so no set of support reactions can balance it. The arrangement is unstable and permits infinitesimal rotation about $B$.
