The distance between $A$ and $B$ on the horizontal beam shown is $5\,\mathrm{m}$. The beam is fixed at $A$. A force with components $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left and $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward acts at coordinate $4\,\mathrm{m}$ measured from $A$. Find the reaction moment $M_A$, taking counterclockwise as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="0 0,4 0,4 m;4 0,5 0,1 m" forze="4 0,-0.6,-1,P_1" titolo="Fixed support and loads on the cantilever"}

- $-40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $50\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The vertical force produces a clockwise moment of magnitude $10\cdot4$. The horizontal component passes through $A$, so the fixed-end reaction moment is $40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise.
