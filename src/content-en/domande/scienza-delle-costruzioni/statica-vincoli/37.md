The cantilever $AB$ shown has length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and is fixed at $A$. A downward vertical load decreases linearly from $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $A$ to zero at $B$. Find $M_A$, taking counterclockwise as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" carichi="A,B,0.9,0,q" quote="A,B,3 m,0.7" titolo="Cantilever with a triangular load peaking at the fixed end"}

- $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $13.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The triangular load has resultant $\dfrac{6\cdot3}{2}=9\,\mathrm{kN}$.

The load triangle is highest at $A$, so its resultant acts one third of the length from $A$, a distance $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from the fixed end. Its moment about $A$ is $1\cdot(-9)=-9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

Taking moments about $A$ gives $M_A-9=0$, hence $M_A=9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise.
