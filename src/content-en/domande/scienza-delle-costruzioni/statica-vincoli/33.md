A horizontal beam has a pin at $A$ and a roller with vertical reaction at $B$, at $6\,\mathrm{m}$. A weightless ideal pulley is attached to the beam axis with its center a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. One rope segment extends horizontally to the right and the other vertically downward, supporting a weight $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ in equilibrium. Find the vertical reaction at $B$.

- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $\dfrac{10}{3}\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $\dfrac{20}{3}\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $5\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The tension in each segment of an ideal rope is $10\,\mathrm{kN}$. The pulley transmits a horizontal and a vertical force of that magnitude to the beam. The horizontal force is at support elevation and has no moment about $A$. Thus $B_{y}=\dfrac{10 \times 2}{6}=\dfrac{10}{3}\,\mathrm{kN}$.
