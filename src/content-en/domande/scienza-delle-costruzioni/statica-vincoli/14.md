On the horizontal beam shown, the distance from $A$ to $B$ is $9\,\mathrm{m}$. The beam is fixed at $A$, with an internal hinge at $C$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. Segment $AC$ has length $4\,\mathrm{m}$. A force with components $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left and $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward acts at coordinate $6\,\mathrm{m}$ measured from $A$. Between coordinates $0\,\mathrm{m}$ and $4\,\mathrm{m}$, a downward load has uniform intensity $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Find $M_A$, taking counterclockwise moments as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,9,0;C,4,0" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" quote="0 0,4 0,4 m;4 0,6 0,2 m;6 0,9 0,3 m" cerniere="C" forze="6 0,-0.3333,-1,P_1" carichi="0 0,4 0,0.75,0.75,q" titolo="Internal hinge and span dimensions"}

- $32\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $60\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $68\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $92\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

Isolate $CB$. Moments about $C$ give $5V_B=15\cdot2$, hence $V_B=6\,\mathrm{kN}$. For the whole structure moments about $A$ give $M_A=16\cdot2+15\cdot6-6\cdot9$.
