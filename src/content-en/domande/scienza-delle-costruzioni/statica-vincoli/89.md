The portal frame shown has a pin at $A=(0,0)$ and a roller at $B=(5,0)$ on an inclined surface with unit normal $\left(\dfrac{3}{5},\dfrac{4}{5}\right)$. Its crossbeam is at elevation $3\,\mathrm{m}$. A rightward force of $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $C=(0,3)$. Find $H_A$, taking rightward as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,0,3;D,5,3;B,5,0" aste="A-C;C-D;D-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,233.1301" forze="C,1.1,0,F" quote="A,B,5 m,0.8;A,C,3 m,-0.8" titolo="Portal frame with an inclined roller"}

- $-8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-4.4\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-12.8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-11.6\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The roller reaction is $R_B\left(\dfrac{3}{5},\dfrac{4}{5}\right)$. Acting at $B=(5,0)$, its moment about $A$ is $5\cdot\dfrac{4}{5}R_B=4R_B$ because its horizontal component acts along line $y=0$.

The force at $C$ produces moment $-3\cdot8=-24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Taking moments about $A$ gives $4R_B-24=0$, hence $R_B=6\,\mathrm{kN}$.

The horizontal roller reaction component is $\dfrac{3}{5}\cdot6=3.6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right. Horizontal equilibrium gives $H_A+8+3.6=0$, hence $H_A=-11.6\,\mathrm{kN}$.
