Roof beam $AB$ of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$ is pinned at $A$ and has a support at $B$ secured by a hold-down anchor. Self-weight $0.5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ downward and wind uplift $1.5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ upward both act over the entire span. What force does the support at $B$ exert on the beam?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,g;0 -0.35,6 -0.35,0.9,0.9,w,90" quote="A,B,6 m,-1.6" titolo="Roof subject to wind uplift"}

- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward
- $4.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward

## Explanation

Both loads are uniform over the same span and combine algebraically. The net load is $1.5-0.5=1\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ upward, with resultant $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward at midspan.

Taking moments about $A$ gives $6V_B+3\cdot6=0$, hence $V_B=-3\,\mathrm{kN}$.

The support at $B$ must therefore pull downward on the beam with $3\,\mathrm{kN}$. A simple bearing cannot do this, so the support has a hold-down anchor.
