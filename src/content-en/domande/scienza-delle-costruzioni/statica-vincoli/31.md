Two symmetric cables support a weight $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ at a ring. From the ring, each cable rises by $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and runs horizontally by $3\,\mathrm{m}$ to its anchor. Find the tension in either cable.

- $30\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

By symmetry the horizontal components cancel. The cable length is $5\,\mathrm{m}$, so each vertical component equals $\left(\dfrac{4}{5}\right)T$. Setting $2\left(\dfrac{4}{5}\right)T=24$ gives $T=15\,\mathrm{kN}$.
