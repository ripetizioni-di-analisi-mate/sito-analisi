A beam has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. Which support can replace the roller without changing the reactions under any loading?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" titolo="Pin at A and roller on a horizontal surface at B"}

- A horizontal link connected at $B$
- A vertical link connected at $B$
- A pin at $B$
- A pair of parallel vertical links at $B$

## Explanation

The roller on a horizontal surface restrains only the vertical displacement of $B$ and has a vertical reaction.

A link pinned to the ground and beam restrains displacement of its connection along its own axis and permits other motions. A vertical link imposes the same constraint at $B$ as the roller and transmits a vertical reaction.

A pin or pair of parallel links would add a constraint and make the beam statically indeterminate. A horizontal link would restrain the horizontal displacement of $B$ and leave vertical motion free. Its line of action passes through $A$, making the arrangement unstable.
