A stepladder consists of two rigid bars joined by a hinge at apex $C=(0,4)$, with coordinates in meters. Foot $A=(-2,0)$ is pinned to the floor and foot $B=(2,0)$ rests on a roller on a horizontal surface. A horizontal chain joins the bar midpoints. A downward load $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at the apex. Find the chain tension.

::struttura{nodi="A,-2,0;C,0,4;B,2,0;M,-1,2,-;N,1,2,-" aste="A-C;C-B" catene="M-N" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" cerniere="C" forze="C,0,-1.1,P" quote="A,B,4 m,-0.7" titolo="Stepladder with a chain"}

- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The load is vertical and the structure symmetric, so $V_A=V_B=6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the base pin exerts no horizontal force.

Isolate bar $CB$. About $C$ reaction $V_B$ has moment arm $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and moment $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The chain pulls point $(1,2)$ to the left with force $T$. Its line of action passes $2\,\mathrm{m}$ below $C$, giving moment $-2T$.

Moment equilibrium gives $12-2T=0$, hence $T=6\,\mathrm{kN}$. The chain prevents the feet from spreading.
