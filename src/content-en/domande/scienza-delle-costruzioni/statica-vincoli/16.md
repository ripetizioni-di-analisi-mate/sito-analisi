A rigid horizontal bar is pinned at its left end $A$. A vertical cable pulls upward a distance $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. A downward force of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and a clockwise couple of $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is applied. Find the cable tension.

- $5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $7\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The pin has zero moment about $A$. The cable exerts counterclockwise moment $6T$. Equilibrium requires $6T=12 \times 2+6$, giving $T=5\,\mathrm{kN}$.
