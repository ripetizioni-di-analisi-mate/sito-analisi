A three-hinged arch consists of two straight members. The base pins, $A$ on the left and $B$ on the right, are at the same elevation and are spaced $8\,\mathrm{m}$ apart. Internal hinge $C$ is at height $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and a distance $3\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right of $A$. A rightward force of $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts only on member $CB$, halfway up between $B$ and $C$. Find $A_{x}$, taking rightward as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,8,0;C,3,4" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" titolo="Three-hinged arch with a horizontal force" cerniere="C" forze="5.5 2,1.4,0,16 kN" quote="A,B,8 m"}

- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Overall moment equilibrium about $A$ gives $B_{y}=4\,\mathrm{kN}$. Vertical force equilibrium requires $A_{y}=-4\,\mathrm{kN}$. Isolating member $AC$ and taking moments about $C$ gives $4A_{x}-3A_{y}=0$. Thus $A_{x}=-3\,\mathrm{kN}$.
