The beam shown has a pin support at $A$, an internal hinge at $C$ a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$ a distance $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $C$. A second roller on a horizontal surface supports the hinge pin at $C$. A downward force of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts a distance $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Find the reaction of the roller at $C$, taking upward as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,0;B,10,0" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello;B,carrello" cerniere="C" forze="3 0,0,-1.2,P" quote="A,3 0,3 m,0.7;3 0,C,1 m,0.7;C,B,6 m,0.7" titolo="Internal hinge above the intermediate support"}

- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Isolate segment $CB$. Its only forces are the pin force at $C$ and reaction $V_B$. The pin force has zero moment arm about $C$, so moment equilibrium gives $6V_B=0$ and $V_B=0$. Segment $CB$ is therefore unloaded, and the pin transfers no force between the two segments.

Segment $AC$ therefore acts as a beam simply supported at $A$ and $C$. Taking moments about $A$ gives $4V_C-3\cdot12=0$.

Thus $V_C=9\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, and vertical equilibrium gives $V_A=3\,\mathrm{kN}$.
