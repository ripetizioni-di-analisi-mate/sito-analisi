The rectangular plate shown has vertices $A=(0,0)$, $B=(6,0)$, $C=(6,3)$, and $D=(0,3)$, in meters. Three rollers restrain it. The reactions at $A$ and $B$ are vertical and that at $C$ is horizontal. A rightward force of $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts on side $AD$ at elevation $1\,\mathrm{m}$. Find $V_A$, taking upward as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,6,3;D,0,3" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-A" vincoli="A,carrello;B,carrello;C,carrello,0" forze="0 1,1.1,0,F" quote="A,B,6 m,0.8;A,0 1,1 m,0.6" titolo="Plate on three rollers"}

- $-3\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The lines of action of $V_B$ and $H_C$ intersect at $C$, so the moment equation about $C$ contains only $V_A$.

About $C$ reaction $V_A$ has moment arm $6\,\mathrm{m}$ and moment $-6V_A$. The applied force passes $2\,\mathrm{m}$ below $C$ and points to the right, giving moment $-(1-3)\cdot9=18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

Moment equilibrium gives $-6V_A+18=0$. Thus $V_A=3\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward.
