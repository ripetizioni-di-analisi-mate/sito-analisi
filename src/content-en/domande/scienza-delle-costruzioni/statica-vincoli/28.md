A horizontal beam is pinned at $A$. Two downward forces act, $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ at a distance $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ at a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. How far from $A$ should a roller with vertical reaction $B$ be placed, beyond both loads, to make the vertical reactions equal?

- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Each reaction must equal half the total load, $12\,\mathrm{kN}$. The two forces have moment $60\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ about $A$. The distance to $B$ is the moment arm needed for the reaction at $B$ to balance that moment, namely $5\,\mathrm{m}$.
