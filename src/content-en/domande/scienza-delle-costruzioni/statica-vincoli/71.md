A portal consists of two columns rigidly joined to a crossbeam with no internal hinges. The column bases $A$ and $B$ are pinned to the ground. Find the degree of static indeterminacy.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,0,4;D,6,4;B,6,0" aste="A-C;C-D;D-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" titolo="Portal frame with two pinned bases"}

- $1$
- $0$
- $2$
- $3$

## Explanation

The rigid-jointed portal is a single rigid body with three degrees of freedom. Its two external pins impose four scalar constraints.

The pins are distinct, so they restrain every rigid-body motion and the constraints have rank $3$.

By rank–nullity the self-equilibrating reaction combinations form a space of dimension $4-3=1$. The portal is statically indeterminate to degree $1$ because equilibrium cannot determine the distribution of the horizontal reactions.
