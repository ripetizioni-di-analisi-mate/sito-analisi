An $L$-shaped frame is fixed at the base of its column of height $4\,\mathrm{m}$. A horizontal arm of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ projects to the right from the top. A uniform rightward load of $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts on the column, and a downward force of $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at the tip of the arm. Find the counterclockwise reaction moment at the fixed support.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,3,4" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,incastro" titolo="Frame with horizontal loading on its column" carichi="A,B,0.8,0.8,4 kN/m,0" forze="C,0,-1.3,10 kN" quote="A,B,4 m,-0.8;B,C,3 m,-0.8"}

- $32\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $92\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $62\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The horizontal load has resultant $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ at mid-height, giving clockwise moment $32\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The vertical load has horizontal moment arm $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and clockwise moment $30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The support balances them with counterclockwise moment $62\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
