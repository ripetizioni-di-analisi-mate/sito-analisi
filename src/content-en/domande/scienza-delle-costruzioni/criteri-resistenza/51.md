The Tresca equivalent stress at a point in a ductile material is $120\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Which value is impossible for the von Mises equivalent stress at the same point?

- $104\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $110\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $90\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $120\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

Every triple of principal stresses satisfies $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\,\sigma_{\mathrm{eq,T}}\leq\sigma_{\mathrm{eq,V}}\leq\sigma_{\mathrm{eq,T}}$.
For $\sigma_{\mathrm{eq,T}}=120\,\mathrm{MPa}$ the lower bound is $60\sqrt3\simeq103.9\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
Thus the von Mises equivalent stress is between $103.9\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $120\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
The value $90\,\mathrm{MPa}$ lies outside this interval, whereas $104$, $110$, and $120\,\mathrm{MPa}$ lie inside.
