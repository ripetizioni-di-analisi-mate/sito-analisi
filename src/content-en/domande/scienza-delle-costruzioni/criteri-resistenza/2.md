Two principal stresses are $-10$ and $-40\,\mathrm{MPa}$. The third is an unknown $s$ that may change the ordering of the principal stresses. Over what range of $s$ is the Tresca equivalent stress at most $60\,\mathrm{MPa}$?

- $[-100;50]\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $[-40;-10]\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $[-70;20]\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $[-60;60]\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

Every principal-stress difference must have magnitude at most $60\,\mathrm{MPa}$. The known pair differs by $30\,\mathrm{MPa}$. The other pairs require $-70\le s\le50$ and $-100\le s\le20$ in MPa. Their intersection extends from $-70$ to $20\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
