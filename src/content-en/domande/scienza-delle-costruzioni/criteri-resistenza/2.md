The principal stresses, in descending order, are $\sigma_1=-10\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_2=-40\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\sigma_3=-80\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Find the Tresca equivalent stress.

::struttura{poligoni="-1,-1 1,-1 1,1 -1,1" frecce="-1.8,0,-1.03,0,;1.8,0,1.03,0,σ_1;0,-1.8,0,-1.03,;0,1.8,0,1.03,σ_3" etichette="-1.9,2,σ_2 = -40 MPa" titolo="State of stress in principal coordinates"}

- $40\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $90\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $70\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $30\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The largest principal stress is $-10\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and the smallest is $-80\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Their difference is $70\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
