The principal stresses at a point are $\sigma_1=100\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_2=40\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\sigma_3=-20\,\mathrm{MPa}$. What are the center and radius of the Mohr circle associated with the pair $(\sigma_2,\sigma_3)$?

- Center $40\,\mathrm{MPa}$, radius $60\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- Center $70\,\mathrm{MPa}$, radius $30\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- Center $10\,\mathrm{MPa}$, radius $30\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- Center $10\,\mathrm{MPa}$, radius $60\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The circle for the pair $(\sigma_2,\sigma_3)$ has endpoints $40\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $-20\,\mathrm{MPa}$ on the $\sigma_n$ axis.
Its center is the midpoint, $\dfrac{40+(-20)}{2}=10\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
Its radius is half the distance between endpoints, $\dfrac{40-(-20)}{2}=30\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
Center $40\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and radius $60\,\mathrm{MPa}$ describe the outer circle. Center $70\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and radius $30\,\mathrm{MPa}$ describe the circle for pair $(\sigma_1,\sigma_2)$.
