All principal stresses in a brittle material are positive. Which comparison determines the Coulomb–Mohr failure limit?

- Compare the largest principal stress with the tensile strength.
- Compare the smallest principal stress with the tensile strength.
- Compare the difference between the extreme principal stresses with the compressive strength.
- Compare the mean stress with the tensile strength.

## Explanation

On the all-tensile branch the ratio is the largest principal stress divided by the positive uniaxial strength ($\eta_{\mathrm{CM}}=\dfrac{\sigma_1}{f_t}$). The other principal stresses are smaller.
