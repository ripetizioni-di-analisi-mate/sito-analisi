The Mohr circle shown represents plane stress in the $xy$ plane. Its center is at $30\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and its radius is $50\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Point $X$ with coordinates $(60,\ 40)\,\mathrm{MPa}$ represents the face with normal $x$. Find $\sigma_y$.

::grafico{curve="30+50*cos(t),50*sin(t),0,6.2831853" punti="60,40,pieno,X" xmin=-40 xmax=100 ymin=-60 ymax=60 uguale=1 nomi="σ,τ" titolo="Mohr circle for plane stress"}

- $-20\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $30\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $-40\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $0$

## Explanation

The face with normal $y$ is rotated through $90^\circ$ from the face with normal $x$.
A face rotation of $90^\circ$ gives a point rotation of $180^\circ$ on the Mohr circle. The point for face $y$ is therefore diametrically opposite $X$.
Its horizontal coordinate is the reflection of $60\,\mathrm{MPa}$ about the circle center, giving $\sigma_y=2\cdot30-60=0$.
The value $-20\,\mathrm{MPa}$ is the smaller in-plane principal stress, $30-50$.
