Two ductile yield criteria calibrated to the same uniaxial yield stress are applied to the same state of pure shear. Which gives the smaller factor of safety?

- Tresca.
- Von Mises.
- Both give the same factor.
- It depends on the direction of shear.

## Explanation

The Tresca equivalent stress is $2|\tau|$ and the von Mises equivalent stress is $\sqrt3|\tau|$. For the same yield strength, the larger equivalent stress gives the smaller factor of safety.
