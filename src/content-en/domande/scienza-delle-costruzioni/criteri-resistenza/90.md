On the Mohr circle for plane stress, the point representing a face with normal $x$ moves around the circle when the normal rotates through $30^\circ$. Through what angle about the circle center does the point rotate?

- $30^\circ$
- $15^\circ$
- $120^\circ$
- $60^\circ$

## Explanation

On a face whose normal is inclined at $\theta$, the normal stress is $m+\dfrac{\sigma_x-\sigma_y}{2}\cos(2\theta)+\tau_{xy}\sin(2\theta)$, where $m$ is the circle center.
Both normal and shear stress therefore depend on $\theta$ only through $2\theta$.
Thus a face rotation $\theta$ corresponds to a rotation $2\theta$ of the point on the circle.
For $\theta=30^\circ$ the point rotates through $60^\circ$.
