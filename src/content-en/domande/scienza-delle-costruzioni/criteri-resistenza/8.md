A volumetric strain measurement yields a mean stress of $-30\,\mathrm{MPa}$. The deviatoric principal stresses are $(75,0,-75)\,\mathrm{MPa}$. What are the maximum normal stress and the maximum shear stress magnitude in space?

- $(45,75)\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $(75,75)\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $(45,45)\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $(105,150)\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

Add the mean stress to each deviatoric principal stress. The total stresses are $(45,-30,-105)\,\mathrm{MPa}$. The largest is the maximum normal stress. Maximum shear is half the spread, $\dfrac{45+105}{2}=75\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
