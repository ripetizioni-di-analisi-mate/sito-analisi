The wire of a helical spring is in pure torsion. Its uniaxial yield strength is $f_y=275\,\mathrm{MPa}$. What shear stress produces yielding according to von Mises?

- $137.5\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $275\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $91.7\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $158.8\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

In pure torsion the principal stresses are $\tau$, $0$, and $-\tau$.
Their differences are $\tau$, $\tau$, and $2\tau$, giving von Mises equivalent stress $\sqrt{\dfrac{\tau^2+\tau^2+4\tau^2}{2}}=\sqrt3\,\tau$.
Setting $\sqrt3\,\tau=275\,\mathrm{MPa}$ gives $\tau=\dfrac{275}{\sqrt3}\simeq158.8\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
The value $137.5\,\mathrm{MPa}$ is the Tresca limit, $\dfrac{f_y}{2}$.
