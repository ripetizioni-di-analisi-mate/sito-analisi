A concrete has $f_t=3\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $f_c=30\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Which of the following principal stress states has the largest Rankine utilization ratio?

- $(0,\ 0,\ -24)\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $(2.7,\ 0,\ 0)\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $(1.5,\ 0,\ -21)\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $(-2,\ -2,\ -25)\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The Rankine ratio is the larger of $\dfrac{\sigma_1}{f_t}$, if $\sigma_1>0$, and $-\dfrac{\sigma_3}{f_c}$.
Uniaxial tension $2.7\,\mathrm{MPa}$ gives $\dfrac{2.7}{3}=0.9$.
The two compressive states give $\dfrac{24}{30}=0.8$ and $\dfrac{25}{30}\simeq0.83$.
In the mixed state the ratios are $\dfrac{1.5}{3}=0.5$ and $\dfrac{21}{30}=0.7$, giving utilization $0.7$.
The tensile stress $2.7\,\mathrm{MPa}$ therefore gives the largest ratio because its ratio to $f_t$ exceeds all the others.
The mixed state would have the largest ratio, $1.2$, only if the tensile and compressive ratios were added as in Coulomb–Mohr.
