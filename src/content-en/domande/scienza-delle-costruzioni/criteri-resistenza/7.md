The principal stresses in descending order are $\sigma_1=125\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_2=45\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\sigma_3=45\,\mathrm{MPa}$. The same stress $-55\,\mathrm{MPa}$ is added to each principal component. Calculate the new von Mises equivalent stress.

::struttura{poligoni="-1,-1 1,-1 1,1 -1,1" frecce="-1.03,0,-1.8,0,;1.03,0,1.8,0,σ_1;0,-1.03,0,-1.8,;0,1.03,0,1.8,σ_3" etichette="-1.9,2,σ_2 = 45 MPa" titolo="State of stress in principal coordinates"}

- $70\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $25\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $135\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $80\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

Subtracting the same stress from all three principal stresses preserves their differences. The two nonzero differences have magnitude $125-45=80\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
