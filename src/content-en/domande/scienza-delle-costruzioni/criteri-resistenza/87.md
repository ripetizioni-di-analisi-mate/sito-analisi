The stress tensor at a point depends on a multiplier $\lambda>0$. Which of these loading laws defines a proportional path for which the factor of safety is the reciprocal of the utilization ratio calculated at $\lambda=1$?

- $\sigma_x=60$, $\tau_{xy}=20\lambda\,$ (in $\mathrm{MPa}$)
- $\sigma_x=60\lambda$, $\tau_{xy}=20\lambda^2\,$ (in $\mathrm{MPa}$)
- $\sigma_x=60\lambda$, $\tau_{xy}=20\lambda\,$ (in $\mathrm{MPa}$)
- $\sigma_x=60+10\lambda$, $\tau_{xy}=20\lambda\,$ (in $\mathrm{MPa}$)

## Explanation

A load path is proportional when the whole stress tensor is a fixed tensor multiplied by $\lambda$.
With $\sigma_x=60\lambda$ and $\tau_{xy}=20\lambda$ the tensor is $\lambda$ times the tensor with $\sigma_x=60$ and $\tau_{xy}=20$. Its principal directions therefore remain fixed and the ratio scales with $\lambda$.
In the law with constant $\sigma_x=60$ and in the law with $60+10\lambda$, part of the stress tensor does not scale with $\lambda$.
In the law with $20\lambda^2$, the ratio of shear to normal stress changes with $\lambda$ and the principal directions rotate.
