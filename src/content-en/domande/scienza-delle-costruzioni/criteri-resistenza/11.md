The principal stresses in descending order are $\sigma_1=12\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_2=0\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\sigma_3=-45\,\mathrm{MPa}$. The tensile strength is $f_t=40\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and the magnitude of the compressive strength is $f_c=150\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Calculate the Coulomb–Mohr utilization ratio.

::struttura{poligoni="-1,-1 1,-1 1,1 -1,1" frecce="-1.03,0,-1.8,0,;1.03,0,1.8,0,σ_1;0,-1.8,0,-1.03,;0,1.8,0,1.03,σ_3" etichette="-1.9,2,σ_2 = 0 MPa" titolo="State of stress in principal coordinates"}

- $\dfrac3{10}$
- $\dfrac1{10}$
- $\dfrac{19}{50}$
- $\dfrac35$

## Explanation

The extreme principal stresses have opposite signs. Coulomb–Mohr adds the tensile ratio $\dfrac{12}{40}$ and the compressive ratio $\dfrac{45}{150}$.
