The figure shows the three Mohr circles for a stress state, with $\sigma_n$ and $\tau$ in megapascals. The outer circle has center $20\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and radius $60\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and the right-hand inner circle has radius $30\,\mathrm{MPa}$. What are the principal stresses?

- $(80,\ 50,\ -40)\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The outer circle has endpoints $\sigma_1$ and $\sigma_3$, so $\sigma_1=20+60=80\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\sigma_3=20-60=-40\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
The right-hand inner circle corresponds to pair $(\sigma_1,\sigma_2)$ and has diameter $\sigma_1-\sigma_2=2\cdot30=60\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
Hence $\sigma_2=80-60=20\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and the triple is $(80,\ 20,\ -40)\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
As a check, the left-hand circle has endpoints $20\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $-40\,\mathrm{MPa}$, giving radius $30\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
