A solid circular shaft carries bending moment $M$ and torque $T$. Neglecting shear due to bending, where in the section is the von Mises equivalent stress largest?

- At the center of the section
- At the surface points on the neutral axis of bending
- At every surface point, with the same value
- At the two surface points farthest from the neutral axis of bending

## Explanation

The normal bending stress varies linearly with distance from the neutral axis. Its magnitude is greatest at the two farthest surface points.
Torsional shear stress increases linearly with radius and therefore has the same maximum value at every surface point.
At each point $\sigma$ and $\tau$ act on the same face, so $\sigma_{\mathrm{eq,V}}=\sqrt{\sigma^2+3\tau^2}$.
At the two points farthest from the neutral axis both $|\sigma|$ and $\tau$ are largest. The equivalent stress reaches its maximum there.
On the neutral axis the normal stress vanishes, leaving only the shear contribution, $\sqrt3\,\tau$.
