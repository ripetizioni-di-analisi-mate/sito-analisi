The principal stresses at a point are $60\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $20\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $-20\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Which pair of normal and shear stresses $(\sigma_n,\ |\tau|)$ can act on a plane through the point?

- $(20,\ 40)\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $(20,\ 45)\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $(60,\ 10)\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $(-20,\ 5)\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The admissible pairs $(\sigma_n,|\tau|)$ lie inside the outer Mohr circle and outside the two inner circles.
The outer circle has center $20\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and radius $40\,\mathrm{MPa}$. At $\sigma_n=20\,\mathrm{MPa}$ the shear stress therefore cannot exceed $40\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
Point $(20,\ 40)$ is at the top of the outer circle. It lies more than $20\,\mathrm{MPa}$ from centers $40\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $0$ of the inner circles, so it is admissible.
At $\sigma_n=60\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\sigma_n=-20\,\mathrm{MPa}$ the circles touch on the horizontal axis, so zero is the only possible shear stress.
Point $(20,\ 45)$ lies outside the outer circle.
