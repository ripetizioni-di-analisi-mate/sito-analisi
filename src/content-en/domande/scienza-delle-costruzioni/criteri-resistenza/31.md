An element is in uniaxial tension with $\sigma_x=40\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_y=0$, and $\tau_{xy}=0$. What is the normal stress on a face whose normal makes an angle of $30^\circ$ with the $x$ axis?

::struttura{poligoni="-1,-1 1,-1 1,1 -1,1" frecce="-1.03,0,-1.8,0,;1.03,0,1.8,0,σ_x" titolo="Uniaxial tension along x"}

- $10\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $30\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $20\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $20\sqrt3\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

With $\sigma_y=0$ and $\tau_{xy}=0$ the normal stress on the inclined face reduces to $\sigma_n=\sigma_x\cos^2\theta$.
Since $\cos30^\circ=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$, the result is $\cos^2 30^\circ=\dfrac{3}{4}$.
Thus $\sigma_n=40\cdot\dfrac{3}{4}=30\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
The value $20\sqrt3\,\mathrm{MPa}$ is the $x$ component of the traction vector, $\sigma_x\cos\theta$. Obtain the normal stress by projecting onto the normal, multiplying once more by $\cos\theta$.
