A stress state has only two nonzero components, $\sigma_x=80\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\tau_{yz}=20\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Find the Tresca equivalent stress.

- $100\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $40\sqrt5\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $80\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $40\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

Shear component $\tau_{yz}$ acts in the $yz$ plane and does not couple the $x$ direction to the other two.
Direction $x$ is therefore principal, with stress $80\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
The $yz$ plane carries only shear, giving principal stresses $20\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $-20\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
The ordered triple is $(80,\ 20,\ -20)\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and the Tresca equivalent stress is $80-(-20)=100\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
The value $40\sqrt5\,\mathrm{MPa}$ follows from $\sqrt{\sigma^2+4\tau^2}$, which applies when normal and shear stress act on the same face, as with $\sigma_x$ and $\tau_{xy}$.
