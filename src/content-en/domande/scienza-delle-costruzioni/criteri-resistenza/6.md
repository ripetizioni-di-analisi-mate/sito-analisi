In plane stress, $\sigma_x=80\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\sigma_y=-10\,\mathrm{MPa}$. The in-plane principal stresses are $110\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $-40\,\mathrm{MPa}$. What do these data tell us about $\tau_{xy}$?

- $\tau_{xy}=+75\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $\tau_{xy}=-45\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $\tau_{xy}=\pm60\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $\tau_{xy}=0$

## Explanation

The Mohr circle radius is $75\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and half the difference of the normal stresses is $45\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Thus $|\tau_{xy}|=\sqrt{75^2-45^2}=60\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Reversing the shear reflects the principal directions while preserving the eigenvalues.
