A sheet is in plane stress with $\sigma_x=100\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_y=40\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\tau_{xy}=40\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Considering all possible planes, find the maximum shear stress at the point.

::struttura{poligoni="-1,-1 1,-1 1,1 -1,1" frecce="-1.03,0,-1.8,0,;1.03,0,1.8,0,σ_x;0,-1.03,0,-1.8,;0,1.03,0,1.8,σ_y;1.18,-0.55,1.18,0.55,τ_xy;-1.18,0.55,-1.18,-0.55,;-0.55,1.18,0.55,1.18,;0.55,-1.18,-0.55,-1.18," titolo="Stress components in the xy plane"}

- $50\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $40\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $60\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $70\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The in-plane circle has center $70\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and radius $\sqrt{30^2+40^2}=50\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
The in-plane principal stresses are therefore $120\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $20\,\mathrm{MPa}$, both positive. The third principal stress is zero.
The ordered triple is $(120,\ 20,\ 0)\,\mathrm{MPa}$, so the outer circle has endpoints $120\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $0$.
The maximum shear stress equals its radius, $60\,\mathrm{MPa}$, larger than the radius $50\,\mathrm{MPa}$ of the in-plane circle.
