The principal stresses at a point are $80\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $20\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $-40\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Among planes with normal stress $\sigma_n=60\,\mathrm{MPa}$, what is the greatest possible shear stress magnitude?

- $60\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $30\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $20\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $20\sqrt5\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

Every plane satisfies $\tau^2\leq(\sigma_1-\sigma_n)(\sigma_n-\sigma_3)$, with equality on the outer Mohr circle.
With $\sigma_n=60\,\mathrm{MPa}$ this gives $\tau^2\leq(80-60)(60+40)=2000\,\mathrm{MPa^2}$.
The maximum is therefore $\sqrt{2000}=20\sqrt5\,\mathrm{MPa}$, approximately $44.7\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
The value $60\,\mathrm{MPa}$ is the radius of the outer circle, attained only at $\sigma_n=20\,\mathrm{MPa}$, the horizontal coordinate of its center.
