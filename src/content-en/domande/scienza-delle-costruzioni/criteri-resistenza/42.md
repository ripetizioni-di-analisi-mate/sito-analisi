A slender steel member is compressed axially with $\sigma=-80\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and the material has $f_y=240\,\mathrm{MPa}$. The pointwise von Mises utilization ratio is therefore $\dfrac13$. Which conclusion is correct?

- The load can triple before any failure of the member
- The material has not yielded. The member must be checked separately for buckling
- The von Mises criterion does not apply because the stress is compressive
- The factor of safety of the member is $\dfrac13$

## Explanation

In uniaxial compression the von Mises equivalent stress is $80\,\mathrm{MPa}$, so the ratio $\dfrac{80}{240}=\dfrac13$ measures utilization toward material yielding at this point.
The criterion applies in compression because it depends on differences between principal stresses.
Buckling of the slender member depends on its flexural rigidity, length, and supports. None of these enter the pointwise utilization ratio.
The yield safety factor $3$ therefore does not establish that the member can carry three times the load. Its stability requires a separate check.
