A steel bar is rigidly fixed at both ends and uniformly heated by $\Delta T=50\,\mathrm{K}$. The material has $E=200\,\mathrm{GPa}$, thermal expansion coefficient $\alpha=12\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$, and $f_y=240\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Find the Tresca utilization ratio in the bar.

- $\dfrac12$
- $0$
- $1$
- $\dfrac14$

## Explanation

The supports prevent the thermal elongation $\alpha\Delta T$, inducing mechanical strain $-\alpha\Delta T$ in the bar.
The resulting stress is $\sigma=-E\alpha\Delta T=-200\,000\cdot12\cdot10^{-6}\cdot50=-120\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
The stress state is uniaxial compression, so the Tresca equivalent stress is $120\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
The utilization ratio is therefore $\dfrac{120}{240}=\dfrac12$ even though the bar carries no externally applied load.
