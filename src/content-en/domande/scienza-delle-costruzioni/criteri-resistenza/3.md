The principal stresses in descending order are $\sigma_1=130\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_2=50\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\sigma_3=50\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Calculate the von Mises equivalent stress.

::struttura{poligoni="-1,-1 1,-1 1,1 -1,1" frecce="-1.03,0,-1.8,0,;1.03,0,1.8,0,σ_1;0,-1.03,0,-1.8,;0,1.03,0,1.8,σ_3" etichette="-1.9,2,σ_2 = 50 MPa" titolo="State of stress in principal coordinates"}

- $130\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $50\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $40\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $80\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The difference between the two equal principal stresses is zero. The other two differences both have magnitude $80\,\mathrm{MPa}$, so the von Mises equivalent stress is $80\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
