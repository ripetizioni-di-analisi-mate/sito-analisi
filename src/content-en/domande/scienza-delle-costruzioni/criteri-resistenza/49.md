A stress state has $\sigma_1=90\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_2=0$, and $\sigma_3=-30\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Find the mean stress $\bar\sigma=\dfrac{\sigma_1+\sigma_2+\sigma_3}{3}$.

- $30\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $20\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $60\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $40\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The three principal stresses sum to $90+0-30=60\,\mathrm{MPa}$, so the mean stress is $\bar\sigma=\dfrac{60}{3}=20\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
The value $30\,\mathrm{MPa}$ is the center of the outer Mohr circle, $\dfrac{\sigma_1+\sigma_3}{2}$, which does not include $\sigma_2$.
The two quantities coincide only when $\sigma_2$ is the mean of $\sigma_1$ and $\sigma_3$.
Here $\sigma_2=0$ differs from $30\,\mathrm{MPa}$, so the mean stress differs from the center of the outer circle.
