For the principal stress triple $(60,\ 30,\ -40)\,\mathrm{MPa}$, consider a plane of maximum shear stress. What normal stress acts on that plane?

- $30\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $50\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $10\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $0$

## Explanation

Maximum shear stress occurs at the top of the outer Mohr circle with endpoints $\sigma_1=60\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\sigma_3=-40\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
The top of a circle has the same horizontal coordinate as its center.
Thus the normal stress on that plane is $\dfrac{60+(-40)}{2}=10\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and the shear stress equals the radius, $50\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
Its normal is at $45^\circ$ between principal directions $1$ and $3$, so $\sigma_2$ does not enter.
