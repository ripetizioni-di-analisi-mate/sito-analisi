Consider the Tresca, von Mises, Rankine, and Coulomb–Mohr criteria. Which give a utilization ratio that depends on the intermediate principal stress $\sigma_2$?

- Only von Mises
- Only Tresca
- Von Mises and Coulomb–Mohr
- All four

## Explanation

The Tresca equivalent stress is $\sigma_1-\sigma_3$ and depends only on the extreme principal stresses.
The Rankine and Coulomb–Mohr ratios use $\dfrac{\sigma_1}{f_t}$ and $-\dfrac{\sigma_3}{f_c}$ and likewise omit $\sigma_2$.
The von Mises equivalent stress includes the differences $\sigma_1-\sigma_2$ and $\sigma_2-\sigma_3$.
For example, $(100,\ 0,\ 0)$ and $(100,\ 50,\ 0)\,\mathrm{MPa}$ have the same Tresca equivalent stress, while von Mises gives $100\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $50\sqrt3\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
