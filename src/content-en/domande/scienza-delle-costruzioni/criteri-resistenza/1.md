The principal stresses, in descending order, are $\sigma_1=85\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_2=15\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\sigma_3=-25\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Find the Tresca equivalent stress.

::struttura{poligoni="-1,-1 1,-1 1,1 -1,1" frecce="-1.03,0,-1.8,0,;1.03,0,1.8,0,σ_1;0,-1.8,0,-1.03,;0,1.8,0,1.03,σ_3" etichette="-1.9,2,σ_2 = 15 MPa" titolo="State of stress in principal coordinates"}

- $85\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $110\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $70\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $40\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The Tresca equivalent stress is the difference between the largest and smallest principal stresses. This gives $85-(-25)=110\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
