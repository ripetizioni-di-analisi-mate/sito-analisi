Two load cases act simultaneously at a point. The first produces only $\sigma_x=90\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and the second only $\tau_{xy}=60\,\mathrm{MPa}$. What is the Tresca equivalent stress for the combined state?

- $150\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $210\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $120\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $30\sqrt{13}\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

In linear elasticity the stress tensors add, so the combined state has $\sigma_x=90\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_y=0$, and $\tau_{xy}=60\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
The in-plane circle has center $45\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and radius $\sqrt{45^2+60^2}=75\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
Its principal stresses are $120\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $-30\,\mathrm{MPa}$, of opposite signs, so zero is the intermediate principal stress.
The Tresca equivalent stress is therefore $120-(-30)=150\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
The value $210\,\mathrm{MPa}$ is the sum of the two individual equivalent stresses, $90+120$. It does not represent the combined stress state.
