The principal stresses are $-100\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $-50\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $0\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Find the von Mises equivalent stress.

- $50 \sqrt{6}\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $50 \sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $100\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $50 \sqrt{5}\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The cyclic differences are $-50\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $-50\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $100\,\mathrm{MPa}$. The square root of half the sum of their squares is $50 \sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
