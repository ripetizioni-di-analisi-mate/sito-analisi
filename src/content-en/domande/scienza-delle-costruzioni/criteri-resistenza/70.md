An element in plane stress has $\sigma_x=50\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_y=-30\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\tau_{xy}=30\,\mathrm{MPa}$. What are the three principal stresses in the order $\sigma_1\geq\sigma_2\geq\sigma_3$?

::struttura{poligoni="-1,-1 1,-1 1,1 -1,1" frecce="-1.03,0,-1.8,0,;1.03,0,1.8,0,σ_x;0,-1.8,0,-1.03,;0,1.8,0,1.03,σ_y;1.18,-0.55,1.18,0.55,τ_xy;-1.18,0.55,-1.18,-0.55,;-0.55,1.18,0.55,1.18,;0.55,-1.18,-0.55,-1.18," titolo="Stress components in the xy plane"}

- $(60,\ 0,\ -40)\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $(60,\ -40,\ 0)\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $(70,\ 0,\ -30)\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $(50,\ 0,\ -30)\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The center of the in-plane Mohr circle is $m=\dfrac{50+(-30)}{2}=10\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
Its radius is $R=\sqrt{40^2+30^2}=50\,\mathrm{MPa}$, giving principal stresses $m+R=60\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $m-R=-40\,\mathrm{MPa}$ in the plane.
In plane stress the third principal stress, along $z$, is zero.
The in-plane stresses have opposite signs. Zero is therefore intermediate and the ordered triple is $(60,\ 0,\ -40)\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
