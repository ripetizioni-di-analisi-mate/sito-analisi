The principal stresses at a point in a ductile material are $-95\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $25\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $-35\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Find the Tresca equivalent stress.

- $60\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $120\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $105\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $240\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

Subtract the smallest principal stress $-95$ from the largest $25$. The difference is $120\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
