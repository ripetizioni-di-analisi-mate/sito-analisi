Keep $\sigma_1=90\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\sigma_3=-30\,\mathrm{MPa}$ fixed while the intermediate principal stress changes from $-30$ to $30\,\mathrm{MPa}$. How does the Tresca equivalent stress change?

- The equivalent stress increases by $60\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
- The equivalent stress is halved.
- The equivalent stress depends on the mean of the three principal stresses.
- The equivalent stress is unchanged.

## Explanation

The largest and smallest principal stresses are unchanged. Tresca uses only their difference.
