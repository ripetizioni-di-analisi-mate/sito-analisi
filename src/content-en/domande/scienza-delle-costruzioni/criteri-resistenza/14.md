The principal stresses in descending order are $\sigma_1=15\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_2=-10\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\sigma_3=-45\,\mathrm{MPa}$. The tensile strength is $f_t=40\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and the magnitude of the compressive strength is $f_c=120\,\mathrm{MPa}$. All stresses are scaled by one load multiplier. Calculate the factor of safety using the Coulomb–Mohr criterion.

::struttura{poligoni="-1,-1 1,-1 1,1 -1,1" frecce="-1.03,0,-1.8,0,;1.03,0,1.8,0,σ_1;0,-1.8,0,-1.03,;0,1.8,0,1.03,σ_3" etichette="-1.9,2,σ_2 = -10 MPa" titolo="State of stress in principal coordinates"}

- $\dfrac83$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac43$
- $2$

## Explanation

On the mixed branch the utilization ratio is $\dfrac{15}{40}+\dfrac{45}{120}=\dfrac34$. Under proportional loading the load factor to the limit is its reciprocal.
