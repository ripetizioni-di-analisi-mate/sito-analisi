At a point in a brittle material the principal stresses are $(30,\ 0,\ -60)\,\mathrm{MPa}$, with $f_t=40\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $f_c=120\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Which statement is true?

- The state is inside both admissible regions
- The state is outside both admissible regions
- The state is inside the Rankine region and outside the Coulomb–Mohr region
- The state is outside the Rankine region and inside the Coulomb–Mohr region

## Explanation

The tensile ratio is $\dfrac{30}{40}=\dfrac34$ and the compressive ratio is $\dfrac{60}{120}=\dfrac12$.
The Rankine utilization ratio is the larger of the two ratios, $\dfrac34<1$, so the state is inside the Rankine region.
This is a mixed stress state, so the Coulomb–Mohr utilization ratio is the sum of the two ratios, $\dfrac34+\dfrac12=\dfrac54>1$.
The state therefore lies outside the Coulomb–Mohr region.
For mixed states the Coulomb–Mohr ratio always exceeds the Rankine ratio. No mixed state can therefore lie outside the Rankine region and inside the Coulomb–Mohr region.
