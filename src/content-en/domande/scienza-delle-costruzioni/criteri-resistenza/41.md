A brittle specimen is stronger in compression than in tension. For equal stress magnitudes, which uniaxial state gives the larger Rankine utilization ratio?

- Compression.
- Tension.
- The two ratios are equal.
- Young's modulus is required.

## Explanation

Both ratios divide the same stress magnitude by the corresponding strength, $\dfrac{|\sigma|}{f_t}$ in tension and $\dfrac{|\sigma|}{f_c}$ in compression. The tensile strength is smaller, giving a larger ratio.
