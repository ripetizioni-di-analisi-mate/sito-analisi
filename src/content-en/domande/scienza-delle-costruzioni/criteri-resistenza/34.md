A material has equal tensile and compressive strengths, $f_t=f_c=f$. For a mixed stress state ($\sigma_1>0>\sigma_3$), which utilization ratio equals the Coulomb–Mohr ratio?

- The Rankine utilization ratio
- The Tresca utilization ratio calculated using $f_y=f$
- The von Mises utilization ratio calculated using $f_y=f$
- Half the Tresca utilization ratio calculated using $f_y=f$

## Explanation

On the mixed branch the Coulomb–Mohr utilization ratio is $\dfrac{\sigma_1}{f_t}-\dfrac{\sigma_3}{f_c}$.
With $f_t=f_c=f$ this becomes $\dfrac{\sigma_1-\sigma_3}{f}$.
The numerator is the Tresca equivalent stress, so the ratio equals the Tresca ratio with $f_y=f$.
The Rankine ratio is $\dfrac{\max(\sigma_1,-\sigma_3)}{f}$, smaller for every mixed state.
