With $\sigma_1$ and $\sigma_3$ fixed and distinct, what value of $\sigma_2$ minimizes the von Mises equivalent stress?

- $\sigma_2=\sigma_1$.
- $\sigma_2=\sigma_3$.
- $\sigma_2=\dfrac{\sigma_1+\sigma_3}{2}$.
- $\sigma_2=\dfrac{3\sigma_1+\sigma_3}{4}$.

## Explanation

The part depending on $\sigma_2$ is the sum of its squared distances from the two extreme stresses. This sum is smallest at their midpoint.
