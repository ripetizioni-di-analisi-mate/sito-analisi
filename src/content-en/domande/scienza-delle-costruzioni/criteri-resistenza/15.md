A plate with a hole carries a nominal tensile stress $60\,\mathrm{MPa}$. At the edge of the hole the stress state is uniaxial, and the circumferential stress is three times the nominal stress. Given $f_y=240\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and proportional loading, find the von Mises factor of safety at the most highly stressed point.

- $\dfrac43$
- $4$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac23$

## Explanation

At the hole edge the hoop stress is $3\cdot60=180\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
The stress state is uniaxial, so the von Mises equivalent stress equals $180\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
The factor of safety is therefore $\dfrac{240}{180}=\dfrac43$.
The value $4$ uses the nominal stress instead of the stress at the most highly stressed point.
