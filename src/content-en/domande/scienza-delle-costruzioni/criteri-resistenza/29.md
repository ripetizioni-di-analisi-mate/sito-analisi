Given $\sigma_2=\sigma_3<\sigma_1$, what relationship holds between the two ductile equivalent stresses?

- The von Mises value is twice the Tresca value.
- They are equal.
- The Tresca value is twice the von Mises value.
- Both are zero.

## Explanation

In the von Mises formula one difference vanishes and the other two have the same magnitude $\sigma_1-\sigma_3$. The result equals the difference used by Tresca.
