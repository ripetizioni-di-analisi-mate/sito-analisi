For a mixed stress state ($\sigma_1>0>\sigma_3$) in a brittle material the Rankine utilization ratio is $\eta_R=0.6$. Which interval contains all possible Coulomb–Mohr utilization ratios calculated with the same strengths $f_t$ and $f_c$?

- $0.3\leq\eta_{CM}\leq0.6$
- $0.6<\eta_{CM}\leq1.2$
- $0.6\leq\eta_{CM}\leq0.9$
- $1.2\leq\eta_{CM}\leq1.8$

## Explanation

Let $a=\dfrac{\sigma_1}{f_t}$ and $c=-\dfrac{\sigma_3}{f_c}$, both positive in the mixed state.
The Rankine ratio is the larger of these, so $\max(a,c)=0.6$. The Coulomb–Mohr ratio is $a+c$.
The smaller ratio is positive and at most $0.6$, giving $0.6<a+c\leq1.2$.
The value $1.2$ is reached when the two ratios are equal. Values close to $0.6$ result when one of them is small.
