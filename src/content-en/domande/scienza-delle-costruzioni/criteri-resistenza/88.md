A thin-walled spherical vessel has radius $r=2\,\mathrm{m}$, thickness $t=15\,\mathrm{mm}$, and internal pressure $p=1.5\,\mathrm{MPa}$. The membrane stress in every tangential direction is $\dfrac{pr}{2t}$, and radial stress is negligible. Given $f_y=250\,\mathrm{MPa}$, find the von Mises factor of safety.

- $1.25$
- $2.5$
- $5$
- $1.44$

## Explanation

The membrane stress is $\dfrac{1.5\cdot2000}{2\cdot15}=100\,\mathrm{MPa}$, giving the principal stress triple $(100,\ 100,\ 0)\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
Two principal stresses coincide, so the von Mises equivalent stress equals the difference between the distinct values, $100\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
The factor of safety is $\dfrac{250}{100}=2.5$.
The value $1.25$ comes from $\dfrac{pr}{t}$, the hoop stress for a cylindrical vessel.
