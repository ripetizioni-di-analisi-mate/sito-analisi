The cooling core of a cast-iron casting develops equal tensile stresses in every direction, $\sigma_1=\sigma_2=\sigma_3=15\,\mathrm{MPa}$. The cast iron has $f_t=20\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $f_c=80\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Which statement is true?

- All criteria give zero utilization because the stress state is hydrostatic
- The Rankine utilization ratio is $\dfrac94$ because the three tensile stresses add
- The Rankine utilization ratio is $\dfrac{3}{16}$ because the stress is compared with $f_c$
- The Rankine utilization ratio is $\dfrac34$, whereas the Tresca and von Mises equivalent stresses are zero

## Explanation

All principal stress differences vanish, so the Tresca and von Mises equivalent stresses are zero.
Rankine compares the largest tensile principal stress with $f_t$.
The Rankine utilization ratio is therefore $\dfrac{15}{20}=\dfrac34$.
Hydrostatic tension thus gives zero utilization under the ductile criteria and positive utilization under Rankine, which depends on the values of individual principal stresses.
