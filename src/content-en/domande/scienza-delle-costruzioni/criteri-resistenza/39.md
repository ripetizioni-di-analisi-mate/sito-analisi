A steel element is in uniaxial tension with $\sigma=200\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Its shear modulus is $G=80\,\mathrm{GPa}$. Find the elastic distortion energy per unit volume, $\dfrac{J_2}{2G}$.

- $250\,\mathrm{kJ/m^3}$
- $41.7\,\mathrm{kJ/m^3}$
- $166.7\,\mathrm{kJ/m^3}$
- $83.3\,\mathrm{kJ/m^3}$

## Explanation

In uniaxial tension the second deviatoric invariant is $J_2=\dfrac{\sigma^2}{3}=\dfrac{40000}{3}\,\mathrm{MPa^2}$.
The distortion energy is therefore
$$
\frac{J_2}{2G}=\frac{40000}{3\cdot2\cdot80000}\,\mathrm{MPa}=\frac{1}{12}\,\mathrm{MPa}.
$$
Since $1\,\mathrm{MPa}=1\,\mathrm{MJ/m^3}$, the energy is approximately $0.0833\,\mathrm{MJ/m^3}=83.3\,\mathrm{kJ/m^3}$.
The value $250\,\mathrm{kJ/m^3}$ results from writing $\sigma^2$ in place of $J_2$.
