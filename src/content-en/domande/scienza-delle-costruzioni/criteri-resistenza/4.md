During a test, $\sigma_1=100\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\sigma_3=0$ are held fixed. The intermediate principal stress can vary between these limits. What is the least attainable von Mises equivalent stress?

- $50\sqrt3\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $50\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $100\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $0\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

Writing $s$ for the value of $\sigma_2$ in MPa, the equivalent stress squared is $\dfrac{(100-s)^2+s^2+100^2}{2}=(s-50)^2+7500$. Its minimum occurs at $s=50$, giving $\sqrt{7500}\,\mathrm{MPa}$ for the stress.
