The principal stresses in descending order are $\sigma_1=100\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_2=40\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\sigma_3=-20\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Calculate the von Mises equivalent stress.

::struttura{poligoni="-1,-1 1,-1 1,1 -1,1" frecce="-1.03,0,-1.8,0,;1.03,0,1.8,0,σ_1;0,-1.8,0,-1.03,;0,1.8,0,1.03,σ_3" etichette="-1.9,2,σ_2 = 40 MPa" titolo="State of stress in principal coordinates"}

- $60\sqrt3\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $120\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $60\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $60\sqrt6\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The cyclic differences are $60$, $60$, and $-120\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Taking the square root of half the sum of their squares gives $60\sqrt3\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
