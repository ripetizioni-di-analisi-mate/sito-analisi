An element is under uniform hydrostatic compression. How do the Tresca and von Mises equivalent stresses compare?

- Both equal the magnitude of the pressure.
- Both are zero.
- Tresca gives zero and von Mises gives the pressure.
- Tresca gives the pressure and von Mises gives zero.

## Explanation

All three principal stresses coincide. All their differences vanish, so both equivalent stresses are zero ($\sigma_{\mathrm{eq,T}}=\sigma_{\mathrm{eq,V}}=0$) in the isotropic ductile model considered.
