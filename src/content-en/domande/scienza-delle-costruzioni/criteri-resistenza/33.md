At its surface a shaft has axial normal stress $80\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and torsional shear stress $60\,\mathrm{MPa}$. All other independent components vanish. Find the von Mises equivalent stress.

- $100\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $20 \sqrt{43}\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $80\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $60\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

With one normal and one shear component, $\sigma_{\rm eq}=\sqrt{\sigma_x^2+3\tau^2}$. The squared equivalent stress is $17200$ in $\mathrm{MPa^2}$.
