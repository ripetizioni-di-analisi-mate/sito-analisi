A thick plate of isotropic linear elastic material with Poisson's ratio $\nu=0.3$ is in plane strain ($\varepsilon_z=0$ with no imposed strains). The in-plane stresses are $\sigma_x=\sigma_y=100\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\tau_{xy}=0$. Find the Tresca equivalent stress.

- $100\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $0\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $70\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $40\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

Since $\varepsilon_z=0$, Hooke's law gives $\sigma_z=\nu(\sigma_x+\sigma_y)=0.3\cdot200=60\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
With no shear the $x$, $y$, and $z$ axes are principal. The ordered triple is $(100,\ 100,\ 60)\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
The Tresca equivalent stress is the largest minus the smallest principal stress, namely $100-60=40\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
The value $100\,\mathrm{MPa}$ would follow from setting $\sigma_z=0$ and treating the plate as being in plane stress.
