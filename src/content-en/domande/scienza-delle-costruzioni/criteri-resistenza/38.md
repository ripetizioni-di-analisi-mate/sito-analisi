In isotropic linear elasticity under plane strain, what must be checked before applying a three-dimensional failure criterion?

- The out-of-plane stress must always be set equal to zero.
- Whether every shear stress component vanishes.
- The value of the out-of-plane normal stress.
- Whether the in-plane principal stresses are equal.

## Explanation

The restraint is on out-of-plane normal strain ($\varepsilon_z=0$). Hooke's law gives the stress that maintains zero strain, and this stress contributes to the principal stress state.
