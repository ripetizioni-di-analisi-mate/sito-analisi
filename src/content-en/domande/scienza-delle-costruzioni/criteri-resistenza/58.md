An isotropic linear elastic cube with $\nu=0.25$ is compressed along $z$ with $\sigma_z=-120\,\mathrm{MPa}$ inside a rigid, frictionless box that prevents lateral strains ($\varepsilon_x=\varepsilon_y=0$). Find the Tresca equivalent stress.

- $120\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $80\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $90\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $160\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

By symmetry $\sigma_x=\sigma_y$, and the condition $\varepsilon_x=0$ gives $\sigma_x-\nu(\sigma_x+\sigma_z)=0$.
Thus $\sigma_x=\dfrac{\nu}{1-\nu}\,\sigma_z=\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot(-120)=-40\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
The ordered triple is therefore $(-40,\ -40,\ -120)\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
The Tresca equivalent stress is the difference between extremes, $-40-(-120)=80\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
The value $120\,\mathrm{MPa}$ applies to a cube free to expand laterally, for which both lateral stresses vanish.
