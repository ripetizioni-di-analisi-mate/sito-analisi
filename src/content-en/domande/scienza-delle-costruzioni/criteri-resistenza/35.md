In a sheet under plane stress, both in-plane principal stresses are positive. What lower extreme should be used in the Tresca criterion?

- The smaller of the two in-plane principal stresses.
- The mean of the two principal stresses.
- The negative of the largest principal stress.
- Zero.

## Explanation

The out-of-plane principal stress is zero. Both in-plane principal stresses are positive, so zero is the smallest of the three ($\min(\sigma_1,\sigma_2,0)=0$).
