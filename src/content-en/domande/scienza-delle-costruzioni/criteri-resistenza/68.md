At a point the stress tensor has components $\sigma_x=20\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_y=-40\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\tau_{xy}=40\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_z=50\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\tau_{xz}=\tau_{yz}=0$. Find the intermediate principal stress $\sigma_2$.

- $-10\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $50\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $20\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $40\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

Since $\tau_{xz}=\tau_{yz}=0$, direction $z$ is principal and its stress is $50\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
In the $xy$ plane the Mohr circle has center $-10\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and radius $\sqrt{30^2+40^2}=50\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
The in-plane principal stresses are therefore $40\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $-60\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
Ordering the three values gives $(50,\ 40,\ -60)\,\mathrm{MPa}$, hence $\sigma_2=40\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
