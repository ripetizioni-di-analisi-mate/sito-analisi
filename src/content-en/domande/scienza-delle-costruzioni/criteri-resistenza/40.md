A pure shear stress has magnitude $55\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and the uniaxial yield strength is $220\,\mathrm{MPa}$. What load factor produces yielding according to Tresca?

- $2$
- $4$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $\dfrac{1}{4}$

## Explanation

In pure shear the extreme principal stresses are $\pm|\tau|$. Their range is $110\,\mathrm{MPa}$, so the load factor is the yield strength divided by this range.
