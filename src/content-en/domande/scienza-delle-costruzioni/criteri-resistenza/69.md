A sheet in plane stress has $\sigma_x=120\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_y=-40\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\tau_{xy}=0$. The yield strength is $f_y=320\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and the load increases proportionally. Find the Tresca factor of safety.

- $2$
- $\dfrac83$
- $4$
- $\dfrac43$

## Explanation

With no shear the ordered triple is $(120,\ 0,\ -40)\,\mathrm{MPa}$ because the out-of-plane principal stress is zero.
The Tresca equivalent stress is therefore $120-(-40)=160\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
Under proportional loading the factor of safety is the reciprocal of the utilization ratio, $\dfrac{320}{160}=2$.
The value $\dfrac83$ compares only the tensile stress with $f_y$ and neglects the compression along $y$.
