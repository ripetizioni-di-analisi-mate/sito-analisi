A brittle material has $f_t=20\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $f_c=80\,\mathrm{MPa}$. In the Mohr plane, draw the upper common tangent $\tau=m\sigma+b$ to the circles for the two uniaxial tests. What is its ordinate $b$ at $\sigma=0$?

- $16\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $10\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $20\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $40\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

Setting $d=\sqrt{1+m^2}$, the two tangency conditions give $\dfrac{m}{d}=\dfrac{f_t-f_c}{f_t+f_c}=-\dfrac35$ and $\dfrac{b}{d}=\dfrac{f_tf_c}{f_t+f_c}=16\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
The first gives $m^2=\dfrac{9}{25}(1+m^2)$, and consequently $m^2=\dfrac{9}{16}$ and $d=\dfrac54$.
Therefore $b=16\cdot\dfrac54=20\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
The value $16\,\mathrm{MPa}$ is the ratio $\dfrac{b}{d}$, the perpendicular distance from the origin to the tangent line.
