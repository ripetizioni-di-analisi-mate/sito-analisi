At a point of a component made of a brittle material the principal stresses are $30\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $0$ and $-70\,\mathrm{MPa}$, with tensile strength $f_t=25\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and compressive strength magnitude $f_c=100\,\mathrm{MPa}$. The component is immersed in a fluid at pressure $p\geq0$, which subtracts $p$ from each principal stress. For which values of $p$ is the Rankine criterion satisfied, limits included?

- $0\le p\le30\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $p\ge5\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $5\,\mathrm{MPa}\le p\le30\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $30\,\mathrm{MPa}\le p\le100\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The Rankine criterion requires the largest principal stress not to exceed $f_t$ and the smallest not to fall below $-f_c$. With the pressure, the extreme principal stresses become $30-p$ and $-70-p$, in MPa. The first condition, $30-p\le25$, gives $p\ge5\,\mathrm{MPa}$, while the second, $-70-p\ge-100$, gives $p\le30\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Both conditions must hold together, so the criterion is satisfied for $5\,\mathrm{MPa}\le p\le30\,\mathrm{MPa}$. The range $0\le p\le30\,\mathrm{MPa}$ accounts only for the compressive limit.
