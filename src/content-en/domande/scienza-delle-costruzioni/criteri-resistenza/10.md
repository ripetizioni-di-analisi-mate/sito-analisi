The principal stresses at a point are $(30,0,-70)\,\mathrm{MPa}$. An additional hydrostatic pressure $p\geq0$ subtracts $p$ from every principal stress. Tensile and compressive strengths are $25\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $100\,\mathrm{MPa}$. For which pressures is the Rankine criterion satisfied, including its limits?

- $0\le p\le30\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $p\ge5\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $5\,\mathrm{MPa}\le p\le30\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $30\,\mathrm{MPa}\le p\le100\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

With stresses in MPa the largest principal must satisfy $30-p\le25$, giving $p\ge5$. The smallest must satisfy $-70-p\ge-100$, giving $p\le30$. Both inequalities must hold at once.
