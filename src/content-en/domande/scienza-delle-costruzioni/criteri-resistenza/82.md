At a point in a brittle material under plane stress, $\sigma_x=20\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_y=-60\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\tau_{xy}=30\,\mathrm{MPa}$. The material strengths are $f_t=40\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $f_c=140\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Find the Rankine utilization ratio.

::struttura{poligoni="-1,-1 1,-1 1,1 -1,1" frecce="-1.03,0,-1.8,0,;1.03,0,1.8,0,σ_x;0,-1.8,0,-1.03,;0,1.8,0,1.03,σ_y;1.18,-0.55,1.18,0.55,τ_xy;-1.18,0.55,-1.18,-0.55,;-0.55,1.18,0.55,1.18,;0.55,-1.18,-0.55,-1.18," titolo="Stress components in the xy plane"}

- $\dfrac12$
- $\dfrac37$
- $\dfrac34$
- $\dfrac54$

## Explanation

The in-plane circle has center $-20\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and radius $\sqrt{40^2+30^2}=50\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
Its principal stresses are therefore $30\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $-70\,\mathrm{MPa}$. The third principal stress is zero.
The tensile ratio is $\dfrac{30}{40}=\dfrac34$ and the compressive ratio is $\dfrac{70}{140}=\dfrac12$.
The Rankine utilization ratio is the larger of the two ratios, $\dfrac34$.
The value $\dfrac37$ compares $\sigma_y$ with $f_c$ without finding the principal stresses.
