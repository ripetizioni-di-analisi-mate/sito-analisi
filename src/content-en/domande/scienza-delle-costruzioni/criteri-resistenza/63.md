At a point in the web of a prestressed concrete beam near a support, the stresses are $\sigma_x=-30\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_y=0$, and shear stress $\tau_{xy}=\tau$. The tensile strength is $f_t=10\,\mathrm{MPa}$. At what value of $\tau$ does the tensile principal stress reach $f_t$?

::struttura{poligoni="-1,-1 1,-1 1,1 -1,1" etichette="1.6,0.25,σ_x" frecce="-1.8,0,-1.03,0,;1.8,0,1.03,0,;1.18,-0.55,1.18,0.55,τ_xy;-1.18,0.55,-1.18,-0.55,;-0.55,1.18,0.55,1.18,;0.55,-1.18,-0.55,-1.18," titolo="Compression along x and shear in the xy plane"}

- $10\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $25\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $20\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $10\sqrt3\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The larger in-plane principal stress is $\sigma_1=-15+\sqrt{15^2+\tau^2}$.
Setting $\sigma_1=10\,\mathrm{MPa}$ gives $\sqrt{225+\tau^2}=25$.
Thus $\tau^2=625-225=400\,\mathrm{MPa^2}$, or $\tau=20\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
Prestressing therefore permits a shear stress twice $f_t$. Without prestress the tensile principal stress would equal the shear stress.
