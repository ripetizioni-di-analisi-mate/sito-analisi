At a point in a sheet the stresses are $\sigma_x=100\,\mathrm{MPa}$, $\sigma_y=-60\,\mathrm{MPa}$, and $\tau_{xy}=0$. Under proportional loading, the required von Mises factor of safety is at least $1.5$. What minimum yield strength must the material have?

- $240\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $210\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $150\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $93.3\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The plane stress formula gives $\sigma_{\mathrm{eq,V}}=\sqrt{100^2-100\cdot(-60)+60^2}=\sqrt{19\,600}=140\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
The factor of safety is $\dfrac{f_y}{\sigma_{\mathrm{eq,V}}}$, so the requirement is $\dfrac{f_y}{140}\geq1.5$.
The minimum yield strength is therefore $1.5\cdot140=210\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
The value $240\,\mathrm{MPa}$ follows from using the Tresca equivalent stress, $100-(-60)=160\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
