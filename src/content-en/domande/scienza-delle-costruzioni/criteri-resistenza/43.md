At a point in a component under its design load, the von Mises utilization ratio is $\eta_0=1.25$. The loading varies proportionally. Which statement is correct?

- The load can increase by $25\%$ before the limit is reached
- The load can increase by $80\%$ before the limit is reached
- The limit is reached by reducing the load to $80\%$ of the stated value
- The given state is exactly at the limit

## Explanation

Under proportional loading the multiplier to the limit is $n=\dfrac{1}{\eta_0}=\dfrac{1}{1.25}=0.8$.
A multiplier below one means the given state is already beyond the limit.
The limit is therefore reached by reducing the load to $80\%$ of the given value.
The stated stress state would be exactly at the limit only for $\eta_0=1$.
