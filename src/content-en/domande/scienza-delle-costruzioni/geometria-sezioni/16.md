A T-section has a horizontal flange of width $100\,\mathrm{mm}$ and thickness $12\,\mathrm{mm}$ and a centered web below it of width $16\,\mathrm{mm}$ and height $92\,\mathrm{mm}$. Find $I$ about the vertical axis of symmetry. Round to the nearest $\mathrm{mm^4}$.

::struttura{poligoni="0,92 42,92 42,0 58,0 58,92 100,92 100,104 0,104" tratteggi="50,-8 50,112" titolo="T-section and vertical axis of symmetry" riferimento="0"}

- $1\,052\,651\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $1\,031\,403\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $1\,062\,805\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $1\,000\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$

## Explanation

For the vertical axis the horizontal width is cubed. Both rectangle centroids lie on the axis, so add $\dfrac{12 \times 100^3}{12}$ and $\dfrac{92 \times 16^3}{12}$ to obtain $1\,031\,403\,\mathrm{mm^4}$.
