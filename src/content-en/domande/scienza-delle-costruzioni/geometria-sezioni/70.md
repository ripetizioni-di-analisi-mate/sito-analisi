The Z-section shown has a web of height $100\,\mathrm{mm}$ and thickness $10\,\mathrm{mm}$ centered at the origin. The flanges have thickness $10\,\mathrm{mm}$ and project $40\,\mathrm{mm}$ beyond the web, the upper to the right and the lower to the left. Find the product of inertia $I_{yz}$ about axes $z$ and $y$.

::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="-45,-50 5,-50 5,40 45,40 45,50 -5,50 -5,-40 -45,-40" quote="5 50,45 50,40 mm,-8;55 -50,55 50,100 mm,0;-45 -50,-5 -50,40 mm,8" frecce="0,0,66,0,z;0,0,0,68,y" titolo="Z-section centered at the origin"}

- $-900\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $450\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $900\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $0\,\mathrm{mm^4}$

## Explanation

The web is symmetric about both axes and has zero product of inertia. Each flange of area $400\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ has zero centroidal product of inertia and centroid at either $(25,45)\,\mathrm{mm}$ or $(-25,-45)\,\mathrm{mm}$. By the parallel-axis theorem,
$$
I_{yz}=400\cdot45\cdot25+400\cdot(-45)\cdot(-25)\,\mathrm{mm^4}=900\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}.
$$
Both flanges lie in quadrants where the two coordinates have the same sign, so the product is positive.
