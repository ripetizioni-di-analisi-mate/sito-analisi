The angle section shown has a bottom leg of width $60\,\mathrm{mm}$ and thickness $10\,\mathrm{mm}$. The vertical leg is $10\,\mathrm{mm}$ wide and projects $40\,\mathrm{mm}$ above the bottom leg on its left side. The origin is at the lower left corner. Calculate the horizontal coordinate $z_G$.

::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="0,0 60,0 60,10 10,10 10,50 0,50" frecce="0,0,74,0,z;0,0,0,65,y" quote="0 0,60 0,60 mm,8;0 50,10 50,10 mm,-22;10 10,10 50,40 mm,10;60 0,60 10,10 mm,7" titolo="Angle section with bottom and vertical legs"}

- $30\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $17.5\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $35\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $20\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The nonoverlapping areas are $600$ and $400\,\mathrm{mm^2}$, with centroid coordinates $30$ and $5\,\mathrm{mm}$. Thus $z_G=\dfrac{600\cdot30+400\cdot5}{1\,000}$.
