A plane section with remaining area $600\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ has its centroid at $z=-4\,\mathrm{mm}$ relative to the design axis. A plate is added without overlap, with its centroid at $z=+12\,\mathrm{mm}$. What area must the plate have to bring the centroid back to $z=0$?

- $150\,\mathrm{mm^2}$
- $450\,\mathrm{mm^2}$
- $200\,\mathrm{mm^2}$
- $1800\,\mathrm{mm^2}$

## Explanation

A centroid at $z=0$ requires zero first moment about the axis. The remaining section contributes $600(-4)=-2400\,\mathrm{mm^3}$ and the plate contributes $12A$. Solving $12A=2400$ gives $A=200\,\mathrm{mm^2}$.
