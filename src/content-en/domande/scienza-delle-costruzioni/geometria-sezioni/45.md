A disk of radius $40\,\mathrm{mm}$ centered at the origin contains a circular hole of radius $20\,\mathrm{mm}$ centered at $(z,y)=(20,0)\,\mathrm{mm}$. Find the horizontal centroid coordinate $z_G$ of the perforated section.

::struttura{riferimento=0 cerchi="0,0,40;20,0,20,foro" punti="20,0,C,centro" frecce="0,0,56,0,z;0,0,0,54,y" titolo="Disk with an eccentric circular hole"}

- $\dfrac{20}{3}\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $-5\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $-\dfrac{20}{3}\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The section is symmetric about the $z$ axis, so $y_G=0$. The full disk has area $1\,600\pi\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ and centroid at the origin. Subtract the hole of area $400\pi\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ whose centroid is at $z=20\,\mathrm{mm}$. Thus
$$
z_G=\frac{1\,600\pi\cdot0-400\pi\cdot20}{1\,600\pi-400\pi}\,\mathrm{mm}=-\frac{20}{3}\,\mathrm{mm}.
$$
The centroid shifts away from the hole.
