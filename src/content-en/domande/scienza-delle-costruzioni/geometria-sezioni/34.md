A solid triangle has horizontal base $120\,\mathrm{mm}$ and height $90\,\mathrm{mm}$. The top vertex is directly above the left end of the base. Find the second moment of area about the horizontal centroidal axis.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 120,0 0,90" tratteggi="-8,30 128,30" titolo="Triangle and centroidal axis parallel to the base" riferimento="0"}

- $2\,430\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $1\,620\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $7\,290\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $4\,860\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$

## Explanation

About the base, $I = \dfrac{bh^3}{12}$. The centroid is $\dfrac{h}{3}$ from the base and the area is $\dfrac{bh}{2}$. Transferring the axis to the centroid subtracts $\left(\dfrac{bh}{2}\right)\left(\dfrac{h}{3}\right)^2$, leaving $\dfrac{bh^3}{36} = 2\,430\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$.
