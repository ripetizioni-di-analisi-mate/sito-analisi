A tubular steel post has mean diameter $200\,\mathrm{mm}$ and wall thickness $5\,\mathrm{mm}$. Model the wall as a line of constant thickness on the mean circumference. Find the second moment of area about a diameter.

- $10^7\pi\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $5\cdot10^6\pi\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $2.5\cdot10^6\pi\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $4\cdot10^7\pi\,\mathrm{mm^4}$

## Explanation

A length $R\,d\theta$ of wall has area $tR\,d\theta$ and elevation $R\sin\theta$. Thus
$$
I_z=\int_0^{2\pi}R^2\sin^2\theta\;tR\,d\theta=\pi R^3t .
$$
With $R=100\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $t=5\,\mathrm{mm}$ the result is $I_z=\pi\cdot10^6\cdot5=5\cdot10^6\pi\,\mathrm{mm^4}$. The exact value $\dfrac{\pi}{4}\left(102.5^4-97.5^4\right)$ differs by less than $0.1\%$. Twice this value, $2\pi R^3t$, is the polar second moment.
