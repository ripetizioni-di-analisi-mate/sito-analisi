The T-section shown has a web of width $20\,\mathrm{mm}$ and height $60\,\mathrm{mm}$ and a centered top flange of width $80\,\mathrm{mm}$ and thickness $20\,\mathrm{mm}$. Find the first moment of the flange area alone about the horizontal centroidal axis of the full section.

::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="-10,0 10,0 10,60 40,60 40,80 -40,80 -40,60 -10,60" frecce="0,0,58,0,z;0,0,0,96,y" quote="-40 80,40 80,80 mm,-16;40 60,40 80,20 mm,8;10 0,10 60,60 mm,8;-10 0,10 0,20 mm,9" titolo="T-section with the origin at the foot of the web"}

- $\dfrac{192\,000}{7}\,\mathrm{mm^3}$
- $112\,000\,\mathrm{mm^3}$
- $\dfrac{304\,000}{7}\,\mathrm{mm^3}$
- $\dfrac{592\,000}{7}\,\mathrm{mm^3}$

## Explanation

With the origin at the foot of the web, the web has area $1\,200\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ at elevation $30\,\mathrm{mm}$ and the flange has area $1\,600\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ at elevation $70\,\mathrm{mm}$. Thus
$$
y_G=\frac{1\,200\cdot30+1\,600\cdot70}{2\,800}\,\mathrm{mm}=\frac{370}{7}\,\mathrm{mm}.
$$
The flange centroid is $70-\dfrac{370}{7}=\dfrac{120}{7}\,\mathrm{mm}$ from the centroidal axis, so the flange's first moment of area is $1\,600\cdot\dfrac{120}{7}=\dfrac{192\,000}{7}\,\mathrm{mm^3}\simeq27\,430\,\mathrm{mm^3}$. This first moment enters the Jourawski shear formula at the flange–web junction.
