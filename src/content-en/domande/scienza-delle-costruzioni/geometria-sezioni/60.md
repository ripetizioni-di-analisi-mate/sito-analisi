A section of area $2\,000\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ has second moment of area $8\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$ about horizontal axis $a$. Its centroid is $30\,\mathrm{mm}$ above $a$. Find the second moment of area about the horizontal axis $b$ a distance $50\,\mathrm{mm}$ above the centroid.

- $20.8\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $13\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $11.2\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $8.8\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$

## Explanation

The parallel-axis theorem relates any axis to the parallel centroidal axis, so the calculation proceeds via the centroid. First obtain
$$
I_G=I_a-Ad_a^2=8\cdot10^6-2\,000\cdot30^2=6.2\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}.
$$
Then transfer the result to axis $b$ to obtain $I_b=I_G+Ad_b^2=6.2\cdot10^6+2\,000\cdot50^2=11.2\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$. The formula cannot be applied directly between $a$ and $b$ because neither axis passes through the centroid.
