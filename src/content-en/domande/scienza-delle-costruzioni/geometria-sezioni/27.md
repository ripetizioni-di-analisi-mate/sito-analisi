Two identical horizontal rectangular plates of width $60\,\mathrm{mm}$ and thickness $10\,\mathrm{mm}$ lie one above the other. Other elements make a negligible contribution. Their combined second moment of area about the horizontal centroidal axis must equal $1\,090\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$. Find the clear vertical distance between the plates.

- $50\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $30\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $60\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $70\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

Each plate has centroidal second moment $\dfrac{bt^3}{12}$ and parallel-axis term $bt\cdot d^2$, where $d$ is its centroid distance from the common axis. Solving $I = 2\left(\dfrac{bt^3}{12} + bt\cdot d^2\right)$ gives $d = 30\,\mathrm{mm}$. The centroid spacing is $2d$, so the clear gap is $2d - t = 50\,\mathrm{mm}$.
