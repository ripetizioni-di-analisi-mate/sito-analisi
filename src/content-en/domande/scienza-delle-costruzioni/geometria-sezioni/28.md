A rectangular section has width $40\,\mathrm{mm}$ and depth $80\,\mathrm{mm}$. Find its polar second moment of area about its centroid. Round to the nearest $\mathrm{mm^4}$.

- $426\,667\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $1\,759\,192\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $2\,133\,333\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $1\,706\,667\,\mathrm{mm^4}$

## Explanation

The squared distance from the centroid is $x^2 + y^2$ and its integral is $I_p = I_x + I_y = \dfrac{bh^3}{12} + \dfrac{hb^3}{12} = 2\,133\,333\,\mathrm{mm^4}$. For a rectangle the polar second moment of area and the torsion constant are distinct quantities.
