A composite section has area $1800\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ of material $1$ with $E_1 = 30\,\mathrm{GPa}$ and area $600\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ of material $2$ with $E_2 = 210\,\mathrm{GPa}$. The materials are perfectly bonded and linearly elastic. Find the area transformed to material $1$ that gives the same axial rigidity.

- $16\,800\,\mathrm{mm^2}$
- $2\,400\,\mathrm{mm^2}$
- $6\,000\,\mathrm{mm^2}$
- $1\,886\,\mathrm{mm^2}$

## Explanation

The materials share the same longitudinal strain and their axial rigidities add. Thus $E_1 A_{\mathrm{om}} = E_1 A_1 + E_2 A_2$. The ratio $\dfrac{E_2}{E_1}$ is $7.0$ and the transformed area is $6\,000\,\mathrm{mm^2}$.
