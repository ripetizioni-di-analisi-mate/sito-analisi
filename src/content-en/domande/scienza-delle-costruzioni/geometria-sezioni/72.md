The parallelogram shown has horizontal base $60\,\mathrm{mm}$ and height $40\,\mathrm{mm}$. Its top side is displaced $30\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right relative to its bottom side. Find the product of inertia about centroidal axes parallel to $z$ and $y$.

::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="0,0 60,0 90,40 30,40" frecce="0,0,106,0,z;0,0,0,54,y" quote="0 0,60 0,60 mm,8;0 40,30 40,30 mm,-8;90 0,90 40,40 mm,9" titolo="Parallelogram with horizontal sides"}

- $0\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $240\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $-240\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $960\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$

## Explanation

Measure coordinates $(\zeta,\eta)$ from the centroid. At elevation $\eta$ a horizontal strip has length $60\,\mathrm{mm}$ and center at $\zeta=\dfrac{30}{40}\,\eta$ because shifting the horizontal strips of a rectangle forms the parallelogram. Each strip therefore contributes $\dfrac34\,\eta\cdot\eta\cdot60\,d\eta$, giving
$$
I_{yz,G}=\frac34\int_{-20}^{20}60\,\eta^2\,d\eta=\frac34\cdot\frac{60\cdot40^3}{12}\,\mathrm{mm^4}=240\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}.
$$
The sign is positive because the upper strips are displaced to the right and the lower strips to the left.
