A section has principal second moments of area $I_1=9\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$ and $I_2=3\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$. Over all orthogonal pairs of centroidal axes, find the largest absolute product of inertia.

- $3\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $6\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $12\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $1.5\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$

## Explanation

Use the principal axes as reference, so $I_z=I_1$, $I_y=I_2$, and $I_{yz}=0$. The transformation law for the product of inertia gives the following expression for axes rotated by $\theta$.
$$
I_{y^\prime z^\prime}=\frac{I_1-I_2}{2}\sin(2\theta).
$$
The magnitude is largest when $\sin(2\theta)=\pm1$, that is, for axes inclined at $45^\circ$ to the principal axes. The maximum is therefore $\dfrac{9-3}{2}\cdot10^6=3\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$.
