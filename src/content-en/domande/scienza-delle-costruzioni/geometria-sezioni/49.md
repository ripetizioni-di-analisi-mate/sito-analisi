An IPE 200 section has area $2\,850\,\mathrm{mm^2}$, depth $200\,\mathrm{mm}$, and centroid at mid-depth. A plate of width $150\,\mathrm{mm}$ and thickness $10\,\mathrm{mm}$ is welded to its top flange. How far above the bottom fiber of the original section is the centroid of the reinforced section?

::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="-50,0 50,0 50,8 3,8 3,192 50,192 50,200 -50,200 -50,192 -3,192 -3,8 -50,8;-75,200 75,200 75,210 -75,210" frecce="0,0,72,0,z;0,0,0,228,y" quote="-75 210,75 210,150 mm,-16;75 200,75 210,10 mm,9;50 0,50 200,200 mm,14" titolo="IPE section with a plate welded to its top flange"}

- $152.5\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $100\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $136.2\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $168.8\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The plate has area $1\,500\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ and centroid elevation $200+5=205\,\mathrm{mm}$. The original section contributes $2\,850\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ at elevation $100\,\mathrm{mm}$. Thus
$$
y_G=\frac{2\,850\cdot100+1\,500\cdot205}{4\,350}\,\mathrm{mm}\simeq136.2\,\mathrm{mm}.
$$
The centroid moves up toward the plate and stays closer to the centroid of the original section, which has the larger area.
