An inverted T-section has a bottom flange of width $90\,\mathrm{mm}$ and thickness $15\,\mathrm{mm}$ and a centered web of width $15\,\mathrm{mm}$ and height $75\,\mathrm{mm}$. Which relationship holds between its extreme-fiber section moduli about the horizontal centroidal axis?

::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="-45,0 45,0 45,15 7.5,15 7.5,90 -7.5,90 -7.5,15 -45,15" frecce="0,0,62,0,z;0,0,0,104,y" quote="-45 0,45 0,90 mm,8;45 0,45 15,15 mm,8;7.5 15,7.5 90,75 mm,9;-7.5 90,7.5 90,15 mm,-16" titolo="T-section with its flange at the bottom"}

- $W_{\mathrm{upper}}>W_{\mathrm{lower}}$, because the top fiber is farther from the centroid
- $W_{\mathrm{upper}}=W_{\mathrm{lower}}$, because both section moduli have the same $I_z$
- $W_{\mathrm{upper}}>W_{\mathrm{lower}}$, because the section is narrower at the top
- $W_{\mathrm{upper}}<W_{\mathrm{lower}}$, because the top fiber is farther from the centroid

## Explanation

With origin at the bottom fiber, the flange has area $1\,350\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ at elevation $7.5\,\mathrm{mm}$ and the web has area $1\,125\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ at elevation $52.5\,\mathrm{mm}$. Thus
$$
y_G=\frac{1\,350\cdot7.5+1\,125\cdot52.5}{2\,475}\,\mathrm{mm}\simeq27.95\,\mathrm{mm},
$$
and the top fiber is $90-27.95\simeq62.05\,\mathrm{mm}$ from the axis. The two section moduli have the same numerator $I_z$, so the farther fiber has the smaller modulus. It follows that $W_{\mathrm{upper}}<W_{\mathrm{lower}}$.
