The right triangle shown has a leg of length $60\,\mathrm{mm}$ on the $z$ axis, a leg of length $30\,\mathrm{mm}$ on the $y$ axis, and its right angle at the origin. Find the product of inertia $I_{yz}$ about axes $z$ and $y$.

::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="0,0 60,0 0,30" frecce="0,0,76,0,z;0,0,0,44,y" quote="0 0,60 0,60 mm,8;0 0,0 30,30 mm,-9" titolo="Right triangle with its legs on the coordinate axes"}

- $180\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $45\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $810\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $135\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$

## Explanation

At coordinate $z$ between $0$ and $60\,\mathrm{mm}$, the triangle extends from height $0$ to $30\left(1-\dfrac{z}{60}\right)$. Integrating first with respect to $y$ gives
$$
I_{yz}=\int_0^{60}z\cdot\frac{1}{2}\left[30\left(1-\frac{z}{60}\right)\right]^2dz=\frac{60^2\cdot30^2}{24}\,\mathrm{mm^4}=135\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}.
$$
The product $Az_Gy_G=900\cdot20\cdot10=180\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$ gives a different value because the centroidal product of inertia of the right triangle is nonzero.
