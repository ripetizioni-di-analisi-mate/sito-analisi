A rectangle centered at the origin has width $80\,\mathrm{mm}$ along $z$ and height $40\,\mathrm{mm}$ along $y$. The $z^\prime$ axis makes an angle of $30^\circ$ with $z$, measured from $z$ toward $y$. Calculate $I_{z^\prime}$.

::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="-40,-20 40,-20 40,20 -40,20" frecce="0,0,56,0,z;0,0,0,32,y;0,0,48,27.713,z′" quote="-40 -20,40 -20,80 mm,8;40 -20,40 20,40 mm,26" titolo="Rectangle and the z-prime axis inclined at 30°"}

- $\dfrac{1\,280\,000}{3}\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $\dfrac{4\,160\,000}{3}\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $\dfrac{2\,240\,000}{3}\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $\dfrac{2\,560\,000}{3}\,\mathrm{mm^4}$

## Explanation

The original axes are principal, so $I_{z^\prime}=\dfrac34 I_z+\dfrac14 I_y$. The two initial values are $\dfrac{1\,280\,000}{3}$ and $\dfrac{5\,120\,000}{3}\,\mathrm{mm^4}$.
