The rectangular tube shown has outside width $100\,\mathrm{mm}$, outside depth $160\,\mathrm{mm}$, and uniform wall thickness $10\,\mathrm{mm}$. Find the first moment of its upper half about the horizontal centroidal axis.

::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="0,0 100,0 100,160 0,160" fori="10,10 90,10 90,150 10,150" frecce="0,0,118,0,z;0,0,0,178,y" quote="0 160,100 160,100 mm,-9;100 0,100 160,160 mm,10;100 150,100 160,10 mm,-9" titolo="Rectangular tube of uniform wall thickness"}

- $75\,000\,\mathrm{mm^3}$
- $99\,500\,\mathrm{mm^3}$
- $320\,000\,\mathrm{mm^3}$
- $124\,000\,\mathrm{mm^3}$

## Explanation

Double symmetry places the horizontal centroidal axis at mid-depth, so the upper half spans elevations $0$ to $80\,\mathrm{mm}$ relative to it. This half comprises the horizontal wall $100\times10\,\mathrm{mm}$ with centroid at elevation $75\,\mathrm{mm}$ and two vertical wall segments $10\times70\,\mathrm{mm}$ with centroid at elevation $35\,\mathrm{mm}$. Hence
$$
S=1\,000\cdot75+2\cdot700\cdot35\,\mathrm{mm^3}=124\,000\,\mathrm{mm^3}.
$$
This is the value used in the Jourawski formula for shear stress on the centroidal axis.
