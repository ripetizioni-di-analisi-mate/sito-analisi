The isosceles triangle shown has horizontal base $60\,\mathrm{mm}$ and height $90\,\mathrm{mm}$, with the apex at the top. Find its elastic section modulus at the top fiber, the apex, about the horizontal centroidal axis.

::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="0,0 60,0 30,90" frecce="0,0,76,0,z;0,0,0,104,y" quote="0 0,60 0,60 mm,8;60 0,60 90,90 mm,9" titolo="Isosceles triangle with the apex at the top"}

- $40\,500\,\mathrm{mm^3}$
- $20\,250\,\mathrm{mm^3}$
- $27\,000\,\mathrm{mm^3}$
- $81\,000\,\mathrm{mm^3}$

## Explanation

The centroidal second moment of a triangle about an axis parallel to its base is
$$
I_z=\frac{bh^3}{36}=\frac{60\cdot90^3}{36}\,\mathrm{mm^4}=1\,215\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}.
$$
The centroid is one third of the height above the base, so the apex is $\dfrac23\cdot90=60\,\mathrm{mm}$ from the axis. Thus $W_{\mathrm{upper}}=\dfrac{1\,215\,000}{60}=20\,250\,\mathrm{mm^3}$, half the section modulus at the bottom fiber.
