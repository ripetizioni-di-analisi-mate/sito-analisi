A rectangular beam of width $50\,\mathrm{mm}$ must have section modulus $W=120\,000\,\mathrm{mm^3}$ about the horizontal centroidal axis. What depth $h$ is required?

- $120\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $169.7\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $49.0\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $84.9\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

For a rectangle $I_z=\dfrac{bh^3}{12}$ and the extreme fibers are $\dfrac h2$ from the axis, giving $W=\dfrac{bh^2}{6}$. Setting $\dfrac{50h^2}{6}=120\,000$ yields $h^2=14\,400\,\mathrm{mm^2}$, so $h=120\,\mathrm{mm}$.
