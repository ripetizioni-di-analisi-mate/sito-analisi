A rectangular box section has width $60\,\mathrm{mm}$, depth $120\,\mathrm{mm}$, and uniform thickness $6\,\mathrm{mm}$. Neglecting corner radii, find the square of the radius of gyration about the weak centroidal axis. Round to one decimal place.

- $577.7\,\mathrm{mm^2}$
- $1\,786.3\,\mathrm{mm^2}$
- $161.8\,\mathrm{mm^2}$
- $300.0\,\mathrm{mm^2}$

## Explanation

The vertical axis is the weak axis. The second moment is $I_y = 1\,164\,672\,\mathrm{mm^4}$ and the net area is $A = 2\,016\,\mathrm{mm^2}$. The squared radius of gyration is $\dfrac{I_y}{A} = 577.7\,\mathrm{mm^2}$.
