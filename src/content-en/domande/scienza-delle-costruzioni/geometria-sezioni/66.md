For which section is the product of inertia $I_{yz}$ about centroidal axes parallel to its sides zero for all choices of its dimensions?

- An equal-leg angle
- A T-section with the flange centered on the web
- A Z-section
- An unequal-leg angle

## Explanation

The product of inertia vanishes when either reference axis is an axis of symmetry. In a T-section with a centered flange, the vertical centroidal axis is an axis of symmetry. Each element at $(z,y)$ has an equal counterpart at $(-z,y)$ and their contributions cancel. In the angles and the Z-section, the component centroids occupy quadrants where all parallel-axis terms have the same sign, so $I_{yz}$ is nonzero. An equal-leg angle is symmetric about its bisector, which is inclined to its legs.
