The I-section shown has a bottom flange of width $60\,\mathrm{mm}$, a top flange of width $100\,\mathrm{mm}$, and a web of height $80\,\mathrm{mm}$. The flanges and web have thickness $10\,\mathrm{mm}$, and the overall depth is $100\,\mathrm{mm}$. Find the centroid height $y_G$ above the bottom fiber.

::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="-30,0 30,0 30,10 5,10 5,90 50,90 50,100 -50,100 -50,90 -5,90 -5,10 -30,10" frecce="0,0,66,0,z;0,0,0,116,y" quote="-50 100,50 100,100 mm,-16;-30 0,30 0,60 mm,8;50 0,50 100,100 mm,12" titolo="I-section with unequal flanges"}

- $42.5\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $50\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $57.5\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $86.25\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The three areas are $600$, $800$, and $1\,000\,\mathrm{mm^2}$, with centroid elevations $5$, $50$, and $95\,\mathrm{mm}$. Their total area is $2\,400\,\mathrm{mm^2}$. Thus
$$
y_G=\frac{600\cdot5+800\cdot50+1\,000\cdot95}{2\,400}\,\mathrm{mm}=57.5\,\mathrm{mm}.
$$
The centroid is above mid-depth because the top flange has a larger area than the bottom flange.
