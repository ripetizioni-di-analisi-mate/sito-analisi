For the same I-section the tabulated first moments are $S_0=72\,000\,\mathrm{mm^3}$ about its base and $S_a=24\,000\,\mathrm{mm^3}$ about a parallel line $20\,\mathrm{mm}$ above it. Heights are measured upward. What is its centroid height?

- $10\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $40\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $30\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $60\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

Moving the origin upward reduces the first moment by $20A$. The readings give $A=\dfrac{72\,000-24\,000}{20}=2400\,\mathrm{mm^2}$. The height is therefore $\dfrac{72\,000}{2400}=30\,\mathrm{mm}$.
