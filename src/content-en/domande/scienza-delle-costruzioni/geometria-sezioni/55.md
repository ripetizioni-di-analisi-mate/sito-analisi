A solid semicircle of radius $30\,\mathrm{mm}$ lies above the $z$ axis, with its diameter on that axis from $z=0$ to $z=60\,\mathrm{mm}$. Find the product of inertia $I_{yz}$ about axes $z$ and $y$. Round to the nearest $\mathrm{mm^4}$.

::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="0.00,0.00 1.02,7.76 4.02,15.00 8.79,21.21 15.00,25.98 22.24,28.98 30.00,30.00 37.76,28.98 45.00,25.98 51.21,21.21 55.98,15.00 58.98,7.76 60.00,0.00" frecce="0,0,76,0,z;0,0,0,46,y" quote="0 -6,60 -6,60 mm,0" titolo="Semicircle with its diameter on the z axis"}

- $0\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $636\,173\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $1\,696\,460\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $540\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$

## Explanation

The semicircle is symmetric about the vertical line $z=30\,\mathrm{mm}$, so its product of inertia about centroidal axes parallel to $z$ and $y$ is zero. Its centroid coordinates are $z_G=30\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $y_G=\dfrac{4R}{3\pi}=\dfrac{40}{\pi}\,\mathrm{mm}$ and its area is $\dfrac{\pi\cdot30^2}{2}=450\pi\,\mathrm{mm^2}$. By the parallel-axis theorem,
$$
I_{yz}=0+450\pi\cdot\frac{40}{\pi}\cdot30\,\mathrm{mm^4}=540\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}.
$$
The product of inertia is positive because the entire section lies in the quadrant where $z$ and $y$ are positive.
