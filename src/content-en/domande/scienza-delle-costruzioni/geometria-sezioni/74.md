A square of side $60\,\mathrm{mm}$ is rotated by $45^\circ$ so that one diagonal is horizontal. Find its second moment of area about the horizontal diagonal.

::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="-42.43,0 0,42.43 42.43,0 0,-42.43" frecce="0,0,60,0,z;0,0,0,58,y" quote="0 -42.43,42.43 0,60 mm,8" titolo="Square with a horizontal diagonal"}

- $540\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $1\,080\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $2\,160\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $1\,527\,351\,\mathrm{mm^4}$

## Explanation

About axes parallel to its sides, the square has $I_z=I_y=\dfrac{60^4}{12}$ and zero product of inertia by symmetry. With $I_y=I_z$ and $I_{yz}=0$ the rotation formula gives $I_{z^\prime}=I_z\left(\cos^2\theta+\sin^2\theta\right)=I_z$ for every angle $\theta$. Every centroidal axis is therefore principal, and the second moment about the diagonal is
$$
I=\frac{60^4}{12}\,\mathrm{mm^4}=1\,080\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}.
$$
