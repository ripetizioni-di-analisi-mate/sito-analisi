Two circular reinforcing bars of diameter $20\,\mathrm{mm}$ have centers at $(z,y)=(30,30)\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $(z,y)=(-30,-30)\,\mathrm{mm}$. Find the product of inertia of both bars about axes $z$ and $y$.

::struttura{riferimento=0 cerchi="30,30,10;-30,-30,10" frecce="0,0,56,0,z;0,0,0,56,y" tratteggi="30,0 30,30 0,30;-30,0 -30,-30 0,-30" titolo="Two bars placed diagonally"}

- $0\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $-180\,000\pi\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $90\,000\pi\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $180\,000\pi\,\mathrm{mm^4}$

## Explanation

Each bar is symmetric about axes through its own center parallel to $z$ and $y$, so its centroidal product of inertia is zero. Each bar has area $100\pi\,\mathrm{mm^2}$. The parallel-axis contribution of the first is $100\pi\cdot30\cdot30=90\,000\pi\,\mathrm{mm^4}$. Both coordinates of the second bar are negative, so their product is positive and the second contribution is identical. Thus $I_{yz}=180\,000\pi\,\mathrm{mm^4}$.
