A section has centroidal radii of gyration $\rho_z=40\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $\rho_y=30\,\mathrm{mm}$. Its polar radius of gyration $\rho_p$ about the centroid is defined by $A\rho_p^2=I_p$. Find $\rho_p$.

- $70\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $35\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $10\sqrt7\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $50\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

By definition of the radii of gyration $I_z=A\rho_z^2$ and $I_y=A\rho_y^2$. The centroidal polar second moment is $I_p=I_z+I_y$, so $A\rho_p^2=A\left(\rho_z^2+\rho_y^2\right)$. Hence $\rho_p^2=1\,600+900=2\,500\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ and $\rho_p=50\,\mathrm{mm}$. The squares of the radii add because the second moments add.
