A U-section opens upward. Its outside width and depth are $80$ and $60\,\mathrm{mm}$, and its base and vertical sides have thickness $10\,\mathrm{mm}$. Find the elastic section modulus for the top fibers about the horizontal centroidal axis. Round to the nearest $\mathrm{mm^3}$.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 80,0 80,60 70,60 70,10 10,10 10,60 0,60" tratteggi="-8,21.6667 88,21.6667" titolo="Upward-opening U-section" riferimento="0"}

- $10\,250\,\mathrm{mm^3}$
- $21\,261\,\mathrm{mm^3}$
- $28\,385\,\mathrm{mm^3}$
- $16\,043\,\mathrm{mm^3}$

## Explanation

Subtract the central opening $60 \times 50\,\mathrm{mm}$ from the outer rectangle. Its center is at elevation $35.0\,\mathrm{mm}$. The area-weighted centroid is at elevation $21.67\,\mathrm{mm}$. Transferring both second moments to this elevation gives $I = 615\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$. The top section modulus is $\dfrac{I}{h - y} = 16\,043\,\mathrm{mm^3}$.
