An isosceles triangle has horizontal base $80\,\mathrm{mm}$, height $60\,\mathrm{mm}$, and its apex at the top. Find its second moment of area about the vertical axis of symmetry.

::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="-40,0 40,0 0,60" tratteggi="0,-8 0,70" frecce="0,0,56,0,z;0,0,0,78,y" quote="-40 0,40 0,80 mm,8;40 0,40 60,60 mm,9" titolo="Isosceles triangle and its axis of symmetry"}

- $480\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $640\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $2\,560\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $853\,333\,\mathrm{mm^4}$

## Explanation

With origin at the midpoint of the base, the triangle has height $60\left(1-\dfrac{z}{40}\right)$ at coordinate $z$ between $0$ and $40\,\mathrm{mm}$. The two halves about the $y$ axis are symmetric and contribute equally, so
$$
I_y=2\int_0^{40}z^2\cdot60\left(1-\frac{z}{40}\right)dz=120\left(\frac{40^3}{3}-\frac{40^3}{4}\right)\mathrm{mm^4}=640\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}.
$$
This value equals $\dfrac{hb^3}{48}$. The formula $\dfrac{bh^3}{36}$ is for the centroidal axis parallel to the base.
