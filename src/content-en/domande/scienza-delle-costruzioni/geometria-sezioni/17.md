The midline of a thin strip of length $120\,\mathrm{mm}$ and thickness $6\,\mathrm{mm}$ is inclined at $30^\circ$ to the horizontal. Neglect terms containing the cube of the thickness. Find the strip's second moment of area about its horizontal centroidal axis $z$.

::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="-50.46,-32.60 53.46,27.40 50.46,32.60 -53.46,-27.40" tratteggi="-62,0 0,0" frecce="0,0,72,0,z;0,0,0,50,y" quote="-53.46 -27.40,50.46 32.60,120 mm,-12" etichette="22,5,30°" titolo="Inclined thin strip"}

- $216\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $864\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $648\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $432\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$

## Explanation

Let $s$ measure position along the midline from the centroid, with $s$ between $-60$ and $60\,\mathrm{mm}$. A length $ds$ of the strip has area $6\,ds$ and elevation $y=s\sin30^\circ$. Thus
$$
I_z=\int_{-60}^{60}6\,s^2\sin^2 30^\circ\,ds=\frac{6\cdot120^3}{12}\cdot\frac{1}{4}\,\mathrm{mm^4}=216\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}.
$$
Inclination reduces distances from the axis by a factor $\sin30^\circ$, so the second moment of area is reduced by a factor $\sin^2 30^\circ=\dfrac14$ relative to the vertical strip.
