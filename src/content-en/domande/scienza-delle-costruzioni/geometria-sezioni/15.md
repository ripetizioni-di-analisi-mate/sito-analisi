A solid circular section of radius $50\,\mathrm{mm}$ is centered at the origin. The horizontal line at elevation $y=30\,\mathrm{mm}$ cuts off an upper circular segment. Find its first moment of area about the horizontal diameter $z$. Round to the nearest $\mathrm{mm^3}$.

::struttura{riferimento=0 cerchi="0,0,50" linee="-40,30 40,30" frecce="0,0,70,0,z;0,0,0,70,y" quote="52 0,52 30,30 mm,8;-50 -58,50 -58,100 mm,0" titolo="Circular section and chord at elevation 30 mm"}

- $64\,000\,\mathrm{mm^3}$
- $83\,333\,\mathrm{mm^3}$
- $42\,667\,\mathrm{mm^3}$
- $9\,120\,\mathrm{mm^3}$

## Explanation

At elevation $y$ the width is $2\sqrt{2\,500-y^2}$, so a horizontal strip of thickness $dy$ has area $2\sqrt{2\,500-y^2}\,dy$. The segment extends from $30$ to $50\,\mathrm{mm}$, giving
$$
S_z=\int_{30}^{50}2y\sqrt{2\,500-y^2}\,dy=\left[-\frac{2}{3}\left(2\,500-y^2\right)^{\frac{3}{2}}\right]_{30}^{50}=\frac{2}{3}\cdot1\,600^{\frac{3}{2}}\,\mathrm{mm^3}.
$$
Since $1\,600^{\frac{3}{2}}=64\,000$, the result is $S_z=\dfrac{128\,000}{3}\,\mathrm{mm^3}\simeq42\,667\,\mathrm{mm^3}$. The chord width $80\,\mathrm{mm}$ applies only at elevation $30\,\mathrm{mm}$ and decreases toward the upper edge.
