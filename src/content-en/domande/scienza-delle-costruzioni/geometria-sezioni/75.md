About its centroidal axes a section has $I_z=10\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$, $I_y=4\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$, and $I_{yz}=4\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$. Find its smaller principal second moment of area.

- $4\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $2\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $3\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $12\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$

## Explanation

The mean of the two second moments is $\dfrac{10+4}{2}\cdot10^6=7\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$ and the radical is $\sqrt{3^2+4^2}\cdot10^6=5\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$. Hence
$$
I_{\min}=7\cdot10^6-5\cdot10^6=2\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}.
$$
The value is smaller than $I_y$ because $I_{\min}$ is the minimum second moment over all centroidal axes.
