The Z-section shown has a vertical web of height $100\,\mathrm{mm}$ and thickness $10\,\mathrm{mm}$. The two identical flanges have thickness $10\,\mathrm{mm}$ and project $40\,\mathrm{mm}$ beyond the web in opposite directions. Where is the centroid?

::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="-45,-50 5,-50 5,40 45,40 45,50 -5,50 -5,-40 -45,-40" quote="5 50,45 50,40 mm,-8;55 -50,55 50,100 mm,0;-45 -50,-5 -50,40 mm,8" frecce="-62,-64,-38,-64,z;-62,-64,-62,-40,y" titolo="Z-section with equal flanges"}

- On the web axis above its midpoint
- At the midpoint of the web
- Outside the section, to the right of the web
- At the centroid of the top flange

## Explanation

A rotation of $180^\circ$ about the web midpoint $O$ maps the web onto itself and interchanges the flanges. For every area element at $(z,y)$, with coordinates relative to $O$, there is an equal element at $(-z,-y)$. Their contributions to $S_z$ and $S_y$ have opposite signs, so both first moments about axes through $O$ are zero. The centroid therefore coincides with $O$ even though the section has no axis of symmetry.
