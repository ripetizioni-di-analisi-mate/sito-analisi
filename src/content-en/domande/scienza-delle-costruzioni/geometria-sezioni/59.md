The rectangle shown has width $120\,\mathrm{mm}$ and height $200\,\mathrm{mm}$. A circular hole may be made at $A$, a distance $20\,\mathrm{mm}$ above the center, or at $B$, a distance $70\,\mathrm{mm}$ above the center. The two possible holes have equal size. Which one reduces the second moment of area more about the horizontal axis $z$ through the center of the rectangle?

::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="-60,-100 60,-100 60,100 -60,100" cerchi="0,20,15,foro;0,70,15,foro" etichette="22,20,A;22,70,B" frecce="0,0,80,0,z;0,0,0,118,y" quote="60 0,60 20,20 mm,10;60 0,60 70,70 mm,26" titolo="Two possible locations of the same hole"}

- The hole at $A$
- Both give the same reduction because the holes have the same area
- The hole at $B$
- It depends on the diameter. For small holes, the hole at $A$ gives the greater reduction

## Explanation

The reduction equals the hole's second moment about $z$. By the parallel-axis theorem it is $I_{f,G}+A_fd^2$, with the same $I_{f,G}$ and $A_f$ for either hole. The term $A_fd^2$ increases with distance $d$ from the axis. That distance is $20\,\mathrm{mm}$ at $A$ and $70\,\mathrm{mm}$ at $B$. Thus the hole at $B$ removes more second moment of area for any hole diameter.
