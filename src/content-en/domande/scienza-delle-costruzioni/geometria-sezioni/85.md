A solid shaft of radius $40\,\mathrm{mm}$ bends in a plane containing its axis. Which elastic section modulus applies to the fibers farthest from the neutral axis?

- $16\,000\pi\,\mathrm{mm^3}$
- $32\,000\pi\,\mathrm{mm^3}$
- $8\,000\pi\,\mathrm{mm^3}$
- $64\,000\pi\,\mathrm{mm^3}$

## Explanation

The diameter is $d=80\,\mathrm{mm}$ and the diametral second moment is $\dfrac{\pi d^4}{64}$. Both extreme fibers are $\dfrac d2=40\,\mathrm{mm}$ from the axis, so the two section moduli are equal. Thus
$$
W=\frac{\pi d^4}{64}\cdot\frac{2}{d}=\frac{\pi d^3}{32}=16\,000\pi\,\mathrm{mm^3}\simeq50\,265\,\mathrm{mm^3}.
$$
