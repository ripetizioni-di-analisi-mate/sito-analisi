A square tube has outside side length $60\,\mathrm{mm}$ and a concentric square hole of side $40\,\mathrm{mm}$ with parallel sides. Find its polar second moment of area about the centroid.

::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="-30,-30 30,-30 30,30 -30,30" fori="-20,-20 20,-20 20,20 -20,20" frecce="0,0,44,0,z;0,0,0,44,y" quote="-30 -30,30 -30,60 mm,8;-20 20,20 20,40 mm,5" titolo="Square tube with a concentric hole"}

- $\dfrac{5\,200\,000}{3}\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $\dfrac{2\,600\,000}{3}\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $\dfrac{10\,400\,000}{3}\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $1\,021\,018\,\mathrm{mm^4}$

## Explanation

A square of side $a$ has equal centroidal moments $\dfrac{a^4}{12}$ and polar second moment $\dfrac{a^4}{6}$. Subtract the polar second moment of the concentric hole about the same point. Thus
$$
I_p=\frac{60^4-40^4}{6}\,\mathrm{mm^4}=\frac{5\,200\,000}{3}\,\mathrm{mm^4}\simeq1.73\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}.
$$
