A solid quarter circle of radius $40\,\mathrm{mm}$ has its center at the origin and its straight sides on the positive $z$ and $y$ axes. Find the product of inertia about axes $z$ and $y$.

::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="0,0 40.00,0.00 39.39,6.95 37.59,13.68 34.64,20.00 30.64,25.71 25.71,30.64 20.00,34.64 13.68,37.59 6.95,39.39 0.00,40.00" frecce="0,0,56,0,z;0,0,0,56,y" quote="0 0,40 0,40 mm,8" titolo="Quarter circle in the first quadrant"}

- $640\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $502\,655\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $362\,166\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $320\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$

## Explanation

In polar coordinates $z=\rho\cos\theta$, $y=\rho\sin\theta$, and $dA=\rho\,d\rho\,d\theta$, with $\rho$ between $0$ and $40\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $\theta$ between $0$ and $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. Thus
$$
I_{yz}=\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\sin\theta\cos\theta\,d\theta\int_0^{40}\rho^3\,d\rho=\frac12\cdot\frac{40^4}{4}\,\mathrm{mm^4}=320\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}.
$$
