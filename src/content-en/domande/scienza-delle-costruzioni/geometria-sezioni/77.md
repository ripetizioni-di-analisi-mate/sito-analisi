A section table gives area $A=28.5\,\mathrm{cm^2}$ and centroidal second moment of area $I_{z,G}=864\,\mathrm{cm^4}$. Find the second moment of area about the axis parallel to $z_G$ at a distance $120\,\mathrm{mm}$ from the centroid.

- $12.74\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $41.13\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $49.68\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $9.05\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$

## Explanation

Convert all dimensions to millimeters before using the parallel-axis theorem. Since $1\,\mathrm{cm^2}=10^2\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ and $1\,\mathrm{cm^4}=10^4\,\mathrm{mm^4}$, we have $A=2\,850\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ and $I_{z,G}=8.64\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$. Thus
$$
I_z=8.64\cdot10^6+2\,850\cdot120^2\,\mathrm{mm^4}=\left(8.64+41.04\right)\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}=49.68\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}.
$$
