About centroidal axes $z$ and $y$ a section has $I_z=6\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$ and $I_y=2\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$. After rotating the axes about the centroid, $I_{z^\prime}=5\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$. Find $I_{y^\prime}$ in the same rotated coordinate system.

- $3\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $2\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $4\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$

## Explanation

The sum $I_z+I_y$ is the polar second moment about the centroid, independent of axis orientation. Thus $I_{z^\prime}+I_{y^\prime}=I_z+I_y=8\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$, giving $I_{y^\prime}=8\cdot10^6-5\cdot10^6=3\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$.
