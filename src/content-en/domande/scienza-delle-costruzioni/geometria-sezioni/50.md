In the equal-flange I-section shown, a circular hole is made in the web on the vertical axis of symmetry above the initial centroid $G_0$. How does the centroid of the perforated section move relative to $G_0$?

::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="-40,-60 40,-60 40,-50 6,-50 6,50 40,50 40,60 -40,60 -40,50 -6,50 -6,-50 -40,-50" cerchi="0,25,5,foro" punti="0,0,G_0,baricentro" frecce="-56,-74,-32,-74,z;-56,-74,-56,-50,y" titolo="I-section with a hole in its web"}

- It rises along the vertical axis of symmetry
- It remains at $G_0$
- It moves horizontally away from the hole
- It moves downward along the vertical symmetry axis

## Explanation

The perforated section remains symmetric about its vertical axis, so its centroid stays on that axis. The original section has zero first moment about the horizontal axis through $G_0$. Removing the hole, whose centroid is at a positive elevation, leaves a negative first moment. The new centroid is therefore below $G_0$.
