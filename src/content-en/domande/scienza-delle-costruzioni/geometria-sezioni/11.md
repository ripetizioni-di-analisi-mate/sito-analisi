The angle shown has equal perpendicular legs, both $60\,\mathrm{mm}$ long and $10\,\mathrm{mm}$ thick, with lengths measured along the outer boundary. From their common outside corner the legs extend along the positive axes. Find the slope of the principal axis with the smaller second moment in the $(z,y)$ plane.

::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="0,0 60,0 60,10 10,10 10,60 0,60" frecce="0,0,74,0,z;0,0,0,72,y" quote="0 0,60 0,60 mm,8;0 0,0 60,60 mm,-9;60 0,60 10,10 mm,8;0 60,10 60,10 mm,-22" titolo="Equal-leg angle aligned with the two axes"}

- $1$
- $0$
- $\sqrt2$
- $-1$

## Explanation

Symmetry interchanges the two legs, so the centroidal second moments $I_y$ and $I_z$ are equal. Divide the angle into its complete horizontal leg and the vertical portion above it. Relative to $G$, each centroid lies in a quadrant with coordinates of opposite signs, so the total product of inertia $I_{yz}$ is negative. The second moments about the bisectors of slopes $-1$ and $1$ are $I_z+I_{yz}$ and $I_z-I_{yz}$, respectively. The first is smaller.
