A solid triangular section has horizontal base $90\,\mathrm{mm}$ and an apex at height $90\,\mathrm{mm}$ above the base. A horizontal cut at height $30\,\mathrm{mm}$ isolates the upper portion. Find its first moment of area about the cut.

- $36\,000\,\mathrm{mm^3}$
- $90\,000\,\mathrm{mm^3}$
- $18\,000\,\mathrm{mm^3}$
- $72\,000\,\mathrm{mm^3}$

## Explanation

The triangle above the shear force is similar to the full triangle. Its height is $60\,\mathrm{mm}$, its base is $60\,\mathrm{mm}$, and its centroid lies $20\,\mathrm{mm}$ from the cut. Multiplying its area by this distance gives $36\,000\,\mathrm{mm^3}$.
