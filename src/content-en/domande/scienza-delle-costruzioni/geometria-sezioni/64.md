Two identical $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ by $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ rectangular plates have centroids at the same distance from a fixed horizontal axis. The first is mounted $2\,\mathrm{cm}$ high, the second $6\,\mathrm{cm}$ high. By how much do their contributions to the second moment about that axis differ?

::struttura{poligoni="0,3 6,3 6,5 0,5;9,1 11,1 11,7 9,7" tratteggi="-1,0 12,0;-1,4 12,4" punti="3,4,G₁,baricentro;10,4,G₂,baricentro" titolo="The two orientations at the same distance from the axis"}

- $36\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $32\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $4\,\mathrm{cm^4}$
- $0\,\mathrm{cm^4}$

## Explanation

Equal areas and offsets give equal parallel-axis terms. The centroidal terms are $\dfrac{6\cdot2^3}{12}=4\,\mathrm{cm^4}$ and $\dfrac{2\cdot6^3}{12}=36\,\mathrm{cm^4}$. Their difference is $32\,\mathrm{cm^4}$.
