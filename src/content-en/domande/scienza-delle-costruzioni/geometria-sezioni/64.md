A doubly symmetric I-section of depth $200\,\mathrm{mm}$ has centroidal second moment of area $I_z=20\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$. Two plates of width $120\,\mathrm{mm}$ and thickness $10\,\mathrm{mm}$ are welded to it, one to each flange. Find the second moment of area of the reinforced section about its horizontal centroidal axis.

::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="-50,-100 50,-100 50,-90 3,-90 3,90 50,90 50,100 -50,100 -50,90 -3,90 -3,-90 -50,-90;-60,100 60,100 60,110 -60,110;-60,-110 60,-110 60,-100 -60,-100" frecce="0,0,74,0,z;0,0,0,130,y" quote="-60 110,60 110,120 mm,-18;60 100,60 110,10 mm,9;50 -100,50 100,200 mm,32" titolo="I-section reinforced with two plates"}

- $20.02\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $46.48\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $33.24\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $22.88\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$

## Explanation

The reinforced section retains double symmetry and its centroidal axis stays in place. Each plate has area $1\,200\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ and its centroid is $100+5=105\,\mathrm{mm}$ from the axis. By the parallel-axis theorem each plate contributes
$$
\frac{120\cdot10^3}{12}+1\,200\cdot105^2=13.24\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}.
$$
Thus $I_z=20\cdot10^6+2\cdot13.24\cdot10^6=46.48\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$.
