The right triangle shown has vertices $(z,y)=(0,0),(90,0),(0,60)$ in millimeters. Calculate the vertical coordinate $y_G$.

::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="0,0 90,0 0,60" frecce="0,0,106,0,z;0,0,0,74,y" quote="0 0,90 0,90 mm,8;0 0,0 60,60 mm,-10" titolo="Right triangle with legs along the coordinate axes"}

- $30\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $40\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $20\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $45\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The centroid coordinates of a triangle are the arithmetic means of its vertex coordinates. The vertex elevations are $0$, $0$, and $60\,\mathrm{mm}$, giving $y_G=20\,\mathrm{mm}$.
