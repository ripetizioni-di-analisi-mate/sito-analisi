The channel shown opens to the right. Its outside width is $60\,\mathrm{mm}$, its outside depth is $100\,\mathrm{mm}$, and all walls have thickness $10\,\mathrm{mm}$. The origin is the lower left corner. Find $z_G$.

::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="0,0 60,0 60,10 10,10 10,90 60,90 60,100 0,100" frecce="0,0,76,0,z;0,0,0,116,y" quote="0 100,60 100,60 mm,-8;60 0,60 10,10 mm,8;0 0,0 100,100 mm,-12;0 50,10 50,10 mm,0" titolo="Channel opening to the right"}

- $30\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $20\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $40\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $35\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

Form the channel by subtracting from rectangle $60\times100\,\mathrm{mm}$ the opening between $z=10$ and $z=60\,\mathrm{mm}$ and between $y=10$ and $y=90\,\mathrm{mm}$. The full rectangle has area $6\,000\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ and centroid $z=30\,\mathrm{mm}$. The opening has area $4\,000\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ and centroid $z=35\,\mathrm{mm}$. Thus
$$
z_G=\frac{6\,000\cdot30-4\,000\cdot35}{2\,000}\,\mathrm{mm}=20\,\mathrm{mm}.
$$
The centroid lies in the opening, close to the web, which accounts for half the material.
