An annulus has outer radius $30\,\mathrm{mm}$ and inner radius $20\,\mathrm{mm}$. Calculate the polar moment of area $I_p$ about the common center.

::struttura{riferimento=0 cerchi="0,0,30;0,0,20,foro" frecce="0,0,41,0,z;0,0,0,42,y;0,0,18,24,R;0,0,-16,-12,r" titolo="Annulus showing the two radii"}

- $325\,000\pi\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $162\,500\pi\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $405\,000\pi\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $485\,000\pi\,\mathrm{mm^4}$

## Explanation

The polar second moment of a disk of radius $r$ is $\dfrac{\pi r^4}{2}$. Subtracting the hole gives $\dfrac{\pi(30^4-20^4)}{2}$.
