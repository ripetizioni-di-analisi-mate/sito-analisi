A solid ellipse has horizontal semiaxis $40\,\mathrm{mm}$ and vertical semiaxis $20\,\mathrm{mm}$. Find its second moment of area about the horizontal axis through its center. Round to the nearest $\mathrm{mm^4}$.

- $1\,005\,310\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $15\,708\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $251\,327\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $4\,021\,239\,\mathrm{mm^4}$

## Explanation

Obtain the ellipse from the unit disk by scaling $x$ by $a$ and $y$ by $b$. The integral of $y^2$ acquires a factor $b^2$ and the area element a factor $ab$. The unit disk's diametral second moment is $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$, giving $I_x = \dfrac{\pi ab^3}{4} = 251\,327\,\mathrm{mm^4}$.
