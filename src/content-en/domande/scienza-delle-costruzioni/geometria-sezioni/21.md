A rectangular section of width $120\,\mathrm{mm}$ and depth $180\,\mathrm{mm}$ contains two circular holes of radius $15\,\mathrm{mm}$. Their centers lie on the vertical axis of the rectangle, a distance $50\,\mathrm{mm}$ above and below its center. Find $I$ about the horizontal centroidal axis of the perforated section. Round to the nearest $\mathrm{mm^4}$.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 120,0 120,180 0,180" cerchi="60,140,15,foro;60,40,15,foro" tratteggi="-8,90 128,90" titolo="Symmetric circular holes above and below the horizontal axis" riferimento="0"}

- $54\,706\,187\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $58\,240\,478\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $56\,513\,093\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $54\,785\,708\,\mathrm{mm^4}$

## Explanation

Symmetry keeps the centroid at the center. From $\dfrac{bh^3}{12}$ subtract twice the hole contribution, $\dfrac{\pi r^4}{4} + \pi r^2 d^2$. Its second term transfers the hole's second moment to the horizontal axis of the rectangle. The remainder is $54\,706\,187\,\mathrm{mm^4}$.
