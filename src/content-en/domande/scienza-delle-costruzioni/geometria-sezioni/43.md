A T-section has a top flange of width $120\,\mathrm{mm}$ and thickness $20\,\mathrm{mm}$ and a centered web of width $10\,\mathrm{mm}$ and height $60\,\mathrm{mm}$. How far above the bottom fiber is the centroid, and in which part of the section does it lie?

::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="-5,0 5,0 5,60 60,60 60,80 -60,80 -60,60 -5,60" frecce="0,0,76,0,z;0,0,0,96,y" quote="-60 80,60 80,120 mm,-16;60 60,60 80,20 mm,8;5 0,5 60,60 mm,9" titolo="T-section with a thin web"}

- At $50\,\mathrm{mm}$, in the web
- At $40\,\mathrm{mm}$, in the web
- At $18\,\mathrm{mm}$, in the web
- At $62\,\mathrm{mm}$, in the flange

## Explanation

The web has area $600\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ and centroid elevation $30\,\mathrm{mm}$, and the flange has area $2\,400\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ and centroid elevation $70\,\mathrm{mm}$. Thus
$$
y_G=\frac{600\cdot30+2\,400\cdot70}{3\,000}\,\mathrm{mm}=62\,\mathrm{mm}.
$$
The flange spans elevations $60$ to $80\,\mathrm{mm}$, so the centroid lies in the flange. The flange area is four times the web area, placing the weighted mean closer to the flange centroid.
