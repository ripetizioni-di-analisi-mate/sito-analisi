The channel shown opens to the right and has outside width $60\,\mathrm{mm}$, outside depth $100\,\mathrm{mm}$, and wall thickness $10\,\mathrm{mm}$. Its centroid lies $z_G=20\,\mathrm{mm}$ from the outer face of the web. Find its second moment of area about the vertical centroidal axis.

::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="0,0 60,0 60,10 10,10 10,90 60,90 60,100 0,100" tratteggi="20,-8 20,108" etichette="20,114,y_G" frecce="0,0,76,0,z;0,0,0,116,y" quote="0 100,60 100,60 mm,-16;0 -6,20 -6,20 mm,6;0 0,0 100,100 mm,-22" titolo="Channel and its vertical centroidal axis"}

- $\dfrac{2\,900\,000}{3}\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $\dfrac{200\,000}{3}\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $\dfrac{4\,700\,000}{3}\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $\dfrac{2\,000\,000}{3}\,\mathrm{mm^4}$

## Explanation

Subtract the opening $50\times80\,\mathrm{mm}$ from the outer rectangle and transfer both contributions to the vertical axis through $G$. The outer rectangle has centroid $z=30\,\mathrm{mm}$ a distance $10\,\mathrm{mm}$ from the axis, and the opening has centroid $z=35\,\mathrm{mm}$ a distance $15\,\mathrm{mm}$ from it. Thus
$$
I_y=\frac{100\cdot60^3}{12}+6\,000\cdot10^2-\left(\frac{80\cdot50^3}{12}+4\,000\cdot15^2\right)=\frac{2\,000\,000}{3}\,\mathrm{mm^4}.
$$
