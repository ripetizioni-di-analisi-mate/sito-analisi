Both layers of a two-layer section have the same width. The bottom layer has thickness $60\,\mathrm{mm}$ and modulus $E_1 = 70\,\mathrm{GPa}$, and the top layer has thickness $20\,\mathrm{mm}$ and modulus $E_2 = 210\,\mathrm{GPa}$. The layers are perfectly bonded. In pure elastic bending, how far above the bottom edge is the neutral axis? Round to one decimal place.

- $34.0\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $70.0\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $50.0\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $40.0\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

With strain linear through the depth, zero axial resultant requires a mean elevation weighted by $EA$. The common width cancels. The layer centroid elevations are $30.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $70.0\,\mathrm{mm}$. Using the weights $60$ and $60$ gives $50.0\,\mathrm{mm}$.
