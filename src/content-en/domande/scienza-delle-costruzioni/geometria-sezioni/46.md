The section shown consists of a vertical rectangle of width $40\,\mathrm{mm}$ and height $120\,\mathrm{mm}$ and a horizontal arm projecting $80\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right. The arm has height $40\,\mathrm{mm}$ and is centered vertically on the rectangle. The origin is the lower left corner. Find $z_G$.

::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="0,0 40,0 40,40 120,40 120,80 40,80 40,120 0,120" frecce="0,0,136,0,z;0,0,0,136,y" quote="0 120,40 120,40 mm,-8;40 80,120 80,80 mm,-8;120 40,120 80,40 mm,8;0 0,0 120,120 mm,-12" titolo="Section with a side arm"}

- $44\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $50\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $60\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $36\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

Divide the area into two nonoverlapping parts. The vertical rectangle has area $4\,800\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ and centroid $z=20\,\mathrm{mm}$. The arm extends from $z=40$ to $z=120\,\mathrm{mm}$ and has area $3\,200\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ with centroid $z=80\,\mathrm{mm}$. Thus
$$
z_G=\frac{4\,800\cdot20+3\,200\cdot80}{8\,000}\,\mathrm{mm}=44\,\mathrm{mm}.
$$
If the two complete rectangles $40\times120$ and $120\times40\,\mathrm{mm}$ are used, the shared square $40\times40\,\mathrm{mm}$ is counted twice and must be subtracted once.
