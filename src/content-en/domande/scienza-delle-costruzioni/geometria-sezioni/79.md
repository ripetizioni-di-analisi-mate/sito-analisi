An angle section has unequal legs of lengths $100\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $60\,\mathrm{mm}$, both of thickness $10\,\mathrm{mm}$. Which statement about its principal axes of inertia is true?

- They are parallel to the two legs
- One bisects the angle between the legs
- They pass through the centroid and are inclined to the legs
- None exist because the section has no axis of symmetry

## Explanation

Principal axes are centroidal axes of zero product of inertia. The principal moment formulas determine them for any section. Relative to centroidal axes parallel to the legs, the component centroids have coordinates of opposite signs, so $I_{yz}\neq0$ and these axes are not principal. The angle bisector is an axis of symmetry, and therefore principal, only for an equal-leg angle. For an unequal-leg angle the principal axes pass through $G$ and their inclination depends on the dimensions.
