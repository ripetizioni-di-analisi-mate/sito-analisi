A homogeneous lamina is a solid semicircle of radius $45\,\mathrm{mm}$ above its horizontal diameter. How far from the diameter is the centroid? Round to one decimal place.

- $9.5\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $19.1\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $28.6\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $22.5\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The semicircle has area $\dfrac{\pi R^2}{2}$ and first moment of area $\int_0^R 2y\sqrt{R^2 - y^2}\,\mathrm{d}y = \dfrac{2R^3}{3}$ about the diameter. Dividing the first moment by the area gives $\dfrac{4R}{3\pi} = 19.1\,\mathrm{mm}$.
