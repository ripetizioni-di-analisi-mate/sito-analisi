An annulus has outer diameter $100\,\mathrm{mm}$ and inner diameter $40\,\mathrm{mm}$. Find its radius of gyration about a diameter to one decimal place.

- $26.9\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $22.9\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $25.0\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $38.1\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The ratio $\dfrac{I}{A}$ equals $\dfrac{\dfrac{\pi(D^4 - d^4)}{64}}{\dfrac{\pi(D^2 - d^2)}{4}} = \dfrac{D^2 + d^2}{16}$. Taking its square root gives $26.9\,\mathrm{mm}$. The factor $\sqrt{2}$ would appear if the polar second moment were used in place of the diametral second moment.
