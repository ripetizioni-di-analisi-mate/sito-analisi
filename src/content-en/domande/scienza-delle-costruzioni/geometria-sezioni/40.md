Line $z_G$ passes through the centroid of a T-section and divides it into upper and lower portions. The first moment of the upper area about $z_G$ is $45\,000\,\mathrm{mm^3}$. Find the first moment of the lower area about the same line.

- $45\,000\,\mathrm{mm^3}$
- $0\,\mathrm{mm^3}$
- $-90\,000\,\mathrm{mm^3}$
- $-45\,000\,\mathrm{mm^3}$

## Explanation

The first moment of the entire section about a centroidal axis is zero. Its defining integral splits over the two portions, so the first moments of the two portions add to zero. The first moment of the lower portion is therefore $-45\,000\,\mathrm{mm^3}$. It is negative because all its points have negative elevation relative to $z_G$.
