A doubly symmetric I-section has overall depth $200\,\mathrm{mm}$, flange width $100\,\mathrm{mm}$, flange thickness $10\,\mathrm{mm}$, and web thickness $6\,\mathrm{mm}$. Find its second moment of area about the horizontal centroidal axis.

::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="-50,-100 50,-100 50,-90 3,-90 3,90 50,90 50,100 -50,100 -50,90 -3,90 -3,-90 -50,-90" frecce="0,0,70,0,z;0,0,0,122,y" quote="-50 100,50 100,100 mm,-18;50 -100,50 100,200 mm,30" titolo="I-section with equal flanges"}

- $18.07\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $12.94\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $2.93\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $20.98\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$

## Explanation

Double symmetry places the centroid at the center. From rectangle $100\times200\,\mathrm{mm}$ subtract two openings of combined width $100-6=94\,\mathrm{mm}$ and height $200-2\cdot10=180\,\mathrm{mm}$, also centered at mid-depth. Thus
$$
I_z=\frac{100\cdot200^3-94\cdot180^3}{12}\,\mathrm{mm^4}\simeq20.98\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}.
$$
