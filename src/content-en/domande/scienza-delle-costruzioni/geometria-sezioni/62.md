A tube of outer radius $40\,\mathrm{mm}$ and inner radius $30\,\mathrm{mm}$ rests on a horizontal surface. Find the second moment of its cross-sectional area about the line $z$ tangent to its outer surface at the point of contact.

::struttura{riferimento=0 cerchi="0,40,40;0,40,30,foro" linee="-60,0 -6,0" frecce="0,0,64,0,z;0,0,0,96,y" punti="0,40,C,centro" titolo="Tube resting on line z"}

- $1\,557\,500\pi\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $1\,067\,500\pi\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $1\,995\,000\pi\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $1\,120\,000\pi\,\mathrm{mm^4}$

## Explanation

The annulus has diametral second moment equal to half its polar second moment, namely $\dfrac{\pi}{4}\left(40^4-30^4\right)=437\,500\pi\,\mathrm{mm^4}$. Its area is $\pi\left(40^2-30^2\right)=700\pi\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ and its center is $40\,\mathrm{mm}$ from the tangent. By the parallel-axis theorem,
$$
I_z=437\,500\pi+700\pi\cdot40^2=1\,557\,500\pi\,\mathrm{mm^4}.
$$
