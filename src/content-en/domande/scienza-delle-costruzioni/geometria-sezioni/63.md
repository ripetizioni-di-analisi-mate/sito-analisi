A section of area $2\,500\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ has the same second moment of area, $5\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$, about two distinct horizontal axes spaced $40\,\mathrm{mm}$ apart. Find its second moment of area about the horizontal centroidal axis.

- $10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $6\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $4\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $4.95\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$

## Explanation

By the parallel-axis theorem $I=I_G+Ad^2$, so two parallel axes with the same second moment are equidistant from the centroid. The axes are distinct, so the centroid lies halfway between them, a distance $20\,\mathrm{mm}$ from each. Thus
$$
I_G=5\cdot10^6-2\,500\cdot20^2=4\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}.
$$
