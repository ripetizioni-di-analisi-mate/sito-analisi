A hollow rectangular section of width $100\,\mathrm{mm}$, depth $160\,\mathrm{mm}$, and wall thickness $10\,\mathrm{mm}$ bends in the vertical plane. Which elastic section modulus applies to its two horizontal extreme fibers?

::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="-50,-80 50,-80 50,80 -50,80" fori="-40,-70 40,-70 40,70 -40,70" frecce="0,0,68,0,z;0,0,0,98,y" quote="-50 80,50 80,100 mm,-18;50 -80,50 80,160 mm,28;50 70,50 80,10 mm,-9" titolo="Rectangular tube with the origin at its centroid"}

- $198\,000\,\mathrm{mm^3}$
- $99\,000\,\mathrm{mm^3}$
- $\dfrac{496\,000}{3}\,\mathrm{mm^3}$
- $316\,800\,\mathrm{mm^3}$

## Explanation

The hole is concentric, so
$$
I_z=\frac{100\cdot160^3-80\cdot140^3}{12}\,\mathrm{mm^4}=15.84\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}.
$$
The extreme fibers are $80\,\mathrm{mm}$ from the axis, giving $W=\dfrac{15.84\cdot10^6}{80}=198\,000\,\mathrm{mm^3}$. Subtracting the section modulus of the hole from that of the outer rectangle gives a different value because their extreme fibers are at different distances $80$ and $70\,\mathrm{mm}$ from the axis.
