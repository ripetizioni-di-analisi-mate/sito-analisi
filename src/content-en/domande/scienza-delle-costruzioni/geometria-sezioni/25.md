A section with nonzero centroidal product of inertia $I_{xy}$ is reflected about horizontal axis $x$ with the Cartesian axes fixed. What happens to $I_{xy}$ and to the largest principal second moment of area?

- $I_{xy}$ changes sign and the maximum doubles
- $I_{xy}$ changes sign and the maximum is unchanged
- $I_{xy}$ becomes zero and the maximum is unchanged
- Both $I_{xy}$ and the maximum are unchanged

## Explanation

For each area element, $y$ changes sign while $x$ stays the same. Their product $I_{xy}$ therefore changes sign. Reflecting or rotating a section changes its orientation and preserves its principal second moments.
