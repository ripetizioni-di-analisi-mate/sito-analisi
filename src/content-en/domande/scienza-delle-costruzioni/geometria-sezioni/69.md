A section of area $2\,000\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ has product of inertia $I_{yz,G}=-3\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{mm^4}$ about centroidal axes parallel to $z$ and $y$. In coordinate system $(z,y)$ its centroid has coordinates $(z_G,y_G)=(15,-20)\,\mathrm{mm}$. Find $I_{yz}$ about axes $z$ and $y$.

- $3\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $-6\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $9\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $-9\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{mm^4}$

## Explanation

The parallel-axis theorem for products of inertia gives $I_{yz}=I_{yz,G}+Ay_Gz_G$. The centroid lies in the quadrant with positive $z$ and negative $y$. The parallel-axis term is therefore negative and equals $2\,000\cdot(-20)\cdot15=-6\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{mm^4}$, giving $I_{yz}=-3\cdot10^5-6\cdot10^5=-9\cdot10^5\,\mathrm{mm^4}$.
