A solid trapezoidal section has horizontal parallel sides. Its bottom side has length $120\,\mathrm{mm}$, its top side length $60\,\mathrm{mm}$, and its height is $90\,\mathrm{mm}$. Find the height of its centroid above the bottom side to one decimal place.

- $50.0\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $30.0\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $45.0\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $40.0\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

At elevation $y$ the width is $b(y) = B + \dfrac{(b - B)y}{h}$. Divide $\int_0^h y\,b(y)\,\mathrm{d}y$ by area $\dfrac{h(B + b)}{2}$ to obtain $\dfrac{h(B + 2b)}{3(B + b)} = 40.0\,\mathrm{mm}$.
