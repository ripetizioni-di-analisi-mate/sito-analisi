The centroidal axis at $20^\circ$ counterclockwise from $z$ is the principal axis of maximum second moment of area. Which is the principal axis of minimum second moment of area?

- The axis at $110^\circ$ from $z$
- The axis at $65^\circ$ from $z$
- The axis at $-20^\circ$ from $z$
- The axis at $40^\circ$ from $z$

## Explanation

The principal axes are orthogonal centroidal axes with zero product of inertia. The second moment is largest about one and smallest about the other. The minimum axis is therefore perpendicular to the maximum axis and makes an angle $20^\circ+90^\circ=110^\circ$ with $z$. The rotation formula contains a double angle, so the two extremes occur at values of $2\theta$ differing by $180^\circ$.
