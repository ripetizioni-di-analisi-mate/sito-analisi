A triangle has its base on the $z$ axis and height $60\,\mathrm{mm}$. Find its radius of gyration about the $z$ axis along its base.

- $10\sqrt3\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $20\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $10\sqrt6\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $10\sqrt2\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

Let $b$ be the base. At elevation $y$ the triangle has width $b\left(1-\dfrac yh\right)$, so
$$
I_z=\int_0^h b\left(1-\frac yh\right)y^2\,dy=\frac{bh^3}{12}.
$$
Its area is $\dfrac{bh}{2}$, giving $\rho^2=\dfrac{bh^3}{12}\cdot\dfrac{2}{bh}=\dfrac{h^2}{6}$ independently of $b$. With $h=60\,\mathrm{mm}$ the result is $\rho=\dfrac{60}{\sqrt6}=10\sqrt6\,\mathrm{mm}\simeq24.5\,\mathrm{mm}$.
