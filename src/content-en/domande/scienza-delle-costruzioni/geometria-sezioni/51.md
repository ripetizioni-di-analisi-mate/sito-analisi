The section shown consists of a square of side $60\,\mathrm{mm}$ topped by an isosceles triangle with base $60\,\mathrm{mm}$ and height $60\,\mathrm{mm}$. Find the centroid height $y_G$ above the base of the square.

::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="0,0 60,0 60,60 30,120 0,60" tratteggi="0,60 60,60" frecce="0,0,76,0,z;0,0,0,136,y" quote="0 0,60 0,60 mm,8;60 0,60 60,60 mm,9;72 60,72 120,60 mm,0" titolo="Section consisting of a square and a triangle"}

- $\dfrac{160}{3}\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $\dfrac{140}{3}\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $55\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $60\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The square has area $3\,600\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ and centroid elevation $30\,\mathrm{mm}$. The triangle has area $\dfrac{60\cdot60}{2}=1\,800\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ and its centroid is one third of its height above its base, at elevation $60+20=80\,\mathrm{mm}$. Thus
$$
y_G=\frac{3\,600\cdot30+1\,800\cdot80}{5\,400}\,\mathrm{mm}=\frac{140}{3}\,\mathrm{mm}\simeq46.7\,\mathrm{mm}.
$$
