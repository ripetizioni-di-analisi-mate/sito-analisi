A solid area has centroid $G$. Two equal holes are drilled with centroid heights $y_G+10\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $y_G-10\,\mathrm{mm}$, both at horizontal coordinates greater than $z_G$. What can be established without knowing the original area?

::struttura{poligoni="-3,-2 3,-2 3,2 -3,2" cerchi="1,0.9,0.25,foro;1.9,-0.9,0.25,foro" punti="0,0,G,baricentro" frecce="-2.4,0,2.7,0,z;0,-1.5,0,1.7,y" titolo="Hole locations relative to the original centroid"}

- The centroid remains at $G$.
- Its height increases and its horizontal coordinate remains $z_G$.
- Both coordinates increase.
- Its height remains $y_G$ and the centroid moves to a smaller horizontal coordinate.

## Explanation

The removed first moments about the horizontal line through $G$ cancel, preserving the centroid height. About the vertical through $G$, both holes remove first moments of the same sign, moving the centroid left.
