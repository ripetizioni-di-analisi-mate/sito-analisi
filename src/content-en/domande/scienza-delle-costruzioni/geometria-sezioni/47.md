The top edge of a rectangular plate of width $100\,\mathrm{mm}$ and height $60\,\mathrm{mm}$ contains a semicircular notch of radius $30\,\mathrm{mm}$ centered at the midpoint of that edge. Find the centroid height $y_G$ measured from the bottom edge.

::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="0,0 100,0 100,60 0,60" fori="80.00,60.00 78.98,52.24 75.98,45.00 71.21,38.79 65.00,34.02 57.76,31.02 50.00,30.00 42.24,31.02 35.00,34.02 28.79,38.79 24.02,45.00 21.02,52.24 20.00,60.00" frecce="0,0,116,0,z;0,0,0,76,y" quote="0 0,100 0,100 mm,8;100 0,100 60,60 mm,9;50 60,80 60,30 mm,-8" titolo="Plate with a semicircular notch"}

- $20.8\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $18.9\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $16.8\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $24.7\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The full rectangle has area $6\,000\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ and centroid elevation $30\,\mathrm{mm}$. The removed semicircle has area $\dfrac{\pi\cdot30^2}{2}=450\pi\,\mathrm{mm^2}$. Its centroid lies $\dfrac{4R}{3\pi}=\dfrac{40}{\pi}\,\mathrm{mm}$ below the diameter along the top edge, at elevation $60-\dfrac{40}{\pi}\simeq47.27\,\mathrm{mm}$. Thus
$$
y_G=\frac{6\,000\cdot30-450\pi\left(60-\frac{40}{\pi}\right)}{6\,000-450\pi}\,\mathrm{mm}\simeq24.7\,\mathrm{mm}.
$$
