An isosceles trapezoid has bottom base $120\,\mathrm{mm}$, top base $60\,\mathrm{mm}$, and height $60\,\mathrm{mm}$. Find the second moment of area about line $z$ along the bottom base.

::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="0,0 120,0 90,60 30,60" tratteggi="30,0 30,60;90,0 90,60" frecce="0,0,136,0,z;0,0,0,76,y" quote="0 -6,120 -6,120 mm,0;30 60,90 60,60 mm,-8;120 0,120 60,60 mm,0" titolo="Isosceles trapezoid with its base on the z axis"}

- $4\,680\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $5\,400\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $7\,560\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $2\,160\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$

## Explanation

Vertical lines through the ends of the top base split the trapezoid into a rectangle $60\times60\,\mathrm{mm}$ and two right triangles of base $30\,\mathrm{mm}$ on the axis and height $60\,\mathrm{mm}$. The rectangle has $\dfrac{bh^3}{3}=\dfrac{60\cdot60^3}{3}=4\,320\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$ about its bottom edge. A triangle has $\dfrac{bh^3}{12}$ about its base, so each triangle contributes $\dfrac{30\cdot60^3}{12}=540\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}$. Thus
$$
I_z=4\,320\,000+2\cdot540\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}=5\,400\,000\,\mathrm{mm^4}.
$$
