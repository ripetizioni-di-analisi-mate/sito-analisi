The area shown is bounded by the $z$ axis, the line $z=60\,\mathrm{mm}$, and the parabola $y=\dfrac{z^2}{40}$, with lengths in millimeters. Find its centroid coordinate $y_G$.

::struttura{riferimento=0 poligoni="0,0 60,0 60,90 50,62.5 40,40 30,22.5 20,10 10,2.5" frecce="0,0,76,0,z;0,0,0,104,y" quote="0 0,60 0,60 mm,8;60 0,60 90,90 mm,9" etichette="18,46,y = z²/40" titolo="Region under a parabolic arc"}

- $27\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $45\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $30\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $22.5\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The area is
$$
A=\int_0^{60}\frac{z^2}{40}\,dz=\frac{60^3}{120}\,\mathrm{mm^2}=1\,800\,\mathrm{mm^2}.
$$
A vertical strip of width $dz$ has area $\dfrac{z^2}{40}\,dz$ and its centroid at half its height, at elevation $\dfrac{z^2}{80}$. Thus
$$
S_z=\int_0^{60}\frac{z^2}{80}\cdot\frac{z^2}{40}\,dz=\frac{60^5}{16\,000}\,\mathrm{mm^3}=48\,600\,\mathrm{mm^3}.
$$
Therefore $y_G=\dfrac{48\,600}{1\,800}=27\,\mathrm{mm}$.
