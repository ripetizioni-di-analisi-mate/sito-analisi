A rectangular bar of width $50\,\mathrm{mm}$ and depth $100\,\mathrm{mm}$ resists bending about its horizontal centroidal axis. Rotate it through $90^\circ$ about its longitudinal axis. Find the ratio of the initial elastic section modulus to the section modulus after rotation.

- $4.00$ times
- $0.50$ times
- $2.00$ times
- $1.00$ times

## Explanation

Initially $W = \dfrac{bh^2}{6}$. After rotation the vertical dimension is $50\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $W = \dfrac{hb^2}{6}$. The ratio is $\dfrac{h}{b} = 2.00$.
