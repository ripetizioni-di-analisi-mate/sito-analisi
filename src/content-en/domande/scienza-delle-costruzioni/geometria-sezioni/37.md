Four bolts of diameter $20\,\mathrm{mm}$ have centers at the corners of a square of side $100\,\mathrm{mm}$ centered on point $O$. Find the polar second moment of the four bolt areas about $O$.

::struttura{riferimento=0 cerchi="50,50,10;-50,50,10;-50,-50,10;50,-50,10" punti="0,0,O,centro" tratteggi="-50,-50 50,-50 50,50 -50,50 -50,-50" frecce="0,0,82,0,z;0,0,0,82,y" quote="-50 -64,50 -64,100 mm,0;64 -50,64 50,100 mm,0" titolo="Four-bolt group"}

- $1\,020\,000\pi\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $4\,020\,000\pi\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $8\,020\,000\pi\,\mathrm{mm^4}$
- $2\,020\,000\pi\,\mathrm{mm^4}$

## Explanation

Each bolt has radius $10\,\mathrm{mm}$, area $100\pi\,\mathrm{mm^2}$, and polar second moment $\dfrac{\pi\cdot10^4}{2}=5\,000\pi\,\mathrm{mm^4}$ about its own center. Its center has coordinates $(\pm50,\pm50)\,\mathrm{mm}$, so its distance from $O$ is $d$ with $d^2=50^2+50^2=5\,000\,\mathrm{mm^2}$. Since $I_p=I_z+I_y$, adding the parallel-axis relations for the two axes gives $I_{p,O}=I_{p,G}+Ad^2$. Each bolt therefore contributes $5\,000\pi+100\pi\cdot5\,000=505\,000\pi\,\mathrm{mm^4}$, giving $I_{p,O}=2\,020\,000\pi\,\mathrm{mm^4}\simeq6.35\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}$ for all four.
