Two members meet at a rigid joint. The couples the joint exerts on the two members are $10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and $-4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, positive counterclockwise. What external couple is applied at the joint?

- $14\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise
- $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise
- $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ clockwise
- $0$

## Explanation

The members exert equal and opposite couples on the joint, $-10$ and $+4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

For the joint to be in moment equilibrium, the external couple $C$ must cancel the sum of these actions, giving $C-10+4=0$.

Thus $C=6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, in the **counterclockwise** direction.

The sum of the couples required at the member ends equals the couple applied at the joint. Value $14\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ adds the magnitudes without their signs.
