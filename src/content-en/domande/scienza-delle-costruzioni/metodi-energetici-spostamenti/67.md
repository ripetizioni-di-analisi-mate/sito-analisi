An unloaded beam of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ with constant $EI$ has both end translations restrained and zero rotation at $B$. Impose a counterclockwise rotation $\varphi_A$ at $A$. At which section is the bending moment zero?

- At a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$
- At a distance $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$
- At midspan, a distance $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$
- At no section, because the moment has the same sign throughout

## Explanation

With $\varphi_B=0$, the external end couples are $m_A=\dfrac{4EI}{L}\varphi_A$ and $m_B=\dfrac{2EI}{L}\varphi_A$, both counterclockwise.

At the left end the internal moment is opposite to the external couple. At the right end it has the same sign, giving $M(0)=-\dfrac{4EI}{L}\varphi_A$ and $M(L)=\dfrac{2EI}{L}\varphi_A$.

With no loads on the member, the moment varies linearly between the two end values.
$$
M(x)=\frac{EI\varphi_A}{L}\left(-4+\frac{6x}{L}\right).
$$
It therefore vanishes at $x=\dfrac{2L}{3}=2\,\mathrm{m}$, one-third of the span from $B$. Value $1\,\mathrm{m}$ interchanges the couples at the two ends.
