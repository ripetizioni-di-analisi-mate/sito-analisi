A prismatic tie rod of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$ has axial rigidity $EA=30\,000\,\mathrm{kN}$. A tensile force of $30\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at its free end. Find the axial strain energy stored in the rod.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="A,B,2 m" forze="B,0.55,0,N" titolo="Tie with an axial end load"}

- $0.060\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $0.030\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $0.015\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $0.003\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The axial force in the tie rod is uniform, $N=30\,\mathrm{kN}$. Integrating $\dfrac{N^2}{2EA}$ over the length $2\,\mathrm{m}$ gives $0.030\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
