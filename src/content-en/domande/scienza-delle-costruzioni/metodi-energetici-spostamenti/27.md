A couple is applied at a stationary fixed end. Which term in Clapeyron’s theorem comes directly from that couple?

- The product of the couple and the beam deflection.
- Half the product of the couple and the midspan rotation.
- The product of the couple and the rotation at the free end.
- Zero, because the conjugate rotation is zero.

## Explanation

The couple and the rotation must refer to the same coordinate. The rotation of the fixed end is zero ($\theta=0$).
