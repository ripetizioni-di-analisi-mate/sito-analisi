A horizontal beam of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ with no intermediate loads carries external end couples $m_A=10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and $m_B=6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, both counterclockwise. What external transverse force acts at end $B$?

- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward
- $1\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward
- $2.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward

## Explanation

Let $F_B$ denote the transverse force at $B$, positive upward. The couples act in the same direction, so a pair of vertical end forces must balance them.

Taking moments about $A$ gives
$$
F_BL+m_A+m_B=0,
\qquad
F_B=-\frac{10+6}{4}\,\mathrm{kN}=-4\,\mathrm{kN}.
$$
At $B$ the force is $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ **downward**, and vertical equilibrium requires an upward force of $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $A$.

Value $1\,\mathrm{kN}$ subtracts the two couples as though they acted in opposite directions.
