A continuous beam is fixed at $A$ and $C$ and has a vertical support at $B$. Spans $AB$ and $BC$ have length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and $EI=6\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. The only load is a downward force of $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ at the midpoint of $BC$. What is the rotation of $B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;E,6,0;C,8,0" aste="A-B;B-E;E-C" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello;C,incastro,0" forze="E,0,-0.8,P" quote="A,B,4 m;B,C,4 m" titolo="Continuous beam with a force in span BC"}

- $1\,\mathrm{mrad}$ counterclockwise
- $2\,\mathrm{mrad}$ clockwise
- $0.5\,\mathrm{mrad}$ clockwise
- $1\,\mathrm{mrad}$ clockwise

## Explanation

First restrain all rotations. The loaded span $BC$ requires a counterclockwise fixed-end couple $\dfrac{PL}{8}=12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at its left end $B$.

No external couple acts at the joint, so the two member end couples must sum to zero, giving
$$
\frac{4EI}{L}\varphi_B+\frac{4EI}{L}\varphi_B+\frac{PL}{8}=0.
$$
Therefore, $\varphi_B=-\dfrac{12}{12\,000}\,\mathrm{rad}=-0.001\,\mathrm{rad}$.

The negative sign indicates **clockwise** rotation, as expected because the right span deflects downward. Value $2\,\mathrm{mrad}$ includes only span $BC$ and omits the contribution of $AB$.
