A downward tip force of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ is applied to an initially unloaded cantilever. At full load, the displacement in the direction of the force is $6\,\mathrm{mm}$. Calculate the strain energy, assuming a linear response and quasistatic loading.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" forze="B,0,-0.58,P" frecce="2.28,0,2.28,-0.46,6 mm" titolo="Force and displacement in the same direction"}

- $0.072\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $0.018\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $0.006\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $0.036\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

For linear quasistatic loading, the work is half the product of the final force and final displacement. Thus $U=\dfrac{12\cdot0.006}{2}\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
