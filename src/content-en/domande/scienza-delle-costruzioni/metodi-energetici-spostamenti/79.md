A portal frame has two columns fixed at their bases and a flexurally deformable crossbeam rigidly connected to both. All members are axially inextensible. How many independent displacement unknowns does the displacement method require?

- $3$
- $2$
- $1$
- $6$

## Explanation

The two top joints can rotate independently, so their rotations are two unknowns.

The inextensible columns prevent vertical displacement of the joints. The inextensible crossbeam requires both joints to have the same horizontal displacement, giving one translation unknown.

There are therefore **three** unknowns, two rotations and one translation.

If the crossbeam were also rigid in bending, both rotations would vanish and only the translation would remain. Value $6$ counts all motions of the two joints without applying the inextensibility constraints.
