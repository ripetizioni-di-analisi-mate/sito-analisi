A beam is supported at $A$ and $B$, a span $AB=3\,\mathrm{m}$ apart, and extends beyond $B$ as an overhang $BC=1\,\mathrm{m}$. The flexural rigidity is $EI=2\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$, and a downward force of $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $C$. Neglecting shear deformation, what is the deflection at $C$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,4,0" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-0.7,P" quote="A,B,3 m;B,C,1 m" titolo="Overhanging beam with a load at the tip of the overhang"}

- $1\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $3\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $4\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $2\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The force at $C$ is conjugate to the deflection sought, so **Castigliano’s theorem** gives $v_C=\displaystyle\int\frac{M}{EI}\frac{\partial M}{\partial P}\,dx$.

On the overhang, a distance $s$ from $C$, we have $M=-Ps$. Within the span the moment varies linearly from zero at $A$ to $-Pa$ at $B$, with $a=1\,\mathrm{m}$.

The two contributions are $\dfrac{Pa^3}{3EI}$ and $\dfrac{Pa^2L}{3EI}$, giving
$$
v_C=\frac{Pa^2(L+a)}{3EI}=\frac{6\cdot1\cdot4}{3\cdot2\,000}\,\mathrm{m}=0.004\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
The deflection is $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ downward. The overhang contributes $1\,\mathrm{mm}$ and rotation of the span at $B$ contributes $3\,\mathrm{mm}$.
