On a linearly elastic simply supported beam, uniform downward load $q=2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ produces a midspan deflection of $6\,\mathrm{mm}$. A downward point force $P=10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acting alone at midspan produces the deflection field $v_P(x)$, positive downward. What is the value of the integral $\displaystyle\int_0^L v_P(x)\,dx$?

- $0.03\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $0.015\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $0.06\,\mathrm{m^2}$
- $0.0012\,\mathrm{m^2}$

## Explanation

Apply **Betti’s theorem** to the uniform-load state and the midspan-force state.

The work of the uniform load through the displacements due to $P$ is $q\displaystyle\int_0^L v_P\,dx$. The work of $P$ through the displacements due to $q$ is $P\cdot6\,\mathrm{mm}$.

Since the two mutual works are equal,
$$
\int_0^L v_P\,dx=\frac{P\cdot0.006}{q}=\frac{10\cdot0.006}{2}\,\mathrm{m^2}=0.03\,\mathrm{m^2}.
$$
Value $0.015\,\mathrm{m^2}$ introduces a factor $\dfrac12$ that belongs to Clapeyron’s theorem and is absent from mutual work.
