A cantilever of span $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and flexural rigidity $EI=8\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ carries a uniform downward load $q=2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over its full span. Neglecting shear deformation, what is the bending strain energy?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" carichi="A,B,0.45,0.45,q" quote="A,B,4 m" titolo="Cantilever under a uniform load"}

- $25.6\,\mathrm{J}$
- $12.8\,\mathrm{J}$
- $6.4\,\mathrm{J}$
- $3.2\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

At a distance $x$ from the fixed end, the load to the right has resultant $q(L-x)$ and moment arm $\dfrac{L-x}{2}$, so the bending moment has magnitude $\dfrac{q(L-x)^2}{2}$.

The integral of the square is
$$
\int_0^L\frac{q^2(L-x)^4}{4}\,dx=\frac{q^2L^5}{20}.
$$
Therefore
$$
U=\frac{q^2L^5}{40\,EI}=\frac{4\cdot1024}{40\cdot8\,000}\,\mathrm{kN\,m}=0.0128\,\mathrm{kN\,m}.
$$
The strain energy is therefore $12.8\,\mathrm{J}$. The value $25.6\,\mathrm{J}$ omits the factor $\dfrac12$ in the energy expression.
