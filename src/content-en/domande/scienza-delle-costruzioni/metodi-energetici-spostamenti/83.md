A beam with $AB=4\,\mathrm{m}$ and $EI=4\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ is fixed at $A$ and supported at $B$. An overhang $BC$ of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$ extends beyond $B$ and carries a downward force of $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$. What is the rotation of $B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-0.7,P" quote="A,B,4 m;B,C,2 m" titolo="Propped cantilever with an overhang"}

- $3\,\mathrm{mrad}$ clockwise
- $3\,\mathrm{mrad}$ counterclockwise
- $4\,\mathrm{mrad}$ clockwise
- $1.5\,\mathrm{mrad}$ clockwise

## Explanation

The overhang is statically determinate, so its action on joint $B$ is known directly. The force at $C$ transmits a clockwise couple $Pa=6\cdot2=12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ to the joint as well as a vertical force carried by the support.

Only span $AB$ resists rotation at $B$. Its far end is fixed, so its rotational stiffness is $\dfrac{4EI}{L}=4\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

Joint equilibrium gives $4\,000\,\varphi_B=-12$, hence $\varphi_B=-0.003\,\mathrm{rad}$.

The rotation is therefore $3\,\mathrm{mrad}$, **clockwise**. Value $4\,\mathrm{mrad}$ uses stiffness $\dfrac{3EI}{L}$, which applies to a member pinned at its far end.
