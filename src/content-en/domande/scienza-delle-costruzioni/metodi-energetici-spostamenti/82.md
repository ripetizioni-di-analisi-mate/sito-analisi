A continuous beam is fixed at $A$ and $C$ and has a vertical support at $B$. Unloaded spans $AB$ and $BC$ have the same length $L$ and the same $EI$. Support $B$ settles vertically by $\Delta$. What is the rotation of $B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello;C,incastro,0" frecce="3.4,0,3.4,-0.6,Δ" quote="A,B,L;B,C,L" titolo="Settlement of the intermediate support"}

- $\dfrac{3\Delta}{2L}$ clockwise
- $\dfrac{\Delta}{L}$ clockwise
- $\dfrac{3\Delta}{4L}$ counterclockwise
- $0$

## Explanation

With $\Delta$ downward, the chord of $AB$ rotates through $\psi_{AB}=-\dfrac{\Delta}{L}$ and the chord of $BC$ through $\psi_{BC}=+\dfrac{\Delta}{L}$.

The settlement contributes couples $-\dfrac{6EI}{L}\psi_{AB}=+\dfrac{6EI\Delta}{L^2}$ from $AB$ and $-\dfrac{6EI}{L}\psi_{BC}=-\dfrac{6EI\Delta}{L^2}$ from $BC$ at $B$, which cancel.

Joint equilibrium therefore reduces to $\dfrac{8EI}{L}\varphi_B=0$, giving $\varphi_B=0$.

The same result follows from symmetry about the vertical through $B$. The deflected shape is symmetric and the tangent at $B$ remains horizontal.
