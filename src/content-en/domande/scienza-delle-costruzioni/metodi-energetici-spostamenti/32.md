The loads in system 1 are $Q_A^{(1)}=6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $Q_B^{(1)}=2\,\mathrm{kN}$. System 2 produces conjugate displacements $d_A^{(2)}=3\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $d_B^{(2)}=-1\,\mathrm{mm}$. What is the mutual work?

- $16\,\mathrm{J}$
- $8\,\mathrm{J}$
- $20\,\mathrm{J}$
- $12\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The mutual work is $6\cdot3+2\cdot(-1)=16\,\mathrm{J}$. The products of loads in one system and displacements in the other do not include the factor $\dfrac12$ used for work during gradual loading.
