A support can only push upward, with reaction $X\geq0$ in kN. The force-method model gives complementary energy $\Pi^*(X)=a(X+3)^2+C$, where $a>0$ and $C$ is constant. What reaction minimizes this function over the physically admissible values?

- $-3\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $+3\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $+6\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The unconstrained minimum is at $X=-3$, which would require the support to pull. For $X\ge0$ the derivative $2a(X+3)$ is positive. The admissible minimum is therefore the endpoint $X=0$, corresponding to an unloaded support.
