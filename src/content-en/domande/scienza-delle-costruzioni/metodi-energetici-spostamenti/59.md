A continuous beam on three supports has two equal spans of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ with constant $EI$. There is a pin at $A$ and rollers at $B$ and $C$. A uniform downward load $q=4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts over the full beam. Take the reaction at the intermediate support $B$ as the redundant. What is its value?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;C,carrello" carichi="A,C,0.45,0.45,q" quote="A,B,3 m;B,C,3 m" titolo="Continuous beam on three supports under a uniform load"}

- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward

## Explanation

Removing support $B$ gives a simply supported beam of span $2L=6\,\mathrm{m}$ carrying $q$ and the unknown upward reaction $X$ at midspan.

The **Menabrea** condition imposes zero downward deflection at $B$. For the simply supported beam, load $q$ produces midspan deflection $\dfrac{5q(2L)^4}{384EI}$ and load $X$ produces an upward midspan deflection $\dfrac{X(2L)^3}{48EI}$. Therefore
$$
\frac{X(2L)^3}{48EI}=\frac{5q(2L)^4}{384EI},
\qquad
X=\frac{5q\,(2L)}{8}=\frac{5qL}{4}.
$$
Substituting the given values gives $X=\dfrac{5\cdot4\cdot3}{4}\,\mathrm{kN}=15\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. Value $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the load on the strip of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ centered at $B$ and neglects beam continuity.
