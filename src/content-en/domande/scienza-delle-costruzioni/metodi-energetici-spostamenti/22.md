A beam of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$ with $EI=5\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ has deflection $w(x)=0.001\,x^2$, with $w$ and $x$ in meters and $w$ positive upward. What bending strain energy follows from the deflected shape?

- $40\,\mathrm{J}$
- $5\,\mathrm{J}$
- $20\,\mathrm{J}$
- $10\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

In terms of deflection, the bending strain energy is $U=\dfrac12\displaystyle\int_0^L EI\,(w'')^2\,dx$.

Differentiating twice gives $w''=0.002\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$, which is constant along the beam.

Therefore
$$
U=\frac12\cdot5\,000\cdot(0.002)^2\cdot2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}=0.020\,\mathrm{kN\,m}.
$$
The strain energy is therefore $20\,\mathrm{J}$. Using $w''=0.001$, as if differentiating $x^2$ gave $x$, would produce $5\,\mathrm{J}$.
