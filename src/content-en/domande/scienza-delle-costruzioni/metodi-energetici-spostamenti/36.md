For conjugate coordinates, $d_1=2Q_1+Q_2$ and $d_2=Q_1+3Q_2$, with $d$ in millimeters and $Q$ in kilonewtons. What is $U$ for $Q_1=2$ and $Q_2=4\,\mathrm{kN}$?

- $36\,\mathrm{J}$
- $72\,\mathrm{J}$
- $28\,\mathrm{J}$
- $44\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The displacements are $8$ and $14\,\mathrm{mm}$. The product $\dfrac12(2\cdot8+4\cdot14)$ equals $36\,\mathrm{J}$.
