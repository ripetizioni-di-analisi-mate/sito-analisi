A single force acts on a linearly elastic structure with stationary supports and no imposed strains. Its magnitude doubles with no change in direction. How does the strain energy change?

- It doubles.
- It increases to four times its initial value.
- It remains constant.
- It increases to eight times its initial value.

## Explanation

The conjugate displacement doubles because it varies linearly with the force. The product $\dfrac12 Qd$ therefore increases by a factor of four.
