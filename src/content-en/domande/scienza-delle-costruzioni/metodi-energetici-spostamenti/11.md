A prismatic beam of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ has $EI=3\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. Both ends have zero translation. The rotation is zero at $B$, and a counterclockwise rotation of $0.002\,\mathrm{rad}$ is imposed at $A$. With no loads on the member, find the external couple at $A$ required to maintain the rotation.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,incastro,0" quote="A,B,3 m" etichette="0.47,0.62,prescribed rotation at A" titolo="Beam with translations restrained and a prescribed rotation at A"}

- $4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

With both end translations zero and the far-end rotation restrained, the rotational stiffness at the near end is $\dfrac{4EI}{L}=4\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Multiplying this stiffness by the prescribed rotation gives the external couple.
