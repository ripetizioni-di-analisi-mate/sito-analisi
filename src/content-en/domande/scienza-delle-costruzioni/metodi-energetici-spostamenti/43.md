A cantilever of span $4\,\mathrm{m}$ has $EI=6\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. A downward load of $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from the fixed end. What is the downward tip deflection?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,2,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="A,B,4 m" forze="C,0,-0.88,P" titolo="Point force within a cantilever span"}

- $4\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $8\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $16\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $10\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The actual moment is $-9(2-x)$ only over the first two meters. The unit tip force gives $m=-(4-x)$. Integration of the product gives $\dfrac{9}{6\,000}\int_0^2(2-x)(4-x)\,\mathrm{d}x=0.010\,\mathrm{m}$.
