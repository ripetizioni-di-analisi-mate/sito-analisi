A simply supported beam of span $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and flexural rigidity $EI=6\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ carries only a counterclockwise couple $C=12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ applied at support $A$. Use the bending strain energy and Clapeyron’s theorem to find the rotation of $A$ in the direction of the couple.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" coppie="A,1,C" quote="A,B,3 m" titolo="Couple applied at support A"}

- $1\,\mathrm{mrad}$
- $3\,\mathrm{mrad}$
- $6\,\mathrm{mrad}$
- $2\,\mathrm{mrad}$

## Explanation

The reactions form a couple balancing $C$. The bending moment varies linearly from $C$ at $A$ to zero at $B$, giving $|M(x)|=C\left(1-\dfrac{x}{L}\right)$.

The strain energy is
$$
U=\int_0^L\frac{C^2(1-x/L)^2}{2EI}\,dx=\frac{C^2L}{6EI}.
$$
By **Clapeyron’s theorem**, we have $U=\dfrac12 C\varphi_A$, so
$$
\varphi_A=\frac{2U}{C}=\frac{CL}{3EI}=\frac{12\cdot3}{3\cdot6\,000}\,\mathrm{rad}=0.002\,\mathrm{rad}.
$$
The value $1\,\mathrm{mrad}$, equivalent to $\dfrac{CL}{6EI}$, is the rotation at the opposite support $B$.
