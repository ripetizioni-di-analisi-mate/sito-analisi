A segment of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$ has $EA=20\,000\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $EI=4\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. The only relevant internal actions are an axial force $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ and a constant bending moment of magnitude $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. What is the total strain energy?

- $20\,\mathrm{J}$
- $16\,\mathrm{J}$
- $72\,\mathrm{J}$
- $36\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The axial contribution is $\dfrac{20^2\cdot2}{2\cdot20\,000}=0.020\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The bending contribution is $\dfrac{8^2\cdot2}{2\cdot4\,000}=0.016\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The sum is $36\,\mathrm{J}$.
