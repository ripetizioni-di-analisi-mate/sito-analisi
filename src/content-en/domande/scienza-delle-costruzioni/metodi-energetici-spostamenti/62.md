The strain energy of the primary structure for an indeterminate beam is $U(X)=0.003X^2-0.024X+0.05$ in $\mathrm{kN\,m}$ as a function of the redundant reaction $X$ in kilonewtons. The released support does not settle. What is $X$?

- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The released restraint requires zero displacement, so the **Menabrea** condition gives $\dfrac{dU}{dX}=0$.

The derivative is $\dfrac{dU}{dX}=0.006X-0.024$.

Setting the derivative to zero gives $X=\dfrac{0.024}{0.006}=4\,\mathrm{kN}$.

Since the coefficient of $X^2$ is positive, this value minimizes the strain energy. Value $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ omits the factor $2$ when differentiating $X^2$.
