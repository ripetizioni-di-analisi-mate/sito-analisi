A counterclockwise couple of $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ acts at the tip of a cantilever of span $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and flexural rigidity $EI=3\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. Find the vertical displacement a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from the fixed end, taking upward as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,2,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="A,B,4 m" coppie="B,1,C_B" titolo="End couple and section C where the response is measured"}

- $4\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $8\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $16\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The actual moment is constant and positive. The upward auxiliary load at the interior section gives $m=2-x$ over the first two meters and zero beyond. The integral equals $\dfrac{6}{3\,000}\cdot2=0.004\,\mathrm{m}$.
