In a frame, coordinate 2 has prescribed displacement $d_2=4\,\mathrm{mm}$, while coordinate 1 has free rotation $d_1=0.002\,\mathrm{rad}$. The stiffness matrix row for coordinate 2 contains $K_{21}=-1\,500\,\mathrm{kN/rad}$ and $K_{22}=2\,000\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. What force is required at coordinate 2 to maintain the prescribed displacement?

- $5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $11\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-5\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Once the displacements and rotations are known, the equation for the prescribed coordinate gives its required force, $f_2=K_{21}d_1+K_{22}d_2$.

Express the displacement in meters, giving $d_2=0.004\,\mathrm m$.

Therefore, $f_2=-1\,500\cdot0.002+2\,000\cdot0.004=-3+8=5\,\mathrm{kN}$.

Value $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ omits the contribution of the free rotation, while $11\,\mathrm{kN}$ reverses its sign.
