In an otherwise unloaded linearly elastic structure, a jack gradually imposes a support displacement from zero to $6\,\mathrm{mm}$. At the end the jack exerts $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ on the structure in the direction of motion. How much strain energy is stored? Neglect friction.

- $18\,\mathrm{J}$
- $0\,\mathrm{J}$
- $3\,\mathrm{J}$
- $9\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The reaction grows linearly from zero to its final value. Work by the jack is the triangle under the force–displacement graph, $\dfrac12\cdot3000\cdot0.006=9\,\mathrm{J}$. Fixed supports do no work and the loading is quasistatic.
