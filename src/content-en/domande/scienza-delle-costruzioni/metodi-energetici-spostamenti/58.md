A beam of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ with $EI=4\,500\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ is fixed at $A$ and simply supported at $B$. There are no applied loads, but support $B$ settles by $6\,\mathrm{mm}$. What reaction does the support exert on the beam?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" frecce="3.45,0,3.45,-0.6,6 mm" quote="A,B,3 m" titolo="Vertical settlement of support B"}

- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $1\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward
- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward

## Explanation

Remove the support and apply an unknown upward reaction $X$ at $B$. In the primary cantilever structure, the strain energy is $U=\dfrac{X^2L^3}{6EI}$.

The support imposes a downward displacement of $6\,\mathrm{mm}$ at $B$, or $-0.006\,\mathrm{m}$ in the direction of $X$. The **Menabrea** condition with prescribed displacement is therefore
$$
\frac{\partial U}{\partial X}=\frac{XL^3}{3EI}=-0.006\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
It follows that $X=-\dfrac{3\cdot4\,500\cdot0.006}{27}\,\mathrm{kN}=-3\,\mathrm{kN}$.

The support therefore exerts a force of $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ **downward**, drawing the tip to the prescribed position. Value $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ uses the stiffness $\dfrac{12EI}{L^3}$, which applies when rotation is also restrained at $B$.
