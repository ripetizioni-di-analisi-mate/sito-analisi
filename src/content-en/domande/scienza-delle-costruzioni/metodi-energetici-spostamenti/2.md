A cantilever of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ has flexural rigidity $EI=3\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. A downward force of $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at the free end. Calculate the tip displacement, neglecting shear deformation.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="A,B,3 m" forze="B,0,-0.7,P" titolo="Cantilever with a vertical force at the tip"}

- $15\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $10\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $30\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $45\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The bending strain energy is $\dfrac{P^2L^3}{6EI}$. Differentiating with respect to $P$ gives $v_B=\dfrac{PL^3}{3EI}=0.030\,\mathrm{m}$.
