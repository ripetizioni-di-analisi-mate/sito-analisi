On a segment of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$, the shear force is $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the effective shear rigidity is $GA_s=30\,000\,\mathrm{kN}$. What is the contribution of shear to the strain energy?

- $15\,\mathrm{J}$
- $\dfrac{15}{4}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $\dfrac{15}{2}\,\mathrm{J}$
- $\dfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

Integrate a constant energy density. The contribution is $\dfrac{15^2\cdot2}{2\cdot30\,000}=0.0075\,\mathrm{kN\,m}=7.5\,\mathrm{J}$.
