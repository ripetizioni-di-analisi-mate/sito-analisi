An actuator imposes a nonzero displacement at an otherwise restrained point. Which generalized force enters the work balance for that coordinate?

- The force exerted by the actuator to maintain the prescribed displacement.
- A unit force in the perpendicular direction.
- The bending moment at midspan.
- Only the force applied at points with zero displacement.

## Explanation

When the coordinate moves, its conjugate force can do work. Evaluate the work contribution $W_{\mathrm{att}}=\int F_{\mathrm{att}}\,\mathrm{d}u$ of actuator force $F_{\mathrm{att}}$ along the actuator’s loading path.
