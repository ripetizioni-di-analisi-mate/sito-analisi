A portal frame has a rigid crossbeam and two columns with the same $EI$, fixed at their bases and rigidly connected to the crossbeam. The left column has height $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and the right column height $4\,\mathrm{m}$. The columns are axially inextensible, and a horizontal force of $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts on the crossbeam. What is the shear force in the left column?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,2;B,0,4;C,5,4;D,5,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" vincoli="A,incastro;D,incastro" forze="C,0.9,0,Q" quote="A,B,2 m,-0.7;D,C,4 m,-0.7" titolo="Portal frame with columns of different heights"}

- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $16\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $2\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The rigid crossbeam imposes the same translation $\Delta$ and zero rotation at both column tops. Each column therefore has stiffness $\dfrac{12EI}{H^3}$.

The ratio of their stiffnesses is $\dfrac{1}{2^3}:\dfrac{1}{4^3}=8:1$.

Crossbeam equilibrium requires the sum of the two shear forces to be $18\,\mathrm{kN}$. Each force is proportional to its column stiffness. The left column therefore carries $\dfrac{8}{9}\cdot18=16\,\mathrm{kN}$.

The shorter column carries nearly all the force. Value $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ distributes the force in proportion to $\dfrac1H$ instead of $\dfrac{1}{H^3}$.
