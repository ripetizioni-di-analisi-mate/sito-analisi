A prismatic bar of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and constant $EA$ is restrained axially at both ends $A$ and $B$. A rightward axial force of $30\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at section $C$, a distance $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. What is the magnitude of the axial reaction at $A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,1,0;B,3,0" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,incastro,0" forze="C,0.6,0,F" quote="A,C,1 m;C,B,2 m" titolo="Bar restrained at both ends with an axial force between them"}

- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $30\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Remove the restraint at $B$ and introduce an unknown leftward reaction $X$. Segment $AC$ of length $a=1\,\mathrm{m}$ carries axial force $F-X$. Segment $CB$ of length $b=2\,\mathrm{m}$ carries axial force $-X$.

The **Menabrea** condition requires zero displacement at $B$, so
$$
\frac{\partial U}{\partial X}=\frac{-(F-X)a+Xb}{EA}=0,
\qquad
X=\frac{Fa}{L}=10\,\mathrm{kN}.
$$
Bar equilibrium gives a leftward reaction of $F-X=20\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $A$.

The shorter segment is stiffer and therefore carries the larger share of the force. Value $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ interchanges the two reactions.
