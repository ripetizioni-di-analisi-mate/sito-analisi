A beam of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$ with $EI=4\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ has restrained end translations and zero rotation at $B$. With no member loads, impose a counterclockwise rotation of $0.005\,\mathrm{rad}$ at $A$. What strain energy is stored in the beam?

- $200\,\mathrm{J}$
- $50\,\mathrm{J}$
- $75\,\mathrm{J}$
- $100\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The required couple at $A$ is $m_A=\dfrac{4EI}{L}\varphi_A=\dfrac{4\cdot4\,000}{2}\cdot0.005\,\mathrm{kN\,m}=40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

The couple at $B$ does no work because the rotation at $B$ is zero. By **Clapeyron’s theorem**, the strain energy is therefore
$$
U=\frac12m_A\varphi_A=\frac{2EI}{L}\varphi_A^2=\frac12\cdot40\cdot0.005\,\mathrm{kN\,m}=0.1\,\mathrm{kN\,m}.
$$
The strain energy is therefore $100\,\mathrm{J}$. Value $200\,\mathrm{J}$ omits factor $\dfrac12$, while $75\,\mathrm{J}$ uses the pinned-end stiffness $\dfrac{3EI}{L}$.
