A simply supported beam of span $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and flexural rigidity $EI=8\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ carries a downward point load of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ at midspan. Calculate the bending strain energy.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,4,0" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-0.8,P" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,2 m" titolo="Simply supported beam with a midspan force"}

- $0.024\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $0.006\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $0.036\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $0.012\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

To the left of midspan, the bending moment is $\dfrac{Px}{2}$. Integrating the strain energy over half the span and doubling gives $U=\dfrac{P^2L^3}{96EI}=0.012\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
