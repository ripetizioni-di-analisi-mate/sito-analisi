Consider two load states of the same linearly elastic structure with stationary supports. The first state has a single load. The second produces zero displacement at the point and in the direction of that load. What work do the loads of the second system perform through the displacements of the first?

- Half the work of the first load system.
- The work of the second load system through its own displacements.
- Twice the work of the first load system.
- Zero.

## Explanation

The loads of the first system do zero work through the displacements of the second ($W_{1\to2}=0$). Betti’s theorem requires the same mutual work when the states are interchanged ($W_{2\to1}=0$).
