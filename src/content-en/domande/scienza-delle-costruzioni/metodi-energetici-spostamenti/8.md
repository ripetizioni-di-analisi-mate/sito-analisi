A cantilever of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$ has flexural rigidity $EI=4\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. A uniform downward load of intensity $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts over the full span. Calculate the tip deflection using the Euler–Bernoulli beam model.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="A,B,2 m" carichi="A,B,0.38,0.38,q" titolo="Uniform load over the entire span"}

- $2\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $1\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $4\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $\dfrac83\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

Introduce an auxiliary tip force. Differentiating the energy with respect to that force gives the desired displacement. Integrate $\dfrac{q(L-x)^3}{2EI}$ over the full span to obtain $\dfrac{qL^4}{8EI}=0.002\,\mathrm{m}$.
