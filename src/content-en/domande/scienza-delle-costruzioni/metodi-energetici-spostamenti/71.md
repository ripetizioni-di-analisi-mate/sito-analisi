A portal frame has a rigid crossbeam $BC$. Column $AB$ is fixed at its base and rigidly connected to the crossbeam. Column $DC$ is pinned both at base $D$ and at connection $C$ to the crossbeam. The columns are axially inextensible, and a horizontal force of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts on the crossbeam. What is the shear force in column $DC$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;C,4,3;D,4,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,incastro;D,cerniera" forze="B,-0.9,0,Q" quote="A,B,3 m,-0.8" titolo="Portal frame with one column pinned at both ends"}

- $0$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $2.4\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Column $DC$ is pinned at both ends and carries no intermediate loads. Its bending moment is zero at $D$ and $C$.

Its moment varies linearly between two zero values, and the shear force is the derivative of that moment. The shear therefore vanishes. The column acts as a two-force member carrying only axial force, with zero transverse stiffness.

Column $AB$ therefore carries the entire force $12\,\mathrm{kN}$.

Value $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ divides the force equally. Value $2.4\,\mathrm{kN}$ assigns column $DC$ the stiffness $\dfrac{3EI}{H^3}$ of a column pinned at only one end.
