A portal frame has a rigid crossbeam and two identical columns of height $3\,\mathrm{m}$ fixed at their bases. The columns are axially inextensible, and a horizontal force of $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts on the crossbeam. What is the magnitude of the bending moment at the base of each column?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;C,4,3;D,4,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" vincoli="A,incastro;D,incastro" forze="C,0.9,0,Q" quote="A,B,3 m,-0.8" titolo="Symmetric portal frame with a rigid crossbeam"}

- $15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $60\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The identical columns each carry shear force $10\,\mathrm{kN}$.

The rigid crossbeam imposes zero rotation at the column tops, matching the zero rotation at the bases. For a column with relative translation $\Delta$ and zero end rotations, both external end couples are $\dfrac{6EI\Delta}{H^2}$. The internal moments at the top and base have equal magnitudes and opposite signs.

The moment varies linearly and vanishes at midheight, giving a base moment of $T\cdot\dfrac H2=10\cdot1.5=15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

Value $30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is the moment for a cantilever column with its top free to rotate.
