A beam of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and flexural rigidity $EI=2\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ is restrained against translation at both ends. End $B$ is pinned, and a counterclockwise rotation of $0.004\,\mathrm{rad}$ is imposed at $A$. With no loads on the member, calculate the external couple at $A$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,B,2 m" etichette="0.47,0.55,prescribed rotation at A" titolo="Prescribed rotation at A with zero moment at B"}

- $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The hinge allows rotation at $B$ and requires zero moment at that section. Eliminating the free rotation gives the stiffness $\dfrac{3EI}{L}$ at $A$. Multiplication by $0.004\,\mathrm{rad}$ gives $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
