The equilibrium equations for two frame joints, with rotations in milliradians and couples in $\mathrm{kN\,m}$, are
$$
10\theta_1+3\theta_2=17,
\qquad
3\theta_1+5\theta_2=1.
$$
What is $\theta_2$?

- $1\,\mathrm{mrad}$
- $-1\,\mathrm{mrad}$
- $0.2\,\mathrm{mrad}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{mrad}$

## Explanation

Multiplying the second equation by $10$ and the first by $3$ gives $30\theta_1+50\theta_2=10$ and $30\theta_1+9\theta_2=51$.

Subtracting the second from the first gives $41\theta_2=-41$ and hence $\theta_2=-1\,\mathrm{mrad}$.

Substitution into the first equation gives $\theta_1=2\,\mathrm{mrad}$. The second equation holds because $6-5=1$.

Value $0.2\,\mathrm{mrad}$ divides the right-hand side by the diagonal coefficient, neglecting coupling between the joints.
