In a linearly elastic structure with stationary supports, a unit force at $A$ produces a horizontal displacement at $B$. To check Maxwell’s reciprocal theorem, which load is applied in the second state and which response is measured?

- A vertical force at $B$ and the horizontal displacement at $A$.
- A couple at $B$ and the vertical deflection at $A$.
- A unit force at $B$ in the chosen horizontal direction and the displacement at $A$ in the direction of the first load.
- The same force at $A$ and the rotation at $B$.

## Explanation

The load in the second state must be conjugate to the response measured in the first. Interchange the load and measurement coordinates, keeping their positive directions.
