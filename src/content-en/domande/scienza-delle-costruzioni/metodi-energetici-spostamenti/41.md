In consistent units, a system has strain energy $U=0.002 Q_1^2+0.001 Q_1Q_2+0.003 Q_2^2$ in $\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with $Q_i$ in kilonewtons. Find $d_1$ for $Q_1=4$ and $Q_2=3\,\mathrm{kN}$.

- $11\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $19\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $16\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $22\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

Differentiation with respect to $Q_1$ gives $0.004Q_1+0.001Q_2$. Substitution gives $0.019\,\mathrm{m}$.
