Two joints in a frame with no sidesway are connected by a member of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ with $EI=4\,500\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. They share no other member. What is the stiffness matrix coefficient $K_{12}$ that relates the couple at joint 1 to the rotation of joint 2?

- $6\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $1\,500\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $3\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $4\,500\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

Coefficient $K_{12}$ is the couple required at joint 1 when joint 2 rotates through one radian and all other coordinates are restrained.

Only the connecting member contributes. Rotation at either end transmits a couple $\dfrac{2EI}{L}$ per radian to the other end.

Thus the coefficient is $K_{12}=\dfrac{2\cdot4\,500}{3}\,\mathrm{kN\,m}=3\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ per radian.

Value $6\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is this member’s contribution $\dfrac{4EI}{L}$ to the diagonal coefficient.
