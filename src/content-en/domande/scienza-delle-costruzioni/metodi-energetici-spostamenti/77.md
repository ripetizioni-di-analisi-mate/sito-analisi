Three members of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ with the same $EI$ meet at rigid joint $N$ of a frame with no sidesway. Beam $NA$ is fixed at $A$, beam $NC$ is pinned at $C$, and column $ND$ is fixed at base $D$. A counterclockwise couple of $22\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ acts at the joint. What couple is applied to end $N$ of the column?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,3;N,3,3;C,6,3;D,3,0" aste="A-N;N-C;N-D" vincoli="A,incastro,180;C,cerniera;D,incastro" coppie="N,1,C_N" quote="A,N,3 m,-0.6;N,C,3 m,-0.6;D,N,3 m,0.7" titolo="Joint connecting three members"}

- $22\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $11\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

All three members rotate through the same angle $\varphi_N$ as the joint. Members fixed at their far ends have rotational stiffness $\dfrac{4EI}{L}$. The member pinned at its far end has stiffness $\dfrac{3EI}{L}$.

The joint stiffness is therefore $(4+3+4)\dfrac{EI}{L}=\dfrac{11EI}{L}$, and equilibrium gives $\dfrac{11EI}{L}\varphi_N=22\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

Each member receives a share of the couple proportional to its rotational stiffness. The column receives $\dfrac{4}{11}\cdot22=8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

Value $11\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ omits beam $NC$, while $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ gives the column stiffness $\dfrac{3EI}{L}$.
