In a linearly elastic structure with stationary supports, a downward force of $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $A$ produces a counterclockwise rotation of $0.002\,\mathrm{rad}$ at $B$. What is the deflection of $A$ in the direction of the force when the only load at $B$ is a counterclockwise couple of $5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$?

- $2\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $4\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $1\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $-1\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The rotation-per-force coefficient equals the displacement-per-couple coefficient. The deflection is $5\cdot\dfrac{0.002}{10}=0.001\,\mathrm{m}$.
