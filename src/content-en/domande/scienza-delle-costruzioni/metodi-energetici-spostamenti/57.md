A cantilever of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$ with $EI=4\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ carries a downward tip force of $10\,\mathrm{kN}$. The tip rests on a vertical linearly elastic spring of stiffness $k=1\,500\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. What is the force in the spring?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" forze="B,0,-0.8,P" linee="2,0 2,-0.15 2.15,-0.22 1.85,-0.36 2.15,-0.5 1.85,-0.64 2,-0.71 2,-0.85;1.7,-0.85 2.3,-0.85" etichette="2.4,-0.45,k" quote="A,B,2 m,-0.5" titolo="Cantilever with an elastic tip support"}

- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Remove the spring and introduce its upward force $X$ on the beam. The total energy includes both the cantilever energy and the spring energy $\dfrac{X^2}{2k}$.

The **Menabrea** condition $\dfrac{\partial U}{\partial X}=0$ gives
$$
-\frac{(P-X)L^3}{3EI}+\frac{X}{k}=0,
\qquad
X=\frac{P}{1+\dfrac{3EI}{kL^3}}.
$$
Since $\dfrac{3EI}{L^3}=\dfrac{3\cdot4\,000}{8}\,\mathrm{kN/m}=1\,500\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, the cantilever and spring have equal stiffness, and $X=\dfrac{P}{2}=5\,\mathrm{kN}$.

Value $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ corresponds to a rigid support, with infinite $k$.
