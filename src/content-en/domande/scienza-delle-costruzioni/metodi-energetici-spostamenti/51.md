A quarter-circular member of radius $R$ and constant flexural rigidity $EI$ is fixed at $A$, where the tangent is vertical. Its free end $B$, where the tangent is horizontal, carries a downward force $P$. Considering only bending, what is the downward deflection of $B$?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,2,0;P1,1.932,0.518,-;P2,1.732,1,-;P3,1.414,1.414,-;P4,1,1.732,-;P5,0.518,1.932,-;B,0,2" aste="A-P1;P1-P2;P2-P3;P3-P4;P4-P5;P5-B" vincoli="A,incastro" forze="B,0,-0.8,P" tratteggi="0,0 2,0;0,0 0,2" etichette="1,-0.25,R" titolo="Quarter-circular member with center O"}

- $\dfrac{\pi PR^3}{4EI}$
- $\dfrac{PR^3}{3EI}$
- $\dfrac{\pi PR^3}{2EI}$
- $\dfrac{PR^3}{2EI}$

## Explanation

Let $\theta$ be the angle between segment $OA$ and the line joining $O$ to the point on the member. The point has coordinates $(R\cos\theta,R\sin\theta)$, and $B$ lies on the vertical through $O$. The force has moment arm $R\cos\theta$ and the moment magnitude is $PR\cos\theta$.

The differential member length is $ds=R\,d\theta$. By **Castigliano’s theorem**
$$
v_B=\frac{1}{EI}\int_0^{\pi/2}PR^2\cos^2\theta\,R\,d\theta=\frac{PR^3}{EI}\cdot\frac{\pi}{4}.
$$
The downward deflection is therefore $\dfrac{\pi PR^3}{4EI}$. Value $\dfrac{PR^3}{3EI}$ is the deflection of a straight cantilever of length $R$.
