The flexibility coefficient relating two coordinates is negative. What does this sign indicate?

- The energy of every load state is negative.
- A positive load at one coordinate induces a negative displacement at the other.
- The direct stiffness of both coordinates is zero.
- Reciprocity requires the transposed coefficient to be positive.

## Explanation

The sign $f_{ij}<0$ refers to the direction of the displacement at the other coordinate. Reciprocity gives the transposed coefficient the same sign, $f_{ji}=f_{ij}$.
