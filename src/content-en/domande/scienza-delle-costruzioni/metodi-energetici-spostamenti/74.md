An unloaded beam of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ with $\dfrac{EI}{L}=1\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ has upward end displacements $w_A=0$ and $w_B=6\,\mathrm{mm}$. The two end rotations are $\varphi_A=\varphi_B=0.002\,\mathrm{rad}$ counterclockwise. What is the external couple $m_A$?

- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise
- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ clockwise
- $4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise
- $0$

## Explanation

The chord rotation is $\psi=\dfrac{w_B-w_A}{L}=\dfrac{0.006}{3}=0.002\,\mathrm{rad}$.

Both end rotations equal $\psi$, so the beam undergoes infinitesimal rigid-body motion. It translates and rotates without developing curvature.

Indeed, $m_A=\dfrac{EI}{L}(4\varphi_A+2\varphi_B-6\psi)=1\,000\,(0.008+0.004-0.012)\,\mathrm{kN\,m}=0$.

The counterclockwise value $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ omits translation, while the clockwise value $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ includes translation alone.
