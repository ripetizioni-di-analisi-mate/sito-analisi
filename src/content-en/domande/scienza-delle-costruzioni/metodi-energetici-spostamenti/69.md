The displacement method shows that the left end $A$ of a horizontal member requires an external counterclockwise couple $m_A=6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. If $M>0$ puts the bottom fibers in tension, what is the bending moment at section $A$?

- $M_A=6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the lower fibers in tension
- $M_A=-3\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the upper fibers in tension
- $M_A=-6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the upper fibers in tension
- $M_A=0$, because the couple is external

## Explanation

Isolate a short beam segment immediately to the right of end $A$. The external couple $m_A$ and, on the right cut face, internal moment $M_A$ act on this segment.

A positive moment puts the bottom fibers in tension and acts counterclockwise on the right face of the left portion. Moment equilibrium of the segment therefore gives $m_A+M_A=0$.

Thus $M_A=-6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, and the **top fibers** are in tension at section $A$.

This is relation $m_A=-EIw''(0)$ from the theory. At the right end the relation is $m_B=EIw''(L)$, with the same sign as the internal moment.
