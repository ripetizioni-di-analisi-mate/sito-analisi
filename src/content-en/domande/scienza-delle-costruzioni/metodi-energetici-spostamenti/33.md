Two reciprocity tests are to be carried out on the same structure. However, one support is removed for the second test. What conclusion follows from the measured coefficients?

- The coefficients are equal for all load positions.
- Maxwell’s equality between the coefficients is no longer guaranteed.
- The coefficients must be equal and opposite.
- Both coefficients are zero.

## Explanation

The proof of reciprocity $f_{ij}=f_{ji}$ requires the same stiffness operator and support conditions in both states. Removing a support changes the problem.
