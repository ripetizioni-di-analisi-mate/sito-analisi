Use Castigliano’s theorem to calculate the change in distance between two unloaded joints $C$ and $D$ in a linearly elastic frame. Which auxiliary system should be introduced?

- A single force at $C$ along line $CD$
- Equal forces at $C$ and $D$ pointing in the same direction, parallel to $CD$
- Equal and opposite forces applied at $C$ and $D$ along line $CD$
- Equal and opposite couples at $C$ and $D$

## Explanation

The derivative of strain energy with respect to a load gives its conjugate displacement.

Two equal and opposite forces $F$ along $CD$ do work $F(u_D-u_C)$, where $u_C$ and $u_D$ are the joint displacements along line $CD$ in the directions of their respective forces. Their sum equals the change in distance between the joints.

Thus derivative $\dfrac{\partial U}{\partial F}$, evaluated after differentiation at $F=0$, gives the increase in separation. A single force at $C$ would give the absolute displacement of $C$, while two opposite couples would give the relative rotation.
