A symmetric portal frame has two identical fixed-base columns rigidly connected to a flexurally deformable crossbeam. The members are axially inextensible. A vertical load acts uniformly over the full crossbeam. Which displacement unknown is zero?

- The rotations of the two top joints
- The horizontal translation of the crossbeam
- The rotation of the left joint only
- None, because all three unknowns are nonzero

## Explanation

The structure and loading are symmetric about the vertical centerline, so the deflected shape is also symmetric.

In a symmetric deflected shape the two top joints have equal and opposite rotations. The crossbeam load makes these rotations nonzero.

Horizontal crossbeam translation would move both joints in the same direction, violating symmetry.

The horizontal translation is therefore **zero**. The remaining unknowns are the two rotations, equal in magnitude and opposite in direction.
