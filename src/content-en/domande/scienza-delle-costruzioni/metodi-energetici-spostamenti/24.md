In a linearly elastic structure with stationary supports and no imposed strains, a force of $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ produces a conjugate displacement of $2\,\mathrm{mm}$. In the same final state, a couple of $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ has a conjugate rotation of $0.004\,\mathrm{rad}$. Both final force–displacement products are positive. What is the strain energy according to Clapeyron’s theorem?

- $27\,\mathrm{J}$
- $54\,\mathrm{J}$
- $15\,\mathrm{J}$
- $12\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The sum of the two final products is $15\cdot0.002+6\cdot0.004=0.054\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The factor $\dfrac12$ gives $27\,\mathrm{J}$.
