Over the only common segment, of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$, the two states produce constant bending moments $M_1=6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and $M_2=-4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Here $EI=4\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. What is the mutual work due to bending?

- $12\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{J}$
- $-24\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The product $M_1M_2$ is negative. The integral equals $\dfrac{6(-4)\cdot2}{4\,000}=-0.012\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
