A cantilever of span $L$ and flexural rigidity $EI$ carries a downward triangular load of intensity $q_0$ at the fixed end and zero at the free end. What is the deflection at the free end?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" carichi="A,B,0.8,0,q_0" quote="A,B,L" titolo="Cantilever with a triangular load peaking at the fixed end"}

- $\dfrac{q_0L^4}{8EI}$
- $\dfrac{q_0L^4}{30EI}$
- $\dfrac{q_0L^4}{16EI}$
- $\dfrac{11q_0L^4}{120EI}$

## Explanation

Measure $s$ from the free end. The load on the segment of length $s$ increases from zero to $\dfrac{q_0s}{L}$. Its resultant is $\dfrac{q_0s^2}{2L}$ and acts a distance $\dfrac{s}{3}$ from the section. The bending moment therefore has magnitude $\dfrac{q_0s^3}{6L}$.

An auxiliary tip force $F$ adds moment $Fs$, so $\dfrac{\partial M}{\partial F}$ has magnitude $s$. By **Castigliano’s theorem**
$$
v=\frac{1}{EI}\int_0^L\frac{q_0s^3}{6L}\,s\,ds=\frac{q_0L^4}{30EI}.
$$
Value $\dfrac{11q_0L^4}{120EI}$ corresponds to the reversed triangular load, with the greatest intensity at the free end.
