The flexibility matrix of a linearly elastic structure, in $\mathrm{mm/kN}$, has entries $f_{11}=2$, $f_{22}=5$, and $f_{12}=1$. Apply $Q_1=4\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $Q_2=-2\,\mathrm{kN}$. What is the displacement $d_2$?

- $-10\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $6\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $-14\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

Maxwell’s reciprocal theorem gives $f_{21}=1$. This gives $d_2=f_{21}Q_1+f_{22}Q_2=4-10=-6\,\mathrm{mm}$.
