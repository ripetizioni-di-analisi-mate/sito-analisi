A beam carries a uniform load. Find the rotation at a section with no applied couple. What auxiliary load should be introduced?

- A vertical force at the section.
- A couple at the nearest support, even if it is fixed.
- A couple at the section in the positive direction of the rotation.
- A horizontal force at the section.

## Explanation

The energy derivative gives the displacement or rotation conjugate to the load. The load conjugate to rotation is a couple at the same section, $\theta=\dfrac{\partial U}{\partial M}$.
