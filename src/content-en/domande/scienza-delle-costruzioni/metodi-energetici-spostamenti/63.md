A beam with no intermediate loads has both end translations restrained and $\dfrac{EI}{L}=1\,500\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Impose end rotations $\varphi_A=0.002\,\mathrm{rad}$ and $\varphi_B=-0.001\,\mathrm{rad}$, positive counterclockwise. What external couple $m_B$ acts at end $B$?

- $0$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise
- $9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise
- $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ clockwise

## Explanation

With zero translations and no intermediate loads, $m_B=\dfrac{EI}{L}(2\varphi_A+4\varphi_B)$.

Substitution gives $m_B=1\,500\,(0.004-0.004)\,\mathrm{kN\,m}=0$.

Thus no couple is required at end $B$. This is the same condition as for a pinned end, at which $\varphi_B=-\dfrac{\varphi_A}{2}$.

Value $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ ignores the sign of $\varphi_B$, while $9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is the couple $m_A$.
