A uniform load of intensity $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts on a segment of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$. The final displacement in the direction of the load varies linearly from $0$ to $4\,\mathrm{mm}$. What does the load contribute to $U$ according to Clapeyron’s theorem?

- $24\,\mathrm{J}$
- $48\,\mathrm{J}$
- $12\,\mathrm{J}$
- $6\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The mean displacement is $2\,\mathrm{mm}$. The contribution is $\dfrac12\cdot6\cdot2\cdot0.002=0.012\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
