On a linear elastic structure, load system I applies downward forces of $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $B$ and $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$. System II applies only $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward at $D$, producing $1\,\mathrm{mm}$ downward displacement at $B$ and $2\,\mathrm{mm}$ upward at $C$. What is the displacement at $D$ under system I, positive downward?

- $+2\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $-1\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $+1\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

By Betti’s theorem, the work of loads I through displacements II equals that of loads II through displacements I. With the stated signs, $2\cdot1+3(-2)=4u_D$. Thus $u_D=-1\,\mathrm{mm}$, namely upward.
