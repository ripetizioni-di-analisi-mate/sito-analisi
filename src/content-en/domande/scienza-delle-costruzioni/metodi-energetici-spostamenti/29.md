In a linearly elastic structure with stationary supports, a downward force of $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $B$ produces a downward deflection of $3\,\mathrm{mm}$ at $C$. If the only load is a downward force of $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$, how far does $B$ move in its positive direction?

- $3\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $6\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $- \dfrac{3}{2}\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

By reciprocity, the common coefficient is $\dfrac38\,\mathrm{mm/kN}$. Multiplying by $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ gives $1.5\,\mathrm{mm}$.
