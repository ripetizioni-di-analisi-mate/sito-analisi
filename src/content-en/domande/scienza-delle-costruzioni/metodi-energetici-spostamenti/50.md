A simply supported beam of span $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and flexural rigidity $EI=12\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ carries a uniform downward load $q=9\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Use an auxiliary couple at $A$ to find the magnitude of the rotation at support $A$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,q" quote="A,B,4 m" titolo="Simply supported beam under a uniform load"}

- $1\,\mathrm{mrad}$
- $4\,\mathrm{mrad}$
- $8\,\mathrm{mrad}$
- $2\,\mathrm{mrad}$

## Explanation

Add an auxiliary clockwise couple $C_a$ at $A$, in the direction in which the load rotates the left support. The moment becomes $M=\dfrac{qx(L-x)}{2}+C_a\left(1-\dfrac{x}{L}\right)$, so $\dfrac{\partial M}{\partial C_a}=1-\dfrac{x}{L}$.

After differentiating, set $C_a=0$ to obtain
$$
\varphi_A=\frac{1}{EI}\int_0^L\frac{qx(L-x)}{2}\left(1-\frac{x}{L}\right)dx=\frac{qL^3}{24EI}.
$$
Substituting the given values gives $\varphi_A=\dfrac{9\cdot64}{24\cdot12\,000}\,\mathrm{rad}=0.002\,\mathrm{rad}$.

The rotation magnitude is therefore $2\,\mathrm{mrad}$. Value $1\,\mathrm{mrad}$ uses coefficient $\dfrac{1}{48}$ in place of $\dfrac{1}{24}$.
