Several coefficients appear in the displacement method for a member of length $L$ and flexural rigidity $EI$. Which coefficient gives a transverse force in kilonewtons when multiplied by an end rotation in radians?

- $\dfrac{12EI}{L^3}$
- $\dfrac{4EI}{L}$
- $\dfrac{6EI}{L^2}$
- $\dfrac{2EI}{L}$

## Explanation

With $EI$ in $\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ and $L$ in meters, ratio $\dfrac{6EI}{L^2}$ has units $\mathrm{kN}$ per radian.

Multiplying by rotation gives a force, specifically the transverse end force produced by rotation at an end.

Coefficient $\dfrac{12EI}{L^3}$ is measured in $\mathrm{kN/m}$ and multiplies a translation. Coefficients $\dfrac{4EI}{L}$ and $\dfrac{2EI}{L}$ have units $\mathrm{kN\,m}$ per radian and give couples when multiplied by a rotation.
