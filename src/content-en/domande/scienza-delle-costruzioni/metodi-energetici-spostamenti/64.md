A beam of span $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and flexural rigidity $EI=3\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ is restrained against vertical translation at $A$. At $B$ a guided support prevents rotation and allows vertical translation. No loads act on the member. What external couple must be applied at $A$ to impose a counterclockwise rotation of $0.004\,\mathrm{rad}$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,doppio-pendolo,0" etichette="0.55,0.5,φ_A" quote="A,B,3 m" titolo="Prescribed rotation at A with end B guided"}

- $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The unknowns are the couple $m_A$ and chord rotation $\psi$ because $B$ can translate. The stiffness relations with $\varphi_B=0$ give $m_A=\dfrac{EI}{L}(4\varphi_A-6\psi)$ and $m_B=\dfrac{EI}{L}(2\varphi_A-6\psi)$.

The guide transmits no vertical force, so the transverse force at $B$ is zero. Moment equilibrium of the member gives $m_A+m_B=0$ and hence $6\varphi_A-12\psi=0$ and $\psi=\dfrac{\varphi_A}{2}$.

Therefore, $m_A=\dfrac{EI}{L}\varphi_A=1\,000\cdot0.004\,\mathrm{kN\,m}=4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

Allowing translation at $B$ reduces the stiffness from $\dfrac{4EI}{L}$, which would give $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, to $\dfrac{EI}{L}$.
