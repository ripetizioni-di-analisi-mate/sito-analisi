A roof truss consists of identical members $AC$ and $BC$ pinned together at $C$ and connected by pins to stationary supports $A$ and $B$. The members have length $5\,\mathrm{m}$ and axial rigidity $EA=125\,000\,\mathrm{kN}$. Supports $A$ and $B$ are a distance $6\,\mathrm{m}$ apart, and $C$ is at a height $4\,\mathrm{m}$. A downward force of $32\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $C$. How far does $C$ deflect downward?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,4;B,6,0" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" reticolare=1 forze="C,0,-1.2,P" quote="A,B,6 m;C,3 0,4 m,-0.5" titolo="Two-member roof truss loaded at the apex"}

- $0.5\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $1\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.64\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $1.78\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

Each member makes an angle with the horizontal satisfying $\sin\alpha=\dfrac45$. By symmetry the two member forces are equal, and vertical equilibrium of joint $C$ gives $2N\sin\alpha=-P$, hence $N=-\dfrac{5P}{8}$ in compression.

Applying **Castigliano’s theorem** to the truss gives
$$
v_C=\sum_e\frac{N_e}{EA}\frac{\partial N_e}{\partial P}L_e=2\cdot\frac{25P}{64}\cdot\frac{5}{EA}=\frac{125P}{32\,EA}.
$$
Substituting the data gives $v_C=\dfrac{125\cdot32}{32\cdot125\,000}\,\mathrm{m}=0.001\,\mathrm{m}$.

The downward deflection is therefore $1\,\mathrm{mm}$. Value $0.5\,\mathrm{mm}$ counts only one member. Value $1.78\,\mathrm{mm}$ uses $\cos\alpha$ in place of $\sin\alpha$ in the joint equilibrium equation.
