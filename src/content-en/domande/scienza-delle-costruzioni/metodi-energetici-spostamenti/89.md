Reconsider a frame solution obtained by the displacement method. A beam and a column meet at rigid joint $N$. The stiffness relations use rotation $\varphi_N=0.003\,\mathrm{rad}$ for the beam and $\varphi_N=0.002\,\mathrm{rad}$ for the column. What does this check show?

- The solution is correct if joint equilibrium is satisfied
- The column is stiffer than the beam
- The joint violates the compatibility condition because all members meeting at a rigid joint must have the same rotation
- The joint translates horizontally

## Explanation

At a rigid joint the member ends are welded together and share a single rotation, the joint rotation unknown.

Different rotations for the members of the same joint indicate a compatibility error in the member equations.

Joint equilibrium based on incompatible rotations cannot validate the solution because the end couples correspond to an impossible deflected shape.

Different end rotations are possible only where the members are connected by a hinge.
