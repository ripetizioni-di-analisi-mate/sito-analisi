A bar of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ has $EA=12\,000\,\mathrm{kN}$. Over the first $1\,\mathrm{m}$, we have $N=12\,\mathrm{kN}$. Over the remaining $2\,\mathrm{m}$, we have $N=-6\,\mathrm{kN}$. What is the axial strain energy?

- $9\,\mathrm{J}$
- $3\,\mathrm{J}$
- $18\,\mathrm{J}$
- $6\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

The contributions of the two segments add. Evaluating $\dfrac{12^2\cdot1+(-6)^2\cdot2}{2\cdot12\,000}$ gives $0.009\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
