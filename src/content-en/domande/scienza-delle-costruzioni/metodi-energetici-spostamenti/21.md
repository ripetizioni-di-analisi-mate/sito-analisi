A beam of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and flexural rigidity $EI=2\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ has a bending moment varying linearly from $M_A=0$ to $M_B=12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Calculate the bending strain energy.

- $144\,\mathrm{J}$
- $48\,\mathrm{J}$
- $72\,\mathrm{J}$
- $24\,\mathrm{J}$

## Explanation

With $x$ in meters, the bending moment is $3x\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The integral of $(3x)^2$ from $0$ to $4$ is $192$ in the units used here. Dividing by $2EI$ gives $0.048\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
