The ends of a beam of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ undergo transverse displacements $w_A=-2\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $w_B=+4\,\mathrm{mm}$, positive upward. What is the rotation of chord $\psi$?

- $\dfrac23\,\mathrm{mrad}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{mrad}$
- $1\,\mathrm{mrad}$
- $2\,\mathrm{mrad}$

## Explanation

The chord rotation is the slope of the straight line joining the displaced ends, $\psi=\dfrac{w_B-w_A}{L}$.

The displacement difference is $4-(-2)=6\,\mathrm{mm}$.

Thus $\psi=\dfrac{6\,\mathrm{mm}}{3\,\mathrm{m}}=2\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{rad}=2\,\mathrm{mrad}$, counterclockwise.

Value $\dfrac23\,\mathrm{mrad}$ uses difference $4-2$ and ignores the sign of $w_A$.
