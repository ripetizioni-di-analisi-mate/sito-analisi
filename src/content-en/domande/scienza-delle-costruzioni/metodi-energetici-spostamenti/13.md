A beam of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ has $EI=4\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. Both end rotations are restrained. The left end is fixed, and the right end is displaced vertically upward by $4\,\mathrm{mm}$. With no loads on the member, calculate the external vertical force at $B$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0,-" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,incastro,0" quote="A,B,4 m" frecce="4,0,4,0.7,Δ_B" etichette="4.17,-0.13,B" titolo="Beam with a prescribed vertical displacement"}

- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

For relative end translation with both rotations fixed, the transverse stiffness is $\dfrac{12EI}{L^3}$. Multiplying by $0.004\,\mathrm{m}$ gives $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward.
