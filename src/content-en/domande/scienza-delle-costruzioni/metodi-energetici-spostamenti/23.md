The given quantities are $M$ in $\mathrm{kN\,m}$, $EI$ in $\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$, and coordinate $x$ in meters. What are the units of $\int \dfrac{M^2}{2EI}\,\mathrm{d}x$?

- $\mathrm{kN}$
- $\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $\mathrm{m}$
- $\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The ratio of $M^2$ to $EI$ has dimensions of force. Multiplication by $\mathrm{d}x$ gives force times length, the dimensions of energy.
