A beam of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ with constant $EI$ is fixed at $A$ and supported at $B$ by a support that does not settle. The only load is a counterclockwise couple of $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at $B$. Neglecting shear deformation, what is the vertical reaction at support $B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" coppie="B,1,C" quote="A,B,3 m" titolo="Propped cantilever with a couple at B"}

- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward
- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $\dfrac83\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward
- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward

## Explanation

Remove the support and apply an unknown upward reaction $X$ at $B$. In the released cantilever the moment a distance $s$ from $B$ is $M=Xs+C$ because both the upward force and the counterclockwise couple on the right portion put the bottom fibers in tension.

The **Menabrea** condition $\dfrac{\partial U}{\partial X}=0$ gives
$$
\frac{1}{EI}\int_0^L(Xs+C)\,s\,ds=\frac{1}{EI}\left(\frac{XL^3}{3}+\frac{CL^2}{2}\right)=0.
$$
Thus $X=-\dfrac{3C}{2L}=-\dfrac{3\cdot8}{2\cdot3}\,\mathrm{kN}=-4\,\mathrm{kN}$.

The reaction is therefore $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ **downward**. The counterclockwise couple alone would lift the cantilever tip, so the support must pull it downward.
