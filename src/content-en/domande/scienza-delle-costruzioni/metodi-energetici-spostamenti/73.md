In a frame model the beam translates without rotating and the two columns act in parallel. A lateral test at $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ gives $6\,\mathrm{mm}$ of translation. One column has lateral stiffness $1\,\mathrm{kN/mm}$. How much lateral force does the other column carry in the test?

- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $18\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Both column heads translate $6\,\mathrm{mm}$. The known column carries $1\cdot6=6\,\mathrm{kN}$. Horizontal equilibrium leaves $18-6=12\,\mathrm{kN}$ for the other column, whose stiffness is therefore $2\,\mathrm{kN/mm}$.
