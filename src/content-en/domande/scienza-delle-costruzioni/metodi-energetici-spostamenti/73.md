A portal frame has a rigid crossbeam connected rigidly to two columns of height $3\,\mathrm{m}$ with $EI=3\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. Column $AB$ is pinned at its base and column $DC$ is fixed at its base. The columns are axially inextensible, and a horizontal force of $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts on the crossbeam. How far does the crossbeam translate?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;C,4,3;D,4,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,incastro" forze="C,0.9,0,Q" quote="A,B,3 m,-0.8" titolo="Portal frame with one pinned base and one fixed base"}

- $3.75\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $6\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $30\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The rigid crossbeam restrains rotation at both column tops. Column $DC$, rotationally fixed at both ends, therefore has stiffness $\dfrac{12EI}{H^3}$.

Column $AB$ is restrained against rotation at the top and free to rotate at the base. It acts as a cantilever fixed to the crossbeam, with stiffness $\dfrac{3EI}{H^3}$.

The two columns act in parallel, so
$$
\Delta=\frac{QH^3}{15EI}=\frac{10\cdot27}{15\cdot3\,000}\,\mathrm{m}=0.006\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
The crossbeam therefore translates by $6\,\mathrm{mm}$. Value $3.75\,\mathrm{mm}$ treats both columns as fixed at their bases.
