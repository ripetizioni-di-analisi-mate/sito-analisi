Two plates have the same $E$ and $h$. The first has $\nu_1=0$ and the second $\nu_2=\dfrac13$. What is $\dfrac{D_2}{D_1}$?

- $\dfrac{8}{9}$
- $\dfrac{4}{3}$
- $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- $\dfrac{9}{8}$

## Explanation

The terms $E$, $h^3$, and the factor $12$ cancel in the ratio. This leaves $\dfrac{1-\nu_1^2}{1-\nu_2^2}=\dfrac98$.
