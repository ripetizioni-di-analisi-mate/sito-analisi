In pure cylindrical bending, the measured value is $M_x=24\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $\nu=\dfrac13$ holds. What is $M_y$?

- $0\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $24\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Since $w$ depends only on $x$, this leaves $M_y=-\nu D w_{,xx}=\nu M_x$.
