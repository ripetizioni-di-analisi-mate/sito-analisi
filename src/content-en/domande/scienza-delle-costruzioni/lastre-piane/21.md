In a plate of thickness $10\,\mathrm{mm}$, the stress is $10\,\mathrm{MPa}$ in the bottom half and $30\,\mathrm{MPa}$ in the top half. Coordinate $z$ is measured in millimeters from the midplane. What is the normal force resultant?

- $100\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $200\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $300\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $400\,\mathrm{kN/m}$

## Explanation

Each half has thickness $5\,\mathrm{mm}$. The contributions add to $30\cdot5+10\cdot5=200\,\mathrm{kN/m}$.
