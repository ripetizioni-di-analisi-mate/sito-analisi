A plate has resultants $Q_x=-3x$ and $Q_y=-4y$ in $\mathrm{kN/m}$, with coordinates in meters. Which load $q$ satisfies equilibrium?

- $-7\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$
- $3\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$
- $7\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$

## Explanation

Transverse equilibrium requires $q=-\partial_x Q_x-\partial_y Q_y$. The two derivatives sum to $-7\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$.
