In pure bending a plate has local deflection $w=Axy$, with $A\ne0$ constant. Which in-plane strain components are nonzero at depth $z\ne0$?

- Only $\varepsilon_x$ and $\varepsilon_y$
- $\varepsilon_x$, $\varepsilon_y$, and $\gamma_{xy}$
- Only $\gamma_{xy}$
- None, because $w$ is linear in each variable

## Explanation

The pure second derivatives vanish because $w$ is linear in $x$ at fixed $y$ and linear in $y$ at fixed $x$. Thus $\varepsilon_x=-z\,w_{,xx}=0$ and $\varepsilon_y=-z\,w_{,yy}=0$. The mixed derivative is $w_{,xy}=A$, so $\gamma_{xy}=-2zA$ is nonzero. The surface is saddle-shaped and carries only twisting moment $M_{xy}=-D(1-\nu)A$.
