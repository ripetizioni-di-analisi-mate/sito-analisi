A plate of thickness $h=0.2\,\mathrm m$ has midplane membrane strain $\varepsilon_{x0}=2\cdot10^{-4}$ and curvature $w_{,xx}=0.004\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$. In Kirchhoff–Love theory, at what depth $z$, measured positive downward, does the strain $\varepsilon_x$ vanish?

- $z=-50\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $z=50\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $z=25\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $z=0$

## Explanation

For displacement $u=u_0-z\,w_{,x}$, the strain along $x$ is $\varepsilon_x=\varepsilon_{x0}-z\,w_{,xx}$. Imposing $\varepsilon_x=0$ gives
$$
z=\frac{\varepsilon_{x0}}{w_{,xx}}=\frac{2\cdot10^{-4}}{0.004}\,\mathrm m=0.05\,\mathrm m.
$$
The zero-strain fiber therefore lies $50\,\mathrm{mm}$ below the midplane, within the plate thickness. Without membrane strain it would coincide with the midplane.
