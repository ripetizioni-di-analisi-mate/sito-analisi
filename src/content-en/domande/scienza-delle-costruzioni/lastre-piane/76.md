A square plate of side $a=2\,\mathrm m$ simply supported on all four edges deflects in the single Navier mode $(1,1)$ with amplitude $W_{11}=2\,\mathrm{mm}$. Given $D=4\,\mathrm{MN\,m}$ and $\nu=\dfrac14$, what is the twisting moment $M_{xy}$ at corner $x=y=0$?

- $1.5\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-2\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-1.5\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Given $\alpha_1=\beta_1=\dfrac{\pi}{2}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$, the mixed derivative is $w_{,xy}=\alpha_1\beta_1W_{11}\cos(\alpha_1x)\cos(\beta_1y)$. Both cosines equal one at the corner, so $w_{,xy}=\dfrac{\pi^2}{4}\cdot0.002\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$. Therefore
$$
M_{xy}=-D(1-\nu)w_{,xy}=-4000\cdot\frac34\cdot\frac{\pi^2}{4}\cdot0.002\,\mathrm{kN}=-1.5\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}.
$$
The twisting moment has its greatest magnitude at the corners and vanishes on the plate centerlines.
