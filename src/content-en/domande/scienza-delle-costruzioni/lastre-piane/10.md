On the right edge of a rectangular plate, the outward normal points in the $+x$ direction. Here $Q_x=-7\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and $\partial_y M_{xy}=3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Calculate the effective shear $V_x$ using the convention $V_x=Q_x+\partial_yM_{xy}$.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 5,0 5,3 0,3" tratteggi="5,0 5,3" frecce="0,0,1.8,0,x;0,0,0,1.6,y;5,1.5,5.8,1.5,n" riferimento=0 titolo="Right edge with its outward normal pointing in the positive x direction"}

- $-10\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $10\,\mathrm{kN/m}$

## Explanation

The effective shear includes the contribution of the twisting moment along the edge. The specified normal gives $-7+3=-4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$.
