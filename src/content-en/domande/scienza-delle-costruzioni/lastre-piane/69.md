The rectangular plate $0\le x\le a$, $0\le y\le b$ is simply supported along edge $x=0$. Which field $w$, with $A\ne0$, satisfies both support conditions on that edge?

- $w=A\sin\dfrac{\pi x}{a}\sin\dfrac{\pi y}{b}$
- $w=Ax^2\sin\dfrac{\pi y}{b}$
- $w=A\cos\dfrac{\pi x}{a}\sin\dfrac{\pi y}{b}$
- $w=A\left(1-\cos\dfrac{2\pi x}{a}\right)\sin\dfrac{\pi y}{b}$

## Explanation

On the supported edge, both $w$ and $M_{nn}=M_x$ must vanish. Along $x=0$, we have $w=0$ and hence $w_{,yy}=0$, so the moment condition reduces to $w_{,xx}=0$. The sine product satisfies both conditions because $\sin\dfrac{\pi x}{a}$ and its second derivative vanish at $x=0$. The fields with $x^2$ and $1-\cos\dfrac{2\pi x}{a}$ have $w=0$ but $w_{,xx}\ne0$ at the edge, while the field with $\cos\dfrac{\pi x}{a}$ has $w\ne0$.
