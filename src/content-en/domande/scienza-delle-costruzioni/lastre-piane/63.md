Which uniformly loaded plate can be modeled in the indicated region as a strip in cylindrical bending with $w=w(x)$?

- At the center of a square plate of side $3\,\mathrm m$, simply supported on all four sides
- Near a short edge of a plate of length $20\,\mathrm m$ along $y$ and width $3\,\mathrm m$ along $x$, simply supported on all four sides
- In the central region of a plate of length $20\,\mathrm m$ along $y$ and width $3\,\mathrm m$ along $x$, simply supported on the long sides, far from the short sides
- At the center of a square plate of side $3\,\mathrm m$, clamped on all four sides

## Explanation

This reduction requires the geometry, loading, and supports to admit a deflection field independent of $y$. In the central region of the long plate supported along its long edges, all transverse strips deflect identically, so $w=w(x)$. In a square plate, the restraints on $y=0$ and $y=b$ force $w$ to vary along $y$ as well, even far from the edges. Near a simply supported short edge, the deflection must vanish on that edge and therefore depends on both coordinates.
