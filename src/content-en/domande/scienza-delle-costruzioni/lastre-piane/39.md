At a point in the plate, $M_x=20\,\mathrm{kN}$, $M_y=8\,\mathrm{kN}$, and $M_{xy}=8\,\mathrm{kN}$. Under a rotation of axes, the moments per unit length transform like the in-plane components of stress. What is the largest principal moment?

- $28\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $24\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $36\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The principal moments are the eigenvalues of matrix $\begin{pmatrix}M_x&M_{xy}\\M_{xy}&M_y\end{pmatrix}$, just as in Mohr’s circle for stress. The center is at $\dfrac{M_x+M_y}{2}=14\,\mathrm{kN}$. The radius is $\sqrt{\left(\dfrac{M_x-M_y}{2}\right)^2+M_{xy}^2}=\sqrt{36+64}=10\,\mathrm{kN}$. Thus the maximum principal moment is $14+10=24\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the minimum is $4\,\mathrm{kN}$.
