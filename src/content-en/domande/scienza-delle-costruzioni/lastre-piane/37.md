The exterior wall of a building is flat and vertical. It carries its own weight and wind pressure acting perpendicular to the wall. How does the plate model represent these two actions?

- Self-weight causes plate bending, while wind pressure produces membrane action
- Self-weight produces membrane action, while wind pressure causes plate bending
- Both produce membrane action because the wall is vertical
- Both cause plate bending because the wall is thin

## Explanation

Self-weight acts vertically, in the midplane of the wall. It enters in-plane equilibrium as distributed force $f_y$ and produces the resultants $N_x$, $N_y$, and $N_{xy}$. Wind pressure acts normal to the midplane as transverse load $q$ and produces deflection $w$, plate moments, and transverse shear resultants. In linear elasticity with small displacements, calculate the two responses separately and add them.
