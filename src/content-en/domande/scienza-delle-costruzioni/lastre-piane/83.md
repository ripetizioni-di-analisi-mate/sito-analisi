A rectangular plate of sides $a$ and $b$ is simply supported on all four edges and carries a uniform load $q=\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$ over only the half $0\le x\le\dfrac a2$. What is the coefficient $q_{11}$?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 3,0 3,6 0,6" tratteggi="1.5,0 1.5,6;-0.2,0 -0.2,6;3.2,0 3.2,6;0,-0.2 3,-0.2;0,6.2 3,6.2" frecce="0,0,1.2,0,x;0,0,0,1.6,y" quote="0 0,3 0,a;3 0,3 6,b" etichette="0.75,3,q;2.25,3,unloaded" riferimento=0 titolo="Uniform load covering only the left half of the plate"}

- $16\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$

## Explanation

Calculate the coefficient by integrating only over the loaded area. The integral of $\sin\dfrac{\pi y}{b}$ with respect to $y$ is $\dfrac{2b}{\pi}$. The integral of $\sin\dfrac{\pi x}{a}$ with respect to $x$ from $0$ to $\dfrac a2$ is $\dfrac{a}{\pi}$. Therefore
$$
q_{11}=\frac{4q}{ab}\cdot\frac a\pi\cdot\frac{2b}{\pi}=\frac{8q}{\pi^2}=8\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}.
$$
The result is half the coefficient $\dfrac{16q}{\pi^2}$ for loading the entire plate because the first mode is symmetric about $x=\dfrac a2$.
