At a point in a plate with flexural rigidity $D=8\,\mathrm{MN\,m}$ and Poisson ratio $\nu=\dfrac14$, the mixed derivative is $w_{,xy}=0.002\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$. Find $M_{xy}$ using the convention $M_{xy}=\int z\tau_{xy}\,\mathrm{d}z$.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 6,0 6,3.4 0,3.4" frecce="0,0,2,0,x;0,0,0,1.7,y" riferimento=0 titolo="Plate midplane"}

- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-24\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-16\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The prescribed integral convention gives $M_{xy}=-D(1-\nu)w_{,xy}$. Substitution gives $-8\cdot10^3\cdot\dfrac34\cdot0.002=-12\,\mathrm{kN}$.
