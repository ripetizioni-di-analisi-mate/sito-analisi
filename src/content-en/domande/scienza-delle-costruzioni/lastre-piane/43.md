In a smooth plate region, a constant $C$ is added to $M_{xy}$ only, leaving $M_x$ and $M_y$ unchanged. According to the local plate equilibrium equations, how do interior shear forces $Q_x,Q_y$ and distributed load $q$ change?

- Only $Q_x$ changes.
- Both shear forces change but $q$ stays the same.
- Only $q$ changes.
- All three stay unchanged.

## Explanation

The shear equations contain first derivatives of $M_{xy}$ with respect to $y$ and $x$, respectively. The derivatives of a constant vanish. The load $q$, obtained from the divergence of the shear forces, also retains its value. This conclusion applies to interior quantities.
