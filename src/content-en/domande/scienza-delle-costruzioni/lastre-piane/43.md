The resultants are $M_x=M_y=0$ and $M_{xy}=6xy$ in $\mathrm{kN}$, with coordinates in meters. Which $q$ satisfies equilibrium?

- $-6\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$

## Explanation

This gives $Q_x=6x$ and $Q_y=6y$. Both derivatives contribute to the divergence, so $q=-12\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$.
