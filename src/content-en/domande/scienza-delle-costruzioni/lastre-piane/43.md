In a region of a plate the moments per unit length are $M_x=M_y=0$ and $M_{xy}=6xy$, in $\mathrm{kN}$, with coordinates in metres. Which load $q$ satisfies equilibrium?

- $-6\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$

## Explanation

The shear equations give $Q_x=\partial_x M_x+\partial_y M_{xy}=6x$ and $Q_y=\partial_x M_{xy}+\partial_y M_y=6y$, in $\mathrm{kN/m}$. Vertical equilibrium requires $\partial_x Q_x+\partial_y Q_y+q=0$. Since $\partial_x Q_x=\partial_y Q_y=6\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$, this gives $q=-12\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$. The value $-6\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$ results from taking only one of the two shear forces into account.
