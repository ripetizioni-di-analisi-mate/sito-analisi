A square plate of side $a=2\,\mathrm m$ simply supported on all four edges carries a point force $P=10\,\mathrm{kN}$ at its center. What is the coefficient $q_{11}$ in the double Fourier sine series?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 4,0 4,4 0,4" tratteggi="-0.2,0 -0.2,4;4.2,0 4.2,4;0,-0.2 4,-0.2;0,4.2 4,4.2" frecce="0,0,1.6,0,x;0,0,0,1.6,y" quote="0 0,4 0,2 m;4 0,4 4,2 m" punti="2,2,P" riferimento=0 titolo="Simply supported plate with a point force at its center"}

- $10\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$
- $40\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$
- $\dfrac{160}{\pi^2}\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$

## Explanation

The coefficients are $q_{mn}=\dfrac{4}{ab}\displaystyle\int_0^a\!\!\int_0^bq\sin(\alpha_mx)\sin(\beta_ny)\,\mathrm dy\,\mathrm dx$. For a point load, the integral reduces to $P$ times the shape function evaluated at the load point. At the center both first-mode sines equal one, so $q_{11}=\dfrac{4P}{a^2}=\dfrac{40}{4}\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}=10\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$.
