A downward line load $P=12\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts along the midspan line of a simply supported strip of span $L=5\,\mathrm m$. It is a force per unit length of that line. Assume cylindrical bending. What is $M_x$ at midspan?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="2.5 0,0,-1.2,P" quote="A,2.5 0,2.5 m;2.5 0,B,2.5 m" titolo="Cross section of the strip with a line load at midspan"}

- $30\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $60\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

For a strip of unit width, the line load becomes a point force $P$ at midspan. By symmetry, each support reaction is $\dfrac P2=6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. The midspan result is therefore $M_x=\dfrac P2\cdot\dfrac L2=\dfrac{PL}{4}=15\,\mathrm{kN}$. The result is a moment per unit width, consistent with a load measured per unit length of the line.
