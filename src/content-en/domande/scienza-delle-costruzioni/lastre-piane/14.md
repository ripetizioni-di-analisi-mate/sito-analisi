A rectangular plate has sides $a=\pi\,\mathrm{m}$ in the $x$ direction and $b=2\pi\,\mathrm{m}$ in the $y$ direction. Its deflection is a single Navier mode $(1,1)$ with amplitude $W_{11}=1\,\mathrm{mm}$. Given $D=4\,\mathrm{MN\,m}$ and $\nu=\dfrac14$, calculate $M_x$ at the center.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 3,0 3,6 0,6" frecce="0,0,1.5,0,x;0,0,0,2,y" quote="0 0,3 0,a;3 6,3 0,b" punti="1.5,3,C" riferimento=0 titolo="Rectangular plate with side b parallel to y"}

- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $4.25\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $1.25\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

At the center, $M_x=D(k_x^2+\nu k_y^2)W_{11}$. The quantities $k_x$ and $k_y$ equal $1$ and $\dfrac12\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$, so the factor multiplying the rigidity and amplitude is $\dfrac{17}{16}\,\mathrm{m^{-2}}$.
