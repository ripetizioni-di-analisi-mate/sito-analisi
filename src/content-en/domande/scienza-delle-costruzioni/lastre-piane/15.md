A plate of thickness $10\,\mathrm{mm}$ has stress $\sigma_x(z)=(20+2z)\,\mathrm{MPa}$, with $z$ measured in millimeters from the midplane. What is $N_x$?

- $100\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $300\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $200\,\mathrm{kN/m}$

## Explanation

Term $2z$ is odd, so its integral is zero. The uniform term gives $20\cdot10=200\,\mathrm{N/mm}$, or $200\,\mathrm{kN/m}$.
