A rectangular plate simply supported on all four edges carries a uniform downward load, with $w$ positive downward. At the center, $w_{,xx}<0$ and $w_{,yy}<0$. Which statement about $M_x$ at the center is correct?

- $M_x>0$, with the upper face in tension along $x$
- $M_x>0$, with the lower face in tension along $x$
- $M_x<0$, with the lower face in tension along $x$
- $M_x=0$ because the two curvatures cancel

## Explanation

The moment–curvature relation gives $M_x=-D(w_{,xx}+\nu w_{,yy})$. Both curvatures are negative, so their combination $w_{,xx}+\nu w_{,yy}$ is negative. Thus $M_x>0$. In pure bending, $\sigma_x=\dfrac{12M_x}{h^3}z$. Thus, for $M_x>0$ the stress is tensile wherever $z>0$. Since $z$ is positive downward, the bottom face is in tension.
