A sandwich panel has two face sheets of thickness $t=2\,\mathrm{mm}$ with midplanes at coordinates $z=\pm25\,\mathrm{mm}$. The core carries no normal stress. The lower face sheet has stress $\sigma_x=100\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and the upper sheet $\sigma_x=-100\,\mathrm{MPa}$, each uniform through its thickness. What is $M_x$ per unit length?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0.5 6,0.5 6,0.6 0,0.6;0,-0.6 6,-0.6 6,-0.5 0,-0.5" tratteggi="0,0 6,0" quote="6 -0.55,6 0.55,50 mm" frecce="0,0,1.8,0,x;0,0,0,-1.1,z" etichette="3,0.2,web" riferimento=0 titolo="Cross section of a sandwich panel showing both face sheets"}

- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The moment per unit length is the through-thickness integral of $z\sigma_x$. Each sheet carries a force per unit length $\sigma_xt$ of magnitude $100\cdot2=200\,\mathrm{N/mm}$ acting at its midplane. The forces are opposite, so $N_x=0$, but their moments have the same sign. This gives
$$
M_x=25\cdot200+(-25)(-200)=10\,000\,\mathrm{N\,mm/mm}=10\,\mathrm{kN}.
$$
