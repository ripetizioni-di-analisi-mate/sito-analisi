In pure bending, at depth $z=0.05\,\mathrm{m}$ we have $w_{,xy}=0.04\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$. What is the engineering shear strain $\gamma_{xy}$?

- $\dfrac{1}{250}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{250}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{500}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{125}$

## Explanation

The two in-plane displacement derivatives make equal contributions. Thus $\gamma_{xy}=-2z w_{,xy}=-0.004$.
