In one region, the curvature is $w_{,xx}=-0.005\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$ and $M_y=0$. Given $\nu=0.2$, what is $w_{,yy}$?

- $\dfrac{1}{1000}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{1000}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $\dfrac{1}{200}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{200}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$

## Explanation

From $M_y=0$, we get $w_{,yy}=-\nu w_{,xx}$. The curvatures have opposite signs.
