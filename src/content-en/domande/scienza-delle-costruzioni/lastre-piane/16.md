A plate of thickness $6\,\mathrm{mm}$ has stress $\sigma_x(z)=3z\,\mathrm{MPa}$, with $z$ in millimeters from the midplane. What is the moment $M_x$ per unit length?

- $54\,\mathrm{N}$
- $18\,\mathrm{N}$
- $27\,\mathrm{N}$
- $108\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

Integrate $3z^2$ from $-3$ to $3\,\mathrm{mm}$ to obtain the moment. The integral is $54\,\mathrm{N}$.
