The shear stress in a plate of thickness $h=10\,\mathrm{mm}$ varies through the thickness as $\tau_{xy}(z)=30\left(1-\dfrac{4z^2}{h^2}\right)\,\mathrm{MPa}$, with $z$ measured from the midplane. What is the shear force resultant $N_{xy}$?

- $200\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $300\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $150\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $100\,\mathrm{kN/m}$

## Explanation

Integrate $\tau_{xy}$ from $-\dfrac h2$ to $\dfrac h2$ to obtain the shear force resultant. The integral of the constant term is $h$, and the integral of $\dfrac{4z^2}{h^2}$ is $\dfrac{4}{h^2}\cdot\dfrac{h^3}{12}=\dfrac h3$. Therefore
$$
N_{xy}=30\left(h-\frac h3\right)=\frac23\cdot30\cdot10\,\mathrm{N/mm}=200\,\mathrm{N/mm}.
$$
Since $1\,\mathrm{N/mm}=1\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, the result is $N_{xy}=200\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Value $300\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ would correspond to a uniform stress equal to the maximum stress.
