Along a cut normal to $x$, for $0\le y\le3\,\mathrm m$, the moment per unit length varies as $M_x(y)=(6-2y)\,\mathrm{kN}$, with $y$ in meters. What is the total moment transmitted across the cut?

- $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The total moment is the integral of the moment per unit length along the cut. This gives
$$
\int_0^3(6-2y)\,\mathrm dy=18-9=9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}.
$$
Multiplying the value at $y=0$ by the cut length would give $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, as if $M_x$ were uniform.
