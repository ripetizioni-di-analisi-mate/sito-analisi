With $x,y$ in meters, a panel has resultants $N_x=4x$ and $N_{xy}=3y$ in $\mathrm{kN/m}$. What distributed in-plane force $f_x$ is required for local equilibrium?

- $-4\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$
- $7\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$
- $-7\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$

## Explanation

Equilibrium in the $x$ direction requires $f_x=-\partial_xN_x-\partial_yN_{xy}$. The two derivatives are $4$ and $3\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$.
