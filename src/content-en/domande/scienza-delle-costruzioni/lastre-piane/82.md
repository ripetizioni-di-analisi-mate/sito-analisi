A rectangular plate of sides $a$ and $b$ is simply supported on all four edges and carries only the modal load $(2,1)$ with positive amplitude. Where is the downward deflection greatest?

- $\left(\dfrac a2,\dfrac b2\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac a4,\dfrac b2\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac{3a}{4},\dfrac b2\right)$
- $\left(\dfrac a2,\dfrac b4\right)$

## Explanation

The deflection has the form $w=W_{21}\sin\dfrac{2\pi x}{a}\sin\dfrac{\pi y}{b}$, with $W_{21}>0$. The factor depending on $y$ reaches its maximum at $y=\dfrac b2$. The factor depending on $x$ equals $1$ at $x=\dfrac a4$ and $-1$ at $x=\dfrac{3a}{4}$, and vanishes at the center. The maximum downward deflection therefore occurs at $\left(\dfrac a4,\dfrac b2\right)$, while the plate rises by the same amount at $\left(\dfrac{3a}{4},\dfrac b2\right)$.
