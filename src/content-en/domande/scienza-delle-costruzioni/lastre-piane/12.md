A square plate simply supported on all four edges is subjected separately to two modal loads of equal amplitude, with indices $(2,1)$ and $(1,1)$. Calculate the ratio $\dfrac{W_{21}}{W_{11}}$ of the positive deflection amplitudes.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 4,0 4,4 0,4" frecce="0,0,2,0,x;0,0,0,2,y" quote="0 0,4 0,a;4 4,4 0,a" punti="2,2,C" riferimento=0 titolo="Square plate simply supported on all four sides"}

- $\dfrac4{25}$
- $\dfrac25$
- $\dfrac{25}4$
- $\dfrac19$

## Explanation

For equal load amplitudes, deflection is inversely proportional to $(m^2+n^2)^2$. The ratio is therefore $\dfrac{(1+1)^2}{(4+1)^2}$.
