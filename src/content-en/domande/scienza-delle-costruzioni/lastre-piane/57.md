A slab is clamped along one edge and simply supported along the opposite edge, a distance $L=3\,\mathrm m$ away. It undergoes cylindrical bending under uniform load $q=8\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$. What reaction per unit length develops along the support line?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.35,0.35,q" quote="A,B,3 m" titolo="Strip clamped at A and simply supported at B"}

- $9\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$

## Explanation

The strip is statically indeterminate to the first degree. Choose the support reaction $R$ as the redundant. In the cantilever clamped at $x=0$, the uniform load lowers the end by $\dfrac{qL^4}{8D}$, while the upward force $R$ raises it by $\dfrac{RL^3}{3D}$. Compatibility at the support requires equal magnitudes of these displacements, giving $R=\dfrac{3qL}{8}$. This gives $R=\dfrac{3\cdot8\cdot3}{8}\,\mathrm{kN/m}=9\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, leaving $\dfrac{5qL}{8}=15\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at the clamped end.
