A plate with $D=2\,\mathrm{MN\,m}$ and $\nu=\dfrac14$ has local deflection $w=0.001\,xy^2$, with $x$, $y$, and $w$ in meters. What is the transverse shear resultant $Q_x$?

- $-1\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$

## Explanation

The second derivatives are $w_{,xx}=0$, $w_{,yy}=0.002x$, and $w_{,xy}=0.002y$. Thus $M_x=-0.002\,\nu Dx$ and $M_{xy}=-0.002\,(1-\nu)Dy$. The shear resultant is $Q_x=\partial_xM_x+\partial_yM_{xy}$, so $Q_x=-0.002\,D(\nu+1-\nu)=-0.002\,D$. This gives $Q_x=-0.002\cdot2000\,\mathrm{kN/m}=-4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. The twisting moment contributes $-3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Using $M_x$ alone would give $-1\,\mathrm{kN/m}$.
