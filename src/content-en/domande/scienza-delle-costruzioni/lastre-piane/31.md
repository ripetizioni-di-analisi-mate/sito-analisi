A slab rests on parallel walls a distance $4\,\mathrm m$ apart. Model it as a strip in cylindrical bending with supports at $x=0$ and $x=4\,\mathrm m$. Load $q=6\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$ covers only the half nearest the left wall, $0\le x\le2\,\mathrm m$. What is the shear force $Q_x=\dfrac{\mathrm dM_x}{\mathrm dx}$ at midspan?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0,-;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,C,0.45,0.45,q" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,2 m"}

- $3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN/m}$

## Explanation

The load resultant is $6\cdot2=12\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and acts at $x=1\,\mathrm m$. Taking moments about the left support gives $4R_B=12\cdot1$, hence $R_B=3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and $R_A=9\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. In the unloaded half, the moment is $M_x=R_B(L-x)$, so $Q_x=\dfrac{\mathrm dM_x}{\mathrm dx}=-R_B=-3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. The same value follows on the left of midspan, where $Q_x=R_A-qx=9-12=-3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$.
