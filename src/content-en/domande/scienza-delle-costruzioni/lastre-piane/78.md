A square plate of side $a=2\,\mathrm m$ simply supported on all four edges carries load $q=\pi^2\sin\dfrac{\pi x}{a}\sin\dfrac{\pi y}{a}\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$. What is the sum of all support reactions, including the corner reactions?

- $4\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $16\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $32\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Overall vertical force equilibrium requires the sum of the reactions to equal the resultant applied load. Each integral $\displaystyle\int_0^a\sin\frac{\pi x}{a}\,\mathrm dx$ equals $\dfrac{2a}{\pi}=\dfrac4\pi\,\mathrm m$. The resultant is therefore $\pi^2\cdot\dfrac4\pi\cdot\dfrac4\pi=16\,\mathrm{kN}$. Treating the load amplitude as a uniform load over area $a^2$ would give $4\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$.
