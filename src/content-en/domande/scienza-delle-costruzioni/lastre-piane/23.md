At the point considered, $u_0=0$ and $w_{,x}=0.02$, and the through-thickness coordinate is $z=5\,\mathrm{mm}$. What is $u$ according to Kirchhoff–Love theory?

- $\dfrac{1}{10}\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{5}\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{20}\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $- \dfrac{1}{10}\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

Substitute the given values into $u=u_0-z w_{,x}$. The product has units of length and is negative.
