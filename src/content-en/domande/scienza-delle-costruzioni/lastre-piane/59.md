What is the plate rotation along a support line if a strip with $D=2\,\mathrm{MN\,m}$ spans a distance $4\,\mathrm m$ between simple supports and carries a uniform load $6\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$? The required quantity is the slope $w'(0)$ of the cylindrical deflection field.

- $0.016$
- $0.004$
- $0.008$
- $0.012$

## Explanation

Differentiating the deflection $w(x)=\dfrac{q}{24D}(L^3x-2Lx^3+x^4)$ gives $w'(x)=\dfrac{q}{24D}(L^3-6Lx^2+4x^3)$. At section $x=0$ this leaves $w'(0)=\dfrac{qL^3}{24D}$. Substitution gives $w'(0)=\dfrac{6\cdot64}{24\cdot2000}=0.008$. The slope is positive because $w$ increases downward with distance from the support.
