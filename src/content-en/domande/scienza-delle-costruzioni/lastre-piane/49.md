In Kirchhoff–Love kinematics, the in-plane displacement is $u=u_0-z\,w_{,x}$ and the downward deflection $w$ is independent of $z$. What is the transverse shear strain $\gamma_{xz}=\partial_zu+\partial_xw$?

- $0$
- $-w_{,x}$
- $w_{,x}$
- $-2z\,w_{,xy}$

## Explanation

Differentiating the in-plane displacement with respect to the through-thickness coordinate gives $\partial_zu=-w_{,x}$. Since $w$ is constant along each normal, $\partial_xw=w_{,x}$. The sum is therefore zero. This follows from the assumption that normals remain perpendicular to the deflected midsurface. The shear resultants $Q_x$ and $Q_y$ must therefore be obtained from moment equilibrium.
