A plate of thickness $h=0.2\,\mathrm m$ with $E=30\,\mathrm{GPa}$ and $\nu=\dfrac14$ is in pure bending at a point where the curvatures are $w_{,xx}=w_{,yy}=-0.002\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$. What is $\sigma_x$ at the bottom face $z=\dfrac h2$?

- $8\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $6\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $6.4\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $16\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The strains on the bottom face are $\varepsilon_x=\varepsilon_y=-\dfrac h2\,w_{,xx}=0.1\cdot0.002=2\cdot10^{-4}$. The plane stress constitutive law gives $\sigma_x=\dfrac{E}{1-\nu^2}(\varepsilon_x+\nu\varepsilon_y)$. Because the two strains are equal, the factor reduces to $\dfrac{E(1+\nu)}{1-\nu^2}=\dfrac{E}{1-\nu}$. Therefore
$$
\sigma_x=\frac{30\,000}{0.75}\cdot2\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm{MPa}=8\,\mathrm{MPa},
$$
in tension.
