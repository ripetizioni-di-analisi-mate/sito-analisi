Find the distributed reaction at the midpoint of edge $x=a$ of a simply supported square plate of side $a=\pi\,\mathrm m$. The deflection is $w=W\sin x\sin y$ with coordinates in meters and $W=1\,\mathrm{mm}$. Given $D=4\,\mathrm{MN\,m}$ and $\nu=\dfrac14$, what is the effective shear resultant $V_x=Q_x+\partial_yM_{xy}$ at $y=\dfrac a2$?

- $-11\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $-8\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $11\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $-5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$

## Explanation

Adding the moment contributions gives $Q_x=-D(w_{,xxx}+w_{,xyy})=-D\,\partial_x\Delta w$. Since $\Delta w=-2W\sin x\sin y$, we have $Q_x=2DW\cos x\sin y$. The twisting moment $M_{xy}=-D(1-\nu)W\cos x\cos y$ gives $\partial_yM_{xy}=D(1-\nu)W\cos x\sin y$. At point $x=\pi$, $y=\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, the cosine equals $-1$ and the sine equals $1$, so
$$
V_x=-DW(2+1-\nu)=-4000\cdot0.001\cdot2.75\,\mathrm{kN/m}=-11\,\mathrm{kN/m}.
$$
The shear resultant $Q_x$ alone would give $-8\,\mathrm{kN/m}$.
