A square plate of side $a=2\,\mathrm m$ simply supported on all four edges has deflection $w=W\sin\dfrac{\pi x}{a}\sin\dfrac{\pi y}{a}$, with $W=4\,\mathrm{mm}$. What is the slope $w_{,x}$ on edge $x=0$ at point $y=\dfrac a2$?

- $0$
- $4\cdot10^{-3}$
- $2\pi\cdot10^{-3}$
- $4\pi\cdot10^{-3}$

## Explanation

Differentiation gives $w_{,x}=\dfrac{\pi}{a}W\cos\dfrac{\pi x}{a}\sin\dfrac{\pi y}{a}$. At point $x=0$, $y=\dfrac a2$, both the cosine and the sine equal one, giving $w_{,x}=\dfrac{\pi}{2}\cdot0.004=2\pi\cdot10^{-3}$. The normal slope is nonzero, which a simple support permits. A clamped edge would require $w_{,x}=0$.
