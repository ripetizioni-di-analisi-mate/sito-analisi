A steel plate of thickness $h=10\,\mathrm{mm}$ with $E=200\,\mathrm{GPa}$ and $\nu=0.3$ carries only membrane resultants $N_x=300\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and $N_y=100\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, with stresses uniform through the thickness and $N_{xy}=0$. What is the midplane strain $\varepsilon_x$?

- $1.5\cdot10^{-4}$
- $1.65\cdot10^{-4}$
- $1.35\cdot10^{-3}$
- $1.35\cdot10^{-4}$

## Explanation

The stresses are uniform through the thickness, so $\sigma_x=\dfrac{N_x}{h}=30\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\sigma_y=\dfrac{N_y}{h}=10\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Under plane stress, Hooke’s law gives $\varepsilon_x=\dfrac{\sigma_x-\nu\sigma_y}{E}$. This gives
$$
\varepsilon_x=\frac{30-0.3\cdot10}{200\,000}=\frac{27}{200\,000}=1.35\cdot10^{-4}.
$$
Term $-\nu\sigma_y$ reduces the elongation because tension along $y$ produces transverse contraction along $x$.
