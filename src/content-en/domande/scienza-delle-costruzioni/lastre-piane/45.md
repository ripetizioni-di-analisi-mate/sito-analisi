For $w=0.001x^2y^2$ in meters, with the coordinates in meters and $D=3\,\mathrm{MN\,m}$, what is $q$?

- $0\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$
- $24\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$
- $72\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$

## Explanation

The pure fourth derivatives vanish. The mixed derivative $w_{,xxyy}$ equals $0.004\,\mathrm{m^{-3}}$ and appears twice.
