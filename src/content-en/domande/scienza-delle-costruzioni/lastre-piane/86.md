In a plate calculation, $D$ is expressed in $\mathrm{kN\,m}$, while $w$ and the coordinates are in meters. What are the units of $D\,\Delta^2w$?

- $\mathrm{kN}$
- $\mathrm{kN/m^2}$
- $\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The biharmonic operator contains fourth derivatives with respect to the coordinates, so $\Delta^2w$ has units $\dfrac{\mathrm m}{\mathrm m^4}=\mathrm{m^{-3}}$. Multiplication by $D$ gives $\mathrm{kN\,m}\cdot\mathrm{m^{-3}}=\mathrm{kN/m^2}$. These are units of load per unit area, consistent with equation $D\Delta^2w=q$.
