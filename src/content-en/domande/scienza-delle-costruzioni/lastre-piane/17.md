Resultant $N_y=-240\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ is uniform along a cut of length $0.75\,\mathrm{m}$. What is the total normal force, taking tension as positive?

- $-320\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-180\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-240\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $180\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Multiply the resultant per unit length by the length of the cut. The product is $-180\,\mathrm{kN}$, indicating compression.
