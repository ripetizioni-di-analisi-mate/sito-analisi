A rectangular panel with edges parallel to the coordinate axes is in equilibrium under uniform $N_x=150\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and $N_y=N_{xy}=0$. What force per unit length acts on the left edge, whose outward normal is $-x$?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 6,0 6,3.4 0,3.4" frecce="0,0,2,0,x;0,0,0,1.7,y;0,2.4,-1.2,2.4,n" tratteggi="0,0 0,3.4" riferimento=0 titolo="Panel with its left edge and outward normal"}

- $150\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ toward $+x$
- $75\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ toward $-x$
- $150\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ toward $-x$
- No force, because $N_x$ is constant

## Explanation

On the edge with outward normal $\boldsymbol n$, the component of force per unit length along $x$ is $N_xn_x+N_{xy}n_y$. On the left edge, $n_x=-1$ and $n_y=0$, giving component $-150\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. The applied force therefore has magnitude $150\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and direction $-x$, pulling outward on the panel as required for tension. An opposite force acts on the right edge, maintaining overall equilibrium.
