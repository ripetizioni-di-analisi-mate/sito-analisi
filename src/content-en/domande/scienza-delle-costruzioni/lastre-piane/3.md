An isotropic plate has thickness $h=100\,\mathrm{mm}$, Young’s modulus $E=27\,\mathrm{GPa}$, and Poisson ratio $\nu=\dfrac14$. Calculate the flexural rigidity $D$.

::struttura{poligoni="0,-0.55 6,-0.55 6,0.55 0,0.55" tratteggi="0,0 6,0" quote="6 -0.55,6 0.55,h" frecce="0,0,2,0,x;0,0,0,-1,z" riferimento=0 titolo="Thickness h and coordinate z positive downward"}

- $2.25\,\mathrm{MN\,m}$
- $1.8\,\mathrm{MN\,m}$
- $4.8\,\mathrm{MN\,m}$
- $2.4\,\mathrm{MN\,m}$

## Explanation

For an isotropic plate, $D=\dfrac{Eh^3}{12(1-\nu^2)}$. In SI units, $h=0.1\,\mathrm{m}$ and the denominator has the numerical value $11.25$.
