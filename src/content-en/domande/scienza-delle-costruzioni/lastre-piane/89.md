A large plate, thin compared with its span, carries a force distributed over a circle of radius equal to the plate thickness. Which Kirchhoff–Love predictions are reliable?

- The overall deflection. Stresses near the loaded area require a more detailed model
- Stresses near the loaded area. The overall deflection needs correction
- Both, because the plate is thin relative to the span
- Neither, because the model applies only to uniform loads

## Explanation

Assess thin-plate theory on the length scale that governs the quantity of interest. The global deflection depends on the span, which is much larger than the thickness. The Kirchhoff–Love assumptions hold on this scale. Near the loaded patch the characteristic length is its radius, equal to the thickness, and transverse shear increases inversely with distance from the load. The local stresses therefore require a model that includes shear deformation.
