In a region of the plate, $M_x(x)=(30-5x)\,\mathrm{kN}$, with $x$ in meters. The twisting moment $M_{xy}$ is zero. Calculate the transverse shear resultant $Q_x$ per unit length.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 6,0 6,3.4 0,3.4" frecce="0,0,2,0,x;0,0,0,1.7,y" riferimento=0 titolo="Plate midplane"}

- $5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $-5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $-30\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $30\,\mathrm{kN/m}$

## Explanation

Use $Q_x=\partial_xM_x+\partial_yM_{xy}$. The second term vanishes, leaving the derivative of $M_x$, equal to $-5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$.
