A cantilever plate with $D=2\,\mathrm{MN\,m}$ is clamped along $x=0$ and free along $x=2\,\mathrm m$. A downward line load $P=6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ along the free edge produces cylindrical bending. What is the downward deflection at the free edge?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" forze="B,0,-0.6,P" quote="A,B,2 m" titolo="Cantilever strip with a line load along the free edge"}

- $8\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $4\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $2\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $16\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The moment per unit width is $M_x(x)=-P(L-x)$, so $Dw''=P(L-x)$. Integrating twice with $w(0)=w'(0)=0$ gives $w(x)=\dfrac{P}{D}\left(\dfrac{Lx^2}{2}-\dfrac{x^3}{6}\right)$. At the free edge this leaves $w(L)=\dfrac{PL^3}{3D}=\dfrac{6\cdot8}{3\cdot2000}\,\mathrm m=0.008\,\mathrm m$. The downward deflection is therefore $8\,\mathrm{mm}$.
