A strip in cylindrical bending is supported along $x=0$ and $x=L=4\,\mathrm m$ and carries the triangular load $q(x)=\dfrac{q_0x}{L}$, with $q_0=8\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$. What is $M_x$ at midspan?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0,0.7,q_0" quote="A,B,4 m" titolo="Simply supported strip under a triangular load"}

- $16\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $\dfrac{32}{3}\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The load resultant is $\dfrac{q_0L}{2}=16\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and acts at a distance $\dfrac{2L}{3}$ from the left support. Taking moments about the right support therefore gives $R_A=\dfrac{q_0L}{6}=\dfrac{16}{3}\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. The load between $0$ and $\dfrac L2$ has resultant $\dfrac{q_0L}{8}=4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, with moment arm $\dfrac L6$ about midspan. Therefore
$$
M_x\left(\frac L2\right)=\frac{16}{3}\cdot2-4\cdot\frac23=\frac{32}{3}-\frac83=8\,\mathrm{kN}.
$$
