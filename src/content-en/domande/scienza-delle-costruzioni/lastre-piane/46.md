A plate has no distributed in-plane forces, so $f_x=f_y=0$. Which of the following fields in $\mathrm{kN/m}$, with $x$ and $y$ in meters, satisfies both local equilibrium equations?

- $N_x=x^2$, $N_y=0$, $N_{xy}=-2xy$
- $N_x=y$, $N_y=x$, $N_{xy}=0$
- $N_x=x$, $N_y=0$, $N_{xy}=0$
- $N_x=0$, $N_y=0$, $N_{xy}=x+y$

## Explanation

The equations are $\partial_xN_x+\partial_yN_{xy}=0$ and $\partial_xN_{xy}+\partial_yN_y=0$. For $N_x=y$, $N_y=x$, and $N_{xy}=0$, all derivatives in the two equations vanish, satisfying both equations. The field with $N_x=x^2$ and $N_{xy}=-2xy$ satisfies the first equation because $2x-2x=0$, but leaves $-2y$ in the second equation. The other two fields both give $1$ on the left-hand side of the first equation.
