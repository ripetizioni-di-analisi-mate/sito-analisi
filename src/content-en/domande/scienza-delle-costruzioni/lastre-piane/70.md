Away from the corners, which conditions hold along a straight, unloaded free edge of a Kirchhoff–Love plate?

- $M_{nn}=0$, $M_{nt}=0$, and $Q_n=0$
- $M_{nn}=0$ and $V_n=Q_n+\partial_tM_{nt}=0$
- $M_{nn}=0$ and $Q_n=0$
- Only $M_{nn}=0$

## Explanation

Kirchhoff–Love theory has one unknown field $w$, requiring two conditions on each edge. The edge twisting moment performs work on the tangential derivative of $w$. Integration by parts along the edge turns this work into the term $\partial_tM_{nt}$. Thus $M_{nt}$ and $Q_n$ need not vanish individually. The conditions are $M_{nn}=0$ and $V_n=0$.
