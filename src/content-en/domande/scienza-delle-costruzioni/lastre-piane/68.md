Near its clamped edge $x=0$, a plate with $D=3\,\mathrm{MN\,m}$ has deflection $w=0.002x^2+0.001x^3$, with $x$ and $w$ in meters and no dependence on $y$. What is $M_x$ at the edge?

- $-6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-24\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

At the edge $w(0)=0$ and $w_{,x}(0)=0$, satisfying the kinematic conditions at a clamp. Since $w$ is independent of $y$, the moment is $M_x=-D\,w_{,xx}$, with $w_{,xx}=0.004+0.006x$. At $x=0$, this gives $M_x=-3000\cdot0.004\,\mathrm{kN}=-12\,\mathrm{kN}$. The cubic term makes no contribution because its second derivative vanishes at $x=0$.
