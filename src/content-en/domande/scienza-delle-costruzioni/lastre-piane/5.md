At a point in a plate, measurements give $w_{,xx}=-0.003\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$ and $w_{,yy}=-0.001\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$. Given $D=8\,\mathrm{MN\,m}$ and $\nu=\dfrac14$, determine the moment per unit length $M_x$.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 6,0 6,3.4 0,3.4" frecce="0,0,2,0,x;0,0,0,1.7,y" riferimento=0 titolo="Plate midplane"}

- $24\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $26\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $32\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-26\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Relation $M_x=-D(w_{,xx}+\nu w_{,yy})$ includes the transverse curvature. The combination equals $-0.00325\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$, giving a moment per unit length of $26\,\mathrm{kN}$.
