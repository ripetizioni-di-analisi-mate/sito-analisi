In a region of a plate the moments per unit length are $M_x=3x$ and $M_{xy}=4y$, in $\mathrm{kN}$, with coordinates in metres. What is the shear force $Q_x$?

- $7\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $1\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$

## Explanation

Rotational equilibrium of the plate element gives $Q_x=\partial_x M_x+\partial_y M_{xy}$. The first term is $\partial_x(3x)=3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and the second is $\partial_y(4y)=4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Therefore $Q_x=3+4=7\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. The values $3$ and $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ result from taking only one of the two contributions.
