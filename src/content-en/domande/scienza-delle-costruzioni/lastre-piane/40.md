In a plate, $Q_x=\dfrac{\partial M_x}{\partial x}+\dfrac{\partial M_{xy}}{\partial y}$. Across a strip $2\,\mathrm{m}$ wide in the $y$ direction, a uniform $Q_x=5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ is measured and $M_x$ is independent of $x$. What is the difference $M_{xy}(y+2)-M_{xy}(y)$?

- $+10\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-10\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $+2.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The derivative with respect to $x$ vanishes, leaving $\dfrac{\partial M_{xy}}{\partial y}=5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Integration across the strip shows that the twisting moment per unit length increases by $5\cdot2=10\,\mathrm{kN}$.
