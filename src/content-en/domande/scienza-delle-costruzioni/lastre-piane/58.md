Under uniform load $q=8\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$, a strip of flexural rigidity $D=1.9\,\mathrm{MN\,m}$ bends cylindrically between two support lines a distance $L=4\,\mathrm m$ apart. What is the downward deflection at one-quarter of the span?

- $14\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $10\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $5\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The deflection of the simply supported strip under uniform load is $w(x)=\dfrac{q}{24D}(L^3x-2Lx^3+x^4)$. For $x=\dfrac L4$, expression $L^3x-2Lx^3+x^4$ equals $L^4\left(\dfrac14-\dfrac2{64}+\dfrac1{256}\right)=\dfrac{57}{256}L^4$. Therefore
$$
w\left(\frac L4\right)=\frac{57\,qL^4}{6144\,D}=\frac{57\cdot8\cdot256}{6144\cdot1900}\,\mathrm m=0.01\,\mathrm m.
$$
The downward deflection is therefore $10\,\mathrm{mm}$, compared with approximately $14\,\mathrm{mm}$ at midspan.
