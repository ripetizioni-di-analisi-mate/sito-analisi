A rectangular panel with edges parallel to the axes carries uniform $N_{xy}=60\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, with $N_x=N_y=0$. Which edges carry in-plane forces?

- Only on edges normal to $x$, acting tangentially to those edges
- Only on edges normal to $y$, acting normally to those edges
- On no edges, because $N_x$ and $N_y$ are zero
- On all four edges, acting tangentially to each edge

## Explanation

On an edge normal to $x$, the force per unit length has component $N_xn_x=0$ along $x$ and $N_{xy}n_x$ along $y$. Thus the edges normal to $x$ carry a tangential force of magnitude $60\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ per unit length. On an edge normal to $y$ the component along $x$ is $N_{xy}n_y$, so these edges also carry a tangential force of the same magnitude. All four edges of the panel are therefore in pure shear.
