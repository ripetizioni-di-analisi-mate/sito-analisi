A simply supported strip in cylindrical bending carries a uniform load and is made of a material with $\nu=0.4$. Compare it with a beam of the same material and unit width, with the same span, thickness, and load per unit width. What is the ratio of the strip deflection to the beam deflection?

- $\dfrac{21}{25}$
- $1$
- $\dfrac35$
- $\dfrac{25}{21}$

## Explanation

The strip deflection is $\dfrac{5qL^4}{384D}$ and the beam deflection is $\dfrac{5qL^4}{384EI}$, with $EI=\dfrac{Eh^3}{12}$ for unit width. The deflection ratio is therefore $\dfrac{EI}{D}=1-\nu^2$. Substituting $\nu=0.4$ gives $1-0.16=\dfrac{21}{25}$. The strip is stiffer because its transverse curvature is restrained.
