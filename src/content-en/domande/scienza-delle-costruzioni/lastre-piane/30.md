A plate locally has deflection $w(x,y)=0.01x+0.02y$, with coordinates and deflection in meters. What is $w_{,xy}$?

- $\dfrac{1}{100}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $\dfrac{1}{50}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $\dfrac{3}{100}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$

## Explanation

The slope with respect to $x$ is constant. Its derivative with respect to $y$ is zero.
