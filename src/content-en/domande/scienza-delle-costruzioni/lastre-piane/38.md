For $w=A(x^2+y^2)$, given $A=0.001\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$, $D=4\,\mathrm{MN\,m}$, and $\nu=\dfrac14$, what is $M_x+M_y$?

- $-10\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-16\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The curvatures are equal to $2A$. Each bending moment is $-D(1+\nu)2A=-10\,\mathrm{kN}$.
