Two plates of the same material have thicknesses $h_1=8\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $h_2=12\,\mathrm{mm}$. Calculate the ratio $\dfrac{D_2}{D_1}$ of their flexural rigidities.

::struttura{poligoni="0,-0.55 6,-0.55 6,0.55 0,0.55" tratteggi="0,0 6,0" quote="6 -0.55,6 0.55,h" frecce="0,0,2,0,x;0,0,0,-1,z" riferimento=0 titolo="Thickness h and coordinate z positive downward"}

- $\dfrac{27}8$
- $\dfrac94$
- $\dfrac32$
- $\dfrac8{27}$

## Explanation

The material and Poisson ratio are the same. The flexural rigidity is therefore proportional to the cube of the thickness, giving $\dfrac{D_2}{D_1}=\left(\dfrac{12}{8}\right)^3$.
