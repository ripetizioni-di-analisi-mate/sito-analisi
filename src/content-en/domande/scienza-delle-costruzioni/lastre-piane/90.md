A segment of the midplane, initially of length $\mathrm dx$, develops a slope $\partial_xw=0.02$ while its projection onto the original plane remains $\mathrm dx$. What is the first nonzero term in the axial strain of the segment?

- $4\cdot10^{-4}$
- $2\cdot10^{-2}$
- $1\cdot10^{-4}$
- $2\cdot10^{-4}$

## Explanation

The length of the inclined segment is $\mathrm dx\sqrt{1+(\partial_xw)^2}$. For small slopes, $\sqrt{1+s^2}\approx1+\dfrac{s^2}{2}$, so the axial strain is $\dfrac{(\partial_xw)^2}{2}$. Substituting $\partial_xw=0.02$ gives $\dfrac{4\cdot10^{-4}}{2}=2\cdot10^{-4}$. Linear bending theory neglects this quadratic term.
