A plate has local saddle-shaped deflection $w=0.002\,xy$, with $x$, $y$, and $w$ in meters. What is the curvature $\dfrac{\mathrm d^2w}{\mathrm d\xi^2}$ along the angle bisector, the line $x=y=\dfrac{\xi}{\sqrt2}$?

- $0.002\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $0.004\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $0.001\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$

## Explanation

Along the angle bisector, set $x=y=\dfrac{\xi}{\sqrt2}$, giving $w=0.002\cdot\dfrac{\xi^2}{2}=0.001\,\xi^2$. Differentiating twice with respect to $\xi$ gives $0.002\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$. The same value follows from $w_{,\xi\xi}=w_{,xx}\cos^2\theta+2w_{,xy}\sin\theta\cos\theta+w_{,yy}\sin^2\theta$ with $\theta=45^\circ$. Along the other bisector the curvature is $-0.002\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$, so the zero curvatures along the coordinate axes are not the extreme values.
