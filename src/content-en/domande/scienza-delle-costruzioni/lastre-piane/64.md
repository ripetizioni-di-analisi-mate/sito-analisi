A strip in cylindrical bending is supported along $x=0$ and $x=L=4\,\mathrm m$, has $D=2\,\mathrm{MN\,m}$, and carries no transverse load. A distributed couple along edge $x=L$ produces $M_x(L)=6\,\mathrm{kN}$. What is the slope $w'(0)$ at the other support?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0,-" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" coppie="B,1,M_L" quote="A,B,4 m" titolo="Simply supported strip with a distributed couple along the right edge"}

- $0.004$
- $0.001$
- $0.003$
- $0.002$

## Explanation

Without transverse loading, the moment varies linearly between its edge values, so $M_x(x)=\dfrac{M_Lx}{L}$ with $M_L=6\,\mathrm{kN}$. Integrating $Dw''=-M_x$ twice with $w(0)=w(L)=0$ gives $w(x)=\dfrac{M_L}{6DL}(L^2x-x^3)$. Therefore, $w'(0)=\dfrac{M_LL}{6D}=\dfrac{6\cdot4}{6\cdot2000}=0.002$. At the support carrying the couple, the slope is $-\dfrac{M_LL}{3D}=-0.004$.
