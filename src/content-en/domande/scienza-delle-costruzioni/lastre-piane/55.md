In a region of the plate with no distributed couples, when does the shear resultant $Q_x$ reduce to the single derivative $\partial_xM_x$?

- When $M_{xy}$ does not vary along $x$
- When $M_y$ is zero
- When $M_{xy}$ does not vary along $y$
- When the load $q$ is zero

## Explanation

Moment equilibrium gives $Q_x=\partial_xM_x+\partial_yM_{xy}$. The second term vanishes precisely when $M_{xy}$ is constant along $y$. Variation of $M_{xy}$ along $x$ enters $Q_y$ instead. $M_y$ does not appear in the expression for $Q_x$. Cylindrical bending is a typical case, with $M_{xy}=0$.
