A plate in cylindrical bending is simply supported along $x=0$ and $x=L$, with $L=6\,\mathrm{m}$. The uniform load acts downward with intensity $q=4\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$. Find the bending moment per unit width $M_x$ at midspan.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.65,0.65,q" quote="A,B,6 m" titolo="Strip under cylindrical bending and a uniform load"}

- $36\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-18\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $18\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

For a strip of unit width, the midspan moment is $\dfrac{qL^2}{8}$. Substituting $q=4\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$ and $L=6\,\mathrm{m}$ gives $18\,\mathrm{kN}$.
