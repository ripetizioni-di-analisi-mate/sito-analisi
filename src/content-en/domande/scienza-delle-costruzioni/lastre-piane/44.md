A plate carries only a downward point force $P=6\pi\,\mathrm{kN}$. Away from the edges, assume that the radial shear resultant $Q_r$ is uniform on a circle of radius $r=0.5\,\mathrm m$ centered at the load. What is the magnitude of $Q_r$?

- $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $24\,\mathrm{kN/m}$

## Explanation

Isolate a disk of radius $r$ centered on the force. The shear around its perimeter transmits a total vertical force $Q_r\cdot2\pi r$, which must balance $P$. Therefore
$$
Q_r=\frac{P}{2\pi r}=\frac{6\pi}{2\pi\cdot0.5}\,\mathrm{kN/m}=6\,\mathrm{kN/m}.
$$
Approaching the load point, the shear grows as $\dfrac1r$.
