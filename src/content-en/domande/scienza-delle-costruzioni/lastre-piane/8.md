A plate strip in cylindrical bending is clamped at $x=0$ and free at $x=L=3\,\mathrm{m}$. The uniform load acts downward with intensity $q=5\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$. Find the moment per unit width $M_x(0)$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" carichi="A,B,0.34,0.34,q" quote="A,B,3 m" titolo="Strip clamped at A and free at B"}

- $-22.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $22.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-45\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-15\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The resultant load on the strip is $qL$ and its moment arm is half of $L$. The fixed-end moment puts the top fibers in tension, so it is $-\dfrac{qL^2}{2}$.
