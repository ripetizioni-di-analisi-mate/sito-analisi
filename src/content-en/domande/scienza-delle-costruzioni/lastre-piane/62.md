A strip in cylindrical bending is supported along $x=0$ and $x=L$ and carries a uniform load. What is moment $M_y$ along the support line $x=0$?

- $\nu\dfrac{qL^2}{8}$, as at midspan
- $0$, because $M_x=0$ and $M_y=\nu M_x$ there
- $\dfrac{qL^2}{8}$
- $-\nu\dfrac{qL}{2}$, proportional to the reaction

## Explanation

In cylindrical bending, $w$ is independent of $y$, so $M_y=-D\nu w''=\nu M_x$. On the support line, the simply supported boundary condition requires $M_x=0$. Thus $M_y$ also vanishes along the support and increases to $\nu\dfrac{qL^2}{8}$ at midspan.
