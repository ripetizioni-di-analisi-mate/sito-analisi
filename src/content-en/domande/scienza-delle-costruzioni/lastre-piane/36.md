A plate of thickness $0.2\,\mathrm{m}$ has $N_x=400\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and $M_x=20\,\mathrm{kN}$. What is the normal stress at face $z=-\dfrac h2$?

- $-1\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $1\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $3\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $5\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The membrane contribution is $2\,\mathrm{MPa}$. At the face with negative coordinate, the bending contribution is $-3\,\mathrm{MPa}$, so the sum is $-1\,\mathrm{MPa}$.
