A square plate simply supported on all four edges carries a uniform load. Which of the modes $(1,1)$, $(1,3)$, $(3,3)$, and $(2,1)$ make a negative contribution to the downward deflection at the center?

- $(1,3)$ and $(3,3)$
- $(1,3)$ and $(2,1)$
- None, because all amplitudes are positive
- Only $(1,3)$

## Explanation

For uniform loading, $q_{mn}$ is positive when $m$ and $n$ are odd and zero when either is even. Mode $(2,1)$ therefore makes no contribution. The amplitudes $W_{mn}$ of the odd modes are positive, so the sign of the contribution depends on $\sin\dfrac{m\pi}{2}\sin\dfrac{n\pi}{2}$. The sine equals $1$ for index $1$ and $-1$ for index $3$. Thus mode $(1,3)$ makes a negative contribution, whereas modes $(1,1)$ and $(3,3)$ make positive contributions.
