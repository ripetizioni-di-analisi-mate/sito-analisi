A rectangular plate of sides $a$ and $b$, simply supported on all four edges, deflects in mode $(1,1)$ alone, with amplitude $W_{11}=4\,\mathrm{mm}$. What is $w$ at point $\left(\dfrac a4,\dfrac b4\right)$?

- $4\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $1\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $2\sqrt2\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $2\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The deflected shape is $w=W_{11}\sin\dfrac{\pi x}{a}\sin\dfrac{\pi y}{b}$. At the specified point, both sines equal $\sin\dfrac{\pi}{4}=\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$. Their product is therefore $\dfrac12$, giving $w=2\,\mathrm{mm}$.
