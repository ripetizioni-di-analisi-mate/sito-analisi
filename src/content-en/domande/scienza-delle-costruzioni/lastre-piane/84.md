Along $y=\dfrac{b}{2}$, the deflection of a simply supported plate is $w(x)=A\sin(\dfrac{\pi x}{a})+B\sin(\dfrac{2\pi x}{a})$. Two sensors read $5\,\mathrm{mm}$ at $x=\dfrac{a}{4}$ and $1\,\mathrm{mm}$ at $x=\dfrac{3a}{4}$. What is amplitude $B$?

- $3\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $4\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $2\sqrt2\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $2\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The first sine has the same value at both sections. The second sine equals $+1$ and $-1$, respectively. Subtracting the second reading from the first eliminates $A$ and gives $2B=5-1=4\,\mathrm{mm}$, so $B=2\,\mathrm{mm}$.
