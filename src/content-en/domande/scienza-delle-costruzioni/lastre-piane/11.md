A square plate simply supported on all four edges has side $a=2\,\mathrm{m}$ and flexural rigidity $D=4\,\mathrm{MN\,m}$. The load is $q=\pi^4\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi x}{a}\right)\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi y}{a}\right)\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$. Calculate the downward deflection at the center.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 4,0 4,4 0,4" frecce="0,0,2,0,x;0,0,0,2,y" quote="0 0,4 0,a;4 4,4 0,a" punti="2,2,C" riferimento=0 titolo="Square plate simply supported on all four sides"}

- $2\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $\dfrac12\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $4\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $1\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

For mode $(1,1)$, the square of $k_x^2+k_y^2$ is $\dfrac{\pi^4}{4}\,\mathrm{m^{-4}}$. Both sines equal one at the center, so $w_C=\dfrac{\pi^4}{1\,000\pi^4}\,\mathrm{m}$.
