What are $M_y$ and $M_{xy}$ along the straight, simply supported edge $x=0$ of an isotropic plate?

- $M_y=0$, while $M_{xy}$ is generally nonzero
- $M_y=0$ and $M_{xy}=0$
- $M_y\ne0$ and $M_{xy}=0$
- $M_y\ne0$ and $M_{xy}\ne0$

## Explanation

Along the edge, $w=0$ and hence $w_{,yy}=0$. Condition $M_x=-D(w_{,xx}+\nu w_{,yy})=0$ then gives $w_{,xx}=0$. Therefore, $M_y=-D(w_{,yy}+\nu w_{,xx})=0$. The mixed derivative $w_{,xy}$ measures how the normal slope changes along $y$. A simple support leaves this slope free. Consequently $M_{xy}=-D(1-\nu)w_{,xy}$ is generally nonzero, as in Navier’s solution.
