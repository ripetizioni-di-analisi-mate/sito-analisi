A rectangular plate simply supported on all four edges carries a uniform load of intensity $q=\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$. Calculate coefficient $q_{11}$ in Navier’s double Fourier sine series.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 3,0 3,6 0,6" frecce="0,0,1.5,0,x;0,0,0,2,y" quote="0 0,3 0,a;3 6,3 0,b" punti="1.5,3,C" riferimento=0 titolo="Rectangular plate with side b parallel to y"}

- $4\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$
- $16\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$
- $16\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$

## Explanation

The coefficient of mode $(1,1)$ for uniform loading is $\dfrac{16q}{\pi^2}$ because each sine integral contains the factor $2$ divided by $\pi$.
