A rectangular plate has loading and supports symmetric about centerline $x=\dfrac a2$. Which quantity vanishes at every point along that midline?

- The deflection $w$
- The moment $M_x$
- The slope $w_{,y}$
- The slope $w_{,x}$

## Explanation

By symmetry, $w(a-x,y)=w(x,y)$. Differentiation with respect to $x$ gives $-w_{,x}(a-x,y)=w_{,x}(x,y)$. For $x=\dfrac a2$ this implies $w_{,x}=0$. The downward deflection and moment $M_x$ are even functions about the centerline and are generally nonzero there. At the center of a simply supported plate they in fact reach maxima. The slope $w_{,y}$ vanishes only on centerline $y=\dfrac b2$ if the system is also symmetric about that line.
