Model a wall of thickness $h=0.2\,\mathrm m$ and height $H=3\,\mathrm m$ as a plate loaded in its own plane. Coordinate $y$ points vertically upward, with the base at $y=0$. The only distributed load is the self-weight $f_y=-\gamma h$, with $\gamma=25\,\mathrm{kN/m^3}$. The top edge is unloaded. If $N_{xy}=0$ and $N_y$ depends only on $y$, what is $N_y$ at the base?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 4,0 4,3 0,3" linee="-0.6,0 4.6,0" frecce="0,0,1.4,0,x;0,0,0,1.4,y;2,2,2,1,f_y" quote="4 0,4 3,3 m" riferimento=0 titolo="Wall viewed in its own plane, with its base at $y=0$"}

- $15\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $-15\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $-5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $-7.5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$

## Explanation

Given $N_{xy}=0$, the second in-plane equilibrium equation reduces to $\dfrac{\mathrm dN_y}{\mathrm dy}+f_y=0$. Since $f_y=-25\cdot0.2=-5\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$, we have $\dfrac{\mathrm dN_y}{\mathrm dy}=5\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$. The top edge is unloaded, so $N_y(3)=0$ and $N_y(y)=5(y-3)\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. The result at the base is $N_y(0)=-15\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, a compressive force equal in magnitude to the weight of the wall per unit length.
