Two parallel clamped edges a distance $L=4\,\mathrm m$ apart restrain a slab that undergoes cylindrical bending under uniform load $q=6\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$. What is $M_x$ along the clamped edge $x=0$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,incastro,0" carichi="A,B,0.45,0.45,q" quote="A,B,4 m" titolo="Strip clamped along both end lines"}

- $-12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-8\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

By symmetry, the reactions are $\dfrac{qL}{2}$ and the two fixed-end moments have the same value $M_0$. Then $M_x(x)=M_0+\dfrac{qLx}{2}-\dfrac{qx^2}{2}$. Since $Dw''=-M_x$ and the slope is zero at both clamped ends, $\displaystyle\int_0^LM_x\,\mathrm dx=0$ must hold. This gives $M_0L+\dfrac{qL^3}{4}-\dfrac{qL^3}{6}=0$, so $M_0=-\dfrac{qL^2}{12}=-8\,\mathrm{kN}$. The negative sign indicates tension on the top face at the clamped edge.
