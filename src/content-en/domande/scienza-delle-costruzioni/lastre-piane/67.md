A strip in cylindrical bending is continuous over three support lines at $x=0$, $x=3\,\mathrm m$, and $x=6\,\mathrm m$, and carries a uniform load $q=4\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$ on both spans. What is the middle support reaction per unit width?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;C,carrello" carichi="A,C,0.45,0.45,q" quote="A,B,3 m;B,C,3 m" titolo="Continuous strip over three lines of support"}

- $12\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $7.5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{kN/m}$

## Explanation

By symmetry the slope at the middle support is zero, so each span behaves as a strip clamped at $x=3\,\mathrm m$ and simply supported at the other end. For this structure the outer support reaction is $\dfrac{3qL}{8}$ and the clamped-end reaction is $\dfrac{5qL}{8}$, with $L=3\,\mathrm m$. Both spans contribute their clamped-end reactions to the middle support, giving $R_B=\dfrac{5qL}{4}=15\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. The reactions sum to $2\cdot4.5+15=24\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, which equals the total load $4\cdot6=24\,\mathrm{kN/m}$.
