Which quantity is generally nonzero along a straight, clamped edge of a loaded plate?

- The deflection $w$
- The normal slope $\partial_nw$
- The normal bending moment $M_{nn}$
- The tangential slope $\partial_tw$

## Explanation

Clamping imposes $w=0$ and $\partial_nw=0$ at every point on the edge. Since $w$ vanishes along the entire edge, so does its derivative along the edge, giving $\partial_tw=0$. The moment $M_{nn}$ is the reaction that restrains rotation and is generally nonzero. For example, a uniformly loaded strip clamped along two lines gives $-\dfrac{qL^2}{12}$.
