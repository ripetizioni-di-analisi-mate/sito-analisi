A square plate of side $a$ simply supported on all four edges carries uniform load $q$. Retaining only term $(1,1)$ of Navier’s series gives downward center deflection $k\dfrac{qa^4}{D}$. What is $k$?

- $\dfrac{16}{\pi^6}$
- $\dfrac{1}{\pi^4}$
- $\dfrac{4}{\pi^6}$
- $\dfrac{5}{384}$

## Explanation

For uniform loading, the coefficient is $q_{11}=\dfrac{16q}{\pi^2}$. For a square plate, $\alpha_1^2+\beta_1^2=\dfrac{2\pi^2}{a^2}$, so
$$
W_{11}=\frac{16q}{\pi^2}\cdot\frac{a^4}{4\pi^4D}=\frac{4}{\pi^6}\,\frac{qa^4}{D}.
$$
At the center both sines equal one, giving $k=\dfrac{4}{\pi^6}\approx0.0042$. This value is about one-third of the simply supported strip coefficient $\dfrac{5}{384}\approx0.013$ because the plate is supported on four edges.
