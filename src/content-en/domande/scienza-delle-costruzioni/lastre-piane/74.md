Apply the cylindrical bending solution $w=w(x)$, with $M_x\ne0$ and $\nu>0$, to a plate with free edges $y=0$ and $y=b$. Which free-edge condition fails on those sides?

- $V_y=Q_y+\partial_xM_{xy}=0$
- $M_y=0$
- Both $M_y=0$ and $V_y=0$
- Neither, because the deflected shape is independent of $y$

## Explanation

In cylindrical bending, $M_y=-D\nu w''=\nu M_x$, which is nonzero wherever $M_x\ne0$. Thus the condition $M_y=0$ on the free edges is violated. The effective shear, however, vanishes because $M_{xy}=0$ and $Q_y=\partial_xM_{xy}+\partial_yM_y=0$ for a field independent of $y$. Near the free edges the actual deflected shape therefore departs from cylindrical bending. A transverse curvature develops that makes $M_y$ vanish.
