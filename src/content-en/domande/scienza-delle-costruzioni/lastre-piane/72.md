A rectangular plate is simply supported on two edges meeting at corner $O$, with nonzero twisting moment $M_{xy}$ at $O$. What concentrated action develops at corner $O$?

- A concentrated couple
- No action, because $w=0$ at $O$
- A distributed shear force equal to $Q_n$
- A concentrated transverse force balanced by a corner reaction

## Explanation

Along each edge the twisting moment enters the effective shear through term $\partial_tM_{nt}$. Integration by parts along the edge leaves a concentrated endpoint term proportional to $M_{nt}$. At the corner the contributions of the two adjoining edges combine to give a concentrated transverse force. The corner support provides the reaction balancing this force.
