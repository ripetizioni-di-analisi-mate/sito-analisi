A plate of thickness $h=8\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries a uniform shear stress $\tau_{xy}=-24\,\mathrm{MPa}$. What is the signed in-plane shear force resultant $N_{xy}$?

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 6,0 6,3.4 0,3.4" frecce="0,0,2,0,x;0,0,0,1.7,y;6,2.8,6,1.2,τ_xy;0,0.6,0,2.2,τ_xy;3.8,3.4,2.2,3.4,τ_xy;2.2,0,3.8,0,τ_xy" riferimento=0 titolo="Uniform negative shear on the faces of the panel"}

- $192\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $-24\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $-192\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $-384\,\mathrm{kN/m}$

## Explanation

The in-plane shear resultant per unit length is the through-thickness integral $N_{xy}=\displaystyle\int_{-\frac h2}^{\frac h2}\tau_{xy}\,\mathrm dz$. Since $\tau_{xy}$ is constant through the thickness, the integral equals $N_{xy}=\tau_{xy}h=-24\cdot8\,\mathrm{N/mm}=-192\,\mathrm{N/mm}$. Because $1\,\mathrm{N/mm}=1\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, this gives $N_{xy}=-192\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, with the same sign as the stress. The value $-24\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ results from forgetting to multiply by the thickness.
