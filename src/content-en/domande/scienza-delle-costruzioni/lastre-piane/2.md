The upper half of a plate is subject to $\tau_{xy}=+20\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and the lower half to $\tau_{xy}=-20\,\mathrm{MPa}$. The origin of $z$ is at the middle surface and $z>0$ points upward. With $N_{xy}=\int\tau_{xy}\,dz$ and $M_{xy}=\int z\tau_{xy}\,dz$, what signs result?

- $N_{xy}>0,\ M_{xy}=0$
- $N_{xy}=0,\ M_{xy}=0$
- $N_{xy}=0,\ M_{xy}>0$
- $N_{xy}=0,\ M_{xy}<0$

## Explanation

Equal thicknesses and opposite shear stresses make the membrane resultants of the two halves cancel. The product $z\tau_{xy}$ is positive both above and below the middle surface. The two contributions to twisting moment add.
