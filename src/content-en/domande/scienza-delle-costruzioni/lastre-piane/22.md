A plate of thickness $8\,\mathrm{mm}$ carries a uniform stress $\sigma_x=18\,\mathrm{MPa}$. What bending moment resultant $M_x$ does it produce about its own midplane?

- $144\,\mathrm{N}$
- $288\,\mathrm{N}$
- $0\,\mathrm{N}$
- $576\,\mathrm{N}$

## Explanation

The integrand $z\sigma_x$ is odd over the symmetric interval. The bending moment is therefore zero, while the normal force resultant is nonzero.
