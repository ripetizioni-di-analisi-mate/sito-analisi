Two vertical segments of lengths $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and $4\,\mathrm{m}$ are joined by a pin with an ideal lateral restraint. The outer ends are pinned. Both segments carry the same compressive force $P$. The stiffnesses $EI$ are $360$ and $900\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. At what value of $P$ does the first mode appear? Round to one decimal place.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,7;C,0,3" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,cerniera,270;B,carrello,0;C,carrello,0" forze="B,0,-1.1,P" quote="A,C,3 m,-0.6;C,B,4 m,-0.6" cerniere="C" riferimento=0 titolo="Model of the compression member"}

- $394.8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $949.9\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $253.8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $98.7\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The restrained pin prevents lateral displacement while allowing the segments to rotate independently. The two Euler buckling loads are therefore $394.8$ and $555.2\,\mathrm{kN}$. The first buckling mode occurs at the lower load, $394.8\,\mathrm{kN}$.
