For a column with conservative restraints, the Rayleigh quotient evaluated with a kinematically admissible trial function is $520\,\mathrm{kN}$. Which of the following values could be the exact first buckling load?

- $560\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $480\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $600\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $650\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The lowest buckling load is the minimum of the Rayleigh quotient over all admissible functions. Evaluating it with a single admissible function therefore gives an upper bound on the exact buckling load. It follows that $P_{\mathrm{cr}}\le520\,\mathrm{kN}$. Of the proposed values, only $480\,\mathrm{kN}$ satisfies this inequality.
