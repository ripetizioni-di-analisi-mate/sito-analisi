A column is fixed at its base and loaded at its top by a compressive force of fixed direction. A horizontal spring of finite positive stiffness restrains lateral displacement at the top. The top remains free to rotate. The effective length factor $K$ of the column is

- less than $0.699$
- greater than $2$
- between $0.699$ and $2$
- equal to $1$ for every spring stiffness

## Explanation

As the spring stiffness tends to zero, the top becomes free and the column approaches a cantilever with $K=2$. As the stiffness tends to infinity, the top cannot translate and the column approaches the fixed-pinned case with $K\simeq0.699$. A spring of finite stiffness provides an intermediate degree of lateral restraint. The critical load therefore lies between the two limiting loads, and $K$ is **between $0.699$ and $2$**, with its precise value determined by the spring stiffness.
