Two rigid bars, each of length $a=1.5\,\mathrm m$, form a vertical column. The lower bar is pinned to the ground with a rotational spring of stiffness $k=90\,\mathrm{kN\,m/rad}$. The two bars are connected by a pin with a second rotational spring of the same stiffness $k$. A compressive force $P$ acts at the free upper end and remains vertical. Calculate the first buckling load.

- $60.0\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $157.1\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $30.0\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $22.9\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Let $\theta_1$ and $\theta_2$ denote the rotations of the two bars from the vertical. The spring rotations are $\theta_1$ and $\theta_2-\theta_1$, while the top moves down by $\dfrac{a(\theta_1^2+\theta_2^2)}{2}$ to second order. The total potential energy is therefore $\dfrac12k\theta_1^2+\dfrac12k(\theta_2-\theta_1)^2-\dfrac{Pa}{2}(\theta_1^2+\theta_2^2)$, with stiffness matrix
$$
\begin{bmatrix} 2k-Pa & -k\\ -k & k-Pa\end{bmatrix}.
$$
Setting $\mu=\dfrac{Pa}{k}$ gives the condition $\mu^2-3\mu+1=0$ for a zero determinant. Its smaller root is $\mu=\dfrac{3-\sqrt5}{2}\simeq0.382$. Therefore, $P_{\mathrm{cr}}\simeq0.382\cdot\dfrac{90}{1.5}\,\mathrm{kN}\simeq22.9\,\mathrm{kN}$.
