The column shown is pinned at both ends in the plane of bending. Its length is $8\,\mathrm{m}$. The flexural rigidity is $EI=640\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. Coordinate $x$ is measured from $A$. Find the coordinate of the interior node of the second Euler mode.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="B,-1.76,0,P" quote="A,B,8 m,0.75"  titolo="Compression member and its flexural end restraints"}

- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The second mode is proportional to $\sin\left(\dfrac{2\pi x}{L}\right)$. Its only interior node is at $x=\dfrac L2=4\,\mathrm{m}$.
