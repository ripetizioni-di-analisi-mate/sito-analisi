The column shown is pinned at both ends in the plane of bending. Its length is $6\,\mathrm{m}$. The flexural rigidity is $EI=360\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. Calculate the Rayleigh estimate using $\varphi(x)=x(L-x)$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="B,-1.32,0,P" quote="A,B,6 m,0.75"  titolo="Compression member and its flexural end restraints"}

- $60\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $90\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $120\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $240\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

With $\varphi=x(L-x)$, the numerator is $4EIL$ and the denominator is $\dfrac{L^3}{3}$. The quotient is $\dfrac{12EI}{L^2}=120\,\mathrm{kN}$.
