A pin-ended column has a first-mode initial imperfection of amplitude $3\,\mathrm{mm}$ and a buckling load $P_{\mathrm{cr}}=200\,\mathrm{kN}$. The section has area $2400\,\mathrm{mm^2}$ and section modulus $30\,000\,\mathrm{mm^3}$. Under a concentric compressive force of $120\,\mathrm{kN}$, what is the maximum compressive stress at midlength according to second-order elastic theory?

- $62\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $50\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $68\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $80\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

The total midspan deflection is $\dfrac{3}{1-\dfrac{120}{200}}\,\mathrm{mm}=7.5\,\mathrm{mm}$. Since the end moments are zero, the midspan moment is $M=Pv_{\max}=120\,000\cdot7.5\,\mathrm{N\,mm}=900\,000\,\mathrm{N\,mm}$. The direct axial stress is $\dfrac{120\,000}{2400}\,\mathrm{MPa}=50\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and the bending stress is $\dfrac{900\,000}{30\,000}\,\mathrm{MPa}=30\,\mathrm{MPa}$. The maximum compressive stress is therefore $80\,\mathrm{MPa}$. The moment arm is measured from the line joining the pins, so the total deflection must be used.
