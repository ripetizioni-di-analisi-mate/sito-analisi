A rigid guided plate supported by two columns moves downward without rotating. The columns have axial stiffnesses $100$ and $50\,\mathrm{kN/mm}$ and individual buckling loads $160$ and $70\,\mathrm{kN}$. The model distributes the load so that the columns shorten equally until one of them buckles. What is the total load at the onset of buckling?

- $240.0\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $230.0\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $210.0\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $70.0\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Equal shortening gives load fractions $0.667$ and $0.333$. The total loads that would bring each column to its own buckling load are $240.0$ and $210.0\,\mathrm{kN}$. The smaller value, $210.0\,\mathrm{kN}$, is reached first.
