The portal frame shown has two identical columns of height $3\,\mathrm m$ and flexural rigidity $EI=900\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$, fixed at their bases. An infinitely rigid beam is rigidly connected to both columns. The frame has no bracing to prevent horizontal translation of the beam. Each column carries a vertical force $P$ at its top. At what value of $P$ does the portal frame become unstable?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;C,4,3;D,4,0" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D" vincoli="A,incastro;D,incastro" forze="B,0,-1,P;C,0,-1,P" etichette="2,3.35,EI = ∞" quote="A,B,3 m,-0.7" titolo="Sway frame with a rigid crossbeam"}

- $400\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $25\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $100\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $200\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The rigid beam prevents rotation at the column tops while allowing them to translate horizontally. Thus each column has zero rotation at both ends and is free to displace laterally at the top. The buckled shape is an antisymmetric half-wave with an inflection point at midheight, so $L_{\mathrm{eff}}=L=3\,\mathrm m$. This gives $P=\dfrac{\pi^2\cdot900}{9}\,\mathrm{kN}=100\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$ for each column. The value $400\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$ would apply to a braced frame with $L_{\mathrm{eff}}=\dfrac L2$.
