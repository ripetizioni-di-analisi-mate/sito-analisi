A column has a rectangular section of width $b$ along $z$ and depth $h$ along $y$. For bending about $y$, the column is fixed at one end and pinned at the other. For bending about $z$, both ends are pinned. For what ratio $\dfrac hb$ are the two critical loads equal?

- $2.05$
- $0.70$
- $1.43$
- $1.27$

## Explanation

The area moments of inertia are $I_y=\dfrac{hb^3}{12}$ and $I_z=\dfrac{bh^3}{12}$, with $K_y\simeq0.699$ and $K_z=1$. The critical loads are equal when $\dfrac{I_y}{K_y^2}=\dfrac{I_z}{K_z^2}$. Dividing by $\dfrac{hb^3}{12}$ gives $\left(\dfrac hb\right)^2=\dfrac{1}{0.699^2}$. Thus $\dfrac hb=\dfrac{1}{0.699}\simeq1.43$.
