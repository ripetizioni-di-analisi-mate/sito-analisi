Two columns have the same effective length. The first has $E=210\,\mathrm{GPa}$ and $I=20\,\mathrm{cm^4}$. The second has $E=70\,\mathrm{GPa}$ and $I=45\,\mathrm{cm^4}$. What is the ratio of the critical load of the second column to that of the first?

- $1.33$
- $2.25$
- $0.33$
- $0.75$

## Explanation

For equal effective lengths, the critical loads are proportional to $EI$. The ratio is approximately $\dfrac{70\cdot 45}{210\cdot 20}=0.75$.
