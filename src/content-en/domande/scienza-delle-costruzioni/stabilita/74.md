The pin-ended column shown has length $5\,\mathrm m$ and flexural rigidity $EI=500\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. A lateral spring of stiffness $k=150\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ resists displacement at midspan. Estimate the critical load using the Rayleigh quotient with trial function $\varphi=\sin\!\left(\dfrac{\pi x}{L}\right)$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="B,-1.1,0,P" linee="2.5,0 2.5,-0.2 2.63,-0.3 2.37,-0.45 2.63,-0.6 2.37,-0.75 2.5,-0.85 2.5,-1.05;2.22,-1.05 2.78,-1.05" etichette="2.95,-0.55,k" quote="A,B,5 m,-0.7" titolo="Compression member with a transverse spring at midspan"}

- $197\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $349\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $273\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $501\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

At midspan the sine equals $1$, so the spring adds the term $k\,\varphi\!\left(\dfrac L2\right)^2=k$ to the numerator of the quotient. The bending contribution to the numerator is $\dfrac{\pi^4EI}{2L^3}$, and the denominator is $\int_0^L(\varphi')^2\,dx=\dfrac{\pi^2}{2L}$. Thus the estimate is $\dfrac{\pi^2EI}{L^2}+\dfrac{2kL}{\pi^2}$. Substituting the given values yields $197.4\,\mathrm{kN}+152.0\,\mathrm{kN}\simeq349\,\mathrm{kN}$.
