A column has a rectangular section of width $40\,\mathrm{mm}$ along $z$ and depth $100\,\mathrm{mm}$ along $y$. The column axis is $x$. Both ends are pinned for bending in either plane. At the first buckling load, in which direction does the member deflect laterally, and about which axis does the cross section bend?

- It moves along $y$ and bends about $y$
- It moves along $z$ and bends about $y$
- It moves along $y$ and bends about $z$
- It moves along $z$ and bends about $z$

## Explanation

The area moments of inertia are $I_y=\dfrac{100\cdot40^3}{12}\,\mathrm{mm^4}$ and $I_z=\dfrac{40\cdot100^3}{12}\,\mathrm{mm^4}$, so $I_y<I_z$. With the same end conditions in both planes, the smaller area moment of inertia governs, corresponding to bending about $y$. Displacement along $z$ causes bending about $y$, so the column **moves along $z$**, in the direction of the smaller cross-sectional dimension.
