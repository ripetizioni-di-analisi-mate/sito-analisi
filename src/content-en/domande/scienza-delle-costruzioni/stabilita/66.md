A steel column with $E=210\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and a compressive elastic limit of $235\,\mathrm{MPa}$ has slenderness ratio $\lambda=40$. Euler’s formula gives $\sigma_{\mathrm{cr}}=\dfrac{\pi^2E}{\lambda^2}\simeq1295\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Which statement is correct?

- It is the stress at which the member collapses
- The value is conservative because it underestimates the capacity
- It overestimates the capacity because the material leaves its elastic range first
- The member buckles elastically at $235\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

Euler’s formula assumes linear elastic behavior up to bifurcation. Here the predicted buckling stress $1295\,\mathrm{MPa}$ greatly exceeds the elastic limit $235\,\mathrm{MPa}$. The material therefore leaves the linear elastic range well before the Euler load, violating the assumptions of the calculation. The Euler value therefore **overestimates** the capacity of this stocky column, which is governed by material strength.
