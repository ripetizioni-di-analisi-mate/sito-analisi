In the plane of bending, the member shown is fixed at $A$ and guided at $B$. Its length is $6\,\mathrm{m}$. The transverse displacement and rotation at $B$ are restrained. The guide permits axial translation. Calculate the effective buckling length.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,doppio-pendolo,270" forze="B,-1.32,0,P" quote="A,B,6 m,0.75"  titolo="Compression member and its flexural end restraints"}

- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Both ends restrain lateral displacement and rotation. The effective length is therefore half the actual length, or $3\,\mathrm{m}$.
