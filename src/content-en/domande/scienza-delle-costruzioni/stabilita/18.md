Two vertical rigid bars, each of length $2\,\mathrm m$, meet at a pin $C$ fitted with a rotational spring of stiffness $k=200\,\mathrm{kN\,m/rad}$. The lower end $A$ is pinned. The upper end $B$ is restrained laterally and can slide vertically. A compressive force $P$ acts at $B$. Calculate the critical load.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,0,2;B,0,4" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,0" cerniere="C" forze="B,0,-1,P" etichette="0.45,2,k" quote="A,C,2 m,-0.6;C,B,2 m,-0.6" riferimento=0 titolo="Two rigid members joined by a pin with a rotational spring"}

- $200\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $50\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $100\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $400\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Since $A$ and $B$ stay on the same vertical line, the only possible mechanism is lateral displacement of $C$, with the two bars rotating through equal angles $\theta$ in opposite directions. The spring at $C$ thus undergoes the relative rotation $2\theta$ and stores the energy $\dfrac12k(2\theta)^2=2k\theta^2$. The vertical projection of each bar decreases by $\dfrac{a\theta^2}{2}$ with $a=2\,\mathrm m$. Thus $B$ moves down by $a\theta^2$ and the force does work $Pa\theta^2$. The total potential energy $(2k-Pa)\theta^2$ ceases to be positive at $P=\dfrac{2k}{a}$. This gives $P_{\mathrm{cr}}=\dfrac{2\cdot200}{2}\,\mathrm{kN}=200\,\mathrm{kN}$.
