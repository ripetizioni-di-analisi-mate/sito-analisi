A linearly elastic column with slenderness ratio $\lambda=120$ reaches its Euler buckling load within the elastic range. What is the mean axial strain $\varepsilon$ in the straight configuration at bifurcation?

- $6.85\cdot10^{-4}$
- $8.22\cdot10^{-2}$
- $2.62\cdot10^{-2}$
- $6.94\cdot10^{-5}$

## Explanation

At the critical load the average compressive stress is $\sigma_{\mathrm{cr}}=\dfrac{\pi^2E}{\lambda^2}$. By Hooke’s law, the corresponding axial strain is $\varepsilon=\dfrac{\sigma_{\mathrm{cr}}}{E}=\dfrac{\pi^2}{\lambda^2}$. The elastic modulus cancels, so the strain at bifurcation depends only on the slenderness ratio. Substituting $\lambda=120$ gives $\varepsilon=\dfrac{\pi^2}{14\,400}\simeq6.85\cdot10^{-4}$.
