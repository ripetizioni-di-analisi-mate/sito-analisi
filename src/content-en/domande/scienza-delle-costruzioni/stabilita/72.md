Estimate the buckling load of a column of length $L$, fixed at $x=0$ and pinned at $x=L$, using the Rayleigh quotient. Which trial function is admissible?

- $\varphi(x)=x(L-x)$
- $\varphi(x)=\sin\!\left(\dfrac{\pi x}{L}\right)$
- $\varphi(x)=x^2$
- $\varphi(x)=x^2(L-x)$

## Explanation

A trial function must satisfy the kinematic boundary conditions, here $\varphi(0)=0$, $\varphi'(0)=0$, and $\varphi(L)=0$. The function $x^2(L-x)$ satisfies $\varphi(0)=0$, $\varphi'(0)=2xL-3x^2\big|_{x=0}=0$, and $\varphi(L)=0$, so it is **admissible**. The functions $x(L-x)$ and $\sin\!\left(\dfrac{\pi x}{L}\right)$ have nonzero slope at the fixed end. The function $x^2$ does not vanish at the pin. The zero-moment condition at $x=L$ is a natural boundary condition and need not be imposed on the trial function.
