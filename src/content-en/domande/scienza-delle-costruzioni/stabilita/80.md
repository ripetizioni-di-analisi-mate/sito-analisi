A pin-ended column with constant $EI$ has an initial unloaded shape $v_0(x)=e_2\sin\!\left(\dfrac{2\pi x}{L}\right)$, with $e_2=2\,\mathrm{mm}$, and a first buckling load $P_1$. Under the concentric compressive force $P=\dfrac{P_1}{2}$, what is the total amplitude of component $\sin\!\left(\dfrac{2\pi x}{L}\right)$?

- $4.00\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $2.00\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $2.29\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $2.67\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

For the shape $\sin\!\left(\dfrac{2\pi x}{L}\right)$, taking the second derivative introduces the factor $\dfrac{4\pi^2}{L^2}$, giving the corresponding buckling load $P_2=4P_1$. For this component, the equation $EI(v-v_0)''+Pv=0$ gives the amplitude $a_2=\dfrac{e_2}{1-\dfrac{P}{P_2}}$. Substituting $\dfrac{P}{P_2}=\dfrac18$ gives $a_2=\dfrac{2}{\dfrac78}\,\mathrm{mm}=\dfrac{16}{7}\,\mathrm{mm}\simeq2.29\,\mathrm{mm}$. The value $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ would follow from incorrectly using $P_1$ in the denominator.
