The column shown is pinned at both ends in the plane of bending. Its length is $6\,\mathrm{m}$. The flexural rigidity is $EI=360\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. What is the load corresponding to the second sinusoidal mode?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="B,-1.32,0,P" quote="A,B,6 m,0.75"  titolo="Compression member and its flexural end restraints"}

- $20\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $10\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $90\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $40\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

For a column pinned at both ends, mode $n$ has deflected shape $v(x)=A\sin\dfrac{n\pi x}{L}$ and load $P_n=\dfrac{n^2\pi^2EI}{L^2}$.

For $n=1$ this gives $P_1=\dfrac{\pi^2\cdot360}{6^2}\,\mathrm{kN}=10\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$.

The second mode has $n=2$, so its load is four times the first.

Therefore $P_2=4\cdot10\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}=40\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$. The value $20\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$ would follow from multiplying $P_1$ by $n$ instead of $n^2$.
