For the ideal straight, uniform pin-ended column shown, a proposed buckling mode is $A\sin(\dfrac{\pi x}{L})+B\sin(\dfrac{2\pi x}{L})$, with both $A$ and $B$ nonzero. There is no transverse load. Can this be an exact eigenmode under one compressive force $P$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="B,-1.32,0,P" quote="A,B,6 m,0.75"  titolo="Compression member and its flexural end restraints"}

- Yes, because both vanish at the ends.
- Yes, by setting $P$ equal to the mean of the two critical loads.
- Yes, if the coefficients have opposite signs.
- No, the two sinusoids require distinct critical loads.

## Explanation

In the equation $EI\,v''+Pv=0$ the first sine has coefficient $A(P-P_1)$ and the second $B(P-4P_1)$, where $P_1=\dfrac{\pi^2EI}{L^2}>0$. The sines are linearly independent, so the two coefficients must vanish separately. For a single load, at least one amplitude must therefore be zero.
