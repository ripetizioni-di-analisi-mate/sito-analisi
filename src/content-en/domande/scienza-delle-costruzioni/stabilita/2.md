The column shown is pinned at both ends in the plane of bending. Its length is $6\,\mathrm{m}$. The flexural rigidity is $EI=360\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. Calculate the load corresponding to the second sinusoidal mode.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="B,-1.32,0,P" quote="A,B,6 m,0.75"  titolo="Compression member and its flexural end restraints"}

- $20\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $10\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $90\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $40\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The first mode has $P_1=\dfrac{\pi^2EI}{L^2}=10\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$. The second mode has twice the wavenumber and four times the buckling load.
