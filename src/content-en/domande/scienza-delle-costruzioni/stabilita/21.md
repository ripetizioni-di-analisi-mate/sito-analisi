A pin-ended member of length $3\,\mathrm m$ and flexural rigidity $EI=900\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ is loaded in axial **tension** by a force of $1500\,\mathrm{kN}$. Its first critical load in compression is approximately $987\,\mathrm{kN}$. In the linearized elastic model, the straight configuration is

- unstable because the force exceeds $987\,\mathrm{kN}$
- stable for every tensile load
- in neutral equilibrium at $987\,\mathrm{kN}$
- unstable only in the second mode

## Explanation

Under a tensile force $T$, the second-order moment changes sign and the deflection equation becomes $EIv''-Tv=0$. With the boundary conditions $v(0)=v(L)=0$, only the zero solution is possible because the general solution is a linear combination of exponentials. In the energy balance the force now contributes $+\dfrac{T}{2}\int_0^L(v')^2\,dx$, which adds to the bending strain energy. The straight configuration is thus **stable** for any tensile force. The value $987\,\mathrm{kN}$ applies only to compression.
