In the plane of bending, the member shown is fixed at $A$ and free at $B$. Its length is $2\,\mathrm{m}$. The radius of gyration for the plane of bending considered is $40\,\mathrm{mm}$. Calculate the slenderness ratio.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" forze="B,-0.55,0,P" quote="A,B,2 m,0.75"  titolo="Compression member and its flexural end restraints"}

- $100$
- $50$
- $200$
- $25$

## Explanation

The effective length of the cantilever is $4\,\mathrm{m}$. In millimeters, this is $\lambda=\dfrac{4\,000}{40}=100$.
