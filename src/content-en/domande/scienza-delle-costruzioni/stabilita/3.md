In the plane of bending, the member shown is fixed at $A$ and free at $B$. Its length is $3\,\mathrm{m}$. The flexural rigidity is $EI=144\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. The force at $B$ remains parallel to the undeformed axis. Calculate the first buckling load.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" forze="B,-0.66,0,P" quote="A,B,3 m,0.75"  titolo="Compression member and its flexural end restraints"}

- $16\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $4\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $8\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $64\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

For a fixed-free column, $L_{\rm eff}=2L$. Thus $P_{\rm cr}=\dfrac{144\pi^2}{6^2}\,\mathrm{kN}=4\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$.
