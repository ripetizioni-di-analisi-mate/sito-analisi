The column shown is pinned at both ends in the plane of bending. Its length is $4\,\mathrm{m}$. The flexural rigidity is $EI=320\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. Before compression, the initial offset has shape $\sin\!\left(\dfrac{\pi x}{L}\right)$ and midlength amplitude $2\,\mathrm{mm}$. The compressive force is $15\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$. Calculate the total deflection at midlength measured from the straight axis.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="B,-0.88,0,P" quote="A,B,4 m,0.75"  titolo="Compression member and its flexural end restraints"}

- $6\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $4\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $2\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $8\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The critical load is $20\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$, so the applied force is three-quarters of the critical load. The total deflection is $\dfrac{2}{1-\dfrac{3}{4}}\,\mathrm{mm}=8\,\mathrm{mm}$.
