In the plane of bending, the member shown is fixed at $A$ and guided at $B$. Its length is $4\,\mathrm{m}$. The flexural rigidity is $EI=240\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. At $B$ the transverse displacement and rotation are restrained, while axial translation is free. Calculate the first buckling load.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,doppio-pendolo,270" forze="B,-0.88,0,P" quote="A,B,4 m,0.75"  titolo="Compression member and its flexural end restraints"}

- $15\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $60\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $30\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $120\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The guide restrains both lateral displacement and rotation. In the plane of bending, both ends are effectively fixed, so $L_{\rm eff}=\dfrac L2$.
