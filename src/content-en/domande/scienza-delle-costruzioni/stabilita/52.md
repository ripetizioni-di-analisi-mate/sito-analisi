A cantilever carries a compressive tip load that remains tangent to the deflected centerline at the tip. This is a follower force. How should the critical load be evaluated using Euler’s formula and $K=2$?

- The formula with $K=2$ does not apply because the free-end boundary condition changes
- It applies with $K=2$ because the support conditions are those of a cantilever
- It applies with $K=1$ because the force follows the tip rotation
- It applies with $K=\dfrac12$ because the force has no moment arm

## Explanation

The value $K=2$ follows for a fixed-direction force. In the deflected configuration it has a transverse component $Pv'(L)$ and gives the effective shear condition $EIv'''(L)+Pv'(L)=0$. A force tangent to the deflected centerline has no component normal to that line, so the free-end boundary condition changes. The direction of the load depends on the deflected shape, making the load nonconservative. Thus the Euler cantilever boundary conditions do not carry over, and the formula with $K=2$ **does not apply**.
