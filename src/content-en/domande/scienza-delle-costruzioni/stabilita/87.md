A pin-ended column of length $L$ and constant flexural rigidity $EI$ is continuous over a rigid lateral brace a distance $\dfrac L3$ from one end. The brace permits free rotation. In which interval does the first buckling load lie when expressed as a multiple of $\dfrac{\pi^2EI}{L^2}$?

- Between $1$ and $2.25$
- Between $4.60$ and $9$
- Beyond $9$
- Between $2.25$ and $4.60$

## Explanation

The segment of length $\dfrac{2L}{3}$ is the weaker one. If it were pinned at the brace, its buckling load would be $\dfrac{\pi^2EI}{(2L/3)^2}=2.25\,\dfrac{\pi^2EI}{L^2}$. The shorter, stiffer segment restrains rotation at the brace, so the actual buckling load is higher. The shorter segment provides only partial rotational restraint. The buckling load is therefore below the fixed-end value $\dfrac{\pi^2EI}{(0.699\cdot2L/3)^2}\simeq4.60\,\dfrac{\pi^2EI}{L^2}$. The exact solution gives approximately $3.4\,\dfrac{\pi^2EI}{L^2}$, within this interval.
