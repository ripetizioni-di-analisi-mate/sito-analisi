A column of length $3\,\mathrm m$ with $EI=720\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ is pinned at $A$. At end $B$, where the compressive force $P$ is applied, a support prevents cross-sectional rotation but allows both lateral and axial displacement. Calculate the lowest buckling load in the plane of bending.

- $80\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $320\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $5\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $20\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

At $A$, the conditions $v=0$ and $EIv''=0$ reduce the solution of $EIv''+Pv=0$ to $v=C\sin(kx)$. Rotation at $B$ is restrained, so $v'(L)=Ck\cos(kL)=0$. As for the cantilever, the first positive root is $kL=\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, giving $L_{\mathrm{eff}}=2L=6\,\mathrm m$. It follows that $P_{\mathrm{cr}}=\dfrac{\pi^2\cdot720}{36}\,\mathrm{kN}=20\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$. The deflected shape is a quarter sine wave with a horizontal tangent at $B$. Reflection about that end gives a pin-ended half-wave of length $2L$.
