The column shown is pinned at $A$ and $B$, has length $8\,\mathrm m$, and has flexural rigidity $EI=400\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. At midspan $C$, an ideal brace prevents lateral displacement and allows free rotation. The member remains continuous through $C$. Calculate the first buckling load.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,0;B,8,0" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello;B,carrello" forze="B,-1.4,0,P" quote="A,C,4 m,0.8;C,B,4 m,0.8" titolo="Compression member with a lateral brace at midspan"}

- $25\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $6.25\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $51.1\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $100\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The brace imposes $v=0$ at midspan. The first mode of the unbraced column, with maximum deflection at $C$, is therefore inadmissible. The second mode $\sin\!\left(\dfrac{2\pi x}{L}\right)$ has a node and zero bending moment at $C$, so it satisfies both the restraint and the continuity conditions. It follows that $P_{\mathrm{cr}}=\dfrac{4\pi^2EI}{L^2}=\dfrac{4\pi^2\cdot400}{64}\,\mathrm{kN}=25\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$. Each half behaves as a pin-ended column of length $4\,\mathrm m$. In the antisymmetric mode, section $C$ rotates freely, so a fixed end at $C$ would be inappropriate.
