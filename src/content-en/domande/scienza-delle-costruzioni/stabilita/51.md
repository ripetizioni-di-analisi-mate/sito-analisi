A column of length $L$ and constant flexural rigidity is fixed at $x=0$ and pinned at $x=L$. It carries a compressive force that maintains its direction. Given $k=\sqrt{\dfrac{P}{EI}}$, which equation determines the critical loads?

- $\sin(kL)=0$
- $\cos(kL)=0$
- $2\big(1-\cos(kL)\big)-kL\sin(kL)=0$
- $\tan(kL)=kL$

## Explanation

The general solution of $EIv''''+Pv''=0$ is $v=A+Bx+C\cos(kx)+D\sin(kx)$. At the fixed end, $v(0)=0$ and $v'(0)=0$ give $A=-C$ and $B=-kD$. At the pin, $v(L)=0$ and $v''(L)=0$ hold, and the second condition gives $C\cos(kL)+D\sin(kL)=0$. Substituting into the first condition and taking a nonzero amplitude gives $\sin(kL)-kL\cos(kL)=0$, or $\tan(kL)=kL$. The equation $\sin(kL)=0$ applies to a pin-ended column, $\cos(kL)=0$ to a cantilever, and the last equation to a fixed-fixed column.
