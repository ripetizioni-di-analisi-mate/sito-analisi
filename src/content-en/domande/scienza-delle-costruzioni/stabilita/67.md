A compression member is a thin-walled square tube with side length $a=120\,\mathrm{mm}$ measured along the wall centerline and wall thickness $t=4\,\mathrm{mm}$. Terms in $t^3$ are neglected. The effective length is $3.6\,\mathrm m$. Calculate the slenderness ratio.

- $103.9$
- $60.0$
- $42.4$
- $73.5$

## Explanation

The wall area is $A=4at$. About an axis parallel to two sides, the two parallel walls contribute $2\,at\left(\dfrac a2\right)^2$ and the two perpendicular walls contribute $2\,\dfrac{ta^3}{12}$, giving $I=\dfrac23ta^3$. It follows that $i=\sqrt{\dfrac IA}=\dfrac{a}{\sqrt6}\simeq49.0\,\mathrm{mm}$. Therefore, $\lambda=\dfrac{3600}{49.0}\simeq73.5$. A solid square of the same side length would have $i=\dfrac{a}{\sqrt{12}}$ and hence a larger slenderness ratio.
