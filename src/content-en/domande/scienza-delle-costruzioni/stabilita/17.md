One column in the model carries the uniform load on a tributary area of $12\,\mathrm{m^2}$. The dead load is $5\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$ and the ideal buckling load is $360\,\mathrm{kN}$. What is the largest variable portion of the load for which the ratio $\dfrac{P_{\mathrm{cr}}}{P}$ is at least $2.0$?

- $25.0\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$
- $5.0\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$
- $10.0\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$
- $15.0\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$

## Explanation

At the specified limit, the total load is $\dfrac{360}{2}=180\,\mathrm{kN}$. Dividing by $12\,\mathrm{m^2}$ gives a total load per unit area of $15\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$. Subtracting the dead load of $5\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$ leaves a live load of $10\,\mathrm{kN/m^2}$.
