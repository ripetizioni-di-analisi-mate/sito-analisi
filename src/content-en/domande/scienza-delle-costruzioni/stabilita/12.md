A pin-ended column is surveyed before and after centred compression is applied. The measured deflection increments at $\dfrac{L}{4}$ and $\dfrac{3L}{4}$ are $+2$ and $-2\,\mathrm{mm}$, respectively. If only the first two sinusoidal modes are present, what deflection increment is measured at midspan?

- $0\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $+2\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $2\sqrt2\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The first mode has equal values at the quarter points, whereas the second has opposite values. Adding the two increments isolates the first-mode contribution and shows that it is zero. Only the second mode remains, and its sinusoid has a node at midspan.
