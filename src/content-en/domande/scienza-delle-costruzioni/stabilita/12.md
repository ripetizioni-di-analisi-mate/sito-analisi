The column shown is pinned at both ends in the plane of bending. Its length is $4\,\mathrm{m}$. The flexural rigidity is $EI=480\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. The initial offset follows the first mode with amplitude $3\,\mathrm{mm}$. The compressive force is $20\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$. Calculate only the additional deflection due to the force.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="B,-0.88,0,P" quote="A,B,4 m,0.75"  titolo="Compression member and its flexural end restraints"}

- $6\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $9\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $3\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $12\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The critical load is $30\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$. The applied load ratio is $\dfrac23$, giving a total deflection of $9\,\mathrm{mm}$. Subtracting the initial deflection gives $6\,\mathrm{mm}$.
