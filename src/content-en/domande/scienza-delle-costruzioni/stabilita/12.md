The column shown is pinned at both ends in the plane of bending. Its length is $4\,\mathrm{m}$. The flexural rigidity is $EI=480\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. The initial offset follows the first mode with amplitude $3\,\mathrm{mm}$. The compressive force is $20\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$. What is the additional deflection due to the force alone?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="B,-0.88,0,P" quote="A,B,4 m,0.75"  titolo="Compression member and its flexural end restraints"}

- $6\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $9\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $3\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $12\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The first critical load of the column is $P_{\mathrm{cr}}=\dfrac{\pi^2EI}{L^2}=\dfrac{480\pi^2}{16}\,\mathrm{kN}=30\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$.

The ratio of the applied force to the critical load is therefore $\dfrac{20\pi^2}{30\pi^2}=\dfrac{2}{3}$.

Since the imperfection has the shape of the first mode, the total midspan deflection is the initial amplitude multiplied by $\dfrac{1}{1-\dfrac{2}{3}}=3$, that is $9\,\mathrm{mm}$.

The additional deflection is the difference between the total deflection and the initial imperfection, so it equals $9-3=6\,\mathrm{mm}$.
