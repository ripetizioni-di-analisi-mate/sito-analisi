A vertical rigid bar of length $L=2.5\,\mathrm m$ has a pin at its base fitted with a rotational spring of stiffness $k_\theta=150\,\mathrm{kN\,m/rad}$. A vertical compressive force $P$ acts at the top and maintains its direction as the bar rotates. Calculate the critical load.

- $60\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $375\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $24\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $120\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Consider a small rotation $\theta$ of the bar about the pin. The top moves laterally by $L\theta$, giving the force $P$ a moment arm $L\theta$ about the pin and an overturning moment $PL\theta$. The spring supplies the restoring moment $k_\theta\theta$. For $\theta\neq0$, the tilted bar is in equilibrium when the two moments balance, which occurs at $P=\dfrac{k_\theta}{L}$. The critical load is therefore $\dfrac{150}{2.5}\,\mathrm{kN}=60\,\mathrm{kN}$.
