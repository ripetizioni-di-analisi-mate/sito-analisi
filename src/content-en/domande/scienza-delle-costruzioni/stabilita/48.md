The column shown has length $6\,\mathrm m$ and constant $EI=720\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. It is fixed at its base $A$ and free at its top $B$. At the midheight section $C$, a cantilevered floor transfers a vertical compressive force $P$ of fixed direction to the column axis. Segment $CB$ is unloaded. Calculate the critical load.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,0,3;B,0,6" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,incastro" forze="C,0,-1,P" quote="A,C,3 m,-0.7;A,B,6 m,-1.5" riferimento=0 titolo="Cantilever column subjected to a compressive load at midheight"}

- $5\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $80\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $20\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $164\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Segment $CB$ carries neither axial force nor transverse load, so its bending moment is zero throughout. The upper segment therefore remains straight and imposes no restraint on section $C$, which can translate and rotate. The compressed segment $AC$ thus behaves as a cantilever of length $a=3\,\mathrm m$ with $L_{\mathrm{eff}}=2a=6\,\mathrm m$. It follows that $P_{\mathrm{cr}}=\dfrac{\pi^2\cdot720}{36}\,\mathrm{kN}=20\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$. The value $5\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$ would follow from using the full column length.
