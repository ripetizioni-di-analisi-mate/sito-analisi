A material has $E=210\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$ and a compressive proportional limit of $240\,\mathrm{MPa}$. In the ideal model, what slenderness ratio separates columns whose Euler critical stress lies within the linear range from those whose critical stress exceeds it? Round to one decimal place.

- $29.6$
- $291.9$
- $92.9$
- $46.5$

## Explanation

Setting $\dfrac{\pi^2E}{\lambda^2}=240\,\mathrm{MPa}$ gives $\lambda=\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{E}{240}}=92.9$. At higher slenderness ratios, the predicted buckling stress is below the proportional limit.
