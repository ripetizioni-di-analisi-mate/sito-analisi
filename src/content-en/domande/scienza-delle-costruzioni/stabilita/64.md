A compression member is a square tube of uniform wall thickness, pinned at both ends for bending in every plane. In Euler’s model, in which direction does the section deflect at the first buckling load?

- In any direction because every centroidal axis is principal and has the same second moment of area
- Along a diagonal because the second moment about the diagonal is smaller
- Parallel to a side because the second moment about a diagonal is larger
- Along a diagonal because the second moment about an axis parallel to the sides is smaller

## Explanation

The section has two perpendicular axes of symmetry with equal area moments of inertia. The product of inertia is zero and the two principal moments are equal. When the principal moments are equal, Mohr’s circle for area moments of inertia reduces to a point. Every centroidal axis is therefore principal and has the same area moment of inertia, including both diagonals. With the same end conditions in every plane, the critical load is the same **in any direction**. Small imperfections determine the actual buckling direction.
