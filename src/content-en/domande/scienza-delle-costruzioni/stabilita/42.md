A column of height $4.8\,\mathrm m$ and constant $EI=576\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ has two inflection points a distance $2.4\,\mathrm m$ apart in its buckled shape. Between the inflection points the deflected shape is a sinusoidal half-wave. Calculate the critical load.

- $25\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $400\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $100\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $51.1\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

At the inflection points the curvature is zero, so the bending moment is also zero. The segment between two inflection points therefore behaves as a pin-ended column buckling in a half-wave. The effective length equals the distance between inflection points, $L_{\mathrm{eff}}=2.4\,\mathrm m$, regardless of the column’s end conditions. It follows that $P_{\mathrm{cr}}=\dfrac{\pi^2\cdot576}{2.4^2}\,\mathrm{kN}=100\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$.
