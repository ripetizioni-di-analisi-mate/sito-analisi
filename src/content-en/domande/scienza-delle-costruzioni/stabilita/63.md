A model of a pin-ended column of length $L$ expresses lateral displacement as a sum of the modes $\sin(\dfrac{n\pi x}{L})$ for $n=1,2,3$. A point brace is added at $x=\dfrac{L}{3}$ that prevents lateral translation and allows rotation. Which individual mode already satisfies the added constraint?

- Only $n=1$.
- Only $n=2$.
- All three.
- Only $n=3$.

## Explanation

At the brace the sinusoid equals $\sin(\dfrac{n\pi}{3})$. Its value is $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$ for $n=1$ and $n=2$, and $\sin\pi=0$ for $n=3$. The derivative can be nonzero there because the brace permits rotation.
