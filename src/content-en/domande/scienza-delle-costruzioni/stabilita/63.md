A steel ruler of length $300\,\mathrm{mm}$ has cross section $30\,\mathrm{mm}\times1\,\mathrm{mm}$ and elastic modulus $E=200\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Its ends are compressed between the fingers and can be modeled as pins for bending in either plane. Calculate the critical load.

- $49.3\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $13.7\,\mathrm N$
- $219\,\mathrm N$
- $54.8\,\mathrm N$

## Explanation

The minimum area moment of inertia is about the axis parallel to the $30\,\mathrm{mm}$ side, giving $I_{\min}=\dfrac{30\cdot1^3}{12}\,\mathrm{mm^4}=2.5\,\mathrm{mm^4}$. With pinned ends in both planes, this smaller moment of inertia governs and $L_{\mathrm{eff}}=300\,\mathrm{mm}$. It follows that $P_{\mathrm{cr}}=\dfrac{\pi^2\cdot200\,000\cdot2.5}{300^2}\,\mathrm N\simeq54.8\,\mathrm N$. The value $49.3\,\mathrm{kN}$ would follow from using the moment of inertia about the other axis, which is $900$ times as large.
