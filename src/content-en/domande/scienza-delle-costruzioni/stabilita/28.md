A rectangular column with cross-sectional dimensions $50\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $150\,\mathrm{mm}$ can be rotated through $90^\circ$. The effective lengths for buckling about $y$ and $z$ are $3\,000\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $2\,000\,\mathrm{mm}$, respectively. Initially $I_y$ is the smaller area moment of inertia. What is the ratio of the lowest buckling load after rotation to its original value?

- $4.50$
- $2.25$
- $0.44$
- $1.00$

## Explanation

The larger area moment of inertia is $9.00$ times the smaller one. Before rotation, $\dfrac{I_{\min}}{(3\,000)^2}$ governs. After rotation, compare $\dfrac{I_{\max}}{(3\,000)^2}$ and $\dfrac{I_{\min}}{(2\,000)^2}$. Dividing the smaller of these two values by the original critical load gives the factor $2.25$.
