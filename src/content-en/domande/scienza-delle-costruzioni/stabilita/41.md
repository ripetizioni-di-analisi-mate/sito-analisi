Four columns have the same length $L$ and flexural rigidity $EI$. Each carries a compressive force that maintains its direction. Case (a) has a fixed end and a free end. (b) pinned–pinned, (c) fixed–pinned, In case (d), both ends restrain rotation and one end is free to slide axially. Rank the lowest critical loads from smallest to largest.

- (b), (a), (c), (d)
- (a), (c), (b), (d)
- (a), (b), (d), (c)
- (a), (b), (c), (d)

## Explanation

The critical load is $\dfrac{\pi^2EI}{(KL)^2}$, so for given $EI$ and $L$ a smaller factor $K$ gives a higher buckling load. The effective length factors for the four cases are $2$ for a cantilever, $1$ for two pinned ends, approximately $0.699$ for a fixed and a pinned end, and $\dfrac12$ for two fixed ends. In order of decreasing $K$, the cases are (a), (b), (c), and (d). The buckling loads therefore increase in the order **(a), (b), (c), (d)**. Their ratios to the pin-ended value are $\dfrac14$, $1$, approximately $2.05$, and $4$.
