A solid square column has an effective length $2\,400\,\mathrm{mm}$ for buckling about its weakest axis. The slenderness ratio must be at most $120$. What is the minimum side length? Round to one decimal place.

- $20.0\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $69.3\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $138.6\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $34.6\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The minimum radius of gyration is the side length divided by $\sqrt{12}$. Condition $\lambda\leq 120$ requires $i\geq\dfrac{2\,400}{120}\,\mathrm{mm}$. Multiplying by $\sqrt{12}$ gives approximately $69.3\,\mathrm{mm}$.
