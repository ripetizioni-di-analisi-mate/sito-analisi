A cantilever of length $3\,\mathrm m$ with $EI=360\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ carries its critical load at the tip, applied in a fixed direction. In the first buckling mode the lateral tip displacement is $20\,\mathrm{mm}$. What is the magnitude of the bending moment at the fixed end?

- $0.99\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $3.95\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $1.97\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $1.40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The cantilever buckling load is $P_{\mathrm{cr}}=\dfrac{\pi^2EI}{4L^2}=\dfrac{\pi^2\cdot360}{36}\,\mathrm{kN}\simeq98.7\,\mathrm{kN}$. The force remains parallel to the undeformed axis, so its moment arm about the fixed end equals the lateral tip displacement. No transverse loads act on the cantilever, so the fixed-end moment is $M=P_{\mathrm{cr}}\delta$. This gives $M=98.7\cdot0.020\,\mathrm{kN\,m}\simeq1.97\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
