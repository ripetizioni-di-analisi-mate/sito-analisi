The column shown is pinned at both ends in the plane of bending. Its length is $4\,\mathrm{m}$. The flexural rigidity is $320\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ on half $AC$ and $640\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ on half $CB$. Calculate the Rayleigh estimate using $\varphi(x)=\sin\!\left(\dfrac{\pi x}{L}\right)$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,2,0" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="B,-0.88,0,P" quote="A,C,2 m,0.75;C,B,2 m,0.75" etichette="1,0.32,EI_1;3,0.32,EI_2" titolo="Compression member and its flexural end restraints"}

- $20\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $30\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $40\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $60\pi^2\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The integral of the square of the assumed curvature is the same over each half, so the effective flexural rigidity in the numerator is the mean $480\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. The ratio is $\dfrac{480\pi^2}{16}$.
