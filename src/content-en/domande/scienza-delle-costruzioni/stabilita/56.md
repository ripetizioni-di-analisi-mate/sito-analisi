A column of length $5\,\mathrm m$ is fixed at its base. Its top is laterally restrained and connected to a rotational spring. The first positive root of the buckling equation is $kL=5.24$, with $k=\sqrt{\dfrac{P}{EI}}$. What is the effective length?

- $3.00\,\mathrm m$
- $8.34\,\mathrm m$
- $1.80\,\mathrm m$
- $0.95\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The root gives $P_{\mathrm{cr}}=\dfrac{(kL)^2EI}{L^2}$. Equating this expression to $\dfrac{\pi^2EI}{L_{\mathrm{eff}}^2}$ gives $L_{\mathrm{eff}}=\dfrac{\pi}{kL}L$. Given $kL=5.24$ and $L=5\,\mathrm m$, the result is $L_{\mathrm{eff}}=\dfrac{\pi}{5.24}\cdot5\,\mathrm m\simeq3.00\,\mathrm m$. The factor $K\simeq0.60$ lies between the fixed-fixed value $\dfrac12$ and the fixed-pinned value $0.699$ because the spring only partially restrains rotation at the top.
