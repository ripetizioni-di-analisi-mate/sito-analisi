A cantilever of length $L$ is fixed at $x=0$ and carries at $x=L$ a compressive force $P$ that maintains its direction. The lateral displacement $v$ satisfies $EIv''''+Pv''=0$. Which set of boundary conditions is correct?

- $v(0)=0,\ v'(0)=0,\ v''(L)=0,\ v'''(L)=0$
- $v(0)=0,\ v'(0)=0,\ v''(L)=0,\ EIv'''(L)+Pv'(L)=0$
- $v(0)=0,\ v'(0)=0,\ v(L)=0,\ v''(L)=0$
- $v(0)=0,\ v''(0)=0,\ v''(L)=0,\ EIv'''(L)+Pv'(L)=0$

## Explanation

At the fixed end, displacement and rotation are restrained, so $v(0)=0$ and $v'(0)=0$. The free-end bending moment is zero, giving $v''(L)=0$. The applied transverse force at the free end is also zero. In the deflected configuration, however, the fixed-direction load $P$ has a component normal to the member axis proportional to $Pv'$. This gives the effective shear condition $EIv'''(L)+Pv'(L)=0$. The condition $v'''(L)=0$ omits this term, even though it is of the same order as the others.
