A column of length $6\,\mathrm m$ and constant $EI$ is fixed against rotation at both ends and free to slide axially at one end. For the first buckling mode, at what distances from one end are the inflection points of the deflected shape?

- $2\,\mathrm m$ and $4\,\mathrm m$
- Only at $3\,\mathrm m$
- $0.9\,\mathrm m$ and $5.1\,\mathrm m$
- $1.5\,\mathrm m$ and $4.5\,\mathrm m$

## Explanation

The first mode of a fixed-fixed column is $v(x)=C\left[1-\cos\!\left(\dfrac{2\pi x}{L}\right)\right]$. Its curvature is proportional to $\cos\!\left(\dfrac{2\pi x}{L}\right)$, which vanishes at $\dfrac{2\pi x}{L}=\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ and $\dfrac{2\pi x}{L}=\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$. The inflection points are therefore at $x=\dfrac L4=1.5\,\mathrm m$ and $x=\dfrac{3L}{4}=4.5\,\mathrm m$. They are a distance $\dfrac L2$ apart, equal to the effective length for these end conditions.
