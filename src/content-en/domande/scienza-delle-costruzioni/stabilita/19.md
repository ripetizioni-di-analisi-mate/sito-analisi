A vertical rigid bar of length $3\,\mathrm m$ is pinned at its base. At midheight a horizontal spring of stiffness $k=80\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ connects the bar to a fixed support. The spring is unstressed when the bar is vertical. A vertical compressive force $P$ acts at the top and maintains its direction as the bar rotates. Calculate the critical load.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera" forze="B,0,-1,P" linee="0,1.5 0.2,1.5 0.3,1.63 0.45,1.37 0.6,1.63 0.75,1.37 0.85,1.5 1.05,1.5;1.05,1.22 1.05,1.78" etichette="0.55,1.95,k" quote="A,B,3 m,-1.3;A,0 1.5,1.5 m,-0.6" riferimento=0 titolo="Rigid bar with a horizontal spring at midheight"}

- $30\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $120\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $240\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $60\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

For a small rotation $\theta$ about the base, the spring extends by $a\theta$ with $a=1.5\,\mathrm m$. It exerts a force $ka\theta$ with a moment arm $a$ about the pin. The restoring moment is therefore $ka^2\theta$. The top moves laterally by $L\theta$, giving $P$ an overturning moment of $PL\theta$. Equating the moments for $\theta\neq0$ gives $P_{\mathrm{cr}}=\dfrac{ka^2}{L}=\dfrac{80\cdot2.25}{3}\,\mathrm{kN}=60\,\mathrm{kN}$.
