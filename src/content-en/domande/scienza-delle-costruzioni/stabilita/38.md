In the plane of bending, the column shown is fixed at $A$ and pinned at $B$. The load can move axially at $B$. The length is $5\,\mathrm m$ and the flexural rigidity is $EI=1000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. Calculate the lowest buckling load, given that the first positive root of $\tan u=u$ is $u\simeq4.493$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" forze="B,-1.1,0,P" quote="A,B,5 m,0.8" titolo="Compression member fixed at A and pinned at B"}

- $808\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $395\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $98.7\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $1579\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

For a fixed-pinned column, the buckling condition is $\tan(kL)=kL$ with $k=\sqrt{\dfrac{P}{EI}}$. The first positive root gives $kL\simeq4.493$ and hence $P_{\mathrm{cr}}=\dfrac{(4.493)^2EI}{L^2}\simeq\dfrac{20.19\cdot1000}{25}\,\mathrm{kN}$. This gives $P_{\mathrm{cr}}\simeq808\,\mathrm{kN}$. This corresponds to an effective length $L_{\mathrm{eff}}=\dfrac{\pi}{4.493}L\simeq0.699L$, between those for a pin-ended column and a fixed-fixed column.
