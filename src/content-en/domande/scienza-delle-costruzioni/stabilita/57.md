A tapered column of variable flexural rigidity $EI(x)$ carries a constant compressive force $P$. Which differential equation governs the transverse displacement $v(x)$ at buckling, regardless of the end restraints?

- $EI(x)\,v''''+Pv''=0$
- $\big(EI(x)\,v''\big)''-Pv''=0$
- $EI(x)\,v''''+Pv=0$
- $\big(EI(x)\,v''\big)''+Pv''=0$

## Explanation

In the energy balance, integrate the bending term by parts twice and the load term once. The coefficient of the virtual displacement in the resulting integrand is $\big(EIv''\big)''+Pv''$. The variation is arbitrary in the interior of the column, so this coefficient must vanish. When $EI$ varies, the second derivative of the product $EIv''$ also includes the terms $2(EI)'v'''+(EI)''v''$. Thus the equation $EI(x)v''''+Pv''=0$ requires constant rigidity. The general equation is $\big(EI(x)v''\big)''+Pv''=0$.
