A straight column is axially restrained at both ends and pinned against lateral displacement. The slenderness ratio is $130$ and $\alpha=12\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{K^{-1}}$. In the linear elastic range, what uniform temperature increase would produce the Euler critical compressive force? Round to one decimal place.

- $97.3\,\mathrm{K}$
- $24.3\,\mathrm{K}$
- $12.2\,\mathrm{K}$
- $48.7\,\mathrm{K}$

## Explanation

The axial restraint imposes $\varepsilon_{\text{mechanical}}=-\alpha\Delta T$ and the resulting compressive stress has magnitude $E\alpha\Delta T$. Equating this stress to $\dfrac{\pi^2E}{\lambda^2}$ cancels $E$ and gives $\Delta T=\dfrac{\pi^2}{\lambda^2\alpha}\approx 48.7\,\mathrm{K}$.
