A cantilever of length $2\,\mathrm m$ with $EI=400\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ is fixed at $x=0$ and carries a compressive tip force of fixed direction. Estimate the critical load using the Rayleigh quotient and the trial function $\varphi(x)=x^2$. What value does this give?

- $300\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $246.7\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $100\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $600\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The function $\varphi=x^2$ satisfies the kinematic boundary conditions $\varphi(0)=0$ and $\varphi'(0)=0$ at the fixed end, so it is admissible. The numerator is $\int_0^L EI\cdot2^2\,dx=4EIL$ and the denominator is $\int_0^L(2x)^2\,dx=\dfrac{4L^3}{3}$. The quotient is therefore $\dfrac{3EI}{L^2}=\dfrac{3\cdot400}{4}\,\mathrm{kN}=300\,\mathrm{kN}$. The exact value $\dfrac{\pi^2EI}{4L^2}\simeq246.7\,\mathrm{kN}$ is lower, as the upper-bound theorem guarantees.
