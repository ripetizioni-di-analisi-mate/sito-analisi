The column shown has a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$, and an internal hinge at midspan $C$ with no lateral restraint. The flexural rigidity $EI$ is constant and the length is $L$. What is the critical load?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,6,0" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" cerniere="C" forze="B,-1.2,0,P" quote="A,B,L,0.8" titolo="Compression member with an internal hinge at midspan"}

- It is zero because the structure is unstable
- $\dfrac{\pi^2EI}{L^2}$
- $\dfrac{4\pi^2EI}{L^2}$
- $\dfrac{\pi^2EI}{4L^2}$

## Explanation

The two segments $AC$ and $CB$ can rotate in opposite directions as rigid bodies while $C$ moves laterally, without either segment bending. This mechanism requires no strain energy, so the column is **unstable** in the plane of bending. Under a compressive force $P>0$, displacement of $C$ produces an overturning moment with no restoring stiffness. The straight configuration is therefore unstable under any positive compressive load, and the critical load is zero.
