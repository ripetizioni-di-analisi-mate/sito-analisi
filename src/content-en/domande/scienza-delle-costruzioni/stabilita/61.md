A compression member with an equal-leg angle section is pinned at both ends for bending in any plane. About which centroidal axis does the cross section bend at the first buckling load?

- About a centroidal axis parallel to a side
- About the principal axis with the largest second moment of area
- About any axis because the second moments about the two sides are equal
- About the principal axis with the smallest second moment of area, inclined at $45^\circ$ to the sides

## Explanation

The centroidal axes parallel to the legs have equal area moments of inertia, but the product of inertia about them is nonzero. These are therefore not principal axes. Because the angle section is symmetric, the principal axes are inclined at $45^\circ$. The symmetry axis has the larger principal moment of inertia. The perpendicular axis has the smaller one. With the same end conditions in every plane, the smaller principal moment of inertia governs, so the section bends **about the principal axis with the smaller area moment of inertia**.
