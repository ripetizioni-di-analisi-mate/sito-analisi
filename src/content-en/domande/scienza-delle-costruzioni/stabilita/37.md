Two identical wood boards of cross section $100\,\mathrm{mm}\times20\,\mathrm{mm}$ are stacked with their $100\,\mathrm{mm}$ sides in contact. There are no fasteners, so the boards can slide freely against each other. They form a pin-ended column of length $2\,\mathrm m$ with $E=10\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$. The column deflects in the plane perpendicular to the faces of the boards. Calculate the critical load.

::struttura{poligoni="0,0 5,0 5,1 0,1;0,1.05 5,1.05 5,2.05 0,2.05" quote="0 0,5 0,100 mm,0.5;5 0,5 1,20 mm,0.5;5 1.05,5 2.05,20 mm,0.5" riferimento=0 titolo="Cross section of two stacked planks without a connection between them"}

- $13.2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $3.29\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $1.64\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $82.2\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Because the boards can slide, each bends about its own centroidal axis, and their flexural rigidities add. Each board has $I=\dfrac{100\cdot20^3}{12}\,\mathrm{mm^4}\simeq66\,667\,\mathrm{mm^4}$, so the pair has $I=133\,333\,\mathrm{mm^4}$. The critical load is $P_{\mathrm{cr}}=\dfrac{\pi^2\cdot10\,000\cdot133\,333}{2000^2}\,\mathrm N\simeq3290\,\mathrm N$, or approximately $3.29\,\mathrm{kN}$. If the boards were glued together, the composite section of depth $40\,\mathrm{mm}$ would have four times the area moment of inertia of the unconnected pair.
