A compression diagonal in a truss has length $2.5\,\mathrm m$ and is pin-connected at both ends for bending in any plane. It is a solid round steel bar of diameter $30\,\mathrm{mm}$ with $E=210\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Calculate the Euler buckling load.

- $26.4\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $13.2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $52.7\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $22.4\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

For a solid circular section, the area moment of inertia is $I=\dfrac{\pi d^4}{64}=\dfrac{\pi\cdot30^4}{64}\,\mathrm{mm^4}\simeq39\,760\,\mathrm{mm^4}$ about any diameter. The pin joints give $L_{\mathrm{eff}}=L=2500\,\mathrm{mm}$. In newtons and millimeters, $P_{\mathrm{cr}}=\dfrac{\pi^2\cdot210\,000\cdot39\,760}{2500^2}\,\mathrm N\simeq13\,185\,\mathrm N$. The buckling load is therefore approximately $13.2\,\mathrm{kN}$. Using the polar moment of inertia $\dfrac{\pi d^4}{32}$, which applies to torsion, would give twice this value.
