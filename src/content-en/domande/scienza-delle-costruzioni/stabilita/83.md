A cantilever column of length $3\,\mathrm m$ with $EI=360\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ was built out of plumb. Its unloaded shape matches its first buckling mode, and its top is displaced laterally by $5\,\mathrm{mm}$. A compressive force of fixed direction acts at the top. Its magnitude is $0.6$ times the cantilever buckling load. What is the total displacement at the top?

- $7.5\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $8.3\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $12.5\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $5.9\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

If the initial imperfection has the shape of a buckling mode, the solution retains that shape and its amplitude is multiplied by the amplification factor $\dfrac{1}{1-\dfrac{P}{P_{\mathrm{cr}}}}$ for that mode. Here $P_{\mathrm{cr}}=\dfrac{\pi^2EI}{4L^2}\simeq98.7\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $\dfrac{P}{P_{\mathrm{cr}}}=0.6$. The total displacement at the top is therefore $\dfrac{5}{1-0.6}\,\mathrm{mm}=12.5\,\mathrm{mm}$. The additional displacement caused by the load is $7.5\,\mathrm{mm}$.
