For a column with a first-mode imperfection the total deflection follows $u(P)=\dfrac{u_0}{1-\dfrac{P}{P_{\rm cr}}}$. The measured deflections are $6\,\mathrm{mm}$ at $P=20\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $9\,\mathrm{mm}$ at $P=40\,\mathrm{kN}$. Which critical load is consistent with both readings?

- $60\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $100\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $80\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $120\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The unloaded imperfection is the same in both tests, so $6(1-\dfrac{20}{P_{\rm cr}})=9(1-\dfrac{40}{P_{\rm cr}})$. Multiplication by $P_{\rm cr}$ gives $6P_{\rm cr}-120=9P_{\rm cr}-360$. Hence $P_{\rm cr}=80\,\mathrm{kN}$, and either reading gives $u_0=4.5\,\mathrm{mm}$.
