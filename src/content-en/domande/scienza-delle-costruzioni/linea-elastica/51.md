A beam of span $L$ and constant flexural rigidity is fixed at both ends and carries a downward midspan force $P$. At which sections does the deflected shape have a point of inflection?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,incastro,0" forze="C,0,-1,P" quote="A,C,L/2;C,B,L/2" titolo="Fixed-fixed beam with a midspan force"}

- Only at midspan
- At distances $\dfrac L4$ and $\dfrac{3L}{4}$ from $A$
- At distances $\dfrac L3$ and $\dfrac{2L}{3}$ from $A$
- At no section, because the moment has the same sign throughout the beam

## Explanation

By symmetry both vertical reactions are $\dfrac P2$, and both fixed ends transmit the same moment $M_0$. Thus $M(x)=\dfrac{Px}{2}+M_0$ on the left half. The slope vanishes at $x=0$ because of the fixed end and at $x=\dfrac L2$ by symmetry. Since $EI\,v''=-M$, the integral of $M$ from $0$ to $\dfrac L2$ vanishes, giving $\dfrac{PL^2}{16}+\dfrac{M_0L}{2}=0$ and hence $M_0=-\dfrac{PL}{8}$. Moment $\dfrac{Px}{2}-\dfrac{PL}{8}$ vanishes and changes sign at $x=\dfrac L4$ and, by symmetry, at $x=\dfrac{3L}{4}$. The curvature changes sign at both sections, so the deflected shape has two inflection points. At midspan, the moment is $\dfrac{PL}{8}$ and retains its sign.
