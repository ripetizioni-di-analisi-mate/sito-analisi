The final segment of a cantilever is perfectly rigid and $2\,\mathrm{m}$ long. At its left end, the measured downward deflection is $3\,\mathrm{mm}$ and the clockwise rotation is $2\,\mathrm{mrad}$. For small displacements, what is the downward deflection of its right tip?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,5,0" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="C,B,2 m" etichette="4,0.5,EI → ∞" titolo="Rigid end segment and root clamp"}

- $3\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $7\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $4\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $1\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The rigid segment has zero curvature and retains the rotation of its starting section. Its tip moves $2\cdot0.002=0.004\,\mathrm{m}$ downward relative to that section. Including the initial translation gives $3+4=7\,\mathrm{mm}$.
