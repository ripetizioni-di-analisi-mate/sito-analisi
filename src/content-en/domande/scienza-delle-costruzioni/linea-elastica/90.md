Before loading, the centerline of a horizontal beam is at elevation $y=3.200\,\mathrm{m}$, with the $y$ axis pointing upward. Under load, the deflection at midspan is $v=12\,\mathrm{mm}$, with $v$ positive downward. What is the new elevation of the beam centerline at midspan?

- $3.212\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3.188\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3.080\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3.320\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The global $y$ axis points upward, whereas $v$ is positive downward. The displacement along $y$ is therefore $-v$. Since $12\,\mathrm{mm}=0.012\,\mathrm m$, the displacement along $y$ is $-0.012\,\mathrm m$. The new elevation is therefore $3.200-0.012=3.188\,\mathrm m$.
