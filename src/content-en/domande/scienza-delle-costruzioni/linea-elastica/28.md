The beam shown has a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$, and an overhang $BC$ of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$ carrying a downward load $5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Span $AB$ is unloaded and the flexural rigidity is $EI=5\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. What is $v''$ at support $B$, taking $v$ as positive downward?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;C,7,0" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="B,C,0.6,0.6,5 kN/m" quote="A,B,5 m;B,C,2 m" titolo="Beam with a loaded overhang"}

- $-2\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $4\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $2\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$

## Explanation

Isolate the overhang to the right of $B$. The load has resultant $5\cdot2=10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acting a distance $1\,\mathrm m$ from $B$. The moment at $B$ is therefore $M_B=-10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, negative because the top fibers are in tension. Since $EI\,v''=-M$, the result is $v''(B)=\dfrac{10}{5\,000}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}=2\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$. The roller enforces zero displacement at $B$. The moment at $B$ is nonzero because the beam extends beyond the support.
