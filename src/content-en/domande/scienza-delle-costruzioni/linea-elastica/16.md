A beam with a rectangular section of depth $400\,\mathrm{mm}$ has flexural rigidity $EI=20\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. At one section the bending moment is $M=40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. What is the longitudinal strain $\varepsilon_x$ in the bottom fiber?

- $4\cdot10^{-4}$, shortening
- $8\cdot10^{-4}$, elongation
- $4\cdot10^{-7}$, elongation
- $4\cdot10^{-4}$, elongation

## Explanation

The curvature has magnitude $\dfrac{M}{EI}=\dfrac{40}{20\,000}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}=2\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$. In the Euler–Bernoulli model, longitudinal strain varies linearly with distance from the neutral axis, which is at middepth in this rectangular section. The bottom fiber is a distance $0.2\,\mathrm{m}$ from the neutral axis, giving $|\varepsilon_x|=2\cdot10^{-3}\cdot0.2=4\cdot10^{-4}$. Since positive bending puts the bottom fibers in tension, the bottom fiber **elongates**.
