Simply supported beam $AB$ has span $6\,\mathrm{m}$ and $EI=27\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. Two downward forces of $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ each act a distance $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from their nearest supports. What is the magnitude of the midspan deflection?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,1,0;D,5,0;B,6,0" aste="A-C;C-D;D-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1,15 kN;D,0,-1,15 kN" quote="A,C,1 m;C,D,4 m;D,B,1 m"}

- $2.5\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $4.81\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $2.41\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $1.2\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

On the left half, the moment is $Px$ up to the first force and $Pa$ beyond it. A virtual unit load at midspan produces $m=\dfrac{x}{2}$ on the same half. By symmetry, the deflection is $\dfrac{P}{EI}$ times the sum of $\int_0^a x^2\,dx$ and $\int_a^{\dfrac{L}{2}} ax\,dx$, or $\dfrac{Pa(3L^2-4a^2)}{24EI}$. This gives $2.41\,\mathrm{mm}$.
