In the beam shown, segment $AC$ is fixed at $A$ and segment $CB$ rests on a roller at $B$. The segments meet at internal hinge $C$. A force acts at the midpoint of $CB$. Integrate $EI\,v''=-M$ separately on each segment to obtain $v_1$ on $AC$ and $v_2$ on $CB$. Which set of conditions determines the four constants of integration?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;D,5,0;B,7,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" forze="D,0,-0.9,P" quote="A,C,a;C,D,b/2;D,B,b/2" titolo="Beam with an internal hinge"}

- $v_1(A)=0$, $v_1'(A)=0$, $v_1'(C)=v_2'(C)$, $v_2(B)=0$
- $v_1(A)=0$, $v_1'(A)=0$, $v_1(C)=v_2(C)$, $v_1'(C)=v_2'(C)$
- $v_1(A)=0$, $v_1(C)=0$, $v_2(C)=0$, $v_2(B)=0$
- $v_1(A)=0$, $v_1'(A)=0$, $v_1(C)=v_2(C)$, $v_2(B)=0$

## Explanation

The fixed end requires $v_1(A)=0$ and $v_1'(A)=0$, and the roller requires $v_2(B)=0$. Both segments have the same displacement at the hinge, so $v_1(C)=v_2(C)$. The hinge permits relative rotation. The slopes of the two segments at $C$ may therefore differ and should not be set equal. There is no external support at $C$, so the hinge displacement is an unknown.
