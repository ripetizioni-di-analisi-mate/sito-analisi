A cantilever of span $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and flexural rigidity $EI=17\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ carries a uniform downward load of intensity $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over its full length. What is the downward deflection halfway along the cantilever?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.6,6 kN/m" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,2 m" titolo="Cantilever under a uniform load"}

- $5.65\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $11.29\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $1.18\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $4\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

With $x$ measured from the fixed end, the moment is $M=-\dfrac{q(L-x)^2}{2}$. Integrating $EI\,v''=-M$ twice with $v(0)=v'(0)=0$ gives
$$
EI\,v(x)=\frac{qx^2\left(6L^2-4Lx+x^2\right)}{24}.
$$
At $x=\dfrac L2$, the factor $6L^2-4Lx+x^2$ equals $\dfrac{17L^2}{4}$, so $v\left(\dfrac L2\right)=\dfrac{17qL^4}{384EI}=\dfrac{17\cdot6\cdot256}{384\cdot17\,000}\,\mathrm m=4\,\mathrm{mm}$. This is less than half the tip deflection $11.29\,\mathrm{mm}$ because the slope increases toward the tip.
