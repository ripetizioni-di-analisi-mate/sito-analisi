In the beam shown, segment $AC$ has length $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and is fixed at $A$. At $C$ an internal hinge connects it to segment $CB$ of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$, supported on a roller at $B$. Both segments have $EI=10\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. A downward force of $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at section $D$, the midpoint of $CB$. How far does $D$ deflect downward?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;D,3.5,0;B,5,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" forze="D,0,-1,20 kN" quote="A,C,2 m;C,D,1.5 m;D,B,1.5 m" titolo="Beam with an internal hinge and a force on the supported segment"}

- $1.13\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $3.79\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $2.46\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $5.33\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

Segment $CB$ has zero moment at the hinge and the roller, so it acts as a simply supported beam between $C$ and $B$. The force acts at midspan, so the hinge transmits a downward force of $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ to cantilever $AC$, lowering $C$ by $\dfrac{10\cdot2^3}{3\cdot10\,000}\,\mathrm m\simeq2.67\,\mathrm{mm}$. The displacement of $D$ has two contributions. The rigid-body motion of $CB$ varies from $2.67\,\mathrm{mm}$ at $C$ to zero at $B$, giving the mean value $1.33\,\mathrm{mm}$ at $D$. Bending of $CB$ as a simply supported beam contributes $\dfrac{20\cdot3^3}{48\cdot10\,000}\,\mathrm m\simeq1.13\,\mathrm{mm}$. Thus $D$ moves down by about $2.46\,\mathrm{mm}$.
