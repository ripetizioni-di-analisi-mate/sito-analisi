A continuous beam of constant flexural rigidity rests on supports $A$, $B$, and $C$, with interior support $B$, and carries a uniform load on both spans. Which statement about section $B$ is true?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,8,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;C,carrello" carichi="A,C,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,B,L;B,C,L" titolo="Continuous beam on three supports"}

- $v(B)=0$, the slope is continuous, and the moment is nonzero
- $v(B)=0$, the slope is continuous, and the moment is zero
- $v(B)=0$, the slope jumps, and the moment is zero
- $v(B)\neq0$, the slope is continuous, and the moment is nonzero

## Explanation

The support prevents vertical translation, so $v(B)=0$. The beam is continuous over the support, giving the two spans a common tangent at $B$. The continuous material transmits bending moment between spans. The loading on both spans puts the top fibers over the support in tension. The moment at $B$ is therefore negative and nonzero. A slope jump combined with zero moment would indicate an internal hinge.
