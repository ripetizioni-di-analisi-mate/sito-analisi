A tapered cantilever of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$ has flexural rigidity $EI(x)=8\,000\left(2-\dfrac x2\right)\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$, with $x$ measured in meters from the fixed end. A counterclockwise couple of $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ acts at the tip. What is the tip rotation, taking counterclockwise as positive?

- $2.08\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $-2.08\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $3\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $1.5\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{rad}$

## Explanation

The tip couple produces a constant positive bending moment $M=12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Since $v''=-\dfrac{M}{EI(x)}$ and $v'(0)=0$, the tip slope is
$$
v'(2)=-\int_0^2\frac{12}{8\,000\left(2-\frac s2\right)}\,\mathrm ds=-\frac{12}{8\,000}\cdot2\ln2\simeq-2.08\cdot10^{-3}.
$$
The integral equals $2\ln2$ because an antiderivative of $\dfrac{1}{2-\frac s2}$ is $-2\ln\left(2-\dfrac s2\right)$. Thus $\varphi_B=-v'(2)\simeq2.08\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{rad}$, counterclockwise. The result lies between the values for uniform rigidity equal to the fixed-end rigidity, $16\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$, and the tip rigidity, $8\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$.
