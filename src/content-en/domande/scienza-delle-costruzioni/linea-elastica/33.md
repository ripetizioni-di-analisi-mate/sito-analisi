A cantilever of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ has $EI=13\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. A uniform downward load of intensity $9\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts only over the final $1\,\mathrm{m}$ before the tip. What is the magnitude of the deflection at the free end?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,4,0" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" carichi="C,B,0.8,0.8,9 kN/m" quote="A,C,3 m;C,B,1 m"}

- $14.77\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $12.03\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.09\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $22.15\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

A differential force $q\,ds$ a distance $s$ from the fixed end contributes $\dfrac{q\,ds\cdot s^2(3L-s)}{6EI}$ to the tip deflection. Integrate over the loaded segment, from $L-b$ to $L$. An antiderivative of $s^2(3L-s)$ is $Ls^3-\dfrac{s^4}{4}$. This gives $12.03\,\mathrm{mm}$.
