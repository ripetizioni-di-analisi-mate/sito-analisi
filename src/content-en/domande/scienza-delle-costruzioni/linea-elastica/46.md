A cantilever of length $L$ fixed at $x=0$ has deflection $v(x)=cx^2$, with $c>0$ constant and $v$ positive downward. What is the rotation $\varphi$ of section $x=L$, taking counterclockwise as positive?

- $2cL$
- $-cL^2$
- $-cL$
- $-2cL$

## Explanation

For small rotations the cross-section rotation is $\varphi=-v'$. Differentiating gives $v'(x)=2cx$, so $\varphi(L)=-2cL$. The negative sign means clockwise rotation, consistent with the downward deflection increasing toward the tip. The quantity $cL^2$ is the tip displacement and has dimensions of length.
