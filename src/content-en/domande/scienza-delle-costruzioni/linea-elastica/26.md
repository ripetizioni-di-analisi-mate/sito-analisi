A simply supported beam carries a uniform downward load over its full span. Plot the function $v(x)$, defined positive downward, on a conventional graph with the ordinate axis pointing upward. Which description of the graph is correct?

- $v(x)\ge0$, concave downward
- $v(x)\le0$, concave upward
- $v(x)\ge0$, concave upward
- $v(x)\le0$, concave downward

## Explanation

The load lowers every interior section, so $v(x)\ge0$, with $v=0$ at the supports. The bending moment is positive at every interior section. Since $EI\,v''=-M$, we have $v''<0$, so the graph of $v$ is concave downward. In physical space, the beam centerline has elevation $-v$ and is concave upward.
