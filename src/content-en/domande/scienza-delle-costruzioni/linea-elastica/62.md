During a load test on a simply supported beam, a dial gauge fixed to the ground measures a downward midspan displacement of $7\,\mathrm{mm}$. Both supports settle by $2\,\mathrm{mm}$ during the test. What is the downward midspan deflection due to beam bending alone?

- $7\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $9\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $5\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $3\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The measured downward displacement is the sum of the rigid-body displacement due to settlement and the displacement due to bending. Both supports settle equally, so the beam undergoes the same rigid-body translation $2\,\mathrm{mm}$ at every section. Bending therefore contributes $7-2=5\,\mathrm{mm}$ downward at midspan.
