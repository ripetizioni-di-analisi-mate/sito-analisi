On a beam segment the bending moment varies linearly, and integrating $EI\,v''=-M$ gives $EI\,v(x)=a_3x^3+a_2x^2+a_1x+a_0$, with $EI$ in $\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ and $x$ and $v$ in meters. What are the units of coefficient $a_3$?

- $\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$
- $\mathrm{kN\,m^3}$
- $\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Product $EI\,v$ has units $\mathrm{kN\,m^2}\cdot\mathrm m=\mathrm{kN\,m^3}$, which each term in the polynomial must also have. The term $a_3x^3$ contains factor $x^3$ with units $\mathrm{m^3}$, so $a_3$ has units $\mathrm{kN}$. Likewise, $a_2$ has units $\mathrm{kN\,m}$, $a_1$ has units $\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$, and $a_0$ has units $\mathrm{kN\,m^3}$.
