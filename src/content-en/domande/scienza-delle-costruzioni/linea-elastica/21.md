A beam of span $5\,\mathrm{m}$ with $EI=24\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ has two simple supports. A uniform downward load of intensity $10\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts over the full span. What is the magnitude of the rotation at the left support?

- $1.09\,\mathrm{mrad}$
- $4.34\,\mathrm{mrad}$
- $8.68\,\mathrm{mrad}$
- $2.17\,\mathrm{mrad}$

## Explanation

The bending moment is $\dfrac{qx(L-x)}{2}$. Symmetry gives zero rotation at midspan, so the magnitude of the left-end rotation equals the area of the $\dfrac{M}{EI}$ diagram between $0$ and $\dfrac{L}{2}$. Integration gives $\dfrac{qL^3}{24EI}$. This gives $2.17\,\mathrm{mrad}$.
