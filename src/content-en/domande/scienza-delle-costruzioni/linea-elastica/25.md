A simply supported beam of span $4\,\mathrm{m}$ has $EI=10\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. A uniform downward load of intensity $8\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ covers only the left half of the span. What is the midspan deflection?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,C,0.6,0.6,8 kN/m" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,2 m" titolo="Uniform load on the left half only"}

- $1.33\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $2.67\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.67\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $5.33\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The full-span uniform load is the sum of the loads on the left and right halves. These two partial loads are mirror images about midspan and therefore produce equal midspan deflections. By superposition, each produces half the deflection under the full load.
$$
v\left(\frac L2\right)=\frac12\cdot\frac{5qL^4}{384EI}=\frac{5\cdot8\cdot256}{768\cdot10\,000}\,\mathrm m\simeq1.33\,\mathrm{mm}.
$$
