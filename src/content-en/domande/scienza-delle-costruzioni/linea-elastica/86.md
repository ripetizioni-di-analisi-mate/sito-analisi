A simply supported beam of constant flexural rigidity carries two point forces at $C$ and $D$. Integrate $EI\,v''=-M$ separately over intervals $AC$, $CD$, and $DB$. How many constants of integration must be found, and which conditions determine them?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;D,4,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-0.9,P_1;D,0,-0.9,P_2" titolo="Simply supported beam with two point forces"}

- Four constants, with two support conditions and two conditions enforcing continuity of $v$
- Six constants, using $v=0$ and $M=0$ at $A$ and $B$, plus continuity of $v$ at $C$ and $D$
- Six constants, with two support conditions and four conditions enforcing continuity of $v$ and $v'$ at $C$ and $D$
- Two constants, using $v(A)=0$ and $v(B)=0$

## Explanation

Each interval has its own bending moment expression, and each double integration introduces two constants, giving six in total. The support conditions give $v(A)=0$ and $v(B)=0$. The beam is unbroken at $C$ and $D$, so impose continuity of displacement and slope at both points, giving four conditions. The support conditions $M=0$ are already incorporated in the moment expression obtained from equilibrium. They do not determine any integration constants.
