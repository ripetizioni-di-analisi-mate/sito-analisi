A cantilever of span $L$ and constant flexural rigidity carries tip force $P$. Extend the tangent at the tip of the deflected beam until it intersects the original centerline. At what distance from the fixed end do the lines intersect?

- $\dfrac L3$
- $\dfrac L2$
- $\dfrac{2L}{3}$
- $0$

## Explanation

At the tip the deflection is $\dfrac{PL^3}{3EI}$ and the slope is $\dfrac{PL^2}{2EI}$. Moving back from the tip, the tangent returns to the initial elevation after a distance $d$ equal to the deflection-to-slope ratio, giving $d=\dfrac{PL^3}{3EI}\cdot\dfrac{2EI}{PL^2}=\dfrac{2L}{3}$. The intersection is therefore a distance $L-\dfrac{2L}{3}=\dfrac L3$ from the fixed end. The result is independent of both $P$ and $EI$.
