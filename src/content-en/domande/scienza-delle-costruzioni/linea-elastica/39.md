Two strain gauges are bonded to the top and bottom fibers of a rectangular beam of depth $300\,\mathrm{mm}$, with $EI=12\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. Under load, the readings are $\varepsilon=-3\cdot10^{-4}$ at the top and $\varepsilon=3\cdot10^{-4}$ at the bottom. What is the bending moment at the section?

- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $48\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

Plane sections remain plane, so strain varies linearly through the depth. The curvature is therefore the difference between the two strains divided by the depth, $\kappa=\dfrac{3\cdot10^{-4}+3\cdot10^{-4}}{0.3\,\mathrm m}=2\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$. The bending moment has magnitude $EI\kappa=12\,000\cdot2\cdot10^{-3}=24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The bottom fiber elongates. The moment puts the bottom fibers in tension and has the positive value $M=24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
