A simply supported beam of span $4\,\mathrm{m}$ with $EI=10\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ carries only two couples of magnitude $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, clockwise at $A$ and counterclockwise at $B$. What is the midspan deflection?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0,-;Z,4.9,0,-" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" coppie="A,-1,20 kN m;B,1,20 kN m" quote="A,B,4 m" etichette="4.3,-0.5,B" titolo="Simply supported beam with couples at both ends"}

- $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ downward
- $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ upward
- $2\,\mathrm{mm}$ downward
- $8\,\mathrm{mm}$ downward

## Explanation

The couples balance, so the vertical reactions vanish and the bending moment is constant throughout. The clockwise couple at $A$ puts the bottom fibers in tension, giving positive $M=20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Integrating $EI\,v''=-M$ with $v(0)=v(L)=0$ gives $EI\,v(x)=\dfrac{Mx(L-x)}{2}$. At midspan, the deflection is $v=\dfrac{ML^2}{8EI}=\dfrac{20\cdot16}{80\,000}\,\mathrm m=4\,\mathrm{mm}$ downward.
