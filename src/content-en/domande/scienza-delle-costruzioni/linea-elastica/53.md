A simply supported beam of span $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and constant flexural rigidity carries a downward force a distance $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from left support $A$. At what distance from $A$ is the deflection greatest?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,1,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-0.9,P" quote="A,C,1 m;C,B,3 m" titolo="Force near a support"}

- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.76\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.53\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Measure distance $u$ from support $B$. Between the force and $B$, the moment is $M=\dfrac{Pau}{L}$ with $a=1\,\mathrm m$, and the deflection is $v=\dfrac{Pau\left(L^2-a^2-u^2\right)}{6LEI}$. The tangent is horizontal where $L^2-a^2-3u^2=0$, which gives $u=\sqrt{\dfrac{L^2-a^2}{3}}=\sqrt5\,\mathrm m\simeq2.24\,\mathrm m$. This value is less than $3\,\mathrm m$, so the section lies between the force and $B$, a distance $4-2.24=1.76\,\mathrm m$ from $A$. The lowest section therefore lies between the load point and midspan.
