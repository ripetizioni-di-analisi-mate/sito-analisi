A cantilever of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ has $EI=12\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. A uniform downward load of intensity $9\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts only over the first $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from the fixed end. What is the magnitude of the rotation at the free end?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,4,0" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" carichi="A,C,0.8,0.8,9 kN/m" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,2 m"}

- $3\,\mathrm{mrad}$
- $1\,\mathrm{mrad}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{mrad}$
- $8\,\mathrm{mrad}$

## Explanation

In the unloaded segment the bending moment is zero, so the curvature is zero and the rotation remains constant up to the free end. At the end of the loaded segment the rotation magnitude is the integral of $\dfrac{q(a-x)^2}{2EI}$ from $0$ to $a$, equal to $\dfrac{qa^3}{6EI}$. With $a=2\,\mathrm m$ this gives
$$
\frac{qa^3}{6EI}=\frac{9\cdot2^3}{6\cdot12\,000}\,\mathrm{rad}=0.001\,\mathrm{rad}.
$$
Therefore the free end rotates by $1\,\mathrm{mrad}$. The value $8\,\mathrm{mrad}$ results from using the full span of $4\,\mathrm m$ instead of the loaded segment.
