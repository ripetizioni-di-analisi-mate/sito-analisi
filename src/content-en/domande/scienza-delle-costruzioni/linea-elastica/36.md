A cantilever has sensors at sections $x=3$ and $x=4\,\mathrm{m}$. All loads act in $0\le x\le2\,\mathrm{m}$, and the end is free. The sensors record downward displacements of $5$ and $8\,\mathrm{mm}$, respectively. In the small-rotation model, what is the tip slope when $v$ is positive downward?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0,-;S,3,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="A,C,2 m;C,S,1 m;S,B,1 m" titolo="Sensor locations on the unloaded end segment"}

- $+2\,\mathrm{mrad}$
- $+3\,\mathrm{mrad}$
- $+8\,\mathrm{mrad}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{mrad}$

## Explanation

The unloaded free-end segment carries zero bending moment, so its deflected shape is a straight line. Its slope is $\dfrac{8-5}{1000}=0.003$, which also holds at the tip.
