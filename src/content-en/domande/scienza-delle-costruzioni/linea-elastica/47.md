At a section of a beam with $EI=15\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$, the bending moment is $30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. What is the radius of curvature of the deflected beam centerline at that section?

- $0.002\,\mathrm{m}$
- $500\,\mathrm{m}$
- $50\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5\,000\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The curvature has magnitude $\dfrac{M}{EI}=\dfrac{30}{15\,000}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}=2\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$. The radius of curvature is the reciprocal of the curvature and equals $\dfrac{1}{2\cdot10^{-3}}\,\mathrm m=500\,\mathrm m$. This radius is much greater than the beam dimensions, consistent with the small-slope assumption.
