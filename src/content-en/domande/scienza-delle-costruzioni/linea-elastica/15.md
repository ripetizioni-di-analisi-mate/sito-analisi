In a beam test, the downward deflections at two nearby sections are $5.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ at coordinate $x=1.0\,\mathrm{m}$ and $3.8\,\mathrm{mm}$ at coordinate $x=1.4\,\mathrm{m}$. With $v$ positive downward and rotation $\varphi$ positive counterclockwise, what is the estimated rotation between the two sections?

- $3\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $-3\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $1.2\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $3\cdot10^{-6}\,\mathrm{rad}$

## Explanation

The mean slope of $v$ between the two sections equals the change in deflection divided by their separation.
$$
v'\simeq\frac{3.8\,\mathrm{mm}-5.0\,\mathrm{mm}}{400\,\mathrm{mm}}=-3\cdot10^{-3}.
$$
Deflections and separation are both in millimeters, so the ratio is dimensionless. For small rotations $\varphi=-v'$, giving rotation $3\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{rad}$. The positive sign denotes counterclockwise rotation, consistent with the downward deflection decreasing to the right.
