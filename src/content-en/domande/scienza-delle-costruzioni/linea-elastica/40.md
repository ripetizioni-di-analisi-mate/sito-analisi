The beam shown has length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and is fixed at $A$. At $B$, a guided support restrains rotation and horizontal displacement but permits vertical displacement. A downward force of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $B$, and the flexural rigidity is $EI=9\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. How far does $B$ deflect downward?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,doppio-pendolo,0" forze="B,0,-0.8,12 kN" quote="A,B,3 m" titolo="Beam fixed at one end and guided at the other"}

- $12\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $6\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $3\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The guide transmits a reaction couple $M_B$ at $B$ and no vertical force, so the bending moment is $M(x)=-P(L-x)+M_B$. At the fixed end, $v(0)=v'(0)=0$. At the guided end, $v'(L)=0$. Integrate $EI\,v''=-M$ once. Condition $v'(L)=0$ gives $\dfrac{PL^2}{2}-M_BL=0$, or $M_B=\dfrac{PL}{2}$. A second integration gives $v(L)=\dfrac{PL^3}{12EI}=\dfrac{12\cdot27}{108\,000}\,\mathrm m=3\,\mathrm{mm}$ downward.
