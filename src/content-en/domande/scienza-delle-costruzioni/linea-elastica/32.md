A simply supported beam of span $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and flexural rigidity $EI=10\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ carries a downward distributed load increasing linearly from zero at $A$ to $12\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $B$. What is the midspan deflection?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0,0.9,12 kN/m" quote="A,B,4 m" titolo="Simply supported beam under a triangular load"}

- $4\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $3.2\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $2\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $1\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

With $x$ measured from $A$, the load is $q(x)=\dfrac{q_0x}{L}$. The function
$$
v(x)=\frac{q_0x\left(7L^4-10L^2x^2+3x^4\right)}{360\,L\,EI}
$$
satisfies $EI\,v^{(4)}=\dfrac{q_0x}{L}$ and the four support conditions, $v=0$ and $v''=0$ at both $x=0$ and $x=L$. It is therefore the deflection of the beam. At $x=\dfrac L2$, factor $7L^4-10L^2x^2+3x^4$ equals $\dfrac{75L^4}{16}$, giving $v\left(\dfrac L2\right)=\dfrac{5q_0L^4}{768EI}$. Substitution gives $v=\dfrac{5\cdot12\cdot256}{768\cdot10\,000}\,\mathrm m=2\,\mathrm{mm}$ downward.
