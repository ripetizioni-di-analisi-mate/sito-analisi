Two unequal downward forces $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $30\,\mathrm{kN}$ act at interior sections of a simply supported beam of constant flexural rigidity. At how many interior sections is the tangent to the deflected shape horizontal?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,1.5,0;D,4,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-0.6,10 kN;D,0,-1.1,30 kN" titolo="Two unequal forces on a simply supported beam"}

- At two sections, one under each force
- At only one section
- At no section
- At three sections

## Explanation

The downward loads produce upward reactions, and the bending moment is positive at all interior sections. Thus $v''=-\dfrac{M}{EI}<0$, so the slope $v'$ decreases strictly along the beam. The end slopes satisfy $v'(0)>0$ and $v'(L)<0$ because the beam descends from $A$ and rises toward $B$. A strictly decreasing function that changes sign vanishes at exactly one point, so the tangent is horizontal at **one section only**.
