A simply supported beam of span $6\,\mathrm{m}$ has $EI=22\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. A uniform downward load of intensity $12\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ covers only a length $2\,\mathrm{m}$ centered at midspan. What is the magnitude of the midspan deflection?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2.0,0;D,4.0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-C;C-D;D-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="C,D,0.8,0.8,12 kN/m" quote="A,C,2 m;C,D,2 m;D,B,2 m"}

- $4.91\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $2.33\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $9.2\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $4.66\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

Each vertical reaction is $qa$. On the left half, set $h=\dfrac{L}{2}$ and $b=h-a$. The actual moment is $qax$ up to $b$ and $qax-\dfrac{q(x-b)^2}{2}$ beyond $b$. A virtual unit force at midspan gives moment $\dfrac{x}{2}$. Doubling the integral by symmetry gives $\dfrac{q\left(\dfrac{ah^3}{3}-\dfrac{a^4}{8}-\dfrac{ba^3}{6}\right)}{EI}$ with $h=3\,\mathrm{m}$ and $b=2\,\mathrm{m}$. This gives $4.66\,\mathrm{mm}$.
