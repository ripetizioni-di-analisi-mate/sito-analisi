A simply supported beam spans $6\,\mathrm{m}$. The influence line for midspan deflection is positive, symmetric and strictly increasing from the left support to midspan. A downward uniform load covers a movable $2\,\mathrm{m}$ length. Which position gives the greatest midspan deflection?

- From $x=0$ to $x=2\,\mathrm{m}$.
- From $x=1$ to $x=3\,\mathrm{m}$.
- Deflection is the same for every position.
- From $x=2$ to $x=4\,\mathrm{m}$.

## Explanation

Deflection is proportional to the area under the influence line over the loaded interval. Moving an off-centre patch toward midspan replaces smaller ordinates with larger ones. Hence the greatest area of width $2\,\mathrm{m}$ has its ends equally spaced about midspan.
