Four beams have the same span $L$ and rigidity $EI$. Their loads all have the same downward resultant $P$. Which one has the greatest maximum deflection?

- The cantilever under the uniform load $\dfrac PL$ over its full span
- The simply supported beam with the midspan force $P$
- The cantilever with the tip force $P$
- The simply supported beam under the uniform load $\dfrac PL$ over its full span

## Explanation

Substituting $q=\dfrac PL$ into the uniform-load formulas gives the four maximum deflections $\dfrac{PL^3}{3EI}$, $\dfrac{PL^3}{8EI}$, $\dfrac{PL^3}{48EI}$, and $\dfrac{5PL^3}{384EI}$. The larger coefficient is $\dfrac13$. The **cantilever carrying the tip force** therefore has the greatest deflection.
