A simply supported beam of span $L=4\,\mathrm{m}$ and flexural rigidity $EI=10\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ carries a downward distributed load of intensity $q(x)=q_0\sin\dfrac{\pi x}{L}$, with $q_0=10\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and $x$ measured from the left support. What is the midspan deflection?

- $3.33\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $8.26\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $2.63\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $1.31\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

Seek a deflection field of the form $v(x)=C\sin\dfrac{\pi x}{L}$. Its fourth derivative is $\left(\dfrac{\pi}{L}\right)^4C\sin\dfrac{\pi x}{L}$, so equation $EI\,v^{(4)}=q$ holds for $C=\dfrac{q_0L^4}{\pi^4EI}$. The function and its second derivative both contain the factor $\sin\dfrac{\pi x}{L}$ and therefore vanish at $x=0$ and $x=L$. All four support conditions are satisfied, so the function is the beam’s deflection field. At midspan the sine equals $1$, giving
$$
v\left(\frac L2\right)=\frac{q_0L^4}{\pi^4EI}=\frac{10\cdot256}{\pi^4\cdot10\,000}\,\mathrm m\simeq2.63\,\mathrm{mm}
$$
downward.
