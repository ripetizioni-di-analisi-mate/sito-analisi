A cantilever $4\,\mathrm{m}$ long, with constant flexural rigidity $EI=16\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$, carries a downward force $P=10\,\mathrm{kN}$ at its free end. What is the magnitude of the rotation of the end section?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" forze="B,0,-1,P" quote="A,B,4 m" titolo="Cantilever with a force at the free end"}

- $3.33\,\mathrm{mrad}$
- $10\,\mathrm{mrad}$
- $5\,\mathrm{mrad}$
- $1.67\,\mathrm{mrad}$

## Explanation

For a cantilever of constant rigidity with a force $P$ at the free end, the slope at the tip is $v'=\dfrac{PL^2}{2EI}$. Substituting the data gives
$$
v'=\frac{10\cdot4^2}{2\cdot16\,000}\,\mathrm{rad}=0.005\,\mathrm{rad}.
$$
Since the rotation is $\varphi=-v'$, the end section rotates clockwise by $5\,\mathrm{mrad}$. The value $3.33\,\mathrm{mrad}$ results from using the coefficient $\dfrac13$ of the deflection instead of $\dfrac12$.
