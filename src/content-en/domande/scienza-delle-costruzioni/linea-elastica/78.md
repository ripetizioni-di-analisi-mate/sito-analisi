In the beam shown, segment $AC$ is fixed at $A$ and carries a downward force at its midpoint. At $C$, an internal hinge connects it to unloaded segment $CB$, which rests on a roller at $B$. What shape does segment $CB$ take after deflection?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,1.5,0;C,3,0;B,6,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" forze="D,0,-0.9,P" quote="A,D,a/2;D,C,a/2;C,B,b" titolo="Beam with an internal hinge and an unloaded segment"}

- A horizontal segment, because the member stays in its original position
- A straight segment joining the lowered point $C$ to $B$
- A parabolic arc with a horizontal tangent at $B$
- A cubic curve matching the slope of segment $AC$ at $C$

## Explanation

Segment $CB$ carries no loads and has zero bending moment at both the hinge and roller. Its bending moment vanishes throughout. With $M=0$, we obtain $v''=0$, so the deflected shape is a straight segment. The segment passes through $B$, where $v=0$, and through $C$, where its displacement equals the nonzero free-end displacement of cantilever $AC$. The slope of $CB$ at $C$ generally differs from that of $AC$ because the hinge permits relative rotation.
