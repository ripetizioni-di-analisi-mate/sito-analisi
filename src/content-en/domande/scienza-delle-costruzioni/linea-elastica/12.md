The cantilever has span $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and flexural rigidity $EI=8\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. A downward force of $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from the fixed end. Find the free-end deflection.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,2,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,2 m" forze="C,0,-0.85,10 kN"  titolo="Beam and loading diagram"}

- $\dfrac{25}3\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $\dfrac{10}3\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $\dfrac{80}3\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $5\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

Up to the load, the result is $v(2)=\dfrac{10}{3}\,\mathrm{mm}$ with slope $0.0025$. The segment with zero moment maintains this slope over the remaining $2\,\mathrm{m}$, adding $5\,\mathrm{mm}$.
