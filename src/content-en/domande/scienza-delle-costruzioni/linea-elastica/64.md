A cantilever fixed at $x=0$ carries only a downward force $P$ at free end $x=L$. Solve equation $EI\,v^{(4)}=0$ over the full cantilever. Which conditions apply at $x=L$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" forze="B,0,-0.9,P" quote="A,B,L" titolo="Cantilever with a force at the free end"}

- $v''(L)=0$ and $EI\,v'''(L)=-P$
- $v''(L)=0$ and $v'''(L)=0$
- $v(L)=0$ and $v'(L)=0$
- $v''(L)=0$ and $EI\,v'''(L)=P$

## Explanation

No couple acts at the free end, so $M(L)=0$ and relation $EI\,v''=-M$ gives $v''(L)=0$. Equilibrium of the end element gives the shear force $T(L)=P$, positive under the chosen convention. Since $EI\,v'''=-T$, the second condition is $EI\,v'''(L)=-P$. Conditions $v=0$ and $v'=0$ apply at the fixed end $x=0$.
