A cantilever of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$ with $EI=4\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ carries a tip force of $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ directed to the right at angle $30^\circ$ below the horizontal. Neglecting axial deformation, what is the downward tip deflection?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" forze="B,0.87,-0.5,10 kN" etichette="1.45,0.12,30°" quote="A,B,2 m" titolo="Cantilever with an inclined tip force"}

- $3.33\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $6.67\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $5.77\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

Resolve the force into its horizontal axial component $10\cos30^\circ\simeq8.66\,\mathrm{kN}$ and its downward vertical component $10\sin30^\circ=5\,\mathrm{kN}$. The axial component produces only axial force and causes no tip displacement when axial deformation is neglected. The vertical component bends the cantilever, giving $v=\dfrac{5\cdot2^3}{3\cdot4\,000}\,\mathrm m\simeq3.33\,\mathrm{mm}$ downward.
