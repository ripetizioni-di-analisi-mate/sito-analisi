A simply supported beam of span $6\,\mathrm{m}$ with $EI=12\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ carries only a counterclockwise couple of $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at right support $B$. What is the rotation at the left support $A$, taking counterclockwise as positive?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0,-;Z,6.9,0,-" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" coppie="B,1,24 kN m" quote="A,B,6 m" etichette="6.3,-0.5,B" titolo="Simply supported beam with a couple at one end"}

- $2\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $-4\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $-10^{-3}\,\mathrm{rad}$
- $-2\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{rad}$

## Explanation

The reactions form a couple that balances $\mathcal M$. The moment $M(x)=\dfrac{\mathcal Mx}{L}$ is zero at $A$ and reaches $\mathcal M$ at $B$. Integrating $EI\,v''=-\dfrac{\mathcal Mx}{L}$ with $v(0)=v(L)=0$ gives $EI\,v(x)=\dfrac{\mathcal Mx\left(L^2-x^2\right)}{6L}$. Therefore, $v'(0)=\dfrac{\mathcal ML}{6EI}=\dfrac{24\cdot6}{72\,000}=2\cdot10^{-3}$. The rotation is $\varphi_A=-v'(0)=-2\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{rad}$, clockwise.
