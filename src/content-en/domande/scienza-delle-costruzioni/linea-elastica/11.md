A counterclockwise couple of $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ acts at midspan of a simply supported beam of span $4\,\mathrm{m}$. The flexural rigidity is $EI=8\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. Find the jump $v^{\prime\prime}(2^+)-v^{\prime\prime}(2^-)$, with $x$ in meters and $v$ positive downward.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,2,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,2 m" coppie="C,1,24 kN m"  titolo="Beam and loading diagram"}

- $-3\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $0\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $6\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $3\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$

## Explanation

The counterclockwise couple produces a bending moment jump of $-24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Since $v''=-\dfrac{M}{EI}$, the jump in $v''$ is $+\dfrac{24}{8\,000}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$.
