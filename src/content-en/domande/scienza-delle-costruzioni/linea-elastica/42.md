A cantilever of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ carries a downward tip load $14\,\mathrm{kN}$. $EI=12\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. What is the magnitude of the vertical displacement a distance $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from the fixed end?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,1,0;B,4,0" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" forze="B,0,-1,14 kN" quote="A,C,1 m;C,B,3 m"}

- $1.07\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $2.14\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.39\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $24.89\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The bending moment has magnitude $P(L-s)$. Integrate the curvature twice using zero deflection and rotation at the fixed end. The displacement magnitude a distance $x$ away is $\dfrac{Px^2(3L-x)}{6EI}$. This gives $2.14\,\mathrm{mm}$.
