A simply supported beam of span $L$ and constant flexural rigidity carries a uniform downward load over the entire span. Integrate $EI\,v''=-M$ only on the left half, $0\le x\le\dfrac L2$. Which two conditions determine the constants of integration?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,C,L/2;C,B,L/2" titolo="Simply supported beam under a uniform load"}

- $v(0)=0$ and $v\left(\dfrac L2\right)=0$
- $v'(0)=0$ and $v'\left(\dfrac L2\right)=0$
- $v(0)=0$ and $v'\left(\dfrac L2\right)=0$
- $v(0)=0$ and $v''\left(\dfrac L2\right)=0$

## Explanation

The support at $A$ prevents vertical displacement, so $v(0)=0$. Geometry, rigidity, supports, and loading are symmetric about midspan. The deflected shape is therefore symmetric, $v(L-x)=v(x)$. Differentiation gives $-v'(L-x)=v'(x)$. Setting $x=\dfrac L2$ then gives $v'\left(\dfrac L2\right)=0$. The midspan moment is $\dfrac{qL^2}{8}$, so $v''\left(\dfrac L2\right)$ is nonzero. The support permits rotation, so $v'(0)$ is also nonzero.
