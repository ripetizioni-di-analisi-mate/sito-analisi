A beam of span $4\,\mathrm{m}$ is fixed at both ends and carries a uniform downward load of intensity $12\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. The flexural rigidity is $EI=8\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. What is the midspan deflection?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,incastro,0" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.6,12 kN/m" quote="A,B,4 m" titolo="Fixed-fixed beam under a uniform load"}

- $5\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $2\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $0.5\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $1\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

Function $v(x)=\dfrac{qx^2(L-x)^2}{24EI}$ satisfies $EI\,v^{(4)}=q$ because its highest-degree term is $\dfrac{qx^4}{24EI}$. It contains the factors $x^2$ and $(L-x)^2$, so the function and its first derivative vanish at $x=0$ and $x=L$. Thus the four fixed-end conditions are satisfied and the function is the beam deflection. At midspan, the deflection is $v\left(\dfrac L2\right)=\dfrac{qL^4}{384EI}=\dfrac{12\cdot256}{384\cdot8\,000}\,\mathrm m=1\,\mathrm{mm}$ downward.
