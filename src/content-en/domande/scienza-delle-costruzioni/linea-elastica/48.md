The $L$-shaped frame shown has column $AB$ of height $3\,\mathrm{m}$ fixed at its base and crossbeam $BC$ of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$ rigidly connected at $B$. A downward force of $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $C$. The column has $EI=15\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. Neglect axial deformation. What is the magnitude of the horizontal displacement of $C$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;C,2,3" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,incastro" forze="C,0,-0.9,10 kN" quote="A,B,3 m,-0.7;B,C,2 m" titolo="L-shaped frame with a force at the tip of the crossbeam"}

- $6\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $3\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $12\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $4\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The crossbeam transmits the vertical force and couple $Pb=10\cdot2=20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ to the top of the column. The vertical force acts along the column axis and produces no bending when axial deformation is neglected. The column therefore behaves as a cantilever under constant moment $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, and its top moves horizontally by $\dfrac{PbH^2}{2EI}=\dfrac{20\cdot9}{30\,000}\,\mathrm m=6\,\mathrm{mm}$. Crossbeam bending produces only vertical displacements, so $C$ undergoes the same horizontal displacement $6\,\mathrm{mm}$.
