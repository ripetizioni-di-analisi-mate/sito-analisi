A simply supported beam of span $6\,\mathrm{m}$ carries a uniform downward load of intensity $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and a clockwise couple of $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at its right support $B$. At what distance from the left support $A$ does the deflected shape have a point of inflection?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0,-;Z,6.9,0,-" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.6,4 kN/m" coppie="B,-1,24 kN m" quote="A,B,6 m" etichette="6.3,-0.5,B" titolo="Uniform load and a couple at the right support"}

- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The deflected shape has an inflection point where the bending moment passes through zero and changes sign. By superposition, the load contributes $\dfrac{qx(L-x)}{2}=2x(6-x)$ and the clockwise couple at $B$ contributes $-\dfrac{\mathcal Mx}{L}=-4x$. Adding gives $M(x)=2x(4-x)$, positive for $0<x<4$ and negative for $4<x<6$. Since $v''=-\dfrac{M}{EI}$ changes sign at $x=4\,\mathrm m$, the deflected shape has an inflection point there.
