A cantilever of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ with $EI=9\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ carries a downward tip force of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$. The fixed support rotates rigidly counterclockwise by $2\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{rad}$ without translating. What is the downward tip deflection?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" forze="B,0,-0.8,12 kN" quote="A,B,3 m" titolo="Cantilever with a tip force and rotation at the fixed support"}

- $18\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $12\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $9\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $6\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The problem is linear, so superpose the response to the force with the support stationary and the response to the support rotation alone. The force produces a downward deflection of $\dfrac{PL^3}{3EI}=\dfrac{12\cdot27}{27\,000}\,\mathrm m=12\,\mathrm{mm}$. The counterclockwise support rotation moves the cantilever as a rigid body and raises the tip by $2\cdot10^{-3}\cdot3\,\mathrm m=6\,\mathrm{mm}$. The net downward tip deflection is therefore $12-6=6\,\mathrm{mm}$.
