A cantilever fixed at $x=0$ carries a downward force at free end $x=L$. With $v$ positive downward, what is the sign of $v''$ in sections where $0\le x<L$?

- $v''<0$ at all these sections
- $v''>0$ at all these sections
- $v''=0$ because the force acts only at the tip
- $v''$ changes sign at midspan

## Explanation

At a cut a distance $x$ from the fixed end, the tip force produces moment $M=-P(L-x)$, negative because it puts the top fibers in tension. Equation $EI\,v''=-M$ gives $EI\,v''=P(L-x)>0$ for $0\le x<L$. The slope starts at zero at the fixed end and increases toward the tip. The curvature vanishes only at $x=L$, where the bending moment is zero.
