In the beam shown, segment $AC$ is fixed at $A$ and connected by an internal hinge at $C$ to segment $CB$, which has a roller at $B$. Both segments have $EI=10\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. A downward force of $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts directly at hinge $C$. How far does $C$ deflect downward?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,5,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1,15 kN" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,3 m" titolo="Beam with a point load applied at an internal hinge"}

- $4\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $2\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $6\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $8\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

Segment $CB$ carries no load between $C$ and $B$ and has zero moment at both ends, the hinge and the roller. Taking moments of the segment about $C$ gives zero reaction at $B$, so segment $CB$ transmits no force. The entire force therefore acts on cantilever $AC$ of length $a=2\,\mathrm m$. The hinge displacement is $v_C=\dfrac{Pa^3}{3EI}=\dfrac{15\cdot8}{30\,000}\,\mathrm m=4\,\mathrm{mm}$ downward.
