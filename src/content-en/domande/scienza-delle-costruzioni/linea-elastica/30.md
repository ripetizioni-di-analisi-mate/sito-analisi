A simply supported beam of span $6\,\mathrm{m}$ has $EI=12\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. The downward vertical load increases linearly from zero at the left support to $18\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at the right support. What is the magnitude of the curvature a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from the left end?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,6,0" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0,1,18 kN/m" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,4 m"}

- $5.33\cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $2.67\cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $3\cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$
- $0.89\cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$

## Explanation

The resultant $\dfrac{qL}{2}$ acts a distance $\dfrac{2L}{3}$ from the left end, giving a left reaction of $\dfrac{qL}{6}$. The moment at a distance $x$ is $\dfrac{qLx}{6}-\dfrac{qx^3}{6L}$. Divide by $EI$ and convert to thousandths of an inverse meter to obtain the required magnitude. This gives $2.67\cdot 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$.
