A beam of span $4\,\mathrm{m}$ with $EI=8\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ is pinned at $A$ and rests on an elastic support of stiffness $k=2\,000\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $B$. A downward force of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at midspan. What is the downward deflection at midspan?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-0.9,12 kN" etichette="4.45,-0.35,k" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,2 m" titolo="Beam with an elastic support at B"}

- $2\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $5\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $3\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $3.5\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The beam is statically determinate, so the reaction at $B$ is $\dfrac P2=6\,\mathrm{kN}$, the same as for a rigid support. The elastic support compresses by $\dfrac{6}{2\,000}\,\mathrm m=3\,\mathrm{mm}$. The beam undergoes rigid-body motion with displacement varying linearly from zero at $A$ to $3\,\mathrm{mm}$ at $B$, giving $1.5\,\mathrm{mm}$ at midspan. Bending adds $\dfrac{PL^3}{48EI}=\dfrac{12\cdot64}{384\,000}\,\mathrm m=2\,\mathrm{mm}$. By superposition the midpoint moves down by $3.5\,\mathrm{mm}$.
