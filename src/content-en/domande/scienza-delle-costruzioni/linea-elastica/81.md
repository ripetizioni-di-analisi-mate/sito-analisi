A cantilever of constant flexural rigidity carries a uniform downward load over its full span. At which section is the magnitude of the curvature of the deflected centerline greatest?

- At the free end
- At midspan
- The curvature is the same at every section
- At the fixed end

## Explanation

With $x$ measured from the fixed end, the moment is $M=-\dfrac{q(L-x)^2}{2}$. Its magnitude decreases from $\dfrac{qL^2}{2}$ at the fixed end to zero at the tip. The curvature magnitude is $\dfrac{|M|}{EI}$ with constant $EI$, so the maximum occurs **at the fixed end**.
