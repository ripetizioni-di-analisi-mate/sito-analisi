Measurements on a beam with $EI=20\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ give the downward deflections at three equally spaced sections as $4.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ at $x=1\,\mathrm{m}$, $6.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ at $x=2\,\mathrm{m}$, and $6.5\,\mathrm{mm}$ at $x=3\,\mathrm{m}$. Using the second difference to estimate $v''$ at $x=2\,\mathrm{m}$, what is the bending moment at that section?

- $-30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $60\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The second difference with spacing $h=1\,\mathrm m$ gives
$$
v''(2)\simeq\frac{v(3)-2v(2)+v(1)}{h^2}=\frac{6.5-12+4.0}{1}\,\mathrm{mm/m^2}=-1.5\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}.
$$
Equation $EI\,v''=-M$ gives $M\simeq-20\,000\cdot\left(-1.5\cdot10^{-3}\right)=30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The moment is positive, consistent with the graph of $v$ being concave downward in this region.
