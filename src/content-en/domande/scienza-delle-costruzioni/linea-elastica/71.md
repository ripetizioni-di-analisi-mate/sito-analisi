The beam shown has a pin at $A$ and a guided support at $B$ that restrains rotation and horizontal movement but permits vertical displacement. A uniform downward load acts over the entire span, and no concentrated force or couple acts at $B$. Which statement about section $B$ is true?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,doppio-pendolo,0" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,B,L" titolo="Beam with a hinge and a guided support"}

- The slope and shear force are zero
- The displacement and bending moment are zero
- The displacement and slope are zero
- The bending moment and shear force are zero

## Explanation

The guide prevents rotation, so $v'(L)=0$. Its horizontal links exert no vertical force, and no concentrated force acts at $B$. The shear therefore vanishes and $EI\,v'''(L)=0$. The guide does transmit a couple, so the bending moment at $B$ is generally nonzero. The vertical displacement at $B$ is free and must be found from the solution.
