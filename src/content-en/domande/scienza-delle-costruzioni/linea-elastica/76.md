A simply supported beam of constant flexural rigidity carries two equal downward forces at $C$ and $D$, symmetrically placed about midspan. What is the degree of polynomial $v(x)$ on segment $CD$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,1.5,0;D,4.5,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-0.9,P;D,0,-0.9,P" quote="A,C,a;C,D,L-2a;D,B,a" titolo="Two equal forces at symmetric positions"}

- $3$
- $2$
- $1$
- $4$

## Explanation

Each reaction equals either applied force, so the shear vanishes between $C$ and $D$. The moment is consequently constant over segment $CD$, and $v''=-\dfrac{M}{EI}$ is constant as well. Integrating a constant twice gives a quadratic polynomial. On the outer segments the moment is linear, so the deflection is a cubic polynomial.
