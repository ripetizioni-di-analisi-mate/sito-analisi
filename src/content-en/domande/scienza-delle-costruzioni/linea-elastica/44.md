A simply supported beam of span $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and flexural rigidity $EI=11\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ carries a downward force of $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ at midspan. What is the downward deflection a distance $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from the left support?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;D,1,0;C,2,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-0.9,24 kN" quote="A,D,1 m;D,C,1 m;C,B,2 m" titolo="Force at midspan and section at the quarter-span point"}

- $2.91\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $2\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $1.45\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $1.64\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

In the left half the moment is $M=\dfrac{Px}{2}$, and by symmetry the slope at midspan is zero. Integrating $EI\,v''=-\dfrac{Px}{2}$ with $v(0)=0$ and $v'\left(\dfrac L2\right)=0$ gives
$$
EI\,v(x)=\frac{Px\left(3L^2-4x^2\right)}{48},\qquad 0\le x\le\frac L2 .
$$
The displacement at $x=1\,\mathrm m$ is $v=\dfrac{24\cdot1\cdot(48-4)}{48\cdot11\,000}\,\mathrm m=2\,\mathrm{mm}$ downward.
