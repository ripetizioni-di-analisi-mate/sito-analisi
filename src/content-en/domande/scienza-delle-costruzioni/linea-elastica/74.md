A tapered cantilever has flexural rigidity $EI(x)=EI_0\left(2-\dfrac xL\right)$, with $x$ measured from the fixed end, and carries uniform downward load $q$. Which differential equation governs the deflected shape?

- $EI_0\left(2-\dfrac xL\right)v^{(4)}=q$
- $\left[EI_0\left(2-\dfrac xL\right)v''\right]''=-q$
- $\left[EI_0\left(2-\dfrac xL\right)v''\right]''=q$
- $EI_0\left(2-\dfrac xL\right)v''=q$

## Explanation

Equilibrium gives $M'=T$ and $T'=-q$, so $M''=-q$. The constitutive relation is $M=-EI(x)\,v''$. Substitution gives $\bigl(EI(x)\,v''\bigr)''=q$. Rigidity depends on $x$, so differentiating product $EI(x)\,v''$ twice also introduces derivatives of $EI$. Equation $EI\,v^{(4)}=q$ applies only to constant flexural rigidity.
