A simply supported beam of span $4\,\mathrm{m}$ with $EI=8\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ carries a vertical downward force of $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ at midspan. Calculate the midspan deflection.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,2,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,2 m" forze="C,0,-0.85,16 kN"  titolo="Beam and loading diagram"}

- $\dfrac83\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $\dfrac43\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $8\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $\dfrac{16}3\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

For a simply supported beam with a central load, $v(C)=\dfrac{PL^3}{48EI}$. The given values give a downward deflection of $\dfrac83\,\mathrm{mm}$.
