A beam of constant flexural rigidity $EI$, simply supported at $x=0$ and $x=L$, has deflection $v(x)=c\left(x^4-2Lx^3+L^3x\right)$, with $c>0$ constant and $v$ positive downward. What load acts on the beam?

- A uniform downward load $q=24EIc$
- A uniform upward load $q=24EIc$
- A uniform downward load $q=12EIc$
- A uniform downward load $q=6EIc$

## Explanation

The polynomial has degree four, so its fourth derivative is constant and equals $v^{(4)}=24c$. Equation $EI\,v^{(4)}=q$ gives $q=24EIc$. The result is positive, so the load is uniform and acts downward. The function also satisfies $v(0)=v(L)=0$ and $v''(0)=v''(L)=0$, the conditions for two simple supports without applied end couples.
