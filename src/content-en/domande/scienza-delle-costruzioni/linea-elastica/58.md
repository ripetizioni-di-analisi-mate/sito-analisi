A cantilever fixed at $x=0$, of length $L$ and constant rigidity $EI$, has deflection $v(x)=cx^2(3L-x)$, with $c>0$ constant and $v$ positive downward. What load is applied to the cantilever?

- A downward tip force $6EIc$
- An upward tip force $6EIc$
- A uniform downward load $6EIc$
- A downward tip force $3EIc$

## Explanation

The fourth derivative of $v$ is zero, so no distributed load acts along the cantilever. At $x=L$ we have $v''(L)=c(6L-6L)=0$, so the tip moment is zero and no couple is applied. The third derivative equals $v'''=-6c$, giving shear force $T=-EI\,v'''=6EIc$ at every section. A constant positive shear force extending to the free end corresponds to a downward tip force $P=6EIc$.
