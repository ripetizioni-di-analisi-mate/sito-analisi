A beam of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ with $EI=9\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ is fixed at $A$ and supported on a bilateral roller at $B$. It carries no loads. Roller $B$ settles by $6\,\mathrm{mm}$. What is the vertical reaction at $B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" frecce="3.4,0.1,3.4,-0.6,6 mm" quote="A,B,3 m" titolo="Propped cantilever with settlement of the prop"}

- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward
- Zero, because the beam is unloaded
- $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward

## Explanation

Removing the roller leaves a cantilever. To move $B$ downward by $\delta=6\,\mathrm{mm}$ requires a downward force $R$ at $B$ such that $\dfrac{RL^3}{3EI}=\delta$. This gives $R=\dfrac{3EI\delta}{L^3}=\dfrac{3\cdot9\,000\cdot0.006}{27}=6\,\mathrm{kN}$, directed downward. The beam is statically indeterminate, so settlement produces a reaction and a bending moment even without any applied loads.
