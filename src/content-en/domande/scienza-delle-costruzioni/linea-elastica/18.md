Simply supported beam $AB$ has span $5\,\mathrm{m}$ and $EI=18\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. A downward force of $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. What is the magnitude of the rotation at $A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,5,0" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1,16 kN" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,3 m"}

- $1.24\,\mathrm{mrad}$
- $0.47\,\mathrm{mrad}$
- $1.42\,\mathrm{mrad}$
- $2.84\,\mathrm{mrad}$

## Explanation

With $v$ positive upward, $EI\cdot v''=M$ and $v(0)=v(L)=0$, giving $\theta_A=-\dfrac{\int_0^L(L-x)M(x)\,dx}{LEI}$. The $M$ diagram is triangular, with height $\dfrac{Pab}{L}$ and centroid at $\dfrac{L+a}{3}$. The formula gives $|\theta_A|=\dfrac{Pb(L^2-b^2)}{6LEI}$. This gives $1.42\,\mathrm{mrad}$.
