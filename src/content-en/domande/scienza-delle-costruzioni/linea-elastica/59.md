A timber beam with a rectangular section of width $100\,\mathrm{mm}$ and depth $200\,\mathrm{mm}$ bends in the vertical plane. Young’s modulus is $E=10\,000\,\mathrm{MPa}$. What is the flexural rigidity $EI$?

- $166.7\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$
- $6.67\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$
- $66.7\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$
- $666.7\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$

## Explanation

Its second moment of area about the horizontal centroidal axis is $I=\dfrac{bh^3}{12}=\dfrac{100\cdot200^3}{12}\,\mathrm{mm^4}\simeq6.667\cdot10^{7}\,\mathrm{mm^4}$. Thus $EI\simeq10\,000\,\mathrm{N/mm^2}\cdot6.667\cdot10^{7}\,\mathrm{mm^4}=6.667\cdot10^{11}\,\mathrm{N\,mm^2}$. Since $1\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}=10^3\,\mathrm N\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{mm^2}=10^9\,\mathrm{N\,mm^2}$, the result is $EI\simeq666.7\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$.
