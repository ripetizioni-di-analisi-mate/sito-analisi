A simply supported beam of span $6\,\mathrm{m}$ has $EI_1=10\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ on the two segments of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$ adjacent to the supports and $EI_2=20\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$ on the middle segment. A downward force $P=12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at midspan. Find the midspan deflection.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;D,2,0;C,3,0;F,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,D,2 m;D,F,2 m;F,B,2 m" forze="C,0,-0.92,12 kN" etichette="0.8,0.27,EI_1;2.82,0.38,EI_2;4.8,0.27,EI_1" titolo="Beam and loading diagram"}

- $3.5\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $5.4\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $2.7\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $1.9\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

By symmetry, integrate over the left half $v(C)=\dfrac{P}{2}\int_0^3\dfrac{x^2}{EI(x)}\,dx$. Segments $0\le x\le2$ and $2\le x\le3$ contribute $1.6$ and $1.9\,\mathrm{mm}$, respectively.
