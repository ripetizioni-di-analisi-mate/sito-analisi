A beam of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and constant flexural rigidity is fixed at $A$ and rests on a roller at $B$. A downward force of $32\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at midspan. What is the vertical reaction at $B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-0.9,32 kN" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,2 m" titolo="Propped cantilever with a midspan force"}

- $16\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Remove the roller and consider the cantilever carrying the midspan force and an unknown upward reaction $R$ at $B$. The force lowers its section by $\dfrac{P}{3EI}\left(\dfrac L2\right)^3$ and produces a slope $\dfrac{P}{2EI}\left(\dfrac L2\right)^2$ there. Beyond the force, the cantilever remains straight, so $B$ moves downward by $\dfrac{PL^3}{24EI}+\dfrac{PL^3}{16EI}=\dfrac{5PL^3}{48EI}$. The reaction raises $B$ by $\dfrac{RL^3}{3EI}$. The roller requires zero displacement at $B$, giving $\dfrac{RL^3}{3EI}=\dfrac{5PL^3}{48EI}$ and an upward reaction $R=\dfrac{5P}{16}=10\,\mathrm{kN}$.
