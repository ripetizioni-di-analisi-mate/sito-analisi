A cantilever of constant flexural rigidity carries distributed load $q(x)=kx^2$, with $x$ measured from the fixed end. After integrating $EI\,v^{(4)}=q$, what is the degree of the polynomial describing the deflected shape?

- $5$
- $4$
- $2$
- $6$

## Explanation

The load is a quadratic polynomial. Each integration raises the degree by one. Obtaining $v$ from $v^{(4)}$ requires four integrations. The highest-degree term is therefore $\dfrac{kx^6}{360EI}$, and the deflection is a sixth-degree polynomial. The integration constants add terms of degree at most three.
