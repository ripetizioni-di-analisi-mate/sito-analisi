A simply supported beam carries a downward point force $P$ at midspan. Its flexural rigidity is $EI$ in the left half and $2EI$ in the right half. Which statement is true?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,6,0" poligoni="0,-0.07 3,-0.07 3,0.07 0,0.07;3,-0.13 6,-0.13 6,0.13 3,0.13" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-0.9,P" etichette="1.5,0.32,EI;4.5,0.38,2EI" quote="A,C,L/2;C,B,L/2" titolo="Simply supported beam with different rigidities in its two halves"}

- Both reactions equal $\dfrac P2$, and the lowest section is at midspan
- Both reactions equal $\dfrac P2$, and the lowest section is in the left half
- The right support reaction is larger, and the lowest section is at midspan
- The left support reaction is larger, and the lowest section is in the right half

## Explanation

The beam is statically determinate. Equilibrium alone gives reactions $\dfrac P2$ regardless of the rigidity. The moment is symmetric, but curvature $-\dfrac{M}{EI}$ in the left half is twice that in the right half. Integrating with $v(0)=v(L)=0$ gives $v'\left(\dfrac L2\right)=-\dfrac{PL^2}{96EI}<0$ at midspan, showing that the beam is already rising toward $B$ there. The lowest section therefore lies in the **left half**, the more flexible half.
