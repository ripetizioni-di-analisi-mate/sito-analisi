The beam shown has a pin at $A$, a roller at $B$, and an overhang $BC$ of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$. The flexural rigidity is constant, $EI=12\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. A downward force of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $C$. How far does $C$ deflect downward?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,6,0" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1,12 kN" quote="A,B,4 m;B,C,2 m" titolo="Overhanging beam with a load at the tip of the overhang"}

- $2.67\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $5.33\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $8\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $16\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The displacement of $C$ has two contributions, with $L=4\,\mathrm m$ and $c=2\,\mathrm m$. If treated as fixed at $B$, the overhang deflects like a cantilever with a tip force and contributes $\dfrac{Pc^3}{3EI}=\dfrac{12\cdot8}{36\,000}\,\mathrm m\simeq2.67\,\mathrm{mm}$. Span $AB$ receives moment $-Pc=-24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at $B$, rotating section $B$ through an angle of magnitude $\dfrac{PcL}{3EI}=\dfrac{24\cdot4}{36\,000}\simeq2.67\cdot10^{-3}\,\mathrm{rad}$. The overhang follows this rotation, so $C$ moves downward by an additional $2.67\cdot10^{-3}\cdot2\,\mathrm m\simeq5.33\,\mathrm{mm}$. The result is $v_C=2.67+5.33=8\,\mathrm{mm}$ downward.
