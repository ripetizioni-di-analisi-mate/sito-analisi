A simply supported beam of constant flexural rigidity carries a uniform downward load only over segment $0\le x\le a$ and no point loads. At section $x=a$, what is the lowest-order derivative of $v$ that has a jump?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2.5,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,C,0.6,0.6,q" quote="A,C,a" titolo="Uniform load over part of the span"}

- The fourth derivative
- The third derivative
- The second derivative
- None of the derivatives have jumps

## Explanation

No concentrated force or couple acts at $x=a$, so the shear force and bending moment are continuous. Since rigidity is constant, $v'''$ and $v''$ are continuous. The beam is unbroken, so $v$ and $v'$ are also continuous. The load intensity, however, changes from $q$ to zero. Equation $EI\,v^{(4)}=q$ implies that the **fourth derivative** jumps by $-\dfrac{q}{EI}$.
