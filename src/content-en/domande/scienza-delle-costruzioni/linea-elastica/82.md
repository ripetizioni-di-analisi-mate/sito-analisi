A simply supported beam of span $L$ and flexural rigidity $EI$ carries no loads. Support $B$ settles vertically by $\delta$ while $A$ remains stationary. What is the bending moment at midspan?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" frecce="5.4,0.1,5.4,-0.6,δ" quote="A,B,L" titolo="Settlement of a support in a statically determinate beam"}

- $\dfrac{3EI\delta}{L^2}$
- $\dfrac{6EI\delta}{L^2}$
- $\dfrac{8EI\delta}{L^2}$
- $0$

## Explanation

The beam is statically determinate and can follow the settlement by rigid-body motion. The field $v(x)=\dfrac{\delta x}{L}$ satisfies $v(0)=0$ and $v(L)=\delta$ and has $v''=0$. With no applied loads, the reactions vanish and the moment is zero at every section. Settlement produces bending moments only in an indeterminate beam whose restraints prevent the required rigid-body motion.
