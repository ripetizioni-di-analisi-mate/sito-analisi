A simply supported beam of span $6\,\mathrm{m}$ carries a downward force $P=18\,\mathrm{kN}$ at a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from the left support. The flexural rigidity is $EI=12\,000\,\mathrm{kN\,m^2}$. Find the displacement at the point where $P$ acts.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,2,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,4 m" forze="C,0,-0.85,18 kN"  titolo="Beam and loading diagram"}

- $\dfrac{8}{3}\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $8\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $\dfrac{16}{3}\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $16\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

If the load is at distances $a=2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $b=4\,\mathrm{m}$ from the supports, the deflection at the load point is $\dfrac{Pa^2b^2}{3EIL}$. The result is $\dfrac{16}{3}\,\mathrm{mm}$ downward.
