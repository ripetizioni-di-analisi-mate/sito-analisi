A cantilever of span $L$ fixed at $x=0$ has constant flexural rigidity and carries a uniform downward load over its full span. With $c>0$ constant, which function can represent its deflected shape?

- $c\left(x^4-2Lx^3+L^3x\right)$
- $c\left(x^4-4Lx^3+6L^2x^2\right)$
- $cx^2(3L-x)$
- $c\left(x^4-4Lx^3+6L^2x^2-4L^3x\right)$

## Explanation

Its deflection must have constant positive $v^{(4)}$ and satisfy $v(0)=v'(0)=0$ at the fixed end and $v''(L)=v'''(L)=0$ at the free tip without an applied end load. For $c\left(x^4-4Lx^3+6L^2x^2\right)$, the derivatives $v''=12c(x-L)^2$ and $v'''=24c(x-L)$ vanish at $x=L$, while $v$ and $v'$ vanish at $x=0$. Function $cx^2(3L-x)$ has zero fourth derivative and corresponds to a tip force. The remaining functions have $v'(0)\neq0$ and violate the fixed-end condition.
