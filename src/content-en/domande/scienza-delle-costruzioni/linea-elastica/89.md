A simply supported beam of constant flexural rigidity carries a uniform load over its full span. At which sections is the magnitude of the slope $|v'|$ greatest?

- At the supports
- At midspan
- At one quarter and three quarters of the span
- The slope is the same at every section

## Explanation

Inside the beam the moment is positive, so $v''=-\dfrac{M}{EI}<0$ and the slope $v'$ decreases strictly from $v'(0)>0$ to $v'(L)<0$. A monotone function on a closed interval attains its extreme values at the endpoints. The slope magnitude is therefore greatest **at the supports**, where it equals $\dfrac{qL^3}{24EI}$. By symmetry the slope is zero at midspan.
