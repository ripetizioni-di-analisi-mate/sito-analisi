Horizontal beam $AB$ has length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and is pinned to a wall at $A$. End $B$ is supported by a tie $BD$ pinned to the wall at point $D$, a distance $3\,\mathrm{m}$ above $A$. A downward force $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $B$. Which internal resultants are nonzero in beam $AB$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;D,0,3" aste="A-B" catene="B-D" vincoli="A,cerniera,180;D,cerniera,180" forze="B,0,-1,12 kN" quote="A,B,4 m,0.8;A,D,3 m,0.8" titolo="Beam supported by a tie"}

- Only axial force, equal to $-16\,\mathrm{kN}$
- Only axial force, equal to $-20\,\mathrm{kN}$
- An axial force of $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ and a shear force of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- Only bending moment, equal to $-48\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at $A$

## Explanation

The tie runs from $B$ to $D$, with components proportional to $4$ horizontally and $3$ vertically over length $5\,\mathrm{m}$. Taking moments about $A$ gives $\dfrac{3}{5}S\cdot 4=12\cdot 4$, hence $S=20\,\mathrm{kN}$.

At $B$ the tie pulls $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left and $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. Its vertical component balances the applied force exactly, leaving only a leftward horizontal force $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ between the cut and $B$.

This force acts along the axis, so $T=0$ and $M=0$ at every section. The axial force is $N=-16\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the beam carries **compression only**.
