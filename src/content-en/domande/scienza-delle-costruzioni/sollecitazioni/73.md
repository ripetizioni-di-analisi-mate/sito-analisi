A cantilever frame consists of a vertical column $AB$ fixed at $A$ and a horizontal crossbeam $BC$ of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ extending to the right. They meet at an unloaded rigid joint $B$. A downward force $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ and a rightward force $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ act at $C$. The members are oriented from $A$ to $B$ and from $B$ to $C$. Which statement about the end resultants of the two members at joint $B$ is true?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,3,4" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,incastro" forze="C,0,-1,8 kN" frecce="3,4,4.1,4,6 kN" quote="B,C,3 m,-0.8" titolo="Cantilever frame with two tip forces"}

- The moment is $-24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in both members, $N_{AB}=-8\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $T_{BC}=8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- The moment is $-24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in both members, $N_{AB}=6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $T_{BC}=8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- The moment is zero in both members, $N_{AB}=-8\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $T_{BC}=8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- The moment is $-24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in the crossbeam and zero in the column

## Explanation

For either member isolate the portion between the cut near $B$ and tip $C$. The external forces on that portion are the same, so their moment about $B$ is the same.

The vertical force has arm $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and produces clockwise moment $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, while the horizontal force passes through $B$. Thus $M=-24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at the end of $AB$ and the beginning of $BC$.

In the crossbeam the force $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ is transverse and gives $T_{BC}=8\,\mathrm{kN}$. In the column the same force is axial and opposite to $s$, giving $N_{AB}=-8\,\mathrm{kN}$. At the unloaded rigid joint **shear in one member becomes axial force in the other** because the members are perpendicular.
