The horizontal beam is $8\,\mathrm{m}$ long. It has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$, a distance $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Segment $BC$ is the overhang. Measure coordinate $x$ to the right from $A$. At $C$, a distance $8\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, force $P$ acts downward with magnitude $14\,\mathrm{kN}$. Find the signed value of $M(6)$ at section $x=6\,\mathrm{m}$. Positive bending moment puts the bottom fibers in tension.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,8,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,B,6 m;B,C,2 m" forze="C,0,-1.68,P" titolo="Beam supports, loads, and dimensions"}

- $28\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-14\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-84\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-28\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

Isolating the overhang, the force of $14\,\mathrm{kN}$ has moment arm $2\,\mathrm{m}$. It puts the top fibers in tension at section $B$, giving $M(6)=-14\cdot2$.
