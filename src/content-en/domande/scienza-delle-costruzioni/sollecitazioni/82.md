On a horizontal simply supported beam of span $8\,\mathrm{m}$, a downward force $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ can act at any section a distance $a$ from left support $A$. For which $a$ is the bending moment at the point of application largest, and what is its value?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2.6,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1.2,20 kN" quote="A,C,a,0.9;A,B,8 m,1.6" titolo="Simply supported beam with a point force at a variable position"}

- $a=2\,\mathrm{m}$, with $M=30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $a=4\,\mathrm{m}$, with $M=40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $a=4\,\mathrm{m}$, with $M=80\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $a=4\,\mathrm{m}$, with $M=20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

With the force at $x=a$, taking moments about $B$ gives $V_A=\dfrac{20\,(8-a)}{8}$. At the load section the left segment carries only $V_A$ with moment arm $a$, so
$$
M(a)=\frac{20\,a\,(8-a)}{8}.
$$
The product $a(8-a)$ is a quadratic with its vertex at $a=4\,\mathrm{m}$. The moment under the force is therefore largest when the force acts at midspan.

This gives $M(4)=\dfrac{20\cdot 4\cdot 4}{8}=40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, or $\dfrac{PL}{4}$. A force at distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$ would give only $30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
