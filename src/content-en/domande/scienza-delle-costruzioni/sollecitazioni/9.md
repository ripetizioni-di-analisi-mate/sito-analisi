The horizontal beam $AB$ is fixed at $A$ and has length $7\,\mathrm{m}$. Measure coordinate $x$ to the right from $A$. At $C$, a distance $5\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, force $P_1$ acts downward with magnitude $12\,\mathrm{kN}$. At $B$, a distance $7\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, force $P_2$ acts downward with magnitude $8\,\mathrm{kN}$. Find the signed value of $T(6)$ at section $x=6\,\mathrm{m}$. Positive shear tends to rotate a beam element clockwise.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,5,0;B,7,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="A,C,5 m;C,B,2 m" forze="C,0,-1.47,P_1;B,0,-1.47,P_2" titolo="Beam supports, loads, and dimensions"}

- $-8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The only vertical force to the right of the section acts at $B$. Its magnitude is $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ and vertical equilibrium of the right segment gives $T(6)=8\,\mathrm{kN}$.
