The horizontal beam is $6\,\mathrm{m}$ long. It has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$, a distance $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Measure coordinate $x$ to the right from $A$. On segment $AB$ between $x=0\,\mathrm{m}$ and $x=6\,\mathrm{m}$, a downward load varies linearly in intensity from $0\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ to $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Find the signed value of $T(2)$ at section $x=2\,\mathrm{m}$. Positive shear tends to rotate a beam element clockwise.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,B,6 m" carichi="A,B,0.0,0.6,0→6 kN/m" titolo="Beam supports, loads, and dimensions"}

- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The total resultant $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, giving $V_A=6\,\mathrm{kN}$. Over the first two meters the triangular load has resultant $2\,\mathrm{kN}$, so $T(2)=6-2$.
