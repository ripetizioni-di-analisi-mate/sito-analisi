A simply supported $6\,\mathrm{m}$ beam carries a load per unit length that increases linearly from zero on the left to $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on the right. Without calculating the reactions, what is the shape of the shear diagram within the span, given $T'=-q$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0,1.3,6 kN/m" quote="A,B,6 m" titolo="Triangular loading along the span"}

- A straight line of constant negative slope.
- A cubic with positive slope at the right end.
- A parabola with a horizontal tangent at the right end.
- A parabola with a horizontal tangent at the left end.

## Explanation

Since $q$ is linear, its integral is quadratic. The slope of $T$ starts at zero on the left and becomes negative toward the right. The reactions determine the initial ordinate of the diagram.
