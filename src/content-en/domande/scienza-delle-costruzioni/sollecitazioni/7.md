The horizontal beam is $6\,\mathrm{m}$ long. It has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$, a distance $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. The coordinate $x$ is measured to the right from $A$. On segment $AB$ between $x=0\,\mathrm{m}$ and $x=6\,\mathrm{m}$, a downward load varies linearly in intensity from $0\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ to $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Positive shear tends to rotate a beam element clockwise. What is the signed value of $T(2)$ at section $x=2\,\mathrm{m}$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,B,6 m" carichi="A,B,0.0,0.6,0→6 kN/m" titolo="Beam supports, loads, and dimensions"}

- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The triangular load has resultant $\dfrac{6\cdot6}{2}=18\,\mathrm{kN}$, applied at two thirds of the span from $A$, that is at $x=4\,\mathrm{m}$, where the intensity is larger.

Moment equilibrium about $B$ gives $6V_A-18\cdot2=0$, so $V_A=6\,\mathrm{kN}$.

Between $x=0$ and $x=2\,\mathrm{m}$ the intensity grows from $0$ to $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, so the load on that segment has resultant $\dfrac{2\cdot2}{2}=2\,\mathrm{kN}$.

Positive shear acts downward on the right face of the part to the left of the section, so vertical equilibrium of that part gives $V_A-2-T(2)=0$.

Hence $T(2)=6-2=4\,\mathrm{kN}$.
