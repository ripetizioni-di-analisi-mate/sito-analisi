On a beam segment, $M(x)=12x-4x^{2}-3x^{3}$, with $x$ in meters and $M$ in $\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Use $T=\dfrac{dM}{dx}$ and take the load intensity $q$ as positive downward. Find $q$ at $x=2\,\mathrm{m}$.

- $-44\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $26\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $40\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $44\,\mathrm{kN/m}$

## Explanation

On an interval with no point forces, $\dfrac{dT}{dx}=-q$, so $q=-M''$. Differentiating twice gives $q(x)=8+18x$. At $x=2\,\mathrm{m}$ the result is $44\,\mathrm{kN/m}$.
