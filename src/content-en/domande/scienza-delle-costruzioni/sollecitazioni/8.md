The horizontal beam is $6\,\mathrm{m}$ long. It has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$, a distance $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Measure coordinate $x$ to the right from $A$. On segment $AB$ between $x=0\,\mathrm{m}$ and $x=6\,\mathrm{m}$, a downward load varies linearly in intensity from $3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ to $9\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Find the signed value of $M(3)$ at section $x=3\,\mathrm{m}$. Positive bending moment puts the bottom fibers in tension.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,B,6 m" carichi="A,B,0.2,0.6,3→9 kN/m" titolo="Beam supports, loads, and dimensions"}

- $27\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $45\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-27\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

Overall equilibrium gives $V_A=15\,\mathrm{kN}$. On the left segment the rectangular load contributes moment $9\cdot1.5$ and the triangular load $4.5\cdot1$, giving $M(3)=45-13.5-4.5$.
