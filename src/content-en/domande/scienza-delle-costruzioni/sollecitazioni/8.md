The horizontal beam is $6\,\mathrm{m}$ long. It has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$, a distance $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. The coordinate $x$ is measured to the right from $A$. On segment $AB$ between $x=0\,\mathrm{m}$ and $x=6\,\mathrm{m}$, a downward load varies linearly in intensity from $3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ to $9\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Positive bending moment puts the bottom fibers in tension. What is the signed value of $M(3)$ at section $x=3\,\mathrm{m}$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,B,6 m" carichi="A,B,0.2,0.6,3→9 kN/m" titolo="Beam supports, loads, and dimensions"}

- $27\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $45\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-27\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The trapezoidal load is split into a rectangle of $3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, with resultant $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $x=3\,\mathrm{m}$, and a triangle growing from $0$ to $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, with resultant $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $x=4\,\mathrm{m}$.

Moment equilibrium about $B$ gives $6V_A=18\cdot3+18\cdot2$, so $V_A=15\,\mathrm{kN}$.

On the segment between $x=0$ and $x=3\,\mathrm{m}$ the rectangle has resultant $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ with lever arm $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ from the section.

On the same segment the triangle reaches $3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$, so it has resultant $4.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ with lever arm $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from the section.

Therefore $M(3)=15\cdot3-9\cdot1.5-4.5\cdot1=27\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, positive because it puts the bottom fibers in tension.
