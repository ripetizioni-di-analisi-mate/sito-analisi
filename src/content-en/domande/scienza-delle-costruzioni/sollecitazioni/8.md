The simply supported $6\,\mathrm{m}$ beam shown carries a downward load varying linearly from $3$ to $9\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Which uniform load over the full span would give the same midspan bending moment?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,B,6 m" carichi="A,B,0.2,0.6,3→9 kN/m" titolo="Beam supports, loads, and dimensions"}

- $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN/m}$

## Explanation

The trapezoid splits into a uniform $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ load and a part antisymmetric about midspan. By symmetry of the simply supported beam, paired contributions from the antisymmetric part cancel in the midspan moment.
