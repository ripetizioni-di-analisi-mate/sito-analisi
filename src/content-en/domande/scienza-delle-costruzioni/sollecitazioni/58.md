Horizontal beam $AB$ of length $8\,\mathrm{m}$ has a pin at $A$ and a roller at $B$. A uniform downward load $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts only on its left half between $x=0$ and $x=4\,\mathrm{m}$. Find the maximum bending moment.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,C,0.6,0.6,4 kN/m" quote="A,C,4 m,0.9;C,B,4 m,0.9" titolo="Simply supported beam loaded over half the span"}

- $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $32\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The resultant is $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ and acts at $x=2\,\mathrm{m}$. Taking moments about $B$ gives $8\,V_A=16\cdot 6$, hence $V_A=12\,\mathrm{kN}$.

Under the load $T(x)=12-4x$, which vanishes at $x=3\,\mathrm{m}$. Shear changes from positive to negative, so the bending moment is largest there.

The maximum is $M(3)=12\cdot 3-2\cdot 3^2=18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. At the end of the loaded interval, $x=4\,\mathrm{m}$, the moment is $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ because the shear is already negative between $3$ and $4\,\mathrm{m}$.
