A Gerber beam is fixed at $A$ ($x=0$), has an internal hinge at $C$ ($x=3\,\mathrm{m}$), and rests on a roller at $B$ ($x=6\,\mathrm{m}$). Its only load is a downward force $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ applied directly at hinge $C$. Find the bending moment immediately to the right of the fixed support, taking bottom-fiber tension as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,6,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1,12 kN" quote="A,C,3 m,0.9;C,B,3 m,0.9" titolo="Gerber beam with a force at the hinge"}

- $-36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-72\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

Isolate segment $CB$. Apart from its hinge force, only reaction $V_B$ acts on it because the external force applied at the hinge can be assigned to segment $AC$.

Moment at $C$ vanishes, so taking moments of $CB$ about $C$ gives $3\,V_B=0$. Thus $V_B=0$ and the roller is unloaded.

Cantilever $AC$ carries the entire force with moment arm $3\,\mathrm{m}$ about its fixed end. The result is $M(0^+)=-12\cdot 3=-36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with top fibers in tension.
