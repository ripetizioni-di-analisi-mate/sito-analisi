A straight inclined beam has a pin at lower end $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at upper end $B$. In projection $B$ lies $4\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right of and $3\,\mathrm{m}$ above $A$. A uniform downward load of $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts per meter of horizontal projection. Find the maximum bending moment.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,3" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="0 3.6,4 3.6,0.5,0.5,6 kN/m" quote="A,4 0,4 m,0.6;4 0,B,3 m,0.6" titolo="Inclined beam with a load per unit horizontal projection"}

- $18.75\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $9.6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The roller reaction at $B$ is vertical, as is the load, so the reaction at $A$ must also be vertical. The load resultant is $6\cdot 4=24\,\mathrm{kN}$ and symmetry gives $V_A=V_B=12\,\mathrm{kN}$.

For a cut at horizontal distance $x$ from $A$ the vertical forces have horizontal moment arms, giving $M=12x-3x^2$. This is the expression for a horizontal beam of span $4\,\mathrm{m}$.

The maximum occurs at $x=2\,\mathrm{m}$ and equals $\dfrac{6\cdot 4^2}{8}=12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Inclination changes axial force and shear, while the moment depends only on horizontal span.
