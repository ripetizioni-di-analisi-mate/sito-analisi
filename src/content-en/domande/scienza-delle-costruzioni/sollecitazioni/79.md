A vertical retaining wall $AB$ of height $3\,\mathrm{m}$ is fixed at its base $A$ and holds water up to its top $B$. For unit width the water pressure gives a horizontal load increasing linearly from $0$ at $B$ to $30\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $A$. Find the base bending moment magnitude per meter of width.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro" carichi="A,B,1.2,0,30 kN/m,180" quote="A,B,3 m,-0.8" titolo="Retaining wall under water pressure"}

- $67.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $90\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $45\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $135\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The pressure diagram is triangular with bottom intensity $30\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at the base and height $3\,\mathrm{m}$. Its resultant is $\dfrac{30\cdot 3}{2}=45\,\mathrm{kN}$.

A triangular load resultant passes through the triangle centroid, one third of the height from the larger side. It therefore acts $1\,\mathrm{m}$ above the base.

Cut immediately above the base and isolate the wall. The horizontal force has moment arm equal to elevation $1\,\mathrm{m}$, so the moment magnitude is $45\cdot 1=45\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The fibers on the water side are in tension.
