Vertical column $AB$ of height $4\,\mathrm{m}$ is fixed at its base $A$. Its self-weight $3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ is distributed along the axis. A downward force $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at its top $B$. Find the axial force immediately above the base, taking tension as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro" forze="B,0,-1,20 kN" quote="A,B,4 m,-0.8" titolo="Column with self-weight and a top load"}

- $-20\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-26\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-32\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $32\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Orient the column axis $s$ from $A$ to $B$. Cut just above the base and isolate the upper portion, including all self-weight and the top load.

The weight has downward resultant $3\cdot 4=12\,\mathrm{kN}$. The axial loads on the upper segment therefore total $12+20=32\,\mathrm{kN}$ opposite to the $s$ direction.

Equilibrium requires an upward force $32\,\mathrm{kN}$ on the cut face, directed into the isolated segment. Thus $N=-32\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the base is in **compression**. The equation $N'=-p$ confirms that $N$ increases by $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ per meter upward, reaching $-20\,\mathrm{kN}$ at the top.
