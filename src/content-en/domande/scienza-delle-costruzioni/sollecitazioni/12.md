A beam supported at $A$ and $B$ continues into a $3\,\mathrm{m}$ overhang $BC$ carrying a downward $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ load. Section $S$ is one metre to the right of $B$. An additional downward $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ force is applied in span $AB$. How does the shear at $S$ change?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;S,5,0;C,7,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="B,C,0.9,0.9,6 kN/m" forze="2 0,0,-1.3,10 kN" quote="B,S,1 m;S,C,2 m" titolo="Section S on the loaded overhang"}

- It stays the same.
- It increases by $10\,\mathrm{kN}$.
- It decreases by $10\,\mathrm{kN}$.
- It becomes zero.

## Explanation

Isolating the segment from $S$ to free end $C$ leaves the same two metres of distributed load. Actions left of $S$ can change the support reactions while leaving equilibrium of segment $SC$ unchanged.
