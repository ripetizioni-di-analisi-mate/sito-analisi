The horizontal beam is $10\,\mathrm{m}$ long. It has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$, a distance $7\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Segment $BC$ is the overhang. The coordinate $x$ is measured to the right from $A$. On segment $BC$ between $x=7\,\mathrm{m}$ and $x=10\,\mathrm{m}$, a uniform downward load of intensity $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts. Positive shear tends to rotate a beam element clockwise. What is the signed value of $T(8)$ at section $x=8\,\mathrm{m}$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,7,0;C,10,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,B,7 m;B,C,3 m" carichi="B,C,1.0,1.0,6 kN/m" titolo="Beam supports, loads, and dimensions"}

- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $18\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The section $x=8\,\mathrm{m}$ lies on the overhang, so the part to the right of the section is the free segment, $2\,\mathrm{m}$ long, that ends at $C$ and contains no reactions.

On that segment the uniform load has resultant $6\cdot2=12\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward.

Positive shear tends to rotate the element clockwise, so it acts upward on the left face of the right part.

Vertical equilibrium of the right part therefore gives $T(8)-12=0$, so $T(8)=12\,\mathrm{kN}$.
