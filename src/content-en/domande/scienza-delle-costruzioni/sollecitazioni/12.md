The horizontal beam is $10\,\mathrm{m}$ long. It has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$, a distance $7\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Segment $BC$ is the overhang. Measure coordinate $x$ to the right from $A$. On segment $BC$ between $x=7\,\mathrm{m}$ and $x=10\,\mathrm{m}$, a uniform downward load of intensity $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts. Find the signed value of $T(8)$ at section $x=8\,\mathrm{m}$. Positive shear tends to rotate a beam element clockwise.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,7,0;C,10,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,B,7 m;B,C,3 m" carichi="B,C,1.0,1.0,6 kN/m" titolo="Beam supports, loads, and dimensions"}

- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $18\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The length to the right of the shear force is $2\,\mathrm{m}$. The load there has a downward resultant of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$, balanced by positive shear.
