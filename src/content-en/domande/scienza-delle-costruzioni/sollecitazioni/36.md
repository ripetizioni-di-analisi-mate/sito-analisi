Horizontal beam $AB$ of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$ carries a uniform downward load $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over the entire span. There is a pin at $A$ and a roller at $B$ on a plane inclined at $45^\circ$. Its reaction acts upward and to the left, at an angle $45^\circ$ to the horizontal. Find the axial force in the beam, taking tension as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,315" carichi="A,B,0.6,0.6,4 kN/m" quote="A,B,6 m,0.9" titolo="Beam with a roller on an inclined surface"}

- $-12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-12\sqrt2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The horizontal reaction component at $B$ acts along the beam axis and has zero moment about $A$. Taking moments about $A$ gives $6\,V_B=24\cdot 3$, hence $V_B=12\,\mathrm{kN}$.

The reaction is inclined at $45^\circ$, so its horizontal component has the same magnitude as the vertical component and is $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left. Horizontal equilibrium then requires $H_A=12\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right.

At every section the only horizontal force on the left segment is $H_A$, giving $N=-H_A=-12\,\mathrm{kN}$. The beam is in **compression** with constant axial force over the entire span.
