Horizontal cantilever $AB$ is fixed at $A$ and has length $5\,\mathrm{m}$, with $x$ measured from $A$. The shear force is $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ between $x=0$ and $x=2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ between $x=2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $x=5\,\mathrm{m}$. A clockwise couple $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ also acts at free end $B$. Find the bending moment immediately to the right of the fixed support, taking bottom-fiber tension as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" diagramma="0,0,2,0,10;2,0,5,0,4" segni=1 titolo="Cantilever shear force diagram in kilonewtons"}

- $-26\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-32\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $38\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-38\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

At free end $B$ the bending moment is zero just outside the beam. A counterclockwise couple $\mathcal M$ produces a jump $M(B^+)-M(B^-)=-\mathcal M$. The applied couple is clockwise, so $\mathcal M=-6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and $M(B^-)=-6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

On smooth intervals the change in $M$ equals the signed area under the shear diagram. Between $A$ and $B$ that area is $10\cdot 2+4\cdot 3=32\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, giving
$$
M(A^+)=M(B^-)-32=-6-32=-38\,\mathrm{kN\,m}.
$$
The top fibers at the fixed end are in **tension**. The clockwise tip couple adds to the effect of the downward forces.
