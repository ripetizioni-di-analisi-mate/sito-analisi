In a straight bar with $x$ positive to the right, a leftward $7\,\mathrm{kN}$ force acts at interior point $C$ and a rightward $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ force at free end $B$. What is the jump $N(C^+)-N(C^-)$ with tension positive?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" forze="C,-1.3,0;B,1.6,0" etichette="3.5,0.55,7 kN;5.5,-0.55,12 kN" titolo="Concentrated forces along the bar"}

- $-7\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $+12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $+7\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $+5\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Immediately to the right of $C$ the axial force is $12\,\mathrm{kN}$. Immediately to the left the leftward point force must also be included, giving $N=12-7=5\,\mathrm{kN}$. The jump is $12-5=7\,\mathrm{kN}$, opposite to the signed component of the applied force at $C$.
