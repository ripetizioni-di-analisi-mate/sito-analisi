The horizontal beam $AB$ is fixed at $A$ and has length $6\,\mathrm{m}$. The coordinate $x$ is measured to the right from $A$. At $C$, a distance $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, axial force $F_1$ acts to the left with magnitude $7\,\mathrm{kN}$. At $B$, a distance $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, axial force $F_2$ acts to the right with magnitude $12\,\mathrm{kN}$. Positive axial force denotes tension. What is the signed value of $N(5)$ at section $x=5\,\mathrm{m}$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" quote="A,C,3 m;C,B,3 m" forze="C,-0.87,0,F_1;B,1.2,0,F_2" titolo="Beam supports, loads, and dimensions"}

- $-12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $7\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The part of the beam to the right of the section $x=5\,\mathrm{m}$ contains only the free end $B$, so the reactions of the fixed support do not act on it.

Force $F_1$ is applied at $x=3\,\mathrm{m}$, to the left of the section, so only $F_2$, of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right, acts on the right part.

Positive axial force is tensile, so on the left face of the right part it acts to the left.

Horizontal equilibrium of the right part gives $12-N(5)=0$, so $N(5)=12\,\mathrm{kN}$, in tension.
