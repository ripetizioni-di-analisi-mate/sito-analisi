The horizontal beam has length $9\,\mathrm{m}$, with a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$, a distance $9\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. The coordinate $x$ increases to the right from $A$. Force $P$ has magnitude $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ and acts downward at $C$, a distance $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Find the signed value of $T(5)$ at section $x=5\,\mathrm{m}$. Positive shear tends to rotate a beam element clockwise.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,9,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,C,3 m;C,B,6 m" forze="C,0,-1.89,P" titolo="Beam supports, loads, and dimensions"}

- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-18\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Taking moments about $A$ gives $9V_B=18\cdot3$, hence $V_B=6\,\mathrm{kN}$. This reaction is the only force to the right of the section, so the shear force is $-V_B$.
