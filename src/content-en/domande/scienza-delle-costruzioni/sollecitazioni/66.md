Horizontal cantilever $AB$ is fixed at $A$ and has length $5\,\mathrm{m}$, with $x$ measured from $A$. A uniform downward load $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts only between $x=0$ and $x=2\,\mathrm{m}$. Find the bending moment at section $x=3\,\mathrm{m}$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,5,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" carichi="A,C,0.6,0.6,6 kN/m" punti="3,0,S" quote="A,C,2 m,0.9;C,B,3 m,0.9" titolo="Cantilever with a load near its fixed end"}

- $-24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-3\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

Cut at $x=3\,\mathrm{m}$ and isolate the segment toward free end $B$. This segment carries neither forces nor couples because the load ends at $x=2\,\mathrm{m}$.

Equilibrium therefore requires $N=T=M=0$ at the cut. The bending moment at $x=3\,\mathrm{m}$ is **zero**.

The entire segment between $x=2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $B$ is unloaded. The moment becomes nonzero only upon entering the loaded interval and reaches $-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at the fixed end.
