A curved member of radius $3\,\mathrm{m}$ forms a quarter circle centered at $O$. Its end $A$ lies at the elevation of $O$, a distance $3\,\mathrm{m}$ to its right, and is fixed. Free end $B$ lies a distance $3\,\mathrm{m}$ above $O$ and is loaded by a downward force $10\,\mathrm{kN}$. Find the bending moment magnitude at section $S$ of the arc, where the radius makes an angle $45^\circ$.

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,3,0;P1,2.898,0.776,-;P2,2.598,1.5,-;S,2.121,2.121;P4,1.5,2.598,-;P5,0.776,2.898,-;B,0,3" aste="A-P1;P1-P2;P2-S;S-P4;P4-P5;P5-B" vincoli="A,incastro" forze="B,0,-1,10 kN" tratteggi="0,0 2.121,2.121;0,0 3,0" quote="O,A,3 m,0.7" titolo="Quarter-circle cantilever with a tip force"}

- $30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $30\sqrt2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $15\sqrt2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

Isolate the arc from $S$ to tip $B$. Its only force is vertical, so the moment arm about $S$ is the horizontal distance between $S$ and $B$.

Relative to $O$, point $S$ has horizontal coordinate $3\cos 45^\circ=\dfrac{3\sqrt2}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$, while $B$ has coordinate zero. The moment arm is therefore $\dfrac{3\sqrt2}{2}\,\mathrm{m}$.

Thus $|M_S|=10\cdot\dfrac{3\sqrt2}{2}=15\sqrt2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}\approx 21.2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. At the fixed support the arm is $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and the moment magnitude reaches $30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
