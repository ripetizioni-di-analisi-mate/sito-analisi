Horizontal beam $AB$ of length $10\,\mathrm{m}$ has a pin at $A$ and a roller at $B$. It carries a uniform downward load $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ from $x=0$ to $x=4\,\mathrm{m}$, a downward force $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $x=6\,\mathrm{m}$, and a counterclockwise couple $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at $x=8\,\mathrm{m}$. At which interior section does the bending moment diagram jump?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,0;D,6,0;E,8,0;B,10,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,C,0.5,0.5,2 kN/m" forze="D,0,-1,10 kN" coppie="E,1,8 kN m" quote="A,C,4 m,0.9;C,D,2 m,0.9;D,E,2 m,0.9;E,B,2 m,0.9" titolo="Beam with several types of loading"}

- At $x=4\,\mathrm{m}$, where the distributed load ends
- At $x=6\,\mathrm{m}$, where the point force acts
- At $x=8\,\mathrm{m}$, where the couple acts
- At no interior section

## Explanation

The moment jump at a section is $M(a^+)-M(a^-)=-\mathcal M$, so a nonzero jump requires an applied concentrated couple.

At $x=8\,\mathrm{m}$ the counterclockwise couple $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ therefore produces a jump $-8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. At $x=6\,\mathrm{m}$ the point force produces a shear jump, so the $M$ diagram is continuous with a change in slope.

At $x=4\,\mathrm{m}$ the distributed load changes without a concentrated action. Shear is continuous there, so the moment and its first derivative are continuous.
