A simply supported beam of length $8\,\mathrm{m}$ carries a uniform downward load $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over the whole span. At a distance $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from the left support, a counterclockwise couple $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is applied. Find the bending moments immediately to either side of the couple, taking $M$ as positive when the bottom fibers are in tension.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" titolo="Uniform load and concentrated couple" carichi="0 0,8 0,0.96,0.96,4 kN/m,270" coppie="3 0,1,16 kN·m" quote="A,B,8 m,1.13"}

- $36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ on the left and $52\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ on the right
- $36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ on the left and $36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ on the right
- $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ on the left and $36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ on the right
- $36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ on the left and $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ on the right

## Explanation

The left reaction is $\dfrac{qL}{2}+\dfrac{C}{L}=18\,\mathrm{kN}$, with $C$ positive counterclockwise. The moment immediately to the left is $A_{y}\times 3-4\times\dfrac{3^{2}}{2}=36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Crossing the couple subtracts $C$, giving $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
