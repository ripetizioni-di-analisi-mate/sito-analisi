Horizontal beam $AB$ has length $4\,\mathrm{m}$. At $A$ a pair of horizontal parallel links restrains horizontal translation and rotation while allowing vertical translation. It rests on a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. A uniform downward load $3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts over the full span. Find the bending moment immediately to the right of $A$, taking bottom-fiber tension as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,doppio-pendolo,180;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,3 kN/m" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9" titolo="Beam with a pair of parallel links and a roller"}

- $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The parallel links exert no vertical reaction, so the roller carries the whole load and $V_B=12\,\mathrm{kN}$.

Moment equilibrium about $A$ requires a support couple $M_A$, with $M_A+12\cdot 4-12\cdot 2=0$. Thus $M_A=-24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, a clockwise couple.

Immediately beyond $A$ the left segment carries only this couple, and $M=-M_{\mathrm{left}}$ gives $M(0^+)=24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. At $A$ the shear vanishes. The bending moment diagram has a horizontal tangent and its **maximum** there, with bottom fibers in tension.
