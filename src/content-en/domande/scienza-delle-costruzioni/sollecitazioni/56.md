A straight inclined beam has a pin at lower end $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at upper end $B$. In projection $B$ lies $4\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right of and $3\,\mathrm{m}$ above $A$. A downward force $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at the midpoint. Local axis $s$ runs from $A$ to $B$. Rotating the unit vector of $s$ counterclockwise by a quarter turn gives the positive transverse unit vector. Find the shear force immediately beyond $A$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,1.5;B,4,3" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1,20 kN" quote="A,4 0,4 m,0.6;4 0,B,3 m,0.6" titolo="Inclined beam with a vertical force at its midpoint"}

- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The vertical force acts at the midpoint of the horizontal projection, so $V_A=V_B=10\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $H_A=0$.

Axis $s$ has unit vector $\left(\dfrac{4}{5},\dfrac{3}{5}\right)$ and transverse unit vector $\left(-\dfrac{3}{5},\dfrac{4}{5}\right)$. On the segment preceding the cut, positive $T$ points opposite to the transverse unit vector. Equilibrium therefore makes $T$ equal to the transverse component of the external force.

Reaction $V_A$ has transverse component $10\cdot\dfrac{4}{5}=8\,\mathrm{kN}$, giving $T=8\,\mathrm{kN}$. Its component along $s$ is $10\cdot\dfrac{3}{5}=6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and gives axial force $N=-6\,\mathrm{kN}$.
