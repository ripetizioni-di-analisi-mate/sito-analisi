A horizontal beam has a pin at $A$ ($x=0$), a roller at $B$ ($x=6\,\mathrm{m}$), an internal hinge at $C$ ($x=8\,\mathrm{m}$), and a roller at $D$ ($x=12\,\mathrm{m}$). At $E$ ($x=10\,\mathrm{m}$) it carries a downward force $12\,\mathrm{kN}$. Find the bending moment at support $B$, taking bottom-fiber tension as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,8,0;E,10,0;D,12,0" aste="A-C;C-D" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello;D,carrello" forze="E,0,-1.2,12 kN" quote="A,B,6 m,0.9;B,C,2 m,0.9;C,E,2 m,0.9;E,D,2 m,0.9" titolo="Gerber beam with an overhang and a suspended span"}

- $-24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-48\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The bending moment vanishes at hinge $C$. Taking moments for segment $CD$ about $C$ gives $4\,V_D=12\cdot 2$, hence $V_D=6\,\mathrm{kN}$.

Vertical equilibrium of $CD$ shows the hinge carries the remaining $6\,\mathrm{kN}$. On segment $ABC$ the hinge therefore transmits a downward force $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$.

Cut at $B$ and isolate the portion to the right as far as $C$. Its only force is that transmitted at $C$ with moment arm $2\,\mathrm{m}$, giving $M_B=-6\cdot 2=-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Above support $B$ the top fibers are in **tension**.
