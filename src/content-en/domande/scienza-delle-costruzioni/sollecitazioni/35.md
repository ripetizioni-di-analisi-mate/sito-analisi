A concentrated vertical force acts at an interior beam section at $x=2\,\mathrm{m}$ with no couple at that section. With $T=\dfrac{dM}{dx}$, the shear force limits are $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ on the left and $-4\,\mathrm{kN}$ on the right. How does the bending moment diagram behave at that section?

- No local extremum, with moment increasing through the section
- A local maximum
- A local minimum
- No local extremum; the moment is decreasing locally

## Explanation

The point force leaves $M$ continuous but changes shear. The sign of $T$ gives the slope of $M$ on each side. Here the moment increases and then decreases, so it has a local maximum.
