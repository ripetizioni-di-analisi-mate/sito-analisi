A horizontal cantilever of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ is fixed on the left and carries a uniform downward load $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over its full length. How does the bending moment diagram meet the free end?

- The moment is $-18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ with zero slope
- The moment and its slope are both zero
- The moment is zero and the slope is $12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- The moment is $-18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and the slope is $12\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

No concentrated force or couple acts at the free end. As the cut approaches the tip, the load beyond it tends to zero and both $T$ and $M$ approach zero.

Since $M'=T$ and $T$ vanishes at the tip, the moment diagram reaches the free end with **zero slope**. It is a parabola with its vertex at the tip.

The value $-18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ occurs at the fixed end, where shear is $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the slope has greatest magnitude.
