Horizontal beam $AB$ of length $10\,\mathrm{m}$ has a pin at $A$ and a roller at $B$. It carries a uniform downward load $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over the entire span and a downward point force $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ at midspan. Find the maximum bending moment.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,5,0;B,10,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,2 kN/m" forze="C,0,-1.2,8 kN" quote="A,C,5 m,0.9;C,B,5 m,0.9" titolo="Simply supported beam with uniform loading and a midspan force"}

- $25\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $35\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $65\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $45\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The load is symmetric, so each support carries half the total and $V_A=V_B=\dfrac{20+8}{2}=14\,\mathrm{kN}$.

The shear force is $14-2\cdot 5=4\,\mathrm{kN}$ immediately left of midspan and $4-8=-4\,\mathrm{kN}$ immediately right. It changes from positive to negative at the point force, so the moment reaches its maximum at midspan.

The left segment carries $V_A$ with moment arm $5\,\mathrm{m}$ and a distributed load resultant $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ with moment arm $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$, giving
$$
M_{\max}=14\cdot 5-10\cdot 2.5=45\,\mathrm{kN\,m}.
$$
Adding the separate effects $\dfrac{qL^2}{8}=25\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and $\dfrac{PL}{4}=20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ gives the same result because both load cases have their moment maximum at the same section.
