The horizontal beam is $7\,\mathrm{m}$ long. It has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$, a distance $7\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Measure coordinate $x$ to the right from $A$. At $D$, a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, force $P$ acts downward with magnitude $10\,\mathrm{kN}$. At $C$, a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, axial force $F_1$ acts to the right with magnitude $5\,\mathrm{kN}$. At $E$, a distance $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, axial force $F_2$ acts to the left with magnitude $9\,\mathrm{kN}$. Find the signed value of $N(4)$ at section $x=4\,\mathrm{m}$. Positive axial force denotes tension.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;D,4,0;E,6,0;B,7,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,C,2 m;C,D,2 m;D,E,2 m;E,B,1 m" forze="D,0,-1.47,P;C,1.4,0,F_1;E,-1.68,0,F_2" titolo="Beam supports, loads, and dimensions"}

- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-9\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-5\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

On the isolated right portion the only horizontal component is a force of $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left. The axial force at the shear force is compressive, so it equals $-9\,\mathrm{kN}$.
