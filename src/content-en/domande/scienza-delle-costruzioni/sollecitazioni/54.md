A horizontal beam has a pin at $A$ ($x=0$) and a roller at $B$ ($x=8\,\mathrm{m}$), with an overhang extending to $D$ ($x=12\,\mathrm{m}$). A uniform downward load $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts over the full length. Find the maximum bending moment magnitude and its location.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,8,0;D,12,0" aste="A-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,D,0.6,0.6,2 kN/m" quote="A,B,8 m,0.9;B,D,4 m,0.9" titolo="Overhanging beam under uniform loading"}

- $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at support section $B$
- $9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ a distance $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$
- $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$
- $36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ a distance $6\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$

## Explanation

The resultant load is $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ and acts at $x=6\,\mathrm{m}$. Taking moments about $A$ gives $8\,V_B=24\cdot 6$, so $V_B=18\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_A=6\,\mathrm{kN}$.

In the span $M(x)=6x-x^2$ and $T(x)=6-2x$. Shear vanishes at $x=3\,\mathrm{m}$, where the maximum positive moment is $18-9=9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

At section $B$ the right segment carries the overhang load, $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ with moment arm $2\,\mathrm{m}$, giving $M_B=-16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Comparing magnitudes gives the maximum of $|M|$, **$16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ above support $B$**, with top fibers in tension.
