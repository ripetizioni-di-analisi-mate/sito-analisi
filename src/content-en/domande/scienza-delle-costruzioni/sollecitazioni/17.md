A cantilever of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$ is fixed at the left. A uniform downward load $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts over its full length and an upward force $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at the tip. How far from the fixed end does the bending moment change sign inside the cantilever?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" titolo="Uniform load and opposing tip force" forze="6 0,0,1.2,6 kN" carichi="0 0,6 0,0.8,0.8,4 kN/m,270" quote="A,B,6 m,0.85"}

- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4.5\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Let $d$ be the distance from the section to the tip. On the right-hand free body the two moments are opposite and cancel when $6d=\dfrac{4d^{2}}{2}$. The interior root is $d=3\,\mathrm{m}$. The distance from the fixed support is $L-d=3\,\mathrm{m}$.
