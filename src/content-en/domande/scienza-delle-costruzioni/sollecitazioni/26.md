A horizontal beam has a pin at $A$ at coordinate $x=0$ and a bilateral roller at $x=2\,\mathrm{m}$. Its overhang ends at $x=6\,\mathrm{m}$ and carries a counterclockwise tip couple $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ with no forces on the overhang. Find $T$ and $M$ immediately to the left of the tip, taking $M$ as positive for bottom-fiber tension and using $T=\dfrac{dM}{dx}$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,2,0" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" titolo="Couple at the free end of an overhang" coppie="6 0,1,C_0" quote="A,C,2 m,0.85;C,B,4 m,0.85"}

- $T=0\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $M=18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $T=9\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $M=18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $T=0\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $M=-18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $T=0\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $M=0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

Isolate the last portion of the overhang. No forces act, so $T=0$. A positive moment is clockwise on its left face. The moment balance is $-M+(18)=0$, giving $M=18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
