Horizontal beam $AB$ of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$ has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. At $C$, a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, an inclined force $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ has components $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left and $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. Find the axial force on segment $AC$, taking tension as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,-0.6,-0.8,10 kN" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,4 m" titolo="Simply supported beam with an inclined force at C"}

- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-10\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The roller at $B$ supplies only a vertical reaction, so $H_A$ is the only horizontal reaction. Horizontal equilibrium gives $H_A-6=0$, hence $H_A=6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right.

Cut between $A$ and $C$ and isolate the left segment. Its only horizontal force is $H_A$, so $F_{x,\mathrm{left}}=6\,\mathrm{kN}$. Since $N=-F_{x,\mathrm{left}}$, the result is $N=-6\,\mathrm{kN}$.

Segment $AC$ is therefore in **compression** because the horizontal force component pushes toward the pin. On segment $CB$ the right-hand portion is free of horizontal forces and the axial force vanishes.
