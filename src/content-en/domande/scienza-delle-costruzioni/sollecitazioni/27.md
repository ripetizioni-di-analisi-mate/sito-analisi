A straight bar is fixed at its lower end $A$. From $A$ to its tip $B$ it rises by $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and extends $4\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right. At $B$ a force of $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right and a force of $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward. Find the internal axial force, taking tension as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,3" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro" titolo="Inclined bar with two tip forces" forze="B,1.0,0,15 kN;B,0,-1.0,10 kN"}

- $-6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $18\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $1\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The unit vector from $A$ to $B$ is $(\dfrac{4}{5},\dfrac{3}{5})$. Taking tension as positive, the axial force is the component of the resultant load along this unit vector. It equals $N=\dfrac{15\times 4-10\times 3}{5}=6\,\mathrm{kN}$.
