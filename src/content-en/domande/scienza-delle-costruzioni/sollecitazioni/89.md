Horizontal beam $AB$ of length $8\,\mathrm{m}$ has a pin at $A$ and a roller at $B$. The shear force is $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ between $x=0$ and $x=4\,\mathrm{m}$ and $-3\,\mathrm{kN}$ between $x=4\,\mathrm{m}$ and $x=8\,\mathrm{m}$. A concentrated couple is also applied at $x=6\,\mathrm{m}$. Find that couple.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,0;D,6,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" diagramma="0,0,4,0,5;4,0,8,0,-3" segni=1 titolo="Shear force diagram in kilonewtons"}

- $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise
- $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ clockwise
- $32\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ counterclockwise
- $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ clockwise

## Explanation

No couples act at supports $A$ and $B$, so both end moments are zero. The total change in $M$, equal to the signed shear diagram area plus any jumps from couples, must vanish.

The signed shear area is $5\cdot 4-3\cdot 4=8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, so the jump at $x=6\,\mathrm{m}$ must be $-8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

Since $M(a^+)-M(a^-)=-\mathcal M$, the result is $\mathcal M=8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, a **counterclockwise couple**. The moment changes from $14\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ to $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at $x=6\,\mathrm{m}$ and reaches zero at $B$ with slope $-3\,\mathrm{kN}$.
