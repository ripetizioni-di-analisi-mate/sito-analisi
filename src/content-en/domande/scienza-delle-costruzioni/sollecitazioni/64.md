A balcony is modeled as a horizontal cantilever of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$ fixed to a wall at $A$. It carries a uniform downward load $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. At free end $B$ the parapet transmits a downward force $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ and an outward horizontal force $1.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ applied a distance $1\,\mathrm{m}$ above the cantilever axis. Find the fixed-end bending moment, taking bottom-fiber tension as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,0;P,2,1" aste="A-B;B-P" vincoli="A,incastro,180" carichi="A,B,0.3,0.3,4 kN/m" forze="P,0.8,0,1.5 kN" frecce="2,0,2,-0.9,3 kN" quote="A,B,2 m,1.2;B,P,1 m,0.4" titolo="Balcony and parapet"}

- $-9.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-12.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-23.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-15.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

Isolate the cantilever to the right of the fixed-end section and sum external moments about $A$, taking counterclockwise as positive.

The uniform load has resultant $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ with arm $1\,\mathrm{m}$ and gives moment $-8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The vertical force at $B$ has arm $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and gives $-6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

The outward horizontal force acts $1\,\mathrm{m}$ above the axis and tends to rotate the cantilever clockwise, contributing $-1.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. On the right segment $M$ is the sum of external moments, giving
$$
M_A=-8-6-1.5=-15.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}.
$$
