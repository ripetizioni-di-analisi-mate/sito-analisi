Horizontal bar $AB$ of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$ is fixed at $A$ and free at $B$, with $x$ measured from $A$. A rightward distributed axial load increases linearly from $0$ at $A$ to $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at $B$. Find the axial force at $x=3\,\mathrm{m}$, taking tension as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" frecce="1,0.35,1.17,0.35,;2,0.35,2.33,0.35,;3,0.35,3.5,0.35,;4,0.35,4.67,0.35,;5,0.35,5.83,0.35," etichette="3,0.8,0→4 kN/m" punti="3,0,S" quote="A,B,6 m,0.9" titolo="Bar with a triangular axial load"}

- $3\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Cut at $x=3\,\mathrm{m}$ and isolate the segment toward the free end. It contains no support reactions. The load intensity is $p(x)=\dfrac{2x}{3}$.

The segment load resultant is the area of the trapezoid from $x=3$ to $x=6\,\mathrm{m}$ with parallel sides $2$ and $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$.
$$
\int_3^6 \frac{2x}{3}\,\mathrm dx=\frac{2+4}{2}\cdot 3=9\,\mathrm{kN}.
$$
The force acts to the right, so $F_{x,\mathrm{right}}=9\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $N=F_{x,\mathrm{right}}=9\,\mathrm{kN}$. The section is in **tension** because the load pulls the right segment away from the fixed support.
