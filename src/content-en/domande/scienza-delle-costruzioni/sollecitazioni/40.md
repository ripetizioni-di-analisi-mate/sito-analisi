Horizontal beam $AB$ of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$ has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. At $C$, a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, a rigid vertical arm $CD$ of height $1\,\mathrm{m}$ is welded above the beam. A horizontal force of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right at $D$. Find the beam's bending moment immediately to the right of $C$, taking bottom-fiber tension as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;D,2,1;B,6,0" aste="A-B;C-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="D,1.2,0,12 kN" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,4 m;C,D,1 m,-0.7" titolo="Horizontal force applied to a rigid arm"}

- $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The force at $D$ is equivalent to a rightward force $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ applied at $C$ and a couple of magnitude $12\cdot 1=12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. A rightward force above the axis generates a clockwise couple.

Moment equilibrium about $A$ gives $6\,V_B-12=0$, hence $V_B=2\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. Vertical equilibrium gives $V_A=-2\,\mathrm{kN}$, a downward reaction.

Immediately to the left of $C$, $M=V_A\cdot 2=-4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. A clockwise couple produces a jump in $M$ of $+12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, so immediately to the right of $C$ the result is $M=8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

The right segment confirms the result. Its only reaction $V_B=2\,\mathrm{kN}$ has moment arm $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and gives $M=2\cdot 4=8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
