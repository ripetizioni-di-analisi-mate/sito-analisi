Vertical column $AB$ of height $5\,\mathrm{m}$ is fixed at its base $A$ and carries two rigid horizontal arms at its top. Arm $BC$ extends $2\,\mathrm{m}$ left and carries a downward force $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$. Arm $BD$ extends $3\,\mathrm{m}$ right and carries a downward force $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $D$. Find the bending moment magnitude in the column.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,5;C,-2,5;D,3,5" aste="A-B;C-D" vincoli="A,incastro" forze="C,0,-1,10 kN;D,0,-1,6 kN" quote="C,B,2 m,-0.7;B,D,3 m,-0.7;A,B,5 m,0.8" titolo="Column with two loaded arms"}

- $38\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

Cut the column at any elevation and isolate the upper portion with both arms. The forces are vertical, so their moment arms are horizontal distances from the column axis.

The force at $C$ produces counterclockwise moment $10\cdot 2=20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and the force at $D$ produces clockwise moment $6\cdot 3=18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

Their moments oppose each other, leaving magnitude $20-18=2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. This value is constant throughout the column and the axial force is $-16\,\mathrm{kN}$.
