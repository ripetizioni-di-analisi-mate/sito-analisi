A simply supported beam of length $8\,\mathrm{m}$ carries two downward point loads, each of magnitude $14\,\mathrm{kN}$. Each acts a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from the nearest support. With $M$ positive for bottom-fiber tension and $T=\dfrac{dM}{dx}$, find $T$ and $M$ between the loads.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" titolo="Four-point bending test" forze="2 0,0,-1.6,14 kN;6 0,0,-1.6,14 kN" quote="A,B,8 m,1.13"}

- $T=0\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $M=28\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $T=14\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $M=28\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $T=0\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $M=56\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $T=0\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $M=0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

By symmetry each support reaction is $14\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. Between the two forces, the load and reaction to the left balance vertically, so $T=0$. The moment is constant at $14\times 2=28\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, its value at the first load.
