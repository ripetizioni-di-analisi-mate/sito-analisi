The horizontal beam $AB$ of a canopy has length $6\,\mathrm{m}$ and is pinned to a wall at $A$. It is supported by a pin-ended strut $DC$, with $D$ on the wall a distance $3\,\mathrm{m}$ below $A$ and $C$ on the beam a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. A downward force $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $B$. Find the axial force on beam segment $AC$, taking tension as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,0;B,6,0;D,0,-3" aste="A-B;D-C" vincoli="A,cerniera,180;D,cerniera,180" forze="B,0,-1,10 kN" quote="A,C,4 m,0.8;C,B,2 m,0.8;D,A,3 m,-0.8" titolo="Beam supported by a strut"}

- $20\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $25\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The pin-ended strut has no loads along its length, so its force $S$ on the beam acts from $D$ toward $C$. Its components are proportional to $4$ and $3$ over length $5\,\mathrm{m}$.

Taking moments of the beam about $A$ gives $\dfrac{3}{5}S\cdot 4=10\cdot 6$, hence $S=25\,\mathrm{kN}$. At $C$ the strut pushes the beam $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward.

Horizontal equilibrium requires a leftward reaction $H_A=20\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $A$. On segment $AC$ this is the only horizontal force on the left portion. Since $N=-F_{x,\mathrm{left}}$, the axial force is $N=20\,\mathrm{kN}$.

Segment $AC$ is in tension because the strut pushes the beam away from the wall. On segment $CB$ the axial force vanishes.
