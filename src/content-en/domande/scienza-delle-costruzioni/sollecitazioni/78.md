A three-hinged parabolic arch has springing hinges $A$ and $B$ at the same elevation, spaced $8\,\mathrm{m}$ apart, and a crown hinge $C$ at midspan a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ above the springings. A uniform downward load $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts per meter of horizontal projection. Which statement about the bending moment in the arch is true?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;P1,1,0.875;P2,2,1.5;P3,3,1.875;C,4,2;P5,5,1.875;P6,6,1.5;P7,7,0.875;B,8,0" nomi=0 aste="A-P1;P1-P2;P2-P3;P3-C;C-P5;P5-P6;P6-P7;P7-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" carichi="0 2.6,8 2.6,0.5,0.5,5 kN/m" etichette="-0.4,0.2,A;8.4,0.2,B;4,2.35,C" quote="A,B,8 m,0.8" titolo="Three-hinged parabolic arch"}

- The bending moment is zero only at the three hinges and largest at the midpoint of each half-span
- The bending moment at the crown is $40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- The bending moment is constant and nonzero along the arch
- The bending moment is zero at every section of the arch

## Explanation

Each vertical reaction is $20\,\mathrm{kN}$. Imposing zero moment at $C$ on the left half gives horizontal thrust $H=\dfrac{qL^2}{8f}=\dfrac{5\cdot 64}{16}=20\,\mathrm{kN}$.

At horizontal coordinate $x$ the vertical forces give the simply supported beam moment $\dfrac{qx(L-x)}{2}$. The thrust acts with arm equal to arch height $y=\dfrac{4f\,x(L-x)}{L^2}$, contributing a moment of opposite sign.

Substituting $H$ and $y$ gives $Hy=\dfrac{qx(L-x)}{2}$. The two moments cancel, so the **bending moment is zero at every section**. The parabolic arch axis coincides with the thrust line under uniform loading, leaving only axial compression in every section.
