Isolate the horizontal beam segment to the right of section $S$. On the cut face, the segment to the left exerts a force of $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left, a force of $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward and a clockwise couple of $4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Take $N>0$ as tensile, $T>0$ as tending to rotate a beam element clockwise, and $M>0$ as placing the bottom fibers in tension. What are the internal forces and bending moment at $S$?

::struttura{nodi="S,0,0;B,4,0" aste="S-B" frecce="0,0,-1.1,0,5 kN;0,0,0,1.1,3 kN" coppie="S,-1,4 kN m" titolo="Internal forces and couple on the cut face of the right segment"}

- $N=5\,\mathrm{kN}$, $T=3\,\mathrm{kN}$, $M=4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $N=-5\,\mathrm{kN}$, $T=-3\,\mathrm{kN}$, $M=-4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $N=5\,\mathrm{kN}$, $T=-3\,\mathrm{kN}$, $M=-4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $N=-5\,\mathrm{kN}$, $T=3\,\mathrm{kN}$, $M=4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

On the left face of a right-hand segment, the positive directions are opposite to those on the face of the left segment. Thus positive $N$ points left, positive $T$ points upward, and positive $M$ is clockwise.

The force $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the left, so $N=5\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the section is in tension. The force $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts upward, so $T=3\,\mathrm{kN}$. The couple $4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is clockwise, so $M=4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

The three resultants are **all positive**, even though their arrows point opposite to those on the face of a left-hand segment.
