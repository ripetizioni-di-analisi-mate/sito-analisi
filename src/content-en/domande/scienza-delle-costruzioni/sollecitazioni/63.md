The vertical post $AB$ of a sign has height $5\,\mathrm{m}$ and is fixed at its base $A$. Wind on the upper panel between elevations $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and $5\,\mathrm{m}$ produces a uniform rightward horizontal load $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Find the bending moment magnitude at the base.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,5" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro" carichi="0 3,0 5,0.6,0.6,2 kN/m,0" quote="A,0 3,3 m,0.8;0 3,B,2 m,0.8" titolo="Signpost under wind load"}

- $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $25\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The wind load has resultant $2\cdot 2=4\,\mathrm{kN}$ at the midpoint of the loaded interval, elevation $4\,\mathrm{m}$.

Cut immediately above the base and isolate the post. The moment arm of the horizontal force is its elevation, so the bending moment magnitude is $4\cdot 4=16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

The interval from the base to elevation $3\,\mathrm{m}$ is unloaded. Its shear force is $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ and its bending moment varies linearly from $4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at elevation $3\,\mathrm{m}$ to $16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at the base.
