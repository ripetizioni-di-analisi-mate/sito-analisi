Horizontal cantilever $AB$ is fixed at $A$ and has length $6\,\mathrm{m}$, with $x$ measured from $A$. The intensity of a downward load increases linearly from $0$ at the fixed end to $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at free end $B$. Find the shear force at section $x=3\,\mathrm{m}$, taking shear that tends to rotate an element clockwise as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" carichi="A,B,0,0.7,0→6 kN/m" punti="3,0,S" quote="A,B,6 m,0.9" titolo="Cantilever with triangular loading increasing toward its tip"}

- $4.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $13.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $18\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Isolate the segment from the cut to the free end, which has no support reactions. The intensity is $q(x)=x$ in kilonewtons per meter, so between $x=3$ and $x=6\,\mathrm{m}$ it varies from $3$ to $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$.

Its resultant is the trapezoid area $\dfrac{3+6}{2}\cdot 3=13.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ directed downward, so $F_{y,\mathrm{right}}=-13.5\,\mathrm{kN}$.

On the right segment $T=-F_{y,\mathrm{right}}$, giving $T=13.5\,\mathrm{kN}$. The load on the half toward the fixed support is only $4.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ and lies outside the isolated segment.
