A portal has a pin at $A$ at the base of its left column and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$ at the base of its right column. The columns have height $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and are spaced $6\,\mathrm{m}$ apart. Wind acts on the left column as a uniform rightward load $3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over its whole height. Find the bending moment magnitude at joint $C$ at the top of the left column.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,0,4;D,6,4;B,6,0;Z,-1.6,0,-" aste="A-C;C-D;D-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,C,0.6,0.6,3 kN/m,0" quote="A,B,6 m,0.8;B,D,4 m,0.8" titolo="Portal frame under wind load on its left column"}

- $48\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The wind resultant $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at elevation $2\,\mathrm{m}$. Horizontal equilibrium gives $H_A=12\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left.

Taking moments about $A$ gives $6\,V_B=12\cdot 2$, hence $V_B=4\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. The only external force on the right column is $V_B$ along its axis. Its moment is therefore zero and at joint $D$ we have $M=0$.

The crossbeam is unloaded and its moment varies linearly from $0$ at $D$ to the value at $C$. Reaction $V_B$ has moment arm $6\,\mathrm{m}$ about $C$, so $|M_C|=4\cdot 6=24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The column gives the same result, $12\cdot 4-12\cdot 2=24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
