Vertical member $AB$ of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$ hangs from a fixed support $A$ at the top. Its free end $B$ is $2\,\mathrm{m}$ below $A$. At $B$ a horizontal force of $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts to the right. Local coordinate $s$ runs from $A$ to $B$, and its positive transverse unit vector is obtained by rotating the unit vector of $s$ counterclockwise by a quarter turn. On the segment preceding the cut, positive $T$ points opposite to the transverse unit vector and positive $M$ is counterclockwise. Find the shear force and bending moment immediately below the fixed support.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,-2" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,90" forze="B,1,0,5 kN" quote="A,B,2 m,-0.8" titolo="Hanging bar with a horizontal force at its free end"}

- $T=5\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $M=10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $T=-5\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $M=10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $T=-5\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $M=-10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $T=5\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $M=-10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The unit vector of $s$ points downward, so its positive transverse unit vector, rotated counterclockwise a quarter turn, points to the right.

Isolate the segment between the cut and $B$, which contains no support reactions. On its face the signs are opposite to those on the preceding segment. Positive $T$ points to the right and positive $M$ is clockwise.

The rightward force $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ requires a leftward force $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ on the cut face, giving $T=-5\,\mathrm{kN}$.

The force at $B$ has moment arm $2\,\mathrm{m}$ and produces a counterclockwise moment $10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ about the cut. Equilibrium requires a clockwise couple $10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ on the face, which is positive, so $M=10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The left-hand fibers are in tension, opposite the positive transverse unit vector.
