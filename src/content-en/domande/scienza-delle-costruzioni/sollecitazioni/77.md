In the beam shown, span $AB$ of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$ between pin $A$ and roller $B$ carries a uniform downward load $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Overhang $BC$ of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$ carries no distributed load and has a downward tip force $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $C$. At which section is the shear force magnitude largest and what is its value?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,8,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,4 kN/m" forze="C,0,-1,6 kN" quote="A,B,6 m,0.9;B,C,2 m,0.9" titolo="Overhanging beam with a span load and a tip force"}

- $14\,\mathrm{kN}$ immediately to the left of $B$
- $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ at section $B$
- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ immediately to the right of $A$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ on the overhang

## Explanation

Taking moments about $A$ gives $6\,V_B=24\cdot 3+6\cdot 8=120$, so $V_B=20\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_A=10\,\mathrm{kN}$.

In the span $T(x)=10-4x$ decreases from $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $A$ to $-14\,\mathrm{kN}$ immediately left of $B$. At $B$ the reaction causes a jump of $+20\,\mathrm{kN}$. On the overhang $T=6\,\mathrm{kN}$ is constant to the tip.

Comparing magnitudes gives the maximum, **$14\,\mathrm{kN}$ immediately to the left of $B$**. The reaction at $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ equals the shear jump. No section carries shear equal to that reaction.
