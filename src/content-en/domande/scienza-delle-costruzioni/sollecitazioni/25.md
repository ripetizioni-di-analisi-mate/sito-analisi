Between sections $S$ and $U$, a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ apart, there are no concentrated couples and the shear force varies linearly from $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $S$ to $-4\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $U$. Coordinate $x$ increases from $S$ toward $U$ and $T=\dfrac{dM}{dx}$. Given $M(S)=10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, find $M(U)$.

- $2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $42\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The change in $M$ is the signed area under the shear diagram. For a linear variation the area is $4\times\dfrac{8+(-4)}{2}=8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Adding this change to $M(S)$ gives $M(U)=18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
