Horizontal beam $AB$ of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$ has a pin at $A$ and a roller at $B$. The intensity of a downward load increases linearly from $0$ at $A$ to $8\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at midspan, then decreases linearly to $0$ at $B$. Find the midspan bending moment.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,C,0,0.8,8 kN/m;C,B,0.8,0" quote="A,C,3 m,0.9;C,B,3 m,0.9" titolo="Simply supported beam with a symmetric triangular load"}

- $36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $48\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The load resultant is $\dfrac{8\cdot 6}{2}=24\,\mathrm{kN}$, and symmetry gives $V_A=V_B=12\,\mathrm{kN}$.

Isolate the segment between $A$ and midspan. Its triangular load has resultant $\dfrac{8\cdot 3}{2}=12\,\mathrm{kN}$ at a distance $\dfrac{2}{3}\cdot 3=2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, or $1\,\mathrm{m}$ from midspan.

Thus $M=12\cdot 3-12\cdot 1=24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, corresponding to $\dfrac{qL^2}{12}$ with $q=8\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and $L=6\,\mathrm{m}$. Shear vanishes at midspan, so this is the **maximum moment**.
