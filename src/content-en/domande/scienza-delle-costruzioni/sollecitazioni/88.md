A simply supported beam carries two downward forces, $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $x=3\,\mathrm{m}$ and $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $x=6\,\mathrm{m}$. Its span is $9\,\mathrm{m}$, with $x$ measured from left support $A$. Where is the bending moment largest, and what is its maximum value?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,3,0;D,6,0;B,9,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-0.9,12 kN;D,0,-1.3,18 kN" quote="A,C,3 m,0.9;C,D,3 m,0.9;D,B,3 m,0.9" titolo="Simply supported beam with two unequal forces"}

- $42\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at $x=3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $48\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at $x=3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $54\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at $x=6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $48\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at $x=6\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

Taking moments about $B$ gives $9\,V_A=12\cdot 6+18\cdot 3=126$, hence $V_A=14\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_B=16\,\mathrm{kN}$.

Shear is $14\,\mathrm{kN}$ up to $x=3\,\mathrm{m}$, $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ between the point loads, and $-16\,\mathrm{kN}$ beyond $x=6\,\mathrm{m}$. It changes sign only at $x=6\,\mathrm{m}$, so the maximum moment occurs under the larger force.

Its value is $M(6)=14\cdot 6-12\cdot 3=48\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. At $x=3\,\mathrm{m}$ the moment is $42\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and still increasing because the shear between the loads is positive.
