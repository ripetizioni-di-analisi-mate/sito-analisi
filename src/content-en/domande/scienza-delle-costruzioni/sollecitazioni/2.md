The horizontal beam is $8\,\mathrm{m}$ long. It has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$, a distance $8\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Measure coordinate $x$ to the right from $A$. At $C$, a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, force $P$ acts downward with magnitude $24\,\mathrm{kN}$. Find the signed value of $M(2)$ at section $x=2\,\mathrm{m}$. Positive bending moment puts the bottom fibers in tension.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,C,2 m;C,B,6 m" forze="C,0,-1.68,P" titolo="Beam supports, loads, and dimensions"}

- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $48\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The reaction at $A$ is $24\cdot\dfrac{6}{8}=18\,\mathrm{kN}$. At the cut through $C$ the point load has zero moment arm, so $M(2)=18\cdot2$.
