A Gerber beam is fixed at $A$ at coordinate $x=0$. Internal hinge $C$ is at $x=5\,\mathrm{m}$, and roller with vertical reaction $B$ is a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right of $C$. Two downward forces act, $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right of $C$ and $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ a distance $1\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left of $C$. Find $M$ at $x=2\,\mathrm{m}$, taking bottom-fiber tension as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,9,0;C,5,0" aste="A-C;C-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" titolo="Gerber beam and section S before hinge C" forze="4 0,0,-1.5,10 kN;7 0,0,-1.8,12 kN" cerniere="C" punti="2,0,S" quote="A,C,5 m,1.27;C,B,4 m,1.27"}

- $38\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-42\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-38\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

On span $CB$ the force at $C$ is $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. Thus $CB$ applies an equal downward force to the left segment. Between the cut and $C$, this force has moment arm $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and the load $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ has moment arm $2\,\mathrm{m}$. Both put the top fibers in tension, so $M=-38\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
