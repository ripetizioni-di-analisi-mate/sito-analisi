Horizontal beam $AB$ of length $8\,\mathrm{m}$ has a pin at $A$ and a roller at $B$. It carries a uniform downward load $3\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over the entire span and a downward force $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $x=6\,\mathrm{m}$. Find the slope of the bending moment diagram immediately to the right of $A$, with $M$ in $\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and $x$ in meters.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,6,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,3 kN/m" forze="C,0,-1,8 kN" quote="A,C,6 m,0.9;C,B,2 m,0.9" titolo="Simply supported beam with uniform loading and a point force"}

- $14\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $18\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Since $M'=T$, the slope of the moment diagram just beyond $A$ equals the shear force there, which is reaction $V_A$.

Taking moments about $B$ gives
$$
8\,V_A=24\cdot 4+8\cdot 2=112,
$$
hence $V_A=14\,\mathrm{kN}$.

The slope is therefore $14\,\mathrm{kN}$ in kilonewton-meters per meter. Uniform loading alone would give $12\,\mathrm{kN}$. The point force near $B$ adds $2\,\mathrm{kN}$.
