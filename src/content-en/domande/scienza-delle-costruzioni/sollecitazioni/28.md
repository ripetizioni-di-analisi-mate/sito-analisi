An $L$-shaped frame has a column of height $3\,\mathrm{m}$ fixed at its base and a crossbeam of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ extending to the right. A counterclockwise couple $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ acts at the rigid joint, and a downward force $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at the tip of the crossbeam. Find the bending moment magnitudes in the crossbeam and column immediately adjacent to the joint.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;C,4,3" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,incastro" titolo="Couple at the rigid frame joint" coppie="B,1,12 kN·m" forze="C,0,-1.3,10 kN"}

- $|M|=28\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in the crossbeam and $40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in the column
- $|M|=40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in the crossbeam and $28\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in the column
- $|M|=40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in the crossbeam and $52\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in the column
- $|M|=40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in the crossbeam and $40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ in the column

## Explanation

A cut through the crossbeam immediately beyond the joint leaves only the tip force on the right, giving $10\times 4=40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. A cut through the column just below the joint also includes the applied counterclockwise couple. It opposes the clockwise load moment, leaving magnitude $40-12=28\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
