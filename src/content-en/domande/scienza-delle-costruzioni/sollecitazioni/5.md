The horizontal beam is $8\,\mathrm{m}$ long. It has a pin at $A$ and a bilateral roller on a horizontal surface at $B$, a distance $8\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Measure coordinate $x$ to the right from $A$. At $C$, a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, couple $\mathcal M_0$ acts counterclockwise with magnitude $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Find the signed value of $M(5)$ at section $x=5\,\mathrm{m}$. Positive bending moment puts the bottom fibers in tension.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,C,4 m;C,B,4 m" coppie="C,1,M_0" titolo="Beam supports, loads, and dimensions"}

- $9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

Overall equilibrium gives $V_A=3\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_B=-3\,\mathrm{kN}$. The bilateral roller can exert a downward reaction. Crossing the counterclockwise couple, the $M$ diagram drops by $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, giving $M(5)=3\cdot5-24$.
