Horizontal cantilever $AB$ is fixed at $A$ and has length $4\,\mathrm{m}$. At $B$ an inclined force $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ has components $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. Find the bending moment immediately to the right of the fixed support, taking bottom-fiber tension as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" forze="B,0.6,-0.8,10 kN" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9" titolo="Cantilever with an inclined tip force"}

- $-40\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $32\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-32\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

Isolate the segment from the cut to the tip. Its only load is the force at $B$. The horizontal component acts along the beam axis and has zero moment about the cut.

The vertical component $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ has moment arm $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and gives clockwise moment $32\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. On the right segment $M$ equals the external moment with counterclockwise positive, giving $M=-32\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

The perpendicular arm of the full force is $4\cdot\dfrac{8}{10}=3.2\,\mathrm{m}$ and gives the same result, $10\cdot 3.2=32\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The horizontal component produces only axial force $N=6\,\mathrm{kN}$.
