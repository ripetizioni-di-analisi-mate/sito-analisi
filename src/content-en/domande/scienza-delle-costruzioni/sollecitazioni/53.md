A Gerber beam is fixed at $A$, has an internal hinge at $C$ a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$, and a roller at $B$ a distance $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from $C$. A uniform downward load $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts over the entire beam. Which statement about the internal resultants at hinge $C$ is true?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,4,0;B,7,0" aste="A-C;C-B" cerniere="C" vincoli="A,incastro,180;B,carrello" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,4 kN/m" quote="A,C,4 m,0.9;C,B,3 m,0.9" titolo="Gerber beam under uniform loading"}

- $M=0$ and $T=0$
- $M=0$, and $T$ has the same nonzero value on either side of $C$
- $M$ is continuous and nonzero, while $T=0$
- $M=0$, and $T$ jumps at $C$ by the force transmitted through the hinge

## Explanation

The internal hinge permits relative rotation and transmits no couple. No external couple acts at $C$, so the moment is zero on both sides.

The hinge does transmit a vertical force. Taking moments for segment $CB$ about $C$ gives $3\,V_B=12\cdot 1.5$. Hence $V_B=6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the hinge carries the remaining $6\,\mathrm{kN}$. Thus the shear at $C$ is $6\,\mathrm{kN}$.

The hinge force is internal to the beam and no external point force acts at $C$. The shear force therefore has **the same limit from the left and right**.
