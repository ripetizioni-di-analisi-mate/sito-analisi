As an interior section is crossed from left to right, the bending moment changes from $15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ to $-5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Bending moment is positive for bottom-fiber tension. Find the applied couple at this section, taking counterclockwise as positive.

- $10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

Moment equilibrium of an infinitesimal segment about the section gives $-M(\mathrm{left})+M(\mathrm{right})+C=0$, hence $C=15-(-5)=20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. A concentrated vertical force would change shear without producing a moment jump.
