A simply supported beam of length $10\,\mathrm{m}$ carries a downward distributed load $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over the first $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and a downward point force $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $x=8\,\mathrm{m}$. Coordinate $x$ starts at the left support. Find the moment at $x=5\,\mathrm{m}$, taking bottom-fiber tension as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,10,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" titolo="Section S in the unloaded segment" forze="8 0,0,-1.83,20 kN" carichi="0 0,4 0,1.5,1.5,4 kN/m,270" punti="5,0,S" quote="A,B,10 m,1.42"}

- $4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $84\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $96\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

Moments about the left support give right reaction $19.2\,\mathrm{kN}$. Isolate the right segment to exclude the uniform load. The reaction has moment arm $5\,\mathrm{m}$ and the force $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ has arm $3\,\mathrm{m}$, giving moment $19.2\times 5-20\times 3=36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
