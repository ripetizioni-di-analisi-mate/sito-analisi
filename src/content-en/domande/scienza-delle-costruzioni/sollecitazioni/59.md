A horizontal beam rests on two supports spaced $8\,\mathrm{m}$ apart and extends beyond each support by the same overhang length $a$. A uniform downward load acts over the entire beam. For what value of $a$ is the magnitude of the support moment equal to that of the midspan moment?

::struttura{nodi="C,-2.4,0;A,0,0;B,8,0;D,10.4,0" aste="C-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="C,D,0.6,0.6,q" quote="C,A,a,0.9;A,B,8 m,0.9;B,D,a,0.9" titolo="Beam with two equal overhangs"}

- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\sqrt2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\sqrt2\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

By symmetry each support carries half the total load. At either support the outer segment carries the overhang load, giving $M_{\mathrm{app}}=-\dfrac{qa^2}{2}$.

At midspan the central span behaves as a simply supported beam with load $q$ and moment $-\dfrac{qa^2}{2}$ at both ends. Thus
$$
M_{\mathrm{mezz}}=\frac{q\cdot 8^2}{8}-\frac{qa^2}{2}=8q-\frac{qa^2}{2}.
$$
Equal magnitudes with opposite signs require $8q-\dfrac{qa^2}{2}=\dfrac{qa^2}{2}$, or $a^2=8$. This gives $a=2\sqrt2\,\mathrm{m}\approx 2.83\,\mathrm{m}$ independently of load intensity.
