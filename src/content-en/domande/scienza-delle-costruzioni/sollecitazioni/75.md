A uniform downward load $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts only on overhang $BC$ of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$. The beam has a pin at $A$ and a roller at $B$, spaced $6\,\mathrm{m}$ apart. Span $AB$ is unloaded. Which statement about its internal resultants is true?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,8,0" aste="A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="B,C,0.6,0.6,6 kN/m" quote="A,B,6 m,0.9;B,C,2 m,0.9" titolo="Beam loaded only on the overhang"}

- Shear force and bending moment are zero because the span carries no load
- The shear force is $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the bottom fibers are in tension
- The shear force is $-2\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the top fibers are in tension, with $M_B=-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- The shear force is $-2\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the top fibers are in tension, with $M_B=-24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The load resultant is $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $x=7\,\mathrm{m}$. Taking moments about $A$ gives $6\,V_B=12\cdot 7$, hence $V_B=14\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_A=-2\,\mathrm{kN}$, a downward reaction.

In the span the left segment carries only $V_A$, so $T=-2\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $M(x)=-2x$. The moment is negative and the **top fibers are in tension**.

At $B$ the value is $M_B=-12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, equal to the moment of the overhang load with arm $1\,\mathrm{m}$. The unloaded span bends because the bending moment is continuous across support $B$.
