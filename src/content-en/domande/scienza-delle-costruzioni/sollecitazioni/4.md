The horizontal beam is $10\,\mathrm{m}$ long. It has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$, a distance $10\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$. Measure coordinate $x$ to the right from $A$. On segment $CD$ between $x=2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $x=6\,\mathrm{m}$, a uniform downward load of intensity $5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts. Find the signed value of $T(4)$ at section $x=4\,\mathrm{m}$. Positive shear tends to rotate a beam element clockwise.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;D,6,0;B,10,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" quote="A,C,2 m;C,D,4 m;D,B,4 m" carichi="C,D,1.0,1.0,5 kN/m" titolo="Beam supports, loads, and dimensions"}

- $2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-8\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The total load is $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ centered at $x=4\,\mathrm{m}$, so $V_A=12\,\mathrm{kN}$. The load to the left of the shear force is $10\,\mathrm{kN}$, giving $T=12-10$.
