At an interior section of a horizontal beam the axial force is $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ immediately to the left and $-6\,\mathrm{kN}$ immediately to the right, with $N>0$ positive in tension. There is no support at the section. Which concentrated axial force is applied there?

- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right
- $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $2\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right

## Explanation

For an axial point force $F_x$ positive to the right, the axial force jump is $N(a^+)-N(a^-)=-F_x$.

The jump is $-6-4=-10\,\mathrm{kN}$, so $F_x=10\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the force acts **to the right**.

A rightward force compresses the segment to its right and puts the segment to its left in tension, consistent with the signs of the two limiting axial forces.
