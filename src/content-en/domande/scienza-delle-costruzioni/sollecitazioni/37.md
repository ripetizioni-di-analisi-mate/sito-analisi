A simply supported beam of length $10\,\mathrm{m}$ carries a downward load $5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over the entire span and a downward point force $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ a distance $8\,\mathrm{m}$ from the left support. At what distance from the left support does the positive (sagging) bending moment reach its maximum?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,10,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" titolo="Uniform load and eccentric point force on a simply supported beam" forze="8 0,0,-2.25,20 kN" carichi="0 0,10 0,1.17,1.17,5 kN/m,270" quote="A,B,10 m,1.42"}

- $5\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $5.8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $8.2\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The left reaction is the sum of $\dfrac{qL}{2}$ and the share $20\times\dfrac{10-8}{10}$ from the point force, giving $29\,\mathrm{kN}$. Before the force the shear is $T=29-5x$ and vanishes at $x=5.8\,\mathrm{m}$. Here shear changes from positive to negative, so the bending moment is largest.
