At an interior section of a beam the shear force is $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ immediately to the left and $-4\,\mathrm{kN}$ immediately to the right. There is no support there. Taking $M$ as positive for bottom-fiber tension and using $T=\dfrac{dM}{dx}$, find the concentrated vertical force, with upward positive.

- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-12\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The jump in shear equals the concentrated vertical force with upward positive. The jump is $T(\mathrm{right})-T(\mathrm{left})=-4-8=-12\,\mathrm{kN}$. Its sign indicates a downward force.
