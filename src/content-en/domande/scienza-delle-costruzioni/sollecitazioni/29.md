A rigid portal of span $6\,\mathrm{m}$ and height $4\,\mathrm{m}$ has a pin $A$ at the left base and a roller with vertical reaction $B$ at the right base. Its only load is a rightward horizontal force $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ at the top. Find the shear force and bending moment magnitudes in the left column at a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from the base.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,0,4;D,6,4" aste="A-C;C-D;D-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" titolo="Section S of the left column of a portal frame" forze="C,1.4,0,12 kN" punti="0,2,S"}

- $|T|=12\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $|M|=48\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $|T|=0\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $|M|=24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $|T|=12\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $|M|=0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $|T|=12\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $|M|=24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

Only support $A$ can exert a horizontal reaction, so $A_{x}$ is $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left. Between $A$ and the cut this reaction is transverse to the column and has moment arm $2\,\mathrm{m}$. Therefore $|T|=12\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $|M|=12\times 2=24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
