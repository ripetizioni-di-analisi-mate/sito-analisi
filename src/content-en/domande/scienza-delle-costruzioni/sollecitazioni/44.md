Horizontal cantilever $AB$ is fixed at $A$ and has length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, with $x$ measured from $A$. It carries a uniform downward load $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over its full length and a downward force $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ at free end $B$. A student obtained four results, taking positive $T>0$ to rotate a beam element clockwise and positive $M>0$ to put the bottom fibers in tension. Which single result is wrong?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" carichi="A,B,0.5,0.5,2 kN/m" forze="B,0,-1.2,3 kN" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9" titolo="Cantilever with uniform loading and a tip force"}

- $M(2)=-14\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $V_A=11\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $M(0^+)=-28\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $T(4^-)=3\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Vertical equilibrium gives $V_A=2\cdot 4+3=11\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward, so the stated reaction is correct.

Immediately right of the fixed end the distributed load has resultant $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ and moment arm $2\,\mathrm{m}$, while the point force has moment arm $4\,\mathrm{m}$. Thus $M(0^+)=-(8\cdot 2+3\cdot 4)=-28\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, which is also correct. Near the tip, only the downward force $3\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts on the right segment, giving $T(4^-)=3\,\mathrm{kN}$.

Between $0$ and $2\,\mathrm{m}$ the shear force is $T(x)=11-2x$ and the area under its diagram is $\dfrac{11+7}{2}\cdot 2=18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Thus
$$
M(2)=M(0^+)+18=-28+18=-10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}.
$$
The right segment gives the same value, $-(4\cdot 1+3\cdot 2)=-10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The incorrect result is therefore $M(2)=-14\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
