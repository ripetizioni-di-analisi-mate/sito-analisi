A straight cantilever rises by $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and extends $4\,\mathrm{m}$ to the right from its fixed end to its tip. A uniform downward vertical load of $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts per meter of actual member length. Find the bending moment magnitude at a section a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from the fixed end measured along the member.

- $36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $27\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $72\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $45\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The cantilever length is $5\,\mathrm{m}$ and the remaining length beyond the cut is $3\,\mathrm{m}$. The vertical load on that portion is $30\,\mathrm{kN}$. Its resultant acts at the midpoint with horizontal moment arm $(\dfrac{4}{5})\times\dfrac{3}{2}=1.2\,\mathrm{m}$, giving bending moment magnitude $36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
