A beam has a pin at $A$ at coordinate $x=0$ and a roller with vertical reaction at $B$ at coordinate $x=6\,\mathrm{m}$. On the left overhang, a downward load of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $x=-2\,\mathrm{m}$. On the span, a downward load $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $x=4\,\mathrm{m}$. At what coordinate between $A$ and the span load does the bending moment change sign?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,-2,0" aste="C-A;A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" titolo="Beam with a left overhang and a load on the span" forze="-2 0,0,-0.9,12 kN;4 0,0,-1.1,18 kN" quote="C,A,2 m,0.85;A,B,6 m,0.85"}

- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2.4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4.4\,\mathrm{m}$
- $\dfrac{12}{11}\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

The overhang load imposes $M(A)=-12\times 2=-24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Reaction $A_{y}$ is $22\,\mathrm{kN}$, giving constant shear $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ from $A$ to the span load. On that interval $M=-24+10x$, which vanishes at $x=2.4\,\mathrm{m}$.
