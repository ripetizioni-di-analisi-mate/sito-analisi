A beam on bilateral supports has two overhangs. Support $A$ is at $x=0$ and $B$ at $x=8\,\mathrm{m}$. The left overhang of length $2\,\mathrm{m}$ has a downward tip load $12\,\mathrm{kN}$, and the right overhang of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ has an upward tip load $8\,\mathrm{kN}$. Span $AB$ is unloaded. At what coordinate inside $AB$ does the bending moment vanish?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,8,0;C,-2,0;D,11,0" aste="C-A;A-B;B-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" titolo="Overhangs loaded in opposite directions" forze="-2 0,0,-1.6,12 kN;11 0,0,1.33,8 kN" quote="C,A,2 m,1.13;A,B,8 m,1.13;B,D,3 m,1.13"}

- $3.2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $2\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4.8\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

At $A$ the moment is $-12\times 2=-24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and at $B$ it is $+8\times 3=24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. With no load between the supports the moment varies linearly. Its slope is $\dfrac{24+24}{8}$ and interpolation gives $x=4\,\mathrm{m}$.
