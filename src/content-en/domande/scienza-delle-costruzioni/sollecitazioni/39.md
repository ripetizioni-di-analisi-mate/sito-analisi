A horizontal cantilever of length $8\,\mathrm{m}$ is fixed at its left end. The downward vertical load varies linearly from $10\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at the fixed end to $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ at the tip. Find $M$ a distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ from the fixed end, taking bottom-fiber tension as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" titolo="Cantilever with a trapezoidal load and section S" carichi="0 0,8 0,1.33,0.267,10 → 2 kN/m,270" punti="4,0,S" quote="A,B,8 m,1.13"}

- $\dfrac{80}{3}\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-48\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $\dfrac{-80}{3}\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

Isolate the right segment of length $4\,\mathrm{m}$. Its end intensities are $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ and $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$. Splitting the load into a rectangle and a triangle gives moment magnitude $4^{2}\times\dfrac{6+2\times 2}{6}=\dfrac{80}{3}\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ about the cut. The top fibers are in tension, so $M=\dfrac{-80}{3}\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
