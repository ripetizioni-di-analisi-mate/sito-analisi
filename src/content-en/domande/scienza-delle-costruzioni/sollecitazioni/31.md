A horizontal bar is fixed at its left end. Over the first $6\,\mathrm{m}$ a uniform axial load $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts to the left. Over the next $2\,\mathrm{m}$, up to the free end, a uniform load $6\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ acts to the right. At what coordinate measured from the left does the axial force vanish inside the bar?

- $6\,\mathrm{m}$
- $3\,\mathrm{m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

For a section in the first interval isolate the portion to its right. The final segment pulls to the right with $12\,\mathrm{kN}$. The remaining length of the first segment loads it to the left with $4(6-x)\,\mathrm{kN}$. Equilibrium with $N=0$ requires $4(6-x)=12$, giving $x=3\,\mathrm{m}$.
