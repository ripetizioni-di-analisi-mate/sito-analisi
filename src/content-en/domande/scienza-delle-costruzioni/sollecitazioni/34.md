On a straight beam segment, the shear force diagram is linear from $x=0$ to $x=3\,\mathrm{m}$ and then constant as far as $x=7\,\mathrm{m}$. Its values at the ends of the linear interval are $T(0)=16\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $T(3)=4\,\mathrm{kN}$. Load intensity $q$ is positive downward and $T=\dfrac{dM}{dx}$. Find $q$ over the linear interval.

- $\dfrac{12}{7}\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $-4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN/m}$

## Explanation

Load intensity is the negative slope of the shear diagram. It is $q=\dfrac{16-4}{3}=4\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ in the first interval and zero in the interval of constant shear.
