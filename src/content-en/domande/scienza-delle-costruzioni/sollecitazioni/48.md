Horizontal cantilever $AB$ has length $4\,\mathrm{m}$, with its free end $A$ on the left and its fixed support $B$ on the right. At $A$ a downward force $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts. With positive $T>0$ tending to rotate an element clockwise and positive $M>0$ putting the bottom fibers in tension, find the shear force and bending moment at a section a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from $A$.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="B,incastro,0" forze="A,0,-1,10 kN" punti="2,0,S" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9" titolo="Cantilever fixed at the right"}

- $T=-10\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $M=-20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $T=10\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $M=-20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $T=-10\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $M=20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $T=10\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $M=20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

Isolate the segment between $A$ and the cut, which carries only the known force. Its vertical resultant is $F_{y,\mathrm{left}}=-10\,\mathrm{kN}$.

Since $T=F_{y,\mathrm{left}}$, the result is $T=-10\,\mathrm{kN}$. Its sign is opposite to that for a cantilever fixed at the left under the same tip force because the force is now to the left of the cut.

The downward force a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ to the left of the cut produces a counterclockwise moment $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ about it. Since $M=-M_{\mathrm{left}}$, the result is $M=-20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ and the top fibers are in tension, as for any downward-loaded cantilever.
