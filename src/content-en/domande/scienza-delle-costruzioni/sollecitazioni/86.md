A statically determinate horizontal beam is $5\,\mathrm{m}$ long with no external loads applied at its ends. Approaching end $x=0$ the internal resultants are $N=0$, $T=8\,\mathrm{kN}$, and $M=-24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, and approaching end $x=5\,\mathrm{m}$ they are $N=0$, $T=0$, and $M=0$. What supports does the beam have?

- Free end at $x=0$ and fixed support at $x=5\,\mathrm{m}$
- Fixed support at $x=0$ and free end at $x=5\,\mathrm{m}$
- Pin at $x=0$ and roller at $x=5\,\mathrm{m}$
- Roller at $x=0$ and fixed support at $x=5\,\mathrm{m}$

## Explanation

At $x=0$ the moment tends to a nonzero value with no applied end couple. Support $x=0$ must therefore exert a couple, so it is a **fixed support**.

A pin or roller cannot exert a couple and would give $M=0$ there. An unloaded free end would give $T=0$ and $M=0$.

At $x=5\,\mathrm{m}$ all three resultants vanish, as at an unloaded free end. The beam is statically determinate and the fixed support alone restrains it, so $x=5\,\mathrm{m}$ is the free end.
