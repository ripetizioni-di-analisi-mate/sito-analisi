A cantilever frame has a column $AB$ of height $4\,\mathrm{m}$ fixed at $A$ and a crossbeam $BC$ of length $3\,\mathrm{m}$ extending to the right. At $C$ the only load is a rightward force $5\,\mathrm{kN}$. Find the bending moment at the column base and identify the side in tension.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,3,4" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,incastro" frecce="3,4,4.2,4,5 kN" quote="A,B,4 m,-0.8;B,C,3 m,-0.8" titolo="Cantilever frame with a horizontal tip force"}

- $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the right-hand fibers in tension
- $15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the left-hand fibers in tension
- $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the left-hand fibers in tension
- $35\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the left-hand fibers in tension

## Explanation

Cut the column just above $A$ and isolate the upper frame, whose only load is the force at $C$.

The force is horizontal, so its moment arm is vertical distance $4\,\mathrm{m}$ between $C$ and $A$, independent of crossbeam length. The moment is therefore $5\cdot 4=20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.

The force pushes the column top to the right, bending it like a cantilever under a rightward tip force. At its base the **left-hand fibers are in tension**, on the side opposite the force direction.
