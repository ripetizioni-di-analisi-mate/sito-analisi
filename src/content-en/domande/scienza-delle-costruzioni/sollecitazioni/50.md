A three-hinged portal frame has external pins $A$ and $B$ at the bases of columns of height $3\,\mathrm{m}$ spaced $6\,\mathrm{m}$ apart and an internal hinge $E$ at the midpoint of the crossbeam. A downward force $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $E$. Find the bending moment magnitude at joint $C$ between the left column and the crossbeam.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,0,3;E,3,3;D,6,3;B,6,0" aste="A-C;C-E;E-D;D-B" cerniere="E" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" forze="E,0,-1,12 kN" quote="A,B,6 m,0.8;B,D,3 m,0.8" titolo="Three-hinged portal frame with a force at the crown"}

- $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $36\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The structure and loading are symmetric, so $V_A=V_B=6\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward.

Isolate the left half $ACE$ and impose zero moment at hinge $E$. About $E$, reaction $V_A$ has arm $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and gives clockwise moment $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The rightward thrust $H_A$ has arm $3\,\mathrm{m}$ and gives a counterclockwise moment. Thus $3\,H_A=18$ and $H_A=6\,\mathrm{kN}$.

Cut the column just below $C$. On the lower segment reaction $V_A$ acts along the column axis and produces no moment, while $H_A$ has moment arm $3\,\mathrm{m}$. Thus $|M_C|=6\cdot 3=18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with the outer fibers of the corner in tension.
