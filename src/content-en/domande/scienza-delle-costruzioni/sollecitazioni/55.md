Horizontal beam $AB$ of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$ has a pin at $A$ and a roller at $B$. Its only load is a counterclockwise couple $18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at section $A$. Find the bending moment at midspan, taking bottom-fiber tension as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" coppie="A,1,18 kN m" punti="3,0,S" quote="A,B,6 m,0.9" titolo="Simply supported beam with a couple at support A"}

- $9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

Moment equilibrium about $A$ gives $18+6\,V_B=0$, hence $V_B=-3\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_A=3\,\mathrm{kN}$. The reactions form a clockwise couple balancing the applied couple.

At end $A$ the moment is zero outside the beam. The counterclockwise couple produces jump $-18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, so $M(0^+)=-18\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. An applied end couple therefore gives a nonzero bending moment at a simple support.

In the span the shear is $V_A=3\,\mathrm{kN}$, hence $M(x)=-18+3x$. At midspan this gives $M(3)=-9\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ with top fibers in tension.
