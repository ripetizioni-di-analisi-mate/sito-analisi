A cantilever of length $6\,\mathrm{m}$ is fixed at the left. Its free end carries a downward force $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ and a counterclockwise couple $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Find $M$ at a distance $2\,\mathrm{m}$ from the fixed end, taking bottom-fiber tension as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,incastro,180" titolo="Cantilever with a tip force and couple" forze="6 0,0,-1.4,8 kN" coppie="6 0,1,C_0" punti="2,0,S" quote="A,B,6 m,0.85"}

- $-32\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $44\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

On the isolated right segment the force has moment arm $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and contributes $-32\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The tip couple contributes $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ under the given convention. Their sum is $M=-20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$.
