A portal has a pin at $A$ at the base of its left column and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$ at the base of its right column. Its columns have height $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and are spaced $6\,\mathrm{m}$ apart. Its only load is a uniform downward load $5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ on the crossbeam. Which statement about the columns is true?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,0,4;D,6,4;B,6,0" aste="A-C;C-D;D-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" carichi="C,D,0.6,0.6,5 kN/m" quote="A,B,6 m,0.8;B,D,4 m,0.8" titolo="Portal frame with a uniform load on the crossbeam"}

- The bending moment is zero and the axial force is $-15\,\mathrm{kN}$
- Both bending moment and axial force are zero
- The bending moment increases linearly to $22.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at the joints
- The bending moment is constant and equals $22.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

All loads are vertical and the roller at $B$ has only a vertical reaction. Horizontal equilibrium therefore gives $H_A=0$.

By symmetry $V_A=V_B=\dfrac{5\cdot 6}{2}=15\,\mathrm{kN}$. On the lower portion of a cut column the only force is the vertical reaction, along the column axis.

Thus in the columns the shear force and bending moment are **zero**, and the axial force is $-15\,\mathrm{kN}$. The crossbeam acts as a simply supported beam, with maximum moment $\dfrac{5\cdot 6^2}{8}=22.5\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at midspan and zero moment at the joints.
