Horizontal beam $AB$ of length $8\,\mathrm{m}$ has a pin at $A$ and a bilateral roller at $B$. At $x=2\,\mathrm{m}$ a downward force $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts, and at $x=6\,\mathrm{m}$ an upward force $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts. Find the bending moment at midspan.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;C,2,0;D,6,0;B,8,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0,-1,10 kN;D,0,1,10 kN" quote="A,C,2 m,1.4;C,D,4 m,1.4;D,B,2 m,1.4" titolo="Simply supported beam with two opposing forces"}

- $10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $-10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The loading is antisymmetric about midspan. The forces are symmetrically placed and point in opposite directions.

Taking moments about $B$ gives $8\,V_A=10\cdot 6-10\cdot 2=40$, hence $V_A=5\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_B=-5\,\mathrm{kN}$.

At midspan the left segment carries $V_A$ with moment arm $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and the downward force with arm $2\,\mathrm{m}$. Thus $M=5\cdot 4-10\cdot 2=0$. The moment diagram is **antisymmetric** and vanishes at midspan, with $10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at $x=2\,\mathrm{m}$ and $-10\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ at $x=6\,\mathrm{m}$.
