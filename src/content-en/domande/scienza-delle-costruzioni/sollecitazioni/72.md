At a section of a horizontal beam the bending moment is $-15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$, with $M>0$ putting the bottom fibers in tension. The bending moment diagram is drawn on the tension side. Which fibers are in tension, and on which side of the beam axis is this value plotted?

- Bottom fibers in tension, value plotted below the axis
- Top fibers in tension, value plotted below the axis
- Bottom fibers in tension, value plotted above the axis
- Top fibers in tension, value plotted above the axis

## Explanation

A positive moment puts the bottom fibers in tension. The value $-15\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ is negative, so the opposite sense puts the top fibers in tension.

The diagram is drawn on the tension side, so plot this value above the beam axis.

With this convention, positive portions lie below the axis, as on a simply supported beam under downward loads. The moment at the support of a loaded overhang is plotted above the axis.
