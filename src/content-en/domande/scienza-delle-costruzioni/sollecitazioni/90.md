Four horizontal beams have length $4\,\mathrm{m}$ and each carries a total downward load $8\,\mathrm{kN}$. Two are simply supported and two are cantilevers fixed at one end. Each carries either a point load, at midspan for the simply supported beam or at the free end for the cantilever, or a uniform load $2\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ over the full length. Which beam has the largest maximum bending moment magnitude?

- Simply supported beam with the force at midspan
- Cantilever with the force at its free end
- Cantilever under uniform loading
- Simply supported beam under uniform loading

## Explanation

For a simply supported beam the maximum is $\dfrac{PL}{4}=\dfrac{8\cdot 4}{4}=8\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ under a midspan force and $\dfrac{qL^2}{8}=\dfrac{2\cdot 16}{8}=4\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ under uniform loading.

For a cantilever the largest magnitude occurs at the fixed end. It is $PL=32\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ under a tip force and $\dfrac{qL^2}{2}=16\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ under uniform loading.

The cantilever with a point force at its free end therefore has the largest maximum bending moment magnitude.
