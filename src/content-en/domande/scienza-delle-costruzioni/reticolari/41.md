Members $AC$ and $BC$ connect joint $C=(4,3)$ to pins $A=(0,0)$ and $B=(8,0)$. Coordinates are in meters. Pin $A$ settles vertically downward by $10\,\mathrm{mm}$, while $B$ remains fixed. The members carry no external loads. What are the first-order displacement components of $C$, taking rightward and upward as positive?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,8,0;C,4,3" aste="A-C;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" reticolare=1 quote="A,4 0,4 m;4 0,B,4 m;8.55 3,8.55 0,3 m,-0.55" titolo="Two members supported on fixed pins"}

- $(u_x,\,u_y) = (3.75,\,-5)\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $(u_x,\,u_y) = (0,\,-5)\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $(u_x,\,u_y) = (0,\,-10)\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $(u_x,\,u_y) = (-3.75,\,-5)\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The structure is statically determinate, so the settlement produces no member forces and the member lengths remain unchanged. The unit vectors are $\mathbf e_{AC} = \left(\dfrac45,\,\dfrac35\right)$ and $\mathbf e_{BC} = \left(-\dfrac45,\,\dfrac35\right)$, with $\mathbf u_A = (0,\,-10)\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $\mathbf u_B = \mathbf 0$. Requiring zero elongation gives the system
$$
\begin{cases}
\dfrac45 u_x + \dfrac35 (u_y + 10) = 0,\\[6pt]
-\dfrac45 u_x + \dfrac35 u_y = 0.
\end{cases}
$$
Adding the equations gives $\dfrac65 u_y + 6 = 0$, hence $u_y = -5\,\mathrm{mm}$. The second equation gives $u_x = \dfrac34 u_y = -3.75\,\mathrm{mm}$.
