A triangular truss has joints $A=(0,0)$, $B=(6,0)$, and $C=(2,3)$, with coordinates in meters. There is a pin at $A$ and a roller at $B$ on a vertical guide, providing a horizontal reaction. The structure undergoes an infinitesimal counterclockwise rigid-body rotation about $A$ through angle $\theta = 0.002\,\mathrm{rad}$. What are the displacement components of $C$, taking rightward and upward as positive?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,2,3" aste="A-B;A-C;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,0" reticolare=1 titolo="Triangular truss with a roller on a vertical guide"}

- $(u_x,\,u_y) = (6,\,-4)\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $(u_x,\,u_y) = (0,\,4)\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $(u_x,\,u_y) = (-4,\,6)\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $(u_x,\,u_y) = (-6,\,4)\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The reaction of the roller at $B$ is horizontal. Its line of action coincides with line $AB$, which passes through $A$, so the supports permit infinitesimal rotation about $A$. In an infinitesimal counterclockwise rotation $\theta$ about the origin, the point with coordinates $(x,\,y)$ undergoes displacement $\theta\,(-y,\,x)$. For $C=(2,3)$, with lengths in millimeters, this gives
$$
\mathbf u_C = 0.002\,(-3000,\,2000)\,\mathrm{mm} = (-6,\,4)\,\mathrm{mm}.
$$
The displacement is perpendicular to $AC$, so the member length does not change to first order.
