A triangular truss has joints $A=(0,0)$, $B=(4,0)$, and $C=(2,3)$, with coordinates in meters, and members $AB$, $AC$, and $BC$. There is a pin at $A$ and a roller at $B$ that moves along a vertical guide and therefore provides a horizontal reaction. No loads act. Which nonzero set of member forces and reactions satisfies equilibrium? Let $t$ denote an arbitrary positive value.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,2,3" aste="A-B;A-C;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,0" reticolare=1 titolo="Roller on a vertical guide at $B$"}

- $N_{AB} = t$, $N_{AC} = N_{BC} = 0$, a reaction $t$ to the right at $B$, and $t$ to the left at $A$
- $N_{AC} = N_{BC} = t$, $N_{AB} = 0$, and zero reactions
- $N_{AB} = N_{AC} = N_{BC} = t$ and zero reactions
- None, because $m + r = 2j$ guarantees a unique solution

## Explanation

The horizontal reaction at $B$ acts along the line of member $AB$, which passes through pin $A$. No external force acts at joint $C$, and members $AC$ and $BC$ have independent directions, so $N_{AC} = N_{BC} = 0$. At joint $B$ only member $AB$ and the reaction remain, and the two are collinear. With $N_{AB} = t$, the member pulls $B$ to the left with force $t$. The reaction at $B$ is therefore $t$ to the right, and equilibrium of joint $A$ requires the pin reaction $t$ to act to the left. This gives a state of self-stress **self-equilibrated** even though $m + r = 2j$, because the equilibrium matrix is singular.
