## Explanation

There are $j = 6$ joints, $m = 10$ members, and $r = 4$ reaction components because each external pin provides two components. There are therefore $m + r = 14$ unknowns and $2j = 12$ equations. The two diagonals in the left panel and the single diagonal in the right panel form triangles with noncollinear vertices. The structure is therefore stable, and the equilibrium matrix has rank $\rho = 12$. The degree of static indeterminacy is $m + r - \rho = 2$. The second diagonal introduces one redundant unknown, and the second pin introduces another.
