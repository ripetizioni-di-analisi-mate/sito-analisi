A diamond-shaped truss is pinned at $A$ and rests on a roller with a vertical reaction at $C$. The joint coordinates in meters are $A\,(0,\,0)$, $B\,(3,\,4)$, $C\,(6,\,0)$, and $D\,(3,\,-4)$. Its members are $AB$, $BC$, $CD$, $DA$, and $BD$. The downward loads at $B$ and $D$ are $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $10\,\mathrm{kN}$, respectively. What is the axial force in vertical member $BD$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,4;C,6,0;D,3,-4" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-A;B-D" reticolare="1" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" forze="B,-0,-1.28,18 kN;D,-0,-1.28,10 kN" titolo="Diamond-shaped truss loaded at the two central joints"}

- $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression
- $14\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression
- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension

## Explanation

By symmetry, the vertical reaction at $A$ is $\dfrac{18+10}{2}=14\,\mathrm{kN}$. At joint $A$, the horizontal components of the two inclined chord forces cancel. Their vertical components both act downward, each balancing half the reaction. At joint $B$, members $AB$ and $BC$ together supply an upward force $14\,\mathrm{kN}$. Consequently $N_{BD}=14-18=-4\,\mathrm{kN}$, taking a pull from $B$ toward $D$ as positive.
