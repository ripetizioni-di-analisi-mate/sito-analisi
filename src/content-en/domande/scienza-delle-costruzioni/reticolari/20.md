A rigid L-shaped member connects pins $A=(0,0)$ and $B=(4,3)$, whose coordinates are in meters. Its first segment extends from $A$ to point $K=(0,3)$ and its second from $K$ to $B$. Only the two pins exert forces on the member, and its weight is negligible. The force transmitted at $A$ has magnitude $10\,\mathrm{kN}$. What is the magnitude of its horizontal component?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;K,0,3;B,4,3" aste="A-K;K-B" cerniere="A;B" tratteggi="0,0 4,3" titolo="L-shaped member between pins $A$ and $B$"}

- $0\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Only two forces act on the member, at $A$ and $B$. The force at $A$ has zero moment about $A$. For equilibrium the force at $B$ must also have zero moment, so its line of action passes through $A$. Both forces therefore act along line $AB$, shown dashed, which coincides with neither segment of the member. The unit vector along $AB$ is $\left(\dfrac45,\,\dfrac35\right)$, so the horizontal component of the force at $A$ has magnitude $10\cdot\dfrac45 = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$.
