In the equilibrium matrix $Q$ of a truss, each column represents one unknown and each pair of rows represents one joint. Member $AB$ connects $A=(0,0)$ and $B=(3,4)$, whose coordinates are in meters. What coefficients appear in column $N_{AB}$ in the rows corresponding to joints $A$ and $B$?

- $\left(-\dfrac35,\,-\dfrac45\right)$ in the rows for $A$ and $\left(\dfrac35,\,\dfrac45\right)$ in the rows for $B$
- $(3,\,4)$ in the rows for $A$ and $(-3,\,-4)$ in the rows for $B$
- $\left(\dfrac35,\,\dfrac45\right)$ in both the rows for $A$ and the rows for $B$
- $\left(\dfrac35,\,\dfrac45\right)$ in the rows for $A$ and $\left(-\dfrac35,\,-\dfrac45\right)$ in the rows for $B$

## Explanation

The member exerts force $N_{AB}\,\mathbf e_{AB}$ on joint $A$, where $\mathbf e_{AB}$ is the unit vector from $A$ to $B$. Since $L_{AB} = 5\,\mathrm m$, we have $\mathbf e_{AB} = \left(\dfrac35,\,\dfrac45\right)$, the coefficient of $N_{AB}$ in the two equations at joint $A$. At joint $B$ the same member acts along the opposite unit vector $\mathbf e_{BA} = -\mathbf e_{AB}$, so the rows for $B$ contain $\left(-\dfrac35,\,-\dfrac45\right)$. The coefficients are the components of a unit vector, so vector $(3,\,4)$ must be divided by the member length.
