A cantilever truss is attached to a wall by a pin at $A=(0,0)$ and a roller providing a horizontal reaction at $D=(0,3)$. Coordinates are in meters. The other joints are $B=(4,0)$, $C=(8,0)$, $E=(4,3)$, and $F=(8,3)$, and the members are $AB$, $BC$, $DE$, $EF$, $AD$, $BE$, $CF$, $DB$, and $EC$. A downward load of $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $B$, and the other joints are unloaded. What is the roller reaction at $D$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,8,0;D,0,3;E,4,3;F,8,3" aste="A-B;B-C;D-E;E-F;A-D;B-E;C-F;D-B;E-C" vincoli="A,cerniera,180;D,carrello,180" reticolare=1 forze="B,0,-1.1,15 kN" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9;B,C,4 m,0.9;8.55 3,8.55 0,3 m,-0.55" titolo="Cantilever truss attached to the wall"}

- $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right
- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $40\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left

## Explanation

Isolate the entire truss and take moments about $A$, where the pin reaction has zero moment. The load has moment arm $4\,\mathrm m$ and produces a clockwise moment of magnitude $60\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The horizontal reaction at $D$ acts along line $y = 3$ with moment arm $3\,\mathrm m$. It must therefore produce a counterclockwise moment of $60\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. A horizontal force applied above $A$ produces a counterclockwise moment when it points to the left. Thus $H_D = \dfrac{60}{3} = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ **to the left**, and the wall restrains joint $D$. The value $40\,\mathrm{kN}$ results from using the entire span $8\,\mathrm m$ as the moment arm.
