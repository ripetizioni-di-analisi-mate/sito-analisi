The joints of the truss shown have coordinates $A=(0,0)$, $B=(6,0)$ and $C=(3,4)$, in meters. The members are $AB$, $AC$ and $BC$. Joint $A$ has a pin support, and joint $B$ has a roller on a horizontal surface. A force of $56\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward at $C$. Find the axial force in member $AC$, taking tension as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,3,4" aste="A-B;A-C;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" reticolare=1 forze="C,0,-0.9,56 kN" quote="A,3 0,3 m;3 0,B,3 m;6.45 4,6.45 0,4 m,-0.55" titolo="Specified geometry, supports, and loads"}

- $-35\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $35\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-28\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-70\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The load and the geometry are symmetric. Member $AC$ must exert an upward force component of $28\,\mathrm{kN}$ on $C$. The vertical component of its unit direction vector has magnitude $\dfrac{4}{5}$, so $N_{AC}=-35\,\mathrm{kN}$.
