Four members meet at unloaded joint $O$ in two collinear pairs. Members $OA$ and $OB$ are horizontal, with $A$ to the left and $B$ to the right of $O$. Members $OC$ and $OD$ lie on the line with direction $(3,\,4)$, with $C$ to the upper right and $D$ to the lower left. The known member forces are $N_{OA} = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $N_{OC} = -10\,\mathrm{kN}$, positive in tension. What are $N_{OB}$ and $N_{OD}$?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;A,-3,0,-;B,3,0,-;C,1.8,2.4,-;D,-1.8,-2.4,-" aste="O-A;O-B;O-C;O-D" etichette="-3.2,0.3,A;3.2,0.3,B;2,2.6,C;-2,-2.7,D" titolo="Unloaded joint with two pairs of collinear members"}

- $N_{OB} = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $N_{OD} = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $N_{OB} = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $N_{OD} = -10\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $N_{OB} = -20\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $N_{OD} = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $N_{OB} = 26\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $N_{OD} = 0\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The unit vectors pointing away from $O$ are $(-1,\,0)$ toward $A$, $(1,\,0)$ toward $B$, $\left(\dfrac35,\,\dfrac45\right)$ toward $C$, and $\left(-\dfrac35,\,-\dfrac45\right)$ toward $D$. Project the joint equilibrium equation onto the normal $\left(\dfrac45,\,-\dfrac35\right)$ to line $CD$. Members $OC$ and $OD$ have zero projection on this normal. This gives $-\dfrac45 N_{OA} + \dfrac45 N_{OB} = 0$, so $N_{OB} = N_{OA} = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$. Similarly, projecting onto the vertical, which is normal to line $AB$, gives $\dfrac45 N_{OC} - \dfrac45 N_{OD} = 0$, hence $N_{OD} = N_{OC} = -10\,\mathrm{kN}$. At an unloaded joint where four members meet in two collinear pairs, the members in each pair therefore carry the same axial force. The value $26\,\mathrm{kN}$ comes from using horizontal equilibrium alone and incorrectly setting $N_{OD} = 0$.
