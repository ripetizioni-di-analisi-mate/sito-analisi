A truss with an overhang has bottom chord joints $A=(0,0)$, $B=(3,0)$, $C=(6,0)$, and $T=(9,0)$ and top chord joints $D=(0,2)$, $E=(3,2)$, and $F=(6,2)$. Coordinates are in meters. The truss consists of members $AB$, $BC$, $CT$, $DE$, $EF$, $AD$, $BE$, and $CF$ and diagonals $DB$, $EC$, and $FT$. A pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $C$ support the truss, leaving the portion between $C$ and $T$ as an overhang. A downward load of $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $T$. What is the axial force in top chord member $EF$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;T,9,0;D,0,2;E,3,2;F,6,2" aste="A-B;B-C;C-T;D-E;E-F;A-D;B-E;C-F;D-B;E-C;F-T" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" reticolare=1 forze="T,0,-1,10 kN" quote="A,B,3 m,0.9;B,C,3 m,0.9;C,T,3 m,0.9;D,A,2 m,1.0" titolo="Truss with an overhang beyond the roller support"}

- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression
- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $45\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension

## Explanation

A section between $x = 3$ and $x = 6$ cuts $EF$, $EC$, and $BC$. The lines of action of $EC$ and $BC$ intersect at $C$. Isolate the right portion, which contains $C$, $F$, and $T$. The roller reaction on this portion acts through $C$ and therefore has zero moment about that point. The load at $T$ has moment arm $3\,\mathrm m$ and produces a clockwise moment of magnitude $30\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The force that $EF$ exerts on joint $F$ is $N_{EF}(-1,\,0)$. Taking counterclockwise moments as positive, its moment about $C$ is $2N_{EF}$, so $2N_{EF} - 30 = 0$ and $N_{EF} = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$. The top chord above the support at the root of the overhang is **in tension**, like the top fibers of a beam under a negative bending moment.
