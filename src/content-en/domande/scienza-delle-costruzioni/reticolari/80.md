Two steel members suspend joint $C=(0,0)$ from pins $A=(-4,3)$ and $B=(4,3)$. Coordinates are in meters, and each member has cross-sectional area $250\,\mathrm{mm^2}$. A load of $60\,\mathrm{kN}$ hangs from $C$. What is the normal stress in the members?

::struttura{nodi="C,0,0;A,-4,3;B,4,3" aste="C-A;C-B" vincoli="A,cerniera,90;B,cerniera,90" reticolare=1 forze="C,0,-1.1,60 kN" titolo="Joint suspended from two symmetric members"}

- $200\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $120\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $240\,\mathrm{MPa}$
- $150\,\mathrm{MPa}$

## Explanation

Each member is $5\,\mathrm m$ long, and its unit vector pointing away from $C$ has vertical component $\dfrac35$. By symmetry, the member forces are equal. Vertical equilibrium of the joint gives $2\cdot\dfrac35 N - 60 = 0$, hence a tensile force of $N = 50\,\mathrm{kN}$ in each member. The mean normal stress is
$$
\sigma = \frac{N}{A} = \frac{50\,000\,\mathrm N}{250\,\mathrm{mm^2}} = 200\,\mathrm{MPa}.
$$
The value $120\,\mathrm{MPa}$ simply assigns half the load to each member without accounting for its inclination. The value $150\,\mathrm{MPa}$ results from using $\dfrac45$ in place of $\dfrac35$.
