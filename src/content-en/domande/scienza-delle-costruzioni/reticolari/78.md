A triangular truss has joints $A=(0,0)$, $B=(12,0)$, and $C=(6,8)$ (coordinates in meters) and members $AB$, $AC$, and $BC$. It has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. A downward load of $32\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $C$, and the roller reaction is $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. A student obtained $N_{AC} = N_{BC} = -20\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $N_{AB} = -12\,\mathrm{kN}$ and checks these results by considering equilibrium of joint $B$. What resultant force on $B$ follows from these values?

- $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right
- Zero
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right
- $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left

## Explanation

Joint $B$ is subject to reaction $(0,\,16)\,\mathrm{kN}$, the force from member $AB$ with unit vector $\mathbf e_{BA} = (-1,\,0)$, and the force from member $BC$ with unit vector $\mathbf e_{BC} = \left(-\dfrac35,\,\dfrac45\right)$. The student's values give $-12\,(-1,\,0) = (12,\,0)$ and $-20\left(-\dfrac35,\,\dfrac45\right) = (12,\,-16)$, both in $\mathrm{kN}$. The resultant is $(12 + 12,\,16 - 16) = (24,\,0)\,\mathrm{kN}$. The joint is in vertical equilibrium, but there is an unbalanced horizontal force. The error is in $N_{AB}$. Its correct value is $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension, which makes the resultant zero.
