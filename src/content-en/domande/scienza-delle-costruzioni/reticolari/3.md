The coordinates of the truss joints shown are $A=(0,0)$, $B=(6,0)$, and $C=(3,4)$, in meters. The members are $AB$, $AC$, and $BC$. Joint $A$ is pinned and joint $B$ is supported by a roller on a horizontal surface. A rightward force of $30\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $C$. Determine the axial force in member $BC$, taking tension as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,3,4" aste="A-B;A-C;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" reticolare=1 forze="C,0.9,0,30 kN" quote="A,3 0,3 m;3 0,B,3 m;6.45 4,6.45 0,4 m,-0.55" titolo="Specified geometry, supports, and loads"}

- $25\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-30\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-50\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-25\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

At joint $C$ the two vertical components must cancel. The members make angles of equal magnitude, so $N_{AC}=-N_{BC}$. The horizontal equation gives $\dfrac{6}{5}N_{BC}+30=0$.
