The bottom chord of a truss includes a horizontal member of span $L = 4\,\mathrm m$ between two joints. A downward load of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ is applied directly at the midpoint of the member instead of at a joint. Model the member as a beam pinned at both ends. What is the magnitude of the bending moment at the midpoint of the member?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0" aste="A-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="2 0,0,-0.9,12 kN" quote="A,B,4 m" titolo="Chord member loaded between two joints"}

- $6\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $24\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

Because the load acts between joints, this is not a two-force member and local bending occurs. For the pin-ended beam under a midspan force, each reaction is $\dfrac{P}{2} = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$. The midspan moment equals the reaction times its moment arm $\dfrac{L}{2}$, so
$$
M = \frac{P}{2}\cdot\frac{L}{2} = \frac{PL}{4} = \frac{12\cdot 4}{4}\,\mathrm{kN\,m} = 12\,\mathrm{kN\,m}.
$$
The value $\dfrac{PL}{8}$ applies to a uniformly distributed load. A model allowing only axial forces would give zero moment and would miss this local bending.
