A truss has a pin at $A$ and a bilateral roller providing a vertical reaction at $B$. The joint $C$ lies beyond the roller. The joint coordinates in meters are $A\,(0,\,0)$, $B\,(3,\,0)$, $C\,(6,\,0)$, and $D\,(3,\,4)$. Its members are $AB$, $BC$, $AD$, $BD$, and $CD$. A downward load $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $C$. What is the axial force in vertical member $BD$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;D,3,4" aste="A-B;B-C;A-D;B-D;C-D" reticolare="1" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,-0,-0.96,10 kN" titolo="Load on the overhanging joint"}

- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression
- $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression
- $30\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression

## Explanation

Taking moments about $B$ gives $R_{Ay}=-10\,\mathrm{kN}$. At joint $A$ the force from $AD$ must therefore supply an upward component of $10\,\mathrm{kN}$. At joint $C$, member $CD$ supplies the same upward component to balance the load. At $D$ the forces from $AD$ and $CD$ are opposite to those at the lower joints and sum to $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. Vertical $BD$ must push $D$ upward with force $20\,\mathrm{kN}$.
