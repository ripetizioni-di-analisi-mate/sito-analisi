An initially unloaded member of length $5\,\mathrm m$ connects two fixed pin supports. Its axial rigidity is $EA = 20\,000\,\mathrm{kN}$ and its coefficient of thermal expansion is $\alpha = 1.2\cdot10^{-5}\,\mathrm K^{-1}$. The temperature of the member rises uniformly by $30\,\mathrm K$. What is the axial force, taking tension as positive?

- $7.2\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-36\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-7.2\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The two fixed pin supports prevent any change in the distance between the ends, so the total elongation of the member is zero. The total elongation is the sum of the elastic elongation $\dfrac{NL}{EA}$ and the thermal elongation $\alpha\,\Delta T\,L$, so $\dfrac{NL}{EA} + \alpha\,\Delta T\,L = 0$. The length cancels, leaving
$$
N = -EA\,\alpha\,\Delta T = -20\,000\cdot 1.2\cdot10^{-5}\cdot 30\,\mathrm{kN} = -7.2\,\mathrm{kN}.
$$
The member is **in compression**, and its axial force is independent of length. The value $-36\,\mathrm{kN}$ results from also multiplying by $L$ in error.
