A section through a truss intersects three parallel horizontal members. The three member forces are sought using only the three planar equilibrium equations for one part. What is the obstruction?

- All three member forces always have zero moment about every point.
- Parallel members must carry equal forces.
- The truss must be a mechanism.
- None of the three member forces enters vertical equilibrium.

## Explanation

All three member forces have only a horizontal component. Horizontal force equilibrium and moment equilibrium provide at most two independent equations in the three unknowns. Another section or a joint equation is needed.
