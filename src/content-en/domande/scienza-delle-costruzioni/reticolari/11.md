The coordinates of the truss joints shown are $A=(0,0)$, $B=(4,0)$, $C=(8,0)$, $D=(0,3)$, $E=(4,3)$, and $F=(8,3)$, in meters. The members are $AB$, $BC$, $AD$, $BE$, $CF$, $DE$, $EF$, $BD$, and $CE$. Joint $A$ is pinned and joint $C$ is supported by a roller on a horizontal surface. Two forces act at $F$, one of $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left and one of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. What is the axial force in member $DE$, taking tension as positive?

- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-16\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-24\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-8\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Horizontal equilibrium of the truss gives $H_A=16\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right. Moment equilibrium about $A$ gives $8V_C+16\cdot3-12\cdot8=0$, so $V_C=6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $V_A=12-6=6\,\mathrm{kN}$.

The section through the first panel cuts members $DE$, $BD$, and $AB$, and the left part contains joints $A$ and $D$.

Members $BD$ and $AB$ pass through $B$, and $H_A$ also acts along line $AB$, so in the moment equilibrium of the left part about $B$ the only unknown is $N_{DE}$.

The force $N_{DE}$ acts horizontally at $D$ with lever arm $3\,\mathrm{m}$, and $V_A$ has lever arm $4\,\mathrm{m}$. Taking counterclockwise as positive, this gives $-4\cdot6-3N_{DE}=0$.

Hence $N_{DE}=-8\,\mathrm{kN}$, so the top chord is in compression.
