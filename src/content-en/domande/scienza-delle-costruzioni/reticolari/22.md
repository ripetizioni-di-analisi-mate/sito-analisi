The deck of a truss bridge rests on longitudinal stringers simply supported by floor beams. Each floor beam transfers its load to a bottom-chord joint. The joints are spaced $4\,\mathrm m$ apart, and the deck transmits a uniform load of $5\,\mathrm{kN/m}$ along the truss span. What load acts at an interior joint of the bottom chord?

- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $40\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $5\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Each stringer spans a distance $4\,\mathrm m$ between two floor beams and carries $5\cdot 4 = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$. Because the beam is simply supported and the load is uniform, each support receives half the load, $10\,\mathrm{kN}$. An interior joint supports two adjacent spans and therefore receives $10 + 10 = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$. An end joint belongs to only one span and receives $10\,\mathrm{kN}$.
