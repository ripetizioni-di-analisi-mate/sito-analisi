The truss shown has joints $A=(0,0)$, $B=(4,0)$, $C=(8,0)$, $D=(2,3)$, and $E=(6,3)$ (coordinates in meters) and members $AB$, $BC$, $DE$, $AD$, $DB$, $BE$, and $EC$. There is a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $C$. Joints $D$ and $E$ each carry a downward load of $20\,\mathrm{kN}$. What are the axial forces in the interior diagonals $DB$ and $BE$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,8,0;D,2,3;E,6,3" aste="A-B;B-C;D-E;A-D;D-B;B-E;E-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" reticolare=1 forze="D,0,-1.1,20 kN;E,0,-1.1,20 kN" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9;B,C,4 m,0.9;8.55 3,8.55 0,3 m,-0.55" tratteggi="4,-0.5 4,3.6" titolo="Symmetric truss under symmetric loads"}

- $DB$ is in tension and $BE$ in compression, with equal magnitudes
- Both are in compression, with equal force magnitudes
- Both are in tension, with equal magnitudes
- Both are zero

## Explanation

The structure and loads are symmetric about line $x = 4$. Since the solution is unique, symmetric members carry equal axial forces, so $N_{DB} = N_{BE}$. At unloaded joint $B$, chord members $AB$ and $BC$ are horizontal. The unit vectors from $B$ toward $D$ and toward $E$ have the same vertical component $\dfrac{3}{\sqrt{13}}$, so vertical equilibrium gives $\dfrac{3}{\sqrt{13}}(N_{DB} + N_{BE}) = 0$. Substituting $N_{DB} = N_{BE}$ gives $N_{DB} = N_{BE} = 0$. This agrees with the zero shear in the central panel, since to the left of $x = 4$ the reaction at $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ balances the load at $D$.
