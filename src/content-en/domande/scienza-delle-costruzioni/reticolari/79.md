Only two members meet at pin support $A=(0,0)$. Member $AB$ runs horizontally toward $B=(4,0)$ and has $N_{AB} = 24\,\mathrm{kN}$. Member $AC$ runs toward $C=(4,3)$ and has $N_{AC} = -30\,\mathrm{kN}$. Coordinates are in meters, and axial forces are positive in tension. No load is applied at $A$. What is the pin reaction?

- Both $H_A = 0$ and $V_A = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward
- $H_A = 48\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left and $V_A = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- Both $H_A = 0$ and $V_A = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- Both $H_A = 0$ and $V_A = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward

## Explanation

The members exert forces $24\,(1,\,0) = (24,\,0)\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $-30\left(\dfrac45,\,\dfrac35\right) = (-24,\,-18)\,\mathrm{kN}$ on joint $A$. Their sum is $(0,\,-18)\,\mathrm{kN}$. For the joint to be in equilibrium, the pin must exert an equal and opposite force. Thus $H_A = 0$ and $V_A = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$ **upward**. Comparing this reaction with the one obtained from overall equilibrium gives an independent check on the two member forces.
