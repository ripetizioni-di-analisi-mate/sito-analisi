The truss shown has a horizontal bottom chord with joints $A=(0,0)$, $B=(4,0)$, and $C=(8,0)$ and a sloping top chord with joints $D=(0,2)$, $E=(4,3)$, and $F=(8,4)$. Coordinates are in meters. The truss consists of members $AB$, $BC$, $DE$, $EF$, $AD$, $BE$, and $CF$ and diagonals $DB$ and $EC$. A section through the first panel cuts $DE$, $DB$, and $AB$. About which point should moments be taken to determine $N_{DB}$ directly?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,8,0;D,0,2;E,4,3;F,8,4" aste="A-B;B-C;D-E;E-F;A-D;B-E;C-F;D-B;E-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" reticolare=1 tratteggi="2,-0.6 2,3.2" etichette="2.2,3.2,s" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9;B,C,4 m,0.9" titolo="Nonparallel chords and section $s$"}

- Joint $B=(4,0)$
- Point $(-8,\,0)$
- Joint $D=(0,2)$
- Joint $A=(0,0)$

## Explanation

To eliminate the forces in members $DE$ and $AB$, take moments about a point on both lines of action. The line of action of $AB$ is the $y = 0$ axis. The line through member $DE$ passes through $(0,2)$ and $(4,3)$, so its equation is $y = 2 + \dfrac{x}{4}$. It intersects the $y = 0$ axis at $x = -8$. About point $(-8,\,0)$, the moments of $N_{DE}$ and $N_{AB}$ vanish. Only $N_{DB}$ has a nonzero moment arm. Taking moments about joint $B$ eliminates $DB$ and $AB$ and gives $N_{DE}$. Taking moments about joint $D$ gives $N_{AB}$, while taking moments about $A$ eliminates only $AB$.
