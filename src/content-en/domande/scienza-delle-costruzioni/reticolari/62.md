Only two members meet at joint $F$ of a truss. Chord $EF$ extends horizontally to the left from $F$, and vertical $CF$ extends downward from $F$. A leftward horizontal force of $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $F$. What are the axial forces in the two members?

::struttura{nodi="F,0,0;E,-3,0;C,0,-3" aste="F-E;F-C" reticolare=1 forze="F,-1,0,10 kN" titolo="Corner joint with a horizontal force"}

- $EF$ carrying tension $10\,\mathrm{kN}$, with $CF$ carrying zero force
- $EF$ and $CF$ both carry zero force
- $EF$ in compression with $10\,\mathrm{kN}$, and $CF$ unloaded
- $EF$ carrying compression $10\,\mathrm{kN}$, with $CF$ carrying tension $10\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The unit vector from $F$ to $E$ is $(-1,\,0)$, and the unit vector from $F$ to $C$ is $(0,\,-1)$. Only the vertical enters the vertical equilibrium equation, giving $-N_{CF} = 0$. Thus $CF$ carries zero force. Horizontal equilibrium gives $-N_{EF} - 10 = 0$, hence $N_{EF} = -10\,\mathrm{kN}$. The chord is **in compression** because its force pushes joint $F$ toward $E$. The rule for two zero-force members does not apply because the joint is loaded. However, a load acting along one of the two members leaves the other at zero force.
