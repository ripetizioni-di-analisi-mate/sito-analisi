The coordinates of the truss joints shown are $A=(0,0)$, $B=(4,0)$, $C=(8,0)$, $D=(0,3)$, $E=(4,3)$, and $F=(8,3)$, in meters. The members are $AB$, $BC$, $AD$, $BE$, $CF$, $DE$, $EF$, $AE$, and $BF$. Joint $A$ is pinned and joint $C$ is supported by a roller on a horizontal surface. A downward force of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $E$. A downward force of $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $F$. Determine the axial force in member $AB$, taking tension as positive.

- $-8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $24\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The reaction at $A$ is $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ because $V_C=24\,\mathrm{kN}$. At unloaded joint $D$, member $AD$ carries zero force. Diagonal $AE$, in compression, must therefore supply a vertical component of $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $A$. Chord $AB$ balances $6\cdot\dfrac{4}{3}$ horizontally.
