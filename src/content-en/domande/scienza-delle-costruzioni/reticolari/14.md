The coordinates of the truss joints shown are $A=(0,0)$, $B=(6,0)$, and $C=(3,4)$, in meters. The members are $AB$, $AC$, and $BC$. Joint $A$ is pinned and joint $B$ is supported by a roller on a horizontal surface. A downward force of $40\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $C$. All members have axial rigidity $EA=25\,000\,\mathrm{kN}$. What is the change in length of $AC$ in millimeters, taking elongation as positive?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,3,4" aste="A-B;A-C;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" reticolare=1 forze="C,0,-0.9,40 kN" quote="A,3 0,3 m;3 0,B,3 m;6.45 4,6.45 0,4 m,-0.55" titolo="Specified geometry, supports, and loads"}

- $5\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $-25\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $-5\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $-1\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The structure and the load are symmetric about the vertical line through $C$, so members $AC$ and $BC$ carry the same force.

Member $AC$ is $5\,\mathrm{m}$ long and rises $4\,\mathrm{m}$, so at joint $C$ the unit vector of each of the two members has vertical component $-\dfrac{4}{5}$.

Vertical equilibrium of joint $C$ gives $-2\cdot\dfrac{4}{5}N_{AC}-40=0$, so $N_{AC}=-25\,\mathrm{kN}$, in compression.

By Hooke's law the change in length is
$$
\Delta L_{AC}=\frac{N_{AC}L_{AC}}{EA}=\frac{-25\cdot5}{25\,000}\,\mathrm{m}=-0.005\,\mathrm{m}.
$$
Therefore the member shortens and $\Delta L_{AC}=-5\,\mathrm{mm}$.
