In a triangular truss with $A=(0,0)$, $B=(6,0)$ and $C=(3,4)$ in metres, $A$ is fixed. During a test, a sensor measures a shortening of member $AC$ by $5\,\mathrm{mm}$. A sensor measures a horizontal displacement of $+2\,\mathrm{mm}$ at $C$. What is the first-order vertical displacement at $C$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,3,4" aste="A-B;A-C;B-C" reticolare=1 vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" tratteggi="3,0 3,4" quote="A,3 0,3 m;3 0,B,3 m;3 0,C,4 m,-0.65" titolo="Triangle in the initial test configuration"}

- $-5\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $-6.25\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $-7.75\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $+7.75\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

The change in length is the projection of the displacement at $C$ onto the original direction of $AC$, whose unit vector is $(\dfrac35,\dfrac45)$. Thus $\dfrac35\cdot2+\dfrac45v_C=-5$, giving $v_C=-7.75\,\mathrm{mm}$.
