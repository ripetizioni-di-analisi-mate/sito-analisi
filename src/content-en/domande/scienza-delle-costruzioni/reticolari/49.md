A cantilever truss is attached to a vertical wall by pins $A=(0,0)$ and $B=(0,3)$. Coordinates are in meters. Member $BC$ is horizontal and member $AC$ is inclined, with $C=(4,3)$. A load of $30\,\mathrm{kN}$ hangs from $C$. What is the axial force in horizontal member $BC$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,3;C,4,3" aste="A-C;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera,180;B,cerniera,180" reticolare=1 forze="C,0,-1,30 kN" quote="B,C,4 m,0.5;-0.6 0,-0.6 3,3 m,-0.5" titolo="Two-member cantilever attached to a wall"}

- $40\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $40\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression
- $30\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $50\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension

## Explanation

Only the two members and the applied load act at joint $C$. The unit vector from $C$ to $A$ is $\left(-\dfrac45,\,-\dfrac35\right)$ because member $AC$ has length $5\,\mathrm m$, while the unit vector from $C$ to $B$ is $(-1,\,0)$. The force in member $BC$ has no vertical component. Vertical equilibrium therefore involves only $AC$ and gives $-\dfrac35 N_{AC} - 30 = 0$, that is, $N_{AC} = -50\,\mathrm{kN}$. Horizontal equilibrium gives $-\dfrac45 N_{AC} - N_{BC} = 0$, so $N_{BC} = 40\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the horizontal member is **in tension**. The value $50\,\mathrm{kN}$ is the force magnitude in strut $AC$, which is in compression.
