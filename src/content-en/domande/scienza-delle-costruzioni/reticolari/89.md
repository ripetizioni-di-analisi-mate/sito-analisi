At a welded truss joint, the axes of the two chord members intersect at $O$. The diagonal carries an axial force of $40\,\mathrm{kN}$, and its axis passes at a perpendicular distance of $50\,\mathrm{mm}$ from $O$. What couple must the connection transmit to maintain joint equilibrium?

- $2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $0\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$
- $0.2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$

## Explanation

The ideal truss model requires the axes of the members at a joint to pass through one point. The joint described here fails to meet that condition. The two chord forces act along lines through $O$ and therefore have zero moment about $O$. The diagonal force has moment arm $e = 50\,\mathrm{mm} = 0.05\,\mathrm m$ about $O$, so its moment has magnitude $N e = 40\cdot 0.05 = 2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. Moment equilibrium of the joint therefore requires an opposing couple of magnitude $2\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The connection transmits this couple to the members, causing them to bend. A zero moment corresponds to the ideal model in which all member axes intersect at a single point.
