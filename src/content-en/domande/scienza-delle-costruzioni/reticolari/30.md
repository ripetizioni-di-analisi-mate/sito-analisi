The truss shown has joints $A=(0,0)$, $B=(4,0)$, $C=(8,0)$, $D=(0,3)$, $E=(4,3)$, and $F=(8,3)$, with coordinates in meters. The members are $AB$, $BC$, $DE$, $EF$, $AD$, $BE$, and $CF$ and the two diagonals $AE$ and $BD$ in the left panel, which cross without a joint. The right panel has no diagonal. There is a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $C$. Which statement is true?

- The structure is statically determinate because $m + r = 2j$
- The structure is stable and statically indeterminate to the first degree
- The truss is unstable and also admits a state of self-stress
- The truss is unstable and admits no state of self-stress

## Explanation

The count balances because $m + r = 9 + 3 = 12 = 2j$, but this is only a necessary condition. The left panel with two diagonals has one redundant member and therefore admits a nonzero set of member forces in equilibrium under zero load. The right panel has no diagonal and therefore permits infinitesimal joint motion without a first-order change in any member length. The equilibrium matrix therefore has rank $\rho = 11$. The degree of static indeterminacy is $m + r - \rho = 1$, and the degree of kinematic instability is $2j - \rho = 1$.
