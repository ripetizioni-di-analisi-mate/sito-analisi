Two triangular trusses are connected by hinge $B=(4,4)$ and supported at fixed pins $A=(0,0)$ and $C=(8,0)$, with coordinates in meters. The left truss has members $AP$, $PB$, and $AB$, with $P=(2,3)$. The right truss has members $BQ$, $QC$, and $BC$, with $Q=(6,3)$. A downward load of $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at joint $P$. What is the horizontal component of the reaction at $C$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;P,2,3;B,4,4;Q,6,3;C,8,0" aste="A-P;P-B;A-B;B-Q;Q-C;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,cerniera" reticolare=1 forze="P,0,-1.1,24 kN" quote="A,4 0,4 m,0.9;4 0,C,4 m,0.9;8.6 4,8.6 0,4 m,-0.55" titolo="Three-hinged trussed arch"}

- $0\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right
- $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left

## Explanation

The right truss receives forces only at pins $B$ and $C$. It therefore acts as a two-force body, and the reaction at $C$ acts along line $CB$. The vector from $C$ to $B$ is $(-4,\,4)$. The horizontal and vertical components of the reaction at $C$ therefore have equal magnitudes and opposite signs, giving $H_C = -V_C$. Now take moments for the entire structure about $A$, where the pin reaction has zero moment. The load has moment arm $2\,\mathrm m$, so $8V_C - 24\cdot 2 = 0$ and $V_C = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. It follows that $H_C = -6\,\mathrm{kN}$. The horizontal component is $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ **to the left**, and the structure exerts thrust on the supports like an arch. A roller at $C$ would give a zero value.
