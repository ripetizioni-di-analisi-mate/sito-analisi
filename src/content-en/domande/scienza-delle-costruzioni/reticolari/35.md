A cantilever truss is pinned at $A$ and has a bilateral roller with a horizontal reaction at $B$. The joint coordinates in meters are $A\,(0,\,0)$, $B\,(0,\,4)$, $C\,(3,\,0)$, $D\,(3,\,4)$, and $E\,(6,\,0)$. Its members are $AB$, $AC$, $BD$, $CD$, $BC$, $CE$, and $DE$. The loads at $E$ are $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. What are the axial forces in $DE$ and $CE$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,0,4;C,3,0;D,3,4;E,6,0" aste="A-B;A-C;B-D;C-D;B-C;C-E;D-E" reticolare="1" vincoli="A,cerniera,180;B,carrello,180" forze="E,0.96,0,4 kN;E,-0,-0.96,16 kN" titolo="Free joint E of the cantilever truss"}

- $DE$ carrying $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension and $CE$ carrying $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $DE$ carrying $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension and $CE$ carrying $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $DE$ carrying $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression and $CE$ carrying $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression
- $DE$ carrying $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension and $CE$ carrying $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression

## Explanation

At the free joint $E$, only member $DE$ contributes a vertical force component. We have $\left(\dfrac{4}{5}\right) N_{DE}=16$, so $N_{DE}=20\,\mathrm{kN}$. Its horizontal component acts to the left and has magnitude $12\,\mathrm{kN}$. Taking the force in member $CE$ as positive when it pulls $E$ to the left gives $N_{CE}=4-12=-8\,\mathrm{kN}$.
