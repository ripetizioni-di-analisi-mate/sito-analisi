A roof truss has a bottom chord $ABCDE$ with joints at horizontal coordinates $0$, $4$, $8$, $12$, and $16\,\mathrm m$. Its upper joints are $F=(4,3)$, $G=(8,6)$, and $H=(12,3)$. The truss consists of the bottom chord segments, rafters $AF$, $FG$, $GH$, and $HE$, verticals $BF$, $CG$, and $DH$, and diagonals $FC$ and $HC$. There is a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $E$. Joints $F$, $G$, and $H$ each carry a downward load of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$. What is the axial force in strut $FG$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,8,0;D,12,0;E,16,0;F,4,3;G,8,6;H,12,3" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E;A-F;F-G;G-H;H-E;B-F;C-G;D-H;F-C;H-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,carrello" reticolare=1 forze="F,0,-1.3,12 kN;G,0,-1.3,12 kN;H,0,-1.3,12 kN" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9;B,C,4 m,0.9;C,D,4 m,0.9;D,E,4 m,0.9;16.6 6,16.6 0,6 m,-0.55" titolo="Pitched roof truss"}

- $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression
- $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $30\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression
- $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression

## Explanation

By symmetry each reaction is $18\,\mathrm{kN}$. A section between $x = 4$ and $x = 8$ cuts $FG$, $FC$, and $BC$. The lines of action of $FC$ and $BC$ intersect at $C=(8,0)$. Taking counterclockwise moments as positive, the external forces on the left portion produce a moment of $-18\cdot 8 + 12\cdot 4 = -96\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ about $C$. The force that $FG$ exerts on joint $F$ is $N_{FG}\left(\dfrac45,\,\dfrac35\right)$, and its moment about $C$ is
$$
(4-8)\cdot\frac35 N_{FG} - 3\cdot\frac45 N_{FG} = -\frac{24}{5}N_{FG}.
$$
Thus $-96 - \dfrac{24}{5}N_{FG} = 0$ and $N_{FG} = -20\,\mathrm{kN}$, so the rafter is **in compression**. The value $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ results from dividing the moment by the height of $G$ as though the rafter were horizontal.
