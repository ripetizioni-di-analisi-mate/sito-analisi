A K-truss has bottom chord joints $A=(0,0)$, $B=(4,0)$, $C=(8,0)$, and $D=(12,0)$ and top chord joints $E=(0,6)$, $F=(4,6)$, $G=(8,6)$, and $H=(12,6)$. Coordinates are in meters. The verticals at $B$ and $C$ have joints $M=(4,3)$ and $K=(8,3)$ at midheight, respectively. The central panel has diagonals running from $K$ to $F$ and from $K$ to $B$. There is a pin at $A$, a roller on a horizontal surface at $D$, and a downward load of $36\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $G$. What is the axial force in bottom chord member $BC$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,8,0;D,12,0;E,0,6;F,4,6;G,8,6;H,12,6;M,4,3;K,8,3" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;E-F;F-G;G-H;A-E;B-M;M-F;C-K;K-G;D-H;M-E;M-A;K-F;K-B;C-H" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,carrello" reticolare=1 forze="G,0,-1.3,36 kN" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9;B,C,4 m,0.9;C,D,4 m,0.9;12.6 6,12.6 0,6 m,-0.55" titolo="K-truss loaded at $G$"}

- $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $32\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $16\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression
- $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension

## Explanation

Taking moments about $D$ gives $12V_A - 36\cdot 4 = 0$, hence $V_A = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$. A section through the central panel cuts four members, $FG$, $KF$, $KB$, and $BC$. At joint $K$, the two segments of the vertical member are vertical and the diagonals have outward unit vectors $\left(-\dfrac45,\,\dfrac35\right)$ and $\left(-\dfrac45,\,-\dfrac35\right)$. Horizontal equilibrium of $K$ therefore gives $N_{KB} = -N_{KF}$. The horizontal components of the two diagonal forces on the left portion cancel, so horizontal equilibrium of that portion gives $N_{FG} = -N_{BC}$. Both diagonal forces pass through $K$, and the chord forces each have moment arm $3\,\mathrm m$. Taking counterclockwise moments as positive, moment equilibrium of the left portion gives $-12\cdot 8 + 3N_{BC} - 3N_{FG} = 0$. Substituting $N_{FG} = -N_{BC}$ gives $6N_{BC} = 96$ and $N_{BC} = 16\,\mathrm{kN}$, corresponding to **tension**. The force magnitude equals moment $96\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ divided by height $6\,\mathrm m$. The value $32\,\mathrm{kN}$ results from dividing the moment by the arm $3\,\mathrm m$ and neglecting the top chord.
