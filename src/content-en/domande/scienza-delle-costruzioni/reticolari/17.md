Member $AB$ of a truss connects joints $A=(0,0)$ and $B=(4,3)$, whose coordinates are in meters. Its axial force is $N_{AB} = -20\,\mathrm{kN}$, positive in tension. What are the components of the force exerted by the member on joint $A$, taking rightward and upward as positive?

- $(16,\,12)\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $(-12,\,-16)\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $(-16,\,-12)\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $(-16,\,12)\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The member length is $L_{AB} = \sqrt{4^2+3^2} = 5\,\mathrm m$, so the unit vector from $A$ to $B$ is $\mathbf e_{AB} = \left(\dfrac45,\,\dfrac35\right)$. The force that the member exerts on joint $A$ is $N_{AB}\,\mathbf e_{AB}$, so
$$
N_{AB}\,\mathbf e_{AB} = -20\left(\frac45,\,\frac35\right)\mathrm{kN} = (-16,\,-12)\,\mathrm{kN}.
$$
The force points from $B$ toward $A$, away from the member. The negative sign is consistent with this result because a member that is **in compression** pushes its end joints apart.
