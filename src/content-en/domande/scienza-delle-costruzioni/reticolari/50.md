A trussed tower has joints $A=(0,0)$, $B=(2,0)$, $C=(0,3)$, $D=(2,3)$, $E=(0,6)$, and $F=(2,6)$, with coordinates in meters. Its members are verticals $AC$, $CE$, $BD$, and $DF$, cross members $AB$, $CD$, and $EF$, and diagonals $AD$ and $CF$. There is a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. At $E$ the wind applies a rightward force of $6\,\mathrm{kN}$. What is the roller reaction at $B$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,0;C,0,3;D,2,3;E,0,6;F,2,6" aste="A-C;C-E;B-D;D-F;A-B;C-D;E-F;A-D;C-F" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" reticolare=1 forze="E,1,0,6 kN" quote="A,B,2 m;2.55 3,2.55 0,3 m,-0.55;2.55 6,2.55 3,3 m,-0.55" titolo="Wind force at $E$ on a truss"}

- $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward
- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward

## Explanation

Isolate the entire tower and take moments about $A$, where the pin reaction has zero moment. The wind force has a moment arm equal to the height of $E$, namely $6\,\mathrm m$, and tends to rotate the tower clockwise. Vertical reaction $V_B$ has moment arm $2\,\mathrm m$, giving $2V_B - 6\cdot 6 = 0$ and $V_B = 18\,\mathrm{kN}$ **upward**. Force equilibrium requires the pin at $A$ to provide $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward and $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left. The value $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ results from using the first-level elevation $3\,\mathrm m$ as the moment arm.
