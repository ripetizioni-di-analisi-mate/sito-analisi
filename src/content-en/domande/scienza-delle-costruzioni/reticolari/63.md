A truss of span $12\,\mathrm m$ and height $3\,\mathrm m$, supported by a pin at $A$ and a roller at $E$, has four panels of length $3\,\mathrm m$. Upper joints $F$, $G$, and $H$ lie above lower joints $B$, $C$, and $D$ and are connected to them by verticals $BF$, $CG$, and $DH$, respectively. The inclined members are $AF$, $FC$, $CH$, and $HE$. Each upper joint carries a load of $10\,\mathrm{kN}$. Which verticals are zero-force members?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;D,9,0;E,12,0;F,3,3;G,6,3;H,9,3" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E;F-G;G-H;A-F;H-E;B-F;C-G;D-H;F-C;C-H" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,carrello" reticolare=1 forze="F,0,-1,10 kN;G,0,-1,10 kN;H,0,-1,10 kN" quote="A,B,3 m,0.9;B,C,3 m,0.9;C,D,3 m,0.9;D,E,3 m,0.9;12.55 3,12.55 0,3 m,-0.55" titolo="Loads applied at upper chord joints"}

- $BF$ and $DH$
- $BF$, $CG$, and $DH$
- Only $CG$
- No vertical member

## Explanation

Horizontal chord segments $AB$ and $BC$ and vertical $BF$ meet at joint $B$, which has no external load. Only $N_{BF}$ enters the vertical equilibrium equation, so $N_{BF} = 0$ and, for the same reason, $N_{DH} = 0$. At joint $G$, segments $FG$ and $GH$ are horizontal and the applied load is $10\,\mathrm{kN}$. Vertical equilibrium therefore gives $-N_{CG} - 10 = 0$ and $N_{CG} = -10\,\mathrm{kN}$. The central vertical is therefore in compression, and $BF$ and $DH$ are the only zero-force verticals.
