In a two-panel truss, $A$ is the pin support and $C$ is a roller providing a vertical reaction. The joint coordinates in meters are $A\,(0,\,0)$, $B\,(3,\,0)$, $C\,(6,\,0)$, $D\,(0,\,4)$, $E\,(3,\,4)$, and $F\,(6,\,4)$. Its members are $AB$, $BC$, $DE$, $EF$, $AD$, $BE$, $CF$, $AE$, and $EC$. The downward loads at $E$ and $F$ are $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $6\,\mathrm{kN}$, respectively. What is the axial force in diagonal $AE$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;D,0,4;E,3,4;F,6,4" aste="A-B;B-C;D-E;E-F;A-D;B-E;C-F;A-E;E-C" reticolare="1" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" forze="E,-0,-0.96,24 kN;F,-0,-0.96,6 kN" tratteggi="1.5,-1.4 1.5,4.8" titolo="Section through the first panel"}

- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression
- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $30\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression
- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression

## Explanation

Taking moments about $C$ gives $R_{Ay}=\dfrac{24}{2}=12\,\mathrm{kN}$ because the load at $F$ has the same horizontal coordinate as $C$. A vertical section through the first panel cuts $AB$, $DE$, and $AE$. Chords $AB$ and $DE$ are horizontal. On the left portion only $AE$ balances the vertical reaction, giving $R_{Ay}+\left(\dfrac{4}{5}\right) N_{AE}=0$ and $N_{AE}=-15\,\mathrm{kN}$.
