A triangular truss has joints $A=(0,0)$, $B=(6,0)$, and $C=(3,4)$, with coordinates in meters, and members $AB$, $AC$, and $BC$. The supports at $A$ and $B$ are fixed pins. Which member can be removed to obtain a statically determinate structure?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,3,4" aste="A-B;A-C;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" reticolare=1 titolo="Triangular truss on two pin supports"}

- $AC$
- $AB$
- $BC$
- None, because removing a member makes the truss unstable

## Explanation

With all three members, $m + r = 3 + 4 = 7$ and $2j = 6$, so the structure is statically indeterminate to the first degree. Member $AB$ connects two joints already fixed by the pins, so it prevents no additional motion. Removing it leaves joint $C$ connected to the fixed points $A$ and $B$ by two noncollinear members, so the structure is **statically determinate**. Removing $AC$ or $BC$ instead leaves joint $C$ attached to a single member, free to rotate about the other end. The structure becomes unstable.
