The triangular truss is pinned at $A$ and supported at $B$ by a bilateral roller that provides a vertical reaction. The joint coordinates in meters are $A\,(0,\,0)$, $B\,(3,\,0)$, and $C\,(0,\,4)$. The loads at $C$ are $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right and $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ downward. What is the axial force in vertical member $AC$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,0,4" aste="A-B;A-C;B-C" reticolare="1" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" forze="C,0.65,0,6 kN;C,-0,-0.65,20 kN" titolo="Vertical member with two components of applied load"}

- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression
- $36\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression

## Explanation

At joint $C$ only member $BC$ contributes a horizontal force component. Its unit vector from $C$ to $B$ is $(0.6,\,-0.8)$. Therefore, $N_{BC}=-\dfrac{6 \times 5}{3}=-10\,\mathrm{kN}$. The force that $BC$ exerts on $C$ has an upward vertical component of $8\,\mathrm{kN}$. The vertical member contributes the term $-N_{AC}$ to vertical equilibrium, giving $N_{AC}=8-20=-12\,\mathrm{kN}$.
