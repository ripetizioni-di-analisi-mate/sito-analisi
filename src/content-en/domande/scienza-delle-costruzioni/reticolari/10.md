The coordinates of the truss joints shown are $A=(0,0)$, $B=(4,0)$, $C=(8,0)$, $D=(0,3)$, $E=(4,3)$, and $F=(8,3)$, in meters. The members are $AB$, $BC$, $AD$, $BE$, $CF$, $DE$, $EF$, $AE$, and $BF$. Joint $A$ is pinned and joint $C$ is supported by a roller on a horizontal surface. A downward force of $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $D$. A downward force of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $E$. What is the axial force in member $BF$, taking tension as positive?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,8,0;D,0,3;E,4,3;F,8,3" aste="A-B;B-C;A-D;B-E;C-F;D-E;E-F;A-E;B-F" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" reticolare=1 forze="D,0,-1.2,20 kN;E,0,-1.2,12 kN" quote="A,B,4 m;B,C,4 m;8.56 3,8.56 0,3 m,-0.55" tratteggi="6,-0.6 6,3.6" etichette="6.2,3.6,s" titolo="Specified geometry, supports, and loads"}

- $-10\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $20\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The load at $D$ acts on the vertical line through $A$, so moment equilibrium about $A$ gives $8V_C-12\cdot4=0$ and $V_C=6\,\mathrm{kN}$.

At joint $C$ the only member that is not horizontal is the vertical $CF$, so vertical equilibrium gives $N_{CF}+6=0$, that is $N_{CF}=-6\,\mathrm{kN}$.

At joint $F$ the compressed vertical pushes the joint upward with $6\,\mathrm{kN}$, the chord $EF$ is horizontal, and the diagonal $BF$ points toward $B$ with vertical component of the unit vector equal to $-\dfrac{3}{5}$.

Therefore vertical equilibrium of joint $F$ gives $6-\dfrac{3}{5}N_{BF}=0$, so $N_{BF}=10\,\mathrm{kN}$, in tension.
