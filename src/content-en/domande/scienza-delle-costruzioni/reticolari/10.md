At a truss joint a downward $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ force acts. One member pulls $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left. A second member, aligned with $(3,4)$, pulls to the right and upward. Which force in the second member closes the force polygon?

::struttura{nodi="C,0,0" forze="C,-1.5,0,9 kN;C,0,-1.5,12 kN;C,0.9,1.2,N" titolo="Directions of the joint forces"}

- $21\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $12\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The second member must supply components $(9,12)\,\mathrm{kN}$. They have the required ratio $3:4$ and magnitude $\sqrt{9^2+12^2}=15\,\mathrm{kN}$. This satisfies both joint equations.
