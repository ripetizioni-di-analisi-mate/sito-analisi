A panel has joints $A=(0,0)$, $B=(4,0)$, $C=(4,3)$, and $D=(0,3)$, with coordinates in meters. Its members are $AB$, $BC$, $CD$, and $DA$ and diagonals $AC$ and $BD$, which cross without a joint. There is a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. Which members can be removed individually to obtain a statically determinate structure?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,4,3;D,0,3" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-A;A-C;B-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" reticolare=1 quote="A,B,4 m;4.55 3,4.55 0,3 m,-0.55" titolo="Panel with two diagonals"}

- Any one of the six
- Only one of the two diagonals
- Only one of the four sides
- None, because every member is required

## Explanation

With all members present, $m + r = 6 + 3 = 9$ and $2j = 8$, so the structure is statically indeterminate to the first degree. Removing either diagonal leaves two rigid triangles formed by the other diagonal. Removing a side, for example $AB$, leaves triangle $ACD$ and joint $B$ connected to $C$ and $D$ by two noncollinear members. The remaining assembly is still rigid, and the pin and roller prevent rigid-body motion. The same argument applies to all four sides. Each removal therefore leaves $m + r = 8 = 2j$ and a stable structure, that is, **statically determinate**.
