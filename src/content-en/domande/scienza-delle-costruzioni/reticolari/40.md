For a plane truss with $j = 5$ joints, $m = 7$ members, and $r = 3$ reaction components, the equilibrium matrix $Q$ has rank $\rho = 9$. What are the degree of static indeterminacy and the number of independent mechanisms?

- Neither, because $m + r = 2j$
- $1$ degree of static indeterminacy and $1$ independent mechanism
- $1$ degree of static indeterminacy and no mechanism
- No static indeterminacy and $1$ independent mechanism

## Explanation

Matrix $Q$ has $2j = 10$ rows and $m + r = 10$ columns. There are $m + r - \rho = 10 - 9 = 1$ independent combinations of forces in equilibrium under zero load. The structure therefore has one state of self-stress and is statically indeterminate to the first degree. The number of independent infinitesimal mechanisms is $2j - \rho = 10 - 9 = 1$, so there is also a joint motion that leaves all member lengths unchanged to first order. The count $m + r = 2j$ is satisfied, but the rank is less than $10$, showing that the equations are dependent.
