The Warren truss has a pin at $A$ and a roller with a vertical reaction at $D$. The joint coordinates in meters are $A\,(0,\,0)$, $B\,(4,\,0)$, $C\,(8,\,0)$, $D\,(12,\,0)$, $E\,(2,\,3)$, $F\,(6,\,3)$, and $G\,(10,\,3)$. Its members are $AB$, $BC$, $CD$, $EF$, $FG$, $AE$, $EB$, $BF$, $FC$, $CG$, and $GD$. The downward loads at $E$ and $G$ are $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $12\,\mathrm{kN}$, respectively. What is the axial force in $BC$?

- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression
- $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension

## Explanation

Taking moments about $D$ for the entire truss gives $R_{Ay}=\dfrac{5 \times 18+12}{6}=17\,\mathrm{kN}$. A section between $B$ and $F$ cuts $BC$, $BF$, and $EF$. On the left portion, taking moments about $F$ eliminates the forces in $BF$ and $EF$ because their lines of action pass through $F$. Thus $3 N_{BC}=6 \times 17-4 \times 18$, giving $N_{BC}=10\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension.
