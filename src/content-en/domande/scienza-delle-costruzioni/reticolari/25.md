A truss consists of two adjacent rectangular panels without diagonals. The joints are $A=(0,0)$, $B=(4,0)$, $C=(8,0)$, $D=(0,3)$, $E=(4,3)$, and $F=(8,3)$, with coordinates in meters. Its members are $AB$, $BC$, $DE$, $EF$, $AD$, $BE$, and $CF$. There is a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $C$. How many independent mechanisms does the structure have?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,8,0;D,0,3;E,4,3;F,8,3" aste="A-B;B-C;D-E;E-F;A-D;B-E;C-F" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" reticolare=1 quote="A,B,4 m;B,C,4 m;8.55 3,8.55 0,3 m,-0.55" titolo="Two panels without diagonals"}

- $1$
- $2$
- $3$
- $0$

## Explanation

The equilibrium system has $2j = 12$ equations and $m + r = 7 + 3 = 10$ unknowns. With no load, only the zero combination of member forces and reactions satisfies equilibrium. The $10$ columns of the equilibrium matrix are therefore independent, and the rank is $\rho = 10$. The number of independent infinitesimal mechanisms is therefore $2j - \rho = 12 - 10 = 2$. This counts the independent joint motions that leave all member lengths unchanged to first order. Adding one diagonal in a panel eliminates one such motion.
