A braced panel has joints $A=(0,0)$, $B=(4,0)$, $C=(4,3)$, and $D=(0,3)$ (coordinates in meters), members $AB$, $BC$, $CD$, and $DA$, and diagonal $AC$. It has fixed pin supports at $A$ and $B$. Under the applied loads, the diagonal elongates by $2\,\mathrm{mm}$. Deformations of the other members are negligible. What is the first-order horizontal displacement of joint $C$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,4,3;D,0,3" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-A;A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" reticolare=1 quote="A,B,4 m,0.9;4.6 3,4.6 0,3 m,-0.55" titolo="Bracing panel on two pin supports"}

- $2\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $1.6\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $2.5\,\mathrm{mm}$
- $3.3\,\mathrm{mm}$

## Explanation

Vertical member $BC$ retains its length and joint $B$ is fixed, so the vertical displacement component at $C$ is zero. The diagonal has unit vector $\left(\dfrac45,\,\dfrac35\right)$ from $A$ toward $C$, and joint $A$ is fixed. Its elongation is therefore $\delta L_{AC} = \dfrac45 u_{C,x} + \dfrac35 u_{C,y}$. Substituting $u_{C,y} = 0$ gives $\dfrac45 u_{C,x} = 2\,\mathrm{mm}$, or $u_{C,x} = 2.5\,\mathrm{mm}$. The value $1.6\,\mathrm{mm}$ results from multiplying the elongation by $\dfrac45$ instead of dividing by it.
