A three-panel truss has a pin at $A$ and a roller providing a vertical reaction at $D$. The joint coordinates in meters are $A\,(0,\,0)$, $B\,(3,\,0)$, $C\,(6,\,0)$, $D\,(9,\,0)$, $E\,(0,\,4)$, $F\,(3,\,4)$, $G\,(6,\,4)$, and $H\,(9,\,4)$. Its members are $AB$, $BC$, $CD$, $EF$, $FG$, $GH$, $AE$, $BF$, $CG$, $DH$, $EB$, $BG$, and $GD$. The downward vertical loads are $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $F$, $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $G$, and $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $H$. What is the axial force in diagonal $BG$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,3,0;C,6,0;D,9,0;E,0,4;F,3,4;G,6,4;H,9,4" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;E-F;F-G;G-H;A-E;B-F;C-G;D-H;E-B;B-G;G-D" reticolare="1" vincoli="A,cerniera;D,carrello" forze="F,-0,-1.44,18 kN;G,-0,-1.44,6 kN;H,-0,-1.44,9 kN" tratteggi="4.5,-1.4 4.5,4.8" titolo="Section through the middle panel"}

- $17.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression
- $5\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $4\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension

## Explanation

Taking moments about $D$ gives $R_{Ay}=\dfrac{2 \times 18+6}{3}=14\,\mathrm{kN}$. A section through the panel between $B$ and $C$ cuts $BC$, $FG$, and $BG$. In the vertical force balance of the left portion, the load at $F$ is $-18\,\mathrm{kN}$ and the diagonal contributes $\left(\dfrac{4}{5}\right) N_{BG}$. Therefore, $N_{BG}=\dfrac{(18-14) \times 5}{4}=5\,\mathrm{kN}$.
