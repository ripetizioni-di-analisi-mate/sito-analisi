The truss shown has a bottom chord $ABCDE$ with joints at horizontal coordinates $0$, $4$, $8$, $12$, and $16\,\mathrm m$. The joints of top chord $FGHIJ$ have the same horizontal coordinates and lie at elevation $3\,\mathrm m$. The truss consists of the chord members, five verticals, and diagonals $FB$, $GC$, $IC$, and $JD$. There is a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $E$. Joints $B$, $C$, and $D$ each carry a downward load of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$. What is the axial force in top chord member $GH$?

## Explanation

By symmetry each reaction is $18\,\mathrm{kN}$. A section through the second panel cuts $GH$, $GC$, and $BC$. The lines of action of $GC$ and $BC$ intersect at $C=(8,0)$. Isolate the left portion and take moments about $C$. The external forces on the left portion produce a clockwise moment of magnitude $18\cdot 8 - 12\cdot 4 = 96\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$. The force that $GH$ exerts on joint $G$ is $N_{GH}(1,\,0)$, with moment arm $3\,\mathrm m$. Thus $-96 - 3N_{GH} = 0$ and $N_{GH} = -32\,\mathrm{kN}$, so the top chord is **in compression**. The value $48\,\mathrm{kN}$ results from omitting the load at $B$.
