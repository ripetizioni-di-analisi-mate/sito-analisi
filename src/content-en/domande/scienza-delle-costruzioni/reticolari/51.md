A triangular truss has joints $A=(0,0)$, $B=(6,0)$, and $C=(3,4)$, with coordinates in meters, and members $AB$, $AC$, and $BC$. There is a pin at $A$. At $B$ a roller on an inclined surface provides a reaction parallel to vector $(-3,\,4)$. A downward load of $40\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $C$. What is the horizontal component of the reaction at $A$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;C,3,4" aste="A-B;A-C;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,306.87" reticolare=1 forze="C,0,-1,40 kN" quote="A,3 0,3 m,0.9;3 0,B,3 m,0.9;6.6 4,6.6 0,4 m,-0.55" titolo="Roller on an inclined plane at $B$"}

- $0\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right
- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right

## Explanation

Let $R$ be the magnitude of the roller reaction, whose components are $\left(-\dfrac35 R,\,\dfrac45 R\right)$. About $A$, the horizontal component acts along line $y = 0$ and has zero moment, whereas the vertical component has moment arm $6\,\mathrm m$. Moment equilibrium about $A$ therefore gives $6\cdot\dfrac45 R - 40\cdot 3 = 0$, hence $R = 25\,\mathrm{kN}$. The horizontal component of the reaction at $B$ is therefore $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left. Horizontal equilibrium of the entire structure requires $H_A = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$ **to the right**. A roller on a horizontal surface would instead give $H_A = 0$.
