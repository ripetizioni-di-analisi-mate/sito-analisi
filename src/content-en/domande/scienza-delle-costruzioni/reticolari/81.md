The truss shown has joints $A=(0,0)$, $B=(8,0)$, $C=(6,3)$, and $D=(2,3)$ (coordinates in meters) and members $AB$, $BC$, $CD$, $DA$, and $AC$. It has two pin supports at the same elevation, at $A$ and $B$. A downward load of $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at joint $D$. Which support reactions can be found using only the three equilibrium equations for the entire structure?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,8,0;C,6,3;D,2,3" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-A;A-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" reticolare=1 forze="D,0,-1.1,20 kN" quote="A,B,8 m,0.9;8.6 3,8.6 0,3 m,-0.55" titolo="Truss on two pin supports"}

- All four components
- Both vertical components, but only the sum of the horizontal components
- Both horizontal components, but only the sum of the vertical components
- None, because the truss is statically indeterminate

## Explanation

There are four reaction components and only three equations of overall equilibrium, so those equations alone cannot determine every component. The horizontal components $H_A$ and $H_B$ both act along line $y = 0$, which passes through $A$ and $B$. Taking moments about $A$ therefore leaves $V_B$ as the only unknown and gives $8V_B - 20\cdot 2 = 0$, or $V_B = 5\,\mathrm{kN}$. Taking moments about $B$ gives $V_A = 15\,\mathrm{kN}$. Horizontal equilibrium gives only $H_A + H_B = 0$. The structure is statically indeterminate to the first degree, so the distribution of the horizontal reactions depends on how the members deform.
