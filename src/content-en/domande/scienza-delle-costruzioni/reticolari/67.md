A triangular truss has joints $A=(0,0)$, $B=(5,0)$, and $C=(4,3)$ (coordinates in meters) and members $AB$, $AC$, and $BC$. It has a pin at $A$ and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. A force of $25\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $C$ along line $CA$, pointing toward $A$. What are the axial forces in the members?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,5,0;C,4,3" aste="A-B;A-C;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" reticolare=1 forze="C,-0.8,-0.6,25 kN" titolo="Force at $C$ directed toward the pin support"}

- $AC$ carries compression $25\,\mathrm{kN}$, $AB$ carries tension $20\,\mathrm{kN}$, and $BC$ carries zero force
- $AC$ carries tension $25\,\mathrm{kN}$, and $AB$ and $BC$ carry zero force
- $AC$ carries compression $25\,\mathrm{kN}$, and $AB$ and $BC$ carry zero force
- $AC$ in compression with $20\,\mathrm{kN}$, and $BC$ in compression with $15\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The line of action of the force passes through pin $A$, so its moment about $A$ is zero and the roller reaction at $B$ is zero. Joint $B$ therefore has no applied load or support reaction. Since the two members meeting there are not collinear, $AB$ and $BC$ are **unloaded**. Only member $AC$ remains at joint $C$. Its outward unit vector is $\left(-\dfrac45,\,-\dfrac35\right)$, and the applied force is $25\left(-\dfrac45,\,-\dfrac35\right)\mathrm{kN}$. Equilibrium gives $N_{AC} + 25 = 0$, or $N_{AC} = -25\,\mathrm{kN}$. Strut $AC$ alone carries the load to the pin.
