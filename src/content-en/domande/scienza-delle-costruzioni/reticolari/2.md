The joints of the truss shown have coordinates $A=(0,0)$, $B=(14,0)$ and $C=(5,12)$, in meters. The members are $AB$, $AC$ and $BC$. Joint $A$ has a pin support, and joint $B$ has a roller on a horizontal surface. A force of $84\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts downward at $C$. Find the axial force in member $AB$, taking tension as positive.

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,14,0;C,5,12" aste="A-B;A-C;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" reticolare=1 forze="C,0,-2.1,84 kN" quote="A,5 0,5 m;5 0,B,9 m;14.98 12,14.98 0,12 m,-0.55" titolo="Specified geometry, supports, and loads"}

- $22.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $-22.5\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $54\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $30\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Taking moments about $A$ gives $V_B=30\,\mathrm{kN}$, hence $V_A=54\,\mathrm{kN}$. For member $AC$ the ratio of horizontal to vertical projection is $\dfrac{5}{12}$. Equilibrium at $A$ gives $N_{AB}=54\cdot\dfrac{5}{12}$.
