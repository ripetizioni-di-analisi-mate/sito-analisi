In the truss shown, $BD$ is vertical and chords $AB$ and $BC$ are horizontal. The downward $28\,\mathrm{kN}$ force is moved from $B$ to $D$ along the same vertical line, keeping the load already at $D$. What changes?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,8,0;D,4,3" aste="A-B;B-C;A-D;B-D;C-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" reticolare=1 forze="B,0,-1.2,28 kN;D,0,-1.2,24 kN" quote="A,B,4 m;B,C,4 m;8.56 3,8.56 0,3 m,-0.55" titolo="Specified geometry, supports, and loads"}

- All support reactions and all internal forces are unchanged.
- The support reactions change but the force in $BD$ is unchanged.
- The vertical member switches from tension to compression of equal magnitude.
- The support reactions are unchanged and $BD$ becomes unloaded.

## Explanation

The moved force retains its resultant and moment about the supports, so the reactions are unchanged. Initially $BD$ balanced the vertical load at $B$. After the load is moved, the other two members at $B$ are horizontal, so $BD$ must carry zero force.
