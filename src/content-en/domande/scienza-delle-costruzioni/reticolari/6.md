The coordinates of the truss joints shown are $A=(0,0)$, $B=(4,0)$, $C=(8,0)$, and $D=(4,3)$, in meters. The members are $AB$, $BC$, $AD$, $BD$, and $CD$. Joint $A$ is pinned and joint $C$ is supported by a roller on a horizontal surface. A downward force of $28\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $B$. A downward force of $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $D$. What is the axial force in member $BD$, taking tension as positive?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,8,0;D,4,3" aste="A-B;B-C;A-D;B-D;C-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;C,carrello" reticolare=1 forze="B,0,-1.2,28 kN;D,0,-1.2,24 kN" quote="A,B,4 m;B,C,4 m;8.56 3,8.56 0,3 m,-0.55" titolo="Specified geometry, supports, and loads"}

- $-28\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $24\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $52\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $28\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Joint $B$ connects the horizontal chords $AB$ and $BC$ and the vertical member $BD$.

The forces of the two chords are horizontal, so only the force in $BD$ appears in the vertical equilibrium of the joint.

If the vertical member is in tension, it pulls joint $B$ toward $D$, that is upward, with a force equal to $N_{BD}$. Therefore vertical equilibrium gives $N_{BD}-28=0$.

Hence $N_{BD}=28\,\mathrm{kN}$, in tension. The load of $24\,\mathrm{kN}$ applied at $D$ appears only in the equilibrium of joint $D$.
