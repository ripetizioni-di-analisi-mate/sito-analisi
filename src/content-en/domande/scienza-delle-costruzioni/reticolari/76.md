A section through a parallel-chord truss cuts top chord member $DE$. Joint $D$ lies to the left of the section and $E$ to the right. Analyzing the left portion gave $N_{DE} = -8\,\mathrm{kN}$, with tension taken as positive. Now isolate the right portion. Which statement is correct?

- The member exerts $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left on joint $E$, and its axial force remains $N_{DE} = -8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- The axial force becomes $N_{DE} = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$ because the unit vector reverses direction
- The member exerts $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right on joint $E$, and its axial force remains $N_{DE} = -8\,\mathrm{kN}$
- The member exerts $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left on joint $E$, and its axial force becomes $N_{DE} = 8\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

The axial force $N_{DE}$ is a scalar that describes the state of the entire member. Its value remains $-8\,\mathrm{kN}$ whichever portion is isolated. The member exerts a force $N_{DE}\,\mathbf e_{ED}$ on joint $E$, where $\mathbf e_{ED} = (-1,\,0)$ is the unit vector from $E$ to $D$. This gives $-8\,(-1,\,0) = (8,\,0)\,\mathrm{kN}$, a force of $8\,\mathrm{kN}$ **to the right**. The compressed member pushes $E$ away from $D$. The same member exerts a force $(-8,\,0)\,\mathrm{kN}$ on joint $D$. The two forces are therefore equal and opposite, as required by Newton's third law. Reversing the sign of the axial force as well would give the same force vector on both portions, violating Newton's third law.
