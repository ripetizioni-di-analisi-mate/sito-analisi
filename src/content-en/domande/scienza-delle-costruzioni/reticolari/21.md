A horizontal beam $AB$ of length $4\,\mathrm m$ is pinned to a wall at $A=(0,0)$ and supported at $B=(4,0)$ by strut $BC$, which is pinned to the wall at $C=(0,-3)$. Coordinates are in meters. A downward load of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at midspan. The weight of the strut is negligible. What is the axial force in the strut?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,0,-3" aste="A-B;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera,180;C,cerniera,180" cerniere="B" forze="2 0,0,-0.9,12 kN" quote="A,B,4 m" titolo="Wall-mounted beam supported by a strut"}

- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression
- $20\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression
- $7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression
- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension

## Explanation

Only the pins at $B$ and $C$ exert forces on the strut. It is therefore a two-force member and acts on the beam along line $CB$. Member $CB$ has length $5\,\mathrm m$, so the unit vector from $C$ to $B$ is $\left(\dfrac45,\,\dfrac35\right)$. Isolate the beam and take moments about $A$, where the wall reaction has zero moment. At $B$, the vertical component $F_y$ of the strut force produces the moment that balances the applied load. Thus $4F_y = 12\cdot 2$ and $F_y = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. Since $F_y = \dfrac35\,|N|$, the result is $|N| = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$. The force on the beam points from $C$ toward $B$. The strut pushes $B$ away from $C$ and is therefore **in compression**.
