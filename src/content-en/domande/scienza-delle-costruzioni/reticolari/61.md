A two-panel cantilever truss projects $8\,\mathrm m$ from a wall. It is attached to the wall by a pin at lower joint $A=(0,0)$ and a roller with a horizontal reaction at upper joint $D=(0,3)$. The bottom chord is $ABC$ and the top chord is $DEF$, with $B$ and $E$ a distance $4\,\mathrm m$ from the wall. The verticals are $AD$, $BE$, and $CF$ and the diagonals $DB$ and $EC$. The only load is a downward force of $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ at joint $B$. How many zero-force members are there?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,8,0;D,0,3;E,4,3;F,8,3" aste="A-B;B-C;D-E;E-F;A-D;B-E;C-F;D-B;E-C" vincoli="A,cerniera,180;D,carrello,180" reticolare=1 forze="B,0,-1.1,15 kN" quote="A,B,4 m,0.9;B,C,4 m,0.9;8.55 3,8.55 0,3 m,-0.55" titolo="Cantilever truss loaded at joint $B$"}

- $2$
- $4$
- $6$
- $3$

## Explanation

Only $EF$ and $CF$ meet at unloaded joint $F$. Their directions are independent, so both members carry zero force. Only $BC$ and $EC$ then remain at joint $C$. Their directions are also independent, so both carry zero force. At joint $E$, after eliminating $EF$ and $EC$, only horizontal member $DE$ and vertical member $BE$ remain. Both therefore carry zero force. The zero-force members are $EF$, $CF$, $BC$, $EC$, $DE$, and $BE$, giving **six** in total. The load reaches the wall through $AB$, $DB$, and $AD$.
