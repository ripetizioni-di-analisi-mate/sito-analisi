The coordinates of the truss joints shown are $A=(0,0)$, $B=(4,0)$, $C=(8,0)$, $D=(0,3)$, $E=(4,3)$, and $F=(8,3)$, in meters. The members are $AB$, $BC$, $AD$, $BE$, $CF$, $DE$, $EF$, $BD$, and $CE$. Joint $A$ is pinned and joint $C$ is supported by a roller on a horizontal surface. A downward force of $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $D$. A downward force of $30\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $E$. Determine the axial force in member $BD$, taking tension as positive.

- $-25\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $25\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $15\,\mathrm{kN}$
- $50\,\mathrm{kN}$

## Explanation

Taking moments about $A$ gives $V_C=15\,\mathrm{kN}$ and hence $V_A=30\,\mathrm{kN}$. On the left portion, the load at $D$ balances $15\,\mathrm{kN}$ of that reaction. Diagonal $BD$ balances the remaining $15\,\mathrm{kN}$, with vertical direction cosine $\dfrac{3}{5}$.
