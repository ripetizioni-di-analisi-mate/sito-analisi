Members $AD$, $DC$, and $CB$ form a four-bar linkage with fixed pins $A=(0,0)$ and $B=(6,0)$ and moving joints $D=(1,3)$ and $C=(5,3)$. Coordinates are in meters. About which point does member $DC$ rotate instantaneously during an infinitesimal motion of the mechanism?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,6,0;D,1,3;C,5,3" aste="A-D;D-C;C-B" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,cerniera" reticolare=1 titolo="Pin-jointed quadrilateral on two pin supports"}

- $(3,\,0)$
- $(3,\,9)$
- $(3,\,3)$
- No point, because $DC$ translates

## Explanation

Member $AD$ rotates about pin $A$, so $D$ moves perpendicular to $AD$. Likewise, $C$ moves perpendicular to $BC$. The center of rotation of $DC$ lies on the line through $D$ perpendicular to the displacement of $D$, that is, line $AD$. By the same argument it lies on line $BC$. Line $AD$ has equation $y = 3x$ and line $BC$ has equation $y = -3(x - 6)$. They intersect where $3x = -3x + 18$, at $x = 3$, $y = 9$. Member $DC$ undergoes pure translation only when $AD$ and $BC$ are parallel, as in a rectangle.
