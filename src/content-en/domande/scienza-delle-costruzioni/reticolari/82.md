A triangular truss has joints $A=(0,0)$, $B=(8,0)$, and $C=(4,3)$ (coordinates in meters), a pin at $A$, and a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$. A downward load of $30\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $C$. All members have axial rigidity $EA = 40\,000\,\mathrm{kN}$. How far does roller support $B$ move?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,8,0;C,4,3" aste="A-B;A-C;B-C" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" reticolare=1 forze="C,0,-1,30 kN" quote="A,B,8 m,0.9;8.55 3,8.55 0,3 m,-0.55" titolo="Triangular truss loaded at the apex"}

- $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right
- $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the left
- $2\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right
- $5\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right

## Explanation

By symmetry, each vertical reaction is $15\,\mathrm{kN}$. At joint $A$, the vertical component of the force in diagonal $AC$, whose unit vector is $\left(\dfrac45,\,\dfrac35\right)$, balances the reaction. Thus $\dfrac35 N_{AC} + 15 = 0$ and $N_{AC} = -25\,\mathrm{kN}$. Horizontal equilibrium of joint $A$ gives $N_{AB} + \dfrac45 N_{AC} = 0$, hence $N_{AB} = 20\,\mathrm{kN}$. The chord elongation is $\Delta L_{AB} = \dfrac{20\cdot 8000}{40\,000} = 4\,\mathrm{mm}$. Since $A$ is fixed and the roller moves along line $AB$, the displacement of $B$ equals the elongation of $AB$, giving $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ **to the right**.
