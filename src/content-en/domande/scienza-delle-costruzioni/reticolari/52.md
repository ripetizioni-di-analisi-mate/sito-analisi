Analyze the tower truss shown by the method of joints. Its height is $6\,\mathrm m$ and its width is $2\,\mathrm m$. Its joints are $A=(0,0)$, $B=(2,0)$, $C=(0,3)$, $D=(2,3)$, $E=(0,6)$, and $F=(2,6)$, with coordinates in meters, and its members are $AC$, $CE$, $BD$, $DF$, $AB$, $CD$, $EF$, $AD$, and $CF$. There is a pin at $A$, a roller on a horizontal surface at $B$, and a horizontal force of $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ at $E$. Which joint can be solved by the method of joints before calculating the support reactions?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,2,0;C,0,3;D,2,3;E,0,6;F,2,6" aste="A-C;C-E;B-D;D-F;A-B;C-D;E-F;A-D;C-F" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello" reticolare=1 forze="E,1,0,6 kN" quote="A,B,2 m;2.55 3,2.55 0,3 m,-0.55;2.55 6,2.55 3,3 m,-0.55" titolo="Wind force at $E$ on a truss"}

- Joint $F$
- Joint $E$
- Joint $D$
- Joint $A$

## Explanation

A joint can be solved on its own when it has at most two unknown member forces in independent directions and no unknown reaction. At joint $E$ only vertical member $CE$, horizontal cross member $EF$, and the known force act. The two joint equilibrium equations therefore suffice. The solution is $N_{EF} = -6\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $N_{CE} = 0$. Three members, $DF$, $EF$, and $CF$, meet at joint $F$, and four meet at joint $D$. Joint $A$ has two unknown reaction components in addition to three member forces, so the overall equilibrium equations must be solved first.
