A vertical load $P$ crosses a truss bridge of span $16\,\mathrm m$. At any instant it acts at just one of the bottom joints $B$, $C$, and $D$, located $4$, $8$, and $12\,\mathrm m$ from pin $A$, respectively. There is a roller at $E$. The top chord is at elevation $3\,\mathrm m$, with joints $F$, $G$, and $H$ directly above $B$, $C$, and $D$, respectively. The end members are $AF$ and $HE$, the verticals are $BF$, $CG$, and $DH$, and the diagonals are $BG$ and $DG$. Which load position maximizes the magnitude of the axial force in top chord member $FG$?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,8,0;D,12,0;E,16,0;F,4,3;G,8,3;H,12,3" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E;F-G;G-H;A-F;H-E;B-F;C-G;D-H;B-G;D-G" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,carrello" reticolare=1 quote="A,B,4 m,0.9;B,C,4 m,0.9;C,D,4 m,0.9;D,E,4 m,0.9;16.6 3,16.6 0,3 m,-0.55" titolo="Load $P$ moves across joints $B$, $C$, and $D$"}

- At $C$
- At $D$
- The magnitude is the same for all three positions
- At $B$

## Explanation

A section through the second panel cuts $FG$, $BG$, and $BC$. The lines of action of $BG$ and $BC$ intersect at $B=(4,0)$. Taking moments about $B$ for the left portion gives $3\,|N_{FG}| = M_B$, where $M_B$ is the moment about $B$ of the external forces to the left of the section. When the load is at $B$, $C$, or $D$, the only external force on the left portion is reaction $V_A$, apart from a possible load whose line of action passes through $B$. Thus $M_B = 4V_A$. With the load at $B$, $C$, and $D$, the reaction is $\dfrac34 P$, $\dfrac12 P$, and $\dfrac14 P$, respectively, so the corresponding values of $|N_{FG}|$ are $P$, $\dfrac23 P$, and $\dfrac13 P$. The maximum occurs when the load is at $B$ because moments for chord $FG$ are taken about $B$, away from midspan.
