A triangular truss is pinned at $A$. The bilateral roller at $B$ moves on a vertical guide and provides a horizontal reaction. The joint coordinates in meters are $A\,(0,\,0)$, $B\,(4,\,3)$, and $C\,(3,\,3)$. A downward load of $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $C$. What is the roller reaction?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,3;C,3,3" aste="A-B;A-C;B-C" reticolare="1" vincoli="A,cerniera;B,carrello,0" forze="C,-0,-0.65,9 kN" titolo="Roller supported on a vertical guide"}

- $18\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $4.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left
- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the right
- $9\,\mathrm{kN}$ to the left

## Explanation

The vertical force at $C$ produces a clockwise moment of $9 \times 3\,\mathrm{kN\,m}$ about $A$. A leftward reaction at $B$ produces a counterclockwise moment and has moment arm $3\,\mathrm{m}$. Its magnitude is therefore $\dfrac{9 \times 3}{3}=9\,\mathrm{kN}$.
