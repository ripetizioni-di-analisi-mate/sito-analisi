Three members meet at an unloaded joint. The angle between each pair of members is $120^\circ$. The first member carries a tensile force of $12\,\mathrm{kN}$. What are the axial forces in the other two members?

::struttura{nodi="O,0,0;P,0,2.5,-;Q,-2.165,-1.25,-;R,2.165,-1.25,-" aste="O-P;O-Q;O-R" etichette="0.25,2.5,1;-2.4,-1.6,2;2.3,-1.6,3" titolo="Three members at $120^\circ$ at an unloaded joint"}

- Zero, because the joint is unloaded
- Tension $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ in each
- Tension $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ in each
- Compression $12\,\mathrm{kN}$ in each

## Explanation

Orient the first member along the $y$ axis, with outward unit vector $(0,\,1)$. The other two members have unit vectors $\left(-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2},\,-\dfrac12\right)$ and $\left(\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2},\,-\dfrac12\right)$. Horizontal equilibrium gives $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}(N_3 - N_2) = 0$, hence $N_2 = N_3$. Vertical equilibrium gives $N_1 - \dfrac12 N_2 - \dfrac12 N_3 = 0$, or $N_2 = N_3 = N_1 = 12\,\mathrm{kN}$. The zero-force member rules require either a joint with only two members or a joint with two collinear members. Neither condition holds here.
