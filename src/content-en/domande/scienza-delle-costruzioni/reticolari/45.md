Joint $C=(0,0)$ is suspended from fixed pins $A=(-3,4)$ and $B=(4,3)$ by members $AC$ and $BC$, both with axial rigidity $EA = 50\,000\,\mathrm{kN}$. Coordinates are in meters. A downward load of $50\,\mathrm{kN}$ acts at $C$. What is the first-order displacement of $C$?

::struttura{nodi="C,0,0;A,-3,4;B,4,3" aste="C-A;C-B" vincoli="A,cerniera,90;B,cerniera,90" reticolare=1 forze="C,0,-1,50 kN" titolo="Joint suspended from two members"}

- $5\,\mathrm{mm}$ downward, with no horizontal component
- $7\,\mathrm{mm}$ downward, with no horizontal component
- $1\,\mathrm{mm}$ downward, with no horizontal component
- $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ downward and $3\,\mathrm{mm}$ to the right

## Explanation

The members have length $5\,\mathrm m$. The unit vectors pointing away from $C$ are $\left(-\dfrac35,\,\dfrac45\right)$ and $\left(\dfrac45,\,\dfrac35\right)$, which are perpendicular. Each member force therefore balances the projection of the load along its own axis, giving $N_{AC} = 50\cdot\dfrac45 = 40\,\mathrm{kN}$ and $N_{BC} = 50\cdot\dfrac35 = 30\,\mathrm{kN}$, both in tension. The elongations are $\delta_{AC} = \dfrac{40\cdot 5000}{50\,000} = 4\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $\delta_{BC} = \dfrac{30\cdot 5000}{50\,000} = 3\,\mathrm{mm}$. Since the unit vectors are perpendicular, the displacement of $C$ is the sum of the elongations along the unit vectors directed from $A$ and $B$ toward $C$. Thus
$$
\mathbf u_C = 4\left(\frac35,\,-\frac45\right) + 3\left(-\frac45,\,-\frac35\right) = (0,\,-5)\,\mathrm{mm}.
$$
