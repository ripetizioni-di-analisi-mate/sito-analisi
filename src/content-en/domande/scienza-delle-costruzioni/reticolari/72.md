A truss bridge has four bays of length $4\,\mathrm m$, parallel chords spaced $3\,\mathrm m$ apart, and a vertical at each joint. The diagonals slope down toward midspan, with $FB$ and $GC$ on the left and $IC$ and $JD$ on the right. The bottom chord is $ABCDE$ and the top chord is $FGHIJ$. The bridge has a pin at $A$ and a roller at $E$. Each of the three interior bottom joints carries $12\,\mathrm{kN}$. What is the axial force in diagonal $GC$ of the second panel?

::struttura{nodi="A,0,0;B,4,0;C,8,0;D,12,0;E,16,0;F,0,3;G,4,3;H,8,3;I,12,3;J,16,3" aste="A-B;B-C;C-D;D-E;F-G;G-H;H-I;I-J;A-F;B-G;C-H;D-I;E-J;F-B;G-C;I-C;J-D" vincoli="A,cerniera;E,carrello" reticolare=1 forze="B,0,-1.2,12 kN;C,0,-1.2,12 kN;D,0,-1.2,12 kN" quote="F,G,4 m,-1.0;G,H,4 m,-1.0;H,I,4 m,-1.0;I,J,4 m,-1.0;16.6 3,16.6 0,3 m,-0.55" titolo="Parallel-chord truss with diagonals sloping toward midspan"}

- $6\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ in compression
- $10\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension
- $7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ in tension

## Explanation

By symmetry each reaction is $18\,\mathrm{kN}$. A section through the second panel cuts horizontal chords $GH$ and $BC$ and diagonal $GC$. The two member forces to be eliminated are parallel, so sum the vertical components of the forces on the left portion. The external forces give a net force of $18 - 12 = 6\,\mathrm{kN}$ upward. The diagonal has unit vector $\left(\dfrac45,\,-\dfrac35\right)$ from $G$ to $C$ and contributes a vertical component of $-\dfrac35 N_{GC}$. Thus $6 - \dfrac35 N_{GC} = 0$ and $N_{GC} = 10\,\mathrm{kN}$, in **tension**. The value $7.5\,\mathrm{kN}$ results from using $\dfrac45$ in place of $\dfrac35$.
