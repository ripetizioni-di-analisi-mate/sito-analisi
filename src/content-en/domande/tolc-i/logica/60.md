One check occurs every $6$ minutes and another every $8$. They coincide at minute zero. How many further coincidences occur by minute $50$, inclusive?

- $3$
- $6$
- $8$
- $1$
- $2$

## Explanation

A coincidence requires a time that is a multiple of both $6$ and $8$. Their least common multiple is $24$, so later coincidences occur at minutes $24,48,72,\ldots$. Only $24$ and $48$ lie within minute $50$. Minute zero is excluded by the request for further coincidences.
