What is the negation of “Exactly one question was left unanswered”?

- All questions were left unanswered
- At least one question was left unanswered
- No question or at least two questions were left unanswered
- Exactly two questions were left unanswered
- At most one question was left unanswered

## Explanation

The number of omitted questions is a nonnegative integer. The original statement requires this number to be $1$. Negating it allows every other value, namely $0$ or $2,3,4,\ldots$. “Exactly two” describes only one case allowed by the negation and is therefore not equivalent to it.
