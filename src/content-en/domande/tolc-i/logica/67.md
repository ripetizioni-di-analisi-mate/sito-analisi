In group A, $18$ of $20$ students pass; in group B, $24$ of $30$ pass. Which conclusion is correct?

- B has both more passes and a higher percentage
- B has more passes, but A has the higher pass percentage
- A has more passes than B
- The percentages are equal
- The percentages cannot be compared

## Explanation

The absolute counts give $24>18$, so B has more passes. The proportions are $\dfrac{18}{20}=90\%$ and $\dfrac{24}{30}=80\%$, so A has the higher percentage. These comparisons answer different questions because the groups have different sizes. Neither fact alone establishes the cause of the difference.
