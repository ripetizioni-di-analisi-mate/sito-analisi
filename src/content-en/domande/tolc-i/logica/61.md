Anna is thirteenth from the front and ninth from the back of a queue, with no ties. How many people are in the queue?

- $21$
- $22$
- $20$
- $23$
- $19$

## Explanation

There are $12$ people ahead of Anna and $8$ behind her. Adding Anna once gives $12+1+8=21$. Equivalently, $13+9-1=21$: subtraction removes Anna’s duplicate appearance in the two rank counts. The direct sum $22$ would count her twice.
