Two classes use different materials, and the class with the new material performs better. Which fact most directly supports attributing the difference to the material?

- The new material has a larger cover
- The better class already performed much better beforehand
- The tests had different difficulty levels
- Students studied for unrecorded lengths of time
- The classes had comparable initial preparation and the same study time

## Explanation

Initial preparation and study time are plausible alternative explanations of the results. Making them comparable reduces these alternatives and supports the link to the material. This is not absolute proof, because other factors might remain, but the comparison is more informative than one between groups that differed greatly at the outset.
