A satisfaction survey is sent only to subscribers who renewed. Ninety percent respond positively. What limits the conclusion “90% of all subscribers are satisfied”?

- Every survey is necessarily false
- 90% means all subscribers
- Nonrenewers are excluded from the sample and may hold different views
- Renewal proves dissatisfaction
- The number of positive responses is certainly zero

## Explanation

The sample is selected through behavior that may depend on satisfaction. The responses therefore describe the renewing subscribers contacted, while nonrenewers’ views are missing. If that group were less satisfied, extending the percentage to everyone would overestimate satisfaction. The collected result is not denied; the scope of its generalization is limited.
