In the diagram, the shaded regions belong only to A and only to B. Which property do they represent?

::venn{tipo="due" nomi="A,B" zone="a,b" titolo="The two regions outside the intersection are shaded"}

- Belonging to both sets
- Belonging to neither set
- Belonging to at least one, including the intersection
- Belonging to exactly one of the two sets
- Belonging to A, including the intersection

## Explanation

The A-only region satisfies A and not B; the B-only region satisfies B and not A. Their union is the symmetric difference $(A\setminus B)\cup(B\setminus A)$. The intersection is excluded, as is the region outside both sets. Every shaded element has exactly one of the two properties.
