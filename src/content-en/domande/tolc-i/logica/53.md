Three boxes contain only apples, only pears, and a mixture of apples and pears. Their labels “apples”, “pears”, “mixed” are all wrong. An apple is drawn from the box labeled “mixed”. What does the box labeled “pears” contain?

- Only apples
- Only pears
- The mixture of apples and pears
- No fruit
- It cannot be determined

## Explanation

The “mixed” box cannot be mixed and contains an apple, so it contains only apples. The mixture and pears-only contents remain for the other two boxes. The box labeled “pears” cannot contain only pears because its label is also wrong. It must therefore contain the mixture.
