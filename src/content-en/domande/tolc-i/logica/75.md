Which formula is true for every truth assignment to $P$ and $Q$?

- $P\land Q$
- $P\Rightarrow Q$
- $P\iff Q$
- $\neg P\land Q$
- $(P\land Q)\Rightarrow P$

## Explanation

Whenever $P\land Q$ is true, $P$ must be true, so the proposed implication cannot have a true antecedent and false consequent. When the conjunction is false, the implication is true as well. Each other formula can be made false by a suitable assignment to the two statements.
