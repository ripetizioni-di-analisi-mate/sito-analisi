Which formula is equivalent to $(P\land Q)\lor(P\land\neg Q)$?

- $Q$
- $P\land Q$
- $\neg P$
- $P$
- $P\lor Q$

## Explanation

Both terms require $P$. If $P$ is true, one of $Q$ and $\neg Q$ is always true, so at least one term of the disjunction is true. If $P$ is false, both terms are false. The formula therefore has exactly the same value as $P$, independently of $Q$.
