Rooms $1,2,3,4,5$ lie in a line, and each step leads to an adjacent room. Starting in room $2$, which room is impossible to reach after exactly three steps?

- Room 1
- Room 3
- Room 5
- Room 4
- None of the indicated rooms

## Explanation

Every step changes the parity of the room number. After three changes, the even number $2$ becomes an odd number. Room $4$ is therefore impossible. The others are reachable, for example along $2,1,2,1$; $2,3,2,3$; and $2,3,4,5$.
