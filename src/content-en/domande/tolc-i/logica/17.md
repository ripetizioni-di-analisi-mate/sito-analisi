A form must contain exactly one of a digital signature $P$ and a paper signature $Q$. Which formula expresses the requirement?

- $P\lor Q$
- $(P\lor Q)\land\neg(P\land Q)$
- $P\land Q$
- $\neg P\land\neg Q$
- $P\Rightarrow Q$

## Explanation

The first part, $P\lor Q$, requires at least one signature to be present. The second, $\neg(P\land Q)$, rules out both being present together. These conditions must hold together, so they are joined by conjunction. Exactly the digital-only and paper-only cases remain.
