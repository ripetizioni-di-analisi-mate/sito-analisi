Three operators A, B and C each receive one of the distinct shifts A, B and C. Nobody may receive the shift bearing their own letter. How many assignments are possible?

- $1$
- $3$
- $4$
- $2$
- $6$

## Explanation

If operator A receives B, operator C must receive A and B receives C. If A receives C, B must receive A and C receives B. These two choices for A exhaust the allowed cases, and each determines the others. In operator order A-B-C, the assignments are BCA and CAB.
