Anna arrives before Bruno. Carla arrives after Bruno. Their arrival times are distinct. What is their arrival order?

- Anna, Bruno, Carla
- Bruno, Anna, Carla
- Anna, Carla, Bruno
- Carla, Bruno, Anna
- Bruno, Carla, Anna

## Explanation

The first statement gives Anna $<$ Bruno; the second gives Bruno $<$ Carla. They combine into Anna $<$ Bruno $<$ Carla. Since these are the only three participants and there are no ties, the chain determines every position. No additional constraint is needed.
