“Having a badge is necessary for entry.” Which situation contradicts this rule?

- A person has a badge and stays outside
- A person has a badge and enters
- A person without a badge stays outside
- A person enters without a badge
- Nobody enters

## Explanation

The requirement translates as entry $\Rightarrow$ badge. An implication is violated only when its antecedent occurs without its consequent. The forbidden case is therefore entry by a person without a badge. Having a badge and staying outside is compatible, because the rule does not state that a badge is sufficient.
