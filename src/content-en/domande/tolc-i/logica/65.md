A test awards $2$ points for a correct answer and subtracts $1$ for a wrong one. All five questions are answered, and the score is $4$. How many answers are correct?

- $2$
- $4$
- $1$
- $5$
- $3$

## Explanation

If $c$ is the number correct, the number wrong is $5-c$. The score is therefore $2c-(5-c)=3c-5$. Setting $3c-5=4$ gives $c=3$. Checking: three correct answers earn six points and two errors lose two, giving $6-2=4$.
