Among coins A, B and C, exactly one is heavier and the other two have equal mass. A and B balance on a two-pan balance. Which coin is heavier?

- A
- B
- A or B
- C
- The information is insufficient

## Explanation

If the heavier coin were on either pan, that pan would outweigh the other. Balance therefore rules out both A and B. Since exactly one of the three is guaranteed heavier, C remains. This reasoning uses the fact that the two normal coins have equal mass.
