An archive has $40$ records: $21$ have a note and $12$ an attachment. What is the largest possible number having neither?

- $7$
- $12$
- $21$
- $28$
- $19$

## Explanation

To maximize the number outside, minimize the union. All $12$ attachments can belong to records already among the $21$ with notes, making the union size $21$. Then $40-21=19$ remain outside. A smaller union is impossible because it must contain all $21$ records with notes.
