Which facts refute “If $P$, then $Q$ or $R$”, with inclusive disjunction?

- $\neg P\land Q\land R$
- $P\land\neg Q\land\neg R$
- $P\land Q\land\neg R$
- $P\land\neg Q\land R$
- $\neg P\land\neg Q\land\neg R$

## Explanation

An implication is false when its antecedent is true and its consequent is false. Here $P$ must therefore be true and $Q\lor R$ false. An inclusive disjunction is false only when both its members are false. Combining the three conditions gives $P\land\neg Q\land\neg R$.
