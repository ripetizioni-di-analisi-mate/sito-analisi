Among $28$ people, $15$ choose the morning shift, $12$ the afternoon shift and $4$ both. How many choose at most one shift?

- $19$
- $24$
- $23$
- $5$
- $27$

## Explanation

“At most one shift” includes those choosing zero and those choosing one. Only the four people choosing both must be excluded, giving $28-4=24$. The value $15+12-2\cdot4=19$ would count only those choosing exactly one shift, omitting those choosing neither.
