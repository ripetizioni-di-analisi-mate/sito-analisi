Exactly two proposals are chosen from A, B, C and D. Choosing A requires choosing B. C and D are incompatible. D is chosen. Which other proposal must be chosen?

- A
- C
- A or C
- None: the conditions are impossible
- B

## Explanation

C is excluded because it is incompatible with D. A is excluded because it would also require B, bringing the total to three proposals. B remains, and BD satisfies every constraint: it has two proposals, contains no A and excludes C. This pair also confirms that the conditions are consistent.
