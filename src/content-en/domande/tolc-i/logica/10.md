A box contains $3$ red balls and $4$ blue ones. Balls are drawn without looking and without replacement. What is the smallest number of draws guaranteeing two balls of the same color?

- $2$
- $4$
- $5$
- $7$
- $3$

## Explanation

With two draws it is still possible to obtain one red and one blue ball, so two are insufficient. With three balls distributed among two colors, the pigeonhole principle guarantees that one color occurs twice. The red-blue case shows that the preceding bound is attainable; therefore three is the required minimum.
