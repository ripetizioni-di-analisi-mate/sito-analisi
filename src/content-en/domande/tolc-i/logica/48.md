A directed network has edges A→B, A→C, B→D, C→D and B→C. How many distinct directed paths lead from A to D?

- $2$
- $4$
- $3$
- $5$
- $6$

## Explanation

From A, one can choose B or C. Through C, only C→D remains. Through B, one can go directly to D or pass through C first. The paths are A-B-D, A-C-D and A-B-C-D, for a total of three. There are no backward edges creating further cases.
