Let $A=\{0,1,2,3,4\}$, $B=\{3,4,5,6\}$ and $C=\{1,4,6\}$. Which elements belong to exactly two sets?

- $\{1,3,6\}$
- $\{1,3,4,6\}$
- $\{4\}$
- $\{0,2,5\}$
- $\{3,4\}$

## Explanation

Element $1$ belongs to A and C, $3$ to A and B, and $6$ to B and C. Each therefore occurs twice. Element $4$ occurs in all three sets, so it does not satisfy “exactly two”. Elements $0,2,5$ occur only once.
