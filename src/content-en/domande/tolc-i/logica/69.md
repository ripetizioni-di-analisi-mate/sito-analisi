Library attendance rises after a new opening schedule is introduced. It is concluded that the schedule caused the increase. Which information most directly weakens that conclusion?

- The library has tables
- The schedule is displayed at the entrance
- Some books have blue covers
- During the same period, library attendance became compulsory for a large course
- The library already existed the previous year

## Explanation

The attendance requirement provides an alternative explanation for the increase, independent of the usefulness of the new schedule. The other facts link no change to attendance. The new information does not prove that the schedule is ineffective; it shows that a simple before-after comparison cannot isolate its effect.
