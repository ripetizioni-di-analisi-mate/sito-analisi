$23$ sheets are distributed among five folders. Each folder contains at least four and at most five sheets. How many folders contain five sheets?

- $2$
- $4$
- $1$
- $3$
- $5$

## Explanation

Assigning four sheets to every folder uses $5\cdot4=20$ sheets. Three remain. Since no folder can exceed five, each remaining sheet goes into a different folder. Exactly three folders therefore reach five sheets, while two stay at four. The reconstructed total is $3\cdot5+2\cdot4=23$.
