A sequence starts at $3$ and successively adds the positive even integers $2,4,6,8,\ldots$. What is its fifth term?

- $21$
- $15$
- $31$
- $23$
- $19$

## Explanation

The first term is already $3$, so four updates are needed to reach the fifth. The resulting terms are $3,5,9,15,23$, using increments $2,4,6,8$. The final value is therefore $15+8=23$. The continuation is determined by the explicit rule, rather than merely by the appearance of the first terms.
