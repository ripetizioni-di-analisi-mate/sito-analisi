Starting from the number $9$, the following rule is applied five times: if the number is odd, $3$ is added to it; if it is even, it is divided by $2$. Which number is obtained at the end?

- $6$
- $12$
- $9$
- $3$
- $18$

## Explanation

At each step the rule is chosen according to the number just obtained. Since $9$ is odd, we get $12$; then $12$ is even and gives $6$, and $6$ is even and gives $3$. The number $3$ is odd and gives $6$, which is even and gives $3$. Hence after five applications we obtain $3$. The value $6$ is obtained by counting the initial number as a step as well.
